UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #603

Day 19: Past, Present, and Pool

6 Aug 2004

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — The Renegade's Abandoned Bar

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Day 19 [Nineteen days.] [Four hundred fifty-six hours.] [Twenty-seven thousand, three hundred sixty minutes.] [Tomorrow will mark “The Renegade” Rich Rollins’ World Television Title reign as the longest in over seven months. The longest reign of 2004, to be exact. The only other man who matches him, Jay Vyper, was the man Rich Rollins beat for the title in the first place.] [In sixteen days (or three hundred eighty-four hours for those keeping track), Beelzebozo’s marathon reign will be breached, leaving only one name left standing. One man to overcome. To last longer than one man, to be recorded as the single longest World Television Championship reign in UWF history.] [Seymour Almasy.] [In fourty days, history will be made all over again.] … […static…static…static…static…] […static…static…] … [The camera fades in from the static and snow to a simple visual: Rich “The Renegade” Rollins is leaning across a pool table from a beautiful blonde, who takes careful aim with her pool cue. Moving with a fluid, slender grace that the curves of her body would suggest, Lacy Reinking sinks the 9-ball in a side pocket, her full lips coming up into a wry grin.] Lacy: Think you can hang with a big girl, hot shot? [Carefully selecting a cue from an unsteady rack on the wall, Rollins chuckles.] Renegade: I don’t know, Lace. You may be more than I can handle. Lacy: Got that right, tiger. [With scary percision, Lacy sinks another ball and flashes The Renegade her pearly whites.] Lacy: Getting tired yet? Renegade: Tired of watching you showboating. Lacy: (laughing) C’mon, Rich… it’s not like you’ve never been one to showboat yourself. I’m just evening the playing field. [Rollins doesn’t return the smile. He surveys the pool table intensly, but it’s obvious his attention is elsewhere. Setting her pool cue down, Lacy gently leans on the finished wood frame and frowns.] Lacy: What’s the matter, Rich? You aren’t your usual angry-at-the-world self tonight. And not this quiet. Renegade: I’m not much of a talker. Lacy: Don’t give me that. You’ve always been able to talk to me. [A deep sigh escapes from The Renegade. He gently places his unused pool cue across the table and leans over on his elbows, massaging his temples with his forefingers and thumbs.] Renegade: Did you watch the news yesterday? Lacy: I heard what you did to one of Becker’s friends through the grapevine, but not the news. Sorry. [Silence.] Lacy: Rollins? [The Renegade looks up at her then, his eyes conflicted. Angry.] [Afraid.] [Lacy had only ever seen that look once. And she dared not think about it in almost two years.] Lacy: You’re starting to scare me, Rich. Renegade: Did you notice that tonight was the first night I’d invited you over to my place since… well, since the incident? Lacy: The thought had occurred to me, yes. Renegade: Something happened a few days ago out on the turnpike. The police found a car. Lacy: That’s what police do. They find cars all the time. Renegade: Not with three severed heads in the trunk, they don’t. [Deadly silence.] Lacy: What are you…? Renegade: Lacy, one of them was Becker. It was his car. Lacy: This isn’t funny. Renegade: Do I look like I’m joking? [A handful of heatbeats pass between the two of them, leaning together across the top of an old pool table. Somewhere outside, a car’s tires screech into the night. A horn blares through the stillness. The city of Chicago refuses to be ignored, even now.] [But none of that bothers these two, locked in one another’s gaze.] Lacy: (frustrated) What the hell does any of that have to do with us? Renegade: Because the other two heads were people I knew. Two wrestlers from the CWU, people I was friends with years ago. The Jhub and Kid Karnage, or as the rest of the world knew them, Alexander Dean and James Holden. That’s too much of a coincidence, Lacy. Lacy: Have… have the police talked to you? Renegade: (shaking his head) Not yet. I don’t know whether to go to them and tell ‘em what I know - which is nothing - or just wait it out. Either way, I was scared tonight. Lacy: You don’t get scared easy, Rollins. [Casting his eyes down, Rollins shrugs.] Renegade: I was afraid somebody might come after you. It’s… it’s like when I was in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, all over again. Lacy: Rich… (she places one hand on his arm)… whoever it was who took me two years ago, he’s long gone by now. I’ve dealt with my past, and now you need to move on, too. I left my boyfriend because of you, did you know that? Renegade: That asshole taxi driver? I had a feeling I had something to do with that. Lacy: Yeah, so did he. [She sighs. Gently rubbing his arm, she eases off the pool table and picks up her pool cue, causing The Renegade to look back up.] Lacy: Do you know what I do when my mind refuses to function? Renegade: What’s that? Lacy: I play pool. [Rollins laughs.] Renegade: My mind stopped functioning for me a long while back, Lace. Hell, I still have a title match to prepare for. On top of all of this… Lacy: A title match? Against who? Renegade: (running a hand through his hair) Somebody named Shane Stone. Never seen him, never heard about him. And on top of that, the Regal Rumble is coming up next weekend. I still need to book my ticket to Japan. Lacy: You’re going to Japan? Renegade: Well, the Pay-Per-View is called “Terror in Tokyo,” after all. What do you think? Lacy: Smartass. [The Renegade grins, his eyes blazing.] Renegade: Just another step to making history with my Television title. It ain’t easy, I’ll tell you. [Sizing up the pool table, Lacy takes aim and fires another ball into another pocket.] Lacy: Well, if you’re as good at wrestling as you are at pool, I’d be able to strip that belt off you. Renegade: Was that a proposition? Lacy: Tiger, I’d wrestle you any day. [With a grin that speak volumes, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins walks toward the small hand-held camcorder that has sat silently, recording everything with an unbiased eye. His hand reaches beyond the lens’ view…] Renegade: This could come in handy, you know… […static…static…static…] […static…static…] […static…] [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
