6/4/2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's Dressing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Well promotion time for Renaissance, UWF’s latest PPV, is rapidly diminishing, and of course the hype has been astronomical. Every member of the UWF new and old have been booked for a whole host of intriguing matches. The Tag Title’s rumble, the IC ladder match, TLC, jobbers/unknown talent matches and of course the World Title match up seeing whether a Communist Scotsman becomes the forefront of UWF talent or if a psychotic clown remains the champion. It’s been a very strange journey between the World Champ and the Number one contender Joshua Dunn, first he asks a favour of him, which was shot down before even being given a second thought, and a new proposal to make the match up No disqualifications match. So far Beezlebozo has deigned not to air a reply, while Dunn continues to manipulate the crowd to his way of thinking, already turning himself into the underdog and making the crowds of people spit out the name Beezlebozo like it has a sour taste in the mouth. The only question remaining is what will happen next?] [The Scene opens in the Dressing Room of Joshua Dunn. The communist décor is predominate throughout, the centre of focus the large red sofa with the coffee table in front. Sitting at the sofa and table is Joshua Dunn, the Communist Scotsman. He looks as if he was expecting the camera this time, not caught off guard like the other times in his promotions. The golden chain with the communist emblem is dangling loose over his red T-Shirt; his elbows perched on his black jeans. The look on his face is calculating, measuring up the opponent who will definitely be watching this promotion at some point. Hand clasped in front of his face in the demeanour of the same calculating stance, index fingers steepled. As the camera gets closer, the shine in his eyes is more evident as his green orbs probe deeply into the people watching at home] [A sideways grin creeps across his face, the calculating finally over, and he’s satisfied with the results of that. He lowers his forearms and lets them hang loose over his legs] JD: Well Beezlebozo… Have you given my latest proposal any thought? You must be, how can you not? I mean getting the change to beat me down in any way possible, anywhere, without having to stop until you feel like it, that must appeal to you mustn’t it? [He leans back and crosses his arms over his chest, muscles bulging] JD: Your obviously thinking very hard and very deeply about it, otherwise I’m sure you would have been on television by now shouting the odds like only you can. There is one thing that I have to think that you might have the most problems with. That being the Players Club. I can insist, on my word as an athlete that neither Mike Montgomery nor Ezra Kazee will be joining us on my behalf. Other parties that both you and I have problems with I cannot guarantee, but I will not have them showing up to aid me in any way shape or form. This I can assure. [His head cocks slightly, as if another brilliant idea has just entered his head] JD: In fact, you just say the word and all members of the UWF will be banned from Ringside on penalty of expulsion from the federation on breach of that fact, Just you, me and whatever else we can get our hands on. That must be much more appealing, no? I can’t make it happen if you don’t give this the go ahead. [A twitch of the upper lip shows that a bad thought passed through the forward planning brain of Dunn] JD: Then again, you may wonder why I want to stack all the cards in your favour, perhaps I have a trick up my sleeve, but the truth of the matter is that there is no trick, just the simple fact that the crowd love an underdog, and that a games not all that fun when all the cards are stacked in your favour. I don’t want to be denied the World Title because the Referee disqualifies you, I don’t want you staying outside the ring until your counted out and retaining the title, if I walk out with out the title I want to be able to say that it was because you were the superior athlete. You mocked my honesty. I have the utmost respect for those who play the game properly, who don’t look for the easy options, this is why I, and probably you too, despise Ruff Ryder with such a passion. I cannot try to pretend to know you, because I don’t. But you must be out to prove yourself to those who consider you all that’s wrong with the federation. I only offer to do this by giving you the chance to do that. I must be the only person that can honestly be considered a rival to your reign as World Champion, I challenge for you to find another Worthy Opponent. If you can defeat me fairly, you have all doubters swayed to your way of thinking. You didn’t choose that option, so instead, I ask for the chance to prove myself against the man holding the top place of the federation that I would occupy if you weren’t here. We’ve both beaten the previous World Champion, Seymour Almasy. Yet we’ve still to face each other in a single’s match. [Dunn unfolds his left arm from his chest to rub his left temple, obviously fatigued from trying to form an argument with himself] JD: Perhaps just the fact our match could be deemed the greatest match in UWF history would sway you to accepting my new terms, the two greatest wrestlers of the federation battling it out with only their abilities and their own initiative to rely on? We could make it the one to remember, the one all other matches are compared to for the rest of the UWF’s existence. How would it feel to have your name written down in history, to be ever remembered? Goading is not the question here, all I want is customer satisfaction, because if they didn’t watch we wouldn’t have jobs, and we’d be arrested for all the things we do to each other here. [He now stands up giving the camera one last long hard look] JD: Consider my new offer, No DQ, pin counts anywhere and all members of the UWF are banned from ringside. Just you, me and the referee. We are what the crowd come to see, they shouldn’t be disappointed [He walks off of camera view; the camera turns to see Dunn moving out of the dressing room. He turns in the doorway] JD: Until Renaissance [Dunn exits the room shutting the door behind him, facing the inside of the dressing room door, The Scene Fades]
