UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #322

Be careful what you wish for

11 Apr 2004

Nick Persia — In the lockerroom

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Just a day after the big Renaissance extravaganza we have an exclusive update on Nick Persia’s condition after the brutal attack he received at the hands of Adien Pryde. The reason he gave was ignorance of his abilities and skills.] (We see a taped up Nick Persia with the anger on his eyes. He seems to be breathing fire. On his shoulder we see the UWF Hardcore Championship) Nick Persia: “Sometimes in life you have to bleed and sweat to get what you want. I paid a prize to get to the Hardcore Championship. I won it, in a fair way. The Plague was an opponent worthy of remembrance. However I got a lead pipe to the back of my head from behind.” Nick Persia takes a deep breath Nick Persia: “That person had accused me of ignoring him. I was supposed to not pay attention to him. I did know all along about your challenge. I didnot accept it, for your sake. I didnot accept because I did you a favour. You and your well being. But since you sent me a strong message about not taking no for an answer you leave me just one option. Yes. I’ll face you at SAS. You will learn what it is like to stand one-on-one with a Hardcore Champion. I don’t care if you bring your cronies with you. I can take them all. You need to realise that you just made a very big mistake. For mistakes you’ll pay the prize. Believe me, you will pay. You said I shouldn’t ignore you? From now on I’ll take you seriously. When my blood flowed I signed your death warrant with it.” Nick Persia spits blood out of his mouth to the ground. Nick Persia: “Be careful what you wish for Adien Pryde because by God you are going to get it. I’m out to get my revenge. You have a target at yoru back. I think you aimed a little bit too high Adien. You should have finished what you started. Don’t leave living people like they are death. I promise you Pryde, there is no hope for you left. God has forsaken you, and punishment has just come along the way.” Nick Persia signs Pryde is death with his thumb and neck. [Screen fades out going into a recap of Renaissance]
