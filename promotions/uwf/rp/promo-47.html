UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #47

Operation: Bunny Propaganda

7 Oct 2003

Bunny Army of Doom — Bunny Camp

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Throughout all of history mighty armies have marched onward toward the ultimate goal of world domination; the Roman army, Napoleon, the Nazis of Germany, and many older and newer. Now there is another threat to the world and peace as we know it. This threat is much different from all the others before it though. This threat is fluffier and much cuter. This time it is the Bunny Army of DOOM~! Laguna: That’s me! Though I don’t like the idea of them claiming we want to “take over the world”. We don’t want that. No silly! We want to unite the people and bring universal peace to everyone. Isn’t that a better word than “taking over the world”? Ward: But Laguna, we do want to take over the world. Laguna turns around abruptly to see his bald friend Ward. Being angry at his last comment Laguna smacks Ward over the head with a backhanded slap. Laguna: Shuddaup your mouth! We’re bringing universal peace. Remember? Ward: Oh yeah…{laughing slightly} how could I forget? So uh Laguna, why did you call me in here outside of wanting to slap me? The leader of the bunny army walks away from Ward and goes to his war desk. After a few seconds of digging through the rubbish on the desk he grabs a rolled up piece of paper. He turns and proudly shows it to his friend, holding the document over his head. Laguna: My new plan to help us take over the….urgh for bringing universal peace: Operation Bunny Propaganda! Ward: Bunny Propa-what? What are you planning now Laguna? And please don’t tell me it involves me dressing up like a chicken and running around stealing eggs in a town. Laguna: {grumbling} That was a good idea….if the farmer wouldn’t have thought you was a real chicken and tried to get the rooster to mate with you. Ward: I’m still scarred. :’( Blowing off that last comment, Laguna starts to roll out the document on the desk. Ward walks over behind him to see what the document says. On the top of the paper in big bold letters is the words “Bunny Propaganda”. Ward tries to read the document over Laguna’s shoulder but all he can make out is a bunch of doodles of bunnies Laguna drew on the paper. Ward: So what exactly is this bunny propaganda stuff you are talking about? Laguna spins around and jumps onto a rolling chair. The force of him jumping onto the chair causes it to flip out from under him sending the Bunny Army of DOOM~! leader flipping backwards onto his back. Ward rushes over to help his friend but Laguna springs back to his feet still supporting that goofy grin. Laguna flashes Ward the “V” victory sign to let him know he’s alright. Laguna: Operation Bunny Propaganda is simple; we will merely convince everyone of what they already know. Bunnies are cute, cuddly, and would never hurt a fly. The people should not fear our bunnies, but rather submit to our way because it is better. If it is carried out perfectly then this could be the deciding advantage we need to clinch this war and completely dominate the UWF! Two bunnies, one dressed similar to Chris Storm and another very similar to Tessa Windsor, hop between Laguna and Ward. They both look at the two bunnies confused. Kiros comes running through the tent chasing after them. The two bunnies and Kiros exit through the other side of the tent leaving Ward thoroughly confused. Ward: Is that part of the operation? Laguna: No that is another project; I’ll get into it later if you like. Ward looks at where the bunnies exited too, still stunned and confused looking. Ward: Uh, I don’t think I want to know. Say….are you touching the Windsor-Bunny? Laguna jumps up and smacks Ward in the back of the head with another solid slap. Laguna: NO! Not focus on the mission at hand. This is why I called you in here; we have been accepted as a member of the UWF. Those stupid bastards, they have even been as kind as to give us a chance to fight two of their very best. Soon they will start advertising for The Bunny Army of DOOM~! v. The Legends. This will be our first chance to carry out Operation: Bunny Propaganda. Ward: So what do you want me to do? Laguna: If you would shuddaup your mouth for a second I would tell you! At the show I want you to pass out fliers praising the Bunny Army of DOOM~! Do not miss a single person in the crowd or backstage worker. You’ll be assigned a small team of bunnies to help you in your mission. Meanwhile one of our super bunnies and I will take on the Legends and destroy them as an example for the rest of the UWF that their new leader is here. Now wear this when you do this. Ward’s eyes become enlarged to the size of a saucer as Laguna pulls out a giant bunny suit. Ward nearly trips over a footrest as he backs away for the horrid costume. Laguna continues to approach Ward, holding out the costume for him. Ward: Oh no Laguna, no way. I am not putting on that costume. No way in hell! Laguna: Oh come on now, it gets better! Only adding to Ward’s horror, Laguna holds out an Easter basket full of fliers with pictures of bunnies on them. Ward continues to back away from Laguna only to trip on maps that had fell off Laguna’s war desk. Laguna looms over Ward holding the basket and costume. A horrific scream fills the lungs of Ward. The plan is laid out, the wheels are moving. The Legends, The Bunny Army of DOOM~!, and Ward in a bunny costume; my god the horror. Will UWF be able to survive? There is only one way to find out; tune in and see for yourself.
