UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #165

Seizing the Moment

8 Jan 2003

Mike Montgomery — The Office of the UWF Executive Vice President of Talent Relations.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As always, we open the promo in the office of the Executive Vice President of Talent Relations, as well as de-facto leader of the Player's Club, Mike Montgomery. But of course, not everything is the same. The UWF itself is in flux. New owners means new rules and new opportunities. The UWF is a time of change, and those who recognize opportunity will rise to the top. Who better to control the game than the best of the Players? Mike sits at his desk, leaning back in his chair and staring up at the ceiling, 'It is good to be the Player's Club right now. And since the camera's rolling, this would be a good time to explain why.' Mike leans forward, steepling his fingers in front of him as he continues, 'You see, winning the game is all about opportunity. When I recruited Joshua Dunn into the Player's Club, it was because it was an opportunity to have one of hte best talents in this business fight by my side. When he agreed, it was because he recognized the opportunity to advance his career. When Dunn brought in the Common Man, Ezra Kazee, it was because he knew, just as I did, that there was nothing common about his talent. He seized the opportunity and brought another talent into the fold. 'Now though, is a chance to seize tremendous opportunities. On the last SAS of 2003, war was declared. The Player's Club vs. the Prime Alliance. Six Man tag team action. Our Intercontinental Champion has concerns over the fact that that match hasn't happened yet. To that I have but one thing to say. Good things come to those who wait. 'We can't forget that wars are fought on multiple fronts. Courtesy my position in the UWF, I have the ear of our new owners. Let's look at the upcoming card, shall we?' The scene shifts from Montgomery's office to a graphic of the card for Saturday Afternoon Supremacy: Six-Man Tag Team Match Joshua Dunn/Mike Montgomery/Ezra Kazee vs. Laguna and the Bunny Army Technically a handicap match, but what the hell? The three men in question have no real reason to be paired together, but let's see if they can coexist against the killer bunnies, shall we? 'Now let's think about that match, shall we?' Montgomery continues in a voice-over, 'three men who have 'no real reason to be paired together,' other than our all being in the same stable of course. Why don't we see what else is on the first card of 2004?' #1 Contender for the UWF World Heavyweight Title Winner gets a title shot on the next UWF Underground. Jamie Shock vs. Malcolm Kennedy In a contest with a lot on the line, no punches will be pulled. The purely evil Jamie Shock and the not PURELY but still evil Malcolm Kennedy will go to war, with a shot at the UWF World Title on the line. Can Shock get his shot to avenge his loss to Seymour? Or will Kennedy shock Shock and claim his first shot at the gold? 'Well, the Player's Club has a warm-up match against the Bunny Army of Doom, while two thirds of the Prime Alliance must fight each other for a world title match. And should the winner of this match go on to take the title, who must they face? The final third of that triumverate, Ruff Ryder. Sometimes things just work out, right? 'Now of course, our new owners weren't familiar with the inner workings of this glorious federation, and so had to consult an expert. Did they go to TPA member and Commissioner-in absentia Jamie Shock? No. When they needed someone to book SAS, they came to me. 'The war I have declared is fought on multiple fronts. In this case, in the office and in the ring. I have fired the first salvos by pitting the Prime Alliance against each other while giving my boys a sweet warmup match. 'And of course, I can't end this promo without a word for the mouthpiece of the Prime Alliance, Ruff Ryder. Boy, you and I both know that you had no business winning the Contest of Champions. You got lucky and you know it. Look around you, your group is crumbling. Let's be honest, Malcolm Kennedy will jump ship once being in the Prime Alliance no longer suits him, Jamie Shock is nowhere to be found, and the Player's Club is both stronger than ever and has tremendous influence among the administration. The UWF is ours while the Prime Alliance will soon be no more. Cut your losses and walk away. We both know you're better off as a lone wolf anyway. 'It's war, and you don't want to be caught in the crossfire. We have the power, and you don't even have a chance.' The scene fades out to the UWF's logo.
