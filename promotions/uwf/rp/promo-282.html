UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #282

After match party, RTP style

12/3/2004

Mystery Opponent — appartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[RTP! RTP! RTP! Yes the chanting crowd is correct Right To Party have returned to UWF Television, and back to their rather abysmal selves, losing their return match, getting powdered by the NVA, but who hasn’t? Well anyway, the big ladies man and the big girls blouse are set for another match up on SAS, against bigger fish, namely Plague, and Mike Montgomery, who can still fire them… or can’t… or… I’m not paid enough for this, ask somebody who cares] [The Scene Opens in the apartment block that we previously left RTP. But as the scene fades in from black we can already hear the party going on, several whistles, belches, and the loud noises that try to pass itself off as music called Dance Music. And as the darkness fades away more rapidly we can also see the intense strobe lighting. Readers sensitive to strobe lighting, please stop imagining the scene right now. Indeed, a party is going on, and rather than before with the innumerable piles of stuff, we can actually see the laminate wood flooring that covers the entire apartment, and the numerous people that are dancing on it. The source of the noise, and the lighting, is situated over in the corner, overseen by a rather dodgy looking DJ who doesn’t seem to be getting paid, rather he got a conditional invitation to the party on managing the decks for them] [Screaming, beer and substances a plenty, the camera goes in search of the members of RTP. Easier said than done, as the fool of a cameraman started the thing up in the middle of the large, drunken dancing crowd, several of them turn to the camera yelling and waving like madmen possessed, several with “cigarettes” in their mouths. Some of the women, most of the blonde ones at any rate give a flash of their credentials, which have had a savaging from the UWF Censor department, it’s surprising they haven’t got such control over a bondage promo, but then again, who wants to look at a blank screen with only the sound effects coming through? At any rate the women’s actions are met with large whoops from the male crowd and some of the women as well. Fighting through the half naked and attention seeking crowd, the camera man fights his way to the edge of the dance floor, with a congregation of people right behind. Panning hopefully across them again, he looks for either Bubba or Smokey, right now I don’t think our UWF Employee really cares that much] [Perhaps by fluke, or the fact that it is hard to miss a 6 ft 6 man in excess of 600 lbs even when he is sitting down, the camera finds Bubba, sitting over by the food table, currently chewing on a chicken wing, once again the camera man braves the tide of dancers and wades over towards Bubba, and I’m sure a couple people disappeared under the camera there… never mind, more importantly Bubba is now the main focus of the camera, possibly because he takes up so much space. Sitting in an equally large Armchair, Bubba’s dressed in black pants and an open Hawaiian shirt showing an awful lot of him, and as ever, his Fez sitting predominately on his crown. Chicken wing in one hand, and scantily clad partygoer in the other. Suddenly the camera’s focus changes, obviously a male operating the machine, as it falls on the Woman, who herself looks about to fall out. I’m not kidding either, I’ve never seen an article of clothing I could sum up with the word “pop”. Use your imaginations people, and if her skirt were much smaller, it would be bikini bottoms] [Realising, rather embarrassingly that he’s lost attention to his job, the camera turns back to Bubba, who is still chewing on a chicken wing, as if the camera wasn’t even there. After a short while, mid chew, out of the corner of his eye, Bubba sees the camera and turns to it, the woman just lying on him. Bubba gives the camera a grin that would make you think that you were the next meal if it weren’t for the bits of the last one that were still stuck between his teeth. Soon growing bored just grinning at the camera he turns his attention back to the bird, shoving picking up another wing and carrying on where he left off. The scantily clad woman, who very well could have been a nymph in a past life just gazes longingly into his eyes, which are concentrating on other things] Woman: Oh Bubba, You really are a wonderful person [Bubba just munches on the wing, spilling grease down him] Woman: I’ve never known a man to listen to everything I say with out at least being a bit bored Bubba: mmmmmmmph [Another section of wing disappears into Bubba. He leans back having eaten his fill, and tosses the end part of the wing away, hitting some poor sole standing not too far away from him. The woman puts her other arm around him and snuggles in close] Woman: Your so good at listening, it’s a wonder why your not taken already [Knowing he’s fighting a lost cause with Bubba, the cameraman moves off again, this time in search of Smokey. He heads over for the central area, where there seems to be some rather strange smoke signals, braving the sea of 15 second famer’s, he ploughs through them. Eventually getting to a collection of furniture holding several people who don’t look like they’ll be getting up in any short space of time. In the centre of a large sofa, with a woman on one side of him and a man with a communal cigarette on the other sits Smokey Wead laying back on the sofa, in the middle of an amusing story, although at this point I think that these people would laugh at anything, even a rerun of a Ruff Ryder Promo, oh wait, their amusing anyway] Smokey: And I said… “So I can fire you right?”… And HE said, “Yes” [This is met with laughter] Smokey: “SIR”! [Make that roaring laughter] Man: Oh man Smokey your too much, he he [A giggle is heard from the blonde on Smokey’s left, best summed up by the word “boing”, and Smokey looks over, and starts giggling like a little girl too] Cameraman: Err… Smokey? [No change Smokey still holds his stomach, laughing uncontrollably] Cameraman: Smokey? [Nothing] Cameraman: SMOKEY! [Smokey stops giggling and looks up at the camera and bursts out laughing again, along with all his associates who weren’t already laughing] [Smokey eventually calms down and looks up to the camera with two bloodshot eyes] Smokey: Yes? Cameraman: A few words on your match this Sunday? [Looking stupidly at the camera, mouth gaping open, Smokey replies] Smokey: Match? What match? I don’t have no match! Cameraman: Yes… You do! It’s against Mike Montgomery and Plague Smokey: Plague? [The bemused look Smokey gives the cameraman looks almost like something you’d see in a cartoon, if cartoons had an asphyxiation problem] Cameraman: Plague, you know Black Death Smokey: Plague isn’t the Black Death, I KNOW Black Death; in fact I think I owe him a considerable amount of money Cameraman: Owe him money? But you’re loaded Smokey: Weeeeeeeell, you see when you don’t pay things up front, there’s… how did he put it… interest Cameraman: So any words about the match now you know about it? [Smokey looks at the others around him who are almost all spaced out, another brunette now has the communal cigarette, and finds no help there, his gaze finally falls on the blonde, who, without no fault of her own giggles again, setting Smokey off on another gut reaching laughing fit] Cameraman: Smokey? [Nothing] Cameraman: SMOKEY! [Smokey stops giggling and looks up at the camera and bursts out laughing again, along with all his associates who weren’t already laughing] [Smokey eventually calms down and looks up to the camera with two bloodshot eyes] [Hmm, déjà vu] Smokey: Yes? Cameraman: What are your comments on the match? Smokey: What match? I don’t have no match! [Exasperated, and just aware that trying to get sense out of Smokey right now will be about as hard as persuading BBB to wear anything other than skimpy PVC, he gives up and decides to call it a day] Cameraman: Ok I’ve had enough of this [I just said that] [Scene fades to black, focusing on the still hysterical Smokey Wead]
