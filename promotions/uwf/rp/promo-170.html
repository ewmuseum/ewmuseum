UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #170

Nemesis

10th January (year unrecorded)

Jamie Shock — A flat somewhere in Boston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ever wondered what a true to life nemesis would look like? Look no further than this poor excuse of living, Jamie Shock. A nemesis is a source of harm and ruin. A nemesis is one who inflicts vengeance and retribution for it's own course. A nemesis is an opponent that cannot be beaten or overcome. A nemesis.. is Jamie Shock. When did we last see the nemesis? The only ever man to capture every single UWF title? The leader of the newest stable, The Prime Alliance? The answer is; when he 'lost' to Seymour Almasy. When Seymour Almasy was moments from being met in the eye with the curved rim of a crowbar, his women assistant got in the way. The outcome was a downed Aerith Heartilly and a distracted Jamie Shock. Seymour took it to himself to then take advantage of the situation, thus 'beating Jamie Shock'. Jamie Shock has been booked in the first card of 2004. The first card after the 2003's pay per view; Contest Of Champions. The card where The Prime Alliance's 'Prime', Ruff Ryder, knocked down opponents in a steamrolling essence of talent and went on to win the tournament. Now the one time UWF Champion will have another shot at the gold - against the winner of Jamie Shock's and Malcolm Kennedy's match. Yes, Shock is booked against Malcolm Kennedy, and yes, that is his own stable mate. The winner though gets the last shot at the World Championship before Ruff Ryder does a week afterwards. The UWF is against it's Prime. We open at the vission of something shiny and red. Something that is commonly used by the Grand Slam Champion himself. An item that currently was grey - until it connected with Seymour Almasy's wife's head. The crowbar. Flakey, dry, faded blood covers the bent metal. It represents the boiling rage of Jamie Shock's cold heart. Only Shock would be proud of striking a women down. Only Jamie would leave the blood on the crowbar as a symbol of his own messed up existance. The scene moves out from the crowbar, revealing that it is placed on a newspaper - a newspaper that is starting to slowly fade, indicating that is has been left in the sun for too long. We move out even more, which reveals that the newspaper is in a glass frame, with the crowbar on top of it, hanging on the wall directly in the sunlight over the Boston sky. Seymour Almasy's name can be seen in bold, but the crowbar seems to cover the rest of the headline. Out a tad more - revealing Jamie Shock, standing infront of the framed possession of his, smiling. His grey hair that would rather kill itself than exist over the man's shoulders lifts in the wind from the open window. One would almost expect this 'creature' to evaporate in the orange sunlight which helps to cast his shadow against the wall. His head is bent, cookedly looking at the camera. His eyes shake, although they arnt. He would be a welcomed guest of the ever lasting fire of hell. 'What would have seemed like a loss, was infact a win. The fact that I was 'pinned' by Seymour Almasy doesnt mean the slightest thing. He snook past me. The win was nothing but a demonstration of why women are wastes of space, and also the cause of everything f***ed up in this god forsaken rot of a planet!' 'With that said though, let me just direct a few words towards Mr Almasy. I enjoyed knocking your pathetic nerdy wife out. I enjoyed spilling her blood. I enjoyed knowing that I cause a huge amount of pain to the thing that means the most to you. I enjoyed it. I dont mind that Hell will welcome me with open arms, for I'm enjoying this life while I'm still here - and that was the highlight of my year. Any title win couldnt come close to the pleasure that I for one experienced when you held your wife in your arms, not knowing if she was going to come through or not, as I held my bloodied crowbar, rubbing my hand up and down which, laughing in the face of your over-rated life. Seymour Almasy, you're overwhelmed. I'm going to deliver the final pin into the balloon, and send you packing. I'm ridding this federation of jokes, and you're next on the list' 'The fact that the PRIME ALLIANCE is awaiting you. The fact that you have a Nemisis, The 'Prime' in Alliance, and the fastest rising star in the history of the UWF Malcolm Kennedy coming for you should only make you realise that the days with your title are numbered. Here they be monsters.' 'Universal talking now, not just to Seymour. Although do listen.' 'I dont mind that Mike Montgomery, the most over-rated thing ever to set foot into the UWF, or any wrestling federation even, has devised a way that would only be seen as a 'loss' situation for the Alliance that is Prime. In booking myself against Malcolm Kennedy will just make us stronger. The Player's Club has a bunch of jock. It's built on the foundation of a union of athletes trying to cheat their way into the upper card. It's built, on losers.' 'The Prime Alliance will be built on two things. Two things that will set it in a bound of concrete. HATE and RESPECT. Our skills are phenomenal. I'm proved that in my record. Ruff Ryder went through three men in one night, he more than proved that, and Malcolm Kennedy is soon to prove that - and will start when he gives me everything he's able to cook up. I dont mind wrestling Malcolm. It would be a great lesson for the rich git..' He pauses while a cold smile makes a few seconds worth of appearance. 'You learn from the immortals of this buisness. I learnt from Lew Spectre. I studied his techniques, I read his book so to speak. Then when I finished the last chapter, I began my road to success. You dont get anywhere looking at blurbs. If you look at Gurgles and see he's a wierd clown, you're nothing but ignorant. If you look into him you see much more, like a sick freak, a man on the edge, and depression that can only be seen with his demented appearance.' 'This union of prime was set up to rid the UWF of it's killers. The Bunny Army Of Doom were fried. The fact that people were only watching MY federation because of the antics of a hundred or so f***in' Bunny rabbits made my skin dry. Me and Ruff Ryder soon put an end to that. Next is Almasy.' 'If another friend of Seymour's gets in the way, and he somehow manages to crawl over a pro such as myself or Malcolm Kennedy - then he still has Ruff Ryder to go through. He still has a diamond to break. Then he's in trouble. Then he is lost. In a matter of weeks Seymour Almasy will be gone. In a matter of weeks a member of the Prime Alliance will be the UWF Champion.' 'The fact is simple. Until a fire erupts in the foundations, nothing can reach us. The defence is phenomenal. All the bookings upon which members of the stable fight couldnt put an end to the Prime. We're the untouchables, we're the furnace which helps to keep the UWF going.' 'We are the Prime Alliance. We will triumph!' The camera slowly zooms back onto the framed crowbar and newspaper. Jamie Shock slowly backs off as the scene fades to black. The first card of 2004 draws ever closer. It's a sellout.
