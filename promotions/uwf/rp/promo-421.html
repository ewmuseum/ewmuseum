UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #421

Welcome Home...CPL STYLE

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Page 1 (There is nothing like the smell of freshly grilled hamburgers on a Sunday morning. Your tonsils can almost taste the scent as it fills your nostrils. But this isn't about a freaking Sunday grill in the park, so quiet down with all that. This is a promo unlike any you have seen this week. Because not only are you about to witness a masterpiece, BUT you are going to have the pleasure of listening to one of the greatest men who ever walked into the squared circle. Don't even think about getting up to grab so much as a glass of ice cold tap water. Because in a few moments you are going to enter the world of the one... the only... master of the Lethal Injection... Corporal Punishment!) (So, put your children to bed, or wherever you put your adolescent hoodlums, and lay back in your recliner because the land of LOCKDOWN has officially opened.) And do not forget... Don't take what I say personal... JUST TAKE IT! CORPORAL STYLE! (Everything appears normal today. The birds are chirping, the dogs are barking, and the vehicles are driving by at their normal pace. People are generally happy and positive in nature. By the look of their appearance, it would appear that today is going to be a somewhat positive afternoon. But that is all abut to change.) (The camera pans around this beautiful suburb with it's children playing in the streets, and then closes in on a nearby sidewalk. And as the camera pans within a few feet of it, a giant Firestone tire suddenly stops directly in front of the cameras lens. The camera then focuses on a pair of size thirteen Enforcer boots that drops from above. the camera follows these boots as they walk from left to right along the dusty sidewalk. Stepping over various debris, the boots come to a stop and a familiar voice can be heard.) Corporal: And just when the neighbors thought it was safe to let their children out to play... (The camera pans back to show the infamous Corporal Punishment standing in front of a house with a FOR SALE sign perched in its front yard. As the Corporal begins to walk towards the FOR SALE sign, a slender well dressed man quickly steps out from within the house. He adjusted his tie and trots down to the place where the Corporal stands. ) Real Estate Agent: Why hello sir. You must be Mr. C. Punishment. What a pleasant surprise it is to meet you sir. I have the house completely ready for you to look at. I have talked to the current owners and they are quite happy with your offer to purchase their home. What was quite surprising was the fact that you said you would purchase this wonderful home for a handsome amount of currency. I am still a little drawn back from the offer, but HEY who am I to judge a man who has the ability to purchase a home for that kind of cash.... You did say you would be paying CASH right? Corporal: Yes. I believe that was my offer. I would pay you approximately Two Hundred Fifteen Thousand dollars for a vintage home that predates its construction to at least 1945. I do not care how much the home appraises for. I just want to make sure that the house is vintage 1930's. I will pay cash up front, and I will not question the value of the home AS long as it meets the year requirement that I have requested. Real Estate Agent: Yes, yes, yes. Although I will tell you that this home could have been purchased for a mere Thirty-Eight Thousand dollars, I am proud to have been the lucky man to sale this home to you Mr. Corporal. (The real estate agent has a look of sheer excitement at the thought of making such a high commission for a house purchase.) Corporal: Please... Just call me Corporal. Real Estate Agent: Why yes... of course Mr. err... uh... I mean Corporal Punishment. (The real estate agent appears to be somewhat perplexed as he slowly repeats the name Corporal Punishment over and over. His ace shows concern as he adjusts his tie and looks up at the Corporal with a look of urgency.) Real Estate Agent: What a unique name you have sir. Corporal Punishment. It sounds almost sadistic, if you will. I... uh... am not meaning any disrespect, but why is your name Corporal Punishment? Corporal: Never fear my little pencil necked paper pusher. I am what I am. And that is about all you really have to know. My friends call me Corporal Punishment, and my enemies call me Corporal Punishment. So that means that you can call me Corporal Punishment. Just leave it at that. Real Estate Agent: Excellent idea sir! And with that, shall we go inside to sign the paperwork to make this official? Corporal: That sounds fine. Let us go inside and finish this. (Both men begin to walk towards the front door of the house for purchase when the Corporal suddenly stops in his tracks. He places his index finger to his chin and begins to ponder something.) Corporal: Wait a minute Pencil Neck. There is one thing we need to do before this sale can go on. Real Estate Agent: What is that sir? (With a very concerned look on his face.) Corporal: How do I know that this home predates the specifications that I am requesting? Real Estate Agent: Sir I assure you ... Corporal Punishment: I mean this home may look pre 1950. It may smell pre 1950. And it may even taste to be pre 1950. But the real test is if it HOLDS up to be a pre 1950 structured home. Real Estate Agent: HOLD sir? I do not follow you. Corporal: Oh you will Pencil Neck. You truly will. Real Estate Agent: Excuse me? Corporal Punishment: No excuse me! (The Corporal suddenly grabs the real estate agent by the throat and lifts him over his head. The Corporal then looks at the camera and gazes into it.) Corporal: For all you freaks of nature out there in TV land who do not know what the difference is between a home made AFTER 1945 and one made BEFORE 1945, it is this. The WALLS ARE NOT MADE OF DRY WALL. They are made of PLASTER, MUD, and FURRING STRIPS. So there is only one way to check to see if this house is made before 1945. (The Corporal winks at the camera and looks up at the dangling real estate agent.) Corporal: So, guess who is going to truly check for me Pencil? Real Estate Agent: WHO? OH NO! PUT ME DOWN SIR!! I DO NOT NEED THE MONEY! I DON'T NEED THIS EITHER! SOMEBODY HELP! HE IS A MAD MAN! NOOOOOO! DEAR GOD IN HEAVEN DON'T YOU DO THIS! PLEASE! SOMEONE HELP! Corporal: You are about to find out WHY they call me Corporal Punishment. Hehehehehehe! (The Corporal turns towards the house while laughing  and with one giant thrust, the Corporal throws the Agent head first into the side of the home. The house buckles temporarily and then the agent crashes to the ground motionless. The house remains completely in tact.) (The Corporal meanwhile shakes the dust off of his palms and shakes his head in the affirmative.) Corporal: YEP! It's pre 1945 alright! Corporal: I'll take it! (The Corporal walks by the lifeless man lying by the house and kicks him in the torso.) Corporal: Don't mind me. You just lay back and enjoy the moment. I'll go in there and sign the papers for you. No need to get up on my account. After all, you look so comfortable lying in my yard like that.. Corporal: Well I need to get some private time anyway. I have a big day ahead of me. I start a new job this weekend little pencil boy. A new career in a new town. Ahhhh the fresh air! SO DON'T TAKE THIS PERSONAL... JUST TAKE IT... C-O-R-P-O-R-A-L STYLE!
