Tied Binds

26 Mar 2004

Iron Lung — Villa Roma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tied Binds Starring Iron Lung The past two weeks for Iron Lung have bascially been hell. Not only had he lost The Hardcore Championship to The Plague, but now a week later, he was considered as a wrestler with the same type of explosive calibur as Malcolm Kennedy, as the match resulted in a draw. Two weeks, two disasterful outcomes for the former champion, how would it come into play this time on Supremacy when he basically had to start at the bottom rung of the ladder once more? He had to prove himself worthy to wear the golden belt around his waist once more, and now he had to go through one Nick Persia, a name he wasn't familiar with yet around these parts. Hell, who was he kidding, he wasn't familiar with anyone. Lung was a loner, and a desperate for attention loner now ever since losing to The Plague. He begged for it inside his solitude confinement of a locker room, and muttered for it through his vivid explotions of interviews when they were called upon. Now, his life in a new company was once again damned. A damaged record, and a lost chance to become a fighting champion in his first defense. What kind of fighter did he consider himself? What exactly was going through the mind of this ticking timebomb? We would soon find out, for we were about to embark on a journey to get this young superstars comments. And if we didn't come out, we would hope that UWF wouldn't bother to send a search party... We didn't really know what to expect from IL as we stood before his cherry oak door, so hesitant to pound upon it with a closed fist. What would his mood be like? Certainly, it wouldn't be all too happy go lucky, considering the circumstances of the past weeks. He possibly, scratch that, he more than likely considered this as another version of a curse put upon him. A curse that had plagued his skin since he entered his first company, getting fired after mouthing off about the value of its competition, just after his debut match. He wasn't one to believe in curses, but he did believe that the owner of that promotion made some sort of fire that burned deep into his veins, perhaps the fire that made his eyes glow with such an intensity. And now, it was filled with such a heat, that it intimidated even the strongest and most confident men. Was it possible that Nick Persia was trembling at this moment? Perhaps, but we hadn't even entered the room of this superstar yet... * POUND POUND POUND * (As a muttering from inside the room) Who the hell is it! 'Dashing' Dane Davis... Good for you, what do you want! Just your comments on your recent tie with Malcolm Kennedy, and of course your comments on your upcoming match with Nick Persia... Why do you care what my comments are? You're just gonna make me look like a jackass. No sir, that would be at your own leisure. Listen kid, I don't have for your bull okay, I'm trying to get ready to go to the gym to train. I suppose that you are training for your match with Nick Persia? The room grows silent on the inside, so silent that 'Dashing' Dane Davis begins to sweat just below his hairline. It is a cold sweat, and becomes ice as we hear a jiggling now. The door was beginning to open for us, and we quickly pin up against the wall, breathing hard. Did we say something wrong? Oh Rod, we hoped he didn't kill us right then and there! Dropping down on our knees, we grovel and begin to sob as Iron Lung swings the doors open, his eyes telling the story, as they are as bloodshot as they can be. With black and blue underneath his eyes from lack of sleep, he growls at us as we shiver on the ground. He then sneers our way and spits on the ground near us. Get the hell up and get whats coming to you, you son of a bitch! (Getting up and wincing) P--puh---please don't hurt me.. (While grabbing Dane by the throat and slowly thrusting him up against the wall) Now you listen to me you punk. I'm training to get some s*** off of my mind, do you have a problem with that? Because if you do, than I'm afraid that I have a problem.. WITH YOU! (With a gulp) No, I don't have a problem with that. It's just my job -- (While slowly letting his grasp loosen and putting Dane down) You're right, I shouldn't be giving you such a hard time.. I guess that I can answer your questions, quickly before I head out onto the road.. I apologize. (Catching his breath) Apology accepted... So yah... I guess I have a match against Nick Persia this Supremacy. But, it won't be him that I'm facing in my mind, for you see I have grown used to picturing the Plague with his dirty and grungy hands around my Hardcore Championship. I've grown used to seeing the Plague in everybody I encounter, including you.. (With another gulp) I see But I guess I have to snap out of it, and come to earth. I have to realize that I don't have the championship around my waist any more, and it's making me feel like an utter mess. I feel like everyone is making fun of me, I feel like everything I'm doing is the wrong thing. And I also feel like I'll never be able to recapture my belt the way I'm going.. (Trying to console I.L.) Well, if you ask me, this match would be a great way to start your path to being the champion once more. If you win, you'll show the Plague that you are worthy enough to face him once more. And a tie isn't exactly a loss, but it isn't exactly a win either. Overall, you still have a decent record in my opinion.. I guess you are right, I mean I still have the fans on my side, win or lose. The best that I can do is what I always do, just go in there and fight this guy, try my best to come up on top. And most of the time, I do come out on top don't I? Ya' damn right... (Putting his hand on Dane's shoulder) You know something? You aren't so bad Dane. I tell you what, why don't you come with me to the gym, and I can show you some of my routine? (Smiling wide) Really, that would be totally sweet! All right then, that's the spirit. Let's hit the road! With that being said, Iron Lung shuffles Dane along the hallway after closing the door to his room behind him. This was indeed an experience that Dane wouldn't soon forget, but would that trend continue to flow at Supremacy? Would Nick Persia have an experience with the ex-champion that he wouldn't soon forget? We would soon find out on Sunday Afternoon!
