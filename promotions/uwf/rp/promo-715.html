UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #715

There Must Be Only Uno!

23 Sep 2004

White Tiger — Masked Monkeys Security, Inc... North American Headquarters

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Behind a door marked 'Masked Monkey Security,' we find... the Masked Monkeys. What did you expect, hula dancers? The Trailer Park Luchador is engrossed with his Gameboy. El Chupacabra appears to be sewing. White Tiger and Pajaro de Presa are deep in conversation. All are still wearing paer bags instead of lucha masks. White Tiger: Madre dios! We're tag team champs and we still don't have masks! Pajaro; But Chupi is working on that. It's not like we can just go to Hot Topic and buy them. That reminds me, can we got to the mall and stare at eleven year old goth girls? White Tiger: No! You still have that restraining order. Pajaro: But they won't recognize me, I'm in a paper bag. Like how the cops don't know bums have booze in their paper bags! White Tiger: I should've stayed in Mexico... Chupi: Ow! Pajaro and White Tiger look over and see El Chupacabra sucking on his finger. White Tiger: Careful, Chupi. It's not like Hayes gives us medical. Pajaro: Or paychecks. White Tiger: You don't get a paycheck? Pajaro: Nope, he just gives me Camel Cash from packs of cigarettes. Just then, a young woman, the same one Pajaro groped last week, enters the office. Woman: Uh, this is the women's rest room. Pajaro: And it's the headquarters of Masked Monkeys Security! Woman: Why not use the men's room? White Tiger: Guys started thinking we were boy whores. At first we let Chupi accomodate them since we didn't want to be rude. Pjaro: And we needed the money White Tiger: Right. But then Chupi got lockjaw. Everyone looks over at El Chupacabra, who nods and rubs his jaw under his paper bag. Woman: Whatever. I gotta do what I gotta do. Pajaro: That's what Chupi would say. Need help? Woman: Freak. The woman goes into a stall. White Tiger turns to Pajaro de Presa. White Tiger: All right, Pajaro. One of us has been challenged to a match against the Green Demon. Which of us should it be? Pajaro: You won us the tag title, so it should be you. White Tiger: But you've been more successful as a singles competitor. Pajaro: Ah, si. Telemundo champion! White Tiger: Television champion. But you get the idea. Pajaro: You ain't done too shabby. You've gone toe-to-toe with World champs. And you beat Sacrifice without being... Sacrificed~! White Tiger: But you're the Bird of Prey. Pajaro: And you're... the Tiger that is White. Seeing the conflict, the Trailer Park Luchador puts down his Gameboy. He reaches down and gets something out of his gym bag, then walk over to the tag champs. TPL: Guy, there's only one way to solve this. And I got the answer right here. The Masked Monkeys look at what the TPL had. its a box marked 'Hungry Hungry Hippos.' TPL: The one with the most marbles faces the Green Demon. White Tiger: I knew there was a reason we kept him around. Pajaro: That and he's one of the only Monkeys with his photo on the UWF website. Not like me. An unseen audience lets out an 'awwwwwww...' for Pajaro de Presa. The Masked Monkeys pick their hippos. Pajaro: I get pink! White Tiger: Um, OK... TPL: Ready, set, GO! A high-energy game of Hungry, Hungry Hippos ensues. The pace is fast and furious. Soon, the game is over. Pajaro: Hey, I an't got any marbles! An off-screen drum goes 'bah-dum-bum.' White Tiger: Well, it looks like I'll be representing the masked Monkeys against the Green Demon. This is quite an honor for me. I shall not only be fighting for myself, but my family, mi amigos, mi raza... and what the hell is that smell? A toilet flushes and the woman steps out of the stall. Cupi enhales deeply. His eyes roll up and he passes out. Pajaro: Ay caramba! How could such a pretty girl make such a nasty stink? Woman: That's none of your business, freak. She storms out of the bathroom, errrr... office. Pajaro: She didn't wash her hands! Let's get her! She's a biological weapon! The Masked Monkeys give chase and ah, who the hell cares?
