UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #402

The Curtain Drops

11 May 2004

The Communist — Backstage SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:the Communist sits at his locker, watching the wrestlers, making note of their styles, for someday he, too, would be fighting among them: Interviewer: Communist? :the Communist, disturbed from his reverie, looks up: Commie: Who are you? Interviewer: I'm here for you interview Commie: AHH yes. What is your first question, comrad? :the interviewer straightend himself and looks down at his cards: Interviewer: First of all, it seems you've started a stable. Will you be Competing in Stable Wars? Commie: Of course! What sort of silly question is that? The Iron Curtain will fall on the Bourguasis and crush them beneath its weight. We will take the cup and Teach the greatness of Communism to the masses. Interviewer: What do you have to say to people calling you the weak link in the Iron Curtain? Commie: UNTRUE! You need only look at the record. My record is Flawless. I am undefeated in this federation. All who cross the Communist end the same. Writhing in pain. Demolished, Anihalated by the Communist Slam. I am not the weak link I am the CORNERSTONE of the Curtain. I am teh strong arm and the Champion. I am the Communist and all the enemies of the Iron Curtain will fall before me. Interviewer: and what of your match against Robb Daniels? :the Communist laughs deep and loud: Commie: Robb Daniels? Much like all in this federation, He will fall to the Communist. He is no different from Loverboy or Gregory. I will walk into the ring, Destroy poor Robb Daniels and leave his broken body in the ring. I may even allow him the honor of covering his corpse in the colors of the CCCP. Interviewer: the what? :the Communist stands to his full 7' and picks the Interviewer up: Commie: The Central Committie of the Communist Party! DO you know NOTHING! :shakes the interviewer Violently: The People who nearly destroyed your pitiful democracy! A nation FEARED thoughout the WORLD! When those words pass you lips, they WILL be said with nothing but RESPECT! RESPECT for the People, RESPECT for our great revolution and RESPECT for THE COMMUNIST! :The Communist drops the interviewer unceremoniously and leaves:
