UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #496

*Sluuuurrp*

July 4th (year unrecorded)

Kevin Starr — Boston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Starr did it! He finally did it! He achieved the impossible… or well, possible in this case as The Players Club was one of the favorites to win the tournament. Kevin Starr did what he should have done last year, win the Stable Wars Championship. It was not a cake walk; even our hero would admit that. First, he had to go through The Chaos Society, a group of extremely talented wresters. Then, afterwards, he went through one of the toughest matches of his life. He had to go through two of the most highly gifted stables the UWF has ever seen. Chrono Cross, or Richard Ray Vaginal’s stable of complete idiocy or something like that. With wrestlers like Seymour Almasy, 2 time World Champion, Kenny Mercury, LOW Foam World Champion, Cpl. Punishment, an overall legend in this company, and the man himself Richard Ray Vodka, once Lew Spectre’s manager. The other stable in the finals, the other favorite to win the tournament, The Resurrection. Hell bent on bringing back the UWF to what was once the ‘glory’ days of the UWF they like to call it… hmph, bunch of snobs is what I call them. But regardless of what they are, they still had a very talented roster. Bondage the Sex Fiend, though he was questioning his whole freaking reason for existence or something in his last promotion, still a very talented wrester. Malcolm Kennedy, the future of the UWF. He should be around for a long time to come. I can see only good thinks in his future. Then there’s Moraless, one of the most talented wresters in our industry. With a little more time and effort he could become the next World Champ. Finally we have Digital Mortality, the man, the myth, the hall of famer~! Really, he’s won countless titles including the World, but all of those accomplishments couldn’t help him in the Stable Wars competition, as just like they said they would, The Player’s Club came out as Stable Wars Champs! *Sluuurrrrpp* *Sluuuurrrpp* *Sluuuuurrpp* [What the hell is that noise? It’s annoying! It’s been going on inside Kevin’s apartment for the past 3 FREAKING HOURS!] [Ugh…] *Sluuuurrpp* *Sluuurrpp* [Okay seriously, what the hell is that? Time to investigate.] *Sluuurrpp* [Okay, it’s coming from the living room.] [We reach the living room to find multicolored balloons and party hats everywhere. There’s bottles of non-alcoholic beer laid out everywhere on the floor because Kevin Starr likes to take care of himself. The camera pans up to see a huge banner that reads “Stable Wars Champs!” hung from wall to wall. The camera pans around some more to find our hero and Mike Michael.] [Kevin Starr doesn’t even have a scratch on him after Stable Wars 2004, so his cunning facial features remain intact and… well, just plain perfect for that matter. Kevin Starr’s hair is neatly gelled up in to it’s beautiful blonde spikeyness… yes, blonde spikeyness. I’m not even sure if spikeyness is a work, but if it is, it would be how Kevin Starr’s hair looks right now.] *Sluuurrp* [What the hell? Oh…. Now I see what that is.] [The camera pans down to reveal the Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee that was given to The Player’s Club for winning Stable Wars. He must have stole it some Dunn while he was braiding his beautiful long hair...*snicker snicker*.] . [ Kevin’s mouth is almost glued to the straw as he slurps up the Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee rapid fire.] *Sluuurrp* [Mike is obviously becoming annoyed with the sound after listening to it for the past 24 hours. Mike lashes out after remaining quiet about the entire time.] Mike: “What the hell are you ever going to be finished with that thing?!” *Sluurrp* Kevin: “Hey! Look at the size of this thing, it’s huge! Mike: “So, what’s your point? It wouldn’t take me that long to finish sucking all of the juice inside.” Kevin: “Bullshit Mike! Let’s be honest Mike, you couldn’t even handle one that was as big as mine!” Mike: “Oh yeah? Is that so? Back in High School I was known for sucking ones that long. In fact, I sucked ones that were bigger, much bigger. I’ve even sucked down a few of the black colored kind, those were my favorite.” […What the hell…?] Kevin: “Those black ones don’t mean anything! Even though they’re bigger, it doesn’t mean anything. It’s not the size that counts. These smaller ones just taste better and are much stronger and stiffer… Yum… […This is disgusting…] Mike: “Oh well who cares… how did you get that thing anyway? I thought they gave it to Dunn.” Kevin: “Yeah well, I was in the locker room afterwards when I saw it. I knew Dunn was half dead in a hospital, so I decided to take it. I can’t believe that after all this time it still hasn’t lost it’s flavor.” [Kevin goes back to slurping the Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee as Mike begins to wonder what title Kevin is going to go for.] Mike: “I’m beginning to wonder what title you’re going for now that you’ve won Stable Wars.” [See, I said that exactly. Narrators just have this psychic ability to know what people are thinking.] [Kevin drops the Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee to the coffee table in front of him.] Kevin: “Isn’t it obvious? With my strength, skill, and looks? It’s all World for me man.” [Kevin smiles in only the perfect way he can with his polished white teeth shining in to the camera with his blue eyes filled with joy.] Mike: “The World Title? Do you think you can do it?” Kevin: “Come on Mike, what the hell. Remember what I said about being a negative nancy last week? Well you’re doing it again, so stop! Now what do we say?” [Mike sighs in the corner of Kevin Starr’s black leather couch.] Mike: “I’m sorry.” [Kevin replies in a very understanding way. Almost like a mother comforting her child after he fell and scraped his knee.] Kevin: “It’s okay Mike, I forgive you… KIDDING! Ha! You fool; I’d never forgive a repulsive piece of smelly cow manure like you! Bahaha!” [After being insulted and shedding a few tears, Mike whips out a blue rectangular card from his pocket. The label of the blue card you ask? Why it’s none other then Wednesday Night Universal-or Underground, whatever WNU is calling itself these days.] Mike: “By the way Kev, you’ve got a match on WNU.” Kevin: “What! Me, WNU! Impossible.” Mike: “No, it’s possible. Apparently they’ve changed WNU around. It’s no longer a show for random jobbers, it’s like SAS but with less matches.” [Kevin begins to nod his head up and down, starting to understand the reason they put him on the show.] Kevin: “Well there’s only one reason for this inconvenience. They needed ratings for WNU so badly, that they decided to throw names like Kevin and Starr on the card to increase those ratings. It’s unbelievable how low some people sink these days just to gain a few extra dollars. Well who is my opponent?” [Mike looks down at the card, trying to decode his opponents’ names with his 3rd grade reading level. Finally, after several minutes.] Mike: “Some guy named Mr. Meaner and a late edition in Malcolm Kennedy.” Kevin: “Mr. Meaner… who?” Mike: “He's new in the UWF. He’s got the frame of Ezra Kazee, he even has a similar appearance, but apparently he’s really, really mean.” Kevin: “Oh well is he now. This guy’s been in this fed for what, 3 days now and he thinks he can just get in to the Main Event with Kevin Starr? No, no, no, no, NO! It’s not that easy to get in to the same ring as yours truly. In fact it takes years just to even stand in the same arena as me. I don’t know who this guy thinks he is, but he is a nobody! And I’m just going to have to show him a thing or two this Wednesday.” Mike: “Well actually Kevin he was part of the old GIWF.” Kevin: “The what now? The Gay Idiots Wrestling Federation? Yeah, I can see why. Just one look at that guy and you know he’s been around, if you know what I mean.” Mike: “I thought you never seen this guy before?” Kevin: “Well with the detailed description that you gave me of him, I’ve got a clear image of what this guy looks like in my head now.” [Kevin reaches down and grabs the Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee, takes another slurp, then drops it back down on to the table.] Kevin: “Hey Malcolm. Yeah, Mac, it’s still okay if I call you Mac, right? How’s it feel to be a loser? No seriously, how’s it feel to lose in the finals of Stable Wars to The Greatest UWF SuperStarr of All-Time? How’s it feel to crumble away at my feet? Anyway Malcolm you talentless scum, this Wednesday is going to feel just like last Wednesday when you lost to myself in the finals. The only difference is that this time it’s going to feel much worse as I SuperStarr Slam you to the mat, breaking every bone in that disgusting thing you call a face! Seriously Mac, let’s trim the eyebrows a little, looks like you have two giant caterpillars sprawled across your forehead. Fact is Mac, you’ll never be as good as me… or as pretty as me for that matter.” [Kevin reaches down and grabs the Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee once again and goes back to work on it.] *Sluuurrp* *Sluuuurrpp* *Sluuuurrpp* *Sluuurrp* [camera fades]
