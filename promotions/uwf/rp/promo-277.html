UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #277

It's all coming together

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins outside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. There is a party going on and people are dancing, drinking, and having a good time as Ryder seems to be the happiest man alive. He is toasting everyone and laughing it up. He has on a black pair of wind pants along with his red and black 'Prime Alliance' t-shirt. A car is seen driving off down the road as Ryder holds his glass high in the air saluting the exiting guest ) Ryder: Good night Kennedy and welcome home! * Ryder calms down and walks over to a girl who is sitting at a table by herself * Ryder: Why are you out here all alone? This is a very special day for the Prime Alliance. Our boy is home, and now we can wreak havoc on the entire world just like we used to. Kennedy said if we win the all comers match he's in and I know the man doesn't back down from his word. We have the One Man Army in the Prime Alliance now and soon others will come. With Malcom Kennedy walking the aisle with me I know there is no stopping me now. * The woman just gives Ryder a funny look before lunging forward and puking over the side of his deck * Ryder: Girl, call me crazy, but I think you're drunk. * Ryder laughs to himself as he climbs on top of the table and calls the attention of his guests * Ryder: Listen up people I got something to say. * Everyone stops what they are doing to listen * Ryder: I just wanna say that The Prime Alliance, the greatest stable ever created is back and we're gonna dominate the UWF just like the old days. * The guests yell and clap * Ryder: We are gonna run through the entire roster and pick em apart piece by motherfuckin piece and I dare one of those Player's Club pussies to step in my way cause they won't last two seconds with us. It all starts though with a sorry ass punk that got lucky the last time he was in the ring with the True Indian Superstar. Beezlebozo, you no talent hack, you and I both know that you shouldn't have walked out of that match with the title because I had your ass beat on more than one occasion, but somehow you managed to slither your way out of the match. You won the battle, but that war is far from over. I will do whatever it takes to make sure that you don't make it to your precious little title match with Joshua Dunn, because you won't be out of the body cast by the time that match rolls around. You will be nothing, but one big damn bruise when I get through with you. * The crowd raises their glasses to Ryder * Ryder: Damn right, and everyone at this party knows that I am the man to get the job done right? Crowd: RIGHT! Ryder: That's right, and if you wanna test me Bozo, you bring your white paint, your red nose, and those fuckin goofy ass big shoes and i'll whip your ass right in front of the entire world. I beat you once before and you tried to play it off by suggesting that it was some dumbass mime that looks nothing like you by the way, but this time you aren't gonna be able to talk your way out of the beating that i've got in store for you. I'm gonna beat you from pillar to post and then when you think you can't take anymore pain...you're goin for the ride. I'll drive you through the ring courtesy of Stompin With The Big Dawgs! That's all I got to say about that sorry ass bastard so you people drink up and i'm gonna pass out now...GOODNIGHT! * Ryder starts to leave, but stops and grabs a girl by the arm and they both walk inside his house locking the door behind them as the party rages on and the scene fades to black *
