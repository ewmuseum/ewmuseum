UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #204

Underestimated

2 Feb 2004

Raid — Turner Field

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with a view of the backstage of Turner Field. You see a couple of guys bringing in stuff for the upcoming event. They are carrying tables, ring equipment, and some props for backstage. The camera starts to pan around the backstage area and you see more of the same. The camera eventually stops and you see a man walking towards the camera. As he comes closer you can tell that the man is Raid. The camera zooms in on his face and upper shoulders.) (Raid:) Well it looks like things are being prepared for the show. Speaking of being prepared I wonder if my opponents are prepared to get a beating like they will never forget. I just want to let you three know that I can't wait to get my hands on them. Ohh and the things I'll do when I do get my hands on them! You see its like this I have been underestimated my entire life and it sickens my that I still am. Yes I am speaking to you Moraless. You said that the only thing you feared in our upcoming match is the man they call Iron Lung. Well I've got news for you, HE is the least of your worries. When I,RAID, am standing alone in the ring victorious then and only then you will realize that you shouldn't have underestimated me. But I have no regrets in being underestimated because then you won't see what hit you when I do strike down upon you. In fact it is better that you don't fear me because then it will make my job of eating you that much easier. ( The camera begins to pan backwards into a full view of Raid. You then hear a man yell 'Watch out! Coming through!'. You see a ring crew member walk in between the camera and Raid. A furious look comes over Raids face.) (Raid): Did you just see that? (The camera moves up and down as the camera man knobs his head.) (Raid): Doesn’t he know who I am?!?! I'll Show him! (Raid is obviously very pissed off as he goes storming after the ring crew member. The camera man quickly follows behind Raid. As Raid catches the man and he grabs the crew member by the shoulder and spins him around so that he is facing him.) (Raid): What’s the big IDEA asshole? What are you blind or something? Can't you see I'm trying to do something here?!?! (Crew member): Hey buddy! Look here I'm just doing my job so don't give me a hard time! Are you even suppose to be here? (As he says thin he pokes Raid in the shoulder. Each time he puts more force behind the poke.) (Raid): Yea I am, and if you don't like it well that’s just to damn bad. And if you continue to poke me you will be EXTERMINATED!! (Crew member): Yea whatever. ( And with that he gives Raid one good shove that pushes him back a foot or so. Raid shakes his head and says,” I warned ya'. Raid then proceeds to lay into the guy with a hard right hand to the face. The guy stumbles backwards and tries to get a shot in on Raid but he gets another right, and this time it lands him onto the floor. Raid then picks the guy up and gives him a few good shots to the gut. Then he throws the guy into the wall and he falls into a pile onto the floor. While on the floor Raid gives him a few kicks and the guy has obviously had enough and quits fighting back) (Raid): There you happy now? Look what you made me do! Oh well. Anyways where was I. Oh yea, so just remember you three WILL fall victim to me just like this worthless piece of crap. Luckily for this guy I'm in a good mood today. As for you guys I doubt I will be as easy on three three of you! So just remember who and what you are dealing with! (The camera begins to zoom out on Raid and then quickly turns and zooms in on the man lying limp next to the wall, and then fades to black.)
