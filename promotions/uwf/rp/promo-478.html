Stable Wars Rundown part 2

28 Jun 2004

Adien Pryde — Raid's Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As seen last time.] Pryde : Joanna, I am disappointed in these guys you have getting this information. This is all you have on the mystery team. I guess this is what we have to work on. [Pryde scrolls down some more and stops and grins.] Pryde : Now here is who I have the most interest in facing in the final three. The Iron Curtain……. [Pryde is sitting at a computer in a black leather chair in Raids office continuing to go over the list of stables competing in Stable Wars. As seen last time, Pryde had already gone over the stables of The Resurrection, Chrono Cross, The Players Club and the mystery stable and had just gotten to The Iron Curtain.] Pryde : The Iron Curtain, the stable I am most familiar in the most recent history. There is the new comer, Takamichi Mori, the man who attacked me from the rafters when I won the Television Title from The Communist. He has joined the Iron Curtain out of shear desperation. If not for their shared dislike for all things democratic he would be out on his own. Any one of the Chaos Society could beat him, and at Stable Wars I hope to get a chance to prove that. Pryde : Then we have the Local NVA Company, the original members of the Iron Curtain. The throwers of the dreaded white powder of doom. These two have been around for a long time and now they are trying to make it again with the likes of these other two clowns. If I were them I wouldn’t hold my breath because I don’t see them helping out one bit. Pryde : Lastly we have the big man The Communist himself. This guy is stupid enough that he couldn’t come up with an original name if his life depended on it so he came up with what he has. A man of his stature could use his size to his advantage against lesser men but with Draco Paladine being the same size as him but with far less experience, he stands no chance at taking a win from us. [Pryde stands up to look into the camera.] Pryde : Many veterans are saying we have no chance to go anywhere in Stable Wars let alone the UWF. We have proven that we can hang with the big boys in most of what we do. Sure there have been many pit falls for any and all of The Chaos Society members, but no one has ever been able to keep us down. We plan on doing everything under our power to push ourselves to main event status, and if that means using chairs or breaking bones we will do it so I suggest that nobody underestimate us because if you do you may regret it. [The scene fades to black.]
