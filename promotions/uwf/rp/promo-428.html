Who cares

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins on Ruff Ryder's private jet as he flies back home to Oklahoma following his match at Supremacy. Ryder is kicked back chewing on some peanuts and drinking a beer as he watches a movie. A woman walks in carrying a camera. She is a very attractive blonde wearing tight low cut blue jeans and an even tighter pink tank top that shows off her small tummy. She walks over next to Ryder and taps him on the shoulder. ) Ryder: What's up? Woman: It's time for you to shoot your promo. Ryder: Already? We just now got up in the air. Woman: Well you wanna get the first shot in right? Ryder: Yea I guess. Woman: Ok then, you've got this match with the Mori kid comin up on Wednesday. Ryder: Yea, I guess I should say a couple things about this little prick before matchtime huh. Thanks Jen. * The woman steps back and begins filming the promo. Ryder springs into interview mode * Ryder: So, another show, and another bullshit match that goes nowhere. Is this really what i've come to? babysitting fuckin rookies? This is horseshit and everyone behind the scenes knows it. I'm better than all of this and it pisses me off that they just throw me into these sorry ass matches. Fuck Takamichi Mori! This pissant didn't even show up for the last match we had and another asshole got lucky and won it. Who is Mori? He's a damn nobody! A waste of a perfectly good roster spot! I'll beat the piss out of him in like 2 seconds, but what happens after that? Throw me in a match with el turtle? That's crap and you all know it. I'm a fuckin former World Champion! * Ryder hurls the beer and breaks it on the door * Ryder: I want Malcom Kennedy and I want him as soon as possible! I know he's a pussy but I didn't know he was chickenshit too. I mean hell I proposed the match to him two damn weeks ago and I have yet to hear shit from him. Digital if you wanna know why I did what I did you can ask your little stablemate. He's the reason you got attacked and until this asshole faces me one on one in the ring i'm gonna keep takin out members of Resurrection. So when all you guys are beaten and bruised just remember it's all Malcom Kennedy's fault. He could have stopped it, but he chooses to hide and let all of you get picked off one by one. Tell that sissy to come out of hiding and let's put an end to this thing. * Ryder glares into the camera * Ryder: Digital you want revenge? Bring that shit on, because I got all that you can handle and then some. I want Kennedy and if I gotta destroy your stable to get him then that's just what i'll do. I gave you a Chin Check, and now it's time for you to realize this is a Gangsta Nation and you got hustled. I'm the man and it's time for the real superstars to join the Nation. I'm always open to new people. The Prime Alliance is dead and I don't think I wanna dig that back up, but what I do wanna do is introduce the world to the Gangsta Nation. Join or not, it's all up to you, but remember this...We will own this shit! ( The scene fades to black as Red letters flash onto the screen that say Gangsta Nation....Kill or be killed! )
