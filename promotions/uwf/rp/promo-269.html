UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #269

A busy night ahead.

March 3 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Malcolms House.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** SAS. Ruff Ryder and Malcolm Kennedy take on all comers. Should be a brawl. Enough said.*** <<< The scene opens up inside a bed room. The room is decked out. Big screen tv, Kind size bed, mirror on the ceiling. Malcolm Kennedy lays on the bed, remote in hand, watching sports center. He looks over at the camera and sits up. As he cuts the tv off he gets off the bed and walks to a closet in the corner. He speaks as he goes through his clothes. >>> Malcolm Kennedy: “ So it seems that me and Ryder will be in the same corner once again as we take on all comers. Ha, so be it. Im not worried.” <<< He reaches up to the shirts hanging from the rack, collared shirts, button ups, t-shirts, you name it. >>> Malcolm Kennedy: “ I mean, who could possibly show up that we would have to worry about? The NVA? Hmmm, no worries there. I beat both of them in singles matches with ease, having Ryder at my side will just make it worse for them. Crazy foreigners, need to board the next boat back home.” “ Who else is there to worry about?” <<< He scans further down the line of shirts.>>> “The legends are dead, Anarchy has already proven how bad they suck, and the Tag Champs have their own business to attend to. The way I see it, me and Ryder wont break a sweat. But hell I could be wrong. I mean, there definitely could and probably be some unexpected appearances.” <<< He stops on a shirt with a picture of Jamie Shock, Ruff Ryder, and Himself on it. The words Prime Alliance below it. He turns and gives a quick strange look a the camera. The continues on.>>> “Oh don’t worry, He wont be there. At least not to my knowledge. It just goes to show you though, that anything and everything is possible here in the UWF.” <<< He stops on a shirt with a picture of a broken table, the THC title laying on top of it.>>> “ Ha ha, point proven.” <<< On he scans stopping at the new red Prime Alliance shirt given to him by Ruff Ryder. >>> “ What could it hurt? TPA could go right back to the top. Hell why not? Tell you what Ryder, we come out with the victory at SAS, and consider me there. Otherwise we are just gonna have to wait longer. But that will happen when the time is right. For now, we have a big match.” <<< He takes the red shirt off the rack and puts it in a bag on the floor that was already half packed. He turns to the camera.>>> “You see, its really quite simple. Ruff Ryder and Malcolm Kennedy will go out there at SAS and take on every single man, woman, or child that comes down that ramp. And we will leave the ring victorious. No team will stop us, not now, not ever.” <<< He zips up the bag and takes it out of the closet. He tosses it down beside his bed and sits down on the bed facing the camera.” “ Don’t think I’ve forgotten about other tasks. Raid, you will get what you deserve. Little bitches like you belong no where near the ring when talent like me and Ryder are in it, because your too big of a bitch to handle it. Yeah anyone can sneak a move in on two tired wrestlers right after their match. Try stepping to either of us when we are 100%. No scratch that, you just do your thing. Ill settle this. Cause Im gonna make sure and step to you at SAS. You can bank on that.” “ There are also a few friends I need to talk to and hammer things out with at SAS. They know who they are. Ill see you people this Sunday.” <<< Malcolm picks the bag up and walks out of the room. The scene fades to black. >>>
