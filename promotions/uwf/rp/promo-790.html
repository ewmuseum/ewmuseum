UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #790

Giving Back to the Children

25 Nov 2004

Tsunami Oni — Spiro Agnew Elementary School

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There’s more to being UWF World champion than money, prestige, ring rats. Ah, ring rats. There is also responsibility. A responsibility to the people that cheer you on, idolize you, buy all your overpriced T-shirts. This is why a blue PT Cruiser is pulling up to Spiro Agnew Elementary School. Out of the vehicle steps none other than the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea, Tsunami Oni. And with him is the Sensation of Domination, Shiatsu. Oni is dressed in a snakeskin suit with an Egyptian cotton shirt. Shiatsu, realizing they will be around children, is looking almost conservative in a gray pinstriped jacket and skirt. Almost, since she has no blouse on and obviously no bra either. Hey, you can take the dancer out of the strip club, but you can’t take the strip club out of the dancer. Shiatsu: God, I can’t believe we have to do this… Oni: What? It’ll be fun. Shiatsu: I hate kids. I hated kids when I was a kid. I hated myself when I was a kid. Oni: It won’t take long. Just relax and it’ll be over just like that. Shiatsu: Wow, you make it sound like sex with Mike Montgomery. Hopefully I won’t have to sit through twenty minutes of excuses and apologies afterwards. Oni: Let’s not go there, all right? Shiatsu: Ooooo… listen to you! You got me, you got the belt, and you still get all huffy thinking about it! The Vivaldi of Violence looks pouty as Shiatsu grins at him. Oni: I’m not huffy. Shiatsu: Awww… I think it’s cute. She gives him a peck on the cheek. Tsunami Oni gives in and smiles. Oni: All right. Let’s get inside. We got people waiting for us. The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea and the Sensation of Domination enter the halls of Spiro Agnew Elementary. They find the classroom they’re looking for and knock on the door. A mousy but cute young woman in glasses opens the door. Teacher: Hello there! I’m the third-grade teacher, Miss Bendonspreddom! Oni: Excuse me? Teacher: Bendonspreddom. It’s Dutch. Shiatsu: That would explain it. Those Dutch… Teacher: What about them? Shiatsu: Errr… never mind. Teacher: Well, the children are waiting for you. They’re so excited! I thought we would start out with a little T & A session. Oni: What? Teacher: Q & A. It stands for “question and answer.” I forgot English isn’t your first language. I’m sorry! Oni: It’s not that. I just thought you said “T & A.” Teacher: Why would I say that? Oni: Uh, I don’t know. Maybe… forget it. Then what? Teacher: Then I thought perhaps you could read to the children. Maybe something from our labia. Oni: Huh? Teacher: Our library. Where we keep books. Oni: Oh. It’s just I thought… Teacher: Yes? Oni: Ummm… actually, I have my own book to read to them. Teacher: Oh, excellent! Now, wait out here while I get ready to seduce you. Oni: Wait wait wait… Teacher: You don’t want me to introduce you? To the children? Oni: Oh. Yeah. Go ahead. As Miss Bendonspreddom goes back into the classroom, Tsunami Oni nudges Shiatsu. Oni: Was I hearing things or did she… hello? Shiatsu? You with us? Shiatsu has a glazed over look on her face. Tsunami Oni’s nudging brings her back to reality. Shiatsu: Oh. Sorry, sweetie. As soon as she opened the door, I zoned out. You know that teacher fantasy I have. Oni: Oh yes. So many times with you in your schoolgirl outfit and me playing the stern headmaster. Shiatsu: Really? I thought I was the “headmaster.” Oni: Shhh… the kids might hear. Miss Bendonspreddom opens the door and motions for Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu to come in. Teacher: And here they are… from the Universal Wrestling Federation, World champion Tsunami Oni and his friend Shiatsu! The third-graders cheer as the Ultimate Power Couple steps into the classroom Tsunami Oni smiles and waves to the kids. Shiatsu makes an attempt to do the same, but lacks a certain sincerity. Oni: Wow! What a great group of kids Are you all little devil-worshippers? Kids: YEEAAAAHHHH!!! Shiatsu: Sweetie, maybe that wasn’t the best thing to ask them. Oni: Huh, didn’t think of that. At least this isn’t a parochial school. Gosh! Look at all of you! I bet you got all kinds of questions for us! A little girl raises her hand. Oni: How about you? Little girl: My daddy says when he sees Shiatsu he can’t stand up. How come? He won’t tell me. Oni: Well… I think he means that he is such a fan of Shiatsu and the other UWF superstars that he doesn’t want to miss anything. Shiatsu: Nice… Oni: OK, who’s next? How about you, young man? Little boy: Where does Shiatsu keep those frying pans? Oni: I’m sorry, I meant the young man next to you. Other little boy: I wanna know where she keeps them, too. Oni: Well… if I told you then somebody might steal them. Shiatsu: Yeah… snatch them away. Oni: Shhh… how about you, little lady? Little girl: Somebody told me Shiatsu was an exotic dancer. Is that like a ballerina? Shiatsu: Yeah. Just like a ballerina. Little girl: Cool! Would you dance for us, Miss Shiatsu? Shiatsu: You kids really want me to dance for you? Kids: YEEAAAAAAHHH!!! Shiatsu: Well how much allowance do your parents give you? Oni: Uh, I don’t think we have time to have Shiatsu dance. Shiatsu: Sure we do! Oni: Uh… but you don’t have your dancing shoes. Shiatsu: I dance in pumps all the time. Isn’t that how you like it? Oni: Hey! Who has a question for me that doesn’t involve Shiatsu? A little boy raises his hand. Oni: How about you? Little boy: Miss Bendonspreddom says she wants to jump your bones, Mister Oni. What’s that? Tsunami Oni looks over at the teacher who is smiling pleasantly. Oni: Well… that’s a game. It’s… uhhhh… what we call “leapfrog” in Japan. The little boy turns to the girl next to him. Little boy: That sounds fun! I’m gonna jump your bones! Little girl: No! I’m gonna jump YOUR bones! Teacher: Class! Class! You can all jump each other’s bones later during recess! Right now Mister Oni has a book to read to you. Kids: YAAAAYYYYYY!!!! Oni: Ok, kids. This is a book called “The Common Man.” I wrote it myself. Tsunami Oni opens the book and begins to read… “There was a common house In a common town It had a common yard And was painted common brown In the common yard There was a common dog And a common girl Chased a common frog In the common house There was a common wife Who paid the common bills Of her common family’s life There was a touch of disappointment In her common eyes Because her common husband Was of less than common size This common husband Had a rather uncommon occupation He was a superstar In the Universal Wrestling Federation He wasn’t quite a jobber But he wasn’t the main event The common man made just enough To pay his common rent His career was quite common Until he got a chance A number one contender’s match That was a four-way dance Against the Communist and Shock And Digital Mortality But in the end the common man Got the one-two-three Oh joy! Oh happiness! The common man sang in victory The only one between him and gold Was that yutz Mike Montgomery But then the common man turned sad As his dream turned into poo Because that mother canucker Lost to the man with hair deep blue Why was the common man so sad He had a title shot all the same But now the belt was held by a real champ One in the Hall of Fame The common man was frantic He needed some kind of plan To beat the devilishly handsome champ And the hottie with the frying pan He thought of his little buddies In the Chaos Society But they were to busy touching themselves As they watched Jessica Simpson on MTV But what else can you expect from a bunch Led by that schmuck Adien Pryde That includes a clown shoe like Reo Dei And a guy named after insecticide The common man couldn’t eat all day He couldn’t sleep at night When Sunday finally came around The common man was quite a fright It was time for the main event And the common man went to the ring But when he stepped in and saw the champ He couldn’t do a thing He couldn’t take a single step Or form his hand into a fist The champ just pushed him over He didn’t even need his mist Just like that it was over The common man’s pride was really hurtin’ No more World title shots for him It was back to jerking the curtain The common man went back home His common wife would make him feel better But when he got home there on the table Was a “Dear Common Man” letter The common man stood there mortified This all came as a shock So he got back in his common car And drove off a common dock The moral of this story Is quite plain to see The common man has no chance in hell Against the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea!” The third-graders cheerd wildly as Tsunami Oni finishes reading. Miss Bendonspreddom smiles broadly at the Vivaldi of Violence and his main squeeze. Teacher: All right, children! Time for recess! Kids: YAAAAYYYY!!!! JUMP YOUR BONES!!! The kids run out of the classroom eager to play. After they all leave, Miss Bendonspreddom closes the door. She turns at looks at Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu. Teacher: Now that they’re gone, why don’t we play “jump your bones?” Miss Bendonspreddom takes her glasses off and stares hungrily at Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu. They look back at the teacher and smile. Oni: Well, this is your class and you make the rules… Shiatsu: But what if we don’t want to follow the rules? Smiling fiendishly, Miss Bendonspreddom picks up a ruler. Teacher: Well, then I guess you’ll have to be punished. And the rest was sheer pornography…
