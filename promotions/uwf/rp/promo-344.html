UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #344

Beach brawl

16 Apr 2004

Beezlebozo — Some beach...who cares, really?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We find ourselves gazing upon a very lovely, white sand beach. The sun’s shining, seagulls are flying about, sandpipers skitter over the sand in their quest for sustenance…and two lovers walk hand in hand along the beach. It’s about the last place we’d expect to see the World Champion. But sure enough, those two lovers are Beelzebozo and Pyretta Blaze, barefoot and wearing sunglasses. The sandpipers scamper out of their way as they walk along the shore. Pyretta Blaze is smiling, apparently enjoying the excursion, or maybe the feel of sand between her toes. Beelzebozo is apparently not a beach person. Beelzebozo: “Can we go yet? It’s too sunny out.” Blaze: “Gee, sunny at a beach in spring. Who would’ve thunk it?” She playfully nuzzles up against his arm. “C’mon, this is fun! Much as I love all the fire and darkness we dwell in, I need to see the sun once in a while to make sure I retain that skin tone that’s sexy milk-white without leaning toward the albino look!” Beelzebozo: “…Do you even listen to the words that come out of your mouth sometimes?” Blaze: “Not when I can help it!” She laughs and dashes off down the beach, leaving the Daemonic Clown standing bitterly by the shore. Beelzebozo: “The things I get talked into by that woman.” He leans back, stretching his spine out a bit. “Still, I suppose it’s good to get away from the wrestling world once in a while and kick back. I just wish it was raining or something.” He shrugs and hunches down, resting his arms on his knees. He scans the water, apparently looking for something. After a moment, he sighs and stands up. And then tenses up. He cocks his head, as if listening for something. At that moment, Blaze comes running up to him. Blaze: “Hey baby! I found a jellyfish washed up on shore! We can dare each other to touch it!” Beelzebozo: “Shhh. Didn’t you hear that?” Blaze: “What?” Beelzebozo: “I’d swear I heard…cheering.” Blaze: “Oh man, the voices in your head brought an audience today?” Beelzebozo: “Shut the hell up. I know what my voices sound like, and this isn’t it.” Blaze: “OK, OK. She stands, trying really hard to hear whatever it is Beelzebozo thinks he’s hearing. “I think that’s just the waves, hon.” Beelzebozo: “I don’t know…maybe.” He shrugs. “All right, maybe I was hearing things. Let’s go.” They begin walking down the shore again. After a few steps, Beelzebozo stops. Blaze: “What now?” Beelzebozo: “I heard it again! A little louder!” Blaze: “You sure about this?” Beelzebozo: “Don’t question me goddammit!” And with that, he takes off running. Blaze: “Hey, wait up!” She scurries off after him. We cut to the two of them running down the beach. The clown stops, and Blaze finally catches up. We can now very clearly hear cheering. Beelzebozo: “See? I told you I heard something!” Blaze: “Sure enough!” Beelzebozo: “The sun’s angle didn’t let me see them before!” We pan to the right, and down the beach we can see a fairly small but vocal crowd seated around… A wrestling ring. There a few men standing near a ice cream truck parked in the sand. Many of them are wearing elaborate costumes, which would mean they’re either awaiting their turn in the ring or cooling off after their matches. A few scantily-clad, fairly good looking women (most with obvious implants) are standing with them. The two men in the ring are currently exchanging punches, and the crowd rises and chants, “DCW! DCW!” We pan back to our couple. Blaze: “Wow! A backyard wrestling show right here! On the very beach we picked! At the very time we were out walking about! What are the odds!?” Beelzebozo slaps her upside the head and walks toward the ring. Blaze follows, rubbing her head. We follow them as they make their way to the back of the crowd. Beelzebozo: “I hope you think twice next time you try to accuse me of setting something like this up. I may be a bastard, but I keep my promises. When I said I’d stay away from business during our little getaway, I meant it.” Blaze: “I’m sorry, baby. It just seems like an awfully damn big coincidence.” Beelzebozo: “Such is the world of wrestling.” Blaze: “Anyway, you can still keep your promise if we leave now.” Beelzebozo: “I supp—“ Fat guy in front of them: “Could you two please shut the fuck up? I’m trying to watch this!” Beelzebozo’s face goes blank. Blaze: “Aw dammit.” Beelzebozo: “You wanna repeat that, fat boy?” Fat guy: “What’d you call me? You wanna—“ His words die on his lips as he turns and sees who’s behind him. His eyes go wide, his jaw drops, and he involuntarily takes a step back, tripping over his own feet and falling on his big flabby ass. He continues to gape at the clown. Beelzebozo: “Do I wanna WHAT, you blubbering land monster? What were you gonna ask me to do?” Fat guy: “I-I-I-I-“ Beelzebozo: “What, are you a sailor now?” Some other guy: “What’s going…” He gapes. “Oh my god…” Beelzebozo: “God’s not here. Only me.” The other guy: “It’s you. It’s really YOU.” Blaze: “He catches on quick.” Fat guy: “Beelzebozo!” Some of the other people standing nearby turn to see what the commotion is about…and they too register shock and amazement upon seeing the UWF World Champion and his lady standing amongst them. Soon, the entire crowd is gazing at them. The two wrestlers in the ring, noticing the crowd’s sudden lack of noise, stop in the middle of a pin attempt (which wouldn’t have mattered anyway since the ref is too staring at the couple) and strain to see what their audience is looking at. One of them, a short but muscular young man with a spiky hairdo, tribal tattoos, several facial and nipple piercings, and his face painted like a skull finally rolls out of the ring and grabs a microphone. Wrestler: “What the fuck’s going on over there!? I thought you people came to see the greatest wrestlers on the planet!” Voice in the crowd: “We are!!” Wrestler: “Well, we’re here in the ring, fucknuts, not out there!” Voice: “Sez you!” The wrestler, clearly pissed off, makes his way through the crowd toward Beelzebozo. Wrestler: “Who the hell’s over there? What is the big goddamn—“ He pauses as the crowd finally clears enough for him to see Beelzebozo. “—deal?” Beelzebozo: “Well, apparently the big deal’s me.” The wrestler, though momentarily awed, quickly regains his composure. Wrestler: “Well well well…if it isn’t Mr. Big Shot UWF World Champion. What an HONOR it is to have you here in DCW!” Blaze: “DCW?” Wrestler: “That’s Death Cult Wrestling!” The crowd starts chanting “DCW!” again. Beelzebozo: “Riiiiight.” Wrestler: “And as such a DISTINGUISHED guest, I think it’s only fitting that I offer you the chance to come speak to all these sick freaks in the audience! Wouldn’t you all like that!?” The crowd goes as nuts as a crowd of 40 people can. Blaze: “Oh hell…” Beelzebozo: “I dunno. I mean, I AM on vacation…” Wrestler: “Whatsa matta? Can’t talk to us PEONS in the minor leagues? Afraid we might TAINT Mr. Big Shot?” Beelzebozo: “It’s not that—“ Blaze: “Honey, if you want to, go ahead. I’ll let it slide.” Wrestler: “See? Your ball and chain says it’s OK! Grow a pair and step into the sickest, most twisted wrestling federation ever…if you’re man enough!” Beelzebozo: “Guess I don’t have a choice now, do I?” With that, Beelzebozo follows the wrestler back to the ring. The crowd is chanting “DCW!” once more. The skull-faced guy’s opponent, some lanky kid in a ripped T-shirt and jean shorts, comes over and shakes Beelzebozo’s hand enthusiastically. Lanky kid: “What an honor to meet you! I’m you biggest fan!” The skull-faced wrestler rolls his eyes, then drops the mic. He grabs the lanky kid and hits him with a surprisingly well-done fisherman’s piledriver. He holds the legs, and the ref drops to the mat and counts to three. The crowd roars, and the skull-faced wrestler tosses the lanky kid face first into the sand outside the ring. Wrestler: “Now that THAT’S out of the way, let me introduce myself. I’m called Voodoo Child, and I am the craziest, deadliest, most fucked-up wrestler in history! Isn’t that right!?” The crowd would seem to agree with this, judging by their reaction. V.C.: “And of course, we all know my surprise guest today…none other than the UWF’s current BMOC, Beelzebozo!” Beelzebozo barely acknowledges the insane noise generated in his name. Beelzebozo: “Can we make this quick? I’ve got a hot woman waiting, and I don’t want her going cold.” V.C.: “Ooooo, sooooo sorry to take up your time, Mr. Corporate Whore! Let’s just get right to this then. As you all no doubt now, Mr. Big-Time Big-Shot here has a match coming up against Bondage, a sick twisted bitch that we all know and love. Recently, he’s been saying that clowns don’t get any tail. Is that true?” He thrusts the mic at Beelzebozo. Beelzebozo: “He said that, did he? Well, my sex life’s neither his business or yours, punk.” V.C.: “Guess that’s a yes then!” The crowd jeers. Beelzebozo: “Playing a dangerous game here, Junior…” V.C.: “Danger? DANGER!? I AM DANGER, bitch!! I sleep danger, eat danger, crap danger…LIVE danger. I’ve taken more glass rods to the skull than Wilt Chamberlain has blowjobs! I’ve plunged through the air from heights that would make a paratrooper nauseous! I’ve broken more tables than Louie Anderson has chairs!” Beelzebozo: “And apparently you blow more hot air than Bill O’Reilly.” The crowd “Ooooooo”s at this, as does Voodoo Child. V.C.: “Pretty big talk, Mr. Man. But no one here at DCW’s Bash at the B—“ Referee: “Shhh! You wanna get sued?” V.C.: “Er…Bash at the…Ocean…thinks you’re that tough!” Beelzebozo: “That a fact?” V.C.: “That’s right, bitch. You better realize where you are. You’re in MY world. DCW. And in my world, I am the alpha male. No one fucks with me and lives to tell about it.” Beelzebozo: “First time for everything, you slack-jawed, pimply-faced wannabe.” The crowd goes nuts. Voodoo Child is shocked by this, but quickly regains him composure and snarls. V.C.: “Well listen up shithead. I think Bondage is gonna fuck you up royally this Sunday. He’s gonna leave you in the emergency room, begging for your momma. And I’m gonna laugh when he does it, you pathetic excuse for a Gurgles ripoff.” Beelzebozo: “That so.” V.C.: “Damn right. In fact, I think he’ll be the one to take your belt away. As a matter of fact…” And on that note, he steps right up to Beelzebozo and pokes him in the chest. “…I think _I_ could take your belt away, you pussy.” The crowd does that “Ooooooooo” thing again. Voodoo Child drops the mic and begins motioning for the clown to “bring it.” Beelzebozo picks up the mic. Beelzebozo: “Well, so much for my vacation.” He casually tosses the microphone aside. And then explodes across the ring, nearly decapitating Voodoo Child with a clothesline. The crowd goes absolutely nutzoid. As well they should. Beelzebozo begins stomping Voodoo Child mercilessly, stopping only when he begins coughing up blood. Beelzebozo leaps out of the ring and begins digging through the sand. The crowd seems confused by this…until he unearths a fairly large shell. He rolls back into the ring and drags Voodoo Child to his feet, only to smash the shell over his head. The young man collapses, and the clown drags him to his feet again. Hefting him over his head, Beelzebozo press slams him to the outside, and follows him. The crowd chants “DCW!” as Beelzebozo begins rubbing Voodoo Child’s face in the sand, leaving it stained with blood. Voodoo Child screams and throws an elbow into Beelzebozo’s face, giving him enough time to stagger to his feet and grab a chair from a nearby fan. With all his might, he swings the chair and catches Beelzebozo over the head with it. His face twists into a sneer of triumph… And quickly turns into a look of sheer horror as Beelzebozo merely blinks at him. The clown gently takes the chair from the paralyzed young man, looks at it, hefts it, and catches Voodoo Child across the face with it, sending him spinning to the ground. The crowd roars as Beelzebozo tosses the chair aside and drags the barely-conscious, badly bleeding V.C. to the ice cream truck. He props the young man’s head on the counter, then reaches up and slams the hood down on his head repeatedly. As Voodoo Child collapses to the sand, Beelzebozo again lifts him up and tosses him up onto the roof of the ice cream truck. He climbs on top of it and positions V.C. on his shoulders. The crowd reaches ear-shattering decibels as they realize what he’s about to do. And sure enough, Voodoo Child eats the Last Laugh from the top of the ice cream truck. Beelzebozo drags the unconscious man to the ring and rolls him inside. He climbs in and picks up the microphone. Beelzebozo: “Hey ref…do I really need to pin him?” The ref reaches over and raises the clown’s hand. The crowd cheers wildly. Blaze rolls into the ring and latches onto Beelzebozo’s arm. “Well, I guess that was fun. I’m glad you all liked it.” The crowd does indeed seem to have liked it. “I really need to go now.” The crowd moans. “But…I tell you what. When Voodoo Chump wakes up, you tell him that, whatever else I think of him, I admire his guts, and his quick wit. If he’s the best DCW has to offer, well…maybe they’re not so bad.” The crowd is ecstatic at this pronouncement. Beelzebozo’s smile suddenly turns sinister. “In fact…I think I have a great idea. I’m inviting all of DCW’s finest to Hell’s Carnival.” The crowd goes apeshit as Blaze tugs on his arm. Blaze: “You’re doing what now??” Beelzebozo: “That’s right. I’m so…impressed by what I’ve seen today, that I’m going to hold a special tournament just for the elite DCW wrestlers. They will all come to Hell’s Carnival and face the best the UWF has to offer…namely, ME. “So tell them to meet me at the UWF offices this Saturday at high noon. “If they’ve got a pair, that is.” With that, Beelzebozo drops the mic, and he and Blaze leave the ring to an rousing “Bell-ze-bo-zo! *clap clap, clap-clap-clap*” type chant. Blaze obviously wants to say something, but she waits until they’ve left the crowd and are walking away. Blaze: “What the hell is this about?” Beelzebozo: “I’m just giving those kids’ careers a boost, dearest!” Blaze: “And why the hell would you want to do that?” Beelzebozo: “Oh, you know me…” The sinister smile returns. “I’m always willing to help my fellow man.” And with that, we fade to black.
