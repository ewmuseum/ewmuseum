UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #841

The One and Only

15Dec04 3:17 am (year unrecorded)

The Communist — The Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Commie picks up the NA title, humming the Hymn of the Red Army, and sits down holding it in his lap. Soviette walks up behind him, peering over his shoulder] Soviette: North American Title, hmm? Commie: Yep. Soviette: How did we win this if we don't even represent North America? [Commie pauses... thinks... looks down at the title... thinks some more] Soviette: Don't strain anything. [Commie stands, sending the NA title Flying off his lap. He raises his fist to the air, as though challanging the world] Commie: We must make reparations! I am NOT the Champion of North America! I am the CHAMPION OF THE PEOPLE! The Proletariot Champion! And THIS *Raising the title over his head* IS the Proletariot Title! Soviette: ... Sure. Whatever. [The Communist runs over and picks up the title and runs off. He takes it to THE PEOPLES JEWELER.] Commie: Shopkeep! I require assistance! Peoples Jeweler: What can the Peoples Jeweler do for ya Jabroni? Commie: This belt *Slaps it on the table* Is NO LONGER the North American Title! This is THE PROLETARIAT TITLE! Yes! Let the rich fat bourguasis Tremble as the Peoples Champion takes his throne! Peoples Jeweler: What was that! The Peoples Champion! There's only one Peoples Champion Jabroni and that Champion is *censored for Possible Copywrite infringment* Commie: Wait... Did I say Peoples Champion? I meant Proletariat. Peoples Jeweler: Better. I can take care of this for you. Leave it to the PEOPLES JEWELER! [Hours Later... ] [The Communist Returns to the gym with the title in a bag.] Commie: Soviette! I fixed the belt! Soviette: Really? Let me see. [The Communist opens the bag, letting her see the finished result] Soviette: umm... Commie? North American has been scratched out and Proletariot was written underneath. In SHARPIE MARKER. Commie: Yes... About that... I couldn't afford the PEOPLES JEWELER's prices... Soviette: *sighs* You're hopeless...
