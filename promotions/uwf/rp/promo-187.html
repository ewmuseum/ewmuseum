UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #187

The calm before the storm.

Jan 22 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Grand Central Casino.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((( Joshua Dunn has claimed that his overlooked in this fed. Claiming no one pays attention to him. No one acknowledges him and the matches he has won, Nor the title he holds. Even the fact that he beat Ryder and technically won the NA title seems to get him nothing. At least in his eyes. But what about other people? What do they think. Lets find out. ))) ** The scene opens up inside the same office that we left Malcolm Kennedy in the last time we saw him. The camera moves closer to the window and begins to scan across the main floor. Watching all the people desperately trying to win big. Suddenly a brief laugh is heard from behind the camera. The camera spins around and finds Malcolm Kennedy propped up against the wall in the shadowy corner beside a plant. He wears a pair of baggy blue jeans, a blue and white striped collar shirt, and a pair of white and orange DC shoes. He looks down at his arm and scratches at the forearm. He casually walks towards the camera and passes by it. After getting on the same side of the camera, he sits down in his chair. He reaches over to his phone..... ))) Malcolm Kennedy: “ Hey Jane? Jane are you still here?” Voice on the phone: “ Yes Mr. Kennedy, It looks like Im gonna be here late again.” Malcolm Kennedy: “Okay. Could you please hold all my calls? Oh and would you cancel my doctors appointment Friday? It looks like Im gonna be caught up in that big meeting.” Jane: “ Yes sir, Mr. Kennedy. Would you like me to reschedule the appointment? “ Malcolm Kennedy: “ No thank you Jane. That will be all.” ** Malcolm pulls his hand off the button on the phone, and moves it to the top right drawer. He opens it up and pulls out a box of Tampa Nugget cigars. He pulls one from the box and places the box back inside the drawer. After closing the drawer back up, Malcolm removes the plastic from the individual cigar. He then gently runs the cigar under his nose, inhaling as it slides by. He smiles. ** Malcolm Kennedy: “ You cant beat a good cigar. Sure, its not Cuban. But these Tampa’s suit me just fine.” ** Malcolm puts the cigar between his teeth and pulls a Zippo out of his right pocket. He quickly flips it open and strikes it all in one flick of the wrist. He lights the cigar and begins puffing on it. After two quick puffs, smoke begins to cloud in front of his face and slowly rise away. Malcolm takes a long drag off of it, and puffs out a few smoke rings. He then sets the cigar down on the edge of the ash tray on his desk. ** Malcolm: “ Dunn, Dunn, Dunn. You seem to overlook that extra weight you wear around your waist and carry over your shoulder. Im surprised the shine of the gold on that IC title hasn’t bothered your eyes yet. OVERLOOKED? Oh please Josh, tell me you don’t actually by the crap that came out of your mouth. You the Intercontinental champion. You technically just won the North American title last Sunday. How are you complaining? Yeah you may not have a shot at the World title. So what? I mean, if that belt on your waste comes as a burden to you instead of a blessing, you could easily give it to any of the wrestlers who want it. You want a shot at that Heavyweight belt? Too bad. Right now I am the man who has earned that. So you cant just jump in line with the rest of the roster. But all of this is so unimportant right now. There are bigger tasks at hand. Bigger fish to fry. ** Malcolm lifts his right leg and crosses it over his left as he takes a few more puffs on the cigar before returning it to the ash tray. He scratches at his thin beard line that runs along the bottom of his jaw. ** Malcolm Kennedy: “ Never tapping out to anyone is a great claim. I can respect that. I never said I would make you tap out. But I did say that I would make you hurt. And that I would break you. You see, making you submit wont do me any good. For me, there is no gratification. You see, I find more pleasure finishing you off with the Jackpot. Putting you down for the 3 count shows me that I was the one who kept you from getting off of the mat. I was the one who beat you with my own bare hands, and left you in the ring the loser. Yeah making someone tap is fun. But its not my bag. You have your crossface, and its pretty wicked. I’ve been on the receiving end of it once before. Yeah it hurts, but do you think it will hurt me enough to make me tap. Hell I got up from it last time and continued to fight you and the players club on my own. Keep in mind. As long as I am on my own two feet, you are still in trouble. Anyways, like I was saying. Submission holds are great. They weaken your opponent, they tire the opponent, and they can hurt like a bitch. But when your facing me in the ring, They DO NOT, I repeat DO NOT, win matches. I implore you to try anyways. By all means. If I tap out, you wont be the first person to prove me wrong. But if I don’t, I have once again proved someone wrong. I have once again proven that I can back up my words. And I’ve proven I am the Fastest rising star in the UWF today.” ** Malcolm puts out the cigar in the ash tray after barely smoking any of it. He pulls out a cigar cutter and clips off the squished burnt end. He leaves the good piece on the edge of the ash tray and stands up from the desk. He stands looking out the far left window. Staring between the blinds he smiles as he looks down on the casino he owns. ** Malcolm Kennedy: “Im finally getting my shots. Finally getting chances to prove I can compete with the best in the business, and win. Dunn, you are just the next chance on my path. The next speed bump. I don’t take anything away from what you have done here in the UWF. Lets face it, if you weren’t a tough son of a bitch, you wouldn’t be holding the IC title. If you weren’t one of the best wrestlers here, you wouldn’t be carrying that gold with you everywhere. I don’t doubt our first official encounter will be a brawl. And I know it surely wont be our last encounter. But make no mistake about it, I will give you the fight of your life.” ** Malcolm turns around and puts his back to the windows.** Malcolm Kennedy: There’s a lot more riding on these matches than meets the eye. Bragging rights play a big role. Being a member of the Prime Alliance, I already know that my partners and myself are better than the Players Club. But its just like in any other sport you can play on this earth, even the best of the best loose some times. The Prime Alliance will always try and keep the best record possible. We try and avoid that unfortunate loss that will eventually come. But it wont be this week. It wont be at supremacy. We will not loose to you clowns. Losing at supremacy is not an option. That’s just the way it is for us.” ** Malcolm walks around the desk and heads back to the dark corner where we first saw him. He speaks over his shoulder as he roots through his luggage in the corner. ** Malcolm Kennedy: “ Josh, at Supremacy. The Prime Alliance and The Players Club will begin what could be a major war within the UWF. It will not be a pretty site at all. Me and you are going to beat the living hell out of each other. We are going to show the whole world what wrestling really is. Only one of us on the other hand, Will show the world what we are really all about. And that’s winning. Only one of us can come out with the win. I plan on being that person. Try your best to stop me. ** Malcolm picks up a book bag off the ground and puts the straps over his shoulders. He then grabs another clothing bag and throws the one single strap of his right shoulder. He grabs another bag by the handle and begins to drag it out on its wheels. He flips off the lights as he walks out the door. Closing the door behind him the room becomes dark. The only light coming from the windows overlooking the floor. The camera then fades to black and the scene ends. ***
