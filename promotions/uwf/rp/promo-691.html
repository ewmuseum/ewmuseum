UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #691

Getting the gang back together

9 Sep 2004

Adien Pryde — A popular McDonalds

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside a, what seems to be a busy McDonalds lately. A familiar black SUV pulls up to a parking stall right in front of the door. Out steps from the passenger side Adien Pryde, dressed on black jean shorts and a red tank top and black combat boots. Out of the driver side steps Reo Dai in her black jeans and red t-shirt and sandals. They walk in the front doors and the camera fades to black.] [The camera fades in inside the restaurant where we see Pryde and Reo Dai walking up to who appears to be Raid and they sit down.] Raid : Dude, how is Joanna? Pryde : She is better than the average woman would be after getting hit in the head by a sledge. She will probably end up with a concussion but other than that she is good. Raid : That is good. Pryde : Anyways, thanks for meeting with us today, we were wondering…. [Pryde looks down on the table and picks up a piece of paper.] Pryde : What is this? Raid : Oh, just something that that new guy, Dreammaker, was handing out to everyone here. You just missed him. Dai : Is he any good? Any reason we should look at him for problems? Raid : No, he lost his debut match and of all things he drives a Ford Focus. How good of a wrestler can he be? I just think he was trying to gain some fan support for his match against that KKK loser tonight. Dai : Weird way of doing that. Not a very high class area to do it. Pryde : Anyway, the reason we called you was to try to get you back wrestling full time again. We know you have had some LARGE contracts with the city lately, but we figured if we helped take care of some of them, would that free up the time you need to come back full time. Raid : It would free it up, but I will have to think about weather I will come back or not. Pryde : That’s all we ask it to think about it. Anarchy and Meaner can only do so much in Chaos Society but you have the muscle to do it. Raid : Give me some time and if I do, expect my impact to be felt. Pryde : That is what we expect. Raid : What the hell are you doing in this 6-man match on Sunday. How did you get involved with Tsunami Oni and Nick Persia. You hate those two. Shit, you were just in a bitter feud over The Hardcore Championship. How are you going to not strangle your tag partners. Pryde : I’m not sure why these promoters decided to book this sick match, but if it lets me get my hands around Plague’s throat, I am all for it. I am going to kill that bastard for what he did to Joanna, after that it is open season on the other four. Raid : I feel sorry for them. Pryde : I have my chance to climb to the top, and if that means taking out everyone, so be it. It was 3 men last week, and Sunday is just twice as many. Remember, Dai and I are tag specialist, I just have to adapt with the new people and I am set. I wrestled Persia enough times to know how to work best with him and Oni has been in the business long enough to wok well with anyone. I see no reason why we won’t kick The Plague, Mike Montgomery, and The Communist’s ass. Raid : True. [Raid looks at his watch.] Raid : Shit, I have a job to do. I will get back to you on your proposition. Pryde : Thanks [All three men get up and Pryde and Raid shake hands. They walk out together talking about the last time they went out and got in a bar fight.]
