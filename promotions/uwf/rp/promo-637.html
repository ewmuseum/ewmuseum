UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #637

Regal Rumble: 3 days of emotion.

August 15th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Everywhere

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Terror in Tokyo. Regal Rumble. 60 men enter, one man leaves the #1 contender for the most coveted title in the UWF. The World Heavyweight Championship. One man who outlasts 59 other and remains the last one left in the ring will raise his hands in victory and prepare to go to Live and Loud to compete for the World title. It baffles the mind. 60 of the UWF’s finest will brawl until only one remains. Some have it easier than others. The Plague for instance, is the last entrant. Jamie Shock on the other hand, is the very first, Courtesy of one Ruff Ryder. And somewhere in between there, amongst all those names, and all those faces, and all those egos, lies the biggest ego of them all....... “Top Notch” Malcolm Kennedy. *** {Time: 9:58 AM.} {Place: Grand Central Resort and Casino.} {Date: August 10th, 2004.} (( The scene opens.)) “God damn it Gloria! We aren’t going to discuss this again. Im obligated to wrestle in Tokyo. If I don’t show, I don’t have a contract. I’ve got two HUGE matches to deal with, and all you can say is ‘Stay home dear’. You know I cant do that.” [The voice carried throughout the office hallways of the Grand Central Resort and Casino. The anger built in the voice, and quickly drifted away with reason. A woman who needed to spend time with her husband sat on the other end of the line, alone. Her husband speaking to her with words she didn’t want to hear.] “Gloria, please. Just calm down, Its only for a few days. I go to Tokyo tonight, and Ill be back the night after Pay Per View is over. Ill be gone for about a week. Its not that long.” [The woman just didn’t seem to understand. Or maybe she didn’t want to understand. She needed her husband by her side. Her kids needed their father. But their father needed to wrestle.] The camera paced down the hallway. Traveling towards the voice as it continued to reason with the woman on the phone. The end of the hallway grew near, and there was only one door left to enter. It sat at the very end of the wall, half open, half shut. The huge oak door blocked the view of the man on the phone as the camera reached the entrance, but the letters in gold on the door told the whole story. MALCOLM KENNEDY “Listen Gloria, I cant discuss this anymore. I’ve got a lot left to do here right now before I leave to catch my plane. Tell the kids I love them, and Ill call you when Im about to board the plane. I love you dear.” [There was a long pause as he listened to her respond. He sighed as he listened to her speak on the phone. She was taking this badly. She wanted her husband by her side, and he couldn’t be there.] “Okay, I love you. Call you later.” [Another long pause.] “Bye Gloria.” The camera pushed the door open to see Malcolm Kennedy sitting on the edge of his desk, still holding the phone in his hand, head hanging. He finally sets down the receiver and hangs up the phone that rests on his desk right beside his leg. He sighs as he picks his head up. Standing up from the desk he walks back behind it and sits in his chair. Glancing at a few papers here and there, nodding at times, shaking his head in disgust at others. Signing a few that need it and ripping up papers as well. He reaches over to his intercom, and with the push of the button he contacts his secretary. Malcolm Kennedy: “Hey Jane, what time does my jet leave?” Jane: “At 4:30 Mr. Kennedy.” Malcolm Kennedy: “I trust its booked first class?” Jane: “Mr. Kennedy. You are sharing the plane with only four other people. Its one of the most exclusive flights out there. Its quite pricey as well. Im sure you will enjoy it. The treatment you will receive will make a first class flight seem like a simple joke.” Malcolm Kennedy: “Nice work. Oh, Hey Jane. One more thing.” Jane: “Yes Mr. Kennedy?” Malcolm Kennedy: “Talk to me about a raise when I get back from Tokyo, okay?” Jane: “Yes sir.” Removing his finger from the intercom button, Malcolm leans back into his chair. He scratched his ruff beard as he relaxed. Malcolm Kennedy: “All the way to Tokyo for a 60 man battle royal? I better fucking win” [ Even with his face looking troubled, and his mind seemingly uneasy, his arrogance shined through. He would walk into Tokyo just another man on the roster, fanning the flames in a heated rivalry with former champion Jamie Shock, who was thrown into the Regal Rumble to take up space and add to the glory of it all. No one expecting him to win, or even last that long. But his plans were different. Not only did he plan on taking down Jamie Shock once and for all, he planned to stun the wrestling world by walking out the #1 contender courtesy of the Regal Rumble.] Malcolm Kennedy: “Lets not forget that thorn in my side either. Stupid twat, Jamie Shock will get what’s coming to him. In the rumble, and in our singles match.” [ His face grew red with anger as he clenched his fists. His was angry. How dare anyone steal his spotlight! Have they forgotten who they are dealing with? He’ll show them, He’ll show them all.] Malcolm Kennedy: “ I don’t claim to be the fastest, the strongest, or the smartest. But I do claim to have more guts than any man on this very roster. I’ve stared the best of the best right in the eye and fought until there was nothing left in me. Even with the odds against me, backing down was never an option. So when I step into the Regal Rumble, and 59 other men want to get rid of me and my ego as soon as possible, I say bring em all on. Backing down never solved anything for me.” [ Who has ever claimed glory for running away? Being a coward has never been an option. Malcolm wanted to get in the ring with 60 other men tonight, not next week. He wanted to show the whole world just what he meant when he called himself the One Man Army. But he couldn’t. Not yet. His glory would have to wait.] Malcolm Kennedy: “ People doubted me when I said I would open my own casino and become one of the most successful businessmen in Nevada. I proved them wrong. So when all this doubt about me coming out as the victor of the Regal Rumble rains down on me, and Im singled out as a joke with a big ego. Ill prove every last doubter wrong again.” Malcolm stands up from his desk and stretches his arms and legs. He closes the blinds that over look the casino floor. He then flips the switch to his desk light. He picks up a ticket that rests atop a black leather luggage bag. He then lifts up the bag. As he walks out the door, he flips the switch and turns out the office lights. Closing and locking the door behind him. He walked down the hallway and into the elevator. [ He wanted to run home and scoop up his two children and hug them in his arms. He longed to kiss his wife’s lips one last time. Just one more night with the family he loved. But he couldn’t, and it was killing him. Knowing that all his work was done and the Grand Central Resort and Casino was in good hands, his only option was to catch his flight to Tokyo, Japan, and get ready for the Regal Rumble.] Malcolm pushed the button to take him to the ground floor. He looked out and down the hallway one last time and breathed deeply. He sighed as the door closed. ((FADE TO BLACK)) {Time: 3:17 PM.} {Place: Red Dragon Hotel.....Downtown Tokyo.} {Date: August 12th, 2004} ((The scene opens)) [ A man began pacing back and forth in his hotel room. His heart heavy with regrets. His mind racing with thoughts. He had obligations to wrestle in Tokyo Japan at the upcoming Terror in Tokyo, Regal Rumble 5 Pay Per View. On the other hand, He had a wife and two kids at home who never got to say goodbye to him in person. Sure a phone call helps, hearing the voice of the ones you love eases the soul. But he wanted to see them. To touch them. To hold his kids. To hug his wife. But he couldn’t.] Malcolm Kennedy comes into view walking back and forth atop an oriental rug. His hotel room decked out in ancient Japanese artifacts and dragons. Bright, fire red walls. Golden rugs with red and black designs. A king size bed with golden silk sheets tossed around atop it. Malcolm wore a pair of black dress pants, a black sports coat, and a red undershirt. A red handkerchief sat inside his front chest pocket. His left hand rested inside his left pants pocket, while his right held a glass of freshly opened champagne. He continued to pace the room, sipping the champagne, deep in thought. “ There’s too many of them. Sixty men? What are you crazy Malcolm? You cant survive something like that! Just stay focused on beating that idiot Jamie Shock. You can earn your shot at the world title later down the road.” [ Could it be? One of the biggest egos in the history of the UWF now doubts himself? He wanted to win that Regal Rumble more than anyone else just two days prior. And now he stood here before the camera, once again, and he’s telling himself he cant win? Deep down he knew that he could never live with not giving his all to win that rumble. He was just nervous. He missed his family, his friends, his casino, everything he loved that was back home in Nevada. Deep down though, he knew better. He still felt this battle royal was his for the taking.] Malcolm Kennedy: “What am I saying? Snap out of it Malcolm! You can do this. This match is all but yours. Screw Shock, you already showed the world that you can beat him. Focus on the rumble. This is YOUR chance. YOUR shot. Not anyone else’s. Have faith. Regain that confidence. Where’s that cocky individual that the UWF fans have come to hate? Come on Malcolm, SNAP OUT OF IT NOW!!!” Without missing a beat, his left hand emerged from his pocket and smacked himself across the face. He stopped in his tracks, staring at the hand that just assaulted him. Breathing heavily, he returned the hand to its pocket, and drank down the rest of the champagne in his glass. He then set the glass on the table and sat down on the edge of the bed. He rested his elbows on his knees as he stared at the rug on the floor. Malcolm Kennedy: “This is my chance. MY CHANCE! Screw those 60 men. I’ve payed my dues. I’ve been fired, beaten, screwed over, you name it. I’ve poured my blood, sweat and tears into that ring. This is my chance to fight for that world title, and Ill be damned if Im gonna let 60 other men stop me. Its time I get what I deserve. Its time I take my step forward to the front of the line. Its time I become the champion.” [ His heart beat faster at the thought of winning that gold. His adrenaline rushed as he imagined himself holding the world title high above his head in victory. He got goose bumps as he imagined fulfilling his dream. That world title couldn’t look any better than it would around his waist. He knew it. He was tired of dreaming. Tired of waiting for the opportunity to fall in his lap. Tired of trying so hard and getting nowhere. He was just tired of it all. But deep down he knew that the Regal Rumble was his chance to leave all of those false hopes in the past, and start over. He planned to take full advantage of it too.] Malcolm Kennedy: “ This is my big chance. 60 men. One match. One winner. I will finally become the #1 contender once again, and this time Im gonna win that damn title. Come hell or high water.” With that Malcolm stands up from the bed and makes his way to the small table beside the couch. He grabs his room key off of it and walks out the door without looking back. He shuts the door behind him. ((FADE TO BLACK)) {Time: 11:00 Am} {Date: August 13th, 2004} {Place: Red Dragon Hotel....Downtown Tokyo.} ((The Scene Opens)) The scene opens up where last closed, the camera located within the plush interior of Malcolm Kennedy’s hotel room. The camera sits focused on a pair of feet hanging off the edge of the bed. It pans up and follows two legs that rest under the golden sheets. As the camera continues upwards, the golden sheets stop at the waist line, leaving the torso exposed. The camera comes to a stop on Malcolm Kennedy’s face. He lays there sound asleep. RING........RING........RING With a jolt Malcolm sat upright in the bed. He rubbed at his eyes as they adjusted to the light of the room. He then pulled the receiver off the phone and answered it. Malcolm Kennedy: “Hello?” Man On Phone: “ Mr Kennedy?” Malcolm: “ Yes, this is him.” Man: This is your eleven o’clock wake up call. Good morning.” Malcolm: “Oh yeah, I forgot about that. Thanks a lot.” Man: “Your welcome sir.” Malcolm: “What time should I expect Room Service with my breakfast and newspaper?” Man: Its on the way right now sir. Should be there shortly” Malcolm: “Thank you.” With that Malcolm hung up the phone. He then slid to the edge of the bed and kicked his feet off onto the ground. He shuddered from a cold chill and stood up. The sheets fell off of him exposing a pair of black athletic shorts sagging below the waist line of his boxers. He reached his hands to the sky as he extended his body and stretched while he yawned. As he lowered his arms, a knock came at the door. Voice: “Room Service!” Malcolm: “Im coming” Malcolm opened the door and stepped back as a large silver cart rolled in. The cart was loaded with fruits and cheeses, orange juice, coffee, croissants, and bagels. Malcolm grabbed a twenty off his dresser and tipped the man, and closed the door. He grabbed a few grapes and began tossing them in his mouth one by one. He looked down at his paper and picked it up. As he did, a small card fell from inside it. Malcolm tossed another grape in his mouth as dropped the paper and picked up the card. He stopped midway through biting his grape, and left it whole between his teeth as he stared at the card. He looked closer at it and realized it was a post card from home. [His heart sank in his chest. How could he focus on winning his matches when his family was sending him post cards from home telling him how much they missed him and wanted him to come home. He was afraid to turn it over and read what was on the back. But he knew he had to, so with a sigh, he did.] Malcolm flipped the card over and began to read the back of the card out loud. Malcolm “My dearest husband, I know I haven’t made this easy on you. But please know that it is only because I love you with every bit of my heart. I also know now, That Im happy for you and what you are doing. You are living your dream, and for that I am the proudest wife on the planet. So Im writing you this to let you know that I support you 100 percent, and I will be cheering you on from home. Make me and your children proud. We know you can do it. In the words of General Dwight D. Eisenhower, ‘I have full confidence in your courage, devotion to duty, and skill in battle. We will accept nothing less than full victory!” I know you can win this, the kids know you can win this, and you know you can win this. So just get the job done. Make us proud. Love, Gloria, Jason, and Andrew.” [ His heart beat faster. No longer sinking in his chest. His confidence restored and his ego refilled. He was ready to do battle in that ring on two separate occasions, and he was ready to win both times.] He smiled as he turned the card over many times. Thinking of his wonderful family. He dropped it on his dresser and picked up a slice of cheese from the tray. He ate it one bite as he turned around and looked himself in the mirror across the room on the wall. The camera zooms in on his face in the mirror. Malcolm Kennedy: “I can do this. Nothing will stop me. Not Jamie Shock, nor the 58 other men. This is my time to shine. This is my chance to put my name at the top. To make it known just how big of a force I am in the UWF. Once I do that, its time I take the world title. Its time I put my name among the greats. Trust me, I will do just that.” “ I’ve been here long enough with nothing to show for it. Its time I change that. I’ve payed my dues, and I’ve earned my respect. Now its time I take that damn title. The Regal Rumble is just my first step towards that. Its starts with my outlasting 59 other wrestlers in a battle royal. Everyone who will step into the battle royal will be giving everything they’ve got to earn that number 1 contenders spot. They will cheat, they will brawl, and they will hurt whoever they can. And so will I.” He grabbed another cluster of grapes and began eating them. He turned and sat down on his bed. He sat there deep in thought, staring at the ground, eating his grapes without really noticing them. [Malcolm knew that this was his perfect shot. This was the big one. He blew it last time his world title shot came along, and he wouldn’t make the same mistake twice. He knew that if he didn’t win this Rumble, he would have to remain in waiting. Waiting for his next big chance at glory, And he didn’t want to. He didn’t want to wait any more. He was sick of it. He hated himself for that. He hated the fact that he sat around waiting for an opportunity to just fall in his lap. Knowing full well, that it would never happen that way. If he wanted anything in the UWF, he would have to go out and get it. But he just never did. Then came Terror In Tokyo, Regal Rumble 5. His big chance to fight for what he has always wanted. His chance to once again be the number one contender for the UWF World Heavyweight title. This time he wasn’t gonna let it pass him by. Nothing would stop him this time. Nothing at all.] Malcolm: “ This is my chance. If I don’t give it all I got, and win this damn rumble. I will never be able to live with myself. This is my chance to prove everyone who has ever had a doubt about me wrong. This is a chance to prove to myself, that I am the best. At the Regal Rumble, Im gonna win, even if I have to throw all 59 other competitors out, Im gonna win.” Malcolm stands up from the bed and stares straight into the camera. Malcolm: “Im gonna win!” ((FADE TO BLACK)) *End Promo*
