UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #284

The play's the thing

13 Mar 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open upon the gates to Hell’s Carnival. The two fire-breathing dragon statues stand guard as always. Outside of the gates sits a very large baby carriage. Something cries and fusses inside. A large note is pinned to the side, but we can’t quite read it. The Quiet One appears behind the gates, and looks around, trying to see who left this thing here. He shrugs, and opens the gates. We cut to the main room of the big tent that serves as Beelzebozo’s headquarters. He’s currently napping in his big, fugly orange chair, with Pyretta Blaze curled up in his lap. The sounds of a baby’s cries slowly becomes audible. As they get louder, Blaze stirs and slowly opens her eyes. Blaze: “Is that…what I think it is?…” The sounds get louder, and she shakes the clown. His left arm lashes out and shoves her to the floor. Blaze: “Ow!” Beelzebozo: “You should know better than to wake me up.” Blaze: “But don’t you hear that?” He opens his mouth to speak, and then apparently hears the cries. Beelzebozo: “What in the hundred hells is THAT!?” He bounds to his feet as the Quiet One pushes the carriage through the crimson curtains and into the room. Beelzebozo and Blaze stare. Blaze: “What in the…” Beelzebozo: “Why the hell did you bring that…THING in here!? What were you thinking? WERE you thinking??” The Quiet One makes calming gestures, then points into the carriage at the crying thing inside. The couple make their way closer and peer inside. Blaze jerks back, repulsed. Blaze: “What an ugly child!” Beelzebozo: “Wow, you aren’t kidding.” Blaze: “Well, what do we do with it now?” Beelzebozo: “I say we toss it into the bonfire.” Blaze: “OOOOOOO!! Yesyesyesyesyes!!” The Quiet One “tsk’s” them with his fingers. Beelzebozo: “To hell with you, mime! I don’t want that thing here!” Blaze: “Well…I don’t suppose we can in good conscious toss a helpless baby into a fire, can we? Wait…what am I saying? Of course we can!” The mime takes off his hat, pulls out a large piece of paper, and hands it to Beelzebozo. The clown reads it aloud. Beelzebozo: “To you, dear strangers…please take care of my little baby boy. I can no longer do so. If you can take care of him for me, you will be greatly rewarded. Signed…” He pauses, his eyes growing wide. “…Ms. Ryder.” Upon the utterance of the name “Ryder,” the thing in the carriage stands up. It appears to be a midget, wearing a large diaper and sporting a hairstyle exactly like Ruff Ryder’s. Beelzebozo: “Man…I really don’t want to deal with this thing right now, but I WOULD like to know what my reward would be…” Blaze: “I suppose there’s only one way to find out.” Beelzebozo: “….Yeah.” The “baby” lets loose a howl that makes everyone else cover their ears. Blaze: “This is gonna SUCK.” We cut to the same room, apparently some time later. The “baby” is seated in a large high chair, and Blaze is trying to get it to take a bottle. Beelzebozo is standing nearby, watching. The Quiet One is resting on the couch. Blaze: “C’mon, baby…take the bottle.” The “baby” responds by wailing and shaking its head. Beelzebozo: “This is getting us nowhere. What does this damn thing want?” Blaze: “I don’t know! Hmmm…maybe we should, I don’t know…ask it?” Beelzebozo: “Can it even talk?” Blaze: “One way to find out.” She turns to the “baby” and smiles. “What do you want, little man?” Baby: “BELT!!” Beelzebozo: “Oh, you want to get punched? Why didn’t you say so?” Blaze: “Honey, I think he meant an actual belt.” Beelzebozo: “But he doesn’t even wear pants!” The Quiet One suddenly bolts from the room. “Where’s he going?” Blaze: “Since when am I able to fathom the hidden mysteries of the ways of mimes?” The mime returns, holding Beelzebozo’s World Champion belt. The “baby” reaches for it wildly. Baby: “Belt! Belt!” The Quiet One hands the belt to Beelzebozo. The “baby” flails for the belt, screeching. Beelzebozo: “Is this what you want?” The “baby” nods. “Well, I’m sorry little man, but you can’t have it. It’s mine.” The “baby” lets loose a horrifying shriek that causes all present to clutch their ears and shake. Blaze: “You have GOT to stop that!!” Baby: “Belt! I want belt! I deserve belt! You cheated!” Beelzebozo: “…The hell??” Baby: “You cheated, and I want belt now!!” Beelzebozo: “Oh sweet merciful…” He clutches his forehead with his free hand. Blaze: “Little guy, you have to understand…he didn’t cheat.” Baby: “He got lucky! I want belt now!” Beelzebozo: “Lucky?! Come on, now, little Ryder…keep your stories straight!” Baby: “I stomp you like big dog! You suck! Fucker!” Blaze: “Holy cow! Where’d you learn a word like that?” Baby: “Shut up, whore! I want belt now! I cry ‘til I get it!” And with that, he does indeed start to cry. Beelzebozo: “All right, all right, I’ve had enough of this. Get him out of here.” The Quiet One picks up the crying “baby” and carries him away. His cries slowly fade away. Blaze: “And people want those things?” Beelzebozo: “I know. Crazy, isn’t it?” Blaze: “Do you think there’s any hope for him? Will he ever grow out of that phase?” Beelzebozo: “I can’t say, dear. Maybe if he learns about sacrifice, honor, dignity, and respect for his betters, there will be hope for him. If not…well, he’ll always be a crying little baby.” Blaze: “Maybe it’d be easier to just toss him in the fire?” Beelzebozo: “You know what? I think it would!” Blaze throws her arms around him and kisses him passionately. Blaze: “I love happy endings!” And the two embrace as the lights fade out. Suddenly a spotlight comes on, and the midget appears, taking a bow. Then the Quiet One appears and bows. Finally, from either side, Beelzebozo and Blaze appear, hold hands, and bow. The four move to stand side by side, grab each other’s hands, and bow once more. The spotlight goes out. The normal lights come back on, and only the World Champ is standing there. Beelzebozo: “I hope you all enjoyed our little morality play today. I hope that midget didn’t frighten you. I know those noises were hideous. “Of course, he was still less annoying and whiny than the real Ryder.” He chortles. “Poor Ryder. He’s not worthy of another title shot and he just can’t stand it, can he? Whining seems to have become his new favorite pastime. Let’s take stock, shall we?” He begins to tick off items, holding up a finger for each one. “One—he REALLY beat me in our first match, and not my mime friend. Whatever. That one’s been hashed out to death, and I’m not bringing it up ever again. “Two—I got lucky during my title defense against him. I thought he’d finally let this go, not saying I cheated anymore. I thought he had accepted his folly in not getting me disqualified in that match, which he could have done, and moved on. But instead, he’s come up with a new complaint. “Three—this upcoming match isn’t for the belt. He still seems to think he’s worthy of such an honor. And no doubt, when I beat him tomorrow, he’ll claim he was half-assing it because he didn’t REALLY care about the match since it was non-title.” He drops his hand and sighs. He leans on the back of the couch and shakes his head. “It’s so sad to see a grown man act like a big baby. Everything is someone else’s fault. He can’t accept his own limitations, his own imperfections. No, in his megalomaniacal mind he’s perfect, and forces out of his control are conspiring against him. Poor…baby.” He points his finger directly at the camera. “Ryder, listen up and listen well. You need to take heed of the moral of our play today. Accept the fact that you’re not as good as me, and move on to other things. There are other belts you might be able to win. But as long as I hold this belt, you will never be World Champion. Deal with this, and move on. “You say you’re going to beat me tomorrow, maybe even kill me? I say you’re writing checks that are sure to bounce. You can’t beat me, little man. You never WILL beat me. And you CERTAINLY won’t kill me.” He twitches, and stares up and to the right. “You can’t kill what’s already dead inside.” He shakes his head and returns his gaze to the camera as if nothing unusual has happened. “No, what’s going to happen is that, once again, you’ll be left beaten in the ring, and I will be adding another notch to my belt. And you’ll be forced to accept the fact that you are nowhere near good enough to challenge me for the World Title. Nor will you have any good excuses for trying to weasel your way into Dunn’s title shot. “I’m telling you Ryder…you need to just accept reality. Accept your inferiority to me. Accept that you aren’t World Champion material. Accept your inevitable defeat. Acceptance is the key, Ryder. “After tomorrow, I’m done with you. I’ll be focusing on more worthy opponents. And you can go back to getting drunk and raping drunk sluts and beating up cameramen. You know, things you’re actually GOOD at.” He stands up to his full heights and crosses his arms. “You can do all the Stompin’ with the Big Dawgs you want, Ryder…it won’t make a lick of difference. As in the past, so in the future… “The Last Laugh is always mine.” Aaaaaaand…fade to black.
