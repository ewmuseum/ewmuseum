UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #451

Little Nicky... CORPORAL STYLE!

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We interrupt your regularly scheduled program to bring you this message from the Corporal of Punishment. Newscaster: Hello and good evening to this Prime Time Channel 25 Wrestling News Channel Special Edition. I am Stan Blowhard with the latest breaking news in the world of professional wrestling. Newscaster: He has come out of the shadows. That is right, Nick Persia has finally made two appearances in the last two weeks of being a no show at defending his credibility as a wrestler. This sudden appearance was brought about by two separate appearances of the one and only Corporal Punishment, Newscaster: Corporal Punishment, one of the most dangerous men in wrestling today, returned to the ring a little over ten days ago. You may have remembered this man of horrific proportions, when he was aligned with one of the most famous wrestling stables in history, The Youngblood's. The original stable headed by notorious Lew Spectre. Spectre defined wrestling for years to come. Newscaster: It was the UWF who sanctioned this evenings match between the Legend Corporal Punishment and Hardcore Champion Nick Persia in a non-title match. And it was the Corporal of Punishment who has been very vocal about his plans with Nick Persia. Newscaster: Some say that this should have been a title match, but there has been no complaints from either camp thus far. We are sure that this will not be the only meeting of these two men and the wrestling world is eagerly awaiting to see the result from this under-card event. Newscaster: With that said, let us go to our correspondent on the street, Willy Nelson Bogart... Newscaster: Bogart? ... ... Bogart: Hello Stan. I am Willy Bogart from Channel 25 Wrestling News Channel. I am standing out in front of 235 Crawford Street. As you can see behind me, several police officers and an ambulance were called to this address when an elderly woman by the name of Beulah Smith called the 9-1-1 operator with this disturbing information. We have a copy of this dreadful conversation. The television fades to black as it prints out the audio conversation that is being played. Operator: Nine one one... Woman: Yes. Quick! Come as fast as you can! My husband... He is bleeding badly! That Bastard has killed my husband! You have to come quick! Operator: Please calm down. Did you say your husband is dead? Or is he alive? Woman: He is alive! But he has been beaten to a bloody pulp! That man beat my husband down over a game of checkers! He is dying! PLEASE HELP HIM!! The television fades from black to the correspondent standing in front of the Corporals home. Bogart: We must cut the audio to bring you up to date to what is happening. The police are right now escorting a man out of 235 Crawford Street Stan. It is none other than The Wrestling legend himself CORPORAL PUNISHMENT! It appears that he is not handcuffed, and the officers are smiling as they escort him towards this very spot. Newscaster: Willy... Can you interview someone there to find out what is going on? Bogart: I do not know Stan, but I will definitely try. The camera follows Willy Bogart as he rushes towards the crowd composed of the Corporal and several law enforcement agencies. Bogart: Excuse me please! Willy Bogart from Channel 25 Wrestling news! Can I please get a comment on what is taking place! We first heard that a 9-1-1 call was sent out only a few minutes ago and a elderly man was brutally beaten in this houses basement! Can you please comment Mr. Punishment? Willy Bogart raises his microphone up to the Corporals smiling face. Before he speaks directly to the camera, he is seen laughing with the officers present. Corporal: Why yes, you can get a comment from me. I have nothing to hide. I have been working out in my basement, minding my own business. When this elderly man comes running down my basement steps with a thirty three pound sledge hammer! In the background the police officers shake their heads in the affirmative to show that they agree fully with the Corporal's statements. Corporal: He was screaming about how I looked at his dear wife in a sexual manner. And then he hit me right here in my little pinky with that deadly weapon! Here look for yourself! The Corporal lifts up his right hand and shoves his right pinky into the camera, the finger appears to have a small scratch on the tip of it. Corporal: You see that! I might have a broken finger from this maniac! Anyway, he slung that hammer with all of his might until it rebounded off my finger and hit him square in the chin. He fell back... hit his head on a shelf I had back in my basement, and then fell out in the floor. The next thing I knew, he was convulsing all over the place and his convulsions caused items to fall off their shelves. Things like an iron I used back in 1978 to press my uniforms... a anvil that was securely mounted on a table given to me by my great grand mother. And a boxing glove filled with sand and cement fell off the wall and hit him at least seventy times in the face, neck, abdomen, and groin. Corporal: Thank God these fine men in uniform came in there to bring this man back into this world! I am just glad I will be able to fight tonight. Bogart: WOW! What a story Stan! So tell me Mr. Punishment. Is there anything you want to say to the wrestling world? Corporal: Why yes there is...Nick Persia. You are quite the commentator aren't you. You wait till the very last minute to make a comment in hopes to sneak in a victory over the Corporal of Punishment. You have a lot to learn my next little victim. You really have a whole lot to learn. Corporal: But you do not understand anything about the way I work little man. You see, I have not called you out for that candy title on your waist. Why would I want that? It is meaningless to me. I am the greatest IC champion who ever lived! I am the greatest enforcer wrestling has ever seen. And what are you? NOTHING! Corporal: You are really going to learn how to take a beating my fine fuzzy friend. You talk very eloquently to be in this sport. But after tonight, I promise you that you will have a speech impediment. One that will probably give you the right to park up front at WAL-MART. You will be able to drive those little senior citizen buggies all around the diaper isle my friend. So enjoy your moment of fame. Because when I am through with you, you will only go by the name Little Nicky... CORPORAL STYLE!
