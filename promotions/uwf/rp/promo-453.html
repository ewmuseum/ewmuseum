Out with Persia & In with Dunn... CORPORAL STYLE

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The world was able to witness a preamble of possibly the greatest match up in the history of wrestling. It was not a world title match, nor was it a main event. But it should have been one of the greatest battles the wrestling world has seen for quite sometime. There was no pay per view showing for this event either. Instead this was an under card match for a regular Sunday house show. But were the powers that be, ready for a brawl like Punishment versus Persia? I doubt it. The UWF was able to witness a true gladiator event. Two men step toe to toe with one another. Neither showing fear, and neither worrying about their own safety. The audience of this past SAS were leaving the arena with a feeling of satisfaction. That is what wrestling is about. And this feeling only comes from beating the living crap out of someone or something. The Corporal barely survived this first rue test. Nick Persia decided to do the unthinkable. He began the match by using a chair. In his appearances, he positioned himself to be one who was above interference, using foreign objects, and fighting dirty. And what was the first thing he did... The match starts out quick as Nick Persia decides to gain an early advantage on Cpl Punishment by hitting him with a chair during his entrance. Persia hits Cpl. Punishment with a couple of more shots to the back before they enter into the ring where Persia leaves the chair out on the floor and the referee calls for the bell The Corporal was obviously taken off guard by this under-handed tactic. Especially after these words came out of Nick Persia's mount when he was speaking to Ben, in an earlier promotion: Ben: 'So you are basically approving what he does?' Nick: 'No, I am not! I said I was admiring him. I was admiring the guts he has. But that doesn't mean the methods he uses are unheard of! But you need to earn your stripes. And although the most honest person might get the most sympathy, those people are often the guys who will never make it. Sometimes, a bit of cheating is for the better.' Ben: 'You got me confused here. You sympathize what he does, but you don't approve it?' Nick: 'Exactly. Deep in my heart even I would want to be him. He is ruthless, doesn't care about rules and has charisma. But I know what he does is wrong. He wins by lying, stealing and cheating. That's something you don't do. You don't cash in at somebody else his expense... Any man who prides himself in being honest, any one who prides himself in shunning tactics that include lying, stealing, and cheating, do not take a chair and slam it into another mans forehead prior to entering the ring for a non title regularly sanctioned match. This last fight was not a hardcore match, yet Nick Persia used those same tactics he criticized the Corporal for doing. In this one match, Nick lied about not lying. He lied about cheating, and he attempted to end the career of the greatest heel in wrestling history. What does that mean? That means that somewhere deep inside of Nick Persia, there lies a inner yearning to use those tactics that he supposedly abhors. Thee is some hope in Nick Persia. The DARK SIDE is within him. He just wants to hide it from those he associates with. One day, these feelings will come out. And the Corporal sees these feelings. It is clear that Nick Persia has some similarities of the Corporal. With that said, the Corporal is now having TO prepare for a new task. One that includes the name Joshua Dunn. A heel no less. Where have all the faces gone? Where does the line get drawn in the sand? Are there no worthy do gooders left in this world? Are they so timid that the UWF must resort to destroying men who have the same honor system in the ring? This is a shame. But the Corporal will gladly enjoy inflicting pain on any man who is willing to step into the ring against him. It doesn't really matter who that person is, as long as they bleed. If they bleed then there is an opportunity to entertain the crowd of PUNISH-ITES who show up each and every week. So sit back and get ready for another episode of Days of our lives Corporal Style! Because the Corporal has now entered the building. *Staggering into the dressing room, the Corporal appears fatigued. Sweat pouring of oF his body... three giant knots are seen on the back of his head where Nick Persia took a chair and crushed his head with it. The Corporal is hurt. He is not seriously hurt, but hurt none the less. It is within seconds that the arena's medical staff rush into his dressing room to administer their assistance to him. *His eyes repeatedly roll back in his head as he loses consciousness. The age of the corporal is beginning to show. *Quickly, they insert an IV into his arm. His blood pressure is monitored as they attempt to bring the Corporals body back inline with what it should be. *After a few minutes of medical assistance, they slowly back away from the Corporal. The look of relief can be seen in their eyes as the Corporals pulse returns to normal. The sweating stops, and the Corporals eyes stop rolling back in his head. *They hand the Corporal a large ice pack for his head, and they place a large bucket of ice at his side. Nurse: You are going to fine Mr. Punishment. You are just a little bit dehydrated. That is all. Doctor: You need to start drinking more fluids. No more coffee, and no more cola. That is just for starters. Nurse: One thing is for sure, you have a strong heart. That sucker was beating out of control there for a while. I think it would be smart if you were checked for high blood pressure. You are not that young little military man any more Corporal. You need to start thinking about slowing down a bit. It not, you are going be around very long. Doctor: I am going to prescribe for you a few medicines that should regulate your heart beat. these will be a thirty day supply. After this thirty day supply has been consumed by you Corporal, I want you to go see your regular doctor. I think you do have high blood pressure. This will be for your own good. Doctor: Are you listening to me Corporal? *The Corporal nods in agreement. Doctor: Good. Now I can not make you do this, but it would be in your best interest to get this checked out. Hyper-tension can lead to a heart attack. So you should not put it off. You are getting older now and you have to take precautions during this stage in your life. *The Doctor begins scribbling on his pad again and hands the Corporal a second prescription. Doctor: And this prescription is for those nasty knots on your head. A chair shot is very dangerous Corporal, in your case, you will be fine. Just take it easy. *The doctor shakes the Corporals hand that the entire medical staff leave the Corporal to himself. Once the door is closed, the Corporal slowly stands to his feet and goes over to the mirror in the restroom. He lifts the ice pack off of his head and admires the large knots administered by Nick Persia's chair shots. He grins and then returns to his chair. Corporal: Now that is a beauty! Corporal: But what a freaking fight he put up! Corporal: The kid improvised didn't he. He did what he had to do in order to try to defeat me. That is what this sport is all about. There is no guilt for using all the ammunition at your disposal. Corporal: This is a classic case of a wrestler who wants to be a bad guy. It is clear to me that Nick Persia just watches too many episodes of the National Geographic channel. He wants to dream of eagles and bears humping in the green forests. He has visions of snakes mating caterpillars. It is clear that this job as a good guy is going to turn himself into a flaming homosexual. He actually has the makings of a great bad guy. But, I have heard it all to many times... once a face, always a face. You just can not take the girl out of some guys. Corporal: ... *The Corporal looks down at the ice bucket. He begins to place his right elbow in it as he grins with satisfaction. Corporal: This Sunday is already starting to make me bored. But I guess I need one week of light work after this unexpected battle from Persia. That is probably a good thing. I' will get a few days of rest, and then I will go do something productive with my new neighbors... *The Corporal looks towards the ceiling and begins to think about his schedule for this week. Corporal: I guess I could go visit Mr. Smith at the local jail. He could use someone to talk to. I can call the jail and get a non contact visit with him for Thursday. We can chat a little bit about how prison life is for the old geezer. Or I can tell him how my finger is getting better.... Maybe we can talk about how his wife is looking at me from their bedroom window. *The Corporal begins to chuckle out loud. Corporal: Man am I going to raise some trash this week. I bet I can get him brought up on more charges after I get to talk to him. Corporal: That is what he gets for beating me in checkers... CORPORAL STYLE!
