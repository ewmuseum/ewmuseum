UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #197

Some very, very sick people out there

28 Jan 2004

Ben Jericho — UWF Car Park

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[What the hell is going on? I mean, first of all Jericho get's the call to some and work a few dark matches because the UWF are in the area, next minute he's being asked to work opening card matches and jobbing to... christ... people like Moraless. It's a hard knock life for someone who recently picked up awards for his work in Japan. Like people really care about that type of thing.] [Probably the worst insult though is booking Jericho... the Spectacle of Technical Wrestling, in a THC Title match. A GARBAGE WRESTLING MATCH? Does our anti-hero look like the type of yutz who'd actively go out his way to not perform crisp submission holds and instead pound on a useless oaf with a chair? What kind of warped match making are we looking at here? Not since they days of the psycho-analyst Freud has anyone seen such a wanton display of sick perversity!] [Alas, it is a title though, and being in the UWF for all of three weeks, it's awfully good of someone to recognise talent when they see it. Here's hoping that the match becomes contested with a little degree of decorum - perhaps allow rope breaks maybe? How about having some shoot fight rules added to the title when Jericho wins it. After all - he will. The only forseeable way that Jericho won't win it is if he decides not to turn up.. and that isn't an option] Jericho: (to someone by his side) I'm not turning up [Damn.... spoke to soon me thinks] (The camera's show the huge UWF Offices situated Over There, Somewhereville. Jericho's hair seems to be shorter than when he last appeared on television, and he's looking slightly scruffy - not the pristene look he had when he once teamed with Kenny Mercury. To his left is someone we've not met before - Minoru Kusanagi a.k.a MINORU from shortly lived shoot fight company EVOLUTION: HYBRID WRESTLING. He trains at Jericho's EVO Dojo's in Yokohama Japan, developing his MMA skills more and more. He looks a sharp character, definatley an idol in Japan due to his boyish good looks and charm. Jericho pulls out a sports bag from the back of his flash car - looking like something out of Japanese Anime 'Initial D'. You'd expect Jericho to be racing around in this thing, doing powerslides and drifts - tbut that's not the point of this promo now, is it?) MINORU: (Stunned) But why? I mean, this is worth wrestling for! Your first UWF title in about a year, so why not take it? It's Bruce Gordy you're up against. Hardly someone challenging (Jericho turns around slowly and cocks his head to the side, looking an MINORU as if he was an idiot) Jericho: Do I look like a god damn garbage wrestler? No.. I am a technical wrestler. I don't 'do' barbed wire baseball bats anymore. That part of me has died off.... (mutters) I hope.... MINORU: (Eager) Who knows what you can do with the title though! Perhaps you could re-define it's meaning. I mean, it did absorb the Shoot Fighting title; maybe you can make it a technical wrestling title again. Jericho: (Dropping his sports bag and adopting a sarcastic overtone to his voice) Yeah - OR.... I could do something else with my time and not waste it on a sad case like Bruce Gordy. Do you know people were actually SURPRISED he won that title. He doesn't seem to endear him to many wrestlers. In fact, I think they view him as somewhat of a joke. MINORU: Really? Jericho (Grinning): Well he's holding a joke title isn't he! A title that once had some esteem when I beat Al Crud for it. Hell, maybe I ought to actually concentrate on the title instead of mocking it. It has had some history. MINORU: It was held by a clown... Jericho: (ponders).... shit, then there is no help for it then really once you think about it. I think the best course of action is to stay away from WNU. I mean, that's the only way it's going to get SOME recognition these days without tarnishing my reputation - no show, and then the title has an element of mystery behind it. 'Why did Jericho no show?'. People will think I fear the title - me and you realise what it should be: chocolate wrapped in tin foil. (As usual, Jericho's strong words are met with some uncertainty as the look in his eyes shows some form of will to actually compete and win the title. After all, the only thing he won in the UWF was the World Title - that was meant to pave his way to superstardom. It never did) MINORU: What about Gordy? Jericho: Gordy Shmordy, I've beaten guys like that a dime a dozen. What really intrigues me is who the hell placed me in this match. That's someone I'd love to wrap my hand around (MINORU looks on puzzled) Jericho: His throat, you homophobic Japanese fool! (MINORU looks on relieved now, showing signs that perhaps he is a homophobe... not that he hates gay people, just doesn't want them to try and seduce him. Jericho saunters around the car park as he readies himself to enter the UWF offices) Jericho: Well Min-Min; another day, another contract being signed. This, the PWW.... it's going to be a very busy time period for me as of late MINORU: But you've still not come to any resolution about tonight! What you going to do? (Jericho turns around and grins) Jericho: Min-Min... I may actually have a plan (With that, MINORU looks on confused as Jericho picks up his sports bag and looks up and the huge eye-sore that is the UWF Offices. He leaves MINORU near the car as he begins his walk to the front doors, ready for yet ANOTHER *groan* chapter in the UWF... and who knows... maybe he'll bloody stay long enough this time!) END TRANSMISSION
