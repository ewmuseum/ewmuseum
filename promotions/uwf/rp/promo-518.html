UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #518

Motivation

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Persia is sitting on a chair behind his desk as he is writing a letter as it appears. But when we close in we see he is filling in some applications for a magazine. However he is interrupted as somebody knocks on the door. He pushes his chair away from him and he walks to the door. He opens the door and to his suprise he sees Ben Johnson, report for the Amsterdam Times.] Nick Persia: “Hello Ben.” Ben Johnson: “Hello Nick. Let me be the first to congratulate you on your Stable Wars victory. Excuse me for my impatiece, but for which title are you going now?” Nick Persia: “Doesn’t it look like losers are profiting more from Stable Wars tahn the winners? And don’t you agree with me that when you lose a title you don’t go for a title the show after you lost a title? Don’t you agree with me you don’t put all titles on the line the show after a PPV where I have fought two gruelling matches? Don’t you agree with me we should have received a standing ovation for our accomplishments? Don’t you agree everybody in UWF is against The Player’s Club? I will tell you why they all talk trash about us. Because they are jealous of the success we have. Corporal Punishment is a good example. He just gives Dunn a new hair style after we beat Corporal Punishment. We beat them all and they still pretend like our victory is a fluke.” Nick Persia pauses for a moment as he takes a deep breath. Nick Persia: “It annoyes me so many people can’t just shut up and admit defeat like a man. I admit I was pinned by Corporal Punishment for the second time. One, two, three! I became one with the mat. But what I do not understand is how people can say that Joshua Dunn’s victory is not deserved. I heard people saying we only won because there was an interference and more of that crap. They have no right to take something away from Joshua Dunn’s victory.” Ben Johnson: “Well, I agree with you on some points. I think it’s not really logical that two people who have lost at the pay per view now receive a shot at your title. Especially when the Television Title is seen as a less valuable title. But I also have to state that Seymour did win his first match.” Nick Persia: “I won two matches. I sacrificied myself for the interest of the team. Just like Kevin Starr. Just like Ezra Kazee. And we all know Kevin Starr and Joshua Dunn were arch enemies. Still, they agreed to cooperate and kept promise. That’s what makes our victory so special. Seymour on the other hand won just one match. He didn’t participate too much. He was more busy with his game boy then with wrestling. But if you want to win you need to stay focussed. You need to pay attention to what happens around you. Seymour lives in his own world. He has four different personalities. But I wonder how the one personality knows what the other has done.” Ben Johnson: “He remembers, and I think that’s the most important thing. And you can say all you want about opponents who don’t deserve their shot but it doesn’t take away the fact you will need to face them with the title up for grabs.” Nick Persia: “I know. But it doesn’t take away this fact either: they don’t deserve to be in the same ring with me.” Ben Johnson: “Why not? Real Deal has been a UWF television Champion and well Seymour......we all know what he did. Former PTC Global Champion. Former UWF World Champion.” Nick Persia: “Everything he did was in the past. All his reigns are over now. The guy is like Hulk Hogan to me. He just doesn’t know when to quit. A strange thing the whole world can see what I see but nobody dares to say a word about it. He hasn’t done a damn thing since his return, but people still think he is one of the best around. But that’s the past. Let the past be the past. Let’s talk about the future. Seymour Almasy is not the future. He is a guy in the autumn of his career. Occassionally he reaches the level he competed on where he was at his best. But the lever declines more and more when time passes by! He is by far the most experienced wrestler in this match so I shouldn’t take him lightly. After all, underestimation is the worst suprise that can happen to you. However I can’t go out there and think I can’t beat him. Because frankly I can. We never clashed off before so there is no reason at all to assume I can’t beat him. On the other hand there are reasons to assume he can beat me too. In PTC Seymour still accomplishes a lot and he can go with the best of the best. He still is near the top of the mountain. But he isn’t the top anymore. He isn’t what he used to be.” Ben Johnson: “Or is that just a dream on your side perhaps. I get the feeling you are looking for some sort of weakness he don’t posess. Aren’t you talking hope into yourself? Aren’t you pepping yourself up? Because when we look at the facts.....[gets interrupted]” Nick Persia: “When we look at the fact we know that I am the longest reigning UWF hardcore Champion EVER! My record is good, but not the best. Real deal’s record is good but not the best. Yes, Seymour is the top favourite to win this match, yes, Seymour Almasy is the main man in this match. Yes, the people have a lot of faith in him. But I put faith in myself as well. I know what I can do. I know what Seymour can do. I know what Real Deal can do. Let’s take last night as example. UWF Stable Wars XI! Real Deal lost in a Fatal Fourway match against Jay Vyper. So he knows what danger relies in these type of matches. You can lose your title without being pinned!” Ben Johnson: “Are you worried about that? Do you think there is a possibility you will lose your title because of that?” Nick Persia: “That possibility is a possibility because it can happen. So yes, I am worried about that. But let’s not forget I am involved in that match as well. Let’s not forget I have to pin one man as well instead of two. So the deck might be stacked against me since I am the champion but as a competitor the deck is stacked even! And there are so many other things that can happen in that match.” Ben Johnson: “Like Corporal Punishment interfering in your match? Because we all know he has been chasing you and your title for quite some time now.” Nick Persia: “I don’t think so. You see, he will have a hard time handling Joshua Dunn. And when I say hair incident you know what I mean.” Ben Johnson: “Joshua Dunn will be angry I guess. But how was the reaction within The Player’s Club lockerroom?” Nick Persia: ‘Well, I can say we weren’t pleased with Corporal’s action. And we didn’t celebrate our victory because of that. I was thinking last night. I was thinking how The Player’s Club can get rid of Corporal Punishment. And I have been able to think of a plan! Corporal Punishment likes to stick his nose in other people’s business? He likes to go out there and ruin somebody else’s night? Soon he will find out what The Player’s Club likes. He will find out, first hand, how the Player’s Clun handles pathetic people like Corporal punishment. If he had any problems with the Club he could have said it and we could have settled this in the ring. No jokes like this. But he will pay the prize for his behaviour. He thinks a sneak attack like that scares us, The Player’s Club, off? If he really believes that I suggest he opens his eyes quick or otherwise he is in for a very rude awakening.” Ben Johnson: “Talking about The Player’s Club and being a unit: There were some talks about you leaving The Club and that you were only part of The Player’s Club until Stable Wars. Is that true?” Nick Persia: “I am still in The Player’s Club. So I think that is enough information for you to answer that question.” Ben Johnson: “Fair enough. But we weren’t here to discuss the match between Corporal Punishment and Joshua Dunn. I was here to ask you some questions about your match. You have just told us your thoughts about Seymour Almasy and ironically you talked about underestimating him, but don’t you underestimate The Real Deal?” Nick Persia: “Of course I am not. I am facing two opponents. And I think they are both very good. I know The Real Deal has captured the Television Title fairly soon, but he lost it soon as well. So I think he has to prove that win was not a one hit wonder. And when you set a goal for yourself you are always a bit more motivated. When a title is on the lin you are even more motivated. And when your opponents are said to be stronger than you you are even more motivated. So he has no motivation problem for this match. I know he will train his ass of to show the world he can go with the best. And I am not talking about myself now.” Ben Johnson: “And how about your motivation? What is motivating you in this match?” Nick Persia: “I think that is fairly obvious. I want to beat a former Global Champion of course. And I want to prove to the Real deal that I am the better man and that he aims a little too high with competing for The Hardcore Title. And I also want to be the best of the best. As a champion you need to prove that you are truly the deserving champion. I am a tarnished champion. I lost to Corporal Punishment. Now I want to set the record straight be retaining my title against two very good competitors. But we all know that they aren’t good enoguh. Seymour Almasy has to deal with two things. He needs to recover from the beatings he took. And he needs to get himself ready again after the loss he suffered. For the Real deal things are a bit different. At least he gets the feelign the booker still believes in his abilities by booking him higher. He only needs to get himself in top shape.” Ben Johnson: “At least we know one man is prepared for battle. But I am still very curious about something. What title do you chose to compete for?” Nick Persia: “I haven’t made my choice yet. I am forced to compete for The hardcore Championship. And let me give you some comments about what I am going to do: It’s not personal, strictly business. So if I beat you down with a baseballbat it wasn’t personal. If I nail you down with a sledgehammer it wasn’t personal. If I pin you to retain my title it is strictly business.” Ben Johnson: “So you hold no personal grudges against them?” Nick Persia: “No, I do not. I can’t blame them for the mismanagement. I can’t blame them that the booked didn’t pay attention to the show. I am the only winner. I am the only champion. I am the only one to do what was asked. I was the only one up for the task. I shall walk out still the champion and still a winner. Unfortunately for the both of you.” Ben Johnson: “I need to piss. If we can have a commercial break?” [Nick Persia looks suprised when Ben walks away in ahurry, leaving Nick persia before the camera’s. After a few seconds to scene fades to the commercials showcasing what happened at UWF Stable wars 6]
