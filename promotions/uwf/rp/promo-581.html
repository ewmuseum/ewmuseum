UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #581

Oh, It's Comming

28 Jul 2004

The Communist — The lot outside WNU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist walks into the parking garage at the arena, wearing a white tee shirt and jeans, working cloths for a working man. Slung over his shoulder is a 16 lb sledgehammer. He walks up to a car, swings the hammer in a two handed, overhead arch and brings it smashing down on the hood of the car with all his strength, folding the hood almost in half and sending parts of the engin flying. The poor poor interviewer that everyone's favorite communist called for is timidly watching from the side: Interviewer: Umm.. Mr Communist? :The Communist takes a side swing, blowing the windshield out and sending broken glass into the drivers and passangers seats. He turns for a second towards the interviewer: Commie: Ahh, Comrad. How are you. I see you still wear the pin I gave you. Interviewer: Ummm, yes... What are you doing? :Commie kicks the hood off the car and starts beating on the engin block, sending it to the ground, hoses poping, oil and antifreeze covering the ground: Commie: Ohhh, Just venting. Interviewer: Do you know who's car that is? Commie: Not sure, truly. They will probably be upset. :He takes a swing at the door of the car, putting deep dents in it: I also need to make sure this hammer is of good quality. Interviewer: Why is that? Commie: Because Communists don't bring cheap weapons to wars. :The interviewer shivers with the weight of that last comment, but he presses onward: Interviewer: You are understandably upset at the Chaos Society for taking the Soviette. What do you plan on doing to them? :The Communist steps up onto the trunk of the car and sends a crashing hammer blow onto the roof of the car, shattering the remaining windows: Commie: Something very similar to what this sign of bourguasis excess is experiencing right now. It may just take me longer on them. You see, the Chaos Society has made a very very large mistake, comrad. They seem to think that they can do whatever they want because they don't belive in order. :Commie continues to beat on the roof of the car, blow after blow crashing down on the steel, now tearing through the metal, sending sparks and paint chips into the cab: Commie: However, order will be enforced. Maintained, if you will. :he hops off the trunk and beats on the hub caps, sending plastic raining down on the concrete: The world will not exist in chaos. :the tire deflates as the Communist beats on the rim. The Communist walks around to the front and beats on the grill, causing the airbag to deploy. Glass bounces off the front seats.: Interviewer: This will happen soon? :The Communist pulls the rest of the grill off, tossing it aside: Commie: Very soon. Very soon indeed. :Commie smiles, looking at the head of the hammer: Look! Not even scratched. That's quality, wouldn't you say? Beautiful. :A mann in an expensive looking buisness suit runs up to the ruined car: Man: My car! What have you done to my car! :The Communist picks the man up and slams him on the trunk, leaving a new dent in the metal: Commie: Now I feel better. It's time to enforce some order. :the scene fades as The Communist walks into the arena:
