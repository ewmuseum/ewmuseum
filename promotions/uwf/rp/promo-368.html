UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #368

Gauntlet thingy

25 Apr 2004

Digital Mortality — Digital's Beach house

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Congratulations to the new UWF Tag Team Champions, Digital Mortality and Moraless! Just like they said they were going to do, they got a victory over The Player's Club, over Mike Montgomery and Ezra Kazee. It was a very hard fought battle, but in the end Digital Mortality and Moraless prevailed. Once Ezra was put on the Digitalizer, there was nowhere he could go. Just ask anyone that the Digitalizer is probably the best submission hold and the one that has had the most victories in the UWF since it's conception. Ezra just figured out why Digital Mortality and Moraless are a team to be reckoned with. The Resurrection is here to stay, and you better be ready.] [Welcome to the Resurrection to the one and only Bondage the Sex Fiend! The future of the UWF has now united with Digital Mortality and Moraless to make Resurrection a real threat to those who thought we were just here to play games. This man has a win over the World Champ Beelzebozo, what else would you need? Now that Past, Present and Future are united, what we need is one more to make this a real stable to get ready for the biggest event in sports entertainment, Stable Wars. This member will have an important part in history, he will be a man who deserves to team up with us, and I think I know a couple who might work] [Stable Wars, the event that everyone waits for day in and day out just to be a part of. The event that is bigger than anything else on the planet, hell on the universe! I'm sure not even Mentok has seen an event as big and as important as this. The stable that wins decides who gets a title shot and which one. The titles are then defended against the winners, what do anyone else would want than a title shot at the one of your choice? There can be so many combinations to this that it's just making me feel tingly inside. It's time to see if Digital really has it to win Stable Wars. He has been so close to winning it twice, but twice he has been screwed! First against Studog Escobar and The Shark and then against that monkey. Not this year, this year is the year of Resurrection and the team will take it all no matter what.] [Now, is it only me or do all of you think that Joshua Dunn is crazy? He has to be mad to ask for this Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. He alone, against three of the best wrestlers in the UWF? Hardcore match, Submission match, and THEN a Last Man Standing match? That is pure lunacy if ya ask me. Instead of letting this man get to the ring, he should be put away in a mental institution, he is out of his mind. Well, if he wants it, he will get it, and Resurrection will make him realize how much of a mistake this was. Plain and simple.] (The scene opens in Digital Mortality's beach house in Isla Verde, Puerto Rico. Digital Mortality and Burton Bell are sitting in the balcony watching the waves arriving at shore. Digital is wearing a black shirt with black cut-off jeans. He has sunglasses on and his red hair tied up in a ponytail. Burton is wearing a swimming suit and no shirt. They are both drinking margaritas, possibly celebrating the Resurrection's victory over the Player's Club.) DM: How did ya like that Mike? I told ya you two were still the same as before. No match for Digital and Moraless. We went in weighing our usual weight and came back out with 10 pounds more. You see, your Player's Club is nothing when two super powers like Moraless and myself join our forces. Now with the addition of Bondage, are you sure you will be looking forward to Stable Wars? Because I think this will be an easy one. Now, you want to hear something funny? I'll tell you. Remember before our tag match, how you accused me of being a liar? Well, guess you were wrong again. What did I say? Everyone who goes head to head with Digital Mortality get launched into the limelight, because Digital and Moraless made you two look so good that you now have a World Title match. Yes Mike, we made you look good. Without that match you would never have had that chance. But we proved you wrong, we proved you and Ezra both wrong. BB: Yeah, you definately did that. DM: Now, I'll come back to these fools in a moment, but now lets go to the part of The Player's Club that is not a fool, he is downright nuts. Joshua Dunn challenged The Resurrection into a gauntlet match which he calls the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. Dunn, Dunn, Dunn... have you gone mad? You decide to go against la creme de la creme of this federation? You decide that since you are now in the World Title picture you can come here and beat three men in one night? Without any help from your little Club? And I was beggining to think you were the smartest of the three. You are either a complete idiot or you have some kind of plan. Now remember, I know you. I know how you play this gaqme, we have been a team, we have been enemies, hell we know our repertoir in and out, but you are not facing me alone. You have to get Moraless and Bondage before you get to me. I just hope they leave something for me. I still remember what you did to Justine. I still dream about killing you, and on SAS I will teach you a lesson. I will teach you a lesson and give you a beating like you have never had. Remember my words Dunn. I don't care if you are in a stretcher, that Last Man Standing match goes. BB: Oh yeah, you said to remind you about Plague and that Communist guy. DM: Right. Communist... you are too late. That era has gone passed. I was once the supreme communist of this federation. The Iron Curtain was master of the UWF, and we saw a rise in communism so big that we had the CIA after us. You are nothing but an antique. So I suggest you go teach communism elsewhere. Now Plague. I'm glad you remember me. Because I remember you really well. Yes, I remember beating after beating I gave you. I remember how I beat you when you challenged me for the IC title. I remember that you were never as good as me and will never be. You lost your precious THC title and now you are coming for bigger thing? Poor guy. If you want to go one on one with me, all you gotta do is ask. You know that I'd be glad to do it. (Digital stands up and goes to the railing.) DM: Stable Wars is near, and although I have had very bad luck on past stable wars, this will be one to remember. The Resurrection will take all it's opposition down. No matter who it is, Prime Alliance, Anarchy, and even The Player's Club. Mike, just because you were Comissioner, it doesn't give you the right to give yourself a title shot. What a crappy excuse! Something about a sound guy. Not even Dunn believed you! You have the audacity to talk about pleasing the fans and talking about getting them on your side, then you go and award youself a World Title match? Stealing it from Dunn? That is what teamates are all about. You sicken me Mike. You and your Player's Club make me sick. We will see who gets to laugh at Stable Wars. See you then. (Scene fades.) OOC-This is late, but what the hell!
