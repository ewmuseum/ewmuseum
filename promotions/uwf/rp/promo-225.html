UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #225

All hell has broke loose

16 Feb 2004

Anarchy — Raid's Extermination Business

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens with 3 figures walking out of Raids office, 1 woman and 2 men. They turn around and admire Raid's new black and red storefront sign. They are all wearing black with a blood red anarchy symbol on the back.] Woman: Looks good, much better then the old white sign. Black and red definitely is more suitable for Raid. Man 1: That's for sure, and it was almost too late for him, he almost called us too late. Any longer and the vermin here in the UWF would have taken over. [The 3 of them turn around to face the camera; the 2 men are wearing title belts.] Man 2: Hey Joanna, when do we get our first match. In the last 5 years in the indies, not a single team was able to defeat use for these WWA Tag-Team Titles and we were told there was competition here. Joanna: Soon, soon, remember, Raid invited us to help with a large roach problem, a man sized roach named Kazee. After that, then Anarchy will get to spread the chaos. Pryde, Dai, What do you think? Pryde: Kazee, what a man, what's he calling himself? The ladies man? Joanna: Ladies man my ass, more like a mans man. Dai: You mean like the Fab Five? I could definitely see the resemblance. No I think it's the unusual man, or the... Pryde: The Common Man, that's what it is. Dai: S#!%, whatever. Pryde: Kazee, you tasted Total Anarchy, but that is nothing compared to what we will do to you when we get or hands on you next time. And there is nothing the Player's Club will be able to do to change that. Dai: You see, Raid here has told us that you have been badmouthing him for taking so long to get to the big times. But what you don't know is that he's been training with two dangerous veterans. We've held 4 Tag-Team Titles between the 2 of, a Hardcore Title each and run 2 feds to ruins, and we are here to add more titles to our collection. Joanna: Mike Montgomery, you throw around your power like candy, but I got to admit, the UWF officials were stupid to cave to your demands, didn’t they realize that with the wrestlers being the owners of the UWF now he would have sued himself, and no self respecting judge would rule in favor of something as stupid as that. [The 3 reach their Black Durango and pause before getting in.] Pryde: Anyways, Player’s Club and all of the UWF, you have been all put on notice. Heel is breaking loose, and we will continue the beatings till we bring all hell to the UWF. Dai: Oh and Kazee, keep looking over your shoulder. Who knows what can happen when you least expect it. [The 3 climb in to their Durango with Pryde in the driver’s seat. Pryde roles the window down and looks directly into the camera.] Pryde: Beware, the Chaos Society! [Anarchy drives off as the camera fades to black.]
