UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #392

SAS #1

5 May 2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's Dressing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Perhaps fate draws two men together, and flings them apart; maybe the will of God, Allah, Buddha or who ever you worship compels their bidding to be done through the people of earth. Perhaps it is time to shed a little light on the past of the link between Digital Mortality and Joshua Dunn. This is exactly what is intended] [First, there was the Stable no one could forget. The Iron Curtain. Communism at it’s best, sure it still lives on in the Scotsman, brainwashed, the final member to be inducted for the very first UWF Stable Wars. It wasn’t all bad, Digital and Dunn made a great team, their different styles complimented each other perfectly. They never lost a single match as a team, until the Stable Wars final where they were first screwed out of the chance for the coveted trophy. After that defeat, the curtain fell apart] [After Stable Wars, the stable was a shell of itself, and was dragging all concerned down to the dirt. Then to top it all off the worst thing that could have ever happened at that moment did. Digital Mortality became World Champion. Corrupted by the gold belt, he drove those who helped him get to the top away. The first being Joshua Dunn, soon followed by the whole of the curtain. The very struts keeping Digi at the top was pulled out from underneath him, and he too fell from grace. While he was floundering with trying to salvage his career that he himself ruined, Joshua Dunn was on a semi-permanent leave of absence. What happened here is unknown] [Secondly Digital and Dunn were thrown together in yet another stable wars, this time under the banner of Jamie Shock’s Prime alliance. Joshua Dunn being the late place filler to help the stable go for gold, Dunn’s triumphant return. But again glory was whipped away from the, still complimentary, duo as Jamie, seeking to keep his title firmly in his own hands seen to it that the best of the federation didn’t get the chance to come after the world title gold. That back fired on him, but left a bad taste in the mouths of the pair of superstars] [Thirdly, after this, Dunn became IC Champion, holding the place that so many have tried for in the past, where Digital didn’t hold any. Seen as a crowd-attracting voyeur for wrestling fans everywhere, the board of directors thrust them together. Digital was sorely seeking his lost love, while Dunn was pursuing his own career. A dark time passed Dunn while he was IC Champion, Kidnap, blackmail and prison. And to top it all off, he found Digitals Girl, and used her to his own advantage, leaving Digital to save her from the jaws of death and go into a semi-permanent leave of absence, while Dunn stayed in the federation to become the longest reigning champion in the federation ever, breaking Tsunami Oni’s record. Are we beginning to see a pattern emerge?] [Lastly, lastly hasn’t happened yet, on SAS Digital and Dunn team once more. Against Dunn’s colleague Mike Montgomery and Bruce Gordy. It has to be said that the duo have had a turbulent past, but could it all be put to the side for the sake of one match? Unlikely, but it is bound to write another page in the chapter of the book of trust and deceit written in the blood, sweat and tears of these two athletes] [The Scene Opens in Dunn’s dressing room. He’s sitting on the commie sofa, wearing much what you would expect him to be. A red T-Shirt and black jeans, the glint of his necklace shining into the camera lens. The war worn face looks into the camera, his hands clasped together and his elbows resting on his legs. The pose he so often adopts. The camera draws closer to him before he begins to speak] JD: Well SAS should be a blast. I mean what are you expecting to happen? Mike and I beating down Digital just because we can? And humiliating Gordy again because it always happens? Well you’d think wrong. [A smile breaks across the face of Dunn, not the Sideways Grin that we have become used to, but a genuine smile that seems out of place on his features] JD: I know it’s what should happen, but that isn’t good entertainment, it hasn’t been since Roman times. And Humiliating Bruce Gordy? He makes it too easy. By only thinking of “Pain” as a euphemism for sex, the UWF column writers would have material on him for months. So what instead I hear you ask? Instead, we have a wrestling match, I’m dying to see what new tricks Monty has learned to help him defeat Beezlebozo, or if it is indeed fluke. I want to know exactly how well Digital and I act as a team after all these months. I want the fans to get what they pay for. Entertainment [The smile fades away] JD: But I don’t want to be made a fool of. This is only on the god will of others that this is thought for. The minute there’s any hint of backstabbing, all that sportsmanship goes out the window. But I think I still know Digital better than that. He has morals. It’s not his style, or at least it wasn’t. But until Sunday I have other matters to attend to, I shall bid you farewell [The Scene Fades] [And the scribes start to write the next passage]
