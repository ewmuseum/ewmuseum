UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #157

CoC RP#2

18 Dec 2003

Joshua Dunn — UWF Complex

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Well one day closer to finding the best of the best, or the person with the greatest number of favours to pull in. In any case the Contest of Champions is another day closer. A few people have given their short views on what they think, Moraless, Ezra Kazee and Joshua Dunn. Everyone else involved have still to speak out to the viewing public, with only a few gracing the screens with their faces. Still no word from any one of the Prime Alliance Members, although some lesser known names have been making an impressive stand. What of Joshua Dunn though? Well, after the all but brief visit to his home in California, we haven’t heard much else, but what else can happen just a day before the cut off mark of all UWF Transmissions?] [The Scene Opens in the backstage area of the UWF, The camera pans over the corridor showing the red door on the opposite wall, the yellow embossed letters Spelling out the name “Joshua Dunn”. The camera moves in through the door and into the dressing room of said wrestler. Joshua is sitting in the room, plonked in front of the rolled in TV screen and video recorder, sprawled over the sofa, remote in hand. This seems unusual for the Scotsman who is normally active and doing something useful. Sitting on the coffee table just to the side of the TV is a cardboard box, in this box is a number of video cassettes which are labelled with the names of the participants involved, one thing in common with them all, is the name Joshua Dunn, and another thing that should be obvious to all the members of Joshua Dunn’s Fan club, is that they are all the matches in which he lost. On the TV just now, is the latest match, which he lost, between himself and Digital Mortality for the IC title three months previously] [Dunn looks on transfixed, another barrier being attacked, the barrier that holds back another necessary trait for the furtherance of his influence. The one no one likes to talk about, but everyone would be lost without. Humility Who can say that they have never suffered the beating loss that make them look like an Idiot, Who can say that they’ve never had to retract what they’ve said because of another persons actions, Who hasn’t been the butt of everyone’s jokes? No one. Humility is another defining trait, making a person stronger for the next time they face someone, to remind them that they aren’t perfect, and are as susceptible to the world as everyone else. Looking on at the video Dunn knows this, as he has done with the rest of his careers loses, that this makes a person attain and retain what is great about themselves, to accept Humility and make it a part of them selves. There is always room for improvement] [Dunn shifts position on the sofa as the first fall of his IC title is realised and the three count yelled out in unison with the referee and the crowd. He presses a button on the remote and the screen reduces to a dot in the centre of the TV and then fades to nothing as he switches it off. He slumps over staring at the floor, coming to terms with defeat, understanding where he went wrong, unlocking the door to that hidden part of his character, locked away all those months ago. His curiosity a battering ram to the steel wall of communism implanted in his head, chipping away at the wall, breaking through] [Brainwashing can’t last forever, especially drug induced ones, as it requires a stream of the intoxicant to keep the meaty organ in the head believing the pink clouds implanted by the other person. Technically the NVA’s influence should have worn off months ago, it’s remarkable it has lasted this long. Even though, Dunn still fights for his right for individuality, unlocking the memories taken from him by those who had no right to] [A crack, a crack in the wall, peering through, he can see what he has been searching for, a glimpse of those memories, the wall fracturing, but repaired, almost but not quite, just one figure managed to escape from his passed, His Father, and a series of numbers with no meaning. Failure] [Or was it? As Dunn looks up to the ceiling exasperated, the numbers re-arrange in his head forming something of sense, a lead, a lead to his past, but where will it lead, what could it mean for his search for who he is? Time will tell] [Dunn reaches across and lifts up the telephone beside the sofa, who ever said good things come to those who wait? He rapidly dials in the numbers as they come from his head and ushers the cameraman out of the room with his hand as the dial tone rings out, reaching for the link to his blocked doff past] [Now finding itself facing the door where it started the broadcast, the Scene Fades to Black]
