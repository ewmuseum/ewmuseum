Flash from the past

28 Jul 2004

Joshua Dunn — Joshua's house

[And It Was No More It is officially over for the Players Club, the return of their founder signified its demise. Although before hand Ezra Kazee made it blatantly obvious that he was going to be challenging for the World Title Belt now held by his former Partner Joshua Dunn, The shaking foundations have finally collapsed. One thing that can be said for the returning Mike Montgomery is that he knows how to make a return performance; he has already booked Joshua’s first title defence on the up coming Terror in Tokyo PPV after failing to book one for himself. A four way match up of most Players Club members for the World title. It didn’t take long for them to want to use Joshua as a stepping stone to greatness again, but Dun isn’t one to be stood upon.] Joshua lay in a near dream state, that version of sleep just before you wake up where you start having hallucinations of some sort. His eyes twitched as all sorts of things popped into his head for an instant, soon to be forever lost to his memory, never to be remembered once he reached full consciousness. It was strange, his eyes flickered, and even opened a few times, but he was still asleep. But gradually he awoke to the world around him, tossing and turning the whole way. It was a fresh morning when Joshua Awoke, he stretched as he started to realise where he was, his room was so warm and musky that he didn’t really want to get up at all, but that feeling wasn’t one to last, he had to get up now he was awake, he couldn’t lie in bed without the intent of going to sleep, once he was awake that was it, he had that quirk since he was a small child, and some times it was useful, but when he woke at times like two in the morning, it did have it’s downside. There was so much to do this fresh new day. He was waiting to receive a very important letter, one that could change his whole life, for better or worse, and the longer the letter took to get to him the longer he would have to decide which he wanted more, a new life in America or to go to University. His parents were against the idea of him leaving home for the states, his parents were against him leaving full stop. It wasn’t that he wanted his independence, he didn’t have to leave the country for that, it was just that America was the symbol of his goal. To be honest with himself he didn’t want to go to university, law held no interest to him, and politics was a bore, he could easily do the course he knew, but he would be doing it for his father more than for himself. His father wanted nothing more than to see his eldest son follow in his footsteps, but Joshua had other ideas. He was a thinker he knew it, many times he had just frittered away the hours by just having a discussion with himself, but he didn’t like that life style. He was a big man, and enjoyed physical exertion. He wanted to be a wrestler, and America just seemed the perfect place to do it, all he needed was the work permit. It had been a month now since he applied for a visa, he was only still in Scotland because he was hedging his bets, just in case it all fell through, he could easily pick himself up and wonder down the other path for his life that was standing in front of him. He picked himself up off of the bed and trotted down the stairs, it was still quite early in the morning, but he knew by this time the post man had already been and gone, and by the smell coming from the kitchen, his mother knew he was up too. He plodded into the kitchen bright eyed and bushy tailed, at the breakfast table was his father, slumped behind a newspaper, his siblings still in the land of nod up stairs. His mother busied herself over a large range, the eggs and bacon sizzled in the frying pan, he ran his hand through his hair, as always there was an annoying patch that was sticking high up in the air, he’d sort that later, far more important things lay on the table, the morning post. Holding his anticipation at bay he casually sat down at the opposite side of the table from his father, where he always sat and picked up the pile of envelopes. He riffled through them and picked out the ones addressed to him, but only one held his attention, the one that was stamped for airmail and had an American postage stamp on it. As he began to open the envelope, his father flicked the news paper, little actions like that Joshua picked up on, his father wasn’t too best pleased with his choice for life, he shunned all forms of physical contact, even though he was a big man in his own right, he just had no interest in it. Out came the letter enclosed, he cast his eyes over it and a wide smile broke across his face, the news was good, no that’s not true, the news was FANTASTIC, he couldn’t contain himself any longer JD: I’m going to AMERICA! His father only coughed, but his mother acted encouragingly, as she always did, it was her default setting, she turned around from the breakfast and cast a sorrowful smile to Joshua, she was just as disappointed for Joshua wanting to leave the country, but for completely different reasons than his father, she just didn’t want to see her little boy leave home. Mrs Dunn: Congratulations! I knew you would be Joshua sprang from the table and gave his mother a huge embrace, and actually lifted her off her feet and swung her around full circle, she squealed at that, she wasn’t used to being swept off her feet, his father still kept his attention to the paper, giving the pages another flick before turning the page, he was sorry to see his son go just as much as his mother was, but he showed it in his own way. Thumping came from up stairs, accompanied by staggered responses of “Shut Up” from his siblings up the stairs, they didn’t share his view about early mornings, although they did have the same quirk of not being able to lounge around in bed, they would be up soon enough. His father spoke for the first time since Joshua entered the kitchen, and used a tone of disapproval, even though his actual words were meaning the complete opposite, and he kept his nose in the news paper the entire time Mr Dunn: Well done son, I’m proud of you This Hurt Joshua badly, he loved his father, like any son does, he couldn’t bear to see him so unhappy about his own success. He still held his mother in his arms, and she used this to her own advantage by grabbing his head with both hands to pull it into her glare Mrs Dunn: Now listen to me! You have a beautiful mind, don’t let it go to waste over there you hear me? Make me proud JD: I will, do not worry He turned to look at his father again, he was still reading the paper, he never really showed his emotions to his family or to anyone else, it was a strange roll model to have around the house, but his disinterest was really hurting Joshua, his eyes started to water, but he fought them back, his father frowned on that too, but when he got to America, he might invite his father over, it would be nice to see a familiar face over there, but he had his doubts whether an invitation would be accepted. But there was so much to plan, so much to do, he had to book a flight, he had to pack… so much to do, he was so excited, the sadness melted away to the back of his mind as he started to busy himself. It was at that moment that his cell phone went off, possibly one of his friends to congratulate him… Wait how could they possibly know about it already… Hold on, he didn’t own a cell phone…Come to think of it, what IS a cell phone? *** Joshua awoke in his room of his house in one of the milder states of America, his room was still clammy and warm, just like he was used to, the smell reminded him of something but he couldn’t put his finger on it, as he regained full consciousness he forgot all that had just went through his head, although he had remembered that he had been dreaming, or was he remembering and that was irritating him, those damn pills were interrupting his sleep again, and he couldn’t get back to sleep again, a quirk he had, he wasn’t sure why, through his groggy mind something else registered, he wasn’t too sure what it was, but as the fog cleared he heard the irritating ring tone of a mobile phone, he wished his neighbours would answer the damn thing, he could rise and shut the window, but it wasn’t going to do much good. Wait, the window was shut, the noise was coming from inside the room, and it was his cell phone… Hold on, he didn’t own a cell phone As that thought went through his head, he sat bolt up right and looked round the room, he could hear the ringing, it was coming from the closet, he sprang from his bed, and the shorts he slept in keeping his decency as he darted across the room to the wardrobe. He opened it wide and looked through it, as the door opened the noise got louder; he could now hear the vibrations of the phone as well, and even more importantly, he could feel the vibrations through the door, he took off the trench coat jacket from the hook on the door and held it up, the jacket was vibrating, the phone was inside, he delved into the pockets and soon found the bright pink phone that was in there. Even if he had forgotten that he had bought a cell phone, this definitely wasn’t his, he looked at the colour screen and saw the ominous looking text that read “Home” he paused for a moment to contemplate exactly where “Home” was, but he couldn’t answer that unless he answered. He hit the green button on the key pad and lifted the phone to his ear JD: Hello? Female Voice: So sorry did I wake you? JD: Who is this? A pause came over the person on the other end of the line; she obviously didn’t know what to say Female Voice: We met in… the park And recollection fell onto Joshua, this was the woman he saved from those two layabouts that night, he still wasn’t sure why he came to be there at that exact time and place, but what happened, happened, she must have slipped her phone into his pocket when she asked him his name JD: Ah yes, I remember now, what do you want? Female Voice: Well I would like to take you out to dinner, to thank you properly for what you did for me, I don’t think I could ever express how grateful I am JD: Believe me you have Joshua didn’t want thanks or praise; he did what he was believed he was brought up to do, the right thing Female Voice: You never even told me your name, I deserve that at least JD: I did tell you my name Female Voice: Yah right! I’m supposed to believe that I was saved by wrestling Superstar Joshua Dunn, Har Dee Har Har The way she spoke that, she really did believe that it wasn’t him, that’s possibly why she slipped the phone into his pocket. He sighed, he didn’t ask for this, he was still tired, he didn’t get much sleep last night, too much to think about, to much to try and remember, he was sure the pills prescribed by that doctor was causing his irregular sleep patterns JD: Believe what you want, I’m going to hang up now Female Voice: WAIT, no, you still need to give me my phone back, just agree to come to dinner with me, that’s all, it’s just something to say thank you Dunn sighed again, what harm could it do? What would be the worst that would happen? JD: Alright, you win, where is it then this place you want me to come to? Female Voice: Alfonse’s restaurant, it’s just off the main street JD: Ok, I’ll see you there when? Female Voice: Say Saturday, about 8:30? JD: Fine Female Voice: It’s a date then, Goodbye, thank you again JD: Ok, you’re welcome, goodbye And with that he hung up the phone and put it back into his coat’s pocket and put it back in the closet, closing the door, what had he just let himself in for? Oh how bad could it be? It was only dinner; he needed to get out more often anyway, more importantly, what did he just think of while he was still in bed? That would annoy him for the rest of the day, perhaps it would come to him later, he’d have to wait and see. [And it had begun]
