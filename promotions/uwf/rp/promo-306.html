UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #306

You mean this is on TV?

4 Jan 2004

Right To Party — Smokey's Appartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OK, let me see now… Ah yes day 5 of round one air time and only a fraction of the contestants have actually been on air for the Cruiserweight tournament in PTC, and the Promotion Deadline is closing in. Meanwhile there is something rather strange going on in a certain apartment block that may be of some interest…] [The scene opens for a few moments, in that few moments, we can see a lone high-rise apartment block up outside a large green expanse. It’s roughly dusk time, and a few lights are on in the apartment building, and a few birds sing innocently in the trees. A peaceful scene in almost suburban cityscape, when all of a sudden, Dance music starts blaring out from one flat, dogs screech, the birds scarper and one flat’s lighting goes out and is replaced with multicoloured disco lighting, accompanied by a few screams and yells from excited individuals, the scene fades out but the music and accompanying sounds stay in the picture] [The Scene fades back in again, the music now gets much louder and this time we’re outside a door. The door has pretty shoddy paintwork and the room number’s fallen off, but a label has been tacked half heartedly to it with two thumb tacks, written in pen is the message “Admittance by invitation only”. The camera pans out a bit to show that a well-dressed muscular man is “guarding” the door against intruders, a bouncer if you will. He nods at the cameraman and opens the door for him. The music, if you could call it that, gets even louder; the apartment room is dark and covered in the multicoloured light. Several people are inside, well more than several, much more than you’d think you’d find in a room this size, and each person either has a whistle or a beer, some have both. It’s like a miniature rave. Amidst the mass of people we can see, an island in a see of littler people, the giant previously adhered to as Bubba, still wearing vaguely the same as before, looking quite lost and quite deafened, still wearing that red fez, the cameraman wades through the people over towards him, seeming as he might know where Mr Wead is] [Taking some time to do this, the cameraman is faced with on coming people from all sides yelling and screeching at him, luckily most of them just get out of the way as he moves over towards Bubba, unfortunately things never are really that simple] Voice: CROWD SURFING! [The camera spins round trying to identify where the sound came from, looking up it just has enough time to capture the last few seconds of the back of some long haired man plummeting from a 6 ft height down onto the cameraman, the camera is thrown out of the mans hands and skitters to a convenient place to see the cameraman with his assailant on top of him without too many legs between the camera and the scene, although the camera is at an angle. The assailant, now more clearly seen, turns out to be no other than Cruiserweight tournament entrant Smokey Wead] Smokey: Aw, man, sorry about that, didn’t know you were there [He helps the man up and takes the concussed body over out of camera shot, while the camera is picked up by some inexperienced person, as is told by the wobbling motion of the picture at first and spun round to see Smokey laying the cameraman onto a sofa, an man however shoves his face into camera view, although we can’t quite tell who he is as he is horribly out of focus, although gradually, swaying this way and that, the new fangled camera auto adjusts it’s focus] Man: Hey Mick, I’m On TV! Man behind the camera: Yeah I know, say cheese! Man: Sure thing… chee- [The man leans a little too far to the left and falls over spilling the contents of a beer bottle over the top of some woman behind him, who looks rather shocked. The new cameraman, Mick, moves much better through the crowd, obviously being much less polite and makes his way over to Smokey and the old cameraman. When he gets there, he almost falls over on top of the cameraman but manages to save himself, although the picture falls all over the place for a moment, Smokey now looks to Mick] Smokey: Wow Mick, you can work that thing? [As the camera focuses on Smokey, we see him wearing another Hawaiian shirt and his eyes are horribly blood shot and pupils dilated so much that they just look like black holes without an iris] Mick The Cameraman: Yeah, it’s not too hard, this is fun, great party, its only been going an hour or two Smokey: Cheers man. You know I recognise this guy, he was here before [He points at the concussed man on the sofa. He looks back at Mick for a moment before scanning the crowd] Smokey: BUBBA! [Mick whirls round and Bubba looks over from his central position and walks over to Smokey at the sofa, unheeded by the mass of people around him, it’s like he was parting the red sea. He reaches the small crowd inside the mini rave and looks lost again] Smokey: Revive this guy would you? [Bubba nods and lifts up one arm and lowers his arm pit to the man, almost when he gets very close to the face, the man pushes his way up violently letting out a scream. The crowd let out a reply scream and carry on regardless. The man starts gagging and yelling hoarsely for water, Smokey obliges by handing the man a beer that he downs almost in one gulp] Man: Oh man that was foul [He looks up and around, and realizes that his yell was lost in the wave of sound floating around him, he looks from Smokey, to Bubba and to Mick and brightens up] Man: Ah Mr Wead, I was hoping to catch you Smokey: Good [Smokey beams looking at the man, possibly not entirely sure what he just said] Man: I was hoping to catch you at a less busy time Smokey: What? Man: I was hoping to- Smokey: WHAT? [He leans down at the man he could hear perfectly fine before cupping one hand round his ear to try and hear him better] Man: CAN WE GO SOMEWHERE PRIVATE? Smokey: SURE THING, Bubba, keep an eye on things here [Bubba straighten up from his slouch and beams with self importance] Bubba: OK Smokey: Come on Mick, you can come two I expect [Smokey, the man in the suit and Mick head on out the door and off down the corridor, the music fading into the background with each step taken. They head off into the stairwell and walk down stairs and into the main hallway of the apartment block, feeling a bit alone Smokey takes out a “cigarette” and sparks up, again causing a bit of a disturbance with the man in the suit, after a long draw on the little white stick, he lets out a cloud of noxious gasses and offers it to the man in the suit who politely declines. Smokey then passes it to Mick] Smokey: So? What have you dragged me out of my party to say? [Over the thump of the music three floors up the man in the suit speaks] Man: Well I have found out a few details about Kirk Urban, firstly he’s an Englishman Smokey: Don’t be stupid; Kirk Urban’s a full-blooded American, like you or me Man: No, he isn’t, I also found out the person your referring to is in fact called Kurt Urban, not Kirk. There is a slight difference there. Kurt Urban is the Music guy with the albums. Kirk Urban is a professional wrestler from WWA and an ex drug user [Smokey looks puzzled for a moment before getting the roll up passed back to him by Mick the cameraman and takes another long draw. As he exhales he asks the question] Smokey: Ex Drug user? You mean people actually quit? Man: Yes, he sports horrendous scars from what he did under the influence. He spent quite some time in rehab Smokey: Re…Hab? Man: Rehabilitation [The blank look of incomprehension still dawns Smokey’s face, he and the man in the suit exchange a long blank stare before continuing] Man: At any rate he seems to be quite determined and quite the athlete, you’ll have your hands full with him, and this is only the first round [Smokey shrugs quite unconcerned with the outcome of the match] Smokey: Win some lose some, but what’s the guy got against me, I mean he doesn’t know me right? Man: No, I don’t think he does, not that well anyway, but he does dislike your… lenience shall we say… with marijuana. Smokey: I told you, it’s all medicinal, besides how does he know about that, it’s not like I’ve been on telly or nothing Man: Well you have, this is all being recorded for air on TV right now [Smokey turns around and looks directly into the camera Mick’s holding with a look of horror on his face] Smokey: I thought that was for your home movies? Mick: No man, it’s this guys [Smokey takes a few moments to look between the man in the suit and the camera before putting two and two together and getting three] Smokey: Mick, turn it off man, turn it off right now Mick: I don’t know how Smokey: It’s the big red button marked off, you can’t miss it Mick: Oh right then man, anything you say [The camera lowers to get the perfect shot of Smokey’s knees which steadily come into focus just before the scene cuts to static]
