UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #543

Greetings (Intro and WNU rp)

14 Jul 2004

Jukei Kutabare — Jukei's house

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera’s fade in, as we are in a typical Japanese Garden, with Bonzai trees and nice ornaments. If you look over the gardens, you can just feel the rest and just ooze of it. You can be stressed on the outside, but when you step in the garden, you will feel all sorrows fall off your shoulders. You’ll feel completely silenced, perfectly balanced, just like you are meditating. The camera follows through the gardens, into the house. When we come into the house, we can see that’s it totally in Feng Shui style. You get the same feeling as on the outside. Complete rest. The first thing you’ll wanna do in here, is sleeping on those nice and cousy-looking couch over there. The camera man walks steadily and slowly towards the couch until……….) Voice: HAAAAAAYAAAAA KARATE!!!!!! (After hearing a sound of a hand clashing against metal, the camera falls on the floor.) Voice: Who dares to enter my realm? (The cameraman grabs the camera and shows the attacker. It’s a, rather mustached, face unknown to the UWF wrestling fans.) Camera Man: Is this the Kutabare residence? Old man: Yes. What do you want? Camera Man: Is your son at home? We need to talk to him about his match at UWF Wednesday Underground. Old man: No, you cannot. Camera man: Why not, if I may ask? Old man: Because he isn’t present. Camera Man: Where can I find him? Old man: I don’t know. Where can you find an unborn child? (The camera man takes a step back and looks in disgust at the man.) Camera Man: You got to be kidding! You’re going to let an unborn child wrestle? Old man: Of course not, you dumb son of monkey-dung! I will wrestle in this match with those inferior people who call themselves wrestlers! (The camera man falls backwards on the floor and starts laughing like a hyena.) Camera Man (laughing) You got to be kidding me? You! In a wrestling match? Aren’t you too old? Jukei: How dare you! I should release my full wrath on you, but the spirits say that I should not hurt you. So you are lucky. (The Camera man stands back up.) Camera Man: Uhm, greetings then. Jukei: Greetings, you wanted to talk? Camera Man: Yes. I wanted to discuss things about your 10 men Battle Royal for the TV Title shot, wich is on Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. Jukei: That is correct. I have fore seen that. You want to talk to me what I think about, excuse my language, buttf*cking brady bunch, known as my 9 opponents on Wednesday Night Underground? Camera Man: Uhm, yup. Pretty much sums everything up. Jukei: They are all weak. They all haven’t learned the art of Jukei-Do. The most perfect Martial Arts there is. It combines the strength with speed and intelligence. The ultimate fighting technique. Seymour Almasy is nothing compared to this. Camera Man (rolling eyes): Right….. Jukei: Do not try to backtalk me, you infidel! I will crush you with my left pinky toe alone! Camera Man: Oh yeah, show me. Destroy that table! (Jukei walks up to the table, and kicks it with his left pinky toe, but nothing happens. You can see he’s in pain, but doesn’t let it show.) Camera Man: Why didn’t it break? Jukei (biting on his lip): This… Was… The… 43 Year… Melting wood technique. Camera Man (rolling eyes): Right…….. Jukei: Anyway, I must meditate. See you people soon. Camera Man: Bye. (The camera man leaves, as Jukei kisses his hand in pain. The screen fades out.)
