Another Day, Another Dollar

6 Apr 2004

Mike Montgomery — UWF Studios, Halifax

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open on what appears to be Mike Montgomery's favorite set in the UWF Studios. You remember the one, right? It's that one made up to look like a back ally. Directly behind Monty is a chain link fence with papers appearing to be stuck to it in several places. In front of him and slightly off to the side stands an oil drum, whatever refuse is inside consumed by the fire that Monty lights as he starts the promo. 'First of all, I must apologize to my partner, the Common Man for my recent absence. Ezra has been carrying the ball while I was out with the flu and I have to applaud his efforts. Ezra, it is your hard work that got us the titles, and your hard work will be what goes on to ensure that we keep those titles. 'Next, I need to address the Chaos Society. Boys, just shut up. Please. You come on UWF television time after time and publicly disgrace yourselves, and frankly it's getting rather tiring. You don't think you're getting enough of a cut of merchandise sales? Might I suggest you re-read your contracts? Take a very close look at the part about merchandise sales. You'll note that the amount you received will correspond with the amount of your merchandise that sells. I should know, I'm the one you negotiated your contracts with. SImple fact of that matter is boys, you don't sell. The people aren't interested in you. It's the same reason you won't win at Renaissance. The people don't want it. 'When you walk down to the ring, and you look across at the Player's Club, remember that you'll be looking at one half of the strongest group in UWF history. A group that has beaten you time...after time...after time.' Monty holds his hands over the fire in an effort to stay warm. He remains focussed on the fire as he continues talking, 'and then we've got the Local NVA Company and Team AWF. One of the most decorated teams in UWF history, and a team that's only won a single match in it's history. Then you throw in the Right to Party, and Lustful Lullaby. Well, I couldn't have come up with a better way to acquire mass revenge myself. The NVA company...who gave me fits for MONTHS when I was in the Asylum, Team AWF...who stole my AWF tag titles, Right to Party...who pinned the Plague a little over a week ago to defeat the two of us, and Lustful Lullaby...well I really don't know why I'd want revenge on them. Frankly, them debuting right after my tag title win was the most fun I've had in a fair while! 'Fact is, all those groups have one fatal flaw. They all think that a tag title win will give them some measure of power in the UWF. The problem is, that's not true. You see, one win doesn't get you power. It doesn't get you stroke. That takes years. Years of waiting...biding your time and seizing opportunities as soon as they open up. It involves allying with people who can help you ascend, then dropping those people when they're of no use to you. 'There's one thing you've gotta remember, and that's the first rule of booking. You don't strip a main event guy of his title after the first defence. Ezra Kazee is a main event guy. Me? I AM the Main Event. So you see boys, none of you can win on Wednesday. It's against the rules.' 'And...CUT!' The director calls out. This is it I think to myself, last thing I gotta get done for today 'How's it look?' I ask the director. I'm not really in the mood for a re-shoot, but if that's what it takes to get the right promo, that's what it takes. The director, though, is going to be on my side for change, 'Nicely done Mike. Straight to the point, focused, nice and old school for a change. Not like the pornography or pro-drug campaigns some of those other guys put out.' I shift uncomfortably, though I smile at the praise, 'Thanks. I just do things the way I like 'em. So you're done with me for today?' The director nods affirmatively, 'We may need to shoot one more promo tomorrow, schedules permitting, but yeah we can run with that for now.' Thank God. Cracking my neck, then taking a luxurious stretch that causes popping sounds in places that really shouldn't pop, I make my way back to the change room. I've been in the building for nearly ten straight hours getting caught up on the work I missed while I was home sick. My mind is still focussed on the match as I change back into my street clothes. Team AWF is there simply to make it an even six teams. They haven't got a prayer. Never did. The NVA company is at the point in their careers where they have to start doing what's good for the business. That's putting the next generation of talent over. They're going to lose because it's what they have to do in order to give back to the business. I didn't have the RTP figured out until earlier today. An old buddy of mine called me earlier today. I'd gone to him when I wanted to buy the services of the RPA with premium Manitoba weed. Some of the strongest pot in the world. Smokey and Bubba are potheads, first and foremost. That's something I can exploit. Finally dressed in a Player's Club jersey, black denim pants and a pair of 'airwalk' skater shoes, I make my way through the corridors of UWF Headquarters mulling the final problem over in my head. Lustful Lullaby. They're certainly not a traditional tag team. Not by any stretch of the imagination. They've never had a match, so there's no tape for me to study. They're going to be the unknown quantity. When Plague and I teamed up against the RTP, that was a warm up. We may have lost, but I got to study them, learn how they operated and figure out how to counter that. I got nothing to study with Lullaby, and I haven't got a clue how to get into their heads. I'm shaken from my reverie by the ding of the elevator. I've been so lost in my thoughts I didn't even know I'd hit the call button. The doors slide open and I step through. My thumb automatically hits the ground floor button. I start pacing as the elevator begins it's slow, twelve floor descent. The one constant about Lustful Lullaby is their obsession with sex. Ninety-five percent of every single promo they've ever put out involves reliving a sexual experience, then they'll quickly say something about their opponent before signing off. To me that says lack of focus. It's something, but not much. It doesn't give me something I can directly exploit. I look up as the elevator dings again and the doors swoosh open. Guess the trip's faster when I'm not focused on it. I step through, heels clacking on the marble floor as I head for the door. I give a curt wave to the UWF Gorilla. Still can't believe we put him on Front Desk security. As I walk through the building's front entrance, my limo pulls up. I nearly jump out of my skin at a voice from behind me. 'Heya Mike.' I turn, having already recognized the voice, a smile crawling across my face. Now standing in front of me is five feet, eleven inches of athletic build, legs from here to eternity, blonde hair just past her shoulders, and possibly my number one fan. 'Caitlin, how are you?' 'I can't complain,' she answers, showing two rows of perfect white teeth, 'you?' 'Reflective. I gotta catch a plane for Italy, HUGE match.' Caitlin nods. I swear she manages to look ever more attractive every time she moves. 'I know, 6 way for the tag titles. You got this so wrapped up.' Now my smile fades a bit, 'I'm not so sure. Lustful Lullaby's too much of a wildcard. No matches to study, no blatantly obvious weaknesses for me to exploit. Their only obsession is sex, and obviously I can't do anything with that.' Caitlin's smile just grows ever wider, 'Maybe not you...but as it just so happens I'm going to Italy for the show. I think I can help.' Now I'm a bit confused, is she saying what I think she's saying? 'What're you gonna do?' Caitlin steps closer. I can feel my heartrate and breathing accelerate as she presses up against me, whispering in my ear, 'I think I can come up with something.' A small part of my brain registers the limo's door opening, 'My dear, I think we have a plane to catch.'
