UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #498

The Return

4 Jul 2004

Slasher — Des Moines, Iowa

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time, it can bring about many different things into the world. It can speed up and make people grow old, make relationships with others turn sour, and even make some people change who they are. It can also slow down, and make people lives seem to last forever. Like the old lady down the road from you, who seems to just live forever and never get older. Time is a beast in it's own right, and a fearsome opponent to those who fear it. The beast known as time as struck again, this time bringing something from the past back, something that was once known quite well in the UWF. Something that set it on edge for a few months, something that ruled this place. That something is the reunion of one of the most dominent tag teams in the history of the UWF, the Drowning Pool. Consisting of Unforgiven and Slasher. Two of the most physical men who have ever stepped into the ring. Indeed it has been a while since these two have competed in a UWF ring, but the rust was not even close to being a factor this past weekend at Stable Wars. The team of the Ressurection was totally destroyed, not even close to surviving the onslaught that was being smashed down on them. Did they really deserve this? Does it really matter? No.. not at all. What was the real motive behind the savage attack? Nothing more than a simple showcase of the true brutality of these two. Did it really have anything to do with the fact that Digital Mortality and Moraless are the Tag Team Champs? Coincidence, or not? Does it really matter? Slasher and Unforgiven left this fed as #1 Contenders for those titles, and they still have their title shot coming to them. It's coming very soon, in one week. One week from now we will see the first match back with the Drowning Pool, and it is for the Tag Team Titles. Those belts, shall become the DP's, something that they should have gotten a long, long time ago. [The wind blows fiercly through the trees in the front yard on 4367 Evergreen Drive in Des Moines, Iowa. The little home that Slasher grew up in now looks to be in even more tatters than ever. The roof has caved in, the windows all smashed, and a large condemned sign is in the broken down white picket fence. Even though it is the start of summer, the yard always has the brown, dead look of autumn.] [Slowly the house is drawn in closer, the front door is blown open by the wind, inside the floors look to be totally upheaved, with the carpet covered in a foul smelling mold. The walls have a mildew look to them, and the feeling of dampness is overwhelming. Slowly the we wander down the hallway entering the living room. The television is smashed in, with a beer bottle sticking out of the glass. Slowly the scene turns to a nearby couch with Slasher sitting on the left side of it. He has his feet up and is staring up at the ceiling. Above him is a family portrait, with two parents a small child in the center. The faces of each person is scribbled out with a red marker.] [Slasher slowly gets up and walks over to the windows, he is wearing a black boiler suit with his hands taped with white tape. His fingernails are painted black, and he is wearing a pair of black Doc Martins. He is of course, wearing his mask as well. Slasher looks out the window, and then turns around and faces the camera.] Slasher: Won't you be.. my neighbor... [Slasher turns and picks up the TV and smashes it off the wall.] Slasher: Welcome to my home.. the home of my past... the place that made me into what I am today. [Slasher walks over near the couch and grabs the picture of his family.] Slasher: I remember when this was taken.. it was the worst fucking day of my life! [Slasher smashes the picture off the floor.] Slasher: This house.. this life.. the memories it contains are what made me what I am today.. it contorted a little boy into a beast. It turned me, the same little boy you see running past your house on a warm summer day.. to the boy that comes into your home after fucking your little slut daughter! From the boy you love.. to the man you hate. Funny how over a slight progression of a few years can change a person so much. Just like watching a plant wither and die, the innocence of a child fades, and the toxic fumes that are adulthood come into play. [Slasher then sits down on the couch and places his hands on the sides of his head.] Slasher: You see this mask.. it represents me.. it represents the beast inside. Sure if I took it off you would see a normal man. a normal man who feels pain. Just like all of you.. sure when you look at me you see this hardcore maniac.. [Slasher stands up.] Slasher: You know what?? THATS EXACTLY WHAT I AM!!!!!!! [Slasher drives his fist into the wall, dust and pieces of ceiling tile crumble down from above.] Slasher: Everyone of you god damn UWF superstars is going to learn about me right quickly. Your going to learn what pain is! For example.. NICK PERSIA! The THC Champion.. don't make me laugh. I am telling you this once.. you had better show me you deserve that title.. else I will come and rip it from your dying hands! Everyone thinks Gurgles is hardcore.. Gurgles is the hardcore legends.. well remember this! Gurgles and I are tied for the most holdings of the THC TItle in the GIWF when it was first brought out. None of you flamers can hold a candle to me! NONE OF YOU! Slasher: Now.. as for my return the other night at Stable Wars.. hehehe.. bet you all never expected that. Especially the Ressurection.. oh how I loved the terrified look on your faces.. especially you Bondage.. especially the blood dripping from your faggot face.. it made me feel all warm and fuzzy inside watching you. Kennedy.. you took your beating like a man.. and for that I would gladly do it again sometime.. name the time and place.. I would gladly hospitalize you. [Slaser adjusts his sitting position.] Slasher: Moraless and Digital Mortality.. of the four of you that got your asses kicked.. I enjoyed the two of you the most. Your attack had meaning.. it also had feeling. You both hold what Unforgiven and I have returned for. The Tag Team Titles, something that we never did get a chance for. The two of you are going to be on the chopping block as of right now. Those belts belong to Unforgiven and I.. they belong to the Drowning Pool. Face it, the two of you stand no chance against us. You could just hand the belts over, which you probably want to do, but thats not going to happen. I want to raise that title over my head and then look down, and see your bodies tattered and torn. Looking up at me from the mat, with the look of pain and loss in your eyes. Then, I will truly realize what those titles mean to me. [Slasher stands up to his feet and looks out the window again.] Slasher: Knightmare Brigade.. I can tell you this. Those belts are ours.. and if you dare try and get in our way.. there will be dire consequences.. extremely dire. I am giving you a fair warning.. the belts are ours.. if you interfere.. you do not value your lives. We will crush you... plain and simple! [Slasher reaches in his pocket and pulls out a pack of matches.] Slasher: A new chapter starts.. a new era. The era of the Drowning Pool.. the era in which Unforgiven and I shall destroy everyone in the UWF. The past is over.. the future is now. I shall destroy the past.. I shall leave my past behind... I shall leave this house behind.. the painful memories.. I shall never more focus my rage on the past.. I shall focus my fury on the future. Unforgiven, I thank you.. I thank you for teaching me how to channel my anger and frustration into what I am today. I am Slasher, I am the Crown Prince of Hardcore.. I am a future Tag Team Champion.. I am.. YOUR WORST FUCKING NIGHTMARE! [Slasher lights the matches and drops them onto the carpeted floor. Instantly the carpet sparks into flames, the fire begins to spread extremely fast.] Slasher: The Drowning Pool is just like fire.. it spreads and destroys all in it's path.. superstars of the UWF.. you are right in the middle of that path.. and the first two are the Tag Team Champions! Remember.. you play with fire.. you get burned.. and this is no ordinary fire.. WE ARE AN INFERNO!! THE BELTS ARE OURS!!!!!! PREPARE TO LOSE THEM!!!! [Slasher runs out the front door of the house. He stops and watches it burn for a few moments. He turns to the camera and says.] Slasher: Digital Mortality.. Moraless.... [Slasher drags his thumb across his throat showing the sign for the Disasterpiece.. the most devastating tag team move in the history of the UWF.] Slasher: The DP are back everyone.. and fucking better than ever! [Slasher turns away and runs down the street leaving his house roaring in flames.]
