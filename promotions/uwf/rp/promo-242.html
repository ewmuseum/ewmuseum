UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #242

2-4-1 Sale

26 Feb 2004

Moraless — SAS #1

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Charisma doesn’t win matches, the people won’t win you matches,” a not so wise man once said. I don’t know why but those words reverb around my head so frequently lately and every time I hear them I smile a little smile. The camera pans out to show John ‘Moraless’ Edwards sat alone in a simple dressing room; he is seen wearing one of the new t-shirts bought out by RPW showing Chris Storm across the front. John is smiling to himself cracking his knuckles on the wooden bench that he is sat on as he stares out almost vacantly at the camera. ”Mike Montgomery, executive vice president of talent relation’s part owner of the UWF, member of the players club and most important of all. One of the latest people to bow before my feet, to humble themselves and allow me to pick up a victory over them. Of course you will argue that it was someone else’s fault that you were distracted and that is why you lost out. The fact of the matter is Mike that at the end of the day is you can’t even begin to cope with what I put out there. That is why you are scared but of course for you revenge is sweet isn’t it, you think you’ve got my balls in a vice grip by making me participate in two matches in which the odds are stacked against me?” Moraless simply laughs and shakes his head. “You have no idea what I am capable of. But of course lets look at this objectively shall we, you know just for shits and giggles. The first match is against someone I don’t know, I will be in the ring waiting and anybody could walk out. Lyle Wile, Bum Boozler, Slasher, Lacuna Debris fuck even Tsunami Oni may come down whip out N.O.G.O.O.D again and try to flying monkey bomb me again. The fact of the matter is I don’t care who comes out in the end because they are going to come out and look me in the eye instead of jumping me from behind.” Moraless stands and begins to pace up and down the dressing room, scooping up a steel chair in his left arm swinging it about wildly a large dent noticeable on it. “Ya see this mate, this is the very chair you decided to smash my head into last week however there is a bit more history to this chair let me fill you in. You guys all call me the RPW sympathiser, the guy who wants in RPW and out of UWF. Well can you blame me? Look around we have more people in our federation with sexual deviances than bloody purgatory itself. This chair though was the start of my fruition into RPW, with this chair I slammed the mighty Chris Storms skull into oblivion in a bout that was only stopped due to some over grown thug bounding in” Moraless holds up the chair so it can be seen on the camera. “If I can take out one of if not the best RPW has to offer, what the hell am I going to do against a man who is too scared to even show his face before the match, but alas you will not be at the brunt of my beating for I shall be saving myself for a certain final fantasy.” John grins once more and slams down the chair returning to a sitting position, his knuckles clenched. “Well I must say Seymour, you did get around a bit these days don’t you, we all hear of your events in Global Wrestling and we see for ourselves what you do here except you also inhabit somewhere else in your, what was it you call it, hunt for experience points. A now defunct federation called NWC: Atlantic that now has branched down into something called Axiom, I dunno sounds like a stupid name to me. Not many people may have known of that one but I guess now they do. What’s the matter Seymour is the money drying up, public experiences not coming in as much as they used to. We all know that your workload is becoming insurmountable what with all of these matches; it has to wear down a man. Hell if you are going to let someone the calibre of my brother beat you, things must be getting to you.” John laughs to himself shaking his head at the thought of James actually beating Seymour seeing as he only has a few years experience in the ring himself. “Seymour, trust me you don’t want this. You have your Global title to defend against a certain man I’m reasonably sure I gave a concussion to on Sunday. I did you a favour, so do me one. Lie down for me. It will save us both a lot of hassle in the long run, so you won’t become the grand slam champion, big deal…there are plenty of other fish in the sea.” John picks himself up and the chair off the floor before walking forwards towards the camera his eyes almost burning into it. “Think about it Seymour, why put yourself through pain you can avoid” With that Moraless swings violently with the chair smashing the camera and disrupting the feed.
