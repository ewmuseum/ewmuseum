Top Dawg!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. He is staring at the television as if he's dazed at what he's watching. The screen shows the latest Moraless promo and Ryder's face shows no emotion whatsoever until the screen goes black. He stares at the screen for a few seconds more before bursting into loud laughter. He doubles over on the couch before composing himself and turning to the camera already set up in his living room. ) Ryder: What in the dumb fuck was that shit? * He fights back more laughter * Ryder: Moraless, who in the hell comes up with that stupid shit? It can't possibly be you can it? How full of drugs are you boy? I'll bet you are shot so full of drugs that stuff is coming out your eyes. You will be victorious huh, well that's a crock of shit if I ever heard it, because there's no way that a little pissant like you is gonna get the best of the Cherokee Thrilla. I'll kick your ass from pillar to post and i'm not gonna stop until it bring Malcom Kennedy out to protect you, assuming he's your friend that is. My guess is he'll stay in the back and laugh his ass off, because Malcom Kennedy has no true friends or alliances. Why the hell do you think he calls himself the One Man Army. That army is gonna get destroyed when he comes to help you though. You better open your eyes if you think you're gonna take me out though Moraless, because everyone knows that you ain't got what it takes. You're weak, and i'm gonna expose you for the puss that you truely are. Don't think for a second that I am underestimating you either, because I know what you bring and it ain't an A game. Hell it ain't even an X game. You flat out suck Moraless and if it wasn't for those assholes you hang out with you wouldn't have all that you have now. You're a dead man plain and simple and i'm the reaper. * Ryder gives the camera a cocky grin * Ryder: I'm the man who is gonna lead you to your grave and then i'm gonna shovel shit all over it and top the grave off by pissin all over it. You ain't walkin away from this match on both legs. You better take all the drugs you can before you get to the ring, because you're sure as hell gonna need em. I'm gonna put you in some extreme pain son. Who knows, maybe when i'm through with you i'll invite Corporal Punishment to the ring and send his ass to hell right along with you. I mean fuck, I thought the dude was dead a long time ago. Who dug his ass up? Ahh well fuck him, he ain't shit anymore anyways. You on the other hand Moraless, you're the target and you're the man i'm gonna use as an example of what Malcom Kennedy has coming. You better pray to God that you are ready for this match, because if you're not....The pain will be unbearable. ( Ryder glares into the camera as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
