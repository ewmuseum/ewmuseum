UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #268

Overconfidence Killed the cat and the Plague was Cured.

3 Mar 2004

Raid — A Park

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see Raid sitting on a large bench ,which is dimly lit by a street light, in what looks like a park. His back is towards the camera, and his head is in his hands. As the camera comes closer he turns around to see who is coming up behind him.) Raid “Good you’re here on time.” ( Raid stands up and starts to walk towards the camera. He is wearing black sweat pants and a black hooded sweatshirt.) “ Well Plague, I think its good that your overconfident.” (He begins to crack the knuckles of both of his hands.) “You see, when your overconfident you tend to let your guard down. (It starts to drizzle and Raid pulls up his hood. He then starts to walk down the pathway in the park.) “And your right when you say that I haven’t won any titles YET!” (He continues to walk down the path as the drizzle turns to a steady rain.) “ You also mentioned that you’ve been back for two matches and you are already getting a title shot. Well if you check the books my first match was a battle royal for a shot at the THC, and my second was a Tag Team title match. Now in my fourth I get a chance , once again, at the THC title. So having a title shot in your second match back really doesn’t mean all that much.” (The steady rain suddenly turns to a down pour. Raid disappears in the heavy rain. The cameraman frantically turns left, then right to try to locate him.) “ Hey over here.” (The cameraman turns to the right and you can see Raid standing underneath the street light. His face is covered by his hood and is not visibly.) “ Plague, you’re offering ME advice? Well I’ve got some advice for you. Get over the past. Yes you were a champion once, but if you think that your just gonna take the THC title with out a fight, well your in for a real wake up call on Sunday.” (He then leans back onto the light pole and begins to sink down it until he is sitting on the ground. His hands are now folded between his legs and he is rubbing them together.) “ I would advise you to bring that sledge of yours, cause your gonna need it.” (The scene then cuts away to show a clip of Iron Lung.) (Putting Victoria back on a vertical base, now Lung faces his back towards her. And then, IL falls back, Victoria catching him immediately with a gasp. And then, the two smiled at each other again. The task had been completed, and Lung felt fabulous. He was finally able to trust somebody, and he owed it all to Victoria. He thanked her over and over again as he prepared to exit the class, Victoria slipping him a piece of paper with her number on it, along with a kiss on the cheek. With a smile in his step and a song in his heart, the young champion left the classroom and walked out to his car, fresh with a new confidence. How would this new attitude effect his opponents? Time would only tell…) (The camera cuts back to Raid and he is still sitting on the ground just as we last saw him.) “Well Lung it looks like you have your mind in other places as of late. That’s all fine and dandy with me. I just hope you’ll have your head in the game on Sunday, because I don’t want to beat you when you’ve got your mind in other places. I want you to be at your absolute best when I beat you.” (Raid slowly rises to his feet and begins to walk down the path, away from the camera.) “ Iron Lung, Plague, I’ll see you two this Sunday. And I hope you two bring your best game , because I will.” (Raid continues to walk away down the path. After a minute or two he disappears into the rain, and the camera fades to black.)
