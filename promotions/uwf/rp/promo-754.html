UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #754

Two Become One?

23 Oct 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens within the all too familiar UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Sitting in his odd office chair is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is looking at the different pictures sitting on the wall. The different titles that he has won. The different pictures from matches he has been involved with in. From his time in the GIWF is there. His several different television titles wins, his IC Title, his Tag Team Title championship……) (Has tag team title championship. The one that he held with his one time friend and leader. The tag team title that he had held with the man whom he thought he could always trust. The one whom he thought would never betray him. As a unit, the Players Club was undefeatable. As a unit, the Players Club was unblemished, undeniably the top stable in the UWF. Greatest of all time? Who knows? It could be argued if only they had existed longer.) (As a unit, what more could the Players Club had to have accomplish. They won Stable Wars 2004. They at one time held every major title in the UWF. The THC Title, the Intercontinental Title, the World Title, and the Tag Team Titles. The fans loved them. The fans adored them. As the saying goes, the Players Club put the Asses in the seats. They were everything that anyone could have ever wanted in a stable. And there was absolutely everything that some wrestlers loved because they got the paychecks raised. And they were absolutely everything some wrestlers hated, because they did have all the success.) (So what happen? What happen to this great super stable? What happen to one of the most dominant forces in the UWF? How did it all start? How did the downfall of the Players Club start? That’s a good question. That’s a real good question.) (SAS. That was it. SAS shortly after UWF Renaissance. Shortly after Moraless and Digital Mortality formed an alliance called the Resurrection or something like that. That’s how I tall started. That’s how it all ended. How could it start there? That’s a good question. It did start with “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee and Mike Montgomery losing the UWF Tag Team Championship. The UWF Tag Team Championship to Moraless and Digital Mortality. When Ezra Kazee tapped out to the Digitizer. That’s how it all started. Isn’t it. Do you remember now?) (And everything was going so well. But after that, it all went downhill. Kazee shortly thereafter lost his Intercontinental Title to his arch-nemesis/best friend/sometime stablemate Raid. Monty lost his World Title, and Persia lost his THC Title. Stable Wars VI came upon us then, where The Players Club showed exactly how dominant they were. Exactly what they could accomplish. And everyone thought there was a resurrection afoot.) Then Dunn and Starr did their little conspiracy. Deceiving everyone, the fans, and the championship committee. Everyone. Shortly thereafter, the downhill turn continued for the Players Club. A four way match for the World Title at the Regal Rumble. A four way match that was contemplated and conceived by “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. A match that made everything fair, or was suppose to. Two referees, a cell, no one should’ve been in and out. And Monty found a way to get through that.) (Hard feelings between Monty and Kazee are an understatement of sorts. There sure as hell are hard feelings. Monty cost Kazee his chance at a World Championship. Monty cheated the fans out of the perfect match of skills, and cheated Kazee out of the match that he had always wanted. And Monty killed it.) Kazee: What was committee thinking? Who the hell decided this match? Why would they team me with Monty? I just don’t get it……. Granted, I do have the shot of regaining the tag team titles. The tag team titles that Monty and I lost. The titles that I knew we could hold on to and could not…… Some will dismiss us; some will just go on with their lives without even thinking about it. Almasay at least realizes the threat that we possess. The threat that former champions can pose in a situation like this, especially if they are on the same page/….. I always work with those who I am put up against. Even with a son of a bitch like Monty, I will work with him. I may not trust him, but at this point I Have no choice. And it will happen……. Let it be known that it will make a difference in the fact that we are going to work together, even if we don’t like each other. Let it be known that the old magic will come back and we will regain the Tag Team Championship. And let the others be put on notice that this time, there really is nothing that will stop us. Personally, myself and Monty, we don’t like each other. That’s not a big secret. But let it be known that professionally, me and Monty are two of the best in this business. Let it be known that Monty and I have accomplished great things as singles and will continue to accomplish great things as singles, but together, as a force, there is almost nothing that can stop us…… Good Luck to you all. You will need it. (Kazee picks up a remote control. He presses a button and the scene fades into a video of Ezra Kazee tapping out to the Digitzer. The tape plays for several seconds. All of a sudden, the scene goes in reverse, and it is played again. It does this a couple of times before the scene fades to the familiar UWF logo.)
