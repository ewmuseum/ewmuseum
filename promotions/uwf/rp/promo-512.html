UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #512

Returning to His Past Part 2

7 Jul 2004

Adien Pryde — A city in China

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to an almost familiar scene of a large city. The streets are unbelievablely crowded but no with cars but with bikes and pedestrians. The people seem to be friendlier then New York but it is in Chinese. The camera pans across the crowd and finally finds its target's, Joanna and Adien Pryde. Dressed in Chaos Society clothes (available at your nearest shopping mall soon), they stand head and shoulders above everyone else.] Joanna : Why in hell did you drag me halfway around the world to this rice eating country? I have no interest in staying here any longer than necessary. When can we get out of here? Pryde : As soon as I finalize my point about my opponents tonight. Joanna : Who Angel and what's his name? Pryde : The Green Demon. Joanna : Ya, what is up with that man, or is he a myth? Pryde : Oh I am sure he is a man, I saw his signature on the contract. I am guessing that he has realized that he is out of his league in this match with me and Angel. Hey, Demon, I'll even make it worth it your time for you to show up. Since I am Evil, which I should check with Raid to see if I can start using too. Joanna : I'm sure he wouldn't mind it. Hell, I think we should all add it to our names. We have Raid, The Evil Exterminator. We could have Evil Pryde, Evil Joanna, Evil Drac, and Evil Fizzy. [They both laugh.] Pryde : Evil Fizzy, I like that. Not very convincing but has a nice ring to it. We can't forget my main man Reo Dai, we have to have Evil Dai to make up Evil Anarchy with me. Where was I? Joanna : Making it worth showing up. Pryde : That's right, show up, lay down for me and I can't make you a happy man. I am willing to break the rules in that way since I Evil. [In his best Kaientai voice.] Pryde : Now onto the reason we are here, to play out the next stage in 'This is your life' for Angel. The place of your revival from death. Joanna : I thought that was in Japan. Pryde : Really, you sure? Well, either Japan or China, they all look the same to me. What matters is that in some Asian country that geisha woke him up and started him down this path of misery to expunge all evil from the wrestling world. [Pryde picks up a broom from an elderly man and starts to sweep up in front of his store.] Pryde : Watch this. [As he continues to work his way to the curb, he picks just the right moment and, WHAM, he shoves the handle of the broom into the front wheel of the nearest bike. The rider goes flying over the handlebars and starting a huge chain reaction of bike accident's] Pryde : Run [After losing Pryde and Joanna in the commotion, the camera man seems about to give up when a hand shots out from behind a dumpster and pulls him behind it.] Pryde : Thought we lost you there. You really that slow or does all that equipment slow you down? Doesn't matter. What we cam to this country to do is follow the path that you Angel took 4 years ago. The path that brought you to you martial arts. Dude, this is the UWF, United Wrestling Federation. [Pryde looks at Joanna.] Pryde : That is what it stands for right? [Joanna nods] Pryde : Wrestling, not the World Kick You in the Head Federation or WKYitHF. You see, I wrestle for a living and love doing it. I may use a shortcut or two, but am proud to admit it. Tonight when I lock you in the Sibco Stretcher and hear that bad knee pop and hear you scream as you wrestling career passes in front of your eyes, you will wish you stayed here in Japan... Joanna : China. Pryde : Where ever. Wished you had stayed there a defeated Evil in the martial arts world instead. [Pryde shoves the camera man back as he begins to stand.] Pryde : Now get out of here, we need to catch the next flight out of this back assed country. [As the scene fades to black.]
