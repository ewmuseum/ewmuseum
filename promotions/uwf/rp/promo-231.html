UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #231

IGNORE MY LAST ONE, READ THIS ONE.

date unknown

Malcolm Kennedy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<< The scene opens sitting outside the tour bus that we saw Malcolm on just before The Clash. Malcolm Kennedy walks past the camera rolling some luggage behind him as well as carrying a bag over his shoulder. Malcolm wears a pair of baggy blue jeans, a pair of Doc Martins shoes, and a button up white long sleeved shirt with the sleeves rolled up to his elbows. A pair of black oakleys cover his eyes. He opens the side of the bus and throws his luggage into the carrying space. He shuts the door and turns around and looks almost surprised to see the camera there. He runs his fingers through his messy jet black hair as he walks to the door of the bus. The door opens and Malcolm sticks one foot in and speaks to the driver. >>> Malcolm Kennedy: “Hey Kenny?” “Kenny you awake?” <<< The man jumps in his seat as he awakes. He flops around adjusting his clothes quickly. >>> Kenny The Driver: “ Jesus Christ! Or Err, Uh what did you say Mr. Kennedy?” Malcolm Kennedy: “I was trying to see if you were awake or not, obviously you weren’t. Listen, go ahead and fire up the bus, Im gonna cut a promo and then we can head out. Ok?” Kenny The Driver: “Ok Mr Kennedy, Ill be ready and waiting.” <<< With that the key is put in the ignition and turned. The diesel engine roars to life as Malcolm steps back down from the bus. He walks over to large plot of grass next to the parking lot that the bus is in. Malcolm walks up to a large oak tree and sits down on the bed of pine needles neatly laid out below it. He rests his back against the tree while extending his left leg all the way out in front of him. His right leg bent up with his foot resting on the ground, his right arm resting on it at the elbow. >>> “Lets talk a little about why we are here. Joshua Dunn. Seymour Almasy. Mount whatever its called death match. Dunn beats Seymour in a brutal match. Then what does he do? He vacates the title he just busted his ass to defend. Ill give credit where credit is damn well do, Dunn your one hell of an IC champion. I guess I can understand your ideas tho, Its time to move on to bigger and better things. Sounds like Beelzebozo has got his hands full. But so do I. You see, I’ve got a nice little spot laid out on my shelf above my fireplace back home, and it would look great with the IC title resting atop it. So my plans are crystal clear, and will be executed to perfection. My goals is to take that IC title in the tournament, and never let it go. But It wont be easy.” <<< Malcolm shakes his head slightly, basically agreeing with himself. He runs his fingernails against the grain of his beard, scratching back and forth. He pushes his glasses up above his forehead into his hair. He sighs. Seemingly frustrated. >>> “ You know I’ve busted my ass in this fed, and what have I got to show for besides the little respect I get for never backing down?” “Damn TV title.” “I mean seriously, I think its about damn time some REAL gold is put on me. How am I gonna do that. Its real simple. I have a multi-step plan to figure it all out. The first step?” <<< He sighs once more. >>> “Ezra Kazee” <<< The tone in his voice is one of disgust. His face distorts upon mentioning Ezra’s name. >>> “It appears that I will be facing Ezra Kazee for the second time at the upcoming SAS. From his last promo he spit out, Im starting to believe he’s a little more worried about those Anarchy ass clowns. Oh well, Ill gladly take that to my advantage. You see the last time we met you got the win. Something Im not proud of. But I lost none the less. So as you can already tell, your record against me says a lot. Hell your track record against everyone is pretty good. Just don’t let that go to your head. Don’t forget, your not just walking into another match. Your walking into a match with the fastest rising superstar in the UWF. Yeah, I’ve had my fair share of losses. I wont deny it. Hell look at last night, you and you buddy Mike Montgomery fought your way to the tag team titles. Well Mike Montgomery bitched and moaned his way to get what he wanted, but hey, its ok. Like I said, I don’t want or need the damn tag team titles, and Im almost glad I lost the match. But lets go ahead and just disregard that stupid match. Im sure you and I both would rather not talk about the nut fuck wack jobs in leather who’d just assume cram a flashlight up their butt than make love to a woman.” <<< He shudders and shakes his head. >>> “The sick little twats” <<< He smirks at his remark. >>> “Well “Common Man” its pretty apparent we don’t like each other. Your in the Players Club, and you got rid of the Prime Alliance. The stable I was proud to call my own. So its obvious that our match wont be pretty in the least. Yeah, Yeah, go ahead and refer once again to the stupid tag match if you must. Its all ancient history to me. You see I’ve decided to start my own little era here in the UWF. Im gonna start doing things my way. Im a solo wrecking crew. Ill destroy everyone who gets in my way, starting with this very tournament. You will be the first of many pillars to fall. Followed by whoever else wins and moves on, wether it be your lover Mike Montgomery, Or the living proof of what inhaling too much bug spray can do to fuck up your brain otherwise known as Raid, Or Seymour Almasy, the walking cut out of some 50 cent comic book you can get at the local drug store. You see it doesn’t really matter. Who’s beaten me in the past, Who’s record is the best, Who’s the world champion, all of that is nothing to me. I am the one man army, and this is the beginning of a era in wrestling. Unfortunately for you Ezra, this could be the end for you.” “ But don’t be fooled by my confidence. I still know full well that you are a highly capable wrestler and that you wont go down easy. Lets face it, if you weren’t that good, Id have beaten you the first time we went toe to toe. You like to just get down to business and wrestle, and save the talking for afterwards. I respect that. Actions have always spoken louder than words.” <<< Malcolm brings the glasses back down over his eyes and gets up from under the tree. He walks back to the bus and puts one foot on and pulls himself in. He stops half way and turns around and looks back to the camera. >>> “But make no mistake about it, this Saturday I WILL beat you. I WILL pin your shoulders down for the 3 count, and I WILL advance on to the next round of the tournament. Im tired of playing second fiddle here in the UWF. This is my calling. MY TIME! No one is going to get in my way. Not this time. I wont let it happen. I intend to have Intercontinental gold around my waist in the VERY near future. And there’s not a damn thing you, or the Playas Club can do to stop this One Man Army. But if your feeling froggy and wanna leap, you just go right ahead. Like Doc Holiday said in tombstone when the cowboy was looking for a REAL fight. “Ill be your huckleberry” Like I said, I agree that actions speak louder than words. My actions will be loud and clear this Saturday at SAS. And that my friend, you can bet your bottom dollar on. This Sunday Im gonna show you exactly what its like to be “TOP NOTCH”!!!” <<< He pulls himself up onto the bus and walks off to the back. The door closes and the bus drives off. As it pulls away into the distance the camera fades to black and the scene ends. >>>
