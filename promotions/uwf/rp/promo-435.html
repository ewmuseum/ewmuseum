UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #435

TV Title, Here I come

2 Jun 2004

Adien Pryde — Unknown

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to blackness. Run to the Hills by Iron Maiden can be heard in the background. A red light starts to strobe and you can see someone working out on a weight bench. He is wearing black shorts and red tennis shoes.] Pryde : So today is the day. My chance at the TV championship. I am up against The Communist, someone who holds the ideals of a dead political party. There is a reason that it is no longer around anymore, it doesn't work, and just like the political ideal, I am going to leave you wondering what happened. [Pryde does a dozen more reps with what appears to be at least 250 pounds, then stops to talk some more.] Pryde : What gets me is how you can get people to rally behind you and your cause. You state that Anarchy will never work and how things will work out in the Communism's way. I am out to prove that Anarchy and Chaos can and does work. After tonight The Chaos Society will be going into Stable Wars with both the Intercontinental and Television Title's in our grasp. Then after we win Stable Wars we will take the other one's and finish our climb to the top stable in the UWF. [Pryde sits up and grabs a red towel from the floor and pat dries his face and neck.] Pryde : Now for who will be our fourth member at Stable Wars. We are all set, and let me tell you, this person is just what we need to take Chaos to the next level. This person will help prove that anything can and will happen when we set out to prove that all hell will break loose. [Pryde stands up.] Pryde : You want to know who our partner will be. How does it fell like to want something? [Pryde walks out of view and the strobe stops flashing and it turns out that the light was flashing the Anarchy symbol the fades to black.
