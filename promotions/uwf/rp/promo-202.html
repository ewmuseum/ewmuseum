UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #202

A Victory Drink

1 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — Random Bar.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene opens in a bar area. Kazee is sitting there with a bottle of bubbly. There is no one else there.) Kazee: Well Ruff Ryder, I said I’d do it, and I did it. In fact, not only did I say I’d do it, so did the Players Club, and well, we did it. The Prime Alliance is now in shambles, there leader is gone, and Ryder, your still puzzled by the darkness…… So what’s next for the Players Club? Who knows. It’ll go and go and well, we just have to wait to see. We have the next PPV coming up and our momentum toward Stable Wars right now is unmatched by any other group in the UWF. We have all established ourselves as elite superstars here in the UWF, and we will continue to keep ourselves as elite superstars…… I shall be going back to my celebrating though. I bid you fair well. Good Luck to everyone wrestling on Wednesday. Trust me, The Players Club will be there just to keep up to date with all of you. (Scene fades to black.)
