My Pain... my suffering...

4 May 2004

Bruce Gordy — -

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Malice... hatred... spite... hostility... Words... these words describe the way I feel... The way I feel towards a man. A man... who holds the greatest prize in the federation, the World Heavyweight Championship belt. The man called 'Mike Montgomery.' My feelings toward this man... the malice and hatred... they were not always present. No, not at all. These feelings were born as a result of reactionary measures. It is not my instinct to hate people. I hate those... who MAKE me hate them. My hostility grows forth from the pain INFLICTED upon me. I am but a mirror, and emulator of this pain... It builds inside me... at first its small... insignificant... but as the malice is shown towards me and the pain increases in velocity... intensity... and frequency... by hatred builds. It collects within... until... until... the breaking point. The first time it ever happened... I'll admit... It was surprised... caught completely off guard... I was never aware of the power... the addictive power that my emotional explosion would have... It felt... so good... I felt... so in control... so dominant... my rage... it felt like... heaven. The idea that my very person could inflict so much pain on another being had never crossed my mind, and now that I had tapped into it, and felt its awesome power... I was hooked on its mystical and erotic allure. When I was hurtiong someone else, I was in total control... I created and destroyed... I was all power... I was a GOD. When I wasn't inflicting pain... I drove my insane... I just HAD to do it again. I wanted to inflict pain... I NEEDED to inflict pain... and I'd stick all by myself in the dark and I'd plot new and better, more gruesome ways to do it. I'd conjure up the most vile and dreadful blue prints for destrution... I couldn't wait to test them out on my victims... I had become.... THE INNOVATOR. But were they really victims? No. I was the victim... I am a reactionary... I was not born with this... it's been a gift... given to me during the coarse of my life and slowly unwrapped over long periods of time... I am the victim... Mike Montgomery was a man that I respected... even liked... I might have even considered him a friend, if there is such a thing. But he... he spat on, humiliated me, he cast me away, degraded me, and dishonored me. All I wanted was a companion... all I ever needed was a friend... Friends... the word doesn't have any meaning anymore... In the beginning... There was Killer Dude, Tommy N. Taylor, and The Plague... but Tommy's gone... and Plague has turned his back on me entirely... my very teacher and the man that I've modeled myself after. I looked up to him, but he too has disowned and disrespected me. Others have come and gone... Jamie Shock... Kevin Starr... and Digital Mortality... I have not yet heard from Digital Mortality... has he forgotten me so easily? Did our friendship mean nothing? Killer Dude... my only companion... perhaps the only 'friend' that was ever true... is gone. I am alone. But this Sunday... I step onto the battlefield alongside the man whom I hate... Mike Montgomery. I have been plotting... and waiting... and thirsting for this moment... Michael... you and I... in the ring... together... I have yearned and ached for this very moment for so long... I'm dying to inflict my pain upon you, the very pain that you have inflicted on me... I shall reflect the suffering upon your body, mind, and soul TEN FOLD! Digital Mortality... I am not aware of your alligiance... your 'friendship' has become blurry... hazed... I am not sure of the move you are to make... but I wish for it to be the wise one... Mike Montgomery is mine... Be a pal, old 'friend', and keep the Scotsman away from me... For the INNOVATOR OF PAIN does not take Sundays off... AND THERE IS MUCH WORK TO BE DONE.... SLEEP TIGHT, MICHAEL... AND KNOW THAT I AM THINKING AND DREAMING OF YOU... and it feels... so... good...
