UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #702

1st Match Back..... and what a doozy it is

15 Sep 2004

Killer Dude — You would love to know, wouldn't you?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera shot opens up upon a dark room. You can see the silhouette of a man sitting on a rather long couch in the corner of the room. The room begins to light up, and from the side, you can see that the man is Killer Dude. You can see that Killer Dude has mixed emotions at this point in time. Killer Dude is struggling to remain calm, as he begins shaking wildly, almost uncontrollably. Mind: You have to do it, Killer. You must do it to gain respect. Killer Dude: No way man, there has to be another way. Mind: Unfortunately, you have no respect, and the wrestlers and fans won't give you respect unless you do what I tell you to. Killer Dude: You lying son of a bitch. Why does it have to be this way? These people love me. Mind: That is where you are wrong. You see, these people could care less about who you are, and what you have accomplished in this fed. All they remember is you wearing that fucked up pube costume a year ago. I'm only trying to help you gain the respect that you don't have, and you know that you don't have it. Killer Dude: Shut the fuck up! I know better than to do that! All the times that I've followed you, you always led me astray. Mind: You will soon find out that it is the only way. Trust me, you will know when the right time has come. You want this as much as I do. You want revenge for the wrongs that people have committed to you. If you listen to my advice, revenge will soon be yours. Killer Dude: You know as well as I do that you can't make those kind of assumptions. You have no fucking clue what is going on in my life right now! Mind: You really are a dumbass, you know that? I am your mind, of course I fucking know what's going on. Killer Dude: All that bullshit doesn't matter to me any more. The past is the past. I need to move forward. Mind: And I can help you do that! Can't you see? I can help get you your career back. I can bring you back to the World Title Reigns. You just say that you want it, and I'll get it for you. Killer Dude: I wish that were true. But unfortunately, it is not. So stop fooling yourself. Mind: You are without a doubt the most ignorant human being I've ever seen or heard of. Killer Dude: Ignorance is bliss, fucker! With a start, Killer Dude stops shaking, and stares at the camera. You can see that he has left sweat stains all over the couch. He glances over at the clock on the table. It says that it is nearing midnight. He decides that it is time to go sleep, when all of a sudden, the phone begins to ring. Suddenly getting a very pissed off look on his face, Killer Dude reaches over to the phone, and yells into the receiver. Killer Dude: Who the fuck is this! We soon find out that the caller is Killer Dude's 'best friend' Punk Rock, who has seemingly followed Killer Dude back into the UWF. Punk Rock: Sorry about calling so late, dude, but I just wanted to tell you about your match at Universal. Killer Dude: What? Oh...... they gave me a match? Punk Rock: Would I have called you otherwise? Killer Dude: Good point. Well, who am I facing? Punk Rock: Well, you are facing two people. The Plague, and the Communist. Killer Dude purposely drops the phone. You can hear Punk Rock asking Killer Dude to pick the phone back up. Killer Dude picks up the phone slowly. Killer Dude: You fucking around? Punk Rock: Nope, deadly serious. Good luck man. Killer Dude: Thanks, I guess. Peace out, bitch! Killer Dude hangs up the phone as Punk Rock begins to protest the bitch statement. He slowly lays down on the couch and falls asleep, as the camera fades to black. ------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------ 'Sold Me' by Seether plays in the background, as the shot reopens with Killer Dude sprawled out on the floor, apparently rolling off of the couch during the night. Suddenly, you can see Killer Dude beginning to wake up. He stretches his legs out as his eyes begin to open. After two or three seconds, he opens his eyes and is now awake. Looking around in confusion, he gets up and sits back down on the couch, looking into the camera to speak. Killer Dude: The Plague..... The Communist....... my first opponents since returning to the UWF ring. The first match since a return is always an important one. Showing the people that you have not lost your step. Showing that you can still hang with the big boys. I used to care about that, but now I don't give a flying fuck. You all have made me start over again, and I am looking for my revenge. Killer Dude: Plague, Communist, you two will only be the first victims of my rage. I will take no pleasure in only fighting you. I am going out there tomorrow night, looking to end somebody's career. Sorry guys, that's just the way it is sometimes. Last time around, I deserved more than what I got. I didn't know what to do back then. But now, I am a smarter man and I know that I can do whatever I want, whenever I want. I can't punish the owner from last time, as he packed up and left town. Killer Dude: But unfortunately for you two, I can, and I WILL punish you. I will take both of your bodies, and crush them like twigs. I will leave the ring on Thursday Night, leaving the wake of the devestation that I have wreaked upon The Plague and The Communist, only because they got in my way. You two will be the first. The first victims to fall by the wayside, as I continue on my quest for revenge, and ultimately, to be the best there is! Soon enough, I will again have the feeling of gold around my waist. Killer Dude: It is a really simple thing, actually. After Plague and Communist, I will move on to the next challengers I must destroy, and then I will move on. It is a very logical process. The more people that I injure, the closer I am to becoming the champion. After he completes his final sentence, Killer Dude just stares at the camera, laying down on the couch, with his blue eyes piercing the camera. Suddenly, someone knocks on his door. Killer Dude slowly opens the door, and peers out, not allowing the camera to see who it is. Man: Look at yourself. You look pathetic. Killer Dude suddenly gets a furious look in his eyes, ready to take the intruder's head off. Man: Oh, rod damnit. I just woke you up, didn't I? Killer Dude: Who the fuck are you? Man: You know who I am. If you don't know, then you really are a dumbarse. Killer Dude: Drop the bullshit and show yourself already. Take off the freaking ski mask, and show me your fucking face. Man: Your showing me! That anger, that look that you just want to beat the shit out of someone! That's the thing I like about you, Killer. Killer Dude: Look dude, I don't roll that way, all right? Man: Killer, Killer, Killer. You know who I am. Hell, you know who I am very well! Open your frickin eyes man. I'm here to help you, and I also want you to help me. Sound of a ski mask getting pulled off. Killer Dude: Jesus Christ Almighty, it's you! Man: You damn right, it's me! Remember the Regal Rumble man. Hell, you were the 15th entrant. You got eliminated 9th from last. You have showed me the potential that I have been waiting to see from you ever since I came to the UWF. I think that we would be perfect together, teaming up, wouldn't you? Killer Dude: What the fuck do you want from me? Man: I want you to help me. In return, I will help you. You see, I've had this nagging problem that's been on my back since before Terror in Tokyo, and I have made a plan that will help me take him out, and to take you into the lime light. Killer Dude: I'm game, I guess. Tell me what we need to do. Man: All right. But first, you must get rid of the camera that is behind your door. Killer Dude: True. Killer Dude waves at the camera and punches the monitor quickly and cleanly. The camera fuzzes out, and slowly fades to black.
