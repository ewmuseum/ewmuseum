UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #346

Winds of Change

17 Apr 2004

Ezra Kazee — The Locker Room.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in the arena. There is very little action, although sitting in the middle of the room is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee along with another man. The other man has a notepad sitting across his lap, and looks to be a reporter of some sorts. Kazee is wearing a t-shirt and a pair of shorts. The reporter is wearing plaid pants and a polo shirt.) Reporter: Hello. My name is Gerald Kommando. And today I’m sitting here with a UWF Fan favorite and current holder of the Intercontinental belt as well as half of the Tag Team Titles match. A man who truly believe in the simple things. A man who is respected by friends and enemies alike for his sportsmanlike attitude and his ability in the ring. That man is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee…… This weekend, he is involved in a Tag Team Title match with his partner Mike Montgomery against the returning Digital Mortality and his newly formed alliance with Moraless. How are you doing today Ezra? Kazee: Fine, how about yourself. Kommando: Great. Thank you so much for your time in granting this interview. Kazee: No Problem Kommando: This weekend, you have a huge tag team title match against two men who claim that the future is very bleak. How do you respond to that? Kazee: The future is bleak? Well, that’s two people’s perspectives. In my perspective, the future is bright and shining. Look at the talent we have in the UWF. Look at all the new blood that has been brought up. From top to bottom we have one of the most talented rosters in the world, and we also have one of the most promising rookie classes in the world. We have a greatly talented man as our World Champion in Beelzebozo. We have extremely talented men and tag teams lining up week in and week out to challenge for the IC belt or the Tag Team Belts…… To put on top of that, we have a great rookie class right now as well. Nick Persia and Iron Lung who are both relatively new to the fed have both already been THC Champions. Chaos Society has been a thorn in everyone’s side. Bondage is still trying to find his niche’ but he’s made quite a few waves. And this week we have more debuts of more promising talent. The future of the UWF is definitely not bleak. In my opinion it is brighter than ever. Kommando: According to Moraless, you and Monty don’t understand what it takes to bring the UWF back to the top and that you two are stuck in the past. Kazee: The past is the past. What else can be said about it? Are we trying to stop the new age of the UWF, not at all. Are we doing something to bring it back to the top? In my opinion, your hella right we are. There are always two paths of thought as to what is right. What have we done, what has happened to bring the UWF back to the top? Like I mentioned before, we have an influx of new talent. We also have old talent coming back. The Plague, Digital Mortality, hell, myself if we really want to bring things back. Kommando: He also attributes fear to the reason y’all are against their form of change. Kazee: Fear can always be an issue. Nobody knows what their plan is. They haven’t said anything except tearing the federation down and building it back up. Does that scare people, your damn right it does. I noticed also that Moraless said something about a turf war and needing three things, those being determination, purpose, and numbers. Well, I have four things for him as well….. It’s called the four room apartment. Basically, when change is happening, everybody goes through these four stages, or rooms, of an apartment. Those rooms are fear, denial, confusion, and acceptance. Moraless, when you knew that Monty and I were trying to bring about change and a better UWF, well, you seem to have moved into the four room apartment. Now, I say your somewhere between fear and denial. And that’s why Digital Mortality is here. You brought him in to stop our change and bring about your own…… Kommando: What is the real issue in this match? Kazee: Well, Moraless seems to have hit it right on the head. This match isn’t about the Tag Team Titles. Why those are on the line is truly beyond me. What this match is about two sets of ideals that are going to meet head on. But this match is just the beginning, I can feel it. This match is going to go on and on until someone can finally win the war. That’s what this is really about. Whose ideals will prevail. Whose ideals will be listened to within the UWF on trying to turn it around. Kommando: Let’s switch gears a little bit. Digital Mortality recently said that you were just in their way. Kazee: Not much I can say to that. Your right, I am in their way, Monty is in their way. Their way in our opinion is wrong. And their way must not be allowed to succeed. Kommando: Mortality claims that you’re being manipulated by Montgomery. Any truth to that? Kazee: Does it look like I’m being manipulated by Monty? I mean honestly here, I’m still my own man. I still do what I want. Monty, he’s a great person, and we disagree sometimes, but that’s just a matter of the fact. But the true fact of the matter is that I could leave the Players Club anytime I wanted to. But there is no reason for me to do that. There is no reason for me to leave and leave Joshua and Monty high and dry…… I joined the Players Club for a few reasons. I joined the stable out of respect for both Joshua and Monty. These two men are great talents, and are men who can and will go a long way. We respect each other. And that is the most important thing in this stable. Secondly, I joined the stable because I know how everything works in the world of professional wrestling, and if I can have a couple of people to watch my back while I watch there’s, seems pretty mutually beneficial to me. Kommando: Digital says the one thing that makes his alliance strong with Moraless is that Moraless knows his mission. Do you know what the mission of the Players Club is? Kazee; Sure I do. It’s to be the best damn stable we can be and give the fans what they want week in and week out. Our mission is to give the show that these fans deserve week in and week out. Our mission is to make sure the fans want to come see us and the UWF. Have we been successful? Sure we have. The net is still a buzz from the TLC match that went down at Renaissance. Unfortunately, sometimes forces work against us. Such as the time that Moraless no-showed for our IC title match. But we still give people what they expect week in and week out. Kommando: Digital Mortality says you should put away your action figures and such that you’re so fond of playing with sometimes. Kazee: I guess that’s just him and his preference. I like to have fun. And there is nothing wrong with that. If there was ever a point were I wasn’t having fun, I’d leave in a heart beat. But this is not only about the fans for me, but it’s also about my own fun. You see, there is another full circle thing. For the fans to care, they have to know that I’m having fun out there, and that I enjoy doing what I do, and for me to have fun, I need the fans to be there giving me their support. It’s a push-push relationship. No, I’m not going to put away my action figures or anything else that I have. What I am going to do is go out there and do what I do best day after day week after week. Kommando: You have any final comments to say? Kazee: Plus ca change, plus c’est la meme chose. The more things change, the more things stay the same. We are using an age old formula to bring the UWF back to glory, and no matter how much we change, things are going to evolve back to the way they were. Abraham Lincoln once said that “It is not best to be swapping horses while crossing the river.” Well, we are crossing the river, and it’s definitely not time to change the horses. Why not work with us Digital and Moraless. Why not help us bring the UWF back to a second Golden Age instead of working against us…… But if you continue to work against us, all I can say is Good Luck, because in this war, we are both going to need it. Kommando: Thank you Mr. Kazee. This is Gerald Kommando for E-Wrestling Weekly with “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee signing off. (scene fades to black.)
