UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #712

reality

18 Sep 2004

Dreammaker — locker room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(scene opens up with a scream heard from an open locker room door. A camera man runs threw the door and finds dreammaker punching his locker.) cm:what the hell is all this noise? DM: you know what MR. reporter! i am sick of disappointment. I am sick and tired of not playing my game. I have wrestled 2 matches and i have tried to get the crowd to like me and i have tried to get the wrestlers to respect me by throwing off unchallenged moves. But thats not my game, I want to slow you down, make you beleive that you pain is real. Reality is but a dream, and i am gonna make everyones dreams my reality! CM: what in the hell does that actually mean? Dm: it means retard, that no more wrestling for everyone else. Now its my time, now i show everyone what its like. I have never been pinned and i wont be. REmember that. they can throw 2 guys at me and i will still keep coming. its not about winnign and losing, its about respect and showing the world that I am here for a reason. cm: and what would that reason be? dm: kinda nosey arent ya? you will all find out. And Mr. wolf. No one is afraid of a has-been. but everyone is afraid of their dreams. Epecially the ones that they cant control. It is time to say nighty night to the world and kiss it good bye. (dreammaker then pushes the camerman aside and grabs his gym bag and ducks out the door and goes down the hall way as the camera fades out)
