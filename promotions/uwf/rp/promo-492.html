In the Beginning

4 Jul 2004

Angel — Various Locations Over Time

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://mysite.wanadoo-members.co.uk/raynus2781/angelrp1.htm Use the RP page above for a more colourful view :P Apologies if this appears several times, im still learning ----------------------------------------------------------- Summer 2000, Angel is in a 20-Man over the top rumble in the GIWF. A quick close line and Angel is smashed out the ring falling heavily on his knee. His career as a wrestler is over, the knee is too far-gone and there is no surgery that can fix the knee to make Angel return at one hundred percent fighting capacity. Angel’s livelihood is no more. Tanya, Enrico and Zeus have left Angel. No money to his name no career to promote, there is no point in staying with the once great wrestler. Angel is left on his own to pay off his creditors, forced to sell everything in his life. From having houses in San Francisco and Rio de Janeiro, Angel now lives in a small squalid apartment in New York barely scrapping in enough money to pay off the rent. It is now a winter; New York has turned into the freezing abyss it does every December and Angel is out on the streets of the city trying to earn himself some money for the bare necessities…food, rent and now drugs. The scene opens with Angel slowly walking down a snow covered road, he is wearing an old torn coat wrapped round his now rather gaunt figure. The snow is falling like icy pitchforks and Angel hurries along the road to make it to the small wooden door leading to his apartment inside. Finding his apartment in the darkness of this dead end place Angel opens the door into his small dank abode. Angel chucks the old coat on the chair near the door and walks over to the window, the snow is falling hard almost as if the sky is about to fall in and end the life of this depressing city. Moving from the window, Angel slumps down on his bed pulling off his old boots. Sitting lonely at the end of his bed, Angel stares blankly at the bare wall trying to remember his days of glory. But nothing appears, the months of drink and drugs have dulled his mind into a stupor and all his can remember is when to pick his pay cheque up. Angel puts his head in hands and weeps. Angel mumbles to himself the same words, over and over. Angel: Where did my life go? Angel wipes away his tears and opens the draw on the small bedside cabinet; he pulls out a jar of pills along with a bottle of Vodka. Pouring more or less the whole pill jar into his hands Angel tips them into his mouth and washes it down with some Vodka. Angel’s eyes grow heavy…he gets up…slowly and walks out of his apartment and out onto the dark icy streets. Angel cannot control himself; he grows more tired and falls into a pile of rubbish outside, falls into darkness and into an unknown place in his mind. Angel’s heart begins to beat slower and slower, the thumps becoming slower and slower…and finally…nothing. But it was not the end, life breathed into Angel’s body and a voice came into his mind. Voice: Wake up Angel, it is not your time. Wake up Angel you have much to do. Angel awoke, he was not in the streets of New York…he was in a Hospital…again Angel falls back into his sleep. The voice came again to Angel. Voice: Wake up Angel, you need to finish what you started. I have sent you a mentor; she will help you with your quest. Angel awoke again, he was in an old wooden building the surrounds seemed blurred to him but he could tell he was no longer in the Hospital. Angel: Where am I? A figure appeared over Angel and Angel tried to focus on the figure. It was a dark haired oriental looking woman, she had a smile on her face as she lent down to dampen Angel’s forehead with a cloth. Angel: Who are you? Ayami: I am Ayami; I am here to guide you. You are in a small village just outside Hong Kong. Angel tries to get up but Ayami forces him down. Ayami: No, you must rest. We have much to do, but first you must get your strength back. Angel leans back on his bed and falls back into a deep sleep, this time he felt calmer and felt it easy to dream of his previous victories in the GIWF. Remember the good times and the bad times Angel slept for several days until he was woke by Ayami. Ayami nudges Angel’s shoulder gently. Ayami: Angel, you must wake now you need to begin. Angel: Begin? Begin what? How did I get hear? Ayami: Your were found slumped unconscious and not breathing on the streets in New York, you were taken to the local hospital. But, you died. We brought your body to this place…then after days of prayer you were revived. Angel: Revived…by prayer? How? Ayami: There is more to you than first meets the eye, tell me this do you remember your parents? Angel: I do not. Ayami: Angel do you have any odd visions or daydreams that you cannot explain? Angel: I do occasionally of an old village in ancient China…then I vision parts of the Middle East and again the land seems ancient to me. What has this got to do with my miraculous recovery? Ayami: Someday, perhaps I could explain those visions to you but what I can tell you is someone will not allow you to die until you have completed your task. You have died in hospital, but we knew that you could be revived by our prayers it seems this task is important to someone. So much so, you will not be allowed to pass until this task is completed. Angel: But I tried, before and was defeated. Exorcizandis was too much for me Ayami: You weren’t trained well enough; you were not versed in my style of Kung Fu. If I teach you my Kung Fu you can defeat the evil…not just Exorcizandis but the evil that pulls this world down into something more terrible than anyone can imagine. The evil is becoming too strong you must train now, 4years then you must return. Angel: So, what is this style of Kung Fu I must learn? Ayami: It is style of Kung Fu that has been kept in my family for more than a thousand years. It has been used to fight evil and the evil that corrupts the world. It is simply know as ‘Snake in the Devils Shadow’. Angel: I must think on this. Ayami: Of course, you have until sunset. Ayami left the room leaving Angel to think on this great task. Was Angel up for such an important task was what Ayami telling him the truth and he was significant in this fight against evil? Angel’s intent had always been to defend against evil, but this seemed on a grander scale. Sunset came and Angel walked out of the room out into the courtyard of this building. Some people were practicing Kung Fu; they noticed Angel and stopped sparing to bow to him. Angel returned the gesture with a nervous bow. Angel noticed Ayami on the other side of the courtyard and walked over to her. Ayami looked up and smiled to Angel. Angel: I have decided to train with you, but one thing I must ask. If we fight evil, why do we do it on a pro wrestling show? Ayami: The pro wrestling shows go all around the world and are televised to the furthest reaches of this planet. It is the best form to spread our word and the best place to turn those corrupted by evil. Angel: Then what federation do we join…the GIWF? Ayami: We have four years of training a head; we shall decide that at the end. So lets stop the chat and begin. [Scene Fades] The years passed and Angel learnt this unique style of Kung Fu, he became stronger more focused and was ready to start to his task once more. Ayami; now his wife, would follow Angel to ends of the Earth to help him with his task. [Scene Opens] The scene opens in the main street of the village Angel has spent the last 4years. Angel is sitting outside the building he trained in, which has now been turned into a small Dojo. Angel is talking to Ayami. Angel: So, the 4years are up and I must begin my task once more. My dearest, my light…you are to travel with me yes? Ayami: Angel, I would travel with you anywhere to the far reaches to help defeat the evil that engulfs this world like a black veil. Angel: Then it is time, I must return to America and I must join the GIWF. Ayami: The GIWF has changed Angel, it is now the UWF it is even more filled with evil than it’s predecessor. Angel and Ayami pick up their bags and walk along the small dusty main road of the village. Angel: When will we return to this village? Ayami: Soon, we will simply get the wrestler’s attentions in the UWF. We must remain here to continue the training. Angel: But have I not learnt all that there is to teach? Ayami: Maybe, but you need to keep practicing to keep in shape to stop this evil we must keep ourselves in sharp as much as we can. Angel: Then that is what I will do, I shall continue to train. I cannot lose this time; I cannot let the evil return to this world and destroy it like it has done before. Angel and Ayami continue to walk along the path. After what seems like hours of silence Angel speaks. Angel: Ayami, you never told me what my visions meant? I still get them regularly even to this day. Ayami: I don’t think now is the time; I cannot explain them myself really. I have to be sure before I can tell you. I wouldn’t want to…scare you. Angel: Scare me, how? Ayami: Those visions are of your past…that’s all I can say. Angel: So you’re saying, those images of ancient China are my past? Ayami: Please let us not discuss this further. Angel: Ok, I understand. I am sorry my love, I trust you whatever the circumstances. Ayami leans over to Angel and kisses him on the cheek. Ayami: I know Angel. [The scene fades] [The scene opens] The scene opens again in the airport as Angel and Ayami are exiting the airplane. Angel looks a little worried and Ayami notices this. Ayami: What is wrong Angel? Angel: It’s just; I haven’t been here since…I apparently passed away. It scares me I feel like I shouldn’t be here. Ayami: Angel listen, your meant to be here. You would have died on that street forever if it not for your task. You must return and you must continue your task, we must enter the UWF and start from the bottom to fight our way up destroying the evil in that federation. This thing is bigger than you and I; you have been given the task to fight for our salvation. It is time for you to return. Do not be scared, this is meant to be. Angel smiles. Angel: Thank you, I think I can handle it. I must be…strong. Angel and Ayami continue out of the airport and the scene fades. [Scene Fades] So it is set, the beginning of the fight against evil. Out for 4years and believed to be dead, Angel is finally returning to complete the task he started all that time ago. Defeated then by the insanely evil Exorcizandis, Angel revitalised and versed in the ancient’s styles of Kung Fu is back. Angel is more focused than before, with him Ayami his wife who herself is more deadly than even Angel. They are a force of good, to help those in need and to destroy those who think of nothing but themselves, and cheat their way to the top. The war against good and evil has started; whoever wins will decide the fate of the world. It is now up to Angel to try and tip the balance in the favour of good. For if it tips in the favour of the bad, then the world is truly lost.
