UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #829

Second Chances

13 Dec 2004

Adien Pryde — Chaos Society Locker Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens it outside of the Chaos Society locker room. A camera crew with the ever wonderful ‘Snap’ Meyer in tow approaches. ‘Snap’ knocks on the door.] ‘Snap’ : Hello, anyone in there? Voice 1 : What do you want? ‘Snap’ : Can I have a word with you? Voice 2 : Depends on who you want? [The door suddenly opens part way and standing in the doorway is Joanna, Manager and trainer for The Chaos Society.] Joanna : You again, what do you want? We have some strategizing to do. ‘Snap’ : I just wanted to get a comment from Adien Pryde about why he attacked Tsunami Oni, his own tag partner at the Venetian Home Rumble. [The door is opened wider and Adien Pryde is standing directly in front of ‘Snap’ now.] Pryde : So you want to know why I turned my back on the great Tsunami Oni. You want to know why I blew my chance at winning the Tag Team Title. DO YOU? [Rather sheepishly ‘Snap’ answers.] ‘Snap’ : Ah, Yes? Pryde : Why? Why? The reason is right there. [Pryde pushes the door open all the way and points to Reo Dai sitting on a chair in the room.] Pryde : That man there is who I came into the UWF with and he has worked his way through surgery and rehab to get back into ring shape and compete for the belts we came into this fed for in the first place and I will not do it with anyone else, not even the man, Tsunami Oni. ‘Snap’ : But it was the fans that voted you into that match and this is how you repaid them, by turning on the loved World Champion. Pryde : Screw the fans. They didn’t vote me in, they wanted to see Killer Dude team with Oni and when the promoters realized that Dude couldn’t be on two teams at once they felt sorry for me and tossed me in the match. ‘Snap’ : How can you say that about the wonderful UWF fans? Pryde : The same way I led them all on thinking I gave a shit that I enjoyed their cheering a few months back. The members of Chaos Society are the only people I need approval from. ‘Snap’ : Funny you should mention the members of Chaos Society. Your match tomorrow is a second chance for you and The Communist to get into the Contest of Champions but with a twist. Your stable mate, Raid is also in it. Pryde : We already know that loser. That is what the strategizing was about. We know that between Raid and I that The Communist is a non-issue. But as far as Raid and I, it really is about who is the better man. Raid has the size and strength and I have the speed and experience. Will either of use make a big deal about it if we lose, only if it is for some ungodly reason to The Communist. ‘Snap’ : So you are telling me that you don’t fell threatened by the UWF promoters pitting member against member? Pryde : How many fucking time do I have to tell you that we are stable mates first and opponents second. If I win, I win. If Raid wins, he wins. Now if Commie wins then that is something that will have to be taken care of. ‘Snap’ : You said that you are ready to go after the Tag Titles again as Anarchy, does that mean that you have no interest in this Contest of Champion? Pryde : Hell no. This tourney could do wonders for our recognition and if I could actually win it, it would only bring another championship to Chaos Society. But our main concern right now is getting our hands on the current champions, Kenny Mercury and Jamie Shock. Once we get them in the ring, it will be all about Total Anarchy and the one, two, three. ‘Snap’ : Any word on Ezra Kazee’s condition? Joanna : That will be taken care of in a separate news conference. [A hand reaches out and grabs the camera lens and aims it toward the body attached to it.] Raid : Is there any problem Adien? Pryde : No problems Raid, these losers were just leaving. ‘Snap’ : We were? Joanna : We were waiting for you. Get in here. Raid : As soon as I am done here. [Joanna closes the door as Raid approaches ‘Snap’ Meyer and the camera fades to black with the words “To Be Continued...”]
