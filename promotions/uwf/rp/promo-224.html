UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #224

House of 1000 Roaches

16 Feb 2004

Raid — Somewhere outside of Detroit

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(As the scene opens the camera zooms in on Raid sitting in a small office, and watching an old black and white TV. As he sits there he reaches to his left side and opens a lunch box and pulls out a sandwich. He starts to take a bite but then realizes the camera is focused in on him. As he turns around to address the camera a telephone rings. He reaches for the phone and presses the speaker phone button to answer it.) (Raid):Raids Extermination service. How can I help you? (Little old lady): Well I seem to have a roach problem and I was wondering what you charge? (Raid):Well it depends on how long it will take to rid you of your infestation. How big of a roach problem do you have Madam? (Old lady):They are all over the place! I’ve had three other exterminators here already and they couldn’t get rid of them. Please sir you’ve got to help me you’re the last one listed in the phone book! (Raid):Well I’m on my way but I can already tell you that this isn’t gonna be cheap! It sounds like you have a major infestation and I’m gonna have to pull out the big one on this job! (Old Lady):Do what ever you have to do I don’t care! I just want these roaches GONE! (Click. Raid hangs up the phone and starts to walk over to the wall where his coveralls are. On the wall you see two different pairs of coveralls one white set that look like the ones he wears when he wrestles and a black set that aren’t familiar. He grabs the black ones and puts them on. After he puts them on and zips them up he turns around and you see a big red “A” with a circle around it on the back. He grabs his gear and heads out the door. The scene cuts to a large house with Raids truck outside. You see Raid and a little old lady standing on the porch of the house. The camera man quickly scurries over to were Raid and the woman are.) (Raid):So where do the roaches seem the worst madam? (Old lady):They’re all over! They just keep coming! But I think they are mostly in the kitchen. (Raid):You stay here madam I’ll be right back. I have to check it out and see what I’m gonna need. (Raid rushes off and goes into the house. A few seconds later he rushes out of the house and stops near the old lady and the camera.) (Raid):HOLY SHIT LADY! How the hell did it get that bad in there? I’m gonna have to call for backup! (Old lady):Do what ever it takes. Just please get those creatures out of my house! (Raid reaches into his pocket and pulls out a cell phone. He starts to dial a number. As he puts the phone up to his ear he starts to walk away from the camera and the old lady. The camera man begins to follow him but Raid motions him to stay where he is. After a brief conversation he walks back over to the old lady.) (Raid):I just had to call in my backup. This job is defiantly to big for just one man. They should be here in a few minutes. (The old lady nods her head and goes over to the porch and sits on a rocking chair. She starts to read he newspaper and just as she flips the page squealing tires can be heard behind the camera man. He quickly turns to see who that was. As the camera pans to the street you see a truck very similar to Raids and two large men jump out. They are wearing the same black coveralls as Raid and are carrying a large cylindrical object. As they get closer you can see writing on the side of the can and it says “BUG BOMB 3000”. They walk up to Raid and set the can down. The camera man then notices something on the back of their coveralls and zooms in on it. It appears to be the same red “A” that was on the back of raids uniform.) (Unknown Men):So how bad it is Raid? (Raid):Well its REALLY bad and that’s why we needed the big one. Well we should probably get to work. ( Raid walks over to the old lady and motions for his partners to follow him. They pick up the “BUG BOMB 3000” as they walk towards the porch.) (Raid):Well madam it looks like were gonna have to use the big one and you won’t be able to go into your house for a couple of days. I hope you have a place to stay. (Old lady):Oh yes I can stay at my daughters house for the week. It should be ok go back they right? (Raid):Yes, it will be ok for you to come back home then. Well we had better set this thing up and you should be on your way. You don’t want to be around when this thing goes off. (Old lady):Could you wait a couple of minutes so I can gather some of my things? (The old lady doesn’t wait for an answer and goes into her house. She comes out of the house a few minutes later and hops in her can and takes off. Raid begins to walk over to the camera man with a gas mask.) (Raid):If your gonna stick around I would suggest that you put this on for your own safety. This ones gonna be a doosey! Well lets get going I don’t want to be here all day. (Raid and his two companions begin walking into the house. Raid motions for the camera man to stay outside. A few minutes later the three walk outside and walk up to the camera. They stand between the camera and the house. Raid has a remote in his hand and he is about to push the button when he looks directly into the camera with a very serious look on his face. (Raid):Seymour Almasy your plans to win the Intercontinental Championship blew up in your face this past Sunday. Well let me tell you one thing. If your not careful you will have the BUG BOMG blow up in your face. It will be very similar to the BUG BOMB that is about to blow up in this very house and believe me just like this house YOU Will Not Survive! You think you can just stroll in to the UWF and win by not doing anything? I can assure you that won’t get you very far. This Sunday at Supremacy I will take my first step towards the Intercontinental Championship. And you are the first roach in my way and you will be EXTERMINATED! (Raid):And as for my FORMER partner, yes I’m talking to you Ezra, if you think you are going to win the IC title I will assure you it won’t happen. See the mistake you made is that you trusted me. And you sat there and talked about how we were the greatest Tag Team the Indys have ever seen. Well you see just soon after you left a new tag team came into the picture and they were phenomenal. The talent and skill these two have is far beyond anything that the UWF has ever seen .You and Montgomery may have won the Tag titles but that’s ok because you wont have them for very long. For when these two come after you and hunt you down there is no escaping. And you had better hope the Players Club is strong enough to withstand the torture that The CHAOS SOCIETY will impose upon you, because we are coming for you! (As camera begins to zoom out Raid holds up the remote and presses the button. The camera starts shaking and quickly zooms in on the house as all of the windows are blown out . There is smoke coming from the holes where there were windows and flames begin to pour out of the house engulfing the entire complex. Within a matter of seconds the flames are 40 to 50 feet above the roof . The camera pans back to find Raid and his friends but the three are already walking towards their trucks. They get into the trucks and speed away leaving the camera man standing in front of a burning house. The camera pans back to the house and begins to zoom in on the flames. All you can see now is flames and the scene fades to black.)
