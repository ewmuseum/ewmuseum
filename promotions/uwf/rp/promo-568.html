UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #568

Day 4: Wrong Place, Right Time

23 Jul 2004

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — UWF Arena, After Hours

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Day 4 [Another sun sets. Another day of productivity sweeps across the Windy City. Another night full of rowdy barfights waits in store for the downtown Loop, another quiet evening along the Pier. So much variety, so much vanity, so much of everything tossed together in the Great American Melting Pot they call Chicago.] [When the city starts to sleep, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins starts to creep.] … … […static…static…static…static…] […static…static…] [The jarred view of the camera slowly comes into focus from the white noise and snow, quickly emerging from the sea of undistinguished blurs and into the sharp world of the backstage area in the UWF Arena, after hours. Rich Rollins - his face unshaven and his eyes bloodshot from little sleep - greets the opening few moments of footage, a wry grin playing across his thin mouth and tickling the corners of those bloodshot blue eyes. Absently working moisture into his lips with his tongue, The Renegade speaks.] Renegade: Welcome back to “A Week in the Life of Rich Rollins,” filmed on location right here at the UWF Arena. It’s been four days since I’ve seen sunlight, boys and girls. But it ain’t as bad as it sounds… the building has a kitchen and a full gym, so I’ve kept myself busy. [The camera starts to pan backward, slowly revealing the room behind Rollins. It’s obviously the same locker room as before, but there are many major differences. A pile of thin linen sheets sits crumpled up on the athletic bench in the center of the room, another sheet spread across the bench itself, obviously acting as a bed. The UWF World Television Championship belt hangs snapped through a coat hanger in a small locker to the left, its goldtone sheen catching a glint in the camera lens from the overhead lighting. Spreading his arms wide to encompass his “bedroom,” Rollins smirks.] Renegade: Home is where you hang your hat, or so they say. Truth be told, I’ve never really had a hat to hang, but I didn’t make the damn saying up. This is where I’ve slept the last three nights, comfy and cozy within the confines of the arena. It’s actually quite an accommodating place… the food sucks, but the janitors don’t seem to mind. Yesterday, the arena was home to the Chicago-based Bull Ropers Club… four thousand toothless Yankees and their smelly steers tromping around where the UWF’s ring usually sits… it was enough to make me want to scream. Pack up your steers and queers and head back to Texas, boys. Chicago ain’t your kind of town. [The Renegade reaches his arms around the view of the camera, and suddeny it shakes and spins, apparently being perched against Rollins’ eye. Now in control, The Renegade sweeps the view back across the locker room and out into the hallway. The camera bounces in time with his footsteps, taking in the chipped plaster and paint along the walls and the cold, cement floor.] Renegade: Today we’re going to take a look into the heart and soul that is the Universal Wrestling Federation. The place where all the decisions get made, where careers are begun and ended at the whim of one particular man… [Rounding a corner, the hallway is noticeably more well-kept and maintained. The concrete floor is replaced by hard polished wood, the walls decorated with promotional pictures for past Pay-Per-View events and specific wrestlers. Slowly taking in the history of UWF memorabilia, The Renegade stops at a single door with a single name written on a gold plate.] [Richard Hayes.] Renegade: That’s right, boys and girls. Let’s see if the big boss-man is home… [Rollins’ hand reaches for the handle and twists.] [Locked.] Renegade: Well… nobody ever accused me of being logical. [Taking a step back, The Renegade charges the door and kicks his right foot out, bashing the thick oak door just below the nob. A sharp snap escapes from the frame, and the door swings wide open.] [His eyes just as wide as the door, Jeremy Gonzolas jumps back from behind Hayes’ desk, almost spilling a full cup of coffee down his button-up work shirt.] Jeremy: Holy SHIT…! Renegade: What the hell…? Are you supposed to be in here? Jeremy: No. Are you? Renegade: Nope. Jeremy: What the hell are you still doing here, anyway? Renegade: I’m filming a behind-the-scenes documentary, exposing the day-to-day rituals of the UWF Arena. I’ve locked myself in since Sunday, and I’m not leaving until after Supremacy this week. Jeremy: Supremacy this week…? Rollins, Supremacy is in Nova Scotia, which is where Hayes and the rest of the roster are heading. So I’ll ask again… what the hell are you still doing here? [Silence.] Renegade: Nova Scotia? Jeremy: Yup. Halifax, to be exact. They won’t return until Wednesday, when Underground comes back here to Chicago. Didn’t you get the memo? [Rollins ignores the question, and instead pans the camera around the incredibly plush office. Ornate wallpaper, covered with plaques and awards and replicas of old GIWF titles hang in cases, centered around the massive oak desk Jeremy had not-so-discretely been snooping through. Zooming the view in a close-up of Jeremy’s pale face, The Renegade emits a laugh.] Renegade: Looks like the documentary will have to wait. So, what are you doing in here, exactly? I know Hayes doesn’t pay you to snoop through his private things. Jeremy: I wasn’t snooping. Renegade: (dripping with sarcasm) Riiiiight… Jeremy: Seriously. I was just… (he motions to the titles hanging on the wall)… I like looking at them. The old ones, the originals. The GIWF titles. They bring back a lot of memories. Renegade: You’ve got a bit of an obssession with wrestling history, kid. My advise to you… either lace up some boots and make some history of your own, or hit the road. It doesn’t do you any good to fetch a cup of decaf every time the boss cracks his whip. Jeremy: Whatever. Don’t you have a plane to catch? Renegade: Yeah, I do. Jeremy: Then you’d better hit the road yourself. [Trying to stay balanced enough to keep the coffee in his hand from spilling everywhere, Jeremy Gonzolas huffs out of the room, brushing past both Rollins and the camera none too gently. Rollins follows his departure down the hall, the footsteps echoing against the history-rich walls of the ornate hallway. Sighing, Rollins speaks to nobody in particular.] Renegade: Politics. Can’t say two words without pissing somebody off. [A finger comes around the edge of the camera lens, reaching for the power switch…] […static…static…static…] […static…static…] […static…] [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
