UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #1494

Mission Accomplished

2 May 2006

"Killer" Kyle Wild — Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens outside the building in Chicago that houses the UWF Offices. A huge banner hangs from the building that proclaims in big bold friendly letters “Mission Accomplished!” Killer Kyle Wild stands at a microphone and makes his announcement. Wild: “I said it… I did it… Mission Accomplished!!!! I came to the UWF for one purpose… To destroy it. To tear it down to it’s base elements, to make it a shell of it’s former self, to abso-fucking-loutely kill the legacy of The Stylemeister, Lyle “In Style” Wile. Let there be no doubt that the UWF is no more…” “WNU has been Cancelled, SAS is no more… Hell, half the freaking roster has moved on to find new and exciting job opportunities in the Fast Food Industry. I take total responsibility for what has happened to your beloved UWF.” “But now there is new work to be done… Killer Kyle Wild is not done with wrestling, far from it…. Now that I’ve accomplished what I set out to do, I’ve set out on a new mission and have decided to build it back up again in MY IMAGE!” “You are about to witness the greatest epoch in Wrestling History, The Era of The Kylemeister, Killer Kyle Wild is dawning… And it starts at Monday Night Mojo, I’m going into that tournament to establish my reign at the top of the Sports Entertainment World. Wrestling will be synonymous with Kyle Wild, The pretenders, hacks and has-beens that you saw in the old UWF are about to be brushed aside, flushed down the toilet of irrelevancy… There can be only one victor at the Lethal Lottery and it is GOING TO BE ME!!!!!” “I know what you are thinking as you sit there at home, on your couch pushing cream puffs and other assorted tasty treats down your throat… You’re thinking, “But Kylemeister, there are so many established wrestlers that are going to be at the Lethal Lottery, How can you win????” “The answer to your question is this… Shut your freaking pie hole!!! Who is the second generation wrestling superstar??? ME!!!! Who destroyed the UWF??? ME!!!!!” “What has Clyde, Axel Action, Sledge or any of the other dollar store, cookie cutter, cut from the same boring old cloth, Disney mass produced cartoon character competitors done to gain the respect of anyone??? Why should The Kylemeister fear these nobodys? How can they actually hope to compete against me?” “Actually it might save a lot of time, trouble and pointless effort on everyone’s part if Monday Night Mojo was more of symbolic passing of the torch ceremony than wrestling card… We could have a little dinner, listen to a little music, watch some little blonde bimbos shake their slutty little asses on stage… then Clyde could do the honourable thing and just give me that silly little UWF Championship Belt… Which incidentally will be renamed something befitting a champion of my stature and talent… Hell, it could save us all a little time and trouble… But everyone knows that ain’t going to happen… But there is nothing that can stop the inevitable… Monday Night Mojo, The Kylemeister will reshape the UWF… Nothing will be the same again!!! The scene fades
