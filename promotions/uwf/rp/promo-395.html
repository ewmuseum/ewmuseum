UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #395

Dead man!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins backstage after SAS as Ruff Ryder has just gotten dressed and heard the news that Malcom Kennedy turned traitor and jumped to Resurection. Ryder is livid, but in true superstar style he doesn't seem all too concerned. ) Ryder: Holding you back? Holding you back? You ain't shit boy! I mean hell, half the time you are no-showing and the other half you're giving sorry ass performances in the ring. You let your ass get beat by Mentok for God's sake! What kind of pussy gets beat by that idiot? Certainly not the True Indian Superstar that's for sure. I beat him and you lost to him so what does that tell you? Who's the dead weight on the Prime Alliance now? Maybe it is dead, but the dead can rise again so you just remember that, and until that day comes i'll just have to whip ass solo. I got no problems with that though, because whippin ass is what Ruff Ryder does best and you better believe he's got a special beatin in mind for you Kennedy. You see if there's one thing I can't stand it's a fuckin traitor. I mean at least Jamie Shock left the fed all together. He didn't jump ship and run off to another stable. You are gonna regret this son, and that you can be sure of. * Ryder starts to smirk * Ryder: I mean you didn't really hurt me any, because let's face it you weren't pulling your weight so all you did was make things alot better for me in the long run. You better get used to seein this face around alot though boy, because i'm gonna be your worst fuckin nightmare. Just ask Mike Montgomery and the Player's Club. We hate each other with a passion, but they sure as hell know that Ruff Ryder will not stay away. I keep coming and coming until I get what I want, and you just did them a favor because now i'm focusing all my energies on some good ol payback. That's righ bitch, i'm comin for you full speed and you better believe that once I get ahold of you there's a fuckin hospital bed with your name on it. Get ready asshole cause paybacks comin, courtesy of the Cherokee Thrilla...RUFF RYDER! ( Ryder gives the camera a half smile, half glare as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
