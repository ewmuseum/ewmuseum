Descend to Death (Part1)

17th June (year unrecorded)

The Real Deal — Back Alley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Speed enduced are the legs of one who are running as fast as they can from something that swoops through the darkness of the land at night. The cities of the world are more crowded in the depths of the night than in the shining wizardry of dawn when the light comes up. Paranoia grows during this darkness as the mind does not know what is going on and cannot use the eyes of the human to look around. Other senses grow more indepth and receptive, the most prolifant being hearing. At night the ears become much more fine tuned as the eyes don't have much work to do. Whispers can come from all angles yet it is unknown where they come from and who they come from. 'Stop right there...' were the words spoken that stopped The Real Deal in his treks walking through the back streets of Orlando Florida on his way back to his holiday home. All he could see around him were the shadows of the trees and each end of the street where the light came at him like the light from the end of a tunnel. A chill shot up Deal's spine as he heard footsteps moving in him from the side. He decided not to move at all, hoping that whoever it was that had spoken to him would totally miss him in the darkness. 'This are dangerous parts young sir...' rang out from from what seemed infront of him. This made him wonder if there was more than one person and else they were moving around him. Deal decided that he would reply as he really didn't have any patience for being held up when he was heading home. He was used to it but he had never really been stopped at three a.m before in a pitch black alley. 'Who's there?' Deal whispered as he swiftly darted his head left and right. The goosebumps began to appear all over his arms and back as a strong wind blew down the entry nearly knocking him off balance. A figure moves out infront of the light so Deal can finally see the shape of the unknown person. 'Come closer and you will see.' The voice was from the lips of a female, from what Deal could see it looked like she worked out quite a bit as her figure was slender and toned. He took a number of steps forward and her face finally came into view. Deal gave a sly grin as their eyes met. 'What exactly are you you stopping me here for at this time of night?' he asked curiously. The woman moved her right hand towards him and grabbed his arm. Her voice was so smooth it calmed Deal down dramatically, 'I want you to touch me...' From a state of complete relax Deal's eyes shot open and he was completely shocked. 'What the fuck?' burst out of him as he couldn't really hold it back. The woman pull him in closer so that her forehead now locked against his. 'I said I wan't you to touch me!' Deal stood back forced the woman to release the grip on his arm. He was totally confused at this point, his mind was at the point of feeling that this was some slut who just wanted to use Deal for a cheap thrill. 'You really don't have a chance...' he said with a cocky look on his face as he started to walk off. 'Oh but I do...' was the reply from the woman who had added a complete sting to the tone of her voice. Deal found himself dragged back by both arms without warning just as she said this and was slammed against the wall. After he observed the area around he noticed that it was two large guys who were holding him up. The footsteps of the woman slid towards him like a cat sneaks through the night. Her hand came up and grips him by the chin then with that she pushed the back of his head against the wall. She slapped him for good measured then turned her back. 'You will regret this...you will definately fucking regret this!' The two men she was with tossed Deal onto the ground and followed her to the end of the alley. She turned around the look at him and their eyes locked on one around. As her flowing hair swung back around Deal lay down flat on his back and looked up to the stars and thought to himself... -'What the hell was that? I mean I'm walkin' home so I can get some sleep and this happens...I wonder what I'll regret, maybe I'll regret not touching her heh...I really doubt it. I'll see what happens. She'll regret doing this to me more than I'll regret not touching her'- Deal stood up at that point and started off home again, his thoughts totally on the events of what just happened. He didn't know what to do at that point so he didn't let it bother him. Fear is an emotion people feel when they anticipate that they are going to get hurt. When darkness falls at night the feeling of fear is heightened because more pain can be unleashed and it can be more unexpected making it worse overall. To be fearless you do not care what stands before you but it is hard to become fearless when you do not know what is going on around you. There can be a mix of the two though. Even those who believe that cannot fear anything will be afraid once in their lives and for those who are afraid of alot of things they will find that they will be able to be brave against all odds in the face of adversity. The Real Deal is a special case though for believes something that not many others can... Fear doesn't effect that which creates it...
