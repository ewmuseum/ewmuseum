UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #927

Precision Title Defense

4 Feb 2005

Kenny Mercury — Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Last Sunday, the UWF witnessed a new chapter begin in the wrestling industry. Owner Richard Hayes turned the page, and reinvented the Intercontinental Championship, turning it into the Insert-Name-Here Championship. Now, in a wrestling company like the UWF, this could potentially be a very dangerous move, knowing all the mentally unstable idiots that tend to flock around the UWF locker room… but rest assured, as long as Kenny Mercury holds that title, which he has dubbed the Precision Championship, that the title will be a symbol of excellence in an otherwise not-so-excellent federation.] [After one week of resting a top the “Precision Division” of the UWF, Kenny Mercury defends his championship against Joshua Justice. Oh goody! We find Kenny in his Chicago apartment, alone, as per usual… I mean, who the hell could stand spending time with someone like him? That’s right… no one! Kenny is lounging around his home, and sits outstretched on his couch, watching Television. For some reason… he’s wearing his wrestling gear, tights and all. Sitting on the couch next to him, on either side, is the Precision championship belt, and the UWF World Tag Team championship.] [Kenny points to the television with the remote control.] Kenny: “Look at that!” [Kenny nudges the Precision title belt with his elbow.] Kenny: “Joshua Justice is full of fecal matter! Wouldn’t you say?” Precision Title: “…” Kenny: “Damn straight! Whatta ya say?” [Kenny turns to the Tag Team title belt.] Tag Title Belt: “…” Kenny: “True dat… true dat… This guy is nutso… COMPLETE NUTSO! Nutso if he thinks he can beat me at my own game, nutso if he thinks that he can win you, Precision Title, and complete and total nutso if he thinks that he can seemingly just blow me off. He seems to be taking this match way too lightly. What did he say? That he doesn’t even care to beat me? That *MAYBE* if he beats me he might win some respect? That there are bigger fish to fry? BAHAHAHAHA! Getta load of this guy, huh? HUH?!” [Kenny holds his stomach and laughs uncontrollably. He nudges the title belts with his elbow.] Kenny: “Oh man, is he gunna get it… oh yeah, HE’S GUNNA GET IT! Look here, Mr. Guy. I don’t know who the hell you think you are, but if you’re going to go into this match with that kind of mindset… you are in serious danger of friggin’ dying. Not that that wouldn’t happen anyways, but seriously, keep your head up your ass and its going to be even worse.” “You say you don’t care about this match? You claim to not have any ‘beef’ with me? Well, look here, you got ‘beef’, you yeah, I’ve got ‘beef.’ You are challenging for my personalized championship title, OH NO YOU DIDN’T! WE GOTS SOME BEEF NOW, MUG~ ! YOU FINNA GO TO WAR, FOLKS?! STEP UP! STEP UP BITCH! I’M KENNY MERCURY, BITCH! HOLD ME BACK! HOLD ME BACK!” Precision title belt: “…” Tag Tag title belt: “…” Kenny: “YEAH, WE GOT SOME BEEF, YOU STEPPIN’?! HOODY HOO! IF YOU GOT BEEF WIF KENNY MER-CUR-Y, HOLLA AT ME, THE D.O.P.~!” Precision title belt: “…” Kenny: “That’s right… D.O.P… THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION, FOLKS~! And you think that if you win you might win some respect around the locker room? DAMN STRAIGHT YOU WOULD! You’d be the biggest thing since sliced bread, you’d be going straight to the top of the federation, you’d be a wrestling GOD… but too bad… too bad that is NEVER going to happen. Because the Precision Championship is only won by submission, and that’s my domain. If you think you can come into my world, push me around, steal my gold, slay my dragon, impregnate my daughter, take a dump in my daffodils, skin my cat, and finagle my Hamburglar, well, DEAD FRIGGIN’ WRONG! For Sunday is the Hamburglar’s birthday, motherfucker, and I’ve just got one thing to say to you…” “Rabble rabble, rabble rabble rabble, rabble rabble… rabble.” “And furthermore… RABBLE~!” “Look, you’re not prepared for this match, and that is your mistake. You are running around in La-La Land, frolicking around in a wet suit. I don’t give a shit if you are Mr. Star Spangled Danny Tanner or whatever the hell, or if you were the Rod damn Super Mario Frog suit, you are NOT going to be beating the shooting star, the technical wizard, the ring doctor, the submission exhibition, the VERY DEFINITION OF PRECISION in this match… This is my world, my realm, my oyster… don’t touch my oyster, ho.” “Now, enough talk on this U.S. douche bag… That’s Uterine Suckah! You focus about 2 seconds on this match, so, I won’t give you the benefit of the doubt and make you the sole subject of this promo, oh no, for believe it or not, little man, there are bigger fish to fry in the federation than you and The Communist.” “First and foremost. Lew Spectre. He’s back, the time of reckoning is NIGH~! This federation will soon crumble under the pressure, under the excellence that wrestling LEGENDS like Spectre, myself, and Jamie Shock. Say goodbye to the UWF as you know it, this is a new era.” “And speaking of Jamie Shock, my wonderful life partner. I mean, well, its not like we’re ever going to be beat for the UWF Tag team titles… EVER… so I guess that means we’re ‘life partners.’ About him, he faces Tsunami Oni in a non-title match. Oni, consider this a preview of The Clash. Shock is only going to give you a taste of the destruction that Lew Spectre is going to spoon feed you at The Clash. Kiss the Heavyweight championship of the World sayonara.” “And I’m not done yet, there’s one more thing I have to cover before I send you pukes on your way. That is the return of this Misfit person. If you think you can just waltz back into the UWF and have your way with me or anyone else, you are sadly mistaken. You should have stayed dead, Misfit. You should have just withered, dried up, died, and blow away. You’re not welcome here, you’re not wanted. I will give you one chance, and one chance only to quit the federation and cut your losses. It’s your choice. Leave UWF forever, or deal with the consequences. And you damn right that those consequences look like crap. I’m going to rip your head off and take a dump down your throat. You bettah recognize. Isn’t that right?” Precision title: “…” Tag Team title: “…” Kenny: “You heard it here first… make your decision… skank.” [End Transmission.]
