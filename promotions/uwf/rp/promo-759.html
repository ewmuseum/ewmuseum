UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #759

Doubts and Truths

10-32-04 (year unrecorded)

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — Chicago, on the Pier

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Like a clear sky after the onset of a devastating tropical storm, the waters are quiet within the hallowed halls of the Universal Wrestling Federation. For one glorious night, we got Live and Loud… and the very foundation of the business was torn asunder from its cushy perch. The Deville has arrived in the UWF. “The Renegade” Rich Rollins, aided by the surprise appearance of Angelo Deville, has won the chance to cement his place at the top of the company by facing the UWF North American Champion. And that North American Champion just happens to be Seymour Almasy. The ominous icy grip of inevitability has hung over Rollins’ head since Live and Loud 4, and there is only one way to remedy it. Kill the un-killable demon. Defeat the undefeatable legend. And claim the UWF Tag-Team Championship in the process. ~<>~<>~<>~ [Sunset.] [Amidst the serene landscape of the waterfront, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins watches the last glimmer of sunlight fade over the tops of the skyscrapers of downtown Chicago. People come and go around him; fitness buffs taking their evening run along the Pier, couples strolling hand-in-hand, lonely old women feeding pigeons like they were lost children… all the little details of metropolitan life, ignorantly parading in front of a man who doesn’t see them. Who doesn’t care to see them.] [All Rich Rollins sees is the rooftops, basking in an eerie orange/gray glow as the sun finally slides behind their towers.] Renegade: Seymour Almasy… [Addressed to no one in particular, the name escapes from Rollins’ lungs with a musing hesitation, like the meaning itself was being turned over in his mind. Continuing to focus on the darkening shadows of the skyline, The Renegade continues.] Renegade: I’ve been askin’ myself a lot of questions in these past few days. A lot of questions. But the most important one, the one with the most obvious answer, was simple: “What bar to I have to break to make it in this business?” Who’s set the standard, and how do I go about raising it? [He chuckles, a dry sound.] Renegade: All roads lead to Seymour Almasy, it seems. Longest-reigning Television Champion. Longest-reigning World Heavyweight Champion. UWF Grand Slam Champion and Hall of Famer, and two-time PTC Global Champion. He’s a wrestling legend with more accolades than virtually anyone… but then again, I’m not just “anyone.” [The Renegade slowly turns his head, leveling his eyes on the camera with a stern confidence that radiates with a brooding anger. An anger for what exactly, is not so obvious. With the calm waters of Lake Michigan at his back, Rollins continues.] Renegade: Obviously, it seems that I’m the man capable of destroying the UWF. I’m the man who’s put a healthy hesitation in Seymour Almasy’s once-infallible step, and all because of my association with one man… Angelo Deville. [Rollins eyes furrow deeply, his expression sinking into a dangerous scowl as a gust of wind whips his shoulder-length brown hair in spirals over his shoulders.] Renegade: So now you’ll pay attention. Now I’m a force to be reckoned with. And why is that, exactly? You think that just because a man like Deville walks in at my side, I’m all of a sudden this deadly pariah hell-bent on destroying this company? Let me break the news to you, Almasy… I’ve always been dangerous, and I’ve always been a force to be reckoned with. Deville is just the icing on the cake… and a rather large cake, at that. If you think you have reason to worry now… [The Renegade merely shakes his head sadly.] Renegade: … you best think again. Is Clyde going to save your sorry ass when I’m taking your North American title? Are you going to be able to conjure another miraculous victory all on your own? Somehow I doubt it. And come Sunday, those doubts will become truths. [Rollins bows his head every so slightly, allowing a few wayward strands of unruly hair to fall over his face like a shield against the unwavering eye of the camera.] [In the end, he whispers.] Renegade: I promise you that. [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
