UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #399

The Game Has Just Begun

10 May 2004

Bret Phillips — Backstage UWF WNU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cameras flick on and show Bret Phillips sitting in a chair sipping a bottle of water. He looks at the camera with a mean stare and throws the bottle away somewhere in the dark secluded room. Phillips swallows the water in his mouth and takes a deep meaningful breath. The only light in the room is focused on Phillips face and its deadly silent. “So here we are. Here I am. Here you are. Here they are. I’ve made my first indentation in the world of wrestling. I’ve made my first move in this game and I rolled a double six. Finally people are sitting up to take note of me as a wrestler. Finally everyone has stop talking about my physical asserts and seen what I can do in a ring. True Kyle Cross was not much of an opponent but you can only beat what’s put in front of you. Kyle Cross is a talented man who had a bright future. I simply made it look easy because for me it WAS easy. I made him look foolish. I didn’t taunt before the pin for fun or to please anyone out there. I taunted to show just how in control I was. I began to lose the punch battle so I changed the game and hit the Implant D.D.T and that was the beginning of the end for Kyle Cross. I decided that I wanted to change the shape of the match. I decided that this style needed to be change and it gave me an edge. An edge I have always had because I lead: I do not follow. I took the game and changed the rules to give me all the aces and that’s what I’ve always done. Do play the game you can’t win: CHANGE THE DAMN RULES. Cross may very well never wrestler again. I may have humiliated him just enough to make him quit. People accuse me of being too confident. Some people even call me cocky: but I know that I can back it up. When you can back it up as emphatically as me then you can afford to do what ever the hell you like.” Phillips pauses for a second to catch his breath and strokes his stubble. “That was my last move and my first move. Now we move onto my second and next move. I’ve been given a huge opportunity to grab my first piece of gold in my second match ever: not just in UWF but also in WRESTLING. I’ve been given another ace and now I get a chance to play it. I’ve been given this ace but I also have a joker in the pack. I’ve been dealt the joker in the form of Bondage Sex Fiend. A guy who doesn’t take his wrestling name seriously. A guy who doesn’t take his impression on others in this business seriously. A guy who doesn’t take his matches seriously. A guy who doesn’t take WRESTLING SERIOUSLY. Make no mistake though; this guy has talent in that ring. He is no Dink The Clown. He is no Repo Man. This guy can get it done in the ring. He wouldn’t be Television Champion otherwise. Despite his skill I KNOW I can defeat him before I even step into that ring with him. I’ve study him since I found out about my shot at the Television Belt. I knew he would retain it: it was obvious to anybody with an instinct such as myself. I know everything about this guy from his past matches. Lets take a look at his stats shall we? The guys six feet and 1 inch. He is undersized in that ring against me and this gives me leverage over him. He is extremely overweight and carries far to much body fat at two hundred and fifty six pounds. This will make him strong but not as strong as me for I may weight less by a fraction but I am pure muscle. He has excess body fat and I do not. I have the power and leverage advantage over him and add to this my superior fitness level and cardio vascular levels I can’t see any other winner in this match. Do you seriously see anybodies hand but Bret Phillips rose at the end? Finally we look at the most important aspect of Bondage and that’s his approach to his wrestling life. He seems to think nothing much of his career here. He expects simply being a fat bastard enough and true over most people his fat would destroy them. However against me he cannot use this strength advantage, as it does not exists. He will be too slow and too out of breath. If that wasn’t enough proof for you though then consider this: I’ve already beaten him before I even lay a finger on him. Just like Cross-knew I’d already won so must Bondage. Bondage must have seen me and knew he had lost already. He may not admit it to himself but sub consciously he knows he cannot beat me. This is what makes me so damn good. Bondage I can assure you will be telling people that he’ll kick my ass and that he guarantees a victory. However he won’t believe this in the bottom of his gut. The bottom of his gut is where all the doubt and fear will win me the match. In contrast I am not here to guarantee you a victory because I don’t give a damn if you believe me or not. I have already guaranteed myself a victory in my gut. My gut holds no doubt or fear. My gut holds confidence and excitement. I have guaranteed myself victory and that’s why I know I cannot lose this coming Wednesday. Phillips stops to take another breath and looks down meaningfully before lifting his head back up and starting again. “What about my future moves here in the game of UWF. After I take that Television Title what’s next for Bret Phillips? Well I do not decide who I face and therefore do not decide my future. I decide that my future will be a success by consistent victories over everybody I face. I think the guys around here have had it easy for too long. I think the big guys around here have had it too easy for too long. I think everybody has had it easy for too long here. Everybody has gone stale and that’s what lack of competition does to you. That’s what taking it easy does to you. I can guarantee Mike Montgomery out Worlds Champion is not half the guy he used to be. Everybody here has taken their foot off the gas because they could afford too. Well guess what guys? I’m here to push you to the limit. You can no longer be in cruise control now you have to push all the way until I break you. There’s nothing like competition to make you raise your game and I’ll raise you all that little beat further: right before I BREAK YOU. When I look into my opponent’s eyes I do not see fear. These guys know I’m not going to give them black eyes or break their bones. However what I do see is doubt because they know I have mental and physical advantages over every single one of them. I am not here to be a boxer and break peoples jaws. I’m here to be a wrestler and win titles. Beware UWF Bret Phillips is the new champion of the game.” The Camera flicks off.
