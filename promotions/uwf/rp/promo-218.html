UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #218

Bozo is a homo!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins outside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. As the scene begins Ryder is seen sitting on top of his red and black Chevy Blazer staring straight forward with a blank stare. The camera zooms in to get the shot of his face which is now dripping drool. The cameraman slowly approaches Ryder, trying not to spook him as the last cameraman apparently did. ) Cameraman: Mr. Ryder? * Ryder starts to giggle * Cameraman: Mr. Ryder...are you ok sir? * Ryder looks straight up into the sky * Ryder: Jamie? Is that you? Cameraman: No sir i'm Tom, the UWF sent me to shoot your promo for your Championship match against Beezlebozo at The Clash. Ryder: Jamie, it's so good to see you. Why did you leave me? What did I do to deserve that? Cameraman: No sir, i'm Tom. Ryder: Jamie Shock does not answer to Tom! Cameraman: Shock is gone sir. * Ryder slowly turns his head toward the cameraman with an angry glare * Ryder: How dare you say that to me? Cameraman: ummm what? Ryder: Don't you ever say that to me again! Jamie Shock is not gone! He's always with me, he said so. He said that the Prime Alliance would never die! Cameraman: I'm sorry. Ryder: That lyin son of a bitch! * Ryder lunges forward off the vehicle landing face first in the dirt. He lays motionless for a few seconds before twitching and flipping around like a fish out of the water until he springs to his feet with blood oozing from his bottom lip * Ryder: Who the fuck are you? Cameraman: What? Ryder: You heard me boy, I said who the hell are you and what are you doin here? Cameraman: I told you earlier sir, my name is Tom and i'm here to shoot your promo for The Clash. Ryder: Well what are you waiting for? Turn your little camera on and let's get this thing started. Cameraman: Yes sir, i'm recording right now. Ryder: Alright then. I've heard the little bullshit statements that Bozo the clown has been making and it makes me laugh hard, because this punk ass bastard actually believes that he didn't show up when I beat his ass in the tournament. Hell that was my easiest victory since joining this piece of shit fed. I whipped his ass in no time flat and he's too embarassed to take it, so he invented this lame story that he sent a mime to take his place. We got it on video Bozo, so you run your little mime story all you want, but it goes in the record books that I beat you in the middle of the ring one...two...three. I'll slap you, your mime, and your bitch all at the same time. You all three want a piece of the Cherokee Thrilla then you bring em with you to the ring and i'll fuck you all three up and walk out of The Clash as the two time UWF World Champion. I'm the best there is and it's about time you found that out. Nobody can beat me when i'm on my game and that includes you Joshua Dunn. As soon as i'm through whoopin Bozo's ass I want you in the ring. I'm tired of you thinkin you are better than me. You're a hell of a wrestler, that i'll give you, but there is no way that you are better than me. I'm the franchise here, and until I have that win over you my time here is not worth it. I want a victory over you JD and come hell or high water I will get it. The next time you step through the ropes to face me, you won't be facin regular ol' Ruff Ryder. You'll be facing the UWF World Champion Ruff Ryder. When it's all on the line I don't think you have what it takes to beat me. * Ryder starts to twitch and tremble * Ryder: Donuts? Camerman: What the hell? Ryder: Jamie Shock loves donuts! I better have some here when he gets here. Cameraman: Are out of your fuckin mind? Ryder: Never insult Jamie Shock's friend like that! * Ryder lunges at the cameraman knocking him to the ground. The camera catches Ryder's latest beating of a cameraman before Ryder picks the camera up and once again places it on his vehicle * Ryder: Sorry Jamie, but that man was just plain rude, and I know you won't stand for a rude man like that. * Ryder starts to spaz out again before composing himself. As he comes to he notices the bloody and unconcious cameraman at his feet * Ryder: What the hell happened to him? * He shrugs as if to say oh well * Ryder: Anyways, back to what I was sayin. Bozo, your time as champ is coming to and end, and i'm gonna love stomping the life out of you. I fuckin hate clowns, and ridding the world of one more clown is something i'm gonna get a thrill out of. You better pray to whoever it is you pray to that i'm in a good mood, because if i'm pissed...it's gonna be a long day for you son. Hope the hospital has cable where you're gonna be stayin. You know something, on second thought i'll just put your ass in the morgue, it'll save me the hassle of hearing your lame ass excuse for losing the World Title to the Cherokee Thrilla. See you son bitch. ( Ryder begins to go into a seizure again before flipping his eyelids back and falling straight backwards into the dirt as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
