UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #511

A history lesson

6 Jul 2004

Mr. Meaner — A rock quarry

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens outside in a pile of rocks. There are rocks everywhere you look. Hammers a skewn about around the rock pit. There are some shackles lying around. Other than that though, the rock pit is empty as empty can be. Nothing more is around, with the exception of one thing. A man, dressed all in black. The man, of course known as Mr. Meaner. He has a Iron pipe in his hand which he is hitting on his other hand. He walks through the pit toward the camera, looking straight for it. He stops in front of it and begins to speak in a low quiet voice.) Meaner: Well, both of my opponents have spoken now. Both of them have said little or nothing about me. Both of them seems to be quite confused as to who I am. Both of them are more concerned it would seem about each other and each others factions……….. That’s fine with me. That’s their choice. You see, there is something nice about being able to fly under the radar so to speak. There is something nice about people not talking about you, not thinking about you, concentrating on someone else. It give you the greatest opportunity to attack them when they aren’t expecting it. It give you the greatest opportunity to make them suffer in a way that they have never felt before….. Both of my opponents, Kevin Starr and Malcom Kennedy both think that I am just some random person thrown into this entire shin ding so to speak. Well, other than the fact that neither of them can wrestle their way out of a paper bag or for that matter survive the cruelty of living behind bars, neither of them are able to do homework, which believe it or not is an important part of this game……. So let me remind you bitches of something. Go back through some of the archives. It’s something y’all should really think about doing once in a while. My name, Mr. Meaner will show up all over the GIWF archives. Mr. Meaner, the man who held the GIWF TV Title 2. Mr. Meaner, the man who took UWF/GIWF hall of famer Lyle “In Style” Wile to the limits. Mr. Meaner, the man who orchestrated the greatest stable of all time. The man who orchestrated a stable that at one time held every single belt that was possible to hold in the GIWF…… Now, Malcolm Kennedy, I’m sure you don’t remember any of this. I mean, you were still wet behind the ears. You were still a snot nosed brat. Kevin Starr, you should at least have heard of it. Granted, you are a geriatric piss bomb. But I know that not even you are senile enough to not remember me and know who I am…….. Of course, when you look at the morons that Starr hangs around with, it’s not hard to see why his intellect has gone down hill. Your idiot Mike there seems to think he is superman. I look like Ezra Kazee? How in the hell could he tell that. I know that he ain’t freaking psychic. And he ain’t superman, so there’s no way he has X-Ray vision to see through my ski mask. I can assure you that no matter what you want o say, I am not anywhere near or like that pansy Ezra Kazee. But you keep going and telling Starr that. He needs to think something……. Now Starr, honestly, I’m hurt. I can’t believe you forgot about me. I can’t believe that you forgot about one of the most ruthless and meanest wrestlers to ever grace the GIWF. Yeah, you go ahead and insult your predecessor. It just shows one thing. You have no respect for the past. Well, you know, for one thing, you wouldn’t’ be here if it weren’t for me. If it weren’t’ for the success that I had, the success of the GIWF, the UWF wouldn’t even be anywhere that it is today….. The Gay Idiots Wrestling Federation? That’s a good one, NOT!!!! Besides for the fact that I’ve had more championship then you’ve had women, you and Joshua Dunn have been playing Butt Darts for years now and neither of you can settle who’s the pitcher and who’s the catcher. You and all your friends in the Player’s Club, you all go on back to your lush hotel and figure out which is which. Maybe someday you’ll be able to hold onto titles and maybe someday your stable will become one of the greatest and hold all the titles like my gathered stable once did…….. Of course, no one could be as stupid and as idiotic as that other son of a motherless goat that is known as Malcolm Kennedy. So you think that I’m a nobody. You think that I’m just some wild card no namer who was thrown into the mix. Well, Mr. Kennedy, I’ve already spelled it out to you punk and I ain’t going to do it again. You see that little remote you have in your fat little hand. Take a good look at it. There is an arrow that points backwards. That is called the rewind button. Now say it with me. Re-Wind. I know I’m talking to fast for you to understand, but feel free to push that button at any time and go back and listen to what I am saying again, maybe you’ll be able to figure it out…… So you’re the hunter. You’re the One Man Army. You say you’ve beaten tougher guys like Kevin Starr. You say that I’m nothing. You are not that tough, and you most definitely are not a One Man Army. Look at you, you’re a little bitch. You’ve lost to Ezra Kazee on three different occasions and everyone knows he is the biggest pansy ass around here, so what does that make you? Well, I’d say that makes you pond scum. But Kennedy, you just keep telling yourself that you are that great. Maybe you’ll believe it some day……. Oh, and Kennedy, my tough guy act, it’s not an act. I ain’t some random tough guy who walked in off the street. I’m an accomplished jack ass. You say that I’m a dime a dozen. I’m not a dime a dozen. I’m a unique bastard that you’ve never fought before, and you’ll never want to fight again. You tell me to check my attitude at the door, well, you know what, Kennedy, you can check this at the door. (Mr. Meaner steps up to the camera, takes the lead pipe, and jams it end wise right through the lens and the camera. The camera goes black instantly.)
