UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #782

Back and ready for action

24 Nov 2004

Craig Wright — Home close to WNU arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introducing 'Highlight of the night' Craig Wright Well well well, The Hot-Shot is back, After a long time out of the wrestling business the Hightlight of the night is back and is ready for action. Craig Wright is here in the UWF and his first match is this Wednesday. Wednesday will be a day of change hear in the UWF. The Hot-Shot has took some time of playing minor roles in films and TV series in the UK. In many TV interviews the Hot-Shot Craig Wright has denied rumours that he would ever step into the squared circle. Why is Craig Wright back after denying rumours? Is the Highlight of the Night Craig Wright back for good? This Wednesday will step in the UWF Arena not in his casual clothing as a spectator and also to promote himself, He will step into the ring with his Casual Clothing not as a spectator but as a UWF superstar and also to promote himself and his auto-biography ** 'The Wright Champion' December 1st. £ 9.99. Get the Regal Rumble 2001 DVD and get the book Half Price. Offer On till 20 December grab your copy now in all book stores. ** Craig Wright will step into the ring with Brazillian star El Baiano a fighter brought to the UWF by Hall Of Famer Lyle Wile. Both men have never met each other in the ring. Who is the odds on favourite. We have all seen both men in action but we havent seen the 'Highlight Of The Night' in action in a long time. Will Hot-Shot be up to scratch? Will he be able to compete with the UWF superstars now. Craig Wright has achieved many things in the pass in his career but he has never indeed won a title. Craig Wright was the Regal Rumble 2001 runner-up. He has beat PWW superstars such as HardKore. Craig Wright has even eliminated legendary wrestlers such as the Cpl Punishment. That was all in the past the question now is can Craig Wright do this again and actually win a title in his career. It all starts for Craig Wright at the bottom again. There will be many expectations made in his match with El Baiano, This will be no easy match for The Badass, The One, The Hot-Shot, The Forever changing his name' The Highlight of The Night Craig Wright. [The scene opens in what looks like Craig Wrights home somewhere close to the arena where WNU will take place. Craig Wright sits on a wooden chair staring at the camera. Since Craig Wright's departure his appearence has changed dramatically. He is no longer the blond bombshell that once pranced around the ring. The Highlight of the Night is a lot more bulkier and well built. His dyed Dark hair covers one of his eyes and his goatee hides the scar once seen under Wright's nose. He wears his usual attire. Craig Wright has a smile on his face. Remembering the past. A time where Craig Wright was laughed at and never really took seriously.The smile on his face reveals times have changed.] Hot-Shot: I can't believe it, I'm back. Im ready. I'm 110 % ready [He looks to his left, smiles and looks back at the camera, The camera looks Left and sees a life size doll of Craig Wright in 2001.] Hot-Shot: Why did I leave this place? How could I let this go? I had fans. Oh I remember now. I was a jobber. I was the Hot-Shot. The kid who was just too egotistical to realise that he was a really poor wrestler. Wanting the money and the fame but not caring about the consequences. I lost a hell of a lot of matches. Craig Wright has put that all in the past now. Sure I like to still make a buck or two now and then like in this book. [Craig Wright picks up a his auto-biography and flashes it infront of the camera.] Hot-Shot: I had to leave. I had to think where my career was going. It was going nowhere. My wrestling standards were too low. I wasnt happy with the money and the fame of being a jobber. I wanted to be known as someone who was able to win championships. I wanted to be the Highlight Of the Night. After months and months of intense training Craig Wright finally is the Highlight Of the Night. By now many people will be laughing. We'll see who will be laughing this time next month. I'm Back and I mean business. I will thrive on every victory I will become stronger. I will find myself becoming a super power. By this time all of you wont know what has hit you. But it all starts at the bottom of the ladder for now. Like a Baby taking his first steps. First he has to stand. Just like I. I will have to get on my feet by defeating the Brazillian El Baiano. [Craig Wright Stands up and begins to walk along a corridor. The camera follows.] Hot-Shot: El Baiano, defeating u will make me happy. It will prove to me that all this intense training has paid out and I am ready to swim with the sharks. See, you got lucky, you were brought into the federation by a superstar. I never. I had to fight my way through constant hell. I started off by having to throw binge-drinkers out clubs then fight in unofficial fight clubs in enclosed rings. I was lucky to have came out alive. Then I was made a Scottish Wrestler in minor federations. I'm here in the UWF now to prove that I am the Highlight of The Night. I will prove to you all I am. El Baiano you will the first person who is capable to warn the other wrestlers backstage that I have the ability to come out of the ring a winner rather than a looser. Wednesday I will step into the ring and fight you one-on-one. We will fight I can't promise the fans it will be worth the money but I can say David Bowie will hit the speakers twice Wednesday night. [Hot-Shot grabs a bottle from the counter and takes a drink] Hot-Shot: I will defeat El Baiano. At the end of the night you will wish that you stuck to the sport your country is actually good at, football. Off the record Scotland will become an elite in Europe. Bring all you have El Baiano I like a good challenge. After I defeat you I will be ready for my next step taking that first little step and this baby doesnt fall. Soon I will be at the top of UWF I will actually be a title holder. None of you will realise what has hit you. [Hot-Shot stops next to the life size doll.] Hot-Shot: Look at this weed in front of me. I can't believe I took myself seriously. I take it the girls weren't really interested in Hot-Shot's appearance. Fortunetely the girls think otherwise now. I apoligise to all the fans that followed me and were left disappointed before. I ask for your forgiveness and promise you that you will be in the ride for your life if you follow the Highlight Of the Night Craig Wright again. You will see unexpected headlines for PPVs such as Craig Wright Vs Tsunami Oni, Digital Mortality Vs Craig Wright World Championship match. What do you think? First things first it will be El Baiano. Good luck my samba friend cause you will need it. [Craig Wright sits back down] Hot-Shot: Be prepared to stumble and crash to the matrass El Baiano and that goes to all of the UWF superstars. The 'Highlight Of the Night' Craig Wright is here and ready to conquer. [Scene Fades]
