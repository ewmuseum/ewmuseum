UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #358

Burned and Bitching

23 Apr 2004

Anarchy — Backstage at Supremacy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to the back stage area from the last Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. A group of people are surrounding something with a lot of yelling and swearing. Raid, Dai, and Joanna can be seen in the group along with a trainer and a doctor. As the camera makes its way through you can see that the doctor is working on the burnt back of Pryde.] Pryde : I don’t care if it is first, second, of third degree burns, I am going to get back at Persia. Bandage me up and I will be on my way. I need to get my hands on the Dutch bastard. I want him next Sunday, and I want that match signed now. Joanna : But Adien, they already have you three signed in a match next week. A 15 person battle royal, its an Ultra Special Battle Royal. Pryde : What, that is Bull Shit. I want Persia in the ring right away. Is he at least in the match so I can get my hands on him? Joanna : Um, no, He has the afternoon off. [Joanna, Dai, and Raid all look at each other. Pryde roles over and sits up with such a flash that he knocks the doctor over. He has a grimace of pain on his face but doesn’t let it bother his outrage] Pryde : He puts me through a burning table, fries my back and he is given the day off. What the Fuck! Montgomery either gave him it off as because he helped The Players Club or because he is scared of what I will do to him when I get my hands on him. Trainer : Um, Pryde, we need to have you lay back down so we can properly bandage you up for the trip to the hospital. [Dai and Joanna back up realizing what could happen. Raid and the crew look confused at them.] Pryde : Hospital!? I said get me ready to be on my way. Screw the hospital, I am going after Persia. I will do whatever it takes to get my hands on him. I will throw all 14 others out to do it to. Let me see the list, I want to see who the other 12 losers are. [Joanna steps forward and hands a piece of paper to him.] Pryde : Hmm, lets see… Dante, Hasn’t won a match yet PhD, Submissions don’t win battle royals, Gregory, you have to be kidding me, Bruce Gordy, I thought he quit, he’ll be no problem, [Pryde looks up at Raid and Dai.] Pryde : Hey guys, I know you two want it too, but I am out for some revenge for these scars on my back and if that means throwing you two over so be it. Raid : Well if you really think you can throw me out go ahead and try, but don’t be pissed if I throw you out! Dai : Hey I understand dude, but I am not lying down for you. I would like some recognition too. Pryde : I wouldn’t want it any other way. Now who else is on this Ultra Special Battle Royal list… Clarence Clark, who the hell is this, I have never heard of this guy, El Chupacabra, whatever, speed bump, Trailer Park Luchador, Dai trashed this guy and he will probably do it again, Loverboy, a guy who is shooting to far up and that will drive him to the bottom, Alan Temple, where are they getting these guys? The Bone Crusher, as smart as a box of rocks and about as much skill to boot. Draco Paladine, name sounds familiar, anything on him Joanna? Joanna : Big man, hasn’t wrestled in awhile, I will get something more on him. Pryde : Thanks, now the last name on the list… El Turtlé, He better hide in his shell cause he isn’t doing anything else. I don’t see any problem other than you two and the unknown. Winning this match will prove I deserve another shot at Holland’s worst export, Nick Persia. Title shot or no, I will get my hands on him and send his ass back to Holland in a coffin. Doctor : Sir, we really need to get you wrapped up. [Pryde looks at the MD and decides to listen to what the man says and again lays on his stomach again] Pryde : Joanna, get me that information on Draco Paladine, I want it by the time I get home tonight! Joanna : Will do, anything else? Pryde : Yes, talk to Montgomery and get me a contact for a rematch with Persia. In or out of the ring I will get my hands on him, and I don’t want to go to prison, visiting Raid was as close as I want to get. [Dai and Joanna turn to leave while Raid waits] Pryde : Raid, get you hands on Kazee’s neck and don’t let go, What have you….. Raid : Don’t worry about Kazee he’ll be taken care of in due time. MUWHAHAHA!!! [Scene fades to black]
