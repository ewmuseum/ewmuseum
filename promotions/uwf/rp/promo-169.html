UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #169

The Final Nightmare of Seymour Almasy

9 Jan 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open upon the three-ring room of Hell’s Carnival. A single spotlight shines on the center ring. A figure slowly comes into the light, his head down and hidden by his fairly long, brown hair. He’s wearing a leather coat with a fur collar, a white T-shirt black pants with a big belt, and black boots and gloves. In his hand he carries an odd-looking combination of a sword and a gun. He pushes his lanky brown hair out of his face and looks into the camera, revealing a face painted with red and black. Beelzebozo: “Seymour…Seymour Almasy…I have travelled far to find you. And at last, the vengeance you so richly deserve shall visit itself upon you, at the tip of my gunblade. In other words, ALL YOUR BASE ARE BELONG TO US. “What’s that? You don’t remember me? You wound me, sir. For you must remember…” He brandishes his weapon and snarls. “GALE LIONHEART.” He stalks closer, his “gunblade” apparently a Super Soaker ™ with a wakazashi attached under the barrel with duct tape. He waves it at the camera. “I know we’ve had our ups and downs, but last we spoke, it was during a major down. I don’t know what foul spell you cast to steal Aerith’s heart, but I shall never forgive you.” Pyretta’s voice: “But what of me, my love?” Pyretta Blaze appears, wearing an odd blue corduory robe or dress over black shorts and tank top. Her forearms are encased with corduory bracers. Her black boots click on the floor as she tosses her long red hair and leans against Beel—er…Gale’s shoulder. Beelzebozo: “I have not forgotten you, Minerva Heartilly. I love you true. But I cannot forgive him for stealing her away, lo so many years ago. It was a blow to my honour, and I must avenge it.” Blaze: “I understand, my love. My boomerang shall aid you.” She pulls a wooden boomerang out of her belt and brandishes it. “And we shall have assistance. Come forth, my friends!” Three midgets dressed in dubious military gear with large helmets and large Super Soakers ™ march into the ring behind the two, who make way for the camera to see them. They stop, about face, and look directly into the camera. Midget one: “I’m Vicks.” Midget two: “I’m Wedge.” Midget three: “I’m Piette.” Just like that, they turn and march offscreen. Beelzebozo: “Powerful allies indeed. But that is not enough, I fear. Who else can help us?” Blaze (pointing offscreen): “Perhaps he can!” The Quiet One suddenly appears, leading another midget on an ostrich that has been somehow colored yellow. A large, ruffled headdress of yellow feathers has been stuck to the top of its head, and a large, parrot-like fake beak has been placed over its own, normal beak. The Quiet One is wearing a long brown coat with brown pants and a darker brown shirt and boots, with a large rifle slung over his back. The midget is dressed in black, with a high collar and studded, fingerless globes. His hair is bright yellow and spiked almost as crazily as Beelzebozo’s usually is, and an odd, lightning-like tattoo covers the left side of his face. Beelzebozo: “Why, it is Irwin Kinneas, as well as Mel Dincht on a faithful Chocobo! Surely their skill with a gun and martial arts prowess, along with their speedy steed, shall give us aid on our quest for victory! Even that smarmy Seymour shall have to fall before us!” The “chocobo” begins to buck and strain against the line “Irwin” is holding. He tries to restrain the creature, but it squawks and runs off, “Mel” holding on for dear life. The marksman takes off in pursuit as the other two watch on, bemused. Blaze: “I thought chocobos had a more distinctive call than that.” Beelzebozo: “Don’t let the noble creature escape!” He turns back to the screen as Blaze leaves. “And just to make sure we defeat you, Seymour…we have enlisted some very special help.” Blaze returns, holding a white teddy bear onto which wings, a large pink nose, and an odd little dealy-mabob on top of the head have been added. “That’s right! We tracked down a legendary MOOGLE to help us!” “Moogle” (sounding suspiciously like Pyretta): “Kupo! KUP-KUPO!!” Beelzebozo: “Oh yeah, you’re SOOOOOO going down now, Seymour!! My vengeance is as good as had, and your Television Title shall be MINE!!” He breaks into an evil laugh, and Blaze joins him. “Moogle”: “KUP-KUP-KUP-KUP-KUPOOOOOOO!!!” Beelzebozo: “See, even the Moogle knows you’re going to lose the match! And Moogles never lie and stuff!” Blaze sets the Moogle down. Blaze: “I thought leprechauns were the ones that never lied, if you held their legs or something.” Beelzebozo: “Isn’t that the Tooth Fairy?” Blaze: “Eh, maybe. Who knows?” She yawns. “Can we be done with this now? I’m bored, and my neck still hurts.” Beelzebozo: “Yeah sure.” He tosses the “gunblade” over his shoulder and into the darkness surrounding the ring. “I’m a little tired of this too. How does he keep this up week after week?” Blaze: “Beats me. I’d think it’d drive him mad.” Beelzebozo: “Yeah.” He stretches his arms over his head. “So, whaddya think of the wig? Like it?” Blaze: “Oh no, your normal hair’s much sexier.” Beelzebozo: “I thought so too. I’m glad you agree, I’d hate to keep wearing it day after day.” Blaze: “Aw…you’d do that for me?” Beelzebozo: “Of course.” Blaze: “You’re the grated cheese, baby.” She leans over and smooches his cheek. Beelzebozo: “Thanks.” He clears his throat. “Anyway, Seymour, the point here is that you have not got a prayer this weekend. I’m going to destroy you. Oh, I know, you’ve held this title for a while, and you must be pretty good to be World Champ. But you’ve never had to deal with someone like me. I’m a raging madman with an electric arsenal of moves, and I’m going to come at you like a Mimiced Knights of the Round. No amount of Materia, Limit Breaks, fighting styles, or super-special weapons will be enough. You thought Ultimecia was bad news? That bitch has NOTHING on me, Almasy. I’m going to crush you, destroy you…and hurt you bad.” He chuckles. Blaze: “Hey, before I forget…where’d you get the midgets?” Beelzebozo: “I dunno. I just thought midgets would be nice to have around the Carnival, so I sent the Quiet One to find a couple for the day.” Blaze: “What, do temp agencies have midgets to hire out to people?” Beelzebozo: “I guess so…hey, as long as they’re here, who cares? I’m happy.” Blaze: “I hope we can afford them…” Beelzebozo: “How much can midgets cost? They eat less than bigger people, right?” Blaze: “I suppose…I never thought I’d have to ponder the economics of having midgets around.” Beelzebozo: “Gotta be ready for anything in Hell’s Carnival, dear.” Blaze: “You have a very good point there.” Before either can say a word, the Quiet One runs back onscreen, pointing the other way. Beelzebozo: “I think he wants us to follow him! What’s that, Quiet One? Timmy fell down the well again?” The mime flips him off and runs off. Blaze: “Let’s see what’s up…” With that, the two of them run offscreen. We fade out, and fade back in to the living room. The three are gathered around the television, watching the latest promo to be shown. Beelzebozo: “The hell?…pause it, please.” The Quiet One does so, having smartly taped the promo for his friends. Blaze: “I dunno…I’m as lost as you are.” Beelzebozo: “I AM fighting for the TV Title, right? Or am I fighting for the World Title without somehow knowing it?” Blaze: “Or maybe you’re fighting for both?…” Beelzebozo: “Seymour…he holds them both, right? Because I sure as hell don’t have a belt lying around here…” Blaze: “Yeah, I think we’d keep better track of that sort of thing…” Beelzebozo: “Christ, they’ve managed to confuse a demonic, crazy wrestling clown. They should get a medal.” Blaze: “Is it really that hard to do?” The clown growls at her. “Shutting up now.” Beelzebozo: “Let’s finish the tape. Maybe the explanation is forthcoming?…” The mime starts the tape, and they watch. By the end, the clown looks even MORE confused. Blaze: “I…I’d forgotten about that.” Beelzebozo: “What?” Blaze: “His wife. She got socked with a crowbar and…I dunno if she’s still alive or not.” Beelzebozo: “Apparently I missed this.” Blaze: “It was the last pay per view. You were too busy puking to watch, but I did.” Beelzebozo: “Who wanted to watch it anyway? Who wanted to see Ryder win that tourney?” Blaze: “There was more. A Mr. Jamie Shock had a World Title match with Seymour. Near the end, the ref got KO’d and Shock brought out a crowbar. He smacked Seymour around, but when he went for the final shot, Aerith interceded and took a nasty hit to the temple. She went down bleeding, all to save the man she loved.” She sniffles. “It inspired him, of course, and he hit some nasty-ass move called the One Hit Kill twice on Shock before pinning him for the win.” She sniffs some more. Beelzebozo: “This explains all the crying you were doing the night of the pay per view…” Blaze: “Yeah. It was beautiful, really. I can only hope to do something as noble someday.” Beelzebozo: “I’d rather have you alive and healthy, personally. Sounds like Seymour’s in a lot of pain, and well he should be. “Of course, he’s going to be in even MORE pain this weekend…” Blaze: “Come on, now. Go easy on him.” The clown’s stares icily at her, and she shrinks back. Beelzebozo: “Oh, what? I’m supposed to be nice because he lost his oh-so-pwecious wifey? I guess I should apologize for our earlier antics and what-not? Maybe we can take him some tea and crumpets and make nicey-nice with him!” Blaze: “I didn’t mean it like that.” Beelzebozo: “Well, that’s good, because it’s not going to happen. I am not a nice man. I’m not here to cry over spilled milk, or blood and brains as the case may be. I’m not here to make friends with some crazy goof who plays dress-up!” Blaze coughs. “Shut up. I’m aware of the hypocrisy, I’m trying to make a point, all right. Anyway, clowns are real; video games are not. AS I WAS SAYING. This is the UWF. The best of the best compete here. And sometimes, bad things happen. People get hurt. Especially non-wrestlers who make it their business to interfere in matches.” Blaze: “She was protecting her husband!” Beelzebozo: “And she knew the risks of doing so!! I’m not going to pretend I feel bad because she made some crazy choice to be her man’s white knight in shining armour! I bet he’d much rather have taken the blow himself than let her come to harm! I feel bad that his wife apparently took a couple of shots to the head before the match, to make a decision like that, but she deserved it!” Blaze: “That’s really cold, man. What about the guy using the crowbar in the first place?” Beelzebozo: “Well he’s obviously a goddamn pussy who couldn’t get the job done, to resort to such pathetic measures to try and secure victory! But that’s not my fault either! Seymour should’ve been ready for anything! The fact that he wasn’t shows he is not a worthy champion, and I intend to prove that once and for all in a matter of days!” Blaze: “It’s a good thing you aren’t about to be hit with a crowbar, because right now I think I’d let it happen.” She stalks off. Beelzebozo: “I wouldn’t ask you to take it in the first place!! I can take that sort of punishment, and wouldn’t expect you to! So don’t do me any favors of that sort!!” Blaze’s voice: “I won’t!” Beelzebozo: “Good!!” He shakes his head. “Damn woman anyhow. This is nuts. Why am I the bad guy here? I’m not the one who needed to be saved by a noble but stupid woman in the first place! I’m not the goof who thinks he’s a video game sprite come to life! I’M the sane one here dammit!!” He shakes his head again. “Seymour, look, I actually DO feel bad that your wife’s hurt. You’re weak and loony, and she’s a sentimental moron, but it would still suck to be in that position.” A haunted look crosses his features. “I know.” He looks close to crying now. “I know all too well.” He suddenly jerks his head to the side, as if listening to someone, and then shakes his head rapidly, like a dog worrying a bone. “Where was I? Oh yeah. If you’re going to use your wife’s injury as some sort of excuse for your losing the title this weekend, well…that’s your problem, not mine. I’m still going to come at you like a Chocobo stampede, and if you’re too busy moping around the ring to defend yourself, that’s also your problem. This is the world of wrestling. You have to be on your game every single time you step into the ring, or you are going to lose. You must be pretty good, to have held that belt…er, belts?…for a good long time. I’m sure you could be a challenge to me in the ring. However…from what I can see, I should make some shelf room for a nice, shiny gold belt. Maybe two. I’m still confused about that part. And tofu too. That confuses me. But like I was trying to tell you…it all comes down to one thing. “You have your Limit Breaks. You have your impressive abilities. You also have your searing, soul-rending pain. You have the fear of an unknown quantity, one you can’t possibly hope to overcome. “I only have one thing. But that one thing is what will guarantee me my victory. “You see, it’s the one thing I ALWAYS have. And the one thing I will always have. “The Last Laugh.” He chuckles darkly. “Sleep tight, and dream…dream of better days, Seymour. Because bad times are coming for you. A nightmare of epic proportions. You’ve lost your wife…well, get ready to lose a belt or two. And some body parts as well, if I can help it. “Because I’m coming for you. And I’m worse than any boss monster…because I’m very much real, and very much willing to destroy you to get what I want. “Sleep tight, and dream. No, I take that back. Sleep tight…and scream. Scream for me, Seymour. SCREAM. SCREAM YOU BITCH!!” He runs toward the camera and begins shaking it, screaming like a banshee and occasionally banging his head into the lens. And with that, we fade to black.
