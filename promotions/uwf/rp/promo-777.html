UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #777

Time for the Talent to Rise

9 Nov 2004

Ezra Kazee — Chaos Society's new bus.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up within a very large tour bush. Sitting in the bus is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. The bus itself is dark. The windows are tinted a deep black color, and the interior is red. The bus is also very lavish, almost looking more like a living room instead of a bus, complete with TV, couches, game systems, and training facility. There are some random locations where you can see the red circle with an “A” in the middle of it. The symbol of anarchy. Kazee sits with a smug grin.) Kazee: Yes, I did it. So what. I did what need to be done. Sometimes, one has to make drastic measures to get things done. I’m tired of the toiling around. I’m tired of looking at the fed and seeing some of these people on top. Especially when there is great talent sitting there not getting the change they deserve. Something had to be done, and it is being done……. Look at the talent in Chaos Society. Look at what we have in here and look at what we are going to be able to do. We have Raid. Raid is a monster. Raid is one of the strongest people ever. Raid can do anything he wants whenever he wants and no one can stop him. Sure, he’s been a little inactive as of late, but when the federation decides to keep people toiling around in places that they don’t need to be, that’ll happen, and I don’t blame him…… And we have Anarchy. One of the most dominant tag teams to ever grace the wrestling circuit. One of the most dominant tag teams to ever grace some of the feds in general. They’ve had a slow start. Reo Dai is hurt. But give it time. Once he gets back. Once everything comes back together, they will be able to dominate the tag team ranks. Joanna in her own right could beat just about anyone on this roster….. And now they have me. The man who has been a force in the UWF for several months now. A man who is going to continue to be a force in the UWF for several months. The man who knows just about anything there is to know about wrestling. The man who can wrestle and counter wrestle better than any wrestler here in the UWF, and that’s just a fact. And with Chaos Society at my back, there is no way I can be stopped…… And soon, the World title is going to come to Chaos Society. Because I’m going to bring it to Chaos Society. Sure, I have this small little match for number one contendership coming up. And I know the competition is going to be tough. But I have a new focus now. A new focus going into this match and a new meaning for what I’m doing. It’s time to bring the talent of this fed to where it should be. Cause it definitely isn’t be given the respect it deserves. I mean, let’s face it, those that I face, they are not talented. They never will be talented. Jamie Shock, sure, he’s won a couple of decent matches, but he’s a brawler. He’s not a wrestler. He wouldn’t know the difference between a wristlock and a wristwatch….. And then there is the Communist. As if he has any business calling himself a wrestler. Everyone knows that 7 footers don’t make good wrestlers. They never have. And I’m sure they never will. He is also a brawler, and everyone knows that any good grappler will destroy a brawler any day of the week. That’s just the fact of the matter….. And finally, there is Digital Mortality. I’ve beat this man before. And I’ll beat him again. He knows exactly what I can do, and he’ll continue to know exactly what I can do. I’ve gotten the monkey off my back from not being able to beat him, and now it’s just all down hill from now on, at least in his case….. These men, they can’t stand up to me. They can’t stand up to anyone in Chaos Society. After all, that is where the talent is. And that’s what people are going to have to realize is that Chaos Society is where the talent is. Chaos Society is going to start getting recognized. And I’m going to start the recognition process by not only dominating this match, but also by going in the next week and dominating the champion, bringing the belt home to Chaos Society….. Now do I care who I face? Not at all. I’d rather face Monty. After all, someone needs to put him in his place for once and for all. But I’ll face either, and when the smoke clears and the fog rolls out, it’ll be “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee reigning supreme with Chaos Society at my side. And then some changes are going to happen…… Good Luck to all of you. You are just the first to feel the wrath of the new Chaos Society…... (Raid and Anarchy walk up into the bus.) Hey guys. Like the bus? Sure hope so. Just bought it and I thought that it was time that everyone in the UWF knew who was coming to town and besides, it’s got everything we could want.) (Scene fades out.)
