UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #509

From the Ahses of Empires

7th July (year unrecorded)

Unforgiven — Remains of Slasher's home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[There comes a time in everyone’s life where they must take a drastic measure to give them a newer outlook on life. There are always times when one must make a sacrifice in order to rework themselves, to re-mold their life and to cast away any doubts caused in the past. And with that in mind, it is the ultimate sacrifice made by Slasher that has somewhat set the tone for Unforgiven. For you see, infront of 'The King of Extreme's' feet lay the ashes of Slasher's home - burnt to the ground in an act of unrivaled passion; a passion to show the world that he has nothing to lose now.] 'And so, as Slasher has shown the world he is ready to give up all worldly possessions in his quest to finally capture a World Title, I too have given up everything, though this has not been by choice - some people may call it a tragedy... I call it a destiny. See, for two years now people have been putting me on a pedestal and then throwing stones and sticks at me for not achieving what many thought... I could achieve. People seemed to peg their hopes on me becoming the big star of the company, becoming a franchise... becoming the big player in the business. You see this all stems from the fact that I was lured into situations with promises of money, glory and fame. And lord knows to some people that is all life is; one big game of Monopoly. Pass go, collect your belt, get out of jail for free' [And with that, Unforgiven's head suddenly jolts, his hair erratically moving as his face contorts into that of sheer anguish and pain] 'WELL NOT ME! NOT ME! I WAS THE ONE WHO ENDED UP IN JAIL, IN A CELL WITH NAYSAYERS NAME CALLING ME FOR WALLS! WITH A CONTRACT I COULDN'T GET OUT OF FOR CELL BARS! FOR AN ADDICTION I COULDN'T GET RID OF AS A WARDEN! I NEVER PASSED GO, NEVER COLLECTED A TITLE BELT AND COULD NEVER LEAVE JAIL AFTER ROLLING THE DICE SEVERAL TIMES! [Unforgiven calms down as he starts looking at the ashes again. Some of them rise into the air looking as if there are still embers burning. Unforgiven wipes his eyes, visibly distressed] You see.... all the time in the past I gout caught up living the lifestyle of a rock and roll star… NOT THAT OF A WRESTLER… and it all began after turning my back somewhat on my fellow brethren. It started when I got a call from the head office, from Richard Hayes, who felt that the expiry date for the Drowning Pool and myself had passed and that a new challenge was needed. I was somewhat reluctant, but they started throwing money and opportunities my way; they paired me up with Kenny Mercury who top my face was the friendliest person in the world, yet behind my back complained this guy who was addled with a drug addiction was his new tag team partner. They didn’t care though – they knew that as long as I took the falls, the bumps and let Mercury shine as the technical spectacle that they’d get their pay day and line their pockets with their tainted cash. It was somewhat of a shame that the huge pay day never happened; I was taunted by my personal demons and everyday I was on that road with the UWF and doing as instructed, bit by bit my sanity started to wane away. My health began to deteriorate and I was destroying the cozy family life that I was living. Every day I lived that silly little BSR gimmick to make the figure heads happy, I was killing my own happiness. I lost my smile the day I got fired from the company due to personal reasons.” [Unforgiven rolls up the sleeves of his hooded top. His hands are taped up with the letter ‘X’ on the bandages and has new tattoo’s running up his forearms. He points to several pin pricks in his arms, as his eyes begin to well up and a somewhat sickening smile comes to his face. He still refuses to look directly into the camera, as he voice begins to shake as he calmly talks again] “Of all the scars on my body, of all the cuts, bruises and gashes I’ve suffered, these are the scars that make me nauseous the most. These scars at one time provided me with exquisite pleasure – a concoction of pain and ecstasy heated over a flame on a spoon. The UWF turned a blind eye to it, Kenny Mercury turned a blind eye to it, EVERY MOTHER FUCKER TURNED A BLIND EYE TO IT….. and so there I was, wallowing in a pool of my own worthlessness and misery. I felt as high as a kite when the days were good, but it soon became a chore to even get out of bed in the morning, let alone wrestle week in week out. But it didn’t matter to the UWF – as long as they got their bump-monkey that’s all that mattered. ‘Thank you Ben, excellent job, no title shot yet but we appreciate your hard work. Here’s some money… oh and the local street corner should be your next port of call.” They may as well just of gotten a big neon sign with the words ‘Heroin Addict’ on it, because everyone knew; the dirt-sheets on the Internet were posting news about my backstage habits and the resulting family life. I got accused of being an adulterer with several different diva’s in the company. People were sending me messages of hope, but I didn’t really care about that because the only person I cared about while addicted to that junk was me. It was a vicious circle for me – I’m upset because my wife doesn’t love me and my child is watching her daddy become a shell. I’m doing drugs because it’s a release from the reasons I am upset. It all came to ahead when in one of my final matches in the UWF I saw a sign in the crowd that read “Heroin > Jericho”. That was the last thing the UWF needed – not so much provocation, but public acknowledgment. ‘How dare you bring us into disrepute! You’re worthless, you’re not a draw and above all else you’re not welcome here. Get out!’ was the goodbye sentiments I received. No more big houses, no more ideal lifestyle and no more heroin. It was cold turkey for me. But nothing….. NOTHING was as worse as when I went back to my wife….” [Unforgiven’s hand reaches into the back pocket of his black cut off ¾ length jeans, and pull out a picture of his wife, their child and himself. He starts weeping softly as his eyes burn into the picture with sadness, anguish and above all else, remorse] “This is what I threw away…. NOT a career in wrestling…. NOT my friends…. I threw away my family. [He wipes his eyes as he is visibly upset, sniffing as he still looks at the photo]. I loved them both – they were everything to me; my alpha and my omega. We had good times together even when my addiction first started, but as much as my wrestling career went on the slide, my home life went on a much steeper drop. We were arguing all the time, my baby girl crying as I stormed out the house with money – I even took to stealing; a 24 year old stealing money his wife earned just to get the next fix. Things got to ahead, and everyone favorite good guy Ben Jericho was a shadow in real life to that of the character who was on television – I took to hitting my wife. I took to shouting abuse to her in front of our child. I was everything my parents would abhorred me to be… AND THEN SOME. A three year old seeing her father drag her mother by the hair into the kitchen, and here the sound of struggling as my temper raged out of control and I begged, pleaded and beat my way into getting more money. She left me, she took my kid, and I can only visit now and again. This was all 11 months ago, when I left the UWF.] [Unforgiven reaches into a black sports bag that is to his side and pulls out a replica UWF World Title belt he received after beating Deke Aurora for the title, bearing the name “Ben Jericho” on it. He puts the picture away after gently kissing it, and finally turns to the camera] “And in the last six months, my life has taken a complete turn around. After what seemed decades of trying to kick the habit, I finally came to terms with my addiction, with the problems in my life – with what went wrong. [Pulls his bottom lip down to show three X’s tattooed on the inside of the lip, and then lets it go] If I was to combat my demons, then I was to combat every last one that could stand in my way – I gave up recreational drugs… I gave up alcohol… and focused on self preservation. I came to realize that that the anger that I was throwing at my wife should have been channeled into my wrestling ability. I lacked passion in the company before and it’s the passion to come back at write my wrongs that is keeping me going. I wronged Slasher… I wronged Misfit… I left them in a vulnerable position with no guiding light or influence whatsoever. I basically turned by back on the one person who could truly understand my torment… Slasher… for someone who didn’t understand me at all. I sold myself out and became a label, I wasn’t the King of Extreme or Patron Saint of Hardcore anymore – I was a marketing ploy for a lot of people! I WAS A GIMMICK, THAT’S ALL, A GIMMICK TO MAKE MONEY FROM… they sold me magic beans when I sold my soul to them. [Unforgiven looks at the surroundings – somewhat of a barren wasteland, and then back at the ashes] “Look behind me, look in front of me, then look at me. There’s a theme – emptiness. There is nothing here but the charred remains on someone’s past. There once was an illustrious history as a backdrop that is nothing now… burnt to the ground inside out. That’s me… I burnt myself out, and then destroyed myself from within. [pure concentration and revelation to his face] I can strangely relate to these pile of ashes, these inanimate things that were once full of fire and awe. And the thing that get’s to me is that these ashes, the remains, they mean nothing… and my ashes, they mean nothing to me anymore…. They mean absolute shit compared to what’s coming in the future for me. You see, this is it for me – this is my redemption. Receiving a phone call from Slasher was some sick bastards way of telling me that I lost touch with those who matter to me the most – my brethren. Slasher, Misfit, and to some degree an opponent who I value as much as I hated… those were the people who made me feel like me.” [Unforgiven kneels to the floor as he places the replace UWF World Title onto the floor, and looks into the air as the heavens open and it begins to lightly rain on his face. He pulls his bandana off, and runs his fingers through his messy blonde and black hair.] “For you Slasher, I wish to make a sacrifice you made for me… no….for US. You burnt your past, and now I am going to make the ultimate sacrifice… no more will I toil in the total horror, anguish, disbelief of my past. You see, I made a blood pact with Slasher when we fought side by side and against each other that we would win a certain set of Tag Team Titles… it’s more than that though. This is my last chance at being something more than a joke in the eyes of many stalwarts to this company, to the GIWF, to the wrestling community, to the PTC… from Ian Childress right down to people like Nick Persia and Mr. Meaner. This… is… MY… REDEMPTION. And for that, I must erase my wrong doings…] [From the sports bag, Unforgiven pulls out a can of kerosene and a box of matches. The rain starts to drizzle heavier now as he pours most of the can over the title belt, and the draws a single match from the pack.] “My sacrifice for you, brother Slasher, is the death of Ben Jericho… and all that came with it” [And with that, Unforgiven strikes the match and dumps it inside the box, causing a minute inferno in his hand. He proceeds to toss the box onto the title belt, which erupts into flames right before his eyes. Unforgiven just lowers his head, as if to commiserate the death of someone close] “Don’t cry for Ben Jericho. He died a long time ago… not in body, but in spirit. And to be honest with you, for the sins he caused, for the pure hate he riled up from the few who loved and cared for him, he deserved to go. He was nothing more than someone with a terminal illness – his egotistical ways caught up with him and caused me… Unforgiven… so much hurt. He took my hates and made them his loves… he took an addiction and made it feel right until the very end when he couldn’t take anymore. And now, without any other voice within myself to tell me ‘Don’t do that, it’s not the cool way to do it… that’s not how Ben Jericho, the charismatic junkie would do it’, I’m free of my inhibitions. I don’t have anything to lose anymore, because I just killed off the last remaining piece of insecurity I had. And I did that not just for you, brethren… I did that for Unforgiven.” [The fire begins to die down as the fake metal begins to warp into shards of sharp, twisted debris. Unforgiven stands back up and adjusts the bandana back on his head.] “With the last breaths of Jericho, he uttered to me ‘why’… and I simply asked him ‘Why not? You took everything from me for your own needs. You used me [laughs] heh, YOUR NOT ME. YOU COULD NEVER BE ME. You’re a cheap whore Jericho, selling your body for cash… for ill gotten needs, for your own addiction. MY ADDICTION IS PAIN… you addiction is something bought off a corner…. MY ADDICTION IS PURE EMOTION.’… and with that, I covered his mouth to stop him breathing. I told him that everything would be ok… I’d look after Misfit… I’d take care of my unfinished business he never contemplated picking up… and then as he looked up at me, tears in his eyes, he had the look of relief on his face. For the FIRST time in his life, he was peaceful… no more worrying, no more caring.. he looked peaceful as his erratic breathing stopped, and uttered to me ‘I’m sorry’. That’s all I could ask… that’s all he could say…. WELL I’M SORRY TOO, BEN… I’M SORRY YOU WENT THAT WAY… and so I once again take the reigns of his destiny, but unlike other times, he is not here to take over when riding the wave of success. You talk about sacrifices Slasher… that’s your sacrifice… Ben Jericho is dead… and standing before you is the man you wanted, and the man who’ll be with you to the bitter end… and before all his senses came crashing down to earth… the last thing he’ll remember is me telling him he is forgiven… but for me.. I shall always be, and always will be dubbed…. UNFORGIVEN…” [Unforgiven picks up his sports bag and solemnly blows a kiss to the title belt, symbolising the deletion of his previous achievements, his former life… and signifying a new book in his life… no more chapters… the life of one Ben Jericho no longer exists… the life of Unforgiven, however, has now become an unholy scripture in the UWF…..] [END TRANSMISSION]
