UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #634

Final Preparations

14 Aug 2004

Mike Montgomery — Downtown Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is late afternoon, the day after Executive Vice President of Talent Relations Mike Montgomery had his interview with the UWF Action Replay and twenty minutes after meeting with the agent of James Edwards, aka Moraless. Montgomery has spent much of those twenty minutes in a second meeting, specifically with former Player's Club member Nick Persia, regarding his role in the World Title match at Terror in Tokyo. The office doors open and both men step through the threshold, clearly very pleased with how the meeting went. They shake hands before Montgomery begins to walk Nick out. 'I must say Mike, this little arrangement of yours is a brilliant idea. Special Guest enforcer...not quite the same as being in the match...' 'True, and again I'd like to apologize for that but it's just not possible. Of course, you do still have your title shot from winning Stable Wars so there's no reason to believe you won't be challenging in the near future anyway.' 'A good point. I still can't believe Ezra didn't push to have me as special referee as promised. I mean, you have to admit that I had more than enough leverage to get it done.' 'Indeed he did. Cut Ez some slack though, I think he's really still just learning the political game. I'm not sure he even knows that Preest didn't make you a ref.' At this Nick raises an eyebrow, 'No? How could he not? He was at the meeting!' Mike nods, 'That he was. The thing is, in his last promo, he says that you are one of the refs even though Preest never named you one. Either way, I think it's safe to say the problem's solved. I trust you'll be able to keep Tropical Storm in line for me?' 'I'll do my bit. Just make sure you get me a partner who can hold up his end of the bargain.' The two men reach the elevator, 'I have every confidence that I'll be able to find the perfect partner for you,' he offers his hand, 'so just take it easy and I'll see you in Tokyo.' The two men shake hands before Persia hits the elevator call button. 'Ms. Allen, have a limo waiting downstairs for Mr. Persia, would you?' The secretary picks up the phone as she answers, 'Right away sir.' The arrangements were made just as the doors shut and Nick Persia was on his way out of the building. Montgomery blew out a large puff of air he hadn't realized he'd been holding in. With all the Player's Club members at odds with each other, he had been fully aware that something was being planned. There was really no reason to truly believe the match would be a fair contest. In a submission match, Ezra would be the biggest threat, and his recent political maneuvers showed that he was not only fully aware of this, but intended to do what he could to get every other advantage possible. 'What's left on my schedule Ms. Allen?' The secretary turned toward her computer, calling up Montgomery's schedule with a few clicks of the mouse. 'Well, you have a session with Deacon Blaise in about twenty minutes. Otherwise you're free for the remainder of the day.' Damn Mike thought to himself. His recent training sessions with one of the most widely known submission trainers on the planet were paying off...they just weren't exactly the highlight of his day. He grabs his coat, hitting the elevator button while calling over his shoulder, 'I'll be out for the rest of the day then. Any calls...just take a message unless it's an emergency, transfer that to my cell...otherwise...I'll see you tomorrow.' His secretary nods her understanding just before the elevator door slides open. Montgomery steps inside, hits the button for the garage, and let's his mind wander as the elevator makes the twelve floor trip. Ultimate Submission in a cell...he had sixty minutes to make three of the toughest men he'd ever been in the ring with tap out as many times as possible. That made this possibly the toughest fight of his career. But then...he couldn't help but realize he thought that about pretty well every title match. If it weren't for the addition of Tropical Storm, this time the odds would've been in his favor. Kevin Starr wasn't likely to be much of a threat. Submission wrestling wasn't really his strong suit, and if he put the same effort into the match as he was his promos, he'd be a non-factor. Ezra was a problem though. A strong amateur background and knowledge of almost every submission hold in the book...and probably a few that weren't...were what had made Montgomery's training sessions necessary. The thing was, Ezra didn't appear to be as focussed as he could be. The sportsman had been replaced by a man with a vicious meanstreak a few weeks ago when he shocked everyone by attacking the World Champion...esse ntially for doing the very thing that the Club was created for. Now though, that more aggressive, take no prisoners version of the 'Common Man' appeared to have been replaced, the world now once again seeing the gracious sportsman. And what of the World Champion? He suffered the greatest distraction of all. It appears that the Universal Wrestling Federation is suffering from puppy love. As men are want to do from time to time, Joshua Dunn seems to be finding himself distracted by a woman by the name of Alexia. The problem was that this meant that Dunn himself, not Ezra, was possibly Montgomery's biggest threat in the match. It all depended on what sort of performance Dunn could put together. It would go one of two ways. First, the presence of his lady love would inspire him to wrestling heights the likes of which his career had never seen, causing him to fight through the tremendous pain that all four men had in their immediate futures, or she would distract him. It was also possible that his focus would be sufficiently split between her and the match that he would not be able to put together the kind of performance that had won him the title, bringing the belt back to the man known as 'The Main Event.' With these thoughts swirling around in his head, the Elevator doors open, bringing Montgomery back to reality and depositing him in the parking garage of the UWF's Chicago offices. A quick jog to his midnight black Dodge Viper and he was off to the gym. Deacon's Gym was located at the edge of downtown Chicago. The neighbourhood wasn't quite high class enough to attract the high rent businesses, but at the same time it wasn't low class enough to be considered 'slums.' Parking in front of the old Brownstone, Montgomery grabs his bag and heads in. Deacon had the ground floor of the building set up for the more physical aspects of wrestling. There was a fully furnished gym, weights...exercise bikes...machines...the whole nine yards. Nearby, mats were set up on the floor in a large square shape. This was where amateur wrestlers sparred and practiced new holds. Much of the opposite side of the gym was taken up with an older Universal Wrestling Federation ring. The building's second floor contained the lockerrooms and several classrooms where wrestlers could study videos and hold meetings with Deacon regarding strategy and technique. Deacon himself, was often at the front door, prepared to meet and greet the high-paying clients. Deacon Blaise was six inches shorter than Montgomery, yet had twenty pounds on the man in sheer muscle. Long black hair hung loosely down to his shoulders, and he'd already changed into his wrestling tights. 'Mike,' Deacon calls out, coming forward to greet the man, 'I trust you've been following my workout instructions?' Mike shook Deacon's hand as he responds, 'Of course. Considering the amount of money I have invested in this place, I'd be foolish not to get the most out of my investment. With luck I'll last five minutes before my first tap-out today.' Deacon couldn't help but laugh, 'Just so long as you remember what I've been telling you...' 'That momentum is key,' Montgomery finishes as he heads toward the stairs to the locker rooms, 'the second you stop moving, is the second your opponent cinches in and your hand starts tapping.' 'Bingo. Glad to see you're paying attention!' Deacon would wait patiently the ten minutes it took Montgomery to get into his ring attire. He guided Montgomery through the usual thirty minutes on the bike before an hour of stretching and weights. Finally, the two men stepped into the ring and began sparring. It was a back and forth battle for the first four minutes and forty five seconds before Deacon had Montgomery tapping out with a variant of the Rings of Saturn. For nearly ninety minutes the two men exchanged holds, a cross face here, a figure four there, before the time finally came for Montgomery's practice to end. Deacon had taken the match with sixteen submissions to Montgomery's fourteen. Never the less...'I have to say that was your best showing yet. The Sharpshooter with a one-handed Camel Clutch was nice. Who taught you that one?' Montgomery just shrugs, answering 'Nobody really, it just kinda came to me so I figured I'd go with it.' Montgomery stopped at a water fountain, drinking from it like a man who'd been in a desert for a month. 'I'd have to say you're as ready as you'll ever be. So long as you're aware of where you are, and you don't lose your momentum, I'll be expecting to see gold around your waist the next time you come in.' 'Well, I guess we'll just have to wait and see.' The two men exchange goodbyes before Montgomery's return to his car. He leans back in the seat, clearly more exhausted than he'd let on in the gym. After his quick breather, the Viper pulls into the road, heading back in the direction of the UWF's offices. Instead though, he parks across the street, in front of the 'Crash Pad,' the bar once owned by the Universal Wrestling Federation itself, now owned by Asylum Industries. Stepping in, he surveys the scene. Business is as brisk as ever. He spies a stool at the corner of the bar and slides on to it. 'Mike! How you doing? Just come in from Deacon's?' 'It's that obvious huh?' Mike asks. 'Well, not really,' the bartender answers, sliding a coke across the counter, 'as your bartender I make it my business to know this stuff. Besides, I did recommend the guy to you.' 'That you did, and let me just say a very sound investment in every sense of the term. Just like this place. I'm glad you keep things hopping around here.' 'Thanks man, that really means a lot. Listen, you take it easy and good luck in Tokyo. Coke's on the house.' 'Much obliged man,' Mike tells the bartender, raising his glass in salute, 'much obliged.' Mike would sit in silence for awhile, enjoying the caffeine and taking in the sights around him. He ws recognized by several fans who he obliged with autographs. 'Hey, you're in my stool!' A voice slurred from behind him. Montgomery turned to see four rather large men had surrounded him. Two of them had beer steins in their hands, and all four stank of alcohol. 'Well whaduhya know? If'n it ain't the former champeen of the U Dubya Eff!' 'Leader of the Playah's Club!' 'Is there playah in the house!' 'The guy woh's gunna bring me some serious cash by losing in Tokyo!' Great Mike thought to himself. Four guys...smallest one's probably two hundred and fifty pounds if he's lucky...and not only do they know who I am... He was narrowly able to avoid the first of the group's middle guy, Naturally they want to pick a fight. Mike kept his back to the bar as much as possible. He launched a hard kick into his attacker's gut, quickly following with a shot to the knee of annother of his attackers. The first guy clubbed him in the back, driving Montgomery to the ground. 'Youse ain't gunna make it to Tokyo Monty!' 'Big Kev's our boy! We bet on him to win!' As the fists rained down, Montgomery thought he saw something strange at one of the tables. A patron, who appeared to have been watching the entire proceeding, calmly put his own beer glass down and stood up. His bald head brushing against the lamp that hung over the table, Montgomery was able to get a better look at him. He was seven feet tall easily, a full beard of black hair covering his face. Tribal tattoos ran up and down both arms, across his shoulders, and down his chest. How far down Montgomery couldn't tell because the man wore a black wife-beater that was making a desperate effort to contain his three hundred something pound frame. Light reflected off his black pleather pants as he made his way over. The man swung Montgomery's nearest attacker around and drove his fist into the man's face hard enough to send him spinning over the bar. Another attacker attempted to charge the man, only to run smack into the man's upraised boot. The man grabbed the third attacker in a full nelson, swinging the man directly into the fourth attacker's path, causing him to smash a beer stein over his buddy's head. The man then picked his victim up, still in the full nelson, and slammed him to the ground in a modified chokeslam. The mystery man grabbed Montgomery's fourth attacker by the shirt and threw him sraight out the front door. Montgomery would slowly pull himself up to his feet, staring in amazement at his savior's performance. 'Mike Montgomery,' he told the man, holding out a hand. The man just looked at it for an instant before deciding to shake it, 'Gavin Frost.' 'Mr. Frost,' Montgomery smiled, 'have you ever been to Tokyo?'
