The Communist — Backstage

:the Communist is sitting on a bench backstage, talking on a cell phone: Commie: Yes Father. I'm still undefeated. The people will soon realize the power of Communism. When I win the TV title on Sunday, No one will be able to deny that the Iron Curtain is a focre to be reconed with in the ... no Father, I won't let you down. I can't. No, I'm not afraid of that sick sex freak. Bondage will be destroyed. Just like all the others who have faced the Communist. Alright Father. Tell the Committee they have nothing to worry about. Goodnight. :the Communist hangs up and takes a deep breath: Commie: I can hear it in his voice. He's disapointed. I'm not respected here. The Committee is not conviced I can do any good here. Everyone is against me. I will prove it to them. ALL of them :the Communist rises and throws the Phone against the wall: Commie: I AM COMMUNISM! I am the CHAMPION OF MY PEOPLE! And they DARE DOUBT ME! :breathing hard, face red with anger: I WILL show them! I will be the gloriouse, Swift end to Bondage's reign. No doubt. No fear. No stopping. I will show this Federation my Power. THis is my only chance. My only chance. :The communist repeats this like a mantra, over and over, as the scene fades:
