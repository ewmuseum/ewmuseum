I'm Drowning In My Sleep... Drowning In My Sleep

11/29 (year unrecorded)

'The Savior' Wes Diable

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The leaves blow across the ground as we pan up to a set of stairs. The concrete steps and black railing in the middle lead up to a pale orange-colored stone building. A sign on the outside of the building in the grassy area reads '-------- Police Department', the name of the department is obviously blacked out. A police officer walks up the steps and gives a friendly nod to two figures walking down. The one on the right gives a friendly wave] [The one on the right that had just waved appears to be somewhat unnerved. We've haven't seen this man before, he wears a white-shirt and open leather jacket along with black sweat pants. His hair is a light brown, but slicked back or thats what the attempt is. He has a bit of uneven facial hair and is slightly shorter and thinner then the man that walks with him. His company is a man the UWF fans are getting to know quite well as 'The Savior'. Wes wears a black zip-up hoodie with Atreyu written across the front in red-script writing and a pair of dark blue jeans. His dark hair sits flat on his head with an off-center part. His blue eyes are somewhat distant from the scene, almost disappointed] [They walk passed the front of the department and we follow closely behind. For a moment there is silence] As they turn the corner up ahead to the right, they stop in front of a white SUV, which Wes leans against as he digs into his pocket. The other man looks around anxiously then finally breaks the silence as Wes gets the cigarette to his lips and lights it] Man: Yo, what're we gonna do man? [Wes takes a drag and then fully inhales to ease some of his stress. He breathes out the smoke then turns his head, his eyes appear much different then before... Much more focused and set with a plan] Wes: You want a smoke. [He holds out the pack to the man. The man gives him a glaring look and after a quick few seconds Wes shrugs and puts them back into the pocket of his hoodie] Man: Man, answer me. What're we gonna do? [Taking another drag, he almost shoots a slight grin, its as though he likes making this man wait] Wes: Listen, Johnny Boy, you're overreacting a bit... [The man's brown eyes could very well be red for with how aggravated he is getting right now] John: Yo fuck you man! You didn't have to be interrogated for hours on end waiting for someone to come get your ass. Wes: And I didn't have to come get your ass! [Wes leans forward in a rare moment of emphasis and then simply leans back against the car once he feels the point has been proven] Wes: But I did because you're my boy and we need you in this operation. [John nods and then joins Wes in leaning against the SUV] Wes: You didn't say anything, right? [John appears almost appalled at the question shaking his head] John: Hell no! I ain't no rat! [Wes nods] Wes: Good. [He stands up from against the SUV and flicks his cigarette away. He turns towards John with his left hand digging into his pants pocket. He pulls out a set of keys and hits the button. There is a slight beep out of the SUV] Wes: Get in. [He walks to the driver's side door as the man looks on in disbelief] John: Wait, but what're we gonna do? [Wes laughs a little bit as he gets behind the wheel. He closes the door and waits, but John refuses to enter without answers] John(muttering): Who the fuck is this guy? [The window rolls down and Wes pops his head out] Wes: I'm the guy who's going to save your ass, now get in. [John looks on in disbelief that Wes was able to hear that. After a moment, of trying to make sense of it; he gives in and walks around to the passenger side] [Cut away.] [The ocean crashes against the waves as the cloud's overhead hang ominous and are colored a slight gray, threatening, but only that. A snack bar and information booth have closed for the fall and the American Flags wave on the beach violently much like a person caught in a riptide. A bikerider brave enough to face the wind and the cold rides by in complete bike gear] 'Its beautiful here in the summer' [An unfamiliar voice speaks, it’s a male's voice though based obviously on the sound and tone of it. After a short pause] 'In a weird sort of way... Its kind of beautiful now.' [This voice is more familiar and as we pan, we find the voice of the man is that yet-to-be-named priest that keeps popping up. His mostly grey hair blows in the wind slightly seeing as it is short. He wears a black Yankees windbreaker over his black collared priest outfit. His companion is also a priest, who wears a black windbreaker that says St. Jude's High School on the front over the right breast area in white writing. He is balding and has only hair on the sides of his head which is short and a mix of grey, white, and blonde] 'What do you mean by that Father Lucas?' [The chubby unfamiliar priest says. There is a slight pause of almost surreal silence that is broken by the sound of the flag whipping in the breeze] Father Lucas: I don't really know. I've always felt an affiliation towards the cloudier days for some reason. [The other priest nods as they both stare out into the ocean] Priest: Well maybe its like... [And before he can finish off his sentence] Father Lucas: A sinner... [The other priest looks at him in total shock and then chuckles that jolly priest-like chuckle] Priest: Normally people know me for longer then a day before they start finishing my sentences. [Father Lucas cracks a grin] Father Lucas: Great minds think alike I suppose. [The chubby priest taps him on the shoulder] Priest: Lets go this way, I feel like walking, too cold to just stand still. [Father Lucas after being tapped on the shoulder lets out a bit of a chill then walks with the priest down the boardwalk, we follow in toe, but slightly father behind] Priest: So what brings you here? I meant to ask, but I never got to. Father Lucas: A cloudy day... [This response leads to a fade to black] [A pounding sound is what we hear as we fade back in to a pair of sneaker slamming away on a treadmill. Immediately from the second we even lay eyes on those legs we know, who this is. As we move up our mouth's water in anticipation of Vivian. Her beautifully tanned skin seduces us from the most obvious spots to the most obscure. She wears a pair of low cut gym shorts that are colored black and a tight white tanktop] [Her dark straight hair is back in a ponytail and a small amount of perspiration falls down the side of her face. In the compartments in front of her sit her CD player and a small towel. There is a slight bit of panting leaving her lips. Close your eyes and pretend really fast] [Into our perfect line of view walks Wes Diable, as he's walking in he's removing his zip-up hoodie. After taking it off he tosses across the room onto a small loveseat. He then comes back into our view of heaven. The cameraman moves slightly, catching Vivian taking one ear-piece out of her headphones] Vivian: Hey love, whats up? [Wes gives a small grin] Wes: Business as usual. [She makes a bit of a fat lip and gives him a bit of a kiss, rather extraordinary that she can do so and remain balanced on the treadmill, there again if you seen her dancing a few weeks ago, you wouldn't be so surprised] Vivian: Cheer up hun, you want me to cut this one for you. [She gives a small glance back and a wink to the camera. Wes laughs] Wes: No thanks, this monstrosity actually gave me a bit of inspiration. [She shrugs slightly] Vivian: There loss, I'm good on camera. [As she puts her earpiece back in. Wes smiles and mutters] Wes: So am I. [Before he walks away, she shoots him one last smile; letting him know that she heard what the last thing he said was. He simply rolls his eyes and makes his way over to the loveseat, he leans against the arm of it. He appears slightly pre-occupied] 'Nick Persia... Deja fucking Vu...' [He shakes his head in disgust, rubbing his face with his hand] 'I swear that I've heard this somewhere before... Oh yeah, that was last week wasn't it. The guy who couldn't beat me after I was got tased. He's real good.' [Rolling his eyes again, but not in that playful flirty-with-Vivian sort of way, rather the I-can't-believe-this-match-is-happening-again way] 'Not to mention it’s a flaming monkey match, which means God knows what. Don't you people have lives? I have better things to do then beat a guy I already beat...' [He rests his head in his hand once again and pauses for a moment. He looks up with a smirk] 'Oh, and I know what you're thinking... Beating him with a flaming monkey or whatever is not one of them, you imbeciles.' [He must chuckle to himself as he says this] 'Well, it is a very creative idea for a bunch of imbeciles.' [There is the glimpse of the Wes Diable that we have all grown to love or loathe. Those eyes ablaze with that I-know-something-that-you-don't confidence] 'Nick, I must warn you I absolutely despise repetition and for someone, who with a clear advantage couldn't get the job done the first time, you're going to have an even harder time getting it done this time. Why might you ask?' [He stands up from the arm of the arm chair and plops himself down onto the actual seat, totally relaxed] 'Simply put Nick, its this little thing called momentum, which I have plenty of since I have won both of my first two matches here and now have been granted an IC title match. Not to mention that fact that winning this match, might just put you on suicide watch.' [He smirks] 'And everyone, but everyone loves a good suicide. I mean I've taken from you, your chance to become the World Champion and now I'm going to take away your safety blanket. You see it’s a little hobby of mine, ruining people's lives... I find it amusing, watching somebody with promise and potential fall out of sight. Nothing brings me greater joy. Nothing.' [He lifts his arms back over his head, stretching at first and then locking his hands making a make-shift pillow] 'You know, sometimes its hard to be this good. I mean look at my life... I've got a beautiful woman, a gorgeous home, undeniable talent. Some would say I've got all I could ever ask for. And maybe thats why I have to take away. Shallow? Maybe. Enjoyable? Absolutely.' [Suddenly a small ringing sound comes out of nowhere. Wes looks surprised at first then realizes its his cell phone, he reaches into the pocket of sweat-shirt and pulls it out. He flips it open] 'Whats up?' [There is a read serious look across his face] 'So its all taken care of?' [A grin from ear-to-ear] 'Good work.' [Fade out]
