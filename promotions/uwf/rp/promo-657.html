UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #657

Punked out?

30 Aug 2004

Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy — Still in Tokyo, for now.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Regal Rumble came and went. And Sean was, for lack of a better term, screwed. Moraless knew that he was one to fear. So did Tsunami Oni. They dind’t beat him. No, they dind’t beat Sean, look what Oni got earlier in the night? Yeah, your number one contender for the World Title was laid out on his back for a one on one fight. But 60 men got into the ring, and Oni dodged and hid, allowing others to do their work for them. Moraless was no different. He took on Sean only after he’d face off with many others. Only after everyone else had a shot in on him. They think they won the war? No. They won one battle, and it was a battle that they had planned better for. But when it comes down to it, one on one? Sean is obviously the best man in the UWF. Mike Montgomery had better realize what he’s got going now. Sean’s already destroyed the Hall of Famer, Tsunami Oni in one on one combat more then once. And now Oni was in the driver’s seat to his next World Title shot? Why? He lost. He lost. He can remember that. But while the KnightMare Brigade lost the Regal Rumble, however Sean may have one his match with Oni… something else happened. The now soon to be two-time PTC Global Champion is now riding high on that Flaming Unicorn, and he’s got Tsunami Oni in his sights also. Seymour Almsasy, Corporal Punishment, the Fomorians and Sean McClancy. Face it, the KB could and will own this place in that ring when the time comes. But first things first. Moraless… John Edwards Moraless. Glad to meet you, you little snake in the grass. You think you can hold the future king of the Seelie court down? You think you can do as you have and not expect Sean to come back and destroy you? Look at Oni. All he did was spit that blue shit in Sean’s face and guess what he got. Dropped on his face and humiliated. How can he lay claim to the Number One Contender slot without being reminded that just a few hours before he was holding his hands high winning the Rumble, he was on his back getting pinned? By all rights and virtues, the BDW World Champion should be right behind him on that waiting list. Not you Moraless. And this week we make sure of that. It’s my turn. And you won’t have help this time. It’s just you and “the Irish Bastard”. What’s Sean got to loose? Remember that. Tokyo, Japan. The Hotel, the few days after the Regal Rumble No, they’ve not gone back to the states yet. Sean is lying on what looks to be a king-sized bed in a hotel room. Kailey, Annie’s handmaiden, is dressing his wounds. A trained churgeon in the UnSeelie court, the pale woman needs little help from Annie, but she’s allowing her to at least try. Sean is lying on the bed eyes closed barely moving even to breathe. His face contorts with the application of salve and bandages to the open wounds on his skin. His upper torso shows some well bruised ribs with what looks to be shoe prints all up and down it. From waist down he is covered by a sheet so it is not known weather or not he has his wrestler trunks on or if he got changed before he passed out backstage with the exhaustion of two matches and the pain of the beaten body. Kailey is humming softly what sounds to be maybe a Celtic lullaby. The sounds are relaxing and calming to the listener. Then again, they’re part of her ‘specialty’ for those who know what she really is on the inside. Annie: ‘E took one ‘ell o’ a beating; I don’na think I have ever seen him so sure about somethin’. I now he wanted tha’ shot, it is the one title ‘e ‘as never held in th’ GIWF family of federations ‘e ‘as belonged to. Those bloody bastards were ou’ there t’ specifically try t’ attack ‘im an’ take ‘im ou’. I mean, look a’ this, they used a fokin’ fork on ‘im. Kailey: Yes mistress I know, but he is strong, and won’t let this hold him back. Annie: Aye, ‘e won’na let it do tha’. If anythin’ it’ll make ‘im slip int’ ‘is other side even more. While I’d be wantin’ tha’, they would’na. I’ve already taken the liberty ‘o makin’ sure ‘e’s ga’ a crack a’ tha’ bastard come next Wednesday. Which means ‘e needs t’ get ‘is mind on th’ game. Plus ye ‘ave th’ fact tha’ in two weeks ‘er so, ‘ell ‘ave t’ be back ‘ere in Tokyo fer th’ big event with Patti competing in th’ Dome. The man’s schedule is ou’ o’ whack riogh’ now. Kailey: Don’t forget mistress, but the Fomorian Twins also have a tag match coming up next Sunday. Annie: Don’cha be remindin’ me. Annie got up from the side of the bed, where she had been cleaning the wound that one of the competitors had used a fork on his shoulder. As she looked down upon him after getting up, her eyes welled with tears. She would of already been married to him if he had loved her from the beginning. But he felt that he needed more time, and went out and tried to break the promise given of marriage. She knew her coming to him would cause problem and it did, but now he was relying on her for things. His sister settled, and becoming a mother very soon. She knew the pull for him to do the same was getting to the point of almost driving him nuts. Which could explain the reason behind the way he had been lately allowing himself to be double and tripled booked: keeping his body in pain so his mind can’t think of anything else but the business. Her feelings for the Celtic Prince were no Secret. She had been betrothed to him at a young age. The blessings of both courts were given to them, but he didn't love her and she knew it. But yet he started to trust her again and was counting on her to get everything ready for them. Something she would fail at this was way to important to them both and to the courts, and to a point, the Order itself. She couldn’t forget that upon loosing Sean to the Greek woman, she had sworn herself to Sean’s own Lord. Her job was written out for her by him: Make sure he succeeds. Kailey: By the way Mistress, the dress maker called. She needs for you to ring her back and explain the Wedding dress to her again, that you want. It seems she has never made a dress with the design that you have asked for. Before Annie can respond, Sean starts to stir. Kailey goes back to humming her soft soothing song and Sean seemed to slip back into the restful sleep, he had been in all this time. Annie: Sometimes yer very ‘andy t’ ‘ave around. Sean would never ‘ave agreed t’ ‘avin’ us take care o’ these wounds fer him he would of said he was fine an’ t’ leave ‘im alone. But, between the bite on ‘is forehead, the fork that was stabbed into his shoulder and the beating ‘e took, ‘e needs rest. ‘E would still argue with us that ‘e is fine as always. Sean could hear them. But he had other things on his mind. “The pain; it bloody well ‘urts to breathe. Me ribs feel like a bull stomped all over me. Not only did I give it me all in a one on one match, bu’ then in a sixty man match, I gave my all once again. Only to have tha’ coward known as Moraless sit there and kick me over and over again. Na’ since I came to UWF ‘ad Moraless came out of ‘is hiding place and then, and only when ‘e ‘ad 60 men to protect him did ‘e step out. This is the same gobshite tha’ dropped th’ PTC Extreme Title after findin ou’ tha’ ‘is next match would be ‘avin’ me in it again. ‘E knew I would take ‘im ou’ then, an’ I’d take it back. Is this the worst I ‘ave ever felt? No, been worse? I ‘ave ‘ad worse injuries. Short shallow breaths, no deep long ones. I wasn’t planning t’ use this to show me worth to her, but I failed. ‘Ow am I now supposed to show a princess I am worthy of ‘er ‘and in marriage? ‘Ow am I supposed t’ show King Oberon tha’ I’m th’ riogh’ one t’ take over when ‘e leaves us again, as th’ rumors are abound riogh now.” Sean lays there half listening to the two women talk, not wanting to wake from his dream. His body, even for the pain it is in, feels relaxed for at least now. He is going to hurt more in the morning, he always does. But for the pain wasn't something bad, no it was good. It kept his mind on the job and not to her. Things like her. Yes, she was a distraction beyond distractions, but a pleasant one. A distraction that he couldn't afford, as he had been distracted in the ring last week with the Regal Rumble and the Oni match. He came out of the match with Oni just to have everyone that could gunning for him in the Regal Rumble. And that is where he went wrong, He didn't keep his mind on the prize instead he allowed his mind to wonder off. This time it wouldn't happen. Moraless was going to get what was coming to him. He knew that Annie had asked for this match. It was the big thing there Moraless not only defeated Sean for the Extreme title of the PTC but also threw him out of the ring for the regal rumble, and to add insult to injury landed on him when he himself was tossed out just a few seconds later. The impact… Sean was sure that a rib or two might be broken. But, then again he wasn’t totally sure his ribs didn’t just hurt from the kicking he took. The humming stopped... Sean started to wake... His eyes were fuzzy his ribs didn’t hurt as much but they were stiff. He had been sleeping but for how long.....He turned his head and saw Annie Sitting in a chair next to the bed reading something on her lap top. Sean: ‘Ow long? Annie: Good morning t’ ye me prince. Two days o’ full rest is what ye ‘ave gotten. We’re due a’ the airport tomorrow. Ye should be getting’ more, I know I wouldn’na be wantin’ me future ‘usband to be tired on me wedding night. Ye have a match in a few days against Moraless in a one on one match. Ye need to be well rested and ready t’ figh’. Sean pulled himself up to a sitting position, keeping himself covered as he did so from the waist down. Sean: So what do ye ‘ave there? Any tidbits on Moraless, that might help me ou’ a bit this week? Annie looks up at Sean and smiles. Annie: No, I am lookin’ a’ th’ wedding cakes fer th’ reception, I am sure th’ groom to be would like a word on what tha’ should look like. I mean you’re na’ allowed t’ ‘elp pick ou’ th’ gown, but th’ cake should be safe enough t’ look at. Sean scoots himself over on the bed and pats the side he was just laying on. Annie gracefully climbs up and sits down next to him on the bed. Sean leans in, putting his head on her delicate shoulder, to see her laptop screen better. Annie: While ye were sleepin’ th’ final two scrolls were returned. Both with good blessings. Sean: An’ th’ other one? Annie: Th’ other one came with a message. ‘E will be there when it’s made official, to give ‘is blessing. And the Seelie an’ Unseelie Courts are joyful. Oberon himself sends the blessings of the merging of the two courts. Yer plans are laid down me Prince. Soon, Oberon will sleep again, an’ ye’ll be in a position t’ assume yer rightful position as the ‘older o’ the Mantle of Bres Mac Elatha. But now on to the important part of this week: Yer match with Moraless. If ‘e beats ye it will be technically th’ third time fer that. It will be up t’ ye t’ give in er face him the way the old Bres would ‘ave. Yer time is comin’. Ye need t’ make sure yer na’ feelin’ anythin’ tha’ would make ye na’ think abou’ victory. Sean picks his head up and looks at her. Sean: Yer saying what? Annie: Sorry me Prince, bu’ ye ‘ave gotten soft in yer time away from the GIWF days. It's time the old Bres comes back. Ye were a monster in th’ PWW, bu’ na’ one tha’ was really concidered anythin’. Th’ other federations knew what ye were, bu’ ye can’na deny tha’ ye’ve ‘ad better days. The man that focused on wha’ was in th’ ring, na’ on wha’ was outside it. I seem remember you once saying tha “if ye can’na bring yer mind t’ th’ game, don'cha bring yer body”. Well it is time t’ bring yer mind also. Sean looks at Annie with a slight surprise on his face. Was this the same unseeliee princess that charmed the men into falling in love with her? Her blood line ran full with evil. She was a leanan, a form of vampire if you wanted to look at it. But one that would dirve any of her suitors insane as they pined for her. Sean himself came from a blood line of the Strongest of the Celtics Kings Bres himself. A blood line that carried both Fomorian and Tautha blood in it. Sean Carried the mantle of him, he also carried the name once. Moraless was no slouch, Sean knew this. He knew he was going to face a man that had one thing on that tiny little brain of his... The defeat of Sean McClancy. But this time Sean couldn't let it happen. He had to give his all in the ring. No matter what the cost. This match was going to grueling hard work, and then again what match wasn't hard work? The match that wasn't hard work didn't need to be a match. You don't give your best, then you’re just second rate. Sean wasn's second rate, he would never allow anyone to talk to him as if he was. It was time for him to step up the game a little. It was time to finish what was started in the Extreme title division only a few months ago. Moraless had to be shown that Sean was a strong man and he wouldn't just lay down for anyone. Yes Sean could use the excuse in the Regal Rumble that he was tired from the match with Oni but then again Oni did better then he did. Oni won but Oni wasn't the one that shoved him out no that was Moraless again, Moraless beat him… kicked him, He was bit, and forked. Now Sean was tired of being treated like shit and wouldn't allow it to happen anymore. Sean: So, what do ye ‘ave planned so far? Annie: Fer yer match or fer th’ “event”? Sean: I”ve ga’ th’ match. Yer deal was tha’ ye’d take care o’ th’ “event” plannin’. Annie: Well I have the dress on the way. At least I ‘ope so. I need t’ go and talk t’ the seamstress meself. There seems to be a problem with the way I described the gown. The jeweler's has the crowns on order; it seems tha’ they ‘ave decided this is number one priotry. So I ‘ad t’ go elsewhere fer th’ other stuff. The rings well, tha’ took a little, but I managed to find a gold smith in Ireland tha’ knows ‘ow to do th’ knot work ye wanted. An’ those should be on the way soon. We’ll be expectin’ th’ engagement ring in a few days after we get back from the states. Th’ pictures looked gorgeous of it. I can’na wait t’ see it in person. Sean looked at Annie with admiration. Sean: Damn, ye didn'na let grass grow under yer feet, on this one. Then again tha’ shouldn't surprise me I guess, ye ‘ave been waiting fer this all yer life. Annie: Migh, ‘ave been waiting fer this all me life, bu’ this isn'na fer me. This is fer ye, Sean Michael McClancy. I am tryin’ to take th’ stress off o’ ye as much as possible. Sean reached his arm up and put it around Annie. Once gain, the tears were welling in her eyes. Sean: I know tha’ this year ‘asn'na been th’ easiest fer ye, bu’ I wan’na thank ye fer stickin’ with me. I know when ye came back into me life, I figured ye were there t’ destroy it. But as of late, I would ‘ave never been able t’ get this stuff done. But, now I ‘ave t’ thank ye fer ye being at ring side fer me, an’ doing all this ‘ard work. Giving me an’ me sister a rest from th’ stress of the research fer each match an’ keeping me up to date on Meab. Ye ‘ave really shown me tha’ ye ‘ave changed. Annie placed her head on his shoulder and smiled. Annie: Ye know ye need to get your mind back on this match, an’ off o’ all this other stuff. Remember it will come in time. All the pieces are set an’ time of the trap to be sprung. She is never going to know wha’ ‘it ‘er, when she finds ou’ the Courts finally ga’ wha’ they wanted. Sean: No, she won’t. But I wouldn’t have it any other way. Fade to green.
