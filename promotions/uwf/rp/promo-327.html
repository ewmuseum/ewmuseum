UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #327

climbing the ladder again

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder sitting on a dock at the lake that is below his home. He is wearing black shorts and a red Prime Alliance t-shirt holding a fishing pole. On his right is a tackle box and on his left is an icechest full of beer. The cameraman focuses on him for a few seconds before Ryder turns and notices him. ) Ryder: What's up dawg? Didn't know ya'll was comin up so early. You can go ahead and record, because there ain't no way i'm gonna stop fishin just to shoot a damn promo. I can promo and fish at the same time, cause i'm just that good. * Ryder begins to reel his line in * Ryder: I got this week's card in the mail and I gotta tell ya it was pretty shitty. I mean you got the greatest team to ever lace em up in a match with a team who shouldn't even buy the laces. Malcolm Kennedy and Ruff Ryder fightin some jackass whose name I can't even remember and a can of bug spray? This is horseshit and I got a pretty damn good idea who is behind it all. * Ryder gets his line in, checks his bait and casts out again * Ryder: Our brand new comissioner is behind all this, because he knows that given the chance me and Malcolm would kick five kinds of shit out of him and his little boy Ezra. We would dominate those two and take their precious titles. Let me tell you all out there something, Mike Montgomery got it wrong, because the Prime Alliance will be back in power soon enough. We do it in the ring though, we don't need to sue anyone or make ourselves bullshit positions, because that's the easy way to do it. No we do our business in between the ropes, and anyone that opposes us gets a swift kick in the ass and they learn very quickly just who is the boss. Mike Montgomery knows who the boss is and that's why he won't ever step into the ring with me again. I don't blame him though, because if I wasn't me i'd hate to have to face me. Mike Montgomery suffers from the disease pussyitis though, which is a fear of a challenge. I am the greatest challenge he'd ever face so it just makes sense that he would duck me. * Ryder reels his line in again * Ryder: As far as Digital Mortality and Moraless are concerned i'd like to say welcome back to my old homie Digi. It's good to see you again old man, and you look alot better than when you last left. Don't think you can just leapfrog the Prime Alliance in this little power struggle here, because I ain't about to let that happen. As for you Moraless, i've let you stay alive on more than one occasion, because like Digital, I think you got somethin goin, but if you step in my way i'll stomp you like roach. * Ryder checks his bait once again and then casts back out * Ryder: The Prime Alliance is the best in the business and just because we've been on a little bit of a slump as of late doesn't mean that we should be written off as something that is dead and gone. We always bounce back and Raid and whoever the hell his partner is better get out of the way. We are gonna walk all over both of you, and if you got a problem with it, then just try and stop us. You can't stop us...you can only hope to contain us. Ryder and Kennedy rule the wrestling world. Stand back and watch us run wild. ( Ryder laughs to himself as he pulls a beer out of the icechest and cracks it open. He begins to guzzle the beer as the scene fades out and comes to an end. )
