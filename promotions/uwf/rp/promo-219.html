UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #219

Dissapointment

12/2/2004

Joshua Dunn — UWF Complex

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Perhaps we’re witnessing the final end of an era, perhaps we’re witnessing the first stage to becoming a recluse, Or maybe we’re witnessing sheer laziness, which ever way you view it, Seymour Almasy has not let our a single promotion to the Match that he arranged. Mount Gagazet Death match II seems to be second place to something in Seymour’s life at the moment, but what ever it is that’s keeping him away from the prying eyes of the UWF camera’s, it’s not the same thing keeping Dunn away. Already seen signing autographs for waiting fans, and at other UWF public events, Dunn has been busy as of late, but who could blame him, possibly the first time he has been put up against a world Champion, and it’s for a belt that he has, the World Championship title no where to be seen. Although the IC title is nothing to be sniffed at, you have to wonder what has been keeping the man in the UWF who holds a win over almost every other wrestler in the UWF away from the chase towards the most sought after prize in wrestling. Already set a record for the longest title reign in UWF history, what is left for Dunn as the IC champion? Could the unique Stability be better used somewhere else? Or are the management of the UWF keeping their greatest asset away from the world title for a reason? We will never know, until the story leaks to the papers] [The Scene Opens in the UWF Complex, in the backstage area, unlike in several other Joshua Dunn promotions, it does not open outside his dressing room, rather at, what looks like a deserted waiting area. There is a single figure visible though, this man sitting on a bench with his back to the camera and wearing a black leather jacket and black denim jeans. Almost instantly recognisable as the IC Champion Joshua Dunn. The camera approaches Dunn silently, as if creeping up on him, closer it gets and almost a foot behind Dunn, he speaks] JD: I am aware your there, I seem to be sought after nowadays. By floundering psychopaths, Disgraced World Champions, hell I’m sure even the PWW are looking for me now. [The camera moves round and looks to face him. We can see that he’s also wearing a red T-shirt and is fiddling with something with his hand near his neck, it’s the gold chain, and he’s rubbing the communist emblem that hangs off the bottom of it. Even though the camera is now in front of him, he still doesn’t look it straight in the lens as he usually does, he just stares at a fixed point quite a distance away. Still holding the emblem Dunn continues to talk] JD: So, not long until the Clash now, seems a shame really, I thought Seymour would have made more of an appearance for the match he requested, maybe he’s gotten cold feet, maybe he’s just thought it’s not worth bothering about me, possibly the UWF’s most underrated title holder ever. Ruff Ryder’s become schizophrenic and a recluse, and as his promo’s lead to believe, a mass murderer too. The Prime Alliance in shatters, the main reason for me pulling the Players Club back from the Brink. Is there any real point in staying as my main rivals have disappeared into obscurity and insanity? I made it perfectly aware the main reason for my staying in the stable was to stop the monopolising of matches by the Prime Alliance. However the Players Club has replaced the Prime Alliance, I still have to make sure it doesn’t become the Prime Alliance. Therefore I remain. [Dunn snorts a laugh through his nose, a motion like an extremely soft sneeze] JD: Ruff Ryder has already issued a Challenge to me for another match, an ego that’s almost causing the man to implode. Seymour can’t be found for love nor money. It’s like the man disappeared after arranging the match. I have only one thing to say to that sort of cowardice. It’s pathetic. It’s worse than being a fraud, because I know that Seymour has that talent, and I’m also the only person standing between him and the Grand Slam. There’s very little between myself and the Grand Slam I have to add, just a demonic Clown and soon, at least one of my own stable mates. But why haven’t I received a World Title shot yet? I’ve proved myself time and time again against whomever the UWF management throw against me. [His hand grips the emblem more tightly now, the hand raised up slightly, a deep breath taken in and held for a moment] JD: Probably because of sheer racism or because of politics, Communists cannot be seen to be better than those that follow democracy. [The hand comes up further, very slowly, a glazed look crossed Dunn’s eyes almost transfixed] JD: I can’t hold onto the past forever, The Curtain is well and truly dead, I am all that remains of a shattered Dynasty, maybe… just maybe… It’s time to move on… [Dunn’s hand pulls away from the rest of his body, the chain, therefore taken with it, snapping it at the rear, removing it from his neck in a rather forceful manner, the last of the greatest Stable in UWF History, finally seeing his own path, and his own past. He gets up inhaling another large sigh and leaves out of camera view, the camera zooms in on the chain that used to be around Dunn’s neck but is now lying on the floor somewhere in the UWF Complex, and on that image, the scene fades to black, a twinkle of light shining briefly for a moment on the emblem of communism before complete Darkness consumes] The Curtain is finally dead
