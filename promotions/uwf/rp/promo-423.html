Almost THERE...CORPORAL STYLE!

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Page 1 (What a wonderful week it has been. The UWF is still in shock that the Corporal has returned to their federation to beat down wrestlers that have no business doing anything except possibly picking up trash after a house show has taken place. But alas, that is what happens when a federation allows soft children to do a tough mans job. You can not hold that against the little men in this federation for that can you? After all, the days of the Youngblood's are over... are they? Of course, we all know that the Youngblood name is eternally etched in the minds of everyone in the world of professional wrestling. Or at least anyone who is ANYONE in professional wrestling. But it is clear that the YB stable has long since separated, and with their separation, a new breed of dimwits have entered this sport and made a mockery of the squared circle. That is... UNTIL NOW! The days of that are closely coming to an end I must add, as you are being privileged to witness a single man return to the world of professional wrestling  to rebuild this federation to the next level of greatness. Not in technical wizardry in moves; not in high degrees of acrobatical skills; and not from risky death defying moves from the tops of arenas. Those types of wrestlers only bring in more circus freaks to join the already overly populated jobber corps of the wrestling world. No, the man you are about to witness brings a simple strategy of brutally bludgeoning his opponents for the sheer pleasure of hearing them cry for submission. He lives to watch ambulances carry off those who stand in his way. He does not care whether you love him, like him, wish to be him, or even care about his well being. All he cares about is making a negative impact on his enemies lives. That is enough for the Corporal of Punishment. He simply wants to hurt people. It is that simple human characteristic that causes thousands upon thousands of men and women to rush into an arena to cheer on a man as brutal as the Corporal of Punishment. And it is that very same reason why you are pathetically craving to go on further with this promo. So get your mothers nipple out of your mouth and enjoy the ride. The CORPORAL HAS NOW ENTERED THE BUILDING...) CORPORAL STYLE! (The medical professionals of this quaint little town have already come and gone. The ambulance has taken the pencil necked real estate agent to a more positive atmosphere, namely the emergency room. And the gathering of neighbors in front of the Corporals small dwelling has finally dispersed after he threw a large canister of tear gas in their direction. The elderly woman who was knocked unconscious after getting hit in the head with the canister, has also been assisted to wherever it is she came from. The Corporal however is enjoying a few Polaroid pictures of the mayhem he caused when he threw the canister his new neighbors gathering. It is clear that Cpl. Punishment has enjoyed his recent antics because as he sits with a freshly brewed cup of coffee in one hand, and several miscellaneous photographs of his neighbors showing outrage and fear in the other hand, he can not stop grinning and giggling.) Corporal: Man that old bag sure can take a hit. Look at her shaking like that on the ground. That is good stuff. (The Corporal begins to examine a second photo closely.) Corporal: Why is that man biting his tongue? You just can not take good pictures any more with these Polaroid cameras. At least not when someone is convulsing on the ground like that. I bet that man is probably wishing right now that he stayed in his room and gazed through the key hole of his grand mothers bedroom as she changed her moo-moo. He would have least been able to hold his tongue out for a few more years. The way he looks in this photo is that he will never be able to stick anything out of that mouth again. Corporal: I am going to have to invest in a digital camera. (The Corporal tosses the photos onto the ground and stands up. Throwing his java out on the lawn, the Corporal takes the now empty cup, and takes it into the kitchen. He begins to quickly wash the cup in the sink, and places the cup in an empty cupboard.) Corporal: Ah, what to do next? It's Saturday. The neighbors are gone, and I have yet to make it to the headquarters of the UWF. I guess that will be the next step. I could let all those candy suck wrestling armatures remain in their secure status for another few days. But then when I do finally show up, one or two of the jobbers might have already left. And I do not want that. If they are to leave, I want it to be solely by my hands and not by their own free will. (The Corporal walks into the living room of the small home, and after picking up a camouflaged laptop computer, he gains access to the internet to look at some of the so called competition of the UWF.) Corporal: Well let us look at the current talent this federation has.... Corporal: Adien Ryder... A queer little college boy from the GWF. Where is or WAS the GWF? What could that even stand for? Let me see hmmmmm. The Groinal Wart Fondle Federation. Who knows! Let me read on... THIS IDIOT HAS YET TO WIN A MATCH !! He if FREAKING ZERO and FOUR in singles competition! This guy is truly QUEER! I first thought that he was queer as in odd, but he is REALLY QUEER QUEER! WHY am I even looking at this idiot. (He shakes his head and quickly clicks the mouse button to move on.) Corporal: BeezleBozo... Another Gurgles want a be! What is up with clowns in wrestling. I could probably get him a second job hiding in a barrel for bulls to urinate on. The man can not seem to find any friends with a name that starts with a letter past 'B' He has BLAZE, and that is about it... No wonder he is a Bozo of Beezles. Can anyone tell me what a Beezle is? Nevermind! (The Corporal clicks the mouse and moves on with his search.) Corporal: DEAR LORD! ANOTHER LUCHADOR! You would think these guys would stay in their Mexican mud huts instead of coming to a federation where I am going to be. And what does EL CHUPACABRA even mean! The guy needs to get his green card and learn to wrestle before he walks into any public forum and make any comment. I would repeat some of the things the idiot has said, but I can not even understand what they immigrant says out loud. PLEASE LET ME SEE SOME TALENT!!! Move on! (The Corporal begins to go through the names quicker as he becomes bored with the prospects he has seen thus far.) Corporal: You have got to be kidding me. Bondage Sex fiend? Another flaming homosexual in wrestling tights. A Bum Boozler copy. What a shame. He's a title holder... or should I say She is a title holder. You know that is what the problem is with the world today. Women's rights. We let them burn their bra's and now they et to do a man's job. That is just sad. I just do not know if I could hit a woman... yes I could. Never mind. Corporal: It is clear that this roster is full of jokes and jobbers. I just need to jump straight ionto the so called leaders of this federation... Like the champions. (He clicks on the various belt icons to see who the UWF considers as their top money earners.) Corporal: NICK PERSIA!!!! What in the HELL is a NICK PERSIA even being allowed to do anything except selling floor rugs at a TARGET store! This is pathetic! It is just pathetic! *I hope it gets better! PLEASE GOD! PLEASE! (The Corporal is beginning to get frustrated as he sees nothing worth commenting on in the roster pool.) Corporal: FINALLY A WRESTLER with a little name sake! Ezra Kazee. I can enjoy humiliating this freak of wrestling. He is nothing but a do-good audience panderer and a fake... AND WHO IS!!! (The Corporals eyes widen in anger as he gazes at the name of Mike Montgomery.) Corporal: You have got to be kidding me! HE IS EVERYWHERE I GO! I just keep beating him down over and over and over and HE KEEPS POPPING UP! What in the freak is this FISH's problem! You would think he would just retire and let the world enjoy real talent for a change!  CANADA! I HATE CANADA!!!! ENOUGH!!!! (He slams the laptops shut and walks out to the front yard where a FOR SALE sign once stood.) Corporal: I CAN NOT TAKE IT ANYMORE! IT IS TIME TO EXECUTE MY RULES ON THIS ENTIRE GOD FORSAKEN FEDERATION! I JUST HOPE TO GOD THAT THERE IS SOMEONE WORTHY TO GET BEAT DOWN FOR THE FURTHER enforcement OF MARTIAL LAW IN THIS FEDERATION! (He falls to his knees and with fists clutched, he looks up in the sky.) Corporal: PLEASE DEAR GOD! SEND ME SOMEONE OR SOMETHING THAT CAN CLEAN THIS FEDERATION UP WITH REAL TALENT! NOT WATERED DOWN GIMMICK FOLLOWERS! PLEASE!
