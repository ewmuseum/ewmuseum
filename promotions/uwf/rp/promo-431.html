UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #431

Things Just Keep Getting Better

29 May 2004

Adien Pryde — Supremecy Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside of the arena that held Supremacy. Various fans and wrestlers can be seen leaving. A black SUV pulls up with the plates ANARCHY on it. Pryde and Joanna come out of the arena headed for the SUV.] Camera Man : Pryde, Joanna, can I get a word with you? [Pryde rolls his eyes.] Pryde : I guess, make it quick, I have a party to go to. CM : Congratulations on you big win tonight, where do you go from here? Pryde : The big congrats should go to Raid, he brought the gold home tonight. I told everyone to not underestimate me and I proved tonight that I can play with the big boys and I will continue to show the UWF that I will be a force to be reckoned with, starting with the Communist. I told him that I am coming after his TV Title and I am going to take it at Underground. CM : Going after the Communist and the Iron Curtain though, isn’t that to much. Pryde : To the Chaos Society, nothing is to much. The Communist is nothing more seven foot idiot and I will show the world that. CM : Joanna, will Reo Dai be ready for next Sunday and Stable Wars. Our last update Pryde said 4-8 weeks. Stable Wars will be only 2 weeks after this injury, there is no way he will be ready to compete. Joanna : Dai will be there, he is determined to be a part of the Wars and there is no holding him back. We will have 4 members entered in and we will win the whole thing. CM : What about Draco Paladine? No one has seen or heard from him. Is he still a part of Chaos Society. Joanna : Drac has been getting thins together for his wedding which is a week after Stable Wars. He will be there and he will be a big part of our win. The other stables know very little of our big man and that will be our wild card. [Pryde opens the back door of the SUV for Joanna.] Pryde : That is enough for now. We’ll call you in a few days to give a more formal interview. No get out of my face. [Pryde closes the door for Joanna and gets in the front seat. Camera fades to black as it speeds away.]
