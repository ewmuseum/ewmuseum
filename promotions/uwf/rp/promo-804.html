UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #804

Via Chrono Purifico: Time Purifies (I)

12/2 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Varying

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the UWF Headquarters in Chicago, Illinois, various interns are doing their various jobs. There is the Monkey Intern, who’s job it is to take care of the pension plans for the various primates who have been on the roster. There is also the Phone Complaint Intern, who takes the approximately 4,196 complaints via telephone that each edition of Supremacy generates. Most important to us on this day, however, is the Mail Intern, who is bringing in the day’s mail. He is not surprised to see many tapes among the letters. After all, UWF somehow manages to attract applications from around the world. The intern IS, however, surprised to find tapes sent from one Seymour Almasy. Mail Intern: Wow! Usually I just get stupid bills from places like Peoria, Illinois and a bunch of subpoenas! This is cool! The intern pulls the set of tapes from the basket of mail. There are three of them, with a note attached. Mail Intern: “Put these on the UWF promo channel, and make sure that Tsunami Oni sees them. If either of these requirements are not met, I will decapitate you with my Divine Blade Imperial and use your severed head as a weapon in my World Title Match at FANdemonium. Love and Kisses, Seymour Almasy. P.S. If the mail intern is a man, replace “Love and Kisses” with “Fuck Off and Die”. Gulping, the Mail Intern quickly runs with the tapes across the hall to the television area, where, appropriately enough, the Television Intern sits, doing her hair while watching a replay of Supremacy. Mail Intern: You’ve got to help me, Tessa! Seymour Almasy threatened to kill me if these tapes weren’t played on the promo channel! To the Mail Intern’s shock, his television counterpart only giggles. Television Intern: That Seymour’s such a kidder! He threatened to do the same to me when I almost erased his first World Title win from the UWF archives when I wanted to tape Desperate Housewives last week, but he was only joking. Funny guy, he is. The intern breathes a temporary sigh of relief, until from another room comes the Final Fantasy himself, Seymour Almasy, clad in full battle regalia. Seymour Almasy: Tessa! Hi! How’ve you been! Television Intern: Great, Seymour! Yourself? Seymour Almasy: Oh, I’m fine. Just waiting for the tapes that I sent here to be played. I swear, if that Mail Intern doesn’t get them to you, I’ll cut his or her head off with my nice sharp pointy sword and use his or her severed head to bludgeon Oni at the PPV. Any idea where he or she is? Wasting no time, the intern drops the tapes on the desk behind Tessa, the TV intern, and dashes back to the mailroom, no doubt to place a frantic call to the Oni residence. Seymour shrugs, before handing the tapes to her himself. Seymour Almasy: Geez, I wonder what got into that guy? Shrugging, Tessa laughs, sliding one of the tapes into the complicated equipment that will beam Seymour’s footage all over the country, NAY, the world, NAY, THE UNIVERSE~! Seymour Almasy: Shall I make us some popcorn? Nodding, the Television Intern points Seymour in the direction of the small microwave in the back corner of the room. Almasy happily goes off, locating a bag of popcorn, and placing it in the heating device. He looks calm, collected, and nothing like he is about to have the biggest match of his life. Television Intern: You seem so, well, not homicidal. I thought you’d be trying to figure out ways to kill Tsunami Oni right about now. Seymour Almasy: Silly Tessa, that’s what these tapes are for. I got all of the yelling and screaming and threatening to stab Oni with the Divine Blade Imperial out of the way already. This way, I can relax and train without getting too mad at the stupid cheating son of a bitch that is Tsunami Oni. Now hit the play button and go over to the couch, the popcorn’s done. The Final Fantasy quickly takes the popcorn out of the microwave, and snuggles up with the pretty intern on the inexplicably placed couch. Tessa reaches out with her universal remote, and pushes the play button once, causing the first of Seymour’s tapes to be seen in homes all over the universe… (???) The image depicted on the screen is an odd one. Almost total blackness pervades the site, which looks something like an old stage. Coming from the left side of this stage are three strobe lights, shooting blue pillars of light into the air. Finally, a strobe light illuminates the stage. Standing there is, of course, Seymour Almasy. Seymour Almasy: Ah…welcome, Tsunami Oni. Welcome…to the End of Time. He slowly runs a hand through his long red hair, eyes focusing straight ahead of him, his other hand extending out to show the scene. Seymour Almasy: We’ve been through so much, haven’t we? We’ve only met in the ring against each other twice…only been one on one once, and yet we’re the biggest rivalry the UWF has to offer. Odd, isn’t it? And to think, Tsunami…that this almost didn’t happen…that after Gagazet, we could have gone on to become the most dominant TEAM the UWF would ever see. But that’s not what happened, now is it? Somewhat dramatically, Seymour shakes his head no. Seymour Almasy: But it could have been, Tsunami. It could have been. Our story is an interesting one. Equal parts past, present, and future. Time flows like a river, Tsunami. History repeats itself. Allow me to show you… With that, Seymour steps forward, leaping into the leftmost blue pillar. Suddenly, the camera shakes, and when it stops, the scene has changed entirely. (September 8th, 2002. “Mount Gagazet”, Vermont, USA) The familiar peak of Mount Gagazet appears before the eyes of thousands. The mountain top is covered with fluffy, white snow, almost untouched by humans. Snow continues to fall, thickening the blanket on the ground. Standing atop the peak, a replica of the UWF’s Television Championship in hand, is Seymour Almasy. Seymour Almasy: This is where it all began, really. After the PETA attacks, the kidnapping of Aerith, it all came to a head here on Mount Gagazet, in the first ever Mount Gagazet Deathmatch. You remember, don’t you? This is the place where you suffered your first one on one defeat in the UWF. And more infuriating for you…I did it the way you would have done it. By crook. He smiles, the memories still clear in his mind. Seymour Almasy: I’m standing on the exact spot where it happened. Where Aerith in her Ronso costume walked by and nailed you in the back with that spear. But considering that you had Shiatsu aid you with the helicopter weapon dump, I’d consider it a fitting end for you and your treachery. When all was said and done, I raised this championship overhead for the first time, the first of thirteen championships that I would hold in the Universal Wrestling Federation. Seymour shakes his head, almost unbelieving of his own past. Seymour Almasy: How naïve I was back then. I fought for good…I fought for Aerith, and I fought to eradicate the evil that was you from the universe. A lot of good that did me. For, while I was Television Champion, you were ascending the ladder. You became the Supreme Master of the Seven Continents, while I was fighting for the Shoot Wrestling Title. You were always one step ahead of me. And finally, I gave in. If I couldn’t beat you, I might as well join you. A cackle comes from Almasy’s lips, not the proud cackle of Tsunami Oni, but a mocking cackle. Seymour Almasy: The Empire was born. We annihilated the Legends, only to have them run, giving us a countout victory. We could have ran that federation, dominated it like no other two men ever. But then…I left, Tsunami Oni. And do you know WHY I left? Of course you don’t. No one does. Not even my ex-wife. And hell, to be honest, not even me. He shrugged absent-mindedly. Honestly, the whole incident is a blank to him. Seymour Almasy: Why I left is unimportant. The fact is that I left. And I didn’t return for six months. And when I came back…everything had changed. Everything. Abruptly, the scene fades…and changes. (UWF Legacies PPV, 2003) The first thing visible is a clip of the end of this particular PPV’s TV Title match. Seymour Almasy stands over the Trailer Park Luchador, Kenny Mercury’s Spell Binder slapped on the Masked Monkey, until he submits. Once that fades, the image of Seymour re-appears. This time, he is in a wrestling ring, but the title over his shoulder is the same. Seymour Almasy: This was the night where it all changed, didn’t it Tsunami? I made my presence felt once more in the UWF, easily capturing the TV Title. But you..you lost the World Title. Without even being involved in the decision. You lost your cackle, and you left. Left me in charge of N.O.G.O.O.D. That lasted a long time, didn’t it, you selfish bastard. I blame YOU for the fact that I’m not a Stable Wars champion. I may hate you, may despise you more than anyone on this Earth, but I recognize your skill. Were you there alongside myself and the Local NVA Company, the nightmare that was the Wimp and Chimp Connection Stroke Nine Mach something never would have happened. I mean, dear God, DJ COOL IS A STABLE WARS CHAMPION! I couldn’t keep the rice farmers in line by myself! The bad memories continue to pour through his head. The mighty N.O.G.O.O.D. losing to the WACC. WACC winning Stable Wars. The damned chimpanzee becoming World Champion. Seymour Almasy: And then, it was up to me to rid the UWF of the Evil Ape Menace. I triumphed, but you weren’t there. It was a hollow victory. I was the best in the UWF…a UWF without Tsunami Oni. Even when we teamed together, even when we were on good terms, I wanted to face you. Wanted to meet you in open combat. Why? (The End of Time) The ring disappears, leaving Seymour in the black End of Time once more. Again, the spotlight shines on him. Seymour Almasy: Because no matter what I have accomplished, no matter who I defeat, I still hear the voices. The people who don’t think I’m the best the UWF has ever produced. The people that don’t think I can beat you. The fans who wanted then, and want now to damn me to second best. And the most annoying part is, they’ve got justification. I’d take you back to the Cruiserweight Tournament finals, but I wouldn’t give you that satisfaction. We’re 1-1, each with excuses in our loss. I lost to you, but I had taken a beating from Montgomery and Phoenix, the other two men in our match. You lost to me, but were never pinned or made to submit. Perhaps it’s ironic that our match won’t end in pinfall or submission. But it will end decisively. An apprehensive look appears on Seymour’s face. The FANdemonium Match that he and Oni are to do battle in is incredibly dangerous, a battle in which fans may interfere, and the loser is to be hurtled down a hole in the mat to the fans below. Seymour Almasy: One of us will walk out of FANdemonium as the World Champion. One of us may not walk out. The past, the video game warrior that I once was, respects you for your competitiveness…that is all, for now. (UWF Headquarters) Television Intern: Wow… Seymour Almasy: Put in the second tape. There is much more that Oni must know before his impending destruction. Loud footsteps can suddenly be heard. Before Seymour can ask what the hell is going on, the Mail Intern charges into the room, and throws himself at Seymour’s feet. Mail Intern: PLEASE DON’T KILL ME!!! Seymour looks down disgustedly at the groveling man, grabbing him by his collar, and yanking him to his feet. Seymour Almasy: What is it? Mail Intern: I-I tried to call Tsunami. No one’s home at his house. Not Tsunami, not Shiatsu, not Bizarro Oni, not even those three creepy little kids that he has go around with him sometimes. Seymour Almasy: Did you leave him a message then? Mail Intern: Yes. But, that’s not the important part. Seymour…Tsunami Oni’s dead. Silence. Seymour’s eyes grow as wide as saucers, an utterly shocked expression on his face. Seymour Almasy: How? How could this happen?! Mail Intern: From the e-mail that Shiatsu sent us, it was a freak, tragic hot tub and vibrator accident… Seymour Almasy: SON OF A BITCH! Despite his slight size, Seymour is powerful enough to hurl the Mail Intern into a nearby wall, cracking it, as well as the intern’s back. Tessa, the TV Intern, cowers on the couch, as Seymour continues to take out his anger. Seymour Almasy: THIS IS NOT HAPPENING TO ME! THAT FUCKING ASSHOLE CAN”T DIE! NOT NOW! NOT WHEN I’M SO CLOSE! NOT WHEN I’M FINALLY GOING TO BEAT HIM! Rather than attack anything else in the studio, Seymour slumps to his knees. Choked sobs, and soon full fledged tears, begin to come from the Final Fantasy. Seymour Almasy: I…gave up everything. The respect of the fans. The love of my life. All for the chance to defeat Tsunami Oni, so I could make my life right once again…and he dies a few days before he can get his just desserts. WHY, YEVON?! WHY MUST YOU TORMENT ME LIKE THIS! Unsheathing the mighty Divine Blade Imperial, Seymour wildly whips the sword around overhead, prompting Tessa to run for a corner. The long red hair of the Final Fantasy flows behind him, as a look of sheer rage appears on his face. Turning to the poor Television Intern, he speaks in a commanding voice. Seymour Almasy: PLAY THE OTHER TWO TAPES! NOW! Understandably terrified, the intern scampers to do as she is told. Now, the studio begins to feel Seymour’s wrath, as a table is kicked over, sending papers flying. The table is then picked up, and thrown into the UWF’s tape archive, causing “The Best of WizHard” to come flying off the shelf, and shatter into pieces on the floor. Seymour Almasy: THEY WILL PAY FOR THIS! Somehow, the poor intern manages to summon up the courage to ask a question. Television Intern: W-w-who’s they? Seymour turns, glaring at her with fierce, enraged eyes. Seymour Almasy: YOU! TSUNAMI ONI! SHIATSU! THE UWF! EVERYONE! Wild-eyed, Seymour races screaming from the room, taking off down the corridors of the UWF HQ building, swinging his sword and scaring the living daylights out of employees. Seymour Almasy: THIS CANNOT BE! Finally reaching the front lobby, Seymour picks up a chair, used by those waiting for appointments, and hurls it through the large plate glass window. Almost immediately, a security alarm begins to ring, and the staff begin to flee en masse, hoping that the arrival of the police will be sooner than later. Unfortunately, one of the fleeing staff members drops a lighter on the way out. Picking it up gleefully, Seymour ignites the flame, and then casually drops the lighter on another upholstered chair. It almost immediately catches fire, and the smoke sets off the fire alarm, now sounding in tune with the security alarm. Seymour Almasy: I…have lost everything. Universal Wrestling Federation, NOW YOU SHALL KNOW MY SUFFERING! Before long, this building will be reduced to ash, cinders as black as my soul is now. The chair is nearly consumed by fire, and the flames spread along the carpet. A steady stream of employees continue to pour from the building, including the UWF Gorilla and the Mail and Television Intern. The latter carries the final two tapes of Seymour, preserving them for evidence, since it does seem to be quite obvious that Seymour’s gone off the deep end. He too, now leaves the building, walking away coldly from the employees who desperately wait and hope for emergency personnel to arrive to save their building. Seymour Almasy: Laura…I gave up the most important thing in my life in order to settle accounts with a dead man. She would never take me back. Sure, I will win the World Title at FANdemonium by default…BUT THOSE DAMNED CRITICS WILL STILL BE THERE! AND NOW, I CAN’T PROVE MYSELF THE BETTER MAN! The sirens of police cars, fire trucks, and ambulances can be heard in the distance. Even enraged as he is, Seymour knows that this is his time to go. Seymour Almasy: There is nothing left for me now. Nothing…except to punish Tsunami Oni for the cruelest thing he could have ever done to me…die. I will find where his body is being kept. And then, I will dismember him. I will chop him up in little pieces and scatter them all over the world, so that Shiatsu will never find them. I will ruin the lives of everyone that ever mattered to him. I will make it so that anyone who ever met Tsunami wishes that they NEVER EXISTED! Perhaps I will go after his father. Or his kid friends. Or Shiatsu. It matters not… As the first police car pulls up, Seymour takes off, running as fast as he can. With all of the arriving officers focused on the fire, none of them see Seymour flee the scene. One of the officers approaches the obviously frazzled Television Intern. Officer: Do you know who did this? Television Intern: Yes! It was Seymour Almasy! Officer: Come with me, please. Same for anyone else who saw anything in relation to this incident. We need to gather as much information as possible. As the police officers herd together the UWF employees, the fire trucks arrive. Working quickly, the brave firemen manage to douse the blaze. The employees let out a cheer. The only unhappy figure in this drama is, of course, Seymour himself, watching from around the corner in another building. Seymour Almasy: They’ll be after me soon. I don’t have much time… With that, Seymour continued to run amongst the streets of Chicago. His eventual destination? California. And more specifically, wherever the hell the body of Tsunami Oni was.
