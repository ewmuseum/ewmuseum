UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #718

Killer Dude Gets A Pep Talk

25 Sep 2004

Killer Dude — Killer Dude's Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in a very small, cluttered apartment. Books and magazines are scattered, stacked in large piles on the floor. A couple of empty dinner trays sit on a dining room table as well as a bowl of fruit that looks to be way past it's prime. As you can see, or not, the room is quite dark, with the small exception of a narrow column of light streaming in through the partially opened window, indicating that the sun is going down. The camera follows the stream of light across the room until it suddenly stops in its trajectory, reflecting off one of Killer Dude's THC Title plaques lying on a small corner table. Next to the table is a guitar and half a can of Budweiser beer. The camera suddenly shifts once again to reveal one half of the New Black Star Revolution, Killer Dude, sitting in his recliner. Instead of his normal attire, he is dressed in a pair of flannel pajamas, with the New Black Star Revolution logo plastered everywhere on the pants. He also has a neck brace on. He is starring at a silver photo frame) Killer Dude: Oh Kara, I miss you so much. Why did you leave me? I wish you were here right now so I could tell you all about what happened at Supremacy. But, since you aren't here, I will talk to the photo that we had taken of each other at the 2003 Regal Rumble. (Killer Dude stops talking for a second and takes a sip of the Budweiser) Killer Dude: If you were watching Supremacy, you would have seen the injustices done to me by Nick Persia, Misfit, and Moraless. They sneak attacked us while we were in the ring, and they tried to beat us down. Nick obviously couldn't pass the test of time as we kicked his ass, but then Misfit and Moraless teamed up on me, and knocked me out cold. OUT COLD DAMN IT!!!!!!!!! (Killer Dude takes another sip of the Bud) Killer Dude: You know, in my first match back, at the Regal Rumble, Nick Persia eliminated me. I'll admit it. I used to admire him while I was sitting at home, rehabilitating my knee, but after that, I guess you can say that I haven't really cared for him much since then. But I didn't think that he'd stoop that low. It seems that Nick Persia is scared of me. He got 2 other men to do the dirty work for him? That's PATHETIC! Killer Dude: It all started at last week's Universal, the beginning of the New Black Star Revolution. The damage that we did to Misfit, I'll tell you, watching the tape, it was sickening how badly we kicked his ass. I was growing confident that all of my troubles were in the past, and that I wouldn't get punked out any more. But now, Nick Persia wants to challenge ME to a match at Supremacy? He thinks that he actually has a chance in hell of beating me? (Killer Dude puts the can of Budweiser down and grabs the guitar) Killer Dude: He can't! I won't let him! The New Black Star Revolution loses to NOBODY! We must remain strong! Hell, we are strong! He can't beat me! Nobody can beat me! Could he beat me? Damn it to hell. Maybe some guitar playing will snap me out of my funk? (Killer Dude grabs the guitar and begins to play 'Sold Me' by Seether. Before he gets to the first verse, he stops and grabs at his neck. He picks up the photo frame once again) Killer Dude: Son of a bitch! God damn Neck! Damn you to hell Nick Persia! That Dutch Destroyer I took on the outside at Supremacy was pretty brutal. Are you happy now, Persia? You almost made me a fucking vegetable. Now, I'm just gonna have to return the favor, you punk ass bitch. I am a part of the New Black Star Revolution. We always overcome adversity. You are the first, but you shall not be the last of the obstacles that we overcome. I will rise to the challenge of you, Persia. I.... (Killer Dude's speech is interrupted by someone knocking on the door) Killer Dude: Go Away! Can't you see I'm delivering a speech? (Instead of the person going away, the door bursts open and in steps Kenny Mercury, wearing sun glasses, with a 'Viva La Black Star Revolution' shirt on. His hair is straggly, and he looks like he hasn't groomed himself in days) Kenny Mercury: Have you been playing pocket pool again? Killer Dude: What gives you that idea? Kenny Mercury: Lard-ass! So, you probably want to know, what's the deal with me barging in, right? Killer Dude: I guess I would like that explained. Kenny Mercury: Have you seen the booking sheet for Supremacy? Killer Dude. Yeah man, I know. I've decided that I'm not gonna take the match. There's no fucking way in hell that I'm taking that match. (Killer Dude gets up and starts pacing between the door and the recliner. Being a very small, messy apartment building, that's two steps both ways, but Killer Dude becomes tired very quickly) Killer Dude: I mean, I don't know what Hayes was smoking when he made that match. Me vs Nick Persia? He's the punk ass bitch who tried to end my career on Supremacy. Giving me the Dutch Destroyer on the concrete floor. He almost broke my friggin' neck. And now, Hayes wants me to face him one on one? I'm not doing it. Kenny Mercury: Well, Killer, I've been thinking. What would it say about the New Black Star Revolution if you didn't show up for the match at Supremacy? We are the New Black Star Revolution, damn it! Killer Dude: That's true, Kenny, but knowing Nick Persia, he's probably got Misfit and Moraless right behind him, and they will use any weakness on our team to their full advantage. And MY GOD DAMN NECK IS THE WEAKNESS DAMN IT! So it's basically me against one, possibly three, rabid fucking dogs. Kenny Mercury: And you don't think you can handle that? (Killer Dude pulls on the collar of his jacket and grins back at Mercury) Killer Dude: Well, Kenny, I'm sure I can handle it. After all, I am the fucking Hardcore Legend! Kenny Mercury: So, what exactly is the problem? You go out there and make the New Black Star Revolution proud! Represent our name with honor! Don't let me down! Killer Dude: God damn it, you're right! I'm going to go to the gym and get in some extra training before tomorrow's show! I'm going to represent the NBSR as only Killer Dude can! With much less of the technical, and a lot more of the hardcore, baby! Kenny Mercury: Great! Now, before I go, can I use your bathroom? Killer Dude: Sorry man, can't let you do it. Remember the last time you used the bathroom. I had to put a Keep Clear sign on the bathroom, and I had to piss and shit out of the window! Kenny Mercury: What the hell? Killer Dude: Oh, forget it. Viva La Black Star Revolution, bitch! (The scene gradually fades out as we see Kenny step into the bathroom, and Killer Dude getting ready to go to the gym)
