UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #352

Flashbacks

17 Apr 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival and Pussy/Liquor

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open our scene with a brooding Beelzebozo, resting in his amazingly ugly orange recliner. His fingers are steepled before his face, and he seems lost in thought. Pyretta Blaze enters the room, and Beelzeobozo’s eyes roll up to look at her. Beelzebozo: “Well, have you found the information I requested?” Blaze: “Not as much as you’d care for, I fear, but I think I have enough for now.” Beelzebozo: “I don’t like the sound of that. It sounds suspiciously like…you failed.” Blaze: “Just hear me out, please, and then decide.” Beelzebozo: “Very well. Make your report on this…Gurgles.” Blaze: “Sure thing.” She clears her throat, and then produces a folded-up piece of paper from between her large, lovely breasts. Beelzebozo: “I envy that paper.” She giggles as she unfolds the paper and begins to read. Blaze: “First, a brief rundown of what we already knew. Several wrestlers have referred to you as either a Gurgles wannabe or ripoff, or else have claimed you are indeed Gurgles himself. Educated guesses led us to believe that he was, at one time, a wrestler. Since they only refer to him when speaking of you, we can assume he had a wrestling clown gimmick.” Beelzebozo: “Yes, yes. Get to the point.” Blaze: “From what I have been able to find, Gurgles was apparently at one time employed here in the UWF, although his tenure was short. He was a master submissions wrestler, and a HUGE fan favorite. He held the Hardcore Title, and last year he was apparently entered into the Hall of Fame.” Beelzebozo: “At least I’m being compared to someone worthy. Even though I’d rather they accept me as me.” Blaze: “Well, here’s where it gets odd. He was indeed a clown wrestler, and he had an entourage which consisted of three midgets…” Beelzebozo: “Crap! That reminds me! We still need a full-time midget!” Blaze: “AS I was saying…three midgets, a harlequin, and…a mime.” Beelzebozo sits up a little at this revelation. Beelzebozo: “A mime? He had a mime with him?” Blaze: “Yes.” Beelzebozo: “Well, no wonder people are making the comparisons, superficial as they may be. I mean, that IS an odd coincidence, but really…clowns and mimes are often seen together. And they’re both evil. So of COURSE they’re going to pal around.” Blaze: “I think there’s more to the story of Gurgles than that, but I couldn’t get into those files. I don’t have clearance, or some such. They’re lucky those files are there or they’d be sitting in a burned-out ruin now.” She growls softly. Beelzebozo: “Still, you did good, dear. I thank you.” Blaze: “I do have one last thing.” She pulls off a small card that was taped to her paper and hands it to him. Beelzebozo: “A business card for a bar?” Blaze: “This was stuck behind my wiper when I came out of the building. Read what’s written on the back.” Beelzebozo flips the card over. Beelzebozo: “You might find some answers here. A friend.” Blaze: “So, what do you say? Shall we get ourselves a drink?” Beelzebozo: “You know…I AM feeling a little thirsty.” We cut to our protagonists that evening. They are standing outside of “Pussy/Liquor.” Blaze: “It’s so…charming.” Beelzebozo: “Hey, you’re the one that suggested we come here.” Blaze: “I know, I know.” They go inside. They find (as do we) a dark, smoky place. Hordes of men (and a few women) are gathered around a main stage devoid of all furnishings save a fireman’s pole and a naked woman writhing to the strains of Nickelback’s “Figured You Out.” A few people are standing near a well-stocked bar, enjoying their drinks and the view. Beelzebozo: “Well, that explains the name!” Blaze: “I wonder how hard she had to fight to be the stripper that got that song. I bet all of them wanted it.” Beelzebozo: “Maybe later we can ask them.” Blaze: “We’re here on business.” Beelzebozo: “Yeah yeah.” The two of them approach the bar. Bartender: “Yes, what can…” His smile falters. His eyes grow very large. His jaw drops. Beelzebozo: “A whiskey sour and a straight whiskey.” The man lets loose a little shriek and points at Beelzebozo. Bartender: “What are you doing here!? I thought you freaks left!!” Beelzebozo: “Excuse me?” Blaze: “You say what now?” Bartender: “Go somewhere else! We don’t cater to you guys anymore!” Beelzebozo: “What in the name of all that’s unholy are you talking about?” Blaze: “Maybe he’s been sampling his wares a little too much?” Bartender: “You’re one of…THEM, right?” Blaze: “Them who?” Bartender: “The…the wrestlers!” Beelzebozo: “Yes, I am a wrestler. What of it?” Bartender: “This place isn’t the GIWF Bar any more! Didn’t you see the big ‘Pussy/Liquor’ sign?? What are you doing here?!” Blaze: “GIWF Bar?…” Beelzebozo: “So THAT’S why we were told to come here.” Blaze: “Sir, believe me, we aren’t here to cause trouble or freak you out. We were told to come here.” Beelzebozo: “Yeah. We’re trying to find out about some guy named Gurgles.” Bartender: “Some guy?…wait.” He leans across the bar, taking a good long look at the two. Finally, he stands back up. “You look about his size, coulda shaved your head…but the makeup’s different…and that’s not the same gal you used to come in with…I guess you aren’t him?” Blaze: “Him who?” Beelzebozo: “I’m guessing Gurgles.” Bartender: “Yeah. You’re not Gurgles.” He sighs with relief. “I know he was a good customer and all, but I’m just as happy not to have guys like him in here. I get a much better clientele now that I’ve changed the style of this place.” Blaze: “So this used to be a bar for the members of the GIWF?” Bartender: “That’s right. They all used to come in here. But they were the only ones. When it folded, I was able to scrape enough together to redo the place and make it open to everyone. Their going under was the best thing for me.” Beelzebozo: “I see. So, do you mind if we stay a little while? I reeeeeally hope you say yes.” He leans in close and flashes a deadly grin. The bartender noticeably shrinks back. Bartender: “Y-yeah, for a little while. But then please go. I don’t word to get out that I’m going back to the original clientele.” He quickly makes their drinks. As he serves them, a lovely young redhead in a small, tight black miniskirt and tank top appears with a serving tray. Waitress: “Hey, I need another round of beers for Table 6.” Bartender: “Sure thing.” The waitress’ gaze wanders over to the clown, and her eyes light up. Waitress: “Oh…my…GOD!!” Blaze: “Who what where?” Waitress: “It’s you!! It’s really you! Oh this is wonderful! I never thought I’d see you guys again!” Beelzebozo: “Ummm…’again’ you say?” Waitress: “So where’d you go? How come that big hunk of yours never said goodbye! I tried forever to get ahold of him!” Blaze: “What the blazes?…” Beelzebozo: “I’m sorry, but do we know you?” Waitress: “Very funny. So come on, spill! Where is he?” Beelzebozo: “He who?!” Waitress: “Senor Mime of course!” Blaze: “….Senor Mime?” Beelzebozo: “You know him?” Blaze: “I…think that was the name of the mime that ran with Gurgles.” Waitress: “Duh! Don’t play coy with me!” She looks past Beelzebozo and really seems to notice Pyretta at last. “Hey, who’s she? Where’s that black-haired gal you used to be with? You two were so great together!” She studies Beelzebozo’s face. “And why the change to the look? You trying to be a tougher-looking clown now?” Beelzebozo: “Lady, you seem to be mistaken. I’m not…Gurgles. I’m Beelzebozo.” His eye twitches again. Waitress: “Suuuure you are.” She laughs as the bartender returns with the beers. She hoists them up. “Look, I gotta go, but I want to talk more later. I’ve missed that studly mime of yours! See ya!” She walks off, leaving Beelzebozo looking very bewildered. Blaze: “Honey? You all right?” Beelzebozo: “I…I don’t know, it’s like…” He shakes his head, then rubs his eyes. “I think the smoke’s getting to me. Let’s get out of here.” He downs his drink, and lays some money on the bar as Blaze hurriedly polishes off hers. They turn to go…only for someone at the bar to lay a hand on the clown’s shoulder. Drunk: “Wassha matta? You don’t like shhmoke?” Beelzebozo: “Not real—“ He’s cut off as the drunk takes a huge drag off of his cigarette and blows the smoke in Beelzebozo’s face. Blaze: “Oh that was smart.” Beelzebozo: “Time for you to learn just how hazardous smoking is to your health.” Beelzebozo grabs the guy’s wrist, causing him to squeal in pain as the clown’s hand bends it the wrong way. Beelzebozo grabs the cigarette from his hand and puts it out right in his eye. He releases the man as he shrieks and stumbles back, clutching his face. Three other men at the bar immediately rise and start toward the clown. Drunk’s friend: “What’d you do to our friend, asshole?” Beelzebozo: “Nothing compared to what I’ll do to you!” And with that, the fight commences. Beelzebozo easily ducks the first man’s clumsy right hook and thrusts his head upward, catching the man in the jaw. He falls to the floor as the clown grabs the other two men by the backs of their heads and begins slamming their faces together. Sick crunching sounds can be heard as their noses burst like ripe tomatoes, spewing blood everywhere. Another man with a chair comes up behind Beelzebozo, but Pyretta kicks him in the groin before he can swing his weapon. Soon, everyone is involved in the brawl. Bodies fly through the air, as do chairs and tables. Beelzebozo destroys all who come close. A man flicks out a switchblade, and is about to bury it in the unaware clown’s neck when a bottle flies through the air and smashes him in the face. He collapses, and Beelzebozo turns, surprised. He looks back, and sees a very short man standing on the bar, holding several more bottles. Beelzebozo: “Thanks.” Midget: “Don’t mention it.” Random voice in the crowd: “The police are on their way! Everyone stop this now!!” Beelzebozo: “Time to bail.” He grabs Pyretta’s hand and, after a moment, scoops up the midget in his free arm. The three head for the door, the midget tossing his bottles at anyone who gets in their path. Soon, they make it outside, and Beelzebozo sets the midget down. Beelzebozo: “Better get going.” Midget: “Yeah. It was fun, but it’s time to go.” Blaze: “Thanks again!” Midget: “No problem.” Beelzebozo: “What’s your name?” Midget: “I’m Zach Dillinger. Good to meet you.” The two men shake hands briefly. “You ever wanna trash a bar again, look me up!” And with that, Zach scurries off. Blaze: “Wait! Come back!” Beelzebozo: “What are you doing?” Blaze: “Don’t you remember? We need a midget!” Beelzebozo: “Aw crap. We can’t go after him, we gotta book.” Blaze: “Shoot. I wonder if we’ll ever see him again.” The two turn and run off. As they run, we can see that a small note has been stuck to the back of Beelzebozo’s coat.
