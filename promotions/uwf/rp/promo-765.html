UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #765

Time

4/34/29 (year unrecorded)

Clyde — ?????

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time. What is it? Is it merely a quirk of human perception, or is it a physical thing that can be manipulated? For, ironically enough, ages, mankind has attempted to answer these questions. In their all consuming attempts to conquer everything, the realms of time have danced beyond their grasp. The one battle they can never win.... The battle against themselves. Their own mortality. It should come as no great surprise then, to find the being who would come to unlock that which has eluded the most devoted minds would be the one who just doesn't care at all. Clyde: Bah! Standing on a lonely street corner, the night wind whips past the immortal demon. He hefts two gold belts over his shoulder, scowling his eternal scowl. From out of the darkness walks the familiar form of his eight year old lover, Bethany. Wearing her own scowl, she stomps directly past the burned man, barely tossing a glance up at him as she continues on to whatever it is she does with her evenings. And then, she stops. She stares at him. Clyde: ......what? What are you looking at? Bethany: Your belts. Clyde: (Glancing at the titles) What, these? What about them? Bethany: I recognize the THC. But why do you have the tag team one, moron? The demon shakes his head in irritation. Clyde: I won it. Honestly now child, you're the one who keeps thrusting me into this ridiculous wrestling business. I do wish you'd pay attention. Bethany merely stares at him momentarily. Bethany: ......but.....you're not even going to have a match for those until tomorrow. How the crap could you have won them already? Clyde: .....you know, that's a good question. Scratching his head, the monster attempts to think. Clyde: Well, let's think this through logically. I obviously did win them, with my godawful partner Seymour. Bethany: No you didn't! I mean, you will, but not until tomorrow! Clyde: If I'm not going to win them until tomorrow, then why am I standing here with the memory clearly in my mind of winning the titles already? Bethany: I don't know! Why the hell are you standing here anyway!? Clyde looks down upon his eight year old lover, his face a mask of twisted sarcasm. Clyde: You know as well as I do, if I don't come out here and do something with my time, I'll end up oddly enough losing to Murderous Fellow tomorrow. It always works that way. Bethany: Killer Dude. Clyde: Whatever. Bethany: Gaaaaaaaaaaah, enough with your breaking the fourth wall! So you're cutting a promo for Killer Dude. I get it. But that still doesn't explain why the hell you've apparently won a tag team title that you haven't fought for yet! Clyde: I did win them. Bethany: When? WHEN DID YOU WIN THEM!? Clyde: ......I don't know. Suddenly the ground shakes beneath the demonic couple's feet. Clyde: (Steadying himself) What the hell....? Another earthquake strikes, fiercer than the last. The night sky begins to churn ominously. Thunder cracks, rumbling, though there is no rain. Clyde: (Looking up) Erm....Bethany....? The little girl follows the burned man's glance, to behold a sight that causes her face to go pale. A crack opens in the black clouds. White light shines through it, brilliant against the starkness of the night surrounding. And through it swarms a flock of little black creatures. Little black creatures with large teeth. Clyde: .......Langoliers? Bethany throws her head back and waves her arms maniacally, looking once again like a demented version of Charlie Brown as she shrieks. Bethany: YOU MORON! YOU MADE US JUMP THE TRACKS OF TIME ITSELF AND NOW WE'RE ALL GOING TO BE EATEN FROM REALITY!!!!! Clyde: Well, good. Wasn't my intention, but hey, if nothing ELSE will kill me.... Clyde waves his belts in the air like an air traffic controller. Clyde: (Yelling) HEY, I'M DOWN HERE! EAT ME! EAT ME!!!!! Bethany: OH NO YOU DON'T! YOU DON'T GET OFF THAT EASILY!!!! Screaming in rage, Bethany grabs Clyde and physically yanks him off of his feet, dragging him away as he kicks and fights, to no avail. The little black eaters of all that is swoop down upon the spot where he was standing but moments before, devouring the very essence of reality. Bethany: (Dragging Clyde) GRUAAAAAAAGH! I'LL MAKE YOU PAY FOR THIS!!!!! Clyde: Bah. And as the demon calmly folds his arms and allows himself to be dragged, the Langoliers move after him, chomping away. One of them turns towards us, opening its mouth wide. And as it swoops in, we mercifully fade to black.....
