9/3 (year unrecorded)

Tsunami Oni — Tokyo, Japan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Regal Rumble was a night of highs and lows for Tsunami Oni, the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. His father was there in the crowd, but he saw his son pinned by Sean McClancy. Oni redeemed himself later by beating all comers in the Regal Rumble. But his celebration was short-lived as he was assaulted by Seymour Almasy. Far worse than this for Tsunami Oni was the fact that he was not the only vivtim. Shiatsu, the Sensation of Domination and the love of Oni's life, was laid out with a frying pan weilded by Laura Almasy. The injury was severe enough to warrant staying in Tokyo for a few extra days. Now Shiatsu is well enough to make the flight back to the states. She is in their hotel room, packing for the trip. The toll of her injury is obvious. Her eyes are red from a lack of sleep and she moves slowly. She hasn't bothered with make-up and his dressed simply in a t-shirt and workout pants. As she zips up her bag, she sighs and sits on the bed, looking exhausted. But she manages a smile when she sees Tsunami Oni standing in the doorway. 'Hey, sweetie,' she says. 'Hey yourself,' Oni answers. 'You sure you're up for the plane trip? It's going to be a long one.' 'God damn, stop fretting!' Shiatsu snaps. 'I get enough of that from my mom. Can't believe she called from Fresno when she saw the Rumble.' 'She was just concerned,' replies Oni. 'Nothing wrong with that.' 'Yeesh, you and your dad make up and suddenly you go all Disney Channel on me!' Shiatsu teases. Tsunami Oni gives her a smile. 'Stop trying to change the subject, you sneak.' 'Listen, I'm fine,' Shiatsu says. 'We got to get back to the States. I feel bad enough that we're not gonna make WNU.' Walking over to the bed, Tsunami Oni sits down next to Shiatsu. 'That's not important.' 'Not important?' Shiatsu exclaims. 'You won the freakin' Regal Rumble! That calls for some major gloating! There are 60 some-odd guys in the UWF that would give there left nut for the spot you're in!' 'I know, Iknow,' says Oni. 'I'm thrilled to have won. I'm thrilled to be getting the World title shot and Live and Loud. I can show everyone that I still have what it takes to be the best in is business. But what is even more important to me is that the person I give a damn about more than anyone is all right! This hasn't been the first time you got assaulted since we returned. Preest and Leanan really did a number on you. And Aidien Pryde is one of my opponents this Sunday. You know how those Chaos Society bastards love putting their hands on valets and managers.' Shiatsu glares at Tsunami Oni, her eyes showing anger. 'So what are you saying?' she says with venom. 'You don't want me there at SAS? You think I'm some frail little thing that can't take care of herself? Listen, I've been with you every step of the way. You and me! Blue mist and frying pans! Anyone messed with one of us, they messed with us both! You just can't tell me to sit on my ass and watch you on TV while you take on some of the sickest fucks on the planet!' 'That's just it,' says Oni. 'Almasy isn't just acting like a delusional idiot anymore. He is seriously deranged. And he has the KnightMare Brigade with him. Almasy is a genuine sociopath now, you saw what he did to his own wife. Aand what's even worse, I helped make him that way.' Rolling her eyes, Shiatsu gets off the bed. 'I don't believe this. You're the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea! Anything sick little Seymour can come up with, you can handle. You got more importnat things at stake. Sunday, you got the Plague along with Adien Pryde. Remember, if the Plague hadn't been there, Jamie Shock would've never pinned you and got the World title. You should be thinking payback.' 'Trust me, I am,' Oni answers. 'So quit worrying about a geek with a video game obsession and start thinking about kicking ass! Don't fret about me. I can handle myself. Besides, the dP takes care of their own. You're not doing this alone this time.' 'You're right,' says Oni as he gets off the bed. 'I am the Devil. I showed everyone that at the Rumble! And after I tear through Montgomery, I'll be World champion again!' 'Damn right!' says Shiatsu. 'But first I need to take care of old business. The Plague is going to learn to stay the hell out of my way once and for all! And if Pryde had any sense, he'd stay home playing Jenga with the other curtain-jerkers in the Chaos Society!' 'God damn, I love it when you talk like that!' says Shiatsu. 'Are you sure we don't have any time for a quickie before our flight?' 'Afraid not,' Oni answers. 'But it will be a long flight and we'll need to pass the time somehow...' Shiatsu grins. 'Mile High Club, here we come! And come again!'
