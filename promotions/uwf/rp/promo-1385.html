I must confess, I cannot recall the exact circumstances of the day my nightmare began. Each day is such a horror, you see, that they begin to blend together into one mad symphony. As usual, my child and lover Bethany was screaming at me. What, exactly, she was screaming about I haven't a clue. I merely nodded, weary of all. '.......and furthermore, basic human greed obviously screws communism out of the gate. So......Clyde, are you listening to me?', she screeched. 'Yes,' I replied. 'Then what did I just say?' '..........things.' 'AGH!', yelled she, stomping her feet upon the cold ground. 'How are you supposed to beat the Communist at this rate?' I paused momentarily, blinking at her. '..........I could have sworn you had me against someone else this week.' 'Did I? All of these UWF morons sort of blend together.' Again I nodded, agreeing entirely with her. Bethany once again faded into the background of my collective misery. I closed my eyes and tried to will everything away.....when something caught my attention. Something I could not avoid. I stared over Bethany's shoulder, scarcely believing what I was seeing. What looked like a giant cigarette was staring back at me. He was floating a bit behind her, steadily up and down. The top of him burned fiery red, as he manically grinned at me. I noticed another, smaller cigarette was clenched in his teeth. 'So anyway, against Trailer Park Communist, you obviously should.......' The little girl stopped abruptly, spinning around to see what I was looking at that wasn't her. Then, she spun back towards me. 'What the christing hell are you looking at?' I blinked again, attempting to dissipate.....whatever it was that I was looking at. But it did not help. 'Can't you see it?', I asked her quietly. 'See what?', replied Bethany, again spinning around in the direction of the horrible thing. 'I fail to see how you can miss it,' stated I, as the cigarette wagged his eyebrows at me. Bethany faced me, her own eyes narrow. 'Oh, so this is one of those stupid fucking 'make Bethany turn around and look at nothing' games, is it? Well it won't work on me!' 'Child,' I sighed, 'I could not make up a giant floating cigarette if I wanted to. Furthermore, why would I possibly want to?' She merely stared blankly at me. Then she spun around, pulling something out of her pocket. 'What are you doing?', I inquired. She did not answer me......rather, she mumbled something, then returned the device to wherever it had came from. 'I called the crazy house. They should be here to take you away any moment now.' Again, I could only sigh as the cigarette floated. Floated and mocked me silently. 'I'm not crazy. At least not any more than usual. And you did not phone anyone, so cease pretending that you......' The noise of a large white van pulling up in the distance cut me off. Several men in white coats stormed out, wielding what appeared to be large butterfly nets. '............you did, at that.' 'It's for your own good,' she said plainly. 'Or at least for my own amusement.' Shaking my scarred head, I turned to walk away from an all too tiresome confrontation. Then I stopped as I saw that the cigarette was steadily following me, still wearing his terrible grin. 'LEAVE ME ALONE!', I shouted at him. As I did, Bethany again stared at me. Then she turned to the abomination. Then to me. She sighed. 'He's over here, gentlemen. Will you hurry the hell up?' A bah escaped me, as I extended my arms and vanished in my trademark burst of flame. Though as I would soon discover, my nightmare was only beginning........
