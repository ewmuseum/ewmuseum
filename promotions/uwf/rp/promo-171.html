UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #171

Addressing the future.

Jan 10 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — The Arena.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Alliance: The union or agreement to cooperate. That’s what Jamie, Ryder, and Malcom all have with each other. So has the fact that two of them are facing each other at Supremacy, and if they win they might face the third when a matter of a couple weeks fazed them? Not at all. This an ALLIANCE, not some third rate Club of idiots. That will be proven down the road as well.*** ( The scene opens up inside a dressing room. The camera sits an angle facing a full size mirror without showing the camera mans reflection. Suddenly Malcom Kennedy appears in the mirror, a wide grin on his face. He leans close to the mirror and adjusts his hair a little with his fingers. He currently wears his wrestling attire. The white tank top, the loose baggy black leather pants, the black wrestling boots. He elbow pads currently around his wrists, only to be pulled up when its time for his match. He flexes in front of the mirror and smiles, proud of the shape he is in. ) Malcom Kennedy: “ Im the fastest rising superstar in the history of the Universal Wrestling Federation.” (Malcom smiles once more.) “ I like the sound of that, it shows my status. It shows that I have earned some respect. It proves that I have what it takes to make it in the UWF, what it takes to win the World Title. So I face my friend and stable mate Jamie Shock, So what? We both are rather happy to meet in the ring. Not afraid to face our friends in the ring. It proves just how strong the Prime Alliance truly is.” “ Mike Montgomery, just because you added that fool Ezraa Kazee to the Players Club, it doesn’t change the fact that you and your Club are a lost cause. I would know, I made the mistake of joining it. You have nothing. You think booking the Prime Alliance against themselves is going to ruin us? You are sadly, sadly mistaken. This will only strengthen our status here in the UWF. And within a few short weeks, the Prime Alliance will hold not only the North American Title, but the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. And you will feel like an even bigger fool than you truly are Mike. The Players Club is a feeble attempt to show you have some sort of stance in the UWF, but the truth is Mike, your nothing now, and a nothing you will be from here on out. “ “ As for Mr. Almasy. If I were you, Id be a little more worried about that world title on your shoulder as opposed to that tramp in the hospital. She can’t save you like she did at the COC. This time someone is going to take that title. Wether its me or Jamie. You’ve had your pleasant little title run here in the UWF, but that chapter is going to come to a close in your book of life, and its going to come to a close REAL soon. I suggest you focus on the task at hand, and not some woman. In the end only one of those can save you from losing your title, and I HIGHLY doubt it’s the latter of the two.” ( Malcom begins to laugh as he stares into the mirror. He grabs a roll of athletic tape from the counter and begins to wrap his hands like he always does for his matches, halfway through wrapping his right hand he walks out of view of the mirror, his laugh can still be heard as he walks away. Finally you hear a door close and silence takes over the room. The scene fades to black. )
