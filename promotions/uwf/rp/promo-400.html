~*~The Scene Opens up in the Ohio Valley Wrestling, Training Facility, where Robb Daniels trains with his brother Eric Daniels during his off time to prepare for his up and coming match-ups.~*~ ~*~The two had just finished sparring in the ring, working on Robb's countering skills, because he would have his first one-on-one match this coming Wednesday Night on Underground.~*~ ~*~Robb exits the ring, and sits on a folding chair and drinks water and wipes his face with a towel. A Man from the office, sends him a post it note saying he had a phone call, and there was no name, just a phone #. Robb walks to the outside of the building and gets into his 2004 Ford F-150, and Eric follows him he pulls his Cell Phone out and dials the # of the person who had called him while he was sparring with Eric. 1-(606)-432-0321.....Ring...Ring...Ring...... Robb Daniels: Hey did someone from this residence call OVW looking for a Robb Daniels. Robb Daniels: Great, it's nice to hear from you what is it you've been needing... Unknown Caller: ............ Robb Daniels: Great so when will you be coming? Unknown Caller: ............ Robb Daniels: This coming Wednesday?! Great it will be good to finally have the group back together. I also have a match that night, maybe you could come down to the ring and congratulate me and we will announce our faction after I defeat 'The Communist'. Unknown Caller: See Ya then. ~*~Robb hangs up the phone, and Eric asks who it is...and Robb smiles...Eric then asks again come on dude who was that?...~*~ SCENE FADES OUT
