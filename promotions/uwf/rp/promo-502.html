The Rage of The Communist

5 Jul 2004

The Communist — The Peoples Training Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:the Communist pummels a locker into scrap, beating it with his fists, blood dripping down the metal face of the ruined door, he picks whats left of the locker and throws it across the locker room. It bounces off the wall, knocking a chunk of concrete out of the wall in the middle of a scythe and Hammer. The Soviette ducks the flying chunk of ruined metal and walks calmly up to The Soviet Champion.: Soviette: Communist. The Committee demands an explination for the utter failure that the Iron Curtain was in Stable Wars. Commie: The Iron Curtain is disbanded. I will punish the wealkings that left me high and dry in that ring. :he punches the wall, leaving cracks in the concrete: Those bastards will feel my wrath. Soviette: One thing at a time Communist. First we need to regain the confidence of the Committee. We need a Belt. Commie: The TV title? Soviette: No. Something bigger. Something to tell them we're moving foreward. Something... agressive. Commie: Don't speak in riddles Soviette. Tell me what you're thinking. Soviette: The Hardcore Title. The one place where you can show your brutalitly and not be Disqualified or fined. it is perfect. You can legaly destroy your opponents and it will only enhance your standings. You will be a king among men. You will show them that you are the most hardcore wrestler in the world. Commie: Yes... Yes! I can see it now! :the Communist laughs his deep, mocking laugh: I will take Persia. He won't even see what hit him. He well be mauled by the Big Red Bear. Maybe I should leave pieces of him in the Ring Soviette: You're getting ahead of yourself Communist. First you need to GET the title match. We need to begin planning. We need to hit Persia hard enough that he can't help but notice us. Hard enough cripple his better judgement. Maybe even hard enough to destroy his body. Commie: Tell me your plan... :scene fades with the Communist and Soviette Plotting Persia's downfall:
