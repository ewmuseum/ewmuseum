UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #751

*sigh*

date unknown

Seymour Almasy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“You know, in retrospect, maybe this wasn’t the best idea after all…” The UWF’s North American Champion, Seymour Almasy, sits in the living room of his apartment. The title belt rests on his lap after a very successful Live and Loud 4, and yet, Seymour isn’t happy. Seymour Almasy: Angelo Deville. It was a name feared by any who knew what they were doing. Deville had beaten the best, plain and simple. No arguments. Seymour Almasy: For the past few weeks, I have focused on conquering the UWF. Focused on destroying it. But now, I find myself in a position in which I must…save it. Almasy sighed. But he couldn’t worry about his petty concerns with the fans. Not with Angelo Deville, and the mastermind behind his arrival, Rich Rollins, breathing down the neck of the UWF. Seymour Almasy: It’s funny how this shit works, isn’t it? One day, I’m hellbent on destroying this place, and now, I want to save it. Fucking sentimentality. Whatever feelings he may have harbored towards the UWF were overshadowed by what he owed the place. It was where he had gotten his start. The only federation crazy enough to take a chance on a delusional bunny hunter. Seymour Almasy: They gave me the opportunity that I needed. From there, I exploded into what I have become, the greatest wrestler in Primetime Central. All thanks to a federation that took a chance on me. A promotion that looked past my mental incapabilities, and somewhere saw potential. I owe them a debt that I can never truly repay. A debt that I can never truly repay. And so now, I must go to war. For myself, for my federation…and for the UWF World Tag Team Championships. He smiled, wryly. He had selected a good partner. A former PCW World Champion, Clyde was as good as they came. A little demented, perhaps, but an outstanding competitor. Seymour Almasy: Clyde and Seymour Almasy is a virtually unstoppable combination. The tag teams we’re up against are strong. The Phenomenons are one of the best teams to step into the ring here. Digital Mortality and the Communist are a great combination of power and speed. Mike Montgomery and Ezra Kazee don’t get along, but if they do for one night, they could walk out with the titles…. Matches like this always made Seymour think. Ten men in the ring left the possibility of massive amounts of battling, and lots of injuries. Seymour Almasy: And then, we have Rich Rollins, and whoever he decides to bring down to the ring with him. It could be El Turtle. It could be Trailer Park Luchador…oh, who the fuck am I kidding? Who the fuck is HE kidding? Angelo Deville is going to come down to the ring with him, and there is going to be full-scale war erupting. He was nervous. Deville was one of the names thrown around with his as the best. Angelo was a stern test. And with Rollins at his side… Seymour Almasy: Deville and Rollins are a formidable tandem. As far as I see it, the only two men in this match who can take Clyde and I out. But there’s something that Deville and Rollins lack…hell, something that Clyde lacks, that I have. Almasy rose, and began pacing back and forth. He was trying to talk himself through the evening, trying to make everything fit into place in his mind. Seymour Almasy: I am determined. Beyond anything Rollins or Deville could ever imagine. This match is about more than a pair of titles. This is about the sanctity of the UWF. On a more selfish level, Seymour wanted Angelo Deville as far away from the promotion as possible. The UWF was his territory. He was the top star in it, and had been for about a year. UWF was his. Seymour Almasy: To come in here and expect to take everything from me takes a lot of courage on the part of Rich Rollins and whomever he chooses to bring to the battle with him. I will NOT be embarrassed, not with Clyde at my side. Not for the UWF World Tag Team Titles. And not in my land. Bring your best. All of you…because you’re going to need it. (Fin)
