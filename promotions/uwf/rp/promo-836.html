UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #836

House Show Hell

15 Dec 2004

Craig Wright — House Show Hell Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the warm up to The Hot-Shot Craig Wright’s title match which is also a Contest of Champions tournament match, the “Highlight of the Night” is taking time out to wrestle in the UWF minor house shows. The reason for this is unknown but rumours suggest that Craig Wright is gaining extra experience in the preparation for the Television Title. [Were outside Ohio and it is the UWF house show. The commentators for tonight’s show are none other than Jesse “Prince” Govern and ex-UES[]-[] [] TF champion Bugs. The commentators get the show underway.] Prince: Welcome folks to another House Show Hell, I’m the prince and alongside me is the one legged Bugs. Bugs: You don’t have to tell everyone every time we go on air. I should slap you in the head to remind you that I am Bugs I’m a legend I’m smart and from the mean streets of Brooklyn. It is an honour for you to even speak to me. Prince: That is right. What a show we have tonight haven’t wee bugs. Four matches are scheduled to go under way tonight. Bugs: That is right Jesse; our first opening match is none other than my favourite teenager in the game. He has the worst mood swings in the business and has a terrible acne crisis. Horny Notro and he will be facing masked luchador Antoniano Del Rebba Asolini Collina. Prince: Certainly going to be a high flying match and I thought your favourite teenage wrestler is Hot-Mask. Bugs: Don’t be silly I am homophobic. You seen that looser in that club a couple of weeks back had guys all over him it was really sick shit. Not for the faint hearted. I mean what is happening to wrestling these days. Why the likes of Hot-Mask should be allowed to even appear on Television. If he was in the House Shows these days I would step into the ring and Dragon Buster him and then position him in the sharpshooter and then see what the bitch has to scream about. Huh what do you think Jesse I’m the man, I’m the man. Prince: You have one leg. Bugs:…… Our second match for tonight is for the No.1 ultimate jobber match. We thought what the hell the big guns are having matches like this then why can’t we seeing as we are a fictitious house show. People Like the Trailer Park Luchador and El Turtle are like gods. They would completely destroy the roster single handily as they are ultimate champions in our eyes, In fact I think we should give them a standing ovation. Prince: you have one leg. Bugs: ……. The second match will see the Pebble Chilly Steven Texas challenging Final Fighter. Prince: This should be an amazing match. Neither man has met each other in the ring. The only time these guys met was at the Cheerleading tournament back in 2001. So this could be an interesting match. The third…… [Surprisingly fireworks go off giving everyone in the audience a shit their selves. No one has a clue to what is going on. Jesse Govern and Bugs speculate as this is not scheduled to happen. The fans are beginning to feel the anxiety of maybe a new superstar or something out of their imagination.] Bugs: What the hell is happening Jesse. Those fireworks made me fall of my chair. I specifically told the board that if they were setting fireworks off then they were to notify me in advance. Balance is very difficult with my condition. Jesse could you give me a hand up. Jesse: I think it’s a leg you need not a hand. What the hell is happening in this arena? [Just then music hits the Amps. The fans take a second to recognise the song and burst into frenzy. Automatically the Arena outside Ohio Erupts. # You remind me of the babe What babe? babe with the power What power? power of voodoo Who do? you do Do what? remind me of the babe Jesse: No it can’t be can it. Bugs are you thinking what I’m thinking Bugs: I can’t get back on my chair give a guy a hand man. #I saw my baby, crying hard as babe could cry What could I do My baby's love had gone And left my baby blue Nobody knew Bugs: I thought he was on a full time contract with the UWF and no house shows. I mean featured on the PPV are they jabbering him. Jesse: I don’t think so. I mean he was in the television title match at FANdemonium which was a great success and he will be fighting on Tuesday night for the Television Title against your best friend Hot-Mask. What is he doing here. # What kind of magic spell to use Slime and snails Or puppy dogs' tails Thunder or lightning Then baby said Dance magic, dance (dance magic, dance) Dance magic, dance (dance magic, dance) Put that baby spell on me Jump magic, jump (jump magic, jump) Jump magic, jump (jump magic, jump) Put that magic jump on me Slap that baby, make him free Bug: Quite a catchy tune huh Jesse, makes me want to get up and boogie on down. Jesse: You have one leg. [Just then the Hot-Shot Craig Wright, the theoretical “Highlight of the Night” enters the small House Show Hell ring accompanied by a microphone. The fans wont stop blaring the Hot-Shot’s name. A small group of fans chant “Get your dick out for the lads.” Hot-Shot waits a moment for the fans to quieten down and begins his discussion.] Hot-Shot: God it’s been a while. Jesse: You can say that again listen to the fans. Four and a half years since we seen the Hot-Shot on a show that brings in rating just over PWW. Hot-Shot: I bet each and every one of you wonder why I am here….. Bugs: Yeah tell us. Are you going to viciously obliterate every member of our roster or something? Jesse: Then you would be out a job. Hot-Shot:….. It’s quite simple. Basically I am here to start a new era…. Bugs: Bring all the stars here. Bring in Shiatsu and that girl on your last promo she was absolutely tasty. Hot-Shot: ……. I have a proposition for you all. In preparation for my Television Title against that looser Hot-Mask, I am going to host and star in the first ever “Craig Wright Testament”….. Bugs: What the hell is that? I want to be on it. Hot-Shot: Principally the “Craig Wright Testament” is for everyone who has the testicular fortitude to come down to the ring and fight me. I promise I will go easy on all of you. Starting now come on I’ll take anyone. Since FANdemonium I’ve heard a lot of you laughing and making jokes about me. I wasn’t born yesterday. You all think that I am the same Hot-Shot Craig Wright as before. Not anymore. As of now I will prove I’m no longer the jobber that I use to be by showing you all no one can beat me in the Craig Wright Testament. Now bring all your jobber asses down to the ring. Jesse: What the hell, Craig Wright has come to House Show Hell to completely destroy everyone in sight. The carnage in the ring at the end of the night could be catastrophic. Hot-Shot: Come on now don’t be scared. [Craig Wright drops the microphone. By this time many of the House Show Hell roster are making there way to the ring. The first to enter is Jack “The Lizard” Robertson. Robertson is defeated via a slap to the jaw. Next up is Horny Notro and he is abolished with a double arm DDT. Next in line is Jammu Goo is put through the cobra clutch and submits in seconds. The jobber elimination continues for a while until no one in the roster is fit enough, Hot-Shot: As you see…… Bugs: You hold on just one second. I’m next. You can’t wipe out our roster and get away with it. Jesse: You have one leg. Bugs:….. Carry me to the ring. [Bugs enters the ring. Hot-Shot stands staring down at the 70 pounder. Just then Bugs drop kicks the Hot-Shot Craig Wright and he stares in disbelief. Subsequent to the dropkick, Craig Wright grabs the leg of Bugs and swings him round his head and lets go. Bugs lands in the eighth row of the screaming fans.] Hot-Shot failed to break a sweat the whole time annihilating 57 of the jobbers.] Hot-Shot: OK this may not be No Contest of Champions. This is a taste of what I have in store for the COC. This is a tournament I know I can win. Leapfrogging many of the steps of success to get to the title sounds like a great idea. This demonstration was for you Hot-Mask. This is a little preview of what I am going to do to you on Tuesday. That’s 57 I have defeated. 57 -0 in the Craig Wright Testament. I’m ready to add Hot-Mask to the record and taking the television title in the process. I’m ready for you Hot-Mask. I was severely distracted in many ways in our first encounter with you. Don’t be surprised this time round. I’ll show you what why I am called the “Highlight of the Night” [As Hot-Shot drops his mic the scene fades
