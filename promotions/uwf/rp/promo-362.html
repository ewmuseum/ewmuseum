UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #362

The Result

24 Apr 2004

Iron Lung — Apartment, Street

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Result with Iron Lung Previously... Iron Lung (Interrupting again) There's one loss on there now, so I wasn't as focused, but I've learned my lesson. And that's where you come in. You can't let those punks throw you around. So what do you say we teach them a lesson, one that they surely won't forget for a while? Davis just nods, and Lung nods back as the two exit the locker room, Lung carrying the large and long bamboo shaft. We now enter Lung's vision, we see everything through his eyes as he approaches the two muscle men. We hear a mutter of 'what do you want kid?' from the first strong man, and Lung replies with a 'you push my crew around, you push me around.' The muscle man just smiles and shoves Lung a little, but Lung stares into the man's eyes, and suddenly he sees the Plague's face beginning to appear. With a large roar, Lung cracks the bamboo shaft into the man's chest, and as soon as he is disabled and bent over in pain, Lung throws him head first into a benching rack. The man flips over the barbell as the bench shutters a little, almost falling over. Now Lung approaches the second man, tapping the shaft to him. The other man tries to run away, but Lung cracks the shaft against his knee, rendering him helpless against the ground as yet again, he sees the face of the Plague when he stares at this man. The man begs for mercy as Lung pushes the shaft to his throat, nodding to Davis as he barks at the man that this would be the last time he tries to mess with him or his company. The man nods as Lung takes the shaft away, motioning for Davis to leave him and the two men alone. And with a slight smile, Davis does do. All Lung can do is smile back, as the scene fades out. He has cleared house, will he do the same against Ruff Ryder come time for Renaissance? And now, we bring you ' The Result '.. Lung was successful with his match at Renaissance, in fact, he was so successful that the owenership of UWF decided to let him take a week off. But now that the week was gone and over with, it was back to action, and this time it was back into the ring with the Plague. How he remembered that name now, the man who took his THC title away from him, but soon there after lost it to Nick Persia. Lung felt that this was due to a curse that he might have put on the 'Hardcore' specialist. You see, the whole time that Lung was away from the Plague, he thought about him, and even dreamed about him. He planned his comeback to the ring, and planned exactly what he was going to do to him when they got there. Now that the chance had risen, how would the young superstar handle it? Surely, Lung had been training for a very long time, most likely the whole week while he was on a so called vacation from work. And now, he had a chance to put that training to work once more. It felt so great to be back in action, and he was sure that it would feel even better once he stepped back into the locker room, breathing the somewhat fresh air of the hallway again. Was this an addiction? No, it was more like an honor. For his whole career, Lung had been developing a moral respect for the game, even though he knew how to play and defeat it at almost anytime possible. This match was just another series in this game, and once again, Lung had the skills to defeat it willingly. The only question remaining in the young superstars mind was, how could he possibly try to attack the Plague when his opponent knew most of his maneuvers? He had to try to outfox the Plague somehow, but what ways were there? Lung hadn't studied his opponent for long enough to come up with any counter moves, but he had enough of his own tapes, and could possibly develop an 'old school' mentality for this match. There were so many other ideas in his head, and soon enough, Lung had developed a headache. Trying to take it easy, he tried to turn the television on, and quickly became aggrivated with the commercials on. All he could think of again was the Plague, and how he wanted to make him sorry for taking his title away from him all too soon. He had a plan, and the Plague ruined it, now he should have to pay the consequences. He would pay in blood, and he would pay in pain and suffering, but it would have to wait another two days when Supremacy hit the airwaves. Ugh, why couldn't he stop thinking about this? It couldn't be good for his health. He had to get out of his apartment and get some fresh air.. As the young superstar took his usual exit out of the building and walked onto the busy streets, he noticed something in particular. No matter where he turned, he would see the Plague's face, wearing the THC championship around his waist. The illusions were beginning again, and there wasn't anywhere to turn for help. Lung was tired of having to pay a doctor to 'examine' him and take down notes on his behavior, he could figure that all out himself. But the fact that his opponent stayed in his mind constantly was enough that he had to run right back into his apartment room, and sit there in silence. Soon enough, the silence was broken by the ringing of Lung's celluar phone, and upon picking it up, he could have sworn that the voice would have been the Plague's. But finally, it seemed that the drama was all over, as it was the friendly voice of an old friend, Damion Patrignelli. Damion: Robbbb... long time no talk. What's up? Iron Lung: (With a sigh of relief) Thank God it's you... Damion: What do you mean? Iron Lung: I've been having these crazy thoughts lately, everyone around me, their faces are turning into the Plague's. I seriously think it's become like an addiction to beat him, and maybe at Supremacy if I am the winner, I'll be able to sleep again at night. Damion: I think you just need a good long rest. You've been really stressed out lately, with trying to keep 'Dashing' Dane or whatever his name is happy by showing him the 'real' you. But the real you is just what you are right now, addicted to fighting, and a great fighter at that. Iron Lung: Thanks for the support, you always know the right things to say. Damion: Don't mention it.. Well, I just called to say good luck on Supremacy, it seems like you've been waiting for this for a while, and now you've got another chance to claim your top spot. Show the Plague that his win was nothing more than a fluke! Iron Lung: Yah, that's exactly what I'm going to do. I'll show him who the real THC Champion should be right now, and I'll give the fans something that they can cheer about along the way. Thanks a lot again man, I've gotta go get ready! Damion: Later -- Iron Lung hangs up the phone and jumps off his Lazy Boy, throwing some shadow box punches as the scene begins to fade to black. It seemed that Damion's words of encouragement worked, and once again the ex-THC Champion was back in winning form, ready to rid of the deamon they called the Plague once Supremacy hit the airwaves in less than twenty-four hours. It was as simple as this, the strong would survive, and Lung would be the total dominant force... End of ' The Result ', but be sure to catch Iron Lung in ' Quarters for a Fortune ' for another exciting adventure!
