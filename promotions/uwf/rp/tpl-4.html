Trailer Park Luchador #4

29 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Are we there yet?” [And so … it begins again.] “If you ask me that one more time, I’m going to gouge your eyes out, you masked buffoon.” [In the beat-up, ’80 Oldsmobile Omega, the Trailer Park Luchador drives towards his destiny. In the seat next to him … the Teddy Bear that has set him on the path of Evilness.] TPL: “Don’t be silly! I mean … hello! You’re a Teddy Bear. You can’t hurt me.” Teddy Bear: “…” TPL: “Although I didn’t think Teddy Bears could talk, either, so … crap. I’m sorry.” “Mmmmmpht!! MMMMMDFKJDSLFJMM!!!!!” [The White Trash Lucha Libre superstar takes the flyswatter that was tucked into the visor, turns around, and rains down hell upon Crab Mask, Jr. The masked superstar is hog-tied and gagged in the backseat. Screams meet the assault that hits him.] TPL: “Quiet back there! I hate having to treat my one of my Masked Buddies like this, but gosh darnit … Teddy says this is the way it has to be, and Teddy hasn’t been wrong yet.” [The Luchador frowns.] TPL: “I mean … if you ignore the fact that he told me a woman would be easy to beat, sent me out after Tigera, and got the shit kicked out of me.” [The Mulleted Champion takes a hand off the wheel and starts counting fingers.] TPL: “… one … two … like … five times she’s kicked the poop out of me now …” Teddy: “But …” TPL: “And there was the whole thing about how turning evil would make people care about me, and they still seem pretty bored.” Teddy: “But …” TPL: “And that washing my mask would get me chicks. I don’t think I even need to say how that’s worked out.” Teddy: “But …” TPL: “And now to keep El Turtlé from eating me, I’m turning on all of my friends one by one before they can figure out it’s happening … which, I admit, is kind of a douche thing to do.” Teddy: “But …” TPL: “But yeah … discounting that stuff … hey! Is that a lady bug?” [The ‘Luchador grins stupidly and taps at the glass where two love bugs happily hump, despite the 60 mph speed.] TPL: “So … err … what was I saying again?” Crab: “MMMMMMPHMMM!!” [The Lucha With the Blistas rains hell down upon Crab Mask, Jr.] TPL: “Are we there yet?” Teddy: “Just a little bit closer …” [And so the trip continued on.] …………………………………… [And roughly a half hour later … they arrive.] [The Trailer Park Luchador drags the hog-tied Crab Mask out of the car, while the Teddy Bear sits inanimately on his shoulder.] Teddy: “We have arrived!” [Before them lies their destination … a massive Victorian monstrosity … a castle that radiates pure evil in every direction! Bats cry … children scream … the monolithic home of our monstrous guest star!] [On the outskirts of Chicago, of course.] [And the doors come flying open … bursting light into our scene, bathing the Luchador and his compatriots in it. And one man stands in the doorway, as hundreds of bats come flying past them … one tall, bald man, with a curling mustache, blood red eyes, and a look of pure hatred on his face.] [He is …] Scourge: “I AM … !” TPL: “I KNEW IT~! DAVID CROSS!!!! Scourge: “What!? No … I’m Baron Black Scourge. You know … the scourge of mankind … the wave of evil that will strike down upon the world … death incarnate … the …” TPL: “Dude. Whatever.” Scourge: “I’m the one that was speaking through your tiny stuffed bear! I, Baron Black Scourge, have set you on this path to realize your true evil potential … that has set you on the path that will be realized on Sunday, at Live and Loud, when you step into the gladiatorial arena for the first time to challenge the Amazon Tigera … I, Baron Black Scourge, have been behind it all!!!!” TPL: “OH!” Scourge: “Yes, ‘Oh.’” TPL: “Dude … I knew I wasn’t crazy. Teddy bear’s can’t talk. That’s just silly.” Scourge: “Well … yes. Although the ease of which I fooled you into thinking they could shows quiet a good deal about your lack of …” TPL: “Ooo, candy.” Scourge: “For the love of …” [Baron Black Scourge drops his head into his hands, shaking them angrily, as the Luchador begins eating Evil Halloween M&M’s.] Scourge: “Fine … you eat the candy. I am going to take your … friend, here … and improve upon him.” TPL: “Mmmwhaevemmmm.” [The Scourge shakes his head, and drags the bound ‘Mask behind him.] …………………………………… [Another half hour later … Crab Mask, Jr. comes flying out of an evil room! The Trailer Park Luchador gasps!] TPL: “GASP!” [And … he grabs his flyswatter, runs towards the masked man, and begins beating him down.] TPL: “I … *whack* … told you … *whack* … to stay … *whack* … bound and gagged! *whack* Loser!” [Deftly, Baron Black Scourge catches the flyswatter, stopping his protégé.] Scourge: “No, my child. The Crab Mask, Jr. you knew is no more …” TPL: “He looks the same?” Scourge: “Looks can be deceiving. This is the new and improved Crab Mask, Jr. … the first of your new army! He is now … EVIL~!” TPL: “Seriously?” Scourge: “Show him, my boy.” Crab Mask, Jr.: “Muahahahahahahahahaha!” Scourge: “See? Absolutely evil.” TPL: “Dude! That’s awesome.” Scourge: “Muahahahahahahaha!” TPL: “Muahahahahahaha!” Crab: “Muahahahahahahahaha!” Scourge: “Excellent. Now … find the others!” [The Trailer Park Luchador and Crab Mask, Jr. descend into the darkness.] [Fin.]
