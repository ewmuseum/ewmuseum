Digging Holes ONE

sept 15 (year unrecorded)

Alex Grey — It varies

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. MARTIN SMALLS “You’re dangerously close to getting fired” “I don’t care Martin...You can’t keep me from going public” I hate days like this. “Going public with what Richard? You don’t have anything solid...just theories” “Like hell I don’t Martin...I’ve kept your books for years...I’ve got enough proof to bury you and this corrupt business” See it’s days like this that Martin Smalls has to stop being an honest business man and start digging a hole... “But maybe I’m rushing things here...maybe I’ll give you a chance to cover your ass Marty” What hole you ask? “See I’ve known for a long time about your dirty little deeds...they say you don’t get anywhere in the business without paying your dues...so no it’s time to pay Martin...and I’m starting to think that Five million is looking like a good solid number” The hole I’m going to bury this asshole in... “Five Milli...Richard I don’t know what you think you’ve got up your sleeve but even “If” you had dirt on me it wouldn’t be worth five million dollars, if you need a raise maybe we can talk about it...but “This” isn’t the way to ask” It’s all I can do to keep from killing this bastard myself, I clench my hands into fists under my desk, it’s the only thing stopping me from pulling the gun I keep in my desk. “You’re scared now aren’t you Smalls?” Like hell I am... “I’m going to give you a day Smalls...One day to get me my money...after that I go public and you go to jail” He turns on his heel and walks out of my office like a man who has it all figured out, the bastard is probably rattling off how he’s going to spend his five mill in his head already. But this isn’t going to happen. See I’ve been threatened before...by better con men...when you run a business the way I do there’s always going to be people who want a piece...and ten times out of ten greed is going to influence these bastard’s to bite off more then they can chew. Picking up my phone I dial my secretary “Kim...Would you please get me Joshua and Alex? The sooner the better” Kim’s voice comes back over the speaker tinged with concern “Is there a problem Mister Smalls” I answer without hesitation “Not at all my dear...not at all...say are you free for dinner tonight?” A brief pause, I can see her blushing at her desk “Of course Martin...will there be anything else?” “Maybe later my dear...Maybe later” I smile...the situation is under control The only thing I have to do is figure out where to dig the hole. –The Troops assembled– “I want you two to bury this bastard...but first I want to you to teach him a long and painful lesson” Joshua nods liking what he hears, the smile on his face at the prospect of violence likens him to a child who’s being told he can have a second piece of cake, Alex on the other hand looks bored. “Martin...” he sighs “Why is it when I sign one of your contracts I end up doing your dirty work...is there a clause in there that says I become your personal hit man every time I sign one of these fucking things?” Vintage Alex...he’s reluctant to do the simplest task I ask of him, but I know him well enough to know that on the inside he’s jumping up and down much like Joshua wanting a piece of that cake. So I let the comment slide and ignore him. “Are you two still here?” Joshua looks like I’ve struck him, Alex looks as if he could care less, but they turn and walk out of my office it’s clear to see they’re all business, probably thinking of just how to do this dirty work and how to have fun doing it. Sick bastard’s “Kim? Could you come here please” There’s no response but a few moments later the door opens, most people have gone home for the day, but I keep Kim on the clock a bit longer...for times just like this “Yes Martin?” “Kim I’m feeling a bit stressed right now...I was wondering if you’d do something about it” Her face breaks into a smile, rounding my desk she falls to her knee’s “Certainly Martin” Laying back in my chair I pray those two don’t fuck this up, They’re both natural born killers but there’s always that small possibility...no it’s just paranoia...sometimes I see this business for what it really is, a delicate house of cards that an ill wind could topple. Which is why you have to deal with these kinds of problems before they get blown out of proportion. “Mmm God Kim...Remind me to give you a raise...” 2. ALEX GREY “I guess congratulations are in order Kid...” “How’s that?” “On your title win” “Hmm well thanks old man I’m touched...almost” Joshua frowns, he doesn’t care for my sarcasm which is precisely why I do it, getting under his skin is too easy. “How’s the knee?” The look on his face darkens, and for a moment I consider that I may have stepped over the line, I don’t fear Joshua by any means but in the inclosed space of the car his long arms could do some damage before I could defend myself, Joshua struggles through whatever demons or thoughts that were on his mind, his face slowly relaxes and he’s looking almost human again. But I know he’s just a tool...wielded by Martin when he needs to get a job done...then again so am I...I’m just a tool that Martin considers invaluable, and I think this angers Joshua more then anything. “Look...Alex” Joshua starts “I don’t like you kid and I know you don’t like me...but we work for the same team...and right now we need to act like it, so for now...can we put these differences aside and focus on what matters?” “Sounds good...Provided you stop calling me “Kid” old man” “Just as long as you stop calling me “Old man” you fucking git” “Fair enough” We travel on in silence through the rain swept streets, the gentle patter of rain on the windshield is relaxing, and in a few moments an idea begins to form in my mind. “So do we have a plan Alex?” “I’ve got one Josh...but I need you to drop me off at my apartment” “Alright...what do you have in mind?” “A long and painful lesson” I smile “I need you to pick him up and bring him to my apartment, if he gets suspicious then just tell him that Martin is willing to close the deal” After that there’s no more need for conversation, we’re now soldiers with our marching orders and conversation would only be a formality, Joshua drops me off at my apartment and I go about preparing for our guest. In the kitchen I keep a small bag of powder...for situations just like this. Three glasses... A bottle of wine... And the powder... All I’m going to need to make this greedy fuck wish he’d never been born. It should be fun.
