UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #858

The journey down the path begins.

Today (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Vegas

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“I looked to the sky After all I knew was gone Cast aside all remaining hope in the bible And found myself here Looking out of the deepest crack of hell on earth.” -Tyler G. When was the last time any of you out there ever felt like giving up? All of you have probably felt that way at least once. The real question is, how many of you did, in fact, give up? Those that have are weak. Cowards How many of you ever stood outside that curtain waiting for your music to hit, knowing full well you were gonna have to cheat to win your match? Those that have are weak as well. Losers How many out there ever listened when someone told you that your dreams were foolish and that you should find new ones? You are weak too. Followers Everyone has a weakness. There is a flaw to every ones game plan. Some just aren’t as severe as others. “Can I still find a place, Where my mind stops all the suffering?” Or will I live In my disgrace All night long?” -Sevendust Malcolm knew from an early age that he would one day be a star of the squared circle. Naturally the day his parents told him he was a fool for wanting to wrestle was a day he would never forget. But it was also a day he gained so much love and respect for his brother Eric. When no one else thought he would be a wrestler, Eric did. When everyone else told him to find a real job and quit wasting time on a foolish dream, Eric encouraged him to get in the ring regardless. All those people who questioned his chances to make it as a wrestler were weak, hopeless fuckers. But Eric.................Eric was strong. Malcolm in turn, found strength through Eric’s faith in him. Malcolm was forever grateful for that. Now Malcolm finds himself at the Contest of Champions. His chance to truly show his dreams weren’t foolish. He cant drop the ball again. It would be too costly. So this is his chance to get one step closer to that world title. Its make or break time. Now or never. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Time: Noonish. Place: Vegas The scene opens up with the camera zoomed way in on a front fender of a car. All you can tell about the car is that its black. As the camera zooms out the car comes into play. It’s a jet black Ford Lightning. The tires in the back are wide and fat. Dual six inch exhaust pipes pop out just in front of the rear tires. The camera pans towards the back of the truck and there, sitting on the tailgate is Malcolm Kennedy. He wears a pair of tattered blue jeans and black boots. And a dark gray hoody. Both of his hands rest in the front pocket of the hooded sweatshirt. He sits there, feet swaying back and forth, staring at the ground. The camera comes around to try and face him. His face comes into view, but you cant see the whole thing as he stares at the ground. He spits to the ground. Malcolm: “Victory is my only option left. To lose is to give up, and I am no quitter. I’ve had the deck stacked against me show after show and still walked into that ring with all the confidence in the world. When this tournament is all said and done. When the smoke clears and the dust settles, I will be the winner of the 2005 Contest of Champions. Simply because, I have no other options.” He took his right hand from his pocket and wiped his nose and returned it to the pocket. Malcolm: “You name once person I’ve ever stepped down from in the ring, and Ill hang up my boots and call it quits. You name one person that’s ever knocked me down and kept me from getting up, and Ill never step in that ring again. I’ve been here two long years now, and I haven’t really done anything spectacular. I was fired by Mike Montgomery, then put through hell in a match 2 minutes after being terminated. But even then, I fought until that final bell rung. I beat Jamie Shock at the end of the first year and earned my spot as the number one contender to face BeelzeBozo for the World Heavyweight title. Only to give it up the next week when Eric died. I won the TV title 2 or 3 times for a few weeks at a time. I guess my biggest accomplishment was when I was forced to enter the Regal Rumble first, and I lasted all the way to the end only to be eliminated at the same time as Rich Rollins. We tied for third. But no big title has ever been around my waist. Up until Eric’s death, I never really thought about it. But when I lost Eric, I was showing everyone they were right about me and my dreams, and even worse, I was proving Eric wrong. I was doing what he told me because he had faith I could succeed, But I haven’t. Not yet. So I made a promise to win that World title in Eric’s name. I intend to keep that promise, and the Contest of Champions is the first big step towards that.” His feet continue to sway back and forth in front of him. Malcolm: “No one can keep me down any longer. No one will prevent me from the glory I seek. Not Seymour Almasy, and certainly not the that piece of trash Duke Williams. You call yourself the Malice Man? You’re even dumber that I thought. You wanna see someone really be malicious? Well you wont have to wait long. Because the first round of the C.O.C. has you and me in the ring together, and Ill show you what its really like to be malicious. I already told you that Im about to release 4 months of pent up anger at the C.O.C. but no one seems to listen. But I refuse to be held accountable for any serious injuries that may occur due to me. You all were warned well in advance. So I suggest you take me seriously.” Malcolm stands up from the truck and closes the tailgate. He pulls the keys from his right pocket and looks at the camera for the first time. Malcolm: “The C.O.C. is finally here. And so is the era of Malcolm Kennedy. Soon I will win the world title that my brother Eric knew I could win. And all of you doubters and weak little bitches will see that I am for real. This is my chance to get what I want. Ill be damned if a white trash warrior, a comic book reject, or any other member of the roster will stop me. This is my time to shine. I will win the Contest of Champions. Wait and see.” Malcolm walks around the truck and jumps in. As the key is put in the ignition and turned, the engine roars to life. The pipes shoot out a few small blue flames. Malcolm drops the truck into first gear and speeds off leaving to long, long, long black marks in his wake. Fade to Black.
