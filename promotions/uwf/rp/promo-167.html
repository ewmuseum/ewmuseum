UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #167

Explanations and tantrums

9 Jan 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open, without fanfare, upon Beelzebozo sitting in his ugly-ass orange chair in the living room of Hell’s Carnival. The scene is lit in shades of red. His red and black-painted face stares at the camera above his steepled fingers. He sits in silence for a time. Finally, he speaks. Beelzebozo: “The so-called Contest of Champions has come and gone. It would appear that the Clown from Hell was handily and soundly thrashed in one of the opening bouts of the tournament to determine the number one contender for the World Title. “I’ve been asked by quite a few people if I meant what I said when I told the world I’d crush Ruff Ryder and win the tournament. Everyone knows what I’m really here for, so I imagine my sudden dedication to an different goal was a bit suspect. I have even been accused of sandbagging. “Well, I need to say this here and now: I never sandbag. Even if I had sandbags lying around I wouldn’t do it. I never give less than one hundred percent when I’m in the ring. That’s a fact. “I know, you’re pointing out my efforts at the end of last year.” He dismissively waves his hand. “I can explain that as well, you monkeys. “You see, no matter how good a shape you’re in, you can be caught by surprise. In this case, I caught the flu. You know, that nasty flu that’s going around this year that’s scaring the crackers out of everyone? Yeah, that one. Sadly I caught it a day or so before the pay per view, and no matter what I tried, no matter how determined I was to compete, the doctors simply wouldn’t allow it. Not only for my own well-being, because competing at less than perfect health can be dangerous, but for anyone I came in contact with. I mean, I could care less if Ryder dropped dead of SARS tomorrow, but I’d hate to give one of those poor, overworked referees the flu, you know? So, I did not compete that night. “You say you saw me there? Well your eyes lied to you. You should gouge them out as punishment, to teach them to lie to you like that. Do it. It won’t hurt much. DO IT. That wasn’t me that night. “Don’t believe me? The voices said you wouldn’t. So I have proof. Quiet One, cue up the footage.” We cut to the opening moments of the match between Beelzebozo and Ruff Ryder. The two men are standing face to face. Beelzebozo’s voice: “Pause the tape.” The tape pauses. “You’ll notice something here. Ruff Ryder is six foot three inches in height. I am six foot five. However, you’ll notice right here that ‘Beelzebozo’ is actually SHORTER than Ryder is. You can turn it off now.” We return to the blood-lit room with the musing clown. Beelzebozo: “You see, that wasn’t me that night. As I stated, I could not compete that night. So, I did the best I could. I sent the Quiet One in my place. Come out here please.” The mime slowly limps onto the screen. His head is bandaged under his bowler hat, and his left arm is in a sling. He stops near the chair, and the clown rises. “As you can see here, the Quiet One is about six foot even. Shorter than myself AND Ryder. He simply borrowed my appearance for the night and competed in my place.” The mime nods. “Of course, if I’d known he’d get BITCHSLAPPED LIKE A TWO-BIT HOOKER I wouldn’t have sent him in the first place!” His face contorts with rage as he turns and punches the mime in the face. The mime falls painfully to the floor, clutching his face as blood begins to leak from between his white-gloved fingers. Pyretta Blaze’s voice: “What the hell did he do now?” The foxy redhead with the juicy melons appears, wearing a lovely black leather bodysuit. “Didn’t he suffer enough last week?” Beelzebozo: “I’ll tell you when he’s suffered enough for embarrassing me like that!!” He aims a kick into the mime’s ribs, who frantically rolls offscreen to escape the enraged clown. “He didn’t get a single goddamn offensive move in! He made me look like a chump, a ham and egger, a total putz!! The bastard’s lucky I let him live without permanent disfigurement!! Maybe he should've stayed in the Hall of Pain for another week! Perhaps that would learn him!” He screams and throws himself back down into the chair. Blaze: “He did the best he could, you asshole! He’s barely had any training at all! I was proud of him for lasting as long as he did!” Beelzebozo: “You’re just soft!” Blaze stares at him for a moment. Now HER face is full of rage. She pulls out a box of wooden matches, lights one, and before the clown can react tosses it onto his lap. His pants catch fire, and he shouts as he leaps to his feet and frantically tries to put his pants out. Blaze: “Don’t you ever call me that word again you son of a bitch.” Beelzebozo: “Jesus Christ walking on the water!!” He finally gets the fire put out. “You crazy bitch!” He lashes out and slaps her. She slaps him back. Then they immediately start passionately kissing. Blaze: “Oh baby you know what I like.” Beelzebozo: “You light my fire, in more ways than one.” She giggles. Blaze: “You say the sweetest things baby.” She leans into him and purrs. “So, you must have told them about the massive drinking binge?” Beelzebozo: “The what?” Blaze: “Oh come on! The enormous drinking session we had a couple of weeks ago! You know, the one that gave you such a bad hangover you had to send the mime in your place for that match?” Beelzebozo: “I had the flu dear.” Blaze: “The flu? What are you talking about? You were hung over! You couldn’t leave bed for two days!” The clown’s face goes blank. Beelzebozo: “I-HAD-THE-FLU-_DEAR_.” Blaze: “Ummm…oh. Yeah. Right. The flu. I’d forgotten about that. My bad.” Beelzebozo: “Indeed.” Blaze: “So, not to change the subject, but I am. I’m not just here for fun lover. I caught the line up for the next show, and you’ve got a title match!” Beelzebozo: “I do? At last…” He leaves her embrace and starts to pace the room. “They’ve finally given me what I want. They realize I won’t be satisfied until I have the Hardcore Title. And, finally, they’re giving me what I so richly deserve!” Blaze: “Well, yeah, about that…you’re in a title match, like I said. And the Hardcore Title IS being defended at that show. But…” Beelzebozo: “You had better not say what I think you’re going to say.” Blaze: “…you’ll be competing against some guy named Seymour Almasy for the Television Title, and some guy named Bruce Gordy is going for the Hardcore Title. But remember I’m just the messenger…” With a wordless scream of rage, the clown turns and picks up his orange chair, tossing it over. He then charges Blaze, who yipes and scrambles offscreen, the clown hot on her heels. Blaze’s voice: “I said I’m just the messenger!!” Beelzebozo’s voice: “And the messenger must die!” The red lights go out, and suddenly the normal lights come on. Beelzebozo’s voice: “Arrrrgh! My eyes! I’m momentarily blinded!” A panting Pyretta appears once more. She looks back over her shoulder. Blaze: “This isn’t such a bad thing hon! I mean, if you win this, you can probably start naming your own matches. At the very least, having a title of any sort is a foot in the door.” The clown stumbles onto the screen, rubbing his eyes and smearing his makeup a bit. Beelzebozo: “I know, but I’m tired of this waiting! It’s been, what, almost two months now? What do I have to do to get what I want here?” Blaze: “Don’t worry. Good things come to those who wait.” Beelzebozo: “Yeah yeah.” He sighs. “All right, let’s assume I care enough about this match to try and win it. What do we know about Seymour Butz?” Blaze: “That’s Almasy.” Beelzebozo: “I give a $%!+?” Blaze: “Good point. Anyway, we know nothing about him other than he’s the World AND TV Champ, and he’s got a very ostentatious, hard-to-pronounce name.” Beelzebozo: “Well, we need more than THAT. Come on, let’s go do some research.” Blaze: “OK then.” He puts his arm around her….and then savagely clotheslines her with his other arm. She spins in the air and crashes down on the back of her shoulders, where she lies unmoving. Beelzebozo: “Told you the messenger had to die.” He leaves the unconscious Pyretta Blaze lying on the floor as we fade to black.
