UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #540

An Evil task, An Evil Explination

13 Jul 2004

Raid — Raid's Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see the door to Raids office. Inside you hear him talking on the phone. He is talking about business as usual but he seems to be getting upset. Dan the interview man knocks on the door.) Dan “Mr. Raid, could I have a moment of your time?” Damn it I’m on the phone can’t this wait! Dan “Well actually sir this time has been previously scheduled for you and if you want your full time slot I have to start this interview as soon as possibly.” Fine! Get your ass in here, but your gonna wait till I’m done on the phone! (Dan opens the door to Raids office and walks in. Raid is reading a news paper and still talking about some extermination job.) (On the phone) Well you see the only problem is I don’t normally do this sort of thing but since it’s a growing problem I think I can make an exception THIS TIME! But trust me this isn’t gonna happen all the time that is unless I like it. (He pauses) Ok that sounds like a fair price, and how many did you want taken care of? (Pause again) Ok so that’s $1000 a head and you said 35, right? Oh by the way I don’t have to kill ‘em right? Good, I’ll see ya tomorrow. (And he hangs up the phone, but he continues to look at the front page of the paper.) Dan “Can we start the interview now?” Your still here? I thought I told you to get lost! Dan “Acutall……” Fine I’ll do the Damn interview! (Raid slams the paper down on the desk and gets up. He starts to walk towards the camera. He has a very impatient look on his face.) Dan “So how do you feel about losing your IC title to Kenny Mercury? How do I feel? You’re asking me how I feel. What kind of question is that? Do you think I liked losing that match? Do you think that I wanted to hand over my IC title? Well the answer to that is a great big FUCK NO! Yea Mercury deserves the belt, yea he’s a great wrestler, but I still think that the belt should be around MY waist! Does that answer your question? Dan “Yea. Tell me, why did Pryde address each member of the Chaos Society as quote ‘Evil’.” Again with these well thought out and highly stimulating questions. Here’s the deal WERE FUCKING EVIL! GET IT! How hard is that to figure out? You don’t have to be a rod damn genius to see that we are mean, nasty and down right evil. Oh, and let me answer your next question before you have a chance to open your mouth and let the shit fall out. We are this way because we want to be. If we wanted to be upstanding citizens, we’d do it. If we wanted to help out the community, we’d do it. If we wanted to do anything nice at all we’d do it. But we don’t! And that’s just the way it is! Dan “How did you know… never mind. Ok here’s my last question. How do you feel about Mr. Meaner being here, in the UWF? “ Wow, now that’s a loaded question. How do I feel about that two timing, back stabbing, no good son-of-a-bitch, being here in the UWF. I LOVE IT! It’s the best thing that has happened to both me and the UWF since I’ve been here. While he is all those things I said about him, I can’t think of anyone else that I would rather be in the ring with. Be it, him in my corner or opposite me, granted I would rather have him in my corner, because once he’s on he’s on. And you best not get in his way cause he’ll stop for no one. So yea I’m glad he’s back! Dan “Well that wraps it up.” Good, I gotta run so you can show yourself out. (Raid grabs a few things from his desk and exits the room. One thing he did leave on the desk was the newspaper. Dan sees it and looks out the door to make sure Raid has left. Once satisfied that he is far enough away, Dan makes himself comfy and picks up the paper.) Dan “ I wonder what kind of job Raid has found in the news paper?” (He is flipping through the paper. After 5 or so minutes he gives up and places the paper on the desk.) Dan “Come on lets go. That guy is crazy I didn’t see any add in their about an extermination job.” (He gets up and walks out of the room. After he’s gone the camera man starts to zoom in on the news paper on Raid’s desk. There is a strange heading on the top of the paper. It reads “Homeless Population on the Rise, Mayor Seeks Possibly Solution”. The scene fades to black.)
