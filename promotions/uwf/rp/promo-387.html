UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #387

The Impact of Evolution!

3 May 2004

Robb Daniels — Lexington, Kentucky

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~*~'In the End' by Linkin Park begins to play throughout the Universal Wrestling Federation, as the fans are in aww...at this Monday Night House Show. The Music continues to play as the crowd is in confusion, and doesn't know what is going on. Finally 2 Large Blue Pyro's from each side of the Stage Explode into the atmosphere of the Rupp Arena. A Mist of Fog spreads in the air. And as the Fog settles to the floor of the jam packed arena, a mysterious person appears on the Rampway. He is wearing a pair of blue jeans, and a blue button up shirt, with his hair spiked and Black and Blue Face Paint Design.~*~ ~*~This Mysterious Man walks down the rampway, and slowly climbs the ring stairs and steps thru the middle and top ropes to enter the ring. Some Fans are pointing, and most are quiet. This person walks across the ring and reaches thru the ropes and motions for a microphone from the ring announcer, they hand him one and he gets back into the ring and paces around.~*~ ~*~The Crowd begins to make some noise and then the man reveals himself to be Robb Daniels, and the crowd begins to go wild as there hometown native of Lexington, Kentucky has come home.~*~ Robb Daniels: Well, Well, Well. I figured most of you guys know me. But I just came out here to introduce myself incase you didn't. My Name is Robb Daniels, I'm no Rookie. I've been in the wrestling business for a while now and I am a experience veteran in some ways. I come from the More Power Wrestling, also known as 'M.P.W'. Now, I'm not advertising this fed, or trying to pull a plug for it on some other feds show. Myself and my partner Jefferson who is not here with me tonight came from this MPW. We were one of the few smart ones, this place was very poorly managed and we got out before it went under you could say. In this fed, we were on top and were top contenders for the World Heavyweight Championship the entire time we were there, we both have tasted Gold. We have also tasted World Tag Team Title Gold, on 21 Seperate Occasions. I'm not here bragging I'm just stating the facts. ~*~Robb smiles and wipes his brow.~*~ Robb Daniels: We are one of the most accomplished tag teams, in this entire business. We've come to UWF, to make an Impact, and this Impact will have an Evolution, the Evolution being Robb Daniels and Jefferson. You can refer to us as 'Evolution'. And I'm sure you all were happy to see us Last Night on the Sunday Show. ~*~The Crowd seems puzzeled~*~ Robb Daniels: Oh Yeah, you guys might not have seen me and Jefferson last night. Let's roll back this footage though. ~*~The Video appears on the huge video screen and shows the ending to the Anarachy vs. Nick Persia & Draco Paladine Tag Team Match when these 2 mystery persons run into the ring and hit Nick and Draco with steel chairs, and then flee from the ring through the crowd. Tape Ends~*~ Robb Daniels: Those 2 Mystery People were myself and Jefferson, we made an Impact Last Night and were gonna make an Impact in our World Heavyweight Tag Team Title Match, against the Tag Team Champions whenever Chris grants us. And come Stable Wars we have our Guys, there Coming, and the 4 of Us Combined will be an alliance you will not want to mess with. ~*~Robb drops the mic, and exits the ring to the cheers of fans, and his entry music. Robb walks up the rampway and nehind the curtain.~*~ SCENE FADES OUT
