Enter the Oni

8/11/04

Tsunami Oni — God only knows...

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Since I'm not in the PPV and Christmas is coming up, I thought I would give a present to you all and post my promos from PTC Survival. I know... I'm a giver.] Tsunami Oni. Some men call him the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. Others call him the Vivaldi of Violence. All right, so he made up those names himself. But hell, he sells a lot of T-shirts so quit being so damn nit-picky. Yeesh, just trying to add some drama and suddenly you act like the official historian of Tsunami Oni. What's that? You don't know the official history of Tsunami Oni? Well then, little Johnny, just hop on my knee and I'll tell you the story. No, don't sit on my lap, just on my knee. I don't want to have a nasty discussion with my parole officer. Now let me go through my books here until I find the story. Hmmmm... Porn. Porn. Porn. National Geographic that I use as porn. Ah, here we go! Totalitarianism Comics #27! What's that little Johnny? What happened the first 26 issues? Well, it was a comic book adaption of things like Mein Kampf, Mao's little red book, and Martha Stewart's Living. With #27, Tsunami Oni became the star and sales went through the roof. Sure, there was some problems when the writers added Shiatsu and the Comics Code Authority objected to a stripper sidekick, but hey, it's not like anyone gets carded at comic book stores. Hmmm? Little Johnny, you think a comic book can't be the authority on the history of Tsunami Oni? Well, maybe there is some editing involved. And embellishment. And couple a couple of whatchamacallits.. lies. But it's the closest thing to the truth you're going to hear so shut up or else I'll get the burlap sack of oranges. Ah, I see you remember the oranges. Don't cry, little Johnny. Just wipe those tears away and I'll tell you the story... Once upon a time, there was a young Japanese couple living in Masuda, Japan. That's a city not far from Hiroshima. This man and woman were named Morimu and Aiko Yamada. The one things the Yamadas wanted more than anything was to have a child. Aiko: I wish we could have a child, my love. Morimu: I know, dear. Aiko: It's such a shame that lingering fallout from the atomic bomb dropped on Hiroshima has cursed you with a low sperm count, my love. Morimu: I know, dear. What did you ask, little Johnny? What's sperm? Well, sperm is the seed that flows from the tree of life and flows into the valley of love to create the miracle we call life. Unless the tree has a condom on it. Personally, I don't use condoms, I have a hard enough time maintaining an erection. Hard enough! I made a funny! I usually use the 'pull and pray' method. Sometimes I like to pretend I'm Ron Jeremy and go for a 'money shot' on the chick's face. It turns out most of them don't like that. But I'm not going to do that anymore. That's how Mr. District Attorney got my DNA. That little skank, I hope she never gets first chair clarinet! Hey, you seem confused, little Johnny. And a little bit scared. Well, let's just go back to the story and take your mind off all that silliness. So the Yamadas are walking hand-in-hand on the beach. Suddenly a huge wave rises from the ocean! It crashes down on the beach, hurtling a large white object to the shore! The projectile crashes into the sand mere inches from Aiko Yamada! Aiko: Sweet mamba jamma! The thing almost killed me! Morimu: Well, if you had been walking behind me like a traditional Japanese wife it wouldn't have come close. Aiko: Hello! What freakin' year is this? Morimu: Hey, I was just trying to add a cultural subtext to the story to prove we're in Japan. Want some sushi? Aiko: Never mind that! Look at this thing! Morimu: Wow! A big old-fashioned bathtub! And it's got feets! Aiko: What? Morimu: Feets! Those brass bird claws on balls! I love those! Hey, this things pretty huge! We could play hide-the-eel in this puppy! Aiko: Excuse me, my dear, but don't you notice the baby in the tub? Morimu: Baby? In the tub? Aiko: Yes, if you stop rambling about feets and hiding eels you'd see him right there. Morimu: Oh yeah, now I see the lil' bugger! This is some mystery! Aiko: No kidding! Morimu: What would a great bathtub like this be doing in the ocean? Aiko: Don't you wonder about the baby? Morimu: Eh, people have babies all the time. Aiko: Except us. Morimu: But a big bathtub with feets, that's a treasure! Aiko: You're a freakin' weird-o, my love. Well, I'm taking the baby home. Morimu: And I'll take the bathtub. Aiko: There's no way you can carry that by yourself. Morimu: You're right. Leave the baby and help me carry the bathtub. Aiko: Are you serious? Morimu: We'll come back later for him! Aiko: Why don't we just leave the baby in the bathtub and carry the bathtub home? Morimu: Oh yeah... guess that could work... So the Yamadas took the baby and the bathtub home. They named him Yoshihiro and raised him as their own son. The baby, not the bathtub. Didn't want you getting confused there. No Mrs. Yamada never could understand her husband's obsession with the bathtub. Now toilets, those were something that Aiko Yamada's blood could get pumping for. She tried all her life to get her adopted son to aspire to become a toilet engineer, but destiny flushed those plans away like a beer dump the morning after an all-night bender... Excuse me, little Johnny? How did a bathtub with a baby end up in the Pacific Ocean? Good question! And for the answers we must turn back the pages of time, ignoring entire decades like they were the articles in Playboy magazine. See the way I carried the metaphor through, little Johnny? Our journey takes us back to the bloody days of World War II! A young pilot of a Japanese Zero is flying over the Pacific. The rest of his squadron had died in a kamikaze attack on an American battleship. This pilot was supposed to have died too, but he thought, 'Screw that.' So now he's flying back to his base, wondering how he was going to explain the fact he was still alive. Pilot: Maybe I could tell them I tried a suicide dive into the American ship, but I missed! The sun was in my eyes! Yeah, and the ocean's a lot softer than people think! Hell, it's made of water! Yeah, that's the ticket... But the young pilot will never get to tell his tale to his bosses, because an American bullet had punctured his fuel tank! Pilot: Crap! Well, at least I still got the secondary fuel tank. Then the young pilot remembers that they don't bother to fill that one since the planes aren't supposed to be coming back. Pilot: Crap! Crap! Crappity-crap-crap! The Zero plummets into the Pacific. There, the pilot experiences something that blow his 'The ocean is soft' theory out of the water. Out of the water! I made another funny! The pilot tries to scream something out that would be a more fitting last word than 'crappity-crap-crap' but his plane hits too soon! Then plane breaks up into pieces on impact and darkness devours the young pilot! When the pilot comes to, he is laying in a bed. A beautiful young woman stands above, dressed in a skimpy outfit of seashells and kelp. Woman: Welcome to Atlantis! I know what you're thinking, little Johnny. Isn't Atlantis in the Atlantic Ocean? I mean, just look at the freakin' name! But that's just what they want you to think. The reason no one was ever found Atlantis is that it's in the Pacific! Nobody looks for it there! Those sneaky Atlanteans! Well, the girl's name was Agua and the young pilot's name was Keiji. And soon they fell in love. Keiji: I love you, Agua! Agua: I love you, Keiji! Using Atlantean magic and/or science, Keiji was able to breathe underwater like the Atlanteans. Just go with it. So Keiji and Agua were able to frolic and do other things there under the sea. Which made the other Atlanteans green with envy! Well, greener. Did I mention they were green? They are. So there. And why were they envious? Because they were Atlantean amazons! No, they didn't sell books over the Internet, they were chicks! Now, Keiji could've gotten more tail than a kitsune with all these green gals. What, little Johnny? A kitsune is a Japanese fox spirit with seven tails. I'm going with a theme here. Anyways, Keiji could've nailed them all, but his love for Agua was true. Plus the longer you're with a chick, the more likely she is to do the really kinky stuff. With all that frolicking in an underwater city with no Planned Parenthoods or men's room vending machines, Agua got knocked up. Agua: I'm pregnant! And you're the father! Keiji: Uh, shouldn't there be a blood test? Agua: There's no other men for a thousand miles! Keiji: Damn! I knew there was gonna be a drawback to this whole only-guy-in-a-city-of-hot-chicks thing! Hmmm? No, little Johnny, Agua wasn't a mermaid. She had a normal woman's legs and no-no place. If she had a fish body, Keiji probably wouldn't have been so keen to get warm to her form. It's bad enough when a chick SMELLS like a fish below the waist, let alone is one! BAHAHAHAHAHA!!! I crack myself up! Eventually, Keiji took responsibility, immediately after the other Atlantean amazons threatened to feed his nads to a shark. While they were still attached to his body. So Atlantis had a wedding and after many months, Agua had her baby. Atlantean doctor: What the hell is that? Keiji: It's a penis. Atlantean doctor: So that's what it looks like. How do you ever... Keiji: It gets bigger later on. Atlantean doctor: Oh, good. Well, congratulations, it's a boy. Keiji: I have a son! Alas, the Atlanteans would never see the baby boy's penis grow! Many years passed and the baby aged very slowly due to Atlantean magic and/or science as does everyone in Atlantis. Just work with me, little Johnny. How else am I to explain how a baby born in the '40's is still a baby thirty years later? One day, Keiji came to Agua with a sad look on his face. Keiji: My love, I have discovered something horrible. Agua: And what is that, my darling ? Keiji: Three decades of dirty diapers has turned the waters around Atlantis incredibly volatile. I fear in two days the whole city will explode! Agua: Have you told the council? Keiji: Hell, no! They'd just feed my nads to a shark! Agua: But we'll going to die! Keiji: And I wanna die with my nads! Can't we just leave Atlantis? Agua: Are you insane? This is a rent controlled apartment! Do you know how hard it is to find one in this neighborhood? Keiji: Good point. Plus I just sent in the cable bill for this month and I'd really like to get my money's worth. Agua: But what about our son? Must Yoshihiro die too? Yes, little Johnny, Keiji and Agua named the baby the exact same thing the Yamadas will end up naming him. Small freakin' world. Keiji: Well, it was his diapers that are causing this disaster... Agua: Noooo! He's just a baby! Keiji: He's a thirty-year old baby! Thirty years of being woken up in the middle of the night! Thirty years of having spit-up on my best clothes! Thirty years of the GOD DAMN TELETUBBIES! Agua: There won't be Teletubbies until the '90's! Keiji: Details! Agua: I won't let our baby die! To calm her husbands down, Agua bends to her knees and... well, let's just saying living underwater gives her amazing breath control, little Johnny. Keiji: Ahhhhhh... you're right, dear. My son must live! To save the life of their child, Keiji and Agua place him in an antique bathtub. Agua: Where did you find this thing? Keiji: It's from the Titanic. Agua: But that sank in the North Atlantic. Keiji: Ummmm... ocean currents... Agua: Ocean currents? Keiji: Ocean currents. Agua: Works for me. Placing the baby in the bathtub, the proud parents swim up to the surface. They kiss their child good-bye. Agua: Farewell, my son! Keiji: How much longer is he gonna be a baby? Agua: Now that he is away from Atlantean magic and/or science, he will age and breathe air like any surface dweller. Keiji: That's convenient. Agua: I wonder what the future holds for our son? Will he be a leader of men? A hero to the downtrodden? A savior of the masses? Keiji: You know what would totally rock? If he'll be a pro wrestler! Agua: Don't ruin the moment, dear. One final kiss from his parents and the baby is of to his destiny. Keiji and Agua return to Atlantis to die a fiery, and poopy, death. Keiji: What's that smell? Oh, crappity-crap-crap... BOOOOOM~!!! The force of the explosion creates a huge wave that catches the bathtub and carries it to the shores of Japan, where it almost squishes Aiko Yamada and Morimu Yamada falls in love with its feets. And that, little Johnny, is the story of how Tsunami Oni came to be. What's that, little Johnny? How did a small baby named Yoshihiro Yamada become the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea? Well, That's a story for another day...
