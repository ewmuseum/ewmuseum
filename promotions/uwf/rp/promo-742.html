UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #742

Hypnotism Doesn't Work, Damnit

4 Oct 2004

The Phenomenons — Yo Momma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The coin swings back and forth from the chain it hangs from.] “You are getting evil.” [As it swings past, the giant, stupid, ever-innocent face of Shane Stone swings with it, eyes glued to the shiny surface.] “Very evil.” [Richard Ray Vaughn stands over the seated Phenomenon, a look of desperate anxiety on his face, doing everything he can to fix the Phenomenons to his diabolical purpose.] “You are getting …” [Shane’s eyes dart back and forth, but he still grins happily.] Shane: “I don’t think it’s working, Richie.” Vaughn: “Shut up! Shut up, shut up, shut up! You’re getting evil!!!” [In the background, Eric Cameron has taken the stolen World Tag Team Title belts and wrapped them around him waist, one sitting atop the other, and flexing wildly.] Eric: “Come on, Vaughn … leave Shane alone.” Vaughn: “No! You idiots have to LISTEN to me! And what I’m telling you to do is TURN EVIL~!” [Shane Stone shrugs and keeps dancing his eyes across the room, following the shiny coin.] Vaughn: “Evil …” Eric: “Can you even turn people evil?” Vaughn: “Shut up, you moron.” [Eric flips Richard Ray Vaughn off while sticking his tongue out, making Shane giggle insanely.] Vaughn: “…” [Vaughn takes the coin and flings it against Shane Stone’s head, making it bounce across the room.] Shane: “Aww … we done already?” Vaughn: “What is with the two of you!?! You idiots are NOTHING without me!” Eric: “Ahem.” [Cameron points down at the belts around his waist.] Eric: “We’re the Phenomenons~!, Richie. We totally kick ass.” Vaughn: “No, you idiots USED to kick ass … the only way you can even see that anymore are old title histories and video collections. You guys haven’t won a match in … in …” Shane: “I won the PTC Titles!” Vaughn: “Shut … up. You two are a JOKE! Three years of bouncing around the UWF hallways with absolutely no point, no purpose, and no use!” Eric: “Hey now …” Vaughn: “You suck!!!” Eric: “YOU SUCK!” [Shane Stone quickly stands up, immediately dwarfing the tiny Vaughn … who immediately learns discretion.] Vaughn: “Hey … you know I think the world of you two. Who was it that got you guys into the Youngbloods? I’ve looked after you your entire careers! Remember Stable Wars? Every tag team title you won while standing next to me, Lew, the Corporal, Raines …” [At the mentioning of his old buddy, who hasn’t returned a phone call of his in four and a half years – and believe me, he’s TRIED – Shane nods ecstatically.] Vaughn: “You guys know you rule … I know you rule!” [Eric goes back to smiling and admiring himself in the mirror.] Vaughn: “But you lost your killer instinct … you lost that drive to win. And the attention span of your average television viewer is … is …” [Vaughn snaps his fingers at Shane Stone, who had begun counting the tiles on the ceiling.] Vaughn: “… it’s really f’n short. It’s all about “what have you done lately” … and you guys haven’t done ANYTHING lately.” Shane: “I won the PTC titles!” Vaughn: “Yeah … that was like a year and a half ago. You guys lost your edge, and now these stupid, crappy, ignorant, attention-spans-like-gnats have turned on you!” [Shane gasps.] Vaughn: “Oh, that’s right. You don’t have billions …” Ghostly Voices: “… and trillions …” [Vaughn looks at the ceiling with a frightened look, then back at Shane.] Vaughn: “… of fans anymore. They grew up … they got older … and they forgot about the Phenomenons! You’re old news, boys. You’re … you’re … you’re like Lyle Wile!” [The mirror Eric Cameron had been looking in shatters dramatically.] Eric: “NO!” [Richard Ray Vaughn nods frantically.] Vaughn: “It’s true! You’re the old guys at the club! The legends that can’t hang anymore! The old porn stars that can only get $10 ring rats! The mantle of the Phenomenons means nothing … the Hall of Fame is just another accolade where people like SEYMOUR ALMASY are the front runners to be worshipped for all history!” Shane: “Stop it, Richie … stop it! I don’t want to hear anymore!!!” Vaughn: “You have to! The wrestling world has changed, boys, and you’re being left in the past … they don’t care anymore! And do you know why?” [Shane Stone shakes his head frantically back and forth.] Vaughn: “Because they have new flavors of the moment. The Masked Monkeys. Even the Local NVA Company!” [Even Eric gasps.] Vaughn: “That’s right. Those are the teams the fans love now. The ones that took YOUR fans from you! Those fickle, stupid, crappy fans that turned on you at the drop of a dime … they don’t want to see your silly antics and legendary pranks anymore. They want giant masks and white powder that you can throw a “~!” onto the end to make it a dozen times more entertaining!” [A tear rolls down Shane’s face.] Vaughn: “It’s time you guys did something about it.” [Shane Stone immediately perks up.] Shane: “What?” Vaughn: “Well, for starters, it’d be nice if you guys WON A FUCKING MATCH!!!!!” [Shane cringes.] Vaughn: “And after that, how about you guys hurt them as much as you can and teach those crappy fans for turning on you?” Shane: “Yeah!” [Eric nods happily.] Shane: “Yeah … that makes since, Richie! To think that the fans turned on us … us! The Defends of Democracy! To cheer those cheating commies! Oh yeah, Richie. We’ll hurt ‘em, AND we’ll win, AND we’ll be the World Tag Team Champions again! We’ll be the bestest ever!” Eric: “Again!” Vaughn: “Yeah! And remember to kick them in the nuts, and preferably break at least one bone.” Eric: “Right. Let’s go get ready, Shane.” [The Phenomenons skip away, as Richard Ray Vaughn laughs maniacally.]
