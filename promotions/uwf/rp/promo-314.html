UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #314

This ones for you

6 Apr 2004

Anarchy — Outside the arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to the arena where the Renaissance PPV is to be held this week. Outside can be seen many large trucks with the UWF logo on the side. Numerous ring crew members can be seen carrying ring parts, lighting and stage items in. A woman is standing with her back to the camera watching the men work. She is wearing a black hooded sweatshirt with a red ANARCHY logo on the back and black biker shorts and a black, leather, elbow length glove on.] Joanna : Renaissance, our first UWF PPV. Tag-Team gold on the line. Just what Anarchy has come to take home. My boys will have their hands full with 5 other teams. One hell of a challenge, but one they can handle. [Joanna takes her sweatshirt off revealing a tight red tank top. A black arm band is visible on her right bicep. She pauses for a bit and wipes her eyes with the sweatshirt before throwing it off to the side.] Joanna : Look at them all. Most of them are up and comers, working as the ring crew to pay for their training as wrestlers. That is how a lot of them do it. Pryde did it that way because his mom couldn’t afford to pay for his training, but she supported his decision to become a wrestler. She did more things for that company than she needed to like organize pot lucks and other charity events to help make that fed more profitable. She did all she could do and all the boys became part of here family. [Joanna pauses some more, looks down and shakes her head] Joanna : She was like a mother to her, and Pryde became the brother I never had. As many of you may remember a few weeks ago that Pryde had to take off abruptly to be her in the hospital. She had been fighting cancer for 8 years and complications from 2 surgeries had set in. You will also notice that neither Dai nor Pryde are with me right now. That is because last week Pryde’s mother passed away and he is finalizing here estate. Don’t worry; they will be ready to go at the PPV. [Joanna slowly turns around. It can be seen in her eyes that she has been crying. The front of her shirt reads “For you Mom”] Joanna : Player's Club, Right to Party, Local NVA Company, Team AWF, Lustful Lullaby. You all have been given notice, this match meant a lot to Pryde’s mom and Anarchy is going to everything in their power to bring the titles home with them. They are out to prove that yes, they are a top team here in the UWF, and Renaissance is there time to shine. [Joanna starts to leave when she pauses] Joanna : I almost forgot. Nick Persia, you seem to be making a big splash in the short time you are here. Hell, you even have a title shot after 2 matches. But where the hell are you. No one has heard a peep out of you since Pryde called you out. Not a word, yet here you are going after the gold. Good for you, you must be under some big wigs desk like Montgomery’s and not be able to make a TV appearance to get a match that fast. Just remember, it is for the Hard Core Belt, and Pryde don’t like either of you. We may just need to get a better view of the competition for that match. [Joanna walks out of camera view as it fades to black.]
