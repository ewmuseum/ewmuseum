UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #484

The definition of team

29 Jun 2004

Ezra Kazee — MSG Locker room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene flares to life in the locker room of Madison Square Garden. There is very little movement around, and very few people. The lockers are empty, and the room, for the most part is empty, except for one person. There is one person sitting in the middle of the room. He is wearing a black Players Club t-shirt with a Players Club hat. He is also wearing a pair of jean shorts and flip-flops. Next to him is a duffel bag, most presumably his. It is maroon and gold. Kazee is staring at the ground as he begins to speak.) Kazee: In almost all sports, there is this idea of team. In almost all sports, there is this idea that you have to be a team player. The idea that the team is going to win. The idea that you can not do it without a team. Very few sports actually do not believe in the idea of a team…..or do they? We can of course start with the obvious sports. How about we look at baseball. Sure you can be like the New York Yankees or Boston Red Sox and go out and sign everyone under the sun. But in 2003, it didn’t help them. In 2003, the team that most exemplified the idea of team won the World Series. That team was the Florida Marlins…… And then there is Hockey. There is nothing but a team sport. If you can’t function as a team, you aren’t going to do very well. Yeah, you can go out and get Jaromir Jagr, Mario Lemuix, or who have you, but when it comes down to it, you have to work as a team. And this years team, the Tampa Bay Lightning, showed they can use their teamwork the best…… And there is also football. Two teams that showed the definition of teamwork went out there and battled back and forth in the Super Bowl. The Carolina Panthers and the New England Patriots. Very few teams ever really exemplify team work…… But I can find you an example where teamwork and the idea of team is even more exemplified. The NBA Championship. The Lakers had 4 hall of famers on their team. Shaq, Kobe, the Mailman, and the Glove. But they were defeated by a bunch of what many people consider nobodies. The Detroit Pistons almost sweep the Lakers. And one reason they did it was because they played as a team…… Auto-racing? Of course. Look at everyone they have on a team. Pit Crew, Spotters, Driver. Golf? Ryder Cup. Tennis? Well, there are doubles titles, and the Davis Cup……. Professional Wrestling is now also going to show that idea of team. The idea of what it means to be a team. The UWF is going to find out who can work best as a team, and who is the best team in the sport today. And that is coming up very, very shortly. Stable Wars VI. The defining moment in a stables career…… And there is a list of 6 stables here too to vie for it. There is a list of 6 stables that believe they can make themselves the best team in the UWF. Can any of them do it? Well, at least one will. The question is who……. We have the mystery stable. Not much can be said about them. They are nothing but an unknown standing in front of another stable. Can’t really comment, don’t know if I want to. I’ll just let them run around in the shadows that they run in. After all, it’d only be speculation on my part…….. We have the team they are fighting against in the first round. The Resurrection. The Past, Present, and Future as they like to call themselves. They are a great team. From top to bottom, they are definitely a great team. You have Digital Mortality, a hall of famer. You have Moraless, the PTC Extreme Champion. You have Bondage the Sex Fiend, who honestly, I think likes to have a lot more fun running his hand over something else. And then we have Malcolm Kennedy. All of them are great athletes in their own right……. Then we have The Iron Curtain. A group of athletes who are brought together by one set of ideals. Communism. I gotta admit, in all my studies, one thing I learned is that Communism is an absolutely perfect theory on paper. There are no flaws in it. The problem is that it takes people to run it. But that’s beside the point. A team that is driven on being equal and everyone sharing in the glory. Not much better you can get there. Can the four of them do it? I’m sure they can, it’s just a matter of if they will…….. We have Chrono Cross. This is definitely a stable for the ages. 4 hall of famers come together. Reminds me of something else. Hmmm, let me think. Oh yeah, that’s right, the LA Lakers. Can Chrono Cross be any better? They have the brains, the ruthlessness, the know-how, and the determination, all wrapped into one stable. The question is how are they going to work as a team. It’s a stable that I would be honored to face any time, any place. If they make it past The Iron Curtain, maybe I’ll see them in the finals, if not, well, maybe I’ll meet some of them some other time, other place….. And finally, we have the stable that the Players Club has first round. Chaos Society. Adrien Pryde. Raid. Draco Paladine, and a mystery partner. They are the first challenge to Players Club in establishing our dominance. They are our first challenge and are a stepping stone into the next round….. (Kazee raises his head and looks into the camera with as much focus as he has ever had.) This match though, for me, it isn’t just about advancing any more. It’s about truly showing dominance. I’m tired of them coming out day after day, week after week, and attacking me from behind. I’m tired of having them think they have the run of the place. That they have the run over me……. I’ve said it once, I’ll say it again, you four don’t know chaos. You don’t know anarchy. You don’t know what it truly means to lose control of everything. But you will. You will know what it means to lose control of everything…… You four, you may know what you are doing. You may know who you are. But you don’t know who I am. You don’t know what I am capable of. You don’t know what I can make……. I have been here before. I have been in the position that you are putting me in. And I have reigned supreme. At Stable Wars, Chaos Society, you will experience something that many people never knew I could do……. (Kazee stands up.) CHAOS SOCIETY, YOU THINK YOU CAN CAUSE CHAOS AND ANARCHY. YOU HAVEN’T FELT ANYTHING. I WILL CAUSE MORE CHAOS THAN YOU HAVE EVER KNOWN. I WILL CAUSE MORE ANARCHY THEN YOU CAN EVEN IMAGINE. AND ONCE I AM DONE WITH YOU, YOU WILL BE NO MORE IN STABLE WARS. YOU STABLE, WILL BE IN CHAOS. YOUR RELATIONSHIPS WILL BE IN ANARCHY. AND YOUR ENTIRE WORLD WILL GO DOWN IN A WHIRLPOOL OF DREAMS THAT YOU NEVER NEW YOU HAD. (Kazee walks off camera as it fades to black.)
