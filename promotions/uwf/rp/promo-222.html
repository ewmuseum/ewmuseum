UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #222

The PLAn

13 Feb 2004

Raid — Huston Astrodome

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene opens and we see Raid standing outside of the Huston Astrodome. He is pacing back and forth , and has a look of anger in his eyes. He finally stops pacing and walks directly towards the camera.) Where do I begin? To tell you the truth I just don't know, but what I do know is that it looks like we got a bunch of LOUD MOUTH, INCONSIDERATE PRICKS around here. I mean, all you people do is run your mouths about how your better than someone else. And the worst part about it is that you don't even know who or what your talking about. (Raid pauses and tries to keep his composure.) Well lets start with you Montgomery. You say that the Players Club is gonna walk away with the gold, and Kennedy and myself are going to walk away empty handed? Well the way it stands now your half right. One of the new tag team champions will be a Players club member, but the other is not. You also say that your contract prohibits you from having to wrestle your stable mates. Well I guess you can try to change the match if you like but I can tell you that is not going to happen. Ezra and myself and you and Kennedy will be partnered up at the Clash, and there isn't a DAMN THING you can do to change that! So your just gonna have to suck up your pride and tag with a Prime Alliance member, or should I say FORMER member after the slaughter the Players Club delivered to the Prime Alliance. Which I would like to say I really enjoyed watching! I liked it because it emphasizes the fact that in the world the strong survive and the weak are left behind to die! This is why I liked to see the Prime Alliance be decimated by the three of you. The Prime Alliance talked a good game but in the end they couldn't back it up. Montgomery, my fight is not with you, but you still threaten me. You say that if I ever touch another Players Club member again I will be out on the street. Well to tell you the truth the only reason I did what I did was because I have been watching Ezra for a long time now. He has changed from the MEAN RUTHLESS SON OF A BITCH I once would have gladly called a friend to a weak pathetic bastard. I'll admit he still has incredible skill in the ring but he has lost the fire he once had. He has lost the fierceness I once saw in him. And that is exactly why I did what I did. I hoped if I shocked him he would regain the flame that once burned brightly, the rage he held so dearly. Obviously I didn't Succeed in doing what I intended to do. The only thing I can do now is to rekindle our friendship and win the Tag Team Championship. That is exactly why this match is perfect because it gives Ezra and myself a chance to work together again. Something that I have been looking forward to for a long time. To be honest with you the Tag Titles would be a great way for me to show the Players Club and the world what I'm about. It would be a great stepping stone for Ezra, myself and the Players Club. You see Montgomery, this sets it up perfectly, Ezra and me will win the Tag titles and it will start the Players Clubs reign on dominance in the UWF. It will catapult the Players Club into history. But it must be done properly and it will start with the Tag Titles and we will then work our way through the rest of the UWF championships. (Raid pauses and reaches for a bag. He pulls out some clothing and begins to unfold it. Once it is unfolded he holds it up.) Do you remember this Kazee? It should because it your old attire from your days as Mr. Meaner. I know that this stage in your life is over but I just wanted to remind you of your past. A past in which we dominated as Tag Champions. I am truly sorry for what I did to you Kazee and I want to make it up to you by winning these titles at the Clash. We just have to show them what the team of Kazee and Raid can do. Our reign as the greatest tag team that the UWF has ever seen will begin in a few short days. And together again we will prove that we are the BEST in the world!! (The camera zooms out and fades to black.)
