UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #737

busness as usual

4 Oct 2004

Dreammaker — dark parking lot

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(the scene opens up with many cars parked in a circle with head lights on the circle. There are some loud grunts and yells coming from the circle. As the camera works its way to the circle you here some cheering and see many figures slap hands and exchange money. Then a familiar voice is over heard naming off two names and the crowd cheers and then goes quiet. The cameraman then is seen by many people they shout and point at him. Then the camera is shut off abrubtly. The screen goes black. Then after a few seconds the video starts again and we see Jeff Johnson pull his shirt coat back on and look at the camera. Jeff: Looks like i passed some sort of security check. This might of been a bad idea to begin with. Im gonna have to check with the network about all access interviews. (whispers) 'Im dont think we should be here.' Anyways lets find out what is going on here and then we can go. Looks like thugs and hullagans fighting it out. (The video shows 2 men fighting in the middle of a group of people. Both men have no shirt or shoes on and the dont seem to worry about the crowd getting involved. Kinda like, if I had a guess, organized fights and there is deffentially money exchanging hands. Oh no, well folks it looks like the guy I am supposed to get to know better is here. Damn you network for this. (Then out of the shadows Jeff sees DReammaker. Who nods his head and walks up to The camera. Dreammaker then laughs out loud and then turns around as one of the shirtless fighters yells 'yield'. Dreammaker then claps his hands and tells everyone to hold off and quiet down so he can talk to the new guy. ) Dreammaker: So welcome to our lets jsut say club. I figured you would be at home with the wife and kids while I work. Jeff: Work! What the hell its after midnight. You have the largest match of your career tomorrow and you are out here on concrete fighting some punks. What is the reason for this luniscy? DM: Reason, do i need a reason to do anything? Mr. Johnson can i go to the bathroom? (the crowd queitly laughs) Jeff: You know what I mean. Why was I sent here? I got a call from my boss saying to interview our UWF employees tonight here? Dreammaker: I might of made a phone call during lunch today, but who cares at least you got out tonight. Why does it matter that I dont take the easy road in life? Why does it matter then i dream all the time? Why do I need to be pampered, just because I am a proffessional wrestler? You want to know why? I'll tell you why. Because nothing in this world comes easy. Nothing. I learned that the hard way. I didnt get this body over night. I lived in a gym. I learned that fighting kept your musles tight and fit. So I found a night job to go with my career. Then I got bored. So now I wrestle. But who in the hell can sleep with all this going on. (stops and looks at the camera and points at it) White wolf enjoy your time with that belt. It is over for you. While you sleep dreaming up of ways you could possibly beat me. I am here working on ways to beat you. So you tell me who deserves anything more. Noone does. And if anyone thinks otherwise, I'll make sure they change their mind. Now (slaps Jeff on the shoulder) since you want to know so much about me, (laughs ) welcome to the club. (Everyone laughs and there are many hand slaps in the air being heard) . Now (turns around to look at everyone) last fight tonight and then we get some food and R&R back at my little shop (more laughing). Lets have Adrien in the ring with the new guy. (everyone makes a path for Jeff to get to the ring)(silence) Jeff: OH no, I can't fight, I don't even wrestle. (he looks at the camera) Help me, I don't want this. No job is worth it. Dreammaker: Come on MR. Johnson. Nobody cares. Look around, nobody is watching, and nobody is listening. Because nobody cares. So why dont you just live a little and let the night go. You never know, Your dreams may come true. (AS Dreammaker finishes another punch line, he pushes Jeff in to the circle. And everyone aound them close up the gaps and start exchanging money as you here Jeff grunt with pain) (the camera fades with the laugh of dreammaker heard in the back ground and then whispered. ' White Wolf, White Wolf, come out and play. WE just want to play. We are here for ya'!
