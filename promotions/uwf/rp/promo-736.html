UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #736

Full Circle

4 Oct 2004

Mike Montgomery — Asylum Industries

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And here we are. Live and Loud Four. The UWF's Flagship Pay Per View. You make it here and no one can deny you that you are truly a legend in this business. But when you walk into the UWF's flagship show as the World Heavyweight Champion, you are not a legend, you are a wrestling God. Mike Montgomery has indeed accomplished this. Of course, there are those who would say that he is an undeserving champion. His reign is built on the blood and sweat of others. He has used people, manipulated people, taking advantage of their generosity, their love, and their faults to get to where he is today. So what would the champ have to say to these people? Would he apologize? Would he try to justify his actions? Tell them it was all about the title and to hell with anyone who got in his way? Well actually, no. What he would say is 'it's all about me. Deal with it.' Quite simply, Mike Montgomery really doesn't care about who you are, or what you think of him. Montgomery is a user. It is simply what a man in his position must do to survive. He studies his opponents, finds their weaknesses, discerns what he has to do to counter everything that makes them a thread. In the case of Tsunami Oni, he turned to the tried, tested and true method of stealing his opponent's woman. Through doctored footage and an altered audio recording, Montgomery convinced Shiatsu, Tsunami Oni's paramour, that she'd been secretly dating the World Champ since his successful title victory at Terror In Tokyo. Tsunami Oni is, understandably, devastated by Shiatsu's seeming infidelity. Exactly what Montgomery planned for all along. But Tsunami is, of course, a legend in his own right. A hall of famer. Perhaps the fastest rising star the world of professional wrestling has ever seen. In their past meetings, Montgomery has never beaten Tsunami Oni. Not even a disqualification. He knew all of this, which was why taking Shiatsu was but the first part of Montgomery's plan. As we open this latest scene in Montgomery's life, we once more find ourselves in the offices of Asylum Industries. Asylum Industries...the company that has given Montgomery the power he needs to bring his plans to fruition. Montgomery sits in his office, eyes closed with a look of pure bliss etched across his features. A pair of female hands make their way up Montgomery's chest, fingers intertwining with his own. Following the hands are the head and upper body of Shiatsu. Their lips lock in a deep, sensual kiss as she curls up in his lap. I'll let you fill in what was happening beforehand for yourself. Shiatsu wraps her arms around Montgomery's neck when she finally pulls away. 'Hi.' Montgomery says. 'Hi yourself.' Shiatsu answers simply. 'How you feeling?' Shiatsu considers the question for a moment, 'Sore. Still kinda in shock, I have to admit though. I still can't believe it. He actually took time out of his tag match to hit me with the Angelwing Madhammer. I mean, I know I hurt him, but I never thought he'd go that far.' Montgomery barely supresses a smile. So far so good. In reality, Oni had been aiming for Montgomery with the move, and Montgomery had pulled Shiatsu in front of him, causing her to take the brunt of the move and giving Montgomery the opening he'd needed. Shiatsu, thanks to the concussion she'd already suffered at Terror in Tokyo, couldn't remember anything just before or just after being hit with the Angelwing. Montgomery kissed her forehead, 'Don't worry so much about it. Tomorrow night it's Live and Loud. I'm going to make him pay and humiliate him on the biggest stage in the Universal Wrestling Federation.' A grin spread across Shiatsu's face. She changes position, now sitting on her knees, mounted on top of Montgomery, 'I rather like that idea,' she says, drawing her tongue across his chin. Montgomery smiles, 'I thought you might.' The two begin making out again, only to be interrupted by the shrilling of Montgomery's desk intercom. The couple pulls apart, 'You have GOT to be kidding me,' Montgomery curses, thumbing the talk button, 'What?' 'Misters Frost, Cale and Blaise are here to see you.' The secretary announced. 'Thanks.' Shiatsu changes position again, once more curling up in his lap, this time facing the door. In walks seven foot two bodyguard Gavin Frost, long, jet black haired Deacon Blaise, and a new arrival. Logan Cale, standing 6 foot two, maybe two hundred fifty pounds or so. His three piece armani suit is crisply pressed, light reflecting off a brand new rolex. A pair of rayban sunglasses is hooked on the suit's front pocket. An attache case made from pure alligator leather bounces slightly off his right hip as he walks. The three men walk in and take seats along the opposite side of the desk from Montgomery. 'Gentlemen,' Montgomery begins, addressing the threesome, 'thank you for coming. Shiatsu here, I believe you all know. She'll be sitting in with us for this meeting. The newest addition to our team, Logan Cale, will be taking up the intelligence division of Asylum Industries. Mr. Frost, this is nothing against your performance in that position, it is simply a matter of focus. You were hired to be my personal bodyguard, and, especially now, I will require your absolute focus in that area.' Frost nods his understanding. 'Now, Mr. Cale, I trust you have some interesting information for us.' Cale stands, placing his attache case on the desk. In one smooth motion, he opens the latches and flips his case open. Withdrawing three manilla folders, he slides one along the desk to each of the other attendees before beginning his briefing. 'Gentlemen, with the help of Ms. Shiatsu, I have put into motion a plan that should help Mr. Montgomery in his match. As we all know, Mr Oni's father has, shall we say, 'unsavory' ties to the criminal element. However, until I recently spoke with Ms. Shiatsu as well as some contacts I have at the FBI, it seems that Mr. Yamada's ties run deeper than first believed.' 'How does this help me?' Montgomery asks. 'Quite simply, sir, you're going to hit Tsunami Oni with a second, equally tremendous, emotional blow on the eve of what may be the biggest match of his career. Mr. Yamada is Yakuza. Very, very well connected Yakuza. To be precise, he's a high ranking lieutenant in one of the Tokyo families with connections to the Hong Kong triads as well. The FBI has been after this guy for nearly three years, but they haven't been able to put together enough evidence for an extradition hearing.' 'I like where this is heading.' Montgomery muses. 'Indeed,' Cale continues, 'as I said, they haven't been able to put together sufficient evidence for an extradition hearing. However, under US law, they do have enough to go to court. They simply have to be able to get him here.' 'But now,' Blaise says, catching on to Cale's train of thought. 'Several weeks ago, Mr. Yamada arrived in the United States to visit his son. Tsunami Oni. Three days ago, I placed a call to the FBI's field office here in Chicago, informing them of Mr. Yamada's whereabouts. Ten minutes before I walked through that door, FBI agents raided Mr. Yamada's room at the Holiday Inn, placing him under arrest for fraud, racketeering, as well as conspiracy to commit felonius assault, using a phone to arrange a crime across state lines, and three counts of conspiracy to commit murder relating to the deaths of the heads of several rival mob families.' Montgomery couldn't contain the jubilant grin that quickly spread across his face. Shiatsu appears somewhat uncomfortable, but Montgomery fails to notice, 'In short?' 'In short,' Cale answers, 'Tsunami Oni's father is now looking at spending the rest of his life in prison because he came to the United States to visit his son.' 'Mr. Cale,' Montgomery beams, 'you have more than earned your first paycheck.' Logan Cale nods in acknowledgement, turning the floor over to Montgomery's wrestling and conditioning coach, Deacon Blaise, 'Mr. Blaise, what do you have for us? With the video you've been provided, I trust I'll be getting my money's worth.' Blaise stands, picking up a remote control and aiming it at the large plasma screen monitor on the far side of the room. 'First off,' Blaise begins, 'we of course have the Onipocalypse.' Blaise points toward the screen, where footage of Oni executing a reverse dragonrana into a back mounted dragon sleeper is running, 'now preparing for this one is rather difficult because Oni uses it so rarely. That being said, your best bet will be to cut him off during the dragonrana. Counter with a powerbomb.' Blaise skips ahead, the footage changing to show the Downfall of Western Civilization, 'Now this is exactly why the match must stay in the ring. If he gets you onto the apron, and hits this, it's lights out. If you catch him early enough, you can probably counter with a suplex, or, if you're creative enough, you can probably pull out a modified Unprettier, but I'd play it safe and stick with the suplex.' Montgomery nods, 'Okay, what about the Butterfly Knife?' 'You shouldn't need to worry too much about the Butterfly Knife. Oni has almost completely removed it from his arsenal because of the extensive scouting already performed. That being said, again, a powerbomb is probably going to be your best counter.' 'Okay, finally, what about overall?' 'Speed. Much as you do, Tsunami Oni relies on speed for much of his offense. If you can keep him grounded, you've got him. Otherwise, this thing turns into a high intensity spotfest that Oni WILL excel in.' Montgomery stands, 'Thank you gentlemen...' 'One more thing,' Cale adds. 'Yes Mr. Cale?' 'Oni's motivated. He's angry, and he's going to be looking for revenge. I would advise extreme caution.' Montgomery nods, 'I know, but I'm not concerned Mr. Cale. I am not concerned, quite simply, because I made Tsunami Oni the legend that he is. I launched his career by bringing him into the Asylum. I facilitated his return with the cruiserweight tournament at Live and Loud Three, and this year, I am going to end the legend of the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. Now, if there's nothing else?' All three men shake their heads negative. 'Okay then. I'll see you all tomorrow.' Cale, Frost, and Blaise pack up their things and quietly file out of the office. When the door closes, Montgomery turns back toward Shiatsu, 'So, where were we?' he asks, smiling.
