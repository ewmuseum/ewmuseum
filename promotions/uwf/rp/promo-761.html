UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #761

Role Reversal

23 Oct 2004

Adien Pryde — Empty Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens inside of an empty arena. The ring is set up and all of the ringside seating. In one of the empty seats sits a lone figure. The figure is wearing a black hooded sweatshirt with the hood up and head down.] Voice : I expected more from the man who holds a title in UWF. More than belittling others and trying to raise ones self onto a higher pedestal than he deservers. I expected better from you Nick Persia. [The figure raises his head and pulls back the hood of the sweatshirt to revel Adien Pryde.] Pryde : You are the Intercontinental Champ and have many fans that look up to you, yet you resort to remarks that are below you. I have looked up to you as the longest reigning THC Champion in UWF history. You have now held the IC belt for a week and have already brought the prestige of it by your comments tonight. Let’s listen to what you are saying tonight. [Cuts to footage from Nick Persia earlier today.] Nick Persia: “Do you know why I am here? Do you have a clue why I am here? Do you have the faintest idea, Mister Adien Pryde? I think not. I am sitting here, enjoying the moonlight, in the middle of the night because I want to. I don’t care what other people think. I don’t care what they might say about me. I simply do not care about poor souls anymore. You see, I think trashing other people is only to make yourself feel better.” Pryde : I may have been that way in the past, but my fans have showed me differently. Damn, I was even complimenting you to. Here is what I said earlier…. [Cuts footage from Adien Pryde earlier today.] Pryde’s Voice : Now is the time of my destiny, and my destiny is the Intercontinental Championship. It has again come full circle and my opponent is again Nick Persia. Nick has pulled some of my best matches out of me, and I need to wrestle better than I ever have before. The Tag Team Championship was not my destiny, nor was THC Belt. I truly feel that The IC belt is my destiny. It is my chance to make my make here in the UWF. Pryde : You stated that I was trashing others but it is you that have started trashing me. This is what happens when I compliment you. So disappointing. You have become such a hurtful person. Let’s look at this other clip… [Cuts to more footage from Nick Persia earlier today.] Nick Persia: “I will tell you the truth now. Not my opinion, not your opinion or the opinion of Richard Hayes, but my own. I am telling you the truth. I have defeated you each and every time. You name it, I have done it. I never failed at winning a title. You on the other hand, failed to beat me. You failed to take a title. You failed to do what you are supposed to do. You failed to do what you want to do. Admit it, you failed you. You failed yourself because you did not live up to your own expectations. You are an embarrassment to your own family as they felt you could have done better. They’d rather have a son who isn’t famous but is someone who people look up to than a famous joke.” Pryde : What do you know of my parents? Nothing. My mother died six months ago due to cancer and she was more proud of me than she ever was. I was making a good living doing something I enjoyed and she didn’t care if I was famous or not. To her, being on TV was more fame than she could ever hope for me. It sounds to me that it my be hitting a little to close to home for you. Maybe it is you who is having problems with your parents. Might be. Then we have what was your first comment today that sums it all up for me. Let’s take a look… L’Histoire se repeter Do you speak French, Adien Pryde? I think that you know what it means, but let me clear it up for you. It means that history will repeat itself. You know what I am pointing at. I am pointing to all my victories I’ve had at your expense. I’ve never lost to you. That is a fact. Another fact is that will not change, Mr. Pryde. Pryde : You say that history will repeat itself. Things are so different this time around that I would have to completely disagree. I have complete confidence that History will not repeat itself. So confident that if I don’t win the IC championship tomorrow that I will give up my hunt for a singles title and go back to the tag title hunt. [The scene fades to black.]
