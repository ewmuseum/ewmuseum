UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #355

Working man.

April 22 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Executive Suite, Grand Central Casino.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Another check in the Win column for Malcolm Kennedy and the Prime Alliance. Another victory over anarchy proves what the Prime Alliance had said all along, Anarchy is just a third rate group of wrestlers trying to prove themselves to a crowd who doesn’t give a crap. So what is next on the list? Ruff Ryder takes care of Kenny Mercury, and Malcolm Kennedy takes on.......A mystery opponent.* { The scene opens up inside the Grand Central Casinos corporate suite. The room is spotless. A plasma big screen, expensive paintings, ancient Japanese artifacts and weaponry, and expensive vases all found in the room. With a loud “whoosh” the door swings open and in walks a very disturbed business man wearing a full suit and tie carrying a manilla envelope and a few sheets of paper. Quickly at his heels is a young, young woman carrying a clipboard and quickly scribbling on it. } Malcolm Kennedy: “DAMN IT JOYCE! I’m tired of this. I bust my ass week in and week out for this freaking company and yet I still get stuck in these stupid ass matches.” Joyce: “Im sorry Mr. Kennedy, I have no control over the executive decisions in the UWF. Im just an intern whose replacing your sick secretary for the week!” Malcolm Kennedy: “Joyce, how long have you been here?” Joyce: “Since 5:30.” Malcolm Kennedy: “No, I meant how long have you been interning with.......” “5:30?!?!?!? Jesus H Christ, why the hell are you still here now. Its almost midnight.” Joyce: “Because you told me to Mr. Kennedy.” Malcolm: “Oh, errr. Yeah, thanks for reminding me.” Joyce: “Umm whatever.” Malcolm: “Rod damn Mike Montgomery! What kind of bullshit stunt is this? I should be main eventing these shows, I put the fans in the stands, but I can only do that taking on the big names of the UWF. Not dicking around in the ring with some mystery opponent.” Joyce: “Maybe they are just waiting for the right time to give you the right push?” Malcolm: “Bullshit Joyce, just plain Bullshit. Everyone in this whole freaking federation knows that I can go toe to toe, eye to eye, with any freaking member of the roster. Beelzebozo, Mike Montgomery, Joshua Dunn, Digital Mortality, Moraless, I can take each and every one of them to the limit, and win too. But no one here knows that! Why? Because each and every week they put me in the ring with no talent idiots like Raid and Anarchy. Joyce: “Mr. Kennedy, Im sure that when the time is right the UWF will gladly put you in the ring with much bigger superstars than they are right now. Who knows? Maybe this mystery opponent could be a big step in the right direction. Maybe your gonna be stepping in the ring with one of the UWF greats! Like that dream boat Jay Phoenix!” Malcolm: “Oh god Joyce, don’t make excuses for them. God knows they can do that just fine on their own. But hey, maybe your right about one thing. Maybe I will step in the ring with a former great of the UWF. Who knows. Maybe Tsunami Oni himself will walk right down the aisle and get in the ring with me.” Joyce: “Can we say wishful thinking? I was just being supportive, not serious. I didn’t think it would go to your head.” Malcolm: “Don’t you have some paper work to do? Last I checked no ones figured out how we came up 1500 dollars short last night when all the money was counted.” Joyce: “If you were here more you would know I took care of that already. The paper works right here.” { Sliding a sheet from under the bottom of the clipboard, she hands it to Malcolm. He glances over it and nods his head. } Malcolm: “Hmm, well done.” { Malcolm then tears the paper length wise down the middle, and repeats the process until the paper is in tiny squares. He then blows the pieces out of his hand into her face. } Malcolm: “Looks like you made a calculating error. Better fix it.” Joyce: “You cant do that! I finished.....” Malcolm: “See Joyce, that’s where your dead wrong. See if YOU payed attention more around here, you would’ve learned that you don’t cross me. You don’t smart off to me. And you don’t EVER try and upstage me. So finish this paper work, collect your things, take your ass home and don’t come back.” Joyce: “FINE!” { With that Joyce storms out of the room. } Malcolm: “ I hope to god that the UWF brings me the best of the best at SAS. I hope they give me a hall of famer on a silver platter, and they send his ass down to the ring. Im finally gonna prove once and for all that I am just as good if not better than any single wrestler in the UWF past and present. But I swear to god if I see one jobber appear from behind that curtain, It will be open season for any member of the UWF executive board and roster that I see fit to inflict pain upon. You see, its time I get the recognition I deserve here in the UWF. No more bullshit, No more games. At SAS I don’t care if Lew Spectre comes downs to the ring in a tank, I will still come out the victor. If 10 of the UWF’s best walk down to the ring, Ill beat every one of them. I don’t care if a fully equipped Japanese army walks down that isle. Ill just stack the bodies to the rafters. I cant prove a thing to anyone by beating these assclowns like Raid and Anarchy. All I can do with them is try and break a sweat. At SAS I will get my respect one of two ways, Ill earn, or Ill take it by force. One way or another though, I will make an impact. You can count on that.” { With that Malcolm walks past the camera over the scattered pieces of paper. He walks out the door and grabs a man in a suit carrying a cup of coffee. } Malcolm: “Get your ass in there and clean up all that paper.” Man: “Y..Y..Yes sir Mr Kennedy.” Malcolm: “NOW!” { Malcolm smacks the coffee cup out of the mans hands, which thankfully, is empty, and throws him to the floor of the suite. He then slams the door as he walks off. The scene fades to black as the man begins to pick up the pieces of paper with his hands, one by one. }
