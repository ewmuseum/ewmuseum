UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #701

Preparation

15 Sep 2004

The Plague — Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Plague is seen leaving the shower room of the gym carrying two bags. He is wearing a “Fear Factory” shirt and shorts and he is also wearing sneakers. His hair is down and to the side of his face. He notices the camera and nods as he walks towards it. He stops places his bags down and smiles.] Plague: “So, Mike Montgomery, The Communist and Myself walked out of Supremacy victorious. And I guess I do owe a little appreciation to Mike, you proved loyal on Sunday, and that’s all I could have asked for. And it was me pinning Tsunami Oni, The Number One Contender, I guess it seems that Oni will be no match for Montgomery, and I guess that’s a good thing, I don’t want to see Oni with that Title again.” [Plague cracks his knuckles and grins at the thought.] Plague: “And Mike, you know me very well by now, you say I am predictable, but you know what, you got to stick with what works. I threatened you, but that was just talk, but just don’t cross me again ok, let’s try and stay allies, it is better to have friends than enemies, right? But I guess that doesn’t mean much for Thursday at Underground. In yet another Main Event Match, and why not, I am The Plague, and I am the single greatest athlete here in The UWF and I proved that by pinning Tsunami Oni, a multi award winner here. I’m going to face The Communist, my former partner, and a person who now that I actually teamed with him, realized he has a lot of potential, but if I need to face him in the ring, well he’s going to need to use all his skills and take me to the limit.” [Plague looks down at his watch and gets a look of shock on his face.] Plague: “But my other opponent, Killer Dude, why? What the hell do I have to do here to prove I am way above Killer Dude’s level here in The UWF? The EoD, come on remember that bullshit, Killer Dude was and will always be nothing. And I’m not going to waste my breath on that Dude. He is done in my mind; he isn’t even worth the time of day in the ring with me. I’ll let my fists do my talking when it comes to Underground.” [Plague grabs his bags and throws them over his shoulders and begins to head out of the gym. He exits the gym and walks toward a black truck parked in front of the gym.] Plague: “There are a lot more important issues at hand, and that’s Adian Pryde, hitting The Black Death on me, whoa buddy, you really crossed the line now Pryde. You think what I did to your bitch was horrible; just wait until I get you in the ring one-on-one. Pryde, you really have your hands full on Sunday, I mean Slasher alone will fuck you up, but than you throw in Nick Persia, man I hope there is something left for me to beat on. Pryde you won’t get away with what you have done and said to me, I’ll get my hands on you sooner or later and when I’m done, so will your career.” [Plague takes his bags and throws them into the back of his truck. He than walks around and opens his door.] Plague: “But I don’t mean to cut this little speech short, but I have a match tomorrow and I need to get to the airport and catch my flight, so I’ll just talk to you all, my glorious and adoring fans tomorrow following Underground, after I leave victorious once again.” [With that The Plague gets into his truck and drives away.]
