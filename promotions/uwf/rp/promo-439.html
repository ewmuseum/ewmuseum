UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #439

The beatings will continue until...

2 Jun 2004

The Formorians — Where do you expect an Irishman?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The fight of good vs. evil always seemsto spark the interest of people that seem to just like the sight of blood. But then again what fan doesn’t come to a show looking forward to someone being beaten on isn’t that the whole point of this sport. Especially with you have a team like the Fomorians their great size makes them formable opponents in their own rights? Most dread the thoughy of facing them. Most fear the tactics they know will be used against them. Then you have those that totally come unprepared to the ring to face them. Underestimating what these two can do. And because of their size thinking they might be slow or heavy footed. Unlikely, with trainers like these to have had in the past. Two of the greats from GIWF days of old: one was none other then their big brother Sean McClancy and the other one was Nephlim who now sits behind a desk running his own fed. Even that other fed, some call it the bastard child of the UWF, couldn’t escape the rise of the KnightMare Brigade. Who in UWF thinks that they will escape it? The twins have learned over the years; rules can be bent like a green willow branch with out breaking them, Then again breaking them is just as much fun. Their fist real match in UWF they used that what they know best to use. Chairs, leaving bodies on the mat. They walked away, not as the victors of a match, that the opponents came unprepared for bit as the winners in many another’s eye. Whose fault was that? Was it the Fomorians? Or those that stood in the ring to face them without studying them first? Sean always said to keep your mind on the game. Fey had always made sure they studied their opponents before facing them. Finding a weak link in a tag team is always something good. But now we go to the twins; as good little Irish boys with their newly crowned champion brother as a role model. We find them sitting in a local pub. They are surrounded by fans, most of which are female. Even with their 6’ 6” and over 300 lb size, they have their fair share of female fans. 5 years ago when they started they were barely of age, now as young men, they are out on their own. It never stopped their brother before. There are a few empty mugs, once containing beer sitting on the table, and a full mug in each of their hands. One is wearing a shirt that says I am with Stupid, with an arrow pointing to the other twin. And the other is wearing a shirt that says the same with a arrow pointing at his brother. As usual they are dressed alike making even harder to know who is who. But as the camera gets closer the twins give themselves away. And, as usual the Interviewer is non-other then Michael Duntz. A man that has been left broken and bleeding more times then most of their opponents, but a man whom they trust to carry out these duties. Once even hiring him as a personal interviewer for a long period of time.. Duntz: Good Evening Tom and Pat. Both the twins look up and see him standing there. Pat: Ah, Mik, why don’cha come an’ sit down. An’ join me brother and I fer a nice cold one. Get this man a Killian’s Red. Tom, or the one that would probably be him, stands up and places a very large, meaty hand on Duntz’s shoulder. Pushing him down into the chair and handing him a mug of beer that has just been brought to the table. Duntz Thanks, but shouldn’t you two be getting ready for the match this week? Tom: Please? We are getting’ ready. There is nothing like a few good beers to get yer body relaxed an’ yer mind focused. Just ask our Brother, a newly crowned World Champion ‘e ‘is. Trainin’ is’na all weights an’ ring work. We ga’ t’ ‘ave a social life t’. Pat: Plus, if I migh add, what is there t’ be getting’ ready fer? Our Opponents this week heard they would be facing us, a’ apparently ran and hid. Bet ye anything they left a trail behind them tha’ took the janitor hours t’ clean up. Duntz: You came here to make your mark on the UWF, why did you allow yourself to get disqualified your first match back? The twins look at each other and start to laugh. Then turn and answer at the same time. Both Fomorians: Because we were bored? Pat: Being th’ new guys on the block, we needed t’ make sure tha’ th’ old folk knew tha’ we meant business. We are na’ ‘ere to be playin’ games. Th’ old green horn thing, ye know? As successful we’ve been in th’ last year, even in th’ ‘istory tapes tha’ the UWF owns from the GIWF, we’re still new ‘ere. Where the old ones from th’ UWF think tha’ the new kids on the block don’na know ‘ow to toe th’ line, so they don’na give them th’ respect they deserve. Well our plans don’na include na getting’ the respect we deserve. I can assure ye tha’ by Stable Wars, the UWF will know tha’ the KnightMare Brigade, no matter ‘ow small er large, means business. Duntz: What has your sister said about you doing what you did to a female in the ring? Pat ehales deeply at that, like he was expecting a comment like that. Tom: Well ye know well as she does. Outside th’ ring, usually we wouldn’na touch a female. Bu’ in the ring? They are equals to every other man tha’ steps in with them. We are equal opportunity arse kickers. They get treated the same. No special moves er kid gloves for them. Believe me. some of the women can do jus’ as much if na’ even more damage then a man of equal or greater size can do. Or sister is a prime example o’ this. Remember she once dislocated me shoulder in a trainin’ accident. Plus women like to use their nails and fight dirty. Always makes it fun when we have to face one of those. An’ if they’re good lookin’ enough, good chance to test th’ goods, an’ let them know wha’ a real man would be like. Pat: Yeah, we are bigger in more ways then one then ‘abou’ ninty percent o’ th’ UWF roster. Duntz: So what do you have planed for your opponents this week? Another beating like the one from last week? Pat: Now, Mik, if we told ye tha’ there wouldn’t be any surprise fer ye t’ watch th’ show now, would there be? Duntz: I guess not. But as usual I am sure we can expect great things from a team that has been carrying the Knightmare Brigade Banner for 5 years now. Tom: Mik, if I didn’t know better I would think you are going soft in yer old age. Let’s jus’ say tha’ Right t’ Party are in fer a party alrigh’. Bu’ they’ll be more like party favors. It’s our party now. We do it our way. An’ someone will be kissin’ tha’ mat. Ye can fokin’ guarantee it. Now, drink yer beer, an’ let us get ready fer our fight. Duntz: Match. Pat: ‘E ‘ad it riogh’ the first time. Fight. We’re na’ ‘ere t’ ‘ave matches. Remember tha’. Duntz: Uh, yeah. With this we fade out as the twins give a toast to fighting, and we wait for WNU to see what will happen.
