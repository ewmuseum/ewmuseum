Trouble?

15 Aug 2004

Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy — Still in Tokyo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One night, one title with two belts, and a shot at being the number one contender for the World Title: This is the task that is coming forth to the three McClancy brothers at the coming UWF Regal Rumble Pay Per View. Terror in Tokyo for sure. In one match you have the Tag Team Titles up for grabs. To win that the Fomorians, Thomas and Patrick McClancy, need to take on the current champions in the Drowning Pool and the Legends. Puh-Lease… neither team is anything that can be considered as a threat to the Fomorians. The Drowning Pool came in with a big bang, but now their just a fizzle. Only Tsunami Oni has done anything of any worth lately, and Slasher and Benji have been, for all intents and purposes, empty promises. And when you talk about The Legends, you have to ask… who? Yeah, the Legends. Who knows about them? Supposed Legends, yet when you mention tag teams around here the former North American and World Tag Team Champions of the GIWF, PWW and a few other federations. The Fomorians are the true legends here. They have a legacy to live up to. The others are just pretnders to the throne. The real kings of tag team wrestling are here. But this tag team match serves another purpose, as the match with Sean vs. Oni will also. It’ll only take a little bit of extra effort and not only will the Legends not have much of a chance in the Regal Rumble if their entered, but all of the Drowning Pool will have beaten and broken bodies taking them into the main event. One person will leave as the Top Contender for the UWF World Title… and that man is meant to be “The Irish Bastard” Sean Michael McClancy. Face it, live with it. But now to find out subjects tonight, as we continue their saga… Once again we find the McClancy twins, Thomas and Patrick, as they sit in front of a huge screened TV. The promo of The Legends is playing; the twins quietly sit and watch the moronic movements of the want-to-be tag team Champoins. Bare-chested with Sweat pants on, they are relaxed watching this. A Cup of something hot and steaming sits on the table in front of them. One cup has the letter P on it and the other has the letter T on it, both in a stylized celtic font, most likely a gift from their big sister. A gift of the only person who is able to tell them apart at any distance. Pat: Ye know, Tom, these two are pretty close to a few others we have known. Completely devoid o’ any intellectual processes goin’ on. I’ve trained dogs smarter then these guys. An’ why is it na one can seem to say our names, riogh? Tom: They are morons… Unforgiven an’ Slasher have’na even shown their faces in public since this match was announced. ‘Ow much ye be wantin’ t’ bet tha’ they pissed their pants when they ‘eard who they ‘ad t’ figh’ in this match. An’ those two, I’ll be surprised if they can find their way to th’ ring with a map an’ compass. We might be wantin’ to make sure they are able t’ find their way. Don’t want them t’ get lost. Pat: Let’s see… Fo…mor…ians… na’ ‘ard at all. Man even th’ Brits can pronounce it riogh. Even some simpletons from the U.S. can do it, even some o’ them from Chicago. Think about it, they be wantin’ t’ talk abou’ Legends? ‘Ow abou’ this one: the Fomorians are the ones that are mythologically associated with the powers of nature tha’ most challenge man: fog, Storm, winter, disease, and crop-blight. A race o’ demonic giants, tha’ conqured all who they came up against. Tom slaps his forhead with the palm of his hand the starts to laugh. Tom: Oh boy, these two are goin’ to be a really big challenge, look at this file Annie got us with a work up on them. Man I am surprised she stayed awake long enough. Pat looks over his brother’s shoulder Pat: Oh man, this one had t’ be a really good friend of the Boozler, ‘e likes t’ grab the package o’ their opponents as their set up move. A package check is prime material t’ be in line fer a friend o’ Mr. Bangemhard. Tom glares at his brother and then contines to read… Tom: There isn’na much tha’ we ‘aven’na seen before. I don’na see too much o’ an actaul challenge ‘ere either. We already know tha’ our sister can beat Benny boy on ‘er own. So I’m na’ worried abou’ Slasher an’ ‘im. Both start to laugh at the memory of that match, where Fey McClancy beat her former baeu, in Benjamin Jackson in a match, fair and square. A voice breaks the laughter of the two huge men, quickly they turn and Stare. Annie is standing there with her long black hair flowing free, her strong green eyes shining in the light of the room, and dressed to kill in a business like suit and high heels. The twins quickly grab the t-shirts that are resting on the top of the couch and pull them on, and down over their chests. Both: Princess, sorry we didn’na see ye come in… Annie Smiles at them, she knows that they will respect her no matter what. Now the quesiton is will they respect her in a few weeks, when they find out what their brother is up to? Annie: You two sould be watching th’ tapes and what na’ tha’ I sent ye. Na’ playin’ around. Yer brother ‘as more on ‘is mind than ‘e needs t’ riogh’ now. An’ ye two are playin’ around like these matches don’na mean squat. Ye two ‘ave th’ chance to bring the Tag titles into th’ fold o’ th’ KnightMare Bridage once again, jus’ as Sean ‘as a chance to become th’ number one contender fer th’ World belt. Tom: Ye know wha’ is goin’ on, don’cha. Ye care t’ tell us? Sean is distracted big time lately, an’ we are worried abou’ ‘im getting int’ th’ ring like this. Ye know ‘e likes t’ be totally focused on th’ matches ‘e is in. Annie: All in good time, boys all in good time. Th’ courts will be informing ye formally as they get all th’ upper crust taken care of. I am sure there are a few ruffled feathers, there, as always when this kinda news hits them. Riogh’ now th’ Courts are in a hussle and bussle with yer sister due in September, on th’ ‘arvest moon. The first twins o’ mixed blood t’ be excepted with no qualms. And can we be ‘opin’ tha’ they won’na be th’ last ones. Two new Ishramsons ‘ave all o’ their panties in a bunch already, an’ yer brother jus’ added t’ it. All o’ Oberon’s court ‘as ga’ t’ ‘ave the McClancy name on’ their tongue, an’ na’ all of it’ ‘as t’ be in good sayin’s riogh’ now. Tom and Pat stare at her with a look of confustion on their boyish faces. She lightly pats their cheeks and moves to sit between them on the couch. Pat: I can’na believe we are going t’ be uncles finally. Personally I though’ Sean would ‘ave been the first t’ produce an heir, th’ way ‘e always went abou’ things. Then again I figured ‘e would ‘ave been married by now, in all ‘onesty t’ ye. Ye know ‘e did love ye a’ one point. But ‘e wasn’na ready t’ settle down. Annie: I know, bu’ let’s na’ think abou’ tha’. Let us focus on yer match and yer brother’s matches. Tom: So, ‘ave ye ‘eard from yer ‘usband, lately? Annie looks down at her feet, and then squares her shoulders picking up the folder sitting on the coffee table in front of her. She purposely doesn’t answer the question presented to her. Yes, she’s a married woman now, married off to a high ranking official in the Order. A field commander under Ishram’s Black Guard. He was a redcap, for all intents and purposes a member fo the Unseelie Fey that had a darker heart then her own Leanan one. But it wasn’t who she wanted. But it was who she had to deal with. One day she would join Fey in the line to help keep the lines of the courts going. But her line would still be pure in the Unseelie. Annie: So wha’ do ye guys think abou’ th’ Regal Rumble? ‘Sve yr given it any though’ a’ all? Yr two ate in it also, and honestly, I would suggest ye thinking of lastin’ long enough t’ ‘elp yer brother ou’. ‘E won’na admit it, bu’ ‘E’ll need all th’ ‘elp ‘e can get. It’s no secret tha’ ‘e an’ Corporal Punishment are th’ top picks t’ win. Tha’ means everyone will be gunnin’ fer ‘im t’ make an impression. I don’na be wantin’ ‘im t’ overly work ‘imself in tha’ match. Or in the Oni match, either. Bu’ I ‘ave a feeling there will be some infterferance from someone. Ye know our rules boys… Bell to Bell.. No one gets into the ring. Tom: Well, don’na count on’ Slasher ‘er Ben makin’ it ou’ o’ th’ ring under their own power as far as I’m concenred. Oni is fightin’ this one on ‘is own. Annie: I am asking Penny t’ make sure tha’ Jaime an’ Miguel are ready jus’ in case, t’ ‘elp with run-ins an’ I will make sure tha’ Ox an’ Ruff are on th’ same page. Fer yer match, DP will lose, ‘ands down. Ye owe them t’ fokin’ much t’ na’ beat them. An’, as fer Th’ Legends? Legends are just stories, unverifyable stories. They’re abou’ t’ face real legends in livin’, breathin’ Fomorians. These guys ga’ nothin’ on ye. Never ‘ave, never will. They’re want-t’-be’s, nothing else. As they sit there and watch the tapes the twins joke around with her about their opponents for this week. Sean walks into the hotel room. Dressed in a sweat suit and looking like he just stepped out of the shower. He stops and watches Annie and his brothers all sitting together. But he can’ help but to feel gulity of wishing that it was their sister-in-law sitting there already, instead of a manger. This whole thing was taking to long but was coming all too quickly with the anwers to the scrolls returning so soon. The three of them talking about what should be expected in the match this week and maybe even in the Regal Rumble. The worst of it will be the KnightMare Bigade will have the biggest target painted on their chests. Tom Dd yefind ou’ yet who sent tha’ video t’ Jaime an’ Miguel? I know Sean is still pretty hot under th’ collar fer tha’. Annie: Well, actaully, na’ yet. I’m still workin’ on tha’ subject. Sean steps heavily torward the three of them. They all turn around and stare at him standing there. Sean: Then why are ye sitting aroun’? An’ na’ out talking t’ me sister abou’ it? She’s ga’ t’ fokin’ ‘ave a damn clue. Annie looks at Sean. She knows what he is going through, it’s no surprise. Seeing his older sister and twin, settled, pregant and happy. The other twin is feeling the same pull. It is a common thing with twins, no matter how far appart; they remain connected. Annie: This time ye ga’ me. Ye slept late, I didn’na wan’ t’ wake ye when ye were sleepin’ so well. As of late ye ‘ave been talkin’ in yer sleep loud enough t’ ‘ear ye aross the rooms. Sean smiles at her, then looks at the twins. Sean: Ye two ‘ave anymore coffee ready fer me? As he speaks, Kailey, Annie’s hand madien appears from the Kitchen of the hotel room with a tray and two new cups of coffee and some breakfast items. She balances enough food for 4 people on the tray and a thermos pitcher of coffee. Sean reaches to help her with what looks to be a over load for a girl of her size. Kailey: Its ok, Prince Bres, I ‘eard the shower in your room start up and I figured now that you were awake. you would be hungry. Her eyes shoot over to Annie. who is just standing there, as she sets the tray down. She courtseys to Sean and Annie then turns back to the kitchen. The twins move on the food like to ravinous animals. Assorted scones and turn overs start to disappear as the large hamhock size hands of the younger, yet larger McClancy boys start to take them. Sean grabs them by the scruff of the neck and pulls them back. Sean: Yer manners are somethin’ to be questioned! Ladies first, ye meat’eads. Annie, ye might be wantin’ t’ get some befer these two, anyways. Otherwise Kailey will be bringin’ ye a separate plate of food just so ye can even eat t’day. Annie smiles coyly at him then moves to take her plate of food. Annie: Thank you, my Prince. We were talkin’ abou’ th’ matches this week. Ye ready for this? It is a big week for the Knightmare brigade. Na’ only d’ ye all get a shot a’ th’ Regal Rumble prize, bu’ yer younger brothers also ‘ave a shot a’ the UWF World Tag Team Titles. They ge’ t’ face Slasher an’ Benjamin well befer ye get yer ‘ands on Oni. All in one night. Then next week, ye get to go and represent th’ UWF and the KnightMare Bridge in the I-pay per view match fer the PTC Extreme title. If ye’ve na’ ‘eard, Moraless ‘as vacated th’ Title. Sean: I had ‘eard tha’. The gobshite really fokin’ better be in tha’ match so I can finally beat ‘is arse from one side t’ th’ other. Annie: Well, ‘e’s na’ in it. Migh’ I add tha’ yer th’ only returnin’ champion, me Prince. Th’ offical list in an’ Elimination Chamber match, ‘as all new commers t’ th’ match. And only one Champion returning and only one from th’ UWF, ye could bring the honor o’ that title back t’ the UWF, also me prince. This week could bring ye great honor. This will look good fer ye when yer ready to take th’ next step, And the other two scrolls return with their answer. Sean: This week is goin’ t’ be a full week. Oni isn’na a slouch, bu’ then again I am na’ t’ worried about facing ‘im either. I ‘ave faced tougher. It seems ‘is little girlfreind keeps ‘im pretty distracted. Which means ye’ll need t’ keep yer eyes on ‘er. Na’ many do understand th’ Bell t’ bell rule. An’ I will na’ LOSE due to someone getting’ involved a’ th’ fokin’ wrong time. Oni and I will both ‘ave t’ try t’ save ourselves fer th’ later matches as will th’ twins. Honeslty, I be wantin’ a shot at tha’ World belt more then Oni’s arse in a sling. Riogh’ now everyone ‘as the mark on them, until I know who sent Tropical Storm th’ video, everyone is me target. Annie nods her head, standing, listening to him speak. His deep voice, his thick Irish accent, has her in a trance like state. She knows it is the effect of being so close to him that causes it. But she won’t let herself get too caught up in that. She can’t allow herslef to. She is assigned to him as his manger by orders of the courts and Lord Irshram himself, she is bound to obey. Even though at one time, she was much closer to him. Sean: Are you ok Annie? Kailey! Sean Reaches his hand out to her and grabs her gentlely but firmly by the arm, pulling a chair out for her with the other hand, guiding to it. Annie: Yes, sorry. It ‘as been a busy few days. Trying t’ keep up with this many matches I don’na see ‘ow yer sister ever did it. She is a remarkable woman putting up with wha’ she did with Ian. How did she ever learn to work with him and keep her feelings so locked away? I wonder? Sean looks at her half understanding, half surprised at her question. As Kailey runs out with a glass of ice water for Annie. Sean: I don’na know. Ye might ‘ave to sit and talk with ‘er one day soon. Maybe after th’ new twins are born, ye can get a audiance with ‘er, an’ give ‘er a ‘and at th’ same time. Annie: I would like that. This past year ‘as changed everyone, Includin’ me. But I am glad to be ‘ere now working fer you, instead o’ against ye. It keeps me busy. Sean: An’ ou’ o’ trouble, I hope. All in the room give out a laugh at that last comment. Annie staying out of trouble? Is that really possible? Fade to Green
