UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #163

2004 RP#1!

8/1/2004

Joshua Dunn — UWF Complex

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[New Year’s Day has come and gone, and with it the year gone by. Now we face a new year, of untold fortunes, mishaps and down right unfairness. Of course all that is to come, and no one knows in what order. At the end of last year, Ruff Ryder, NA Champion, won out the contest of champions. Close second, only by a narrow three count, was Ezra Kazee. All said and done, it really has nothing to do with the man at hand, apart from the man that came in second, is the stable mate of Joshua Dunn, IC champion. As for Dunn, he has taken it easy over the UWF break over Christmas. Although not visiting his family does not mean they have been far from his thoughts, almost the opposite in fact, especially when it comes to one man in particular, his father. One image from his past, robbed from him by the local NVA Company, was that of his father, that’s all that has escaped the void so far to our knowledge. But now the UWF is back in session, and the time of reflection is passed, the time of action is here. On the first SAS of 2004 Joshua Dunn teams up with the rest of his stable, including the estranged Mike Montgomery to face the dwindling Bunny Army of DOOM~!!! And on that matter, the man in question surely has some words to say] [The Scene Opens, and focuses on the name in front of the camera in yellow letters backed onto wood painted red, the name reading “Joshua Dunn”. The camera pushes on, through the door and into the interior of the room. Sitting on the sofa, leaning towards the camera, elbows on his knees is the Communist Scotsman mentioned earlier. His open leather jacket and the stomach section of his red t-shirt sag beneath him due to the unavoidable forces of gravity, also swinging slightly backward and forward is his gold chain he wears round his neck, communist emblem shining in the room’s light. A wry smile passes over his face as he turns a wrist to face himself before shortly replacing his arm to its previous position. He looks at the man behind he camera] JD: Well, nice to see you’re on time… Well near enough [The smile vanishes from his face as he gets down to business, his hands rise up to his face, his thumbs bearing the weight of his face, the rest of his spurred fingers resting over his mouth, as he begins to talk the hands move again, as he leans back and folds his arms, crossing his legs as he places his feet on the coffee table] JD: The contest of Champions was an interesting one wasn’t it? You gave me quite a shock Ezra; you’re quite the fiery fighter when you want to be. [Dunn rubs his left cheek where Ezra slapped it in the first round match. Returning to how he was before, adjusting it for his own comfort, he continues] JD: Hat’s off to you on that matter nice to see someone shake up the odds somewhere. Then again we don’t always get what we want. For instance, so far our “commander” as it were, hasn’t got the match against the Prime Alliance he promised us. Then again, we do get to have a hit between us at Laguna and the remnants of his rabbit army, I for one want to get a shot at him for costing me my chance to go for the World Title all those months ago in that match. Very underhand it was too I have to admire him for that at least. You can’t lead an Army without being ruthless. As for Ryder, that’s another matter, sitting there with his World title shot, as cosy as pie. He hasn’t even had to defend his NA Title more than 3 times since he won it, but, as I hear, that’s about to change isn’t it. Or so I’ve heard at least. The New management of the UWF don’t hang around to shake up the old regime. [Dunn smiles, almost predatory, as he slips out that statement, as if he knows something that few else know. It sticks on his face as he reflects on that matter, almost dreamily. Snapping back into focus, Dunn sits bolt upright and faces the camera] JD: Well I do have other matters to attend to [He looks at his watch again almost beside himself, as if he’s forgotten something] JD: I bid you farewell [Dunn gets up and rushes out the door, obviously out of sync with his schedule, no one likes unpunctuality. With it’s object of focus gone, the camera fades out to black]
