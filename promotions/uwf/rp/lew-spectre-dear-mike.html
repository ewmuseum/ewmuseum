UWF (2001–07) Roleplay

Dear Mike

12 Feb 2005

Lew Spectre

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[It’s been a long time since Hot Mask made an appearance on UWF Television.] [This is his first appearance in a long time, in fact.] [He’s seen better days, though. The tire marks going across the white face of the mask show that. And apparently the face beneath the mask has become Styrofoam.] [Hot Mask’s “head” rests in the palm of Lew Spectre’s hand, staring back at the wrestling legend with dead, blank eyes. And Spectre stares back into them with cold, dead eyes of his own.] [His blonde hair falls down around his head in wet, sweaty clumps. A towel is draped over his shoulder … his chiseled chest rising and falling rhythmically under it. There’s no shirt, but Lew is back in the tights he wore for his entire career … blue tights, a black ‘V’ design going down from the waist, “Symbol of Excellence” drawn down each side.] [Spectre sits on the bench in an empty locker room … just staring into the disfigured mask.] “Does he bore you as much as he does me, ‘Mask?” [Lew smiles sadistically.] “Dear Mike. “Your profiling is more ignorant than you are. “And you’re even more boring than I remember. “You truly think I CARE about what the public ‘wants or needs’ ? “I get that you’re Canadian, so maybe this isn’t the easiest concept for you to understand. Me, Mike … I’m American, born and raised. The biggest haven for Democracy on the face of the planet. “And there is no bigger example than American for just how stupid the “public” really is. They have no concept of what they want. They have no concept of what they need. “I do not play to the simpletons. I do not care what they want, or what they think about me. For a man that’s faking a multi-million dollar corporation around him, you’d think you could do your research a little bit better. “I don’t pretend to be a businessman, Mike. That’s one of many, many differences between you and I. I don’t try to be more than I am. Because I know what I am. “I am a wrestler. “Do you really, for one SECOND, think I do not know what I’m doing here? “Are you really ignorant enough to think this is just another ‘wrestling return’ ? That I’m so simple that the only thing I have driving me is some ignorant, childish desire to run back for ‘one more run on the top’ ?” [Spectre sends Hot Mask flying across the room.] “ARE YOU REALLY THAT STUPID YOU INSIGNIFICANT CHILD!? “I don’t care about being at the top anymore, Mike. I transcended that. I’m a LEGEND. I’m everything you dream of being every miserable second of your miserable little LIFE. “And you’re so STUPID that you think the only thing driving me is ONE MORE GOD DAMN CHAMPIONSHIP!? “I have more respect for the World Heavyweight Championship than any man alive, Mike. But it doesn’t drive me anymore. Guess what? I’ve had it six … fucking … times. There is no man alive who has felt the weight and the responsibility that comes with that championship more than I have. No man who better understands what that position means. “But six times is more than enough, Mike. The World Championship is not my goal anymore. The main event is not my goal any more. “Me? “Mike, you ignorant little child. “I am on a suicide mission. “One more run on top means nothing. “One more championship could not possibly mean any less. “I am 36 years old, Mike. I can hardly even walk without this brace on my God damn knee. My face is scarred. My elbow clicks when I move my arm. “I’m a millionaire, Mike. Not like you … not this phony, childish game you play with private jets and foundations to give you a better image on camera. I was the marquee player of this company for over four straight years. I’ve never come off this company’s payroll. I am a millionaire because I fought every day of my God damn life, televised or not, for this company, and gave up everything I had. And for it, I received enough money to rest easily when I was done. “But no matter how much money I have, my nose is always going to bend at an angle from the night Al Crud shattered it on the side of a cage at March Maelstrom. I will never have it fixed. There are things men need to keep for themselves as reminders of what they’ve been through. Something a person like you could never, ever understand. “My best days are beyond me, Mike. It has been years since I wrestled day in and day out … not since that night against Crud. “You really think a man like me – a man that managed to stay at the apex of this business for years, a business so polluted and convoluted with backstabbing, politics, and everything else – is so simple that I can’t understand that? That I’m so childish I think that I can run back whenever I feel like it and compete at any given level? “You probably do, Mike. Because you’re never going to be smart enough to understand what makes this business work. It’s why you’re always going to be a nothing. “Like I said, Mike … I’m on a suicide run. I understand what I have and what I can do. I understand who I am … what I am. “I’m the personification of the past. I’m the catalyst of the future. “I change the status quo. “I only have so long here, Mike. I intend to make the best of it. “Hot Mask was first. He was the simplest, and the easiest. He offered absolutely no redeeming traits … childish, simple, mediocre at best ability. He was a cancer. Everyone understood it, but no one was willing to do what was needed to rectify the situation. “So like any cancer, I removed it. One quick movement and he was gone. “Tsunami Oni? Do you really think I’m entertaining the concept that I’m going to return after years of retirement, the broken down retiree that I am, go head to head with the elite superstar this company has to offer, and just annihilate him and take his title!? “Oni is phenomenally talented. More than any man I’ve seen in a long, long time, Mike. “But he’s lacking. “He’s lacking a lot. “He doesn’t get it. He doesn’t get the business. He can have all of the talent in the world, but Tsunami still has a ways to go before he can stand among the titans of the business. “He still has a ways to go before he is a man. “I can’t do this anymore, Mike. I can’t be the star now. I can’t be the main event. “But I can be the catalyst. “I can be the surgeon, eliminating the cancer. “Pushing the right buttons. “I can take a sweeping glance across this business and see the people that have it, and the ones who don’t. And I can affect the right changes in the people that have it. “And I can break the ones who don’t. “I know what you think of Kenny Mercury and Jamie Shock. You don’t think, Mike … you take the most obvious answer. You think I need henchmen … allies. “And that’s why no matter how much you try, Mike … the Player’s Club will always be a JOKE. An afterthought. No matter how much you try to emulate my success … no matter how much you try to pretend to be the Youngbloods … you lack the vision to see what’s most important. The Youngbloods always meant more than winning, Mike. I surrounded myself with the future. Because I knew I could be that catalyst even then … and even though I only had two hands, I could use them to spark that change. Effect them enough to be more than just my allies … to be ME. “You could never understand that, Mike. You lack vision. You lack understanding. You lack everything it takes to be a MAN. “So where do you fit in, Mike? The cancer … or the future that needs that nudge in the right direction? “You’re nothing. “At all. “You just bore me. “You are going to be an example, Mike. Nothing more, nothing less. You have no place in the future. You don’t understand this business … you don’t understand what it takes to be one of us. And it’s why you never will be. You’ll never be one of the elite. “You seriously think the Hall of Fame decides that? “You might end up in the Hall, Mike. That’s what happens when the business falls to shit like it did over the last year. When the only thing this business has to focus on are people like you … and Joshua Dunn … and Ezra fucking KAZEE. “Even Tropical Storm was put into the Hall of Fame, though. What you don’t get is that no one cares about Dunn. No one cares about Kevin Starr. No one cares about Ezra Kazee. “AND NO ONE CARES ABOUT YOU!! “You’re never going to be among the elite, Mike. And I’m going to show you that on Sunday. “Because after years of retirement, I probably won’t be enough to put down Tsunami. “But I could have both my arms amputated, and I would still be MORE than enough to eliminate you. “And that’s the difference, Mike. Because at the end of the day … you just can’t compete at this level. “I’m going to outline the difference. I’m going to show you how far down the ladder you still are. “I swear to fucking God, Mike, I’m going to hurt you. I’m going to break you. “I’m going to scar you. “I’m going to break YOUR nose. “I’m going to put YOUR knee in a brace. “But you’ll get plastic surgery to wipe away the scars. “You’ll have your nose reset. “You’ll have your knee repaired. “You’ll pick yourself up. You’ll dust yourself off. You’ll tell yourself it was all a fluke, and it doesn’t mean anything. “And you’ll sweep the reminders under the rug. You’ll hide from your past and lie to yourself. “And the scars … and the pain … and the things men use to remind themselves of just what they are will never matter to you. “And that’s why you’ll never be me, Mike. Never. “Because it’s about more than mindgames. About more than titles, and championships, and awards … it’s about more than victories and action figures and paychecks. “And you’ll never understand that. “I’m a wrestler, Mike. “And you’re nothing.” [End Transmission.]
