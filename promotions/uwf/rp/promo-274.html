UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #274

The Return

5/3/2004

Mystery Opponent — Appartment Block

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[No boring monologue, there’s not much to say, perhaps this could be the most irrelevant piece of UWF TV ever] [The Scene Opens in what appears to be the 3rd storey of an apartment building, this is made apparent by the large three on the stairwell that we have just entered through. In the corridor, we can’t see much, although there is a tapping of shoes on concrete as a man climbs up after the camera man, the man appears from the door wearing a smart black suit and tie, he seems to be a little exhausted from the climb. He removes a handkerchief from his pocket and mops his brow before passing the camera muttering something about elevators, he moves on down the corridor and the cameraman follows] [He arrives at a door, and swiftly knocks on it, causing a disconcerting rattling noise as he does so. As the cameraman catches up we see the mangy door, almost bare of paint and the room number almost falling off, with a pale representation left by them the colour of the original door colour. We can hear a small groan from inside, but no one comes to open the door and no one calls out. Slightly exasperated the man hammers on the door again almost causing it to fall off its hinges, this time however the man yells out to enhance the urgency of his call] Man: Mr Wead, open up I have come to discuss matters of money. [Now we can hear a flurry of activity behind the door as several things are moved, lifted and generally shifted around, one large bang is heard and a large amount of inaudible cursing, but still no one comes to the door] Man: It’s to do with your Job [This however, does insight a reaction] Voice: Job? What Job? Man: You and your associate are members of the Universal Wrestling Federation are you not? [In the dawn of recognition the voice replies] Voice: Oh…. That job [Still no one answers the door though, even though there is an almighty crash of furniture from inside, met with more swearing, the man outside, growing ever more impatient speaks again] Man: Yes, may I come in? Voice: Erm… just a second [Another flurry of activity is heard and a movement of tins, bits of plastic and paper can be heard before finally the door is unlatched by the person inside and swung open slightly so only one bloodshot eye becomes visible. The face can only partially be seen, but we get the impression of somebody who has just woken up] Man: Mr Wead? Face: Yes Man: Mr Smokey Wead? Smokey: Yes that’s me, now who the hell are you? Man: I represent the UWF, on some matters concerning you contract and I… [He doesn’t even get the chance to explain himself fully, the door is slammed shut again and locked on the inside, almost in complete shock, the man hammers on the door again and yells out again] Man: Mr Wead, We are not terminating your contract. [From inside Smokey yells back to the man] Smokey: We still have one? Man: Yes, you were never officially fired nor did you officially resign, meaning your contract is still valid [There’s a small pause, the door doesn’t move but Smokey yells out] Smokey: I’m listening Man: Haven’t you been wondering why there have been large sums of money being deposited in your bank account over the last few months? Smokey: I thought I had a lot of rich relatives popping off Man: No, that was your salary that the company have been forced to pay you since your contract was never terminated. Now can I come in please? Smokey: Give me one good reason Man: If you do not let me in the company will sue you and your colleague for breach of contract, leaving you bankrupt Smokey: We’ve been like that before Man: Think of it this way, no food Smokey: We get a great deal at soup kitchens Man: No fancy cars to swan around in Smokey: We can walk Man: No more money for your… substances Smokey: I’ve been meaning to quit Man: No more wild Parties Smokey: We get an awful lot of invites Man: They’ll repossess your Ghetto blaster [This time there is a pause, and the door creeks open; a skinny little man occupies the door wearing a grubby white vest and a Hawaiian shirt and baggy jeans, his long hair is matted and he smells strongly of… cigarette… smoke.] Smokey: Come in Man: Thank you [The man walks into the flat, with the cameraman in hot pursuit. The room can only be described as a dump, stuff litters the sitting room covering every available space, and that’s all you can describe it as, stuff, as it is hard to tell where the clothes and stereo speakers end and the floor begins, party poppers and silly string are also littering the mess. Obviously this is the aftermath of their last Party, as a strange odour fills the air and some three women lie unconscious on a rather oddly designed sofa wearing barely anything, probably what they came in wearing. Smokey spoons some of the… stuff… off of an arm chair and sits down facing the man who is still standing] Smokey: Pull up a speaker [The man looks around for a while and unearths a speaker from underneath a pile of stuff and sits down on it] Smokey: So what exactly are you here to tell me? Man: I have been instructed to tell you that unless you show up for a match on this week’s card your contracts shall be terminated, and you shall be sued for breach of contract, basically because the company needs the money after Mike Montgomery won his court case Smokey: If you need the money, why don’t you just sue us just now? Wouldn’t that be a lot simpler? Man: Well… yes, but there is just one slight problem to that… Smokey: And that is? Man: Well you and your colleague, since you were still part of the roster when the new management was inducted, you are both part owners of the company [Smokey just looks blankly at the man for a moment before replying] Smokey: So, we employ you? Man: Technically… there is a special management team, but the wrestlers in it now own the federation, and under the new revised contracts you have to be active members, i.e. regularly on TV to stay in the federation. So now we have to see you on TV or your written out of the management agreement and we can then sue you for breach of contract. Smokey: But If I’m an owner, can’t I fire you? Man: No, well not yet anyway, a wrestler can’t fire another wrestler, unless they hold a managerial position, and right now, your teetering on the verge of expulsion by Mike Montgomery. Smokey: Hey I know him; he’s a wrestler, so he can’t fire me! Hey I think I’m getting the hang of this Man: No, he can fire you, he has a managerial position. Smokey: What? Man: It was part of the agreement of the court case I told you about Smokey: So to become part owners, Bubba and myself have to appear on SAS? Man: Yes, and on a regular basis. [Smokey, drops his head forward and begins massaging his temples] Smokey: This is making my head hurt Man: And you are notoriously hard to keep track of. Smokey: So if we show up on SAS, we become part owners and I can fire you? Man: Well… No not even then, you’d need a managerial position Smokey: So what’s the point? Man: You get a share in the companies profits. [Smokey’s eyes light up, as if someone just gave him the presidency of Cuba, his eyes could have been replaced with dollar signs, if this were a cartoon that is] Smokey: Profits? Man: Yes, half of the profits made are divided among the owners of the UWF, the better we do, the better you do. Smokey: So what match on SAS are we in? Man: Ahh, so you’ve agreed? [The man reaches into his inside jacket pocket, instinctively, as he comes from a background of drug related shootings, Smokey dives over the back of the armchair and lands with a crunch behind it as the stuff there crumples on impact. The man pauses for a moment after looking on, and produces a folded piece of paper, and holds it out to, a now rather embarrassed Smokey Wead. He moves around the chair, and gingerly takes the paper, half expecting it to explode on contact; he sits back down and reads it, his eyes darting back and forth over the paper] Man: The match your in, has been unofficially dubbed the Prime Alliance Invitational, seeing as only one of the named tag teams is officially a member of the Prime Alliance. You will be thrown in there along with every other pair of wrestlers that team up and run into the ring. [Smokey looks up from the piece of paper and gives the reporter a quizzical look for the umpteenth time] Smokey: So we’ll be in this match? Man: Yes, you’ll be in this match or your contracts will be terminated Smokey: Democracy at it’s very best I see [He folds up the paper and hands it back to the man] Man: Yes, and I have to insist that both of you turn up, where is your partner by the way? Smokey: Over there [Smokey points over towards the outrageously coloured sofa with the scantily clad women on it, the man just looks back, almost disappointed at Smokey] Man: I meant your wrestling partner Smokey: Yeah, my partner’s over there Man: Bubba? Smokey: Of course Bubba, who do you think I was talking about? Man: Bubba’s a woman? Smokey: WHAT? Man: There are only those three women over there Smokey: Are you blind man? Bubba’s over there with them Man: Surely you don’t expect me to believe- [Smokey cuts the man off by yelling] Smokey: BUBBA, get up you lazy bum, this man wants to see you [There’s a rather masculine grunt that comes from the three ladies, startling the man, the sofa, or what was presumed to be a sofa, shudders and starts to rear up. The unconscious women slide off and slump into another pile of stuff. A head appears on the sofa man, which starts looking dazed and confused. Bubba looks around and even under his Hawaiian shirt, before finding his fez, which he promptly places on his head and stands up slowly and makes his way over to the pair] [Bubba moves along, clambering amongst the assorted stuff, and stands, all 6 ft 6 of him beside the small man in the suit, who whimpers slightly, looking down as the man looks up bubba’s stern face looks blankly at the man, who’s eyes water as they look back to the 600lbs excess giant. The look holds for a moment before Bubba’s face explodes into what could have been a friendly smile, if it weren’t for his teeth bearing a striking resemblance to that of a predatory animal. In effect it only makes the man whimper louder] Smokey: Honestly, do you need glasses Mr? Man: Ah, um, ah… [The man still looks up to bubba almost transfixed by the shear size of the man] Smokey: Bubba, he says we’ve got a match against some guys [Bubba breaks the hypnotic trance he’s holding the man in and looks over to Smokey] Bubba: Match? [The voice echo’s, not because of the rooms acoustics, but just because of the deepness it booms out with, and it also sounds as if He’s having trouble speaking the language, or just speaking in general] Smokey: Yeah, can you do me a favour and put some pants on? I’ve just had breakfast [Bubba, the man, and the cameraman all look down at the same instant to reveal that, yes indeed; Bubba is as pantless as Jerry Kohler, the sight is probably not the one you might want to see first thing in the morning, and as such, this narrator is not about to even attempt to describe it, needless to say, both the man and cameraman look away almost as quick as the first looked on] Bubba: Sorry [Bubba moves off, head lowered and trails out of camera view, which swings back to Smokey] Smokey: That’s Bubba for you, Looks intimidating, but is really just a big teddy bear; I think that’s why he’s such a hit with the ladies Man: Tha..Tha…….. The Ladies? Smokey: Yeah you know [He points back over to the still sleeping women which were previously perched on top of Bubba] Smokey: They can’t keep their hands off him [Not even attempting to argue with Smokey this time the man simply shrugs off the thought] Man: So you will be on SAS? Smokey: Sure, we could do with the fresh air; I mean you can’t inhale cana-, I mean, you can’t stay cooped up in the same place ALL the time Man: Great, I’m glad to say that your contracts will be saved [Smokey turns around and yells through the apartment looking away from the man] Smokey: Hey Bubba, while your back there, find our ring outfits will you? We’ll need them soon Bubba: OK [Smokey turns around and then looks at the man again, there’s motion from the pile of stuff that the three women are lying on, as the shouting seems to have roused them] Smokey: Well as you can probably tell we’ve got lots of stuff to do, and maybe you should get the camera out of here before they find out it’s here, you know what women are like Man: Yes, I suppose. Well we’ll be seeing you again Mr Wead Smokey: Yes I think you shall Man: A pleasure doing business with you finally [With that he holds out a hand and Smokey takes it shaking it vigorously, maybe because he’s anticipating his return to the ring in competitive wrestling, or more likely he’ll think that the man will go sooner that way, either way, the camera fades out of that image]
