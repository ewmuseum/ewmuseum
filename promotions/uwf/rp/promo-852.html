Short Lived But Duke Retires From The UWF

1 Jan 2005

Duke Williams — His Green Trailer

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He’s not a saint, vampire, wealthy jerk, musician, communist, or narcissist. As a matter of fact he’s nothing special, just you’re “A” typical average guy. He likes whiskey, playing Nintendo, getting high, and looking at porn on the internet. As matter of fact Duke Williams is very content with that being his daily routine. Well at least it was for him until recently. They say once a lady gets the black dick she never goes back. I mean how could they after they up graded from bic to sharpie it change’s the whole “size” perspective. The same thing happened to Duke, sorta. Three weeks ago Duke went over to Larry Johnson’s, a friend of his from three trailer homes down. Larry always has the best weed in the park and Duke was fixin’ for a high. Being polite, Duke went into Larry’s trailer and saw his big screen plasma TV that Larry was bragging on. It changed “The Malice Man” forever. How could he watch “Sanford and Son” on his 19” Daewoo after seeing “The Golden Girls” on a 52” flat screen HDTV Mitsubishi!! He couldn’t. To make a long story short the “Malice Man” has all his funds tied up in “special interests”. Mainly being alimony, child support, and stocks. He’s letting his money work for him but little does he know his money is lazy and an alcoholic. So when Duke went to the bank to get the four grand to buy the TV he fell short. Not wanting to pawn his world titles and awards from past promotions he decided to write a book. That’s what all old wrestlers do when they are strapped for cash since wrestling fans are losers and suckers. Duke figured he could pull in twenty to thirty thousand dollars after publishing fees and taxes. That surly could land him a big screen TV with out having to wrestle which he dosn't want to do. So Duke opened up Microsoft word, poured a large glass of whiskey, and cranked up the Waylon Jennings. The music played, the whiskey faded, and the word program remained blank. Duke had what we writers call inspirational constipation and the last few years of heavy drug and alcohol consumption didn’t help any. So left with no other alternatives and a burning desire for a flat screen TV Duke decided on returning to wrestling. Duke signed a per appearance deal with the UWF, put on his tights, got hammered at a local Mexican restaurant and showed up to wrestle. The thing is the guy he was to wrestle Larry Mercury or some shit was a bit of a pussy. At least that’s how the Malice Man perceived him as the guy backed out of a fight. This did Duke no good as he only got paid a mangers fee since he didn’t have to actually wrestle. That didn’t help him towards his goal of a plasma TV since all that money went to paying the tab at the Hispanic watering hole next to the Addison Red line El stop. That brings us to now, Christmas day where we find the old brown couch which has seen its better years. Kind of like Duke who lays on it in his white underwear eating natural vanilla ice cream out of a Byers half gallon container watching reruns of the A-team on his pathetic 19” TV. “God damn it Guthrie why don’t you buy your own fucking icecream,” yells Duke. Guthrie is Duke’s best friend. He is a small, racist, pug dog, with a nasty attitude and a craving for ice cream. Guthrie gives Duke a “fuck you” stare with his big black eyes as he backs out of the ice cream with some still on his nose. “You don’t share your food with me,” Duke provokes the dog, “I don’t see you offering me any dog food you fat prick.” Guthrie replies with a few swift licks to his balls almost to taunt Duke who’s sticking his spoon in the same vanilla goodness Guthrie just licked. Duke ignores the dog as a TV spot catches the aging eyes of the Malice Man. “Attention: Do you want a BIG TV,” booms the announcer like he’s selling used cars. That line alone almost makes the Malice Man wet himself with excitement but he somehow finds a way to remain composed. Duke learned never to trust a TV commercial again after finding out Count Chocula is a homosexual. He does however pick up the clicker and turn the volume up loud enough to disturb the dog from his rectal licking pastime. “How about a giant 52 inch, flat screen plasma, high definition television free” Duke darts up from his laying position on the coach to watch the TV ad with the greatest of concentration. Had God possessed the television to show his love to Duke? It had to have been a miracle or some cheesy gimmick. “This is not a cheesy gimmick,” the announcer boasts, “this is a promotion for K-billy radio and we’ll give this TV away to the first caller at 1-800-Hill-Billy right now!” Faster then the General Lee speeds with a crooked cop chasing it in Hazard County Duke runs to the cordless and dials up the contest line of his favorite radio station. A few rings later Duke yelps with such excitement Guthrie runs out of the room thinking the world was going to end. It is official Duke was the first caller and the winner of the 52” TV ending his Hierarchy of Needs. With the TV Duke can go back to his simple life of retirement from wrestling and not have to worry about working. Just Duke, his cat, and his kick ass TV.
