Sword Dance Part 1 [Regal Rumble]

16 Aug 2004

The Real Deal — Real Deal's mind/Other

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Regal The word regal means to be like a king. It is an insignia of royalty and can be an emblem of someone in a high office. In realtion to the upcoming Regal Rumble taking place at Terror in Tokyo, it really shows that this match is going to be one of very high improtance. The winner of the match truly gets to show that they are worthy of being 'king' and maybe can be as they go onto face the champion. Champion Champion status relates to royaly because if you are champion, you are the one who excels all others. The same case being with royalty also. The winner of the Regal Rumble gets their chance at the top superstar, the one who excels. They get to show that they are worthy of being champion and show that they are better than everyone else. Real Deal is Regal...Real Deal will be champion... For the first time, The Real Deal will step into the ring and participate in the Regal Rumble. His thoughts on the match are most likely very different from the others going into it. For The Real Deal, this match is not to get a chance to become champion, it is not a chance for him to show off his skills. The Regal Rumble match in Deal's eyes is solely to show that he is the best. He does not need a shiny title around his waist in order to do this but rather survive the onslaught of the rest of the federation. When Deal wins he will go up against the champion but not for the gold. He will go into the match to show that he excels over the rest of the UWF. Accustomed to the taste of gold If Real Deal goes on and wins the Regal Rumble, followed by the UWF championship, he will feel no different in his career than he does now. Back in the days of DWO he held both the Extreme and Intercontinental Titles. Both of these belts were highly acclaimed and not far off the value of the World title. After a while of winning titles and winning more titles, Deal has become bored being known as the champion, all he wants is victory... Picture Time Deal awakes, finding himself laying on a large floating rock in what seems to be a cave. Being used to these weird places popping up on him he has no worries about standing up and taking a look around. The rock is a perfect square shape, roughly 10 metres by 10 metres. It is brown and black in colour and is far from flat, alot of it has mini craters and hills. Deal walks to the edge of the rock and looks over the edge into the wide expanse of nothing below him. Before he gets a chance to turn back he is startled by the sight of a red orb ascending infront of him. The red orb visits Deal frequently, giving him advice or taking him to far away place that he could never go to normally. Of course these are dreams but they prepare Deal for what is to come in life. The begins to flicker slowly and moves within inches of Deal's face. The inside of the orb can be seen through it's translucent body. It is like a molten lava texture which fills the orb to the top. On the other side a small green eye rotates around to the front to look straight at Deal. Within seconds the orb speaks to Deal, it's voice one of optimism and joy... 'Deal you have come very far since our last meeting. Now is your chance to be on top of the world and show everyone who is the master.' The orb always referred to Deal as being the master of evil, but also the master of all things. Deal replies with a grin on his face... 'The Regal Rumble is what you speak of?' The eye on the orb blinks and a picture of the UWF ring can be seen. It continues to speak... 'Real Deal, you have been in the UWF for a very short time and yet you have been Television Champion and have had your shot at the Extreme gold too. You should be very proud.' Deal sighs as he doesn't think the orb understands how he feels... 'This is only the beginning of my reign of terror in the UWF. Though championships are not what I seek. I just seek domination over all my rivals.' The orb's eye reappears and grows larger... 'I want you to tell me what you think each time I show you a picture, whatever comes straight to your head...' The orb brings up a picture of a burning building. Deal takes a look at it, a feeling of warmth comes over him. He also feels a little worry so he replies to the orb... 'I would have to say tragic.' The orb blacks out the picture and brings up one of a person being hanged. Deal feels sick as he remembers the incident with Kaiowas... 'I've been there so the word has to be suffering.' The orb blacks out the picture and speaks... 'This is the last one Deal.' A picture of Deal's wife who has been missing for a 1 year is shown on the orb. Deal steps back, trying to look away from the woman who walked out of his life. Anger boils up inside him as he remembers the last day he saw her... -Flash- Broken Glass, Shattered Dreams... Time seemed to switch into slow motion as The Real Deal's wife, Erin threw the glass vase across the living room of their home in Houston. She was in tears and destroying everything in her path. The vase connected with the wall and broke into multiple pieces sending Deal reeling back. Erin looked straight into the eyes of Deal and began to yell with much fury... 'You son of a bitch! You're never home, even when your on vacation, you're never home! What the hell do you do? Find yourself some strippers to get yourself off on?' Deal really had no idea how to explain himself. When he wasn't on vacation he was out on the road with the various wrestling federations he had been a part of. When not on vacation he would be out with his friends, at clubs and restaurants, catching up on old times. He knew within himself that his wife had a point. Every night without fail she would sit up and wait for him, wishing that he would come home and keep her company. It wasn't that he didn't want too, it was just that his schedule was just so busy. Any chance that he got to spend with her was a god send to him as his mind was in constant termoil with his work load and social needs. It was true that he needed to go out, something had clicked in him one day that made him hate staying in the house. This is all Erin wanted to do, she wanted to curl up in bed and watch a movie or television. It was clear to him now that their marriage was going haywire. He moved forward to try and embrace her but was met by a barrage of items being thrown at him. At that point he was too shocked to do anything else. Months and months of anger that had built up within his wife were all coming at him at once. It wasn't the throwing of items that was hurting him but her words and tone of voice. She spoke once more while still throwing items such as clocks, books and CD players... 'I'm so sick of this Will! I'm so sick of not seeing you, I'm so sick of you coming in at good knows when in the morning, only to tell me your too tired to do anything! You are not the man I married!' These words hit Deal right in the heart and the realisation kicked into his mind. He thought to himself... All this time I thought she was alright with it... All this time she never said anything... All this time she could have said something! For the first time during the arguement Deal harnessed the power to get a word in during his wife's tyrany... 'Why didn't you tell me?' Erin stopped moving, her head snapping round the give Deal a look of death... 'Wasn't it not obvious to you? Did you not hear me crying all those nights? If you didn't...fuck...you! This is over!' Erin broke down into uncontrollable sorrow, her eyes swollen from the tears and her hands shaking from the rage inside. She stormed out of the room and a large slam was heard from the front of the house. Deal stood, not able to move, his eyes fixated on where his wife stood mere seconds before. He observed the damage throughout the room before placing himself down on his couch and placing his head in his hands. He softly spoke to himself as his eyes started to swell... 'And so begins my descent to death...' -Flash- Deal looks straight at the orb as his mind wanders back to the present...he stares at his wife and pulls the orb closer...he speaks... 'More than one word for this...love...hate...anger...over...' He pushes the orb away and turns his back to it. The orb remains in the same place but it's voice rifles through the ears of Deal as it speaks deep and loud... 'You must not let these things phase you Deal...if you do failure will be associated with you. Failure is not in your vocabulary...forget the past and move onto the future...' Deal slowly turns back round and faces the orb once more...A small smile appears on his face as he tries as hard as he can to block the past. With much more happiness he exclaims by shouting out to the whole expand before him... 'I am the future!' With this the orb emits a blinding light which takes out Deal's vision...This is most likely not the last time the orb will appear though...it shall return... -End-
