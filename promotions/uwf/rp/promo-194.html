UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #194

Forgotten

27 Jan 2004

Moraless — RP#1 WNU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What is it to be forgotten? Not in the true sense as noting is every really forgotten, we can say that we forgot to pick something up at the store but that doesn’t necessarily mean we did forget truly, it would just be in some lost memory store in the back of our minds. People can feel forgotten though; they can be around for ages, doing what they do yet no credit being given to them. No sense of acknowledgement for the hard work and effort that is put in. Instead you are simply put to the back of their minds, an after thought you may say. Moraless knows what that’s like. Moraless: They’re joking right? They have to be fucking joking. Moraless cries out in what can only be described as pure rage and anger, throwing a glass vase across the room which shatters into numerous pieces scattering about on the floor a small segment of glass flies back and cuts Moraless’ arm causing him to become even more agitated, it’s a wild guess but he may not be happy about something. Moraless: So let me get this straight, I appear at Supremacy, less than a hundred percent but willing to get the job done, to go out in front of the crowd and face Ben Jericho in a match which would normally push me to my absolute physical limits, a match which I was worried about competing in originally. Then came the mighty Ben Jericho, the unforgiven one as I remember him, sauntering to the ring with his head in the clouds not giving a damn. Hell I can’t put my finger on it but it was almost as though he didn’t even try to win as though the match meant nothing to him. Moraless clenches his fist as he swings out wildly to his right connecting solidly with the wall, his fist simply bouncing off it, not giving any give a small trickle of blood forms from a split between his knuckles and begins to seep its way down his hand. Moraless: Which is why it surprised me when I had a look at the Underground card, just imagine my surprise, my unbridled joy when I see that Ben Jericho had been placed ever so willingly in a title match against Bruce Gordy, now whoa hold on a minute surely there was something wrong with that sentence and I’m not talking about that joke Gordy being THC champion either. Let me get this straight, this is a company where you are rewarded for losing, hot damn I wish I knew that the first time round I would have lost all those matches I had with fucking Chaos. Moraless begins to pace up and down, a clear look of aggravation on his face, his teeth grinding together both fists still clenched tightly, so much so that his knuckles are turning white with the pressure that is being pushed on them, then his demeanour changes his look of anger fades as he slumps down almost limply into a large brown arm chair carefully situated in the centre of the room which appears to be a study of some sort, a large desk covers the south wall looking out into a beautiful garden. Moraless: Have I really been that much of a let down to everyone? Do they really think I don’t deserve to be here? It’s not a question of needing work here, more a question of wanting to work here. There have been other opportunities but I want to prove myself in the UWF, the last time I was here I blew my shot at the THC title, I tried my best but Kenny Mercury proved too much for me, that I understand but I appeared at the contest of champions, I just beat former world champion Ben Jericho so does that really constitute this? Moraless holds up a piece of A4 paper, which has clearly been faxed to him, UWF’s logo in bold black in resting at the top of it. As you scroll down the piece of paper it becomes apparent that the piece of paper is next weeks card, Moraless’ face just peering over the top a distinct look of dejection in his eyes. Moraless: Here have a nice long read of this, UWF newcomer battle royale. Yep newcomer I’m someone that held the television title and has already had a shot at the THC title, just over 8 months ago, but it gets better I’ll carry on reading, with no TV Title to fight over, our newbies go at it in a shot for the THC gold! So let me get this straight because they got rid of a title I used to hold they are giving me the next best thing. A shot at the next lowest belt. My opponents Axel Action a man that can barely string two words together and Raid someone I know nothing about so I can’t begin to judge him, it’s not my style. Moraless screws up the piece of paper tight and throws it into a waste paper bin which is situated just under the desk in the study, he stands up from the arm chair and moves towards the door, pulling it towards him and leaving via it, into a long hall way numerous doors either side of the corridors as he begins to walk down staring straight ahead. Moraless: There is one prospect I do not favour heading into this match, something that is worrying me somewhat, the person who is Iron Lung, an old LOW competitor that in itself indicates that he is something to be feared. Without heart or feeling going into this match what I have to ask myself is do I really care what the result is? Should I just enter the ring and lie down, at least that way I would have a reason to be forgotten.
