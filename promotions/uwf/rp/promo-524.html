UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #524

The Beginng

6 Jul 2004

'The Shocker' Dave Storm — UWF Headquarters

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((The scene opens up at UWF Headquarters. The camera notices a black cadillac escalade entering the main parkinglot and follows it until it parks and the back and driver's seat doors open. Out of the driver's sear comes a fairly tall man in a white t-shirt with the word 'SHOCKINGLY XTREME' across the front and long jean shorts. He goes to the back of the car and reaches in, retriving his black gymbag. He hauls the gym bag over his shoulder and closes the doors, heading for the entrance. He walks in and comes up to a large desk with two chairs on on side. One is occupied by an old lady, the other unoccupied. There is various papers spread all over the desk. The man stops infront of the desk.) The Man:: 'What is this?' Old lady::' The Universal Wrestling Federation.' The Man:: 'Obviously...I mean who are you?' Old Lady:: 'Ohhh, I'm Edna. Do you have an appointment?' The Man:: 'No, I'm new to the roster.' Old Lady:: 'Oh?OH! Oh yes, I remember hearing about you.. Let me look over some papers and you'll be all set.' The Man:: 'Alright, I'll be right back.' (The man heads toward the bathroom and when he comes back he finds the old lady at the desk doing a crossword puzzle with the daily newspaper.) The Man:: 'Did you get the papers?' Old lady:: 'Yes, it's right here.' (The lady lifts up the newspaper.) The Man:: 'NO! I mean the my frickin' contract papers.' Old Lady:: 'Ohh, hold on sir, there hear somewhere.' (After rumaging through the papers on the desk she finds a thick packet and hands it and a pen to the man. At the top of each paper reads 'The Shocker' Dave Storm-UWF Contract'. He signs the dotted line of each page and hands the packet back to the old lady. He continues on down the main corridor until he stops and asks a man for directions to his dressing room.) 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Hey, dude. Do you know where Dave Storm's dressing room is?' Backstage Official:: 'Uh, yeah. I think it's down there(points down a hallway).' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Alright, thanks.' (Dave begins to head down the hallway where his locker room is when he heres the Backstage Official holler his name.) Backstage Official:: 'Hey! Dave! Mr. Storm!' (Dave turns around as the Backstage Official jolts toward him. He haults a few feet infront of him.) Backstage Official:: 'I'm Jon Focker, I'm an interviewer here at UWF. I believe this is your first time at a UWF function, if I'm not mistaken?!?!' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah. Just got signed.' Jon Focker:: ' Well, would you please grant me an interview?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, but let's make it quick.' Jon Focker:: 'Sure. Well, I understand your fresh out of World Cyber Championship Wrestling. What are your goals in the Universal Wrestling Federation?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'I always have only one thing on my mind. And that is WINNING. I live by the phrase DTA. I can be your best friend, or-the more likely choice, your worst enemy.If I don't get what I want, I can become a sadistic MothaTrucka! You cross paths with me, it's not going to be a pleasureable experience. Mark my damn words.' Jon Focker:: 'What about people like Moraless, Digital Mortallity, Seymour Almasy who are UWF legends and PrimeTime Central vetrans?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Quite Frankly, I don't give a damn. You got your Seymour Almasy's and your Digital Mortallitys, but I don't care. They don't phase me. The only thing I care about is winning championship title gold. Nothing else matters to me. I'm going to win at all costs.' Jon Focker:: 'For the better part of your career, you've been a tag team wrestler. Do you have any intent of entering the UWF tag team division?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Hell no. That's like asking an ex-con if he wanted to go back to prison. I've been through alot as a tag team competitor, and I'm finished with it. Everytime I've trusted another person in the ring, I've ended up screwed. And plus, there's noone out there that can run on my level. After four years of being a tag team wrestler, I've only had one tag team championship!ONE!And in the same time frame, Four singles titles. What does that tell you?' Jon Focker:: 'Well, it gives me an answer. So, who are you targeting first in UWF?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm::' Whoever my first match is against. Noone here impresses me, noone here can compete on my level. When someone can prove to me that they are worthy, then you can check back with me. You may think, wow Dave your all talk. Well you know what, I don't give a damn what you think, you Focker, because your not the next little boy who is going to get his ass whipped by the Shocker. Your no threat to me, and quite frankly none of mine or your colleagues are, too. I'm going to show everyone during my stay at the Universal Wrestling Federation just how Shocking I really am. My actions are unpredictable and soon you'll all know my name. I'm going to work my way up through the underground and become a damn LEGEND. Go ahead, damn Focker, laugh. You think I'm kidding? You think this is funny? Just wait until Next Wednesday night. You just wait, Focker. Soon you'll know what the hell I'm talking about. It's nothing personnal, it's just BUSINESS!''' (With that line, Shocker heads off toward his locker room as the scene fades.)
