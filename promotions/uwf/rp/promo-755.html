UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #755

Destiny

23 Oct 2004

Adien Pryde

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Scene opens to a black screen. Then slowly the following appears.] “The following is paid for and approved by Adien Pryde and The Chaos Society.” [Slowly the words fade out and then the following fades in.] des•ti•ny n. pl. des•ti•nies The inevitable or necessary fate to which a particular person or thing is destined; one's lot. A predetermined course of events considered as something beyond human power or control: “Marriage and hanging go by destiny” (Robert Burton). The power or agency thought to predetermine events: Destiny brought them together. [As those words fade out a voice begins to speak, the voice of Adien Pryde.] Pryde’s Voice : Destiny is a powerful word. Many people don’t believe in destiny, they feel that they create their own fate. They feel that there is nothing other than their own abilities and decisions to drive them to their goals. [The screen shows images of Anarchy in various matches against other Tag Teams as they racked up loss after loss, never achieving the Tag Team Championship.] Pryde’s Voice : Being a Tag Team specialist at the time, my destiny turned out not to be in the Tag Team division. I heart will still always be Reo Dai and our search for the title, but after his injury in my Stretcher match with Nick Persia, I set my sights on a singles title. The title that the person that my opponent that night, Nick Persia held, The Hardcore Championship. [The scene changes to more pictures from matches, this time they are singles matches of Pryde and Persia in the long term feud over the THC. Scene after scene shows Nick Persia getting the better on Adien Pryde in every match.] Pryde’s Voice : Again, I would have my hopes crushed by the Dutch Destroyer and I would never attain my goal of holding The Hardcore Championship belt. After holding my own in the lower card I started to get the admiration of the fans. I began to understand how many of the greats such as Tsunami Oni and 'The Common Man' Ezra Kazee could act as they do just for the admiration of their fans. [The scene begins to change to pictures of Pryde winning more and more matches and him interacting more with the fans all ending with his win over The Plague in their best of 3 falls match for the Intercontinental Championship.] Pryde’s Voice : Now is the time of my destiny, and my destiny is the Intercontinental Championship. It has again come full circle and my opponent is again Nick Persia. Nick has pulled some of my best matches out of me, and I need to wrestle better than I ever have before. The Tag Team Championship was not my destiny, nor was THC Belt. I truly feel that The IC belt is my destiny. It is my chance to make my make here in the UWF. [The scene shifts to the Intercontinental Championship.] Pryde’s Voice : Tomorrow I will prove to all my fans what they mean to me by winning the belt. Then and only then will they truly understand how they have helped my true self come out. This match is for all of you. [The scene fades to black.]
