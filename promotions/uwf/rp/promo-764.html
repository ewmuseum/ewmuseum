The World's Worst Political Satire

26 Oct 2004

Bunny Army of Doom — Alexander

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We are… We are… We are coming… …uh…back. Yeah…we are coming back…so prepare and shit…or something. Is all the spacing and … annoying you? …Good… Lost is never easy to deal with. Different emotions seem to pull at your strings until you feel ready to snap. However, lost in a war is something unimaginable by people except for the ones who had actually loses. You must retreat and lick your wounds, vowing to get your revenge one day. The nights are the worst. You never sleep, and when you do you see yourself losing looping over and over like the background to old cartoons. But in time all wounds do heal and you find yourself rebuilt. It would be foolish to rush back in to extract revenge, no nothing has changed and surly you will feel defeat once more. While you had to rebuild your enemy only got stronger. So now changes must be made to unsure a victory. Change is, however, dangerous. You must consider the options when thinking about change. Is the change going to be good or bad? It may seem like a simple answer but you never truly know. Inside the walls of Alexander castle in a tower deep in the center Laguna sits at his desk and looks over paper work. Knock, knock His study is interrupted by the sounds of someone at the door. He looks up from his desk to see the door opening. In walked his good and loyal friend Kiros. Laguna rose and greeted Kiros. “Good to see you Kiros, how goes the troops training?” Kiros walked by Laguna and stood in front of the window that looked out over the courtyard. From there you could see the main social area for the troops where they commonly spent their free time when not training or working. At the moment the courtyard was full of hustle and bustle as it was in the middle of a break between training and work. “Kiros” Laguna asked again, feeling more concerned as his friend seemed to be staring out into space “is everything ok?” A deep breath escaped from Kiros. A sad look draped over his face as he turned and looked at Laguna. “No my friend, they are not.” Feeling an eerie weight suddenly pressing down on him, Laguna took a seat at his desk. His poor soft eyes looked up at Kiros and begged “what is wrong?” “The troops” Kiro stated, looking back out the window, “they suffered great number of causalities in the war, many lost family. The lost has struck them hard. Most seem disenfranchised by the whole ordeal.” Kiros cold hard stare pierced into Laguna’s soul. “I think they could be discussing the idea of a revolting.” The last words struck him like a hot knife, cutting away at his pride. So this was the change they wanted, a new leader? But who, who would lead them? “What should I do” Laguna asked. His words spoke were from deep down, emotion rattling off each letter. Suddenly in a great commotion the door flung open. Laguna and Kiros snapped their attention from their immediate problems to this new intruder. Storming in like a bat out of hell, Wards stopped just inches away from colliding with Laguna. “I’ve got it!” he exclaimed between heavy pants of breath. “Got what?” Kiros asked dumbfound. “I know how to keep Laguna is control as Commander!” Hearing these words Laguna rushed to Wards and grasped his shoulders. “Tell me, how can I keep my soldiers loyal?” He shook Wards, unable to wait for the response. The ideas of revolution already had him on his wit’s end. Heavy pants filled the room as Wards tried to catch his breath. He was a large man and running up the many stairs to get to the office high in the tower was no easy task. “I was thinking about it as soon as I heard the rumors floating among the camp and I finally got it!” “Damnit man, just tell us already!” Kiros shouted, having lost his patience as well. A waggery smile spread from cheek to cheek on Ward. “We need the people to want Laguna to be the leader again and yet make it so they can not just as easily change their mind later.” He knew he was pulling at their strings by purposely dragging this out instead of giving them a direct answer but it was too much fun for him to stop. “How?” Laguna and Kiros asked at almost the same time. Casually Ward flexed and arms and placed his hands behind his head. Leaning back his smile only spread wider across his face. He turned his back to them as to leave but kept his head titled so he could see over his shoulder to them. “Let’s hold an election.” Laguna and Kiros nearly fell over in shock. “An election,” Laguna sputtered “how will that keep me as Commander?” Kiros added in “besides how can we get them to elect him when they already want to revolt against him?” “It’s simple, we just need an opponent in which there is no way they could possibly get elected over Laguna” Ward opened the door “and I know just the man for the job.” With a jolly laugh that came from the depths of his belly, Wards walked out of the room leaving both Laguna and Kiros stumped as to what Wards had planned. An election for Commander, nothing like that had ever been tried before. And more importantly, just how were the bunnies suppose to vote? What is this, Florida? Maybe if the author can get off his lazy ass he’ll write part 2 and we can all find out just who this mystery opponent will be.
