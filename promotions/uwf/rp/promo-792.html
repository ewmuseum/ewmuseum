UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #792

Recuperation

November , 2004 (year unrecorded)

Mike Montgomery — A Chicago Hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Clyde We open in a room of a downtown Chicago Hospital where former UWF Heavyweight Champion Mike Montgomery lays in bed, unconscious since the beating he received last week at the hands of Hardcore Champ and Dark Ages member Clyde. An attack to which one of several motivations could easily be attributed. Perhaps it was revenge. Perhaps Clyde sought payback for Montgomery's costing Seymour Almasy the North American Title. No, that can't be right. Does Clyde really think so little of Seymour that he believes that he has to fight Seymour's battles for him? No, that doesn't really make much sense now does it? Therefore the motive must be something simpler, something more common. Perhaps...perhaps it is the simple fact that Clyde is nothing more than a seven foot tall coward. Since his arrival in the UWF, he has attacked Montgomery from behind, trash talked him, claimed he was better...that Montgomery wasn't worthy of being in the ring with him, yet not once has Clyde done anything to back up a single one of his boasts. That must be it. Clyde, the seven foot former World Champ of Bush League PCW is scared of a six foot six cruiserweight. He doesn't have the guts to face Montgomery like a man. It's as simple as that. Clyde Despite a beating that would have cost any normal man hsi life, despite the severe head trauma Montgomery suffered that should've left him a vegetable, he is actually in surprisingly good shape at the moment. Okay, bandaged up and a tad unconscious, but considering the life threatening situation his employer left him in about a week ago, you've gotta admit he's doing good for himself. At his bedside for every day this past week sits the NASA-engineered super-intelligent monkey known simply as 'Spiff.' Manager...business partner...friend. All words that could easily describe the little guy, and really all words that do in fact fit him, he has sat dilligently by Montgomery's bedside, conducting business by cell-phone and keeping a constant vigil over his friend. 'So I called the lawyers over at Asylum Industries' legal department today. Once they get a hold of the booking sheets for last week's Supremacy, they figure an unsafe working conditions lawsuit will be a slam dunk. 'Course it'd be nice if you were able to enjoy this with me. The way those people keep trying to screw you over, you'll be owning the company by New Years.' No worries buddy, I hear you. Starting now, everything's about payback. Clyde...the company that tried to kill me...soon everyone's gonna get what's coming to them. 'Hell, right now I'm not even sure what they're going to do about your match tonight.' My what? 'Triple threat with Joshua Justice and Digital Mortality...and here you are in a coma.' You have got to be kidding. 'I mean seriously, who let's the UWF's biggest draw get beaten within an inch of his life, then books him in a match with a rising star and a hall-of-famer...' Digital Mortality...I need time to recuperate, but this is a great going away present. Some how, some way, I'll be there. He broke my leg. Cost me my first World Title. That's payback that's been waiting for far too long. 'And who the hell do they think they'll replace you with anyway? Clyde? The man makes Ruff Ryder look like Main Event material.' I may be going out for awhile, but I'm going out with a bang. Digital Mortality is a dead man walking...I feel sorry for Joshua Justice though. He's simply a poor kid caught in the cross-fire. 'I just wish you could be there. I just wish you could show them what they're losing.' Oh I will... 'Afterall, you're the main event, you should be there.' I will be...and when I'm done with those two... 'Someone's gotta put this right. Starting with...' Clyde
