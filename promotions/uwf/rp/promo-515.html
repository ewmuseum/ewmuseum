UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #515

Decisions Decisions

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. As the camera pans in Ryder is seen holding the black t-shirt he was given at Stable Wars. Ryder has a huge decision to make, but what side will he choose. He slowly raises his head from staring at the shirt and looks toward the camera. A look of intrigue is on his face as he begins to speak his peace. ) Ryder: Join the force? Don't join the force? What is a Thrilla to do? I mean on one hand I could join up with four of the baddest men that ever hit a wrestling ring, but on the other hand I could go solo and start my own stable. Before I get to the decision though I gotta ask myself a question. Why me? I mean of all the people on the roster they chose to throw the shirt to me. Guess being in the Order awhile back paid off in more ways than just being part of a dominant stable while I was an up and comer. In the end though there's only one thing I can say to Sean McClancey, The Fomorians, and Corporal........i'm in! * Ryder takes off the tank top he was wearing and puts on the shirt that was given to him and begins to fly the colors * Ryder: Now if that was a serious offer i'm in, and I hope that the UWF is ready for the carnage that this group will bring. You already saw what we can accomplish on Stable Wars. My opponents saw it first hand and so did Malcom Kennedy and Joshua Dunn. I'll slap anyone's teeth that dare speak a bad word about my new crew, because one thing that you can say about Ruff Ryder, i'm very loyal. I believe if someone offers you a position as great as this one you don't turn around and fuck em over when their back is turned. If you don't want to be down for the long run then you don't need to take the offer in the first place, so boys, i'm down for whatever. I'm ready to go whenever you say the word. ( Just then there is a knock on the door and Ryder gets up to answer. It is a man delivering a tan envelope. Ryder tips the man and then sends him on his way as he opens the envelope in front of the camera. It's the UWF card for Supremacy. Ryder's look goes from normal to pissed in like a half second as he slowly raises his head back toward the camera again. ) Ryder: What the fuck is this shit? * He holds the paper up * Ryder: Ruff Ryder vs Sean McClancey? Are you fuckin kidding me? Is this some kind of sick joke? You asshole bookers knew that I was offered a spot in the stable and yet you go and book us in a match against each other? You know what, you can take your little match and shove it straight up your asses because Ruff Ryder isn't gonna be the one to fight McClancey. I will no show if you schedule this match, and if I do show i'm layin down and lettin him pin me to prove that I do what's best for the group and i'm not all about individual shit. I'm not like that anymore, and you can just wait and see what happens at Supremacy. Early congrats on your win Sean. ( Ryder kicks the camera over causing the screen to go black and the scene to end. )
