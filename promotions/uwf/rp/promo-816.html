UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #816

Preparation

4 Dec 2004

Mike Montgomery — The Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One down...one to go. Acceptable results. I let the match go too long though, have to watch my emotions. Digital Mortality stole from me, and he's paid for it. Joshua Justice was the wildcard. I underestimated him. He's a lot better than people give him credit for. I'll have to be careful of him in the future, he's got true potential. One week before UWF FANdemonium, Mike Montgomery is in the downtown Chicago gym he uses for training...despite Doctor's orders that he partake in no physical activity. WIth two severe concussions in the last six months as well as his second skull fracture in that same time frame, this one his most serious to date, he's preparing to go into both his first Venetian Home Rumble as well as a sixty minute Iron Man match. Both involving the seven footer at the heart of Montgomery's thirst for vengeance, Clyde. At his side, or in this case perched on a nearby treadmill, is the super-intelligent monkey desperately trying to serve as a voice of reason, Spiff. Montgomery dances in front of a punching bag, rattling it's chain with furious rights and lefts, both punches and kicks. Spiff nervously paces back and forth on the treadmills handlebars. 'I'm telling you Mike, this is NOT a good idea! You can't do this!' Montgomery continues to work away on the punching bag, completely ignoring the Monkey. Punch..kick..punch punch 'You're not ready for a regular match with Clyde, forget an Iron Man!' Punch...kick kick...punch kick 'I mean hell, your *skull* is cracked! This match could kill you!' Still, Mike continues to ignore his friend's pleas. His focus on the punching bag is almost obsessive. Sweat pours down his forehead as he relentlessly punishes the bag with blow after blow after blow. 'Listen, I know the fans decided this and you don't really have much say, but there's gotta be a way to get around that! Just give the word and I can have our lawyers on it in an instant!' I can hear what you're thinking, All your doubts and fears, And if you look in my eyes, in time you'll find, The reason I'm here. You're right buddy, the fans did make this match, and that's WHY I have to do it. They've given me a gift. Sixty minutes alone with Clyde. No where he can run to, no where for him to hide. That's a gift that doesn't come often. 'Mike, you've GOT to back off on this!' For some reason, this is what stops Mike. The ex-champ catches the bag, staring at the small monkey as though he has only now realized that he's there. Once the punching bag stops moving, Montgomery steps around it, slowly walking toward his long time friend. His face barely masking the shock and rage at Spiff's suggestion. 'Back off? Clyde comes into MY company...costs me MY title...interjects himself into MY part of the show...shows me nothing but disrespect in my own house and you tell me to back off?! I did NOT get to the top of the Universal Wrestling Federation by backing off when things got risky and I will NOT start now! And in time all things shall pass away, In time, you may come back someday. To live once more, or die once more, But in time, your time will be no more. 'Clyde has taken away everything important to me and right now all I have left is revenge. He will not get away with his crimes and the UWF's fans have handed me my revenge on a silver platter!' 'But this will KILL you!' came Spiff's immediate protest. Mike takes a deep breath in an effort to calm himself, 'I know. It doesn't matter though. I would rather die knowing I'd fought for what I believed in, then lived knowing that I let my path in life be dictated by another person. What would you have me do? Play it safe and always wonder what if?' 'No...' Spiff answered. Mike leaned down to his friend's level, 'I gotta do this. I get Clyde in my kind of match. The kind of match where I can do some real damage.' You know your days are numbered, Count them one by one, Like notches in the handle of an outlaw's gun. You can outrun the devil, if you tried, But you'll never outrun the hands of time. 'What're you talking about?' 'I don't think Clyde saw Terror in Tokyo. Kevin Starr's career is probably over. Joshua Dunn? He may never regain consciousness and why? Because of things I did! I have one solid hour to beat on him in any way I see fit, and I will NOT be stopped!' Spiff still appeared confused, 'Look, I can almost understand that, but what the hell was that thing with Oni last Sunday?' 'That? It's not that hard, really. Tsunami Oni, Nick Persia, Malcolm Kennedy and I all have one thing in common. We both love and respect this business. We know that the wrestling business is what made us who and what we are, and that is WHY we love and respect the business as much as we do. It made us and we give back to it every single time we step into that ring. That's something that people like Clyde or Seymour can't ever begin to understand.' 'Respect?' Mike nods to his friend, 'Respect.' 'What about your tag title match?' 'Tag title match? Who's my partner?' 'You don't want to know.' And in time all things shall pass away, In time you may come back some say. To live once more, or die once more, But in time, your time will be no more. 'It's HIM isn't it?' Spiff simply nods his head. Nodding in understanding, Mike returns to working over the punching bag, a renewed sense of fury punctuating every blow. Well that's always the way of things around here isn't it? It won't matter, really though. After FANdemonium, I'm done. I'll finally be able to rest...finally find peace. But in the meanwhile, I'll need to train harder than ever. Sic vis pacem, para bellum. If you want peace, prepare for war. I can hear what you're thinking.
