UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #743

Live and Loud P3

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For Nick Persia Live and Loud Four will be a night to remember. In his mind, he is already holding his North American Championship! In his mind……..but in reality he has nothing yet. But that’s about to change really fast. Nick Persia is sitting next to Larson before a table promoting Live and Loud four in the town where it is held. A lot of people have gathered, or more likely were already there, to see Nick Persia! Or Larson of course. Larson: “Nick, it’s great you can be with us now!” Nick Persia: “Not a problem at all, not at all.” Larson: “This week, on SAS, you received and delivered some ass kicking. What’s up with that?” Nick Persia: “Some people thought it was necessary to teach me a lesson or send me a message or whatever! But as they found out, in the end it is always me outsmarting them. Take Killer Dude. He takes me out in the middle of the show, but at the end of it I delivered him my Destroyer. What could he do about that? Nothing! He was just another victim. And there will be more of them of course….victims.” Larson: “Recently you’ve had multiple matches with your friend and mine The Final Fantasy Seymour Almasy. You’ve already stated that you are looking forward to this encounter, but you also said this wouldn’t be the last.” Nick Persia: “You can’t settle the differences between another in a six man ladder match. You need to do that in a match from man to man. He will probably be afraid to do that, especially after he has felt my wrath for a second time. Seymour used to be a great wrestler with a heart and much courage but nowadays he has lost it. Talking about Yevon and such. What the fuck is Yevon?” Larson: “I’m not actually sure, but he’s a God or something in the video game.” Nick Persia: “Well, yevon is as much fantasy as Seymour’s chances of winning a title are! You see, I would love to do nothing more than win a title, but if I need to prevent him from winning a title and somebody else escapes with the title I am fine with that as well. Although Seymour is a weird guy he has a name, for being ridiculous, but still a name! So when I beat him I will get even more fame!” Larson: ‘And if you win the North American Title as well nobody will doubt your skills!” Nick Persia: “You are catching my drift! Indeed, they will see how good I really am. They will see how I am going to beat Killer Dude. They will see how I am going to beat Seymour Almasy. And they will see how I am going to beat Misfit.” Larson: “Misfit, I always like to call him Miserable Fit.” Nick Persia: “I won’t repeat all the names I gave him, but I swear they are a hell of a lot worse and efinitely more insulting. But there are little children here so don’t say too much harsh comments! But Misfit is a straight up guy and I can appreciate that. Although he sounds like a pussy to me at some times. But he remains one of the few wrestlers who fight fair and square. As it is supposed to be.” Larson: “And Moraless? He’s not a talker, but you have been in the ring with him before.” Nick Persia: “He’s definitely a guy to look out for. He’s the current North American Champion and, that’s something I haven’t accomplished yet! But he has one little tiny problem and that is that he is English. Just like Jamie Shock! And I hate Shock so for a bit I must also hate Moraless. But he is a nice guy, for an Englishman that is.” Larson: “The Black Star revolution knows one other member beside Killer Dude and that is Kenny Mercury.” Nick Persia: “The technical wizard himself. The very definition of precision. But as good as he is with his technical wrestling, it isn’t going to help him much in a hardcore match. What’s he going to do? Put me in a submission hold? And let someone else grab the belt? He will have to do things hardcore. He hates hardcore. I like hardcore. So we are going to see Mister Mercury if this is the code that can break you!” Larson: “Or else the Dutch destroyer will help doing that.” Nick Persia: “You’re damn right.” Larson: “So you believe your chances of taking a title are good?” Nick Persia: “In a normal match you have fifty percent chance of winning a title. And I win almost everyone of them. Now there are three titles on the line in one match. There is just no way I am going to lose. Not one way. I will take a title. The question only is which one. And if it’s up to me, and it is, it’s going to be the North American Title.” Larson: “Seymour has said he would take the Intercontinental Title first and then he would take the North American title soon after!” Nick Persia: “Seymour can tell me all he wants but in the end it is just his word. And we all want something, but that doesn’t mean we always get it. Seymour can say that he is going after a title, but there are five other men that are really going to try and prevent that! And they are trying really hard to do so. Especially me!” Larson: “So you have basically set two goals for tonight?” Nick Persia: “Indeed, and I am going to achieve both goals. Both the first and the second one.” Larson: “And if you and Seymour both win a title?” Nick Persia: “Then I have partially failed. But I am not going to fail. I just know it.” Larson: “How do you know that then?” Nick Persia: “A bit more trust. You know that I always keep my word. You know that when I promise something I am really serious about it. I promise that I will succeed. Failure is once again not an option for me. I am going to do this, for me. Not for my deceased father or for my brother but for me alone. Because I’ve earned it. Just like everything else I have accomplished.” Larson: “Word has it that Misfit is out of this match, what can you tell us about this?” Nick Persia: “Buy the pay per view and find out yourself. But if he would participate in this match he could easily go out after this match as well, if you catch my drift again.” Larson: “You would……injure him?” Nick Persia: “Not me! But I can tell you a few names who would try to injure him. Kenny Mercury and Killer Dude. They have been getting it on him for so long it wouldn’t surprise me if they would injure him. They are cowards who would take out anyone in this match if they felt this would increase their chances. Of course, they are hopeless, but they are the only ones not realising that.” Larson: “Are you shooting back at Killer Dude’s earlier comments?” Nick Persia: “No, he’s not worth firing back at. He’s not worth standing in the ring with me and he’s not worth of holding a title. Believe me when I say will beat him. Believe me when I say all of his comments are nothing more than ordinary lies. Believe me when I say I am in this to win. Believe me! The rest will be just fine.” Larson: “if all of this is really true, you will either face Shock, renegade or Kennedy!” Nick Persia: “Whoa! That are some names. But as we all know I am the Dutch destroyer. I can take either one of them. But they all are one though challenge. Just like I am one.” Larson: “Do you feel you are alone in this match? Since everybody is forming cliques and stables and you are not in one at the moment?” Nick Persia: “They can bring everything they want to bring. But they need to remember one little thing. I am Nick Persia. I have no remorse. I have no regrets. Make damn sure, that I am more precious to you than your life is. Mark my words, all of you! Not just my opponents, but also the rest. Because I would really hate to say….told you so.” Larson: “Thank you for this interview.” Nick Persia: “No problem at all.” [Scene fades out as Nick starts to begin answering the Q&A-session with the fans and signs some autographs.]
