UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #831

Getting Ready for the CoC

13 Dec 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killer Dude: ROD DAMNIT!!!!!!! How in the fuck could this have happened to me? Losing three title matches in one night, and then, someone has the balls to steal from ME!!!!!!! It's apathy I tell you. Everyone just loves to turn their backs on me. Don't you see it? The greed for gold has consumed them, it has consumed people like Craig Wright, Kenny Mercury, and others. There is no kindness left in this god damn federation, just a bunch of low-lifes who have no chance to become anything. (The scene opens up with Killer Dude standing outside of a hotel in the Wisconsin area, chatting to no one in particular. A woman stands across from him, staring at him patiently, waiting for him to finish his spiel. She is wearing her work uniform, which reads 'McDonald's', right next to her nametag, named 'Jessica'. Killer Dude stops blabbing, apparently waiting for a response, but she continues to stare at him. She brings her hand through her hair, and looks at him, sighing.) Jessica: Sir, I'm sorry, but no matter what you say, I'm not going to get you a free cheeseburger. (Killer Dude shakes his head angrily) Killer Dude: Whatever happened to generosity in this world? Jessica: Are you trying to tell a joke? Killer Dude: Do you think I'm laughing? Do you know just who the fuck I am? (Jessica nods) Killer Dude: Then you know that I'll pay you back. Jessica: Killer, I'm sorry but if you don't have enough money to get your own cheeseburger, then you'll never pay me back. Killer Dude: This is the attitude of my generation. Jessica: What in the hell are you talking about? Killer Dude: I ask you, when did the UWF become a place of hatred and indifference? Jessica: I don't have the exact time, sir. Killer Dude: Fine, be a smartass, see if I care. (Killer Dude walks out of the scene) (Scene reopens as Killer Dude walks down the street) Killer Dude: WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU PEOPLE WANT FROM ME?!!!!!!!!!! I have little left to give this federation except my blood. My life of having extravagant amounts of money is no more. It has been systematically taken away from me. First, with the Tables, Ladders, and Cats match, then with the North American Title Match, which I was blatantly screwed out of, and the Venetian Home Rumble, and that fucking fat ass Communist. I now am in major amounts of debt, and I'm going to be evicted from my home soon, if my fortunes don't raise. The uncaring eye would see me as a man who has lost everything, and they laugh at me because they get to witness another great wrestler lose everything. So laugh at me all you want, Kenny Mercury. It will not soften my resolve. I do not need a piece of paper to tell me how to live. I just need to feel the air in my lungs and the wind at my feet to live. I have gone through too much, and I have lived through it all to care about things so trivial. That is the difference about me today, instead of the person that first came into this buisiness. I cared only for the almighty dollar. A big contract is only secondary now, because I have been there and done that, and to tell you the truth, it's not what it was hyped to be. You can have your goals of nothingness and I will keep my goals profitable. Don't get it wrong. My goals are ones that will make ME happy, not you. And that's all that matters. So, do not question my actions. I was given a contract, one that was being thrown in my face, with false promises and ideas attached to it. Kenny Mercury thought he was bringing back his savior, but he will soon learn wrong. I can only laugh now at the lengths that he went to, because all along I had no intention of working together with him. I am my own man, a man who doesn't need money or power to wrestle. I am here to perform, and do what I missed for so long. So when I took out Kenny Mercury at Live and Loud, it was to send a message. Excuses are excuses, and they began as soon as I showed up at the Regal Rumble, making dreams of stardom, just that, dreams. Talk your shit to each other all you want. Killer Dude is overrated; he is gonna get straight to the top without paying his dues. You know, the usual banter that goes on when one of the best returns. Well, here's a newsflash. Fuck management, fuck puppets, fuck backstabbers, fuck phoney friends, and fuck cry babies. I don't care if you like me. I don't care that Kenny Mercury tried to take my style. I don't whine about shit like this, because he has never proved to me that he can beat me. Where is the man who said that I am nothing, and boasted about me being stupid? Seriously.... could it possibly be Kenny? I spit in your face. The same man that once mentioned me in promos, and in television skits, like we were best friends, is now going to try to stop me in my quest to win the Contest of Champions? The fool spoke to me five times, and then thought that we could work together. What a dumbass. Kenny Mercury is worthless. I am eyeing all of these morons. It doesn't matter who it is, Kenny Mercury or anyone else, I don't use gay innuendos in my matches, all I will do is come out victorious. People are starting to feel sorry for you Kenny. I am going to go through you without even trying, and if you see my rap sheet and accomplishments you better be able to understand why. But I don't go around backstage proclaiming that I will win every match with ease. Just isn't my style, you know? I would rather just prove it. So strut all you want, make all the irrelevant comments you wish and wipe the sweat off of your face, because I am not here to lose to a pathetic, over the hill loser, who can't beat a 'lowly' superstar like myself. Rest assured, Kenny Mercury, everything I do is gained by MY OWN sweat and tears. So keep your's away from me. I am just a has-been, who will make you a never-was. I am just an asshole, who will do whatever he wants. I am a man who believes in the truth more than in hurting one's feelings. I am not a good guy. I have difficulty understanding moral standards. But at least I have some, Kenny. Be ready, for I WILL SEE YOU IN HELL BITCH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Scene fades to black as Killer Dude walks off into the distance)
