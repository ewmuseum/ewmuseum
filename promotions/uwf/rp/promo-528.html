UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #528

Rodents Rodents and more Rodents

10 Jul 2004

Raid — Rural town house

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see Raid’s van parked on the side of the street in a small rural community. There is some noise coming from the back of the van, as the camera man walks towards the back you realize that Raid preparing for another extermination job. He pulls out a large black duffle bag and sets it on the ground. As he looks up, he notices the camera and looks shocked.) ‘Bout time you showed up! You were supposed to be here an hour ago. Now your either gonna have to wait till I finish this job, or tag along. But if your gonna tag along I would advise staying out of my way, cause this house has a major pest problem, and major problems call for major solutions. (As he says that he reaches into the duffle bag and pulls out a 12 gauge shot gun and pumps the loading mechanism (in a very Ramboish fashion). Now its time to get down to business. You coming? (The camera nods as if to say ‘yes’) Good, you can carry these. (With tat he hands the camera man a box of ammo and begins to walk towards the house.) Cameraman “So what’s so special about this house that you have to use these EXTREME extermination methods?” Well, you see, the problem is a giant rodent infestation. And these rodents have been mutated by the toxic waste dump that is near by. I have already used the normal pesticides and they seem to have no effect on the furry beasts. So then I thought to my self ‘what should I try next’ and I decided to set traps. I came back later only to find out that the rodents had broken my traps. Upon further investigation of the possible extermination methods I concluded that there were only two things that would get rid of these infernal bastards 1. Blow the fucking house to pieces or 2. Blast the sons-of-bitches to hell with a shoty. I chose the latter. (The scene cuts to Raid inside the house where he is promptly unloading the gun into the masses of mice. Each time he fires there are a handful of mice that fly into the air and fall back to the floor creating a dead pile in the wake of the blast? This continues for a half hour or so.) (Outside of the house some hour or so later.) Cameraman “Did you get them all?” Did I get them all!? What kind of stupid morons do they hire for this job? Of course I got them all, that’s my job you idiot! Cameraman “Oh, right. On another note, how do you feel about having to defend your title against Kenny Mercury this Sunday? I feel fan-flippin tastic. I mean how tough is it gonna be, he’s a frikin human lobster. This is gonna be one of my easiest matches to date, probably even easier than my match against Kazee to win the belt in the first place. Here I’ll give you a rundown of how the match is gonna go. The bell rings, the fans start chanting my name, I then execute a text book knock out punch to Kenny’s char-broiled face, he falls down, I pin him. There’s nothing to this match. Honestly can you tell me that the walking dead is going to put up a fight. I don’t think so. Now on a day that he is healthy, yea he’d put up a damn good fight but not this time, he’s to badly injured, hell Mercury you had better just stay in that hospital and rest up boy. I’ll tell you what if you no show on Sunday, that’s cool, and I’ll even give you a shot at the belt when you recover. How’s that sound! (Raid starts to pack up his equipment. As he is putting stuff away he picks up a bag that he did not have with him before. The bag is white but is stained red. He lifts it off the ground and holds it for a second before he dumps it onto the ground. There is now hundreds of dead rodents lying on the street in a pile.) Mercury if your not careful your gonna end up just like these pests. You say that your gonna use me as an example of what your gonna do to Misfit. You just had better hope that I don’t decide to use you to test out my new extermination tactics. Take my advice and stay in the hospital and rest up. But if you do decide to step into the ring with me just be fore warned I won’t hesitate to put you into the Bug Bomb and EXTERMINATE YOUR ASS. (Raid hops into the van and fires it up. He takes off and the camera focuses in on the pile of dead rodents as the scene fades to black.)
