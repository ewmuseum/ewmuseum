UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #155

The march to greatness continues...

17 Dec 2003

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We fade in to the twisted, barren landscape of Hell’s Carnival. The camera makes its way past the grotesque trees and statuary and approaches the enormous bonfire we last saw Pyretta Blaze cavorting before. Today, there are three silhouettes standing near it. As we approach, we see it is Pyretta, The Quiet One, and Beelzebozo. They appear to be roasting marshmallows. On reeeeeeeeeeeeeally long sticks. I mean, really big and long. Like tree branches, really. You get the idea. Pyretta is just pulling out her marshmallow, burning intently to a cinder. She smiles with nearly-psychotic glee as she watches it burn. Beelzebozo: “You know, hon, you should really try TOASTING one of the marshmallows. They’re really good that way.” Blaze: “What fun would THAT be? I want them burnt to a crisp…that’s the way they SHOULD be!” As the flames surrounding the marshmallow die down, she deftly pulls the burnt, crispy outer layer off and pops it into her mouth before returning the core of the marshmallow to the fire to reignite. Beelzebozo: “This explains your habit of making toast with a blowtorch…” He slowly retracts his enormous ‘mallow stick from the fire to devour the marshmallow impaled upon it, and then places another near the flames. “Still, I’m glad I let you talk me into this eternal bonfire. It’s really made our cookouts fun!” Blaze: “You can trust my judgment on all things flaming!” They both look at The Quiet One and giggle. The Quiet One flips them off, causing them to laugh some more. Suddenly Beelzebozo drops his stick and leaps on the mime. He lifts him over his head and starts carrying the flailing mime toward the fire. “Honey, drop him now! He was playing around! He didn’t mean it!” Beelzebozo turns to look at her, then looks up at the mime, who violently nods at him. The clown shrugs, drops the mime onto his stomach, and returns to his cooking. The Quiet One slowly picks himself up and dusts himself off. Beelzebozo: “Watch your fingers, Quiet One, from now on.” Blaze: “You really need to relax, dearie.” Beelzebozo: “How am I supposed to relax when the Hardcore Title is being bandied about by worthless pansies? How can I rest knowing that the children are weeping to see what’s become of the THC division? How can I let my guard down for even a moment, when I constantly hear their cries echoing in my mind?” Blaze: “You’re hearing children crying now?” Beelzebozo: “Yeah, but maybe that’s just coming from the maze. I put up those ‘No Trespassing’ signs but do the little bastards read and obey? Hell no! Well, it’s their funeral.” The young woman clears her throat, and the clown pauses to look at her. Blaze: “In other news, once more I offer you congratulations on a job well done! Kennedy looked like he’d been in a plane crash when you got done with him!” The Quiet One nods in agreement. Beelzebozo: “Thanks hon. But really, it was nothing at all. That poor bastard never stood a chance. Hell, I didn’t even need to bust out my finisher!” Blaze: “I was meaning to ask about that. What happened to the Last Laugh? And what was with that crazy submission hold you put him away with?” Beelzebozo: “You think I was really going to waste my finisher on a loser like Kennedy? It is to laugh!” He does so. “As for that hold…I dunno. It just kind of came to me. I think I remember seeing some wrestler years ago use it. But then, that might have been a dream I thought was a TV show. It wouldn’t be the first time.” Blaze: “Well, the important thing is, you’ve taken the first step toward reaching the goal of getting a shot at the World Title! Wow that was hard to say.” Beelzebozo: “I suppose. I’d rather be making my move on the Hardcore Belt though. I have a feeling I’m not going to get a shot until next year now.” Blaze: “Chin up, buttercup! You’ll get it soon enough! Let’s concentrate on this tournament!” Beelzebozo: “I hope my opponents are a little more talented than that last one. Geez, it was almost no fun pounding him into the mat like that…” Blaze: “Um, about that…I heard that he’s still in the tournament.” Beelzebozo: “Oh, is it double elimination?” Blaze: “Well, no. Apparently he ran down during a later qualifying match, and pinned the guy, and won a spot in the quarter finals.” The clown turns and stares at her, unblinking, for so long that eventually she starts to shuffle her feet. The Quiet One continues eating marshmallows. Beelzebozo: “And they actually let this go? The officials let this ruling stand?” She nods. “Wow, that’s perhaps the most ridiculous thing I’ve heard in a great while. If I’d known it was that easy I’d have run in on the last THC defense and pinned someone. I didn’t realize we had such loose rules here in the UWF.” Blaze: “You and me both. It’s like that southern rapper guy…” Beelzebozo: “Outcast??” Blaze: “I meant ludicrous.” Beelzebozo: “Oh, yeah. Right.” Blaze: “Anywho, he’s back in the tourney now. So you may have to crush him again.” Beelzebozo: “Oh, THAT’LL be hard. It’ll be as easy as puking on a log after an all-night bender. Or something. Hell, I bet he doesn’t even get into the semis. Anyway, who’s my first opponent?” Blaze: “Some schmuck named Ruff Ryder.” Beelzebozo: “Ah yes, the flaming homosexual who stars in gay porn and tries to bugger every man he wrestles. I’ve seen him before. He’s pretty funny, although I’m not into the buggering part.” The Quiet One and Pyretta exchange puzzled glances. “What?” Blaze: “I don’t think that’s who you think it is.” Beelzebozo: “You sure? Ruff Ryder…I mean, yeah, that’s the guy. I’m sure of it.” Blaze: “Honey, you sure you don’t mean the Bum Boozler?” Beelzebozo: “I…wait, hold on. I think you’re right. That WAS the Bum Boozler. So, Ruff Ryder’s, what, his gay sidekick?” Blaze: “No….I think he’s a biker.” Beelzebozo: “A gay biker, like the Village People guys?” Blaze: “No!” Beelzebozo: “Oh, come on! You’re pulling my leg! His name is RUFF RYDER ferchrissakes.” Blaze: “I know, but seriously, he doesn’t assrape anyone. That I know of. Not that there’s anything wrong with that.” Beelzebozo: “There isn’t?” Blaze: “Honey, remember the times we live in, and that we’re on national television.” Beelzebozo: “…You’re absolutely right! There’s nothing wrong with forced anal entry at all! I do it all the time!” He then immediately slaps his forehead. “I didn’t just say that. Oy. THAT’S gonna come back to haunt me, isn’t it.” Blaze: “ANYway, this Ruff Ryder guy isn’t the anal intruder.” Beelzebozo: “You’re SURE on this?” She nods. “Well then. That throws my whole game plan out the damn window. I was counting on my need to keep from being sodomized in the ring by this guy, in front of millions of wrestling fans, to push me beyond my limits and secure my victory. Now what am I supposed to do for motivation?” Blaze: “You could still PRETEND he’s the butt-humper…” Beelzebozo: “Not now I can’t! It’s like Santa—once you know the truth you can no longer pretend! The gibblies no longer run down my spine! You ruined everything, dear!” Blaze: “Well, then, how’s this…his name is Ruff Ryder. How tough can he be? Hell, he probably makes Kennedy look like Vin Diesel in Pitch Black.” Beelzebozo: “…Hey, good point my little habanero!” She giggles. “I knew I could count on you to make everything right again!” He leans over to smooch her. “All right, so I first bulldoze my way through this Ryder guy. What next?” Blaze: “You smash the next two guys, and get your title shot!” Beelzebozo: “Sounds simple enough. I think I can handle that. Any guesses as to who those two will be?” Blaze: “Not really. It’s really a matter of luck and skill, and I don’t know who here has it. All I know is that, so far, they’re too embroiled in their own little vendettas and feuds to even mention you.” Beelzebozo: “That should really insult me. However, if they’re as distracted with their petty little squabbles as you seem to think, it might make things even easier than they were for me. I mean, Kennedy tried to look past me, and see what happened to him!” Blaze: “He stole his way back into the tournament?” Beelzebozo: “Well, yes, that too. My point was, he underestimated me and got schooled. The same will happen to the rest of the chumps here, and by the time they realize what they’re up against it’ll be far too late for them to stop me. That title shot will be mine!” Blaze: “And then, to ring in the new year, you can begin your bid to become the next, and greatest, THC champ!” Beelzebozo: “Yeah! That’s the spirit!” He picks up his marshmallow stick and crosses it with Pyretta’s. “A toast…ed marshmallow to victory and stuff!” Blaze: “Here here!” The Quiet One adds his stick. Beelzebozo: “Who invited you?” The Quiet One withdraws his stick. Blaze: “He’s joking, Quiet One.” The Quiet One once more holds forth his stick. Beelzebozo: “With you two on my side, I don’t see how I can lose! Unless you two screw it up for me!” And with that, they turn back to the fire, and we fade out.
