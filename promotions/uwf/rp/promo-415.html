UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #415

The Truth, The Future, The End and more!

15 May 2004

Nick Persia — Secret

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Things have gotten totally out of control. Attacks from behind, the police, residence permit problems.......it can’t get any worse, or can it? This Sunday a Stretcher match. Hardcore title on the line, and the champion might not even be there. Will a disaster of this nature strike this week’s SAS? There is only one way to find in and that’s to tune in to SAS!) (Scene opens in a dark room, so we can’t see where Nick Persia is. It looks like he has taken precautions to make sure nobody fins out where he is. Nick Persia stands up and looks into the camera and makes a sign with his thumb, pointing to the floor. Then he changes his sign to a finger. Then he suddenly breaks the silence.) Nick Persia: “You probably know what I was signing to you, but I’ll break it down for you. You are going down in one day. One day........Pryde.......One day where you can say your prayers. You can do what you always wanted to do. You see, you got what you wanted.......again.” “Last time you wanted something of me, you got it but couldn’t handle it. This time will be no different. You can do what you want, you can pull of all trick you have........but it will make no difference, because I am not better than you but MUCH better than you. You want to get the best out of me? You will never be able to get the best out of me in a thousand years. Let me explain to you why.” “You run your mouth against a guy that is the reigning Hardcore Champion. You are telling him that he ignored you. You want your match, your got your match and you lost your match. Thereafter you proved to be a very sore losed with attacking him, trying to make sure he isn’t able to figth the stretcher match. But the things you could not do, and still can not do is beating him fair and sqaire. I know guys that have more guts than you’ll have in a thousand years.” “I will tear you from limb to limb and make your beg for mercy. The stretcher match is not the end, but it’s the beginning of the end. You see, you failed to take me out. You failed to beat me. You failed to do what you had to do. You are a failure, living in a dream world. You honestly think you are better, and I feel sorry for you. But I won’t regret what I’ll do to you. Since it’s nothing more than sending a message.” “The truth’s gotta be told.......you will get beaten. Whether it will be in five minutes, or whether it lasts 30 minutes the fact remains that you will get down. And I hear you wonder why. I tell you why. You said you wanted something out of me. You said you could beat me. You said so many things. But you never EVER did what you said.” “I can’t be scared of your treaths any more. How could I? You never backed up your words. You never did what you told us you would do. So when you say you win I KNOW you lose. You made a joke out of yourself. I’ve seen a lot in this business. I’ve done a lot of the business. Sometimes I lose, most of the times I win. But when I lost I walked to the back knowing I had given my all, I was truly beaten. However, I must admit I never had these feeling with you. You are a sneaky bastard. You will do anything in your powers to make sure I go down. That’s okay.” “This Sunday we’ll have ourselves a rematch. The sequel, and just like the Godfather part two the sequel will be better. I’ll kick your ass even harder, I’ll make you tumble down even harder and I’ll end you faster. In every single way I will hand you defeat. I will prove that I am the best, pound for pound. I will outwrestle you, I will outbrawl you and I will oursmart you. Simply put, I will once again come out on top.” “You think that you have a real advantage here by having this match turned into a stretcher match, huh? You can throw with chairs, you can let your friend beat me down too, but you forgot one thing. What about me? What about Nick Persia? I give the word hardcore a whole new definition. I’ll take hardcore to a new level. Hell is just the seventh ring. I’m going to the ninth. Through high water and brick stones I will win. My victory won’t come easy, but it will come.” “I bet you probably never thought of the fact MY friends can go to the ring. You probably never thought og the fact that I can smash a s\chair into your face. You never thought of the fact that I can make you blead, make you sweat and make your cry. This so called advantage will turn out to be the biggest disadvantage you can EVER think of.” “It’s the passion that keeps you alive in a lot of matches. Each time you get beaten down but you still stand up and you continue. But not this time, not this time. This time is the last time. You asked for me. You want to go beyond your limits. This time, and for this time only I will take you beyond your limits. And you can bet on that.” “This will be your one day. You will reach your pinnacle. You will get to the top. Honestly, I mean this. Because I take you to the top. You can have your little momentum, but then you will be over your top. It’s not until then you realise how quick a rise and fall can go. You will be a true UWF superstar, just for a minute. You will get into the recordbooks of the UWF, for getting beating so hard you won’t wake up AGAIN.” “I’m not underestimating you, Adien Pryde. Just like anybody else you got your strong points and your weak points. I know you can deliver a good Falcon driver. But I know I can take a Falcon Driver. You see, your weak points will cause your defeat. You have so many, I don’t even need to work on something. It’s just kick, punch and you’re down. It’s that EASY.” “I’m not only talking strong, but I’m doing strong as well. You can say all you want. You can do all you want. Whatever, really. Just remember that in the end it doesn’t even matter. You will not win. Period. Don’t be ashamed of yourself. You were born a nothing and you will end up being a nothing. After all, death is not that far away. You get what you want.” “Will I be there, or will I not? Questions that need answers. Let me put it like this, if I can be there I will be there. And who says I’m not already here. You see, the suprise element is on my side. All you can is watch your back. While you watch your back, you must look before you. I will strike so fast, you won’t even notice it. You should have two pairs of eyes, but they are not to buy in a store. Rip them out of Reo Dai, he doesn’t even use them.” “Perhaps you should take them out of the former Television Champion. The only thing I caused for him was his defeat. You see, whatever I do will be so improvised that you can’t see it coming. You know what comes, but you can’t see it. You are like a black hole. You attract everything to yourself. You wanted my attention? You GOT my attention. You attracted it. But what can you do when the end comes to you? There is nothing left for you.........” “Unless I let you...........what if I let you...........yeah what if you........survive?”Would you still be able to go on with wrestling? Would you still be able to walk? What can you still do after I’m finished with you? I don’t know as I speak, but I do know you won’t walk out the same. In fact, you won’t be walking out, you will be out on stretcher. After I hit the Dutch Destroyer on your sorry ass and I lay you down on that stretcher. My music will play twice, while yours once. I hear the people chant while Limp Bizkit with Rollin’ hits the PA System.” “What you will be hearing is the Angle talking in your left hear asking i you want to go. Don’t worry, I’ll make the choice for you and I’ll ease your passing. Before you go..........I just want to let you know that I feel sorry.” “I feel sorry for every single moment after I saw you for the first time. I knew it, I blew it. I should have done the proper thing at the proper time. Now we see what the results are when you don’t finish what you start. I should have beaten you into a bloody pulp in our first match. This time, you will see no horizon. This time you will fall victim to the Dutch Destroyer. I will shatter the last remains of Chaos Society. I take you all down. I’ll make you suffer until you can no-longer suffer.” “They say I should wrestle you, but those times are over. This match will count even much moves as a normal Goldberg versus Hogan match, and that’s not a compliment. This is a one man match only. The only thing you will be doing is getting beat up, while I am working my ass off to make you look...........bruised, battered and defeated. I won’t hand you defeat in a minute. I won’t give you the chance to lay down. I make you do what I want you to do.” “You see, I gave you your chance. I gave you so match, and this is your appreciation? Your ungraceful son of a b*tch. I gave you fame. I turned you into a megasuperstar, almost like myself. And you just waisted every single bit of it. And now you are in for the match of your life against the man you pissed off really bad.” “My question to you is........Can you count? If so, don’t count on victory. I hope you double checked your insurance, because after this match you will claim it. It’s almost a retirement match. Ever wondered what Bret hart is doing today? He’s recovering from a concussion, and he blames Bill Goldberg for it. Bill Goldberg is making good money now. After this match, it will be the same between us. You can say all you want about me....and make a good living out of it. You can charge me, you can take me to the court if you want to..........but please don’t forget that it was you who wanted this match.” “Quite frankly............I don’t care what you do at all. I don’t care what your fate is. I don’t care anymore about you. All the people in the back will be showing compassion for you when I rip the intestines out of your stomach. Not because they care for your well being, but because they know they will be standing in your place one day.” “You matched your superior, Adien. Defeat is the first thing you will get. But i didn’t discuss all the other things. You will be humiliated in front of the fans, your family and maybe the biggest of all.........your peers. They’ll laugh at you and I know you would like to slap the hell out of them at that point but you can’t because I paralysed you. The only thing you will be able to do is watch.” “Sounds familiar? I know......because you will be doing no more than watching yourself. Must be frustrating for you to know before the match even has begun to know that your are on the losing end. You hope for a miracle, but the only thing which can fall out good........is me feeling sorry for you. But I will take pleasure in every single moment I will spend in this ring with you. I will squeeze the will out of your bones. I just hope you enjoy it too........because it will be your last minutes in the ring as an actual wrestler. I am on a rage of fury here.” “You hope I won’t show up. You hoped your little plan worked. Maybe.........maybe not. I can garantuee you one thing........be sure of what you do. And when you do something make sure you do it the right way. Everytime you are walking on the street and you hear somebody walking behind you........you can just hope it’s not me. You don’t dare to watch, because there is a fat chance you will be recognised and you get a beating of a life time. When the pace quickens your heartbeat raises at five and you almost got a heartattack.” “Even thinking about me standing before you will almost cause the end for you. And hwne you realise I actually stand before you will kill youf if it wasn’t for me. I won’t even give you the time to think about it. As I made fairly clear by now...........you are nothing more than MY PERSONAL BITCH.” “You are nothing more than a pain in the ass, not only for me, but for UWF as well. I just wanna say one more thing......be prepared.......Exitus acta probat.” (Latin for: The end justifies the end) “Please remember.................your fate is in my hands. MINE! You stupid f*ck. No brainer is what you are, you are going down under the sheer weight from my heel..a warm welcome is what you get...Ab abyssus....Irae gloriam.” (from hell…Wrath’s glory) (Scene fades out with a diabollically laughing Nick Persia)
