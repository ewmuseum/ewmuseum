UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #450

The Voice speaks out.

Today (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — Airport

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Who controles the past, controles the future. Who controles the future, controles the present.” [Scene fades in with Nick Persia in front of the Airport. Ben Jameson is conducting an intervie with Nick, but it’s already underway.] Ben: “Nick, how do you feel about the comments made by Corporal Punishment?” Nick looks at him, before answering the question. Nick: “Obviously, Corporal seems to be confident about beating me. But I let him continue to be under the illusion he actually makes a chance. Because we all know actions speak louder than words, I will tell you this. Just a few months ago i was a nobody. I was just wrestling matches. Nobody cared. At the Renaissance pay per view I won The Hardcore Title. Since then I’ve defended it with pride and honour. Exactly how it was meant to be.” Nick lookes down at the floor and a short moment of silence follows. Nick: “When we talk about records, about all the things I have accomplished you can definitely say I made an impact. But my win/loss record isn’t important to me. However, the appreciations I get from the fans is important to me. When the fans cheer you it means they approve what you do. When the fans boo at you, there is something wrong.” Ben: “A lot of wrestlers seem to be after you, and your hardcore title.” Nick: “Yes, that’s true. Apperently the comments I made in the past insulted them. A lot of people don’t like me for speaking the truth. But the truth’s got to be told. I am going to beat Corporal Punishment and I will set the standard for weeks to come. You see, things need to change around here. People are always complaining about having a bad night and that they lost the title because of that. Now if you gave me the chance I would make every title match two ouf three falls. And to give very belt some value they need to be defended at every pay per view, with the stipulation added that if somebody tries to keep the title by seeking a disqualification or count out finish they will immediately forfeith the title. That way, people like Moraless and Digital Mortality won’t be able to keep that title for so long. That way Raid never would have won the Intercontinental title and Ezra kazee would still have what belongs to him.” Ben: “But what if Corporal Punishment does win?” Nick laughs at first, but the expression on his face turns to a more serious tone. Nick: “Exception confirms the rule. The few matches I have lost are an exception when you look at my record. Because I win most of my matches. And the losses I have won’t prove my downfall, but the rule. You see, I am a consistent factor. I don’t have very bad showings, nor very good showing when we compare each match that I’ve had to my average. My average is of course a lot higher than that of a lot of other people here.” Ben: “So you think Corporal won’t make a chance?” Nick: “No, Ben! All I am saying is that Corporal is hanging around with the wrong people. I mean, what person right in their mind would team up with Kenny Mercury and Seymour Almasy? I think Corporal is a great wrestler, and the only one of that entire group to show his skills instead of hiding them.” Ben: “Is that an invitation for Corporal to join the Player’s Club?” Nick: “No, because the tactics Corporal uses are against the guidelines set by The Player’s Club. But on the other hand, guidance is what he needs. And he can be a great wrestler if you ask me. Ocassionally I even admire his courage. Because he goes out there, each and every night, to kick somebody ass. And the fact he likes to take pleasure out of his work doesn’t change my opinion a bit!” Ben: “So you are basically approving what he does?” Nick: “No, I am not! I said I was admiring him. I was admiring the guts he has. But that doesn’t mean the methods he uses are unheard of! But you need to earn your stripes. And although the most honest person might get the most sympathy, those people are often the guys who will never make it. Sometimes, a bit of cheating is for the better.” Ben: “You got me confused here. You sympathise what he does, but you don’t approve it?” Nick: “Exactly. Deep in my heart even I would want to be him. He is ruthless, doesn’t care about rules and has charism. But I know what he does is wrong. He wins by lying, stealing and cheating. That’s something you don’t do. You don’t cash in at somebody else his expense. Furthermore I think Corporal is a very smart guy. Because when you hear him talk he doesn’t say stupid things. He likes to paint his opponents off like they are weaker and more vulnerable. Of course, this will have little affect on well educated people like myself. But when we talk about the average, non player’s club, wrestler you will see what I mean. Ruff Rider was pretty intimidate by his slam talk.” Ben: “And he is a former World Champion!” Nick: “Indeed, Ben. But as I was stating just a moment ago, I feel Corporal is somebody to be concerned about. Even though he has been out of UWF for quite some time. He should not be underestimated. Especially when we know what he has accomplished in the past. So when I step into that squared circle I know everything about him I need to do.” Ben: “What do you need to know?” Nick: “All I need to know is what his strategy will be, and I know he will have one, because he is an army guy for christ’s sake. And I already know what kind of moves he uses. I am confident that I can take him to the max.” Ben: “Well, I am sure you can put up a nice match with him. Are you afraid for any interferences in this match? Perhaps from people like Seymour or Kenny to send out a message to the Player’s Club?” Nick: “I’d wish I could say me and Corporal would have a non-interference match! But I can’t make such promises. Therefore, I can only ask Seymour and Kenny to stay out of this match. Now I can threat them with saying they will regret it when I see them at Stable Wars, but I already know that will be to no avail. You see, my voice has no big reach. In their eyes, that is. They will probably not be running scared when they hear my name. And that’s okay. Because my voice is the action in the ring. And the fact I was always booked against low life wrestler isn’t their fault. But tonight I will prove that I have what it takes to transform from a minor superstar to a major superstar. And to accomplish that noble goal, I will take it to the limits.” Ben: “I’m not too sure if Corporal has any limits. I think he’s capable of anything if that means he will win. The guy is a lunatic!” Nick: “His limit is action radius. Corporal might be a bastard, but he wasn’t born a bastard. He was made a bastard. And hwne you are not a pure blood you can be turned back into what you once were. In what you was meant to be. Corporal Punishment was meant to be a fair and honest wrestler. They only thing we need to do is getting that side out of him.” Ben: “I don’t know if Corporal has any good in him.” Nick: “But I do. He has been blinded by his so-called friends. People who he thinks have befriended him. People like Kenny Mercury and Seymour Almasy. They are acting all good now, but come Stable wars we will see their true colours when they will betray the all of us. But I am prepared for that moment. We all have these moment that can define your career. They either make you or break you. Now this is a moment that will make me. All the people who challenged me will see what I can do. Moraless, Corporal, Slasher, Adien Pryde......the list goes on and one. They have all challenged me, for a title match. I don’t like people who are merely interested in titles. I only want to face people for the title who are interesting in doing the best thing for the UWF. Now anybody can say they want that match for the sake of the UWF, but I won’t buy it. You can tell me you want it by your actions in the ring. But probably they will go to their friends in the office and tell them about their intentions and they will be granted the match. I think a good 50 per cent of the people who receive title shots have bought them.” Ben: “You mean people like Moraless and Digital Mortality?” Nick: “Those are your words, Ben. But I have to admit that I do think it are these people among others who have been buying their way into the ranks. Joshua Dunn and Kevin Starr were just an inch away from beating them for the titles, but they someshow managed to keep the titles.” Ben: “Sorry to interrupt you, but it was Kevin Starr who costed The Player’s Club the match!” Nick: “Damnit, I always had a feeling something was wrong with that arrogant brat.” Ben: “You aren’t suprised to hear this..?” Nick: “No, I am not. Let me explain it to you. Kevin Starr is the weakest link of our team. He has publically spoken out about his disliking towards Joshua Dunn. Joshua Dunn on the other hand pushed his personal feelings aside for the interest of the club. Personally, feel what Starr has done is simply not done. But I will speak no further about this before I speak to Mr. Starr himself.” Ben: “That sounds very professional to me.” Nick: “Why thank you, Ben. But anyone with their brains on the right place would have done that. But let’s not judge Starr’s deeds here. Before people start to think the whole Player’s Club is falling apart, which isn’t true of course.” Ben: “What is the point with your last challenges?” Nick: “I thought you were following the rumours on the internet? I read them sometimes. Accidentely, I checked it out yesterday and I read some people thinking The Player’s Club is balancing on the edge of their excistence. I am just telling you that TPC is alive and well. But there are several people in the lockerroom who will attack us when they smell blood. And this is a warning to those.” Ben: “Any final comments on your match with Corporal Punishment?” Nick: “When we’ll clash off in that ring I hope you bring your a game. I already made it clear to you why they call me The Voice. Now I will make it clear to you why that the Dutch Destroyer is that damn succesful.” Ben: “Thanks for the interview, Nick.” Nick: “No problem at all, Ben.” [Scene fades out to the commercials.]
