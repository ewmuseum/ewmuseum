Angel — Hotel & UWF Gym

http://mysite.wanadoo-members.co.uk/raynus2781/angelrp2.htm Copy and Paste the above link for a clear easy to read version :P ----------------------------------------------------------- The scene opens in what looks like a large Romanesque temple, a few people are sitting and kneeling in front of a large statue of the God Zeus. Outside sits a Roman guard talking to a fellow guard of the Roman Legion. From outside of the temple a pathway stretches for what seems like miles in a perfect straight line through the desert. It is apparent this area is situated in the Middle East one of the furthest stretches of the Roman Empire. At the temple the two guards are still talking. Arius: So why are we guarding this temple? There is hardly anything worth robbing from it anyway. Atermis: We have been picked to guard this because of what goes on in there, do you not know they sacrifice people in there? It is a most unholy place. They are afraid that someone will try to stop this. Arius: Stop this, why? Atermis: Because by sacrificing our Legion is selling it’s soul to Hades, sure that seems bad but in return it will give us untold power and tip the balance in our favour. Arius: So who will stop this? Atermis: Those of the East, they seek to stop this believing the act to be evil. So they have sent you and I to guard this place to stop the Wanderer. The Wanderer seeks to bring balance to good and bad, but he will have to get past us first. A quiet voice speaks Voice: And that is what I intend to do. A quick flash of silver and the two guards drop to the floor with their throats cut, the blood spurting out onto the temple steps. A dark figure enters the temple and runs towards the alter where a priest is cutting open the body of a sacrificed human. Unbeknownst to priest, the figure stands over him. Pulling his blade the unknown figure is about to cut priests neck; suddenly a spear shoots into the back of the unknown figure. [Scene Fades] [Scene Opens] Suddenly Angel wakes up drenched in sweat he sits up staring at the wall; his heart beating fast. Ayami is woken by the commotion Ayami: Angel is something wrong? Angel: No…nothing, I am fine. Angel still staring at the wall notices a flashing red light over near his laptop. Angel: Ayami, what is that flashing light? Ayami: That is the answering machine; I had one brought up from the reception. It’s to see if we receive messages on our first match. I imagine after your entrance at Stable Wars some people might have gained interest. Angel ties his hair back and slips out of bed walking over to the answering machine, being still dark outside he turns on a side light and sits on a small stall next to the desk. Angel presses the play button on the answering machine. The machine clicks and starts playing the message. Voice: Hello? Oh…damn I hate these things. Mr…err Angel, this is Robert Robertson from the UWF. I have your first match booked. It is at this week’s Wednesday Night Universal. You face…damn wait. In the background of the message rustling paper can be heard. Voice: Oh yes its, The Green Demon and Adien Pryde. Well Mr. Angel I must go… The message cuts out and the machine beeps. Angel sits for a few moments thinking, then turns to Ayami Angel: So do you know about these wrestlers? Are they a threat? Ayami wipes her eyes and gets out of bed; wrapping a dressing gown around herself she walks over to Angel and presses the power button on the laptop. Ayami: I think I have some information on Adien Pryde on this laptop. Angel: What about The Green Demon? Ayami: I have no information; I think he is a relative newcomer like you. Now let me see if I can find that file on Mr. Pryde…ah yes here it is, let me load it up. The computer begins to loads a small Word file on Adien Pryde, on the screen stats and past matches are being displayed. Ayami: Adien Pryde has lost more times than he has won, he seems more suited towards tag-teams seeing that is he has had his only two recorded wins in that field. Says here “Adien Pryde is a Grapple/Submission wrestler using high impact grapples with obscure submissions”. Angel: Then it seems he is a worthy opponent, what is his alignment? Ayami: Unfortunately heel. Angel: So he walks the path of evil, then it is in my first match I can begin my task to destroy the evil from this federation. If I beat Adien, I have made it to the first step and from there I can fight the truly evil foes in this federation. So can you find any information on this Green Demon? Ayami: No I am afraid I cannot, there is no information entered on this particular wrestler. So I cannot tell you his wrestling style or his alignment. He is a mystery. Angel: Then I believe I must be cautious in this match, if I know my opponent I know how I can handle them and how to defeat them. But with no knowledge on them, the only time I can tell how they wrestle is when I meet them in the ring. Angel looks at the clock next to his bed and notices the time. Angel: It is 7am; I need to get to the UWF gym. I think if workout there I can at least focus again. I don’t seem to be sleeping well. Ayami: Is it the dreams again? Angel: Yes. [Scene Fades] [Scene Opens] The scene opens with Angel sparing with Ayami, they are going over some basic moves to help relax the muscles in the body. Angel holds up his hand signalling to stop and he sits down on the bench. Ayami: Is there something wrong dear? Angel: No, I just want to rest for a while. You are a hard trainer. Ayami: I have trained you for 4 years and your still have not gotten used to it. Angel smiles Angel: I know. Whilst relaxing a man walks into the gym holding a microphone with a cameraman in following closely behind, the man with microphone walks gingerly up to Angel. Interviewer: Hello…Mr. Angel. I am here to interview you. Angel: Is that so, why do I need to be interviewed? Ayami walks over to Angel and sits next to him. Ayami: Interviewers ask you some questions about your upcoming match and your opinions on the wrestlers you face. It is so those wrestlers and the public will get to see your views on the match, leaving a chance for your opponents to respond. Angel: I see, I recall doing this…from my other… Ayami coughs. Angel: Err never mind. Sure, I would not want to disappoint the fine people who watch the wrestling. Interviewer: Great! The interviewer points to the camera to set the camera up on its tripod and to begin filming. Interviewer: So Angel, it has been 4years since you were last seen in the wrestling circuit. What have you been up to in those 4 years? Angel: I have been training, training hard for my eventual return. Interviewer: And that happened at Stable Wars in a spectacular show of wrestling skill on your behalf. It seems the 4 years have paid off. Angel: Well there is much to do, I am here to wrestle…I am here to defeat evil and complete my… Ayami taps Angel on his shoulder and whispers in his ear. Angel: …my, my dream of wrestling again. It has been so long and I thought I would never wrestle again. Interviewer: Yes that is something that intrigues us all, your knee was apparently injured beyond repair. But I look at Stable Wars and there is no noticeable sign that you ever had injury and I can see close up there is no scaring. The interviewer points to Angel’s knee. Angel gives a rye smile to Ayami. Angel: I guess someone up there is looking out for me. I cannot explain it, I just managed to overcome this injury and now I am here in the UWF. Interviewer: So where have you trained for 4 years? Angel: It is in a small town just outside Hong Kong, I cannot say more. Interviewer: Ok then, moving swiftly onto your opponents Green Demon and Adien Pryde. What do you think of them? Angel: As wrestlers or in general? Interviewer: As wrestlers. Angel: Well I don’t know about Green Demon, I think he is a relative newcomer like me. Adien Pryde he has lost a few matches and of course won a few. I don’t fear my opponents but I respect them even though Adien does walk the path of evil? Interviewer: Evil? Is this because he is known in the wrestling trade as a ‘heel’? Angel: Yes that is why; I follow the path of good. Now when two people fight it is out of respect to see who is the better man and who has trained the best. But people with that…heel persona only cheat to win, that is dishonourable. Interviewer: So you believe wrestling is about honour? Angel: Most certainly, that is what I follow. Anyway I am sorry but I must get back to my training, it has been a pleasure. Interviewer: Of course, may we return tomorrow to get an update? Angel: Of course, but now I must train. Interviewer: Angel, thank you. The Interviewer stands up and walks out of the room. Angel: Thanks for reminding me. Ayami: Reminding you for what? Angel: Not giving the whole idea of the task out. Ayami: That is ok; I understand it is hard to keep under wraps with all these cameras and interviewers buzzing around the building. We must keep it quite for now when the time is right then we will have no choice but to reveal our plans. A great evil lurks in the shadows; we must not let them know of our intentions. Angel: A great evil? Ayami: Something stirs in the background of this federation it is hard to tell what it is. But it is there. Angel: So what can we do about? Ayami: For now just train and keep training. Angel: I understand, lets continue. Angel and Ayami return to the wrestling mat and continue to spar with each other. [Scene Fades] So soon Angel will face his first opponents for 4years. How we will fair, only time will tell. But it is in Angel’s heart he can and must win. Angel must rise through the ranks proving to those who follow the path of evil it is not the correct path to follow. The evil that lurks in the shadows of the federation stirs and is yet to reveal itself, but it will do. When it does Angel will have to face it no matter how big the evil is Angel must fight it and defeat it, humanity depends on it.
