UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #403

The art of the Stretcher

11 May 2004

Adien Pryde — Local Hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside of what appears to be a hospital. Cars and trucks are driving in and out of the parking lot bringing people to visit their sick and injured family members. To one side we see an ambulance drive up to the Emergency Room. As the camera zooms in we see a familiar black Navigator. The scene fades to black. As the scene fades in again we see the black SUV in a parking stall behind the ER and Adien Pryde getting out. Pryde is wearing black jeans with a red t-shirt and a black jean jacket. As he closes the door he begins to speak.] Pryde : Hey, sorry Nick but I couldn’t stand by and have someone that isn’t allowed to make money in America while there are other starving wrestlers wanting to make a living doing what your were doing. Make it legal for you to work here and that will be a different story. America if the land of opportunity, but please don’t steal from us, just do it right so I can kick your ass legally. [Pryde starts to walk toward the entrance of the ER.] Pryde : I thought that since you accepted my little stipulation that I would go to the experts to learn the art of putting people on a stretcher, the local hospital emergency room. This place gets dozens of people on stretchers daily and they have no room for mistakes so I figured, who better to learn how to do it. [As he makes his way though the sliding door and a family walks out, the little boy with his arm in a cast and sling.] Pryde : Eeeewww, that hade to hurt. [The scene fades to black and then when it fades back in they are in the ER.] Pryde : Wow, a busy place today. There should be plenty of opportunities to learn the art of stretchering people. Hey there is one now. [Pryde points over to the doorway where some paramedics are wheeling a man in on a gurney.] Pryde : Excuse me sir, I see that that man is strapped down rather securely, could I ask you what the trick in doing that is? [The paramedic looks at Pryde very strangely then up to the camera with a look like he doesn’t believe this is going on.] Paramedic : Please get out of the way, I have a injured man in dire need of some emergency care. [A nurse makes her way over to Pryde and the camera man.] Nurse : Sir, may I help you? Please turn that camera off, they are not allowed in here. Pryde : Sorry miss? Nurse : Nurse Jinkins. Pryde : Nurse Jinkins, I am looking to learn how to put a helpless man on a stretcher and I thought this would be a good place to start. Nurse Jinkins : What? This is a place of healing, not a school. I asked you to turn off that camera, if not I will have to call security. [Pryde motions not to.] Pryde : So are you telling me that you won’t help me? I guess I will have to ask that man over there. [Pryde brushes past the nurse and walks toward a doctor. Nurse Jinkins picks up a telephone.] Nurse Jinkins : Security, I have two people down here harassing the staff and one of them is filming it. Pryde : Doctor, can you help me? I was hoping that you could teach me a efficient way to stretcher someone. Doctor : You have to be kidding me. Pryde : No, I’m serious. [Two security men walk into the room.] Nurse Jinkins : There they are over there. Security Man 1 : Sir, I demand that you turn that camera off. Security Man 2 : Sir do you have anything to do with this man? [The security man point to the camera man.] Pryde : No sir, he has been following me all over the place. I was hoping that if I came in here that he would stop. Now that you are here I can safely leave without him following. I have to thank you very much. [Pryde starts to walk out of the ER as the two security men take the camera away from the camera man and then the scene fades to black.]
