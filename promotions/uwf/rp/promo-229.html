Origins of Moraless#2

18 Feb 2004

Moraless — That ol jail place again

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“So are you ready to begin again?” the directors voice cut through me, making me take a cautionary step back, natural instinct I suppose it tended to happen a lot. I wasn’t sure exactly where I was in my mind at the time; it was as though everything was a blur. I had lost against Iron Lung in a match I felt I should have won, everything was there, the ability and the training, but I had lacked the focus allowing him to get me from behind and toss me over the top. I was supposed to be filming again that night, but I didn’t I simply went home and slept, mulling over the things I had done wrong, things that I could do better next time. “Yes, sure I’m ready” I replied a lack of confidence clearly evident in my voice in the way it squeaked out. I was standing in front of the large fire blackened ominous door, which had been part of my keeper all that time ago, just a simple iron door which prevented me from freedom. Yet now I was a freed man, no need to fear it any longer as my hand touched the cold metal, a chill went through my spine, emotions and sensations running through me the type of which had not experienced for a long time. “You can feel the emotion look at him this is great” came the voice of the director once more, not quite a whisper and extremely over enthusiastic. John had not even realised that they had begun filming again, this had become more taxing than he had first imagined, a little bit of television exposure to help his career, he didn’t realise how hard it would actually be. “Care to give us a bit on an introduction before you go off onto the main body of the piece again?” asked another man I think it was the producer you could never be too sure, there were always people wondering about runners, sound engineers yet none actually listened to you, too busy with their own work and deadlines. I gazed up at the door one last time, a huge sigh followed as you could almost see the butterflies leave my stomach; it was just another show and just another performance. I pushed open the door slowly, the hinges had become rusted and worn through time and weather, creaking and snapping as I pushed the imposing door forwards revealing a large room, also damaged by fire. A long bleak stretch of corridor with cells two stories high, John let out a shudder as the smell of charred surged from the room attacking his nasal cavity. ”This is C-wing, it was never given a nickname as it wasn’t something whispered in the shadows, unlike the red room, for me and many others this was my life. Murderers, rapists, fraudsters every single member of society who could be deemed a low life were held captive here, a shared cell ten feet by six feet. The smell of pain and suffering in the air, this is where you were broken mentally, the squalid degradation of the human condition happening before your very eyes. Seeing people bend and break until they had been reduced to just a number” The words flowed from my mouth freely, a touch of bitterness tainting the words as I spat them out, part of it was dramatic flow, putting on a nice show for the cameras but most of it was real, this had been my life I had lived it, you can’t begin to judge an existence without living it. “Ok that’s great we’ll move into the building now and set up the lighting for the next shot, John great work I could really feel something there, is it possible to build on it a little more, give it a bit more oomph though?” smiled the director, he wasn’t even looking at me as he spoke he was already half way into C-wing shouting instructions at his crew. More oomph I should show him more oomph, like grinding his face into the course gravel below how would that be for oomph. I took a few steps into C-Wing and looked around, memories flooding back this had been where the fire had began that devastated the prison, fear gripped me as it did so, wanting to leave my first instinct was to turn and run, to not look back and simply leave, I could have forced my way through but that would be running away from my past and I was a man…I was simply going to avoid it for a while. “Hey Steve” I called at the director who was still busy shouting random instructions to those around him, he turned his head as I had caught his attention “Do you mind if we do this tomorrow, I have got a lot of stuff on I need to go training for a start” my voice flickered as I spoke showing that It was a lie but I still carried on the charade in a hope that things would work out for the best. The director came towards me, looking angry his eyes bulging and his cheeks a tone of red the type of which was commonly associated with alcoholics. “Do you understand how important the filming is? We have a deadline to meet don’t you understand?” Spat the director numerous small blobs of spittle connecting on my forehead and nose. I snapped, I don’t know why or how but I did. This was always in me, the ability to pop like a firecracker at any second, grabbing him by the throat and pinning him against a wall. The camera crew instantly stopped what they were doing and looked on with shock and awe. “You don’t understand, I lived this ok. You have no idea how hard it is just being here stepping into this place again, the memories and fear I don’t need to deal with it ok. I am not a television personality, I’m a wrestler. I’m going to be facing someone who has more power in the UWF than anyone at Supremacy and I know that I will have to be at the top of my form to even be in with a chance.” The directors eyes were still bulging but now due to the lack of air getting to his brain, I released my grip slightly before turning to walk away ”If you leave now you are breaking your contract with us, I could sue you!” The shout from the director was more through fear than threat, he was losing his star draw, he was losing his television show and his hope of being regarded once more. “Then sue me” I replied, I didn’t care. “I have to face Mike Montgomery in the tournament ok, I’m pretty sure you don’t even know what I’m talking about but it’s all true. He said himself it may as well be called the players club invitational tournament and do you know why? Because they have all the balls in their court, the power, people to back them up and what do I have huh? Hell I’m going to be going to RPW soon. I overheard them the contract is in talks right now. On Sunday I’m not just going to be fighting for the right to advance in the tournament, I’m not just fighting Mike Montgomery, hell I’m not even just fighting the Players Club I’m fighting every single member of UWF as they want to get rid of me.” I turned and left, my piece said ignoring the shouts and cries that came from behind me, I was through with them. If being in UWF had taught me one thing, it was that you are always on your own, even the most close nit groups eventually implode it was only a matter of time. So on Supremacy I would stand alone against all the odds, but it wouldn’t be that simple. Montgomery and Kazee wanted to get rid of me for a reason and in all fairness I was going to give them what they wanted… In a way they would never expect.
