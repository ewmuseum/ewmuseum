Lustful Lullaby: Meet Calvin; Bondage's Monologue

6 Apr 2004

Bondage Sex Fiend — Midnight Flight....

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'We are now ready to begin boarding flight 901 for Portland, Oregon.' The voice over the intercom woke Calvin from his dream, and he rubbed his eyes and stretched out his arms as he returned to consciousness. He checked his watch and saw that it was almost two hours past midnight, the time his flight had been scheduled to depart. He looked around the gate and noticed that most of the other passengers were just as groggy and annoyed as he was. He checked his bag to make sure he had everything, then stood up and walked toward the attendant who was collecting the tickets. He handed his ticket to the gentlemen in the blue vest and matching tie, who ripped it and handed Cal back the stub. He checked his seating assignment as he walked down the hallway to the plane. 34E. Back of the plane, he realized, which wouldn't be so bad as long as he had some peace and quiet. Activity on planes as late as this were usually kept to a minimum, and he just wanted to sleep the entire way from New York to Portland. He was returning home for his sister's birthday, and this would be the best chance for rest before spending a week with his family, and then returning back to school to finish his finals. He was returning for his sister, not his parents, and he was hoping his week would be even more peaceful than the red eye flight that was about to carry him home. When he found his seat Cal realized 34 wasn't just near the back of theplane, but the very last row there was. E was the middle seat. He stowed his backpack in the overhead bin then sunk into the middle seat, hoping that nobody would show up to claim the seats on either side of him, so he could lay back and sleep in comfort for the duration of the flight. He waited about ten minutes before deciding nobody else was coming, then slid over a seat and sat next to the window, snapping his seatbelt tight over his lap. People continued to trickle into the plane, but very few, and none of them came close to the last five or so rows of the plane. He was about to lean back and close his eyes as he noticed a young woman walking toward the rear of the flight. She didn't seem to be slowing her pace, so Cal realized she was coming toward his row, or at least close to it. I hope I'm in her seat, he thought to himself, so at least I'll have a reason to talk to her. She was gorgeous, definitely one of the most beautiful women he'd seen in a long time, and the way she carried herself and the way she dressed made her appear all the more arousing. Bondage the Sex Fiend steps out as the frame freezes and he looks at the woman. He nods in acceptance and then looks over at Calvin, and grins. Bondage than looks at the camera and speaks. 'It's funny how this average man wants what he can't get... She's drop dead beatiful and to see her even talk to him during this flight would be ridiculous... Alot like the Tag team titles here in UWF. See these titles are this fine little number right here and well, I'm not Calvin. I am the dude who actually gets the chicks and wrestling has only fed my libido but not made it full. I am the kind of person who stays with a girl long enough for them to take off their pants. I am the kind of person who will step in the ring and finish anything I have started...' Bondage turns to the woman's frozen framed body and circles her. 'She truely does have beauty, the beauty of gold around my waist. Nothing will stop me from getting those titles, even after that tag match I will not leave the ring, but instead take on Malcolm Kennedy, the icing on the cake. Those tag titles will look great as I hold them up, this tag team is me... Nymph, she is just a slave to me, she will sit and do what I tell her, but, and that's a big but, no one will hurt her. It is fair warning to all I will not be waiting for someone to hit her either, I will snap all five teams in half and show them what Bondage the Sex Fiend is all about.' Bondage stops in front of the woman's face and places his fingers on her lips and slowly moves them down, past the cleavage, belly button and eventually losing body to travel down as he goes over the vulva and off. He turns back to the shot. 'I have nothing to say, I have no preparations that need to be completed, I don't sit in my locker room and contemplate my big matches, I go out there and I get it done. And in my first match back after my horrible loss to Erza... I do not take losses lightly and those who are in the tag team title match may not want to challenge me, because I am fresh out of the box, only two matches into my UWF career and the Tag Team Titles are as good as mine. This match is for nothing more than pride for the other teams becasue the titles are as good as mine...' Bondage walks away from the woman and heads over to one of the vacant airplane seats and sits on the outside as the scene pans to get in front of him. 'Malcolm Kennedy, good luck, for you will need it... you will need everything that you have to beat me, even after a match. You will have to push yourself to your limits to even try to defeat me, because at Rennaissance, UWF changes... UWF changes in a BIG way. No longer will it be all fun and laughs, or shooting the shit, it will be Bondage's World, it will be my federation, the fans will love ME, because I will give them what they all want. What the bald guy in the third row wants, and the little groupee in the nose bleed section wants... They all want SEX...' Bondage laughs to himself and runs his hands on top of his mask. 'You can't provide them with sex, Dunn can't, Beezleboo can't, but I can... And that's why when UWF sees what I am, and sees how much of a hot topic I truely am I will go to the top and you will all be left with nothing but jobbing gigs to none other then myself... This may be a true bout but it will be the last time that anyone steps in the ring with Bondage the Sex Fiend, and I don't have a title around my waist... ' Bondage stands up and begins leaving the scene but turns around and looks straight at the shot. 'Don't order the lingerie bowl, order Rennaisance, you won't get blue balled....' END...
