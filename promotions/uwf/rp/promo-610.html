UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #610

Want of a lonely heart

10/8/2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's Garden

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a successful night on Saturday; Joshua did indeed go along to meet the woman he had “met” in the park. His arrival in the restaurant was shocked at first, mainly because he was wearing his head bandages still, he refused to take them off no matter where he was, people staring at the bandages was better than people staring at his scars. Then people started to realise who he was, and then he couldn’t get away from the peering eyes of the other diners in the room, he shrugged them off and found the person he was meeting, she had recognised him before he did her. Possibly because she was looking neat and tidy, and a lot different than when he saw here on that dark night, having her hair up didn’t help. He knew it was her from the way she was waving to him, it was like being summoned. He had wandered over and sat down beside her, and immediately felt under dressed, or thought he should have at least put some effort into the clothes he had put on, he had just thrown on a clean shirt and a pair of trousers, she looked like she had spent hours on the look of her hair alone, the ends of the hair splayed up, cascading like feathers of a peacock. He was in as much awe as she was, but he could feel that there wasn’t much privacy for the two of them, especially with the room going eerily quiet, the conversation soon started up again when he chose to ignore them, if only he had thought about that before, it could have saved him so much trouble many times now. But he wasn’t about to let his crowded mind get between what he thought now might be a special evening indeed. And she talked and talked and talked. All through the starters, prawn cocktail and a bowl of soup, all through the main course, chicken Maryland and roast beef, and even through desert. A good lot of the start was just garbling about how she couldn’t believe how he was actually Joshua Dunn, but he fobbed that off, and tried to change the subject, and did so to some success. She wasn’t even talking about anything in particular, she was just talking and talking, Joshua only did what he did best, listened. And while listening he found out so many details that other men would have missed with just ogling her slender figure. He found out important things like her name, she went to a high school in Boston, graduated university with honours, and now is a successful business woman. But is wasn’t just one sided small talk, Joshua Did his own amount of talking, although he wasn’t able to tell her so much about his own up bringing, granted this was because of the NVA’s notorious meddling in his career, but he put it down to it being uninteresting rather than anything else, and she mostly knew his life since becoming big news in the UWF, snippets about him was cropping up everywhere, they basically exploded onto the covers of wrestling magazines when he won the UWF World Championship. She didn’t mind much about that though; it was just amazing to be around a man that she thought would never walk into her life in over a million years. But eventually, long after he first walked into Alfonse’s restaurant, it was time to go, it came around so quickly it practically pounced on the pair. Neither of them wanted to part company much, but what was left to do? Nothing, but being caught up in the moment, he almost forgot to give her phone back. Now he wished he had forgotten. Joshua sat in the spacious area of his garden, in all honesty he was moping around, but he wasn’t about to admit that to himself, he had had so much fun with the girl he wished he could do it again, and he probably could if he had bothered to get any contact details at all. He was angry at himself for that, but he couldn’t do anything about that now, it was almost a week after the dinner date, he had other important things on his mind now, His first Title defence for one, and not only that, but it was set to be a show and a half, still eclipsed by the Regal Rumble, but it was going to give it a run for it’s money for being the best match of the night. Of course he didn’t want to lose the title, no one wants to lose a title they fought for, but there was still a little niggling detail at the back of his mind distracting him ever so much from actually getting some decent training in, and that little something had brown hair stood at about 5’ 10”. IT wasn’t that he didn’t have his work cut out for him either. The other three men that were in the ring with him were all former colleagues, he’d fought along side them and knew what all of them were capable of, well most of them. There was something different about Ezra Kazee that he couldn’t quite put his finger on, something was different about him, he was sure of that, the Ezra he had come to know and call friend was a more quiet and submissive person. He was down right shocked when he attacked him all that time ago on SAS, then when he started all the babble about suing the UWF, it just didn’t fit, especially when the whole thing didn’t really involve him in the first place, he couldn’t see how he could see himself as an injured party in all this. He did have style though, and the stipulation he named was impressive, although why he wanted to go for an ultimate submissions match was beyond him. He wasn’t a technical wrestler, it puzzled him. Kevin Starr was possibly the wild card of the match up, he wasn’t sure how he would act/react in there, he had faced him the least out of all the opponents, and since the loss of his pinky, he seemed even more aggressive than he had been before, his style had changed so drastically it was no surprise he gave him such a run for his money in the World Title match that had gotten Ezra in such a stew. She wore a pink sleeveless top; it looked like it could have been a thick and warm jumper possibly, going all the way up to a turtle neck like part around the neck. It was strangely alluring while revealing nothing at all. He wasn’t entirely sure how it managed to achieve that. The light blue jeans she wore went perfectly with the top, blue and pink was something he never thought would be two colours that would have gone together and the naturally faded patches on them made parts of it look white. The jeans were skin tight over the thighs, but lose around the calves, boot legged, and over a pair of pointed black boots. She was absolutely stunning. Lastly there was Mike Montgomery, looking back on it now; it was strange how close the two had become. Before they were worst enemies, they only banded together to help each other along the road on their careers, over the months the hatred subsided, and even a fondness between the two had formed. He’d even forgotten why he had despised the man so much. They even thought similarly, and that was a scary though, he was the person he knew the most in the match up, but that went’ both ways, he was possibly the biggest threat to him. Possibly not of course, it just depended how well Montgomery actually knew him. When they parted company he immediately wanted to go back, in the brief time they had spent together he had bonded with her unbelievably well, he wondered if she felt the same, and not just because he was a sports superstar. It would be nice to see her again, he knew he was falling in love, and it shouldn’t be a bad thing…. The name would haunt him for months, possibly even years… Alexia
