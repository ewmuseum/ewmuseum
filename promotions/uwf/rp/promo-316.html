UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #316

Flames of a Friendship

6 Apr 2004

Raid — unknown shed

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see a shed, almost like the UWF Shareholders Branch Office, but this one is a little bigger and on the top of the shed it says “UWF’s BLA BLA BLA Office”. As the camera closes in on the shed you can hear some one talking. The voice is loud and it sounds like the man is yelling at someone. He stops yelling and pauses, and then begins yelling again. The camera moves in closer to the door and you see the camera mans hand knock on the door.) Raid “Hold on a minute damn it, I’m busy.” (All of a sudden a loud thud is heard from inside the shed) Mikey Hipcheck (the camera man) “Is everything ok in there?” (This time a loud CRASH is heard followed by a “ Shit that hurt!” and then “ you little son-of-a-bitch get back here!” All of a sudden the doors on the shed fling open and a baseball bat comes flying out almost hitting the camera. Raid follows shortly after knocking the camera man out of the way. He then looks at the camera man and says.) “Where the hell did he go? I told that little bastard to stay put!” (Mikey) “Who are you looking? There wasn’t any one else that came through the doors besides you.” “What do you mean no one else? Padro just ran out of here. Why didn’t you stop him? What are you working for him now? How much is he paying you? Tell me damn it!” (Raid is now shaking the camera man violently and he starts to raise a fist, about to hit the camera man when all of a sudden Raid is hit from behind and he stumbles forward. Mikey quickly turn to see what hit Raid (and saved him from certain doom) and all he sees it the baseball bat laying on the ground.) (Mikey) “What the hell!” (Raid is now standing back up and he quickly dives for the bat and yells “I got you now , ha ha ha”. As he grabs the bat and stands up it strikes him on the forehead. Raid now has a puzzled look on his face.) “ So you wanna play rough do ya? Well I’ll show ya.” (And with that Raid starts to beat the ground with the bat. He hit’s the ground a couple of times and then the bat hits him on the head again, and again and again. Raids head is now bleeding badly from the gash in his forehead. Raid shakes his head and now has a very violent look on his face. While still holding the bat he goes into the shed and returns a moment later with the bat and a can of gasoline. He begins to douse the bat with the gas, and after he is satisfied with the moistness of the bat he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a Zippo tm. and lights the bat a blaze as he holds on to the bat he stares at it fondly but with a sinister grin on his face. As the flames consume the bat he drops it onto the ground , but he continues to stare at it.) “ I really hated to have to do that to you Padro, but you gave me no other choice.” (Raid begins to walk back towards the shed as he enters the shed you notice hundreds of little Ezra Kazee action figures hanging from the ceiling. You also see a huge pink teddy bear sitting in a chair behind a desk. The bear has two toy belts around its neck. One of which is the IC title and the other is a Tag Team belt. Raid begins to walk towards the bear , and as nears the bear he removes the IC title from around the bears neck.) “Soon enough this will belong to me. Kazee, you have disappointed me. And the way you won the IC title is despicable. You call yourself an athlete? HA! If you were a true athlete you would have not taken the belt like that. I on the other hand am a true athlete and I will prove that to you at Renaissance. I am willing to put everything on the line, both life and limb. This mortal life means noting compared to the glory achieved when one obtains champion status and that is what I intend to do.” (As he finishes saying that he begins to pour gas all over inside the shed. He finishes pouring out the 5 gallons of gas and he again reaches for his Zippo tm.. He then lights the outside of the shed and the blaze begins.) “The time has come for me to prove my dominance over you Kazee. Our friendship has been lost and now its time for me to do what I should have done to you so long ago. And Ezra your dreams of keeping that title are going to go up in smoke.” (With that said Raid walks into the burning shed leaving the camera man to ponder the insanity that we call life.)
