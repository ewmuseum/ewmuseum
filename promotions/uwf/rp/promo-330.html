UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #330

SAS I shall Return!

12 Apr 2004

Dante — Church

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside an old abandoned church, the pews have been burnt and the windows smashed in, a lone figure sits down on the floor cross legged in silent prayer, they lift their head and slowly get to their feet before walking over to the statue of Jesus on the crucifix, he strokes it's cheast and mutters a few things under his breath) Figure: Forigve us oh Lord for we have sinned, this is what all of you peons come here moaning to the Lord, you wish to be forgived for your petty sins, and no not the true torture and battle that rages upon this world, you know not the evil war that awaits to rear it's ugly head between the Good force and that of pure Evil.... I was on the side of Evil for a while, and it felt good, but whilst under it's control I was enveloped into the Darkness and was made to wander it for months! (The figure turns around sharpley knocking his hood of and we see it is a more disfigured and scared Dante than we saw the last time, this Dante shows scars of battles long since past) Dante: In the darkness, the time goes by so much faster, what is a day here is about one year there.... I was stuck inside that thing for months, and it changed me, I saw the horror I was helping to unleash upon the world, but no more! I am back to stop the horror now! (Dante holds onto a pew to keep himself balanced as his breathing turns deep and ragged, he coughs clears his throat before looking back at the screen, his face is a lot older than it was when he left, The Darkness has aged him considerably,) Dante: SAS, my first match back at the UWF, I have got to take on two unkowns to me... Gregory and Loverboy.... Gregory and Loverboy? Loverboy must be punished for his evil sins... You may be wondering what evil sins but allow me to introduce you all to a nice little one I call Jealousy.... Loverboy suffers from Jealousy and lying, heck with a name like Loverboy I am sure he is overcompensating for something.... Beware my opponents for come SAS you shall both face my wrath! (Scene ends)
