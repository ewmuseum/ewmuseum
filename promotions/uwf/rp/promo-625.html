UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #625

RR: Decisions

12 Aug 2004

Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy — ??

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Oh, how things have turned between the two sibling federations that came from the GIWF’s loins. It’s no secret that the holder of the BDW world title, and one that will be looking forward to the UWF world title at the end of the Regal Rumble is engaged to be married to the holder of that sibling federation’s World level title, it’s North Wind Title. But things have happened since we last got an update on this relationship. For that other ‘little brother’ federation is in Japan for this month also. Their date at the Tokyo Dome is but a month away, but this week their just a few hours plane ride south in Okinawa. This means more distraction for Sean McClancy yes, but it also means he’ll be more focused, as he knows that his lover is not far away. And he also knows that this last week, his lover’s enemy did something that he shouldn’t have. For Sean knows all about Patti’s past. Yes she has made some tapes, or movies of an adult nature with a certain Lucious Lady Luck, aka Diana King. But that was back in the days of being a starving actress in Australia. Not that Sean doesn’t mind watching the film itself, but it’s something that was years ago, and now some have found out about it and have decided to capitalize on it. Sean knows that his sister and brother-in-law wouldn’t purvey anything like that, but there are a couple who would if they could. And then you have the fact that another video, one that was made by Patti when she and Sean had first started to ‘date’, if you could call it that. There wasn’t much to it in the line of wining and dining, but there sure was a lot of other stuff. And now that has been made public for all to see by her enemy. But there was a surpise when he did. He gave a ‘shout out’ to two boys that have recently returend to the UWF under the guise of being Penny Preest’s two bodyguards to protect her. A duo known as Tropical Storm, Miguel Lanza and Jamie Dermont. Sean knows these two almost as well as he knows his own two cousins. For at one time back in the GIWF, they were the ones who managed to win a match in which his sister had to disrobe in front of the word to prove the fact that she was a real red head, and they still try to distribute the posters for this on their meager homemade website. What do you get when you cross Tropical Storm with a couple of rated X movies of Patti Anderson? Trouble that’s what. We find the two tropical ones less then three feet from the screen of a large HDTV, There is a package ripped open sitting on the coffee table just behind them. As the camera pans closer you see a letter {To my boys, Jaime and Miguel, I hope you enjoy these, and dont let Mr. McClancy catch you looking at them. Kyo} On the screen we find a black and white moving picture of what appears to be a very muscluar, but still feminine woman and what appears to be a scruffy looking Sean McClancy locked in a rather, well it’s not a romantic embrace, it’s more of a primaly erotic one in the shower of what looks to be a hotel room. Miguel’s head is sideways as he looks at the picture; Jaime is staring blankly, tossing a few pieces of popcorn from a microwave bag into his mouth every few seconds. Jaime: Mon, can you belive dat? For a big man, he sure as hell is limber. Miguel: So is she. Damn. Jaime: An’ if anyone ever tried to say tha’ Sean had no balls, well… Miguel: Crap man. This movie is going to make us a fortune man. Jaime: Ya mon. And we didn’t even have to pay heem. He just sent it to us. I guess that offer to spiffy up his clothing set well. Miguel: how long is this one man? Jaime: I t’ink he said it be 2 hours. Da man has some stamina too. Miguel: I want to check the other one too. Patti and that Lady Luck chick getting freaky in a womanly way. Jaime: Ya mon. The door to the room suddenly opens… A figure darkens the door way..... Very distictive figure.... Mclancy: I was told I would find ye two misfits in ‘ere. Penny said ye ga’ a package t’day an’ ye’d asked if ye could use th’ lounge fer th’ big screen TV. Both men stop dead in their ‘tracks’ and a good, solid ‘oh shit’ looks goes on their face. Jaime almost chokes on his popcorn as he swallows down hard. McCalncy: I was looking fer ye two, t’ see if ye guys wanted t’ ‘elp me get some training in befer the big match this week. Ye two are in th’ Regal Rumble t’, so it migh’ be good fer all o’ ye. The twins are waiting down at the gym fer me. We have 60 men in one ring... and ye two gobshites are going to ‘elp me get ready fer tha’ match... I’m intendin’ t’ win it an’ take me shot a’ the UWF World Title an’ then wear it riogh’ next t’ the BDW one. Mean while, we’ll work a little of tha’ rum off of yer out of shaped arses Jamie suddenly drops the remote control and then drops down onto his hands and knees trying to recover it quickly. The sounds of passion are coming from the TV screen and Sean’s eyes narrow as it, although he’s not in the right direction to see the TV itself yet. Miguel: Yeah, sounds like a good idea Sean, just let us turn off the Video we rented and we’ll be right there! Miguel starts to get up to dive for the TV to turn it off and he trips right over Jaime as he does. Sean walks over and picks up the letter..... Sean: Wha’s this? Sean picks up the note and reads it. A frown forms on his face. The TV isn’t turned off yet and he looks in their direction. Sean: Wha’ are ye watchin’? Both Jaime and Miguel make like cartoon characters, and take off, running in different directions to go around Sean and head out of the room. They slam into each other as they try to leave the door at the same time, Jaime gets tripped by something on his way out and he slides across the tile floor a bit. Sean is seen looking at the TV and the frown going to a face that rage. He picks up something from the table with a dvd case with the picture of a familiar red headed woman and a blonde haried woman in an embrace on the front, the title of it is “Double Your Pleasure”… Jaime looks up after falling, looking into the deep brown eyes of Annie Leanan. Annie: Where ye two runnin’ off t’? Miguel turns around and tries to help his partner up to his feet as they’re trying to get away… Then suddenly a loud crash comes from the lounge room, the sound of a very expensive flat screen HDTV forcefully going from one wall to the other… Jaime: Shit mon, we’re dead… we’re dead! It was good knowing you mon! The table suddenly flys at the doorway, breaking into shards as it hits the door jam. Miguel: Shit man, get up, get up! Annie: Wha’ did ye two foks do? Sean’s face comes into the doorjam again, a scowl still on his face. Sean: Ye two fok’s ‘ad better get t’ th’ fokin’ gym NOW. An’ I migh’ get a chance t’ na’ be fokin’ wantin’ t’ kill ye befer then! Annie: What’s this? Miguel: Yes sir. We’re on our way now. Jaime: Fokin’ a’ mon. Take a chill pill! Sean: I did’na fokin’ stutter. At that, Miguel pulls Jaime up by his collar and the two head hurridly down the hallway. Annie: Wha’s up yer arse now? Sean hands her the DVD case, as well as the non marked one that he ripped out of the TV’s built in player. It’s still in the disc carriage itself from where he had ejected it and then just ripped it off. Annie: Oh, shite. ‘E did send them to them? Sean: If those two make it t’ the Rumble, it’ll be a fokin’ miracle… We fade out here to now go to the aftermath, later on that day. Sean has 60 men to worry about. No one in the ring can be considered friend of foe in this type of match. The KnightMare Brigade brothers of Cpl. Punishment, his brothers, and even Ruff Ryder if he feels the need to redeem himself, can be given any quarter. It’s a match only one can win. Sean would love to come out of this as the Number One Contender, and maybe if he can get his hands on Rich Rollins, even leave with the Television Title, a title that he once held a GIWF record for his tenure as champion with it. He’d not argue with leaving as both. And just add them to his collection that has been started with the BDW Title. Yes, he has Tsunami Oni to worry about also, a man who’s also worried about his own time in this match and his match before hand with Sean. But Sean isn’t too worried about his first match with Oni. Sean knows the real prize comes later on that night. He also knows that Onit’s buddies will be out for him, to tryt o soften him up before the Rumble. But they won’t phase him. They can’t be allowed to stop him. 60 men in the ring, entering one at a time, only one can be the winner..... There will be enough in there to keep even the likes of Sean McClancy on his toes... although fate has smiled upon him being one of the last to enter the ring. He can live with that. Sean a strong man but has had his mind on other things as of late and needs to get his mind into focus on what is needed to become the world Champion... The one belt he never got a shot at in his first time in the GIWF. The PWW never let him even get close to that level. He held it three times in his third federation, and once he had left, they relized that they couldn’t survive with out the likes of him and the rest of the KnightMare Brigade, so they closed. And the BDW closed as soon as he got the belt which he now carries with him. The Man who has made hard core a house hold name, and continued the legacy of his trainer in the hardcore arts, Ace Thunder. This is the type of match he lives for, a match that will test him and the others in that ring. Why go thru with it? Because he has to ? No because he wants to YES. Sean now stands at the door to a new life the one with out his sister calling the shots. His twin who is now married and waiting on the birth of her twins. Sean Stares at the back wall of the arena. Empty and not in use at this time. He is the only person sitting in the dark he realizes that the three scrolls he sent out not even a week ago will change his life once they are all opened and accepted as his choice. He’s made his decision. Everything that Ian had told him about the last few months had come to a major head. And now was the time to make his move. He was bidding for his placement in the royal line. To be considered as a full prince of the Seelie Courts. He’d kept his promise and had become a World Champion. He had done eveyrthing he was supposed to do, except one. His blood ran hot, mostly that of the blood of the Fomorian in him He needed to be awakened fully as the inheritor of the mantle of Bres Mac Elatha, The Prince of the Fomorians, and once King of the Tautha de Dannan. It was time for him to make his choice; something that was out of his hands. Without this side of him he wouldn't be able to pull off two matches in one night. He knows his first opponent Oni was going to be at least a slight challenge, but 59 others would be more of a challenge. Not one name on that list should be taken lightly. If you take one lightly you drop your guard and your mind doesn't stay focused.... Focused on what though? His love of Patti Anderson? His now Fiancée for little more then a year. She’s been on his mind constanly since he met the fiery red head at a show, and moved into a whirlwind of an affair with her. And then she won the right to be his valet for one night… And then that tape… Sean was mad… OH that blasted tape..... How did they get it? Sean's mind went wondering on who could of sent it to Tropical storm and why. The one group of guys who would be STUPID enough to try to captalize upon it’s potential use as a money making machine to try to embarrass Sean and Patti at the same time. Could it have been that DeAngelo guy who Sean kicked out of the KB? Would he be that maliciuos? Sean would have to find out. He felt bad for what he did to them, Jaime and Miguel, as he wasnt very gentle in pulling his punches for practice. He beat them pretty good. But then again he had a few marks himself where the twins had caught him off guard and sandwiched him a few times. Over all the practice went well, four on one wasn't at all what was going to be happening in a few days no it would go as much as 59 on 1; if no one was shoved out. Then again there was enough heated blood between the men on the roster to set that dome on fire. Tokyo would never be the same once the UWF left that's for sure. His sister’s fed may have to find a new venue for their Pay Per View in a month, as there may not be much left of the Dome when their through. As the Irish Bastard sits there and wonders what will be next a hand comes down and slides down his arm he quickly pulls away. Annie: Ye were so far away, ye did’na ‘ear me callin’ yer name. Sean : ‘Ow long ‘ave ye been ‘ere? Annie: Abou’ 30 minutes. It looked like ye where unwinding er thinking abou’ some of the mistakes made in practice tonight, so I didn't want t’ be botherin’ ye. But they are ready to close up fer th’ nigh’, and ye weren'na answering them so they asked me t’ come an’ talk t’ ye. Sean stands up and starts to head down to the center of the arena, it seems that like other times before, a woman would be his guide even through the darker times of the day. He knows what has to be done. Sean: ‘Ow are Jamie and Miguel ? Annie: They ‘ave told Penny that they won’na be volunteering to ‘elp[ye ou’ any time soon, ye beat on them pretty good. They’ll feel it in th’ mornin, an ‘fer the next few days. Sean: Yeah, well they deserved wha’ ever they ga’. Trust me on that one. Annie: As always, me Prince. I take ye fer yer word and would never question it. Annie gave a slight smile to him, as she placed her hand at the small of his back he stopped and turned around. Annie: I didn'na mean anythin’ by it. Sean:I know, I’m jus’ worried. I guess ye can call it jitters after sendin’ ou’ the scrolls t’ announce me intentions t’ the Court. Yer in th’ Order, yer assigned t’ me. Ye should know wha’s goin’ on. Annie: Yes, I do. I get updates every day. I can tell ye tha’ ye’ve started a stir that will make Ohgma tear in ‘is ancient eyes. In fact, I can tell ye tha’ Ishram ‘imself ‘as left Spain t’ ‘ead t’ Ireland t’ address th’ Court. Sean: Bu’ did I do th’ riogh’ thing? Annie: Oh, ye did th’ riogh’ thing. Even I can tell ye tha’. Annie takes a step ahead of him and turns around and delivers a kiss onto his cheek. Annie: I think it is a wonderful Idea, just wish ye ‘ad done it sooner. Sean: I bloody well ‘ope yer riogh. And with this, we fade until the next one.
