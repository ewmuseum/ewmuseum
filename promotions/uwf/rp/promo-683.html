UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #683

Getting MEAN...

Sept 8th (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Chuckie Cheese's....#1 for SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The THC Title. The only of Seymour’s numerous belts won that he never actually pinned anyone for. He yoinked the title away from Beezlebozo, only to lose it thirty minutes later. But now, Almasy has a chance to actually win the title, and become a two time champion by earning it. His opponent is the demented prisoner persona of Ezra Kazee, Mr. Meaner. If Kazee wants to get Meaner, well then… Seymour will just have to get….oh God, do I have to say it? Seymour Almasy: Yes, you do. Fine then…. Meanerer. ------ One of the UWF’s all time great superstars stands outside a Chuckie Cheese restaurant. Inside all is mirthful laughing, 50 cent arcade games, and pie after pie of really bad pizza served by a guy in a mouse costume. But outside, all is gloom and doom. Seymour Almasy is making sure of that. Seymour Almasy: Ah, I have arrived, in a place to do my mean and evil deeds. Almasy is dressed in full battle armor, the Divine Blade Imperial II at his side in its scabbard. Seymour Almasy: My opponent’s name is Mr. Meaner. Apparently, he is a very mean man indeed. So mean, that he was placed in the prison for the meanest people in this land, Alcatraz. That is a mean sounding name, so surely, Mr. Meaner must be mean. And in the THC division, where weapons and other mean things can be used with impunity, meanness is an excellent quality to have. The Final Fantasy counts on his fingers, as if to check the placement of all of his uses of the word mean, and their proper usage. Seymour Almasy: Hence, in order to defeat Mr. Meaner, and become the THC Champion, I must become… Silence reigned, before Almasy’s voice boomed out. Seymour Almasy: MR. MEANERER! No sooner than the words escape his lips do a mother and child walk out of the Chuckie Cheese. The mother is yapping on a cell phone, while the child happily carries a balloon. Wordlessly, Almasy draws his Divine Black, taking careful aim…whispering his evil intent. Seymour Almasy: Cursed balloon, die. Now. With a mighty, looping swing, Seymour cuts the balloon into two half-balloons. Unfortunately for the child, half-balloons don’t float so well, and the latex pieces fall to earth. Immediately, the poor youth begins bawling. Child: WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH!!! The mother doesn’t even look back, continuing to yap away as her child bawls and attracts all sorts of attention from passersby. Seymour Almasy: MWAHAHAHA! I AM…MR. MEANERER~! Resheathing the mighty Divine Blade, Almasy begins to walk away from the Chuckie Cheese, gloating. Seymour Almasy: Could Mr. Meaner do such a truly vile and nefarious deed? Of course not! He’s too busy beating up on defenseless videogames! Certainly, video games are innocent targets, but they have no emotions! No souls! A true mean person does his mean deeds to people! Human beings cry, shed tears. I am truly meaner than Mr. Meaner! NOT HIM BUT MEEEEEEEEEEE! A car swerves out of the way to avoid Seymour, who is walking in the MIDDLE of the street. It crashes into another parked car, and the two owners of the vehicles begin to argue. Seymour Almasy: In all seriousness…Mr. Meaner, if you think you can frighten me by beating up a bunch of video games that I happen to enjoy, you’re sadly mistaken. You’re just lucky that they’re games. If you really were in Final Fantasy I, Chaos would beat the crap out of you in under five minutes. Unfortunately, you are in a worse situation than that. You are in MY Final Fantasy. And as far as you’re concerned, I may as well have eleventh billion HP, since you’re simply not going to put a dent in me, even with your MEAN weaponry. I mean, how can a chair, or a bat, or even barbed wire stand up to the might of one of the finest swords in the world, the Divine Blade Imperial II? Almasy draws his sword again, presumably because he enjoys showing it off…or is compensating for something. Your call. Nonetheless, he makes several admirable slices and swipes with his long blade, and smiles. Seymour Almasy: So you’ve spent time in Alcatraz. Pssh. That’s not tough. I’d love to see you survive for a DAY in the D-District Prison, toughest place in all of Galbadia! I was so bad that they BEGGED me to leave! They pleaded for me to get out so I wouldn’t cause any more damage. I’m bad, and I’m mean. FAR meaner than you could ever be! He trips a passerby by extending his foot, sending the man careening head over heels. The Final Fantasy laughs coldly, before running to let the air out of the back rear tire of a nearby car. Seymour Almasy: The Hardcore Championship of the Universal Wrestling Federation is a belt that should only be held by the MEANEST of the mean in the UWF. For a while, Mr. Meaner, you have been able to lay claim to the title of the meanest man in the UWF. But now, there is a new meanest man in town. And his name is Seymour Almasy. I’m so mean that mean’s my middle name! Well, actually, I don’t have a middle name, BUT IF I DID, IT WOULD BE “MEAN”! Almasy turns back, cackling at all of the damage his MEAN acts have caused. People stand yelling, crying, and violently kicking things. Seymour Almasy: With a mean man like myself in control of the THC title, the UWF as a whole will be meaner. Battles will rage out of control! Violence will reign supreme! And this era of the UWF will begin this weekend, when I capture the THC title after MEANLY beating you to a bloody pulp. I don’t need weapons, Mr. Meaner…Ezra Kazee, whatever or whoever the hell you are. I’m a former UWF Champion. Former Global Champion. I have more accolades than you have “e”’s in your miserable name. But give me those weapons…and you will see the most dangerous man in this game today. That is what you face. That is who you face…and if I was you, the fright would be setting in about now. The challenger sets his jaw. The stubble on his face combines with his MEAN expression to produce a frightening combination. Seymour Almasy: It’s too bad for you, really. The UWF brass know that I want a shot at every title. And they have chosen to give up the THC Title first. In doing so, they have given up you, Mr. Meaner. They have led the chocobo to the slaughter. But please, try to fight fate. Bring every trick you learned in Alcatraz. Bring every weapon you are capable of wielding. It shall make your inevitable defeat all the more amusing, and crushing you all the more fun. The Final Fantasy cackles madly, eyes gleaming. Seymour Almasy: And then, one phase of my title hunt will have concluded, as I put the THC Championship around my waist. Then, there shall be only one question left… Seymour drags his thumb across his throat in a slashing gesture. Seymour Almasy: Which champion’s next? (Fade).
