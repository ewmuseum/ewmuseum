UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #248

The ecstasy

27 Feb 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We see Pyretta Blaze and the Quiet One hanging out in the main room of Hell’s Carnival, watching some wholesome UWF programming. The Quiet One appears to be taking notes. Blaze appears to be seething. Blaze: “How DARE he say that!? I swear I’m gonna set his dick on fire!” Beelzebozo: “I certainly hope it’s not my dick that’s in danger…” Blaze smiles a little as the World Champion enters the room. Blaze: “Of course not, dear.” She cocks her head. “You don’t look angry.” Beelzebozo: “No, unfortunately I’m at a loss as to how to get back where I need to be. But let’s dwell on that later. Right now, I need to know what’s going on with my opponents this weekend.” Blaze: “But—“ Beelzebozo: “First things first. Quiet One, the notes, if you would please.” The mime obligingly hands him his notes as the clown seats himself on the couch next to him. Beelzebozo studies them. “Joshua Dunn…wait, wait, wait. He didn’t even mention the match?” Blaze: “No, he was too busy playing with kids and toys to care.” Beelzebozo: “I’m not taking that set-up. It’s way too easy. But still, what’s up with this behavior? Is he so confident of victory that he’s willing to completely ignore myself and my partner? Or is he just that stupid?” He pauses. “Either way, I suppose I should watch out for him. He did win that Mt. Gagazet match after all, and that’s a somewhat impressive feat.” He returns to his studies. “Iron Lung…apparently just rattled off some tired old crap about how his team’ll win, how my team is a couple of clowns…well, he’s half right…our egos will make us unfit partners, blah blah blah, nothing interesting or original. Oy.” He tosses the notes to the floor and leans back, rubbing his eyes. “Whose bright idea was it to book this match? It makes sense to have me facing off against Dunn, but why the hell are this no-talent greenhorn and a loud-mouthed egotist being tosses into the mix?” Blaze: “Speaking of your narcissistic partner…” Beelzebozo: “Sorry hon, not right now.” Blaze: “But—“ She shuts her mouth as the clown turns to stare at her. Beelzebozo: “LATER. I have something to go do, and I need the mime’s help.” Blaze bolts out of the ugly old orange chair and moves right in front of the clown. Blaze: “Will you wait one goddamn minute please??” Beelzebozo: “Honey, you’re about to get stomped.” Blaze: “He besmirched my name!!” Beelzebozo: “………..All right. Who are you talking about? The mime? Do I have to put him in the Box again?” Blaze: “I’m talking about your damn partner, Kenny Saturn or Neptune or whatever.” Beelzebozo: “Mercury, I believe. Anyway, what’s the problem? What’s he saying?” Blaze: “He…he called me a little skank!!” Beelzebozo: “…And?” Blaze: “What do you mean, ‘and?’ He called me a nasty name and I want you to kick his ass for it!” Beelzebozo: “Oh for…” He puts his arm around her. “Baby, you’re just going to have to get a thicker skin about this sort of thing. It’s professional wrestling; there’s going to be a lot of name calling going on. Sometimes, people will direct it at you, hoping to get me riled up. But what you have to understand is that it’s just part of the game. You can’t take it seriously, no matter HOW much they mean it. I mean, sure, Mr. Mercury will get his sooner or later, and your honor will be fine and dandy once more. But for now, I have to team with him, so let’s just let this sort of thing go for now, OK?” Blaze pulls away from him and puts her hands on her hips, stamping her foot as she throws a mini-tantrum. Blaze: “No, it’s not OK! I demand vengeance, and I demand that you get it for me! I demand you kick his stupid ass!” The clown hauls off and slaps her. She glares at him, holding her cheek. Beelzebozo: “Settle down, woman. You do not DEMAND anything of me, do you understand?” Blaze sullenly nods her head. “Good. Now, unless there’s anything else I need to know about, I must be away from this place.” Blaze: “He said you wouldn’t be World Champ for long, implying he’d be the one to take the belt off of you.” Beelzebozo: “I’ve heard that one before. And I’m sure I’ll hear it many more times to come. Honey, just go have some tea, set something inexpensive on fire, and we’ll talk later.” The two men turn to leave. Blaze looks desperate to make the clown angry. Blaze: “…He said the THC is crap, not a real wrestling belt, and people who wrestle hardcore are making up for other deficiencies in their wrestling abilities.” Beelzebozo: “I’LL RIP HIS GODDAMN SPINE OUT AND STRANGLE HIM WITH IT!!!” The World Champ turns toward the camera and snarls. “You think hardcore wrestling is for losers and idiots, Mercury? Is that what you think!? You pathetic little peon! Why don’t you come have a match with me and see who the loser ends up being! I won’t even HAVE to rely on the weapons and tables, because I can not only outfight you, and outthink you, but outwrestle you too! You’re a walking dead man, you hear me!? YOU HEAR ME!? You are dead by dawn!!!” Foam begins to build up at the corners of the clown’s mouth. He simply stares into the camera, breathing heavily. Blaze: “YAY!! He’s angry again! And my honor shall be secure!” The Quiet One tugs on her arm. When she looks at him, he begins furiously scribbling on his notepad. She reads it, comprehension dawning on her face. “Crap in a hat…you’re right. He can’t kill him until after the match this weekend; otherwise he’ll have to wrestle a handicap match. OK, think! How do we focus his anger?” The mime scribbles again. “Hey…good idea!” Blaze carefully approaches the raging clown, and taps his shoulder. Beelzebozo: “YOU DIE NEXT.” Blaze: “Honey, before you go off and kill Mercury, you should know that…well…” Beelzebozo: “WHAT!?!” Blaze jumps and lets out a little yip. After she regains her composure (which must be hard when a psychotic clown is staring at you with unbridled hatred) she speaks once more. Blaze: “Iron Lung and Joshua Dunn made him say those things.” The clown cocks his head, seemingly confused. “Yeah, you see…well…they have one of his favorite ring rats held captive, and if he doesn’t besmirch you and I, and all the things we care about…they’re going to shoot her and leave her in a ditch.” Blaze backs away, warily eyeing the clown. Beelzebozo turns back to the camera. Beelzebozo: “You scumbags. How DARE you try and turn my partner and me against each other? You’d actually kidnap some poor girl and force Mercury to say such nasty things about the THC belt?” He growls. “You two bring new meaning to the term ‘bastards.’ “Well, I assure you that this travesty is not going to go unpunished. You hear me? You slimeballs are in for the match of your lives, and when it’s over, Mercury’s rat will be returned to him, and you two will be lying in shallow graves somewhere, just like you deserve. I’m going to tear you two apart, and if you’re lucky I might let Mercury have a go at you as well.” He sneers. “Your pathetic attempts at mind games have failed miserably. I know your game, and it isn’t going to help you now. I’m dedicated to making sure my partner and I win that match on Sunday. “Sure, Kenny Mercury has an ego a size of his namesake, but I certainly do not. I merely state the facts. So when I say Joshua Dunn and Iron Lung are in for a world of suffering, that’s exactly what’s going to happen. Don’t try to deny it, don’t try to run from it…simply accept your fate and prepare for pain. “I don’t have to like my partner…but I sure as hell think more highly of him than I do you two. Between the two of us, there’s not a chance in all of creation for you. “And when I break you, and you’re lying there choking on your own blood, you’re going to hear a sound through the hazy mist that’ll guide you on your journey to the final destination. “That sound will be Kenny Mercury…and Beelzebozo…having the Last Laugh.” Beelzebozo screeches and rushes the camera, and we quickly fade to black.
