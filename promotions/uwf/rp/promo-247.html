UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #247

The agony

2/26-2/27/04 (year unrecorded)

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open on a darkened room, with only five candles providing light. The candles, somehow, are emitting reddish flames. The World Champion sits in the middle of the room cross-legged, the candles forming a loose circle about him. He appears to have shaved his head, and he wears only a loose-fitting pair of black warm-up pants. His hands lies atop each other on his lap, palms up, and a portable CD player lies atop them, the headphones covering his ears. The flickering red light gives the impression that the clown is submerged in a sea of blood; only a lack of air bubbles destroys the illusion. Well, that and the glowing candles, of course… Beelzebozo’s face is slack, but alert. His breathing is shallow, almost nonexistent. No more than the most necessary of movements does he make. He almost seems…at peace. Luckily, Pyretta Blaze appears from the darkness behind him and gives us something else to look at besides the apparently comatose champion. Blaze coughs politely, eliciting no reaction from Beelzebozo. She coughs again, louder this time. Still no reaction. She begins hacking like a 50-year, 3 pack a day smoker. Beelzebozo: “I’m a bit busy, in case you didn’t notice. You’ve got 30 seconds before I pound your face into a bloody ruin.” Blaze: “Just wanted to give you a report on the progress of things, hon.” Beelzebozo: “Very well. Your face is spared for the moment.” He slowly reaches up and pulls the headphones off. As he does so, some sort of loud, heavy metal music reaches our ears. His eyes slowly roll open. “Speak.” Blaze: “Well, I…say…you shaved your head.” Beelzebozo: “I did.” Blaze: “How come? I kinda liked your crazy hairstyle you had going.” Beelzebozo: “Let me ask you something. Have I always had that hairstyle, since you’ve known me?” Blaze: “Yes you have, sweetie pie.” Beelzebozo: “And have you ever wondered why I had my hair like that?” Blaze: “I dunno, not really. Just figured it was some sort of fashion statement.” Beelzebozo: “Well, you’re not the only one. I have no idea why my hair was like that either.” Blaze: “……..Oooo-kay.” Beelzebozo: “Every day, I styled my hair into that garish array. I have no idea why. That style meant nothing to me. It looked like something a cartoon would have on his head. Why would I do that? Because I’m a clown? I mean, really…what possible reason could I have for it? I only seemed to do it because…that was what I was supposed to do. As if some puppet master was pulling my strings, or some ghost of ages past whispering in my ear, urging me on. I realized that there really WAS no reason to have my hair shaped in that way. No good reason to go through all that effort.” Blaze: “So you decided to do something about it.” Beelzebozo: “Indeed. And I like it, I do.” Blaze: “I think I could get used to it.” She smiles. Beelzebozo: “Now, tell me what is so important that you had to interrupt me. And be quick about it.” Blaze: “Interesting music for meditating.” The clown glances down at his CD player, and switches it off. Beelzebozo: “Who said I was meditating?” Blaze: “Well, looking around, I figured—“ Beelzebozo: “You figured wrongly. I am not looking for inner peace or ascendance. Quite the opposite, really.” Blaze: “What do you mean?” Beelzebozo: “Look at me. I’m the World Champion. I’ve made my first successful defense of that title. I am on top of the world, in so short a time. And I am bound to stay there for as long as I choose.” Blaze: “Yes, that’s all true…” Beelzebozo: “Furthermore, the naysayers are silenced. They may have scoffed at the idea of a devilish wrestling clown, but now they are silent, their wagging tongues cowed by the sight of my devastating prowess in the ring. They no longer whisper behind my back, for they fear I will hear them and come down upon them with great vengeance and violent retribution.” Blaze: “Also true.” Beelzebozo: “And thus my dilemma. I…cannot find a good reason to be full of rage. For it was my anger that drove me to become the best here, it was my fury that drove me to prove them wrong. My malice towards the company, and Ryder, for slipping in that stipulation drove me to my greatest heights yet here in the UWF, and even propelled that match into a classic that shall long be remembered. It set a precedent for violence and spectacle, and Ryder and I both realize that. “And now…what is there to anger me? I am comfortably seated at the top of the UWF’s hierarchy. The wrestlers own the company. There are none who can possibly provide a worthy challenge for me. And so, the fire is dying down. The anger is slipping away from me. I fear I am growing…content.” Blaze reacts with surprise, starting a bit. Her eyes are wide. Blaze: “Content?…How can this be?” Beelzebozo: “I do not know, my dear Blaze. I do not know. But I fear this could make things…difficult for me in the future. If I cannot summon forth all my hatred to use against me enemies…what weapon have I left? Skills and athleticism can only carry one so far…what good are the tools if one does not have the drive to use them properly? As the fire needs the wood, and the rain needs the storm, so do I need my anger. What can I do? What SHOULD I do??” Blaze: “I…I don’t know.” Beelzebozo: “Thus my solitude. I sit here, listening to heart-pounding, hate-drenched music and think nothing but bloody, horrid thoughts. I am trying to bring back that which I feed on, that which fuels me. For if I cannot bring myself to feel the rage once more…what is to become of me?” The two sit and stand silently for a few moments, their heads downcast. Eventually, Beelzebozo raises his head once more. “Now…tell me, before I am forced to hurt you…what did you want in the first place?” Blaze: “Oh, that. Right.” She clears her throat. “…You know, perhaps it can wait. I don’t know if it’s that important after all.” The clown’s face darkens, and he seems to be growing upset. Then, suddenly, his face clears, and he smiles sadly as he shakes his head. Beelzebozo: “I adore you for trying, but I’m afraid it won’t be that easy to bring back what I lost.” Blaze: “Damn.” She sighs. “Can’t blame a gal for trying.” Beelzebozo: “Run along now, dear. I shall emerge from here soon, and you can tell me everything you need to.” Blaze: “As you wish.” She blows a kiss to the back of the clown’s head before leaving. The clown sighs. Beelzebozo: “I must focus…I must anger myself…must let the loathing build…” His eyes close. “Ruff Ryder…he wants to be thrown into the mix of my upcoming title defense against Joshua Dunn.” He frowns. “As if he provided me enough of a challenge the first time to dare try and worm his way into another shot at the belt.” His voice grows louder. “Such folly, thinking himself good enough to deserve yet ANOTHER chance, after failing the first time?! How DARE he?! HE…” His face softens, as does his voice. “But then…he didn’t complain about the way our match went. He didn’t dwell on the chaos that reigned, or the call from that goofy referee…he took his pain like a man and came back for more. He didn’t use the loophole like I thought he would, even when he had every right to do so. He provided me with worthy sport that night, and who knows? Perhaps he could again. “Maybe…I should consider…” His voice falters, and he looks up, pained. “What the hell am I saying!?” He buries his head in his hands. “What am I going to do?…” And with that, the scene fades out.
