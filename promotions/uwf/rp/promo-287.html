UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #287

Who wants it more.

March 13 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — UWF Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<< No man can predict the future and be 100 % accurate. No man can guarantee anything. Yet every day someone guarantees something to happen, and eventually is found with their foot in their mouth. So would it not be considered foolish for Iron Lung to be so overly confident that he will beat Malcolm Kennedy at SAS? Malcolm Kennedy, The fastest rising superstar in the UWF, The man who has fought and hung in with the best of them, The man who has beaten UWF greats like Jamie Shock, Kenny Mercury, and each member of the Local NVA Company, Simply put, The One Man Army. No doubt he will share his opinion. >>> *** The scene opens late at night. The camera is outside the arena for the upcoming SAS. The camera is aimed at the top of the building and it slowly starts to pan down. As it nears the ground Malcolm Kennedy comes into view. Just standing on the sidewalk, looking at the stars. He wears a pair of black jeans and his black One Man Army shirt covered up by a large leather trench coat that is half buttoned. A gold chain around his neck. He continues to stand there and stare at the stars. His breath steams in the cold night air as the camera draws near him. The camera comes a halt about a foot and half from his body. Still no reaction from him. He suddenly speaks, not breaking his stare on the stars above. *** Malcolm Kenned: “ First off, Iron Lung, lets clear something up. The THC division is a joke. A shell of what it used to be. So no one really cares wether or not you lost that stupid belt. Move on. You sound like me when I thought the THC meant something. Forget that belt, If you want your words to hold water here in the UWF, you need to start facing some real competition. But hey, tomorrow night is your chance when you step in the ring with me.” “ You talk about having the odds in your favor. So be it. Think what you want. It doesn’t matter to me. My history here in the UWF is a lot more impressive than yours. But Ill let my actions against you help back that up just in case.” “You suggest I stay in the locker room and think? Your confused Lung, sad and confused. I don’t give two shits about what people think or say, I don’t care if all the odds in the world are against me going into a match. Because every single Sunday when my music hits, I still walk down that isle and step into that ring and put my blood, sweat, and tears into my match. Every single time. You cant go back and look at ANY of matches and tell me I didn’t try my hardest to get the win. Does that mean I’ve won them all? Hell no. I don’t care if you just lost your first match or if your on a 2,000 match winning streak. When that bell rings, your just another opponent.” *** Malcolm moves steps a little in his spot without really moving, still staring at the sky. *** “You wanna make me your first victim on your way back to THC. That’s fine. Ill be happy to show you what THC can truly be about. I can go with the best of the best. Hardcore, Straight Wrestling, High Risk, it does not matter. If it’s a fight your looking for, Im the man who will gladly give it to you.” “ You surround yourself with some stupid obsession of THC. Soon you will see what a lost cause it is. And be left in the ring embarrassed when you look out in the crowd and realize no one in the world gives a shit. No one cares about everything you’ve done, about the physical hell you put yourself through match after match, about how you put your body on the line night after night to put food on the table. All for nothing. THC is dead my friend. No one cares about it.” “ You want to put me flat on my back? All you gotta do is show up and Ill give you all the shots you want. One word of advice though, boy, You better use them wisely, because all it takes is one slip up and before you know it your on the flat of your own back, defeated and worthless.” *** Malcolm snaps his finger once to show how quick he means. *** “ You see Lung, so many people have stepped up to the plate against me and promised to beat me. To destroy me. To end my career. And every night I walk out on my own two feet. So if you want to go ahead and make these idle threats and false promises, that is fine with me. Just remember I’ve made better wrestlers who have had more on the line eat their words, so making you do the same wont be too hard. You can bank on that.” *** Malcolm finally looks down at the camera. A sliver a smile on his face. His eyes word and baggy from lack of sleep. *** “ Tomorrow I will show up for out match. Ill hear my music roar, and the fans cheer my name as I walk down the isle. Once again that bell will ring and I will be in the place I perform best. The place where I’ve proven night after night that I can hang with the best, and I can beat the best. Your just like every other hopeful I’ve stepped into the ring with these past couple months. Blinded by the way you want your future to be, too stupid too focus on the present. Yeah one day history might repeat itself and you will once again be the THC champion. But you got another thing coming if you think your gonna start your path to the title with me. But hey, Stranger things have happened and there have been much bigger upsets by wrestlers much lower on the ladder than you.” “ Lung I want you to bring you’re A+ game. I want you to be in perfect running order. I want you to see that no matter how bad you want to make it big here in the UWF, I want it that much more. And that in the end, that’s what really makes the difference. Not your past, not a piece of gold around your waist, not the number of fans that come to see you wrestle Sunday after Sunday. All that matters is how bad you want to be the best. And that’s whats gonna leave you staring at the ceiling tomorrow night and not me. Tomorrow we are both going to battle with each other, But I will be the one that walks out the victor. Ill see you in the ring.” *** Malcolm stares for a couple more seconds at the camera. He then looks back up at the stars. After a good 30 seconds or so, he walks out of view of the camera, still staring at the stars and he goes. The scene fades to black. ***
