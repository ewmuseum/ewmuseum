Revelation and Realisation

23 Jul 2004

Joshua Dunn — A Park in the Small hours

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Finally, after all this time, the crowning achievement of Joshua Dunn’s career has been reached, he has finally won the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. The Mystery Main event was the challenging for the World Title between the two men that fought for that right at Stable Wars, Joshua and Kevin. Why was the Main event kept secret? Did you see how many more tickets were sold for SAS? Or how many more people tuned in? That’s why, and they were not disappointed, with a gruelling match that ended in Dunn’s coronation, as the pinnacle of the federation. But so many people have been corrupted by the power that comes with the title. It may just be a matter of time before the Crowds enjoy Booing the Communist Scotsman again, but after just winning their support, isn’t it about time they had a champion they could actually cheer for?] The air was crisp and cold; His breath froze in mid air, the moon bright in the sky above him. Joshua walked through the desolate park alone, a duffel brown trench coat wrapped around him trying to keep himself warm and a black hat hugging close to his head. Joshua enjoyed the midnight stroll, it wasn’t because he liked being in desolate public places, it was the cold sharp smell, and the clean feeling that came with the night that he enjoyed. Ornate street lamps lined the path, coating it with a soft glow that was company enough for him. The park was clean, hardly any litter thrown around, and the silence was comforting, no one around, and he could be alone with his thoughts. The crunch of the gravel under him made its own rhythmic tune, accompanied by a melody played by the wind in his ears, he felt completely relaxed. It may seem strange, after just winning his biggest prize in such a close proximity to winning Stable wars the Joshua wasn’t out celebrating, but He didn’t feel like it. The party would seem hollow, as he knew well what has happened to may greats that have held the World Title before him. Seymour Almasy fell from grace with a bump, now the crowd see him more like a relic than a man, Digital Mortality switched from face to heel almost instantaneously. Tsunami Oni, people grew tired of him and as for Jamie Shock? He was responsible for giving the title to a chimpanzee He took in a large breath of the crisp air around him, holding it in for a relatively long period of time before exhaling, the cool feeling inside his lungs was refreshing, and it was almost as good as some medicines. His whole body felt the chill of the night air, apart from his head, even in the coldest weather, the bandages kept his head warm and itchy. He removed his hat and put it into his trench coats pocket before he tried to wrap the trench coat further around him, but he couldn’t, his large frame prevented it. The light on the path gradually faded out in the next few steps, the lamp here had been hit by some kid who was bored and alone, why do people turn to vandalism for comfort? This always puzzled Joshua, as he never understood it. Instead of contemplating on the matter further he merely shook his head. The chill was getting to him, and almost placed for his own personal convenience, there was a bench sitting there in the darkness. He huddled down on the wooden thing and pulled himself into one corner. The heat would return to him soon, but in the mean while, he could use the rest, he’d been walking for some time now, what was it 3, 4 in the morning? He’d lost track of time, his thoughts just carried him further, and his feet wandered where they wanted to. Some people who believed in fate would say it was that that had brought him to that specific place at that specific time, but no one really knows in the end. World Champion Joshua Dunn. It had a certain ring to it, just as Intercontinental Champion Joshua Dunn and Shoot Champion Joshua Dunn. It was basically just another hunk of tin to brag about, and some use it more than others, Ruff Ryder keeps going on how he was World champion, even though he only held it for a matter of minutes. Bragging doesn’t make someone sound better than anyone else, it was just a way to try and make your existence mean something to other people, and Joshua Loathed it. There was a time where he would have revelled in winning the world champion as if it was his crowning achievement, but somehow the feeling felt hollow, nothing like how he’d imagined it would have been. There was something missing, a feeling he hadn’t felt since the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. He knew exactly what the problem was, he’d become a show man. He desired to be a spectacle, the centre of attention, usually that’s what world champs got, but he wanted his memory etched firmly into the minds of millions. He was beating himself up about it, he didn’t like the feeling that it brought, but it’s the only thing that would make the feeling go away, for a time. He was terminally caught in a catch 22 situation. Either way he’d still end up feeling miserable. Speaking of which, the medication he was given by the doctor wasn’t much help, the cream soothed his irritated scalp for a while, but not for that long. The amnesia pills weren’t taking effect yet, not properly, their side effects were fully set in, his stomach hadn’t been still since starting the medication and he was having trouble getting to sleep and getting up in the mornings. When he was awake he was incredibly drowsy… he refrained from using them 24 hours before his matches he was booked for, it would only hinder him in the ring, but their effectiveness was reduced dramatically by this, on the plus side, names and faces that were all but lost to him started popping into the fore front of his mind now and again, with no warning, it was as if the inactive part of his brain was in a spasm and throwing memories around all over the place. This bench is actually rather comfortable, the feelings of his outer extremities were returning and the breeze was quite relaxing, his head started to roll forward. Those damn pills, he could feel himself drifting to sleep on a park bench, but he would be lucky if he could get a wink of sleep in his bed. The next gust of shrill wind prevented that from happening though, his body automatically tensing to try and preserve the heat; it would possibly another couple of hours before he could settle again. He listened to the wind, and looked out to the view, it was a strange view for a bench really, you couldn’t see anything pleasing to the eye, in fact, it just looked onto a rather neglected looking area of shrubs, he looked down at the gravel at his feet, he hadn’t noticed before, because of the darkness, but there was score marks in the grass, the bench had been dragged several yards from where it was originally, wherever that was. It was very odd, the drowsiness left him and a slight paranoia set in, he wondered exactly the purpose of this arranging, the lights were actually out for some distance around the bench, and shrubby area prevented any viewing from the road. He suddenly felt slightly unsafe in his surroundings, sitting bolt up right like a mere cat, this is a feeling he hadn’t felt often, seeing as not many people bother 6 ft 4 men weighing 284 lbs. The anxiety was exhilarating. It seemed to counteract the effects of the pills, all that was going through his mind at the moment was what’s about to happen. And then it happened, it filled his entire universe. He heard the scream of a woman off to his left. Woman’s voice: HELP SOMEBODY PLEASE HELMMMPH! Man’s voice: You try that again we might kill you afterwards right? Over in the area of light, a few tens of feet away Dunn could make out three figures, and one wasn’t happy about being there, obviously a woman, her hands here being held behind her back by one man, and another was helping in marching her forward, her feet was dragging along the gravel of the path, but her feet were gaining no purchase, Joshua was almost insulted, he’d stumbled onto a rapists den. It made him want to get out of there as quickly as possible, but he just couldn’t leave that poor girl to be done with as they pleased. He felt so angry, but they were too far away for him to do anything yet. He knew where they were coming to though, so he could wait. His head flung to his bandages all of an instant after that thought, they would see them, bright white in the darkness, he pulled his black cap out of his pocket and put it on, preferring to have an extremely hot head rather than being discovered. He watched intently as the events unfolded, the men disgusted him; he’d make sure they regretted ever doing this to a woman, let alone several, as was probably the case. Man1: I’ll take first go Man2: You took first go last time Man1: and I’ll take first go again, ok? He raised his voice halfway through that question, he was obviously the brains of the operation, and the other man was just some lackey. Ugh, they sent a shiver down his spine, all the time the woman was struggling to free their grasp, and crying torrents, she knew full well what was about to happen to her, she liked the idea almost as much as Dunn did. She must have been just coming back from a late night at the office, her pin striped outfit of a jacket and skirt betrayed that fact, he hand bag was still flung over her shoulder, one of the men had a hand firmly clenched over her mouth. This secluded spot must have been well thought out. Another shiver went down through Joshua’s spine, but he was shrugging off the cold now. They pulled the woman into the centre of the path to prevent her from trying to latch herself onto a lamp post as she almost did. She soon started to lose her energy with all the struggling and the men were finding it easier to keep her under control. Joshua stood up for the first time and stood stock still, wondering exactly what they would do when they spotted him, the light of the street lamps were still helping hide him from them, but they would soon come into the darkness, their eyes would adjust and they’d see him, then what, so many things could happen, he wondered if they were armed, they probably were, he’d noticed that about people in America. They wouldn’t notice he was there, once they were out of the light they forced her against one of the broken lamp posts and one man held her arms to the other side, with her legs free she tried to kick the man in front of her, out of sheer desperation, and got a great money shot that Kevin Starr would have been proud of. As the man rolled around on the ground in obvious pain, the other on reached into his back pocket and pulled out something that shined in the dark, a knife. That got Joshua’s blood boiling, he started to edge closer to the scene edging slowly along the grass, keeping virtually still and silent, hoping his erratic breathing wouldn’t alert them. He held the knife to the woman’s throat and moved around to the front to look her in the eyes and intimidate her some more, he enjoyed to look of terror in their eyes before he had his way. Man1: You won’t try that again if you know what’s good for you. You ok down there? A few coughing noises came from his accomplice and he got to his feet Man2: I’m fine, just a little sick Man1: I should make you bleed for that bitch! The woman shook her head as much as she dared with the knife against her throat, her eyes stinging with tears Woman: Please, please no Man1: You know what? I think I will make you bleed! Joshua Pounced The man who had earlier had a shot to the family jewels went down, his face grinding down the gravel, Joshua clasped round his waist taking him down like a regular American footballer. The sudden movement made the man with the knife turn around to see what the commotion was, not thinking the woman tried to run for it, but the man grabbed her by the wrist and spun her round in front of him with the knife held to her throat again. Joshua got up from the ground and turned to the other man just to see the mess that he’d gotten the woman in, he knew he should have thought of something better, but he couldn’t just do anything. The woman looked terrified, and with good reason. The man behind him was clutching at his bleeding face; Dunn’s hands were bleeding, but not severely. He rammed a heel into his back for good measure to make sure he stayed down, but coming down on gravel with 284lbs on top of him should have been enough. Man1: Wrong Move mate, do anything wrong again and her death will be on your conscience A smile came across his face, the woman was looking horrified, pleading with him silently to do something, but the attacker wasn’t amused Man1: You think I’m joking? He tightened his grip and held the knife up to the top of her neck, forcing her head back; she let out a terrified yelp Man1: You think I wouldn’t do it? Joshua’s arms went up slowly, to emphasise that he wanted the man to calm down, but that wasn’t going to work, he knew that but he wanted to buy some time JD: Of course you’d do it, I’d do the same if I were you, but you can’t Man1: And WHY not? JD: Your legs are open Man1: What? It was at this point he was glad the woman wasn’t blonde, she picked up on the hint almost instantaneously and threw a leg back forcefully, and he attacker bent over double releasing his grasp and dropping his knife, holding something much more dear to him, not thinking the woman turned around and delivered a nasty knee to his face and sent him to the ground, blood pouring from his nose. Dunn walked over to him and picked up the knife. He pointed it at the guy who himself was now looking quite frightened before scrabbling to his feet and running for it, trying to get his friend to his feet too and they ran off JD: I’ll be waiting for you! He threw the knife far into the park, later he thought it wasn’t the best of idea’s, but he wanted the thing as far out of his sight as possible. He looked towards the woman who was still crying, but she was smiling all the same. She threw herself onto Joshua, her arms around his neck, catching him off guard, not expecting such a response, but he hugged back, the girl was probably just looking for the nearest person to comfort her Woman: Thank you thank you thank you thank you idon’tknowwhatiwouldhavedoneifyouhadn’tshownupiwasjustwalkingalonggoinghomefromtheofficeand- JD: It’s ok, it’s ok, you’re all right now That’s all the woman wanted to hear, she burst into another wave of tears right then, Joshua just patted her on the back, she’s had a bit of a scare, it was understandable. She stopped the embrace and looked at her saviour, wiping the tears from her eyes. Dunn noticed her face, under the make up that was running, she was still very attractive. Woman: how can I ever repay you? Dunn recognised the look on her face, he knew exactly what she meant, she was offering exactly what she didn’t want to let her assailants have. His blank look seemed to amuse her as she giggled, raising her hand to her mouth as though it was impolite. JD: Just keep smiling, that’s payment enough And just like that Joshua Turned to walk away, back the way he came, retracing his steps. The woman’s smile faded and she delved into her hand bag and produced something palm sized and ran after Joshua, grabbing his coat, and slipping the thing into his pocket. Woman: Wait, I don’t even know your name Dunn turned around, he hadn’t noticed what she’d done, but he looked at her one more time. JD: Joshua Dunn. Go home, rest and have the next day off, you’ll feel better for it And with that he walked away, leaving her in the light of the path watching him go The rotten feeling had left him, he now knew what he was missing, he wasn’t a showman at all, he just wanted to make people happy
