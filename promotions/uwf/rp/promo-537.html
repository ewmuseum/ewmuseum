Where else? The hospital. The two twins are lying strapped in place. To keep them from moving the after the fall they took. And thanks to the flaming dumpster that was shoved on top of them the chared ripped jeans and t-shirts and the smell of burnt human hair lingers in the air A doctor appears dressed in what looks to be a surgical outfit. The nurses that are attending to the twins are in almost the same dress. As they gingerly cut the jeans and t-shirt off the twins, the look of amazement can be seen even from behind the surgial masks they wear. Sean looks on from the outside of the room his red hair still has a lingering bluish color to it.The Doctor walks out to talk to Sean shaking his head. Doctor: These guys are from a wrestling show? If I didn’t know better they look like they have been beaten and then and attempt at burning them alive. I’m surprised they are in good condtion at all. Sean: It was a piss poor attempt t’ keep us from getting involved in a stupid fokin’ match. Bu’ we ‘ad no plans what so ever fer tha’. Ben knew tha’. ‘E knows me rules. Fok’ their partially th’ rules ‘e kept breakin’ t’ get thrown ou’ o’ the KnightMare Brigade. The Dead Pool isn’na even on our radar. They are na’ worth the KB’s time. At least they were’na. Now they ‘ave put their names on our blood list. If I ‘ave me way, they’ll be joining me brothers ‘ere in the ‘ospital. Doctor: Well just to let you know; I think they might be out for a week or two but it looks like your brother’s cloths took most of the damage, not their skin. I can’t explain it. I need to have their necks and back cleared before taking them off the boards. But first, what burns are on their skin need to be cleaned and dressed. It will be a while before you can go in and see them. Your friend… the doctor looks down a tthis clip board Mr. Baker, err, Cpl. Punishment is stiched up we had to wait to remove the Permanet marker from his forhead because of the open wound. But skin is a wonderful thing for that, a good scubbing to take the first layer off, and it’ll be like new. Almost like an exfoliating treatment. I don’t think he cares. He is just pissed off. He doesn’t have a concussion from the impact of the trophy So he has been released under his manger’s watchful eye. As for you I see most of the spray came off and your eyes were checked so you are free to leave when you are ready. Sean grimmaces at the thought of someone who he couldn’t care less about trying to take him down with a trick that makes him want to really just puke. But the doctor was right about the Twins. He coulnd’t exactly tell him that he was dealing with a couple of people who were in reality a cross breed of human, Dryad Faery and, their namesake, a giant species known as Fomorian. Most thought of it as a name for them, so they can play on Irish legends. Most didn’t know the truth, that it was also what they really were. For ages the Tautha De Dannan and the Fomorians, comprising the Seelie Courts had hidden, as with their Unseelie counterparts, like Annie. They’d hidden in Tir Na Nog, Ireland, and no one knew they were really there. Sean: They’re jus’ thicked skinned. Tha’s all. What do you want fer Irish boys tha’ grew up in the ring er th’ pub? I’ve sent fer their regular Doctors, though. They are on their way as we speak, flyin’ in on a TaleSmith Productions plane. They know the twins better then anyone. No offence t’ ye, but I be wantin’ me brothers t’ ‘ave the best, and these Doctors are the best money can buy. Doctor: I’ve dealt with your organization before, Mr. McClancy. Your sister already called us to inform us of their pending arrival. She’s in charge of the insurance so we have no choice but to give way. My nurses will be there to assist them as needed, and we’ll get them up to date with everything as soon as they’re here. Sean: Thank ye. Now, I think I’ll be wonderin’ aroun’, bu’ I’m na’ leavin’ yet. Sean walks away, his mind engaged in thought. It’s been a while since he’s been on his own now. . How do you explain a bloodline that most don’t think really exists in the real world? You can’t now can you. The Doctors from the order know the twins better then any other civilain doctor would. The plus side of being a member of the Order, and having your sister as the Mistress of the Secrets. You can call on her anytime you need it. She wasn’t happy to hear what happened; Sean had to promise to take care of this himself. Other wise she would of bbeen on the next plane out here to beat on Unforgiven herself. His name is fitting he is truly unforgiven. And to think of the history that man has with Sean McClancy, the twins, even… Fey. At one time Unforgiven Ben Jericho, Benjamin Jackson, was going to be Sean’s brother in law. Fortunatly Fey came to her senses. Now she’s married to the man who she was meant to be with. But still, at one time, The Fomorian brothers, all three of the giants, had accepted him as one of their own. And this is how they get repaid? By him assuming they’d get invovled in his match? If anyone had any reason to get into that match, it would have been Sean… for he wants Moraless’ head on a pike, to mount in his and Patti’s front fence post on their new ranch land in Washington State. But that’s beside the point, Moraless can wait if he needs to… Sean McClancy once made Ben Jericho, the man who now just goes by the name of Unforgiven, and he can unmake him also. Dp’s name has been added to the list of many that the KnightMare Brigade will take out. Mind you the twins know better hten letting a piece of ass distract them, let alone some piece of trash like that one “Two Bit”. After all they deal with Annie all the time. And she is a big distracetion for any man, except for one: Sean who has already shown that he only has eyes for one woman. Then again one time, many years ago, he’d of been all over her. Annie is his former fiancée after all. Sean wonders thru the hospital: Down hallways and through doors, finally up a flight of stairs… Some how ending up on the roof of the hospital….. He walks over to the side past the many ventilation fans and the likes, and then looks down to the large parking lot and at the freeway across the way. As he stands there the rain starts to fall, it seems even Mother Nature herself knows how he feels inside right now. To bad Patti wasn’t around. Sean’s muse, the one that can keep Sean from losing him-self within the Fomorian blood that runs in his veins. Most don’t know the truth of the Order, let alone the full on truth of the McClancy family. Sean turns his face up to the rain holding his mouth open to allowing it to fill his mouth and then he spits it out. A soft voice comes from behind him. Annie: I knew I would find ye up ‘ere. As he turns around in a start, and there stands Aine Leanan, or just Annie, the Manger of the KB. Her rain soaked hair is sticking to her face and the damp clothing she wears clings to her body. Sean: Yer suposed to be with Punishment. Annie: Ah, Ox is doin’ jus’ fine. Ol’ Ox-T can ‘andle ‘imeself. ‘E is at the ‘otel waiting for me to call him. Ye ferget tha’ I am na withou’ me charms. How could he forget? Sean hasn’t forgotten the trouble she has caused between him and Patti. But then again that wasn’t all her fault eather. Sean had forgotten to tell Patti he was bethrothed to someone, until that someone came to claim her prince. Sean: No I didn’na ferget. What do ye want? Maybe ye should be doing yer manger duties and getting me a match with someone. Or even putting these two on the list for a match with DP next. You know this won’na keep them down for a leng time. No matter what these quacks say. They’re Fomorian Princes, jes’ a few years younger them me. They’re the inheritors o’ the blood of our long line. Annie: It has to be the McClancy blood line! ‘Er am I mistaken tha’ your sister also didn’t listen t’ ‘he doctors and stayout o’ the ring. I swear ye McClancys are a tough breed of man an’ Faery. The twins are awake an’ they’re asking fer ye. They’re giving the regular docs a ‘ard time. Luckly the Order’s doctors ‘ave shown up and taken over. Sean: Just wonder what made Benny-Boy even think of attackin’ the twins? He truly doens’na think, na like he ‘as ever done it befer. Thinkin’ was never ‘is strong suit. I Guess I was ‘opin’ after all these years, ‘e would ‘ave grown up jus’ a little. Annie: Then again, I can see it swelling up inside o’ ye. Ye need to control it. Otherwise it will consume you. Just as Ian’s consumed him when Fey was injured. Sean: This ‘as nothing t’ do with tha’, Ian ‘ad always loved Fey. ‘E jus’ fergot it fer a little while. Plus I am NOTHING like Suriel. Unlike Ian, who is na’ of th’ Courts, ‘e ‘as th’ means o’ bein’ possessed. I d’ na’. I’m the inheritor o’ the Mantle o’ Bres, King of th’ Tautha De Dannan an’ the Fomorians. This will be a blood debt that will be owed. Those two idiots could ‘ave killed Tom and Pat if they were anyone else. DP must be stopped, DP will be stopped. The twins are na’ Patti, and thank the gods that Patti is no where near this. She is safe… Annie looks down to the ground, her booted foot skipping back and forth while she wobbles on the one heel left still. Sean knows the behavior, she’s hiding something from him. Sean: Wha’ th’ fok are ye doin? Annie: I’m na’ supposed t’ tell ye Sean. I’m sworn t’ secrecy. Sean: Ye’d better tell now, ‘er I’m goin’ t’ find it ou’ meself some’ow. Ye can either tell me ‘er ye can let me find ou’ an’ ‘old it again’ ye. Annie: Let me start out by telling you that this Sunday, I’ve already gotten you a match. An’ the twins t’. Bu’ the docs may be na’ lettin’ th’ twins wrestle. Sean: Fill me in. Annie: Ye ga’ th’ ‘All o’ Fammer, th’ man who sprayed ye in th’ face, Tsunami Oni. I’m workin’ with th’ New Commish t’ make this one fair an’ square. We know th’ rest o’ DP will try t’ get invovled. Especially Ben. Sean: Ben gets invovled, I’m goin’ t’ treat ‘im like I did our trainer an’ Idol. I’m th’ one who broke Ace Thunder, an’ Ben ‘ad better remember tha’. Bu’ I owe Oni fer tha’ shot with th’ blue mist. Foker ‘ad better be ‘opin’ tha’ I don’na beat ‘im an’ is woman. Annie: Th’ twins are supposed t’ ‘ave a non-titile match with Ben an’ Slasher. Sean: Ah, of course. Well if they can’na wrestle, then they’ll ‘ave an easy time. ‘Ow’s Corporal comin’? An’ Ruff? Annie: Ox is fine, I ‘elped ‘im get some o’ tha’ marker off ‘is fore’ead, an’ Ruff is doin’ okay. I’m lookin’ a’ the dossiers of th’ new guys t’ see jus’ who would be th’ best one t’ add t’ our fold. So’ don’cha worry abou’ anything. I’m takin’ care ‘o stuff. Sean: Good, now tell me wh’ yer sworn t’ na’ tell me. Annie: Sean? Sean: Damnit, tha’s stuff ye should tell me, na’ stuff yer na’ supposed t’. Fill me in, now. Annie again looks nervous. Sean: Tell me, now. Annie: It’s Patti. Sean: Wha’ abou’ ‘er? Annie: There is trouble over in th’ 4WW. She’s joined a stable, th’ Shadows Ascension. Under a guy named Tarrant. He’s some wannabe immortal who wishes ‘e was like us. ‘E’s possessed ‘er with somethin’. They don’na know wha’. Bu’ she’s na’ exactly th’ little sweet an’ innocent thing anymore. Sean: An’? Annie: There is something out to get her. A person was hired, name of Shoudou. He’s tried to hurt her, an’ recently burnt some o’ ‘er stuff. Like a certain dress an’ photos an’ that. Dark Angel ‘as taken up ‘is position as ‘er knight t’ stop ‘im. Sean: Shoudou? Annie: Bu’ some are sayin’ tha’ Dark Angel, Darek Angelus ‘is trin’ t’ get t’ Patti. Fer ‘imself. An’ this Shoudou is apparently an’ agent of th’ Orders. Sean: Wha’? Annie: Yeah, ‘is tag team partner, Ukyo Kushinaga, th’ sister o’ Kyo Kushinaga, ye know, the Masked Avenger, she was seen lookin’ through some o’ ‘is things an’ she found one o’ th’ Order’s Medallions. Sean: Really? Shoudou ye said? Annie: Yes. Sean smiles. Sean: Then all will be fine. I’m sure o’ it. Annie: He’s tried t’ hurt ‘er. Sean: Na’. ‘E’s a message from th’ Order. Tha’ means tha’ Ian is involved. I’ll leave it be fer now. Bu’ I’ll keep me eyes on it. I be wantin’ all ye can find on this Shoudou guy. Tha’ name is familiar. Annie: Aye, I’ll get it fer ye. Now come down an’ save th’ docs from th’ twins. I’m goin’ t’ ‘ead back t’ be with Ox-T fer a bit. Keep ‘im from goin’ nuts an’ tryin’ t’ find DP somewhere in some dark alley an’ show them ‘oiw t’ use a cattle prod. Sean: Na’, jus’ go with ‘im if tha’ ‘appens, so ‘e don’na kill them. Annie: Aye. At that, Sean and Annie both turn and head back inside of the building. And we fade to a nice emerald green.
