UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #449

Nick deserves Punishment

13 Jun 2004

Nick Persia — Germany/Holland

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Persia walks trough the trees in the forest, while the sun shines. There are few clouds in the air, and we can see a clear, blue sky. Nick is surrounded by plants, animals and there is sand under his feet. As he walks the sand changes to grass was we walk on a big field with beautiful animals on it. We see a very big snake rising in the air. At first you only see his head, but then we see his upper body as well. The snake approaches rapidly, with fast, swift moves. The Snake suddenly moves his body and head forwards as his split tongue reaches my face. However, he doesn’t bite me but lick me. He picks me up and put me on his head. The camera angle changes and we go up in the air. We look down and the field we were just walking on becomes everytime a bit smaller. Then we stop rising and we are at the same height. When we look down Nick sees more mud, and more water. We are entering the swamp as the smell becomes really bad. The space between the forrest trees declines more and more. It seems that we are entering the heart of the boss. Suddenly a big light flash blinds us, but it fades after three seconds. We see a white eagle in front of us, moving his wings in an upwards direction. He screams, and a terrible sounds comes out his voice. Nick grabs his ears and close them trying to avoid to hear the eagle screaming. The eagle starts to fly circles around the snake. He attacks the snake by picking him with his beak. The hits the snake with his wings as well. The snake falls down and so does Nick. They hit the ground with a smash. The eagle takes a dive to ground, killing the snake and picking him up with his legs. He doesn’t fly away but appears in front of Nick. He smashes Nick down again with his left wing as the screen fades black after we saw Nick closing his eyes] Nick is laying in bed and as he turns himself over the sweat breakes out. He suddenly jumps up as he now sits on the bed. He is breathing heavily, as we can hear he’s breathing. He jumps out of bed and makes himself a sandwich. He walks to the livving room while he is eating his sandwich. He turns the Television on. Nick wasn’t watching television. He turned it on, because it was routine. He did it, because he always did it. After the passing of time it became a habit. Something you can’t easily change. Nick looked at the clock: 4:30 am. Should he go back and try to get some sleep? Or should he stay awake? A heatwave had been plagueging Holland for a few days now, which was why he had gone back to Germany. In Germany the weather conditions were a lot better. In this house he had air conditioning, and a pool. Still, he was sweating and he looked tired. He dressed himself after he had taken a quick shower. He was still tired from last night. From the beach party, where he had a very good time. A lot of beautiful girls in bikini’s, and there was a very good DJ known as Tiesto, and as a bonus it was a foamparty. The foam was a meter high and everybody was dancing on the dancefloor. Nick Persia was having some fun. He took some beers, and remembered promising a girl to say goodbye tomorrowq before stepping on the plane. He left in a hurry, knowing he had to face Corporal Punishment at Supremacy this week. He drove to Enschede, in Holland. It was just over the border with Germany. He arrived in Enschede seeing a statue remembering the firework disaster that happened in 2001, causing several people to die after a firework deposit exploded in a residential area. He found the home, and he stepped out his car. He walked to the door in quick steps. He knocked on the door, but nobody answered. He knocked again, but this time a little bit harder. He took a look at his watch, it was 8 oçlock now. It was a bit early to pay a visit, so maybe she was still sleeping. He took a look trough the window, but only saw a dog. The dog started to bark. He took a step back and stood before the door. He waited for several minutes, but nobody opened the door. He walked back to his car, thinking about what happened. Maybe he had written down the wrong adress. Maybe she gave him the wrong adress. Or maybe.......it didn’t happen at all. Sometimes in life you are thinking about something and you ask yourself if it really happened. This is one of those things. Dream or Reality. The dream about the Snake and the Eagle comes back. It was not the first time he had this nightmare. Everytime he thought he was dead after the Eagle knocked him down with his wing as his eyes fade to black. He had no sense of time or space anymore. He just thought about things without feeling the pressure of time or space. He was staring into the future. He looked at his match with Corporal Punishment. Punishment made the challenge a while ago. He challenged Nick Persia, because he wanted The Hardcore Championship. Now he can have the champion, but not the strap. Some might wonder if Corporal is interested in any title. Corporal only likes to hurt people to the extend of making them beg for mercy. He don’t look at the UWF wrestlers as human beings, but as dogs. We are the animals and he is the hunter. He tries to track us down, with everything he has. Now that can be a lot considering he is from the Army. He had learned anything he needed to survive in the army. First you need to learn what your basis is. His base was the UWF. Then you determine your strategy. His strategy was beating people down, so he picked up the win. After that you think about the way you can do that best. Corporal just uses his fists and feet. Then you look for ways to cause more fear. An intimidating name would help: Corporal Punishment. It looks so simple. But as we all know simplicity it the hardest thing to see around you. People are thinking about what Corporal Punishment does and says. People wonder why he does it. People start to think about it. Thinking is something you simply won’t do. You need to immerse yourself into Corporal Punishment. When I immerse myself in Corporal Punishment I know why he does all of that. Because he likes doing it. He is confident about what he does. He makes no bones about the fact what he is going to do next. People don’t run from him, but they freeze in fear. Fear from Corporal Punishment. Nick fears Corporal Punishment. Nick is afraid of what Corporal could do to him. Corporal doesn’t play by the rules set for the game. He makes his own rules. Corporal is somebody you can’t trust. Now in Stable Wars somebody like him could work in your advantage. In a one on one singles match somebody like him could be the end of it all. But Corporal forgot what fear does. When you have two sides fighting it out on the battle field, and Corporal is on one side and Nick on the other. Corporal is the guy with more experience. He is the one with more power. He is the guy who knows more about pure warfare. But Nick is the guy who is driven by the fear from Corporal. He comes up with something innovative. Something that knocks your sock off. Corporal would be suprised if he knows hat kind of a weapon fear is. Fear is a weapon of mass destruction. So with that in mind we know that Corporal is in trouble. A weapon of mass destruction is more than a one man army. Corporal deserves credit for all that what he has done. He has been a former hardcore champion, television champion and intercontinental champion. He is a force to be reckoned with. Still, the fact remains Corporal is up against a champion. A champion who defends his title against anybody. Whether it is The Plague or Corporal Punishment, he couldn’t care less. He fought for his honour in that ring. He had the burning desire to win. He was, and is, a very bad loser. Nick was somebody that rised to the occasion. The more the odds are stacked against him, the more he trains to pull of the upset. Nick was looking forward to facing Corporal. In his mind he had transformed his biggest weaknesses into his biggest strenghts. He was using his fear for Corporal and the odds stacked against him as inspiration to train. He was eager to prove all the sceptics wrong. He wanted to prove them wrong. So in some way, Nick deserves Punishment.
