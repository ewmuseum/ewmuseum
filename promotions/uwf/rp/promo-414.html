UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #414

No Malcolm, Actually No It's Not...

16 May 2004

Mike Montgomery — Backstage area

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a few hours before show time now. Oddly enough I have absolutely no clue what I'm gonna do for this match. I know I can take Malcolm pretty easily, been there done that. That isn't what concerns me. What concerns me is that once you win the World Title, it's a whole new ball game. Everybody wants a piece of you, and it doesn't matter when or where. That's what concerns me because at any point in time, somebody could easily just run down to that ring, beat the living hell out of me, and get themselves a world title shot. But Malcolm...Malcolm doesn't concern me. He's the hot potato of the stables world. In the last 6 months he's been with every major stable in the UWF. Player's Club...Prime Alliance...Resurrection...he's done it all. Kind of makes you wonder when he'll jump ship yet again. The thing is though, Malcolm made one major mistake. He pissed off Ruff Ryder. Okay, Ryder's not an actual threat to anyone, but he's still an unwanted variable. It can work in my favor though. If he decides to get involved in our match, and I think it's a given he will, who does he go after? The World Champ? Or the guy who just stabbed him in the back? You give Ryder an inch, and he thinks he's entitled to a mile. Either way Malcolm, I watch your videos...I watch you in the ring, and I can really only think one thing...you're an idiot. We don't both get what we want today. I'm just plain tired of watching you in the ring, so I want you gone. I want to move on to bigger and better things. But I won't be doing that. This Sunday, I won't get what I want. But that's okay, I'll simply have to settle for getting the next best thing, which is whipping your ass yet again. I mean, you still don't get it Malcolm. You come out here and talk about how great you are, how you're World Title material, how you're going to put me down for the count. The problem is, and I think deep down you know this to be true, is that you can't back any of it up. You're not World Title material and never will be. You were barely able to last in the ring with Joshua Dunn...a man who truly is World Title material. You can't put me down for the count because, just like every other player out there, I beat you within an inch of your life on a regular basis. You don't even understand the business Malcolm. I'm not going to 'try to pull something' on you. I don't need to. It'll be Hangman's Joke, Fall From Grace, pin. Seems pretty simple, doesn't it? Almost academic. That's how it always goes between us. Always has, always will. What can I say, some things just never change. Wanna know why I brought Kevin Starr back? Let me let you in on a little secret. He has something you don't. It's that unknown quantity...that je ne sais quoi...that unique mix of athletecism and charisma that makes a wrestler great. I brought him into the club because he went toe to toe with the best we've ever had, but I brough him in mostly because when you put him, Dunn and myself in the ring...it's magic. Think back to the last Regal Rumble...IC Title on the line...ladder match...easily one of the best things I've ever been a part of. THAT'S why I brought Kevin back. Because he can put on one hell of a show. That's what the people will get today Malcolm. I'll be throwing you on my back and carrying you to one damned good match. Sadly, I'm afraid you won't be conscious for the end of it.
