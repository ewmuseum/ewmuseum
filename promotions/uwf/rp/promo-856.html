UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #856

Ill walk this path alone.

Today (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Las Vegas

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The COC is drawing near. The chance for man to earn a shot at the World Title. A chance for one man to prove hoe good he really is. The COC is made up of two paths. One is the beaten path, which is the path that everyone but the winner will take. The other, is the path less traveled, the path only the winner will take. It’s the path to glory and fame. It’s the path to the world title. At the beginning of these paths stands Malcolm Kennedy. But this is not the Malcolm from a year ago. This isnt the man who tied for 3rd in the 2004 Regal Rumble. This is not the same arrogant bastard with the worlds biggest ego. His ego has disappeared, his attitude and trash talk have vanished. His actions now speak louder than words. Malcolm stands before these two paths now a cold heartless bastard. The only things that matter to him are the world title and bringing glory to his and his brothers name. True, nothing will ever bring his brother back, but it’s the only way to bring himself to peace. The COC is a stepping stone towards inner peace in Malcolm’s life. It’s a step that must be taken. Time: Midnight Place: Las Vegas Streets. The streets were littered with people. Nights like this one, its near impossible to not cross path with some asshole handing out flyers that admit to selling the exact same food as the buffet two casinos down, except his buffet is better. Its near impossible to walk the streets trying to get from Point A to Point B without some slut insisting you want a good time so that she can afford another night of cheap rent and Vodka. Now a days, it just seems like the city’s reputation for great gambling and hotels covers up the inner filth that live here. Filth like some of the men in the UWF. Men like Seymour Almasy, Clyde, and the White Trash Superstar himself..........Duke Williams. Men that disgrace the UWF. The scene opens level with the side walk. A cigarette nearing its end drops down in front of the camera. A foot immediately squishes it out and continues down the side walk. The camera follows the foot as the rest of the body comes into view. The owner of the foot, is a man. He wears a pair of blue jeans and a buttoned up, thin white collared shirt. His jet black hair neatly gelled. The camera follows the man down the walk. As he reaches an intersection, he comes to a stop and waits for the Walk sign to lite up. While he waits, his cell phone rings. As he removes it from his pocket, he turns to his left and answers the phone. When he does this, his face comes into view. Malcolm Kennedy spoke into the phone. Malcolm : “Hello?” Voice: “Hey Malcolm, its me, Jerry.” Malcolm: “Hey Jerry, how is my old apartment treating you?” Jerry: “Not too bad. Listen, the reason I called is because you got a letter sent here by accident. Its from a UWF Headquarters, and it says COC info on the envelope.” Malcolm: “I need that letter. Is it cool if I run by and get it, Im only a block away?” Jerry: “Yeah man, its cool. I got some beer in the fridge.” Malcolm: “Alright, later man.” Jerry: “Bye” With that, the conversation ended and Malcolm closed his phone. The walk lite had just lit up and he continued onward. Upon reaching the other side he picked up his pace. Eager to reach his old apartment that he had given to his friend, and receive his mail. The camera stopped as Malcolm sped off. And the scene faded to black. Time: 12:20 AM. Place: Jerry Young’s loft apartment, Las Vegas Nevada. Malcolm stood in front of the door. He knocked three times and the door quickly swung open. With a huge grin Jerry extended his arms to Malcolm and the two briefly hugged. Malcolm walked in and the door closed behind him. Jerry walked towards the fridge as Malcolm stopped at the island in the kitchen. Jerry: “Beer?” Malcolm: “Yeah sure. So wheres my mail?” Jerry: “I think its right beside the fruit bowl in front of you.” Malcolm slid the fruit bowl to the side and picked up a manilla envelope. In big green letters it said C.O.C. Information. Malcolm just sat there in silence as Jerry returned with two cold bottles of beer, the tops already twisted off. Jerry: “Whats wrong man? Arent you gonna open it?” Malcolm: “Nothings wrong. Im just thinking about what this holds inside. Im thinking about what wall will be put in front of me. About what wall I will have to tear down.” Jerry: “What are you talking about?” Malcolm: “Inside this letter holds all the information for the C.O.C. It holds the brackets, the competitors......Everything.” Jerry: “What is the C.O.C. anyways?” Malcolm: “The contest of champions. The tournament to earn a shot at the world title. My chance to fulfill the dream Eric told me to follow. The dream I have had since I was a boy. The dream that I always seem to wake up short of fulfilling.” Jerry: “What does Eric have to do with this?” Malcolm: “When we were kids, and I told my parents I wanted to be a wrestling champion, they laughed at me and told me look for a real job. Even owning my own multi-million dollar casino wasn’t a good enough job for me. But Eric didnt. He understood it was my dream. He encouraged me to achieve my dream. He believed in me.” Jerry: “But Erics dead now man.” Malcolm: “True. But I will fulfill my dream regardless. Because Eric believed I could do it, and I am determined to prove he was right. Its all I have left Jerry.” Jerry: “Thats a noble cause man. Eric would be proud regardless. I know I would.” Malcolm: “Thanks Jerry.” Jerry: “So are you gonna open it and see who you face first or not?” Malcolm: “Jerry, inside here is the brackets. Inside this envelope, the UWF has placed the life of some other man in my hands. In here some guy sits in the first round with my name beside his. Do you understand what that means?” “It means that I am about to look at my hit list. I am about to look at each every man I will have to take out in order to win. Not ever single man inside here will be an absolute target, because like always, some will fall off before I can get to them. But one thing is certain. Whoever I face in the first round. Who ever drew my name as an opponent...................” Malcolm stopped talking and just stared at his letter. Jerry: “What Malcolm?” Malcolm: “Whoever has to face me, will feel my pent up anger from the past 4 months. That TLC match a while back meant nothing. I am about to unleash everything I’ve bottled up since Eric died. And when I do, those who stand before me will fall hard. Inside here is my road to glory and the walls I must tear down to reach glory. The first wall I will destroy is...........” Malcolm opened the letter and read his first opponents name. Malcolm: “Well, well, well. It’s the king of white trash.” Jerry: “Who?” Malcolm glanced at Jerry with a look that plainly expressed hate. His eyes were red with fury. He read the name aloud. Malcolm: “Duke Williams is a dead man.” Fade to black.
