UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #523

In the realm of real

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the realm of the real [The camera pans away from the city shot to reveal that is being played out on the Titan Tron screen and as the camera zooms out and down we see that camera man is standing inside of the ring, and Nick Persia is standing leaning back on the ropes of the ring facing the camera, Titan Tron looming behind him as he rests his arms across the top rope to support his casual but upright stance. ] “I live for this true life without it, I would have been cast down or be in jail for attempting to the live this life outside of the blessed four corners. The holy grail of my damned existence, it is within that squared circle I can justify my existence, my purpose, my fulfilment. To crush and maim all who oppose me in my task of being the best warrior on God’s f**ked up green Earth. For if all fear me, then I am king. If no one can beat me then I will stay king. Yet unfortunately I am not perfect, for I am human and as such it is inevitable I will make mistakes it is in my DNA to fail at least at certain times. This I cannot be held accountable for, it is the very design I fight against if I can not be perfect I will get much closer than anyone else ever has. People who oppose me have and always will fall before me in the end, it is only a matter of time before they make a mistake and I can capitalise. There are many who believe they are untouchable, but they are not. No matter how good you become, if you are constantly assailed and tested you will inevitably fail at some point. I know this it is main reason I lost my recently gained tag team title, but just as I have lost it all the times before I have won it back and I can again against anyone no matter who you are. You will fall prey to me in time for am patient and I will wait for your downfall. When that time comes I will be around to pick up the pieces and take away your glory to add to my own. I will be beaten from time to time, this I have to accept. Yet with a focus and training and constant strive to become the best I will surpass you all. The time is ever closer and ever nearer. As long as my focus stays, so will so and I will the Player’s Club for our time is now and we are the dominating force even in our times of defeat. It is clear to me that this match with Seymour Almasy this week at SAS, is going to be epic in all sense of the word. He carries a legend status with him that I’m not even sure he has anymore. Maybe you have come to rekindle that, and prove to yourself or others again that you still have it Almasy. When I began fighting I thought I could beat you instantly but I was but a rookie, cocky in my abilities but I have learnt so much since then, I am a changed species and a changed man, for all the more deadly. I know of your great skill in the ring and I look forward to what might possibly my greatest challenge and closest match up in the history of my career, with me at my peak and you’re in resurgence. We are not the weak fools; we know what this is all about. Personally I cannot wait to test myself against one of the greatest wrestlers this federation has seen. I know you will not hold back, and I can promise anyone watching that I will not either. This is make or break for us both. If you are crushed you’re legend status will be in doubt, you will fall from your previous grace, are you scared? Are you willing to put that reputation on the line? Of course you are, that is why I almost respect you in a way, but ultimately I can never respect the enemy, it is an automatic reaction, so don’t take it personally.” * Sinister laughter * “If I lose then I have not lost as much as you could have for I am not of the same rating as of the opinion of outsiders, I still have much more to prove. I risk nothing in facing you…nothing. I have set a good stance in my career and one loss against a “legend” will not change or dent that achievement. I do not fear this match, and I certainly do not fear you, for all that you are capable of. Even if you manage to beat me I will make sure you are put through hell to get there! I will certainly enjoy the experience of breaking you down and grinding you in what I can guarantee whatever the outcome will be a bloody and brutal contest. In fact I don’t really care about if I win or lose, all I care about is beating you down, I want to kill that past prominence of yours. I want to be hailed as the one who shattered you in the ring on your comeback to this “business”, this federation. I will break you no matter what. [Nick Persia moves off the ropes and moves round to stand tall in the ring facing the camera directly as he clearly becomes more pumped and intense. ] It is all that I strive for any more. I do not truly care for titles or championship gold, for the reasons I have explained that always comes and goes no matter what. It is the status that lives on, that matches that really made an impact in peoples minds that are the greatest matches in the world ever. Those contests are timeless. You legacy lives on, I want that and I am unashamed to admit it. It is what we are all here for, some for the gold too but we all want that clear dominance. As many can testify before me anyone who stands in the way of my goals gets hurt very badly, and I am not a subtle man when it comes to actions in the ring, I do what is necessary to win at all costs, no matter the opponent and no matter the rules. I take a great deal of pleasure in shattering people into a shadow of their former selves. This time is no different. This match is one to set the video up to record, I can tell you now. This match up will not disappoint. I just hope that Almasy lives up to his legend and still has it because not only would that be a big let down it would also make it a lot more enticing to smash his bloody face into the canvas come SAS! [Nick Persia bends down on one knee to the canvas one arm resting on the upper leg as the other arm reaches down fingers spread mimicking planting a face into the floor. He then slowly rises again still looking down as if imagining the sight and feel of it, then with a slight smile turns away from the camera to head out the ring. As the lights on the Titan Tron fade the arena is plunged into a faint dim light in which just the outline of Voice can be seen slowly and purposefully walking up the ramp way.]
