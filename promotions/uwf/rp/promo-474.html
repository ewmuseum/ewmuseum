UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #474

Long time No see.

June 27th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Hotel Room.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Its time to go to war my friends. Stable Wars 6 is now upon us. The time to put up or shut up is now. In one weeks time one Stable will be crowned the Stable Wars champions. The others will hang their heads in defeat and exit stage left. The Resurrection, The Players Club, Chrono Cross, The Chaos Society, and finally the Iron Curtain. Oh and don’t forget the Mystery Stable~! That’s quite a line up isn’t it? Only one of these teams will earn the right to be called champions of Stable Wars 6. And the smart money is on The Resurrection. *** ( The scene opens up inside a poorly lit room. The camera focused in on an alarm clock. The time reads 5:00. The camera zooms out to find an unmade bed resting beside the night stand that the clock sits upon. The blanket balled up on the end, the sheets spread amongst the mattress, no pillows to be found. As the camera focuses on the bed a black leather suitcase drops on it. Immediately following the bag is a jacket and a pair of sunglasses that land neatly on top of it. The camera looks up to find the source of it all and there in the middle of the room stands The One Man Army. Malcolm Kennedy stands there smirking at the screen with his arms crossed over his chest. He wears a pair of baggy blue jeans with holes in the knees, A pair of brown Rainbow Sandals, And a white T-shirt with the words “Top Notch” on it in black letters. Malcolm turns his back to the camera and walks into the bathroom right behind him. He pulls out a small overnight bag and tosses it onto the bed with the rest of his stuff. He takes a quick look in the mirror and touches up his neatly gelled jet black hair. He turns off the light and leaves the bathroom. ) Malcolm Kennedy: “Its been a while my friends. Im sure every camera man in the UWF was begging to shoot this promo when they found out it was gonna be me doing it. I mean I haven’t been heard from in weeks, So I know everyone is excited that Im finally talking.” “ And there’s so, so much to talk about. Stable Wars is here and The Resurrection is finally ready to prove to everyone what we have said all along. That we are the best of the best. We are what everyone here in the UWF should strive to be like. That’s why we are rebuilding this place anyways. The UWF is in shambles. All of these punks thinking they can come in and run the world. That they can shoot to the top. I hate to break it to you fools, but none of you will ever be like me. Im the fastest rising superstar in the history of the UWF. At Stable Wars I will simply back up that claim the best way I know how to, By giving everything I’ve got into this match. And when I do that, Im unstoppable. I know that my performances have been pretty sketchy as of late, but I’ve been a little preoccupied with my casino the past couple weeks. You know, Beefing up the security and installing the highest quality alarms systems, A better safe......You get the picture.” ( Malcolm picks up a belt off the floor and loops it through his jeans. He tightens it and goes back to talking. ) “So who do we have to face first at Stable Wars? Your guess is as good as mine. Some Mystery Stable it seems.. But we all know that’s not gonna affect the Resurrection at all. We are focused on the goal, and what we must do to get to it. No mystery group of ass clowns will ever change that. They can come out there, shock the world by some big surprise return, or by forming some seemingly “unstoppable” alliance. But when it all comes down to it, and that bell rings, that match is gonna be all Resurrection. One hundred percent dominance on our part, and we will go back to the back to rest up for the final match. But who will we be meeting in the final match? What other two stables will claim a spot in the finals? Honestly it doesnt matter. But the possibilities are endless needless to say.” ( He begins to pace the room as he continues to speak. ) “They are endless indeed. I mean anything could happen at Stable Wars. The Bunny Army itself could return and completely destroy the UWF and leave it for dead. But wait, they couldn’t even do when they were on the active roster.” “Jamie Shock could show up and a try and reintroduce the Prime Alliance once more....... Fat chance. Jamie Shock is a washed up fool. He used to be my friend. Now he is simply a has been with nothing to show for it.” “The fact is people, anything can and probably will happen at Stable Wars. But when its all said and done, the one thing that will not change is that The Resurrection will be the Stable Wars champions. And that will simply be another step in our rebuilding process.” “At Stable Wars 6 we will claim victory over every single person who feels brave enough to try and step in our way. Me and my stable mates will show the whole world that we are for real. No one is gonna stop is. Not Seymour Almasy, Corporal Punishment, or any other idiot in Chrono Cross. I’ve already proven single handedly that no one in the Chaos Society can hang with us. The Iron Curtain was never a factor to begin with. Just a cluster of idiots used to fill in space. The Mystery Stable is a friggin joke. No ones worried about those idiots. They cant even tell anyone who is in their stable. Chances are because they don’t want to get laughed at. The Players Club? Please, their “fearless” leader Mike Montgomery was just a small example of what lies ahead for them. You think Nick Persia will make a difference? Your sadly mistaken.” “ The real battle here lies between The Players Club and The Resurrection. Over the weeks the hatred has slowly built itself and at Stable Wars, we’re both gonna reach our boiling points.” “The Players Club has already been dealt a tremendous blow thanks to Digital Mortality. Mike Montgomery, The UWF World Champ, is not even a concern now. He will be left to the sidelines, nursing his leg. For a second The Players Club not only looked down, they looked knocked out for Stable Wars. But then Mike saves himself complete embarrassment and brings Nick Persia to give the Players Club and all its fans a shimmer of hope at Stable Wars. Fat chance Montgomery. Your gonna need a lot more than Nick Persia to succeed at Stable Wars. Nick Persia is good, hell Ill be the first to admit it. But its not gonna make any difference. Because no matter how good he is, No matter how good Kevin Starr, Joshua Dunn, and Ezra Kazee are, The Resurrection will always be better.......” ( He picks up the leather jacket and hangs his sunglasses from the neck of his shirt and throws the bag over his shoulder. He gives the camera one last look. ) Malcolm Kennedy: Always! ( Malcolm walks to the door and opens it. He flips the switch and the lights go out as the scene then fades to black. )
