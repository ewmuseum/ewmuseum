UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #288

One Loss Too Many

14 Mar 2004

Iron Lung — Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One Loss Too Many Starring Iron Lung 'List'n to me Antonio, I swea' if you lay one more finger on my daughta' again, it's gonna be your head in a duffle bag...' 'Tony, I swea' I didn't lay a hand on 'er!' 'I tink you're trying ta bull**** me...' 'No, why woula I do 'dat Tony. Come on, you've known me for --' 'Ova two years 'dis month Antonio, and I still can't trust you. You've been hidin' somefin, and when I find out what dat somefin is, you aren't gonna be showin' ya c*** suckin' face here anymore, you got me?' 'You can trust me Tony..' 'No, I can't Antonio. Because you've f***ed with ma family for long enough, and it's time for a little change of things around here.' 'Tony, you don't wanna do 'dis, trust me. I'm one of your best dealers.' 'Money talks Antonio, and as of right now, you've just about ran dry...' Pulling a gun out, Tony shoots Antonio in the chest. Antonio falls back in his chair and spills over onto the tiled floor, with everyone else in the restaurant watching with a shocked look on their faces, most of them filled with gasps. Tony then shoots up in the air, and the crowd around them flees the restaurant, sooner or later leaving just him and the now barely breathing Antonio in the scene. 'You've f***ed with ma family for the last time 'Ton.' 'Please, DON'T!' Pulling the gun to Antonio's head, there is dramatic music surrounding this intense stand off. And then suddenly, we hear a deep sigh out of nowhere, and along with it the screen begins to switch on us. We were going to another channel? Sure enough, when the camera begins to pull out a little, we realize that we were watching a recent episode of 'The Sopranos', and now we were seeing the latest trends in technology on the Shop at Home network. Scratch that, we were now watching 'Crank Yankers', and sooner or later the television was completely shut up with a grunt from our viewer. Been there, done that. Another night of useless television, you think that maybe they'd come up with another reality show that actually has some sort of sense to it or something, rather than having these idiots on an 'island' trying to survive or having people from supposedly different worlds colliding into one house. What has this media frenzied world come to? Every station you turn to has commercials, trying to drag you into trying the newest products. Some stations are even a constant commercial, and some of the shows, scratch that, most of the shows now promote violence and or promote use of coarse language in a typical days conversation that you would have over a cup of coffee. There was then a quiet moment as Iron Lung collected his thoughts inside of his Villa Roma apartment room. He took a quick sip of Vanilla Coke that was mounted on his coffee table from last night's 'self party'. It was meant to cheer his own ways up, but it didn't quite work, as we could tell from the broken cheap plastic cd player on the floor. Ah, but sometimes you've got to love it. The media pumps you up so much, and if you stand up to their pressure, you're like a God among men or a king among a bunch of peasants. But, if you make the wrong move or say something that isn't to their liking, they jump on you like a bunch of sharks on a feeding frenzy. Sometimes I think about why I even try to stand up to these guys anymore, they try to make my life a living hell every single time I fight. Why do I keep answering their questions again? Oh yah, that's right, because it's my job to inform them about what's going on inside of my head, which nobody will ever be able to figure out unless I were to tell them. Damn these contractual negotiations, and I'm always the one to get screwed by not reading the fine print.. Ah.. no bother, I might as well give them all what they want. If they wanted to know how I feel about losing THC to The Plague, I let them know backstage after the show. If they wanted to know how I felt about facing Malcolm Kennedy tonight, I let them know backstage after the show. And if they want to know what my reaction is after my match tonight, win or lose.. I guess I have to let them know if I want to keep my job.. Taking another sip of Vanilla Coke, Iron Lung now just stares at the blank television screen before him. There was something more on his mind, a whole lot more. If you were to ask him, he probably wouldn't tell you a thing. But in a private and exclusive interview, he would bear his soul to you if given the chance. It was always scary to talk to a man like this, especially not knowing what he was up to inside of his train of thought. He was a very jumpy and very emotional person, especially when things didn't go his way. He had already promised to have a certain angry swagger in his step when he approached the ring tonight to face Malcolm Kennedy, and he already promised that the match would be over quickly and that he would get his redemption for losing THC so quickly.. So tonight, I'm up against Malcolm Kennedy. You've already gotten all the comments you could out of me after the last Supremacy show, and I'm sticking to them jack. I'm going to walk into that ring, still thinking that I'm the Hardcore Champion, and when I see Malcolm's face, it will be substituted by The Plague's. In fact, everyone that I face from now on will become The Plague, for you see he has no infested my mind with such a passion that I would do anything to get my hands back on him and that I would do anything to get my title back into my grasps. So Malcolm, if you decide upon cheating to possibly try and overcome me tonight, it will fail without a doubt. Because fairness will always over come adversity, and simply because a man without nothing to lose, is a man you do not want to mess with... The scene gradually fades to black and then to static, showing Iron Lung's passionate flourescent red eyes, flamed up like fireballs in waiting for his match tonight. What could we expect this ex-champion to do? The scary thing was, we only had to expect the un-expected!
