UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #300

The customer is always right

4 Apr 2004

Joshua Dunn — UWF House Show

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Beezlebozo has made his first appearance in weeks, and is acting as if he never left… What was the World Champion doing in this time? We don’t entirely know, and perhaps we’ll never know, maybe we don’t want to know. But Beezlebozo has been angered by the actions of Joshua Dunn. The mind trick he tried to ploy against him backfired, or has it? How do you second-guess a second guess? Has Beezlebozo just walked into a trap, or has Dunn just made a severe miscalculation? Time will tell] [The Scene Opens at a UWF House Show, Some local talent goes up against the likes of The Fool and TPL seeing how they pair up against them, trying to land UWF contracts or at least be taken notice of, but what the crowd doesn’t know is what’s in store for them next. The Ring has just been emptied after a rather nasty Hardcore match of wrestlers we haven’t heard of, yet to become household names. The crowd still yell loudly in some parts. Suddenly the entire arena goes pitch black, silencing most of the screeches into slight murmurs, no announcer, so this isn’t the start of the next match. Red pyro’s go up on the side of the stage while yellow one’s cross over each other at the universaltron. The Video screen flares into life, the Player’s Club Logo in full view of every one, a slow heartbeat is heard over the speakers. The beat quickens as another image flashes in and out on top of the logo, red back ground with a yellow symbol, as the flashes grow closer together and the crowd realise what the image is they explode in delight as they see the hammer and sickle of communism. The arena is flooded in red light as Joshua Dunn’s video shots of some of his more memorable moments are played over and KoRn’s “Divine” plays over the speakers. At the head of the ramp, drowned in red light, chin tucked into his chest and arms outstretched, is Joshua Dunn wearing a red shirt and dark red trousers, mic in hand. The rest of the house lights come up and we can now see that Dunn is actually wearing Black jeans and a white T-shirt. He snaps his limbs in beside him and up with his head and moves with a purpose down to the ring, getting cheered along, not because the crowd love him, more that they’re glad to see him here. He passes the ramp side camera, which gets a close shot of him, there’s an uneasy glint in his eye and a partial sideways smirk on his face. He gets to the ring and jumps up onto the apron and goes in through the middle ropes and goes into the middle via two corner posts to entice the crowd. When there he raises the mic, his entrance music fades away, the video screen shows his face and he holds up a hand to quieten the crowd] [The crowd grow silent eventually and Dunn holds the pose for a moment, a couple of camera flashes going off to capture the Kodak moment. He suddenly becomes more fluid and more statue like, ruining one last person’s photo] JD: Well, I never expected such a warm welcome. It’s good to be here amongst all you people. [This entices a pop from the crowd that lasts all of two seconds] JD: Well I don’t know how many of you have followed my career through thick and thin, from my lowly days in the TV title division to my record-breaking reign as IC Champion. [The mention of the achievement grants Dunn another Crowd Pop, the crowd obviously behind the communist no matter what happened in his past. The ability of this man to manipulate a crowd of spectators is amazing, no wonder he was a valuable member of the Iron Curtain and now Players Club] JD: Now I finally get my Chance at the World Champion, a man I have nothing against, someone who I can see with the need to be respected, the man you people love. [Another pop, at the mention of Beezlebozo, our not quite all together world champion, but the tone in Dunn’s voice changes, no longer the crowd working voice of before] JD: Now I do respect this man [He pauses for a moment] JD: He’s our world Champion you can’t help but respect him; it takes a lot to get to the top. I can’t take that away from him. This first time that I go up against him is the first ever, first ever chance at world gold, first chance at the man himself. [He starts pacing around a bit holding out on arm] JD: Renaissance has to be the greatest card line up since Live and Loud 3. Beezlebozo the world champion and me, the number one contender, number two in the federation. I like to think of this company as a franchise, and in any good franchise you try to give the customer, that’s you guys, what they want. [He stops and looks up from his pacing trying to face the whole crowd at the same time, his crowd working voice coming back] JD: You want to see me take on the World champ right? Crowd: YES! JD: You want to be there at renaissance to see the best line up of wrestlers in history battle it out? Crowd: YES! [Falling back into the previous voice again, and returns to his pacing, every now and again changing his hand signals and looking towards the crowd] JD: Good that’s what I knew you’d say, who wouldn’t want to be there, or watch the Pay Per View on box office or catch the highlights after the actual event? No one that’s who. You can’t escape this thing. The monumental Main event will be spectacular. Well, at least now it’s going to be bloody [He continues pacing but stops speaking for a minute, hand wavering in the middle, the crowd a bit thrown back by this] JD: You see the thing is, Beezlebozo has decided in his divine right that he’s going to tarnish the gold of the World title by smearing it with blood [He faces the crowd again and switches back on his working voice] JD: Is the World title a hardcore title? Crowd: NO! JD: Didn’t think so [Again voice changes and pacing resumed] JD: Now this causes us a predicament. The minute he uses that weapon from his duffel bag, he gets disqualified and I win, but he retains the World Title. Now somehow that doesn’t seem quite fair. However I also said I would go the cowards way out like Ryder did and impose a change of title on a DQ, and I wont go back on my word. Although I can’t have Beezlebozo having an easy time of it. So I propose this [He spins and faces one of the ringside cameras. He holds it’s gaze, his face displayed on the video screen, sinister and down to business as per usual with Dunn] JD: Beezlebozo, since you don’t like the sound of my first proposal, I’ve got another one for you, one that may be more to your liking because it will stack the odds firmly in your favour. [Mid proposal the voice switch again, raising the voice so the crowd get an earful] JD: I propose that the Main event of Renaissance have No disqualifications- [The crowd start cheering, he’s not even finished yet] JD: - pins count anywhere! Is that more to your liking? Everything’s legal, whatever you do, whatever I do. Let’s make a game of it. [The crowd’s cheers get louder, maxing out the microphones with cut off yells, absolutely ecstatic with the proposal] JD: Now you see, if he won’t adhere to the rules, we might as well make it all completely legal. There’s no easy way out that way. For EITHER of us. And hopefully only one of us will be walking out of the arena, the one that is World Champion. [“Divine” hits the speakers once more and Dunn throws the microphone out of the ring to the announcer and jumps out of the ring himself heading for the arena curtain, the crowd start an almost unanimous “Dunn” chant accompanying his exit mixed through with cheers and gagging fans asking for more. Dunn just moving up the ramp, a happy grin on his face, everything going according to plan no doubt, how could Beezlebozo refuse?] [Dunn exits the arena, only to be met just off stage by another camera staring him on the face, the grin only gets wider] JD: You hear that Bozo? [He cups a hand behind his ear to hear the chanting of “Dunn” and the cheering, not a single boo audible] JD: That’s the sound of happy customers, there’s no doubt in their minds who should be the next champ, and you know what they say… [He gives that now infamous sideways grin to the camera] JD: The customer is ALWAYS right [The grin gets an emphasis for a moment] JD: You must want that no DQ mustn’t you? Give it a thought; it is only a game after all [He walks off camera shot and the scene fades to black]
