UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #468

Stable Wars Rundown

26 Jun 2004

Adien Pryde — Raid's Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to the office of Raid, The evil Exterminator. Sitting in a luxurious black leather desk chair in front of a computer is Adien Pryde. He is busily going through business document when a voice can be heard.] Computer : You got Mail. Pryde : Hope this is what I have been waiting for…. Excellent, Joanna’s boys came through and got me the background of all of the competitors in Stable Wars. Now lets see what we have. [Pryde scrolls through the information and stops to read something.] Pryde : Interesting, We all know that The Players Club is falling apart at the seams with Dunn and Starr at each others throats, and Nick Persia is really out of his league with the other 3, but this about Kazee’s past is very interesting. This doctors report will com in very handy. [Pryde looks further down the document to see what else he can exploit.] Pryde : Chrono Cross, what the fuck kind of name is that. What ever, this is one of the teams I fear the most. The brutality of Cpl. Punishment, the technical wrestling of Kenny Mercury, the experience of Seymour Almasy, and the mind of Richard Ray Vaughn. They have it all in one stable, but do they have the experience as a team to succeed to the end? If they make it to the final match we will have to make sure that we keep them on the ropes. Pryde : The other team we may have trouble with is The Resurrection. With a combination of experience, youth and championships, these four may prove tougher than Chrono Cross. But with the championships come cockiness and underestimation. That is what we need to make sure that they will keep their guard down till we eliminate them one by one. Pryde : Joanna, I am disappointed in these guys you have getting this information. This is all you have on the mystery team. I guess this is what we have to work on. [Pryde scrolls down some more and stops and grins.] Pryde : Now here is who I have the most interest in facing in the final three. The Iron Curtain……. [The scene fades to black with the words in red….To Be Continued…]
