UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #690

Who's Mean?

9 Sep 2004

Mr. Meaner — Old McDonald's Farm

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in front of what looks like a farm or something of the like. There is a yellow school bus there, and a lot of children there as well, ranging from 1st grade to 3rd grade. They are all milling around with their teachers, yelling and screaming. As they are standing their looking around, and not doing anything, the man in black known as Mr. Meaner walks up in front of them.) Mr. Meaner: Hello children. (A few scattered hellos are heard.) Mr. Meaner: We are going to try that again. HELLO CHILDREN!!! Children: HELLO!!!! Mr. Meaner: Hi, I’m your tour guide today here at Old McDonald’s Farm. This farm has been here for several years now and is one of the best farms in the world. We’ll start walking this way and we can start touring the farm. (Although you can not see his face, you can almost sense he is smiling as he leads them into a metallic building. This building is obviously not a whole lot barn like looking, and oh if the children only knew what they were about to see.) Mr. Meaner: The first animal that we are going to see is cows. Now, as most of you know, we get things like steak, hamburgers, and beef from cows. Also, from female cows we get milk and other products that are needed for us to have strong bones and muscles. (As they are walking along, they see a cow.) Mr. Meaner: I’d like everyone say hi to Daisy. She is one of our Dairy cows here at the farm. She loves to be petted and given the grand treatment. Would everyone like to come up and touch her? (The children all file up to the cow, petting her. They all then back up only to see the cow being tied around its feet and being hung upside down to the ceiling. Mr. Meaner walks up to the cow and grabs a very long knife.) Mr. Meaner: Look at this cow, isn’t it so cute and nice. Just wants to make you want to pet it, doesn’t it? But you know, if we just pet this thing, we wouldn’t have any food, so we have to do something, and that is slaughter it. (Mr. Meaner takes the knife and runs it across the cow’s throat, letting blood pour out over the floor. Screams of children can be heard, along with their cries.) Mr. Meaner: Ah, what’s wrong? You know we have to kill it to eat it. This is how we kill it. This is how all animals are killed. See, after this process, it is taken to a butcher, where he takes out all of the important meat like the steaks. This might mean that sometime soon that our friend Daisy here might be found on one of your plates. (Vomiting can be heard coming from the children as well as “Eww gross” and other lines of the like. Mr. Meaner starts walking toward another machine, obviously a meet grinder.) Mr. Meaner: Of course, we can’t take all of the meat and make it into a prime cut of Beef. There is only so much that you can use from the cow to make the prime cuts. There are several parts that are left behind as well. (He takes a handful of stuff out of the bucket that is before him and shows it to the children.) Mr. Meaner: The tails, the nose, the hooves, that kind of stuff doesn’t make for good meat by itself. So another thing we have to do with the cow is figure out how to use this stuff. SO here is what we do. We grind them all up into ground beer. (Meaner takes the different pieces and throws them into the meat grinder. He turns the machine on, and a lot of noise can be heard. Shortly, he turns the machine off, takes the bull off of the machine, and holds it so that everyone can see it.) Mr. Meaner: And thus we have ground beef or the thing that all of your hamburgers are made out of. All the hooves, tails, noses, and anything else we can’t use go into this here. And then we just kinda take it, fry it up and….. (Meaner walks a little farther along to where there is a hamburger made and sitting there on the table.) Mr. Meaner: I’d like for you to meet someone else quickly. (Mr. Meaner pulls out a picture of a calf.) Mr. Meaner: This here is our friend Daisy’s Daughter. She is even cuter than daisy, wouldn’t you agree? (Mr. Meaner walks over to a pre pared hamburger. He picks it up and takes a huge bite out of it.) Mr. Meaner: MM MM good. I just can’t tell you how good of a hamburger our friend Daisy’s daughter makes. And think, even better, you maybe be having Daisy or her child next time you go to oh, say McDonald’s (There are more screams and crying and such coming from the children. The teachers all usher them out of the meat processing plant as best they can, disgusted with the presentation that they have just gotten.) Mr. Meaner: Well, Almasay, as long as we are doing a battle of one upsmanship on who can be the meanest, I thought that I would at least make it fun and educational. I mean I’m sure y’all have wanted to know where we get our beef and such from…… Almasay, here is the problem though. You claim to be mean. You claim to be one of the meanest people to ever be a part of the UWF. Problem is that you do not know what it means to be mean. You have a very narrow minded view of what it means to be mean…… See, jack off, here is the thing. You think being mean has to do with physicality. You think that being mean includes beating everything up, making people get hurt, and making people loose personally property in one way or another. That is just one aspect of being mean…… Today, dumbass, I have proven another aspect of being mean. I have proven another way to be mean in the world and another thought on meanness that you couldn’t fathom. Psychological. These kids here today, they are going to be traumatized. Quite possibly for the rest of their lives. How many people do you think out of there are going to have thoughts when they eat any kind of meat, especially beef. You think your mean; you haven’t even scratched the surface of being mean. You haven’t even scratched the surface of what it is to be mean…… You can attack things with swords all you want, ya pansy. You can even bring your damned sword to the match if you so choose. I have no qualms about that. But there is an old saying that you probably know. Don’t bring a sword to a gun fight. Because to me, every inch of that ring is a gun waiting for me to use…. You say that you are the greatest wrestler in the UWF. You may very well be. You may very well go down in history as the greatest of all time. But there is a difference here. See, Sunday, we aren’t having a wrestling match. We aren’t having the type of a match you are use to. What we are having is nothing more than a street fight. What we are having is nothing more than a prison brawl. An area, that unlike you, I excel at. So you go on and live in your fantasy of being the greatest wrestler of all time. Because in reality, while it may be true, I AM THE GREATEST STREETFIGHTER OF ALL TIME!!!! And your about to wake up from your fantasy of being the greatest because at SAS, I will defeat you. (Mr. Meaner finishes eating the rest of the hamburger from earlier as the scene fades to black.)
