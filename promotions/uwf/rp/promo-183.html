UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #183

Fair and Fair alike

21 Jan 2004

Joshua Dunn — UWF Complex

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[5-1 That’s how the score lies now; five out of six times Dunn has beaten Ryder, and Dunn winning the NA title, now becoming one with the IC title. What is rightfully his is now his. Of course no one saw what happened when the lights went out, no one actually knows why the lights went out, but Dunn picked up the win and clocked up another notch on the Titles front. Now only Two titles have never been held by the Communist Scotsman, that being the Tag Title and the sought after World title. Time, that’s the only limiting factor to the Scotsman, soon he’ll be a true grand slam Champion. Holding every title ever to exist in the UWF. On Sunday though, something spectacular will happen, the Prime Alliance are no longer able to hide behind bookers and Jamie Shock, the new UWF owner has seen to it that the clash between the biggest stables in the UWF will face each other sooner rather than later. A two out of three match up, one of those matches being Joshua Dunn, the big shot killer, against Malcolm Kennedy, the traitor. Just what has the Communist Scotsman got to say about al this? We just don’t know, but thanks to UWF Television, we’re going to find out] [The Scene opens, where are we? We are in the backstage area of the UWF Complex. The camera is pointing at the door to the dressing room of one particular wrestler, the Red colour and yellow letters plainly give away who we are going to visit, as the letters spell out we’re about to intrude upon Joshua Dunn. The camera moves through the door, and walks into the room, the red and yellow motif automatically gives the room an air of communism. On the red sofa, sits the man in question, Joshua Dunn. Laying back on the sofa with feet up on the coffee table. He’s wearing black jeans and a red T-Shirt and the gold chain attached to the emblem of communism around his neck is clearly visible glinting in the 100-watt light bulb light. His arms behind his head relaxed, the IC title lying to his right, and the NA title? Appropriately disposed of. As he looks down towards the camera, tilting his head to face it, he sits up, his arms running along the back of the sofa. A smile rightly over his face. He savours the silence before opening his mouth and spilling out whatever he has to say] JD: The first SAS of 2004, and what a great way to kick things off, by purging the UWF of the last mention of the PWW and humiliating the only remnant of the invasion. Well Ruff Ryder, obviously you can’t hack it any more, Once you would have been a challenge, but unfortunately with the lights out your embarrassment wasn’t realised. I have beaten you time and time again, and still you dismiss me, the number 2 in the federation, as nothing. You’ve become a shell of what you were, your all washed up, yatta yatta yatta. [Dunn looks away from the camera and makes a circular motion with his left hand, dismissing the issue] JD: Unlike you I tire of saying the same thing over and over until it becomes a catchphrase. I’d much rather go on to something new, like what’s going on concerning the main event of this weeks SAS. Three matches, best of three, the Players Club versus the Prime Alliance. Each a match worthy to be a main event in it’s own right, but brought together in something that’s bound to be spectacular. But enough hype, it’s not what I was expecting; a six-man tag team match is what I expected. Of course I’ll have to do with what I’ve been given. Do I face Ruff Ryder with a chance to make it 6-1? No. Is it against the headman of the Prime Alliance, a man I have a score to settle with for wrongs done a long time ago? No. I’ve been given former member of the Players Club and relatively new to the business, Malcolm Kennedy. Also not what I expected. [Dunn takes his arms off of the back of the sofa and takes them to his front, bunching the tips of his fingers together Dunn inspects his nails, and starts to pick the dirt out from under them, he still looks at the camera while he does this and continues] JD: Perhaps I’m not aloud to make astounding progress like some of the rest of the Roster, always held back, never pushed to see what I can do. Frankly I don’t like being unchallenged. Not that I’m discrediting Malcolm Kennedy, oh no, I’m Discrediting the booking staff for deciding that their number two man is not good for anything more than being here to keep crowds interested. Large clashes between the big names in the federation just don’t seem to happen. I feel like a spare part, never number one, always being left to one side. It’s no different now. I could be world champion, and people would dismiss me as a jobber. THIS is what I find the scourge of my being. Ever since joining this federation oh so many years ago, I have always been underestimated. [Dunn pauses for a moment, with his eyes closed, his eyebrows slightly furrowed, a deep anger burning within, the missing pieces of the puzzle still out of reach] I make my point with Former world Champion Jamie Shock, who believes your ability is affected by the company you keep, In some cases this is true, In YOUR case this is true, as your stable members help you to help yourself to whatever takes your fancy. I have never stooped so low; I have never seeked assistance from any of my stable mates, ever. You on the other hand have used every associate to further your career, Lew Spectre, when he was the owner of the UWF, The Shark and Stud Dog Escobar who carried your name onto the Stable wars trophy and most recently Ruff Ryder and Malcolm Kennedy, to prove that you can still lead and others will follow. This does make you an excellent tactician, but it makes you a lousy wrestler, resorting to weapons to make up for your lack of skill. Mike Montgomery will be facing you, if that is required, as the two matches beforehand could have already decided the winners of our clash. Ruff Ryder? [Dunn shrugs, crossing his arms afterwards] JD: It’s been said already. Leaving Only Malcolm Kennedy to discuss, but first I have something to clear up. I am no one’s lackey. [He now sits forward, hunched, towards the camera, looking deep into the lens] JD: I never have been, and never will be the running Dog of Mike Montgomery. We may be teammates, but I have not forgotten the history between us, and don’t forget who took over the Players Club from you, me. Ezra Kazee did not join Mike Montgomery, he joined me, after a long time of just leaving me to it, I make the first decision as leader of TPC, you step back in and take the lime light once more, just remember a man can only take so much. We keep together from necessity, not of friendship. As long as there is a Prime Alliance, I shall see to it that the Players Club lives on, while Mike swans about his executive office looking big. All three of us make a good team because we know how each other work, and how we do that the public knows, each member has been selected because of how well they fair against us. It happened to me, to Malcolm, and to Ezra. This is what makes us strong as a team, but just be careful of the toes you tread on. [He sits back in the sofa again, the left arm over the back of the sofa and the other on top of the IC title] JD: Don’t read too much into that though, just like Ryder sitting under a tree, I say it only to prove a point, and while the Prime Alliance stands I shall be ready to challenge it. And I do it against Malcolm Kennedy. Now Malcolm I appreciate your gall, of course it’s what got you into the Players Club in the first place, but don’t think you can break me in one match. I was the Shoot Championship, and you don’t get that title for being easily broken. I have spent my entire career increasing my stamina and resilience to submission holds, and I have never once tapped out of a match. I prefer the glee of hearing my opponent call out I quit to just getting a simple three count. Don’t you think that’s what wrestling should be about? I do [That sideways grin slips across his face for a moment] JD: This reason means if you try to use endurance to win a match you are going about the wrong tactics to win the match, anyone who has faced me will tell you I last as long as a Duracell battery. To get my first reign as IC champ I went through hell with Mike Montgomery and Kevin Starr, and that match must have been a contender for match of the year, an award usually reserved for anything involving the World Title. Just think about that and pick up a VHS of the Regal Rumble 2003. It will help you decide what may be the best tactic to stop my streak of wins that are bringing me so much. [Dunn adjusts his seating] JD: Is this luck? No, It’s just how well I cope and adapt to any given situation, and my long fought for stamina, becoming legendary here in the UWF, as time after time, I pull wins out long after every other wrestler here has given up. And this I give you is not me preaching about what might happen in our encounter like everyone else has, I have just spoken about everything that has happened, and that is the god given truth. I am a Scotsman, British as Jamie Shock put it, but I am distinctly different from him, who you defeated, I have morals that I uphold and the sense not to underestimate any opponent, and that includes you Mr. Kennedy. [He genuinely smiles to the camera] JD: I look forward to seeing what you can do [The scene fades out on the smile]
