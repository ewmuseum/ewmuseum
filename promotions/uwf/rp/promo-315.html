UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #315

Pump it Up

6 Apr 2004

Iron Lung — Golds Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pump it Up with Iron Lung and 'Dashing' Dane Davis Previously... Receiver Hello? Caller Lung, it's Dane.. Iron Lung What? How did you get this number? 'Dashing' Dane Davis You gave it to me after your match, remember? Iron Lung Oh, ok. What do you want? 'Dashing' Dane Davis Forgetful aren't we? We're supposed to go to the gym today! Iron Lung (Looking at his clock) Shit, you're right. You know what? Just meet me at the Golds Gym in about an hour, I have to get ready! 'Dashing' Dane Davis All right, I'll meet you there. Ta! Shaking his head lightly, Iron Lung gets out of bed and mutters 'damn, what a crazy ass dream' before yawning once more. He is slow to get around the room, as he is still trying to shake away the sandman, but none of the less, he arrives at his destination. We wouldn't watch him get his daily routine through with, we would respect his privacy. So, with that being said, the camera fades away on this part, only to pick up briefly when the gym was in sights. And now, we bring you ' Pump it Up '.. The scene opens up to us with a strangely familiar gym, in fact, it's the same gym that was captured in the freaky dream of Iron Lung the other night, but this time there weren't any gangsters hanging around with their jive talk. Also this time around, the gym wasn't run down, but rather bustling with business for the day. This was the day that the sleepish Lung had been waiting for, and with a shot at the IC Championship on the line, he had to drag along a cocky reporter to show him his training regimen. Lung was always hesitant to let reporters in on his personal business, especially something as intense as this. For Lung, it all began and ended with the training he would partake in. For Lung, training was where he did his best work before stepping into the ring, and to let somebody document it was out of the question, so as a previous stipulation, he sincerely asked 'Dashing' Dane Davis to not bring a camera or any other recording device. Lung would check him well over after getting out of the car and heading for the doors of the local Golds Gym. Only Lung would have his own personal record of this session, which is why we were here, providing the secret footage tucked away underneath his favorite baseball cap. As the duo walked toward the gym doors, 'Dashing' Dane Davis smiled wildly at Lung, anticipating such a long and hard workout, anticipating a deeper look inside this young superstar. But little did he know how grueling this former THC Champion's training would become, for Lung always followed a regimen that was a mix of the 'Pumping Iron' movie, staring Arnold Schwarzenegger and Lou Ferrigno. When the doors opened for the gym, the two men stepped aside, and they couldn't look more out of place with all of the bodybuilders focus. But none of the less, Lung lead 'Dashing' Dane Davis to the locker room area, and once again Davis lit up like a Christmas tree ornament. It was as if he had never experienced something like this before, as Lung opened his locker, pulling out his familiar black duffel bag. Unzippering it, he keeps a close eye on Davis, as he keeps smiling like a kid lost in a candy store. Iron Lung (After noticing the constant smiling)... What in the blue hell are you smiling at? 'Dashing' Dane Davis ... Nothing, I'm just excited. This is my first time being in some place like this. Iron Lung Well, don't pass out or anything. We're just getting started. Now get the hell out of here so I can get changed, I don't want you starin' at me! 'Dashing' Dane Davis stares at Lung for a while, and Lung shakes his shoulders towards him to intimidate, making Davis jump off the bench and head for the locker room door, exiting as he was told to do so. The scene slowly fades away for a moment, and fades back in with Iron Lung sitting on the bench with his workout gear, zipping his bag back up and beating the closed fist of his left hand into his open right palm. He then gets up off the bench, sighing a little bit and pushing the door open, looking around for 'Dashing' Dane. Iron Lung Dane, are you ready? 'Dashing' Dane Davis (In a muffled town) Yah, just get this door outta my face! Iron Lung (Letting the door go back) Oops, I didn't see you man, sorry. 'Dashing' Dane Davis (In a sore, grimacing tone) Yah, no problem. Let's just get to work. Iron Lung (Coming out of the doorway and patting Dane's back hard) That's the spirit. Come on, I'll show you my warmup routine. Iron Lung helps 'Dashing' Dane catch his footing, and upon looking to see if he had done any damage with the door swing, he sees nothing. The two continue to walk to a padded mat, perhaps the safest object of the day so far. Iron Lung stands upon it, stretching his legs and his arms for a few minutes, and barely breaking a sweat as well. Davis just watches with glee as Lung goes into a jumping jack set of twenty, and Lung keeps giving him an evil look. Iron Lung Do you have to keep staring at me like that? People are going to think something is going on between the two of us, and I don't drive on the other side of the road, if you know what I mean. 'Dashing' Dane Davis Of course, I just can't believe how well conditioned you are, it amazes me. You're sure to give Ruff Ryder a hell of a fight tomorrow night! Iron Lung (Talking with each landing of a jumping jack) You think it'll ... be a hell of a fight? ... I think that it'll ... be an ass whooping ... on Ruff Ryder's part ... He doesn't know ... who he is going into the ring with ... I'm going to make ... his life a living hell! 'Dashing' Dane Davis So you're doing jumping jacks, what's next? Iron Lung Some crunches, and some pushups with help from some dumbells. (Chuckling a bit) But since you are busy interviewing me, go grab me those two fifteen pounders over there. 'Dashing' Dane Davis Yes sir! Coming right up! Davis makes use of his name and dashes towards the dumbell set, and while he is looking upon the weights a couple of muscle men push him to the ground, grabbing both fifteen pound pairs and laughing in unisen. Davis sighs on the ground as he helps himself up, and Lung looks over at him, shaking his head in disappointment before getting up and going over to help him. Iron Lung (Shaking his head) What's the matter with you? Are you just going to let those guys shove you around like that? 'Dashing' Dane Davis They're... they're bigger than me... Iron Lung Dude, you gotta stand up for yourself. It doesn't matter how big they are, because if you have enough confidence in yourself, you can defeat the best of them. Here, come back in the locker room and let me show you something. Taking Dane Davis with him, the duo heads back to the locker room area, and once again Iron Lung pulls out his duffel bag, unzipping it. The bag is empty, so Iron Lung becomes concerned, scratching his chin a little. He then has a revelation as he checks the locker next to him, a standing up longer that is big enough to fit a man inside of it. He then pulls out his trusty bamboo shaft, showing it to the young reporter, but not allowing him to touch it. Iron Lung Do you see this shaft? Dane Davis nods slightly as Lung shows him the black tape on one end and the white tape on the other. Davis notices that the white end of the shaft is a lot larger than the darker side, and is quick to confront the ex-THC Champion about it. 'Dashing' Dane Davis What do the two sides -- Iron Lung (Interrupting) This was given to me after I graduated the Lion's Den Wrestling Dojo by my master Jade. The white represents my victories, the black represents my loses. I carry this with me everywhere, and mark it after my matches. I have a shaft for every organization I have been in at home. 'Dashing' Dane Davis Wow, thats -- Iron Lung (Interrupting again) There's one loss on there now, so I wasn't as focused, but I've learned my lesson. And that's where you come in. You can't let those punks throw you around. So what do you say we teach them a lesson, one that they surely won't forget for a while? Davis just nods, and Lung nods back as the two exit the locker room, Lung carrying the large and long bamboo shaft. We now enter Lung's vision, we see everything through his eyes as he approaches the two muscle men. We hear a mutter of 'what do you want kid?' from the first strong man, and Lung replies with a 'you push my crew around, you push me around.' The muscle man just smiles and shoves Lung a little, but Lung stares into the man's eyes, and suddenly he sees the Plague's face beginning to appear. With a large roar, Lung cracks the bamboo shaft into the man's chest, and as soon as he is disabled and bent over in pain, Lung throws him head first into a benching rack. The man flips over the barbell as the bench shutters a little, almost falling over. Now Lung approaches the second man, tapping the shaft to him. The other man tries to run away, but Lung cracks the shaft against his knee, rendering him helpless against the ground as yet again, he sees the face of the Plague when he stares at this man. The man begs for mercy as Lung pushes the shaft to his throat, nodding to Davis as he barks at the man that this would be the last time he tries to mess with him or his company. The man nods as Lung takes the shaft away, motioning for Davis to leave him and the two men alone. And with a slight smile, Davis does do. All Lung can do is smile back, as the scene fades out. He has cleared house, will he do the same against Ruff Ryder come time for Renaissance? End of ' Pump it Up ', but be sure to catch Iron Lung in ' The Result ' for another exciting adventure!
