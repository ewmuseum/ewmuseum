UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #669

Jet Lag

9/4 (year unrecorded)

Tsunami Oni — San Francisco Airport

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Standing in the Airport at San Francisco is UWF reporter 'Snap' Meyer holding a microphone. He's a small young man in a suit that looks like it came from 'Leave It to Beaver.' He seems to to waiting for someone or something. Suddenly, his eyes light up and he goes running toward a gate.] 'Snap:' Mister Oni! Mister Oni! 'Snap' Meyer here! Got time for a few questions? [Tsunami Oni has just flown in from Japan. He looks worn out. But Shiatsu looks worse. She seems very pale and wears dark glasses.] Oni: Not now. Shiatsu: It's all right. We have to wait for our luggage anyways. Oni: I suppose. But just a few. 'Snap:' Yippee! Shiatsu: I'll be sitting over here. Oni: OK. I'll join in a second. Tsunami Oni gives Shiatsu a peck on the cheek and she walks sluggishly over to a chair and sits, burying her face in her hands. 'Snap:' Is Shiatsu doing all right? Oni: Well, in case you missed it, she received a concussion after the Regal Rumble. And the long flight didn't do her any favors either. But after some rest, Shiatsu will be back to her usual... energetic... self. 'Snap:' That's great to hear. Speaking of the Rumble, what are your feelings about facing Mike Montgomery at Live and Loud for the UWF World championship? Oni: How do I feel? Like Christmas came early. Montgomery's come a long way in the last year or so, but one truth remains. He has never and will never beat Tsunami Oni! I used to slap him around all over the place. Hell, I put him on the shelf for months! 'Snap:' Oh yeah. You ran him over with an ambulance. Oni: Don't forget the train. 'Snap:' I wish I could! But moving on to this Sunday, you get the chance to be in the ring with someone you have a bit of a grudge against. Oni: Ah, yes. The Plaque. 'Snap:' Don't you mean 'the Plague?' Oni: Are you kidding? Have you seen his teeth? I guess oral hygiene isn' a priority to hardcore mofos like him. I think the Infection is named after what's happened to his gums! But seriously, if not for the Plague, I wouldn't have lost the World title. So a little payaback against him is perfect to set me on the road to Live and Loud. 'Snap:' But he's not your only opponent. Adien Pryde is also there. Oni: Yes, but why? I really don't care about him or his little Chaos Society antics. And he certainly is no threat. My best advice to him is to just stay out of the way. He's in the main event, don't embarass himself. 'Snap:' Are you worried about Chaos Society inteference? Or maybe Mike Montgomery? Or... Oni: Seymour Almasy. 'Snap:' Yeah. Him too. Oni: I don't worry, I anticipate. Tsunami Oni is not just the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. I am the best in the business. Former World title holder. Hall of Famer. Champion of Champions. And, of course, winner of the Regal Rumble. Anyone not wanting some advantage over me would be a fool. They just need to remember that whatever Tsunami wants, Tsunami gets. And anyone that gets between me and what I want will crumble like a castle of sand! So if Almasy, Montgomery, or any other yutz wants to dance with the Devil, step up! This is MY world! Tsunami Oni walks away towards Shiatsu. 'Snap:' Strong words from Tsunami Oni. But will he be able to back them up against all the opposition in front of him? We'll find out this Sunday aytSupremacy! 'Snap' Meyer saying... hasta la bye-bye!
