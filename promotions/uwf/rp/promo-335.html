UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #335

The past relived, again

13 Apr 2004

Ezra Kazee — The UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The UWF Shareholders branch office is the location again, for as we all know, that’s where most Ezra Kazee promos are held. We see “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee, sitting at his Desk, with the Digital Mortality action figure sitting on the desk in front of him. But there isn’t just one, there are four of them sitting there. All of them are attired differently, and all of them are “staring” at Ezra Kazee. Kazee is also staring them down. Kazee is wearing a blue button up shirt.) Kazee: So what am I suppose to thing. What in the world am I supposed to think about what you says Digital? Honestly, all I hear is blabber blabber blabber. You talk about how the federation is in ruins. You talk about how what you built with your blood sweat and tears is being destroyed. Well, I only have one thing to say about that, and that thing is very simple. IT”S NOT!!!!!!!! Your running around that Nick Persia is the THC Champion. Well, you know what; he fought for that title long and hard. He did what he needed to do to win that title. He went out there with his own blood sweat and tears to win that title. No thanks to Anarchy, who seem to think that they can just walk around on whoever they like to…… You complain about Beelzebozo being the world champion. Seems to me the last time I was around here there was a guy named Gurgles who was doing the same thing and raining supreme while I was here before. And this during the “Golden Age” as you calls it. And he’s been right there fighting it day in and day out. He’s given his blood sweat and tears to win that title……. Finally, you complain about me being the IC Title holder. You complain about “The Common Man” being the holder of the IC Title and that your federation that you built up is being torn down. Well, keep complaining, cause we like to hear you complain. Maybe we should do a little flashback of our own, shall we? (Kazee takes one of the action figures, stares at it intensely, and sets it up on the shelf.) I seem to remember being here during the original “Golden Age.” When the GIWF was in it’s prime. Before it became the UWF. Back when names like Lyle Wile, Cpl Punishment, Studdog Escobar, Al Crud. I remember teams like The Phenomenons and The Dragoons. Seems to me that that was a Golden Age there too. And I was there, right in the middle, a rookie, as you called it…… Mr. Meaner was the man who did what it took. And he never got very far. Until the one day when he decided to orchestrate the plan to steal all the belts. And he would’ve had them all too, except for the interference of that damn Nefilliam, or whatever his name was All of them now Hall of Famers, and all of them mentors to me in one way or another…… I seem to remember leaving everything on the line then too. I seem to remember going out for a period of time with a broken neck. I seem to remember that it was with that neck that allowed everything to get back into place……. (Kazee takes a second of the Mortality Action figures, places it on the edge of the table, and then flicks it off of the table.) Your Iron Curtain, your right, I don’t remember anything about that. But you know what, that’s a different time. I wasn’t around then. I wasn’t around when that was going on. I was off doing my own thing, studying, having a good time with my family. You know how that can be, I’m sure. But now I’m back, and I’m better than ever……. You claim that you still know me, Digi. You think that you know what I’m about and where I’m coming from. You knew the old Ezra Kazee. Who I am now and who I am then, quite a bit different. Before, I was the nice guy who would compliment and compliment and compliment. Now, I do still compliment, when it’s due, but my confidence is regained. My ability is more sure than ever. And my knowledge of the sport is continuing skyrockets……. You, you’ve been out of the sport for a long time. Your knowledge, may still be there, but your rust, you being use to the ring, the wear and tear on your body, it’s something that you haven’t experienced in a long time. It’s something that you haven’t felt in a long time. And it’s something that you are going to have to overcome if you want to win the belts from us……. You though, your confidence, it’s allowed to be there. All over the place your being picked to win. From the rag sheets, to the magazines, to the internet. Everyone is picking you. Well they should. They know about your former victory over me. They know what you have done in the past. They know where you are and who you are….. Me, my life although it is an open book, is not as storied as yours. My career is not as successful as yours. May never be as successful as yours. I mean, look at what has been done. Look at where we’ve been. My title reign as IC champion has been marred by a lack of showing, and I actually won my title with a no show victory over your partner. Of course, that’s probably easily explainable isn’t it. He was in Jamaica or wherever I was at the time……. I hold a victory over your partner, you hold a victory over me. Sounds pretty even to me. But in the end, who will win. It’s another day, another match. And while you may claim to be this better age, this better time, well, let’s just say that even the sun shine’s on a dog ass some days…… (Kazee takes another of the Mortality action figures, tosses it up in the air, and lets it fall to the floor.) On Sunday, maybe it will be one of those days that the sun will shine on a dog’s ass. It may be one of those times where you will not be able to do what you want to do. You are facing The Players Club. Your facing two men who not only represent all that is good here today, but also two men who can and will continue their dominance in the tag team division…… Mortality, Moraless, there’s not a lot left to say, but I’m sure you’ll come up with something more to say. But I’m sure y’all will find more to say. But in the end, it will be the match the fans deserve. The match that everyone will love. A definite Main Event. And in the end, the fans will get what they deserve, the match of a life time, and the tag team match for the ages. And in the end, The Players Club is going to escape from the ring, aside from what all the rags say, aside from what all the experts say. I’m use to facing the great odds. I’ve faced them all of my life, and this is going to be no different. Good Luck Mortality and Moraless, your going to need it. (Kazee take the last of the Mortality action figures, and starts unwrapping it to show that it is nothing more than a chocolate bar, which Kazee eats in one swell bite while the screen fades to black.)
