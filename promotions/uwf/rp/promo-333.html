UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #333

Registration: Main Office

13 Apr 2004

PhD — UWF Headquarters

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Back inside the Universal Wrestling Federation headquarters, Paul Henry Dawson is seen trying to follow the directions that the UWF receptionist gave him; while standing at the front desk. Walking down the hallway he has his head lowered: not making eye contact with anyone that passes by him, slightly turning his head in the opposite direction that they approached him in. He seemed to been mumbling to himself as he ever slowly quickly threw his head up to look at the nameplates on the door. Paul Henry Dawson: “Just down the hall and to the left,” Paul recited the directions that the front desk personal gave him. Paul finally reached the end of the hallway, where he then made a sharp left turn – bumping into a man with a coffee in his hand. Paul’s, who head was down probably wouldn’t even of bothered to look except the fact of the man screaming at the top of his lungs due to his hot cup of coffee being all over his shirt. Business Man: “You blundering idiot!” Paul slowly raised his head from his chest. Knowing that he had done wrong – he did not look the man in the eye at first: he was too ashamed. Instead he focused on the coffee stain on the man’s white buttoned up shirt. Paul felt it was easier to deal with trouble if he focused in on one thing. As he looked deep into that coffee stain, he pictured happy thoughts going through his head. Coming home to mother’s on Thanksgiving to sample a taste of her famous pumpkin pie, sitting at home playing with his little dog rex – the entire time the man complained to Paul. Business Man: “Hello, are you listening to me you moron!” Paul Dawson’s head unwillingly looked into the man’s eyes – as he appeared a bit shocked and nervous at the same exact timing. Paul Henry Dawson: “Uh, yes. Really very sorry about that. Please is there anything I can do to fix this?” Nevertheless, before the man could answer Paul, he took off: only giving the man a weak smile and a very fast good-bye wave. Steadily walking down the hallway rubbing his face repetitively mumbling the word “idiot” to himself – even smacking himself on the side of the head a few times. Paul Henry Dawson: “That is just the same kind of stupid crap that gets me in trouble in the first place. I have a degree to teach people – but who wants to be taught by a moron. This is the same type of attitude that Paul Henry Dawson always took with himself once things started to go wrong for himself. He never once would put the blame on anybody but himself. Sure this particular incident was technically Paul’s fault – but he cannot be blamed. As of late, Paul has a lot to think about. Less than one week ago, Paul made his first ever-wrestling debut – at Universal Wrestling Federation’s pay per view: Renaissance. Where he was chosen to be the mystery man against Kenny Mercury in a submission’s match: a match that The Professor won by making Kenny Mercury tap out in the match – his match, hand chosen. To Paul it was a night to remember, not only his win over Kenny Mercury – but also his little spat that he had with another newcomer to Universal Wrestling Federation, The Bone Crusher. As both Paul Henry Dawson and The Bone Crusher came out to be the mystery man. Technically, nobody knows who fault it was – or just how it happened. Except for two men, one of those men being Paul Henry Dawson, and the other man being the one that official signed Paul Dawson to a verbal contract with the Universal Wrestling Federation: Rick O’Connel. After having a brief sit down interview with Paul Dawson, he was given an opportunity to compete at Renaissance, against Kenny Mercury – O’Connel not thinking of The Bone Crusher. But The Bone Crusher was not the only man upset by this, after catching wind – the brand new Universal Wrestling Federation commissioner: Mike Montgomery ordered to see Paul Henry Dawson – about Paul’s actions and a written contract for a spot on the Universal Wrestling Federation’s roster. Needless to say, after the phone call, Paul was a bit shook up by it. Having to go through another wrestling interview, but this time with a man upset about Paul’s appearance in the UWF. While Paul was walking, he extensively looked at the nameplates posted on the front of all the closed doors before him. Until he finally reached a door that read, UWF Commissioner: Mike Montgomery. Upon seeing this Paul froze where he stood, just staring in at the nameplate. Paul Henry Dawson: “Here already – maybe I should make a quick round before going in to see him. You know, just to get to know the place.” Paul was about to take off, but stopped before he could take more than two paces. Paul Henry Dawson: “No, I can’t go anywhere. I just have to face the music like a man. But what kind of music does his drum beat?” Paul rubbed his face, as he put on a sour face. Paul Henry Dawson: “What could he be mad about, how should I act? Definitely shouldn’t go in there like he needs me, I’m nobody special. No, no need to try to be cocky –“ Paul said with a slight chuckle hidden in his voice. Paul Henry Dawson: “Like I could ever be cocky. Maybe I should try to butter him up a bit, a little butt kissing perhaps. That never failed me in high school – then again people always wanted to beat me up in high school too: the bullies, the football team, even the cheerleaders – maybe even a few of the teachers who’s butt I kissed. Paul Henry Dawson:”No, that won’t do.” Paul looked up at the ceiling, as he then closed his eyes and began to smile. ”You know Paul, when trying to make friends – it is always important to be yourself.” Paul’s mother’s voice echoed through his head. Paul Henry Dawson: “That’s crazy –“ However, before Paul could finish his sentence a voice was heard from inside the office. “Who the hell is outside my office?” screamed the voice as Paul had a shocked look on his face. The Professor then took in a deep breath. Paul Henry Dawson: “So crazy – that it just might work.” Paul then slowly opened up the door enough for his voice to be heard in by the UWF Commissioner: Mike Montgomery. Paul Henry Dawson: “It’s me Mr. Commissioner – Paul Henry Dawson, you sent for me remember?” Mike Montgomery: “Of course I remember you idiot, and I am still waiting. Paul Henry Dawson: “Sir?” Mike Montgomery: “I sent to talk with you, not the door.” Paul Henry Dawson: “Oh,” Paul Dawson said stupidly as he opened the door fully leaving nothing but space between Paul Henry Dawson and Mike Montgomery. Mike Montgomery was sitting down at his desk, with his feet up on the desk dressed in his casual wear – but his style of casual: Torn blue jeans, his black sunglasses, and a bandana covering his obviously messy hair. While Paul Henry Dawson stood in the doorway with his arms tightly at his side: looking a lot more professional looking than his counterpart, Montgomery. Dressed in a black suit, with a white tee shirt underneath his black sports jacket. His jet black hair, which once was tidy, is now a mess from worrying so much scratching his head and slapping himself in the back of it. Paul Henry Dawson: “So sir –“ Mike Montgomery just looked at Paul Dawson, waiting for him to finish his sentence. Paul Henry Dawson: “You wanted to see me –“ Mike Montgomery: “I did, so why don’t you actually come in my office, sit down and we can talk. Do you need easier directions?” The Professor’s face looked a bit flushed, already being downsized by Mike Montgomery – Paul knew that this wasn’t going to go over well. He slowly took a few paces into his office until he stood at Mike Montgomery’s desk. Standing there for a moment he exchanged respected looks with Mike Montgomery, as Montgomery pointed down at the chair, which surprised Paul Dawson as he practically fell in his seat. Mike Montgomery: “So why don’t you tell me what is going on Paul?” Paul Henry Dawson: “Sir?” Mike Montgomery: “Why are you here?” Paul Henry Dawson: “Because you sent for me, sir?” Mike Montgomery: “No, not in my office. I mean in the Universal Wrestling Federation.” Paul’s emotions seemed to calm down a little bit, as he seemed a bit more relaxed now with Mike Montgomery. Paul Henry Dawson: “Well sir, it has always been a dream of mine to become a professional wrestler. It is what I always wanted to do, ever since I was a little kid. Watching this week’s wrestling was the biggest highlight of my life.” Mike Montgomery: “Pathetic life – sorry to hear about it. But I don’t care about your child hood fantasies. What I care about is the Universal Wrestling Federation, and how to improve it. To bring in real talent – talent that I can mold into stars one day. Paul Henry Dawson: “With all due respect sir, I did come into the Universal Wrestling Federation’s pay per view, and defeat Kenny Mercury. I won my debut match against a UWF veteran – doesn’t that tell you something?” remarked Paul as he smiled – but quickly whipped it away once he realized what he had just said. Mike Montgomery: “That is exactly my point. I want to know what you were doing at my federation’s pay per view: Renaissance. I don’t see you on the roster, I looked through my folders upon folders and don’t see a wrestling contract for a man name PhD or Paul Henry Dawson. So I want to know just what you were doing at Renaissance, why you were in a match that was suppose to be for The Bone Crusher – an actual member of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Paul Henry Dawson: “Well sir, I actually do have a contract with the Universal Wrestling Federation. I met with Rick O’Connel two weeks prior.” Mike Montgomery: “And does he have your paper work?” Paul Henry Dawson: “No sir, it is actually a verbal contract. He is also the one that signed me as the mystery opponent against Kenny Mercury at Renaissance.” Mike Montgomery: “So you don’t have a contract – then you’re not a member of the Universal Wrestling Federation. That’s always I wanted to find out, thank you.” Mike Montgomery then put his head down, to look at some paperwork that he had laid out on his desk: as Paul Henry Dawson sat in his chair in complete bewilderment. Paul Henry Dawson: “I’m sorry, sir? “ Mike Montgomery: “That’s all that I needed – you are free to go.” Paul Henry Dawson: “And my future with the Universal Wrestling Federation?” Mike Montgomery: “Oh that, none.” Paul Henry Dawson: “Excuse me.” Mike Montgomery: “None I said, we don’t need people in the Universal Wrestling Federation that sneak behind people’s backs – god knows we have enough of them already. Paul Henry Dawson: “I did what I was told to do, I played by the rules, I even proved myself in the ring. You can’t give me an opportunity and then take it right away again.” Mike Montgomery: “I didn’t – Rick O’Connel did, and now I’m taking it away.” The whole conversation that Paul was having with Mike Montgomery, his hands were clenched on the arms of the chairs, gripping tighter with every word that Montgomery was telling him. Finally Paul Henry Dawson jumped up out of his chair and hovered over the desk of Paul Henry Dawson. Paul Henry Dawson: “I need this damn it, don’t take this away from me.” Mike Montgomery’s eyes widened as he stared deeply into the eyes of Paul Henry Dawson. Seeing what he was doing Paul slowly sat back down in his chair. Paul Henry Dawson: “Please, don’t take this away from me – I need this.” Sitting there for a moment, Mike Montgomery appeared to be thinking to himself as he finally let out a smile. Mike Montgomery: “All right Paul, you want a chance? I’ll give you a chance, this Saturday on Saturday Afternoon Slam: you get you chance to earn an official Universal Wrestling Federation written contract. If you win your match of course. Paul Henry Dawson: “My match?” Mike Montgomery: “That’s right, I’m not the only one sore with you after Renaissance. This Saturday both you and The Bone Crusher will make your Saturday Afternoon Slam debuts against one another. You win, and you get to live you weird childish fantasy.” Paul Henry Dawson: “Oh, I’ll win – in fact, The Bone Crusher is going to get schooled!” Mike Montgomery: “You know, the creative department is just down the hall.” Said Mike Montgomery laughing at his own joke. Paul Henry Dawson stood up, a little embarrassed but nonetheless still proud. He was going to be given a shot at becoming a part of the Universal Wrestling Federation’s roster. All that he would have to do is win a match and he was in. Paul nodded in the direction of Mike Montgomery who did nothing in return, as he made his way back over to the door way as he turned and looked back at Mike Montgomery. “Class is in Session” he stated to the commissioner of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Mike Montgomery: “Creative is down the hallway, to the right.” Paul simply turned his head by Montgomery’s remark, there was nothing that could get him down – Paul felt like a million bucks. He then stepped out of Mike Montgomery’s office, closing the door behind him. As he closed the door shut, he jumped up in the air and shouted “Wahoo” with a few doors opening up to see what the noise was all about. Even Mike Montgomery’s voice could be heard yelling “freaking idiot”. But Paul didn’t care, he was just one small step away from actually becoming a member of the federation he so desperately wanted in. Paul Henry Dawson: “I can’t believe it – just one match and I am in. I get to fulfill my dream, I get to be a part of Universal Wrestling Federation, I get – a paycheck. The only thing that I have to do is to beat Bone Crusher. Which should not be too much of a problem, I mean after all – he did step away once he realized that I was the one in the match up and not him. It’s not like he has any kind of advantage over me, if anything I am the one going into this match with the advantage. It’s obvious that I have more brains than the man – after all he was the idiot that tried to pin Kenny Mercury in a submission only match up, that should tell me something. Paul Henry Dawson: “Brains is definitely in my advantage, there is no doubt about that one. Size can be a factor but not when you are going against somebody with the IQ of one of the turnbuckles. Besides, it’s not like I am some kind of scrawny kid.” Paul Dawson said to himself, as he stopped his walk for a moment to look at his right bicep. Paul Henry Dawson: “No doubt about that one, and technically I am one up on the guy. After all, in the record books I am the one with the win: not Bone Crusher.” Paul said chuckling to himself. “So he is a bit upset that I am the one that got the win, and not him. What should he care, he didn’t get a loss – I’ll even go out and be a nice guy and tell the guy he can have the assist. Paul continued his walk down the hallway, trying to figure a way to get out of the Universal Wrestling Federation headquarters, still contemplating his upcoming confrontation with The Bone Crusher. Paul Henry Dawson: “So I guess he has a bit of a mean streak going on right now, a bit ticked off if you will. However, by playing it smart: which is a person of my education’s expertise, I know that he will trip up. Always going into something ill tempered will always blow up in your face, keeping it cool and calm headed will be my advantage over Bone Crusher. The only thing that he is going to have going through his mind is revenge – revenge over something that does not require vengeance. While I go into this with passion and will. I am not going to let a little thing like Bone Crusher ruin my chances at becoming a UWF superstar. Finally Paul Henry Dawson finds the lobby, where he first entered the Universal Wrestling Federation’s headquarters, – the receptionist is still there, now arguing with another man at the front desk. Seeing her brought a smile to Dawson’s face, because sooner or later she would have to respect him – maybe even like him. Paul Henry Dawson: “It’s not a surprise to me that Bone Crusher is a bit upset, and might of complained to the higher ups here in the Universal Wrestling Federation about some guy not even officially in the UWF, get a win in his match up. After all, the guy did come after me when the bell rang. Nevertheless, I figured best, not to make enemies in the company that I am trying to get a position in. So I left it go, took my win and went to rejoice – not thinking twice about it Bone Crusher. However, it’s time that I do start to think about it, because this Saturday we both get what we want. We both make our official debuts, you get a chance for revenge, I get a chance for a written UWF contract – plus I get to give you something that I forgot about at Renaissance. Paul Dawson began to make his way to the doors of the UWF headquarters, as he passed the front desk he looked over at the receptionist who caught a look back at him: in retaliation Paul simply cracked a big smile. In return, she gave him the middle finger. The second that Paul saw that his smile went away and he lowered his head down, with his cheeks a rosy color – as he continued for the exit until he ran into the door. Upon rubbing his head, Paul quickly looked around and saw people looking over at him; some laughing. He hastily opened the door and bolted out of sight, leaving the door close for itself.
