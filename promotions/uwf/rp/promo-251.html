Two Heads: Part Two

Iron Lung — Apartment

Two Heads: Part Two Two Heads: Part Two Starring Iron Lung The scene is quiet as it opens up, but it quickly fills with a static sound, revealing a little more with each passing moment. Suddenly, the television changes to full color, a man and woman beginning to take shape. It is quiet on the television as the man stands with crossed arms in front of the woman. The woman is misty-eyed, and quickly runs her pointer finger under her eye lid to dry a tear as she sobs. You could feel the emotion in the air, even if there weren't any words being said between the pair. Was this perhaps some sort of relationship trouble? We would soon find out when the silence broke, but for now, the drama behind this moment was beginning to set into us. The sobbing continued, the man remaining speechless. All that came out of his mouth was a sigh. Perhaps it was out of anger, or it might have been out of confusion. What could Sophie want from Brandon? She was already with Mike, and just found out that she was pregnant with John's son! Brandon was just playing with Sophie to get back at David for calling him an inconsiderate punk, but he also wanted to show Joyce that he could be a better man than Clint. As the scene progresses, the camera swoops around to Iron Lung, staring at the screen and munching on some Crunch n' Munch. He actually seems absorbed in the programming. What was going on? Was he too lazy to change the channel, waiting for another show to come on, or did he really want to see it? We had to find out.. Oh, it's you.. Um, I can explain... Clearing his throat a little bit, the pale tone on his cheeks have turned slightly red. Alas, he regains his composure and tries to explain his actions with what he put on the television. Sense on Supremacy, I'm going to be in my first UWF tag match, I thought it would be appropriate to do something I haven't done in a while. Sit down on the couch, and experience this thing you all like to call 'feelings' and 'emotions'. So now, I'm watching this program, trying to see what it's like to actually give a rat's ass what a person thinks or says. And the funny thing is, I'm actually doing this because I want Joshua Dunn and I to get along in the ring. I know it sounds crazy, but I think by watching this, I can actually learn how to listen to opinions and care enough to give some sort of feedback. Taking a slight break, IL takes a sip of water from the corner of his coffee table. Tisking his teeth as if to say that it was refreshing to him, he puts the cup back down and prepares to talk again.. To tell you the truth, the words that Kenny had for me, well they hurt a little. Calling the belt I have worthless and all. I think that its something to be proud of, considering that I grew up having nothing. But you wouldn't know about that Mercury, because you grew up thinking everybody was wrapped around your finger. And frankly, people like you get on the very few nerves that I have left inside of me.. You make fun of my name and disrespect me, without knowing what my partner can do or what I can do personally. I can make your life a living hell, I can make you wish you never stepped into the ring. But you seem to overshadow that, because you think that you are better than everyone else. I guess that I'm just going to have to prove that wrong come Supremacy. The camera slowly rolls back to the television screen, where Sophie slaps Brandon. Cutting back to IL's reaction, we see a grimace on his face and him mouth 'that had to hurt.' Well, I hate to be a pain in the neck, but you're making me miss my show. I have to get more in touch with my 'softer side' if you will. So if you please, nows the time to shut the cameras off. Thank you for your time... The scene quietly begins to fade away to black, and then from black to static with Iron Lung's flourescent eyes glued to the television screen.
