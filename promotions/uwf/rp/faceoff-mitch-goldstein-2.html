More shmekel to be kicked! More dreidle of doom~! And—and I’m up against Candy Concannon, so more boobies!!!” [The RJD’s tongue falls out of his mouth as he grins stupidly. Wait… don’t tell me. I, like, know soooooo much about wrestling, Mr. Destroyer. I bet I can guess it!” “Shvayg!” “Let’s see… your brother died, and he made you swore you’d win a wrestling tournament, and dedicate it to him.” “Bah!” “You—want to get married, but her evil father is a redneck and only respects wrasslers and Nascar drivers!” “Bubkis!” “You’re fighting to defend your religion’s heritage and pride?” “Little Timmy, sit down.” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer and Little Timmy sit down in the middle of the busy sidewalk, ignoring the curses and shouts from the other pedestrians.] “At least tell me you’re going to buy a really sweet ride, Mr. Destroyer! Like a Mazda 626! VROOM VROOM!” “None of the above, I’m afraid, Little Timmy.” “Then what, Mr. Destroyer? WHAT?!” “Little Timmy, when I win this tournament, I will receive $25,000.” [Little Timmy nods.] “And do you know how much money that’ll be when you compound for interest?!?” [Little Timmy shakes his head to the negative.] “Well, Little Timmy, by opening a savings account, I can place that $25,000, and over time it will grow exponentially! Compounding at an interest rate of 2.35% in an APR savings program, I can see an increase of $490 just in the first month!” “Wow!” “And that’s not all. Within thirty years, I could have—like—billions!” “Super!” “And then, Little Timmy, I can move to Florida, play shuffleboard, and never work again for as long as I live. And touch as many boobies as I want. And—Little Timmy? Why are you crying?” [Little Timmy wipes his arm across his face quickly.] “It’s just so—it’s just so wonderful, Mr. Destroyer. You have the greatest dream I ever heard. I can’t wait until I can be a wrestler, kick people in the shmekel, touch boobies, and learn the inner intricacies of compounding interest so I can live my life like an old Jewish widow, too.” “It’s a dream worth having, Little Timmy. And—“ “THE LORD WILL SAVE YOU!” [Little Timmy and the RJD look around, surprised.] “What was that, Mr. Raving Jewish Destroyer, sir?” “I don’t know, Little Timmy. Let’s go investigate!” “THE LORD WILL PROTECT YOU!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer and Little Timmy cautiously wonder down the sidewalk. Turning a corner, they spy a man in a trashy blue blazer, a book open in front of him, talking non-stop as loudly as he can.] “HE WILL FORGIVE YOUR TRESPASSES AS YOU FORGIVE THOSE WHO TRESPASS AGAINST YOU!” “Wait here, Little Timmy.” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer wanders over to the shouting Bible-thumper. He begins to circle around him, picking at his clothing, and examining him like some sort of animal.] “JESUS WILL BE YOUR INSPIRATION!” “Who?” “JESUS WILL – wait. You don’t know who Jesus is?” “No?” “Dear lord, my child. Who do you think your savior is?” “Gigantor?” “Jesus!” “GIGANTOR!!!!” “JESUS!!!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer snatches away his Bible, strikes a pose, and begins bellowing himself.] “GIGANTOR! GIGANTOR! GIGANTOR FOREVER~!” “You… you sacrilegious child!!” “ME?! A chaleyre! A plague on you! A PLAGUE!!!!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer points an accusing finger at the man.] “But my child… Jesus loves you.” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer… drops his arm and accusing finger, melting before our eyes.] “He—he does?” “Yes, my child.” <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Little Timmy stood about fifteen feet away, poking his head around the corner to watch the ensuing fight between the Raving Jewish Destroyer and the sidewalk preacher, just waiting for the RJD to kick him in the shmekel and run like his Gefilte Fish was on fire.] [But when the fight suddenly came to an end, Little Timmy became concerned.] “Oh no! I’m concerned!” [That’s what I said, Little Timmy.] “I didn’t think you drove home the point very well, Mr. Narrator, sir. I’m going to go check on Mr. Raving Jewish Destroyer and make sure he’s alright.” [Little Timmy left his hiding place, approaching the RJD to make sure he was alright. He came to a screeching stop, however, as he spied IT on the ground: The Raving Jewish Destroyer’s yarmulke! DISCARDED!] “Oh no! Mr. Destroyer would never let his yarmulke touch the ground! Something must be wrong!” [Little Timmy hurried over to the Raving Jewish Destroyer’s side.] “Mr. Raving Jewish Destroyer, what happened!? You dropped your yarmulke, sir!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer swung around, smiling at the child. The preacher does as well, speaking first.] “He had impure thoughts in his mind, my child.” “I had impure thoughts in my mind, Little Timmy. About boobies.” “He worshipped false idols.” “I worshipped Gigantor.” “Gigantor Forever?” “No, Little Timmy, not any more. Join us, Little Timmy!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer throws an arm around the sidewalk preacher.] “JESUS! JESUS! JESUS FOREVER~!” [Little Timmy gasps! RJD sees this, and pats his child follower on the head.] “I’m converting to Catholicism, Little Timmy. I have found my calling in life. I’m going to be a Catholic Priest!” [Little Timmy gasps again.] “But Mr. Destroyer, sir—“ “FREEZE SCUM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer shrieks like a woman as a hundred cops swarm out from every crevice and sewer grate. They all have their guns pointed at him. The RJD wets his pants.] “OY VEY!!!!” [Three cops tackle the Raving Jewish Destroyer, throwing cuffs on him.] “You’re going to prison for a long, long time, you disgusting little pedophile!!! I HOPE YOU ROT!!!!” [The RJD is yanked up to his feet harshly.] “NO!!!! Mr. Sidewalk Preacher … I think I need to be transferred to a new location!!!!” [The RJD swings his head around everywhere.] “Mr. Sidewalk Preacher? Oh dreck.” [The RJD is quickly whisked away.] “Wait for me, Little Timmy!! WAAAAIIIIITTTTTTT FOOOORRRRRR MEEEEEEEE AAATTTTT THHHEEEEE JEEEEEEEEEEWWW CAAAAAAAVE!!!!!!” “Oh gosh, Mr. Raving Jewish Destroyer. But… Mr. Cop, sir? He has a wrestling match! He can’t be arrested.” [The cop bends down, patting him on the head.] “It’s okay, kid. That freak is going away for a long, long time. You’re going to be okay; he’ll never touch you again. We just need you to come with us… but look on the bright side! When this is all over, you can write a book about it and become a millionaire.” “Really!?” “Yep!” “Oh! And then—then I can put it in an interest gathering savings account, and by the time I reach my retirement age, I can buy an island!!! And all the boobies I could ever want! Sweet!” [Little Timmy is whisked away with the parade of cops.] … [WHAT will happen now that the Raving Jewish Destroyer has abandoned his religion?] [WILL he rename himself the Raving Catholic Destroyer?] [HOW will he possibly get out of jail in time to wrestle in the FaceOff tournament later tonight?] [WHAT kind of book deal will Little Timmy receive, and what sort of rate will he get in order to compound interest and buy an island?] [WHY did this go on for so long?] [WAS the author just phenomenally bored?] [IS Gigantor really a false idol?] [All those answers and MORE will be answered next time!
