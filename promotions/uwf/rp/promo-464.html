UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #464

The Opening Shot

24 Jun 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up just outside what people have to know as the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Standing there is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a pair of jean shorts and a tank top. He is just looking off into space when the camera comes on, not really paying much attention to anything. Seconds after the camera comes on, he looks at the camera. saying nothing, blankly staring into the camera. He takes a couple of steps forward, not really paying attention to the camera. He then turns to the right and continues to walk what seems almost aimlessly, with a purpose only known to him. Soon, he stops, so that his profile is all that the camera sees. He begins to speak.) Kazee: Stable Wars is coming up shortly, isn’t it? Stable Wars is our next card, isn’t it. And everyone is looking for a new team to take it. A new stable to rise. They say that the Players Club is on the downfall. They say that we are loosing our edge…… Maybe they are right. We’ve lost the Tag Team gold. We’ve lost the IC Gold. We’ve lost the World Champion to injury. We are a stable that has had quite a few downfalls lately, and may have a hard time getting ourselves back up on top. There are so many other stables out there that are extremely good, and have a lot more momentum going for them…… The Resurrection is on a hot streak. They’ve got the tag titles. They have the new PTC Extreme Champion. Digital Mortality himself has taken out the World Champion, our leader Mike Montgomery…… Chaos Society is probably just as hot right now. They have the TV Champion in Adien Pryde, who just recently won it. Granted, he also recently lost it as well, but he still seems to be on a hell of a roll. They have Raid, the new Intercontinental Champion, and the man who put me in the hospital. They have a little extra edge too. That edge being that they have a mystery partner. A person who nobody knows who it is, or the only people who will know who that person is is Chaos Society until Stable Wars when we all shall find out…… We also have our problems within the Players Club. Starr and Dunn have never gotten along. Our leader is gone. I’m just returning from an injury. And who knows what else is going to be against us going in….. Yes, things are looking very bleak for the Players Club, aren’t they? Things are looking to be on a downward swing for us. I’m sure the experts, the odds makers are betting against us. And many would say they are right to be……. But they would be wrong. We have one of the most talented stables in stable wars. We are heads and tails above everyone else, and we always will be. Look at who is in the Players Club….. First, we have Nick Persia, the biggest thing from Holland since Dutch Chocolate. This guy is a man who is a former THC champion. This is a man who many people say is the future of this business. This is a man who many say will become the one of the most dominant wrestlers of all time in the UWF……… Next, we have Kevin Starr. This man is a hall of famer. He has won just about every title there is to hold in the UWF/GIWF. This man has been to the top of the mountain. This man has wrestled the best and come out on top. He will continue to wrestle the best and come out on top…….. Next, Joshua Dunn. What can be said about the Communist Scott. He beat Almasay in the Mt. Gagazet Death match. He is one of the longest reigning UWF IC Champions of all time. He is going to be a hall of famer some day. He is probably the most talented person we have in the Players Club…… And finally, we have me. The ultimate sportsman. One of the greatest technical wrestlers to ever be in the UWF. The man who is a former Tag Team Champion, a former 2-time TV champion, a former IC Champion. A Clash of Champions finalist. A man who many believe will be on top some day, if not soon. A man who everyone seems to respect, and everyone knows can get the job done…… We have the deck stacked against us, you bet we do, but we are what has been called the greatest as semblance of wrestlers to ever exist. We are the stable that everyone comes to see, that everyone wants to see and be like. We are the top stable here……. And our first round draw is Chaos Society. A bunch of upstarts who think they can come in and run with the big dawgs. A group of wrestlers that tried to end my career. And a group who thinks they will have the upper hand because guess what, they have a mystery partner. Well, mystery partner or not, it doesn’t matter. Chaos Society, you guys are running into the greatest stable of all time. The stable that will not fall, that will overcome the odds. You bring your monster. You bring your exterminator. You bring your Martial Arts expert. You bring the freaking 82nd air born if you have to. It won’t matter who you bring, because you will find out that the Players Club isn’t playing and you will be eliminated first on our way to glory. (Kazee stares blankly ahead again. He starts walking again blankly and aimlessly. The camera fades to black.)
