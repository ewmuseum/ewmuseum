UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #334

Ready to whoop ass

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with the True Indian Superstar Ruff Ryder asleep on his couch. He is snoring away when there is a knock on the door. He raises up and looks at the door for a few seconds before laying back down thinking the person was gonna just give up and leave. Another knock brings Ryder to his feet, extremely pissed off. He picks up a black and red Prime Alliance t-shirt and puts it on as he jerks open the door. ) Ryder: What the fuck do you want? * There is a cameraman standing outside the door, and in true cameraman style he says nothing and just holds the camera pointed toward Ryder * Ryder: Answer me damnit!! * Still nothing * Ryder: You want a fuckin promo don't you? Well that's just tough shit, because I got a hangover like you wouldn't believe and I don't have time to bullshit around with some asshole cameraman from the UWF. You go and you tell Mike Montgomery he can take that camera that you are holding and stick it up his no talent having ass! * The man just stands there for a few seconds, and then turns to leave, but Ryder stops him * Ryder: Wait wait, sorry about that. It's not your fault that Montgomery is a prick and sent you all this way. I tell you what, i'll give you five minutes, but then I really gotta get some sleep. * The man nods at Ryder * Ryder: Ok, so it's the Prime Alliance against this so called chaos society and that roach infested bastard Raid. Let me tell you something boy, you got an assbeating coming anyway for what you did to me and Malcolm Kennedy not too long ago. You see the Prime Alliance doesn't just forget that kind of crap. We knew we'd see you somewhere down the line and it's now the end of the line for you. I just want you to remember one thing when your chocking on your own blood after the beating we hand you...you brought it on yourself. You could have stayed out of our way and none of this would happen, but you fucked up and now you're gonna get knocked out. As for your bitch partner, those stupid comments about us not being tag team wrestlers? Who fuckin cares. All one of us has to do is throw you around for a few seconds until we get bored and then we'll knock your ass out and pick up the victory...no big deal. Your little society ain't shit when it's standing next to a giant like the Prime Alliance. We are the best and always will be. Fuck the Chaos Society and anyone who looks like em. That is all. ( Ryder slams the door in the cameraman's face before taking off his shirt and slowly walking back over to his couch. He yawns and stretchs and then falls face first onto the couch trying to go back to sleep as the scene fades out and comes to an end. )
