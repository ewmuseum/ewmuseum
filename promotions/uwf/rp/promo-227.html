Once More Unto the Breach Dear Friends...

17 Feb 2004

Mike Montgomery — The Office of the UWF Executive Vice President of Talent Relations.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And we're back in the office of the Executive Vice President of Talent Relations. If this return surprises you in any way, stop reading right now. Those of you that pay attention to these promos, let us continue... The Clash was a tremendous night for the Player's Club. Righting a wrong of bad booking, Mike Montgomery and Ezra Kazee teamed up, as promised, to win the UWF Tag Team Championship. Joshua Dunn voluntarily ended the longest title reign in UWF History after defeating Seymour Almasy, possibly the greatest World Champion ever, in Mount Gagazet II. Dunn even went as far as to ensure that the Player's Club retains the Intercontinental Title by entering both Ezra Kazee and Monty into the eight man title tournament. However, all is not well in the Player Nation. In a move that really made little sense to anyone, after singing the praises of both Ezra Kazee and the Player's Club, Raid decided to bring in his own thugs to attack the Club. After that, Bondage the Sex Fiend showed up and...well nobody's entirely sure what that was save for it being really, really...well really kinda cool let's be honest. But then they brought the fan in and...then it wasn't quite so cool. Anyway, as we've already established, we're having this little promo in the office of the Executive Vice President of Talent Relations. Mike sits at his desk, glass of coke in hand, head tilted back, reliving what was...the Clash. Go, go, go, go Go, go, go shawty It's your birthday We gon' party like it's yo birthday We gon' sip Bacardi like it's your birthday And you know we don't give a fuck It's not your birthday!
