Trailer Park Luchador — Angle #2

20 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[With a perplexed look, Tommy Waters steps into the ring, his microphone at his waist, a note in his hands.] Rick: “And it looks like the night is about to get started early … we’re not ready for our first match, so I’m not exactly sure what this is.” Larson: “I guarantee you it is something very, very bad.” “Ahem!” [Waters stands in the middle of the ring, microphone raised, staring at his note, as he begins his introduction.] Waters: “I have been asked to make a very special announcement tonight. For one night only, the Universal Wrestling Federation is proud to welcome a phenomenal wrestling talent to our shores. A name that has been whispered on the lips of wrestling aficionado’s for generations … a legend in our sport.” Larson: “If this is Cave Hulk again, I fucking quit.” Waters: “Ladies and gentlemen … boys and girls … please join me in welcoming, the …” [Tommy Waters frowns at the paper, and shakes his head.] Water: “Please welcome the Baby Seal Clubber.” Rick: “…” [And just like that … SEAL’s “Newborn Friend” hits over the sound system.] I wash my faith in dirty water, Cause it gives my mind a little order. And I play that game, just like I should do. But my whole world, slips away. I live my life, I live it slowly. Rick: “This is a joke, right?” Larson: “I … err …” And I take my time, I'm in no hurry, yea... I'm livin'... And when I go then I would surely. I would dance with a total stranger, And hold them in my arms. [As the crowd is lulled into a false sense of security, a figure comes suddenly bounding out onto the entrance ramp. A figure dressed in a white snowsuit … quite like an Eskimo. An EVIL Eskimo.] Rick: “Yep. This is going to end very, very badly.” Hell, there could be no danger if I open up, Cause when the cryin' starts so, If I chant for happiness, Maybe that will make me feel better I can't change my ideals, I can't lose my desire Ohh, if I chant for happiness maybe that will make me feel better I'd be a newborn friend for the world. [In addition to his giant snowy white parka, the man on the stage wears a blue and white mask, with a seal on the forehead. Of course, the seal’s eyes are just little X’s. One hand holds a giant bag that is moving all on its own. Another hand holds a giant club. And under one shoulder … a teddy bear.] Well I wash my face in dirty water. For life I have no order. Cause I, Know that feeling when you've tried so hard to make your way out, Nothing mends. So you search for the breed of the problems there lying, Deep down in the soul. Rick: “Am I imagining things, or … fuck it. That’s the Trailer Park Luchador.” Larson: “I’m telling you … this is just bad.” Rick: “I guess it makes sense, though. If I was pummeled as badly by a woman as he was by Tigera last week, I’d change my name, too.” Larson: “Not to mention that it was a mediocre name to begin with.” Even if, they never end, There's a whole bunch of reasons, tryin' to get out But they can't make it out of the door. Ohh, I know the reason, I'm not enough. There's nothing more... But, If I chant for happiness, maybe that will make me feel better. I can't changes my ideals, I can't put out the fire. Ohh, if I chant for happiness, maybe that will bring us together, For the better... [With an Eskimo war cry – or what the masked man believes sounds like something Eskimo’s would chant out before running into war – the Baby Seal Clubber comes charging down the rampway, charging at full speed into the ring.] I can't changes my ideals, I can't lose my desire. I'm just a newborn friend for the world... Just a newborn friend for the world... I'll be your newborn friend and I'll... Sing loud... Sing loud... Sing loud... my friend Gonna sing louder sing loud sing loud my friend To my friend my friend and I... I'll sing love, sing loud, sing a little louder of my friend. Ohh, my darlin friend my friend my friend [In the ring, the ‘Clubber tosses his bag down and snatches away the microphone from Tommy Waters.] Clubber: “Hello, losers!!!” [The ‘Clubber gets a smattering of jeers. The Masked Eskimo bends down to the bear and starts whispering to him.] Clubber: “See! Insulting the crowd, just like you told me. I love being a bad guy.” [Jumping back to his feet, the Baby Seal Clubber points out at the crowd.] Clubber: “Ladies and homo’s … my name is the Baby Seal Clubber. And I am here because the Yoo-Dubya-Eff has fallen on hard times. We need things to spark imagination! To bring emotion back into the world of wrestling! And that is why, with the help of my friends, I have reached out to other organizations to find the next generation of wrestling fans … please welcome the Boy Scout Troop 96, coming all the way to Chicago from the great state of Florida, the winners of an all expense paid trip to see Supremacy live!” [The ‘Clubber points to the grouping of young children standing along the front row, cheering and waving stupidly while they get on television.] Clubber: “By appealing to the youth of America, I am helping to bring the next generation of fans into our lovely world … and that is why I am out here today! And that is why I have brought my little friend to the ring tonight. Please welcome … Mr. Cuddles!” [The Clubber lifts up his sack and dumps out …] *insert pic* [Mr. Cuddles immediately tries to waggle away, but the ‘Clubber steps on his tail. Quickly, he bends down and picks the little guy up, rubbing him against his mask and coo-ing to him.] Clubber: “Mr. Cuddles is my bestest buddy. He’s so cute, and cuddly, and white, and soft, and cute, and … awww … oh crap!” [The Baby Seal Clubber drops Mr. Cuddles to the mat, getting a cry from the little guy. The ‘Clubber quickly steps on his tail again.] Clubber: “PSYCHE!!!! I’m not the ‘Clubber … I am … THE TRAILER PARK LUCHADOR~!!!!!!” [The mask comes flying off, and the Trailer Park Luchador stands in the middle of the ring, wearing a parka, and holding a cricket paddle.] Rick: “He’s not really going to go this far, is he?” Larson: “It really is a good thing PETA has our press secretary on speed-dial at this point.” [The crowd … groans. The Luchador of the Mobile Home Community grits his teeth.] TPL: “You know what … I wasn’t going to do it before, but now you’ve driven me to it! I deserve respect~!” [The Boy Scout Troop 94 of Tallahassee screams out.] [And the Mulleted Champion of Goodness kicks Mr. Cuddles several feet.] Rick: “…” TPL: “Mwahahaha! I’m so evil! Boo me~!” Larson: “…” [The camera instinctively cuts away to save the UWF as much money from lawsuits as humanly possible.] … … … … [And it cuts back just a few minutes too soon. The Trailer Park Luchador is standing in the ring cackling EVILLY, while Boy Scout Troop 94 is screaming in horror at the bloody, fuzzy carcass that’s been thrown on them.] Rick: “Wow. That was way, WAY too far.” TPL: “Oh! Oh! Wait … I had a tag line for this … what was it?! Christ!” [The Trailer Park Luchador bites his lower lip as children in blue uniforms covered in seal blood flee the arena.] TPL: “It was something about how I’m evil and scarring the next generation … dear sweet lord, I had to practice that line so many times, how did I forget it …” Larson: “I’m so torn. I don’t know whether to laugh or vomit on that idiot.” Rick: “…” [As TPL goes on and on movement comes from within the audience at his back. Out of no where Tigera leaps over the barrier and base-slides into the ring. Quietly getting to her feet as the crowds goes wild with screams she smiles sweetly.] TPL: “NO! Stop that … I’m the bad guy! Boo me! BOO M—” [Tigera suddenly unleashes a deadly and well placed round house kick to the side of his head, taking the Luchador down to the mat.] Rick: “Tigera is here … oh thank Rod Tigera is here! That asshole would amputate orphans if it got him attention.” Larson: “Again: torn. You kind of have to respect that.” [Tigera signals for a mic as TPL tries to get up only to put planted face first with a foot stomp to the back. Tigera shifts firmly planting a foot across his face as she leans down with mic in hand.[ Tigera: “So you think you funneh man huh? You think you got de better of Tigera? Think again.” [Stomps him again.] Tigera: “You mama not teach you manners. So I teach you for her.” [Tigera kicks him in the head.] TPL: “Don’t you talk about my m—” [Stomps him YET again.] Rick: “Never been so happy to see that fuzzy hair out here, Larson.” Larson: “I’m always happy to see those jiggling funbags out here.” [Finally she crouches down to get next to his face.] Tigera: “You come near me again and I will cut off your man parts and feed them to you. NEVER TOUCH ME AGAIN!” [After having said her piece the tigress tosses away the mic and side mounts TPL's prone face down body to punch furiously at his head.] [And she continues to do so.] [And … yeah. Still hitting him.] [One or two more times.] [Alright, maybe more than that. Still smacking him around.] Rick: “You know … I have no problem with this.” Larson: “As long as she’s straddling him, neither do I.” [And after about another dozen punches … the Masked Security Detail arrives! With a cry, and led by Plucky the Cockfighting Duck, Pajado de Presa, White Tiger, Crab Mask Jr, El Turtlé, and the rest of the collection of idiots – followed by a dozen rent-a-cops in Batman masks – come pouring out of the back, charging towards the ring to save their masked brethren.] Rick: “Damnit.” [Tigera roars as the Masked Security Detail pours into the ring, looking to intervene. She levels Pajaro … takes Crab Mask Jr. out with a stiff roundhouse kick … Plucky the Cockfighting Duck is sent cascading over the top rope after a well placed punt, hitting a traumatized, crying Boy Scout on the outside of the ring when he comes down.] [But eventually the vastly superior numbers overwhelm her, and Tigera is pulled by, kicking and screaming, from the bloody carcass of the Trailer Park Luchador.] Rick: “And a fitting end. Commercial time?” [White Tiger wanders off to the Trailer Park Luchador … and bitch slaps him back into consciousness. The Hero of the Mulleted leaps to his feet, eyes wide, stumbling backwards dazedly. Tigera fights with the security force to get at the Luchador again, but to no avail.] [And with her back turned to him, the Luchador – eyes have swollen, barley conscious, but still EVIL – sees his opportunity.] [He darts across the ring … he slips … he slides! Before Tigera has any clue what’s happening, the Luchador pinches her ass with both hands. Tigera roars with indignation … the Luchador screams like a girl and leaps out of the ring.] Rick: “Oh God … what is wrong with him!?” Larson: “Damnit … I’m torn again. You have to respect that.” [With Tigera’s anger renewed, you begins lashing out at every security guard within reach as the Champion of the Double-Wide darts up the ramp to safety.] Rick: “I just don’t understand the thought process here … Tigera has absolutely obliterated him every time he was within reach. Why keep pissing her off!?” Larson: “Maybe he just wanted a little feel? If Ahnold can do it, I think you should keep your outrage to yourself.” [And with that finished, Tommy Waters steps tentatively back into the ring. He looks at the rampage Tigera is causing, looks back down at his notecard, gulps … and raises the microphone.] Waters: “I was asked to say one last thing. Ahem … “The Trailer Park Luchador Wins.” [Tigera glares at Tommy Waters. Then, after knocking Pajaro de Presa away, she stalks over to him … and kicks him in the groin.] Waters: “Yelp!” [Tommy Waters drops like a brick. Tigera kicks White Tiger in the head, and then chases up the ramp after the Luchador.]
