Fan #1 This Old House

4 Dec 2004

Adien Pryde — Location of the Venetian Home Rumble 5

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside the house set for the Venetian Home Rumble 5. Three figures are standing outside the gate on the sidewalk talking. All are wearing the Black and Red of The Chaos Society. The three people are Adien Pryde and Reo Dai, the tag team known as Anarchy, and the manager of The Chaos Society, Joanna.] Dai : This is asinine. We are a tag team, but the fans vote you and Tsunami Oni to go after the championship. I knew that the fans of the UWF were stupid but this is over the top. Joanna : Don’t worry, Adien and I have thought it all out and have the plans set in motion. When this is all over, Anarchy will be lined up for a title shot, but we will just need to put a final couple of pieces into place. Dai : This should be a walk in the park though for you. With the great Tsunami Oni on your team, and you being a tag team specialist, you should have this match in the bag but that does put our shot at the title a bit of a problem. How would we compete for the title when you actually hold them with someone else. Pryde : That will be something we will deal with when it is time but now we need to figure out how to deal with the other teams. Joanna : That should be easy, just look at who they are. First you have Killer Dude and Nick Persia, They hate each other. Their lust over the same belts has put them at each others throats more than once. Pryde : What about Montgomery and Clyde. They are both big names in the UWF, main eventers in their own right. Joanna : Yes but they hate one another more that anything, Clyde caused Montgomery to lose the World Title to your Partner and hell, they are facing off against each other in the main event. With any luck, they will tear each other apart in this match and we won’t have to be put through their single match. Pryde : Commie and Justice are both worthy singles wrestlers but I don’t think that the opposites on ideals will let them co-exist but they have the least amount of hate between them after Oni and I so they may be my biggest challenge. Dai : What about Fizzy and Seymour Almasy? With Fizzy in Chaos Society, he should be fairly understanding. Pryde : Maybe. Fizzy has been spending a lot of time with that loser Dreammaker lately, shit, he has been spending more time with him then us, so I would put him as a possible boot from us. Seymour Almasy is so rabid about title reigns and the fact that my partner is who he is facing for the world title after this match that he could have enough in his to do it al by himself in this match. They won’t be easy any way you look at it. Then you have Kenny Mercury and Jamie shock. If they don’t kill each other on the way to the match, they could be a problem. I really don’t see that happening though with their history. Dai : Now what are the final few pieces to the puzzle? Joann : See that house? With this being a hardcore style match, interference is allowed and… [Just then the Black and Red bus that Ezra Kazee bought for the Chaos Society pulls up and block the view of the camera and with the music blaring as loud as it is from the moving party mobile nothing can be heard either. After a few minutes, the bus begins to pull away leaving an empty sidewalk. The camera zooms in onto the front door of the house and begins to fade out when we see the curtain in the window move.]
