UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #256

A Quiet Riot

28 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — The Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up inside the arena. There is no one around except for “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is sitting in the ring. The ring itself is already set up. Looks like just about everyone had cleared out of there several hours ago. Kazee just is sitting in the empty arena, all by himself. He is wearing a white t-shirt with black lettering that says “GOOD LUCK” on it. He is also wearing a pair of cream colored khakis and a pair of tennis shoes. He is leaning back upon a turnbuckle, head down, and arms limply lying to the side. He looks like he is in a state of total relaxation.) Kazee: We are less than 48 hours away and no one has heard anything from Bondage. Does anyone wonder why? Has he decided not to show up for the match? One has to wonder. You would’ve thought that we would have heard something by now. Maybe he’s too busy “getting his groove on” to even pay attention to me. But we shouldn’t dwell on such things…… It does seem that people are starting to focus on you however Bondage. Your over the top, pushing the envelope, shock value gimmick seems to be getting you the attention you want. I hope that is what you wanted, because it probably isn’t the greatest thing in the world. I mean you’ve got Anarchy’s attention. Well, from the tapes I’ve seen from these guys, it’s not a team you want to piss off. But that’s another issue all together…… The issue that is at hand is our match on Sunday. Our match in the IC title tournament to see who will advance to take on Seymour Almasay or Moraless. Fact of the matter is, Bondage, is that the next round won’t be important for you because you aren’t going to the next round. This is your stop, Bondage. It’s time to get off of this train and jump onto another……… Maybe what you need to do is just stay in your hotel room. Have yourself a couple of ménage y’ trois, and call it a night. Maybe you can keep yourself entertained at home while I go on to win the IC title and I go on to establish myself as a top notch competitor here in the UWF. Granted, I already have, but what a great way to cement that place in history…….. You have gained yourself a great opportunity Bondage. I mean first series of matches out of the box are in the IC title tournament. I don’t exactly understand how that works, but I guess whatever works. I could see if you were an established star how you could be placed into the tournament, but you are just a Johnny-come-lately. You haven’t even worked your ways up the ranks of this federation. You should be down on your hands and knees thanking Joshua for putting you into this tournament. You should be thanking him for ever and ever until the end of time…… But you, you’re quiet. You have not said a thing. Hell, since you’ve entered the fed we’ve heard you actually speak twice. One was at the PPV, and the other was in a RP before your first match. That’s it. Do you really expect to make if far like that? I think your two women there have gotten to you. Your two women seem to have taken control over your life and keep you where you are. They must have lost the keys to the hand cuffs or are just not willing to let you go. Who knows, women are like that sometimes. Can’t be explained….. (Kazee grabs the top rope from where he is and pulls himself up to a standing position. He climbs out of the ring, starts making his way up the ramp, and toward the exit. As he walks up the ramp, he grabs his duffel bag, reaches into it, pulls out his half of the tag team belts, and turns around to face the camera. He places the belt over his shoulder.) The first night you decided to make your appearance, it was with your little ladies coming out and leading myself and Monty away after our Tag Team Title victory. This being shortly after I had taken the “Total Anarchy” or whatever move that was. You decide to come and take advantage of us as we were in a weakened state…… Now, you again are coming out for a piece of one half of the Tag Team champions. This time, though, I am not in a weakened state. This time, your little sex slaves will not be able to lure me away so that you can have a nice free time in the ring. This time, you are going to find out what a true amateur wrestler can do. This time, you are going to be feeling the true wrath of a master of suplexes……. Kenny Mercury calls himself the best technician in the UWF. He may very well be. But what that means is something totally different from what I can do. He has taken a few moves, learned them extremely well, and knows how to put them on so that they maximize their damage. You Bondage are facing the most knowledgeable wrestler in the UWF. I have forgotten more moves than you ever knew. Hell, for that matter, I’ve forgotten more moves than you ever knew both in the ring and in the bedroom. Your repertoire, it’s fairly puny compared to my long list of moves, counter-moves, and counter-counter-moves. Are you ready to see and experience things that you have never seen or felt before? I hope so……. It’s time to face the facts Bondage. It’s time to realize what you are up against and that there is nothing that you can do. It’s time to face the facts that you’ve run into the Players Club. You’ve run into the club that specializes in not only our expertise in the ring, but also specializes in stealing women’s hearts and breaking them. Maybe by the end of the night I’ll be walking away not only one step closer to the IC title, but a couple of women to party with, or at least for the rest of the Players Club to party with, since we all share……. So in the end, Bondage, your dreams, your hopes, your goals, plop, all down the toilet. You’ve run into a buzz saw. You’ve run into someone who is on top of his game and is going to be very hard to take down. You’ve run into someone who is going all the way in the tournament and is going to win the titles. And if you don’t like that, I’ve got two words for you, but I’m sure you’d do that already….. Good Luck, Bondage, you’ll need it. (Kazee walks to through the back area and out of view of the camera as the scene fades to black.)
