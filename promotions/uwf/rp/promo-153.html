UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #153

Conflict of interest

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I never thought it would happen....... Yes, definitely a sentence that applies to the six man tag at this week's edition of Sunday Afternoon Supremacy as Adien pryde is supposed to tag with The Dutch destroyer Nick Persia. But completing the team is Tsunami Oni! The legend! The former World Champion! But also the guy defeated by Adien Pryde. Going into this match, Nick Persia has no idea what his role in this match is supposed to be. Sure, you can say he should be on his own side. But there's a conflict of interest in this match. he could help out Mike Montgomery, but that would mean he had to suffer a loss. He could play his role in his team, but that would mean that he breaks his promise. Either way, somebody is not going to be hapy. Like it's supposed to be. Nick Persia knows what he has to do. Nick Persia knows he has a few though battles to fight. he's very well aware of the fact what's happening. Not only here, but also at home. His eyes are always on the look out. So he won't be caught by a nasty suprise. Because he leaves nothing to fate. Sure, somebody can win the first battle, but he always wins the war. Ask Corporal Punishment. Ask Adien Pryde. And after SAS, ask Communist. Don't be suprised by hearing that name. You see, it was him costing Nick the THC title. It was him finally ending the longest THc reign. It was him ending the Persia domination in the hardcore division. And now, after a very long two months, he has the chance to get his well deserved revenge. It might sounds old fashioned to some people, but it's the truth. The truth hurts. Nick Persia found that out first hand when Communist took that title away from him right under his nose. Nick felt humiliated. Nick felt robbed. And those feelings made it personal to him. Those feelings made him feel like he was the biggest loser on the earth. It was Communist that was the cause of all problems. In his mind, at least. Nonetheless, Nick will be looking for him. If he can, he will injure him. Nick's pain will become his. Only then Communist will realise what the THC meant to Nick. And there's no other way to fully realise it. But Nick also has to face Plague. And in Plague's midn Nick is the cause of all his problems. Nick has damaged his legendarious reputation and got the wrath of Plague upon him. Plague has said on more than one occasion he wanted revenge. And now he has his chance. As it looks right now, Nick has a nice little target on his head. But Plague has a nice target on his head as well, because Oni has delivered him a defeat also! To make things even more complicated, Montgomery wants Oni bad. So, all in all, everybody wants everybody sounds like the only right conclusion. But there can be only one winner, and I know it's going be Nick Persia. Face the facts and look what he has done. He destroyed legends like Almasy and The Plague. he has fought battles with Motgomery and Dunn. He has been the man giving Sean McClancy so much injuries he had to retire. The word will be spreading around! I mean, on a nice evening like this one, somebody must notice it's always Nick Persia injuring people. You'd almost swear he's UWF's own crippler. ___________ [Larson approaches Nick Persia at the UWF arena. As it appears, he's looking for an interview.] Larson: 'Nick, I'll make this hort. Two words: The Communist' Nick Persia grins, but no humor could be found in it. Nick Persia: 'Three words: Rest in Peace.' Larson: 'That's a clear statement. But what's also clear is that you can make a lot of enemies in one match.' Nick Persia: 'that's your point of view. I'll say this just once.......you're either with me or against me. I think that says enough.' Larson: 'What about Mongomery then? he's not against you.' Nick Persia: 'Obviously he's not with me.' Larson: 'So he's against you?' Nick Persia: 'Your words Rick.' Larson: 'Is something wrong with you? You seem so quiet. Normally you always have lots to say.' Nick Persia: 'I know when it's time to keep my mouth shut.' Nick Persia walks away without saying goodbye. (OOC: Sorry for the abrupt ending.)
