I Want to Ride My Bicycle

24 Sep 2004

Dicky Flint — some road in the middle of....NOWHERE!!!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[the scene begins with dicky riding down the street on his bicycle with his boombox in the basket infront of the handles, coming out of the speakrers is queen's bicycle race song is playing......loud, because honestly, why play music if your gonna play it soft?!?!?! anyways...] Dicky singing along... 'you say black, i say WHITE, you say bark, i say BITE....' dicky:man, i feel so insanely awesome right now, i mean granted i completely didnt show against kenny but hey, the guys completely insane anyways, it was probably for the best i didnt show, hes crazy, like not the good crazy either.....is there a good crazy? oh well, now i just have to concentrait on.... [just as dicky was about to speak his name, fizbane the fabulous comes rolling down the street in his disco-tastic car pumping who knows what god-aweful disco shit, dicky almost choked from the shock, why the hell was fizbane on the little gravel road down south blaring his disco junk, heh oh well, what happens happens.] dicky:cough ugh choke, what?!?!?! -fizbane came right up next to dicky and rolled down his window, dicky was like wtf and without thinking twice about it he full on punched him in the face!! fizbane didnt even get to say some stupid disco lingo. dicky was like- 'shit shit im ridiculous, why did i do that, fightin time is for sunday not now, eeek' -so dicky like did a full on 180 on his bike and peddled away as fast as his negro-kickin feet could go, oh yeah and then to end matters emanuel ran up and totally bit two of the tires off fizbanes car, hehe, which was completely ingenius because you only carry one spare tire......and he bit two of them!!!! muahahha!!- dicky to himself:hehe, dude im so bad ass, haha right in the face, instinct man, instinct. -thats all folks-
