UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #526

Connection

7 Jul 2004

Nick Persia — Somewhere

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Persia is for real. He has one goal in mind. Well, more to be honest, but they don’t matter much at the moment. So we keep focussed on that what matters in the here and now. Because Nick Persia wants to be for Real. Interesting choice of words. Keyword of this match is Real. What is real? How would you define real? Hard to say.] [Seymour Almasy. Opponent number one. The legend. The Hall of Famer. The man with the biggest ego of all. When you are a self proclaimed hero you have ruined it for me. You never EVER say you are a hero. The people decide if you truly are a hero. If the people say you are a hero then you are a hero. Not like you, Seymour. Unlike you I actually earn all my nicknames. Think about it. I am called The Voice because I always speak the truth and I always say what pops in my mind.] [The Real Deal. What do we actually know about you? Not much I can say. You talk about being the real deal, but my question to you is: What is real? Can you tell me? Probably not. Like all the other guys you run your mind before thinking. I, on the other hand, think before I talk. I think before I act. Therefore is every move I make so succesfull. Every word I say is spoken with a purpose. Whether it’s warning you, scaring you off or getting your a$$ back in REALity it serves its purpose. That’s where you and me are different. You won your title really quick. You are making a name for yourself in a REALly short amount of time. But I kept my title defense after defense.] [I did what nobody thought I could do: I beat the odds. I won THC from a legend in The Plague. I retained the title against the likes of Adien Pryde. I broke the record of longest reigning champion. With that in mind we should question ourselves how long the reign will last. Especially now so many people are after him, and more importantly, his title.] [Scene fades in and we see Nick Persia. He is in his in ring gear and he is wearing his new T Shirt which is now for sale at UWFonline.com.] Nick Persia: “What’s the resemblance of me, The Real Deal and Seymour Almasy?” He is quiet for a moment. Nobody says anything. Nick Persia: “Doesn’t matter. You weren’t prepared for that question. Life is something that is unpredictable and you will always need to be ready when some kind of creepy monster comes down and tries to, shall we say, kill you. You need to be prepared. You need to be at the top of your game.” Nick Persia starts to laugh. A laughter he apparently can not suppress. Nick Persia: “Game......Seymour likes to play games. The Final Fantasy likes to play games. I don’t need to ask myself the question what game. Seymour, please tell me who is your biggest opponent in Final Fantasy?” Voice: “Sin.........” Nick Persia: “That’s right: Sin. You never saw him. You never saw him coming. He controls the seas. He looks upon you from the water. He destroyes all ships that enter the water. He drowns innocent children. And he is not a bad man. Really he isn’t.” Sin: “Correctemundo.” Nick Persia: “Because dying is better than the alternative. Who wants to live in the same world as a man who is confused? A man who is stuck in two worlds. His mind and the real world. The world I live in. The world in which I became a legend.” Sin: “Don’t get ahead of yourself. Cause you don’t know in what world Seymour is the legend. Both perhaps.” Nick Persia: “Matter of time before memory fails Seymour. People will slowly start to forget him. Each second that passes signs a loss of memory of Seymour Almasy after I am done with him. I will prove my worth. People still think he is going to win, but my reign was a 100 times larger than his reign. My legacy will become a hundred time bigger than his. I truly am immortalised.” Sin: “Yes.....you will. You will live on in memory and you will always remain.......legendary! And with my help we shall end the dreams of Seymour.” Nick Persia: “His dream of being the Final Fantasy. The only thing what is final in our match is that this will be his Final Destination. Or am I confusing one of his brain childs here? Doesn’t matter though! I shall lay down a fury in him and The Real Deal like its never seen before. Be Prepared.” Sin: “Don’t underestimate the new guy.......Real deal could cause some inconvenience in this match. Maybe he will benefit from the assualt you will deliver on Seymour.” Nick Persia: “Real Deal had better start praying. the almighty lord will try and be forgiving on his soul but it’ll do no good...as a rip the flesh from his body...tearing and ripping through him...I am like an animal...cutting through its prey...and I am the predator...ready to bring down the axe of damnation...imagine it...Seymour Almasy’s head bouncing from pillar to pillar...brains inside getting scrambled...I am going to bring his blood to the surface and smear it all over the ring...a ritual beating...sacrificed to darkness...Simmons I am going to punish you...more severely then you have every been before...The Real Deal probably my biggest challenge in this match...but like all the ones before him...I will destroy...it is as easy as one...bang...two...bang...three...BANG!!!” Sin: “It’s not going to be easy....but I will help you with my powers. I shall give you powers like if you are a god yourself.” Suddenly A hissing sound like air escaping from a sealed container was heard next to Nick, a hatchway opened and inside Nick Persia saw a sledgehammer. Sin: “Do you see? The Magic Sledgehammer is my gift to you........” Nick Persia: “Never heard of it. I never heard of a MAGIC sledgehammer. But I believe you. I shall use the sledgehammer with my bare hand. I will smash their heads. I shall make them bleed. That blood that flows out of their veigns shall be the price I won after days of hard work and intense training. I come fully equipped, do you?” Nick Persia: “The desire to succeed and destroy...death to Seymour...death to them all...Seymour your blood will flow like wine...all those fans eyes watching on in total shock horror as I drink your blood from the cup...all those eyes watching and waiting...watching and waiting for the perfect moment...and as the clock handles strike the magic moment..Seymour will be the lamb to the slaughter...I shall drink from him...I am going to promise you that, right here and now!!” Voice: “Excellent...the public will vomiting in their seats as you lay into those poor unfortunate souls...at Sday Afternoon Supremacy...Nick Persia will be re-born.” Nick Persia: “Last time I spoke about ants. Now I am doing a masterpiece of work. And when I look in my oponents eyes I see fear. The same fear that ripped my heart. I felt a sudden rush of panic when I first wrestled a bigger opponent. But now I am more experienced. Those days are behind me. You see, I am a fast learner and you will notice that. I say adios now, see you there...........for the last time ever.” Voice: “Seymour........may your soul Rest In Peace.” [Secene fades out. And it was really Sin’s voice. I promise.]
