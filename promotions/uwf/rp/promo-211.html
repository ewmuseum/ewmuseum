UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #211

A Week at Lungie's V

9 Feb 2004

Iron Lung — Road, Villa Roma

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Week at Lungie's V A Week at Lungie's V: The Final Destination Starring Iron Lung Co-starring Damion Patrignelli Previously on A Week at Lungie's Robert Pendragon took us on another trip to Stop & Shop, this time stopping into the installed Dunkin' Donuts station for an early-risers breakfast. His day once again surprised us, as it contained little to no action. And on the last seconds of the fourth episode of this work week-long series, we gradually faded out with a chisled look on our faces. Perhaps we were all confused to the facts that were being layed out before us, perhaps we were shocked that this could in fact be the only things Pendragon did all day. It was mind altering and drove us insane, thinking that he was playing games with us because he knew that we had our eyes on him the whole time. His private life was dealt with within the eyes of the general public. Everyone knew him by name when he walked into their stores, and when he sat at a restaurant the waitress would ask if he was having 'the usual'. Everybody was friendly to him, and he returned the favor. But, we weren't being so friendly now. We wanted answers, and we wanted them now. Was this some sort of joke? Was he just taking us on some insane journey to nowhere, and when the production crew went away he carried about his business? There had to be another side to the Pendragon true story, and we were going to find out what that other side was. And now, we present part five of the A Week at Lungie's series, The Final Destination. The camera expands on a scene already in progress, with the tires of a car slowly rolling along on the pavement below. Moving up a little at a time, we recognize the midnight blue color of this newer model, but we can't quite put our finger on who it might belong to until the camera pulls up more and we get the reflection of the side mirror. Inside of the car we see the trademark Oakley sunglasses and backwards hat of Robert Pendragon. He drives with an innocent grin on his face as the camera pans the interior of the car, the speedometer right around the speed limit and Pendragon's cell phone resting in the drink holding compartment next to him. Crusing along with a rap beat coming from the speakers, Pendragon taps the palm of his hand lightly on the steering wheel. Taking a straight road, we pass many glorious houses and also a public beach. Nobody is at the beach since the winter weather is still around, but the blue waters are enough to tempt anybody to try and take a dip. Going past a busy corner store, we slow down and stop at a red light. A slight sigh comes from our subject's voicebox, but he keeps tapping his wrist slowly and awaits the light to change green. When it does, Pendragon checks both ways to make sure that nobody ran the light on the other end. After that, he continued foreword. But little did he know that not so far away from his course, laid any drivers worst nightmare. A traffic jam... The tooting, the tapping of breaks, the swearing, and the pure ugliness were all swallowing Pendragon and his Saturn. He was stuck in the right lane with nowhere to go, and time was slowly passing as the breaklights could be seen for the next couple of blocks. How would Pendragon figure his way out of this? The radio kept bumping and blaring as Rob tried to figure out a way, any way at all, to get out of this mess. He tried to think of another route to get back to the Villa Roma, and then, the idea came to him. He could sneak around the car in front of him somehow, and then he'd be able take a shortcut through the 'ghetto' part of town to get back to his hotel room. Pendragon looks infront of him, but his front bumper has been forced too close to the car in front of him. There wasn't any room to make the maneuver. He had to sit and wait, and it would prove to be frustrating. Even with semi-relaxing music on, it would be a long wait. A half hour went by at a crawling pace, until finally the road opened up for our hero. He sped the rest of the way home, this was perhaps the most exciting moment of all the other trips that we had taken with this young superstar. The rush of adrenaline was with us as Pendragon almost blew through multiple red lights, just going through the amber tint as it flickered into crimson. Now we were on the street that held the Villa Roma. The sound of the radio seemed to fade away into the distance slowly, leaving us with silence and whispers as Pendragon turned into the parking garage. Finding his particular parking spot at the end of the row, he pulls into it and turns his car off, sitting inside of it for a few moments and sighing with relief. He was finally home, and he could finally catch some shut eye. Or so he thought.. For when he headed into the elevator after getting into his complex, he didn't know what was going to be waiting for him once he entered his room. It started as soon as the elevator doors opened on the seventh floor, and as soon as Pendragon stepped onto the Persian rug installed in the artistic hallway. Gracefully pacing his way to the door, Pendragon gathers his key card out of his left pants pocket. Sliding the card into the key slot while turning the knob, the door opens in front of him. Entering the room and closing the door behind him after getting his key card back into the pocket, he carefully walks in the daylight-filled room. Pendragon seeks his Lazyboy sofa, and he waddles his way over to it, taking his shoes off and lying down on it. He pulls a pillow underneath his skull and is just about to shut his eyes, when he hears a familiar tune coming from his coffee table. His cell phone was ringing. Stretching his hand over to reach for it, Robert looks at the caller identification that is built into the phone. He doesn't recognize the number, so he puts the phone back onto the table. Putting his body back down to rest, Pendragon sighs a little as his head hits the pillow once more. Again, as he is just about to shut his eyes, the tune disturbs his partial slumber. Looking at the number again and realizing that it is the same as the last caller, Pendragon puts the reciever up to his ear and accepts the call. What he would hear next would be a very familiar voice, a voice that he hadn't heard since he became a supstar in the UWF roster. Yah.. Well, well, well. If it isn't Robert Pendragon. Long time no talk my friend. Who is this Come on man, you don't remember me? I'm calling from a pay phone man. Damion? Yah man, it's me. Haven't heard from you in a while, how are you holding up? I'm doing all right. Is this a bad time? You sound exhausted. It's been a long week. You stressed out? You sound like somebody beat you up. More like three people beat me up. (Jokingly) Aw man, not another bar room brawl. (Chuckling) I wish. But it was worth it in the end anyway. Oh yah? What fame and glory is it going to be for this time? UWF's the Hardcore Championship. I won a Battle Royal to become the Number One Contender for it. UWF? You're finally moving up in the wrestling world huh? That's a high class place. Yah, too bad I had to go against lower-classed fighters in my first couple of matches. I got a match in the upcoming pay-per-view now by winning the rumble. I gotta get some training done, and that's why I've been wanting to get at least four hours of sleep in. It's been a really stressful week dude. Ah, the good old interviews? Nah, not even that. It's just that, I feel like somebody's been watching me or something. I feel physically and emotionally exhausted, looking over my shoulder every two seconds you know. It's like the eyes of the world are on me or something. Well, you are a pretty popular guy. That's not what I mean -- I know man, I'm just pulling your leg... So, who's your opponent? Ben Jericho. I know nothing about him, like usual. All I know is that he's tough, and he's looking to end my undefeated streak. Undefeated huh? Impressive... Well, good luck to him. Because once you get on a roll, forget about it. And as for you, I guess I'll let you get some shuteye. You deserve it if you feel that way. It's good to hear from you again Damion. And it's always a pleasure to hear from you. Take care Rob. You too Damion, you too... Punching the key to stop the conversation, Pendragon turns the power off his cell phone and rests it back on the coffee table. He was finally able to lie back down on the sofa. His head hit the pillow with ease, and he was able to shut his eyes this time and drift away into dreamland. But little did he know what dreams, or perhaps even nightmares, awaited him once he was there... The end of A Week at Lungie's V, but be sure to catch Iron Lung in his next series, The Quest for THC .
