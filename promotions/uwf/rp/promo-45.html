Celebration goes Sour Part 3

6 Oct 2003

Digital Mortality — Digital's beach house in San Juan, Puerto Rico

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(I am in a loss of words. I haven't felt so sick as I am feeling right now. Joshua Dunn had the courage, the audacity to pick Digital Mortality's girl out of every single one at that club and get jiggy with her? It is sickening. She is a slut and you Dunn are a bastard. You say Digital left you this and that, but you left him to rot, you betrayed him and you have just shattered his dreams, you have just taken something from the man's life. It wasn't the title, because everyone knows you would never be able to take the title away, it was something more sacred. You are a pig Dunn, you are a disgusting human being, if you could call yourself even that. You have gone so low that you can't be dug up. I really hope you get run over by a truck, or maybe, just maybe, the truck is already on its way.) (White Wolf has been saying things on television that he shouldn't be saying. We all know that he has no chance in hell of winning this title. The NA Title will be in Digital's waist, just like the IC title. There is no way around it. He will be the first that has both the IC title and the NA in the UWF. Nobody will be able to stop him, not Dunn, not White Wolf, not even Ezra Kazee or Mentok. Even though Mentok is quite entertaining, but that is not the point! Digital Mortality will take out everything that is bothering him and destroy Joshua Dunn and WHite Wolf. They are nothing against a mad Digital Mortality.) (The world will be surprised as Digital Mortality runs over his competition, giving everyone no chance to win. You will all see the wrath of the Digital One, and it won't be very pretty.) [The scene opens up in Digital Mortality's house in San Juan, Puerto Rico. He is sitting in his couch with a beer in one hand and dark hollow eyes. The sliding door is open and there is heavy wind. His long red hair is not tied up, and it is just waving around with the wind. His face is grim and sitting right next to him is Burton Bell.] [They are both wearing their usual attire, Digital his black cut-off jeans and black shirt. Burton has black jeans and black shirt. His long black hair tied in a ponytail. He is looking at the floor and there is not much happening. You can feel the darkness in this room where yesterday it was filled with joy.] [Digital motions to the controller but Burton quickly grabs it away from him. Digital has it in his hands, but Burton doesn't want him to use it.] BB: No man! You have seen that like 100 times! [Digital's eyes glow red with anger and pain at the same time. He grabs the controller without saying a word and the video starts to play. It is Joshua Dunn's promotion where he introduces Justine as his new girl. Digital at the sight of Justine closes his eyes and clenches his fists.] DM: Why? BB: Aww man... not again. DM: How could she do this to me? [Burton stretches back and rolls his eyes.] BB: Snap out of it! You need to focus on your next match, this won't be a walk in the park. This is going to be a very difficult match, you need to forget about this, this is what Joshua wants, he wants you out of your game so he can take advantage of it. You need to wake up! You need to forget her, she has brought nothing but trouble to you. Why can't you accept it? [Digital gets on his feet and points directly at Burton.] DM: You have no idea how I feel. How can you say she has been nothing but trouble? She is the love of my life. BB: Was is what you meant. DM: Damn it Burt! Can you help me here? BB: I am trying! But you are blind! You cannot see the fact that she doesn't want you anymore, that she wants Dunn, your bitter enemy! DM: He has tricked her! I know it! He might have used the NVA brainwashing powder! BB: Rumors, bloody rumors! We do not know if they are true! DM: They have to be! I remember that the NVA gave us some of that powder, I think I have some of mine too! BB: They have to? Or do you hope they are? [Digital lowers his head and drops down on the couch.] DM: It is useless. I have nothing else to say... leave now! [The cameraman heads to the door.] BB: Wait! There is something here from White Wolf. [The cameraman comes back as Digital Mortality and Burton focus on the big television. As the promotion is finalising, Digital lets out a laugh.] DM: Ha! What is this guy? A joke? I have many things on my mind, and at the bottom is White Wolf. You actually believe you can beat me. I am sorry to burst your bubble, but it's not going to happen. Not now, and not ever. And yes, you are facing a Hall of Famer, so you better get on form and shut your mouth. Do you realize how many fools like you have come trhough this here federation? A lot, and they all disappear after a match with Digital Mortality. They cannot take the pressure. BB: This foolhas no idea what he is getting himself into, this should have been a one on one match between you and Dunn. Guess they just love to milk their wrestlers dry! DM: What they would do for a buck or two. BB: There you are mistaken my friend, it is not just a couple of bucks, it is big ass bucks. They will try and put you two together to fight in every single way possible. You two are drawing audiences. You are making the show. That is why they put you against Dunn in three consecutive matches, they want you two to kill each other. DM: Then that is what I will do. I will beat Dunn senseless. I will defeat him just like I did last time, and if White Wolf gets in the way, he will get a beating too. I am tired of people not giving me the respect that I deserve. BB: That's the spirit. Forget about her, there are plenty women in the world. I am sure you will find someone else. DM: Justine, you left a hole in my heart that will probably never be filled. I fought for you, I gave you blood, sweat and tears, and you go over to my enemy and become his girl. That is probably the biggest act of backstabbing I could have ever experienced. You are nothing to me. BB: White Wolf, be careful what you wish for. You are talking too much trash about Digital and what you are asking is for a whoopin. I am sure Digital will gladly give it to you. DM: I hate you Dunn, and I especially hate you Justine. Ruff was right after all, you are nothing but a whore. Never thought I'd agree with him on that. And don't even try to come out to ringside to distract me, because I will hit you hard if you do. Everytime I see your face, I see Dunn, so I will throw my hardest punches. Until then... see you in the ring where you will get... DIGITALIZED! [Scene fades]
