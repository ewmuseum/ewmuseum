SAS Rp

July 18th (year unrecorded)

'The Shocker' Dave Storm — SAS Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(( The scene opens to Dave Storm running through the UWF Sunday Afternoon Supremecey Arena parkinglot. He has his black gym bag harrnessed around his shoulder and has a 'pissed off' look on his face. He is walking into the building when Jon Focker runs toward him and greets him in the lobby with a microphone in hand.) 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'What the hell do you want?' Jon Focker:: 'Just a quick word, Mr. Storm.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Why? Your the damn reason I lost Wednesday night, you Focker.' Jon Focker:: 'Uhh, yeah, sorry about that?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: ' You should be. I was THIS CLOSE. But all because of you, Focker, my feet touched the ground RIGHT BEFORE that damn Rollins guy's. I shouldv'e won that damn match. And now, that TV title is another event away from me. I should be preparing for my Televsion title match, but I'm not. I have another obstacle to overcome, and her name is Fizbag the Faggotous.' Jon Focker:: 'You mean Fizzbane the Fabulous, and it's a he.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'I don't care. All I know is I survived the Battle Royal longer then he did, and I shouldn't even have to deal with him. But because of the damn replay, I do.' Jon Focker:: 'What are your thoughts on Fizzbane, your oponent tonight?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'I just told you, goddammit. But since your a def little Focker, I'll tell you again. He got his name from a book and think's he's a white pimp from thirty years ago. How original. But none of that matters when your in the ring. And I'm going to do my job and call it a night, with the number one contendership to the Television title in my ownership. Fizzy and little girl groupies may think different, but they're wrong. I mean, They smoked a lot of pot in the seventies.' Jon Focker:: 'What do you have to say about the two men you could potentially face if you beat Fizzbane the Fabulous, Jay Vyper and the winner of the Battle Royal on Underground, 'The Renegade' Richard Rollins?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'What did I tell you about saying IF when it comes to my winnng?' Jon Focker:: 'I know, I know. Will you please just answer the question?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Ahh, sure. Quite frankly, my opinion hasn't changed about Jay Vyper. So what if he has one more defense under his belt, he's still not up to my level. And the only reason Dick Rollins beat me was because of dumb luck. I had that match in the damn bag, but the backstage reviewer or whoever the hell gave the decision smoked too much pot that day, and now I'm stuck having to beat the Fizzbag. All that partying that batard does is gonna make back and bite him in his fat ass once I step inside the ring later today. Mark my words, Focker, for they will prove to be the truth. Fizzbag can't even remember what he's doing without his sisters-slash-girlfriends telling him what to do. It's nothing personnal, its just BUSINESS!' (With that, 'The Shocker' jolts away as the scene fades.)
