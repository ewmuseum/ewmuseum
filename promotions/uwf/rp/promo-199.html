A Week at Lungie's III

Iron Lung — Stop & Shop

A Week at Lungie's III A Week at Lungie's III: Cash Returns Starring Iron Lung Co-starring Frank the deposit station worker Previously on A Week at Lungie's Iron Lung was in his apartment gearing up for another fabulous day. The town of Norwalk didn't know his business for this morning, for he had kept his personal life a safe-guarded secret for his whole wrestling career. That was, until the cameras came rolling in. Now ambushed and very open to any media attack, Lung quickly thought of a master plan. What could he do to make us believe that he was just an average guy? When the idea came to his head after much thought, it was off to the parking garage. Driving us around the town, Lung kept a whistle in his lips, not letting us lead onto any clues on the possible goings of this early rise. But, we eventually find out that Lung has indeed set up a date with the local Stop & Shop. The big adventure of the day: bringing back glass, plastic, and metal for a deposit. We now return to A Week at Lungie's, with the third installment, Cash Returns The clanging of glass bottles can be heard as the camera begins to gain its focus, finally lighting the full extent of its power onto the oversized black garbage bag being dragged across the paved walkway. A hand with a tightly secured fist held on to the bag, almost as if it were a large sack of toys to be delivered to needy children. When the camera rolls along the body we notice Lung's trademark multiple zippers and pockets on his jeans, even if they were blue jeans instead of his wrestling black. The camera keeps going up and indeed it is our lonesome hero, and he has a slight grimace on his face as he lugs the bag to the deposit center's automatic doors. The doors open before him, and we see two machines for each variety of recycling. Dragging the bag inside the center, Pendragon wipes some sweat off his forehead and sighs in relief. A man in the corner of the center sits at a small desk, the kind of desk you would get at school. On it he had a cordless telephone and a small electric cash register. He perked a toothless grin toward Pendragon's way, but Rob kept his face stiff as a rock while he sorted through the bag. First he took out the cans of YJ Stinger, Starbucks Double Shot Cappuccino, Sobe Energy, and Vanilla Coke that he had been drinking for the past month. Putting the cans in one at a time and waiting for the machine to recognize the barcode, once again Pendragon had a song that he was whistling. The cashier in the corner bobbed his head along to the tune, and again managed to sneak in a gum-filled smile. You got lots of 'dem dere cans. Yah, it was a long weekend. He sounded as if he had just come to the United States. His English was butchered, as we would soon find out. None of the less, Pendragon respected the man and turned a listening ear, while keeping his face directed toward the cans deposit machine. I 'member one time I tried to drank dis' hooge can of Beck's and smack de can on mi forehead. Didn't work out sowell. You must have needed an extra Advil that night. Noffing a cup ov herbal tea cun't cure mi frend. Ah, herbal tea. Perking an eyebrow and trying to hold back his laughter, Pendragon pushed the receipt button after he was through depositing the cans. It came out to be $1.45 in deposits, and he still had plastic bottles and glass bottles to put through the machines. Thrusting the lighter bag over to the plastic returns, Rob searches through the mess of carbonated beverages to find the bottles. Putting them in one at a time, once again he waits for the machine to read the barcodes. And once more, there weren't any difficulties. The cashier kept his eyes on Pendragon as he kept emptying his bag of plastic returns. You'z gonna be a rich mon after 'dis is ova wif. I wish, but it'll probably only be a couple of dollars. A coopel ov dollars cun do et for ya. Nex' thang ya know, ya' win dat Powerball. I'm not much of a gambling man. I think it's a waste of money. I'm just sayin' mon, ya neva know 'til it's in ya hands. Turning his head back to the machine after another smile from the cashier, Pendragon deposits the rest of the plastic bottles and wipes some more sweat from his forehead after pushing the receipt button, getting another $1.55 from them. Now having three dollars in receipts, Rob goes and kicks the almost empty bag towards the glass bottle machine. I see ya almost dun dere. Yep, just have a couple glass bottles to put away. When ya dun, bring ya slips to mi. Can do chief, thanks for all your help. Any tyme mi frend. Filling the machine with the rest of his bag, he prints out a final receipt of $1. He had four dollars in receipts, and a slight grin came over his face as he approached the cashier. The cashier smiled back and took the receipts, looking them over and using the register to add up to the total. Ah, four dollas. I told ya you'd be a rich mon. Yeah, looks like you were right. Ya don't spend dat all in one place now, ya hear? I'll try not too, have a good day. Da same to ya, mi frend. Rob his empty bag and puts the four dollars in his pocket, offering a final wave to the cashier. The cashier waves Pendragon on his way as he exits the automatic doors with a smile on his face. The cameras would keep rolling while he made his way to his car, putting the bag in the passenger seat and starting the engine. Where would our next adventure lead? End of a A Week @ Lungie's III: Cash Returns, but be sure to catch Iron Lung in A Week @ Lungie's IV: Chai's & Coffee
