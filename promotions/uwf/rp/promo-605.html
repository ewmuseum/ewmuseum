UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #605

Beat people up?

7 Aug 2004

Mr. Meaner — Warehouse

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens up inside of an open area. This are is a very empty warehouse. There is a single solitary light on, and standing directly underneath the light is Mr. Meaner. He is dressed in all black, big surprise there. Standing on just the outside of the light are several people. They are all ready to pounce. Mr. Meaner is holding onto his lead pipe.) Mr. Meaner; So you like to beat people up, do you Communist? That’s all that you seem to be doing all over the place anyway. I mean I know that you can handle random people, but that really isn’t that big of a deal. Who really cares. So you just beat a few punks out there who wouldn’t know what it means to fight if there lives depended on it, so what’s your point…… And Sacrifice is just standing around doing nothing. Probably hurting from that power bomb and the other beating he got the past two cards. He’s at home licking his wounds and crying and crying, because he is after all a pansy-waist…… We have two people here who are complete nothings, one who pretends to be a champion, and the other who pretends to be a member of the UWF. They are both pathetic bottom feeders through and through. We know that neither of the losers could even hold a candle to me, let alone shine my shoes……. I think Sacrifice is hiding under a rock somewhere. He hasn’t been heard from. He hasn’t been seen. He hasn’t been anywhere. I d love to see Hayes and Preest actually do his job and fire his ass. But I guess it doesn’t really matter. After all, he does have a place in this federation. He is the Jobber to the Stars. And that’s all he’ll ever be. And Communist, wow, you are definitely losing it aren’t you. First your at the gold course rubbing elbows with the rich and the well to do and those who don’t know what work is. And you hit an old man with a sledge hammer. While I must admit that it was a great thing and something that I would’ve done myself, it is sad and pathetic. I mean we all know that’s all that you could ever beat up…… And then your going to church. Wow. Just wow. Our hardcore champion is praising god. Everyone knows that religion is a crutch for weak willed and weak minded individuals, so you going there just proves that you are nothing and never will be anything. Maybe you should go back to the synagogue and worship some more. Maybe your god will save your title…… But now this matter of beating people up. You know, I can do the same thing. And I don’t even have to do it behind people’s back or to old men. Hell, here are a bunch of guys that I recruited from the local martial arts school…… (The camera pans around to each of the different men. They are all wearing their different suits and such, but there is not one person bellow a red belt. They have a variety of weapons and are all in very good shape.) So what the hell, if your going to beat some people up, then so am I, because I can. The only difference, these people agreed to fight me. Yours were just random people you decided to assault. So let’s rock on this one, shall we? (The first of the men charges at Meaner. He takes his stance. Meaner just tells him to bring it on. The man starts to attack at him with a couple of quick kicks. Mr. Meaner blocks them both and kicks the opposite knee out from under him, before dropping an elbow on him.) (The second guy rushes at him. This one has a little more piss and vinegar about him. He rushes at Meaner with rights and lefts. Meaner blocks them. He goes for a high kick, which Meaner is able to catch up on his shoulder. Meaner positions his lead pipe and drops down with the pipe over the guys legs, hyper extending the knee and sending the man down.) (The third guy rushes at Mr. Meaner. Mr. Meaner just side steps this guy and clotheslines him with his lead pipe. The fourth guy comes at Mr. Meaner. Meaner side steps him, bends him over backwards, and smashes the lead pipe down across his chest, sending him crashing to the mat. A fifth guy rushes out only to be met with a right straight to the face, and a sixth guy gets flipped for his rush.) Mr. Meaner: Well, that was fun. Not a whole lot of effort though. You guys really sure you want to mess with me though? You guys sure you don’t want to just hand over THC Championship to me. It’ll save you all a lot of trouble. Just think about what you are getting into her. Do you really want to be a part of it?….. This is going to be lead pipes. This is going to be chairs. This is going to be tables. This is going to be ladders. This is going to be everything under the sun. It isn’t, however going to be golf balls. It isn’t going to be alters. It isn’t going to be gods and crosses. This is an all out fight. And that’s all this match will be. Bring your gods, bring your crosses, bring your alters, and see how they stand up against my lead pipe and every other weapon I will have at my disposal…… I am going to win this match. I am going to be THC Champion. And I am going to send someone somewhere they don’t want to be. You have all been warned. (The scene fades to black.)
