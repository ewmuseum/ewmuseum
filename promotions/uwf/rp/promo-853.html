Duke Williams — Cook County Jail

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last time we left our whiskey drinking outlaw retiree he was on top of his world. He just won a giant TV the eye of his desire from the local radio station after catching a TV ad or at least he though he had. You see when the Malice Man Duke Williams showed up to pick up his winnings he instead won a free trip to the gray bar hotel and a huge fine. The feed that the contest was ran on was a closed circuit, unfiltered ad, put on by the cable company. The only people who could have seen it were people illegally stealing cable. Duke happened to be one of those people stealing cable. The sting operation worked and now Duke is spending his holidays in the pookie and owes a fine of 50,000 putting his goals of a big screen TV on a major set back. Leaving Duke two options, write a book or wrestle. We all know how his book idea worked last time. Thank God Duke never called the UWF to cancel his appearance in the Contest Of Champions as that may be the Malice Man’s last resort. Faced with uncertainty and a craving for whiskey Duke sits in the communal cell with three other convicts. We have the burly and bush haired Duke, a black guy in his early 20’s with a big Afro who goes by the name of “Fro”, and a middle aged con-artist dressed up like Elvis. Duke: I have got to get a hair cut when I get out here. Fro: You’ve only been in here for two days and you get out tomorrow. Duke: Well I needed one before I got in hair and I just relived by bushy beard and long hair is out of control I need to go see my barber in Kansas City. Elvis: That’s a whole lot of miles to travel to get a hair cut man. Duke: You see I trust nobody with my hair except Big Al Larcher at Big Al’s Big Ass Hair Emporium of greater Kansas City. Elvis: Sounds like this cat can clip. Duke: Hoss you don’t even know. He is so committed to his work he had his dick tattooed like a barbers pole. Elvis: I ain’t never seen a barbers pole man. Duke: What do you live in a cave? A barbers pole you know a pole that’s red, white, and blue. Elvis: I never knew barbers were that patriotic. That’s great man brings a tear to the kings eye. Duke: Well the history is a lot more disgusting then that King. “The history of the barber pole is intertwined with the history of barbers and their bloodletting practices. Patients would grasp a rod or staff tightly so that their veins would show, and the barbers would cut open their arms and bleed them until they fainted. Later, when leech therapy became popular they allowed for more controlled bleeding, leeches were applied directly to the vein areas. After the procedure, the washed bandages were hung outside on a pole to dry, and to advertise the ghastly therapeutic specialties offered in the barbershop. Flapping in the wind, the long strips of bandages would twist around the pole in the spiral pattern we now associate with barbers.”** It wasn’t till these butchers of skin and hair moved to the USA that they added the blue. Fro: Man how do you know so much about barbers and shit? Duke: I’m retired and have stolen cable. You learn a lot from the history channel. Elvis: That’s some sick shit. Fro: One more question. How do you know this Big Al’s dick is tattooed like a barbers pole? The Malice cracks a smile as he looks over Fro with funny look. Duke: I screwed his wife who told me all about her husband and his tattooed tinker. Hey hoss I don’t swing that way so you can get that out of your head right now. Fro: I was just asking dawg. So King what are you in here for. Elvis: I conned some old bag out of a her life savings then fled. Duke: You’re a sick man. Elvis: Don’t criticize what you don’t understand, son. You never walked in that man’s shoes. The king don’t judge you ya dig. Duke: What are you in for Fro. Fro: I hacked into some stupid website and moved some funds and shit. Duke has a shocked look in his eyes as he looks over the baggy pants cell mate. Fro: Yo, don’t give me that racist shit! What you ain’t never heard of a nigger on a computer? Shit. You like in the stone ages Fred Flintstone. We be everywhere now man from your ghetto’s to Oprah. I may just run for president and paint that motherfucking white house black bitch. I got a masters degree in computer science what do you have? Duke: A fucking headache. Chill man I was just thinking how I was going to tell my racist cat this. It’s really going to burn his whiskers and nothing I love doing more then pissing him off. Fro: You have a racist cat. Elvis: I had a monkey that hated fish. I guess that similar? Fro: Whoa Whoa Whoa what kind of cat is it? Duke: A racist one. Fro: I understand that but what race of cat is he? Duke: A pug. Fro: I racist pug? I have got to stay off of the drugs this is surreal. Duke: Tell me about it I have to live with his little Hitler like ass. The three men sit in silence for about a half an hour. Duke closes his eyes and dreams of being Snoop Dog and owning women and a plasma TV. Then his dreams turn to a nightmare as his subconscious reminds him of his wrestling duties. Duke awakes in a cold sweat to Elvis singing in the corner as Fro stands pissing in the steal communal toilet just a few feet away. Nothing is stronger then a mans wish to be free except that wish for a big screen TV. An idea puts into the Malice Man’s head making him smile almost as warm as Ray Charles. Duke: Do any of you hosses watch wrestling? Elvis: I never miss it man. Duke: So you know who I am? Elvis: Does a cow have tits man? Yea your Duke Williams. The black guy shakes three times and zips up as he too recognizes the Malice Man. Fro: I knew I’ve seen your white ass before. All that extra hair and age really fooled me I just though you were some old Chicago hillbilly. Duke: So you guys watch the UFW? Elvis: Never miss it man. Fro: Yea once in a blue moon. Duke: Good then you guys can help me profile my opponents coming up in this big tournament which may be my answer to a big screen TV!! Fro: Big screen TV? Duke: Long story, lets get to work. First round I have some guy named Melcolm Kennedy. Fro: The only Melcolm I know is X and his ass isn’t about to wrestle anybody. The only Kennedy I know is in the same situation as X yo. Elvis: Yea, yea I know who you mean. He’s one of these young punks who thinks he knows how to fight because he learned some stupid submission wrestling. Duke: Oh submission wrestling is code for a guy with no personality and zero mic skills. The King of Con’s smiles as he nods at Duke’s comments. Elvis: You nailed it listening to this guy talk will make your remote want to submit to the public access channel. Fro: I got into a fight with one of those fucks who are supposed to know how to fight. One fist to his nose and the bitch went crying home to mama. Duke: You nailed it Fro these punks who think they know how to fight are nothing but mama’s boys on the inside. I didn’t survive in the wrestling business for as long as I have with the nickname of the Malice Man for nothing. Shit I’ve seen more action in and out of the ring then this kid has been alive. Little Melcolm Kennedy has about as much chance of beating me as the Bears making the playoff’s this year. Ok lets put him down for an easy Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound and move on. Elvis: Well who’s next. Duke scratches his head trying to remember the name of the two putzs he may have to deal with in the following round. Fro: Shit we all got 24 more hours in this shit hole take your time. Duke: Oh yea Larry Mercury and Alvin Almasy. Elvis: You mean Kenny Mercury and Seymore Almasy. Duke: Yes those are the ham and eggers in the next round. Don’t worry about Mercury we were supposed to fight last week but he wet his tights and didn’t have an extra pair so he pussed out of our match. Fro: What a little bitch: Duke: He’s the guy who thinks its still funny to just come at somebody making fun of them with cuss words. We’ll let me tell you this I will not sink down to that douche bag sniffing, condom licking, cock sucker who enjoys sipping on the sperm of a donkey while masturbating to images of Jimmy Kimmel on the internet. No I will not reach that level of cock loving Kenny and his stupid British accent. Not me. No way in hell. Elvis: The king admires your comment to excellence. Duke: Your right that’s what I’m on a comment to excellence and a big screen TV!! Fro: Big screen TV? Duke: Long story. Who’s next. Elvis: We still need to talk about Almasy he’s the real deal. Fro: You I heard of that brother! Duke scratches his head. Duke: He’s a black guy? Fro: No man can’t a black man call another dude brother with out him being black. Duke: I guess. Fro: The so mote it be. Elvis: He’s as white as a sheet man but he wins tournaments. He is like the god of the tournaments he takes names and kicks ass all the time. Duke: Ah fuck there goes my chance at the fucking TV. Fro: The what? Duke and Elvis: LONG STORY!!! Fro: Yo man you can’t have that defeatist attitude and expect to win. Duke: You don’t understand I’ve lost every fucking tournament I’ve ever been in. I’m like the God damn Buffalo Bills of the tournament world. Elvis: More like the Chicago Cubs at least the Bills make it to the last game. Duke: What ever. The only tournament I ever did well in Clyde fucked it up and some how ended it instead of facing me. Elvis: So you going to pack it up and not get your chance at the Communist? Duke: A communist? I hate commies almost as much as I hate guys in Hot Masks!! I’m a republican and an American God damn it. The black guy cringes when he hears Duke a Republican. Fro: Shit man I’m not going to hold that against you. Duke: I’m on a mission from God to get a TV. None of these guys have the drive or the want that I have to win this tournament. I have a purpose God damn it. I want to watch TV and pay off this stupid cable fine. My life is renewed and it has been renewed in this prison. I SEE THE LIGHT! Duke stares up out of the cell as the two men try to figure out what he sees. Duke: Do you see the light? I see the light I’m on a mission!! Duke does a cartwheel in the small cell as he beams with excitement and energy. Duke: Those sons a bitches are going to be sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND and HOSS that’s the HONKEY TONK TRUTH! Fro: So mote it be.
