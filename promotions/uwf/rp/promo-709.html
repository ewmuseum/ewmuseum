UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #709

It's in the bag!

9/18 (year unrecorded)

Pájaro de Presa — Backstage at the arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
White Tgier is looking for for his tag team partner. We know it's White Tiger for the tiger striped trunks. However, he is wearing a paper bag over his head due the Local NVA Company running off with the lucha masks of each and every Masked Monkey. White Tiger: Never have I felt so humiliated. And I'm a masked Monkey! I get humiliated all the time! Now where is that partner of mine? Turning a corner, White Tiger see the Rudo with Attitude-o stumbling around the hall, with a paper bag on his head as well. He collides with walls, equipment, and a make-up lady, who he manages to land on top of in a rather compromising position. Make-up Lady: You pig! Pajaro: Aw, don't be a hater! What's you've had chicano the rest is just guano! The lady shoves the lecherous luchador off of her and storms off. White Tiger comes over and helps Pajaro up. Pajaro: What? A sneak attack? It's those commies again! The Bird of Prey begins swinging away, none of his blows coming anaywhere near White Tiger. White Tiger: Pajaro! It's me! White Tiger! Pajaro: Where? White Tiger: Over here. Pajaro: Why can't I see you? White Tiger: Because you didn't cut any eyeholes in your bag, you schmuck. Pajaro: Ohhhhh... White Tiger: Listen, we got a big match coming up and I want to talk it over with you. If we win, noone will laugh at the Masked Monkeys again1 Pajaro: Really? Because we get laughed at a lot. White Tiger: I know. Pajaro: Like all the time. White Tiger: I know! Now listen up.I got an idea for some revenge. But first let's find some scossors. Pajaro: You think I need a haircut? Chicas dig the long flowing tresses. White Tiger: For eyeholes! Pajaro: Oh yeah... With that the Masked Monkeys wander off, one a little more aimlessly than the other...
