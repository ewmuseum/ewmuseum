UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #679

Final Fantasy History Lesson

7 Sep 2004

Mr. Meaner — A game store

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of a video game store. The store is empty, for some reason. And standing in the behind the counter of the store is the man wearing all black, Mr. Meaner. He has several objects laid out in front of him, lined in a specific order, changing size and shape from cartridge to CD. They look like they are video games. In his hands Mr. Meaner has a sledgehammer.) Mr. Meaner: So this weekend, I have to face the bitch that is known as Seymour Almasay. Some big shot who has come and gone from the UWF and has ran his mouth and decided that he is all that. Hell, he even claims he is the best in PTC since he is the Global Champion. What a joke this guy is. Nothing more than a chump…… The only reason that you hold the Global Championship is because I’m not there. The only reason you’ve been able to jump in and out of the UWF is because there has been no one there to stop you from doing it. The only reason that you have sustained your life this long is because you have not encountered me…… Let’s look here at the facts. You are Seymour Almasay, supposedly the greatest wrestler of this time. And in a feud with another jackass named Tsunami Oni. Hell, I honestly don’t even know why you two are feuding. You both are old farts who have no place in this federation. You both are people who don’t know when to quit. Let me point something out to you real quick like. See, the geriatric ward, it’s that way. (Mr. Meaner points to the trash can.) You might as well get out now before you have to face the young lions of this federation…… And you want the THC Championship. Like you call yourself hardcore. You wouldn’t freaking know hardcore if your life depended on it. You definitely are not the best wrestler in the business. And you sure as hell ain’t hardcore. You wouldn’t know hardcore if it bit you in the freaking ass. And hardcore might just bite you in the damn ass at SAS…… Let’s be honest here for a moment, shall we punk. You’re a video game geek who got lucky a few times. You have no talent, that’s for sure. You could never have talent. You not only stole your entire image from a video game. You stole the freaking video game itself. Try something new. Don’t even bring that weak crap my way. Much unlike Ken Jennings, you are wrong in all of what you are trying to do…… Think about going home. Think about going back to wear you came from. This is America. Where it takes someone who is tough and is able to fight to be able to win. Don’t confuse luck with the ability to win, because it is totally different. I am true talent boy. And at SAS, I will prove to be your master….. How about we look at this for a second…… (Mr. Meaner picks up the first game.) Final Fantasy; The first game in the series, developed for the NES in Japan. It did so well that it was translated to English and sold in North America, as well. Recently it has been released along with Final Fantasy II in the package titled Final Fantasy Origins, for the PlayStation. Even less American. Can’t even find something created in America. (Mr. Meaner takes the game, places it back on the table, pick up the sledgehammer, and smashes it.) How about we look at the next on? (Mr. Meaner picks up the next one.) Final Fantasy II; The second game, also for the NES. Never released to the English world until recently, in the above package. We could kill two birds with one stone here. Final Fantasy III; The third game, again for the NES. Never released to the English world. So now we have two games that we’re never designed to be English. Can we say pathetic and obsessive? (Mr. Meaner sets one game on top of the other, and then smashes them both with a sledgehammer. He then picks up about three cartridges.) How about we look at Final Fantasy Four then next, or two, or whatever you want to freaking call it. The fourth game, developed for the then-new SNES. I'll get to its first English incarnation in a moment. For now, let it be noted that it has somewhat recently been released for the PlayStation, as part of Final Fantasy Anthology (Japan and Europe) and Final Fantasy Chronicles North America. When the game was released, it was thought by many to be too hard. Square then kindly released this easier version. As an example of the changes, there was only one item type to cure all of the status ailments, rather than one item type per status. With me so far? Good. Because it's about to get complicated. Because now it becomes Final Fantasy II, at least to some. Yes, there's that II again. Pestered by English-speaking fans for another Final Fantasy, Square finally released Final Fantasy IV Easy type, with a few slight modifications, as Final Fantasy II for the SNES in North America. The translation, it may be noted, is heavily censored and often inaccurate. The later English version for the PlayStation, used the original harder version of the game, and has a more accurate translation. As it was the only Final Fantasy since the first one released in North America, they renamed it to avoid confusion. And it worked so well, too. (Mr. Meaner then puts the cartridges down and smashes all three cartridges. He then shrugs his shoulders and proceeds to smash all of the rest of the games lying on the counter, along with the counter itself.) I really can’t go farther into detail on the rest of them. But you can see the confused style that you are hiding behind, can’t you Almasay. It’s time to come out from behind the confusion and come to the realization. You are done. You are done in the UWF. All this information I provided, couldn’t have been done with the help of the External Legends website, but the information that I can give you for sure is that your career is washed up. Just go away and stay away Almasay, because I promise you, your career will be over. (Mr. Meaner then proceeds to start smashing the rest of the stuff in the store as the scene fades to black.
