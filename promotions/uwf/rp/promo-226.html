UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #226

The Beating Post?

17 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — A workarea

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a room. The walls are white, there are several wood shelves standing next to the wall, with various items on them, ranging from pictures, to various sports balls, to other random things you’d probably find in a wood shop type area. Standing in the middle with his head down, breathing heavily is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a blue muscle shirt and a pair of jean pants. He looks like he has exerted quite a bit of physical energy, but doesn’t look like he’s done yet. He also has a bandage over his head. Very quickly, he snaps his head up staring directly at the camera.) Kazee: I’VE HAD IT. THIS CRAP IS GONE FAR ENOUGH. I’M SO SICK AND FREAKING TIRED OF ALL THIS CRAP THAT, ARGGGGGGHHHHHH!!!! (Kazee walks over to one of the shelving areas. Leans up against the wall, trying to calm himself down. He stands there quietly for a few seconds, just breathing heavily, head against the wall. All of a sudden out of no where, he grabs a hold of the shelves on the left side of him and yanks them down, tumbling everything down to the floor with a loud crash.) WHO THE HELL DECIDED THAT I WAS THE DAMN WELCOMING PRESENT FOR EVERY JANE, DICK, AND JOHNNY WHO DECIDED TO WALK INTO THE UWF. WHO THE HELL DECIDED TO TELL EVERYONE THAT THEY COULD ATTACK ME CAUSE I WOULDN’T FIGHT BACK!!!!!!!! I’M SICK OF BEING THE DAMNED WHIPPING BOY FOR NEWCOMERS COMING INTO THE UWF. AT ONE TIME, I WAS MORE THAN HAPPY TO COMPETE FOR THE TV TITLE WHEN IT WAS HERE. I WAS MORE THAN HAPPY TO BE THE STANDARD BEARER FOR ROOKIES TO DEBUT AGAINST. NOW, I AM NOT THAT ANYMORE. I’M THE STANDARD FOR IF YOU WANT TO GET ANYWHERE IN THIS FED. (Kazee moves to the other side of the room slowly, then with a very quickly jumps up and axe kicks one of the shelves on the other side, splitting it in half, once again making everything tumble down to the floor.) FIRST IT WAS YOU RAID. DAMN IT, ALL I WANTED TO DO WAS COME BACK STAGE AND WELCOME YOU TO THE UWF. DAMN IT DUDE, WE USE TO BE RUNNING BUDDIES. WE USE TO BE THE MAN AROUND WHERE EVER WE WENT. WE USE TO DO WHAT WE WANTED, WHEN WE WANTED, AND HOW WE WANTED, AND AS SOON AS YOU STEP FOOT INTO THE UWF, YOU JUMP ME LIKE THERE IS NO TOMORROW. DAMN IT RAID, I HAD RESPECT FOR YOU. NOW, I’M NOT SO SURE. (Kazee turns toward the front of the room. He starts walking toward the camera. He then paces backward toward a work bench in the middle of the back wall.) RAID, THE REASON I HAVE LOST RESPECT FOR YOU IS BECAUSE YOU COULDN’T FACE ME AS A MAN. YOU HAD TO GO FIND HELP. YOU HAD TO RUN LIKE A LITTLE GIRL AND FIND SOMEONE TO HELP YOU. AND LOOK WHO YOU PRODUCED. DID YOU NOT THINK YOU COULD GET THE JOB DONE YOURSELF RAID. WERE YOU AFRAID THAT IF YOU CONTINUED THIS LITTLE FEUD WITH ME THAT THE PLAYERS CLUB WOULD COME AFTER YOU?...... (Kazee finally calms down a bit, but the fire is still in his eyes and in his voice as he talks.) You know what Raid; I have no control over the Players Club. I never have, never will claim to. As a unit, we have proved we are unstoppable, but as individuals, we are very capable of taking people out anytime, and I can not and will not be held responsible anymore for what Dunn and Monty do to you if they ever get their hands on you….. And that brings me to Anarchy. I don’t even know what to say about you. So you guys want to run with the big boys do you? You too “dangerous veterans” think that you can run with The Players Club. Well, that’ll be known in due time, but I think y’all have to get your facts straight as well. I’ve been on the phone day and night with UWF officials trying to get Raid up here. I’ve been doing everything I could to get him up here. Video Tapes, Phone Calls. Hell, I’ve even provided them with plane fare and tickets to see Raid in action live……. Me, bad mouthing Raid about taking so long to get up here? Not at all. Fact of the matter was is that if I didn’t lay the pressure on the brass he wouldn’t be here by now yet. He’d still be down there training with the two “dangerous veterans.” He deserved to be up here. He is better than half of the roster here in my opinion. When you put him against such veterans as El Turtle, Trailer Park Luchador, and El Chupacabra, he should’ve been here a long time ago…… IT’S NOT MY DAMN FAULT THAT THE BRASS TOOK SO LONG TO GET HIM UP HERE. DO YOU HEAR ME ANARCHY. IT’S NOT MY FAULT…….. (Kazee grabs a sledgehammer off the wall, and smashes it into the work bench leaving a huge hole in it. He then grabs a white board that is hanging on the back wall with a marker. On the white board is the name Ruff Ryder in blue ink.) You see Anarchy, Raid, I keep here a list of wrestler who I want to make eat their words. A list of wrestlers who I want to beat respect into for one reason or another. Ruff Ryder, he knows I have problems with him. The self proclaimed Cherokee Thrilla who wouldn’t know a Native American if it bit him in the ass. He will soon see the error of his ways…… But now, I have three new names to add on to my list. Three new people to put on here. Three new people who I will beat the respect into that they obviously have no idea what it is. You three can keep attacking me from behind. That’s your prerogative. But from what I know of you three, you are all great athletes who don’t need to do that. But if it makes you feel like big men, then please, feel free too. The Bug Bomb. Total Anarchy. Whatever else you want to throw at me, I can handle that. The question is who will come out in the long run. Do you have the heart to go the distance. Do you have the determination to make a difference and to try and destroy “The Common Man.” Many have tried, they all have failed…….. (Kazee writes on the in red ink the names of Raid, Pryde, and Dai. He then makes a line connecting both Pryde and Dai and writes Anarchy, and finally, draws a half circles around the three of them, writing Chaos Society.) My heart is true. My determination is full. And I will survive for now and always. You, as great of athletes as you may be, don’t have the ability to take me out. You have awakened a beast that you can not kill, and as the old saying goes, “don’t hunt what you can’t kill.” None of you will be able to destroy me. Collectively you cannot destroy the Players Club. And when it is all said and done, The Players Club will be standing on the top of the mountain as we are now, looking down upon the rest of you…….. Good Luck, Anarchy, Raid, Chaos Society, you definitely need it……… Now, you’ll probably want to leave so I can clean this up. (Camera fades to black.)
