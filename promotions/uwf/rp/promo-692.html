UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #692

Pissed

9 Sep 2004

The Plague — Backstage (Following Supremacy)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera is outside The Plague’s locker room door backstage at Supremacy just following his defeat at the hands of Adian Pryde. You can hear screaming and stuff smashing. The door opens and flying past the cameraman’s head was a piece of the locker that is destroyed in the corner. Plague is only in shorts and boots. His hair is tied back, ice on his shoulder and neck area and he is just standing there smiling and waving to the camera. The room looks like the effects of Hurricane Ivan (look out Flordia). Plague: “Fuck!!!” [Plague punches his fist into his hand, than picks up a chair and throws it across the locker room. Hitting a mirror, the chair falls to the floor along with hundreds of shattered pieces of glass.” Plague: “Seven years bad luck.” [Plague shrugs his shoulders and smiles.] Plague: “I already have that, I mean Adian Pryde got the pinfall on Sunday, and I was the victim. Thanks to Tsunami Oni and his crazy shit off the ropes he does, that’s some nice stuff Oni, you knocked me the hell out. But what I really want to know is, what the hell was Montgomery doing at ringside. If you are trying to start something with Oni, you shouldn’t have done that on MY TIME.” [Plague picks up his sledgehammer and smiles holding it to his face.] Plague: “Don’t you know me well enough by now Mike, come on, I don’t have to tell people much, but I’m a little unstable.” [Plague kisses the sledgehammer, red is on his face, it is blood, Joanna’s blood.] Plague: “Mmmm, still warm too. Ewww, skin.” [Plague takes a piece of flesh off the hammer and throws it behind his shoulder.] Plague: “No, I didn’t smash Joanna’s skull on purpose, I bet everyone wanted to know that, but you know what, I’m not sorry. Pryde wanted to run his mouth about me doing nothing since my return, disappearing and appearing whenever, and getting into the Main Event, well what I did was help get you into The Main Event Pryde, and I shattered your bitches skull. A thank you would be nice.” [Plague grins, places the sledgehammer on the table, he than out of nowhere smashes the table with the hammer.] Plague: “Sunday, another chance to get my hands on Pryde and Oni. And Oni, I’ll get to you later, but damn, I forgot just how good you were, and well are. Take that as a compliment Oni, cause that will be the only one you’ll hear from me for a long time. The next compliment will be the one you give to me for ending your skyrocketing career.” [Plague pauses.] Plague: “Montgomery, what were you thinking though? Now it seems like you and I are once again on the same side along with The Communist, taking on Tsunami Oni, Adian Pryde and Nick Persia, ahh, another good buddy. Mike, I came back trying to end this little beef you and I had, but I just don’t know anymore Mikey. I don’t know what’s been running through your little Canadian mind of yours. But you got The World Title now don’t you? And, ummm, well, that piece of gold you have, you want to keep it for a while don’t you?” [Plague cracks his knuckles and stares into the camera with an evil, more maniacal look.] Plague: “Take that how you want to Mikey, it may be a threat, it may be talk, hell, it may be a promise, but just don’t let what happened on Sunday, happen EVER AGAIN.” [Plague calms.] Plague: “Now, I want to win Mike, Sunday I want to defeat Oni, Pryde and Persia, and I’ll do it alone or with you guys behind me. Mike, you may be the Champ, you can lead this team into the match, but I’ll lead us to victory. No fuckin’ around.” [Plague takes the ice off his shoulder and neck and throws it against the wall.] Plague: “I’m a little hostile tonight, more so than usual. I mean a lot has been going through my mind since getting pinned. Montgomery, The World Title, Tsunami Oni who is facing Montgomery at Live and Loud. Isn’t that great. Shit there’s something else to think about, plus Pryde, Joanna. I got tossed into some shit didn’t I. Great, thanks a lot Hayes, and Pryde talks his shit about me being a nothing, “Thinking” I don’t deserve to just get thrown into Main Event Matches. Who the hell is Adian Pryde anyway?” [Plague grabs his bag and the sledgehammer, looks around at the room, shakes his head and shrugs his shoulders.] Plague: “Take it out of the profits of Supremacy, cause you know it’ll be packed Hayes. And of course everyone wants to see Plague. I’m technically a Legend here in The UWF, one of the only survivors since day one. Numero fucking uno, damn, where’s my respect folks? The only person I see it from is Hayes, ain’t that some shit, who would have thunk it?” [[Plague starts to walks to the door, which is wide open still. Walking past the camera he leaves the room and heads down the hall, the camera follows.] Plague: “O, your still here?” [Plague shrugs and keeps walking. He heads past a few crewmen, but the hall is mostly empty. He heads out the door to the parking lot. Plague goes to his truck and stops.] Plague: “Come on bitches lets end this little speech on the right foot, in true UWF promo fashion.” [The camera catches up to Plague, and stops.] Plague: “Oni, I know you are facing The Champ at Live and Loud, it may be Montgomery it may not, all I know is good luck Oni, I want to see you win, just so when I finally do gain The UWF World Championship, it is from your broken and bloody hands. It was my fault, you lost the Title once, you say this only cause you know you lost, no excuses. Next time you lose it it will be really my fault. But now I got to relax and train to get my neck and back ready for Sunday, but I’ll talk again. Bye.” [Plague gets into his truck and drives away blasting “Nobody” by Skindred..
