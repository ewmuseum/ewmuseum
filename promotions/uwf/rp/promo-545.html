UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #545

One Liner battle royal

13 Jul 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — A gazebo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in an outside gazebo area. There is music blaring in the background and a lot of outside noise. Sitting on one of the benches in the Gazebo is Fizbane the Fabulous. His face is painted like normal. And he is wearing a pick leisure suit. He is just bobbing along with the beat of the music, which is “I Will Survive.”) Voice: HEY FIZZY. HEY FIZZY. (Fizzy doesn’t respond right away. He is lost in the music and his dancing. All of a sudden Venus and Aphrodite come running into the scene. Venus is wearing a purple sparkly dress and Aphrodite is wearing bell bottoms and a long sleeved blue shirt.) Aphrodite: HEY FIZZY. YOU MIGH WANT TO SAY SOMETHING ABOUT YOUR MATCH TONIGHT!!! (Fizzy just continues to be lost in the music, not really paying attention. Aphrodite and Venus give each other a glance and smile slyly. Both Venus and Aphrodite turn their back toward Fizzy, and pressing their behinds into his face, they shake their booty’s. Fizzy instantly snaps out of the music, jumps up, and starts spinning in circles. Venus and Aphrodite both watch him and start giggling.) Venus. This guy is so much more fun than our previous boss. Aphrodite: Girl, you are telling an understatement there. (Fizzy stops spinning and turns and looks at the two beautiful women who just “snapped” him out of his trance.) Fizzy: What’s up Chickadees? Venus: We just wanted to make sure you said a little something about your match for tomorrow. Fizzy: I already did, didn’t I? Aphrodite: Well, yeah, but we think you should say more. Fizzy: You do, do ya? Venus: Yes, we do. I mean you do have to give a reason for your “guests” to come to your “Party.” Fizzy: AND WE ARE GOING TO PAAAAARRRR-TTTTAAAAAAYYYY ALL…. NIGHT…… LONG!!!! Aphrodite: So who do you want to start with? Fizzy: I have no clue. I tell y’all what sugarplums, why don’t you tell me who to start with.. (Venus reaches down her dress and pulls out a card that was seemingly tucked in there. Fizzy sits back down on his bench. Venus and Aphrodite both sit on one of his knees so they can each see the card. Fizzy wraps his arms around their waist.) Venus: Well, there is Draco Paladine. Fizzy: Hey, that guy sounds familiar. Why do I know him? Aphrodite. Well, he is part of Chaos Society like you. Fizzy: Yeah, but it seems like I know him from somewhere else, like a previous life. If I remember right, this guy is one of those people who knows how to get down get down. Who’s next? Aphrodite: Fosta. He was talking some crap about you before. Fizzy: It’s all good. He just doesn’t know how to enjoy the good times and thinks that by insulting me he’ll bring my spirit down. Ain’t ever going to happen though. Ain’t no thang. Next? Venus: The Green Demon. Fizzy: The Who? Venus: The Green Demon. Fizzy: Whoever he is. I like green. Green is a good color on me. Along with pink, and blue and neon colors, and pastels. Venus: Everything looks good on you Fizzy. (Venus and Aphrodite laugh a bit.) Fizzy: Next? Aphrodite: Jukei Kutabare. Fizzy: Oh yeah, I just heard from him a little bit ago I think. He’s got some good moves, let me tell you. But he is way off beat on his moves. I might have to show him how to follow the beat. Next? Venus: Nick Nitro Fizzy: As in Nitrous Oxide? Sweet, I was running a little low on my supply. Wonder if he can hook us up for the party. Next? Aphrodite: Justin Morris Fizzy: MEOW MEOW MEOW MEOW, MEOW MEOW MEOW MEOW, MEOW MEOW MEOW MEOW. Aphrodite: (puzzled) What are you doing? Fizzy: Didn’t you say Morris, as in the cat from Meow mix? Aphrodite. Um, I think it’s a different Morris, but we’ll move on. Venus: “The Shocker' Dave Storm. Fizzy: The shocker? I’ve heard of that before. Venus: Yeah, our old boss showed us that. Fizzy: Whoa girl, your talking about the wrong thing here. I was talking dancing. It thought there was a dance called the shocker. Venus: Could be, but we can move on. Fizzy: Wow, you chickies move fast. Aphrodite: Well, we would rather get out there and party you know. Anyway, next is Sacrifice. Fizzy: Sacrifice? I ain’t touching that one. I don’t even want him there. We are talking about a party here, not a freaking execution. Venus: I don’t blame you. Finally we have 'The Renegade' Rich Rollins. Fizzy: Well, if he’s a renegade, then he’s gotta be a blast. Renegades are always fun and always know how to party hardy. Anyway, you said that was the last one, right? Aphrodite: Yep, that is correct. Fizzy: Well then lets go hit the town. (“Saturday Night Fever” blasts through the loud speakers. Fizzy gets up and spins on his heels. Venus and Aphrodite walk around Fizzy the opposite way he’s spinning before grabbing on to his elbows. He groove off camera as it fades to black.)
