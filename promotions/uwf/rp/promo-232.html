UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #232

The end is near

20 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — a random forest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The in a forest area. There are several trees around. Oaks, pines, birch, box elder, whatever; the type of tree might just be there, except for maybe a Banzai. Standing in the middle of the woods is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a pair of black jeans and a generic white shirt. He is also wearing a UWF hat. He stands there looking directly at the camera, walk toward one of the trees, and leans against it.) Ezra Kazee: Mr. Kennedy says that I am distracted. That I’ve got Anarchy, Raid and the Chaos Society on my mind. Well, maybe I have been a little distracted. I mean lets be honest here for a moment shall wee. While they haven’t cost me any matches per say, they’ve put one hell of a beating on me the past two weeks. So to say that they are on my mind would be an understatement… But Mr. Kennedy, rest assured that come Sunday, I will have one focus. That one focus will be you. You and only you. There won’t be no Raid. There won’t e no Anarchy. There won’t be anyone except you, and me in the middle of the ring, going move for move… Now Mr. Kennedy, I do have to say that this is the third time we have met in one way or another. The first time was my return match, where I defeated you to get into the NA Title Tournament, and the second time was when Montgomery and myself defeated you to win us the gold. This I what some people would’ve called the rubber match, if I had not been able to gain the win over you twice before. Instead, this is just the third match against you, and again, a lot is riding on the line… The second time we have gone against each other in a Title Tournament. The first time, as mentioned, the NA Title. This time, the IC title, which is basically the same thing as the NA title, since they were combined. The brass must enjoy having us two meet for opening rounds in title tournaments, since we seem to do it all so often… Our previous matches, your right, they are ancient history now. The first time we met, it was in a triple threat match. The second time was in a tag team title match. This time, and for the first time in our careers, it is a one on one, mono y mono match. No one else for us to fight. No one else for us to have to worry about. Just you and I, in an attempt to move on to the next step in the IC title tournament, and to cement our places in history… Mr. Kennedy, also, do not take my position as a member of the Players Club as a position of me not liking you. You are an accomplished athlete. You have all my due respect. I may not enjoy some of the people you associate with, especially that Native American wannabe called Ruff Ryder, but you, I truly have never had any problems with. You made a wrong decision. We all make wrong decisions. We cannot be held accountable for that. Now, The Prime Alliance is gone, you are on your own, and you seem like you are ready to put your own notch in the history books of the UWF……… That is very commendable. You have been overlooked sometimes. You have bee overshadowed on some occasions. And you deserve to be put in an aura of respect. But I as well am ready for bigger and better things. Granted, I am part of something bigger and better, but it’s time to form my own niche’ as a singles wrestler, and to show the Commonwealth exactly what I can do… I also would love to point out that you, Mr. Kennedy are telling me that this may unfortunately be the end. Wow, where have I heard that before? How many times have I heard that and how many more times am I going to hear that? I really don’t know. I’m kind of tired of hearing it myself. “The is the end for you Ezra.” Well whoop-di freaking do. All I have to say is bring it. Bring on the end for me. I’ve survived it before; I’ll survive it again. How many times must I hear that this is the end for me? I swear, I hate turning people into liars, but what else can I do… (Kazee starts walking to the right, behind and in between the trees.) Mr. Kennedy, this is a title that I have focused on for a long time. This IC title is something that I want, and I have wanted for quite a while. It has eluded me for so long. Your confidence, I will not be fooled by it, as I know you will not be fooled by any confidence that I have. I know that you are more than a capable wrestler. I know that you have the ability to sneak up on someone, catch them flat-footed, and beat them one, two, three… But on the other hand, I can do the same thing. I can come out of nowhere and strike for the pin fall. I can do things either methodically and wrench on a body part, using my amateur and submission background to make you tap, or I can just take a beating, which I am use to doing, and drop you down with the Bomb de Commonwealth. It doesn’t really matter too much to me. Either way, someone is going to get our of here in the split second it take to count to three… This is finally my chance to put myself with the elite in the UWF. The womanizer known as I took Lyle Style to the limit, barely able to escape with his title intact. Joshua Dunn was able to derail my challenges twice, be it through interference or some other reason. Now, instead of 1 athlete, I have three to go through. Malcolm Kennedy, you are my first. But make no mistake, while I was unable to beat just one athlete, my drive has continually increased and I am driven to beating three athletes… In the end, who knows what will happen, but the carrot is being dangled, and only one-person can grab it. I am determined that that one person will be me. I am putting my whole heart and soul into this. All my blood, sweat, and tears are going into this tournament, and in the end, if it be deemed that it shall be, I will emerge as the IC champion. Mr. Kennedy, good luck in this match up. (Kazee walks behind one of the trees, and is not seen as the camera fades to black.)
