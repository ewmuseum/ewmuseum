(A Matter of Respect) Brushing off the memories

23 Nov 2001

Toxic Shock

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Electric, Amasing, Beautiful, cocky, these are all words that have been used to describe Toxic Shock over the past few days when he's been in the ring fighting off his opponents. Toxic Shock, even though injured still, is very slightly improving on his in ring ability. He has found time to go to a jym on thursday, in which he was given some good advice, and that was; too win anyway you can, the last thing you want is to suffer another broken leg or arm. Toxic Shock's head is still hurting alot, Killer Dude managed to connect a bad ddt that almost had Toxic Shock down for the three but Toxic Shock's charisma and strength made him fight his way back up and continue to win. Well not win, but give his opponent a complete ass kickin' that will warn his ass never to mess with Toxic Shock again, before he does something really bad like throw someone off a stage. Team A.I American IMPort, hopes are high for this group at the pay per view 'A Matter of Respect' The Plague is going one on one with Jack Union, and Ben Jehrico is going one on one with Deke Aurora. The EoD on the other hand are going to suffer, what chance has El Matrella got against his opponents, and what chance has Killer Dude got against his!? Even if they are jobbers. Also what about Digimortal, the strong, rough, cowardly, dumass, piece of trailer park trash that will get the Whiplash. Digimon you are going to put your career on the line because at the moment Toxic Shock can get in a temper very easily these days. The scene opens on a deserted street, Toxic Shock is visable sitting on a bench outside a small shop. His hair flys through the wind as he looks at his watch, he looks up at the camera and smiles his cocky smile. He takes his sunglasses from their seated position above his nose and places them in his top left pocket on his jacket. He stands up revealing some black pants and some black shoes. As he stands a few cars pass nearby on the busy road, newspapers fly past the camera as Toxic Shock dodges them. Toxic Shock: Digital Mortality, it is you who is a victim of the Whiplash, it is you who will get beaten at A Matter of Respect because Digital,when have you paid any respect to the shock? The fact is you haven't, because basically you've spent too much time with Killer Dude making plans that never work, or recovering from matches that you've lost. You only are getting a shot at my belt because you lost against White Wolf, hear that? I said you lost. Toxic Shock laughs and avoids some more paper that has been blowing in the wind Toxic Shock: You were beaten. And it seems you better get used to being beaten because there ain't anyway that you can win tomorrow night against Toxic Shock. I am the force in thise federation, I am the tornado on a destructive path that will end when I hold the UWF Heavywieght title above my head. Toxic Shock closes his eyes for a moment, like he's getting power from somewhere, he opens them again to reveal a huge pupil in his eye, the pupil then shrinks as he continues to talk Toxic Shock: Digital you're just another win on my rankings, another step forward to the stars. My path will be tough and rough, but when you're concerened I've had enough. I've had enough of facing jobbers like you, the Sick Ricks, Chris Cripplers, I hate them all, they all suck. They are all a discraise to the UWF, and you my friend Digital Mortality, are a discraise too. He catches a paper in the wind, its a fishing magazine with a man on the front holding a 16 pounder. Toxic Shock throws the paper letting it catch the wind again, the streetlight he's under flickers as the howling wind makes it's way through the gaps in between the buildings and shops nearby, Toxic Shock steps a bit to the left so he's parallel with the lamppost, the wind now doesn't catch him as much as before and he regainst his stance. Toxic Shock: At the end of A Matter of Respect it will be my colours on the Universaltron, my belt will be above my head shining as photos all round the arena are took of me. Digital Mortality I've beaten you enough times, I don't need to prove I can beat you again. I could.. I might just end the match in a DQ, but I'm a sporting type of person, I win a fair match.. most of the time. Toxic Shock's hair starts to regain its normal position as the wind dies down, the shops behind Toxic Shock become more clear as the camera pulls out. Toxic Shock: Here, is the UWF Arena, notice my face is one of the faces on the official poster, along with Ben Jehrico, Deke Aurora, Luca, and The Plague. Where it belongs, with the stars, you, Digimon, don't diserve this belt, this belt doesn't deserve a super heavywieght as its holder. It deserves a normal average superstar with average moves, average fans, just plain average. Not some Digital freak with long nit filled red hair and some dark black clothes. Not some member in the EoD. Toxic Shock steps back as a huge lorry almost flashes past him bringing along with it a gust of windcapable of lifting a sumo of the ground. Toxic Shock's jacket almost rips its sleeves but the wind dies down. The camera stops shaking and pans round to reveal a huge poster above the entrance way to the arena. Toxic Shock: There is no doubt that the arena will be full of fans, mostly my fans I might add. The atmosphere in there will be electric. The fans are excpecting a great deal out of the UWF on Sunday. It has to beat Ground Zero which will be a hard task as Ground Zero had the hour and 17 minutes long Hardcore Deathmatch for the hardcore title which I was involved in. Toxic Shock looks at the empty street, about 30 metres away a lorries headlights become visable. A few seconds later the camera turns away from the blinding light as a stempede of sound rushes past to fade into the distance as the red back lights fill the area Toxic Shock: Our match will be like that Digiphantom, as soon as you enter you'll leave to a mass of booing, you'll be used as target practice for thousands upon thousands of fans packed in that arena. Along by yourside will be Mrs Digital, the flatchested slut you carry around with you. Giving her a dollar every hour to stand your breath. Which is a hard task I might add, heh I know. Toxic Shock closes his eyes yet again, but this time to stop dust coming in his eyes as a car passes by. A small man carrying a brief case walks past in the background fighting against the wind. All of a sudden a huge lightning flash fills the screen and an outbreak of rain swoops down and fills the area with density. The small man s walk in the background turns into a jog and then a run. Toxic Shock: Digimon, Digimon go away, come again another day. That song I think best fits this situation, as I'm fed up of your popeye antics. I'm fed up of your madness that is not needed in the UWF. This fight will end your efforts to try capture my title and you will crawl back to your precious locker room. I will show you what flammable means on Sunday when I open the gates to hell and allow you to enter my wrath of demons and dragons. And that's all I got to say 'bout Digital Re-inhanced trash tonight Toxic Shock pulls an object from his pcoat pocket, he moves his thumb to reveal an umbrella. He opens it and places his glasses back onto his nose. He starts to walk away from the camera and into the dense rain as the scene fades to black. Fri Nov 23, 2001 11:22 pm
