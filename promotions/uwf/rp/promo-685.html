UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #685

Getting MEANER!

Sept 8 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — #2 for SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Hardcore Championship. To many men, it is the pinnacle of their tenure in the UWF. Greats such as the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid never reached a higher title. To Seymour Almasy, however, the belt is a trinket. A symbol. A sign of power over the federation. A sign of his ability to dominate. ------- Seymour Almasy has left the area of the Chuckie Cheese, and now, once more, is back in his apartment. He seems to be sated with being MEAN for a little while, and instead relaxes on his couch. Seymour Almasy: Mmmm, such a lovely full day of being MEAN to people. But what to do now? I’m quite tired, but I can’t fall asleep… As the former UWF World Champion begins to close his eyes to disprove his own words, he suddenly falls off the couch. Seymour Almasy: Okay, the hell? Shrugging the odd occurrence off, Almasy remounts the couch, and lowers his head to the cushion. Seconds later, he again falls off the couch. Seymour Almasy: This couch is testing my patience… Almasy leaps in the air, landing hard on the couch. This time, he practically slides off, and is up to his feet in a second, glaring at it angrily. Seymour Almasy: YOU DARE DEFY YOUR LORD?! The couch, of course, is silent. Seymour Almasy: That’s it. I did not want to have to do this. I was saving it for Mr. Meaner, but now, seating device, you have incurred my full wrath. Prepare to face something the likes of which you have never before felt. Prepare to face….Trance. The couch looks on, well, a couch doesn’t really look…but it waits there, MENACINGLY, no doubt, as Seymour glares directly at it. Seymour Almasy: By focusing my energy thus so, and dropping into meditation, I can put myself in a state where all pain I feel is lessened, just slightly. I’m not Sephiroth or anything…but it gives me just that little tiny extra bit of adrenaline that I need….COUCH! PREPARE TO FEEL TRANCE! With a piercing cry, Almasy leaps forward, and throws a perfect flying side kick to the couch cushion. Of course, the cushion absorbs the blow. Seymour Almasy: Blast it! My foot should have gone clean through that couch! I must be…MORE AGGRESSIVE! Almasy launches himself at the couch, raining fists, feet, and head butts. The couch finally turns over, but still, no real damage is being done. Seymour Almasy: I have upended my adversary! YET STILL, HE DOES NOT YIELD! This is terrible! How am I supposed to beat Mr. Meaner when I cannot beat my SEATING DEVICE back into subservience? Suddenly, the answer is quite clear. It lies on the table across the room. “It” is the Divine Blade Imperial II, Seymour Almasy’s sword. He leaps across the room, taking his blade, and turns back to face the couch. Seymour Almasy: FACE FATE, VILE COUCH! With a mighty swing of the sword, the rebellious couch is cleaved in twain. Almasy slashes his sword several more times, posing mightily, and finally sinks the sword into the defeated couch. Suddenly, however, he has an attack of conscience. Seymour Almasy: I have…erred. I am no better than Mr. Meaner! He attacked defenseless video games, just as I have assaulted this defenseless couch. I may be MEAN, but never let it be said that Seymour Almasy is without heart… The Final Fantasy bows his head, giving the couch its due moment of silence. During the quiet spell, Seymour thinks of all the good times with the couch. All the times he spent on it watching TV, all the naps he had, all the fun he had with La-- Seymour Almasy: BAH! The single word erupts from Almasy’s soul as if a vulgarity. Clearly, the memory of Laura is a subject he does not want to breach. Seymour Almasy: Noble couch, it was an honor…but your betrayal was too much for me to bear. Now having no other place to rest, Seymour heads for his bedroom, and gracelessly belly flops upon his bed. Seymour Almasy: I must not sink to Mr. Meaner’s level. I am superior to him, better. Just as I am to every other athlete in the UWF. Have THEY won any tournaments? No! He looks up at the plaques on his wall, symbols of victory in tournaments and contests the world over. Seymour Almasy: I am the best wrestler that the UWF has ever known. Period. Lew Spectre, Kenny Mercury, Bum Boozler…all mere pretenders to my throne. My name is known throughout the lands, and is echoed throughout the cosmos. And Mr. Meaner would challenge me. Mr. Meaner would try and tarnish the reputation that I have created?! Almasy dismisses the thought with a laugh. He shakes his head, as if such a thought was completely and utterly out of the realm of possibility. Seymour Almasy: If he has such illusions, he is more delusional than I believed him to be. A hardened criminal he may be, but he is no idiot. From what I have been told, many prisoners of Alcatraz went insane. Stir-crazy, as they call it. If such is the case, this clearly explains Mr. Meaner’s delusions! His time in the penitentiary has obviously diluted his thinking capacity! Warped his mind! If he thinks he can beat me, plain and simple… He’s living in a fantasy world. (End)
