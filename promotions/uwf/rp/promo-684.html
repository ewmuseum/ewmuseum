UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #684

black doll

8 Sep 2004

Dreammaker — a busy mcdolalds

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(the scene opens up with dreammaker handing out little cards to local people at a very busy mcdonalds early in the morning.) dm:here you go ma'am, and dont forget little dicky needs all the girls he can get to come see him. (as he hands out a card to an 80 year old woman).. dm his phone number is on the back if you get lonely .. you are just his type... old lady: thank ou so much this will be fun.. dm :ya ya run along now.. ( a pause to look at the camera, and laugh) dm:U probably want to know why i am at this certain high priced etablishment.. i figured that this is where littly dicky goes to get chicks, or at least that is where he met his last girlfriend, i mean his cousin. (just then another older woman comes up to dreammaker holds out her hand for the free card) old lady #2: whats going on with the cameras and stuff?? dm: well im trying to help my special friend, he has his first real wrestling match downtown tonight and he needs some fan support. so im trying to help him out.. if you notice on the back there is his address and phone number just in case you are lonely. hahaha dm: well little dicky it sure seems like you have questions? well this nightmare wont be over soon so stop crying.. when over looking your bio the other day when i was leaving the arena i found out you liked fan mail and there was a number on it too.. so im trying to help a 'BROTHER' out. you look kinda lonely all the tiem with your 'pet'!! (after a short laugh dreammaker turns around to greet some more people and give some cards away.) dm: you are right though(looking back at the camera and flashing a large smile) i am new. and noone knows me yet. i therefore have an advantage. no other wrestler knows what i can do. but they will all know after i show you why rednecks dont belong on this big stage. its time for you to wake up! (after yelling at the camera man to pack up and get on with his job dreammaker walks around the tables and gives out more cards to people. stopping at the last table before the door to give a card to a large man dressed in a black exterminaters uniform, nods his head and exits the building. ) (then the camera zooms in on a dropped card and it isnt a card but ticket to wednesdays show and it reads: free admission if you bring a black doll to the show. )
