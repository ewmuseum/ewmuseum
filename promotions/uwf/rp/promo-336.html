UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #336

Vengence (Short and Sweet)

13 Apr 2004

The Plague — Backstage at Rennaisance

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open with a shot of The Plague sitting on a table being wrapped up by paramedics. All he is wearing is a pair of shorts. His hair is tied back and you can see a tight wrap around The Plague's midsection and head following his THC loss at the hands of Nick Persia. The Plague glances over at the camera and shakes his head in disappointment.] Plague: 'Let me first go out and say congratualtions to Mr. Nick Persia, you beat me, you were the better man. I hope you enjoy that title Nick, because who knows how long you will hold it. This is THC Nick, your lucky I didn't splatter your head all over that ring with my sledgehammer. You truely are talented, but lets see if you can hold on to the gold in another match. Hardcore Nick, injuries that could put a man out for a career. I have been through a lot fighting my way throughout that division, and look at me now, what do I have to show for it, a bloody and beaten body.' [Plague shows a look of pain on his face as he stands up. The paramedics put away their instruments and exit the room. The Plague picks up his shirt and throws it over his shoulder.] Plague: 'THC flows through my blood, but it seems that The UWF wanted to throw me in a tag match a few weeks ago and than when The PPV comes I get put in a THC Match, my first defense of the title since winning it. What's up with that? one title defense, I held that belt since March 7th. I guess it is time for me to move on to bigger and better things in The UWF. I lost in my first PPV match back in this company, I tried living up to my own hype, and I proved that I could gain the Hardcore Title in only my second match back. But now it seems that it is the new breeds turn to battle over THC, and that's fine with me, but this Supremacy, somebody will be the victim of my vengence, somebody will walk into the arena, but they will not be walking out.' [Plague cracks his knuckles and grins into the camera.] Plague: 'It is time once again to prove that no one is safe from The Infection no one is safe from The Black Death. I hope you all have eyes in the back of your heads cause Sunday somebody will get beat, and it sure won't be pretty. No one is safe anymore.' [Plague walks out of the room and heads down the hall as the scene fades.]
