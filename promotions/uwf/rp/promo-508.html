Returning to His Past Part 1

6 Jul 2004

Adien Pryde — New York

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a New York street. All over the place people are rushing to a fro like chickens with their heads cut off to busy to give any passer by a greeting of any sort. Cars and taxies stuck in rush hour honking their horns and yelling out a random obsinity, pedestrians crossing the street or hailing a cab, all looking out for number one as they try to get where they want to go. As the camera pans the crowds it picks up two familiar figures, Adien Pryde and his manager/trainer Joanna. Both are wearing the signature black and red of The Chaos Society.] Joanna : Who the hell would want to live in this hell hole anyways? A person wouldn't dare drive a car here, the people on the sidewalks are assholes. [A woman looks at Joanna crossly as she hears what was said.] Joanna : Ya, that includes you bitch. [Pryde laughs out loud as the woman gets a look of shock on here face, sticks her nose up in the air and continues to walk on.] Joanna : The air is un-breathable and the mass transit system sucks. Pryde : But many people call it home including at one time you Angel, didn't you. Mr. high and mighty, I must destroy all evil used to live among these people and pollutants and even polluted his body more. [A man reading a newspaper, not looking where he is going walks right into Pryde.] Man 1 : Sorry, I wasn't paying attention. Pryde : Losers like this are all of this pile of a city. Man 1 : Who you calling a lo… [Pryde pushes the man away with one arm and sends him sprawling into a pile of trash. Looking down at the man Pryde continues to speak.] Pryde : Throw in snow, sleet, and some biter cold and this could have been you 4 years ago. That's right, you were drunk and overdosing on sleeping pills weren't you? What a pathetic way to end up. [The man looks up at Pryde and Joanna, scrambles to his feet and runs up the street, looking over his shoulder and mumbling something about pro wrestlers and the crazy camera men that follow them.] Pryde : But look at you now, 'Back from the Dead' all on account of prayer. Hey, I am as religious as the next man, but even that is far fetched for me. I don't know what drugs that geisha girl has you on but she has you believing that story and even suckered you into marrying her. Then again you were a user so it must not have been that hard. Joanna : Now that is carrying it to far Pryde. I didn't teach you to act that way in boot camp. Pryde : Yes you did, you said that if it is true, put it out there. Joanna : Oh that's right, I forgot. [They walk a little bit further and come to a park.] Pryde : Recognize this place Angel? I'm sorry you wouldn't, not anymore at least because they tore down that rat infested hole of an apartment building you live in and built this wonderful place. [The camera pans to the block sized park that has people all over it. Mothers with their children playing and talking with others like them, men in business suits reading the Wall Street Journal and eating their lunch, and a man playing Frisbee with his dog.] Pryde : Quite a piece of work they did. Who would have thought that it could be done. Tomorrow I plan to send you back to the way you were. I intend to destroy your knee and end your wrestling career again. I want to see you miserable and crushed to the shell of a man you were last time you lost everything. Mean? Maybe. Cruel? Most defiantly, but you are the one that says I am evil, and if you believe that your destiny is to vanquish all evil you must believe what you say about me. All I have to say is just bring it and you can find out just how evil I can be. [Pryde and Joanna start to walk into the park as the scene fades to black.]
