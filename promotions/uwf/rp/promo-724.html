Killer Dude speaks for the Last Time before the PPV(PPV #3)

30 Sep 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in as you see a very dark room. In the center of the screen, you can see Killer Dude sitting on a steel chair. He actually looks like he has actually tidyed up since the last time we saw him, which is something new. He begins to speak to the camera) Killer Dude: At this moment in time, there a lot of the serious, dark, brooding superstars here in the UWF. Some names that come to mind are Misfit, Moraless, Nick Persia, and to some extent, Seymour Almasy. Also fitting into this category at certain times are Kenny Mercury and myself. It seems now that this type of character is the 'in' thing. It is now considered 'in' to be deep, dark, and always having problems with your life. It is now considered normal to think that you are a video game character that can shoot fire out of his fingertips. That's why the new Black Star Revolution is so unique to the UWF. We will not try and make the fans feel sorry for us, feel sorry for the situations we are put in. We don't dress like goths, and act like them just for the sake of a crowd reaction. What we do is called realism. Misfit and Moraless are just trying to make you, the UWF Fans, feel sorry for them. We just like to have a little fun, and of course, we know just the way to do it! Hell, we pretty much do it at every show we are at. We don't make the crowd feel sorry for us, we make them feel sorry for the people that we are beating the heavenly shit out of with golf clubs and steel chairs. And that's the way we operate, and I'm sure that the fans respect that. Actually, I really don't give a shit, so it doesn't matter. Every time you see us on PPV, Television, or out on the road, we give a little bit more of ourselves, and we show the people what we are really like. But a lot of the people that like to pick on and taunt the New Black Star Revolution keep going on and on about things like Kenny Mercury picking a bad choice for a partner, that I'm a loudmouth who can't do anything. They misinterpret the real us, and that is that we don't give a shit what you say. We prove ourselves each and every night, and if involves weapons to do so, then we go out there and fuckin' do it. They actually come out and say that we aren't a threat to their titles, to their livelihood, and to their health. They claim that we aren't contenders to anything in this federation. Well, I'm here to set the record straight. What you people are saying is nowhere even close to the truth. (During Killer Dude's speech, the camera has slowly zoomed in on Killer Dude until his face is the only thing shown on the screen. He stares into the camera with his piercing eyes as he continues to speak) Killer Dude: I can't speak for Kenny Mercury at this point in time, but I know that I am a threat to anyone's health, to their livelihood, to their chances of making it here in this federation. I always have been. You see, hardcore wrestling is the only thing I truly love in my life. It's the thing that has cost me many long friendships. Hell, now I can count my friends on one fuckin' hand. (Killer Dude smirks at the camera) Killer Dude: It's because of this business that I can barely walk on some nights, because of my knee. The specialists say that because it has happened 5 times, I'm risking being not able to walk. I'm risking losing my fucking knee, god damnit. I was never a fuckin' joke, even though some people will dispute that case, but I know that I WAS NEVER A FUCKIN' JOKE. I am a threat to all of you people, especially Misfit, Moraless, Nick Persia, and Seymour Almasy. In even some cases, I'm an extreme threat to Kenny Mercury himself. You can't just not take me seriously, or I will fuck you up so bad, you'll be in the hospital for a month. This Tuesday night, Misfit, Moraless, Nick Persia, Seymour Almasy, and Kenny Mercury, I want all of you to keep what I'm saying in mind. I'm sure that the punishment that you deal to me will add to what I said before. Hell, I might even rip my knee up again. But when all is said and done, and the smoke is cleared, and all of you are trying to figure out what's going on, you will have to realize, it's a give and take situation. The more punishment you give out, the more punishment I take, the more punishment I give back in return, bitch. It's time for Killer Dude to step up to the plate. It's time for Killer Dude's name to go down in the fuckin' history books. It's time for the New Black Star Revolution to take hold. VIVA LA BLACK STAR REVOLUTION, BITCH! (The screen slowly fades away into snow)
