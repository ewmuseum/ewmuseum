UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #818

Via Chrono Purifico: Time Purifies (II)

12/4 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Varying

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Chicago, Illinois) In one of Chicago’s numerous police precincts, several officers, along with many UWF employees, sit huddled in an interrogation room, attempting to figure out exactly what the hell has happened to them. The UWF Headquarters still stands, thanks to the efforts of firemen, but the employees are still clearly shaken. The police chief stands at the head of the table, trying to take control over a situation in which he has absolutely none. But one fact is utterly clear. Police Chief: This Seymour Almasy has gone off the deep end. We manage to get anything on him from a background check? Police Officer: Seymour Almasy’s his real name. Had it legally changed from Jason Seymour Wilson. Mother charged with child neglect five years ago, for leaving him at home alone throughout most of his childhood. Has had psychological counseling. Police Chief: Lovely, so he’s a nut job! A nut job that’s probably already split the state, and we have no leads on him! Slowly, tentatively, the Television Intern, still carrying the two remaining tapes that Seymour gave her, raises her hand. Police Chief: Yes? Television Intern: Seymour gave me three tapes to play. Presumably, they’re promos for his match against Tsunami Oni at FANdemonium. Police Chief: Tsunami Oni? One of you mentioned that name earlier. What does he have to do with anything? Sheepishly, the Mail Intern raises his hand, and doesn’t wait to be called on. Mail Intern: Oni’s the UWF World Champion, and Seymour’s mortal enemy. The two of them were set to face off for the title at our next pay-per-view. Apparently, however, Tsunami Oni’s dead. I told Seymour…and he snapped. Police Chief: Alright then…at least now we’ve got something to work with. Surveying the three other officers in the room, he begins to delegate tasks Police Chief: You, go call up San Francisco, get any information you can about this Tsunami Oni. You, send out a bulletin to other states. Give them a picture and some info about Almasy. You, go back to the UWF Headquarters, see if we can find anything. The three officers immediately leave the room, spreading out to accomplish their tasks. Television Intern: What about you? Police Chief: I’m going to stay here with you all and watch the two tapes you have… ----- (San Francisco, CA) Seymour Almasy: WHAT DO YOU MEAN HE’S NOT HERE?! A very mad looking Seymour Almasy stands before an orderly at one of San Francisco’s morgues. The man’s nametag reads, of course, “BERT”. Bert: He’s not! I don’t know what happened! We took him in earlier in the day, I know it! I checked him in! But then this pretty woman came and-- Seymour Almasy: Shiatsu. SON OF A BITCH! Before Bert can pee his pants in fear, Almasy dashes out of the morgue. He looks desperately for somewhere to go, but can find nowhere, before finally sitting on the sidewalk, defeated. Seymour Almasy: This…can’t be happening. It’s like a bad dream. Championships no longer matter. Nor does victory, nor defeat. All of Seymour’s accomplishments, every single thing in his life, is now meaningless. Seymour Almasy: I gave it all up. I gambled, and I lost. I had everything. A great career, a loving wife, fans who cared about me…and only one annoyance, Tsunami Oni. I sacrificed everything I valued to scratch an itch. And now, after paying everything I could, that itch has physically disappeared. But it will torment me for the rest of my life. Slowly, he rises, hand reaching into his pocket, pulling out the ticket that he had purchased after landing in San Francisco. Seymour Almasy: And when there’s nothing left, there’s only one place for a man to go. The ticket is non-stop from San Francisco to New York’s La Guardia Airport. One-way. ----------- Police Chief: That second video didn’t help us at all! Television Intern: I TOLD you that it was just a wrestling promo…hey, wait a sec, there’s a note in with this last tape… Slowly unfolding the piece of paper, she reads it aloud. Television Intern: “Please only play this tape if I lose to Tsunami Oni. Thanks-- Seymour.” The look on the face of the police chief grows almost immediately grave. Police Chief: Give me the tape. His unwavering gaze somewhat unnerves the intern, who immediately hands the tape over. The chief ejects tape two, and slides in tape three. What they see immediately sends shivers up their spines. ------ Seymour Almasy: If you’re seeing this tape, well, that means that…I lost. The Final Fantasy sits alone in his apartment, clad in only a black shroud. His bed is his chair, as his fingers drum nervously on the nightstand. Seymour Almasy: I probably gave it a great effort. I always do. But in the end, Tsunami Oni was the better man. I can live with that. Well, actually, I can’t. Laying on the bed, next to Seymour, is his Divine Blade Imperial, unsheathed. Seymour looks at it, wistfully. Seymour Almasy: Good sword…too bad I’m not strong enough to wield you anymore. Friends, enemies, what you are now watching is the last will and testament of Seymour Almasy…of Jason Seymour Wilson. --- Jaws drop. The Television Intern is too shocked to speak, watching on helplessly as Seymour continues. --- Seymour Almasy: To Tsunami Oni, my conqueror, I leave my Divine Blade Imperial. Use it well, Oni, for you have earned it. Somewhat tearfully, Seymour bids farewell to his weapon. He extends it outward in both arms, nodding his head. Seymour Almasy: It is a tradition, the conquered gives the conqueror his sword. Just as Lee did Grant, so I now do to you. Placing the sword back down on the bed, Seymour continues. Seymour Almasy: With that out of the way, I leave everything else in my possession to my ex-wife, Laura Winters. ---- Police Chief: Ex-wife?! Television Intern: Yes, Seymour and Laura were married for almost a year… Police Chief: Do you have contact information for her?! Television Intern: Yes, we do. Police Chief: Give it to me, I’m going to go call her, and the local police. If this guy’s going to kill himself, he’ll probably do it somewhere familiar to him. Television Intern: Like his apartment? Police Chief: You have an address?! Television Intern: Yes. Police Chief: Give it to me! Give me all the information you have on these two, I’m going to go make some calls RIGHT NOW, we have to stop this at all costs. The police chief dashes out of the room, as Seymour finishes up on the tape. --- Seymour Almasy: Laura, you gave me some of the best years of my life. And I wanted you to know…I was wrong. I let Tsunami Oni get to my head. If I could have done things differently, I never would have turned my back on you. And so, as my last act on this Planet…Laura, please forgive me. The tears begin to roll down Seymour’s face proudly. A great weight has been lifted from his shoulders, but it appears likely that he is soon to lift the greatest weight of all… Seymour Almasy: That’s it, I guess. Seymour Almasy, 1981-2004. Ah well, it’s been a good ride… Abruptly, the tape cuts out. ------- Television Intern: I don’t believe this… Mail Intern: Yeah, I know. He never seemed capable of this. A bit nuts, sure. Sure, he liked to decapitate bunny rabbits for experience points. Sure, he wasn’t the most sane person in the world. But I didn’t think he would be capable of killing himself! Suddenly, one of the police officers comes running back into the room, breathlessly. Officer: Tsunami Oni’s not dead! Mail Intern: Oh thank God! We still have a World Champion! The Television Intern glares at him, before promptly slapping him across the face. Television Intern: SEYMOUR’S GOING TO KILL HIMSELF FOR NOTHING NOW! We have to tell him Tsunami’s alive! Call him! Try his cell phone! NOW! Did you tell the chief? Officer: Yes, I did, he’s still on the phone with various people, trying to get in touch with anyone he can. Mail Intern: Damnit! He’s not answering! Television Intern: This is bad. Really, really bad… ---- In his apartment, Seymour Almasy lay upon his bed. His nightstand had been cleared off, and sitting on it were five glasses of liquid. Seymour Almasy: Ah….Russian Glass. The ultimate gambling game from Illusion of Gaia, and it shall be how I test my fate. Five glasses. For the next five hours, I shall drink one glass each hour. Four are filled with regular wine. One has poison in it. So, we shall soon see just how much longer the life of the Final Fantasy extends. Considering the methods possible, this doesn’t seem like the most efficient way to kill oneself, but nonetheless, it is the method that Seymour has selected. Seymour Almasy: Glass number one… Raising it skyward, Seymour lowers it to his lips, and drains the contents. Seymour Almasy: Ah, sweet Elixir… ------ Police Chief: Hello, is this Laura Winters? Meanwhile, back at the police station, the chief has finally gotten in contact with someone close to Seymour. Laura Winters: This is she. Who is this? Police Chief: I’m Officer Ryan Moore, Chicago police department. Laura Winters: Why would the Chicago police department be calling me? Police Chief: It’s about your ex-husband, Seymour. We have reason to believe that he plans on killing himself. Silence. And then a *click*. Police Chief: Miss Winters? Miss Winters? ----- In her small apartment, Laura’s eyes closed. She couldn’t believe what she had been told. Seymour was never the most stable person in the world…but suicide? Even though they were separated, tears began to flow from her eyes. She couldn’t help it? How could she? Feelings such as hers don’t just disappear. Laura Winters: Seymour… She made her decision. She was going to Seymour’s apartment. She would never be able to forgive herself if she didn’t. (To be Continued)
