6 Aug 2004

The Communist — Outside a Jewish Temple

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist stands outside a Jewish Temple, looking up at the massive building. His large Russian hands clench and unclench, his eyes looking up at the Star of David: Commie: Religon, the Opiate of the People... The mind controling, life eating beast that the People put their trust in. And yet, they seem so content with their lives. So happy, so knowledgable of their fate. I find myself without direction... Now at the loss of my manager. The Soviette, my compass. My political Officer. :The Bells ring and people walk into the temple, Yamakas in place, their Torahs under their arms, children scampering under foot as parents try to reign them in. The Communist sighs, seeing lives he can never have, assurances in a God he doesn't beleve in. One of the Elders walks up to The Communist: Elder: Oy my Friend, what are you looking for? You're more then welcome inside. Commie: I'm not so certain of that... Elder: What is your name? Commie: The Com... Nikoli. My name is Nikoli. Elder: You are looking for something? I can tell, I know these things. Have an eye for it. Commie: Not exactly, Comrad. A friend of mine is in trouble and I'm not sure how to help her. Elder: A friend? A Lady friend? Oy gavelga, You should look to the Lord for help. Commie: I don't believe in your god. Elder: You don't have to my boy, he believes in you. And sometimes, that's enough. Come inside my friend, I'll introduce you to the People. You are Russian, yes? The Jewish people owe yours thanks for WW2. Please, be our guest. Commie: Very well, but I'm not sure how long I can stay... Elder: As long or as short as you feel you should. :On the way in, some skinheads walk up, shouting slurs and generaly being assholes: Skinhead: You freaking Jews! Where's your star! Elder: Oy... Those troubled youngsters, I wish they wouldn't bother my people... Nikoli? Nikoli? :The Communist is already on his way over. He picks up a trash can and throws it across the lawn, nailing the leader in the head. One of the little neo nazis feels froggy, runs at the Communist and gets a tilt-o-whirl back breaker for his trouble. The Communist grabs the third and gives him a straight right to the face. The Poor skinhead stumbles in a haze, Commie gets behind him and executes the Communist Slam on the trash can: Commie: Never again bother these people, or face the wrath of the Communist! :The skinheads run, and the people surround the Communist. Some smiling and cheering, some suspicious. The Elder walks up to him, smiling: Elder: Well, thank you Nikoli. Please, come inside, You can have a bagel with us after services. It is the least we can do for you. Commie: Yes. Yes I think I will. :The Communist walks into the Temple with his new found friends:
