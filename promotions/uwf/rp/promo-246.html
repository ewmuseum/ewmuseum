UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #246

Look It Up!

2/26-27/04 (year unrecorded)

Kenny Mercury — Random UWF House Show

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A Universal Wrestling Federation house show. An event where people pay good money, the very money that they work until their hands bleed, to attend. And why? Why sacrifice so much to get that little ticket that let’s them into a UWF House show? No body really knows because UWF suck! Nothing happens! There are no big stars wrestling that night! Basically, its Random Jobber A beating on Random Jobber B who is an even bigger jobber than A, so he loses. But people don’t know that because they really can’t identify either one of them. So what the hell’s the point to even being there, why would someone just through their money away like that?!? Well… sometimes… someone big shows up. Of course, not to actively compete because… well, that’s just a big waste of time, but sometimes they just show up, gracing the common-folk with their presence! And sometimes they might even say a few words! HOLY CRAP! I wish that would happen! Because I’m at one of these UWF house shows RIGHT NOW! And guess what, this house show really really sucks… See, Jobber A just pinned Jobber B… big whoop. Now they’re leaving… good. Now what? Man, this house show really blows… wait a second… OH MY ROD I CAN’T BELIEVE MY NARRATING EYES!!! COULD IT BE?!?!] [The Universal-tron springs to life, and yes, this is the only reason the production crew broke their backs to put that huge thing up. ‘Stranglehold’ by Ted Nugent rocks the arena. The screen slowly climbs up, first showing two black wrestling boots behind a midnight backdrop. It steadily pans upwards, up the legs, past the little planet embroidered on the tights, up the rock hard abs, the perfect pecs, all the way on up to the face of one very sinister looking Kenny Mercury. He slowly runs his fist across his neck, declaring that: “Yes, I am Kenny Mercury, and I am very bad ass and shall decapitate any swine who tries to oppose me.” No, he doesn’t literally declare that, but that’s what it implies to everyone. Well, at least it does to me! You guys are all a bunch of fruit cakes…] [Kenny Mercury steps out onto the entrance ramp from backstage. He’s wearing with black wrestling tights with the little red planet near the crotch area. Kenny always wears his in-ring gear. Most guys only wear it when they wrestle, and prefer other clothes when they’re not at work. But, no, not Kenny Mercury. His spirit is one with his tights… that or maybe he just can’t afford anything else. But tights are kick ass anyhow, so it’s ok. Mercury cracks his signature smirk… very subtle and self confident. Kenny eventually reaches the ring, and is handed a microphone by a nerdy looking UWF cameraman/lackey/monkey/bitch/slave… He rolls into the ring and stands in the center of the ring. The crowd showers him with boos, for the most part. Kenny just smirks again, and slowly brings the microphone to his lips.] Kenny Mercury: You know… a wise man once said… *ahem* and I quote… “NO ONE CAN STOP MR. POWERFUL!!!”… End quote. [The audience stares at Mercury, dumbfounded.] Kenny Mercury: And do you know what that man’s name was? Huh? Do you? Well… it was Mr. Powerful of course! Geez, what a bunch of imbeciles… Everyone who’s anyone knows that Mr. Powerful was the one who said, “No one can stop Mr. Powerful!” You see, the key words are ‘Mister’ and ‘Powerful’, it’s a dead give away, you nonsensical piles of moose manure! I’m surrounded by dip-shits… oh-shit for dip-shits… [Kenny Mercury waltzes over to the turnbuckle and leans his elbow on it.] Kenny Mercury: Now, you may or may not be wondering… I’m guessing not since the average IQ of this crowd is below that of a mildly retarded, and moldy, frozen beef pie… But! The reason I shared that little quote with you is… Well, first off, Mr. Powerful kicked boo-tae, but the REAL reason was that, since Mr. Powerful has been out of the federation since like September or whatever of 2002, and because I’m the Shooting Star of the federation, the cornerstone, the foundation… and now PART OWNER of the UWF, I have bought the rights to use ole Mr. Powerful’s catch phrase… So now… I bring to you… the Dinguses of Douchbaggery, the NEW Kenny Mercury t-shirts! [Kenny flings his arm back at the Universal-tron, presenting his t-shirts. The Universal-tron slowly fades to life, showing a large white t-shirt with “NO ONE CAN STOP MR. POWERFUL!!! KENNY MERCURY!!!” written in black permanent marker across the front.] Kenny Mercury: Now I know what you’re thinking… or not thinking.... WHERE IN THE NAME OF KENNY MERCURY CAN I GET ONE OF THOSE!?!? Well, first of all, I have now replaced ‘God’ in the English dictionary… American AND English too… See, look… [The Universal-tron changes from the T-shirt to an open dictionary. It zooms in on a single entry. It reads…] “God- n. (god) – See ‘Precision’” [The dictionaries pages rapidly flip to the ‘P’ section. The camera once again zooms in on another entry.] “Precision – n. adj. – See ‘Kenny Mercury’” [The Universal-tron fades back to its display of the prototype of the new Kenny Mercury T-shirts.] Kenny Mercury: So you see… not only am I, Kenny Mercury, THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION! But I have also earned, and I do mean ‘earned’ the right, to be one and the same with God. And so, as my first godly task, I hereby add a commandment! And this commandment is… THOU SHALL BUY KENNY MERCURY’S NEW T-SHIRT! And, as I was getting to before, you can do so by going to my webpage! www.technicalwizard.com and purchasing a T-shirt for the low low cost of 99.99, not including shipping, and the proceeds go into the “Mercury’s Misfits” fund, which was set up for sad little broke orphan children living in Chicago area with the last name of ‘Mercury’ and first names beginning with ‘Ke’ and either ending with ‘Nny’, ‘N’, or ‘Nneth’. And now, its time to get down to business… [The Universal-tron fades off.] Kenny Mercury: The business being wrestling, and wrestling being what wrestlers do… and that’s what I am… and that’s what I do **grumble**except-for-last-SAS**grumble**, and I just so happen to be the best at. Now, as we all know, that the UWF booking team is a bunch of fruity nincompoops simpletons… and they really have NO idea about the fine art of good wrestling booking, but nonetheless I am somehow still contractually bound to wrestle in UWF and thus, I must appease the booking gods—I mean… booking Kenny Mercuries…and wrestle whoever the tell me to. And HEADLINING this Sunday’s Afternoon Supremacy… it’s going to be… The technical wizard! The ring doctor! The shooting star! The Mussolini of mat! The Kremlin of Canvass! THE FREAKING DEFINITION OF PRECISION!!!!! KENNY MERCURY AKA ‘GOD’!!!! In the MAIN EVENT! As I should be, by the way, teaming with the World Heavyweight Champion **cough**not-for-long**cough** Beelzebozo against some Scottish Scallywag and a disabled guy who smoked too much or something… Wait, Iron Bung? What or Who is this guy? Some kind of bad joke! Is he some disgruntled and hardcore aerobics instructor? Is he pissed that he had to teach Buns of Steel to the grannies at the nursing home and now he wants to KILL SOME WRESTLER’S FLABBY ASSES!? Is his catch phrase ‘FEEL THE BURN?!’ Buns of Steel… Iron Bung… Pft… Man, that’s the WORST pun I’ve EVER heard. At least ‘Beelzebozo’ is a funny pun… God-… Kenny-Mercury damnit!! [Kenny begins to pace back and forth across the center of the ring.] Kenny Mercury: Joshua Dunn… pft… What a waste of payroll right there. The man comes from a place where men where dresses! DRESSES! No girlie can defeat Kenny Mercury! And, according to his latest promotion I saw on UWF T.V., he spends his spare time hanging around playgrounds, trying to lure little boys into his car, promising them the ‘candy’ that he has hiding under his kilt. Well, Mr. Dunn, you may have candy under your dress… but I have C.D.’s! [Kenny Mercury raises both of his arms and flexes his biceps proudly. The members of the live audience look at each, puzzled.] Kenny Mercury: C.D.’s… See… These!! See Deez! [Mercury flashes his biceps some more as the crowd groans…] Kenny Mercury: Right, and with these, I shall vanquish Dress-up Dunn and the Iron Bung… You might have the THC champion on your side… but pft! As if I care! THC? La-de-freaking-dah! You have the champion of GARBAGE on your side… The THC title is crap… It’s not a REAL wrestling championship. I’ve said it before and I’ll say it again, hardcore is NOT and I repeat… NOT wrestling… it is pure and utter trash. I already bested the ENTIRE THC division, without utilizing the THC match stipulations that most hardcore wrestlers use to make up for their lack of talent and idiocy. I didn’t break the rules, I never picked up a weapon of any sort, unless you count these lethal weapons I’ve go right here! [Mercury makes a fist and smirks at himself…] Kenny Mercury: I’m not afraid of Iron Bung and I am definitely not afraid of the sassy lass, Joshua Dunn. With this said, what of Beelzebozo? What do I think of him, my very random partner for this match? Well… I don’t think much of him. Nope, honestly… I don’t. I don’t agree with his tactics… I told before I hate THC with all my body and soul, I freaking HATE clowns, and I think Pyretta is quite the little skank… BUT alas… with all aside, be he Gurgles or not, I must say, bravo, Bozo, on the title win. I was about freaking time someone got that belt off Sey-man… The guys a TV title contender at best! Purple Haze beat him! But seriously, Bozo, good job. And after Cookie’s death too… It must be a very hard and trying time for you, but the title win was superb and I couldn’t have done I better myself. I lied, I could have, but really…. Nice job. And so, for SAS, we will try and win this little tag team match we have here? Right? You see, I’m in the hunt for that very championship belt that you have around your waist, and beating these two nitwits will look very good on my résumé. You see? So, I know damn well that you are going to try your best, isn’t that correct, Mr. Bozo? You better bet your face painted tush you are… Or else, I’ll… I’ll… um… OR ELSE!!! [Kenny Mercury looks up at the rafters and smiles once again, just suddenly remembering something of interest to him.] Kenny Mercury: Oh yes… I almost forgot to mention this. You see folks, Iron Bung was a big star in a dead federation known as LOW… Losers Only Wrestling… Or so I hear. Well, Mr. Bung-holio, I know damn well that you’re coming into this match with a little freaking chip on your shoulder, because you’re the Bung of LOW, right? OF COURSE I’M RIGHT! Well, believe you me… That will not make any difference in the outcome of this match… Kenny Mercury and Beelezebozo shall reign supreme, and why? Why does your LOWliness not affect this match? Well… You see… I just so happen to be the proud owner of… THIS!!! [Kenny Mercury is handed something from a chubby UWF cameraman. He holds it up in the air for all to see.] Kenny Mercury: This! This…ladies and gentlemen… and metro-sexual Scotsmen and all Bung’s in attendance… is THE LOW World Heavyweight Championship of the WORLD! YES! INDEED! Well… it is made of foam and was bought for 30 dollars from a vendor at one of their shows… BUT none-the-less, it represents my accomplishment. I, Kenny Mercury, defeated Tristan Belmont in the Global Title Tournament last fall… The Tristan Belmont that just so happened to be the LOW World Champion… so, all logic tells us that I, Kenny Mercury, should have been crowned LOW World Champion… unfortunately those douche-meisters wouldn’t let me have what was RIGHTFULLY MINE… the LOW title belt… so I got this instead, as an everlasting token of my excellence! An excellence that never ends! Not even this Sunday, you lame-o ass-hat jack-illacs! For I and the Technical Wizard… The Shooting Star… THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION!!! I AM KENNY MERCURY!!!! [Kenny tosses the microphone over his shoulder, not giving it any more thought. ‘Stranglehold’ by Ted Nugent re-plays over the arena sound system as Mercury exits up the entrance… or would that be ‘exit’ ramp? Mercury smirks as he passes into the backstage area.] [End Transmission.]
