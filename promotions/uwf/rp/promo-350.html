UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #350

The Resurrection Part 3

17 Apr 2004

Digital Mortality — Digital's Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The stage has been set for what will be the biggest Sunday Afternoon Supremacy in a long time. The Main Event features none other than the Tag Team champions Mike Montgomery and Ezra Kazee defending against Digital Mortality and Moraless. You see, Digital not only brings fans to the stands, but he gives the UWF the spark it needs, it gives the UWF the excitment it needs to be one of the biggest federations of all time. It doesn't really matter who he is up against, Digital is the spark that will ignite the UWF back to it's status as the best damn federation in Primetime Central. Because that is what he wants for the UWF, that is why he is back, and now with his new partner in crime Moraless, it will be even harder to stop them. The resurrection has begun, are you ready to go?] [Mike Montgomery already tried to cheap shot the team of Digital and Moraless by trying to turn them against one another, but that just doesn't work. When two powers unite for some reason or other, it is easy to come between them, they only want power and money. Greed can kill us all, but this is not so with this team. They are not in it for the power or the money, they are in it because they are tired of this Circus that is the UWF. We have a clown as the UWF World Champion! Isn't that all the proof you need? The UWF is in a state of serious distress, and Digital is the help that it needs.] [Now Ezra on the other hand, goes against things head on, unlike Mike, who goes for the sneaky way to solve problems, and I respect that. The only problem is, going against a bunch of dolls won't help you much. Believe you me, Digital at least can hit, unlike those dolls. So if you think that is the only training you need, you are in for the surprise of your life. You will find out first hand why Digital Mortality World Champion, you will find out why he will get rid of the trash and clean this place up. Only one day away. In one day you will all see that Digital Mortality is no joke, and that he and Moraless are the greatest thing that has happened to the UWF in a long time.] (The scene opens with Digital Mortality working in the ring with Burton Bell. It is a big gym, there are a bunch of weights in one corner and people pumping up iron, there are punching bags, bicycles, and all the stuff you usually see in a gym. Right now, there are a bunch of people watching the wrestling ring as they see the guy who owns their gym wrestling against a friend.) (Digital punches Burton until he is back against the turnbuckle. He then gives him a huge elbow to the side of the face. He moves towards the turnbuckle again and is met with a kick to the face by Burton. Burton jumps to the top turnbuckle and goes for a flying clothesline, Digital moves out of the way. Digital then get his up, throws him to the ropes and clothsline hims hard. Digital gets him up again and does a sidewalk slam. He then goes for a leg drop that hits perfectly. He gets Burton up and Burton punches Digital. They trade punches until Burton gets the better hand. He runs back to the ropes and is met with a huge dropkick.) (Digital grabs him up and turns him around. He then grabs both his arms and twists them, and suplexes him but lets go. He pins Burton one, two, three. After the match the crowd cheer both men as they walk down towards two chairs set up right beside the ring. There are two towels and two bottles of water waiting for them right there. They both sit down, Burton is rubbing his head.) BB: Damn man, that elbow fuckin hurt! (Digital grins.) DM: Yeah, new to the arsenal. I'm trying to stay a little more on the ground instead of going up the turnbuckle everytime. You should not be so hasty. Wear the guy down before trying to go to the top rope. BB: Yeah... the same thing always happens. (Rubbing his head) DM: I have been trying a new move recently that I might also add to the arsenal, dunno when yet, but it might be a good idea to start soon. BB: How is it called? DM: Don't know yet. You will know in due time my young padawan. (Digital laughs.) BB: Fuck you! (Throws the towel at Digital.) DM: Anyways, did you see anything new this morning on UWF TV? BB: I saw that dude you kicked around, Raid, and i also saw the clown. DM: Damn, how I hate clowns! Since I was little, clowns have been freaking me out...(shudders) BB: Well, your fear of clowns better stop, because Beelzebozo is one mean mother. DM: Yeah, in my life, I have only seen two clowns wrestle, and they are both strong opponents, but I will worry about him when the time comes. Now I have to worry about Ezra Kazee and Mike Montgomery. They are the ones that can put a dent in my plans for the UWF, Mike being comissioner and all that beurocratic shit. Mike is nothing but someone who could never get power by fighting, he has to go looking for it by other means, just like that damn Jamie Shock. BB: Well, I think you and Moraless have it won already. DM: Yeah, I know. We just have a mental advantage that they could never have. When such a high profile wrestler like myself goes back to the federation that made him famous, he always has a kind of advantage, and when he teams up with someone who is not at the top yet, but talented nonetheless, people notice. You know, I never thought I'd be back. I was tired of all the crap I had to put up with and all the bullshit going on backstage. It was taking a toll on my personal live. That asshole Dunn... what he did to me and Justine, I wish I could just destroy him one last time. BB: Focus man, focus. Tomorrow you go against Mike and Ezra. Dunn will be left for another day. DM: Yeah I know. I can't wait till SAS, it's like my debut in the GIWF! This match I'm gonna remember for a long time. If you are a fan, what esle would you want? Digital Mortality and Moraless vs Mike Montgomery and Ezra Kazee. This will be the match that starts it all, you see, this will be thye match that starts the Resurrection of the UWF. This will be the match that gives the pulse back to the stagnant state the UWF is in right now. This is THE match. BB: Well, we better keep training. DM: True. Mike and Ezra, get ready, because tomorrow night, you will have to fight for your lives. (Scene fades.)
