UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #855

Contest of a Champion: Part One

2 Jan 2005

Seymour Almasy — Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seymour Almasy had failed. Down, down he fell, his screams silenced by Tsunami Oni slamming the trap door shut. Seymour could do nothing but wait, expecting for a great deal of pain as he hit…but none came. Instead, Seymour landed on a crash pad, hidden from the view of the audience. He ceased screaming on impact. He was motionless, not from pain, but from dissapointment. Seymour Almasy: Shit… Seymour’d had better weeks. He’d tried to burn down the UWF Headquarters, and take his own life. After failing at both of those, he went on to fail to defeat Tsunami Oni for the UWF World Title. He could hear fans leaving the arena, and resolved to stay where he was. He didn’t want anyone to see his broken, defeated form. And so he continued to lay there, on a crash pad. Alone. For an hour. Mercifully, by then, the fans had all left. He crawled out of his prison, and emerged into the empty arena. The ring was gone, as was all evidence of the UWF’s stay. Well, except for his things anyway. Seymour Almasy: Now, to get my shit, and get the hell out of here… Considering he had just been through a war, Almasy moved quite well through the serpentine corridors of the arena…before the ground promptly opened up underneath him once more, swallowing him whole. It all happened before he could scream. And then, he woke up. ---- Seymour Almasy: Unngghhh…my head. Seymour was lying in his bed, in his apartment. Everything was how it was supposed to be…save for the clawing feeling in the pit of his stomach. The Final Fantasy was hungry. He sat up in bed, and cast the covers off, revealing him in all of his Cactuar-boxer wearing glory. Standing, he began to make his way through the darkened apartment. Seymour Almasy: Where the hell’s the kitchen…? He groped blindly in the darkness, before finally managing to locate the apartment’s small kitchen. Cooking never was one of Seymour’s better skills, but he kept a well stocked refrigerator and pantry in case he needed to eat while home. Seymour Almasy: Mmm…here pudding, pudding, pudding.. The Final Fantasy opened the refrigerator, and pulled out a six pack of Jello pudding cups. Taking a dirty spoon from the sink, he quickly cleaned it with a quick rinse and dry with a paper towel, and was now ready for action. Seymour Almasy: And now, for the one thing that makes me happy anymore. Sweet, sweet pudding… One pudding cup was quickly opened, and devoured with equal speed. Soon enough, six emptied pudding cups laid at the former UWF Wrestler of the Year’s feet. Seymour Almasy: Mmmm… He shook his head. He was binging again. He’d been doing it semi-regularly ever since losing to Tsunami Oni at FANdemonium. As if that wasn’t bad enough, he’d been assaulted with the dreams, those damnable dreams that made him relive the ignominious moment again and again. Seymour Almasy: Come on Seymour, you need to focus. Retribution is at hand… All was not lost for the Final Fantasy. He had one more chance at Tsunami Oni and the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. All that he had to do was win the Contest of Champions….beat three of the UWF’s best in one night. And then…then he could have the rematch he wanted. Seymour Almasy: The Contest of Champions is my last hope. A chance for me to kill two birds with one stone. Number one, I can and will win this tournament, one of the few honors that I have not yet achieved in the Universal Wrestling Federation. And number two, victory gives me the title shot that I crave. A chance for vengeance against Tsunami Oni, in a match that I WILL not lose. He smiled to himself, and looked around. Save for the open refrigerator, the apartment was completely dark, just like all the others in the complex. He was alone, and hungry. Seymour Almasy: You know, it’s been ages since I went on a quest. Things were so much simpler back then…when I was getting a new sword…chopping off rabbit heads for experience points. It was a more innocent Seymour back then. A Seymour who truly loved what he did. Aerith and I had fun…but now, look at me. I have the PTC Global Championship. I’m PTC’s co-Wrestler of the Year. All the professional success in the world, with none of the personal. Finally, Seymour flicks the lights on. Light illuminates the kitchen of the apartment, allowing Seymour to take a closer look at the men who he must face in the Contest of Champions, names written on the official card sent to him. Seymour Almasy: This shall be the third Contest of Champions. An event that determines the next contender for the World Heavyweight Championship. But this year, it’s a lot more than that. Bracket A versus Bracket B. The old guard….versus the new. Despite being only 24, Almasy is acutely aware of his veteran status in the UWF locker room. Seymour Almasy: My round one contest at the Contest of Champions is against a man I know well, Kenny Mercury. Mercury and I go way back…he managed to beat me as Purple Haze, and in doing so dealt me my first loss. I got my revenge though…at the Primetime Invitational Tournament. Beat Mercury, and went on to beat Roulette and win the Global Title. And ever since that day…it’s driven you nuts, hasn’t it Kenny? The fact that I beat you, and have since gone on to more glory and fame than you could ever HOPE to achieve. It tears you up inside… The Final Fantasy shook his head. Even now, victory and fame were just pleasant side effects of his career. He fought because he enjoyed it. Because it was all he knew how to do. Seymour Almasy: We’re not that different, you and I Kenny. We each strive to achieve something we’ve never done, to strike a black mark from our records. You want to avenge that loss to me in the PIT. You were on the roll of your career at that point. You shocked the world by discarding Tristan Belmont like he was nothing. But then, you ran into me… He shrugs innocently, smiling. Seymour Almasy: But more importantly, you want the UWF World Title. A prize that has eluded you for your entire career. And were this Contest of Champions like the other two, I have no doubt in my mind that you would win it. But you see, Kenny, there is one slight problem. I’m in this one. This is my first Contest of Champions. My first chance to add this accolade to the others that I proudly hold. Almasy stands, leaving the kitchen in favor of his living room. He plops down on the comfortable couch, looking over at the long-idle sword laying in an glass case atop his big screen TV. Seymour Almasy: I…have been comfortable for far too long. Living the life of a complacent warrior has left me fat and out of shape. The time has come for me to take sword in hand once more. Seymour carries the cased sword off of the TV, and places it upon the carpet. He lifts the case carefully off, and the weapon of weapons is exposed to his view once again. Seymour Almasy: The Divine Blade Imperial II. Ah, there is still rabbit fur strewn over your blade, still blood from countless sources covering your fine metal. My friend, you have served me well. But soon, noble weapon, you shall have your final resting place. It is a new year now, and as such, I must prepare adequately. I must discover myself anew. The Final Fantasy sheathes the mighty Divine Blade Imperial II in its scabbard, and ties the weapon to his side. A quick glance at a nearby clock tells Almasy that it is 4:00 AM, a few hours away from dawn. Seymour Almasy: At dawn, my mission begins. A quest for myself. The Contest of Champions shall see nine men compete, nine men at the top of their games, trying to prove that they are the best at what they do. Eight of those men are prepared. I, however, am not ready yet. Kenny Mercury can brag as much as he wants. He can say everything he wants to say in an effort to convince the world that he is better than I am, but this is simply not the case. I am a better wrestler than anyone in the Contest of Champions, plain and simple. Now, I must set out to become a better person than all others. To find motivation beyond merely being the best. Everyone has that. It is the REAL motivation that drives us. Personal motivation. And it is this that I must find on my quest. My TRUE reason for wanting to win this tournament, a reason beyond Tsunami Oni, glory, and a World Title. Almasy unsheathes the Divine Blade Imperial II, raising it high in the air, and nearly striking the fan. Seymour Almasy: On what little honor I have left, I shall find that motivation. And I WILL come into the Contest of Champions, tearing through opponents until I am victorious. Nothing shall halt my quest now! Suddenly, the Final Fantasy frowns, and looks down. He realizes for the first time in several minutes that he is STILL only clad in his Cactuar boxers. Seymour Almasy: Correction. ONE thing can halt my quest. I can’t go out in my underpants! The PTC Global Champion retreats back to his bedroom. Much rummaging about can be heard, until Seymour finally emerges once more, clad in his traditional armor, sword more properly attached to his side. Seymour Almasy: NOW, nothing can stop my quest. It is time. In an hour or so, the lights of dawn shall appear, and my quest will begin. And once it is finished…there is no man in the Universal Wrestling Federation who can stop me. Anyone who believes otherwise? Almasy reaches threateningly for his word, before shaking his head and grinning. Seymour Almasy: They’re living in a fantasy world. He waits for dawn, pacing back and forth like a tiger, as the scene slowly fades.
