UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #493

A mean mean man

4 Jul 2004

Mr. Meaner — UWF Shareholder's Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up outside a shed. The shed is blue and has a UWF Shareholder’s Branch office sign out in front of it. The doors are closed, there is really not much happening around the shed. However, the camera pans outward, and there is smoke rising up from the back of the shed. It is a thick and heavy white smoke that is rising up and continues to rise up for several seconds. A man starts to walk around from behind the shed. The man is dressed in all black. Long black pants, long-sleeved black shirt. He stands about 6’4” tall and about 240 lbs. He wears a black ski mask over his head. He seems to be walking with a purpose and really doesn’t care about anything. He walks up in front of the camera and stares straight at it.) Man: What the hell do you want nimrod? You know, you UWF guys are freaking quick. I’ve barely been back in the fed and your already on top of me like white on rice. Don’t you think I have anything better to do with my time like kicking freaking puppy dogs or something like that? Hell, for that matter, don’t you have a little old lady to help across the street ya damn boy scout?…… I suppose though you want my thoughts on my match this Wednesday. You want me to tell you what I think about Kevin Starr and Malcolm Kennedy. You want me to tell you how I’m going to whip their ass from one post to the other and back again without breaking a sweat, don’t you? Cameraman: (his voice trembles a bit.) Yes sir, but I think you might want to introduce yourself first seeing as it’s been almost 4 years since you’ve been seen. Man: I suppose that would make sense. I mean we are talking about a bunch of low level idiots that this damn federation draws. 25% of them are illiterate, another 25% are drunk, and the rest of them are illiterate and drunk, we all know that though, don’t we. Lord knows they couldn’t pick up a magazine and read it if their life depended on it……. Anyway, my name, my name is Mr. Meaner. Who am I? Well, that’s just something you’ll have to find out on your own. I am one of the most ruthless people to ever grace any federation. I have been called cruel, sadistic, and just plain mean spirited. Well, guess what, they are all true. You don’t spend time at Alcatraz unless you are all of those. You don’t survive Alcatraz unless you are all of those……. Yes, I’ve been here in the UWF before. Or more specifically, I’ve been in the GIWF, it’s predescesor. I’ve held the GIWF TV Title twice. And lord knows that they had to hit me with a crowbar to take it out of my hands. Well, now I’m back here in the UWF, and punks, it couldn’t feel better. I’ve been waiting a long time to be able to step up into that ring and kick someone’s a$$. I’ve been waiting a long time to step up and do something to people that they deserve because well, they are just to nice to people……. It seems though that I’ve stepped into a bit of a gang war in my first match. I’ve got Kevin Starr, who is with the Players Club, and I’ve got Malcolm Kennedy who is with the Ressurrection. So the UWF wants me to be a gang buster, is that the idea here? You want me to go in and stop this entire feud between these two groups of no-talent fruitcakes? Not a problem, I can do that…….. Of course, it’ll be there honor to have one of me come in and do it. When you have a bunch of wusses and wimps, you have to expect that they are going to get their butt kicked all over and that’s what I’m going to do. I don’t care about your two little stables. I don’t care about your nice little wars that you are all having. I only care about one thing, coming in, beating on some people, and leaving. And that’s exactly what I’m going to do is come in, beat on some people, beat on more people, and leave. And you can all have an all expenses paid trip to the hospital. Of course the expenses are going to be paid by the UWF, but hey, that’s ok with me……. Fact of the matter is is that in the end, you have walked into the lion’s den on this one panzies. You have walked into a sleeping lion’s den and have woken up the lion. And this lion is grumpy when he wakes up. This lion is mean and ferocious. And this lion is going to take some heads and take no names because that’s what this lion does best….. And if you have a problem with it, well, that’s too bad, because your problems don’t matter to me. Your problems will never matter to me. So go whine to someone else, because sympathy is one thing I don’t have. (Meaner starts to walk away from the camera as it fades to black.)
