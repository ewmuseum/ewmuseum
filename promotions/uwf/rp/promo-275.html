UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #275

Debut

6 Mar 2004

Nick Persia — DelViaggio Restaurant

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Persia opens his mail that has just been delivered. There are serveral envelops but one specific envelope draws his attention because it is a big grey envelop. He takes it out of the rest of the mail. He opens the letter with his bare hands. He takes out the letter inside. It says that he should go to the DelVaggio restaurant where he is supposed to meet with a reporter from the Amsterdam Times. In addition to that the UWF feels he should introduce himself to the main stream public. He orders a cab and they drive to the restaurant. He meets up with the interviewe when he steps out] Interview: Hello, my name is Ben Johnson and I work for the Amsterdam Times. Nick Persia: Hello. I heard about you, and I am supposed to have an interview with you. So I suggest we walk inside. [ They walk inside the restaurant where an ober guides them to their table. They order a drink and kick off the interview right away.] Ben Johnson: What do you expect from your debut match in the UWF? Nick Persia: I expect some great action and I hope I can make an immediate impact. I have some advantage, but I feel I have more disadvantages because I have no home country match. I have to fight some obstacles like a crowd that is against me and off course I have two opponent instead of one. I can lose without being pinned myself. You see, I can tell a lot about myself here, and what I do on a daily basis but who gives a f*ck? I mean, the only thing what matters are results. You can win and you can lose, but it ain’t cheating unless you get caught. And I am fighting in that mtach for a win, not to lose. You see, I want to become the absolute best the UWF has to offer, period. The debut match is going to make the difference. I can either establish myself a winner or as a loser. Now if you leave me the choince I am walking out as a winner. And since I have my own fate in my own bare hands I will proove the world that I truly am the great Nick Persia. Ben Johnson: Well, you have some though opponent to beat though! Hunter: Like Bondage the Sex Friend for instance? He surely isn’t my friend. I know he can go in the ring, but isn’t the top. He can become the top if he wants too. Everybody can do that. I don’t know his history or his background and to be quite frank about it, I don’t care. You see, he might be a 100-time world champion, but if he’s high at the show and he can’t do shit in the ring I still win. It all comes down to the moment in the ring, and how’s the longest breath. And we have Adien Pryde. Confident man. But I am confident too. One way to settle things, and that’s the ring. I am a newbie in the UWF, but that doesn’t make me the underdog. Sonce he is so occupied by entering that all comers match. Go ahead, but pride goes before a fall. But you do what you think is best, and believe me you’ll learn of your mistakes soon enough. Especially of your biggest mistake till this very moment which is underestimating me. You can say all you want, but only the facts remain. I have no facts that I can come up with. I have no examples I can come up with. I haven’t lost nor won in this federation. I have not even had a single match.! So I am a complete stranger, like a John Doe with a name to them. Adien Pryde thinks anarchy will do the tricks? With anarchy anaybody can declare the champions, fools. I will not speak any further of your stupid behaviour because it is lowering my level. He has no time for me? I have plenty of time to handle him in the ring. Ben Johnson. What are you expecting of your UWF-career? Nick Persia: Like I said I want to make an impact, turn the UWF upside down and inside out so I will shake it in it’s foundations! I am here to stay. I promised the owner that I would bring all I have and more. So I will. I always keep my word, and if I don’t it’s too bad for him. Ben Johnson: maybe you can highlight some most of your memorable moments in this sport? Nick Persia: You are getting on my nerves now. I will speak slow and clear so anybody can understand and especially you, Ben. Well...for the record, I don't know of highlights to speak about...so you can immediately dismiss that idea! But just because I have been through a lot, I will tell you. Just because I am new in the Uwf doesn’t mean I am a newbie to the bussiness. I have already spent quite some time in this bussiness, but before I can run my mouth about my accomplishments I need to make an impact so they’ll believe me. They’ll realise it when it’s too late. I always go for a counter attack from behind. Sneaky, but more important, smart! Shall I put it another way? I'm a legend in my own right! I've done too much! What has Bondage the sex friend ever done? Not a lot, I'll care to bet! But take a look at what I've done! I stand before you as a former SDNL World Heavyweight Champion, AHWA Lord of the Ring, AHWA European Champion and two time AHWA Television Champion. There's only ever been a HANDFUL of people that have as many championships as I have. I mean real championships with some importance! Doesn't that say something to you about me? I'm a dominant force! I have a certain demeanor about me! And yet, I look at Adien Pryde or Bondage the Sex Friend and I think to myself 'My God! How?' Ben: How? Nick persia: Indeed, how? That’s the question I thought about for quite some time. Then the moment of realisation came and I knew it. He had not done anything worthy of rememberance at any time. He is a loser. He is a complete moron. Who calls himself Bondage the sex friend? He does, apperently. I don’t care about his name. I only care about my record, about my reputation. Ben: You basically say you only care about yourself? Nick Persia: Are you looking for some confirmation? Yeah, I do only care about myself. Why, you ask? I tell you why. Because I have no back up myself I won’t back up others. I have to take care out there for myself. For my own sake. For my own bestwill. I woulnd’t say I am egotistical, I would prefer the word preservation. Ben: With that said, with what mental condition are you going into this match? Nick Persia: With the everlasting spirit to prove myself off course! I have a great, non-destroyable will to prove myself. I am one of the few guys who learned how to work some decent matches. I said work, not doing some spots. Ben: You are also known as “the Invader”. Nick Persia: Yes, I am. I am called The Invader because I invade a company like UWF, and tear the whole goddamned place down. I will invade this company with only one goal. Total dustruction. Ben: And you are also known as The Fabulous Dutchmen...... Nick Persia: Yes, now tell me...are you going to give me some more fact or are you going to ask me some real questions here! I can’t believe you are an actual interviewer for the Amsterdam Times. You are doing a lousy job if it’s true. I would have fired your ass a long time ago if I was your chief. So I guess you have luck. Ben: So at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy you’ll show them what supremacy is all about? Nick Persia: No, I’ll show them what the Silent Assasin is all about. I will strike soon and swiftly. They won’t even notice that the attack was already launched until my hand hits his face. I am fast, strong and young. What doe sthe UWF need more? You’re right! Jobbers! You’ll give them a funny name liek Adien Pryde and Bondage the sex Friend and they’ll act like clowns. No, wait...that’s not right...they are clowns. They’re pathetic. Ben: You are bashing them, while you haven’t proved anything yet. Nick Persia: Mark my word. There ain’t no half stepping. I am going to shine luminous. Everything else is simply ludricous. [With that the screen fades into the SAS promo advertisements.]
