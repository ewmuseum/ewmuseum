UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #261

A Chair for Two

1 Mar 2004

Raid — Raids Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see the office that is infamously known as the headquarters for both Raid’s Extermination service and the local for the Chaos Society. You can hear a voice from the inside that is familiar, in fact the voice is recognizable as Raids. It sounds like he is either talking to himself or he is on the phone, because there is no one answering his questions/ comments. After a minute or so you hear his yelling loudly and then you hear the sound of something hitting the wall. The door abruptly swings open and slams against the wall, and then Raid comes rushing out the door way. He has a very serious look in his face as he rushes past the camera.) Cameraman “Raid, could we speak to you for a second?” Raid “ What the hell do you want? Can’t you see I’m busy?” Cameraman “We were just wondering how you felt about your match at the previous SAS and the one coming up?” Raid “Couldn’t it wait?” Cameraman “Well actually no, we kind of have a schedule we have to keep.” Raid “Well since you have already held me up this long I guess I could.” Raid “Lets see, where do I begin?” Cameraman “How about with your match with Bruce Gordy.” Raid “Shut up you ass clown! That was a rhetorical question! But that sounds like a good place to start. Ok this is how it is. He is one tough bastard and that’s the bottom line. He put up one hell of a fight, in fact for a little while I didn’t think I was gonna win. He took me places that I haven’t seen in a long time, and likewise I took him to the edge of his game. It felt good to see how far I can push myself. And from what I have heard he was once a hardcore champion! He was defiantly deserving of the hardcore championship, he has skill , determination and he is one BADASS MOTHER FUCKER! If your listening Gordy , I just want to tell you that we could use someone like you in the Chaos Society!” (Raid reaches into the bag that he was caring and pulls out a t-shirt. He unrolls it and holds it up and you can see large red letters on the front that say “CHAOS SOCIETY”. He turns it around and on the back you see a picture with Raid and Adien Pryde in the middle and on the outside of them you see Joanna and Reo Dai.) Raid “That’s right Gordy I want you in the Chaos Society. If your man enough to accept you know where to find me.” (Raid tosses the t-shirt onto his shoulder and starts to crack his neck.) Raid “And as for my match this Sunday, I am looking forward to it.” Raid “Iron Lung it seems that our paths are going to cross again. And again it involves the THC title. The first time we met you flat out kicked my ass. Yea that’s right I said that you kicked my ass, I’m not afraid to admit it. In fact I had it coming, I didn’t prepare like I should have, this time I won’t make that mistake again! You may have more experience than me and you may be the champ, but one thing that is working against you is that you don’t have to be pinned to lose the belt. and believe me I plan to use that advantage to the fullest. You also have another wrestler to worry about, so that means there is two people gunning for you. The first time we met you came out on top but this time I doubt that will happen, but in the case that you do win I’ll be coming after you! And you can count on an ass beating. So to you Iron Lung I say “good luck”, and your gonna need it!” (Raid then places his hand over his mouth and wipes it down his face, as if to indicate he is thinking hard. He then leans down to his bag and pulls out an article of clothing. It is black but is very ragged and looks like it has been on fire. As he unfolds it you can see large holes all over it.) Raid “Look at this! This was my favorite set of coveralls! But now there ruined because of some no talent monkey who thinks a table on fire is a good way to get my attention. Well guess what YOU GOT IT! And I’m gonna make you pay, Plaque you had better watch yourself this Sunday.” Cameraman “ Um sir, I believe his name is The Plague.” Raid “ Damn it don’t interrupt me. I don’t care what his name is, he ruined my best set of wrestling duds! And now he is gonna pay! And what was that about exterminating ME! If he thinks that a flaming table is going to be enough to put me away he’s got another thing coming. And what am I suppose to be learning? Seriously if I wanted to learn something I would go to a library, or maybe I would talk to ‘The Common Man” he is pretty knowledgeable. But no, this guy thinks that I learned something by that. Well I’ll tell you what I did learn, I learned that my supposedly flame retardant jumpsuit, wasn’t! And to top it all off he now gets a shot at the title. Well he may have got the jump on me this time, but this Sunday it will be a different story. You think you are hardcore Plague? I’ll show you what hardcore is all about!” (Raid goes off camera for a brief moment, and then returns with a chair in his hand.) Raid “This chair is a special one, one that I have used before in the past.” (Raid holds up the chair, and written on it you can see many names.) Raid “I have used this chair many times. And each time it is used to win a hardcore match the name or names of those I beat get added to it. As of this far all of the names have been of those I have beaten in the indys. This Sunday I intend to add two more names to chair. I will enjoy writing your name on it Plague. But for now your name will remain unwritten but come this Sunday if will be forever etched in the history books that I “RAID” beat the crap out of you. You talk about all of the championships that you have held, well do you have them now? I don’t think so, the title histories are just that HISTORY. Those titles you won don’t mean crap right now, yea sure you’re a former champ. But you lost them! All that means is that you aren’t that good because you lost them! It just means that you are human and can be beaten. And I intend to do just that. Granted it won’t be easy, but nothing that is worth having is easy to obtain.” (Raid turns away from the camera and picks up his bag, and begins to walk away. After a few steps he stops and turns head to the camera.) Raid “Iron Lung , Plague I’ll see you two on Sunday and you had better be prepared for the fight of your lives!” (With that he starts walking down the long hallway as the scene fades to black.)
