UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #623

RR: Learning to Party

13 Aug 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — The Limo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of a limo. Sitting in the limo is Fizbane the Fabulous. He is wearing a salmon leisure suit. He has his face painted and is sitting back in the limo. Sitting to his left are Aphrodite and Venus, and to his left are Athena and Freya. They are all wearing different color go-go dresses and everyone looks like they are ready to party. “Stayin’ Alive” is playing through the limo speakers.) Aphrodite: How goes it Fizzy? Fizzy: It’s one hell of a ride so far. And it’s going to continue to be a helluva party coming up soon. Venus: You mean tonight, right hon? Fizzy: Not at all sweet stuff. I’m talking about this weekend. I’m talking about the Regal Rumble. I’m talking about the party of the night and the night is going to be a big ol’ party. Athena: How can that be a party? Fizzy: Don’t worry; I understand your new hot thang. It’s always a party. Life is too short to not party. Freya: But it’s a match, your suppose to win it. Aphrodite: He can win it, but it’s a party. It’s all a big party. Life is one big party. Fizzy: Testify my sistah. You know my little chickadees; people get all worked up about this kind of stuff. They are all worried about winning the match and winning everything and going on to win the world title that they forget to have fun. Venus. And fun is so the essence of life. Fizzy: We’ve got a ton of cool cats in this match who are worried about one thing, and that is winning. They are so worried about winning that they don’t have fun in their jobs and they don’t have fun doing anything else, and that’s what this is all about. It’s all about the party and it’s all about the fun. Freya: And it’s a battle royal too. I mean everything is coming from everywhere. Fizzy: That just makes the party so much more fun heart throb. You see, your right, this party is a battle royal, but as the song goes. I’m gonna be Staying alive. (Fizzy breaks into song.) Stayin’ Alive, Stayin’ Alive, oo-oo-oo-oo-oo STAYIN’ ALIVE. And that’s what this battle royal is all about. And that’s what I’m going to do. Athena: Your right, this is going to be a party. And you are really going to get that party started. Fizzy: Now you’re getting it chickadee. This is going to be a party, and we are going to PAR-TAY ALL….. NIGHT….. LONG!!!!! (The scene fades to black.
