The Downside (This is my Intro RP, I posted it in the wrong place)

06/04/04

Gregory — Minneapolis, USA

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*There's a soft wind blowing north-east. A man in a blue vest walks up the concrete path of Gauntley Park, California. He stops and decided to take a look around. He smiles. What most people see in the position of this man are sweet little kids, adorable grandparents out for a picnic, still deeply in love, gazing lovingly into each others eyes. Of course, there is always one man with a completely different opinion. At this moment in time, this man's name is Gregory.* Gregory: There is always at least one time in somebody's life where they realise the biggest dream they have ever had. Some people live with it. They live in glory for a while. Sometimes they have it for a lifetime. But then again, most of the time it will be a few days, and then something will creep up and spoil it for them. It's like marriages. Sure, at first you're out for romantic dinners, people will die for each other when they are in love. But there is such a thing as change. There's no such thing as loving each other for the rest of your life. The man can see some other woman, he'll cheat on his wife, and it will end in tears. Of course, I am not talking about love. It's called a metaphor. What I am truly talking about is the average wrestler's career. Gregory: It is like the career of my fellow UWF wrestlers. I know that you OK wrestlers, I won't underestimate you. But it isn't as if you are something special, is it? You see, you have wrestled some matches yes, but you need to remember that these matches were all against nobodies. And whether you still actually won or not is another question. UWF, You should all know that I am your warning sign. For danger. I have been accepted into the UWF, and believe me anyone who crosses my path on my way to greatness will regret it for the rest of their lives. The Spear, my move. My most dominant force I hold within myself. Never, in all my wrestling career have a come across a man strong enough to kick out of a pin after my spear, also known as The Blade. And I know this will be true in the UWF aswell. I realise I may have to work my way up the UWF ladder for a while until I become known as a legend, but hell I'll do it. I know for a fact that I have the potential to do it. Now, I would love it if in my next match I could wrestle the World Heavyweight Champion. I would love the chance to be able to prove myself. But for now I can't. Someone ig has left the UWF. I missed my chance to wrestle him. Maybe he left because he knew I was coming. *Gregory chuckles to himself.* Gregory: Only joking. I know that I have a long way to go to prove myself worthy of a Main Event in the UWF, but I'll be damned if I don't do it at some point or other. *Gregory stops talking and stares into space again for about 2 minutes. Dead, brown leaves begin to flow along the ground, the grass. A child accidentally lets go of his kite, and he tries to chase it. The small child makes one final jump to grab the kite, but misses. He starts crying, and his mother comes and takes him away from the 'scary man on the bench'. About 10 seconds later, a 15 year old boy walks past Gregory with a fag in his mouth, and just glares at him the whole time. Gregory soon starts to speak again.* Gregory: Wow. I never realised how many things you could find to use as metaphors in as little as two minutes. Take the small child and his little kite. It's just like what I said earlier, and it represents what will happen to many wrestlers in the UWF. Think about it. A boy holding his kite closely, doesn't that sound like a wrestler holding on to his dream tightly? Yes it does. But alas, it doesn't last, the dream is broken, the dream is gone and that is exactly what will happen to any UWF wrestler in our match. Now think about that teenager with the cigarette. He stared at me, he obviously didn't like the look of me, he considered me a rival. Now why we do that, I do not know, nobody does. But we do it, and as far as I am concerned I am the same as him. Everyone is a potential enemy. Anybody you see could turn up in a few weeks and become your worst nightmare, your biggest fear, and lo and behold, I am your worst nightmare, UWF, your worst fear. Gregory: But being the better wrestler, as amazingly stupid as it sounds, can never guarantee you a victory. I am the better wrestler, of course I am. But I also have what you need to seal the victory, which what makes me the winner already. I have grit, I have effort, I have the determination and most of all I know my own talents, and I'm not afraid who knows it. I don't care if anyone thinks I am being cocky. I don't care if people think I have my head in the clouds. You want to know why I aint bothered? Because I know it is true. I think I am soon to be the biggest thing to happen to the UWF and I don't call it big-headed, I don't call it cockiness. I call it believing in myself. And whether you or anyone else likes it or not, believing you can do it, just believing in yourself is a big step towards actually proving you can do it. Seeing is Believing. And I have seen myself do it in other federations, so I believe it. This federation, the UWF, is my biggest challenge though. I got very easily bored in my last fed. 7 matches was all it took before I got my title shot I desired, and obviously I won it. And I was the champ for six months, and I was still champ when I quit a week ago. But I know this will be different. I know this will be a challenge. But I know I am still going to win. I don't care who they give me, I don't care what match stipulation they put on me, somehow, some way, I will come out the victorious one. Gregory: UWF, the federation where the biggest wrestler to hit wrestling was introduced. That will be a Hall Of Fame headline in the future. I will be remembered in the UWF Hall Of Fame in years to come. Yes, I can imagine that. UWF, you haven't been in reality yet. You haven't tasted the fear and destruction that is Gregory. I can spear you half the way to hell. I can end your career in one match. And believe me, I will try to do so as much as I can. It's time the UWF realised their fate. Sooner or Later, I will be the World Heavyweight Champion, and no-one will be able to stop me on the way to greatness, and that is one promise I won't be breaking! * This proves to be the last thing Gregory says, as he gets up from the bench and starts walking towards the black, metal gate that leads into, and out of, the park.*
