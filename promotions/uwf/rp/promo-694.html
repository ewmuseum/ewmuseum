UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #694

Getting...MEANEREREST~!

Sept 10 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — RP#3 and final for SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The time of reckoning had come. Seymour Almasy’s first quest since returning to the UWF had reached its climax. Soon, there would be a titanic clash between Mr. Meaner, the reigning UWF THC Champion, and Seymour, one of the UWF’s most indomitable superstars. Hardcore rules, anything goes. Needless to say, it’s time for the Final Fantasy to get mean again. Meaner. MEANERER! And if the former Alcatraz inmate is especially unfortunate, Seymour just might even become… The MEANEREREST~! ------------ Seymour Almasy: Mr. Meaner’s demise is at hand! Sitting backstage in his dressing room at Supremecy, the Final Fantasy appears to be extremely confident. Due to his status in the Universal Wrestling Federation, the Final Fantasy has a well-appointed dressing room, with a couch and television. On a small coffee table sits a bottle of sparkling apple cider, ready to be opened if and when Seymour defeats Mr. Meaner. Seymour Almasy: Meaner has no idea what is in store for him. To hear the man prattle on about his hardcore and mean accomplishments is to be put to sleep! With a shake of his head, Almasy continues, in a mocking voice: Seymour Almasy: “I’m Mr. Meaner, and I’m mean because I scare little kids by killing cows!” What a crock of shit! If you want to discuss psychological warfare with me, Alcatraz freak, this is one battle you’ll lose. Do you want to talk about psychology? Fine then. Almasy is seemingly prepared to launch forthwith with a vicious diatribe, but for some reason, he begins to feel reluctance. Seymour Almasy: Psychological warfare against children is easy. You’re facing off against inferior minds. And, your subject matter is simplistic. Everyone knows the way that animals die. I’ve seen whole Chocobos roasting on spits before. It can be heartbreaking, and yet, I can still eat chocobo. Roast chocobo, fried chocobo…all delectable delicacies. You frightened a bunch of grade schoolers. That’s not mean. That’s cowardice. That’s like stealing a lollipop from a baby, which is undoubtedly on your list of crimes, felon. Yeah, you’re so MEAN that you got caught by the cops. Congratulations! Almasy turns his attention over to the sparkling apple cider, and its sour goodness that Seymour can soon partake of if he defeats Mr. Meaner. Seymour Almasy: To be truly mean, you must break an equal. Leave him or her totally destroyed mentally, emotionally… Before he can help it, Almasy finally blurts out the thought on his mind, practically shrieking. Seymour Almasy: PSYCHOLOGICAL WARFARE IS BETRAYING YOUR OWN WIFE! It’s telling her that she’ll be in on the plan, all the while knowing that you’ll turn on her! It’s sleeping with her at your side every single night for two weeks knowing that it’s all coming to an end. THAT, Mr. Meaner, is psychological warfare. It may not be a crime to leave my wife…but the manner in which I did it makes any crime you could have committed in your life PALE in comparison. Even in bragging, more than a touch of remorse lies in Almasy’s voice. Instantly, Almasy tries to hide it, his voice adding a touch of steel. Seymour Almasy: Meanness isn’t simple physical violence. I know this. But, nor is it psychological warfare alone, as you seem to believe it is. The THC Championship requires a certain level of meanness. It requires one to be both sadistic and masochistic at once. It requires a competitor to enjoy dishing out pain, and revel in receiving it as well. It has been fought for in Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatches, golf courses, anything Slasher’s ever invented, and many, many others. It has seen a cyborg, a soulless being designed to inflict pain in Sirian, as a champion. You may indeed be mean, Mr. Meaner. And you may even be hardcore. But you clearly lack the capability to pick on those your own size, those bigger than you. In the UWF hardcore ocean, there has long been but minnows, while you have served as the shark. Now, however, Mr. Meaner…Emerald WEAPON is coming directly at you. And anything that happens in that arena tonight, there will be no remorse. No recriminations. No regrets. Once again, Almasy glances at the sparkling bottle before him. Shrugging, he takes a glass from next to the bottle, and pops the top of the bottle. Seymour Almasy: I was going to save this for after tonight’s match…but since it’s a forgone conclusion anyway… Almasy pours half of a glass of the sparkling cider, and places the bottle back down to the table. Seymour Almasy: Of all of the titles in the UWF, the UWF World Heavyweight Championship is undoubtedly the sweetest. As it inspires one to go after the big prize, the UWF Intercontinental Title is the saltiest. The UWF Television Title, due to its constant defending, is a bitter pill to swallow. But the THC Title…like my cider, is quite sour indeed. The Final Fantasy downs the glass of cider, his face contorting from the sourness of the drink. Seymour Almasy: To the layman, hardcore matches are sour proposals indeed. Few men are willing to enter a match with no rules. But, unlike bitterness, sourness is not an unpleasant taste. At first, it can be…but then, some individuals start liking it. Unfortunately for Mr. Meaner, I am one of them. Smiling, Almasy sets the glass down, grinning evily. Seymour Almasy: The Mount Gagazet Deathmatches, Ultimate Battle Arena…all have only served to whet my appetite for more hardcore competition. Three men have fallen already in my quest. One more will soon fall. Mr. Meaner is confident. Arrogant. Believing in his own abilities. But his belief will soon be shaken. Soon, he will understand why I am everything that I say I am. Why I am a former UWF World Champion. Ezra Kazee may be as delusional as I am. But for his most demented persona, Mr. Meaner…believing that he can defeat me is downright insane. Seymour raises his glass once more, and pours a second glassful of the sparkling cider. This glass, however, is not for drinking. Seymour Almasy: To you, Mr. Meaner, I propose a toast. To our impending battle. To our war over the Hardcore Championship. And most importantly…. Almasy wings the glass across the locker room. It strikes the far wall, before shattering, splattering the cider everywhere. Seymour Almasy: To your impending destruction. Hold onto your fantasies of retaining the THC Championship. Fantasies are nice, aren’t they? In them, anything you want can happen. But when the cold, hard reality of Seymour Almasy greets you in the ring, your fantasies will fade to ashes, as will your reign as the UWF’s king of hardcore. The former Global Champion cackles to himself, as the scene finally fades to black.
