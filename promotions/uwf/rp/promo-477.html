UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #477

Inner peace

29 Jun 2004

Joshua Dunn — Somewhere in town

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[His head felt warm, It was comforting to know, as the rest of him was as cold as ice, but he knew why it was only his head that was warm, and wasn’t as nice. Steadily Joshua Dunn came too. As he lifted his head the warmth faded away, like his illusion of comfort that went with it] [Then the pain hit him, his forehead feeling like several kitchen knives were being stabbed through it slowly. He raised his hand to try to sooth the pain, and it stuck there. He groggily took the fingers into his eyes view. He didn’t even need to let his eyes focus properly to see the congealing blood on his palm. His face scrunched up in twisted agony. The only question in his mind was “What happened?” He looked over to the passenger’s seat. It was empty. Something told him that that wasn’t right, but trying to remember hurt his head worse, so he didn’t bother. He got his bearings, he was in his car, and it was a little worse for wares, putting it lightly. He looked out the smashed windscreen and saw the trees. It brought it all flooding back to him, and he was swept out to sea] *** [Dunn tries to straighten out the car again, but he steers too much, just causing the swerve to be amplified forcing the car off of the road and onto the side embankment through the trees. With Jack screaming all the way, the car tires suddenly find grip in the sludgy mud of the grass around the ornamental trees, catapulting the car further into the trees. Joshua swings the wheel first to the left, and then to the right, and to the left again, somehow getting past several of the trees] [In the centre of the field of vision of the camera, eerily centered on the windscreen, directly in front of the car and fast approaching is one of the first planted of the trees, and there was no avoiding it, but nevertheless Joshua tried] *** [What he remembered didn’t phase him well, and an almost primeval sense of self preservation came over him and panicking, breathing so rapidly that he posed the danger of hyperventilating, Dunn struggled to remove his seatbelt and flung the car door open and rolled outside onto the moist grass. From the rain, which had long since stopped, the ground was basically mush. Looking around himself, and smelling the sweet fresh smell after a heavy rainfall, Dunn slipped and stumbled to his feet, his bloodstained shirt now also covered in the grey brown mud. Upon reaching an upright position, he leaned against a tree off to the right of the crash scene, looking around he can see in the twilight of day break, the speed way from which he careered off the road] *** [In the road a stray dog, mangy and soaked to the bone by the rain, has wondered onto the lane Dunn’s car is driving in, caught in the headlights, the retched creatures eyes are picked out by the dipped headlights of Dunn’s car. Mesmerized with fear it just stands looking at the impending doom coming towards it, stopping it from running away to the relative safety of the central crash barrier. Too late does it decide to make for that haven] *** [Convinced now that the speed way isn’t the correct way to go, Dunn runs towards the city block at the other side of the artificial wood. Perhaps it would have been in his best interest to go the other way, because standing at the edge of the speedway, jabbering quite hysterically into his cell phone is the new UWF reporter Jack Kipling. Looking rather unscathed from the experience he’s desperately making a phone call to try and save the life of the man that has just walked away] Jack: ….And then we sort of went through the wooded area and, and, and we hit a tree and spun around and hit another tree, now he’s lying in the car unconscious… do you think he’s dead? [Through the phone’s ear piece a calm, collected and assuring female voice sooths the edgy Jack. Undoubtedly used to this kind of phone call all the time] Voice: its ok, if you could tell me if you require an ambulance or you will be able to take him to the hospital yourself. Could you tell me if he still has a strong pulse? Jack: Just let me check… [Jack runs back to the car, cursing his own stupidity for not trying that earlier, dashing back to the car to find an open driver side door. He thrusts his head into the car, but no sign of the Wrestling superstar. All the blood drains from his face leaving a horribly pale complexion there; he lifts the phone to his ear again and slowly tells the person at the other end of the line] Jack: He seems to have… gone [Far away from the crash site now, Dunn it stumbling along the sidewalk, keeping himself upright by leaning against the buildings on his right, leaving a muddy stain marking his trail. The time is still relatively early; a distant clock chimes 6 o’clock, and not people see Dunn apart from some early morning Joggers and some all day grocery store keepers. Each one as they open up shop or run past give the muddy man with blood pouring down his face give him a strange look, but think nothing of it. Feeling a bit dizzy, Dunn stops to catch his breath and to think, letting his head catch up with his actions] [What was the last thing he could remember…?] [He was in the UWF arena; he was in a match… The Local NVA jumped him from behind and dragged him backstage… It’s nearly time for Stable Wars… and he doesn’t have a stable… he doesn’t have long to find one… hold on this isn’t quite right…] [He looked up, and on the opposite side of the road is a wooden fence and on it is a poster. Something drew him towards it, if he were to be asked afterwards why; he wouldn’t be able to answer. He crossed the road without looking, if it had been nine o’clock and not six he would have been in trouble, as it was, he was unhindered. He neared the poster and cocked his head to one side as he saw himself looking back to him. It was a UWF promotional poster, words saying “Stable Wars 6: The Players Club”. Underneath that were pictures of three men he couldn’t quite identify at the moment, and himself, in the centre of it all, looking out at himself with the dogged determination that he knew was his] [That’s when that block of the last two years of his communist beliefs came rampaging to the forefront of his mind. He could remember it all, and he wished he couldn’t. All the things he had done, it was despicable, and in fact it was worse, he couldn’t find it in himself why he would do those things. Let alone be communist. He fell to his knees, still transfixed on the image that showed him as the evil, conniving and manipulative man that he had become. He lowered his head in shame; he didn’t understand why this had to happen to him. Then the darkness of his mind reached out once more, and he remembered the pleasure he felt once inflicting pain in others, his glee in managing to make people work to his every whim while giving them the illusion of control. He raised his head in rejoice, with a sadistic sideways smirk that we all came to know very well] [He picked himself off the sidewalk and headed back across the road and in all of the two minutes that it took him to reach the other side, he relived the last few months of his career. His raw edge weathered and the Dunn we now know returned as he should be People’s champion and giant killer. Upon stepping foot on the other side again, he looked up and down the street looking for some place that was open, he found a convenience store with their offset sign clearly reading “open”. He wandered in from the street and walked up to the store counter, much to the distress of the store keeper] JD: Excuse me [He leaned down on the counter, looking the Asian man in the face] JD: Can I use your bathroom? [Not likely to refuse a man covered in mud and blood, but unable to articulate a response from his petrified vocal chords, he merely pointed a shaking finger towards the store toilets at the back of the freezer isle. Dunn followed the finger with his eyes before turning back to the store keeper] JD: Thank you [He pushed himself up from the counter and moved swiftly to the toilet door, soon wishing that he hadn’t as the wound on his forehead caused a brief spell of nausea and tunnel vision to come over him causing his pace to falter momentarily before it passed. In the toilet he switched on the light and took one good look in the mirror, no wonder the shop keeper had been so reluctant to speak, he looked like something from a second rate horror film. Blood ran through his hair and down his face, tinting his skin giving off the look of a Texas red neck. He turned on the tap, letting the water run out at a high pressure, until it was almost scalding hot, then proceeded to wash the blood from his face and hands, and the refreshing sensation that came with it was indescribable, but it made all the fatigue of the crash and the pressure of the upcoming stable wars tournament drain down the plug hole along with the now red water. When he next looked himself in the mirror, he saw the rather pathetic gash on his forehead; he thought it wasn’t possible for it to produce that much blood. He also saw the man he was gave a smile to his image in the mirror, and he liked what he saw. He had reached his apex, and he wasn’t going to change it for the world]
