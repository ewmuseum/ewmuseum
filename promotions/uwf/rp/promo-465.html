VIDEO..er... STABLE WARS...CORPORAL STYLE!

date unknown

Corporal Punishment — 235 Crawford

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Once again, the Corporal of Punishment has stood the test of time and defeated another young opponent. This time, the casualty was a person named Joshua Dunn. A man whose downfall was attempting to create a VCR out of a video recorder. Everyone knows that a VCR stands for a Video Cassette Recorder. A video recorder does not exist. It is a shame that the Corporal spent $199.95 plus shipping and handling to find out that a video recorder is just a box that plays video, WITHOUT a format to play videos. Oh well, this saga has not ended yet. But truly, there are more important things to deal with than a VR. Stable Wars is now upon the UWF. Four men are going to step up and attempt to take this award for 2004. Those men are Corporal Punishment, Seymour Almasy, Richard Ray Vaughn, and Kenny Mercury. A team who is now named Chrono-Cross... Why on Earth was that the name of choice, I'll never know. But the name is not as important as the product. And it could be a team comprised of two monkeys and a hamster, and with a single team member added on like the Corporal, you have a winning team. The Corporal brings success even to the lower depths of talent. The world has already heard from Ezra Kazee. He has the delusional pre-conceived thought that his stable THE PLYERS CLUB will be the Stable Wars champion. It is a shame to crush minds with logic, but the fact of Stable Wars being won by any team that has a member who can not learn the difference between a VCR and a Video Recorder.... why would you even consider a prize as grand as the SW title? Facts hurt sometimes. So it is time to step up and allow you the viewing audience, to observe the most sadistic man in this federation. We would ask for you to place your children in another room but the Corporal is feeling the need to let you bond with your children this evening. So expect the unexpected because... The CORPORAL has entered the building...) CORPORAL STYLE! *The scene opens up as the Corporal of Punishment sits in his kitchen table at 235 Crawford Street. In front of him is a dis-membered black box. There are electronic boards, fuses, wires and nuts with bolts lying across the table as well as on the kitchen floor. The Corporal is gazing at the debris while holding a screw driver to his temple. Corporal: I swear to GOD I am going to get this thing to work. Although I have no idea what I will do with it once I have it working... BUT THAT IS NOT THE POINT! DUNN SAID I COULD WORK THIS THING AND I AM GOING TO WORK IT BY GOD!! *The Corporal begins to dismantle more of the parts as the phone begins to ring. Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Corporal: I haven't had this much down time since Lincoln Logs were put on the market. Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Corporal: Now those were the days. I could take those little brown logs and in a matter of minutes, make a fort that a nuclear bomb couldn't tear down! Those things were simply a great tool for my youth. Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Corporal: That blasted phone! When will they ever leave me alone! Phone: ring---ring---ring--- *He looks over at the phone ringing and finally slams the screw driver onto the table. Standing up, the Corporal shrugs in disgust as he walks over to the phone and picks it up from the receiver. Corporal: Who is it! Kenny Mercury: Why it is the shooting star... the technical wizard... THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION Kenny Mercury! Corporal: Before you go on Ken, please do not do that Dixie cup thing. Kenny Mercury: The Dixie cup thing? What are you talking about? Corporal: You know, the thing where you pick up the Dixie cup and crush it in you hand while saying that the Dixie cup is 'you' thingy. Do not get me wrong, the intent to intimidate is there, but for god's sakes use a can or something. Do not use a flimsy cup made of paper. Kenny Mercury: You saw that... well you have to admit, it was a quality promo, and seeing as it was yours truly who created such a grand atmosphere in that promotion, I must say that the shooting star looked mighty impressive in that scene. Corporal: I am sure that Mr. ???? was deeply impressed. I for one do not like little cups with flowers on it.... come to think about that promotion, you were pretty much brilliant with that scene. Just do not fondle any paper cups with flowers on it while I am around. Fair enough? Kenny Mercury: Fair enough. Not that I really care about how you feel in this matter. I believe that you and I, along with two other men, have a series of matches together that are coming up. Stable Wars is here, and I wanted the opportunity to contact you and schedule some practice sessions where the entire world can watch in awe as I, the great architect of perfection can show off my superior talents.... Kenny Mercury: You of course can stand around and do what ever it is you do when someone like myself is in your presence. You can bow down at my feet, or break something. It really does not bother me in the least. I just want to get this ball rolling. Corporal: Yeah right. yadda yadda yadda Mercury. You have been sucking those temperature gauges way to long if you think that you are considered to be anything greater than my pinky. The fact is that this temporary team of wrestlers is only a great as I want it to be. You are nothing compared to me. You know it, I know it, and the rest of the world knows it. So as for bowing down to you, you are smoking CRACK boy. Corporal: Now we can talk about getting together IF you allow me to break some things. Things like video recorders. Things like pathetic jobbers. Things like wrestlers who attempt to step up to the plate with the Corporal. Those things I can do while you are prancing around in your girly clothing, and your freaking pajamas. Corporal: You do whatever you want... just stay out of my way. We can work together for the common cause of stable Wars, but you remember that you are not talking to some freak like Mr. ???? or Ezra, Dunn, Digital, Maraless, Bondage, Kennedy, Raid, Pride, Draco, Star, Song, Trang, Communist, Mori or Persia. Did I miss any of those jobbers? I didn't think so, so don't answer that question. Corporal: You are talking to the greatest enforcer of our entire lifetime. And I will put the lethal injection on anyone who attempts to screw with me. That includes you or anyone else BIG enough to try and stand in my way. Kenny Mercury: Please stop boring me with your semantics Corporal Punishment. I will gladly give you the pleasure of making the biggest mistake of your life. You ever decide to lose your career at my hands, simply give the word. But for now we are a team. And I refuse to allow our tenacious appetite for success to cloud our ultimate purpose for Stable Wars. Corporal: Victory. Kenny Mercury: Yes victory. So, shall we meet Tomorrow? Say 9 am in the UWF Chicago Towers. The entire team will be there. We can go over our game plan and decide exactly how to approach our opponents. Corporal: Sounds good to me. I will be there. *Kenny Mercury ends the phone conversation without saying goodbye. The Corporal slowly lowers the phone to it's receiver, and then walks back over to the pile of electrical parts lying on the kitchen table. Corporal: I guess you will have to wait Dunn (VIDEO) Receiver. I need to get packed for Chicago. *The Corporal turns away from the table and walks towards the dinning room. The phone begins to ring again. Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Corporal: Not again. Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Corporal: If this is Kenny Mercury again, I swear I am going to... Phone: ring---ring---ring--- *He walks over to the phone and answers it. Corporal: Hello. Seymour Almasy: This tournament is what is left for me Corporal. I needed to speak to my fellow members prior to entering the arena for battle. I hope you are ready for what awaits us Corporal Punishment. Joshua Dunn is a fierce opponent in times such as these. He has defeated me in the past, and my opportunity for redemption is near. Seymour Almasy: Raid too is a large man. Capable of destroying the average mortal with one blow. As I have also seen in my prior confrontations in the UWF. His poison attack is a brutal one. And our union must stand strong when we face these and other adversaries. Corporal: You must be Seymour Almasy right? Seymour Almasy: You are correct in your thinking Corporal Punishment. I am he who comes from afar. I have traveled a very long distance to rekindle my experience with the UWF. Corporal: I have heard of you before. You were a champion here in the UWF. In fact I think you lost to a freaking chimp. *The Corporal begins to chuckle. Seymour Almasy: 38 days I held the UWF world championship. I believe that is 38 more days more than you Corporal, and the fact is that I defeated a chimp for that title. I lost to Ruff Ryder. *The Corporal quickly becomes serious as he continues his conversation. Corporal: Fair enough there Seymour. You make a good point. Still I have never lost to a monkey and Ruff Ryder is the closest thing to a monkey... unless you count a Canadian as a monkey. Then I understand how you feel. Seymour Almasy: However Corporal I then recaptured this coveted title from Ruff Ryder, and for 138 days I held that belt again. These events in my history are proud moments in my life. I shall cherish them for an eternity. However Stable Wars must is the greatest accomplishment left for me at this current time. The ability to take four men and have them fight as one is an extraordinary feat for any stable. And this week, we shall hopefully climb to that pinnacle and embrace victory. Corporal: First off Seymour, I do not understand a single word you have said except Stable Wars and title. Corporal: Along with those jobbers you have named. I for one have already destroyed those wrestlers you have mentioned except for that Insecticide in a can. Corporal: they don't bother me in the least. In fact, they are terrified at meeting me in the ring a second time. Corporal: But as far as you and I working for the common cause of Stable Wars, never fear. I am going to exceed everyone's expectations at this years Stable Wars. You can count on that. You just pick up the pace and be responsible for yourself and we will do fine. I am just not willing to carry you or anyone else this year... unless I get all the credit for destroying my opponents, and then if my destruction of everyone in this event causes our team to become victorious then so be it. I am just wanting to get in there and fight for the cause of MARTIAL LAW. Corporal: Have you heard from that annoying Kenny Mercury yet? If not, he wants our team to meet him tomorrow at the UWF Chicago Towers. So throw out some pixy dust and wiggle your nose to get there on time. I do not want to have to wait on the GREAT rodent killer because he is out there making little chirping noises with the squirrels in the trees. Seymour Almasy: Excuse me? Corporal: SORRY, GOT TO GO! I HAVE A SCHEDULE TO ATTEND TO! BYER BYE NOW! *The Corporal slams the phone down without hesitation as he shakes his head. Corporal: I never have understood those D&D freaks of nature. They want to float on air, walk on water, control the elemental makeup of the universe. When all you need is a very big stick! If your stick is bigger than everyone else's, then you will have no problem making them do what you want them to do. Who cares about who can make the best potion IF you punch it out of their mouth before they drink the darn stuff. Corporal: Well, again I need to go and get off to Chicago... Phone: ring---ring---ring--- *The Corporals mouth drops open as he watches the phone ring again! Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Corporal: SCREW THIS! I have more important things to do than talk on the phone all day! The answering machine can pick up... I wonder if the video recorder is similar to the answering machine... hmmmm. *The Corporal walks away as the phone continues to ring, and the answering machine picks up. Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Phone: ring---ring---ring--- Answering Machine: 'Hello please leave a message after the beep...CORPORAL STYLE!' ... ... Answering Machine: 'BEEP!' Richard Ray Vaughn: Hello? Is any body there... I would like to order a large cheese pizza and a coke!... Hello? Is anyone there? HELLO...
