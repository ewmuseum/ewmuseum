Chronicles of Persia, part 1

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When I woke up this morning I felt something I could not place. I didn't feel happy, nor did I feel statusfied. I had enjoyed a full night of rest. Most of my wounds are healed by now. The wounds on the outside at least. But from the inside I feel the everlasting darkness getting a hold of me. I feel empty in some way or form. I feel like I am missing something. I know I had a dream which warned me for something or somebody, but I cannot remember it. All I can remember is that I was happy winning my GTT 4 Round 1 match against Antonio DeMarco. On such a big stage I performed quite well, given the circurmstances. However, I wasn't at my best. That's what I believe at least. I cannot seperate truth from lies right now. My mind is a chaos. I have had blasts from the past. They all looked randomly picked. I saw myself as a little child laying in the arms of my mother. I saw myself travelling in a plane. Each of these memories made me emotional. Not that I was about to cry, but still, the memories touched me. I do not understand why I am having so many flashbacks, since I normally am focussed on the future instead of the past. Normally I can't remember I have dreamed. But now, coindentally, I remember I have dreamed and I have flashbacks. Is this is God's way to tell me I need to watch out? I just don't get it. But then again, I don't have the power to understand much. I can see why I am doing most of the things I do. But sometimes I get a strong feeling I need to do something. Just like somebody forces me into doing it. Well, I guess that is what I am feeling right now. That's what I am doing right now. I am on the road right now, heading to the Chigago airplane. It's fairly quiet on the road, but it's early in the morning still so I don't expect it remains like this. The radio is on, blasting through the speakers. Ever heard that song from Bomfunk MC? It's called Freestyler. You should really listen to it as I absolutely feel it rocks. The song has been on a high and on a low for the past 4 years. It's been number one in the rankings, but there are times you don't hear the song in like three months. A strange thing. But how would you define strange? Strange is something that isn't normal for you. I have different habits then somebody from China. In a reversed situation that persom from China would call me strange. So strange is normal and normal is strange. Would be a nice hypothetical theorem. But then again, you could do that with every word. Everybody sees the world in his own unique way. I look at the words as a place where we meet each other. One common place where we all come. Maybe that is our dream. I guess you can't remember what you did now in your sleep. So how do we know this is real? Just one of the many thoughts I'm having. I see all together. I can't think clrealy. What I do is what I feel is right. Does that make me a good person? No. Does it make me a bad person? No. We are forces of nature. We don't know what's good or wrong. Who are we to decide that? In our arrogance we became blind. Now we don't see much. Lies and deceit is all we have. But as I was telling you, I was on the road, listening to that damn good song. I turned off the highway riding straight into Chigago. All of these high buildings I see getting bigger and bigger each time I get closer. After all these time I spend here I still can't get used to these high buildings. They intimidate me by their size. I can't imagine that anybody else likes them. The skyscrapers aren't very colourful as well. They look boring and sober like they didn't want to brag about it while in reality the height resembles how rich someone is. Oops, again off topic. Anyway I drove on and I turned after a good fifty meters to the left. The signs for the airport already appeared for me at my right side, but I don't need them since I have a navigation system in my car. Not that those always work, but hey I got to spend my money on something. Finally the airport was in the action radius of my eye. It was much bigger than any airport in Holland, but for the American standard it was not the biggest. But in America, anything needs to be big. Nothing can come in small packages. Take the McDonalds for example. You can buy here in America a liter of coca cola! No, I don't mean to make a commercial here, but it's just not normal. I am not suprised that most Americans weigh too much. Watch your health is what I think to myself when I see some fat bastard walking out of the fastfood branch. I park my car and I step out of it. The parking lot is so big it reachers as far as the eye can see. Not suprised by this anymore I walk into the airport. When I come in I notice that there's a very big line standing in front of the counter. With the knowledge I have to wait a very long time I know for sure that my mood will not be good by the time I'll check in. The time passed by like it normally does. Second after second, minute after minute, and hour after hour. So after a good 2 and a half hours I'm able to check in. 'Can I see your papers, please?' the lady behind the desk asked with a nice, gentle tone. I handed my passport and my ticket to her. 'Here you go.' I replied. She opened the passport and took a look at it. Then she looked up to me. 'If you are from Europe you have to check at the second encouter to your right, sir.' she stated calmly. I was already in a bad mood, and I became a bit more agitated then I already was, but I remained nice and friendly. 'Madam, if you'd be so nice to check me in here I would very much appreciate it.' I suggested to her. 'I know who you are, Nick Persia! I've seen you on television many times. But if you thik you can persuade me with one of your famous love escapes you are at the wrong adress.' She suprised me with that sudden change of attitude. Before I knew it, I made a very stupid comment. 'Right! And the next thing you are going to see to me is that because I am a star I won't be helped because I would have an ego then? While the common man would have been helped?' She didn't reply to me verbal, but I felt two hands on my shoulder. I turned around and saw two strong guards standing behind me. 'We wouldn't want to make a whole story of this now, would we?' the man said with a smile 'So do what any man would do and just wait for your turn in that line over there.' He pointed to an equally large line I had just been in. I was about to explode, but I bit on my tongue and just walked to that line. I knew it wouldn't make any difference if I complained. I would only give them the pleasure of kicking me out. That's typically America. If you are a star you get much attention, but also a special threatment. Just because they're prejudiced you get to hear I have sexual escapades! That's news for me as well. I mean how easily can someone be influenced by the media. Americans take anything from their own natives, but if an immigrant says something it should be investigated. They are their own sort. They have their own habits. Gosh, they are so frustrating. I spend all my time cursing Americans in my mind. This time I am helped properly and they tell me to go gate 5. I know why I was helped properly by that nice lady. She was from Europe. I could clearly hear her accent. If I'd to bet I'd say southern germany of nothern Switserland. Somewhere near the rampart though. I have a different mentality. And that's what seperates me from the Americans. I take my seat in the plane, half conscious of what happens and half lost in thoughts. I am collecting my thoughts about what I should do. Destiny is at hand, like it always has been. Thomas Harris once wrote that you should think to yourself that everyday is your last. Something you didn't expect will come as a welcome suprise then. I don't really believe in afterlife, but I think he has a point though. Why plan your future when you posses the knowledge that everyday could be your last? The answer is that hope remains. Even in the latest hour. Hope is something human's created just like God. In desperate times humans do not fight but run. It's in our nature that we fear the unknown. And suddenly I know what feeling I had just this very morning when I woke up. I feared the unknown. When it hit me was obvious, but my thinking process was clearly different. Was this the work of God? No, it was just something I could not understand. And every human does it when he or she doesn't understand. They claim it's unreal ot it's the work of God. Why not investigate this? Why not believe in our own talents? Why wouldn't this be a suprise? I gathered unconsciously all my thoughts and forged a solution. How I do not know, but I know I did. Inside my head questions raise. That's called curiousity. Every human is curious. But what we do not know we fear. Why? Eventually we will find out since destiny is at hand. The logica can't be denied, then why fear? It's in the nature of a human being. Why keep repeating? Why getting back to this one frustrating line? Why keep on concluding the same things? Why, why, why? I couldn't understand what I was thinking. 'Hey you.' a guy yelled in my ear so hard that I was scared stiff. 'Can I have your autograph?' he continued. 'Huh? What?' That were my instant replies. 'Oh right..a minute please.' I shook my head and stretched my arms. I felt the blood flowing trough my veigns. I saw a little boy sitting next to me, wearing a UWF T-shirt. 'Do you have pen and paper?' I asked the boy. He smiled at me. 'I only have a pen, but you can sign my t-shirt.' In his eyes I detected happiness. His mind was unspoiled. His toughts were so pure. The words of the child were innocent. He said what he thought. Such a strange fate that we all change from the most honest creatures to the most despicable liars. 'Oh no, not that quick.' I said 'What does your mother say about this?' 'My mother concurs with it.' the young fan answered. I took the pen and signed my autograph on his shirt. 'Thank you.' was all he said and off he was. Totally happy with his autograph. He brought a smile across my face and he delighted me. I cherished the feeling for the small moment I had it. Welcome to the madness. But do not forget..... It was just a new old day!
