UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #729

A Few Things

2 Oct 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up within the friendly confines of the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Sitting there in his chair behind his desk is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. The room itself is fairly bland. And there is not much going on around there. He is just sitting there staring at the camera.) Kazee: Good evening everyone. I hope y’all are having a wonderful day. Today we are going to talk about several things. We are going to talk about Live and Loud. We are going to talk about Digital Mortality. We’ll talk about the upcoming UWF video game. And we might even touch on Mike Montgomery, the chump……. But I’m sure y’all want to know first of all is what is this about a Video Game? Yes folks, that’s right, the new UWF Video Game scheduled to come out on November 2. UWF: Insane Lunacy is the newest title for Video Entertainment. Bring all the chaos that is part of the UWF into your own home…….. All of your favorite wrestlers will be there. Joshua Dunn, Digital Mortality, Raid, Nick Persia, and Moraless. Also included in the game are UWF legends such as Beelzebozo, Toxic Shock, Seymour Almasay, and Curtis Rains. The create-a-wrestler mode is so in-depth that you can customize your wrestler as far down as each and every freckle on their face……. With the career mode as well as the ability to play just about all matches you’ve ever seen in the UWF, this title is a must have for any fan. You can play Joshua Dunn vs. Seymour Almasay in a regular singles match or you can play Anarchy vs. The Legends in a Venetian Home Rules match, which is brand new to this game. This game is going to be out for all systems, including your home PC for only $59.99…….. But enough of that information. How about we get to Live and Loud. A solid card up and down even if I do say so myself. And when we are looking at the card, there are to things that need to be pointed out. First is the Montgomery vs. Oni match for the World Title. Well, while I don’t like to wish anyone anything harsh, I do have to say that I hope that Oni comes out of this match as the World Champion…… Montgomery is nothing more than a paper champion. He doesn’t deserve the belt. He doesn’t deserve to be where he is. And he sure as hell doesn’t deserve to be headlining the biggest PPV of the year. It’s just because of his power that he is there, and fact of the matter is in a fair match, he can be beaten, and Oni is just the man to do it. Oni, I do wish you all the luck. I know you don’t need it, but just for arguments sake and not saying this match is a push over, which it is, I’ll wish it anyway…… But the real reason I should be sitting here saying anything is my match. My match vs. Digital Mortality. My bane. At one time my downfall. The man who cost me everything at one point and now I get another shot at him…. I have been waiting for this day. I have been waiting for the day that I am able to get back into the ring with you. And I can’t wait to face you once more. I can’t wait to go one on one with you. You are no doubt a legend in this business. You are without a doubt a man who is able to make anything they want happen. And you are without a doubt one of the most talented individuals in the UWF…… I relish this match. I relish my chance to go one on one with one of the greats in the UWF. I relish the opportunity to avenge my losses and to prove that I deserve to be at main event level……. There is a lot riding on this match for me. My silencing the doubters. My chance to get back up to the World Title level and finally achieve every wrestlers dream in the UWF and finally prove that sportsmanship and being nice does have it’s advantages……. Digimon, this match, isn’t totally non-personal. You did take the Tag Titles away from me. You did cost me several other opportunities. This is my chance for revenge. T his is my chance to pay you back, so to speak, for relieving me of my belts at a time I wasn’t ready for. This is my match to avenge myself for past transgressions at your hand. And I can, and I will….. Good Luck Digimon. I know this is going to be a great match, and I am ready. Are you ready for me? (Scene fades out.)
