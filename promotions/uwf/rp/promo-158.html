UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #158

The War of Words

18 Dec 2003

Ezra Kazee — The Mall

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera fades in onto an area with lots of people who are just milling around. It looks like the camera just opened up in one of the main hallways of a shopping mall. Lots of bags are being carried; lots of store signs are around. Sitting on one of the benches there is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He has a few bags next to him of various colors. He himself is wearing a heavy winter jacket signifying that it is winter time. The jacket is green and gold. He is also wearing a hat with the UWF logo on it.) Kazee: The time is drawing near to the contest of champions. And everyone is waiting for the time. People are expecting a great card. People are anticipating the start of the show, and they are already paying their money to the TV companies for the chance to view the greatness which is known as the Contest of Champions…….. And everyone looking up and down the card is continuously saying the same thing. How in the world did Dunn and Kazee get each other in the first round? What happened and whose master plan is it to do so? Is the UWF brass really that hell bent on having the best match first?..... Well, of course we know the answers to all of these questions. It is really quite simple as anyone who has been paying attention can tell for sure. This is all part of the master plan of the Prime Alliance. You can’t tell me it’s not. Look at how the brackets are set up. Not only do Dunn and I face each other in the first round here, but if Ruff Ryder and Malcolm Kennedy meet, it won’t be till the finals. Does anyone else here see the scheme of letting Dunn and I soften each other so that the Prime Alliance has an easier time? Does anyone else feel like a bright egg here?...... At this point though the bitching and moaning doesn’t really matter anymore. We just have to go out and do what we need to do. Dunn and I both know this. And well, if the Brass wants the first round match to be the best match in the entire PPV, then so be it, myself and Dunn are up to the challenge. We will go out there and tear up the show, because hey, that’s what we do best. It’s just a natural order of progression when seeing where the talent is, and the talent is in the first match…….. But you know, I really shouldn’t focus totally on the first round match, should I? I mean there are 6 other people in this thing. I mean in one of the matches you have Moraless vs. Fagan Gilchrist. Well, Moraless, I don’t know much about you, but I do know a little about you Gilchrist. And you are going to go around and call me a no-hoper? Well, I guess that’s your opinion, but the fact of the matter is that hope is one thing that I always hold near, and will always hold near. You can continue to run your mouth, but in the end, it’s either this no-hoper or Dunn who is going to be facing the World Champ…… Another match that there is Beelzebozo vs. Ruff Ryder. I know a little bit about both of you two. Ruff Ryder, you yourself are a man who has no idea as to what life is all about. You claim to be the Cherokee Thrilla, but yet you turn your back on you so called Native American roots. Your cheer for teams like the Kansas City Chiefs. There is no self respecting Native American that would cheer for the Chiefs. There are only a few things worse than that, but you may as well have denounced your heritage by doing that. I don’t know how your Prime Alliance got it arranged, but you have a nice little route, don’t you…… And Beelzebozo, what can I say about you. Your style reminds me of another great, reminds me of a man who I have beaten before in a match for the GIWF TV Title. Of course that was before you went on to other things, but that doesn’t matter. Your style, is so familiar, dare I say it. I know you and where you come from. The psychotic circus seems to have lost another one. First it was Gurgles, now it’s you. It doesn’t matter, you can talk all you want, but in the end, you will be left like all the rest……. And finally, there is Laguna and Malcolm Kennedy. Laguna, I don’t know much about you. Your training techniques are quite impressive though. How you get all of those bunnies to follow your lead, who knows. Maybe you could have all of those bunnies jump into a nice hot boiling pot of water. Some rabbit stew does sound kind of good right about now. Especially with the chilled weather that has taken over the state of Minnesota…… And Malcolm Kennedy. How you got yourself back into this thing I don’t know. You lose one match and somehow are able to run down and pin someone in another match to that you are not even involved in and win yourself the TV title and earn a spot in the Contest of Champions PPV. I don’t know how the Prime Alliance swung this one either. How in the world do you do it? I guess it helps to have friend in high places doesn’t it? Well, we’ve beaten you once, we’ll do it again……. See, al of this talent, it doesn’t matter. Dunn or I, we’ll find a way to win this thing. In the end, it won’t matter. It’s time for the Player’s Club to take over here, and that’s exactly what we are going to be doing. Like the great phoenix that rises from the ashes, the Player’s Club is doing the same thing and it will continue to do the same thing and scorch the rest of the UWF as the lava that the phoenix raises from does to the land that it sits on……. Good Luck to everyone else, you’ll need it. (Camera fades out.)
