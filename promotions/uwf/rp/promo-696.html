UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #696

Fantasy and Reality

11 Sep 2004

Mr. Meaner — Ground Zero

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up near the site of the 9/11 attacks. Standing there is Mr. Meaner. He is not dressed in his normal black, but is instead donning the colors of the American Flag. From head to toe he is nothing but Red White and Blue. Next to him is a television cart complete with TV and VCR. Mr. Meaner looks is looking at the site of the attacks before turning to the camera and just looking out toward the camera.) Mr. Meaner: I have only one request at this point. That request. Stop it. (Mr. Meaner turns on the VCR, which comes to life with a section of one of Seymour Almasay’s promos,) Seymour Almasay: I’d love to see you survive for a DAY in the D-District Prison, toughest place in all of Galbadia! (Mr. Meaner pauses the tape.) Mr. Meaner: Stop It!!! (Mr. Meaner turns the tape back on again, with another section of Seymour Almasay’s promo.) Seymour Almasay: I’ve seen whole Chocobos roasting on spits before. It can be heartbreaking, and yet, I can still eat chocobo. Roast chocobo, fried chocobo…all delectable delicacies. (Mr. Meaner turns the tape off again, this time pushing the entire device over and thus destroying the TV.) Mr. Meaner: STOP IT!!!!!! JUST FREAKING STOP IT ALREADY YOUR DAMN NUT!!!! DON”T YOU UNDERSTAND THAT YOU ARE LIVING IN A FANTASY WORLD!!!!! DON’T YOU UNDERSTAND THAT NONE OF THAT IS REAL?? DON’T YOU REALIZE THAT THIS IS ALL JUST A FIGMENT OF YOUR IMAGINATION?!?! Of course not. Why would you? Why would you understand any of that? You wouldn’t understand it if it bit you in the butt, would you. So here, why don’t we just explains things for you a little bit…….. (Mr. Meaner pulls out an atlas. He starts flipping through the pages. He keeps flipping and flipping. A man walks by.) Hey You. Man: What the hell do you want? Mr. Meaner: I want you to do me a favor. I’m having trouble finding a place in this atlas, would you mind looking for it for me? Man: Screw you, do it yourself. (Mr. Meaner pulls out a lead pipe. And points it at the man. The man’s eyes get big.) Mr. Meaner: I’ve got a better idea. How about I let you screw this by shoving it straight up your ass. Man: No, that’s ok. I’ll do it. What are you looking for? Mr. Meaner: I’m looking for Galbadia…… (The man starts looking through the atlas.) And while you’re doing that, I’m going to look in here for Chocobo……. (Mr. Meaner takes out a book of species and starts paging through it. After a couple of seconds he looks up at the camera and throws the book away.) See, I told you, you can’t find it in there. There is no such thing as a Chocobo. They are microchips that some guy made up with. It’s a fake overgrown chicken that doesn’t exist. There is no way you’ve ever eaten Roasted or Fried Chocobo. You see, the C in KFC stands for Chicken, not Chocobo. They don’t exist. (The man taps Mr. Meaner on the shoulder.) Man: Um sir, there is no such place as Galbadia. At least not that I can see. Mr. Meaner: Thanks now get lost…… (The man starts to run away. Mr. Meaner just starts to laugh.) You spent time in a fake prison in a fake city eating fake food. Just further proof that you are, without a doubt, a fake. Your life is all fantasy. Your life is nothing more than an imagined world that will never exist. And your dreams of taking my THC Championship from me are just as much of a fantasy as your life is……. And why the hell do you keep referring me to a persona of Ezra Kazee you dumb ass. I have no ties to that wuss what so ever. How anyone could even compare me to a goody to shoes such as Kazee is beyond me. How anyone could even compare me to being anything like him is way beyond me. Seriously. The entire comparison is even more of your fantasy that you live. But don’t worry, at SAS, I’ll beat that Fantasy out of you……. (Mr. Meaner turns his back toward the camera, thus facing the site known as Ground Zero.) Bur your right Seymour, I’m not all bad. I’m not totally as mean as I claim to be. Your right, I don’t care about much. I don’t have a problem taking things from people, senselessly hurting people for my own advantage. Or for that matter even just doing things that do cause psychological harm……. But this, this was too far even for me. Three years ago. Something that is even too mean for my tastes. Something that even as underhanded as I am could never have fathomed. An attack so dastardly that even the meanest of people, even the meanest of people in the prisons could never have imagined and wanted to get back at something….. And the mourn I have felt, the uncharacteristic mourn I have felt for this country, and the anger I have built up and the anger that I have felt since that fatefully day is one I will never forget. This week on SAS, I will not only be fighting for the THC Title. I’ll be fighting for those who have lost love ones on this infamous day. I’ll be fighting for those who will only know loss because of this. I will be fighting for those who will never be able to remember this day, September 11, without shedding tears…… At SAS, not only do I fight for my THC Belt, but I fight in honor and memory of those who perished on September 11, 2001. (Mr. Meaner pulls out a copy of the SAS program for September 12, 2004, signs it “In Memory of all those who fell,” throws it out into the crater, along with a replica THC belt. Mr. Meaner then silently walks away as the scene fades to black.)
