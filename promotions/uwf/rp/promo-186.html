UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #186

Darkness blows!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins out in the same open field, and under the same tall tree as the last Ryder promo only this time it is dark outside and Ryder is sitting on a log behind the flames. He is staring straight up at the full moon almost in meditation as the camera zooms in on his face, before Ryder can't hold the pose any longer and bursts into laughter. He looks at the camera and waves with a cocky grin on his face. ) Ryder: Hi Ezra! Just thought i'd give you something to look at before I berated you in front of the entire world. You are such a stupid ass I can't even stand to look at another one of your retarded fuckin promos. Why do you have to take something I say and turn it into an essay report. I mean fuck son, when I said you were like a fly, that didn't mean do a history report on the damn insect. It's an annoying little fuck that hangs around shit all day, much like yourself. I mean you hang around pieces of shit like Mike Montgomery all day long and you get on my damn nerves so I put two and two together. I know i'm probably ruining the whole mood you set with your serious promo. That was a nice promo to by the way. The drama, and focus, oh it was some great acting on your part. The problem is that when we step into the ring boy, you can't act your way through the match, because i'm gonna be beating you for real. I'm gonna be really making your life hell and there's nothing you can do to stop it from happening. Sure, you can make fun of me losing the title in the dark all you want, but that match was against Dunn. We all know he's the strongest member of the Players Club. You are not Joshua Dunn though, you're nothin but a wannabe bitch and i'm gonna expose you for the no talent wreslter that we all know you are. Dunn even knows you're going to lose boy, and that's pretty sad. He knows first hand what I can do and he knows that it's a whole hell of a lot more than you can do. I may not like Dunn, but the man knows what it takes to get the job done, which makes me think he's a stupid fucker for hangin around someone like you, but hey what are ya gonna do. He's got sorry taste in friends, and I got the greatest stablemates that anyone could ask for. We will destroy your little club, and we're gonna do it unanimously. Three matches...three wins for the Prime Alliance. * Ryder throws a log onto the fire * Ryder: I mean come on Ezra, what makes you think you can even stand in the ring with me? I've proven that I can throw you around like a little bitch. I have shown the world that I am the superior man, and i've taken your great leader to his limits. If we'd been given a fair damn match I know that his little score would be 4-2, but does the Players Club do anything fair? Hell no! That's not a problem though, because neither does the Prime Alliance. We don't play fair and you can bet your ass that we always get even. Payback is not far away, but we'll let Dunn savor his victory for now. He knows and I know that it will be a short celebration. Bring all your firepower Ezra, because I can guarantee you that you're gonna need it. You and the Cherokee Thrilla are gonna go toe to toe one more time, and you'd better hope I let you walk out of that ring on your own two feet. Your ass is mine. ( Ryder laughs mockingly before pulling out a flashlight and pointing it at himself. He turns it on and off obviously poking fun at Ezra's last promo as the scene fades to black and ends. )
