I need an hero!

7 Nov 2001

Luca The Imp

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Still your IC Title holder... but shhh, we're trying to keep it quiet so no one notices. You don't think this big ass illuminous green lettering will give it away do you? Luca The Imp Finally the drought is over… By his own admission Toxic Shock has carried the torch for this federation in the wake of Ground Zero and at last he is willing to let others get on TV to record their own promos… he's a sweet kid. And his kindness has not been wasted… Deke Aurora has returned fresh from injury and is ready to go once again... ready to prove once again why he is the World Champion in perhaps his toughest match yet. Ben Jericho is preparing himself for his first real taste of UWF competition, and perhaps an appetiser for what lies ahead… Plague as always is showing off his latest Masochist's tattoo in the form of a grotesquely stapled stomach. And of course then there is Luca… declaring that his career has transcended yet again… now on the final furlong of his chosen career path… a path that has seen, Lackey to Wrestler, and now Wrestler to Manager. This month is only just beginning… The scene opens up on this cold Wednesday Afternoon within the confines of the well-maintained lawn that serves as Luca's on Mansion Five a Side Soccer pitch. Luca stands drinking a Red Bull with one foot on a soccer ball and staring off into the distance, beyond the wired fence... his thoughts on the WNU card later tonight. Quickly paced footsteps heavily tread the ground behind him. Luca turns around and smiles up at the blue kited out Hector. Hector: "Why couldn't we pick the teams?" Luca: "It's better this way god dammit." Hector: "Yeah, for you perhaps." Luca shakes his tiny head. Luca: "Look… it's easier this way… there's no confusion as to who is on who's team god dammit." Hector: "Ok, then why don't you have the 'Supers', and I'll have the 'Mans' then." Luca smiles and shakes his head. Luca: "Ah, but I want to be my favourite Soccer Team." Hector: "Which is?" Luca: "Man United of course… anyway, why are you friking complaining god dammit… You have Superman on your Team… he's able to leap buildings with a single bound… although ever since he misjudged that Twin Trade Tower leap, I think the City has put a stop to that." Hector looks at Luca poisonously. Luca: "Hey, it's time now Hec… it's time." Hector: "Ok, I'll give you that… I have Superman… but that's where my advantage ends… I've got Supergran in the bloody nets!" Hector points into the distance where Supergran wheels up and down on the goal line in her wheel chair. Supergran: "Och aye laddie, dae ken what a me'n?" The camera swivels back to Hector as he shakes his head. Hector: "And if that wasn't bad enough I've got bloody Superted up front." Hector looks over to where Superted unzips his teddy bear suit and steps out to reveal his kit. Luca smiles. Luca: "Nice of Jonny to lend us his bear… I'm surprised he doesn't need him… the nights must sure be tearful and lonely for the poor baby. Anyway, what are you complaining at… they're SUPER." Hector: "Luca… a Super 80 year old woman with a dodgy Scottish accent and arthritis isn't really super, with the chair - bionic maybe at a stretch but with a distinct hint of senility… hell the fish would have a better chance of remembering which side he was playing on. And as for Superted… sure he may be super, but how super can a Super bloody teddy bear actually be, he's still a stuffed damn toy!" Luca: "Ah, but what about your fifth member?" Hector: "I don't have a fifth member, I'm a man down." Luca looks surprised. Luca: "Oh… didn't he turn up?" Hector: "No, we couldn't think of another hero with Super in his/hers/its name…" Luca: "What about the mouse?" Hector: "That's Mighty…" Luca: "Oh…" Hector: "Look the sides aren't fair… you've got Batman, Spiderman…" Luca: "Hey, Spiderman is rubbish at soccer, every time he tries to kick the ball it sticks to his feet… I only friking invited him cos we need some nets god dammit!" Luca points over to where Spiderman, Spidey to his friends, currently spins up some webbing over the goalposts. Hector: "Ok, but you also have Batman… your team way outclasses mine… I want one of your players." Luca sighs. Luca: "Fine… who do you want?" Hector smiles. Hector: "I want Mr. Tickle!" Hector points to the strange orange chap with the little blue hat and five-foot long arms. Luca: "NO WAY GOD DAMMIT! That Mr. Man is the best goalkeeper in the world!" Hector: "You said I could choose one, and I choose him." Luca: "Have Ice Man." Hector: "No… with Superted and Supergran I can't afford any more slip ups." Luca: "Lame Hec." Hector smiles and shrugs. Luca: "Screw it… I'm taking my friking ball home." Luca picks the ball up and heads back towards the house; Hector follows. Hector: "Why were we doing this anyway, we hate Soccer." Luca: "Someone has to keep the comic book genre alive Hec, and it looks like it's down to us to do it." They enter the house through the patio. Hector: "Oh… can we talk shop now though?" Luca: "Sure…" They step into the kitchen where the delectable Lucy finishes off her soup while watching a Python promo on the TV… Luca stops and looks at the TV. Python: "Like you said there was no need to ask BUT i am glad that you did, the reason being that now Python has an excuse to tell you that he will beat Slasher, if you didn't ask I wouldn't have been able to say it coz it wouldn't have made any sense. Luca: "Thank Christ he asked then!!! Imagine who that'd have sounded if he hadn't? Hah, ha, ha, ha." SO Python is going to, without a shadow of a doubt, pound Slasher into the ground because Python is more superior than any opther man in this federation Luca: "I don't even know any 'opther' men in this fed… do you?" Hector: "Nope none at all." Hector grins. and why is he do you ask/ that would be because I am more than a man, I am a TYRANT!" Luca: "More like IGNORANT… moron! That's the guy that wanted a shot at my IC title right?" Hector: "The one and only…" Luca interrupts. Luca: "Thank god." Lucy looks at the boys. Lucy: "I thought you were playing Soccer?" Luca: "Nah… too many IMPortant things are happening for us to slack off right now." Hector sits down and grabs a slice of bread while Luca moves over to the fridge, and walks in via the Custom Cat Flap. Lucy: "So you heard about Plague then?" Luca walks out with a Red Bull and a puzzled expression. Luca: "Huh?" Lucy: "About his stomach." Luca: "Oh, hah, ha, ha, ha… you mean his friking stomach staples… yeah, I saw. I'm just glad Toxic Shock is trained in the craft of subtlety as well as well as the Jedi Art… cos I didn't want to be the one to have to tell Plague he was getting a little fat, hah, ha, ha, ha." Luca flies onto the table. Lucy: "You shouldn't make light of the situation, that could have killed him." Luca: "I'm not, it's Toxic Shock." Lucy: "Huh?" Luca: "Trying to make light of Plague, hah, ha, ha, ha." Lucy shakes her head. Luca: "Have you watched all the promos?" Lucy: "Well… I wouldn't describe it as all, but yes." Luca: "Did Deke shoot a promo?" Lucy: "Sure did." Luca grins. Luca: "I knew he would if he was booked… Was he angry?" Lucy: "About having to shoot a promo? I don't think so." Luca: "No god dammit! About what Ben and me did to him at Ground Zero!" Lucy licks her bottom lip. Lucy: "I don't think so, I mean he didn't seem to be." Luca slams the table. Luca: "WHAT?!?!" Lucy: "He seemed glad to have some real opposition for once…" Luca adopts a slouching, disappointed posture. Hector: "Who can blame him after nearly two months feuding with Danny and Blade… I hate Blade." Lucy smiles as she stands up and walks to the sink. Luca: "Doesn't he ever get friking mad???" Lucy turns around and smiles. Lucy: "No, he just gets even." Luca: "Shut up retard." Lucy turns back around and turns the faucet on. Luca: "I didn't go through all this to make friking Deke happy… I want him broken like LUCABOT, demoralised like Storm… not friking chirpy like corny Corky god dammit! Where's Ben? When's he getting here god dammit!" Hector looks up at the clock. Hector: "In about an hour…" Luca: "I don't want these two to be friendly friking competition… the UWF doesn't want that and my FANS certainly don't want THAT! I want them to hate each other, despise one another… lose every ounce of respect for one another… I want them to want to kill one another… and most of all I want to make sure Ben has no scruples when the time comes to condemn Deke into the depths of obscurity by destroying everything he's worked for, like he did to LUCABOT, no matter what it takes!!!" Luca picks up his Red Bull, trembling slightly from his heated rant. Lucy looks at the stunned Hector… Hector: "I err… don't know if it's possible to make someone hate someone else as easily as all that." Luca stares at Hector and raises bold brows. Luca: "Oh… I didn't say it would be easy Hec." Leap grabs his Red Bull and leaps to the floor. Luca: "I'll be in the projection room looking over some old Ben and Deke archives… show Ben in when he arrives." Hector: "Will do." Luca walks out of the kitchen. Hector looks at Lucy, obviously deep in thought. Lucy: "What?" Hector snaps out of it. Hector: "What? Oh… Just thinking." Lucy: "I know that, but what about?" Hector: "Well… I know Ben, and I know that Luca is capable of manipulating Ben a long way… just as he did with and for Gabriel…" Lucy stares at Hector, with undivided attention. Hector: "But I also know Ben was pushed too far once… and we all know what happened then." Lucy nods. Lucy: "Then it's our job to reign Luca in a bit…" Hector solemnly nods… as the scene fades.
