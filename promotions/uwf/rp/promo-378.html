UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #378

SAS Bloodbath

29 Apr 2004

Anarchy — WNU Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to in the arena just after Wednesday Night Underground. Pryde, Dai and Joanna are sitting in seats of a now empty arena. In the background can be seen the cleaning crew cleaning up all of the mess left by the UWF fans. Pop glasses, popcorn cartons and candy wrappers that litter the floor keep these people employed. The three Chaos Society members are conversing amongst themselves. They all seem to come to an agreement of some sort before they acknowledge the camera.] Joanna : First I would like to congratulate Raid on his first successful defense of his Television Title. They are lining them up for you to knock them down, leading you to be one of the great Television champs. Pryde : Now with that out of the way, lets talk about our tag match on Sunday. Seems Montgomery has decided to team Nick Persia with a newcomer to try to protect him from me destroying him. [Pryde and Dai get up from their seats and make their way down to the ring area.] Dai : Draco Paladine, newcomer to the UWF. You use you 7’ frame to its fullest, or so I am told. Last Sunday you were a joke and I will guarantee that after this Sunday you will not want to show you face around here again. You see, you are not of the caliber of Anarchy, now on the other hand, it is fitting that you are teamed with Persia. You can see first hand what The Chaos Society does to people who cross us. [Pryde climbs into the ring while Dai sits on the ring apron. Joanna continues to sit in the empty arena seat.] Pryde : Last Sunday The Chaos Society destroyed every other wrestler in the battle royal for the TV title. We knew that it would come down to the three of us and did what needed to be done. It may have been a bit unorthodox, but we proved that we can dominate. Now this Draco Paladine, I don’t know what you are trying to prove. You come in enter the battle royal without a word, now you are in a tag match teaming with that reject from Holland and still no word from you. Being the Strong, Silent and Stupid type will get you nowhere here and on Sunday I suggest staying out of my way as I trash that Dutch loser team member of yours. [Pryde begins to take off one of the top turnbuckle pads to revel the steel under it. After the first one is off he continues to do the same with the other three corners.] Pryde : Now Nick Persia, you kept that Hardcore belt of yours during our last meeting and I have to admit that you took what I gave you and still won the match. But from my hardcore championship experience in other feds, they continue to take their toll on the body till it is ready to break. [Pryde then runs and backs into one corner at high speed like someone irish wiped him in. A grimace of pain comes over his face but he walks it off.] Pryde : I found out then that pain becomes your friend. Every time you get slammed or punched or kicked it makes you aware of what you are here to do. [Pryde then does it again to the opposite corner, he walks out arching his back in pain.] Pryde : You realize that hardcore has to be a way of life. You begin to take great pleasure in you matches because you know that you are dishing out just punishment to your opponent, hoping to make the sorry S.O.B. break in two, taking all of his screaming and cries of pain as a reward for all of your hard work to get where you are today. [Pryde charges into a third corner, shoulder blocking the turnbuckle. He walks away from it holding it and trying to shake out the pain.] Dai : Dude, I think you are going a little over the edge. Pryde : A little over the edge isn’t enough when you are serious about The Hardcore Champion. Nick Persia, I am coming for you and when I get my hands on you I am going to destroy you. Tag Team is my bread and butter but hardcore can and will be my way of life. [Pryde walks over to the fourth turnbuckle and head butts it four or five times before stopping and walking toward the camera, blood running down his forehead profusely. He gets his face very, very close to the lens.] Pryde : Nick Persia, can you say that hardcore is a way of life for you? [The scene fades to black.]
