ANALYZE THIS...CORPORAL STYLE!

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Welcome to yet another appearance of the one and only true heel in wrestling. There is no words that can describe the mindset of The Enforcer. Sure there are some who try and comprehend what goes on in the Corporals mind. But they are all wrong. Some say that his mind works this way because of a traumatic experience as a child. Some say that it was the Army that did this to the Corporal (Even though the Corporal was not in the Army, he was in the marines). That is not correct either. Some even think that the Corporal's twisted views on this planet are from those who he has associated with in the past. Lew Spectre, Major Payne, The Youngblood's ect... But have they not forgotten the man that goes by Studog Escobar? Was he not a face in this sport? Did he not show a positive outlook on life. And did he not live a life of pure niceness? We know that this friendship that the Corporal and Studog enjoyed did not change the Corporal so much as an inch. So, why is the Corporal a fierce monster in public and in the ring? Why is the Corporal a man who enjoys hurting people? These questions and more will hopefully be answered momentarily, as the Corporal has now entered the building... CORPORAL STYLE! *What a week it has been boys and girls of the UWF. We have finally seen the little mole known as Nick Persia surface. And the attempt to wait to the last minute to reply has been countered by the Corporal of Punishment. Some questions have been asked by this Persian myth of minimum proportions, and it is time to attempt to understand the Corporal a little bit better. Some questions have been raised dealing with WHY the Corporal behaves the way he does. Several errors of thinking have been made by Mr. Persia, and for this reason, we bring you this special episode. So enjoy, and take notes, because the Corporal is ready to expose himself. The scene opens up as the Corporal steps out of his camaflauged CJ7 military jeep. He is dressed for tonight's battle and he has a large black duffle bag over his shoulder. Stepping onto the VIP parking area, the Corporal strolls into the arena for tonight's match up. Several of the Corporals devout fans have already arrived. Signs in their hands, face painted, and the majority of them are wearing the same attire that the Corporal wears in the ring on a nightly basis. The Corporal salutes them as he passes, and then enters his dressing room. It is a rather large dressing room. Not like the ones he recalls entering in less prestigious events. 6 independent lockers, a refrigerator, couch, bathroom, and television adorn this room. The Corporal throws his duffle on the nearby couch and he enters the bathroom. Exposing his penis, the corporal begins to urinate in the toilet when a towel boy enters the dressing room. Towel Boy: He will be here in a few minutes! I have got to get this stuff done! If not, I have already heard what this monster will do to me! The towel boy begins to lay assorted items on the table beside the couch. Items like peanut M&M's, a legal note pad, a complimentary pen from the arena, a fruit basket, complimentary soap and deodorants, and a few facial towels. While the boy strategically places these items on the table, the Corporal grabs the boy from the back of the neck. He lifts him up and turns him so that he faces the Corporal eye to eye. Corporal: What do you want BOY? Towel Boy: Nothing Sir.... (GULP!) I am just here bringing you complimentary items. We want to show you the respect you deserve sir... Welcome to our facility (GULP!) The Corporal looks over at the table and observes the several items lying there. He grins momentarily and then without warning, he throws the towel boy across the room and begins eating the M&M's. Corporal: It is about time BOY! I am hungry! Be glad you brought me something to eat. Towel Boy: Well thank you sir. I was hoping to have this done before you arrived. The towel boy adjusts his smock and fixes the now wrinkled uniform he has on. Corporal: You see kid. There is a reason why you were wanting to have my gifts ready before I got here. And that reason goes further than just a pay check. Am I right? Towel Boy: Yes sir. The boy has a look of fear in his eyes as he listens to the Corporals rant. Corporal: It is because you have an pre conceived idea that I am a very cruel and mean person. Am I right? Towel Boy:Uh... sir... I really do not know how to answer that. Corporal: Understood little man. I would not answer that question either if I were you. After all, you do not know why I am the way I am. So sit down for a minute, and you can get an inside scoop of the corporal's mental frame of mind. The towel boy slowly slides onto the couch. His face appears to be showing the same sense of fear. Corporal: Do not be afraid yet little man. I am going to assist you in feeling more comfortable when you walk into my presence. I think that it is time I gave out a few inner workings about myself. After all, there have been several comments made about me, by my opponent, wondering why I do the things I do. Corporal: Is it because I have been dealt a harsh hand in life and that I should have gotten a more compassionate hand from my mother and father? Corporal: Is it because of the influences in the military? Did something happen back then that changed me? Some type of horrific event that forever molded me into this creature? Corporal: Was it those who I associated with? Did they mold me by manipulation to become a assassin for hire? Am I misguided? What could it be? The Corporal stops momentarily. His face showing the look of an innocent man framed by society. Corporal: Why do they say these things about me? I am just a man. I have feelings too you know. Why can they not see that I am just as normal as anyone else in this sport. Do I not put my pants on the same way they do? Do I not smile, cry, laugh, and sing? What in injustice! I am simply a man misunderstood. Corporal: Do you really want to know why I act the way I do? Towel Boy: Why yes I do sir. The boy has a total look of suspense in his eyes. Corporal: It isn't because of all those things Nick Persia has said. It truly isn't. I mean, I was never in the ARMY. I was in the marines. My associates did not coax me into doing what I have done. I simply was willing to do it myself. Corporal: Why do I take men and rip of their body parts, and then beat them with them? Why do I shove cattle prods up the anuses of Canadians who attempt to analyze me? Why do I manipulate people to the point that they cower in fear at my presence? Why am I what I am? Corporal: It is simple... The Corporal stops again, and this time he grins evilly at the towel boy. He smiles and begins to laugh sinisterly. Corporal: I LIKE IT! Corporal: Nothing more. I like hurting people. I like tearing peoples limbs off their bodies, and then beating them with it. I like all those things because I am a man who likes to hurt things. Why when I was a child, I ripped of all four legs of my pet poodle. Did I cry? No, I laughed as the blood sprayed all over me. I am the most evil man on this planet. And when I do things that no one likes to do, I get pleasure out of it. Corporal: Do I care about the titles... NO. Do I care abut the fans...NO. I only care about the BATTLE. That is my desire, to fight and keep fighting. Day in and day out. The love for the battle is why I do the things I do. And it is a shame that there are few that can say the same. Corporal: The secret to defeating me is not mental. It is sheer physical. If that puke Persia thinks he can analyze me for a victory, he is literally dead wrong! He is just going to have to pray that he has enough HEART to finish the task of facing me in the ring. That is all. Pure and Simple. The HEART wins battles, and there is no man on this Earth who has more heart than me... NO ONE! Corporal: So little towel boy. There are some things in life that are the way they are because they CHOOSE to be that way. I am one of those things. And when you see me walking around the corner, you had better be afraid because you might by my next POODLE... and what would you do IF you had no arms and legs? Corporal: THIS is the question you need to ask yourself... Corporal: IS IT WORTH IT? Corporal: Now get out of my dressing room you little freak of Sesame Street. I have to get ready to destroy Nick Persia... CORPORAL STYLE!
