UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #1497

Replica Dreams

5 May 2006

Pencil-Necked Geeks — The Home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of the home are of the Pencil Necked Geeks. Sitting at his chair, reading one of his millions of books is Book Worm. All of a sudden, in burst the Computer Nerd, carrying a large cardboard box.) CN: It’s here. It’s finally here. This is so awesome. BW: What are you babbling about now? CN: I finally got that thing I won off of E-Bay not to long ago. BW: You’ve won millions of things off of E-Bay. Especially since we got our contracts with the UWF. So what could be so exciting that you have to bust into here and disturb my reading? (Computer Nerd yanks something out of the box, letting the box fall against the wall in a violent manner. He reveals what the back of a championship belt.) BW: Oh yeah, it’s a belt. CN: It’s not just any belt. This is the UWF INH Belt. This is an exact replica. (CN turns the belt around to show how the belt has some of the same details of the UWF belt.) CN: But’s that’s not it. This belt is signed by its current title holder Clyde. Check it out. (CN tries to reveals the signature and tries to show it up close to Book Worm.) BW: Ok dude, I get it. I don’t see what the point is though. CN: WHAT THE POINT IS? This is a piece of wrestling history. This is going to be awesome. BW: You’re such a mark. CN: So what’s your point? BW: Well, why not just win the real thing? You have all of these replica belts, but now you have chances for the real thing. CN: Well, I know that we are going to have a chance at the tag team titles here at some point. After we beat those two Commies, we get a shot at those. But how do you figure we have a chance at another belt. BW: Have you not even read the upcoming card? Lethal Lottery. All we have to do is win a fatal four way, then a tag team match, and then a singles match, and then one of us will have a shot at Clyde. And the way you act, it probably won’t be you. CN: You’re such a brat. BW: True, but don’t forget that it is this brat who has always beaten you, no matter what. CN: That’s just because you spend your entire time in those books of yours. BW: Yeah, and these books are so much better than your computer. CN: Whatever. We’ll see what happens on Monday. I think you may be surprised in the end. (The scene fades to black.)
