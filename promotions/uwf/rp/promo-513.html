UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #513

Redemption

7th July 2004 (year unrecorded)

Unforgiven — Unknown cellar, near WNU arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Having killed off whatever remnants of Ben Jericho there were previously, it would seem that Unforgiven had his mind set on re-writing the wrongs of the past. His sentiments were that of resentment and sheer callous towards Jericho, yet he did indeed feel sorry for him – a puppet; a puppet was all Jericho became in the view of Unforgiven. Jericho became somewhat of a toy to the promotions hierarchy, and it wasn’t even in the UWF were this began. Unforgiven felt the wrath of that himself whilst competing in other companies.] [Picture this if you will – young talent who was getting rave reviews but his temperament was a little off putting. Of course he would need molding, he was on the indy circuit and in small Japanese feds for a while and when picked up by a promotion making a name for itself, they saw his potential and not long put him in a programme with one of the big midcard feuds at the time. He got rubs from one of the biggest face athletes in the company and his face stable made him an honorary member. He rebelled when he saw the plight of what was happening in the group he was in and helped in the downfall of a certain “Dark Lords” of the company.] [Yet he was getting stuck by the man just for entertainment values. Months went past and it was tiresome gimmick change after gimmick change after gimmick change to fit in a gain some respect from his peers, which seldom he got. Management thought it was fun to tease him with bigger opportunities that he once had, and he got some of them – but the big things he worked for seemed to end up as a joke; a Stable Wars win, yet getting his title shot on a non existent, non televised card.] “Joseph…. Rosso….” [The ominous voice rings out as the scene opens in a dark, squalid cellar. Brick walls are lined with moss and damp stains. The mold on the walls symbolizes the murky smell as the steps have water trickling down them. The camera looks around to show the cement walls again, some damage to them as if hit with the impact of a blunt instrument. There’s nothing remotely civil about the place – it’s not cavernous, and very claustrophobic.] “…. Yes…. I remember Joseph Rosso quite well. Liked to wear Versace suites, tailor made for him. He seemed like a good friend of Lew Spectre somewhat – not as much as Richard Hayes, but still the were on admirable terms. Rosso hired me for the GIWF and then decided to stick me in a plot with Gabrielle, Evil, David Rico and Team Esco. I remember that quite well as it was the first time I thought for myself, as I revolted against The Dark Lords and helped out Studog Escobar. A friend in need is a friend indeed; and as much as he was thankful towards me… a rookie… that day, I was all the more thankful for his acknowledgment. That was the day I believe most of the GIWF roster resented me” [The camera still pans around to find Unforgiven, but picks up a few scattered wrestling magazines, an old wrestling mask, and then the feet of someone. The camera traces across…. The feet with Doc Martin’s boots on turn into a baggy pair and denim jeans. The denim jeans turn into a black Machine Head shirt, and the Machine Head shirt turns into the vision of Unforgiven; his hair no longer restricted by the bandana he wore on his earlier expedition. Leant up against the wall, the camera pans out to show him seated in a full slacker slouch – one leg out straight, the other bent upwards as he rests his weary, cracked hands on it; dirt underneath his nails where it looks as if he was scavenging for something through the dirt when he left Slasher’s previous abode. In the background, there is soothing music – it’s Jeff Buckley’s “Hallelujah”, very soothing but quite a emotional song.] “You see, it was unlike a rookie to take off in such fashion like I did. I did jobs to people like Ace Thunder, Twiztid and Corporal Punishment, and I was good (nodding) yeah, I was pretty fantastic getting beaten up for the sakes of making others look good. (stops nodding and looks around, contemplating) I think what really got to people though was the popularity I was getting – I got some good accolades from people when I first started in a big federation like the GIWF, they saw this bright talent that could be cultivated into something big. That was me…. Unforgiven… not Ben Jericho, that SHAM that was Ben Jericho in the UWF… it was me. I began getting monikers such as “The Patron Saint of Hardcore” for the battles I had with my mentors, Ace Thunder and Twiztid. I was the last person to train with Twiztid before his unfortunate demise… I’m proud to carry on his legacy. Twiztid and Thunder both saw me as a talented individual – someone who with them could revolutionize garbage wrestling and hone it into something more cultivated and clever; mixing strong style Japanese wrestling with sick spots. We took hardcore wrestling to a new art form – none of this chair hitting shit, PROPER hardcore wrestling; lightbulbs, barbed wire, fire, dropping from high places. I went one on one with The Preacher in a Hell In A Thunderdome match back in the GIWF, and fell through the Thunderdome, nearly killing myself in the process. I jumped off the Global-Tron twice and survived. It’s the same kindred spirits who take hardcore wrestling as a science rather than a sluggish affair which has granted Slasher and Misfit my respect – not everyone would dare cut my forehead open and gouge it with a screwdriver… not many people would STAB ME with a broken lightbulb tube…” [Unforgiven runs his hand down the bottom of his back where the scar is still visible, and starts to scratch it a little. He tilts his head and stares ahead of him as he smiles] “I actually gained the respect from one of the greatest men I ever faced, Deke Aurora, after Jericho made him tap out. It may have been Jericho who was there, but I represented the eager, the will, the strength to beat Deke Aurora. But my greatest credit has to be the respect from a man for whom has set the benchmark to others in the UWF…. Tsunami Oni. I got respect from people who mattered; maybe that was my downfall in the UWF as much as it was in the GIWF. Maybe that’s why people like Lew Spectre, The Prodigy and Richard Hayes disliked me – because on a good day I could match them toe to toe; and they hated someone younger and not as sophisticated as them doing that. Maybe they were so vain that they would stop at nothing to keep me down, and even push other guys over me who showed less potential but more gusto. I guess that’s in part my fault – I gave up caring about the GIWF in the end, and Joseph Rosso won – he destroyed his pet project that became a bigger threat to him and his cozy little business than ever expected.” [Unforgiven turns to the camera, with a look of disdain in his eyes, somewhat covered by his blonde and black hair] “I busted my ass off at Stable Wars three for a title shot I deserved; my ambition to headline a PPV, to have my name at the top of the card as a competing athlete – not as a special guest referee. What happened? I ended up fighting The Prodigy at the Chris Williams Memorial Show, in the undercard matches and lost. No television coverage, no clips – just a result. That was it for me; that killed my interest in the wrestling business until the UWF came knocking at my door – and even when that happened, as soon as someone bigger and better came in, I was shut out in the cold. The owners mistreated me after doing so fucking much for them – take your paycheck Unforgiven, go home now. Or in the instance of Richard Hayes, here’s your paycheck Ben, 47th Street’s where you can score.” [Unforgiven turns away again and looks at his boots. He looks around the room and just smirks as he runs a single finger down the wall – debris comes off, as the tip of his finger goes green with the mildew on the walls. He sighs before he beings to talk, become very diplomatic with his voice] “Straight-Edge. CM Punk says ‘Straight-Edge is better than you’, and I agree with him. For my point of view, I’ve been on the opposite end of that statement – the boozy, alcohol addled nights with everyone – the mindless cheap sex whilst my wife and child were at home… the drugs; I could go days on end with food or drink, but I couldn’t go a straight twenty four hours without a smack hit. I acted like top fucking dog back in those archaic days when real men drank bottles of whiskey, went to bed at three in the morning and woke up a six to do their usual workout routine. It was a drag… it was a long slog and I regret every moment of that lifestyle. It was that start in the GIWF of binge drinking that lead to my misdemeanors that would come in time. I can honestly look back at what was going on before I left 11 months ago, and the week running afterwards, and say that ‘Yes… I messed up royally. I didn’t bring the company into disrepute, I brought myself into disrepute’. And then I think to myself, ‘is being a straight-edge something I can accomplish? Unforgiven, you’ve gone half a year drug free, alcohol free, clean living and self preserving yourself for the bigger battle. You’ve conquered your demons that crippled Ben Jericho, and you destroyed the monster not long ago, putting him to rest…. What do you think?’ And with that self invested power, with that renewed source of confidence, I can proudly forget about whatever happened in the UWF, whatever happened in the GIWF… I can forget about the actions of Lew Spectre, Richard Hayes, The Prodigy to some degree… but I’ve not forgiven them. They were idiots trying to find a ‘short term’ solution to success – I’m looking at the broader scale and seeing that there is a longer, more prestigious road to my fate. And I can say to everyone (turns to the camera with a hostile look on his face) THAT STRAIGHT EDGE WAS BETTER THAN BEN JERICHO….” [Unforgiven turns away again and brings both knees up, as he rests himself on both knee, one arm on the knee still but another rested upwards, the palm helping him rest his goatee covered chin. He beings to speak again, no longer hostile but somewhat wary. ] “Sunday Afternoon… a programme they call SAS… I get a chance to fulfill a promise… a PACT I made to Slasher. For some unknown reason, the UWF finally granted us the title shot we worked long and hard for and never got, against the team of Digital Mortality and Moraless. These two were the subject of the Drowning Pools first beat down when we returned. I have nothing against either of these two, it’s just unfortunate they are standing in the way of the path of Slasher and my own destiny’s. At the end of the day, to accomplish something of this magnitude, I would stop at nothing….. NOTHING…. To make sure that the promise is kept. I would walk the ends of the earth to keep the promise. I would put my entire body on the line to keep this promise. I would bleed until I’m purged dry just to keep this promise, Mortality… because it’s not just about the promise… it’s my chance for redemption. See TOO LONG… have people in this company put me down. Bratty youngsters who think they’re big shots because they cruised their way to success that they have to point out their achievements every god damn minute, EVERY GOD DAMN DAY. They look on at me, and they think ‘he’s back…. Again…. How long this time… what a joke… I’m better than him,” WELL YOU’RE NOT…. YOU’RE NOT…. On a good day I could beat you fairly in the ring, and there’s a concept for you kids, FAIRLY. None of this heel turn shit, none of this cocky, arrogant ‘I work for the company, therefore I get to veto your sorry ass’ shit. With bloody fists I’d beat your face like a ten ton hammer, and I’D ENJOY EVERY MINUTE OF IT. You may have been better than Ben Jericho….. BUT BEN JERICHO IS DEAD…. You may have humiliated Ben Jericho….BUT BEN JERICHO IS DEAD… you may have even thrown his name in the mud, and I know you have you little bastard, I know you have…. BUT BEN JERICHO IS DEAD… and Unforgiven…. Heh…. Unforgiven has taken his place AND IS GOING TO GET RESPECT AGAIN.” [Unforgiven’s movements become very agitated as he has a sick smile on his face, one very much a characteristic of Slasher] “You see… if I win the World Tag Team Championship with Slasher… that means that I’m no longer a sorry excuse for a wrestler. That would mean that any remaining doubt of Ben Jericho would be dead and buried along with him. To win the titles would mean that I am capable of achieving something when I put my mind to it. I just find it unfortunate the circumstances that I am thrusted into… see…. As I’ve said before, I have no problems with you Digi… you have to believe me on that one… you’re a shining light, a beacon in this federation… and I applaud you for it Digi, I really do… I’d no sooner destroy you in the ring than I’d shake your hand. But what you must realize Mortality… what you must realize Moraless…. Is that at the end of the day, you’re stood in my way to my path of redemption. And the consequences maybe dire for your two, but if I fail at this chance, the consequences for me are much… much more dire. You two may forgive me not long after the match for what I’ve done…. But it doesn’t matter…. Because I will always be….” [The camera cuts out as Unforgiven turns to it, staring closely into the camera with a look of contempt on his face, rubbing the hair on his chin and proceed to talk with the utmost venom in his voice] “Unforgiven” [END TRANSMISSION]
