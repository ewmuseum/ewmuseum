UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #552

Fragile as Fame

16 Jul 2004

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — The Navy Pier, Midnight

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Like the dull bright spot a flashbulb leaves in your vision when you look straight at the camera, the initial excitement of Wednesday night's debut has begun to fade away. For twenty minutes, that ring belonged to Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins, the roar of the ignorant crowd and taste of sweet victory all for him. The first taste of fame.] [How bitter that first bite can be.] [The pale cloud-covered crescent moon glows down upon the head and shoulders of Rich Rollins, his hair tied back, stripped down to his bare chest and the torn blue jeans that have nearly become his second skin clinging to his legs. Empty storefronts pass him to one side, and to the other Lake Michigan lays silent and calm, her smooth surface not so much as reflecting a single star. This was the Navy Pier at night, when all the gawkers and hawkers went back to their homes, leaving the Pier to sit on the edge of the water like an abandoned, haunted circus.] [The Renegade enjoyed the atmosphere.] [Jogging around the outer walking deck along the lake's edge, nothing but the distant sound of car horns and the squeak of city buses greet Rollins' ears, the sounds that have come to soothe and calm him over the years. Once a week, the Pier was his playground after hours, as a cool twenty in the local security guard's pocket bought him all the time he wanted. Alone.] [The sound of a second set of footsteps cause The Renegade to slow down and stop, a sudden gust of wind whipping his hair into his eyes. Turning around and brushing the wayward hair behind one ear, Rollins looks back down the walking deck's silhouetted darkness, annoyed that someone else was intruding on his time.] [Climbing over the guard rail from the upper deck, UWF coffee boy Jeremy Gonzolas drops down onto the main deck a few feet away from Rollins, dressed down in a pair of athletic pants and a simple black tee-shirt. Wiping his hands on his pants, Jeremy turns to The Renegade.] Jeremy: Ah... there you are. Renegade: What the hell are you doing here? I told the security officer I didn't want to be disturbed. Jeremy: You mean ol' McDermott? He used to work security at the UWF Arena, so he cut me a deal. I heard you liked it out at the Pier at night. Renegade: Well congratulations, you found me. Now leave. Jeremy: Hold up a minute, Rollins. You skipped out of the arena on Wednesday before anybody could get your reaction on your win in the battle royal. I just wanted to talk a minute. [Silence.] [Sizing up the smaller office jockey, The Renegade takes a few steps closer, his bare chest glistening and his cold blue eyes dull as dirty copper. Jeremy shrinks back, but slightly.] Renegade: Your boss really sends you out on some shit jobs, you know that? Jeremy: (chuckling nervously) Well, that's Hayes for you. He's your boss too, you know. Renegade: You got two minutes. So start talkin'. Jeremy: You beat out nine other competitors in your first match in the UWF, Renegade. That's mighty impressive... and you get a shot at Jay Vyper this Sunday for the Television Championship. That's gotta make you feel good. [The corners of The Renegade's mouth begin to hint a a grin, but there is not mirth in his eyes. His voice underlined with steel, he responds.] Renegade: Fame is a fragile thing, Gonzolas. So I beat Dave Storm... what a shock. But a month from now, nobody's going to remember we ever locked up on that night, or care. It isn't the curtain-jerkers that get the attention, kid. It's the ones that aren't afraid to be primal. Aren't afraid to be brutal or masochistic. You want to see some ratings, Gonzolas? Split a guy's head open with a cheese grater, or smash his face with a sledgehammer. Either way, when your opponent is down for those precious three seconds, pieces of his nose and teeth had better be littered around the ring canvas, or you didn't do you job. Jeremy: Is that a message to Jay Vyper? Renegade: A message? (he laughs) Try a warning. The guy could take the hint and call in sick on Sunday and save himself the effort. Fame is too fleeting to bother with it half-assed... you've gotta go in balls first or not at all. Winning a ten-man battle royal over a bunch of relative no-names won't feed me through next week, but brutalizing an established champion just might. I don't even give a good Goddamn about that Television title... I'm goin' after it because it's been dangled in front of my face. The string just happens to be Jay Vyper. Jeremy: Jay Vyper made pretty short work of Richard Ray Vau... Renegade: It wasn't a match, it was a massacre. I was there Gonzolas, you don't need to give me a play-by-play. Vaughn was just another nobody who got a chance to be somebody, and struck out. But I don't play hit-and-miss, kid. I don't go after somethin' I don't intend on winnin'. And I damn sure don't coddle some ring jockey who thinks he's tough shit just 'cause there's a shiny strap of gold and leather around his waist. Come Monday morning, people will know that Rich Rollins is for real... as much as the Television Championship will afford me, anyway. Because at Supremacy, the UWF's Television champion is going to have his face spread across the canvas in pieces... I promise you that. [The wind and the water and the sounds of the city, that's all that passes between Jeremy Gonzolas and Rich Rollins. Rubbing the back of his neck out of habit, Jeremy shrugs.] Jeremy: I guess I'll see you Sunday, then. Renegade: Better keep a hot pot on the burner for me, coffee boy. Wouldn't want your actual duties to fall behind, now would we? [Flashing Rollins a fake grin, Jeremy starts to say something... but The Renegade is already off and jogging in the other direction, the shadows of the looming ferris wheel, silent as a statue, swallowing him into the bottomless abyss of the night. Sighing once again, Jeremy speaks out to nobody in particular.] Jeremy: With cream and sugar. Asshole. [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
