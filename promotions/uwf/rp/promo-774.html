Tsunami Oni — The OmniNational Industries gym

Mickey D was in pain. Agony, in fact. Every part of his body ached, his lungs felt like they were on fire. His lower back was the worst, it was almost as if he was having acupuncture with icepicks. Mickey laid on the concrete, eyes closed. A voice kept telling him to open his eyes, but he was afraid to. He knew what he would see. The face of his tormentor. The one responsible for his pain. Mickey dreamed that if he kept his eyes closed long enough, the voice would go away. But it didn’t. Even worse, the voice took control of Mickey D. Against all sense of self-preservation, he opened his eyes. And there was the face. That horrid face. The smiling face of Tsunami Oni. Oni: Get up, you lazy sack! Mickey: Damn, man! Show some respect for the dead! Tsunami Oni laughs at his pupil. The youngster looked pathetic. Mickey D was covered in sweat, his dreadlocks limp from the weight of his perspiration. His tee-shirt and sweatpants were soaked. The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea looked quite the opposite. He looked completely fresh in his wrestling gear. Although some of his skin was still pink from Mike Montgomery’s “Fireball of Redemption,” Oni was right as rain. Oni: What a little girl! I haven’t even touched you yet! Mickey: Don’t remind me! I’m in enough pain from all these drills! Oni: Hey, it has to be done. Being a wrestler takes more than moves. You need to be able to last in that ring long enough to get the job done. It doesn’t matter how many ways you know to tie a guy in knots or how sweet you look coming off that top rope. You get blown up and it’s over. Wincing, Mickey D rubs his back. Mickey: I get all that. I just didn’t think running the ropes would hurt so damn much. Oni: Consider that lesson one, then. Now get your sorry ass up and walk it off. I don’t want you whining about sore muscles tomorrow. Mickey: Whatever. Just help a bruthah up, yo. Tsunami Oni extends a hand and pulls Mickey D up. Mickey: did you have to go through all this when you trained? Oni: This? This is nothing. MY training included getting kicked off a pyramid in the middle of a jungle. Twice. Mickey: I thought that was just a comic book story, man. Oni: You think comics aren’t fact? Next you’ll be telling me wrestling is fake! Mickey: Shoot! My bones tell me what’s what! So long before I’m ready for the UWF? Oni: Simmer down, hot rod. UWF is at the top of the food chain. I spent years wrestling in Japan and Mexico. And even when I got to the states I started out in a small promotion in the south. Mickey: Really? What was it? Oni: Uh, it was called NPW. Mickey: Doesn’t ring a bell. What’s that stand for? Tsunami Oni actually looks a little sheepish. Oni: Uhhhhh… No Pirates Wrestling. Mickey: What!?! Oni: Yeah. The promoter was kind of… odd. Mickey: This I gotta hear! Oni: Well, it was a long time ago… Mickey: C’mon, man! It’ll be educational! Teach me about paying dues! The Vivaldi of Violence sees the look on the young man’s face and realizes he’s stuck. Oni: Fine. But don’t let this get around. It went a little something like this… ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- After I was trained in the lucha libre style in Mexico, I bounced around the country for a while. I worked for different small promotions, not staying in any one too long But I always put on good matches. Still, I was surprised when I got an offer to work for a promotion in the US. Sure, it had an odd name, No Pirates Wrestling, but that didn’t matter to me. Hey, I’m from Japan. We invented Pokemon so who am I to judge? It took some doing to get a visa for the States. Back in those days I wore a mask I refused to take off. And listing “super-villain” as my occupation didn’t exactly help either. But things got settled and I was off to exotic Greensboro, South Carolina. I went to the small gym that NPW used as their headquarters. I went in and looked around. There were a couple wrestlers sparring in the ring. Others were hitting weight machines or jumping rope. I really wasn’t sure how to find the promoter. I got some help when a hefty middle-aged wrestler came up to me. He kind of stood due to the fact he had on a cowboy hat, cowboy boots, and a bandana. And that was it. “Howdy, partner!” he said. “You muster be that new feller from Jay-pan. I’m Buck. Buck Naked.” He held out his hand, which I shook. “Uh… hello,” I answered. My English was still a little shaky back then. Plus I was talking to a heavyset nude man. “I guess you noticed my attire, or lack there of,” said Buck. “You see, I’m a nudist. This here is my lifestyle choice. And I appreciate NPW letting me live the way I wish. It’s got its advantages in the ring. For instance, when I run at a guy to give him a lariat, the sight of my swinging pecker distracts the poor sucker so I can nail him really good. And let me tell you this. I got the most feared powerbomb in the South. Most fellers dread feeling my testicles on the back of their head.” “Ah,” I replied. “I see.” What the hell else was I supposed to say? “Listen to me flappin’ my gums!” said Buck. “Let’s get you to the boss man.” I followed Buck Naked down a hallway, trying not to look at his huge bare ass. He led me to a door labeled “Zebediah Love, Promoter.” Buck knocked them opened it. Inside behind a desk was a tall skinny man. He looked more like a mortician than a wrestling promoter. “Mr. Love,” said Buck. “This here’s that feller from Jay-pan.” “Thank you, Buck,” replied Zebediah Love. “That will be all.” Buck gave me a friendly smile and then got his naked self out of there. Zebediah Love stood up and walk around his desk, stopping right in front of me. He towered above me, which really isn’t that hard. He had this cold stare, like he was looking into my soul. “The new wrestler, eh?” said Love. “You’re not a pirate, are you, boy?” This was definitely a question I didn’t expect. But maybe I should have. “Uh, no,” I managed to get out. “No pirate.” Zebediah Love kept staring. I don’t think he ever blinked. “You call yourself Tsunami Oni, boy?” he said. “That means ‘tidal wave devil.’ Be a mighty fine name for a pirate. Especially one from Japan. All that ocean. Perfect for pirates. And what this I hear about you being a super-villain, boy? Ain’t any super-villain worse than a pirate.” “Uh, I am not pirate,” I protested. “You better not be, boy,” said Love. “This here is No Pirates Wrestling and that means one thing. No pirates. I can stand anything else. Hell, you saw Buck. But no pirates. My daddy hated pirates. His daddy hated pirates. And I hate pirates too. But as long as you ain’t a pirate, things’ll be right fine, boy. Now we got you booked this Saturday in a match. You’re taking on Esinem.” “Thank you,” I said, then I got the hell out of there. Zebediah Love was one creepy bastard. I was back in the gym and heading to the exit when a young man stepped in front of me. He had short blond hair and was dressed in hip-hop clothes. And he was fronting a lot of attitude. “Yo, bitch,” he said. “I am Esinem and this is my house! I’m gonna fuck you up, bitch! Gonna chew you up like you chew dogs, chinaman!” “I am Japa-“ “Shut the fuck up, bitch!” he said. “You think you so smart? Show you how smart you really are in that ring Saturday. Gonna fuck your shit up, bitch!” As stimulating as this conversation was, I pushed my way past Esinem and headed out. I wasn’t sure what I had gotten myself into, but I had made a commitment. I pretty much stayed in my motel room until Saturday. The night of my US debut, I’m in the locker room when Buck Naked comes up behind me. Let me tell you, nothing is more disturbing than realizing a fat naked man has snuck up behind you. “Good luck ou there, partner,” he said. Buck was a really nice guy. A nice guy in need of pants. Anyway, it was time. My music was playing so I made my way to the ring. Esinem was already in there, looking pissed. We stood nose to nose. He was taling is ghetto smack. Then he hauls off and slaps me! Then bell rings and the match is on! I retaliate with a slap of my own. “Oh yeah,” he says. “that’s what I like!” Still takling the trash. I give him a chop across the chest. Everyone in the gym could hear it. “God, yes!” he yells. “May I have another?” OK, this was getting weird. But I give him another chop. “Sweet Jesus, thank you!” says Esinem. I’m not sure what’s going on but I get him in a headlock. “Squeeze harder!” he tells me. “And pinch my nipples!” Sure, English wasn’t my first language, but I was pretty sure what he said was pretty messed up. I release the headlock quickly. Esinem looks a little disappointed. He just stands there as I sweep his legs and send him to the mat. Quickly, I clamp on a scorpion deathlock. “Yes! Yes! Yes!” he screams. “Hurt me more! I’ve been bad!” I turn my head to see if Esinem is going to tap. What I see is my opponent with one hand down the front of his pants! It’s moving, but not to tap out! I let go of the scorpion deathlock. Esinem stands back up. I pray that’s a foreign object in his pants, but I know better I decided this had gone on long enough. A kick to the jaw knocked my perverted little friend out cold. There was no way in hell I was putting my body on top of his, though, Instead, I just place a foot on his chest and the ref made the three count. That was it. I was victorious in my American debut! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Oni: So you see, Mickey, we all have to pay our dues. Mickey: Damn! I hope I never gotta go through that. If some white boy started beating off when I was in the ring with him, my ass would be gone! Oni: Hey, you have to put up with a lot if you want to be a champion. Mickey: Speaking on that, you all pumped for Sunday? Tsunami Oni grins. Oni: A rematch for the World title? You know it. And I don’t have to worry about my family being put in jail or the love of my life turning against me. Mickey: So are thing with you and Shiatsu cool? Just then the elevator door opens and there stands Shiatsu wearing a little white dress that cling to her body. Actually it looks more like a tank top than a dress, but if that’s what she wants to wear, who’s going to stop her? Shiatsu: Hey, sweetie. I could stare at you and Mickey all day, but we got reservations. So get that fine ass in gear! Mickey: I guess they are! Oni: I told you… hard work pays off.
