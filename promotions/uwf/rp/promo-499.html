Jessica Dunn part 1....CORPORAL STYLE!

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Wires, cables and extension cords writhed like snakes across the floor of the governor's conference room as photographers dragged them along the floor in search of the nearest receptacles. At the same time, reporters bumped and shoved each other for position in front of the podium. *The governor would arrive at any moment and everyone had to be in place. *Arizona was about to go on a death run. Executions for four death row inmates fell on the same day. Three of the inmates faced death by lethal injection, while the fourth legally fought for, and won, a more ancient method: death by hanging. *The suit-wearing helmet-heads and make-up mavens of the news world barked into cell phones, and snapped orders to their blue-jean, t-shirt clad photographers. Conversation crackled around the room like static electricity; not so much about the executions as it was about one television station's intentions. Warden:' The federal appeals court just ruled.' Secretary 'Do you think the governor will let them do it?' Warden: 'I don't know, but the court said the state can make the decision, not Gibbons.' *Bubba Dunn was facing the gallows and looking forward to it. In fact, he wanted everyone to see it. The white supremacist was convicted of bombing an NAACP meeting 14 years before in Phoenix. The pipe bomb was crudely crafted and placed outside near a window of the church where the meeting was taking place. A young, white girl pedaled by the church on her bicycle. She was killed instantly. No one else was hurt. Once his appeals ran out, Dunn started petitioning the courts to have his execution televised. News 37 in Phoenix was backing the case, providing its attorneys to Dunn. Warden: 'Who's here for News 37?' Secretary 'That crazy reporter.' 'It's Mr. Blowhard,' yelled one of the photographers from the back of the room. Blowhard: 'At your service,' *As he bowed to the group of speculating reporters. Stan Blowhard was the proud owner of an outrageous reputation for taking 'reporter involvement' in his news reports to a painful level. In the past two years, the dark-haired 27-year-old was hit in the face with pepper spray, zapped by an electric fence, wrecked a car so he could take the blow of an exploding airbag, and shot while wearing a bulletproof vest, all for News 37's cameras and a man known as The Corporal of Punishment. Blowhard also knew he would never fulfill his dream of working for any national tabloid television show if he couldn't show he was fearless and willing to try anything. And this occasion had to be one of the greatest opportunities he had up to date. Governor: 'Hello everyone,' *As he approached the podium. He wasn't expected for another five minutes. The press scrambled like mice. Chairs clanged. Barks of 'excuse me' and 'he's here' ricocheted around the room. *Governor Williams seemed to enjoy the ugly sight. 'Governor, governor, governor,' came the chant of attention. The Governor smiled and held up his hand until the echoes faded into silence. Governor: 'As you all know,' *the governor began as silver streaks in his otherwise black hair glimmered under the camera lights. Governor: 'This is a dubious day for Arizona. Putting three people to death on the same night, one after the other, in the death chamber at the state prison in Florence, is not a laudable distinction for this fine state. Nor is hanging one. But I didn't pick the dates. Their respective judges did. The law is the law. And it will be carried out. And with that said, tonight I will have a single man do the honors of pulling the level that will end the lives of three men. That man will be a person whom everyone here respects and loves dearly for his contribution to corrections. I give you Corporal Punishment.' * In mere moments, Corporal Punishment steps out from beneath the shadows. Still bruised from Stable Wars Vi, he smiles softly and waves at the media before him. In his left hand, he holds a large Lock of Joshua Dunn's hair. *The governor smiled and nodded, signaling the end of his statement and triggering the media battalion before him to explode in a simultaneous, 'Governor, governor, governor.' He pointed at a young woman who seemed surprised she had been called on. Press agent: 'Uh, Governor Williams,' *Just as Williams was about to point at someone else, she piped up. Press Agent: 'The judges did a ruling thing, uh, about the, uh, hanging thing.' *Groans dripped like acid around the room. Governor: 'Yes,' *He was too much of a politician to actually patronize the young reporter before rolling cameras. Press Agent: 'The three-judge panel ordered that a prisoner does not have the right to request media coverage for his own execution. He can't invite in cameras the way he's allowed to invite a family member.' *More hands stabbed the air, but the governor wasn't finished. Press Agent: 'However, the judges ruled the state, specifically the governor, can decide if cameras would be allowed in.' *The reporters were on their feet, renewing the chant. *The Corporal raised his hand, pointing at Blowhard. *Stan Blowhard grabbed the cue. Blowhard: 'Governor, will you allow News 37 to air the hanging live?' Governor: 'No.' *The Governor snapped and turns away from the podium as he hurries out of the room. Corporal: 'That is fine Stanny Boy. Get back to the newsroom and figure something out. This is no different than Last nights Stable Wars episode where it took time for you to get the scoop on Joshua Dunn's transformation into Jessica Simpson Dunn. With that said, I will go talk to the Governor about me assisting this inmate into the other side by way of hanging. I will allow his other guards to do the other three executions. That way, you can at least interview me directly afterwards.' *A short time later, Stan Blowhard ran into the News 37 newsroom, sprinted past the cubicles, hurdled a trash can and turned the corner into the news conference room, just in time for the afternoon meeting. The Reverend was already preaching to the choir. Reverend: 'If it bleeds...' *the large black man bellowed, immediately followed by the raised voices of every reporter, producer and photographer in the room. 'It leads!' Reverend: 'That's right!' *shaking his fist at the group as sweat beaded up on his half-bald scalp. Reverend: 'Never forget it. Our job is more than information. If we don't show them the blood, how can we help the people?' 'We can't,' shouted a red-headed photographer in the back of the room. Reverend:' That's right, Harry. We can't. If we hadn't showed them the blood of Vietnam, our boys and girls may still be over there. If we hadn't showed them the blood of the civil rights movement, I wouldn't be standing here before you. Am I right?' 'Right,' answered the congregation. *The big man was called the Reverend because he had spent five years pounding the pulpits of several churches throughout the South. Before taking up his traveling preaching job, Jackson Jesse was a popular news director at one of the top stations in Texas. Blowhard: 'Reverend, the governor said...' J. Jesse: 'I know what the good governor said Mr. Blowhard,' *the news director says without turning around: 'But what about the hanging? How do we cover it now?' *The Reverend swaggered away from his congregation and stepped toward the young reporter. J. Jesse: 'Mr. Blowhard, I appreciate your enthusiasm for death.' *The meeting room filled with laughter. Stan held up his hand and the laughter died. Blowhard: 'I've made a call,' he said. 'I spoke with an old friend of mine at the state prison. Since we cannot air the execution live, my friend will let us have an exclusive look at the gallows... Corporal Style' A news reporter frowned:. 'But everyone has video of the gallows already.' J. Jesse: 'That's right, but no one has been allowed on it.' *'Oohs' and 'ahs' rose from the group. Blowhard: (smiling) 'We can get on the gallows and my good friend Corporal Punishment will explain exactly how a hanging works?' J. Jesse:' That's right, Stan. But we can't do it live. You've got to get yourself out to the prison right now and record it. And you do a good job. I want to see you standing on that trapdoor with the noose around your neck. Got it?' Blowhard: (tightened his red tie and pretended to smooth out non-existent wrinkles on his blue blazer.) 'Not only do I have it,' he said. 'But I'll look good doing it.' 'All right, Mr. Blowhard,' *said a blonde woman in the group. 'You go, Stan,' *said another young woman. 'Take it to the rim, baby,'* the sports guy in the back of the room called out. *The Reverend turns and points at Harry the camera man. J. Jesse: 'You go with him, Harry.' Harry: 'I'm on my way,' *Harry and Stan run out of the room. ******************************************************* *Within an hour, Blowhard and Harry were inside the prison walls at Florence. The reporter stared at the maze of beams and cross braces which a couple of prison guards seemed to be double-checking. The gallows was made of rough, unfinished lumber and placed in the corner of an open courtyard. The prison spokesman, Corporal Punishment, was explaining to Stan Blowhard how the hanging would take place. Corporal: 'You see that burlap bag attached to the rope?' *he asks the reporter as he points to a large, bulky brown sack. Blowhard: 'Yeah,' (Trying to hide his eagerness to get up on the platform and start recording himself going through the paces of a hanging.) Corporal: 'That bag is filled with dirt and weighs exactly the same as Bubba Dunn. Right now, it's sitting on a trap door. The same trap door where Dunn will stand in about 9 hours.' *The Corporal grins at Blowhard. Corporal: 'Barring any stay of execution, of course.' Blowhard: 'Of course. But how do you open the trapdoor?' Corporal: 'That's the only modern addition.' *The Corporal strolls over to the gallows. *Adjacent to the steps was a phone book-sized electronic console mounted on a steel pole stabbed in the ground. A cable ran down the pole, along the ground and up one of the wooden legs of the gallows. *Punishment pointed to a single red button about the size of a half-dollar in the center of the console. Corporal: 'When the warden gives the cue, the hangman will press this button,' *he then turns to point to the underbelly of the platform. Corporal: 'An electronic locking system under the trap door will slide a steel bolt backward, the door will drop and Bubba will meet his maker.' Blowhard: 'Why a new system? Why not just do it the old way?' Corporal: 'Because the old way of dropping the door sometimes didn't work. The door would jam. In the old days, when that happened, it was seen as divine intervention and the prisoner was allowed to go free.' Blowhard: 'So, in other words, you don't want God interfering,' *Corporal Punishment frowns and he gives the reporter a stern look. Corporal: 'The good lord is all powerful. He has the power to interfere if He wants. What we've done is improve the design from past human mistakes. As a great man once told me, 'Justice is only as strong as the message it sends to the masses.' Blowhard: 'Do you mind if we get started?' Corporal: 'Be my guest.' *Just as the reporter was about to take the thirteen steps up to the platform one of the guards stop him. *The guard was a large man with rum red cheeks and a boulder for a belly. Guard: 'Don't do anything cute, T.V. boy,' Blowhard: 'Cute?' Guard: 'Yeah, like standing on the trapdoor or putting the noose around your neck. That's off limits.' *Stan glanced up at the platform as the other guard tossed the burlap bag off the platform and came down the stairs. Corporal: 'Don't worry, I'm a professional.' *The camera man tosses Blowhard a wireless microphone. The reporter reaches behind his back and clips the matchbox-sized transmitter to the waistband of his slacks. He clips the tiny microphone on his tie, being careful to hide the wire. Blowhard places his foot on the first step. Corporal: 'It's Corporal time! I mean, It's show time!' *As the reporter made his way up to the platform, The camera man was calling out reminders. Camera man: 'We need to shoot three versions. One each for the 5, 6, and 10 o'clock newscasts. Got that, Mr. Blowhard?' Blowhard: 'What will you call me after this? The hangman, maybe?' Corporal: 'Nope, it will be more like HANG NAIL if you asked me. The only reference to a hanging will take place to a girl this Sunday and this HANG GIRL has hair as rough as a mop head.' *The prison spokesman looked on as Blowhard strolls around the platform, recording his explanations of a death by hanging. The large prison guard stands near the console and the gallows' steps, staring at the reporter. Blowhard: 'That should take care of 5 and 6. Now for the 10.' *Blowhard rubbed his chin and stared at the noose laying on the trapdoor then followed the rope to the overhead beam where it was wrapped around several times. He then looked down at the prison guard who had one leg hiked up on the first step. Blowhard:' Do you think I could at least pick up the noose?' *The guard scowled and glances at the Corporal. *Corporal Punishment smiles and nods. Guard: 'Okay. But stay off the trap door.' *Blowhard picks up the noose. Blowhard: 'Got it.' *The reporter steps to the rear of the platform. Blowhard: 'Ready, Harry?' Camera Man: 'What are you going to say?' Blowhard: 'Since we'll be less than two hours away from the execution by the time this airs, I'll make a time reference. Then I'll take a couple of steps toward you so you can get a good shot of me holding the noose.' Camera Man: 'Gotcha,' *The Camera Man places his eye behind the camera's viewfinder. Camera Man: 'I'm rolling.' *Stan Blowhard clears his throat. Blowhard: 'Three. Two. One. Barring a miracle. Barring a stay of execution. Bubba Dunn will stand here on this platform in less than two hours to face the ultimate punishment.' *Stan takes a step forward. The prison guard moves his foot to the second step, craning his neck to see where Blowhards feet were. The reporter was inches from the trapdoor as he held up the noose, which hung in the air like a hollow teardrop. Blowhard: 'This rope will be placed around his neck.' *Stan Blowhard slips the rope over his head as he speaks. *The guard's head swiveled toward the Corporal, who watches Stan, then back at Stan. Blowhard: 'His hands will be handcuffed behind his back,' *He places his own hands in the same position. Blowhard: 'Then the convicted killer of a nine-year-old girl will step on this trapdoor.' *Stan Blowhard steps forward, placing both feet on the trapdoor. 'Now?' *The big-bellied prison guard shouted as he placed one hand on the corner of the console and one foot on the second step. Corporal: 'AFFIRMATIVE SOLDIER! AFFIRMATIVE!' *The guard removes his foot. Blowhard:'Aauugghh,' *Stan Blowhard yelps as he tumbles, his arms flailing, giving him the look of an obese, panicking pinwheel. *Cpl. Punishment looks over his shoulder at the now motionless Stan Blowhard. Corporal: Is this not a strange situation for that reporter to be in. Look at him. See how accidents can happen? It is not so different than at Stable Wars when Jessica Dunn fell into my cattle prod by accident. He, excuse me... *He clears his throat. Corporal: I mean SHE fell head first into some pretty toxic glue. Now what are the chances in that? And then to have a mop head right there directly beside the glue! What a pity. Corporal: And now he as sure as I am standing here, and Stan is dangling over there, is Dunn contemplating why or even how could this have happened to him? I feel for Jessica right now. She must be devastated as she curls her corn rolls and clicks those red slippers her mother gave her. Can you see her right now with her eyes shut saying, THERE'S NO PLACE LIKE HOME, THERE IS NO PLACE LIKE HOME!' Corporal: 'But the fact still remains that Dorothy is no longer in Kansas, she is in the land of LOCKDOWN, where the KB rules the roost. *He drops his head momentarily Corporal: Now Jessica Dunn, don't let the mop head get you down. Because that is just the start. As long as you try and get in our way, I will have new and improved ways of making you hurt really bad. You can believe every word I am saying right now Jessica, You have not even touched the surface of what I am capable of doing. The KB stable is ready to make your life miserable. You have a choice Miss Dunn. Either walk in that ring Sunday and take the next level of cruelty by my hands, or stay home and soak that head of yours in some tomato juice. The choice is truly yours... CORPORAL STYLE!
