Necessity...

29 Sep 2004

Seymour Almasy — Ruined Church. RP#1 for Live and Loud 4

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trading up. Wealthy businessmen trade loyal, loving wives for gorgeous money-hungry supermodels. With the help of credit, car owners trade their shoddier vehicles for a better, newer model. So too does Seymour Almasy seek to trade up. The THC Championship, while a noble prize, is not the biggest prize in the UWF. There are bigger. Two of them, the Intercontinental and North American, will be on the line in Almasy’s match. Which one, then, will it be? ---------------------- The site of today’s tale is an old, ruined church. Its location is unknown, especially to the average person. However, Seymour Almasy is anything but average. Outside the broken building, the UWF’s reigning ruler of hardcore wrestling stands. His chest is bare, allowing the roman numerals tattooed into his flesh to be easily visible. Seymour Almasy: Since my return to the Universal Wrestling Federation, I have been undefeated. Every single battle I have fought, I have been victorious in. But now…the task is not simple victory. It is determining WHICH victory to achieve. Almasy smiles, patting the THC Championship which rests around his armored waist. Seymour Almasy: For you see, my match at Live and Loud 4 contains six men, three ladders, and three titles. I have three chances to walk out of this prestigious event the same way I came in…a champion. He takes a seat on the ground, his back to the ruined house of worship. All around him, the rubble bears witness to the continued collapse of the once proud building. Seymour Almasy: At worst, I can simply grab my THC Title from atop one of the ladders, and leave in exactly the same station. But…no. I am undefeated at Live and Loud. I wish to make a bigger impact. THAT shall be accomplished by taking either the Intercontinental or the North American championships. But alas…I have a small dilemma. Which one shall I choose? The confident expression on the THC Champion’s face deepens. He brushes back a lock of his now black hair, eyes burning with a combination of passion and aggression. Seymour Almasy: Moraless possesses the UWF North American Championship. Of the three titles in the match, it is the most prestigious. It is also a title that I have never held, despite being in the UWF for two years. On the surface, it seems the easy choice. And yet…there is one title I would, for the moment at least, rather have. The Intercontinental Championship resides in Seymour’s mind as “the one that got away”. It was a belt he had the opportunity to wrest from Joshua Dunn on Mount Gagazet, only to fall short. Seymour Almasy: Yes, it is the UWF Intercontinental Title around Misfit’s waist that I crave. The only championship to elude me that I have truly set my sights on. And now, it is within my grasp. All I must do to get it is simply climb a ladder, and pull it down from the wire that it is hanging on. Truly, it is unfortunate that I cannot simply stay in the match, and pull down every single title in the contest. I would be a triple champion, and complete my collection of all seven championships the UWF has ever recognized. But the powers that be are afraid to let me gather that much power. With good reason, of course. It is unlikely any of those titles would leave my waist once strapped on. So…they force me to trade my THC Championship if I want to improve my title situation. Once again, the belt on his waist is patted. This time, however, it is more wistful. A farewell pat, as it were. Seymour Almasy: As important as titles are…this contest is about much more than gold. This match is about instilling fear and respect in the superstars of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Last year, I main-evented Live and Loud 3, and defeated Kevin Starr. I must demonstrate this year that I am an even MORE dangerous and capable competitor than the man who was the UWF World Champion for over six months in 2003. That is…why I am here. The Final Fantasy rises, and for the first time, affixes his eyes on the dilapidated church. A slight nod of appreciation is the only response that Almasy has. Seymour Almasy: I stand here before the site of my transformation. The Universal Wrestling Federation has stood far too long and far too complacently in the glory of the light. True evil has disappeared. No matter how much the fans may wish to paint me as evil, none of my actions thus far have been evil. I have simply stood up for my belief…my belief that Tsunami Oni has not changed one iota. Nonetheless, if it is evil that the simpletons want, it is evil that they shall receive. An evil so great that it has never yet been unleashed to its full power. Even when I was possessed by the Millennium Necklace, I was unable to demonstrate true power. Now, however, I have no such shackles. The laugh that follows is bittersweet. To Seymour, good and evil are now just concepts. The world is a far greater shade of gray. What he fails to realize, of course, is that the business he is in has made its money for generations on black and white… Seymour Almasy: My actions towards Moraless, Misfit, Kenny Mercury, Killer Dude, and Nick Persia can all be blamed squarely on the fans. I told them, no, I told EVERYONE that Seymour Almasy would be Seymour Almasy! Not good, not bad, just Seymour! But they refuse to listen. I’m a 23 year old person. A man who wants to live his life and do what he loves. But they won’t let that happen! Therefore, they leave me one option. Everywhere else I go, I shall be Seymour Almasy. But here…in the UWF…I shall be their ruin. A slow, sly grin comes over Almasy. Clearly, he sees himself as having a plan that no one else could possibly know, a plan so demonic that it could even DESTROY THE UWF! Seymour Almasy: Ruining the UWF is a difficult task indeed. A federation known for bunnies and clowns is difficult to ruin, by any stretch of the imagination. That is why I cannot ruin it through stupidity. That’s commonplace around here! No, I must ruin it through intelligence and violence. Through deceit and destruction…and through conquering all in my path. Almasy’s eyes pan over the church, its stained glass windows long since broken by any number of the rocks lying around the ruined building… Seymour Almasy: Potential is now what I look for. Those with the potential to join me. Those with the potential to damn the hellhole that we know as the Universal Wrestling Federation. Those willing to make the transformation with me…or those simply twisted enough to be prepared to join me as they are. And once I find them, my army will be ready. But no army can survive without a general. As such, I must prepare myself for this task…a task unlike any I have ever undertaken. With a slow and steady gait, Almasy makes his way to the door of the ruined church. Oddly, unlike everything else, the door stands, seemingly unharmed by time and rocks. Seymour Almasy: That is enough of my plan for now. I must deal with the Triple Ladder Match at Live and Loud 4 first. The champions, however, are only part of the equation. Also in this contest is the “New Black Star Revolution”, consisting of Kenny Mercury and Killer Dude. Congratulations, Mr. Mercury. You have succeeded in finding an even more loudmouthed and idiotic partner than Ben Jericho in the form of Killer Dude. I think I’ve beaten Killer Dude something like four times over the course of my UWF career. And Kenny? Well, I’m sure you remember the Primetime Invitational Tournament semifinals as well as I do, don’t you? A triumphant grin appears on Almasy’s face at the comment. Seymour Almasy: It was you and me, Kenny. UWF vs. UWF in the semifinals. Just like always, Kenny, you came up a little bit short. That’s why you talk so much, isn’t it? You hope that by ranting and raving about how good you are, people will take it as gospel…and that they will forget that every time you have had a chance to step up, you have not been successful. They may forget, Kenny. I do not. In this match, you will fall short yet again. Short of gaining any championship. Short of proving yourself the Technical Wizard you seek to be. And most certainly, short of the ability level of the Final Fantasy. Almasy enters the church. The pews are battered and bruised, some broken entirely in two. Seymour runs his hand along one of them, much of the wood still smooth despite its destroyed state. Seymour Almasy: Soon enough, the time for talking will be over. I have tried to be reasonable with you all…but the fans and superstars of the UWF would not listen. They leave me no choice. They have driven me to a remedy that I will find painful to use. A remedy that I myself may regret someday. But I have no choice. They WILL respect Seymour Almasy. If not for who I am, they will respect and fear me for whom I will become here. The Final Fantasy winces slightly as he advances down the main aisle of the church. As he moves, he steps upon broken glass, shards of wood, and all manner of rubble. Seymour Almasy: I have no friends here. Not anymore. Even those who will join me…they are not my friends. They are but allies, those who side with me due to similar goals of their own. Destruction. Mayhem. Heartlessness. Before long, Almasy has ascended the dais. The Catholic Cross that should mark the building, however, is nowhere to be found. Seymour Almasy: Once upon a time, this was to be my headquarters. The place where I would spread my word to all who would listen. That plan halted…but now, it shall be utilized once more. Stepping behind the podium where the bishop would speak, the THC Champion bends down, and the sounds of exertion can be heard, as if Almasy is attempting to lift something heavy. Seymour Almasy: My suffering will be returned to those who have forced me to this extreme tenfold. They shall receive the punishment that they deserve for making me resurrect an era of my life I had left behind. They will understand my pain, my sorrow…and learn the definition of their own. Slowly, the object that Almasy lifts begins to come to light. It is a giant, heavy wooden Y, embellished with various designs and drawings. Seymour Almasy: Fantasies are for the week. It is those who SEIZE their destiny that are the truly powerful! Grunting mightily, Almasy finally gets the Y vertical, settling it into the spot where the cross would normally be. Seymour Almasy: Seymour Almasy, Maester of Yevon has returned. May Yevon have mercy on the souls of those who get in my way. I will not. His arms extend in a modified crucifix, forearms turned outward in a Y. Seymour Almasy: Welcome, everyone, to my nightmare. If it’s this bad for me…just imagine what it shall be like for you..
