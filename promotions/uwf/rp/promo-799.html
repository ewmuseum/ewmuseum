UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #799

Fandemonium RP 1! The enemy is watching!

30 Nov 2004

Joshua Justice — Russia/New York

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Five Months Ago—Russia, Near The Kazakhstan Border.] (The nighttime sky is lucid and dreary. An aged and slightly rusted tank rolls by a perimeter surrounded by a barbwire fence. Deep within the scarcely secured perimeter is a structure. Two Russians drag a man wearing the colors of red and white, Russia’s colors, towards the facility.] <Translated from Russian> Russian Soldier: <We caught this one trying to violate the perimeter barrier to the north, General Krumen.> (The unconscious man in red and white starts to come out of it as the two soldiers haul him towards General Krumen.) Prisoner: <…uhhh… What is…?> (They drop him at General Krumen’s feet.) Russian Soldier: <The neural blasters took him down just as expected.> Prisoner: <…uhnn…> (Standing next to the Russian madman known as General Krumen is standing with the German wrongdoer known only as Herr Faust. The most wicked German man alive is standing next to the most wicked Russian man alive. How interesting. Was it mentioned that both of these men hate Joshua Justice? And was it also mentioned that the feeling is reciprocated?) Krumen: Pardon the disturbance, Herr Faust… This will only take a moment, and then we can get to our business. Faust: Don’t ask for forgiveness General… I’m always happy to wait when entertainment is provided. (Herr Faust walks over to the man lying down at their feet and kicks him in the ribs. General Krumen looks slightly away.) Krumen: Keep your satisfaction to yourself, sadist. What I do here, I take no delight in. (General Krumen bends over and starts to help the man, known only as the Scarlet Sentinel, up to his feet.) Krumen: <So, even after the fall, The Crimson Corner still produces men such as yourself? I would have thought that era was long passed. Did they tell you what happened to your predecessors, Scarlet Sentinel?> Sentinel: <…General Aleksander Krumen… under the authority of President Putin…> (The Scarlet Sentinel looks up into the eyes of Krumen.) Sentinel: <…you are hereby… under arrest… for abandoning your position… for theft of government… secrets… and weapons… and crimes of treason against Mother Russia…> Krumen: <Mother Russia? I’m sorry to tell you that I am all that is left of the true Mother Russia, boy…> (General Krumen lifts up a small firearm that he has taken out of his coat. He puts it to the Scarlet Sentinel’s head and pulls the trigger.) BLAM! (The Scarlet Sentinel lays hushed and unmoving in the mud, dead as a doornail. General Krumen puts away his firearm with a disgusted look on his face. He looks over to the Russian soldier.) Krumen: <Treat his remains as if he were one of our own. When we’re finished here, I’ll let them know where they can salvage his body.> (General Krumen turns and starts to walk towards the structure with Herr Faust shortly behind him as the two soldiers start to drag the Scarlet Sentinel’s carcass away.) Krumen: Now, Herr Faust… I assume you still want to examine the items I have for sale? (The two walk deeper into the building when Herr Faust pulls out a bizarre looking gun and points it up into the air. He starts to mechanically look at the gun as if inspecting it.) Faust: What is this, exactly? Krumen: According to the schematics, it opens diminutive windows into some other dimension… Faust: This is all experimental work? Or has it been field-tested? Krumen: Some has. What is here will work, I guarantee you that, Faust. This is the last of Comrade Tupikov’s facilities, and where he kept his most priceless treasures. (Faust looks at Krumen out of the corner of his eye.) Faust: Krumen… how would your old mentor feel about what you’re doing with his inheritance? Selling KGB-developed weaponry to the highest bidder? (They walk through a pair of double-doors into a large room.) Krumen: Why do you think he left his in my hands and not his superiors? He knew if the Soviet Union were to collapse, there must still be men willing to do the right thing for the cause. And I am not selling everything you see here. Most of it is leaving this sad country, along with my men and myself. Faust: I see. Then I— (Faust stops in his tracks as he stares into a clear glass cylinder.) Faust: --mein gott! (A look deeper into the glass cylinder shows some moldy water. Barely visible is the outline of a man. Faust gets up closer and sees what looks to be a hand pressed up against the glass.) Faust: This can’t be what it looks like?! (Faust places his hand on the glass in total awe.) Krumen: Ah, yes. I’ve been going over the paperwork Tupikov left on this one. He was apparently very valuable in the Cold War. A secret weapon, of a sort, against the United States. (Barely taking his eyes off of the glass, Faust asks a question.) Faust: How much do you want for it? Krumen: I think not, Herr Faust. I have my own devices for that item. Unless, of course, you would be willing to exchange it for the Celestial Quadrangle, as it is known? (Faust turns his attention back towards Krumen.) Faust: The CQ? What do you know about that? Krumen: We know of many things you hold close, Faust. And I would value this CQ quite highly if it is what I have heard. Faust: Oh, it is, believe me. But it’s not in my possession. Even if it was, you can’t think you’d have anything that would make me give it up. (Faust turns around and does a Nazi salute to an invisible commander.) Faust: Though I can see why you’d desire it… You’d have the power to rebuild your Socialist Republic, wouldn’t you? Krumen: That is one possibility, among many. (Faust turns around and faces Krumen again, this time with a small smirk on his face.) Faust: Well, you can keep dreaming… My spies are combing the globe for signs of it even as we speak. The CQ will be mine, and no one else’s. (Faust turns away from Krumen. He smugly smiles.) Faust: And when that day comes, this whole world will know fear, General… like you’ve never seen. [The Present Day—New York City] (Being the evil Nazi that Herr Faust is, he is also rich. He stands in his penthouse suite with a fine glass of aged wine in his hand. He stares out at all of the buildings, looking at their gleaming lights. Also like the maniacal Nazi that he is, he talks to himself!) Faust: The CQ is mine again, after so many years of searching and waiting… (Herr Faust takes a sip of his wine while admiring the view.) Faust: …and this time it won’t go to waste. (He turns back towards his suite and walks into the middle of the room.) Faust: Because this time my plans are laid out perfectly. (He kneels down in front of a glass case in the middle of the room and admires a glowing almost translucent cube sitting in the middle of the case.) Faust: Just a few hours more to go until midnight, and then Paris, London, and, of course, Manhattan… will all burn for me. And we will harvest this death and destruction… (He gulps down the rest of the wine in the glass and then stares at the cube.) Faust: And transmute it into power… to fill this inadequate CQ, melded together from shards of the ones that were taken from me… (He turns around and starts to walk back to the window.) Faust: …or even shattered in my own hands by him. Joshua… Joshua Justice. (His eyes turn all evil and dark as he stares out at the city some more. He sure does that a lot.) Faust: This night is especially for you, Justice. Which is why I’ve got a ringside seat to watch this city you love die. But then, I’ve had months to prepare, lying in a cell and staring at blank walls… (Down in the city, unbeknownst to Herr Faust, Joshua Justice is walking down the street. A few guys in ski masks run out of a bank holding guns, some bags, and a woman. Justice attacks them at full force. Herr Faust continues talking to himself up in his suite.) Faust: …while my spies kept you under watch. They’ve seen your tragedies… everything you’ve lost. (After busting them all up, a ladder comes down off of a helicopter. Justice looks up and then starts to climb up the ladder.) Faust: And they’ve kept you distracted, too, while you try to keep your game face on. Try to act like everything is just the same as it was before. (Faust looks in the window seeing his slight reflection stare back at him. But what he truly sees is Joshua Justice staring back at him.) Faust: But I know you, Justice… I know what losing people does to you. You’re at your lowest point, and that’s why you’ll never see me coming this time… The enemy is ready to strike! Joshua Justice doesn’t know it, but his strongest enemy is ready to beat him down! How much does that suck!? If Herr Faust gets his hands on everything he needs, the world will be in chaos! TOTAL CHAOS! And it all starts at UWF Fandemonium! Not only do I, Joshua Justice, have a shot at regaining the Television Title, I am placed in a match of HUGE proportions. I am in the UWF Tag Team Match along with many other teams. There are some big names in this match that will take place in Venetian Home. Killer Dude and Nick Persia… Fizbane the Fabulous and Seymour Almasy… Adien Pryde and Tsunami Oni… Mike Montgomery and Clyde… Jamie Shock and Kenny Mercury… And then there’s my team. Joshua Justice and THE COMMUNIST! What in the hell were the fans thinking!? I hate The Communist with passion that nobody knows around here! He stands for everything I am against! But I don’t care. I don’t care that they have put me in this match with my most hated enemy. I’m still going to come out the winner. I am still going to walk out of that house holding the UWF Tag Team Championship around my waist. I am Champion material and I will show you that! Speaking of having a Championship Title around my waist, let’s talk about this UWF Television Title. I was robbed out of that title! I had it won for three freakin’ days before it got stolen from me! Now I get yet another chance. A ‘Tables, Ladders, and Cats’ match. What the HELL is that!? Hot Mask… Adien Pryde… Dreammaker… Craig Wright… Killer Dude… Malcolm Kennedy… AND ME, Joshua Justice. They booked me against a bunch of losers why again? Are they sorry that I got booked into a match with my most hated enemy that they thought they would go easy on me? I can handle ALL of these guys. They are NOTHING compared to me. Hell, I’ve beaten Dreammaker like two times already! Well, I would go on and on about how I am the best and I will win, but I know that you all of you already know this! You know that I am going to win this match! I wouldn’t be surprised if you just sat back and let me win. In fact, I think maybe you should. And with that, I’ll tell you one last thing. Make sure to drink plenty of orange juice because it’s good and healthy!
