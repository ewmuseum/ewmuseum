UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #46

Lonely Road To Glory

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* The top of the mountain is a lonely place. There are always people trying to knock you off, and alot of times those same people are the very people who helped put you there. Who can you really trust? Some would say it's the people you are fighting against everyday, because they don't hide their feelings. They make it known to you that they want you dead. Those people who pump you up and then turn on you the second you reach the mountain top are the real enemy. Trust is a small word with huge limits. Who can you trust? * ( The scene begins on the front porch of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. He is sitting in his porch swing staring out into the sky as the sun is beginning to set. He appears to be off in his own world when he notices the camer that has been set up by Tina. He looks down at the boards under his feet for a few seconds before raising his head back up and looking into the lense. ) Ryder: Why do we fight? I mean it doesn't really change anything, because once the match is over the same thing happens again another night. A fight between two people who truely hate each other doesn't ever change those feelings. All it does is give the person who wins bragging rights for a few days until the rematch. Well I don't know about the rest of the men here, but I fight for me. I fight for a chance to hold the gold and the opportunity to show the whole world that I am the best in the business. I fight for the World Title, because when you hold the gold it means that you are the top dawg. Everyone wants to be you, but that also means that for them to be you they have to eliminate you. I'm one of the eliminators, because I want gold and that means I must take it off the man who wears it now. That's a fight that will come on another day though. The gold that i'm fighting for now won't come off the waist of another man. It's being held in a tournament, a tournament I intend to win. * Ryder looks back down at the ground a few second before continuing * Ryder: In order for me to win this tournament it's very likely that i'll be facing a man that I hold in high regards. There is one man in this tournament that I won't be talkin trash to. One man will get Ruff Ryder the man, instead of Ruff Ryder the wrestler. I will go toe to toe with one of the best wrestlers in the UWF right now and that man is White Wolf. The other wrestlers in this tournament don't matter. There are only two people who will be allowed to walk away with the North American Title and that's either White Wolf or myself. My first round opponents are a joke. Neither have even spoke a word since the match was scheduled. That's good on their part because I will destroy the first person who utters a sound. The time is now. The lonely road to the gold begins here. White Wolf and Ruff Ryder are about to travel two lonely paths, but what will happen if those paths should happen to cross....only time will tell. Good luck Wolf, it's you or me...nobody else. * Ryder looks up at the sun as it is almost out of view, but before the promo ends he turns back to the camera * Ryder: Oh and Mr. Shock....all your friends are backstabbers? We'll see. ( Ryder stands up and looks out as the sun disappears. The scene fades to black as the promo comes to an end. )
