Darkness Envelops All

date unknown

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Darkness envelops all.] [Children scream.] [Women cry.] [Rodents everywhere suddenly explo--] Rick: Oww! […] Rick: Who the hell just kicked me in the leg? Turn the Rod damn lights back on!! Larson: Hehehe … something just touched my— [The lights suddenly come back on!] [In the ring, the Trailer Park Luchador, an awe-inspiring force of nature at 5’6” and 165 lbs., is bouncing up and down, primed and ready for his upcoming match!] [In between Rick and Larson, the demonic force, and the brilliantly dark mind behind the Trailer Park Luchador’s rise to prominence (which, in case you’ve forgotten, got him all the way to the point where he ALMOST beat Tigera at Live & Loud) … Baron Black Scourge has thrown on the headphones, a guest announcer for the upcoming match.] [In Larson’s lap sits El Chupacabra, masked jobber brainwashed into subservience to the desire of the Scourge. Larson screams.] Larson: Oh Rod!!!! [El Chupacabra is thrown off of Larson’s lap. Larson opens his bottle of water, empties it out on his lap, and brushes it off psychotically.] Larson: Unclean! Unclean! Unclean!!! [Around the announcer’s table, the Dark Army has gathered. Joining El Chupacabra is Crab Mask, Jr., both of their masks now marked by BLACK … to signify that they’ve been brainwashed into evilness, obviously.] Rick: I … umm … welcome, Mr. Scourge? Scourge: Thank you, fool. Tonight is going to be a big night for myself, and thus I have decided to grant my precence here at the announce table … you may thank me. Larson: I take it that means you have a plan for tonight? Scourge: I have seventy-two different nefarious plans for tonight, Larson. Each and every last one more insidious and evil than the one prior to it. Mark my words … the Trailer Park Luchador shall not lose this match! Rick: Speaking of which … wasn’t this plan with the Luchador going evil meant to get him victories? Scourge: As I have said, there is no better way to move up the ranks of an organization than a turn to … the dark side! Rick: How’s that worked out for him? Scourge: … there have been … complications … Rick: He’s lost every single match for three months since you corrupted him. Scourge: Complications! Rick: He’s just as bad as he was before! Scourge: Silence!!!! Let this match get underway! [Baron Black Scourge nervously twirls his moustache and goes silent.] Larson: I wish I had a generically evil moustache … Rick: And here comes Gary Clark! [The opening strums of … uh … actually, nothing comes out of the audio system. Gary Clark steps out onto the stage, confused by the lack of music, but shrugs and acknowledges the cheers anyway.] Scourge: Yes, Clark … shrug! Shrug you fool! My power extends everywhere! Rick: You corrupted the audio technician? Scourge: The psychological edge, commentator #1! Rick: Rick! Scourge: Whatever. My plan begins to unfold beautifully! RUN IN FEAR, CLARK! [Completely unaware of the Scourge’s maniacal ramblings, Gary Clark walks down to the ring in relative silence, slips under the bottom rope, and stretches out, his eyes never leaving the bouncing Trailer Park Luchador.] Larson: And we’re about to get under way, Rick! So … Baron … over/under at 45 seconds? Scourge: … [The referee, Corky Spectre, steps between the two men. He approaches the Luchador … checks each elbow pad, then each knee pad, and finally both of his boots, making sure the evil Lucha didn’t bring any decidedly foreign objects in with him. Satisfied, he nods stupidly.] Scourge: Like a man with the pedigree of the Trailer Park Luchador would need to bring in outside objects! Pshaw! Rick: … [Spectre moves to Clark, checking his elbowpads, and then his kneepads. The Trailer Park Luchador immediately drops down, slipping a hand into his kneepad. Clark lifts up one leg for Corky to check his boot, and …] *wham!* [The Trailer Park Luchador goes flying across the ring, dropping Gary Clark instantly with a single shot!] Rick: What in the hell was that?!?! Scourge: Look at that punch, Rick! Only a fist possessed by the DEVIL HIMSELF could drop a man like that!!! Larson: Ahahahahaha … *DING DING DING* [The Trailer Park Luchador, brandishing an obvious pair of brass knuckles, leaps onto the body of Gray Clark, making the pin.] ONE! TWO! THR--!! [Gary Clark kicks out, and the Luchador goes flying.] Rick: What … he has brass knuckles! And that was the fastest count I’ve ever seen in my life!!!! Larson: YES~! Rick: You paid off the referee, too!?!? Scourge: My evil influence knows no bounds, foolish generic commentator! [The Trailer Park Luchador makes another leap at Gary Clark, trying to land another loaded blow, but Clark spins ‘round, drilling him with an elbow. The Luchador screams out in pain, grabs his nose, and rolls around on the mat. Clark kicks him in the gut, reaches down, and rips off the knuckles. He turns around, holding the knuckles, and screams at Corky Spectre.] Scourge: Referee!! He’s got a foreign object!! DISQUALIFY HIM~! Rick: He took it off your man!!! Scourge: DQ~! [Corky Spectre reacts to Gary Clark’s complaint the only way an unbiased referee can.] [… by spinning around, dropping trouser, and mooning him.] Rick: This is absurd … it’s two-on-one! Larson: Yes it is, Rick … but to be fair, if you combine the Trailer Park Luchador and Corky Spectre, you still only have half a normal man. Rick: Point taken. Scourge: Idiots. Larson: Plus! Gary had this coming! He had no reason to assault Corky Spectre last week (slash month). Get your revenge, Corky! [Corky shakes his bare rump from side to side as Gary Clark shakes his head. Gary throws the brass knuckles out of the ring, and …] *Testicle crunch!!* [Upper cut to the testicles, and Clark goes down!] Rick: … Scourge: My evil plan is working, you fools … working!!! Larson: Which evil plan is this? Scourge: That was #13 of the seventy-two possibilities I told you of earlier. Clark will not win this match. Rick: … [Satisfied, Corky yanks his pants back up, just as the Trailer Park Luchador dives onto Clark’s prone body once again.] ONETWOTHR--!!!!! [Gary Clark sends the TPL flying once again.] Rick: I have NEVER seen a count that fast! Larson: Who knew Corky could finally find something he was good at? Scourge: Grr. [Gary Clark continues to argue with Corky Spectre, massaging his broken testes as he does so, but the Trailer Park Luchador has other plans. He turns to the announce table, nods, and deftly catches the chair that El Chupacabra throws to him.] Rick: For the love of … [Trailer Park Luchador charges, chair overhead …] *CRACK*~! [And down goes Corky Spectre.] Scourge: … surrounded by fucking INCOMPETENCE!!!!!! [The Luchador cries out in horror … the moment he spins around, Gary Clark levels him with a brutal dropkick, square to the nose! And down he goes!] Scourge: Attack! [Within seconds, both El Chupacabra and Crab Mask, Jr. are both standing on the top turnbuckle, ready for their assault! They dive … they twist … they twirl … … … … … … they both cry out as Gary Clark steps aside, and they crash into one another.] Scourge: … Rick: What evil plan are we up to now? Scourge: I could have your innards eaten by rabid pigs if it pleased me. [Gary Clark scoops up the chair … seconds later, both El Chupacabra and the Crab Mask are back to their feet, but one chair shot takes out both men.] [And just to be safe, Clark hits the Luchador with it as well the second he gets up.] [And that’s when the next wave started.] “Dreidel dreidel deridel … I made you out of clay … dreidel dreidel dreidel … with dreidle I will play.” [As the haunting voices of children fades over the speaker system, Chad Gadya’s “Adir Hu” kicks in … and HE arrived.] [A lone figure stood on the ramp … dressed in a simple white singlet, a blue Jewish star across his chest, a long, black, curly mop of hair atop his head. The fans look dumbfounded, but the pyros erupt … and he roars within the shower of sparks as if he were Goldberg himself!] [Until his curly hair catches a few sparks, causing him to scream, patting at his hair frantically, and running in a dead sprint toward the ring to get away from the pyro.] Rick: Wait a second … I know that guy. That’s … that’s … Scourge: THE RAVING JEWISH DESTROYER~! MWAHA! Rick: … you recruited the eighteen year-old from FaceOff?! Scourge: … I take what I can get, Rick. Being evil incarnate doesn’t mean I have an unlimited budget. Larson: Ahem … Mitch Goldstein, Rick! The RJD is here! Fifteen people are marking out as we speak! Rick: Rather liberal estimate there, Larson … [Gary Clark obviously has absolutely no idea who this scrawny figure is, but looks on bemused as he charges forward. The RJD deftly leaps to the edge of the ring, and in another leap he’s on the top turnbuckle … and there it is! Goldstein leaps into the air, curling his legs inward as he goes into a cannonball move, and he spins within the air, flying straight at the prone Gary Clark!] Scourge: THE DREIDEL DEMOLITION! FEAR IT!!!! [Gary Clark steps aside, and the Raving Jewish Destroyer crashes onto the mat with a yelp.] Scourge: … … … I’m going to slip my fucking wrists before this night is over. Rick: You are the worst evil genius EVER. Scourge: … [Mitch Goldstein gets back to his feet, holding his rear with both hands, in obvious pain. Wincing, he turns to Gary Clark, and …] *CRACK* [Down goes the RJD with a brutal chair shot!] Scourge: … next? Teenage mutant ninja turtles Teenage mutant ninja turtles Teenage mutant ninja turtles Heroes in a half shell, turtle power! Rick: Oh fucking hell. Scourge: Yes … go, my demented turtle … kill in my name!!! They're the worlds most fearsome fighting teens [ we're really hip ] Teenage mutant ninja turtles They're heroes in a half shell and they're green [ hey, get a grip ] Teenage mutant ninja turtles [And there he comes! El Turtlé, the EVIL ninja turtle, comes charging out of the back. The crowd gasps in horror as they see the extent of the damage done on their favorite turtle, however. Deranged, nearly melted facial features … disfigured in every way … rabid and hate-filled, he charges the ring!] When the evil shredder attacks These turtle boys don't cut him no slack Teenage mutant ninja turtles Teenage mutant ninja turtles Larson: I think this is the first time we’ve seen El Turtlé ever since Clyde … umm … trying to disfigure him in boiling acid, if I remember right, Rick. Rick: No, he tried to kill him. Disfiguring him just sort of happened. Scourge: And now he belongs to me! Nyaha! Splinter taught them to be ninja teens [ he's a radical rat ] Teenage mutant ninja turtles Leonardo leads, Donatello does machines [ that's a fact, Jack ] Teenage mutant ninja turtles [Gary Clark swings the chair, but it just bounces off of El Turtlé’s skull … literally, because it’s showing through in a few places. Clark cries out, but El Turtlé opens wide and begins trying to eat Gary Clark’s head.] Raphael is K00L but rude Michaelangelo is a party dude Teenage mutant ninja turtles Teenage mutant ninja turtles Teenage mutant ninja turtles Heroes in a half shell, turtle power [Gary Clark lashes about frantically … kicking, punching, scratching, but nothing will make El Turtlé, the evil ninja turtle, release his lockjaw on his scalp.] Rick: This has gone on long enough … this is … wait … it’s six-on-one at this point! Larson: So five-on-one was okay, but it was that sixth guy who REALLY pushed it over the edge? Scourge: Eat him, my demented turtle, eat him! [And just then … El Turtlé grimaces and spits Gary Clark out.] Scourge: What? NO! [El Turtlé spits again, wiping at his mouth, trying to get the taste of Clark’s hair-gel out of his mouth. Disturbed, Turtlé eats El Chupacabra and walks off.] Rick: … Larson: … Scourge: … … … … … … … … [Gary Clark shakes his head from side to side … happy to be out of the fire … but then he finds himself standing face to face with yet another member of Scourge’s demonic army. A massive, freakish brick wall of a man stands before Gary Clark, his chest bare, a giant motorcycle helmet-looking object over his head. Clark gulps noticeably.] Rick: What are we at now, Scourge? Seven-on-one? Larson: Technically, El Turtlé left, and he ate one of them before leaving, so I think we’re back to five-on-one. Rick: I hate you. Scourge: I told you, Rick! The Trailer Park Luchador cannot lose this time! [Gary Clark continues to stare at the brick wall in front of him … who casually motions at him to attack.] Scourge: Behold my newest addition, you fools … my terminator, my agent of destruction! Forged in the fires of Chernobyl all those years ago, my monster has now reached the age of maturity … and just last week, I bought him from a traveling freak-show for a pack of cigarettes, using my evil negotiating tactics! And so I have brought him here … an irradiated freak of nature! DESTROY HIM, MY NAMELESS CREATURE! [Gary Clark has been unloading against the monster through Baron Black Scourge’s entire soliloquy, but to no effect … after hitting him with everything he has, he hasn’t even managed to make the monster take a step backwards. Gary gulps, knowing what’s about to happen … but it doesn’t change the result. The monster reaches out, palming Gary Clark’s face, and lifts him high into the air … Clark’s head now ten feet off the ground.] Scourge: OBLITERATE HIM! Rick: Dear lord … [And it would have continued this way as well. With no way to fight back, Gary Clark would have been ripped in half by the freak of nature … torn asunder, piece by piece, with no way of retaliation.] [That is what WOULD have happened, had not the massive freak in the ring seen something shiny in the audience.] Freak: Ooo … pwetty. Hehehehehehehehe. [Completely forgetting his mission, Gary Clark is thrown aside, and the monster bounds out of the ring, going after the person in the audience, who is immediately terrified for his own life. Screams erupt from the crowd, which parts like the Red Sea, and the monster chases after the man with the shiny object.] Scourge: … Rick: … Scourge: No … this can’t be! Larson: Umm … Baron? Scourge: That’s it … that was my last plan! HOW!? I planned everything so perfectly … so incredibly detailed was my plan! Backup after backup … and each and every God damn one of them failed! Rick: … Scourge: I hope you all die painfully of syphilis and gingivitis, you fools!!!!!! [Rubbing his poor head, Gary Clark stumbles around the ring … but he quickly sees the hurt Trailer Park Luchador and knows what he must do.] Rick: Wait a second … there seem to be something happening in the crowd… Larson: Who the hell is that!? I think … oh dear lord, we’re being invaded by terrorists! HELP! [A lone man dressed in all black, his masked face covered by his wet, long, black, unkempt hair, can be seen running through the crowd and towards the ring completely disregarding anyone in his path, elbowing, pushing and punching anyone who comes into his way -- fan or otherwise. As the fan attention and camera's move away from the match and onto the ongoing commotion in crowd, we see that the lone man dressed in all black appears to be none other than Slasher ... climbing over the guardrail, he ducks by the announcers table and waits and watches as the TPL receives one hell of a beating from a commanding Gary Clark(e)..] Rick: What's Slasher doing out here?! What has Slasher got to do with either Clark(e) or the TPL?! Maybe he's after you.. Larson: Where the fuck is security..?! I don't trust that mentalist! [Baron Black Scourge … nods silently to Slasher, grinning from ear-to-ear as he does so.] Rick: Wait … what?! You said you were done! Scourge: Yeah. I lied. I’m evil. Sue me, eh? Rick: But … but … Slasher … no! [Gary Clark(e) executes a snap DDT on the TPL before violently tossing him outside of the ring and all the way onto the entrance ramp. Standing in the middle of the ring, his head down and hands on his hips, Clark(e) catches his breath totally unaware that Slasher has now entered the ring like a silent assassin and is stood crouched behind him.] [The crowd goes MENTAL!! It's like a pantomime with many of the thousands in attendance shouting "behind you" with the other half cheering or Jeering. A confused Clark(e) looks up and catches a glimpse of the manic and crazy Slasher stood behind him on the Univeral-Tron in front of him… he spins around, futilely trying to defend himself, but it does no good at all.] Kick! Wham! Coup De Grace! [Clark(e) is caught with a kick to the stomach and the sweetest of Ace Crushers.. as Clark(e)'s face hits the mat with a sickening crunch some in the crowd Cheer and some Jeer.. the TPL is beginning to come around outside the ring and is helped up by Corky Spectre, who is desperately trying to drag him back into the ring to finish the Baron’s dastardly plan. Slasher takes that as his exit cue, diving out of the ring.] Rick: Slasher!! Slasher has just laid out Clark(e) with the CDG!!.. This is a direct message for James Mason from WNU! Larson: Or so it would seem... [The TPL is helped into the ring by Corky Spectre, who is grinning from ear-to-ear, finally seeing his chance at revenge. Corky dives forward with one last push of strength from his tiny body, throwing the body of the Trailer Park Luchador onto that of Gary Clark. Corky drops to the mat, extends his arm, and proceeds to make the fastest count in wrestling history.] ONETWOTHREE!! Larson: THE LUCHADOR HAS DONE IT!!! FOR THE LOVE OF ALL THAT’S HOLY … THE TRAILER PARK LUCHADOR HAS WON A MATCH!!!!!!!!!!!! Rick: And it only took eight fucking people to do it for him!!!! The Luchador … Corky Spectre … El Chupacabra … Crab Mask, Jr. … El Turtlé … I still don’t even understand why the Raving Jewish Destroyer is here, and I don’t know who in the hell that brick wall was before … and now SLASHER?!?! Scourge: My brilliance knows no limit, fools! Rick: I don’t get why Slasher would have anything to do with this disaster, though. It makes no sense to me, and … what!?!? [And just like that, as if things hadn’t been confusing enough up until this point … the maniacal Slasher, clad in casual blue jeans and a white T-shirt, comes hurtling down the ramp. This second man looks around the ring frantically, searching.] Rick: Wait … what? Slasher changed clothes!? Scourge: Err … this wasn’t supposed to happen yet. [Meanwhile Gary Clark(e) opens his eyes, begins to sir and crawls onto his knees, he shakes his head still feeling the effects of the CDG. The first thing he sees is Slasher bullying the referee. In a moment born of pure frustration, anger and with Slasher firmly in his sight he charges and spears him through the ring ropes, out of the ring and onto the cold hard floor, taking Corky Spectre with them.. the referee and Slasher taking an awkward fall on his head seem to be out cold whilst Clark(e) writhes in pain and holds his shoulder around on the floor.] [Meanwhile, the "original" Slasher dressed in all black slides out from under the ring, casually dusts himself down and picks up a nearby steel chair. He unhooks his mask from the back and pulls it off... the fake long black hair coming off with it… the crowd erupting in a roar of jeers!] Rick: It's JAMES MASON!! It was Mason all along... of all the dirty tricks! He screwed Gary Clark out of the match … AND he turned Clark and Slasher against each other! Larson: Genius!!  Scourge: Like I would ever do business with a worthless piece of trash like Slasher. I prefer to do my business with the devil himself. [Mason smiles, wipes his nose on the mask and throws it to one side.. steel chair in hand he casually walks over to a wounded Gary Clark(e) still holding his shoulder.. Clark(e) swings for Mason with his good arm but Mason easily ducks the punch and Clark(e) loses his balance and falls to his knees.. Mason positions himself behind a fallen Clark(e) and strikes the steel chair on the back of his head.. Gary Clark(e) is dead.. or at least knocked out.. Mason smiles and casually walks over to a fallen Slasher still on the floor feeling the effects of the Spear and crash landing.. Mason kicks Slasher onto his stomach, uses his left foot to stand on Slashers neck and brings the chair down on the back of his head.. Slashers body convulses momentarily before going limp.. Mason sighs with relief and goes to remove Slasher's mask but then changes his mind at the last minute.. pulling a microphone from his back pocket..] Mason: When the pair of you wake up I want you to ask yourselves why this has happened.. neither of you had to be in this position.. this has happened because you've put yourselves in this position .. All you had to do Slasher was know your role, teach yourself some discipline and act like a human being for once but you just couldn't do it and now you pay the price.. and GC, take some advice from an old friend.. some questions have no answers and some things are not meant to be found.. the path you're taking leads you to career suicide and nobody wants that, least of all you, I'm just an old friend giving you a heads up.. don't take this personally..  [Mason laughs, drops the microphone and heads up the ramp soaking in the jeers. The Trailer Park Luchador, Corky Spectre, Crab Mask, Jr., the Raving Jewish Destroyer, and finally Baron Black Scourge follow behind Mason as an evil entourage.]  Rick: … Larson: Ahahahahahahaha … Rick: This has been one of the most confusing matches I’ve ever seen in my life. Larson: Ahahahahahahaha! Rick: James Mason has … the Prodigy has certainly staked his claim and made it clear he’s back in the spotlight here in the UWF, but I don’t understand the goal here. He’s infuriated both Gary Clark AND Slasher now … Larson: Who cares, Rick? Look at that entourage behind him, and that’s only half of them … James Mason has an ARMY! The devil is back!
