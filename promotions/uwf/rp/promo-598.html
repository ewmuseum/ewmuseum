UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #598

Leading on

4 Aug 2004

Adien Pryde — SUV

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to the interior of a SUV. As the camera pans around the inside from the back seat, it catches a glimpse of Joanna also sitting in the back seat and Adien Pryde sitting in the front passenger seat.] Joanna : Well, Well, Well. Here we are, on our way to see a couple of our new friends. What friends are they, well; I’m sure Commie and Angel know who they are. We told you that we would even up the odds and that we did. Ever since Angel lost his woman and his old friend Meaner, he has been known were in sight. Commie on the other hand is reacting quite the opposite, he has gotten aggressive but also sloppy. He had a chance to take one of us out with his sledge but he gave Raid time to get away. That will be his critical downfall. Pryde : We will get to them another time Joanna, I have a small obstacle, a speed dump if I may, in front of me for tonight, the Green Demon. I wrestled you in you debut/return match and you were pretty much nonexistent then. I offered you a good sum of money to lay down for me and you did not take it. Now I am facing you again I guess I will just have to teach you a lesson for not doing me that favor. It is not a good idea to turn down an offer from The Chaos Society, it may come back to haunt you. See you tonight. [The scene fades to black as Pryde directs the driver to turn.]
