Kenny Mercury — Chicago, IL

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the Universal Wrestling Federation's latest Pay per View, FANdemonium, the unthinkable happened. Kenny Mercury and Jamie Shock outlasted... like... a shit load of people to win the Venetian Home Rumble and become the World Tag Team champions. Reports say that Kenny Mercury was 'a jolly fellow' when asked about this title win... ... NOT! Those fuckers! Kenny is pissed as all hell! And you would be too! He had signs dropped on him, his nuts destroyed, his tongue mutilated... To tell you the truth, the match was so long and filled with so many nasty things happening to Kenny, that we can't even name them all here. Anyways... that aside, Mercury is always glad to add gold around his waist, right? ... WRONG! Those morons, The Legends, took the tag team title belts... Oh man... Insanity... Time to check into with the man himself. The man who needs no introduction.... because, well... He'll introduce himself quite enough times in the span of five minutes. We find Kenny Mercury walking the streets of his hometown, Chicago. It's cold as hell, and Mercury wears a black leather jacket to block out the wind... And he's wearing his wrestling tights to warm his legs right? Yeah, Kenny's outside in his ring attire, in like - kazillion degree weather, and that's not even with the wind-chill factored in. Kenny looks to the camera, as he walks towards it. Kenny Mercury: 'Greetings, mortals... I thank you for spending your personal time with the technical wizard... the shooting star... the ring doctor... the submission exhibition... and according to Miriam Webster... The very definition of precision... Kenny Mercury... NOT! Shut up, sit down, and open your ears, for your God is speaking.' 'First order of business... FANdemonium... EAT IT! EAT IT! EAT IT!!!!! IN YOUR FACE!' 'And that brings me to the tag team titles... the belts actually... those dirty little not-so-closet cases, The Legends, sachet-d off with the belts... Now I'm not going to lose my temper... Oh, I will be reasonable... I'm going to be cool and calm about this.... quite frankly and simply... Luke Warren.... Lance Rose... I am going to murder you. I am going to murder you unless you hand over MY tag team title belt... ASAP! And then, if you do.... and do so swiftly.... I might not murder you... maybe... BUT!
