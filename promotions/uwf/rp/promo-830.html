UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #830

#1 aftermath

14 Dec 2004

Craig Wright — Oberlin, Ohio

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Thrilling”, “Spectacular” “Impressive” “One hell of a night for everyone to remember” and “out of this world” these are several of the quotes used to describe FANdemonium. What a night it was for the UWF. In a sold out arena the whole of the UWF roster went out and showed the world what the UWF is all about. We had the Under the Rope jobber match. For a match full of wrestlers with the potential of a wooden spoon they certainly rocked the arena in various ways. We also had the exciting Veteran Home Rumble hosted by the Phenomenoms. It would be impractical to leave the Flaming Monkeys match out of the greatest matches ever in UWF history. Both superstars gave there all to enliven the fans. The World championship match was also exciting Oni walking out victorious. The second ever Table Ladders Catch match was also immense and also a little revealing. The T.V Title was the only match scheduled for the Hot-Shot. The self proclaimed “Highlight of the Night” Walked into the Arena full of confidence, bursting with buoyancy. Craig Wright wanted one thing and one thing only from the match and that was the Television Title. The match was brutal and sick. We saw puddles of urine, cat shit and also the mini Hot-Shot. In a match that thrilled the crowd it wasn’t to be Craig Wright’s night as Hot-Mask of all people retained his title leaving Dream Maker, Craig Wright, Killer Dude, Malcolm Kennedy, and Aiden Pryde fuming. Craig Wright wanted the title desperately but failed to win a title with more importance to the Hot-Shot than his sex life. It’s not all gloom for the “Highlight of the Night” though as Tuesday Night the Hot-Shot will get his second chance to win a title Craig Wright has tried so hard to get in the past and present. Craig Wright maybe one of the only creditable superstars to walk into the UWF and never hold a title. Tuesday Night one on one will be The Hot-Shot Vs. Hot-Mask for the television title. Not only will it be for the title but it is also a qualifying match for the Contest Of Champions. This television title match will be unlike some of the past title matches as 100% will be required to win the belt and be eligible to compete in the COC. It is the Aftermath of FANdemonium there will be some cheerful superstars and some mystified wrestlers. Craig Wright for one will be shocked and embarrassed. The arena could erupt on Tuesday night as some wrestlers will be demanding payback. For Craig Wright he wants something that he feels he truly deserves and that is the Television Title. [A limousine pulls up to one of the numerous homes that the Hot-Shot Craig Wright owns. Rain falls heavily onto the 3 story high house and also on the roof of the car. The camera stays focused on the car. The chauffer exits the vehicle and opens the door to UWF superstar Craig Wright. At this point the front door of his home opens and what appears in front of him is complete beauty. A girl standing 5”5, Long Dark Hair, attractive long tanned legs and ocean colour eyes. She waits patiently as the Hot-Shot walks slowly up the twisting path to his home. Hot-Shot is greeted with a peck on the check but he simply walks past ignoring her, grabs a beer and sticks on the T.V. Girl: I watched your match Craig, looks like I’m not the only girl to see what’s below your trousers after all. Hot-Shot: Not now, as you can see from my facial expressions and lack of interest in you right now I am not in the mood to be speaking about my embarrassment on live TV. Girl: Just forget it, think about it Hot-Mask’s face was dipped in cat shit. That was comical. I don’t think I blinked watching the whole PPV it was amazing but it would never have been the same without the Hot-Shot. Hot-Shot: He wears a mask. Girl: Oh come on Craig not everything is bad is it. I mean you have had a lot of fan mail coming through the post why don’t you read some. [The girl retrieves some of the fan mail as Craig Wright slumps on the seat watching football highlights.] Hot-Shot: I really thought it was my night to shine. I honestly thought destiny was about to unfold right in front of me. I looked at the title in amazement. It was in my reach and as I climbed and climbed the ladder all I thought about was me standing at the top of the ladder with the title high in the air. The title that I have tried so hard and desperately to get my hands on .When I was about to grab the little bastard Hot-Masks reveals my dick to the whole of the world and the image is gone. I had that title and I was robbed. [Craig Wright opens one of the letters] Letter Reads: Dear Hot-Shot, unlike in your match honey. I loved the sneak peak of your pecker I would love to lick it all over and put it in a place you would love. I’ve left my contact details if you would like to hook up. Girl: See its not that bad who was the from. Hot-Shot: Steve Fisher. [The girl smirks as she takes a drink of her red wine.] Hot-Shot: This sucks I really tried for that belt it was mine how could I let that Hot-Mask keep what should be mine. I can’t believe that he has it. I want that sucker in the ring alone. I would teach him a lesson or too. I’ll show him some old school wrestling he was still in nappies when I hit the wrestling scene. Girl: He’s like 19 now that would have made him 14-15. Hot-Shot: Exactly, he was still in nappies then I mean he’s probably still wearing them. I have far more experience. I was training properly when he was wrestling with his stuffed animals in his parent’s bed. It is the UWF television title that he possesses. The TV title deserves someone who respects the history and the courage and audacity of some of the past worthy champions. The title shouldn’t be around a horny teenager’s waist. The tile will have to be disinfected before it can go round anyone else. Girl: Yeah those crabs were a bastard to get rid of…….. So I got told from Tom the cross dresser at the local. Hot-Shot: Why would even Tom lower himself to that? I really want him in the ring I will teach him a lesson. Ah great here comes some of the Highlights of FANdemonium. I should have won that title, I want a rematch. A normal match if I smell another cat I will spew. Girl: If you won the belt it would have been a lot harder for me to get your trousers down. Hot-Shot: If I had the belt Hot-Mask would never have got my trousers down. Girl: What the hell is Shiatsu wearing. Hot-Shot: I think the fans miss spelt bare. Girl: She has more love handles than cup handles. [There is a uneasy moment of silence. As on the Television Hot-Mask grabs the TV title. Shades of red appear in the Hot-Shot face as he walks past holding his package.] Girl: That reminds me you got a letter from the UWF today here it is. [Craig Wright opens the letter, his facial expressions change dramatically as leaps with joy. The Girl is confused and Hot-Shot runs to the kitchen grabbing a bottle of champagne. Girl: Have you been compensated after the embarrassment or something. Hot-Shot: Hell yeah. Girl: Good because I need a new pair of shoes. Hot-Shot: Not Money Girl: What then, can’t be that damn good. Hot-Shot: Oh but it is…. You see in this hand I hold a title match for the Television title against that little bitch Hot-Mask. It is also a qualifying match for the Contest of Champions. I mean I can get revenge so badly on Hot-Mask. Not only will he be loosing his title but I can knock him out of the competition. I’m looking forward to this match. I can picture it now. Poor little Hot-Mask going limp as I take his title and leave him with sweet FA. I have to take this opportunity for the title. I have had loads of chances without success its time I win the title. I have to stay focused and not get too carried away and make sure I triumph. There are no more excuses it is now or never. It is time that I prove to all of the fans and the entire roster of the UWF that I am the “Highlight of the Night” Hot Mask you had luck on your side. This time round it will be just me and you in the ring. I’ll show you what wrestling is all about. When you step in the ring with me on Tuesday you will loose your title and qualification. You can laugh it all now but you won’t be on Tuesday. Everything you have now Hot-Mask will slowly vanish. As I Craig Wright stands smugly over your decrepit paralysed body. I will give you something to cry about. If you thought Skankalie toppled you over then you wait and see what I can do. It is a disgrace that you hold the title. You’re nothing. I will demonstrate this to the Fans on Tuesday night. Hot-Mask throw everything that you have got at me. You will not succeed I will win the title by any means necessary. Hey girl you want to see mini me close up. Girl: Is that an invite. Call me Hot-Shot……….. [They were strong and convincing words from the Hot-Shot as he prepares for his match with Television Champion with the Hot-Mask. On Tuesday we will found out if Craig Wright’s prediction folds out for him. With that the scene fades to a shot of Cat shit on the mask of Hot-Mask.]
