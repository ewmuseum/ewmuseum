UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #272

Money Matters

4 Mar 2004

Ezra Kazee — 'UWF Shareholder's Office'

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up outside of a blue shed. Nothing really special about this shed, it looks like just a plain old regular shed that’s laid out in the middle of a property. There is one odd thing; on the shed there is a sign which says “UWF Shareholder’s Office.” The camera walks up to the door, and it opens. Sitting inside at what looks like a card table is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. It looks like he is sitting on a spool with a single light over his head. There is a ton of paper on his table and he is examining two pieces of paper very closely. Kazee is wearing a pair of shorts along with a grey suit and tie with a white shirt on underneath. Kazee looks up as the cameraman walks in.) Kazee: Ah, welcome, welcome, please, come in, make yourself feel at home. Since you guys take so much gruff from the other wrestlers you might as well have a good time here at one of the UWF’s official branch offices. And yes, this is an official UWF branch office since I do own part of the company…… Would you like a beer? Something to eat maybe? (The camera shakes yes. Kazee reaches behind him and pulls out a can. The can says A&W BEER on it. There is masking tape over an area between the two. He also pulls out a piece of break sandwiched between two slices of ham. He hands them both to the camera man.) There you go, now why don’t you set up the camera and go ahead and enjoy that. I’ll just sit here and do my business while you take a load off…… (The camera turns dark for a moment, and then all of a sudden comes on.) So how is that? I hope you like that stuff. Nothing but the best for the workers of this company. I mean someone needs to make sure y’all are happy. Someone needs to make sure that we don’t lose you to the RPW or some other company. And this is the best way to do it….. So I suppose the next question is what I am doing here instead of training for my match with Moraless. Well, someone has to sit here and go over these numbers. I mean yeah, we already have accountants and all, but I am an owner here at the UWF. I might as well go over the numbers as well…… These numbers tell some very interesting things. Like how much we’ve had to pay in releases from contracts because of people not doing there jobs. How much we have to pay for their air fare, their damn lodging. All that kind of stuff. I think it’s time someone stop this kind of stuff. I mean here is my contract. Look at it, if you will. If you pay special attention, I get paid barely $25,000 a year. The rest of the contract is basically my travel and lodging……. Then look over here at a guy like Raid, who is one of the lowball pay people within our roster. He is making $50,000 a year. He gets his lodging, his travel. I mean he is making 2 times more than I am. But you know what, I’m happy with it. I’m happy making my $25,000 and going out and entertaining the fans…… You know, we need more wrestlers to go out there and do the same thing. We need more wrestlers to go out there and save some money for this company. We are just barely in the black. That doesn’t say much. We need to do more in the black. We need to make some money for this company. We need to reexamine why we are actually going out here to do what we do. But that’s another matter……. (Kazee puts the papers down and looks directly at the camera.) Fact of the matter is is this Sunday; I have Moraless, another one of these high priced free agents. Now he has the skill. He has what it takes to be here in the UWF. He has what it takes to be champion. The question is why he is being so silent. Why has he not said anything? I know that he is busy training, but I must admit I’m worried. Worried about not getting the fans into this match like they should be….. Now I made the mistake of saying that Moraless was a member of the Prime Alliance. So Ruff Ryder says he isn’t. Oh well, my bad. I apologize. So Moraless actually does have some intelligence to him. That’s a good thing. I’d hate to think that he joined the Prime Alliance….. But the fact of the matter still is is that I will not be denied the IC Title again. I will not be denied my shot at it. And I will not be held back. This title is mine. This title is something I want and I will win. And in the end, there is nothing that Moraless can do to stop me……. As far as Ruff Ryder and Macolm Kennedy and their open challenge match, well, I’m not going to deal with them right now. See, I and Montgomery, we have business to attend to on our own. And this business is a little bit more important to us than your little match. Don’t get us wrong, we’d love to come down there and show you how the Prime Alliance is still beneath the Players Club, but hey, we are on to bigger and better things, and if you really want a shot at the champs, you have to earn it. Let us know when you do, because we will be more than happy to send you back to the bottom…… But at this point, the bottom line is I that Moraless is going to be running into a chainsaw he can’t stop. He is going to be running into both the immovable force and the irresistible object. He is going to be grabbing the bull by the horn and that bull is going to gorge him. Moraless, you are going into this match with little to no chance, and in the end, you will be watching me walk out with the belt….. (All of a sudden, the phone rings. Kazee picks it up.) Hello, this is Ezra Kazee. How may I help you? OK, sure, I can do that. Just hold on a second please……. Hey this is a call to appear on Live with Regis and Kelly... I gotta negotiate this. Moraless, final words, good luck, cause you’ll need it. (Kazee goes back to his phone call and the scene fades to black.)
