UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #173

The Respect of Hatred

14 Jan 2004

Joshua Dunn — Richmond

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SAS, suspended for a week, tickets sold now invalid, uproar, riots and misdeeds, all from the new UWF Owners decision to hold back the SAS Card to a week after the proposed return of UWF TV. But not all is bad, there are a few changes to the card made, and the fans that bought their tickets early are having replacements sent to them… supposedly. As for the extra matches, this features the UWF Debut of LOW man Iron Lung against ex-jobber killer El Turtle. Of course that alone might not be all that would have been needed to entice the fans to buy tickets to the re-dated SAS. With a brilliant board call of the UWF higher ups, the NA/IC Unification match has been interjected into the card, obviously to pull in a bigger crowd to the Robbins Centre of the University of Richmond. For those of you who have been in a box for the past month or so, the NA and IC champions are Ruff Ryder and Joshua Dunn respectively, so hurry and buy your ticket before their sold out. These two athletes have spilt blood because of each other on many occasions, each trying to get the better of the other, so far in their saga of hatred, the score lies 4 – 1 in Dunn’s favour, with the narrow victory of Ryder over Dunn being in the distant past of the PWW Invasion. Since then Ryder has never ever beaten Dunn in a singles match, and never held a pin over him personally. Where as Dunn has pushed back every attack on the IC title put forth by Ruff Ryder. On SAS however, things may change, or continue on their current course. Ryder, recently holding a shot for the World championship must be sitting on cloud nine, but is it up to Joshua to take him down a peg or two?] [The Scene Opens, where we are, we’re not quite sure, but we can see a lot of people milling about, this street is just bursting with people going about their lives, unknowing of the camera watching them all as they pass, There is however a man standing in front of the camera, this man is wearing a suit, a blue suit, with light blue shirt and dark blue, almost navy tie, but most noticeably about this man, he is wearing a neck brace. In his hand he is holding a microphone, on closer inspection we can see it’s a UWF microphone, the mans dark brown hair is blowing in a gentle wind while his crystal blue eyes stare fixated into the centre of the camera lens, a wide smile across his face, as he nods to the camera man and raises the microphone to his mouth and starts to talk] Man: Hello sports fans, and welcome to UWF TV, I am glad to announce my return to active service to the UWF Reporter institute after a long time spent in intensive care, I am George Thomson bringing you the latest information on upcoming UWF events, and right now, I have finally tracked down our Intercontinental Champion Joshua Dunn, to a café just outside the hotel the UWF superstars are spending their time before the event in the Robbins Centre of the University of Richmond. I have a few questions to ask the Communist Scotsman before the re-dated return of UWF television, let’s go say hello shall we? [George spins around on his heal, his suit’s coat flurrying around as he does so, he makes his way through the crowd and comes upon said café. Sitting at a table for two, by himself, the IC Champion sits with a cup of tea sat in front of him, he is holding a paper, reading the sports pages, he leisurely drinks from the cup oblivious to the reporters presence, George just walks up beside his and begins to speak] GT: You are still a notoriously hard man to track down Mr. Dunn. [At hearing the words, Dunn sets down the paper and turns slowly towards the reporter with a look of disgust on his face, obviously not liking the interruption to his solitude, he turns and gazes up to the face of George Thomson and the look of disgust almost instantly melts way to that of first shock and then joy. He throws himself up and greets George with a shake of the hand] JD: George! I thought you were retired. Its good to see you again, did you get sick of hospital food? [George returns the greeting and twitches slightly at Dunn’s unknowingness about how he actually got into the hospital in the first place at the hands of the rest of the Iron Curtain. Letting it pass him by though he accepts Dunn’s invitation for him to join him and laughs at his quip.] GT: Well, eventually you get sick of cold peas and sour grapes. [They both sit down facing each other and Dunn moves the paper onto the floor out of their way. Dunn sits back in the chair relaxed while George leans forward poised with the microphone] JD: I am glad to see you back working though, the man they replaced you with was… Disagreeable. GT: I’m sure, but nevertheless I do have a few questions to ask you. JD: Ha, when haven’t you, I see your time off hasn’t blunted your thirst for knowledge. [Dunn and George laugh for a moment before George actually gets down to business] GT: Well do you have an opinion on the delay of the SAS card? Is it an annoyance, or do you like the extra break? JD: Well on the fans side it is a bit of an annoyance, I have heard of the mess the ticket administration has had with the last minute date change, but I’m sure the fans will have their anger melted by an extra large card for the same price as the one they already paid for, including new faces and my own personal favourite, the NA/IC Unification match. [Dunn lets out a menacing sideways grin towards George, which hangs on his face for a few moments before dissipating] GT: Ahh yes, I do understand there has been a certain amount of blood lust between you and Ruff Ryder as of late. What do you think of your chances when you two meet in the ring now? JD: Well, without sounding dismissive of Ruff Ryder, I do think my chances are high, after all he hasn’t held a recent win over my head, where as I have beaten him time and time again, each time shutting his mouth from spouting crap for at least one whole day. Although I will have gone through another match previously before we go head to head, this sends an advantage to Ruff Ryder, but when push comes to shove, I really don’t think Ryder gives one hundred percent against me, I feel he’s holding back. This disconcerts me, as it seems as if he’s hiding something. I’m sure though that he’ll pull his finger out of his ass for this one though. [A smile flashes across Dunn’s face for a moment before Georges next question] GT: You don’t sound like a man with a passionate hatred for Ruff Ryder; you almost sound as if you respect him, not loathe him. [Another smile crosses Dunn’s face, amused at the way George picks up the little details, what he missed while he was in the hospital] JD: Well, there are two sides to Ryder, there’s the athlete who demands respect, something in this line of business you can’t ignore, otherwise you fall to the bottom of the game. I know myself underestimating your opponent just leads to and easy victory to them; this could be how I hold the upper hand against him when we meet in the ring, as Ryder is outwardly dismissive of everyone but himself. GT: And the other side? JD: The other side of the character is the man. This I detest, because of his attitude to everything that does not contain himself, he talks down to those who he has never beaten, he holds himself above all others, even those who have shown him that he is not the invincible character he portrays himself to be and further more his copy and paste method of promotion frankly disgusts me. GT: Copy and paste? What do you mean? JD: Basically in any Ruff Ryder promotion, change the name of the opponent, rearrange the order of the speeches and you get his next promotion. Every one seems like a rerun. And in sport, what good is a rerun? GT: So what your saying is that you admire the athlete but detest the man? JD: Exactly. [George intakes a large breath and gets up from the table, and Dunn courteously gets up also as he leaves] GT: Thank you for your time Mr Dunn, and I wish you luck in your matches this Saturday. JD: Thank you. [Dunn shakes George by the hand again and he returns to reading the paper, while George walks towards the camera while the camera backs away] GT: Another Insight to Joshua Dunn, the Communist Scotsman. Going into his match up, he holds Ruff Ryder both in Contempt and Respect as often happens with bitter rivals. The only thing left to see is how the result of the North American and Intercontinental Championship Unification match will turn out, and who will become the second man in the UWF. This is George Thomson signing off. [The Scene fades out to the UWF logo]
