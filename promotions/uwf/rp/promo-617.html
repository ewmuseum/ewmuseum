UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #617

Regal Rumble: He is coming....

13 Aug 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in slowly. Then a narrator begins to speak slowly, reading a diatribe, obviously written in advance.) Narrator: Things happen all the time that you can't explain. Some wrestlers get hurt, while some wrestlers retire. But this Sunday, one thing is clear. He is coming...... ('Sold Me' by Seether begins to play in the background, as a video package begins to play over the narrator's speech.) Narrator: The man who defied all the odds (Several images appear of Killer Dude begin to appear on the screen. The man, as you can all remember, is physically impressive. You can see that he is tall, at least 6'9', and also is well built, as he weighs almost 300 pounds of pure muscle. He has short brown hair, and his blue eyes stare menacingly into the camera. Most of the images that you can see are of Killer Dude working out or doing his wrestling training, with an occasional photo of him kicking ass in the ring.) Narrator: The man who brought glory to the THC division in the GIWF and the UWF. (You can see many shots of Killer Dude winning his 2 THC Titles. There are a series of images from a very well contested matchup with Gurgles, culminating in Killer Dude jumping off a third story building to give Gurgles an elbow drop, ultimately getting the three count. More shots of him winning the title from Kenny Mercury, inter-cut with the words 'Killer Dude'.) Narrator: The only man that could humiliate Kenny Mercury three times, and has gotten away with it. (Shots of Killer Dude, having an argument with Kenny Mercury. Lots of shouting, and most likely saying very mean things about each other, culminating in a shot from their match, which culminated in Killer Dude rolling up Mercury for the three count. Shots of Killer Dude pinning Mercury at Stable Wars. Finally, a shot of Killer Dude putting a jar of ass cream in Mercury's wrestling bag, and later beating the shit out of him in the parking lot) Narrator: The man who has come back from knee surgery five times. (This series of images begins with Killer Dude raising his arms in the air after eliminating Shadow Blade at last years Regal Rumble. Then, as 'Sold Me' begins to wind down to the last chords of the song, we see Killer Dude get eliminated by Kevin Starr, landing awkwardly on his knee, and effectively ripping it to shreds.) Narrator: Now it is time for Killer Dude's storied career to continue. The fans have demanded it for some time now, repeatedly asking him when he is going to return. But his story will not have an ending in sight, for he has returned. They say that a legend is returning. The Hardcore Legend has returned to the Universal Wrestling Federation. Things happen all the time that you can't explain. Some wrestlers get hurt, while some wrestlers retire. But this Sunday, one thing is clear. He is coming...... (After the narrator finishes his speech, the camera fades into an area where you can only see marginally at the time being. The camera pans down, and we see a lone figure standing in the middle of Lambeau Field, the home of the Green Bay Packers. The man is dressed entirely in black. His short brown hair is spiked up as high as it can go, and his face seems calm as he stares back at the camera. It is clear now that the figure is Killer Dude, who is returning to the ring for the Regal Rumble at Terror in Tokyo. He continues to look into the camera. With a loud clap of thunder, 'Open Your Eyes' by Alter Bridge begins to play in the background, and the scene is transported to a training facility just down the road in Green Bay, Wisconsin. Here, we see Killer Dude, this time dressed in sweat pants and boxing gloves. He is punching a heavy bag that is suspended from the ceiling as hard as he can, knocking it around in circles. We now hear the lyrics of the chorus sung by Miles Kennedy clearly, as Killer Dude continues to train.) Will they open their eyes, and realize we are one..... (Killer Dude continues to punch the punching bag, with a look of determination on his normally calm face) On and on we stand alone........ (Killer Dude continues his training, pounding the bag continually with hard rights and lefts) Until our day has come...... (Killer Dude punches the bag hard one more time. The chain supporting the bag snaps, and the bag flies out of your picture) When they open their eyes...... (Killer Dude slowly takes off the gloves and tosses them out of the picture as well. He turns around and stares at the camera, his blue eyes piercing it. For the first time in a year, Killer Dude directly addresses the UWF, just as Miles Kennedy finishes up the song.....) And realize we are one! Killer Dude: You know, I don't think I'm ever gonna get used to this. Five times, and I still hate re-introducing myself. I mean, starting over isn't a foreign concept to me, but god damn. Every fucking time, it's for this fucking knee, and it's really starting to piss me off. (He pauses for a moment to catch his breath, glancing down at the floor and clearing his throat) Killer Dude: Back the last time around, I had someone to help me get through all the re-introductions and crap. But now, I am alone. It's definitely been a while since I have stepped foot in a wrestling ring. For those of you that don't know me..... well, this coming Wednesday, you will know plenty enough of me, probably more than any of you ever wanted to. For those of you that do know me, and I'll list off the names..... Kenny Mercury Digital Mortality Jamie Shock The Plague, and so on and so forth.... you know not only who I am, but also what I'm all about. I'm here for one reason, and one reason only. I am going to win the Regal Rumble on Wednesday, and then I'm going to the World Heavyweight Title at Live and Loud 4, the biggest event this industry has ever seen. And then, when I'm through with that, on to my next goal, TO BE THE BEST GOD DAMN WRESTLER IN THE BUSINESS TODAY!!!!!!!! (During his last diatribe, Killer Dude seems to have grown in physical stature. His pride has shown up, and it makes him seem like a bigger man.) Killer Dude: As I'm sure most of you know by now, at last year's Regal Rumble, I tore up my knee pretty bad. Now it's been over a year since that point, and a lot of people have told me to hang it up, to quit, to retire. You can not fight a knee injury, and you can't get revenge on your own knee. So I have found a way to gain a measure of revenge for my injuries. I will systematically destroy everyone in the UWF, for you are all the same. You all are leeches, sent to suck the life out of anyone who wants to watch the UWF, and anyone who wants to participated in it. I will have my vengeance, one way or another. My fans demand it of me, and I DEMAND IT OF ME!!!!!! To all of my many fans, thank you for everything you have given me over the past year or so. As a return gift, this is my gift to you. I will return to the ring for the first time this Wednesday night and you will see me reap the fruits of glory. For those of you that want to see my career end, I'm sorry, but you have come to the wrong place and area code. There will be no ending for me. You all say that shit happens? Well, some wrestlers get hurt, and some wrestlers retire..... but Killer Dude will always be here, kicking ass and taking titles. I will not fade away quietly into the dark oblivion that is the Earth. I REFUSE TO ACCEPT MY FATE, FOR I WILL WRITE MY OWN, AND IT BEGINS AT THE REGAL RUMBLE WHERE I DEFEAT 59 OTHER MEN AND GET MY SHOT AT THE WORLD TITLE!!!!!!!!!!! At the Regal Rumble, I hope you all are prepared, as I WILL SEE YOU IN HELL BITCH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The last thing you see before the camera is cut is Killer Dude's face, set with determination and hatred. Then it dissapears, leaving the TVs with static)
