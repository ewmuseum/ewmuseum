UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #164

Guess who's back in the motherfuckin house!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. He is sitting in his black leather recliner wearing a Kansas City Chiefs hat with a huge smile on his face and the PWW North American Championship Title laying across his lap. The camera zooms in as the smile grows even bigger. ) Ryder: Oh you knew Ryder wasn't gonna stay quiet didn't you? * Ryder pats his belt * Ryder: There ain't a chance in hell that Dunn gets to speak about the Cherokee Thrilla and i'm just gonna sit back and listen. Now Joshie, if you'd have made it to the finals of the Contest of Champions we wouldn't even need to mention this upcoming match of ours would we? I'd have beaten your ass for the world to see and ended this silly little feud we have goin once and for all. You couldn't even get past the man that you're so blindly leading around could you? You can't beat your followers and you expect to stand in the ring with the man who punked him out in front of the entire world? The Players Club has never stood a chance against the True Indian Superstar alone, much less the entire Prime Alliance. You boys might as well just pack it up and call it a day, because you will always and forever play second fiddle to the greatest stable of wrestlers ever to lace em up. I mean you have the greatest champion of all time in Jamie Shock, and the best damn up and comer in the world in Malcom Kennedy, not to mention the Thrilla, and Players Club Killa that is the True Indian Superstar Ruff Ryder. We don't settle for second place, and that's why we are the Prime Alliance. * Ryder chuckles to himself before taking a drink from a beer mug that has the letters future World Champ stuck to the mug. He looks at the mug and then back at the camera and smirks * Ryder: Now I must say that I am in a bit of a pickle here as to who to bet my money on in this match between my two dawgs Jamie & Malcom. Both of them are easily better than the damn world champ we have now. I say whoever wins takes that gold and runs with it. Whoever wins the Shock/Kennedy match will be the UWF World Champ and will turn this damn place upside down. Prime Alliance runs the world folks, and it's time you all realize that fact. I hope you are ready, because 2004 is the year of the Alliance! * Ryder starts to stand up and leave, but stops as the camera focuses in on his face once again * Ryder: Oh and a Shock, Kennedy, my services will go to the highest bidder boys. Not that you two would ever think about cheating, because we all know...cheating is wrong. ( Ryder starts laughing before walking off. The camera focuses in on his PWW North American Championship as the scene fades to black )
