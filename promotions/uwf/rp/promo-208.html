UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #208

Generic Promo

8 Feb 2004

Mike Montgomery — The UWF Studios

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike pulls a match, lights it, then tosses it into the barrel. An orange glow is seen coming from inside the barrel. Mike prods at the debris inside, causing the flames to rise into view as more and more debris is consumed. 'Ezra, you and I both know that the two of us could headline any show we wanted to. We'd blow the roof off every building we walked into. You and I alone could bring in some serious cash at the gate. But you know what? So long as you and I are running in the same crew, that match won't happen in any form. Titles on the line or no. The Player's Club is as powerful as it is because we are a cohesive unit. We respect each other. I respect you Ezra, both for the man you are and the things you can do in that ring. Dunn bringing you into the club put a big old smile on my face. Why? Because it told me that he saw in you what I see in you. A whole hell of a lot of talent. But in order for a group like ours to function, there has to be some degree of trust. The firm belief that we won't start attacking each other if it gets us what we want. 'This match as it is, it won't take place. I'm prepared to guarantee that. The nice thing about a contract like mine is that it gives me a degree of choice over who I do and don't fight. And in that contract, it clearly states that I am not to be booked against a friend or stablemate. So if the UWF continues with this match as is, they will be in breach of contract. So to the owners I say this, ask the previous administration about the last time the UWF breached my contract. Think you can afford another lawsuit? I know you can't, and that's why I know you'll play ball.' Reaching down behind the barrel, Mike picks up a Prime Alliance t-shirt. He looks at it thoughtfully for a moment before he continues, 'So Raid, Malcolm, where do you stand in all this? Looking to capture old tag team glory, Raid? Please. Try to win a match first. Last Wednesday, you made a mistake. A very, very large mistake. You attacked a member of the Player's Club, one of MY boys, on OUR show! Now THAT I won't stand for. No matter what your history with Ezra might be, you attack one of us, then as far as I'm concerned, you attack all of us. And what happens when you piss off the Player's Club? Let me give you a demonstration.' Mike holds the t-shirt up to the camera, 'You see, this T-Shirt of course represents the Prime Alliance. That late, lamented stable of losers and jobbers. The fire in this barrel? That would be the Player's Club. Watch what happens when the two meet.' When Mike tosses the shirt into the barrel, the flames shoot upward with a tremendous roar, consuming the shirt. All trace of the garment is gone in a matter of seconds. 'Much like with the real thing, the second the Prime Alliance comes in contact with the Player's Club, only the Club remains. Remember that Malcolm? Remember watching all your hopes and dreams go up in flames because you chose the wrong side? Well it really doesn't matter if you do or not because at the Clash, you're going to be getting a reminder. You're going to watch your dreams of UWF tag team gold go up in smoke because the match WILL be changed, you WILL team with Raid instead of me, and you WILL be steam-rolled by the Club. 'When you step into that ring, you're invading the Playa Nation, and we don't take kindly to invaders. You're going to be stopped and sent packing. And it'll all be over before you realize that the match has even started. Now time to see some bookers about some changes.' Mike walks off as the camera zooms in on the fire before fading out.
