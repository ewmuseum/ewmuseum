UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #216

Yes, another generic promo. I'm lazy, what can I say

11 Feb 2004

Mike Montgomery — UWF Studios

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We find Mike Montgomery once again standing in front of the UWF's 'Back Alley' set in it's television studio. The chain link fence, assorted papers atuck to it, stands behind him, the decrepid oil barrel in front of him. A fire once again rages inside the barrel. 'Four days to the Clash. Four days and still not a promo from Raid or Malcolm Kennedy. I must admit that I'm rather disappointed by this. If you boys expect Ezra or myself to carry you to the win, you are sadly mistaken. If you do nothing, you get nothing. Simple as that.' Mike brings his hands up, holding them out and warming them over the fire, 'But then, it really won't matter will it? Once I'm done this promo, I return to the head office for meetings with our new owners. Meetings that, if the new ownership is as smart and reasonable as I expect it to be, will result in the Player's Club fighting against you two losers for the Tag Team Titles. 'It could've been you once, Malcolm. You could've been one of the ones enjoying the wealth, power, and influence that the members of the Club enjoy. You could've been one of the ones practically guaranteed to take home gold. But you walked away. And that, my friend, was one of the biggest mistakes you ever made. Remember what happened when you attacked Spiff, tried to force me to give you my shot at the hardcore title? Remember how I came this close to ending your career? Shall we dance again Malcolm? Will I have to show you once again the power I posess? So far Malcolm, I have beaten you, broken you, and destroyed almost everything you hold dear. Your precious Alliance, remember it? Your fearless leader, Jamie Shock? Remember him? What happened after they met the Player's Club? The Prime Alliance is dead, Shock's career is over. Will we end yours next?' Mike looks up at the camera now, face bathed in the orange glow of the fire, 'And you Raid, whatever shall we do with you? Your precious Bug Bomb, the move you put my friend Ezra Kazee through a table with? Do you really think I'm going to be intimidated by it? You see, the Bug Bomb is a Diamond Cutter, nothing more. Or maybe, just maybe, we should call it by another name. The Hangman's Joke, perhaps? You see Raid, no matter what happens at the Clash, no matter who you and I are partnered with, one thing will be a certainty. You won't be able to put me down. You can't. Your Bug Bomb is utterly useless against me because, frankly, I know it better than you do. I know when and how you'll execute it, and I know how to reverse it. 'I've decided that, since the two of you clearly have some history, I will leave your fate in Ezra's hands. But make no mistake, if you EVER lay a finger on a member of the Player's Club again, I will *personally* see to it that you're on the street without a contract in less than twenty-four hours. 'In short boys, no matter what happens, no matter who Ezra and I are partnered with, the Player's Club *will* walk out of the Clash with more gold than it had walking in and you two, Malcolm and Raid, will be left with nothing. The members of the Playa Nation are crying out, crying out for Gold, and they *WILL* be satisfied.' The silence as Montgomery finishes is cut off by the sudden, incessant trilling of a cell-phone. Mike opens it, listens for a moment, then clicks off. 'Excuse me gentlemen, I have a meeting to decide your fates.' The scene fades out as Mike walks off.
