UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #250

The Return

27 Feb 2004

Mystery Opponent — Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene is black but you hear a voice talking. Voice: 'A wrestling ring, boy it's been a while since I stepped foot into one of these things.' You hear the sound of someone jumping up and down on the mat. Voice: 'Ring rust? Yeah I haven't been in a match or a ring in months, but as you saw last week, I can still swing a chair. Glad you got some good use out of that chair Moraless, or shall I just call you John.' You hear laughter coming from the voice. The lights begin to go on and off flickering like a strobe light. You can make out a silhoutte of the man in the ring but you can get a look at the guys face. Voice: 'You call me a coward John. You are the one trying to flee The UWF. The RPW, come on what do they really have to offer? I mean yeah I am not going to lie, I checked them out. I was considering joining the company, but I saw that they wanted to rival The UWF, and I may have done a lot of terrible things in my life, but I will always stick by and defend The UWF.' With the lights flickering faster now the camera zooms in on the face of this mysterious man. Expecting to see a face. Nope he is wearing a mask, but not just any mask, it is Tsunami Oni's mask. Voice: 'Boo!!' Laughter again comes from the man behind the mask. Voice: 'I bet you just shit your pants John. But don't worry I got this straight from The UWF store. But Who am I? Maybe I am someone from your past as well. Mike Montgomery knows, but he is smart not to run his mouth. He looked into my eyes, he saw the desire, and that is why he gave me this shot, even though I had to pursuade him a little. Sorry about your match Mikey, hahaha.' The lights now go off again, complete darkness. Voice: 'Scary isn't it? I see how the rumor boards are going off around The UWF, everyone is trying to figure out who I am. You will get a glimpse of what I am capable of doing on Sunday. Johnny, you might not be able to step into the ring a second time on Sunday. You are really looking forward to facing Seymour, you know I am looking froward to seeing him beat your ass, but I don't know if I can save enough of you for him. This is my return, I need to make a statement, and Johnny, you will be the sacrificial lamb to the slaughter. I will beat you down untill you are left defenseless in the middle of the ring, a UWF ring.' You hear the sounds of knuckles cracking. Voice: 'You want to go to The RPW, no one is holding you here, you can break your contract and leave. But atleast you are nice enough to stick around for an ass beating. Have a nice weekend, cause on Sunday you world will come crashing down.' The screen goes from darkness to static.
