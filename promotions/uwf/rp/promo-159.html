Ryde or Die!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins in the penthouse room of a fancy New York hotel. People are partying and dancing to loud music as the drinks flow. There are women everywhere and two women are dancing on one man's lap. Just as the camera zooms in for a closeup the girls get up and walk away as the camera focuses in on the True Indian Superstar Ruff Ryder. He grins for the camera before turning his red hat to the front to show the Kansas City Chiefs logo. He pulls up his black t-shirt to show white letters that say Prime Alliance. ) Ryder: Well well, guess who it is. It's the cameraman come to get an interview from me. So I take it I got a match comin up huh? * The camera nods up and down * Ryder: What punk are they throwin my way this time? * Just before the cameraman can respond Vanessa, Ruff Ryder's friend walks up and sits on the couch next to him * Vanessa: You are facing Beezlebozo Ryder: Who the fuck is Beezlebozo? Vanessa: He's a new guy Ryder: So then why the hell is this such a big deal? I whip new guy's asses all the time. What the hell makes him so damn different? Vanessa: Cause this guy actually is trying. Ryder: Big fuckin deal. I know who this guy is, and he ain't shit. I'll whip his ass and be partyin before the beer gets warm. Fuck Beezlebozo or whatever his damn name is. Vanessa: Oh and get this, he's usin the gay stuff. Ryder: Usin the name Ruff Ryder as a gay guy huh, let me tell you somethin bozo, everyone in wrestling has used that angle, and it didn't get them anywhere so why don't you step to me when you have some new material alright, until then go take a hike you no talent waste of a fuckin roster spot piece of shit. You ain't got half the skills I do, and you're not even worth my damn time. Whoever does the booking around here fucked up on this one, because they are sending their up and comer into the ring with the Cherokee Thrilla, and when you step through those ropes, you don't come out. I will grind you into the mat boy, and when i'm through with you, you'll wish you'd never fucked with the True Indian Superstar. You can laugh and joke about me all you want, but there comes a time when you have to step into the ring and prove what you've been sayin...can you do it? I don't think you can, because i've seen guys like you all my life. You talk a big game and impress the ladies, but when it comes time to back up the talk you fold under the pressure and embarass your damn self in front of those very same women. You don't even compare to me bitch, and I resent the fact that you were even put into this match. That bein said, i'm still gonna fuck your world up beyond belief. You won't last five seconds with me once that bell rings, and i'll be surprised if you walk out of the arena on your own two feet. I'm gonna plant you through the concrete. You will spill blood before this match is over with, that I can guarantee you. Get ready son, cause you have no idea what you just got yourself into. * Ryder starts to laugh as the camera pulls away, but Ryder stops them as the camera turns back to him * Ryder: Whoa, you ain't goin nowhere, because I got another asshole I wanna mention. Ezra Kazee, you think you're some big shot, because you joined the fuckin player's club? Well let me tell you somethin boy, I killed that crew one before and i'll be more than happy to do it again. You bitches ain't shit to the True Indian Superstar and if you step to me again i'll fuck you both up at the same time. You can come at me as much as you want, but we all know who the superior athlete is. Joshua Dunn, your bitch ass wasn't runnin your fuckin mouth when it was you by yourself, but now that you think you got backup here you go spewin bullshit again. Why don't you fuckin be a man and stand up for yourself. I don't need the Prime Alliance to beat your asses, because you two little girls can't hang with me on my worst day. I run this place, and it's about time that you two found that out so why don't you come and get some. I'm through playin around with you two, and i'm ready for your ass. Ezra, you couldn't even make it to the North American Title match, because you fuckin suck dick so why the hell are you even runnin your mouth? If your dumbass couldn't even beat White Wolf what the hell makes you think you have a snowball's chance in hell with me? I'll fuck you up boy, and then i'll beat your little girlfriend's ass and send you both to hell where you belong. Fuck the Player's Club, they ain't shit, but a bunch of no wrestlin pussys! * Ryder glares into the camera * Ryder: Oh and as for mister damn Jamie Shock, I see you got yourself a little World Title shot huh. MY World Title shot? Well good luck homie, and no hard feelings. Go bring that gold to the Prime Alliance. Get us some more hardware so that we can own this motherfucker...Prime Style! ( Ryder goes back to partyin as he pulls Vanessa in and gives her a kiss. He smiles at the camera and winks before the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
