UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #370

Screwing Up is Hard to Do

26 Apr 2004

Ezra Kazee — Hospital Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a hospital room. Kazee is sitting there, in a bed, and laying there pretty helpless. He is half under the covers, half out. He is wearing the white hospital gown which is pretty normal. He is trying to force a smile, but he is obviously very disturbed. He gets ready to speak, and starts to bow his head.) Kazee: It all going away, isn’t it? The entire world is crumbling down upon me isn’t it….. I must give credit where credit is due. Digital Mortality and Moraless were able to beat myself and Monty. They were the better men that night……. (Kazee’s voice is sounding very down, very haunted. He no longer attempts to force a smile across his face.) My best laid plans are falling at my feet. It’s true that Monty won the World title. But yet, I was unable to help my friend. My vow of sportsmanship, and not attacking from behind, is useless. No matter how much I try to change the sport toward the better, it seems useless. But yet, when I do attempt to help, BANG. I got super kicked into oblivion……. Twice now. Twice now in two weeks I have screwed up. Twice now in two weeks I have let down my partner, my stable mates. Two weeks now, I have been a liability to The Players Club. Two weeks now I have become a liability to Mike Montgomery……… Two weeks ago. Two weeks ago, it was me who lost the match. Two weeks ago it was I who tapped out to the Digitizer. Two weeks ago, it was I who lost Monty the Tag Team Belts. Two weeks ago it was I have started to show chinks in the armor of The Players Club…… Last week. Last week I made my way down to the ring in order to fight fire with fire. Last week I came down to show that The Players Club could play the game that Resurrection, Chaos Society, and Prime Alliance could play. I came down to show that the Players Club could bend the rules so that we could use them to our advantage just as much as everyone else. And what happened. What happened? I came down. I grabbed Beelzebozo, and I get super kicked from the ring apron. Last week, I showed that interference, obviously isn’t our game. Last week I showed that I could mess anything up. Last week, I proved that I was the hole in the fence that everyone sneaks through……. After the last two weeks, I sit here, wondering why they haven’t thrown me out yet. I sit here after the last two weeks wondering when my phone is going to ring with a call from Monty or Dunn saying that I’m no longer in the Players Club. I’m waiting to hear from Ann Robinson. I’m certain that I’m going to get a call from some British woman saying “ You are the weakest link! Good-bye.” That call can not be far away……. Sunday, maybe I’ll be able to redeem myself. Sunday, maybe I’ll be able to make things right. Sunday, when Monty and I meet Digi and Moraless…… Match two between us. Is this the second match in a series? Will this be a series that will define the UWF in the future? Quite possibly it could be. I need this match. I need to go in there at this match and redeem myself. I need to go in there and do even more than I have been doing……. I’ve been a screw up lately. I’ve been a disappointment to The Players Club. I’ve been no better than a no-talent, sole-smelling jabroni. I’ve been worthless. Worthless I tell you….. (Kazee starts to smile. This time it’s not forced at all. There also is a fire in his eyes that people have never seen in his eyes before.) This Sunday, I will change that. This Sunday, all will be changed. I WILL prove my worth \The Players Club. I WILL prove to everyone why I am where I am today. I WILL prove to people that things are on the upswing for me. No longer will I be looked upon as a joke. No longer am I going to be looked up on as a screw up. I’ve screwed up to much. I’ve screwed up to many times. I’ve cost The Players Club too Much…… HO MORE!!!! NO MORE WILL I SCREW UP!!!!! (All of a sudden a nurse rushes in, after hearing the yelling to check on him. It is at this point the screen goes to black.)
