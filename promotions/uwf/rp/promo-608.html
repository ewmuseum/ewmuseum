UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #608

The most brutal, vicious Triple Go-Go Cage, Double Ladder, Handicap, Hardcore Title match ever.

9 Aug 2004

Adien Pryde — SAS Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to the arena after Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. The house lights are off with just a single spotlight directed at the middle of the ring. A voice can be heard in the darkness.] 'We are in the home stretch to the Regal Rumble. Everything is on the line, the World Title in a 4 way match, the Tag-Team Titles in a triple treat match. Hell, even the Television Title is up for grabs in the 60 man battle royal. But there is only one match that really matters to me.' [A figure begins to walk into the light. He is wearing black combat boots and black military fatigues. As he gets closer you can finally see a red t-shirt with the black ANARCHY symbol on the front the words 'Chaos Society' above it and 'A Way of Life' below. It is Adien Pryde, co-founder of the fastest climbing stable in UWF history, The Chaos Society.] 'The Triple Go-Go Cage, Double Ladder, Handicap, Hardcore Title match. A match that will soon go down as the most brutal, vicious Triple Go-Go Cage, Double Ladder, Handicap, Hardcore Title match ever.' [Pryde turns and faces the Universal-Tron and motions for the camera to do the same. It lights up and shows a picture of Angel] 'First we have Angel. This is a man, who has not been seen or heard of since we took that whore bitch of his, Ayami.' [Next we see a picture of Ayami tied up and gagged, head hung low. Joanna is standing behind her with her ever present studded falconer glove on.] 'She was his reason for coming back, and apparently his only reason for sticking around. That's ok, she is getting along beautifully with Joanna, shit she is even learning how to take a beating and like it.' [The slide switches to The Communist.] 'And here we have the 7' Russian, The Communist. Since we recruited his ho, he has gone to church, beat a few men up, and is where he rightfully belongs, in jail. Oh, don't worry, he will be out in time for our match, but where will his mind be. Maybe on Soviette?' [Again the Universal-Tron changes, but now it reveals Soviette. Still in the bra and panties that she was last seen in. Crouched in the corner of a dark room.] 'Don't worry Commie. We are making sure she is well taken care of. She gets her food, water, and plenty of exercise, out in the yard, running from the dogs.' [Pryde stops to think a bit than shakes his head.] 'Angel, you made me do this, you made me do these bad things, you called me evil. Adien Pryde evil, I never considered myself to be a bad person. I may have done some questionable things in the past, I even admit to doing some bad things to some men in the past, but they had it coming. But evil is such a strong word that it must be true. Then I sat down and thought about it. Maybe you were right, maybe I was evil, but if I was, I really hadn't put my full effort into it. Then it hit me, I needed to do something so horrendous that I could prove to myself and the world that I was what you say I am. Evil. Now that I have done that, I can go into this match with no doubt that I can and will do everything in my power to, beat, maim, or even kill to ensure that The Chaos Society wins our match on Sunday and we bring home The Hardcore Title. [The scene fades to black.]
