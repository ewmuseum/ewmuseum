UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #772

All great things must come to an end

6 Nov 2000

Adien Pryde — SAS Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens outside of the arena before Sunday Nights Supremacy show. We find Adien Pryde sitting on the curb around the back by the loading docks and he appears to be talking to himself.] Pryde : I don’t get it. I work my ass off to prove that I have changed and am good enough to be a title holder and I still come up short. I will hand it to you Nick Persia, you did what you said and put me in my place. You are my only weakness here in the UWF. I am like 0 and 6 against you. There seems to be nothing I can do to beat you. Some day I will shine and pin you in the center of the ring and get some of my self respect back. [Pryde picks up a rock and gives it a toss out into the back lot, skipping it on the tar like one would do across a lake.] Pryde : But there is one thing you said that struck a cord with me. You stated and I quote… “You play along very nice with some aggression, but always by the rules. Not once you have done something against the rules. You claim to be an anarchist, but I don’t see why they call you an anarchist. I think it’s more ironic than serious.” Pryde : This is very true, I do try my best to do things the right way, why, because if I don’t I get disqualified and lose the very match I am trying to win. Anarchy and Chaos doesn’t get along with rules which means something has to change because going from competing for the Intercontinental Championship to wrestling White Wolf, a curtain jerker that even Dreammaker pinned is a harsh slap in the face. [Pryde picks up another rock and skips it again, this time hitting a car, a Ford Focus, setting off an alarm.] Pryde : Shit, oops. [Pryde gets up and runs around to the other side of the building.] Pryde : Speaking of Dreammaker. Anyways, I am wrestling White Wolf tomorrow, for what reason I don’t know. I am doing it again to trove to you fans what I need to do to get the job done. [Just then some screeching of some car tires are heard at the entrance of the parking lot and the camera pans to see what the commotion is. Speeding toward Pryde is the black Lincoln Navigator that we have all learned to love, the one that belongs to Pryde and Anarchy. As it pulls up next to Pryde the Drivers side window rolls down show Reo Dai in the driver’s seat.} Dai : Dude, where have you been? The last few weeks you haven’t been yourself and we haven’t been able to get a hold of you. Are you ok? [Pryde looks down, kind of ashamed.] Pryde Ya, I was just giving myself a chance for another way of life, a change of pace for the way I live. Dai : You have got to be kidding. You are Adien Pryde, Original member of Anarchy, Co-Founder of Chaos Society. Anarchy has always been your way of life and you can’t change that. Pryde : Hey I had to give it a try. I wanted to know what it was like to have the fans to cheer my name, what it was like to have the fans want my autograph, and I will tell ya, it feels great. Dai : What the fuck has this fed done to you? You were once a feared man and now you are kissing the ass of the fans. I think you have finally taken to many bumps to the head because you are nothing like yourself. Tell you what, look up me and the rest of The Chaos Society when the old Adien Pryde comes back. Till then I don’t want anything to do with you. [Reo Dai slams the Navigator into drive and steps on the gas squealing the tires and leaving a cloud of smoke in its wake as it speeds off. Pryde continues to look off after his tag-team partner, back turned to the camera as it fades to black.]
