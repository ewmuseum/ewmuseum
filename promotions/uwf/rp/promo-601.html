UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #601

no time to worry, its time to hate

6 Aug 2004

Dicky Flint — dicky's house

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[the scene begins with dicky sitting on his porch, in a rocking chair, and -of course- in his kkk grand wizard uniform] dicky's thoughts:oh man, im a bit nervous for sunday... i mean im like hella used to beating up/kicking the shit out of/killing tons of people, just now no one will have my back in the ring. like here the police are on my side, and if they decided not to have our backs well, 'we' as in all kkk members, aka the whole entire town would well, just get rid of the police and substitute in our members, but now that i think of it, the police would never go against us, ha, because most of them are in the kkk themselves....yeah, so yeah, .........BUT STILL, i wont have anyone to get my back...well unless emanuel can, hehe] dicky:hey emanuel (emanuel is laying on his side, sprawled out on the porch, and hearing dicky talk he raised his head toward dicky, almost knowing some rediculous idea was going to come out of his mouth) emanuel:hmmm (i have no idea how to write bear noises, so im just goign to translate the bear noises to people noises/speach. oh yeah emanuel is a polar bear, if u didnt know) dicky:DUDE, dont give me that look!! fuck you, honestly, i...im just a little nervous, and i just want like a back-up plan..well, the plans goign to have to wait, you, you ass-a-saurus. im way to pissed and nervous to think, AHHHHH, ok ok ok, emanuel.........ITS HATE CRIME TIME, woot woot, im so taking my anger out on some minority, hell yes, c'mon! emanuel:ok whatever, this should be entertaining [emanuel and dicky walk side by side into the distance, against the light of the sun, their sillouet is quite interesting--the massive hulk of the polar bear and the pointed head of the kkk grand wizard.....touching indeed]
