UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #497

Revenge, in all good time

4/7/2004

Joshua Dunn — A Doctors Surgery

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Stable Wars has now past, and what a PPV it was. It saw the Roster of the UWF swell to almost double its original size, and the biggest news coming from that night was that, as they always said would happen, the best stable won on the night and the Players Club are now inscribed onto the Stable Wars trophy, when they find it. But the celebrations were cut short when a newly formed Knight Mare Brigade stormed into the winners circle after the belt and publicly humiliated Joshua Dunn. The last man standing in the Stable Wars Final was shaved bald before the entire viewing public, before the head of a mop was glued to his gleaming crown by the KB] [Still you have to look on the bright side right? Well it’s easier said than DUNN. But come SAS Dunn has a chance to have some retribution of sorts, as he is paged to go up against Corporal Punishment again in the main event. But he also has to announce which Title he will be going for, as if anyone needs to guess. Mike will not be too best pleased, put it that way] [Humiliated, he knew how it felt like, but then again, when you’re made a laughing stock on national TV, it’s understandable. There was but one thought pouring through every recess of Joshua Dunn’s mind, and that was of painful, hard won and delightful revenge. His mind rotted with the anger he felt towards Corporal Punishment and to the Irish Bastard Sean McClancy. The festering thoughts boiled over giving Dunn the look of a man on the edge about to snap at any minute. The fire, the fire that was in his eyes when he was faced with the problem of the Revolution is dwarfed by the pure hatred you can see in them, his every being suffering from an all consuming rage] In the doctor’s surgery, Dunn sits patiently on the bed, his foul look centred on a specific point on the floor, his eyes glazed over; he obviously isn’t studying the floor, but deep in thought, thinking of bitter sweet revenge. The walls are lined with different promotional posters, for anti drugs campaign, how smoking damages your health, and a couple of kid orientated body diagrams, various different utensils are scattered on the benches behind Dunn. Dunn himself is immersed in the shadow of the room; all we can see is the outline of his body, his head bald as the Corporal. His private fuming is interrupted by the door of the room being opened, cascading light from the hallway beyond falls into the surgery and lights up the body of Joshua, showing the plain red T-Shirt and the black jeans he is normally found in. The light creeps up to his chin but stops just below the eyes, and his body now lit up like some holy monument it makes it even harder to make out what he looks like now, and we still do not know the extent of the damage done by the glue applied to his scalp. In steps the doctor, his frame blocking some of the light in the doorway before stepping completely inside and reaching for the light switch, without even moving Dunn lets out a commanding bellow from deep within himself JD: Leave it The doctor stops his finger just in time and removes it from the light switch, Joshua just stays motionless, his face still pointing towards the same patch of floor, the shadows concealing the top part of his head, all we can see are tight pursed lips stay rigidly in place in a horribly unpleasant straight line. That it the lips are completely straight, no curves present or noticeable, the lips are a nastily off white colour, all the blood being forced out of them. The doctor Turns to Dunn and sighs, but sympathy is not what Dunn wants right now; he has to wait until Sunday to get what he wants Doctor: You know, you’re pretty lucky there. That glue they used was toxic to human skin. I’m afraid your head may be like that for a while. Joshua doesn’t move, nothing said has been of any interest as of yet, he’s too busy feeding his own desire for vengeance Doctor: I also noticed that you have a nasty cut across your forehead, have you had and dizzy spells lately? Still no reaction from the Communist Scotsman. His hands grip the bed fiercely as his own mind wonders, imagining what he would like to do to the Corporal and the Irish Bastard, and in fact the entire McClancy family clique in the UWF Doctor: You’re not suffering from concussion? It’s a wonder; it looks like a text book case to me How would the best way to gain retribution be? Murderous thoughts entered his mind, if he wasn’t so intensely angry, they may have left as soon as they appeared, but in his current state of mind they just mounted on top of the festering pile of ideas that were poisoning his mind, and the strange thing was, he liked them. He knew nothing would come of them; one spell in jail was more than enough, and he was certain Montgomery wouldn’t be there to bail him out this time. To best know how to gain the perfect revenge, he would have to know what would really hurt them. But for a man who knows no defeat, he had the perfect plan to hurt him the best. As for the Irishman, he’d have to wait and see Doctor: To be honest, it may be a month or two before your hair starts to grow back, it’ll take that long for your scalp to fully regenerate. Nothing still, the rubberised mattress on the bed squeaks horribly as his fists close and the knuckles turn white. Somehow he felt like Sampson, his hair gone, he suddenly felt very vulnerable, and he wondered if the McClancy’s were stout Catholics, and if that biblical story was their inspiration for their heinous act. Well if they thought that Dunn’s strength lay in his hair, they were gravely mistaken. Perhaps a demonstration of how wrong they were was on the cards, actually, he would make sure it was on the cards. There is no way in the direst hell that they would get away with this. Doctor: Have you seen the damage for yourself? Suddenly Joshua’s interest has been triggered, his eyes move up from their transfixed position to look the doctor square in the eye JD: No Doctor: you might want to, but I warn you, it’s not pretty Joshua Gets up, pushing himself to his feet, sliding off the rubber mattress making a strange screeching squeaking noise that you normally make moving over rubber or leather, and springs to his feet, somehow still managing to keep his face in the shadows, and moves over to the medical cabinet with mirrored doors. He peers into the mirror in the darkness, but is unable to make anything out in the darkness, obligingly the doctor reaches for the light switch and banishes the shadows from the surgery, and Dunn is confronted all at once of his new enforced look. It was horribly, his scalp was red raw in some places, others it resembled the disfigurement known as a port and wine stain where his blood vessels lay close to the top of the skin where his actual skin had to be cut away to remove that damn mop head from his crown. He stares in disbelief looking at the atrocity that now is the dominant feature of his head, courtesy of the Knight Mare Brigade. His mouth gaped open, unable to articulate any other kind of response, the rage he was feeling subsiding for a moment as he just stared and stared and stared. Then the festering pile of possible revenge tactics all came flooding back in a single instant action. Yelling like something you would hear at a primal scream therapy session, Joshua draws back his arm and slams a fist into the mirror, shattering it into several pieces, he then starts to attempt to compose himself, but the thoughts of bloody revenge filtered back to the forefront of his thoughts and he dropped his arm to his side. A thin trickle of blood slid down the knuckle of his ring finger and dripped onto the floor, the shards of mirror tinkled gently as they landed, contrasting to the almighty crash just previously. Doctor: Are you trying to give yourself another injury?! Do you know how long that is going to take to clear up? Dunn just looks angrily into the remaining pieces of the mirror breathing heavily as if trying to null his hate filled head, the doctor remains silent waiting for a response from the shattered man. Eventually Joshua looks up from the mirror and speaks without turning around to the doctor JD: Get me a roll of bandages Doctor: Whatever for? JD: NOW! Not wanting anything more expensive than the mirror broken in the surgery, the doctor rummages around the various cabinets and produces a roll of white bandages and hands it to the irate communist. Joshua takes it from him and holds the free end to the left side of his head and unrolls it around his head, covering the horribly scarred head that he now has to live with, he proceeds until all the bandage roll has been used and his head is entirely hidden from the view of the outside world, tucking the end of it under the bottom most layer securing it tightly to his head to make sure it wont slip off easily. Staring back into the shards of the mirror, Dunn looks down on his new image. They shall pay for doing this to him. The Inner Beast had been Unleashed
