UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #722

A Message to a Fellow Competitor

29 Sep 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Footage of Killer Dude attacking and beating up various superstars in and around the ring comes up on the screen as the screen fades in from snow. The voice of Killer Dude can be heard speaking over the footage, seemingly in a monologue to his opponents) Killer Dude: I'd like to say what's up to all of my opponents in the 6 way Triple Ladder Match for the North American, Intercontinental, and THC Titles at Live and Loud 4. So, what's up Nick, Misfit, Moraless, Seymour, and Kenny? You know, since Seymour Almasy is the only one that has enough balls to talk back to me, well, I guess that I'll start off with him. Seymour, I don't understand it. I mean I played the game, and YEVON IS DEAD, DAMNIT!!!!!!!!!!! But, you know something, since you somehow still think that you are a part of the Final Fantasy universe, I decided to watch TV this morning and I decided to watch a show that I'm sure you used to watch when you were a little kid, and you probably still watch it now. You damn right, I'm talking about Sesame Street bitch. Now, I realize that this show is rather dumb and annoying to any normal grown adult in the world, Seymour isn't exactly a normal adult now is he? So, I watched this show trying to get a feel for your level of intellect, and to see if you get any ideas for your matches through this show. And I'm ashamed to admit this, but as I was driving to the gym this morning, a song from Sesame Street just decided to lodge itself into my head. Here's how I think it went..... 'One of these people are not like the other One of these people does not belong.' And then, of course, since it's Sesame Street, they just have to have the stupidest visuals in the world, and in this case, the visual is a bunch of puppets running down a street, but one of the puppets is asleep. Obviously, you can tell that he is the odd man out. Seeing that made me realize something about you Seymour. So I did some research on the matter, and indeed I have found some really interesting stuff. Hell, with your level of intelligence, you might not be able to believe what I just said. (Over the footage of Killer Dude wrestling, names begin to scroll down the screen, very slowly, one after another.) Killer Dude: First off, we've got the Plague, one of the best THC Champions of all time. Hell, he's the one that got the THC Title vacated and thrown in the trash in the first place. He's the reason why anything Hardcore wasn't allowed in any matches for a long time. Then, we have Jamie Shock. Before he went on to becoming the biggest pussy in the business, he was actually a pretty good Hardcore wrestler. He was a part of one of the best matches of all time UWF History. And he actually put up a fight against the Plague, but of course, Plague kicked his ass and sent him packing. Then, we have Kenny Mercury. Not exactly the best THC Champion of all time, but he got the job done. He didn't always use the weapons, until I beat him of course. But his mind began to deteriorate when I pulled off the impossible and pinned him in the middle of the ring, albeit probably because the pubes on the top of my head caused him not to be able to kick off, because then they would actually touch his body. Lastly, we have (sighs) Nick Persia. The longest reigning THC Champion of UWF History. Over 150 days. That's more than 5 months man. Albeit, I hate that guy's guts and I know that I will whip his ass at Live and Loud 4 as well as your's, Misfit's, Moraless's, and unfortunately, Kenny's. And then, there is you Seymour. All of the men I have mentioned have been named legends of the Hardcore game. Like them or hate them, they have all elevated the name of Hardcore in one way or another. Except for one, of course. You are pretty retarded since you think that you are a video game character, so I'll speak slow for you Seymour. You, unfortunately for your sake, are the odd man out. You are the one who does not belong. Hell, you are a former World Heavyweight Champion, and now you are down here fighting for the lower than thou THC, IC, and NA Titles? And then you say that you need people to help you destroy the UWF? If that's the case, then you are nothing but a PATHETIC has-been. Last year, you would've been able to destroy the UWF with one of your fuckin' fingers, and now you have to have the help of others? You just lost all the respect you had from me, man. (The name Seymour Almasy reappears on the screen, but then another name comes crashing down onto it, destroying the name of Seymour Almasy. The name continues to spin around in a fast circle, until finally, it comes to a stop with a new name on it. KILLER DUDE) Killer Dude: You damn right, that's what gonna happen at Live and Loud 4. Killer Dude and Kenny Mercury, together forming the New Black Star Revolution, will conquer all and win 2 titles, therefore making the New Black Star Revolution the power of the UWF. It's only a matter of time, Seymour, Misfit, Moraless, and Persia. And Misfit, I'm sick of you always getting Moraless to help you fight your battles. I mean, I swear that whenever you are fighting someone else, Moraless always comes out and helps. Hell, here's an example. A couple of weeks ago at SAS. Ring any bells? I hope to hear from all of you before the show. Not that it will matter. The New Black Star Revolution has a chance to get some gold, and as Tyrese says in 2 Fast 2 Furious, we're hungry. By the end of the night on Tuesday, you will know that the New Black Star Revolution has dominated all of you, and you will soon be hearing the chants of....... VIVA LA BLACK STAR REVOLUTION, BITCHES! (The name of Kenny Mercury appears on the screen, and then the video fades out, with the New Black Star Revolution logo taking over the screen. Then that visual fades into snow)
