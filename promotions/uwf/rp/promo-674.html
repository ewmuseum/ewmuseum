To beckon a means! pt 1 (*kill that rust*)

5/9/04

Seraphiend — Los Angeles

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The winds of change had never smelt so rank. So quickly had they morphed from the sweet smelling breeze that softly caressed the nape of your neck. Replaced with the ravaging blitz, clawing away at the seems of that which was once was. Tossing away the old and destroying all that was new. The darkness had fallen almost instantly, engulfing the entire city as everything almost came to a halt. Cars came to silence, people came to bed and the nightlife…came to life. As the street lamps began to flicker on simultaneously a multitude of different, new faces hit the streets. The people for whom night is day. Switching elegantly to a back alley only just a few metres from the somewhat busy streets the angle allows you to pick out the slightest hint of a silhouette, scaling the back walls at a hastened pace. Proceeding to follow we soon find it to be a minute figure…a woman-sized figure. Hidden within a seemingly large black hooded overcoat, trailing all the way down to their feet. Splashing through the puddles and hurdling trashcans left and right. Slowly coming to a halt under a barely lit doorway. The neon sign above was half dead, struggling to exude enough light with only the single word ‘Mike’s’ being lit. Revealing a gloved hand through the robe the feminine like figure raps at the door ever so quietly. The large iron door just barely emitting enough sound to throw any sort of awareness to the occupants inside. However almost instantly the snap of several bolts echoed down the alleyway, instantly followed by the opening of a small slit at the crest of the door. A pair of aged brown eyes stared down at the figure, peering into the darkness of the hood. “Yes, what do you want?” Crookedly deep voice said. “We have business to attend to…Mike!” “What business would you have with me?” Watching as the figure began to peel back the hood. Slowly a small part of her face began to come into the light. A nose followed by some eyes, a chin, some lips…and entire face. A face of a young woman with hazel eyes. Staring directly up at the small pair of eyes staring back down at her. “Business of some import. For our transaction shall be swift…as it has already had the dealings done!” “Oh, Indigo?” “Indeed” “Give me a second young one, I’ll let you in” The slip slamming shut as a volley of commotion murmured from under the door. With a clank and a large screech the door slowly began to open. Fully revealing the pint sized man and a small set of steps “I didn’t recognise you in your disguise. How have you been by the way?” “Better once I have the single source of my desire. Do you have it?” “Of course I do…have I ever failed him…you!” Winking minutes after. “Can I see it?” “Yes, come in, come in out of the cold” Ushering her in through the door…slamming it shut after. “I’ll just go and get it for you…wait here and don’t touch anything” Leaving her in a small entry hall as he disappeared into another room. The dimly lit room allowed almost no visibility, but the little that could be seen would suggest that ‘Mike’ was some sort of antiquities dealer. Ancient looking oak cabinets, well-worn, well-used sabres adorned the bottom shelf of a large cabinet. “You’ll never know the amount of trouble I’ve had to go through to get this thing. Months of chasing, hours of negotiating. It’s been difficult!” “But you do have it!” “Oh yes…and here it is” Appearing through the opposite doorway with a red velvet cloth clenched in his right hand. “I can’t say I can see why so many people want it…after all its just a rock tinted red ever so slightly.” “So many?” “Yes, upon my research I found that this is quite a wanted item. There are people all over the world that would kill to get a piece of this action. And I, Michael Parsons have got it…just for you!” Presenting the cloth to the now un-hooded Indigo. Slowly peeling away the cloth to reveal what looks like a small piece of granite. Small red dots adorn the minute piece of rock. “Having delved deeper into what it is I…” “Will take what’s owed and speak nothing further of this matter. Not to anyone will y6ou speak about this matter. A deal between us and us alone” “Found out that it’s of Pagan origin. Its Cornish Granite, mined from the cliffs of Porth Island…an ancient hill fort forged into the cliffs. This rock is reported to have been dug from the burial mound of a…” “We must settle on the original price” “Original price, my dear the original price is now obsolete. I know what this baby is…and I know how much it can fetch elsewhere. The price has just doubled!” “I don’t think so” “Tripled…and if you don’t have the money I have a buyer waiting in Singapore.” A taunting smile slowly beginning to adorn his face. A crooked smile revealing a set of rotted black teeth. “Give me a second ‘Mike’!” Reaching into her robe, rummaging around for something. Within seconds she reveals a mobile phone. “Ringing the big man?” The smile getting bigger and bigger. He could smell the carrot, no longer pulling the cart, and it was getting bigger and bigger after every second. Having dialled an undisclosed number she placed the phone into her hood and to her left ear. Patiently waiting as it obviously rang. “Greetings Sire…” Turning away from the grinning little goblin, that seemed only a blink away from hopping around like a jackass. “No, I’m afraid we’ve hit a slight bump. He knows the reason behind obtaining the stone, and in reaction has detoured from your original agreement” “What does he say?” His patience was obviously beginning to wane. Like a little boy desperate for the toilet, stood up on stage in the school play. Indigo rifling a hand in his general direction, her index finger erect ushering a moments silence. “Are you sure?” Turning to look again at Mike. “Then it must be done…I will have it shortly! Removing the phone from her ear and snapping it shut. “Have you come to an agreement over my new terms?” “Indeed we have, and I think it will be one to your liking. For I have been given full permission to negotiate the situation as I see fit” A smile matching the small little mans now begins adorn her face. Her black lips slowly parting to reveal a set of ‘pearly whites’ any Hollywood celebrity would be proud of. “What does that mean?” “It means that I get to do anything that I want…in order to get a hold of that rock” Teasingly beginning to peel open the top of her robe. “Are you serious?” “Serious enough” Getting closer and closer, revealing more with every step she took. Soon enough she was stood only inches away from the guy. And with a gloved hand she slowly began to caress the left side of his face. Stroking back and forth before lunging for his ear. “AAARRRRHHHH!!!” Yelping like an injured dog. Clamping hold of his ear she twisted it violently, almost ripping it clean off. As he fought to free himself Indigo patiently awaited for the perfect moment. Watching his throat like the hungry lioness on the prowl. Watching as it throbbed more and more, harder and harder as he fought to break free from her hold. “L-l-l-l-et me g-g-g-o-o!!!” Just as he’d finished the sentence an unglove hand shot out from within the robe, clamping firmly around his throat. His breathing soon became audible even more so as the fight for breathe had begun. Each breathe taking longer and longer to inhale as the grip got tighter. “By any means necessary to the cause of my being. What did you think I meant, you perverted little man” The smile replaced with an almost elegant faze of anger. “Now I’m going to give you the chance to leave this situation much the better off. You can relinquish it for the original price…the agreed price. Whilst also living to see the sunrise tomorrow morning. Or you can continue to disregard our agreement…there fore forfeiting that which any one would hold much dearer than money…your life!” Pushing him back against the wall. “A hard choice I think not! Be swift and make it quick, before you pass out” Taking a moment to ponder his options…like you would. “O-o-o-kay-y-y…t-t-t-a-ake it” Ceasing to struggle he reached into his pocket and retrieved the velvet cloth. Handing it to her as she released her grip. “A wise choice indeed” Pocketing the item she turns and makes for the door. “You can’t get away with this…its not right” “Get away with it? Who's going to know this event even occurred. Be thankful that you have even the smallest desire for self-preservation” Not turning once to face the little man. “Oh and by the way, should you find it upon yourself to seek favour from anyone in this matter. Rest assured that you and they shall be dealt with...as swiftly as the sun dies daily” Leaving him with the warning she passes out of the door and into the night. The scene fades with Mike rubbing his throat.
