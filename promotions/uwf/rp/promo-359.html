UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #359

A Cupcake Sized Problem

23 Apr 2004

Raid — wouldn,t you like to know!!!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see Raid loading up his van with extermination supplies. He is very concentrated on is work, so much so that he doesn’t notice the camera man and Steve, the UWF #1 interviewer, walk up behind him. They both stand there for a moment wondering if he will see them. After realizing that he is to involved in his work, Steve decides to say something.) Steve “(cough, cough) Excuse me Raid, but I was wondering if I could ask you about the UWF’s roach problem? I figured since you are the V.P of Pest Control it would be best to ask you.” (Raid stands up and turns to see who is talking.) Raid “ Gees, I’m glad the UWF finally has gotten rocket scientists to do interviews! Sneaking up behind a guy and then asking stupid questions isn’t the best way to start an interview, but since you asked so freaking nicely I guess I could answer your questions about the roach problem. You see the problem seems to be stemming from one of the UWF’s branch offices. You see it all started with the Intercontinental Champion, err, I mean a cupcake. It seems that after the roaches took control of that office they began to branch out, via a series of underground tunnels. Using these tunnels the roaches have begun to transport themselves to other UWF locations. Once there they lay eggs and begin to multiply. Luckily for the UWF they have a professional exterminator on there roster.” Steve “And who would this professional exterminator be?” (Raid then proceeds to smack Steve on the back of his head) Raid “Me you FUCKING MORON!” Steve “Oh, right , well I just figured you were just the VP of Pest Control and not the operator as well. Now I really feel like a dumb ass.” Raid “Well you should. They don’t call me the Evil Exterminator for nothing.” Steve “What do you plan on doing to combat the roach problem?” Raid “Well I intend on starting with the source of the problem and hopefully that will take care of the whole problem.” Steve “And where would this so called ‘source’ be located?” Raid “The source is located at the UWF’s Shareholders Branch office. It seems that SOMEONE left the IC champ out on a desk for a few days and it began to rot. Now I have to take care of this problem that this certain SOMEONE has caused.” Steve “So what is going to be done to this SOMEONE to insure that this won’t happen again?” Raid “I have something special planned for him.” (As he says that he reaches into his bag and pulls out a can. This can resembles a soda can but it is covered in masking tape, and ‘A&W BEER’ is written on the side.) Steve “So what do you intend on doing with that, and what the hell is in it?” Raid “Unfortunately I cannot answer either of those questions at this time, but if you want to join me at the Shareholders branch office you can have a first hand view of what it is and what it does.” (Steve looks at the camera and shrugs his shoulders) Steve “What the hell!” Raid “(with an evil grin on his face) Excellent!” (And with that they all jump into Raids van and head for the UWF’s Shareholders branch office. Once they arrive Raid immediately starts unloading his equipment. After several minutes everything is unloaded and he begins to suit up. While doing so he motions for Steve to put on a suit as well.) Steve “I…I… don’t know about this. I have never done anything like this before.” Raid “Don’t be a pussy! All I need you to do is help me carry some of this stuff inside.” Steve “Than why do I need… Raid “Just shut up and put the suit on!” (Steve reluctantly begins to put the suit on, and then after he is done they begin to carry the equipment into the office. After all of the equipment is inside Steve lays down on the grass outside. He is breathing very hard and is sweating even harder.) Raid “What the hell is wrong with you boy? Didn’t your momma ever make you do physical labor?” Steve “I’m (huff, huff) so (huff, huff) tired!” (Raid walks over to him and gives him a few good kicks, and after two or three Steve gets up. He then goes over and leans on the office wall.) Steve “Why do you need all of this stuff for just one little office?” Raid “Just who in the hell do you think you are? And why do you feel the need to question my methods? But if you must know. The reason for all of this equipment is because I am going to take care of the entire problem at once! And this can only be accomplished with a large amount of insecticides, sprays and other needed items. Now I must go to work, so leave me the FUCK alone!” (Raid then goes into the office and starts to set up various insecticides, bug bombs and the like. After he is finished with all of those he starts to feed hoses down the tunnels that the roaches have built. Once the hoses are in place he connects the hoses to tanks that will pump gallons of toxic gasses that will extinguish the lives from the corpses of those little bastard roaches. He then strolls calmly out of the office and makes his way over to Steve.) Raid “ Would you like to do the honors?” (He pulls a remote out of his pocket and holds it out for Steve to take.) Steve “Sure I’d love to.” (Steve takes the remote from Raid. He looks at it for a moment before pressing the button. Then just as he begins to press it Raid yells “NNNNOOOOOOO!!!!” and jumps to the ground. Realizing that it is to late to not push the button, Steve turns away from the office and covers his head with his arms. After a moment of nothing happening he turns towards Raid to see him rolling on the ground laughing. Minutes later Raid stands up and brushes himself off.) Raid “(still laughing) That one always gets ‘em. Here maybe these will help.” (Raid pulls out some batteries from his pocket and hands them to Steve.) Steve “Is it ok to push the button now?” Raid “Yea, go ahead and push it.” (Steve then presses the button. After a second or two smoke starts to pour out of the cracks of the office, and then it quits a few minutes later. Raid walks over to the fancy double doors on the office and swings them open. A large cloud of smoke pours out of the now open doorway. The cloud of smoke engulfs him and he seems to be enjoying it.) Raid “Ahhh, can you smell that! I love it!” Steve “Love what? That smells horrible (as he covers his face)!” Raid “The smell of dead rotting roaches delights me. And now for the final touch.” (Raid pulls the can of A&W BEER out of his bag. He then begins to make his way into the office. Steve is closely following him.) Steve “What’s the final touch? Come on tell me.” Raid “Oh you’ll see.” (Raid places the can down on the desk inside of the office. He places it so it faces the chair that is behind the desk.) Raid “ Just you wait Kazee! This can of A&W BEER will teach you a lesson that you will never forget. I’m coming for you Kazee and you had better watch your back. Oh yea, and if your watching this Kazee I hope you enjoy this can of beer. MUWHAHAHA!!!” (The camera zooms in on his evil face as he continues his evil laughter, and then the scene fades to black.)
