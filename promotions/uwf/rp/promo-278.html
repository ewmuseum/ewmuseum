UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #278

Down with Clowns!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. He is wearing a black pair of shorts with a red t-shirt that has True Indian Superstar in black letters and a picture of him on the front. He is kicked back watching television while a man sits on the couch across from him recording the whole thing. The man clears his throat as if to tell Ryder he's there. ) Ryder: You ready to record? Cameraman: I've been recording. Ryder: Sorry, but I had to finish watchin my movie. It's my house and you're nothin but a guest so you do what I tell you or you can leave. Cameraman: Yes sir. Ryder: Well? Cameraman: I have to stay, if I go back without a promo from you my boss will fire my ass. Ryder: That's what I was thinkin. Now shut up, sit back, and enjoy my rampage. * Ryder leans forward and goes into interview mode as he looks at the camera * Ryder: So, Ruff Ryder has a match against Bozo. A former World Champ against the current World Champ. A match that could draw millions of dollars, and break pay per view records all over the world. Oh, but wait, this isn't pay per view is it? And it's not for the damn World Title is it? Oh hell no it's a sorry ass non title match that nobody will give a shit about tomorrow. We'll go out there and i'll beat his ass in front of the world, but tomorrow morning it won't mean shit because the title wasn't up for grabs. It was just another match between two guys. That doesn't really bother me though, because no matter what happens they will get to see a match that they paid for. I'm gonna tear you apart and even if they don't remember it tomorrow morning, they will see it on the news that a wrestler died in the ring. You will be on the news, and when you are dead and the title is vacated i'll be the one who comes out on top holding it high above my head. I'm on the verge of having all that I want. The Prime Alliance is forming and soon we will take back the fed from the Player's Club. I'm gonna take the title from you one way or another and life will be good. You're not a threat to me and you know it. You got lucky with that last win, and everyone knows that match should have been thrown out. I'm not gonna cry and make excuses though, because i'll handle my business in the ring and take my frustration out on you. * Ryder grins * Ryder: Why don't we make this interesting Bozo? How bout when I slap you around and make you my bitch in the ring and come out the winner. How about you let me in that World Title match with you and Dunn. Either way i'm gonna be involved. We can do it the nice way or I can just walk down to the ring and whip you both like a slave stealing food. You can't stop what's happening Bozo, nobody can. The Prime Alliance is taking over, and nothing is gonna stop us. The best anyone can do is slow it down, but the ball will keep rolling along and that ball is in our court. We will be on top of the world soon, and once we're there we will get rid of the weaklings. For now though i'll just settle for getting rid of you. ( Ryder glares into the camera as the scene fades out and goes to black. )
