UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #643

Small Message For Malcolm

16th August (year unrecorded)

Jamie Shock — Locker Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The promotion opens abruptly in the backstage area of the Tokyo arena. The Regal Rumble stage and ring are currently being set up, and the fans wait in anticipation outside. We are Jamie Shock’s locker room, where he sits on the bench below the pegs where his gear hangs in a sports bag. He wastes no time at all at addressing the viewers.] Shock: I’m going to make this short and sweet. [The Grand Slam Champion leans forward.] Shock: Malcolm Kennedy, what you fail to realise is that with every crowbar shot you decided to cheap shot me with, came a shovel of dirt out of the ground. In basic terms Kennedy, you’ve quite frankly been digging a bigger, and bigger hole for yourself my boy. [He smiles.] Shock: Fortunately I’m the man who will push you in it. You’ve been running your mouth like you’re an engine on unlimited fuel; well I think we know I’ll put a stop to that. You didn’t know whom you were messing with do you Kennedy? [Jamie shakes his head.] Shock: No, you obviously didn’t. What you saw when you were in the Prime Alliance was someone who certainly wasn’t in his prime. You saw a cheap grizzled, poor excuse of a man Malcolm. Nothing like I was, and I hope to have rekindled now. I pity you. Tonight I will show you Malcolm the true meaning of Defiance, tonight Kennedy I will show you the picture that is my legacy. [He frowns.] Shock: Tonight … “Top Notch” … I’m going to destroy you.
