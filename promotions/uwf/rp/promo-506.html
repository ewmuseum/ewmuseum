UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #506

Triple Threat.

July 6th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Locker Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** No excuses. The bruised and bloodied Resurrection got beat by a healthier Players Club. Shit happens and you move on. The rebuilding process is far from over. Every great plan comes with a few hitches in it. But rest assured, the Resurrection is far from finished. *** ( The scene opens up inside a locker room. A bloody ripped up white t-shirt sits on the floor. A few pieces of busted glass litter the floor. In the corner sits a man all alone. His head hangs low, his shoulders slumped. One hand is tied off with a towel. He uses that hand to unwrap the tape from his other hand. As he pulls off the last little bit of tape, he drops to the ground. He runs his hand across his shirtless torso, and wipes away the sweat. His baggy black leather pants shimmer in the little bit of light that sits in the room. He lets out a loud sigh as he stands up and moves towards the camera. ) * BAM! BAM! BAM! * ( He suddenly lets loose a quick three hit combo on a locker. He begins to shake with anger. After a few deep breaths he calms back down. And looks towards the camera.....) ( Malcolm Kennedy’s face comes into view. His matted black hair, his scratchy beard, and a face that is red from total exhaustion. ) “You think that Stable Wars will change anything? You think the Nightmare Brigade will keep us from our goals? You think anything has really changed here now that the Players Club have won Stable Wars six? Your wrong. All of you. Your dead wrong.” “ We are still gonna rebuild the UWF to where it once was. We just have a few extra names to squash beneath our boots on the way. And that we will do. Time is what we have plenty of, and we will use it to our advantage as we take over the UWF.” “ But there are more pressing matters to attend to. I’ve got a match soon.” ( He opens up the locker he punched just moments ago. Inside he pulls out a towel from the bottom. After that he pulls a piece of paper taped to the inside of the locker door. As he wipes the sweat from his chest he reads off the paper. ) “The Wednesday Night Underground main event for July seventh. Kevin Starr versus Mr. Meaner versus Malcolm Kennedy. Looks like a get a lovely triple threat match to win right after stable wars. How nice.” “ Mr Meaner? I don’t know this character. Never met him. Never seen him in action. But we all know that doesn’t really matter. The outcome wont change just because some wild card no name idiot is thrown into the mix. Mr Meaner could be Tsunami Oni in a clown mask with a 6 foot sword in his hand and it still wouldn’t make a difference. Im really out there just to hurt Kevin Starr. And that is exactly what Im gonna do. Ill brush the little fly that is Mr Meaner out of the way, and take Kevin Starr down for the count.” “You call yourself a lion Mr Meaner? Well Im the hunter. And as ferocious as you claim to be, I’ve got the guns. I hold all the cards. So when you come charging at me thinking it will get you somewhere, and try to go for the kill. Ill pull up that double barrel and aim straight for the heart. And once I take your heart. You will have nothing. You claim to be this total bad ass. Some heartless evil doer that will do what he wants when he wants. WAKE UP FOOL! Bad Ass’s like you come and ago in the UWF almost as much as the TV title switches hands. You better go ahead and check that sort of attitude at the door. You can try and put off your bad ass vibe all you want. Running your mouth in front of the camera, talking shit in the back, pushing around the crew. Whatever you want. But actions always speak louder than words here in the UWF. And you still have to step into the ring. Once you do, you will realize that there are so many more people in the UWF that are better, and more bad ass than you, without even trying. You think you will change anything between the Players Club and The Resurrection, you’re an idiot. If you know what is best, you’ll keep your nose out of our business and take you whipping like a man. Your gonna find out that what goes on between the Players Club and The Resurrection is much more than hate, and it goes a lot deeper than Stable Wars. At WNU your gonna become a casualty of war. I suggest you don’t show up.” ( Malcolm swings the locker door shut and walks back to the bench in the corner. He stares at the piece of paper a little longer and then crumples it up. He drops it and the towel to the floor. ) “Kevin Starr. Is it ok if I call you douche bag? Great! Well douche bag, you need to wake up just like Mr Meaner needs to. Break every bone in my face from the Superstarr slam? Last I recalled your stupid ass gave me one off the side of a cage in our first encounter, and look at me, Im still here. Come to think of it, that was also a triple threat match! How about that? Lets face it Kevin, This match will boil down to me versus you. Mr Meaner was just thrown in for a little extra spice. You think that your gods gift to wrestling and women? Your half right. The women might love you. But wrestling? Jesus Douche Bag, anyone in their right mind knows that your wrestling ability barely matches that of Axel Actions. Kevin you can pat yourself on the back all you want for being part of the winning team at Stable Wars. But the fact is, the only thing you did to win it was align yourself on the right team. That’s all. At WNU your gonna get what you deserve. Your gonna get beat like a red headed step child by the One Man Army. I’ve stepped into the ring with people twice as good as you, and won. You’re a joke, and at WNU I am going to prove it to the entire world. I suggest you smarten up realllllll quick. Otherwise your gonna get hurt.” ( Malcolm grabs the towel from the floor and walks off. The camera follows him to the door of the locker room, where he picks up a bag from the floor next to the door, and then without looking back walks out of the room. The scene fades to black. )
