UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #803

Return to the ring.

Dec 1. (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — A flashback or 12.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Times change. People change. As a matter of fact, everything at some point, changes. Its so easy these days to go from the top of the pecking order to the bottom of the ladder. One minute you’re living the life of a king. Big houses, champagne, fast cars, enough money to last you three lifetimes. And the next, you’re at the bottom of the barrel, alone, dirty, and miserable. But every great life, every great person, has some major downfall in their life. Some manage to save themselves before they fall to far, but some just never make it. Some just never recover. Its been proven time and time again, that the greatest downfall of every great man, is himself. Malcolm was at the top of his game. He had just returned to his locker room after a hard fought victory over his team mate, Jamie Shock. He had just become the number one contender for the World Heavyweight Championship. What more could he ask for? His casinos profits had doubled in the past month. He just had a new house built, big enough to hold half the UWF roster. He had enough money to last him three lifetimes. And a beautiful wife to share all of this with, as well as his two 3 year old children, and his third child on the way. Then his phone rang......................... Malcolm, currently walking back from the showers, and only in his boxers. Sped up his pace to get to his phone. He snatched it off the table and read the called I.D. Seeing that it was his wife, he quickly flipped open the phone and answered. Eager to tell her about his number one contendership. Malcolm: “Baby! You’ll never guess what happened.” A smile stretched across his face. Jason Kennedy: “ Malcolm, its your dad. How soon can you be home?” Malcolm Kennedy: “Dad, whats wrong? Wheres Gloria?” Malcolm looked worried. Jason Kennedy: “Gloria’s fine, shes gone to the airport to find your brother.” The smile returned to his face. Malcolm: “Eric’s back from the war? Thats great! When does he get in?” Jason Kennedy: “Malcolm, Theres no other way to say this.......” Malcolm Kennedy: “...............” Jason Kennedy: “Malcolm, Eric......Eric is.........Malcolm, Erics dead.” Malcolm Kennedy: “What? How?” Jason Kennedy: “He was killed by a suicide bombing while he was on patrol in Baghdad. Gloria has gone to the airport to ID his body.” Malcolm: “Ill be home tonight.” With that, Malcolm closed his phone and stared at the floor in shock. He was white as a ghost. Just then, the door flew open and in walked team mate Ruff Ryder. Malcolm dropped onto the armrest of the couch and continued to stare at the floor as Ryder spoke. Ryder: “Way to go man. You really kicked some ass out there. Even if it was against our teammate. You’re gonna take that world title from Beelzebozo next week at SAS. I just know it.” Malcolm just sat there, staring at the floor. Shaking his head in disbelief. Ryder: “What the hell is wrong man. You just became the number one contender!” Malcolm began shaking his head more and more violently, then in a flash, he jumped to his feet. With one swift throw he launched the cell phone across the room and into the wall. He then slid on a pair of jeans and flip flops, and turned to face Ryder. Malcolm: “The world title shot is yours Ryder, Im not gonna be there.” With that Malcolm grabbed his bag off the floor, and rushed out of the room. *** That morning, 3:30 AM, at Malcolms house. *** Malcolm: “Dad, what the hell do you mean Eric wasn’t there? Where the hell is he?” Jason Kennedy: “They say it means he is missing in action. And they will keep us updated.” Malcolm: “Jesus Christ, why the fuck did you people put me through this? Rushing me home like that when it wasn’t even his body. You had me worried outta my fucking mind.” Gloria: “Now you wait just a damn minute. How dare you be so selfish. How dare you speak that way. Eric means just as much to us as he does to you. You have no right to be that way!” Malcolm: “What was that? Who the hell do you think you are talking to. He was MY brother. MY blood. Not yours.” Gloria: “What do you mean WAS? No one said he was dead.” Malcolm: “Yeah well, I might as well accept it now.” Gloria: “What the hell is wrong with you. Have you no faith? Have you no hope? Your not acting like the man I married!” Malcolm: “Then why the hell are you still here? Why are you in my house, using my stuff, wasting my time? Don’t you preach to me about faith. I gave up on god long ago. Just like Im giving up on Eric. Just like Im now giving up on you, and everything we had.” Gloria: “What’s wrong with you? Where are you going.” Malcolm: “Away........For good.” Malcolm grabbed his bags from the bottom of the steps and stormed out the door. He threw the bags into the passenger side of his car, and jumped in. He sped off into the night, his wife standing there in the doorway, crying. A Chevrolet Corvette lay twisted and mangled nose first into a tree. Smoke rising from the engine. The tree, broken in half, resting on the hood of the car. Malcolm sat unconscious on the ground. Blood ran from his forehead down his face. The smell of whiskey escaped with every breath. With a jolt he sat up. Immediately grabbing his head in pain. Groggy and still not quite aware of where he was. He looked around, his door wide open, the car destroyed. He grabbed the open door panel, and tried to pull himself up. The door snapped off. So he slid his knees under him, and placed his hands on the ground, and with all his remaining energy, he lifted his drunken self up, and reached his feet. After gaining his balance, he leaned into the car, and grabbed his bags. Transferring all the contents of the smaller bags into the big one, and then sliding the strap of the big one over his shoulder, letting it hang behind him. He took one last look at the carnage behind him, shaking his head in disbelief. He reached into his pocket looking for a cell phone, only to realize he smashed his to bits. So he turned around, and made his way back towards home. All alone, just Malcolm, and the stars above. Hours later, as Malcolm Reached the outskirts of Reno. A car stopped beside him. The driver door swung open, and out stepped a man. It was his brother, Eric Kennedy. He walked up to Malcolm, and hugged him. Malcolm: Eric? Wha......What are you doing here? They said you were missing in action in Iraq!” Eric: “Don’t worry about that right now Malcolm. Im here to talk about you. Whats going on man? Why are you destroying your life? Malcolm: “I don’t know what else to do. I don’t know how Im supposed to keep going on.” Eric: “Damn it Malcolm, you’ve already given up your chance to fulfill your dream of becoming World Champion. But don’t give up on your dream of being a great husband, and a great dad. When your casinos no longer standing, and when the UWF has long since closed down, your family is still gonna be there to love you just the same. Get your shit together man, don’t let the loss of your brother make you loose everything else.” Malcolm: “What do you mean the loss of my brother? You’re standing right here.” With that Malcolm awoke face down in the wet grass, aware that this must have been where he had passed out from the alcohol. The sun was just rising, the sky a bright pink. Malcolm rolled over onto his back. Then quickly sat up, looking for any signs of his brother. But there was nothing. He dropped his face into his hands, and began to cry. Minutes later, a black car stopped beside him. The passenger window rolled down and Malcolm looked up at his father as he sat in the car, staring at Malcolm. Jason Kennedy: “Get in the car Malcolm, we’re going home.” Malcolm grabbed his bag and climbed to his feet. He then opened the back door, and got into the car. Its been almost a year since Malcolm lost his brother to the war in Iraq and tried to walk out on his family. He went to counseling with his wife, and is still with her and his three beautiful children to this very day. But nothing has been the same. Malcolm returned to his one escape from all the hassles in his life last week. He stepped out from behind that curtain once again, and returned to UWF television. But he still isn’t who he used to be. He still has all his possessions. All his cars, all the money, the big houses. But he has died on the inside. His marriage is a fake. His life is in shambles. So he has nothing left to loose. This Saturday Malcolm returns to the ring for a chance to once again, win the Television title. So the question that all his opponents must ask themselves is, how do they try and beat a man who has nothing to loose? The answer is very simple.......... YOU CANT.
