Mori Lands In UWF

12 May 2004

Takamichi Mori — Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Takamichi Mori stands infront of the Chrysler Building. Helooks up and sees the skylights and sits on the steps infront of the building:: America, the big mecha of sports entertainment, the pioneering wonderland of pro wrestling has now turned into the land of sports entertainment. You have your high flying, innovative, money making venture into a new world that strays the path of traditional pro wrestling and yet walks a fine line between the two worlds of old and new. This is where I enter ::Mori looks right infront of the camera. He's a stout fellow not very tall, but imposing with his massive frame ofmuscles. He's wearing a long trench coat, a white long sleeved shirt and some jeans. Mori has a darker complexion tthan some Japanese folk, and his hair is dyed blond:: I am Takamichi Mori, I am a representative and graduate of the world reknown Demon Dojo, based out of Nagoya,Japan. I was trained by a man many in the U.S. remember as The Green Demon. He made his name known by working hard, working consistantly and never stopping untill everyone from Saskatchewan to Ecuador knew his name and spoke it with excitement and awe. Well I am here to tell you, the American audience. I shall be doing the same, my mentor and Iwalk different paths. I feel that the path he walked when he was here was a unique and noteworthy one. He became a warrior known for his brutality and composure of being one stone cold bastard. I am different I came to America to endear the ways of your culture which is all but unsimilar to the ways of my native Japan. I grew up a big fan of the old school and I love the way the wrestling of the old southern territories went down. Where the fans knew the evil intentions of the 4 Horsemen, The Funks, Bruiser Brody, Stan Hansen and The Midnight Express. They accepted that they were just some mean bastards who wanted to make a buck and decimate the competition not to be the number one fan favorite that everyone loves. But to whoop some ass and make a name for themselves and that is who I am. I am here to pummel, crush, punish and brutalize all who dare oppose me while I am in UWF. I dont care for winning belts and I dont care for endorsements and that kind of crap. I am here to hurt and to get respect, the moment you step into the ring with me, you'd best respect me and my abilities because the moment you dont. I will put you down and you will not want to get up, because the pain is going to be so intense you will wish to die. Be parepared UWF, I am here, and I'm not leaving, not for anyone. ::Mori walks away and we pan back to the American flag sitting ontop of the building::
