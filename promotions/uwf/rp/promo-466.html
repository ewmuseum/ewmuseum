UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #466

Tv Title is mine!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder sitting on a training table following SAS. His ribs are bandaged and his biceps are taped as he is still recovering from the beating he recieved at the hands of Resurrection. He is a little slumped over and still breathing hard following that all out brawl between every UWF stable that he just happened to get caught up in. The look on his face is anything but a happy one. As the camerman walks into the room to get an interview Ryder slowly raises his head toward him as sweat drips from his bangs and nose. ) Ryder: What do you want? Cameraman: I'm here to get your comments on your match with Malcom Kennedy. Ryder: What match? Did you see a match ? There was no match! It was a draw and all those ot holes decided to run in and use MY TIME to play pillowfight with their friends. That's cool though, because I expected a run in. I figured someone would interrupt the match so i'm not at all suprised at what happened. Just a little annoyed is all. Cameraman: So the Ryder vs Kennedy feud is over with? * Ryder gives the cameraman a sly grin * Ryder: Hell no, not by a long shot. Cameraman: Well i've been told that you have an elimination style match at Stable Wars. Ryder: Elimination style huh? I should be in the damn Stable Wars tournament. * The cameraman gets a confused look on his face * Cameraman: Don't you have to have a stable for that though? * Ryder shoots him a ed glare * Ryder: I do have a stable ya jackass. Actually I have more than a stable. The Gangsta Nation isn't just a stable, it's a way of life. We're better than best friends...we are brothers. Once you're in the Nation, you're as G as can be. Nobody walks away from the Gangsta Nation and nobody turns their back on a brother. Anyone wants true loyalty they know where to find me, but recruiting because that don't work. If someone wants in they can come and find me. Now about this match at Stable Wars, what the hell is it about and who am I beatin down? Cameraman: It's you vs The Real Deal, Jay Vyper, and Loverboy and it's for the Television Title. * Ryder immediately jumps to his feet and takes a couple steps toward the cameraman causing him to back up * Ryder: The Television Title? Is that what the UWF s think i'm good for these days? I'm a in former World Champion damnit!! it, you know what i'm gonna take the TV Title to new heights. When i'm through with that belt it'll be more sought after than the World Title, because when Ruff Ryder puts his name on something it skyrockets. Who the hell is this Real Deal anyways? Cameraman: He's a newcomer to the UWF and a talented rookie. Ryder: Well I figured he was new assbreath. It don't really matter though, because i'm the crafty veteran in this match. I'll slap him around a couple times and then use Jay Vyper to slap him in the head a couple times and when it's all through i'll use Loverboy shove his head up both their asses then i'll eliminate all three at once. Stable Wars will be the first step in Ruff Ryder's journey back to glory. I hope all three of you boys like studying tape cause if you don't study then there isn't any chance that you can beat me when i'm on top of my game. I'm the best and you're all three gonna find that out at Madison Square Garden. I just hope you bring your dental records with you...cause you'll need em. Cameraman: Anything to say before I end this? Ryder: Yea, Corporal Punishment and his cattle prod. He can take that and stick it in his mom's for all I care. ( Ryder walks past the cameraman and out the door as the scene fades and comes to an end. )
