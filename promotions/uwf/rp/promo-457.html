Joshua DUMB&DUMBER...CORPORAL STYLE!

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(When you open any spiritual book, whether it be a Holy Bible, Torah, Kabala, scroll, or even the Koran, on thing is for certain. And that is age equals wisdom. I am sure we have all snickered at the old man perched in the center of the mall. Or the elderly lady pushing a shopping cart underneath a main street bridge. At times these things tend to sway people into believing that the elderly are no longer important or needed. Somehow we train our minds into thinking that youth equals superiority. This way of thinking has brought down several different empires throughout the ages. The ability to ignore the wisdom of age has destroyed many a nation. And tonight, you will see why age in the ring equals superiority in the squared circle. Just recently, one Joshua Dunn opens his mouth with statements like the following: ...There comes a point where a man can no longer implement the experience he has accumulated... ...Thing is Corporal Punishment may have done it all, but that was back in the day, something to sit kids on his knee and tell them. 'I did that' and they will look on, captivated. Now he's only a relic of what once was, he can't stand a chance against the fresh faces here. Youth can and does win over experience. He's past his expiry date, now it's time to get his state pension to make his existence bearable until he goes to guard that great big prison in the sky... OR ...I'm off to a Gym, even though he is ancient in athletic terms, there is no truth to the saying you can't teach an old dog new tricks. He can work a video player... As you can see, the Corporal's next victim makes about as much sense as a two dollar whore going on the Montel Williams show to tell her boyfriend that she is really a man. Everyone who has an ounce of intelligence knows that age equals wisdom. The Corporal has more ring knowledge than two hundred pimple infested Joshua Dunn's. Now that is factual. This man, who thinks he needs not be ready for a loss against the Corporal, has opened a door he will for ever regret. What has he accomplished in the world of wrestling except mediocre results. Can anyone vouch for this man who claim the Corporal is too old to be useful? I doubt there are few people in this world of wrestling who would step out and speak such nonsense. But like all jobbers in professional wrestling, they get paid to talk big and come up short. Take for instance this sign of emotion from the 'TRASH DUNN-STER himself: [[Dunn looks up, he looks just as we speculated, the gasp of air leaving his mouth before he speaks says it all, Why Try] Do not get me wrong, These attempts at making Dunn look like he is a contender... WAIT! STOP! I wouldn't try either. After all, in a few hours, this man is going to face the 'SHOCK & AWE' of the UWF. He knows he has a zero chance of defeating the master of the Lethal Injection. It is truly futile for him even to show up IF he thinks he will win. But this is not my place to 'SPECULATE'. You are simply listening to the moderator. It is time to hear it from the true Master of MAYHEN. So, once again, put those children to bed and get ready for the ride to L-O-C-K-D-O-W-N-V-I-L-L-E because it is time to allow you to stand in the presence of the one... the only... Corporal of Punishment.) CORPORAL STYLE! *A few days have went by since the Corporal staggered into his dressing room. Feeling the aftermath from a rough event like last week has given the Corporal a feeling of hope. Coming back to the squared circle after an extended lay off takes some of the finesse out of you. This is true with anyone, especially the Corporal of Punishment. *The corporal has almost fully recovered from his battle with the wrestler named Nick Persia. He has already begun to prepare for his next match against Joshua Dunn. He has already heard that Joshua seems to be acting as if he does not worry about facing the Corporal. Although this action shows a definite weakness in Dunn, the Corporal still recalls how Persia appeared to speak against foul play in his appearances prior to their recent match. But when the match was about to begin, it was Persia who grabbed a chair and assaulted the Corporal with it before the first bell was rung. These actions were so contradictory in nature that it caused the Corporal to misjudge his opponents intentions. *With that said, the Corporal has already begun to evolve from his previous way of thinking. He has heard his next opponent making comments that appear to be lack lustered. He sees the sigh of discontent in the facial expressions of Joshua Dumb&Dumber. Because of Persia's recent plan of events, the Corporal does not look at this man's expressions. Instead the Corporal is about to look at Joshua Dunn as if he were more than a three dollar watch salesman on the streets of California. To the corporal, Joshua Dunn is going to be be-headed like the average American tourist in Iraq. *The scene opens up with the Corporal reading Tuesdays morning paper. Yes it is Saturday, but the Corporal was awe struck at a specific comment made by his next opponent. *The first thing the Corporal did after reading the comments of Joshua Dunn,, was to get on the telephone and call National TV & Appliances. This company specializes in electronics. *As the Corporal reads the four day old paper, a knock is heard at his door. The Corporal begins to speak as he strolls over to answer the knock. Corporal: Why in the world would he say such a thing? It just does not make sense. *The Corporal opens the front door and standing there is a UPS delivery person. He holds a box in his hand. Corporal: Yes? UPS Delivery person: Sir, I am looking for 235 Crawford Street. Are you Mr. C. Punishment? Corporal: You found the right place and you found the right person. What do you have for me today? *The delivery person looks down at the box to find a senders name. He then scribbles the name on the shipping receipt. UPS Delivery person: This is from National TV & Appliances. I just need your signature here. *He hands Corporal Punishment the shipping receipt, and the Corporal returns it to the delivery boy after he signs it. The delivery boy then hands the box over to the Corporal and then he leaves. *The Corporal closes the door and walks into the living room. *Sitting down on the couch, the Corporal looks at the box for a moment. Corporal: So this is what I ordered. It is about time. *He slowly rotates the box in his hand as he examines the size and weight of it more closely. Corporal: It does not appear heavy. And it doesn't feel to difficult t handle. *The Corporal then stands up and walks several steps away from the box. He grabs the old newspaper and browses through Joshua Dunn's words of nonsense. Corporal: I still have no idea why he would say such a thing? What makes him think that I would have any thoughts about THAT? Corporal: I mean, I know that I have been around a long time. But just like wine, I am aging nicely. My warrantee is far from expiring. So that does not bother me in the least. This little rodent of a wrestler has said the following. And I quote: *He begins reading word for word from the latest interview. JJD: Thing is Corporal Punishment may have done it all, but that was back in the day, something to sit kids on his knee and tell them. 'I did that' and they will look on, captivated. Now he's only a relic of what once was, he can't stand a chance against the fresh faces here. Youth can and does win over experience. He's past his expiry date, now it's time to get his state pension to make his existence bearable until he goes to guard that great big prison in the sky. *The corporal then pauses for a moment. Corporal: What was back in the day? It is as if I have been gone for several years! Has the world of wrestling DE-VOLVED so badly that the new little panty wearing candy sucks have forgotten what makes a wrestler great? Age has nothing to do with a person being defeated. In fact one of the first things history teaches is that the PAST gives information to SUCCEED in the future. People learn from their mistakes and become better. I on the other hand, am more dangerous than ever. I am not weaker than I was when I left. I am stronger. I am not dumber than I was before. I am much more intelligent than I APPEAR. But to call me a relic of what once was, is like me calling that stain in my underwear a relic of what Dunn once was. Is that fair? *The Corporal thinks about what he just said about Dunn. He then shakes his head in the negative. Corporal: Bad example. The stain in my underwear is exactly what Dunn was. But I am sure we all get the right idea. I am far from being a relic. Dunn confuses the word relic with veteran. That is all. He wasn't the brightest bulb in the box, when he was born. But that is why he says what he says. Corporal: Like Dunn saying I can not stand against the FRESH FACES here? What is he smoking? WHAT FRESH FACES? This delusional behavior is simply a sad moment in time for Dunny Boy. I mean look at this comment again: Youth can and does win over experience. Corporal: Did he just say that? Oh my God... Corporal: This kid actually thinks that youth can mute experience. Does he not know that my mind is so more advanced than his when it comes to torture or manipulation? Surely this little worm of a weasel understands that it was not youth that taught me how to hurt people, but instead it was practice or experience that makes me more dangerous than he will ever understand. *The Corporal lays down the paper a second time and he walks back over to the box and prepares to open it. Corporal: Even after all of those things I have pointed out... they do not bother me as much as this one statement he made. I still can not figure it out. But none the less, I must evolve to the point that even these complex things must be understood. And by God I am going to understand them! *The Corporal lifts the box in the air, and begins to open it. He pulls out it's contents and lays the black box on the coffee table,throwing the cardboard box behind his back. Corporal: Mr. Dunn, you said this, and I am holding you to it... *The Corporal begins to talk in a sarcastic voice as he repeats the one sentence that has bothered him so much from Dunn's last interview. Corporal: I'm off to a Gym, even though he is ancient in athletic terms BLAH BLAH BLAH, there is no truth to the saying you can't teach an old dog new tricks BLAH BLAH BLAH. ...He can work a video player. Corporal: So THIS a video player is. Hmmmm. Who said I know how to work it!
