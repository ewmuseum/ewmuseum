UWF (2001–07) Roleplay

Corporal Punishment: Corporal Style!

19 Mar 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay for Corporal Punishment, written by his handler and posted to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 19 March 2002 under the subject “My latest RP(UWF)...CORPORAL STYLE!”. It replays the end of a UWF main event in which the Corporal pinned the World Champion Shawn Eclipse before the head referee Graham Poll reversed the decision to a disqualification win for Eclipse over the Youngbloods’ interference; it replays a speech Lew Spectre gave the Corporal on a UWF card; and it closes on the Corporal’s own address to the crowd before a non-title match against Danny Jaye.

Between Sunday Afternoon Suicide — 10 Mar 2002 and Wednesday Night Universal — 27 Mar 2002, whose cards do not carry the main event it replays. Served whole, as written.

There is nothing like the feeling you get when you cover someone for the three count. ... There is nothing like the feeling you have when you have your hands raised in victory. ... And there is no feeling that can compare to having the World Championship title placed on your shoulder after laying the so called World Champion on his back. ... The entire world was able to watch as Corporal Punishment was announced as the undisputed World Champion this past Sunday. The whole world was there to see the common man successfully defeat that man who is called Shawn Eclipse. [The camera fades to black and then returns to show footage of the main event's ending] Corporal Punishment covers a battered Shawn Eclipse. James Beard counts: 1. Harrison: "Foregone conclusion." James Beard hesitates for a moment, with the gears in his head almost visible to the viewing public. James Beard continues: 2. Dick: "Don't tell me. Corporal Punishment - Champion?!?!?!?!?!" James Beard holds his hand several feet off the mat, glancing around the three men standing over him. Hesitantly, his gaze moves up to the entrance ramp -- whatever he sees there makes him gulp visibly. James Beard finishes: 3! The winner and NEW UWF World Champion. Corporal Punishment! Time of Match: 0:23:36 Harrison: "Jeez!" Dick: "HUH?!" Harrison: "I... I. I honestly don't believe this." Dick: "Did that actually just happen?" [The scenes freezes on the screen as the monologue continues] The ref slapped the canvass three times. He rung the bell, and he raised the Corporal's hand in victory... BUT THEN POLITICS reared it's head in the UWF, and the decision was reversed simply because the powers that be, felt sorry for the man known as Shawn Eclipse. [Again, the scene fades to black and then returns to show a portion of the final outcome which returns the World title back to Shawn Eclipse.] Harrison: "I can't honestly believe my eyes. We have a new World Champion. but with such a tainted victory. Three interfering Youngblood's... the referee was bought and paid for before this match even began... they stacked the deck to a point that was almost ridiculous." Dick: "You can't condone that, but the fact still remains that the Corporal is the new World Champion ... a man who has been so close to the top for the last three years, always failing at the last split second to grab that brass ring. It may be a tainted victory, but there are few people who deserve that belt more than the Corporal." Harrison: "Whatever. I hope the Corporal's proud of himself and the way he won that belt, because his first reign on the top is a joke." Dick: "And... why is that other Referee running to ringside?" (As The Youngblood's are celebrating over Eclipse; the World Title held high above Corporal Punishment's head, head referee Graham Poll runs to ringside and confronts James Beard.) Dick: "Graham Poll is speaking to James Beard about. something. What can it be?" Harrison: "No idea. I don't really care. I'm still in shock." (James Beard hangs his head in shame as Poll shouts obscenities at him. After several minutes of rating, Poll finally calls the Ring Announcer into the ring. Beard goes to say something to the referee, but Poll angrily points towards the ramp and Beard follows obediently. Poll finally whispers something into the announcer's ear.) Announcer: "The Referee has informed me that, due to The Youngblood's interference in this match, the decision has been reversed. The winner by Disqualification, and STILL UWF World Champion. Shawn "The Reality" Eclipse!" (Every fan in the arena jumps to their feet and cheers emphatically.) Harrison: "YES! Justice prevails!" Dick: "Can he even do that???" Harrison: "Apparently he can, could, and has!" (Corporal Punishment holds his head in frustration and stomps furiously. He immediately takes his aggression out on the fallen Shawn Eclipse and kicks him repeatedly. The Lost Prophets and Shinoda look at each other and give a nod. Blade nearly decapitates Graham Poll with a clothesline, with Hybrid immediately joining him as they both descend on the unfortunate ref. Shinoda hurls the Ring Announcer out of the ring.) [Again the scene freezes, and the monologue continues.] But in the eyes of the Corporal, the World Champion is now his. The camera's caught it, and the referee made the initial decision to finish the count. So after of this, Wednesday's card has been announced. The REAL World Champion will be facing none other than Danny Jaye, in a non title match. And this is not a good time for someone like Danny Jaye to enter the ring against the man known as the Beast of the wrestling world. As of late, the showing of one Danny Jaye, has been less than desired. He has been in a slump recently, and his mental stability is weakening by the hour. This is a feeling that the Corporal knows all to well. As this was his state when he first entered the ranks of the now non existent GIWF. Back and forth, his mind went. He was a mindless robot for many a man. He was a valiant face one minute, and a demonic heel the next. It was only after his second return to the federation, that the Corporal found his true calling. A heel he will remain, and a Youngblood he was destined to be. Many eyes will only remember the ending of Sundays card. But one thing took place which will forever be remembered in the Corporal's mind. It was the night Lew Spectre gave the Corporal his blessing for being loyal. And it was the night that Lew Spectre gave the Corporal permission to take his own career over his loyalty to the Youngblood faction. This is something no other wrestler in YB history has ever been given. And this honor was something that made the Corporal more loyal to Lew Spectre and the Youngblood stable, than he ever was before. [The camera fades to black and returns to record the words of one Lew Spectre earlier on in the Wednesday night card which was directed specifically to the Corporal of Punishment.] "In the GIWF, you were useful to me as the mindless muscle backing me ... but we're not in the GIWF anymore, and I'm not even a wrestler now. You don't have to stand in the background anymore ... I don't want you to stand in the background anymore. I want the World title, and I want it tonight, and you standing loyally by to let someone else take the glory is NOT going to get me what I want. "For the love of all that is holy, stop holding back now. Stop reeling in your abilities for once and do what's best for yourself ... because we both know damn well you've held back in almost every way imagineable at every chance. I want the title, Corporal, and you being a loyal little puppy isn't going to get me that. I want you to rip his head off and bring it back to me as a trophy ... I want you to end his entire God damn career, and you standing by anxiously because you can't handle the spotlight isn't going to get me that. "I'm not good at pep talks ... but I want that belt on you, Corporal. Not on the enforcer you let the world see so others can look like gold ... I want it on the single most physically decimating opponent I've ever had the pleasure of facing in this ring. I want the title on the real you ... the one that isn't restrained by any sort of loyalty when you step into that ring, that isn't restrained to playing by the rules or trying to figure out what I'd want you to do in any given situation. I want the man that would just as soon kill his opponent as glance at him as the champion ... that is who you are, Corporal. It's time you let that out... For the first time the powerful and direct Lew Spectre awarded Corporal Punishment verbally and sincerely for his loyalty to the cause of Youngblood supremacy. And now the Corporal knows exactly what he needs to do in E-fed wrestling. Now it is time for the Corporal to put that FORCE into play against Danny Jaye. And this time, Danny is in for a real beating that only the Corporal can give. But do not take it from me, you an hear it from the man himself because the Corporal has now entered the building! The capacity packed audience of over 25,000 have entered the Madison Square Garden arena to see one man... Corporal Punishment. Just like in the olden days, the Corporal has decided to go before the people in a way that assisted him in becoming the man he is in today. With the lights flickering all over the arena, suddenly the sound of the Corporal grunting, " IT'S LOCKDOWN TIME! " is heard flowing through the arena's p.a. system. The lights go on to expose a giant stage which has been erected center stage of the Garden. On this stage stands on backdrop which is depicted as a prison cell. On this stage is also a single stainless steel toilet, and a concrete bunk. And in front of these objects is one single podium colored in green camouflage. On the backdrop is the writing, "CORPORAL STYLE!" and within a mere second or two, the shadow of a man stands at the entrance ramp. It is none other as Corporal Punishment, and as the spotlight beams down on the Corporal, the lights once again go black. This is when the Corporal's anthem begins to play ( Tom Sawyer by RUSH ) and the Corporal slowly begins walking down the isle. He is dressed in his typical black wrestling tights, green gunny cap, sunglasses, and black t-shirt. But one thing is also on his shoulder, and that is a exact replica of the UWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE. Proudly position on his shoulder, the Corporal boastfully enters the ring, and addresses the crowd. Corporal: And all I can say to all you MAGGOTS is... The crowd becomes silent as the looks to them in complete arrogance and pride. The audience becomes electrified however when he begins to say those famous three words. And as he starts to say them, the entire arena repeats them with him as loud as they can. IT'S LOCKDOWN TIME!! Then they begin chanting the Corporal's name, as he stands there and allows them to. It is when he raises his hand for them to quieten, that he nods to them in approval. Once the arena settles down, the Corporal speaks to the crowd. Corporal: My faithful PUNISH-ITES, Wednesday night, the world saw an absolute travesty unfold. We all witnessed as I, the peoples General soundly covered the paperweight and politicians champion Shawn Eclipse for the three count and the victory to be called the RIGHTFUL UWF CHAMPION! Corporal: I was there... YOU were there... and the ENTIRE WORLD WAS THERE! We all saw Shawn Eclipse down on HIS BACK FOR THE THREE COUNT! [The completely all pro Punishment audience begins boo-ing signifying that they believed the Corporal should have received the victory. The Corporal briefly pauses as the audience shows their disgust. Then he raises his hand which signals to them to once again quieten down.] Corporal: But ALAS, my fellow fans of the old school of wrestling. We were politically removed from the contest by ONE CORRUPT official, and his cowardice at doing what was RIGHT in the EYES of the RULES and ethics of this sport! Corporal: Yes, I said ETHICS! Corporal: You see, this man could have ended the match once my loyal and valiant MUSKETEERS came to the ring! Corporal: BUT DID HE? No. Corporal: Clearly after my fellow stable mates came to my aid after Eclipse attempted to pull out a foreign object from his tights, and strike down the Corporal to win unhonorably... AND THANK GOD MY FAITHFUL FRIENDS saw this and came to my assistance BEFORE Eclipse could stoop to such a level, as to STRIKE me with whatever he had in his possession! Because had they NOT responded so quickly, I would not be before this audience tonight! Corporal: It is CLEAR that the referee saw Eclipse doing this. The video shows clearly that Eclipse POKED HIS OWN EYE WITH SOME BLUNT WEAPON in his hand. But did the referee disqualify Eclipse and reward me with the title then? Why of course not. He wanted to see Eclipse defeat me. And why not? Why it is clear that this referee was PAID to do such a thing! Corporal: But AGAIN, let me repeat, the Youngblood faction was there to make SURE that JUSTICE would prevail... All they did was simply take the item out of Eclipses clutches! That was all, and still after all this, CORRUPTION AGAINST THE YOUNGBLOOD FACTION to this day! Corporal: But never fear my fine loyal following, the wrath of the Enforcer has just begun. Because I AM THE REAL WORLD CHAMPION! I AM THE REAL UWF WORLD CHAMPION, and this Wednesday night, I shall enter into a non title match against a man who knows me well...and all I can say is DOWN WITH DANNY JAYE! Corporal: He has been a thorn in my side here in the UWF. And these actions can not go unpunished. I have said it many of times in the past that HE should NEVER RETURN in the ring to face me AGAIN! And what does he do? Like a stray cat who receives a small portion of food from a sincere resident... it comes back! Corporal: Well all I can say is that Danny can change his logo, he can change his music, he can change his style, attitude, hair style and whatever else he wants to change; and the same exact thing is going to happen when he enters that squared circle against me. Corporal: I am still going to snap his head like a pimple on a babies butt with the Lethal Injection. And after he falls to the Lethal Injection, I the Corporal of Punishment, am going to send him to the old folks home for wrestlers. He will be the center to my wrath, and he will feel pain like he never felt before. Corporal: When the SYSTEM stripped me of their belt, then my wrath must be directed at those who just sit around and allow this travesty to continue. And it is people like Danny Jaye who allows these things like taking something that rightfully belongs to me, to continue. And tonight, the line has been dropped in the sand. The CORPORAL MUST TAKE OUT ALL THOSE WHO OPPOSE the Youngblood faction. The Corporal must make an example of anyone who tries to stop the Youngblood name from accomplishing what their tasks are set to be. Whether it be Canada. Whether it be the Canadian Clique. Whether it be the INNER CIRCLE, or whether it be the crooked officiating that this Federation continues to use against the Youngblood members and Lew Spectre himself! Corporal: Today begins a new day for those of us who are burdened down with politics as usual in professional wrestling. And Wednesday night, the Corporal will LAY THE FOUNDATION on the future of this sport... as well as laying the unconscious body of one Danny Jaye on his back for the victory. Corporal: My fellow Punish-ites, PREPARE FOR WAR... CORPORAL STYLE!
