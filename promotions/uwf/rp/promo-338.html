UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #338

The Present

14th April. (year unrecorded)

Moraless — RP#2 Tag title match

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jealousy makes people say funny things, it tries to turn friends against each other the fear captivates their bodies and their only form of release is to try and neutralise the threat at the source instead of facing things head on. Sadly these people are idiots. They don’t understand the bond is a lot deeper than what the fans see on television and at the shows. This was a genuine unity forged on the principals of one thing. Saving the UWF. The camera flickers into life to show Moraless stood at the top of the entrance ramp staring down at the ring crew setting up for a small scale house show later that evening, scary thought huh UWF has started to commission house shows. He sat there just watching the over weight crew sweating and swearing as they clanged various bits of metal around, dropping it on their feet building the shoddy looking ring. UWF was sparing no expense for the house shows lately. “Look at them, mindless zombies building something that won’t stand up to more than a nights worth of punishment, they don’t care what happens as long as they get their pay and that is what is wrong with this company at the moment. It is corrupted, effected at the very core. I sit silently and watch it spread through the company like a plague of locusts swarming across the land.” The words flowed from Moraless’ mouth as he continued to watch the workers, his eyes never moving away from their sweating unpleasantly bulging bodies. “People seem to think that Digi and myself are all talk, we want to save UWF but sometimes the only way to save something is to raze it and rebuild from the smoking ashes. We have both seen the future it is all around us now workers and I use the term lightly like the brand new Ben Jericho who came back rejuvenated after his break a new and better man, a man who had two matches and fell at both of them.” Moraless rose to his feet and turned his back on the workers heading to the backstage area of the small arena hired out for the forthcoming show. “What happened to the Ben Jericho who used to wrestle as Unforgiven, the wrestler who I looked up to the most after Tsunami Oni? He returned and saw the plague spreading and was corrupted straight away, he let his talent fail him when he needed it most, he turned his back on the fans by only giving a half assed performance in the ring before leaving like a scolded dog, tail between his legs.” Moraless sighed as he reached the locker room he had been assigned for tonight, twisting the door handle open he entered, it was small and dingy but it offered him a small slice of solitude something that was going to be absent from his life for the next few weeks maybe months. Sitting down on a horrible green couch Moraless gazed forwards as he interlocked his fingers behind his head, which he promptly rested them on. Things were going to change radically around UWF if things went to plan, but there was always going to be problems in the way, problems that could get out of hand if things didn’t get nipped in the bud soon. The players club were proving themselves to be a problem. “The problem with the likes of Montgomery and Kazee is that they are stuck well within the past, believing that things will eventually go full circle without any forceful push, they don’t understand that a flame must be lit in order to move things along. They fear the changes that are on the horizon, fear is good. Without fear we would not know when to back down we would take on things far above our control and we would end up getting hurt. They fear the new dawning that Digi and myself will bring to the Universal Wrestling Federation.” Moraless leans forwards now as he speaks resting his chin on his hands as he scrunches up his face as though in deep thought, trying to form the right words in his head. “Montgomery had a plan, quite an intelligent plan really. He thought that by trying to drive a wedge between Digi and myself that he may somehow give the players club the edge on Sunday, what they didn’t take into account was the true determination that we have. We are bringing about the resurrection of UWF and in the end they shall reap the rewards too. I know how Monty works he likes money, in the new dawning the revenue will increase ten fold no more renting out cheap and tacky storage rooms to double as locker rooms.” Moraless glanced around his surroundings and let out a clearly disappointed sigh. He knew that things were going to get better, they had to. It wasn’t like they could get any worse. “The teaming of myself and Digi isn’t something that was thrown together as a random experiment, this is something that we wanted and is something that is going to work. The sad truth is the Players Club has talent and we respect that, you just have to look at the silverware they carry to know that they are formidable adversaries. The tag team titles may have been thrown into the battle but both teams know that far more is at stake, the future of UWF, however both teams hold different ideals and this is why we must cross paths.” Sitting back on the chair Moraless smiled, the confidence on his face was evident, the sound of a door opening and swiftly closing can be heard the camera focusing on Moraless constantly at his command, whoever had entered wasn’t ready to be seen not yet anyway. “The problem with getting involved in a turf war, which is essentially what this is, is that it always boils down to three little things. One. Determination, how much are you willing to put into the battle, the fights that are ahead? Are you going to be willing to put it all on the line for the greater good? Two. What are you fighting for? If you fight without purpose you have no drive and motivation and three. Numbers a lone soldier is never going to win a war, it must all be worked out evenly to the finest detail there is no point in spreading troops too thin. I look at the players club and I see they are already winning the number game. So steps have been taken, the resurrection will still happen” Moraless grins as the person who entered the room comes into view there back is to the camera so there is no indication of who the person is, only one thing is certain…it’s not Digital Morality. “And we shall not fight alone”
