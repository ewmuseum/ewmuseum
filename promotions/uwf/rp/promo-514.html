UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #514

The Dawning of a GOOD Era

Today (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Almasy Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The UWF lacks many things. Sanity is among them. But more importantly, the UWF lacks…a hero. Yes, for at Stable Wars, the Player’s Club and Ressurrection did battle. Two forces of evil. I mean, come now! Bondage the Sex Fiend, Kevin Starr, THESE ARE NOT GOOD PEOPLE! UWF has been waiting for a hero. THEIR hero! And he has returned! FINALLY, THE FINAL FANTASY HAS COME BACK…..TO THE UWF! And, he has a THC Title match no less! ---- “So, they finally convinced you to come back, didn’t they?” Sitting in the apartment that he shares with his lovely wife Laura, Seymour Almasy can only nod. Seymour Almasy: Yeah, they did. I know, I know, but without the UWF, life’s just plain boring. Laura Almasy: No wonder they recruited you under the slogan of “No one ever really leaves, they just take vacations.” Seymour Almasy: And in my absence, the UWF has moved on. Much is new now, yet much has also remained the same. Many new athletes have joined this land, and two of them shall be my first opponents. The Final Fantasy smiles, brushing back his long white hair. Seymour Almasy: The Real Deal, a former member of the vile organization known only as the… He shudders. Seymour Almasy: DWO…thank heavens it’s dead and gone. He is my first of two opponents in the match, and unfortunately, it is a contest under rules the DWO is used to…no rules. Laura Almasy: Well, it IS for The Hardcore Championship, you know. Seymour Almasy: Ah yes! I held that championship! Laura Almasy: In a loose sense of the word held, yes. Seymour Almasy: Hey! My name’s on the title history! Laura Almasy: You won it by stealing it from Beezlebozo and lost it when Bruce Gordy stole it from you. Some title reign. Seymour Almasy: So, that simply means that I will have to avenge such a short reign by taking this title and establishing its longevity record, just as I did with the Television and World titles! Laura Almasy: Well, your World record is still longest world reign, but not the longest reign EVER. Seymour Almasy: W-w-what? Someone HELD A TITLE LONGER THAN ME?’ Laura Almasy: Joshua Dunn held the IC title 140 days…but wait, you were AROUND for that! Seymour Almasy: Oh yeah! I remember that! Nonetheless, I must now take hold of the THC belt. For if The Real Deal was not a sufficiently odious adversary, I must face a member of the Player’s Club. Nick Persia. Laura Almasy: The Player’s Club is still around? Seymour Almasy: Yes, they won Stable Wars! So, I’m assuming that’s why he has this title match…and I’ve been thrown in for one clear reason. Usually, whenever Seymour Almasy has a clear reason for something, bad things result. Now shall be no exception. Laura Almasy: What would that reason be? Seymour Almasy: The UWF needs a hero. Laura Almasy: What?! Seymour Almasy: Think about it, Laura. Ever since I left…the UWF has been lacking a hero. A good guy. Mike Montgomery has used his authority as commissioner to give his Player’s Club every advantage they could ever want. Unforgiven and Slasher have returned to wreak unspeakable havoc upon us all. And the McClancies and Corporal Punishment seek to turn the UWF into a nightmare. Laura Almasy: Yeah…it doesn’t seem like a good time to be coming back, does it? Seymour Almasy: On the contrary, Laura. This is the PERFECT time to be coming back! There is more than enough evil for me to fight! Laura Almasy: It’ll be dangerous though! Seymour Almasy: Of course it shall be! No journey is worth anything unless it is perilous! Yet I shall survive this peril, and continue to make my name as the greatest UWF star of all time! Laura Almasy: Just be careful, sweetie…I know you have a tendency to try to rush into things too quickly, and you end up hurting yourself. Seymour Almasy: I know. As good of a hero as I am…the UWF is now too large for me to purify alone. As usual, however, the smile on his face belies the stupid plan that is about to come. Seymour Almasy: However, if I had…allies, it would be far easier to battle the evil that pervades my homeland! And so in order to gain them…I hereby declare the formation of the Giving Openly Outstanding Do-Gooders~! Laura Almasy: Oh no…. Seymour Almasy: Yes, the forces of GOOD shall prevail in the UWF! Laura Almasy: We kind of need members first though Seymour. Seymour Almasy: Yes, my lovely wife, I am well aware of this fact. And so, this week on Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, I shall declare next Sunday to be…GOOD Sunday! Whoever does the best deeds shall be invited to join GOOD! Laura Almasy: I’m speechless. Seymour Almasy: As well you should be! This is my best plan in hours! With that, Seymour scurries away, ready to make plans for GOOD. Will Seymour find members for GOOD? Will he be able to purge the evil scourge (Hey, that RHYMED!) that is the Real Deal? How about Nick Persia? STAY TUNED!!
