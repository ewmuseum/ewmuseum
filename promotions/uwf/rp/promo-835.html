The 'I want to beat Duke Williams with a crap rp' ... rp!

14th December (year unrecorded)

Jamie Shock — A sofa

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[This is a dream.] [I just wanted to make sure you knew that see, as odd things happen in Jamie's head. Once he had an affair with Anthea turner in the summer of '97! What a year that was. If he knew then he'd be a UWF Grand Slam Champion, a UWF Hall Of Famer, and STILL somehow in a relationship with his first serious girlfriend I'm sure he ... would be pleased. Although then it WOULD ruin the suprise when it did happen.] [So we're in a classroom. The year is 1990. Jamie wasnt called 'Jamie Shock' back then. It would be a few years before he became a nationally recorgnised athlete. At this moment in time he's called simply Jamie Penderson. He was, at this age, normal. No thrills, like Sainsbury's own beans. This was the stage in his life in which wrestling wasnt in the back of his head.] [Next to Jamie is an inflatable. She was called Sarah.] [This IS a dream.] [Penderson turns to the inflatable, and then looks at the notepad infront of it. He copies her answers onto his notepad. The teacher, who looks like a dodgy cars salesman, rambles on about abortion. Jamie dropped General Studies to get out of pointless talks about that, and the lesson he replaced it with is no better. Lesson after lesson they learn about 'famous' scientists which are about as famous as your milkman. Jamie didnt take the lesson all that seriously. He often asked questions such as..] Jamie: 'Can animals have abortions?' [The class laugh.] Asian Girl In The Corner: 'Oh he's so funny!' [This is a dream.] [That incident actually did happen though. It didnt matter that the teacher was on about Edward Jenner, the scientist that shoved a whole arm up a cows rear ... according to Jamie's notes anyway - no. The teacher was a push over and Jamie didnt care one bit. The teacher, though normally answering Jamie's questions, failed to this time. Maybe he never heard - or maybe he didnt have the patience today to deal with a twat. The cheap laugh faded and the lesson continued.] Jamie: 'Oh this is bollocks..' [He said it quietly, to himself. Jamie just wanted to get laid. He hadnt touched a girl in a whole three months, bar hugging. He was desperate. It only took one look of an attractive girl in a skirt to get a ... 'bone on'. Jamie turned to Sarah.] [This is a dream.] [But why is Sarah an inflatable? - because she wasnt available, thats why. She was all outside, ... no inside. According to Jamie, the perverted teen. What was the point makng the effort even talking to her, I mean, she had a boyfriend. She was fit, but no progress would be made. That and she apparently liked to take things slow. Jamie didnt have time to take things slow! He had the affection of a humping dog. He wanted to use his tool and fix as many pipes up as he could. It was the 14th of December and god he was horny. Horny doesnt even begin to describe how sexually orientated Jamie was - but enough about that.] Jamie: '... do you like this bloody lesson?' [The inflatable just sits there.] Jamie: 'So, do you like pop music?' [Nothing.] Jamie: 'Has your boyfriend ever let you down?' [Of course it didnt reply, it's just a large balloon.] [He slowl-] =======[Reality]======= Sarah: 'JAMIE WAKE UP!' [She sits on top of Jamie, whacking him over the head with a pillow. The UWF Grand Slam Champion slowly opens his eyes, his fight sight seeing a coushin move towards his face before smacking him in the jaw.] Jamie: 'AHH!' [Shock jumps, grabbing Sarah by the waist as he does so, before diving off the couch and powerbombing her onto the floor.] *Thud* Jamie: '... shit.' [It would take years of flowers to make up for this normally. Luckily, Jamie is facing Duke Williams on Tuesday and therefore can use the fact that he is under alot of 'emotional stress' as an excuse. Sarah slowly gets up.] Sarah: '... its alright, I'll live.' [She hobbles to the kitchen. Jamie gets up himself, all on his own!] Sarah: 'Duke is getting to you isnt he?' Jamie: 'What? Piss off!' [He realises what he just said.] Jamie: 'Yeah..' [We zoom into Jamie's face. He raises his eyebrow and nods.] Jamie: 'He is.'
