UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #325

The Past

11th April (year unrecorded)

Moraless — RP#1 Tag title match.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWF had always been a home of sorts to Moraless he knew the in’s and the outs of it. That was before the changes that had to take place, back in the days when times were good and UWF was a federation that was feared. Moraless first came to UWF about a year ago during what many would deem to be the golden age of UWF. Tsunami Oni, Danny Jaye and Jamie Shock decimated all of those in their path and were feared for their ability in the ring, the lower card jam-packed full of potential a certain Seymour Almasy, Dante, Lacuna Debris and of course then there was Moraless. Up until his injury Moraless had made great waves within the federation a few high profile matches and even more high profile bumps thrust him forwards into the spotlight, it has often been commented that one of the greatest moments in UWF history was Moraless’ vein attempt to stand up to N.O.G.O.O.D and in turn received the almighty flying monkey bomb. What the public didn’t see was the backstage happenings, the meetings with executives, there were plans going on in the federation to create a new stable to face up to the might of N.O.G.O.O.D and Moraless was to be an integral part of it. ~The Past~ Moraless was sat alone backstage nursing his back, the flying monkey bomb still stinging. He actually had doubts over whether or not they would have been able to pull it off. In the talks before the match he had simply nodded along, not realising the effort that would be needed by all four wrestlers to actually pull it off, but it was worth it the reaction from the crowd had been immense a memory that would live with him for a long time. Moraless winced as he leant over to reach for his kit bag; another hand reached down and pulled the bag up handing it to him. “There you are man, sorry about the back, I tried to take as much of the brunt as I could but the momentum took over, it went well though huh” The man behind the mask of Tsunami Oni was looking down at him behind him the local NVA looking on in concern. Everyone knew that wrestling was just an act, everyone got on well backstage some were a bit more arrogant than others but everyone generally got on well, UWF was mostly about fun and we all took it that way. “Yeah, I’ll be sore for a bit but hell I saw a tape of it when I got back, it was phenomenal, you guys back there did amazing getting me that high I honestly didn’t think you had it in you.” Moraless said looking over at the local NVA who nodded happily before wondering off, happy that there was no lasting damage leaving just Moraless and Oni in the locker room in silence. It was always strange for Moraless backstage especially around Oni, although he had been in more federations than Oni compared to him he still considered himself an amateur, the mask less Oni sat down next to Moraless and sighed letting his head rest against the wall staring vacantly at the roof of the locker room. “So are you ready for next week, that is when things are really going to kick off for you John are you ready as that’s when we are going to have to take things to the next level.” Oni said a solemn note of seriousness in his voice. Moraless knew what was supposed to happen now but he still couldn’t come to grips with the idea of main eventing especially with Oni. “Yeah I’m ready man, I just still find it hard to believe that it’s going to actually be happening. I mean I look around Digi was world champion before you and Lacuna was the most dominant television champion. You are the world champion and the NVA are tag champs, I just feel like I don’t fit into this mix very well.” Moraless said with distain in his voice, the doubts were beginning to flow through his mind. Oni turned to the Moraless and put a reassuring hand on his shoulder, he could tell just how nervous Moraless was the nerves and tension riddling his body. “John, you wouldn’t have been picked for this angle if you weren’t good enough, they see talent in you we all do but if you don’t take this opportunity and bury all the doubt then you are letting down no-one but yourself, stop worrying about the rest of us. Digi and Lacuna can take care of themselves as can I. You just need to focus on this, you proved tonight you can play with the big boys, and we put on a show and a half out there. If we can do this every night then we are going to keep UWF at the top.” And with that final rallying cry Oni rose and left Moraless alone in the locker room with a lot to think about. ~Cut to present~ “At the time I didn’t realise that would be the last time I would see Oni, everything was set for me then, the future was clear. Digital Morality, Lacuna Debris and Moraless. We had the potential to be one of the greatest stables ever, anywhere our styles complimented each other and things just clicked backstage we always hung out, some of the others even called us a clique whatever that meant. I guess I threw that away just because of some own deep seated personal questions” Moraless hung his head slightly before a hand rested gently on his shoulder. “But times change John, what’s a year to wait for what is rightfully ours” spoke a voice as the figure of Digital Morality stepped forward out of the shadows into view of the camera. Moraless stood smiling side by side with Digital Morality. “The past is something that cannot be changed it has been done and we can simply learn from it. The past two times I faced Ezra Kazee I failed miserably, yet the past two times I faced Montgomery I was successful, this whole thing is a learning curve and now that I have my own back up. The past will be nothing of significance.”
