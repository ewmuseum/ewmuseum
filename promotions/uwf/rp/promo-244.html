UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #244

Arrested?

26 Feb 2004

Raid — Backstage at SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene opens and we see a man sitting on a chair. His back is towards the camera and on his back the Anarchy symbol is visible. He relizes that the camera is behind him and he stands up and kicks the chair over. As he turns around you see that he has an orange piece of clothing in his hands.) 'I've done alot of things in my day.I've also had alot of things done to me in the past. BUT THIS!( He swiftly holds up the orange shirt) THIS is the worst thing that has EVER been done to me.' 'Montgomery not only have you disrespected me you have PISSED ME OFF! Just who in the hell do you think you are? You think that you can push people around just because you have a fancy office and a reserved parking spot in the UWF parking lot? Well your DEAD wrong! I'm not just another lacky for you to jerk around. Do you think that you have any control with the authoritys? Well it seems that I am now free. And why do you think that is? Well I'll tell you. You see there was nothing that I did wrong, so there was no reason for me to be arrested, but you had the police come down to the ring and arrest me. I was then taken into custody and interrogiated about the allegied arson that had occured, but once the authorities had asked me a few questions they quickyl realized that I did nothing wrong. You see Montgomery, I keep very discriptive loggs and such about my profession. What you didn't see in my promo is the contract that the woman had signed. One of the clauses in my contracts is that incase of accidental destruction of property, death or anything else that I can be sued for, my ass is covered. Its what we like to call INSURANCE. You may have heard of it before.So Montgomery , your lucky I don't sue your ass for wrongful arrest. ' 'But I'm not like that. I would rather get on with my career and get back to wrestling. Which is exactly what I am going to do, and there isn't a damn thing you can do about it. You maybe V.P of talent relations but there is nothing you can fire me for. So with that said I will stop working about it. What I am worried about is my match.' (Raid starts walking towards the door and the camera man begins to follow him. He continues walking without saying a word. He stops at a door and on it is the name of BRUCE GORDY.) 'This man is what I am worried about. He is a hardcore legend in his own mind and he has the right to think so. (Raid looks right into the camera and has a very serious look on his face) 'Gordy, you are a very dangerous wrestler, this I am aware of . But I am not afraid of you. There is nothing in this world or the next that scares me.I know you have the hardcore background and have held the THC title on more that one occasion. Unfortunatly this is not that time and it is MY TIME. Its my time for THC gold and that is all that I want. There is nothing I wouldn't do to achieve my goal. I am not afraid to put my body on the line, and I'm not afraid to bleed! Honestly I am looking foward to this match with you. You are a fierce wrestler and I want nothing more than to be able to show the UWF brass that there decision to hire me was a good one. This match also gives me a chance to break my losing streak, which I am not proud of. This match is going to be a hard faught battle and I can't wait to lock up with you this sunday. I wish you good luck and believe me you will need it. As will I. See you on sunday Bruce and you had better bring your 'A' game.' ( Raid walks away from the door and the camera fades to black.)
