UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #695

Can't go anywhere to get away from the press.

11 Sep 2004

Adien Pryde — Local Hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside a local hospital. A half dozen of so reporters have gathered outside because there is a rumor that a UWF superstar or two are in there.] Reporter One : Who is in there? Is it someone big like Oni or Misfit? Reporter Two : No, no one that big. It is some up and comer though. [A commotion starts to gather near the entrance as Adien Pryde and Reo Dai come out. The reporter questions start to fly.] Reporter Three : Pryde, What were you doing in there? Are you or Dai injured? Pryde : Dude, don’t you pay attention to the UWF broadcast? Joanna was hit in the head with a sledge hammer two weeks ago. She has been here ever since with a severe concussion. Reporter One : How is she? When can we expect to see her at the side of The Chaos Society again? Pryde : The Doctors say that she will be released next week. After that look out Plague. Reporter Four : A few questions about your match. Do you think your team will be victorious tomorrow and what are your plans after that? Pryde : Of coarse we are going to win, that is a stupid question. With the next world champion Tsunami Oni and the longest reigning THC champion in UWF history on my team, there is no one that can beat us, not even them scum Mike Montgomery and The Plague. Only cheap pieces of shit like those to would victimize women like they have. I mean kidnapping them is one thing but hitting them with sledges or taking advantage of them with doctored voice recording is another. Pryde : As far as after we kick their ass, I plan to kill The Plague for what he has done to Joanna and The Chaos Society then prove to the UWF that he is no longer the MAN he used to be. Both Mike Montgomery and The Plague are wrestling greats of the past. This is our time to shine, wrestlers like Persia, 'The Renegade' Rich Rollins, and myself. Reporter Two : What is the story with Raid? Is he retired? Pryde : That is something between Raid and himself. If you want to know that go find him and ask him yourself. Reporter Three : Do you think you stable mate Mr. Meaner will retain his title at Supremacy? Pryde : It must be true that even idiots can be reporters. That is a stupid question. It’s a hardcore match and he is a member of Chaos Society. Nuff’ said. Now get out of our way, we need to go take care of some business. [Pryde and Dai push and shove their way their way through the mass of journalists and get into the black Navigator parked at the curb and drive off as the camera fades to black.]
