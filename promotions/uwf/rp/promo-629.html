UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #629

Rememberace and Realisation

14 Aug 2004

Joshua Dunn

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He sat in the minimalist seating of the airplane, making him rather uncomfortable, it was the first time he had flown anywhere. He was flying to the US of A as we speak, 3 hours down, he had a long way to go yet before landing, but he was still filled with the frivolous energy that a person gets from a new experience or Job, this time he had both. His father didn’t travel abroad much; he didn’t have to, his firm was situated in Edinburgh. The furthest he’d been away from home till now was Aberdeen on an excursion for the whole family. He could barely sit still in his seat, much to the annoyance to the man sitting next to him, already crammed into one corner just with the size of Joshua. He felt like a big kid, and was acting much the same. What anticipation can do to a man. He had yet to experience Airplane Cuisine too, but that would come soon, the trolley was coming down the isle as we speak, the stewardess carting it along was eye candy, as Americans would say, but he would say she was a bit of all right. Thoughts fleeted through his head faster than anything had before; at this rate he would figure out the meaning of life before the plane had landed. One thought triggered a muscle spasm in one arm, shoving the man sitting beside him into the window of the plane. JD: Oh, I’m so sorry He calmed down a bit with that, it’s strange how the body knows exactly when to become sensible, or more alert, but thousands of years of evolution would do that. Looking at the man more closely now, he didn’t seem to be that much smaller than Joshua, he just took up less space. Man: It’s quite ok, really Joshua was far from convinced that things were ok; he even scooted over his seat a bit to give the man some more room. JD: I didn’t mean to hit you like that Man: It’s fine. Is this your first time going to America? Dunn nodded; it was the quickest response he could muster at such short notice, but he followed it up nicely JD: It’s my first time flying too Man: Excited? JD: You could say that They exchanged a glance, and made an immediate bond that would last for the rest of the flight. Well at least talking idled away the hours Man: Holiday? JD: No, work. I’m going to be a wrestler. He practically beamed when he said that, the man saw exactly how enthusiastic he was about it too. Something told him that this man could easily go far if he set his mind to it, and not just because of his size, but that did help. Man: Really? The man looked somewhat familiar, more to the viewers at home than anything, and he said the word as if he was genuinely surprised JD: Yes, I just got my application accepted this week, now I’m flying out for the try outs. Man: Well congratulations. You know I could see you going far. What’s the name of the Federation? He quoted the next phrase from memory, as crisply as if he had read it off a cue card JD: The Universal Wrestling Federation Man: Well maybe not quite so far… The sudden change in the momentum of the conversation threw him a bit. He was so taken aback that he just had to ask… JD: Why? Man: Because I’m there. JD: What? You’re a Wrestler? Man: Yes, I’m James Pederson, pleased to meet you He extended his hand, in the limited space available, but the gesture was the same, Joshua took his hand and said his piece too JD: I’m Joshua Dunn Man: So what’s your wrestling name then? Again Joshua was thrown by his lingo, he was unsure of all the wrestling terms as such, but perhaps this man could actually get him familiar with all the complicated terms before he landed and went blazing ahead without a clue. JD: Eh, Joshua Dunn? The man smiled, he realised he was new to all this, he had possibly been through this himself at one stage, ahh to be naïve again James: No, no, you’re WRESTLING Name, you’re handle, you’re alias. Joshua just looked blankly at James, he had absolutely no idea what he was on about, and just with the blank stare he was able to get that message across perfectly. James: Look, my name is James Pederson, but my Wrestling name is Toxic Shock. You see? And realisation dawned on him, and his facial expression also got that message across perfectly JD: Oooooooooh, right I get you now James: And your wrestling name is? JD: Erm… I don’t have one… is that a bad thing? James: No, not really, you could wrestle under your own name, it’s just more usual to have an alias. Something like Killer Dude or The Plague, those are couple of names I’m sure you’ll come across in your career. JD: So I would need to make up a Name for myself? James: It’s not necessary, but most people do. A silence came over the pair, Joshua turned and faced the seat in front of him, and didn’t say another word, quite stumped as to why, James just shrugged it off and returned to gazing out the window. To him he never would have guessed that Joshua was Scottish, for a start, he didn’t have the accent. The tone he spoke in was so bland it could have been from anywhere, he just presumed the man was from London, seeing as their flight took off from there, and oh so many people came from London. JD: The Scotsman James: What? Joshua Turned back to face James, and repeated his words in roughly the same tone as before JD: The Scotsman… How’s that for a Wrestling name? James: The Scotsman? JD: Is there something wrong with that? James: Why the Scotsman? JD: Because I’m from Scotland James: You’re from Scotland? JD: Yup, Born and bred, then again most people make that mistake, guess it’s the accent James: Well it’s definitely not Scottish, and I think it needs a bit of work? JD: The Accent? You think it would help? James: No, the name, keep trying. You’ll find something that fits JD: So what should I expect in the UWF? The conversation went on for the rest of the flight, just wiling away the hours of the flight exchanging the stories from their pasts, of what pasts they had, they were young men the pair of them, their whole lives and careers ahead of them. So much yet to go through, so much which would later become history. But for now, just wistful dreaming of rising stars. After the flight, Joshua Swore this was a defining moment, and one he wasn’t about to forget. *** Joshua had finally managed to drag himself out of the house and down to the gym, convinced somehow that the exercise would help take his mind off the woman he’d met almost two weeks ago now, he still couldn’t get her name or her image out of his head, he was hooked, like he was on some imaginary drug, and was having so many emotional ups and downs that he could easily pass for it too. Currently finding himself on the rowing machine, the overall body work out gave an adequate distraction from the world outside, time enough to sort out the clutter of his mind, just time to think, hearing nothing but the rhythmic motions of the rowing machine itself. Almost completely zoned out by now, Joshua was thinking about only one thing, the thing he didn’t want to be thinking about on the build up to possibly his defining moment as a World Champion, and that was Alexia. The training was having the converse effect that he thought it would, entrenching the girl in his head all the more, the longer he took, the more time he had to think and the more engrained her name became into his. He had far more important things to be thinking about now, for a start because of his flittering mind state, he hadn’t booked a flight until late on, leaving him now much time to get used to the Tokyo Time Zone, one of many things he had neglected to do, but the worst so far. Days seemed to pass in a matter of minutes for him, he hadn’t had a decent nights sleep for want of another look of her beautiful smile. It was annoying him more now, perhaps he was just getting edgy, he thought he had had roughly 10 hours proper sleep in the past four days, the effects were seeping in, but insomnia was keeping him from sleeping it off. He felt rotten, he looked rotten, and he had neglected to shave for a couple of days, and the stubble was a fair length, possibly the only publicly visible hair he would be able to grow for a while. Large rings were around his eyes, and he was pretty sure he wasn’t that clear for driving, but he had no fear of falling asleep at the wheel, just as long as it wore off before he got to Tokyo, he was going to need all the help he could get, and a good nights sleep was paramount for feeling good enough to even step into the ring. But a good day’s training was even more important to fill himself with the determination to go on for as long as it took in that ring. He had seen what Ezra had to say; still he wasn’t convinced that the stipulation was to any advantage to him. Dunn prided himself on being able to go for long stretches of pure wrestling, some of his most glorious match victories were also his longest, he just seemed to be able to go that extra distance when exhaustion would have claimed any other man. The mount Gagazet death match II should have proven that, equally, if not even more so with the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. He was well enough known too for his technical prowess too. It just didn’t add up. Didn’t add up Of course, he was purposefully stacking the odds against him, like Joshua had done so many times before; He wanted to humiliate him by beating him at his own game. But it was a hell of a gamble to take, but with everything to win and hardly anything to lose, forcing the opposite onto Joshua’s very own shoulders, it could very well be worth it. He never had seen that calculating side to him before. He stopped on the rowing machine and just sat there in a bemused stupor. It startled him that he overlooked that entire side to the man that had helped him to win stable wars. How much more could he have missed? All thoughts of the woman had all but disappeared from his mind as he was pressed by a far more serious matter. He had more work cut out for him than he thought. So much to do and so little time. He got up, he had a new focus... but Alexia would be back, niggling at his cranium again, as soon as he settled down again, this he knew, and it almost set in again, but he wouldn’t let it, he had to get ready for Tokyo.
