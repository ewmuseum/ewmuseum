UWF (2001–07) Roleplay

Spots and Entrances — World Title Match: Killer Dude vs. Misfit

date unknown

Killer Dude / Misfit

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[“Remedy” by Seether hits the loud speakers, as the crowd gives Killer Dude a mixed reaction at first glance.] Frail, your skin is dry and pale, The pain will never fail And so we go Back to the Remedy [Killer Dude walks out on stage, and stands at the top of the ramp, taking in the sight of the 15 foot electrified cage surrounding the ring..] Rick: Killer Dude getting his first up and personal look at the electrified cage. Larson: (moaning) I don’t know what to do? Rick: What? Larson: I’m torn between Killer Dude and Misfit. I mean, I’m the announcer of Generation NeXT, but its Misfit man. Rick: Does it look like I care? Larson: This is all Stable Wars’ fault, damn it! Rick: Not that crap again. Clip, the wings that get you high And leave them where they lie And tell yourself You’ll be the death of me [Killer Dude begins to slowly saunter down the ramp, still not looking away at the demonic structure that lies in wait for him.] Larson: Betcha he’s regretting signing that contract now. Rick: Larson, this is Killer Dude we’re talking about here. He’ll probably try to grab it and see if it works. Larson: True. [Killer Dude makes it down the ramp, and stands by the open door to the cage.] Rick: Now, people, that door has been open all night, seeing as no one has the guts to touch it. Larson: That’s true enough, Rick. I can’t wait; it’s almost time for these two to turn into vegetables. Rick: I didn’t need to hear that. [Killer Dude climbs into the ring, and climbs to the top turnbuckle, raising his arms to the crowd, even though he doesn’t really care.] Rick: Well, Killer Dude is still acting like he cares about the crowd. Larson: But we all know that he doesn’t give a shit. Rick: Language, Rick. Larson: Fuck that, we’re on Pay Per View. Go ahead and fuckin’ sue me. Rick: Oy vey. [Killer Dude gets down from the turnbuckle, and begins to prepare himself. As he stands in the ring, the lights in the arena drop and the opening noise of “Counting Bodies Like Sheep to the Rhythm of the War Drums” begins.] Larson: And here he comes! [Red spotlights begin to scan the arena as the tension builds, finally stopping in the center of the stage where Misfit stands with his UWF World Heavyweight title draped over his shoulder. The crowd explodes into a chorus of boos, the World champion simply standing there with a disdainful grin on his face.] Rick: There he is, the UWF World heavyweight champion. Larson: AND The King of Pain, you know. Rick: Will you just pick a side already?! [Misfit makes his way down the aisle, looking more at the cage than his opponent. He stops outside the entrance for a moment, looking the structure up and down before stepping into the ring.] Rick: Now, this cage stands over 15 feet high and is amped up with… well… let’s just say it’s high voltage. Larson: No, c’mon, how much is it? Rick: It’s… a lot. Larson: I need a number here, Rick! Rick: I… uhh… I can’t tell you. Larson: What?! Rick: Yes, that’s right. I can’t tell you. In fact, it’s so high we can’t say it on live TV. Larson: But this is PPV. Rick: … shut up. [Misfit hands his title off to the ref and stands in the center of the ring, the two men already face-to-face. They start to trash talk as the music fades, and the bell sounds.] Rick: And we’re getting things underway quickly here! [The two continue to stare at one another as the excitement builds before coming to blows. They exchange a flurry of punches as the crowd explodes, neither man gaining the clear advantage until Killer Dude nails Misfit with a vicious forearm. KD stomps on him a few times for good measure, then Dude picks him up and charges, ramming him face first into the cage!] Larson: Dear Rod! [But, to the shock of everyone, nothing happens.] Rick: What! Larson: SHIT! It doesn’t work! Rick: Calm down, Larson. Larson: All that build-up for nothing! [Killer Dude, confused at this turn of events, grabs on to the cage. He suddenly begins to spasm as electricity courses through his body and he falls to the mat.] Larson: Ummmmm……. Never mind. Rick: Killer Dude has just proven that the cage does in fact work. However, the damage that caused to his body might be too much to overcome. Larson: He ruined it. He ruined his chance, just because he became curious. [Misfit gets to his feet, looking down at Killer Dude for a moment before taking off towards the ropes and coming down on him with an Asai moonsault.] Larson: Way to capitalize! [Quick as a cat Misfit is back up and runs in with a short legdrop before spinning around and going for the cover.] 1… Rick: Misfit going for the quick win there, but Dude managed to get out. [Killer Dude kicks out at two, but Misfit grabs onto his hair and lays a few closed fists into his face. Pulling him up, Misfit whips him towards the cage… but Dude holds on!] Rick: Dear Rod! [Both men go crashing into the cage, Misfit taking the brunt of the blow and screaming out in pain before he collapses to the mat. The King of Pain lies there convulsing as Dude bounces back with a shock to the arm. He drops down to one knee and holds his arm for a moment before smiling, seeing that Misfit is still down on the ground, holding his face in pain.] Rick: Oh, Jesus Christ. Larson: I KNEW IT! KILLER DUDE IS GOD! HE FEELS NO PAIN! Rick: He’s probably just drunk again. Larson: DON’T YOU START THAT AGAIN! [Killer Dude picks Misfit up over his shoulder and chucks him towards the cage, but he kicks Dude on the throw, sending him towards the cage as well.] Rick: ……. Larson: …….. Rick + Larson: We work with dumbasses. [Both men lie down on the mat for a moment while the fans cheer. They begin to stir, Misfit getting up to his knees first as Dude slumps back down. The King of Pain struggles to his feet, grabbing Dude by the hair and pulling him up. He lays into his stunned opponent with a hard right hand, followed by another, and another.] Rick: Misfit laying into Dude with some vicious right hands. Larson: That’s the way to do it! Don’t worry about the cage, just beat on the guy. Rick: … I’m shocked. That actually made sense. [Misfit finally nails him with a haymaker, sending Dude back down to his knees. He gets pulled into position between Misfit’s legs, the World champion wrenching him upwards and charging across the ring with a running sitout powerbomb.] Rick: Dear Rod!! Larson: Did you hear the impact on that?! I think they dented the ring! [Indeed they have, the spot beneath Killer Dude slumped down just a bit as Misfit stands back up and looks down at his opponent. He raises the horns in the air and sticks his tongue out, taunting the fans with the old dP signal, before picking Dude up again and preparing another move.] Rick: If these two aren’t careful, one of them is going to go straight through that ring! Larson: Not to mention that cage! Shocking! Rick: … You are so lame. [Misfit hooks Dude’s leg and signals for the Evenflow… but Dude holds on! Both men go crashing into the dented mat, ripping the canvas apart and sending them into a heap on the floor beneath! The crowd explodes with cheers, clapping their hands and chanting “This match rules! This match rules!”] Larson: Holy shit!! Rick: THEY JUST WENT THROUGH THE RING!! [The camera looks down on the two men lying tangled in a heap, neither man moving at the moment. The ref sticks his head down, checking on them and signaling for the ringside medic crew to be ready. Just as they’re about to cut power to the cage, Dude throws his hand up and grabs hold of the edge of the mat. Pulling himself out he leaves Misfit down in the hole, crawling out and collapsing on the mat.] Rick: Looks like this thing is still a go. Larson: I told you he felt no pain. Rick: I swear it’s all the beer. Larson: … shut up. [Dude gets to his knees and shakes the cobwebs loose from his head, turning back towards the hole… CRACK! He gets nailed in the face with a Singapore cane by Misfit!] Rick: Where did he get that?! [Dude falls back as Misfit tosses the cane out onto the mat and reaches beneath the ring again, pulling up a trash can full of weapons and tossing it into the ring as well.] Larson: Yippie! Rick: Yippie? Larson: The King of Pain just found his toys! [Misfit hops out of the hole and picks up the trash can, shaking out the loose weapons before lifting it over his head and hurling it down at Killer Dude’s head. It bounces off, leaving a dent and falling back into the hole.] Crowd: FUCK YOU MISFIT!! FUCK YOU MISFIT!! [Misfit scans the crowd with a smug grin, extending his arms into a crucifix pose and soaking up the jeers before going back to the weapon pile. He finally picks up a stop sign and moves over to Dude, who has picked up a golf club.] Rick: And Dude with his weapon of choice there, I don’t think Misfit sees it! [He does not until Dude jams it straight into face when he comes near, followed by another two cracks with the head of the club. Dude barely gets to his feet and fires another swing at Misfit’s abdomen to allow him enough time to stand firmly. He reaffirms his grip and swings, cracking Misfit in the skull with a sickening thud!] Larson: Ouch! Rick: He might have just cracked his skull! [Misfit is busted wide open, blood pouring from a gash on the wide of his head! The King of Pain is down on one knee as Dude tosses the club aside and hooks his head, lifting Misfit up into a vertical suplex position.] Rick: This could be dangerous… [Dude steadies himself for a moment, then hurls Misfit in the upside down vertical position straight into the cage!] Larson: HOLY SHIT!! Crowd: U-W-F!! U-W-F!! U-W-F!! [The King of Pain sticks to the cage for a moment, electricity coursing through his body until he finally falls off and slumps against the ropes just inside the ring. Killer Dude charges forward and rams a boot into his skull, knocking his skull back against the cage for just a moment and bouncing back with some visibly singed hair.] Rick: Dude is on it tonight! He’s absolutely vicious in there! Larson: It takes a real man to go toe-to-toe with The King of Pain, and Dude is only further proving himself here tonight. Rick: So you’re back on his side? Larson: I’m with whoever’s on top, Rick. You haven’t figured that out yet? [Dude slaps Misfit and rolls him out onto the mat before grabbing the stop sign and heading to the top rope.] Rick: Uh oh, what does he have planned here? [Dude positions himself and leaps off the ropes with a frogsplash, the stop sign held across his stomach and driving down onto Misfit’s chest with the impact.] Crowd: LET’S GO KILLER!! LET’S GO KILLER!! Rick: Dude is really getting the crowd behind him tonight. I don’t know how great a feat I’d consider that, though, since he’s in there with the most hated man in professional wrestling at the moment. Larson: It’s only because they can’t see, Rick. Rick: Oy vey, don’t start this again. I’ve had enough of your shit for one night. If Misfit Wins [Dude rolls off of Misfit and holds his own abdomen, the damage done to him as well as Misfit. The King of Pain lies almost motionless in a pool of his own blood as Dude struggles to turn around and drape his arm over the champion.] 1… 2… KICKOUT!! [The fans jeer, but still clap to show appreciation as Misfit kicks out. Dude, a little frustrated, looks at the ref for a moment before pulling Misfit up and hooking him into place for a Killer Smash.] Larson: Here it comes!! Rick: Dude looking for the Killer Smash, this could be it! [Just as Dude pulls Misfit’s head into position, a loud drum beat and driving guitar riff comes over the loud speaker. The crowd explodes as Mudvayne’s “Not Falling” blares into the arena, Slasher’s entrance video playing on the screen.] Larson: I knew that freak couldn’t stay out of this! [Dude drops Misfit and looks towards the ramp, but no one appears. He starts screaming for Slasher to show himself, but nothing comes into the arena. He turns around straight into a low blow for Misfit!] Larson: YES! Rick: What the hell?! [Misfit hooks Killer Dude in and pulls himself to his feet, draping Dude’s arm over his shoulder and lifting him up as the music stops.] Rick: It was a ploy all along! [The King of Pain drops Killer Dude straight down onto the stop sign head first, his neck practically bending in half as the crowd gasps.] Larson: STIGMATA~! Crowd: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! [Misfit manages to roll himself over and drape an arm across Dude’s chest.] 1… 2… 3!!!! Tommy Waters: Your winner, and STILL UWF World Heavyweight Champion… MISFIT!! [Both men lie motionless in the ring, their chests heaving as they suck in one deep breath after another. The ref holds Misfit’s hand up and drops the belt next to him, which he quickly grasps onto as his hand hits the mat. Neither man makes an attempt to stand, not that they could if they wanted to. Misfit’s music comes over the speaker as the crowd chants “THAT WAS AWESOME!”] Rick: What a dirty trick by The King of Pain! Larson: By any means necessary, Rick. Rick: Oy! That was a dumbass, and this is the guy they pay to sit next to him 3 times a week. We’ll see you soon! [Fade to black.] If Killer Dude Wins [Dude rolls off of Misfit and holds his own abdomen, the damage done to him as well as Misfit. Dude pulls himself over to the hole and reaches down inside, his upper body hanging down while his legs keep him in the ring. With some maneuvering he manages to pull himself… and he brings a table with him!] Rick: Dude has a table now, this can’t be good for Misfit. Larson: He’s soooo close! [Dude pulls himself up and leans the table against the cage with the metal legs out and quickly jumps back, getting a small zap from the conduction of electricity. He picks Misfit up and starts to position him for a Killer Smash.] Rick: Oh, dear Rod, no… Larson: He can’t be serious! That’ll kill him… maybe both of them! [Dude raises a fist to the crowd, soaking in the cheers for a moment before hoisting Misfit up… but he fights back! Dude has him up on his shoulders, but Misfit is throwing fists like there’s no tomorrow trying to get down.] Larson: There’s still some fight in him! [Misfit manages to slide off and nails Dude with a boot to the stomach, then hooks him in for Stigmata.] Rick: And the tables have turned now, but… [Just then Mudvayne’s “Not Falling” begins to blare into the arena!] Rick: It’s Slasher!! Larson: Please, no, not that freak! You had your shot!! Rick: And he was cheated out of it by Misfit! [The distraction is enough for Killer Dude to reverse the position again, ducking behind and lifting him up again. The music continues to play, but still no Slasher! Dude sends Misfit crashing down into the table with a vicious Killer Smash, the electricity coursing through his body for a moment before the table shatters.] Crowd: HOLY SHIT!! HOLY SHIT!! [The music fades as Dude falls down to make the cover.] 1… 2… 3!!!! Tommy Waters: Your winner… and NEW UWF World Heavyweight Champion… KILLER DUDE!! [The crowd explodes with cheers, a scattered chant of “THAT WAS AWESOME!” audible as well.] Larson: Wait! Rick: What? Larson: I’ve just gotten word that a pig has been seen flying through the area. Also, Satan has just spoken to me, and has said that Hell has suddenly turned cold. Rick: What are you talking about? Larson: We once said that hell would freeze over and pigs would fly if Killer Dude won the World Heavyweight Title. Rick: … You’re such a dumbass. Larson: Thank y… HEY……………….! [Both men lie motionless in the ring, their chests heaving as they suck in one deep breath after another. The ref holds Dude’s hand up and drops the belt next to him, which he quickly grasps onto as his hand hits the mat. Neither man makes an attempt to stand, not that they could if they wanted to. Killer Dude’s music comes over the speaker as the crowd chants “THAT WAS AWESOME!”] Rick: It was all a ploy! Slasher was never there to begin with! Larson: That dirty, psychotic bastard! He just cost Misfit the World title. Rick: One good turn deserves another, Larson. Larson: You and your logic! Rick: That’s all the time we have for tonight, folks. Live & Loud is in the books, and Killer Dude is your new World Champion! We’ll see you soon. [Fade to black.] No Contest Ending (Note: We want to build for a triple threat match at the next PPV no matter who wins, and I think with both of us turning out decent RPs, if the voting is close we should go with this finish. We’ve all worked on making this a long-term story that will eventually have Dude split off from Misfit and Slasher so they can have their singles feud and work on their new angles.) [Dude rolls off of Misfit and holds his own abdomen, the damage done to him as well as Misfit. Dude pulls himself over to the hole and reaches down inside, his upper body hanging down while his legs keep him in the ring. With some maneuvering he manages to pull himself… and he brings a table with him!] Rick: Dude has a table now, this can’t be good for Misfit. Larson: He’s soooo close! [Dude pulls himself up and leans the table against the cage with the metal legs out and quickly jumps back, getting a small zap from the conduction of electricity. He picks Misfit up and starts to position him for a Killer Smash.] Rick: Oh, dear Rod, no… Larson: He can’t be serious! That’ll kill him… maybe both of them! [Dude raises a fist to the crowd, soaking in the cheers for a moment before hoisting Misfit up… but he fights back! Dude has him up on his shoulders, but Misfit is throwing fists like there’s no tomorrow trying to get down.] Larson: There’s still some fight in him! [Misfit manages to slide off and nails Dude with a boot to the stomach, then hooks him in for Stigmata.] Rick: And the tables have turned now, but… [Just then Mudvayne’s “Not Falling” begins to blare into the arena!] Rick: It’s Slasher!! Larson: Please, no, not that freak! You had your shot!! Rick: And he was cheated out of it by Misfit! [Dude fights back, the distraction giving him enough time to work out of the hold. Both men struggle for the upper hand as Slasher’s music continues to play but he is nowhere to be seen. Misfit wraps and arm around Dude’s head, and Dude does the same as both men try to fling each other in the table… but they go together!!] Rick: DEAR ROD!! Crowd: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! [Both men go crashing through the table, a huge course of electricity causing a small explosion in the ring upon impact. The ref falls back from the blast and scrambles to get to his feet and access the situation as Mudvayne stops playing.] Larson: That was… awesome? Rick: More like painful. Neither man is moving! [The ref checks on the two competitors, each man now bleeding and covered in debris. He lifts a hand each and lets them fall, then signals for the bell.] Rick: What in the… Tommy Waters: Ladies and gentleman, I’m being told that neither Misfit nor Killer Dude is fit to continue this match. Therefore I regret to inform you that the referee has no choice other than to declare this bout… a no contest!! [The crowd boos as the electricity to the cage is cut and the door opened, medics rushing in with stretchers.] Rick: Folk, I… I don’t know what to say. Larson: That was pure carnage in there, and we don’t even have a winner! Rick: I guess we’ll be hearing what comes of this situation next week, but for now we’re out of time. See you then. [Fade to black.]
