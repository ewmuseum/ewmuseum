UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #276

Contiuing the fight

9 Mar 2004

Anarchy — SAS Locker room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to the locker room after the SAS 'All Comers' match.] Pryde : What the hell was that Ruff Rider? Who did you pay to drop that powder from the rafters? That has to be the most chicken shit way to win a match, and you call yourselves tag team champs. [Pryde punches a locker denting it.] Pryde : I have seen many cheap ways to win a match, heck, we've used many of them, but having someone do the tactics for you is low. [Pryde picks up a duffle bag and whips it across the room.] Dai : Prime Alliance, we will be coming for those titles. Hell, we will even make sure it is your asses we kick for them. Keep looking over your shoulders, especially you Ryder, you never know when it will be, maybe a week from now, maybe a month from now, maybe Sunday after your match. All I know is you can't predict Chaos. [Dai opens a locker.] Dai : Trailer Park Luchador, you were just a speed bump. Luchador, whatever, keeping you grounded was no problem. [Dai pulls a replica TPL mask from the locker, looks at it and laughs] Dai : Now I see why you wear this thing. You don't want people to see who you really are, you want to go out in public without being recognized. If you were, you wouldn't be the laughing stock of the trailer park world. I either suggest a new line of work, or continue hiding behind the mask. [Pryde grabs his bag and pulls out a towel and some shampoo then looks back up toward the camera.] Pryde : Nick Persia, taking the cheap shot from behind while I was finishing off Bondage. My mistake for turning my back to the new comer, hell, it was my fault for not pinning you after the Falcon Bomb earlier in the match. Next time I won't make the same mistake. I am challenging you to a one on one match. Anytime, anywhere. [Pryde and Dai both look at the camera man, then the door, then the camera man again.] Pryde : Now get out of here. We need to wash the stench of chicken shit off of us. [Dai stands up and starts to push the man back, straight out the door.] Dai : This interview is over. [The door gets slammed in his face and the last thing you see is the name CHAOS SOCIETY on the door as the camera fades to black.]
