UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #558

What memory?

17 Jul 2004

Joshua Dunn — Hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Next” Joshua watched a man coughing and wheezing get up from the seat across from him and make towards the GP’s office, he was here for a single reason, a medical check up. Common practice amongst UWF wrestlers, the federation had top notch medical cover for all its employee’s, and it’s no wonder why. Every fortnight the wrestlers of the UWF are required to be given the once over to see if their eligible to compete in the next fortnight of events. Before every PPV every wrestler is given a check up too, as it’s essential to know whether they’ll be able to put up a good performance, as well as making sure the people them selves are ok. That’s why Dunn is now surrounded by sickly people, coughing, in plaster or just looking miserable. He himself is still wearing his head bandages on his skull, so doesn’t look too out of place here, unlike everywhere else he goes now, people just stare at him, as if he had some horrifying disease. It wasn’t that Joshua wasn’t used to the attention, it was just that it was a different sort of attention now, people now look at him just to make sure their out of arms reach. But he liked his space, so it is a useful tool; he wondered why he never thought of that sooner actually. Even now amongst other people with their own ailments, they still look differently towards him, even in the company of sick people, he is managed to be singled out. It was a strange feeling; he felt as if their eyes were boring into his skull through the bandages. A couple of people were staring at him now, others in their own conversations, only now and again giving him a fleeting glance. Joshua himself just looked at the carpeted floor, having nothing better to do. Eventually a door opened in one of the GP’s offices and a man stepped out, wearing a neck brace, just behind him came the doctor, blond curly hair and blue eyes, a stethoscope hung around his neck, the young fellow seemed chirpy and full of life, maybe not that long out of medical school, but a life time of grief and other people’s pains would soon change his tune. He looked around the waiting room a bit, and called out. “Joshua Dunn” That was his cue, and he seized it, he slowly pushed himself up from the bench and drew himself to his full height of 6 ft 3 inches and made his way towards the doctor and the office. The Doctor showed him inside, pointed him towards the chair making sure to close the door behind him to preserve doctor patient confidentiality. Nothing about this meeting left this room. The Doctor seated himself behind his desk before finding Joshua’s medical notes from the filing cabinet. He punched something up on the computer screen on the desk and then turned his full attention towards Dunn, who was just waiting patiently for him to do so. Doctor: So I see you have had a bit of a mishap on your scalp The small talk just irritated Dunn; he couldn’t help but feel that with the stupid grin on the man’s face, he thought that he was talking to a person with the same IQ as a child. He let the ill feeling simmer for a while, just looking at the doctor through the top of his eye socket, waiting for the grin to fall from his face, which it soon did. Joshua used this precise moment to come back with his reply, as he now knew the doctor saw his humour was not doing anything for him. JD: I had noticed, in fact it was one of your colleagues that performed the operation The smile came back, and Joshua was disgusted by that, as he knew it meant that he was going to try another vein attempt at humour, and it probably would be a bit more malicious seeing as he got no response last time Doctor: Yes, nasty business that. Was a mop head wasn’t it? Perhaps Joshua would have to try a bit harder to tell the doctor that he wasn’t going to play that game. He looked at the teeth, perfectly set and white. He knew now where the arrogance came from, he had never had a problem in his life, and was prepared to step on anyone to get to where he wanted to be. Dunn had seen enough people like that to make a fair judgement about it. JD: It should say in the notes, you have read my notes? Touché, he could see it written all over the doctors face, a sort of satisfaction of being beaten at his own game, perhaps now they would be able to get somewhere. The doctor opened the file and battered away on the keyboard again, Joshua actually wondered if there was anything on the computer or if he was only doing that to make himself look more important, to be honest either could be true, the man seemed false in every aspect, he only hoped that his medical opinion wasn’t as false. The doctor turned back to Joshua a more professional look about him Doctor: Well, let me have a look at the scarring then, see how it’s healing As much as he didn’t want to, Dunn knew he’d have to remove the bandages, the only reason he was wearing them was to hide the scars from the eyes of the world, the looks he got for having bandages on his head were mild to the looks he knew he would get other wise, only one look in the mirror was all he needed to know that. But still, the doctor didn’t have x-ray vision, so he reached up and unwound the bandage from his head, there was a bit of yellow seepage on the under layers, but nothing too spectacular and as the last of the bandages fell from his head, the doctor could see the extent of the damage He couldn’t help but cringe at the sight, he was supposed to be professional about things but he really couldn’t help himself, there was unsightly scabs, blistering, and just redness of the skin that resembled something like a rash, but it was just blood vessels close to the top of the skin. Well the sight was enough to bring the feeling of vomit to the throat of any person, but he tried his best to force those urges to one side as he assessed what was the best course of action. The years he spent in medical school soon cam up with the most apt answer he could think off the top of his head. Doctor: Well the best thing I could suggest was a cream to apply and sooth any irritation and hopefully speed up the healing process Soothe the irritation indeed, well what was Joshua to expect, there’s no miracle cure to return him to the state he was in before Stable Wars. Well if it was the best he could offer, he would take it JD: It sounds good to me The doctor seized the chance the rapidly write down something on a scrap of paper, he wanted the sight taken away from his view as quickly as possible. He handed the scribbled note to Joshua, who took it calmly and read it Doctor: That’s the name of the product, go into any chemists and ask for it, it’s the best for this kind of ailment. Over the counter cream? Well, that was just taking the biscuit wasn’t it? Well if it would do the job, what was the point in importing some posh crap that does the exact same job? JD: Ok, thank you He wanted the man out of his practice as soon as possible, but there were a few things he was required to ask, well he could make it quick at least, the sooner he didn’t have to look at those scars the better Doctor: Have you had any other health complaints since your last check up? Joshua actually gave this some serious thought, he couldn’t remember any, but that means nothing, some injuries you just learn to live with, and you never notice them or remember to bring them up when in a situation just like this JD: Nope, I can’t think of any The doctor lowered his head to the notes again, flicking through the pages, just looking for anything else that could still be causing the man problems, he’ relished the chance to not have to look at his head JD: Could I get some fresh bandages? I don’t fancy putting the old ones back on The doctor looked back up again, and noticed the old bandages hanging out of the bin, the bag would be going to the incinerator, company policy, you can’t have this stuff just lying around in a dumpster, and it’s just not hygienic. He reached into the bottom drawer of his desk, and fumbled around and removed another roll of bandages, and Joshua started to apply them to his head again, he was relieved that the scars would be out of his sight soon enough, and it was just as well, as he had just found something that would mean that the guy would have to stay a bit longer. Oh well, you can’t have it all your own way, sometimes the job comes first. Doctor: Interesting, it says here you’re up for your annual CAT scan, we could send you through just now if you like Mr Dunn CAT scan? Joshua had never gone over the fine details of his medical cover contract, but it seemed to make sense, after all the scrapes the wrestlers of the UWF go through, it makes sense to have a CAT scan now and again. Well he’d never stuck around long enough to need one before, or it could have been a recent amendment to the contract he wasn’t aware of, whichever it was, it was probably a good reason. He finished tying his bandages. JD: That would be great The doctor got up out of his chair, closed down the file that was up on his computer screen and took the medical notes under one arm and showed Joshua out with the other. Dunn needed no other incentive to get out of the GP’s office, it wasn’t that he disliked going to the doctors, but it was the insufferable disinfectant smell he could not stand, and hospitals have it everywhere you go, you can’t escape from it. Moving around seemed to stifle the smell a bit, He wondered why that was so, but if it works, why question it? They left the GP’s office, the doctor in front, partially to show Joshua the way, and another so he wouldn’t be caught staring at the bandages on his head, and took him through various corridors which eventually took them to the CAT Scan room, there was a warning light to tell if it was in use, at the moment it wasn’t. All the better Dunn thought, as there would be less waiting around, the doctor took him straight into the room and showed him the slab that he had to lie down on, he flicked open the case notes and had a glance at them again, Joshua wasn’t sure why he was doing that, maybe it was just to busy himself while waiting for him to take off his coat and shoes. Joshua lay down on the slab as the doctor looked up from the notes. He attempted to make himself comfortable there, although he was sure it was impossible to do so, his neck was already hurting, and he’d only been on it for a few seconds. Doctor: This should only take a few minutes; you may want to close your eyes. Your only required to have your head examined, the rest of you will cost extra I’m afraid JD: No, no, the rest of me is fine; just do what you have to do The doctor shrugged and reached out and pushed a button, Joshua slid slowly into the machine, and he was quick to close his eyes, he had read somewhere about how damaging this could be, and he wasn’t going to be staring into the scanner as it passed over him. A buzz and a click later, a ten minute long buzz and click, he was taken back out of the machine and sat up on the slab he had previously lying on. Over by a computer screen the doctor was looking on with a puzzled look on his face, trying to decipher what was on screen, it was the image of Joshua’s brain. Curious to what the doctor was looking so puzzled over, the grabbed his coat and shoes and wandered over to the doctor. JD: What’s up Doc? He’d always wanted to use that line, but never had the opportunity to say it, but he was genuinely worried about what was wrong. The doctor pointed to an area on the screen. Doctor: You see this area on the screen; this colour indicates an area of the brain that isn’t being used JD: but that’s nothing right? I mean we only use 10% of our brains anyway Doctor: True enough, but this is in the area of the brain used for memory, have you had a bad bang to the head Mr Dunn? Or suffer from amnesia? It seemed like a silly question to Dunn, the only knock on his head recently was the one that gave him a cut across his forehead in the car accident, and that didn’t make him forget anything. Amnesia? He could remember everything… right back to… Damn those mal nourished Vietnamese twits, he still wasn’t rid of their influence, and he can’t remember anything since coming to America, apart that he’s Scottish, that’s about it. Perhaps the Doctor could help him that way. JD: Now that you mention it, yes, there are a few things that I can’t remember at all since coming to this country Doctor: Why did you never say anything at any other of your other consultations? JD: It never occurred to me until just now What was he going to say? Because some communist militants kidnapped and brainwashed him into following them under their red and yellow banner to victory, and erased his past to do so? That’s a great idea; why not commit yourself in the same sentence? Doctor: It seems to me that this area hasn’t been used for a couple of years, what I can do is prescribe you some drugs that can hopefully jog your memory, there may be some side effects, but they should be mild, just take two pills twice a day and you may remember what you’ve forgotten He scribbled down a note to give to the pharmacist, and handed it to Joshua with his signature on it Doctor: Hand this into the pharmacy n your way out. Joshua: Ok thank you, I will Doctor: Then I’ll see you next week He accepted the note without a second thought to what the side effects might be, if it could help him recapture his past, he’d take them Maybe now he could rid himself of the Local NVA’s influence once and for all
