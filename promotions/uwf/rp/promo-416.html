UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #416

Another fuckin roadblock

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins just after Supremacy as Ryder is walking out of the arena. He is wearing black wind pants and a red t-shirt that says Cherokee and then his picture and under the picture it says Thrilla. He is carrying his bag over his shoulder and is slowly making his way to his red and black Chevy Blazer. As he walks he notices something a little unfamiliar. The fans are actually hollering compliments at him. This is strange considering the fact that just last week he was being hurled expletives at every turn, and it causes him to stop and look up at the hundreds and hundreds of fans that have gathered after each show to catch even the slightest glimpse of a superstar. Ryder shoots the fans a smile as he soaks up the compliments. ) Fan: Don't worry bout Malcom Ruff, you'll get him. Fan 2: Yea, that piece of crap doesn't deserve you as a friend. Whip his ass! Drunk fan: Ryder, you got a perty mouf!! * Ryder gives the man a funny look * Fan 3: Hey Ryder, you gonna slap Luke Warren around? Ryder: I think I just might have to, after that little pissant interrupted my match and all. You all know me...am I just gonna let that slide? Fans all together: HELL NO!! Ryder: Thanks for the nice words you guys and does anyone remember my match with Mentok the mindtaker? Cause one of those punkass announcers jumped me from behind, and I lost my copy of the match so I can't tell which one it was, but if anyone knows i'd appreciate the help. That boy's gonna get a little visit sometime. * Ryder waves to the cheering fans before climbing into his ride and driving off. He drives down the street and pulls into an empty parking lot. He reaches into his bag and pulls out a mini camcorder. Ryder gets out of his vehicle and puts the camcorder on his hood and prepares to shoot a promo right there on the spot * Ryder: Was tonight a success? Hell no, not by a long shot, because I didn't get my hands on Malcolm Kennedy. Instead, someone else got to do what I had been waiting to do, but that's alright. At least the little bastard got the hell knocked out of him. I was sure Monty was gonna slap him around like a bitch anyways so I wasn't too worried in the first place, but it's all good. Don't think that i've lost focus of where my anger is Malcolm, because I haven't. Luke Warren isn't gonna be a distraction to me. Oh he's gonna get the hell beat out of him, that you can be sure of, but it's not gonna throw my focus off of you and the payback that I will get. Trust me boy, once i'm through with you there's gonna be nothin left but a shell of the man you could have been. See the jumpin ship isn't what pissed me off, that did bother me, but I can deal with that. The stable was laggin a bit and you made a career move. Nobody can fault you for that, but when you go and trash my name just so you can have a reason to jump ship? Fuck that! Now you gotta pay, and pay you will buddy. You will hurt and bleed for each and every insult you throw my way, because boy, there ain't an army in this world that can stop me from getting my revenge. * Ryder shoots the camera a cocky grin * Ryder: Ask Montgomery and the Player's Club. You're gonna see more of me than you can stomach, and I will not stop comin until the job is done. You see with the Player's Club I got cocky. I thought I could take on the whole group by myself and they've proven time and time again to everyone that has tried that one man can't take that crew. I'm not sayin they can't or won't be taken out, but it's just gonna take a little more time and planning and some help from a friend or two, but the job will get done. That's on the back burner for now though, because my main focus is on exposing you for the weak link that you are. Why is it Malcolm that stables are always great until you join? Why do you think that is? There comes a time boy, when you gotta look in the mirror and ask yourself...am I the cancer? I think you and everyone you've ever wrestled with knows the answer to that little question. I got you next week though, and even though it's not a one on one match...you can pretty much bet on me gettin my hands on you. We'll see what happens then, and you can bet your ass that i'll be ready when the bell rings to start that match. I hope you're ready too, because I want no excuses from you when I show the world just how much of a pussy you really are. All the talent in the world won't do you any good if you don't know what to do with it. You got it comin, and i'm the one bringin it. * Ryder smiles and nods his head at the camera * Ryder: Now as for the other men in the match, i've been you and there's one I haven't even wrestled. Study up, because you don't know the Cherokee Thrilla, but you can rest assured that I will be bring the heat when the bell rings. Don't make a wrong move, because the mood i'm in lately...I just might put you in the fuckin hospital. Good luck, because you're all gonna need it. ( Ryder's smile slowly changes to an angry scowl before he presses stop on the camera and the scene comes to an end. )
