UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #292

HIM~!

27 Mar 2004

Moraless — SAS#1

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You can never be too sure of what the future holds, of what lies around each corner. You can never be certain of what lies ahead of you and the destiny that you are to be involved in. Yet what happens when your own kin. Your own flesh and blood turns against you in a heinous act, which could be deemed unthinkable. Would you stand back and simply take it or would you retaliate. Would you decide. NO MORE am I going to be a stepping-stone, allowing the world to tread on me. Would you allow yourself one simple chance to grab the future and for once decide? Yes I am going to be a man. I mean it wasn’t as though he was the lesser man. Whilst James may have a match against Coricidon and some other bloke named Raijin but that was in the first round of the cruiserweight tournament at PTC plus it was looking very sketchy over whether or not he was going to get through or not. Whereas Moraless was already established his shot at the extreme championship guaranteed now, yet there was still something that was bothering him beyond all else. The fact he had already been over shadowed by his brother. James Edwards was the rookie and John ‘Moraless’ Edwards was the seasoned pro, he knew the business and had been around for years, James however had barely completed his rookie year, a year he spent three months in a training facility. Moraless wasn’t stupid. He knew that eventually James would return to wrestling after his very brief stint in Axiom ended, he just didn’t realise he would return to PTC. He didn’t realise that if the rumours were true, he would join a federation that Moraless himself had fought a brief and unsuccessful war against although he had taken the blood of Chris Storm…something that always made him smile. Moraless sat alone in the darkness seething with rage on the rageometer it would be about nine. The sound of bone cracking is audible as he grinds his hands, a brief but fulfilling release of pent up aggression briefly. Moraless: Of all the places he could have gone he had to go to RPW. Why can’t he just leave me alone when I saw him last I was happy to see him but now things are different. He had no right coming here. I have far too much on my mind than looking over my shoulder wondering if my own brother is going to stab me in the back. Moraless rose to his feet and sighed, flicking the switch on to the right of him causing the room to be lit by an eerie glow. The room was well furnished but desperately untidy. Imagine if you will the end of time itself and all that is left is garbage. Apparently Moraless hasn’t taken the news of James’ imminent arrival into his life all that well. Stalking slowly away from the sofa in which he was sat he sifts through the garbage that is strewn about the floor and makes his way to the hall before opening the front door and leaving his house walking under the night sky. He had always been a nocturnal person, the day time was just too crowded, people rushing about as though they had something important to do, not taking the time to look around them and see the world for what it was, beautiful. It was just people that ruined it. The night was warm with a slight breeze blowing through Moraless’ dark brown hair. He walked until he reached a solitary bench and sat down slowly staring up at the stars as he had done so many times before. Moraless: Why did he have to come now too, I was finally making a name for myself, people were beginning to pay attention to me in my own right. No longer was I living under my brothers past experiences, just because he got lucky in PCW and OSW. They don’t ever talk about how he only got into PCW because of a roster hiring spree in which half of them never even performed and OSW…wow he was there for such a long time and of course he earned so many awards and accolades there didn’t he. Moraless’ head drops into his hands as he lets out a long sigh, he isn’t so much frustrated now as upset. Fear does that to a man nobody wants to be forgotten, not really. Moraless: Now I am expected to focus on Sunday when I face Joshua Dunn. Dunn is a man that took out Seymour Almasy. I mean sure that’s not such a hard thing to do these days but there was a difference. I took him out in a three way match, another man inflicting punishment on him. Dunn beat Almasy in a Mount Gazaget match, how the hell am I supposed to compete with that. I am no where near focused enough to be able to do anything spectacular. I doubt I will even be able to beat him and all because he is there…in the back of my mind haunting me. He will mock me through his cruiserweight tournament too, the fans will flock back to him and I shall be left alone in the dark once more. I can’t allow that to happen to myself. I just can’t. I can’t… I can’t…
