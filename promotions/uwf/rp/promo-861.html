time is now

5 Jan 2005

Dreammaker — at a local jym..

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to Dreammaker lifting wieghts at what seems to be a very busy jym. He does some more reps and then towels himself off. After looking around at the many people working out he walks towards teh camera. He is obviously taking his time to get to this interview. ) DM: Well. Well, look at you Mr. Johnson. Fresh out of the house. Did the wife let you out or did you lose your keys? JEFF: Not funny Dreammaker. Just give me a few minutes and I'll be allowed to go back home and have a life. DM: Whatever suits your fancy. How did you get stuck with me? I mean with a name like yours, you should be working with Hotmask? You two could be playing 'poop chute' games all night long. (laughs and starts to towel himself off again) Jeff: What the fuck!? Just knock that crap out. We don't need anything like that here. Can't you be serious for at least five minutes? DM: (stops him with his hand and looks right at him) What do you mean serious? I mean won't it be you that is knocking the crap out? With your poop chute games? (laughs as he sits down on the bench). (laughter is heard behind them) JEFF: Anyways! ( mumbles to himself. 'And I do this why?') (In the back ground someone shouts 'Because Commi's arm is larger then your body') DM: (looks around and waves his hands in a quiet down motion) ( has a weird smirk on his face) Jeff: This is your first real test in the UWF. What can you say you are doing for preperation? DM: Wow! An original question! Man, it's so hard to find good talent these days. I mean look at our TV champ. But, back to your question. Well, we all know that I at home have been working really hard watching footage on Persia and other no names in this fed. And of course (looks at his watch and then holds out his hands behind him to point out the weight benches) Nobody works harder for anything in this fed.. And I mean nobody. I have been pushing those metal, American made, weighted bars more then me. I mean take a look! ( He then stands up and takes time to flex for the camera after throwing the towel to the ground). Jeff: So, what gives you the advantage over these other wrestlers? DM: (holds up his hands and there is cheering heard from all around the camera) ADvantage? (gives the camera a quick smile before walking around the jym. And he stops before a group of kids who are all very excited to be on TV) ADvantage! I don't need any advantage. I am prepared. I am skilled. ( pauses) I am ready for any challenge awaiting for me . I wrestle two men every week. SO, who is more prepared then me for this first match. And after I finally get that ass pirate to submit to one of my many sleepers, I 'll have an open door way to the finals. You see, I have never lost in a singles match. So who is more prepared? And then off to Oni, for a chance to prove myself in front fo the world that I can compete in the main event. Advantage. I have one that Oni has too, I have (raises his hands in a 'raise the roof ' fashion and the crowd goes noise is very loud) I have the fans. Who are ready for a true contest. A true contest of champions. No more cheating bastards, No more Ass pounding action you get on every card from your TV champ. No more choas society thinking they can do what ever they want. I have seen the future and it looks good. Get ready America. Get ready for the NEW YEAR! The time has come. Get ready for the real action on UWF. (with that said, Dreammaker walks over to his Gym bag and packs up his gear. The crowd is quiet. He then fstands up and flexes , showing off his massive bicepts and throws on a teal colored shirt. In black letters it reads, 'American made, American Dreams. Is it in you? ' Dreammaker then points at the words and mouths them to the camera. And the crowd cheers as Dreammaker walks out the door towards a focus. This purple car looks brand new. We see a young man walking up the street, obviously laboring from the Minnesota winter. He walks over to Dreammaker and gives him a good luck pat on his back. While holding his duffle bag in one hand and shaking dreammaker's other hand, we see this man is smiling as large as Nick Persia's women. Then Dreammaker waves good bye and the man walks towards the jym. After a second the man turns around and the keys to the focus are thrown at him. He then catches them and smiles. Dreammaker then picks up his duffle bag and points inside the building at the camera. He then walks on down that lonely road. That road that winners have to walk everyday in their lives. That road to victory. ) (the screen fades to black) *** i did spell gym wrong for a reason. if you actually read all the rp's you get my reason why? lol
