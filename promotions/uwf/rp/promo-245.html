Two Heads: Part One

26 Feb 2004

Iron Lung — Apartment

Two Heads: Part One Two Heads: Part One Starring Iron Lung As we open up our scene inside of the Villa Roma apartment complex, there seems to be a certain glimmer upon the glass windows as we walk past them, and also a very fresh scent as we grazed our feet along the Persian carpeting. It smelt like a new car, so fresh and clean, almost like baby powder. As we walked further into the Villa's hallways, more specifically on the thirteenth floor, the smell got stronger and the vibes were the same. It was a great feeling with every step drawn closer to the familiar red cherry oak door of Iron Lung, the newly crowned THC Champion. As we approached the door, we were basked in the gleeming light that shined from the golden plaque, reading his name of course. The rumors were true, he was now a signature 'golden' member of the complex, meaning he had spent more than five years there. It took a little long to engrave the gold by hand, but it was worth every ounce of sweat to ensure that it was going to the correct person. We stood outside the door, and didn't have any patience in our bodies at all. So, we urgently rapped on the door three times in succession, and on the third time the reply came to the door. It was almost as if we were in Oz and the 'gatekeeper' was going to tell us to go away, but Lung's voice was a little stronger than any oompa-loompa... Yah, come on in... We slowly turned the knob counter-clockwise, pushing it slightly so a crack formed in the door jam. Peeking inside, we see IL's hand wave towards us, motioning us to come all the way in.. (Immitating a 'hillbilly') Come on lil' feller, I ain't gonna hurt 'cha Now we are all the way inside of the room, and the first thing we see on the coffee table in front of us is the THC Championship. It has been shined and buffed out nicely, and we can see our own reflection within it. We look upon it while trying not to drool as Lung relaxes on his Lazy Boy. It's a beaut, I know. It took a lot of hard work to earn that thing too, so don't let anyone else tell you otherwise. I had to go through three other men just to get my SHOT at it, so your not just whistling dixie when you say it took me a lot to not just give up on it. And now that I have it in my hands, I'm not going to be letting it go for a while... We sit quietly, and the camera nods a little in approval. Lung was good at giving speaches, we were surprised that he never thought of becoming a politician. But then again, why would he want to lie and make promises that he knew that he wouldn't be able to keep? Ah, so I almost forgot that I'm not going to be having to let it out of my grasps this Supremacy, as I have a tag team match lined up. I'm not exactly a tag team type of guy. You see, sometimes I have a hard time trusting people that I haven't exactly been formally introduced to yet, so no offense to you Josh... I've always said that two heads are better than one, and I'm sure we'll be an awesome tag team come Sunday afternoon. Hell, we're going to have to be if we want to win this thing right? So, I've sort of come up with a plan... We both have to work together, and not try to steal each other's spotlight. Because I know with Beelzebozo and Kenny Mercury in the corner opposite ours, there's going to be a big ego crash. We can turn that against them, and pick up an easy victory! Lung sits in silence for a moment, perhaps thinking to himself about how great of an idea that was. But then, he raises an eyebrow as if to think about how it might not have been in his best interest to blurt those words out, for now Beelzebozo and Mercury knew their plan and they could exploit it against them. So Josh, I expect you and I to be a force to be reckoned with for this night and this night only. We'll pick up the win, and then go about our seperate ways. Let's face it, we all have our own careers to chase after, and I'm sure you don't want to be stuck with me for too long. Come to think of it, we may learn a thing or two about each other from this match, about our strengths and weaknesses that is. I just hope that if after this match, if we go one-on-one, that you have a backup strategy. Because once I get a feel for an opponent in the ring, I can counter anything they throw at me. I'm not trying to lay down a challenge, I'm just saying to be on the lookout. Hey, what's the wrestling world without a little friendly competition? Not much but a bunch of trash talking. But come Supremacy, we'll be putting our words into actions against these two CLOWNS. And when it's all said and done, you'll rack up another win for your career, and I'll remain undefeated for the fifth week in a row... We take one more look at the golden glow of the TCH Championship, and then we move back up to Iron Lung's chisled face, which actually now shows a slight grin on the corner of his lip. What could he possibly have up his sleeve? Josh, I have a feeling that at Supremacy, we're going to get along just fine. We can think of this as, just a slight inconvenience for our careers. We can work as one unit, two heads joined as one, and we can defeat these misfits. So I guess what I'm saying is, I'm putting my trust in you and extending my hand in partnership for this night... I await your response Josh, and I'll see you in my corner at Supremacy... The scene gradually fades to black with Iron Lung's flourescent red eyes peering into the camera one last time.
