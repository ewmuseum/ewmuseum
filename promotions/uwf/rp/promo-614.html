UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #614

Hard Time

13 Aug 2004

The Communist — Prison

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist sits in a small, dank cell. His cell mate sits on his bunk, reading letters from his girlfriend and sporting a black eye, bruised rib, and bloody nose from trying to make the THC champion his bitch. A light beating given the condition of the last few victims of the Commie. He leans against the stone wall, staring into space and wondering what his next move will be: Cell Mate: So, what're you in for? Commie: Assault and battery. Bringing the Revolution to the people and defending the innocent. Cell Mate: *chuckles* Assault and batter? What, did you beat up your girlfriend? Commie: No, One man I knocked through a window with a 16 lb sledgehammer, then I tied one man to a golf cart with his clubs and sent him down the lains. Then I beat on some skinheads who were attacking a group of jewish people. Cell Mate: ... well shit, I which I'd know that before I came after you. Why's you do all that? Commie: Some for training. Some to bring the revolution to the bourguasis. Some to help friends. :The Communist streaches out, and looks out the window, such that it is: Commie: I must prepare for my match on Sunday. I will cause Chaos Society much pain. They will feel the anger of the people, the rightious anger of the Communist. Cell Mate: Whoa! YOU are the Communist! I thought you looked familiar! THC Champion! Wow, that was an awesome match against Nick Persia. The Communist: That is neither here nor there. I must concentrate on my next match. Cell Mate: The Triple Go-Go Cage Double Ladder match for the THC championship? That's gonna be brutal. I almost wish we had Pay Per View here. The Communist: If this were a communist society, all people could see it, regardless of social status. Your country lies to you and tells you that you are all equal. But how equal is the man who works ten hours a day to the man who sits and counts the money made for him by the working man? Equality, a joke to your people. And now, the Great Revolutionary is locked behind bars so his message cannot reach the people. I must be free. I must spread my word to the people and tell them of the Revolution. I must free the Proletariot, and destroy the Bourguasis. Cell Mate: Yah, ummm, good luck with that... :The guard walks down the hall, accompanied by a small man with a long beard and a yamaka. They stop at the Communist's cell.: Guard: Alright Commie. Your bail's been paid. Get up and out. :The guard opens the door and the Communist steps out, looking at the Rabbi: Rabbi: Well, Nikoli, It's good to see you again, though I wish it weren't under these circumstances. Commie: Elder? Why have you paid my bail? As I remember, it was quite large... Rabbi: We owe you a debt of gratitude. Those skinheads haven't been by sence you whuped'em. We have some lawyers working on your case and we're pretty sure we can help you win. Now, we must be quick, Need to get you to Tokyo, you have an important match unless I'm mistaken. Commie: Yes... The Triple Go-Go Cage Double Ladder match. For the THC title. I must save the Soviette. Rabbi: Yes, must save the lady friend. Very important. You know... I have some idea's that may help you with that match. We need to call some people. Make some friends. *he smiles* And take care of some problems. Trust me, this will be good. You may even keep that title of yours. Commie: Really? Rabbi: Yes really. Come now, we've wasted enough of the wardens time, we need to get you to the airport. Commie: Yes. I will have my revenge.
