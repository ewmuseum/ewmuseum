Seymour Almasy — RP#3 for LiL IV

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reverence. It was all Seymour Almasy ever really wanted. To be given credit as the star he was. Primetime Central gave him that credit, as a two time PTC Global Champion and winner of two sanctioned tournaments. The UWF, however, did not show him respect. The fans cheered Tsunami Oni, a villain of villains. Now, however, the federation’s judgment was at hand. The light would cease to shine. The Dark Ages would begin… ------------------ In a small park near his apartment, Seymour Almasy sits on a rather shabby bench. Its odd location means that no one else is there, leaving Almasy alone. Large shady trees surround the Maester of Yevon. Seymour Almasy: Soon, it will all be over. Soon enough, my plans will be realized. The Intercontinental Title will be mine. And I will be but one belt away from having held them all. And yet…even then I am not satisfied. The Final Fantasy sighed deeply, looking around him. Lonliness was something he had grown used to ever since he and Laura…grew apart. It was part of him…a huge, aching gap. Seymour Almasy: All I have ever asked for is the respect due me. Primetime Central gives me that respect. They acknowledge that I am one of the best to ever set foot in a ring. Yet the UWF gives me…nothing. I am cast aside in favor of Tsunami Oni. Forced to sit by the sidelines and watch as any manner of no talents are hired and fired by this company on a weekly basis. And now, I am rehired…and at the same time as the company realizes the threat I am…they throw any manner of athletes in my path. To the UWF, Moraless, Killer Dude, Nick Persia, Misfit, and Kenny Mercury are five warm bodies that are being thrown in my path in an effort to derail my mission. Skill, talent, it matters not to the UWF. All that matters are that they are physical roadblocks. Lost in his conspiratorial thoughts, Almasy continues. Seymour Almasy: And the match itself. Three ladders. A recipe for disaster. An attempt to injure me…but sadly for the UWF, I will be in and out of that match as fast as I can. While others brawl mindlessly, I will ascend the ladder to the Intercontinental title, grab it, and leave with my pride and health intact. Such is what I will do. Yevon commands it, I will it, and so it shall come to pass. To Seymour, the matter is that simple indeed. Yevon gives him the power to succeed. He has the ability to make Yevon’s whims come to pass. Seymour Almasy: Yevon, in your infinite wisdom, you have given me the power to smite my adversaries, and become the greatest star the UWF has ever seen. Thank you for Thy gift, for it is a truly powerful one. I will do your bidding, now, and evermore. (Fade.)
