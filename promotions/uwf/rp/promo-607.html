UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #607

Life goes on...

7 Aug 2004

Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy — Republic of Singapore?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life continues on…. The twins are healing, Sean is once again with his beloved, Fey is getting ready for the birth of Ian’s and her twins. Things are getting back to the way their supposed to. But for the last few weeks, ever since the KB-UWF had to go onto their sister federations show to give a member of the KB his walking papers, Sean hasn’t been the same. He’s spent the last two weeks flying around the world on the Jet that the Order gives him and Annie to use to get around, taking his time between the United States for the UWF and the last few weeks, Australia and Singapore to spend some time with his fiance, Patti Anderson. Needless to say, it’s been an interesting few weeks. Take the reincarnation of a Greek Goddess of love and lust, and toss in a male Dryad from Ireland and you have quite the romantic interlude that would make Hugh Heffner and Larry Flynt blush. It’s been a distraction that Sean needed though. A few rough months with jumping from the PWW to the BDW, and now to the UWF. And what was his welcome to the UWF? The twins being attacked by his old stablemate, forming the KB and now what’s going on? Ruff Ryder was a member of the KB. He got hurt and now he comes back and does something to side with a putz like Jamie Shock? A man who’s using a stolen move that was given to Sean McClancy to use by his former teacher in the Hardcore ways? Ace Thunder was put out of wrestling in all honesty by one match… his match where Sean McClancy won and secured the THC and North American Titles back in the days of the GIWF. Yeah, Ace tried to come back once, and failed due to his lingering injuries sustained by his fellow Knight of the Order, Sean McClancy, but the legacy of the Hardcore Icon was passed from Ace Thunder to Sean McClancy… the bumps and the ThunderClap is his to claim… someone doesn’t know what it is to steal something as precious as gift that was given to only one person. Ruff Ryder and Sean will need to have some words. And Jamie Shock is now on a personal list. Sean’s shit list. But this week he’s got to deal with the former THC Champion, Nick Persia. A man who appears to be one of those who thinks themselves too big for this sport. He’s got no clue what freight train is coming for him right now. Defeating a former THC Champion would do good for Sean right now. But while he’s got many things he needs to do, he’s also got other things on his mind. Many other things. We now find the Celtic Night as he wakes up in the guest room of the Childress Villa in the Republic of Singapore. He and Patti stayed a few extra days after the graduation ceremony of his cousin. Sean being the Headmaster of the 4 Winds Academy had some things that he had to take care of here. He looks at the tussled mass of red hair that peeks out from under the covers next to him and thinks strongly at rolling over and just not leaving her again, but he’s already been informed of his match that is coming up. He’s got a job to do. He had traveled to Australia to deal with a man who had been put in charge of the KB-4WW and had then turned his back on the brotherhood of the Order and the Brigade to try to move in on Sean’s own woman. She had moved to spare the man, but she didn’t know fully what went on after that. As Sean left the ring, carrying her… He’d given the execution order to his cousin, Blaise and the hit was still made. No one left the KnightMare Brigade without a McClancy boot up your ass, and well, he got his in the form of Blaise McClancy. But then the two of them, Sean and Patti has gone off on a binge of finally getting some time together in almost 6 months of full seperation. Almost a dream state had been constant as they drank slowly from the old bottle of wine that had been given to Patti and then they had made love in ways that no human should be able to. But then again, we weren’t dealing with normal people here. On the outside you saw Sean McClancy the wrestler, but that was only for appearances. He crawls out from the under the sheets and slides on a pair of shorts, ensuring he does it slowly and gently so as to not awaken his lover. One last longing look and then the flame haired Irishman turns, knowing that he must leave again to go face someone else for the UWF…. Nick Persia He thinks he is gift of god to the UWF but then again what new kid on the block doesn’t. Sean has seen pleanty of new kids come and go in this busness… To many to name… Sean picks up his cell phone Sean: Call Annie. The phone makes a serous of tone clicks as it dials a number. He gives it a moment and then a soft female voice comes from the other side to greet him. Sean: Annie, is it ready? Ok I’ll be there shortly. No I’m na waiting until she wakes up. I ‘ave to leave befer then, er else ye won’na be seein’ me fer a while. I’ll be a’ th’ airport in a few ‘ours. I jus’ ‘ave one small thing t’ take care of an’ then I’m off t’ the airport. He reaches down and grabs his duffle bag and opens it up. Pulling out 3 long cylinders, he looks at them and then looks over at the sleeping woman. He stuffs them back into the bag, pulls out a t-shirt and zips it up quickly. Sean folds his phone up again slding his shirt over his chest and grabbing his bags, he heads out the door, and then out to the backyard of the Villa. He notices that no one is really around, only one of the hired help. Ian and Fey had already left for Souel, South Korea so as to ensure that their show would be ready for broadcast. He walks out of the yard and heads down the road a bit. While taking a taxi isn’t bad here, but it’s a small island country. A mile and a half down the road and he walks into a noted shipping store. It’s gold and brown sign denote it as a Singapore office of a noted United States shipping company. Sean quickly walks in and a small Oriental man on the other side of the counter greets him. Shipping Clerk: Can I help you, good sir? Sean pulls the three tubes from his duffle bag, and places them onto the countertop. Upon closer inspection they are seen as official looking scrolls, each with a wax seal, and then encased in a clear plastic shipping tube with an address label on them. Sean: Each o’ these ‘as an address label on it. It’s a document, bu’ it needs t’ be encases as this. I’ve ga’ me forms on file with ye. I was stayin’ a’ the Childress Villa over t’ week. Shipping Clerk: Not a problem sir. I’ll put them on the company charge for TaleSmith Productions. I’ve seen you around and know who you are Mr. McClancy. It is official TaleSmtih Productions business right? Sean: Yeah. It is. Bu’ their all goin’ t’ three differnet places. Two t’ places in Ireland an’ one in Spain. Shipping Clerk: No issues Mr. McClancy. Please, just sign this form and we’ll get them out right away. Are these going Express? Sean: Yes. Please. He takes the clipboard and fills out the details quickly and then signs it. Shipping Clerk: Thank you Mr. McClancy. Sean: No, thank you. ----------- Fade out here, as we fast forward to this side of the pacific as at a small business airport near the location of this weeks UWF show, we find the TaleSmith Productions jet as it’s landed with the Celtic Knight on board. ---------- Sean is walking down the ramp from the jet, and Annie Leanan is waiting for him next to a car arranged for his transport. Annie: It’s abou’ time ye ga’ back. Ye are days from yer match and ye ‘ave’na even ‘ad time t’ rest. An’ no, th’ sleepin’ ye been doin’ with tha’ woman, is na’ rest. ‘Ow are ye suposed to beat someone like Persia? ‘Er are ye jus’ goin’ t’ screw it up like ye did with Unforgiven? Sean glares at her but doesn’t say anything he knows she is right, no matter how much her jealousy of the one who defeated her for his hand, shows through. He isn’t ready for this match his mind is still filled with Patricia Andonia Anderson, and not what he’s supposed to be doing. One thing Sean has always siad “If you’re going to get in the ring, get in with a clear mind and be thinking about the match, not the outside world.” Annie: Now, Ox is off doing promo work. I’ve na’ fokin’ ‘eard from Ruff Ryder since ‘is shite at th’ last show, seein’ as ‘ow we were elsewhere. An’ th’ Twins are up an’ movin’ fully as ye know. Ye ga’ Nick Persia this week, an’ seein’ as ‘ow ‘e’s a fokin’ former THC champion, this could solidify yer position in tha’ division. Sean: Yeah, while idiots like Montgomery disappear for months an’ fokin’ come back an’ get a shot a’ the title they threw away. I’m still th’ BDW world champion, due t’ Penny ownin’ it an’ getting’ th’ UWF t’ recognize it. Wha’ is this shite? Annie: People finding ou’ tha’ Penny don’na play softball. Let th’ peacocks strut around. Ye also ga’ t’ be thinkin’ abou’ th’ upcomin’ PTC Extreme Title rematch an’ th’ Rumble comin’ up in Tokyo. Ye win tha’ an’ ye’ll be top dog aroun’ ‘ere again. Sean: I know. He leans back a bit in the car, looking to be off in thought. Sean: So, ‘ave ye talked t’ Ian an’ Fey? Annie: Worried abou’ yer sister? Sean: Well she’s 7 months along. Bryan an’ Breanna are goin’ t’ be a ‘andfull. I’m jus’ opin’ everythin’ goes will with all this travelin’ she’s doin’ fer ‘er company an’ th’ Order. Annie: She’s been sent ‘ome t’ Seattle. Ian is coverin’ everythin’ else, bu’ ‘is sattelite phone is on constant on riogh now. Bu’ I’ll call ‘er later if ye want me t’? Yer goin’ t’ be gettin’ ready. Bad enough we ga’ tha’ damn photo shoot fer the’ Pay Per View tomorrow. Sean: I know. Always ‘ated don’ those things. Annie stops for a moment and then smiles at him. Annie: Sean, ye know, ye an’ I ‘ad a talk th’ night befer I left Singapore. When ye ‘ad let Patti go off fer a bit t’ spy on yer cousin. It was an interestin’ talk an’ all. Sean: Yeah. I know. Annie: Well, yer a bit amiss t’day. Ye did it didn’t ye? Sean: Did wha’? Annie: Ye did it? Annie gives him a stern look. Sean: Ye migh’ as well know. Yes, I did. All three notices ‘ave been sent, an’ should be arrivin’ where they need t’ within the next day. Annie: Why? Sean: Don’cha be askin’ me questoins ye already know th’ fokin’ answers t’. Yer in no position t’ question me anymore. I’m ready fer it. Annie got the message there from the tone he used. Annie: Aye, me Prince an’ future King. Sean: Now, what’ya got on Nick Persia. We ga’ a fokin’ 3 hour drive ‘ere. Let’s get me filled in. I ga’ enough sleep on the plane seein’ as ‘ow I gained a day comin’ back. Annie: Very well. And at this, we fade out.
