UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #286

I have no idea where I'm going with this

13 Mar 2004

Mike Montgomery — The Doctor's office, I suppose. I'm not feeling overly creative here.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open our little (and I'm thinking hte emphasis will likely be placed on 'little') promo in a typically cramped Doctor's office. The walls are covered with posters about health related stuff, there's that little table thing they make you sit or lie down on along one wall, with a sink/counter along the opposite wall. Oh, and there's things to poke and prod people with. Lots of those. Sitting on that 'table thing' we find the Executive Vice-PPresident of Talent Relations, Mike Montgomery. Him being here really shouldn't be much of a surprise since, well, if you got whacked in the head with a brick, you'd be here to, wouldn't you? Anyway, the gauze has been removed from Mike's head, the cut finally closed up though still visible. Of course the rather large bruise surrounding it probably makes it rather difficult to hide the thing. The room's door opens, signalling the entrance of our generic, non-descript Doctor. He carries a manilla envelope with 'X-Rays' stamped across the front. He opens the envelope, removing it's contents and clipping them to the front of one of those light panels that every good Doctor's office has for just such an occaision. 'So give it to me straight Doc, am I gonna live?' Mike asks. 'Live, yes,' the Doctor answers, 'wrestle any time soon without risking severe brain damage? No.' 'That's not the answer I wanted, I've got a match this Sunday...no idea why, but I have the match.' 'Well I'm sorry but I can't in good conscience say that you won't do serious damage by stepping into the ring. Come look at this.' The Doctor flicks a switch beside the light panel, making the x-ray visible. 'Now you can see here where you took the brick shot,' the Doctor begins, pointing to a darkened area of Mike's skull, 'this line here is a minor skull fracture. The foggy portion around it is subdermal bruising. Quite simply, if Ruff Ryder had put more force into the blow, you would potentially be a vegetable right about now.' Mike nods, taking in the information, 'Good thing it's a tag match then.' 'Who are you facing, if I might ask?' 'Right To Party, why?' 'Quite simply, if it was someone like Ruff Ryder, I would be forced to have you banned from active competition because I have no reason to believe he wouldn't go for the same spot again. If he wanted to, he could easily put you into the hospital...or worse. Though I must say there's a certain degree of irony in you facing Bubba and Smokey.' Mike raises an eyebrow, 'Why is that?' 'A man with a damaged brain facing two men who are trying to destroy theirs?' 'Oh. Good point.' 'Getting back on track here, How have you been feeling?' 'Well, the odd bit of nausea, dizziness, nearly passed out at a bar about a week ago. I can work through it, probably just going to have to let the Plague fight the majority of the match or something.' 'Excellent,' the Doctor nods, 'that would indeed be in your best interests. While I certainly would prefer that you not compete at all, I have a feeling that there's very little I can do to keep you out of that ring.' Mike's voice is tinged with a touch more conviction than usual as he says, 'You got that right Doc.'
