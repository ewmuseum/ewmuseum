UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #205

A Week at Lungie's IV

3 Feb 2004

Iron Lung — Dunkin' Donuts

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Week at Lungie's IV A Week at Lungie's IV: Chai's & Coffee Starring Iron Lung Co-starring Herb the Dunkin' Donuts employee Previously on A Week at Lungie's Robert Pendragon took us on a wild ride to the Stop & Shop deposit center. During that four dollar adventure, Pendragon ran into the likes of a tooth-lacking and uncultured cashier. It proved to be amusing, but for some reason it wasn't fitting for what we originally thought this programming was going to give to us. The days were seemingly getting longer with each moment we awaited Rob to do something exciting, but it was as if he was playing some sort of game with us. This couldn't be the private life of a wrestler, this was more along the public life of a slob or a bum. Roaming around without a care in the world? That definitly wasn't what went through Pendragon's head when he stepped into the ring, why would it be the way he acted in a typical day? Where was the drama, where was the spark of excitement? Life couldn't be this dull for a mind that was so complex. Something was going on, and we were going to find out just exactly what that something was, wheter he wanted us to find out or not... We now return to A Week at Lungie's, with the fourth installment, Chai's & Coffee The cameras turn on with a view from the inside of Robert Pendragon's car, first focusing on the spedometer that reads 35 miles per hour. Next it shifts to the dated stereo system which is playing the latest hit from some trance artist (trance music always put him in the mood to go about the day for some odd reason). The camera now shifts down to the automatic gear shifter, the main joystick for controlling the cars movements. A firm hand grips onto it, pretending to be shifting as if in a standard transmission. We can see his white bare knuckles, and we soon enough go up his arm and to his other hand, which is firmly placed on the steering wheel. Fingers tapping to the beat of the radio, we drive along the paved road ahead of us, coming to a stop at a red light. Letting out a brief sigh, Pendragon hums to himself now as the light slowly but surely changes to green. Quickly accelerating, we keep a view on the scenery. Moments later, we're slowing down again as Rob puts his signal light on, ready to turn into a very familiar parking lot.. The local Stop and Shop What were we doing here again? That's what we kept asking as Pendragon pulled into his usual spot, away from any idiot that might try to 'accidently' hit him or something. Getting out of the vehicle after going through a shut off protocol, Robert closes the door and locks the car with his keyless entry. A beeping sound is heard coming from the car as he starts to swiftly walk towards the Stop and Shop for the second time in this broadcast's history. Going past the deposit center doors, Pendragon looks upon the store's entrance doors, which are also automatic to provide customers an easy shopping experience (either that, or to have the customers keep being lazy). Outside of the store are a couple rows of specials, flowers meant for spring planting and firewood for keeping warm were in attendance. All a customer had to do was bring it into the store and pay for it. Now that's what I call convenience! Now walking into the store, the cameras keep a close eye on Rob's movements. He makes his way around the snacked goods endcap. This was a first site for customers, being on a properly placed block inbetween the exit and the rest of the store. And now, our hero walks straight past the aisles, where was he going? Suddenly, our view shifts as Pendragon's body turns to an orange and purple blinking light. The sign reads Dunkin Donuts. Surely, he had to be kidding right? We hoped that he was just pulling another prank, but as he looked upon the menu, our hopes disappeared into dust. Now stepping up to the counter and still looking at the various choices, we can only look in utter shock as the cashier looks out from the Munchins he is putting into their baskets. He gives Rob a brief smile as he comes closer, tapping his hand on the counter slightly. How can I help you this morning? Still looking upon the selections, Pendragon scratches at his freshly-shaven cheek in a Billy Kidman-esque manner. He then lets out a murmer from his lips to let the cashier know that he's still deciding. Noticing his deep concentration, the cashier tries to push the special of the day on him. Perhaps you'd like to try our brand new Espresso or Latte with any donut, it's only $2.75! Hmm... Coffee with any donut is the same price, it's sort of a promotional deal because of new donuts we have! Rob looks at the cashier's tag, and reads 'Herb' from left to right. He then smiles at Herb and nods his head. Herb is clueless on what exactly it is that he is doing, but he manages to smile back as well. Pendragon then lets out a little chuckle as he taps his hand on the counter gently and places his order. Yah, um... get me the coffee and donut thing Herb.. Sure thing. How do you like your coffee? French Vanilla coffee, cream with two sugars. And which donut are you going to have with that? Just a Boston Creme donut. All right, excellent selection. Coming right up! After hearing that it would come right up, Pendragon smiles again and slowly turns to the side to reach into his wallet. Taking out three dollars, he puts the wallet back into his pocket and awaits his order. Herb was just finishing up the coffee, and soon after that he grabbed the Boston Creme donut using a foil-papered wrapper. Putting it into a 'to go' bag, Herb rings up Pendragon's order with a hum in his work. Two seventy-five please. Here's three dollars, you can keep the change boss man. Thanks for the speedy delivery. You're quite welcome, and thank you too. Have a good day! You too Herb. Taking his order and sipping at his coffee, Pendragon slowly walks away from the Dunkin' Donuts and out of the Stop & Shop doors with a smile and a laugh. Retreating back to his vehicle, Rob keeps sipping the access coffee that spilt onto the lid. Putting the coffee up on the roof as he opens the door, Pendragon, puts the coffee in the cup holder and brings the bag with the donut in it inside, placing it on the passenger seat as he closes the door and buckles up. Starting the car up and listening to the engine purr for a moment (he loved that sound) before pulling out of the parking space, Pendragon now takes us on another illustrious journey. Our week was almost over, and it had been on the very dul and boring,l to the point of irritating side. When was this madness going to stop? Damn it, we were getting sick of these pointless journies. There had to be someway to get Pendragon going, but how?... The end of 'A Week at Lungies IV', but be sure to catch Robert Pendragon in the final episode of this work-week long documentary, 'A Week at Lungies V: The Final Destination'. Until next time, keep us tuned in!
