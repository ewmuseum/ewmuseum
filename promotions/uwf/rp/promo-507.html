UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #507

One step closer to Revenge

7 Jun 2004

Joshua Dunn — Production building

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Where are we now? A day closer to SAS and a day closer to when Joshua Dunn faces Corporal Punishment to gain some form of retribution for the embarrassment he felt at his hands. Perhaps we shall see exactly what sleeping beast has been awoken inside Dunn on that day, angers a terrible thing to unleash, as it never fits back into its box properly after it’s been used] “Ok and say it like it is!” It was management’s idea, the target audience of the federation is those aged between 8 and 39, that’s some range, but there are a few fools that just have to go out and do what they see on TV, and that is something that, at least the officials of the UWF, do not want to happen. And in the shadow of Stable Wars, and the events held within, they think it would be appropriate to release a “do not try this at home” video to discourage such stupidity. Wanting some of the better known people to take part to try and hammer the message home, they tried all the most popular wrestlers, and most were more than eager to stop some stupid kid from hurting themselves by mimicking what they see on the TV. Joshua Dunn was one of the first names that jumped into their heads, especially in hindsight of Stable Wars. Naturally, Joshua jumped at the chance. He may have had a dodgy past in the UWF, but he knows a good idea when its ran past him. Even if it discourages one child from seriously injuring one of their friends, he’d consider it a success, one less casualty, it’s better than none. And right now he sat in a black, director style, canvas chair facing a camera and the production crew, his head bandaged up to cover the ghastly scars from having the mop surgically removed. The little red light on the camera soon flickered on meaning it was his queue to say his piece JD: I’ve had 4 bruised ribs, 2 cracked and 1 broken, I’ve sustained countless head injuries and have had a hairline fracture in my skull, and most obvious is having toxic glue applied to my bare head, the doctors say it will be scared for some months Voice: Cut! The director called out on the set, the little red light flashed out, and Dunn took a deep breath glad that his new manufactured screen appearance was over with, well in the mean time, there were a few more snippets the Director wanted to splice through the footage. He had been told the final thing would last a whole five minutes in all, it doesn’t sound like much, but when it’s all filled with UWF Superstars recalling their past injuries from past to present mostly one after another, it seems a bit like over kill. Director: That was great, absolutely sincere, just what we need, just a closing don’t try this at home should do… Let’s try that right now, just point at the camera, looking menacing and say “don’t try this at home” Dunn lifted his finger to the camera as asked and fixed a domineering glare at the camera, something felt a bit strange, he wasn’t quite sure what, maybe that it was he was so used to there being another person at the end of his stares instead of a blank camera lens JD: Don’t try this at home The director strokes an invisible beard on his chin, and wrinkles up his nose, he had been viewing how the scene would look in the end product via a monitor hooked up to the camera to allow for exactly that. He didn’t seem too pleased with what he saw. Director: No… No I don’t’ think that worked, it’s not right; it didn’t feel right did it? It was a rhetorical question but he looked around for a response anyway, a few of the other members of the crew shook their heads and/or murmured something in way of an answer. Director: Ok, I think for you Mr Dunn, I think we should try a Please. Yes, I’m sure it would feel much better, and keep that serious face Joshua sighed, well he was never going into the film business, especially if they were as finicky about every trivial thing like that but he complied with the request. He leaned in towards the camera, and the director leaned and whispered to the camera man, Dunn managed to catch out of the corner of his eye the TV screen showing him zoom in closer, suddenly he felt a lot more comfortable with the whole thing JD: Please, Don’t try this at home Director: CUT! That’s a wrap, that was much better don’t you agree? That pause was just the perfect length too Mr Dunn. Have you ever considered a career in the movies? A smile passed over Joshua’s face. In fact it had never crossed his mind at all, having to read from a script rather than just saying what was on his mind seemed as alien to him as a man from Russian is to an American. And in his current state he’d have to pass up, he didn’t know of many disfigured actors there were in the world. JD: No, I have a career The Director let out a bellowing laugh, and the crew let out an accompanying giggle from some of the crew members wanting to get a bit further in their own careers by playing suck up. It was rather pathetic really that they had to resort to living in another man’s shadow just to get recognition. The Director’s laughter faded after a while, Dunn patiently waiting for the moment. The Director wiped a tear from his eye sighing out the last laugh from his lungs Director: I like the Cut of your gibe Mr Dunn Hah, Dunn knew a suck up when he saw one all right; he found it pathetic when a man tried to live off of another man’s success to feed his own need for power. But he was far too polite to say anything to the director, but no doubt some day someone would, right before they threw him out on his rear. JD: Can I ask what footage you’re using of me in this? Obviously glad that he asked, the Director fed his own ego by revealing his own brilliance to those who asked. Dunn had asked for it all right. Director: Well, it’ll start with something from your cage ladder match for the IC title all that time ago, you took a shot to the ribs with a ladder then I think, then a couple of shots of you in a hardcore match, maybe from your Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet, and finally round it off with the footage from Stable Wars of you getting the mop glued to your head and the medical picture of what your head looked like afterwards Joshua wasn’t too keen on having his hideous scars broadcast to the world close up, but he knew that the graphical images are the one’s people remember the most, and if it stopped a few idiots from trying the same thing for a kick it had done its job. JD: That sounds like a fair display of the injuries sustained, and this is the same for all the wrestlers is it? Director: Of course, no half measures when I’m around Again he let out a bellowing laugh, but it didn’t seem like a true laugh to Joshua, it sounded like a put on, he wondered what the man was trying to hide, if it was how much he wanted Dunn to help him further his career, he wasn’t making a very good job of it. Dunn pushed himself up and off of the canvas chair leaving the crew’s camera view JD: Well then I’ll be off, you seen very busy Director: I hope I’ll work with you again some time Mr Dunn Dunn turned away from him and walked off the set, under his breath adding his final words on the matter JD: Not if I can help it Leaving the Crew to do what ever it is they do to make the whole thing watchable on TV, Dunn saw several other people milling about throughout sets set up all over the place, with various wall covering and wooden backdrops to make it appear as if the industrial process was actually a personal thing in rooms individually, he had to give kudos to the lighting teams making the perfect ambiance of room lighting throughout the all the sets, but then their paid for it. Not able to stand much more of the hustle in the building, or being offered another coffee, he left as swiftly as possible slipping past a few people who said a complimentary hello, which he replied to, and he knew they would all go home to their kids and tell them that they had spoken with Joshua Dunn, the exaggeration in that was so sickening he almost went a bit green at the thought, but no matter, it wasn’t any of his concern anyway On exiting the building the fresh air hit him almost as hard as some of the knocks he had been given in the ring, the air was crisp and clean smelling, his only regret was the that itching bandages stopped the air from refreshing his scalp as it normally did, one more thing to thank the Corporal for he thought to himself. Even the slight mention of his name was enough to trigger the festering thoughts of revenge in his head again, his fists closed and started turning white before he even notice himself. Upon feeling his finger nails digging into his palm, he consciously drifter his thoughts from revenge, which is easier said than done, and looked out at the scene surrounding him. It was a pleasant enough day, the sun was out and the sky was only filled with light wispy clouds. A few kids were playing on the park opposite from the building of which he emerged. It was nice to see them not cooped up inside either watching TV or playing computer games, if only all children were brought up that way. Then he spotted something that was about to make his day just that little bit worse. Voice: Hey Mr Dunn! Running up the steps that lead to the building’s main doors was Jack Kipling. The man had been in a car accident with Dunn not that long ago, he had escaped unharmed, good news for him, but felt a bit guilty seeing as Dunn had a large scar across his forehead to show for it, which was now covered by the bandages he was using to hide his scalp. Ever since he has been following Joshua Around like some lost puppy, to be honest e both irritates the hell out of Dunn and at the same time finds it a bit sad. He was sure there could be more important things that jack could be doing right now rather than what he was right now Jack: I’ve been looking for you since stable wars ended; you are one hard man to find at times Joshua knew exactly why he had been hard to find, he was in a doctor’s surgery getting that damned mop head removed, the ignorance of him made Dunn a bit mad, but he kept it inside, putting it to one side to come out at a more appropriate time JD: Well I do try That made jack laugh, he was far too ignorant to take that the wrong way, to him, suffering happened to other people, he hadn’t even had a broken bone in his life, lucky git Jack: You’re a funny one Mr Dunn There was then a silence, Jack scratched his head, and Dunn kept a nonchalant look fixed on him, drawing out a long awkward silence, he was playing a little game to see how long it would take Jack to find something else to say or give up and leave, he knew the latter result wouldn’t happen without some persuasion on his part, so he simply waited to see if he had anything else to say Jack: Erm… Do you need a hand with anything or want me to get you something? Out of sheer lack of something to say Jack gave that reply, while saying it he also scratched the back of his neck, he felt insanely awkward around Joshua, and Dunn revelled in it. JD: You really want there to be something don’t you? Jack: Well if it’s that obvious I must have it written on my face Jack let out a small laugh, pleased at his own wit, and Dunn chuckled also, even though he was annoying, he couldn’t deny him the laugh that the line deserved. Hold on a minute… What did he just say? JD: What did you just say? Jack: It’s kind of obvious? JD: No, no, after that Jack: I should have it written on my face? Dunn ran the words through his head, his mouth spelling out the words visually as he ran the idea through his head. The more he did it the better the idea seemed. Yes, yes, that’s it JD: Maybe there is something you could help me with Dunn leads jack down the steps and off to catch a bus, do you really think he’d be thinking of driving after just having an accident? [The Scene Fades out]
