UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #551

From Hong Kong

15 Jul 2004

Angel — Hong Kong Harbour

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Use the following link for a html & colourful version: http://mysite.wanadoo-members.co.uk/raynus2781/angelrp1.htm ----------------------------------------------------------- The scene opens in the bay of Hong Kong, its night and the lights from the many buildings and advertisements glisten on the sea. The boats speeds up and down the bay, taking their cargo and passengers to and from the mainland. We focus in towards to the city of Hong Kong itself; coming closer to the shoreline we see a small Chinese restaurant more or less sitting on the edge next to the ocean, Angel can be seen sitting and looking out at the sea whilst enjoying some food. Angel continues eating his food and turns back to face the counter. Angel: Mmmm, Szechwan Chicken…hot and spicy just how I like it. I can’t believe I have to come to this restaurant every time I want some good Chinese food. No place in the world makes it this good. The proprietor, who is half listening to Angel, looks up and begins to look suspiciously at him. Angel notices the proprietor staring. Angel: Erm, what is it? Do I have something on my chin? Proprietor: No, I recognise you…I seen you somewhere before. Angel: Yes, you saw me here last week eating more Szechwan Chicken. Proprietor: No, no…I mean like on television. I know who you are…you’re that wrestling man from the…UWF? Angel smiles Angel: That is correct, so do you watch wrestling much? Proprietor: A little, I just remember you because you said you trained outside of the city here. Angel: That is true, I did for four years. The proprietor smiles and continues to cook food for the others customers. Angel continues to eat his own food not noticing Ayami walking up next to him. Ayami: Angel, there you are…sneaked of for some dinner then? Angel turns with an innocent look on his face and noodles hanging out his mouth. Angel: Me? Never, I am merely sampling this local cuisine to get a taste and appreciation for the Chinese culture. Ayami: But you come here nearly every week. Angel: That is true…because it takes a lot of time to appreciate the Chinese culture. Ayami: You’re an awful liar Angel. Angel: Well I am the defender of good; I can hardly lie now can I? Ayami: Anyway, you do realise you have a match this Saturday? Against Mr. Meaner? Angel’s eyes widen Angel: You mean Ezra Kazee right…Mr. Meaner is his former name. Ayami: No, he has turned to heel he is bad…again. Angel: But I turned him, I made him see the light and the error of his way. He can’t have changed; I thought he was strong to resist that temptation to resist returning to his old criminal ways. Ayami: Unfortunately he has turned, so what are you going to do? Angel: I don’t know I have to fight him and defeat him because he is evil…but he was a friend and a good friend. Ezra spearheaded the New Era with me he helped me spread my word in the GIWF and continued to do so when I left. Ezra was voted most gracious in defeat; a man of evil would never be voted for something like that. Ayami: I know Angel, it baffles even me but he has changed his name and his style of fighting something has brought the evil out of him and I cannot say what that is. But whatever has turned him evil must be very strong because he was a good man. Angel: Do you believe he has another chance for salvation from evil? Ayami: I think he may, but he needs to see the error of his ways and that’s something you will have to drag to the surface. Ayami runs her hands through Angel’s long white hair and smiles. Ayami: Come on, the night is young lets take a walk and discuss this further. Angel: But I haven’t finished my food. Ayami: You can finish it later. Ayami grabs Angel by his arm and pulls him away from the stool. Angel: You’re so bossy you are…if you weren’t so good at Kung Fu…I would stay here. Ayami: Quit pouting. Angel: I’m not pouting… [The scene fades] [The scene opens] The scene opens on the bustling streets of Hong Kong, the car and trucks speed up and down the road going to their various ports of call. The street is bathed in a luminescent yellow from all the streetlights, shop lights and glowing pulsating advertisements that line the large buildings of the city. Angel and Ayami are walking down the road weaving in and out of the other citizens of the city. We focus in on Angel and Ayami as they enter a small street side café. Sitting down at one of the tables the two order a coffee each. Angel leans over to Ayami to try and keep the conversation private. Angel: So, the match that I have how do you think I should play it? Shall I just fight and defeat Ezra…I mean Mr. Meaner or try and turn him? Ayami: I think it would be very hard to get Meaner in a position to turn him. If that’s all you going to try and do, it’s likely he would get the upper hand and you suffer a defeat. The last time you ‘turned’ Meaner was when you had people to help you pin him down. In the UWF, you have no one. Angel: I understand and then I must just defeat him. Another time will emerge when I can turn him. Angel closes his eyes tight, pinching the bridge of his nose. Ayami: Something wrong? Angel: Not really, its just there is hardly any wrestlers that walk the path of good in the UWF. They are mostly evil; they believe it some how cool to torture the innocent and listen to devilish music. It is not a good example for the young of today. Ayami: The young are impressionable; they see these wrestlers and mimic their ways and music. It is an easy way to get at the fans; no one wants to be good…they believe good guys finish last. Angel: I don’t understand why, why is it cool to take drugs and hurt your fellow man. Why is it un-cool to just do the right thing and follow the rules? Sure there is a time certain rules need to be broken, especially when it is to save another life. But I do not understand this modern cultures fascination with the evil and unjust. Ayami: Quite simply it is human nature; its peoples right to choose what they do. But when you have such evil it turns these people unwillingly then that’s when action needs to be taken. When someone tempts another to take drugs, that’s when action should be taken to stamp out that evil. That is why we are here. Angel: Then it is up to me to defeat Mr. Meaner, I know he will be a tough challenge but I know I can beat him. I have to beat him, but I have to turn him. Maybe not at that match, but someday I will turn him. I can’t stand to see a friend be consumed by this evil. Ayami: I understand that Angel, if that is what you think you need to do that then you do it. I will be always by your side helping you with your task. That is why am here. Angel smiles Angel: I hope you will be always by my side; I couldn’t bare it to be without you. I know I can be strong, but you’re my wife and mentor I would find it hard to continue without you. Ayami smiles also. Ayami: I am not going anywhere, don’t you worry I can take on most foes if they choose to attack me. Angel: Your were knocked over my Joanna last Wednesday though. Ayami: That woman caught me off guard; a woman who hits from behind is dishonourable. I will face her again in the ring and defeat her. Angel: You look so attractive when you get that determined look on your face. Ayami blushes Ayami: Sweet talk only gets you so far Angel. Angel: I know it gets me as far as I want to go. Ayami looks at her watch. Ayami: Anyway, it is getting late we need to get back to the hotel and then leave for the U.S again to have your match, if it wasn’t for your need for authentic Chinese cuisine we could’ve stayed in the U.S and trained. Angel: I have trained enough to last me a lifetime. Ayami smiles. Ayami: Keeps you in check though. Angel and Ayami finish their drinks and leave the café as the scene fades [Scene Fades]
