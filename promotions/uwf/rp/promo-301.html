UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #301

And the name of the game is....

5 Apr 2004

Ezra Kazee — Shareholders office.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up just outside the familiar shed of the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Inside, sitting behind a pile of papers is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. For some odd reason, he is wearing a Joshua Dunn t-shirt, probably just to support his stablemate. There is the familiar glow of Christmas lights are seen, as well as the glow of a television.) Kazee: Well well well. Here I sit, doing paper work. It never seems to end. It’s the life of an executive ya know. The life of being the manager of the UWF Shareholders Branch. The life of which I have chosen to live. But there are some things going on I just don’t understand. And most of it comes from the Chaos Society…… First of all, you wanna question where your cut is from the action figures? That’s simple enough. You know all those tables and weapons that people are so fond of using. We have a nice contract where all of our equipment is furnished to us by the company that makes the action figures. So every time you use a lead pipe, or break a table, or use a chair, it’s all paid for by the sale of those action figures. As well as any other equipment we need. Seemed like a good deal to me when I signed it….. Also, let’s get some other things straight here, shall we. Now you two up and comers are getting paid $25,000 a piece. I, a veteran am getting paid $25,000. Now I think that someone else is getting jipped on salary the way you put it, but it’s not about the money to me. It’s about the fans. Your nice little SUV, yeah, so what’s your point? I’m sure you’ve made the money somewhere. I really could care less, but the way you talk, you make it sound like it is all about the money…… See, this business, it isn’t about the money, as you two seem to think. This business is about the fans. This business is about the people who pay for our tickets or for our licensed products. Without them, you two wouldn’t have a job, let’s be honest here……. And as far as your $25,000 dollar salary, you should be grateful you get that. There is an old leadership philosophy that says 80-20. It means that 20% of the people do 80% of the work. Well, as far as that other 20% of the work, you guys are part of the crew that does that. I mean, lets honestly think about who does what here. The fans come to see The Players Club. The fans come to see Joshua. They come to see Monty. They come to see me. They come to see the best in the business because they know when we are there; they are going to get a great show…… Are there others they come see? Yeah, there are, but fact of the matter is it’s not you. If anything you guys are costing us money. While I’m out reserving arenas for you to wrestle in, you’re out beating up camera men. While I’m making sure our finances are in order, you guys are off complaining about being underpaid. While I’m going through complaints, you guys are off causing more. Maybe you should think about that when you’re complaining about money….. I mean look at here, will you? (Kazee holds up a piece of paper for the camera to zoom in on. It’s got the seal of some random state on it.) You know what this is? Probably not because I just received it today and I’m sure they are trying to find you guys. This is a summons to court that I received saying that the UWF has to appear in court in order to answer on a civil suit for the charge of assault for that camera man you beat up. And it’s not like we can do much about it. It’s on tape for rods sake. It’s right there in broad beautiful color. You know, we ought to fine you guys for that incident. Our lawyers are trying to figure out a way out of this as I speak…… Oh, and you also want to complain about the $5 million dollars that Monty paid Plague. You might actually want to get the facts right on that one. See, that wasn’t’ a signing bonus, if you’d ever check the books as co-owners that you are. That was a pure payment out of Monty’s pocket into Plague’s pocket. There was no transfer of funds from any UWF account that I can see from the accounts that I have here. And all of my assistants have affirmed that, isn’t that right guys? (Kazee looks behind him at a cage. Inside the cage are a bunch of Guinea Pigs under a heat lamp. Inside there cage, on the floor, is a copy of all the books. They start squealing a little bit.) See, I told you guys so. You know, like I said, you guys are co-owners. Why don’t you guys look at the books? It’s all legit……. Now as far as these entire bumps on the head thing, well, you know, this is called professional wrestling. I don’t know if you haven’t noticed that yet. This isn’t some pansy little ballet thing like so many other federations are. This isn’t some mosh pit or controlled chaos. Well, ok, sometimes it’s a controlled chaos, but the name of the game is professional wrestling. Hell, in my years as an amateur wrestler I’ve had a lot of bumps on my head, and I’m use to taking bumps on the head…… I don’t know what it is about you guys. Anarchy seems to think that a person doesn’t take bumps on the head being a part of professional wrestling. The Luscious Lullabys seem to want to do some sort of co-ed naked wrestling. Will someone please explain to them that this is real honest for goodness wrestling where yes, people get hurt. Yes, people take bumps on the head…… As far as the Lullabys go, don’t worry, I’ve got your share of complaints too. Here is a whole stack of nothing but letters from fans all about this entire sexual theme that you guys have going on. (Kazee points to a stack of letters to his right which reaches at least a foot tall.) I honestly haven’t had a chance to go through them all yet. It takes time ya know to get through that much stuff. And I do have other paperwork to do. But from what I have read there are some parents who are really upset about it. And then there are some computer geeks who seem to like it. I think you have the whole range of emotions here Lullabys. But this isn’t the place for the kind of wrestling that you guys want to do. That kind of stuff is reserved for the bedroom. That kind of stuff has no place in the UWF ring……. As for all of my other opponents, I haven’t heard much from you. IC Title match seems to be pretty quiet from Raid and Moraless. There seems to be like some sort of a waiting period or something. I’m really not sure. The same thing seems to have happened to the rest of the tag teams in the battle royal. Well, who knows. I can’t worry too much about it. I’m just gonna keep on keepin’ on. I’ll study my tapes, I’ll go through my training, and it’ll be double time of course since I have twice the matches. Fact of the matter is though that when the dust settles, when everything is clear, I will still be standing with both of my titles. Yeah, I’ll have to go through a bunch of great athletes, and a couple of hot women, but it won’t matter……. The way the night ends, incase your curious, is this. Monty and I eliminate all the other teams in the tag team battle royal and walk out of there still Tag Team Champions. Right after that, I come back, climb the “ladder of success” and reclaim my IC title, making sure that both titles stay on there proper places outside of my door. And finally, Joshua Dunn pins Beelzebozo in some totally random location and wins that World Title. After that, I’ll have my two belts strapped around my waist, Dunn and Monty will each have their belts strapped around their waist, and we’ll have the Lustful Lullabys tied up on a leash to be led out of the arena. After all, to the victor go the spoils……. So with all that said, I only have one thing left to say. Good luck to everyone. You’re going to need it. The Players Club is on a role and we aren’t going to be stopped any time soon…… (Kazee puts on some ridiculously huge reading glasses and goes back to the paper work as the scene fades to black.
