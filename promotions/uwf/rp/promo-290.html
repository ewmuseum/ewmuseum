UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #290

Complain Is All They Do.

26 Mar 2004

Ezra Kazee — St. Judes

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up just outside a Hospital. The sign above the Hospital says St. Judes Children’s Hospital. Standing near the entrance to the hospital is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He has a couple of large boxes setting next to him, both labeled UWF Merchandise. He is wearing a black t-shirt with white lettering that says “IT’S THE SIMPLE THINGS THAT MATTER,” which can be yours for only $20 + S&H and is also available at the UWF merchandising tables. He is also wearing a jean jacket and a pair of brown slacks.) Kazee: Well, y’all caught me at a good time. I had a few extra moments to spare. Since I wasn’t booked this week, thought I would spend sometime doing what I love to do, and that’s making a difference in people’s lives. And what a better place to do it then right here at St. Judes. A hospital that is totally free for children who have cancer and is totally free for their families. It provides a place for their families to stay; it provides everything the child needs. And they continue to research and make things so much better…… If I can even brighten the smiles on one of these kids I’ll know I’ll have done my job. These kids in here, they’ve suffered enough. They’ve been through hell. And they will continue to go through hell until their cancer is in remission. That’s what St. Judes is all about. Finding a cure. Finding a way to fight back and making life better……. Of course, that’s not why you came, I’m sure. I’m sure that there was another reason you came to see me. I’m sure you want to know my thoughts, what I think about the whole lot of things that has happened lately, from my title victory, to the assault on Raid, to a whole lot of things……. Well, let me give it to you straight, because that’s the only way I know how. Am I disappointed on how the IC Title Tournament ended? A little. I was looking forward to my match with Moraless. I was looking forward to a hard fought match. I don’t know what happened to him that day, but I’m sure it was a good reason. Fact of the matter is though is that I’ve made it. I’ve finally done what I tried to do four years ago. I’ve finally done what I tried to do earlier this year and last year. I’ve finally made it to the next level. The Intercontinental Championship. Life can not get much better….. And not only that, not only do I have the IC title; I also am a co-holder of the Tag Team Title with Monty. When I was here before, four years ago, people would’ve said this was impossible. There would be no way I would achieve this status. Well, I have now, I have finally made it to that level, and I am prepared to be the hunted. Your right, I’m not the top dog, that honor is of course reserved for Beelzebozo, but I’m sure that won’t last for long as Dunn is right on his heels…… Like always though, there are those who complain. No matter what happens, they’ll complain about everything. And those that are complaining just don’t understand the concept of being that much better than everyone. They don’t understand the concept that there are those who rise above the rest. It’s like oil when mixed with water. It just naturally rises to the top. And like the Players Club has done, we will continue to rise to the top. I listen to Anarchy complain about this and that. They compare us to other people in other feds and other companies. But what they don’t seem to understand is that their comparisons are completely unfounded. I mean, obviously you have just as much of a share in the company as everyone else, but fact of the matter is that we are that good. This isn’t your precious WWA anymore. This isn’t your father’s federation. This I the UWF, this is the cream of the crop…… You talk about us using the feds resources. Well, you are right to an extent. Someone has to keep tabs on the resources in order to make sure the company stays afloat. And it’s not like you two are doing all that bad anyway. That’s a nice Navigator you’re riding in. Let’s be honest, if someone wasn’t keeping tabs on your resources you’d be driving around in an AMC Gremlin and not a Lincoln Navigator…… You talk about us bringing in people for our benefit? Well, seems like that is exactly what you two are. You two seem to be brought in for someone’s benefit. And that person would be Raid. Let’s be honest here. You two are nothing more than exact copies of what you claim to be against. You have had just as much benefit from the UWF as anyone else. I’m sorry y’all are pissed about not having title shots, but let’s look at your record for a second shall we. As a tag team, you two are 0-1 against Ruff Ryder and Macolm Kennedy, the Prime Alliance, who if memory serves me right, the Players Club annihilated. Maybe you should check your status before making your claims of absolute power. I’m sure the WWA would love to have you back. I hear that they are looking for someone to put the most dominant tag team in the WWA in their place, you know, The Lords of Fate……. But I’m not done yet. How about you Raid. Hope you’re not feeling to down from that huge knock you took at SAS. But as the saying goes, you play with fire, your going to get burned. Nice try about your claims. Fact of the matter is we won these titles fair and square. I didn’t walk into them. I didn’t get them hand delivered to me. I went in their and faced the biggest and baddest talent the UWF had to offer, and I won these titles fair and square. You, on the other hand, are still sitting in your little shop their where the closest thing you’ll ever come to gold is what is contained in that damned bug spray of yours. I think it’s time to back up. You’re not on my level. You’re not hardcore. You are nothing…… My titles were won fair and square. Through hard work and sacrifice. And you can’t take that away, no matter who you are……. As far as SAS goes, I don’t’ know if I’ll be there. I might come just to get a light work out against Bruce Gordy in that open challenge, but fact of the matter is I’m giving back to my Commonwealth and continuing on my victory tour. And if you don’t like that, that’s just too bad. (Kazee picks up the two boxes and walks into the hospital as the scene fades to black.)
