UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #826

Destruction of life.

Dec 4 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Reno gym.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“DESTRUCTION” “Tap out! Alex you better tap out before I snap your fucking neck!” The sound of a hand slapping against the mat never came. Malcolm Kennedy stood over Alex’s motionless body. He showed no emotion. No sympathy. Alex’s eyes were rolled back in his head. His face covered in blood. His body in sweat. It had been nearly five minutes and Alex had yet to regain consciousness. A trainer sat by his side. He was extra careful not to move his body. He just kept talking to him, hoping for the best. But he was fading fast. The EMT’s were on the way. But Alex was gonna die. One way or the other. *Two hours prior to the incident.* Malcolm sat in the back of a lime. He sipped from a glass of whiskey as he watched the lights of Reno flash above him. He returned home today. He returned to “The biggest little city in the world”. He let out a brief grin. He flipped open his cell phone and quickly dialed a number. Malcolm: “Hey Gino! Its Malcolm. How ya been?” “Listen I gotta run by the casino and sign a few papers, but I was wondering if you could get me a little ring time?” “Thats great. How old is he?” “24? And his name is Alex Samson?” “Alright, sounds good buddy. See you in a while.” *1 Hour prior to the incident.* Malcolm closed his locker and sat down on the bench. He began lacing up his boots when a cocky laugh came from behind him. “Well, well, well. If it isnt Mr. Top Notch himself. You don’t look so tough. That tv really fools people.” Malcolm: “You must be the newbie. Alex is it?” Alex: “How did you guess that?” Malcolm: “I can smell the fear on you. The fear all the rookies have. Let me give you some advice........” Alex: NO! You listen to me. This is my time to make it big, and I’ll be damned if im gonna lose that chance to some two bit has been that blames his poor wrestling skills on his brothers death.” Malcolm froze. He fought the urge to maul Alex and quickly replied. Malcolm: “Don’t dig a hole that you cant get out of. You already gotta step to me in the ring, don’t make me angry.” Alex: “Let me ask you something Malcolm. What would your excuse for sucking be if your brother was still alive?” Malcolm quickly tightened the final knot in his boot and spun to face Alex. He grabbed him by the shirt and pinned him against the wall. Malcolm: “Ill see you in the ring.” Malcolm stormed out of the room. *The Incident.* Malcolm stared down the rookies. His nostrils flared. His heart raced. He wanted to beat him to a bloody pulp. Gino: “Okay boys. You know how it works. Start and stop on the bells. Listen to my orders. And Alex, if you need to quit at any time, just.......oof!” Alex shoved him to the ground. Alex: “Fuck that Gino. I don’t need your help old timer.” Malcolm helped Gino to his feet. Malcolm: “Fuck the rules. Fuck your bells. Im gonna make him feel my pain.” The two squared off in the middle of the ring as Gino climbed out. For a good 30 seconds that sat staring at each other, sizing the other up. Alex: “This one is for your brother.” Alex launched a closed right fist at Malcolm’s face. Malcolm caught the punch without a blink and with a quick downward lunge, head butted Alex on the bridge of his nose. Alex hit the ground hard, dazed, bloody, and with a broken nose. Before the pain could even sink in, Malcolm picked him up and whipped him into the corner with all his strength. Alex struck the corner chest first. He bounced out and stumbled backwards into Malcolm in the middle of the ring. Malcolm pulled his head back and set him up for a revers ddt, only to drop him down, neck first onto Malcolm’s knee. He quickly rolled Alex over and locked him tightly in an STF. Alex squirmed and screamed in pain but refused to tap out. Too proud to admit defeat. Gino: “TAP OUT FOR CHRIST SAKE!” Alex refused. He continued his feeble attempt to squirm free and break the hold. Malcolm: “Tap out! Alex you better tap out before I break your fucking neck!” Malcolm pulled back harder. So hard that Alex could barely squirm. All he could do was scream. With all his remaining energy, Alex tried once more to break free. At the same time, Malcolm used all his remaining strength to pull back on the STF. Alex let out one last gasping scream and fell limp. Malcolm immediately released the hold upon hearing the sickening pop from his neck. He stood up and spit on the back of Alex as the trainer came to his side. Malcolm: “Lesson one. Check your ego at the door.” *45 minutes after the incident.* Malcolm sat alone in a wooden chair. He was fully dressed and ready to go. His bags packed, and by his feet. He stared deep into the camera without blinking. Malcolm: “Lets get this clear right now. I don’t care if your old, young, black, white, tall, short, fat, or skinny! If you get in the ring with me, I will make you suffer.” “This Sunday I can once again reclaim the TV title. If that is not enough, its in a Tables, Ladders, and Cats match. Almost an hour ago I probably ended a mans life with my bare hands, well this Sunday they decided to give me weapons in the match. So be it.” “I’ve been telling all of you this week that I was doing this all in the name of my brother. You all made the same mistake as young Alex did. You’ve all taken me lightly. Well this Sunday, you will regret ever blowing off the idea of not preparing to face me. You all will, in the same way, shape, or form, feel the pain I’ve felt the last two years of my life. When the night is done, you will fear the man I’ve become as well as the man I have become this for. This is all for Eric........Allen.........Kennedy.” With that, Malcolm grabs his bags and walks out of the room. FADE TO BLACK.
