UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #773

Pain has Arrived for Persia

6 Nov 2004

Killer Dude — Killer Dude's Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens as the camera pans in on Killer Dude. Killer Dude can be seen sitting at a desk with a brand new computer on top of it. He seems to be downloading music on to his computer. He occassionally looks at a few written pieces of paper on the desk, probably to make sure he was downloading the right things. The camera man sneaks ever closer to Killer Dude, however, Killer Dude sees him out the corner of his eye, and starts talking to him, without looking at him) Killer Dude: What do you want!? Can't you see that I'm busy!? (The camera man looks at the back of Killer Dude's head and speaks) UWF CREW: The UWF sent me to get your thoughts on your opponent at Supremacy, Nick Persia, for your North American Title. Killer Dude: You know what, it fuckin' figures that that loudmouth thinks he can try to punk me out with his words. Saying that he will end my career? Saying that he will rid the UWF of me? Well, guess what, you little fucker. Killer Dude is here to stay, and there is no chance that you will ever end my career, or anything close to it. I don't understand why you think you're so good. You can't even talk right, what makes you think you can beat me. Well, guess what, believe what you want to believe, live in your own little fantasy world, because come this Sunday, you have no fuckin' chance. UWF CREW: Why is it that you have set your sights on Nick Persia? Killer Dude: Well, that's a good start. You could have a good career as an interviewer in this business, kid. I'll give you a reason. Nick Persia is an ignorant fool, who rants and raves, and talks about nothing in particular, and he needs to be taught a lesson in respect. He has a very big mouth, and he actually thinks that he can beat anybody. He thinks that beating me will be a stepping stone, for him to acheive greater things. Well, let me tell you something Persia. Every time the UWF has put an obstacle in front of me, I have overcome it. Every time the UWF tried to keep me down, I knocked out the competition, and I made an everlasting impression on the management. The only impression that you are making to me, is that you are an ignorant asshole! (Killer Dude pushes himself away from the computer desk and stands up, walking over to a file cabinet in the other corner of the room. He pulls out a folder, and begins to read the contents) Killer Dude: Let me point out a few things to Nick Persia, that he might not know about right now. He claims to be the most extreme wrestler this federation has ever known. News flash, you are no where close. I am the Hardcore Legend, damnit. It seems to me that his goal right now is to take me out. He wants to take the Hardcore Legend out!? He must be a delusional freak! Oh wait, he is! What he is trying to acheive is a very lofty and impressive goal, however it will not come to pass. As of right now, Persia, you haven't earned a god damn ounce of respect from me. You have done nothing to deserve it, and I will make sure that on Sunday, you will never even have the shot to beat me. The only thing that you are acheiving right now, is giving me a god damn migraine, and I've taken a lot of aspirin over the past few weeks just listening to you talk. (The camera man follows Killer Dude as he paces around the room) Killer Dude: Making sure I feel pain in more ways than anyone has ever made me hurt before? Nick Persia, I think that you underestimate me more than you should. You sound like a 10 year old child, playing with tough words just so you can sound big. Let me let you in on a little secret Persia. It's not that easy to make a man feel pain, when he has no regard for pain, and cares very little about anyone else's safety. Now as for you, I guarantee that you will feel the pain Persia, but I want you to do something for me. You see, Persia, I like the feeling of my own blood dripping down my face. Makes me feel alive, and it makes me feel like I'm doing a good job. So, Persia, make me bleed, if you dare, cause when you do, you will see what will happen. I hope that you are now beginning to understand the consequences of your words and your actions. The mind is a dangerous weapon Persia, and as I have come to find out, yours is very easy to manipulate and to play with! (Killer Dude steps forward towards the camera man, and begins to smirk as he speaks again) Killer Dude: You blink one time, Persia, and I will have already snapped your neck right in two, and I'll be laughing as I watch you suffer in agony and despair. Blink one time, and I'll already have beaten you down and watch as you get back to your feet, only to get beat down again. Blink one time, and there will be a new pain, only in a different place. Blink one time, and your nightmare will have begun all over again. Blink one time, and you will realize that you are receiving the most brutal beating that you have ever received in your lifetime. (Killer Dude turns and walks back to his desk, sitting back down in the chair. He rubs his chin, and then turns back towards the camera.) Killer Dude: Better yet, Persia, close your eyes and pray real hard. Because whatever god that might be up in the heavens looking down upon you right now, had better make sure that he can save your fuckin' ass. By the time your prayer is answered, it's going to be too late to take the tube out of you. And remember this, while you are sucking down milk shakes through straws and recuperating from the injuries I inflict upon you, I hope that you will realize that you were only fucking with the wrong person at the wrong time. (Killer Dude smirks and then smiles sadistically) Killer Dude: Nick Persia, you have received my full attention, and as you will find out, that was not a smart move on your part at all. Get ready to feel the pain of everlasting brutality from the one man that you think you've already defeated. Killer Dude: Nick Persia, you have been officially put on notice. Welcome to my world, my playground, and when you leave you will never be the same again. You will have the shakes at night, and you will breathe labored breaths. You will always look over your shoulder, looking for the psycho that is going to beat you within an inch of your life, and then do it again, only because he can! Welcome to my life. You will know what real pain feels like when I am through with you, Persia! (Killer Dude turns around and continues his work on the computer as the camera fades to black. Red words begin to form in the darkness) SEE YOU IN HELL BITCH!
