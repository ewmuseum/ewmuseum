UWF (2001–07) Backstage Area — Incarders #9

Kenny Returns

5 Feb 2004

Kenny Mercury — WNU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[“Purple Haze” by Jimi Hendrix hits the sound system.] Larson: Oh dear Rod no. Purple haze all in my brain… Lately things… just don’t seem the same… Actin’ funny, but I don’t know why… ’scuse me while I kiss the sky… [The lights dim, dry ice smog pours out of from backstage and rolls down the entrance ramp clouding up much of the platform surrounding the Universal-tron and the rapid flashes of a purple strobe light fill the arena.] Rick: Purple Haze? What the hell?! Larson: Oh my Rod… don’t tell me… Rick: P-Purple Haze? Larson: Jesus H. that’s a lot of smoke… [The smoke continues to pour out from backstage, now consuming the ring and the commentators table, filling up much of the floor level of the arena.] Larson: **COUGH**COUGH** GAH! I can’t see anything! Rick: Or breathe for that matter! [The crowd begins to cough and choke, finally, UWF security come out with leaf blowers and corral the smoke up the entrance ramp.] Larson: Roddamnit! What the hell was that all about!? This federation is so screwed up… Rick: Look! In the ring! [In the center of the ring a cloaked figure is kneeling. It wears a purple cape wrapped around its body, with a masked head, peering out at the audience.] Figure: Through countless bloodstained battles… I have fought… and I have bested my foes in every way… No one could stand against me… no foe had the power… so I was cheated… cheated from my victory… cheated by those who envy me… those who hate me… cheated by those who failed were I excelled… I WAS CHEATED! Rick: What the hell is this all about? Larson: He was cheated for Chrissake!! Don’t you even care!?!? Rick: Cheated? What the hell? Figure: I am here… returning to this hell-hole… to claim what is rightfully mine… my birthright… my destiny… I am here… to claim my place, to ascend to the throne of the Universal Wrestling Federation… To win what is rightfully mine! THE UWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!! I am the technical wizard… the shooting star… the very definition of precision… look it up if you don’t believe me… I AM… [The figure leaps to its feet, throwing out its arms out in the air. The cape flies off, and flings into the corner of the ring. One hand grabs the purple mask by the eye-holes and tears it off. Golden locks pour out as the mask is removed. A confident smirk cracks across his face. He tosses the mask backwards into the audience.] Larson: Oh man! Rick: IT’S KENNY MERCURY!!! Kenny Mercury: You Rod-damn right, its Kenny friggin’ Mercury!! Yes, and as Jehovah and each and every one of these slugs in attendance tonight… The king… has returned. And the Sultan of Sleepers, the Kaiser of Crossface, and the Baron of Butt-whipping has a Rod damn decree that he wants to… um… decree. SO LISTEN UP!! Rick: Just as humble as always… Kenny Mercury: I SAID LISTEN UP!! Rod damn, you people disgust me! No friggin’ manners what-so-ever… NOW! Feast your eyes on the greatest technical wrestler of all time… ME! Rick: As I was saying… Kenny Mercury: Take a good look, folks, because you’re not going to be seeing me like this for long… Because there’s something missing from this picture, isn’t there? Could it be… the beautiful long flowing hair? No, no… that’s still there. Could it be… the perfectly toned body? No, no… that’s there too. How about the greatest wrestling prodigy in the history of the UWF? I’M RIGHT HERE, MAN… Well then… what is missing? Rick: My gun, so I can put myself out of my misery? Kenny Mercury: No, you nincompoop! What’s missing is my Championship title belt!! I know, you know it, hell even Corky Spectre knows it… No offense, Corks… [Out of ring UWF referee Corky Spectre nods in acceptance.] Kenny Mercury: Everybody in the whole world knows that I’m the greatest thing that ever happened to UWF: The greatest in-ring talent, the greatest mic-worker, the greatest looking guy, and the greatest nick-name-maker-upper. But there’s always been something missing, and it wasn’t my Rod damn fault. What’s been missing have been the Championship titles. Yeah, so, I’ve held the TV title one… two… eleventy-billion times, and I even held the THC twice, and hell, I don’t even like THC, I despise the stuff… keeps kids out of school and makes a friggin’ schitzo out of you… in fact, I tried everything in my power to eliminate the division, and after deafeating its best… TWICE, they still keep the garbage around to this very day… Rod damn them… But, what I’m getting at here, cretins… or was it… croutons? What I’m getting at, is that EVERY SINGLE FRIGGIN’ TIME I WAS ABOUT TO ASCEND TO THE TOP OF THE FEDERATION, I GOT SCREWED!!! Larson: Now, not even you can argue that one, Rick… Kenny’s had his share of screw jobs. Rick: And he’s given just as many… Kenny Mercury: Every time I had a chance, someone interfered, whether it was a Rod Damn bunny or some other Ass-Mongol, it doesn’t make a difference, the point is, I was screwed over. The UWF… heh… the UWF owes me the World Title. But, I’ll get to that eventually. We all know I could return tonight, fight Beelzebub Bozo Buttocks whatever on Sunday and be inducted into the Hall of Fame before you could say “Jamie Shock’s severed left testicle” but that wouldn’t be any fun, now would it? No, oh no, no, no, no… NO! Destiny… heh, destiny and I have a little understanding. It’s our understanding that I am to completely rape and annihilate the entire roster of the UWF, winning the Intercontinental Championship first, then onto the World, I want to savor every single moment of it, you get me? I deserve this. This is my time, and NO ONE, and I mean… no… one… is going to stop me. I’m through with playing around, and I’m through with counting on other people. I’ve heard, through the proverbial “grapevine” if you will, that Ben Jack-illac has returned to the UWF. Well isn’t that just fine and dandy. Jericho-mo… the biggest jackillac in the history of the GI-Whatchamacallit-F… has returned. And I am supposed to be impressed? Well… I’m not… Do you want to know what I am? Huh? Do you? Larson: Oh, do tell… do tell… Kenny Mercury: I’ll tell you… I’M FRIGGIN’ ENRAGED, I’M FURIOUS! JERICHO, YOU ASS-HATTED BACK-STABBING CAULK-FOR-BRAINS POOP-STAIN… That’s right, caulk… not ‘cock’, but ‘caulk’. You see, at first your brain was just mush but then after being hit in the head a few thousand times with a steel chair with barbed wire in your primitive THC matches, it hardened and became even more useless than before, the only good part is that sometimes the gooey inside oozes out your ears like your disgustingly enormous ego. Oh, look, at me, I’m Extreme, and I’m all…better than you… blah blah blah… It’s a wonder that more modest wrestlers, such as myself, don’t get the hype that you do. Last time around, I made a mistake; I counted on you to be there for me. Instead, it was me counting on you to be there, but you, off in the corner playing with yourself in the corner, I know it’s disgusting so please forgive me but I must reveal the truth of Benji and I’s failed relationship no matter how vulgar it gets. But basically, Ben, you only cared about yourself, and while you clipped your nose hairs in the mirror, I was being beaten from behind by some punk jobber named X-Treme Donald or “The Shaman Man” Lester Commie or whatever, and let’s not forget those friggin’ bunnies… I gave, but I did not receive, and for that, I hope something… really…really…really…really… bad happens to you… or something. The worse thing I can think of is… Well, facing me in the ring! Wow, I can’t believe I thought of that, so, “Benji-whenji pudding in a pie, kissed the girls and made them cry, when the boys came out to play, Benji-whenji ran away…” What do you day? How about it? You ready to be just the first, or like one of the first… it depends on those dingus bookers… Do you want to maybe be the first perhaps… oh screw it… DO YOU WANT TO GO ONE ON ONE WITH THE TECHINCAL WIZARD? THE SHOOTING STAR? THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION? Kenny… Mercury…? Well? Do you? Goodnight, UWF… and remember, I’m back, and the Age of Mercury… has now begun. Now hit my REAL music!!! [“Stranglehold” by Ted Nugent plays as Kenny Mercury strolls back up the entrance ramp to the backstage area.] Here I come again now baby … like a dog in heat … tell it's me by the clamor now baby … I like to tear up the street, and I been smokin’ for so long, ya know I’m here to stay … got you in a stranglehold baby … you best get outta the way … Road I cruise is a bitch now baby … but no you cant turn me round … and if a house gets in my way baby … ya know I’m tearing it down … you ran the night that you left me … you put me in my place … I got you in a stranglehold baby … I’m gonna crush your face! Larson: MERCURY’S BACK! THERE IS HOPE FOR US ALL YET! Rick: And from the sound of things, he has not changed one bit.. Larson: Why would we ever want KENNY MERCURY~! to change? Rick: I can only think of eleventy billion reasons. But, forget that for now! IT’S TIME FOR OUR MAIN EVENT!
