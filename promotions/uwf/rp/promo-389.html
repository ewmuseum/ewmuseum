The Party Line

The Communist

From your Neighbor? From your Country? Then Join the American Communist Party :The words Amercian Communist Party slide onto the screen: :The screen shifts to show several shots of the Senate and the House arguing among eachother: narrator: Demorcacy is slow and self serving, they care nothing for YOU :the Word 'YOU' shows up in Bold letters: the common working class citizen. You labors go un noticed by the Higher ups and the well to do. :Screen shifts to show an obese man counting money on a table covered in american propaganda: Narrator: Greedy Men are using your hard work and labors to grow fat and wealthy. But it's Your Work :the words 'Your Work' com up to the screen: Shouldn't it be Your Money? :the words 'Your Money' come up: Thats right YOUR MONEY! :the words strobe for a second: :Screen shifts to a man working in a factory, sweat dripping from him as he works tirelessly: narrator: Do you work tirelessly for your family, barely scraping by? Then you are a member of the Proletariot, the hard working, underappreciated backbone of out world. We Represent YOU :again the word YOU comes up on the screen: YOU the Hard working man. You :screen shifts to the man going home to his family: The Bread winner. You :screen shifts to show the man looking up at the Soviet flag: the proletariot. :the Screen once again shows the Communist, the proletariot Champion, pointing at the Camera: Narrator: We want YOU to be a Communist. paid for by the Central Committee, Communist Party
