Hot Mask — Rocky Mountains

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Gentleman...look around you at the gifts of God, the clear sky, the pure air, the tender grass, the birds; nature is beautiful and sinless, and we, only we, are godless and foolish, and we don't understand that life is a paradise, for we have only to understand that and it will at once be fulfilled in all its beauty, we shall embrace each other and weep.' - Fyodor Dostoevsky Hot Mask: 'Remarkable. Simply...[sighs] remarkable.' [Camera: ON.] [Throughout our lives, in everyday surroundings via picture books, photo albums, delicate wall framings, and Hallmark calendars, we sometimes intake settings so serene that distract from whatever immense burdens sit on our minds like circus elephants. You catch yourself imagining that for one moment you are within the portrait, frolicking in a meadow painted in luxurious greens and blues as you pretend you've met your soulmate in some fate-twisted stroke of wonder, rolling over one another down a grassy tuffet resulting in the perfect climax of laughter and embrace. Or, staring out at a gray sea with a sanguine moon overhead as the tide rolls onto bare feet with an eerie warmth, pondering just how long it would take if you dove in and began to crawl to an opposing shore. Collecting shells for no real purpose, as your plastic bucket fills further and further to the top with three-by-three memories, pressing your ear to each one of them to make sure that you can hear the remnants of the ocean on the journey inland. Perhaps you are taken in with the muggy yet somewhat settling tranquility of a desert sunset over a canyon, as you had only planned on coming out of your sedan for a quick stretch of your legs, but find yourself soaked into cacti structures and tumbleweeds and finding beauty in the most desolate of places. You want to kick yourself for not bringing your camping gear along, but the lukewarm temperature at nightfall coaxes you to sleep out under the constellations and drift into slumber content with a sandy mattress and a rock for a pillow.] [The lens metamorphosises into focus, and much like a sponge, our eyes soak up the crispness that surrounds. A primitive roadside rest area with only a few picnic tables constructed concrete into the rocky terrain below our feet, but these are just mere distractions for the allure that only a four foot steel guard rail and open air confines. Birds squawk overhead as they seem nonchalant that their domain is shared with rivaling humans, and the camera catches a glimpse of one shooting at high pace into the range beyond. September in the Rockies, while there is little trace of white covering the infinite structures beyond, is still quite a picturesque sight to behold. One of many constant reminders in the world that whatever force is behind nature is indeed a caring one, bestowing upon us gifts that even the most shallow of souls can appreciate.] [Focus: our subject, relative unknown in the ranks of Primetime Central, at a side view sitting across on an outer stone bench, with a look not usually associated with his persona...one of thoughtfulness. Not a scowl, not a frown, but a reserved mouth expression. We take a look at his apparel, as he is dressed in a wool-lined denim jacket, a pair of relaxed fit Levis 505's, and his cover that masquerades his entire face save for mouth and eyes. His left arm remains relaxed on the picnic table, occasionally pulling his hand to his mouth to puff on a cigarette and bring it back to rest again. We also notice a white piece of paper in his right hand that lies on top of his knee, but as to what it is, we cannot decipher.] [Ladies and gentlemen, this is Hot Mask. And if you knew anything about this pompous little jerk, you'd be shocked as to how almost one minute has gone by and he hasn't made a reference to his natural scythe yet.] Hot Mask: 'Penis.' [I asked for it, didn't I?] Hot Mask: 'The driving force behind the apparatus known as my brain. I know you all think it. I know you all have some kind of inclination that I'm about as shallow as a piss-dilated Holiday Inn kiddie pool, but you're wrong. It's not the only thing I'm concerned with.' [Could this be the breakthrough that many are searching for? Could it be that the answer is indeed in nature's own little miracles...the one cure that can tame the savage beast that lies within the blue jeans of one Hot Mask? Do trees, shrubs, and mountainous structures hold the key to leave even the most arrogant of pricks in a state of...awe?] Hot Mask: 'Take a look at what you see here. [motions with cigarette in hand] God's creation. I don't know if it's the air, or what...but it's shit like this that makes you want to inhale it all in and never breathe again. I couldn't think of a more suitable death...save for Jennifer Lopez sitting on my face and forgetting I'm there, but some things are just wishful thinking.' [Mask draws his hand forward, taking one last puff of his filtered Camel Light, and carefully extinguishes it into the granite behind him, leaving the butt on the bench (no pun intended) to trash upon his departure.] Hot Mask: 'Not at all content right now, however. I hope I'm able to afford to come out here later on this year, when there's actually a little snow on the caps. To those of you watching who have been able to witness such grandeur, you know exactly what I'm talking about...and excuse me for being so blunt when I say this... '...but man, the peaks in winter look like they've been covered with God's own precious semen...like he blew his Holy Load at the sight of such immense beauty!' [Suddenly, the pace changes, as we see a whimsical smirk cross the face of Mask. This smirk is quite familiar to fans and employees of Neon Wrestling, and we can almost predict what will happen as a result...] Hot Mask: 'Just kidding. I actually psyche myself up once a year to provide Colorado with their local tourist per capita. Remember the early powder last year? That was somewhat of a blunder on my part...I was aiming for Anna-Nicole Smith's mouth and accidentally spewed the love liquor all over the Rockies. Hard to swallow...heh, literally...but it's the truth. Swear to ME, it is. 'Although I usually have better aim, but the gabby bitch caught wind of a nearby In and Out and fatblasted her big ass outta there before I could finish the job.' [A cackle from Mask.] Hot Mask: 'Meh, you win some and you lose some. I'm just glad I got outta that ordeal without any permanent teeth marks on the Giggle Stick! But, I must admit, there's something erotic during good fellatio if you're in anticipation as to whether or not the calico queen's not gonna mistake it for a six foot Hoagie and take a bite! Hell, from where I was in the bedroom, she was within reach of the refrigerator in the kitchen and could've easily slathered some mustard on the purple-headed yogurt slinger.' [Hot Mask stops for a moment, shuddering at the thought of the former Playmate making a meal out of his pride and joy. He begins to rub his crotch lovingly with his left hand, and reassures its safety.] Hot Mask: [looking downward] 'Don't worry, pal. I wouldn't let that happen to ya!' [A nice pat of the genitals leads to Mask's well-documented case of attention deficit disorder, as he notices the piece of paper that he holds in his right hand. The Pelvic Lord slowly begins to unfold it, as our cameraman stands and makes his way around the table behind the shoulders of the still-seated wrestler.] Hot Mask: 'Neon Wrestling has come to a close. You think I'd hold a bit of nostalgic value, considering I mopped up those no-talent hacks like a cum janitor in a porn flick...but, I ate a lot of Doritos last night and pooped out my memories. It's a clean slate for the Dongersaurus, folks. And besides...I already pawned off that rattan piece of tin No Holds-Barred belt for this awesome slammer!' [Mask takes a reach into his pocket, pulling out a shiny golden object...a thick metal slammer (circa 1993) with the adorning mug of one OJ Simpson on the front.] Hot Mask: 'This finally rounds out my collection of OJ pogs! Definitely worth the ten bucks I paid for it.' [Placing it back into his pocket with the hand that the paper is in, he gives a sigh as he then brings it close and unfolds it. The camera moves in to get a better shot at the document, and remarkably, we can read the writing. A UWF header crafted in WordArt sits atop it, and the rest of the letter reads:] Mr. Hot Mask: You fight El Baiano in your debut match on Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. Thanks to this convenient letter, you won't really have to make light of it in an interview. Oh, by the way, LYLE WILE IS EL BAIANO'S MANAGER! HOW COOL IS THAT? SERIOUSLY THAT'S AWESOME RIGHT? Sincerely, UWF Guys [The ink signature is also signed 'UWF Guys' in cursive writing. Mask folds the piece of paper back up, stands to his feet and gives us another nice shot of the mountain range to add to the 'serious' effect.] Hot Mask: 'Sadly, I couldn't do any better than accepting a multi-zillion dollar contract to come to liven up the sewer known as the UWF. Where 'zany' things happen, like heads coming off! Give me a break. I was the sole reason for the decapitation of a ham-n-egger, as well as the only wrestling wonder responsible for the firings of TWO employees! I'm AMAZING! 'The UWF doesn't have a Trojan strong enough to stop this load from bursting. And let me tell you...it's pretty easy to control this bad boy down here, but there's only so much endurance a mere Lord and Saviour like me can exert. I will bust. 'El Baiano might just be the lucky one to be covered in my...debuting glory. Consider yourself lucky, greenhorn. Sperm banks would club baby seals for the chance to be my cum dumpster.' [Mask casually allows the paper to fall to the ground. He steps on it gingerly with his worn-out hi-tops and smushes it into the soil before taking a deep breath, stretching his arms out for emphasis.] Hot Mask: 'But until then, let me enjoy a bit of rest and relaxation. The oxygen here is as pure as a nun's vertical smile! It's sad to live in a world where we neglect stuff like this...where people just leave their trash lying around all over the place, and methodically destroy our beautiful Mother Earth. Makes me sicker than looking at that Tubgirl photograph, if you ask me. I just wish that...' [HM is cut off in mid-sentence with an annoying stopwatch alarm. Startled, he takes a gander at his timepiece, and with a look of concern, pops the collar of his jacket and hunches it straight on his shoulders.] Hot Mask: 'Whoa! Baywatch comes on in 30 minutes...I gotta jet.' [Jogging off, the camera focuses in on the litter that Mask has left behind in the scenic rest area of the highway. With a shift, we see him climb inside a ragged lime green '77 Ford Pinto and crank the engine. Black exhaust begins to spew from the pipe as he revs the nearly dead car to full blast. And, as a final farewell, Mask squeals the tires and zooms off in the distance, leaving nothing but an empty parking lot behind...] Cameraman: SHIT~! How the hell am I supposed to get home? [Fade.]
