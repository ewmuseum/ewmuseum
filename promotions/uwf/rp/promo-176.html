UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #176

Now *THAT'S* a card

19 Jan 2003

Mike Montgomery — The Office of the UWF Executive Vice President of Talent Relations.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As always, because let's face it it's good to see that at least SOME of the Administration is doing it's job, we find the Executive Vice-President of Talent Relations, Mike Montgomery, in his office, looking over contracts as well as the card for the upcoming Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. But in a surprising change, this set-up will have a tiny bit of actual originality. You see, sitting on Mike's desk, just to his left, is none other than his good (and until recently comatose) buddy Spiff the Magic Monkey. 'Dude, I love what you've done with the place,' Spiff comments, 'but I gotta know. Malcolm Kennedy in the Player's Club? After what he did?' Mike turns toward his trusted friend and business partner, regarding him a moment before answering, 'Well, you gotta admit the kid's got balls. But really, it's a matter of the old axiom 'keep your friends close and your enemies closer.' Besides, it makes for good TV.' 'Mike, the guy beat me with a chair! I still can't believe PETA didn't sue!' 'Trust me, they did. We just quickly settled out of court, then wrote the whole thing off as a business expense.' 'Nice.' 'So anyway, in professional wrestling, good TV is about keeping the people guessing. Right?' 'Yeah.' 'Well, Malcolm takes you out with a chair. That shocks the people. He screws over Ruff Ryder to join the Player's Club. That shocks the people even more. Now we've got the people guessing. What will happen when Spiff comes back? Is Malcolm a dead man? Will all be forgiven? After yesterday, obviously not.' Spiff nods, 'Obviously.' 'So now that that question's been answered, the audience moves on to new questions. What will happen now that Malcolm has joined the Prime Alliance? Will Ryder forgive Malcolm's past transgressions or forgive and forget? When Ryder looks across the ring and sees Malcolm Kennedy as his partner, is he going to remember what happened the last time he tagged with the guy and ask 'what if'? And what about Jamie? When the time comes does he side with his friend or his protege?' 'So basically you want me to believe that you predicted everything that's happened since I was attacked and then planned for it?' Mike shakes his head, 'Hell no. Just this, 'I love it when a plan comes together'.' 'Well, you've got three guys now who couldn't possibly trust each other for different reasons. If nothing else, it's got merit.' Mike begins rummaging through the papers on his desk, finally pulling out a sheet with the official UWF letterhead on it. 'Let's take a look at next Sunday's card, shall we? Let's see...Iron Lung vs. Axel Action? Who the hell re-hired Action? I'd like to see what Iron Lung can do in this match. He gives me a good show, I may have to put an extra zero on his paycheck.' 'Let me guess,' Spiff interjects, 'you want him in the Player's Club.' 'You blame me?' 'Well, frankly no. Man's got skill.' 'So let's see...Moraless vs. Ben Jericho. I like Moraless, this is a guy who could go far if he stayed healthy. But I like this main event. Ryder vs. Ezee.' 'That's not the main event.' 'What?' 'Keep reading.' 'Oh, it's Dunn vs. Kennedy? That's an odd choice.' 'Dude...' Spiff points to the bottom of the sheet.' 'WHAT?! You know, I thought the owners had brains, then they go and do something like this!' Spiff looks at his compatriot, the confusion plain on his face, 'Dude, you're facing a former world title holder. A man who's scared of monkies. Besides, you gotta admit, it HAD to happen!' Mike leans back his chair, regarding Spiff a moment before replying, 'Yeah, but Montgomery vs. Shock. That's a Pay Per View match...it should be on a main event marquis at Madison Square Gardens or something. Where's it at?' The two scan over the top of a page before Mike says 'Charolotte, North Carolina. Ric Flair country. I like it.' 'The Main Event needs a hot crowd.' 'Hell yeah.' 'Gonna be a real barn burner.' 'Oh hell yeah!' 'Match might not even take place.' 'OH HE...what?' Spiff points down toward the bottom of the sheet, 'Best two out of three. When Ezra and Dunn both win their matches, that means we take it two to nothing. There'd be no point in having your match.' 'We're having the match.' 'Dude, there'd be no point.' 'We're having the match.' 'Mike. You won't need the match.' 'Spiff, we're...having...the...match!' 'Fine, fine.' Mike leans back in his chair, head tilted backward, eyes closed, 'can you imagine it? Complete and utter domination of the Prime Alliance? Their fearless leader battered and broken? The Prime Alliance no more, and the Player's Club having cemented it's place in the UWF? Finally, Jamie will realize his place in the UWF is beneath me, his career ground into nothing beneath my boot!' 'Mike, I swear if you start cackling I'm outta here.' 'Hey, a man can dream. Either way though, so long as I have something to say about it, Jamie *will* feel what it's like to fall...from...grace...'
