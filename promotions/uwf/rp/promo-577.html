UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #577

First match

26 Jul 2004

Jonny Roxx — Stan's Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
7:31 AM – Jonny Roxx’s Apartment [Soaking wet, Jonny steps out of his shower and wraps a towel around his waist. He takes another towel and dries his hair and pushes it back. He checks himself in the mirror to monitor his cuts and bruises from training. He has a very red chest with a few handprints here and there from the many chops he has received for every time he screws up. He has bruises on his arms, legs, and back from takes falls and receiving shots from sparring partners.] Jonny: What the hell did Donnie get me in to? I’m not cut out for this business. [Jonny shakes his head and then continues to get ready. He dries off the rest of his body and puts on a pair of black sweat pants, tennis shoes, and a red tank top. He grabs his gym bag and then heads out of the door.] 7:53 AM – Stan’s Gym [Jonny pulls into Stan’s parking lot and parks his car. As he steps out he can already hear the squealing and backfiring of Donnie Rock’s crappy car. Donnie coasts in and parks next to Jonny’s space. He gets out of his car and then stares at it in admiration.] Donnie: Still the best on the road. Jonny: Dude, get over it. She’s dead. Donnie: Are you mad? She’ll outlive me by at least ten years! Jonny: How many miles have you driven this thing? Donnie: I lost count… Jonny: Well just look at the odometer. Donnie: It’s…uh….broken. Jonny: How about this – when I get the band back on track, I’ll put in a completely new engine and restore this piece. [Donnie advances towards his car and hugs the hood.] Donnie: Did you hear that, baby? You’re gonna make it. Jonny: Donnie, you need serious help, bro. You’re starting to weird me out. Donnie: Don’t worry. Now come on, Stan’s gonna be pissed. [They enter the building and Stan immediately confronts them.] Stan: Jonny! I didn’t think you would come back after yesterday’s workout. Jonny: It was hard getting out of bed, I can tell ya that. Stan: Today, we’re going to work on some stuff that I’m not too big on, but your manager here thinks it’s important. Jonny: And just what is that? Stan: We’re going to work on pleasing the crowd. Jonny: I already know how to do that. Donnie: On stage you do, but the ring is another story altogether. We’re going to work on your high-risk moves and your mic skills. Jonny: So let me guess – today instead of being slammed to the mat by sparring partners, I get to throw myself to the mat from a higher altitude. Stan: Exactly! Let us begin. [Stan moves to the main ring area and slides into the ring. Donnie attempts to follow, but Jonny stops him.] Donnie: What is it, Jonny? Jonny: I just wanted you to know something. You’re getting me to do things I would never have agreed to do before and…. Donnie: You don’t have to thank me, dude. Jonny: I hate you. Donnie: Thanks for the gratitude. Jonny: No problem. [Jonny slides under the bottom rope into the ring while Donnie waits on the outside to give support and shout encouragements.] Stan: Now the first move I want you to do is a simple splash off the top rope. Jonny: So you want me to climb up the ropes and then belly flop onto the mat? Stan: Yes. Jonny: Can you do it first? I just want to make sure it’s safe. Stan: Hell no. Do you know how much that would hurt…..er…..I mean that I’m not the one training here. Get up there and do it! [Jonny reluctantly agrees and he begins his ascent to the top rope. He gets to the top and he stands up. He wobbles a bit but then gains his balance.] Donnie: You can do it, Jonny. Just block out everything but the sound of my voice. [Jonny takes a deep breath. He gets set to jump when Donnie blurts out.] Donnie: Couldn’t he just use that big cushion over there? Stan: Shut up, idiot! [Jonny can’t stop himself as he hears this, but he attempts to and only spins himself and he lands flat on his back.] Donnie: Sorry, Jonny. My bad. Jonny: I’m okay. A few broken vertebrae never hurt anyone. Stan: Alright. Get up there and try it again. Jonny: Can I use the big cushion? Stan: Fine. I’m just trying to make you tough. Jonny: Just get the cushion. [Donnie slides the big cushion into the ring and Stan moves it into place. Jonny climbs back up to the top rope and without hesitation does a picture perfect splash on to it.] Stan: Very nice. Not get up there and try a moonsault. Jonny: What’s a moonsault? Stan: It’s a backflip. Jonny: Gotcha. [Jonny repeats his climb to the top and this time stands with his back towards Stan. Jonny takes another deep breath and while he does this Stan slides the cushion out of the ring without Jonny noticing. Jonny performs a beautiful moonsault and then collides with the mat. He lies there motionless as Stan and Donnie check on him.] Donnie: Jonny. Hey, Jonny! You okay, man? Stan: That was amazing, kid! [Jonny turns on to his back and he holds his stomach with his right arm.] Jonny: Stan. Stan: Yeah? Jonny: Come closer. [Stan, with a concerned look on his face, inches towards Jonny.] Jonny: Closer. [Stan moves and little closer and then Jonny gives him a left hook to the nose. Stan falls back and Jonny stands over him.] Jonny: Don’t you ever do that to me ag…..OH, SHIT! [Before Jonny can react, Stan trips him up and puts him in a side headlock.] Stan: Where do you get the nerve to go after me? [Jonny begins to tap out and Stan releases the hold.] Stan: Now let’s get to the part that annoys me the most. Get this kid a microphone. [Stan and Donnie clear the ring and someone tosses Jonny a microphone. He takes a little time to collect himself.] Jonny: What the hell am I supposed to be doing? Donnie: Cutting a promo. You’ve been booked for your first match this Wednesday against two guys. Jonny: Two? What the hell? Donnie: It’s not two-on-one. It’s every man for himself. You’re up against Jukei Kutabare and Justin Morris. Just talk about how much better you are than them. Jonny: That will be very easy. [Jonny takes another deep breath and gives it his best shot.] Jonny: This Wednesday, the new sensation of this federation is going to be reigning as your new icon, your new idol, your new god! You are all going to witness, first-hand, an athlete the likes of which you have never seen. The smooth execution of moves you can only see to believe is what awaits the UWF viewers. First, I will breeze through this first match with some Japanese tourist and a Jonny Roxx wannabe. To be honest, I am a rocker, through-and-through. This may be my first match ever, but I am prepared for the worst and I am ready to take down the best. At the best of my days, women wanted me and men wanted to be me. And hell, who am I kidding, everything is still the same. Jonny Roxx is the new name in wrestling and I am going to go straight to the top. [Jonny throws down the microphone and then turns to Stan and Donnie.] Jonny: How was that? Donnie: Not bad at all. We still need to work on every aspect of your game, though. Stan: Yeah, especially the wrestling part. Let’s get back to training. [As they begin to train once again, the camera fades to black.]
