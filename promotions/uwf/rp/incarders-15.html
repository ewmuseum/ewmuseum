UWF (2001–07) Backstage Area — Incarders #15

Stable Wars: Return Of The King

3 Jul 2004

Ben Jericho — Stable Wars 2004

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Resurrection -v- Mystery Stable [The members of The Resurrection are all standing together in the ring. Digital Mortality, Moraless, Malcolm Kennedy, and Bondage the Sex Fiend. They all looked poised and ready to take on this mystery stable that everyone is wondering about.] Rick: I still can't figure out who this mystery stable is going to be. I mean could it be current UWF stars grouping together? Or could it be a whole group of new UWF stars? Larson: I have no idea Rick! But whatever happens here is going to be really interesting. I can't wait to find out who is in this mystery stable. [The Resurrection look to be getting quite restless in the ring. The fans are ringside are poised and ready to explode when they see who can be in this mystery stable. The lights in the arena slowly begin to dim, the fans explode to finally see who this is going to be. A spotlight his the entrance way, and nothing comes out. The fans become silent. The Resurrection looks around in a total state of confusion.] Larson: What the hell is going on? Did the mystery stable no show? Rick: I have no idea.. but maybe this is just a ploy.. [The spotlight is still showing on the entrance way and then cuts out again. The crowd start chanting 'bullshit' amongst themselves. Suddenly a booming sound can be heard from the P.A System] Voice: 'I push my fingers into my eyes..... it's the only way, to slowly stop the ache.... but it's made of all, the things I have to....' [Slipknot's 'Duality' begins to play as two figure in boiler suits appear on stage. The crowd already know who it is.... as do the stable in the ring..... and the announcers, well....' Rick: Oh.... my.... god! [The crowd goes berserk as both men storm the ring with chairs and automatically slam them simultaneously into Bondage the Sex Fiend's face, sandwiching his face and smashing it wide open, blood flying from the mouth of Sex Fiend. Unforgiven just drops the chair as he turns to face the rest of the stable. Slasher, however, is still slamming the chair into the face of the fallen foe] Rick: THAT'S THE DP! THAT IS THE DP! Larson: THEY'RE BACK?!?!?!? [Digital Mortality and Moraless push Unforgiven into the corner as Kennedy tries to drag Slasher off. Slasher lashes out as Kennedy punches him in the gut, keeling the masked madman over, clutching his stomach. Unforgiven looks on struggling as Moraless goes over to help Kennedy] Rick: This is a shock here, ladies and gentlement. It's been nearly a year since we last saw these two as a unit, and it seems like their on the same wavelength! Larson: Yeah... the same sick wavelength! [Moraless and Kennedy look at Slasher, as Mortality releases his grip on Unforgiven somewhat. Slasher slowly get's to his feet, as Unforgiven lowblow Digital Mortality. He grabs 'himself' and falls on his knee's as Slasher spots Unforgiven. With Moraless bent over with Kennedy to see Slasher, Unforgiven jumps in the air and lands a legdrop over the back of Moraless' head at the same moment Slasher springs up and nails Kennedy with a spiked DDT. The crowd roar with approval.] Rick: And that's the tandem team work that we've expected from this team! [Slasher walks over to Mortality, still on his knee's, and lands a vicious football kick right into his face. Unforgiven stomps on the two new fallen foes as Slasher calls out to him. They pick Mortality up and through him out the ring, Mortality landing hard on his back with referee's running down. They try to enter the ring until Slasher turns and let's out a blood curdling roar, in which the ref's turn to check on Digital Mortality] Larson: This is wrong! Get the riot police out for christs sakes [Slasher picks up a chair again and slams it into Kennedy's raised head. Kennedy rolls out the ring try to get out of dodge, leaving Moraless and Sex Fiend in the ring. Slasher looks at Unforgiven, his eyes showing a sick pleasure as Unforgiven slaps a Camel Clutch on Moraless. Slasher takes the chair, bounces of the ropes, and then jumps, placing the chair on his feet and crashing into the face of Moraless. The crowd just scream and shout with joy at the Drowning Pool.] Rick: JESUS CHRIST! Larson: HIS NECK? HIS JAW? HIS NOSE? IT'S ALL BROKEN IN MY OPINION!!! [Unforgiven slides out the ring to get a few 'utensils' as Slasher whips Moraless, bloodied from the nose down, into the ropes. As Moraless comes back, Slasher executes a Manhattan drop, his knee connecting between Moraless' legs. Moraless stands in agony as Slasher runs past and executes an Asai Moonsault... and landing it into a DDT!] Larson: GOOD ROD NO! Rick: SKIN TICKET! AND UNFORGIVEN HAS JUST PULLED OUT LIGHTBULBS!!!! [Unforgiven throws in what seems as a barrage of tube lightbulbs. Slasher picks one up as Unforgiven takes the one he is holding and drags Sex Fiend onto his feet and counts on his hands..... one..... two..... three..... as both again sandwich Fiend's face, this time with lightbulbs. Glass and white powder fly from the head of Fiend as he collapses again. Digimortal and Kennedy are now at the top of the ramp, looking on in anger and frustration. Slasher and Unforgiven just play to the crowd as they point to the turnbuckle.] Rick: Oh jesus no..... [Unforgiven drags Moraless over to the corner as Unforgiven starts to stack rows and rows of lightbulbs on top of one another, making it look like a campfire. Slasher slaps Moraless around the face, laughing and trying to wake him up. As if you didn't already figure out, Sex Fiend's face is a crimson mask... that doesn't stop Unforgiven picking him up, putting him in a front face lock and executing a hammerlock, dropping him into the lightbulb debris face first. The crowd mark out hard] Larson: UNFORGIVEN DDT V.2! UNFORGIVEN DDT V.2! SHADES OF 2002 COMING OUT!!! Rick: HE DESERVES TO GET HIS BEATING DELIVERED RIGHT BACK! Larson: FROM WHOM??? MORTALITY AND KENNEDY ARE ON THE STAGE AREA, AND MORALESS IS SLUMPED IN THE CORNER [Unforgiven just jumps onto his feet and starts stomping around the ring, pointing his finger with hatred towards Moraless. He looks at Slasher, and then runs his finger across his throat.... and both signal one of the deadliest moves the UWF have ever seen] Rick... Larson: Oh no.. [The arena are on their feet as Slasher climbs the turnbuckle, dragging Moraless up. Unforgiven springboards over as the a million camera's get set to take photo's. Slasher hoists Moraless into a top rope powerbomb, and as he's about to leap off the turnbuckle, Unforgiven jumps onto the third rope and grabs the opponent in a rock bottom. All three men crash land into the pile of lightbulbs, Moraless's body taking the brunt of a deadly, unforgiving....] Rick: DISASTERPIECE! JESUS H CHRIST RIDING A SEATLESS UNICYCLE.... SLASHER AND UNFORGIVEN HAVE JUST DESTROYED THE RESURRECTION! Larson: Oh jeez... which yutz gave him a microphone? [Slasher stands with the microphone to the 'mouth' of his mask, as Unforgiven just cockily kicks Moraless. He walks over to Sex Fiend and pushing him out the ring, as Slasher looks up the ramp. Slasher get's a very loud 'You Sick Fuck!' chant from the crowd, in which you can see in his eyes he is smiling ecstatically] Slasher: I'd ask if you missed us... but I think you sick bastards did. [The crowd roar with a loud 'hell yes!'] Slasher: Thing is I've been sat on my ass, watching the federation lack a certain something. That certain something of course is ~!ULTRA-FUCKING-VIOLENCE!~ [Slasher get's a pop from the crowd]. And seeing as I'm numero uno sick bastard who ever graced this company, far be it from me not to come back a stir a little trouble. Bottom line is... your staring into the face of depravity, violence and sickness. And I have no problem saying.... I FUCKING LOVE EVERY MINUTE OF IT!!!!! [Slasher tosses the microphone to Unforgiven, who suddenly get's an arena wide 'He's Extreme' ovation] Unforgiven: Slasher.... you forget one thing. See before the shit hit the fan in the UWF, I believe we were number one contenders to that tag team championship, correct? [Slasher nods as EMT's rush down to check on Moraless and Bondage the Sex Fiend]. And I don't rightly believe we got a fair shot, if a shot at all. Bottom line UWF, is that I've come back because I owe this man, my brother in blood, Slasher, a promise. I promised him a World Title and therefore my word is my bond. I'VE COME BACK TO GET WHAT'S RIGHTFULLY OURS.... THE UWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP!!!! [Slasher begins to pull shards of lightbulb from him mask and from his jaw, which is bleeding somewhat] Unforgiven: We ain't going to stop tonight. For every show we don't get the desired result, we're going to start attacking the 'higher' clients in the UWF. We started with a former World Champion and his lackeys tonight, and we'll keep going. This is a new chapter in the book of the DP.... this is the second coming of the Horrorcaust we caused before... and if we don't get the desired results.... Slasher: [grabbing the microphone] We'll rip this fucking place [low pitch death metal apart] APART!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Suddenly a wave of armed police officers run down to the ring, spearing Unforgiven to the floor. Slasher tries to help him, but is met with a shot from a nightstick. More officers come down and all dive on Slasher, as Unforgiven is handcuffed. The crowd start booing and chanting 'Bullshit' and 'Pigs' as Slasher get's handcuffed. Unforgiven is picked up and start grinning, talking trash to the officers. Slasher get's stood up, and headbutts the officer facing him. The crowd cheer loudly as Unforgiven starts laughing, then getting roughed up by an officer. The crowd boo as the police officers drag the two up the ramp, and out the arena. There is a huge chant of 'S-W-O' although, which is one of the loudest pops of the night] Rick: My Rod... this is turning out to be one of the biggest nights in UWF history, Unforgiven and Slasher are back... Man what a night...uh... What now?
