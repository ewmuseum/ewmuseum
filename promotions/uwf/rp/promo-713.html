Back To School #1

19th September (year unrecorded)

The Legends — Classroom of DOOM~!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[School.] [Hell with tables.] [The Legends love school. They were over achievers. See, the only truthfully good thing The Legends are actually good at is learning. You wouldnt think it would you? A team, one of which uses an armpit in the face as a finisher, achieved good grades at the end of school? Bollocks you'd think.] [You could me more wrong.] Lance: 'Dont you love it Luke? being back in school?' [The Legends find themselves in a class of 8 year olds. Their legs are squeezed under small desks. They're the tallest people in the class by a clear two feet.] Luke: 'It's great! I love doing English' [The Teacher puts her finger on her lips and looks at The Legends. They shut up.] Teacher: Ok, I hope you have all been revising for the spelling test we're going to have later. [She looks around the class.] Teacher: Donald! - Donald, have you been revising? Donald: Yes miss! [The Legends scarl.] Luke: 'Teachers pet' Teacher: Ok Donald, the test is later, but I'll give you a chance to prove yourself now.. [Everybody turns to a poor white faced kid.] Teacher: Spell, 'Office' Lance: Oh that's piss easy! Teacher: LANCE! Lance: Sorry.. [Donald thinks to himself.] Donald: Erm.. O ... F ... I, C, E! [Luke claps.] Luke: 'Oh well done Donald. You thick shit!' Teacher: 'Sorry Donald, you missed out an F. You havent been revising have you? - I told you to. That's one homework not completed!' [She looks at the class.] Teacher: 'And how do we punish Donald?' *SMACK* Teacher: 'WHAT THE?' [Donald bursts into tears moments after a tag team championship belt hits him in the face. The Teacher puts her hands on her hips and looks around the class.] Teacher: 'CLASS! Who did that? Come on!' [Luke and Lance point to a girl behind them.] Teacher: 'Does the rest of the class agree it was Sally?' [The Legends frown at the class. They forcefully nod.] Teacher: '... ok. Sally, you now miss a breaktime.' Lance: 'Haha, bitch' Teacher: 'LANCE!' [He snarls.] [Donald is still crying, his head against the desk.] Teacher: 'Well, ok moving on. Lets do some maths. I'll pick up where we left off from last week, on adding big numbers with small numbers. Edward, see if you can do this one..' [The teacher writes '60 + 3 + 6' on the board. Lance and Luke smile.] Edward: '69?' [The Legends erupt into laughter. The whole class looking at them.] Lance: 'HAHA! 69! HAHA!' [The Teacher frowns.] Teacher: 'Ignore them two.. any questions about that?' Sally: 'Why are these two gay men in our class miss?' [The teacher frowns at The Legends.] Teacher: 'It's not relevant to the maths, but good question. Why are you two queres in my lesson?!' [Lance Rose and Luke Warren stand up.] Lance Rose: 'We're taking over here love.' Luke Warren: 'We were sent to assess your teaching, and we've decided it's shite. So we're taking over. We're role models, and you're a whore.' [The Teacher's eyes bulge.] Teacher: 'HOW DARE YOU use language like that in my classroom!' Lance Rose: 'Oh screw you. Are you a Legend?' [She frowns.] Lance Rose: 'No. We are. Go away.' ------------ 10 Minutes later.. ------------ [Lance and Luke are stood at the front of the class.] Luke: Ok, how do you spell this? ... Barry. Spell this; Legendious~! Barry: '... L, e, g, e, n, d, o, s ...?' Lance: 'Fuck you!' [Barry bursts into tears.] Luke: 'Why did he bother coming to school?!' Lance: 'Ok, next?' [The poor kids, now all white, bar a few crying, wait in silence. The Legends smirk.]
