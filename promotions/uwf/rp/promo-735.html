UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #735

Live and Loud P2

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nick rubbed his hands to warm them as it was a cold day. The air was breezing the snow in your nose and in your eyes. You couldn’t see much further than ten steps away from you. Anything you do is dangerous as you can’t hear or see anything coming. In a way you have lost all of your senses and navigation. As he continues his walk at almost no speed, but he has to reach that point. He’s been there before, but he was much younger then. His inner woe. Anyone has one, but some have not found it yet. Most people die when they find it. The peace you find there overwhelms most people. But not Nick! He’s taking classes in this stuff called yoga. “Breath in and out!” instructed the man. All the people were breathing extremely loud so the man could clearly see of their rhythm was right. But not Nick. He was being in full concentration. He has lost all knowledge. He sees nothing. “Nick?” the man asked. “Are you there?” Nick didn’t respond, as he continued to keep in his position not giving the man an eye wink. “Nick!” the man started to shout. “Return to reality.” Suddenly, Nick stands up and screams something we can’t hear. The man looks shocked. “Ah come on,” Nick laughed. “This stuff isn’t going to work for me. I’ll just do fine without it.” “Nick, I’ve told you before.” The man said patiently. “It’s for your own good.” “I know, Raymond.” Nick apologised. Nick knew Raymond was only helping him dealing with the pain. But Nick was thinking too much. All the troubles laying on his mind weren’t him doing any favours. But as we all know you must be able to concentrate on one subject. “You have to know I am really busy right now. With Live and Loud 4 coming up.” “That’s why you need to de-stress.” “I need to win.” “You need to de-stress.” “I really don’t need it, believe me!” “Those yoga lessons helped you before.” “But that was different.” “It’s not. This lesson applies to every situation. It makes you able to think things over without being distracted. And, you find some rest. Some rest you really need to take.” “After Live and Loud 4!” “And then after GTT 4? Don’t fool me Nick! I know who you are. We are brother. I am very much alike. More than you are willing to admit.” “That might be true, but this yoga stuff is not made for me.” “It helped you before. Sometimes you need to take my word for it. Trust me.” “Well, I am not to keen on thse things. Last time I did yoga it took me five whole days to get out of that trance. And that headache was killing me. I will not do it again. Not now, and not tomorrow. Not ever.” “Don’t say that! You know it helped you. So why not give it another chance?” “No thank you! I’ll take my chances. I’m not a fool either! Raymond, you are my brother, but as much as I love you I love wrestling too. And I know you don’t like me battling in the ring and I know you oppose people whom wrestle but that’s my way of life. Just like this is yours. I don’t believe in a God like you do.” “Well, you always had to read the bible though!” “Let’s not get to that. The topic Dad is still…….delicate.” “You need to put an end behind that chapter. To everything comes an end. What will you do if you can’t wrestle anymore? Cry and whine? That’d be a total waiste of time.” “Dad has been a part of me for a very long time. I know he is death. End of story! There is nothing more to say. But as you know just as well as I do that I have a back up plan if I stop wrestling.” “Don’t even say it! Stop with that nonsense plan of yours!” “What’s wrong with making a fashion line in wrestling clothes? It worked for music! It worked for the movie industry! Why not for wrestling?” “It’s a different kind of industry for instance! Your audience is totally different.” “People who watch wrestling often like music as well!” “Please, you know what I mean. How many people are interested in buying a wrestling pant?” “I am, to make a start! But I have no time to argue with you now. I have got some training to do. And an interview I believe.” “Who?” Nick looks surprised by this question, but his brother is deadly serious. “Come on, who is your opponent?” “I’ve got five opponents actually. Almasy, Moraless, Mercury, Killer Dude and Misfit.” “That’s going to be one hell of a massacre then.” “Probably.” “Promise me this! Make damn sure I won’t lose a second family member this month!” “I promise. Now relax, those five are in for the shock of their live.” “What do you mean?” “You just wait and see.” Nick walks away leaving his brother alone. Nick knew what he had in mind. It was a ferocious plan. But it was good. Good enough to capture a title. A title he deserves. More than that actually! But as we all know, you need to take what belongs to you. That’s exactly what Nick will do.
