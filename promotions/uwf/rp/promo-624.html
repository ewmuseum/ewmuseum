UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #624

RR-> Intro to O'Hara's

14 Aug 2004

Brody O'Hara — Murphey's Pub

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene begins outside a tavern. The lights and sounds from inside emit from inside in the night. The sign over the door reads “Murphy’s”. The camera goes in the front door. Inside, its your basic bar, filled with drunken Irish Americans mostly. The bartender, a fat bald man sends two frothy mugs down to two men at the bar. They were seen on UWF television last Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. It’s the Red Reefers tag team: Ash and Bowie O’Hara. Bowie is wearing his trusty leather jacket while the barrel chested and bulldog faced Ash wears a plain old beige button up shirt, with the first 5 buttons unbuttoned, letting his red chest hair be exposed for all of the TV viewers and drunks to see. Ash grabs a mug and just downs it like its nothing. Bowie takes a drink and sets his mug down, just as Ash finishes his and slams it down onto the bar. Bowie turns to his older brother.) Bowie: Jesus fucking Christ man, what the fuck is that shit, you gotta fucking slow down. Ash: Bahahahaha! You wee girl… Bowie: Don’t fucking call me a fucking girl you fucking fat fuck. Ash: You’re not a girl… You’re a prick, how can a prick be a girl? I’m just saying that you drink like one. Bowie: Fuck off, you fucking fuck… Ash: ACK! That’s all you say… dirty tongue… Ma’s rolling over in her grave, she is. Bowie: This one’s for Ma! (Bowie takes a big drink of beer. He wipes the foam from his upper lip.) Ash: ‘Atta boy, lad. Murph! I need another down here. (Murphey, for ‘Murph’ for short, the bartender, nods and fills up Ash another mug.) Murph: Lads, I remember the first time you came in here, and lemme tell you something… you’re two are something else. This ones on me, lads… For the O’Hara brothers, and to their new career in the Universal Wrestling Federation… maybe you be something else there, too! (Murph hands the drink over to Ash. Bowie, Ash, Murph, and about five other random drunk guys toast to the Red Reefers success.) Murph: I’ll tell ya who else is something else… your brother, Brody. Ash: Yeah, he’s something else alright…. Where is that girl anyways? Bowie: Girl? Better him than me I guess. Ash: You know his girly locks… Bowie: I suppose… (The camera goes outside the pub. Away from the laughter and the drunks and the fun. To the outside world. Brody O’Hara sits on a bench outside, watching cars and people go by. He’s wearing cut off jean shorts, and a white wife-beater. His black braids cover part of his face, until he leans back on the bench, revealing himself.) Brody: Ah…. I love nights. I really do. I don’t mean to miss out on the good times inside... but sometimes, you just gotta get away. I’ve always been a little different. I don’t need to swear like Bowie… and I don’t need to drink like Ash… they need it… Ash fights better when he’s drunk… and Bowie has to cuss… if he doesn’t his head might implode or his brains might ooze out his ears… that’s just how they are, my brothers. And I love ‘em. Everyone in the world has needs, even me. My needs… sometimes I just gotta get away from it all. I like to just watch sometimes…. It makes me feel good. I’ve got a big week ahead of me… debuting in the UWF, in the Regal Rumble, no less. Though the others more or less have their sights set on the tag team titles, and are basically just in there to kick some ass… I’m the one flying solo in the singles competition. The Red Reefers are a tag team… I’m an O’Hara… but I’m not one of them… The O’Hara family… we’re a fighting family. We’ve had to fight for everything we have, and we’re proud. We’re proud of everything we’ve got because we earned it through hard work and there’s no better feeling. UWF… UWF is going to be a lot of work, getting to the top. But I can do it… I can make it to the top, and I’m going to turn some heads in the Regal Rumble. They don’t know me… they don’t know the O’Hara’s… yet… But they will. We’re a fighting family, and the fight has just begun. (The scene fades to black.)
