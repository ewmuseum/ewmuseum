UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #706

Preparing for the future

17 Sep 2004

Adien Pryde — Hotel Pool

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside a nice hotel on a sunny day. We are at poolside with the members of The Chaos Society, Reo Dai and Joanna. Adien Pryde is doing laps as Joanna counts them off.] Joanna : That’s 50 Pryde, I think you can stop. Take a break. [Pryde stops in the shallow end and stands up, remaining in the water while he catches his breath.] Pryde : I just have to let out some frustration. With you back with The Chaos Society and Plague still out and about knowing that I have bested him, I am just waiting for him to pop up and try something. He will pay for what he did to you, he will pay dearly in the ring. I mean, really, just because I executed the Black Death better than him is no reason to be upset. [Pryde looks into the camera.] Pryde : Just a little more practice Plague and maybe you can get it right like I did. [Pryde get hit with a towel as it covers his face.] Joanna : Get your head in the game Adien. You have a big 4-way match Sunday against 2 veterans Slasher, Ruff Rider, and you old pal Nick Persia. You need to stay focused if you want to achieve your goal. Pryde : I don’t know what these losers in the front office think they are doing. 3 weeks in a row I have been put into matches with more than just me and my opponent. 2 weeks ago I was in the ring with Tsunami Oni and the Plague. Who won that again. Dai : You did. Pryde : That’s right I did, pinning you Plague. Then last week, a cluster fuck of a match with Tsunami Oni, Nick Persia and I against the world champ ,Mike Montgomery, The Communist, and The Plague. And who won that? Dai : The Plague. Pryde : What, oh ya, our buddy Plague. Only after Montgomery sprayed Oni with his on mist the nails him with the Hangman’s Joke. What the hell kind of name is that anyways? I mean Hangman’s Joke? Anyways, Plague couldn’t do it without some help. Joanna : Enough with The Plague already. Pryde : Right, We know my record with Persia so we won’t get into that, just know this Nick. I have gotten much better then the last time we met as opponents. Now Slasher, here is a man that scares me. I have been in a few hardcore matches in my time, but this psycho takes it to a new level. Keep him in the ring and then we can be on even ground. Lastly, Ruff Rider, oh how the mighty have fallen. Here is a supposed former world champion, always trying to pal up with others to help him accomplish what he can’t do on his own anymore. Get it through your thick skull, NO ONE LIKES YOU. Go ride off into the sunset and find a reservation to retire to. Pryde : After I win on Sunday, I am expecting a one on one match. Against who is up to the bookers, but just know this Plague. I am still coming for you, but we are going to do it on my terms. Don’t go walking down any dark alleys. You never know when you won’t make it out the other end. [Pryde tosses the towel back to Joanna and starts doing laps again.] Joanna : Jesus Christ, enough with the laps already. [The camera fades to black.]
