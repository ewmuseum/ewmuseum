UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #686

Disaster?!

8 Sep 2004

Joshua Justice — In the air? And... WASHINGTON!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A jet flies in an open sky. The weather is outstandingly great.] Pilot: We’ll be touching down at Ronald Reagan Airport in about ten minutes, Mr. Justice. Please fasten your seat belt. [On an early Sunday morning, near Washington, D.C., on May 2004, Joshua Justice sits in his private jet. He finishes off his bottle of orange juice and puts away his laptop. He moves the table in front of him over to the side after clearing it off and goes to buckle his seatbelt. Before he gets it buckled, the nose of the jet drops and Justice flies forward into the seat ten feet in front of him. The jet starts to twist and turn as it plummets down towards the ground. Justice quickly lunges towards the pilot’s door and using his shoulder breaks through.] Justice: Holy Toledo…! [The pilot is unconscious. As the jet dives perpendicular to the ground, Justice jumps in the empty co-pilot’s seat and grabs the controls. He pulls them up with all his strength and levels out the plane. Justice looks out the side of the plane and sees black smoke pouring out of the engine area. He grabs the radio.] Justice: Mayday, mayday, over! Please respond, over! [He can’t control the plane. The plane turns completely upside down as Justice tries to call for help.] Justice: Can anybody hear me? Please respond, over! [The plane levels out once again. Justice looks at one of the dials going crazy and then looks towards the dial measuring the gas. It’s on empty. Justice throws down the headset as he sees two government jets souring straight for him. He grabs the controls and turns the plane just enough to fly directly between the two government jets. The jet, with black smoke pouring out, flies past the Washington Monument and towards the water. He levels out the jet and steers towards the water. He looks out the window and he notices that the other engine has stopped moving. As the jet starts to dip down, Justice uses all of his strength to pull up, causing the jet to level out. Dials start to bust. Parts begin to fly. The jet is about to hit the water as Justice pulls back as hard as he can.] Justice: Come on… [The jet hits the water, which is very shallow, and bounces. It comes back down hard and screeches on the cement floor below the shallow pool of water. It continues to move all the way down to the end of the water and thumps into solid ground. People run over to help when a huge foot kicks the door of the jet open. Justice runs out with the unconscious pilot in his arms.] Justice: Stay back, the plane may explode! [A few seconds later, the police and EMTs have arrived. A few tourists, who were watching the whole incident crowd around the plane, which didn’t explode, taking pictures. One of them looks over towards the where Justice and the EMTs are looking over the pilot. A policeman walks over towards Justice, who stands to meet him.] Policeman: The co-pilot’s missing and you better get the D.C. bomb squad A.S.A.P. [The tourist smiles real big.] Tourist: Hey, aren’t you Joshua Justice? [Justice turns towards him, still a little stunned at the day’s events.] Justice: Uh. [The tourist gets closer and pulls out a pen and pad.] Tourist: Yo, Justice! Can I get your autograph? Justice: Sorry. It’s not a good time. [Other police grab the tourist and push him back towards the watching crowd. The first policeman puts his hand on Justice’s shoulder.] Policeman: Mr. Justice, that was one of the most amazing things I’ve ever seen! How in the world did you do that? [Justice looks over to the plane with serious thought in his eyes. He starts to walk away.] Justice: I didn’t do anything… [Justice looks out among the crowd.] Justice: …I was just lucky. [Justice walks over and sits down under a tree. He looks over to his crashed private jet as he sees a bomb squad searching it.] Justice: Could it have been Dicky Flint looking for revenge after loosing to me like that? Or was it my opponent this week? This mysterious Seraphiend… The person that I know nothing about… I guess on tonight’s show, I better find out. [Justice stands back up and walks over to the policeman.] Justice: Get a helicopter to me ASAP. I have a show to be at and a criminal to bring to JUSTICE! [Justice puts his hands on his hips and stares off into the sunset as the policeman radios for a helicopter.]
