UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #213

The Last Shot

10 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — airport

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in an airport. Sitting in one of the chairs is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee, wearing jeans and a black “Players Club” shirt... All around him there are different people, some who know who he his, some who don’t. There is a small crowd surrounding him asking for autographs, which he happily signs. He also poses for pictures occasionally. Through all of this hustle and bustle, he notices the camera coming around to get a side shot of him.) Kazee: Well, hello there. How are you doing? It’s good to see you’re here. I was hoping that the UWF would send someone for my final interview before the Clash. I guess y’all found me. Well, that’s good……. A few days have passed since the card was announced and well, let’s look at who has actually said something about this. Myself, Montgomery, Myself, Dunn, Iron Lung, and Ruff Ryder, and now me again. Wow, we can at least say that the Players Club is going to be there in force. Where is everyone else though? I mean I can understand if they are a little hesitant to step in the ring with the most dominant force, but still, have some guts. I mean y’all aren’t going to be facing us…….. This tag match though, still kind of has me bothered. The fact that I am going to be facing Monty is giving me some real problems here. Now granted, I never said anything about not doing so, and I do have thousands of open contracts out there to face anyone, but the UWF has lost its senses on this one. But hey, it’s all good. We’ll deal with it…… I do have a few things to say though. I’ve talked about Raid. I’ve talked about Monty. Now, there is only one person left to talk about. And that person left for me to actually say something to is a guy named Malcolm Kennedy. What is there to say about him? Well, Kennedy, I know you now. I’ve defeated you, and well, if I have to, I’ll defeat you again. I know that you have a storied past with Monty, and I know that you were part of the now defunct Prime Alliance….. Kennedy, I’m still trying to figure out how in the world you ever got your World Title shot. That should’ve never happened. That, as most of the wrestling world will agree, was a fluke. And I’m sure that that will be proven if and when you ever face somebody for the World Title……. You, Kennedy, your also a bum, a wife beater, and several other things. You don’t even deserve to be in this federation. Your ethics are those of the Jerry Springer Show, and it continues to show all the time through your actions. Like the way you got into the Clash of the Champions. Quite underhanded even if I say so myself. Why the brass even let you in is a totally different matter. I’m sure it’s because they felt sorry for you. Well, have no mistake that I don’t feel sorry for you. I never will feel sorry for you…. But you can count one thing for sure, you even try to harm your partner at the PPV, you can bet your bottom dollar that I will be on your butt. And I know that you are already down to your bottom dollar, so it’s something you don’t want to part with…… I don’t know if you and Raid have something going here. If this is some attempt to revamp the Prime Alliance. If you’ve just kicked Shock out and are following Ryder as your leader? I just don’t understand. I do understand this though. One way or another, The Players Club is coming out of the Clash with the titles, but whether or not their partner is still there in order to celebrate is another thing all together…… There will be no way to know, till it happens, but I’m guessing that you won’t be leaving with a belt Kennedy, because it’s time that I got some gold, and if the chemistry I had at one time with Raid comes back, well than it is most definite that you will not be leaving the Clash with the title…… The only thing you need to know is that you’ll probably want to have the $2 bottle of bubbly cold for you in the locker room when you’re done. I’ll guarantee you that you’ll need something to drown your sorrows in…… This Sunday, two people will be leaving with the title. I’ve got to figure that I’m guaranteed to be one of them. The only question is whether it is with Raid or with Monty. The only other thing that we can be guaranteed of is that Kennedy will not be leaving with a title, because hey, let’s face it, either he is facing one of the greatest Tag Teams to ever run on the upper Midwest Indy scene, or he’s facing the Players Club, and either way, those definitely are not good odds for you, Kennedy….. Now, as I said, this is my last promo till after the Clash, so please feel free to get your cheap shots in, but it really won’t matter, because I won’t have time to pay attention. I’m going to represent the UWF as only the Players Club members can. And y’all oughtta be thankful for that, cause if it weren’t for us, there’d be pennies in your pockets instead of $20 bills. (Kazee continue to sign autographs and take pictures as the scene fades to black.)
