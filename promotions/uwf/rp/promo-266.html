Trust Excercise

3 Mar 2004

Iron Lung — Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trust Excercise Spin-off of 'Two Heads' Starring Iron Lung The scene opens up to us slowly with a shot of wall padding, a humming sound flowing through the whole room before us. And then, some oriental music gradually comes into play. It was meant to fill the air with the upmost harmony, but for one of the students here for the day, it would soon become an emotional journey to try and trust another being to do no wrong. Lung thought that with this sort of newly found training, he would be able to at least trust his partner and not end up fighting with him, thus costing them the match. In the last episode, he was seen watching a daily soap opera to try and gain some emotion for his partner, perhaps evevn mellow out his attitude in the ring and show some feelings. He was always a focused individual, but he also had problems with showing any sign of caring for what his partner was saying to him. That was solved with the 'boob tube'. Now he had a trust issue. He was always thinking that his partner, no matter how spectacular they were according to statistics, would end up blowing the match for their team. He was always a loner, and not used to dealing with tagging anybody in for support. So now, this is where the excercise came in. It was a quite simple excercise. All you had to do was fall backwards, and let another partner catch you before hitting the ground. You had to cross your arms so you didn't have any choice but to trust. Of course, IL thought this was the craziest idea since sliced bread, but he would go along with it. Come on, where's this trainer. He's already five minutes late, I don't have this kind of time to be playing around with-- some -- Suddenly, a magical mist comes into the young Pendragon's mind. He can't think straight, he can't even hold his balance as a young woman walks into the room. She is beyond any description of beautiful, and as soon as she laid eyes on the class, he felt like his heart would jump onto the floor like in the cartoons. -- A humana humana She had a fair complexion, despite having some freckles. She was a brunette, and had amazing brown eyes to match. Even if she was a little smaller than Pendragon, his mind couldn't distinguish anything. He shook his head in amazement, trying to loosen the brain fog. She stood before the group in a warmup outfit, fitting her bodly tightly, making Pendragon even the more concerned about seeing her personally train him. He had to try to keep focus as she introduced herself with an angelic tone to the rest of the class. Hello everyone, I will be your teacher today, and you can just call me Veronica. I've been a trust excercise teacher for about two years now, and before that I was a background dancer. I also teach a Yoga class here if any of you are at all interested in that. So, let's get to know each other a little better. I'll go around the room, and you all can introduce yourselves and tell us a little about yourselves too. Since we have to feel comfortable around each other, this is a good way to break the ice. Let's start with -- Iron Lung's face glows with glee and his knees begin to shake in nervousness. Thoughts of Victoria picking him kept running through his head, but what could he say about himself? He wasn't a big self talker, that was for sure. As Victoria's finger trailed the group, Lung's heartbeat raced with passion. More thoughts ran through his mind, he had to try to regain his composure so he could focus on trusting others, and not getting another girl to have dinner with him. And then, her finger pointer at him. Lung's heart stopped for a dramatic moment, and he wiped a bit of sweat off his brow with his fingertip, stuttering for words before he even started talking. They were stuttering in his voicebox, even before they left his lips. She was all too beautiful, and he didn't want to make a fool of himself. How about you first. ... me? Sure, tell us a bit about yourself. Well... I'm not really that exciting. (Letting out a slight giggle) That's ok, I just want to know about you so I can make this a very personal experience. Well.. ok. My name is Robert Pendragon. Hello Robert, nice to meet you. It's nice to meet you too Victoria. Hmm, let's see what else. I'm from Norwalk, and I'm nineteen years old. I'm pretty much here at this trust excercise because I'm having a hard time trusting somebody as my partner, as far as being a professional wrestler is concerned. So you are here for personal trust development. Yes ma'am.. Well, that's great. I hope that you learn a lot from this class, and also hope that you are able to make that development. Thank you very much, I'm sure that you'll be able to teach me very well. The pair smile at each other, and Victoria is left speechless for a moment. She then shakes out of it and continues along the crowd, keeping a menacing eye on Pendragon with each person she pointed to. This couldn't be good for Lung, not good at all. He was trying to keep his concentration on the excercise, and not the teacher. Why did he have to get all the bootylicious teachers anyway? Was it something about his suave style that drove them to him, or was he just lucky enough to have to handle these babes? Whatever it was, he was happy it came to his side. He hadn't been very lucky throughout most of his life, and now it was finally beginning to come up on the bright side of things. Suddenly, the room grew silent. Victoria must have finished with introductions, and now it was time to learn to trust each other. Oh man, how could Lung ever find it in him to trust a total stranger? He could barely trust his own well being at times. Okay, now that we've learned a little about each other, let's jump into the excercise. Whoever is next to you will be your partner for it, so look to your left and you shall find your trust-filled friend... Friend? Pendragon shuttered to think of that word actually used in a sentence. He thought he might have to wash his mouth out with soap after this. How bad could it be though? Pretty bad, he could let Lung fall flat on his back. All these ideas were clashing around in the young champion's head. He had to try and shake them out as his partner turned to him with a smile on his face before getting in front of him with his back facing Lung, ready to drop into his arms. And then, he fell. IL took a deep breath as he cradled him, lifting him back up to firm ground. Now, it was his turn to trust his partner. Lung sighed with a slight disturbance, he didn't think that he would be able to do it. He also thought that his partner would be careless. What's wrong Robert? I'm a little edgy about falling into his arms. I mean, I think that he might drop me and stuff. Well, he trusted you to catch him and you caught him right? I guess, but everybody's been so mean to me in the past... Well, sometimes you have to get over the past and live for the now. Do you need any help? I don't know, I might... I'll be your partner if you want (giving a slight wink) (With a slight grin) I'd like that... Victoria slowly comes closer to Lung as his partner steps aside. With her back facing him, Victoria drops into IL's grasp. He smiles down at her and she smiles back, cradling her tightly. There you go, now it's your turn. Okay, I'll try... Putting Victoria back on a vertical base, now Lung faces his back towards her. And then, IL falls back, Victoria catching him immediatly with a gasp. And then, the two smiled at each other again. The task had been completed, and Lung felt fabulous. He was finally able to trust somebody, and he owed it all to Victoria. He thanked her over and over again as he prepared to exit the class, Victoria slipping him a piece of paper with her number on it, along with a kiss on the cheek. With a smile in his step and a song in his heart, the young champion left the classroom and walked out to his car, fresh with a new confidence. How would this new attitude effect his opponents? Time would only tell...
