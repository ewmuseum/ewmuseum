UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #356

Mentok revolutionizes the Matrix

Today (year unrecorded)

Mentok the Mindtaker — Right .... here.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“ … the NARRATOR~!, are you there?” Mentok walks around in boredom, kicking a can as he passes it by. It goes by flying, crashing back to the ground and letting out its signature sound, a clinging and a clanging that could not be mistaken for any other sound. As soon as he hears me … the NARRATOR~!, he stops and claps his hands together, he knows that I am around. “I was getting lonely without you being here, it’s a mighty long trip between Nebular and Earth” Though he is the greatest of Mind Takers, even Mentok sometimes mistakes who I truly am. See, I … the NARRATOR~! I am like Jesus, I am here but I am also every where. “You know … Jesus was a Mind Taker. He started us all!” With that, as I … the NARRATOR~!, am left in great confusion, Mentok nods his head and lets out his signature child like giggle. “Did you see The Matrix Revolutions?” Of course I saw The Matrix: Revolutions, in theatres now. I am every where, so yes, indeed I saw it. Mentok claps his fingers and disappears, and just as he does so, the signature sound for any disappearing act cues us. You know, that sound that you can do by hitting the butt of a knife against a wine glass? Ok, you got it now? Can we continue? Yes? Good! Now, the sound cues up again, and Mentok appears on his throne like chair. In front of him, a TRALSMA Television appears, which cues up the “Cliiiiiing” sound again. Oh yes, Mentok is better than all you people with Plasma, he uses the top notch quality coming from Nebular, which is TRALSMA, beat that bitch! Slowly, Mentok’s smile disappears as he gets annoyed of me … yet again! But hwo can you get annoyed at …. THE NARRATOR~! “Keep on talking, soon, my Living Soundtrack will drown you out> I can’t wait! A theme song for every action and feeling. When I get peppy, I’ll have the Living Soundtrack play Great Balls of Fire, and when I am heart broken, I will play Bye Bye Bye from N*Sync. And when … oh when … the NARRATOR~!, when I train my army of Mind Slaves, the Oompa Loompa song will play in all it’s glory. And for normal times like these … Darth Vader baby! He is the only human I liked. Wait, was he even human? He had a cool mask” Are you done rambling? “I am Mentok, I will ramble if you choose? Why you ask? Because Mentok … WILLS it so!” With that Mentok jumps out of his seat and does the Mind Taking stance, legs slightly crunched, at shoulder length. Feet at 45 degree angles, always parallel. Open handed, one arm extended far out, with the other one close to his chest. If he had a third arm, he would probably pick his ass with it, but no, he does not have a third one. With the pose comes the noise of Mind Taking. Oh yes, it even has a sound, produced by Mentok of course.
