UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #101

Decay back to disillutionment

26 Oct 2003

Joshua Dunn — The UWF Complex

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Sunday Afternoon Supremacy draws near once more, and what is in the main event? The Bunny master, Laguna, the Commissioner Jamie Shock, the Has Been, Digital Mortality, and the ever strong Communist Scotsman Joshua Dunn, each fighting for the right to go head to head next week against the Seymour Almasy for the World Hevyweight Championship. No mention of Ruff Ryder. Just as well] [The Scene Opens in the dressing room of Joshua Dunn, we can see the man in question sitting down on the communist decor sofa. His Black T-Shirt and Jeans as limp as his head as it hangs down from his shoulders. his hands lowered between his legs and elbows perched on his knees, again with the inner hatred, the burning and the holes] [Lying on the Coffee Table is a news paper. Local news of a place hardly anyone has heard of the Headline: 'Population Subdued by Rabbit Swarm' Obviously Dunn was doing some research into what has been going on over there in the Bunny front line. But now he has other things on his mind. Perhaps the agrovation at his former partner Digital Mortality who he has to face also this sunday. Maybe the hatred of the Commissioner. Maybe all that and more, because for now Joshua's fighting with himself, wrestling the greatest oponent of all: Sanity] [He shifts position whislt inhaling, throwing his head back and moving his hands to clutch at his head, elbows high in the air] [Through the battered wasteland of his mind Dunn finds himself caught in the no-man's land of contiousness, facing the untouchable steel fortress which locks away all his past memories, feelings, and attitude, the fort knox in his head, partolled by the infestation of communism bestowed into him by the NVA. He aproaches the fortress, but all it does is cause the pain to get greater, sprouting more fire, more burning, seering pain that prevents the reclaiming of his former self. Still he pushes on, the pain obvious on his physical face as he tries to gain access to the forbidden region of his head, holes to fill, and plenty material held within the fill them all, if only he could reach them. Progress is hard, the more he tries to get closer the more intense the pain gets, the quicker the communist within can persuade him to turn back] [Still clutching at his head in aggony he folds over, head between his legs, for the little good it does him, still the infestaton holds him back from his goal, the oneness he could recieve from reuniting with the hidden, compassionate, self sacrificing self of old. Who knows what He could be capable of once he finds the completion he would have after scaling those walls?] [Wading through the thick soup hindering his progress he gets closer, and closer to the goal, the steel barrier. as he gets close, the pain intensifies, he can see a crack in the impenetrable wall, beyond a green glow, and a glimps of what he wants, the Ying to his Yang, the Joshua to the Dunn. He reaches for it, close, so very close. But he succums to the forces preventing him getting there, catapaulted back to the hear and now with a delightfuly painless woosh sending him back to reality] [Dunn looks up, he was so very close, but the pains now gone, he still has the gaps to fill, the missing pieces to the puzzle, so very close. But the burnings faded now, and the gaps are still to fill but he has gained one thing from the experience, he knows it is there, he just needs a way to get at them] [He notices the camera] JD: I'm sorry, you caught me at an awkward moment, I suppose you want my oppinion on the match that is soon forthcoming? [the camera does nothing, but the cameraman obviously agrees] JD: Well, The two people that possibly could have caused the most trouble have been completely quiet, that being Jamie Shock and Digital Mortality, there contempt of the match is slightly worrying, as if they both know something I don't, but I'm almost certain Digital's still licking his wounds from losing Justine to his Rival. [He pauses, thinking, staring up to the ceiling, he looks back at the camera and continues] JD: Digital, you should have to learn that careing leads to hurt, If you had nothign to care about how could people hurt you? No one to care about, no psycological advantage to a person who takes them from you, when was the last time you saw me fretting over anyone elses fortune appart from my own, you need an emotional fortress. That's the only way to prevent becoming the snivelling mess that you have. Jamie Shock on the other hand, Him with the supposedly broken foot, who has also been very silent as of late. Perhaps using the foot excuse to prevent himself being thrown into the ring with two other people who would gladly ripp his arms off and bet him down with them. He knows exactly why, he's trodden on too many toes to be unconcerned about it. Perhaps it would be best if Mr. Shock kept out of that ring on sunday's show. And As for the Leader of the great fluffy, nose twitching Bunny Army. He's the only person to have made his voice heard for this match, but the only thing he proves is that he is nothing on his own. He hides behind his henchmen and his 'Troops' [The apropriate finger movements for the quotatin marks are used] JD: In his las promotion what did he say? he didn't even lift a finger other than to sort his hair, leaders don't get their hands dirty. Or at least that's what he thinks. When was the last time you actually did something without your army? lets think, never. How do you propose to turn this around now? Once you've ran your own path it's hard to change direction. Your imperfections shall be shown to all [The Scene Fades to black as Joshua Sits back in the sofa, trying to resume where he left off]
