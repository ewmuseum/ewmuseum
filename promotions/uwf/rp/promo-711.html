UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #711

The Blair Wolf Project

18 Sep 2001

White Wolf — A Larson SAS promotion

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
White Wolf The Blair Wolf Project [The TV Title… The prestigious of guaranteed booking at every show. The ultimate challenge in title defense. What kind of man can hold on to this power stricken burden? Very few have held for a large period of time. And therefore we haven't deduced what type of man it takes to hold said title. But with White Wolf's dramatic change… we just may figure it out.] [Larson is seen standing in a long stadium hallway giving a quick promotional feed to the television world for the upcoming SAS. He's clothed in suit attire, and is looking very serious. He addresses the camera.] Larson: …and we'll be seeing White Wolf making his first TV title defense since winning the title upon his return. Now, after that we have a great match-up… [WOOSH] [Larson stops as he catches something running by the end of the hall.] Larson: Stop for a minute guys… Security, check out that end of the hall. Random Crew: What'd you see? Larson: I don't know… In fact roll camera, we'll see what happens. [The security guards head down to the phantoms origin. Larson looks at the camera.] Larson: Ladies and gentlemen I don't know what just happened but it seems that there may be someone or something in the building with us. Let's watch… [The guards make it to the end of the well-lit hallway. They look down each way. Both guards look at each other and shake their shoulders. Maybe Larson's paranoid.] Guard 1: Ain't nothin' down here! [The first guard steps forward away from the second. The second disappears as our phantom whooshes by and grabs him. The second guard turns around.] Guard 2: Bert?… Bert? STOP FUCKING AROUND! Larson: What the hell's going on? Random Crew: I think w should leave sir, let security and the police handle it. Larson: No way, its just some jerk playing a sick joke! [The guard walks back down the hall cursing his co-worker. Suddenly, he appears. He dropped from the ceiling and landed with spread knees and hands on the floor. His pale head crooked to greet the guard in a most menacing manner. His long jacket lain spread on the ground behind him. His television title hanging proudly around his slim waist. The phantom… He was White Wolf.] [BANG! White Wolf hits the ground.] Random Crew: JESUS! [The camera is dropped on its side and you can see feet running around it. The damage from the drop is loss of color in the picture as everything is now black and white. Larson picks up the camera.] Larson: Get out of here you pansies, here's a chance for me to get more TV time! [The guard hauls out his baton and points it at White Wolf. White Wolf grins. The echoing yell soars down the hallway.] Guard: Stay the hell back White Wolf. You have to leave, or you'll get in trouble! This area is also closed… [White Wolf walks towards the guard, still smiling. The guard swings the baton hard. White Wolf catches his arm on the inside. White Wolf delivers a vicious head butt to the man's nose. He goes down. White Wolf walks furiously down the hall towards Larson. Larson begins to walk backwards with the camera on his shoulder.] Larson: So… uhh… heh heh… White Wolf. How about the title win... Oh rod don't hurt me! [The camera turns around and the motion of running is apparent. Larson is running from White Wolf with a black and white broken camera, that's tapping the whole fiasco. White Wolf's footsteps can be heard in the background.] Larson: What do you want from me?!?!?! White Wolf: I want nothing from you! I want strike FEAR into the UWF… Not just the locker room either! Larson: But why?!? [Turning down another hallway, Larson finds a door. He opens it and shuts it right away. Instantly the camera view is engulfed by darkness. Heavy breathing is heard and Larson lights a match revealing his face and suit.] Larson (whispering): This is crazy… The man needs some help. I'm afraid of what he might do to me if he finds me. [From outside the room you can hear the steps of the big bad Wolf.] White Wolf: You know I beat some mafia like man for my title? You know I've beaten many a caliber of wrestlers in my day? You know a lot about me don't you? Well think again world… You don't know jack shit about me! [White Wolf kicks the door that Larson happens to be behind in a small storage room. Larson screams as sweat puts out his match.] Larson: Uh-oh… White Wolf: Little pig little pig LET ME IN! Larson: Ain't nobody here but us chickens? [BANG! CRACK! SNAP! White Wolf kicks the door open . Standing above the threshold makes a for a really bitchen silhouette of White Wolf from front on. Larson jumps to his feet and dodges around White Wolf. He begins running down the hall again trying to lose the Wolf.] Larson: You have your title! You're respected! White Wolf: Respect is for people who don't mind loosing! I'm a man of integrity! I must have fear coursing through me! I must strike people mentally before I strike them physically! Larson: PLEASE! DON'T STRIKE ME PHYSICALLY! [Larson continues to run through the halls. However, behind him, the footsteps stop. He slows down. Eventually, he stops. Larson turns around with the camera. White Wolf seems to have given up or failed in his pursuit of Larson. Larson walks over by a main entrance. He checks the locks.] [BOOM! Without warning, one of the security guards is forcefully pressed up against the other side of the glass doors. His head squashed against the glass leave a large blood streak and the man is let go and falls to the ground; revealing behind him is White Wolf. Larson walks back. White Wolf glaring in. Both men now yelling to obtain a volume of hearing.] White Wolf: When I step into the ring tomorrow with Seraphiend and Dreammaker… whoever the hell they are… I'm going to hit them with my hardest weapon. Fear. This whole match will be a basis on how I can destroy people before we meet. This match is to set an example. I will soon make this title more difficult to obtain then the World title. And soon, people will forget about that Canadian player, and they'll all say, 'my dream is to beat White Wolf and win the Television Title…' Larson: You certainly seem to have quite the little plan there Wolf. But why do you have to beat on the staff. White Wolf: I'm simply putting the fear into people that I'm dangerous. Larson: But we know that! All too well. You always were a hazard… Literally, your considered under our insurance policies to our workers… Not wrestlers… Employees like me. White Wolf: Good… But it's for the wrong reasons. They think its cause… [White Wolf stops. He looks to his right. He grins sinisterly and looks back into the black and white camera.] White Wolf: I'm crazy… But I'm not. I'm simply torn. Torn by my own in acceptable failure in the past. Torn by loosing to such insolent dicks as Lew Spectre, or constant agonizing from a green little creature! Larson: You mean Luc… White Wolf: SHHH! We don't speak his name anymore. Larson: Loosing to Spectre is no big deal! He's arguably one of the best we have ever seen! White Wolf: Am I not one of the best? I'm considered one of the veterans… but I'm still looked down upon. But not anymore. Larson: You say that every time you return. White Wolf: But this time, I'm in touch with the Mowglou tribe. I've revisited my initial roots and so I'm back to the very first White Wolf… Except with more skills and experience… I have sights set out for the UWF. And I already accomplished the first on the list in my return match. You, Larson, will deliver this tape to the UWF and the World… Soon my message will be passed to all wrestlers of all calibers and there will soon be no more doubting of the Wolf's abilities. Larson: No body doubts you that much! White Wolf: I believe what I have grown accustomed to is the forever set doubt on White Wolf. And there will be no further heckling of my name… Larson: Can you leave now? White Wolf: Fine… you're scared. And it seems Dreammaker and Seraphiend are also scared. We have yet to hear from them correct? Tomorrow will be my example night. You will see the brutality I can indeed inflict on people. I'll teach these little fucks that when they get into a war with the Wolf, you cannot ignore him. You have to address him, and you have to fight like it's for your life… Because I have nothing to loose, and after a certain incident, I fight like it's always for my life… because it just might be… [White Wolf turns from the camera and walks away. Larson zooms in and the scene fades to black with text scrolling up the screen.]
