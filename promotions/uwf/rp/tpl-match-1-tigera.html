Chupa goes flying past her, over the top rope, and cascades into the guardrail. Crab Mask, Jr. gasps … and then Tigera tries to decapitate him with a roundhouse kick. Out he goes!] Larson: “And it ends.” [Baron Black Scourge shakes his head in disgust. Trailer Park Luchador breaks his run in mid-stride, screeching to a stop, and watching Barry the Gimmick Thief run straight past him, giving an awkward sideward glance as he goes by.] [The camera stays on the Trailer Park Luchador as he watches the obliteration that follows: first cringing, then gasping, then covering his eyes in horror. A moment later, Barry the Gimmick Thief is lying at his feet, groaning.] [And Tigera growls from the middle of the ring.] Rick: “And there you have it, boys and girls … Tigera has just obliterated the Luchador’s entire army!” [On the outside of the ring, the Trailer Park Luchador looks longingly towards Baron Black Scourge, trying desperately to hear him over the drunken roar of the crowd.] [Baron Black Scourge points to the Luchador … then to himself … and then to the ring. The ‘Lucha grins devilishly … gives his evil mentor the countdown …] [Three …] [Two …] [One …] [The Trailer Park Luchador and Baron Black Scourge dart toward the ring, and the lone Tigera.] [… and then Baron Black Scourge stops himself right before he hits the ring, grabs a soda from someone in the first row, and sits down next to Tommy Waters.] Rick: “Baron Black Scourge really has been great for the Luchador’s career, hasn’t he?” [In the ring, the Trailer Park Luchador makes a desperate attempt to get away … but Tigera doesn’t let that happen. With a roar, Tigera brings him down, tackling him to the mat. Her hands immediately clasp around his throat, her knees rifling into his midsection, and the Luchador trying to get away the entire way.] Larson: “Rick! Look~! In the sky! It’s a bird … it’s a plane …” [And suddenly, the giant, steel monkey cage comes descending from the sky … a giant gorilla hanging from the top of it.] Rick: “It’s a giant cage with a gorilla hanging from it!” Larson: “…” [Tigera takes a break from pummeling her opponent to grin upwards at the giant cage coming down.] [And as it slides down to place, the massive defacto-head of the Board of Directors, the UWF Gorilla, hops down … wearing only a UWF hat and a referee shirt.] UWF Gorilla: “Oooo oooo RAR!” Rick: “…” Larson: “So … wait? The Gorilla’s the referee?” Rick: “…” [Tigera looks quizzically at the massive gorilla standing next to her, but shrugs her shoulders as he sits down and begins passively picking away and eating his lice.] Rick: “I’ve got to say … still not the worst referee we’ve ever had.” [Tigera motions to the ‘Luchador to stand up, but Chris only throws up his hands, begging off.] Larson: “Wait! He’s calling a timeout! The ‘Luchador wants a—” [Tigera runs over and delivers a boot directly into the mulleted wonder’s stomach. As the Luchador cries out, Tigera begins raining down kicks, pummeling him into the ground] Rick: “This is just a brutal, brutal beating.” Tigera: “Don’t!” [Kick!] Tigera: “You!” [Kick!] Tigera: “Ever!” [Kick!] Tigera: “Touch me!!!!” [Kick, kick, kickity kick kick kick.] Rick: “…” [Bleeding and broken, the Trailer Park Luchador … grabs her foot, and then rubs her calf.] TPL: “TOUCH!!!!” [The Trailer Park Luchador RIFLES a kick into Tigera’s groin! Tigera doubles over, holding herself, as the ‘Luchador leaps to his feet and looks surprised. He quickly points at Tigera and laughs.] Luchador: “Muahaha! You have testicles! You’re a—” [Tigera spins around, smiling at the Trailer Park Luchador. Before he can even yelp, she grabs him by the end of the nose and sends him flying into the monkey cage.] Larson: “He’s just a dumb, dumb man. It’s kind of sad.” Rick: “Kind of?” [Tigera punches Trailer Park Luchador, and then gets back up. Tigera then sets up and nails a slow vertical suplex. Trailer Park Luchador slowly starts to get up holding his back. Tigera grabs Trailer Park Luchador by the hand and whips him into the ropes.] Larson: “Run, Lucha! RUN!” [Tigera’s eyes widen as Trailer Park Luchador dives at him. Tigera ducks - and Trailer Park Luchador lands on his feet, having just missed a rough shoulder tackle. Tigera grabs the back of Trailer Park Luchador's head, and brings it down onto her knee. Tigera then raises one arm in the air, before slowly walking over to the ropes, while Trailer Park Luchador remains on the mat holding his head.] Rick: “All Tigera has received in this match is one kick!” Larson: “And look at what the Luchador’s been through … this has been one of the most lopsided matches I’ve ever seen. They stacked the table against the Luchador!” Rick: “Yeah. Because there’s four of TIGERA’s brainwashed buddies on the outside of the ring.” Larson: “… the Scourge isn’t brainwashed. And technically Barry’s only here because he’s a douchebag.” [Tigera is now on the top of the turnbuckle. She looks down at the Trailer Park Luchador, and then jumps... extending her elbow outwards as he does - and bringing it down onto the chest of Trailer Park Luchador. The ring shakes.] Rick: “Great elbow by Tigera!” [Tigera isn’t done.] Rick: “Ooo! Another elbow! And another elbow! Ouch … and that one must have hurt. [Tigera puts his hands on his knees as the Trailer Park Luchador slowly starts to get up. Tigera waits... almost stalking her prey as the Trailer Park Luchador slowly gets up off the mat. Tigera delivers a stiff kick - but Trailer Park Luchador grabs it, and spins her round!] Trailer Park Luchador: “Eat it, bitch!!” [The Luchador gabs Tigera, hoists her up... and drives her into the ground with the- NO!] Larson: “HEY!” [Tigera slides up and over Trailer Park Luchador, dropping down to the mat.] Larson: “That idiot can’t hit ANYTHING!” Rick: “ What's that idiot doing NOW!?! [Trailer Park Luchador jumps up and down... before sprinting towards the ropes... and diving straight at Tigera! ] ..*CLASH* ... [Tigera had time to move though, and The Luchador dives headfirst into the steel monkey cage.] [The UWF Gorilla shrugs his shoulders and picks at his ass.] Larson: “Oh no...” Rick: “So much for a heel turn being all you need to be successful, eh?” Larson: “It’s not over yet, Rick! And if a heel turn can make THIS guy matter, I think it’s proof that everyone should be evil!” [Tigera growls as she looks down at the ‘Luchador. The Trailer Park Luchador though... like a MONSTER... sits up!] Larson: “Oh dear sweet lord its true … the Trailer Park Luchador has sold his soul for success! TIGERA CAN’T STOP HIM!!! He just keeps getting up!!!” [Blood runs down his face. He gets up, stumbles around the ring... and picks up a chair, that El Chupacabra had finally managed to slip into the cage! He then screams like a mental patient. Which, admittedly, he basically is.] Larson: “I think he's snapped.” Rick: “Yeah. Because up until NOW he’s been acting sane.” [The Trailer Park Luchador comes charging forward, ignoring the UWF Gorilla’s roar to play by the rules … by Tigera dodges again!] Larson: “ARGH!” [Trailer Park Luchador stumbles forward, missing the chair shot. Tigera, before even waiting to stand up, spins around, her foot flying upwards, throwing a spinning roundhouse kick at her opponent … but this time the ‘Luchador ducks!] Larson: “YES~!” Rick: “That was … like … the Luchador looked like a wrestler there!” [Tigera is shocked as her kick misses … and the Trailer Park Luchador drops right behind her! He rolls Tigera up with a school boy, dragging her tights with her … and happily taking advantage of the situation to touch her exposed ass as much as possible.] Larson: “I knew I was cheering for this idiot for a reason! YAY!” Rick: “I don’t know how in the hell he’s actually turned this around, but …” [The UWF Gorilla shrugs and slaps his massive hand on the ground to begin the count.] One! [Tigera is lashing about on the ground, frantically trying to get out of the pin … and especially trying to get the Luchador’s hand off her ass.] Two! Thr— [Actually, Tigera kicked out at one-and-a-half, but it was more exciting to bring it almost to three.] Kickout~! [Tigera is up in seconds, screaming, and dragging her pants back up. The Trailer Park Luchador sits on his knees, smelling his hands with a blissful look on his face.] Rick: “Kill him. Kill him now.” Larson: “I don’t know … I think I might be starting to like the little bastard.” [Tigera turns around, her eyes blazing with hatred. The Trailer Park Luchador immediately stops being a perve and jumps to his feet, begging off. She’ll have none of it, though … with a scream, she charges …] [… and misses! The Trailer Park Luchador side-steps at the last second, swinging one hand around her waist, and slipping around behind her.] Larson: “Go go go go go go go go go~!” [The Trailer Park Luchador does the first thing possibly available to him … he grabs two big handfuls of Tigera’s hair, and then viciously snaps backwards, throwing Tigera to the mat … where her head connects right onto the steel chair with a nice:] *THUD* [Tigera immediately stops moving.] Rick: “Rod damnit, no!!!” Larson: “YES~!” [Trailer Park Luchador looks shocked as he stares down at the unconscious Tigera. His eyes wide, he pokes her with his boot, trying to stir her back to life. He looks out at Baron Black Scourge and shrugs.] Scourge: “Pin her!!!” [The ‘Luchador bends down … and touches Tigera’s booby! Trailer Park Luchador giggles happily as he honks Tigera’s left breast.] Scourge: “PIN HER YOU IDIOT!!!!!!!!” [Baron Black Scourge has begun jumping up and down on the outside of the ring, throwing things at the monkey cage. The UWF Gorilla sees this, spies a kindred spirit, and begins jumping up and down on the inside of the cage, flinging his ape poo at the Scourge.] Rick: “Why isn’t he pinning her!?” Larson: “Who cares?! Take off her top!!!” [Suddenly, inspiration strikes the Trailer Park Luchador. He looks to the crowd.] [The Trailer Park Luchador looks out over the huge, sold out audience, screaming and throwing things at the ring in a drunken rage.] [He looks at the cameras … staring into the homes of all those fans watching around the world.] [He looks at the banners for Live and Loud … feeling the years of history that came before him. The spirit of all those challenges … all those legends that stood right where he was now.] [… though they likely didn’t have a seven-foot-tall gorilla standing a few feet away from them, nor had they just honked a woman’s breast in the middle of the ring. Unless you count Tsunami Oni, of course.] [And with the spirit of wrestling in his body … in his soul! … the Trailer Park Luchador points to the sky. He points to the top of the steel monkey cage.] Scourge: “NO!!! PIN HER YOU FUCKING IDIOT!!!!!” [The Trailer Park Luchador begins climbing the cage.] Rick: “He’s not going to …” Larson: “He is! What a showman! What a retarded, horny showman!!!” [With Tigera still not moving, the ‘Luchador reaches the top of the giant monkey cage. He shakes the large door at the top angrily … and somehow manages to throw it open.] [And just like that … the Trailer Park Luchador is standing high atop the world, on top of the big monkey cage … and basking in the roar of the audience.] [The Trailer Park Luchador throws both of his hands into the air, his head tilted backwards as he soaks up everything.] [And then … he goes for his patented finished, the flip-flopping, hip-hopping, dip-diving, corkscrew frogsplash of evil~!] [For ten seconds of air time, the Trailer Park Luchador has everything he ever wanted.] [Every eye in the arena is fixed on him. Flash bulbs go off. Children stare into the sky and watch him fly.] [On the biggest event of the year, the Trailer Park Luchador is the center of attention … he is everything, and the only thing that matters.] [And for those fifteen seconds as he flies through the air … the Trailer Park Luchador’s smile is wider than it ever has been.] [For those fifteen seconds, he is the happiest man in the world.] [And then those fifteen seconds end. And when they do, Tigera has had plenty of time to roll out of the ring … and the ‘Luchador comes down HARD, crashing to earth on nothing but mat.] [And it’s lucky for him that he’s unconscious, because he can’t hear the laughter of the audience.] Scourge: “You … fucking … IDIOT!!!!!!” [Tigera glares at him with no pity in the least … her eyes blazing with anger.] Larson: “…” Rick: “…” Larson: “Dear sweet lord.” Rick: “You have to admire the effort … I guess?” [Tigera doesn’t. She reaches a hand inside of the mask and uses it to yank the unconscious Trailer Park Luchador up to his feet. She rips off his Luchador mask, leaving his blonde mullet to fall out. Tigera spits on him … then rips at his face and his eyes, clawing at his face, and bloodying the broken Luchador even more. She ducks his head under her arm … runs to the turnbuckle … leaps onto the second turnbuckle, spins, throws herself, and drives the Trailer Park Luchador into the mat with a hurricane DDT!] Rick: “The Cat Scratch Fever!” Larson: “I can’t watch …” [Tigera looks down on him with disgust … and grabs him by his mullet, dragging him up to his feet one more time.] [Baron Black Scourge throws his hands into the air and walks off, leaving a depressed contingent of brainwashed drones, one retard, and one broken redneck behind.] [Tigera somehow manages to coax the UWF Gorilla into holding the ‘Luchador up … and then leaps onto the top turnbuckle. She glares at him for only a moment before flying through the air … catching the Trailer Park Luchador’s head … and driving him into the mat with a flying somersault DDT!] Rick: “Pussy Whipped!” Larson: “…” [Finally satisfied, Tigera places one hand on the Trailer Park Luchador’s chest and waits for the count.] One … Two … Three!!! *DING DING DING* [Tigera slaps him one last time before hopping to her feet and basking in the roar of the crowd.]
