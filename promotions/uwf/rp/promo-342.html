UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #342

Backyard

Yesterdays Tommorow (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Malcolms House

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Its time for a change of pace once again. Im slowing down and I don’t want to be. SAS my pace will once again be back where I want it. Full speed ahead. ” “Loss after loss after loss. This is unacceptable. Its time I got back to business. Back to kicking ass like I used to do all the time. No more losing, No more screwing around. No one puts there name in the record books by losing, No one talks about the guy who kept on losing. Losers are forgotten. Losers disappear quicker than dust in the wind. And thats just not an option for me. ” “I will be one of the best. Or I will die trying.” (((The scene opens from black overlooking a large wheat field. Behind the field, a large pine forest. The camera stands in a well kept back yard with beautiful green grass. A chain link fence rests where the grass and the wheat field meet together. The camera pans to the left to the corner of the yard. In between two trees lays a man in a hammock. He wears a pair of blue jeans and a wife beater. A Yankees hat is pulled down over his face. The camera zooms in on the man who turns out to be very young. Late teens or early twenties. As his face becomes clearer, so does his ear piercing snore. As he continues to snore, a figure comes into view standing over him. The figure reaches out, grabs the hammock and flips it over sending the snoring man crashing to the ground. With a scream he leaps to his feet.))) Man: “You freaking idiot, why the in the hell did you do something stupid like...............” Figure: “Go on?” Man: “Oh man. Mr Kennedy I am so sorry. I didn’t know it was you. Honest!” Malcolm Kennedy: “Jason, I swear to god boy, If I come out here one more time and find you sleeping on the job, Im gonna fire your ass. I don’t pay you 12 bucks an hour so you can just lay around in a hammock and sleep. Your supposed to be taking care of my land. Trimming the grass, watering my wife’s flowers, keeping the animals out of the garden. You got plenty of stuff to do, so just freaking do it.” Jason: “Yes sir Mr. Kennedy, It wont ever happen again sir!” (((With that Jason takes off at a sprint. Malcolm laughs to himself as he lays down on the hammock sideways, leaving his feet on the ground.))) Malcolm: “Dumber than a pile of freaking rocks. Good worker, just dumb.” “Anyways, I suppose you’re here to see what I have to say about my upcoming tag match right? I mean, why else would you be here? Check up on my supposed ass acne?” (((A muffled laugh from behind the camera))) “Laugh it up clowns! Get a real good chuckle. Listen, you guys are paid to listen to what I have to say and to record it, if you don’t do that, you don’t get your pathetic 30 dollar pay checks. So shut up and listen, or leave.” (((The laughter stops.))) “Thats much better.” (((Malcolm uses his legs to push himself back and forth on the hammock.))) “So this upcoming SAS will feature The Prime Alliance taking on Raid and Reo Dai. Whoopty Doo! This will make the headlines. Look, me and Ryder have already proven that we are without a shadow of doubt that we are 100 times better than these two clowns. We destroyed Anarchy, and we will destroy them again even if their line up is a little different.” “This is all just one big waste of time. The Prime Alliance is not a joke. Yet we are treated like one. Well that will all change soon enough. I’ve got big plans for me and Ryder, and Soon enough, you will all see what I mean.” “Soon, the UWF will see what the Prime Alliance is really made of. We will walk through every person who stands in our way, and leave them a bloody stain on our path to glory. We will place our names among the greats. We will leave our mark so that we are never forgotten in the UWF. We will go down as the best of the best in history of the UWF. We will be mentioned with greats like Jay Phoenix and Tsunami Oni.” “We have sat back in the shadows long enough. Is time for the Big Dawg and the One Man Army to step back up. Time for us to put our names back at the top of the later where we so rightfully belong. At SAS we shall make short work of Raid and Reo Dai. I intend to teach you both a lesson in pain, and make examples of you both. The Prime Alliance will no longer be holding back on anyone. Our goals are simple, Inflict pain, and win matches. And trust me when I say that, that is exactly what we are gonna do from now on.” (((Malcolm gets up from the hammock as a mild breeze begins to blow. He walks passed the camera and back towards his beautiful home. The scene fades to black as Malcolm walks through his back yard.)))
