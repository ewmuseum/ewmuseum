UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #671

To early in the morning

5 Oct 2004

Adien Pryde — Hotel

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a hotel hallway. The camera comes to a stop in front of a door and a hand reaches out and knocks. A voice can be heard inside.] “Huh,…….What the fuck, its 7 in the morning. Who the hell is it?” 'Snap' Meyer : 'Snap' Meyer here with the UWF. I have some questions for you. “Who the hell is 'Snap' Meyer, never mind, let me get some clothes on.” [A couple of minutes pass before you hear the locks on the door being worked and the door opens to revele co-Founder of Chaos Society, Adien Pryde.] “What the hell do you want, do you know what time it is, I have jet lag from hell and you are knocking on my door at 7 AM. Snap : I have a few questions that have been brought up about weather or not you deserve to be in the main event. Both Plague and Oni have stated that you should just stay out of the way. Here is a clip from my last interview with Oni. ['Snap:' But he's not your only opponent. Adien Pryde is also there. Oni: Yes, but why? I really don't care about him or his little Chaos Society antics. And he certainly is no threat. My best advice to him is to just stay out of the way. He's in the main event, don't embarrass himself.] Snap : Do you have any comment? “No threat? That will be the day. Sure this is my first shot at the main event but that has nothing to do with my ability. I has everything to do with the veterans at the top like Montgomery, Kennedy, and Dunn that never give the new comers a chance to show what we got. Dai and I came into the UWF as a tag team but when Persia took my partner out with a leg injury that gave me a chance to prove something that I have put on the back burner for the better part of 6 years. To prove that I can hang with the top dog in the singles matches, and tonight is just that chance. In the same ring as a UWF Hall of Famer. Can’t get any closer to proving my ability than that.” Snap : Is The Chaos Society going to be at ringside? “My tag team partner in crime will be there now that he is ready for his come back and my ever present trainer Joanna will also because that is where she belongs in my corner. But as for Meaner and Raid, Raid has his THC number one contenders match against Almasy, Persia, and Slasher to worry about and Meaner, I don’t even think he has made it back to the states yet, he had some problem at customs.” Snap : Any suggestions for our viewers. “Yes, Watch Sunday Night Supremacy tonight because a new UWF superstar is about to be born.” [Pryde begins to close the door then stops.] “One other thing, Ever wake me up at 7 in the morning again and you will get much worse than you have.” Snap : Much worse than I have, what do you Mean by that? [A just as Snap turn to leave, a size 12 boot connects with his chin knocking him right out. The camera turns to show Reo Dai looking satisfied with himself.] “Like my main man said, I am back and ready to leave the UWF in Total Anarchy.” [The scene fades to Black]
