UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #530

What Legends Are Made Of

12 Jul 2004

The Communist

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist sits on the weight bench in the Peoples Gym: What is a Champion made of? I have a cause, I have the power, I know I have the skill, what could I be missing? I have desire, I have ferocity, what do I lack? I should be dominating, but I am failing. I am missing something. :he watches the moniter before him, watching the moves of his opponents. No, not opponents, emenies. His Prey. The Enemies of the People. Enemies of the Proletariot. Enemies of Communism.: I know what I lack. The Edge. I am missing the unnamed thing that makes Champions. The Spark, the Need. I have never NEEDED to be a Champion. I've never NEEDED the Belt. Any belt. :hmph: I even said I'd give up the TV title for a chance at revenge. Vengence. Such a petty thing. Never lasting. What did I gain when I won my Vengance? The TV title. And I knew pride. Pride like I'd never known before. Wearing that belt made me feel... It made me feel like a man. Like I had justified my place in the Federation. And as quickly as I had it, I lost it. Why? Because I didn't have the edge. I lost the belt. :His face turns red and his hands clench, Breathing grows heavy and he shakes with the rage of lost glory, known all too well by all wrestlers: I formed my Stable for Stable Wars. I was prideful. Overconfident. I thought that our pure ideal would take us to the Cup. I thought that Mori would be the last piece in a perfect puzzle. I thought that the Soviette would make a difference. I thought that the aged wrestlers we faced in the first... the ONLY match we were in would matter. I though wrong. We were anhialated. We were destroied. But I learned. I learned that I was the most powerful among them. That I could have taken ANYOTHER MAN in that ring singly and prevailed. Definatly my worthless 'teammates'. And that So Called Super Squardon of Doom. Even Corporal Punishment. The Corporal. He taught me something. Something very very important. To win, you must be willing to do ANYTHING. A lesson I learned again when I faced Jay Vyper. After the Dirty Snake choked me with a chair, beat me with a 2x4, a baseball bat, even beating me against a steel turnbuckle, he had to jump from the top of a ladder in the ring and elbow drop me through a table on the ground, causeing us both amazing pain. He had to gamble that he would recover fast enought to pin me. He had the edge. I need to find that edge. I need to be willing to sacrifice my body, my career, my very life. I must be more dedicated to my title than the State. I must me more dedicated to my title than Even Communism. :The Communist sighs, exhausted physicaly from his workout and now mentaly from scouring his soul for the edge he needs: Ironic. I must dedicate myself to Championship to raise Communism to its rightful place in the world. I cannot dedicate myself to both. Choose... Communism, or Championship... I must choose Championship. I must sacrifice for the belt. For the better of Communism, I must focus on Championship. As I said, Ironic. :The Communist pulls his Red Book, the Communist Manifesto, written by Karl Marx, the manuscript held with near Religous reverence. He looked to it, almost pleadingly: What would Marx do? Karl Marx would sacrifice everything for the good of the Party. What is good for the Party is gaining THC. To gain the Hardcore Championship, I must focus on it. :He rises and grabs he scyth off the wall, he begins carving in the unadorned stone wall across from him: What would Marx Do? He would sacrifice. He would be agressive. He would be Powerful, Skilled, and Ferocious. He would Dominate. He Would be Champion. :Communist steps away from the wall, admiring his work. His fingers bleeding from the effort, the scyth bent and broken in his hand: It is my time. It is Communisms time. No... It is THE COMMUNISTS TIME!!!!! :The Communist walks away from the wall, the camera turns to the wall, showing the letters Scratched deep into the stone surface. WWMD:
