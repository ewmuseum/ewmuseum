UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #156

A Clockwork Wizard

18 Dec 2003

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open upon…well, darkness. Lots of it. Very dark. Eventually Beelzebozo’s face comes to us out of the dark. His red and black painted face is lit from below, making him look more daemonic than ever. Although the bowler hat is an odd touch…and the white shirt and suspenders… Beelzebozo: “What say you, my viddy droogs?” His eyes slide to the left, and the lovely Pyretta Blaze materializes from the inky depths behind the satanic harlequin. Her hair is braided, and…it appears she’s wearing a blue and white gingham dress. His eyes slide right, and The Quiet One appears behind his other shoulder. He appears to be wearing a silver funnel on his head, and his facepaint is silver rather than the normal white. Blaze: “Viddy droogs?…” Beelzebozo: “Yes! It’s time for some of the ultraviolence!” Blaze: “Honey…what in the Samuel Langhorne HELL are you on about!?” The clown turns his back to the camera and looks at his friends. His shoulders slump. Beelzebozo: “#$@#.” Blaze: “Oh no! I thought we were doing the Wizard of Oz thing today! I mean, it was on this weekend…” Beelzebozo: “But what did we just watch last night?” Blaze: “I know, but I didn’t think you’d have a costume ready that fast!” Beelzebozo: “And, really…who the hell’s gonna be scared of Dorothy and her little friends? I mean, come on…Alexander and his droogies were the scourge of their world! Just as I shall be the scourge of the weak and unworthy here in the UWF!” Blaze: “OK, when you put it like that…” Beelzebozo: “Well, this thing’s shot now. I suppose we should just forget it and hit the road.” He turns. Blaze: “Aw, come on honey! Let’s go ahead and have some fun with this!” Beelzebozo: “I don’t wanna!” Blaze: “Look, we rented these goofy costumes, let’s at least put them to use!” The clown shakes his head. “Aw, come on…where’s the fun-loving, ready-for-anything clown I fell for?” The clown turns his back to her, and we can see he is rolling his eyes. Beelzebozo: “When have I EVER been fun-loving?” Blaze: “What about that trip to Disney World? Remember how you jumped into the water to save me from those nasty bubbling hippopotami?” The clown smiles a little. “Remember how we got the princesses to turn the luncheon into a burlesque show?” The smile widens. “Remember how we laughed as we reenacted the Salem Witch Trials with the help of those Pilgrim-looking people in the Colonies?” Beelzebozo: “OK that was fun, I must admit.” Blaze: “See, you know how to have fun. So let’s have some now, OK?” She rests her hand on his shoulder. “For me?” Beelzebozo: “Well, I could never refuse a lovely lady. All right. What did you have in mind?” Blaze: “Follow my lead.” She clears her throat. “So…you’re not afraid of anything at all?” Beelzebozo (after a pause): “Nothing!” Blaze: “What about that nasty Ruff Ryder?” Beelzebozo: “I’ll step on him like a little spider!” Blaze: “What if you again face Kennedy?” Beelzebozo: “I’ll pin that punk down, one two three!” Blaze: “And what if it’s that weirdo Moraless? Beelzebozo: “I’ll just show him the clown’s the best!” Blaze: “What if Fagen faces you?” Beelzebozo: “I’ll turn him into Gilchrist Stew!” Blaze: “If Ezra Kazee stands in your way?…” Beelzebozo: “For such affronts I’ll make him pay!” Blaze: “Joshua Dunn may be your foe…” Beelzebozo: “I’ll thrash that bitch from head to toe!” Blaze: “The Master of Bunnies might face you too!” Beelzebozo: “I’ll…er…hold on. There’s a Master of Bunnies in this thing?” Blaze: “Yeah. Laguna, Master of the Bunnies.” Beelzebozo: “……OK, that is the most @$#%ed up thing I’ve ever heard of. And I’m an insane devil clown living in a hellish playground of unearthly delights with a pyro and a mime.” Blaze: “That was my reaction too. But I guess when he’s not wrestling he’s helping out a tag team made up of craploads of rabbits.” Beelzebozo: “They really WILL let anyone into the UWF, won’t they?” Blaze: “Sure will!” Beelzebozo: “Wow. You know, that’s so crazy it’s almost cool. I kind of hope he faces me later in the tournament…he sounds like he might be my kind of crazy.” He nods. “Yeah…Laguna…I shall remember that.” He claps his hands together. “Well, we’ve had our fun, now shall we run?” Blaze: “OK, my lovely little droogie!” Beelzebozo: “I know all my opponents think they’re going to have an easy time of it with this tournament. I’m sure they all dream of greatness. Well, they may find that greatness someday. But it’s not going to be in this tournament, I’ll tell you that much right now. I’m not taking this thing lightly, not at all. In fact, the more I think about it, the more I think that maybe I DO deserve the World Title. I love hardcore wrestling, but hell, I can win that anytime. This is a special opportunity, and I am going to make the most of it. I am going to make a huge splash at this show, and I’m not just talking about the splashing of my opponents’ blood. I will show everyone in this federation that Beelzebozo is not a name to laugh at, but a name to scream at, to soil yourself at…to tremble with terror at. “When it all comes down to it…I will have the Last Laugh.” With that, the three turn and vanish into the darkness once more.
