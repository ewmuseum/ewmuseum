Maybe this isn't the best time...

4 Dec 2004

The Communist — Soviette's Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:Everyone's Favorite Communist knocks on the door of him managers office, clutching a small box to his massive chest. He waits a moment, then hears Soviette's voice calling him to enter. Commie pushes the door open and steps inside: Commie: Good Morning Comrad... Soviette: Ah, Communist, just who I wanted to see. We need to talk. Fandamonium is tomorrow. You need to step your training up a few notches. I have planned some hardcore drills, ladder climbing, and a little tag team training. Also, I've set up a walkthrough of the Venetian home so you can get a feel for it before your match. We need to hurry, there's not much time left. The faster we get started the better. Commie: Well, Yes, about Fandamomium... Soviette: We also have a brief on your opponents, going from the long illustrious carrer of Digital Mortality, to the short disappointment of Killer Dude. And all points inbetween. I think the hardest match will be the VHR. Mostly because of the sheer number of people involved, but I think you can handle it, even with your Bourguasis partner, as long as he doesn't betray you. But that doesn't seem likely. Ironicly, you and him have a similar sence of honor, dispite your opposite political alignments. Commie: I was thinking you could... Soviette: I was thinking that even after the Pay-Per-View, you and Josh Justice might still be tag partners. Of course, I still need to bounce the idea off the Council, see what they thing, wait for it to be debated for a few weeks, then we'd get an answer. But by that time, you and him might even have a victory. Who knows. It's amazing the lengths the Council goes to, expanding the idea of what communism is, it's hardly even Maxism anymore. Commie: I have... umm... something... Soviette: What are you holding? Commie: I was hoping you'd notice... *nervously* You see Soviette, all the other managers are wearing special outfits for Fandamonium... Uhh... what... what were you planning on wearing? Soviette: The red buisness suit skirt I always wear to the ring... *her eyes narrow a bit* Why? Commie: Well... I... um... was, you know... looking around. and... umm, I uh, saw a costume... I mean OUTFIT... that I was thinking... that ... I'd ASK you to wear to the... umm... Ring... with me. please? Soviette: Show me. :The Communist sets the box on her desk, opening the top and shows her the outfit: Soviette: You can't be serious. Commie: Wait wait wait! You see.. it'll be a... TACTICAL ADVANTAGE! :Soviette looks sceptical: tactical. Advantage. Commie: You see... Ummm... :Making this up as he goes along: Sence Digital Mortality and Killer Dude don't have Managers, If... you wear less cothing, they may be... distracted. :Commie smiles, impressed by hisown ability to think on his feet: Soviette: I'll think about it. Commie: good, good, that's all I as. Soviette: Get out. Commie: Yes ma'am. :he pushes the door open and slips out of the office: :Soviette looks the outfit over, sighs and rubs her eyes:
