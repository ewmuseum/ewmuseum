FAN#3 - The Time Has Arrived

4 Dec 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The shot opens with the logo of Killer Dude filling the screen, only this time, there is text written underneath the axe, saying 'The Time Has Come'. As the logo fades, the scene opens to a shot of the streets of Las Vegas, apparently where the card is being held, as nobody actually knows these things these days. The camera pans from left to right, looking around for something in particular. All of a sudden, we hear an engine revving in the background, and we see that Killer Dude has just pulled up in a 1968 Dodge Charger. He opens the passenger door, and begins yelling at the cameraman in disgust) Killer Dude: Are you getting in or not?! I have so many better things that I could be doing right now, but no, some people just don't know when to quit making fools out of themselves. (The cameraman quickly jumps into the Charger, slamming the door behind him, as we actually see Killer Dude for the first time. Killer Dude is wearing a black t-shirt with the Fandemonium logo on it, reading on the back 'See you in hell bitch', and blue warm-up pants. A pair of pricey black sunglasses sit on his face, doing their job quite nicely. Killer Dude stares out of the driver side window, looking at a homeless man across the street) Killer Dude: You know something, it's actually kind of funny. You know, the way things can change in a blink of the eye. Just this morning, when I got here, this guy was sitting at a craps table, living it up, laughing, all in all having a good time. Now look at him, sitting there on the street corner, begging people for money and food and the like. He even offered a blowjob to Hot Mask, and I think he took the offer, because they both walked into a back alley together. It's actually rather pathetic. You see, he failed to realize how high the stakes were in those kinds of games. He got in over his head, and as you can see, he ended up paying the ultimate price. That reminds me a lot of the people who I will facing tomorrow night at Fandemonium. The Communist, Digital Mortality, Craig Wright, Hot Mask, Dreammaker, Joshua Justice, and Adien Pryde? You know, all of these wrestlers are alike in one way. They do not realize what they have gotten themselves into. They do not realize that I don't fight for petty things like wins and losses, no. I deal in titles, I deal in money, and I deal in the ending of careers. They are special to me, because they are the ones that I will make examples out of. Through these individuals, I will prove to everyone that I have what it takes. I will prove to everyone that I walk the walk, as well as talk the talk. Dreammaker likes to talk about how no one cares about me, I'm not good enough to be in the ring with the likes of him. Well, Dreammaker, I'll tell you right now that you are wrong. I AM ABOVE YOU IN EVERY SINGLE FUCKING WAY POSSIBLE!! When I play, I play for keeps. As for their useless babbling all week long, I tell them all to bring it. Bring the fucking pain bitches, because I'm gonna bring the carnage. They are going to need all the help that they can get. It's too bad, really. No matter what they bring, the outcome will still remain the same. It will still end with everyone laid out in the ring, staring into the lights, and the pinfall is counted, and I walk out the champion. They will be hurt so badly, that they will be unable to kick out, and I will be laughing the entire walk back up the ramp. (Killer Dude turns his attention to the road as he presses down on the gas. As the smell of gasoline fills the air, the car begins speeding down the street. Killer Dude does not find the time to speak during the ride. After driving a few blocks, Killer Dude makes a very sharp right hand turn onto a side street. Killer Dude continues driving for a few minutes before pulling into the parking lot of a small bar. Killer Dude puts the car in park and opens the door) Killer Dude: Let's go, I'm supposed to meet Punk Rock in a few. I'm gonna get a beer. (The cameraman exits the door as well, and walk slowly in to the bar. As they walk to the back of the bar, some fans shake Killer Dude's hand and ask for autographs. However, Killer Dude does not notice them, and continues past them coldly. They take a seat at a small table. A waitress comes over to the table) Waitress: Hello, what can I get you today? Killer Dude: I'll have a Corrs Light, and he'll have a...... Camera Man: Nothing, I don't drink. Killer Dude: Don't mind his babble, he'll have a Budweiser. Waitress: I'll be right back. (As the waitress leaves, the cameraman turns to Killer Dude) Camera Man: Budweiser?! I hate you man! Killer Dude: It'll be good for you. You need to learn how to live a little. (The waitress returns to the table with their order. She sets the glasses down on the table and walks away. Killer Dude takes a large gulp of his Corrs Light as he motions for the cameraman to start drinking his. The cameraman quickly takes a sip of the Budweiser, and begins violently coughing at the taste. Killer Dude begins to laugh hysterically at the cameraman's misfortune. He looks back into the camera) Killer Dude: Did you see that waitress? God damn, she must have been 50 years old. I hate people like her. You know, the people who don't know when it's time to move on. The kind who try and recapture their glory days, even though they do not deserve it. It reminds me a lot like Digital Mortality. He comes back to the UWF, and I see that he is a former UWF Champion, one of the greatest of all time. Is that supposed to mean anything to me, Digital, because right now, it means jack shit to me. The letters UWF and the words Heavyweight Champion do not mean a god damn thing. As far as I am concerned, they are nothing but three letters in the alphabet that Richard Hayes just decided to string together. As for being the World Heavyweight Champion, who cares? How in the hell does that increase your chances against the greatest up and coming superstar this federation has. I could sit here all day and name different titles that I've won in my travels, but I'm not going to. Do you know why, Digital? I'm not gonna name them, because they make no difference, here and now. But I'll humor you for just a minute, Digi. You have decided to come back and make your mark in the Universal Wrestling Federation. So what happens? You do nothing but fail horribly. You sit around in the back, and dissapear from people's minds just like that. I can't blame you though. If I was as overrated as you are, I'd stay as far away from the public as I could. There is one thing however that you did not take into account. I don't play well with others, especially cheap knockoffs of real talent. So, Digi, even though I don't like you very much, I am gonna give you one last chance. Pack your bags, book your flights, and go back to Puerto Rico because if you don't, you will leave me no choice then you put you back there forcefully. (Killer Dude lifts his Coors Light to his mouth and finishes it in one gulp. As he waves the waitress back over to the table to order another drink, a drunken man stumbles over to the table. The man looks at Killer Dude in a drunken manner) Drunk: Yer Killer Dude, correct? I'm yer biggggessssst fffaaannn! Killer Dude: Is that so? Well good, now get out of my face. The stench that you are making is gonna make me puke. (Looking dejectedly back at Killer Dude, the man stumbles away as the waitress arrives at the table with the glass in hand.) Killer Dude: I hate the drunks in this town, they always come up to me, telling me that they're my biggest fan, when I know that they know nothing about me. They probably don't know anything other than the fact that I wrestle for the UWF. It kind of reminds me of the all mighty communist god, The Communist. You see, The Communist likes to pretend that he actually understands what he is saying when he is talking about you. He likes to act like he has an intellect far beyond his reach. The Communist is nothing more than a two-faced bastard. He talks to everyone as if they are lower than him, as if they are not even on his mind. In reality, The Communist only does thing for one person, only looks out for one person..... himself. There is nothing more pathetic than a bully. Unlike a real man, a bully is rarely ever able to back up what he says. This reminds me of all of my opponents. They are all like that fat kid that attacks kids and makes them give him their lunch money everyday. Strangely enough, this is just another attempt for them to fit in. A way for them to be part of the crowd. They have entered the UWF to little fanfare. They have had their chances. And they will get another one tomorrow night. And once again, they all will fail miserably. I have taken down men, bigger and stronger and a hell of a lot more talented than you all, without blinking an eye. So you can talk all you want about how you know how to use 'cats' in the TLC Match, and how you need this victory. Because all the time for talking is over, and it's time to make good on your words. See you in hell bitch! (The usual ending plays, as the scene fades to black after the logo shot)
