Underground this Sunday!

July 23,04 (year unrecorded)

Justin Morris — Charlotte NC

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
/The scene opens up right out side of Comfort Inn on West Park Drive in Charlotte North Carolina. Hardly anything is visible through the buzzing traffic heavy fogg. The camera man wipes down the lenz a few times, and quickly makes it down the C.I parking Lot. The camera man(panting and out of breath) moves his heavy set body as fast as possible to catch Morris before he leaves for his flight. Finally we reach a jet black BMW that just pulled up in the parking lot. A slim and casually dressed man, about 5 ft' tall steps out of the car, adjusts his tie, and slicks back his jelled down hair./ Chip(The BMW guy): Are we rollin' Dave? Dave(camera man): Yep... There's a bit' of a problem though... Chip: What? Dave: No sign of Morris so far... Chip: Dammit... F.S.N probably did this! Dave: Huh? Chip: He's probably still at the damn convention center... Dave: Doing what? Chip: That's where they host Slam T.V now. Dave: Oh... Well uhhh... Shouldn't we go down there and get him? Chip: The damn producers from HCW el' get pissed. (he looks down at his watch.)We still have like ten minutes left. Dammit, dammit, dammit! I don't have time for this kind of sh*t! Dave: Well, look... Get out your equipment and we'll set up in the lobby. Chip: Yeah but... We're already cut back 5 minutes. Shouldn't we just reschedule? Dave: Not on my time. Chip: Alright then. / Chip pulls out a suit case from teh back and they both rush into the Comfort Inn Lobby. After waiting for almost five more minutes, Morris busts through the swinging doors and preps for the interview. Finally everyone is ready. The interview begins./ Chip: Hi wrestling fans! I'm Chip Tallus here with HCW star, and UWF newcomer, Justin Morris. (He turns his direction to Morris). Well, I guess were here to talk about you three way match on Underground this Sunday. Any comments before we get started? Morris: Well, I would like to mention that I'm very pleased with the match setting. I've seen Underground plenty of times, and I've seen Supremacy plenty. Out of the two I'd have to say that I like Underground better. Chip: Why? Morris: Look at the match set up for this Sunday. It looks a lot more entertaining to me. Chip: Okay then... For the first question. What do you think of Roxx? Morris: Well, I usually don't comment on other wrestlers... But why not? I think this guy may be a bit of a challenge. BUt you know what? Chip: What? Morris: I'm not worried at all. I've been in this biz' for a while and I've out wrestled the best, and I've out smarted the strongest in the World of Pro Wrestling. Chip: Ummm... This other guy... How do you say his name? Morris: It starts with a J right? Chip: (he looks hard and examins the paper.) Yeah. Morris: Uhhh... Jueki I think. Chip: Yeah, that's it. Jueki Kutabare. What do you think about him? Morris: Obviously the tougher apponent of the two. I have to say that I am a little worried about this guy right here, but he's nothin' that I can't handle. Chip: Any stradegys? Morris: Heh... Youwon't me to give out my strategys? Sorry boss, but I can't do that. I will tell you that I have a few tricks up my sleeve though. (Breef laughter). Chip: Any hopes to get to Supremacy soon? Morris: Sure... I mean, everybody wants to climb the latter and reach the top, as do I. I'm not in a hurry though. I'm taking this thing one at a time starting with these two. Chip: (he turns back around to the audience) Okay then folks, I guess it's time for me to wrap this up. I'm Chip Tallus, and this has been an interview with the rising star, Justin Morris. / Chip gives a brief smile and waves as Morris turns around and heads out the doors, to a taxi. The scene fades out./
