CoC #3

20 Dec 2003

Joshua Dunn — UWF Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Again Joshua Dunn gave us a mystery tour of his head, missing objects flying into plain sight, the figure of one man, his father. What has become of this, no ones quite sure, but Dunn himself remains normal, or how we have come to know as “normal”. Otherwise Dunn seems in top condition going into the Contest of Champions. Representing the revival of the Players Club, still all to prove, still the respect he deserves to be acknowledged, The way it’s always been, always the underdog, never the favourite, but always a house hold name] [The Scene Opens in a gym, more precisely the UWF Gym. The camera looks through the deserted room in the complex, racks of weights and pillars of punching bags litter the Gym at regular intervals, and the treadmills and rowing machines stand like solitary beasts waiting for their next victim. In this Jungle of keep fit, we can hear a noise, just off screen, grunts followed by a series of thuds; the camera pans round to examine this. Laying into one of the punch bags is Joshua Dunn. He is wearing his red T-Shirt and black jogging trousers; his gold chain with the communist symbol on it bounces off of his chest every thrust he takes at the punch bag. Sweat stains the T-shirt in an uneven ring that spreads down his front and his back, white tape is wound round his wrists. His head gleams in the lighting, every now and again a bead of sweat drops off from his face and lands on the ground in an inaudible splash. His eyes alive with fire, pent up aggression is finally getting an outlet, and pumping up Dunn as he does so. The world’s original anger management treatment. One of those burning eyes flick away from the target for a moment and focuses on the camera, his mouth twitches and looks back to the punch bag, when it falls towards him again he catches it and steadies it before moving away, breathing deeply to catch his breath, he makes for a towel that has been left on a near by bench and mops his brow, and takes a swig of water from the plastic bottle also on the bench] [After tipping back a considerable volume of water his pulls the bottle away from his mouth, letting out a gasp of satisfaction as he replenishes the liquid he lost through his exertions. He sits down on the bench and continues to mop his brow with the towel as his breathing steadies] [Breathing with the towel on his face for a moment, Dunn throws it aside and sits back, feeling refreshed. And gives the camera man another hard stare] JD: I have a lot to do you know, it’s not like I make time for you people, do you expect me to do a promotion at your beck and call? [Dunn just keeps staring into the camera screen, trying to stare it out, he gives in and sighs] JD: All right, I take my business here in the UWF very seriously, I’m usually here training even when I’m not expected on a card, although that is a rarity [The irony in that last statement hits the camera just as Dunn averts his eyes from it, thinking on his annoyance at that point. He refocuses on the camera and continues] JD: I find it quite absurd that I have had so many title defences since I won the IC title, and I find it even more annoying that Ruff Ryder, the man looking for someone to shut him up has only had two defences in his reign. Well at least I can hold my head high and admit that I haven’t had people skipping me out of matches to prolong my title reign, and as a result there is hardly anyone on the UWF Roster who can say that I haven’t beaten them. [The retribution sets in as Dunn leans back and folds his arms for a moment, letting the fact sink in that he is, as always, right] JD: Of course the “convenient” arranging of the Contest of Champion Card is just another fact to prove what I mean. The Prime Alliance members, presuming you take you fingers out of your arses, wont meant each other until the finals, where as there will only be one Players Club member from the semi finals onwards. [Instead of the look of distaste that you would expect, Dunn’s face actually breaks out into a wide grin, obviously something works to his favour, and he’s probably going to tell us what] JD: Suits me and Ezra fine, We go head to head for the umpteenth time, we enjoy it, as we know better how we each work, so making us a better team, unlike the ramshackle bunch the Prime Alliance are. Also, if things go well, Either of us will get to have a shot at both members of the Prime Alliance and say, without a doubt that we beat them fair and square. Either way we win, we either succumb to the odds that have been placed against us, or we over come them, if we lose, we suffer no shame. The Prime alliance however… [A large Grin crosses his face] JD:… Well you get the idea As for the other people in the contest, you can’t count them out just yet, Beezlebozo has impressed me so far with the showing he’s putting in, at any rate I would find it hilarious if Ruff Ryder were to lose to him in the first round after all that crap he said. Good luck to you Beezlebozo, I’m sure I’ll be hearing a lot more of your name after this Pay-Per-View is over. But at any way, I’m starting to waffle now, and if you excuse me, I have some training to do [Dunn gets up, rubs his right arm for a moment, and again begins to pummel the punch bag, not expecting to get much else from the Scotsman the camera backs away and the Scene Fades to Black]
