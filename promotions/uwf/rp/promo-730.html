UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #730

Maester of Misery

10/03 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Church, #2 for LiL IV

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Necessity. It is, of course, the mother of invention. Or, in this case, re-invention. Seymour Almasy has once again assumed his most evil form. As a maester of Yevon, he will use his faith to challenge and destroy those who drove him to this point. Sucks to be facing him, doesn’t it? --------------------------- Inside the ruined church, all is quiet. If possible, it appears even more shattered than before. A thin coat of sawdust covers everything in the dilapidated building. On the dais lies the Divine Blade Imperial II. Seymour’s faithful sword is broken in two, hilt lying on one side of a fallen body, blade on the other. Seymour Almasy: Unnnnnghhh…. Slowly, the Final Fantasy begins to rise. His bare chest and torso are covered with cuts and bruises, in addition to the Roman numeral tattoos that now adorn him. In his hand, in the place of the Divine Blade, he now holds a long staff, a staff as tall as he is. Seymour Almasy: If I am awake, it must be complete. After hours of prayer, my god has heard my cry. He stands, shaking the sawdust from his body. His staff appears made from the finest cherry, and as such, is difficult for Almasy to hold. Seymour Almasy: This simple piece of wood is now imbued with the power of Yevon himself. Before such a weapon, a mere sword pales in comparison. This is why the Divine Blade Imperial II lies, broken…just as my adversaries shall now be. The stave is placed gently upon the ground, as Seymour searches for a more comfortable seat. After quite a bit of looking, he finds the least damaged pew in the church, and sits down upon it. Seymour Almasy: Yevon has informed me that one of my opponents, at least, has been quite busy. Yes, the man best known for gluing pubes to his head, Killer Dude, has decided that it is time for him to commit suicide. I’m sure he doesn’t see it that way…but in insulting Seymour Almasy, he has shown a complete lack of regard for his life. The Maester grins, balling his hands into fists. Seymour Almasy: Killer Dude has the audacity to question my bravery. To question my ability to destroy this pitiable place on its own. Killer Dude, I ask you…have you ever tried to conquer? Of course not, you’ve been too busy gluing pubes to your head and teaming with golf clubs to try and conquer anything. For that reason, I will inform you of why I won’t simply charge ahead like a blithering idiot to try and conquer this shithole. Almasy shakes his head at what he feels to be Killer Dude’s blatant ignorance. Seymour Almasy: Because, all of you peons would try and stop me! I may be a Maester of Yevon and the greatest superstar ever to grace this jerkwater federation, but even me in my divinity and with Yevon’s grace can’t fight twenty of you at once. That is why I need allies. But believe me…I alone am more than enough to destroy you, Killer Dude. However, I am feeling benevolent. You can have the THC Title. Truly, I do not CARE what happens to The Hardcore Championship. Because, somehow, your pea-sized Rabite-like mind managed to get one thing right. I AM above the THC Title. I am above the Intercontinental and North American championships. But do you know WHY I want them? Do you, peon? No? Once again, it seems as if I must enlighten you. With a dismissive wave of his hand, Seymour continues. Seymour Almasy: I wish to have held every title in this land. The only man to do is the idiot known as Jamie Shock. Therefore, it is evident to me that this distinction needs some class. I can lend the Grand Slam that class. Two titles have not yet been risen in the air triumphantly by my hand, and those are the Intercontinental and North American titles. One of them, preferably the Continental, will be coming home with me at Live and Loud 4. Almasy stretches out, his slight frame taking up one of the sections of the long wooden pew. Seymour Almasy: And then, once the championship is in my grasp, I will be honored as the latest entrant in the UWF Hall of Fame. This is my first year on the ballot, but of course, my credentials speak for themselves. Even this hellhole understands good talent when they see it every once in a while. And thus, my Live and Loud 4 ends on an even better note, with a title and a Hall of Fame spot. Ahh… He pantomimes a pillow beneath him, relaxing comfortably on the pew. Seymour Almasy: A perfect end to a perfect year. A year in which I recaptured my PTC Global Title, and held the UWF World Championship for longer than anyone. Even with the GTT4 tournament going on, I still have more than enough energy, time, and thought to devote to Moraless, Kenny Mercury, Misfit, Killer Dude, and Nick Persia. Of the five of us in both this match and the tournament…only Persia and myself remain. Once again, Mercury proves that he can’t live up to the hype…let’s all be sad for him. And considering that the North American champion Moraless lost too…that forces me to regard my major threat in this match as…Yevon, I can’t believe I’m about to say this…Nick Persia. I feel dirty now. Seymour shivers slightly in mock disgust. Seymour Almasy: We have little in common, Persia. We’ve both held a title longer than anyone else in UWF history. We’re both in the third round of the tournament. But while you’re busy losing to Mr. Darkness, I’ll be beating Joey Vendetti…oh, and destroying you with three ladders en route to becoming the UWF Intercontinental Champion. But like Killer Dude, there is salvation for you. You might even become the North American champion at Live and Loud 4. That’s right, you could become the second highest ranking title holder in the UWF! Above even me with the Intercontinental Title! Of course, there’s one small, minor, eentsy-weentsy catch… The Maester of Yevon laughs, loud and well. Seymour Almasy: You see, I got tired of the THC Title rather quickly, which is why I agreed to this little triple ladder match. It’s only a matter of time before I get tired of the Intercontinental title as well. And when I DO tire of it…I will be coming for the North American champion so that I may complete my collection of nice, shiny, and golden belts. And if you’re the North American champion at that point, well, then, it sucks to be you, doesn’t it Persia? Sitting up on the pew, the THC and PTC Global Champion nods his head once, twice, three times, before grinning. Seymour Almasy: Once again, Yevon is on my side. Soon, the Intercontinental Championship will be mine. And the Universal Wrestling Federation will bow before me for forcing me to this extreme. Life…is good. He stands, striking an evil pose, with his arms crossed over his chest. It is an imposing sight, despite Almasy not being the largest person in the world. Seymour Almasy: Soon enough, everyone shall learn the perils of crossing Seymour Almasy. It is a lesson that men from other ends of the Primetime Central universe know well, and yet, the superstars of the UWF have forgotten it. Evermore shall they now be reminded. There are three months left in 2004. In them, I shall leave no doubt that I am what I was in 2003, the best wrestler in the Universal Wrestling Federation. And it all begins at Live and Loud 4, as I stand atop a ladder, Intercontinental title raised high. You have all created this. Tuesday night…you will come to understand why to contend with Almasy is to lose. He says no more, returning to reclining on the pew, with dreams of glory in his mind. To him…the fans and wrestlers of the UWF started this. At Live and Loud 4 would be the beginning of the end.
