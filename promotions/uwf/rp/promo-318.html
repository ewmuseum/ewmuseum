UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #318

Stars and doubts

7 Apr 2004

Nick Persia — Lockerroom

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Persia is in the lockerroom changing his clothes. His mind is somewhere else though. He is in deep concentration and has a very serious look on his face. In his mind he is busy with his lost match last week. He is wondering what went wrong.] **Why.....why? Why in the blue hell did I need to lose that match? What in the hell did I wrong? This is definetely not a good sign going into the pay per view. Jesus Crhist, even my grand father would have done a better job against Iron Lung. Yet, I should have done somethign right since I have this important hardcore title match against Plague coming up. But he must see me as a joke by now. The loser of a match from last week’s SAS will get to face him. Not a good thing, certainly not. I have to make sure people get faith in me again. I have to make sure my presence is felt. Too long people have been wondering what I can do. Toolong people questioned my abilities as well as my skills. I am watched by a hell of a lot people, and still people seem to recognise me, but don’t dare to say anything to me because they are not completely sure. They are not sure of what I am made of. Even Plague has no idea of what I am made of. How hardcoore is Nick Persia? I’ll show them how extreme I am. I always prefer the name Extreme over Hardcore. Hardcore is this music style, a stream within the trance-act. I am not in trance. I am not in for a dance. I’m in to kick some ass. Yet, when I will come out and I am announced as The Invader people will be laughing at me. What am I going to do about it? I’m going to send them a message. What kind of message? I will show them the true face of a true sportsman. I will show them the class of a true Dutchman. I will show them the pride we take in our work. I will make them beg for mercy. I will take the Hardcore title. I will show.......** Somebody knocks on the door, but Nick Persia doesn’t seem to notice it. But after knocking two times on the door of the lockerroom, he gets a little but frustrated. A sigh of disgust soon trespassed the air, offending the frustrated Nick Persia. He walks towards the door and opens it woithout warning. The reaching arm of the reporter misses the door, and almost hits the face op Nick Persia.[/u] Nick: “What in the hell are you doing here?” Reporter: “I am here for an interview.” Nick: “Where is your backstage pass then?” Reporter: “Wait a minute......I have it right here in my right pocket.” The Report searches for the pass, but doesn’t seem to find. The time passes. Bu each second, the frustration on the face of Nick Persia grows. Nick: “Hurry up, I haven’t got all day. I have an important match coming up later on.” The reporter akwardly searches in his other pockets. After a minute, a long one for Nick Persia, he finds it between hsi blocknote. Reporter: “Here you have it.” He hands him the Backstage pass with a relieved look on his face. Nick Persia: “Go ahead and ask what you need to ask.” Reporter: “How is your mind set going into the Rennaisance pay per view extravaganza? Nick Persia: “Physical I am in a very good condition, but mentally I don’t feel like I normally do. You see, I have been fighting two wars at Rennaisance. First of all I am fighting to give my name mu dignity back. Second of all I am fighting to take The Hardcore Championship. So I set two goals for the pay per view, and I promise you, they’ll be fulfilled. Reporter: “What do you think of your opponent?” Nick Persia: “He is the champion. He isn’t champion for nothing. But when that bell rings, all the respect I have for him will be gone. I will forget everything I saw from him on Television. I will forget everything, but hitting him. I try to take him down. With all means neccesary. Wether it is a table or a chair or maybe even a kendo stick.....I can’t give a damn as long as I take what is mine.” Reporter: “I never thought you were hardcore....” Nick Persia: “I am not hardcore. You’re completely right! I am extreme. I am taking things to the limit. For way too long people wondered who I am and what I can do. Now I am at the place. Now I am there in the bright spotlight. You didn’t actually think for a second here I am going to let things slide here? I am going to take that belt tonight, and when I do so I may be bleeding, tired, beaten up and a hell of a lot more, but I will be THE undisputed champion. The Plague is the current hardcore champion, I will be the new extreme icon. I will be the new Icon. I will be a force to be reckoned with. The Invader will honor his name.” Reporter: “Your first title is just a matter of formality?” Nick Persia: “Yes, and for Plague punishment is just along the road. I will hit him as hard as I can with malice and without warning. With his blood I will sign his death warrant. I have enough from The Plague. You are going to infect me? Go ahead, but don’t be suprised if I resist. My resistance will be way bigger than your futile attack.” Reporter: “The Plague is no new guy. He has a lot experience. Are you up for it?” Nick Persia: “What do you think? Of course, I am up for it. I am all ppumped up and excited about my first title as well as my first pay per view match here. It’s always a special moment for the wrestler himself.” Reporter: “So this the dawning of a new era?” Nick Persia: “Not only the dawning of a new era, but the dawning of the nex extreme icon as well.” Reporter: “Why do you keep calling it hardcore whilst it is the hardcore title?” Nick Persia: “Because I say so. Deal with it.” Reporter: “Don’t you think that’s bad news for UWF?” Nick Persia: “You have no idea what bad news is. It’s bad news for The Plague that I am going to take THC-belt. It’s bad news for the UWF that The Invader has chosed to come to this company. It is bad news that I am a though SOB. It’s bad news for the UWF that I will be the next extreme icon.” Reporter: “Obviously you can talk the talk, but can you walk the walk?” Nick Persia: “Take a look at me. 220 pounds of solid steal and sex appeal. I’m just too good to be true. But I am true.” Reporter: “It’s going down tonight, any last comments?” Nick Persia: “I have an opportunity to take the belt, and it is my job to sees that opportunity. His catch phrase says I need to be prepared. Well, I am coming with everything I got. We will find out if that’s enough. Big hurray for Plague. Look at the competition back then, and come on… what’s so great about hitting each other with two-by-fours and flaming, spiked boards rigged with explosives, just to name a few of the preposterous weapons available? Anyone could become Hardcore champion. In my opinion that is one of the most easy to capture titles out there Since I am easy, I am going after that title though. I am ready to explode. I am ready to go at it and prove my point.Wether people agreed with my views or not, they appreciated me. The appreciated the fact that I stood up for what I believed in. Can you say the same Plague? Can you say straight out without lying you are here to wrestle and you’re not in for the money? Can you tell me what’s the difference between you and me? I can tell you. You have been busy with everything I am war, I am pain I am all you've ever slain I am tears in your eyes I am grief, I am lies I am pure, I am true I am fear in the night” Reporter: “Thank you. It was a pleasure.” Nick Persia: “At least one person was having fun.” The Reporter leaves the room and Nick Persia is left behind with his thoughts. He continous to change clothes as he is now wearing his pants. He truns on the radio with a nice volume. We hear Nirvana with Smells like teen spirit. Nick Persia (talking to himself): “Rest in peace Kurt and Plague. Maybe we will see a true Renaisance. Maybe we will end this swell up of an old period. Maybe times do change. We’ll find out, Plague. Very soon. Time has changed, opponents as changed. Now your destiny will change. You have been bragging about being the hardcore champion, that you won’t be denied? I will not deny you. You are an old hero Plague. Old heroes don’t die. They just fade away when time passes. Compared to me you are just crumbs on the table. That’s the gospel, and I always speak the truth. At Renaissance I will shine liminous, everything else is simply lidicrous.” Screen fades out into the UWF Renaissance commercial.
