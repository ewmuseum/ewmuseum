UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #665

Reliving a War

3 Sep 2004

Ezra Kazee — Washington Monument

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in front of the Washington Monument, The monument, itself, extends high into the sky, towering over several other building and monuments. The camera pans down toward the bottom of the monument. As it comes farther and farther down, the ring of American Flags that surround the monument can be seen, as well as the line of people waiting to get in. It continues to pan down and standing there, a good distance from the Washington Monument is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a gray shirt with the letters USA across it all colored as if it were overlapping an American flag. His black hat is turned forward, so to shield his eyes from the bright son of the day. He stands there, proud, looking directly into the camera.) Kazee: It is quite marvelous here, isn’t it? It is quite a sight to be able to look at the monument to commemorate the first President of this great country. And it is great to see all of the flags that stand around the monument flying in all their glory and flying so proud and free, much like the people of this great country…… That flag can mean many things to many people. It commemorates the freedoms we have in this country. Our freedom to express our opinions. Our freedom to worship however we please. Our freedom to assemble as we may want. And our guarantee that we will never be oppressed by the government……. The flag stands for all the people who have died to give us what we have. All the men and women who have fought for us. All of those who would gladly stand up again and fight for those same beliefs again. It stands for the sacrifice of those men women and children…… It stands for the American Dream. The belief in life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness. It stands for how this is the land of opportunity and the land of freedom. The land that every man is allowed to choose their own destiny, no matter the circumstance. The land that we may choose to be successful and take the chances that will put us there, or that we may toil around and do nothing and thus live our lives that way…… And this week, I will have the right to fight for the flag. This week, I will join all of those men and women who have fought for those freedoms and done what they could do to defend the flag in all of its glory….. This week, it wills almost a flashback of sorts. A flashback to one of the most stable eras ever to grace the world. A flashback to where there was only one worry for anyone. That worry was when it would happen. Or would it happen. Would the button be pressed on accident? Would the end of the world happen by a fluke? Or would the final war just come in the Great War between ideologies known as the Cold War…… The warfield, a wrestling ring this time. The prize, a flag, this war will cause no causalities. The war will cause no deaths. This war is only a war of peace and athleticism. And in the end, one nation’s flag will be flying high over all the others. In the end, win or lose, I will salute whichever flag is flying higher, and I will be sure to respect it in any way that it happens. In this classic battle, I and the Communist will go toe-to-toe. The Communist is a great athlete. A former THC Champion. A big man with unlimited potential. A man who can go and beat anyone whenever they want. And I will have the honor of defending that American Flag against him. Good Luck, Communist. (Kazee turns around, salutes the flags, and the scene fades to black.)
