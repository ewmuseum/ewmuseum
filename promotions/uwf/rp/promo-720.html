UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #720

Preparing for the 6-Way

28 Sep 2004

Killer Dude — Killer Dude's Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens as you see Killer Dude and Punk Rock, one a member of the New Black Star Revolution, and the other, a close friend of the former, are seated at either side of a fold out table. You can pretty much figure out right away that they are playing a rather shitty game of Poker. Punk Rock wears his $5 sunglasses, and Killer Dude has on a New Black Star Revolution t-shirt, and a backwards cap. After a few moments, Punk Rock lifts his head up and smiles) Punk Rock: Royal Flush, baby! (Killer Dude suddenly jumps up and flips over the card table, seemingly pissed off with his hand) Killer Dude: Why do I always get the fuckin' 7-2 off-suit. DAMN IT ALL TO HELL! This game absolutely sucks my left nut. You keep getting Flushes and Straights, and I keep getting 7-2 off-suits. Punk Rock: I'm gonna have to agree with you on that one, Killer, Texas Hold 'Em really isn't my cup of tea. Yo man, have you seen all the shit that's been going down recently, man? Killer Dude: Oh you damn right I have! A 6 way match, contesting for 3 Titles? What dumbass thought up this match. Oh yeah right, it was probably Persia, Misfit, and Moraless. The 3 fuck buddies all coming up with a plan to screw over the New Black Star Revolution. Well, guess what? You guys make think that you will get one over on the New Black Star Revolution, but that's not the way I see it going down. Hell, you guys aren't even that smart. Hell, all you do is brood around in the back. And you know what that means, Punk? Punk Rock: What? Killer Dude: They're goths. And I like to play Whack-A-Goth. As you could see the last couple of weeks. Punk Rock: Uh, Killer, I usually get your wierd ass comments, but this one's gone right over my head. Killer Dude: Sorry man. Let me show you the tape. (Killer Dude shows Punk Rock the tape of Mercury and himself going medieval on Misfit and Nick Persia. At the end of the video package, you see that Punk Rock and Killer Dude are picking the card table back up, and chuckling to themselves at the same time.) Punk Rock: So this guy, Misfit, he sits there, does what he does, in his little corner of the backstage area. I wonder if he's thinks that he is Brian Rafferty? Remember that kid from high school? Killer Dude: (summoning a really 'evil' voice) He is the master of his domain! Punk Rock: Yeah right! (The two burst out laughing once more) Killer Dude: So, what would that make Moraless? Chris Choina? Rafferty's bitch? Which would then make him Misfit's bitch? Punk Rock: And Nick Persia? That stupid kid that used to lick himself, and hung out with those two? What was his name again? Oh yeah, that's right. Sean Ferris. Killer Dude: Well, whichever way you want to put it, they are fast becoming a royal thorn in the New Black Star Revolution's asses. And as you well know, Mercury and myself will be facing off against Moraless, Misfit, Nick Persia, and the ever strange individual himself, Seymour Almasy. Punk Rock: Looks like somebody got real busy, real quick. Killer Dude: Looks like after Kenny and I teamed up, I jumped straight out of the frying pan and into the fire. I can't wait to beat up Persia and knock that sneer right off of his fucking face. The New Black Star Revolution do not like seeing crap on our screens, and Nick Persia is just that. A stinking pile of crap. Hopefully at Live and Loud 4, I can remove him from our sights...... FOR FUCKING GOOD! Punk Rock: You tell him, Killer! Killer Dude: Then, on to Moraless. This is a wierd one. You see, Moraless, you lost all respect from me when you cheap-shotted my bad knee last week. And for that reason, I'll also be gunning for you on Sunday, and don't think for a second that Kenny doesn't want a piece of you either. He told me that he'll be gunning for your ass too, bitch. And then, on to Misfit. You see, you are the main reason that I joined up with Kenny. You see, you made Kenny the way he is today. You sent him over the edge, and that my friend, is the reason why the things that are going to happen to you at Live and Loud 4 aren't just the things you've heard about in dreams. I am going to make them a reality. You will feel pain that you have never felt before. You all will find out that you don't want to fuck with the New Black Star Revolution. Because if you do, the things that appear in your nightmares will become reality. Things that you never dreamed could be possible, will become possible. Watch your backs, all of you, because the New Black Star Revolution expects no mercy, and we will give you none back. VIVA LA BLACK STAR REVOLUTION, BITCHES!!!!!! (Killer Dude and Punk Rock sit back down to their game of cards and Punk Rock begins to shuffle as the scene fades to snow)
