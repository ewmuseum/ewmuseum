The Sensation of The Nation

June 29 (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — Portugal

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nieuwe pagina 1 {Who would have ever guessed where we are now. Thousands and thousands of enthusiastic fans are partying, celebrating the victory Holland has had. They beated the team of Sweden in the quarter finals by a penalty serie. The past five times they lost by penalty series so it was an extra special win for not only the Dutch soccer team, but for the Nation as well.} {The Sensation of the Nation is for Holland definitely Holland. Those 23 select people are now the hero's of the Dutch society. Maybe, and I say maybe, Nick Persia will be able to gain even much fame by becoming the sole survivor in the Stable Wars event. That would be something special.} {But let's be realistic here, because we all know that is NOT going to happen, not matter what. You see, The Player's Club is just too strong. When you look at each member of the Club you can see why they are a member of the Club. They all of their specialties and weaknesses. But there is a MAJOR difference with all those other teams when we look at The Player's Club: Nick Persia} {Nick Persia is one of the few to have stayed true to his believes, laying the foundation for a new standard in UWF. He has been very honest with what he is going to do. he always spoke the truth. There are quite a few people who have said bad things about the Dutch Destroyer.} {Don't think The Voice didn't notice what the people have said about him. He was the tail of the talk as it appeared. The spotlight was aimed at him. The Invader has made his impact suprising everybody from the top of the ladder to the foundations of the federation.} {People of all ages, please be aware that the comments being made in a few moments are not suitable for Children of all ages and people who are easily offended. If you are in one of these categories or both I suggest you turn off and save yourself some time. (damn, who said this one day?)} [The scene fades in at the football stadium in Lissabon where we just saw the end of the thrilling quarter final between the upcoming team of Sweden versus the old Force Holland. The old force still holds his defense. Fans are going nuts as we suddenly see Nick Persia covered in Orange appearing out of the arena with a smile ofn his face. He looks happy.} 'Sign of things to come, don't you agree? Holland just pulled off what they were supposed to do. They did what was expected from them. Although they had to put in everything they hard they accomplished the mission. Come Wednesday I will do what is asked from me.' [Nick Persia is still walking as he is surrounded by the thousands of fans.] 'Just like Holland I will overcome the semi final syndrome. It's something that goes trough your head, even when you don't want to think about it. It plays in your mind every time you are in the semi finals. Yes, this semi finals are the first round matches as well, but still.' [The fans scream when they see the huge scream on the market garden when the face of Dick Advocaat appears.] 'I hear a lot of people talking about The Ressurection, Chrono Cross and a lot of different opponents. For I can care about them we need to be up for another task. The Chaos Society. Some people are underestimating them and I think that the most stupid thing you can do.' [Nick Persia turns to the right, seeing a Church appear behind him. It looks like an old, medieval church with a very high tower.] 'Adien Pryde is a guy I met before. Remember? I do. We clashed off for my UWF THC Title. I won the stretcher match after we had a gruelling battle. A hell of a lot of people looked down at our great encounter. But the win was something beautiful, very beautiful.' 'Especially when you realise I won after all the terrorising he did to me. He had me thrown in jail. He attacked me on numerous occasions from behind. I bled due to him. I suffered due to him. I lost due to him. He kept stalking me and he knocked me down to the mat time after time. But I keep my mind clear. I didn't let the anger didn't take the better of me. I picked up the victory because I was the man with the ability to keep my eye on the ball.' 'Nowadays I see a lot of people talking about me. About the word I have spoken. They can beg to differ with me all they want. They can disagree with me all they want. They can go out there and say I am a liar. But you can't deny the truth and we all know I always speak the Truth.' 'I am not a replacement, Malcolm (would like to be) in the middle. I am a full member of the Club. I am the only one to have a belt in my possession. I am the only won in the Club to succesfully retain my title week after week. I am the only one not to forget to talk about first round matches.' 'Most of you are underestimating the force of your opponents. You all count on your OWN power. However the problem is you should focus on how much of a unit your team really is. Look at your team and ask your self the question if your team really is a team. Don't look for silly excuses and throw them out on Tv like the all of you have been doing. Look deep down in yourself and you will see what I mean. You will see that your team isn't a team.' 'The Chrono Chross for example. They are all loners. Hell, Seymour has made one appearence in UWF after he so-called returned. But he unitied himself with Corporal Punishment, Kenny Mercury and Richard Vaughn. They can barely put their differences aside when they are just talking to each other. I don't want to know what happens when they are actually wrestling together. They are fighting Stable wars for their own good. Not for the good of the Stable.' 'There are just three teams fighting for the good of their stable. The Chaos Society have shown their dedication together and now they will face us, The Player's Club. But we are a unit as well. We are more than up for the task. We have had all the preperation we needed. We studied some matches, by VCR (just ask me next time how it works, Corporal). We noticed quite a few things and we know what your weaknesses and strenghts are. With that in mind we made a game plan.' [The camera turns to the left and we see some people standing before the traffic lights. they turn from green to Orange and the people start to cheer. This is the ultimate example of the Sensation of the Nation.] 'But you have two matches at one night at Stable Wars. So I need to look a bit further than the semi finals in case we win. Of course there is a fairly large possibility we will advance. We can face so many teams I won't say something about the all of them, but I will say something about some of them.' 'First of all I would like to address something to the man who beat me last week: Corporal Punishment. I give you credit for what you did. You looked very focussed and you were really intense. I admit you were the better man at the time. I don't know if I didn't had my day, but that doesn't really matter. In wrestling you have to be in top condition every day of the week. No matter what.' 'You said I was just little Nicky. You said I couldn't play with the big boys yet. Then please tell me if it is luck or if it are my skills I am holding onto this belt for quite some time now. I will break the record, and you know it. I did what you failed to do. Is that why you took a break?' 'Now I know you beat me so you will probably ramble on the fact you could have taken that title from around my waiste. The answer is no. because in title matches you must handle with the pressure as well and when I hit you it's perfectly legal. And I have a lot of reasons to throw a chair in your face. I have a lot of reasons to smash you into oblivion. I have a lot of reasons to hate you. I have more than enough reason to Voice my opinions about you.' 'You can say The Player's Club is weak. But do NOT ever say we are no match for you. You had a hard time handling me. You will have even a harder time handling the rest of our team. Just look at them. All warriors who will sacrifice themselves for the team. One for all, all for one. That's how we are thinking. Not one for all like Chrono Cross.' 'And I am not the only shimmer of hope for The club. No, I am not a desperate attempt to bring back the former Glory of the club. I am here to bring my own strenghts. And I needed a spot myself so I was invited into the Club. They gave me a favour and now I will give them the favour back. You see, there is no rebuilding needed in UWF.' 'The Resurrection didn't take all our titles. Look at my hardcore title. LOOK it's still around my waiste. Because they are not superior to us. They claim to be superior. All words, but we all know you can't deny The Truth. The gold is in The club and the gold will stay in the club.' 'Sure, we have been forced to make some sacrifices. But it didn't really make us weaker. because title don't say everything. Sometimes it means more if you are carrying a prestigious name. You can be reckoned as the top dog in the federation but you don't need to hold the world title to be the top dog.' 'It all depends on Opportunities. The Player's Club was never given any of them. We always earned our place on the rankings. We did everything underhand possible to become the number one. Let's cut to the chase here we are the most talked about stable. And why would you talk about people standing loer on the ranks than you?' 'That's why I don't talk about you. Damn Digital Mortality is the past. He is irrelevant. Moraless might be PTc Extreme Champion, but in his own fed he wasn't even able to win the hardcore title. Simply because he can't beat me. In other words my title means more than the PTC Extreme Title. And I won't be waisting any air time on Malcolm who thinks I am the only hope. The fool. Bondage the Sex Friend is a circus act, not a wrestling act. He should change his profession.' 'I will let you continue your talk about how you will reshape the UWF and all that crap. Quite frankly, we can't give a damn. Because your words mean nothing. Just like all those never beens you will go down in history as a nobody.' 'The Reflection of Perfection is The Player's Club. We are a thight unit. We are a force to be reckoned with. We are the top favourites. We are the ones with the world and hardcore title. We are the ones who will regain all of our titles back. I am not aiming for a title right now.' 'Therefore people keep asking me why i should compete in Stable wars then. Like I said a moment ago I shall sacrifice myself to the team. I will give them a chance to get their titles back. And I want the honour of being the sole survivor. Watch out because I am coming after you.' [The scene fades out as we see the thousands and thousands of Holland fans chanting and rooting for The Dutch.]
