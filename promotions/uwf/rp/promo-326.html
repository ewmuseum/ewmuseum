UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #326

Happy Easter

11 Apr 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens up in a very familiar place. We are inside of the shed that is known as the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. The Christmas lights are blazing, the two titles are placed on respective mantles on either side of the shed. And sitting at his “desk” with his head lying down is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. There are thousands of colored eggs around him, along with assorted candies and such. In the background are several more shelves, all of them decorated with the various UWF action figures. Under Kazee’s left arm is a giant pink bunny rabbit. Kazee is snoring fairly heavily.) Cameraman: (Kind of quiet) Excuse me, Mr. Kazee? (Kazee just lies there and continues to sleep) (A little Louder) Mr. Kazee? I’m here to do your interview. (Kazee is unbothered by his sleep. The camera man reaches out an arm in order to gently shake him) (Normal voice) Mr. Kazee, wake up dude. (As soon as the camera man touches him, Kazee, bolts awake, and some of the colored eggs go flying off the desk as Kazee accidentally knocks them off. As they fall, they crack, only instead of being the normal boiled eggs one would expect for the holiday, they are still raw eggs, and yolk and white are laying all over the floor and on the cameraman’s shoe as he pans down to see it.) Kazee: What’s going on? Who woke me up? I’m awake, I’m awake. Cameraman: (A little hesitant) Um, Mr. Kazee, I’m here because you requested my presence for an interview. Kazee: Oh yes, that’s right. I must have dozed off last night when I was coloring all of these eggs for the children I expected to see here today. You know how much I try to do an outreach effort for the kids on behalf of the UWF. I mean it would only make sense as the Manager of the Shareholders Branch…… But I’m sure your not here to talk about that. I’m sure you wanna know what my reactions are to Renaissance and to the next SAS card and all of that kind of stuff, am I right? Well, you know I don’t mind tell you all of that…… So let’s see, where do I start. How about I start with the tag team title match from Renaissance. I mean, wow that was some great competition. And they almost had us. I mean honestly, I don’t know how much I really can say. It was a great match that almost saw us loose our titles, except for the turning on each other by a couple of people. Just goes to show you that sometimes you have to work together if you want to achieve anything. Of course, I wouldn’t expect someone outside The Players Club to know that….. Between people turning on each other and people eliminating themselves, what can I say. It was a great time, another one of those matches I’m unaccustomed to, but in the end, it all worked out. Monty and I walk away with the Tag Team Belts, everyone walks away with nothing, and my first prediction comes true. Advantage, Players Club…….. Fast-forward to the second match that evening. The TLC match for the IC belt. The match of the evening, as I told everyone it would be. Three men, Raid, Moraless, and myself, all vying for the IC title in what would become a landmark match. A match that seemed to steal the show. Even with the interference by Anarchy…… My hat’s off goes to you two. My body still hurts from that match. My body is still trying to recover from going through the tables. Going through the punishment. But you know what. It doesn’t matter. I enjoyed every single moment of it. A match that was definitely out of my element. A match that was definitely out of what I’m use to. And like the cream of the crop always does, I rose to the top of the heap. I rose to the occasion and showed what I was about. Just like my Commonwealth, I didn’t give up, and I showed people what it was all about. What it is all about to do what it takes, to use your heart, to never give up, and to let it all lay on the line. The respect and the principals that have gotten me to where I am today and will continue to get me to where I am going. But definitely mad props to Moraless and Raid……. But as I said I would do, I climbed the ladder of success, and I recaptured my IC belt. I went out of my element to win it. Prediction number 2 came true. Once again, advantage, Players Club…… What can I say, I’m feeling very good about my predicting ability right now. Maybe I should be consulted by Jimmy the Greek. I think my prediction abilities are so much better…… But then that brings me to the low point. And Digital Mortality seems to have a way of ruining things for people. And he has aligned himself with Moraless. What can I say. Well, there is a lot I can say, but I’ll reserve judgment for now…… You see Digi, the fact of the matter is that you forget sometimes what I am about. Your right, I’m at the IC Title level right now. Could I challenge for the World Title? I suppose I could. Do I want to right now? Not really. Joshua Dunn is there right now. Joshua Dunn is the man who is going after the world title. I know this is a foreign concept to you, but it’s about being a team player. And being a team player I have no reason to want to go after Dunn’s spot as the man challenging for the world title…… Time to hang up my gloves? Well, probably not. You see, I’m only 26 years old. I’m half the age of some of the people who are out there wrestling today. And at this point, I can only get better, and that’s exactly what I will do. I’ve got my friends, I’ve got my mates, and I’ve got my compatriot. The Players Club right now is flying high. With Monty being named the new commissioner of the UWF, what else can be said. And with Monty standing by my side as half of the UWF Tag Team Champions, there is no way things can go sour for us…… Yeah, your right, you have beaten me before. That was then, this is now. I was young and green in the wrestling ring. I was a multi-time TV Champion though. I won the TV Title several times, and now, I have added a duel championship to my resume’. Of course, Digi, you have been laying low for some time, out of the loop for a while. I’m that much better, you, are still you. I’ve risen in the ranks, you have not gone anywhere. You’ve been out of the game. The question is how long does it take for the rust to shake off? On SAS, you have a match with your buddy Moraless against The Players Club for the Tag Team Titles. Is this something your ready for. It’s a tall challenge for your first match back. It’s a tall task for being out of the game for so long. You’re going to have a great fight on your hands, but in the end, as much as I hate to predict it, The Players Club will once again rein supreme…… The Golden Age of the UWF as you call it, well, I’m glad you remember all of that. That age though is over now. And it’s time for the new age of the UWF. It’s the age where the fans are important, and the age where it’s all about everyone. This is a new age, not your age Digi. If you want to live in the past, I have all the tapes. If you want to hate the UWF, then please, by all means, do so. But not on my time are you going to tear the UWF down to its foundations. Not on my time is anything going to happen to this federation…… Moraless knows what he’s up against. You, Digi, don’t know the combined forces. You don’t know exactly how far I’ve advanced, how much more I’ve done, or where I am. Your debut match, will be a debut match you may want to forget. It may be something you may never want to remember. But either way, I shall wish you good luck, because you will need it….. Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have to be at the VFW for the Easter celebration. (Kazee grabs a large basket and starts putting in the colored eggs with the candy. He raps the IC Belt around his waist, and then the Tag belt, just lower than the IC belt. With that, he starts walking away from the office as the scene fades to black.)
