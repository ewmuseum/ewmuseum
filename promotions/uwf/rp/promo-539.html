UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #539

An Unexpected Guest

13 Jul 2004

Kevin Starr — Boston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Starr finally got what he deserved. It took a bit of intense planning, some precise timing, and a little bit of perfection, but Kevin Starr managed to pull it off. He stealthily snuck in to the top floor of the UWF Headquarters in downtown Chicago, where all the heads of the federation reside at when there’s not a card. He was able to sneak all the way up to Lt. Commissioner Kenny Suck-ury’s office and there, he found the official UWF Rule Book. Unfortunately for Kevin, there was one minor flaw to this “Plan of Perfection” as he likes to call it, and that was that he left his freshly open pack of pencils in his other pants… ugh… they were bought just a situation like this to. Anyway, luckily for our hero, an unexpected guest arrived behind Kevin Starr, in this man’s right hand was the key to getting the World Title shot, unfortunately this man was none other then Joshua Dunn. Surprisingly, Joshua Dunn tossed the pencil over to Starr, apparently he’s out for revenge as well. Kevin Starr quickly scribbled over a strategic part in the rule book, then right when he was about to turn around to beat the living hell out of Joshua Dunn, he was gone… sneaky little snake! The night was everything but over for our hero, he quickly hopped on his own private jet to Springfield, where SAS was taking place, and arrived just in time to make his announcement and~! He even had time to have a nice quiet nap in his locker room before the Main Event where… well, nevermind. Still, the fact of the matter is, Kevin Starr has a World Freaking Title Shot! [It was a dark Monday night, real dark in fact. The only thing that can be made out inside of Kevin Starr’s apartment home in Boston, is his Little Mermaid Night lamp sitting next to his bed. Yes that’s right, Kevin Starr is fast asleep. He’s had a long day… a few hours at the tanning booth… a French manicure… plucking the eyebrows and clipping the nose hairs… yes, a tiring day indeed.] [The only other form of life inside of his apartment, besides the delicate lives of plants living in Kevin’s spice garden, is Mike Michael, sleeping on the black leather couch in Kevin’s living room. Mike’s having a hard time getting to sleep. He’s basically trying to force himself to sleep by jarring his eyes shut and trying to find a comfortable position, but he’s not tired. All the caffeine from the 6 pack of Coke he drank earlier is still having it’s effect on him. Finally, Mike’s patience have run thin, he pulls himself up to a sitting position on the couch, having thoughts about pulling an all-nighter… by himself, kind of sad when you think about it… well I guess not when you just can’t get to sleep… but still.] [Mike sits up on the couch and opens his eyes... nothing. He tries to wait for his eyes to adjust to the darkness, but still, he can see no more then an inch in front of his face.] [Suddenly a small rumble can be heard. Mike looks down at his empty stomach and decides to search the fridge for a way to satisfy his hunger. He slowly pushes himself off of the couch and cautiously walks across the room, making sure to not bump in to anything or worse, stub a toe. Man that sucks. Stubbing a toe is like the worst pain in the world, I think, even worse then paper cuts.] [Well Mike successfully makes it across the living room floor, all 10 toes still intact. He steps on to the cool white and black checker tiles on the kitchen floor. Still, Mike is walking is blind, he decides to turn the kitchen light on. He carefully reaches over to the wall and uses his hand to search for the switch panel. After a few touches he finds it and swiftly swipes his hand upwards, catching the light switch, light explodes in to the surrounding area, lighting up the entire room.] *BOOM!* [As quickly as the light filled the room, Mike drops to the floor unconscious. What the hell happened? A black figure stealthily scurries over the motionless body of Mike Michael and in to Kevin Starr’s room.] [Obviously not sure of the layout of the apartment the figure abruptly stops at the door, trying not to wake Kevin Starr up. The figure gives a sigh of relief, realizing how close he was to screwing up his little scheme. The figure pauses for a moment and gazes over at Kevin Starr’s face which is lit up by his Little Mermaid night light. A small pleasant grin crosses the figure’s face as he turns to go back in to the kitchen. Suddenly, as the man is about to leave the room, Kevin Starr speaks out faintly.] “Hey.” [The figure stops at the doorway. His heart skipping a couple beats. The figure holds his still stance as best as he can, even trying to hold his breath, hoping he blends in with the darkness around him.] “What are you doing?” [The questioning voice of Kevin Starr strikes right though the heart of the man like a knife. The figure can’t believe it, how can Kevin see him? Different thoughts begin to race his mind… Should I run? Or should I just stand here completely still? Try and play it off? The figure, still questioning what he should do in his head, slowly turns his head to Kevin Starr, keeping the rest of his body still. He uses his peripheral vision to spot Kevin Starr. Kevin’s eyes are still closed shut, how can this be? Kevin Starr speaks out again this time, showing the anger in his voice.] “Those are my cookies. Give them back.” [The figure releases his tension with another sigh. He’s just having a nightmare… well in Kevin Starr’s case, this is a nightmare.] [The figure proceeds back in to the shadows of the kitchen and out of sight.] [Camera fades out.] [It is now approximately 8 A.M.] [Kevin Starr opens his blue eyes, squinting as the early morning light is a little too much for him to take in all at once. Kevin sits up in his bed and rubs his eyes. He then begins his early morning ritual by exercising his voice as he likes to call it.] Kevin: “*AHEM* La, la, la, la, la. Do, Re, Mi, So, Fa, La, Ti, Do.” [Kevin’s always had quite the singing voice. Thanks in partly to his looks; Kevin can make any woman drop to her knees in tears while singing the lyrics to “Amazing Grace” with his amazing singing talent.] [Kevin finishes his ritual and hops out of bed. He begins to strut over towards the kitchen, on his way over, something catches the corner of his eye. He stops quickly and looks over, and there he is… there standing in his Hello Kitty PJ’s, 6’4” 233 pounds, is the man himself, Kevin Starr. Kevin turns towards the mirror and makes two guns with his fingers and points them at the mirror. He speaks in an admiring tone directed as his reflection.] Kevin: “Stay Beautiful, baby.” [Kevin continues on towards the kitchen towards where Mike lay on the floor with a white piece of paper taped to his face. The figure must’ve left a note for Kevin.] [Kevin finally makes it to the kitchen, but it too caught up in examining his own finger nails to even notice Mike on the floor.] Kevin: “That son of a bitch! She cut my nails too short! That… That Whore!” [Kevin does however manage to step right over Mike on the way to the refrigerator while cursing out the lady who gave him a manicure. Kevin grabs the fridge door and swings it open. The door of the fridge now blocks the view to Mike Michael. Kevin carefully looks inside the fridge, trying to decide what he’s going to devour next.] Kevin: “Hmm…” [Finally, Kevin settles on the half empty jug of milk and brings it up to his mouth. He closes the door shut and guzzles down some of the milk while turning back towards his room.] [As Kevin finishes the 45 degree turn towards his room and his eyes finish focusing, he spews the milk out of his mouth in all directions, shocked. His eyes widen… he takes a few steps back as his lip begins to shutter up and down…] Kevin: “…Oh… my… Rod…” [Kevin raises the milk jug and looks at it in disgust.] Kevin: “This milk is sour! Ugh! Disgusting! Better go brush my teeth.” [Kevin puts the cap back on the milk jug and lunges it behind him over his head with such accuracy, that it manages to find it’s way in to the trash can. Perfection at it’s best. Kevin keeps walking towards his room as if the amazing feat of throwing a half full jug of milk in to the trash can, 20 ft behind him, without looking was nothing.] [Finally, as Kevin reaches Mike, Kevin looks down to his feet as his jaw drops. Kevin bends down to pick up a quarter.] Kevin: “Cool beans!” [Kevin puts the newly found quarter in his pocket.] Kevin: “Wait a minute! Mike? Is that you?” [Kevin looks down showing concern for his friend. He lifts the note off the top of Mike’s face and begins to slap his friends face back and forth trying to help him regain consciousness. Kevin obviously had no idea with what happened last night. Finally, for the first time since the incident happened, Mike slowly opens his eyes to see Kevin Starr leaning over him. Mike shakes his head a little before sliding back and sitting up against the wall.] Kevin: “What the hell were you thinking? Scaring me like that. I was getting ready to call 911!” [Mike looks around confused, not remembering how he got to the kitchen floor.] Kevin: “Mike!... Mike?” Mike: “Ugh... what happened? How did I get here?” Kevin: “Hey, I asked you first!” [Mike looks around, memories from last night suddenly surge back in to his brain.] Mike: “Well it’s a little blurry, but this is what I remember. I was trying to sleep when I got hungry. So I decided to get something out of the fridge but…” [Mike suddenly stops… the rest of the story is still one big blur.] Kevin: “…but?... Did you trip and fall or something?” Mike: “No... I turned on the light and…” Kevin: “…And…” Mike: “I remember looking up scared… there was a guy with a black ski mask on and then after that I don’t remember anything.” [Kevin slumps down to the floor next to Mike, not knowing how he should feel. Should he feel mad that the guy broke in to his apartment? Relieved that he didn’t himself in his sleep? Kevin looks around the room, trying to see if anything is missing.] Kevin: “You’re telling me that someone broke in here last night? I wonder if he took anything.” [Kevin quickly pounces up to his feet, walking in to the living room to see if any of his furniture is missing.] [Mike still sitting up against the wall, still shaken by what happened to him last night. Mike looks down next to him to see the piece of white paper that was taped to his head. From his seated position the only words he can make out are “To Kevin Starr…”. Automatically, Mike jumps on to the note and begins to read through it. From the living room, Kevin calls out in a relieved voice.] Kevin: “Well he didn’t take anything from the living room.” Mike: “Hey Kev, come here for a second. I found this note.” [Kevin walks over, still double checking his living room to see if anything is missing. Mike reaches up and hands Kevin the letter.] Kevin: “Let’s see…” [Kevin Starr reads the sloppy written letter in his head.] To Kevin Starr… It’s been awhile, hasn’t it? Anyway, meet me down at the docks and I’ll give you What you want Here’s a hint since you were never the brightest kid And it’ll probably take you awhile to figure it out You drink it and it rhymes with curry… sort of You’re probably wondering who this is… Well let’s just say I’m a man from the past… [The hint slowly begins to decode inside of Kevin’s brain. After using his investigative skills that he got from reading The Hardy Boys, he rushes over to his living room coffee table.] Kevin: “Please don’t tell me...” Mike: “What? What did it say?” [Kevin rushes up to his coffee table, where he usually keeps his prized Super Big Gulp Wild Cherry Slurpee that he won from Stable Wars. He looks up and down the table, but no slurpee! He drops to his knees to look under the table, still no slurpee! Kevin remains at his knees with his fans at his face. Thoughts of killing cross his mind. Mike rushes in soon after and looks at the empty table in disbelief.] Mike: “No… tell me didn’t take it.” [Kevin, still on his knees, raises his hands towards the ceiling and raises his head to the sky.] Kevin: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” Mike: “Oh and Kevin, now that I think about it, we’ve run out of Cool Ranch Doritos.” Kevin: “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” [Kevin Starr quickly gets up, the only thing on his mind is to go down to the docks and beat the crap out of whoever stole the slurpee, he quickly runs over to his closet and disappears in it, Mike stands at the edge of Kevin’s room, leaning against the wall as various types of clothes come flying out of Kevin’s closet. Mike questions Kevin looking puzzled.] Mike: “Umm.. what are you doing?” [Kevin replies all while searching through his massive size closet. Clothes continue to fly out of the opening.] Kevin: “I’m going down to the docks and I’m going to kill that son of a bitch who stole my slurpee!” [Mike rolls his eyes.] Mike: “It’s a freaking slurpee, Kev. I’ll give you $2.25 to go down to the store and get another one.” Kevin: “It’s not just the slurpee I’m mad about. It’s the principle of thing.” Mike: “Just forget it. We’ll get the cops down here and they’ll take finger prints, case closed. Besides, you’ve got the biggest match of your career coming up, what if this guy injures you or something.” Kevin: “The only one that’s gonna be getting injured is him!” [Kevin Starr finally waltzes out of his closet in a huge grey raincoat that covers his entire body, along with a matching grey hat with a black stripe running across it because Kevin Starr likes to stay in style.] Kevin: “Now if you’ll excuse me Mike, I’ve got to go down to the docks.” [Kevin tips his hat towards Mike and brushes by his shoulder on the way out of his house. Mike quickly turns to grab his coat.] Mike: “Wait… I’m coming with you. [Kevin stops and turns his head just before stepping out the door.] Kevin: “No, I’m doing this on my own.” [And with that, Kevin Starr walks out his front door to go catch a Taxi.] [Camera Fades.] [The heavy rain slams down to the floor as the wind howls through out the city. Boats, everywhere, have been chained down to poles and anchored so that they don’t carry out to sea. What is usually a flourishing part of the city, is now more similar to a desert wasteland thanks to the storm. Visibility is extremely low under the heavy fog, but we’re able to make out two headlights poking out through the fog approaching. A cab pulls up as Kevin Starr steps out after tipping the cab driver. The driver rolls off as Kevin stands alone on the docks.] [Kevin begins to make his way around the docks, holding tightly on to his hat so that it does not blow off thanks to the extreme wind. Kevin marches around, unable to find any sort of human life. His patience is slowly beginning to fade as thoughts of turning back start to cross his mind, but no, something deep down inside of him tells him to continue on.] [Suddenly, movement in the distance grabs his attention. He watches it as it slowly become bigger and bigger, through the fog now, we can make out another man walking straight towards Kevin. Kevin doesn’t bother to move, he knows the man sees him, so he holds his ground, waiting for the man to emerge through the heavy fog. The man stops, we can see his figure clearly, but other then that we can’t make anything else out of him. Kevin decides to call out.] Kevin: “Who are you?” [The man remains silent. Kevin calls out again but still the man stays quiet. Slowly, Kevin makes his way towards the man, keeping his eyes squarely focused on the man’s hands, to check for any type of weapon that he could use against Starr… or for the slurpee. Kevin calls out again, but still, nothing. He begins to grow angry as the tone of his voice changes dramatically.] Kevin: “Answer me Damnit!” [Finally the man decides to speak, his voice flows in to Kevin’s ears as it quickly registers in to his brain… he’s heard this voice before, but it’s still unclear to him of who it belongs to.] ?????: “You mean… you don’t even recognize me?” [Kevin Starr stops about 7 ft away from the man. He can now see what the man is wearing, a large raincoat like himself, and his face is covered in shadows from the hood of his raincoat. Kevin gazes up and down the man, trying to get a clue on who he is.] Kevin: “Who are you?!” [The man takes a step forward and throws his hood back revealing his face, Kevin’s eyes slowly widen. The figure replies in a soothing way, in a way that Kevin hasn’t felt since he was a child.] ?????: “It’s me Kevin, your Father.” [To Be Continued…]
