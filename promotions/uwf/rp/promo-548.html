Back in business

17 Jul 2004

Tsunami Oni — OMiNational Industries in San Francisco

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Twain once said that the coldest winter he ever spent was a summer in San Francisco. That rings true on this night as a chilling breeze cuts through the city by the bay. A limo pulls up to the curb near an ominous looking building, the sign outside reading 'OmniNational Industries.' The driver steps out and opens the door for a middle-aged man. The man seems suprised at the chill and rubs his arms as he stares at the building, trepidation in his eyes. He begins the trek to his destination. The driver starts to follow, but he is waved off. Alone, the man enters the halls of OmniNational Industries. The interior is deserted. The man checks the directory and finds his destination. Entering an open elevator, he sighs nervously as the door closes. His stomach fall as the elevator rises to the top floor. After what seems like an eternity, the penthouse is reached.He exits and walks down a short hallway, stopping in front of a pair of large oak doors. Swallowing, he knocks. 'Enter, it's unlocked. You were expected, after all.' The man gathers his courage, opens the doors, and steps in. He is in a living room tastefully decorated in a Japanese theme. In a glass case is a gold belt labled 'Champion of Champions. A manquin head wears a horned mask. A Komodo dragan dozes peacefully on the floor. And sitting in a divan is a blue haired young Japanese man, staring intently at him from behind glowing green eyes, a devilish grin on his face, which is painted white on the upper half. This is Tsunami Oni. He is dressed in a linen suit with no shirt. 'Please have a seat... Mister President.' George W. Bush takes a seat in a chair not far from Oni. The Vivaldi of Violence gestures to a bottle of wine and some glasses on a coffee table. 'Feel free to help yourself to a tasty beverage.' 'Actually, I'd really like to get down to business.' 'Oooohhhh...' purrs a female voice. 'I love it when a man talks like that...' A stunning young Asian woman has entered the room, her hair dyed a bright red that complements the short black silk robe she wears. She curls up onto Oni's lap like a cat and begins to nibble his ear. Oni makes the introduction. 'President Bush, this is Shiatsu, my paramour.' Shiatsu stops nibbling to look at the President. 'I also love a man of power. Too bad you're not one. We could've had fun...' Bush looks nervous as Shiatsu giggles at his expense. 'Now, now.' Oni chastices. 'This is our guest and we have serious business to discuss.' 'Booooooorrrrrring!' says Shiatsu with a roll of her eyes. 'Come find me when you're done.' Shiatsu hops off Oni's lap and starts down the hall. Before she diappears from view, she turns to Bush and casually unties her robe. 'Tell Jenna and Barbara that I said 'hi'' Shiatsu then vanishes to another part of the penthouse. The president is looking very frustrated, much to the amusement of Tsunami Oni. 'You will be able to stand at the conclusion of our business, won't you?' 'What?' asks Bush. 'Oh. Nevermind that. I just want what belongs to me.' 'You mean the launch codes to America's nuclear weapons arsenal? You really should watch what you leave in a bathroom.' Oni lifts up a briefcase and sets it on the coffee table. 'Look familiar, Georgie-boy?' asks Oni. 'Now, before you get too grabby, Shiatsu is placing a call to my banker. If our agreed amount isn't in the account I designated, she will transmit photos of herself with your daughters to several interested web sites.' 'You bastard!' the president exclaims. 'Hey, at least I'm not profitting off a war like your puppeteer is.' Shiatsu appears from down the hall. She hasn't bothered to re-tie her robe. 'We got loot from the coot, sweetie.' 'Excellant,' Oni replies. 'Our business here is concluded. Now please get the hell out of my home.' Bush grabs the briefcase and storms to the door. He stops and looks at Tsunami Oni. 'This isn't over! You haven't seen the last of me!' 'No kidding, jackass,' says Oni. 'You're the President of the United States. You're on TV all the time.' Bush doesn't reply. He simply exits quickly, slamming the door behind him. 'What a rude man,' Oni observes. 'I was merely requesting a finder's fee for returning his lost property. Ah well, perhaps you should transmit those photos anyways, Shiatsu.' 'Already did, sweetie,' Shiatsu answers as she sprawls onto a couch. 'Larry Flynt e-mailed me the nicest thank-you card.' 'Excellent,' Oni replies. He picks up a wine glass and takes a sip. 'Now I can concentrate on new business, such as my match with the Irish bastard.' 'Hey!' Shiatsu exclaims. 'I love the Irish! You know all about my dream of nailing all the Corrs!' 'Yes, you've described it to me in great detail. 'Irish Bastard' is what McClancy calls himself.' 'Really,' Shiatsu replies. 'He couldn't come up with something cool like 'Vivaldi of Violence' of Sensation of Domination?'' 'Apparently not,' answers Oni. 'I suppose that is why Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu merchandise sells so much better. But I can't be taking McClancy lightly. He has spoken some pretty strong words, some of them I can even understand!' Shiatsu gets off the couch and walks over to Oni, straddling his lap. 'Well, then,' she purrs. 'Maybe we should see how your endurance is...' 'Indeed,' answers Oni.
