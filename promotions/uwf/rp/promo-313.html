UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #313

Staying in Shape

April 6 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Malcolms House.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Scars on society. Blemish’s of mankind. Outcasts who give the world a bad name. Freaks in leather. Unwanted and unneeded. Much like Bondage the Sex Fiend. Malcolm Kennedy has been dwelling on this for a long time, and its time he finally lets all of you out there know just what he is feeling going into his match at Renaissance against Bondage the Sex Fiend.*** ((( The scene opens up in a dark, poorly lit room. “The Melting Point Of Wax” by the band Thrice plays quietly over a radio in the distance. Every few seconds a clank of metal on metal contact can be heard, a gasp of breath immediately following it. The camera moves into the room and around a corner. There sitting on the bench of a white workout machine is Malcolm Kennedy. He pulls a bar from above him down below his chest, as he does the all but 3 of the weights in the stack are lifted up. He lowers them back down exhaling as he goes.))) (((CLANK))) (((Malcolm releases the weights as the drop the last couple inches to the ground. He grabs a towel off his shoulder and wipes the sweat from his forehead. He stands up and walks away from the bench towards a mirror. Beside the mirror is a shelf with a bottle of water in it. He takes a drink from the bottle and sets it back down. He then flexes to his full extent in front of the mirror. His ripped, tanned, sweat covered body glistens in the mirror. He relaxes his muscles back. He then rests his back against the wall, and slides down until he is sitting on the ground, his legs extended in front of him.))) Malcolm Kennedy: “Its time to get back to action. Back to the ring. Back where I perform the best. And that’s just what Im gonna do at Renaissance. I will step in the ring with Bondage the Sex Freak. Err, the Sex Fiend.” (((He snorts in disgust. Shaking his head he looks towards the ceiling and continues to talk.))) Malcolm Kennedy: “I’ve faced a lot of crack pots in my time here in the UWF. The Fool, Axel Action, uhh, you get the picture. But this will be the first time against some sex crazed whips and chains psycho. I guess there is a first time for everything. God willing, this will be my first and last with this freak. I just want to get the win real quick, like I know I can, and get out. No screwing around.” “So what will I have to do to come out the victor against Bondage? That’s real simple. I just have to stick to my game. Do what I do best. Get the upper hand and keep it.” “Yeah, yeah, I know, It sure as hell wont be easy. But if everything was meant to be easy, what would be the point?” (((He wipes his face with the towel once again.))) “There are only two kinds of people that wear masks. People with something to hide, and idiots. Im pretty sure your not an idiot, which leads to the first answer. What it is you have to hide, I have no idea. Im not into the kind of crap your in to, so for all I know it could be commonplace.” (((Malcolm rolls the towel up and puts it back over his shoulder. He stands up and glances once more at himself in the mirror. He then grabs a gym bag off the floor and makes his way out of the room. The camera follows him into the next room, a large living room with three couches, two recliners, and a huge flat screen plasma tv. Malcolm tosses the gym bag behind the couch before hopping over the top of it and landing on the cushion. He kicks his feet up on the coffee table in front of him and continues on.))) Malcolm: “You seem so lost in your sick little fantasy world of sex, that you would never be able to compete with me. People say looks can be deceiving, and not to judge a book by its cover. But look at your previous performance against Ezra Kazee. You had your ass handed and fed to you on a silver platter. But hey, Bigger upsets have happened.” “ But wait, you already have a match before that for the tag titles. How do you dare compete in a match like and still expect to hang with me in the ring. Let me go ahead and tell you, that wont happen. Im the fastest rising superstar in the UWF today. Im the ONE MAN ARMY. A member of the elite, The Prime Alliance. Your just some clown in black leather getting off to a swift kick in the nuts by some chick with high heels.” ((( Malcolm snorts once again, laughing at his own joke.))) “Let me make this real simple for you Bondage. At Renaissance, me and you are gonna meet face to mask in that ring, and Im going to be the one who comes out with his hands raised as the winner. Im gonna hit you with a borage of moves on you. Im gonna make you feel pain you’ve never felt, and never imagined. Im gonna hurt you so bad, you wont know which way is up. Now I know I cant promise all that. But I can damn sure GUARANTEE it!” ((( Malcolm cuts the tv on and watches the highlights of UCONN winning the NCAA tourny. The camera turns around and walks out as the scene fades to black.)))
