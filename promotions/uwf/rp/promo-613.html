UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #613

Regal Rumble: An itroduction to The Saviour

12 Aug 2004

'The Savior' Jason Christy — An airport in Tokyo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
.:We fade in from black to an airport. At the entrance closest to the car park several high school artworks are exhibited. The smell of coffee entices us towards a café where vacationers are buying books and magazines to pass time with while on their journey. Sitting amongst a collection of others eager to welcome those arriving, we peer out a glass panel as high and wide as the wall it is a part of and watch as weary travelers walk off a passenger jet and towards us. Of particular interest to us is a younger couple. The male of the pair plods smoothly and deliberately while his partner moves briskly, almost at a jog to keep up with him. She looks so small compared to this man that we imagine her weaving through his massive strides without needing to duck. The man catches our attention first. He appears to be somebody who demands it of us. He has a strong jaw with an untamed five o’clock shadow that further pronounces the angularity of it. His dirty-blonde hair is blown around in the wind. It falls to his shoulders, pulled back in a ponytail. Blue eyes sit below prominent eyebrows and scan the area like spotlights in the towers of a prison. Although better judgment tells us that it’s not wise to stare at his partner, the mind’s obsession with aesthetic perfection leaves us with no choice. Her small five and a half foot body contains everything a man could dream of. Long, dark, red-brown hair bounces playfully with each step, loose curls in it like fingers luring us in to come and hold hands. Following it all the way down as she brushes her hand through it, we guess that it ends near her shoulder blades. A black dress hugs her curvaceous body. The straps on it reveal smooth shoulders and two perfect collarbones that point to a slender neck we imagine her partner rests his hand on, while pulling her body in towards his. The dress ends, in our favour, at the middle of her thigh. Tight bronze legs draw our attention to black high heels that give her an extra inch of height. As the two enter the building they glance at us for a second. We pull our recorder out of our pocket and rush after them:. Us: Jason! Kristy! .:Jason turns towards us. Standing so close to him we realize how massive this man is. He towers above us. We have always been small, but standing next to this monster makes us feel like we’re ten again, tugging on our father’s pants to get his attention. We liken the act of him turning to face us like an oil tanker turning one hundred and eighty degrees. It seems like it takes forever:. ‘The Saviour’ Jason Christy: Who the hell are you? .:Having heard his thick English accent, we think we may have been wrong. He sounds more like a luxurious cruise ship. We are Warren Garrett. A journalist for the Universal Wrestling Federation. We feel the perspective of the audience move out of Warren and into nothing:. Warren Garrett: I’m Warren Garrett. I’m a journalist for United Wrestling Federation. Jason Christy: Then I have no business with you. I wrestle for the Universal Wrestling Federation. Warren Garrett: I’m sorry Mr. Christy, I meant the Universal Wrestling Federation. .:Jason looks at him curiously, slightly amused by the character standing in front of him hopefully. Warren wears a brown business suit slightly too big for him, and a pair of large framed glasses also oversized for his small stature. He seems to stand at about five feet, five inches with black hair styled using generous amounts of grease:. Warren: I’m a little nervous. This is m- Jason: You should be nervous, having drooled over my wife in front of me like that. .:Kristy smiles at Warren, and he clumsily tries to form one himself. Having failed, he feels warmth run to his face:. Jason: That’s the reason people leave bars bleeding. I’ve spent the last five hours trying to resist the impulse to head butt somebody. I can’t believe the UWF would give me anything short of first class. .:Jason scans the area again after reading the time of his wrist watch. Everything about this man looks expensive and deliberate. Everything down to the disregard for keeping a clean shaven appearance adds to the man’s class:. Jason: I don’t feel like spending any more time here. I want a glass of wine and a spa. .:Warren fumbles and drops several words as Jason turns to leave:. Warren: Could I just get a few words, please? .:Jason notices the high school artwork on exhibit. He looks for a moment at it in dislike:. Jason: What the fuck do they call this? I would more enjoy staring at a shit I made after drinking Guinness. .:His pace hastens as he glances back behind his shoulder to see Warren in pursuit:. Jason: Can you come with me? Warren: I suppose… Jason: Good. Then you’ll get your interview. I’m in the perfect mood to give you one anyway. .:The trio reach a car parked towards the front of the car park. Jason pulls a set of car keys from his pant pocket, and we hear the car alert us that it has been unlocked. Jason moves towards the driver’s seat and slides in, griping to himself as he pushes the seat back to allow room for his legs. Kristy slips in next to him, and Warren botches an attempt to successfully operate his seatbelt on the first attempt. The ignition is started, and the car is brought to life. Jason reverses it first, then begins driving out of the car park in a more responsible manner than Warren expected. Jason takes a quick look at Warren in the rear view mirror. Warren makes eye contact with him for a second before looking away. The car park is a far less intense thing to look at:. Jason: Well then. I can’t ignore you now. You’re in my car. What is it you want to ask me? .:Having expected to ask questions once arriving at their destination, Warren is brought to life. He grabs a piece of paper from his pocket and unfolds it. The recorder is pulled from another one and turned on:. Warren: Basically, what are your opinions of the Universal Wrestling Federation, and what are your thoughts on the Regal Rumble at the up coming pay per view. Jason: My thoughts on the UWF? .:He chuckles to himself while making a right turn onto a highway:. Jason: Any place that gives me the ability to kick someone’s ass without the chance of being arrested is fine by me, Warren. .:Warren laughs with Jason, more out of nerves than anything else:. Jason: However, the wrestlers here are a different story. You probably know that I haven’t been actively competing for about a year. I don’t know if it’s just this place, but the characters that are passing themselves off as athletes in this place are embarrassing to this sport. Smokey Wead? Fizbane the Fabulous? They sound more fitting for a role on Jerry Springer or Cops. I didn’t become a wrestler to wrestle people like these fools; I became a wrestler to awe audiences in matches of athletic precision; to make history in grueling, hour-long battles; to lift the bar of human potential… Instead I made it to the UWF in time for disco and drug taking. That’s why I call myself The Saviour, Warren. Because I’m here to save the sport of professional wrestling from it’s own ruin. I’m here to pick up the pieces, and return wrestling to something legends from the past would be proud to say they built. It’s become something people watch for violence and sex. I know I tend to get a little crude at times, but when I walk through those curtains and make my way down to the ring, I’m a different person. I strive to put the most effort I can into the match, not to dance around and make a joke out of it, like a clown. That’s what it’s like, Warren; like a magician having to share a stage with a bunch of clowns in a circus. .:Jason’s mouth has become taut. Warren has visibly brought up a subject that Jason feels strongly about. He attempts to change the subject:. Warren: How do you feel about your part in the Regal Rumble? Jason: I feel great about the Regal Rumble. I’m debuting in the main event of a pay per view, for starters, and I’ll go into my second show fifty-nine nil. Warren: You’re saying you’re going to eliminate every man in the Rumble? Jason: I’m saying I’m going to get into that ring and get the job done. I’m saying I’m going into that match the freshest fighter in the UWF, and I’m coming out the most revered man in the UWF. Warren: What about the Television Title stipulation? Jason: Warren, titles are shams. They’re the consequence of winning a particular match at a particular moment in time. That’s all they are. I’m going to hunt for one of course, they make men wealthy. And you know what rich men say when someone asks them how much money is enough, don’t you Warren? Warren: Ahh… Jason: They say, ‘Just a little more’. .:Jason smiles for a minute and looks back at Warren:. Jason: But if I do come out of that match the Television Champion, I’ll tell you what I’ll do. I’ll use it to bust The Renegade’s head open, spit on the piece of tin, and throw it back to him. Warren: What would you do that for? I thought you said you wanted a title. Jason: Warren, I’m a man of honour. Of pride. Things that you’ll see me do will ultimately reflect that. Winning the Television Title by wrestling Rich would be like catching crabs from sleeping next to a homeless man, do you understand me? Warren: I think so… Jason: I won’t win a title from anyone, Warren. To lose your title to me you need to be worth the notch you’ll put on my belt, and Rich Rollins simply isn’t worth it. I’m going into the match with no fear and no prejudice. If you’re within my grasp then I’ll throw your worthless corpse over that top rope, damn it, whether you have a title or not. Whether it’s your debut match or you’re a UWF veteran. Warren: Do you have anything to say in response to other wrestlers that have made comments concerning the Regal Rumble? .:Jason laughs openly at Warren’s question. He licks his lips, and a smile comes over his face:. Jason: Of course I do. So far I have seen nothing at all. Tsunami Oni? This man is the biggest joke I have ever seen, a drunken fool with a hooker girlfriend. Who lets his girlfriend out with no underwear on? The only title Oni’s going to get a shot at in the Regal Rumble is, ‘The man thrown furthest from the ring by Jason Christy’ and he has a damn good chance of that. Champion of champions my fucking ass. And Ruff Ryder? This man is the materialization of why I’m always so pissed off. If the ladies loved a man who dressed like a child then he would be the envy of every man. If he is going to wear something as juvenile as a baseball cap, then he could at least wear it in a style less similar to the way the special kid at my school wore his when he was in year four. He’s living in a dream world. If women watch him and it makes them want to have sex with their husband or boyfriend, it’s because they started doubting the male race and needed something to reinforce their beliefs that we’re not all dick heads. Fat losers? I got your fat right here (Jason grabs his crotch), loser. It only gets worse, Warren. I’m starting to wonder why this match is the main event until I remember the attention span of modern wrestling fans. With sixty men in the match, it’s like children watching fireworks. All the colours and sounds… idiots. Mr. Meaner. He has two matches in Tokyo, which is a complete mystery to me. Someone so bland and boring, and people will have to watch him twice. He fails to realize that the reason he has been put in a match that involves such a ridiculous stipulation is because he couldn’t wrestle open a can of baked beans, and you know what wrestling promoters say: if your wrestlers can’t wrestle, give them weapons and people can laugh at their stupidity. If both his opponents are as bad as he says they are, then maybe he should consider why he has been put in a match with them. I’m sorry, Warren. I can’t go on. If I have to remind myself of the stupidity surrounding me any more, I’ll want to crash this car into something. Have you got your interview? Warren: Yes, Jason. I think that’s enough. Jason: Good. .:He pulls the car over to the side of the road:. Jason: Because I wouldn’t want to take you all the way to our motel. You’d never leave me alone. .:Warren sits in the car shocked for a minute:. Jason: What are you waiting for?! Get the fuck out! .:Fade to black:.
