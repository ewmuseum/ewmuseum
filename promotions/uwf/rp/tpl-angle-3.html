Trailer Park Luchador — Angle #3

23 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Ahhhhhh!” [Deep within the confines of the UWF Arena … the Trailer Park Luchador flees.] TPL: “She-Devil!!!!” [The Luchador throws himself against a wall, hiding next to a bush in an empty office room. His chest heaves up and down, his hand clutches at his pained, swollen groin, and his eyes dart back and forth. Somewhere – somewhere! – Tigera is searching. And while the Hero of the Double-Wide would surely like another pinch of that sweet, sweet ass …] TPL: “I … *huff* … really would …” [… he knows another meeting would likely end up with a loss of many teeth.] TPL: “It really would.” [Suddenly, horrified and fearful turns to blind rage. The Mulleted Legend grabs his Teddy Bear around the throat and begins shaking it insanely.] TPL: “Rar! This isn’t what you said would happen! ‘Oh, it’s a girl! You can beat a girl! Just go be evil and everyone will know you and you’ll get success!’ Rod damnit! You sent me out there against the chick Terminator!!! Without the personality!!!” [The Luchador continues to throttle the silent Teddy Bear.] TPL: “RAR!” [Eventually, the Barefooted Bastard becomes exhausted, and slumps down, letting his little Teddy Bear fall to the ground.] TPL: “You think I could go back to being a good guy, Teddy? I mean … I lost all the time … like … ALL the time back then … but still. Everyone loved me.” [Teddy Bear just stares at the Luchador.] TPL: “Alright, alright. No one loved me. But I still had my friends. And I wasn’t getting assaulted by crazed Latina Terminators every time I turned around. And I didn’t have to hurt little cute cuddly seals, and … oh Rod damnit, Mr. Cuddles, I’m so sorry!!!!” [The Luchador weeps openly.] TPL: “And those poor kids! AH!” [The Luchador dives onto the ground as some anonymous clerk walks past. Shaking, he’s surprised when he sees the little arm of Teddy Bear pointing down the hall. Pointing at one large door.] TPL: “What is it, Teddy? Is there something in that room?” [Teddy Bear does not answer.] TPL: “Whatever it is, it has to be better than waiting out here for Tigera to find me and start kicking me again.” [The Luchador from Wisconsin hops up to his feet, tucks Teddy Bear under one arm, and stalks toward the big door. The door leading to the very last room … the big conference room of the UWF Committee, on the top floor of the UWF Towers. Happily, Trailer’ stalks off, cautiously opens the door, and slips into the big room.] TPL: “Man, it’s dark in here. I’ll just flip this little light switch, and OH DEAR SWEET MOTHER OF ROD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [As the big conference room is suddenly bathed in light, the Trailer Park Luchador finds himself standing on one side of a long, long table, opposite … the giant, monstrous, UWF Gorilla!] UWF Gorilla: “RAR!!!!!!!” TPL: “AWWWWWWWW!!!!” [The Luchador falls on his ass, pointing one shaking finger at the giant silverback sitting in a big oak chair. The UWF Gorilla points back.] UWF Gorilla: “Rar!!” TPL: “Wait!!! I know you! You … you’re that gorilla that ate the UWF’s Board of Directors!! But I thought you were just a legend!” UWF Gorilla: “Rar!” [The Gorilla continues to point an accusing finger, but he just sits there. The Luchador’s brow furrows, but eventually he looks down at the chair.] TPL: “You want me to sit down? Okedokey.” [The Mulleted Champion of Nobody sets Teddy Bear down on the big table, on top of a stack of banana peels, and sits down in the chair. The Trailer Park Luchador and the UWF Gorilla stare at each other across the table.] TPL: “Man, this is so cool. I never thought I’d get to meet the UWF Gorilla.” UWF Gorilla: “Rar!” TPL: “Wait … you saw what happened tonight!?” UWF Gorilla: “Rar.” TPL: “Yeah, I mean … I know it was messed up, but Teddy said I had to …” UWF Gorilla: “RAR!” TPL: “What!? Talking to a Teddy Bear is NOT ridiculous. And …” UWF Gorilla: “Rar!!” TPL: “No! You can’t be a fan of Tigera!” UWF Gorilla: “Rar!” TPL: “WHAT?! No~! You can’t book me into a match with her at Live and Loud … that’s insane!” UWF Gorilla: “RAR!!!” TPL: “No, no, I didn’t mean YOU were insane, I meant … wait a second!!! You’re a gorilla!” UWF Gorilla: “Rar?” TPL: “I mean … you’re a gorilla. We both know that a gorilla doesn’t have the power to book wrestling matches. This is ridiculous.” UWF Gorilla: “Rar rar rar rar, rar rar rar, rar rar rar, RAR!! Rar rar rar.” TPL: “Oh. So you used to be a working at a zoo, but then you fled, escaped into the UWF Towers, and ate the Board of Directors. And by a bizarre rule that Al Crud placed in the UWF charter when he was running the place, anyone who eats the acting Board of Directors takes their powers, and thus you have the authority to do whatever you want. Man … Al Crud thought of everything. He really was the coolest.” UWF Gorilla: “Rar.” TPL: “Wait! So you mean I actually have to fight her at Live & Loud!? NO!!! That’s not fair!!!” UWF Gorilla: “RAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” TPL: “OKAY, OKAY, JUST DON”T EAT TEDDY!!!!! I’ll wrestle her if I have to! But I’ll find a way around it!” UWF Gorilla: “Rar!” TPL: “No DQ?!?!” UWF Gorilla: “Rar!” TPL: “In a cage!? Like a steel cage?” UWF Gorilla: “Rar.” TPL: “Oh. Like a giant animal cage. That sucks.” UWF Gorilla: “Rar rar.” TPL: “I hope you ate them for that.” UWF Gorilla: “Rar.” TPL: “Dude … this totally sucks, and I can’t believe you’re making me wrestle Tigera … but I just can’t stay made at a gorilla. Want to play Chutes and Ladders?” UWF Gorilla: “ROAAAARRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!” [Seconds later, the Trailer Park Luchador dives out of the meeting room for the UWF Board of Directors, slamming the door shut behind him, and clutching Teddy Bear to his chest.] TPL: “I’m so confused.” [And as the Wisconsin Luchador hops to his feet and goes off in search of a better hiding place from the Latina Terminator, we cut away.]
