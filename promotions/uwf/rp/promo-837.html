UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #837

A lesson in CHAOS

14 Dec 2004

Raid — School

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens and you see a door to a class room. And on this door is a sign that reads Chaos 101. There is absolutely no sound coming from the room. All of a sudden you hear foot steps coming up from behind the camera. It quickly swings around to see who it is, and all you see is a man’s chest. The camera zooms out to reveal that the cheat belongs to Raid.] Raid “You gonna stand there all day or are you gonna move out of my way so I can get into the room?” [The cameraman starts to back away from the door, and Raid enters the room and the camera man follows.] Raid “You’re in for a real treat, today I a, teaching my extermination students about the Chaos Theory. Now all of those people who don’t believe me that chaos is all around us will learn that it is. My class should be here shortly so you just wait right there in the back.” [The cameraman makes his way to the back and finds a desk to sit in. As they wait Raid is busy setting up charts and graphs. After a few minutes students start coming in. soon the classroom is full.] Raid “Alright class today I have a special lesson planned for you. It seems that some of my other colleagues in the UWF don’t believe that there is chaos all around us. So I have invited them to join us today to show them that they are wrong.” [He turns around to the white board and begins to write ‘The Chaos Theory’ on the board.] Raid “We’re gonna start off with an explanation of the Chaos Theory. The name 'chaos theory' comes from the fact that the systems that the theory describes are apparently disordered, but the chaos theory is really about finding the underlying order in apparently random data. This is the literal meaning of the Chaos theory. An easier way to describe it is; the flapping of a single butterfly's wing today produces a tiny change in the state of the atmosphere. Over a period of time, what the atmosphere actually does diverges from what it would have done. So, in a month's time, a tornado that would have devastated the Indonesian coast doesn't happen. Or maybe one that wasn't going to happen does.” Student “So what you’re saying is that a small change in the initial conditions of a system can drastically change the long-term behavior of that system.” Raid “Exactly!! Another way to explain this is by the example of a water wheel. At the top, water drips steadily into containers hanging on the wheel's rim. Each container drips steadily from a small hole. If the stream of water is slow, the top containers never fill fast enough to overcome friction, but if the stream is faster, the weight starts to turn the wheel. The rotation might become continuous. Or if the stream is so fast that the heavy containers swing all the way around the bottom and up the other side, the wheel might then slow, stop, and reverse its rotation, turning first one way and then the other.” Raid “Does that make it clear?” [The class seems to all nod or agree is some fashion.] Raid “Ok just so I make it clear to all of those ass monkeys that don’t believe me I have one more way to describe the Chaos Theory. In this description I will be using humans. Subject (A) is introduced into an environment that is full of middle class citizens. Unknown to the citizens subject (A) was from a bad neighborhood where there were daily shootings, murders and other crimes. At first the citizens try to welcome (A) into the community by introducing them selves to him. One would think that kindness and politeness would soften (A) but he soon returns to his old ways. He begins to steel from the citizens. After time they begin to retaliate against (A) but that only makes him lash out more. And soon the neighborhood is no better off than the one (A) was taken from. You see one small change in an environment causes a ripple effect and it begins to change that around it. So my point is proven once again.” [Raid reaches behind his desk and pulls out a bag of baseball bats.] Raid “Now for my real world experiment to prove the Chaos Theory. What I want you all to do is go out into the rest of the school with these and try to change your environment by causing a ripple effect.” [He then dishes out the bats to the students and they all storm out of the class room. Almost immediately screams are heard from the hallway. The camera focuses in on Raid’s face as he gets a large evil grin on his face. He begins to laugh.] Raid “I have taught them well.” [Raid stares deep into the camera.] Raid “Now do you believe me? Chaos is all around us, sometimes it just needs a little shove in the right direction. And that’s why I’m in the UWF to give Chaos a little shove to get it going.” [Raid begins flipping chairs and desks over as he makes his way to the door. As he reaches the door he stops.] Raid “They’ve pushed me just one to many times, I warned them of the things that would come of it but they didn’t believe me. Maybe now they believe me, now that I have started the tornado known as CHAOS!!” [Raid exits the room and the scene fades to black.]
