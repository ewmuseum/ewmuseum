UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #461

Promotion... CORPORAL STYLE

20 Jun 2004

Joshua Dunn — disembodied voice ville

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There is no I in team. But there is no team, so there can be I. For corporal punishment, who needs an entourage of people to do the more complicated tasks, like getting up from the sofa, he is convinced that wrestlers need a conglomeration of people, because since that is his way, there is no other way. Flawed judgement from someone who has been swept back into the sea of time. For once there’s a small change in the Corporal’s technique, of course he doesn’t need to do what people say in promotions to get his point across, but maybe he does it because he’s just too old to do it any other way. He’s past it, he knows it, but he wants to deny his lack of later years. This is called a midlife crisis. Finally we know the reason of Corporal Punishments return to the UWF, it’s not that he wanted one last outing before his retirement age rolled along, no, he wants to prove he can still do it to himself, driven by this sudden urge you get around the age of 39 to hang onto what youth you have left. Maybe next he’ll buy a motorcycle and insist in buying clothes one size too small. Still, what are we too say these things? Well it doesn’t really matter, this is only the introduction, so let’s make this as large as possible, so we don’t have to bother Joshua Dunn for any proper length of time. Has anyone else noticed that we only see Corporal Punishment for only two minutes at most? You see I’m certain this is because he’s finally deteriorated to the stage that he requires the use of an iron lung, only allowing him out for certain lengths of time. That’s why he’s so reliant on other people to do his speaking for him, like in his ridiculously disembodied monologues, which he pays someone to do. One of the first things to go as you get older is the mind. We have a perfect example in the Corporal, as he seems to have forgotten that VCR stands for Video Cassette Recorder. Now even though he’s reading instructions that clearly read in his promotion “Video Recorder” he is still convinced that a video player and a VCR are two separate pieces of equipment, and are not, in fact, one in the same, when the converse is true. As the corporal would say, “Should they not be held responsible for their ignorance?” And he should really. One mistake Joshua Dunn has never done is to mimic the strategies of Corporal Punishment, he doesn’t need to, and the Corporal still wants to think that everybody remembers him, but he has been forgotten, and he doesn’t want to accept that fact. He is a shell of his formal self, a hollow shell. And this husk will crumble at the faintest hit. Hold on, does this seem a bit familiar at this point? Does it feel monotonous and boring? Do you want to see some action? Pity, because we’re only half way through. You see this little Corpish type promotion is to prove a point, nothing more. I am fully aware that is not a word, and nevertheless I’m sure the Corporal will bring it up in his next promotion. He seems to worry over the littlest things. He’s about to face one of the fore runners of the federation, and no matter what he says will not change that fact. Joshua Dunn is about to face him and he’s more worried about how Joshua used the word trawl in a sentence. To trawl through something is to say, find out something, it is a turn of phrase. Perhaps the Corporal didn’t know about this particular phrase, but it was no excuse for him to think it was a PLACE. “We at Corporal TV have still to see such a place as TRAWL” Now we know that punishment has just descended into a joke. A has been that will be never more. Who in sweet hell could think that trawl is a place? And as for it being a fishing net, couldn’t he put two and two together to get four for a change? For someone who feigns intelligence and dismisses anyone else’s achievements as nothing, why should he then think that anyone cares what he’s done? North American Title? Who cares about that? The last champion was Ruff Ryder, and that just tells you what kind of a joke title that was. And he holds the most title reigns in that division. That equates to what exactly? Well I will tell you, The Corporal was stuck in a rut, unable to get any higher than he was, stuck in the North American Title division because he wasn’t good enough to be anywhere else, he has the most title reigns there, that also mean he has the most title LOSSES in that division, unable to keep hold of the title for any decent length of time. Perhaps the greatest Joke that The Corporal came out with in his last promotion was concerning wrestling terminology. He can’t seem to see past one syllable phrases, we already know he doesn’t understand how to trawl through something without having something to do with fishing for plaice. He saw “New” “face” “Jobber”, instead of “New face” “Jobber”. This failing is just another flaw in the head of the old man who should be in a home rather than in a wrestling ring. Your mind gets dirtier the older you get, and since being round the block a few times immediately says to him that it meant he was a prostitute. That gives you a rough guide to how old he is then. *** I bet you thought we were about to cut to see what Dunn was getting up to, but you’d be wrong, I’m not done with my pointless jabber yet, huzzah! Yes indeed the days of creativity are gone, when one must listen to scores of introductive monologue, and not just once, every single time you switch on your television set. If you can switch it on that is, because we can never take something that simple for granted where Corporal Punishment is concerned. “The lack of creativity has left the Youth of today”. Did he say that, you can bet on it and it means that yes, youth is full of creativity. Therefore the lack must belong to the elderly, so stuck into a pattern that everything they do appears to be the same. When was the last time you could distinguish one CP Promotion from another without looking at the recorded date? And yes, the productions from Camp Punishment may be solidly built, built as solid as a Zen box, a box that does absolutely nothing! I can’t wait until the Corporal tries to find out about that. And finally to the actual promotion *** Only joking
