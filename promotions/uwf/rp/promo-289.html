UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #289

Lost in the shuffle

25 Mar 2004

Anarchy — Outside Raids business

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to just outside of Raid's extermination business. To the left of the building sits two black vans with the Raid Extermination logo in red on the sides. In front of the building sits a black Lincoln Navigator with the license plates with ANARCHY on it. A discussion can be heard inside.] Pryde : ...I understand that but they blindsided us in back. But don't worry; they will get what's coming to them. First Kazee, then Montgomery and Dunn. The think they are king shit, but once their titles are gone they will be nothing. [The front door opens and out steps 3 figures, Joanna, Dai, and Pryde.] Dai : Keep your head on straight, and stay with smaller jobs for awhile. [The three turn to finally notice the camera man] Joanna : About time you got here. You were supposed to be here 2 hours ago. Montgomery buy your watches or what, cheap pieces of crap. We have some things to say and don't have all day to do it. Tell him Dai. Dai : When we came here, we wanted to do a few things. One was to help out our friend Raid. We haven’t moved as fast as we like, but we are doing it. Two, we wanted a run for the Tag-Team Titles, and after one tag match we were delighted to singles matches. Meanwhile this stoner team from the UWF past has one match and has title matches in the future already, what the hell. [Dai opens the back of the Navigator and pulls out a kendo stick] Dai : Meanwhile we get paired up against El Turtlé/Tommy Waters, jobbers to the stars. Why should we even bother. We want challenge's not this crap. Stoners, losers, whatever you names are, get in line and fall before us. We have our eyes on the belts, and we will plow over anyone that gets in our way. Pryde : We have speed bumps this Sunday, but after that it is one to some competition. Hey Persia you never did answer my challenge. You got a lucky win with my back turned in our 3-way match and I want revenge. You think you are the next big thing from Holland, well, you are the only thing from there. Why, because it sucks. There has never been a wrestling superstar from there, and if I have my way there never will. [Dai hands Pryde the kendo stick] Pryde : You don't want to respond to my challenge. I am going to make you respond to it. [Pryde starts to hit his open left hand with stick] Pryde : And if you don't, who knows what could happen. [Pryde catches the stick and with a sharp downward force breaks it over his knee and tosses it into a nearby garbage can.] Pryde : You may be just discarded like JttS we meet on Sunday. Joanna : Finally, The Players Club. The quote, unquote, elite stable in the UWF. Every fed has them. A group that uses their ass kissing and wrongly appointed power to keep them in the main events and title way longer than they should be, can you say the Clique in the WWE? You use the feds money and resources for your own personal use such as bringing in people like the Plague as your own personal lackey. And you change matches to give you more of an edge. That's the reason you will never be great wrestlers, because you cannot win the match when on an even playing field. Chaos Society won't rest till you are brought down to the level you belong, ass kissing want to be's. [The three start to get into the SUV when Dai steps out and motion's for the camera man to come closer. As he does, Dai reaches out and grabs him by the back of the head and slams it into the hood. As the screen starts to fade to black you hear.] Dai : Next time don't be late.
