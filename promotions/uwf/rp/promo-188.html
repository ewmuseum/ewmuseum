A Week at Lungie's II

23 Jan 2004

Iron Lung — Villa Roma apartments, car

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Week at Lungie's II A Week at Lungie's II Previously on A Week at Lungie's, Iron Lung kicked the famous Lifestyles of the Rich & Famous host, Robin Leach, out of his apartment for asking him 'repetitive questions'. Lung obviously wasn't in a very good mood the morning after Supremacy went off the air. Perhaps he was just tired from doing his work in the ring, or maybe he was irritable from having the same questions asked to him as if he were a child. Lung was young at heart, but he was grown up by far, and didn't understand why people, especially higher class people, would treat him that way. We now find ourselves in the Villa Roma apartment complexes once more, the second day of our week-long journey through Iron Lung's trials and tribulations. This is much like an E! True Hollywood Story type of documentary, where cameras will be following Lung's every step, hoping to catch a glimpse of his very private and not so candid life that has lives. Iron Lung is seen with his eyes closed, a red throw blanket over his body. It's not long enough to cover his whole self, so just his feet are left hanging out on the smaller sofa. A couple of empty Jolt cola bottles lay on the floor in front of the couch, and everything seems so peaceful until the preprogrammed radio alarm goes off. Springing up from his slumber, Iron Lung rubs his eyes to remove any signs of sleep. He yawns wildly as he sits up from the couch, using one of his remotes on the granite coffee table in front of him to turn the radio off. He scratches at his stubble face, he just shaved the other day. Getting up with a couple of moans and while putting his empty palm to the back of his neck, he mutters a couple of things that can't be picked up by our microphone. He then slowly maneuvers his way to another room of his apartment, the space he uses to change. Folding out an oriental screen to block our view, we are only left with the shadow of Lung as he changes into some street attire. Where would he take us today? What adventures would we embark on? We would soon find out. After some time, the shadow disappeared from behind the screen. Lung must have slipped into the bathroom to brush his teeth and comb his hair. He had just gotten his hair cut the other day as well, and didn't need as much grooming as when it was a little bit longer. Once again we see a shadow emerge from the screen, and then our subject unfolds it, putting it against a wall. He adjusts the collar of his fleece pullover and clears his throat before heading to his coffee table again. He grabs his apartment key, car key, and wallet. Putting the wallet in his right jeans pocket and the rest of his belongings in his left, he looks around the room and grumbles some inaudible phrases once more. He then heads to the door, turning the knob clockwise and opening it before him to expose the elegantly decorated hallway. Closing the door behind him after taking a check to make sure he had everything, he goes down the hallway and looks upon the elevator buttons. Pressing for down and sighing a little, our cameras continue to watch his patience be a virtue. After a few moments of agonizing wait, the elevator doors open with slightly old music bumbling through the crevice. Pushing the button for the parking garage now, the elevator door closes with a muffled thud, leaving Lung and the crew alone for the journey to the bottom floor. Cracking his knuckles as the elevator stopped at the garage, the doors opened. Lung immediately walked out to the subzero temperatures, and scurried to remember where he had parked his automobile. Finally realizing that he had parked with enough space so that nobody would hit him or prove as a threat to his vehicle, Lung walked down several spaces, almost to the very end of the row, to find his car there safe and sound. Taking a quick inspection of things and finding no damage done by anybody or by any force of nature, Lung unlocks his door using the keyless entry pad and opens the door, getting inside of the car and sitting on the seat. His feet still dangled out as he clacked them together, he liked to keep the car clean. After starting the engine of the car, Lung crept inside and finally closed the door beside him, buckling his seat belt. Letting the vehicle warm up for a few moments, Lung finally put the gear into reverse and slowly crept out of his spot before putting the gear back into drive, adjusting the wheel, and proceeding to leave the garage. Getting out of the garage and taking a right, we're still left to wonder where we exactly are off to, and why we're off to there so early in the morning. A whistling sound comes from IL's parted lips, but we can't quite make out what tune it is. Perhaps he was just going with whatever he felt like whistling at the moment. But whatever it was, it was completely off tune. After driving for quite a while, IL puts his signal light on and turns into a neighborhood Stop & Shop. Finally, we knew what this trip entailed, but it wasn't exactly what we would call an adventure. Popping the trunk of his car, Iron Lung shuts the engine off and unbuckles his seat belt, hopping out and closing the door behind him. Going towards the trunk, Lung pulls out a garbage bag, and we hear a persistent clinging sound, the sound of glass bottles hitting against each other. Returning cans and bottles? This was our big adventure? To be continued...
