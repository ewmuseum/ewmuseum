UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #259

Bring em on!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins just after Supremacy as Ruff Ryder is putting his bags into his hummer limo. He walks around the back of the vehicle and opens the door, but just as he is fixing to get inside he notices the cameraman standing next to his ride. He stops and rolls his eyes before turning to the man. The camera just pans up and down Ryder showing his black wind pants and red 'Prime Alliance' t-shirt. ) Ryder: Get a good look? * The camera focuses back on his face * Ryder: You gay or something fool? Don't be lookin me up and down like that unless you plan on throwin down. If you want some of this then you're just gonna have to wait in line like everyone else, because i've had my fill of violence for the night. I'm heading home so get out of my way. Cameraman: But I was just... * Ryder interupts * Ryder: You were just what? You just wanted to get an interview even though I been through a bunch of shit tonight right? You wanted to get an interview about how Moraless is in the Prime Alliance, about how I helped him out and now he's one of us. Well you know what, FUCK THAT SHIT! That skinny ass runt isn't in the Prime Alliance. I don't know where they came up with that horseshit, but it ain't happening. We only take the best and Moraless is far from the best. The only decent thing he's done is degrade the UWF and mistakes like them announcing him as Prime Alliance is why I hate this fuckin place anyways. That prick ain't one of us and he never will be so fuck him! * The cameraman takes a couple steps back * Ryder: What the hell? Did I scare you or something? It's aight boy, come on in a little closer. Don't be scared. What else would you like to discuss? Maybe you'd like to talk about the fact that some bitch decided he was gonna be a big man and hit me and Kennedy with some assfuck move called the bug bomb. That was pretty funny wasn't it? Well I don't think it was! That was a pussy's way out and that bitch will get what's coming to him, but he's not even on my level so fuck him until i'm not busy, then i'll handle him. * The cameraman starts to leave * Ryder: Oh come on now, don't be like that. I'm just a bit upset, it's not your fault. Stay..really. * The cameraman decides to stay * Ryder: Yea, I want you to stay, because of this fucked up match that myself and Kennedy have coming up now. It's us against all comers, and you know what i'm totally fine with it, because we're the best in the business and this is just another match to us. We can take anyone on the roster, and come matchtime we're gonna be proving that to the world. Come one, come all, and one team at a time we're gonna destroy each and every one of you boys. You step through that rope, and you better be in top form, because you're not just dealing with another stupid tag team, oh hell no. When that bell rings and the lights shine on you and the music hits and fans scream you're dealing with the very best that this business has to offer. I mean Kennedy is the fastest rising star this company has seen in along time. I'm a man who can beat em down and slap em around. I'm the Cherokee Thrilla and the enigma that is the True Indian Superstar. I am the show, and i'm what every fan comes to the arena to see. When those lights dim and 'Chin Check' hits those speakers the fans jump to their feet and the ladies cream their panties because they know that Ruff Ryder is on his way down the aisle to give them a show they'll never forget. Fuck the rest, because Kennedy and Ryder are the best you know what i'm sayin? We dominate and decimate, and that's exactly what's gonna happen in this all comers match, because we're untouchable. We can't be stopped and I don't care who they throw at us, because it won't be good enough. The one man army? The Cherokee Thrilla? Are you kidding me? That's a lethal combination right there and you're gonna let that tag team loose in front of millions of fans? Fans that get the blood pumpin and the adrenaline goin? We'll fuckin snap and rip someone's heart out! * Ryder winks at the camera * Ryder: Who are they gonna throw at us? Anarchy? Hell no, cause we already punked those losers out and embarassed them in front of their friends and family. They ain't worthy of a rematch so they are out. Kenny Mercury and Beezlebozo? Fuck em, Mercury doesn't know a damn good compliment when it's staring him in his faggot face then he can go fuck himself and Bozo, I will get my hands on you soon enough. Don't you think for a second that i've forgotten about you, you fat clown bastard. Joshua Dunn and Ezra Kazee? Hell no! Those two are bad enough on their own, what makes the UWF think they'd be entertaining as a team. I mean Dunn could carry the team, but he does that anyways. He carries the whole Player's Club on his back, but that's a communist for ya. Fuck em! Bring whoever you want UWF because we are ready for all challengers. We will walk through everyone you put in front of us and prove just who is the best in this business. When Ruff Ryder and Malcom Kennedy are at the top of our game there isn't anyone that can touch us and you punks makin the matches know that. Bring em all and we'll send em all out in bodybags. * Ryder starts to get into his limo, but stops and turns back around with a perplexed look on his face * Ryder: And what the hell is up with Mike Montgomery? Montgomery does the right thing? Demanding that the True Indian Superstar be put back into the tournament? How about that, maybe the man does have a soul. I can't even bad mouth you after that Monty, but don't worry I still hate ya. As for the Prime Alliance and that little life support comment, I don't think so. Moraless is your business, we want nothing to do with that prick. The only thing that I did by clockin you with that brick was announce the return of the Prime Alliance, the fact that Moraless beat you had nothing to do with us recruiting him. Whip his ass each and every day if you want. Now if you will excuse me...i'm out! ( Ryder pushes the camera out of his face before climbing into the back of his hummer limo and speeding off as the scene fades out and comes to an end. )
