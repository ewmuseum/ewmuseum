UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #641

The Time is Nigh

August 15th (year unrecorded)

Kenny Mercury — Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The time is NIGH~!] Nigh? What the hell does that mean? I means that its just about that time…. What we’ve all been waiting for. The moment when two wrestling phenomenons clash, head to head. The long awaited match between the Intercontinental Champion, Misfit, and the challenger, Kenny Mercury. Misfit and Mercury have gone through a lot, there intertwining story has been forever etched into the history books and videos in the UWF library. From the days of Purple Haze and The Slaughterhouse to the original drowning Pool and Violent Reaction to the Youngbloods and a Fire Inside to now. Nearly two years of history, coming to a pinnacle. The build up has been near perfect, the climax is now. Terror in Tokyo… The Regal Rumble 5… A One Way Street Fight for the Universal Wrestling Federation Intercontinental Title… Two men go in… only one will emerge victorious and a champion… the other… shattered and faced with the truth that they are lesser than their most hated enemy. To be less than… a plague on their soul forever… incurable. The only way to beat it, is to prevent it. Vaccinate it, the best way is to never get infected…. Kenny Mercury’s plan is just that. [We find Kenny Mercury at his Chicago apartment. He’s packing for the trip to Japan, to Tokyo, for the big Pay per View. A suitcase is laid out open on his bed. It’s empty. Kenny stands at the foot of his bed, staring down at the empty suitcase.] Kenny Mercury: Leaving what I know… I’ve lived my whole life in UWF, aside from when I was on the road wrestling… but Chicago was always my home. But now I’m supposed to go across the world to Japan… Across the world to face my enemy… my nemesis… the one being in this world that threatens me more than any other being or force… my archrival… my opposite… my one true foe… Misfit… [Kenny’s hands grab hold and clutch the sheets of his bed tightly.] Kenny Mercury: Leaving home… leaving what I know… three words… One… Way… Streetfight… or is that four? Street... Fight…. Fuck it. For too long… I’ve been held back. The world has thrown every obstacle in my way that it could get its hands on… stacking the odds against me, piling the opponents up… But I’ve knocked them all down. I’ve knocked down everything the world and fate’s thrown against me. Even still, some have undercut me in the process… stacked the odds to impossible. I’ve been framed, I’ve been set up… I’m no fool… I’m not a dunce… Sometimes not even the Shooting Star can win when there’s no winning option… The Impossible is always just that…. The Shooting Star just has knack for destroying the improbable. That is the definition of greatness. Misfit… Misfit… Misfit… that name echoes in my mind… I’m a friggin’ drone… with one thing on my mind… a solid mindset and I can’t stary from it. Misfit… hate… hate… Misfit… break Misfit…. Break…. BREAK! [Mercury lashes out and knocks his suitcase to the floor.] Kenny Mercury: He wants to play mind games with me… Misfit wants to mess with my head… So be it. Misfit, you’ve let loose the shooting star… the technical wizard… the definition of precision… I’ve lost it… Misfit… heh… do you realize what you’ve done?! [Kenny runs his hands through his golden locks… as he reaches the end of his long hair, he grabs a chunk and rips it out. He watches intently as the hair falls through his fingers.] Kenny Mercury: I’ve lost it… you’ve broken the definition of precision… you’ve broken the rules… the rules of which I’ve lived and abided by… You’ve broken the old Kenny Mercury… this is the dawning of a new era… [End Transmission.]
