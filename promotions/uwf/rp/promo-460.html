UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #460

Loverboy AGAIN?!

19 Jun 2004

The Communist — Backstage

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist reads the latest card: Commie: WHAT! Who wrote this card?! LOVERBOY! I've already beat him! Do you REALLY want him dead?! *sigh* Good greif, It's like taking a step backwards. Have I not proven myself yet? Do more people need to pay for my voice to be heard? I'm a former TV Champ. The Iron Curtain is a driving force in the UWF. And yet, doubters remain. And yet, there are still those who insist that the Communist is a flash in the pan gimmick wrestler with no future. The have not felt the biting sting of the Communist Slam. They have not been decimated by the Proletariot Champion. :The Communist walks down the hall to his locker and pulls his cell phone out: Commie: Hello? Father, it's good to talk to you. You saw our new member? Yes, he is talented. Yes father he is a true Communist, like the others. The Committee approves? Good good... Yes Father, I know they are displeased that I lost the title. I will retreve it from the chaos-mongers who stole it from us... Loverboy? *at this, the Communist laughs loud and long* no Father, he wasn't a problem the First time, he won't be one now. The match will not be as breif this time, I fear. He was out for a long time after the last beating he suffered, I promise, this time He won't be back. ... I was wondering, could the Commitee send me another agent? Someoneto assist me with... things? ... I see. HER? When? soon. Yes Father. Good night. :The Communist closes his cell phone and puts it in his locker: A woman? peh. Women were ment to cook, clean and make more sons of the Great Revolution. Why are they sending me a woman? *sigh* oh well. No matter, I must prepare. the Rematch, *chuckles* this should be good for a laugh.
