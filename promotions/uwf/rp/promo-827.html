UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #827

Back to the hunt

12/04 (year unrecorded)

Raid — Outside the arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens, and you see a black Hummer pull up to the curb. A shadowed figure steps out of the Hummer, the man is dressed in all black and he has the hood pulled over his head. On the front of his hoodie there is a large red Anarchy symbol. Under the hood you can see the mans chin and he has a thick black goatee and there is hair draped over his face. As he walks towards the camera he starts to lift his head.] Stu the Interview Guy “Is that, really you? Wait it is, I can’t believe it your finally back! Do you have any thing to say to the fans?” Raid “Damn it feels good to be back! But honestly who the fuck are you? Each time I come here there is a new interview guy. Stu “I’ve been here for a few months now. And where have you been?” Raid “What do you mean where have I been, every body knows I’ve been cleaning up the homeless bums. And damn it there are more of those filthy stinking bastards than I thought. But now I have returned to bring back the true meaning of Chaos!” Stu “But what about Pryde and Dai? They have been doing a pretty good job at bring about Chaos here in the UWF.” Raid “Those Guys have been doing a damn good job at raising hell, but with me back it’s gonna be CHAOS MULTIPLIED! There will be no safety with us around, no man woman or child is safe, all of the UWF superstars have better watch there backs, there titles are no longer safe. The tag titles will soon be where they belong. And the THC is going to be in my possession, Hardcore is taking on a new face!” Stu “But Kazee, and the THC, what if he wins it?” Raid “If by some chance he does win it than I will do what I have to in order to get the belt. He is a damn good friend and one hell of a wrestler, but that title will be mine!” [Raid then pushes the camera out of his face and as it swings away you see a man unloading Raids hummer, and the man appears to be Dreammaker. The camera then crash to the ground and the scene goes black.]
