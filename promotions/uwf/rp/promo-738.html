UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #738

Giving Back

4 Oct 2004

White Tiger — A children's hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Being a UWF superstar means more than fast cars and loose women. There's a responsibility too. A responsibilty to the UWF fans. That's why White Tiger is visiting a children's hospital the day before Live and Lound, where the Masked Monkeys will defend their tag team gold against the Legends, the Phenomenons, and the Local NVA Company in the first ever Pizza Parlor of Pain match. The Monkeys are still without their lucha masks so the Tiger is wearing a surgical mask instead. He steps into a room, where little Bobby is very sick and on life support. White Tiger: Hola, amigo! Little Bobby: Who the hell are you? White Tiger: I am White Tiger! One half of the UWF Tage Team champions! Little Bobby: No you're not! You're not one of the Legends! White Tiger: Legends? The Masked Monkeys won the titles from the Legends! Little Bobby:Not according to the web site. And I've seen ads for Live and Loud. It says the Legends are the champs! White Tiger: Those are just production errors. I got a belt, see? Little Bobby: You probaly stole them, filthy Mexican! White Tiger: What? Little Bobby: My daddy says Mexicans steal good American jobs, so you probaly stole that belt, wetback! White Tiger: Uh, my partner should be here someplace. Would you like to meet him? Little Bobby: He's not Mexican too, is he? White Tiger: Ah, kids say the darnedest things. Pajaro! Get in here! Pajaro de Presa is coming down hall. Very quickly. Because he has gotten a hold of a gurney and is riding it like a skateboard at a rather unsafe speed. Pajaro: Look at me! I'm Tony Hawk! As the gurney passes Little Booby's room, the Rudo with Attitude-o hops off. The gurney keeps going and stops... eventually. Most likely with a crash. But Pajaro de Presa has forgotten all about it. He's busy checking out the room. Pajaro: Wow! Everything is so white! White Tiger: That's because you have a rubber glove pulled over the top of your face. Pajaro: Oh yeah. Don't the fingers look cool? Like the proud Bird of Prey that I am! Uh, where are you, Tiger? White Tiger reaches over to Pajaro de Presa and teas two holes into the rubber. Pajaro: I can see clearly now, the rain is gone. What's theis say? Grabbing the clipboard from the door, Pajaro gives it a read. Pajaro: Man-boob removal? Oh, that's the guy in the other bed. Let's see... you caught pneumonia by going outside with wet hair? Damn, that really happens? Boy, Mama was right! And you poked your eye out running with scissors? Dang...What are they giving you? 20cc's of morphine? Seems kinda small. I'll add a few zeroes. And prescribe some Viagra. Hey! What's this? Pajaro de Presa has just found Little Bobby's life support machine. Pajaro: They got you a Playstation 2! Does it have UWF Supremacy:Where Are My Pants? I wanna play Lacuna Debris and make her jump up and down! Flicking buttons, Pajaro de Presa soon manges to undo all the hard work of the doctors and makes Little Bobby flatline. Pajaro: What's that sound? White Tiger: Gah! You idiot! Pajaro: I can fix it. No problemo. Using his vast technical knowledge, Pajaro de Presa attempts to fix the life support machine. He uses the same technique men use to fix TV's across America... he smacks it. A lot. Pajaro: Almost got it. Almost... That's when the machine goes crashing to the floor. White Tiger: No! Pajaro: Oops! Little Bobby: [says nothing because he is now comatose] Just then, an attractive young nurse runs in. Nurse: Oh my God! Pajaro: Yes, it is I, legendary luchador Pajaro de Presa! Would youy like to take my temperature? The Rudo with Attitude-o wiggles his tushy at the nurse. She doesn't seem to notice, being more attentive to the flatlining youngster. Nurse: I must get the doctor! She runs out, but returns moments later with the doctor. Doctor: Oh my God! Pajaro: Wow, you guys don't get many celebrities around. Must be because this is a children's hospital. Hey, ever examine the Olsen twins? The doctor doesn't reply. He's busy examing Little Bobby. Doctor: I'm going to have to go in. Nurse! Prep him for surgery! Pajaro: Sweet! Can I shave him? You know... his cajones... White Tiger: Pajaro... Pajaro: Eh, you're right. He probably don't got pubes yet. White Tiger: Why don't we go to the cafeteria and get some of that jell-o with the fuit you like so much? Pajaro: Woo-hoo! Can we get the green kind? White Tiger: Of course. Your Tag Team champions leave Little Bobby to the doctor and nurse. Will Little Bobby be all right? Only God knows. But we don't know you don't want to miss the Masked Monkeys in the Pizza Parlor of Pain match at LIVE AND LOUD!!!
