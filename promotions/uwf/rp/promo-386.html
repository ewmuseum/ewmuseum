Losing Streak (Short had to rush)

3 May 2004

The Plague — Parking Lot Following SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens following Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, another losing effort for the Hardcore Icon, The Plague, losing to Joshua Dunn. This scene is outside the arena, The Plague is seen walking to his car, he is wearing a black sweatshirt and jeans, his hair is down and he has a bag over his left shoulder. He is stopped by a cmaeraman.] Cameraman: 'Yo Plague, can we get a few words?' [Plague turns around with a pissed off look on his face.] Plague: 'What?' Cameraman: 'Well I just wanted to get a few words following your match with Joshua Dunn.' [Plague drops his bag and crosses his arms over his chest.] Plague: 'You would, wouldn't you? I mean I return to this company trying to reinvent my career and I can't even hold onto The Hardcore Title for one defense, then I loss to Iron Lung and Josh Dunn. I haven't won a match since I won The Hardcore Title. What the hell is wrong with tihs picture?' Cameraman: 'Can't tell you, but at least you are proving yourself with that THC victory.' Plague: 'Yeah proving that I can do what I have always done before. I want top go in a new direction, away from this hardcore shit.' Cameraman: 'Well I don't want to piss you off or anything but you got a hardcore tag match next Sunday, and your teamed with The Hardcore Champion, Nick Persia, the man who defeated you.' [Plague's eyes widen and you see the frustration on his face.] Plague: I know who he is, what the hell is wrong with The UWF? I want to get back on the winning side of things here.' [Cameraman interupts.] Cameraman: 'Well Nick Persia does have a pretty good record here, and he is THC Champion.' [Plague cracks his knuckles.] Plague: 'Yeah yeah, good for Nick. He beat me, and now I have to team with him. Fine, anything to make the booking committee happy. Who are we facing?' Cameraman: 'Dante and Draco Paladine, Paladine turn his back on Nick Persia tonight if you didn't see.' [Plague smiles.] Plague: 'Yeah I saw that, I found it funny, but now that I find out I need to kick his ass next week it will be me laughing again, this time at Draco's expense. See Draco and Dante, you guys think your hardcore, but next week you will be facing the current and former THC Champs, I hope your ready for war. I gave Nick his props when he beat me, lets hope he can hold his own on Sunday and keep his winning streak alive and help me to avoid another defeat and the hands of some punk ass bitches.' [Plague picks up his bag.] Plague: 'Nick I want to see what you got next week, I hope you can make me proud, maybe somewhere down the line we can do it again, but right now I just want to win a match. And if I need to be teamed with a man who defeated me, than I guess I am going to have to do what I got to do. Don't fuck up or it will be your ass.' [Plague turns around and heads towards his car as the scene fades out to black.]
