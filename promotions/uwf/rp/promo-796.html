Kenny Mercury Promo! HOLY SHIT!

Nov. 30 (year unrecorded)

Kenny Mercury — Duluth, Minnesota

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[It has been a few weeks since we've seen the face of the self- proclaimed technical wizard, Kenny Mercury. Indeed… it's been a long time… TOO LONG!] [But why would a first class competitor like Kenny Mercury just decide to drop in on UWF television after ignoring it completely for so long?] [Well…] [THE VENETIAN HOME RUMBLE, MUTHA FUCKAH~!!!!!!!!!] [What? Kenny Mercury in the VHR? That makes no sense.] [Of course not. This is UWF. Nothing makes sense. Even more than usual nowadays. The Universal Wrestling Federation has always displayed a silly, goofy, and mischievous side. It's infamous for the antics of its wrestlers and the federation itself. But in modern day UWF, the degree of shenanigans has been multiplied several kazillion times. It seems the entire federation is now based upon the fruit of the cake that the monkey made. This federation has lost its marbles, and that fat fucker who took them won't give them back even if you beat him in the chalked circle. He's really mean and he's bigger than us. I want my marbles back.] [However, one person still retains all his marbles, and some really cool jacks and some kick ass pogs on the side. This one man is Kenny Mercury. The prodigy of the UWF. The man who can do anything… if only it weren't for the entire world being against him. But this righteous dude possesses a power unlike any ever seen before. He possesses the power to win any wrestling match he's put in. I swears it, precious. Though he's been sorta waiting in the shadows recently, the fans have smoked him out of his hiding place… a sock filled closet in Chicago. The fans have voted the greatest wrestler in the history of the universe to wrestle in the greatest match in the history of the history of the history of the universe. THE VENETIAN HOME RUMBLE, MUTHA FUCKAH~!!!!!!!!!!] [Anywho, now we cut to the UWF houseshow… The greatest show on earth… and in a house. Suddenly…. OH SNAP!] Larson: OH SNAP! Rick: Huh? ['Stranglehold' by Ted Nugent hits the sound system, rocking the arena. The crowd boos so hard their pee-pees hurt. Kenny Mercury strolls out from backstage. He looks back to normal form… Like I said, its been a while… No longer the hardcore nutjob that he transformed into at the hands of his arch-enemy, Misfit, Kenny Mercury is b ack to his holier than thou, pretty boy, sexay slab of biatch man flesh self. His gorgeous hair is flowing and is so fucking pretty like a hundred babes just all out wet themselves as he waltzes down the steel walkway. And like four dudes totally blow their loads… in the faces of the ladies. Orgies break out everywhere throughout the arena. Kenny smirks and waves to the crowd. He slides into the ring, snatching up a microphone from a UWF worker monkey on the way up.] Larson: This crowd hates Mercury so hard. Rick: What? Larson: Saaaaaaaaa-NAP! [Kenny brings the microphone to his lips. He scans the random orgies breaking out in the audience briefly before speaking…] Kenny Mercury: Who knew Duluth was so crazy… Rick: I was thinking the same thing… sheesh… Kenny Mercury: I DID! THAT'S WHO KNEW! Because…. Because I know EVERYTHING! Take a good luck at me, UWF. [Kenny holds out his outstretched arms and spins around for all to see. More people are probably blowing their loads and/or becoming sterile.] Kenny Mercury: This is the face and body of the FUTURE of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Rick: I swear to Rod he said that EXACT same thing two years ago… We're still waiting… Kenny Mercury: The fans have spoken, and I have been voted into the VENETIAN HOME RUMBLE--- Entire Audience: MUTHA FUCKAH~!!!!!!!!!!! Kenny Mercury: CAN IT, INFIDELS! Ass-hat shit-bats… The Venetian Home Rumble has long since been the most retarded thing in existence. We don't even know what number VHR this is… it depends on which VHR's have technically counted or not… But that's not important. What IS important is that Kenny Mercury is in the match, and that Kenny Mercury will destroy everyone. [The crowd boos… so hard.] Kenny Mercury: I know what you're thinking… you're thinking why would the technical wizard… the shooting star… the ring doctor… THE VERY DEFINITION OF PRECISION… The submission exhibtion, Kenny Mercury, give a rat's behind about the total lame- sauce match that is the Venetian Home Rumble? Larson: Mutha fuckah. Kenny Mercury: Well, because, damn it, I will show the world… just how friggin' AMAZING my technical skills are. YOU SEE… I WILL WIN THE VHR WITHOUT USING HARDCORE TACTICS! Forever proving that my technical prowess is far superior than anything you jokes can throw at me… if I haven't proved it enough yet. Sluts. Larson: YES! MERCURY IS ROD! Rick: He's going to get his ass kicked… Kenny Mercury: Now… there are five other teams of people looking to win the vacant tag team tiles… Not that it matters… let's go down the list... Larson… HIT ME WITH THAT FUCKIN LIST OF NINCOMPOOP TAP-OUT WHORES! [Kenny turns to Larson and points to him with his index finger.] Larson: Mike Montgomery and Clyde. Kenny Mercury: Jobbers. They'll kill each other anyways. NEXT~! Larson: The Communist and Joshua Justice. Kenny Mercury. Jobbers! They'll kill each other anyways! NEXT! Larson: Killer Dude and Nick Persia. Kenny Mercury: Well, Killer Dude is currently in therapy after his latest fight with his golf club and his partner…. Well… who is that again? NEXT~! Larson: Fizbane the Fabulous and Seymour Almasy. Kenny Mercury: A jobber and someone I've beaten MANY A TIME BEFORE! This is be the real prize, tearing Seymour a new one… AGAIN. For far too long he has been considered the best… while its OBVIOUS that I am by far superior. Consider the facts… I've beaten him several times… I take you down memory lane… Recall two years ago and the birth of Kenny Mercury from the ashes of Purple Haze. In Mercury's first two matches, Mercury was pitted against a gauntlet of the UWF's finest… Tsunami Oni, Danny Jaye, Lyle 'In Style' Wile, The Plague, Silent Rage, and Seymour Almasy…TWICE!. Okay… no one cares about Silent Rage… but Tsunami Oni, two time world champ and Hall of Famer… Danny Jaye… two time world champ and Hall of Famer… Lyle Wile… world champ and hall of famer… Seymour Almasy… two time world champ and hall of famer… and The Plague, long considered one of the best to never win the world title…. After me, of course. You see, I not only beat a long list of UWF greats, but I beat Seymour Almasy TWICE on top of that, and right after the other… SAS and WNU, with only a couple days rest. Mercury greater than all. Larson: There's also the teaming of Adien Pryde and Tsunami Oni. Kenny Mercury: PSHAW! Some jobber and a man that I totally whooped, as I just reminded you. So… as I win the VHR… possibly causes Tsunami Oni to tap… It will be clear too EVERYONE that I am the true #1 contender to the world title. So, no matter who wins at FANdemonium… Oni or Seymour… I SHALL BE THE NEXT IN LINE TO FACE…. And indefinitely defeat… THEM! [The crowd boos… SO HARD!] Kenny Mercury: Now there's one more matter I'd like to address before leaving you wieners… and that is my partner, Jamie Shock. Well… AS IF! As if I even need a partner… besides… Jamie may be an imbecile… Rick: Shock and Mercury on one team… the world's two largest egos collide…. Oh Rod, I'm going to get a head ache... Kenny Mercury: But he's an imbecile that knows how to win. Just look at his track record… Jamie Shock wins… SOMEHOW. But he wins… So… even though I don't even need him… I'm not worried about that douche. So now, UWF… I bid you adieu… ADIEU! See you at the VENETIAN HOME RUMBLE! Crowd: MUTHA FUCKAH!!!!!! Kenny Mercury: The Definition of Precision has spoken. Sluts. ['Stranglehold' hits the speakers and Kenny Mercury tosses the microphone over his shoulder and exits the arena. Now it's THE END….] [The END.]
