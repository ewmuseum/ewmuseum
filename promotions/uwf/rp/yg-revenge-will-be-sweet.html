<WNU> Revenge Will be Sweet

6 Nov 2001

The Plague

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Still Your UWF Hardcore Champion: **So not even a Lightsaber to the gut could dethrone The Plague as UWF Hardcore Champion. But enough of that cause in just two days The Plague will have an opportunity to once again kick Deke's ass. And that isn't it he gets to take out Mr. Jericho, and some Mystery Partner. He will do whatever it takes in order to finally take The World Heavyweight Champion down for good. Lets get to the scene it is outside a local hospital The Plague has just been released after observations by doctors. He is walking along side Jon Focker, The Plague's personal reporter. He is wearing a black Outcastz shirt, jeans and a black bandana. He is holding a suitcase as well. Jon turns to The Plague while walking and speaks.** Jon Focker: "So Plague what the hell did the doctors say about that slice in your side?" The Plague: "Slice, you want to see a slice?" **The Plague lifts up his shirt to reveal the inch deep gash which is being held together by staples. Jon flinches back and The Plague puts his shirt back down.** Jon Focker: "WOW, thats really fucked up looking." The Plague: "Your telling me. It hurts like a bitch too. So now since joining The UWF, I have had pins in my arm, god knows how many stitches, damn staples in my gut. What the hell is next? All to become and continue to be, The Hardcore Champion." **The Plague shrugs it off as they continue on in the parking lot back to Jon's car.** Jon Focker: "What's your take on your upcoming match?" The Plague: "WHAT!!?? I have a match for Wednesday?" **The Plague looks at Jon in disbelief.** Jon Focker: "Yup, it is Deke Aurora. . . " The Plague: "Hold on there I am in a match with Deke, yeah, finally. Too bad I am in barely no condition to fight him." **Jon waits for The Plague to finish and then continues.** Jon Focker: "That's not all. Also in the match is the man who made his surprise appearence at Ground Zero, Ben Jericho. And finally there will be a mystery opponent to be named later." The Plague: "Damn, how the hell did I get put in a match with names like that? Deke doesn't even want to speak about me anymore, it's like he has forgotten about what I did. And Ben Jericho, well he is aligned with Luca, that means he will be going straight after Deke's title. And all I want is to injure Deke, for good. I don't want his belt, I just want to help contribute to him lossing it. As for the mystery opponent, I could care less who it is, just as long as he doesn't get in my way off destroying Deke." **The Plague laughs as the get to Jon's car and stop outside of it.** Jon Focker: "I know you probably don't want to be bothered with questions right now, so I will ask you one more alright?" The Plague: "Yeah sure whatever, it is always one more question isn't it?" **Jon shakes that off and asks the question.** Jon Focker: "This really doesn't pertain to your match but it has to do with The UWF TV CHampion, Killer Dude. I watched his last promo, and he was stealing The Outcastz catch phrase "Built to Last" what do you think of that?" The Plague: "Killer Dude said that? That stupid ass son of a bitch. He ain't built for nothing except lossing. He beat crap ass guys for that title he holds. He will eventually lose that belt, whoever he will end up fighting next will kick his ass." **Jon clears his throat to get The Plague's attention.** Jon Focker: "Well if you want to know he is going against Toxic Shock." The Plague: "Really? Well it seems there will be a new TV Champ come Wednesday, I mean Toxic kicked my ass good, I managed to somehow survive, barely, but if he can do that to me, I sure as hell don't want to be Killer Dude. And for copyright infringement, I won't sue, but I might have to take matters into my own hands if Toxic leaves anything left." **With that the scene cuts away to black and then to the original image you all know by now, take a good look Killer Dude:**
