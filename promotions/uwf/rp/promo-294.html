UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #294

A fun filled night

31 Mar 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders branch office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Well, please let me be the first to get this party started. The scene opens up just outside the shed that has been labeled “UWF Shareholders Branch Office.” On either side of the double doors that swing outward to allow people into the shed are the IC Title on the left, and the Tag Team Title on the right. Inside, is sitting “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is have one of his A&W Beers with the masking tape over something. He also has setting on his “desk” a bunch of the UWF action figures and a giant pink stuffed teddy bear. There is also a phone which is shaped like a poodle. The only light is from Christmas lights which hang around the walls. In his opposite hand is an oversized pen and I do mean oversized, which he is writing stuff onto a piece of poster board. He is wearing a tie-dye shirt and a baseball cap with the UWF logo on it.) Kazee: (singing) I'm back in the saddle again, out where a friend is a friend. Where the lowly cattle feeds on the lowly jimson weed. I'm back in the saddle again. I'm ridin' the range once more, totin' my old forty-four. Where we sleep out every night, and the only law is right, I'm back in the saddle again……. (Kazee looks up, surprised to see the UWF camera there, but has a smile on his face, like usual.) Hey guys. I was just sitting in here. Been a pretty slow day here in the UWF Shareholders Branch Office, so I thought I’d spend some time with another one of my passions. I was just kinda sitting here doodling. What do you guys think…… (Kazee holds up the piece of poster board. On the poster board is the absolutely worst drawn stick figure of a cowboy riding a horse.) You like it? (The Camera tentatively shakes yet.) I knew y’all would. But it’s not done yet. I still got some work to do on it. I promise I’ll let y’all see it when it’s done…… But I know y’all aren’t here to watch me doodle. Y’all want me to say something about Renaissance. Y’all wanna know what I think about all of this all of what is going on. Well, who knows what is going on, or what is going to happen. All I know is that I am going to be busy that night. I’ve got two huge title matches. And not only do I have two huge title matches, they are back to back from what I can see on the card that they have out. Oh well, good times, I guess. Might as well be back to back and let the world see me at my best……. So let’s talk about the Tag Team Titles first, shall we. It’s myself and Monty against well, basically everyone in a battle royal for the belts. What a disadvantage we are going to be in too. We have all those great teams to wrestle against. We have all those great teams that think they should be on top and are where we are. Well, if they want to be wear we are, they are going to have to work for it. You gotta look at it this way….. (Kazee grabs the giant teddy bear and the figures that are setting on the desk.) See, I’m much more of a visual learner, and I thought that maybe it would be easier for some people if I were to give them a visual of what will happen then just talk about it. I know from my point of view that things will be so much easier anyway. And besides, it’ll be fun…….. So here we have Anarchy. (Kazee grabs the two action figures, one that look like Dai, and the other that looks like Pryde, and sets them on the desk.) Next, we have the Lustful Lullaby. (Kazee grabs the action figures that look like Jessica Horaldez and Meagan Heath, and set them on the desk next to the two Anarchy figures.) You know, I honestly don’t know how they got these action figures out so fast, although they are the loveliest part of my collection….. Let’s see, we also have Team AWF. (He places the El Turtle and the Tommy Waters figures next to the others.) The Local NVA Company. (He grabs those two, and examines them for a second.) Hey, wait a minute, you guys shouldn’t have the Tag Titles, and those are ours. (Kazee quickly grabs the Titles from around the wais of the NVA and places them next to the other 6. He puts the mini tag titles off to the side) And finally, we have Right to Party. (He grabs the Smokey Wead and Bubba figures and places them next to the other figures.) Now we have the teams in the ring and then the bell rings. (Kazee grabs a cow bell from somewhere, and rings it.) The teams all get started beating on each other and trying to win the belts. ( He randomly grabs different figures and rams them into each other over and over. He uses the Anarchy dolls to perform Total Anarchy on El Turtle. He sets Jessica Horaldez and Meagan Heath on top of each other in a 69 position.) Ooops, I think that was an accident. Of course, than again, it seems to be every guy’s fantasy to see them in this position. (He uses the Right to Party figures to throw the Local NVA off the table.) And while all these teams are fighting, all of a sudden in comes me and Monty, the Players Club. (He grabs the giant teddy bear and start moving it toward the massive pile’o figures.) And once we get there, it’s all over. (Kazee takes the teddy bear and smashes it down on the pile. Hey then use it to sweep everyone off the table, so that the teddy bear is the only thing left sitting on the table.) And the winner of the Tag Team Battle Royal is the Players Club. (Kazee takes the mini belts that were on the NVA Company and puts one on each ear of the teddy bear.) I hope this little demonstration helps. I and Monty are going to rule this battle royal, and we are going to keep the title belts in the players club. But of course the Lustful Lullaby are always welcome to come party with us afterward, cause everyone knows that know body parties like The Players Club…… Of course, like I said, I have more than one match at Renaissance, as would be expected of a duel champion. Outside of my Tag Team Championship match, I also have a match for my Intercontinental Championship. Against Moraless and Raid. Two men who I’m sure are very qualified to hold my belt…… Let’s look at each one for a moment shall we. (Kazee pulls out action figures of both Raid and Moraless.) First we have Moraless. Let’s think of what Moraless might say here. (Kazee puts the Moraless figure in front of him and moves it back and forth to simulate talking. He also does a very poor impersonation of him.) “Hahaha, look at me, I can’t take a joke. I don’t understand the difference between sarcasm and reality. I jumped to the other fed as a double agent when Monty and Kazee made fun of me. It’s ok though. I’m gonna win the belt because gosh darn it, I’m smart enough, I’m good enough, and gosh darn it, people like me.” (Kazee puts the Moraless figure down and also stops doing the voice) Well, ok man, if you say so. I’m not going to stand in your way…… And on the other side, we have Raid. We all know exactly what Raid is going to say, now don’t we children? (Kazee picks up the Raid figure, starts talking in a deep voice in another bad impersonation.) “Kazee you have lost your fire. You are not the man I once knew. You use to be so great when you were at my back. I could always count on you to “come”when I needed you too. Now I’m just jealous of you cause you have something else around your waist other than me. I’m going to put that fire back were it belongs.” (Kazee puts the Raid figure down and stops said impersonation) Yeah, we won’t touch that one. Let me demonstrate here something. Courtesy of course of the UWF line of action figures and accessories. (Kazee pulls out 3 small replica items, a chair, a table, and a ladder. He also pulls out an Ezra Kazee action figure.) Now where did that IC belt go, oh yes, now I remember. Sorry, these figures are a little old. (Kazee grabs the Joshua Dunn figure with the IC belt and carefully removes it from him.) Sorry man, I need to borrow this. (Kazee then places the IC belt above the ladder and somehow it stays in place) That’s impressive, huh? No strings or anything. (He then grabs the Raid action figure, places it on top of the ladder, and the Moraless action figure on top of the table.) Now that y’all have done all this talking, let me finish this for both of you. (He grabs the Kazee action figure, as well as the Raid action figure. He uses the Raid figure to put Kazee up in a Gorilla press, as if he is going to Bug Bomb him off the ladder through Moraless and the ladder. He then simulates the reversal of the Bug Bomb as he uses the Kazee figure to grab a hold of one of the Raid figures arms and arm drag it through the table. And Moraless, and the small table break. Then, for good measures, the live Kazee takes the replica chair, places it on top of the figures of Raid and Moraless, and then takes the Kazee figure and has it leap off the top of the ladder doing a Phoenix Splash on top of the chair and the victims figures of Raid and Moraless.) And once you two have been destroyed, I will climb to the top of the ladder, and grab my belt. (Kazee replicates this motion with the figure Kazee) And it will all be said and done….. So you see, it really doesn’t matter what y’all say, it’s just gonna be a grand ole fun time at Renaissance, and I will be the one having the most fun. And by the end of the night, not only will the Players Club regained their Tag Team Titles. (Kazee grabs the Kazee figure and a Mike Montgomery figure, places the tag team belts on them, clears all the other figures off the desk, and places Kazee and Monty on top of the table.) Not only will I have regained my IC Title. (Kazee takes the IC belt and places it on Kazee.) But Joshua Dunn will capture the World title. (Kazee takes the Joshua Dunn figure and puts the world title belt on him and stands him next to Kazee and Monty.) And then, Prime Alliance, Chaos Society, all you’ll be able to do is look up. Look up from the defeated positions which y’all shall lay, and watch as The Players Club establishes it dominance once again, here in the UWF. And watch as The Players Club rises to the top of the heap. If you look closely, it even happens now. (The camera man pans down to where all the figures are laying.) And then, it’s just a matter of time that as y’all try to rise to the top that the Players Club will crush y’all. (Kazee grabs the giant teddy bear and throws it down upon the pile of figures.) Good luck to all y’all, cause you sure as hell need it now. (Camera fades to black.)
