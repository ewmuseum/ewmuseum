UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #698

6 man or 4 on 2?

Sunday, September 12, 2004 (year unrecorded)

Mike Montgomery — UWF Headquarters

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Today's promo opens on the roof of the UWF's Chicago offices. A Bell 210 Jet Ranger lands on the roof's helipad. Gavin Frost, bodyguard to the World Heavyweight Champion, waits patiently at the roof access door, briefcase in hand. Two crewmen, ducking down to avoid the still turning blades, quickly run up to the chopper with a two step staircase. They open the cabin door before Mike Montgomery, Executive VP of Talent Relations AND heavyweight champ, steps down and quickly makes his way over to Frost. The crewmen remove the steps and run off as the pilot finishes shutting down the helicopter. Frost opens the door for Montgomery and the two head down a staircase to the top floor of the building. Frost pushes the down button, and the two step into the immediately opening elevator doors. 'Well, Mr. Frost, so far I must say our association has been a most profitable one. Ten days with you watching my back, and my world title is returned to me. Tell me Mr. Frost, what's next on our agenda?' 'Six man tag on SAS,' Frost answers, 'you, the Plague, and the Communist against Adien Pryde, Tsunami Oni, and Nick Persia.' Montgomery considers this a moment, 'Me, the Plague and the Communist eh? Well, that should be interesting. I'm not sure if you've heard, but the Plague and I have something of a history.' Frost nods, 'I hadn't been aware of that...until Plague's recent promo. He's not overly happy with you.' Montgomery smiles, chuckling half to himself, 'He never is.' The elevator issues the traditional ding before stopping. The twin doors slide open, admitting the two men onto the building's twelfth floor. They step out, walking down the hall toward the UWF's Talent Relations division, specifically Montgomery's office. 'Plague's been demanding to know why you were at ringside,' Frost says, continuing his report, 'he's gone so far as to insinuate a threat against your world title reign.' 'Well, the Plague needs to realize something. Quite simply, he can make all the threats he wants. He can make all the demands he wants...it just doesn't matter.' Montgomery stops for a moment, taking the messages from his secretary as he accompanies Frost into his office. 'This is my company,' Montgomery continues, 'if I want to be at ringside, that's where I'll be. Now, I take it Plague took the traditional 'I'll win it with or without you' approach, right?' Frost nods, 'That's exactly right.' 'Well, nice to see Plague's as predictable as ever. In this case, though, I'm thinking that's a good thing.' Montgomery walks around his desk, sliding into his chair, 'As far as Sunday goes, this is what we have. The power of the Communist, the anger and unbridled fury of the Plague, and...well, me. Coming up against that is that incompetent little twit who frankly I didn't know we were still paying, Adien Pryde, my surprisingly silent challenger, Tsunami Oni, and my own number two man, Nick Persia. Seriously, one has to wonder who the hell books this stuff.' Frost takes a seat on the opposite side of the desk from Montgomery, 'Well, it sounds like it will work to your advantage, no matter what.' 'It does, doesn't it?' Montgomery asks, 'In fact, there are all sorts of ways I could spin this, especially after Wednesday. I must say, picking up the #1 contender's girlfriend...I've truly outdone myself.' 'Another man's woman...' Frost muses, 'you're playing with fire there. You may well end up giving Tsunami Oni more than enough motivation to take the title from you.' Montgomery shakes his head, 'No, I don't think so. You see, let's look at our last world champion. Removing his girl from the equation made him volatile, emotional...enraged. Where is he now? Comatose.' 'True, but Joshua Dunn's no Tsunami Oni.' 'No, that's true. He's not. If all goes according to plan, Shiatsu will be absolutely convinced that she's been cheating on Oni with me. She'll be convinced that she doesn't remember it because of the concussion she suffered at Terror in Tokyo. A situation she was involved with because of...' '...Tsunami Oni.' Frost finishes. 'Exactly. And once I offer her the one thing that Tsunami Oni just can't seem to...namely protection, she joins me willingly, and everything else just falls into place.' 'Still, I'd advise caution.' Frost says. 'Caution?' Montgomery asks, actually somewhat surprised. 'On the surface, this is almost a four on two handicap match, but people are unpredictable. I've often found that no matter what you plan for, no matter how much thought you put into it, there's always going to be something to cause a problem.' 'Best laid plans and all that, eh Mr. Frost?' Montgomery asks. 'Essentially, yes.' 'Well, if things do go south, I guess there's really only one thing we can do,' Montgomery says. He stands and turns toward the window. Hands clasped behind his back, he adds, 'we activate our contingency.' Frost leans forward a bit in his chair, “You’ve heard the latest from Adien Pryde?” “No,” Montgomery sighed, “summarize for me?” Frost pulls a manilla folder out of the briefcase and places the briefcase on the table. Opening the folder, he scans it briefly, “Let me see…understandable concern for Chaos Society manager Joanna…he claims you and the Plague are ‘scum’ and ‘cheap pieces of shit’ for your actions toward Shiatsu and Joanna respectively…you are the past, he and Nick Perisa the future…it’s now his time to shine…” Mike’s shoulders drooped for an instant, a second, louder sigh being heard, “Does no one understand the word creativity anymore? Does NOBODY pay attention to what happens around here? People like Pryde and the Plague need to understand something. It’s not their time. It’s not their time until I say it’s their time. This is my time. I am the one at the top, and I have no intention of being dislodged from my perch. Tonight, I shall simply have to show them that.” “Still,” Frost mutters, “it’s ‘any given Sunday’ and all that.” “No, not exactly Mr. Frost. It’s not any given Sunday. It’s my Sunday. This Sunday, as with every future show until I decide otherwise, it’s all about me. Oni, Pryde and even Nick will have to deal with that. What’s next on the agenda?” To be continued during GTT 4 in 'Jimmy Who?'
