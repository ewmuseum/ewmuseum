UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #154

Fantasies Final Part 1

12/16 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Almasy home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Aerith…..I must know. I must know who I am.” Seymour Almasy has been through amnesia, psychosis, and lord knows how many other mental disorders in his life. But through it all, one thought has remained in his head. Normalcy. Seymour can likely never be normal. To tell him that the world isn’t an RPG would shatter him, destroy him beyond repair. At least, however, he can return to his normal deluded life. With Aerith at his side. In married bliss. But Seymour faces the challenge of his career at the Contest of Champions. He must go toe to toe with the Universal Wrestling Federation’s ultimate evil. Jamie Shock is the next challenger for the Final Fantasy. Almasy is ready. Kind of. _____________ Seymour Almasy: Aerith….I think it goes without saying that this is the most difficult battle of my career. Aerith looks at her husband, at the solemn expression on his face. There is something deeper there than worrying about a match. Aerith Heartilly: Seymour…what’s wrong? Seymour Almasy: I’m tired, Aerith. Aerith Heartilly: Awww, then go to sleep, sweetie! Seymour Almasy: Not tired like that, Aerith. Just…tired. Tired of battle. Tired of everything. The look in Seymour’s eyes alarms her. It is a look equal parts exhausted and frightened. Afraid of him closing up, she steps forward, gathering Almasy into her arms desperately. Aerith Heartilly: It’s okay Seymour! This happens to everyone. Everyone doubts themselves at some point, everyone gets frightened. Seymour Almasy: Aerith…I don’t know who I am. You told me yourself. I have amnesia. I have no idea if or when my memories will come back. All I am is a warrior. A warrior fighting without a reason. Aerith Heartilly: You have a very good reason! You want to fight evil! Seymour shakes his head, placing a finger delicately on her lips. Seymour Almasy: But that’s not the whole reason. The question is, WHY do I fight evil? That question is one that is not answered so easily. Unless I know why, I cannot fight and defeat Jamie Shock. A pained look comes over Aerith’s face. She sighs deeply, looking at her husband, unable to lie. About to tell him something no one should have to tell one they love. Aerith Heartilly: You do have a reason. You just don’t remember it. Seymour Almasy: Out with it, Aerith.. His eyes searched hers, finding the dread held within. He braced himself, knowing that he was likely not in for good news. Seymour Almasy: Tell me, Aerith. I do not care if it hurts me. I must know. Aerith Heartilly: You know, Seymour….I’ve known you my entire life. Since you were a child. I was your best friend…no, your only friend. You fight evil because of all the evil you have confronted in your life. The words seem to trigger something in Seymour. Long buried memories of a childhood of hell resurface in Seymour, and tears begin to fall from Seymour Almasy’s eyes. He weeps bitterly, arms shaking. Seymour Almasy: You….you’re right, Aerith. That IS why. The images surface in Seymour’s head, coming at him like bullets from a gun. Being shunned by the other children in elementary school. His father’s death. The funeral. A childhood of torment. Seymour Almasy: My life has not been easy…but then, few people’s are. We are born, we live, and we die. What matters is the life stage. It is what we make it. Aerith Heartilly: You remember your childhood….do you remember the rest? Seymour nods. Seymour Almasy: Yes. Do you know, Aerith…that we’ve been doing this for nearly a year and a half now? Aerith Heartilly: I know. It seems a lot longer though. Seymour Almasy: That it does. We have been through a lot. Multiple championship victories. Our marriage. Aerith nodded, looking at her husband. Seymour Almasy: And Jamie Shock is just one more evil that must be vanquished. I’m going to get my armor. Wait here, okay? She smiles, as he kisses her. Seymour heads off, as Aerith’s facial expression immediately shifts. A look of concern hits her, as she settles back onto the couch of the Almasy home. Aerith Heartilly: Why did this have to happen? From her pocket, she pulls a single piece of paper. One that will change everything.
