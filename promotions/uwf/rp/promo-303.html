UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #303

Battle lines.

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pressure grows through life, at times it seems as though it is hard to get through the day, times like this when you have to give it your all grabbing life by the balls just to survive. Having to defend two title belts in one night is a harder task than acquiring them. When you are the champion all you consider is how you are going to stop yourself from winning. For me it was slightly different, I had a whole new perspective on things, I wasn’t worried about losing in the slightest, and I had done it last week against a far more dangerous opponent from XUW. This week I was concerned with winning and that’s why the ball was in my court. Also it is obviously clear that Ezra Kazee is no more than a simple delusional crackpot bastard who played with children’s toys…I well I liked to play with something a little bit bigger. Sat atop the twelve foot ladder I looked at the floor below me, it was only twelve feet me standing on my own shoulders, yet when you were up here you felt strength and worry two conflicting emotions equally as strong as each other, from up here I would stretch up and attempt to claim the title to be my own, I could turn around and leave a face imprint indented on Raid or Kazee’s face for a week and yet there was always that tiny little worry, what if you fell wrong even from this height the results to the neck, the spine could be fatal. I leapt off of the ladder and landed squarely on a large wooden table, resting on top of it cautiously as it bent slightly taking my weight with it, the idea was the same. At renaissance someone would be put through a table it was almost a given the only difference between this table and the ones that would be scattered around would be a lot thinner and wouldn’t be able to take my weight. If I jumped on one of those badgers I would have gone through without any bracing at all, maybe a blessing in disguise. I paced around the ring slowly looking around at the objects that would be my toys in just a few days and couldn’t help but smile, extreme was my domain, in the upcoming months I would be the PTC Extreme Champion, regardless of whether I had to beat Sean McClancey or Mike Montgomery for the title, to me this was nothing more than a playground. I had been thrown through glass had blocks of wood shattered across my chest and skull hell I had a cage fall on top of me just to defend that which I owned…that’s what Ezra is lacking. He doesn’t want to defend he just doesn’t want to be beaten. To want to defend you have to care about something yet Ezra doesn’t. Does he parade his title? No. Does he take care of it? No. He is even willing to have it hung above the ring; it’s leather being scratched and tainted and all the fall the gold could be tarnished or trodden on a lack of care is what Ezra brings to the ring. Will he take the belt away with him? More than likely for I have warmer things in the oven… The players club… The prime alliance… You have no idea what you are dealing with for weeks you fight back and forth and yet somehow I always end up being caught in the middle. Beelzebozo gets all pissy thinking I was sided with the Alliance, as did Montgomery and Kazee. Then the tables turned around and The Prime alliance thought I was against them then it dawned on me like the land of the rising sun. They all feared me. Why else would there be such trouble in the federation they could deny it all they wanted but I was a PRIME asset to the CLUB, well federation anyway I had seen it before three years ago in wCw I single handily destroyed two strong stables, both from the inside out but that is not my style this time round. No longer will I be a one-man army, my true colours shall be seen at renaissance but I want you all to remember this and remember this well. Come Renaissance my side will finally be chosen…and those I turn against shall tremble before our might for we… Are the past, the present and the future of UWF.
