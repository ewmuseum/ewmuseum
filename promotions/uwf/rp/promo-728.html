UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #728

Making an ass out of The Plague

10/0/2004 (year unrecorded)

Adien Pryde — Locker room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Scene opens to a locker room in your everyday gym. Adien Pryde has just gotten out of the shower and has nothing but a white towel. He is not paying attention till he almost walks right into the interviewer.] Pryde : Whoa, what the hell are you doing here? Don’t you even knock? Cameraman : I did but you didn’t answer. The shower must have drowned out the noise. Pryde : Whatever. What do you want? Can’t you see I’m busy? Interviewer : We just wanted to get your comment on your match with The Plague at Live and Loud 4. Pryde : My comments, and it couldn’t wait till I came out of the locker room? Well it’s simple. At Live and Loud I am taking my biggest step in the UWF. I am going to become the #1 contender to the Intercontinental Championship. The best two out of three match to prove just who is the best between the two of us, the old generation in The Plague or the new blood in me. Interviewer : Why a Hardcore match? Isn’t this giving to much of an advantage to The Plague? He is considered a legend when it comes to that match. You had full control of the stipulations and you chose that. Why? [Pryde takes a step toward the interviewer who takes a step back as Pryde pokes his finger into the mans chest.] Pryde : Why? You ask why? It’s simple, I am going to show the UWF that it doesn’t take a rocket scientist to be a hardcore icon. I have held a few hardcore championships in other federations and I don’t consider myself an icon. Hell, when I beat him in his own match it will just help elevate my value in all divisions. Interviewer : We all know your wrestling style favors the second match but Plague does have some submission maneuvers in his arsenal. Do you have any concern that that stipulation might come back to bite you in the ass? Pryde : Are you kidding? I have already proved to the world that I can do Plagues maneuvers better than him and I can have no fear that I can and will beat him in this match. Interviewer : Then there is the third match is a regular match. Why something simple? Pryde : You have to be kidding me. This is wrestling, and most are regular matches. If it come to a third match then why not have it a simple one on one match to prove just who is better. Now will you please leave so I can get dressed? Unless you want to be responsible for the FCC coming down on the UWF’s ass. Interviewer : Why, what do you mean? [Pryde turns around to walk towards the lockers and drops the towel to reveal his bare ass as the interviewer steps quickly in front of the camera.] Interviewer : And there you have it. The words right from the man’s mouth. Now back to your regular programming. [The camera fades to black as the interviewer complains about the FCC.]
