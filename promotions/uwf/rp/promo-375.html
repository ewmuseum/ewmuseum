UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #375

Class is back in session

27 Apr 2004

Raid — Random school

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens and you see Raid walking into a room. He abruptly slams the door shut. The camera man moves to the door and knocks on the door.] “Come on in.” [The camera man walks into the room and Raid is sitting behind a desk. The camera pans around to the other side of the room and you see a class.] “You’re just in time to watch my little presentation.” [He stands up and begins to walk around the room slowly.] “Ok class, today we are going to watch a video on how to effectively use chemicals to eliminate pests. Now before I begin does anyone have any questions?” [One of the students near the back raises his hand.] “What’s your question?” “Do those chemicals we use have any harmful effects on people?” “I’m glad you asked because today’s film answers that exact question. Now if there aren’t any further questions, on to the movie.” [Raid walks over to the TV, turns it on and presses play on the VCR. The camera focuses on the smirking face of Raid, then on the TV] [(Steve then presses the button. After a second or two smoke starts to pour out of the cracks of the office, and then it quits a few minutes later. Raid walks over to the fancy double doors on the office and swings them open. A large cloud of smoke pours out of the now open doorway. The cloud of smoke engulfs him and he seems to be enjoying it.) Raid “Ahhh, can you smell that! I love it!” Steve “Love what? That smells horrible (as he covers his face)!” Raid “The smell of dead rotting roaches delights me. And now for the final touch.” (Raid pulls the can of A&W BEER out of his bag. He then begins to make his way into the office. Steve is closely following him.) Steve “What’s the final touch? Come on tell me.” Raid “Oh you’ll see.” (Raid places the can down on the desk inside of the office. He places it so it faces the chair that is behind the desk.) Raid “Just you wait Kazee! This can of A&W BEER will teach you a lesson that you will never forget. I’m coming for you Kazee and you had better watch your back. Oh yea, and if your watching this Kazee I hope you enjoy this can of beer. MUWHAHAHA!!!”] “Now this is where the movie will answer your question, so pay close attention.” [The screen goes black for a second but then the film is back, but this time we see the UWF office from a different angle. You see the desk from a wide shot, and the room looks the same as you last saw it. There is nothing going on for about a minute, but then all of a sudden you see the door open, and in walks “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He goes over to his desk and sits down. He then begins riffling through his papers. He then looks up for a brief second, and he gets a goofy grin on his face. He starts to lick his lips and rub his hands together, and then he takes to can off of the desk. He inspects it for a second, and not realizing who put it there he begins to open it up. Just as he cracks the seal on the lid, the top of the can blows off and smoke begins to pour out. He looks puzzled for a moment, but then as he catches a whiff of the odor, he leans to the side of his desk and vomits. He tries to take control of him self but the smell it to powerful and he explodes again. He tries top stand up, but as he does he topples over into the pool of vomit that he has created on the floor. Once on the floor he proceeds to pass out. All of a sudden the door to the office opens and in walks Raid. He casually walks over to the desk and sits down on it. He looks down at the helpless Kazee and he begins to laugh hysterically. He abruptly stops laughing and stares into the heart and soul of the camera. A very eerie and dominating look makes it way onto his face. “I hoped all of you enjoyed the show, but don’t worry this won’t be the last time you see me standing over a helpless and laid out Kazee. Did all of you think I have forgotten about how Kazee and the rest of the Players Club humiliated me? I never forget ANYTHING! You see I had to wait to exact my revenge; I had to wait until Kazee showed signs of weakness. I know that there will be some sort of retaliation by the Players Club, but I am prepared for it. Kazee I want your ass in a match. I don’t care when, I don’t care where, and I don’t care what type of match it is, but the match WILL be for your precious Intercontinental Title. And I will take it from you MUWHAHAHAHA!!!!!” [The video then goes fuzzy and cuts out.] “Well there you have it class. That is what chemicals can do to your untrained bodies. But, did you notice how my trained body could handle the toxins? One day you all will be able to handle the poisons like I did, oh what the hell I think its time for you all to experience the gas chamber. Now don’t worry I won’t use the extremely harmful gasses, at least not yet.” [The class leaves and both the camera man and Raid are left standing in the room.] “God its great to get paid to cause others pain and suffering! I mean where else can I boss people around, yell at them, and be just down right mean.” [Raid walks over to his desk and picks up some papers and looks through them. He stops on one and seems to be reading it. As he is done reading it he chuckles a little.] “Who the hell is this PHD fella? The name doesn’t sound familiar. Oh yea, I remember now. I have a match with him tomorrow night.” [He looks at the paper again.] “He looks impressive, and his past wrestling experience looks pretty good, but why in the hell does he deserve a shot at my title. Oh well I guess he will just be the first man to fall at the hands of RAID. Look out PHD I’m gonna teach you a lesson that you won’t forget.” [Raid throws the papers across the room and he walks out the door. The camera zooms in on the door and then fades to black.]
