UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #255

a word...

28 Feb 2004

Joshua Dunn — Backstage UWF

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[So, finally we have had word from Joshua Dunn’s opponents, both Kenny Mercury and Beezlebozo have had their say on their view of the main event on Sunday. Iron lung has also made a bumbling attempt to console Joshua on his partners abilities, but what the Communist Scotsman has to say on this matter we don’t quite know yet. But what we do know is that after Mercury’s allegations of paedophilia against him, Dunn can be anything but pleased] [The Scene opens in the dressing room of the Scotsman in question, without the palaver of walking through the door, which possibly could have been a recurring mistake on the cameraman’s part. But we can see Joshua Dunn, alone in the room, sitting on his red and yellow commie couch, feet up on the coffee table arms behind his head, staring straight into the camera. That look, it looks so very… calm? At the time where he’s been accused of being a paedophile by more than one person and threatened by a psychotic mad man dressed as a clown he’s calm? This makes no sense, or it could make perfect sense… He’s wearing that red T-Shirt and his muscles gleam as if just through with some exercise, in fact that could well be true, as there is a sweat stain around the collar of the T-shirt. On his neck though, lies a gold chain, recovered from where he discarded it, the emblem of communism now shines around his neck once more for all to see. Still there’s that look of calm, Dunn just looks deeply into the camera, his mouth turned up at the corners. He drops his feet to the floor and leans forward, his expression still unchanged] JD: Well, Sundays coming up fast, looks like we’re running out of air time… [The grin gets deeper almost frighteningly, and his eyes narrow, exaggerating the effect] JD: Well, aren’t we the clever one’s eh Beezlebozo? Found your driving force again? That’s great, for all the good it will do you [Dunn snorts, the air gushing out his nose making the noise similar to a bull getting ready to charge] JD: Anger’s all well and good, but just like love or despair is a driving force, anger is an emotion, a flaw, it makes you make mistakes, Have you ever wondered why Ryder never used his own stipulation to his advantage? His anger towards you clouded his judgement, he made a mistake, he failed because of that one flaw in his character, and the same flaw you share with him. Unbridled hate. [Dunn leans back and waves a hand dismissively] JD: Sure you’ll say that anger is your ultimate driving force, the thing that drove you to become World Champion, but as you have shown, that feeling is not always there, you lose your anger, you lose your ability. This my friend is what your weakness is, once your sapped of anger, what’s left? A clown in his own pit of self-delusion. It will be great to see how long your anger can last in the ring, because once you’ve started the beating, your anger will dissipate, fade away to nothing, then what will happen? Once that anger that fuelled your abilities is gone you’re running on empty and then my friend, you’re through. [Dunn grins again, like a predator that has just found a limping deer] JD: Of course the problem is making that anger dissipate. That will cause me a minor inconvenience, but I know you shall eventually run out of steam. Emotions are the incorrect way to find you strength. Because eventually they let you down, you grow to love, soon your heart breaks, you grow to hate, as you’ve shown, you grow content. You’re balancing on a tightrope, you fall off, and your anger will be lost forever, nothing would bring it back. You have to remember that eventually that fuel will run dry. What then? What will replace it? Love? [Another snort] JD: I think you’ve already shown everyone your incapable of that. It’s only your anger that keeps you going, without it you fumble and fall. I am not encumbered with anything like that. I’m driven by the thrill of athleticism. The joy of confrontation. I hunger for the challenges that stretch my limits, no matter what, there is always a challenge waiting to be attempted, I have the correct motivation. Because during a match up the competition doesn’t dissipate like anger, love or vengeance would. I live for competition. [Dunn’s face falls back into that calm, disconcerting smile that is probably more fearsome than staring into death itself, it says “you know your gonna die, but how do you think it’s going to happen?”] JD: Your partner lives for himself, another flawed driving force, because every defeat causes that force to diminish, the bigger the defeat the bigger the chip off the shoulder. And if you want proof that his spouse has been kidnapped then here she is, I ran in and got her out of his locker room myself, and yes he did say those awful things about you because of it. And here she is… [Dunn fishes off screen for something and returns, and in his hand is a box, the name on it is “Pump up Polly: Pump her up and pump her good”] Straight from Kenny Mercury’s dressing room, the thing that he lives for, his blow up doll, which he uses to make himself feel better after every loss he suffers, which, we have to be honest, is more often than not. [Dunn smirks, he knows the reaction this is going to give, hopefully Beezle’s anger will last till the match] JD: I do hope you stay angry Bozo, because then I can truly see what the World Champion is made of, and test the water so to speak [The grin of a predator flashes across his face again] JD: But there are a few things I’d like to say to Kenny boy. You talk about being the definition of precision, how your technical game is better than anyone else’s in the federation [He makes a single solitary “Ha” noise in the back of his throat, almost reminiscent of a growl] JD: Great talk from a man who never held the Shoot wrestling title, who never got beyond the THC division, who has never had a single worthwhile match in his entire career. I mean some people are natural born jokers, and you mercury make me laugh harder than I do at the Fool, and that man is an imbecile. Your mouth seems to have more control than your mind, and that is another of your fatal flaws. Of course it’s important to talk big about yourself, but you’ve got nothing to back it up with, what have you done here? You’ve held the TV and THC titles, both of which you yourself have dismissed as pathetic wastes of metal. You seem to have an impressive history of nothing… you’ve done nothing, you’ve beaten no one, you are nothing. A cist on the ass of humanity, as big a waste as the titles you’ve held. [He pauses briefly for a moment, letting all he just said sink in to those watching] JD: If anyone has to make you eat your words once more you’ll start to choke. On SAS I have the pleasure to face the World Champion, and the honour of kicking Mercury’s head in, and if you’re not careful Mercury, Beezlebozo will be kicking it too. I have just one other person who’s in the mix… Iron Lung [He hangs his head and shakes it letting out a small sigh] JD: Just keep out of the way kid, you’ll get hurt [The Scene Fades to black]
