Line em up!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder walking down a busy street in Tulsa, Oklahoma. There is a cameraman walking next to him filming the promo, and a man wearing a dark blue suit is walking with him carrying a microphone. Ryder is wearing a black jacket with the Prime Alliance logo in red letters on the back and jeans. ) Man: Hello UWF fans and welcome to a special edition of Ryding Shotgun, where I tag along with different wrestlers and find out just what makes them tick. We're here today with the man who is trying to insert himself into a match for the UWF World Heavyweight Title against Joshua Dunn and the current champ Beelzebozo. Mr Ryder, how do you think you'll get yourself into this matchup? * The man holds the mic up to Ryder who stops and slowly turns to the man before leaning in * Ryder: Who the hell are you? Man: Oh i'm sorry, my name is Askin Z. Question. Ryder: What the...whatever man, I ain't even thinkin about tryin to get into their match right now. What's on my mind right now is getting Malcom Kennedy to join the Prime Alliance once again and at the top of the list is this all comers tag match that we have in just a few days. Other than those two subjects right there the rest of the world can fuck off. Askin: Did you happen to catch Beelzebozo's last promo? Ryder: yea I saw that shit and it was fuckin hilarious. I mean first of all, I did a promo before leaving the building, and there wasn't a chance in hell that I was runnin from that sorry ass bastard, because I know and he knows that had he caught me he'd have gotten his ass whipped. Second of all, i'm gonna get my hands on him again and when I do it won't be pretty. And last of all, Malcom Kennedy has made enough money to buy and sell Bozo's idiot ass so what makes him think that Kennedy even sweats him putting that guy through the windshield. He's a fuckin moron. Askin: Mr. Ryder, could I get you to watch your language? * Ryder just looks at the man with a pissed expression * Ryder: Fuck you, does that answer your question? Askin: Sorry sir, it's just that children do see our tv show here and I wouldn't want to corrupt them by using bad language. Ryder: Isn't that sweet. Children are gonna hear bad language no matter how much you try to shelter them so what good is me trying to change who I am for some little kid that I don't even know. If it's a Player's Club fan then hell yea I want the kid to cuss and maybe his mom & dad will bust his ass for it and then he can blame it on Montgomery. Is this shit almost over cause I got some ladys to see. Askin: Girl Scouts? Ryder: They definately got some cookies I wanna nibble on if you know what i'm sayin. * Ryder hits the guy in the shoulder and the man just rubs his arm clearly not understaning what the Cherokee Thrilla was trying to say * Ryder: Nevermind. Anyways, wrap this shit up cause I got places to be. Askin: Ok, umm any final comments before we end the segment? Ryder: I'm glad you asked, because hell yes I got something else to say. The all comers match is gonna be a showstoppin event if people weren't too much of a pussy to show up. I mean hell how long have we known about this match and i'm the only damn person willing to mention it? Show some sack boys and speak up. I know ya'll are chickenshit, but damn at least give me something to work with. Prime Alliance is always gonna come out on top, but if ever there was a chance to beat us this is it. It ain't happening though, because we are at the top of our game and we know how to get the job done. We can and will dominate any team that is put in front of us. Bozo, you can lick my asscrack, because if you're put in that match i'll beat your head in and make it two wins to your measley one which you had to cheat to get. I hope you show up with a partner, because i'm gonna love beating the shit out of you. As for Joshua Dunn, if you wanna step through the ropes little man, you go right ahead. I got somethin for you and the partner of your choosing. You can step into the ring, but you'll go flyin right back out and that's a promise. I'm comin for both of you. Like it or not someone's gonna lose that title to the True Indian Superstar and that's a fact. Now I got some ladies to attend to so you can get the fuck outta my face. ( Ryder runs across the street leaving the man on the curb in an unfamiliar neighborhood as people start to surround him and his cameraman. The scene fades just as someone grabs him by his tie and comes to an end. )
