Running the Lesbian Gauntlet (Quick Cuz I Feel Terrible right now)

July 28 (year unrecorded)

Slasher

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Funny, how a man who is known to all by nothing more than a simple garbage wrestler can reach such heights. For somebody who is known for only being a total nut ball as opposed to a great wrestler can achieve things that other's dream about. Well the dream had been captured, and the dream is being lived out. Slasher has finally realized his dream of being Tag Team Champions with his fellow drowingPool friend, Unforgiven. The two had deftead Moraless and Digital Mortality to win the tag team championship. The glory was indeed cut short, by the Formorions taking a bitch fit. They brought in the Lethal Lesbian to stop the two champs from running rampant on everyone. The DP did attack the Formorians, but only because everyone expected an attack from them. Don't get it wrong, the DP could care less who they pound on. It could be your Momma as far as we are concerned, they simply don't care. So the Formorians are out of business, and they get the Lesbian to take care of things. Standing behind a women, one of the least manly things you can do. The Formorians are nothing more than Irish pieces of shit who need a women to run things for them. If you need management to take care of your business, you must be a total wuss ass. So whats going on with the DP? Well the Lesbian is forcing them into a Gauntlet or sorts. Each member must face a chosen opponent in a match for their tag titles. 2 out of 3, which pits Misfit against Kenny Mercury, Unforgiven against The Leprechaun, and Slasher against Corporal Punishment. The Corp and Slasher, they are not strangers. Slasher has one victory over the Corp, and the Corp has two on him. However, Slasher has never tapped to the Lethal Injection, he has passed out twice. So this time, it will be different, Slasher will defeat him, because now Slasher has something to fight for. The Tag Gold, something he holds dear. Tonight, Slasher defeats the Corp, and the DP goes on to keep thier tag titles for good. [The locker room, the most obvious spot for a segment, well thats where we are. The locker room of the DP, which is covered in posters of various bands and such. Slasher can be seen sitting in his wrestling gear by himself in the room. His travel bag sitting next to him, and his Tag Team Title is in his hands. He is staring down at it, seemingly lost amongst the gold detail and the UWF logo. The camera draws in closer and Slasher lifts the title up and draps it over his shoulder. Slasher stands up and raises his face to the camera. He is not wearing his mask, but his hair is covering his face somewhat. His mouth and lower chin can be made out, and a large scar can be seen going up from his chin.] Slasher: Tag Team Gold... me and Unforgiven... Momma I did it... I won the title! [Slasher can be seen with a small smile growing on his face.] Slasher: It seems Unforgiven and I have reached the top of the mountain, which is somewhere we both have always been, just not recognized with gold. Something I have wanted for a long time, this is not my first taste of gold, but my first taste that I actually enjoy. The THC title is my baby, I will admit that, but World Title Gold is something I was always told I would never achieve. Well here is the proof you ignorant pricks! SLASHER IS ONE OF THE TAG TEAM CHAMPS! [Slasher takes the belt and sets it down next to him.] Slasher: But as of right now, people are trying to ruin that.. somebody that I like to call The Fucking Fisheater! [Slasher turns his back and grabs his mask. He slides it on over his face.] Slasher: We all know how I feel about women.. I like to think my example out of Sam Gordon showed you all. I have no problem beating the living shit out of a women. I just don't care.. it could be your mother or wife.. I don't care. I got no problem breaking a bitch's nose or jaw. So Fisheater... I will let this one slide because I get to beat the hell out of the Corporal, something I have been waiting to do for years now. Corp, me and you go way back.. back to the GIWF... where I beat you, and you beat me twice. The Lethal Injection... hehee.. yeah it hurts alright.. but did I ever tap? NO!!! I NEVER TAPPED OUT ONCE! I PASSED OUT TWICE FROM PAIN.. BUT I NEVER QUIT YOU ANAL RAPING FAGGOT BASTARD! Too bad Pete Steele isn't still around, he would have beaten you within an inch of your Faggot Ass Life! [Slasher sits down on the bench next to his belt.] Slasher: I really think all that time in the prison's has turned you a bit to the gay side my friend.. I mean any man who enjoys sticking things in another mans ass has problems. Instead of being called Corporal Punishment you should be called Colon Punishment.. a nick name for your favourite past time. I will tell you this you faggot.. you dare try to do that to me.. I will break your arms.. your legs.. and your back! You won't ever touch another man's ass again.. which for you would be a fate greater than death! [Slasher lets out a slight chuckle.] Slasher: Look Corporal.. I mean Colon.. I know your a fiece man in that ring.. probably in bed as well but thats beside the point.... tonight I am gonna to beat you within an inch of your life. The match is in my rules.. THC Rules my friend.. something I specialize in. I swear to god, tonight.. your will get dropped on your head with the Skin Ticket.. and you won't be getting back up anytime soon. I have something to fight for besides myself this time.. I am fighting to keep my title, and Unforgiven is fighting to keep his. Misfit is als involved in this, and we are all going to overcome it. We all know Misfit will crush that backstabbing Mercury.. and Unforgiven will crush The Irish Bastard.. and your already in a rundown with a transport. Your going to get steamrolled Corp.. and why? Because you follow the lead of a ignorant lesbian bitch! [Slasher stands up, grabs his belt and faces the camera.] Slasher: Last words here Colon.... tonight your mine.. your my bitch this time you faggot.. your already a walking dead man.. and I plan on slamming you firmly into your grave. Tonight Colon, your reign of terror ends.. Slasher is putting and end to you... and a start to his REIGN OF TERROR! [Slasher grabs the camera and slams it down to the floor.] Slasher: TONIGHT YOU DIE YOU FAGGOT BASTARD!!!!!!!! SEE YOU SOON!!!!!!!!! [Slasher stomps down on the camera breaking the lens and making the scren.. FADE TO BLACK!]
