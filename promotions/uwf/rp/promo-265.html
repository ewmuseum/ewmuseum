UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #265

A moment of his time

3 Mar 2003

Joshua Dunn — A Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Another win notched up on what could be the longest undefeated run in the UWF, Joshua Dunn, the Communist Scotsman and alleged #1 Contender for the World Championship managed to pull off another win after being attacked by Ruff Ryder moments before with a baseball bat. That is some feat in itself, and possibly leaving Ruff Ryder writhing, as moments before he set up Beezlebozo to win the Tag Team match up. Why did Ruff Ryder attack? We’re not quite sure, the fool seems to think this will make him a more adequate choice to go for the World Title, but he may have spoiled his only chance on getting inserted into the match. But onto this weeks SAS, Joshua Dunn is again on UWF TV, again against Kenny Mercury, with a special guest referee, The World Champion Beezlebozo… But you already knew that right?] [The Scene Opens, we can see a whole room filled with keep fit equipment, from bench presses to treadmills. We can’t see anyone, but we can HEAR some one, there is an audible breathing noise, accompanied by the sound of feet landing, and something very soft going “thud” every so often in a steady rhythm. The camera turns to see the origin of the noises, and we can see him, limbering up style, Joshua Dunn is pummelling a 4-foot punch bag suspended form the ceiling, bare knuckled. He’s wearing black jogger bottoms and a red muscle shirt that is almost drenched through and through with sweat, darkening the vivid primary colour. All his skin that is showing is gleaming with sweat; obviously we have joined him a long time after he started training in this Gym. Another small detail that is noticeable, the chain sporting the communist emblem of the hammer and sickle is back round his neck, currently bouncing up and down as Dunn lays into the punch bag. Still looking uninjured from Ryder’s attack last SAS. A flick of his eye shows us that he has noticed the camera, but he continues on hitting the punch bag] JD: So, you want a few words about last SAS? [The camera moves up and down for a while as if to say “yes”] JD: Thought you might [Dunn continues punching, the bag swings back and forth, coming back to be met with the fist of Dunn again, we can just hear the faintest sound of chains rattling at the top link of the punch bag] JD: Ryder has to have some guts; I mean it must take a really big man to run down to the ring with a weapon to a group of unarmed men. Who exactly do you think you are Ryder? Well, I have chosen to ignore your little outburst, because just like your plan to get interjected into MY World title shot, your plan to screw me out on that match was flawed. First of all you lay an unconscious man on top of me thinking that He’d pin me? Well, that little game came up short. [He gives one final hard right into the bag, and moves away leaving it swinging back and forth, and goes over and sits down on a bench, which has a towel draped over it and a black sports bag laying at it’s foot, open. He reaches inside and produces a bottle of water, which he takes a large swig out of and throws it back into the bag. He then picks up the towel, mops his brow] JD: I’m giving you a friendly warning Ryder not to turn up in any of my matches again, otherwise your little life won’t be worth living, and you may just find that your pay check comes in with one less zero in it. [An evil grin passes his face, accompanied by one single snort, as if it were an audible punctuation mark to emphasise what was just said] JD: Well, THIS SAS I face Mercury again, the so-called shooting star, the Technical Wizard, that’s big talk from the same man who has never held the shoot wrestling championship. Mercury, your mouth runs where you can’t keep up, all your talking blinds you to what is really going on, take last SAS, It was to my advantage. [Another smirk drifts across his face on recollection of that moment] JD: But this is not the only thing to make this match interesting… I do understand we have a special guest referee, Beezlebozo, the world champion. Well I do admit, being in the ring with him again is a privilege, seeing as most of the time UWF management seem to think I’d do better in the lower leagues. The only point that I don’t like about this is that you’re not my opponent. [Dunn pauses for a moment] JD: But that is still to look forward to, and I respect your judgement. I’m sure you’ll make a great referee for this match. After all, you heard all the things Mercury said about Hardcore Wrestling. Some people just don’t understand how to play the game. You and I…. We’re on the same wavelength, we know how to go about our business don’t we? Of course we do, that’s what makes us the best in the business. [Dunn reaches into the bag again and takes up the bottle to take another chug of water into his system to replace fluids lost during training] JD: Only a few things left to see, and one of these things is to see how far Mercury’s mouth runs from his control this time, but until that time, I still have some much needed training to be getting on with [Dunn places the bottle of water down, and shrugs off the towel and moves off camera to restart his training] [The Scene Fades to Black]
