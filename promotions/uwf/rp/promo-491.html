UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #491

The Celebration Begins

3 Jul 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up outside of a blue shed. The shed is a shed that is very familiar to most people, as the shed has the UWF Shareholders Branch Office sign upon it. A figure comes walking out of the door, shutting it behind him. The man is 6'4' tall with Brown hair. He is of the same size and build as the man we have seen outside of this office so often before. He is very similar in stature to 'The Common Man' Ezra Kazee. However, his face is painted in the discoball style with light blue and pink. He is wearing a purple leisure suit and has lots of jewelry upon him. He is carrying a staff with a multicolored jewel upon it. The man stands infront of the door and laughs for a moment. He plants the staff into the ground.) Man: Well, I'm sure y'all are wondering what I'm doing here. I'm sure you wonder why I'm standing infront of the Branch office. Well, because I can. It's just as much the wrestlers property as is Kazee's property. But that's besides the point...... Me and Kazee, we're old friends. We've known eachother forever. Nobody knows him as well as I do. Now if he wasn't so uptight, he would be so much better off. He' a great wrestler and a great person, but he is just uptight about everything for some reason. I'll never understand people like him...... I suppose though that people out there are wondering who I am. I'd hate to think y'all think that I'm someone else. My name is Fizbane the Fabulous. You all can call me Fizzy if you want, but that's up to you. I really don't care. And I am here in the UWF finally because, well, why not. It's gonna be fun. And that's all it's about is fun. If we can't have fun, then what is the point....... And it seems to me that my opponent on Wednesday is a person who takes things too serious as well. She did her promo from a gym, training. Seriously, what's that about. How can you expect to have a good life when you are spending most of your life training and working out. It doesn't seem like much of a life to me...... Me, on the other hand, I'm going to listen to some music, have a few beers, order a pizza, and popcorn wings, and dance the night away. Life is nothing more than a celebration. (Fizzy starts dancing in a very disco style dance and starts singing as well.) Celebration Let's all celebrate and have a good time Celebration We gonna celebrate and have a good time (Fizzy stops dancing and singing and turns his attention back to the camera) Your right, I may not know what I'm getting into, but what the hell, I really don't care. It's gonna be fun, that's all I know, and that's all I care. We are going to have a hell of a time and we are going to celebrate afterward...... You know Kitsune, your more than welcome to accompany me after the match too. I mean, it's gotta be tough out there and never having free time to unwind and make yourself feel good. Tell you what, after the match, you just come with old Fizzy for a while and I'll show you a good night on the town, because that's what you've been missing. Yep, me and you, ALL........ NIGHT........ LONG........ You need to know how to parrr-tayyy. You need to know how to unwind...... Our match, don't worry about it. It's just a match. After the match, that's when you should be ready for, cause baby, I'm going to show you something you ain't never seen before. (Fizzy laughs, picks the staff out of the ground, and starts to walk around behind the shed. There is a little bit of a commotion. All of a sudden there is a loud explosion and smoke rises up from behind the UWF Shareholder's Branch Office.).
