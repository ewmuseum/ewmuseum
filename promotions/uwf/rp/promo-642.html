On a roll (RR)

Today (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — Middle of nowhere

[Nick Persia. Two words, one man. More wins, less losses. But the victories he has had were easier to deal with then the losses he suffered. After each loss he had to pull himself together. He would need to let his wounds heal. He would need to review the match on tape and eveluate what’s wrong and need to improve. But he he no time to see if his problems are really solved because he has no match to try out his new strategy. But at the same time he needs to get his self esteem back. After all, he needs to make a good impression. If it isn’t for himself then it is for the fans. He never fights for himself alone. He fights for himself and the fans. That’s why he won. That’s why he became the longest running THC Champion ever. But now......everything seems to have changed. I mean, let’s take a look at the facts. Not two months ago the UWF would have had serious problems to organise a 20 men rumble. That would mean the whole roster had to participate. Now we can organise a 60 men rumble which is a very huge change indeed. Nobody knows how its possible and probably nobody ever wondered. Neither did Nick Persia. He had other things that required his attention. Like a Sean McClancy. Like a John “Moraless” Edwards. Like a Digital Morality. Like a Jamie Shock. Like a Tsunami Oni. Like a Eric Cameron. Like a Kenny Mercury. Like a Corporal Punishment. Like a Rich Rollins. Like the three losers of the world title match. Not randomly thrown out names. They are his biggest opponents. They are THE fishes to fry. If you are able to outsmart them you will for sure gain the respect of the fans and maybe more important: your peers. The roster. The guys who make fun with you or about you. And they better make fun with Nick Persia. Because the more fun they make the moe they feed him. The more they feed him is exactly how more greedy he gets. You see, if you want to make him more greedy go ahead and make fun of him. Just tell on national televisionn you are better than him. Those words will come back to haunt you. Just like Nick Persia always comes back to haunt you. There is nobody who can match his burning desire to win this match. There is nobody more willing to win this match than Nick Persia. Because you see, Nick Persia will grab this chance with two hands. There’s no doubt in his mind he will be the one winning. The one who will be delivering instead of receiving. You want to know why? Because he has a grudge with almost everybody. Corporal Punishment beat him. Sean McClancy beat him. Both fair and square. But they aren’t rid of Nick Persia. Now he’s back and better than ever. Now he’s back, fully equipped! Now he’s back with new knowledge. He learned from his past mistakes. He will be eager to meet them in the rumble. But he won’t lose his patience and attack before he gets the chance. But you don’t get chances, you create them. You need to set people up against each other. You need to confuse them and divert the attention from yourself. Finally, when all the smoke has cleared, you get away with the victory. And that’s exactly what Nick will do. No doubt.] Larson: “Hey, Persia! I was wondering if you have a minute?” Nick Persia: “For what? You want to do a rundown of every single wrestler that’s going to be in the Regal Rumble 60 men main event?” Larson: “No, actually I wanted...” Nick Persia: “What do you want? You want me to go and tell the people at home that I am going to win the whole rumble? You wanted me to tell the people I am going to throw the majority of the wrestlers in the match over the top rope so I will be the very last man standing? The last man standing inside that ring being the number one contender? The last man inside that ring, being able to go for two titles because I worked so hard I can go for two titles? Larson: “Well, no....” Nick Persia: “No? There is no denial. I am going to be the Tidal Wave Demon destroyer for Tsunami Oni when I kick his ass over the top rope that the only thing he will catch is the iron guard wall. While I am busy destoyring I could just as well take down the Communist’s wall and show him once again his victory was a fluke. Is that what you meant?” Larson: “I meant that...” Nick Persia: “You meant what? You probably meant that I will pummel Kenny Mercury in non existence in so many ways that there aren’t enough numbers on this world to give a real estimation? That his code will be broken by the Dutch destroyer? You meant that I will give some people wings so that they can fly into the sky after I threw them out of the rumble.” Larson: “Could I just....” Nick Persia: “Could you just what? Could I just tell you that Adien pryde really doesn’t stand a chance against such greats as myself and either one of the guys of The Player’s Club? That The Real deal isn’t that much of a deal. But that the only thing for real is his elimination in the regal Rumble?” Larson: “I wanted to......” Nick Persia: “To tell me that Kevin Starr would be the most dedicated man in this rumble because if he was in he would be the only guy to win the rumble two years in a row? I already knew that. What I also already knew is the fact that Malcom Kennedy will not be standing in the middle after the match is over.” Larson: “What about....” Nick Persia: “What about Corporal Punishment? That the guy is back in the hospital after he fought a couple of matches and now has a burn out for the next couple of months? And that he isn’t the only Knightmare Brigade member awaiting a terrible fate. Call it a date with destiny.” Larson: “He isn’t the...” Nick Persia: “He isn’t the only guy who doesn’t got his ass handed to him? I know, because I will be the delivery man in this match. Boy, will I have a mountain to climb in the match. I need to beat down the biggest ego in the business today namely Luke Warren.” Larson: “Would you let me finish.....” Nick Persia: “Let you finish? I didn’t even know you were in the rumble. Just like I am going to finish off all the returning superstars? That they really aren’t that much of a contender against the new name of the game: Nick Persia? That they will have another thing coming if they didn’t take me into account? All true.” Larson: “Just like it is true......” Nick Persia: “That Digital Mortality will be a few card short of a full deck? And that with a game of poker Moraless would be left with two azes and two eights? The deadman’s hand, that’s right! And that he will not be the only one suffering a knock out from the deadly hand? He, Moraless, the three losers of the world title match, Digital Mortality, The Plague and Jamie Shock are all after the world title but they could just as well forget about that title because it belonmgs to me now? And they only things they shouldn’t forget is their name because after the rumble nobody will remember it except for themselves?” Larson: “All I’m trying to say...” Nick Persia: “You shouldn’t try! Those people in the rumble are trying. But I am not trying! I am going in the rumble as a winner and I will step out of the rumble as a winner. Now speak up and say to me what’s on your chest because your trying to speak a full setence if miserably failing!” Larson: “Thanks....” Nick Persia: ‘You aren’t nervous, are you? Because the only one that should be nervous are my opponents in the rumble. The rest should be excited. Just like you. Relax.” Larson: “Something alike. Anyway, you have an advantage because there are some people who will need to fight twice on the same night.” Nick Persia: “Correction: One of the many advantages I hold over them. You see, I am bigger than them. I can deal more effectively with the pressure than them. Not only that, but I’m also the only guy winning at each and every pay per view. Say it ain’t so. But you’re right. Unlike usual.” Larson: “Are you aware of the fact you can challenge or hold for three titles if you eliminate Rich Rollins, win the rumble and use your stable wars victory shot?” Nick Persia: “I was PERFECTLY aware of that. Just like I am aware you did your research. Just like I did mine. Did you know that most people in the rumble actually never won one? Did you know that I am the only Dutch guy in the rumble? And did you know that El Turtle will be the first guy tossed out of the rumble?” Larson: “Speaking of El Turtle, he’s bringing his friends along.” Nick Persia: ‘Dear God, they are doing it again! Filling up the rumble with worthless competitors. Now I’ve count all the people who don’t stand a snowball’s chance in hell and guess how high the number was.” Larson: “Ten perhaps?” Nick Persia: “Completely wrong? Have you been living under a rock or something? Get real.” Larson: ‘Twenty?” Nick Persia: “Come again?” Larson: “Fourty then?” Nick Persia: “Oh my God, are you stupid or something? You put WAY too much faith into people.” Larson: “I know....fifty-nine.” Nick Persia: “I thought you would have never guessed it.” Larson: “But what about people like Moraless?” Nick Persia: “They will be more or less thrown over the top rope. Moraless...don’t make me laugh. The idiot that couldn't poor water out of a boot with instructions on the heel? The idiot that, if he threw a brick at the floor, he would miss? The moron who can't even speak three sentences straight? He might have a chance of winning the Regal Rumble if he wasn't so god damn dumb!' Larson: “And guys you never fought before like Jukei Katabura and The Shocker dave Storm? Couldn’t they cause some inconvenience of at least pull some out that you aren’t pleased with?” Nick Persia: “Definitely! The lame jokes he will be putting out of his ass. Jukei is by far the worst joker I must have ever witnessed on the whole planet, and dare I say it, he’s even worse than, let me say it again, dare I say it, The Fool. The again, we could just as well talk about the worst wrestler there is. The Shocker Dave Storm.” Larson: “Talking about bad wrestling, he isn’t the only one.” Nick Persia: “As hell he ain’t! We got carbage like Slasher, Unforgiven, Corporal Punishment and other hardcore freaks who think barbed wire should be used instead of a rope. They are all sick. I mean, I brought a little wrestling to hardcore. But they way they ‘wrestle’ isn’t exactly what i meant by it. The more appropriate name for them is redecoraters because the only thing they can do succesfully is bringing down the house in every sense of the word. They would use their own blood to paint the walls. Disgusting. There are no words that can express how shocked I was by their antics. But that’s all over now.” Larson: “Just like the Player’s Club is over now, but the connection seems to remain intact. How about that?” Nick Persia: ‘We all came to a mutual agreement that it was best for all parties to seperate ways. But if you can use each other’s help we’ll still accept it. Once a Player, always a Player. And I am a Player. In the ring I always come with my smacktics. In the ring I always come prepared. In the ring I always come to win.” Larson: “Do you have some words to the ones you didn’t mention?” Nick Persia: ‘Probably not worth mentioning or they slipped trough my mind. No words, all actions. I will let my actions in the ring speak for themselves. Just let them know I watched their tapes. And some do have a future like Rich Rollins. But it just won’t begin today.” Larson: “Even from people who make theior debut in the rumble?” Nick Persia: “What? Did I hear that right? Are they letting newbies in? They are really on a mission in the UWF Office. A mission to tarniosh the reputation of the World Title. Because then you’ll get involved in a war you really don’t want to be in.” Larson: ‘Just like some people really don’t want to be in the rumble.” Nick Persia: “Wow! Are you sick or something? That was one hell of a comment. Now get out and get yourself some candy as a reward for that outstanding comments. I’ll be off training.” Larson: “You are sick as well.” Nick Persia: “I would watch my steps if I were you. Otherwise you’ll be taking Shcok’s place in flying out first.” [Scene fades out with Nick Persia starting to train and Larson leaving off with some candy.]
