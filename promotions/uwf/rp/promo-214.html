UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #214

The Quest for THC

10 Feb 2004

Iron Lung — Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Quest for THC The Quest for THC Starring Iron Lung We enter our scene with a pitch black screen. Silence fills every crevice, you could hear the tip of a pin hit the floor. That was, until a slight tapping noise came into play. The sound began soft, but it would soon be a repetitious pattern, almost forming a beat as it hit another thing to stop the noise briefly. As a light gradually fills the room up, we focus on a wooden shaft, slowly banging against human flesh. More light enters the scene, and we get a clearer view of a bamboo stick hitting against the palm of a hand. As the camera moves down the bamboo, we notice many rings of tape wrapped around the end. The camera keeps revealing more of the scene as it pans. The edge of a black t-shirt can now be seen, and now as the scene rolls across our subjects chest, an 'IL Rocks' embled is revealed. Finally, we were able to solve the mystery of the subject of this interview. It was Iron Lung. A full shot of the number one contender to THC was now in order, and he stared upon us with his flourescent eyes, almost looking like he was setting us ablaze all the while. He kept tapping the bamboo shaft upon his palm, keeping calm and without any sign of pain on his face. He looked down upon his hand, seeing the red that his tapping had created. No emotion was shown as we got a close look at the slight cut he had formed from a removed sliver. The crimson color was natural to this Hardcore Icon, and in his line of work you couldn't be faint of heart from it. A slight grin came across Iron Lung's chisled face, the face of a man possessed. Drunk with power? No, far from it. Filled with envy? Not a chance. But hungry? You bet your sweet ass he was that. He was hungry for The Clash to come upon the calendar date. He was hungry to get into the ring and put up his undefeated mark. But most of all, he was hungry to get his hands on the newly crowned champion, one Ben Jericho. The grin faded away, and soon was a distant memory as Lung's face became cold again. Tapping the stick a bit harder, and a bit faster. He seemed to be feeling something taking over him, and he kept tapping harder and faster until the shaft was a blur and made a whipping sound. One last smack against his palm, and then the action stopped in mid air before his bloodied hand became more damaged. Slowly resting the shaft on the table in front of him, Lung swipes a couple fallen strands of hair back into their spike, breathing with a Zen-type concentration. There was definitely something on his mind, but the answer to that remained a mystery. That was, until he parted his lips to speak to us.. Ben Jericho.. His voice was powerful and seemed to shake the camera lens. He held his bloody hand in a fist shape, squeezing tightly but once more, not showing any signs of pain. Lung just continued with his speaking, and we only hoped that he could explain just exactly what was going on. This shaft before me was specially carved from the thickest bamboo wood, in the deepest forest of Fuji. Although it may look to be of little monetary value, this shaft holds the key to why you will be defeated before my own bare hands at The Clash. We were a little confused with what exactly Lung meant by what he was saying. But just as we were about to shrug our shoulders, he explained a little more deeply so that we could fully understand. I received this shaft as a gift from Master Jade upon leaving the Dojo, and it has been with me ever since. Every ring of white tape on it's end, is a victory on my behalf. Every ring of black tape, is a loss. As you can see, I haven't had many people defeat me, and if they have, I put up one hell of a battle. Taking a breath from his speech, Lung caresses the bamboo softly and looks upon it. Sighing slighty, he continues his evaluation of his upcoming match at The Clash against Ben Jericho. This has been with me for a long time Ben, and I'll be damned if I have to put a black ring around it, especially for a match that I've been in too many times to even count. I know a lot about hardcore Ben, more than you'll ever come to find out in your professional career. You may underestimate me now, but let me assure you that I have been in every single type of hardcore stipulation match that you can think of, and even the ones that only you can dream about. THC means everything to me, even if I just got here. It's a symbol of how I do my dirty work. I sacrifice my body every night just to hear the roar of the crowd. I give up my blood, my sweat, and my tears to every organization I step foot into. I'm lucky that my blood hasn't all been shed by now, but you Ben, won't be so lucky when The Clash comes in a matter of days. I'm undefeated, do you know what that means? It means that right away, three white rings have been added to this shaft. They weren't the most respectable victories against the most respectable opponents, but it still proves a point that I am still rolling on to be the very best that I can be, even after this long venture that I've had in the wrestling business. Taking another break from his speech, Lung wipes a bit of sweat from his brow, and tries to get back to his Zen-like breathing. He is becoming overpowered with confidence and passion to capture THC belt, but he knew that to win, he would have to calm his nerves and not let Ben Jericho get the best of his emotions. Slowly but surely, Lung returned to his normal state and began to speak again. Just think about what you are up against Ben. You may think that you have the upper hand, but in fact, you might not have a chance in hell come The Clash. It's just going to be you and me, one on one, in a tables match. And believe me, I know a lot about wood and what it takes to put you through it. For instance, I know that it would take some power moves like a powerbomb or piledriver to crack open the pine table. But now you ask, how can a little guy like me get you up in the air? It's quite easy really, and it's a little thing that I like to call adrenaline. And once I get that pumping Ben, I'm like a freight train coming to plow you over. Another fact is that it might take a splash off the top rope to put you through the table. My aerial arsenal is top notch, and that wouldn't be a problem for me at all. There wouldn't be any time for you to move out of the way, and I would be expecting it even if you tried to. It would be an easy alteration and I'd still get you. Now taking perhaps his last break, Lung stares long and hard at us, perhaps trying to peer into our very soul. His flourescent red eyes are like fireballs as the flames seem to dance and intoxicate us. Wiping sweat once again from his brow, Lung takes one last breath and concludes his confrontation with the camera. So Ben Jericho, I advise you to think long and hard before you try and put this match in the win column for you. Because when I look at you, I don't see THC. All I see is another white ring around my shaft, and all I see is another reason for people to call me The Hardcore Icon more. All I see is my hand being raised at The Clash, and I see me becoming the fastest THC title winner, and you becoming the shortest THC title reign. Ben Jericho, this Wednesday, there isn't going to be a thing that you can do. There isn't going to be a place that you can hide, and there isn't going to be a time that you aren't in suffering at the hands of me. Look into my eyes, and see my hunger. Now look into yours, and see the emptiness. I deserve it more, and you damn well know it. And if you don't by now, you better deal with it. Because I'm coming for you at The Clash, and I'm not holding back until you are through the forest of pine... With those final bone-chilling words, the camera slowly begins to fade into darkness once more. We keep looking upon Lung's flourescent red eyes as the scene becomes pitch black, hearing the slight tapping once more as we go away into complete silence and static fills the screen.
