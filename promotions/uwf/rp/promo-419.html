UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #419

A New Direction

20 May 2004

Adien Pryde — Hospital in Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside a hospital in Chicago. Pacing back and forth in front of the entrance is Adien Pryde. He is wearing black jeans, black t-shirt and a black baseball cap. Pryde looks up and sees the camera man and motions for him to come over.] Pryde : About time you got here, I need to get back in and see what the x-rays revile for Dai’s leg. The Doctor thinks its just a serious sprain but the x-ray will tell for sure. Now I have to say, Nick Persia, you did what you said, you beat me and kept your title. Congratulations. Now I will hold up to my part and leave you alone, but I will warn you, you are aligning yourself with the wrong crowd namely The Players Club. That’s your choice and one you need to live with. Just remember, if we meet in Stable Wars at all I will be gunning for you. [Pryde pauses for a bit to decide what to say next.] Pryde : Now on to this Sunday at Supremacy. I am facing 4 other men, 3 main eventers and a rookie. I may have lost my title match last week but I am not going to keep looking forward to what may be a blessing in disguise. I get to test my talents against some of the top names, Ruff Ryder, The Plague, and Malcolm Kennedy. Any one of these 3 could be World Champ and I get to try to take them out before Stable Wars. I don’t thinks anyone likes anyone else in this match and that can be nothing but good news to me. Just one little role-up when they have their attention else where and I could have my status catapulted to new heights. [Pryde pauses again to think of what he just said.] Pryde : Why the hell would I do that when I could just smash their skull in with a chair. I really don’t care if I pin a person legally or if I steal the pin with underhanded tactic, I am here to take my frustration on anyone that gets into the ring with me. I am going to simply destroy my opponent and walk away the winner. Pryde : Takamichi Mori, I have nothing against you and I strongly suggest that you stay out of my way. I am going after the big dogs and all you will prove is a speed bump for me. Raid, good luck on you match against Kazee. The Chaos Society is counting on you to bring home the IC Gold to get the momentum going as we head into Stable Wars. The Chaos Society is going to start leaving it’s mark on the UWF, and our first dent will be this Sunday. Pryde : Takamichi Mori, Ruff Ryder, The Plague, and Malcolm Kennedy. Don’t underestimate the effect that a little CHAOS can cause on a man, because it will come back and bite you in the ass. [Pryde turns and walks into the hospital, leaving the camera to fade to black.]
