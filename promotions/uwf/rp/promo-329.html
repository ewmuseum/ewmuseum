UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #329

Resurrection

11 Apr 2004

Digital Mortality — Digital's Beach House in Puerto Rico

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Ah... It has been too long since I have been able to appear in UWF TV. Me, the disembodied voice of Digital Mortality, will speak before the master himself. I will tell you people what has been happening in Digital's life and what he is up to you now. So you better get ready to listen to me a LOT! Well, I have been the voice of Digital Mortality since his rise to superstardom and his leaving in the UWF. Unfortunately, he didn't go to work on any other federation, so I was out of a job for quite some time. I don't hold it against him though. A few weeks back he told me he was coming back to the UWF, I was extatic. To have Digital come to me and ask me to go work for him once more was an experience that not many people can say they have had. Anyways, enough about me... let's recap.] [After leaving the UWF to take a break, Digital Mortality went back to his homeland of Puerto Rico and to his beach house to have a nice time and enjoy his time off. He kept going to the gym he owns and kept training and training no matter what. He trained himself and he also trained his very good friend Burton Bell. Well, after a night in which he had absolutely nothing to do, he turned on his TV and flipped to Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. What he saw, he definately didn't like. He saw a clown with the World Title, and he saw a bunch of nobodies in the ring. He had left the federation for a couple of months and suddenly it was down in the floor. He saw the federation he had made famous crumble to the ground, and he definately didn't like that.] [So he began to plan his return. He knew that he needed to get the best talent in the UWF to join him so that he could raise the bar once more. So he could rebuild the UWF to what it was. The most dominating federation in the world. That is when he found Moraless. Well, he knew Moraless from way before. He met him in the old days, and a sparkle in Moraless eyes told him that this guy had a great future. They were prepared to create what was going to be the most dominating stable in the UWF, Moraless, Lacuna Debris and Digital Mortality, alas, it didn't happen, but it didn't mean that Digital forgot about Moraless, so a while back he gave him a call...] (Scene opens with Digital Mortality, he is wearing black sunglasses, and his hair is tied up in a ponytail. He is shirtless as he is outside in the beach. His black trunks wet since he had gotten into the water. There was another seat, Burton Bell was there. In between the two seats a table had two beers on top. Digital was dialing a number, and he placed the phone on his ear.) DM: Hey man, how's it going? I'm good man. I was thinking of something, and I think you might be interested. I sent you plane tickets for Puerto Rico today, the flight is the day after tomorrow. Okay, I'll see you then. Burton: Was that Moraless? DM: Yeah. I have a great idea for the UWF. Burton: You didn't tell me you were going back... since when have you been planning this? DM: Not much, about a week ago I got the idea. Burton: Awesome, theres a PPV coming soon you know. Something about Rennaisance. DM: Yes, that is where we make our move, that is, if he accepts. Burton: He will. [The rest was history. Now we go to the new Digital.] (The scene opens with Digital watching UWF TV in his living room. He just watched an Ezra Kazee promotion and is laughing. He points at the cameraman, who comes closer. Digital takes the beer sitting on the table and drinks a sip.) DM: Well, I guess everyone liked our little surprise. I have been planning this for weeks, and it finally came to be. The union of past and present, to create the future. The future of the UWF. I am here to bring life to a federation that was once great. To a federation that had such names as Danny Jaye, Deke Aurora, Shawn Eclipse, Digital Mortality... what happened? Now we have Nick Persia as THC champ, Ezra Kazze as IC champ and a clown as World champ... pathetic. What I had built with my blood sweat and tears thrown to the floor, like trash. I couldn't stand for that. Now, as most of you know, Moraless and I go way back. He has been my pal since the good ol days of the UWF, and when I asked him to join me, he was happier than I thought. Because he knew that some changes would be happening in the UWF. Everytime a Hall of Famer come back to his federation, everything changes. That is why I am here for. You are about to see the Resurrection of not only myself and Moraless, but of the UWF as a whole. The Player's Club and the Prime Alliance watch out, because we are here to take it all. (He stands up and starts pacing around the room.) DM: I heard what Ezra Kazee was saying earlier in his promotion. In his whole promotion he only said one thing that was true. I did beat him. It may have been a long time ago, but to me you are still the same Ezra. That is nothing. You are right though, a new age will begin now that Moraless and I have joined our forces. The fans don't matter Ezra, the fans are just there to worship us. You know Ezra, you talk about being a team player too much. You must not have been in the UWF when my stable reigned supreme. The Iron Curtain was unbeatable. Nobody could defeat us, we were too much for everyone else. And don't say Stable Wars, because we were blantantly cheated out of it. I had it in my grasp. You talk about teamwork. Why don't you ask The Local NVA Company, Joshua Dunn, Jamie Shock, Ruff Ryder and all those who have been teamed up with me. They went from nobodies to superstars. And the ones who oppose me also get superstar status, that's because when you face me, everyone is watching. That is why Moraless will skyrocket to the top. Come this Sunday we will be Tag Team Champs, unless our shitty Commisioner does something we don't expect, and I know he will. I have known Monty for so long now. We have had battles after battles and he knows that he is no match for me. Why do you think he is Commisioner? He needs to feel important. It doesn't really matter. Moraless and I will beat you easily, because that is what we do. The UWF is going through a Resurrection. Can you make it through? (Scene fades.)
