UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #385

Refocusing the technicallities.

3 May 2004

Ezra Kazee — A gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A wrestling ring is seen in a big open area as the scene fades in. There are various gym machines and other equipment around the area. The only light that is seen is coming in from a window overhead somewhere. Standing in the middle of the ring is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is looking into the camera, almost expressionless. He is wearing a work-out suit of some sort or another. It’s blue in color with black stripes. He just stares into the camera.) Kazee: Where do we go from here? Where do we go from this moment on? Where does the world lead, and where will it end? What is there out there that keeps the world turning and keeps the world going round and round to an almost seamless end? These are all questions that people want to know the answer to…… And Sunday, none of those questions seemed to get answered. Sunday seemed to be just a continuation of all the chaos that is this world. And I’m sure that this next Sunday will be no different. This Sunday, things will just get that much foggier in this world and in the UWF……. This past Sunday, what can be said? Monty, I was truly touched. Your right, I have proved a bit of an asset to the Players Club. Our Title Victories have been mostly by my doing. And our success has been mostly of my doing. The IC Title is mine and I have proved why I am deserving of that title….. But then there are things that prove that I have been a detriment to the Players Club. Together, we won the titles. However, it was I who lost the titles. It was I who tapped out to the Digitizer and cost us the Tag Team Titles. It was I who almost cost you the World Titles when not only did I distract you; I caused you to hit the wrong person. This past Sunday, it seemed that I couldn’t even get the job done myself. It seems that I needed Raid to come down and help me. Not only am I a screw-up, but I needed assistance to do my job. This isn’t Players Club Material. This isn’t IC Champion material……. But I was brought into the Players Club for a reason. Like you said Monty, you only bring in the best and the brightest. You said it yourself, my potential is endless here. Someday I maybe holding the World Title. Someday I will be making my way to the top of this business. The Resurrection says that they have the future of this business in Bondage. Well, the Players Club has their own future in this business, and that future is me. I will make my way to the place that people say I can make it. Someday, the announcer will be saying “Your winner, and NEW WORLD CHAMPION, “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee.” That day will come. And that future is brighter than any other future that any other group could put forward……. And all of this will start this weekend. All of this will start this weekend on SAS when I meet Kenny Mercury……. This is a match that I have been looking forward to for a long time. This is a match that the fans I’m sure have wanted to see for a long time. And it will happen this weekend. The technical showcase of a lifetime will take place this Sunday at Supremacy. I take on the shooting star, the technical wizard, the very definition of precision, Kenny Mercury….. Could anyone ask for a better wrestling match? On one side you have one of the best technical wrestlers in the business. The man who has made precision a fine art. As some people would call him, he is the excellence of execution. Many claim him to be the best technical wrestler in the business…… And on the other side, you have me. A man who has made it priority to be one of the greatest technical wrestlers ever. I have studied ever style of wrestling. I have studied every move that there is. I spend hours looking at tape and finding new moves to put into my repertoire. I’ve forgotten more moves than almost anyone has ever used. And that is a fact I am proud of……. Sunday, we are going to have a catch as catch can match. A pure wrestling match. Back and forth action of move and countermove. Two of the greatest technical wrestlers in the history of the UWF and the GIWF will tangle this weekend, for one of the greatest prizes in the business, the IC belt…… Kenny, I must admit, I never thought I would get the chance to take you on. But on Sunday, I do get my chance to take you on, and I will get the chance to go move for move with you. We will find out who is the best Technical wrestler in the UWF. And it will all be over a little piece of gold known as the UWF IC Title….. I’m excited. I’m pumped, and I’m focused. I would wish you luck, but for you, as for me, luck isn’t necessarily a part of it. It’s all about performance and execution. I can’t wait, the fans can’t wait, and neither can anyone else out there. This match is going to be one hell of a match. (Kazee grabs a rope that seems to be hanging from the ropes. He starts jumping rope in the center of the ring as the scene fades to black.)
