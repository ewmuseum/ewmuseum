UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #237

Hardcore Match RP 1

24 Feb 2004

Bruce Gordy — Hell's Kitchen

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene begins at Hell's Kitchen, New York. Bruce Gordy is there and he's doing pushups. After that, he gets up and does some pullups. He finishes and punches a punching bag. His fist goes through it.) :Bruce: Hardcore Match for SAS, well 'HARDCORE' is my middle name. (He withdraws his fist) :Bruce: I’m not a joke anymore… and I don’t care what everyone thinks. I'm a former holder of (T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship. A title that I had been trying to win since 2001 back in GIWF. You all don't understand. I worked until I bled, I worked hard, I fought many battles in the squard circle. I gave everything I had for over three years. And for what? For a couple weeks of THC gold. Oh yeah, you better believe it. (Bruce punches the punching bag again.) :Bruce: People like Mike Montgomery can try and mess up my dreams, but this isnt a dream, this is reality bitch. The Sole Surviving Element of Destruction is coming for you, and I will get you... watch your back, bitch. (Bruce punches again.) :Bruce: And now I return to the THC scene with a hardcore match against a new kid called Raid. Well Raid, you're stepping into the ring with a former THC champion. There have been many of you. Kids like you come and they go. The THC division has seen tons of poeple like you. Lambs to the slaughter. Like I said, Raid, Hardcore is my middle name... (Another punch.) :Bruce: There's only one hope I see for you... Mike Montgomery had you arrested at SAS... There's only one clear path that I see for you... The rest lead to career suicide and THC failures... But this one path, I think it may holds some promise. But know this, The Innovator of Pain, Bruce Gordy, I, me, yo.... The Sole Survivor does not lose in THC matches. This fight will be MINE! (Gordy punches the bag and it explodes and sends stuffing all over the place.) (FADE TO BLACK)
