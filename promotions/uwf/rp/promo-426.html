The Formorians — Somewhere

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Fomorians….. The Fomorians are a race of demonic giants ancient occupants of Ireland… Supposedly the first ogranized peoples or entities to occupy Ireland. A race of warriors that could not be stopped. In the end, even with superior power and the ability to claim the land as their own, the Tautha De Dannan ended up conceding and stopping hostilies. Making the Fomorians their ally, because the damage done to them by the force of giants was too great. Giants? does this word describe the twins? Two Men that are six foot six and a half inches each, and in the three hundred twenty plus pound range…. Give or take a roast beef. Have they returned to UWF, as some claim? Not to their knowledge as far as they are concerned this is all new… There are a few names that they know Ruff Ryder: a former member of the Order of the Hammer when he was in the PWW with them. Cpl. Punishment a former member of Crime and Punishment, at one time the sworn enemy of the KnightMare Brigade. Especially after they had stolen one of his good friends in Draven “Pogo” Lox to their own fold just prior to Stable Wars 2000. Ezra Kazee, or better known to them not as the Common Man, but as Mr. Meaner, but those were the days of PWW and GIWF something that these new kids on the block wouldn’t know about. The head Formorian himself, Sean McClancy, not of the UWF said it best last week when he said that this was nothing but a shell of a once great federation. It’s a lifeless and souless husk compared to what was. No, the former GIWF Tag Team Champions are not returning to the UWF. They are coming for their first time. They are coming to raise the bar of tag team wrestling. What do those like Lustful Lullaby, Nick Persia and Joshua Dunn know of the former champions? What do they know of the days when the Fomorians left the Underground beaten and broken as they attacked them on their bus? What do they know of the days of the battles with hall of famers like the Phenomons and Tropical Storm? This will be a debut in this federation. This will be the first time that these people see the twin terrors once went by the names of Tehra and Eriu. Thomas and Patrick McClancy. Just call them the Fomorians. Or just call them your owners, your masters, the means of your destruction. Nick Persia says that he’s out to end careers. But he’s never seen the Fomorians at work. He’s never seen the way that they hoist a man up into the air and then drop him down onto his face with one of the huge monsters helping them down. Oh, wait, maybe if they were watching last week, they would have seen Moraless and Digital Mortality, a hall of famer in his own right, be dropped like the punks that they truly are when it come to the tag team titles. They have them, the Fomorians are here. Their goal: Tag Team Titles. But first, first they have to show the world that the UWF’s newest tag team acquisition is one that can get the job done. They have to show the world that the Fomorians are here for business, not playing tiddly winks. Who are these people who think they ‘re so damn good? Nick Persia? What type of name is that? Josua Dunn? Dunn what? Who cares. Lustful Lullaby? Sounds interesting, but sounds like the only way they can think of winning a match is putting their opponents to sleep. But this time, the Twins are not under the thumb of their sister as she is still running her own successful federation. No sister, means no reigns. No reigns means that the UWF will see the side of the Fomorians that the GIWF never got to see. The Fomorians come from the GIWF, the mother ship of the UWF. The place where the best matches were held. Such wonderful memories like the Naked Roseanne Barr, wraped in barbed wire with miget stuffed between her butt cheeks. Ahhh yes the strange minds that came up with that match. Some of the greats of GIWF , Twizid, Nephilim, Bres, RIMJOB, Deke Aurora, Tremor, Lew Spectre, Pete Steele, Al Crud and Ace Thunder. And when nameing great you have to mention the Tag team that has always been a thorn in the side of the Fomorians, The Hall of Famers, Tropical Storm,. This souless husk of a federation has nothing like them to prop them up like the titan Atlas, carrying the world on his shoulders. At least they didn’t, until now. The camera comes into Focus on what looks to be an explosion of a Photo Album or someone is just a really bad house keeper. Two male voices can be heard laughing and talking about something as the camera man clearly tries to traverse the maze of boxes, books and tapes. He pauses at one and picks up a picture so it can be seen clearly. The Picture has Fey McClancy, now Childress, standing in a pleated mini skirt with a tartan look to it and a white shirt that has a private catholic school look to it. Next to her is her twin Brother Sean McClancy, and behind her are two VERY huge boys. Which to all are known as Tom and Patrick McClancy, or the Fomorians. The three brothers are all wearing the T-Shirt that once was a symbol of a force to be reckoned with in the GIWF, the flaming Unicorn that showed they were with the KnightMare Brigade. Above and behind the group shot are the letters GIWF. The Camera man flips the picture over to the back and the words “Bres, Fey, The Formorians” are clearly printed on the back with the qualifying words words, “Sean’s first show, OCT 99” The camera man tosses the picture back on to the pile and starts to slowly walk towards the door that is open just a little bit. Voice: Ye remember tha’ one? Man he was the cooles thing ever.. Voice2: Ye know, Sean would kick yer arse ‘earing ye call someone like ‘im cool. Oh wait look a’ this one… I found the’ one o’ the fish suit. A shuffling of papers can be heard. Voice 1: Oh man! I remember tha’ match, Midnight Flounder an’ Ian. Tha’ was funny as all ‘ell. I still can’na believe the guy thought ‘e ‘ad a shot t’ beat him. A hand reaches out and pushes the door open and reveils that the two voices heard were from the two larger McClancy brothers. They’re sitting on a couch, more pictures and such strewn bout them. Identical twins, unless they tell you who is whom, you would never know. Only their older sister has been able to just up and tell them apart by looking at them. Now 25 years old, they look not unlike they do in the pictures still. Pat: Thomas, look who finially showed ‘is arse.. Tom looks up and smiles, while Pat clears out a chair for the camera man to have a seat. As the cameraman sits down and starts to prop the camera up onto his tripod for it, another face comes in behind him. Another face that has not been seen in some time, and never in the UWF, Michael Dunz. At one time the official interviewer, and driver of the RV that the KnightMare Brigade used to travel around the country. Dunz: Hello I’m here for the firs tFomorians Interview since returning to the hallowed halls of the GIWF, even though it bears a different name. Patrick: Why don’cha jus’ be sayin’ tha’ it’s a debut on th’ UWF. This federation is nothin’ like th’ GIWF was. Ye ga’ t’ many loosers runnin’ aroun’. Like this players club crap. Dunz: Okay, the debut of the Fomorians in the UWF. So, guys, what are you up to? Tom: We’are just reminisin’. After ‘earin’ jus’ wha’ we ‘ave comin’ up fer us, we decided t’ look t’ see if there was some shred o’ a chance tha’ we’d remember some o’ the faces around. Only problem is, there are only like 3 ‘er 4 tha’ we can remember riogh’ off th’ bat. Although some ‘ave gone through some pretty big changes. Dunz: Yes, things have changed. So, already gunning for the Tag Team Titles? I see that you already put the tag champions in their places. Tom: Yeah, I guess ye could say tha’. But this place is different. We’re startin’ off a’ the bottom. We’re goin’ t’ jus’ ‘ave t’ beat the livin’ ‘ell ou’ of everyone an’ get our shot. We don’na be wanti’n t’ na’ be called ou’ fer na’ earnin’ our shots. Moraless, ‘e jus’ learned a bit abou’ th’ way things get done aroun’ ‘ere now. Pat looks at his brother Pat: Just the McClancy way er the KnightMare Brigade way? They both start to laugh. Pat: Anyway’s, Mik, ye’re ‘ere fer an interview an’ a good glance at the next tag team champs of UWF after we get done droppin’ th’ rest o’ them on their gobs.. Considerin’ that title has been marred by the likes of Moraless and his little buddy, it should’na be t’ fokin’ ‘ard t’ get there. Ye see the GIWF or what ever name it goes by nowadays, will never forget the KightMare Brigade and what we do. We’re legends ‘ere. Bu’ some people jus’ don’na know it yet. Tom: Ye ‘ave t’ admit tha’ Moraless won’na ferget us anytime soon tha’s fersure. Neither will any o’ our oppoents this week. Face it, they can’na even tell us apart even though we are as different as nigh’ and day. Pat: They know not much aboot us know. Bu’ fer Rod’s sake, they’ll know who th’ fok we are come th’ end of this match. We aim t’ ensure tha’ people will be talkin’ abou’ th’ Fomroians fer years t’ come. We’ll jus’ add this weeks an’ every weeks stuff t’ th’ chronicles o’ the KB an’ it’s members. Michael Dunz stops for a moment and then reaches down and picks something up as it falls from one of the photo albums that Pat apparently had in a stack. Dunz: Hey, whats this picture about? Mr. Dunz pulls the picture up and reads the back softly, it reads as “Natural Red head” He flips it over and the camera blurs out the bottom part of the picture. Mr. Dunz’s face goes a beet red. Dunz: Oh my, ahh... isnt this your sister? Tom: Oh yea, tha’ is when she made a deal with Tropical Storm t’ get a shot a’ the North American Tag Team Titles. It was their belts against Fey strippin’ live t’ prove t’ th’ world tha’ she was a natrual red head. Dunz: So, uh, I take it you guys lost? Pat: Yeah, they’ve been distrubitin’ tha’ picture by their website fer years now. Bu’ it don’na matter. Tha’ was only a few weeks after we ‘ad gone so far as’ t’ end th’ career o’ the Underground. After tha’, it was start up th’ freight train an’ run over ‘everyone until we were th’ GIWF Tag Team Champions. Very few teams can claim t’ ‘ave owned both GIWF an’ UWF titles. An’ soon, soon we’ll be facin’ off fer them. Tom: Bu’ first, we got t’ get by a duo of Sexual Fetishist, who obviously shouldn’na be wrestlin’, an ‘a couple o’ guys who think tha’ bein’ a ‘Playa’ is th’ way t’ go. Dunz: The Players Club is a stable to be reckoned with here as of late. They’ve got Moraless with them to. Tom: Bu’ Moraless will be wrestlin’ me brother this Sunday in wha will be a brutal match. While ‘e moves t’ fokin’ break Moraless, we’re goin’ t’ be movin’ t’ squash th’ rest o’ them. An in the end, win loose ‘er draw, someone will know all t’ well tha’ the Fomorians are ‘ere fer. They’ll know tha’ were ‘ere t’ beat the livin’ ‘ell ou’ o’ people. An’ eventually, no one will be able t’ deny us ever again. Ever again. Pat: Ye can count on tha’. Dunz: I sure hope so. Do have anything else for the camea? Pat: Yeah, Mik, we sure do. I need t’ ask our opponents this week t’ forgive us. Dunz: For what? Tom: Fer comin’int’ the feederation. I know that a’ one time they migh’ ‘ave been considered a big name. Bu’ now tha’ we’re ‘ere, we’ll try t’ leave room fer them. Dunz: Sounds like you guys have a plan. Pat: Yeah, we do. I jus’ ‘ope it’s wha’ Nick Pesia though’ our plans were. Seein’ as ‘ow this is th’ first we’ve spoken with anyone since dropping Moraless on ‘is gob. Dunz: Yeah, I find it strange that neither Dunn nor Persia mentioned you attack on their stable mates last week. Pat: We’re na’. They’re jus’ shadows of a real stable, jus’ like this is the shadow of a federation. Now, now things will get interestin’. An’ they’ll be runnin’ scared as the Giants ‘ave invaded. Tom: Yeah, if Nick Persia thinks we’re so scared o’ ‘im. I mean ‘e says we’re only legends in’ Ireland. No, we’re legends in the ‘istory of this federation. We may na’ ‘ave been ‘ere, bu’ we’ve been in it’s two parents, the’ GIWF an’ th’ PWW. We are jus’ legends, an’ they’re jus’ rookies as far as we’re concerned. This’ll be like takin’ candy from babies. Pat: Big babies. Dunz: Well I think that’s all the time we have left. Thank you gentlemen. And can I have this picture? Tom: No, Fey would kill us. Bu’ Jaime an’ Miguel sell poster fer it online a’ 20 bucks a pop. Dunz: Thanks for th einformation. At that, we fade out.
