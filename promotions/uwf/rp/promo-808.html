UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #808

This is my world. Are you ready to play?

2 Dec 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens as you see Killer Dude's logo filling up the screen. As the logo finishes filling up the screen, you hear a voiceover....) Killer Dude has arrived. Are you ready to play in his world? (The logo fades away from the screen as the scene begins to fade in. The scene opens to a shot from high above an apartment complex in the mean streets of Green Bay, Wisconsin. It is dark outside, meaning that it must be night time. As the camera pans down however, you can see that it is still early evening, as you can see car headlights below. However, there is no noise coming from the traffic. Instead, it is strangely quiet and quite unnerving. Suddenly, you can hear a voice from behind the camera, causing the camera man to jolt in surprise, bringing the camera with him) Voice: Glad to see you finally fuckin' made it. I've been waiting for hours, you numbskull. (The camera quickly turns around and you see the face of Killer Dude, who is leaning next to a metal door that leads back inside the building. He is wearing a Killer Dude T-Shirt, a pair of black running pants, and a leather coat. Killer Dude pushes himself away from the wall and begins to stroll towards the camera as he continues to speak) Killer Dude: I'm surprised you even came in the first place. Most camera men are scared shitless when they are asked to be around me, and on top of a roof top at the same time. You have balls, kid. Camera Man: Well, it's kind of my job.... Killer Dude: Shut the fuck up! Did I say you could speak?! (Killer Dude stops walking, directly in front of the camera as he continues) Killer Dude: Let me ask you a question. Are you being paid to speak to me?! Do you think that I sent for you, just so we could have a little conversation?! (The cameraman looks on in silence and in fear) Killer Dude: Heh. That's what I thought, you piece of shit. Now do your job! (Without the slightest bit of hesitation, the camera man focuses in on Killer Dude's face. He puts his hand on the right side of the camera and begins to count Killer Dude down from five. Killer Dude adjusts his hair to smooth out the rough edges. He then looks directly into the camera and prepares to begin the promo. As the cameraman reaches zero, Killer Dude begins) Killer Dude: I must admit, I was surprised to look on the UWF Air Waves this morning to see that a lot of my opponents this Sunday Night at Fandemonium have already spoken. Of course, no one has decided to say diddly squat about me, and this is for the simple fact that they are all afraid of me. I can see that I'm beginning to put worry and fear into the minds of my various opponents. I'm not here to blame you, and I'm not here to make fun of you all. I'm here to address the facts, such as the fact that all of you will suffer quite a loss at Fandemonium. I mean, the fact of the matter is that you all are NOTHING compared to me. And I can see it in your faces. It's going to be hard for all of you when you lose your matches, all of them containing great magnitude and prizes. At the end of the night, I could have 3 titles. Who would have thought it? It must be hard for you all, knowing that you will not be able to live up to the expectations that your peers and society put on you. A loss like you all will suffer, well, it would leave me helpless as well. Then again, I will not lose a match of this much importance. I will not choke under the pressure like you all. No, in fact, I will rise to the occasion and live up to my standards. We can't all be me, though, can we? (The camera begins to follow Killer Dude as he begins pacing across the rooftop of the building) Killer Dude: No, there are some of us that will simply not get the job done. These are people like The Communist, Digital Mortality, Hot Mask, Joshua Justice, Malcolm Kennedy, Craig Wright, and Dreammaker. I am happy about one thing though. I am ecstatic to see that you all are not the type to place blame on something else for your shortcomings. Hot Mask, you have one weakness, I can tell already. You are weak against women. The Communist, you can't stay focused if you tried. Dreammaker, you are a bad researcher. Hell, you haven't even researched me yet. All of you, the blame will rest on your shoulders when I beat you all. You have all lost before, and come this Sunday, you will all lose again when you come face to face to the horror that is known as Killer Dude. (At this point in time, Killer Dude walks over to a stone ledge on the side of the building and takes a set, overlooking the street) Killer Dude: The next generation, the 'future' if you will, of the UWF is here people, and his name is Killer Dude. I am the man that will surpass all of you in every way. People want to talk about heaven and hell and how they believe that none of those things exists. I will ask one thing of you all come this Sunday. All I ask is to wait until you step into the ring with me that night, and then make your judgment. Wait until you face me before you tell me that hell does not exist. Because it is my belief that after the PPV, you all will think differently, all because of me. (Killer Dude stands up and begins pacing again, and the camera gets a nice view of the Green Bay skyline behind him) Killer Dude: You see, much like you all, I understand that what we do is a buisiness. It is a means of making money. I also know that it means more about what you do inside the ring rather than what you do outside it. Take a look at my record, people, and let it speak for itself. Look at my history and tell me that I don't know all about that. I might not seem to be respected by the majority of the wrestlers in this federation, but I have always been a main player in the UWF. Who beat Digital Mortality? Who is one of the only men to pin Kenny Mercury three times? Who is the man who defeated Seymour Almasy for the North American Title? You damn fucking right, it was me. Admit it or not, I am more of a prime time player here than you all could ever hope to be. (Killer Dude stops pacing, and faces the camera) Killer Dude: So you all can sit back and talk about how all my wins have been flukes until you are lying in bed because your face is turning blue. You can tell me how people win matches for me, even though I do not deserve victory. I know the real truth. I know that you all fear me, as well you should. The remarks that you all throw at me are nothing but attempts to deter me from my mindset. Your ploys will not work. If anything, all of you and your worthless banter have done nothing but motivate me even more. It has done nothing but add fuel to the fire. A fire that will leave you all as nothing but piles of ashes, scattered in the ring. You have made yourselves marked men. I will expose you all for what you truly are. I will show the world each and every one of your weaknesses. I will rip you all apart, limb by limb, layer by layer until there is nothing left of you. No useless babbling about women, penises and the like. Nothing but a limp body that your soul has left behind. Killer Dude: Do you people actually think that I am afraid of pain and suffering. I can tell you right now that I am not. I have built my entire career on pain and suffering. No matter what has been put in my path, I have overcome it, and I will overcome the odds once again on Sunday night. I am not worried about losing my matches. I am more than confident that you all, just like the rest of them will be less than a mere annoyance to me. Call it my ego if you like, but you all are just stepping stones to me. Stepping stones to get to the man who has it all, Tsunami Oni. There is not a god damn thing that any of you can fuckin' do about it. So prepare yourselves well, because you all will be suffering crushing losses. For I can see the future and it is a future in which I am on top. On top of the world, with no equal, who will reign supreme. (Killer Dude turns his back to the camera, gazing into the night) Killer Dude: This is my world, and you all are just barely breathing in it. I will show you all that size truly does matter even though others like to tell you differently. I will show you that the big dog always wins. It's a fact of life, there are those who make waves and there are those who ride on top of those waves. I am the former. Don't feel sorry for yourselves, however, because there is nothing you can do about it. You can't change the nature of the game, you can only play with the hand you are dealt. Even if it might be a losing one. So quit the acts and stop bluffing because it is nothing more than a waste of my precious time. It's time to lay it all out. I'm gonna end it all right now, let's finish this. The games are over, the bluffing stops now. (Killer Dude turns his attention back to the camera and he gazes into the lens) Killer Dude: I want you all to understand one thing. I do not need, nor do I want any of your respect. All I care about now is that all of your times are coming to an end. You can't change fate, so why do you even bother trying? Accept the inevitable and release your grasp on 'reality'. I welcome you all to take your best shots. Show me what all the hype is about. I will be more than happy to teach you the true meaning of my name. I will be more than happy to add each and every one of your names to my list of people that I have defeated. It doesn't matter to me one bit, because the outcome will remain the same. I hope you all are ready, because once you step into my world, you all better be ready to play. (Killer Dude smacks the camera away from his face as the screen cuts back to his logo. The screen then fades to black)
