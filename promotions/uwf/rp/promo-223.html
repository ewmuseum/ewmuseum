UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #223

Just making sure Im heard.

Feb 15th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Tour Bus

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** The last few weeks have been a roller coaster for some people. Malcolm Kennedy may have had it the worst of them all. The Prime Alliance falls after Shock disappears. His mother hospitalized after a near fatal car crash. His brother reported Missing In Action in Iraq after himself and the helicopter he was on go missing. Malcolm of course, refused to even show up for his rematch against BeelzeBozo. Thankfully, the new owner of the UWF excused his absence when he got word of the reason he would not be there. So where does the all leave Malcolm now? He is left to team with his worst enemy Mike Montgomery in a Tag Team Title match against Ezraa Kazee and Raid. This can only spell more problems for Malcolm and his future.*** <<< The scene opens up inside a plush touring bus. A big scree tv to the left, a large leather couch to the right. Back further is a close door with the name Malcolm Kennedy on it in gold letters. The door opens and from behind it appears Malcolm Kennedy. He face rough and overgrown in hair. His jet black hair matted and uncombed. He wears a pair of baggy blue jeans with holes in both knees, a wife beater, and a pair of Nike running shoes. He walks forward and sits down the large couch. He leans up against the arm rest of the couch and kicks his left leg up onto the couch, his left arm sprawled along the back of the couch. He lets out a lackluster smile and scratches at his face.>>> Malcolm Kennedy: “ For the first time in my career as a UWF wrestler, Wrestling took a backseat to something else. Wrestling became just something I didn’t care to do. Something that was not important to me in the least. But I have grown from this all. My mother, thank the great god above, will pull through her tragic car crash. My brother, well, for now his story will remain untold. Only because he has yet to be found. But I have faith, and I wont let foolish thoughts of what might have happened to him run through my head. My brother, Just like myself, was one of the best at what he does, and I have to believe that he is fine, and there has simply been one big misunderstanding. Time will tell. Until then, I have to get back to business. Oh, I don’t need to rush back and bust my ass to keep my Casino running, I have some of the best in the buisness looking over that for me. The only thing I have to attend to now is The Clash.” <<< Malcolm brings his arm forward from the back of the couch and his leg to floor. He rest his elbows on his knees with his hands point towards each other as he leans forward.>>> Malcolm Kennedy: “ You know, normally I would put up a fight about the fact that I am teaming up with Mike Montgomery at the clash. But, for once in my life, I truly don’t care about the outcome of this match. I don’t want or need the tag titles, so It wont bother me either way, win or loose. But don’t let that fool any of you, I still intend to try my best. Im still one of the fastest rising superstars in the history of the UWF, and I can still perform with the best of them.” “ Now Mike, for once your right. Me and you should not be teamed up. It should be you and Kazee on the same side, and Raid on the other. But at no point in time should I have ever brought into this. But unfortunately I have, and there is nothing I can do to change that. So I will fight along side you and show you that even though the Prime Alliance may have failed, I haven’t. That I am still alive and kicking, and that you will never be able to keep me down. Make no mistake about it, at the end of our match I will hate you just as much as before it. But I simply don’t care to waste my time fighting my team mate and my opponents all at once. I may not be scared of a 3 on 1 match, but it doesn’t mean I care to partake in it. So for once, when I see your sorry face, I won’t have the urge to strike it with my fist. My only goals tonight, are to wrestle my match, and get back to my other problems. “ As for my opponents tonight. Well its real simple. Good luck. I know, I know, that is something you will almost never catch me saying, but I mean it this time. Do what you gotta do out there, and hell, maybe you guys will leave tonight Tag Team champions of the world. But I honestly don’t care about the outcome. The only thing I want to clear up, is with you Raid. Now you may have been able to punk Ezraa after your match at the last show. But that doesn’t mean for one second, that you are someone special here in the UWF. Your just another third rate wrestler trying to make it big here in the UWF because you heard about the table that won the THC title and thought since it could do that, you could make it big. But Im here to tell you, that as long as there are people like Joshua Dunn, Seymour Almasy, Ruff Ryder, and Myself are here in the UWF, you will remain just some joke of a wrestler who caught a break when you signed with the UWF. Your MEDIOCRE abilities might be something that you take pride in, but rest assured, it wont get you anywhere here in the UWF, except for the trainers room and the hospital. I suggest you wake up real quick BOY, or your going to be in for a rude awakening here in the UWF. I suggest you analyze your thoughts next time before you consider taking shots at me and criticizing my career and abilities, Yeah the Prime Alliance may be dead. BUT I AM NOT! And if you think for one second you can talk down to me and push me around, you dead wrong. And before you know, you will be in over your head wishing you never heard the name “TOP NOTCH” Malcolm Kennedy! <<< Malcolm Kennedy rises from the couch and stretches his arm over his head. His cell phone rings and he answers it. He walks back into the back room and shuts the door as he talks to someone on the phone. The scene fades to black.>>>
