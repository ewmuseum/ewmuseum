Via Chrono Purifico: Time Purifies (III)

12/4 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — The Almasy Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time has been ally and enemy to mankind for generations. Hours, minutes, and seconds have determined much of history, in realms from the martial to the maritime. Now, such a situation faces the Universal Wrestling Federation. One of its greatest stars, love him or hate him, is on the verge of suicide. Seymour Almasy sits on his bed. Three of the glasses in his Russian Glass game have been drained, and Seymour still survives. This means that two glasses remain. Laura Winters, Seymour’s ex-wife, is mere minutes away. Can she stop him from draining one of the last two glasses? And if she cannot…will Seymour’s choice be fatal? --------- Disgustedly, Laura Winters gets out of her car. Traffic is simply too congested for her to drive any further. Knowing that time is of the essence, she runs through the New York City sidewalks, pushing pedestrians aside as she does. The apartment that she once shared with Seymour is in sight, motivating Laura to double her speed. Laura Winters: Please…please don’t be dead, Seymour. It sounds kind of silly when she whispers it to herself, but it is what she truly feels. Even though they are no longer married, she still feels a bond to Seymour, a bond that she knows Seymour feels to her. Finally, she reaches the apartment building, charging through the door, and entering the elevator, pushing the button for the top floor. She takes a slow, deep breath, before realizing what the hell she’s gotten herself into. Laura Winters: What do I say to him when I see him? “Please Seymour, don’t kill yourself?” That’s not going to work. There’s got to be a reason Seymour’s so sad or upset that he wants to take his own life, and I’ve got to find out what it is. Reaching into her pocketbook, Laura pulls out her key to the apartment. As it turns out, when she leaves the elevator and heads to the door, the door is slightly ajar. The first thing that she can think of to do is scream. Laura Winters: SEEEEEEEY-MOUR! She charges through the apartment as she screams. The noise startles Seymour, who sits upon his bed, a glass in his hand about to be downed. Seymour Almasy: Who is it?! Mere moments later, Laura bursts into the bedroom, looking at Seymour sitting down on the bed that he once shared with her. She spots three glasses on the floor, one in Seymour’s hand, and the final glass on the nightstand. Realization dawns on her face. Laura Winters: Seymour! Stop this at once! Seymour Almasy: Why should I? I’ve already lost everything! I gave up everything for my chance to face Tsunami Oni, and now he’s dead! She has no idea what he is talking about. Laura assumes that he is delusional, but this bit of information really doesn’t help, because Seymour’s BEEN delusional for a very long time. She knows that she has to diffuse the situation. In order to do so, a bluff is necessary… Laura Winters: Seymour, don’t make me do something you’ll regret… With that, she darts over to the nightstand, snatching the last remaining glass. Laura Winters: Well then, Seymour. We each have one. You know what that means, don’t you? The Final Fantasy squirms uncomfortably, looking at his former wife with wide eyes, unable to speak. Laura Winters: One of us has a regular glass of wine. I’d suspect that you’ve managed to put something nasty enough to kill you in the other glass. You don’t know which is which, do you Seymour? Silently, he shakes his head no. Laura Winters: Alright then, Seymour, if you really want to die so much, let’s drink them together. Finally, Seymour finds the courage to speak. Seymour Almasy: No! Don’t drink it! It could be… He can’t even bring himself to finish the sentence. The hand holding the glass begins to shake of its own accord. Laura Winters: Give me the glass, Seymour. Or I WILL drink from the glass in my hand. Seymour stands. There is no doubt in his mind anymore about what to do. As much as he wants to end himself…he has no desire for anyone to die in his steed. He hands the glass over to his ex-wife, who smiles. Laura Winters: Thank goodness! Seymour, you scared me! She runs to the kitchen, to pour the two glasses down the drain. Sure, one of them contains poison, but this is New York City! We have mutant turtles living in the sewers, what’s a little poison? This leaves Seymour alone, utterly astounded at the recent turn of events. Seymour Almasy: How…how could she have found me? Laura Winters: I heard that, Seymour. Seymour’s head turns, as if on a swivel, to find Laura standing in front of him once again, hands on her hips. Laura Winters: If you must know, Seymour, I got a call from a chief of police in Chicago. He told me that you were about to kill yourself, and I hung up on him to come here…but I’m sure that you managed to do something to cause this call… Almasy can only look up at her sheepishly. Seymour Almasy: I was told that Tsunami Oni died…and I kind of lost it. Laura Winters: And what exactly does “losing it” entail, Seymour? Seymour Almasy: I, um, well… Understandably, Seymour really has no desire to explain his temper tantrum to Laura. But Laura’s fierce gaze leaves him with no other options. Seymour Almasy: It entails throwing things and trying to set the UWF Headquarters on fire. He looks down at his hands, shaking his head sadly. Laura Winters: And finding out that Oni was dead made you want to kill yourself? Seymour Almasy: Not originally. I was going to if I lost to him at FANdemonium anyway. Laura’s jaw drops. Laura Winters: What?! But why? Seymour Almasy: I already told you why, Laura. I gave up everything for the chance to face Tsunami Oni and defeat him. If I were to lose, I would have nothing. Laura Winters: Seymour, don’t say that. Never say that. Seymour Almasy: Why not? It’s true. And it’s my fault. One of the things I’ve learned since understanding what this world really is, is that you reap what you sow. After everything I did to you, I deserve this. Laura Winters: No, you don’t, Seymour. No one does. She slowly takes a seat next to him on the bed, and places a hand on his shoulder. Seymour momentarily shrinks from her touch, before giving into familiarity, and letting her do as she pleases. Laura Winters: We do have to accept the consequences of our actions. But at the same time…I did a very poor job of releasing you into the real world. There was no counseling, no psychological help. I just sort of let you go. That was my fault, Seymour. I’m sorry… Laura frowns, looking at her sad former husband. The impulse to hug him takes her over. It is a gesture that she hasn’t initiated in months, and yet everything is just as familiar as it used to be. Seymour still feels soft and warm in her arms. His head still cradles itself against her shoulder. Everything feels…perfect. Almost too perfect, as Laura realizes to a combination of shock and happiness that she is somewhat aroused. Laura Winters: I guess I should probably tell you that Tsunami’s not dead, shouldn’t I? Seymour Almasy: He’s not?! The Final Fantasy attempts to stand, but Laura holds onto him tightly, preventing him from doing so. Laura Winters: You’re not going anywhere, Seymour. Not if you want me to tell you the rest… She teasingly reaches out into his hair, and flips it in the air. Seymour gives her a surprised look, one that causes her to giggle. Laura Winters: Anyway…Tsunami Oni’s quite alive. Turns out the tabloid report of “UWF World Champion Dies In Vibrator Electrocution Accident” turned out to be fallacious. He’s ready to take you on at FANdemonium in a….what bastion of stupidity have you managed to get yourself booked in this time? Seymour Almasy: A FANdemonium Match. It takes place on an elevated ring, with a steel cage surrounding it. There’s a big hole cut in the middle of the mat, and it’s suspended above the audience. The winner is the man who throws his opponent out of the structure, and into the audience. If that’s not bad enough, wild fans are being released into the match for us to fight. Laura Winters: Lovely, all the fans hate you. Seymour can only sigh in response. Seymour Almasy: Yes, Laura, I know. I feel so alone now. As much as I love competing…it blows traveling all over the world alone. After a long night of wrestling…having the people boo the living crap out of me, I go back to a lonely hotel room, and know that its entirely my fault. It’s a lot to bear sometimes. The words cut to Laura’s heart. She knows that their relationship fell apart for a lot more than a clothesline on a UWF Pay-Per-View. But at the same time, Seymour feels so good in her arms that she doesn’t want to leave. The decision that she is about to make is in no way rational. In fact, it is largely based on personal desire and emotion. Seymour is hurting inside. Laura wants to make it better. Therefore, Laura leans close to her former husband, and aggressively kisses him, catching Seymour completely and utterly off guard. Seymour Almasy: Mmmpph… For that blissful moment, thoughts of suicide, Tsunami Oni, and World Championships are lost in the warm glow of togetherness. For that moment, it seems as if nothing has changed. As Laura breaks the kiss, however, both know that things most certainly have. Seymour Almasy: Laura…why did you-- Laura Winters: Shhh, Seymour. You’ve been so worried lately, so stressed. Watching you on TV, I figured out why. Seymour Almasy: Why, Laura? Laura Winters: Somehow, somewhere down the line…you and Tsunami Oni switched places. You used to be such a happy person, just happy to be in the Battle Arena. But then, you began being obsessed with the titles, obsessed with victory. You became Tsunami Oni. When Oni unmasked back at Live and Loud 3...that began it all. You couldn’t deal with the fact that he beat you. And so, you became this. A man who would kill himself denied the opportunity for vengeance. Silently, the Final Fantasy buries his head in his ex-wife’s shoulder, unable to disagree with a word. Seymour Almasy: What…do I do to change it? Laura Winters: I don’t begrudge you this. On a small level, I understand why you want this so badly. You need to face Tsunami Oni at FANdemonium. After that, win or lose, we can begin helping you…God, I’m almost afraid to ask, but what do you DO in your spare time on the road? The question is a clear indication of the split within Laura. Part of her wants to simply help him, yet another part wants to get close to him. Even knowing that re-entering a relationship would be disastrous, that still doesn’t mean that they can’t be on good terms. Seymour Almasy: Well, I play some Final Fantasy XI online on my laptop, and I go out clubbing. The image makes Laura burst with laughter. Laura Winters: YOU? Clubbing? You can’t dance! Seymour Almasy: I can too! I’ll show you sometime! Laura smiles. For the first time in months, Seymour has a smile of his own on his face. Seymour Almasy: Well, I’ve managed to screw everything up again, haven’t I? I’m sure I’ll have charges waiting for me back in Chicago. Laura Winters: I doubt it. The UWF never presses charges. They just find a way to air everything and use it on TV. Seymour Almasy: Oh! Yay, I’m off the hook! Laura Winters: Err, not quite, Seymour. We still need to get you mentally right. It might take a while….but for your own sake, we must. Seymour Almasy: Alright, Laura. I’ll do whatever you say. Almost immediately, Laura smirks wickedly. Laura Winters: Does that count for everything? Seymour Almasy: I, um, shouldn’t I call and tell everyone I’m okay? Laura, however, has already pulled up the covers to the bed, and slid herself under them. Laura Winters: Oh, don’t worry, I already did that when I was pouring out the poison. Now, come here. A thousand reasons not to enter Seymour’s mind. Like, for one, the fact that they’re divorced. Emotion, on this night, however, won over passion. Seymour calmly cuddled up next to Laura, with a mile-wide smile on his face. Laura Winters: Tommorow, Seymour, you’ll leave here. You’ll travel to FANdemonium, leaving me behind. But I’ll be there for you Seymour. I may not be your wife anymore, but always, even after all of the unpleasantness, I will always be your friend. Seymour Almasy: Thank you, Laura. You don’t know how much that means to me. Tommorow, Tsunami Oni and I will settle our score. One of us will win, the other will lose. Only one of us can walk out of that struggle with the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. But then, the next day, the real struggle begins. The struggle for Seymour Almasy. Laura Winters: But as for tonight… Seymour Almasy: Laura! The Final Fantasy is more than a little bit shocked. After all, his beautiful former wife, now in bed with him, is insinuating that after barely seeing him in months, she wants to engage in a rousing round of “Hide the Ultimate Weapon”. Laura Almasy: Don’t argue with me. It’s been such a very long time…and besides, you need to relax, don’t you? Nothing Seymour could have said would sway her. Hell, he didn’t WANT to say anything to sway her. For one night at least, he would have true bliss. Seymour Almasy: Yes, Laura. Some things never change. Laura Almasy: Like my sex drive? Seymour Almasy: And my willingness to give in. Laura Almasy: Of course you’re willing, sweetie. Tonight, I have no idea what this means. I don’t want to even think about. What I want, what I need…is you. And with that, Seymour turned out the light, although the two continued to speak. Seymour Almasy: Tonight…one perfect night. Laura Almasy: The calm before the storm. Seymour Almasy: And tomorrow will be a new day. A day filled with aspirations. A day filled with hope. Laura Almasy: And the day that your professional fantasy is fulfilled…now, let’s go about fulfilling some of your OTHER fantasies… And talking ceased. (Fin.)
