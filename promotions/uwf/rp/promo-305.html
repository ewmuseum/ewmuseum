UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #305

Who did What in the Where Now?

28 Mar 2004

Right To Party — Smokey's Appartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Well, this is the Cruiserweight Tournament, yes; the stars like Seymour Almasy to The Fool Ian Feltzer are all out to prove themselves against the other competitors in PTC. All flags are flying in the Tournament, XUW, GW, and PWW. And one measly little teacloth being flown as the banner of the UWF, which has shown such concern for the tournament that only a Californian Stoner and a malnourished Vietnamese Communist step in to represent the glorious UWF, even former UWF World Champion Seymour Almasy doesn’t fly the UWF Flag anymore, you’d think there was something wrong with it] [The scene fades in, and we can see, what appears to be a pile of clothes, there is a rather large man sitting passed out in a chair out of focus of the camera snoring, that man is wearing a Hawaiian shirt and a red fez on the top of his head. Perhaps the cameraman can’t operate the machine properly, as the fat man remains out of focus, still snoring] [There’s a knock at the door, off screen, we hear the thudding against the door, soon lots of bustle can be heard outside that door, and the pile of clothes stir in front of the camera, and out from under the clothing, steps a rather tired looking, rather scrawny man, wearing a white vest and Hawaiian shirt, and possibly by accident, a pair of underpants on his head. The longhaired man blinks strenuously as he looks around getting his bearings, before going to the door, to try and get the bustle and the knocking to stop. He answers the door, and is almost lost in the swathe of reporters and cameramen that swamp through the door, flashes, questions and the occasional strange look at the pair of underpants on his head. Taken slightly aback by this, the skinny man blinks a bit harder and rubs his eyes, still looking dazed and confused, the questions sailing clean over his head. The noise however has awoken the giant that was asleep on the armchair, who stumbles over to the ensemble of people and stands beside the skinny man, dwarfing him and a large amount of the people trying to barge through the doorframe] [Out of the murmurings of the crowd of reporters, one single man yells above the crowd, with the importance of a man with a clipboard] Voice: Smokey? Excuse me…. Pardon me… GET OUT OF MY WAY! [A man in a corporate suit is expelled from the crowd and almost careers into the skinny man, stopping just short and stops to smarten himself up] Man: Ah, good, I caught you Smokey [The Skinny man turns and looks at the man] Smokey: What’s all this about [Just then, somehow, both Smokey and the camera pick up on one of the reporters’ questions] Reporter: Smokey, what made you sign up for the Cruiser Weight Tournament in PTC? [Turning to the reporter, Smokey seems to have just woken up] Smokey: Tournament? [He looks back to the man in the suit giving him a puzzled look] Man: Maybe I can explain, but if you could get these people out of here? [Smokey turns to the giant of a man and speaks to him, while the rest of the crowd grow quiet to see what Smokey is saying] Smokey: Bubba, could you get rid of these people please? [The big man takes his gaze off of the people in the apartment and then swivels his head over to Smokey, Bubba grins and replies to Smokey in a voice that is as deep as a base guitar and sounds as if the person using it doesn’t use it that much] Bubba: Yes [He turns to look at the crowd of reporters and moves between them and the apartments main light, causing a mini eclipse that casts most of them into his shadow, almost as quickly as they burst in the ensemble rush out again, leaving Bubba to only shut the door, however the hubbub that was audible at the start, remains outside the door. Smokey, now glad that the people are out of his property turns back to the man in the suit] Smokey: What Tournament? Man: Well… It’s the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament, it’s just started you know, and well, we didn’t want to bypass this opportunity as a federation, so we took the liberty of putting your name down to participate. Smokey: You put my name down for the tournament? Man: Yes Smokey: But I don’t even like this Job, why not some of the other guys here? [The man in the suit looks up at Bubba who is still standing next to them, as he does this, Bubba Grins down with a friendly grin that could frighten a whole country *cough*France*cough* into submission] Man: Well… yes, most of the people here can surpass you on every level of competition, but the only problem is that there is a maximum weight limit on the tournament entrants, and most wrestlers, like your friend here… [The man in the suit gives another quick glance at Bubba, who’s still grinning that grin] Man: … are a bit over that limit. Your just one of the very few that can compete. Smokey: Hold on, I can refuse this right? Man: Yes, I suppose you could do that, but then your contracts would be terminated Smokey: What?!?! Man: Yes, you heard me, you’ll be fired Smokey: But I thought you said to me a while ago that we were part owners of the UWF Man: You are, but lets not get into the details of that Smokey: No, it makes my head hurt Man: But if you refuse, you can wave your contracts and paycheques goodbye. Smokey: But I claim my marijuana off that health form Man: Marijuana? [Smokey ‘s eyes dart around the room at this point looking quite embarrassed at his running mouth] Smokey: It’s all medicinal [As Smokey’s eyes fall to the ground he spy’s a bag filled with his “medicine” that he promptly kicks underneath the arm chair his colleague was previously occupying. At this point the man in the suit chances another glance at Bubba, who is still looming over him grinning, perhaps it’s the fez that’s so unnerving] Man: Anyway, you’re entered in this tournament, it’s a knock out tournament, you go in and win as many matches as you can and come out with your head high or your tail between your legs [Almost under his breath Smokey replies] Smokey: I’ve got a feeling I’ll be coming out high any which way Man: Pardon? Smokey: Nothing… So how many matches am I in? [As a booming sound is heard, the man in the suit flinches so dramatically, it’s like he moved several inches to the left, before he realised that the noise came from Bubba] Bubba: Match? [Hearing a key word mentioned, the behemoth now is looking towards Smokey instead of grinning at the man in the suit] Smokey: Yes Bubba, a wrestling match Bubba: Us? Smokey: No Bubba, just me, you get next time ok? [Bubba’s bottom lip rolls out under the other one, giving the most horrible pout you’ll probably set eyes on, as his head lowers to the floor] Bubba: Ok [He lifts his fez and lying on his head is a handkerchief, which he takes from his head and uses it to blow his nose before replacing, the now wet, handkerchief under the fez and generally looking miserable] Man: Yes, you are just on your own, no Bubba to help you this time, you could be in as many as five matches Bubba: FIVE? [Bubba starts sniffling and lifts his fez again, this time a chicken wing is on his head, which he removes and starts to eat, replacing his fez and comforting himself with the food, is it any wonder why he’s that size?] [Smokey goes over and pats the sniffling giant on the back] Smokey: It’s ok Man, just go and take a load off in the chair, in a minute you’ll forget all about it [Bubba mopes off back towards the chair, the floor almost shaking under the feet of the man, he sits down and continues to eat the chicken leg, Smokey Wead turns back to the man in the suit] Smokey: Five matches? [Another whimper is heard from Bubba] Man: Yes, that’s right. Hopefully you should get at least two, our executives seem pleased with your performance in the federation. Smokey: Pleased? Man: Well, they’re happy to find someone to volunteer to enter Smokey: I didn’t volunteer Man: That’s besides the point, your entered, and that’s it, you can’t back out or you’ll be fired Smokey: You already told me that Man: So I did, and you’ve got the thirteenth match in the tournament Smokey: THIRTEENTH? Man: Yes, that’s right, why is that a problem? Smokey: OF COURSE IT’S A PROBLEM, why don’t you just throw me under a ladder or force me to go out a different door from the way I come in? Man: Your not superstitious are you? Smokey: Of course I am. Bad luck doesn’t just appear out of thin air you know, I’ll have to look for my white heather now Man: White heather? Smokey: Yes, someone ate my rabbit’s foot [Smokey looks through the corner of his eyes towards Bubba, who belches, just finished the chicken leg] Man: Ok, that’s slightly weird… Smokey: Your telling me, I didn’t suspect a thing until the guy started getting all the hotties running after him at the parties [The man in the suit gives Smokey another strange look] Man: Your facing someone from the WWA, Kirk Urban. Smokey: KIRK URBAN?!!! [Smokey’s eyes light up and both his hands shoot towards his head in astonishment along with glee, he finally finds the underpants on his head, taking them off to look at before throwing them away, landing on the floor, the joyous expression still on his face, not being able to be removed by such nastiness as finding underpants on your head] Man: You know Kirk Urban? Smokey: What do you mean do I know Kirk Urban? He’s like the greatest guy I know Man: He is? Smokey: Yeah, I was lucky enough to meet him once, but I don’t think he noticed me; he was too busy signing albums of other people Man: Albums? Smokey: Yeah, music albums Man: Kirk Urban? Smokey: Yeah, the man with the foremost and best Urban music around, I must have all his albums, you couldn’t have a party without them… although I didn’t know he was in the wrestling business… Man: I think you’ve gotten your wires crossed; either that or this isn’t the same Kirk Urban your thinking of. I’m not too sure about this one… or the other one for that matter, but I’m pretty sure their not one in the same… [A puzzled look crosses the face of the man in the suit. Looking rather lost himself; Smokey plunges a hand into a pocket of his baggy jeans and pulls out a “cigarette” which he lights and proceeds to smoke it, much to the horror of the man in the suit] Smokey: What? I told you, it’s medicinal. Man: Look, how about I go see what I can find out about this guy and then get back to you? [Taking a large draw as the man was speaking, Smokey doesn’t reply for some time, holding in the intoxicant for a lengthy period before exhaling, and therefore replying] Smokey: Sure, you do that… and I’ll try and find a way out of this tournament Man: What? Smokey: Just kidding, you want a drag? Man: NO [Hold his hands up defensively Smokey replies] Smokey: Ok, only asking Man: Right, I’ll go and see what I can find out, and I’ll be back in a day or two Smokey: Sure [The man exits the apartment, the exit punctuated with large babbling voices from reporters and a few flashes from cameras, before the door shuts and silence is regained. Smokey wonders over to Bubba, taking another draw on the roll up, exhaling once he reaches the king of lard] Smokey: That reminds me, you would have thought you’d have passed that rabbits foot by now [Bubba just looks up at Smokey and shrugs] [The Scene Fades to Black]
