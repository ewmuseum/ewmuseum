RP#2 For SAS

12/04/04

Gregory — Minneapolis

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene fades on inside a taxi in the middle of a fairly rich looking city, some large buildings with about 100 windows in them, clearly office buildings. And also there's some nice, average sized houses, near quite a few nice green grassed parks, squashed with all the people treading along it. It's the west of Minneapolis.* Gregory: I wish I could say this is the part of minneapolis I lived in, with a happy, normal life. But obviously I can't. This isn't the place that I was often mugged, often beaten by my parents, my Mother to be precise. Today I go to visit her. Today I see my mother, someone who once would punch me around the face if I just looked her in the eye. But life's not perfect, you have to deal with shit like that. *The taxi continues driving, and it seems that within every mile or so, the town that they are in is getting duller, and duller, and duller until finally they reach the place it appears Gregory's looking for home. The best homes in the area are slightly big muddy shacks with a sheet to sleep on. In this area that was considered well off. Gregory didn't even get to sleep there. The taxi stopped outside of a building with crap apartments in, and Gregory pays the taxi driver the Ten Dollars he owes him, and gets out the cab, which drives away instantly, who wouldn't from this craphole?* Gregory: I even wish I could say that this is where I live, inside these rubbish apartments. But hey it was a poor place, when you had no money you still had a place to stay. It was just missing the walls, the roof, the door, basically everything. That's right, me and my Mother lived on the street when my wealthy dad left her to go with another, what he called respectable, woman. *Gregory continued to walk in silence, treading in a muddly puddle every 3 steps or so. Obviously, the bottom of his jeans were getting muddy, and as they dangled and hit Gregiry's ankles, he winced. Clearly he didn't like dirt. When he got to the other side of the building (outside of it) he saw a woman laying on the floor motionless. She had brown hair, brown eyes and was just wearing a brown rag over herself.* Gregory: That there is my mum. Yes, I will admit it, she is laying there still because she is dead. And yes I know it. I know what you're all thinking. Why is she still there? Because no one around here is self-respecting enough to care that a dead body is free on the floor, in their neighbourhood. And now you're probably thinking, 'Why don't you move her, you have money?' Well I've got some news for everyone, at this moment in time I have only just started wrestling at the UWF, and I only just have enough money to rent an apartment wherever I am wrestling. Maybe in about five matches time I will hire someone to remove her body into a peaceful coffin. Though it's not like anyone would find being in a small box underground very comfortable, I do have to say. I'll be back mother, and when I am back I promise you that i'll get you a beautiful coffin, with roses on it just like you wanted them. *Gregory walks back to the other side of the building completely silently, and stands staring at space for a while, obviously thinking very hard. Just as the taxi he had earlier pulls up, a small tear starts to shine in Gregory's eye. When he gets in the taxi, he begins to speak to two people who aren't even there.* Gregory: Dante, Loverboy, I would like to give you a message. Well it's more of a warning than a message. I am going to win on Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. And you know why? Because I have a reason for doing it. You two are wrestling for either the money or just for a hobby. Well to me wrestling isn't a job, wrestling is a dream. A dream I have to be successful in to prove to my dear mother that all the training, all the hard work I did when I was younger did pay off. And I will beat you two for her, I want her to be impressed. Because she is watching, she is above me now listening to exactly what I say, and she will watch me beat the ass off you, Dante, and I will rip the lips from your face, Uglyboy. Sorry it's Loverboy isn't it. Well anyway, I will be showing you what it means to be known as a good wrestler. Once I win this little debut match I will be going onto better things, and you bet your sorry ass that soon I will win the UWF Championship! *The Taxi continues to drive down the road, and it cuts back through the rich part of Minneapolis, and back to the UWF Arena 2 days before Gregory's debut show, Sunday Afternoon Supremacy...*
