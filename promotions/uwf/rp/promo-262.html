UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #262

Backstage rampage

2 Mar 2004

Beezlebozo — Backstage at Supremacy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(((NOTE: events occurred immediately after the Feb. 29th edition of Supremacy.))) We open up backstage at Supremacy to find the UWF World Champion, Beelzebozo is raging through the hallways, with Pyretta Blaze right behind. Beelzebozo: “RYYYYYYYYY-DEEEEEEEEEEEER!!! Come out and PLAAAAAY-AAAAAAYYY!!” Beelzebozo grabs a stage hand as he passes by, and pulls him face to face. “WHERE IS HE?!” Stage hand: “W-who?” Beelzebozo: “RYDER.” Stage hand: “I am so sorry, but I-I have no idea.” Beelzebozo: “Then you serve no purpose!!” The clown hefts the man above his head and lawn-darts him into the wall. The man crumples to the ground and lies, twitching, with blood beginning to run from his nose. The clown continues his search, screaming Ryder’s name. Blaze: “Honey, hold on!” Beelzebozo: “NO TIME MUST FIND RYDER!!” He stops outside a door labelled “Ruff Ryder” and smiles wickedly before kicking it open. He stomps inside the room, but finds only a cowering makeup woman. Beelzebozo: “WHERE IS HE!?” Woman: “He left! He left! Don’t hurt me please!” Beelzebozo lets loose an unintelligible screech and takes off in a dead run. Blaze gamely tries to keep up with the rampaging harlequin. The World Champ bursts through double metal doors into the parking garage. The attendant, apparently unaware of what’s been transpiring inside the arena, comes over with a friendly smile. Attendant: “Can I help you, Mr. Beelzebozo?” Beelzebozo: “RYDEEEEEEEEEEEEER.” Attendant: “Ah, I believe he’s gone sir. He came in here a couple of minutes ago, running at a dead clip. I couldn’t get a word out before he’d leapt into his car and taken off. You’d think the Devil himself and all his minions were after him!” Beelzebozo: “That’s pretty much what’s happening!! But he got away goddammit!!” He growls, then suddenly swivels his head and stares accusingly at the attendant. “You let him get away.” Attendant: “I did what, sir?” Beelzebozo: “Yooooooou let him get awaaaaaaaaay…” The attendant’s smile falters, then slips away as the implications of what the clown is saying sink in. Attendant: “Sir, please! I couldn’t have possibly stopped him, he was running away too fast! I had no chance!” He turns to run, but Beelzebozo is upon him and grabs him by the shoulders, shoving him down to the pavement. The daemonic clown begins stomping the hapless attendant, as Blaze looks on with a mixture of pity and…lust? Beelzebozo eventually stops, his boots dripping with blood. He lifts the attendant up over one shoulder and carries him toward a nearby car. He points at it. Beelzebozo: “Whose car is that?” Attendant: “I--*cough cough*--I think it’s…Mike Montgomery’s.” The clown frowns, and walks over to the next car. Beelzebozo: “How about this one here?” Attendant: “That one…oh God…is Kenny Mercury’s.” Beelzebozo: “Hmmm…he DID cost us the match tonight…” He nods to the next one. “And that one?” Attendant: “*cough*I think it belongs to Malcolm Kennedy.” Blaze: “I think he’s friends with Ryder...” Beelzebozo: “Perfect.” With a malevolent grin, he climbs up on the hood of Mercury’s car. Blaze: “Honey, come on! He was just doing his job…” The clown slowly turns to stare at her. “Shutting up now.” The clown steps up onto the roof of the car and hoists the attendant face up over his shoulders. Attendant: “OH GOD PLEASE NO!!” Beelzebozo: “Don’t worry, I wiped my feet. I won’t get prints on Mercury’s ride here.” Blaze: “Sorry pal. Better you than me.” Attendant: “HELP ME SOMEBODY!!” Beelzebozo: “I AM THE JUDGE AND YOU ARE ALL GUILYY!!!” With a satanic laugh, the clown leaps off the hood and plants the unfortunate man through the back windshield of Stee’s car with the Last Laugh. He rolls off the car and dusts himself off. The attendant bleeding body lies sprawled over the trunk of the car. Beelzebozo turns and looks at Mercury’s car. Beelzebozo: “Well no wonder he freaked. I DIDN’T wipe my feet first. Oops.” He chuckles. “Ah well, I’m pretty sure blood can be washed off pretty easily…” Blaze: “Hey, you put a nice hole in the windshield there!” Beelzebozo: “Thanks. It’s a gift.” He sighs. “I suppose there’s not much else we can do now. That pathetic, cowardly, snivelling little bastard’s probably in another state by now. I really hate spineless wimps like him.” He punches the attendant’s head out of frustration. Blaze: “So what’s the plan for Ryder? What do we do…?” Her voice trails off as she begins staring at the unconscious attendant, a strange little smile on her face. Beelzebozo: “I imagine that he has some sort of reason for this, and I also imagine it’s a really stupid one. So, there’s really not much to do right now until we find out what that reason is. Then we can go from there. I guess all that’s left to do tonight is find that makeup lady and make her pay for helping Ryder look slightly less ugly on TV tonight.” Drunken (offscreen) wrestling fan: “Heeeeey, bozo! Ruffy kicked yer ash good! Ha ha!!” Beelzebozo: “Then again, I think there may be more pressing matters.” With a snarl, he takes off. Blaze, still staring at the attendant, waves after him. Blaze: “OK…I’ll catch up in a sec.” She pulls a small squirt bottle and lighter out of her pocket and approaches the attendant. After squirting the bottle content’s all over the attendant’s shoes, she puts the lighter to them. The man’s shoes begin burning, and Blaze’s face goes blank as she stares into the flames. The man groggily comes to, sees his burning feet, and screams, causing Blaze snap out of her reverie. She squeals and claps her hands with glee as the man screams and rolls off the car, the flames starting to lick at the cuffs of his pants. More screaming suddenly begins somewhere in the garage, and Blaze whips her head around in its direction. “Crap! I thought I’d have more time to enjoy this.” Blaze skips off as the man frantically tries to get his shoes off. Blaze (offscreen): “Wait up dearest! I wanna watch the fun!” And that’s when the screen goes black.
