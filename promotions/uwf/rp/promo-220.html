UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #220

Setting the Ruff One straight

13 Feb 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We open upon the World Champion and his main squeeze standing before the enormous fire we recently saw. The camera jiggles, and then the Quiet One emerges and heads for the two. Beelzebozo: “So the lens is all right, then?” The mime nods. “Good, I’d have hated to buy another one.” Blaze: “Hey, I was going to ask before you went on your tirade earlier…where’d you two get the matching coats?” Beelzebozo: “The mannequins. They looked so nice I couldn’t bear to burn them, you know? And it’s not like they needed them. I think it suits me, and the mime too!” Blaze: “I agree!” She rubs up against the clown and purrs. “So, you feeling better now, dear?” Beelzebozo: “Not really, but I don’t think I’m going to be violently assaulting cameras any more today.” Blaze: “Oh good.” Beelzebozo: “Still, I’m mighty ticked about this whole conspiracy against me. It’s pathetic! I mean, who the hell wants another homogenized, company-approved champion? Other than the company, of course.” Blaze: “Yeah! Can you imagine how much it’d suck if you were the type of champion they want? Just imagine…imagine…” As she repeats the word “imagine,” the scene becomes wavy and blurry, almost as if we were headed for a dream sequence or something. When the picture clears up, we see our indomitable trio in what looks like a sitting room. Books line the shelves, a globe sits atop a wooden desk, and an expensive-looking vase rests upon a stand under a small window. The three of them are wearing Victorian period clothing. The Quiet One is smoking a pipe, and Beelzebozo is sipping from a tea cup. They still, however, have on their face paint. Except Blaze, who doesn’t wear it anyway. And when they speak, they’re all affecting British accents. Beelzebozo: “I say, old bean, what a lovely day, eh wot?” The Quiet One: “I do agree, old chap!” He takes a deep drag on his pipe. Blaze: “May I say something?” Beelzebozo: “Of course, my dear!” Blaze: “I must say how wonderful it is to see you as the World’s Wrestling Champion. You do a great service to its lineage with your current reign.” Beelzebozo: “Why, thank you, my dear! Such wonderful words! You are a credit to your bloodline! How lucky I am to have stolen your heart!” The (Formerly) Quiet One: “And it’s so nice that we’ve decided to go along to get along, and become the willing thralls of the UWF! I love that fact that they believe mimes do not equal positive ratings, so that I can once more be allowed to speak!” Beelzebozo: “I know, my friend! But the thing is, now you never shut up!” They all laugh, and Beelzebozo sips his tea again. Blaze: “Ah, such a wonderful time we live in!” Beelzebozo: “After I have finished my Earl Grey, shall we off for a bit of adventuring in the countryside? I daresay the day will be this lovely long enough for us to take a walk!” The Quiet One: “Smashing idea old man!” Blaze: “Capital!” Beelzebozo: “Quite.” And with that, we un-dream sequence back to the three of them as we know and love (?) them. Blaze: “Jesus H. Christ riding an ass to Nazareth, that would suck hardcore.” Beelzebozo: “No kidding. But I suppose it could be worse. I mean, what if they wanted me to be like their last champion, seeing as how he was so successful? I mean, imagine that…imagine…imagine…” And once again we’re off to Imagination-Land! This time, we see what appears to be a large matte painting of a land with green grass, mysterious floating bricks, and large pipes. A turtle shell suddenly comes sliding across the floor. Following it is Beelzebozo, dressed in a plumber’s outfit. A big goofy mustache is obscuring his upper lip. Beelzebozo: “Well, that’s-a the last of-a the Koopas! Now, to rescue the princess!” Blaze appears in a lovely pink taffeta gown. Blaze: “Oh, Luigi! You’ve come to rescue me! But where is my love Mario?' Beelzebozo: “Screw him! He’s-a not man enough for you, what with-a his fat ass and-a his being so short! I’m-a your amore now, paisan!” Blaze: “Oooo…I like the way you talk, Luigi! I never realized how manly you were!” She bats her eyelashes at him Beelzebozo: “I hope that-a nasty King Koopa shows up soon! I need-a to thrash him, so’s I canna get to the arena in time to take on my next opponent, capische?” He pulls out a large, pinkish flower. “Come onna out, Koopa! I’ve-a got my last Fire Flower ready to-a toast-a your ass!!” The Quiet One suddenly hops onscreen. He’s wearing a fox costume, with two tails dragging behind him. The other two stop and stare at him. He looks at them, comprehension dawning on his face. He hangs his head in shame…and Beelzebozo pulls out a large monkeywrench and smashes the back of his head with it. And we’re back to reality once more! Beelzebozo: “Man, that would possibly suck even more than that first daydream sequence!” Blaze: “No kidding.” She pauses, seeming to consider what’s just occurred. “Hey, what was with the goofy-ass interludes, anyway? That doesn’t seem your style.” Beelzebozo: “Eh, I thought we could use a bit of lightening up, you know? Besides, ideas like that seem to come naturally to me. I mean, look what we did before my match with Seymour.” Blaze: “That’s true.” Beelzebozo: “Besides, it’s hard being all dark and evil all of the time. I know I’m stressing over the stuff going on here in the UWF lately…but even daemonic clowns need a little fun now and then, right? There’s nothing wrong with that, right?” Blaze: “I just hope Ryder doesn’t make a stupid promo later on and ruin your mood.” We dissolve to the three of them standing in front of their television in the big tent that dominates Hell’s Carnival. Beelzebozo’s face is twisted into a hideous frown. Beelzebozo: “You were saying?…” Blaze: “I didn’t know he’d do that! Much less two of them!” Beelzebozo: “There goes my good mood. Even with him confirming my theory about the gay pornos, what with him screaming that he wants my head and things smelling like ass and all…” Blaze: “Well, should the two of us leave you to address him?” Beelzebozo: “I don’t really want to. He’s SO obviously trying to rip off my whole ‘psychotic wrestler’ bit. I mean, did you see him pretending he’s having blackouts and randomly assaulting people and stuff? That’s so fake I can’t believe he doesn’t have ‘100 percent grade-A HAM’ stamped onto his forehead.” Blaze: “Yeah, but it might help if you get it off of your chest, you know?” The clown sighs resignedly. Beelzebozo: “All right. Let’s get this over with so I can move on to something more important than Ryder. You know, like taking a crap or scratching myself.” His two friends move offscreen, leaving the World Champ standing alone. He takes a deep breath, holds it, and slooooowly lets it out. Beelzebozo: “All right, Ryder. Here we go. Listen close, because I’m not going to repeat myself, jackass. “I’m not going to repeat the facts concerning the Clash of Champions. You said it yourself—it’s on tape. Anyone with more than two firing synapses in their cranial confines can see that wasn’t me. And that’s all that needs to be said. “I’m also not going to offer you any comfort over the fact that your little friends left you behind. ‘OH, my little friend Jamie left me alone! He said he’d always BEEEE there for me! WAAAAHH!’ Suck it up you moron. Putting your trust in others is just stupid, and asking for them to betray you.” He looks off to the side. “Like a certain alky I know…” He suddenly starts shaking, and only stops when he punches himself in the mouth, causing a small trickle of blood to ooze from his busted lip. He straightens up and looks into the camera once more. “All I’m going to offer you is a whole mess of pain and suffering. If you’re lucky I’ll hit you so hard you’ll forget how miserable and ‘insane’ you are, and then maybe you can be a more productive member of society. Which wouldn’t take much, I grant you.” He crosses his arms and sighs. “So anyway, it looks like you’re not doing the smart thing and pulling a Kennedy. You’re obviously not nearly smart enough to turn tail and run. So I guess that means you WANT to get pounded into the mat like a tent peg. I mean, surely you’re not entertaining some pathetic delusions of BEATING me, are you? “Oh, wait. I forgot. You’re CRAAAAAA-ZEEEEEE. You’ll believe anything you tell yourself. Or are you suddenly filled with confidence following your recent victories over helpless UWF cameramen?” He snorts. “Yeah, way to prove your worth as a top contender for the UWF World Title…beating up people half your size and not trained in any way to defend themselves. Bra-VO.” He claps half-heartedly. Blaze can be heard giggling offscreen. “You keep going on about winning the title and then going after Mr. Shock, as if it were already a given that you’re going to breeze right through your match with me. Well, Cherokee Manilla, I have a little secret for you…you’re not winning a damn thing except ‘Victim of the Worst Beating of 2004.’ “You think you can just spout off about me, disrespecting me like that? Taking me lightly? Is that what you think, I’m just a pushover? Well you’re going to be sorely disappointed. NO ONE takes the Clown lightly and walks away with a win.” He pauses. “God that sounds familiar.” He shrugs and continues. “You know, I don’t recall YOU ever taking the title off of Seymour Almasy. Or maybe you weren’t GOOD enough to face him? I don’t care enough about your history to find out. Either way, you LUCKED into this title shot and now you suddenly think you’re the Second Coming of All Things Wrestling. I’m here to tell you—you’re not. You’re NOTHING, do you hear me? NOTHING. Jamie Shock abandoned you because you were holding him down, I bet. I know I wouldn’t want to be associated with you. Your skills are laughable at best, and you couldn’t thrill a fat chick if you had a pizza stuffed down your pants. Your little rants are all the same: ‘blah blah gonna kill you blah blah I am the Cherokee Chicken Choker blah blah kick your ass BLAH.’ Come up with something remotely original to say and maybe I’ll consider you worth more than a fart in a strong wind.” The clown begins to pace. “You see, I have to beat you for one reason—you represent the very worst that wrestling has to offer. A loudmouthed idiot who can barely wrestle his way out of a wet paper bag and prides himself on being BORING. Right, Cherokee Vanilla? You’re about as fresh as a week-old diaper and as exciting as a bowl of overcooked rice. And as much as this company thinks I’m an unprofitable champion, they’ve got to be wetting themselves in fear and panic over the thought of someone like YOU as their World Title holder.” His pacing stops, and he comes closer to the camera. “I notice you never mentioned your sad little attempt to stack the deck in your favor, Ryder. Why not? Are you actually embarrassed that I was able to so easily see through your ploy? Or do you think no one will notice your underhanded attempt to sneak in through the back door to glory and the championship? Maybe it actually got through to you that I’m not about to let you win thanks to a last-minute stipulation that was thrown in because you weren’t MAN enough to go up against me one-on-one, relying on nothing but wrestling ability. Which, at least, means you WERE smart enough to realize you couldn’t beat me in a fair fight. It doesn’t change the fact that you’re a coward with nothing hanging between your legs, but hey, if you don’t mind living with that reputation, then I guess I shouldn’t mind either. “Another thing. You hate clowns? Guess what? They don’t like you either. Especially this one. And this one is in a position to do something about it. Like, I don’t know, thrash you within an inch of your life. You think I’M the one who’s going to the hospital? Please. I’M the one who’ll die? PUH-LEASE. You don’t have the balls, or more importantly the ability, to make such threats against me. You’re a worthless wad of flesh walking around like it’s something. “Well, junior, I’m here to remind you of your place in the food chain…and that’s down at the very bottom, with the rest of the filth and slime. You’re going to end up VERY disappointed, and VERY much in pain, come this weekend. I’m not about to lose to a joke of a wrestler like you. And yes, the evil clown wrestler is calling YOU a joke. Take that to the bank and cash it, Sally. “You sit there and think about that. Think about how much you suck. And think about THIS…” He begins to approach the camera, as the lights dim and turn blood red. “When it’s all said and done… “I will still be the champion come the morrow. “I will be the one standing over your broken body.” His voice drops to a whisper. “I will be the one having the Last Laugh.” He chuckles, and with that the lights go out. But from the darkness, the World Champion's voice can still be heard... “See you at the Clash, Ryder.”
