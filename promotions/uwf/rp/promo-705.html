UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #705

Partners...not allies.

9/16 (year unrecorded)

Seymour Almasy — Office. RP#1 for SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life. A puzzle far more difficult than the 500 piece Lord of the Rings one most people have buried somewhere deep in their closets. With his wife no longer at his side, Seymour Almasy is virtually alone in the world. He, however, knows three things. He knows he is the UWF’s THC Champion. He knows that at PTC’s Survival, he has a PTC Global Title shot. And he knows that he hates Tsunami Oni. But soon, more discoveries will be made, of much larger scope. But even before that comes another basic discovery. He hates Mike Montgomery, too. ----- The UWF’s reigning king of hardcore is not in his home. Nor, for that matter, is he out on a quest. He simply sits in, of all places, an office. Yes, a typical office, with a desk, chair, and computer. Of course, Almasy’s well-built yet tattooed torso is bare, and the armor on his legs which reside on the desk give away that he isn’t exactly used to a desk job. Seymour Almasy: Ah, it is nice to have gold around my waist once again. It’s not exactly the world title, but it will do for now. The THC Champion reclines back in his nice cushy chair, letting out a deep, relaxed sigh. Seymour Almasy: To some men, this is the life. To sit in a chair, type on a computer all day…and make lots of money doing it. Hell, my partner this week, Mike Montgomery, does it quite well. He makes a good living off of it, as Executive Vice-President of whatever the fuck he is around here. For all I know, he makes the matches. And if so…heh, I can’t blame him for this week’s main event. His hands ran through his long, white hair, as he shrugged, continuing to ponder his situation. Seymour Almasy: He faces Tsunami Oni at Live and Loud 3. He wants Oni out of the picture before hand, so who better to get the job done than me? Look, Monty…I’ve got no problem beating the crap out of Tsunami Oni…but the least you could do is ask. You see, I’m not exactly planning on working cheap. Especially not considering who you are. A devil’s gleam appears in Seymour’s eyes, pretending to type on the computer. His fingers move back and forth like lightning, indicating Almasy’s computer prowess. Seymour Almasy: If you want, I can send you an invoice for my services. Perhaps a memo? Come on Montgomery…let’s level with each other a bit. We don’t have a heck of a lot in common. Maybe two things, to be generous. We’re both two time UWF World Champions, and we both don’t like Tsunami Oni. Beyond that…you’re a treacherous son of a bitch who specializes in using his power to his advantage, and fighting people outside the ring to weaken them in it. I just go down to the ring and hurt people. I find your approach disgusting and distasteful…but then again, I’m not you, and thank God for it. Almasy gives a disgusted snort of derision, before continuing. Seymour Almasy: Here’s how its going to go on Sunday, Monty. You and I are going to beat the living hell out of the waste of humanity known as Tsunami Oni, and the unlucky ‘wrong place wrong time’ sap that is Malcolm Kennedy. Tsunami Oni will be crippled and unable to make it to the PPV. Hell, you can stay on the apron and exchange cookie recipes with your bodyguard, for all I really care. But, when the match is over Mike, you will then give me what *I* want. You will give me a shot at the richest prize in the Universal Wrestling Federation, the UWF World Title. If you do not…I assure you that your bodyguard will not save you from what I will do. But…enough of that. I’m sure you’re a smart enough businessman to give me my shot, especially since you seem to value your life so much. He can no longer take sitting, and so he stands, drawing himself to his full 5’7” height. Seymour Almasy: Tsunami…you and me in the ring again. For the first time in almost a year. Sure, we have a partner each, but we have the real issue. You’ve got Montgomery for the title…provided you survive me. And I don’t have a match at Live and Loud yet. But really…if I gave you the choice, I know you’d face me. We’re some sort of demented opposites. I define you. You define me. Whether you are evil or good, it matters not one jot… With a grin, the Final Fantasy leaps up onto the desk, cackling to himself. Seymour Almasy: I hate you. I despise you more than any human being walking the face of this earth. And yet…yet, unlike Mike Montgomery, you have my respect. You may have kidnapped my ex-wife…but you met me like a man on Gagazet. You faced me with every ounce of venom you could muster…and in truth, you were probably the better man that day. For that, you will eternally have my respect. My hatred, but also my respect. The THC Champion begins to pace in the office, like a tiger. His face is contorted in a deep scowl, as he growls to himself. Seymour Almasy: And then it’s Malcolm Kennedy. A poor soul caught in a situation he doesn’t even know the scope of. Kennedy, you’ve been drafted as the partner of the man that I hate more than anyone else in the world. I’m going to give you your way out. It’s nice and simple. When I’m in the ring, make sure that you’re not in it. When Montgomery comes in, beat the fuck out of him until he runs to tag me, then get the hell out. It’s the safest move you can make. Slowly, the Final Fantasy reached into the desk, and pulled out several sheets of paper, with scratching and drawing all over them. Seymour Almasy: Soon…my vision will be complete. Soon, you will bear witness to my grander plans. You will see what I have in store for the Universal Wrestling Federation. Because, gentlemen, let us all face facts. Tsunami Oni and Mike Montgomery are both former world champions. Malcolm Kennedy has achieved his fair extent of success in the UWF. But when it comes right down to it…I am the man that the UWF uses as its flagship. I am the name everyone knows. Universal Wrestling Federation and Seymour Almasy are near-synonymous. So too are Primetime Central and Seymour Almasy. And yet…Oni and Montgomery are trying to take me on in my territory, the GTT4 tournament. At least Kennedy was smart enough to stay out of it. You two gentlemen have now made things professional, as well as personal. The reigning PTC God of Tournaments smiles, stretching his arms out overhead. Seymour Almasy: It’s a matter of pride. As it will be when we face off on Sunday. I am the man with the reputation, the man with everything to lose. Montgomery, though I don’t like the fact one bit, is my partner. I’m not going to get along with him, but I’m sure he’s not stupid enough to anger me. Thus, we can co-exist long enough to tear Oni and Kennedy asunder. And then….Montgomery…we shall see what you are willing to do for me. For your sake, I pray that it is a good offer… Silently, Almasy begins to leave the office, anger etched on his face. … But he can’t resist a parting shot. Seymour Almasy: The leader of the Player’s Club. The leader of N.O.G.O.O.D. And the best competitor in the UWF, ever. It’s gonna be one hell of a show… He turns, hair waving behind him, as the office is exited, and the door slammed shut. (Fin)
