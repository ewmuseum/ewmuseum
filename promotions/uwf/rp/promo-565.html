UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #565

Day 1: Recording Reason

21 Jul 2004

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — UWF Arena, After Hours

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Day 1 [Once the lights go out and the last car disappears from the lot, any arena can look desolate. Deserted. Hollow.] [Such is the UWF Arena, hours after the final bell sounded and a new World Champion was crowned.] [A new THC Champion, nursing his bruises but also victorious.] [And a new Television Champion, winning his first UWF title less than a week into the new phase of his career.] [Sitting alone in the confines of his ten-by-fifteen locker room, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins leans back on two legs with his folding chair, the Television title draped lengthwise across the bench in front of him. Even in the dim pseudo-light filtering in from underneath the door, the reflective surface of the goldtone plates on the belt create a distorted face. The reflection of Rollins’ face. Looking back as if to say, “What now?”] [It was past midnight, and Rollins was pretty sure he was one of the only people left in the building. The last whimper of crowd noise died down hours ago, but The Renegade didn’t want to lose this moment. After two years on the sidelines, he was able to come back in five days and win an established championship.] [All-in-all, he had very little to complain about.] [In six more days, Dave Storm will get his second chance against Rollins, and the thought has been playing itself repeatedly in his mind since the title was awarded to him. His upper lip twitching slightly, The Renegade wonders where “The Shocker” is at right at this moment, celebrating his victory and his second chance at glory. Partying with his former tag-team partner, or with some bimbo hooker who happened to catch his eye at the first street corner he came across. Either way, Dave Storm was not in the building, feeding off of the energetic residue the night had left behind.] [And Rollins didn’t intend to leave anytime soon.] [A lock-in.] [Why not? After all, he was a UWF employee. Who said he had the leave the building at all?] [Like a bolt of lightning striking his brain, The Renegade’s eyes suddenly light up with a revelation. Leaving the Television title on the bench, Rollins places all four legs of the chair back on the floor and gets up, heading straight for the door. Pushing beyond into the dimly-lit utility hallway, he begins walking the empty corridors of the UWF Arena, searching for a very specific piece of equipment in a very specific room.] [The media center.] [It takes less than five minutes for Rollins to find the simple wooden door marked “Media,” holed up in the section of the building reserved for the production crew. Jerking the handle, he forces the door open and feels the wall for a switch, triggering the rows of flourescent lightbulbs hanging on the ceiling. The room is fairly large, cluttered with television monitors attached to editing consoles and switch boards. Pieces of paper with production notes sribbled on them have collected on desk corners. A half-empty cup of coffee sits collecting dust, getting stale.] Renegade: Perfect. [Winding his way through the maze of television consoles and production equipment, The Renegade stops in front of a simple tri-pod stand, a small camera perched on top like a small bird, a small black raven, beckoning to be used. Calling out to be noticed.] [A grin spreads wide on Rollins’ features.] Renegade: Nevermore. [Without hesitation, The Renegade flips on the power.] […static…static…static…static…] […static…static…] [The camera’s view cuts in after a few moments, the lens whirring and spinning to adjust to the dim lighting and The Renegade’s proximity. The view is slightly slanted, as if somebody had set it down and hadn’t intended to use it anytime soon. The smug grin hidden in Rollins’ eyes makes it obvious that that won’t be the case tonight. Reaching both arms around the edges of the camera’s vantage point, he rights the image so it looks at him straight-on, his face and upper chest filling the viewfinder. Clearing his throat, he speaks.] Renegade: Welcome to my world now, kids. Ever wonder what happens when the lights go out, when mommy and daddy are asleep and nobody’s around to watch? (he chuckles) I’m that sick feeling you get when you’re all alone, with nobody to talk to. ‘Cause I’m there, in those shadows. And tonight we’re going to shoot a little documentary, a little behind-the-scenes featurette on what goes on when those shadows are right at your front door. [The camera’s view suddenly shakes wildy as The Renegade disappears from sight, apparently picking the device up from its tri-pod stand and getting behind the lens. Now settled and slightly taller than before, the camera begins to bounce with the rhythm of footsteps as Rollins leads the way out of the media center and back into the hallway. Aimlessly heading for anywhere but here, The Renegade presses onward.] Renegade: So here we are, in the heart of Chicago’s own UWF Arena. Not quite Wrigley Field, but it gets the job done. These hallways have been home to some of the most famous men in the sport… Lew Spectre, Lyle Wile, Seymour Almasy, Digital Mortality… the list ain’t a copy of Reader’s Digest. This company has seen some of the best - and still houses some of the best - this business has ever seen. [Bouncing its merry way down through the random hallways, various production packing crates and television cables lay shoved up against walls and in corners, passing on either side of the camera.] Renegade: What’s the defenition of “best,” anyway? What makes somebody the best, the untouchable? Is it truly a product of talent, or just some bullshit propoganda title slapped on by the media, creating legends out of nobodys? That’s some food for thought… but then again, we all need legends. We need somebody to point out, somebody who is the definitive epitome of excellence, be it for good or evil. [The Renegade laughs, the hollow sound vibrating off of the equally hollow walls.] Renegade: Listen to me ramble on. What kind of Buddha do I think I am, anyway? [Silence fills the screen. Footsteps fill the air. All the while, the camera bounces.] Renegade: We all need a hero, and a hero needs his villain. What people never consider is what the villain needs. What the villain wants. Who says there has to be a villain, anyway? Maybe he’s just misunderstood, that’s all. I don’t know… I guess it got to me, listening to the crowd boo me to death tonight. I’m not used to that. But then again, I’ve never given a shit what the average Joe-asshole thinks of ol’ Rich Rollins. Bunch of mindless consumers. Sitting in their twenty-dollar seats, shoveling a ten-dollar box of popcorn into their million-dollar mouths. Everybody wants to be somebody, and people used to want to be me. Be The Renegade. [Silence.] Renegade: Now what do they want? [The footsteps. The silence.] [Suddenly, everything stops. The lens stops bouncing. The footsteps stop echoing. Slowly, the camera starts to spin around, following one wall all the way to look in the opposite direction, straight into Rich Rollins’ waiting face. The Renegade’s intensity can be felt, like a hot iorn at the back of your brain, even through the medium of the camera.] Renegade: Nobody knows what they want anymore. Nobody but Rich Rollins. And you know what I want? I want to prove, beyond the shadow of the shadow of any possible doubt, that the future of the UWF is spelled “Renegade.” I want to slap the silly smiles off of everyone in the back who thinks they have it made, from the newest bunch of curtain-jerking parasites I’ve already dominated to the established big-boys. Anyone who thinks they’re off-limits… (he grins, a cold expression)… well, let’s just say you’d better keep track of the playing field, and whether you’re on the sidelines or in the thick of the fight. ‘Cause anyone I see… is a potential enemy. [Setting his cold blue eyes straight through the camera, The Renegade becomes almost serene, lost in his own head. His words are harsh, but distant. But their meaning remains the same.] Renegade: For the next six days, until Supremacy this Sunday, I’m not leaving this building. For the next six days, I’m going to document a week in the life of Rich Rollins, living right here in the UWF Arena. But most importantly, for the next six days, I’m going to show “The Shocker” Dave Storm what it takes to be a Champion, to be a somebody. [His focus snapping sharply on the here-and-now, The Renegade finishes.] Renegade: And what goes through the mind of somebody who isn’t afraid to maim a man for the sake of entertainment. Best be taking notes, Mr. Shocker. I’m about to take you to school, Renegade-style. […static…static…static…] […static…static…] […static…] [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
