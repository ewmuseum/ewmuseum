Judgement Day?

9 Jun 2004

The Real Deal — Abyss (Real Deal's Mind)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...The love of ones heart is pushed towards a sport with all the effort that one can put forth. I have done this one numerous occasions and then then sometimes haven't put any effort into the sport at all. The love has departed so many times I've stopped counting. Fact is though that I do not need to count again. The Real Deal stands on a plot of grass. It is in a perfect circular shape and is as bright as grass really can get. Surrounding the land is shining waters stretching for miles on end. Deal looks about from side to side and a grin appears on his face. 'Ahh, peace at last' he swalks as laughter pounces from his open lips. He sits down quickly and then retracts to lie flat on his back looking up to the clear sky. 'Hmmm...why's the ground shaking?' The ground starts to shake a little. Once every couple of seconds though the shakes become more violent each time. A few minutes of this pass and the ground starts to get really rough so Deal stands to his feet to try and see what's happening. A voice booms from up above, 'Time for judgement Real Deal!' Deal looks around up to the sky which begins to cloud over and soon it turns to black. This starts to worry Deal and he minorly freaks out at the fact of the ground shaking and the sky changing colour. 'What type of Judgement?' Deal keeps his eyes fixated on the sky but no replies come so he repeats himself. 'What type of Judgement?' Again he stands still, his hands now shaking from the cold that envelops him. 'WHAT TYPE OF JUDGEMENT!?' A vigourous shake shurders Deals' spine and he falls over to see the grass dying right before his very eyes. He shifts himself back as fast as possible in fright of his sightings. The bombing voice gets his attention once more. 'Time for judgement' The small plot of dead grass stops shaking and Deal returns to his feet and takes quick breaths to regain his composure. Just as he does though the sea implodes downwards and the plot of land is the only thing left in the abyss of darkness which now detroys all the sky and light that comes with it. A shadowy hand starts to move down from the blackened sky. Lit up only by a small light coming from inside the hand. The light brightens as the booming voice comes again. 'Time for judgement!' Deal starts to get really pissed off as he will usually do when annoyed. 'Well hurry up and give me my judgement you fool. You with your little light inside your big hand! You're nothing to me, you're nothing but a failure. You think you can judge The Real Deal' The large hand moves swiftly right infront of Deal's face so that it's only inches away. The voice becomes nearly deafening as the light is right beside Deal. 'I cannot judge you. Only you can judge yourself' The hand moves it's index finger to point behind Deal. He turns slowly to the side then swings his head to see what is going on. His mouth opens in shock. The light before him is as bright as the sun but somehow he can still look at it. That is not what ails him though. 'Holy shit...' drifts from his lips as he see's the enormous silver dragon staring straight at him. The hand moves behind Deal and taps him in the back to get him to move forward. Deal does so and makes his way slowly towards the light and the dragon. As Deal is nearly directly under the dragon it descends and lands directly infront of him. The smoke flares from it's large mouth straight into the face of Deal. 'Judge me' slowly jut out from Deal. The dragon's head creeps closer and closer to him and then moves his head to the left of Deal's face and then to the right, both eyes locked on each other. The dragon's gaze then fixes straight at Deal as their faces meet head on and touch. Not being able to control himself Deal moves his arm up and strokes the dragons neck. As this happens the dragon accepts this but has something to say. 'You have been judged and have been chosen to be the great leader of evil' Light blinds Deal and when it disipates he finds himself at home on the couch. He thinks to himself. -heh heh, watch out UWF, the great leader of evil is coming, heh heh-
