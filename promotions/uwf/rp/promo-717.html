UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #717

A new dance

25 Sep 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — Disco

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up with the colorful Fizbane the Fabulous standing in the middle of a disco dance. Along with him are his four different ladies of wonder, Aphrodite, Venus, Freya, and Athena, along with Soviette. The music is loud and the atmosphere is bright and fun. The four women are off dancing and having a good ol’ time, including Soviette, which is different than normal. Fizbane the Fabulous just watches the action and enjoys it.) Fizzy: It’s been a long time since I’ve been invited back to the part. It’s been a long time since the fun of the UWF has been around. But this SAS, we are going to PAR-TAY like there is no tomorrow….. It’s been since Regal Rumble. That was a hell of a party in and of itself. And this week’s party is going to be just as much fun. Kind of a pre-party if you will as it is going to lead into Live and Loud….. Me and my chickadees, we are going to dance into the arena. We are going to dance all around and party like we only know how. Because that’s who we are and that’s what we are all about…. I got some cool cat named Dicky Flint for this one. Apparently he belongs to some group called the KKK. You know, I don’t know much about the KKK. I really don’t worry about how they party. I’m sure there are some hot ladies and some cool nights happening within that organization. But that’s that organization and this is my organization…… And my organization is all about a party. We are going to dance this weekend Dicky. We are going to dance and party and have fun. Because that’s what I know about and seems to me you know how to party to. Are you ready for the party? I know I am…… It’s been too long for the party. It’s been too long since I’ve been sitting on the sidelines. It’s been too long since me and my chickadees have been able to do anything. Well, this weekend, we are going to party into the arena, and we are going to party out of the arena. And we are going to party ALL……. NIGHT…… LONG!!!!! (Fizzy joins his ladies out on the dance floor and starts dancing with them.)
