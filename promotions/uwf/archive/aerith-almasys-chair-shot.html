Aerith Almasy's Chair Shot

c. 20 Feb 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Larson: "Poor Kenny is getting killed!" [Tsunami Oni taunts the crowd.] Rick: "Is this what Lew Spectre should prepare for?" [Oni picks Kenny Mercury up by the hair and executes the TIDAL RAGE.] Larson: "It's over!" ONE.. TWO.. *CRACK* !!!!!! Rick: "Oy vey." [Aerith Almasy stands over Tsunami Oni, holding a chair, breathing heavily, and shouting out obsceneties.] Larson: "YES! The boobies are here!!!" Rick: "Wait? Aerith!?" [Aerith continues looking menacing while Tsunami Oni stumbles up to his feet. Jerry Kohler is mumbling around the ring.] Kohler: "Wait!? What's happening?! Where am I? Where are my pants?!" [The camera swivels around, and suddenly Shiatsu has appeared in the ring ... a frying pan in her hand as she yanks it the rest of the way out of her tight dress.] Larson: "I love that woman." [Shiatsu rears back and ... clocks Aerith Almasy with the frying pan.] Kohler: "What was that?!" Larson: "Cat fight!! Cat fight!!! Cat fi--ght?" [Shiatsu jumps on Aerith, pummeling her with the metal frying pan, and just getting dull thuds.] Rick: "Oh dear." Larson: "Wait ... cat fights are supposed to be slapping and hair tugging ... not her nose!!!" [Tsunami Oni gets to his feet, his eyes going wide as Shiatsu begins jabbing the frying pan into Aerith's ribs, and starts pulling away his woman.] *CRACK* !!!!!! [Tsunami Oni drops again as Jamie Shock comes a swingin' with his chair. Shiatsu ignores it all and continues battering Aerith.] Shock: "Nyahaha! This is why I'm the captain, Ken--ny?!" [Tsunami Oni kicks Jamie Shock in the nuts, and the Wonder Boy goes down.] Kohler: "Someone tell me what's happening!!!!" [Tsunami is quickly up, battering Jamie Shock, who desperately tries to crawl away.] Shock: "No! Off me, twat!!!! GAH!!!!!!" [Tsunami Oni pummels Jamie Shock.] *CRACK* !!!!!!!! [Tsunami Oni goes down AGAIN, as Kenny Mercury batters him with Shock's chair. The Wonder Boys descend upon the World Heavyweight Champion, slapping, kicking, and yanking at his blue, fuzzy hair.] Larson: "Wait ... the boobs are on that side of the ring, and the cat fights on the other. Rod damnit." [And then Metallica's "Unforgiven" hits, and Lew Spectre makes his arrival.] [But we've seen this before, and we don't need to see it again, so let's go to the backstage area. Where we can be new! Fresh! Invigorating!] [In the backstage area, Corky Spectre holds court over members of the UWF roster. Misfit has been dragged into it somehow, sitting in the background, next to a soda machine. Nick Persia is in the middle of beating Malcolm Kennedy for the fifth straight time at arm-wrestling, as both men try to ignore the referee.] Corky Spectre: "So I said ... NO!!!! Don't you know who I am!? I'm Corky!!!" [Corky throws his arm into the air.] Corky: "And he was all like ... "Dude, I don't care who you are, you have to pay for that milk." And I was like "NUH UH!" So I hit him over the head with the milk." [Corky beams proudly.] Corky: "And that's how I got arrested the first time." [Nick Persia rolls his eyes and stands up, walking off to leave.] Corky: "Hey! You! Sit back down!!!" [Persia thumbs his forearm at Spectre.] Persia: "Eat me." [Corky Spectre looks in horror as Nick Persia goes towards a muted monitor, where we can see Kenny Mercury, Jamie Shock, and Lew Spectre taking turns bouncing Tsunami Oni's head off the mat. Shiatsu seems to be paddling Aerith with an evil glee using the frying pan.] Corky: "RAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!" [Corky Spectre goes flying across the room, onto Persia's back. One hand immediately goes for Persia's eye, the other yanking at Persia's mohawk.] Persia: "Hey!!! God damnit!!! OFF ME!!!!" [Nick Persia begins spinning around, trying to get at Corky Spectre ... but the Corky is wiry, small, and hardened by a thousand purple nurples and a million wet willies, and he's going nowhere.] Corky: "RAR!" [Persia spins 'round and 'round, trying to throw Spectre off. Malcolm Kennedy massages his upper arm, and just points and laughs at the spectacle. So we cut back to the ring.] [Lew gestures to the Wonder Boys, having them pick Tsunami up. He spits into Tsunami's face and raises the microphone.] Spectre: "Having fun?" [Lew Spectre yanks Tsunami Oni up, grinning at him. But Tsunami, eyes fluttering, mumbles something back.] Spectre: "What was that, little man?" [Lew yanks Tsunami up closer.] Oni: "I ... said ... you ... have incredibly long nosehairs ... and you should really get a trimmer for them ... geezer." [Tsunami Oni erupts with ... THE EVIL BLUE MIST~!!!!!!!!!] Spectre: "ARGH!!!!" [Lew Spectre stumbles away, his face covered in wet, blue, sticky fluid. Tsunami Oni grins devilishly, but Kenny Mercury and Jamie Shock are on him in seconds, pummeling him once again.] Spectre: "I HATE YOUR GOD DAMN MIST!!!!!!!" [Lew punts Tsunami directly in the face, breaking Oni's face open into a bloody mess within seconds. The Wonder Boys drop on him again.] [And back in the backstage area~!] [Corky Spectre is still on Nick Persia's back, who is spinning round and round in circles.] Corky: "Oy vey ... I think I'm going to be sick." [Persia just spins harder and faster.] CorkY: "Rar?" [Corky Spectre goes green, eyes floating in his head ... and seconds later he shoots off Persia's back, landing in a huge bowl of apple sauce with a splat. Nick Persia grins, laughs ... and then falls face first into the ground.] [Two minutes later Malcolm Kennedy finally stops laughing long enough to get Nick Persia back up to his feet. He yanks him up ... and then, finally sees the television monitor, where Lew Spectre, Kenny Mercury, and Jamie Shock are taking batting practice on Tsunami Oni's head Kennedy: "OH ROD DAMNIT!!! Misfit!!!" [Misfit is up in seconds, running towards the hallway to get to the arena. Kennedy follows, dragging a barely conscious Nick Persia on along with him.] [And two minutes after THAT, Misfit comes flying through the curtains, charging down the ramp. Malcolm Kennedy follows seconds later ... and thirty seconds after that, Nick Persia stumbles out, nearly falling down the ramp, and stumbles on after them.] [Lew Spectre and Kenny Mercury run like hell, and Jamie Shock runs over to Shiatsu. He grabs her boobs, ducks a frying pan shot, and tosses her aside. He scoops up a battered and bloody Aerith Almasy and darts into the crowd after his teammates seconds before Misfit and Kennedy make it to the ring.] [And so the carnage went.] Kohler: "What happened?" *DING DING DING*
