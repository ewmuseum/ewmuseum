Forces of Darkness Wrestlers Name: Jared Crafter Face/heel or neutral: Neutral Height: 7'4" Weight: 325 lbs. Hometown: Death Valley, AZ Entrance Theme: Metallica's "For Whom the Bell Tolls" Style: Brawler Finisher: The Soul Stealer Description of Finisher: A Chokeslam immediately followed by a "Last Ride" style powerbomb. Wrestler's Appearance: Standing nearly seven and a half feet tall, Jared Crafter strikes a truly imposing figure. His long, black hair is usually tied back, a pair of sunglasses almost always resting on his nose. Crafter usually wears a black muscle shirt along with a pair of black, leather pants and black work boots. His goatee is neatly trimmed and dyed black. Background Info: Hailing from Death Valley, this Gothic Warrior has struck fear into the hearts of all his opponents. Usually alongside other members of his "Forces of Darkness," Crafter has run roughshod over his competition, successfully winning every major title in every Federation he had been in before arriving in the GIWF. On his arrival, Crafter quickly established himself, leading his Forces of Darkness into battle against Maximum Damage. With his partner Riggs, Crafter soon took the World Tag Team titles from the RPA. They pair would continue their winning ways until Stable Wars 2001. The pair would unite with Mike Montgomery and a fourth, unknown (since I can't think of his name) individual and despite a strong showing from Montgomery and the FoD, it was the fourth man who would cost the threesome the victory. The team fell into obscurity after that, until their recent return to the UWF. Accomplishments: Former GIWF World Tag team champion (Partner - Riggs) Too many "indie" titles to list.
