'06 February, 2006 Named for one of the cornerstones of the UWF's history, the Clash will make it's fourth appearance on the PPV Calendar in 2006. Since the Contest of Champions was created, the Clash has been used as it's mate -- the first PPV back in the new year, the reigning Champion of Champions earns his place in the main event, challenging for the World Heavyweight Championship. Along with that match, all UWF titles will be on the line as well as all of the best (and likely worst) of the roster. As the first match signed for the upcoming Clash, Misfit will be defending his World title against Contest of Champions winner Clyde. The Clash! '05 - February 27th, 2005 The Clash! '03 - February 9th, 2003 The Stable Wars VIII April, 2006 As the single longest running PPV event in the UWF's world, Stable Wars made it's debut as the GIWF's third event in June, 1999. In a constantly shifting organization for the event, the UWF roster will pair itself off into teams of four and go into a massive, one night orgy of tournament goodness! The teams that have won the event have become names wrapped in legend: The Youngbloods, the Order of the Hammer, Pete the PG Tips Chimp's WACC Mark 4 Stroke 9 ... even Mike Montgomery's Player's Club ... twice. It certainly isn't a typical PPV event, but for many the Stable Wars are the highlight of the UWF's year. In addition to their place in history as Stable Wars champions, the winning team will also receive a title shot apiece for any championships they want, worked out amongst themselves. The Stable Wars VII - August 14th, 2005 The Stable Wars VI - June 6th, 2004 The Stable Wars V - June 1st, 2003 The Stable Wars IV: Survival - July 28th, 2002 The Stable Wars III - May 30th, 2001 The Stable Wars II - May 28th, 2000 The Stable Wars I - June 2nd, 1999 FANdemonium III July, 2006 The newest event of the UWF's PPV Calendar, FANdemonium earned it's place on the yearly roster after being perhaps the best event of 2004. Hoping to recapture that magic, the same formula will be used: The UWF fans will hold all the cards. And when your fans are as border-line retarded and generally drunk as ours are, that's just begging for trouble. All challengers to the UWF Championships ... all matches ... every gimmick ... for the second time, everything will be in the hands of the fans who built this promotion and kept it going for the last 7 years. And after an event that included the first ever Flaming Monkeys match, the second ever Tables, Ladders, and CATS~! match, and a Soup Kitchen Brawl, it'll be right spiffy to let the fans worry about one-upping themselves for once. FANdemonium II - May 8th, 2005 FANdemonium I - December 5th, 2004 The Regal Rumble 7 August, 2006 For the sixth straight year, the Regal Rumble will return to television sets around the globe ... so eat it! See ... we reinforce the ring. Then we take the names of every last monkey on our roster. Then we make them draw numbers. Then we make them pummel the shit out of each other under they're thrown over the top rope, in which case we burn them with cigarettes and tell their mother's they're worthless. But in the ring, all of those folks on the roster do battle ... and in UWF tradition, we try to include lots of people that aren't on the roster. Which kind of bit us in the ass that year when the Bunny Army of Doom invaded, bringing the total number of entrants up to roughly 2,300 and killing our feed for ten minutes. But with a No-Bunnies-Allowed policy in place for the second year, the 7th ever Regal Rumble is in place for July ... as the wrassler's of the UWF will do battle in a multitude of matches leading up, and followed by a match that has won the Match of the Year award TWICE! And at the end of the night, one man will be left standing ... earning their place in history, and a shot at the World Championship. Regal Rumble 6 - September 17th, 2005 Regal Rumble 5: Terror in Tokyo - August 15th, 2004 Regal Rumble 4: The Coronation - July 27th, 2003 Regal Rumble 3: Zero Tolerance - January ?th, 2002 Regal Rumble 2 - January 31st, 2001 Regal Rumble 1 - January 30th, 2000 Live and Loud 6 September, 2006 The UWF's marquee event and anniversary show, Live & Loud marks the 6th anniversary of the promotion's biggest change: The day the GIWF ceased to exist, and the UWF took it's place, completely redefining it's place in the world of wrestling. As has been the case since 2001, however, Live & Loud will mark the centerpiece of the UWF's PPV Calendar ... legends do battle, champions face off, the best and brightest and worst and dumbest knock boots, and at the end ... the World Heavyweight Champion will face off against the winner of the Regal Rumble in the biggest match of the year, the Live and Loud main event. Live & Loud 5 - October 30th, 2005 Live & Loud 4 - September 26th, 2004 Live & Loud 3 - October 15th, 2003 Live & Loud 2 - October 6th, 2002 Live & Loud 1 - September 30th, 2001 Halloween Hell V October, 2006 Returning for the first time in year's to the UWF's calendar, Halloween Hell has the distinction of being the oldest ongoing Pay Per View event: the original event was held by the IWF in October, 1998, six months before even Stable Wars had made it's debut to the wrestling world. Returning from the grave, Halloween Hell will be held on the eve of Halloween ... a night filled with cheesy & clichéd gimmicks and references for everyone! See ... it's a normal PPV event, but everything will have a generic Halloween feel to it. You can't get any more creative than that. Halloween Hell IV - November 3rd, 2002 Halloween Hell III - October 29th, 2000 Halloween Hell II - October 31st, 1999 Halloween Hell I - October ?, 1998 Contest of Champions 5 December, 2006 Quickly becoming one of the best PPVs of the year, the Contest of Champions' premise is simple: We take everyone on the roster and whittle them down to 8 (... or so) of the very best. On the very last night of the UWF's 2005 calendar before closing down for the Christmas break, those eight (... or so) champions and stars are organized into the simplest of brackets and pitted against one another. At the end of a one-night, single-elimination tournament, the fourth ever Champion of Champions will be crowned ... a distinction already held by Tsunami Oni, Ruff Ryder, Hot Mask, and Clyde before them. Their place in history is cemented, the World Heavyweight Championship shot a month later set in stone, and their place as the cornerstone the new year will be built on is theirs for the taking.
