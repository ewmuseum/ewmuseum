What this is The main page of the UWF’s own site (main.html) as the Wayback Machine captured it on 10 October 2004: its two posts, the notice of 13 August 2004 about the application service being down and the tag team division recruitment post of 7 August 2004. Application Troubles… 08/13/04 It seems that our Efed Productions Applicatino services is down for the time being, and has been down for a while now… SO! I bid this of you… if you are going to apply or have applied to UWF in recent weeks but did not hear back at all from UWF offices… please fill out the form, or re-fill out the form, found on the ‘Join singles’ page and e-mail it to [detail withheld] Thank You. Bitches~! Join The UWF Tag Team Division Today! 08/07/04 The UWF. Many know it as the place where polar bears are hurled off stages, monkeys bite balls off, white powder mixes with the blood on the mat, bunny rabbits get torched with a flame thrower, imps wrestle in robots, bla bla bla.. it seems everybody when the UWF is mentioned thinks of something. Nothing serious mind, which is our own fault. We're just asking for it putting naked wrestlers on PTC's splash page. It just shows we can have fun. You see, the UWF isn’t all fun and games. Oh no. Wrestlers get beaten to a bloody pulp on most nights, and that's just the jobber's division! But regardless, the UWF needs one thing to regain it's true form as a key player in the wrestling world. It's tag team divison. Now, before you suddenly presume the UWF is short of wrestlers, let me clear that speculation up by saying that is not the case! - I mean, we have dozens of wrestlers we can force together and tag in an instant. We snap our fingers and two (un)willing superstars are going for the tag titles. No, the UWF has a fair amount of tag team talent, which, in all honesty, needs a bit more competition. It needs to feed. Since the GIWF, we have had a leading tag team division, maybe perhaps the best. Just look at some of the top teams which have stepped into the mat of the UWF; The Phenomenons, The Local NVA Company, Tropical Storm, The Dark Lords, The Legends. We rank the UWF World Tag Team Titles just below the Heavywieght Title, that's how highly tag teams are rated here in the UWF. I'm sure many of you will remember our total dominence of the PTC Tag Team Division with Homo-Phobic. The tag team division brings fame, and glory. Many tag team wrestlers turn out in many cases to be the best. The fans love a good tag team match, and where better than a tag team to perform than in the UWF ring? Tag Teams are a very big factor of the UWF. Most of the famous UWF loved characters come from these duos of steel. There's special events that only tag teams can compete in, like the Venetian Home Rumble for example. Where numerous loved characters decide to kill each other in a Venetian home. The UWF's tag team division isnt just a tag team division.. it's a guild for the elite. If you honestly think your team have what it takes to wrestle with the best, however demented they are (and believe us we've seen it all, from "Al Crud & A Christmas Tree" to "The Bunny Army Of Doom~!") then give us a call!
