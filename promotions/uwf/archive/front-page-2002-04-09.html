UWF Fanzine Shock at UWF(TM) Censorship -- April 8, 2002 Latest News… As expected, the UWF Fanzine was removed from shelves yesterday by the very company that it aims to support. As of yet, UWF top dogs have not commented… but chief editor of the magazine yesterday exclaimed: 'The UWF will have to learn that it survives solely because of media attention from the reporters and magazines dedicated to its shows… it is a partnership. If they continue to show flagrant disregard for our reporters creativity then I will be forced to take things further." He added… 'I will get to the bottom of this, the article in question though close to the bone for some EWA wrestlers no doubt was not at all based on any falsehood and all stories have and were substantiated either by their actions, fondness for the word 'AND' or confirmation from the nut house of their certification. The hypocrisy of all of this is that the very people whom took our magazines off the shelves and censored our material are the very same people circulating propaganda over at the EWA… They even provide scripted direction to their commentators to mock and berate the EWA. One rule for one, and another for others… this is the same ethics that got long standing publishing purchases the Welsh Academy of Noble Knowledge bared from the UWF Forums. Mark my words, heads will role for this, and there may be some UWF higher ups that are among them… we take no prisons and show no discrimination.' Report by UWF Fanzine UWFanzine Reports... quick before we're taken off the shelves!!! -- April 5, 2002 Have you seen the Youngbloods? As news came in earlier this week of the untimely death of the formerly invulnerable Under Lords speculation is rife with the possibility of Youngblood deaths. The Youngbloods, established way back in the IWF in the late 90s has always had a cult following and a reputation of signing up members it once feuded with in a bizarre twist of allegiances pinned on the hopes of the lack of a good memory on other people's parts. And now, it seems that like all cults centred around one hugely inflated egotistical megalomaniac, that's Lew by the way, may have actually been commanded to take part in a mass suicide… as they make a protest to Cadbury's to try and get Cream Eggs sold throughout the whole calendar year. So far these rumours are yet to be confirmed, but by the same token of Youngblood laryngitis, they haven't been denied so we're running with it. In other news… the UWF was said to see Shadow the Happy yet Crappy Goth leave due to forces beyond his control. Yes, he wasn't allowed to rig fights so that he was victorious, something to do with the lunacy that 'no he wasn't better than any of the other PWWers, let alone anyone else, so he didn't deserve it'. He now leaves to Pursue his non Gothic Gimmick called… Shadow… which is pretty much the same as the old one. The UWF was also sorry to see the man who loves the word 'and' go too, Mr Blaze… due to other commitments. Mr Blaze, we salute you with a tribute of your typical work… 'Blaze slides out of the ring and walks over to his black gym bag and grabs the towel off the top of it and pulls a bottle of water out and takes a few sips.' 'Blaze puts the towel over his head and walks towards the ropes and leans on the top rope and looks down at the camera.' Mr Blaze we wish you luck in future and wish you the best and you and your federation and 'and' all and every success and triumph. The UWF was also sorry to see the man they had invested much time in, in the form of Chris Steel, leave due to a lack of time on his hands… as opposed to the other guys who gives his ANDS too much time. On a good note, the EWA is pleased to welcome Shadow into the position of 'Yes I'm capable of now rigging matches the way I think (Though remember I'm totally unhinged, mad, and deranged… you got the mails right?) they should go Commissioner. It also welcomes the new addition of Chris Steel… hmm… Obviously the demands of one card a month PWW style isn't quite as taxing. And finally… the UWF is pleased to reveal that Jay Phoenix is still with us and working has hard as ever… Go Phoenix. Report by UWF Fanzine Danny's Dominance Has Begun! -- April 4, 2002 Much to the surprise of UWF fans everywhere, as well as Phil and Connie, Danny Jaye took the dominant position in the Hardcore Brawl on Wednesday Night Universal. From the start of the match, Danny and Draven Lox put Slasher into a very submissive position. Draven also looked very good that night, often working with Danny in tandem, and as a team. In the end, though, it was Danny and The Nuke that ended the match. Afterwards, we saw no ill will towards Draven on Danny's part, and it's quite possible that we could see a unity between these 2 superstars. There has been no comment on this from Draven, but announcements will be made at the appropriate time. In other news, the rumors that Danny Jaye will undergo a CAT scan are greatly exaggerated; Why ruin a good thing? Report by Trey Bamban More Gold For The Circle! -- April 3, 2002 The Inner Circle has yet gained even more gold as Gurgles the clown beat Showtime for the Intercontinental Championship tonight. The Inner Circle seem to be bringing in the titles by the second. Snake Phoenix is the Shoot Wrestling Champion, Jamie Shock is a Tag Team Champion (along with the World Champion, Shawn Eclipse), The Plague is, although it is retired, The Hardcore Champion, and Gurgles is of course the new IC Champion. The only title that isn't with the Circle is the TV Championship, but whether any of them care about that is unknown. Report by Kyle the Cameraman The Where abouts of Joshua Dunn Revealed -- April 3, 2002 After finally "breaking into" Joshua Dunn's dressing room, which he reclaimed after the apparant disapearance of the occupants of it, we found that behind the barricaded door there was a note left on the coffee table addressed to the Scotsman which read, "Dear Joshua, I was just in the area and thought I'd drop you a line, can't say I aprove of you rolling around with other men, but I've seen the money you make. I'm staying in a hotel, the name escapes me, but it's quite close to the wrestling centre that you are in, sure you can find me," Dad. "P.S. your mother says hi." After searching through several hotels in the area we are still unable to find anyone by the name of Dunn that has checked in, I'll be following this up as soon as information is available. Report by The Phat Man Snake reveals his "Special Match" -- April 3, 2002 After being relatively silent for the past weeks, Snake Phoenix appeared at a special conference today concerning this ‘special match’ he has been talking about in the past weeks. Well I can now reveal that the match which is titled “Rumble in the Urban Jungle” is a I-Quit match with a twist. The twist being the whole match takes place in down town Los Angeles (which happens to be Snakes hometown). Basically the two competitors (Snake and Ryder) will battle it out for the Shoot Wrestling Championship in the city using anything and anyone they can find to cause harm to their opponent. The only restrictions being that match ends either after an hour is up or one of the wrestlers says, “I-Quit”. The match is believed to take place at No Where to Hide (which is also in Los Angeles) and will hopefully draw in a bigger television than ever before in the UWF Pay-per-view history. Report by Bob the Interviewer Under-Lord Tribute Planned -- April 2, 2002 As you may already know, the recent announcement of the Under-Lord's demise has left the entire wrestling world in a state of shock. Speechless fans wearing Under-Lord merchandise have been wandering streets across the nation, dazed and confused, incapable of understanding what has happened to their lives. In an attempt to give these many fans of the Under-Lords some form of closure, a recent press release from the UWF offices has announced that a live tribute will be held in the honor of these fearless superstars. The UWF is apparently set to spare no expense in the tribute, and it will surely make the show one that no true wrestling fan can miss. I ... *sniff* ... I need a tissue. Report by Bill Schwartz Under-Lords found dead! -- April 1, 2002 The Under-Lords were found dead in a half beried pyramid in the early hours of Wednesday morning, how the news took so long to travel is unknown. The Under-Lords, who are basically a rip off of The Dark Lords, lost their tag team championship shot against The Lost Prophets a few weeks ago. We never saw another promotion from them since.. probably because all the other men who had the task of filming them ended up in hospital trying to catch their fights against monsters and such, or brawls in fuel stations.. one even got shot in the leg. A monster with giant wings and a red twisted body was found near the scene stabbed with three gold daggers. According to only Under-Lords fan, it is a women who goes by the name of "Natla" (the one who took the role of using the Under-Lords to do evil deeds and purchase huge limos). Which should eliminate everything to do with The Under-Lords, perhaps making them extinct. Or so I, and everyone else, hopes. Report by Kyle the Cameraman SAS Shocker! -- March 31, 2002 Sunday Afternoon Suicide came and went, and delivered some shocks during the course of the show. First up was the UWF newcomer Draven Lox taking on former PWW star, and current UWF wrestler Shadow. Lox came out on top in the match, but after the match the twisted invididual Mike Montgomery ran down to ringside and massacred Lox with a Steel Chair, and almost broke Lox's ankle. Unfortunately, security were too slow in their approach to get Montgomery and he escaped through the crowd. Max Queue was quoted as saying: "I...I... You... You... CORWUMPH! I'll set Murphy on him next time..." Next up, was the Television Title match between champion, Dex and Flip of Team MIV, Slasher and Digital Mortality. This one swung back and forth but in the end, Danny Davis got caught up and was knocked unconscious... leaving both Dex and Flip pinning their opponents. Referee James Beard made the count as well as Davis and both men were crowned half-champions. Rod only knows what might happen in the future with these two. The next match was a difficult one for the Shoot Wrestling Championship, as Danny Jaye took on the Champion Snake Phoenix. Ruff Ryder, the #1 contender to Phoenix's title, came down and assaulted Danny Jaye of all people, in an attempt to get Snake Phoenix to book the match for UWF: Nowhere to Hide, but that wasn't all that happenned... Danny Jaye recovered and then beat on Snake Phoenix with the ringbell and his manager Trey declared that the "Ole Butt of Jokes" Danny Jaye is through, and a new, meaner Jaye will be seen from now on. The main event saw UWF World Champion Shawn Eclipse and his partner Jamie Shock taking on the Dragoons and the Lost Prophets on for the World Tag Team Championships. The match was a long, drawn out one, only decided when Jamie Shock used the ropes for leverage to get the pinfall over Bahamut of the Dragoons and to lift the titles for the Inner Circle. UWF Commissioner Lew Spectre will have something to say on Universal... lets just hope he can keep the swear words down to a minimum.
