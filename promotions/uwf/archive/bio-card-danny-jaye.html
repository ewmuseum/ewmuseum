UWF bio card: Danny Jaye

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Danny Jaye Wrestlers Name: "Da Bomb" Danny Jaye Face/heel or neutral: Face Height: 6' 10" Weight: 310 lbs Hometown: Memphis, TN Entrance Music: "FEAR" by Disturbed Style: Powerhouse, with a touch of lucha libre Finisher: The Nuke Description of Finisher: Corkscrew-style slam Wrestler's Appearance: As seen above, or similar. Background Info: Danny Jaye was first exposed to unarmed combat as a kickboxer in the United States Air Force. After his honorable discharge, he entered the arena of professional wrestling in an independent federation in his hometown of Memphis, TN. After this, he was picked up by the TCW. The rest, as they say, is history. Danny loves to pop the crowd. Sometimes, it seems, the more the crowd is into the match, the better he wrestles. His colorful style and unique personality are matched by his raw power and skill. These qualities together help to make Danny Jaye the formidable contender that he is. Accomplishments: UWF Television Champion, TCW World Champion, TCW International Champion, TCW Tag Team Champion (F.E.A.R. - w/ Jay Vyper), TWF Tag Team Champion (F.E.A.R. - w/ Jay Vyper), TWF Internet Champion
