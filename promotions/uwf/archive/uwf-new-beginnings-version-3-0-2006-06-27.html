New beginnings: UWF version 3.0

27 Jun 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An announcement posted by the UWF's owner, Jaymes Faulkner, on the RPCommunity forum (announcement thread 3023) and e-mailed to the forum's members; this is the e-mail copy, from the Janitor's own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The mail's automatic unsubscribe footer is not carried; the sender's address is marked. It tells the roster that the UWF will stay open, that the running storylines cease, and that it begins again as version 3.0, with a first show on 31 July.

Follows “UWF’s future”. Served whole, as written.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: RPCommunity Forums [detail withheld] Date: Tue, Jun 27, 2006 at 3:30 AM Subject: New announcement: New beginnings - UWF version 3.0 OK, before anyone panics there is nothing wrong with your eyes, there is nothing wrong with your monitors and there is nothing wrong with the forums - we simply control the horizontal AND the vertical ;) Seriously though, the subject should say it all really, and should let you understand why the forums have been 'wiped clean' so to speak. After reading through the last couple of weeks posts and taking on board a lot of helpful ideas/suggestions from you guys the following decisions have been made: 1/ The UWF will be staying open. 2/ The idea to try to keep all ongoing angles/storylines going, struggling as they were, from the last few months isn't working and so will cease. 3/ A new staff structure will be setup with clearly defined roles. 4/ A new schedule of shows/PPVs will be setup and kept to as closely as possible. 5/ UWF gets a brand new start - basically put we are going into UWF V.3.0. :) So, what happens next? That is simple ... we do this as if we are starting from scratch in many ways. That doesn't mean that everyone's (who is staying around) hard work will be forgotten about, oh no, in fact they will form the basis/core of the new start. All the new guys that have signed up in the last few weeks won't be forgotten either. While the details are not confirmed as yet (the new staff need to be consulted) the idea/concept behind what is going to happen to kick-start this and run through the first story arc is planned and ready to go - and should give a fed-wide angle for people to play with if they want to. So all I need next is you guys to reply and let me know if you are on board or not - emails to [detail withheld] with some brief details (who you are, character, etc) would be great or even a reply on the forums. The date for the first official show of the new era is July 31st, and that date will not change (there will be a 'show' prior to that which will explain what is happening IC and setup the new era, just so that you know, and I will be asking for oncard/segments for that ;) ). Any questions/suggestions/feedback/thoughts, you know where I am. You can view the full announcement by following this link: http://forum.rpcommunity.com/index.php?topic=3023.0
