Working papers: the Live and Loud 4 running order, in three revisions

15 October 2004 (the show; file dated 16 October 2004)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The promoter's planning notes for Live and Loud 4, in three passes in one file: the running order (angles, matches and Hall of Fame inductions, with some of the writer's own doubts left in), a checklist of the same order with the writers' first names beside the pieces they had taken, and an editing checklist in a different order. These are planning papers, not a card. The served show page is the result.

Belongs to Live and Loud 4. Served whole, as written.
