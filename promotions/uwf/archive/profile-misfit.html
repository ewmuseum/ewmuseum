B I O G R A P H Y One of the most well-travelled men in the busines, Misfit has done it all. His pre-wrestling life is virtually non-existant in documentation, the only scraps available are his birth certificate (born in Seattle, Washington to a single mother) and his high school diploma (he graduated near the top of his class, though none of his schoolmates seem to remember him). His career began at the age of 18 with little to no training. Thus, like many others, he turned to the American garbage scene and quickly established himself as a god amongst the hardcore cicuits. For a couple of years he would travel the country, learning the wrestling basics from the trained wrestlers he stepped into the ring with night after night. In 1997, Misfit decided to leave the wrestling world for a short time to recieve more in-depth training. He once again packed up his van and travelled the country, spending various amounts of time at the best schools money could buy. He would reimerge towards the end of the year to begin dominating the west coast indie scene, wracking up a near flawless win/loss record and several championships. Even with his training, his passion for American hardcore could not be tamed and he soon began to balance his appearances on the west coast indies with runs in the biggest garbage feds in the country. 1999 brought about Misfit's first run with a major company, pulling him out of the indie and garbage feds and into the homes of the average American wrestling fan. The critics loved his ring psychology and charism, though his lack of variety in style caused him to falter. After failing to draw as he was expected, the company let Misfit go and he soon seemed to disappear once again. In early-2002, Misfit seemingly rose from the dead to join the UWF roster. He claimed to have travelled the wrestling world during his absence, though no documents were available to prove his claims. His in-ring performance, however, told the story differently. Not soon to forget, the backlash was near-immediate as his presence in the hotshot fed was heavily protested. Upon being given a chance to shine in the ring, however, the critics swiftly changed their tune. Misfit has since become a figurehead of the UWF, claiming almost every prize available and continuing to impress everyone with his innovative, unmatched style. He blends every possible style of wrestling together almost seamlessly with his excellent psychology, and continues to deliver mindblowing, charismatic promos. Having paid his dues, its now just a matter of time before Misfit finally has his time. M O V E S E T Wrestling Style Innovative (brawler, light technician, daredevil) Basic Moves Ender (Misfit's submission finisher, a variation on the Dragon sleeper) Falling Star (a variation on the shooting star press) Trifixion (a trio of Death Valley Drivers) Evenflow DDT Fabulous Disaster (a variation on Sonjay Dutt's Hindu Press) Halo Angel's Wings Descending Angel (a variation on the Swanton Bomb) Spear Five Stories Falling (a crucifix backdrop from the top rope) Spellbinder (a reverse Boston crab; ripped off from Kenny Mercury as an insult) spinning heel kick wide variation of suplexes (Northern Lights is favored) standing moonsault sitout powerbomb old school brawling in-ring offensive and definsive innovations Signature Moves A Fire Inside (A spiked Falcon Arrow delivered from suplex position.) Finisher Stigmata (A fisherman DDT.)
