LEAP YOU LATINO BASTARD! LEAP~!”   [Digital Mortality does.]   [His hand flies through the air … dropping past Brad Johnson’s and hitting the rope moments before it follows the rest of his body.]   Rick: “I … what?”   Larson: “Digi missed the tag? In the history of wrestling!!! MINORITY IN TROUBLE I SAY~! JAAAAAAAAAAMMMMMMIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!”   Rick: “Shock’s gone … Dunn’s gone … and Johnson has just turned on him! DI-GI-MON~!”   Rick: “And it couldn’t happen to a nicer guy!”   “AND ROLL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!”   [DJ Cool, instead of finishing the move, quickly realizes he’s now on fire as well. Not happening … won’t believe it … refuse … to … accept it.”   [DJ Cool rolls over the form of the UWF Hall of Famer, trying to beat off the flames now enveloping his own clothing as he makes the pin attempt. Someone must have spiked our last round of drinks.”   [Jerry Kohler bends down, doing his best to fend off the popping of his joints as well as the swirling blaze … and, amazingly, ignores his missing pants long enough to count.]   One …   Larson: “This is silly.”   Two …   Rick: “I just … Brad Johnson … that man might have just made a HUGE mistake tonight.”   Larson: “Or he would have if this wasn’t just a hallucinogen-fueled dream I happen to be ha –“   Rick: “There it is!!!”   THREE!!   Rick: “DIGITAL MORTALITY IS GONE!!!!”   Larson: “No … no, no, no … NO!!!!!”   [Brad Johnson instantly rolls into the ring, flying over to where the cover was made. Don’t we employee some sort of security?!?! What the fuck do we pay these idiots for when they never stop anything?!?”   [The second count triggers a change as quick as the flip of a light switch. Can you hear me?!?!? Mortality … Shock … Dunn … you’ve just crossed the biggest guns in the wrestling world, and I hope they skin your delusional ass ALIVE for this!!!”   Four!   [Instead of falling one way or the other, Mortality simply hangs across both ends, slowly see-sawing on its rigid base … as the Firestarter approaches.]   Rick: “Oh no …”   Larson: “DON’T EVEN THINK ABOUT IT YOU FREAK!!!”   [Lifting up the portion of the ring steps he’d brought along for the ride, Brad Johnson now stands over Digital Mortality’s upturned and dangling upper-half … his green eyes completely glazed over and uncomprehending of what is about to happen to him.]   Five!   Rick: “Don—“   [The stairs come down in an arch, directly across Mortality’s face and chest, knocking him off the guardrail with a full and direct thud. Starr’s made a career on capitalizing on any opportunity that presents itself.]   [And with DJ Cool virtually out of it, and only a chimpanzee left on the outside to stand in Starr’s way for one of the biggest crowns in existence … the opportunity does not get any better.]   [DJ Cool begins to slowly get to his feet, still too loopy to have any idea of what’s actually going on or where he even is … and while he does, Kevin Starr simply waits anxiously … his hands open and close rhythmically, his eyes set firmly on his opponent … his heart beating a million times a minute as the crowd slowly builds its cheers into a fever pitch.]   [DJ Cool is up, spies Starr, and rushes forward … and at the very same instant, Starr dives to the side.]   Rick: “Wha--?!”   [Pete the PG Tips Chimp flies through the air, his eyes widening in shock as he passes right by where Kevin Starr had been standing seconds before … DJ Cool’s doing the same as the furry missile comes in at full-speed towards his crotch.]   Larson: “Oh … God.”   [Unable to stop himself, Pete hits DJ Cool full on … clamping on the Chimpy Chompy Crunch!]   Rick: “PETE’S HIT HIS OWN PARTNER!!! PETE THE PG TIPS CHIMP AND JUST CRIPPLED HIS OWN PARTNER WITH ONE OF THE MOST DEVASTATING MOVES KNOWN TO MAN!!!”   [DJ Cool drops like a sack of bricks … and the attention immediately turns to Kevin Starr, who now stands atop the top rope, looking out over the cheering crowd.]   Larson: “He’s going to … !”   [With Pete still desperately trying to disconnect from DJ Cool’s groin, Kevin Starr leaps up … flipping backwards … and moonsaulting down onto both opponents!]   Rick: “WISH UPON A STARR!!! WISH UPON A STARR!!!!! KEVIN STARR HAS DESTROYED EVERYONE!!!!”   [Pete is thrown free, his furry chest rising and falling rapidly several feet away now … and Starr immediately rolls up both of DJ Cool’s legs, not taking any chances at having the biggest moment in his career ruined. Stable Wars is at an end!!!!”   [The rush forward … Starr goes for a field-goal kick, but Pete slips between his legs. Come on you little bastard … even Bruce Gordy makes a better Stable Wars Champ than a chimpanzee!!!!”   Rick: “I—“   [A haunting melody begins to play over the loudspeakers … almost medieval in its tune. DESTROY THEM ALL SEYMOUR!!!!”   [Kevin Starr is immediately ready for the attack. Kevin Starr has one more roadblock in his way to the Stable Wars crown … but at the very least, his only opponent left is a chimpanzee.”   Larson: “Starr can’t win if he’s decapitated!”   [The standoff between the two long-time stars only lasts a few short seconds, however … Almasy rushes forward, roaring. No one has EVER survived that move … and he’s got it on in … in …”   Larson: “I KNOW!!!! PETE THE PG TIPS CHIMP IS GOING TO WIN STABLE WARS!!!!!!!!!!!
