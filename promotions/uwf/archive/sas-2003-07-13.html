Sunday Afternoon Supremacy — 13 Jul 2003

13 Jul 2003

[Begin Transmission.][In an usual place.] *cue Trailer Park Luchador monolouge* Artist: Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds Song: Sunday's Slave Sunday's got a slave Monday's got one too Sunday's got a slave Monday's got one too Our sufferings are countless Our pleasures are motley few Spend all day digging my grave Now go get Sunday's slave Tuesday sleeps in a stable Wednesday's in a chains Tuesday gathes up the crumbs under the table Wednesday dare not complain My heart has collapsed on the tracks of a run-a-way train Just whisper his name And hee comes Sunday's slave The hands in the stable are willing and able to pay If you feel at a loss, man, just who is the boss-man Ask the blood of one of its bad days For his nerve is to serve but the service is a mockery He insists that he piss in your fist But he still takes the money anyway The master's a bastard But don't tell Sunday's slave Thursday's angered the master O.K. so Friday's gonna pay Thursday's angered the master Yeah, so Friday's gonna pay One night on the rack and he's back saddling up Saturday You can only whisper his name But not on Sundays Never on Sundays O Not on Sunday's slave [The Atlantian Dome, Phoenix, Arizona.] The scene opens backstage where The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid is giving an interview with resident interviewer Toby Foster. All of a sudden, as he's about to leave, a voice yells out:   "Let's see how you like this!" An unseen person speaks, as both HIT and Toby turn to look in the direction of the new voice. Toby jumps back as a fist connects with the jawline of the Hardcore Math Teacher. HIT staggers back holding his jaw as none other than his opponent, Killer Dude, steps into the scene. Toby uses his limited intelligence and gets away from the scene before it becomes too violent. Killer Dude throws another punch at HIT but HIT throws up his arm and manages to block the punch. He returns a punch of his own and sends Killer Dude reeling. He follows up with a kick to Killer Dude's stomach and then he grabs a handful of Killer Dude's hair and whips him into the chain link fence in the background. HIT: You're not so tough now, are you Killer?! HIT walks over to where Killer Dude is hunched over on the ground. As HIT kneels down to grab Killer Dude, Killer swings upward holding something in his hands, hitting the Math Teacher in the head with it. It's a stereo! HIT falls backwards, hitting the floor hard as Killer Dude stands to his feet. Killer Dude holds the side of his head as he walks towards the fallen HIT. Killer Dude pauses above HIT and looks down upon his prey. He stomps HIT's body a couple of times. He takes a break from the stomping and promptly kicks him hard in the ribs. Killer Dude looks up and notices Toby Foster standing around the corner watching the attack on HIT from the safety of her vantage point. Killer Dude: Hey Toby. Get over here. Bring me that microphone! Toby slowly walks towards him. He stops in front of him and holds out his microphone. Killer Dude snatches it from his hand and then pushes him away. The man almost falls to the ground. He leaves before anything else can happen to him. With Toby gone, Killer Dude looks down at HIT and kicks him again before speaking. Killer Dude: What happened to being 'hardcore' now man? You can't even take a stereo shot? You're not so tough are you, Mr. PCW Superstar? It's the people like you that make me sick to my god damned stomach! It's the people like you that have kept me out of the main event! But guess what? You can't keep me out anymore! I have reached the top Math Teacher, and there is nothing you can do about it! HIT begins to stir again so Killer Dude delivers another vicious boot to his jaw, knocking him back down again. Killer Dude: As I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted, what are you going to do tonight HIT? What are you going to do at Coronation? You can't even handle a stereo shot, just wait till I get my golf club. You can't handle me in a brawl. How in the hell do you expect to stand up to me in a golf course match? You gonna answer me? I didn't think so... SEE YOU IN HELL BITCH!!!!! With those words, Killer Dude hurls the microphone down, smacking HIT in the chest with it, knocking the air out of him, and making a loud static crackle noise in the process. Killer Dude walks away from the scene, leaving HIT laying, leaving Toby Foster stunned, and leaving the fans booing from inside the arena. Referee Winner: Danny Jaye & Max Damage The Wimp and Chimp Connection locker room is a peaceful place. Maurice sits in his wheelchair, as the World Champion Petey enjoys a nice banana. Unfortunately, this tranquility is about to be shattered..."STATUS EFFECT: DARKNESS!" A cloud of black mist is spewed into the room, and directly into the face of Maurice. The wheelchair bound man flails wildly, as his flailing overturns himself. An armor-clad hand grabs Petey by the scruff of his neck. The Final Fantasy has come for vengeance. Seymour Almasy: I SUPPOSE YOU THOUGHT THAT WAS FUNNY, VILE CREATURE! WELL, IF YOU LIKED THAT, YOU'LL LOVE THIS! ENEMY SKILL: BAD BREATH! Seymour sprays green mist into the face of the hapless monkey. It rubs at its eyes, as Seymour reaches into his pocket, and pulls out a cork. Seymour Almasy: Your Poison attack may have made me surrender on Universal, but now I shall take that weapon away from you! Seymour lifts the monkey up skyward, and places the cork on a table. Petey looks at Seymour in horror, as the Final Fantasy slams the monkey down onto the table. Petey shrieks out in pain, as the cork had wedged itself into the poor creature's rectum. Seymour Almasy: Now that you cannot poison me, my victory is assured! Oh, I almost forgot... Going into his other pocket, he pulls out an autographed picture of Aerith in revealing lingerie. He hands it to the agonized monkey. Seymour Almasy: There. That's the final stipulation. See you this Sunday!
