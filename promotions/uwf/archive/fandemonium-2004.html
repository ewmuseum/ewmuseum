Oni is still on the mat clutching his ribs as Almasy walks over to him and slowly pulls him to his feet. Almasy locks in an abdominal stretch, causing Oni to let out a scream of sheer agony. As Almasy cinches the hold in even tighter he starts delivering rapid punches to the ribs.] Rick: The experience of Almasy is starting to show through now. He sees an injured part of the body, and he is going to attack it. Larson: Glad to see that all that tournament experience is good from something other than his bank account. [Almasy grabs the rope behind him, and Oni starts to flail his one free arm in pain. The referee moves in to check on Oni but just as Almasy refused to give up earlier the World champion turns his head away from the referee and tries to fight back against the pain.] Rick: Come on referee, you may have to stop this if Oni’s ribs are broken! Larson: Hey look Almasy is playing nice and letting go! [Almasy releases the hold and shoves Oni, face first, to the mat. Touching his forehead, Seymour wipes away fluid that is building up there and then looks down at the blood on his hand, shick evident on his face … quickly replaced by anger. Pulling Oni up to his feet Almasy drags him over to the ropes and the chain mesh behind it. Almasy starts to slam his head into the cage, repeating this for a count of ten that some of the crowd start to chant in time, then lets Oni fall to the mat. The camera zooms in on Oni on the mat and blood starts to flow from his forehead, seeping through his mask.] Rick: Both men are now bleeding in this one. Larson: Well what is good for the goose is good for the gander you know. Rick: Sorry? Larson: Turn around is fair play. Rick: Excuse me? Larson: An eye for an eye, and all that. Rick: What are you babbling about? Larson: * SIGH* Oni did it to Seymour so now Almasy is doing it to Tsunami! Rick: Oh … why didn’t you just say so?!?!? Larson: I DID!! [Quickly climbing the turnbuckle, Almasy bounces off the ropes and drops a leg across the chest and throat of Oni. Almasy rolls off and gets up to his feet, pulling Oni up with him as he grabs his head in a lock. With one fluid motion Almasy slams Oni’s head into the canvas with a version of a tornado DDT, quickly rolling his opponent over as if for a pinfall. With a small grin to himself Almasy bounces up to his feet and starts to walk towards the cage door.] Larson: Almasy so used to covering from the Shadow Flare that he went for the pin! Rick: Don’t know why he is heading there, he hasn’t got the key yet, and leaving the ring doesn’t win him the match! Larson: Our is not to reason why … Rick: Oh shut up with the damn metaphors! Larson … ok … [Almasy opens the door to the cage and starts to step through, just as Oni staggers to his feet and dives at him with a double axe handle towards the back. Quickly moving out of the way, Almasy slams the door against the head of the oncoming Oni, launching him back into the ring as he clutches at his forehead with both hands. Oni drops to both knees and Almasy turns around with a large grin on his face] Larson: Not a metaphor but THAT is why he went to the door … to slam it into Oni’s face! [Almasy reaches down and pulls Oni up and whips him towards the opposite ropes, not waiting for his return but instead running towards him with his arm extended for a running lariat, but as Oni hits the ropes he somehow finds the energy to spring back himself and leap into the air, nailing Almasy with a huge flying cross body that drives him into the doorframe. Almasy slumps to the apron as he clutches his back before Oni pulls him back into the middle of the ring.] Rick: Almasy taking some damage to that back from that impact. Larson: Hell Oni almost knocked him out of the cage, but I don’t think that it helped his ribs any! [Oni drags Almasy to his feet and picks him up, putting him on the top rope. Oni then quickly climbs up on to the top rope himself and stands next to Almasy on the ropes while Seymour sits on the turnbuckle. Oni leaps into the air and catches Almasy with his legs around his neck and attempts to pull him off with a hurricanrana, but Almasy blocks it. Grabbing hold of Oni’s waist Almasy launches himself off the ropes and drives Oni to the mat with a powerbomb.] Rick: Huge mistake on the part of Oni. Larson: Almasy is up to something, look at his face!! [A look of pure concentration fills Almasy’s face as he looks down at the prone Oni. Leaving him lying there Seymour staggers back to the turnbuckle and hoists himself onto it] Rick: Almasy going for the key here! Larson: … no … Almasy going for the punishment here! [Looking down at Oni who has managed to turn himself face down in his attempt to regain his feet Almasy grins as he readies himself, and then with a burst of effort he throws himself from the top rope to land a harsh knee drop into the neck of the World champion, causing him to spasm wildly. Not content with this, Almasy drags Oni to his feet and pats him on the top of the head as he whips him to the corner, where he slumps against the ropes, seemingly barely conscious. Almasy slowly walks towards him and grabs him by the head before turning around and climbing up to the middle rope. Throwing himself backwards Almasy sends Oni crashing to the canvas with a springboard tornado DDT.] Larson: It’s all but over now. Almasy just has to climb the cage to the key that is waiting for him! [Almasy calmly gets to his feet and slowly climbs to the top rope, then starts to climb up the cage. Almasy reaches the top as Oni starts to pull himself up.] Rick: There is a lot of fight left in Oni it seems … though I have NO idea how he is still breathing let alone fighting! [Grabbing hold of the mesh Almays starts to swing his way towards the centre of the cage’s roof, where the key hangs waiting. The wire digs into his hands slightly and it takes his whole concentration and effort to keep his grip on the unforgiving links as he swings his way slowly towards the key. Unseen by him, a steel ladder has been slipped into the ring from one side of the fan’s enclosure while on the other side a folded table and two steel chairs are slipped through the slots. Wearily getting to his feet a chilling smile plays across Oni’s face as he sees these items and ignoring Almasy for the moment he reaches down to the table and starts to set it up near one corner.] Rick: You know the fans had been so awestruck by the match that they had forgotten that they could take part in this match! Larson: I had forgotten about them as well! Rick: … well it looks like they have chosen now to get involved, and it may end up being something that Seymour Almasy never forgets because Oni seems to be setting something up! [The table is unfolded and ready as Oni bends down and picks up the ladder, opening it as he stands it up in the centre of the ring … right below the key! Finally realizing what is going on Almasy starts to move slightly faster towards the dangling key as Oni starts to climb the ladder one handed. … his other hand holding one of the steel chairs. Just as Almasy is about to reach the key, one hand holding his weight as the other reaches out for the object just a finger’s width away, Oni reaches the top of the ladder and wraps his arm around the top rung as he brings the steel chair up ready to slam into Almasy. Reacting faster, Almasy reaches up and grabs the cage with both hands as he swings his feet up and out, catching the chair before it can hit him and sending it crashing into Oni’s face. Swaying backwards Oni nearly loses his balance but is able to grab hold of the ladder with both hands … the chair dropping to the mat below … as he groggily shakes his head to clear it] Rick: Vicious chair shot to the head of Oni there … twenty feet above the ground! Larson: … why can’t I see the key anymore? Rick: It is in Almasy’s hand … still attached to the cage but it is IN HIS HAND!!! [Almasy looks up as he too realizes that when he grabbed the cage he also grabbed the key and a large smile spreads across his face as he starts to pull it free. A smile that doesn’t last long] Rick: Dear God in Heaven!!! [Realizing that his opponent is about to get a large advantage and get even closer to taking his title away, Oni reacts before his brain can work through his actions fully. He throws himself off the ladder and tackles Almasy across the ribs, spearing him in mid air. For an instant, a split second, the two men seem to defy gravity and hang suspended and then reality takes over and they come crashing downwards. Seymour Almasy and Tsunami Oni in a wild embrace that mimics lovers, one cushioning the other slightly. Onto the table. Into the table. Through the table. … and finally, as the audience go deathly silent and the crash of splintering wood fades away they come to rest on the mat itself in a torn and twisted heap of body parts and wood] Rick: … Larson: … Audience: … Rick: Jesus … are they moving? Larson: … are they ALIVE? [The referee runs across the ring and stares down in stunned shock at the carnage in front of him, glancing up in disbelief as his eyes trace the twenty foot arc that the two wrestlers traveled from the top of the cage, through the table, to the canvas below. Pulling shards of broken table off the fallen men he reaches down and checks on them. Neither one moving. Each one barely breathing. Facing back to the entrance ramp he raises his arms and crosses them, a look of panic on his face.] Rick: Oh no, something is seriously wrong here folks, the referee is calling for help! Larson: … what is happening, is he stopping the match? Who won? Rick: Dammit Larson, I don’t know who won, I don’t even know if those two guys are still breathing! [A small sign of movement comes from within the carnage as Tsunami Oni rolls away from the fallen Almasy. The referee drops to his knees and starts to talk to the World champion, pointing backwards to where EMTs are starting to come down the rampway accompanied by UWF staff, their faces ashen. His gaze following where the referee indicates, realization dawns on Oni and he starts to shake his head before his body is wracked with convulsive coughs. Leaning away from the referee he spits out a wad of blood. Slowly getting to his feet he wobbly looks around him before a small … almost invisible … smile plays across his face as he reaches down to one of the slots near a fan] Rick: Oni is up, barely on his feet, but at least he looks like he may be ok. Larson: Did you see the blood that came out of his mouth, he is not ok … he is broken inside and Almasy isn’t even moving! Rick: Seymour took the brunt of the blow, and if Oni looks THAT bad just imagine what Almasy must feel like! Larson: The EMTs are at the ring now … hang on what is Oni doing?!?!? [As the EMTs reach the cage and open the door, Oni suddenly lunges forwards and pushes the referee out of the ring. As he falls into the EMTs they all collapse onto the ground in a heap as Oni fumbles with the door, closing it as he wraps his arms around it] Rick: Is he hugging the door? Larson: No … something worse, he is locking it! [Falling backwards into the ring, Oni collapses on the canvas as he holds his ribs and convulses with coughs again. The EMTs and referee get back to their feet and attempt to open the cage door, only to find it chained closed and padlocked shut. Oni looks towards them and grimly shakes his head in the negative] Rick: Oni must have got that chain and lock from a fan … it must have been intended as a weapon during the match. Larson: … and now instead it could mean the end of one or both of these men’s careers as Oni is making it VERY clear that he won’t allow this match to end this way. He wants to FINISH it! Rick: He can barely breathe without coughing up blood, Almasy hasn’t moved in about three minutes … how the Hell can this match continue?!?!? Larson: I don’t know … but the referee cannot get that door open and Oni and Almasy are still inside, so it looks like it WILL continue! [Dragging himself over to Almasy, Oni clears more of the rubble of the table out of the way, moving the fallen ladder off Seymour’s chest as he throws splinters of wood across the ring. He kicks one of the steel chairs to one side before finally reaching down and then pushing his hand into the air as if claiming victory.] Rick: What is he doing? Larson: It is the key Rick, he has the key! [Looking up at the small piece of metal that may have ended Almasy’s life let alone career Oni bows his head, holding his ribs with his free hand. Moving to the centre of the ring Oni slumps to his knees as he starts to fumble with the lock that will open the padlock. He rubs at his eyes, maybe trying to clear blood or sweat … or fighting off a concussion caused double vision … as he fights to fit the key in the lock] Rick: … my God, I think that I believe in miracles … LOOK! [From behind Oni, unnoticed by the Champion, Seymour Almasy’s body writhes slightly looking for all intents and purposes as if he is being resuscitated by an unseen electric shock. Slowly at first and then with more vigor his body moves until with a spray of blood he suddenly breathes out and rolls to his knees. Looking around him his eyes don’t seem to focus on anything as he crouches forwards and leans his head against the canvas. … and then his hand closes around the discarded steel chair. Finally getting the key into the lock, Oni makes a small noise … half laugh and half sob … as with a barely audible click the lock springs open. Reaching forwards Oni grabs the edge of the small trapdoor with his fingers and pulls it upwards, visibly straining his tired, beaten and broken body as he pushes the lid of the trapdoor away from him, exposing the circle of blackness that seems to devour all light that enters it. Peering down into it’s depths Oni shudders and then slowly gets to his knees first, then finally … unsteadily … to his feet. Letting the key fall into the hole he slowly turns around to where he left the fallen Almasy, ready to give the final effort of dropping the challenger into the hole at his feet. … and is met with his last sight of a bloodied and torn visage of Almasy before a steel chair explodes into his face. Without conscious effort, and with the unconscious grace of a ballet dancer, Oni limply falls backwards. His knees buckling his upper body falls against the edge of the hole itself and he starts to slip into it … and then stops, half in and half out, precariously balanced between light and darkness, between being the champion and the former champion] Rick: Jesus Christ … Almasy just rammed that steel chair right into the face of Oni … he nearly decapitated him! Larson: … I don’t know how Almasy did it, how he managed to move at all let alone swing that chair like that, but he has done it. He is barely conscious … barely even alive himself … but he is know only seconds away from victory and only seconds away from reclaiming the UWF World title!!! Rick: It was a great match … a brutal match … and I just hope and pray that both men have no lasting damage done to them, but all congratulations have to go to Seymour Almasy for the sheer guts he has shown by getting this far and beating Oni … [Dropping the chair to the ground and almost falling to the mat himself Almasy lurches forwards and kicks weakly at Oni’s legs, propelling the limp man further into the hole, now at least half of him over the edge. Falling down to both knees Seymour wipes the blood from his face … exposing lips that are tinged with blue … and reaches out to the waist and chest of Oni, straining tired and abused muscles … muscles that were used to lifting men high above his head … to their limit to push his opponent mere inches. … inches enough so that Oni starts to slip completely into the hole, his own body weight now conspiring against him to help Almasy in his goal. With a sob of relief Almasy leans forwards, his strength gone, as he watches Oni start to vanish into darkness] Rick: … impossible … IMPOSSIBLE! [… and then a blood stained hand reaches back and grabs Almasy by the throat as Oni stops his descent by using his erstwhile challenger as his anchor. Reaching up to his throat Almasy grabs Oni’s hand trying to loosen the hold and not only drop him into the hole once and for all, but simply allow himself to breathe. Struggling to free himself, Almasy leans backwards which actually helps pull Oni out of the hole a little more … and realising this Almasy pulls back one hand and balls his fist. His eyes tightening in concentration he aims a punch at Oni’s face. … and is stopped dead in his tracks as the face splits wide, the mouth opens and a jet of blue … tinted heavily with red blood … mist sprays up directly into his own face and wide open eyes. A gasp of shock is all that Almasy manages as he reflexively reaches up to wipe his eyes … and that is all the chance that Oni needs, as with a lunge of sheer unadultereated effort he uses Almasy’s body as the anchor he needs to pull himself out of the hole … at the same time pulling the challenger, one of the best men to ever set foot in a UWF … a PTC … a WORLD wrestling ring, backwards over his head and down … headfirst … into the hole.] Rick: Jesus God Almighty! Larson: Amen … AMEN! Rick: … what has happened … is it over? [Leaning with his upper body on the canvas and his lower body in the hole, Oni gasps for breath as he looks over his shoulder and down into the darkness … and sees nothing. With more effort that he looks capable off he pulls himself completely out of the hole and as the referee finally gets the lock of the cage door Oni kicks out at the trapdoor, slamming it shut with a resounding … and final … crash. Lying back on the canvas, arms spread eagle beside him, blood coating his whole body … some of it his, some not … as well as most of the mat, Oni doesn’t move. Even when the referee kneels gently down beside him and lays the UWF World title at his side, Oni doesn’t move. Even when the EMT’s flood the ring and start to bring out breathing apparatus and IV fluids, Oni doesn’t move. The UWF World champion … victorious in his battle … lies unmoving and unknowing.] Tommy Waters: Ladies and gentlemen ... your winner, and STIIIIIIIIIIIIIILL UWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION ... TSUUUUUUNNNNAAAAAAAAAMMMMIIIIII OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONI!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [The arena erupts in a drunken orgy of applause for the Vivaldi of Violence!] Rick: It IS over … Tsunami Oni has beaten the challenge of Seymour Almasy and retained his title in what will have to go down in the history books as a TITANIC struggle! Larson: … but … Rick: ... what? [Leaving the EMTs to check on the fallen Oni, the referee gingerly opens the trapdoor and peers down into the darkness, his face a mask of perplexity. Looking back up over his shoulders the camera catches him full in the face and the full confusion is self evident there as he slowly shakes his head] Larson: … where is Seymour Almasy?
