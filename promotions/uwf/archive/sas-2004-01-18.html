Rick: And Beezlebozo can damn well do it. If he is Gurgles, which we’re pretty sure he is…Seymour’s a dead man. Main Event~ UWF World/UWF TV Titles ~Main Event Seymour Almasy -c- vs. Beezlebozo -c- "Bizarr/Bizarre" by The Insane Clown Posse hits as the fans boo heavily for the entrance of the deranged clown, Beezle. He shoots a sick smile to the ringside fans as he makes his way down the aisle, grinning menacingly at the children at ringside. He makes the "champion" motion about his waist, letting all know what he's in this match for as he enters the ring, mounting the tunbuckle and raising his arms up in a threatening manner. Ring Announcer: FROM THE TWISTED CIRCUS....WEIGHING IN AT 298 POUNDS.....BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEZLE!!!! Beezle merrily runs about the ring, whipping out a black creme pie from his oversized pants and SLAMMING it into the ring announcers face. The fans hardly find it amusing, but Beezle just breaks into laughter...but it is broken shortly by the sounds of "Otherworld" as the fans erupt into a frenzy Rick: THEY KNOW WHO THAT MUSIC BELONGS TO...PERHAPS THE GREATEST UWF WORLD CHAMPION EVER! Larson: Will you stop riding his jock for just TWO minutes. Ring Announcer: -wipes the cream pie from his face- AND THE CHAMPION....FROM SECTOR SEVEN, WEIGHING IN AT 177 POUNDS....SEEEEEEEEEEEYMOUR ALMASAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAY!!!!! Seymour PLUMMETS to the mat, smacking off it chest first as he holds his ribs in utter pain. Larson: PUNISH HIM BEEZLE, PUNISH HIM! The evil clown uses the ropes to slowly pull himself up, Seymour peppering him with punches and kicks all the while in hopes of keeping him down longer...but that is stopped as Beezles fires back with nasty forearms and strikes to Seymour, landing him in the corner limply. Larson: YOU SEE THAT, YOU SEE THAT...Beezle is, pun intended, NO JOKE!
