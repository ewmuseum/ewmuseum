Montgomery's Farewell Match The Plague Mike Montgomery -Versus- The Plague -v- Mike Montgomery It was an emotionally charged event for Mike Montgomery ... the match where the perenial under-achiever would finally hang up his boots, and in a match against both a former stable-mate and a rival in the Plague. Coming to the ring, the Plague received a bizarre mixed reaction ... but the crowd was behind Montgomery all the way. The ex-THC Champion took five minutes to clap and shake hands with every fan within reach before finally following Plague into the ring for what would no-doubt be an epic match. With the crowd's anticipation rising by the moment, the two men approached each other in the ring ... and made a sign of good sportsmanship rarely seen on a UWF show. The Plague stepped forward, extending his hand ... and Montgomery accepted. The crowd gave a riotous response to both men for the brilliant display of affection, as Plague smiled broadly ... ... a split second before he swung Montgomery around, LEVELING him with the World Championship belt! The crowd reaction was immediate, but the Plague was going for anything but a typical match, as he hammered down onto Montgomery's body over and over and over again before his head began bleading enough to coat the part of the mat around him. The Plague didn't stop to prance around the ring ... he didn't signal to the crowd. He just brought the belt down onto Montgomery's body for four minutes straight as Corky Spectre tried desperately to pull him away. Finally, the Plague tossed the belt aside, smiled, and handed Corky Spectre a note written by Sam Gordon herself. Corky adjusted his glasses, shrugged, and called for the bell ... finally starting the match. The Plague placed one foot squarely on Mike Montgomery's chest, and Corky counted to three. Security flows down the ramps and through the crowd, holding a near-riotous crowd in check as the Plague simply picks up his belt and walks off. Winner: The Plague Mike: THE PLAGUE WINS IT!
