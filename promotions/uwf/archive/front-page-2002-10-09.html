Report by Bob the Interviewer Tonight's SAS - "Unmissable" -- September 22, 2002 The UWF will tonight air one of the most awaited shows in its year career. Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, although being an action packed card, has been promised to be the best SAS ever released on UWF television. The card, made up of a ten man elimination match, the first of four matches that all make up round one of The "Legend" Shoot Wrestling Championship tournament, Jay Phoenix and Ruff Ryder going head to head in a P-W-W battle for the ages, and Danny Jaye, the World Champion, squaring off against one of The Legends, Luke Warren in what has been pradicted as a "One sided battle". As if The Legends weren't involved enough with their tournament matches, they have also promised to unvail 'The Fan'. Who is 'The Fan'? The insane man who has put Kevin Starr in and out of a C..C..C..Coma, and a wheelchair? ..costing him The Hardcore and Shoot Wrestling Championship. Rumors are indicating a number of people, but it's guaranteed that the only men who know for sure are The Legends. SAS is unmissable.. so tune it tonight, at nine, on Primetime. Report by Kyle the Cameraman UWF Webmaster a "gibberish wreck" states UWF source -- September 8, 2002 It has been reported that the UWF Webmaster has been a gibberish wreck since the departure of a integral part of the UWF. Janitor Harvell, formerly the UWF monkey, now Webmaster, is apparently "distraught" since he took over, according to a UWF source. "Harvell's lost the f***ing plot! He said he could take over, but obviously he didnt have the power. I just wish Larry had never left. At least things got done when he was here." The UWF didn't officially release a statement, but were "understanding" of the position of the Janitor. Report by Hypocritical Skinflint Jamie Shock Found De.. sorry wait a sec, Jamie Shock Destroyed In Last Match -- August 18, 2002 Yes Ladies and gentlemen, and kiddies, Jamie Shock was beaten to a bloody pulp after he lost his Word Title Match in what has to be the BIGGEST upset of Jamie Shock's career.. even Danny Jaye couldn't believe it when Sarah counted the One, Two, Three.. the match changed sides multiple times.. Jaye was put through the ramp, Jamie had his ankle almost broken for the second time in his career, Danny Jaye powerbombed Jamie Shock into the crowd, Sarah made a return and helped Jamie Shock.. well actually she counted the three for Jaye but she came down when NO other referee would, pure bravery by the women previously called a 'tramp'. It was then afterwards when Jamie Shock was getting up, when the shadow-man who had been attacking superstars left right and center over the past weeks made his appearance, and the steel cage that had been hanging from the top of the arena, that nobody had noticed before, came down and prevented Shock's escape, and unfortunatly Sarah's too. The pair were destroyed by the shadow-man.. who later revealed himself as.. Curt Ra.. Chris Cage! Or the EWA Shooting Star Champion, Sirious. The fans were left shocked as UWF went off the air. Jamie Shock will now, sadly, have to spend his 'retirement' in hospital.. the place that he was quitting because he didn't want to be there again. Although one thing is guaranteed.. Sarah will be there to keep him company. Report by Kyle the Cameraman Internal Power Struggle Amongst Supposed UWF Staff! -- August 8, 2002 Following a Janitorial dispute after Head Janitor Mik allegedly started swapping Wrestler’s equipment around, including Gum Shields, Jock Straps and the odd Electrical Phallic Item, Janitor Mik was cruelly stripped of his Key Chain… a Key Chain that held all the keys to all the doors of the UWF and the secrets that laid behind them… such as Digimortal’s love for porno! This reprimand was the final straw in a sequence of events which has seen countless allegation made against Janitor Mik… One disgruntled Ass Bandit said: Disgruntled Ass Bandit: “Janitor Mik changed the name plate on my door… it used to say *beep*ymortal, now it says *beep*mortaility!” Gosh! If that wasn’t terrible enough, another Ass Bandit alleged. Disgruntled Ass Bandit: “Imagine my utter shock to discover on my return from a five week break, following that arduous week of effort I put in, to discover that some one has stolen my Sega Megadrive Mortal Konbat game! Umph!” We caught up to Janitor Mik sneaking quietly about outside the Bum Boozler’s Shower Block-Cum-Dressing Room. Janitor Mik: “It wasn’t me! I was framed! Framed I tells ya… how can they expect me to work without my keys huh? How? I don’t even have the key to my own room… and my typewriter is in there… which means I can’t write until I get my keys back! I wouldn’t mind, but I write to my Kids back home… it’s the kids I feel sorry for, they love my letters.” Although Janitor Mik protests his innocence, sceptics claim that the real motive behind all of this is Janitor Mik’s jealousy on the discovery that former lackey, Assistant Janitor Harvell, is to be given a massive promotion… sending him straight to the top of the UWF Corporate Ladder, ahead of Janitor Mik. We spoke with Larry to see if he could confirm these rumours. Larry: “Look! It’s true Janitor Harvell will be given a promotion very shortly, but it is a very well deserved promotion and is in no way linked to the sexual favours that he frequently performs for me! No one cleans arse better than Harvell… and when you’ve had eight bowls of bran flakes for breakfast, you need that sort of man behind you!” So, those rumours certainly seem to be the damning truth, or at least what will be in a weeks or so time. Where does this leave Janitor Mik? It’s hard to say… out in the cold I guess… his house keys were on that key chain too see. The last we heard from Janitor Mik, he tried to break into the backstage area after evil Janitor Harvell gave him a false set of keys… the key broke in the lock, and now he’s locked in the broom cupboard… he may never get out, or rather back into the UWF. Sad news all around. We at the Fanzine wish him the best in his dark confines, and hope that this will lead to the capture of the real culprit who seemingly goes on to commit these heinous crimes against Computerised Mortals.
