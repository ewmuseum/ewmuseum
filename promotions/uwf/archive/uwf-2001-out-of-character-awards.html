UWF Out of Character Awards (2001)

2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The out-of-character twin of the first UWF Annual Awards: the handlers' own year-end vote, with the UWF president's year-end address. Handlers' full names are given here as initials; bare first names and nicknames stand as written.
The UWF Out Of Character Awards: Well, guys... here it is. This is the UWF Out of Character Awards, as voted for by yourselves with regards to yourselves. First of all though, I'll just run through the UWF's history for the guys who werent here when the UWF was formed. As most of you will know, in August, the Global I*cough* (even though it is now no longer in business, the I word shall never be uttered :-)) Wrestling Federation was closed down by D.G., a man who had spent three years of his life typing up cards, pay per views and listening to most of our bitchy comments, as well as refereeing some OOC "slanging matches". With a roster of about 30 active characters (I believe) at the time of closure, it was decided by a few of the senior handlers that we should all try to carry on in some format, and I offered to open up a new fed where the GIWF's handlers could all move across to and continue to enjoy a game which has obviously seized us all. And so, the UWF was born. Although it wasn't the biggest of successes to start with, the UWF has grown in stature and in effort ever since that first card late September. We've had handlers come, and go (Ryan especially :P) but at the moment, the UWF has its biggest roster since the formation four months ago, and the last card (Christmas Capers) was the best card we've had, effortwise as well as the writing of it (even if I do say so myself!). Needless to say, the UWF is hard work to run, and if I'm honest with all of you, I didn't believe it'd last until Christmas. But, saying that, I didn't think a lot of the handlers from the GIWF would rejoin with the same effort, and with no exceptions, I can say that you've all proved me wrong. I have finally gotten into some sort of rhythm now, but it is still very, very hard work. This award ceremony is for the handlers of the UWF to show their fellow handlers their appreciation. May I make clear at this stage that these awards contain NO HIDDEN PRIZES like an extra push in the UWF, although they do carry bragging rights on the OOC board as the former GIWF handlers will be able to recall (If Ryan wins anything, he'll go on about it all year... :-)). First and foremost though, its a bit of fun and something to pass away the festive season. Winning an award is something to be proud of, but its nothing to be upset about (and definitely nothing to complain about) if you don't win an award. My personal choice will be made for most of the awards but its not a criticism or me choosing my favourite people. Its literally whom I think has deserved it MOST. But, thats enough about the UWF's history, as most of you guys have been in the GIWF at some stage, and the others will (hopefully) have grasped the concept of these awards. First up, is the award for Best Gimmick for a Wrestler. This award is one of the more "fun" based awards. Its here to let you know that everyone likes (or has liked) the way your character does what he/she/it does. You know that you're on the right path if you get nominated for this award! The nominations for this are the following: · For the handpuppet carrying character, the Fool - C.W. · For the Wizard who is Hard, WizHard - G.W. · For the apparent Bastard Son of Lew Spectre that is Corky Spectre - D.R. · And finally, for that damned Imp - M.G. My own choice for this award would have to be Dale's portrayal of Corky Spectre, because lets face it, Lew Spectre would never admit to having a child who is a geeky, spoilt brat! I must confess though, that for all of these guys, they've had me in stitches at some point over the UWF's existence, especially Luca with some of his put downs. Anyway, this is how you guys voted. · In third place with 12% of the vote - G.W.' WizHard. · In joint second place with 24% each of the vote - M.G.'s Luca and C.W.'s The Fool. · And the winner with 40% of the vote - D.R.'s Corky Spectre. Congratulations to all four guys who got nominated for this award, and special congratulations to Dale for winning the award, and I hope we can see more funny gimmicks over the next year! Next award of the ceremony is the award for Most Entertaining Roleplayer. This award is, arguably, the most important award after that of the Best Roleplayer. Basically, we all play this game for Entertainment, and if you have been nominated for this award, then you can really be proud that you've had the desired effect on everyone who is in the UWF. Nominated for this award, are: · B.J. for Ben Jericho. · M.G. for Luca (and now, Shawn Eclipse) · G.W. for his various characters, including WizHard, the Council of Superheroes and more notably, the GIWA's very own Gold E Fish. · R.H. for Baron Black Scourge and Lew Spectre. My own choice for this award is definitely a tough one, considering how I enjoy all of the roleplays from these four, but I think just for the entertainment value I've got out of all of his characters, it has to be G.W.. I've been in hysterics with the likes of Gold E Fish's idiosyncrasies, and the Council of Superheroes have made everyone (well, everyone who understood them :-P) chuckle. Thats not to say that I've not laughed at the others and been thoroughly entertained by all, but especially Mik's recent roleplay where Luca ends up at the Circus. Heres how you've all voted for this one. · In third place with 17% - R.H. for BBS and Lew Spectre. · In second place with 33% - M.G. for Luca and Eclipse. · And the winner with 39% - G.W. for WizHard, CoS and Gold E Fish. I'm sure that Dub will be pleased with that, so congratulations Gareth. The next award is going to be one for a very, very difficult one to set the rule for, as it is the Best Newcomer to the UWF award. Given that we are all Newcomers to the UWF, I decided that the best way to judge this one, would be to include everyone in the UWF right now, but have it that the person who has made the most progress since joining the UWF. Nominated are: · B.J. - Ben Jericho · Jamie Shock - Jamie/Toxic Shock · S.A./M.G. - Luca and Shawn Eclipse · M.S. - El Metralla My own choice for this, is again a very difficult one to choose, but I'm going to go with Jamie Shock. James has been consistently improving as a roleplayer, along with everyone in the fed, but I've been especially impressed by them lately, so that would be my choice, although Ben, Mik and Mark have/had adjusted well to life in the UWF. Lets see how you've all voted. · In third place with 17% - B.J. · In second place with 22% - M.S. · And the winner... with a resounding 50% of your vote - Jamie Shock Congratulations to Jamie, and commiserations to the others, but don't forget, these awards are just a bit of fun and shouldn't be taken too seriously. This next award is the Most Dedicated to the UWF. I'm really quite embarrassed as to how this one went. The reason I'm embarrassed is because I was actually nominated for this one. I didn't ever consider myself to be nominated for any of the awards, and to see the nominations come flying in, made it all worthwhile. I won't beat about the bush with this one because the word Landslide wouldn't really do justice to it. The nominees are: · M.G. - Luca and Shawn Eclipse · G.W. - GIWA and WizHard · Jamie Shock - Jamie/Toxic Shock · G.C. - Numerous guys including Max Queue, Richard Hayes, Link Anchorman and AJ Frost. My pick for this award would have to be M.G.. As I said before, I didn't consider myself for any of the awards. The reasons I'm saying Mik, would be because he is constantly thinking ideas up and every night (almost) when I check my email, I've had at least one email from Mik (normally more than one!) and even when he doesn't have a match, he is roleplaying. Anyhow, lets get this over with - heres how the voting went: · In joint third place with 5% each of the vote - M.G. and Jamie Shock · In second place with 11% of the vote - G.W. · And the winner with a whole 84% of the vote - G.C.. What can I say? It is nice to know that you all appreciate the work that I've put in, but it's still embarrassing to receive 84% of your votes. However, its not just because I'm running the UWF that it is the success it is. It's you guys who keep it going, by being dedicated, and the commitment is very, very credible. The e-group is buzzing with roleplays, and its been a very, very good few months, but your thanks are appreciated. Anyhow, lets move on to the next award now, which is the award for the Most Gracious in Defeat. This award is one which I really, really rate as one of the more important awards in this ceremony. This basically shows that, even if you are not one of the best roleplayers in the UWF, your exploits are recognised and that you are an important part of the UWF. Lets put it bluntly though, nobody likes a moaner. As the person who runs the UWF, there are only a few things that really wind me up. Out of Character messages through the e-group is one, roleplays that are above 70KB's is another, and having someone moaning to me at the result of the matches. I can say that most of you, if not all of you, have been fantastic in the last four months, but these are the guys that YOU respect the most for not moaning, and getting on with the job. · M.G. - Luca and Shawn Eclipse · L.V. - Digital Mortality · D.B. - "Sick" Rick Daniels · J.P. - Deke Aurora All of these guys, plus most of you not nominated have been model handlers and should be praised, but my choice for this is L.V. because he has been roleplaying constantly, and he's been unlucky in a lot of his matches not to have won, but hasn't moaned to me once, which I respect. As I mentioned before though, everyone in the UWF deserves praise for the way that you have all conducted yourselves. Before I reveal how the voting went, I will say that there was a tie for first place, and also for second place. · Tied for second place, with 11% each is M.G. and D.B.. · But the joint winners, with 39% each, are L.V. and J.P.. Congratulations to all four guys and indeed to all the UWF, but special congratulations to Lorenzo and James for winning the award. I was originally going to use my discretion to decide the winner, but I couldn't find anyway to seperate them, so its a joint award! The next award is one designed to reward the creative genius that all of you have inside you. Its the award for best angle of the year. One of the stronger points about the UWF, is that the matches are more often than not, part of a storyline or lead into a storyline. To me, the UWF is very similar to the "big" feds like the WWF because of these storylines, and all of the guys who have been involved in the angles nominated can be proud of being nominated. Here are the nominations: · L.B. - The White Wolf Resurrection. · M.G./B.S./B.J./J.P./Jay Faulkner/C.J. - The Creation of Team American IMPort, the rise and then just lately, the fall of Luca. · G.W./L.B./L.V. - The GIWA InVasion. · R.H./M.E./G.C. - the Return of Lew Spectre and Corporal Punishment, and the Commissioners role. This was one of the tightest awards across the board, and the voting does reflect this. My choice for this one, would have to be the return of Lew Spectre and Cpl. Punishment simply because I had more input in it than the others, but I enjoyed/am enjoying all of them greatly, especially the whole Team American IMPort angle, which came to a head at UWF: Christmas Capers. Lets see how you voted. · In joint second place, with 24% each - The White Wolf Resurrection, the GIWA InVasion and the Return of Lew Spectre. · In first place with 28% of the votes - Team American IMPort and it's IMPlications. Congratulations to all the guys who were involved in the angle, and commiserations to those who didn't win. I know how much work went into the Team A.I. angle simply because Mik was sending me up to 4 mails a day regarding it, but all the angles and handlers working on them have worked extremely hard and deserve praise. It really was a close one to call, with only FOUR percent seperating all four. The next award is the one for Best Roleplayer No Longer with the UWF. When you look at the votes here, you notice the quality of the UWF. This is an award that shows how much gratitude we owe to those who aren't still with the UWF and how much of an impression they made whilst in the UWF. Lets get straight to the nominations. · D.S. - Blade · J.D. - Silent Rage · M.S. - El Metralla · Ric H. - Jonny Storm I'm not going to say much on this one, other than all four are/were fantastic roleplayers and that I'll agree with whomever wins this because its a tough one to decide, for me. · In joint third place, with 12% each - J.D. and Ric H. · In second place, with 17% of the votes - M.S. · But the winner... with an amazing 71% of your votes - D.S.. Congratulations to Simmy, and I hope that his workload begins to shrink a bit, so that he can get back to the UWF! Well done to the other guys, who have been notified that they're up for awards and hopefully they'll all stay in contact with the UWF, although John may still be returning to the UWF. And now, its time for the penultimate award of the evening... the best current roleplayer. I'm not going to offer my opinion on this one for obvious reasons but I'm going to say that everyone nominated for this award thoroughly deserves to be nominated for this. For everyone who hasn't been nominated for this one, please stick at it! I've had a few people asking me on MSN Messenger how to improve their roleplaying, and I pointed them to the same place each time - my first roleplay in the GIWF. For those of you who have seen it, it is absolutely shocking. I can safely say that no-one in the UWF's history has roleplayed THAT badly. All it takes, is practice... I, in my opinion, improved 1000 times over the course of the 2 years I was in the GIWF (although Ryan and most others would probably disagree :P). All you need to do is practice. This award has been reduced down to three guys due to Mr Gavin's admission that he is, indeed, Shawn Eclipse. Any votes cast for Mik will not be added to Mik's total because he's been a cheating git (:P). Here are the nominations: · In joint second place with 30% each - J.P. and R.H.. · And the winner, with 40% - M.G. I can see it now - Ryan will demand that Mik's disqualified for cheating and Mik'll do a bit of gloating over Ryan seeing how Ryan beat him last year! Congratulations to all three guys but special congratulations to Mik for winning. The final award now, is the big one. Best Overall Roleplayer. There isn't a lot I can say about this award that hasn't already been said in the previous two awards, so here are the nominations: · J.D. - Silent Rage · M.G. - Shawn Eclipse and Luca · J.P. - Deke Aurora · R.H. - Baron Black Scourge and Lew Spectre Drum roll time now... The results in reverse order are... · In joint third place with 15% of the votes.... J.P. and J.D. · In second place with 30% of the votes... R.H. · And that means in first place with 40% of the votes.... M.G.. I think Ryan's probably gone to sit in his corner on his own and cry :-). Congratulations to Mik for winning this award. I've put a run down of the results under this bit, and then theres a little closer from me. Best Gimmick: 3rd: G.W.(WizHard) 2nd: C.W.(The Fool) and M.G. (Luca) 1st: D.R. (Corky Spectre) Most Entertaining Roleplayer: 3rd: R.H. 2nd: M.G. 1st: G.W. Best Newcomer: 3rd: B.J. 2nd: M.S. 1st: Jamie Shock Most Dedicated: 3rd: M.G./Jamie Shock 2nd: G.W. 1st: G.C. Most Gracious In Defeat: Joint 2nd: M.G./D.B. Joint 1st: L.V./J.P. Angle of the Year: Joint 2nd: White Wolf Resurrection, Return of Spectre and GIWA InVasion 1st: Team American IMPort and its IMPlications Best Former Roleplayer: 3rd: J.D./Ric H. 2nd: M.S. 1st: D.S. Best Current Roleplayer: Joint 2nd: J.P./R.H. 1st: M.G. Best Overall Roleplayer: 3rd: J.P./J.D. 2nd: R.H. 1st: M.G. I just want to finish off by thanking everyone, past and present for the hard work that has been put in over the past 4 months to get the UWF started, and to keep it as entertaining as it has been. Each and everyone of you has played their part but there are a few people in particular I'd like to thank. First - M.G.. Mik's been helping out pretty much from day one in the UWF, just by stopping me from jacking the job in at the beginning, and then by pretty much masterminding all of his angles so that I don't need to do any work on them. The ideas he's had have been irreplaceable, and I'd just like to thank him for his help. Secondly - R.H.. Ryan has taken a bit more of an active role in the angles he's sent in for the UWF lately (with the return of Spectre, and with the Venetian Home Rumble) and has taken a lot of the burden off my shoulders by just doing the angles instead of me writing them. Cheers Ryan. Thirdly and equally - I.B. and B.J.. Both of these guys have done commentary for me over the past few weeks, and will be doing more commentary as the year goes on, I hope. Thanks to them. A special thank you to Daz for getting me into e-wrestling in the first place, and for giving us all the pleasure of working in the GIWF. Only now, do I appreciate how much effort he added into the GIWF, and it is and forever will be - sorely missed. And finally, thank you to everyone for the effort that they've put in. It's because of you guys that the UWF is still running, and still entertaining yourselves and entertaining me! Lets hope that I'm back here saying this stuff next year too! MERRY CHRISTMAS AND A HAPPY NEW YEAR G.C. President of the UWF
