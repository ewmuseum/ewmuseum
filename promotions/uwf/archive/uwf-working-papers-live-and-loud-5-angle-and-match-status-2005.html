Working papers: the Live and Loud 5 angle and match status sheet

30 October 2005 (the show)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The status sheet kept while Live and Loud 5 was being written (one character on the card is named as on the served show page, James von Doom, and one writer credit is withheld): which angles and matches were written or still waiting and on whom, one-line angle descriptions (length and placement), and the card lineup. It is a planning paper, not a card. The served show page is the result.

Belongs to Live and Loud 5. Served whole, as written.
