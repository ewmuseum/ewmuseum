Snake Phoenix Leaves to Drink Beer. -- May 27, 2002 Due to the events that occurred at Rekindled Spirits, Snake Phoenix has decided to leave the UWF for an undisclosed amount of time. Snake did comment last night saying “I need beer and sex, with the wrestling I cannot do these”. Report by Bob the Interviewer Toenail Surgery Rocks Foundations -- May 20, 2002 Over his three years in the company, the Corporal of Punishment has proven to be the single most physically dominating mad-man in wrestling. Simply sitting back and waiting to be given instructions by whoever has employed the monstrous enforcer this month, the mindless charge of the Corporal has crushed everyone and everything that has been put in his way. Until now. The Good Corporal has become synonymous with physical decimation and torture within the confines of the UWF ... and it only makes it even more amazing that the man who has destroyed World Champions and Hall of Famers alike would finally be put out of action, not by a fellow wrestler bent on revenge, but ... by an ingrown toenail. Ever since his dominating performance against then champion Shawn Eclipse at the UWF's last Pay Per View, Nowhere to Hide, on April 17th, the Corporal has been on the injured reserve, suffering in painful silence as his left-pinkie toe was torn from one side to the other by the devastating effects of the vicious attacker. Even the mental anguish he has suffered by the knowledge that even a monster of his caliber could be defeated by such a simple ailment couldn't be compared to the pure physical torture the man-monster has lived through over the last month. Report by Bill Schwartz The Return of JD -- May 8, 2002 We have finally caught up with the Scotsman of the UWF in his home in Florida and have successfully found out the intended return of Joshua who has been absent for some time. He says that he will be returning roughly at the time of the up coming PPV. When asked about the visit from his father, however, he gave us the following answer - "He's still around, He didn't have enough money for a return flight", at the time of the interview he was apparantly applying for an American visa. More Information will be available shortly. Report by Mysterious New Commissioner appointed? -- May 4, 2002 The race for the UWF Commissioner's role is apparently very close to ending, with Richard Hayes having decided his successor to Lew Spectre. Hayes has been interviewing for a new Commissioner for a few weeks, but only in the last few days, has a position been offered. The word on the street is that Hayes was due to announce the position at the upcoming Pay Per View, Rekindled Spirits, but due to the recent assault on Jamie Shock by the Bum Boozler and Lew Spectre, the UWF President is said to be "concerned" with recent events and may even rush through the paperwork in time for Universal on the 9th May. Report by Kyle the Cameraman Digital Mortality's Whereabouts -- April 18, 2002 It has come to our attention that Digital Mortality was found in jail after violently assaulting three men in a bar near the Los Angeles area. The owner of the bar told us that he was very drunk and said that he was going to kill Draven Lox, Danny Jaye and Mike Montgomery, apparently he tought these three men were fellow wrestlers, so he attacked them unmercifully. Digital was fined for $8,000 for the assault and about $600 for drunk driving. The Chief of the LAPD said that they went easy on him since they knew that he was a big shot wrestler. Digital Mortality payed the fee and was picked up at the jail by a man called Burton Bell, Digital's reporter, he said that Digital went crazy after losing the match that he needed to win. This is all the news on Digital, we might be seeing him on TV at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy even though he was not scheduled for a match.
