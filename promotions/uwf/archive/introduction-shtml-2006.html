The UWF! ... Insert Your Own Cheesy Tagline Here The Universal Wrestling Federation was opened in 2001 to continue the legacy of the then recently dead Global Internet Wrestling Federation, which was created to continue the legacy of the Internet Wrestling Federation after that closed. The UWF, GIWF, and IWF share a legacy that as I type this has lasted more than seven years, making it one of the oldest federations of it's kind. An eight-year-old federation is bound to have some history, and the UWF is by no means an exception. One day of looking through the archives of historical feuds, matches, and events, and you will be sure to see that this is an action packed federation where there is never a dull moment ... or if there is, it's the calm before a storm ... and we don't mean Chris Storm either, or Johnny Storm, or any of the dozen wrestlers ending with storm we've had. Of course, when it comes to wrestling federations it's good to have wrestlers. We've had our fair share! Just take a look at the 'All Time Roster' that was compiled under the 'Superstars' tab on the website. If you're new to the UWF, it's most likely you've heard of some of our talent unknowingly. We credit ourselves on taking social retards or rabid chimps and turning them into champions. Seymour Almasy? He was just a gaming nerd when he came here, now look at him - he's one of the most famous superstars there is, and he's just one example. The UWF takes the wrestlers that other federations wouldn't dream of accepting, makes them into huge stars with more drawing power than Jesus, and then uses them to win all the tournaments we can, waving them in their faces. We pride ourselves in that, as some say it's what we do best. We aren't just a federation who takes social rejects though; we've been known to have the very cream of the pie. Wrestlers come from all over to fight for the prestigious championships the UWF has to offer. Only fourteen men can say they've held the UWF World Championship, and only thirty-one men can say they've held the World Championship of the UWF, GIWF, or IWF over the seven years they've been around. Wrestlers such as Jay Phoenix, Lew Spectre, and Digital Mortality, men feared throughout the wrestling world. Whether your wrestler is a heavyweight contender, or a midcard lightweight, the chance is always there for you to achieve and entertain the masses. Only few federations have titles that mean so much. You, yes you little man, could hold the same title that Corporal Punishment had over his shoulder as he shoved a cattle prod into the cranium of Mike Montgomery. YOU could have that prize. Unfortunately, the UWF isn't for everyone. Being one of the oldest federations in PTC, it's no surprise that the UWF attracts a lot of attention. Wrestlers such as Duke Williams, Angelo Deville, and Chryste, PTC legends, have all come to the UWF and have not managed to adapt. Why? - Because as we like to think, we're too good for them. Too darn good. All in all, the UWF is a place for the toughest of competition, a place for the most personal of feuds, and a place where enjoyment is the key. It is also a place where the most prestigious of championships can be won, and where an Imp in a robot can become a champion. The UWF is also a place where cheap writers with appalling English are hired, who are unable to reach a good conclusion.
