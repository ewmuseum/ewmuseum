handler: [name withheld] contact: [detail withheld] team name: The Viking Terror wrestler 1: Gunnar Karlssen wrestler 2: Gabriel Karlssen height and weight: 545 (Gunnar 5'10 185, Gabriel 6'8 360) alignment: Neutral styles: Gunnar - High Flying, Gabriel - Power division: Tag entrance music: "Vicious Circle" by Caliban hometown: Umea, Sweden titles: none appearance: Gunnar's small frame is well toned, for what he has to work with. He has dark brown hair, cut short, and a chinstrap beard. Gabriel is 360 lbs of pure muscle. He has long dark brown hair, and no facial hair. Both brothers wear royal blue tights, and royal blue boots with yellow strings and yellow soles. On the right leg of each of their tights, in yellow, reads "Svenska Stothlet". personality: Gunnar and Gabriel have tremendous pride for their family, and their country. They aren't fan favorites, but they're not the most hated people, either. They could care less what fans think. Their gimmick is their intelligence. They pound on any weakness they can find and exploit it until their opponent can't take anymore... then the real pain starts. They're both extremely smart wrestlers, and frustrate opponents into making mistakes. Their weakness is that there are some people you really don't want to make mad. history: Gunnar and Gabriel were trained by their father, legendary Swedish greco-roman wrestler Konstantin Karlssen. After learning the basics of greco-roman from their father, they took any european underground wrestling federation that they could find by storm. They have no titles of reknown, but they are extremely well known by european wrestling fans. notes: n/a. double-team finisher: The Rain of Terror its description: The opponent is reverse irish whipped into the corner. Gabriel or Gunnar lifts the opponent up and sets them up sitting on the top turnbuckle, facing out. At this point, Gabriel is tagged in if he wasn't already in. Gabriel stands on the second rope behind the opponent and executes a full nelson slam from the top rope, as Gunnar executes a springboard corkscrew frontflip legdrop from the apron onto the opponent. second double-team move: no title its description: Gabriel irish whips an opponent into the corner, then gets down on all fours a few feet from the opponent. Gunnar runs at the opponent, springboards off of Gabriel's back, and spinning heel kicks the opponent in the head. Gabriel's finisher: The Valhallabomb (Gabriel) its description: The Valhallabomb is a reverse Death Valley Driver, only executed by Gabriel. Gunnar's finisher: The Fall of Hardrada (Gunnar) its description: The Fall of Hardrada is a reverse DDT used on an opponent when both Gunnar and his opponent are on the outside apron. He will use it anywhere, but the apron is a preference. move: Spinebuster (Gabriel) move: Short-arm Clothesline (Gabriel) move: Insiguri (Gunnar) move: Hurracanrana (Gunnar) move: Double Underhook DDT (either)
