A spoof Universal Promos index: 175 rows, every one SKuLL, September 2004

September 2004 (file dated 12 September 2004)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A mock "Universal Promos" index page from the roleplay board's own markup: 175 rows, every one posted by the handle SKuLL, each with the same thumbnail, an embedded video and a blank date and location; the ids run 151 down to 1 (there is no 6), then 722 down to 698. The last two rows hold a wall of abuse, the sentence "Fuck you all!!!!" repeated several thousand times, in place of their fields. Ids 44 to 47, 101, 143, 144 and 698 to 722 belong to real board posts that are served as such, so these rows are not a lost run of posts: it is a spoof or defaced index page, a record of an incident on the board in September 2004 (the file is dated 12 September 2004). The fan-club satire of the same week mentions a poster of this handle being fired; this page does not say whether the two are connected. The embedded videos are off-site files and are not held here.

Belongs to the roleplay board's Universal Promos index; see also the fan-club satire of the same week. Served whole: the rows as a table, the wall in full below it.
