W R E S T L E R P R O F I L E Date Of Birth: October 13, 1984 Height: 5'11' Weight: 140 lbs Alignment: Face Theme Music: 'The Red' by Chevelle Home Town: Norwalk, CT T I T L E H I S T O R Y UWF Title History: UWF Awards History: Other Titles: Other Awards: B I O G R A P H Y Growing up on the streets is always hard, and that's exactly what the young IL did. He was abandoned as a child with no collection of who he was or who his parents were, he made a dark alleyway his home for his childhood. He lived in a cardboard box next to drunken bums in an area which cops always kept under close watch, there was gang warfare at every turn on this turf. One day would live with him the rest of his life and it would change his view of life for a long time to come... Awakened by the calls of 'fight', Lung was oh so curious as to what was going on. Lung walked playfully to this large circle with children and what he saw shocked him, a fight was going on between two children and one was obviously getting beat to a bloddy pulp. The person who beat the other child laughed and scared off the rest of the children, but Lung stayed put not knowing what to do. The child turned out to be the neighborhood bully Johnny Rico, he shoved Lung and laughed while Lung stayed put. Rico shoved Lung to the ground and thats when the children came back out and noticed that Lung had snapped, Lung started fighting Rico back and then kneed Rico in the gut giving him a hard slam onto the cement knocking Rico out cold. The children came out and cheered, Rico had been defeated and needed paramedics to put him on an 'Iron Lung' so he could get back to breathing normally, that's how Iron Lung got his name... Over the years Lung became an awesome fighter, he competed in neighborhood competitions and eventually one the Neighborhood Fight Championship. After nine years of neighborhood fights, Lung decided to leave Norwalk and persue a real wrestling career. At the young age of eleven he started to train in the Lion's Den Wrestling Dojo under the supervision of Master Jade, the grown men all laughed at this youngster they were all in their teens. Lung was determined to prove them all wrong and at the end of his two years training there was a tournament which Lung entered hoping to become the Dojo Champion. Lung defeated all oncomers and won the Championship, Jade told him he had learned everything he could at the Dojo and now it was time to persue a real wrestling career.
