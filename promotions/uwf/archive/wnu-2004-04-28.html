Wednesday Night Universal — 28 Apr 2004

28 Apr 2004

[Supremacy..] Mike struggles to get to his feet.  Beelzebozo charges forward, but Montgomery ducks.  He grabs Beelzebozo as though setting up for the Hangman’s Joke, but instead runs up the ropes, flips over Beelzebozo, and plants him with a mid-air Tornado DDT! Rick: Burninator!! Where the hell did that come from! With Beelzebozo in the middle of the ring, not moving, Mike drags himself to the corner.  He climbs to the top turnbuckle and leaps off, driving his elbow into the sternum of the psychotic clown. Larson: NO!! Rick: The Executive Decision!!  It’s gotta be over! Larson: Dammit no!  Not like this!  Digi!  Moraless!!  Ruff Ryder!! SOMEBODY STOP THIS!! Despite Larsen’s pleas, nobody heads to the ring.  Instead the referee slides down to the mat.  He slaps the mat once…twice…three times!! The arena explodes as the referee pulls Montgomery to his feet and raises his arm in victory. Rick: HE DID IT!!  I don’t care what other people may say, but you can’t deny that Montgomery earned the victory here tonight! Mike takes the World title from the referee, clasping it in both hands and staring at it in absolute shock.  He hugs the belt to his chest, tears streaming down his face.  Mike’s legs are seen to buckle and he drops down to his knees, still clutching the belt with all his remaining strength. And don't you wish you were me? The King of all you see Don't you wish you were me? It ain't that easy Don't think you'll ever be, Everything a man should be Cause Don't you wish you were me? Keep dreamin', you'll never be me Rick: My God I don’t believe it!  Mike Montgomery…after five long years…has made his way to the top of the mountain…defeated the man who beat the best champion in history…and won the World…Heavyweight…Title! Mike lays the belt out in front of him and begins bowing to it, as though it were some sort of idol.  He finally finds the strength to get back to his feet.  Picking up the belt, he climbs the turnbuckle and raises it high in the air to a thunderous ovation. [UWF Arena, Chicago IL.] Rick: HELLO AND WELCOME TO WEDNESDAY NIGHT UNDERGROUND! Larson: WNU IS BACK! Rick: And we have a NEW WORLD  HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! You just saw the clip folks, and no joke… Mike Montgomery is the World Champion of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Larson: I still don’t think its real. Its like when Ruff Ryder won the belt, or when Bruce Gordy had it. Somethings gotta give and its going to give tonight. Rick: I really don’t think so… Larson: Fawk you.. Rick: Right-o! Let’s open this bad boy up! Larson: The opening match… Loverboy against The Communist. Rick: Both competitors are quite new in the UWF, actually this is The Communist’s debut. Larson: Loverboy did well in that T.V. title battle royal on SAS… but he’s still got a lot of work to do. Rick: We’re not reallysure what The Commie can do, I guess we’ll just have to find out right here. Larson: Any know ties to the Local NVA Company? Rick: I wouldn’t doubt it… They must be connected to The Communist somehow, I mean… it just makes sense. Larson: Then again, this is UWF, where things seldomly make sense. Rick: Right! To the match! Television Title Match Loverboy The Communist -Versus- [The match began with the two competitors staring each other down and trying to get a feel for the other guy. Loverboy was totally dwarfed by the enormous Communist, who stood at 7 feet and weighed 380. Loverboy attacked with some fast moving combos of kicks, but to no effect. The Communist just strugged them off, then grabbed Loverboy’s head like a grapefruit and litterally tossed him across the ring. The ragnarok Russian lumbered over to his prey and planted his gigantic foot onto the chest of Loverboy. Loverboy screamed out in pain as nearly 400 pounds came down on his body. The Commie then scooped up the broken figure of Loverboy and executed The Communist Slam, his finishing maneuver. From there, The Communist simply put his foot once again on Loverboy chest, but this time for the 1, 2, 3.] [The Winner: The Communist.] Rick: Aaaaaaand The Communist takes the win in his debut match! Larson: The NVA HAVE to be behind this guy… there’s no way they aren’t. Rick: Honestly, I don’t think so, but maybe a partnership will be made for Stable Wars? Larson: Perhaps… perhaps. Rick: Well, its time for our second match of the night. Larson: It’s the return of the Television Championship. Raid won a battle royal at SAS, winning the nwely returned belt. Raid has all the momentum coming into this match coming off of his big win. Rick: PhD is a very talented rookie, but I think you’re right, that momentum might be too much for the youngster to handle. Larson: Let’s just see… Television Title Match Raid © PhD -Versus- [The match began with Adien Pryde and Reo Dai with JoAnna chilling down at ringside. Raid and PhD squared each other up. PhD seemed somewhat nervous and unconfident. Raid locked up and executed a series of snap mares. PhD would easily recover, and then fired back with a series of hip tosses. A little crowd candy, showing that the two big men did have some speed, but they wanted some high impact. Well, they didn’t have to wait too long. After circling each other, and then some random brawling, PhD went for a charging spear to the mid-section of Raid, but Raid caught him in mid charge and powerbombed to hell. Raid then went on to dominate the match. Raid showed off some of his rarely used aerial moves, dropping a flying elbow on the prone PhD. Dawson’s offense was basically non-existant, save one point where he almost started a come back, conencting with a forearm, and following with a right hand. His ensuing running clothesline was ducked however, and Raid connected with an awesome spinebuster that spent an echo shooting through the entire arena. Raid would hit a few more simple brawling maneuvers until he finally set up the Bug Bomb and covered PhD for the 3 count.] [The Winner: Raid.] Rick: RAID RETAINS! Larson: Another amtch basiclly dominated. Rick: Yeah but… RAID RETAINS! Larson: I noticed. I guess this proves that his win was no fluke on SAS. Raid moves on along with his T.V. title. That title is probably one of the hardest titles to hold, and history shows us this. Defending it on each and every card can really drain a guy. Rick: I think Raid is up to the challenge, though, but I guess we’ll just have to see at SAS. Larson: Aye… Rick: Well here we are, a good way through our new format of WNU and will be getting onto the next match of tonight, maybe… [The lights of the arena go out and “Digimortal” by Fear Factory comes over the speakers enticing a huge round of boo’s from the crowd] Larson: Or maybe not. [The lights come up and standing, quite predictably at the head of the stage is Digital Mortality.  Microphone in hand. He doesn’t wait for the crowd to stop booing; he just launches himself into a rant] Digi: I don’t have time for this crap; I’m just out here to let this out in the open, and let it fester for a while.  I have a busy schedule you know. Rick: Good, maybe we’ll get to a match some time. [Digital starts pacing back and forth on the stage] Digi: I have one thing to say, and it’s concerning the Tag match on SAS where I am now sent out against the Players Club, the people we just faced to win the Tag Titles, only this time with Bondage, and now I have a little something to say about that. [The crowd start booing again] Digi: SHUT UP! I’m not going to be cancelling the match however, I’m not letting the Players Club say Resurrection are scared of them after the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. [He rolls his shoulder at the mention of the match.] Digi: It’s just to say that we’re making things a little interesting, and there is going to be a special Guest Referee at the match up, and it is to be announced on SAS just before our match.  And until then No-one will know who it is. [Digi throws the microphone away and sweeps back through the backstage curtain] Larson: Well that was brief… Rick: I guess the tag team match at SAS will have a special guest referee! Can’t wait to see who it is. Larson: We’ve got more than a month before Stable Wars and things are already red hot… Actually, there’s no way we’re going to be able to keep the heat for this long… the Pay per View is doomed. Rick: BAH! Nonsense, this could very possibly be the best Stable Wars EVER! Larson: The 6th time around is a charm, I supposed. Rick: Stable Wars VI… who would have thunk it? Larson: Now I KNOW that ‘thunk’ is NOT a word… Rick: Well, with everything going down… It can get a little confusing. So, let’s make a little run through of the current Stables. Larson: Good idea. Rick: First of all, there’s the Player’s Club, run by the newly crowned World Champion, Mike Montgomery. Backing up Monty is ‘The Common Man’ Ezra Kazee and Joshua Dunn. In my humble opinion, both Dunn and Kazee are World Title contenders. This is a VERY powerful trio and are going to be hard to beat at any rate. The Plague has been seen with Montgomery in past weeks, but its not sure if he’s an actual member of the Player’s Club or not. Larson: Then there’s The Resurrection. The past, the present, and the future of the UWF. That’s Digital Mortality, Moraless, and Bondage the Sec Fiend. Digital Morality is a hall of famer and there’s no arguing what he can do in the ring. Then there’s Mory and Bondy. Each of these guys have a lot of talent, and have risen up the ranks in the UWF in a very short time. Digimon and Moraless are the current tag champs. It should be interestingt o see who they pick as their 4th man. Rick: Next is the Chaos Society. This team is made up of the Anarchy tag team of Adien Pryde and Reo Dai together with their long time friend Raid. They basically attack everyone and anyone, because… well… they’re anarchists! Pryde has been having issues with THC champ Nick Persia and Raid ha shad his with Ezra Kazee. I think this group, like the other two, really need that 4th man to flesh out there stable before any real predictions can be made. That 4th member is like an x-factor. It can make or break a stable. Larson: Last but certainly not least is The Prime Alliance. Hell yes, they are still around, even a whole full year since last year. The ranks only consist of Ruff Ryder and Malcolm Kennedy, and they really haven’t impacted as much on the scene after being beaten down by the Player’s Club a few weeks back. These guys ARE MORE THAN CAPABLE. Ruff Ryder is a former world champ, Kennedy is a multi-time T.V. champ. All these guys need is another member or two, and they will start kicking ass, trust me on this one… all they need is a quality wrestler or two and they will dominate. Rick: Looking over the roster, I realize that a majority of it isn’t even aligned with a stable as of yet, and I’ve also heard rumors of free agents being signed and of some un-namable superstars that maybe perhaps returning to the UWF squared circle. The balance of power could easily tip if one of these stables picks up a neutral wrestler or a free agent or even an old friend from the past. Larson: That or new stables can be formed that can just kill all the others. PEOPLE NEED TO GET IN A STABLE *COUGH**COUGH* Rick: Right. Stable Wars 6 is still a long ways off, and we’ll just have to see what goes down! Larson: Right! Now to the Main Event! Right to Party against Lustful Lullaby! Main Event Tag Team Match Right to Party Lustful Lullaby -Versus- [The match began… and ended as The Local NVA Company jumped the crowd and threw the WHITE POWDER OF UNSPEAKABLE HORRORS AND SUFFERING in the eyes of all four competitors. The referee called for a no-contest, just before  he was stabbed in the temporal with a plastic fork.] Thai Trang: Hit it! [Cue ‘Fresh Prince of Bel Air’ theme music.] Duc Chu Song: … … Duc Chu Song: PSYCH! REAL Vietnamese Communist don’t parody stupid American rap songs! DIE! VIVA LA RESTITANCE! Thai Trang: HA! PSYCH! The mind games begin! Duc Chu Song: Eeeehehehehe~!! NVA VANISH! [Duc throws down a small white ball, which hits the ring canvass and explodes into a cloud of white powder. When it clears, the devilish duo is gone without a trace.] Rick: Er… right… Larson: Well… that was pointless! Rick: AND THAT’S UNDERGROUND FOR YA! GOODNIGHT WE LOVE YOU ALL! Larson: You sound like Ozzy at the end of ‘Paranoid’… Rick: Don’t ask me, I don’t know! Larson: Stop that! Rick: No more tears… I just want you… bark at the moon. Larson: Now you just aren’t making sense… Rick: Over the Mount-- [Feed Cut.]
