[The Plague, still pounding away at Adien Pryde, fails to see Tsunami Oni rolling into the ring.] Rick: Never put your back to that man! Larson: Here comes ONI! [Tsunami Oni jumps onto the second rope, before flipping back in an asai moonsault, bringing both feet down onto The Plague's head. The fans cheer as Tsunami Oni connects with the Diablosault.] Rick: What a moonsault! [The Plague crawls into the corner, but Tsunami Oni beats him to it. Oni jumps onto the second rope, then flips back, over The Plague, wrapping his arm around The Plague's neck as he does so, bringing The Plague to the mat with a reverse ddt.] Larson: Flipping heck! Rick: A reverse ddt! That could be it.. One.. Two.. [The Plague kicks out. Tsunami Oni pokes The Plague in the left eye, before getting to his feet.] Larson: The Plague meeting fierce opposition from the Contest Of Champions, the Regal Rumble, and World Title winner. Rick: Quite an impressive list of awards there. [Oni grabs The Plague, hits an uppercut, and then delivers a kick into the gut. The Plague bends down with pain. Tsunami executes the Deep Blue Bomb on The Plague.] Larson: Oni with his trademark Crossed-arm shoulder-mounted face-first powerbomb~! Rick: This could be it ... and I mean it this time! [Tsunami Oni covers The Plague.] One.. Two.. Larson: Nope! Adien is up! [Adien Pryde intercepts the count. The fans give a small jeer.] Rick: And Pryde stops the count! [Pryde pulls Tsunami up, but gets an elbow into the face for his troubles. Adien stumbles backwards into the ropes. Tsunami charges at Adien, but Pryde lowers the ropes, and himself!] [Tsunami Oni flies over the top rope.] Larson: Adien Pryde just caused Oni to fly to the outside! [Adien Pryde looks up at The Plague, motionless in the middle of the ring.] Rick: Pryde! He could take this! Larson: All he has to do is cover The Plague! [Pryde tries to walk towards The Plague. His legs almost turning to jelly as he realises a realistic chance of victory is in his reach. He slumps forward onto The Plague.] Rick: ADIEN PRYDE COULD PICK UP A HUGE WIN! [The referee begins the count.] One.. Two.. [Adien Pryde turns to the referee, who isnt there. Instead he's on the outside of the ring flat on his face, since Tsunami pulled him out of the ring.] Rick: ... HE ALMOST HAD IT! Larson: I thought he did for a moment! [Pryde gets to his feet, his hands over his face.] Rick: You can see the devastation on that young mans face. What a win he almost picked up. Larson: Unfortunatly he's forgot the all important rule.. [The Plague quickly gets up.] Rick: NEVER TURN YOUR BACK ON AN OPPONENT! [Adien Pryde is attacked from behind. His arms are then locked with The Plagues, and THE BLACK DEATH is executed!] Larson: BLACK DEATH! Rick: The Plague has hit The Black Death! [Tsunami Oni rolls into the ring.] Larson: But here comes Oni! [Tsunami Oni brings a well aimed foot down onto the back of the head of The Plague. The Plague goes to his knees. Tsunami Oni slips something into his mouth. The fans know whats coming.] Rick: He's LOADED! [The Plague gets to his feet, using the ropes to stand. He slowly turns around..] [EVIL BLUE MIST~!] Larson: THE EVIL BLUE MIST~! HE'S GOT IT! [Tsunami Oni grabs The Plague by the (now blue) hair and throws him over the top rope. Oni then turns to Adien Pryde and circles his motionless body.] Rick: The Plague is eliminated from the equation, and it seems X = ONI! Larson: It certa.. WHAT?! [Oni grabs Adien Pryde under both arms and hoists him to his feet. Adien Pryde stumbles back into the ropes.] Rick: Adien Pryde is OUT OF IT. Larson: Tsunami Oni looking to finish the match here.. [Oni approaches Adien Pryde, but is suprised as Adien Pryde staggers towards him, fist raised into the air. The crowd look as Adien Pryde gives Oni ONE ... LAST ... OFFENCE.] Rick: ADIEN PRYDE IS STILL GOING! Larson: HOW?! [Tsunami Oni ducks the shot, spinning Adien around as he does so.] Rick: Is it.. [Oni executes the Single Perfect Orchid Driver on Adien Pryde.] Rick: .. it is! It's over! Larson: Good god.. Adien Pryde giving it one last shot despite everything when we all though he was.. Rick: Dead? Larson: Exactly. [Tsunami Oni covers Adien Pryde.] One.. Two.. [The Plague pulls the referee out the ring.] Rick: The Plague is saving the match! Larson: Oni did exactly the same earlier in the contest! The Plague is taking a trick out of Oni's book! [Tsunami Oni looks rounds. The referee and The Plague are argueing outside.] Rick: Oni had the victory! Larson: Tsunami now knows how it feels to be on the other end of that dirty trick! [The Plague slides into the ring and runs at Tsunami Oni. The Plague knocks Oni down with a clothesline. The Plague grabs Tsunami Oni and forces him into the corner. Oni grabs The Plague by the neck and forces him into the corner instead.] Rick: Counter by Oni! [Tsunami lifts The Plague onto the top turnbuckle.] Larson: I sense a high risk move folks! [The Plague and Oni exchange punches, but Tsunami wins the exchanges with a rake into the eye. Tsunami Oni climbs onto the top turnbuckle and wraps his arm around The Plague's head.] Rick: Tsunamu Oni going to either suplex or ddt him! [Tsunami Oni hooks both arms.] Larson: He's going for that double arm ddt thing! Rick: The Downfall Of The Western Civilization! - apart from instead of the ring apron he's going off the TOP ROPE! [The fans cheer as Tsunami executes the double arm ddt off the top rope. The Plague's head bounces off the mat. As Tsunami no longer has the arms hooked, The Plague bounces forward, layed out in a star.] Larson: Tsunami Oni just took some damage himself! Rick: - but The Plague was knocked into next week! It payed off! [The fans give a mixed reaction as Mike Montgomery runs down the ramp.] Larson: There's Mike Montgomery! Rick: The last time we saw him, he tried to give Sean McClancy the World Title.. but Sean refused. Larson: The dumb irish bastard! [Mike Montgomery stops at ringside.] Rick: Montgomery not venturing into the ring, hopefully he wont interupt this match. [Adien Pryde slowly sits up. He slowly gets to his feet, using the ropes to do so. He staggers towards The Plague, still motionless after The Downfall Of The Western Civilization.] Larson: Pryde is back up! Rick: What a night it has been for this young man! [Adien Pryde grabs The Plague's right foot and pulls him away from Tsunami Oni, previously an inch or two away from him, and into the opposite corner of the ring.] Larson: Pryde is moving The Plague away from Oni?! [Pryde covers The Plague.] Rick: He's going for the pin! - and he wants to make sure Oni cant interfere! [Tsunami Oni slowly sits up as the referee falls to the mat. Tsunami Oni rolls onto his chest, ready to move forward and stop the count. Adien Pryde hooks The Plague's leg.] One.. Larson: Adien Pryde could get the win! The Plague is motionless! [Tsunami Oni goes to crawl forward, but Mike Montgomery reaches under the bottom rope and grabs onto his boot.] Two.. Rick: Montgomery is preventing Oni from breaking the pin! [Oni kicks Montgomery away and dives forward..] THREE!! [..but is too late.] The Winner Is Adien Pryde! Rick: ADIEN PRYDE! ADIEN PRYDE HAS WON! Larson: Mike Montgomery prevented Tsunami Oni from getting to the pin! [Mike Montgomery backs up the ramp smiling.] Rick: For the whole match Tsunami Oni, although being the focus of both men, survived to the very end, only to be screwed! Larson: And oh boy does he looked pissed off! Tsunami Oni looks at him, snarling, as we go off the air.] [End Transmission.]
