Sunday Afternoon Supremacy results index, 2003

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Sunday Afternoon Supremacy archive index for 2003 on the UWF’s own site (cards/sas2003.htm; the page’s title bar reads “[UWF] Sunday Afternoon Supremacy -- 2003 Archives”), as the Wayback Machine captured it on 3 February 2004: the table of the year’s shows, each with its date (or the pay-per-view’s name) and its main event, a (c) marking the champion. The page laid each row out in two mirrored halves, two shows a row: date and main event on the left, main event and date on the right, the left-hand show the earlier of the two. Every line here is one row of the table and keeps the page’s own order of cells, separated by bars; where the page printed no main event the cell is left empty, as on the page. Every date and pay-per-view name but 7 (June 15th, 2003; June 29th, 2003; July 6th, 2003; August 3rd, 2003; September 21st, 2003; Live & Loud 3 PPV; November 11th, 2003) was a link to that show’s card page; the links, the link to the other years’ indexes, the navigation and the background are not carried. The same page is in the promoter’s own drive copy of the site, with the same table.
