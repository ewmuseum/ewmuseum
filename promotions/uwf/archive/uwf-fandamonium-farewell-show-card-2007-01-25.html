UWF Farewell Show: Live&Loud: FANdamonium

25 Jan 2007 (the announcement; the show was set for 28 Feb 2007)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An announcement posted by the UWF's owner, Jaymes Faulkner, on the RPCommunity forum (announcement thread 3141) and e-mailed to the forum's members; this is the e-mail copy, from the Janitor's own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The mail's automatic unsubscribe footer is not carried; the sender's address is marked. It carries the farewell show's card as announced and its rules and deadlines; it is a card announcement, not a result.

Follows the proposed card of 4 January 2007. Served whole, as written.
