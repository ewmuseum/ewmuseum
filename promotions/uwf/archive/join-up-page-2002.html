The join-up page — the fed's own application form

9 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks for visiting the UWF! Your comments are appreciated! Just complete this form. Click on Submit when ready to send. PLEASE NOTE - AS OF 3rd February 2002, the UWF is not accepting any more singles applications. You may send one in, but you will be placed on the waiting list. Tag Teams can still be applied for. Joining the UWF is pretty straight forward. Follow the instructions below and you can't go wrong. Before even attempting to join you MUST read the General Information and rules section of the site. The first stage is to complete the form below and press the submit button. Once the form has been sent a link will tell you to proceed to STAGE TWO of the joining process. It is absolutely VITAL that you follow this link and complete stage two. Without it your application will be rejected. The form is divided into three sections. Section one is your wrestlers basic details, it is the only section of the form that must be completed. Section two deals with selecting your wrestlers moves or alternatively copying the moves of an existing wrestler. Section three is additional information. Section 1 Basic Details Your name: Email address: Wrestlers Name : Nickname: Hometown: Height: Weight: Face/Heel/Neutral: Finisher Name: Finisher description: Manager (If appropriate): Will manager interfere ?: You have 125 points to distribute between 'Work Rate' and 'Stamina' of Your Wrestler; how will you distribute these ? Work Rate : Stamina : Is he part of a tag team ? If so:- With who: What is the Team Name: What is the Team Finisher Called: Please complete another application form for your tag-partner. Section 2 Your wrestler can have between 11 and 50 moves. List below as many as you would like to have. Please give details of what style your wrestler has, is he a powerhouse, a shooter etc ... Alternatively please complete Section 3. Section 3 If, instead of going through the hassle of filling out Section 2 you would sooner use a default moves list and style then please specify whose moves and style you would like to copy. Remember, that if someone else has already chosen that wrestler your list will be amended to avoid duplication. If you actually want to specify someone with your own amendments, ie- a 'Bret Hart' type character who uses the Spear, the Jack-knife and Spinning Heel Kick then feel free to do so. Wrestlers Name: Any Amendments : Personality Traits In this section please provide as much information as you can about your wrestler and how he will behave in the ring, please remember that this is not the place to give a bio or give me your wrestlers life history, a bio form ought to be used for that. Will he sit-up like the Undertaker; No sell move like Goldberg; Is he so cocky he'd miss a pinning chance to work the crowd; Will he juice; Is he hardcore; Will he use tables; Is he like Mankind and will never quit or is he just very difficult to make quit. If your wrestler is the sort of person who works the crowd, how will he do it? Roleplay If you could just produce a sample roleplay, it'll speed up the process of applications. Once you have completed the form I will send you a mail within 48hours letting you know whether or not you are accepted. If you are you will receive a mail giving you instructions on how to begin your UWF career and giving you a copy of the current mailing list. Thanks for spending the time to complete the form. Read the Bottom paragraph before submitting the form.
