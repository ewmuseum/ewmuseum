An angle from September 2004: the Phenomenons in the shower room

September 2004 (file dated 23 September 2004)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short backstage script for the build to Live and Loud 4: the Phenomenons, carrying the stolen World Tag Team Title belts and the Local NVA Company's challenge to annoy every tag team, raid the shower room to steal the Legends' clothes on the theory that clothes trump masks. The file is dated 23 September 2004.

Belongs to the build to Live and Loud 4 (15 October 2004). No show of its own. Served whole, as written.

“Rod damn NVA think they’re soooooo smart.” [Deep within the bowels of the UWF Arena, Eric Cameron looks thoroughly bored … Shane Stone appears to be obsessed with a desire to show up the communist whores at every opportunity, however. One of the stolen World Tag Team Title belts is wrapped around his head like a bandana, the other looped around one broad fist.] Eric: “This seems like a really bad plan, Shane.” Shane: “Nonsense.” Eric: “Then why didn’t you ask Vaughn?” Shane: “Richie’s cramping out style, man! We’re the Phenomenons. Since when did we need him?” Eric: “Since we went … like … YEARS without a tag title reign, stupid.” Shane: “… shut up.” [Stone waves his partner off, slowly marching towards a door marked with a small stick figure, apparently trapped within a slightly larger blue box.] Eric: “I’m telling you, Shane … this isn’t a good plan. At all.” Shane: “When did you get so whiney? This is our chance! The NVA can’t win the challenge to piss everyone off … that’s like our thing! What we’re best at! Pissing people off!” Eric: “We do it well, true.” Shane: “Then let’s go.” [Shane reaches for the door … then looks back at Eric with apprehension dancing in his eyes.] Shane: “You … you go first, alright? I … have bad memories.” Eric: “Oh for Rod sakes … you’ve got to get over this fear of the Boozler.” Shane: “He could be anywhere …” [Blowing his partner off, Eric marches into the shower room, only to be greeted with a horribly off-key singing of some random show tune. Shane cringes again, but pushes on.] Eric: “And?” Shane: “Shhh! Grab their clothes … there’s no WAY masks can trump clothes!” [Eric rolls his eyes, grabs the clump of clothing on the floor – holding them as far from his body as he can – and wanders back to his partner.] Eric: “This isn’t really very exciting.” Shane: “Wait … let’s do like they did. AARIBA~!!” [Eric smacks his partner across the back of the head.] Eric: “What in the hell did you do that for!?!?” [Before Shane can answer his fellow Phenomenon, THEY emerge~!] [Lance Rose and Luke Warren, obviously confused, and completely naked.] Shane: “Alright … bad plan.” Eric: “Very bad plan.” [Lance turns to Luke, grinning.] Lance: “Hey! Now this is interesting.” Luke: “Aye. How long’s it been since we wrestled naked?” Lance: “We haven’t … oh. I guess there was that time at the …” [Shane shrieks.] Shane: “Put them on put them on put them on put them on put them on!!!!!!!!!” [Horrified, Eric Cameron flings the clothes at the Legends, and the Phenomenons run.] [Well away from the bathroom, Eric kicks Shane in the shin as hard as he can.] Eric: “IDIOT!” Shane: “Alright … masks trump clothes. This contest sucks.” [The Phenomenons wander away.]
