UWF Announcement: the closing of the UWF

28 Sep 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An announcement posted by the UWF's owner, Jaymes Faulkner, on the RPCommunity forum (announcement thread 3105) and e-mailed to the forum's members; this is the e-mail copy, from the Janitor's own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The mail's automatic unsubscribe footer is not carried; the sender's address is marked. It announces that the UWF will close its doors in December 2006 after a last pay-per-view, Live & Loud: FANdamonium.

See also the farewell show card. Served whole, as written.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: RPCommunity Forums [detail withheld] Date: Thu, Sep 28, 2006 at 4:06 AM Subject: New announcement: UWF ANNOUNCEMENT Ok guys and gals the time has finally come to make the announcement that I am pretty sure a lot of you knew was coming sooner or later (and for some of us it is quite a few years later than expected ) The UWF is going to be closing its doors - December 2006 to be more specific. Between now and then we willbe tidying up and preparing for the 'farewell' show. Many factors have led to this decision, among them being that the people behind the scenes all have been hit by real life time constraints that mean that we cannot give 100% to the fed and while it may not always seem like it – outsiders view us, for example, as a ‘comedy’ fed with little effort required – anything less than 100% just isn’t enough. Over the last year or so there have been too many late cards and too many changes to really recover from. A clean sweep and reboot of the fed ‘may’ work but also it may just prolong the inevitable as well. We would rather go out in a blaze of glory than fade away and get worse and worse. So, to that end, there will be one last hurrah for the UWF, a PPV extravaganza called Live & Loud – FANdamonium~! It will be a card where dream matches, grudge matches and tribute matches take place to showcase the great talent that the UWF has – and has always had! I don’t know what the card line-up is yet – you guys will be helping to decide that – and I don’t know when it will be. I do know that it will be very soon (in UWF time that means before Christmas! ) though and between now and then the staff will be putting together special ‘nostalgia’ shows that highlight things that have gone before, so watch out for them. The UWF has been – and will always be – so much more than just another fed, it is a family and that is the single most important factor in the hobby. Some of us have been ‘together’ in this family for close to a decade and just because the fed itself won’t exist we will still be able to have that legacy. I talked to the XUW … a fed that grew up out of the GIWF just as the UWF did … fed heads currently and hope that all of you guys that want to keep efedding will chose to jump ship to them and keep some of our family, and all of our legacy, going! Another fed that we have close links with is the GCW (one of their fed-head type people - RJ - was in the GIWF, PWW and 4WW). WWA is another fed that I would say is close to us. At the end of the day if you post on the PTC Free Agents forum I will ensure that the PTC fed-heads know about you :) … and each year, there ‘may’ just be a little get gathering in the form of a special super card that brings us together again I hope that you have all enjoyed whatever time you have spent in the UWF and I thank you from the bottom of my heart for making this place a pleasure to run and be part of! Jay You can view the full announcement by following this link: http://forum.rpcommunity.com/index.php?topic=3105.0
