UWF Rules: general information, 2004 edition

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The general information page of the UWF’s own site (general.htm), headed “Rules” on the page, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 12 June 2004: the sections on the Awards Ceremony, the Hall of Fame, links, I.M. Productions, Primetime Central and Poser. It is a later edition than the rules pages of 2002 and 2003 already on this wing; the same page is in the promoter’s own drive copy of the site, with the same text. A credit that names a person reads the first name and last initial. The page itself carries no date.
R U L E S We don't have too many rules here in the UWF, as we have always believed in keeping things as light-hearted as humanly possible. It is however essential that you read and accept them before even attempting to join. The rules are intended to ensure the smooth running of the fed and should also give you all the extra information you need. A W A R D S C E R E M O N Y The Awards Ceremony takes place annually, and is held after the December PPV during the Christmas break. All award categories will be announced early in December, and from there each Handler will be encouraged to send in their nominations. The top four nominees in every category, with a possible direct addition from the owner, will be put in direct contention for each award. Later in the month, a series of polls will be made The only thing to be gained by winning the Awards Ceremony is prestige and the respect of your peers, winning an award will not in any way affect your push within the federation. H A L L O F F A M E In previous years, the Hall of Fame inductions have always been held in conjunction with the annual Awards Ceremony. Currently, the Hall of Fame voting will be opened in September as part of the festivities for the UWF's Anniversary. The owner will open the floor for nominations from all handlers, and the characters who receive the most nominations, most likely along with one or two additions from the staff, will be put in contention for the illustrious honor. After the nominations have all been collected, the inductions will be made using a series of polls in mid-to-late-September, and the two singles wrestlers and one tag team that are inducteed will be announced at the annual Live and Loud PPV. Like the Awards Ceremony, there won't be any extra "pushes" given to the winners ... the only award comes from lifelong enshrinement in the UWF's Hall of Fame, and undying respect. Because we force them to. L I N K S If you have your own website and wish to have a link included simply mail me the HTML for your banner and the link will be added within a week. There is no vetting procedure for any of the links and the UWF makes no representations about the standard or content of any of the sites on the links section. Links to other e-feds will only be included on a reciprocal basis. I. M. P R O D U C T I O N S Many aspects of the UWF site function on a program designed by long-time handler Mik G. under the guise of I.M.Productions (Get it? IMP? ...). The program quickly and easily produces rankings, bios, contact lists, and a variety of other options. While the program was initially designed for the UWF, it is very much open to other E-Feds, and can be found at BMW, FAW, FedWars, and many other sites. If you run a fed and are interested in getting IMP for your own site (and who isn't!), you can easily find their website off of any of the numerous links across the site. Or right here. P R I M E T I M E C E N T R A L The Universal Wrestling Federation is a proud and happy member of Primetime Central, a large inter-fed hosting many of the top smaller feds on the 'net. Generally this will mean absolutely nothing to the majority of our handlers. From time to time you may be asked to participate in a PTC event (like the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, or one of their regular PPVs), but participation is purely optional. Additionally, if you have an inter-fed and wish we would join, we're not interested. Unless you intend to hail us as the second coming, of course ... and pay us large sums of money. P O S E R The UWF has Poser. Thus, we rule. You know it's true ... Poser = Rule-ness. To the many people who would be interested in this sort of thing, the general UWF stance is that no one will be given a pretty little Poser image until they've been in the fed for at least a month, although that could be changed depending on how you fair in the fed.
