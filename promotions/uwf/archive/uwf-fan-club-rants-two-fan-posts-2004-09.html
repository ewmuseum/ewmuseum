Fan satire: two UWF fan-club posts, September 2004

September 2004 (file dated 14 September 2004)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Two in-character fan voices written for the UWF's own site: "UWF Fan Club rants" and a second fan-club homepage post. They comment on Mike Montgomery's World Title win, the Regal Rumble, a poster who had just been fired from the roleplay board (see the spoof index of the same week), and the Phenomenons and the Local NVA Company. The abuse in it is the joke: the voices are written to be unbearable fans. Site satire, with no show of its own.

Belongs to no show in the record. Served whole, as written.
