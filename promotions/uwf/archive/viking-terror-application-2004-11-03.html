The Viking Terror (a UWF tag team application)

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The application of the tag team The Viking Terror, Gunnar and Gabriel Karlssen of Umeå, Sweden, as the UWF’s form processor mailed it on 3 November 2004 and a UWF staff member forwarded it, with its debut roleplay; from the Janitor’s own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The applicant’s name and address, the staff member’s name and address, and the staff addresses are marked.

The character file the application carried is served as the character file. Served whole, as written.

Front Page. www.yahoo.com ---------- Forwarded message ---------- From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Cc: Bcc: Date: Wed, 3 Nov 2004 07:42:12 -0600 Subject: eFed Productions Form Processor name: [name withheld] email: [detail withheld] Wrestler_Name: The Viking Terror Age: Gunnar Karlssen Height: Gabriel Karlssen Weight: 545 (Gunnar 5'10 185, Gabriel 6'8 360) Alignment: Neutral Style: Gunnar - High Flying, Gabriel - Power Division: Tag Theme_Music: "Vicious Circle" by Caliban Home_Town: Umea, Sweden Accomplishments: none Appearance: Gunnar's small frame is well toned, for what he has to work with. He has dark brown hair, cut short, and a chinstrap beard. Gabriel is 360 lbs of pure muscle. He has long dark brown hair, and no facial hair. Both brothers wear royal blue tights, and royal blue boots with yellow strings and yellow soles. On the right leg of each of their tights, in yellow, reads "Svenska Stothlet". Gimmick: Gunnar and Gabriel have tremendous pride for their family, and their country. They aren't fan favorites, but they're not the most hated people, either. They could care less what fans think. Their gimmick is their intelligence. They pound on any weakness they can find and exploit it until their opponent can't take anymore... then the real pain starts. They're both extremely smart wrestlers, and frustrate opponents into making mistakes. Their weakness is that there are some people you really don't want to make mad. Background: Gunnar and Gabriel were trained by their father, legendary Swedish greco-roman wrestler Konstantin Karlssen. After learning the basics of greco-roman from their father, they took any european underground wrestling federation that they could find by storm. They have no titles of reknown, but they are extremely well known by european wrestling fans. Associates: n/a. Finisher_Name: The Rain of Terror Finisher_Description: The opponent is reverse irish whipped into the corner. Gabriel or Gunnar lifts the opponent up and sets them up sitting on the top turnbuckle, facing out. At this point, Gabriel is tagged in if he wasn't already in. Gabriel stands on the second rope behind the opponent and executes a full nelson slam from the top rope, as Gunnar executes a springboard corkscrew frontflip legdrop from the apron onto the opponent. Signature1: no title Signature1_Description: Gabriel irish whips an opponent into the corner, then gets down on all fours a few feet from the opponent. Gunnar runs at the opponent, springboards off of Gabriel's back, and spinning heel kicks the opponent in the head. Signature2: The Valhallabomb (Gabriel) Signature2_Description: The Valhallabomb is a reverse Death Valley Driver, only executed by Gabriel. Signature3: The Fall of Hardrada (Gunnar) Signature3_Description: The Fall of Hardrada is a reverse DDT used on an opponent when both Gunnar and his opponent are on the outside apron. He will use it anywhere, but the apron is a preference. Basic_Move1: Spinebuster (Gabriel) Basic_Move2: Short-arm Clothesline (Gabriel) Basic_Move3: Insiguri (Gunnar) Basic_Move4: Hurracanrana (Gunnar) Basic_Move5: Double Underhook DDT (either) Roleplay: < As we're brought back from commercial, we're taken to the beautiful landscapes of Umea, Sweden. > voice: The landscapes of Northern Sweden. On his right is Gabriel... a freak of nature. > voice: ...but simply known as The Viking Terror to everybody else. < "Vicious Circle" by Caliban hits the PA and we see video of Gabriel and Gunnar working their way up through the ranks in Europe. > voice: After destroying everything in their path in the Scandinavian countries they were raised in... theye set their sights on America, and the Universal Wrestling Federation. Much like their ancestors centuries ago, Gabriel and Gunnar Karlssen are bent on destruction, and won't rest until they take over the UWF... < A montage of Gabriel and Gunnar using their famous "Rain of Terror" on random opponents is shown. > voice: ...Is the UWF ready for The Viking Terror? Only time will tell... <The words "TIME TO DECIDE IF THE GODS ARE ON YOUR SIDE" appear on the screen. The letters fade out as the screen cuts to static, and then to black.>
