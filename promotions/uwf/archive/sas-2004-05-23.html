(Kazee and Raid both regain their feet and start exchanging lefts and rights.  Raid finally gets the advantage again by hitting Kazee in the gut with a knee.  He sets Kazee up for a power bomb, unfortunately for Raid, it’s to close to the chair, which Kazee quickly grabs and nails Raid right between the eyes.  Kazee leans against the cage, waiting for Raid to regain his balance.  As soon as he does, he hits Raid with the Bomb De Commonwealth.  Both men are down.) Rick:  BOMB DE COMMONWEALTH.  Kazee can’t get the pin, but it puts a definitely damper on the entire situation here for Raid. Larson;  True, but Kazee can’t capitalize.  They are both down for the count. (Kazee and Raid both start to stir about the same time. Each one is using the ladder to hoist themselves up and each one starts to climb up it.  Raid has got the chair with him and they are both climbing up to the top.  Both of them reach the top at the same time, now standing on top of the Tower of Doom, inches away from the belt.) Rick:  They are both on top.  Who is going to grab the belt first. (Kazee and Raid start exchanging rights and lefts again. Raid drops the chair as it is.  Raid staggers Kazee with a huge right hand and goes for the chair again.  This time, as Raid is about ready to swing it, Kazee springs himself up and nails the chair with a drop kick, sending it back to the ground, and Raid with a look of shock on his face. Kazee leaps up on Raid’s shoulders so that he can get that extra boost that he needs to grab the belt, which Kazee is able to grab the ring the belt is holding onto.  Not to be out done, Raid pushes Kazee off making the belt swing like a pendulum and leaps up to grab onto the ring with Kazee.) Rick:  Both men have their hands on the prize. Question is who will bring it down first. (Kazee and Raid both try to pry each other off the ring, Kazee seems to be trying a little harder than Raid.  All of  a sudden there is an explosion that rocks the arena.  Kazee jumps at this noise and looses his grip on the ring, falling down.   However, the third level of the Tower is no longer there, and Kazee falls almost 20 feet down to the roof of the second level, hitting his head on a chair.  All of a sudden there is a second explosion.  The second level of the Tower of Doom implodes in on itself, causing Kazee to fall another 15 feet.  Finally, a third explosion rocks the arena, causing Kazee to fall down to the mat below where he is totally motionless.) Rick:  GOOD ROD!!!  GOOD ROD!!! SOMEONE HAS BLOWN UP THE TOWER OF DOOM!!!!   AND RAID IS HANGING FROM ALMOST 60 FEET IN THE AIR. (Raid removes the belt from the ring with one hand, and secures it over his shoulder.  Suddenly, the ring starts to move upward toward the roof.  The spotlight follows the ring as it goes up. A the ring gets up in the catwalks, Adian Pryde and Joanna are there to Pull Raid over to the catwalk, belt in tow.) Ring Announcer:  Your winner and NEW INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPION, RAID!!!! Rick:  Raid has done it.  Raid has won the belt from Kazee.  And Kazee is out. (Joanna walks over to Raid with a couple of bottles of champagne.  She kisses Raid on the cheek and hands him one of the bottles.  Raid pops the cork on it and starts to pour the champagne over the catwalk down onto the unconscious body of Kazee.) Rick: WHAT A NIGHT! We have a new Intercontinental Champion! Moraless is the new PTC Extreme Champion! There's just TOO much to summarize about! I'll just quit while I'm ahead! GOODNIGHT! [End Transmission.]
