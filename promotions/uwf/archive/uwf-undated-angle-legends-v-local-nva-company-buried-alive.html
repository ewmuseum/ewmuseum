An undated angle: Legends v Local NVA Company, Buried Alive... With Animals

undated (file dated 15 May 2005)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Three pieces of script in one file from the promoter's own drive: two short "random angles" in which the Phenomenons (who style themselves the Commissioners of the UWF Tag Team Division) kidnap the Local NVA Company and then the Legends, and a full UWF Tag Team Title match script, the Legends against the Local NVA Company in a "Buried Alive... With Animals" match with the Phenomenons as special guest referees, which ends with the Calcutta Wrecking Crew winning the titles. The file carries no show, date or card; only the file's own date (15 May 2005) is known, so the page is served undated. No served UWF page carries this match, and the file date falls between the registry's last February show (20 February 2005) and its first October one (9 October 2005), a stretch that holds no show.

Belongs to no show in the record. Served whole, as written.
