An angle from September 2004: the Phenomenons crash the World Tag Team Title four-way

September 2004 (file dated 21 September 2004)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A script for the build to Live and Loud 4: a World Tag Team Title four-way (the Masked Monkeys, the Legends, the Local NVA Company and the Phenomenons) interrupted when the Phenomenons run in to pester every team in the ring, ending with the Masked Monkeys winning the belts and the Phenomenons stealing them. The served Live and Loud 4 page carries the same four-way in a different script; this version is not the one on it, and its file is dated 21 September 2004, before the show.

Belongs to the build to Live and Loud 4 (15 October 2004). No show of its own. Served whole, as written.

[As the bell rings, Lance Rose stands firmly in the center of the ring … and begins rubbing his nipples, his eyes rolling back in his head. White Tiger stands across from him, disturbed, as the rest of the competitors move to their corners.] Rick: “And here it is … the big World Tag Team Title match!” Larson: “The Masked Monkeys are seriously going to try to win the Tag Titles with paper bags on their heads?” Rick: “I think so, yes.” Larson: “How expensive can new f’n masks be?!” [Rose’s eyes roll into the back of his head, and White Tiger, horrified, just kicks him in the head, dropping the Legend in a second.] Rick: “And that is …” “BOOOO!” Larson: “Oh … no.” [Interrupting the match, because that’s what they do, the arena erupts in a bored sort of cheer as Shane Stone and Eric Cameron, the Phenomenal Phenomenons, erupt through the curtains, jogging towards the ring.] Shane Stone: “Booooooooooooo!” Rick: “I guess we should have seen this coming, huh?” Larson: “Yeah, but … they were arguing with the NVA last week. I thought it was a pretty safe bet that they’d have forgotten about this stupid challenge by this week.” [The Phenomenons reach the ring … and plop down on the ramp. Eric grabs the microphone from his partner.] Eric Cameron: “Ahem … carry on.” [Shrugging, White Tiger turns, and kicks Lance Rose in the head again.] Shane Stone: “BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [White Tiger swings back around, glaring at the Phenomenons and muttering Spanish obscenities. Shane looks up at White Tiger with a stupid grin, pretending not to notice as Eric Cameron gets up and runs to the other side of the ring.] Shane Stone: “What? Wrestle your damn match, you bagged monkey!” [White Tiger turns around, kicking Lance Rose in the head yet again.] Shane Stone: “Boooooooooooo!!!!!!!!!” [White Tiger screams in Spanish, then turns to tag in the Green Demon …] [... only to realize that both the Green Demon and Ryuhaku Iizuka are slowly backing away from the ring as a giant polar bear stalks after them, and Eric Cameron is standing in their corner.] Rick: “I have a feeling this is going to end horribly, you know?” Larson: “Oh I do.” [White Tiger turns around to find someone else to tag in, but Shane Stone jumps to his feet.] Shane Stone: “Alright … this sucks.” [Stone jumps into the ring.] Shane Stone: “This is just silly now. Silly! So …” [White Tiger stalks after Shane, but Stone wags a finger.] Shane Stone: “See … Richie Vaughn explained all this to me already. If you try to fight me, then we’ll have to fight back, and then someone will get DQed, and then you won’t get to win the Tag Titles … and that would suck!” [White Tiger growls.] Shane Stone: “Ladies and gentlemen, we apologize, but as you know we have been met with a challenge from the Local NVA Company to sufficiently piss off every tag team in the you dubya eff. And we intend to win … oh yes! The communist monkeys will never defeat … the Defenders of Democracy~!” [Eric nods on the outside.] Shane Stone: “And now we’ve got a crappy match with like the whole stinkin’ tag division, so …” [Shane taps his foot.] Shane Stone: “If you guys could just get in a line, we can piss you all off kind of sys … syta … syste … in order! Actually, Monkeys … if you could go back to your corner, we’re going to leave you for last. You know what … everyone just go to your corners. Lance, you’re first.” [Lance Rose wanders over to Shane tentatively.] Shane Stone: “You know, you Legends, like, totally rule. You …” [Shane kicks Lance Rose in the nuts, dropping the Legend like a sack of crap.] Shane Stone: “Mwaha!” [Shane kicks him in the nuts again.] Rick: “I think the Phenomenons have lost their f’ing minds.” [Stone turns over to Danny and Bryn Jaye.] Shane: “Danny boy … oh Danny boy … the sheep … the sheep are caaaaaaaaalling …” [A small finger taps Danny on the shoulder. Jaye immediately swings around, swinging wildly at whoever was behind him … but misses by at least four feet.] Shane: “Oh! So high!” [Danny Jaye doesn’t even see it coming … but the scream can be heard a mile away. Pete the PG Tips Chimp~! dangles from Jaye’s groin, his teeth cinched in deeply.] [Bryn Jaye doesn’t even know what to do … he simply stares, horrified, as his father falls off the ring apron, the chimpanzee still crunching his manhood.] Rick: “Oh God … the Phenomenons got Pete back.” Larson: “Why? How!?” Shane: “Mwaha! What’s the count to now?” [Eric Cameron looks down at the clipboard in his hand, checking off Danny Jaye.] Eric: “Hmm … two down, six to go.” Shane: “Alrighty … Bryn, just … wait there I guess. We really didn’t have anything planned for y—“ [Shane yelps and dives away as Bryn Jaye comes charging at him. With Shane rolling away, looking like a complete retard as his near 300 pounds rolls past, Eric Cameron smacks Jaye’s large head with the clipboard.] [Bryn ignores him, getting ready to charge at Shane, with Eric smacking him over the head with the clipboard again and again and again. Unphased, Bryn Jaye charges forward …] [But Shane just ducks away, and Bryn Jaye goes sailing over the top rope. Stone scrambles back to his feet. Shane sticks out his tongue at Bryn, who sticks out of the second row upside down.] Shane: “Wait … does that count? Screw it … I’m counting it. Now …” [The Green Demon and Ryuhaku Iizuka are screaming at the Phenomenons.] Shane: “Herbert … eat them!” [Now the Green Demon and Ryuhaku Iizuka scream for another reason, and charge into the crowd with the Trekkie eating polar bear hot on their heels.] Shane: “That counts too!” Eric: “That makes it five down.” Shane: “Kick ass. Luke! BOO!” [Luke Warren, who had been fixing his hair, yelps and falls off the ring apron, hitting his head on the guardrail.] Eric: “Six!” [Outside of the ring, Richard Ray Vaughn is stuffing money in Corky Spectre’s pocket as the referee conveniently sees nothing that’s happening. Danny Jaye is still screaming and beating at the chimpanzee on his groin.] [Shane kicks Lance in the nuts once again.] Shane: “Hmm …” [Stone turns to his partner.] Shane: “What about the Monkeys? I kind of like them.” Eric: “We can let them win, I guess? That’d really piss off the Legends, and we might get extra points.” Shane: “Deal! Masked Monkeys … or Paperbag Monkeys … enjoy.” [White Tiger and Pajaro de Presa stare at Shane Stone, confused.] Shane: “Oh for Rod sakes …” [Shane Stone picks up Lance Rose, and flings him at the Masked Monkeys. Pajaro de Presa and White Tiger both kick Rose while he’s still in the air, attempting to decapitate the Legend. The Phenomenons are already out of the ring trying to get Pete off of Jaye before Rose even hits the ground.] [Vaughn shoves Corky, who immediately slips into the ring. White Tiger awkwardly stands on Lance Rose, and Corky Spectre makes a blindingly fast cover …] 1-2-3~!! [And the Masked Monkeys are now the World Tag Team Champions.] [And Shane Stone and Eric Cameron bitch-slap them both with the World Tag Team titles.] [Shane and Eric dive out of the ring just as fast as they got into it, running up the ramp with the titles, cackling.] Eric: “And that makes eight, baby~!” [With the World Tag Team Titles in tow, the Phenomenons flee. Pete still hasn’t let go of Danny Jaye’s groin.] Rick: “Well … the Green Demon and Ryuhaku Iizuka are fleeing for their lives from a giant polar bear, Danny Jaye is being assaulted in the groin by Pete the PG Tips Chimp, Bryn Jaye is upside down in the crowd, the Legends have been screwed out of the World Tag Team Titles … and the Masked Monkeys just won the World Tag Team Titles, only to have the belts stolen.” Larson: “So we’re done?” Rick: “Yes … I think that means we’re done.”
