UWF (2001–07) Card Archive

Rules & Regulations

17 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
How does the UWF work ? Roleplays Generally Titles/Defending/Holding Creative Direction Cards Joining H O W   D O E S   T H E   U W F   W O R K ? The UWF is unique in the world of e-feds. We offer the facility to roleplay by both e-mail and message board. If you don't like having to search the web for roleplays and you prefer to have them arrive direct in your inbox ... we can do that. On the other hand, if you don't want your inbox tied up with messages and would sooner look on the web at the rp's ... not a problem. You do not have to type the roleplay twice, it is done automatically for you. The UWF is a booked federation. This means that the quality of your roleplays or angle suggestions directly affects your success. Be aware however that it is a sad fact of the wrestling world that occasionally people get jobbed. If it improves an angle you may well find yourself jobbed from time to time; the UWF is all about entertainment and sometimes losing a match adds to that entertainment. Whatever the outcome of the match it will not be discussed. Everybody, including Hogan, Austin, and The Rock loses a match at some time or another and so will your wrestler !!! If I receive an e-mail complaining about the result of the match it will be ignored, if I receive another the handler that sent it will be warned, one more and he/she will be fired. The best way to cope with a loss is to pick yourself up and get on with some decent role-playing. Those of you with experience of e-feds will note from the cards that TNM logfiles appear. This is merely for the purpose of writing the matches and is not used to decide any matches other than those which are too close to call on a booked basis. Each handler may take control of One singles Wrestler and one Tag-Team, but will be expected to fulfil the role-play requirements for each one, i.e.- two per week for the singles wrestler and two for the tag-team. Requests for additional wrestlers will be considered but this will require extra role-plays, simply doing a joint role-play for all of your wrestlers will not be enough. R O L E P L A Y S   G E N E R A L L Y Role-plays are the cornerstone of the UWF. It is down to the discretion of the individual handler as to what goes into their role-plays but the following must be borne in mind when writing your role-plays: Swearing is permitted but only to a degree. Try and think about the sort of thing you usually hear in the WWF & WCW. This rule is fairly relaxed but if any particular handler constantly over uses swearing of an unacceptable nature he/she will be warned once and then fired. Absolutely no personal attacks on any other handlers in the federation. As far as the wrestlers/characters are concerned handlers don't even exist. Do not speak out of character. If you have something to say from a personal point of view you should do so by means of a private e-mail and not under any circumstances passed off as a role-play. Never include other handlers wrestlers in your role-plays without their permission. If, for example, you role-play and have your wrestler attacking and beating up another handler's wrestler without their permission, you will receive one warning and one warning only. If it happens again then you are gone. The idea behind role-playing is that it is what your guy can do and say, not attacking someone else when they can't defend themselves in another role-play. If you are unable to role-play for any reason, such as holidays, exams, or illness mail the President and say so. It will be necessary to provide an estimate of how long you will be out of action in order that you can be removed from future cards. If you are a title holder and your expected absence takes you beyond the mandatory defence period [see below] you may, depending on the circumstances be stripped of the belt. T I T L E S:   H O L D I N G   /   D E F E N D I N G At the moment there are four singles titles; The World Title, Intercontinental Title, Shoot Wrestling Title, and Television Title; and one tag title, The World Tag Team Titles. An unofficial THC title [(T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship] is known to float around, though not technically an official title. More may be added depending on demand. There is no limit on how may titles one wrestler may hold ... you get what you earn. Don't expect a champion to have to defend his belt week after week. Title defences are compulsory in only one of three weeks except the TV Title which has to be defended every card. This does not prevent a fighting champion from putting his belt on the line on each and every card if that is what he wants to do. All titles will be defended at PPV's. C R E A T I V E   D I R E C T I O N Generally speaking the direction that you want to take your wrestler in is left entirely to you. It is down to you to contact other handlers, arrange feuds, or form alliances. From time to time you may be asked by the President to begin a certain angle with your wrestlers. He may want you to begin feuding with another handler or simply say that your current gimmick is going nowhere and suggest an alternative. You do not have to comply with this request. It is a request alone and not an order of any kind. The intention behind an angle request is a suggestion only. Refusal will not have an adverse effect on your wrestlers push. C A R D S The UWF runs two Cards a week: Wednesday Night Univeral and Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. These are the cards that are generally used to build angles and generate heat between wrestlers. In addition to this there will be twelve Pay-per-Views per year. WNU and SAS will both have 4 matches each. These matches will be given to people who regularly roleplay and are a first come, first served basis. The angle deadlines for the cards are at 6 PM Tuesday (Angle Deadline) and 6 PM Wednesday (RP deadline) for WNU and 12pm Saturday (Angle Deadline) and 12pm Sunday (RP Deadline) for SAS. PPV's will be monthly and will contain between 6-8 matches depending on the amount of heat going into the card and the demand for matches. Sneak attacks, etc ... are permitted, and to make one you must e-mail a request to the President no later than 24 hours before the appropriate card. Do not think that every sneak attack will be permitted. If the attack is pointless or if you are seeking to attack someone who is already heavily involved in angle with someone else it will not go ahead. The attack must advance an angle. As far as stables are concerned any angle requests that include your stable mates will require a mail from you and a confirmatory mail from the other parties concerned. Please remember that the purpose of Stables is to have a colleague watching your back, it is not to have a crowd of wrestlers constantly watching your back and "running security" for you. Angles involving Stables that would effectively make your wrestler impervious to attacks will not be permitted. By all means a Stable can come out and help once an attack has taken place [if you are anticipating one] but they will not be permitted to prevent that attack. In a similar vein the use of outside 'security' such as having your wrestler accompanied to the ring by security guards or anything else is absolutely prohibited. One wrestler must be as susceptible to an attack as another. In the two cards leading up to a PPV, no attacks on another wrestler will be allowed at all unless the attack is from his PPV opponent or unless you can convince the President that it is leading somewhere. Nothing should take away heat from an upcoming PPV. Attacks by others at the PPV are permitted. J O I N I N G You join the UWF by mailing the UWF president. On receipt of your mail an Application form will be sent to you within 24 hours. The Application asks for a lot of information and may take some time to complete. You get out of the UWF what you put into it so please don't be put off by the application. Once your application has been received and reviewed you will receive a mail welcoming you into the UWF and giving you instructions on how to begin your career. It is vital you act on this mail or you will not receive anything to do with the UWF. As stated above you are permitted one singles wrestler and one tag-team per handler. To create a tag team you must submit a sheet for each wrestler who forms that tag team. From time to time you may decide that your current gimmick is going nowhere and wish to change your wrestler completely. This is fine, except that you will not be permitted more than four changes a year. If you require more than this then there is obviously a problem with your role-playing and it creates unnecessary work on the site. Final point - Original Wrestlers only are accepted into the UWF. Do not bother submitting an application for Hulk Hogan, Steve Austin or anything that copies existing wrestlers gimmicks, ie- 'Stone Cold' John Smith. If you are not able to generate your own ideas and gimmicks the UWF is not the federation for you.
