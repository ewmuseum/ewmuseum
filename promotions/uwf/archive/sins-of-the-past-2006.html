Rick: I don’t know my friend, I really don’t know, but if Clyde is willing to break his own bones like that, and go through that much agony, who can possibly stop him? Larson: I don’t think that there is anyone more powerful than Clyde in the UWF right now! Rick: … I don’t think that there has ever been anyone more powerful than Clyde in the HISTORY of the UWF! “Wanna bet?” [The voice rings out over the P.A. system and echoes around the arena. Larson: I don’t know Rick, but I am having Déjà vu of the ears right now … I know that voice, I am sure that I do! Tommy Waters: … a former World Heavyweight Champion … Rick: … it must be him, it is Almasy! Tommy Waters: … a UWF Hall of Famer … Larson: This explains why Almasy is so sure that he will be able to bring the UWF down … he OWNS the place! Tommy Waters: … and a legend from the UWF AND GIWF … Rick: What?!? Larson: … but Almasy was a UWF legend, he wasn’t part of the GIWF!!
