Sunday Afternoon Supremacy — 22 Jun 2003

22 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin Transmission.][In an usual place.] "Please?!"[The Trailer Park Luchador stands solemnly but hopefully, his foot tapping on the ground.] "I ... said ... no." [Lew Spectre isn't as hopeful. It's been a long day already, and the show hasn't even begun ... he snarls as the Luchador pokes him.] "Pretty please?" "NO!!!!!!!!" [The Luchador goes to poke Spectre again, but Lew grasps his hand, curling the redneck high flyer's fingers back, bringing the Lucha to the mat. [The video screens light up and the lights around the arena shimmer down as Prodigy’s “Jericho” starts up] Walls of Jericho Walls of Jericho Walls of Jericho Walls of Jericho [The song stops though and breaks out into Led Zeppelin’s “Kashmir” as gold lights and blue strobe lighting occurs. The Black Star Revolution logo appears on the video walls as the majority of the crowd boos the entrance of Kenny Mercury and Ben Jericho. Both men are in their wrestling gear, but Jericho has his usual god awful random Hawaiian shirt on, opened up to show his ribs taped heavily] Rick: Well I thought this show may start on a high, but instead we got two low life’s coming out to the ring Larson: Low life? You want to talk about low life, how about Misfit stabbing Jericho and nearly killing him? He could have punctured a lung and not just cost Jericho his career, but his life also. Rick: Well, I’ll have to agree with you – losing Jericho would have been a severe casualty to our already depleting UWF roster. Jericho losing that mouth of his though wouldn’t be an unwelcome change though. Larson: You just don’t like the fact he’s telling it like it is! [Both men enter the ring, as Kenny Mercury grabs the microphone and Jericho slowly rolls into the ring, clutching his ribs. Jericho gets up and proceeds to walk around the ring while Mercury speaks] Mercury: I would like to address the situation involving me and Ben Jericho participating in a three way dance the next Pay Per View involving the tag team champions at present, The Local NVA Company, myself and Ben Jericho, and of course Misfit and Lew Spectre. Can I please point out that upon hearing this, I for one was OUTRAGED by such a maligned injustice. We had the UWF Tag Team Championship won when we last challenged for it, only to have that little… what did you call him Ben? [Jericho utters words over to Mercury, who smiles] Mercury: Oh yes that’s right – Jack White want-to-be interfering. He seemingly thinks he is being rewarded for screwing us over. On the contrary – being put in a sanctioned match with us is punishing him. And if the NVA Company thought they were lucky to get away with the titles last time, this time around it’s going to be a trip from Heaven to Hell. And it’s nothing personal… it’s Strictly Business! [Jericho ambles over to where Mercury is stood and proceeds to take the microphone out of his hand. The hard-core marks give Jericho a pop as he looks around to the seemingly growing amounts of “Where is Chris?” chants] Jericho: You know, I’ve done some crazy, insane things in my time. I’ve dove off Global Trons in the GIWF, I’ve sewn together the lips of a man I thought was Lew Spectre, and I’ve dabbled in the black arts and had a hand in killing a Dark Lord. I’ve gone through anything and everything – fallen from a balcony through a metal structure, nearly been buried alive, had several layers of skin torn thanks to barbed wire, light-bulbs and other sharp objects. I’ve put men in hospital thanks to countless char shots and stiff bumps. I’ve done a lot of things, and influenced a lot of things in my time… … However STABBING A MAN AND NEARLY KILLING HIM IS WHERE I DRAW THE LINE! [The crowd burst into cheers] Jericho: [Walks over to the ropes as if to lash out at the audience] You guys like that? [The camera shows Jericho looking at the first few rows that give him the middle finger and start nodding and cheering with approval] You’re all inbred degenerates anyway! Rick: That’s harsh! Larson: [laughing] It’s funny! Because it’s true! Jericho: And so I suppose if a man was to come out here you all like and tried to kill him in cold blood just to get a cheap pop, you blood thirsty moron’s wouldn’t ring an ambulance? You’d all sit there, drinking your beers from your plastic cups, waving your foam hands in the air, shoving popcorn down your throat and scratching your genitalia an the most in-optune time? [Crowd cheers] Jericho: You all make me sick… a superstar… no… a supernova could have had his wrestling career and life taken away from him. All because of some jealous gothic deviant wanted his moment in the sunshine and couldn’t be arsed to do some wrestling training. Remember who taught you the AFI, Misfit? Because sure as hell, someone with your low mental capacity couldn’t of…. [Slipknot’s “Heretic Anthem” begins as the crowd jumps to their feet. Misfit comes walking out the entrance and down the ramp to a huge ovation of cheers as Misfit runs over to the ropes and Jericho cowers away a little, clutching his ribs. Misfit slides into the ring as Jericho pulls Mercury away, pointing for him to get into the corner and leave him. Misfit pulls a microphone from out his pocket] Misfit: [Amidst the “He’s Extreme” chants] Benjamin Sebastien Jericho. Never in my wildest fantasies would I have expected you to come out here and renounce yourself as the King of Extreme. Never have I heard such mindless twaddle that you have done this and that – you’re living in the past; your no longer the future, you’re a scrapbook of past feats that will be shattered in the future by yours truly, The Evolution of Ultraviolence! [Crowd cheers as Jericho beings speaking] Jericho: Wow Misfit – your words really touched me somewhere no one else would… my freaking colon. You melodramatic gimp! [Mocks Misfit] ‘I am the Evolution of Ultraviolence’. Not-at-all, you’re the Evolution of Ultra-sucking-ass-ness! I mean you took a simple tool that I may or may not have grazed your head by accident with, as my cohort Kenneth there will back me up as a witness, and instead tried to kill me with it. Misfit: And I enjoyed it…. Jericho: I wouldn’t brag about that fact! Misfit: Why no? [Starts walking over to Jericho] I enjoyed the look on your face as the steel penetrated through your flesh [closer] I enjoyed the feel of the skin tear on the jagged edge of the screwdriver [right in his face] and I’d do it again Jericho: [Now with his trademark cocky grin] But you don’t understand, Angry Kid… you tried to kill me… and that’s attempted homicide…. [Suddenly a swarm of police officers bombard the ring, tackling the seemingly startled Misfit to the ground and begin reading his rights. They handcuff Misfit after a struggle with Jericho kneeling and looking him in the eye] Jericho: You know what that means! I’ll see your ass in court, you dumb bastard! And I think Lew Spectre’s dealing with the restraining order. That means you and me at the PPV isn’t going to happen without resolution….. Because I AM BEN JERICHO…. And YOU… are just a surly child who watched ‘The Crow’ once too many times prick! [And with that, Jericho spits right in the face of Misfit, who cannot do anything but shout obscenities at him. Jericho steps up and just laughs at Misfit, who gets dragged out the ring and away as Black Star Revolution just wave to him, as the camera shows the sheer aggression on Misfit’s face] Opening Match~Tag Team Match ~Opening Match A.D.D. Thunder & Lightning Referee: Jerry Kohler Winners: Thunder & Lightning Include angle from Local NVA Company & Lew Spectre? Match 2~ Television Title Match ~Match 2 Shadow Blade (c) Axel Action Referee: Corky Spectre Winner: Axel Action?! The Universal Wrestling Federation is Hiring! That's right, ladies and gentlemen! Did you ever want to be brutally assaulted with little provacation on national television while your friends, family, and children watch on, morbidly horrified at the impending assault brought down upon your helpless being? Did you ever dream of being thrown through plate glass windows? We don't even pre-break ours ... too lazy. Did you ever fantasize of chasing around grown men in provacative tights only for those same men to turn around and beat you within an inch of your life? Did you ever want to have a closer relationship with Bobby Brian Bangemhard, the Bum Boozler? Do you suffer from a mental illness or any other disability that could impair your judgement and lead to you being given the "best" jobs around the UWF's property? Did you ever want to be hit a lot? Well now's your chance! Because we need new cameramen. We broke all of ours. No experience necessary. Simply call 1-800-UWF-KILLS to speak to a representative in your area. The UWF: We Care (Straight after coming back from commercials, we cut towards the ring where we find Kevin Starr standing in the middle of the ring with the Intercontinental Title draped over his shoulder and a microphone in hand.) Rick: He's probably out here to answer Mike Montgomery's challenge from a week ago. (Kevin Starr looks around the ring as fans give him mixed reactions. Some fans are cheering for Mike Montgomery, while the others are cheering for Starr himself. Kevin Starr brings the mic to his lips and begins to speak.) Kevin Starr: "Monty... you want to know why I never offered you a rematch? After all that we've been threw, you want to know why I never offered you one? Wanna' know why? It's simple... you turned my entire career into a joke! It was all your fault!" (Again, the fans show mixed reactions towards Kevin Starr.) Kevin Starr: "You see, when I joined The Asylum, I didn't know what to expect. I was a rookie at the time and I didn't have anyone to call an 'ally'. So I when you offered me a position in The Asylum, I accepted it so I could finally have someone to watch my back in the UWF. Maybe even someone to call my friend... but I had no idea what I got myself into... The Asylum... the biggest fucking joke in this federations history! We we're the ass of the federation for The Asylum's entire duration in the UWF. As soon as I saw what The Asylum was about, I wanted out of it as soon as possible. Why do you think I rarely tagged with you against The Local NVA Company? Because I wanted no part of The Asylum." "...But you know what Mike? Even though you were the so called 'leader' of The Asylum, I knew that I was much better then you. Not only me, but every single person *points to the ramp leading up to the backstage area* backstage knew that I was better then you! To this day, I still know that I am better then you Mike... but I have yet to prove it. So that little challenge of yours... sure, I'll accept it. You've got 3 days to prepare for the match Mike. That's right, this WNU, you and me, one on one. I will finally be able to show you why they call me... "The Greatest UWF Superstarr of All-Time!" [Kevin Starr drops the mic to the mat to some more mixed reactions from the crowd.] [The Universaltron flairs into life just as the lighting adjusts slightly darker than before and red and yellow fireworks go off at the head of the ramp. "Divine" by KoRn hits the speakers, just as Joshua Dunn's logo flashes across the Screen, and is met by an instantaneous round of jeers and cheers from the crowd. After a little while Dunn himself emerges, wearing his "Commie" muscle shirt and his black Jeans, he's holding a microphone and stands at the head of the ramp] [Starr stops and stands up on the inside of the ropes, almost insulted by the presence of the communist stepping out right now to confront him] [Dunn waits for the sounds from the crowd to die down; keeping his eyes fixed intently on those of Starr's, who still doesn't know what to think at the precise moment, but returns the stare. Once the commotion has expired Dunn raises the microphone to his mouth and points to Starr with his free hand] JD: You don't get rid of me that easily Starr.. No, no, no, no, no..... no... [A whooping comes out from the crowd, and Dunn lets it pass, and keeps pointing at Starr, and lowers it only when the whoops from the crowd starts to ebb away slowly. Starr stares looking confused...] JD: What's the matter Starr? Didn't you expect to see me out here after I got screwed in that match of ours? Did you think that I would simply fade into background after that.. That atrocity? [Dunn pauses for effect, but Starr holds his own microphone up to his mouth] Starr: I was hoping you'd had enough of a beating [Dunn raises his eyebrows at this statement, truly astonished by his words] JD: You'd like that wouldn't you [He sneers as the crowd vouch their opinions in jeers. Dunn lets it pass] JD: I don't think you should be saying things like that, especially not to me. The way I see it, your running scared, instead of finishing what you started, and you know that you were about to taste defeat, and the loss of your title, you could feel the mental pain of one who is deemed to lose to the hands to your greatest adversary. You're running scared, Starr. Instead of rising to a challenge, like all Sportsmen should, you slink away and try to consolidate your sorrows by accepting the challenge to someone who you are certain you can beat, one you have beaten before. Thing is, you've never beaten me fairly, and you know it, you can't accept that I Joshua Dunn, Another Wrestler, A Scotsman, A COMMUNIST could be your Equal. So you avoid me whenever possible, you push me to one side as if I were nothing, but without ever beating me without outside help, you have no grounds for that assumption. I would have gotten that three count if it were not for that bumbling Idiot Montgomery, but I have.. Plans.. For him But Life is not about what if's, It's about facts, and the fact is, I will be getting another shot at this title, whether you like it or not! [Joshua Dunn lowers his mic and stares at Starr from the entrance ramp] Kevin Starr: You know what Dunn? You want a shot at... *Kevin Starr looks down at the Intercontinental Title over his shoulder* this little baby right here? Well guess what? Your not getting it! [Kevin Starr smiles as he rubs off a small smudge that was on the face of his title with his hand.] JD: ... I don't think you heard me right Starr. I'm GETTING that Intercontinental Title shot whether you like it or not! [Joshua Dunn's voice is a little more of a serious tone this time.] Kevin Starr: And Dunn... I don't think you heard me either. Your NOT getting that shot! Whether you like it or not! JD: We'll see, we'll see... I'll get that shot, just you wait. [Joshua Dunn smiles at Kevin Starr, then turns his back and walks into the back through the curtain. Kevin Starr shrugs in the ring, not taking Joshua Dunn's words seriously.] Match 3~Singles Match ~Match 3 Black Star Revolution The Legends Referee: I.M. Referee Match 4~ "Your Partner Ate One of My Balls" Revenge Match ~Match 4 Jamie Shock Maurice Referee: Graham Poll Main Event~Intercontinental Title Battle Royal  ~ Main Event Mike Montgomery (c) Sacha Ruzhyo Bruce Gordy Referee: Corky Spectre [End Transmission.]
