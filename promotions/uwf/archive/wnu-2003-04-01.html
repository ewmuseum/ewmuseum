[Sam Gordon appears at the top of the entrance ramp, accompanied by her theme music. He struts down to the ring to a massive earth-shattering outburst of boos. She is escorted by eight security guards. She enters the ring, as do her entourage, who form a perimeter around the ring as we stands proudly in the center. She is handed a microphone by the ring announcer. She brings it to her lips, as the boo's and jeers climax. She waits patiently as they die down... she begins...]   Sam Gordon: Now I'm sure the events at last Sunday Afternoon Supremacy are still fresh in your minds... as it is in mine... but that is not the reason I'm here right now... That'll all be addressed at another time. You see, I care. I really do care about this promotion... oh yeah... believe it or not. I'm here to please you! And have I got a surprise for you... though you surely don't deserve it from the response I get... the boo's and hate mail... the internet columns insulting my intelligance. Well, quite frankly, I am above that, and I know you all will just be all tickled up about this...   Sam Gordon: *AHEM* Ever since it's creation in the GIWF in 2000... the (T)(H)(C) division has brought in ratings... though a few months ago while Richard Hayes was in control, the title lost its appeal to many of the fans and even the wrestlers. But now, the division has been resurrected, so it seems... and I am going to keep it that way. You see... (T)(H)(C) means ratings... and ratings mean profit. And I'm ALL about profit. So, in honor of (T)(H)(C)... I'm throwing a little celebration in it's honor... a ball... a dance... and since it's just that time of year... I give you, the UWF fans... THE HARDCORE PROM AT (T)(H)(C) HIGH~!!!!!   [Half of the fans go nuts at the mention of (T)(H)(C) while the other seem a little confused...]   Sam Gordon: And this is how it all works... I have rented out the gynasium at the local publice high school, Ridgemont high... my staff will be working hard to decorate it for the prom... one of the most magical evening of the year. The only thing different is that this is a 20 man hardcore death match~! 20 men, by invitation only, will enter the event... if you're pinned... you are eliminated and must leave immediately... There will be plenty of roaming referees to make sure no eliminations are missed. As you might have guessed... there's No DQ... No holds barred... and falls count anywhere~!!   [The crowd cheers.] Sam Gordon: The last man standing will be named the (T)(H)(C) Prom Queen~! And will be granted a dance with the (T)(H)(C) king... the (T)(H)(C) King... our champion... Dude/Sirian. There will be a winner... and the champ is not required to compete in the prelimary dance... he only has to show up to meet his queen. And just to make sure the whole thing goes to hell... my very own Corporate Hitman Trevor Rayburn will be the head chaperone supervising this clusterfuck. He has the final authority. And now... the invitations... [Sam pulls out a piece of paper from her pocket and unfolds it... smiles slyly.]   Sam Gordon: You ready?   [The crowd screams 'YES!']   Sam Gordon: The first three competitors will be representing the Corporate Hitmen... my personal enforcer, Corporal Punshiment and the star teaming of Jay and Elijah. [The crowd boos.]   Sam Gordon: Well now that I've esstablished the favorites to win... how about the rest? World Reknowned Harcore Teacher... and some one I'm quite fond of... The Harcore Isosceles Trapazoid. 'The Dark Angel' Dante... The international superstar luchador, White Tiger. The Innovator of Pain, Bruce Gordy. Sirian/Dude, Former (T)(H)(C) champion and extremely dangerous man, Shadow Blade. The returning Kevin Starr. The True Indian Superstar, and former fellow PWW'er such as myself, Ruff Ryder. The new and improved 'Hot Shot' Craig Wright. The soon to be unemployed Chaos... unless he decides to win this match... The Trailer Park Luchador. One of the all-time premiere (T)(H)(C) wrestlers EVER... The Plague. The jobber killing Giovanni the 5th Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle. Cybernetic Hardcore Luchador #69. Two mystery opponents for good measure... and the 20th and final entry... the match who WILL be destroyed in this match. Appearing in his final mach... the match in which he will suffer from multiple career-ending injuries... The saddest excuse for a wrestler in the entire company... Slasher.   [The crowd pops at the mention of Slasher.]   Sam Gordon: Slasher's career ends next Wednesday Night Underground. You all have one week to prepare for the 'dance'. Pick me up at 7:00... I hope you're all fucking happy...   [Gordon's theme music hits the loud speakers and the Universal-tron plays her 'tron video. She exits the ring, security escorting her out.] Debut Match Craig Wright Ryusei -Versus- Winner: Ryusei "Mwayahayanyakwa!" [The Trailer Park Luchador shakes his head sadly as he stares into the mirror ... watching as Ryusei slowly walks up behind him, toweling off after his victory over Craig Wright. TPL breathes deep and tries once more:] "Nyahakwahmwahta!" [Ryusei stops in his tracks, staring awkwardly at the Luchador.] Ryusei: "What are you doing NOW?!" [The Luchador from the Trailer Park swivels around, waving off his partner.] TPL: "You fool! Do not interrupt the greatness that is born from the evil bossom of the Trailer Park ... involve yourself and perish!!" [Ryusei blinks.] TPL: "Argh! I'm never going to be able to take Tsunami's place at this rate." [Again.] TPL: "Do you live in a whole or something!? NOGOOD is recruiting!!! "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy ... the Local NVA Company ... I have a chance at standing alongside them now that Tsunami is off attempting to conquer a day care center in order to get his cackle back! "So I have to find his cackle before he does!" [Ryusei squints hard.] TPL: "Moron! If I get his cackle, I can take Tsunami's place ... I can be a member of NOGOOD! Just imagine it, Ryusei ... it was because of Tsunami that I wanted to come to the UWF ... well, that and they're the only promotion that hires retards like Chaos, so I figured I had a shot." Ryusei: "..." TPL: "I've been in the business for a few weeks, and I can be a Stable Wars Champion with them!!! Lew Spectre ... Curt Raines ... the Phenomenons ... Studog Escobar ... The Order of the Hammer ... Unforgiven ... Lyle "In Style" Wile ... Danny Jaye ... they're legends, and MY name can be sitting right next to them!" [Ryusei shakes his head.] Ryusei: "Why do you not just team with me? That White Tiger could be a very formidable partner ... he seems to have defeated you enough times." TPL: "... shut up." Ryusei: "We don't need to team with the big names to make our presence felt here ... all we have to do is win." TPL: "NONSENSE!!! ALL WE HAVE TO DO IS CACKLE! CACKLE~!!!!" [Ryusei drops his head.] TPL: "Just you watch! I'm going to channel the spirit of Tsunami Oni ... and I will defeat the White Tiger tonight! NOTHING CAN STOP ME!" [The Trailer Park Luchador bounds down the hallway.] TPL: "GWAHAHAHAHAHA~!" Do something to set up the next angle :-) [We cut to the backstage area ... where the Trailer Park Luchador is being wheeled out after his last match, strapped to a gurney as medics rush him off. Ryusei smiles as they move towards him.] Ryusei: "Nice match, friend." Jamie Shock grabs the referee and pushes him into the path of Gordy's feet! - the referee bounces back as he is hit in the face.] Rick: Darn Shock! That's an innocent official! [Bruce Gordy falls to the floor, and then starts to get up. Shock puts his hands on his knees as Gordy lifts his upper body up.] Larson: Whiplash time Rick! Larson: get up you stupid referee! [The fans roar in excitement as Killer Dude sprints down to the ring, barbwire wrapped trashcan in hand!
