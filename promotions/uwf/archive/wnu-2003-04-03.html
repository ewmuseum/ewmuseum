Debut Match White Tiger Trailer Park Luchador -Versus- White Tiger -v- Trailer Park Luchador For one of the UWF's generic debut matches, the crowd was extremely hot for the first "pure" lucha libre match in recent memory. Mexican star White Tiger came out against the rather unorthodox and inexperienced Trailer Park Luchador with his sights firmly set on victory. The hot dog vendor himself was certainly focused on claiming his first victory in professional wrestling, creating a brilliant frenzy of high flying acrobatics as both men attempted to out-do the other. With the Trailer Park Luchador in command for a moment, he went to the top rope, waiting for the White Tiger to get up. TPL flipped ... then flopped ... flipped again ... twisting around and around and arond and around in mid-air, somersaulting inside and out, before finally splashing down at the White Tiger ... ... who calmly stepped aside, allowing TPL to crash head first into the mat, drawing a horrific chant from the crowd as the luchador from the trailer park made a brilliant mistake. Continuing to draw off of his vast experience, going to the top rope and hitting the shooting star legdrop known as the White Lightning to pick up his first victory. With the match over, and TPL near tears at the loss, he was given a plunger and a boiler suit and told to see Janitor Mik for his next duties. Winners: White Tiger Mike: WHITE TIGER! Tag Team Match Wimp & Chimp Connection A.D.D. -Versus- Wimp and Chimp Connection -v- A.D.D. Seemingly a lopsided match on paper, it became just that inaction as well. The extreme experience of the WACC as a tag team, combined with the fact that Pete is ... well ... a chimpanzee ... served to easily destroy anything A.D.D. were able to throw at them. The first several minutes of the match were absolute domination as Maurice and Pete schooled the newcomers in the arts of tag team warfare. The surprise of the night, however, certainly came while the Wimp and Chimp Connection were only seconds away from the victory. Dangerman was stumbling around the ring, slowly turning in the right direction ... where Pete the PG Tips Chimp was waiting, mouth agape, in the corner to make the charge for the Chimpy Chompy Crunch. Unfortunately for Pete, just as he launched forward for the move, Dangerman apparently forgot he was in a match, saw something pretty on the outside, and skipped away from the action. Pete flew forward ... driving himself face first into the crotch of Maurice, who had been standing behind Dangerman. Jerry Kohler tried desperately to get Pete off of his own tag team partner, while Daisy and Arctic attempted to remind Dangerman he still was in a match. With A.D.D. finally refocused and Pete pried off of his partner, Arctic and Dangerman picked up the still-in-shock Maurice, nailed him with the Total Elimination they titled "Think About It," and picked up one of the biggest upsets in the tag team division's history. Television Title Match Trevor Rayburn (c) Dante -Versus- Trevor Rayburn (c) -v- Dante A mauling from bell to bell, Trevor Rayburn assaulted Dante the second he stepped through the curtains, refusing to even allow the match to start. After a grouping of UWF Referees finally ran out to make the save, Rayburn was forced to enter the ring ... which he happily did, dragging Dante by the hair the whole way. Magical powers not withstanding, Dante was hit with every move Rayburn could think of ... an idea that was made very clear when Rayburn actually took his cellphone out in the middle of the ring, called his agent and wrestling legend Lew Spectre, and audibly asked him for ideas on what to hit Dante with next. Lew's ideas apparently spurred Trevor on to six more minutes of senseless destruction, before finally hitting the gothic wonder with the Burnout, taking the win and retaining his Television Title.
