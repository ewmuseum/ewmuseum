[Sirius is dragged up to the top of the rampway, underneath the massive video screen that shows Danny Jaye picking up the barely moving Lew Spectre, and this sight seems to give him a second wind, because he starts to struggle with the hooded man, catching him with a couple of strong right hands, that releases the hold on his hair.] [Sirius wrenches the Shooting Star title from around his waist and runs at the hooded man, trying to catch him in the face with the title almost like a lariat, but at the last moment the hooded man ducks under the belt, allowing Sirius to keep running past him as he reaches out with one hand to grab the belt from Sirius’ grasp.] [Turning around in shock, Sirius sees the hooded man standing there with the title belt, and for one second freezes … and one second is more than enough, as with a sudden movement, the hooded man lunges out and catches Sirius directly under the chin with a straight superkick, launching him right over the edge of the rampway, his body almost gliding through the air as the audience is stunned into silence, until the loud crack of his body hitting the amassed chairs on the floor below breaks both his fall and the silence.] [As the crowd give a collective gasp of amazement the camera pans down to show Siruis laying half on some chairs and half on the concrete, his eyes closed as a steady stream of blood comes from below his head, a tiny trickle also coming out of his nose.] [The hooded man on top of the rampway seems to be staring at the title belt that he is holding, as the image of Sirius is shown above him on the video screen.] [Reaching up to his hood he slowly pulls it down] Auron: It is Judgement … it is the man that was in the EWA with Siruis … the man who represented the UWF in the recent Golden Turnbuckle Tournament! Tidus: … the man who was recently screwed out of the GTT you mean! Auron: … the man that may have just ended the career of Sirius … we need to get some EMT’s out here right now, that man is seriously hurt!! [As Judgement stares at the title, Danny Jaye has Spectre up on his feet and with one release of energy Irish whip’s him into the ropes, and as Spectre comes rebounding off he catches him and spins him in the air, bringing him down into a bone crunching atomic drop … the male members of the audience giving a collective gasp of sympathy … holding Spectre up as he almost seems to collapse into himself.] Tidus: Thankfully we have some EMTs out here now, and it looks like Sirius could be in bad shape here, as the emergency doctor is with them, and they all look pretty worried. Auron: Well, he is in good hands, and I hope that as they stretcher him out of here we can get some feedback on his condition shortly … for now though it looks like Danny Jaye is in total control here! [Without seeming to make any effort, Danny Jaye … with an obvious renewed burst of adrenaline, and a large grin on his face … hoists Spectre up onto his shoulders, then strains his arms as he brings him up over his head in a massive display of power.] [As his arms lock out, Jaye gives a loud shout of pure aggression and almost throws Spectre up into the air, walking out from under him as Spectre’s body crashes face first to the mat below him, where he lies unmoving.] Tidus: Burn in Hell Spectre … Burn … in … Hell!!! Auron: … and Jaye is going for the pin! [Judgement looks up at the images on the video screen, watching as the referee slides into position as Danny Jaye rolls Spectre onto his back and covers him] One … Two … Auron: Spectre’s shoulder is up! Tidus: … no, he can’t get out of Jaye’s grasp, the champion is just too strong! Three …….. *DING … DING … DING* The winner of the match: Danny Jaye Auron: Danny Jaye has done it! Tidus: Unbelievable … Jaye has beaten Lew Spectre and retained the UWF World title!! Auron: … though I have to think that things would have been very different if it was not for that man standing up on the rampway … is he had not stopped Sirius I think that we would now be calling Lew Spectre the World Champion once more! [As the Danny Jaye gets to his feet, the referee hands him the title and raises his hand in the air.] [The crowd cheer loudly as this is done, and Judgement turns to stare down at the champion, nodding once in his direction, to which Danny pats the title belt over his shoulder, smiling up in thanks.] [Behind him, Lew Spectre rolls groggily out of the ring, grabbing a chair from under the time-keeper before sliding back in, behind the back of the unsuspecting Jaye.] Auron: Oh no … Spectre has lost the match but it looks like he is going to make sure that Danny Jaye is in no fit state to celebrate his win! Tidus: … I think that Judgement may have something to say about that! [Judgement begins to run down the rampway, as Danny Jaye begins to slowly turn around to wear Spectre is waiting … a large, evil grin on his face, as he measures up the champion for a chair shot.] [Leaping to the apron, and then quickly to the turnbukle, Judgement reaches the top rope, pulling of his mask as he does so, just as Spectre is about to unload on the now aware Danny Jaye.] [… the look on Spectre’s face, along with the stunned reaction of the crowd makes him glance over his shoulder at Judgement … who now stands revealed as …] Tidus: … JAY … PHOENIX!!! Auron: My God I cannot believe this, Judgement is … was always … Jay Phoenix, the former PWW and GIWF star! Tidus: We know that he has no love for Siruis, who as Chris Cage attacked him at the last Chris Williams Memorial Cup, as well as during the last months of the GIWF … and as for Spectre, well I think that we can say there is no love lost there either! Auron: So the notes were from him to warn Sirius away … and now it looks like Danny Jaye finally has an ally in this war with Spectre … I am shocked and stunned!! Tidus: … and it seems that Spectre is as shocked as you are, he has stopped dead in his tracks! [Giving a grin up at Phoenix, Jaye turns around and points at Spectre, shaking his finger in an obvious indication that he is telling him ‘no’ … that he won’t get to use the chair against him now.] [Spectre backs off slightly, holding the chair up in front oh him almost like a shield, as Danny starts to approach him, glancing backwards to check that Phoenix still has his back, who nods down at him, glancing around the stadium as if watching for any of Lew’s allies to arrive] Auron: Well, I think that we are finally going to see Lew Spectre get all the payback that he deserves, as he is in that ring with the World Champion … who has Jay Phoenix watching his back … and no-one to help him as his man Sirius is on his way to hospital right now! Tidus: … and it looks like Phoenix can’t wait to get a piece of Spectre either … incoming! [With a push from his legs, Phoenix launches himself up into the air, gliding slightly as he aligns himself for a missile drop kick, indicating over Danny Jaye at Lew Spectre, who gives a look of fear …] Auron: OH … MY … GOD!!! [… that is quickly replaced by a wide grin of triumph, as Phoenix connects with the wide back of Danny Jaye, forcing him forward so that his face crashes into the upraised steel chair, that buckles as Spectre puts his weight behind it.] Auron: WHAT IN THE HELL WAS THAT?!? [Danny Jaye slumps to the mat, his face red and streaming as his head opens up and blood starts to spill down around him.] Tidus: Phoenix … did Phoenix just make the biggest mistake of his life there? Auron: How could he miss Spectre … how could he hit Jaye like that? [With a nip up, Phoenix is back on his feet, glaring at Spectre who lifts up the chair in front of him menacingly.] [Phoenix glances down at the unconscious and bleeding champion, before walking slowly and steadily towards Spectre, and as he reaches him …] Tidus: … there’s your answer … HE DIDN’T! [… they both break into matching wide grins, and dropping the steel chair on the mat, Spectre reaches out and both men hug, slapping each other on the backs as they laugh.] Auron: It can’t be … say it ain’t so! Tidus: I think that it is! Auron: JAY PHOENIX HAS JUST TURNED ON DANNY JAYE!!!! Tidus: Lew Spectre just lost his own henchman … was just cleanly pinned by the World Heavyweight Champion … and that bastard has still come up smelling like roses!! Auron: JAY PHOENIX!!!!!! [Separating, Phoenix and Spectre look down at the fallen and bloodied World champion, and start laughing again.] [Phoenix picks up the World title belt, and hands it to Spectre who looks at it for a few seconds before giving another smile to Phoenix and throwing it over Danny’s face.] [Spectre points at the EWA Shooting Star title on Phoenix’s shoulder, and Phoenix just gives a quick grin as he says something to Spectre that makes him laugh.] [Together they slide out of the ring, pointing at the video screen and the images of the referee checking on the blood covered form of Danny Jaye.] Auron: What on Earth is going on … what in the hell are Phoenix and Spectre doing together? Tidus: I don’t know … and I don’t think that we are going to find out tonight either … those two bastards have just screwed over the World Heavyweight Champion, and they’re just going to walk away! Auron: This is … dear God, Danny Jaye has been fucked three ways from Sunday! Tidus: we are out of time folks … join us on Universal to see if we can find out what the Hell is going on! Auron: JAY PHOE … !!!!! [End Transmission.]
