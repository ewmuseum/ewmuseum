Wednesday Night Universal — 9 Jul 2003

9 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin Transmission.] Rick: Hello fans and welcome to another slamtastic episode of your favorite Wednesday Night American Past time, Wednesday Night Universal! Larson: And along with Rick, I'm Larson! Hello fans! Rick: We've got another great show for you tonight and it all starts with the opening match! We've got Axel Action vs. The Fool coming at you first. Larson: Well Axel Action finally got back at DJ Cool this past week for all of the disrespect that DJ has been showing him since they both came to the UWF together. Now who's the bitch DJ? Huh? Yeah... that's right! Rick: Yeah, Axel has been showing alot of promise this week. Not only did he damage DJ Cool's ultra high ego, he's also been busy sending out promotions. If Axel keeps up this effort he might get a shot at the Television Championship. Let's not count out The Fool either. He's also been showing much effort since his return. Laron: Didn't he blow up or something? Rick: Umm... I can't confirm that, but I heard that it did happen. Larson: Hmph... The Fool... what a moron... Rick: These two have been at each others throats this week and it all comes down to this match. Both of these men are also fighting for the same thing, a chance at the Television Title. Look to see the winner of this match going for that title in the next week or so. Larson: I'll admit, for two guys low in the rankings, this does appear to be a pretty good match. Rick: Like I said before, if they both keep up the effort, they won't be low at all. These guys could possibly be the future of the future of the UWF! Larson: Well, let's get this match started. Singles Match The Fool Axel Action -Versus- Winner: The Fool Rick: The Television Championship is on the line as Shadow Blade defends his Television Title against El Turtle. Larson: Giovanni- Rick: EL TURTLE! I don't think EL TURTLE likes to be called Gio- well, you know who. Larson: Well, EL TURTLE better be prepared. Shadow Blade is a Television Title master. I think he does have the record for most Television Title reigns. Rick: And that he does with 4, yes, 4 Television Title reigns. That's alot of Television Title reigns. Larson: Yes that is alot of Television Title reigns... Rick: Yes it is... anyway, Shadow Blade looks confident going into this match. Larson: As does El Turtle. Trust me Rick, I know my Spanish. Rick: Sure you do Larson... Well this is shaping up to be a great match. At this point, I've got no idea who's going to win. It could go either way. Let's get to the match. Television Title Match Shadow Blade El Turtlé -Versus- Winner: Shadow Blade Tag Team Match Thunder & Lightning Abnormal Reality -Versus- Winners: ??!? Singles Match Sirian Max Damage -Versus- Winner: !?!??! Main Event -- Triple Threat Match Danny Jaye Misfit Mike Montgomery -Versus- Winner: Misfit [End Transmission.]
