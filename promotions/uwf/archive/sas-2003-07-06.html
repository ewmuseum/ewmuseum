Sunday Afternoon Supremacy — 6 Jul 2003

6 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin Transmission.] Walls of Jericho [“Jericho” by The Prodigy plays throughout the arena, as all eyes look up to the stage area. A loud mixture of cheers and boo’s erupt as ‘Y-2-Ego’ appears on the video screens and Ben Jericho cockily swaggers down to the ring, in a pair of baggy PVC trousers, black rock boots and Hawaiian shirt. His hair is tied back as he just grins at the signs shown in the crowd – “Not my King”, “Why Unforgiven Why” and of course “Jericho fears” with a name added for comedy effect] Rick: Well, here comes Y-2-Ego, and I don’t think there is any love loss between him and Sam Gordon. Larson: I don’t care, just look at that haircut! Doesn’t he just look spiffing! Rick: I thought you couldn’t stand Unforgiven! Larson: But you see, he’s not Unforgiven! He’s Ben Jericho! He’s Mister Controversial! And I for one am welcoming this new change and attitude with open arms. Rick: He’s sold out his beliefs, his ethics… Larson: How so? He has always been a very gifted technician. Just the fickle fans wanted to see him throw himself from anything and everything – and he pandered to them. He’s seen what they really are after wrestling under a mask and decided they should appreciate him, not what he does! Rick: Well this should be an interesting encounter – Sam Gordon and Ben Jericho haven’t seen eye to eye since she joined, but maybe tonight is the night [Jericho slides into the ring, drawing a microphone from out his pocket and staring at Sam Gordon, grinning] Jericho: [With the die-hard marks still cheering him] My name is Ben Jericho and I have a problem…[Everyone listens intently as Jericho sternly points a finger at the stage entrance] the rest of this place! [The crowd boos lividly! As Jericho points the finger at Sam Gordon] Rick: Good rod, he’s going to start a riot back in the locker rooms. Jericho: And you, Samantha Gordon, are to blame for it. You are to blame for the steady stream of absolute shit that has surfaced in the UWF – my beloved UWF that I adore more than the sharp image I see in the mirror any time I walk past one! Larson: It’s sharp! Rick: Hay-zeus Christ… Jericho: You see Samantha… honey… at first I disliked you because it seemed the in thing to get [uses finger to exclamate the use of a term] ‘over’ with the fans. Then I sat down and thought those exact fans cheer idiots like, oh let’s say, Danny Jaye or Digital Mortality whenever they enter the ring. No offense to any of them – I mean, you needed Mortality just to keep the Equal Rites officers off your back now that they can see you hired a minority… and Danny Jaye I am sure can leave now he’s kicked that nasty hormone therapy treatment. [Loud boo’s from the crowd, as Sam Gordon is actually getting cheers] Jericho: So what brings me down to the ring is I wanted to tell you face to face; thank you for turning this place into a dump. Like you turned the PWW into a dump with daddy’s money. Like you sold out the careers of Jamie Shock, Prodigy and Tsunami Oni just to let idiots like Slasher, Seymour Almasy and Mike Montgomery. And worst of all, there’s a monkey as the champion…. A FREAKING MONKEY AS THE CHAMPION Gordon: Unforgiv…. Jericho: [Stern voice, walks closer to Gordon, backing her into a corner] Listen, woman [sizable boo’s from the crowd], don’t address me by that name. Here’s my point – I could of gathered some names from various members of my Gundam Wing in the crowd [the marks cheer – they know who they are] then I thought you’d just ignore that. So here’s what I came out here to do. I am not asking you to do it – I am telling you woman… you fire Petey the P.G Chimps Monkey right now, or suffer the consequences! [The crowd erupts into a loud explosion of boo’s, as Gordon looks around, now becoming increasingly trapped in the corner. Gordon looks on worried as Jericho leers over here smirking, tapping her on the chest] Jericho: Come on now Samantha [running his fingers through her hair], maybe I’ll make it worth your while. You put most of the boys in the back ‘over’ so I heard. Hell, I bet you even elevated that damn primate. Just fire him, and then hell, you can ‘sack’ me, baby cakes… [And the moment Jericho winks at her, Gordon winds up and spits in his face. The crowd cheer as Jericho backs off to wipe the spit, Gordon walking over to the temporarily blinded athlete. But she is met with an ear splitting slap to the face, as she drops to her knees in shock she just got slapped.] Jericho: You just wouldn’t listen, would you bitch??? [And as Jericho gets ready to stomp on her, Metallica’s “Unforgiven” plays as the crowd gives Lew Spectre a mixed reception as he sprints down to the ring. He grabs Gordon’s microphone and gets in Jericho’s face] Spectre: What the fuck are you playing at Jericho? You think you can just come out here and insult a member of the staff like that – someone who pays your wages? Your useless, Jericho… [Jericho walks over to the ropes as Lew Spectre helps Gordon up. She looks in the eyes of Spectre thankfully] Spectre: Why don’t you show Ms. Gordon how we really treat her type in this company [And to the horror of everyone watching, everyone in the back and the announcers, Jericho grabs Gordon in a rear waist-lock, lifts her up high and tosses her over his head as he bridges back, dumping her on her head with a release German suplex. The crowd boo hysterically as Lew Spectre gets in Gordon’s face] Spectre: What did I tell you Sam? I told you I wouldn’t let you destroy my concept, didn’t I? [Looks to Jericho] Benji – finish the job! [And with that, Gordon’s lifeless body is picked up and set up for a powerbomb. Jericho lifts her and dumps her for one, then lifts again and dumps her for two, then lifts her, but throws her over his back, clinging onto the legs in between the back of his neck and jumps up, landing firmly on his rear and driving her head into the canvas] Spectre: You know what I want Samantha Gordon. For now, just think about the consequences while my associate and I go celebrate. [Jericho gets up, with a snarl on his lips and an evil look in his eye. He spits at Gordon’s limp body and then grabs the microphone] Jericho: I know you loved every minute of that honey… your still daddy’s little girl… it’s just I’m your daddy now. [End Transmission.....] At least, until 'Otherworld' by Nobuo Uematsu hits. Rick: ROD DAMNIT, GET THOSE CAMERAS BACK ON! The crowd begins to cheer, as Petey looks on somewhat nervously from ringside. "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy emerges from the back, carrying a videogame, a tea set, and a look of disdain on his face. Aerith Heartilly is at his side, as is his Divine Blade Imperial II. Immediately, Ben Jericho goes ballistic in the ring, screaming obscenities at Seymour that he most likely doesn't even know the definition of. Seymour walks over to Petey, kneels, and hands him the English translated FFX-2 game, and the Final Fantasy teaset. Petey nods, and holds up one finger, as if to say "The most important one's left." Seymour nods in response, and climbs up onto the ring apron, glaring daggers at Spectre and Jericho. Seymour Almasy: What kind of fiend are you? You attack an innocent, well, not really innocent woman...BUT SHE CANNOT DEFEND HERSELF! WHAT IS THE MATTER WITH THE TWO OF YOU?! Spectre is silent, wondering what the hell Seymour is doing out here. As per usual, Jericho has no such restraints on his mouth. Ben Jericho: You know, I could ask the same thing. This has NOTHING to do with no-talents such as yourself. I'm trying to SAVE this company. I don't know what's worse, the fact that we have a monkey as a champion, or the fact that YOU'RE going to be challenging for it! Jericho's cocky smile persists on his face, that is, until Aerith Heartilly steps in the ring, and smacks it off with a vicious open handed slap. Jericho's head recoils, as he turns back, a smile on his face. Rick: That son of a bitch is smiling. Larson: TAKE HER OUT, BEN! Seymour pushes Aerith behind him, and steps in front. He goes eye to eye with Mr. Controversial, neither man blinking, until Lew Spectre comes at Seymour from the side. Rick: LOOK OUT! Seymour catches Spectre out of the corner of his eye, and blocks Lew's right hand, as the World Champion chimpanzee bounds into the ring, seemingly angered at Ben Jericho's comments. Rick: This isn't good.. With a mighty leap, Pete jumps up, AND LOCKS BEN JERICHO IN THE CHIMPY CHOMPY CRUNCH! Larson: NOOO! CURSE YOU, YOU STUPID MONKEY! Seymour fires back on Spectre with a series of right hands, then scoops Lew up on his shoulders, taking him down mid-ring with THE SHOCKWAVE PULSAR! Petey releases the Crunch on Ben Jericho, as Seymour looks down at the Chimp. Petey leaves, heading to the back with Maurice, as Seymour looks at a testicularly traumatized Ben Jericho. Seymour Almasy: You know what? I've made a decision. Once I defeat the monkey for the World Championship ... you're up next, Ben. And then, I will banish you to the Farplane for your crimes... Jericho does not respond, instead, he launches himself at Seymour, the two men exchanging violent right hands, as Aerith tries to revive Sam Gordon. Rick: These two guys are the future of the UWF, and barring a minor miracle, we'll be seeing these two next month for the World Title! Jericho gets the edge, whipping Seymour into the ropes. Almasy ducks a Jericho clothesline, however, and comes back with the JECHT SHOT! Larson: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Rick: The Jecht Shot by Seymour Almasy! An enraged Ben Jericho slides out of the ring, continuing to smack talk Seymour as he and Spectre head up the aisleway. Aerith and Seymour stand in the middle of the ring, arms raised. Rick: SAM GORDON HAS BEEN DESTROYED BY BEN JERICHO AND LEW SPECTRE, WHEN SEYMOUR ALMASY, OF ALL PEOPLE COMES OUT AND SAVES! Larson: Sure! But when it comes down to it, Ben Jericho is the real winner in this situation! Seymour's going to beat Petey, and then, Ben Jericho gets what he wants! A shot at the UWF World Title! Rick: FOR LARSON, THIS IS RICK! TUNE IN TO SUPREMACY! I had such big plans for you." [The camera swivels away from the unmoving body of Sam Gordon ... and focusing in on the man who, up until weeks ago, had been Gordon's head advisor ... a man who, up until two years ago, had been the epitome of wrestling excellence ... Lew Spectre.] "But the best laid plans of mice and men ... and all of that, eh? You never did understand much more than 'Hop on Pop' anyways, you ignorant slut. "My heritage, Sam ... it's all this is. You understand that, right? It never was anything against you ... it's just the simple fact that I spent my entire career building a franchise ..." [With a speed unseen in years, Spectre is across the room, grasping Gordon's head by her hair.] "And in six fucking months, you turned that franchise into a carnival sideshow. Bravo, Sam ... Gurgles would have done a better job than you. "But I guess it still comes down to the same thing, Gordon. I wish we could have finished this already, but I suppose the Almighty Seymour Almasy has decided he's a good guy this week ... sticking up for the damsels in distress and all of that. Funny how you go from the bane of wrestling to being saved by Seymour in a matter of a week, no? "It's still just business, Sam." [Spectre grins devilishly.] "Alright ... maybe it's a little bit personal." [Lew's fist tightens in Gordon's hair.] "You raped my child, you fucking whore." [Gordon's unconscious body is dragged out of her bed.] "I put you here, Sam. Consider this your discharge." [With a sick snap, Sam Gordon is flung through the window. Shreds of a hospital night gown are followed immediately by shards of glass as the UWF Owner lands in a bush outside.] "And Jesus Christ, Sam ... how stupid do you have to be to let me work out the contracts for the purchase of a wrestling company!? "One little line about how the possible, though obviously improbable, inability of the new owner to run the fed would immediately leave its control under the leadership of someone more than suited for the job ..." [Spectre flings open the door, his trenchcoat flapping behind him as he walks.] "... and daddy's home." [Kenny Mercury stands next to the door, staring quizzically at Lew. Lew only smiles ... clapping one hand down onto Mercury's shoulder.] Spectre: "It's going to be a good night. I can feel it in my bones."
