News on The Stipulation for The Plague vs. Showtime at The PPV -- March 28rd, 2002 Well in case you all missed it last night, The Plague hit Showtime with The Black Death in the center of the ring and pinned him for the one, two, three. The stipulation for that match was that the winner could choose the special gimmick match for their match at The Pay-Per-View. I have talked to The Plague last night and instead of spoiling the surprise in a promo, he decided to have me, Jon Focker report this news on The UWF website so everyone will be able to see it. This is what The Plague had to say about the gimmick he will be choosing for The PPV match-up, “I went out and challenged Showtime to a Cage Match at The PPV, but now since I pinned him and I get to pick a stipulation, I was doing a lot of thinking and a Cage Match would be too easy for Showtime to get away from the beating I will give him at The PPV, so instead of just a basic Cage Match, I have decided to make it a Hell in a Cell, Last Man Standing Match. That means there will be no escaping Showtime, and no pinning. The winner will be able to walk out with The IC Title, while the loser will have to be stretchered out of the arena.” So you heard it here first UWF fans, The Plague vs. Showtime at The PPV in a Last Man Standing, Hell in a Cell match. Report by Jon Focker All the News Fit to Print -- March 23rd, 2002 Because the Web-Master has been dead over the last few weeks, then reincarnated this morning, I'm being forced to recap two weeks of news in one post. The UWF killed WNU and went to an extended SAS ... the UWF then killed the extended SAS and went to two small cards a week ... the Youngbloods reformed and the world rejoiced, except for those who hadn't yet noticed ... Cpl. Punishment won the World title for precisely three minutes and twenty seconds ... Johnny Blaze was fired ... Chris Steel quit ... we're down to three PWWers ... Star Motion~!! are back as commentators ... Snake Phoenix captured the Shoot Wrestling title ... the Prodigy lost to Cyclone, Cyclone~!! I say! ... Cyclone quit ... for some unrodly reason, Dex is still a champion ... Slasher is back! ... Gurgles is back! ... Hardcore Wrestling is back! ... Commissioner Lew has a migraine again ... apparently Shadow, Slasher, and Plague will all be challenging for the GIWF THC title that Gurgles holds ... why hardcore matches are being held in shopping malls is still anyone's guess. And that's all the news that's fit to print! Join us next week, when we hopefully won't be this sad and lazy. Report by "B.S." Bill Schwartz Webmaster Alive; Ignore Previous News -- March 23rd, 2002 Apparently the UWF Webmaster was not murdered by Head Janitor Mik and CHZ Parking Assistant Dub as previously reported. Assistant Janitor Harvell checked himself out of a Michigan hospital this morning, only stopping briefly on his way to his parked El Camino to explain his absense to the reporters. "I was on Spring Break, Rod damnit," the previously missing web-head said. "So I was missing for a while ... juggling work and drunken orgies at the same time is very difficult. So I ended up in a lake and died ... you didn't have to tell the whole fed to die! Someone find me my notes so I can update." The web-master/janitor was apparently suffering from several concussions when giving the statement, thus explaining why he sounds like one of those jobber people. Report by "B.S." Bill Schwartz UWF Webmaster Found Dead -- March 22nd, 2002 After an exhausting two week search, the body of Assistant Janitor Harvell, Web-Master of the Official UWF site, was finally discovered floating in Lake Eerie this morning. Janitor Harvell, well-known for his longstanding feud with Head Janitor Mik, had reportedly been in a heated argument with said janitor as well as CHZ new found parking assistant "Dub", following a UWF card at the Palace of Auburn Hills in Michigan. Police have not yet released a statement, but what this means for the UWF site is anyone's guess. Report by "B.S." Bill Schwartz The PWW Wins. -- March 4th, 2002 It was what none of us expected when they invaded. We all thought they'd just disapear within a few weeks -- and they did. We thought they would leave little to any lasting impression. It's not like the PWW ever managed to beat the GIWF in the ratings wars, so surely they wouldn't be able to beat them in a head-to-head battle ... would they? It seems they would. It's with a feeling of intense depression that I report on the PWW's victory over the UWF to you all. Not in a match, of course ... but as of this afternoon, the UWF is now testing the idea of moving back to one card a week, and most likely one PPV every two months if that is adopted for the future. The PWW's laziness has conquered the UWF Front Office ... how we can look at ourselves in the mirror after this is anyone's guess. Report by "B.S." Bill Schwartz Showtime Reigns Supreme! -- March 4th, 2002 Only a matter of days following the PWW implosion, the 100% Show has achieved surely the biggest accolade of his strange, strange career. Thanks to a well placed "assist" from the always perverse Shadow, Showtime managed to slap on the Whole Ten Inches on the UWF Front Office's own Enforcer and walk out of the arena as the brand spanking new Intercontinental Champion. UWF fans have apparently been in an uproar ever since the travesty of injustice occurred, with millions of fans running to and fro in an attempt to decide when the fed created an Intercontinental title. Nonethless, it was an accomplishment for the ages ... and even though Showtime could not be reached for comment, Showtime's Ass was more than willing to share his thoughts. "It's ... it's just the happiest day of my life," Showtime's Ass told this reporter. "I don't know what to say ... the cheering fans, the champagne, the prostitutes in the limo afterwards. It's all so much ... I ... I feel like crying." On a side note, I apologize for any inaccuracies in this statement; I was writing it down as Showtime's limo drove past me. Report by "B.S." Bill Schwartz The PWW Dies ... Again! -- March 1st, 2002 It seemed like only a matter of time to most UWF fans, but Professional World Wrestling, a promotion that closed down after a three year run last fall, has now finished its run as a stable as well. The quest for respect may have been a dismal failure, but the incorporation of talent has begun ... of the people that are actually left. Celest and Dream have disapeared into the wrestling abyss, Phoenix has gone into a form of purgatory just below the television line, and Hatchett seems to be continuing what he does best -- which is anything but maintaining consistency. It was with the success of former PWWers that the grouping came to an end, though ... it was Blaze's first act after picking up the Shoot Wrestling Championship as he allienated himself from the world. Even still, we here in the UWF News Department only see it as a matter of days before they do something wacky ... like revealing it was all a rouse to make us drop our guards! NO! Yikes ... I'm just phoning it in now. Report by "B.S." Bill Schwartz Primetime Central -- February 27th, 2002 This morning, the UWF's negotiations with Primetime Central, a large coalition of "independent wrestling promotions" finally came to a close. After weeks upon weeks upon ... err ... two meetings, the details were ironed out and we happily joined the PTC family. The UWF will now, in theory, gain even more publicity ... the PTC will, of course, benefit from the millions upon millions of visitors that click on our site, and the twenty five people that scroll all the way to the bottom to click the image. UWF fans will remember the PTC from their use of Jay Phoenix in a talent exchange (wherein they took our talent and we got squat), where the Mute Indian came in a disapointing second place in the 128-man tournament for the PTC Global Title, though, honestly ... no one really cared. In an attempt to show the unprecedented nature of this union, Commissioner Lew Spectre has released a statement to the masses. "Since the fateful day the PWW closed, we have been searching endlessly for a rival to call our own. Our new alliance with the PTC is aimed at benefiting the minor league promotions they host in an attempt to raise at least one to a position viable to our manipulation," Spectre said. "And if that doesn't work, we'll simply destroy them all and raid their best talent. I don't really have a preference either way." Report by The Front Office Shill Tag Team Wrestling Lives! -- February 25th, 2002 No, you didn't read that title wrong ... tag team wrestling is officially alive within the confines of the UWF! After nearly five months of complete apathy to tag teams, the company began a massive hiring campaign over the last few weeks that culminated in the crowning of new World Tag Team Champions ... and really, what better way to start off the division right than crowning two singles wrestlers as the champs? Adam "Hybrid" Finn and Blade, collectively the Lost Prophets, captured the tag titles from the tag wrestling poster-boys, the Dragoons, at the Test of Faith PPV, cementing the fact that everyone's favorite division is alive and well. With high caliber teams like the Prophets and Dragoons, former GIWF Champions in the FoD, and extremely impressive rookies in Team MIV, Phat LTD, and new signees Hot Stuff, the division is expected to get a big push in the fed over the next few weeks ... just as long as the Dragoons get the titles. Singles Division Over-Flow A recent declaraction by UWF President Richard Hayes (and possibly The Rotund Fellow) has effectively closed the Singles Division from further applicants. Chat Room Opened In the proof that the UWF caters to its fans first and foremost, the management has set forth to make the company's web-site as user friendly as possible. In showing that the UWF not only leads the world in Pure Wrestling Entertainment but also leads the technology front, a new prototype called "The Chat Room" has been hosted by the site. Using this new invention, UWF fans can speak with each other in real-time using "words" and "sentences." Considering the blazing speed at which words go by, this is no-doubt an attempt to raise the IQ of wrestling fans so that the UWF offices will no longer receive e-mails asking when Luca will return. What future technological improvements this could lead to is anyone's guess. But the UWF will surely remain at the forefront of the civilized world. Email the Boss!
