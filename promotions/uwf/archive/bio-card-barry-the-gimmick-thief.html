BARRY THE GIMMICK THIEF W R E S T L E R P R O F I L E Division: Single Date Of Birth: July 19, 1980 Height: 6 foot 3 Weight: 285 pounds Alignment: Face Theme Music: The James Bond Theme Home Town: Gimmickville, USA G I M M I C K Hello...Barry the GIMMICK THIEF!! There isn't an original bone in the man's body. B I O G R A P H Y Appearance: Built much like the WWE's Brock Lesnar, Barry wears a black muscle shirt, blue denim pants and grey/black sneakers...much like every single wrestler who weighs more than 250 pounds. He wears black gloves on his hands, and despite the fact everyone knows who he is, he wears a grey/black mask. Don't ask. Associates: Numerous parts of the criminal element, as well as the Asylum of course. Background: None yet, since I just created him. Accomplishments: Absolutely none...save stealing more gimmicks than the WCW M O V E S E T Wrestling Style Any Basic Moves clothesline powerbomb Big Boot Snake Eyes Choke Slam Signature Moves The Super Starr Ask Kevin Starr about it. The Lethal Injection Stolen from Corporal Punishment The Rock Bottom Oh like he wasn't going to do it. Finisher The Hangman's Joke Same as Mike Montgomery's. The finishers of his stablemates were the first ones he grabbed.
