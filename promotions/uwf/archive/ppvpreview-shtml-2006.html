The UWF! ... Insert Your Own Cheesy Tagline Here The UWF Presents Live & Loud ... 5! Live, October 30th, 2005 For the fifth straight year, the Universal Wrasslin' Federation has come together in their hometown of Chicago, Illinois to celebrate its own birthday. Five years before, the reign of the GIWF came to a close, and the UWF came to life … chock full of tournamenty goodness, imps in giant robots, and all the clowns you can shake a giant steel bar at! With a stocked lineup, the birthday celebrations begin anew as the UWF returns to normal – which is just not good for anyone, really. World Heavyweight Championship Match Misfit © -v- Killer Dude ... in an Electrified Steel Cage! For six years, they’ve mocked him. Killer Dude came to the shores of the Universal Wrestling Federation in 1999 … for the majority of his career, managing to actually FIND the arena was his biggest accomplishment. The Phenomenons, tag team legends, toyed with him … as Commissioners of the tag division, they made the big guy tag team with a miniature golf club for three months. Dude wasn’t happy about it. Kenny Mercury obliterated him, glued pubic hair to his head, and turned him into a laughing stock … err … a bigger laughing stock. He won the Jobber of the Year award on three different occasions, more than anyone in UWF history … and we’ve had some bad, bad wrestlers come through here. For six years, he was ‘pube-head,’ ‘jobber,’ ‘asshole,’ or ‘hey, you.’ His name was enough to inspire bathroom breaks from a hundred different audiences. But at some point, after six years of trying, Killer Dude found success … and no one was more amazed than the UWF fans. It might have been steroids. He might have sold his soul to the devil. However it happened … Killer Dude had … talent?!? After six years of an unremarkable career, Killer Dude shocked the UWF’s world when he went into the Regal Rumble and decimated the opposition. The best the UWF had to offer … the best of a dozen other feds that had accepted offers from the UWF to compete … and somehow, some-fucking-how, Killer Dude survived, thrived, and destroyed everyone. At Live & Loud … on the UWF’s biggest stage … the longest Cinderella story in UWF history has a chance to finally come to an end. His name still blows … he still gets snickers when he’s not in the room … but the dirty, disgruntled Killer Dude finally has his opportunity to take his place at the very top, the latest in a long line of home grown talent that overcame mediocrity to become legends … and by far the man who came the farthest. All that’s standing in his way is the savior of the UWF … the man who finally rid the world of old “One Testicle” Jamie Shock. At the Regal Rumble, while Killer Dude was erasing six-years of mockery over the course of two hours, Misfit was ending his own Cinderella story … a run to the UWF Championship that had been three years in the making. He’d survived modest beginnings … survived the Local NVA Company … survived Lew Spectre … survived one of the most brutal feuds of all time with Kenny Mercury … survived his own Drowning Pool … survived his own death wishes … and finally came out of it all as the dominant force in the world of wrestling. And on top of all that … the only man in the UWF who might actually be willing to get electrified on that cage just for the pleasure of ruining their match, Slasher, waits in the wings. (T)he (H)ardcore ©hampionship/Insert-Name Here Championship Match Nick Persia (THC) -v- Clyde (INH) ... in a Steelworks Foundry, with both titles plunging to certain doom in a foundry of liquid steel! To retain/win titles, you must save the championships from becoming part of the new line of Steel UWF Figures. Nick Persia is a good buddy of former World Champion Mike Montgomery. Clyde is as well. Though Clyde’s friendship with Montgomery has been more in the way of murderous rampages, death and destruction. Actually, decapitating Montgomery and using his hollowed out skull to defecate in is probably the only thing that even keeps Clyde’s attention at this point. So their match is less of a hug fest than an eternal demon trying to rip the squishy eyeballs out of the reigning king of hardcore’s grinning face. In the first ever Foundry match in UWF history, the two opposing champions will attempt to kill each other, and, if they have the time, save their championship belts from being completely melted into a pool of liquid metal. If Clyde saves the THC title and Persia the INH, the men will swap straps … if one recovers both, he takes both championships. And if they’re too busy attempting to slaughter one another to even care about them, the UWF’s lineup of championships is dropping by two. North American Title UTLC Match James von Doom © -v- Axel Action ... with barbwire chairs, flaming tables, bunches of ladders, and an extra U thrown in for no reason! Up until Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, James von Doom had no problems in his life: With both the North American and Insert-Name-Here Championships, and a rather long run without any losses, von Doom was on easy-street going into Live and Loud. Then his match with the demon known as Clyde happened. Or to be more accurate … the match that was supposed to have Clyde, but instead ended up with von Doom staring into the empty eyesockets of Clyde’s father, the Dark Gimmick. That began a rather bad series of events for von Doom. His arm was broken. He was temporarily paralyzed. He was set on fire. And then Clyde came out. And then he was pinned. And then he lost his championship. Over the course of five minutes, von Doom went from easy-street to a near cripple with only one remaining title, and an incredibly embarrassing loss. After Action’s victory on Supremacy to guarantee his title shot, von Doom has a phenomenally tough task to retain the only title he has left. World Tag Team Title Match The Hollywood Bombshells © -v- Nick Persia & Sledge The UWF’s Tag Team Division has always been a thing of beauty … an incredible wealth of talent in an ocean of wrestling promotions utterly devoid of their own. Yeah. We miss those days. Nevertheless, as the UWF returns to its former standards, the dreams of tag team excellence dance in the background. At the birthday celebration, the remaining gem of tag wrestling, the Hollywood Bombshells, look for any challenge to their championship dominance they can get … and thanks to Mike Montgomery, they have it. With newcomer and former Television Champion Sledge, Nick Persia and the Player’s Club make their first grab at tag team glory, using their Stable Wars tournament prize to go for the gold. Television Championship Match Foster Nackedy © -v- Winner of Wednesday Night Underground Tournament On Supremacy, UWF Commissioner Sam Gordon began her plans to make the once-hot-potato title matter again, and it began simply enough, when Foster Nackedy won two matches in one night to rip the Television Championship away from Sledge. The undefeated Nackedy goes into the biggest PPV of the year with his first championship, and any one of eight possible challengers. On WNU, eight newcomers will attempt to prove themselves: "The Prophet" John Adams, The Demond, "The Beauty" George Ross, Lotus Dragon, Ethan Frost, Rebecca (aka "The Kindergarten Conqueress" Laura Whittle), Jon Plex, and Joshua Taro Freedom. One person will survive the test of the Underground and go onto the Birthday celebration to challenge for Nackedy's championship. Kenny Mercury -v- Kitsune Oni ... in an I Quit Match! Months ago, Kenny Mercury and the Youngbloods ended the career of one of UWF's most beloved and talented wrestlers EVER... Tsunami Oni... Now his boo is all pissed and shit. Kitsune Oni, formerly known as Shiatsu, seeks her revenge against former World Champion, Kenny Mercury. Will Kitsune Oni's quest for justice be fulfilled or will Kenny Mercury's experience and underhanded wank-ass-ho tactics prevail? Trailer Park Luchador -v- Tigera ... with no disqualifications, in a giant, steel monkey cage! The Trailer Park Luchador has had enough and he ain't gonna take it anymore! Enraged at losing ... pretty much every time he ever goes into the ring, he did what any half-way intelligent wrestler would do: He turned heel. On the advise of his Teddy Bear. Who is really just Baron Black Scourge, ebil would-be world conquerer, using a microphone, but really ... like there's any chance TPL will notice that. Assuming that a woman would be an easy fight, the TPL sought out his first victory ... only to be obliterated by Tigera. Enraged over the Luchador from Wisconsin's attempts to get over at her expense -- and because he grabbed her ass -- Tigera now seeks to kill him. ... all that, and so much more~!
