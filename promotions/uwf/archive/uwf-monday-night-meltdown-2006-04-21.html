Monday Night Meltdown, 8 May 2006

21 Apr 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An announcement posted by the UWF's owner, Jaymes Faulkner, on the RPCommunity forum (announcement thread 2853) and e-mailed to the forum's members; this is the e-mail copy, from the Janitor's own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The mail's automatic unsubscribe footer is not carried; the sender's address is marked. It announces a show, the Lethal Lottery, that by the announcement of 9 May 2006 had not been written, and carries the out-of-character rules and deadlines.

The same announcement text, under the show name Monday Night Mojo and without the out-of-character rules, is served at Upcoming show - Monday Night Mojo. Served whole, as written.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: RPCommunity Forums [detail withheld] Date: Fri, Apr 21, 2006 at 8:45 AM Subject: New announcement: Monday Night Meltdown - 8th May 2006 Just one month before the UWF PPV, The Clash, and a new era of the UWF begins as a revamped flagship show, Monday Night Meltdown, is unveiled to the public. In the fine tradition of the UWF the show is guarenteed to be unpredictible ... mostly because ony two people know who will be wrestling, Sam Gordon and Clyde. Gordon due to the fact that she has organised the show and Clyde because he is the only named wrestler booked to appear on in a special one-night tournament for his title in what has been named the 'Lethal Lottery'! The whole roster has been informed that attendance at Monday Night Meltdown is mandatory, failure to comply will result in the quickest firing ever seen in the industry. Sam Gordon will then pull sixteen names out randomly to decide who gets a chance in her Lethal Lottery. Those sixteen men will start the night with only one goal - to make it through to the end and a chance at the title that Clyde has made his very own. He will be fresh and waiting for whoever it is that manages to get through a fatal four way match, a tag match with an unknown partner and opponents and then a match where they have to face their erstwhile tag team partner of just minutes before! ... and then they stop foot in to the ring with the UWFs very own sadistic demon, the 'Burning Man' himself. The INH Champion. Clyde! UWF presents: Monday Night Meltdown special event, the 'Lethal Lottery' Main Event INH Title Match Clyde (c) versus winner of lethal lottery Finals of lethal lottery Winner of tag team semi-final match Semi-final tag team match Winner of Fatal Four Way 1 & Winner of Fatal Four Way 3 versus Winner of Fatal Four Way 2 & Winner of Fatal Four Way 4 Fatal Four Way 4 Fatal Four Way 3 Fatal Four Way 2 Fatal Four Way 1 OOC rules: This is an open tournament, I hope/expect everyone to RP for this as if all they have been told is taht they have to be at the show and that they 'may' get a shot in the lottery/tournament. The decision will be decided on RPs and RPs alone for the lottery, if you RP and RP well then you will be in the match and may even win the whole thing - if you don't then hey at least I will know who is still here :D Deadlines: Friday 5th May at midnight GMT is the deadline for RPing - there is 1 rp minimum from everyone and a 2rp maximum. Saturday 6th May at midnight GMT is the deadline for all segment/angles. Make sure that if you want it included it is 1/ written out properly (not just a sentance saying I want to attack wrestler X) and 2/ actually does something, and is not just there for the sake of it. That said this is a new show so if you want to get your character noticed then go for it ;) Monday 8th May at 5pm GMT is the deadline for matches to be back to me. I won't actually know who is in the matches until the Saturday so if you think that you can help out (PLEASE!) and can guarentee to write a match within two days then volunteer here. If not I can cover them all but they will be smaller matches ;) You can view the full announcement by following this link: http://forum.rpcommunity.com/index.php?topic=2853.0
