Opening Match Memory Loverboy -Versus- [Loverboy kicks Memory’s ass. He hits the Loverpress on Memory. It’s all over… 1…2..3…4… oh wait, you only have to count to 3… oops…] WINNER: LOVERBOY Larson: YEAH BITCH! Rick: HOT BLOODED! LOVERBOY WINS! Larson: Schnoogins! Rick: Next~! Robb Daniels VERSUS The Communist! Larson: Ha-cha-cha-cha… Rick: Robb Daniels equals impressive… Good new guy… He should kick ass, and in high quantities if he keeps it up. Larson: The Communist equals… my baby daddy! Rick: HOODY HOO~! Larson: Daniels seems up to something… He’s been calling some mystery man on the phone. Rick: And The Communist is huge… Russian… and as you have claimed… impregnated you. Larson: Snoogins. Rick: Start the match. Larson: O.K. Rick: DON’T START THE MATCH! Larson: Aw… Rick: GO! Match 2 Robb Daniels The Communist -Versus- [The Communist starts the match very aggresively. He strikes Daniels twice before putting him down with a hard clothesline. Daniels made a small comeback by reversing a powerbomb and going to the top rope for a splash, but The Communist evaded it. After hitting him with consecutive snapmares and spinebusters, The Communist gorrilla presses Daniels, goes to the top rope and hits him with a moonsault. 1...2...3] Rick: And the Communist takes it! The Communist has been very impressive after he formed his alliance with the Local NVA Company and reformed the best stable ever, the Iron Curtain. Larson: The Curtain is back! Rick: Well, just 1/4 of the Curtain... as you know, the Curtain had like a million members at one time. Larson: It's the Iron Curtain! Nobody could beat them! Rick: Earlier in the day, we’ve received something, and we’re going to show it to you all right now. Larson: Well this is a video message from the for now still reigning hardcore champion. Rick: What are you trying to say with that? Nick is an excellent worker, better than some other people here, Larson! Larson: Always taking the cheap shots, Rick. [Screen fades to Universaltron with sometimes voice overs from Rick and Larson] Nick Persia: “Where am I, where am I? That’s the big question for you Adien Pryde. You are hoping for the match not to continue but let me explain you a thing or two here. First off there is no chance in hell that I will give you the hardcore title. Why? I’ve beaten you before. So I won’t give my titles to loser, certainly not sore losers. I‘ve fought all over the world, and I’ve never met anyone who has less respect for his opponent than you do.” Larson: “You have no respect for the Law, hypocrite freak!” Nick Persia: “If you’d think for a minute going into that stretcher match that I worked for a solution to get there you’re wrong. And I got help for finding a suitable solution. That man is Mr. Montgomery. So time is something I don’t have really, but I wanted to get something off my chest. You can pull off all tricks you have. You can continue living your worthless life. You can try to do any more cheap shorts, but realise this: This Sunday there is nowhere to hide, nowhere to run.” Rick: “Teach that little ****” Nick Persia: “One lesson I give you now, another I give you at SAS. You said Americans are better than Dutch people. That you have more right to live and work there than me? Let me say you this........it were the Dutch that layed the foundations for New York. It were the Dutch and other Europeans who started to blank population in America, since it orginally belongs to the indians. So know what you are saying, Adien Pryde. And know what you are doing. Because you are just a copy while I am the orginal. And as we all know.........the orginal always wins.” Larson: “What a bullsh*t. Don’t hide behind something that happened over fourhundred years ago.” Nick Persia: “Because it will be a matter of time........and then your weaknesses will show. Compared to me you are just a fluke. I’m like a darting hitting bull’s eye........I will hit my target. Now you do what you have to do........and I’ll be there at SAS with my residence permit and we’ll see who comes out on top. By the way.......sleep tight and good luck.” [Universaltron fades to black] Larson: “The arrogant fool is just trying to intimidate Adien here.” Rick: “At least he gave him a warning.” Rick: Now, our next match is for the TV Title, where a member of the Resurrection and TV Champ Bondage the Sex Fiend faces off against Brett Philips. Larson: I love when Bondage comes out to wrestle! Rick: And why would that be? Larson: SUGAR AND SPICE! Rick: I should have guessed. Larson: BOOOOOOBS! Rick: Now, to the match! UWF Television Title Bondage the Sex Fiend ( c ) Bret Phillips -Versus- [Bondage comes out to the ring with belt on shoulder. Philips has a determined look on his face. Bondage begins the match with some takedowns and some taunts. The fans are all cheering "SUGAR AND SPICE!" at the top of their lungs. Bondage powerslams Philips and holds him down with a sleeper hold. Philips is getting back in the match but his efforts are in vain. He clotheslines him once and goes for an elbow drop. A theme song is heard on the speakers... it looks like the Ninja Turtles theme song... Yes, El Turtlé runs down ringside and and goes up the ring. He runs towards Philips and attacks him with his shell while Bondage distracts the ref, he leaves and Bondage does the 96. 1..2..3] Rick: Bondage wins with the help of... El Turtlé... Larson: BOOOOBIES! [Sugar and Spice appear on the ramp and show their boobs.] Larson: *drools* Rick: Er... I... um... *gulps* Larson: Now that is what I call entertainment! (The UniversalTron comes to life all of a sudden.  Standing there is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee.  He is wearing a tank top and a pair of khakis shorts.  The scene is looking at him from inside of a jail cell. The wall is gray, and all the “accessories” commonly associated with a jail cell is scene. Kazee is just standing there holding onto the bars.) Kazee:  So Raid, you think that I’ve made a mistake. You know, I’ve made many mistakes in my time.  I’ve screwed up plenty of times in my life.  But fact of the matter is that this time, I have not screwed up. You, Raid, are entering into a realm that I am very familiar with.  You Raid are entering into something that you are probably by no means accustomed to……. You see Raid, before I met you.  Before I ran with you and we dominated the Indy circuit, this was my home.  The steel bars you see here, I saw those every day.  The cold steel toilet, the piece of wood that is laid out and bolted to the wall, the single solitary chair.  This was all I knew for a time.  This is all that my life was.  A twelve foot by twelve foot area contained by brick and steel….. People don’t understand that it’s a fight in here.  People don’t understand that you have to lay it all on the line everyday just to survive.  And we weren’t fighting for a piece of gold on a leather strap.  We weren’t fighting for the admiration of the fans.  We were fighting for our lives.  We were fighting for what we needed to live.  If we wanted a candy bar, there was no walking down to the local 7=11 and buying yourself one.  No, if you wanted a candy bar, you had to beat up the guy who had a candy bar.  That’s how you got a candy bar…… The freedom you have always enjoyed Raid. It’s different.  All you have to worry about is what to use to kill the bugs.  You didn’t have to worry about whether or not some guy was sneaking behind you with a knife to kill you.  You could sit in your lush office or even in your beautiful home and relax.  You didn’t have to be on guard at all times and make sure that your back was protected even though you were in your “home…..” In about 10 days from now, Raid, we will meet in a cell like this.  We will meet somewhere where you will be surrounded by the steel which I have known and had become accustomed too.  You will be entering a realm unfamiliar to you, except for that brief sting that Monty put you behind for, but that was nothing.  That would have been like a vacation for me compared to the time that I spent in here….. You will get a taste of what it feels like to be n here.  You will get a chance to find out what it is like to be locked in an unforgiving place.  There of course are some differences.  In this, you can bring any weapon you want.  You don’t have to worry about some officer strip searching you.  You don’t have to worry about hiding it.  Just bring it on out.  Bring your bat.  Bring you chair.  Bring the damn kitchen sink for all I care.  It’s all legal……. Secondly Raid, another difference, and you should be thankful for this one.  You can escape.  There are three stories to the Tower of Doom.  And the roof is accessible, after all, if it weren’t, one of us couldn’t get the belt.  You can escape this Raid.  Unlike the cell I have been use to Raid, you can leave….. Bottom line though Raid is this.  You say that I’ve made the mistake. Not so.  This was like my second home.  This is where I had spent some of my life….. (Kazee walks a couple of steps to his left, opens the cell door, walks out, and slams it shut.) The difference this time when I am in the cell is I’ll be walking out.  You will be left in the cell watching me as I leave with my gold. (Kazee walks toward the exit of the prison.  The camera follows him toward the exit, which Kazee opens and walks out.) Kazee:  This is definitely one time that you will need all the luck you need. (Kazee slams the door, leaving the camera to stare out through the steel bars at him as we rejoin Rick and Larson.) Rick: Well, now we have our main event! Larson: Who cares? I have to see a Sugar and Spice mud match! Rick: You wish! Larson: Yes i do! Rick: Our main event, Kevin Starr vs The Fool. Larson: That's too easy. Rick: The Fool is a great...er... To the match! Main Event Kevin Starr The Fool -Versus- <The Fool rushes Kevin, telegraphing his move enough for Kevin to land a missile dropkick to the chest. The Fool gets back to his feet just in time for Kevin to Irish whip him into the ropes. On the way back, Starr gets him in an arm drag, taking the Fool back to the mat. The animated puppet rolls back to his feet just to receive the business end of the Rising Starr. With the opponent duly whuped, Kevin Starr pulls the Fool to his feet and executes his Superstar Slam. The Fool, half dead, tries to pie Starr, but Kevin catches it, eats it, then covers the poor Fool for the 1, 2, 3> Rick: Well, it is over! Kevin Starr wins his comeback match against the Fool! Larson: Booooooobies! Rick: We'll see you all at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy! [Scene Fades] [End Transmission.]
