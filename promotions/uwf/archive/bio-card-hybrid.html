UWF bio card: Hybrid

Wrestler's name: Hybrid Face/heel or neutral: Heel Height: 6' 2" Weight: 248 lbs. Hometown: Ottawa, Canada Entrance Music: "Butterfly" by Crazy Town Style: High Flying wrestling. Attention-seeking, flamboyant, controversial attitude. Finisher: The Exclamation Mark Description of Finisher: Irish-whip into Tornado Backbreaker Wrestler's Appearance: Hybrid has a handsome face, mid-length blonde hair with black tints and long slanted sideburns. He often wears black Raybans and a blue headband with a white Japanese sun. He wears a short-sleeved black t-shirt with the Hybrid logo on the front and "So Far In Front Of The Curve I CONTROL The Damn Thing!" on the back in the same, albeit smaller, gold lettering. He is clad in black pants/trousers with a trim of a flashing lights pattern and the same Hybrid logo at the top centre of his trousers/pants. Background Info: Hybrid was born around 26 years ago and lived happily until the age of 4 when his brother was born. He felt the attentions of his parents leaving him and so he degenerated into a rebellious, spiteful child. He has sustained this immature attitude ever since; attention-seeking at every opportunity. A year after his brother's birth, he ran away and became a child actor assuming the fake identity of Adam Finn. He managed to get a fair few top notch bits of work but couldn't reach his full potential due to him being difficult to communicate with. His arrogance never left him despite being under-worked. He was lucky to receive a job at the circus but managed to get fired after acting as though they weren't good enough for him and most noticeably, killing a circus dog with the move now known as The Exclamation Mark. Soon after, a rich tycoon felt sorry for him and liked his persona so took him in and made his life one of riches. Hybrid was waited on night and day. When the tycoon lost all his money after an enormous fine given to him for setting his ex-wife's house alight, Hybrid was rejected by him. So Hybrid now had no house, no money and a feeling that he was an outcast. A few months later, an official for the new wrestling federation, No Nonsense Wrestling, saw Hybrid attack a man on the street who had mocked him for being homeless. This official immediately signed Hybrid to a contract and there he wrestled as the obscure "Marshmellow God" who was a youngster kept in his bedroom by his parents and restricted from things that weren't innocent. Hybrid hated the gimmick and promptly asked for a change. That spawned "Shrapnel" who was a much darker, more serious role. "Shrapnel" managed to win the TV Title there. Again, Hybrid had major problems with the character as he had to wear a mask and he thought that the fans should've seen his face. NNW unmasked him and put renamed him Adam "Shrapnel" Finn. Hybrid got instantly bored in this role as well as he hated to appease the crowd. Before he had chance to complain, NNW had closed. GDW was formed by an NNW official and Hybrid finally got the opportunity to be himself without any restrictions. In GDW Hybrid was absolutely despised by wrestlers and fans alike. He was not afraid to *piss on* any issues, even religion and (strangely enough) homelessness. Hybrid won the TV Title there and many said he would've been GDW's first World Champion.... until the federation closed due mostly to many complaints about Hybrid. After about 9 months of silence, Hybrid had rushed at the chance to enter Chris Williams Memorial Cup 2 but did disappointingly. He entered GIWF and achieved a lot in a relatively short time with big wins over Lew Spectre, Jaxx and was a big part of The Youngbloods 2001 Stable Wars winning team. He arrived in UWF upon GIWF's demise as El Metralla. After a reasonably impressive run, he had to leave to help his wife Julia care for their baby Ben during his partial illness. Metralla returned a couple of months later and denied vigorously that he was Adam Finn. This was revealed to be no lie as Blade was revealed as the one under the mask! Blade and Hybrid formed a tag team known as "The Lost Prophets" and joined the UWF's new incarnation of The Youngbloods.
