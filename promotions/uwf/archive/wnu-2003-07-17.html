Wednesday Night Universal — 16 Jul 2003

16 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Do you think it'll look better around my waist ..."[The Luchador from the Trailer Park quickly swivels around, moving the sweaty towel around as he poses in front of the camera.] "... or over my shoulder?" [TPL swings around, a happy grin on his face, as El Turtlé, the masked Ninja Turtle can only stare.] "They're both bodacious, dude!" [The Luchador swivels around, tying the towel around his waist.] "I think we should stick with the traditional look ... I mean, once we're the UWF's WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS~!, we'll ..." [A giant green finger taps the Luchador on his bare shoulder, making him swing around once more. El Turtlé looks sad.] "Dude ..." [TPL looks happy!] "Dude!" [The joyousness does not rub off. El Turtlé remains sad.] "Dude ..." [TPL is always happy, though.] "Dude!" [Sad.] "Dude ..." [Happy!] "Dude! What is it?" [Sad.] "Dude ... how am I going to get one of those bodacious belts around my shell? I mean ... I don't wanna shout 'Cowabunga' as a soprano!" [TPL's smile half falters.] "Oh man, dude ... that sucks ... there's no way that bad boy will fit around your wais--" "AMERICAN COMEDIC SWINE~!" [Giovanni drops like a bad habit as a barrel-full of EVIL WHITE POWDER OF HORRIFYING AUTROCITIES~! hit him like a sledgehammer.] "DUDE~!!!!!!!" [The Local NVA Company descend on the Trailer Park Luchador. Thai goes after him with a fork ... TPL slaps him away as best he can. Duc poses in the background.] "You will fail, Americans ... you will always fail! Not even the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles and his redneck buddy with no shoes can stop the incredible might of Communism~!" [TPL looks sad as he bitch-slaps Thai away.] "DUDE!" [Trang stabs him ... and the luchador goes down.] "Huh. American swine are getting weaker. Come ... let us steal that Kohler's pants and taunt him like only evil foreigners can, Thai." "Yes! Let us." [They disapear ... and so do we.] Rick: Anyways... Let's just open this show up right with a good old fasioned TV title match. Newly crowned champion Axel Action takes on The Fool in a re-match of their bout at the last edition of WNU where The Fool won. Axel Action won the belt on Sunday, in an amazing upset against Shadow Blade. Larson: Excuse me while I vomit... Rick: So naturally, now Axel faces The Fool, the very man who had just beat him.   Larson: MAN! I don't care...   Rick: Larson... just shut up for a second... Larson: The best thing abuot Axel Action was when after his match with Shadow Blade, Justin Donovan came down and whooped his ass so bad, I could hear Mama Action crying all the way from Canada. Rick: I'd like to think that whoever wins this match and the TV title will be defending against Justin Donovan shorty... Larson: And I'd like to think that this match is over within 20 seconds so I don't have to set my alarm clock... so, maybe you cna just wake me when it's over? Rick: Argh... fine... but I think it's going to turn out to be a good opening match here tonight. Let's get to the ring! Television Title Match Axel Action (c) The Fool -Versus- [Axel Action drops The Fool with a Super kick.] Rick: He's going up top! This could be it! [Axel Action signals to the crowd, then goes for a shoot star press frog splash, his 'Purge' finisher, when suddenly, Justin Donovan hops over the gaurd rail from the audience, and pushes Axel Action just as he leaps. Axel panics and only rotates about 200 degrees, and lands sickly in the middle of the ring as his head and upper body crunch downward in an accordian effect.] Rick: OH MY GOD! WE NEED PARAMEDICS OUT HERE RIGHT NOW! Larson: Hooray! 'Ata boy, Justin! Rick: I think he's broken his neck! [The Fool dances a little jig around the limp body of Axel, before turning him on his back, pieing him in the face, then going for the cover.] ...1....2...3~! The Winner and NEW UWF TV Champion: The Fool [Justin hops off the apron and leaves up the ramp as The Fool celebrates in the ring. EMT's are tending to Axel Action.] Rick: I just can't believe that... Larson: I know, who would have ever though that The Fool would EVER hold a title in the UWF... le alone actually WIN a match... Rick: YOU ASS! I'M TALKING ABOUT AXEL ACTION'S INJURY! Larson: Oh... yeah... that... Rick: Well... Axel Action's being carted away on a stretcher now, I'm being told that we'll be kept updated on the situation as it developes and that we should just move on with the next match... Larson: Bah, who cares! Rick: ...Ignoring you, and moving on... we have a VERY important match up right now pitting Digital Mortality against Sirian. The winner getting a World Championship title shot at SAS. Larson: Digimon world champ again? Rick: It could happen, and this could be the start of that reality. Larson: Sirian's going to tear him apart. Our very own gothic L.U.C.A.B.O.T... Rick: Sirian's been all over the place, he's fought from the low card, battled in the mid card, and challenged the upper card. He's held the (T)(H)(C) belt and this could be the final test for him as far as his position here in UWF. Digital Mortality, a former TV, Intercontinental, and even more impressive, he was World Champion. Not too mention being a hall of famer. If Sirian could win this match... who knows to what heights his career could be boosted to. Larson: And let's just say Digimon beats Sirian. Digital Mortality will continue to cling to his upper card status while Sirian remains a peon in a primate's federation. That sounds a lot more simple to me, so let's just go with that. Rick:...In any case... this should be an awesome match up, LET'S GET IT ON~! Winner Receives World Title Shot on SAS Digital Mortality Sirian -Versus- Winner: Sirian Rick: The Tag-Team Title are on the line next as the Local NVA Company defend against El Turtle/ TPL and Thunder & Lightning. Larson: Bahness... The Local NVA Company need more members! Did you see how many people were at their top secret mission that the entire world saw so it's not really top secret! Two! Duc and Thai! That's it! Where'd Oni and Seymour go? It's just not the same without them. Rick: Suure Larson. Well TPL has been absent as of late... actually for the past few months he hasn't said a word. This may lead to a big disadvantage for El Turtle in this match as The Local NVA Company and Thunder and Lightning seem to be on their A game. Larson: Yes, let's get to the match. World Tag Team Title Match Local NVA Company (c) El Turtlé & Trailer Park Luchador Thunder & Lightning -Versus- Winners: Local NVA Company Rick: And The Local NVA Company takes the win! Larson: Another successful title defense for NOGOOD. Yipee! Rick: Yeah whatever... anyway our Main Event is set to be a great one as the two teams competing tonight are all opponents at the upcoming UWF PPV, Coronation! Larson: Yeah, Mike Montgomery and Kevin Starr will be tagging and Monty has had his eye on Starr's IC Title ever since he beat him for it. Rick: And Seymour Almasy and Petey will be going one on one for the World Title at the PPV. Larson: This is a no brainer, of course Seymour and Petey will take this match. There going for the World Title! Rick: Don't count out Starr or Monty though, their both great competitors... perhaps even the future of the UWF. Larson: Yeah right... Rick: It's true. Anyway, will our competitors be able to put their differances aside for this one match? Or will their anger take over causing them to explode mentally and go nuts on their tag-team partner. Let's get to the match! Main Event -- Tag Team Match Pete the PG Tips Chimp & Seymour Almasy Kevin Starr & Mike Montgomery -Versus- Winners: Pete the PG Tips Chimp & Seymour Almasy * Mike Montgomery Tags out to Kevin Starr [Two Figures appear at the Head of the ramp. One is immediately recognisable to the Crowd, in a flurry of jeers and Cheers, as Joshua Dunn has entered the arena. The other, much shorter figure is beside him, tied at the hands, and a black cloth bag over his head] Com1: "What does that evil bastard want?" Com2: "And more importantly who's that he has with him" [The fighting in the ring stops for a moment as Starr looks towards Dunn almost quizzically. Dunn holds a microphone to his lips] JD: "You wondered how I was going to get into the little IC match you and Monty have there, well this is how." [Dunn lifts up the hood, to reveal the crying, scared face of David, Starr's Nephew] JD: "You want this little guy back, you know what must be Dunn, HAHAHA" [Dunn slings David across his Shoulder and hightails it out of the arena. The Pictures on the Universaltron Screen is replaced with a pre recording of Dunn laughing diabolically. The expression on Starr's face is Agony enough, as he gives chase after the diabolical Scotsman] Com1: "That is just plain sick" Com2: "Or a stroke of genius" Com1: "But Starr's giving Chase" Com2: "Yes, but more importantly he's the legal man" Com1: "So?" Com2: "Look" [Unsure of what else to do, the Referee starts to count out Kevin Starr] 1. Com1: "This just isn't right" [Mike Montgomery can't believe it, but is helpless to stop it. The expression of realization on his face shows that he knows the match doesn't have to end though. Mike immediately charges up the ramp and taps Kevin Starr on the shoulder.] Com1: "What the hell?" [Kevin starts to ask Montgomery what he's doing, but then sees the referee. The referee who's just signaled a tag making Mike Montgomery the legal man.] Com2: "Montgomery's legal! We've still got a match!" Com1: "Yes, but now it's two on one! Montgomery's just volunteered to take on both the Final Fantasy AND our World Champion in what's now a handicap match!" [Mike is seen pointing to backstage and encouraging Kevin to go after his nephew and Dunn, barely heard to say "Family's more important, I got this!" An obviously angry Montgomery charges back to the ring, climbs to the top rope and hits a high cross body on Seymour Almasy, rolls through, and locks in the Eternal Struggle.] ... [End Transmission.]
