February 07th, 2004 !~THE CLASH CARD~! THE CLASH! February 15, 2004 Live from the Houston Astrodome! UWF World Title Match Title Can Change Hands on a DQ or Countout Beezlebozo -c- vs. Ruff Ryder Ruff Ryder is nobody's fool. Knowing entirely well the reputation of Beezlebozo, he had a special stipulation worked into the contract for this UWF World Title Match. Can the winner of the Contest of Champions take down Beezlebozo? And if not, can he force the clown into handing him the title off a DQ? UWF Intercontinental Title Match Match One of the Via Infinito Mount Gagazet Deathmatch II Joshua Dunn -c- vs. Seymour Almasy Two of the UWF's best in one of it's most dangerous matches. Dunn and Almasy have not met in one on one competition since Seymour's return to the UWF last spring. Seymour fights to complete the elusive Grand Slam. Dunn fights for pride, and to elongate the longest UWF title reign, ever. Anything goes on the slopes of Mount Gagazet in a match that should live up to all expectations. UWF Tag Team Title Match Raid and Ezra Kazee vs. Malcolm Kennedy and Mike Montgomery WHAT DA FAWK?! Yes, the UWF has really done it this time. MORTAL NEMESESESESESES as TAG PARTNERS! Raid and Ezra?! Kennedy and Montgomery?! With TAG TITLES on the line? IT'S GONNA BE GREAT! Special Attraction Match Kenny Mercury vs. ?????? On WNU, Kenny Mercury came out and insulted anything that breathed in the UWF. Apparently, the new ownership doesn't take kindly to braggarts, so they gave Mercury a match against...A MYSTERY OPPONENT!! Could it be HARDKORE? Or TPL? OR EVEN EL TURTLE? Or has UWF really lost its mind and found Kenny actual competition? UWF THC Title Match Tables Match Ben Jericho -c- vs. Iron Lung Iron Lung has gone 3-0 in UWF competition thus far, earning himself a shot at the THC Title. But Ben Jericho is no Axel Action. He is one of the greatest competitors to grace a UWF ring. Can the LOW star Lung make his name in the UWF here, or will Jericho stop the rapid THC Title changes, in a match where a table shall determine the outcome? Moraless vs. J.D. Rhine Moraless is back in action in the UWF and has been turning heads with his awesome performances in the ring. Unfortunately, he lost on WNU after being attacked from behind, hitting a small bump in the road to glory. He looks for revenge against the newest UWF superstar, J.D. Rhine... but J.D. isn't your everyday jobbing rookie... Talent Scouts are estatic at the signing of this prodigy, but can he best the man with a troubled past who's ready to climb the ranks of UWF, Moraless?
