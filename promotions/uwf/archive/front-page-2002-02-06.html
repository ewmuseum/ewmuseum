Sunday 24th Febuary 2002 The Universal Wrestling Federation Welcome to the homepage of the UWF, the wrestling world's only premiere wrestling event and the only home in the world of e-wrestling for people who enjoy watching little green men drive giant robots while former prison guards exact revenge by violently shoving a cattle prod into a man's colon. Run-on sentences are appreciated. PWW Invades -- February 2nd, 2002 In the aftermath of Zero Tolerance, the remnants of the lost and mostly forgotten Professional World Wrestling, the wrestling world's premiere second place promotion and former competition (and loser) to the GIWF, have set the wrestling world ablaze. Eight of the industry's biggest stars arrived at the UWF's "only true PPV" on Sunday, laying waste to the new World Champion Shawn Eclipse. Jay Phoenix, Chris Hatchett, Celest, Showtime, Ruff Ryder, Johnny Blaze, Shadow, and Todd Dream became the centerpiece of both the PPV and Wednesday Night Universal, where they made their intentions clear. The gay retreat is off and the PWW is here to stay. No information has been given as to whether or not their first match will be accidentally delayed three months ... though it is implied. Commissioner Enraged -- February 1st, 2002 With Commissioner Lew Spectre's recent string of luck, from his chosen champion Luke Shinoda losing the Shoot Wrestling Championship to the Plague, to Corporal Punishment's massacre at the hands of the returning Mike Montgomery and subsequent loss in the Regal Rumble match for the World title, it's obvious that he would be anything but pleased lately. And with the crowning of Shawn Eclipse, a man Spectre is anything less than pleased with, as the first "deserving" World Champion, it's only become that much more. With his war against hardcore wrestling being a resounding success, and needing something to turn his aggravation towards, Commissioner Spectre has turned towards ridding the UWF of all wrestlers with "The" in their names. It is also assumed he will be ridding the UWF of all single named wrestlers, and possibly all those who use gimmicked titles. It is unknown whether or not Corporal Punishment will now be known as Abernathy Matthews from this point on, but Digital Mortality is said to already be looking for work in the MWF. "Honk if You Love ASS" -- February 1st, 2002 The Wrestling World was stunned on Wednesday Night Universal when Alexander Sebastian Saunders III and colleague Oliver, the backbone of the WNU Commentary team, effectively went on strike mere moments after being given their contracts. The reasoning was lost on most viewers, but that comes as little surprise since Saunders has been acting strangely ever since being jobbed into the ground by a local indie promotion following a "tift" with the management. Saunders had floated in obscurity for months following the close of the GIWF, so why he would go off television again is obviously due to a hormone imbalance. Nonetheless, residents of Chicago have reported several sightings of the former Television Champion over-seeing the picketing process from a Lay-Z-Boy, reading from a dusty book to his colleagues. No reports on whether or not any of the attempted ASSassinations have been successful, despite numberous fans driving their pick-up trucks onto the sidewalk and aiming for him. Singles Division Over-Flow A recent declaraction by UWF President Richard Hayes has effectively closed the Singles Division from further applicants. Luckily for new applicants, the PWWers will most likely find the UWF too stressful and leave for a promotion where cards are once again held monthly. Singles need not click. Tag Team Explosion! The Tag Team Division is currently the only way for wrestlers hoping to hit the big time to make it into the UWF. This decision by Hayes is expected to send the roster exploding with new applicants. When asked in a press conference yesterday, Hayes predicted at least five new teams to be hired by the end of the week. At least two of those teams are expected to release at least one promotion. Tag Teams must click here. Those who do not will have their first born children fed to Studog Escobar. Chat Room Opened In the proof that the UWF caters to its fans first and foremost, the management has set forth to make the company's web-site as user friendly as possible. In showing that the UWF not only leads the world in Pure Wrestling Entertainment but also leads the technology front, a new prototype called "The Chat Room" has been hosted by the site. Using this new invention, UWF fans can speak with each other in real-time using "words" and "sentences." Considering the blazing speed at which words go by, this is no-doubt an attempt to raise the IQ of wrestling fans so that the UWF offices will no longer receive e-mails asking when Luca will return. What future technological improvements this could lead to is anyone's guess. But the UWF will surely remain at the forefront of the civilized world. Initializing...
