UWF's future

9 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An announcement posted by the UWF's owner, Jaymes Faulkner, on the RPCommunity forum (announcement thread 2911) and e-mailed to the forum's members; this is the e-mail copy, from the Janitor's own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The mail's automatic unsubscribe footer is not carried; the sender's address is marked. It puts the fed's future to the roster as a poll after the show announced on 21 April drew only seven roleplays.

Follows the Monday Night Meltdown announcement. Served whole, as written.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: RPCommunity Forums [detail withheld] Date: Tue, May 9, 2006 at 5:51 AM Subject: New announcement: UWF's future OK, on April 13, 2006, at 08:07:17 AM, I posted the details for the next card, which was to take place on Monday 8th May. There was plenty of time to RP - 22 days in fact - with a nice open ended card that gave everyone in the fed the chance to step up and make their own mark on this place. By the deadline there were seven RPs posted, and out of those seven five of them were posted at the last minute (so to speak). Out of the whole roster only seven people could be bothered RPing. That is why there is no show up (yet ... possibly). Neither Ryan nor Chris can do this currently - I don't know when or if they will be back to helping with the fed let alone running it. There are no other full time staff at all here anymore (though there are offers of help and I was intending on recruiting more staff if things picked up). That leaves me running this solo. I have to ask if I can be (or should be) bothered trying to run the place and keep things going if no-one on the roster wants to so am asking the question in the poll. Do we call it all quits right now ... do we keep going in a slump and hope that it picks up sometime ... merge with a fed (I have two in mind, up to you guys if you went along though) ... or do you guys have another idea? I am not here to have fun (though it would be nice if that happened too :) ) I am heer to do what you guys want, so let me know and it will be done. You can view the full announcement by following this link: http://forum.rpcommunity.com/index.php?topic=2911.0
