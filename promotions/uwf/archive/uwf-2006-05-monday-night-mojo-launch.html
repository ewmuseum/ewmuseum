Upcoming show - Monday Night Mojo

21 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just one month before the UWF PPV, The Clash, and a new era of the UWF begins as a revamped flagship show, Monday Night Mojo, is unveiled to the public. In the fine tradition of the UWF the show is guarenteed to be unpredictible ... mostly because ony two people know who will be wrestling, Sam Gordon and Clyde. Gordon due to the fact that she has organised the show and Clyde because he is the only named wrestler booked to appear on in a special one-night tournament for his title in what has been named the 'Lethal Lottery'! The whole roster has been informed that attendance at Monday Night Mojo is mandatory, failure to comply will result in the quickest firing ever seen in the industry. Sam Gordon will then pull sixteen names out randomly to decide who gets a chance in her Lethal Lottery. Those sixteen men will start the night with only one goal - to make it through to the end and a chance at the title that Clyde has made his very own. He will be fresh and waiting for whoever it is that manages to get through a fatal four way match, a tag match with an unknown partner and opponents and then a match where they have to face their erstwhile tag team partner of just minutes before! ... and then they stop foot in to the ring with the UWFs very own sadistic demon, the 'Burning Man' himself. The INH Champion. Clyde!
