UWF wrestler profile: Seymour Almasy

What this is The profile page of Seymour Almasy on the UWF’s own site (cgi-bin/singles.cgi, name=seymouralmasy), as the Wayback Machine captured it: the profile (6 November 2004). The profile lists, as far as it was filled in, the wrestler’s vitals, theme music, hometown, biography, moveset and quotes, and a handler block; the handler block credits Sean Williams by name, and the messenger name it prints is marked as withheld. The page’s navigation and background are not carried. The page itself carries no date.
W R E S T L E R P R O F I L E Date Of Birth: Unknown, but is 23 years old. Height: 5'7' Weight: 181 lbs. Alignment: Heel Theme Music: 'Fight With Seymour' by THE BLACK MAGES Home Town: Highbridge, Bevelle T I T L E H I S T O R Y UWF Title History: UWF Awards History: Other Titles: Other Awards: B I O G R A P H Y Titles and Awards Held: UWF Television Title (2x), UWF World Tag Team Title (2x, with Max Queue (RPQ~!) and with ex-wife Aerith Heartilly), UWF THC Champion, UWF World Heavyweight Champion (2x), Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Bracket Champion, Primetime Invitational Tournament Winner, PTC Global Champion, PTC Cruiserweight Tournament Winner, PTC Match of the Year 2003 (Versus Roulette), UWF Face of the Year 2002, UWF Gimmick of the Year 2003, UWF Wrestler of the Year 2003 M O V E S E T Wrestling Style Cruel Submissionist/High Flyer Basic Moves After an extended period of time off, Seymour Almasy has practically shelved his entire moveset. With a few notable holdovers/exceptions, the man once know as the Final Fantasy has tightened his moveset up, and created a much more torturous style, which he has dubbed “Birth of God” Wrestling Style: Birth of God The Birth of God style is based around controlling the contest. While good, Almasy would take tremendous amounts of punishment, only to come back with quick, sudden moves to win. In Birth of God, Almasy focuses on wearing down the neck/head, arm, or leg of an opponent, and then striking a suitable signature blow. Neck/Head: Seymour is not an immensely strong man. However, UWF has athletes smaller than most other promotions, allowing Seymour to use a more power based offense than he would elsewhere. Against opponents under 230 pounds, this is his most likely focus area. Against heavier opponents, he may use the tactics here to stun them, but would likely try to work a limb for a submission. Basic moves: Various DDTs, Various hurricanranas, clothesline to the back of the neck, head scissors, brain buster, Michinoku Driver, etc Signature Moves: Terror of Zanarkand- A top rope knee drop delivered to a face-down opponent. It is a precision strike, designed to further weaken the neck. Often used as a setup to one of Almasy’s more dangerous moves. Shadow Flare- A Double-Arm Tornado DDT. Always followed by a cover. Light of Judgment- Full Nelson Front Russian Legsweep. Usually done ala Chris Jericho’s Breakdown, but on taller foes, Almasy will get on the second rope to execute it. Fallen Wings- Sitout Pedigree The Heretic Condemned (Setup)- Throat-Clutch STO Time Compression (Finisher)- A straitjacket piledriver. On occasions where Almasy is extremely mad at an opponent, he will execute it off the ring apron to the floor, or through a table. Shockwave Pulsar (Finisher)- Shoulder mounted fireman’s carry to a Fisherman’s Buster. Picture the F-5, except ending in the Buster. Not a common move due to the strength it requires. One Hit Kill (Super-Finisher)- The deadliest weapon in the Almasy arsenal. Seymour sets the opponent up for a piledriver, but steps over both arms, and drops to his knees, piledriving his unprotected opponent into the canvas. Arms: Arms are considerably weaker than legs, so Seymour given a choice, will work an arm rather than a leg. However, he has fewer tactics for punishing the arm, so what he gains in ease, he loses in predictability. Basic moves: Fujiwara armbar, cross arm breaker, hammer locked body slam, knees/elbows/leg drops to arm, kimura WWind- A Dragon Screw, except executed on the arm. Multiple WWinds are often chained together. Total Annihilation (Finisher)- Seymour will pull an opponent lying on his back to a seated position, and then step over his opponent’s right arm, bracing the left leg against the opponent’s neck. Almasy then simply pulls up with an armbar until he gets the submission. Fallen One (Finisher)- Almasy’s sleeper hold/leg trap armbar. Known as an arm submission because the arm portion works faster than the sleeper. Pain (Finisher)- Figure Four Arm Lock Legs: Almasy has many basic techniques that he can use to work the leg of an opponent. He’ll work the leg if he has an opponent with an evident leg weakness, or if he wants to take a larger opponent off of his feet. Basic moves: Dragon screw leg whip, elbows/leg drops/knee drops to leg, ankle lock, Shawn capture kneebar, figure four leglock Paralyze- A simple hard round kick to the back of a knee. Hell’s Judgment- Seymour bends an opponent’s leg around the ringpost, and pulls back in order to torque the joint. Pyramid (Finisher)- The Haas of Pain Almasypocalpse (Finisher)- Seymour’s favorite finisher due to the sheer sadism involved. With an opponent down, Almasy grabs hold of an opponent’s knee, and repeatedly rams it into the mat. He will variate with occasional punches and other blows to the knee, always going back to the joint, forcing the opponent to eventually submit or risk complete destruction of the knee joint. Signature Moves The Heretic Condemned (Throat-Clutch STO) Finisher Time Compression/Shockwave Pulsar, et al.
