Wednesday Night Universal — 5 May 2004

We’ve got four great matches lined up for you tonight! Larson: Woo! Late Night television air slots rule! You know we could probably do anything we want… say anything… do any type of profane or explicit act… and no one will know! This is the worst time slot in T.V. history… Rick: Yeah but… er… yeah, you’re right. Just don’t encourage them! This is UWF! Where EVERYTHING and ANYTHING can happen… Larson: AND USUALLY DOES! YEAH BABY! Rick: Who’s this? 'Time To Play The Game' by Motorhead hits the PA system and after a few seconds Bret Phillips appears from the behind the stage curtain and at the top of the ramp with water bottle in hand. He looks towards the ring with a mean stare and begins to walk with a purpose towards the ring tipping water onto the top of his head as he walks. He reaches the side of the ring and takes a swig of water from the bottle before throwing it into the crowd. Phillips then jumps onto the appron and faces the crowd with his head down and waits for the cue in the music. Phillips then raises his head and arms and spits water vapour into the crowd he then looks at the crowd with a mean stare for a while and spits a little more water vapour out before entering the ring and approaching the far left turnbuckle. Phillips climbs to the second rope and lifts his arms above his head and adopts a mean look on his face. Phillips then drops down from the turnbuckle and demands a mic from ringside and he snatches one from the grasp of the ring announcer. “So here I am on Jobber TV and of course I have a jobber to get out the way before I’m taken seriously. Well that’s ok because when you see me step into that ring tonight you’re going to see a annihilation of the highest standard and you’re going to see why I am so damn good. Kyle Cross you better hope I am in a good mood because I could end your career before it’s even started.” Crowd chant “asshole” “What is wrong with you losers? (Crowd boo) Can’t you see the best damn wrestler in this business is standing before you? Why won’t you respect me? Who gives a piece of crap what you losers think anyway? (Crowd boo) You people back there in the locker room and you people on Sunday Night Supremacy make sure you watch my match and see the potential standing in front of you. I am Bret Phillips and I don't give a damn if you're 2 ft nothing, I don’t give a damn if you’re 11ft tall, because there is not one opponent I will ever or have ever met that I can't beat.' Phillips tosses the mic down and exits to the back. Larson: Aaaaand… who the hell was that? Rick: Seems to be Bret Phillips… A new recruit here in the UWF. He’ll be in action later on tonight… Larson: Seems… confident. Rick: Right. Let’s just kick the night off. Larson: Opening the show… Gregory versus The Communist. Rick: Gregory hit the UWF a couple weeks ago and scored an impressive victory against Dante and PhD… since then, he hasn’t been heard, but rumor has it he’s been appearing on 4WW television… Larson: The federation run by Nephilim? Ewwww… Rick: Nephilim was one of the greatest competitors to eveer grace our squard circle, and one of the best World champs of all time! Larson: BAH! Gimme good old Tsunami Oni or Al Crud over him any day! So now Gregory has jumped ship to 4WW? Rick: UWF’s contracting guidelines do not restrict wrestlers from competing in other federations… Larson: Yeah well… I hope Commie-boy whoops his Gregor-ian ass! Opening Match Gregory The Communist -Versus- [The match started off with The Communist going on the offensive immediately. Using his huge size as an advantage, The Communist easily tossed Gregory around the ring for a few minutes with some massive hip tosses, high angle slams, air plane spins, and gorilla press slams… big moves like those. Suddenly… ] [Two shadowy figures hop the guardrails and slink around the ring apron. They slip up… one going on either side of the ring. Suddenly, one hops up on the apron and starts shouting some gibberish, jumping up and down, and waving his arms around wildly. The referee catches a glimpse of it from out of the corner of his eye and he turns to see what the hell is going on. As this is happening, the other figure leaps into the ring, and blasts Gregory in the face with a fistful of the… WIHTE POWDER OF DOOM~! The figure rolls out of the ring, and The Communist grabs up the recoiling Gregory and destroys him with the communist slam. He covers him and the referee, turning back around, goes for and completes the 3 count!] [Winner: The Communist.] [The two figures hit the ring once again… They are none other than the Local NVA Company, consisting of Duc Chu Song and Thai Trang… the five time UWF World tag team champions and hall of famers. Duc Chu Song pulls out a microphone.] Duc: Well! UWF! Did you not just see that!? Thai: Of course they did! Not everyone’s eyes are as squinty as yours! Duc: PHUC OFF, THAI! UWF! Did you just witness that? THAT! Ladies and gentle-capitalists… was… TEAMWORK! Thai: Honestly! Duc: And TEAMWORK… is the essence… of COMMUNISM! Thai: Honestly! Duc: And Communism is the essence… of… Um… us! THE LOCAL NVA COMPANY! Thai: No shit, Sherlock Holmes-Chi-Minh! Duc: The greatest tag team alive… honestly… We, the Local NVA Company have noticed something… And that is that Stable Wars is coming up… Wow, it is coming up isn’t it? Thai: Yes, I noticed it earlier… Duc: RIGHT! And what has evaded the greatest tag team alive for two years in a row now? Thai: The Stable Wars Cup! Duc: THAT’S RIGHT! And it hasn’t been our faults! We’ve always been paired with DISMAL partners… scumbag capitalist loser NO BODIES like Tsunami Oni, Seymour Almasy, Digital Mortality, and Joshua Dunn…. That’s the bottom of the barrel right there, pigs, THE SCUM OF THE FEDERATION! Not even good even to whipe Loverboy’s ass. Thai: What about ‘Mysterious’ Matt Jones and A.S.S. III? Duc: I don’t remember no ‘mysterious’… And everyone already knows that A.S.S. sucked! They don’t need to be reminded! Thai: Honestly… Duc: This year however shall be different… much much different. Brother Thai-san here, and I are going to form the GREATEST STABLE ALIVE! Thai: HONESTLY~!!!!!! Duc: And the first recruit to our Stable… who else? THE COMMUNIST!!! The Communist: It’s time to rise up… the communist party has called us to action! Thai: Damn skippy! Duc: Welcome abroad, comrade. A true communist right here, people… you better believe it… The Iron Curtain is descending once again! Thai: THE IRON CURTAIN IS BACK! Duc: HONESTLY! Duc & Thai: EEEEHEHEEHEHEHEHEHEHEHEHEEHHEEHEHEHEHEHE~!!!! The Communist: Grrrr… Duc: And now! AWAY! And don’t worry, UWF… we shall be recuiting our fourth member soon! Buh-bye! [Duc and Thai scurry off, cackling to themselves. The Communist lumbers slowly behind them silently.] Rick: Good Rod… Larson: The Iron Curtain is back! I can’t believe it! Rick: I think it was pretty obvious that the Local NVA Company and a guy called ‘The Communist’ were pretty much inevitably going to team up at some point… Larson: Oh, you shut up… this is huge! We’ve just gotten another team for Stable Wars… The competition has just gotten that much more heated. Rick: Right. But! We’ll have more on that whole deal as it unfolds throughout the week. Next up is a debut match, featuring two brand spanking new UWF superstars. Kyle Cross and Bret Phillips. Larson: Bret Phillips already graced us with his divine frigging presence… what a cocky bastard… Rick: While Kyle Cross… well… hasn’t done much at all. Larson: I hope Cross breaks every bone in Phillips’ body! Rick: Yay, meaningless carnage! Let’s get to the ring. Debut Match Kyle Cross Bret Phillips -Versus- [The match began with each man in his first ever UWF match. Each was out to show the UWF viewers what they were made of. They locked up, and Phillips started the match up with a quick uppercut. Phillips whipped Cross into the ropes and hit a high knee into Kyle’s face. Kyle would return fire with a dropkick. The two squared off again.] Rick: Neither man with a clear advantage… yet… [For a few minutes, the two would trade off basic brawling moves. Bret punches Kyle, Kyle punches back… this went on for a little while. The tides turned when Bret pulled a DDT out of nowhere, totally taking everyone by surprise. Kyle Cross would eventually struggle to his feet… Only to be hit with a spinning kick to the stomach, doubling him over. Bret Phillips would execute his finisher, The Lockdown. Afterwards, Kyle Cross lay prone on the mat. Phillips showed off to the crowd, then covered Cross. The referee would count… 1…2…3!] [Winner: Bret Phillips.] Rick: It’s all over! Bret Phillips wins in his debut match! Larson: That was quick… Phillips goes in and gets out… he doesn’t mess around. Rick: Indeed. By the looks of the way he man-handled Cross in there, Bret Phillips should have a very prosperous career in UWF. Larson: What about Kyle Cross? Rick: Er… I dunno! That depends on what his next move is… does he roll over and quit, or get his butt in gear? I think his future is totally up to him. Larson: Right… Rick: Moving on to our third match… We have Anarchy taking on a new tag team known as Evolution. Larson: This new team… Evolution… They attacked Draco Paladine and Anrachy on SAS, did they not? Rick: Indeed. Jefferson and Robb Daniels, the members of Evolution, jumped Chaos Society in the ring on Supremacy, while Anarchy introduced Draco as the new member of Chaos Society, thus setting up this encounter between the two teams. Larson: Obviously, Anarchy would want some revenge after that assault. Rick: And Evolution would love to kick off their UWF careers with a win. Larson: Anarchy needs to keep their focus. Adien Pryde has been having troubles with THC champ Nick Persia. He must keep his attention and concentration on THIS MATCH. He’s not going to be doing his partner, Reo Dai, any favors if he doesn’t. Rick: And we’re not sure what Evolution can do… I guess its time to stop all the talk and let the actions take center stage. Larson: LET’S DO IT! Tag Team Match Anarchy Evolution -Versus- [Adien Pryde came down to the ring wheeling a stretcher along with him, sending a message to THC champ, Nick Persia, to whom he had challenged to a stretcher match. Joanna accompanied the two Chaos Society members down to the ring. Robb Daniels opted to start off the match for Evolution. Adien Pryde started for Anarchy. Robb Daniels kicked things off with a fireman’s carry, then getting Pryde in a reverse chin lock. Eventually Adien Pryde fought out of it with some elbows. Pryde would counter-attack hitting a superkick on Daniels. Adien went for the cover, but Jefferson intervened, breaking up the count. Pryde dragged Daniels over to his corner, and tagged in Reo Dai. Pryde held Daniels as Reo threw a combo of punches into Robb’s face. Adien stepped out onto the apron. Reo Dai was now on the offensive. He hit Daniels with some basic brawling moves, then whipped him into the ropes. Dai caught Robb in mid stride and broke him in two with a lightning quick tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Reo went for the cover… 1…2..kickout! Close but no cigar. Reo hit Daniels with a vertical suplex. Reo Dai climbed the ropes and leapt off, attempting a body splash… but Daniels rolled out of the way! Robb had turned the tide. Daniels German suplexed Dai, but only got a 2 count out of it. Robb Daniels executed a piledriver on Reo Dai, then tagged out to Jefferson. While they were doing that, Reo Dai rolled over to his corner and tagged out to Adien Pryde.] [Suddenly the lights turn quickly off and back on. Then we see Nick Persia holding his UWF The Hardcore Championship belt. He starts to talk while the match is still continuing] Nick Persia: “Adien Pryde.......You made me a challenge? Hardcore title on the line? In a strechter match? Well what can I say......you’re either gone nuts or you really have a game plan. My answer to answer to your challenge is Yes. I accept your challenge to a match. By the way, Good luck because you are going to need it!” [Nick Persia disappears from the titantron] Rick: He’s taken up the challenge! [Adien Pryde is distracted by the Universal-tron and Persia’ message, and Jefferson takes full advantage, charging Pryde. At the last second, Pryde notices Jefferson and kicks him in the stomach. Pryde executed the Falcon Driver and goes for the pin… 1..2…3!] [The Winner: Anarchy] Larson: And Anarchy wins! [Suddenly, Draco Paladine hits the ring.] Rick: Draco is here! [Robb Daniels charges Draco, but Draco catches him and choke slams him. Jefferson struggles to his feet… only to be choke slammed.] Draco Paladine: Don’t play with fire… unless you want to be burned… [The Chaos Society exits the arena.] Rick: Well… I guess that’s a little revenge from Sunday! Larson: Right… wait… isn’t that Ezra’s music? Rick: Huh? (“You Just Don’t Know Me At All” goes over the PA system as the fans stand and cheer the arrival of “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee who seems to be focused, but also seems to be somewhat back to his old self high-fiving the fans and playing to the crowd. He is in his maroon and gold wrestling singlet and looks like he is ready for action.) Rick:  Wonder what Kazee is out here for.  He’s not scheduled for a match. Larson:  He’s probably just out here to show off. (Kazee jumps in the ring and is handed a microphone by the ring announcer.) Kazee:  It’s great to be out here on Wednesday Night Underground.  This show has a special place in my heart.  And it’s great to see it back. (The crowd pops.) Kazee:  So I suppose I wonder why y’all are out here.  I mean, obviously, I don’t have a match. At least not one that is listed. See, this Sunday, I have a match against one of the greatest technical wrestlers in GIWF and UWF history. Kenny Mercury is the definition of precision.  Well, honestly, I’m not to shabby myself in that department.  As I’ve said before, and I’ll say it again, I’ve forgotten more moves than most people ever knew. So that brings me to the reason I’m out here tonight…… I thought I would come out here because A.) I love performing in front of y’all and B.) I thought I would show off my technical skills a little bit before this Sunday.  So here is what I have set up.  I’ve set up 5 different men who are all looking to get into the UWF and are all looking to make a name for themselves.  So I’m not only going to give them a chance, but I thought I would show off my technical knowledge by “running the gauntlet” so to speak.  I will be each of the 5 competitors with different submission moves.  Some you know, some you don’t know, and in the end, it’ll all be good.  So send out the first guy. (A rather tall and lengthy younger guy makes his way out to the ring.  He has blond hair and is wearing a pair of red tights.  He slides into the ring and looks fairly excited.  The bell rings and the rookie charges across the ring. Kazee sidesteps him and sweeps the leg out from under him.   The kid stands up and charges again and again he gets his leg sweeped out from under him.  The kid stands up again.  Kazee and the kid start circling each other. The kid goes for a quick kick on Kazee.  Kazee grabs the leg and reverses it into a quick dragon screw. Kazee holds onto the legs and places the kid into a standing figure four leg lock. Before cinching it in, he grabs the head of the kid and makes him sit up, his head locked in front of Kazee’s legs and his arms locked behind the legs.  Kazee then locks in the move and starts wrenching on it.  The kid quickly starts tapping.  As soon as the kid starts tapping, Kazee releases the maneuver.) Rick:  Well, for those of you who don’t know that move, that was called The Thievery.  A devastating maneuver that Kazee used when he first entered the UWF as Mr. Meaner. (Kazee shakes the guys hand as the second guy makes his way up.  This guy is shorter and quite a bit more muscular than the first guy. He has brown hair and blue trunks. The first guy exits the ring as the second guy jumps in the ring and starts circling Kazee.  They go to lock up, but before they get in a collar and elbow, the guy knees Kazee in the gut.  Kazee is taken by surprise and doubles over. The guy whips Kazee into the ropes.  As Kazee comes back, the guy bends over to set up for a back body drop.  Kazee takes quick advantage of the situation and hits the guy with a swinging neck breaker.  Kazee picks the guy back up and executes a hangman’s neck breaker on the guy.  He then flips the guy over so he is belly down, crosses the guy’s legs at the shins.  Kazee then interlaces one of his legs into the guy’s legs and bridges backwards, grabbing a hold of the guys head and pulling upwards in a reverse STF.) Larson:  The Common Man seems to be pulling out all the stops tonight.  That right there is the second finisher that he used in the UWF under the guise of Mr. Meaner.  That right there is the Jailhouse Break.  Quite possibly even more painful than The Thievery. (The second guy taps out and Kazee releases the move, helps him to his feet and shakes his hand. The third guy makes his way to the ring area.  This guy is quite a bit shorter and is wearing a mask.  He is wearing psychedelic trunks and quite possibly looks like a luchador.    The second guy jumps out of the ring as the masked man has made his way to the top rope where Kazee hasn’t noticed him yet.  As soon as Kazee turns around to notice him, he gets hit with a missile dropkick flush on the jaw. Kazee goes down as the masked man goes for an Asai moonsault.  Kazee reacts at the last moment and raises his knees to counter the move.  Kazee picks the luchador up and whips him into the ropes, but is surprised, by a hand spring elbow to the face.  The luchador goes to the top rope once again, but takes a little too much time, as Kazee is back on his feet.  He runs to the ropes, climbs up them quickly, and hits the luchador with an overhead belly to belly suplex but holds onto an arm in the execution of this maneuver.  As soon as both men hit the mat, Kazee rolls the guy over and locks him into a Crippler Crossface.) Rick:  A tribute so to speak to his stable mates.  Montgomery calls it the Power Play.  Dunn uses a variant of it and calls it the St. Andrews Crossface.  Kazee truly is showing his technical know how here. (The luchador taps out. Kazee releases the hold and shakes the man's hand as a fourth competitor comes out. This guy is huge.  Short and stocky with balding hair and a full beard and mustache.  This guy stalks into the ring and stands before Kazee.) Larson:  This man is huge.  What can Kazee do to him? (The man ties up with Kazee and quickly hits him with a belly to belly suplex, absolutely crushing Kazee.  The man then drops a huge elbow on Kazee, further inflicting damage on him.  Kazee looks in bad shape as the man picks Kazee up.  The man whips Kazee into the ropes and attempts to clothesline Kazee as he comes back.  Kazee ducks this and springboards off the far ropes and hit the guy with a flying clothesline of his own, which only staggers the man. Kazee runs off the ropes again and again hits the man with a flying clothesline, but the man still will not go down.  A third time Kazee springboards off the ropes, and this time hits a leaping leg lariat on the big man, which brings him to one knee. Kazee shrugs his shoulders and point to the top turnbuckle, which he quickly ascends. As soon as the guy gets up and looks toward the turnbuckle Kazee is on, Kazee leaps off and performs the Bomb De Commonwealth, riding the big man all the way down.) Rick:  BOMB DE COMMONWEALTH!!  WHAT A WAY TO GET THIS HUGE MAN OFF HIS FEET. (Kazee scissors the opponent's head and leg together, and gets on his hands in a pushup position out to the side of them and arches his back.  The man starts tapping out.) Rick:  Kazee sending a message so to speak to Mercury. That’s Mercury’s Leg Cradle that he just used there.  A move we haven’t seen in a long time.  And Kazee seems to have executed it to perfection. And here comes the final challenge. (Kazee shakes the man’s hand as he exits the ring.  The final man comes down to the ring.  This guy towers over everyone at near 7 feet.  He steps over the top rope as the fat man exits the ring.  Kazee decides to take the fight to this 7 footer, but receives a boot to the face for his troubles. The 7 footer than picks up Kazee and lifts him up in a Gorilla Press and then unceremoniously dumps Kazee behind him.  The huge man then continues his assault by propping Kazee in the corner and choking him with his huge foot. Kazee is finally released by the big man.  The big man sets Kazee up in the middle of the ring and runs at him full steam Kazee side steps him and hits him with a drop toe hold, sending the man flat on his face. Kazee quickly explodes onto the man's legs, placing him in a spinning toe hold.  Kazee quickly moves up to the arm, twisting the around his leg and rolling his opponent over onto his stomach.  He then secures the arm behind his knee and grabs a firmer hold on the man’s leg, wrenching it backward.) Rick:  Tequila sunrise.  Definitely something that hasn’t been seen here in a long time. (The big man tapes out.  Kazee helps him to his feet and shakes his hand. Kazee then grabs the mic.) Kazee:  That is just a small sample of my technical expertise.  I think I have more than proved that move for move I can go with Mercury.  Now this Sunday, we will find out who is truly the better man that night. (With that, Kazee hands the mic to the ring announcer as his theme song hits.) Larson: Mercury versus Kazee for the Intercontinental title! THIS SAS! Don’t miss it, it shall rock! Rick: Indeed! And now to our main event of the evening! Raid is defending his Television title against Bondage. Larson: Here we go! Main Event Television Title Match Raid Bondage the Sex Fiend -Versus- They started of by circling the ring; chain wrestling to kick of the match. The early minutes of the match went back and forth with neither really getting the upper hand. The worked a mat-based match the first minutes of the match with some pin attempts by both competitors. After about six minutes Bondage gained the upper hand and he was in control of the next three minutes of the match with several supplexes and other power moves. Some near falls followed but Raid was unable to score the pin. Bondagestarted to fight back and around the 15 minute mark he was clearly in control. Bondage managed to hit the 96 trade mark move  at 17 minutes but was unable to score the pin because Raid put his foot on the rope just in time. The match continued and once again went back and forth. At the 20 minute mark Nick Persia came walking down the entrance ramp as Bondage hit another 96 vertebreaker. But instead of going for the pin, Bondage noticed Persia. He stood watching towards Nick Persia was he stood at ringside. The referee was warning Persia not to get involved. Behind the back of Bondage and the referee Persia slide into the ring from ringside just as Raid regained his strength. Persia quickly connected with the Persia Grunchwench Suplex on Gekko and left the ring. Bondage and the refree quit their decision. Bondage  and the referee turned around while Raid  was down and out. Bondage lifted up Raid and hit a third 96 for the victory in about 25 minutes of action. Winner and NEW TV Champion: Bondage the Sex Fiend Rick: HE DID IT! WE HAVE A NEW T.V. CHAMP! Larson: Bondage holds UWF gold for the first time! Rick: Bondage doing something for the first time? That’s pretty rare… I’m pretty sure he’s done it all… Larson: Not funny… Rick: Thank you everyone, for Larson, I’m Rick… GOODNIGHT! SEE YOU AT SUPREMACY! [Feed Cut.]
