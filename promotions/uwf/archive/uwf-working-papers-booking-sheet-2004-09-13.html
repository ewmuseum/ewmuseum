Working papers: the booking sheet for the week of 13 to 19 September 2004

13 September 2004 (file date)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A booking sheet from the promoter's own drive, 90 words of match names and nothing else: a Thursday card (headed "ThursdayNU" in the file) and a Sunday Afternoon Supremacy ("SAS") card, booked for the week of 13 to 19 September 2004. It is the only record that cards were being booked for that week; it does not say whether the shows aired as booked. It is a planning paper, not a card.

Belongs to no show in the record. Served whole, as written.
