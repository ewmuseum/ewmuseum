The profile lists, as far as it was filled in, the wrestler’s vitals, theme music, hometown, biography, moveset and quotes, and a handler block; the handler’s name there reads first name and last initial, and the messenger names and e-mail addresses it prints are marked as withheld. W R E S T L E R P R O F I L E Date Of Birth: February 9, 1980 Height: 6 foot 4 Weight: 212 lbs Alignment: Neutral Theme Music: Trapt's 'Headstrong' Home Town: Halifax, Nova Scotia Canada T I T L E H I S T O R Y UWF Title History: UWF Awards History: Other Titles: Other Awards: B I O G R A P H Y Sellout to some...ambitious bastard to just about everyone else, Mike Montgomery has reached the position he's in through taking advantage of opportunities. After having contract after contract breached by the UWF, Mike sued the company...and won. He received 50 million dollars from the company as well as a position as the Executive Vice-President of Talent Relations. This lead to the formation of 'The Player's Club.' The Player's Club consists of people who know how 'the game is played' and are willing to do whatever they have to to get ahead. People who recognize that wrestling is more than simply about getting the win. He uses his backstage influence to ensure that 'his boys' get the biggest pushes and an extra zero added to their contract. Now primarily an administrator, Mike may well be back in the ring soon, despite recently taking a leave of absence from active competition. After all, Jamie Shock can only be allowed to bring TV ratings down for so long... M O V E S E T Wrestling Style Highflyer/Martial-Artist Basic Moves Mike's moves of choice include the following: 'What Goes Around Comes Around' (619) 'The Eternal Struggle' (Ankle Lock) 'Power Play' (Crippler Crossface) 'Lightning Strike' (Super-Kick) 'The Burninator' (Diamond Cutter set up where instead Montgomery runs up the ropes, flips over his opponent, and executes a tornado DDT in mid-air) 'The Executive Decision' (flying elbow) 'Ask the Oracle' (Hurricarana start, then float over into a standing Rings of Saturn 'Dead Aim' (A Corkscrew Moonsault into a DDT) A prolific cruiserweight and martial artist, Mike's attack relies heavily on speed and the ability to get airborne. A basic move list would include the following: Swinging Neckbreaker Spinning Heel kick Hurricarana Corkscrew Moonsault Spear Rolling Thunder Top Rope Elbow Enzuiguri Sharpshooter Dragonsleeper Tarantula Tornado DDT When in the ring, Mike 'talks' with his feet, often using his long legs to bludgeon his opponents before they can get within reach of him. He's also known for taking a great many risks and will pull out the following dangerous maneuvers from time to time: Top Rope Martial Arts Kick 'Fall From Grace' (Frog Splash) 'The Executive Decision' (Flying Elbow Drop) 'Flames of Redemption' (An actual fireball) Signature Moves The Hangman's Joke/Destiny Manifest (A Diamond Cutter/The Twist of Fate) Finisher Fall from Grace (Five Star Frog Splash) H A N D L E R Name Mike M.
