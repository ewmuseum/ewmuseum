Ethan Frost, howling at the pain the weight of Joshua Taro Freedom is causing his arm, gathers up the last of his energy to leap up and drop his opponent headfirst to the canvas!] Rick: Frost Bite! Frost Bite! Frost Bite! [Drained of everything he has left, Ethan Frost throws himself on top of Joshua Taro Freedom and hooks his leg with his remaining good arm…] One… Two… Three! [The arena erupts into cheers as the referee makes the final count!] Tommy Waters: Your winner… and number one contender for the UWF Television title… ETHAN FROST!!! [The ref goes to raise the hand of the victor, but Ethan Frost shakes his head. Instead he wearily climbs to the second rope, basking in the glory received from the UWF fans.] Rick: He’s done it! Ethan Frost is going to Live & Loud! Larson: No! JTF totally dominated! That was a fluke! Rick: Fluke or not, Ethan Frost will be taking on Foster Nackedy for the UWF Television title! The question is... the gauntlet he just went through, the pain Joshua Taro Freedom inflicted on him… will Ethan Frost be anywhere near his best on Sunday to win gold? We’ll find out at Live & Loud! [End Transmission.]
