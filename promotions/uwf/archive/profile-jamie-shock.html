B I O G R A P H Y He did it all. Entered the UWF as a rookie, as Toxic Shock, with his girlfriend, and hoped of just 'making it OK' as a normal, average, superstar. He ended up being, possibly, the most hated man in UWF history. How? ..I mean.. look back in the summer of 2001.. and the start of 2002.. he was a fan favourite. He was a man with a plan. Greed. After only accomplishing the tv and tag team titles after a year.. he left. He was retired - then he came back. He won all the titles he had been unable to win in the short space of 4 weeks. Then.. then he got beat by a Chimp. He lost the World Title to a chimp. ..of course, that wasnt the end. He returned, still a crazed mad man.. became the new commissioner.. then started swinging his crowbar at his enimies like a butcher cutting up his meat. He cost Seymour Almasy the world title to his *only friend* Ruff Ryder. Then he invited Malcolm Kennedy, a young rookie, to join himself and Ryder in 'The Prime Alliance' before mysteriously dissapearing. Then months later he returned again, a changed man. Wanting to right the wrongs, his first target is Malcolm Kennedy; a cocky trash talker, who has done nothing but stab knifes into Jamie's back since he left.
