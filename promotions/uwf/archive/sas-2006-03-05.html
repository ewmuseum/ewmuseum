[Clyde thrashes about a bit on the mat, before turning himself around and punching Clark in the head, breaking the hold.] Larson: Told you. [Clyde gets to his feet, waiting for Clark to rise. As soon as he does, he throws another punch, only for Clark to duck and again take him down by the leg, slapping the kneebar on again!] Rick: You were...... Larson: I was saying shut the hell up. [Clyde flails his arms, then tries to turn again, but this time Clark shifts his weight to prevent it. Clyde then reaches out, stretches and grabs the ropes, prompting the referee to begin a five count.] Larson: I thought this was no DQ. The ref can't exactly disqualify Clark or anything if he doesn't break it. Rick: Did anyone bother to tell the referee? Larson: I guess not. [The ref gets to four before Clark releases the hold. Clyde pulls himself up on the ropes. Clark runs in and again dives for his knee, but the demon sidesteps, causing him to fly straight out of the ring to the concrete floor!] Rick: Got too overzealous. Larson: Ya think? [Clyde shakes his leg, then climbs through the ropes. As soon as he does, Clark springs up and yet again wraps up his leg, picking him up by it. He then DROPS him across the ring apron with a modified kneebreaker!] Larson: Ow, frigging ow! Rick: Gary Clark has come here tonight with a plan! He's going to make sure Clyde can't stay on his feet to use any of his offense! [Clyde actually lands on one foot, hopping around and clutching his knee. Clark makes his way over to the announce table.] Clark: No DQ, right? Larson: Um, yeah? Why the hell are you talking to me? Go away. [Clark nods, then grabs a nearby chair and makes his way back to the demon! He makes a beeline for the champ's knee, swings, but at the last second Clyde falls out of the way causing him to strike the ring.] Rick: Lucked out of that one. [Gary Clark follows Clyde as he scrambles backwards along the ground. He again raises the chair, only for Clyde to thrust a hand forward and toss out a fireball!] [However in an astounding display of reflexes, Clark raises the chair in front of his face, blocking the flame! He then raises his weapon and SMASHES it down across Clyde's knee!] Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOH! Rick: I don't know if I've ever seen anyone take it to Clyde like this! Larson: Well, aside from Bethany. Rick: Only you'd want to see that. [As Clyde shouts in agony, Clark tosses the chair aside and drags him up by the hair, depositing him into the ring. Clyde attempts to escape, but can only manage to limp as Clark rolls in as well.] Rick: Clyde better do something here. He may be immortal, but he's also about to be crippled. [Clark stalks around Clyde, readying another tackle with a glint in his eye. As he runs in behind him though, Clyde spins around and launches an forearm that connects with his forehead, knocking him back into the corner!] Larson: Went to the well too often. Dummy. [Limping heavily, Clyde charges in. Clark sticks his feet up. Before he can run into them however, Clyde stops again, grabs him by the feet and pulls, causing the challenger to crash back first!] Rick: I don't know if you could ever call Clyde a ring general, but he caught him there. [Grimacing with each step, Clyde marches over and mounts Clark's chest, battering him with heavy punches. He then stands, grabs him by the neck and lifts him. However Clark escapes by falling to his back, grabbing Clyde by the legs and pulling, launching him to the corner and causing his chin to bounce off of the turnbuckle!] Larson: Do something, Clyde! [As Clyde holds his chin and grimaces, Gary Clark rolls him over in a schoolboy!] 1 . . . . . 2 . . . . . [Clyde kicks out!] Larson: If you would have put your feet on the ropes you would have won. Loser. [Clark and Clyde both get to their feet. Clark moves in first, and in a very quick motion locks Clyde into a front facelock before driving him down with a DDT!] [The crowd then boos as a figure emerges in the entryway.] Larson: Who the hell? Rick: The world heavyweight champion, Misfit! But what's he doing here? [Misfit stands with arms folded, smiling as in the ring, Gary Clark stomps away on Clyde's already battered knee.] Rick: This one should just about be over. I don't think even Clyde can take much more of this. [Clark stomps Clyde's leg once more for good measure, then climbs the turnbuckles. The demon attempts to move frantically, but his leg appears to be locked into position.] Larson: This isn't good! [Gary Clark leaps off of the ropes with a frogsplash! Just before he connects though, Clyde sticks out his arms and CATCHES HIM IN MIDAIR!!!!] Rick: OH MY ROD!!!!! [The crowd gasps in amazement as Clyde rises from the mat with Clark in his arms, thrashing and attempting to escape! The demon limps heavily, but makes it to his feet, tossing the challenger up and catching him in a powerbomb position!] Larson: YOU KNOW WHAT COMES NEXT! [Misfit begins to move, but Clyde suddenly spins around with Clark in his grasp, freezing him in his tracks with a glare.] [As the world champ stares, Clyde snarls. He frees one hand, pointing directly at him. Then he wraps up Gary Clark and DRIVES HIM HEADFIRST WITH A THUNDEROUS SHATTERING!!!!] Rick: AND JUST LIKE THAT, CLYDE HAS PUT OUT THE LIGHTS!!!!! [Still snarling, still staring a hole through Misfit, Clyde plants his skeletal hand on Clark's chest.] 1 . . . . . .2 . . . . . 3!!!!! Tommy Waters: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH.........AND STILL I'M ABOVE FACING GARY CLARK CHAMPION........CLYDE!!!!!! Rick: ...
