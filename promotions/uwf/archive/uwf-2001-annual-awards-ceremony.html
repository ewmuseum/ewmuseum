UWF Annual Awards Ceremony (2001)

2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in the highly decorated, highly elaborate and highly attended UWF Arena. The main arena is being used for the evenings proceedings, with the plastic seats removed and put into storage along with the ring. They are replaced by antique oak tables, which seat about 10 persons, but the rampway has two lecterns on it, decked with a gold design. The Arena is awash with polite conversation between both superstars and backstage employees who are being looked after by the management commitee. Every so often, a champagne glass is clinked, and a toast made - either to absent friends, or to those who have fallen by the wayside. Anyone who is anyone in the UWF is in attendance for the evenings festivities, and that obviously makes security extremely awkward. Max Queue, the UWF Head of Security, has already been seen once checking passes and making sure that the evening goes off without a hitch - its just a shame that he is the only one really concerned about the arrangements in case of a fire, flood or earthquake. The rest of the security are all standing around looking bored, and generally whiling the time away. The front tables have been reserved for the all-important current UWF roster, but there are various names from the UWF's short history all seated in the "cheap" seats behind the current roster. Richard Hayes is seated at the top table, accompanied by Lew Spectre, Corporal Punishment and the rest of the "Corporate" party. The expensive champagne is sitting in an ice-bucket whilst Hayes and Spectre are seemingly discussing some sort of tactics (although, for most of the roster, the main aim of the evening is to forget work). The UWF Intercontinental Title sits next to Corporal Punishment on the table, shining with a lacquer not seen since the UWF was first formed. Corky Spectre sits on the next table across, although security does stop the Boy Wonder from approaching his apparent "father" at Lew's request. April Summers looks bored by the way that Corky keeps making her repeat the phrase "Hey, Hey, Hey!" as does the Prodigy who has seemingly drawn the short straw by being placed on the same table. The Circus of the Damned, though, are making every effort to enjoy their evening, with Zoompa Doompa the Oompa Loompa running amok amongst the rest of the crowd. Senor Mime is standing at the furthest end of the table trying desperately to put an imaginary balloon in an imaginary box, but failing miserably, whislt Gurgles and Lady Luck watch intently. Standing on the top of the ramp behind the first lectern is UWF Business Executive, and part of the UWF's commentary team, Andrew J Frost (AJ to most), dressed to the nines in a white tuxedo with a black bow tie and cummerbund. Frost looks more nervous than he has done before; obviously the pressure is great. Adjusting the bow tie, taking a long gulp of ice water and arranging his notes, AJ Frost looks towards the cameras and smiles a small smile.] Frost: "Good evening, ladies and gentlemen and welcome to the first UWF Annual Awards Ceremony. Tonight, we commemorate the progress that the UWF has made since it was formed in late August this year, and we also pay tribute to the people who have made the UWF what it is today - one of the leaders in this industry. When you look at the world of Wrestling and Sports Entertainment in general, you would, at first think that there are only a few guys who make this possible. The commentary team of both myself and Link Anchorman, the shareholders of the company and the backstage crew are just a few of the guys who keep the UWF running. However, after thinking for a few seconds about who makes the UWF as successful as it is, it is obvious to see that the people who keep the UWF running are in the main, here tonight. They risk their lives and bodies to entertain us... they give us enjoyment - they are the UWF superstars, and tonight we will be acknowledging the achievements of these guys in the awards nominated, and voted in by you, the UWF roster. Lets get straight to the main aim of the evening, and introduce my co-presenter for the evening - he is the UWF's Head Talent Supervisor, and the other half of the "Dream Team" - Link Anchorman." [A polite round of applause, led by Frost, greets Link, whom is dressed as smartly, if not immaculately, as AJ Frost.] Link: "Thank you for your kind words, AJ, but let us get to the first award of the evening, and its the award which is probably least prestigious, although only one of the nominees is actually in attendance tonight. It's the "WizHard" UWF Jobber of the Year award, and here are the nominations." [The nominations are shown on the UniversalTron behind Link and AJ.] · For the lack of a promotion in 3 months, despite constant promises - Flatliner · For being a Hard Wizard, and for the over elaborate death scene - WizHard · For his poor exploits in BOTH of his UWF outings - "Billionaire" Joe Forbes · For the lack of grammar and lack of promotions past his first outing - "Mr. England" Jack Union. Link: "And the results are as follows:" · With 17% of the vote - in third place: Jack Union · With 28% of the vote - in second place: "Billionaire" Joe Forbes · But with a massive 50% of the vote - the "WizHard" UWF Jobber of the Year 2001 is - the man whose name is on the award, WizHard!
