[Larson follows.] ~Opening Match~ Singles Match ~Opening Match~ Chester Windham -v- Scott Angel The show was kicked off nicely with a bout that featured two of the UWF's most impressive newcomers. Chester Windham and Scott Angel entered the UWF ring and were seen on UWF television for the first time ever. They would not disappoint. Angel and Windham started things out with a fast paced move set that had each man drop the other with an arm drag or wrist lock take down, then the other recover, and counter his opponent. Angel would get the upper hand eventually after hitting a dropkick that sent Chester reeling. Angel would follow that up with a belly to belly suplex. Windham would kick out after a two count. The two UWF rookies squared off again. Chest Windham would drop Scott Angel with a lightning quick single leg takedown. Windham locked Angel in a figure four leg lock… but Angel refused to give in. Eventually Angel got the ropes and the hold was broken. Angel caught Chester off guard with a wicked spear, which lead to a Michinoku Driver on Windham, once again, however, the former Harvard student kicked out. The two wrestlers then traded blows, with Windham eventually slipping behind Angel and hitting the Bermuda Triangle. Windham went for a cover but only got a two count. Angel was back up and the duo went at it again. Windham once again used his speed to his advantage and tried to slip Scott into the Choke 'N Smoke, but Angel dropped down and rolled him up in a small package.. 1… 2…. 3. And with that, Scott Angel walked out of his debut match victorious. ~Second Match~ Singles Match ~Second Match~ Terry Truman -v- "The Anarchist" Jamie Thomas Jamie Thomas, very much a man of mystery as I was totally unsure of what he looked like, how tall he was, how much he weighed. Hell he didn’t even show what moves he favored in the press box, it was as though UWF had picked a random guy off the street to fight. *Which wouldn’t be the first time.* Terry Truman on the other hands approached the ring with a set purpose; his eyes bright like the fires of Hades. As the Foo Fighters played him down the ramp. Jamie Thomas entered to Ernie The Fastest Milkman in the west by Benny Hill due to a sound mix up which will be sorted by next week. *Fill in your bio properly!, it will save mistakes like this happening again. * Truman kicked off the match with a collar and elbow lock, quickly gaining the advantage, pulling Thomas down into a standing side headlock. Thomas pushed Truman towards the ropes and quickly dropped to the mat, Truman clicked this though and upon rebounding from the ropes sent a swift boot into the gut of Thomas, lifting him a good few inches off the mat. Truman then continued his assault sending the base of his boot into the face of Thomas a number of times, stomping away like there was no tomorrow, before the referee pulled him back to check on Thomas, who had a bit of blood pouring from his nose, early on in the bout. This didn’t stop Thomas however as he quickly pulled himself to his feet and charged at Truman tackling him to the mat, dropping a series of sharp elbows across his throat in a veteran attempt to cut off his air supply. He followed this up with a slingshot, ending up with Truman almost decapitating himself on the top rope. Thomas not one to miss an opportunity (I would imagine) continued his assault and grabbed Truman before hitting a brainbuster suplex, before floating over for the pin. A close two count followed, as Truman got his shoulder up and shook his head slightly, trying to shake the cobwebs out. Thomas by this time had headed towards the turnbuckle, but before he could even begin to climb it, Truman had charged at him with a spear, causing his back to contort in a horrible way before his face splashed off the top turnbuckle, spraying blood everywhere. From this point on Thomas didn’t know where he was, a mixture of pain/concussion and blood in the eyes, always fun. Fortunately it didn’t last long. As Thomas pulled himself to his feet and stumbled around Truman was waiting for him. KICK~! WHAM~! STUNNER~! Sorry… KICK~! WHAM~! THE DROP OFF~! However you want to write it Thomas was out for the count. Truman covered and he won, simple as that. ~Third Match~ Singles Match ~Third Match~ Lotus Dragon -v- Trailer Park Luchador Wednesday Night Underground a time for those that want to shine to prove themselves, which is why many people were shocked to see the Trailer Park Luchadore wrestling, after his loss at Live and Loud. LotusDragon, yes there is no space in that, also coming off the back of a loss at the hands of Joshua Taro Freedom at last Wednesday Night Underground. The bout kicked off with TPL, being what he is best EVIL~! By running around the ring trying to avoid LotusDragon who remained perfectly still in the center of the ring watching TPL do laps, before sticking out arm sending the master of evildom flying backwards to the mat. Immediately LotusDragon pounced on him, sending a series of stinging elbows into the face of TPL as he screamed and shouted like a little girl, it was embarrassing to some extent. The son of the Chinese diplomat then dragged TPL to his feet and send him flying across the ring like a pre-wheel chaired superman with an belly to belly suplex which left TPL, sprawled across the far side of the ring. LotusDragon then pursued him but as he bent over to pick him up once more, received an evil thumb to the eye. TPL kipped up in a feat, which surprised even him and charged at LotusDragon, sending a series of fists into his face, before a roundhouse kick dropped LotusDragon to the floor. TPL dropped to his knees and nonchalantly covered LotusDragon but barely even registered a two count. TPL picked up LotusDragon by the hair only to receive a swift knee to the meat and two veg causing him to fold in half at which point LotusDragon put TPL in a standing scissor lock before hoisting him up and sending him to the mat like a sack of …s…potatoes with his patented Lotus Bomb. Hooking TPL’s leg so that his foot was nearly in his mouth the referee dropped down. 1 2 3 And that was all she wrote. TPL would no doubt later go on to blame it on and injury from Live and Loud, but the truth of the matter was LotusDragon was just hungrier this time round. ~Fourth Match~ Tag Team Match ~Fourth Match~ Stars of the Proletariat -v- The Uprising This match featured The Communist's new protégé's, The Stars of the Proletariat, and The Uprising's Louis Bourbon and Lance Williams. Hammer and Bourbon would star the match off. Hammer used his stout stature to his advantage, throwing Bourbon around the ring like a rag doll with a wide assortment of suplexes. Bourbon would eventually sneak by to tag out to Lance Williams. Lance came out like a lightning bolt, hitting Hammer with his fast paced high flying offense. Williams was destroyed, however, when attempting a moonsault… Hammer caught him midair and plastered him with an uber powerful reverse powerbomb. Hammer would then tag out to his partner Sickle. Sickle would proceed to make a mockery of Williams, slapping him around the ring. Eventually, Uprising member Oleg came out from backstage and charged down to the ring to aid his comrade. The Communist did not take too kindly to this and caught Oleg on the entrance ramp with a wicked big boot. The Communist then Communist Slammed Oleg off the entrance stage. Sickle tossed Williams to his corner, allowing Louis Bourbon to enter the ring. Bourbon charged in and was caught with a devastating Samoan drop. Hammer then entered the ring and the Stars of the Proletariat executed the Prote-lariat. 1. 2. 3. It was over, The Communist and The Soviette would enter the ring to stand victorious with the tag divisions new power, The Stars of the Proletariat. ~Main Event~ Tag Team Match ~Main Event~ The Tumble Bees -v- Los Mucho Macho Mascaras The Main Event of the evening offered more of the UWF's revitalized tag team division in a two on two match.
