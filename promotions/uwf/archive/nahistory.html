North American Title History

captured 26 Jul 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The fed's own title-history page, hosted on uwf.rpcommunity.com — Jaymes Faulkner's own domain, from the era he ran the UWF as its Fed Head. One page, three tables, spanning the belt's entire recovered life across three eras of the same lineage (IWF → GIWF → UWF). Reproduced here as tables, unedited; see the UWF North American title page and its GIWF predecessor for how this reconciles against the registry's own structured ledger — it corroborates the GIWF era in full and extends the UWF era a further year past where the promoter's own drive-held copy stopped.
