The Regal Rumble — elimination log

2000s
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bryn Jaye has been eliminated at: 49:44 In the ring: Shock, Oni, Mory, Sean McClancy, Real Deal, Persia, Killer Dude, fool, Bubba, Ruff Ryder, The Commie, Rich Rollins, Jason Christy, Patrick McClancy, Seraphiend, Danny Jaye, Dicky Flint Bubba eats Pom Tom Longtongue and they both get eliminated (Bubba/The fool) Ezra Kazee enters Seraphiend is eliminated (UT like character) Patrick McCLnacy is eliminated Sacrifice enters and is eliminated Unforgiven enters Danny Jaye is eliminated Aquillia De Flagaro enters with special intro and such The Plague enters Dicky Flint is eliminated (Grand Wizard of the KKK gimmick, talks to a polar bear?!) Unforgiven is eliminated Aquillia De Flagaro is eliminated Ideas: Random fan joins the Rumble, Phenomenon intervention … Beginning - Shock, Oni, Mory, Sean McClancy, Real Deal, Persia, Killer Dude, fool, Bubba, Ruff Ryder, The Commie, Rich Rollins, Jason Christy, Patrick McClancy, Seraphiend, Danny Jaye, Dicky Flint “TIME OUT!!!” The random pounding comes to a screeching halt as Jamie Shock stands predominantly in the center of the ring … his hands forming the shape of a ‘T.’ With supreme confidence, he regards the other men in the ring. Rick: “What is he doing?” Larson: “Oh come on, Rick … you saw the brutal beating Jamie suffered earlier! I’m with him. I think it’s only fair that Jamie gets a breather while the other wrestlers pound one another into comas.” Rick: “It was nail polish.” Larson: “Have you ever sniffed nail polish? The fumes are STRONG, my friend. Strong.” Jamie continues to command the attention of one and all in the ring. Shock: “My friends … my fellow athletes. What are we doing?” The crowd responds with something that sounds like “Booyoo.” Shock: “Can’t we all just get along? Can’t we just … psyche!” Jamie Shock kicks Unforgiven in the nuts, dropping him like a sack of stones. Shock: “Mwaha! Back to what you were do—“ Tsunami Oni bounces off the ropes, bringing a stiff straight kick right into Jamie Shock’s face, immediately shutting him up. Rick: “I really hate that guy.” Larson: “Me too. And Tsunami used to be so cool.” Rick: “What? No … I hate Shock, you retard.” Bubba/The Fool Elimination - Shock, Oni, Mory, Sean McClancy, Real Deal, Persia, Killer Dude, fool, Bubba, Ruff Ryder, The Commie, Rich Rollins, Jason Christy, Patrick McClancy, Seraphiend, Danny Jaye, Dicky Flint Ruff Ryder rears back, slapping the taste out of Bubba’s bloated mouth, sending the big man reeling backwards, trying to keep his balance. But Bubba does no go over … oh no! Regaining his composure, Bubba looks down pitifully on the tiny Ruff Ryder … just as one hand goes to his stomach, gently rubbing, and a tongue comes out to lick his hungry lips. Rick: “Oh no.” Larson: “Bubba’s going to eat Ruff Ryder?” Bubba begins moving towards the now very frightened Native-American Superstar. Rick: “That would be … yeah. I think that would be the first time a former World Heavyweight Champion was ever eaten.” Larson: “Not counting Jamie’s missing testicle?” Rick: “Yeah. Not counting that.” Larson: “Speaking of Jamie, where is he?!” The camera switches position, showing Jamie Shock desperately wrapping himself around the top rope as Sean McClancy, the Real Deal, and Patrick McClancy all try to get him out of the ring. Jamie sticks his tongue out at them and mocks their effort as he doesn’t even slide. Larson: “Oh. There’s too many people in the ring to keep track of anymore.” Rick: “Well that was four of them … Bubba’s about to eat Ruff Ryder, Tsunami Oni is fighting Nick Persia, Seraphiend, Killer Dude, and Dicky Flint single handedly from the looks of things … I’m pretty sure Danny Jaye is over there trying to turn Jason Christy into a helicopter … the Real Deal and Moraless have been trading blows for five minutes now … and I think the Communist has just sat down in the corner and started laughing at everyone else.” Larson: “Oh. What about the Fool?” Rick: “The Fool …” Rick’s cut off by a horrible scream as Bubba steps on the Fool’s foot. Larson: “Never mind then.” The Fool begins leaping around in the ring, holding his injured foot in horrific pain. With Bubba’s attention gone, Ruff Ryder takes the cue to run away in a hurry, diving into the little circle that had been made around Tsunami. Bubba slowly turns around, now obviously meaning to eat the Fool … until a cupcake hits him in the back of the head. Larson: “Who in their right mind brought food anywhere near that insane, four-hundred pound junkie!?” Bubba swings around, a finger around grabbing a clump of icing from the back of his fez and plopping it in his mouth … when he comes face to face with … Rick: “Pom Tom Longtounge!!!” Or face to puppet face. Either way, Pom Tom sits insanely on the top turnbuckle, a custard pie sitting in his demented little puppet lap. His face looks lifeless, but his eyes … his eyes also look lifeless. Larson: “I can’t believe you just marked out for a puppet.” Pom Tom Longtongue: “Pick on someone your own size, lard-ass!” Bubba … looks very confused. Pom Tom Longtongue: “You heard me, fatso! You’ve got nothing—“ Bubba: “Mmm … custard.” Bubba eats Pom Tom Longtongue, pie and all. Rick: “Oh sweet Jesus, Bubba just ate the evil talking puppet.” Larson: “And I can’t believe you just said that.” The Fool bellows in a horrible, tortured scream, his mentor already disappearing into whatever atrocities could lie in wait within Bubba’s massive stomach. Rick: “Pom Tom is trapped! Trapped within Bubba!!! First Big Ted, and now Pom Tom! And he didn’t even leave us a limb to remember him by … and continue booking in random matches for no real reason whatsoever!” Larson: “What?” Rick: “Wait … what am I saying? Thank God he didn’t leave a limb for us to continue booking in random matches for no real reason whatsoever.” Larson: “Thank you. Like half of our roster isn’t already here for just that anyways.” 3~! Rick: “And it’s just about time for one more person to join the fun!” Larson: “How f’ing long went between these entrances!?” Infuriated, the Fool begins pummeling Bubba’s stomach, screaming for Pom Tom Longtongue to move towards the light … to just move … towards … the light! Rick: “You can just see the pain in that man’s eyes.” Larson: “Bubba! Eat him too. And Rick if you want.” 2~! Bubba looks down at the small Fool, smiling stupidly. Enraged further, the Fool leaps to the top rope, managing to be a bit taller than his opponent now … and bitch-slaps Bubba’s fez right off the big man’s head! Larson: “Uh oh.” Bubba roars with horrible intensity. Horrible! Larson: “He shouldn’t have done that.” 1~! Bubba grabs the Fool by the head … and flicks him. Ezra Kazee comes running towards the ring, the latest entrant into the Regal Rumble, and only barely misses being hit as the Fool goes flying past in a horrified, screaming ball. Rick: “And Ezra Kazee has entered the Regal Rumble!” The Fool has been eliminated at: 54:37 Larson: “And the Fool has exited the Regal Rumble!” Kazee stops in mid-stride as he sees Bubba take one angry step over the top rope of the ring, brushing off his Fez with a frenzy of strokes … and then the big man drops to the outside, shaking the ringside area. Bubba has been eliminated at: 54:51 Larson: “That stupid piece of crap just eliminated himself!” Rick: “Shut up! If you make any quick movements he might come after us.” Bubba growls as he passes Ezra Kazee … who, in-turn, kicks him in the gut. And Bubba, in-turn … pukes all over the entrance ramp. Larson: “Oh … God.” Ezra Kazee wisely grabs his nose and flees into the chaos of the Regal Rumble. Rick: “Is that … Pom Tom?” The Fool flies down the entrance ramp, leaping into the pile of vomit, desperately grabbing at the remains of his evil puppet mentor. Larson: “That … is just plain wrong.” Looking queasy, Bubba adjusts the fez back on his head and heads for the back. Flint’s Elimination – Jamie Shock, Tsunami Oni, Mory, Sean McClancy, Real Deal, Nick Persia, Killer Dude, The Fool, Bubba, Ruff Ryder, Communist, Jason Christy, Dicky Flint, Ezra Kazee, the Plague, Random Fan “Wait, wait, wait!!!” The convoluted massacre in the middle of the ring stops abruptly. The ring silently becomes like a paused video: Sean McClancy desperately trying to heave Jamie Shock over the top rope, Jamie himself pausing while he actually bites McClancy’s forehead; … Eric Cameron stands heroically on the entrance ramp, slowly, methodically, walking down towards the ring. Larson: “Wasn’t he already eliminated?” Rick: “I think so. We have so many people doing this we’ve lost all continuity, though.” Larson: “I hate when that happens.” Rick: “Me too.” Nevertheless, the action in the ring remains suspended as Eric Cameron stalks to the ring … where he instantly hops in, demanding full attention. Eric Cameron: “My apologies for the interruption, but unfortunately, YOUR Hall of Fame heroes, the Phenomenons, have discovered something so horrible … so disturbing … so mind-blowingly awful! … that we have been forced to interrupt the ever-stupendous Regal Rumble. We have been forced! to set things to rights. Ladies and gentlemen, I direct your attention to …” Cameron spends around in circles wildly, his finger breezing past each of the wrestlers in the ring in turn: Jamie Shock, Tsunami Oni, Moraless, Sean McClancy, the Real Deal, Nick Persia, Killer Dude, the Fool, the random hopeless fan that ran into the ring, Bubba, Ruff Ryder, the Communist, Jason Christy, Ezra Kazee, the Plague, and … Eric Cameron: “DICKY FLINT~!” The loveable Grand Wizard lets loose a small scream in surprise as the wrestling legend points maddeningly at the torn robe that hangs about his body loosely. Eric Cameron: “Did you truly, actually think you could get away with it, you thieving whore!?!?!” Cameron marches across the ring, slowly and steadily, his finger pointing accusingly at Flint the whole while as he crosses the width of it. Eric trembles with rage. Eric Cameron: “Do you know who I am? I, little racist monkey, am Eric Cameron … Stable Wars Champion! Tag Team of the Year! Tag Team Hall of Famer! Three time World Tag Team Champion! Four time North American Tag Team Champion! I have so many titles I use them as a footrest! And you … you!” Cameron leaps the rest of the way across the ring, landing a foot away from Dicky Flint, who still appears horribly, horribly confused. Eric shoves his finger into Flint’s forehead. Eric Cameron: “How dare you steal the Phenomenal Polar Bear for use in your pathetic, heatless promos! How dare you!!!!” The crowd jeers the horrible theft maddeningly. Eric Cameron: “For five years, Herbert, the Phenomenal Trekkie Eating Polar Bear has been a beloved and intriguing member of the Phenomenal Clique. He is an integral part of the Phenomenal image … a beautiful and lasting image of GIWF superiority! “And your pathetic thievery dares to piss on the beautiful image our Phenomenal fans hold so dear! Well I say to thee NAY!!!” Cameron pokes Dicky Flint in the head repeatedly. Around him, the other wrestlers began beating the crap out of each other long ago, and they wrestle around the intruding Phenomenon without paying any attention. Eric Cameron: “Do you know what I’m going to do to you, little Dicky Flint, you pathetic disgrace for a member of the Democracy we have Defended for so many years!?!?!? I … we! … we are going to …” Eric drops his hand dramatically into his pocket, and … yanks out a sheet of paper. Eric Cameron: “Serve your candy ass with court summons for gimmick infringement, thank you very much. Could you please sign here?” Cameron holds the paper and a pen out kindly to Dicky Flint. Tsunami Oni has begun spinning around in circles in the middle of the ring, spraying Evil Blue Mist~! Into the eyes of every person still in the Regal Rumble, turning the back of Eric’s head a dull blue color. Dicky Flint ducks the mist, but just stares at Eric stupidly. Eric Cameron: “For the love of Rod … will you just sign the papers? You know you stole our gimmick, and we’re not happy about this.” Dicky Flint shakes his head defiantly. Eric Cameron: “Yeah … I figured that. Shane! Let’s feed him to Herbert.” And from the crowd, a monumental roar comes forth … Shane Stone, giant of the Phenomenons, rides high upon the massive white form of Herbert the Phenomenal Polar Bear. Herbert silently eats someone wearing Vulcan ears … Shane only grins stupidly. Eric Cameron: “See! This is what happens, Dicky. You let hate into your heart … you light a few crosses on fire … lead a few lynchings … next thing you know you’re breaking international copywrite law and being fed to the scourge of the great white north. Didn’t your mother teach you better than this?” Herbert the Polar Bear is staring oddly at the barrier going around the ringside area … Shane knocks on it, trying to figure out how they’re going to get over it. Neither man sees the might of Eugene~!, the pathetic copywrite infringing polar bear, coming down the entrance ramp to save his thieving master. Larson: “Is this over yet?” Rick: “No. Finish eating.” Shane Stone desperately tries to heave Herbert over the guardrail, somehow managing to actually gain ground as Herbert claws at the top. Eric taps his foot on the mat, rather annoyed at how long it’s taking. Dicky Flint still seems very confused, and slightly frightened as the polar bear comes to eat him. Eugene has begun sniffing the unconscious body of the Trailer Park Luchador that lies on the ground near the ring … then lifts a leg and urinates on him. Eric Cameron: “Oh for the love of Rod. Could you wait a second, Dicky? Actually …” Eric turns around, taps the Plague on the shoulder, and drops a quarter in his hand. Eric Cameron: “Could you beat on him for a minute?” Cameron nods backwards at Dicky Flint … then leaps over the top rope, going to help Shane Stone heave Herbert over the guardrail. The Plague looks at the quarter, then shrugs, throws the quarter as hard as he can at Flint’s head, and proceeds to beat him into the ground. Eugene has begun trying to climb into the ring … but only managing to somehow hump the side of the ring. Larson: “What in the hell is happening?” Rick: “I’m not sure … but somehow the Regal Rumble has come to a crashing halt, and a polar bear is having sex with the ring.” Larson: “That’s … actually not surprising. Pass the casserole.” Outside of the ring, the Phenomenons sway from side to side as they carry Herbert the Polar Bear’s huge body towards the ring. Herbert’s tongue is out as he pants like a dog, and seems to be mimicking Superman as he moves through the air. With a giant heave, the Phenomenons send Herbert the Polar Bear through the bottom and middle ropes, leaving the giant white beast in the ring. No one else in the match pays the least attention except for the Plague, who wisely bitch slaps Dicky Flint one more time, and then dives at Tsunami Oni … getting a spinning kick to the jaw for his troubles. Dicky Flint backs away in a hurry from Herbert the Polar Bear, but he quickly reaches the end of the road. Herbert stalks after him, his huge teach gleaming in the bright lights of the stadium, the Phenomenons cheering him on … … and Herbert licks him. Shane Stone frowns, while Eric Cameron pokes the polar bear with a stick, urging him to eat their newly crowned Dreaded Rival of Doom. Herbert licks Dicky Flint again, and Flint sticks his tongue out at the Phenomenons, adjusting his Grand Wizard cap arrogantly. … and then Eugene flies from the other side of the ring, tackling Herbert the Polar Bear in an insane polar bear death rumble! Shane Stone and Eric Cameron grab popcorn from someone in the front row, drop down into Indian style sitting positions near the guard rail, and cheer Herbert on. Dicky Flint shrugs and leaps atop the rolling bears. Herbert rolls over and pimp slaps Eugene, getting a roar of pain in response. Dicky Flint is immediately crushed beneath the angry beasts. And remembering they had a point out here, Eric Cameron slides into the ring, yanks Dicky Flint up … tosses him over one shoulder, and leaps out of the ring.
