Monday Night Mojo - 21 August 2006 The first regular show since the Sins of the Past PPV made sweeping changes to not only the championship structure within the UWF but also the political backstage environment with the revealation that the new owner was none other than multiple champion and Hall of Famer, the three foot diabolical creature from Hell known as Luca the Imp! Tune in to see just what the new dawining age of the UWF is going to bring! Main Event Fallout From The Sins of the Past Rumble Match - Handicap Match Axel Action / Sledge -v- Cockjockey Cocky eliminated both men in the battle royal at Sins Of The Past and is now the number one contender to the TV title that Action holds and Sledge wants. Sledge beat Axel Action in a two out of three falls match at Sins Of The Past, but Axel still went away with the title due to the last fall being a result of a disqualification. Axel Action is the Television Champion and is desperatly still trying to hang on to his title ... and simply because the Cockjockey is smaller than he is Luca the Imp, the new UWF owner, is pitting both Action and Sledge against their dimuniative challenger! The question is who is in worse trouble, Cockjockey himself or the hated enemies who now have to work together? Fallout From The Sins of the Past Rumble Match - first fall wins Carlos Castro / Pajaro De Presa / White Tiger -v- Boogerman / Bruce Gordy / The Communist A match that was a huge talking point after Sins Of The Past aired was the battle royal. In the rumble, and his first ever UWF match, Carlos Castro managed to earn the #1 Contenders spot for the NA/INH titles as well as convince Pajaro and the Tiger to join forces with him to try and reverse their losing streaks. Will his luck continue with a win in this match, against three competitors ... two of whom are former champions themselves and one who is just downright disturbing ... who are probably jealous, wanting to take out their frustrations on the recently debuting UWF star? Valentyne -v- 'The Beauty' George Ross At Sins Of The Past Valentyne put up an impressive performance in his first ever match, during the battle royal. George Ross, 'The Beauty', has been quiet as of late, choosing not to enter himself into the open battle royal at Sins Of The Past unlike the rest of the roster. Will there be any love in the ring when Valentyne and 'The Beauty' collide? Pencil Necked Geeks -v- Deadpool The Pencil Necked Geeks aren't having much luck lately, coming out of Sins Of The Past with a loss to UWF Tag Team Champions The Tumble Bees, they desperatly need to pick up some momentum if they want to get back onto the tag title scene. How will they do against the debuting Deadpool? Plus... - What does Luca, the new owner, have in store for the UWF? - Will Tessa Windsor's stalker be revealed, and just what is the condition of the new crowned Unified NA/INH champion after the brutal attack by Clyde, the new UWF World Champion? - Will any light be shed onto the situation behind the World title's #1 contendership ... is it Seymour Almasy, is it Lance Rose ... or is it really both men? - Will Seymour Almasy answer Jamie Shock's $10m challenge, and possibly forfeit his chance at another World title? - What part does Digital Mortality play in things and why did he help Seymour Almasy during the Rumble? - What is the status of the wrestlers who were injured during the Sins of the Past PPV ... Chris Storm was stretchered off, Tessa Windsor may have sufferred a concussion, Clyde may have broken a leg and Misfit, the former World Champion, defintely broke a leg! - What new twists and turns will the feud between Axel and Sledge take? - ... and can the UWF survive this new age intact?!?! Thoughts, predictions, feedback and the meaning of life would all be appreciated!
