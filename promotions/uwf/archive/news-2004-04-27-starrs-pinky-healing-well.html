April 27th, 2004 Starr's pinky healing well It has now been around 6 months to the day where Kevin Starr and Seymour ALmasy faced off in an epic battle for the World Title. Unfortunatly for Kevin Starr, his pinky was sliced off in the match, which is really the only reason he lost, since, well, he was kicking Seymour's butt the entire match leading up to that point. Anyway, Starr's been in rehab the past several months trying to get back up to tip top shape and according to the doctors, things are looking well for the Greatest Superstarr of All-Time. When I asked Starr about when he would return to the UWF, he replied: "The UW-who? OH! the UWF, my old place. You mean, they're actually still up and running? Don't they only have like 12 active wrestlers on their roster? I'm not about to go back to a 1/4 of a fed. Besides, it'll be at least another 3-4 months before I can get back in the ring again. I still have to wait for the doctor's to give me the green light to get back in the ring." Afterwards we gave the man who did the sergury on Kevin Starr's right pinky finger a call. We asked him when Kevin Starr will be cleared to wrestle. "He was cleared to wrestle over 5 months ago... I don't even know why you're here really. I haven't seen Mr. Starr since the sergury over half a year ago." So when can we expect Kevin Starr back in the ring? Tomorrow? 2 week's from now? A month? A year? Perhaps he will never return. Only the man himself knows.
