The Rules

The UWF! ... Insert Your Own Cheesy Tagline Here The UWF is all about fun, and doesn't have many rules apart from these below. Any rules that appear below are due to a repeat of something we don't like, and don't want to happen again. Bar these rules, there are the obvious "rp on time", "send angles in on time", and generally don't be an ass. We don't like donkeys, and never will. I myself am allergic to them. "So You Want to Leave the UWF?" The UWF is happy-go-lucky. We're not strict about anything. Especially leaving. There's a number of feds I've seen in PTC force their handlers into "contracts" and things like that which prevent them from even going to other PTC feds ... it's stupid and we've never tried to force anything like that on to you guys. It's a game and it's about having fun ... if you're not having fun, then there's no reason to be here, and you're welcome to come back when it starts being fun again. It's simple, it's easy. The only thing we're asking is that you DO NOT QUIT THE UWF ON THE FORUMS. Like I said ... we're about fun. If you're not having fun, then you're welcome to quit at any time ... but the fedheads here are just as much members of the fed as you are, and leaving us with absolutely no warning and having to work ourselves out of a hole is NOT fun for us. That means because you're not having fun, we're not having fun ... and then not only are you leaving, but members of the staff are quitting. And members of the staff quitting means the fed closes and then no one is having fun. It's simple. When it's time to leave the UWF: 1) Let the staff know. Before ANYONE. NEVER post that you are quitting on the forums before you've talked to the staff about it. 2) Work your character out of the fed. It's not a lot to ask ... if your character is in a feud, or getting a push, or holding a title ... take five minutes to figure out a way to work your character out of whatever he's in, help us out, and have him exit however you want. 3) Wait until after the card your character leaves, and THEN post that you're leaving and have your good-bye thread. There is no interest in cards where you already know what's going to happen. It's not much to ask ... but I am serious about this. It's not fair to the people putting their time in to keep the fed going to screw up everything they're doing and then have to come up with ways to work your character out as well. Next person that doesn't follow this will have their character given to Thommy, or Josh, or someone like them to dispose of as creatively as possible >=) It's not anyone in particular, Mask is just the breaking point for me ... I've seen this happen way too many times and it takes away a lot of the fun for us. "No-Showing" We all dislike no-showing. We especially dislike no-showing when there is a one RP limit and you have two weeks to RP! There is no way anyone here can't do one RP for a card. That is an absurdly low RP limit, and likely won't remain that low for much longer. From this point on, anyone who no-shows without a decent excuse will not be booked again until they have RPed. Anyone who no-shows twice in a month won't be booked again until they've RPed twice. Newcomers who no-show their first match without warning are fired immediately.
