Report by Bob the Interviewer Toenail Surgery Rocks Foundations -- May 20, 2002 Over his three years in the company, the Corporal of Punishment has proven to be the single most physically dominating mad-man in wrestling. Simply sitting back and waiting to be given instructions by whoever has employed the monstrous enforcer this month, the mindless charge of the Corporal has crushed everyone and everything that has been put in his way. Until now. The Good Corporal has become synonymous with physical decimation and torture within the confines of the UWF ... and it only makes it even more amazing that the man who has destroyed World Champions and Hall of Famers alike would finally be put out of action, not by a fellow wrestler bent on revenge, but ... by an ingrown toenail. Ever since his dominating performance against then champion Shawn Eclipse at the UWF's last Pay Per View, Nowhere to Hide, on April 17th, the Corporal has been on the injured reserve, suffering in painful silence as his left-pinkie toe was torn from one side to the other by the devastating effects of the vicious attacker. Even the mental anguish he has suffered by the knowledge that even a monster of his caliber could be defeated by such a simple ailment couldn't be compared to the pure physical torture the man-monster has lived through over the last month. Report by Jon Focker Nowhere To Hide shaping up nicely! -- April 11, 2002 We are less than a week away from UWF Nowhere To Hide. This Pay Per View is being looked upon as the event that turns the tide and dawns a new and improved era for the federation. Therefore, it must be successful for UWF to blossom. Things look great right now. The Plague is scheduled to combat his friend Gurgles for the Intercontinental Title. How will they interact beyond a match environment? Snake Phoenix will take on Ruff Ryder in defending his Shoot Wrestling Title which will probably end their epic feud. There will be all to wrestle for in this one - it transcends Titles. It means more than that. The stakes are very high indeed. It seems that there will be some sort of Tag interaction between The Dragoons and Youngblood members. Flip and Dex will be involved in some sort of rematch with Draven Lox, if my sources are correct. It is apparent that the World Title scene is a mixed bag. No one knows what will happen here but I am told that the bookers have something major planned that will set the road clear for Nowhere To Hide. Look for something to happen on Wednesday Night Universal. Expect impressive new team Local NVA Company to be involved, also we're bound to see Digital Mortality, Jamie Shock, Hybrid, Corporal Punishment and Mike Montgomery involved somewhere on the card. At this stage, I'd expect Jamie Shock to be involved in a programme with Draven Lox but doubt it to go down in a match capacity on Nowhere To Hide. After all, Draven Lox is being managed by Sarah Shock. Montgomery and Mortality are likely to interact, despite Montgomery's challenge to Team MIV for the Pay Per View. As for Hybrid and Corporal, we'll know very soon if they are ready to raise the bar and move into Shawn Eclipse's league. The Local NVA Company will probably just be involved in a squash match to set them on more winning ways. Expect a few surprises at Nowhere To Hide including a massive twist that even the bookers are questioning due to its enormity. I'll be back to preview it when I have more details. Stay tuned. Report by Ronald Gonnait Censorship Shock Scandal Update -- April 9, 2002 After a press release issued by our very own magazine by way of an editorial yesterday, proceedings were incited to further actions. The UWF Fanzine has never, nor will ever, censor its reporters was the opening statement by editor and chief at the hearing in Chicago's Court House yesterday morning. Everyone had tuned up for the exchange, the prosecution in the guise of Jayson Phoenix among his council members; the defence had hired multitalented Janitor Mik to handle their argument… UWF Owner Larry Clark had also turned up to show his support for the Fanzine that has kept the federation in good stead since day one. Shadow turned up and caused quite a stir in how he was presented in his nicely turned out suit. And even Digital Mortality was in attendance, sat on the railing for lack of any proper fence, as he told both parties in his best scouse accent to 'Calm down, calm down'. And of course Judge Finger in every Pie Harvell overlooked proceedings… GW was invited, but declined the offer for fear of a contagious virus spreading throughout his household. Jay took the stand first and began with his little nostalgia trip about how things used to be… back in the good ol'e days, which for many was welcomed since they had to get up rather early to be here and so appreciated the few minutes snooze. Janitor Mik quickly retorted to the story after being rudely awakened by the gavel and spat out: 'Perhaps our right honourable Phoenix hit his head a little too hard when he took that trip, for his recollection is greatly perverted.' Laughter drowned out all the noise pretty much after the word 'honourable'. The Judge banged his gavel again and asked for order… Larry was pretty hungry by this time. (Fat jokes are a prerequisite in the UWF when speaking about Larry or any of his incarnations and thus the Fanzine is not liable under any circumstances, Nemesis Inc. are the ones responsible.). Jay then went on to list how the UWF Fanzine, although funny (He mentioned it about twenty times, so we believe him to be sincere) had made his life a living hell and was now doing the same to others like him… The congregation shuddered at the thought of there being others like him. Janitor Mik now took the stand and retorted… He defended his case eloquently, and like all good defence lawyers went for the juggler… He started to pull evidence A, B, C… down to Z to substantiate the claims made by the Fanzine; even Shadow when called to the witness booth had to corroborate in a round about way. Janitor Mik then defend his own reputation, saying that he loved to clean the UWF, in fact he always had apart from when Phoenix was leaving a great mess in his trail of sloth. Janitor Mik then ended by highlighting Jay's own slanderous remarks made towards the UWF and refuted these to be preposterous. The case is set to continue for quite some time, but when asked to comment Jay said this: 'Screw you, this surely constitutes as a promotion… I just won't do it.' Janitor Mik was more approachable. 'We will fight them on the sea, we will fight them on our beaches but I draw the line at stepping onto EWA soil. I suggest they drop their case now and save everyone the heartache of being dragged through the courts… I have surprise witnesses clambering to be heard and each one is more surprising than the next!' We'll keep you update as the case progresses. Report by UWF Fanzine UWF Fanzine Shock at UWF(TM) Censorship -- April 8, 2002 Latest News… As expected, the UWF Fanzine was removed from shelves yesterday by the very company that it aims to support. As of yet, UWF top dogs have not commented… but chief editor of the magazine yesterday exclaimed: 'The UWF will have to learn that it survives solely because of media attention from the reporters and magazines dedicated to its shows… it is a partnership. If they continue to show flagrant disregard for our reporters creativity then I will be forced to take things further." He added… 'I will get to the bottom of this, the article in question though close to the bone for some EWA wrestlers no doubt was not at all based on any falsehood and all stories have and were substantiated either by their actions, fondness for the word 'AND' or confirmation from the nut house of their certification. The hypocrisy of all of this is that the very people whom took our magazines off the shelves and censored our material are the very same people circulating propaganda over at the EWA… They even provide scripted direction to their commentators to mock and berate the EWA. One rule for one, and another for others… this is the same ethics that got long standing publishing purchases the Welsh Academy of Noble Knowledge bared from the UWF Forums. Mark my words, heads will role for this, and there may be some UWF higher ups that are among them… we take no prisons and show no discrimination.' Report by Bill Schwartz Tag Team Wrestling Lives! -- March 31, 2002 No, you didn't read that title wrong ... tag team wrestling is officially alive within the confines of the UWF! After nearly five months of complete apathy to tag teams, the company began a massive hiring campaign over the last few weeks that culminated in the crowning of new World Tag Team Champions ... and really, what better way to start off the division right than crowning two singles wrestlers as the champs? Adam "Hybrid" Finn and Blade, collectively the Lost Prophets, captured the tag titles from the tag wrestling poster-boys, the Dragoons, at the Test of Faith PPV, cementing the fact that everyone's favorite division is alive and well. With high caliber teams like the Prophets and Dragoons, former GIWF Champions in the FoD, and extremely impressive rookies in Team MIV, Phat LTD, and new signees Hot Stuff, the division is expected to get a big push in the fed over the next few weeks ... just as long as the Dragoons get the titles.
