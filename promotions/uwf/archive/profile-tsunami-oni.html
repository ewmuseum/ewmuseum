W R E S T L E R P R O F I L E Date Of Birth: May 28, 1976 Height: 5'11' Weight: 217 lbs. Alignment: Face Theme Music: 'Just Because' by Jane's Addiction Home Town: Originally from Masuda, Japan, now residing in San Francisco, CA T I T L E H I S T O R Y UWF Title History: UWF Awards History: Other Titles: Other Awards: B I O G R A P H Y Tsunami Oni... When he burst on the scene, he was a masked supervillain determined to take over the world with his foul-mouthed dominatrix sidekick, Shiatsu. While he never did take over the world (although he did steal the launch codes to America's nukes when Dubya left them in a White House bathroom, subsequently using one to blow up the Loch Ness Monster), he did manage to capture the TV, Intercontinental, and World titles (the last two he held simultaneously for a brief time before 'losing' the IC belt in a Paper, Rock, Scissors Match against his then-ally, the Prodigy) as well as winning the notorious Contest of Champions. Some career highlights include... 1. Having PeTA activists in bunny suits assault Seymour Almasy 2. Beating Digital Mortality with an aluminum baseball bat while singing 'Take Me Out to the Ballgame.' 3. The Paddle on the Pole match between Shiatsu and the Dominatrix. 4. FAT MURPHY~! and the Table, Ladders, and Cats match. 5. The Evilutionary War and the subsequent ladder match against Unforgiven. 6. Using ShadowBlade's head as a tee after running him over with a golf cart. 7. The formation of the Network of Operatives for the Global Overthrow and Obliteration of Democracy, better know simply as N.O.G.O.O.D. 8. The debut of the Flying Monkey Bomb, greatest triple-team move ever, on Moraless (with the assistance of the Local NVA Company.) 9. Creating an imperfect duplicate of himself known as Bizarro Oni. Following Live and Loud in 2003 (in which he won the right to be called Greatest Cruiserweight Ever), Oni unmasked and vanished. He recently returned with a new look and slightly changed attitude, but still a success, winning the fifth Regal Rumble at Terror in Tokyo. Now more of an anti-hero that a supervillain, he's allied with Unforgiven, Slasher, and Misfit in the drowningPOOL. Hide the children and womenfolk... Strengths - Quite possibly the UWF's greatest high-flyer. Supremely confident and daring. Cunning strategist and master at rule-bending (not to mention rule-breaking). Weaknesses - Usually gives up size and strength to his opponents. High-risk moves have resulted in multiple knee surgeries. Past betrayals have made him an untrustworthy ally. M O V E S E T Wrestling Style Lucharesu Basic Moves Although the Onipacalypse is his favorite finisher, Tsunami Oni realizes the difficulty of the move and has other weapons in his arsenal to take down his opponents. This has the added bonus of making him less predictable and more dangerous. Seahawk Death Dive - 450 moonsault with a twist into a senton bomb. This move is a frequent finisher. Last Wave Before Sunset - Front superplex into an off the top rope sitout uranage (side effect). Another finisher and an effective counter to other top rope moves. MXC - Off the top rope cradle DDT. A potential finisher, although not as effective as the previously described moves. Named after Japan's other great import, Most Extreme Elimination Challenge. Baka Breaker - Forward Russian leg sweep with a hammerlock and a half nelson. 'Baka' is Japanese for 'fool.' A typical use of the move is to stop an opponent on the offensive, such using it after ducking a clothesline or slding out of a slam or powerbomb attempt. Tridentbuster - A trio of rolling vertical suplex brainbusters. A set-up move for a finisher. Deep Blue Bomb - Crossed-arm shoulder-mounted face-first powerbomb. Like an ocean cyclone suplex,but he powerbombs his victim on his face instead of falling back. An effective set-up for a finisher, but not the easiest move to perform quickly. Tidal Rage - Underhook powerbomb (tiger driver) into a spinebuster. A wear-down move. Diablosault - Top-rope quebrada (asai moonsault) double footstomp. Oni likes to use this before attemping a submission move. Sacrificial Alter Lock - An inverted surfboard with a chickenwing crossface. Oni's favorite submission move after the Onipocalypse. Anaconda Clutch - Step over toe hold with a cobra clutch. Yet another submission move Oni has added to his arsenal. Hellrazor - Inverted facelock neckbreak with an elbow smash. Usually good for a two-count, a wear-down move. Circus Tsunami - Ropewalk enzuigiri. Oni has hold of his opponent's arm and follows through a magistral (roll-up with a step over armbar). Another move good for a two-count. Butterfly Knife - springboard dragonrana - Tsunami Oni's original finisher, now less effective since it's been scouted so much. Still, it take most foes down for at least a two-count. Viscous Circle - springboard shooting star leg drop. Normally precedes another high-impact move. Imperial Crush - inverted suplex with hammerlock brainbuster. Another precursor to other high-impact moves. Poison Blowfish Special - pumphandle gutbuster. Mainly a wear-down move. Thermonuclear Elbow - handspring back elbow smash on the genitals of an opponent caught in a tree of woe. Saved for no DQ matches or when the referee is distracted. Angelwing Madhammer - Running no-hands springboard somersault knee smash suicida to the back of a bent-ober opponent's head. Multiple knee surgeries and Oni still does moves like this. Rising Pulsar Destroyer - Corkscrew shooting star press suicida. Definately an all-or-nothing move. Downfall of Western Civilization - Double-arm DDT off the apron to the floor. The move that Oni 'greeted' Seymour Almasy to the UWF, setting off their 2002 Feud of the Year. Shotei palm thrust - One favorite spot of Oni's is to deliver series of kicks to the legs and torso of his opponent, flip them off, the nail them with the shotei.
