NGW Sunday Night Carnage

2000-09-10
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

If you like what you see in this card then join NGW at http://ngwrestling.20m.com       (The camera opens up looking down upon a jam packed crowd awaiting the start of NGWs first ever Sunday Night
Carnage. The fans are holding up such signs as "Technical Ecstasy is Sexual
Ecstasy" and "Touch Down, Falco!". The fireworks then explode on the ramp signalling the start of the first ever Sunday Night Carnage and the crowd goes wild. As the fireworks die the camera cuts to the announce table where your commentators are Alex "Suicide" Hyde and Karl Hendrix.)
Hyde: Welcome everyone to the first ever Sunday Night Carnage. I am Alex "Suicide" Hyde and I am along side Karl Hendrix. We have agreat night instore for you folks at home and in attendance.
Hendrix: Thats right Alex, tonight I will debut a wrestler who will be NGWs first World champion.
Hyde: Remember our bet Karl, if he does not win the World title at Undisputed, you have to fight an opponent of my choosing.
Hendrix: I remember. (President McGoverns music booms over the speakers and he walks down the aisle and takes a seat beside Karl Hendrix and Alex Hyde at the announce table.)
Hendrix: Mr. McGovern, what a surprise it is to have you here. McGovern: It is your pleasure Hyde: Forgive me for asking Mr. McGovern, but why have you joined us out here? McGovern: Is it not obvious. The first match of the night involves the commisioner of NGW and I havve come down to have a closer look.
Hendrix: Fine by me. Mofo and "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero Vs Blaze and The Mad Cow- Tag tables match John Kent: The following match is a tag team tables match!, introducing first, the team of Mofo and "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero!!!!!! (Mofo and "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero make seperate entrances.) John Kent: And their opponents, the team of Blaze and The Mad Cow!!!!!! (Blaze and The Mad Cow make seperate entrances.) Hyde: This match will be a good warm up for Blaze seeing as he is in a tables match for the Internet title next Sunday. I do not have a clue what Victor Romero is even doing in the ring after that beat down last
Thursday. McGovern: Because I own him, he does what I tell him to do or I will fire his ass! Hyde: The winners of this match will be the team who puts both opponents through tables after offensive moves.
(The bell rings and all 4 men go at it in the middle of the ring. Mofo pairs off with The Mad
Cow and Romero pairs off with Blaze. Mofo then drop kicks The Mad Cow through the middle rope and to the outside as Romero whips Blaze to the ropes and nails him with a running clothesline. Romero then picks Blaze up and tosses him to the outside.)
Hyde: It has been all Victor Romero and Mofo thus far. (Mofo then nails The Mad Cow with a series of rights and slams him onto the announce table. Mofo then slides into the ring and climbs up to the top rope and looks down upon The Mad Cow on the outside laying on the announce table.)
Hendrix: He is not going to do what I think he is, is he? McGovern: You see this is why I chose this man as my commisioner. Not only is he good with paperwork but he can also prove his point in the ring.
( Mofo then launches himself through the air in an attempt to put The Mad Cow through the announce table witha flying elbow but The Mad Cow moves out of the way at the last second and Mofo is sent crashing through the announce table by himself.)
Hendrix: Does this mean Mofo is eliminated from this match? Hyde: No, it has to be an offensive move. (On the other side of the ring Romero starts to beat Blaze up the ramp. Blaze and Romero then reach the top of the ramp with Romero still in control. Romero then looks down the ramp to see two tables stacked on top of each other. Romero then grabs Blaze and tosses him off of the ramp, 10 feet and through the two tables.)
Hyde: That is Blaze eliminated and in need of medical attention! (Romero then starts to head back to the ring where The Mad Cow has picked Mofo up out of the table wreckage and is pounding away at him in the ring. Romero then looks under the ring and pulls out his kendo stick wrapped in barbed wire. Romero then sneaks up behind The Mad Cow and spins him around to nail him with a barbed wire kendo stick shot right to the head. The Mad Cow is then busted wide open as Romero orders Mofo to fetch a table. Mofo does so and sets up a table on the outside of the ring. Romero then drags The Mad Cow onto the apron where he attempts to powerbomb him through the table but The Mad Cow blocks it and turns it into a hurracurrana on Romero through the ringside table.)
Hyde: My God! did you just see that?!?!? ( Mofo then pounces on The Mad Cow straight away and whips him into the steel steps. Mofo then grabs another  table and sets it up and places The Mad Cow on top of it. Mofo then pulls a ladder from under the ring and sets it up beside the table. Mofo then climbs to the top of the ladder and looks down upon The Mad Cow laying on the table when suddenly Blaze comes out of nowhere and also climbs to the top of the ladder. Mofo then see's Blaze and the two of them exchange punches on top of the ladder.)
Hyde: How is Blaze even able to walk let alone fight?!?!? (Blaze gets the better of Mofo on top of the ladder and has Mofo reeling 15 feet above the table. The Mad Cow then rolls off of the table as Blaze nails Mofo with a big right hand and sends him flying 15 feet off of the ladder towards the table but before he can crash into the table Victor
Romero comes out of nowhere and shoves the table out of the way and Mofo falls onto the hard concrete.)
Hendrix: I do not think Victor Romero was doing Mofo any favours there but he has kept his team in the match! (Blaze is then furious about Romero moving the table and Blaze jumps 15 feet off of the ladder to nail Romero with a cross body as the crowd chants "Holy Shit!". The
Mad Cow and Blaze then put a major beatdown on Mofo and Romero and drag them towards the ring. Blaze then sets up another table at ringside and powerbombs
Romero through it.)
Hyde: That does not count as Victor Romero is already eliminated. (Blaze then rolls Mofo into the ring along with a table. Blaze then sets up the table and shouts at The Mad Cow to get something. The Mad
Cow then looks under the ring and pulls out a can of gasoline.)
Hyde: Oh My God no! McGovern: If they go through with this they wil both get a huge fine for doing this to the commisioner.
Hendrix: Is this what is instore for Dr. Lobotomy next Sunday? (Blaze then grabs the can of gasoline and pours it all over the table. He then pulls a lighter out of his tights and sets the table on fire as the crowd explodes in cheers. Blaze then shouts something at The Mad Cow again as he climbs up to the top rope. At Blazes instruction The Mad Cow then hoists Mofo up onto his shoulders.)
Hyde: My God! They are going to give Mofo a Doomsday Device through the flaming table!!! (Just as they are getting ready to attempt this Victor Romero recovers on the outside and grabs the ladder and positions it on the outside right next to the corner on which Blaze is standing and  climbs to the top of it. At the same time Blaze flys through the air on a Doomsday Device attempt
Romero dives off the ladder and flys through the air and spears Mofo off of The
Mad Cows shoulders and down to the mat. Blaze then flys over The Mad Cow and crashes into the flaming table and screams out in pain as he catches fire.)
Hyde: Holy Shit! Romero just speared his own partner from 15 feet up off of The Mad Cows shoulders just so he would not be put through the flaming table!!!!
(The ref then grabs a fire extinguisher and puts Blaze out as Rommero quickly grabs the barbed wire kendo stick on the mat and nails The Mad
Cow with it. Mofo then goes to the outside and slides a table into the ring and sets it up. Romero then powerbombs The Mad Cow through the table to win the match.)
Hyde: That is a match that will not be forgotten in a hurry! McGovern: I am out of here. ( President McGovern then leaves the announce table and joins Mofo in celbrating in the ring.) John Kent: The winners of this match, the team of "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero and
Mofo!!!! Hendrix: I am getting a copy of that match so I can watch it again and again at home. Hyde: Only in NGW will you see action like this! (The camera then cuts backstage where we see Blaze walking around in his scorched ring attire after the match when suddenly Dr. Lobotomy comes from behind and smashes a glass container of anesthesia over Blazes head. Blaze is then knocked out cold as Dr. Lobotomy stomps him a couple of times and walks off laughing. The camera then cuts back to the announce table.)
Hendrix: It looks like Dr. Lobotomy got revenge for Blazes attack last Thursday. Hyde: I can not wiat for theri match at Undisputed next week! Hendrix: You know what time it is Alex? Hyde: No, what time is it? Hendrix: It is time for me to debut my mystery wrestler. Hyde: Thats right, I can not wait to see what loser has alligned himself with you. Hendrix: He is no loser, he is the future NGW World champion!

Join NGW at http://ngwrestling.20m.com        Dr. Lobotomy Vs ?-Karl Hendrix's Mystery Man John Kent: This contest is schedualed for one fall, introducing first, Dr. Lobotomy!!!! ( Dr. Lobotomy makes his entrance.) Hyde: Dr. Lobotomy was seen attacking Blaze just a few minutes ago with a glass container. ( Karl Hendrix then leaves the announce table and enters the ring where he grabs the microphone off of John
Kent.) Hendrix: I ask everyone at home to press record on their VCRs because this will be a moment that you will want to watch over and over again.
You are all about to lay your eyes on the future NGW World Champion. Ladies and
Gentlemen may I introduce you to your ne hero.................. The
Multicoloured Avenger!!!!!! ( The NGW jobber the Multicoloured Avenger then makes his entrance with his outlandish ring attire and cape and some patriotic music.)
Hyde: Oh goive me a break, The Multicoloured is nothing more but a mere jobber in NGW and Karl Hendrix is hyping him up as the future
World Champion. I think my bet is as good as won.
( Karl Hendrix then starts clapping for The Multicoloured Avenger as he grabs a microphone.) Avenger: I was watching wrestling at home one day and I saw all the filth and villans that were corrupting this sport and I thought to myself that this sport needs a super hero and I am just that.
I am The Multicoloured Avenger and I fight for Truth, Justice and the American way. I will be a champion that your children will be able to look up to. No longer will you have to fear for your childs mind being corrupted by wrestling because I am here to rid NGW of all the evil villans.
Hyde: Give me a break, did Karl find this guy in a loony bin? Avenger: Dr. Lobotomy, you are the first evil villan on my hitlist. (The bell then rings as Karl Hendrix climbs out of the ring and sits back down at the announce table   )
Hendrix: What do you think Alex? Hyde: I think you are crazy if you think you are going to win our bet with him. ( Dr. Lobotomy then clotheslines The Multicoloured Avenger and covers him.) 1 2 3 John Kent: The winner of this match in 12 seconds, Dr. Lobotomy!!!! Hyde: Ha ha, that is a record time win for Dr. Lobotomy and he won with a simple clothesline.
Hendrix: What the Hell! Hyde: That is your so called future champ, huh? Hendrix: Don't worry this is a minor set back, I will have him in shape by next Sunday. Hyde: Sure you will. Ha ha. Hendrix: Stop laughing damn it! Hyde: I can't help it. Hendrix: Lets just move on to our next match ok! Hyde: Ok, ha ha Hendrix: Grrrrr! "Technical Ecstasy" Travis Martin Vs Clive John Kent: Introducing first, "Technical Ecstasy" Travis Martin!!!! ( Travis Martin makes his entrance.) John Kent: And his opponent, Clive!!!!! ( Clive makes his entrance.) Hyde: This should be good. (The bell ring s and both men lock up. Martin works Clive down into a headlock but Clive forces his way out of it and shoves Travis Martin to the ropes. Martin then comes off of the ropes to receive a back body drop from Clive. Clive then runs andjumps to the top rope and attempts a moonsault but Martin moves out of the way and Clive is sent crashing to the mat.)
Hyde: It is back and forth in the opening stages of this match! ( Martin and Clive then both get up at the same time but Martin beats Clive to the punch and clotheslines him down to the mat.
Martin then starts stomping away at Clive and then picks him up. Martin then whips Clive into the corner and Clive bounces back out to receive a samoan drop by Martin. Martin then covers.)
1 2 Kickout Hendrix: He needs to wear Clive down a bit more. (Martin then quickly locks Clive in a figure four leg lock and Clive screams out in pain. Martin then tightens the hold and
Clive has nowhere to go because he is in the middle of the ring. Clive then sits up in the figure four and nails Martin with a series of right hands in an attempt to break the hold but Martin holds on and applys more pressure which sends Clive on his back.)
Hyde: If Travis Martin applys any ore pressure he will break Clives leg! ( Clive then sits up again and starts punching Martin but Martin still holds on. Clive then rolls on his side and trys to reverse the hold but Martin is using all his strength to hold Clive where he is.
Clive then suddenly gets a surge of energy and turns over to reverse the hold and apply the pressure on Martin. Martin then breaks the hold straight away and both men stand up with a limp.)
Hendrix: They do not call Travis Martin "Technical Ecstasy" for nothing! ( Clive then charges towards Travis Martin but Travis Martin takes him down to the mat with a boot followed by a
DDT. Martin then quickly jumps on top of Clive and locks on his finisher "Spinal
Tap". Clive then taps out within seconds as he can not stand the pain)
John Kent: The winner of this match, "Technical Ecstasy" Travis Martin!!!! Hyde: That is 2 wins out of 2 matches for Travis Martin both with that lethal finisher "Spinal Tap" I wonder if anyone will be able to hold out when that move is locked on them.
Hendrix: I would not want to be locked in it thats for sure. Hyde: It is time to move on to our main event!

Join NGW at http://ngwrestling.20m.com   "All Pro" Nick Falco Vs Moke Doshky Vs Janus- Olympic Challenge match John Kent: Introducing first, representing the USA, "All Pro" Nick Falco!!!!!!! ( Nick Falco makes his entrance.) John Kent: And his first opponent, representing Germany, Moke Doshky!!!! (Moke Doshky makes his entrance.) John Kent: And the final participant in this match, representing the United Kingdom, Janus!!!!
(Janus makes his entrance.) Hyde: All men representing their country here in this Olympic Challenge match. (The bell rings and all 3 men meet in the middle of the ring.  Janus sends Moke Doshky down to the mat witha right hand and Falco does the same to Janus. Falco then waits for Janus to get back up before spearing him back down to the mat.)
Hyde: Touch down by Nick Falco! Hendrix: Where are the cheerleaders when you need them! ( Nick Falco then covers Janus.) 1 2 Moke Doshky breaks up the count. (Doshky then picks Falco up and whips him to the ropes. Doshky then goes to the ropes and comes off of them to nail Falco with a cross body.
Doshky then picks Falco up and whips him into the corner. Doshky then follows up the whip with an avalanche on Falco.)
Hendrix: I think Falco needs to get in a huddle and discus his game plan here. (Doshky then boots Falco a couple of times and then sits him on the top rope and climbs up with him but
Janus then sneaks up from behind and climbs up as well. Doshky then goes to give
Falco a superplex at the same time that Janus goes to give Doshky a German
Superplex. The reult ends up being Janus giving Doshky a German superplex while
Doshky gives Falco a superplex. All three men hit the mat hard and are sprawled on the mat.)
Hyde: I have never ever seen a move like that! (All three men stay on the mat as the ref begins a ten count. 1-2-3-4-5-6-Janus gets to his knees. Janus then drops an arm on top of Moke Doshky.)
1 2 2 3/4 Kickout Hyde: How did Moke Doshky kick out!?! ( Janus then gets to his feet at he same time as Nick Falco. They both then charge to each other and both have the same idea. They both put there arms out for clotheslines and the result is a double clothesline which knocks the wind out of both men and sends them crashing to the mat. Moke Doshky then starts to stir and crawls over to the two fallen men and places his arm over the chest of
Janus.) 1 2 3   Hyde: Janus has been eliminated! Hendrix: I guess Janus will have to settle for a bronze medal. (Moke Doshky then crawls over to Falco who is also winded and puts his arm over him.) 1 2 2 3/4 Shoulder Up. Hyde: It was nearly over there! (Doshky then gets up and starts stomping away at Falco. Doshky then goes to pick up Falco only to receive a low blow. Falco then sticks
Doshky uin between his legs and goes to nail him with his finisher "Intentional
Grounding" (powerbomb) but Moke Doshky manages to slip out of it and down behind
Falco. Falco then turns around to be locked in the "Mokey Squash" (Bear Hug).
Falco trys to fight out of it but Moke Doshkys grip is too tight. Falco then starts to wear down.)
Hyde: Will Nick Falco be able to break out of the Mokey Squash? (Falcos head then droops down as the ref goes to check on him. The ref lifts his hand up once and drops it. He lifts it up twice and drops it. He lifts it up a third time and.........drops it. The ref then calls for the bell.)
John Kent: The winner of this match, Moke Doshky!!!! ( The ref then puts a gold medal over the head of Moke Doshky.) Hyde: Well folks thaty is all the time we have for you tonight. There will be no Bombshell! or Carnage this week because they have both been cancelled so that NGW can prepare for one Hell of a Pay-Per-View next Sunday. This is Alex "Suicide" Hyde for Karl Hendrix saying good night.
( Sunday Night Carnage comes to a close.)
