A free agent? Or just looking for something new? Join! NEW GENERATION WRESTLING EXPLANATION OF N.G.W.- We at New Generation Wrestling come to you to offer something fresh. Something Original. Most E-Wrestling is just competition between feds and "Superstars". New Generation Wrestling's goal is Entertainment first, Not Ego's first. You see the Producers of New Generation Wrestling are
Professional's who have been around Wrestling and can call themselves Veterans without having to worry about what others will say because the NGW staff has seen everything..... NGW is not one of those highly organized,if you don't join at the beginning of the league your screwed,and rarely get a match because of it.It's a laid back-have fun league wich doesn't favor anyone that joins earlier over later joining members.I know how that feels that's why it's a first come,first serve league. The NGW ENCOURAGES newcomers to join who want to prove something and discourages Veterans who feel they have nothing to prove,Unless they can back it up.... Whenever their is a league-tourney for a belt,whoever E-mails me first with their wrestler(s)
will get in first rather than me putting in who I think are the best wrestler's in the tourney and the rest don't even get a chance.I don't think that's fair and it's not the type of league I wanna run.That's why this league won't have to many people in it so everyone will get a chance.DON'T join if your in like 20 wrestling leagues already,because those people tend to pay less attention to the league and get it mixed up with other leagues.
And you cannot expect to join and get a match,I'll give you some matches but other than that I'm not gunna manually give you matches you'll have to challenge people and roleplay alot.Well anyway fill out and read the rest of the form and I hope you enjoy the league.REMEMBER You can send this back. but please have your Wrestlers name where it says "SUBJECT" on the mail form And Erase the Useless Crap I
Don't need.

4.NWF CHAMPIONSHIP BELTS -NWG World Heavyweight belt -NWG Intercontinental belt -NWG Extreme Heavyweight Belt -NWG Cage Belt (belt can only be won if wrestling in steel cage) -NWG Cruiserweight Belt (light weight belt) -NWG Tag Team Titles Each belt must be defended at least once a month. 5.STATS Divide 55 POINTS into these categories (10 points maximum in a category) -STRENGTH 10=Superior in that catergory -TOUGHNESS 1=Not superior in that catergory -INTELLIGENCE 0=has no realm of that catergory -CHEATING -BRAWLING -TECHNICAL WRESTLER -AERIAL -QUICKNESS -HARM (how much harm your wrestler move have on the opponent) -FAN SUPPORT -TEAM WORK 6.WRESTLERS IT's pretty simple,their is a 4 wrestler limit to each person. 7.WINNING THE CHAMPIONSHIP BELT To win the championship belt you must win by pinning your opponent!You cannot win by Disqualification,Draw,or Countout unless it is a Special Match
8.MEMBERS Members who don't reply back from letters,don't participate,or not hear from at most in 1 month are subject to suspension or ejection out of the NWF because we want participating members or else it ruins it for everyone.I don't wanna make this sound like Oh I'm leaving Better tell him That I'm leaving but please tell me when your going on Vacations or give a new address to where your going if you want your mail delivered there.
9.JUST IN CASE Their are real-life things like injury,fatigue,suspension, and fines in the
NGW.If it happens to happen to your wrestler don't worry it's just part of the WWFL and no offense should be taken.If I suspend your wrestler it just means that particular wrestler you own can't wrestle for the suspension time.All other wrestlers you own will still be put on cards.
If I suspend YOUR WRESTLER it doesn't mean YOUR SUSPENDED from the NGW. Just to make everything clear. 10.CARDS- Cards are every Sunday.Sometimes your wrestler might go up against a jobber for
3 reasons,practice,to get your name out,Variety.These cards are not kid friendly,in other words they MIGHT sometimes say profane words.But I won't put profane words in just to say profane words,it'll be
'cause of the match.Just so your warned and you don't have a cow.All Cards are sent through E-MAIL.
11.CONTRACTS Yes the NWF is as real as it can get,so that means in the NWF we have contracts.After you send me your guys I will send a contract and money offer.You can either reject the offer and ask for more weeks/months/or years or just more money (the fake part of the deal).After your contract is up the
NWF might send you another contract offer,if he's getting alot of action,or
I might not offer you another contract and that wrestler might not be in the
NWF for a while 'till I decide I want that guy back in the NGW.The NGW has the right to release any Wrestler in the NGW before his contract is up.And also if I release or not offer another a contract to one of your guys and not the other you can create another guy because you only have 1 guy currently wrestling in the NGW.(Example:I release one guy of yours and not the other,you can create another Wrestler because you only have 1 wrestler in the
NGW,Because the other ones currentley not in the NGW)
12.LEAVING THE NGW You can also tell me if you want a wrestler of yours to leave the NGW for a little while or forever to use that spot to make up another Wrestler,and just to have him leave.You as a screename can also leave the NGW also.I won't take it personally,but when you get tired of the league just tell me that you want to leave it.
