NGW Thursday Night Bombshell!

2000-09-07
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you like what you see in this card check out and join our fed at http://ngwrestling.20m.com      (The NGW logo appears on the screen and stays there for about 5 seconds until it is broken through by the Thursday Night Bombshell! logo. The camera then fades in on a screaming crowd who can not wait for the first ever NGW event. Thousands of fans rush at the sight of the camera in an attempt to get their faces on TV as the fireworks explode on the ramp signalling the start of the first ever
Thursday Night Bombshell!. As the fireworks die the cameras cut to your commentators who are Alex "Suicide" Hyde and Karl Hendrix.)
  Hyde: Welcome one and all to the first NGW live event, Thursday Night Bombshell! I am Alex "Suicide" Hyde and this along side me is Karl Hendrix.    Hendrix: We have a big night in store for you all so don't change the channel!   (The camera then cuts backstage and we see President McGovern chatting away to someone who we can not see as he has his back turned to the camera.)
  President McGovern: Just sign here and you will be the commisioner of NGW.   (The man then takes out a pen and signs a contract out in front of him.)   President McGovern: Glad I could do business with you Mr. Commisioner.   (The camera then cuts back to the announce table.)   Hendrix: How come we were not informed about the change in staff?   Hyde: It is just another person for you to suck up to Karl but
I am also curious to see who our new commisioner is.
  Hendrix: You and me both.   Hyde: Well it is time for our first match. It will put "Technical Ecstasy" Travis Martin against "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero. Both men should be honoured to be in the opening match for NGW and this should prove to be an exciting contest.
  "Technical Ecstasy" Travis Martin Vs "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero   John Kent: The following match is schedualed for one fall, introducing first, "Mr. Violence" Victor Romero!!!!!
  (Victor Romero makes his entrance.)   John Kent: And his opponent, "Technical Ecstasy" Travis
Martin!!!!   (Travis Martin makes his entrance.)   Hendrix: I am looking forward to this one!   (The bell rings and both men lock up in the centre of the ring. Romero over powers Martin and tosses him into the corner. Martin pauses for a moment to get his plan back together and him and Romero start circling each other in the middle of the ring. Romero then lunges towards Martin but
Martin moves out of the way and drop toe holds Romeros throat across the middle rope.)
  Hyde: Travis Martin is getting the early advantage.   Hendrix: I see that Victor Romero has brought a kendo stick wrapped in barbed wire with him, I wonder if that will come into play.
  Hyde: Well, he will be disqualified if he uses it.   (Travis Martin goes to work on Romero with a series of stomps. He then picks Romero up and bashes his head into a turnbuckle. Martin then sits
Romero up on the top rope and climbs up with him. Martin then attempts a superplex but Romero blocks it. Romero then knees Martin in the groin and flips over his back down to the mat. Martin is then left on the top rope holding his groin as Romero gets underneath him and powerbombs him from the top rope and down to the mat. Romero then covers Martin.)
1 2 Kickout   Hyde: You have to be made of tough stuff to kick out of a move like that!   Hendrix: I could have kicked out at 1!   Hyde: How about we get in the ring and I give you a powerbomb from the top rope and we will see if you kickout at all!
  Hendrix: No, I am fine thanks.   (Martin lays dazed on the mat as Romero slowly climbs up to the top rope. Romero then attempts a flying leg drop but at the last second
Travis Martin moves out of the way and Romero is sent crashing down to the mat. Both men then stir to get up but Martin beats Romero up and clotheslines
Romero down to the mat. Martin then grabs Romeros legs and locks Romero in a boston crab and Romero screams out with pain.)
  Hyde: There is Technical Ecstasy for you right there!   Hendrix: I could do a better boston crab than that!          (Romero then slowly inch by inch makes his way to the ropes and grabs on forcing Martin to break the hold. Martin then goes to pick up
Romero only to receive a low blow followed by a DDT. Romero then picks Martin up and piledrives him into the mat and covers.)
1 2 Kickout   Hyde: I do not know how Travis Martin keeps kicking out.   (Romero pounds his fists against the mat in frustration and slides to the outside of the ring and grabs his kendo stick wrapped in barbed wire.)
  Hyde: Is he crazy? If he uses that he will be DQed for sure!   Hendrix: Use it!   ( The crowd screams for blood as Romero enters the ring with the barbed wire kendo stick and takes aim at Travis Martins head but just as he is about to swing the ref comes up from behind him and snacthes the barbed wire kendo stick away from him. Romero then turns around and starts shouting things at the ref that are bleeped out by the production crew. The distraction is all Travis Martin needs to recover as he sneaks up on Romero from behind and clotheslines him down to the mat. Martin then quickly locks his finisher "Spinal
Tap" on Romero and Romero taps out within seconds from the awesome pain.
The bell rings but Martin keeps the hold locked on.)   John Kent: The winner of this match via submission, "Technical
E
cstasy" Travis Martin!!!!!   
  (A bunch of NGW officials then run down in an attempt to break the hold but Martin keeps it locked on. Martin keeps Romero in the hold then for another two minutes and Romero passes out from the pain of this vicious finishing move. Martin then finally releases the hold but he is not done yet.
The officials tend to Romero as Travis Martin grabs the barbed wire kendo stick on the mat. He spins head referee Earl Johnson around and nails him with a shot right to the head.)
  Hyde: That is going to get Travis Martin fined!   (Travis Martin then knocks all the officials out of the way and  puts Romero on his kness. Martin then nails Romero with a full force shot from the barbed wire kendo stick right to the head and Romero gets busted wide open. Travis Martin then leaves the ring with a smirk on his face as Victor Romero is stretchered to the back.)
  Hyde: "Technical Ecstasy" Travis Martin has made a statement in his first match that he is not to be messed with. And I am sure that he will be fined for this.
  Hendrix: If I was in that ring I would have made shorter work of Romero.   Hyde: Listen Karl, I have been a wrestler before and you have not. I know for a fact that you would not know the first thing about wrestling.
Just stick to your job and call the damn matches!   Hendrix: I beg to differ, I bet I could take anyone from the back and manage them to a NGW World title. If I win you have to wear a dress for the rest of the year and if you win, I will get into a wrestling ring against an opponent of your choice. The end of this bet will be in a months time, so how about it Alex, or are you starting to doubt yourself?
  Hyde: Your on Karl and I look forward to seeing you in the ring.   Hendrix: I will debut my mystery wrestler on Carnage and show the World just how much about wrestling I know.
  Hyde: Sure you will Karl. It is time for our next match!   The Mad Cow Vs Mofo   John Kent: The following contest is schedualed for one fall, introducing first, The Mad Cow!!!!
  (The Mad Cow makes his entrance.)   John Kent: And his opponent..............   (John Kent is interupted by the sound of the song "Why does it always rain on me?" by Travis and President Michale McGovern makes his way to the ring and grabs the microphone from John Kent.)
  President McGovern: I will handle this John.   Hyde: I wonder what business President McGovern has out here.   President McGovern: Now introducing The Mad Cows opponent, he is the new commisioner of NGW, he is Mofo!!!!!!
  (Mofo makes his entrance.)   Hyde: So it was Mofo who President McGovern was talking to earlier on!   President McGovern: I also forgot to mention that this match is now no DQ!   (President McGovern then steps out of the ring as the bell rings and the match gets underway. Mofo and The Mad Cow then go at it head on.
The Mad Cow then takes Mofo down with a clothesline straight away and picks him back up and whips him into the ropes. The Mad Cow then gives Mofo a back body drop down to the mat.)
  Hyde: This start to the match by Mofo does not seem to be pleasing President McGovern.   (The Mad Cow then goes to the ropes but President McGovern hooks onto his leg and trips him up. The Mad Cow then bounces right back up and hops to the outside where he chases President McGovern into the back. The Mad
Cow then starts to head back towards the ring when he is confronted with a vicious clothesline  from Mofo in the aisle. Mofo then picks The Mad Cow up and whips him into the security guard railing and clotheslines him over it and into the crowd.)
  Hendrix: They are in a sea of NGW fans!   ( The Mad Cow and Mofo then brawl through the crowd. Mofo loses his advantage as The Mad Cow beats Mofo for the crowd with hard lefts and rights. Then with one big right The Mad Cow sends Mofo flying into the security guard rail. Mofo then charges towards Mofo but Mofo ducks and back body drops
The Mad Cow to ringside. Mofo then climbs on top of the security guard rail and jumps off of it in an attempt to give The Mad Cow a hurracurrana but The Mad
Cow blocks it and turns it into a powerbomb on the concrete on
Mofo.)
  Hyde: I could feel that one myself.   (President McGovern then suddenly runs back out as The Mad Cow rolls Mofo into the ring and covers him.)
1 2 2 3/4 President McGovern breaks up the count   Hyde: That dirty son of a.......   (The Mad Cow then gets up and stares at President McGovern with hostile inten when suddenly Mofo crawls up behind The Mad Cow and rolls him up from behind and President McGoverns runs over and holds down The Mad Cows shoulders.)
1 2 3   Hyde: President McGovern and Mofo have just robbed The Mad Cow of a victory!   John Kent: The Winner of this match, Mofo!!!   (President McGovern and Mofo then leave together.)   Hendrix: That was a good match.   Hyde: It was but The Mad Cow was still screwed.   Hendrix: Get over it, anyway it is time for our next match.   Hyde: Yes, it is between Blaze and Moke Doshky, lets get you to ringside.   Blaze Vs Moke Doshky   John Kent: The following contest is schedualed for one fall. Introducing first, Blaze!!!   (Blaze makes his entrance.)   Announcer: And his opponent, Moke Doshky!!!   (Moke Doshky makes his entrance.)   Hyde: Moke Doshky is all the way from Germany.   (The bell rings and Moke Doshky takes Blaze down with a clothesline. Moke keeps Blaze down with some stomps to the face before picking him back up. Moke then whips Blaze to the ropes and goes to the ropes himself and comes off to nail Blaze with a flying clothesline. Moke then picks Blaze back up and snap mares him down to the mat and into a rear chin lock.)
  Hyde: The start of this match is all Moke Doshky!   (Moke then locks Blaze in a sleeper and Blaze trys to fight his way out of it. Blaze then starts to wear down and he falls to his knees as Moke keeps the sleeper applyed. Moke then wears Blaze down to the mat with the sleeper as the ref goes to check on him. He grabs Blazes hand and raises it and drops it for one. He picks it up again and drops it for two. He then goes to drop it a third time but this time Blaze keeps his hand up.)
  Hendrix: Blaze managed to stay awake.   (Blaze then starts to feel the andreneline flowing through him and he rises to his feet while still in the sleeper. He nails Moke with a couple of elbows into the stomach and Moke lets go. laze then goes to the ropes and comes back off of them to nail Moke with a Fame-ass-er. Blaze then covers
Moke.)
1 2 Kickout   Hyde: Moke Doshky is not done yet.   (Blaze then goes to the ropes again as Moke gets back to his feet. Blaze attempts a cross body but Moke catches him in mid air and locks him in his finisher "The Mokey Squash" (Bear hug). Blaze trys to fight out of it but just like the sleeper he gets worn down but he refuses to give in and stays up fighting in the Mokey Squash. Blaze then raises his handand gives Moke a thumb in the eye which gets him to break the hold.)
  Hyde: Blaze uses an old school tactic to break the Mokey
Squash, I used to use the old thumb in the eye in my day.
  Hendrix: How long ago was that? Egyptian times?   (Moke then covers his eye as Blaze goes to the ropes again and comes off of them to nail a spinning heel kick on Moke. Blaze then goes to pick up Moke but Moke quickly gets up and locks another Mokey Squash on Blaze but it turns out not to be a Mokey Squash but a belly to belly suplex. The suplex takes Blaze by surprise and knocks the wind out of him as  Moke covers and the ref counts.)
1 2 3   John Kent: The winner of this match, Moke Doshky!!!!   Hyde: Moke Doshky picks up the win, and I hear it is already time to movve onto our next match.
  Clive Vs Dr. Lobotomy   John Kent: This match is schedualed for one fall. Introducing first, Clive   (Clive makes his entrance)   John Kent: And his opponent, Dr. Lobotomy   (Dr. Lobotomy makes his entrance.)   Hyde: This is the main event of the night   ( The bell rings and they both lock up. Lobotomy gets the better of Clive and shoves him back. Clive then comes back and nails Dr.
Lobotomy with a hard right. Dr. Lobotomy then punches back and before they know it, Clive and Dr. Lobotomy are exchanging lefts and rights in the middle of the ring. Dr. Lobotomy then gets the better of the exchange and sends Clive reeling into the ropes with a big right hand.)
  Hyde: Clive is finding it hard to get around Dr. Lobotomy.   (Dr. Lobotomy then nails Clive with a few more lefts and rights against the ropes before clotheslining him over the top and to the outside. Dr. Lobotomy then follows Clive to the outside and whips him into the steel steps. Dr. Lobotomy then charges towards a dazed Clive but Clive moves out of the way at the last second and Dr. Lobotomy is sent crashing into the steel steps.)
  Hendrix: Ouch!   (Clive then picks up Dr. Lobotomy and rolls him into the ring. Clive then goes to pick up Dr, Lobotomy only to be rolled up for a cover.) 1 2 2 3/4 Kickout   Hyde: That surprise roll up took Clive off guard, it was almost over right there.   (Dr. Lobotomy then gets up before Clive and clotheslines him down to the mat. Dr. Lobotomy then picks Clive up and powerbombs him down to the mat but as he does so Blaze runs out from the back carrying a steel folding chair. Blaze then runs into the ring and nearly takes Dr. Lobotomys head off with the chair busting him wide open as the ref calss for the bell.)
  John Kent: The winner of this match by DQ, Dr. Lobotomy.   Hyde: Dr. Lobotomy seems to have an unresolved issue here with
Blaze.   ( Blaze nails Dr. Lobotomy with a few more chair shots before leaving. Dr. Lobotomy is then helped to the back as Clive just shrugs and leaves.)
  Hyde: What a night it has been but unfortunately it must come to an end. Join us on Sunday Night Carnage where a lot of things will be explained and Karl Hendrix will debut his so called wonder wrestler. This is
Alex "Suicide" Hyde for Karl Hendrix saying good night.
  (Thursday Night Bombshell! comes to a close.)                NGW http://ngwrestling.20m.com
