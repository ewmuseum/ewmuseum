Lone Star Wrestling Union

4 December 1999

Lone Star Wrestling Union http://lswu.cjb.net Saturday Night Breaking Point: December 4th 1999 Live from The Astrodome in Houston, TX [The Breaking Point music starts to play and the camera flashes into the
Houston Astrodome which looks about half full. The lights go out inside the arena and red laser lights kick in making the shape of the LSWU star on the canvas in the middle of the ring. The star spins around and the
Breaking Point logo is now in the ring. The lights return and the camera pans over to the announcers' table with Lance Richardson, Richie Delaney and Jerry Cook.]
LR: Welcome to SATURDAY NIGHT BREAKING POINT! I'm Lance Richardson and to my right are Richie Delaney and Jerry Cook.
RD: It's nice to be here in Houston, Texas for tonight's show. I'm sure we are going to have a lot of fun watching after last night's highly successful Uncensored!
LR: We have 2 interviews to begin with tonight and I am told that it will be followed by the big one! The battle royal for the LSWU
Southwestern Championship Belt! RD: That's going to be dinoasaurus! Who are these interviews with anyway? LR: I'm not sure, but we'll find out soon enough! RD: God damn you and your large amount of patience! I wanna know now! ["Hear It" by Bad Religion plays over the speakers without much crowd reaction as Thomas Rainka steps out from the back and raises his arms in the air. He is wearing black jeans, black boots and gray framed sunglasses with orange tinted lenses. He down to the ring and calls for a microphone with one of the ring crew throwing him one.]
TR: Finally there is someone here in the LSWU that can entertain all of you minimum wage working, beef jerky eating lowlifes that are sitting in the crowd!
[BOO!] RD: I kind of like this guy! TR: Finally the greatest technical and power wrestler you will ever see in your pointless lives is here to show you what wrestling is really all about! In less than 30 minutes is the Southwestern Title Battle Royal and by the end of Breaking Point I will be the Southwestern Champion...
I know it and every single on of you know it! Everybody knows it and just to prove it, let's have somebody come up here... umm...
[Rainka points at an average man in the crowd who is wearing the LSWU
T-shirt and black tracksuit pants. Rainka tells him to come into the ring and this man climbs over the barrier and into the ring and stands next to Thomas Rainka.]
TR: So... what is your name? Man: Steven... Steven Hall! TR: Hey Steven, can I call you Steve? Anyway Steve, you look like a smart man! Are you a big wrestling fan?
SH: I sure am, I've been watching it for years! TR: That's great, so who's your pick to win the Battle Royal tonight at
Breaking Point? SH: I've seen a bit of VWA so I'll have to say Clement Tyme would be my tip! TR: Is that so? Well you are entitled to your opinion... but that doesn't mean that I have to like it!
[Rainka drops the microphone and punches Steve in the face. He falls to the ground and Rainka stomps him a few times and the crowd keeps on booing. Suddenly "Give it Away" by the Red Hot Chilli Peppers hits the arena and the crowd go into a frenzy as President Tuckwell steps out from behind the curtain and looks directly at Thomas Rainka.]
BT: Rainka, I will not have you beat down any innocent fans like that in my arena, so I'm going to withdraw you from the battle royal later on tonight!
TR: HEY, YOU CAN'T DO THAT TO ME! BT: I can do whatever the hell I damn well please because I write the rules, I run the game! But... if you get down on your knees and beg like a good little boy, I might reconsider my decision!
[LARGE POP!] TR: I'm not doing anything for you! BT: Alright then, if you want your spot back in that Southwestern Title battle royal, you gotta earn it! So right now in the middle of that ring... it will be Thomas Rainka versus not one person, not two people but three people!
[HUGE POP!] BT: So let's bring out your opponent's shall we? Please welcome Shawn
Brown, Ray Hughes and Darren Harvey!
[Shawn Brown, Ray Hughes and Darren Harvey enter the arena to a small pop wearing identical clothes consisting of a Houston Rockets basketball singlet and jeans.]
BT: It will be a Texas Tornado Match meaning that everyone will be in the ring at the same time. Oh yeah, I almost forgot! This match is a
TOUGHMAN MATCH... meaning that to win, Thomas Rainka must pin all 3 guys, so let's get this thing underway!
[Tuckwell leaves to an absolutely giant pop as Rainka's three opponents run down to the ring and slide under the bottom rope.]
LR: Well we have a 3 on 1 handicap match about to start here after
Thomas Rainka attacked a fan and was made to earn his spot for the battle royal later in the night! The odds are stacked up against the wall for Thomas Rainka!
RD: It won't be that hard for him! His opponents are all crap and I could defeat them! I'm the best wrestler here, aren't I Lance?
LR: Umm... no. Rainka throws Hughes out of the ring with a hiptoss. Darren Harvey looks over the ropes to see if his friend is alright and
Rainka clotheslines him over right on top of Hughes!
JC: He crushed Hughes there! He's 300 pounds at least and Hughes is only about 170. It's just like fat and skinny!
RD: Shawn Brown runs towards Rainka going for a clothesline but Rainka ducks and executes his finisher, the Rainka Legsweep, a front Russian legsweep.
LR: He's making the cover! [ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!] LR: Shawn Brown was eliminated pretty quickly! Harvey is up on the apron and Rainka nails him with a Standing Dropkick. Harvey falls back... OH
NO, HIS RIGHT ANKLE IS CAUGHT IN THE ROPES! RD: Rainka will be quick to take advantage of this! He hops out of the ring and immediately goes to work on that right ankle.
LR: Meanwhile, Ray Hughes is searching underneath the ring for something... HE'S GOT A TAZER!
RD: Rainka is in trouble. He turns around to be met with that tazer in the midsection. He's being electrocuted!
LR: Hughes stuns him once more. Rainka is hurt. Hughes is attending to
Darren Harvey, trying to free his ankle.
RD: Thomas Rainka is still hurt right in front of us! Hughes has almost worked that ankle free.
LR: The ankle is free and Harvey falls on top of Hughes once again. Hughes slides out and throws Rainka into the ring. Harvey's is clutching at his ankle still and I suspect is may be broken!
RD: Don't be stupid, at worst it's sprained! JC: I believe that he should stop being a pussy and get in the ring and fight like a man! RD: Mmmm... pussy! Anyway back to the action! Hughes makes the cover. [ONE!!! TWO!!! SHOULDER UP!!!] LR: A weak pin attempt! Hughes is arguing with the referee about a slow count and Rainka rolls him up in a schoolboy pin!
[ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!] RD: That's two down, one to go and Darren Harvey can't be that hard to beat with a sore ankle. Rainka rolls Harvey into the ring and turns him flat on his stomach.
LR: He's got that right ankle into the reverse ankle lock submission! Darren Harvey taps out and the referee calls for the bell. Thomas Rainka is still in the battle royal for later tonight!
RD: Rainka exits the ring and takes a chair from ringside. He goes back into the ring and wraps the chair around the ankle of Darren Harvey!
What's he going to do? LR: It's obvious what he's going to do! He wants to put Darren Harvey out of commission for a long time! Rainka goes up top and jumps up the chair!
RD: Aww... take a look the bone of Harvey's ankle. Rainka is leaving, laughing at everybody as the medical crew run out and take Harvey away in a stretcher!
LR: Thomas Rainka is disgusting! JC: I like that quality in a person! RD: Rainka won't be at his best tonight after being electrocuted by that tazer. Hey, it's still lying there!
[Delaney gets up from his chair and picks up the tazer. He sits back down.] RD: Hey Lance... LR: Yeah wh... [Delaney shocks Richardson with the tazer. Lance stops moving.] RD: Oops... Lance... Hello Lance! JC: I think you've killed him! RD: It was just a joke damn it. [Richie Delaney throws a bit of water on Lance's face and he shakes it off and looks at Delaney with an angry look on his face.]
LR: You son of a bitch, I'd beat your ass right here if we had the time but we gotta hear from David Rage.
RD: Hope his bitch comes out with him, she has the best set of tits I've ever seen! ["Soul Crusher" blasts through the arena and the crowd erupts as 'Maniac' David Rage and Tiffany make their way to the ring. David is in his wrestling gear, ready to go and Tiffany is wearing her short skirt, a black 'Maniac' David Rage t-shirt and thigh high boots. David and
Tiffany climb through the ropes and Maniac climbs the turnbuckle and raises his arms high above his head and the crowd is very happy to see this guy. Tiffany walks around inside the ring, making eye contact and sensually moving for all the fans. Maniac goes to another corner of the ring, climbs the ropes and the crowd responds in the same way. Tiffany holds her hand out and someone gives her a microphone. She hands the mike to David.]
MDR: Hello Texas! [He speaks with enthusiasm and the crowd cheers him. He climbs down from the turnbuckle and paces around the ring. Tiffany leans on the ropes.]
MDR: Now I have a problem. No, I'm not gonna bitch about losing the flag match. Lord, you won, literally by the skin of your teeth. I know it.
The fans know it. Tuckwell, Delaney, Richardson, Cook, they all know it. And you sure as hell know it... good for you! [The crowd boos, remembering how Lord Bradbury won.] MDR: Bitchin' ain't my way. And I'm not gonna complain about Thomas
Rainka smashing me in the back with a chair. Hey Thomas, you gotta do what'cha gotta do. Hell if I thought I needed to, I'd smash your skull in with a chair too. But I think it would be cooler to break you in half with the RageFix, right before I toss your ass over the top ropes tonight.
[The crowd cheers. Maniac stops in the center of the ring. He takes one last calm breath.] MDR: TONIGHT, I'M GONNA DO WHAT I GOTTA DO! [The crowd erupts. He paces again.] RD: I hope he doesn't piss in the ring! Isn't the saying if ya gotta go, ya gotta go? MDR: You see boys, a maniac has been let loose in Texas tonight, driven by one vision, possessing the Southwestern Strap. And when you boys stand between the 'maniac' and his vision. It's not a safe place to be.
So bring your chairs, say your prayers, bring your courage and bring all that you are. Chairs don't hurt me. Prayers will only heal you after I'm through with you and courage will help you to move on. But they won't stop me!
[Tiffany walks to David and whispers something into his ear. He smiles and nods his head.] MDR: Tiffany has something to say, I'll see everyone later! [He hands her the mike and climbs the turnbuckle, interacting with the fans. Tiffany watches him. She has a devious smile on her face. She walks around the ring, steps on the bottom rope and leans seductively into the ropes.]
T: I have a surprise for the loudest Maniac in the house! [The crowd goes crazy, chanting "RAGE, RAGE, RAGE, RAGE!". Tiffany and
Rage step out of the ring and they walk around the security railing, listening to the fans. Tiffany stops in front of a pimply faced teenage boy who is chanting really loud. David points to him and the kid smiles.
Tiffany pulls the David Rage shirt off, revealing a tight bikini top underneath it.] RD: WOOHOO ALRIGHT! [The t-shirt is black and reads "MDR" on the front and on the back it reads "Released and Relentless" in bloody red letters. She hands him the shirt and kisses him on the cheek. Maniac shakes his hand. The kid is smiling and blushing. "Soul Crusher" by White Zombie blasts through the arena as Maniac and Tiffany leave the ring to the roar of the crowd.]
RD: That's one lucky kid, he got to see those hooters up close and personal! He's putting on that MDR shirt right now!
LR: That's one of the wide range of LSWU Merchandise that you can buy from top stores around Texas.
RD: It's time for the battle royal with the winner to pick up the Southwestern Title Belt. [The English National anthem plays and Lord Bradbury walks out to a large boo. He sings to the tune and is followed out by THE QUEEN.]
LR: That's Lord Bradbury, the first man ever to win a match in the Lone
Star Wrestling Union!
["Stars and Stripes" by John Philip Souza begins to play and Clement
Tyme walks out to a large pop. He raises the lightweight title belt up high in the air and walks down to the ring, flipping over the top rope and into the ring.]
LR: Clement Tyme last night became the first ever LSWU lightweight champion without even wrestling after he was given the title by
President Tuckwell. ["Hear It" by Bad Religion plays over the speakers as the crowd boo when
Thomas Rainka steps out from the back and raises his arms in the air. He puts them down and walks to the ring, yelling and pointing at members of the crowd.]
RD: Thomas Rainka still looks a bit fatigued from the 3 on 1 handicap match he had to go through earlier in the night to make it to this battle royal.
LR: The President put him in that match after he attacked a fan named
Steven Hall! RD: You sure do have a good memory! ["Soul Crusher" plays and Tiffany walks to the ring. Maniac runs from the back to the ring. He slides under the bottom rope and begins pummeling on Lord Bradbury.]
LR: The bell has been rung and David Rage is going at it with Lord
Bradbury while Thomas Rainka is fighting with Clement Tyme.
RD: Rage takes Bradbury down with a hiptoss. I'm sure revenge for last night is on his mind!
LR: Rainka has Tyme in the corner and is laying the 10 count punches to him on the turnbuckle. Rainka is looking good for somebody who is not fully fit.
RD: Rainka tries to whip Tyme from one turnbuckle to the other but Tyme crashes into Lord Bradbury. Bradbury turns around and begins exchanging fists with Clement.
LR: Rage is just standing back in the corner and Rainka charges towards him and nails him with a running clothesline.
RD: Lord takes Farmboy down with a snap mare takeover. They're getting closer to those ropes and will try to eliminate the other opponent.
LR: Thomas Rainka kicks Rage in the gut and delivers a DDT. He turns
Maniac over and applies the figure four leglock. Submissions don't count in this match but he's probably just wearing him down.
RD: Tyme and Bradbury tie up. Clement gets the advantage and hits a bulldog. David Rage meanwhile is trying to roll over and reverse that figure four.
LR: He's done it! Rage releases the hold then lifts Rainka up to his feet. Maniac puts his hand around Rainka's neck and drops him with a chokeslam.
RD: Rage sees Tyme and Bradbury near the ropes and he clotheslines them both over and onto the mat outside.
LR: That means Lord Bradbury and Clement Tyme are both eliminated. The
Southwestern Champion now will either be David Rage or Thomas Rainka.
RD: Rage is looking like he has the advantage at the moment. He whips
Rainka into the ropes and brings him down with a powerslam.
LR: Rainka looks like he's about to lose. He is sent into the ropes once more and is nailed with a boot to the face.
RD: This is the setup to his finisher, the RageFix. He lifts Rainka up to his feet and puts his head between the legs.
LR: He's got him up on his shoulders but Rainka is fighting hard to escape. Rainka slides down Rage's back, great escape!
RD: Rage turns around and gets a kick to the gut followed by the Rainka
Legsweep. Both men are down on the mat.
LR: Rainka is first to get up. He's still hurt from that tazer attack from Ray Hughes. Rainka drops the elbow across Rage's chest.
RD: He goes for it again but David Rage rolls out of the way to the crowd's delight. David Rage sends Rainka into the ropes and charges him with a flying knee smash, sending Rainka back into the ropes.
LR: It will take only 1 clothesline more to eliminate him. Rage does exactly that and goes for a clothesline but Rainka ducks it and backdrops Rage but Rage lands on the apron.
RD: Almost gone was Maniac. Maniac gets Rainka and tries to suplex him to the outside but Rainka blocks it and suplexes Rage back into the ring.
LR: Rainka whips Rage into the ropes and executes a Spinebuster Slam. This could be all. Rainka is going up top. RD: This is suicide in these type of matches. If David Rage gets up, it could be all over. LR: Rainka comes flying off with a cross body press but David Rage catches him and lifts him above his head.
RD: This looks like a Gorilla Press Slam. Rainka is trying to get free but has no success. David Rage tosses Rainka to the outside and is our new Southwestern Champion!
[President Tuckwell comes down the ramp with the Southwestern Title belt in his hands. He gets a microphone and slides into the ring.]
BT: It's my pleasure to introduce you to the new Lone Star Wrestling
Union Southwestern Champion, 'MANIAC' DAVID RAGE!
[Tuckwell hands him the belt and Maniac raises the belt high and the crowd goes crazy. Tuckwell leaves the ring as Tiffany enters and she hugs him tight.]
RD: What the hell is this? A member of the crowd is jumping the guard rail! He is built like a wrestler and is sneaking into the ring.
LR: Oh no, he taps Rage on the shoulder and delivers a devastating modified version of the Cradle Piledriver.
RD: The man throws Rage to the outside where he is met by a chairshot from Thomas Rainka. Tiffany is trying to help but Rainka is obviously angry with Rage.
LR: The man in the ring has a microphone! [The man in the ring has blonde hair, blue eyes, and a chiseled physique. His hair is short, his eyes are intense, and his body language is confident.]
CJB: I am "Hateful" CJ Bender and I have arrived in the LSWU. That great move was called the Gender Bender and I am ready for anyone and to start with, I want the Southwestern Title. David Rage, ask me if I am really that hateful and the answer will stay the same, Damn RIGHT I AM!
[The man throws the microphone to the interview Thomas Matheson at ringside. President Tuckwell looks at the man and begins to speak.]
BT: You come into my federation and begin demanding title shots? Well
I'll tell you what, next week at Uncensored, there will be a triple threat falls count anywhere match for the Southwestern Title. It will be
David Rage, the worthy champion against you, CJ Bender and Thomas Rainka who deserves even more punishment! CJ, be ready to face the power of
David Rage, because next week he'll give you an education in the process!
[Tuckwell leaves the ring to a large cheer. David Rage and Tiffany follow behind with CJ Bender standing, staring at them with intensity in his eyes.]
LR: What a great show! Our new Southwestern Champion is David Rage and next week he will defend the belt in a triple threat match against newcomer CJ Bender and Thomas Rainka.
RD: There will be no Breaking Point next week but Uncensored will be coming live to you from the Freeman Coliseum in San Antonio, Texas.
That's all for this telecast, goodnight! [Thomas Rainka sneaks into the ring with a chair and goes to hit CJ
Bender, but Bender turns around quickly and kicks Rainka in the midsection. Bender takes the chair and levels Rainka as the Breaking
Point copyright message comes to the screen before fading to a commercial.]
