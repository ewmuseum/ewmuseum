LSWU — Card

Lone Star Wrestling Union

3 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Lightning Crashes" by Live begins to play and the camera flashes into the packed LSWU Arena in Austin, Texas. The Uncensored logo hits the screen and spins around twice and loud cheers begin as a set of five cheerleaders run down to the ring wearing tight white sweaters and red skirts. They do a short dance routine to the pleasure of the crowd before making the shape of a star with their bodies on the ring mat. Fireworks shoot from all four turnbuckles, then from each side of the ramp before a series of booming fireworks are launched from the ring apron. The Lone Star Wrestling Union logo on a black sheet is lowered from the rafters and into the ring before the cheerleaders run off with it up the ramp. The camera sweeps across the arena of screaming fans and over to the announcers’ table at ringside where three men sit. The man on the left is clean cut with short dark brown hair.
The man in the center has long red hair and is dressed in a Texas Rangers baseball shirt and track pants. The man on the right has got a crew cut and has a brown goatee. The man on the left smiles and begins to speak.]
Lance Richardson: Welcome to the first ever event for the Lone Star Wrestling Union, UNCENSORED! I'm Lance Richardson and sitting beside me is
Richie Delaney and Jerry Cook and you are looking at the recently built LSWU
Arena right here in Texas capital of Austin.
Richie Delaney: That's right, a lot of money was spent on this project by my good buddy Ben Tuckwell and I'm expecting a pay rise soon if this show is good!
LR: I'm sure you are and let's begin by taking a look at the lineup for tonight's card. After an interview with President Tuckwell, we see Shawn
Brown and Ray Hughes face off for the lightweight title.
Jerry Cook: I'm sure that their mothers are watching this telecast and if they are, then I must say that you are a failure of a mother and a pathetic excuse for a human being.
RD: You're damn right about that! LR: That's enough from both of you. Our main event tonight is a flag match between Lord Bradbury and Thomas Rainka. Bradbury is representing England and Rainka will be fighting for Canada.
RD: Why the hell isn't there a representative for the mighty United States of America. To be quite honest, I'm going to rethink my quest to get a pay rise if this is what Ben's going to put up for entertainment!
["Give it Away" by the Red Hot Chilli Peppers plays over the loud speakers and LSWU President Ben Tuckwell steps out onto the stage dressed in denim cargo shorts and a long sleeved blue collared shirt. He walks down the ramp and calls for a microphone from one of the ring crew. Tuckwell makes his way up the steel steps and into the ring.]
BT: WELCOME TO UNCENSORED TEXAS! [SMALL POP!] BT: I hope you enjoy the show and when you leave, we have something special for all you little kiddies. Every child under the age of 15 can pick up their free very own LSWU transfer tattoos at every gate.
[GOOD POP!] BT: Tonight, we will crown our very first ever lightweight champion but enough of this talking, let's get the show under way. I promise that tonight will be full of surprises that have never been seen in wrestling before. To begin with, I have changed the rules for the flag match. You must pin your opponent then go outside and take the flag. Remember, tickets for Breaking
Point tomorrow night are still available at half price, so if you're watching in Houston, get onto the phone right now and order your tickets half price. Before I go, I WANT A LITTLE MORE NOISE UP IN THIS PLACE!
[The crowd begins to cheer really loudly and a smile comes to the face of
President Tuckwell. Tuckwell steps out of the ring and sits down at ringside. Interviewer Thomas Matheson steps into the ring with a microphone and begins to introduce the first match on the card.]
TM: The upcoming match is a lightweight title match scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, weighing in at 221 pounds, he is SHAWN BROWN! [A man steps out at the top of the ramp. He is wearing a black baseball cap and overalls. He makes his way down to the ring without getting much reaction from the crowd at all.]
TM: His opponent, hailing from El Paso, Texas, weighing in at 183 pounds... RAY HUGHES! [Another man steps out to no music again. This man is wearing black shorts and a white tanktop. He runs down the ramp and slides under the bottom rope.]
RD: Shawn Brown is laying the boots to Hughes. He lifts him up and sends him into the ropes and connects with a running clothesline.
LR: Brown against the ropes and Hughes ducks the elbow attempt. Hughes with the kick to the gut followed by a kick to the head.
JC: Aren't lightweight matches supposed to be exciting? I think watching mud dry is more exciting that this crap!
LR: Hughes goes up to the top rope and leaps off for a legdrop but Brown rolls out of the way.
JC: Up the tempo a little! RD: The crowd is starting a 'BORING' chant. I don't think Ben looks to impressed with this display. Shawn Brown plants his knee right into the face of Ray Hughes.
LR: President Tuckwell is getting up from his seat at ringside. He's up on the apron and jumps up onto the top rope.
RD: He's waiting for Brown to turn around. Ben Tuckwell jumps off and delivers a springboard missile dropkick to the face of Shawn Brown.
LR: The President lifts up Hughes and kicks him in the shin before nailing him with a Fameasser like move.
RD: Woh, both of these men are out of it. Tuckwell goes back up top... AND
COMES OFF WITH A HUGE SHOOTING STAR PRESS RIGHT ONTO SHAWN BROWN!
JC: This is the kind of stuff I want to see! High flying and extremely exciting! LR: Tuckwell throws both men out of the ring to the crowd's approval and calls for a microphone.
BT: I'm sure that all of you agree with me when I say that was one boring match! [POP!] BT: Who is the lightweight champion then? Is it Shawn Brown? NO! Is it Ray
Hughes? NO! I've found somebody worthy of this award, somebody who is willing to work hard to defend this title, so without further to do, I'd like to introduce you all to the NEW LONE STAR WRESTLING UNION LIGHTWEIGHT
CHAMPION... "FARMBOY" CLEMENT TYME! [Clement Tyme walks down to the ring to quite a large cheer. President
Tuckwell hands him the microphone.]
FCT: Thank you, to all of my fans. I know you all wanted me to come here and shine and now I am. Mr. Tuckwell, I thank you for putting your faith in me and giving me a chance to wear your Lightweight Title. I promise to wear and defend it with honor and dignity. Thanks everyone!
[At this, Tyme takes a forward roll and lands on his feet. Then he jumps straight onto the top rope, belt in hand and raises his right fist in the air. He gets a huge pop and gets three more in the other corners. "Stars and
Stripes Forever" by John Philip Souza begins to play and Tyme does a plancha over the top rope onto the floor. He then walks to the back with President
Tuckwell following behind.]
LR: What an announcement by President Tuckwell. "Farmboy" Clement Tyme is the LSWU Lightweight Champion without having wrestled a match!
RD: I support everything that Ben Tuckwell just did and I must say that he did add a certain element of excitement to that extremely boring match!
JC: He sure did! LR: Well it's time for our MAIN EVENT, a flag match between Lord Bradbury and Thomas Rainka! Remember that President Tuckwell changed the rules so that you must pin your opponent then retrieve your flag! It's kind of like two out of three falls!
RD: I'm still disappointed Ben didn't put in anybody to represent our country! TM: The upcoming match is a FLAG MATCH! Coming to the ring at this time, accompanied by his valet THE QUEEN, straight out of Sussex, England, representing England, weighing in at 264 pounds, LORD BRADBURY OF SUSSEX!
[The English national anthem "God Save the Queen" begins to play and Lord
Bradbury walks out singing to his theme music. He is followed by THE QUEEN who is holding the British Flag proudly in the air. They walk down the ramp and into the ring and Lord Bradbury snatches the microphone from Thomas
Matheson.]
LB: Well... I guess it had to happen, I guess sometime I had to come back to the good ol' US of A. You know what a fantastic place this is! There's toxic waste everywhere and the people don't even seem to care! Your favorite sport is where a guy hits a ball with a big stick and then run around. But enough about the US of A, here we are in the capital of backward, the headquarters of hillbilly, the state full of skanks, TEXAS!!!
[HUGE BOO!] LB: Now I have no idea what I was thinking when I signed that contract. In fact, I can't even remember it, the Prez probably drugged me up or something. I hate Texas so much that I make my temporary home in Louisiana and bear the 8-hour commute for each match cause I could never live in a hellhole like this.
[ANOTHER BOO!] LB: Well now that I have met you nice people lets discuss my Canidean opponent. You can't blame old Tommy Boy for how he turned out, you know how they say a product of his environment? Well that’s a perfect example. In a country who's national sport is a bunch of big guys sliding on ice with a stick, in a country that the only appealing thing that they have are water and trees, Canidea is on the same level as far as I'm concerned with the likes of Mongolia and Haiti. So Tom, I don't blame you for your utter lack of talent, the fact that it is hard to look directly at your face, and the fact that you can't string two coherent words together, I blame your country. So in say about 15 minutes, I fully expect to be wiping my ass with that leaf flag, and you to be laying in the ring out cold with a beautiful
Union Jack draped over your prone body. Ring announcer, you may call out the beast, for I am ready to start the match.
[Bradbury throws the microphone back to Thomas Matheson and the crowd is still booing at his outburst towards the state of Texas.]
RD: I want a piece of that English son of a bitch! How dare he insult my homeland like that!
LR: Calm down, I'm sure that he'll get some from somebody one day! TM: His opponent, from Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada, standing at 6'3" and weighing in at 240 pounds... THOMAS RAINKA!
["Hear It" by Bad Religion plays over the speakers and Thomas Rainka steps out from the back and raises his arms in the air, he puts them down and walks to the ring while yelling and pointing at members of the crowd, he walks up the ring steps and steps into the ring. One of the ring crew pull out a Canadian flag from underneath the ring and puts it down next to the
English flag at ringside.]
RD: Thomas Rainka and Lord Bradbury are sizing each other up. The referee is about to call for the bell... and what the hell? Who owns this music?
["Soul Crusher" by White Zombie blasts through the arena and all eyes go to the top of the ramp.]
LR: Lord Bradbury and Thomas Rainka are looking awfully confused! [Out from behind the curtain walks 'Maniac' David Rage and Tiffany. Rage is in black wrestling gear with pants, boots, elbow pads and kneepads. His hair is long and blonde. He's a big man, 6'4", and about 275 lbs. He looks angry.
Tiffany is a beautiful blonde, she looks like a stripper.] RD: That woman is HOT! [They walk to the ring. Tiffany smiles and struts her stuff. David's eyes are set on the ring. He climbs onto the apron, and steps through the ropes. Tiffany stays at ringside. His music fades, and he has a microphone. Lord Bradbury and Thomas Rainka are pissed, but they stay away from Maniac because he has a very angry look on his face.]
LR: It looks like he has something to say! MDR: I see a problem here tonight. A flag match, in my beloved Texas, without the United States of America being represented...
[David paces around the ring as he talks.] MDR: I can tell you this... it isn’t gonna happen. So, President Tuckwell, I want you to bring the red, white, and blue down to the ring, because we're going to have ourselves a triple threat match!
RD: ALL RIGHT, DAVID RAGE IS REPRESENTING THE STATES! [Rage stops right in front of Bradbury and Rainka and stares them both down.] MDR: And I'm going to beat your ass! [Rage begins immediately nailing a hard right fist to the face of Lord
Bradbury.] LR: The bell has just rung and the match is underway. President Tuckwell is making his way down to ringside with a big grin on his face. He is carrying the USA flag.
RD: I knew that Ben Tuckwell is a smart man and wouldn't allow the USA not to be represented in this match.
LR: Woh! David Rage just hiptossed Thomas Rainka right across the ring! JC: That is a very impressive show of power I must say! LR: Bradbury is up and picks up Thomas Rainka and sets him up... and sends him flat on his back with a powerbomb.
RD: David Rage is taking a breather. Bradbury sends Rainka down once more with a vertical suplex and applies a Boston Crab.
LR: He's applying great pressure and Rainka may tap out. Rage is making his way up to the top rope.
RD: Neither Bradbury nor Rainka can see this! Maniac leaps off and nails
Lord Bradbury with a flying clothesline while he had Rainka in that Boston
Crab.
LR: Rainka rolls out of the ring to get back his breath. Rage sends Bradbury into the ropes and gets him with a great Tilt-a-Whirl backbreaker.
RD: Thomas Rainka has pushed Thomas Matheson off his chair at ringside and has taken the chair and folded it up.
LR: David Rage wraps both of his large hands around Lord Bradbury's neck and lifts him up high in the air.
RD: He's going to send him down with a double handed chokeslam. OH NO,
THOMAS RAINKA SLID INTO THE RING AND CRACKED THAT STEEL CHAIR RIGHT ACROSS
DAVID RAGE'S BACK! LR: Referee Al Baxter is not calling for a disqualification! Bradbury is slowly getting up and Rainka has that chair raised above his head, ready to strike!
RD: Bradbury turns around and Rainka brings the chair down BUT BRADBURY DROPKICKS THE CHAIR INTO RAINKA'S FACE! JC: WOW that had to hurt! LR: Bradbury picks up Rainka and sets him up for his finisher, the brainbuster. Rainka counters with a small package!
[ONE!!!! TWO!!!! KICKOUT!!!!] RD: Excellent counter from the Canadian Thomas Rainka. David Rage holds
Thomas Rainka and sends him into the turnbuckle with a Turnpost Slam.
LR: David Rage is back in it! He sends Rainka across into the ropes and gets a big boot to the face.
RD: I heard that's the setup move to his finisher! David Rage picks up
Rainka and has him up for the crucifix powerbomb and drops him! This match should be over!
LR: That move is called the RageFix! Lord Bradbury pulls David Rage away and sends him against the ropes and backdrops him out of the ring. Bradbury makes the cover.
[ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!] RD: LORD BRADBURY WINS! LR: No he doesn't, remember he still needs to get his country's flag to gain the victory. He's leaving the ring right now and going over to his flag!
RD: David Rage is over there. Bradbury walks right into Rage and Rage picks him up and drops Bradbury with the RageFix right onto the English Flag.
LR: Aargh! Lord Bradbury's back has to be sore after that! David Rage picks up the USA flag and waves it in the air! He thinks he's won this match!
JC: Stupid idiot! He has to make the pin first! RD: I think this match is falls count anywhere because the referee hasn't started counting them out. David Rage makes the cover and Al Baxter slides out of the ring and begins the count!
[ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THR...] JC: HOLY [BLEEP]. THOMAS RAINKA JUST LEAPED OVER THAT TOP ROPE AND COME DOWN
WITH A SUICIDE DIVE RIGHT ONTO THE HEAD OF DAVID RAGE! THAT WAS SIMPLY
LEGENDARY! RD: Rainka is working the crowd and David Rage is still on top of Lord
Bradbury. [ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!] LR: David Rage gets the pinfall. Now all he has to do is get the USA flag! RD: Maniac looks to be unconscious though after that insane suicide dive from Thomas Rainka.
LR: Thomas Rainka comes back and covers David Rage. [ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!] RD: Every man has scored a pinfall, now it's first person to get his country's flag will win!
LR: Thomas Rainka looks to be the safest bet right now! RD: Rainka picks up the Canadian flag but cannot lift it because David Rage is lying on the flagpole. Rainka mounts David Rage and begins to punch him, trying to make him move.
LR: David Rage pushes Rainka off and delivers another RageFix... BUT LORD
BRADBURY HAS PICKED UP THE ENGLISH FLAG! LORD BRADBURY WINS THE MATCH!
RD: He's still lying on the ground but that flag of England is raised up in the air! David Rage looks pissed!
LR: Rage goes over to Lord Bradbury and delivers the RageFix once again. RD: That's four times today! I believe that David Rage should've won that match but Lord Bradbury was too close to his own flag!
[David Rage climbs into the ring along with Tiffany. He demands a microphone from Thomas Matheson, who obliges straight away.]
MDR: I KNOW I SHOULD HAVE WON THAT MATCH BUT BE SURE TO WATCH ME BEAT SOME
ASS AT BREAKING POINT WHERE THERE WILL BE A BATTLE ROYAL FOR THE
SOUTHWESTERN STRAP, WHICH YOU WILL SEE ME WALK AWAY WITH! All you boys better watch out, because I'm the maniac in town, and you're all going to be torn out of my way.
[He gets a grin on his face.] MDR: One way or another... [His music blares over the loud speakers and David Rage leaves the arena with Tiffany. Lord Bradbury and Thomas Rainka are still down at ringside and the camera pans over to the announcers.]
LR: What a great show that was! Clement Tyme won the Lightweight Title without even having to wrestle and Lord Bradbury won the flag match, which turned into a triple threat when 'Maniac' David Rage entered.
RD: David Rage should've won and everybody would agree with me! He dominated but Bradbury somehow managed to get his country's flag and win the match.
LR: I'm Lance Richardson for Richie Delaney and Jerry Cook. We'll see you tomorrow at Breaking Point where a Battle Royal will happen for the
Southwestern Title Belt. Goodnight! [With that, the LSWU Copyright Message appears on the screen along with the website address '
http://lswu.cjb.net ' and the screen fades slowly to black.]
