Sunday Night Battleground (1/27/02)

27 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a blank, black screen. The BSCW logo flashes on the screen, as "Scrape" by Unsane begins to play. The logo begins to flash more rapidly, and explodes in a momentary flash of bright light.
Then, in perfect synchronicity with the music, some of the most insane, suicidal events of the past of Blood Sweat and Chairs
Wrestling are shown. Sykopath sends Shrapnel crashing though three stacked tables with a
Syko Krush-R. Dan Owens locks the Constrictor on "King of Submission" Quinn Charbonneau. As Tyson Bryson comes charging off the ropes, Quincy LaCroix nails
Bryson with the Quinninoku Driver.
Whiskey Jakk nails Dan Owens with a Samoan Drop off the top rope. Blacktop puts Boz through a flaming table with a Career Ender off a ladder. Max C puts Matt Wilson out with the Offer You Can't Refuse. "Deadly" Derek Irvin busts Justin Keith wide open with a shot from the
BSCW World Championship belt. The scene then fades to black.
[The scene opens up a large arena somewhere in Western Massachusetts. The sky is dark, showing that it is night time. A large sign reads
Worcester Centrum, and on the marquis, "National Rodeo Association.
The people that are seen walking in, many wear cowboy hats and cowboy boots. From behind the camera in the parking lot an old Garth Brooks song is heard being played through a crappy car stereo sound system.
Heard coming from behind and yelling, "What the hell?", an obviously inebriated Richard "The Redneck Wreckin' Machine", staggers up to the camera crew. Many make the motion of him smelling heavily of alcohol.]
Harris: Can a man have a f'in minute here? I thought I'd drive to
Worcester and catch a f'in rodeo. I reckon, I'd be able to have a solid night without you bastards chasin' me around like ah'm some drunk sorority girl. Can I ask you for just two f'in hours of peace and quiet? No interviews, no deep thoughts about the inner workin's of Whiskey Jakk. I just want to watch some good ole boys ride and rassle some steer?
Of course now that ah'm standin' in front of you bastards, I guess I should say somethin' about the match at Capital Punishment. Carver and I are gonna beat the hell out of Greed and maybe if he's lucky we'll let Mr. Jakk get a couple of licks in. If we feel real
sportsman-like. But I wouldn't count on it. Right now, there is a ten dollar seat in there with my name on it. Why don't you go and find Emmit, he needs a camera crew to follow him around or somethin'. I'll see you at Capital Punishment, boys.
[With that said, Harris stumbles to the entrance of the Worcester
Centrum, leaving the camera crew scratching their heads. The scene fades out as the masses continue to enter the Centrum.]
[The scene opens on to Shrapnel's painted face, which has an eager tone to it. As the camera pulls back a little, we see he is on a ladder in a red lighted plain room. His punk trench coat is tied and covering most of his body. He folds his hands on his lap and begins to speak.]
Shrapnel: Well Jaji, you seem to be ready. Glad to see you have accepted my challenge to a ladder match. A match were missing a move could be much more dangerous. But I like danger, do you Jaji? Gavin seems to think so, I hope you do. I also hope you like pain. Because I have plenty to dish out, but I can take even more. This match is my type of match, it combines the two areas that I excel most in. Even though I pride myself at being a cruiserweight. The simple fact is, I am One Hardcore [CENSORED]. That ladder, will bounce off your skull.
It will be stained with your blood. Then I will set it up and climb that mother[CENSORED]. I will reach up, and grab your tarnished title.
A title that can only be cleansed by going back to me, and by being covered in your filthy blood. The blood of a liar, a cheat, a no good piece of [CENSORED]. Blood that I wouldn't let a wolf lick up from the floor. You will not keep that title, I know it and you know it. Do you feel that lump rising in your throat? That lump that is suffocating you, making you wonder why you can't breath? That lump is fear Jaji.
That suffocating fearful lump is because of me. You fear me. Your fear me and what I am going to do to you. Last match you just got a taste of my explosive ability. But Gavin cut the fuse short. This time, the fuse is a lot shorter, and there is more fuel to the fire so to speak.
Jaji, you have more to worry about than Gavin, so shut up about him and get prepared. Get prepared for one of the most HELLISH nights you have ever had.
[Shrapnel removes his coat to reveal a plain black t-shirt, and black wind pants. He turns around on the ladder and carefully does a handstand on the top step. From this position, he starts to do push ups, as the scene fades to black.]
[The camera fades into a living room setting. It's quite obviously a warehouse of some kind, but the setting is there. Sykopath walks up behind the couch with a cigarette in his fingers, drinking a glass of milk. He puts the milk on a table and hops over the back of a couch and sits down, drinking again from the glass of milk. His bandana covers his hair and eyes, but he seems to see quite well. He finishes the milk and sets the glass down. He takes a drag from his cigarette, flicks off the ash and takes another long drag.]
Sykopath: Capitol Punishment... Todd Royal and I fight for a single prize... It's not a hardcore match per say... but I don't think there are any disqualifications or ring outs... so... you know what... maybe
I'll do just that damn much better. Todd has chosen not to show his face... on two occasions. I had stepped back from one, to let him catch up... but... apparently... he came up with no words because his throat still has that lump he gets when he is afraid of something.
It's almost sickening. I guess he wants me to totally destroy him, I will create an example of him. Royal... you will be the first of the last. You will be the first on my list of competitors for the North
American Title, and after you, is anyone that gets in my way. You are the beginning of the end my friend... and you cannot hide from it.
[Sykopath takes another drag of his cigarette.] Sykopath: Royal... you have shown nothing but cowardice in this moment. I have given you ample chance to make up for your mistake. I am disappointed with the results. So I believe I will stop talking about you... and do a little reminiscing.
[Another drag of the death stick is taken with gusto.] Sykopath: Richard... The Truck, or the Redneck Wreckin' Machine... Harris. He beat me for my title... ONCE. He failed to defend it against me. He beat me the first time because he was more prepared, and more extreme than I was... the Second time around... not so. I won it over because I wasn't afraid to put my body on the line, and make sure I destroyed his. He has never been more of a hardcore mother[CENSORED]er than me. He just got lucky one night by going the extra step that he forgot to take this time around. I caught his mistake, and capitalized on it. I took back what was rightfully mine.
Make NO mistake about it, he gave me a run for my money like no other man. I pushed me farther than any of my opponents ever have... except for maybe one man... Harris is one hell of a hardcore fighter, maybe he's just not as Hardcore than me... I sure as hell know he isn't MORE hardcore than me. Harris... you will fall every time I face you...
you can never truly win what is mine.... you will never take away from me what I can do. I cannot ever completely beat you... or take away the true talent you have for Wrestling... whoever comes out on top never matters, it's who is still standing at the end. If that person is the one who defeats the other, than so be it. We may never reconcile, but we will always have that mutual respect. Harris, I salute you. You are a true warrior.
[Sykopath takes a last drag and smothers the cigarette in the ash tray.] Sykopath: Now... one more man... Shrapnel... he has been missing for a short while... I am somewhat disappointed. He is a great hardcore fighter as well. He is an extreme man, he takes anyone and everyone to the limit, to the breaking point. Just remember who you owe for that belt Shrapnel, and remember who will be there to collect his rightful payment. Even if he has to take it out of your skin.
[Sykopath pauses for a moment.... looks around and leans forward, placing his arms across his knees.]
Sykopath: Lastly... Royal... Capitol Punishment... will be your end.... [The camera fades to black as Sykopath begins to smile and laugh in a sick manner.] [The scene is a locker room, a wooden bench and three lockers are all we see. (Hey its a locker room, what more detailed description do you want? Smells?) The camera pans around the room and stops on the entrance to the room. The door opens and through it walk Megan Skye and the BSCW North American Champion, Todd Royal. Royal sits on the bench and begins to tie his boot laces. He looks at the camera, then his watch, and smiles.]
TR: Heh, back already huh? What do you mean I'm late? Late for what? Oh, that match with Sykopath? That unification crap? I was supposed to show up for that? Well Todd Damn, you guys just demand much too much from your champions.
So Sykopath, you're first in line for my....Oh wait. No one wants to hear about you, you guys don't even know where I've been. I apologize for not informing you about my whereabouts, but you simply weren't worth the time.
I realize that everyone out there has been suffering from Todd Royal withdrawal for the past month and a half. Ever since I won this belt,
I
haven't been around. Those are the perks of being me. I walked into the ring, and grabbed this belt, just like I said I would. None of you peanut-brained idiots believed me when I said there was no match, but
I'm here aren't I? And after fulfilling my commitments to walk out and get my belt, I decided that, since I was ME and I COULD, I would take a vacation.
So I took my belt and I went down to the carribean, I sailed around, I swam and I sunned, I was having a grand old time...but then again, I was
spending time with ME, so fun is kind of a given. I had it all, like I always do, when my cell phone started ringing. And ringing, and ringing and ringing. Every call was from Richard Vail, complaining that fans were rioting. They would show up at the arenas, Todd Royal signs in hand, simply there to see me, and when I wasn't there, and crap like Seth Harker wrestled instead, they rioted. Vail was losing money faster than Enron paying up all the bills for damage repair. He begged me to return, but I exercised my right as champion to a full paid vacation and politely told him to screw off.
And Vail struggled, I mean he tried, but he struggled. Puttling crap like Shrapnel and Jaji on Supercards made him about as much dough as a
400 lb hooker. And again with the phone ringing. "Todd PLEASE come back." "Todd, I'll be bankrupt without you!" and so on and so forth.
Eventually, I just couldn't stand the man whining to me any more and agreed to return to the ring to face Sykopath. But Todd, that was two weeks ago, why return now, so late? That's easy, minions, It's
Sykopath! Like the bum requires my full attention or something. I had a facial last Tuesday, and no chair loving schizo was going to disrupt my schedule.
And so, our story ends. I'm back, so cue the parades, flowers and tribute songs. Life is back to normal and all is right with the world.
So if you will kindly excuse me, I must get back to my boots. Todd knows they're more interesting than you people.
... ... ... ... Huh? Sykopath? You want me to respond to his comments? Fine, but I don't see why the mindless drool spew of a glorified retard deserves my
attention. Next time I sign a contract, I make sure it includes a clause where talking about worthless opponents is not a requirement. Syko says I'm a smart man. Damn right, I never believed in this idiot savant thing, but maybe there is something there after all. Syko says
I know when to showboat and when not to, also correct, the situation where "Not to" is appropriate has just never presented itself. Syko says I will never humiliate him. No, Syko, I won't, because nature went and damn well beat me to it. Syko says I have drug problems, No,
Forrest, I don't have ANY problems. Syko says the best man will win.
No rainman, I don't PLAN to. I don't have to PLAN. It takes minimal thought to beat garbage like you. Syko says, Syko says, Syko says.
Nice. I mean, nice that we have a working relationship with the Special Olympics in this organization. Real thoughtful. Good business
I guess. Vail's never been one for good business though. He advertises the match at Season's Bleedings as the "last Hardcore title match EVER" and then goes and schedules a unification match. So technically, THIS is the last match ever. And Todd Royal will go out in BSCW history as the last man to hold the BSCW Hardcore title. Not Shrapnel, Not
Whiskey Jakk, Not DDI, Not even YOU Rainman. ME. Todd Royal. Stings huh? But No, Syko says that he will hold onto his belt because it is REAL. Real, because it takes people with real blood, with real sweat, with real tears to win that belt. No Forrest, that belt is trash. Trash because it takes people who DO SWEAT, people do DO BLEED, people who
DO CRY to stoop to that level. Thats you, isn't it Sykopath. A
Bleeder, a crier. I DON'T SWEAT! I DON'T BLEED! I DON'T CRY! It's a real belt huh Syko? Is that the best excuse you've got for me? It's real because you cry and my belt's fake because I'm not some retarded pussy? Seriously Skyo, I could eat alphabet soup and shit out a better excuse.
So nurture your "real" belt. Water it with your pussy tears and shine it with your idiotic sweat. Cuz when that's my belt, all I'll use it for is to hold my pants up. Have fun, rainman, cuz your life is about to get a whole lot tougher.
[Royal finishes tying up his boots and walks out the door... laughing.] ["Scrape" replays as the closing credits roll. Finally, we fade to black.] =================================================================== BSCW - http://efeduniverse.com/bscw
