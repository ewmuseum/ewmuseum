BSCW — Card

From Pillar To Post

25 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To join the BSCW Mailing List, send an e-mail with [JOIN LIST] in the subject header to [detail withheld] ============================================================================== [The BSCW logo flashes on the screen, as "Blood Covered Microphone" by
Afro Skunkiak begins to play. Several scenes of last week' s Sunday
Night Blockbuster are shown.
Lieutenant Buzzcut Gorilla Pres Slams Grinnin over the top rope, sending the Grinman crashing into the steel guard railing.
Max C sets Andrew Sampson up for a Short Arm Clothesline, only to float it into a devastating Death Valley Bomb.
Andrew Sampson nails Max C with a Hip Toss, using only the considerable power in his legs. Taurus cracks Joker over the head with a kendo stick, splintering the weapon in half. Finally, Memphis Outlaw Leap Frogs over brian Deegan, only to be caught with a Tornado Spinebuster by John O'Riordan. That scene fades into another as the BSCW television studio, the scene of "From Pillar
To Post" is seen. Sitting behind the desk awaiting their cue are Brian
Stephens and Ian Finnegan. Brian nudges Ian, who seems to be talking to someone off-camera. Ian laughs and looks to Brian with a scowl.]
Ian Finnegan: What!? Can't you see I'm having a conversation over here? Brian Stephens: (Looks to Ian) We're on....Umm...Welcome to Pillar to
Post, I'm Brian Stephens and joined by Ian Finnegan. Less than seventy-two hours away to Detonation. This card is shaping up to be one of the best Pay Per Views ever in the history of BSCW. From the dark matches to the main event, only the best of BSCW's talent will be showcased.
Ian Finnegan: Dark Matches? Enlighten me and the viewing public,
Brian. If someone has a ticket to the event, who can they look forward to seeing in the Dark Matches?
Brian Stephens: I think it's its the old meeting the new, with "Deadly" Derek Irvin will be facing the BSCW's former champ, Austin
Blacktop. We also have Darkcyde Nitechild facing recently off the injured list, Jose La Parka.
Ian Finnegan: Wow. Darkcyde Nitechild. Stop my feet from dancing. But, at least we get the return of "El Loco" out of the deal..You take the good with the bad, I suppose. Speaking of which, what the hell happened to the JwO?
Brian Stephens: (just shrugs) Don't know, don't care. We have quite a few topics to cover. The mass exodus of half the roster, your new show, the Weigh-in and predictions for Detonation. And of course a close analysis of each and every match at Detonation.
Let's first start with the release of the "dead weight" here in BSCW. That included Hellz Angelz, Crucifix, Barbwire Jack, (stops to take breath) Whew! McGregor, Liza Monelli, the Olsen Twins, and the
Rebel... Ian Finnegan: He's still here! Brian Stephens:(turns to Ian) You really want to piss on my picnic, don't you. Ian, what do you think triggered half the roster going south?
Ian Finnegan: I think the technical term is called Laxicus Skillicus. I mean....Crucifix had the start of a decent feud with Swinger, but other than that...Does anyone care? No. Also, the word is that memphis Outlaw may very well be out of the sport permanently, due to the brutal attack he faced at the hands of the Deegans and the
O'Riordans. Brian Stephens: I hope he's gone due to lack of interest. He was about as entertaining as dingleberries. I will say, the Significant
Brothers, "Barely of Age" and the "Innovator of Offense" have proven alot to me over the past few weeks. Zodiac need to be watching their backs, because there's a new group in town, and they're not going to lie down.
Ian Finnegan: When those two groups collide, you can bet sparks will fly. One thing we'd be remiss if we didn't mention about all these
"releases" is the oppurtunity it's provided for so much of the up and coming talent here in BSCW. As a result of Crucifix being taken down for the count by Swinger, Goo is able to get a shot at the Canadian
Title. Another man who has benefitted by all of this is "Barely of
Age" Brendan Deegan, who in his second BSCW match will get a shot at
Vic Simmons' Hardcore Title.
Brian Stephens: It is a blessing for everyone. Instead of the fans having to listen to the indecipherable rantings of Barbwire Jack, this give more time to Goo, Lt. Buzzcut, and the others that will actually contribute to BSCW. BSCW's fan base is growing exponentially day by day, gaining steam. With new found success, you have gotten your own show, Verbal Beatdown. A pleasant surprise from the new creative team.
Ian Finnegan: The response has been great thus far. Anything that helps the great talent we have here in BSCW get over and ultimately get in a position of being able to be seen by the public can't be all that bad. Also, the reviews are in for our last Pay Per View event, with great recomendations all around. Footage of Bloodfest 2001 has been shopped around, and the reviews should be in shortly. Of course, that brings us to the stellar event this sunday, Detonation. And what an event it's shaping up to be, with all major titles on the line. Of course, it's a perfect example of new talent being given an oppurtunity to shine is the opener, with Lieutenant Buzzcut taking on the newest addition to the Fearless Family, "The Innovator of Offense"
Matt O'Riordan. John O'Riordan has already impressed fans across this county Brian, can his brother do the same?
Brian Stephens: Only time can tell. If he's even half as good as his brother, we can expect nothing but good things from him. This will be a good gauge for both men. Lt. Buzzcut, being relatively green to the
BSCW, I think that this could be a surprise, or even a main event in other feds.
The next match is one I'm filing in the "Why Files" the Rebel against
Mr. MAK. Okay, I've been a little rough on the Rebel as of late, but for a former champion, his latest outings have real lackluster. This is a battle between two of the firsts here in BSCW. This is something for you true BSCW marks, and as for the rest of you, the season finale of Sixty Minutes will be on.
Ian Finnegan: Ouch. Well, this is my take on it. Mr. M.A.K. has proven himself to be a welcome addition to the BSCW locker room, even to this day. Many were wondering if he was going to retain his poor attitude of old, but that hasn't been the case. In my eyes, this is
The rebel's chance to prove that he still has something to offer BSCW.
If he fails here, who knows what his future with this company will be. Up next is the Hardcore Title Match, with reigning champ Vic
Simmons taking on the sizable challenge of Brendan Deegan. Vic was on unpaid vacation last week for some reason, and he couldn't have picked a worse time to gte lazy. If he isn't on his "A" game, Brendan will break him in half without even trying.
Brian Stephens: When did we hire Kevin Nash? The only way I see Vic beating Brendan, is if Brendan never shows up, or is hit by a car driven by Simmons. Long and short of it, Simmons will be spanked like a little girl at the hands of Brendan Deegan.
The next match at Detonation is the Canadian Championship which pits two undefeated wrestlers, Goo and J.C. Swinger. I'm going out on a limb here, but I don't think this will be one for the archives. It will be entertaining, but don't forget about the second part of the
Sixty Minutes season finale.
Ian Finnegan: It's a hard one to call. Sure, Swinger's undefeated, but he's only gone against guys who's workrate is that of an old woman. Go, on the other hand, showed that he could put on a great showng against a man twice his size. Goo has proven himself as a man that can put on a real show, the real question has to be if the
Canadian Champion can hang with someone who knows something other than a Powerbomb. Following that match will be a contest that's anticipated by the fans only second to the main event. A Triple
Threat Iron Man Match for the Cruiserweight Championship. I think this might just be the first time a match of this kind has taken place. Add in the fact that it involves three of the most exciting high flyers in the business, and I'd say we have a potential match of the year.
Brian Stephens: We have Joker who doesn't like Leo, then we have
X-Kalibur that doesn't like Joker, so it appears that Joker is the odd man out. You do have to look at it like this, Joker has been former
Tag Team Champion, as well as a Cruiserweight Champion. Joker knows what's like to be a champ here in BSCW, and even though he learned about Taurus the hard way, it is only a matter of time before Joker attains a belt of some kind here in BSCW.
X's reign was short, but he still yearns for that Cruiserweight belt. We all know just how comfortable Leo is with that belt. It will take a lot to wrestle the belt away from the former Morlock.
Ian Finnegan: Morlock? BWA-HA-HA-HA!!! Man, did that gimmick ever suck. Wait...Wasn't that your contribution to the booking meetings?
Brian Stephens: No, no no... Dark Holy Matt was mine. Ian Finnegan: And for that sin against all of humanity you should be shot. Anyway, getting back on track. Up next will be a rematch from last sunday, as Max C will once again attempt to wrest the United
States Championship Belt away from the waist of Andrew Sampson. They had a hell of a match last week, which saw Andrew on the verge of victory. Did Buzzcut do Max a favor by interfering, and will Max change the tide this week?
Brian Stephens: Since his return, it is apparent that Max has shown no signs of ring rust, which means this monster can only get better in his career here in BSCW. Andrew Sampson has been one of the luckiest men in BSCW, will his luck continue. This could be one of those matches that people will be standing around the water cooler at work on Monday and will talk about. I'm not saying it's going to be match of the year material, but it will be one of the better ones we've seen in recent months.
The next match will be the Tag Team Title match, putting Gemini against the Significant Brothers in a No Interference Match. I'm not sure exactly who that is going to help. As of late, Gemini once again has been pushed to the back by the larger egos of Taurus and Leo. I think this alone will cause Gemini not to be at their best, giving the opening the Brothers they need to come out the new Champions.
Ian Finnegan: Edge and Christian? The Hardly Boys? In the words of my good friend Hannibal Carver, "Fook those bum punchers". This will be the match that revolutionizes and re-energizes the tag scene here in BSCW. The unstoppable squash machines that are Gemini against the high flying, quick as greased lightning Brothers. While I believe
Deegan and O'Riordan are destined to sit at the top of the tag team mountain, I don't think that day will be this Sunday. Gemini have worked too damn hard to give up the ghost now. it'll be a hell of a match, but I wouldn't call the reign of Gemini over just yet. Then, the reason we're all here. The main event. The Champion of the World versus The Japanese Akita. I can say without being a complete shill for this company that it simply doesn't get any bigger than this.
Brian Stephens: This is a match that is about two years in the making. It is know secret that these two men abhor each other. This
Sunday, at Detonation, two years of rage and angst will be delivered.
One man may walk away with the belt, but he will lose a part of his soul. Taurus against Justin Keith, you shouldn't have to say anymore than that.
Ian Finnegan: It's not often that a match doesn't need buildup. This is one of those cases. The Rebel needed the Greenhouse Match.
Citizen-X needed the Extreme Violence Rooftop Match. Taurus doesn't. This main event has stips, but instead of a stip that makes the match the star and the athletes participating secondary, it's designed to push both men up and beyond the stratosphere. Two out of Three Falls, with a different stipulation for each fall. I Quit, Submission Match, who knows. All that we do know is that this will be the night and the match where BSCW stakes it's claim as leader of the wrestling world.
Brian Stephens: [Looks to the stage crew.] We have just gotten word that the two main parties involved in the main event are ready for the first ever Weigh-in. We go via satellite to Ronan Alexander who is standing by.
[The scene changes to find a group of reporters standing around with notebooks, cameras and tape recorders so not a minute is missed. Seen standing on the stage is Ronan Alexander, he is about to greet the audience.]
Ronan: Thank you Ian and Brian, and welcome to the official weigh in for the main event at Detonation. Taurus if you please.
[Taurus, seen wearing nothing more than a pair of jogging shorts, approaches the scale. He takes a deep breath, an official plays a little bit with the scale, and waits for the arm to stop moving. He announces to the media in attendance.]
Official: Two Hundred Thirty Six Pounds and Three Quarters. [Taurus steps off the scale and proceeds to sit down. The official gestures to Justin Keith. He approaches the scale, and the official goes through the motions with the scale and announces to the crowd.]
Official: Two Hundred Sixty Seven and Four Ounces. [The official steps back. The curtain behind the main table opens, and out walks BSCW President Richard Vail, who receives a polite round of applause. He steps up to the podium and waits a moment for the applause to die down, and begins to speak.]
Richard Vail: Thank you ladies and gentlemen. We are assembled here for not only to weigh in these two great combatants, but for all of you to get some final thoughts before their epic contest this sunday at The Joint in Las Vegas, Nevada. We will hear from Taurus, the challenger first. His comments will be followed by the BSCW World
Champion, Justin Keith.]
[President Vail nods at Taurus, who walks up to the podium. President
Vail returns to his seat as Taurus walks up to the podium. He begins to address the media.]
Taurus: Thank you, Mr. Vail. I look back to some of the greats here in BSCW, the "Franchise", Deathcow, Austin Blacktop and the Ninja.
What I see there is a set of men that made that belt a worthy prize. But for the past year or two, men like Citizen X, Philippe Lafleur, and you, Justin Keith. Three men that have tainted the BSCW World
Belt. Three men that should have never been called champion.
For the longest time, I sat watching and letting others have their chance in the limelight. Well, after watching what's become of that belt, I have decided to take it upon myself to bring some sort of prestige back to the World's belt.
This Sunday, Justin, you may walk in the champion, but I will guarantee you will be leaving about ten pounds lighter. To go into me giving you the beating of your life is cliche and so overdone. The time for talk is just about over, and soon you will need to deliver.
Not since the "Franchise" faced Golden Fleace has there been such a mismatch in talent. I OWN YOUR ASS JUSTIN. And on Sunday you will find that out first hand.
[Taurus steps away from the podium and sits down. Justin picks up a stack of 3x5 cards and stands at the microphone. Justin begins to speak.]
Justin Keith: [Pulls the World Title off his waist and tosses it on his shoulder, gives a devious smile to the reporters as he begins to speak.] Around two years... I have been working for the BSCW since it first started two years ago. Back then, it had great wrestlers like
Shane, myself, Marcus among others. Now... the BSCW has changed a great deal since the beginning. Today... I stand here as the last man standing of the original BSCW on the active wrestling roster, everyone else has either passed on, left wrestling, come and go as they please or took there business elsewhere. [Turns to where Taurus is sitting as his face slowly turns to anger.] Crapus... you wanna talk about bringing prestige, honor and dignity to the World Championship? What kind is that, bloodlust, knife slinging, flesh tearing, barbed wire steel chair swinging? You come from Japan before or after the second
BSCW, talk about all your devastating matches and your immediately pushed into huge feuds with MY crew. You see, if it weren't for my crew back then, you never would have become as popular as you are today, if it weren't for my crew, Zodiac would just be another gang trying to get over with the fans. If it weren't for my crew... There would be [Begins screaming.] NO CRAPUS IN THE BSCW!!! YOU'D JUST BE
ANOTHER MORONIC JAPANESE REJECT WHO HANGS WITH TWO BLACK TWINS AND AN
ARROGANT IDIOT!!! MY CREW MADE ZODIAC!!! I..... MADE...... CRAPUS!!!!!... Sunday, kid, your dreams and fantasies of becoming
World Champion will come to a screeching halt. Being in the BSCW this long, being in the business this long I should say, I know how certain people work... And how to make those people fall. Crapus... You may be a tough cookie my friend, but you are NO WRESTLER BY ANY MEANS! What my strategy to take your ass down is for me to know... And everyone of you to find out. [A smile grows on his face as his eyes and Taurus'
meet eye to eye.] So Crapus... Come Sunday, you will be facing by far... [Yells again.] THE GREATEST WRESTLER IN THIS BUSINESS, THE MOST
POWERFUL FORCE IN THE HISTORY OF THE BSCW AND I SURE AS HELL AM NOT
THAT DAMN GOOD... [Yells at the top of his voice.] I AM BEYOND ALL
THAT BULLS**T! [Drops his head and begins giggling some more as he slowly speaks up to the reporters.] Heheh, the past couple weeks I have gotten my ass beating by the likes of Zodiac, Significant
Brothers, Rebel, and Blacktop. Sunday, this Sunday not only will
Crapus pay for everything he has brought upon me, EVERYONE WILL!
Then... When it is all said and done... The crowd has dropped their heads in disappointment... BSCW Officials' and Wrestlers' jaws drop in disbelief... As they look in the ring after our match... And see one man standing on the turnbuckle... Holding HIS Title high into the air... And that man will not be Crapus. [Begins whispering.] It will be... [Stands up straight and yells.] ME GOD DAMN IT! IT WILL BE I WHO
RETAINS MY CHAMPIONSHIP! IT WILL BE I WHO DESTROYS CRAPUS AND IT WILL
BE ME WHO WILL DOMINATE THE BSCW FOR AS LONG AS I PLEASE! Sunday is coming, I'm sure as hell ready... [Looks at Crapus with a huge smile.]
But are YOU really as ready as you say you are? [Justin steps down from the podium after smirking at Taurus. He goes back to his seat, as President Vail walks back up to the podium for a final time.]
Richard Vail: There you have it, ladies and gents. The time for talking is almost over. Other promotions have their little, pissant events...But none can compare to this. The time has come for BSCW to once again show the world that we might not be "where the big boys play", but we are where the absolute best the world of professional wrestling can be found. This is only the beginning... The first salvo in our world against all the pretenders. The Japanese Akita and Mr.
Keith will be the new kings of a new age. This is the beginning of a new era, members of the press... Don't be left behind. Thank you all for attending, there will be refreshments served in the lobby.
[President Vail steps down, as what appears to be millions of flashes go off. Justin Keith and Taurus stand up, and the scene switches back to the studio with Ian and Brian as we see them staring a hole into each other.]
Brian Stephens: Well, it appears that our words a couple of weeks back lit a fire under the ass of the champ. The main event doesn't look like such a massacre as we thought. President Vail can finally start to pay me again if the Pay Per View puts him back on the map.
Ian Finnegan: If everything goes according to plan, it appears that
Vail's words will prove to be prophetic. An intense, determined
Justin keith is good for BSCW, without a doubt. Many people have said that Blacktop turning away from Justin and Joker did his career a world of good, but I believe it did the career of the Champion of the
World an even better service. Justin has his killer instict back, and
Taurus might have something to worry about for the first time in his
BSCW career. Folks, I'm not going to shill Detonation, because I don't have to. This will be the event that puts BSCW back on the map,
I have no doubt as to the veracity of that. Folks, we have gone so unbelievable over our time slot it's not even funny. Stay tuned for more great BSCW action, and for god's sake order Detonation! Jesus!!
Brian Stephens: What is the name of the Pay Per View again? I think what the Pay Per View really needs is a Super Market Sweep Match.
Something like that'll be REALLY over, then again I sniffed glue during the Seventies. Right now, it's time to do Brian's Babble.
Rumor has it that newcomer "Deadly" Derek Irvin will make his presence felt during Detonation's Cruiserweight Ironman match.
Goo, seen walking out of a certain house of ill repute just outside of
Las Vegas, has proven that even hookers have standards.
This just in, no one cares really about the Rebel. Lt. Buzzcut lives and dies by the military motto "Don't ask, don't tell" and has been seen slipping his number to the former champion
Philippe Lafleur.
Justin Keith, has in fact, donated a large sum of money to various children's charities in the Wedgwood Washington Area.
That's it for this week's Brian's Babble. Ian Finnegan: Okay, we're out of time. But, I'm only three beers into this case... [leans over and picks up a case of Guinness.] So
I'm not going anywhere.
Brian Stephens: Right now, we are going to give our predictions for the card at Detonation. First we have Lt. Buzzcut and Matt O'Riordon.
I'm going with Buzzcut. Ian Finnegan: [Burp.] It should be a great match, but I have to agree. The monstrous Buzzcut will be too much for O'Riordan's debut.
Up next is Mr. M.A.K. versus The Rebel. The rebel has a lot to prove in this one, so I'm going to pick him to pull out the slight upset.
Brian Stephens: [looks over to Ian] Did you get any on you? Okay, I think what would make the crowd pop is if both guys had soda bottles and hit each other over the head repeatidely. The winner will be, I hate to say it, but the Rebel, only because no one knows what exactly
Mr. M.A.K is talking about.
The next match is hardcore match with Vic "Tim Dogg" Simmons and
"Barely of Age" Brendan Deegan. There shouldn't be any question, Vic will be beaten and beaten bad. "Barely of Age" will emerge new
Hardcore Champ. Ian Finnegan: Add in the fact that Vic no-showed last week with
Brendan being from the same state as me and the outcome is easy.
"Barely of Age" all the way. Up next we have the Canadian Championship on the line as reigning champ J.C. Swinger defends against the always entertaining Goo. Like I said, Goo has proven himself as a versatile performer here in BSCW, so I'm predicting that the Candian Title will hop aboard the Goo Train this Sunday.
Brian Stephens: I have one word for you, Goo-goo-ga-jube. Gooamania will be hitting BSCW like a tidal wave. Oh, I see Goo beating
Swinger. Next we have the Three Way Iron Man Match for the Cruiserweight Belt. I see Leo pulling it out and defending his belt, but it won't be a walk in the park for him however.
Ian Finnegan: I think it will most likely be a match of the year candidate, it'll be one hell of a war, but I think leo is just being established as a major player here in BSCW, and he's not ready to give up his gold yet. Up next we have the United States Title on the line, as Andrew Sampson defends his gold against Max C. With Dionysus in
Japan, and Million Dollar Dragon away in what I hear is a court-related leave of absence, Max C is really the only viable contender. I think this is just the beginning of a great feud based on mutual respect. I pick Andrew to retain, but this is not the last time these two will meet, not by a long shot.
Brian Stephens: I see Andrew retaining the belt as well. I just hope he knows that he's wrestling and not out picking out doylies with
Lila. Max C will come close, but he's just not up there yet with
Andrew. The next match is the Tag Team War between Gemini and the
Significant Brothers. I see the Significant Brothers taking it to the
Twins, but Gemini will narrowly retain their gold.
Ian Finnegan: As I said earlier tonight, "The New Sensation" and "Bad
Religion" are destined for tag team gold, just not right now. of course, a moneth ago you never would have guessed they'd be the second team in the company, so who knows if they'll shock the world yet again. Up next is quite possibly the most anticipated match in BSCW history. The Japanese Akita versus Mr. Keith. the two top men in this industry, going for the top prize in our business. At one time I would have picked Taurus by a landslide, but the newly rediscovered killer edge of Justin has made me change my mind. I think this will be one for the ages, but at the end Justin Keith will retain his treasured ten pounds of gold.
Brian Stephens: This is a match that I really don't feel comfortable making a prediction. I have to agree with you on one point, a week ago I would have chosen Taurus to slap Justin Keith, but now it appears that Taurus will be as busy as Jesse Jackson on Mother's Day.
I have to go with Taurus if I were to make a decision. He appears to be much hungrier than Justin, and this is something that Taurus hasn't had yet, and this may be his only time to get it.
There you go, how we see the matches going at Detonation. If we haven't reminded you enough, Detonation, live at the Joint, brought to you from the City of Sin, Las Vegas. Contact your cable providers, do what you need to get Detonation. For Ian Finnegan, I'm Brian
Stephens, and we'll see you after Detonation. [As "Porn" by Gaskill plays we see the closing credits as Ian and
Brian talk amongst themselves.]
