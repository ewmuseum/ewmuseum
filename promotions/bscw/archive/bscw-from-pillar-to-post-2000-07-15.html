From Pillar To Post

2000-07-15
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The BSCW logo flashes on the screen, as Swahili Death Chant by Immortal
Alice begins to play. Several scenes of last week' s Tuesday Night Terror are shown. A bloodied Kid Jobber taps out to the Armbar, Violent M lays out
The "Real" Slim Shady with the Violent M-Anator, Citizen-X nails the
X-Acution, Jabroni Mahoney nails Citizen-X with his trademark DDT, and "The
American Bad Ass" Austin Blacktop stares intensely at "The Ticking Time
Bomb" Tim Palmer. That scene fades into another as the BSCW television studio, the scene of "From Pillar To Post" is seen. Sitting behind the desk awaiting their cue are Brian Stephens and Richard Vail.::
Richard Vail: Good evening folks, welcome to the first installment of From
Pillar To Post. I'm BSCW President Richard Vail, and I'm joined tonight, and every night we do this if the check clears, Brian Stephens.
Brian Stephens: Good evening. Tonight we will bring you highlights and thoughts on the BSCW World Title Tournament's first round. We saw no big surprises during the first round.
Richard Vail: Oh come on. Kid Jobber? I thought he was on his way to be the next Ric Flair. As president, I always try to be objective, but I have to come clean. I mark for Kid Jobber.
Brian Stephens: (chuckles) Seriously, we saw one of the odd's on favorites,
Citizen X, advance over Jabroni Mahoney.
Richard Vail: Citizen-X held the big one for a while before we were taken off the air and had to file bankruptcy, a lot of people, myself including, see him going all the way. Of course, there's a group or three or four guys that could take the gold home.
Brian Stephens: My pick to win it all is the "American Bad Ass", can I say that? Oh well, he had by far the toughest first round competition in "The
Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer. I did see something I haven't seen in BSCW for a long time, a show of respect.
Richard Vail: Well, BSCW isn't exactly known for respect, more like a lack thereof. If those two could ever put the past in the past, they could be a frightening duo.
Brian Stephens: That would be one hell of a tag team, speaking of tag teams, we have a new arrival. Probably one of more "family friendly"
additions to BSCW, the former pWn, (to Vail) what the hell is that?....Tag
Team Champions.
Richard Vail: I believe it stands for Pro Wrestling News, which makes about as much sense as David Arquette as World Champ. Maybe they should have called themselves The Walter Cronkites.
Brian Stephens: I think I caught pWn's show between a couple of infomercials about hair removals. Did you know one of their (sarcastically)
stars poops on people. Richard Vail: Yeah, which I'm sure all our hardcore fans can remember Violent M doing to the the Canadian Title about a year ago. They do seem quite proud about having held the titles sixteen times. I find that odd, however, for it means they had to have lost it fifteen times. Not the kind of thing I'd think you'd brag about in public. More like the kind of thing you tell your therapist.
Brian Stephens: Well, you're swallowing...I mean making deals with the president, Nate Nations, you get certain "favors." I think I've killed a few too many brain cells on these two. They have a match against formidable opponents, Nighthaunter and new comer Dementor. That's gonna be the dark horse match next Tuesday (a note of sarcasm in his voice).
Richard Vail: Fright Night going up against Pee-Wee's Playhouse is what it'll be. Send the kids to bed early for that one folks. just worry about young kids watching Penguino eating stamps and trying it themselves. You could choke to death doing that. Tag Teams these days, no respect for the
U.S. Postal Service.
Brian Stephens: Eating stamps? Isn't that a federal offense? Richard Vail: I guess they don't worry about that over in Antarctica. I just shiver when I think what they do to those baby seals.
Brian Stephens: Richard, we have given them more heat in the last two minutes then they've had in their entire time in pWn. Let's get back to business, the two time Lechenstein champion, Philippe Lafleur. Made quite a debut in BSCW, with the assist from Violent M, over the "Real" Slim Shady.
Who's next, the "real" Slim Whitman? Richard Vail: What can I say? The kids love him. And if you get the cheap seats, you can almost be fooled into thinking he's the real thing, so if enough word gets around, MTV should be knocking on my door. Why did Violent
M attack Shady? Then again, why did Violent M join up with Justin Possible directly after being engaged in a bitter feud with him? You never know what's going on inside his head, and perhaps that's for the best.
Personally, I thought Lafleur had a great showing, up until the point where he tried to walk to the back after getting one too many closed fists to the face.
Brian Stephens: Lafleur is a fa...a little light....a flaming....he's a little come ci, come ca. Richard Vail: Does that mean "Pepe"....Nevermind. I don't want to know. That reminds me of that movie Big Daddy, the part about the "old balls". Brian Stephens: Richard, this is supposed to be a family show talking of old balls, this reminds me of the Christmas when you...
Richard Vail: I was cleared of those charges. Let's get back on track, or make the effort, anyway. On the subject of Philippe, he has to contend with
Count Xavier Darkblud this Tuesday. Both hail from Europe, both have a submission-based attack, both employ submission moves as a finisher, but that's where the comparison ends, wouldn't you say?
Brian Stephens: This should be an interesting match, we have the technical arsenal of Philippe, then we have the bend ya over attack of Darkblud.
Innocence will be lost that night. Richard Vail: Like I said about Shady, the kids love him. The long hair, the heavy metal music, the candles, it's a winner. I think what it'll all come down to is what is the more effective move, the Arc D'Triumphe or the Gore
Grind?
Brian Stephens: We'll know on Tuesday. I'm not a big fan of Darkblud's music, but I'd rather listen to that than watch tapes of old pWn pay per views.
Richard Vail: People paid for those? Wow, whoever bought those...I have a bridge in Brooklyn I'd like to sell you.
Brian Stephens: Well, all of the pay per views were in countries that didn't speak English.....coincidence or dismissed as chance. Did you know, they had someone using the name of a former BSCW wrestler, the great Viper. Richard Vail: I hear that Leo's stablemate, Taurus, fell on hard times and actually found employment there. After six months as an employee, he was able to afford a brand new cardboard box.
Brian Stephens: I think the court called it "community service." Or the money made was to support his smack habit.
Richard Vail: Ok, enough of this. We are here to discuss sports ENTERTAINMENT. Note the word entertainment. With that in mind, let us speak of that backyard fed no longer.
Brian Stephens: We have via satellite, "The Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer, he joins us from the streets of New York (a split screen opens to see the
Time Bomb as well as our hosts) Tim, welcome to the show...
Tim Palmer: Thank you...glad to be here...Your pleasure Brian Stephens: Of course. Why don't you start out by telling the fans at home how you got into the business, and what led you to Blood Sweat and
Chairs Wrestling. Tim Palmer: Their were a number of things..... First, I was born in a small town outside of Rochester New York. At the age of 2 my father left me and my mother alone. My mother was forced to Move deep into NYC to be able to afford a home to support me and herself on a low income. This of course, was too much for her to handle..and At the age of 13, I was kicked out of the house...and onto Main Street, the most Dangerous Street In All of New
York. I found myself with no food, shelter, or friends.....
Richard Vail: Must have toughened you up pretty early. Tim Palmer: Your Damn right it did...I did the only thing I new how to do, and that was fight. I fought for everything: Clothes, Food, Women. You name it..I have Brawled for it.
Richard Vail: I see. Where did you get your first exposure to pro wrestling? Tim Palmer: When I was 18 Years old...I decided to put my bad temper and abnormal strength to good use, and I joined the Killer Kowalski Wrestling school. Their he taught me how to use my temper to my advantage. Soon after a received a phone call from President Stephen Bunn, and was welcomed to the XCW
Brian Stephens: Kowalski's school, for those at home who don't know, is based in Malden, Massachusetts. Just next door to Boston, home of BSCW.
Richard Vail: What were some of your most memorable moments or matches in XCW? Tim Palmer: In Xtreme Championship Wrestling, I held the World Title not once, not twice, not even three times....but four damn times. Defeating the likes of Bret "The Ass Kicker" Hart, Stone Cold Steve Austin, The Macho Man
Randy Savage, and Paul White. The most memorable match of all time, was when I defeated Ric Flair for my first ever World Title. Many people consider Ric Flair the greatest wrestler that ever lived...and I pinned his shoulders to the mat for the 1...2....3
Brian Stephens: That is impressive. What caused you to leave that promotion? Tim Palmer: The reason I left was that their was becoming no competition what so ever. So I retired from the XCW still wearing the world title. And left for bigger, and better things
Brian Stephens: Which brings us to the present day. Obviously we all saw the five star match you had with Austin Blacktop in your BSCW debut. What are your thoughts on that match, looking back?
Tim Palmer: Looking back, I would say that I have a new found respect for
Austin. We fought tooth and nail for damn near 20 minutes, and it was one of the greatest matches of my career. Austin has amazing strength and power, and is one of the top prospects in this business. I lost that match, that is all their is too it, I am not going to make any excuses, Austin was the better man, and the better man won. But the issue between me and Blacktop is far from over
Richard Vail: So you would not decline a rematch, if one was offered? Tim Palmer: Definitely not, Tim Palmer is always ready for a fight...and I don't lay down for anybody
Richard Vail: Now that you're out of the running for the world title, what or who are in your sights for the future? Tim Palmer: Are you saying that I am out of the ranks for the world title
Richard? You don't think that I could capture that belt at any moment?
Richard Vail: Woah, don't get crazy, I'm just saying you've been taken out of the tournament. Who knows what can happen after the champ is crowned. But getting back to my original question, what is in the cards for Tim Palmer? Tim Palmer: After the champ is crowned, I am going to be right their ready to snatch it from him. Because I am the best that the wrestling world has to offer...and I always will be. My goal is the BSCW World Title...and anybody that gets in my path to gold, will be taken out
Brian Stephens: I'm going to do a little word association right now. I'll say a name, and you tell me whatever comes to your mind. Ready?
Tim Palmer: Shoot Brian Stephens: Citizen-X Tim Palmer: washed up....he has done everything that he can, and it is time to step down, and let other people have a shot.
Brian Stephens: Philippe Lafleur Tim Palmer: confused.....I don't know what is going on in that Frenchmen's head, but something is not right. He thinks that he can run with the big boys, and I don't think he could do that on his own
Brian Stephens: Boz Tim Palmer: I'll give you 3..Cocky, Arrogant, SOB. This guy is so full of himself, that he doesn't realize that he is not all that great. I would love to get my hands on BOZ...and show the world, what a pathetic piece of trash he really is
Brian Stephens: And finally, "The American Bad Ass" Austin Blacktop Tim Palmer: Respect...He has my complete 100 percent respect as a pro wrestler. As a person? I'm not sure what to think. But Having traveled around the world with Austin, I know that his work ethic, and talent...are almost immeasurable. Austin Brings out the best in me, and everytime that you see "The Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer -versus- "The American Bad Ass"
Austin Blacktop, you know something special is going to happen in the ring
Brian Stephens: I would definitely agree with that last statement. We thank you for your time, Mr. Palmer, and look forward to more good things from you in the future.
Tim Palmer: You can count on that....I am going nowhere but up in the BSCW
::The split screen returns to a full screen, as Richard Vail turns to Brian
Stephens.:: Richard Vail: Well, very interesting and controversial comments there by Tim
Palmer. Did you see his face turn red when you mentioned Boz?
Brian Stephens: I think that's a can of worms he just doesn't want to open. Boz was one of the best in BSCW, right next to Dark Holy Matt. Richard Vail: (Look of bewilderment) Whaat? Are you on the sauce? Brian Stephens: Just making sure you're awake, Boz was never defeated when you made those bad deals...you know the powdered water stocks.
Richard Vail: Who knew? I just got some bad advice, the same people that told me S.D. Jones was going to be the future of my company.
Brian Stephens: (rolls his eyes) Blacktop has a real test in the semifinals, facing the winner of the Citizen X, Blooden match. Does the newcomer have what it takes to continue his winning ways? Blooden was of course a former U.S. champ, and came within a hair of grabbing the World
Title from the man he takes on this Tuesday, Citizen-X. Of course, that loss to Citizen-X seemed to take the wind out of him, so there's a chance even if he scores a win against X, he might be too psychologically drained to put up a full-out assault against Blacktop. Citizen-X, on the other hand, might be too tired after taking on a monster like Blooden to withstand the attack of the nearly seven foot Blacktop.
Richard Vail: I have to disagree, experience will be the factor. Blacktop had one killer of a match, but does that stand up against the wars that
Blooden and Citizen-X have had here in BSCW? I have to say that Blacktop is one hell of a talent, but if he makes it past Lafleur or Darkblud, Blooden or Citizen-X will be nothing more than a learning experience. In time I believe Blacktop will be ready for such a challenge, I just question if this is all too early for him.
Brian Stephens: Well, let's just ask him, joining us via the phone, The American Bad Ass, Austin Blacktop... Welcome to the show Austin, and let me just congratulate you on your win against "The Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer in your debut here in BSCW.
Blacktop: Thank you Brian. I must say that was a hard fought match against Palmer and I see this kid going somewhere in the future. Richard Vail: It was definitely a five star match. Why don't we start out with you giving the good people at home a little background on your history in the world of pro wrestling
Blacktop: Well Richard it all started at the tender age of 13 thirteen. Were I turn to the streets of Detroit to get away from the trouble life at home. The only way to escape the life I was living....ex-biker and convict...I turn to wrestling
Brian Stephens: Yes, a very similar background to the man you fought in your debut, Tim Palmer. Richard Vail: How did you find the transition from street fighting to wrestling in the squared circle? Did you have a rough time adapting? Blacktop: Yes that is very true about Palmer background. And in some way I see a lot of me in Palmer. Richard the transition from street fighting to wrestling in the squared circle was a pretty rough time. I was living on gimmick from gimmick...fed to fed. Paycheck to paycheck. Then I finally said to hell with all the gimmick sh*t. I am just going to be myself and do what
I know how to do...and that is kick ass.
Brian Stephens: Which is basically what today's fan wants. Seeing as how you and Palmer have so much in common, any chance of the two of you forming an alliance of some kind in the future?
Blacktop: Well Brian I am not at reason now to talk about...but the fact of the matter is...if we do form an alliance no one will be prepare for what we bring...and neither will you.
Brian Stephens: Well, I can't argue with that. Two behemoths like you and Mr. Palmer on the same side, that just spells destruction. Richard Vail: Seeing as how you are a newcomer here, you run the risk of being a target for some of the veteran talent. Violent M and Boz to just name a couple, any thoughts on that?
Blacktop: Well see Violent M...I have seen his talent and I know what he can do..but he is no American Bad Ass. As far as Boz...I can't trust that son of a b*tch as far as I could throw him. And let's leave it at that.
Richard Vail: Fair enough. Obviously you have your sights set on the World Title Austin, but beyond that, what are your future plans and goals here in BSCW? Blacktop: Well lets just say that were I go in the BSCW I will leave a body count bigger than the holocaust. And goals...who needs goals...they are just like dreamers...can be shatter at anytime.
Brian Stephens: Definitely something to ponder. As our fans at home know, I did a little word association with Mr. Palmer earlier in the program. If it's all right with you, Blacktop, I'd like to toss out a few names, and you just tell me whatever comes to mind. Are you game?
Blacktop: Let the games begin Brian Stephens: Ninja Blacktop: Great Wrestler...been missing for over 6 months...BOD through and through Brian Stephens: All right, Philippe Lafleur Blacktop: Great International Talent...the Marlon Wayans of the BSCW Brian Stephens: Blooden Blacktop: Crybaby...Oh I want to whine about my past...he would do us all a favor if he would shut the hell up Brian Stephens: Ooh, that'll get some heat. Citizen-X Blacktop: A stepping stone in my path Brian Stephens: Yikes. Finally, "The Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer Blacktop: Like I have said before...a man I respect...a lot of me in him but he just does not see it Brian Stephens: Very interesting. Well, Austin, that's all the time we have for this interview. I thank you for taking time out of your busy training schedule for having this talk with us.
Blacktop: And thank you guys for having me. ::The phone hangs up, and the cameras zoom in on Brian Stephens.:: Brian Stephens: Marlon Wayans? And you asked me if I was on the sauce? I see Lafleur as more of a Shecky Green, kids ask your parents.
Richard Vail: I thought you saw him more as a Liberace? Brian Stephens: Maybe, the teletubby "Poe". Did you know pWn had a world champion by the same name.
Richard Vail: That's disturbing. But...I sense we are putting America to sleep with all this pWn talk, so let's continue. Brian, I assume you've had your ear to the floor listening for rumors..any hot news items to report?
Brian Stephens: I have plenty. It was reported that a certain pWn head was interested in selling popcorn at BSCW events, the official was not available for comment. Someone else was on the pay phone.
Violent M should be out for a couple of weeks nursing his injury. It appears that talent from all over the globe are flocking, to BSCW. Right now, a lot of them are training in the "bush league." Rumors are circulating about Krovorkian returning to BSCW. That's something
I really hope is true.
Richard Vail: Speaking of old starts returning, have you heard anything on the whereabouts of the members of Zodiac?
Brian Stephens: I did. Roll the film. :The camera finds Virgo turning tricks in a leper colony. Someone tells her, Keep the tip! The camera cuts to Gemini, they are starring in gay pornos. The cover of the porn videotape reveals that it was a directed by
D. Cow. One more scene shows Leo, realizing that his gimmick did indeed suck so much that not even pWn would have him. He has taken a job with WCW as a creative consultant. ::
Richard Vail: That was indeed a revealing and depressing piece of film. Brian Stephens: It's a shame, such a great stable, hey didn't they work for you before? Richard Vail: Yes, but then we hired Boz. Do I need to explain why I decided to switch dance partners? I didn't think so. Didn't you say that Leo was going to be one of the big talents of the new millennium?
Brian Stephens: (at a loss for words) umm....Let's ummm...take a look at the card for next Tuesday.
Richard Vail: Fair enough. Up first we have the cartoonish Antarctics going up against the apparently mute Nighthaunter and Dementor. I'll have to go with the Penguin and the Ski Instructor here.
Brian Stephens: And I would just go for popcorn. Maybe empty myself out. That would be as interesting as watching snails do the nasty. Richard Vail: And with that oh so attractive mental picture dancing in our heads, let's move on to the second match. The "Real" Slim Shady going up against "The American Bad Ass" Austin Blacktop. In this one I think we'll see The Career Ender, and very well could be a career ender for Shady.
Brian Stephens: I have only one word to describe that match...SQUISH. Richard Vail: That's another way of putting it, sure. Then we have a handicapped match, "The Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer going up against the team of the new millennium, Kid Jobber and Jabroni Mahoney. I have to go for
Palmer to get one in the loss column, just because you have to love Jobber and Mahoney.
Brian Stephens: There is safety in numbers, every curtain jerker, and all the main eventers of pWn will be pulling for their peers, Jobber and
Mahoney. I too see Palmer losing, after all his mind isn't all there after the disappointing loss to Blacktop.
Richard Vail: Which leads us to Philippe Lafleur taking on Count Xavier Darkblud. If Philippe can stay out of the way of the Metal Fart, I think he can pull off a victory here.
Brian Stephens: Who comes up with these names? A good scientific wrestler can beat a good brawler. Who knows, maybe Billie could get physically involved in the match.
Richard Vail: He certainly was instrumental in Lafleur's victory over The
"Real" Slim Shady. Which leads us to our main event, Blooden versus
Citizen-X. This one is almost too close to call. If Citizen-X can dazzle Blooden with his quickness before Blooden gets a chance to use his arsenal of power moves, this one might belong to the master of the X-Acution.
Brian Stephens: I think Blooden can take it, if he gets therapy in time. If not, Citizen-X will score an upset. Richard Vail: It should be very interesting, when all is said and done. Well,that's all the time we have for now folks, join us next month for another installment of From Pillar To Post, where we will have an exclusive interview with the new BSCW World Champion. For Brian Stephens, I'm Richard
Vail. Good night everyone.
