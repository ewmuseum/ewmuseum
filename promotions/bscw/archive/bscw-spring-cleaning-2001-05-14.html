BSCW — Rules

Spring Cleaning, spots open

14 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, BSCW is a e-wrestling league that recently switched from Message Boards to an E-mail base of operations. This hasn't been well received by some of the die hard board fedders, and thus we have quite a few spots open. All interested parties should send applications to
[detail withheld] Rules ========================================================= Rules 1. All flashes (i.e. Roleplays) and strats are sent to [detail withheld] . After a certain amount of flashes have been sent to my inbox, they will be presented on our flash show, "Verbal Beatdown". Please only send one flash at a time, as only one will be used for each show.
2. No over-exaggerated super powers. 3. You may handle up to four wrestlers, as long as you have a Heavyweight, a Cruiserweight, and a Tag Team. Your Tag Team may compete in the Tag Team Division only, and only be tapped for Singles Competition if the storyline/angle calls for it.
4. No harassment of the other handlers. 5. If you have a question as to why you lost or why you aren't getting a title shot, contact me via e-mail at [detail withheld], but please do not complain over the board.
6. You can only hold one singles title at a time. If you do defeat a champion and are a champion yourself, expect to relinquish the title you held previously to that win. The exception to this rule is if you hold a singles title and win the Tag Team Titles.
7. All Out of Character posts are to be put on the OOC Board. Posting out of character comments in a flash or "roleplay" are prohibited and will not be tolerated. If anyone posts an out of character comment in a flash, they will be given a week-long "Tour of Jobbing" where they will be defeated in a most humiliating manner at any shows that occur that week. If it occurs a second time, you will be fired, no questions asked.
8. If you do not roleplay for your first two weeks in BSCW, you will be fired. If you fail to roleplay for two weeks without letting me know you're unable to roleplay, you will also be fired. If you're unable to roleplay for any reason, let the president of BSCW know, and something will be worked out until you are able to roleplay again.
9. If you use another handler's character, or one of the BSCW staff characters without permission, you are immediately fired. 10. Lastly, the most important rule of all...Have fun! Now that you've read the rules and feel that you can adhere to them, fill out the application below. If you have a form application that you prefer to use, that will be accepted as well. If accepted, you should hear something back within two days. If you're not accepted, you won't hear back from us at all.
Application ========================================================== Handler's Name AIM/MSN Messenger (Please verify which) Wrestler's Name Height Weight Description of Wrestler (If you have a .gif or .jpg of your wrestler, send it and it will be put on your profile page)
Hometown Style (Hardcore,Technical,High Flyer,etc.) Twenty Signature Moves (These are moves other than your finisher that your wrestler uses on a regular basis)
Set Up Move (This is the move your wrestler uses to set up his opponent for a finishing move.) Finishing Move Secondary Finishing Move(s) Manager / Valet (If applicable) Tag Team (If applying as a tag team, please fill out seperate applications for the individual wrestlers)
Twenty Tag Team Signature Moves Tag Team's Finisher Tag Team's Entrance Music Stable Entrance Music Wrestler's Gimmick / Personality Weaknesses & Strengths (It's common knowledge, no one's perfect. As a rule, have the same number of weaknesses as you do strengths.)
Ring Strategy (In other words, how your wrestler operates in the ring. Include what he will do in certain situations.)
History of your wrestler Sample Flash ========================================================== Thanks for your time and I hope to see you in the (make believe) ring! Rich [detail withheld] http://efeduniverse.com/bscw
