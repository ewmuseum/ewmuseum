BSCW — News

Strange Brew

21 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yep, I'm still alive. It's Ian Finnegan back with all the news that fits. Let's go. First, the comings and goings. As we heard from my internet news smark Damon Gleave, The Reaper is indeed out of BSCW. NAWF star he may be, but it didn't work out for him here in BSCW. Speaking of NAWF guys, it looks like Marty Howe is gone too. Although seeing as how he never made an in-ring appearance, he may just be taking some time to get ready for a return to ring action. And as Gleave mentioned, all signs point to the Process of Weeding Out to being the time where
Blake Storm, Dan Owens and The Mob Squad are weeded out of their BSCW contracts. Dan Owens is a big maybe on that list however, as he has impressed quite a few of the right people with his in-ring ability, especially in the Cruiserweight Tournament.
People that are coming are the Kill Army. For those of you unfamiliar with Reverand Black and Lupe Blanco, they are unlike any team we've ever had here in BSCW. Apparently their interest in BSCW was piqued when they saw the antics of Debonair, who as Damon said will be facing
Kill Army among others in a Teag Team Ladder Battle Royal at the last ever Master of the Ring. Hannibal Carver, who had a short stint in
ACW [Kill Army's previous promotion] was reported to have put the final coffin in the deal, telling the higher ups that Black and Blanco could be the team that solidifies the Tag Team division in BSCW. Only time will tell.
In another follow-up to Gleaver 2000, The Grinman has indeed returned to BSCW. In an even more shocking turn of events, Seth Harker, no longer "The Darksider", has returned. He seems to have gone through some serious gimmick surgery, so it'll be interesting to see if that aids him in finding a niche here in BSCW.
There are rumours around that the feud between "Deadly" Derek Irvin and Max Caschera may have to be scrapped. No idea why, but with feuds with Whiskey Jakk and Taurus right around the corner, it shouldn't pose to much of a problem as far as the main event scene is concerned.
Isn't it weird how Taurus is considered a "veteran" by the likes of
Greed, even though his time in BSCW equals about two years? Wacky.
Speaking of Greed, apparently the promo which "Deadly" Derek Irvin taped earlier this week to be shown at Battleground was the cause of much controversy and curse words around the home office. Should be interesting to see what all the fuss is about.
Carver's impromptu match with Whiskey Jakk at Ascension was no one time deal, apparently. Carver has healed up completely and is looking forward to a full return to the ring. A match with Carver teaming up with Richard Harris against Debonair is reportedly in the works for the Supercard after Process of Weeding Out, tentatively titled
"Season's Bleedings". "Deadly" Derek Irvin is reported to be part of another interpromotional tournament, this one run by the VWF. Damian Payne is reportedly in talks with the promoters of VWF to take part in that event as well.
And that's it for me. Enjoy Process of Weeding Out, and I'll be talking to you a week or so afterwards with more news from BSCW.
