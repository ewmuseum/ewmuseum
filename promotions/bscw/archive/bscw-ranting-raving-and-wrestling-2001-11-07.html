BSCW — News

Ranting, Raving & Wrestling

7 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey there, BSCW fans, it's Damon Gleave coming at you for the second edition of "Ranting, Raving and Wrestling." We'll take a quick look back at Hostile Intentions and a quick look ahead to Ascension, and give you some inside dirt about what direction BSCW could be headed in. Let's get this mother started, huh?
Hostile Intentions was another solid show put out by BSCW recently. I'd have to say that it wasn't quite as good as Ragnarok or Bloody
Hell, but Matt Wilson leaving BSCW like a little sissy girl took one match that would have been dynamite off the card, leaving only 4 real matches.
I really liked the opening match, as it gave each guy a real chance to shine. Well, everyone except T. Read, that is. He may be done with
BSCW and had only one guaranteed appearance, so he probably got jobbed out right away in it. The match was booked correctly, as it allowed
Damian Payne and Jaji both to get over their respective talents, with
Payne being a dominating power wrestler and Jaji an exciting high flyer with an asshole manager (Sabu, anyone?), while still allowing
Matt O'Riordan to take the pinfall and keep his push going. Like I said before, Matt O'Riordan is known backstage as a stand up guy, and
BSCW loves his great attitude and great work ethic, and he's getting pushed for it. And, believe it or not, Matt O'Riordan may find himself in his first main event really soon. More on that later. We'll see where Jaji goes from this match, but it looks like Payne will form some sort of alliance with Max C, and he has a big match against
Blacktop at Ascension. Will the veteran put the strong rookie over?
We'll have to wait and see. The "End of the Road" match at Ascension was good. It started slow, but finished pretty well, and the right man won. This doesn't hurt
Rebel at all, and pushes Boz further up so he can go on to feud with someone like Justin Keith. Whether that issue will be over the world title remains to be seen. Nice work by both these guys here, but it's
Rebel and Boz, and the veterans showed why they're BSCW elite.
The Taipei Death Match... What else can you say besides the fact that it was quite possibly the most insane match in BSCW history? This was one of those matches that you don't rate on psychology or mat work.
Hell, the guys were hitting finishers two minutes in to the thing! It was just one spot after another, and high quality, dangerous, bloody spots at that. All three men in this match gave everything they had, and I thought each man got put over well. Sykopath won in the end, continuing his undefeated reign in BSCW. Whiskey Jakk looks to be pushed, as the Thunderdome match showed later, so I hope Shrapnel isn't the odd man out here. Maybe he'll win the Cruiserweight title tournament, but my bet is still on Joker.
The main event was a good mix of old school mentality and intense wrestling. The crowd really was into things, and that can mean everything at a live event. This was a really unpredictable match once the tag teams got out of it. I don't think anyone really expected much from them anyways, which is nothing against their work rate or anything. But when it was down to 3-on-1, did anyone NOT think that
Keith would then pin Matt James? I was surprised Richard Harris put over Whiskey Jakk after Jakk had been in the Taipei death match, but it looked like they REALLY wanted to push Whiskey. And then I was
SHOCKED when Whiskey pinned Justin Keith to win the world... Oh wait, he and DDI won the tag team titles. I can see that they want to continue the slow descension between DDI and Whiskey and give us one more DDI and Keith world title match, but I don't know if I liked the result. I mean, DDI has upcoming feuds with Max C and Taurus, does he really need one with Whiskey Jakk, too?
On to Ascension we go, where the main event will feature Keith and
Irvin in a ladder match. Should be pretty good stuff. The semis of the
Cruiserweight tournament will take place, and I think Joker and
Shrapnel will be the ones to move on, but now that Debonair lost the tag titles, maybe Bryson will. Whiskey Jakk takes on Richard Harris in what should be a really good big man match. I'm REALLY looking forward to that. The rest of the card features a lot of new guys looking to make an impression. I'm really hoping that NAWF superstar Reaper and
Jaji have a great match, but we'll have to wait and see. Expect good in ring wrestling and some angle development.
So where does BSCW go from here? Well, Keith and Boz appear like they will feud, as will DDI and Max C, until Taurus is completely back and ready to go. BSCW really wants to make the DDI and Taurus feud a long, money making feud, as it is a combination that has never been done before between two of the best wrestling talents in the world. And who
WOULDN'T want to see a hair vs. mask match between those two? That stip also leaves open the possibility that they could feud with the world title NOT on the line…and around Max C's waist. Whiskey
Jakk is the favorite to be the next North American champion, but many others will have a say about that. Sykopath filmed a spot at Hostile
Intentions where he was hit by a car, and may miss some time. BSCW could have had a wrestler run him down to start an angle, but I guess they didn't want to go the WWF way.
After Ascension comes "The Process of Weeding Out," which may have been named when someone was on weed themselves. We're looking at a main event with, believe it or not, DDI and Matt O'Riordan versus Max
C and Damian Payne. BSCW management really wants to push O'Riordan and
Payne, and will see what a match here will do for them. They also don't want to blow a money match between DDI and Max C right off the bat. The rest of the card should be solid, as apparently we'll see a battle royal for the North American title, and matches earlier in the night to earn title shots later in the night. Stay tuned. What would be interesting is if Whiskey Jakk won that battle royal, and DDI and
Whiskey had to defend the tag team titles on the same night that DDI and Matt O'Riordan teamed up. I don't know why that would be interesting, but it just would.
Okay, "Gleaver 2000" is done for now, but I'll be back after Ascension to give you some more dirt. Catch you later.
