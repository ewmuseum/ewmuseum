BSCW — News

Strange Brew

7 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's the drunk with the slow internet connection, back with another edition of Strange Brew.
First off... Screwy finish, thy name is the Thunderdome Match. It seemed as if Whiskey Jakk had done the impossible by becoming BSCW
World Champion after taking the beating of a lifetime in the Taipei
Death Match, but it wasn't to be. After missing an eye popping DDI off the cell from Derek Irvin, Jakk stopped Justin Keith from putting
Irvin away, ultimately putting the World Champ away with a Jakked Up
Powerbomb. But due to the unique rules of that match, it would end with Irvin and Jakk being crowned as new BSCW Tag Team Champions. One has to wonder if this has caused a rift within Greed, since Irvin and
Jakk's stablemates in Debonair went into Hostile Intentions as tag champs. One thing that is known is that Irvin is not too pleased with
Whiskey, as he claims that if not for Jakk he would be the World
Champion right now.
Speaking of the Taipei Death Match, that had to be one of the bloodiest, most brutal and most physical matches I've ever seen. In the end it was BSCW's Hardcore Messiah, Sykopath, once again retaining his title. It had to be one of the closest calls for the champ in his
BSCW career however, as there were too many near falls in the contest to count. The status of the Hardcore division has to be in question however, as Sykopath was seriously hurt in an accident in the parking lot of the Showplace Theater. Obviously the thoughts of the entire
BSCW staff are with the champ during this time.
Something else of interest is that the two other combatants in the
Taipei Death Match are on the cusp of getting to a whole new level here in BSCW. Shrapnel is heavily favored to make it to the end of the
Cruiserweight Championship Tournament, and to take the whole thing.
Whiskey Jakk, on the other hand, impressed all the right people with his amazing performance at Hostile Intentions. Not only did he take
Sykopath to the limit, he went all the way in the main event at
Hostile Intentions, grabbing the Tag Team Championship for himself and
"Deadly" Derek Irvin. As a result of this, the booking committee is ready to push Jakk towards the vacant North American Championship. It seems that Irvin is not the only BSCW rookie to have a breakthrough year in 2001.
Speaking of which, another individual who has stepped up to the plate as of late is "The Icon Killer" Matt O'Riordan. From Jobber To The
Stars to the latest addition to the super stable Greed, Matt has shown that he's so much more than just the brother of former Cruiserweight
Champion John O'Riordan. His recent wins over Whiskey Jakk and Damian
Payne have caused the powers that be to look him over again, so don't be surprised if Matt is the next man to come gunning for Sykopath's
Hardcore Championship.
There were plenty more shocking moments at Hostile Intentions, cardinal among them being the return of the Japanese Akita. Taurus came back making his intentions clear, to pay Greed back for trashing his home and training grounds, The Grinder. Later in the night he got into the action, sending Greed packing with the aid of Max Caschera,
Damian Payne and The Mob Squad. Needless to say, it's great to have him back.
As you all know, this week's Battleground was a repeat of last week. Expect a brand new episode next week. Other than that, the big news is the next Supercard, Ascension. In it we'll see the conclusion to the Irvin / Keith feud, as a undisputed
BSCW World Champion will be crowned in the main event, a Ladder Match.
Both the World Championship that is held by the reigning champion
Justin Keith and the Shane Delaney memorial championship currently held by "Deadly" Derek Irvin will both be hanging high above the ring.
Elsewhere on the card, it's debuts galore, as The Reaper, Blake Storm and Todd Royal will make their Supercard debuts at Ascension. Also, the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament will continue, as we will find out who will compete in the final match of the torunament, Tyson
Bryson, Joker, Dan Owens or Shrapnel. As an added bonus we'll have a battle of the big tough mofo's as Whiskey Jakk and Richard "The Truck"
Harris tear it up. The supercard after Ascension is tentatively titled "The Process of
Weeding Out, and promises to shake things up for BSCW for some time to come.
That's it for now folks. Enjoy the upcoming week's Battleground, and
I'll talk to you via this wonderful invention called the internet shortly after Ascension.
