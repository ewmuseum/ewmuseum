Strange Brew

15 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi folks, this is Ian Finnegan...Welcome to another edition of the drunken ramblings known as "Strange Brew".
Apparently the television re-debut of "From Pillar To Post" starring myself
(And to a lesser extent, Brian Stephens) was the talk of the wrestling world this past week. The reviews have more or less been positive, with the exceptions of a few bruised egos. So, if there are any irate NAWF fans out there remember....Brian Stephens is the one you want.
BSCW is gearing up for another blockbuster Pay Per View event. This time it'll be the second annual "Detonation". Last year we saw the return of Taurus and
Hannibal Carver, as well as the bizarre site of the BSCW Hardcore Champion taking on what I believe was over ten preliminary wrestlers. This year's event should be no different, as there have already been a number of matches signed.
The most obvious signing is the main event. It will pit current champ Justin
Keith against the man who has been tracking him like a savage beast, Taurus.
It has been announced that this will be a "Two Out Of Three Falls Match", but with a twist. Each fall will have a different stipulation. Cage Match, I
Quit, Hell In A Cell...No one knows for sure at this point in time. Regardless, it should be a hell of a contest between these two bitter rivals.
Another highly anticipated matchup will be the Tag Team Title Match. As many have predicted, reigning champs Gemini will face "The New Sensation" Brian
Deegan and "Bad Religion" John O'Riordan, the duo known as the Significant
Brothers. These two have met once before, but that match did not come to a satisfactory conclusion due to the interferance of the Deadly Quartet. The word is that we will not see a repeat of that, as this match will have a No
Interferance stipulation attached. It should be interesting to see how effectively BSCW officials are able to enforce the no interferance ruling.
This year we will see what I believe might be a first for our sport, a Three
Way Iron Man Match. In it, BSCW Cruiserweight Champion Leo will defend his coveted title against the former champ, X-Kalibur, and one of his most hated enemies, Joker. Leo will be looking to get revenge on Joker for attacking him backstage a few weeks back, X-Kalibur will be trying like hell to reclaim his mantle of champion, and Leo will have his hands full getting out of this in one piece. When you combine the lightning quickness of these three competitors, I truly believe you get a "can't miss" proposition.
Obviously, this is only the beginning. Andrew Sampson is slated to defend his
United States Championship, but against who? The smart money is on Million
Dollar Dragon, but for that Andrew will have to succesfully retain his gold against Max C this weekend. A grudge match for the Canadian Title between J.C.
Swinger and Crucifix would appear to be a given, which leaves the Hardcore
Title. Reigning Hardcore champ Vic "Tim Dogg" Simmons has beaten every obstacle in his path since attaining his ten pounds of gold. The questions remains, who's left? Is there really no one that can beat BSCW's reigning king of extreme?
Needless to say, this year's Detonation looks like if anything, it will top last year's offering. There's a whole lot at risk, and even more heated rivalries to be settled. The road to Detonation begins this sunday at
Battleground. It'll be emanating live from The Palace in Hollywood, and I wouldn't advise anyone to miss it. I know I won't. That's all for this week folks. Be sure to tune in to this week's Sunday Night Battleground, and be sure to contact your local cable provider and order Detonation, live from The
Joint in Las Vegas, Nevada, only on Pay Per View.
