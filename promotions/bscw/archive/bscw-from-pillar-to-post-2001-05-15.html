The teaming of Justin Keith, former BSCW World Champion Austin Blacktop, and Memphis Outlaw. Unfortunately, Blacktop's return has been less than stellar. He and Outlaw have has their asses handed to them on a regular basis by everyone but the JwO. I believe that the return of Joker will get the Trio back on track, and help get Blacktop and Outlaw on the road to tag team gold.
Zodiac, on the other hand, are more dominant than ever before. Where before Leo got embarrassed by Boz, Gemini was lost in the shuffle, and Taurus slapped with embarrassing angle after retarded angle, now they are on top of the world.

Cruiserweight Champion, and Taurus looks to be a breath away from becoming
World Champion, and breaking Justin Keith's arms in the process.

Mcgregor and Jack had a great debut, finally putting away Armageddon with an awesome Powerbomb / Neckbreaker combo. McGregor and Jack are off to a good start, it should be interesting to see how they fare as they move up the ladder. Up next was J.C. Swinger beating both his hated nemesis Crucifix and

That match reminded of that time when Vail and I went to that bar, and I was pinned up against the wall by two drunk nuns. It was really just a bad scene.

If and when these two teams get together, I think we will see something we've haven't seen in BSCW for a long time, a match where the opponents actually respect each other. Personally, I would love to see the Outlaws thrown into these two teams, and just wait to see what kind of scraps are left. Have we said enough, what kind of a disappointment the American Outlaws are. Any housen, the next match was the main event, probably one of the best displays of pure athletics we've seen here in BSCW, it put the former champion Andrew Sampson against the arrogant Canadian Million Dollar Dragon. From the opening bell to the end, a pure wrestling clinic was put on.

Add in all the reversals both men put to work, and the several "on the fly" innovative maneuvers showcased...You couldn't ask for anything more out of the main event.
Andrew turning a Flying head Scissors into a Super Facebuster was one of the most amazing feats of strength I've ever seen, and MDD's Springboard 360
Dropkick is an image that won't leave the minds of BSCW fans anytime soon. Brian Stephens: The fans didn't feel as cheated after that match was over, as for the rest of the card. Well, everyone has an off night. But I can guarantee one thing, that on May 27th, will be the much anticipated Pay Per View,
Detonation in the City of Sin, Las Vegas, Nevada. Here is my opinion on what some of the matches should be. J.C. Swinger and Crucifix beating each other to a pulp for the Canadian Championship. I see Darkcyde and Mr. M.A.K. in a " who the hell really cares" match, with the winner doing about as much as the loser, nothing. I see Vic battling Lt. Buzzcut for the Hardcore Belt.

Buzzcut. For the sake of wrestling fans everywhere, I'm demanding that Million

Akita. If this were to happen, I see Justin being dethroned and Zodiac truly ruling the world. Now is the time for the show where we delve into the talk from the locker room, that we like to call Brian's Babble.

newbies. Grinnin and Goo will take on Lt. Buzzcut and the returning Max C. Buzzcut is fresh out of wrestling school, so I'm going to call for experience to rule the day with Grinnin and Goo getting the win, hopefully with the oh so markable Gobsmacker.
Brian Stephens: This will be a match that people will actually want to watch, a first in a long time for the opening matches. I see Grinnin and Goo as well on their way to victory, but I also see Lt. Buzzcut learning the hard way, and will be back for another day. The next match is well a good time to call and order a pizza, call your aunt. I pick Crucifix, Jack, and McGregor, with J.C. Swinger getting his ass beat due to the fact that he has no help from his team mates whatsoever. Up next we have the debut of two new and very unique teams. Vic Simmons will once again enter tag team competition, this time with his fellow Dawg, Nubian Nightmare.
They will face Million Dollar Dragon and his lackey, Emelio. Both teams are untested, so I can't pick. Whoever wins this I believe will get pushed way up the ladder in the tag division, which is severely lacking in serious competition.
Brian Stephens: I have to go with Vic and Nubian Nightmare. Nubian has some experience. Hell, I'd really be surprised to see if any of these teams show up.
They both aren't the most reliable. I see this as yet a chance to stay on the phone with your aunt, just a little bit longer. The next match, is for the
United States Champion, it pits the current champ, Andrew Sampson against the phone in opponent, "Technically Sound" Taylor James. The thing that I have noticed is with Andrew Sampson, if you have anything close to a feud, you disappear. I wonder if he has some type of mob connections. Dionysus, gone.
Taylor James, gone for two weeks. So I see Sampson holding on to the title, and
"Technically Sound" getting cement shoes.

Butte, he's skipped town. Hopefully he's finally found his nutsack, and will show up. If he shows up it'll be a great match. But I don't see that happening, so I'll say Sampson by a landslide. One match where both competitors will actually show up will be Joker versus Taurus. This is the latest stage in the rekindling of the Zodiac / Deadly Trio feud. Joker has been away from regular competition for a while, and I say that he's yet to fully knock off his ring rust. I saw Taurus snaps the little twerp's legs in half, and goes on to kick his buddy Justin's ass at Detonation.

Ian Finnegan: Deegan and O'Riordan have had revenge on their minds ever since the last Pay Per View, when Justin stabbed the Significant Brothers in favor of the quite frankly piss poor American Outlaws. With the addition of Brendan,
Deegan and O'Riordan are more mentally ready than ever. I think O'Riordan and
Deegan Squared win this one, with Brendan hopefully murdering Memphis Outlaw.

Battleground live from the Palace in Los Angeles. For Ian Finnegan, this is
Brian Stephens saying goodnight.
