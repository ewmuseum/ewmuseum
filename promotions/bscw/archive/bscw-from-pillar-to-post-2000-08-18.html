::The BSCW logo flashes on the screen, as Swahili Death Chant by Immortal Alice begins to play. Several scenes of last week' s Tuesday Night
Terror are shown. DA Judge nails Rangrel with a Swinging DDT onto a steel chair, "The
Divine One" Damien Darkstorm nails Destiny with the Darkstorm Driver, "The
Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer nails "The American Bad Ass" Austin Blacktop with the Explosion, and The Rebel puts Palmer through a flaming table with the
Confederate Drop. That scene fades into another as the BSCW television studio, the scene of "From Pillar To Post" is seen. Sitting behind the desk awaiting their cue are Brian Stephens and Richard Vail.::
Richard Vail: Good evening folks, and welcome to another edition of From Pillar
To Post. I'm Richard Vail, and joining me once again is my colleague, Brian
Stephens.
Brian Stephens: Good evening, the most talked about story is the return of the
Rebel. His return marks the end of Tim Palmer's time as an overachiever.
Richard Vail: Only time will tell on that, but it definitely marks the end of
Tim Palmer's reign as a flame retardant wrestler. Oddly, Tim has decided to focus his efforts on Boz. I have to say, if someone put me through a flaming table, I'd be thinking revenge. The events of this past week have turned BSCW into total chaos. Why did The Rebel do what he did? Why did Darkstorm help him attack Palmer? Why did "The Night Stalker" Terrance Levy attack Darkstorm? And where does the world champion fit into all of this?
Brian Stephens: Maybe more importantly, how does Boz fit into this equation? Is this some kind of master plan of his to send BSCW into a complete tailspin, and when the dust settles, come out with the World's belt. One thing does puzzle me, Who is this Beautiful Brandon that Boz keeps referring to? A ghost from the past of "The Ticking Time Bomb" or just a really badly named wrestler.
Richard Vail: That is very odd. He sounds like someone that former BSCW star
Deathcow would be interested in meeting. I think the real question is if Palmer wants Boz that bad, or is he just that scared of The Rebel. The last we saw of
The Rebel, he was fighting Boz and former BSCW star Leo for the World Title.
Where has he been, and more importantly, what is his reason for coming back when he did? Apparently the friendship between Blacktop and Rebel is much more close than we were previously lead to believe. One thing is certain, when The
Rebel meets Palmer in a one-on-one encounter, it could mean the end of "The
Ticking Time Bomb".
Brian Stephens: Well, Palmer has bitten off more than he can chew. Speaking of that, have you noticed that you and Hank the peanut salesguy are the only two that haven't been called out by the "Divine One" Damien Darkstorm. Does this guy think he can take on the entire BSCW by himself? Hey, now that's an
idea for a match Darkstorm vs Everyone else at Detonation. Richard Vail: And the fact that he handed you your balls in a sling would have nothing to do with that, right?
::Stephens begins to whistle and look around the studio.:: Richard Vail: But you do have a valid point there. That young man could be headed for some very bad trouble if he isn't careful. He's already incurred the wrath of "The Ticking Time Bomb" Tim Palmer, Boz, "The Night Stalker" Terrance
Levy, and of course, his opponent at Detonation, reigning U.S. Champion
Philippe Lafleur. We of course saw how Darkstorm cost Lafleur his World Title
Tournament Semi-Final match with Blacktop a short time ago. I'm sure Philippe has some revenge in mind come the twenty-seventh.
Brian Stephens: Darkstorm needs to find focus, does he want to become the next
U.S. Champion, or does he want to take out Hank, the retarded inflated hammer
salesperson. Darkstorm, one thing or the other. U.S. Championship, or being an annoying pain in the as...err I mean butt to all that step into a ring. Hey, Mahoney's held a title, call him out. RIchard Vail: Uhh...Darkstorm's first BSCW match WAS with Mahoney, genius. Brian Stephens: Just making sure you' re awake. Philippe Lafleur has impressed many here in BSCW including the likes of Blacktop, and Boz. Is Lafleur riding the wave of momentum, or just one lucky son of a bitch? Richard Vail: Well, you don't get "The American Bad Ass" on the verge of tapping out, or beating Tim Palmer by submission on luck alone. Lafleur is a definite talent, and anyone that takes him for granted because of his goofy ways is in for a big surprise. That would be Darkstorm's biggest mistake at
Detonation, to forget that when he has his game face on, Lafleur is a very dangerous man.
Brian Stephens: We'll see what will happen with Terrance Levy on Tuesday. Will
Darkstorm look past this matchup, and possibly lose any momentum he's gained over the past couple of weeks. Levy is a tough competitor, and will give
Darkstorm a run for it on Tuesday. Richard Vail: Something else Darkstorm will have to concern himself with is that there will be some unfriendly faces at ringside on Tuesday. Boz and
Palmer, while not friends, both have scores to settle with "The Divine One". If he's not careful, Darkstorm might find himself watching Detonation from a hospital bed. That brings up another interesting possibility...Will Boz and
Palmer just forego the setting of the Lumberjack Match and go at it tooth and nail? They will meet each other earlier in the evening in a Last Man Standing
Match with the number one contendership for the BSCW World Title on the line.
But will that be enough, will they just turn the Weapons Lumberjack Match into a street brawl?
Brian Stephens: Sure hope so. This Last Man Standing Match will be one of the best matches of the year, even though Boz is involved. In my opinion, they both can beat each other senseless, some may say 'Too late'. Personally, I want to see Mahoney in the World Title Match at Detonation....hmmm....
Richard Vail: Hey, now that's an idea. That'd be a great match...if we were allergic to ratings. We've seen Palmer versus Blacktop, and while it was a hell of a match, I'd be lying if Boz versus Blacktop doesn't have me seeing dollar signs. And with the presence of The Rebel, Palmer, and this mysterious rafter-dweller, who knows what could happen. Blacktop has been uncharacteristically quiet. I wonder what he has to say about all of this. I'd have to say his status as champion is in serious jeapordy for the first time ever.
Brian Stephens: Strong words from the "I Like Blacktop" Vice President in charge of cardboard cut-outs. Blacktop will continue his reign as champion for a long time. Boz has factors like Darkstorm,and everyone else right down to Randy Mulkey, can't stand him and wouldn't piss on Boz if he wasn't on fire.
Boz doesn't have too many friends in the backstage area. Richard Vail: Yes, but if you remember, he won the BSCW World Title before all by himself. If he can do it once, I say there's the chance that he can make it happen again. But of course, he has to gert past the Time Bomb first.
Brian Stephens: Boz has never faced anyone like the powerhouse known as Palmer
(laughs) Richard Vail: ::snickers:: That was a good one. But seriously, Palmer did pull of an upset against Blacktop, it is conceivably possible that he could do the same to Boz. One odd thing I've been noticing is Boz actually throwing a compliment or two in the way of Philippe Lafleur and the man known as "The
Japanese Akita". Could we be seeing a potential teaming?
Brian Stephens: As long as the eclairs keep coming, I could see anything. Philippe and Boz, that's an odd concept. Hey that would be a good match at
Detonation. Whatcha think Richard?
Richard Vail: I think your booking skills are on par with Kevin Nash. Which is to say, they are virtually nonexistant. What should be a good match, or at least an interesting one, is the Tag Team Title Match at Detonation. Westside
Express will have to put their gold on the line against either the team of "The
Night Stalker" and Bonz, or "The Ticking Time Bomb" and Jabroni Mahoney. Two very odd teams, to say the least.
Brian Stephens: If I didn't know any better, I'd think you're a mark for
Palmer. ::Vail slaps Stephens across the face.:: Richard Vail: Watch your mouth! This is a family show god damn you!!! Brian Stephens: Okay, I was just saying that Palmer could be involved in most of the matches at the pay per view, if everything goes well for him. I still can't believe he turned on Blacktop. Blacktop made him what he is today...... ::Vail slaps Stephens across the face again.:: Richard Vail: It's none of my business if you want to ask the world champ out on a date, but do it on your own time. What is truly puzzling about the entire scenario is Palmer ridiculously claiming that 81st Street is as strong as ever.
If he was such good buddies with Blacktop, would he have creamed his pants at the thought of a title shot like he did? I think not. I'd have to agree with
Boz's estimation, 81st Street is dead thanks to the ego of "The Ticking Time
Bomb".
Brian Stephens: Geez, you would think this show was called 'The We really don't know what Palmer is thinking show'. He had the entire world at his fingertips but let it alll go all because of one little blowup doll dressed up in his likeness. I heard Philippe has taken it home, and well, christened it.
Disturbing thought, but not as disturbing was what was going through the mind of Slim Anus this Tuesday at the hands of Just....The "Real" Slim Shady.
Richard Vail: And the thing going throughthe mind of Slim Anus was of course, toilet water. Even more disturbing that that is the fact that Slim Anus is a hired lackey sent by Violent M. Even more disturbing is that Violent M sent someone not to beat up Shady, not to put him out of action, but to anally rape him.
Brian Stephens: I thought I saw Shady slip the midget his number. Not sure though, you've worked with M for a while, what is his thing with the dwarves?
A fantasy he's never quite lived out. Hey that could be a match, Slim Shady versus Slim Anus and the entire cast of the Wizard of Oz in over the middle rope battle royal.
Richard Vail: I don't know what to say. Oh wait, yes I do. ::Vail slaps Stephens across the face yet again. Stephens has a hand-shaped red mark on his face by now, and looks quite dizzy.::
Brian Stephens: (in a confused state) I LIKE MONKEYS! Richard Vail: ::sigh:: Enough with the WCW-esque booking. Now ladies and gents,
I bring to you a close to blacking out Brian Stephens with Brian's Babble.
Brian Stephens: Rumblings and rumors from the front office to the backstage area, it appears that many faces from BSCW's past will be making returns.
Some welcomed, others the jury is still out about. Philippe Lafleur has published a cook book entitled "How To Keep Your Heel
Happy" it contains many desserts included puntang pie, knuckle sandwich, and creme of whoop ass.
Palmer has been sent in to take care of the wildfires, since he can kill heat with the mention of a single word.
Darkstorm has called out and beaten Bea Arthur, to become Blanche on the revised version of the Golden Girls.
There has been a rumor that the "Real" Slim Shady might be in fact Dark Holy
Matt, but that's Just In Possible.
In late breaking news, pWn has found a sucking partner. It is a promotion known as Extreme World Order.
That is all for now, right now let's look at the matches for next Tuesday, first up is the surprising Rangrel, versus the Original Gangsta, (turns to
Vail) when did we sign
Ice-T? Richard Vail: Well, after his many failed television and movie projects, I'm glad to see him get back on his feet. My pcik here i Rangrel. He's on a baffling winning streak as of late, and I think that'll continue on Tuesday.
Brian Stephens: I think Rangrel will win as well, because the way he wins is by psyching out his opponents by using Kane's music. His opponents shit themselves, making them less mobile, and an easy target for Rangrel. The next match is the tag team match to settle who meets up with Westside Express, from
Florida, at Detonation. I feel that Mahoney is gonna be the main force in this match and carry Palmer to the realm of tag team champion.
Richard Vail: Usually I would go with the double tough levy, with his experience in pit fighting. But seeing as how Palmer is going into battle with a former champ, I too am looking for Palmer and Mahoney to go to the PPV as contenders for tag gold. Up next is Citizen-X taking on The "Real" Slim Shady.
If Citizen-X wins, he gets a Hardcore Title shot at Detonation. Citizen-X has not had his mind on business for quite some time, and I think the distraction of his newfound money will lead him to defeat here.
Brian Stephens: How much exactly are you paying X? Has the IRS looked into his money dealings with his friends from the Westside of Miami. My opinion,
I'm going for the midget in this one. That's entertainment. BRING ON THE
DWARVES! Richard Vail: Yes, they are pretty damn funny. They are, after all, God's practical joke. Up next we have Boz going up against "The Ticking Time Bomb"
Tim Palmer in a Last Man Standing Match. Usually I'd go with the more seasoned
Boz, but Palmer did make mention of bringing some help with him to the ring, so that might be the downfall of Boz right there.
Brian Stephens: Judy Bagwell? Judy Bagwell will be at BSCW, former WCW tag team champ, what a hottie. As much as I can't stand Boz, he will be without a bag of tricks in this last man standing match. Boz over Palmer, because Palmer will also be looking over his shoulder for Blacktop, Rebel, and rubber sex dolls. The main event will be the "Night Stalker" Terance Levy versus Damien
Darkstorm in a Weapons Lumberjack, I see Darkstorm getting into it with a ring attendant, camera man and the guy selling cotton candy, even before the match starts, so I say Levy will pull this one out.
Richard Vail: I'd have to agree with you there. Aside from Ralph The Ring Guy,
Darkstorm has to contend with Palmer, Boz, and possibly Vacant. Levy, on the other hand, has only Damien to contend with.Darkstorm will be too preoccupied, and as a result will get hit with the Death Warrant for the loss.
