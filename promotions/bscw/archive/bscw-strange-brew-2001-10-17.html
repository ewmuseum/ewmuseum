BSCW — News

Strange Brew

17 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another month, another edition of Strange Brew. Let's get into it. First off, Bloody Hell. What can I say but wow? Lots of surprises, including an impromptu Contendership Match between "Deadly" Derek
Irvin and BSCW World Champion Justin Keith. How it came to be such is being called one of the most despicable acts in BSCW history. A commemoration ceremony was taking place, where BSCW President Richard
Vail was going to be handing special Hall of Fame awards to Justin
Keith. One for Keith himself, and a posthumous award for Keith's deceased brother, former BSCW great The "Franchise" Shane Delaney. On top of that, Justin was to be presented with a commemorative World
Championship belt with Shane's name inscribed on it. This ceremony was then disrupted by Irvin, who virtually spat on Delaney's grave by calling him "Some wrestler from the past who no one cares about". This enraged Justin, but before he could do much about it, he was blindsided by Irvin, who stole the belt with Shane's name inscribed on it! Irvin challenged Justin to make their main event match a
Contendership Match, and Justin was only too eager to oblige. Later that night, after a grueling battle, Irvin won... By knocking Justin silly with his own brother's belt! Richard Vail then decreed that
Irvin would get his shot, along with six other men in a Thunderdome
Cage Match. The stipulation being if one man survived at the end, he would be crowned as BSCW World Champion. Regardless of that, Irvin might very well have cemented his place as the most hated man in BSCW history.
Elsewhere on the card, "The Icon Killer" Matt O'Riordan and Dan Owens had their first ever pinfall victories since their debut here in BSCW.
O'Riordan scored a huge upset over Whiskey Jakk, cementing, or so it seems, a spot in Greed. Of course, Greed as a whole has been very tight-lipped as to whether O'Riordan will get in even after making the monstrous Jakk tap out. Dan Owens, on the other hand, moved forward in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament by besting former co-holder of the BSCW Tag Team Championships, "King of Submission" Quinn
Charbonneau. Speaking of Dan, I spoke with him earlier tonight about that win and many other topics. The following is the transcript of that interview.
Ian Finnegan: First off, thanks for taking the time to sit down for this interview, Dan. Dan: No Problem. I have nothing else to do but kick butt and win titles. Ian Finnegan: Well I suppose the first thing I should do is congratulate you on your win at Bloody Hell. How does it feel to not only get your first BSCW victory, but to continue on in the
Cruiserweight Tournament? Dan: Thanks, Ian. I knew that my win was coming. All I had to do was get my stuff together and focus on what I want and who I'm after. Now
I am making some progress in BSCW by advancing on in the tournament.
You just watch, I'll be the next Cruiserweight champ before long. Ian Finnegan: Speaking of which, any thoughts on the man you'll face in the Semi-Finals, Shrapnel?
Dan: Well Shrapnel... I have only met him once and he is as fake as a three dollar bill. He acts big and bad with his trench coat and piercings, but once you get in his face, he will back down. I think that he will try to put up a fight, even though it is certain now that
I will be the next Cruiserweight champ.
Ian Finnegan: Certainly you can't doubt his toughness after seeing his
Helter Skelter Match with Sykopath?
Dan: Yeah that was interesting but... Let me see... Oh yeah, he lost! Ian Finnegan:Changing the subject, what's your reaction to being
Confederate Dropped by The Rebel, immediately after it seemed that
Hell On Earth had come to congratulate you on your win?
Dan: Well I think that Shrapnel isn't the only one around here with a screw loose. I'll admit that I do admire Hell On Earth's work, but it's either they're with me or against me. There are no neutral parties when it comes to me. Right now I'm flying solo and I like it.
I guess Hell On Earth just don't know what they're missing. Ian Finnegan: Having said that, would you like to get either Blacktop or The Rebel in the ring?
Dan: Boy you really can't hear good, you need to clean the shit out of your ears and listen. I said that all these guys in the BSCW are either with me or against me. So yes they proved that they are not on my side so I would like to see them on the other side of the ring soon.
Ian Finnegan: Of anyone else in BSCW, is there anyone or any number of people in particular you'd like to take on?
Dan: Well, right now I will take on all comers, but yeah there are a few I would like to get my hands on. First off Hell On Earth did something that nobody will ever do and get away with, and that's attack me. Then, I would really like to get this Sykopath guy in the ring and see how "Sykotik" he can be. Other than them, anyone with gold around their waist is on my list.
Ian Finnegan: If memory serves, you faced Sykopath in a Ladder Match for the Hardcore Championship some time ago. Having faced him already, is there anything you'd do differently if given the chance?
Dan Owens: Yes, you're right but when I first faced him, I was inexperienced and didn't know what I was doing. Since then, he has gotten an attitude that he thinks nobody can break him. Well, I have some time under my belt and some experience. I think that I could take, no KNOCK him off his little power trip if I was given the chance.
Ian Finnegan: Finally, what do you see in the immediate future for yourself? Dan Owens: I see gold very soon. I see myself stirring up some trouble with certain people and maybe putting some people in the retirement home early. Ultimately, I think that in the next couple of weeks, I am going to have some real fun.
Ian Finnegan: Well, thanks for taking time out of your busy schedule, and good luck at Hostile Intentions.
Dan Owens: Thanks, but I won't need the luck. Send your prayers to
Shrapnel. Well, there you have it. A very confident Dan Owens. Needless to say,
Shrapnel also scored a win in a bit of a mild upset over Ninja, who has parted ways with BSCW for unknown reasons. Shrapnel looks to be headed into a confrontation with Boz, who got involved physically in
Shrapnel's match with Ninja.
Finally, in what was a simply spectacular match, Sykopath put North
American Champion Matt Wilson down for the count. After the match, Max
C made a surprise return to BSCW, kicking Wilson's ass all the way out of the ring, setting up a North American title shot for himself at
Hostile Intentions.
In other news, a big signing has taken place as IWF/WOW and MLW star
"Dangerous" William Devonshire has signed with the company. rust me,
I've seen this man's work, and the upper card of BSCW has plenty to be worried about.
In other signing news, Jaji, an unknown who reportedly has performed all over the world won an underground tourney to win a BSCW contract.
"The Awesome One" a former EWA World Champion, has signed on with
BSCW. EWA may be familiar with fans that remember former BSCW Canadian
Champion J.C. Swinger's tenure here, as he was from the very same promotion. Finally, journeyman wrestler "The Innovator of Insanity"
Damian Payne has been given a contract, after some glowing praise by
Max C, who has teamed with and fought against Payne many times in the past.
Folks, that's all we have for now. I'll be back again in a few weeks to review Hostile Intentions as well as go over everything that's going on here in BSCW. Before I leave, I want to remind anyone that you can still order copies of Ragnarok and/or Bloody Hell by contacting
