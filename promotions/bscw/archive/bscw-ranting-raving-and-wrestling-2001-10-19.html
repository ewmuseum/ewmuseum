Ranting, Raving and Wrestling

19 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ahoy, hoy, BSCW fans! I'm Damon "the Glove" Gleave, and you're reading my first column here for BSCW. Richard Vail recently hired me to give you the fans some thoughts, wit, and award winning writing to indulge in during those "long" two week periods without BSCW wrestlers giving their hearts and souls for you people. You're all slave drivers, you know that? But let "Gleaver 2000" get on with his first column…
We'll start off with this Sunday's big show, "Bloody Hell." It took place from the Showplace Theater in Buffalo, New York, and results are already at BSCW.com. The tape should be available sooner than later.
For those of you that haven't caught up in what's happening, let's the show, shall we? Our first match was Joker vs. Quincy Lacroix in the first of four Cruiserweight title tournament matches. It was pretty short and sweet, but at least and some good energy. Joker cut a big prematch promo to get himself over as a heel, which is strange because his pal Justin
Keith is getting popped until arenas explode… could we see a confrontation soon? Anyways, Joker won this one with the "Nothing to
Joke About." Moving on…
Matt O'Riordan shocked the world with his first real victory here in
BSCW. This guy was almost becoming Jimmy the Homeless Guy until this match. He and Whiskey Jakk had a GREAT match, one of the surprises on the night. The story of the match had Matt working over Whiskey's left leg and ankle while Whiskey overpowered O'Riordan. Matt won after reversing the Bamboozled into an ankle lock, and actually got Whiskey to tap for the second time in the match. Good match, and great booking that shows the win as for real, not a fluke. I thought for sure
Whiskey would win after seeing the ref miss the first submission, but
I was wrong. A real pleasant surprise of a match, both guys worked hard.
The third match was Tyson Bryson versus Matt James, the battle of
Debonair. You had to know to expect something different than an ordinary match, and the story of the match had Matt and Tyson
"wrestling" a terrible match until Richard Vail threatened their tag team belts. After that a pretty good but short match resulted, in which each guy then "tried". Get it? Funny start, and the match after that was okay, but compared to the rest of the stuff on the night, it wasn't as good. These guys are better in tag team competition.
Next up we got an awesome angle as Richard Vail presents Justin Keith an honorary world title belt in honor of his late brother Shane
Delaney… only to be jumped by "Deadly" Derek Irvin and Greed. DDI steals the belt, and we've got a real feud on our hands now, folks. Nice segment to further the main feud in BSCW right now.
The fourth match of the night was the third in the tournament. Quinn
Charbonneau of the Q's took on Dan Owens in a battle of "Kings". This match was exciting and fast paced, with solid mat work all around. It says a lot that neither of these guys needed tables, ladders, or chairs to keep the crowd in the match. The wrestling was fast paced and intense, not always seen in a battle of submission moves. I thought the end was going to be when Charbonneau reversed a monkey flip into the Alaskan Crab, but Rebel helped Owens to the ropes. Owens soon applied the Constrictor to get the win. I love that move! After the match, Rebel turned on Owens in a "What the hell?" moment. The Q's were 0-2 on the night, but who expected the tag teams to win?
The last quarterfinal match pitted Shrapnel against Ninja. These guys wrestled an intense match that featured lots of high flying action and brawling outside the ring. Late in the match, Shrapnel tossed about 6 chairs into the ring to use on Ninja. A piledriver on the chairs followed. Later, Ninja super duper plexed (both guys on the top rope)
Shrapnel onto the six chairs, but Shrapnel got a facejam on a powerbomb attempt, and followed with the Shrapnel Spike to move on.
Nice match, although I don't know if all the chairs were really needed. It was still kind of cool, though.
Right before the main event we just had one other match… oh yeah, just between the BSCW North American Champ Matt Wilson and the undefeated Hardcore champ Sykopath…no big deal, right? Yeah, whatever. This match rocked the house, as these guys pulled out everything in the bag to pick up a victory. I really liked the style contrast, with Wilson's technical game versus
Skyopath's…well…what it is that he does. Sykopath showed that he could wrestle, too, but an awesome Mental Illness followed by the Sykotik DDT put Wilson away. I really think Sykopath carried this match and should be in line for a world title push in the next few months. After the match, Wilson was jumped by Max C, who is returning to BSCW for good! I'm a big Max C fan, and I see him taking the North
American title from Wilson at Hostile Intentions, but many people think that, and Wilson is really going to want to silence his critics.
The last match of the night was that little grudge match between
Justin Keith and "Deadly" Derek Irvin. Great stuff here, as Keith jumps Irvin on the moving cart and tosses him down off the ramp to the electrical equipment below to start the match. This match did one thing well, and that's make this feud that much more heated. Am I going to want to pay to see Irvin and Keith go at it again? You're damn right! For his case, Irvin put Keith through the announce table with a top rope elbow drop that was insane. Keith superplexed Irvin from the top to the floor, in another sick spot. But it wasn't just a spotfest, as both guys wrestled well and worked very hard. This feud is just sizzling now, with DDI going by the "Deadly Franchise" now, and becoming more hated than Kathie Lee Gifford.
Next, I've got some random news and notes for you! The injured Goo is apparently looking into a possible announcing or interviewing career. And you thought BSCW didn't hire fat people!
Verbal Beatdown is about to undergo a change, as it will now be called
Sunday Night Battleground and feature promos and matches now. Good stuff.
Matt O'Riordan really has impressed BSCW officials with his good attitude and hard work ethic.
Getting Max C. back for good is a big boost for BSCW, and we hear the big man is in for a big push.
Are the Q's in the BSCW doghouse? They both jobbed at Bloody Hell, and one has to wonder how they will fare at Hostile Intentions…
Speaking of H.I., it's shaping up to be an excellent show. We've got
Matt O'Riordan versus three new guys in the opener in a match where every guy can show his stuff to a crowd that hasn't seen it before.
Hopefully these guys come up big. Wilson vs. Max should be great, as these two have really worked at their feud. The "End of the Road' match between Boz and Rebel is one I'm REALLY looking forward to, and shows you why BSCW is a step ahead of every other federation out there. I hear Ninja is gone, so we'll see what effect having Blacktop in his corner will do for Rebel. Remember Boz left Ragnarok on a stretcher… Then we've got the Taipei Death Match, a Richard Vail favorite, for the Hardcore title. I don't really know why Whiskey Jakk is in this match, as Sykopath and Shrapnel had a fine feud going by themselves, so maybe when nobody is expecting it (after a loss to
O'Riordan), Jakk will sneak off with the Hardcore title? Okay,
Sykopath will probably stay undefeated, it was just a thought… Finally, we've got the 8-man Thunderdome cage match. I don't even know where to begin predicting this one. If one man is left, he is the world champ, and if 2 men survive, they become tag champs. But what if
3 or 4 men are left? I don't know. I don't know what'll happen in this one, but I think it'll come down to Keith and Irvin, one would have to think so. I don't think we'll end up with new tag champs, because I don't see the Q's going over DDI and Debonair and Jakk, although maybe a team like Keith and Harris could win them? And if anyone wins the world title, it'll be Irvin. It doesn't make sense any other way. Lots of things could happen, and expect many surprises at Hostile
Intentions. In the future, look for the Irvin and Keith feud to sizzle just a little while longer before we move into new programs. I see Debonair feuding with Hell on Earth down the road, too, but that's just a guess. And the next event after Hostile Intentions will be titled
"Ascension." That should take place around mid-November. We'll also get a new Cruiserweight champ in November, with the semi's at
Ascension and the finals at the next show. My guess for the next champ? The one, the only… Joker! Why not restart the BSCW
Cruiserweight division with the first ever BSCW Cruiserweight champ? That's it for the Glove in this first edition of RR&W, I'll catch up with you guys with more news and thoughts after Hostile Intentions.
Make sure to check out the show, and I'll see you guys later! ----------------------------------------- Rich E-Fed Head / Master Of All That I Survey BSCW - http://efeduniverse.com/bscw
