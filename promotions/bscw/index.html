Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Blood, Sweat & Chairs Wrestling survives in one served document, a comeback angle posted 1 May 2012, opening on owner Rich Vail and hanger-on Ian Finnegan driving out to recruit two of its own former stars, hardcore champions Vic “Tim Dogg” Simmons and Bruce “Jabroni” Mahoney, from a lumber yard where both now work.

The pitch turns out to be a reunion in name only. Vail wants neither man back in the ring — the Hardcore Championship, he tells them, has been “dead as disco” since their run — and instead offers Simmons a job as a senior referee and Mahoney a spot as a backstage interviewer, over Mahoney's own protest: “I held gold and now you want me to be a zebra?”

Both men accept, the scene closing on Vail's own line, “I knew I could count on you guys,” as the three drive off. The dialogue itself places the promotion's earlier run more than a decade before this reunion, and the roleplay was circulated through its own Yahoo Group mailing list.

What that revival became, or whether it ran another card, isn't in its surviving record.
