Taurus: I would have been out sooner, but the management team wanted to talk to me about a couple of personal appearance they have scheduled for the upcoming week. I'm barely going to have enough time to sleep. What do the three of you have planned?
Gemini I: After we get ourselves settled in the hotel, I figure I'm going to hit a couple of the buffets, and try my luck at the roulette wheels.
Gemini II: Yeah, I hear one of the hotels has an all you can eat shrimp buffet for under ten dollars, I'm there. As for the rest of my leisure time, I might go to check out a show or two. Not big on the gambling, we don't make enough as it is anyway. To throw it away just isn't me. Maybe I've been spending too much time with you, Tim.
Taurus: How about Rick? After all it is the Las Vegas, the City of Sin. If I didn't know any better, I would think it was custom built for you.
Leo: Did you almost make a joke? Cool. Unpack my bags, catch a little bit of shut-eye, then its off to the showgirls. I figure I can find at least two or three to spend some quality time with, if you know what I mean?
Taurus: Have any of you looked at the gyms there? I mean, we each have career matches next week. To take any of the opponents lightly. I might even fly back to Boston and go the Grinder for a little special training. Something I know I won't find out here. So, if any of you are interested, just let me know, I might fly out on Wednesday and come back on Saturday afternoon, just in time for the weigh-in.
Gemini II: Weigh-in? What the hell is that about? Taurus: They thought it'd be a good idea to hype the main event a bit more. The new guys in creative have some decent ideas, let's just hope they can pull it off without making us like like, well, assholes. [Looking around.] So where is the rental car, and who's driving first, we have about an eight hour drive ahead of us.
Leo: Well, my parents, who were so pleased at what I've done, have chartered a bus for the four of us. Tim baby, we're going to Las Vegas in style. We're going like rock stars. Booze, loud music, women, whatever you want on the bus right at your fingertips.
Taurus: How about some sleep? After all, I did have one hell of a match tonight. And besides, after what we went through trying to get that damn
Quartet handcuffed, well was pretty tiring. Gemini II: Did you see the look in Blacktop's eyes when the handcuff went snap. That was priceless. So did you know about Blacktop and Justin?
Taurus: No, but who really cares. Come next Sunday, each member of Zodiac will be holding a piece of gold. We will be staking our claim as one of the most dominating stables ever in BSCW.
Gemini I: C'mon quit the jawing and let's get on the bus. There's a buffet with my name on it.
[The four of head toward the bus. The name plate switches from "Journey" to "Sin City, Sturgis Style." The scene slowly fades out as the bus pulls out and drives east down a street.]
[The scene fades in to find the bus traveling through the desert between California and Nevada, which state no one is really sure. Loud snores are heard coming three of the bunks. Seen sitting looking out a window is Taurus.
He is obviously too wound up to sleep. The camera comes in closer to a close up of Taurus. We hear his interior dialogue, as though he himself is narrating.]
Taurus: Mother, father, I stand at a threshold, soon and very soon I will have my chance at making you truly proud of me. As you look down on me, and see that I haven't always made the right decisions. I know after Detonation, both of you will be smiling that your only boy as made good of his life. I will no longer hang my head in shame, for all that I've done, but now can hold my head up and look in the mirror and respect the man I see staring at me from the other side...
[Motion is heard from one of the bunks, and Leo comes strolling out. Wearing only his boxer shorts, he scratches his ass, and sees Taurus sitting and just watching the world go by. Leo puts his hand on his friend's shoulder. Leo has a puzzled look on his face.]
Leo: Can't sleep either? I've been tossing and turning for about a half hour. I've been thinking alot. I mean about a bunch of things. Taurus: Should I ask? Is this about where you're going to go to find a girl? Don't they just come to the room. Leo: No, I mean about serious things. All my life, I've been able to skate through on my charms and looks alone. Very rarely having to work for anything.
Then there's you. I mean you not the most personable guy on the planet, and quite frankly with all the scars on your body, you're a slight displeasing to the eye. But through dedication and perservance, you are about ready to step up and become something that others, including myself, have only dreamed about.
I've just been thinking, what is going to happen to me, if I pull a ligament in my knee or get into a disfiguring accident, what exactly will I do? I have no real skills other than being a con artist or a telemarketer.
Taurus: I guess Rick, that is something you really need to think about, because once father time gets his hands on you, the looks will fade and so will the athletic abilities. And the last thing you want to do is look ridiculous in the squared circle, or be old enough to someone's grandfather.
Leo: I enjoy this, don't get me wrong, but I know there is more to life than wrestling, I just hope I know before it's too late. Thanks Tim for listening.
See you in the morning. Taurus: G'night, Rick. [As Leo heads back to the bunk. Taurus stares back into the desert night. His minds drifts away once again. He once again, narrates his inner thoughts.]
Taurus: In seven days, I can attain a dream. Did I ever see myself becoming a World Champion, let alone the federation that gave me my chance back in the states. Did Hannibal Carver, my mentor, my father figure for so long, did he ever think that his star pupil would become the best. I think from the very first days I met him, he was grooming me into something better than a Japanese pit fighter. For that I am truly thankful.
To my sensei back at the wrestling dojo. Taking a special interest in me, being I was more a less a man without a country. He took me under his wing, and treated me like his son. The look in his eyes when I told him that I was leaving for America. He told me do what you do to the best of your ability and he said he would be proud of me. Well, master, I hope you are watching and I hope I can make you proud.
To Leo, and both Gemini I and II, the rest that round out my "family". For all of the trials and tribulations. From the internal struggles to everyday squabbles, I am sure glad that the four of us can work it out and look at each other for support. After all isn't that what being a family is all about.
This really isn't only a win for me, but a win for the four of us proving all of the critics wrong.
[Taurus turns away from the window, and heads toward his bunk. He looks one more time into the desert night. The scene slowly fades out as Taurus crawls into bed.]
