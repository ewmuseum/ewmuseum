[points to the sky] my Mom and dad. [Holds up the belt] This is for you. I hope your little boy is making you proud.
For all the new fans of BSCW, what you will see is just the creme de creme of BSCW's past and present. I'm not talking about of out of shape old man making a mockery out of what they used to be, (see WCW)
but six men still in the prime of their career still able to make an impact in BSCW. And a couple of words for Andrew Sampson, it's a shame that BSCW has truly lost a talent like you, but sometimes we all need to be reminded of things that are a little more important than wrestling. I know I speak for the rest of Zodiac, when I say our prayers are with you.
Now is the time for some training, I don't want to become like the others and go soft after I've won the belt. I still have to prove to myself everyday that I truly deserve the title "World Champion." I will not become one that rests on his laurels, and starts to concern himself with getting his face on a nutrition bar, or becoming a spokesman for a long distance telephone company. I am a wrestler first and foremost, and I will not lose focus on where I've come from to get where I am today. I will not forget the people that helped me get me here today.
That is the problem, once someone gets to the top, the people that helped are quickly replaced by people that only want one thing. And once that one thing is gone, so are the people, and the only thing that person has is loneliness.
[Taurus walks over to a small door, and opens it. The small door appears to be a closet where Taurus keeps his first aid items. He rifles through to make sure he has everything. A knock is heard, and
Taurus yells "It's open." Minutes later a small group of men, equipped with buckets and mops talk to Taurus. He points to a door in the corner. As the scene fades out they proceed to the door as Taurus continues to check the stock of his medical closet.]
======================================================================= [The scene opens up to a House Show, and the crowd is certainly energized after the match between two of BSCW up-and-comers.
Suddenly, the lights go down and "California Uber Alles" by Disposable
Heroes of Hiphoprosy comes over the P.A. With a microphone in his hand the current Cruiserweight Champion, Leo appears at the entryway.
The crowds reaction is mixed. Boos and cheers can be heard throughout the arena. Leo makes it down to the ring, and the crowd wait to hear what the Cruiserweight Champion has to say.]
Leo: Well, I guess I'm the best thing that has ever rolled into this flea bitten town, since Boston did the State Fair last year. I know that mere thrill of having me here in, where the hell am I again? Oh nevermind.
On Sunday, Zodiac will once again show their dominance as we go against the teams of Asshole and Clown, and Hell on Earth. One side we have a former champion and an idiot that just couldn't get the job done against me. Well Justin and Joker, I hope the two of you are comfortable being number two in your respective title hunts. Simply because, as both Taurus and I hold the gold, you will never, ever be good enough to beat us. Maybe in the golden age of BSCW when you and
Shane romped through the Pacific Northwest hand in hand. This is no longer the BSCW that you answers to you. Now everyone answers to
Zodiac.
And I look at the Rebel and Austin Blacktop. I look at the two of you and all I can think is what ever happen to King Kong Bundy and Koko B.
Ware. Your better days have past you, but either one of you just haven't realized it yet. Sure both of you have been former
Heavyweight Champions, but allow me to stress the word "former". In other words, someone has beaten you. However, if you notice that both
Taurus and I are current champions, which means no one has beaten us for these belts.
I know that none of the belts are up for grabs, but Taurus and I know that we need to be at one hundred percent. The last thing we need to do is lose and have someone will have some type of false hope and believe that they can beat either one of us.
You might be wondering where my tag team partner Taurus is? Well, he has decided to return to Boston and get in some training. While I decided to stay here on the left coast. He and I will be meeting soon, and be talking strategy. So don't you worry all of you Zodiac fans, we will be at full strength. Just because we're not side by side, doesn't mean we won't be a team come Sunday.
Now a word for some of the others in the Cruiserweight Division looking to dethrone the best looking and working wrestler in the BSCW, me. If I were you I would reconsider, until you all are "truly" ready enough. I will let you know when you are, so until then, you can just jump around and fall on each other. Once anyone of you get even half the timing and coordination that I possess, I might be able to carry you through a match, but I am not a miracle worker. So to Deadly,
Joker, the disappointing X-Kalibur, and however else decides to rear his ugly head, I will remain the Cruiserweight Champ, until I decide to give it up to someone.
Come Sunday, Taurus and Leo will walk into Portland, Oregon as two of the most prolific and marked men ever to stand in a BSCW ring, and we will walk out victorious, proving that the rest of the best of BSCW, just falls a little short.
[Leo looks at the crowd for a little bit, he just smirks and walks back to the back. The announcers begin to talk about what they've heard as the scene fades out.]
