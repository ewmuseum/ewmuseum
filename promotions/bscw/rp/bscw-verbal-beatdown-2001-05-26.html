Justin, there are so many misconceptions of me. First, not all wrestlers trained in Japan, are partial to barbed wire. That is very far from the truth, in fact, the only ones that crave the C-4, and the
Death Matches are Americans that can no longer make a living in the
United States. Sure I may have done my time in "those" types of matches, but one thing you will learn, is that inside and out, I live and breathe the art of puroresu.
Something else you are misinformed about is, without you, I am nothing. I can remember a time here in the BSCW when you weren't here, and I was doing very well for myself. I never had to refer to any matches in the past with members of the roster that are no longer with us, I don't have to relive my past glory to try and score points with the fans. I am here in the present, about to become the future of BSCW.
And lastly, you say that you "made" me. Without you I would be some
Japanese freak, hanging out with an arrogant SOB and a couple of twins. It was Leo that made me, proving that I could do it on my own, without the help of others. I staked my own claim as one of the premier stars here in BSCW. It wasn't you, Joker, Shane, the Rebel,
Ninja, no one but myself.
The sound of you voice in you latest interview ringed of uncertainty. Almost bordering on fear. Being used to having everything handed to you on a silver platter, never really having to work for it, is what you are used to. Now you face, the man you say you help "create," the toughest opponent of your career.
Let me use an example of what may best describe what you are feeling. Okay, you are Doctor Frankenstein, and I am the monster. You're biggest fear is the very same thing that you made. But unlike the story, I will not be turned away by fire, or driven into the woods by a mob. Just the opposite, I will come at you with all of the anger and the rage that has been built up for years. For your sake, I hope we have a merciful referee that will have pity on the lump of mass that was once your body.
This two out of three falls match will not be for ones with weak stomachs. This will show everyone what we are each made of, physically and relatively. I am ready to shed some blood, and let's hope you are too. To think that either one of us will get away without "juicing" you are sadly mistaken. Sometimes you need to bleed for your art. I hope you're ready to face what is the single most important match in both of our careers. No, not any of your matches with the Franchise even come close to the magnitude of this one.
This is going to be the defining moment for both of us. Which one of us is indeed, the number one guy in BSCW? I guess we'll find out in a couple of hours, won't we.
[Taurus sits up from the bed and walks into the small bathroom to the left, the follows him to the doorway of the bathroom. Minutes later, he emerges dressed, and walks out the front door of the room. He gets into his rental car and drives off into the Las Vegas afternoon. The scene fades out as Taurus drives out of the scene.]
