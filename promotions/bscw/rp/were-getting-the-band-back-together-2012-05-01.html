We're Getting the Band Back Together!

1 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in.] [A long, winding road in what appears to be an industrial complex in a fairly suburban area. Trees and the rare building go by as we're clearly in a moving vehicle. The view pans away from the view of the outdoors and we see two men in the front seat. Behind the wheel, Ian Finnegan. Former failed pro wrestler and Hannibal Carver hanger-on. Seated next to him, Rich Vail. Owner of Blood, Sweat & Chairs Wrestling. There seems to be trouble in paradise.] IF: ... I mean, how many of them visited or even wrote you? RV: They're not the kind of guys who write letters. You were outside, I was inside. You were supposed to keep in touch with the staff. I kept asking you if we were gonna open again. IF: What was I gonna do, take away your only hope? Take away the very thing that kept you going in there? I took the liberty of bullshitting you. Okay? RV: You lied to me. IF: It wasn't lies. It was just bullshit. [Vail groans.] RV: Christ, whatever. Is this the place? IF: Yeah. Looks nice, I wonder if they can get me in there. RV: Yeah, you in a wood shop. Power tools and circular saws as far as the eye can see. Phe-fucking-nomenal idea. [The car pulls into a mid-sized parking lot. Off in the distance we see a building with a sign reading "CCL - Cape Cold Lumber" above a pair of glass doors. Vail and Finnegan stay seated in the car, looking intently at the doors, as if waiting for someone to exit. And sure enough, the doors swing open and two semi-familiar faces emerge. It's been well over a decade and the ravages of age and time have had their way with them both, but old school BSCW fans can still recognize former hardcore champions Vic "Tim Dogg" Simmons and Bruce "Jabroni" Mahoney. Vail exits the car, slowly followed by Ian. Upon seeing their former boss, Mahoney runs full speed ahead, thrill and excitement clearly showing on his face. Simmons smirks, then shakes his head and sighs.] BM: Oh yeah!! Is this for real?! Are we finally getting called back up?! RV: In a manner of speaking... yeah. VS: Oh _hell_ no. Getting put through tables and smashed in my grill with beer bottles and set on _fire_? No thanks bro, I'll stick with making doors for rich folks. BM: Come on man! The blood, the sweat, the CHAIRS!! VS: Motherfucker, you dropped an elbow with a raw salmon under your arm. On the other hand I got chokeslammed so many times I can't believe I'm not in adult diapers right now. [Awkward silence, as everyone - Vic included - ponders what the hell _that_ is supposed to mean.] RV: Well, that's the thing. I want to sign you both to contracts to be on-air talent. BM: Hell yeah! Hardcore title, baby I'm back! IF: Uh... this is awkward. RV: [Sighs.] Well, two things. First, the Hardcore Championship has been dead as disco since you guy two worked for me back when. And second? I don't want you two on the roster. VS / BM: EH?! RV: I need a senior official, Vic. [Vic blinks.] VS: So, like... getting paid to wear a striped shirt and count to three? RV: That's about the size of it. [Vic laughs, pumping his fists towards the sky.] VS: Oh man, I am _in_! Goodbye splinters in my hands and fiberglass dust in my face! BM: Wait, what? A ref? I held gold and now you want me to be a zebra? RV: Oh, no no. Of course not. BM: Damn straight! RV: I want you to be a backstage interviewer. BM: WHAT?! [Mahoney staggers forward a few steps, then slumps to a bent-over position, as if someone just removed the string that keeps his spine upright. Vic pats him on the shoulder.] VS: [_Dripping_ with sarcasm.] Come on. The blood. The sweat. The chairs. MB: [Sighs.] Fine. You got me. [Vail slaps Bruce on the shoulder, shaking Vic's hand with his other hand.] RV: I knew I could count on you guys. I'll be in touch. [Turns to Ian.] RV: Let's roll.
