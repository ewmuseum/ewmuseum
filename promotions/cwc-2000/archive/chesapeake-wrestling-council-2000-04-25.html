CWC Card Archive

Chesapeake Wrestling Council

25 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introduction Hello. Welcome to the Chesapeake Wrestling Council. Frequently Asked Questions. Q. What exactly is the CWC? A. The CWC is a Maryland-based independent wrestling organization. Most of our cards will originate in Maryland, though we will occasionally foray into parts of Delaware and Virginia. As the organization gets bigger, so will our reach. We hope to one day become a nationwide wrestling federation.
Q. When will it open? A. As soon as I have at least 16 wrestlers, and can start a tournament. Q. What belts can be held in CWC? A. There are three belts. The first, and most prestigious, is the CWC Heavyweight Championship. It is equivalent to most feds' World Title. Since
CWC is not a nationwide fed, it is not a "World" Title, persay. The next belt, is the CWC Maryland State Championship. The holder of this belt is considered the #1 contender to the Heavyweight Title. The third belt, is the CWC Tag Team Championship. This is self-explanatory.
Q. What cards will there be? A. There will be one weekly card, hosted on Fridays. This card is known as "CWC Friday Night Warfare." Staff for this card will be: Stacey Sydnor, (Color commentary): Stacey, a vehement lesbian, despises wrestling, but finds it a quick way to make a buck.
Crank, (Commentator): Former drug dealer/manager now turns to commentary to atone for the loss of his best friend.
April Donaway, (ring announcer) Referees: Bobby Erwin (senior referee) Tim Furrow Dave Sceptor El Gordo Bastardo (mysterious fat masked guy) Q. What about Pay-per-view? A. As a low-budget organization, CWC has no "pay-per-views" so to speak. Maybe one day, when our budget allows, pay-per-view will become an option. In the meantime, we have the following "SuperCards." January- New Year's Revolution February- Cupid's Revenge March- Shamrocks & Suplexes April- Gang Wars (Featuring the two-ring, four stable War Games) May- Wicked Game June- Meltdown (from Three Mile Island in New Jersey) July- SummerFest (main supercard) August- Critical Mass September- Night of Carnage (all matches are no-holds-barred- "Extreme") October- Devil's Night Bash (Held Halloween Night) November- Turkey Day Massacre (Held Thanksgiving Thursday) December- Jingle Hell (Christmas Eve) Q. Who runs this place? A. The organization is OWNED by the seldom seen former Yeti-cult leader, Teddy Booze. The last time he ran a fed, he had midgets doing his bidding and keeping his wrestlers in line while he comfortably enjoyed the profits.
This time, he has engaged the services of one masked man, known as "Mr. Riddle."... a strange little white man from Mexico that, while speaking fluent English, has a few eccentricies. Mr. Riddle acts as the
Commissioner. Q. How do I join? A. Fill in the application at the bottom and send it to... [detail withheld] Q. How many people can I handle? A. Either of the following: A singles wrestler and a tag team. or 2 singles wrestlers or A tag team Either of these can be combined with a manager. Q. Who booked this crap? A. Basically, it's like this. Your win/loss record has 90% to do with your effort and performance in RPs and strats. The other 10% of it has to do with the current angles. It's simple, everyone loses at some point or another. However, just because you got screwed out of a title this week, does not mean that your efforts have gone unnoticed. Suck it up, and remember it's just an angle. It could be that the next week, you're extracting violent and bloody revenge on the guy that wronged you the week before. Just remember, kids, angles play a big part in the wrestling world, as well as in the world of e-wrestling. Any bitching and moaning will be subject to castration.
Q. What about RPs? A. It's simple. Everything goes to [detail withheld] I do mean everything. Strats. Apps. RPs. Just make sure you put [CWC] in the subject so that my delete-happy fingers don't send it to roleplay hell.
Make your RPs good, or as good as you can. If you're not that good, but you're trying, you'll be recognized. If you can do better, and you start half-assing it, well, I reserve the right to half-ass your participation in the match. Fair is fair, right? Oh yeah, and if you have a match, RP. At least once. Otherwise, your life is forfeit.
Q. Do I have to do strats? A. No. But In CWC they play a big part in getting the most out of your character. It's simple- If I know what you plan for your character to do in the match, it's easier to write for him, and the better chances of you seeing the angles that you like. It's kinda like how you don't HAVE to use a sledgehammer to break a cinder block, but it sure as hell beats using your hands.
