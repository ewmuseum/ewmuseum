NWC Interpromotional PPV: Purgatory

10 February 2000

It is their latest interpromotional Pay-Per-View, entitled "Purgatory". Truly, it is one of the greatest cards ever, in terms of being capable of building and following through a interpromotional card.
Please take a second to check out all of these resources for Purgatory, the nearly two-hundred flashes posted for it by wrestlers from multiple federations, the real media, and so on, and if it catches your interest in the slightest, read our card. I guarantee it to be at least an interesting read, and that's why I'm posting it there.
Hope you enjoy it. Thanks.. - - - - - - - - - - The Purgatory Lineup: Below is the mega lineup for both Night One: "Malevolence" and Night
Two: "The Seventh Level" Twenty Four matches in all this promised to be the biggest National Wrestling Council Pay-Per-View ever.
http://nwc2k.hypermart.net/ppv7.html Purgatory Real Video & Animated Promo: Although Real Video has been around for quite some time the NWC decided to take it to the next level. A live animated promo by NWC E.D. Chris
Schukar promotes this show to something that lived beyond the wildest expectations of the hundreds of handlers that compete in the five regions of this council.
http://members.tripod.com/nwconline/purgatory.ram Ticket Watch for RTP Tournament: Although I don't believe this was kept up to par once the entire card was announced, the entries into the 16-man "Road to Purgatory"
tournament were being announced in the weeks leading up to the PPV. As two sides, Alex Pierce and Chris Schukar, fought for control over the
NWC they traveled across the nation searching for the best eight men they could find to represent their side in winning the lost shares of former NWC E.D. Ken Nemesis. Review just how the first warriors were selected.
http://nwc2k.hypermart.net/ticket.html The Road to Purgatory Brackets: With ten days until showtime, the entire 16 man tournament was announced. Check out the large graphic of the seedings for the tournament. They are not ranked but randomly placed throughout their respected side.
http://nwc2k.hypermart.net/fullbracket.jpg Purgatory Flash Postings (RP Board) What was the count? Did someone say 200! Yes, nearly two hunred RPs (175) were written in hopes of many wrestlers obtaining their goals in the NWC. Whether it was the tournament, respected regions main title, or even the National Wrestling World Titles (Singles, Tag-Team, J-
Crown/cruiserweight). Relive some of the best work that the National
Wrestling Council has ever seen.
http://www.insidetheweb.com/mbs.cgi/mb966284 Purgatory Night One Real Audio Show (Nt. 1) Two men, stars of the famous GCW Reaction, break down the entire line- up for Night One: Malevolence with their predictions and comments on the matches.
http://nwcradio.tripod.com/preshow1.ram Purgatory Night Two Real Audio Show (Nt. 2) Like Night One, Night two: The Seventh Level, was also run down by the same two men. Thumbs up? Thumps down? What wrestlers were predicted to win the big shows.
http://nwcradio.tripod.com/preshow2.ram Purgatory Night One: Malevolence The Card! Night one featured the opening round of the "Road to Purgatory" Tournament, a loser leaves NWC forever match, a deadly
Scaffold Match, the BSW Heavyweight Title match, and the main event, the "Seven Layers of Hell" World Tag-Team Title Match between the champions, Demigods (Loki/Ra) and the challengers, Malignance
(Abomination and Wretched). http://nwc2k.hypermart.net/purg1.html Purgatory Night Two: The Seventh Level If you thought Night One was amazing then you'll be swept off your feet with Night Two. Not only did we witness the second round of the tournament but we saw the Schukar and Pierce bracket finals, the SCCW
Heavyweight Title match inside a Caged Inferno, a Crucifixion match, A
Cruiserweight number one contender match on top of a floating barge, the Tournament Iron Man Finals for control of the NWC, the unveiling of the mystery man (a complete shocker) and in the Main Event, the NWC
World Title Match between the champion, "Showtime" Trey Slater and the challenger, "Bermuda" Billy Page. AND, the unveiling to who Alex
Pierce really is!!!
