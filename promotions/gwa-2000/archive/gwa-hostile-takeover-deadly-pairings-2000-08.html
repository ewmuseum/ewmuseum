GWA — Show Script

Hostile Takeover: Deadly Pairings

16 August 2000 &ndash; 19 August 2000 (compiled)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

16 August 2000 — "{GWA Promo} Hostile Takeover: Deadly Pairings"

*** The Global Wrestling Alliance, in cooperation with the $$Cash Money Brothers$$ proudly brings to you their exciting upcoming September PPV....... "Hostile Takeover: Deadly Pairings" *** The PPV will be just like WCW's - Starrcade Battlebowl: The Lethal Lottery. For those of you that aren't familiar with that past PPV, then let me tell you about it. All of the wrestlers in the GWA will have their names put into the "proverbial hat". Their names will be drawn at random. Once a name has been drawn, another will then be drawn to see who his partner will be; the next two names pulled will form a tag team and face the other team. So on and so forth ... after all of the names have been drawn and all the teams are assigned, then the event begins. The team that manages to score a victory over their opponents moves onto Round 2. In Round 2, all of the winners of Round 1 square off in tag team matches with their partners from the 1st Round. The winners of the 2nd Round go to the finals; which is a two ring battle royal. All of the remaining wrestlers start off in "Ring 1" ... you cannot be eliminated by going over the top rope from Ring 1. You must throw your opponents into Ring 2; now from Ring 2 if you're thrown over the top rope and to the arena floor, then you're eliminated. The remaining wrestler in Ring 1 waits and rests until there is only one man left in Ring 2. Both of these men face one another in an attempt to throw the other over the top rope and onto the arena floor. This can be down from either ring. The man to eliminate his final opponent is declared the winner. Now onto the good stuff, the prize. *Grin* The victor is rewarded with a solid gold ring with either the GWA or $$C.M.B$$ encrusted atop it in diamonds. Also, a purse of $500,000.00 and a title shot at any title that the victor chooses at the card. I won't say the GWA is the best fed on the net, or even one of the best. What I will do however is ask that you check us out. Join the league and see if like what you see. Every rper as his or her own idea about what makes a great fed/rper. The only way you're going to know if the GWA is right for you, is if you join. You like, you stay. You don't, you leave. Simple as that. So if you want in on this great PPV... now is the time to join! The link for the GWA is listed below. Thanks for your time! http://www.homestead.com/GlobalWrestlingAlliance/index.html

19 August 2000 — "GWA-- $$Hostile Takeover$$ 'deadly pairings'"

***The television screen showed a solid black background, broken only by the GWA logo emblazoned in the center, the green, blue, and red colors vibrant and bright. Without warning, static shot through the transmission, but quickly faded away as if it'd never happened. Then it happened again ... and again ... and again ... and then the golden $$C.M.B$$ logo fell and landed atop the GWA logo, shattering it and sending the jagged shards careening around the screen. In small burning letters the words "HOSTILE TAKEOVER: DEADLY PAIRINGS" appears beneath the infamous Cash Money Brothers' logo. Transparent pictures of all of the GWA Superstars' faces ... Brimstone ... "The Main Event" Michael Stevens ... Bret "The Hitman" Hart ... Jason Reichl ... "The Copy Cat" Chris Caine ... "Simply Perfect" Chandler Sinclair ... Dark Mark X ... the Adonis ... and many more all flicker across the screen, their faces set in tense, serious expressions.*** [Voice Over]: "Allies ... Enemies ... Feuds ... Friendships ... this time it's not about wrestling ... this time it's about the luck of the draw! Hostile Takeover: Deadly Pairings ... live from Bern, Switzerland on September 23 only on Pay-Per-View!" ***In smaller white text the following appeared at the bottom of the screen: "Contact your local satellite or cable provider now or order on the Net at http://www.homestead.com/GlobalWrestlingAlliance/index.html or at http://CMBcentral.homestead.come/CMB1.html !" The screen gradually faded out into normal programming.*** To join the battle....sign on at http://homestead.com/globalwrestlingallinace/ppvtitle.html
