Global Wrestling Affiliation: Top Stories

4 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GWA's Top Stories: The GWA has brought in the reinforcements. With federation owner
Michael Whisson at the helm of the project, wrestlers such as Mark
Randall, Lance Storm, RVD, Brimstone, Chris Thunder, and even the recently retired Peter "Shadow" Winters have all came together to join in the battle against the "Brotherhood" and the Syndicate.
Lance Storm made his appearance known this week when he brought back a new form of the Impact Players. Within this stable are names like
Triple H, Test, Rick Flair, Justin Credible, "Old School" Steve
Corino, Torrie Wilson, and Lance Storm himself. After the loss at the
Revenge Pay Per View where Lance lost the GWA World Title to Triple7
(Leader of the Syndicate Stable) to what was, at the time, a newly formed $$CMB$$ group who was only after one thing...bringing down the
GWA. Amongst the list of people returning to the GWA...for the first time ever...the "Professional" Mark Randall came back with the former
"People's Champion" Shadow for a remarkable comeback just 2 weeks ago.
This comeback was a very unexpected one to say the least since Shadow and Randall have never worked together.
RVD sticks it all out on the line once again this week for the GWA, with a rematch of sorts, to face off with Shadow in a hardcore tag team match. Both men pick their opponents...and it's all about who get's the Title Shot. If RVD's team wins the match, Shadow is banned from getting a GWA World Title shot...however...if Shadow wins, he automatically gets a title shot against RVD. Now as many of you already know, RVD is part of the "Dangerous Alliance" lead by Paul E.
Dangerously. They too are in this for the GWA. As for Chris Thunder, well this week was a hard one for him as he faced off against the Syndicate's own Don Rodrighez, and as I'm already sure, it is going to set the stage for everything coming up at the Shattered Dreams Pay Per View.
The CMB on the other hand have plans of their own. The CMB tries to head off the Syndicate at the pass with every chance they get. With
CEO of the Cash Money Brother Inc. still not in physical condition to wrestler, it has brought a huge consern to the CMB as to exactly how much more of the brutal punishment they can recieve.
Rick Martel was quoted to saying that the CMB "is just like any business. Sure with Martel being out, it will make it hard. But each of us must step up against the Syndicate so that we can fight out battles with the GWA and put an end to this horrid federation".
Van Hammer is expected to make his return sometime in the near future, but with him out of the picture, it has seemed to make the CMB weaker than they have ever been seen before...or is this just what the CMB wants everyone to think???
And as for the Syndicate...well, things are not as easy as they make them look. It seems that these days, the Syndicate has a lot on their minds. With the GWA gaining power, and the CMB seemingly at a loss of power, the Syndicate too have had their downs in the federation. Last
November brought a side that the GWA thought they would never see...the leave of Triple7, Mrs. Stiener, ExodousRage, TwistedHeat,
Synn, and Kristy Myst.
Recent reports show that in some way, Triple7 has still managed to involve himself in the Syndicate, leading his band of men into the depths of the CMB, even helping them to establish a name for themselves.
However, many people feel that the return of the "Phenom" isn't as far away as many people think. With the recent "Crusifications", caskets, and bloodbaths, many feel that this is a sure sign of his return.
Could the fans be right? Or is this just someone from the outside trying to play the game that Triple7 takes pride in?
