GWA recruitment postings, 2000

16 March 2000 &ndash; 30 May 2000 (compiled)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

16 March 2000 — "GWA brand new e-fed"

Join the Global Wrestling Association. It has 5 belts (GWA World Title, Television, Cruiserweight, Razorwire [hardcore], and Tag Team) and is looking to hire only quality RPers with original characters. Check it out at: http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

17 March 2000 — "GWA wants you!"

Well, the Global Wrestling Association is in the process of acquiring about 8 very experienced wrestlers. If you are a good role-player, you might want to check this one out. We could also use some young talent and some tag teams. We will be able to start in about a week if we get some more people signing up. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

17 March 2000 — "GWA needs quality RPers"

The Global Wrestling Association is a brand new e-fed for good role-players. I have an experienced co-president now who will be in charge of writing and booking. If we can get some good talent, it has the potential to be a very excellent fed. Please go and sign up so we can get going. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

19 March 2000 — "top ten list for GWA"

Top Ten Reasons You Should Join the Global Wrestling Association 10. Four belts 9. Only the best roleplayers means more fun 8. Handwritten cards with no simulator (this was not the case when I first created it, but it is now) 7. Your mom won't let you wrestle for real, but this is the next best thing 6. GWA Magazine, with rankings, news, featured wrestlers, weekly awards, and an article by the president 5. A card writer who is a real wrestler so he knows his stuff 4. Two cards a week and one PPV a month 3. Fair matches based on roleplaying and angles (no under the table deals w/ wrestlers) 2. Free beer! (Just not from me. Go ask your neighbor or something.) 1. I took the time to write this freakin top ten list Sign up at: http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

25 March 2000 — "-->GWA looking for staf & original wrestlers!!!!"

The Global Wrestling Affiliation is looking for good quality roleplayers. Go to http://gwa2000.mainpage.net and check it out! We also need a VP and a Media Personality. The VP would basically fill in for me when I can't do something, and would provide suggestions. The Media Personality would be in charge of GWA Magazine and GWA Radio. He would have to have a microphone for his computer to do this job. You can look at the beginnings of these publications at the website http://gwa2000.mainpage.net and e-mail me at [detail withheld] if you are interested in filling either of these roles. Thanks! [name withheld] GWA--living proof that you don't need a title with "hardcore" or "extreme" to be a great fed. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

25 March 2000 — "==GWA=="

The GWA. Tired of the thousands of feds who say they are "hardcore to the extreme?" The GWA doesn't have some stupid overused cliche. We're just a great fed. So check us out. http://www.gwa2000.mainpage.net

25 March 2000 — "_--***GWA***--_"

Join the Global Wrestling Affiliation. We're not one of the thousands of e-feds who claim to be "hardcore." We're not just some cheap knockoff of every other fed out there. So come on in and join the GWA. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

26 March 2000 — "+++++GWA+++++"

Join the Global Wrestling Affiliation. You start as a "jobber", and progress in status to an "opener", then a "midcarder", and then a "superstar". Everyone starts on a level playing field and earns their position by roleplaying. As soon as the roster fills up, we'll have a 16-man tournament for the GWA World Title. We've got a great writer and some great roleplayers so sign up now and get your spot on the tournament bracket. The addy is http://gwa2000.mainpage.net [name withheld] GWA Prez Global Wrestling Affiliation--living proof that you don't need "hardcore" in your title to be an awesome fed http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

27 March 2000 — "~~~GWA needs SHW's~~~"

Join the Global Wrestling Affiliation right away!! We've got a few guys between 240 and 265 pounds, but I'd like to have a few super heavyweights, too. I know there's a lot of people out there who love to make giants, so come to the GWA and handle one! We'll be making a 16-man tournament for the first ever GWA World Title as soon as the roster fills up, so hurry and join! The addy is http://gwa2000.mainpage.net [name withheld] GWA Prez Global Wrestling Affiliation--living proof that you don't need "hardcore" in your title to be an awesome fed http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

28 March 2000 — "_##GWA##_"

The Global Wrestling Affiliation is a great new booked fed with an experienced writer, 2 cards a week, one PPV a month, 5 belts, and the graduated status system--you begin as a "jobber", and progress in status to an "opener", a "midcarder", and, finally, a "superstar". Only the best roleplayers will advance to superstar. If you think you've got what it takes, join the GWA! We're living proof that you don't need the word "hardcore" in your title to be a great fed. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net [name withheld] Global Wrestling Affiliation http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

6 April 2000 — "-->GWA<--"

The Global Wrestling Affiliation is an awesome RP fed with two writers who really know their stuff. One is a pro wrestler in real life; he also does the booking. We've got a lot of good roleplayers already and will start as soon as a few more people join. Tag teams and cruiserweights especially needed! http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

7 April 2000 — "GWA needs cruisers and tag teams"

The Global Wrestling Affiliation is in need of cruiserweights and tag teams. Comptetition is pretty slim now, so your chances of a belt would be pretty good. We also need heavyweights, but not as urgently as cruisers and tags. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net [name withheld] GWA President http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

8 April 2000 — "GWA needs wrestlers!!!"

Upcoming tournament for the GWA World Title. Get your spot on the roster fast! Cruiserweights and Tag Teams especially needed, but all are desired! Two great card writers, one of which is a real professional wrestler. Several excellent roleplayers. http://gwa2000.mainpage.net [name withheld] GWA President http://gwa2000.mainpage.net

11 April 2000 — "GWA 3:16 says "Join Now!""

We are having a tournament for the GWA World Title, and the deadline for people to be in the tournament is Thursday. You can still join after then, but you will not be included in the tournament. We especially need Cruiserweights and Tag Teams, but all wrestlers are needed. Join now! 2 cards a week, 1 PPV a month, original wrestlers only, 2 excellent writers, plenty of fun! http://gwa2000.mainpage.net [name withheld] GWA President http://gwa2000.mainpage.net
