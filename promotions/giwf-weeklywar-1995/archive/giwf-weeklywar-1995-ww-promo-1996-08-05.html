Global Internet Wrestling Federation — Other

GIWF WW Promo

5 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A large banner scrolls across the screen. It is rainbow colored, and in jet black letters, "The Global Internet Wrestling Federation" goes by. Then, the rainbow colors bleed down the screen, leaving only blackness. Then, A Large flash of light goes across the screen with the words.. "GIWF Light-Heavyweight Tournament".. The Blood Dragon flashes on the screen.. The Pan-Dimensional Impossiblist flashes on the screen. Another flash.. PDI suplexing Blood Dragon outside to the concrete! Another flash.. Blood Dragon with a Handspring Elbow in the corner! Flash.. PDI with Enigmation (Million Dollar Dream). Douglass checks the arm.. falls 3 times. Flash.. Storm Lords hit the ring. They pummell PDI. Flash.. Storm Hammer (Spiked Brainbuster) on PDI! Flash.. "The Bod" Theseus Devlin locks Beauty Sleep (Million Dollar Dream) on PDI! The view swirls around in a pinwheel of many colors. It reopens to show a flash of Tex Deathstar.. Another flash.. The Wingman, Major Hawk Hunter.. yet another Flash.. Belly to Belly by Hunter!.. Flash.. Flying Body Press off the Top onto Tex! Flash.. Tex with a Torture Rack.. Flash.. Tex drops him, John Johansen raises Deathstar's hand. A fireball consumes the picture. As the flames die down, Billy Matson is shown in the ring. The Arsenal enter the ring as Live and Let Die plays. Flash.. Gregg "Comet" Matthews and Sal DeFazio raise the GIWF World Tag-Team Title Belts over their heads. Flash.. Joe Williams tirading about Steve "The Slasher" Latini. The screen fades out, and back in. The words "GIWF Women's Title Tournament" flash on the screen. Another flash, showing Bunny.. Flash.. Coach Chris is shown.. Flash.. Flying Dropkick by Bunny! Flash.. High Cross Body off the Top by Bunny CAUGHT by Coach Chris and she Body Slams Bunny. Flash.. Flying Elbow Drop off the Top by Bunny! Flash.. Academic Ineligibility (Throws opponent up horizontally so that her neck/head comes down on the top tournbuckle) applied to Bunny. Flash.. Coach Chris with the pin. The view scrolls up off the screen.. simultaneously a new view scrolls up from the bottom of the screen. It is "King" Guy Newman. Flash.. Vince "The Viper" Vertigo is shown. Flash.. Newman with a Butterfly Suplex! Flash.. Suplex by The King, blocked & reversed into a Brainbuster by The Viper! Flash.. Newman slams Vertigo's head into the steps.. Flash.. Double Underhook Piledriver by Guy Newman! The Viper Kicks Out! Flash.. 2 men in all black enter the ring and attack Arizona Solberg. Flash.. The men hold The King in an arm and Vertigo hits the Snake Bite (Superkick)! The scene pops out and back in.. The Proclaimer is shown in the ring. Flash.. Diamond Dan appears.. Flash.. Proclaimer with a Spin Kick followed by a Crescent Kick! Flash.. Clothesline by Proclaimer, but Diamond Dan just grins at him! Flash.. Diamond Dan with The Keg Stand (Holds opponent in the air in a suplex possition)! Flash.. The Def Drop (Face First Suplex) by Diamond Dan! Flash.. Wheels congradulating DD. A flash goes across the screen with the words.. "GIWF Light-Heavyweight Tournament".. Flash.. S.J. Americana is shown. Flash.. The Lawmaker appears. Flash.. S.J. with a Senton from the apron to the floor on Lawmaker! Flash.. Moonsault through a table by S.J.! Flash.. Frankensteiner by S.J.! Flash.. Frankensteiner off the Top by S.J., but Lawmaker holds on, and S.J. hits the mat hard. Flash.. Moonsault by Lawmaker! Gets the 3 count. Flash.. Lawmaker opens a delivered briefcase and reads a letter. Chaos wants him in his group. The view gets all smokey. It abruptly blows away, and covering the screen is Blobber.. Flash.. American Patriot is shown. Flash.. Bearhug by Blobber! Flash.. Spinning Toe Hold by AP! Flash.. American Boy is shown.. Flash.. Mr. Universe & Mr. Everything are shown.. Flash.. American Boy & Mr. U going at it. Flash.. Mr. E hits the ring, but gets arm dragged by AP! Flash.. Side Suplex by Blobber! Flash.. Mr. U Choke Slam to American Boy! Flash.. American Patriot with his arm raised. The camera view sways back and forth, distorting the view of American Patriot and then sways in a different picture.. This one, of Mr. Universe and Mr. Everything again. Flash.. The Men In Black appear on the screen. Flash.. Flying Karate Kick by Mr. E to Krill! Flash.. Headbutt off the 2nd rope by Mr. U as Mr. E holds Krill down. Flash.. Sven Black and Mr. U with chokes on each other. Flash.. Mr. U with a Choke Slam to Sven! Flash.. Mr. E with a Drop Toe Hold & Mr U with a Knee Drop to the back of the head of Sven. Flash.. Sven Clotheslines Mr E into a 360! Flash.. Sven with a Choke on Mr. E.. Flash.. The Bell rings. Flash.. Cross Face Chicken Wing applied by O.H. Krill to Mr. U. The screen picture explodes. A cage is being set up! Flash.. Standing on an interview platform is Billy Matson. Flash.. Lonestar comes out wearing the GIWF World Heavyweight Title around his waist. Flash.. Desert Storm and Lt. Liberty come out. Flash.. DS challenges Lonester, and leaves the ring to applause. Flash.. "Carry On" by Man Of War plays and The Warrior comes out. Flash.. Warrior has words for Lonestar "MAY THE BEST MAN WIN !!!!" Flash.. Warrior enters the cage.. Flash.. Lonestar enters the cage.. Flash.. Lonestar Rams Warrior's head into the Cage! Flash.. Again, blocked! Lonestar's head hits the Cage! Flash.. Both big men fly into the air, and their Heads Crack Together! Flash.. Lonestar with a running start.. rams Warrior's head into the cage! Flash.. A closeup of Warrior's bleeding head! Flash.. DDT by Lonestar! Pins him.. Kickout! Flash.. Warrior hits a Flying Clothesline off the Top! Flash.. Choke Slam by The Warrior! Pin, but Lonestar with the foot on the ropes. Flash.. Lonestar Slams Warrior's already bloody head into the cage! Flash.. He Falls! Flash.. The pin on Warrior, with both legs hooked! Flash.. Lonestar, out of the cage, holds his GIWF World Title Belt high above his head in 1 arm as he walks slowly back to the locker room. The view fades to black. The camera pops up to a blue background. Electricity runs from the corners of the screen. A loud thunderclap is heard, and words drop suddenly from the top of the screen. "GIWF WEEKLY WAR.." Lightning strikes the words, and they shatter! Another thunderclap is heard! More words fall suddenly.. "RETURNING TO WHERE WE BEGAN.." Lightning strikes the words again.. they crumble. On the left side of the screen, two giant Question Mark's appear surrounded in a purple haze.. on the right, All-American and American Boy with the Stars and Stripes in the background. They fade out... Fading in to show The now unmasked Storm Lords of Cyclone and Hurricane, with Christopher Andrews between them. Above their heads, is #1 Contenders, surrounded by what looks like swirling winds and blowing water. To the right of them, Jingoist.. Lt. Liberty, in his classic Double Biscep Pose and Hawk Hunter holds his helmet in under his arm. Above them is #2 Contenders, Soon to be #1. They fade out... Fading in we see The Giant Power Surge X'd out in red, and Tiwan Hong taking its place. To the right, just a plain, large, Black X on a white background. They fade out... Fading in, we now see The Royal Court of Sir Lance A Lot and Lord James Royale with Charles Eden standing between them. The background is a royal blue. To the right, The Men In Black. Sven Black and O.H. Krill stand in all black. The background is blackness. Barely visible is a large, lighter Black, large Question Mark in between them. They fade out... Fading in we see "The Bod" Theseus Devlin X'd out in red, and "80's Man" Tyler Smythe taking his place. To the right, "Substance" Chad O'Donnell stands holding his electric guitar. Above him the words, Smythe.. then Wug.. They fade out... Fading in, we see Astro along with Stardust. A multitude of fans cheer in the background. To the right, we see Sandman, with a swirling mist of sand flying all around him. They fade out... Fadin in, Eliminator is shown, not with Regan, but with a Huge man, even larger than himself. They fill up the entire screen. To the right, Lord Foul the Despiser stands with his hands on his hips. The words The Chaotic Dynasty are above him. They fade out... Fading in we see Mean Man-Mama with the Million Dollar Belt over her left shoulder, and the GIWF Women's Title belt over her right. Mr. Burns stands next to her, but barely fits in the screen. Above her, it says Non-Title Match. To the right, La Glace stands with D'Karng the Mad Klingon next to her. Above them, the words But a win will get a shot. They fade out... A loud thunderclap is heard as the screen explodes in many colors. Swirling back together, we now see "GIWF World Heavyweight Title Match!" Flash on the screen. Then, The Head Patriot, Desert Storm is shown with the GIWF World Heavyweight Title Belt around his waist, standing on the left. Above him, are the words "The Champ". To the right, American Patriot stands with the U.S. Flag behind him. Above him it says #1 Contender. The screen then fades out. And exploding once more! Words drop from the top of the screen.. "GIWF WEEKLY WAR.." The letters break apart at the bottom of the screen. Another loud thunderclap is heard, and more words come down.. "BE THERE!" The screen then explodes in light and sound! Then.. silence.. and darkness..
