"The GIWF Hotline #1"

13 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello GIWF fans. This is Capt. "Crunch" O'Mally, and I'm here to present to you the GIWF Hotline. The source for all of the important stuff that goes on here in the GIWF. We will have every thing from profiles, interviews, match predictions, inside information, anything and everyting. Just to remind you, that the hotline cost .99 cents a minute and .50 cents each additional minute (for the formality of it all). Kids, make sure you have your parents permission. Right now, I'm at Madison Square Garden,.. home of the New York Knicks, the New York Rangers, and site of a couple Wrestlemania's. But tonight, in a few short hours, this place will be sold out as wrestling fans from all over come to see the GIWF at tonights War Of The World's. Now, for those of you who don't have tickets there is still time to order WOTW on PPV, just call up you local cable system and say, "I want the GIWF WOTW!" As you can hear, the ring is being assembled behind me for tonight's collosal match ups. We have several tag elimination bouts including, Damage-D Goods vs. The Lyons' Den, the highly anticipated Arsenal vs. Freedom Brigade/Mercenary Enforcement Agency match up, The Alliance vs. The United Patriots, The Millionaires vs The Penultimate Warriors, The Blood Hunters vs. Team Justice, Female teams The Millionairs vs. The Good Girls, Sal Defazio vs. Lonestar in a Former Champions Match, The Light Heavyweight Champion, Chaos to defend his title vs. The Lawmaker, and the main evnet, the GIWF U.S. Champion, The "I-Man", Jacob McDonahey challenging the GIWF World Heavyweight Champion, "The Bod", Theseus Devlin. Now, an inside source has informed me that a manager here in the GIWF is not managing any more. And reports say that the manager takes part in at least two of the matches tonight. It is not know, however, if this manager has been fired, lost it's mangerial liceince, quit, or left the GIWF for other shores. We tried to contact Presiden Stritar on the mater, but his office said that the person which it concerns asked for no information to be given out untill after WOTW. Well, there are only a few men possible, but I don't know who it could be. And for some uplifting news, the GIWF has been ranked in the top five federations that are independent from the summit leagues. This coming from fans, T.V. and PPV ratings, and from editiors at Pro E-Wrestling Monthly. Quite an honor for only being around for five months. Well, that's about it for the GIWF Hotline. If you are watching on PPV, you will see me doing commentary for a match. I can't wait for the action tonight. Well, till next week, this is Capt. "Crunch" O'Mallay for the GIWF Hotline. -- --- "Halls of Justice painted green, money talking. Power wolves beset your door, hear them stalking. Soon to please their appitite, they devour.
