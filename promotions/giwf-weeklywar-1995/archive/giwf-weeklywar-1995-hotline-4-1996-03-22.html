"Crunch" O'Mallay!!!! ******** OH YEAH!!!! How are you today. This is Capt. Crunch O'Mallay and welcome to my home for the next edition of the GIWF Hotline. Well, I hope everybody had a great St. Patty's day. I did,..but to bad it was on a Sunday, you know. Well, anyways...this week, I have some shocking news about a few GIWF wrestlers that are not here anymore. And one of them is a shocker. Plus, we will be discusing the World Title shot that is in question for five wrestlers here in the GIWF. And this week, I have two new features to the Hotline....The Crunch Of The Week and GIWF Profiles. Right now,..lets take a look at last week's WEEKLY WAR!! ******** WEEKLY WAR FOR MARCH 19, 1996 Before I get started,...I was thinking, and Ross,...Ross is our manager here,...Hey Ross,...what do you think about having audiences here for the Hotline. Hahaha,...Ross just freaked. He has enough trouble with just the 7 of us running the show,..especially me. That might be a option I might persue in the future. Anyways,..let's talk about those matches. Lt. Liberty vs. D'Karng First match, first one right for the week for my predictions. Damm that Lt. is cocky. I like that alot. This match was pretty much one sided for the whole contest. D'Karng was able to throw a few moves in there,..but it was not enough to do damage to the 273 pounds of twisted steel and sex appeal. Lt. made short work using some of his awesome strengh to put D'Karng down. And obviously Lt. is a fan of Ric Flair, as he took out D'Karng's knee with elbows and knee drops. A figure four leglock was all that it took for Lt. to get his singles debut victory. The Defender vs. Mr. Everything Well, there was a little loop hole planed by M. Misson that got his man to fight Lawmaker instead of The Defender,...but I still picked Mr. E to win, and well....another victory for me. In a match up that pitted both wrestlers at almost the same weight and height. However, Mr. E had most of the match with Lawmaker on the mat. Mr. E worked on Lawmaker's legs and kept him there for the first half of the match. But Lawmaker was able to retaliate to almost a near fall. Unfortunatly, Lawmaker missed a Moonsault Press that led to the end. Obviously hurting from the moves, after delivering a Fall Away Slam, he was easily rolled over in a crucifix pin and kept there for the count. Mr. E was victorious. Johnny Banger vs. Power Surge Well,.....what can I say. It's not that I picked this one wrong,..but my man Johnny Banger was DESTROYED by the 375 pound Power Surge. My god, PS was not phased by any of Banger's early attacks. I have not seen a TOTAL one sided match so far in the GIWF, but I think this is a first. I almost felt like Banger "jobbed" for the match. For the sake of the other wrestlers here in the GIWF,...I hope so. Could we have a new strongest man in the GIWF? Who knows? I thought that muscle bound giants were going out of style. Anyways,..for the match...if you call it one,...Power Surge delivered several high impact powermoves that would do in anybody. Obviously Power Surge is sending somebody a message, if it wasn't Banger with those THREE Powerbombs of shear destruction. He finally finished him off with a Running Powerslam for the easy pin. You know,...I just thought of something.......I have a possible new favorite wrestler here in the GIWF,..and it might be Power Surge. Royal Court vs. Justice Jackson and Bruten Black The new team of Justice Jackson and Bruten Black showed that they are an upcoming team here in the GIWF. The Royal Court started out good, but slowly fell by the wayside by all around great wrestling by JJ and BB, especially Justice Jackson. Jackson was able to use his great power to deliver some high impact moves. In end after some excellent tag team moves by Royal Court, it was not enough to stop JJ and BB from winning. Even with the four back and forth pin counts, it was Jackson and Black getting the victory and giving me another loss for the week. Tiwan Hong vs. Substance Chad O'Donnell This match pitted a very impressive newcomer, Hong, with the seasoned veteren, O'Donnell. SCO'D quickly went to work on a leg, which would prove to be the key at the end. SCO'D was able to take the big asian off his feet with an impressive Powerslam. But soon, Hong took control with some lethal moves, like a vertical suplex and a Belly to Belly suplex, all which rung SCO'D's bell. Even after a Gourdbuster, Chad still had enough in him to know where he was in the ring. After a cross body block off after being throw into the corner, SCO'D again worked on that leg. After putting back down Hong with a Full Nelson Suplex, he applied both a Figure Four Leglock and a Indian Death Lock, that for most people would of made them submit,..but obviously Hong has a great deal of pain resistance which will be a virtue for him here in the GIWF. SCO'D then put the finishing touches on, when he set up Hong for his famed and most feared move, the Flying Piledriver. With that,..it was a easy 1,2,3. SCO'D has climed the rankings in blinding fashion. It wasn't that long ago that SCO'D planned on putting his singles carrer on a slight hold to persue a tag team carrer. That has all seemed to change as the five year veteren has shot to #2 in the GIWF rankings. We profile SCO'D on a new Hotline feature,..GIWF Profiles. Red, White, and Blue vs. Men In Black Again, my insight serves me well. The match quickly started into a brawl....a fight that MIB won't lose. Sven Black managed to take down American Boy with some power, and of course, brawling moves. After a powerbomb and near fall, MIB went for a killer of a move,..a dropkick to AB on the top rope to send him over the ropes,...but AB was able to retaliate with a flying dropkick. Quite impressive for a 7 foot, 300+ wrestler. I have a question. Where in the hell are all of these 7 foot+ wrestlers comming from? Did they all drink the same water? My oppinion, you can have all the weight and height you want....if you can't wrestle,...it ain't going to do no good. Anyways, All American started to take on Krill, but even after a small offensive strike, including a Revolution Sharpshooter, it wasn't enough to stop MIB. MIB was able to do that dropkick move to AB, and it was basically over from there. After some punishment outside the ring from Sven, Krill slapped on the cross face chicken wing and that was it. Victor Zubov vs. Johnny Banger Well, Johnny was not able to make it to the ring for this one, after his severe beating from Power Surge. But Zubov came to wrestle,...and issued an open challenge to anybody to come and wrestle him. Well, one man took that challenge,..and to the crowds surprise, it was none other than Wheels from The Arsenal. Obviously taking in the trend of managers turning to wrestling. Wheells came in running. He was deffinatly pumped up for this. However, Victor had other plans. Victor started using his strenght and brutality to deliver moves like a hangman and big forearm blows. However, Wheells took a break outside and took some energy from the fans. Wheels came back in and did what everbody else has done that night,...go for the legs. He droped five elbows on Victor's leg. Victor, though, was able to catch Wheels in a Spinebuster from there. Wheels end up being thrown out of the ring by Victor after a failed Slingshot Clothesline. Victor then did the Russian Nightmare and that was it. Afterwards, Zubov beat on Wheels in the middle of the ring with a Hockey Stick. Preparing to blow a last blow, Steve Latini flies to the ring and stops his partner from beating on Wheels anymore. Zubov can't belive,..the crowd couldn't belive it either. Victor ended up leaving, but Steve made a motion to hit Wheels, but then left. HUH???!!!!! I don't get this one folks. Pressure Point vs. The Protectors There was nothing too spectacular in this match,..as I feel both teams are to bent on not breaking the rules, but it was, none the least an impressive showing for the Protectors. It went back and forth for the first part of the match, but soon, The Protectors showed that they have some ring intelligence, as they not only managed to kept a good portion of the match in there corner,..but it was Sal DeFazio that they were doing it too!! A good combo move was the Grapvine followed by a Flying Elbow Drop. However, Sal knew where he was in the ring, and got a foot on the ropes. Sal was able to tag in to Gregg, and The Comet brought it to and end. While action was going on in the ring, The 80's Man, Tyler Smythe came down to ringside and sprayed Mace in Sal's eyes. Both Kane, and Diamond Dan, who appeared out of nowhere chased after Smythe, but as for Ryder, the Figure Four applied by Mathews was too much as it forced Ryder to pin himself. This Week's Predictions: 6-2 Overall: 10-5 ******** Well, fans,...The Hotline has picked up some unfourtnate news about a few wrestlers here in the GIWF. Lonestar is no longer in the GIWF! This could make for a big impact, as Lonestar was former World Heavyweight Champion, and has been battling for a rematch in recent weeks. Also gone are The Blood Hunters, Silencer, Huntsman, and Nimrod along with their manager, General Nefarius. Also gone are The Pandimensional Impossiblist, Doug Pandimen, Hawk the Killerfragger, although he might be seen around, he won't wrestle, and The Savior, after his brutal beatings. So, with Lonestar gone,..what does this leave for the GIWF World Title Shot that is up in the air. Let's take a look at it. ********* GIWF WORLD TITLE SHOT We all saw last week, when the GIWF World Heavyweight Champion, The Bod, Thesius Devlin, said of the top stars challenging him to....."figure it out yourselves". My question is,...how. And how do we determine it. Do we leave it up to the title board. Who knows. Lets take a look at the five men in question. First off, I have been in formed that The GIWF U.S. Champ, Jacob McDonahey is not in the running, as well as Steve Latini, as,..well, I don't think it is official,..but I have a feeling that we will be seeing Jacob defend his title against Steve in the near future. So that lowers it down to four wrestlers inculding,..Sal DeFazio, The Head Patriot, Desert Storm, The Warrior, and Victor Zubov,..all which are in the top 7 in the GIWF. For Sal, the reason is obvious, he's a former champ,..and he split two matches with Lonestar. With Lonestar gone...this puts him up there for a top spot. For Desert Storm, he has made an impresive move to the top and is the number #1 seed. With only having one title shot so far in the GIWF, his time maybe due. For the Warrior, up untill two weeks ago, he had a firm grasp of the top five in which he had stayed there from the begining. Plus, that length of time and only one title shot gives him a legit chance. And For Victor, he has moved up the rankings and has a solid hold, but he has yet to see a title shot, and the waves he is making in the GIWF give him just as good of a chance than the others. This is going to be a tough one. I don't know who should get it first. We'll be watching this in the upcomming weeks. ******* Not much to report from happenings around the main circuts of the WWF and WCW. Johnny B. Badd, or Marc Mero, is heading for the WWF. Maybe some of you on RSPW saw his recent post to the group. I hope the best works for him. Goldust was on the Connan O'Brian show last night,...and all I can say is....this is not the Dustin Rhodes that I know. Dusty and I are good friends from way back in when I circulated between the NWA, AWA and Mid South, and I've seen Dustin grow as a wrestler. But Goldust is nothing more than a paycheck,...and I'm not saying money is bad,..heck...it' the greatest. But for Dustin to be doing this with Goldust,..all I can say is Vince is probably paying him a good amount of money. Another thing from WCW is causing a big stir in RSPW land,..and that is of "The Final Solution." Now, I don't want to get into details, but if this three word title means nothing to you, never mind. For those who know what is going on in RSPW, hang on while I say this...anybody who is offended by the three word title,..don't be. It's rediculous to be. For those of us that know what is going on, well, I think it's stupid that it's even being brought to a head. I'm not going to get into the concrete matter of it...we all know it was a bad and terrible thing, but I don't think this is what Bishoff is emplying at all. So, lets just end it at that. ******** GIWF PROFILES Well, here comes one of two new features here on the Hotline,...GIWF Profiles,...where we profile a current wrestler here in the GIWF. For our first one...let's take a look at Substance Chad O'Donnell (Editor's note: Yes, I'm using my guy,...but just to show you what can be done.) Substance Chad O'Donnell 6'5'' 275lbs. From: Ottawa, Illinois SCO'D started his carrer in the original IWA back in December of 1990. After having been trained for 3 plus years, SCO'D, who was wrestling under the name of "Substance" started to make a name for himself, especially with his blockbuster move,..The Flying Piledriver. SCO'D then teamed with his younger brother,.Matt (Desert Storm), and under the rapid popularity and ring savy of him, he help his brother form the Freedom Brigade. Both he and his brother had taken on some tough opponents and had defeated them, while also winning regional titles along the way. With the sucess and popularity of his brother over shadowing him, SCO'D then went to Germany in 1992 to wrestle. Again, he turned heads with his high impact moves and the awesome Flying Piledriver. Chad stayed on the Eropean circut till late 1992, where he returned to find not only his brother but his best friend, Lt. Liberty, and Hawk Hunter in the Freedom Brigade. With SCO'D back as the back bone, the FB continued to dominate the IWA. But a motercycle accident by Lt. Liberty force all of the members to reevaluate everything. They decided to take a break from wrestling. SCO'D started a band in the Chicago area and was bound to be signed. But SCO'D backed down, seeing his brother retraining to return. SCO'D also found a Jacob McDonahey, who had heard of SCO'D both in Europe and in Chicago. SCO'D led Jacob in to training, helped his brother strat training, and also hit the weights himself. After helping lead Desert Storm to the INWF World Title in May of 95, SCO'D and Jacob started rigours training where in August, they enterd the CCWF and the GIWF shortly after. Chad and Jacob abandonded the CCWF for the GIWF soley and started to wrestle as Irsih, Inc. Since then, Chad has yet again been a backbone not only in the FB but in the GIWF, allways causing people to watch and see what is SCO'D is going to do next. And as allways,..to get a glimpse of the master of the Piledriver and it's most lethal form,...The Flying Piledriver. ******* Being from the Chi-land area, I have met both of the two O'Donnell brothers,... and never have I seen or heard the intesity that they have. They remind me of the Steiner Brothers. ******* Now, for the final feature of the Hotline....The "Crunch" Of The Week. There were a few ones that get my Crunch of Approval, but for this week... I'd have to say it goes to THE MEN IN BLACK for the dropkick to American Boy,...sending him sprialling down with a splat on the concrete floor. That was deffinatly as "Crunch" Of The Week!!! ******* Well, the matches for this week's Weekly War are not out yet,...but I promise to you,..that I will get to you my thoughts on how I think the matches will go. Well, this has been a short Hotline,...but don't forget to tune in everyweek for the most inavated concept in wrestling today...THE GIWF HOTLINE. So,...as I was quoted this week on RSPW...Win if you can, lose if you must,.... but allways cheat. Till next week,...This is Capt.
