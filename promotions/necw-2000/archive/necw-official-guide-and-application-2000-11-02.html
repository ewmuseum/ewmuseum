Application Background The Northeast Championship Wrestling organization is a federation based out of the northeast. All cards will take place in cities in the northeast. Just because it is a local fed doesn't mean that its going to be smalltime. I expect the competition to be great. Now you probably want to be reassured that this fed won't be a flop, well I have ran a couple feds as well as been on the board of directors for a couple. I ran the EIWA for awhile a year and a half to be exact before giving control of the fed up to a board of directors because
I had to go into knee surgery. I than ran the USWA for 2 years up until the end of July. I gave that one up to one of my friends because it was time for me to go to college. Now that I am settled in Cornell University it is time for me to start another fed. This fed will be run through a 3 man board if you will. Myself,
[detail withheld] , and [detail withheld] . We will decide the outcome of matches. The most important thing is to have fun. We will not have any wrestlers and we will be fair to everyone. Just remember everyone has to lose once in awhile. I have never met a Goldberg of Fantasy Wrestling.
Rules 1. Original wrestlers only please. No ripoffs of the WCW or WWF. They will not be accepted and you will not be allowed in the NECW. It makes it more fun when you are creative with your own guy. Anybody can imitate a Steve Austin or the Rock.
2. Roleplaying is the way you win matches. However there is a limit. One roleplay a day. And make your roleplays count. I don't want to see any "I am going to kick your ass" for 10 lines and thats it. That doesn't even count towards your match as far as I am concerned. Think them out and take some time to write a good one. If I am going to take the time to write you cards I want you to take the time and write good roleplays.
3. This fed will be run through a mailing list. After we get up and running if it is wanted I will make a website but at the beginning there is no need for one. I will send out the mailing list after I get your application. You will roleplay to that list. Absolutely no spamming. Any spamming of the list results in immediate termination from the roster. Please place a NECW in the subject of every roleplay followed by your own title.
4. Cards and types of matches will be outlined later in the guide. 5. If you have any ideas about angles feel free to email them to me. I am not making any promises that I will use them but I will think about. Angles will only go through if you roleplay enough to deserve the win. If there is an angle for someone to get a world title shot somehow and they stop roleplaying I will stop that angle faster than it was started.
6. Stables are allowed but not at the beginning. Once the roster increases in size stables will be allowed but don't let them get too big. 7. Titles will be talked about later also. Cards and types of matches There will only be one card per week. That allows the roleplays to be of the best quality possible. There will be 1 big card per month and if there is not enough heat there might not be a big card. These are not payperviews. Instead we will call them Supercards. They will have names like pay per views but remember this is a local federation so we can't afford to be put on pay per view. They will be named accordingly.
The weekly card will be called. Sunday Night Meltdown with the hosts to be determined. I am now in contract with many different possible hosts. There will be 2 per card with one other doing interviews in the back every once in awhile.
Now on to the type of matches available. This is by no means a hardcore federation. So there will be no absurd matches except maybe on the Supercards.
Weekly cards will be normal matches. The exceptions are as follow: Triple Threat Match: This match is just like a normal match. First one pinned loses. Some of them will become elimination matches.
Falls Count Anywhere: This can happen on occasion. There has to be a lot of heat. Cage Match: This one also has to have a lot of heat. This is reserved to title matches. Anything else will have to be brought up to me. But the fact is to get any different types of matches you must roleplay and build a lot of heat. The more heat the better possibility of a different kind of match. However, If I do decide to make a hardcore type of belt I will come up with some different kinds of matches.
Titles We will only start out with 2 singles belts and tag team belts will be made if there is a huge demand for them, however that is quite unlikely since most people don't make tag teams.
The two belts are: The NECW Television title: This belt is defended every week on Meltdown. Meltdown is broadcasted on the Empire Sports Network for anyone who is from the
New York area. This is where you get a good start to go after the World Title.
THE NECW Championship belt: This belt is the biggest in the fed. The one everyone is after. It says you are the best in the fed. Defended on Meltdown and definately at every supercard.
Now if we get a lot of members I will create another title. But that will be all we need for now.
Application Now that we have gotten all that out of the way here is the application. No sample roleplay is necessary. I trust Dave when he says you guys are good roleplayers. I hope to see your application.
