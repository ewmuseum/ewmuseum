Introduction Hello, and welcome to the world of the WPWF. This is a wrestling federation, dedicated to serious roleplayers who will commit themselves to this federation for a long term, gaming experience. The people who run this federation have been e-wrestlers for a long time and are fed up with federations unexpectedly closing, running late and overall jobbing on the affair. So, we decided to start up a federation that is dedicated to you, the wrestler and will promise not to do any of the above. We thank you for reading over this handbook, and we hope you will enjoy your stay in the WPWF, what will surely become the driving force of e-wrestling!
------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------- 2. Staff 2.1 Fictional Staff Active President - Mr Gabriel Skinner The president, and the man who has the last word on all WPWF goings on, Mr Gabriel Skinner. Gabriel is a man who tries to do the best by the fans and likes to give the fans the best show possible. Gabriel doesn't care whether it is heel or face who wins the match up, as long as it gives the fans an entertaining encounter.
Vice President - Rick Raymond To say that Rick Raymond likes wrestling would be a blatant lie. Rick Raymond loves money, the only thing he likes more than money is manipulating the wrestlers under his command. Rick and Gabriel have never saw eye to eye over matters in the WPWF. Although Rick, also being the commisioner, has a more interactive role in the federation than Gabriel will ever have. Rick knows the wrestlers on a street level and isn't above getting along in feuds.
2.2 Actual Staff President - The actual presidency of the WPWF is run by three different people. This hereby insures that all cards will be out on time as all three people can work together to take this fed to the heights that it rightly deserves. All work will be shared between the three and no-one will have more than his own share of work. We hereby promise that every single angle, strat and roleplay will be taken into account and used to further the career of the lucky wrestler, participation is the key.
Roleplay Manager - [name withheld] Will is adept at managing roleplays as he has been an e-wrestler for the last two years and has grown to appreciate the different styles of many of you e-wrestlers. Will will not only send out roleplay packages but will apply critical commentary as he sees fit, under the guise of the two WPWF commentators.
Angle Manager - [name withheld] John, like Will, has been an ardent roleplayer for quite sometime and has produced many angles, that in all truthfulness has occasionally changed the course of federations. John will create angles based on roleplayers' gimmicks, styles, and aggressiveness. However, for this to work well he will require ideas from the people who ultimately make the angles exciting, the wrestlers. John is also in charge of strats and sneak attacks.
Overall ajudicator and admin man - [name withheld] Chris has also been around the e-wrestling world for the same length of time as the others. He has noted the way various different federations have been run and has taken extreme interest in the overall structures of different federations. Chris knows what works and what doesn't.
Chris is in charge of the day to day running of the federation, and the overall discipline of the wrestlers. It's Chris's job to get things out on time in a way that can be understood.
3. Federation Personalities 3.1 Commentators Marvin Samuels Marvin used to be a trainer and one time manager and as a result can appreciate the finer arts of wrestling. Marvin is the face commentator of the WPWF and takes his work very seriously. He is known to get highly emotional during the matches. Marvin is always compassionate and has a big soft spot for the underdogs and the down and outs of the federation.
"The Icon" Shawn Steele Shawn was once a highly successful flamboyant wrestler. He quit after announcing that there was no more competition for him in the e-wrestling world. Always liking the sound of his own voice, he decided to turn his attention to commentary and was quickly approached and signed by the
WPWF as their heel commentator. Shawn was once a major player in a variety of federations and is always on the look out to extend his influence on the WPWF and its wrestlers.
3.2 In-ring reporters Bernard Fedderwinkle Bernard is the host of Saturday Night Style's Boiler Room Banter. Bernard is a very neutral character who doesn't sway to either heels or faces. Bernard's show can often get very physical as it often has more than one guest. Bernard likes to promote feuds on his show but does have a tendency to swell some wrestlers' egos and then deflate them with his sharp condescending wit.
**NB Bernard cannot be used in roleplays. A formal request must be made to appear on his show on Style. 3.3 Roving Reporters All roving reporters are freely usable in roleplays. HOWEVER, they are not to be attacked unless given the consent by any of the presidents.
If you do, you will be disciplined appropriately. Jade Nightingale Jade is a prim and proper lady who takes her job very seriously. She has a big future ahead of her in the wrestling business. Being an independent, career minded woman, Jade will not tolerate any form of harrassment from the wrestlers although she will not be offended by the odd chat up line. Jade is a valued employee of the president Gabriel but does not get alone with the leering vice president Rick.
Cherry Divine Cherry has made her way up the career ladder by her stunning looks and flirtatious ways. Cherry was hired by Rick behind Gabriel's back and as a result Cherry reports to Rick only. A well known jezebel in the back,
Cherry is known for her uncanny ability to obatin the dirtiest pieces of information from the wrestlers. Cherry and Jade absolutely despise each other and have been known to catfight in the back.
**NB Both women can be used in roleplays to generate some heat for the wrestler. 3.4 Referees Norman Watts Norman Watts was bullied as a child and still is by the wrestlers. Norman favours the good guys and is known to crack under pressure. Antonio Rivera Many heel wrestlers have found fame and fortune due to the bribable hands of the ruthless Antonio Rivera. Not surprisingly, he was appointed by the vice president Rick Raymond.
Sam Danson Sam is one of the most respected officials in our sport today. He is uneffected by outside inteference and plays religously by the rules. An excellent referee if ever there was one.
3.5 Ring Announcers Bob Bobson, Jim Jimson and Rob Robson These three like minded individuals have incredible vocal skills due to their immense training in operatic singing. On special occasions they are known to work together and sing the ring introductions and national anthem at pay-per-views. It must be noted that Rob Robson is a ladies man, a fact that the other two do not appreciate.
4. Cards 4.1 Weekly Events Wednesday Night War This show is the secondary weekly event in the WPWF. It is a two hour show which will contain a number of dark matches and two live matches, hour one main event and hour two main event. Although titles may get defended on War, it is most unlikely that they will actually change hands, although, having said this it is not impossible.
Saturday Night Style Saturday Night Style is the premier WPWF event. It is a perfect blend of flamboyance, excitement and antagonistic titillation. This is where title will be defended and of course the Style title will be defended every week at the end of hour one. This is where feuds and angles will develop. Due to the obvious rivalry between the president and vice president, the show is split into two, hour long, halves. The first hour is run by Rick Raymond, Rick will run the show in the first hour, he will make the matches and generally call the shots. The second hour is run by the loveable Gabriel Skinner. Expect two very contrasting hours of sports entertainment, every week on Style.
4.2 Pay-Per-View events January - New Year's Revolution The year in the WPWF kicks off with a bang with this grudge match type pay-per-view. Last years unresolved feuds are settled to make way for the new and fresh. This is Rick Raymond's favourite pay-per-view as all the matches are action packed and heated. All titles except the Style title will be defended.
March - Spring Time Slaughter This event is the most dangerous, barely legal, event on pay-per-view all year, that puts even the UFC to shame. Every match is the hardest of hardcore and thanks to Rick Raymond's sick and twisted mind the matches promise to be sadistic to the extreme. This event is designed to end the feuds that are too bitter to be settled any other way. Every title will be defended, the main event will be the Hardcore title.
May - Stairway To Heaven Get lots of beer in for this one as this is a marathon event. At Stairway To Heaven, mini tournaments will be held to decide the number one contenders for each and every title. The best of the best will be chosen to compete in the tournaments for a shot at the respective titles at the showpiece event Resolution.
July - Resolution This is the showpiece event of the WPWF. Everyone from the owners to the drink dispensers look forward to this event. This will be glamorous, prestigous and filled with celebrity special guests and of course thrilling matches from start to finish. All titles will be defended here and every year a special match will take place.
September - ?????? This pay-per-view is kept very much top secret. Wrestlers are not made aware of matches until the week before. This pay-per-view has left many a jaw dropped as the matches are filled with surprised and stipulations.
November - The Day Of Reckoning The last pay-per-view of the year and this is an event which truly is the Day Of Reckoning for all feuds. Again all titles will be defended here and two special wild card, Survivor Series type matches will take place.
5 Titles 5.1 Style Title This title is owned by Rick Raymond. It gives newcomers and youngsters the chance to gain a reputation and some prestige. Rick has decided that the title will be defended every week as he quickly loses patience in his champions.
5.2 Tag Team Titles The tag team titles are what every tag team strives for. These can be defended at either War, Style or a PPV. On running tag team feuds will results in gimmick matches to resolve them.
5.3 Extreme Title Only the sickest, bravest and stupidest wrestlers aim for this title. Matches are often filled with blood and guts. Wrestler's careers have been known to end prematurely in these matches. Warning, this title is not for the weak at heart.
5.4 Intercontinental Title This title is known as the ultimate stepping stone towards the main prize of the WPWF. Only the most promising and dedicated wrestlers will be considered for the IC title but those who win it are truly destined to become one of the greats.
5.5 WPWF World Title This title belt is Gabriel Skinner's most prized possesion. Only future hall of famer's will win this title. Gabriel is always honoured to hand this title over to those who are worthy of representing the WPWF. Rick
Raymond has tried for many years to get one of his Style proteges to hold the title, if this ever occurs...
6. Wrestler Involvement 6.1 Roleplays Roleplays are the what make the backbone of the federation and are what make it enjoyable for the e-wrestlers. A roleplay put simply, is when your wrestler is talking about the forthcoming matches and general events in the federation. The roleplay consists of a setting where the wrestler is speaking, this could be anywhere from the changing rooms before a match to an old abandoned graveyard. Then comes the dialogue of the roleplay, this is where the wrestler needs to get his point across clearly, accessible and in character. Roleplays will be judged on quality NOT quantity. You don't need to write a 20 page manuscript to win a fight. Short and snappy roleplays are often the best.
Roleplays must not be unrealistic, they must not have references to real wrestlers or federations and must contain no racism or unnecessary swearing. For more information please e-mail the president, if you are new to the world of e-wrestling the president will be glad to help out.
6.2 Strats Strats, short for strategy. These involve any actions that you wish your wrestler or manager to perform during the match. Strats can also be used to perform sneak attacks. If a person sends in a tactical strat for their fight and their opponent doesn't then the person who sends in the strat will probably win.
6.3 Angles An angle is something that the federation needs to add a high level of excitement. Angles are used to describe on going story lines and to justify sneak attacks and other things a wrestler would not normally do.
6.4 In-ringers In-ringers are the same as roleplays only conducted on the event. These are used to create heat for your wrestler and to insult his opponent.
Note that these will not count towards your match. You can only send in an in-ring if you have roleplayed that week.
