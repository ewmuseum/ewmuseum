Atemi - Strike to a vital point Set Up Move: (Repeated shoulders to opponents ribcage. ) Finisher: Uncontrolled RAGE (Uppercut followed by a right-left hook combo ) Trauma Fighting Title History: n/a Trauma Fighting Award History: n/a Other Federations: n/a Other Federation Awards: n/a Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Bio DaShea Takeo Strong was born on the night of March 20th, 1980 in the back of his fathers' pickup truck. His parents were on their way back to Johnstown, Pennsylvania from their relatives' house, when DaShea's mother, Angei, went into labor. DaShae's father, Takeo, was there to deliver the child, and he was the one who rushed both of them to the Lee Regional hospital in downtown Jonstown. DaShae grew up in a third-story apartment, high over the town of Johnstown. DaShea was the normally good kid... That is, until his teenage years. Every now and then, DaShea would have random fits of rage, ranging from just being uncontrollably mad, to punching holes through the walls in his room. DaShea got through all of this though, and by the time he turned 18, he was a full-grown man, heading for a 4 year stint at the University of Pittsburgh, where he would study Human Pyschology. One night, after one of his later classes, he returned to his dorm room at about 11:15 at night. His roomate informed him that both his mother and father had been killed in a driveby shooting walking home from one of their nephew's basketball games, held in the park not too far from the Strong household. This news was the 'straw that broke the camel's back'. His uncontrollable fits of rage returned, and he was later expelled from his college and thrown in jail for an assault and battery sentence. He later got out of jail, but with a vengeance. That is why he's here in Trauma Fighting... To let out all of the caged up anger he bottled up in prison.
