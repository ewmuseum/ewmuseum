Damon 'The Black Panther' Washington Real Name:Damon Washington Birthdate: 1/6/83 Hometown: Chicago Height: 6`3 Weight: 235 lbs. Nicknames: The Black Panther Status: Face Manager(s): Sam Rock Current Teams: none Entrance Music: Murder Rap-Insane Clown Posse Fighting Style: Freestyle wrestling and Street fighting Signature Moves: shoulder tackle, running elbow, grab head and knee to face, step side kick to chin, right hook, uppercut, punch to throat, arm bar, rear naked choke, choke hold Set Up Move: The Beginning of the End (He locks in a full nelson and lifts his opponent off of the ground until his opponent is close to tapping out then he just drops them to the ground. ) Finisher: The Fury (As his opponent lays on the ground Damon straddles there chest with there arms pinned to there side so they cannot get out. Damon then begins to slam his elbows into his opponents face until they pass out or tap out ) Trauma Fighting Title History: Trauma Fighting Award History: Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Handler Information Handler Name: Erik AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Bio Damon Washington was born to a moman at Loyola hospital in Illinois. That is all he knows about his past, his true past. Until he was thirteen Damon thought he was the child of William and Tina Washington, two working class people living on the south side of Chicago. They managed to give him a great childhood despite where they lived and Damon always loved his life. Since he turned thirteen though a rage began to build up in him, a rage that he found only one release for...wrestling. His parents first noticed that may help his rage one day when they were watching him fight with a neighborhood boy and they saw the rage in his face leave for only a few seconds. It was within that few seconds though that they realized they had to keep it up so one day Damon would not end up in jail. They found a free style wrestling camp and immediately sent him there. That is where he went three times a week and one weekend day. Damon kept that up until he made it to High School where he joined the wrestling team and was put on Varsity that very same day. Through high school everything looked like Damon was doing better but inside he was torn apart with the thoughts of his parents and why they would put him up for adoption. On the inside he was always sad, lonely, but mostly angry. He channeled all of those emotions into his wrestling and became one of the greatest wrestlers his school had ever seen. On the day of graduation though instead of annoucing which college he would be going to since he had his pick of schools, he just disappeared. He took his diploma and left to search out his parents. He has had people searching the entire U.S. but he personally searched all around Illinois. He occasionally will pop up at his parents house but all those lead to are fights so he tries to stay away from there as much as possible. He mostly has been staying with Sam Rock a man he met when he left for the first time. Damon stumbled into his gym and has been getting taught by him ever since. Sam was a former all star wrestler that was not able to continue because of a steroid addiction that was found out by wrestling officials before he went to college. As Damon trains with Sam, he works at a night club called Rip Rocks in order to make ends meet and make money for his search. Than he fights in an underground fighting promotion called Stix where he is the reigning champion. Roleplays Archives The News The News Quotes
