Concept Trauma Fighting, is a diabolical scheme that skipped through Mr. Adriano Kaitos, who himself was a professional fighter. Adriano wanted to always make something huge that nobody could top, and with the launch with an unknown date set, for the year 2003, his plan would un-ravel. Trauma Fighting is a name delivered to you, the fans of tF show that the level of action this brand new fighting organization will very soon bring. Adriano Kaitos, who is a Greek Fighter has won many championships among Greece and other countries and has settled down, and with financial help has established this organization to prove that he was set out for something. tF will bring you all hard-hitting, fierce, competitive martial art's action among the best in the business. American, Canadian or any foreign country has welcomed it's doors for the best fighters to do action for the most competitive brawls that will be shown on Direct TV, or any satellite provider. These shows will prove how gruesome fighting can be, and how really professional fighting isn't just entertainment, it's a business. People running in and out of the so-called "Cage" to do battle is the style of sport that America has loved, and will soon continue to. tF however can't start off on the top, and plans to bring action to put itself into the highest spot of being the highest rated federation among the globe. Fighters have been invited, welcomed and asked to compete, as the showcased talent within this league will be completely unstoppable. The brutal fighting world has been shown, but with tF, the world is about to be blown as the fighting industry is about to skyrocket in anticipation for co-owner Sean Williams' and a long time friend to launch a plan. This however, isn't JUST a plan, it's a complete company that will never ever be touched, the fierce competitors that have been selected and invited will not be seen on UFC, PRIDE or any other fight organization, if they have not already. The four men that plan to run this federation, and whose names have been classified except for the two front men; Mr. Kaitos and Mr. Williams have been working hard to establish this project. The hype around America is surrounding as the new thing if tF, watch out for the killer show in the near future as nothing has hit Direct TV this hard, the anticipation is growing by the day. The purchase of the satellite's companies product, has sky rocketed as the four channels, completely covering tF have been all ordered, over 2 million homes in North America have asked for this, and soon everybody in the world will be introduced. Are you ready for Trauma?
