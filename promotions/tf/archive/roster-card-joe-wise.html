Sarah Fletcher’s Baby was adopted two days later by a middle aged Couple, Martha and Brian Wise. They raised the child as if it were there own, naming him Joseph Wise. Joseph Lived a happy life with the Wise Family until the tender age of 5, when Martha’s life on earth was terminated by a fatal Car-Crash. Brian Wise fell quickly into depression and was no longer the loving father he once was, but a violent alcoholic. The Only light in Brian’s life was at the bottom of an empty bottle. Brian took his anger out on little Joseph and often beat him, but some how Joseph still managed to attend the local school. Apart from during school hours, Joseph was never in the presence of another human bieng, as father, Brian’s home was now the local bars. But one day Joseph’s Life changed, whilst left at home, Joseph did something he had been forbidden to do by his father. Joseph turned on the television. A bright light flashed and the word’s “WWF: World Wrestling Federation” appeared onto the screen. Joseph was amazed, never before had he seen such things, it made him wonder why his father had been so persistent as to constantly warn him not to watch television. Joseph became enchanted by Wrestling and tuned in for several hours a day whilst his father was at the bars. He even skipped school on several occasions to watch the Sport. And further more, Joseph discovered there were more publications than WWF, there was also another company named ‘tF: traumaFIGHTING' Joseph preferred this company and became an avid watcher of the show. At Age 15 Joseph started High School and was amazed to discover they had a wrestling team, beore you could say “hallooballoo” Joseph was enrolled and pinning his first opponent. Joseph was a natural and wrestled in competitions right the way through High School and College. On January 12 2003, Joseph was competing in the “Coca-Cola Annual Amatuer Wrestling Competition”, After hard training and many hard opponents Joseph made his way to the final where he was faced against a young wrestler named Billy Josper. Sadly Josper won, and was immediately approached by Talent Seekers from WWF. Joseph hung his head in shame, he knew he could of won.
