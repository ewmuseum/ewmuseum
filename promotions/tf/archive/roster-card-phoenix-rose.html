Phoenix Rose Real Name:Marcus Steiner Birthdate: 26th September 1978 Hometown: Manchester, England (currently resides in Tampa Bay, Florida) Height: 5 feet 10 Weight: 220 lbs. Nicknames: Rosey Status: Face Manager(s): N/a Current Teams: Phoenix places his left arm around opponents and bends opponents arm up and over his left arm. Phoenix drops down as if to be going for a neck breaker, driving the arm in to the mat hard, thus injuring the whole arm. Entrance Music: Private eye - Alkaline trio Fighting Style: Brawling athleticism. A mix of professional and amature wrestling moves along with a twist of Jiu-Jitsu Signature Moves: Set Up Move: The Ash Tray (Bicep curl toss ) Finisher: Rosearana (Opponent standing, Phoenix jumps up, pulling opponents right arm, he wraps his legs around the head and armpit of opponent in a guillotine choke hold before pulling himself up to be sat on opponents shoulder, he dives off the shoulders and pulls opponent in to single arm DDT, then locks in full body scissors. ) Trauma Fighting Title History: N/a Trauma Fighting Award History: N/a Other Federations: EyE, OWL, tFZ, DWF, ACW, RUSH Other Federation Awards: OWL 2 time outright champion, EyE gold champion Handler Information Handler Name: Sc00t AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Born as a normal child, playful, happy, carefree, Phoenix, Or Marcus as he was known then, would joyfully play with his many friends in Manchester England. He came from a poor family. But as a small child most poor children just fit in. As soon as you hit those teen years however it is all change. Expensive clothes are a must if you wish to fit in, a nice house, ‘cool’ car. All the material things become the important things. He stole. He was a thief. He stole from the rich, from the poor and he gave only to himself. Purchasing material things. The ‘in’ trainers, ‘that’ top, everything a teenager needs to get places in their own little world called high school. Another ‘in’ thing at the time was weed Pot Marijuana Grass And of course, he wanted to be cool. Who doesn’t at that age? So he started to smoke weed, which led to the harder stuff, which led to addiction. His career in theft would grow and grow until eventually he was caught not only in the middle of a mugging, but also with quite a large amount of heroin on his body. He was arrested and convicted. Now with a short sentence of three years in prison he began to thrive. Fighting and dealing in drugs, he ruled his cellblock. He loved his new life on the inside, but soon came to the realisation that once he was out of prison, he really should ’Get his act together’. He started to slowly fade in to the background, became ‘just another inmate’ until his release. On his release he looked around at the doors of the prison and saw nobody. Not a soul in the world cared for him. His parents had shunned him; his friends had bailed. Homeless, jobless, penniless he would live on the streets for the next few years, at the time he would feel saddened by people passing him by. Now a days he would love to go back there. Fighters Career History: After fighting his way through his short prison sentence he left the prison jobless, penniless, friendless and jobless. For almost three years he slept on the streets, fighting for money. He was scouted by the FWF who were looking to push their hardcore division at the time. From there onwards his ego and bank balance grew together, moving from FWF to DWF, from DWF to EyE, getting a champions pay check at EyE, from EyE on to OWL, once again getting a champions pay check, along with the money came the fame at OWL. Finally after OWL he decided it was the end of wrestling for him. And so it was... But fighting was in his blood... Return Rose. tF. the new home of the household superstar, Phoenix Rose.
