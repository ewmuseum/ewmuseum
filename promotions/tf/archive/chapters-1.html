Chapter One

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Jersey, USA: Adriano Kaitos is standing down the back of a narrow, dark alleyway. Smoke rises from his mouth and lifts up in to the air. Adriano is smoking a cigarette. It has been a long day for Adriano, a day that he would remember for the rest of his life. -=-=-=-=- 7:00am EST, New Jersey, USA: A phone lent against Adriano's ear as he made the final inquiries in to his large venture. "Yes, that is correct, a tall circular mesh wire cage." He said to the person on the other end of the line. "No. Real steel." "Uh-huh, positive." "So how much will that cost?" He asked as he placed the pen in his mouth and bit the lid of, spitting it to the desk. "26. Okay, that's a nice deal. You're delivering it, right?" "What? How am I expected to get a circular cage, canvas doors and outer shell from your workshop to my arena? You gotta be kidding!" "Whatever." Click. -=-=-=-=- 8:30am EST, New Jersey, USA: "Look man, I just need it for an hour." This time Adriano was on the telephone to a special friend of his. "Five hundred." "Damn it… Five fifty." "Done. I'll be round in thirty minutes, get it out of the parking lot for me." Click. Adriano took his feet from his desk and rummaged in his draw, pulling out a wad of bills, he removed five one hundred dollar bills and, as he stood up, stuffed them in to his pocket. -=-=-=-=- 9:00am EST, New Jersey, USA: Adriano reached the destination and parked up his Ford Pinto in the parking lot. He set his alarm and left the lot out on to the road. He took a few steps down the street until he reached a truck which would usually be used for transporting large amounts of wood. It was a long truck. A stocky man stood along side the truck, he held out a single key, Adriano paced up to him whilst digging in his pocket and removing the money. The two exchanged items and Adriano hopped in to the truck. He knew that very soon his adrenaline would rush as he attempted to illegally transport the huge steel object. He started up the engine. -=-=-=-=- 9:30am EST, New Jersey, USA: Adriano had recently backed the large truck in to a garage, the main compartment of the truck stuck out in to the road. He sat, waiting anxiously for the huge structure to be loaded on to the truck. A banging was heard on the side of the truck, signalling the completion of the loading. Adriano put pedal to the metal and thundered out of the garage and down the road. And there it was… The circular structure which the fighters of TRAUMA.fighting would be competing in. An unforgiving steel structure, A square wrought iron cage on the outside with 2 steel doors, one at each side which were welded in to the circular iron mesh cage on the inside. Attached at the bottom of the circular cage was a canvas similar to the style of modern day televised wrestling. The truck thundered over the horizon and out of vision. -=-=-=-=- 9:45am EST, New Jersey, USA: He sighed a sigh of relief as he backed 'the shell' in to his large warehouse. But now there was another drawback… How to get it off. -=-=-=-=- 10:18am EST, New Jersey, USA: "Thanks man, this means a lot. You shall be highly rewarded." "Excellent. I will see you here in a few hours." Click. -=-=-=-=- 1:07pm EST, New Jersey, USA: After almost half an hour 'the shell' had been removed from the truck and set in place in the centre of the warehouse floor. Benches, the sort you would expect to see at a high school football match, were set up against the walls around the warehouse, golden sand covered the floor, 2 ramps came one from each side of the warehouse and met at the doors of 'The Shell', this is where the competitors would make their entrances from. -=-=-=-=- 4:00pm EST, New Jersey, USA:. Adriano Kaitos is standing down the back of a narrow, dark alleyway. Smoke rises from his mouth and lifts up in to the air. Adriano is smoking a cigarette. It has been a long day for Adriano, a day that he would remember for the rest of his life…
