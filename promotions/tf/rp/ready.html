Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplays Main Phoenix Rose Entitled: Ready. Written: 12th March 2003 Location: The New Apartment. Killed off in SE, the world thought he was gone for good. People thought that the outcast Phoenix Rose would never be seen or heard of ever again. But this wasn’t true. Phoenix Rose wasn’t dead. He wasn’t buried. It was all a lie, a ruse to make people forget about him. Until now… Months have passed. Months of living in the gym training for the day he would return to a ring. This wasn’t a ring. This… This was a steel hell. This was trauma fighting. This… Was… Fighting… --- Phoenix sat in a section of his new luxury apartment located in the western section of Tampa Bay, Florida. The room was long and narrow, on one side of the room a long stretch of windows, floor to ceiling looked out on to a beach view. It was twilight now; a lone surfer caught the last wave of the night before heading off home. Phoenix held a bottle of mineral water. A rare occurrence in the home of Phoenix Rose. As any Rose fan would know Phoenix drank nothing but Pepsi in the past. He drank it so often people thought he had a sponsorship deal with them. Mineral water? No way. Phoenix screwed the cap on his bottled water and stood up. He had been sat on a small stool observing the surfer. A new past time of his ever since he was given his apartment by Adriano Kaitos, tF owner. --- Now we enter the seating room, Phoenix Rose takes a seat, leaning his arms on the armrests. He felt the aching subside for a few moments. Months of intense training day in day out for over 12 hours each day had made his muscles large, that much was true. But on top of that they ached and throbbed. But he knew he was ready. As he flicked through the multiple cable channels provided by Adriano Kaitos he let off a grin. He knew. He knew it so much that he said it out loud. “I’m ready.” :~Fin~:
