Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplays Main Colin Gear Entitled: An all new low. (Part 1) Written: 13/2/03 Location: Some Council House, Glasgow, Scotland Colin Gear, a man who hit an all time high a few months ago being cWo Lightweight Champion and Head Booker had just had a fall from grace. It turns out his booking decisions were not exactly ratings grabbers and cWo had to shut down. Once again Colin Gear found himself jobless. “Why am I evening trying anymore? Any time I make an effort I end up with zero respect and a whole hell of a lot of back stabbing” He thought to himself while downing another beer. “I mean it isn’t my fault my opponents got injured they just weren’t trained enough! Is that reason enough to be jobbed out a company?” Colin stood up and looked at himself in the mirror. He shook his head and picked up his beer and just kept staring at himself in the mirror. “Look at yourself! You’re a mess and you don’t know why. You weren’t even good at your peak and you’ll never be missed when you’re gone. The only thing you’ll ever be remembered for is the death of cWo…that is unless you have other ideas.” He sat back down and took another drink of his beer. He was indeed a broken and lonely. For the first time in his life he was finally considering giving up the sport that he loved and has put 7 years of his life into it. All that time he was never respect by the boys in the back or even by the crowd. Sure there were some matches the crowd got into but he was never more than a curtain jerker, a jobber if you will. To be honest he sucked.
