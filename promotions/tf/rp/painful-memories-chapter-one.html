Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplays Main DaShea Strong Entitled: Painful Memories--Chapter One Written: 03/04/03 Location: Johnstown, Pennsylvania Thud, thud, thud, thud. The sound of DaShea Strong’s left fist crashing into a punching bag. The sound of his fist is all that fills the air of the third story apartment where he spent most of his life. This was the last place he would ever see his mother and father again. It was August 22nd, of his freshman year of college. He was all set to get to Pittsburgh, but he was all the readier to stay home and continue his life in Johnstown, where his friends, and more importantly, his girlfriend, reside. Thud, thud, thud, thud. DaShea’s right hand hits the punching bag, only this time the impact is more fierce! His dark brown eyes glare at the punching bag with every burst of impact of his fist. Now, his mind focuses onto that one fateful night when his roommate would inform him that his parents were no longer alive. Now, the rhythm of his punches get faster and more intense as time goes by. DaShea starts to perspire profusely, and the rhythm of his fists crashing into the punching bag gets louder and more intense! Thud, thud, thud, ka-pow! He thinks back... He can see the gun stretching out of the window. He can visualize the first bullet being fired and hitting his mother square in the chest, killing her instantly. He sees her drop to the ground, dying before she even hits. Then, he sees the murderer loading the second bullet, the one that would victimize his father. He sees the second bullet fired, and hitting his fathers head on impact, killing him immediately also. DaShea sees the murderers flee away in their 1989 Chevy, flying down the street, through a red light, and out of sight. Thud, thud, thud, WHAM! DaShea’s final blow sends the punching bag straight to the ground. He looks down, and sees the face of the gunman who killed both his mother and father many many years ago... Now, the pain and anger inside him are too much to keep inside himself. He picks up the 100+ lb. punching bag, and with no struggle whatsoever, hurls it through the side window of the apartment, sending shattered glass everywhere. DaShea looks down onto the sidewalk so far down below him, and sees the now deformed punching bag, with half of its stuffing spilled out onto the road, and smiles, thinking that he has just settled the anger within him... But he is wrong... Oh so very wrong, indeed...
