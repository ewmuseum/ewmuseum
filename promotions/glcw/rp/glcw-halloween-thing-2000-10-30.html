GLCW Roleplay Archive

Halloween Thing

30 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open up on a scene in progress. We see Perro Culero and MoonGirl, side by side, dressed in street clothes that're dripping wet from the storm outside. They look to be very, very confused. We cut to a shot of Eddy
Jacks, wearing an old suit and a bald cap. Behind him is a number of other
GLCW superstars, all flamboyantly dressed- this includes Killdozer, TORA
Wanizame, Egg Baby, CAC*CAC, Eddy Jacks Junior, Curmudgeon, an annoyed Jamie
Randall, David $mith, CUCO Travieso, EMWC Mask, Mike Calhoun, Jeff Stewart,
Dave Ritter, Rob James, Bruise Bombast, and the Tex Violence Girls. A familiar tune starts.]
EJ: It's astounding... Time is... fleeting Madness... takes its toll But listen closely. [Timmy Van Rocketz appears, wearing a frizzy red wig and a french maid's uniform.] TVR: Not for very much longer EJ: I've got to... keep control I remember! Doing the Time Warp! Drinking! Those moments when The blackness would hit me! [The hell?] EJ & TVR: And the void would be calling! [The entire roster sings in unison.] GLCW: LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [Man, is Killdozer into this.] GLCW: LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [Everyone dances. TORA, Egg Baby, and CAC*CAC are especially into it. Jamie Randall, however, seems to be pretty grumpy. We quickly cut to Andy
Wilczak, who's dressed in a suit and tie.]
AW: It's just a jump to the left... GLCW: And then a step to the right! [Nijikon steps to the right!] AW: With your hands on your hips... GLCW: You bring your knees in tight! But it's the pelvic thrust, That really drives you insane LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [Cut to Timmy Van Rocketz, who sidles up to the camera like this was the role he's been waiting his entire life to play.]
TVR: It's so dreamy... Oh, fantasy free me So you can't see me No, not at all. In another dimension... With voyeuristic intention Well secluded, I see all [Frazer Boy wanders onto the set, and after letting everything sink in for a few moments, a horrified look comes across his face. He tries to run across the room to his friends TORA and CAC*CAC, but Nijikon trips him, and Frazer
Boy takes a face-plant, causing Nijikon to laugh heartily.]
EJ: With a bit of a mind flip... [Tex Violence walks into the shot, casts an odd glance in CAC*CAC's direction, and walks away.]
TVR: You're into the time slip. EJ: And nothing... can ever be the same. TVR: You're spaced out on sensation... EJ: Like you're under sedation! GLCW: LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [MoonGirl looks awfully confused, and matters aren't helped much when Curmudgeon comes charging out of the crowd, spearing Perro backwards, and the momentum carries them both backwards out through the front door, leaving
MoonGirl behind, the sole witness to this bizarre spectacle.]
GLCW: LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [Enter Danger Boy, wearing a short brown wig and a hastily assembled French
Maid's uniform, his mask still on.]
DB: Well I was walking down the street Just having think When a snake of a guy gave me evil winks He shooks-a me up, he took me by surprise He had a pick up trucks and devil's eyes He stared at me, I felt change Time mean nothing, never would again [Calhoun gives Danger Boy an odd look for botching his lines, but before he can correct him, CAC*CAC comes along and socks him one right in the groin!
Calhoun goes down like a bad habit, and, fearing for his safety, Jeff Stewart quickly leaves the set, leaving CAC*CAC triumphant.] GLCW: LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [Go, Killdozer, go!] GLCW: LET'S DO THE TIME WARP AGAIN! [Back to Wilczak, who's standing up on his desk.] AW: It's just a jump to the left! [Cut to Rob James, Eddy Jacks Junior, and CUCO Travieso, who are all happily singing along, and pretending not to notice CUCO's attempt to lip-sync.]
GLCW: And then a step to the right AW: With your hands on your hips. GLCW: You bring your knees in tight But it's the pelvic thrust That really drives you insane Let's do the Time Warp again Let's do the Time Warp again [Suddenly, Nijikon charges across the room and takes TORA down with a kouppo kick! Egg Baby, CAC*CAC, and Killdozer all chase him away! MoonGirl begins spinning around, or maybe the room begins spinning around her, and she eventually begins walking backwards, as the music ends.]
MoonGirl: Who the hell are all of you? [Suddenly, it occurs to us that MoonGirl has her back up against an elevator shaft, and someone is coming down it as we, uh speak...
Someone wearing very high heels... Someone with thick, tree-trunk like legs... Someone with... Oh, God, I'm going to be sick... Not just someone, but someone's Fathah- The Robfathah, at that. In drag. With a black wig on. And a ton of make-up. Oh, man, I don't think you need any more details.]
MoonGirl: Uhh... [Suddenly, more music begins to play, and The Robfathah begins to sing, unfortunately. The entire cast of characters is still on hand, except for
Jamie Randall, who had something better to do.]
RC: How do you do? I see you've met... my faithful handyman He's just a... little brought down, because When you knocked, He thought you were the... candyman! Don't get strung out, by the way I look Don't judge a book by its cover I'm not much of a man, by the light of day But by night I'm one hell of a lover! I'm just a sweet transvestite! From Transexual, Transylvania! [MoonGirl looks over her shoulder just in time to see Agent Chambers, dressed in the same street clothes Perro had on earlier. Chambers walks up to MoonGirl's side, keeping his eyes off of The Robfathah. MoonGirl blinks.
but Chambers pays her no mind.] RC: Let me show you around, maybe play you a sound You look like you're both pretty groovy Or if you want something visual That's not too abysmal We could take in an old Steeve Reeves movie AC: I'm... glad we... caught you at home Could we... use your... phone? We're both... in a bit... of a hurry MoonGirl: Right! AC: We'll... just say where... we are Then go... back to the... car We don't... want to be... any worry [MoonGirl turns and looks over at Agent Chambers, her confusion growing.] RC: Well you got caught with a flat? Well how 'bout that Well babies don't you panic! By the light of the night! It'll all seem alright, I'll get you a Satanic mechanic! I'm just a sweet transvestite... From Transexual, Transylvania! Why don't you stay for the night? [On over to Eddy Jacks.] EJ: Night! RC: Or maybe a bite [Now to Danger Boy.] DB: Bites! RC: I could show you my favorite obsession. I've been making a man... With blonde hair and a tan. And he's good for relieving my... tension. I'm just a sweet transvestite From Transexual, Transylvania! Hit it! Hit it! I'm just a sweet transvestite! [Now everyone!] GLCW: Sweet Transvestite! RC: From Transexual... [MoonGirl blinks.] RC & GLCW: Transylvania! [In the background, TORA gets back up to his feet and tackles Nijikon from behind, and the two go tumbling out of the picture.]
RC: So come up to the lab... And see what's on the slab... I see you shiver with antici...pation But maybe the rain! Isn't really to blame... So I'll remove the cause... [The Robfathah laughs.] RC: But not the symptom! [The shot slowly pulls back, as our story continues. Unfortunately, this is all the time we have... for now.]
