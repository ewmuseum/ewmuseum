Press Conference: CWLA Closure, New Arena

13 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The shot pans over a sea of reporters, each vying for position close to the makeshift platform that was erected for this press conference. Upon the table is a podium, flanked on either side by a table. The overhead lights reflect off of the small golden placards that mark each seat, the camera slowly panning across each...
Jeff Marcus ... Rick Kilgore ... Andrew [name withheld] ... Sasha [name withheld] ... Z ... The throng before the table seem most interested in the one unfamiliar placard, specifically, the lone letter it bears.
A barrage of questions erupt from the press as the representatives of Great Lakes Championship Wrestling make their way to the table. Chief of Security Jeff Marcus and hired-gun Rick Kilgore both in black suits and ties, Kilgore looking more than uncomfortable.
Co-President [name withheld] wears a similar suit, sans tie. Beside him is Sasha [name withheld], the stunning Co-President commanding the attention of all those in the room in a leopard print Halston suit.
That attention is suddenly drawn away from the GLCW's femme fatale and focused on the last member of the entourage, former Championship
Wrestling of Los Angeles co-owner, the man known simply as ... Zodiac. As the others take their seats on either side of the podium, [name withheld] stands in the center, raising his hands to indicate that the press should settle. [name withheld] and Kilgore sit to his right, Zodiac and Jeff
Marcus to his left. [name withheld] waits for the others to sit, then adjusts the microphone.]
AW: I'd like to thank you all for being here today. Most of you are familiar with the members of the GLCW represented, and as I'm sure you all have some idea as to why you're here, we'll dispense with the formalaties and get right to business.
Championship Wrestling of Los Angeles, the CWLA, has been forced to close. This comes as a tremendous blow to the CCWA, as we were in the midst of discussions regarding our first pay-per-view, scheduled in
March. However, all is not lost. [He pauses, clearing his throat.] AW: When the first hint of trouble with the CWLA came to the attention of myself and President [name withheld], we informed the management of the CWLA that we would do anything in our power to aid them. That aid included the possibility of the federation folding, which it obviously has.
Because of our agreement with the CWLA, ladies and gentlemen, the GLCW will at long-last have an arena to call it's own. The old CWLA Centre is being dismantled piece-by-piece as we speak, and will be reconstructed right here in Ann Arbor! That's right ... Ann Arbor,
Michigan. [Another volley of questions is quickly fired from the crowd, [name withheld] once again motioning for them to be patient. Sasha [name withheld] then rises from her seat and takes her place at the podium, a hushed exchange of words between she and [name withheld] before he sits.]
SE: House always wins. [she smiles at the press] You might not think so, given the circumstances here, but its a creedo I live by. The GLCW gains an arena, something it's wanted and needed for quite some time, especially now. For those of you who are GLCW fans, you know full well that our roster is increasing in both size _and_ talent.
The CWLA, while it's true ... is no more, several of its wrestlers have been offered contracts within the GLCW. We plan on retaining many of its non-wrestling staff as well.
[[name withheld] nods in agreement, sitting back in his chair watching his partner.] SE: On January 27, 1999 ... Caesar's Palace in Atlantic City, New Jersey will host the GLCW's 'All-Stars of Wrestling'. This is a first for both the GLCW and Caesar's. The GLCW has never held a venue outside the state of Michigan, and Caesar's has never hosted an event like this. Preparations are in the works, and I can assure you - 'All- Stars of Wrestling' will be the _hottest_ ticket on the Boardwalk.
[[name withheld] glances over to Zodiac, meeting his gaze with her own as she inclines her head, acknowledging him. Looking back at the press, she offers up a dazzling smile, causing another round of flashes to go off.]
SE: This acquisition brings another big win to the GLCW, that coming in the form of it's new Commissioner, the former co-owner of the CWLA ...
Zodiac. [Zodiac rises along with [name withheld], as [name withheld] steps back from the podium
... extending her hand to the new Commissioner as the reporters and photographers begin throwing out queries and taking pictures. [name withheld]
then shakes Zodiac's hand, placing an arm around the man's shoulder as the three stand side by side. The buzzing ceases as the GLCW owners take their seats, giving Zodiac center stage. Zodiac, decked out in black
slacks and a shirt and tie, looks to the press. He's silent for a brief moment and then beings.]
Z: I guess I have a few questions to answer. First and foremost, why the CWLA closed. It's not secret that a free-enterprise system like ours is a competitive market. One corporation opens up only to be swallowed by another shortly after. That was the fate of the CWLA. The extended hiatus that was felt over the holiday season came because co-owner Zach Carter suffered a mild heart attack. He was able to heal.
Unfortunately, his doctors advised that he take time off from the hustle and bustle of the wrestling world. [He pauses again.] Z: That left me as the sole runner of the federation. I'm a semi-retired wrestler myself. However, I didn't have the expertiese or the know-how to run the organization as well as Mr. Carter did. Thus, I felt it was the right decision. Los Angeles is a big city and the competition wanted the talent that we had. They were able to acquire some of the talent, but not all of it. As Ms. [name withheld] mentioned, contracts to current and former CWLA stars have been offered. Some have answered, but I won't give away
who. As far as the selling of the promotion goes, Mr. Cater, while sad to see his promotion go, knew it was the best decision.
[Zodiac pauses for a brief moment again.] Z: Now, let me talk about my role in the GLCW. As Ms. [name withheld] mentioned, I'm the new commissioner of the league. You can just look at me as the muscle in the front office. Some tend to believe it's okay to push around their superiors. I'm here to prevent that. I'm also in charge of a few more ideas. The moving of the old CWLA Centre was part of the deal that brought me here. When the GLCW Center opens in a few weeks, it will house the premier show of the newly formatted Motor City
Rewind. In connection to the old show, the new format of Motor City Rewind will be something similar to what the CWLA's Thursday Ultimate show promoted. It'll be a similar format with a few new twists and ideas. Apart from that, the show will also be the show that keeps the
GLCW fans up to date about the current dealings as far as the umbrella promotion of the CCWA is concerned.
[A few reporters offer up questions, but Zodiac holds up his hand in a signal for them to wait.]
Z: For my last announcement, Zodiac was the name I dubbed myself as a wrestler. My career, while not officially over, is pretty much done. I may don the tights in the future and I'll bring the name back into play.
However, from here on out... I'd like to be called Michael Yinessa. The GLCW is about business. I aim to assimilate myself into the GLCW Corporation. Thank you for your time. [Michael takes his seat again as reporters buzz with questions. [name withheld] takes the podium again, prepared to field them.] AW: We have time for a few questions ... [He points at a short, balding man in the front row.] REPORTER: Will _every_ GLCW show be held out of the GLCW Center, when it opens? [[name withheld] pauses, turning to Sasha briefly before answering.] AW: We're still working on the details of that ... when the Center opens in February, it will house every edition of Motor City Rewind -- as far as All-Stars of Wrestling goes, we'll be airing out of arenas throughout the upper mid-west and south-eastern Canada, however focusing on the
Great Lakes and the East Coast. [He points to a tall string-bean of a woman off to the right of the crowd.] REPORTER: Now that your company has gotten back into the spotlight, are we in for another name change?
[[name withheld] responds with a chuckle.] AW: No, no ... this federation will be known as the GLCW from here on out. [Before he can call for another question, the same reporter fires off a second one.] REPORTER: What about your deal with the CCWA? Isn't the amount of exposure the GLCW will be getting be in violation of this deal?
[[name withheld] arches a brow, adjusting his glasses.] AW: No ... not in the least. Are deals with the LWC and CLW are both rock-solid. In fact, i've been on the phone with LWC President
[name withheld] discussing the terms of this deal for the past several days. I assure you, this deal is going to go off without a hitch.
[[name withheld] turns to [name withheld] and Yinessa, and with a nod, the entire GLCW contingent rises.]
AW: I'm sorry, but we cannot field any further questions ... [Fade.]
