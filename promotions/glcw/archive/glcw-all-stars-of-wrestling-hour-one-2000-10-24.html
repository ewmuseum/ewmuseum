"Punk Bitch", Jamie Randall. The perpetually pissed off youngster's wearing what passes for his wrestling gear already- his fingerless black leather driving gloves, a pair of thoroughly torn-up jeans, and black wrestling shoes- along with a black t-shirt that bears the legend "Jamie Randal < You"

Blaze doesn't seem bothered by it, pulling Sanders up for another chokeslam, but Sanders manages to connect with a low-knee to free himself, falling backwards and grabbing onto the official to try and steady himself, only to bring him down with him!]
JS: Sanders just took down the ref... AND HERE COMES BARRINGER WITH A CHAIR! [_CRACK!_] MC: BOOYAH! JS: AND BARRINGER DENTS THE STEEL CHAIR OVER THE SKULL OF TERRY BLAZE! THE COVER BY SANDERS! 1.. 2.. [NO! This time, Rich Blaze breaks up the count- connecting with a dropkick to the forehead of Tim Sanders, and then scrambling out of the ring.]
MC: Damn you, Rich! JS: Rich Blaze breaks up the count... And Sanders makes the tag out to Matt
Barringer! [Sanders drags Terry Blaze up to his feet, twisting his arm to give Barringer an opening- which he takes, driving his elbow into Blaze's shoulder!] JS: If these two wanna finish this one off, they need to take advantage of
Terry while they can...
MC: And try and keep that runt Rich out of the picture! [Barringer sends Terry crashing into the opposite neutral corner, and then comes charging in after him, connecting with a big ol' avalanche in the corner, and then showing an impressive amount of strength with a nice
belly-to-belly suplex, taking Blaze down!] MC: YEAH! GET SOME, BARRINGER! GET SOME! JS: He's... Matt Barringer is going up top! [Barringer actually scales the turnbuckles with surprising quickness, and is soon perched up top.]
JS: Barringer off the top... SENTON ONTO TERRY BLAZE!! MC: OH MY GOD! JS: THE COVER! 1.. 2.. [NO! Blaze _powers_ out of this one, practically throwing Barringer off! Blaze starts to pull himself back up, and Barringer goes for the decapitation with a jumping sidekick- but Blaze pulls back, and Barringer crotches himself on the top rope!]
MC: NO! JS: TAG TO RICH BLAZE!! MC: NO!! [Rich enters the ring, jumps up onto the second rope, and knocks Barringer out of the ring with a perfectly executed dropkick!]
JS: Out goes Barringer! [Sanders charges back into the ring, and attempts to take Rich's head off with a running lariat, but Rich ducks it, and turns Sanders inside-out with an enzugiri!]
JS: AND HE NEARLY KNOCKS SANDERS' HEAD OFF! [Blaze goes to continue the offensive on Sanders, but neglects to keep an eye out for Barringer- who slides into the ring behind him and tries for a second jumping sidekick, which connects this time, knocking Blaze flat on his face!]
MC: YEAH! JS: AND HERE COMES TERRY BLAZE! [Terry goes for another spinebuster on Barringer, but Barringer manages to counter it with a huge DDT!]
MC: DDT ON TERRY! SHOTGUN THIS! JS: Here comes Rich! Barringer turns around... WILDSIDE SUPERKICK ON MATT BARRINGER! THE COVER! 1.. 2.. 3!! [DING! DING! DING!] MC: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! JS: OUT OF NOWHERE! THE BLAZE BROTHERS ARE THE NEW GLCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!! MC: THAT WAS THE FASTEST COUNT I'VE EVER SEEN! MC: If this wasn't highway robbery, I don't know what is! [The Blaze Brothers, both tired and sore, hold the GLCW tag team titles over their heads, showing them off to the fans. After a few moments of celebration, Rich Blaze motions for the house mic.]

Terry and I, we didn't come back to wrestling to lose in our big GLCW debut. We came back to show why we kick ass in any promotion that we set our sights on. Tell 'em how it is Terry."

Terry Blaze: "It's like Rich says. We came back to make a big splash in a big pond. Winning the GLCW tag straps does just that. If any tag team around here wants to step up, we're not hard to find."

JS: Rich and Terry Blaze are our new tag team champions, but there's no way in hell that this is the last time we're going to see them and The Ego Force going at it!
MC: Ego Force was robbed! Rich Blaze can say that the better team won all he wants to, it won't make it true!
