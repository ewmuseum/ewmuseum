GLCW Merry Christmas!

December 18, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Huge red letters set against a black background: GLCW PRESENTS IN ASSOCIATION WITH ROB JAMES DISCOUNT VIDEO PRODUCTIONS "MERRY XMAS TO ALL OF YOU" And then we segue into a well-furnished study, very similar to the reading room at the opening of an A & E's 'Biography'...only much cheaper-looking.
It appears that more pretentious titles ('Cents & Sensability,' 'Bleke House,' 'How to Win Friends & Inflewnce Peoples') have been taped onto thick, out-of-print AMC owner's manuals from the early 1980s. Also, it seems that the Oriental rug beneath the leather chair in the center of the room has been painted onto the floor; the paint stains along the bottom of the chair and the front of the bookshelves are a dead giveaway.
But, unless you're either Joel Tennuchi or [name withheld], you're not especially interested in the appearance of the room. You are, however, interested in the strange little man seated in the aforementioned leather chair, his feet dangling above the floor in the manner of a small child.
He's wearing a blue suit, red tie, white shirt, a black mask with a bunch of red !'s, and a gray beard glued around his jaw. On top of his head is a curious-looking stocking cap, leading us to wonder...could this be Kris
Kringle himself? But of course it's not. It's only the semi-legendary President Boy, a very hungry character who is quite possibly working this gig to get a next meal.
He's got a huge book in his small yellow hands, possibly another worthless auto manual, but we can't tell because it's wrapped in silver foil.]
PB: Listen babies. This is a big day, when all of you stop watching the dirty movies and the Friends show and kiss one another flat on both cheeks!
[He holds up the book.] PB: I am here to tell you a greatest story for the holiday! This is time for the big loving, all of yous, so snuggle up with your daddys and light the Chanukkah candles! GLCW, as part of the moral debt Andj Wilczak has accumulated with these filthy wrestling movies, owes PBS fifty minutes of the 'charity work!' So we all gots together and made this greatest special, which is like Chase Hunter because it is short and stupid!
[Long pause. He flips through the book.] Yes! [Another pause.] Okay, here is the story! It is very funny, babies, like Richard "Chris" Brue, and also you can learn a lesson! Like the lesson I learned about not rubbing bacon on my skin after the Kraken tried to eat me! Oh, babies, I was very scared! But let's go...
[We fade out and find ourselves in an upscale apartment. The Christmas season must've taken a real toll on Andj Wilczak's pocketbook, because he's reading a 1981 copy of Hilites for Children and eating directly from a can of Dinty Moore soup.
He looks to the ceiling in search some kind of sign that God has forgiven him for the morally repugnant status of GLCW, and, as if one cue, a popular
1980s New Wave hit begins to play in the background. What choice does the beloved fedhead have but to sing along?]
AW: "Bah, humbug!" No, that's too strong 'Cause it is my favorite holiday But all this year's been a busy blur Don't think I have the energy [He takes another spoonful of the soup, the screen goes momentarily blank, and then, through the miracle of televison, we're in the gym with Killdozer as he chokeslams a very convincing Jin Tao lookalike into the canvas.
'Dozer notes the presence of the camera, listens to the pounding New Wave rhythms in the background, and sings in unison with the downed Spirit
Warrior! KD & JT: To add to my already mad rush Just 'cause it's 'tis the season. The perfect gift for me would be Completions and connections left from [And now we're in Eddy Jacks Junior's Ottawa bedroom. Junior, nude save for his leopard-striped lycra speedo, is lying forward on the bed, legs crossed behind him in the air. He's staring intently at a not-so-recent issue of
Tiger Beat. An opened can of vaseline rests conspiciously on the nightstand to the left of the bed, just beneath a poster of Satin Sheets on which the word 'Goddess' has been written in red magic marker. The ever-ambiguous
Junior continues the improbable trend of singing superstars...] EJJ: Last year, ski shop, Encounter, most interesting. Had his number but never the time Most of 2001 passed along those lines. [He sighs and kisses the cover of the magazine. And then, just like that, we're backstage with a half-in-the-bag Tim Sanders. He's making short work of multiple red SOLO~! cups, but has more than enough time for a brief serenade.]
TS: So deck those halls, trim those trees Raise up cups of Christmas cheer, I just need to catch my breath, Christmas by myself this year [We cut outside as he takes another swig, find ourselves face-to-face with the much-maligned Ribera Kid. Already pissed off at his poor stateside reception, he furiously attempts to start his car in spite of the bitter
Michigan winter.] TRK: Calendar picture, frozen landscape, Chilled this room for twenty-four days, Evergreens, sparkling snow Get this winter over with! [Now the spotlight's back on Andj Wilczak, still nursing the can of soup and looking at a glamour photo of Tommy James, perhaps harboring secret ambitions of re-signing the popular "Mr. Workrate" for what we would be the grappler's record-breaking 65th term of duty with the fed.]
AW: Flashback to springtime, saw him again, Would've been good to go for lunch, Couldn't agree when we were both free, We tried, we said we'd keep in touch. [Now we're back with Jacks Junior, who is looking very ruefully at the back of his 'Rob James' Greatest Hits' betamax tape...]
EJJ: Didn't, of course, 'til summertime, Out to the beach to his boat could I join him? No, this time it was me, Sunburn in the third degree. [And then back on Tim Sanders, who has succeeded in refilling DACUP~! about a dozen times since we last saw him. His voice has begun to slur, but "Da
Priceless One" is as loud as ever.] TS: Now the calendar's just one page And, of course, I am excited Tonight's the night, but I've set my mind Not to do too much about it. [And he doesn't, passing out against the wall. Now, we're at a local pub, where Rob James--clad in a form-fitting red latex conductor's outfit--is leading Shane Andrews, Tracy Hudson, and Guh in song. The makeshift group, it might be added, sings it loud and sings it proud.]
Merry Christmas! Merry Christmas! But I think I'll miss this one this year. Merry Christmas! Merry Christmas! But I think I'll miss this one this year. Merry Christmas! Merry Christmas! But I think I'll miss this one this year. Merry Christmas! Merry Christmas! But I think I'll miss this one this year. [We go from being inside a bar to outside of one, as John Wayne "Gunnar" Maines, clad in his finest Judas Priest t-shirt, is thrown out of still another drinking establishment. He shakes a meaty fist at the proprietor of the establishment, then begins making his way down the road, the vast rips in his Jordache jeans widening with each step.]
JWM: Hardly dashing through the snow Cause I bundled up too tight Last minute have-to-do's A few cards, a few calls 'Cause it's r-s-v-p No thanks, no party lights It's Christmas Eve, gonna relax Turned down all of my invites. [He turns a wistful eye to pair of very masculine-looking street walkers, mumbling a vague 'hoo-hee' under his breath, before continuing with our timeless ballad.]
JWM: Last fall I had a night to myself, Same girl called, halloween party, Waited all night for her to show, This time her car wouldn't go, [Adding, again under his breath, 'remind me not ta git no toofless girl wit a Pinto; next time I'll order me one a' them e-mail brides from the
Yew-es-es-ar.'] Forget it, it's cold, it's getting late, Trudge on home to celebrate In a quiet way, unwind Doing Christmas right this time. [He reaches into the pocket of his trousers and produces a pornographic postcard that appears, from this angle, to involve a horse and a rather heavyset woman. But we haven't time for speculation about how Jay-Dubba's going to spend his Christmas Eve, because now we're at the Robfathah's dinner table. The largest turkey this side of whatever wrestling federation
[name withheld] has decided to open rests on the dinner table, but
Christie--dressed in his finest Ebenezer Huge topcoat and tails--regards it with contempt.]
RC: A&P has provided me With the world's smallest turkey Already out of the oven, nice and hot [He looks at the table, and slaps himself on the forehead for neglecting to purchase one crucial item.]
RC: Oh damn! Guess what I forgot? [And now the camera follows the flabulous Fathah as he races from the door of his apartment and down the street, passing Matthew Reason as the crazed
Reason, doing his best Phill Bennett impersonation, bangs his head repeatedly into the side of the building while cursing the south and its
'motorized homes.' Christie 'sings' as he 'runs.'] RC: So on with the boots, back out in the snow To the only all-night grocery [Christie charges into the grocery store, surging past a table offering 'Free Samples of Adult Diapers' that appears to be hosted by a certain 6-foot-10 rattlesnake eater, and seizes the cranberry sauce off a rack the young Leslie Jacks has been dutifully stocking. He then hurries to the cash register, where he runs smack into the Gremlin!]
G: When what to my wondering eyes should appear In the line is that guy I've been chasing all year! [Gremmy grabs Christie by the lapels of his coat, and, in very short order, heaves him into a rack of tabloid magazines! Christie does his best Bear
Brown impression, no-selling the heave, but the force of his girth sends the rack toppling over anyway!]
G: I'm spending this one alone, Need a break; this year's been crazy. [He knees Christie in the groin, leaving the manager doubled over in pain! Christie composes himself and...] RC: Me too, but why are you...? You mean you forgot cranberries too? [The Gremlin stands poised for yet another punch, but somehow, something about Christie's pathetic condition causes the Triple Crown champion's heart to grow, let's say, ten sizes or so. Gremmy reaches out a hand and embraces the Robfathah, or at least as much as any person without a 92" wingspan could hope to embrace Christie.]
G: Then suddenly we laughed and laughed Caught on to what was happening That Christmas magic's brought this tale To a very happy ending! [And then, as if on cue, the Rob James singers, Andj Wilczak, a loincloth-clad Eddy Jacks Junior, JW Maines, the Ribera Kid, a still-in-uniform Leslie Jacks, a barely-conscious Tim Sanders, and a diaper-wielding giant of man with a cigarillo clenched in his fierce Texan jaw all converge on the cash register! Needless to say, a lot of ludicrous dancing and singing ensues, with James and Junior engaging in the most ferocious bump n' grind this side of the pecos!]
EVERYBODY: Merry Christmas! Merry Christmas! Couldn't miss this one this year! Merry Christmas! Merry Christmas! Couldn't miss this one this year! [And, as the song begins to play out, we return to President Boy's study, where he still sits, round-faced and sleepy, on his leather chair.]
PB: And that, my babies, is the story of Christmas! Except I didn't see Jebus or the funky legend DA$ Money anywhere, and also I think John Wayne Maines is the unwashed, like Nyx! Only he is less hairful. [He closes the book, throws it onto the floor, and walks off. PHADE TO BLACK.]
