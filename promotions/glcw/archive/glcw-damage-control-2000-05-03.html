Damage Control (5/3/00)

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GLCW: Damage Control May 3rd, 2000 1. Frazer Fury 2. TORA Waziname 3. Tim Turner & Akira Saito 4. Agent Chambers 5. Nicholas Bailey 6. Rob James [The scene backstage is one of controlled chaos. Frazer Boy paces back and forth, muttering something loudly and shaking Rob James Baby at a member of the GLCW backstage crew. The last quarter of TORA Wanizame's body can be seen, as he is standing on top of a bench. His back side faces the camera, so the shot of his knees and legs is...exquisite.
Right in the middle of it all, is Frazer Fury. Behind the kind of rosy pink sunglasses one rarely finds outside of a Dearborn Heights, Michigan gas station in odd Arabic/Polish ghettos, Fury looks oddly sedate. He has changed out of the Masked Inlaw attire, and now wears a neon yellow t-shirt, the word "BRAINCHINCHILLA" written on it in bold black. Two rubber bands, one bright green and one red, wrap around Fury's neck. He's very colorful.
Almost as colorful as the language Frazer Boy and Rob James Baby are shouting at the GLCW employee, now. This outburst is temporary, however, as
Frazer Boy recoils in shock at the sight of Frazer seated on the bench.]
Frazer Boy: Uh-ohh! Bill Cosby! Ghostoooh daaaaad! [Frazer Boy leaps into the air, turning 180 degrees and waddling off at a high speed. The camera pans back to Frazer Fury.]
Frazer Fury: I'm in fewer pieces than you thought I was. [A momentary grin.] FF: Figured you might as well know how... [Fury picks up an uncomplicated remote control, and presses a button. The shot pans to reveal a television, perhaps a little over 20" in size. On the screen, the pineapple explosion runs silently.
Stillness. Explosion. Burning green felt. So what's the catch? The clip is paused, as the felt burns.] FF[off screen]: Watch closely now... [The tape rolls on... Another view of the pineapple, this time from the rear. Five seconds pass, before one section of the gargantuan fruit is pulled back. Out pops Frazer Fury, wearing his bright green Teletubby costume. He hustles away from the pineapple, and moments later it explodes.
The cameraman is moved back slightly by the explosion, but is obviously fine. As Frazer is behind the cameraman by this juncture, he too is obviously unharmed by the pineapple and felt shrapnel. Stop.]
FF[still off screen, with a really odd and unnatural accent]: Bayck...an' to th' left. [The clip is rewound and rolled again.] FF: Bayck...an' to th' left. [Rewind. Roll.] FF: Bayck...an' to th' left. [The shot is panned once more, revealing Fury's head tilted to one side. His medium-length honey blonde hair having fallen into his eyes, somewhat,
Fury watches the clip vacantly.]
FF: Bayck...an' to th' left... [Fade.] * * * * * [Fade in to a waist-up shot of a man, wearing a black and orange tiger shark mask, who is wrestling with a green bottle, a look of minor annoyance on his face. The walls behind him are nondescript and pale grey. Judging from the fact that - aside from the mask - there's not a stitch of clothing to be seen on him, plus the considerable amount of water pouring from something off-camera, it's a safe assumption that this would be a shower room. The man, recognizable as TORA Wanizame, holds his hand out expectantly under the upside-down bottle and gives it a squeeze to no avail. On the front of the bottle is a picture of a segmented catepillar or some such creature with quite slanty eyes. Though the majority of the print is in Japanese, in a yellow starburst are the words "Made of 100% intestine of most silky worms!"
TORA gives the bottle one last squeeze, and finally, with a rude sound, the bottle desposits a glob of lime green goo into his hand. As he begins to massage the green goo into his mass of matted down pink hair, he seems to notice the camera for the first time.]
TW: Ahh! [TORA quickly twists his body to the side, a seriously peeved look on his face.] TW: Hey! You wanna lose an eye!? [Potential injury averted, TORA returns to massaging the gunk into his hair. He begins to moan as if experiencing some great pleasure. The moans gradually become screams and he begins to vibrate fiercely in the midst of this "organic
experience". Before the camera man can decide that it's probably safest to leave, the rapture subsides. He heaves a sigh of satisfaction before speaking again.]
TW: Gah... what kind of promotion is this? Bursting in on people's showers... do you realize how close to an extreme occular plugging you were!? No wonder Frazer nearly killed himself...
[A lopsided grin appears on TORA's face and his wags his finger chidingly.] TW: Ah, but then his ZS fighting spirit kicked in! And in that last instant,
Frazer escaped his deadly pineapple and made it to safety! Ah, but you did not know! While you all mourned his "death", little did you realize it was all _your_ faults! All because not a single person in GLCW had the brave heart to be a Teletubby.
[TORA shakes his head sadly.] TW: It's true. So many of you "sports entertainers" lack anything even close to a brave spirit. Your inane prattling, your total lack of talent... I, too, am tempted to seek refuge in exploding pineapples. But instead, I will draw upon the courage of smiling children! And I will give my brave fight and not be a washed up old man riding other people's coattails!
[Smiling crookedly, he winks.] TW: You know who you are... kutabare subeta®! [With that, TORA pokes his index finger up his right nostril, then, withdrawing it, makes a quick flicking gesture toward the camera. Fade out.]
* * * * * [The shot opens outside of the arena, just after All-Stars of Wrestling. A back door opens and Tim Turner and Akira Saito step out into the cool evening. Saito is wearing his customary black, this time in the form of a black windbreaker and jeans. Turner is wearing a taupe suit with contrasting tie. As they head toward the parking lot they are intercepted by Red Ledret with a mic in hand.]
RL: Tim! TNT: Oh, hey Wrench. I'm sorry to hear about... RL: Yeah, well. They are still letting me do interviews for now, which is more than Rusty got.
TNT: If Bailey doesn't realize what he's got, come and talk to me. I can always use a vet like you.
RL: Thanks Tim...but back to work. What did you think of the first GLCW show fully under the new regime?
TNT: I liked Wilczak, everyone knows that. But this Bailey character seems to have some good ideas. I think this is just the opportunity we needed to show the wrestling world that the Age of the North is upon them.
RL: Akira? There has been a lot of questions about where your loyalties really lie. AS: Understandable. All I ask is for the fans to allow me to prove to them that I am the real deal. I will stand beside Tim and fight with honour. As soon as Mr. Bailey gives me a match...I don't care against who...I will prove that.
RL: Earlier you had claimed you wanted the winner of the Unification match...what do you think about that now?
TNT: First off, I am very glad that Gremlin won. It is true that we have had some problems in the past, but he is a lot better than Mordenheim.
RL: Still angry at him? TNT: Some things never change. RL: Bailey announced that it would be Tommy James who got the title shot at the Next Level...
TNT: He also laid down a challenge for others to step forward and claim that spot. That's what I'm doing. Nicholas Bailey? You have got your top contender right here. Tommy James? Step aside or get stepped on. There is nothing that will stop me from making it to the top of the federation. You couldn't stop me from getting the Cruiserweight belt months ago, and there is no way you can stop me from getting the Unified belt now.
RL: Those are strong words. TNT: Words are just words, Wrench, but I can back up everything I say. Just look at my record here in the GLCW.
RL: True enough. Thanks Tim...Akira. AS: No problem. TNT: I was serious about my offer, Red. RL: I know. I'll be in touch. [Cut away] * * * * * [SCENE: The night sky dominates the scene as the camera appears to reside on the side of a road. It's focused on a well-lit sign proclaiming the City
Limits of Detroit. The sound of an approaching automobile is heard coming from behind, as it approaches and passes the camera and sign. It is a black limo.]
[CUT: From somewhere on the ground of outskirts at the fairgrounds where the event is currently taking place. Two security guards stand on guard, as to not let any unauthorized folk into the event. Then a low rumble hits the area as the aforementioned black limo slowly pulls up near the guards. The rear right door opens before the car comes to a halt, and as it does a black shoe emerges from the door.]
Security Guard #1: Oh no.. [From the limo emerges a very familiar man. He appears to be a young man, dressed head-to-toe in a federal agent suit with his black hair slicked back. His stone face blends in well with the rest of his competely black suit. He wears black sunglasses, and an earpiece in his ear can be made out.
There is what appears to be a a white shirt underneath his black blazer and a black tie. He also wears black pants and black shoes, but we covered that one. The man is clean shaven, and doesn't appear to wear any jewels.]
[He closes the door to the limo with governmental authority, and whoever it may be that is driving shuts the engine and the car's power off in response.
He emotionlessly surveys the outskirts of the area where the show is taking place, and begins to approach the men without really looking at them. He seems to be preoccupied with... investigating.]
Agent: Sir.. Sir, I'm sure you were given specific orders.. Security Guard #2: Hey, look! The GLCW is in there! There's matches going on right now! [The special agent seems to be unconcerned with the man's words. He continues to survey the area without giving a clue to what he is thinking.]
Security Guard #1: Ya can't go in there! Those wrestlahs and suits would tear ya apart if ya were in there illegally!
[The agent turns his head to face the security guard just speaking, but you can't tell if he's actually looking at the guy, or just checking out the area around him because of the shades. After a pause, he looks toward the entrance.]
Agent: No, sir.. your company is already infected.. [He begins to head toward the entrance as the security guards look at each other in confusion and the camera fades.]
* * * * * Voice: Mr. Bailey! Mr. Bailey! [The camera opens up on GLCW President Nicholas Bailey as he's just about to step into his limo after All-Stars of Wrestling. The voice calling his name belongs to the GLCW's own Cameron Davies, who looks more than a little winded right now. Bailey arches a brow at him, but stops long enough to see what the little man wants.]
CD: [gasping for air] Mr... Bailey... Sir... I wanted to ask you some questions about tonight's show!
NB: Ask away. CD: Well... What do you think of Frazer Fury making his return to the GLCW! After all, everyone did think he was dead, and this is a pretty uncommon occurrence... [Bailey shrugs.] NB: Frazer Fury is a damn good wrestler - dead or alive, he's better than most guys in the business today. Am I glad he's back in the GLCW?
Absolutely. He put on one hell of a show out there tonight... A lot more impressive than he probably realizes. I don't know much about this TORA character, but Frazer, he's going places.
CD: What about our new Six-Man tag champions? Kousou Butai? NB: They're a good example of what it means to grab an opportunity before someone else does. I'm sure they'll do a great job representing this company and everything that we stand for. Now, if you'll excuse me...
[Bailey quickly enters the limo, slamming the door behind him. Davies doesn't seem to be too satisfied with the length and quality of this interview, but... them's the breaks. Fade.]
* * * * * [We fade in on on the not-unattractive-by-any-stretch-of-the-imagination face of Frazer Boy. He seems pretty... static, for some reason. And glossy. And flat.
Oh my God. It's a picture! Or, to be more precise, it's a picture in a magazine, a magazine that caters to the substantial portion of the young male populace that craves homoerotic violence as often as they can get it, preferably accompanied by really lame caricatures, and maybe some really dumb, pseudo-intellectual drivel about whatever. This subculture stretches across the globe, from the icy steppes of Canada to the golden shores of Nippon, from the robustly industrialized nations of Europe to the windswept plains of Michigan. Some like it boring, some like it unintelligible, and a sick few like it grammatically perfect, but they ALL like it... gay.
Yep... it's a wrestling mag. And, don't forget, a picture of Frazer "Bad" Boy. In the magazine.] Voice: Man... [A sigh. The camera pulls back, and we see hot pink dreads, and matching chinspike, framing a face whose dashing good looks have had absolutely no effect on his wrestling career. We can see that he's wearing a black t-shirt, the words "I'M STRAIGHT" clearly emblazoned across the chest of it in, if your television is to be trusted, neon green letters accented by small, phosphorescent borders, and even though they're off camera, presumably pants. Presumably. He looks up at the camera, arching an eyebrow that in-and-of-itself is more exciting than many of the GLCW's past cruiserweight champions... and again, he speaks. Behold! Rob stuff!]
Rob: This is the happiest day of my entire heterosexual life. [A nod, causing fuschia dreadlocks to begin a furious tussle for... eh, screw it.] Rob: I've been immortalized in yarn, buttons, and popsicle sticks before... but this time? This time is unique. This time, it's yarn, buttons, popsicle sticks... and love.
[Somehow, the young Arizona native keeps a straight face.] Rob: Yeah, baby. [Fade to black.]
