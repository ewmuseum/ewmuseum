Damage Control (5/14/00)

14 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GLCW Damage Control Sunday, May 14th, 2000 1. Keith Stebbins 2. The Northpac Coalition 3. "Titan" Mikhail Therbenko 4. Curmudgeon 5. Sean Watts * * * * * [The camera cuts to an empty locker room as the sounds of All-Stars or
Wrestling show still goes on. The door suddenly opens as a jubilant Keith
Stebbins comes in.]
KS: Yes! I can't believe, but I - Wow! [Stebbins, still happy over his first win in over a year, pumps his fist in the air. Why he's celebrating as if he won the Unified Title. Keith nods to himself as keeps saying "Yes! Yes!".]
KS: Oh, wow! This... This feels like I'm a kid again. I just can't - [Keith smiles some more, not able to stop himself.] KS: I'm just so happy! To be out there, see the people, be in a match - it was just too much. Just too much -
[Keith shakes his head as he keeps that wide smile on his face.] KS: But to win too, after all I've been through... Just amazing - I can't even believe it! I won out there! I feel like a rookie again -
[Stebbins throws some punches in the air, but then grabs his shoulder.] KS: Heh, well I may feel like a rookie, but I still got my same ol' body. Ah, but to just walk into that ring was enough, but to win... Wow, to win - and Tim Saunders - err, Sanders... He wasn't half-bad. A little too, well, I don't of what, but it was something.
[Yeah, Keith's not making much sense, but who can blame him? Stebbins starts to the back of the room as we hear the crowd cheer loudly from the arena.]
KS: Oh, wow, something else good's out there... But, wow, I gotta, well, I gotta soak these limbs - maybe, or celebrate more, or -
[Keith looks back at the camera.] KS: Heh, I'm not sure what I'm going to do, but I've got to do something... Wow, a win... [Stebbins own disbelief in himself trails off as he disappears from the camera's shot.] * * * * * [The shot opens in a locker room where Red Ledret is pacing back and forth in front of a tv monitor with its screen facing away from the camera. He looks visibly nervous.]
RL: Yeah...yeah...watch Bryant! Good...good! Yes! Now the...yes! Splashdown! [Red pumps his hand in the air and runs out of the locker room. The camera follows as he walks quickly through the corridors. He reaches the entrance area for the arena itself and waits. After what seems like an eternity, Tim
Turner and Akira Saito hit the backstage area.]
RL: Ha ha! Way to go Tim! [Everyone is all grins as Red slaps Tim on the back.] TNT: I couldn't have done it without you, Wrench! You had all three of them pegged dead on!
AS: What was with Watts? He wasn't even trying to fight? RL: Who cares? Those three guys are yesterday's news. We have to find out who Bailey has you booked against for next week. You've got to keep working your way up...
TNT: Uh, didn't you hear Bailey's announcement after the match? RL: No. I headed here as soon as you were announced as the winner. What's up? TNT: He's given me a spot in the main event at the Next Level! It's going to be T.J., that Frazer idiot, Gremlin, and me for the Unified title!
RL: That's great news! You can't slack of now, though! TNT: Hardly! He also said that if any of us drop the ball between now and then we will be pulled from the match and thrown in a cage with Reaver.
AS: Instant purgatory. RL: Well, we had better get to work. I'll start reviewing the tapes of the other three guys tonight. You get some rest and hit the gym first thing...
[His voice fades out as the three men walk away.] * * * * * [The scene opens at backstage of last All Stars of Wrestling card at Westland Sports Arena... When a massive man appears at backstage, looking tired, but with a smile on his face...
The Russian Locomotive... "Titan" Mikhail Therbenko.] MT: I guess my nyght were all ryght. I helped my lyttle fryend, Bullwug, to defeat that Japanese Kontygent...It were a good match. I am happyer that we wyn.
Shogun, Waty... Waty... Whatever. Ryght now, if you messes wyth my good fryend Hudson, you messes wyth me.
And I kan dealyng wyth people who messes wyth me. I krush them. So stayng out of my way... Or face the kons... kons... AWWWWWWW BOZHE MOI! [Therbenko goes for his pocket... Where he pull a Micheals Russian-English
Dictionary...] MT: Kon-se-quen-ces. That's it, Japan Kontygent... I am ready for you. Dosvidanya. [The scene fades as Therbenko walks away.] * * * * * [The camera opens to a backstage locker room. Curmudgeon lays sprawled out on a sofa,his head lying in Vampyra's lap with a towel draped over his face.
MoonGirl has resumed trying to revive her digital pet, frantically pressing buttons and cursing at the pixel animal on the screen.]
Curmudgeon: Ahhhhhhhhh... my neck is <bleep>ing killing me now! Damn, I felt like a ton of bricks out there tonight.
Vampyra: The cause of that is obviously jet lag and exhaustion. You wrestled on our little visit of Japan the night before we left the next morning. And that plane ride.. it's inconceivable that you were made to wrestle on a schedule like that.
Curmudgeon: And where the hell was Rob at, while I was out there getting my ass handed to me??
Vampyra: That I have no answer for. MoonGirl: Knowing Rob.. I'd think he met some adoring fifteen year old fan before the show and proceeded to taint her.
Curmudgeon: Makes me think twice about giving him that surprise. What the hell, everyone deserves another chance. Right now, payback is on my mind though. TORA and his <bleep>ing walking cactus have alot of pain due to them. If I have to wreck myself to make sure they get it, then by god thats exactly what I'll do.
Vampyra: The cactus will regret this night in days to come, and wish it had
_never_ hit me. If I have to tear him to pieces with my teeth and nails..
<grins> MoonGirl: I've got something for him.. <MoonGirl stands up, picking up her broomstick.> I *am* a bitch witch Cac..!! And I'm gonna ram this broomstick so far up your ass you feel it on your back teeth!!! So just try me you
FREAK!!! [MoonGirl starts to violently swing the broomstick around in front of her. Theres a loud crack as it accidentally, or not so accidentally hits the camera.. it rockets toward the floor and the scene goes black as it crashes against the concrete.]
* * * * * [Shortly after the main event of All Stars has ended, a pre-shower Sean
Watts steps up to the camera still in his wrestling gear. His folding chair which has the moniker 'Power Of Attorney' rests on one shoulder, though it has a tendency to slide down.]
SW: Well, we got the answer to one question now, didn't we? Though some people might not like the answer. And like the small type at the bottom of the lineup it is 'subject to change'.
So Tim... Can you hang onto your spot now? [Sean hefts the chair back onto his shoulder with a shrug while his head tilts to peer at the camera.]
SW: I don't think you can. And I don't think it matters if you could. Tommy has a shot at beating Gremlin, but I don't think that you do. So you know what? I might just step up. I might just take your spot. [His free hand pats the chair with meaty thunk.] SW: And I've got the answer to the Turner question. [Fade.]
