GLCW Change in management

May 18, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi everyone, not that I know many of you, but that's besides the point. My name is [name withheld], and I'm the new fedhead of Great Lakes Championship Wrestling, formally run by [name withheld]. [name withheld] has had to take a small leave of absense, and I will be running the fed while he is away. So, for the next few months, you'll be putting up with the bizarre booking styles of myself, with help from a few others, until [name withheld] finds his way back to GLCW.
For those of you who don't know GLCW, I'll give a quick rundown. GLCW is a regional fed based in the Michigan/Great Lakes area. The fed has gone from being regional to national back to being regional again over the past several years. Recently, GLCW implemented an "IRC card" format, which allows for on-time cards and the feel of a live television show such as you'd see on
Monday and Thursday nights (well, Monday nights). The fed boasts the talents of such wrestlers as The Gremlin, "Blackheart" Casey James, "Black Sheep" Rob James, Tiger Claw, Killdozer, Kraken, Chi Chi LeVaughn, Perro Culero, Brian Von Braun, Eddy Jacks, Ego Force, and many others. My wrestler, "Mr. Workrate" Tommy James, also competed in the GLCW, but will not during my time as fedhead.
Anyway, for those that are interested, check out the website at www.efeduniverse.com/glcw . There is enough info there to get you signed onto the mailing list and to check out a few cards.
Anyway, feel free to browse around, and for those that are interested, the next IRC card is on Wednesday, May 30th, at 8PM on channel #ASOW on the Starchat servers. Oh, and one more thing. If you have a tag team or possibly even a face singles wrestler, swing it by...we could stand to have a few more wrestlers on the roster.
See you at the matches! [name withheld]
