Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Council of Affiliated Wrestling Organizations announced itself 27 April 2016 as “an umbrella wrestling organization” uniting four regional promotions — Atlantic out of New York, Central out of St. Louis, Pacific, and an International arm rotating between Europe, Japan, Canada and Mexico — each running its own roster and regional title, feeding three CAWO-wide championships: a World Heavyweight title, a United States title, and a World Tag Team title. Its own out-of-character explainer, posted the next day, describes the project as modeled on “the old-school NWA” and grown out of a solo booking exercise on a text-based wrestling simulator.

Its debut two-night pay-per-view, Red Dawn, opened 30 April 2016 at the Allstate Arena under CAWO Central's own chief executive, Steven Skinner. Night 1 crowned all four regional champions at once: Ryan McCain won a ladder match to become the Atlantic's own first titleholder; “Lionheart” Lee Marshall outlasted Lazarus for the Central title in a two-out-of-three-falls war settled by a fast count that left Lazarus livid; “The Strangler” Shane O'Neil forced a submission win over Red Claw for the Pacific title; and “The Ravager” Reed Cromier won a steel-cage match for the International title.

The same night's main event crowned the first CAWO World Tag Team Champions: Wraith and The Apparition, a team billed as Abomination and backed by a villainous faction, the Covenant of Darkness, won a tournament final that spilled to ringside when the Covenant clashed in the aisle with a rival faction, the Empirical State.

Night 2, advertised to crown the promotion's first World Heavyweight Champion, was never completed. The operator's own last post, 2 June 2016, cites a personal-life interruption and promises a return that no later document shows arriving.
