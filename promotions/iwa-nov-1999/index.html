Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The IWA hosted at iwawrestling.gq.nu was founded, by its own account, in November 1999; every document that survives from it dates from its final year, 2001.

Its 2001 recruitment drives sought a new women's division, with a champion to be crowned by the middle of the month, and tag-team partners to round out a roster built entirely on original characters — no wrestler was to be a straight clone of an established star, though a writer could freely mix elements of more than one into something new.

One of those posts offered a full sample match to draw in new writers: a contest for a newly created IWA gimmick championship, staged inside a four-level stacked cage tower that wrestlers had to escape hatch by hatch, weapons scattered through every level, the belt presented at the end to Mad Dog, the first man out the ground-floor door.

On 1 December 2001 the league posted its own closing notice: it would shut down as of 16 December, with thanks to everyone who had visited or applied.
