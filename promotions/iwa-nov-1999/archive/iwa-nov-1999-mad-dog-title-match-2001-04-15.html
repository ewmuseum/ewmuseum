IWA looking for writers and wrestlers

April 15, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The IWA..located at http://www.iwawrestling.gq.nu/ is looking for new people. Wrestlers if your interested there is one rule...no clones...we dont need a rock or SCSA wondering around lookng for a hold. they can have elements of them...like say if you want to have the recoup powers of undertaker thats fine...mix him with some of hbk's flare or even flairs attitude...
Writers...below is a sample of a match...this is what we want...not sojemthing run through a simulator and the brackes removed.
