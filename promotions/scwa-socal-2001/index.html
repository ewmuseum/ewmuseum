Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Southern California Wrestling Association started up in December 2001 with an eight-man tournament for its first championship, its brackets drawn from a hat. It booked the Los Angeles Sports Arena for the occasion and wrote the night as television, called by Mike Marston and Jodie Ryan.

The first SoCal Wrestling card ran the whole tournament in one broadcast, the final contested in a roofed cage, and “The New Blackheart” Johnny Black left with the belt as the first Southern California Wrestling Association champion after a bloody final against “Bitin' Dog” Carlos Sanchez. The next update ranked the roster behind him and set a Christmas Eve card on which every match carried a stipulation, from an old-school rules bout to a fluorescent light bulb main event.

Whether the Christmas Eve card was ever written is not recorded.
