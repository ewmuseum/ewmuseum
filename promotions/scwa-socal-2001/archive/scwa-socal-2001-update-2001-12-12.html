Southern California Wrestling Association: Update, December 12th

12 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCWA UPDATE - 12/12/2001 ---- NEWS ---- First of all, I wanna thank everyone who participated for the last card. I meant to mention that I was going to summarize round one from the beginning, but I forgot to put it into the update, so I hope nobody's too put out by the fact that they RP'd for a summary (in some cases).
Anyway, from now on, I'm going to go with five match cards, and until
I can secure a few writers, the first two matches will be summarized, and the last three written out. The summaries will largely depend on the participation levels of the participants -- just because your match is going to be summarized as of one Update doesn't mean that it won't get written out.
We're getting some new members as of this update, and I'll go ahead and display the roster below, in a rankings format.
The next card is scheduled for Christmas Eve, so I guess we can do something sort of special, just for the hell of it.
-------- RANKINGS -------- SCWA Champion: "The New Blackheart" Johnny Black 1. "Bitin' Dog" Carlos Sanchez 2. Lance Mitchell 3. "Midnight Star" Joey Spencer 4. Vic Tsunami 5. Dain Sorenson ("Bloody" Vladimir Vostok) 6. "Razorblade" Jake Cutler 7. Jebediah Miller The following have not yet competed in SCWA, but are members: Necro Arkady Stygian "Hollywood Bound" Eddie Sterling Jacob Cross ------ LINEUP ------ Venue: Not yet decided. Date: December 24th, 2001 Since it's Christmas Eve, we're going to give a little gift to the
SCWA faithful -- every match on this card is going to have a stipulation of some sort. Yay!
JEBEDIAH MILLER vs. DAIN SORENSON will face off in an OLD SCHOOL RULES match! You know what that means...no moves off the top rope, no throwing over the top rope to the floor, no low blows, and, of course, no piledrivers!
"RAZORBLADE" JAKE CUTLER vs. VIC TSUNAMI in a SUBMISSIONS ONLY match! In this match, there's only one way to win -- you guessed it, your opponent must either submit or be unconscious.
ARKADY STYGIAN takes on NECRO in a FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE match! The
Commissioner took one quick look at the resumes of these two and decided that trying to contain them in a ring for their first match probably isn't that great an idea, so instead, falls will count
_anywhere_ in the building! "HOLLYWOOD BOUND" EDDIE STERLING vs. LANCE MITCHELL in a TABLE match! No pinfalls, submissions, or even KOs...you _must_ put your opponent through a table to win this match!
And, in our MAIN EVENT... JACOB CROSS will take on the man who nearly captured the SCWA Championship, "BITIN' DOG" CARLOS SANCHEZ in a FLOURESCENT LIGHT BULB match! In this match, there will be two waist-high bins full of the bulbs at ringside...do with the contents what you will. This match will be to one fall or a submission finish.
Strats are due by Saturday, December 23rd, 12 midnight CST. RPs are due by Sunday, December 23rd, 12 midnight CST.
