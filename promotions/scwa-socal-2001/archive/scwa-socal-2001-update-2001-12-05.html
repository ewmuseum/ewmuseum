Southern California Wrestling Association: Update, December 5th

SCWA Update - December 5th, 2001 We're starting up...tournament brackets are below. I drew them from a hat, so if anyone doesn't like the brackets...talk to the hat. Thanks to everyone for joining...let's make it a good one. VENUE: Los Angeles Sports Arena, Los Angeles, California. This is the shitpit that the Clippers used to play in, so it didn't cost us too much to get it. We're crowning the first Southern California Champion, so why not splurge? * ROUND ONE * - SoCal Championship Tournament: "The New Blackheart" Johnny Black vs. Vic Tsunami "Midnight Star" Joey Spencer vs. Jake Cutler Jebediah Miller vs. "Bitin' Dog" Carlos Sanchez Dane Sorensen vs. Lance Mitchell * ROUND TWO * Winner of Black/Tsunami vs. Winner of Spencer/Cutler Winner of Sanchez/Miller vs. Winner of Sorensen/Mitchell * FINALS - TO BE CONTESTED IN A ROOFED CAGE * Winner of Black/Tsunami-Spencer/Cutler VS. Winner of Sanchez/Miller-Sorensen/Mitchell DUE DATES - Strats are due by Saturday, December 8th, 12:00AM CST. RPs are due by Sunday, December 9th, 12:00AM CST. Hopefully, the card will be out Monday night. Send everything to [detail withheld]...this address will change shortly, once I find a real e-mail provider.
