Friday Night Blitz — January 18, 2002

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Night Blitz Results:. 18/01/02 Dan Marsh > Welcome to CWF Friday Night Blitz! Match 2: Standard Match Kyrupt vs The Living Highlight Title: None Match 3: Tag Team Match SeVen (f) & Zero (f) vs X-Tinct (f) & Avirex Title: None Match 4: Standard Match 2 Cool Leroy vs Nick McCleud Title: None Who Cares! Phantom vs The Punisher vs Destructor vs Dane Gerous The Winner of this Dark Match, Phantom! Mike Maddness vs Xtreme T [CWF Infomercial: "Stay away from drugs" by Kula] The cameras return as Mike Madness is making his way to the ring. X-Treme T is already standing in the corner waiting for his opponent having made an earlier more low profile entrance. Madness climbs into the ring. Stephen > This one is about to kick off The two tie it up in the ring. Mike overpowers T and pushes him to the ground. T gets right back up and runs into a quick punch from Mike. T retreats out of the ring for a breather. Madness goes out following T but he gets hit in the face with an electronic fan from commentary. T smashes Mike's face hard into the table two times. T then grabs a chair and folds it up. He pushes it into the ribs of Mike and then over the back of him as he bends over favoring his ribs. Stephen > That steel chair is legal in this hardcore environment T sets up the chair beside the ring post on the outside and sits Mike down on it. T then lifts the steel steps apart and lifts the top deck over to where Mike sits. He charges at Mike but just in the nick of time Mike collapses off of the chair and T cracks the ring post and his own momentum causes him to charge into the steel steps. Dan > Man that's gotta hurt T favors his ribs as Mike rolls him up into a schoolboy. The referee counts…1…2kick out by T. Stephen > That was a close one. Mike picks up T and slams him out on the mats. T cradles his back and Mike stomps away on him. Mike then lifts up T and lays him on the commentary table. Mike goes up onto the ring apron and dives off with a fist drop to T. He then pulls him up and rolls him into the ring. Mike searches under the ring and pulls out a trash can. He throws it into the ring and goes in himself. He grabs T and takes him over to the trash can which is laying flat in the middle of the ring. Mike then T-Bone DDT's T through the trash can. He covers for a…1…2…3 Stephen > There's the victory. What a big win for Mike Madness. Hes the number one contender for the CWF Hardcore Championship! eXodus vs Dr. Doom Exodus' music hits, as the fans react he walks out onto the stage and milks their response. Stephen > This match is a straight wrestling bout between two of CWF's finest - Exodus and Dr Doom. Exodus gets into the ring and Dr Doom's music plays. He comes out from the curtain pointing at Exodus in the ring and talking trash. Exodus laughs at him and Doom runs in under the bottom rope as the bell sounds. Dan > We're off! Dr Doom takes the advantage with a serious of punishing body blocks. He whips his opponent into the ropes and delivers a spinning roundhouse. Exodus takes the fall but gets back up sharp. He stares into Dr Dooms eyes for a second, until Doom hits a knee to the groin. He takes the arm of Exodus and pulls it between his legs setting up a pump handle slam. Stephen > Dr Doom is taking this match straight to Exodus. Doom stomps his opponent before running to the ropes. He bounces off them and jumps into a centon splash but Exodus rolls out of his way. Dan > Did you see the ring dent!! Exodus gets back to his feet using the ropes. He runs at Doom and baseball slides him in the right shoulder. Doom grabs his shoulder and sits up. Exodus hooks the same arm of Doom ; stretching Doom flat on his back he places his left leg over Dooms head neck, he pulls the right arm through his legs towards his own chest, and hooks his right leg under his left leg - then stretches. Stephen > Will he give up? Doom wriggles in agony as Exodus stretches and stretches the arm of his opponent. Eventually Doom reaches the ropes causing the hold to be broken. Exodus stomps the shoulder of Doom until his opponent rolls out the ring. Exodus follows him out, delivers some right fists, then whips his opponent into the ring post - shoulder first. Stephen > Exodus has focused in on the right shoulder of Dr Doom. Exodus throws his injured opponent back into the ring. He climbs up on top himself and back in over the top rope. Doom gets to his feet just in time for a hard clothesline from Exodus. Apocalypse vs Kula [ CWF Infomercial : Don't try this at home ] Stephen > What a Blitz its been so already tonight! Dan > Check out second row blonde. WOOHOO! Stephen > Second row blondes with d cups, get a camera on them, get a camera on them!! Dan > Flashers! WOOHOO! Stephen > Second row blondes, hardcore wars, a night of madness and we've still got Apocalypse versus Kula to go. Dan > I'm sure its pronounced Koala. Stephen > Why don't you ask him in a minute. This match is NEXT! [ CWF Infomercial "Don't try this at home…unless you have a video camera" ] The show cuts back with Apocalypse on the ramp and his entrance music playing. The crowd are giving a modest boo noise to the big bad man. Stephen > It may be no where near the end of the world but the CWF is the only place to get your Apocalypse fix, folks. Dan > I only have one problem with this guy Stephen > What's that? Dan > What's he got against lesbians? Stephen > I dunno. Maybe they ran his dad over in that humorous car accident he was talking about? Dan > Hey maybe you're right. Poor lesbians. Apocalypse hits the ring apron and points into the crowd as a crumpled hot dog wrapper crashes off his legs. Before he gets into the ring he is cut off with the entrance music of his opponent, Kula. Stephen > Here comes Kula! Apoc jumps off the ring apron and marches back up the ramp way as Kula walks out from the curtain holding a steel chair in his hand to a big pop. Stephen > This is no DQ, folks. Anything goes! Apoc begins to backtrack as Kula stalks him with the chair. Stephen > What a pop for Kula. Dan > Apparently only the poor and sick children pop like that for Apocalypse. Stephen > And his gum! Apoc continues backing off until he gets to the ring edge and cant back off anymore. Dan > Get a weapon, find a weapon! Kula throws the steel chair straight off Apocs skull, only softened by the defensive arms coming up to block the blow. Stephen > Listen to that sound! Steel and skull that's a CWF mix! Kula makes use of the early advantage by delivering a few kicks to Apocs gut. He takes the big man by the hair and throws him towards the steel steps. Apoc crawls over the steps and round the corner of the ring. Stephen > He's trying to escape! Dan > He's…he's…he IS trying to escape!?! Kula picks up the steel chair and stands on top of the ring steps as Apoc clambers back to his feet. As Apoc gets back up he turns round to face Kula on the steps. Kula leaps off the steps with a 187 chair shot on top of the head of Apoc while the crowd roars in approval. Kula slides into the ring and steps on the first turnbuckle of the corner to milk the crowd. Stephen > Ladies and gentlemen…Kula! Dan > His damn entrance music is still playing. This isn't a proper match! Apoc slides under the bottom rope as the music stops. He gets to his feet in time for Kula to whip him into the ropes. He returns off and ducks the clothesline returning with one of his own. Dan > Lariant! Apoc took his head off with that! Stephen > Some rest time for Apocalypse. Apoc regains some composure while stomping in his opponent. He goes over to the turnbuckle and sits on top of the third with his feet on the second. As Kula gets to his feet Apoc comes off with an elbow smash to the head. Kula goes back down to his knees allowing Apoc to lock in a hammerlock. Stephen > Apocalypse is taking this to the mat. That's just SMART wrestling! He holds the move for several seconds before Kula twists out and into a reversal. He holds the left arm in the hammerlock and inserts his right arm in front of Apocs before pulling over to hook Apocs head. Stephen > Watch the ring move… Kula wrenches Apoc with all his strength and lifts the big man up, turns and falls with him landing face first. Stephen > SIDE BELLY TO BACK HAMMERLOCK SUPLEX!!!!!!! Dan > Holy F*beep*ck Stephen > A side belly to back hammerlock suplex from Kula. That was incredible. Kula gets back up, shakes the cobwebs away, and picks up the steel chair left in the ring. He places the chair over the back of Apocs head. Stephen > Kula has started to climb the turnbuckle. What's he got planned? Kula gets to the top rope, stands for a second, then dives off into a Kamikaze head butt on to the steel chair. He hits, and rolls over laying motionless with a busted nose and blood all over his face. Dan > AHHAHAHAHAHA Stephen > Kula has been busted open and rendered unconscious at his own move. Dan > What an idiot. Roll that again, again! Apoc stirs up to his feet, realising Kula's mistake he grins. The big man pulls Kula to his feet and throws him about the ring with a serious of roundhouse rights. Stephen > Apocalypse is using Kula as a punch bag. He sends Kula to the ropes delivering a tilt a whirl slam to him on his return. Stephen > This one is history, cover….1…2……..shoulder up! Apoc looks stunned as the referee indicates a two count. Apoc rolls out the ring and steals the ring keepers bell, throwing it into the ring. He gets back in the ring, lays the bell on Kula's head and smacks it with the hammer. Dan > HAHA Apoc sits Kula up in the corner, resting his head against the second turnbuckle and his arms draped over the ring ropes. Stephen > What's this? Apoc picks up the bell, holds it high up, and then tosses it at Kula. The bell hits Kula straight on the top of the head, causing a ding to be heard around the arena. Stephen > GOOD GOD! Dan Marsh > Jesus! What won't these two guys do to win! Apoc covers Kula, the ref starts a slow count! 1...2... Stephen > He kicked out! He kicked out! Apoc flips at the referee for not counting the three. He grabs him and tosses him over the top rope and signals for a new ref! Dan Marsh > Wait a damn second! He can't do that! Stephen >Sure he can Dan! Hes in the Corporation! He can do whatever the hell he wants and no one can argue! A new referee emerges from the back! Stephen > Oh yeah! Now things are going to heat up. Dan Marsh > I smell a screw job! Phoenix rolls into the ring dawning the black and white as Apoc pummels Kula in the corner! Apoc draggs a lifeless Kula into the centre of the ring and Phoenix makes a count! 1.2.kickout! Dan Marsh > Yes! Yes! I knew he could do it! Kulas still in this! Kula just kicked out of a double speed count! Stephen > Yeah hes still in this but the question is for how long! Apoc draggs Kula back into the corner. He walks to the opposite corner as Phoenix places a chair over his head. Apoc charges but Kula finds the strength to throw the chair against him! Apoc goes down and Kula with a newly found vitality stands tall and hits a somersault legdrop into the back of his fallen foe! Kula grabs the steel chair and sets up for what appears to be an arabian facebuster but Phoenix grabs the chair! He takes a swing, Kula ducks! Phoenix turns as Kula leaps into the air with a spinning heel kick - pushing the chair into the face of the CWF owner! Stephen > Oh my god! Phoenix! Are you okay! Kula takes the chair again and sets up for the arabian facebuster! He hits it. Dan Marsh > Hes going for the cover! Stephen > But theres no one to make the count! Hes screwed! Kula grabs the hand of the fallen Phoenix and presses it against the mat. 1... and again, 2... and once again, ...3! Dan Marsh > Kula has done it! Kula has defeated Apocalypse! Stephen > Oh god theres gonna be consiquences to this! Kula better watch his back from now on! Dan Marsh - What will happen next week! (CWF Blitz goes off the air.) ©Copyright 2001 - 2002.
