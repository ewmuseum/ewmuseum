CWF Company Rules

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rules:. Rules :1.0 (i) You may only join as a created wrestler, actual wrestlers - past and present will be turned down. (ii) By submitting an join application, you are signing an agreement whereby you promise to do at least one roleplay every week. (iii) You may only submit one join application. Anyone found to be handling more than one character will be released from CWF. (iv) The Owner reserves the right to reject any application he so pleases without question. Rules :2.0 (i) Upon joining, you will be added to the next card made. You will be expected to produce at least one roleplay for this card. (ii) This roleplay should be at least 1200 words in length. Failure to comply can results in expulsion from the fed. (iii) You are required to request leave from the CEO if you cannot roleplay for a card. This can be done by e-mail or messenger, or OOC Board post. (iv) Failure to roleplay for two weeks without a valid excuse will result in the termination of your fed contract. Rules :3.0 (i) The Roleplay Board is for you to post roleplays - not and I repeat - not OOC messages! This is one of the most commonly ignored rules in e-fedding. Lets try to change that. (ii) Any discriminative, racist, sexist, etc. comments that are intended to offend posted on the Roleplay, OOC or PPV boards can results in the firing of that handler from the fed. (iii) If you spam or advertise on any CWF board, you will be fired. This covers all advertising. Rules :4.0 (i) In your roleplays, you should not use other wrestlers without first recieving their permission. (ii) You cannot attack any wrestler in your roleplays without first recieving their permission. (iii) You can kill insignificant characters in your roleplays but no one envolved with the fed. (iv) All roleplays posted after a deadline will not be counted, even if they are only over the deadline by a minute. Rules :5.0 (i) A staff decision is final so its not even worth attempting to argue or hastle them. (ii) Continuous argument with staff members can result in be being fired from the fed. (iii) And the most important rule of all. Its only a game and you are ordered to have fun! ©Copyright 2001 - 2002. Continental Wrestling Federation & Baileyk85. All Rights Reserved
