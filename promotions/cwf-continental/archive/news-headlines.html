News Headlines:. Hired ~ Quite A Lot Of People Fired ~ No One 03/02/02 ~ The Beginning of the New Beginning I have updated the roster page for those who have signed up. Everyone but Mike Maddness, Spike Maddness and Bloodthirst has joined the mailing list. I will add all the board passwords sometime over the next couple of weeks. Those of you who were already in CWF can use your old passwords to post OOCs. CWF will reopen in about 3-4 weeks. I am going on holiday for a week this friday so there will be no updates. I would like to thank the old members for staying faithful. Finally, congrats to Kula on becoming the TWF World Champion. I'm not complaining as long as he still does good roleplays for us. 28/01/02 ~ A New Beginning Well CWF has gone from hero to zero. Nearly all the roster have disappeared. I was going to shut CWF down but I've had requests from the roleplayers who remain to keep it going and that is why I am going to try and make it work one more time. The roster has been wiped so if you still want to be here, you're going to have to rejoin. Also join the mailing list while you're at it. I have posted winners for the PPV matches (well for all the matches where someone actually roleplayed). The Champions list has not been wiped. If you had a title - its still yours. 25/01/02 ~ Deadlines I have added headers to the roleplay boards displaying deadlines. These will stop all the queries I get as to when the deadlines are. There have been few roleplays for the PPV so far. Its a pity. I was hoping that you would all try and make CWF's first PPV something special. 22/01/02 ~ The PPV Board Is Up Sorry it took so long. Special thanks to Dr. Doom for donating it. All your passwords have been added. I'm hoping that after the big cutbacks I made that we are nearing consolidation. I hope to have a roster where everyone roleplays. If we achieve that then I am happy. 18/01/02 ~ Very Bad News I never realised how most of the roster are lazy bastards! You all better buck up quickly! The Blitz results and the PPV card have been posted on the corresponding pages! 13/01/02 ~ Very Good News CWF now has a results writer who isn't me! I'd like to welcome [name withheld] to the CWF staff! From what I've seen, hes very, very good and appears to be a one in a million find! He has been sitting on my MSN contacts for ages and I just never knew he did fed results. The CWF roster has now reached 20, not including myself. 12/01/02 ~ Friday Night Blitz Update Blitz Card for the 18th is posted on the corresponding page! Sorry about the results being late but I've had a tough week. I will post the Blitz card later today. The roleplaying standard this week has improved, lets try and do that again this week. CWF still desperatly needs a results writer. I just don't have the time to do them. If we don't find one by next week, expect highlights coz I'm not using Zeus again, it pisses me off. 11/01/02 ~ Friday Night Blitz Update The deadline is up and the results are being written. If you signed up in the last week, you are guarenteed a matchup on the next Blitz card which will be posted sometime tomorrow. Those of you who didn't roleplay this week (you know who you are) have one more week before being fired. CWF desperately needs a results writer. Doing these results again will probably kill me. With the death of [name withheld] and some other stuff, this is turning out to be one of the worst weeks of my life. 10/01/02 ~ New Site Design Here it is! A site design that really looks good! I'm damn proud of this and I hope you all are too! This is going to be the final design aswell. Nothing more will change. Of course, its going to take me a little while to update every page to this style but when its done, its going to be so wicked! This design gives a pro feel to CWF and I'm hoping that this will attract new members! ©Copyright 2001 - 2002. Continental Wrestling Federation & Baileyk85. All Rights Reserved
