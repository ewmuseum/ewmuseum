Application -(Introduction)- They say great leaders arise out of times of chaos...well, i'm attempting to see if the same thing is true of Ewrestling federations.
Gentleman, i've made this federation for a few reasons. First, I made this reason to have some fun. As many know, i, "Mister Sexy" Brian Gold retired from the Ewrestling world for a few months not too long ago. Basically, i did so because Ewrestling had really lost it's fun in my mind...this federation is an attempt to bring that back. Second, i made this federation because lets face it...there aren't that many great feds out there right now and so I did this for you! Don't get me wrong, the third reason is because i've always wanted to have my own federation and that's what this is for. Now, if you receive this application, there is a good chance our wrestlers have met before.
Let me go on the record right now, no matter if i loved you or hated you in the past years...I will be totally fair. Lets face it, if you did better rping, you'll win, but if not, don't think you'll win 'cause we are friends.
There is a lot of complaining in Ewrestling right now and ya know what...that's fine. If you want to whine or complain to me, go right ahead. Although, i'll tell you right now, if you do, there is a lot less chance you'll get what you want. Like in a baseball game, you complain too much, i'll be the umpire and throw ya out. If you have a problem or a request, just talk to me like a person and most of the time, you'll get what you want. That's 'bout it, please read on 'cause i know you'll like it more and more. This federation is going be great. I don't know if it will be another CWF or IEUW, but i'm goin' do my best to make the federation as great as it can be...it's up to you guys to make it greater than the CWF or IEUW...good luck to everyone.
Thank-You, Brian Gold -(G.W.F. It will hold between 7 to 9 matches with the bigger angles and all. It is announced by Greg Tompson and Guy Gilette. This is also the longest weekly show and the highest rated. This show is usually going to be written by myself, Brian Gold.
Saturday Night Sparks: The only sparks aren't those from Victoria Gold, but from the wrestlers as well. The show that's announced by Victoria Gold and

Teamwork! **There will also be House Shows without warning that are just sums of matches that will give winners and losers...this is to make sure you are rping! -(Pay-Per-Views)- January: New Year: New Way- Like the name says, it's a New Year and a new way...all champions must put their titles on the line at this pay-per-view.
Also the pay-per-view where champions must be crowned. February: Broken Hearts- In this pay-per-view, all female escorts are put on the line along with championship belts.
March: Birthday Bash- This is the month of the Presidents B-day and so, this is the federations biggest pay-per-view. This is the one where movie stars to wrestling stars come from around the globe to. This is the pay-per-view that always changes the federation.
April: Showers of Powers- In this pay-per-view, many wrestlers are split into different teams and fight eachother...like survivor series.
May: Mayhem- In this pay-per-view, the GWF gets extreme. All matches are extreme in one way or another.
June: Summer Stampede- In this pay-per-view, the matches are all held in an outdoor arena...matches are fought rain or shine!
July: Freedom Bowl- This pay-per-view has regular matches, but then the main event is a giant Royal Rumble called the Freedom Bowl in which almost every wrestler of the federation is involved in.
August: Full Moon- No, this pay-per-view has nothing to do with the wrestlers' a*ses...most of the time. In this ppv, the winner of the match chooses any way he wants to embaress his opponent after the match.
September: Labor Day- In this pay-per-view...in ways with Labor Day, every match has a stipulation such as a ladder match, cage match, lumber jack match and so on.
October: Frightfest- In this pay-per-view, there are regular matches, but certain scary things can happen such as lights suddenly turning off or worse!
November: Thankful Steel- In this pay-per-view, along with the Thanksgiving holiday, winners receive turkey...but more importantly, all matches are fought in steel cages!
December: Holiday Horror- From Santa Claus to Hanukah Harry and many superstars...this is a huge pay-per-view like Birthday Bash. As a gift to it's wrestlers from the GWF...the wrestlers choose what kind of matches they want to have!
-(G.W.F. Titles)- G.W.F. World Title- This Title is for the best of the best and the best of the best only. If you win this title, you are a legend in the sport. This belt will have to be defended at least twice a month unless the President says otherwise.
G.W.F. US Title- This Title is the second highest in the Gold Wrestling Federation. This is also only for great wrestlers and you have to work hard to get it and defend it as it has to be defended at least three times a month.
G.W.F. Extreme Title- This Title is the third highest in the Gold Wrestling Federation and it's for extreme wrestlers. Like the US Title, it has to be defended at least three times a month. For this belt...ya gotta be a little crazy to get it and alot crazy to win it!
G.W.F. Televison Title- This is a very important Title and it can give young wrestlers a huge push to win it. It takes alot of work to win and especially to defend this title. This title has to be defended every week, but it's a great stepping stone to a higher position in the federation!
G.W.F. Cruiserweight Title- This Title isn't easy to get, but it's only for wrestlers under 235 pounds. This could be a great divison or a weak divison...really, it's all up to the wrestlers that want to go after it. This
Title has to be defended every week as well. -(Role-Playing)- There are a few rules for the GWF's rping. They are easy to follow and her they are: 1) You must rp twice a week or you will be out of the federation! (Now, if you don't have a match or I say you can take a break, you don't have to rp, but this is really for if you have a match. Now, if you don't have a match, it would be great to send out on rp or something, but it's not a big deal)
2) If you don't rp...you will lose your match. If I see you aren't rping much, i will talk to you personally and if it keeps happening, i will ask you to leave the federation. Feds are only fun if people rp!
3) Don't just send out little 20 liners with no work done with them at all, please. You are just wasting everyone's time and filling up mail boxes. I'm not saying you have to write really long ones, but make sure they aren't really, really weak or i'll personally talk to you.
4) Match outcomes are determined by myself and the Co-President. We will look at 70% rping, 20% current angle and 10% current push...if you have any complaints, talk to me! 5) Basically, when looking at rps...we are looking for quality and not quanity. Quanity will help, but the better the rp, the better you'll do...not the more rps you do, the better you'll do.
6) That's about it...any questions, ask myself or the Co-President! -(G.W.F. Rules)- 1.) Like in all federations...NO TOSING!!!! If you have any problems, anything at all with another wrestler or myself or the Co-President, e-mail me, but do not TOS anyone. If you TOS anyone, you will be thrown out of the federation without any questions. Come on guys...TOSing really isn't cool.
2.) No Junk Mail!!! Do not send chain letters or anything like that at all. First time is a warning and second time you will be thrown out of the federation! Crappy 10 line rps count as junk mail!
3.) Wrestler must be made up...no "Stone Cold" Tom Austin or stuff like that! 4.) Do NOT complain at all to the Co-President...his job is not to deal with that. If you have a problem or have to complain...go straight to the top and complain to me!
5.) No Swearing...if you have to swear, use something like f*ck or sh*t...if they say it on TV, you can say it, though (that doesn't include like HBO or movie channels!)
6.) Most importantly...HAVE FUN! Honestly guys...this federation is for you. This fed is to get back down to basics and have some fun. For example, if you have wanted to finally fight someone for awhile or finally try an angle or idea...e-mail me and i'll set it up most of the time. Lets get it on! Any
questions...e-mail me! -(G.W.F.
