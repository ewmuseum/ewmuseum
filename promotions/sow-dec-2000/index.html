A real-wrestler federation launched in December 2000, recruiting handlers for a scheduled debut card headlined by a Rock-Flair main event. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SOW put up its own webpage on 20 December 2000, billing itself plainly as “a REAL wrestler fed” and pointing to an already-updated roster hosted at trgwrestling.com.

A community reply the next day welcomed it warmly: its own new member describes joining alongside characters he portrays as “Stunning” Steve Austin and “Lord” Steven Regal, and makes the appeal of a real-wrestler fed explicit — the chance to write angles for real performers “that you KNEW would get him over,” citing Stevie Richards by name as an example of a wrestler the actual WWF, in his view, kept misusing.

By Christmas week SOW was still recruiting handlers, and had set a tentative debut card for Tuesday 2 January 2001 under the name TURMOIL, “live on USA”: Kevin Nash and Scott Hall against Chris Candido and Lance Storm, Lex Luger against Shane Douglas, a three-way between Terry Funk, Mick Foley, and Hardcore Holly, Steven Regal against Shane McMahon, and a main event pairing The Rock against Ric Flair, alongside a drawing for the top two contenders to its own United States Championship.

No closure notice survives, and the tentative card itself was posted as still “subject to change.” Whether the 2 January debut ever aired, or any of those matches were actually contested, isn't known.
