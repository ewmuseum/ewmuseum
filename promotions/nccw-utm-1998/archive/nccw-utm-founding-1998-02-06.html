"Just give this one a look"

6 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now, I know there are a lot of new feds starting up right now, but just take a look at this one at mars.utm.edu/~tronbarr/UWC/normal.htm . It's the new UCWF, some of you may remember us from a couple of years ago. We're back and better than ever. Check it out. President of UCWF, PaladinRed I apologize for the call letters of our fed. We have just gotten started up again and our original call letters were taken. Ultimate Cyber Wrestling Federation was close to our original and we didn't pick up on another fed with the same call letters. The fed will be renamed and we meant no disrespect to the Ultimate Championship Wrestling Federation. Thanks, PaladinRed
