NCCW Monday Night Rage

3 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens to thousands of screaming fans eagerly awaiting the next edition of NCCW Monday Night Rage. The camera pans around the cheering crowd, many of whome are waving signs dedicated to their favorite NCCW stars, and finally settles on the broadcast table.] MP--"Hello everyone, welcome to NCCW Monday Night Rage. We are live at the Independance Arena in Charlotte, North Carolina. I'm Morgan Penn, and with me is Bobby Vaughn. We have a great show for you tonight, including two title match-ups." BV--"Not just title shots, but NCCW history in the making. NCCW's first ever double-title match. Two men, two belts, only one winner. Those are my kind of stakes." MP--"The second title match is a defense of the World Heavyweight title. In an unprecidented move, NCCW officials have let champion Max Maxwell choose his opponent for the title defense. Lucky Lambert gets the title shot, but will he be able to capitalize?" BV--"Lucky's been acting weird lately. He's not same happy-go-lucky guy we saw at the first couple of cards, and what's this we he's been saying about finding help?" MP--"I don't know, maybe we'll find out later. Right now, let's not waste another minute. We're ready for our first match here on Rage. Shocker will take on The Mad Goat." BV--"Shocker's a rookie here in NCCW. I've seen him in warm-ups and he looks pretty impressive, but the Goat is a tried and true veteran. He's wrestled some of the best in the NCCW and has held his own the entire time. In fact, he's one of the favorites in the upcoming cruiserwieght tournament." MP--"Shocker has the definite weight advantage, but Mad Goat is one of the fastes men in the NCCW. It'll be interesting to see what developes. Mad Goat is making his way to the ring with Mother Earth now." [Mad Goat and Mother Earth make their way to the ring. Mother Earth waves to the fans, but Goat seems focused on the task ahead. Once at ring side, Goat enters the ring and Mother Earth stays outside to cheer him on to victory.] MP--"Here's his opponent, making his first appearance here on Rage, Shocker." [Shocker makes his way down the ramp with Sarah Sapphire. Arm in arm, they walk to the ring. As shocker climbs inside, Sapphire gives Mother Earth an evil look.] BV--"I thought Goat was a wierd guy, but I think Shocker ranks right up there too. Shaved head, earing, what's with this guy?" MP--"Uh,...Bobby, there's a cross dangling from your ear and about three hairs on your head." BV--"Yeah, well, that's different. Listen, are you going to comment on my appearance or on the match?" MP--"Right. Referee Don Weis is ready to begin and there's the bell. As usuall, Mad Goat starts quickly. He charges in and levels Shocker with a quick dropkick. Shocker gets back up quick, but Goat sends him down with a hip toss. Shocker opts to roll under the ropes and slow the pace of the match down." BV--"Good move by Shocker. Once Goat gets started its hard to slow him down. Uh, oh." MP--"Doesn't look like Goat wants to be slowed down. He charges across the ring and dives over the top rope, tackling Shocker. Both men are slow to their feet, but Goat is waiting as Shocker stands. Frankensteiner! The Mad Goat sends him back to the concrete floor." BV--"Shocker looks dazed as Goat picks him back up and slides him into the ring. Goat's always been a strong starter, but this is something else!" MP--"They're back in the ring now. Goat picks Shocker up and whips him into the ropes. Shocker comes running back and is hit with a flying body-press! Goat his him pinned. 1...2...only two and a half." BV--"I don't know how Curly got out of that one. Must of been a spastic twitch." MP--"Shocker's up to his knees and Goat starts to lift him up...Ouch! Shocker levels him with a low blow! Goat is down and shocker gets to his feet." BV--"That's the way to win a match." MP--"I bet you pulled the wings off of butterflies when you were a kid." BV--"So, I won those fights too." MP--"Shocker lifts the Goat and puts him on his shoulder...shoulderbreaker! Shocker follows it with a fist drop." BV--"I don't think Goat is even aware he was hit with those last two moves. He's still hurting from that low blow." MP--"Shocker picks Goat up again and sets him up for the Shockwave. There it is! That big modified inverted atomic drop he calls the Shockwave." BV--"That'll crack the ole diamonds for sure." MP--"Shocker covers for the 1-2-3, but Goat isn't offering a fight. The winner of the match, Shocker." BV--"Shocker has won his first match here on Rage, but I'm worried about Goat. He may not be in shape for the cruiser tournament. If he's not, I'll need to call my bookie." MP--"We'll know Wednesday if Goat is ready for the tournament. You're right though, many consider him the favorite for the title. Our next match is about to get started." [Mr. Manchi walks down the ramp and is followed by 'The Great' Hiro Akura. Manchi whispers in his wrestler's ear the whole way down the aisle.] BV--"I wonder what that's all about?" MP--"Who knows? Here comes his opponent." [Lady Anne walks down the entrance ramp waving a British flag. Behind her comes Essex and the boos of the fans. He strolls to ringside, seeming to enjoy the jeers from the audience.] BV--"He's an arrogant SOB, but he gets the job done. That's the whole point." MP--"I have to give him credit for that, even though I can't say I like his methods. He'll have his hands full here, Akura is another of the quick cruiser weights." MP--"There's the opening bell. Akura closes with Essex to the center of the ring. Essex takes control with a kick to the gut and follows by running the smaller man into the turnbuckle. Akura tries to fight his way out, but Essex drives him back in with an European uppercut." BV--"Essex is using his head here. He saw what happened when the last cruiser got the tempo going. He wants to keep this match slow and keep Akura wihi arms reach." MP--"Essex locks Akura in a full-Nelson and forces him to the mat. He's really putting on the pressure. After what seems like forever, Essex stands back up with Akura still locked up and turns it into a full-nelson slam!" BV--"Essex isn't giving a chance to start that high-flying stuff. If Akura gets moving Essex may not be able to catch him again." MP--"Essex doesn't give him a chance to let up, but drops down and applies a side head-lock. Essex finally releases and drops an elbow onto the prone Akura. Akura is then hauled to his feet and sent running into the ropes." BV--"Uh, oh. Some people never learn." MP--"Hiro bounces off and comes running back...he ducks under a clothesline and rebounds off of the opposite side. Essex is slow to turn...bulldog takedown! Essex is on the mat! Akura tries for a jumping knee drop...he hits! Control has definatly shifted now." BV--"Essex had control. He should have kept the cruiser on the mat or pinned in the corner." MP--"Be tha as it may, Akura has the upper hand now. He bounces off of the ropes again and leaps intot he air for a big leg drop, but Essex rolls aside!" BV--"Ha! Essex was playing opossum!" MP--"Akura hits hard and Essex takes advantage by dropping a knee onto Akura's leg. Essex then rolls him over and slaps on the STF! Akura taps out!" BV--"That's a classic example of how to wear down a faster opponent. Keep the pressure up by applying submission holds and when he's tired he'll make mistakes. It's easy from there." MP--"Well, Essex sure made it look easy. He gets the victory in resounding fashion. The next scheduled match is Crippler vs. Zeus Sinclair. This is a rematch from one of the earliest NCCW cards." BV--"Both of these guys are cruiser weights. Sinclair is probably the faster of the two, but Crippler has an advantage on mat skills and technique. Crippler has been having a tough time winning matches here lately, but he's put out some tough talk. It'll be interesting to see if he can back it up." [Crippler is the first man down the ramp. He walks out and barely pauses at the bottom of the ramp before heading to the ring. He has a intense look in his eye and a determined posture.] [Next out is Sinclair. The cruiser trots down the aisle with a bounce in every step. He uses the ropes as leverage for a flip as he enters the ring.] MP--"Both men look ready for action tonight. Sinclair is as energetic as ever, and Crippler me be as prepared as I've ever seen him." BV--"The winner of this match will be the one who controls the pace. IF Crippler keeps the fight out of the air he will have the edge." MP--"Don Weis is ready to go, and there's the opening bell. They circle each other looking for an opening. Sinclair charges forward and Crippler catches him with a drop toe-hold. Sinclair bounces back up, but Crippler takes him down with a snapmare." BV--"Crippler is off to a good start. He's wrestling better than he has in weeks." MP--"Crippler lets Zeus back up and then sends him running into the ropes. Zeus comes back and is lifted into the air by a big back drop...but he manages to land on his feet! Crippler turns and is met by a big dropkick that sends him reeling into a corner." BV--"These guys change directions so fast it makes my head hurt." MP--"It doesn't take much. Sinclair runs in for a splash, but Crippler steps forward and gives him a boost that almost sends him over the top! Sinclair is stunned and slowly sliding down from the top turnbuckle. Crippler grabs him from behind and hits him with a BIG catapult suplex! Sinclair hit hard." BV--"What a move! Crippler reminds me of me when I was wrestling." MP--"Except he has hair. Crippler's taking advantage of Sinclair being down. He climbs to the top turnbuckle. Flying elbow drop! He connects! Instead of going for the pin immediatly, he stands up and poses for the crowd, they love it! Crippler covers...1...2...3! Crippler wins in a very convincing manner." BV--"Convincing? I'll say. He tore Sinclair limb from limb. Crippler may be able to back up what he says, or maybe not. He stills has a long way to go." MP--"We have a powerhouse match next on Rage. The Enforcer vs. Mad Jester. Enforcer has won some impressive victories here in NCCW. Mad Jester doesn't have the record, but has still shown himself to be a strong competitor." BV--"Both of these guys are big and tough. They were part of a triangle match on an earlier show and have been waiting for a shot at one another ever since." [Gravity kills blasts across the load speakers as the Enforcer maks his way down the ramp. He stops at the bottom to peer at the crowd, then continues to the ring. The crowd voices their approval.] MP--"Enforcer has developed a following here at NCCW. Look at those Enforcer T-shirts." BV--"T-shirts won't win you matches. You have to get the job done in the ring." [A purple spotlight illuminates Mad Jester as he makes his way to ringside. The big man laughs the whole way.] MP--"There's the bell. Jester starts off strong. Both men lock up and Jester drives a knee into Enforcer's midsection and follows it with a headbutt. The Enforcer staggers back into the ropes and Jester charges in with a big splash. He connects and knocks the air out of Enforcer." BV--"Wow! That hit shook the entire ring." MP--"Jester pulls Enforcer to his feet and whips him into the ropes...NO! Enforcer reverses. He took a pounding but he's still going strong. Jester is sent into the corner and Enforecer follows him in with a Mafia kick! What a move by the big man!" BV--"Enforcer is agile, but so is Jester. If the Enforcer doesn't keep on him, Jester could put him down quick." MP--"Jester staggers out of the corner and Enforcer pounds him with kicks and chops until he falls. Jester is sitting in the corner being pummeled until Don Weis pulls the Enforcer back. Jester has shown he can take a lot of punishment and still smile, but he seems a litle dazed here." BV--"Darn right he's dazed. Enforcer just put a major hurtin' on him." MP--"Don Weis lets the Enforcer go again. Enforcer charges in and hits the sitting Jester with a double knee drop. Enforcer hauls him up. Jester fights back with an open handed strike, but Enforcer doubles him over with kick. Enforcer locks him up...fall back piledriver! Enforcer wastes no time going for the cover. 1...2...3! Enforcer wins!" BV--"That was an amazing display of strength by the Enforcer. He came out of the gate strong and never let up. He is well on his way to becoming a major force here in NCCW." MP--"Next here on Rage is the first ever NCCW double title match. Southern Heritage Champion Bobby "Bad Apple" Durango takes on Television Champion "Cajun" Huey Armstrong. The winner walks away with both titles." BV--"Both of these guys are great. Durango is new to the NCCW, but has already captured a title. Armstrong has been here from the beginning and has fought tooth and nail for his title. I don't know. Even my bookie wouldn't put a spread on this one." [The Cajun is the first down the ramp. The TV title belt glitters around his waist as the spotlights sweep across him. The fans get to their feet screaming as he walks down the aisle.] MP--"These fans love their TV Champ." BV--"Sure they do. The TV champ defends his belt every single card. Of all the title holders, he's the most visible." [Pearl Jam's 'Black' plays over the speakers as the Southern Heritage champion makes his way to the ring. Durango has the Southern Heritage belt slung over his shoulder] MP--"This promises to be a memorable match. Cajun has a real size advantage over Durango, but Durango is the quicker of the two." BV--"I'm not going to say a word. With these two in the ring, anything can happen." MP--"Referee Weis has collected both belts and called for the opening bell. Both men start out slow, looking for an advantage. They lock up and battle for control. Armstrong shoves the smaller man back, but nothing comes of it. They lock up again and this time Durango is sent into the ropes. Armstrong sets up for a backdrop, but the Bad Apple delivers a kick to the face instead." BV--"If Armstrong gets cocky this will be over quick." MP--"Durango follows his advantage with a kick to the knee and a standing dropkick to drop the big Cajun flat. Before Armstrong can get up, Durango puts a crosslock on his knee and begins applying pressure." BV--"He's trying to take away the big man's source of leverage. Without his legs, Cajun can't do much at all." MP--"Durango still has the big man locked up, but Armstrong is slowly pulling towards the ropes...he has them. Durango lets the Cajun go and backs off a couple of steps. Durango rushes forward and tries to take the Cajun by surprise, but Armstrong locks up with him and gains control. He has him up and executes a running sidewalk slam!" BV--"That'll change the momentum of the match in a hurry. Durango's going to need to work on Armstrong for a long time to match what the Cajun can do with one move like that." MP--"Both men slowly get to their feet. Durango seems a little shaken up, but Armstrong also appears to be limping slightly. Armstrong sends the Bad Apple back with a series of chops then sends him back into the ropes. The Cajun follows him in and goes up for a dropkick...but Durango grabs the ropes! Armstrong tries to stand, but Durango hits him with a lariat before he can get up all the way." BV--"These guys are really going at it. They both want these two titles so bad they can taste it." MP--"Durango tries to follow up, but Cajun trips him as he gets close. Armstrong is able to get to his feet before Durango and grabs ahold of the smaller man. He lifts him into the air with a vertical suplex! Durango hits hard on his back and the big man tries to picks him back up. The Bad Apple counters with a punch to the midsection, slips a foot behind the Cajun's knee, and shoves him backward. Armstrong falls to the mat and Durango quickly applies another cross-knee lock." BV--"This is a good move on Durango's part. It works on the big man's leg, setting him up for the GBH, and gives Durango a chance to recover. Those high impact moves by the Cajun took a lot out of him." MP--"Durango works on the leg for a while, then stands and plants a fast stomp on the Cajun's knee. Armstrong slides to the ropes and uses them to stand. Durango tries to wrench the arm of Armstrong, but the big Cajun reverses and nearly jerks the Bad Apple's arm out of the socket. Armstrong then jabs an elbow into the ribs under Durango's raised arm and flips him over his shoulder." BV--"Cajun's big, but you can't make the mistake of thinking he's only a powerhouse. Armstrong was a great collegate wrestler and possess good technical skills." MP--"Armstrong takes hold of Durango's legs and tries to turn him over, but the Bad Apple counters by kicking him back. Durango rolls to his feet as Armstrong advances. Armstrong tries to lock up, but Durango drives a shoulder into the big man's gut. Cajun doubles over and Durango hits him with a DDT! Durango tries for the cover. 1...2...3! Durango wins!" BV--"Oh, my! Can you believe that? These guys are awsome!" [Durango is presented with both the Southern Heritage and TV title belts. He puts one over each shoulder and leans against the ropes, breathing heavily.] MP--"Bobby "Bad Apple" Durango is NCCW's first double title holder. He has defeated "Cajun" Heuy Armstrong in a fantastic match." BV--"Don't think for a minute that we've seen the last of Armstrong. A guy like this doesn't have to look too far for another chance at the gold." MP--"We'll see what developes in the rankings, but now its time for the main event here on Rage." BV--"I don't know if that last match can be topped." MP--"Maybe not, but the upcoming match is a title defense for the World Heavyweight championship. Max Maxwell will face Lucky Lambert. Maxwell is on a roll here in NCCW, but Lambert has had his share of setbacks." BV--"Lambert is 'lucky' to have this shot. Maxwell picked him and the executive comittee backed him up. I don't know why." MP--"Whatever the reason, Lambert has a chance at the belt. Here's the challenger now." [Lucky Lambert comes down the ramp. He is much cleaner than he has been on the past few cards. He's wearing a NCCW T-shirt strolls calmly to the ring.] BV--"Uh,oh. Here comes the crowd." [Everyone in the audience is on their feet cheering at the top of their lungs. If possible, the noise gets even louder as "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell steps out onto the ramp. The World Heavyweight belt shines around his waist as he slowly makes his way to ringside. He stops several times along the way to give high fives or greet fans.] MP--"The crowd really loves the Champion. Hey, whose that?" [Another figure has come down the ramp. It's Essex. He has his cane in his hands and is sneaking up behind the champ.] MP--"Maxwell doesn't see him. He's right behind the champion. Wait, Lambert is yelling from the ring. Maxwell looks up and Lambert pulls off the T-shirt." BV--"That's a British flag!" MP--"Lambert has a British flag painted on his chest! He must be teamed with Essex! Essex strikes! He hits Maxwell in the back with the head of the cane then starts pounding him with it. Lambert is out of the ring now and running down the aisle. Now he's kicking Max too!" BV--"Max is in real trouble here! He's getting pummeled out there." MP--"Lambert and Essex are both beating on the World Champion. Here comes someone else. It's Crippler! He charges out from back stage and clotheslines Essex! He's defending Maxwell! Crippler throws Lambert off of Maxwell and sends him stumbling back to the ring. Essex is back up, but Crippler drops him with a superkick! Oh, my!" BV--"What's Crippler doing? Word is he's getting a title shot next week." MP--"I don't know, but Maxwell is up now. He was caught by surprise before, but he's getting back on top of things now. Essex scampers back up the ramp, but Max and Crippler are just staring at each other. Crippler turns and walks back up the ramp to the dressing area. Max looks at lambert up in the ring and slowly shakes his head. He is not a happy camper." BV--"That's like saying it rains a little in Seattle. Maxwell is pissed!" MP--"Maxwell climbs into the ring and hands the World title belt to the referee. Lambert is as far back in the corner as he can get. Weis calls for the bell and this title defense is finally underway." BV--"Its about time." MP--"Maxwell steps to the middle of the ring and challenges Lambert to come forward. Lambert finally does and locks up with the champion. Lambert is staggered by a series of vicious chops and kicks from Maxwell. Lambert backs away and is leveled by a lariat from the champion! Maxwell pulls him to his feet and whips him into the ropes...powerslam! Maxwell is up and working the crowd. They love it!" BV--"He says he's their champion and they believe it. Max is really pumped here. I have a feeling Lambert is going to come out of this looking like cream corn." MP--"Lambert came up behind Max while he was rousing the crowd and hits him with a double ax-handle! The champion stumbles forwards and lambert rakes his back! Lambert rushes him with a lariat...but Max ducks and Lambert goes flying over the top!" BV--"You don't stay champion by being stupid." MP--"Max and Lambert are both taking their time getting up. Finally, Lambert climbs to the apron. Max is in his face. The champion grabs him and uses a suplex to bring him inside! Maxwell doesn't want to let Lambert slip away. He's still too fired up." BV--"I would not like to be Lambert right now." MP--"Maxwell picks Lambert up and hits him with an atomic drop and follows it with a BIG lariat. Lambert is flat on his face. Maxwell signals to the crowd...he's going for the Maxwell stretch! He has it on! Lambert taps out! Maxwell has defended his title dispite the interference of Essex." BV--"What does Crippler get out of helping Maxwell?" MP--"If the talk is true and he does have a title shot next Monday, maybe he just wants to make sure its Maxwell who has the title. Whatever the case, we'll have to wait for another day. We're out of time. Here with Bobby Vaughn, I'm Morgan Penn speaking for NCCW Rage, goodnight!"
