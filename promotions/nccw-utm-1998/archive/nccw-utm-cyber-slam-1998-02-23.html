NCCW Cyber-Slam

23 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens to thousands of screaming fans in the Superdome. Many are waving signs dedicated to their favorite NCCW wrestlers or wearing NCCW T-shirts. The camera pans over the top of the crowd, showing the ring with the NCCW Cyber-Slam logo in the center. Then the camera sweeps down to show the broadcast table with Morgan Penn and Bobby "Viper" Vaugn sitting ready to call the first NCCW Pay-Per-View.] MP--"Hello, everyone, and welcome to Cyber-Slam, NCCW's first Pay-Per-View. I'm Morgan Penn,and with me as always is Bobby Vaugn. We have a great show for you tonight. We have challenge matches, rematches, and three, count 'em, three title matches." BV--"That's right. We're going to see a title defense for the Southern Heritage Title, a brand new TV champ will be crowned, and the very first NCCW World Heavyweight Champion will be decided on tonight's main event." MP--"Three titles, but only five wrestlers will battle for gold tonight. One wrestler, Crippler, the current Southern Heritage champion, will be defending his title against new-comer Bobby "Bad Apple" Durango, as well as competing for the TV title against "Cajun" Huey Armstrong." BV--"Crippler has proved himself to be a top contender, but no one has seen or heard from him since he won the Southern Heritage title on NCCW's very first program. Except for matches, of course. This could be his way of preparing." MP--"We'll find out tonight. As we said, we'll also find out who will bve crowned NCCW World Heavyweight champion. The main event will feature the Mad Goat, an explosive cruiserweight, against "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell, a powerhouse fan favorite." BV--"Mad Goat has suprised me twice already, there's nothing to say he won't do it again. But before we get to the main event, we'll get to see some really big men in action." MP--"Yes indeed, the Mad Jester will take on the Dominator in a rematch. Those two were the finalists in last week's triangle match and both were eliminated at once. Tonight a winner will be decided. The other participant in that triangle match, the Enforcer, will wrestle tonight in a challenge match against Essex." BV--"It'll be interesting to see what strategy Essex uses against the Enforcer. I like that Essex. He'll do whatever it takes to win." MP--"We have one other match tonight, a top contender match for the TV title. Lucky Lambert, who has been anything but lucky, will take on Hill Billy Braddock for first shot at the TV champ. Neither Braddock nor Lambert have won a match here in the NCCW, but both have been fierce competitors." BV--"Luck hasn't been with Lambert lately. He needs to work more on his wrestling skills and less at his card game." MP--"We'll answer a lot of questions and crown at least two new champions tonight. Stay with us as we begin the first ever NCCW pay-per-view, Cyber-Slam!" [Camera pulls back and again shows the capacity crowd. Fireworks explode throughout the arena and the NCCW theme music blasts over the loudspeakers. As the noise dies down and the smoke dissipates, the camera zooms in on an announcer in the center of the ring.] Announcer--"Ladies and gentlemen, NCCW is proud to present Cyber-Slam! The first match tonight is set for one fall. First to the ring, from Great Brittian, accompanied by Lady Anne, Essex!" [Lady Anne exits wearing a tight dress patterned after the British flag. Behind her, dressed in his usual formal attire, is Essex. He actually seems to enjoy the jeers of the crowd.] Announcer--"His opponent, weighing in at over 300 pounds, The Enforcre!" [Gravity Kills plays over the loudspeakers as the Enforcer walks down the ramp. He pauses breifly to look at the fans, then continues to the ring.] MP--"That Enforcer is a big guy, Bobby. How do you think Essex will wrestle him?" BV--"Well, Essex isn't going to let size bother him, he frimly believes he can beat anyone. I'm sure he has something up his sleave." MP--"Don Weis is now in the ring and cautions each of the wrestlers to have a clean match. He calls for the opening bell, and we're underway! The Enforcer steps forward and raises his arms in challenge. The crowd cheers, but Essex isn't impressed. He just keeps his distance and sneers at the big man. The Enforcer tries again, working the crowd. Essex doesn't change his attitude, he still refuses to accept the challenge." BV--"I don't blame him. Enforcer has 50 or 60 pounds on Essex. Essex doesn't have a chance to out power him." MP--"Enforcer is determined to get things started here. He strides up to Essex and stares down at him. Essex has had enough. He shoves the Enforcer, but doesn't move him at all! The Enforcer grabs Essex and slings him across the ring. Essex comes running back and the Enforcer presses him high over his head before letting him fall back to the mat!" BV--"Essex must have been dropped from eight or nine feet up! That is not something you want to make into a habbit." MP--"Essex rolls away under the bottom rope. Lady Anne is with him and together they look up at the Enforcer in the ring. Essex turns around and heads towards backstage! He's leaving!" BV--"I'm not sure I blame him. Enforcer is a big guy ready for business." MP--"Wait a minute! What's this? Someone has come out from backstage...Its Dominator! He's just standing there between Essex and the ramp to the dressing area. He won't let him pass. Here comes the Enforcer! He grabs Essex by the neck and leads him back to the ring." BV--"What's Dominator doing here? This isn't his match. Is there something going on here between him and Essex? Him and The Enforcer?" MP--"I don't know, but Enforcer has gotten Essex back to the ring now and has thrown him back inside. Both men are back in the ring. Enforcer locks up with Essex and slams him down to the mat. Essex backs into the corner on his knees, holding his hands up in front to ward the Enforcer off." BV--"Essex doesn't want any part of this. Look at Lady Anne. She's really worried about her man." MP--"Enforcer sees he has control and turns to the crowd with upraised arms. As soon as his back is turned, Essex charges out and hits the Enforcre in the back! Enforcer staggers and Essex starts choking him in the ropes! Don Weis is finally able to pull Essex away, but the damage has been done. The Enforcer is gasping for breath and looks like he doesn't even know where he is." BV--"That must have been the plan all along. Essex made him think he was in control and then surprised him at a crucial moment." MP--"Essex picks up the Enforcer and pushes him into a corner. He then drives a series of knees into his midsection. Essex climbs the ropes and begins pounding on the Enforcer's head! The Enforcer is really taking a beating now." BV--"Look at Dominator. He's still standing out there in the aisle, staring at the ring. I have no idea what's going on behind that mask of his." MP--"Right now, that's the least of the Enforcer's worries. Essex is still pounding him with fists. Wait! Enforcer grabs Essex and literally throws him halfway acrosst the ring! What power! Essex is back against the ropes with his hands up again. Lady Anne is behind him begging the Enforcer not to come any closer." MP--"Lady Anne doesn't want to see her man get busted up. Enforcer took quite a beating but he's still in control now." BV--Lady Anne is up on the apron now, pleading with referee Don Weis to stop the match. Weis is trying to make her get down, but she grabs ahold of his arm to make him listen to her. Enforcer looks towards the commotion for an instant...and then Essex is up! He has something in his hands...its his walking stick! Lady Anne must have slipped it to him then gone to distract the referee." BV--"Love 'em or hate 'em, you have to admit that was well executed." MP--"Essex swings the silver-handled cane at the Enforcer...the Enforcer catches it midswing! He caught the walking stick! He rips it out of Essex's hands and flings it out into the crowd!" BV--"There's a lucky fan." MP--"Essex is stunned. He's just staring wide-eyed at the Enforcer and Lady Anne ahs given up trying to coerce Don Weis. Enforcer rocks Essex back with a chop then plants a kick to his stomache. He grabs Essex and spins him into piledriver possition. Wait...he lifts him up over his head. Inverted powerbomb! Essex is out cold! Enforcer rolls him over and covers him with one hand. 1...2...3! Oh, my!" BV--"Essex's plan backfired. Enforcer was waiting for something like that to happen. Dominator is leaving now. He must have seen everything he wanted." MP--"The Enforcer is your winner. Lady Anne is still trying to get Essex up. What an opening match here on Cyber-Slam. Essex is finally able to limp back to the dressing area with Lady Anne's help. Here comes the announcer." Announer--"The next contest is set for one fall and will determine the top contender for he Television Title. Entering first, from Mississippi, Hill Billy Braddock!" [Banjos play as Hill Billy makes his entrance. He makes his way to the ring with a ceramic jug under one arm.] Announcer--"His opponent, from Las Vegas, Nevada, Lucky Lambert! [Lambert walks down the ramp with no music playing in the background. He isn't smiling and walks to the ring slowly, without his usual fanfare. Instead of his normal white and red, he is wearing all black.] BV--"What's with Lucky? Look at him. He looks like he hasn't shaved or bathed in days. He looks more like "Hard Luck" Lambert now." MP--"Lambert has been on a losing streak. Maybe its finally gotten to him. Anyway, the referee calls for the bell and the match begins. Hill Billy charges, but Lambert is just standing there as the 330 pound Braddock closes in...sidestep! Lambert sidestepped at the last possible instant and Braddock crashes into the corner!" BV--"Another half a second and Lambert would have been roadkill." MP--"Lambert isn't making any move towards Hill Billy and Braddock finally frees himself from the corner. Braddock moves in again, this time more slowly. They lock up and Lambert is sent into the corner. Hill Billy charges in after him...but Lambert drops to the mat and Braddock slams into the turnbuckle again!" BV--"Lambert is playing a dangerous game here. One wrong move and he's toast. He's wrestling like a man with nothing to lose." MP--"Maybe he doesn't. Lambert pushes Hill Billy back out of the corner and nails him with a series of chops. Hill Billy comes around and tries for big roundhouse punch. Lambert ducks underneath and kicks Braddock in the back of the knee. Braddock goes down and Lambert follow up with a jumping knee drop to the back of Braddock's leg." BV--"That's gotta hurt, and more than that it's going to weaken that leg quite a bit." MP--"Hill Billy is slow to get to his feet and Lambert takes advantage by delivering more kicks to his weakened leg. Braddock finally stands and locks up with Lambert. Lambert is sent into the ropes and comes back towards Hill Billy. Bearhug! Braddock has Lambert locked into a big bearhug!" BV--"This could be it. Lambert may have worked on his leg, but Braddock's arms are still strong as can be." MP--"Braddock is still squeezing Lambert. Lambert is struggling for a way out. He kicks Braddock in the knee and Braddock's hold loosens somewhat. Lambert kicks again and Hill Billy loses is grip completely." BV--"Lambert found a weak spot. That knee can barely hold the big guy up now." MP--"Lambert kicks him one more time and Braddock falls to the mat. Lambert grabs the weakened leg and locks on the leg twisting, knee wrenching submission hold he calls the Roulette. Hill Billy taps out at once, but Lambert refuses to let go. Finally, Don Weis is able to get Lambert to let go. Lucky Lambert wins the match and will be the first person to challenge the new TV champion." BV--"You mean "Hard Luck" Lambert. Look at him, he didn't even wait around to hear his win be announced. He just turned around and walked backstage." MP--"There have deffinatly been some changes with Lambert. The ring is clear now and the announcer is ready to call the next match." Announcer--"The following match is set for one fall adn is a title defence for the Southern Heritage title. First, the challenger, from New Zealand, Bobby "Bad Apple" Durango!" [Durango comes down the ramp and takes a look around at the audience. He laughs once then continues on to the ring.] Announcer--"Ladies and gentlemen, I now give you the Southern Heritage Champion, Crippler." [Fireworks explode as the Southern Heritage champ enters the arena. The fans cheer loudly as the Crippler walks down the aisle with the title belt around his waist. He steps into the ring and hands the belt to referee Don Weis.] MP--"Crippler looks ready as ever, but this Durango guy is a complete unknown. He signed just under the deadline to participate in the pay-per-view and challenged for the Southern Heritage title." BV--"I've heard about this Durango character. If half of what they say is true, we're going to see a very exciting match." MP--"The opening bell has sounded and both wrestlers begin to circle, looking for an opening. Crippler strikes first with a chop to the chest, but Durango counter with a boot to the midsection. Next, the Crippler is sent into the ropes, but ducks under Durango's clothesline on the return." BV--"Both of these wrestlers have a similiar style. Intense and all-out." MP--"Durango locks up with Crippler again and this time Crippler gains control with a side headlock. Durango counters by pushing both of them into the ropes and forcing Crippler to let go. Crippler connects with an overhand fist to the side of the head and Durango staggers back. Crippler grabs him...belly-to-belly suplex!" BV--"Crippler just exploded with that move. Durango is flat on the mat." Mp--"Crippler delivers a stomp to the downed Durango. Durango rolls away from the second and uses the ropes to climb to his feet. Crippler charges at him, but Durango ducks down and pulls the top rope down as well. Crippler goes flying out of the ring and crashes into the guard rail!" BV--"Crippler needed a pilot's license for that move." MP--"Durango is outside with Crippler now and pulls the Southern Heritage champ to his feet. Durango smashes Crippler into the guard rail and then into the ring post. Crippler is just being led around by Durango until "Bad Apple" rolls him back into the ring. Crippler somehow manages to get to his feet before Durango can follow him back inside and kicks him on his way in." BV--"Crippler may be fighting back, but he's still woozy from the beating he took outside the ring." MP--"Durango is standing now. Crippler tries to toss him into the ropes but Durango reverses and Crippler is sent running instead. Durango hits him with a belly-to-belly as he comes off the ropes!" BV--"Turn about is fair play." MP--"Durango picks him up and nails him with a back-breaker! Crippler is sprawled out on the mat. Durango rolls him over and locks on an STF...wait, Durango rolls both of them back over until the Crippler is pinned in a modified STF above Durango. Its the GBH! What Durango calls the Grievous Bodily Harm! Crippler submits! Bobby "Bad Apple" Durango wins! We have a new NCCW Southern Heritage Champion!" BV--"Durango out fought him, plain and simple. Crippler still has a shot at the TV title later, but he better get some rest while he can." MP--"Don Weis presents Durango with the belt and he climbs to the top turnbuckle displaying it to the crowd. The audience loves it! This is only the first title match here on Cyber-Slam, we still have two more to go!" Announcer--"The following contest is a rematch and is set for one fall. First, standing 6'9 and weighing over 300 pounds, the Dominator." [Dominator makes his way to the ring with his theme music playing over the loudspeakers.] MP--"Here's Dominator again, just what was that all about during the Enforcer/Essex match?" Announcer--"Next, his opponent, The Mad Jester!" [Jester makes his way to the ring with without music and guided only by a purple spotlight. As always, he's laughing hysterically and carrying his scepter." MP--"Jester is a strange one alright. But no time for that now. Jester has attacked Dominator before the bell! Weis officially starts the match, but Jester has already started pounding on Dominator with knees and forearms." BV--"Jester is just getting a jump on things, namely Dominator." MP--"Dominator is fighting back now. He hits the Jester with double ax-handle that sends the laughing man reeling and follows it up with a big lariat. Jester doesn't drop, but does fall against the ropes, laughig the whole time. On his way back, Jester leaps high into the air and slams into Dominator. He uses his weight and momentum to drop the bigger man." BV--"Jester's pretty agile for a heavy man." Mp--"Jester stands and attempts to hit Dominator with a big splash, but Dominator manages to roll aside. Both men are standing and lock up. Dominator drives a knee into the chin of Jester and leaves the laughing man stunned! He follows up with a headbutt and a bodyslam!" BV--"Dominator is taking control here. He doesn't want to give Jester a chance to get back in this thing." MP--"Dominator leaps into the air for a legdrop...and hits! He covers Jester. 1...2...only a two count. Dominator stands and pulls Jester up. Dominator's right hand is blocked, and Jester slams a shoulder into Dominator's gut! He leans into the ropes for momentum and then topples the Dominator with form of flying body-press! Don Weis counts. 1...2...kick out at two and a half." BV--"Jester is still in this thing, you can't count him out yet." MP--"Jester slings him into a corner and follows him in with a BIG splash! Now he climbs to the second rope and begins pummeling the Dominators head. Dominator may be finished. Jester picks Dominator up and sets him on the top turnbuckle! He may be going for a super-plex here! Wait! Dominator pushes him clear off the turn buckle! The big man is now standing up top...and leaps off! The big man is airborne! Leg drop from the top! Dominator rolls him up. 1...2...3! Dominator wins!" BV--"Both of these guys are actually very agile. They're some of the quickest big men I've ever seen in the sport." MP--"Dominator gets the win here. Guess what, Bobby." BV--"Your underwear is riding up?" MP--"Well...actually I was going to say that our last two matches tonight are both title contentions. The Television title is next followed by the World Heavyweight title match." BV--"Sure, there's that too." Announcer--"Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest will determine the NCCW Television champion. It is set for one fall. First, the former NCCW Southern Heritage Champion, Crippler." [Crippler walks down the entrance ramp. He doesn't look happy about losing the Southern Heritage title but looks ready for the match.] Announcer--"Next, his opponent, from Natchitoches, Louisiana, "Cajun" Huey Armstrong!" [Cajun comes down the ramp to the cheer of the crowd. He looks pumped up and ready for action.] MP--"These two were semi-finalists for the World title, so this should be a good match." BV--"Cajun has a lot of size on Crippler, but Crip is a tough fighter." MP--"Theres the bell. Crippler charges straight in, trying to take control early, but Armstrong uses his momentum to throw Crip into the ropes and catches him with a big boot as he bounces off. Crippler is right back up and trading chops with the bigger Cajun." BV--"I told you he was tough. It's hard to keep him down." MP--"Crippler connects with a standing dropkick that sends the Cajun down. Crippler is able to stomp Armstrong a couple of times before he gets to his feet. They lock up again, but this time Crippler is lifted high into the air slammed back down. Armstrong picks him up again and flings him into the ropes...Tilt-a-Whirl back-breaker! Oh, my!" BV--"I don't care how good of condition you are in, two matches in one night is going to take a lot out of you. Crippler is not at 100% here." MP--"Maybe not. Crippler tries to fight back, but is having no effect on the Cajun. His punches are doing no damage. Cajun doubles the Crippler over with a boot to the stomache. He hooks him up...Jawbreaker! Crippler pops back up but the lights are out, that move sent his brain on a vacation. Cajun catches him with a dropkick, the second half of his Shrimp Kabob combination! Crippler is down and Armstrong covers! 1...2...3! "Cajun" Huey Armstrong wins the TV title!" BV--"I don't know is Crippler was exhausted from his first match or what, but Cajun just overwhelmed him in there." MP--"Yes, indeed. Don Weis gives Cajun the TV belt and he proudly displays it to the capacity crowd here. He probably has a lot of fans in the audience, being from around here and all." [The ring is finally cleared and the moment everyone has been waiting for arrives. The lights dim as the announcer makes his way to the ring.] Announcer--"Ladies and gentlemen, this is the last time you will hear me say that tonight! The following contest is set for one fall with no time limit. The winner will be crowned NCCW World Heavyweight Champion! First, accompanied by Mother Earth, The Mad Goat!" [Mother Earth and Mad Goat make their way to ringside amidst fireworks and cheers from the crowd.] BV--"Goat is one tough competitor. Maxwell better not let that weird costume fool him, Goat isn't someone to take lightly." Announcer--"His opponent, wieghing in at 257 pounds, "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell!" [Maxwell steps out from backstage and the arena erupts. Fans are screaming and several women are holding signs that read "We love you Max!". Maxwell slaps hands all the way down the aisle to ringside.] MP--"Maxwell is definatly a favorite of this NCCW crowd. They love to see him in the ring." BV--"He loves it too. When they start to cheer its like he has a partner in the ring with him." MP--"The opening bell sounds and the first ever NCCW World title match is underway. Mad Goat goes straight for Maxwell, but Max steps out of his way, slowing the pace down." BV--"That's smart. If the Goat picks up the tempo, he can hit Maxwell with so many moves he'll think he's fightin an army." MP--"Finally, Maxwell lets the Goat lock up with him. Maxwell takes control and locks Goat with an armbar. He keeps the pressure on for a minute, the steps back and twists the arm around. He keeps pressure on the arm and forces the Goat to the mat. Wait, Goat bridges up, stands, and flips until the pressure is off of his arm. Maxwell still has Goat's arm, but the cruiser hits the Natural Wonder with a standing dropkick anyway! Both men go down, but the Goat is free." BV--"Goat has more tricks than a stage magician." MP--"Both wrestlers are up. Goat charges Maxwell and hits him with a shoulder. Maxwell stumbles back but quickly recovers and sends Goat back with a shoulder of his own. Maxwell presses his advantage by locking on a side headlock." BV--"Max is in control right now. He's keeping Goat on the mat and keeping the tempo slow." MP--"Max has looked like he's wanted to work on Goat's legs several times, but the Goat has never given him an opportunity. It's almost as if the Goat expects something like that and has been careful to avoid it. Anyway, maxwell has control now, but the Mad Goat drives an elbow into Maxwell...and another. After four elbow shots, Maxwell lets go and the Goat backs off to think things over." BV--"Goat still amazes me, I thought Maxwell had him down twice already, but Goat keeps finding a way to get free." MP--"Maxwell is still keeping the pace slow as Goat charges again. Maxwell readies himself for another launched attack...but the Goat slides between his legs instead! Goat tries to roll Maxwell up, but The Natural Wonder counters by dropping down on the cruiser's chest. Mad Goat's shoulders are pinned! 1...2...Goat kicks out at two and a half, barely. Maxwell drags the Goat to his feet and puts him back on the mat with a bodyslam. Max tries for an elbow drop, but the cruiser rolls aside and Maxwell comes crashing down." BV--"Maxwell gave Goat two seconds to recover. Only two seconds, but Goat was able to capitalize." MP--"Goat kips back up to his feet and Maxwell stands back up. Head scissor take down and Maxwell is back on the mat! Goat turns around...standing moonsault body press!" BV--"Just like that the Mad Goat was able to pick the tempo way up and start wrestling his kind of match. Maxwell better be careful now." MP--"The Goat is the first one up. he runs into the ropes and comes off just as Maxwell regains his feet. Goat tries for a sunset flip! He has him down. 1..2..only a two count. Both men are back on their feet. Goat leaps up and puts The Natural Wonder on the mat with a crucifix takedown and then turns it into a modified crucifix submission! Maxwell is down in the center of the ring with nowhere to go. This could be it." BV--"Uh, oh. Listen to that crowd. They're starting to chant for Max. This could spell trouble for the Mad Goat." MP--"As if in response, Maxwell is trying to get up. The Goat is finally forced to let go of the submission and begins kicking the Natural Wonder. No effect! Maxwell is on his feet now and the crowd is roaring. The noise is deafening! Goat charges and tries for a Frankensteiner...Oh my lord! Goat set up for the Frankensteiner, but before he could execute the flip, Maxwell dropped him down into a modified powerbomb! Maxwell rolls him over and applies the Maxwell Stretch! Tap out! The Goat taps out! We have our winner and very first NCCW World Heavyweight Champion. "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell ahs defeated the Mad Goat in an increadible match." [Mother Earth helps the Mad Goat backstage while Don Weis presents Maxwell with the title belt. As Maxwell raises the belt high over his head in victory, the audience screams even louder until the volume is almost painful. The cheering lasts for several minutes until finally, Maxwell goes backstage with his new belt.] MP--"Wow, what an evening! Three title matches, three new champions, and lots of excitement." BV--"You forgot unanswered questions. Why was Dominator so interested in the Enforcer/Essex match? Why was Zeus Sinclair not on the card tonight?" MP--"Maybe we'll find out the answers on another NCCW card, but for right now we're out of time. For Bobby Vaugn and everyone else here at NCCW, I'm Morgan Penn, goodnight."
