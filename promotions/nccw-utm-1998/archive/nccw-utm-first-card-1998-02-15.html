NCCW First Card

15 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens to the first ever NCCW Monday Night Rage. It sweeps accross the cheering crowd, some of whom are waving signs dedicated to their favorite NCCW stars. The camera settles on the NCCW logo above the runway, then pans down to the opening. From back stage walks the ring announcer. He strides to the ring and takes hold of a microphone lowered from above.] Announcer--"Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the Pyramid in Memphis, Tennessee and the first ever NCCW Wednesday Night Rage! I would like to introduce the commentators for tonights action packed card. First, hailing from Austin, Texas, a five time World Heavyweight Champion, Bobby "The Viper" Vaughn." [Out walks Vaughn (JV), waving to the crowd. He walks to the broadcast booth and takes a seat after putting on his headphones.] Announcer--"Next, from Augusta, Maine, a veteran announce for the New England Wrestling Board, Morgan Penn." [Penn (MP) enters from backstage and takes his place next to JV at the broadcast booth.] Announcer--"Now, NCCW is proud to present ... Wednesday Night Rage!! [As Announcer exits the ring, the camera pans the crowd again and comes to rest on the broadcast booth.] MP--"Hello everybody and welcome to Wednesday Night Rage. I'm sure we're all looking forward to an exciting card this evening. Bobby, what do you think about our match-ups tonight?" BV--"Well, I was backstage earlier, getting a look at some of the talent the NCCW has, and I just want to say that I'm glad some of these guys weren't in the sport while I was a contender. This is going to be a wild night." MP--"Here we go! The opponents for our very first match here on Rage are ready to make their apperance. First out is the Cajun, Huey Armstrong. Wow! He's a tall one." ["Cajun" Huey Armstrong steps out from back stage and fireworks explode around the entry ramp. He lifts his arms and waves to the screaming crowd.] BV--"This guy was a great collegate wrestler. Physically, one of the biggest to ever compete." MP--"According to his bio, he was very good indeed. What kind of advantage does that kind of size give you in an on-themat type of contest, Bobby?" BV--"Leverage. Its all about leverage. The Cajun's mat skills are going to be helped out a lot by his size." [The Cajun has entered the ring and begins to loosen up. His opponent then enters from backstage.] MP--"Here's his opponent. The big man from somewhere in Mississippi, "Hill Billy" Braddock. That's another big athlete, Viper." BV--"I don't know if you could call Barddock an athlete. He's more like a bulldozer on two legs." MP--"How do you think he stacks up against Armstrong?" BV--"Armstrong has the advantage on skills, but I don't know if he can stand toe-to-toe with the Hill Billy. That's a lot of man." MP--"We're about to see. Referee Don Weis calls for the bell and we're underway here at NCCW." BV--"Right away Armstrong locks up with the Hill Billy, testing his strength." MP--"Ohh! The Hill Billy just shoves him down! Braddock is showing power early. Armstrong is back to his feet and now they're exchanging slaps and punches. Braddock sends him to the mat again!" BV--"The Cajun is going to have to use something besides brute strength here. He's getting pulped." MP--"I think he realizes that now. He's circling Braddock, looking for an opening. He backs against the ropes and runs at the Hill Billy. He dives at Braddock's feet, taking the big man's feet out from under him!" BV--"There you go! Armstrong delivers a big elbow drop then hauls Braddock to his feet. Braddock seems to be limping a little. They lock up again. Armstrong sends him into the ropes and hits him with a dropkick." MP--"Braddock staggers against the ropes but comes charging back. He knocks the Cajun flat with a shoulder block then follows it with a BIG splash. Both men are up. They're locked up. This time Braddock is sent into the ropes. Armstrong takes him down with a dropkick to the knee!" BV--"Now Armstrong's thinking. Work on the big man's legs and you can run rings around him." MP--"Armstrong's is indeed working on the legs now. Hill Billy seems to be in some pain. The Cajun pulls him to his feet." BV--"Bad move. Should have kept him on the mat." MP--"They trade chops then the Cajun is pushed into a corner. Braddock is on him tight, pounding on him with big right hands. Armstrong seems stunned and Braddock lifts him to the top turnbuckle." BV--"Armstrong better be careful here. Hill Billy could end this quick." MP--"Braddock climbs up, could be going for a superplex. Wow! Armstrong nails him with a forearm to the face." BV--"He was playing dead, waiting for Braddock to get cocky. Good strategy against this bruiser." MP--"Armstrong stands up and grabbs hold of the Hill Billy. OH MY GOD!! A reverse bulldog from the top! Braddock isn't moving. Armstrong is slow getting up. He finally makes the cover. Don Weis starts the count. 1...2...3! The winner of our very first match is "Cajun" Huey Armstrong! An incredible first match here on Rage!" BV--"Armstrong finally decided he couldn't out muscle this guy. I don't know if anyone can out muscle this guy. Armstrong had to out maneuver him. Braddock got cocky, but I bet it won't happen again." MP--"The Cajun advances in the NCCW Heavyweight tournament and we have to prepare for our next match." BV--"Here comes next guy now." [Fireworks explode around the walkway as "Lucky" Lambert makes his way to the ring. He waves to the fans and even tosses poker chips into the audience.] MP--"He seems sure of himself." BV--"He's got a cocky grin on his face. We saw what happened to the last guy who got too cocky." MP--"We'll see soon if he has the skills to back up his attitude. Here comes his opponent." [From backstage comes "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell. He raises both arms out from his sides and gestures to the crowd. The Pyramid erupts. Fans, especially the women, are screaming madly. He starts walking to the ring amidst the cheers.] BV--"What's he..? Oh, my." MP--"He's stopped to let a female fan feel his biceps. Those are large biceps too." BV--"He's not the tallest wrestler in the NCCW, but he sure has a lot packed onto his frame." MP--"The crowd really loves him. Let's see what he can do in the ring." [Maxwell enters thte ring and referee Don Weis calls for the opening bell.] MP--"The match is underway here. The two wrestler are circling each other. They lock up and Lambert gains control with a side headlock. Maxwell pushes him into the ropes and catches him with a knee to the midsection as he comes off." BV--"Both of these guys are just waiting to pick up the tempo. These are two high impact wrestlers. They would really do better to pick up the pace." MP--"Maxwell picks Lucky up and hits him with a body slam. Picks him up again. Maxwell tries for a vertical suplex, Lambert blocks it ... and reverses! Maxwell is on the mat now, and Lambert locks on an armbar." BV--"Its going to take a while to wear down those arms." MP--"Yes, indeed. The Natural Wonder is reaching out to the ropes now. He has them and Don Weis tells Lambert to break it up. Both men are on their feet trading chops. Maxwell shoves Lambert into the corner and follows him in with shoulder to the midsection. He gives him one more for good measure, then whips him accross the ring to the opposite corner. Maxwell follows him in again... and catches a boot to the face from Lambert." BV--"He's going to have to do better than that." MP--"Lucky Lambert hauls him to his feet, nails him with a huge knee lift, and sends him right back down again. Lambert picks him back up and sends him into the ropes. Lucky tries for a clothesline...Maxwell ducks underneath, bounces off the opposite ropes...and nearly takes Lambert's head off with a BIG clothesline of his own." BV--"Lambert didn't know what hit him. He just turned around and and got flattened with that huge forearm." MP--"Maxwell looks like he's trying to take control now. He raises his arms above his head and listens to the cheers from the crowd. He pulls Lambert to is feet, lifts him up, and hits him with a backbreaker. Lucky is fighting to get to his feet, but Maxwell is working the crowd again." BV--"Maxwell better press the advantage here. If he lets Lambert get up, he could be in trouble." MP--"Well, Lambert is on his feet now, but he's not very steady. Maxwell, grabs Lambert's arm and twists it around. Ooh! Lambert makes him let go with a headbutt then takes him down with a snapmare." BV--"Lucky may be able to back up his attitude after all." MP--"Lambert's working on the arm again. The crowd's really cheering for Max Maxwell. Maxwell is trying to get to his feet now, but Lambert is still keeping pressure on the arm. Maxwell is up! Maxwell reverses Lambert's hold and then sends him running into the ropes. Wow! Maxwell catches him in a powerslam that shakes the whole ring." BV--"My teeth are still rattling over here." MP--"The Natural Wonder is up. He's got Lambert's legs. He locks him into big Boston Crab he calls the Maxwell Stretch. This could be it!" BV--"Maxwell really has great technique on that. The legs are locked tight and he's sitting way back. That gives him lots of leverage." MP--"Referee Don Weis is checking on Lucky Lambert now. Lambert taps out! We have our winner! [Don Weis raises The Natural Wonder's hand in victory. The crowd, especially the women, are cheering madly. Maxwell enjoys the moment, then heads backstage.] MP--"Max Maxwell joins Huey Armstrong in the winner's bracket for the Heavyweight tournament. We're really having an action packed night here on NCCW Rage!" BV--"I've been impressed with the caliber of these wrestlers so far. All four of them have been terrific athletes." MP--"Yes they have, but there's still more to come." [Fireworks explode as the Mad Goat exits from backstage with Mother Earth. He looks at the cheering crowd solemnly then continues on his way to the ring.] MP--"The participants for our next match are now on their way to the ring. First is the Mad Goat, accompanied by Mother Earth." BV--"Mad Goat? This guy must be a little nuts...Is that woman wearing leaves?" MP--"Mother Earth, yes she is wearing a dress of leaves." BV--"Well then, I'm all for deforestation." MP--"You would be. Now making his way to the ring is Zues Sinclair." BV--"He looks like he needs to switch to decaf." [Zeus Sinclair makes his way to the ring. He bounces from foot to foot with barely contained energy. He dives beneath the bottom rope and kips up to his feet. He continues to jog in place until Don Weis calls for the opening bell.] MP--"Immediatly the two wrestlers charge in and lock up. Zeus gains control with a side headlock, but the Goat quickly pushes him into the ropes. The Goat drops flat as Sinclair comes off of the ropes and jumps over him. Sinclair rebounds agian and this time the Mad Goat leaps over him! Zeus bounces off the ropes for a third time...and the Goat nails him with a dropkick." BV--"I think I'm getting dizzy." MP--"Zeus is back on his feet quickly. Charges the Mad Goat again...armdrag takedown. Zeus is up...another armdrag takedown." BV--"This guy has a lot of energy, but I don't know about smarts." MP--"Again, Sinclair is quick to regain his feet. The Goat locks him up from behind, he may be trying for a German suplex. Zeus Sinclair does a standing switch...and hits him with a big over-the-top suplex! The Mad Goat was way up in the air on that one!" BV--"Maybe he has more upstairs than I thought." MP--"Sinclair pulls the Goat to his feet and takes him right back down with a snapmare. The Mad Goat is down in the middle of the ring. Sinclair climbs up top. He's standing on the top ropes gesturing to the crowds. The Mad Goat leaps up! The Goat dropkicks Zeus in the leg and knocks him to the mat." BV--"How many times do I have to say it? Sinclair got cocky. He's got an Olympic sized ego." MP--"Now the Goat is going up top! He leaps off and hits Sinclair with a big legdrop across the face. The Mad Goat gets up, but Sinclair is still down. Goat runs to the ropes, vaults off of the second rope, and leaps back towards the prone Sinclair. Ohh! Sinclair rolls away at the last second and Goat hits hard." BV--"These guys are almost suicidal." MP--"Zeus stomps the mad Goat, then pulls him to his feet. The Goat gets whiped into the ropes...Sinclair tries for a sunset filp! The referee counts..1..2..Goat kicks out. Both men are back on their feet now. The Mad Goat is still groggy and Zeus has no trouble sending him into the corner. Sinclair backs into the opposite corner and then charges at the stunned Goat with a full head of steam. Sinclair leaps into the air...and the Goat ducks! Zeus Sinclir dove shoulder first into the turnbuckle and is now hung up in the ropes." BV--"I told you these guys are crazy. He may have dislocated his shoulder with that move." MP--"The Mad Goat drags Sinclair out of the ropes and into the center of the ring. The Goat slams him with a swinging neck breaker! Goat pulls him up and sets him up for his finisher! Running Powerbomb! He calls it the Earth's Fury! Whatever it is it put Zeus down. Don Weis counts. 1...2...3! The Mad Goat pins Zeus Sinclair and advances to the next level of the NCCW tournament." [The Mad Goat and Mother Earth return backstage. Zeus Sinclair is slower getting to his feet and has to be helped backstage.] MP--"That was quite a match, Viper. What do you think of the Mad Goat's chances in the next level?" BV--"Well, he doesn't have the size or power of Maxwell and Armstrong, but he's fast and he's not afraid to try those high risk moves. He's got a chance, but he has to wrestle his match, not theirs." MP--"We'll see. It's just about time for our main event here on Rage." BV--"Yeah, I see the penguin's on his way." [The ring announcer, wearing an tuxedo, is making his way into the ring. He pulls down the microphone and looks into the audience. The crowd falls silent as he begins.] Announcer--"Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time for the main event! This contest is set for one fall and will determine the NCCW Southern Heritage Champion! First, with the lovely Lady Anne, Essex!" [The largest fireworks yet explode, leaving a smokey haze around the entrance from backstage. Throught the haze, holding aloft the British flag, is a very attractive woman. She steps out and gestures back towards the ramp. Behind her comes a muscular man wearing a gentleman's claok and holding a silver-tipped walking stick. He surveys the crowd once, then he and the woman continue to ring side.] Announcer--"Next, his opponent, accompanied by no one, the Crippler!" [The fireworks leave even more smoke, making it almost impossible to see through. Striding out of this haze is the Crippler. He doesn't even pause as he walks down the ramp towards ring side. He enters the ring opposite of Essex and waits for the bell.] MP--"This has all the makings of a great match. Two very competitive wrestlers, a packed crowd, and a title on the line." BV--"Both of these guys will do whatever it takes to win. That's exactly the kind of wrestler who should hold the Southern Heritage Title." MP--"Don Weis is ready, there's the bell and here we go! The Crippler immediatly locks up with Essex. They struggle with each other and Crippler is sent into the ropes. As he comes back Exxex sends him to the floor with a hip toss. The Crippler gets back to his feet quickly. Essex backs the Crippler into a corner with a series of European uppercuts. The Crippler looks dazed." BV--"Crippler's already taken a lot of punishment. Essex came out really strong. If he can keep this pace up the Crippler doesn't stand a chance." MP--"Essex delivers a headbutt and the Crippler sinks to the mat. Essex doesn't seem to be worried about Crippler. He's back in the center of the ring showing these American fans exactly what he thinks of them." BV--"It's not very nice, I'll tell you that." MP--"The crowd doesn't like it at all. They're booing like they're ready to throw fruit. Uh-oh...Crippler wasn't as out of it as we thought. He's on his feet and moving toward Essex. Lady Anne sees him. She screams and points behind Essex...too late. Crippler takes him down with a lariat as he turns around." BV--"Maybe I should teach a class for these guys: How To Not Be A Cocky Bastard 101." MP--"Whatever. Crippler picks Essex up and hits him with a boot to the midsection. Essex doubles over and...Crippler hits him with a swinging neck breaker! Crippler delivers some stomps to the back then Essex gets to his feet." BV--"The way he's wobblin' I'm surprised he can stand at all." MP--"Crippler bounces against the ropes...and falls face first into the mat! He was tripped by Lady Anne!" BV--"That's the problem with fighting someone with a manager, you have to have eyes in the back of your head." MP--"Essex has had time to regain his senses now. He sees Crippler down and picks him up by the hair. A European uppercut sends Crippler back into the ropes. Essex spins him around and is choking him with the second rope. Referee Weis is finally able to break it up and pushes Essex back. Oh my! While the referee's back is turned, Lady Anne starts choking Crippler again! She stops before Weis sees her." BV--"That little lady is something else. I'd like to see her after the show." MP--"I'm not sure you would live through it. Crippler really is dazed this time and Essex has no trouble sending him into the corner. Essex lifts him to the top rope, climbs up with him, and sets him up for a superplex. He's got him up...superplex! Essex is slow to his feet, but Crippler isn't moving at all. Essex falls on him for the cover...1...2...Crippler kicks out!" BV--"I never would have believed it. Crippler must be made of iron or something." MP--"Essex is confused alright. He thought he had him. He pulls Crippler to his feet...and takes a fist to the gut from Crippler! Crippler's fighting back now. He kicks Essex in the midsection to double him over...snap suplex! Crippler isn't waisting any time now." BV--"Good man. He knows when time is important and when not to fool around." MP--"He has Essex up again. Tries for a suplex...can't lift him." BV--"He's just worn out...no strength left." MP--"He still has him hooked for a suplex. Essex starts to stir, he bettr do something soon. DDT! Essex is out cold! Crippler covers...1...2...3! Crippler wins! The Crippler has won the NCCW Southern Heritage Title and advances in the heavyweight tournament. [Don Weis holds Crippler's hand up in victory and presents him with the Southern Heritage belt. Crippler climbs to the top tournbuckle and holds the belt over his head. The crowd explodes with cheers and all over the Pyramid fireworks erupt. The noise is deafening and lasts for over ten minutes, even after the Crippler has gone backstage and Lady Anne helps Essex up and back.] MP--"We have had an exciting night here on NCCW Monday Night Rage. We've had four wrestlers advance to the second round of the Heavyweight Championship Tournament. We also have a brand new NCCW Southern Heritage champion and it will be interesting to see if he can hold on to it." BV--"That's a hard title to keep. Almost as hard to keep as the World Title. Who knows, Crippler may win that one as well." MP--"Who knows? Well, for Bobby Vaugn and everyone here at NCCW Monday Night Rage, I'm Morgan Penn. Good night."
