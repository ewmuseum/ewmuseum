NCCW Wednesday Night Rage

19 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera zooms into the NCCW logo spray painted on a brick wall. Suddenly, the wall explodes in a shower of sparks and smoke. Behind is a view of the Sports Arena in Greer, South Carolina taken from high up in the rafters. Below, thousands of fans have packed into the stadium, eagerly awaiting the action of Wednesday Night Rage. The camera pans across the audience and settles on the broadcast booth.] MP--"Hello everybody, I'm Morgan Penn and here with me is Bobby "Viper" Vaugn. We have a great show for you tonight. We have second round action in the Heavyweight Tournament as well as other exciting action. We'll even get a look at some brand new wrestlers tonight." BV--"Your right, it should be a very exciting night. We should see some great action." [Classical music begins to play over the stadium speakers. From backstage walks Lady Anne carrying a British flag and waving to the crowd. Behind her steps Essex and he is greeted by a chorus of boos and catcalls.] MP--"The fans here don't seem to care much for Essex." BV--"He doesn't mind, though, he feels the same way about them." MP--"He's got his work cut out for him tonight, though. "Hill Billy" Braddock is a tough opponent." BV--"Speaking of ...here comes the Hill Billy now." [Dueling banjos plays over the loud speakers as "Hilly Billy" Braddock walks out from backstage. He is dressed in coverally and carrying a ceramic whisky jug under one arm. He yells and belches all the way down the aisle.] BV--"This guy is disgusting, but he's like a bulldozer in the ring." MP--"He is deffinatly a powerhouse. Referee Don Weis is in the ring and ready to start the match. There's the opening bell and we are on our way! Essex is circling, looking for an opening to use against Braddock. Hill Billy is just standing there looking at him. Essex makes his move! He drives a shoulder into the midsection of Braddock, but the Hill Billy doesn't move and inch." BV--"Ouch! Essex may have bitten off more than he can chew here." MP--"Essex backs off in a hurry and circles again. He holds up his hands and moves in close again, attempting to lock up with Braddock." BV--"What is thinking? He can't lock up with that mountain." MP--"Maybe not, but that's what he's trying to do. Hill Billy locks hands with him...and Essex stomps his foot! The big man staggers back and Essex stomps him again. And again! Braddock is leaning against the ropes with his injured foot lifted. Essex runs at him and slams a shoulder into Braddock's gut. This time the Hill Billy feels it." BV--"You have to be careful with Essex. He's sneaky." MP--"Essex grabs one of Braddock's arms whips him across the ring. Braddock only makes it about halfway before his foot gives out and he falls. Essex works the crowd and is rewarded with more boos and shouts. He seems to enjoy it." BV--"Oh, he just loves it. He's showing these american's who's boss." MP--"Essex delivers a series of kicks to the Hill Billy as Braddock tries to get to his feet. Once he's up, Braddock gets hit with a series of brutal European uppercuts that sends the Hill Billy into a corner." BV--"This match has been all Essex. Hill Billy needs to do smething soon." MP--"Essex has him in the corner. He climbs to the second rope and starts pounding on the top of Braddocks head. Braddock is really taking a beating here. Wait! Braddock grabs hold of Essex and throws him halfway across the ring!" BV--"Essex may have worked on the legs of Braddock, but Hill Billy's upper body is fine and dandy." MP--"Hill Billy starts out after Essex...but stumbles on his injured leg. Both men regain their feet. Essex grabs Braddock and whips him into the ropes. Reversed! Essex bounces off of the ropes and is lifted high into the air by Hill Billy's backdrop. Braddock follows up with BIG splash." BV--"Hill Billy weighs over 300 pounds. That's a lot of cornbread to fall on you." MP--"Braddock covers. Weis counts...1...2...ropes! Lady Anne put Essex's leg on the ropes and Don Weis stops the count. Essex seems dazed as Braddock pulls him to his feet. Essex is hurled into the ropes by Braddock. Essex bounces off and hits Braddock with a jumping knee lift! Braddock goes down. Essex picks him up with some difficulty." BV--"Like I said, Braddock is a BIG boy." MP--"Essex has him on his feet. Braddock doubles over from a shoulder to the gut and is then sent to the mat with a double ax-handle. The Hill Billy is face down. Essex applies the STF!" BV--"Essex is really putting pressure on Hill Billy's bad leg. This may not last long." MP--"Don Weis checks out Braddock. Tap out!! Hill Billy Braddock taps out! The winner by submission is Essex. BV--"Whatever else you may think about Essex, you have to admit he can get the job done in the ring. He had a game plan, he stuck to it, and won with it." MP--"Love him or hate him, he got the win. Our next matches promises to be interesting. The lightning fast cruiserweight Zeus Sinclair versus the larger brawler Lucky Lambert." BV--"Lambert has the size and strength advantage, but Sinclair may have the edge on style. It'll be interesting to see who takes control of this match." ["Ride the Lightning" plays over the loud speakers as Zeus Sinclair charges out. He waves his arms and really pumps up the crowd on his way to the ring.] BV--"This guy is like a living high voltage line. He's always jumping around and waving his arms, that's why he's hard to pin. he won't hold still for three seconds." [As "Lucky" Lambert steps out, the King's "Viva Las Vegas plays out over the arena. He wears a confident grin from ear to ear as he strolls down the aisle to the ring. Along the way, he throws pocker chips and playing cards out into the crowd.] MP--"Lambert is just oozing confidence here tonight. Referee Don Weis calls for the bell and we're underway. Lambert charges in and locks up with Zeus. Sinclair is quickly overpowered and sent into the corner. Lambert rushes in after him, but Sinclair ducks out. Lambert turns to face him...and is knocked down by a standing dropkick! Lucky's back up...another dropkick! Lambert rolls out of the ring to think things over." BV--"Lambert can't afford to let Sinclair control this match. He's got to stay tight on him and slow Sinclair down." MP--"Lambert's walking around outside the ring now. He better pay attention. Here comes Sinclair. He dives over the top rope and splashes down on top of Lambert! Both men are down. Finally, Sinclair gets to him feet, lifts Lambert up, and rolls him back into the ring. Sinclair follows him inside." BV--"Sinclair is fighting his match right now. He is totally in control." MP--"Both men are on their feet. They lock up and Zeus is sent into the ropes. Lambert catches him as he comes off with a lariat and sends him to the mat. Lambert is slow to pick him up,he still looks a little dazed from Sinclair's high risk move. Lambert picks him up...vertical suplex! Lambert tries for an elbow drop, but Sinclair rolls aside and Lambert hits hard. Sinclair hits with a quick leg drop and goes for the cover! 1...2...Lambert kicks out at two and a half! Both men get slowly to their feet." BV--"These two guys are exhausted. Neither one has much fight left, but neither one will give up either." MP--"Lambert tries a roundhouse right, but Sinclair counters with a backslide! 1...2...only a two count. Both men are back up. Lambert staggers Sinclair with a big chop. Sinclair stumbles back against the ropes and comes back towards Lambert. Lambert misses the clothesline and Zeus bounces off of the opposite ropes...bulldog takedown! Lambert is on the mat. Sinclair locks on the Olympic Sleeper! Lambert taps out! The winner, again by submission, is Zeus Sinclair. What a match!" BV--"Look at Sinclair. The Referee is the only thing holding him up. He's exhausted and Lambert is still flat on the mat." MP--"Zeus Sinclair, the lightning fast cruiserweight, is our winner. This has to be a real eye opener for those who thought Sinclair couldn't compete with the larger wrestlers." BV--"Well, Sinclair fought his match, he didn't let Lambert keep control of things. Sinclair could be have a serious on NCCW." MP--"Now its time for semi-final action in the NCCW HeavyWeight tournament. Four talented wrestlers remain and the winners of the next two matches will compete for the first NCCW World Heavyweight title. We're looking forward to some fierce competition here tonight. The first match features "Cajun" Huey Armstrong and "The Natural Wonder" Max Maxwell. What do you think the outcome will be, Bobby?" BV--"I don't know. Armstrong has the size advantage, but Maxwell has proven he can get the fans behind him when it counts. If Armstrong is going to win he has to use his size and mat skills to put Maxwell down early, if it goes longer, I think Maxwell has the advantage." MP--"We'll see how it goes. The Cajun is on his way to the ring now." [From backstage comes "Cajun" Huey Armstrong. Cajun stops to look at the cheering fans for an instant, then strides to the ring. He seems totally focused on the match ahead. The crowd gave him a good ovation, but he barely noticed.] BV--"Cajun looks fired up! He's ready to rumble." MP--"Be careful of copyright infringment. You don't want to get sued. The crowd's starting to pick up the volume, The Natural Wonder must be ready to come out." [The crowd roars its applause as Max Maxwell enters the arena. He stops at the foot of the ramp to pose for a few seconds, then continues to the ring. Along the way he slaps hands and talks to the fans. It takes some time for the cheers to die down after he gets into the ring.] MP--"Both of these guys are ready. They lock up as soon as the bell sounds. The Cajun uses his size to try to gain leverage, but neither wrestler can budge the other. They release and circle slowly. They lock up again, but this time Armstrong drives a knee into the midsection of Maxwell. He follows it up with a double ax-handle to the back of the neck then locks Maxwell in a side headlock." BV--"That's Armstrong's experience working for him. Gain control and keep your opponent immobilized." MP--"Armstrong is really putting pressure on Maxwell's head. The Natural Wonder is able to break free by pushing the Cajun into the ropes. Armstrong comes off the ropes fast and slams a shoulder into Maxwell. Maxwell bounces off the ropes and is thrown high into the air with a backdrop!" BV--"Like I said, Cajun is going to work early. He's got to keep the crowd out of this." MP--"He isn't waisting time, that's for sure. He picks the Natural Wonder up and hits him with a body slam. Maxwell looks stunned. Cajun bounces off the ropes and leaps high into the air for an elbow drop...he connects! This could already be over for Max Maxwell." BV--"It doesn't look good. Armstrong is pounding the heck out of him. Any plan Maxwell has has been put on hold. Cajun isn't letting him have time to do anything but bruise." MP--"Armstrong goes for the cover. 1...2...kick out." BV--"Cajun should have hooked him up better. Maxwell barely kicked out as it is." MP--"Armstrong picks him up. He may be trying for the Shrimp Kabob to finish things. No, he whips Maxwell across the ring into the corner and charges in after him. Ouch! Somehow Maxwell manages to lift up a boot in time and Armstrong catches it in the face. Cajun staggers back covering his head and Maxwell tries to catch his second wind." BV--"Max isn't out of the woods yet. Cajun is a powerful wrestler and it'll take more than a single move to bring him down." MP--"Armstrong is back on the move again, but Maxwell bought himself enough time to regroup. They lock up and the Cajun is sent into the ropes. Max drops him with a running lariat. Armstrong is back up, but another lariat sends him flat! The Natural Wonder turns to the crowd and strikes a pose. The crowd's going nuts. He's really pumped now!" BV--"He's got the crowd behind him now. This is a real match again." MP--"Cajun climbs back to his feet and faces Maxwell. They're standing nose to nose...well, nose to chest at least. They're trading chops and kicks until the Cajun finally staggers back from a fist to the head. Maxwell whips him into the ropes and Armstrong throws a clothesline as he comes back. Maxwell ducks underneath and hits the Cajun with an inverted atomic drop! He grabs him and executes a big belly-to-back suplex!" BV--"Armstrong landed flat on his back and got the wind knocked out of him. Maxwell better pin him quick. You may be able to stun that big guy for a few seconds, but he isn't staying down long!" MP--"Maxwell covers. 1...2...kickout at two and a half! Maxwell drags the Cajun to his feet and they lock up. Max tries for a vertical suplex, but Armstrong stops him." BV--"That's the Cajun's collegate background again. He knows just how to use leverage to counter an opponent's move." MP--"Maxwell tries again to lift the big man without any success. Instead, Armstrong sends him spinning into the ropes. Maxwell takes a kick to the midsection on the way back and Armstrong hooks him for a neckbreaker! Ohh! Maxwell slips out! The Cajun was going for the first part of his devistating Shrimp Kabob, but Maxwell slipped out! The Natural Wonder has him. German Suplex! He bridges for the pin. 1...2...3! Maxwell wins! Oh my, what a match! BV--"Maxwell was able to slip out of that neckbreaker. That was the difference. I don't know if I've ever seen that close of a match. Max Maxwell advances to the Heavyweight FINALS, but a wrestler like Armstrong won't be without a belt long." MP--"Funny you should mention that Bobby. We've been informed that the two semi-finalists that don't advance to the finals will contend for the Television title on the first pay-per-view." BV--"Wow, that means there will be three titles on the line. The World Heavyweight, the Southern Heritage, and now the TV title." MP--"Way to recap, Bobby. We're waiting for the next match to be introduced, but it may be a while before the crowd calms down enough to let Maxwell out of the ring. They love this guy." [First out is The Mad Goat accompanied by Mother Earth. The Goat, in his usual horned mask and furry trunks, waves to the crowd as he makes his way to the ring. Once there he hands his cloak to Mother Earth and vaults into the ring.] BV--"I still say that he is one disturbed individual, but you can't deny his wrestling skills. He's quick, tough, and he's not affraid to try high-risk moves." MP--"He is a talented wrestler, and he has developed quite a following here in NCCW. Sitting behind Mother Earth now are a half-dozen or more fans wearing Green Peace T-shirts. He's going to have a tough match here tonight, though. Here comes his opponent now." [Fireworks explode as the NCCW Southern Heritage Champion, The Crippler, walks down the ramp. He stops at the foot of the ramp to let everyone in the audience see the Southern Heritage belt around his waist. He then continues on to the ring. His eyes stare straight at the ring, never looking at the fans. Once in the ring, he hands his belt to referee Don Weis.] MP--"Crippler looks ready. Weis makes sure everyone knows that this is not a title defense then calls for the opening bell. The Goat comes charging out and locks up with the Crippler. Crippler just powers him back against the ropes and drives a knee into his stomache. Crippler whips Goat across the ring and into the opposite ropes. Goat bounces off...and tries to pin Crippler with a sunset flip! 1..Crippler kicks out." BV--"The Goat went to work early trying to pin the Crippler. He really wants the win." MP--"Both men are back on their feet. Crippler plants a kick in the Mad Goat's midsection then drives him to the mat with a bouble ax-handle. Crippler drops down and lock the Goat in a triangle choke." BV--"Crippler didn't like Goat trying to pin him so quick. He's trying to take some of the fight out of him." MP--"Crippler has the Goat locked up tight and well away from the ropes. he finally lets the Goat go and pulls him to his feet. Sets him up for a vertical suplex, lifts him up, but the Goat twists and lands behind the Crippler! Standing Russian leg sweep and the Crippler goes down! Crippler jumps up and the Goat nails him right away with a swinging neck breaker! Crippler looks stunned and the Mad Goat climbs to the top turnbuckle." BV--"Goat better do something fast. Crippler won't stay down long." MP--"No, as a matter of fact he's getting up now, but he's groggy. He's looking around for the Goat...sees him up top. Goat leaps off! Missle dropkick! Both men are down. Goat stands up, and amazingly enough so does Crippler. Crippler throws a punch. Goat blocks and delivers a spinning back kick. Crippler doubles over and Goat sets him up. Earth's Fury running powerbomb! Don Weis counts. 1...2...3! Mad Goat advances to the FINALS of the heavyweight tournament." BV--"Goat came out strong and never let up. He just overpowered Crippler." MP--"The first pay-per-view will see Max Maxwell take on The Mad Goat for the NCCW World Heavyweight title. What do you think of Goat's chances, Bobby?" BV--"Goat's been surprising me. Frankly, I didn't think he would make it this far. He seems to pull out a new trick whenever he needs one, but I don't know if he can compete with a guy like Maxwell. When Max gets the crowd behind him he's nearly unstoppable." MP--"It will be one exciting match, no doubt about it. Now its time for the main event here on Rage. A three man, over-the-top, elemination match featuring three of the newest, and biggest, wrestlers here in NCCW. Who knows what might happen here." [The lights dim and "Guilty" by Gravity Kills begins to play. From backstage steps the Enforcer. He pauses at the bottom of the ramp for only a second before proceeding to the ring. The audience is quiet, not sure what to think of this big man.] BV--"Wow! That is one BIG guy." [Abruptly the music changes. Now, "As Some Day It May Happen" plays across the crowd as the Dominator walks out. Long black hair hangs in front of his mask as he lifts his head to stare out at the crowd. He then slowly makes his way to the ring.] BV--"Where do they grow these guys? I keep expecting to hear "Fe, fi, fo, fum" or something." [For the last participant the music falls silent and the lights go out completely. A purple spotlight is all that guides the Mad Jester to the ring. The spotlight shines off of the jingling bells at the end of his cap as he skipps to the ring. He has a huge smile on his face looks more maniacal than humurous.] BV--"This one isn't nearly as tall, but he probably weighs as much as the other two." MP--"In the interests of referee safety, Don Weis will not be in the ring. These three will be all alone in there. This match will be an over the top elimination style bout. With these three it should be very interesting." BV--"Lets hope the ring makes it through all right." MP--"The bell sounds and the match is underway. Enforcer and Mad Jester immediatly go after one another, but the Dominator hangs back, sizing up his competition. Enforcer and Jester are trading punches, Jester laughing the whole time. Jester is finally able to sling the Enforcer into a corner and follows him in with a BIG body splash. Jester backs up for another run, but Dominator grabs him by the shoulder and spins him around. Jester looks up and laughs into the face of the taller man. Jester backs up and leans into the ropes for leverage, then comes charging out at Dominator, but Dominator simply sidesteps and drops Jester with a standing lariat." BV--"There's a lot of power in the ring now. It could light up half of Baltimore." MP--"The Enforcer is up now. He sizes up the situation and moves in to join the fight. Jester is pulled to his feet by Dominator. He's still smiling. Dominator whips him towards Enforcer...powerslam! That really knocked the wind out of Jester." BV--"I don't know if that was intentional or not, but that was one heck of a combination." MP--"Enforcer ignores the Mad Jester for the moment and stands face to face with the Dominator. Dominator just returns his stare, neither man is making a move, just measuring up the other. Jester is back on his feet now. He runs across the ring slams into the Enforcer's back. The Enforcer is knocked into Dominator and all three men bounce off the ropes and fall to the mat." BV--"It's like a demolition derby in there." MP--"The Enforcer is the first man up. He hauls the nearest man, Jester, up starts pounding him with overhand blows. Dominator stands up and joins in. All three men are pummeling each other! The Mad Jester throws a shoulder into the midsection of Dominator, driving the big man back. While Dominator is off balance, Jester grabs him and pushes him into Enforcer. Enforcer displays amazing strength by lifting the 360+ lb Dominator up onto his shoulder. Enforcer turns to throw Dominator over the ropes. Here comes Jester! He runs into the Enforcer from behind, causing him to drop Dominator to the mat and lose his balance. Enforcer has fallen over the top rope! Wait he still has hold of the ropes! He isn't out yet!" BV--"If he touches the floor he's out. He has hold of the rope, but he still has to hold his legs up. That would be easier for a shorter man." MP--"Meanwhile, Jester is delivering kick after kick to Dominator. Dominator finally stands, but is thrown against the far ropes. He comes back and sends Jester sprawling with a shoulder block. Wait...Jester was knocked back into the Enforcer! The Enforcer was just now standing up on the apron but was sent to the floor after Jester was slammed into him. The Enforcer is out! It's just the Dominator and Mad Jester now." BV--"I was beginning to wonder how anyone would be eliminated. These guys are all so big and tough." MP--"Jester is back up. He locks up with Dominator in the center of the ring. Dominator gains control and stuns Jester with a HUGE kneelift. Dominator just shoves Jester flat to the mat! Dominator lifts Jester...and Jester hits him with a low blow! Dominator staggers back against the ropes. Jester runs at him and leaps high into the air." BV--"That's amazing. I never would have guessed the big guy could jump like that." MP--"Dominator catches him! They both fall over the top! Both men are eliminated! Oh my! BV--"Now what?" MP--"The executive committee has been watching and they've given the decision. Dominator and Mad Jester will be given a rematch on the pay-per-view. We're out of time now, so for everyone here at NCCW, good night."
