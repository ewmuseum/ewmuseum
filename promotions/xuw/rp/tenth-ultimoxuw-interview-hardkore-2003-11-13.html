TENTH ULTIMOXUW.COM INTERVIEW: hardkore

13 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The tenth of the weekly interviews XUW ran on its own site, posted 13 November 2003 and billed on the fed’s interview index as “The most controversial character in XUW history speaks out about... well, everything!” hardkore answers every question in the gimmick’s own voice: the profile questionnaire, the interviewer’s questions and those sent in by readers, and a closing word-association round on the Cockjockey, Sheri Dedmon, Saij, Jack Nobbledy, Joey Vendetti and Al Crud. He says DJ recruited him and Crud into XUW, and calls Big Ted’s Arm his protégé, sent to conquer the UWF for him. The interviewer, Blake Wolfson, closes by announcing the next week’s subject, X-Factor.

Served whole, as written.
