XUW X-Terminated Strobe lights flicker on the stage, and the whole of the arena is silent. When suddenly, pyros explode from the ceiling. Charges are set from the ring to the stage and the music hits. The audience stunned from the noise finally begins to torrent into a mob. MM:Welcome to The Kilpatrick Dome and XUW's X-Terminated! I'm here with another 17,000 crazy people, and as always my loyal dog Atomic Tomato! AT: This is guaranteed to be a wild one, so hold on to your hats, it's about to begin! Backstage, Supa Thug is relaxing with a pre-match honey bun. Al Crud walks toward him. Al: What do you have there? Esco: Honeybun, fool wat dis look like? Al: Honeybun? What the hell is that? (farts) Sounds like something those Flashtastics would like. Esco: Yo, don't be dissin' my sweets sucka. Al: I'm more of a fine dining type myself, I perfer a seven course meal. Esco: Tell me he did not just say dat. Don't diss da honeybun knamean? Dis da Thug, but not da bun! XUW Hardcore Title Match. Elimination Match Cereal Killer vs. Raven X vs. Rob The Throb vs. DDT © Referee: Brad Bice,( in ring.)  Shooter (out of ring) "Your Disease" hits the speakers and Rob The Throb comes down the ramp with his sign. He scoots under the bottom rope after throwing his sign in. "Praise" jumps off the speakers and Cereal Killer comes down, looking for any pop he can get, and posing zealously. “Liberated” plays and Raven X comes down the ramp, to moderate heat. He pauses before getting into the ring, but does when the other two step out. “Creeping Death” Explodes over the system, and the crown goes berserk. DDT flies down the ramp and dives into the ring with his title in his hand. DDT begins by blasting Raven X with his title. Rob and CK are outside the ring, and have begun their own version of the match. Rob runs at CK, and lays in a flying back elbow. Rob picks up CK and hits him with a spinning neckbreaker. Meanwhile back in the ring, Raven grabs DDT’s berries, and gives him a testicular claw. Raven then picks him up by DDT’s not so happy berries, and turns it into an implant DDT. Men in the audience all cross there legs at the same time. Most have grimices on their faces. Raven bows. AT and MM: (at the excact same time) Oh MAN! Ouch! MM: That was just….ooooh, man. DAAAMN. AT: Hey, who’s that? MM: Say what? Where? AT: That guy crawling out from under the ring…Hey wait, I know that face! That’s JP Blood! My Gawd, IT”S JP BLOOD AND HE HAS A CHAIR! MM: He’s getting into the ring! As Raven soaks up the audiences sympathy for their fallen hero DDT, JP Blood sneaks into the ring behind him, and picks him up from to drop a flaming hammer. JP Blood goes to his old comrade DDT, and picks him up. JP helps DDT to his feet. Once DDT has Raven pinned, JP jumps out of the ring, and takes off through the crowd, just as security comes running down the ramp. Bice counts the three, and DDT rolls Raven out of the ring. Meanwhile CK and RTT are still outside the ring. MM: Well, one down. That was quick, and might I say, probably VERY painful. CK has Rob’s head and is blasting it onto the ring stairs. He then follows up with the dreaded Nsync Slam. DDT now walking a bit more normal, he runs to the corner CK and Rob are at, and jumps over the ropes to hit Cereal with a 360 senton bomb. He gets up slowly and tosses Cereal back into the ring. As DDT enters the ring he is speared by CK. CK goes after one of the two chairs lying in the ring. Rob gets up and into the ring and swipes the chair from CK, as CK is reared back. DDT gets up and grabs the other chair. DDT looks at Rob and they have the same idea at the same time. They blast Cereal with a connchairto. DTT goes for the pin, but is pulled off by Rob. Rob then tries, but is kicked off by DDT. He then picks up Rob and throws him to the corner. DDT goes for the pin once more. 1….2….Kick out. DDT picks Cereal up and gives him the Shock Treatment onto a chair. Rob runs over and gets back body dropped by DDT. Rob gets the pin 1….2…..3! MM: That’s two! Here is where it’s gonna get bloody. I just know it. AT: I think I’ve already seen enough of this carnage. DDT and Rob now begin to trade punches. Rob Backs off for a second while DDT is a little leary. Rob swiftly executes a spinebuster, followed up by a series of quick knees to the face. Rob gets up and places a chair next to DDT. He grabs a second chair. Rob rolls DDT onto the chair with his foot, and reels back with the one in his hands. He brings the chair down several times until DDT begins to bleed. Rob picks up the barely conscious DDT and levels him with the Throbbing Headache. He rolls DDT up and Bice slides in 1…2….3! Winner and new XUW Hardcore Champ: Rob The Throb! AT: Damn that was … MM: HARDCORE!! You twit. AT: Well next up is a our Flatulent fighter, Al Crud and he will be facing the one man, NOONE can understand, Supa Thug Esco. MM: Whaddya mean foo. Dat Bro is da shizzy yo. AT: Exactly Spanish Jobber looks really confused. Supa Thug Esco Vs. Al Crud Referree: The Hungry Mexican “Renegade” hits, and Esco comes out to substantial heat. Esco flashes up some hand signs. This seems to generate more heat. “One Bourbon, One Scotch, and One Beer” washes over the arena, and The crowd pops hard. Al casually passes through the curtin and down the ramp. He shakes a few hands on the way to the ring. They lock up. Al picks Esco up into a shoulder-breaker, and then up again so he can be body-slammed. Al follows with an elbow drop, and hits. Al picks Esco up and throws him to the ropes, But Esco slides out of the ring instead. Esco seems to be re-thinking his game plan. Al follows Esco outside the ring, and Esco hits Al with a drop-toe-hold into the nearby stairs. Esco rolls into the ring to stop the Mex’s count. He climbs over the ropes and prepares for a 450 splash, but Al rolls out of the way. Esco lands hard onto his head and is laying beside Al. Al reaches under the mat and pulls out a ……….French to English dictionary! Al then grabs Esco’s head by the hair up a little so that he can see it. Al opens the book. Al: Come on dude, translate. Tell me what you have been saying all this time! Esco: Uhhhh Al: Come on man, I have to know! Esco…. Al: Well this book has been useless….Until now! Al takes the book and bashes it across Esco’s head. But Esco no sells the soft cover dictionary head shot …OF DOOM! Al throws Esco back into the ring and the book out into the crowd. Al: Maybe one of you can figure it out! Al slides back into the ring to meet a very pissed off Esco. Esco kicks Al in the stomach, and gives Al a gutwrench power-bomb. Esco picks Al up and hits him with The Finisher. Esco pins. 1….2….Mex notices Al’s foot on the rope. Esco picks Al up and whips him into a corner. Esco climbs up and drops Al with a super-plex. Esco climbs to the second turnbuckle and delivers an elbow. Al catches it in his sturnum and rolls into a ball. Esco gets cocky with a rolling knee drop, and a couple of stomps. Al pulls Esco’s leg out from under him, and straddles over him, delivering, not one, not two, but 10 punches to Esco’s face. He farts while over Esco, then puts Esco into a Boston Cab. Esco drive’s off into the worst Traffic America has ever seen….Er wait, I meant Boston Crab. Al holds the submission move for awhile, but Esco will not tap. Al processes the previous night’s beer, but even with the stench, Esco still won’t tap. Al lets go and Esco staggers to his feet. Al grabs him and gives him the belly to beer-belly suplex. Al gets up and walks over to Esco. Al goes to pick him up, but Esco grabs Al and rolls him into a small package. 1….2…..kickout. Both get up and Esco goes for a Drive By, but is reversed into a MotherCrudder. Al goes for the pin. 1….2…..3! Winner : Al Crud. AT: That Al sure has an unusual style of wrestling, huh Mikey? AT: Mikey? Mr. Mikey is seen arguing with the hot dog vender about the price of a dozen dogs. Mikey feels he should get a bulk price. The vendor disagrees. Finally Mr. Mikey Pays the $75.00 for a dozen hotdogs and offers one to Atomic Tomato MM: Sorry, I needed something to eat, I have missed the last three meals! AT: Why? Were you to hurried to stop and eat? MM: No, my ride partner is Al Crud, and you can’t eat anything when you smell what he is cooking. AT: Well next up we have the a first for XUW. A hell in a cell match! Derick Jackson from Exodus, and Admiral John from The Four Horsemen, are going to face off one on one, in that steel cage. MM: Mmmph. AT: That’s right Mr. Mikey, it’s next! Derick Jackson vs. Admiral John Hell In a Cell Match Referee : Brad Bice “How you remind me “ plays, and within’ two beats of the song, people are booing. The heat continues as Derick saunters down the ramp. The overwhelming insults and heat feeds Derick, who is all smiles. “Gets me through” by Ozzy hits, and everyone in the arena stands! Admiral John appears giving the sign of the horsemen. He stops near the top of the ramp absorbing the massive Pop. Derick looks right through him with a steely glare, as he gets into the Cell. John comes down the ramp, but pauses when he gets to the door of the cell. He looks around expecting an ambush. Derick stands in the opposite corner waiting. Finally AJ climbs in and the door is closed. John and Derick lock up in the center of the ring, but not for long, John gets the better hand and delivers a snap suplex. John then begins with stomps and elbows. After enjoying a round of cheers, John picks DJ up and whips him to the ropes, DJ rebounds and is met with a high knee. John drags DJ closer to the back side of the cage and show Mr. Mikey what his pal looks like having his face ground against the cyclone fencing. John moves closer to one of the support bars and throws DJ into it. DJ lies, barely moving. John begins to enjoy his early advantage and wants to do some real damage. So he climbs to the top rope and flies through the air for a fling headbutt. But DJ rolls out of the way, and John belly flops the mat hard. DJ picks John up, determined to give him a little cage treatment. DJ spins John around him until John is nearly airbourne and lets go. John flies across the mat and through the ropes to hit the cage door. DJ notices that the cage door has become slightly ajar, and decides to kick it a couple times to see if the bracket will give. With Johns slumped body against the door, if it comes open, John will most defineatley fall out and onto the floor. DJ continues to kick above John’s (now moving) body and the door comes open, and John falls backwards onto his head. DJ gets out of the ring, and cage. He lays John out and begins to climb the outside. But John suddenly gets up and gives chase to Derick, who is now nearly to the top. John gets a hold of DJ’s ankle and tries to pull him down. But Derick slips free and lands on the top of the cage. Derick gets ready to kick John in the head as soon as he gets to the horizon of the cage. John does, and Derick goes for it, but John ducks. John quickly gets to the top of the cage and stomps DJ down. DJ gets a hold of John’s leg and pulls it out from underneath. DJ picks up John, and sets up for a tombstone onto a support beam, but John slips free , and reverses. John bounces a couple times on the springy fence, and hits it. Derick slumps into a pile, and John raises his arms in victory. John begins to jump on the springy cage, and hits DJ with a flying elbow. Just then the cage gives, and DJ falls head first 15 feet to the mat. John , right behind him, lands on DJ’s airborne legs driving his head and neck into the mat even more. John grabs one of the legs he just landed on and pins Derick. Bice runs in and counts. 1…2…3! The Admiral gets up unhurt as the crowd goes ballistic, and looks at A.T.. John is making some kind of signal with his hands to A.T. and looks unnatural. AT: Um folks, Derick’s hurt. Um…..It doesn’t look good. (The camera stays locked on AT’s face. MM is not at the announce position.) AT: We will keep you updated, ……..Right now Paramedics are working on Derick. He IS moving a little, He is breathing, but it doesn’t look good. Mr Mikey is in the ring with Derick, who is ….as a matter of fact, a good friend of his. We’ll be right back with the Hurristoner’s vs. The Riot Squad. Winner: Admiral John A commercial is played about XUW Shopzone. “You can’t get these items anywhere but Shopzone. Like What? How about Kat’s Trenchcoat? Nope, won’t find that at Walmart! At XUW Shopzone!” Hurristoners vs. Riot Squad Referee: Odain Nurse “Stay Together For The Kids” plays and Chase Wilson, Fatbadger, and Sakie appear at the top of the ramp. AT: Where’s Hodgo? MM: I don’t know, but those hot dogs were great (belch) They sure can make a dog in….what town ARE we in? AT: I’m not really sure, I’ve lost track. Was it Vegas yesterday or LA? MM: It was Detroit! Damn you are lost. AT: No actually I think Hodgo is. Badger and Sidhe lock up, and Sidhe gets the upper hand with an armdrag take down, into an armbar. Badger grabs a rope, and Odain calls the break. Sidhe goes from a wristlock, to a headlock, and decides on a bulldog. He then gets to the top rope and hits a splash. Pin. Odain dives in. 1…Kick Sidhe drags Badger to the corner and knees his face a few times. Sidhe decides on a suplex, but Badger reverses and hit the Fat Burner. Kat pull Sidhe out, and Badger tags in Chase. Chase goes out and tosses Sidhe back in. Chase then sets up for the “Bwhahahaha” Just then Hodgo races down the ramp and blind tags Sidhe. Chase hits Sidhe with the Short Term Memory Loss. Hodgo goes behind Chase and nails him with a full-nelson suplex. Picks Chase back up for the Riot Breaker, smiles at Sidhe, waves to the crowd, and Chase counters into a small package. Odian’s right there. 1…2….3 Winners: Hurristoners. Sidhe gets into the ring as the Hurristoners leave and as Hodgo gets to his feet. Sidhe and hodgo stare each other down nose to nose. Kat grabs a Riot Squad T-Shirt, and hands it to Sidhe. Sidhe shreds it with his bare hands and throws it at Hodgo. Hodgo stares back at Sidhe in disbelief, while Sidhe turns to Kat and they begin to leave. Hodgo slides out of the ring and take’s the time-keeper’s chair. Sidhe is halfway up the ramp when hodgo catches up to him and nails him in the back of the head. Hodgo then lays the chair flat, and gives Sidhe a Riot Breaker onto it. Kat runs to Sidhe as Hodgo backs up the ramp spitting and cursing. AT: What does this mean? MM: I think the Riot Squad, is now an Angry Couple. AT: We will have to see in the future. What becomes of yet another mess here tonight. XUW World Tag Team Championship Match Doggy-Style verses the Technitions Referee: Shooter “Control” Hits the speakers and Woof-Woof, and Blake Wolfson appear and are met with moderate heat. The crowd begins to throw containers of hot nacho cheese at Woof. But somehow Blake manages to be missed by the garbage. Just then, even before Doggy Style is off the ramp, the Horsemen music hits. Bionic Carrot and KSC Trunks come running from behind and bulldog both Blake and Woof. The crowd goes crazy! BC grabs Blake and KSC grabs Wolf and drags them to the ring. Woof goes off the ropes and nails KSC with a clothes-liner. Woof then applies the Texas Cloverleaf to KSC. But KSC hits the ropes and Shooter calls for the break. KSC gets to his feet, but not for long, as Woof comes back off the ropes again and drops KSC with a spear. BC is within reach and blind tags KSC. BC grabs Woof from behind and Bear Hugs him ’til Woof is limp. Both Horsemen lie in the ring wounded. The crowd is furious. AT: I guess the new tag champs aren’t joking around anymore. MM: Yes, I guess it’s time that we take Doggy-Style seriously. Are you sure you don’t want a hot dog? AT: Next up we have Henrik the King verses the Ripper for the Submission Title match. Henrik slides into the ring and before he can get up he is put into a Camel Clutch, but Henrik is close enough to the ropes so Shooter calls for the break. Henrik gets to his feet and they lock up. Henrik drops and hits Ripper with a Drop Toe-Hold. He maneuvers around and gets the Ripper into the Texas Cloverleaf. Henrik refuses to tap and manages to roll onto his back breaking the hold. Henrik drops Ripper’s feet, bounces off the far rope, comes back preparing to Clothesline the Ripper, but the Ripper ducks and reverses flipping Henrik over the top rope.
