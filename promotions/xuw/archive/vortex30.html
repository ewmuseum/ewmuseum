XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #30

15 Aug 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Pyro goes off as the unmistakable theme of Vortex blares throughout the arena. The fans are popping like crazy spotlights swerve around all over the place. There are signs in the crowd that say “Kat = TV Champ = Titles defenses in Pudding!” “I always knew Kore was a zombie,” and “Usual Suspects = Usual Champions.” The camera heads to the Atomic Tomato and “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson down at ringside. AT: Welcome everyone to Vortex. As you already know, I am the Atomic Tomato and along side me as always is “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson. We got a great show for you tonight. BJ: Our opening match of the night pits the former Professor, The Stun Gunn Ric Austin against No Fear Man. AT: You want a grudge match? You got one, as Hurrisakie takes on a member of the dastardly gWo, Kore, with Supa Thug Esco as a Special Guest referee. BJ: Want an obscure match? Sidhe takes on Kat in a TV title defense. I don’t know what the hell to expect from these two. We could have Bazooko’s Circus come down to be lumberjacks for all we know. AT: Another AoD member puts his title on the line as DDT defends against the returning X-Factor. Will X be able to dethrone the champ, or will DDT continue to build himself up to be a Submission icon? BJ: And finally, the Intercontinental title gets defended for the first time in over a month as new champion Woof Woof takes on BC of The Usual Suspects in what’s sure to be a stellar matchup. The camera heads backstage as it manages to find its way over to X-Factor. X-Factor: For heaven’s sake this place is a mess without me around. Doesn’t anyone around here know how to run a company when a star isn’t around? Everything went to hell, including the titles, when I was gone, but I’m back so all the wrongs will be righted. All I need is my World title shot that I rightfully deserve. The camera heads back to the announce position. AT: <Sarcastically> Oh joy, I missed X-Factor so much. BJ: <Excited> Oh I know I did. X is my hero and I can’t wait until he rights the wrongs. AT: Oh shut up. Lets head to the ring. ”Fear”  by Disturbed hits as NFM makes his way from the backstage and out towards the ring to a moderate pop. He climbs in the ring and walks over to the turnbuckle and poses as “Back In The Saddle” by Aerosmith hits and “The Stungunn” Ric Austin makes his way to the ring. He climbs in the ring and walks over to NFM as they get in each other’s faces. The ref tries to separate them as they talk trash to each other. They look at the ref with tension on their faces and talk to him and the ref backs away holding his hands out and nods. The ref goes and rings the bell as both men leave the ring to the outside and start to take stuff from out under the ring. NFM vs. “The Stungunn” Ric Austin AT: Well I think they asked for a hardcore match and the ref has decided to give it to them. This match should be interesting to see. BJ: I love a good hardcore match to open a show. Austin throws in some garbage cans with lids as NFM tosses in a fire extinguisher and slides in a table. Austin slides into the ring with a lead pipe as NFM rolls in with a roadway cone. Austin goes to nail NFM with the pipe, but NFM ducks and nails Austin with the cone as he slams it on top of his head. The cone sticks on top of Austin as he falls to the mat. He attempts to get it off his head, but can’t seem to jar it loose. NFM goes to grab the fire extinguisher, but amazingly Austin manages to swing the pipe and nail NFM in the kidney before he can pick up the fire extinguisher. Austin finally gets the cone off his head as he sees NFM hunched over holding his kidney. He jabs the pipe into NFM’s kidney a couple of times and finally jabs it into the back of NFM’s head. He goes for a cover. 1… 2… Shoulder up! Austin picks up NFM by the head and connects with a DDT. NFM lays on the mat as Austin walks over and climbs the turnbuckle. He comes off with The Stun Gunn Special (top rope frog splash), but NFM counters with a roll away. Austin hits the mat hard as NFM crawls over to the fire extinguisher. Austin turns around and stumbles towards NFM and NFM sprays the fire extinguisher right in his face. Austin stumbles to the ropes and tries to gather his senses. AT: NFM sprayed that right in Austin’s face! If NFM can capitalize on Austin’s blindness, the match is his. BJ: You call using a fire extinguisher in the face hardcore? Maybe if you like hit him with it instead of spraying him with it. Come on, use some real weapons! NFM gets up and goes after Austin, but Austin ducks and backbody drops NFM to the outside, but NFM lands on the apron. NFM grabs Austin by the throat and goes to deliver the Chokeslam from Hell, but Austin kicks him in the gut. Austin then armdrags NFM back into the ring over the top rope. NFM his the ring and Austin walks over to him and puts him in a standing armbar. Austin goes to climb the turnbuckle and he gets to the top rope. NFM knocks his feet out from under him and Austin lands crotched on the top turnbuckle, but perfectly enough that he split legs and catches NFM with a reverse DDT to finish Simply Stunning. Both men are down as the ref surveys the damage. Both men struggle to get to their feet when….. AT: Look Barry! Its Michael Bolling with a chair in hand! BJ: If you have to ask what he’s going to do with that chair, I’m gonna smack you. Bolling slides into the ring and bangs the chair on the apron. As NFM goes to get up, Bolling swings at his head and NFM ducks as Bolling clocks Austin. Bolling turns around and is clotheslined over the top rope by NFM. NFM picks up Austin and nails him with the Lightning Strike (jumping Tombstone piledriver) He makes the cover. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner – NFM AT: NFM really dodged a bullet by ducking out of the way of that chairshot from Bolling. Something tells me that this little feud between NFM and Bolling isn’t going to end too easily. BJ: Bolling thinks its his time to be the man and getting rid of NFM, the last leader of FEAR, is the means to do that. He missed getting him this time, but knowing Bolling, he won’t stop until he gets what he wants, no matter how long it takes him. AT: Well up next is our match pitting Hurrisakie up against Kore. Some say that Hurrisakie cost Kore a chance at winning the Intercontinental title at Road Rage 2 by Hurrikicking him, and others say that Kore had it coming. Kore Well tonight, Kore finally gets his chance at redemption against Hurrisakie with his own stablemate Supa Thug Esco as the Special Guest referee. BJ: Esco is also the tag team partner of Hurrisakie, so who knows what’s going to happen. Lets get to the match. ”Doggz” hits as Supa Thug Esco makes his way from the back and down the ramp towards the ring. He climbs in the ring as he gets a rather indifferent reaction. “Greed” by Godsmack hits as Kore gets massive boos from the crowd as he comes to ring alone. He plays off the crowd’s hatred of him, which causes the crowd to boo him even more. He climbs in the ring as “Hero” hits and Hurrisakie comes out from the backstage to a monstrous pop from the fans. He spins around at the top of the rampway and then makes his way down towards the ring. He climbs in the ring and poses on the turnbuckle. He climbs off the turnbuckle, takes off his cape, and sets it on the outside. Esco rings the bell. Hurrisakie vs. Kore with Supa Thug Esco as Special Referee Kore jumps Hurrisakie from behind and starts to nail him with shots to the back of the head. Hurrisakie stumbles down to one knee as Kore proceeds to shove him throat first onto the middle rope. Esco gets down there and starts yelling and counting to 5 so Kore gets off of Hurrisakie. Kore gets off Hurrisakie at 4 and gets in Esco’s face, but Esco doesn’t back down. Kore finally quits trying to win the staring contest and turns his attention to Hurrisakie. Hurrisakie spears Kore and gets on top of him and starts to nail him with a flurry of right hands. After about twelve or so right hands, Esco gets Hurrisakie off of Kore. Hurrisakie looks at Esco with a “Wassup wit dat?!” look as Esco looks right back at him. AT: Well it looks like Esco is going to remain neutral and just officiate a good match. BJ: And it looks like Hurrisakie is trying to get Esco to leave the gWo. AT: <Annoyed> How can you tell? BJ: Just the way he’s lookin at him. He’s trying to ruin the gWo family! You homewrecker! AT: Oh quit it and just commentate. Hurrisakie is still trying to talk to Esco, but Kore takes down Hurrisakie with a rollup and he grabs the tights. Esco counts. 1… 2… Esco stops counting and rips Kore’s hand off the tights of Hurrisakie. Kore looks at Esco with a look of  “I didn’t grab the tights.” Esco then points to the instant replay on the Ulti-tron showing Kore with a hand full of tights. Kore sees it, looks at Esco, and still denies it. AT: Even with obvious proof he still denies he did it. BJ: That’s typical Kore for ya. Hurrisakie turns Kore around and starts to hammer him with some lefts and rights. Hurrisakie backs Kore into the ropes and sends him to the other side. Hurrisakie goes for a clothesline, but Kore ducks, and comes off the other side with a stiff lariat. Kore then picks up Hurrisakie by the head and nails him with a knee to the sternum. Kore then shoves Hurrisakie under and delivers a piledriver. Kore makes a cover. 1… 2… Shoulder up! Kore picks up Hurrisakie by the head and picks him up by the side and delivers a gutbuster. Kore then goes to the outside and climbs up the turnbuckle. He comes off the top with the Peaked Point (five star frog splash onto the lower back), but Hurrisakie moves and Kore eats canvas. Hurrisakie makes his way to his feet and catches a stumbling Kore with a boot to the midsection and nails him with the Truth and Justice (Twist of Fate). Hurrisakie makes the cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! Hurrisakie looks over at Esco and looks down at Kore and then back up at Esco. BJ: He did it again! He’s trying to wreck the gWo family! AT: Oh will ya give it a rest. Who cares if he is, its Esco’s decision whether he leaves for not. God, I wish you felt like leaving. BJ: Sorry Cabbage, but the fans think elsewise. AT: Which fans would those be? BJ: My fans of course. Every single fan in this arena is one of “my fans.” AT: How popular do you think you are? Samurai Bob popular? If there’s anything I can’t stand its those who think they’re as popular as the most over guys in a company. Hurrisakie picks up Kore and goes to send him to the ropes, but Kore reverses. He catches Hurrisakie off the ropes with a kick to the midsection. He goes to deliver The Royal Flush (Flapjack), but Hurrisakie counters with a double leg takedown and turns and slingshots Kore into the turnbuckle. Kore falls out of the turnbuckle as Hurrisakie nails him with a Hurrikick. Hurrisakie makes a cover. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner – Hurrisakie AT: And Hurrisakie captures the match. I must say that Esco was a great official. He called the match right down the middle and the better man was victorious. BJ: Sure, the better man is a homewrecker. Yeah he’s the better man alright, you heartless bastard. AT: Good lord man, just give it a rest. Seriously, take a nap, it sounds like you need one. The camera heads to the backstage area. The camera comes upon X-Factor, who’s obviously had the crap beaten out of him. A note is on him that says "I may be the face from the past, but this scouser's time has past.” The camera then heads back out to the announce position. AT: Looks like X has been laid out by the infamous face from the past. BJ: Really? I thought maybe the note on his chest was from Sin Yun. AT: Sin Yun is the face from the past?! BJ: <Sarcastically> Yeah, he’s coming back to kick some ass, take some names, and start off where he left off. AT: Jobbing on Xposure? BJ: Bingo. AT: <Shivers> Uhhhhhuhuhuh. Horrible, horrible memories of Tuesday nights with Grandma. BJ: Lets head to the TV title match before the Cabbage ruins this broadcast anymore. ”Big Gun” hits as Kat makes her way down to the ring. Tons of signs saying ‘Pudding’ arise from the audience as a ‘Pudding’ chant begins as Kat climbs in the ring. “Add It Up” hits as Sidhe comes from the back with the TV title belt draped on his shoulder. He looks down at the ring and smiles as Kat returns the smile as well. He climbs in the ring and takes the TV title off his shoulder and hands it to the ref. The ref rings the bell. TV Title Match – Sidhe © vs. Kat Sidhe and Kat circle around each other before locking up. It’s a rather weak lockup, but Sidhe wins it anyways and puts Kat in a headlock. Sidhe then lets go of the headlock. Kat looks at him strangely and then slaps Sidhe right in the face. The audience lets out a moan as the echo of the slap is heard around the arena. Sidhe just looks at Kat, smiles, and points to his cheek again. Kat slaps it again as the audience lets out a moan.  Sidhe then slaps Kat across the face as the same moans echo throughout the audience. Kat looks at Sidhe and smiles and points to her cheek as well. Sidhe slaps her again, the echoes verberate, and Kat of course smiles back at Sidhe. Sidhe and Kat lock up again, but Sidhe counters with a go behind waistlock and hits Kat with a rolling reverse cradle, but instead of making the bridge, he starts to slap Kat’s ass to a rhythmic pattern. Kat of course kicks out at two. AT: Did Sidhe just play “Babaloo” on Kat’s ass during that pin attempt? BJ: Babaloo? Is that even a song? AT: Its what I call any song played on the bongos. BJ: Seriously, this is the most fu**ed up TV title match I’ve ever seen. Kat gets up and grabs Sidhe and sends him into the turnbuckle. Sidhe hits the turnbuckle what appears to be really hard, but he overemphasizes Kat’s strength and falls down back into the corner. Sidhe relaxes his arms on the bottom ropes and smiles at Kat who’s on the other side. Kat then charges Sidhe and delivers a Bronco Buster, which he thoroughly enjoys. Kat then picks up Sidhe by the head. Sidhe counters by putting Kat under. He picks up Kat and goes for a powerbomb, but she counters with a hurricanrana and she holds on and sits on Sidhe’s midsection with her crotch right in his face. Sidhe smiles as the ref makes the count. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner and NEW TV Champ – Kat O’Shea AT: Well that was umm……interesting. BJ: I think The Majestic One needs to make a comeback and it would only be appropriate if I was put in the TV title division for starters. I mean a guy that’s been out of the game for so long should have some practice before stepping it up to the big time, right? AT: You know she could kill you if she wanted to, right? BJ: Oh who would want to kill The Majestic One? AT: Lots of people……that I would pay….. As Sidhe and Kat are celebrating, “I Stand Alone” begins to play over the arena loudspeakers as the crowd heat escalates for the entrance of the XUW Heavyweight Champion, Blake Wolfson. Sidhe and Kat stop their celebrations as they turn their attention to the entranceway, where the Lone Wolf appears, dressed in a neck brace. He begins to slowly limp down the ramp, with an uncomfortable look on his face. He slowly climbs through the ropes and grabs a microphone and motions for the music to be stopped. Sidhe motions for Kat to get out of the ring as he wants to face the Lone Wolf face to face. Blake: Congratulations Sidhe, you won. Are you happy now? Sidhe: What are you talking about Wolfson? Blake: Last week you powerbombed me through a table, that’s what I’m talking about. I’ve had enough. No Title is worth this much. No career is worth this much. Nothing is worth this much. That’s it, game over. Sidhe: I know I can’t understand you normally, but what are you talking about? Blake: Look at me you son of a bitch! Look what you’ve done to me! Here, you want this Title? It’s yours. Blake then proceeds to throw the Heavyweight Title down on the mat. He looks straight at Sidhe who is focused on the belt down below. Blake: Go on then, take it. Sidhe: No, I won’t. This is not how I’m going to win this Title. I know what you’re doing, you’re only doing this so you can get out of facing me. I’m not th.. Blake: Stop right there. Do you think this is all an act? Do you think this neck brace is fake? Do you really think I’d just give this Title up if I didn’t want to? It’s done, all over. I’m out of this place. Now pick up the belt Sidhe, and do it now, because it’ll be the only chance you’ll ever get to hold that Title, you a**hole. Blake and Sidhe lock eyes and proceed to stare at each other for a few moments. Sidhe eventually lowers his eyes to the belt. As he goes to pick up the Title, Blake kicks Sidhe in the gut and hooks up his arms and delivers the Crying Wolf on the Heavyweight Title belt! Blake springs to his feet and rips off the neck brace with a huge smile on his face as the crowd lets out a mixed reaction. AT: How did I know he was going to do that? BJ: You’re psychic? Blake starts to put the boots to the fallen Sidhe, until 2 men come running down to the ring and reveal themselves to be Jack the Ripper and the DDT! BJ: Uh oh.. AT: Look out Wolfson, you’re about to get your ass handed to you! The Ripper and DDT slide in the ring and Blake sees them both. He lunges at both of them with right hands, able to subdue them for a few moments, until they are able to overpower him and put the boots to him themselves. Luckily for the Lone Wolf he’s near the ropes and is able to roll to the outside. Blake grabs a mic. Blake: Ah so this is how you want to play the game huh? Strength in numbers, I should have known. I knew you weren’t man enough to take me on by yourself Sidhe, so you had to get your little buddies to watch your back. Sidhe: I didn.. Blake: Shut up, I’m not done. If this is how you really want to play the game Sidhe, then I can play this game too. How about a little challenge? Let’s say, this coming Monday on Mayhem, I, the Lone Wolf, step into that ring to take on you Sidhe, and your buddies Jack the Ripper and the DDT, in a 3-on-1 handicap match, what do you say? The crowd lets out a huge pop at the notion of the match while all 3 AoD members share a smile. AT: Uh Barry, has Wolfson lost his mind? BJ: Apparently.. Sidhe: I like the soun.. Blake: Damnit Sidhe, you keep interrupting me, I’m not finished yet. Let’s say we raise the stakes a little higher, shall we? How about we say that if by some huge fluke, one of you d***heads are actually able to pin me, then that person will become the #1 contender to my Heavyweight Title. Sound good to you? Sidhe waits for Blake to finish. Blake: Well answer me, we don’t have all day you know. Sidhe: Oh good, you’re finished. I think Jacko and D agree with me when I say that we look forward to that match, we look forward to that match a lot. The crowd let out another pop at the acceptance of the match. Blake: Brilliant. I know you like to call yourselves the most dominant force in XUW, and that you yearn for darkness, but I’m not afraid of the dark, and I’m not afraid of any of you. It doesn’t matter to me how many people you want to put up against me, I’m not backing down from anyone. On Mayhem, you’ll see who the real dominant force is, because he doesn’t hide behind anyone. ”I Stand Alone” begins to play throughout the arena as Blake slowly backs up the ramp, not taking his eyes of any off the 3 AoD members in the ring. AT: What a huge match signed for Mayhem! It’s going to be the Heavyweight Champ, Blake Wolfson, up against the might of the Army of Darkness, Sidhe Nimh, Jack the Ripper and the DDT in a 3-on-1 handicap match, and whoever is able to pin Blake becomes the #1 contender for the Heavyweight Title! That’s huge news Barry! BJ: Why do you say “Whoever is able to pin Blake”? You don’t think the Lone Wolf could beat the AoD on his own? AT: Are you serious? BJ: No! BWAHAHAHAHAHA! That filthy Australian doesn’t even stand a chance! AT: Well while Sidhe, Jack, and Kat head to the back, DDT has remained in the ring awaiting X-Factor’s arrival. DDT stands there as X”s theme music doesn’t play. He leans up against the ropes waiting for his opponent. Out of nowhere from the crowd, MV Pete flies down the aisleway, unbeknownst to DDT. Pete slides into the ring and attacks DDT as the fans pop for the newly returned Suspect. Pete goes to send DDT to the ropes, but DDT holds on and reverses, but Pete reverses the reversal and slaps on the Junk Yard Stretch (Crippler Crossface). DDT looks to be in pain as Pete locks in the hold as the fans cheer. DDT manages to get on his knees and soon enough he manages to shove MV Pete into the turnbuckle. Pete drops the hold as DDT starts to lay into him with some lefts and rights. After a few of those, Pete throws some rights and lefts of his own to DDT. DDT backs away as the Suspect’s blows start to get to him. While DDT is dazed, Pete goes to put his hand around DDT”s throat, but DDT kicks Pete in the gut and delivers a Shock Treatment (DDT) to him. Pete lays out on the mat as DDT lays into him with some boots. DDT then leaves the ring and heads to the back. AT: Looks like DDT has another contender for his Submission title. Had Pete really locked in that Junk Yard Stretch, we might be seeing DDT laid out in that ring instead of Pete. BJ: Pete was a little over zealous and for that he paid the price, but not before he got his licks in. AT: Well up next is our Intercontinental title match between Woof Woof and Bionic Carrot. Woof recently won the title at Road Rage 2 and is making his first wrestling appearance since winning the title. Can he defend it against the newest Suspect, Bionic Carrot? BJ: Woof didn’t win that title for nothing. He’s going to go in that ring and show exactly why he’s the Intercontinental champion and why Bionic Carrot is not. I don’t remember the friggin Carrot ever being in the tournament so how he got this shot, I don’t know. Lets head to the ring. ”Short Skirt, Long Jacket” by Cake hits as Woof Woof comes out from behind the backstage area and onto the rampway. The fans boo as he walks down to the ring with the Intercontinental title around his waist and no Grand Wizard. He climbs in the ring and takes off his title as “Bad Moon Rising” by Creedence Clearwater Revival hits and Bionic Carrot comes out from behind the curtain to a large pop. He makes his down to the ring and climbs in as he stares at Woof. Woof gives the ref his Intercontinental title and the ref rings the bell. Intercontinental Title Match – Woof Woof © vs. Bionic Carrot Woof goes to attack BC from behind, but BC moves out of the way as Woof hits the turnbuckle. BC turns him around and starts to punch him. He lays about eight punches into him before and winds up and throws Woof to the other turnbuckle. Woof hits it and bounces out and BC levels him with a clothesline. BC picks up Woof and goes to deliver a suplex, but Woof blocks it. BC tries again and Woof prevents it with another block. Woof then picks up BC for a suplex, but BC slides behind and catches Woof with a hangman’s neckbreaker. BC goes for a cover. 1… 2… Kickout! BC then picks up Woof and gives him some knife edge chops back into the ropes. BC then sends Woof to the other side. BC ducks his head down to give him a back body drop, but Woof stops, nails BC with a kick to the skull, then locks him in for a snap DDT. BC hits the mat hard and lays there as Woof sits trying to regain his senses. AT: BC had this match until he took his eye off Woof. BJ: You know, wrestlers never get any smarter. They always, always, always duck to give a backdrop when everyone knows that ½ the time it will be countered. If I were still wrestling, I’d be a champion, and an undefeated champion at that. AT: You’re so full of yourself. That would get you in trouble here. BJ: Two words. Woof Woof. AT: That’s one word said twice. BJ: Hey Woof! Cabbage here is a Zombie! AT: Sit your ass down, Barry. Woof picks up BC and sends him into the turnbuckle. BC connects with the turnbuckle as Woof connects with a clothesline into the turnbuckle. BC stumbles and falls to the mat as Woof walks over to BC and goes to roll him over for a pin. Before he can get BC down, BC catches Woof with a small package. 1… 2… Shoulder up! Woof quickly gets up and starts to nail BC with some kicks to the skull. BC tries to fight it, but can’t and eventually collapses on the mat. Woof stops and goes over to BC’s head. He picks up BC and puts him under. He hooks BC’s arms and looks to be setting up for The Bad Touch (Tiger Driver 91), but BC quickly unhooks his arms and takes down Woof with a double leg takedown. BC then sets up Woof and slingshots him into the turnbuckle. Woof hits the turnbuckle hard and BC catches him stumbling backwards with a Spine Buster. BC stays in the same position he delivered the Spine Buster with and shoves one of Woof’s leg’s forward and holds it down. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! BC falls to the side as Woof lays on the canvas trying to catch his breathe after the powerful move. BC slowly starts to get to his feet as does Woof. Woof gets to his feet first and attempts to punch BC, but BC blocks and nails Woof with a right hand. Woof stumbles back, but stumbles forward to go hit BC with a right hand, but BC blocks and connects with another punch. Woof stumbles backwards into the ropes, bounces off of them, and goes to deliver a clothesline to BC, but BC ducks and turns around and catches Woof with a kick to the midsection. He puts Woof under and delivers a Reverse Shoulder Breaker (Powerbomb into a shoulderbreaker). BC goes for a cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! AT: The tide may be turning towards BC’s favor, Barry. We could see a new Intercontinental title champion tonight. BJ: Yeah, and I could have saw Engelbert Humperdink in concert tonight as well, but you don’t always get what you want. AT: Yeah, but you get what’s best. <Smirks at Barry> BC looks up at the crowd and signals for the Nail in the Coffin as the fans go wild. BC picks up Woof and puts him on his shoulder. BC heads over to the turnbuckle, but Woof slides down his back, locks him in and delivers Wag The Dog (Angle Slam). The fans are in shock as to what has just happened as BC lay prone on the mat and Woof is on all fours. He crawls over and makes a cover on BC. 1… 2… FOOT ON THE ROPE! The fans cheer as Woof looks at BC’s foot on the bottom rope. Woof grabs BC’s leg and hooks it. 1… 2… THE OTHER FOOT IS ON THE ROPE! BJ: How in the hell did BC get the other foot on the bottom rope? AT: I don’t think I’ve ever seen anything like that. You gotta be like a body contortionist or something to do something that rare. BJ: That’s it, BC has a prosthetic leg and he can extend it so he can cheat and screw Woof over. I bet “The Man” has something to do with this. AT: Oh will you give it a rest and just enjoy the match. Woof has the advantage and all I hear about is you bi**hing about how Woof is being screwed over. He could win the match right here and you wouldn’t know it. Woof picks up BC by the head and picks him up for a bodyslam and slams him down. Woof then goes over to the turnbuckle and climbs up it. He jumps off attempting to connect with The Discount Nosejob (Flying elbowdrop to the nose), but BC moves and Woof connects with canvas. Woof stumbles around as BC catches Woof with a kick to the midsection and connects with a Stalling Brain Buster. BC then looks around at the crowd and signals again for the Nail in the Coffin. BC picks up Woof onto his shoulders and walks over to the turnbuckle. He gets on the second turnbuckle and looks to be going to deliver the move when Admiral John runs to the ring and nails BC in the back of the head. BC drops Woof as he lands on the apron while Admiral John knocks him off the turnbuckle. The ref calls for the bell. Winner by DQ – Bionic Carrot Woof and AJ continue the beatdown as BC remains outnumbered. All of a sudden, the crowd pops huge as Numark charges the ring and starts to go at it with AJ. Woof tries to attack Nu, but BC stops him and takes it to him. Out from the back, Esco and Kore come running in to join the fray, but MV Pete comes out to try and keep the numbers from being too much of a disadvantage. AT: The gWo and The Suspects are going at it like crazy in that ring. We need some officials out here to stop the carnage. BJ: I love it. Nothing like a good old fashioned brawl to end a show. AT: Well everyone, that’s it for Vortex. Until next time, this is the Atomic Tomato, speaking for “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson in saying goodnight. The Suspects and the gWo continue to brawl as refs and officials come charging from the backstage to stop the madness going on inside the ring.
