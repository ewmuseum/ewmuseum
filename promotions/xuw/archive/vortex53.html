XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #53

13 Feb 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Pyro flies and people cheer - the time for Vortex is finally here!!! BOOM - A - BANG - BANG!!! Sparks fly, people mark out and the Hungry Mexican, wherever he may be, probably dies in a strange accident. In the Gaylord Entertainment Center, Nashville, Tennessee though everyone is very much alive and hyped up for some big, big matches on this cold February night. Some of the crowd have even brought signs with them. Ah, bless. "Saij can roll me up any time he wants!" "I have butterflies in my stomach" "Ultra Violents? Ultra Candy Asses!" "Hot Boy D in da hizzzzzzouse!" "I always knew X Factor was a degenerate" The camera cuts to the announce desk, where The Atomic Tomato and 'Mr Majestic' Barry Johnson are sat. AT: "Hello folks and welcome to another packed edition of Vortex. We've really got four of the best here in our matches tonight. We're going to see an angry Hot Boy D take on Ultra Laudust, who i still trying to impress Wolfson and The Anarchist with his Ultra Violent behaviour." BJ: "Forget that, what about the match between the latest XUDF call-up, Darkseid, and The Ripper?" AT: "That should be a colossal match between two of the biggest men in XUW. They've been trying to out-do one another in the past few days, and it's been ... well ... sickening to watch at times." BJ: "I, for one, have loved it." AT: "Yet that is very much overshadowed by he Main Event tonight, which is a rematch of the great fight from Cause and Effect between Saij and M V Pete. There's been a lot of talk that Saij is the greatest Intercontinental Champion we've seen here in XUW, and I have to say that he's got to be up there with the greats." BJ: "He sucks! Pete is gonna tear off his head and play soccer with it." AT: "You truly are a sick man." BJ: "Thank you, I'm thinking of forming a Darkseid Fan Club." AT: "Yuck. Well, in any case, to kick us off we have a handicap match between X Factor and the Lunch Room Patrol!" BJ: "I hate those goody two shoes Doughboy and the Laxative Kid." AT: "That's not his name." BJ: "Okay, Douchebag and the Laxative Kid. That better?" AT: "No." The D-Generation X theme plays over the speakers, and some pyro flies off in an 'X' formation for the arrival of X Factor. He comes down to the ring looking cocky, and with a microphone in his hands. He enters the ring and his music ends, as the crowd continue to boo him. Factor: "I can't believe that I, a former World and Intercontinental Champion, am being forced to fight a pair of useless rookies. Did I stay here and turn down WWE for no reason at all? This kind of disrespect from Mister Webster is really starting to get on my nerves and piss me off. You know what, I might not even bother fighting, I think I might just go home and have a nice little rest. It'll be the same as fighting those two Lunchroom Losers." "Lunchlady Land" hits the speakers, and out come Doughboy and the Latch Key Kid to a decent crowd pop. AT: "I don't think that the Lunchroom Patrol are taking X Factor's words too well!" The two XUDF call-ups start walking down the ramp towards the ring, and slap hands with some of the fans on the way down. They then enter the ring, as X Factor slides out and walks round to the ramp, starting on his way to the back. Doughboy and LKK call for a couple of microphones. Doughboy: "Hey, X Factor, are you scared of us?" LKK: "Are you running away like a little bully who's bitten off more than they can chew?" Doughboy: "You think you should be fighting people more experienced than us? You lost to the Latch Key Kid here on your own, so if you want a title shot of some description then maybe you should put us in our place." LKK: "If you really think that you can." X Factor looks back at the team in the ring waiting to fight him. He shrugs him shoulders and charges into the ring. Handicap Match The Lunchroom Patrol v X Factor Factor catches the LRP a little unawares and fires off hard right hands to the heads of both of the members of the tag team. He then whips LKK to the ropes and hits a Dropkick that sends LKK over the top and to the floor on the outside. Factor turns round, but right into a kick to the gut and a DDT from Doughboy. X Factor gets up, and Dough whips him to the ropes,  hitting a Spinebuster as he returns. Dough goes for the early cover. ONE... KICKOUT! AT: "It'll take more than that to keep X Factor down." BJ: "You say that kind of crap every week. Be more original!" Factor is brought to his feet by Dough, and subjected to a German Suplex, after which Doughboy locks on the Taffy Pull (STF). The Latch Key Kid gets back up from the floor of the arena and stands by the turnbuckle, ostensibly waiting for a tag. BJ: "Do the Lunchroom patrol have to tag in and out?" AT: "I'm not sure, but they must see it as the honourable thing to do." Back in the ring, X Factor is clawing himself closer and closer to the ropes, and finally grabs a hold of them. The ref tells Dough, and he releases the hold immediately. BJ: "Wuss, he needs to hold it on for four more seconds, to annoy his opponent!" Factor gets to his feet, and Doughboy whips him into the corner, next to the Latch Key kid. Dough walks over and tags in LKK. Dough lifts Factor up on to his shoulders, as LKK climbs the turnbuckle. He dives off and hits a big Clothesline as Doughboy falls backwards, completing the Meal Ticket. Dough leaves the room and LKK goes for a cover. ONE... TWO... SHOULDER UP! AT: "It's all been about the Lunchroom Patrol so far, Barry. they're looking good as a team." BJ: "Yeah, but... they smell... and stuff." AT: "Good insights." BJ: "Thank you." Factor gets to his feet and rakes the eyes of LKK, allowing him to hit a Snap Suplex. LKK hits the mat hard, and Factor goes for a cover. ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! LKK gets to his feet, but as he does so, Factor rolls him up into a Small Package. ONE... TWO... NO! Broken up by Doughboy! AT: "That dirty Factor had a handful of tights there!" BJ: "No, he was just... ummmm... putting LKK's tights back into the right place?" AT: "You keep telling yourself that, okay?" BJ: "Sure thing, boss." Doughboy leaves the ring again, and LKK starts to unload on X Factor with stiff Forearm shots. He whips Factor to the ropes and then nails him down with a Clothesline. Factor gets to his feet, slowly, and as he does so, LKK ascends the turnbuckle and comes off with a beautiful Top Rope Dropkick that knocks X Factor flying. LKK decides not to go for the pin yet, and lifts up X Factor from behind, placing him in to position for the Dead Bolt (Scorpion Death Drop) and nailing it. This time he goes for the cover. ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! LKK whips Factor to the ropes and tag in Doughboy, who hits the Bully Buster (Tilt-a-whirl Gutbuster). Dough picks up Factor and looks to hit the Cookie Cutter, but Factor drops down and hits a sneaky low blow. Dough doubles over and Factor nails the X Marks The Spot (Reverse Twist of Fate). Factor goes for a cover, but Dough kicks out quickly, as LKK steps through the ropes. Factor runs at LKK and hits a Superkick which sends him to the mat. He then charges in desperation at Doughboy, who is getting up, but Dough dodges to the side and Factor nails the ref with a big Spear. Undaunted, Factor hits a few Forearm shots on Doughboy and then leads him to the turnbuckle for a Springboard Top Rope Hurricanrana! AT: "that took as much out of X Factor as it did Doughboy, and all three men are down!" BJ: "Four men! The ref isn't looking too hot either." In the ring, Dough and Factor get to their feet at the same time. Dough grabs Factor and looks for a Suplex, but Factor blocks it and sticks his thumb right in Doughboy's eye. The rookie grasps at his face as Factor hooks him up and nails the D-Evolution (Testdrive). He hooks a leg. ONE... TWO... THREE! AT: "No ref!" BJ: "Factor's being robbed!" AT: "That's funny, it's usually the other way around." BJ: "Hey, what's that up on the Ultitron?" The Ultitron show footage of a second referee hurrying through the corridors to come and join the match. He gets to the final door before coming out to ringside, and opens it... only to have a bucket of water drop on his head. Fatbadger and Sakie jump out from the side and start laughing uproariously. Fatbadger: "FUWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" Sakie: "Dude, we totally cooled him down!" The Ultitron cuts off again, in the mysterious fashion that it sometimes does. In the ring, Factor is staring at it in disbelief, as his opponents get back to their feet. AT: "Well, I don't think that there will be a second referee getting here any time soon." Factor turns round to see LKK advancing on him. The Latch Key Kid ducks away from a punch and then grabs Factor, hitting the Cheese and Crackers (Atomic Drop and Big Boot Combo). LKK then whip Doughboy to the ropes, and Dough comes back with the Jelly Roll (Rolling Thunder) and nails it on X Factor. Dough gets to his feet and signals for the Cookie Cutter, as LKK tries to revive the ref. AT: "This could be it." Factor gets up slowly, turns round and walks straight into a big Cookie Cutter from Doughboy. The crowd goes wild and Doughboy hooks the leg as the ref wakes up. ONE... ... TWO... ... THREE!!! Winners, by pinfall, The Lunchroom Patrol! AT: "A win there for the Lunchroom Patrol, but X Factor nearly had them there." BJ: "He was cheated!" AT: "Well, maybe he shouldn't have Speared the ref then?" BJ: "They double teamed him too!" AT: "It was a handicap match, you fool." BJ: "Bah." Camera cuts backstage to the office of Mister Webster. He is sitting behind his desk and looking up at the 'Mega Powers' of Henrik the King and Samurai Bob. There is a huge crowd pop for the fan favourites. Webster: "Gentlemen, you've both been doing well in the fight against the AoD, the Ultra Violents and the other distasteful factions in XUW. Now perhaps we turn our attention to the leader of the Army of Darkness, the World Title Holder, Jack Nobbledy. Bob, you and The Ripper go back a long way. you've fought him a number of times, and often come out on top. Lately though, in the past few months he has led his army through your Usual Suspects, dismantling them and taking from you one of your prized assets, M V Pete. Yet in these recent months never did the two of you fight, one on one." Henrik starts to look a little pissy as Mister Webster concentrates on his appointed 'saviour'. Webster: "Henrik, you've come so far of late. A few months ago it would have been almost unthinkable to say that you would find the confidence in yourself to straighten out and get the crowd behind you, so much so that you won the Deadly Draw and earned a place in the Main Event at Reborn." Now it is Bob's turn to look a little peeved at the boss talking up the other man. The two men look at each other as Webster pauses, their eyes reflecting that they are two men with the same goals, but that there is still a certain tension between them, from their past, and from where their future paths may cross. Webster: "Now, one of you will have to take on Jack at Reborn, and so I was thinking of getting the match signed sooner rather than later." The two men just glare at Webster, who looks surprised and unnerved by it. Webster: "Well, perhaps we'll leave the details for another day as to which of you will be entrusted with the task of cutting he Ripper down to size..." The camera cuts back to the announce desk. AT: “It looks like Mister Webster is getting ready to decide who will try and regain the XUW World Title belt from The Ripper, that is if nobody does so before Reborn.” BJ: “Who’s going to do that, Sin Yun?” AT: “Ummmm… No.” "Turn Me Loose" by Loverboy hits the speakers, and out comes Ultra Laudust for his match. he snarls a little at the crowd, and slides under the ring in preparation for battle. "Area Codes" by Ludacris plays, and Hot Boy D walks out to a decent crow pop. He is wearing a 'Mr Perfect' T-Shirt in tribute to the recently deceased wrestler. He points to the shirt and thumps his chest, and then comes running down to the ring to get the match underway. Singles Match: Laudust v Hot Boy D The bell rings, and that is promptly followed by HBD ringing Laudust's bell with his fists, knocking back the enigmatic one. Laudust tries to fight back, but oly receives a kick to the gut. As Laudust bends over, HBD hooks hte arms and delivers a Double Underhook Brainbuster. AT: "That's the Destruction, and this could be over already!" HBD goes for a cover. ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! BJ: "Hah, stupid HBD didn't even hook the leg." HBD picks up Laudust and whips him to the corner turnbuckle. He follows it in, looking for a splash, but Laudust moves out of the way and HBD goes chest first in to the corner. He staggers off and Laudust grabs him, placing him in an Abdominal Stretch. HBD tries to fight out of it, but Laudust grabs the ropes for leverage, causing HBD to yell in pain. The ref goes round to have a look, and with shades of Earl Hebner, kicks the arm of Laudust away from the rope. HBD gets out of the hold and hits a Running Bulldog on his opponent. Laudust is down, and HBD goes up top to look for the Self Destruct (450 Splash). He flies into the air, but Laudust gets his knees up! AT: “Could that be a turning point in the match?” BJ: “I sure hope so. Nail him, Laudust!” HBD rolls around in pain, and Laudust gets to his feet. He whips HBD to the corner, and then ascends the turnbuckle to hit the 30 Second Trailer (30 Mounted Punches). After ten of them, Laudust takes a second out to taunt the crowd, and in that instant HBD Powerbombs him down to the canvas! AT: "Hot Boy D has taken control of this match once more!" BJ: "Where are the Ultra Violents when you need them, eh?" Laudust struggles to his feet, only to be knocked right back down by a Lance Storm style Superkick (the WWE wrestler, not the former XUW champion... I think). Hot Boy D stalks round to behind Laudust as he gets to his feet again, rubbing his jaw. AT: "Are we going to see the Hot Shot here?" Indeed we are, as Hot Boy D locks in a Tazzmission on Laudust for a few seconds, and then drop down to deliver a Pinning German Suplex. The ref counts. ONE... TWO... THREE! Winner, by pinfall, Hot Boy D!!! AT: "Impressive display there by Hot Boy D, he really took it to Laudust. He's been looking better and better recently, and he's not going to be a pushover to anyone!" BJ: "Ah, shut up... Ooooh, goody, here come the Hardcore Icons!" DDT and Raven X run down to the ring, equipped with a tazer and a baseball bat respectively. HBD moves to one side as DT picks up Laudust from the floor and smashes him right in the face with the baseball bat. Laudust crumples backwards, and DDT zaps him with the tazer. Laudust hits the ground, motionless, but the Hardcore Icons aren't done. The pick him up and do a Double Team Powerbomb out of the ring and through the Spanish Announce Desk. Laudust is left in a broken, bloody mess on the floor. AT: "Dear Lord, that was uncalled for! We need to get some EMTs out here right away!" The EMTs come out immediately, and put Laudust on to a stretcher, but he does not appear to be moving at all. Meanwhile, back in the ring, Hot Boy D seems to be angry with the Hardcore Icons, getting up in their faces about something. AT: "I think he's annoyed that they're overshadowing his victory. He worked hard for that win, and they're stealing his thunder!" BJ: "He deserves to have it stolen!" AT: "Why?" BJ: "He... ummmm ... he smells too." AT: "Riiiiiiiiiiiiiight." HBD shoves Raven X, and then DDT blindsides him. The Hardcore Icons pick up HBD and hit a Spike Piledriver on him, leaving him out cold on the mat as they make their way to the back. AT: "Damn those Hardcore Icons. You get shot of the Ultra Violents for one week and then they come along and ruin everything." Camera cuts backstage to where X Factor is striding down the corridors backstage, with a furious look on his face. X Factor: “Where the hell is Sakie?” Factor continues onwards, and kicks over a table full of drinks and canapés. He sees an XUW employee and grabs him by the throat. X Factor: “Where is Sakie? He cost me that match tonight by stopping the second referee get to the ring. Tell me where he is!” XUW employee: “I honestly don’t know… sir.” X Factor: “Bloody Webster! Bloody Sakie!” He storms off again, in search of the Tag Champion, and we cut back to the announce desk. AT: “Wow, Factor didn’t look too happy.” BJ: “I keep on telling you, he was robbed!” The lights in the arena dim, and "Enter Sandman" by Metallica creeps through the arena, to greet the arrival of Darkseid. He appears at the top of the ramp, wearing his long dark cloak, and slowly walks down to the ring to a degree of heat from the XUW crowd. As he does so, a midget dressed in a Darkseid cloak and mask runs up to him from behind and tugs on his cloak. Seid turns round to face the midget, and then kicks the midget into the crowd, showing a degree of skill sorely lacking in the England soccer team. AT: "The fans of XUW are not sure what to make of Darkseid. He seems to be a nasty, evil man, but his promos have that 'car-wreck' factor that compels you to watch it, even if you should really be looking the other way." Darkseid enters the ring, and his music is replaced by the juddering guitar riff of "One Armed Scissor". The heat increases, and goes right off the charts for the XUW World Champion, Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy. He walks down to the ring, black lights picking out flashes of red on his otherwise black jacket. He enters the ring and drops the coat on the outside. The bell rings for the match to begin. Singles Match (non-title): Darkseid v Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy The two behemoths lock up in the centre of the ring, and it goes back and forth between them, trying to push one another away and gain an advantage. Seid has a couple of inches on The Ripper, but Jack makes up for it in body mass and raw power. Despite that, Darkseid is the first to get a hold, moving round the back of Jack with alarming speed for a man standing at nearly seven feet tall and grabbing a Hammerlock. Jack tries to find a way out of it, and starts to back up on Darkseid, but the bigger man wrenches on the arm and keeps Jack from going any further. DS drops down and executes a Single Leg Takedown on The Ripper, grabbing a hold of the leg and putting him into a Boston Crab. Jack raises himself up on his arms to relieve the pressure, and edges towards the ropes, but Darkseid just drops the hold and swiftly moves around to lock in the Salvation (Rings of Saturn). Jack looks shocked at all this offence from the XUDF call-up, and tries to get himself out of the predicament. AT: “Well, of all the matches for The Ripper and Darkseid, two of the largest men in the fed, to get in to, I was not expecting a technical masterclass!” BJ: “You’ve got to hand it to them. They have mat skills as well as power.” AT: “Indeed they do, especially Darkseid. Jack is going to need to change it back to a power game to win this one.” Jack struggles with his legs to get some leverage, and eventually manages to push himself into the ropes. After the regulation count of four, Darkseid releases the hold and stands away from Jack, who gets up stretching his broad shoulder out. Darkseid moves in and looks for a kick to the gut, but Jack catches the leg, spins Darkseid around, and then flattens him as he explodes forwards with a Big Boot that damn near kills the butterfly eating superstar. Darkseid rolls on the floor in pain, and Jack moves his arms around, getting the strength back in them after they were twisted around previously. The Ripper picks up Darkseid, and displays his strength by lifting the big man over his shoulder and nailing him with the Call of Destiny (Alabamaslam). AT: “Jack’s back in his element now, using his power to dominate his opponents. It’s a crude method of working, but he seems to have done okay out of it in the past.” BJ: “Can Darkseid fight fire with fire, or will he keep on with the submission style moves, trying to weaken Jack for the Silver Lining.” Jack drops down and covers Darkseid. ONE… … TWO… … KICKOUT! Jack gets to his feet and lurks on the other side of the ring, waiting for Darkseid to get up. He does so, and Jack rushes him again, looking to hit another Big Boot, but the giant Darkseid uses his surprising speed for a big man and just dodges it, allowing him to turn and hit a big Release German Suplex on The Ripper. He moves quickly, picking Jack up from the floor and hitting a quick Piledriver. Darky goes for a pin. ONE… … TWO… SHOULDER UP! Jack gets to one knee and catches Darkseid with a European Uppercut, which staggers him. Jack follows up and grabs Darkseid in a Gut Wrench, hitting The Break (Backbreaker). Jack holds Darkseid there for a few seconds, across his knee, before he has to let go of the bigger man. Jack rolls Seid off his knee, and then springs on to him, locking in The Fear (Steiner Recliner)! He cranks it back, and Seid looks to be in trouble. AT: “That move has twice won him the Submission Title in XUW. It’s as deadly a finishing hold as they come, and Darkseid will have his work cut out to get out of this one.” BJ: “His only hope is that he still has enough strength to use his power to break the hold. Has Jack done enough to wear down Darkseid?” Darkseid is having his neck cranked back, but just manages to get one knee under himself, and then the other. He uses his leg strength to stand up and force Jack to release the hold, but Jack immediately smashes Darkseid on the back of the neck, and the big man goes down to one knee. Jack immediately locks in a Full Nelson and hits the Circle of Hell (Spinning Full Nelson Slam), which looks decidedly impressive when done on a man close to seven feet tall. Jack hooks a leg. ONE… … TWO… SHOULDER UP! AT: “Jack’s power is taking control now. I feel it might just be a matter of time if it goes on this way, though I’d have to ask if Jack could hit the RipSaw on Darkseid.” BJ: “Darkseid is looking in a bad way. He fought well, but it seems to be troubled times for the XUDF man. Perhaps it’s just too big a step up?” Jack whips Darkseid to the ropes and hits a big Clothesline to bring him down. The Ripper shakes his arm after the hard impact, and reaches down to pick up Seid once more. Jack looks to finish things, and sets Seid up for the Event Horizon Jackhammer. He lifts Seid up, but he slips round behind The Ripper and hits a Cobra Clutch Slam to take the AoD member down to the canvas. Darkseid grabs some breath and then drags Jack over to the corner. He lifts him up to the top rope (which is a feat in itself) and then follows him up, hitting the Penance (Top Rope Gutbuster). Jack rolls on the floor in pain, which seems to only make Darkseid grow stronger. He lifts up Jack and whips him to the ropes. Jack comes back and is lifted into the air, and powerbombed to the canvas. Darkseid stands over the prone figure of the XUW World Champion. AT: “Amazing, Darkseid has chosen to fight fire with fire, and he’s right back into it.” BJ: “I have no idea how these two guys have the strength to pull off these moves.” Darkseid rolls Jack over and places him in the Last Rites (Surfboard and Dragon Sleeper Combo). Jack’s arms flail around as he tries to get out of it, but the big man has it locked in pretty tightly. AT: “Now he has the upper hand, Darkseid is going back to his submissions style. I’ve not seen someone manhandle The Ripper like this in quite some time!” BJ: “If I was Samurai Bob with the submission title, I might be worried.” AT: “Hell, if I was Jack Nobbledy right now I’d be worried!” Darkseid cranks it back harder and harder, as Jack struggles in vain to get out of it. Then, finally, with one giant heave, Jack manages to tilt to one side and fall over. From there, he uses his free right arm to edge to the ropes and grab a hold of it. Darkseid releases the move, but Jack looks to be in a bad way. The crowd are still fairly quiet, unsure of which of the two men they should be cheering for. Jack gets to his feet, but Darkseid is quicker, and lifts him to the corner and place him on the top turnbuckle again. He climbs up himself, and hits the Dark Cloud (Top Rope Piledriver). Jack seems to be out cold. Darkseid covers. ONE… … TWO… … TWO AND A HALF… … KICKOUT! AT: “I thought Darkseid had him there!” Jack rolls to the side of the ring, in obvious pain. Darkseid can scent victory and moves in, but Jack counters with a desperation Clothesline. Darkseid gets up, but Jack hooks his head and this time nails the Event Horizon. He signals for the end, and picks up Darkseid one more time and sets him up for the RipSaw… but Darkseid counters with a Back Body Drop! Jack rolls away and dodges an attempted grab from his opponent and hits a kick to the gut, He tries for the RipSaw once more, but Darkseid pushes away and spins Jack around, hitting the Absolution (Gory Guerrero Special), slamming Jack’s head into his shoulder, backwards. Jack drops to the floor and Darkseid covers again. ONE… … TWO… … KICKOUT! AT: “Jack didn’t get to be World Champion for nothing, but Darkseid may well have his number here!” Darkseid looks to lock on the Silver Lining, but Jack pushes him away as he grabs for the head. Jack snakes out a boot and kicks Darkseid right between the eyes. The Ripper gets to his feet and places Darkseid in position for the RipSaw… and in an incredible display of strength he hits it!!! He hooks the leg. ONE… … TWO… … TWO AND THREE QUARTERS… … THREE!!! Winner, by pinfall – Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy AT: “Jack did it, but what a performance from Darkseid!” BJ: “He proved that he can hang with the World Champion. What a performance in one of his first XUW matches. Incredible!” AT: “Well, we’re going to have to look at a few replays there, as Darkseid shattered some misconceptions as to how big men wrestle.” The Ultitron shows some of the highlights as Jack walks to the back, and then Darkseid follows, actually getting applause from the XUW crowd out of respect for coming closer to beating The Ripper than most manage. Eventually the highlights finish showing, and we cut back to the announce team. AT: “Next up is the Main Event, between M V Pete and Saij, and what a match it promises to be.” BJ: “These two have been at each others throats for a long time now.” AT: "Well, now all the talking is done, and it's time for Saij and M V Pete to go head to head one more time. Saij pulled off the victory in their previous match, and that infuriated Pete no end. He's hit back at Saij, and now Saij seems to hate Pete as much as Pete hates Saij." BJ: "These two competitors are quite evenly matched, and there's not just pride at stake, there's the Intercontinental Title." The crowd is hushed, and then the music kicks in. AT: "What the hell?" BJ: "Woohoo!" "Hello Time Bomb" bursts through the speakers, and the New Royal Legion burst through the curtains, sending the arena into huge bursts of heat for the self-proclaimed holders of the L.O.S.T. Tag Titles. They come down to the announce desk and put on a couple fo spare headsets. AT: "What the hell are you doing here? You're not booked for this show at all!" BJ: "I'm sorry guys, Fruit-boy here's an idiot." Bailey: "He sounds like one." Danyon: "He sounds like a conspirator too." Bailey: "He might be, and that's why we're down here to do some guest commentary for the match." Danyon: "You might also notice we have brought with us a briefcase, and maybe you can guess what's inside?" BJ: "A bar of gold?" Bailey: "No." BJ: "Some important legal documents?" Danyon: "No, you'll never guess." AT: "A fake wooden title belt?" The New Royal legions stare at the announcer with a mix of shock and fear. Bailey: "Conspirator!" Danyon: "How did he know?" Bailey: "Look, we have with us the Legionnaires' Only Significant Extremely Remarkable Intercontinental Title." AT: "Wait, it's the L.O.S.E.R. Intercontinental Title?" Danyon: "Don't try and fool us with your acronyms, you conspirator." Bailey: "We're here to make sure that if either of these competitors get screwed unfairly by that Mister Webster, then they might be considered for a shot at the only official Intercontinental Title recognised by the New Royal legion." Bailey brings the L.O.S.E.R. Intercontinental Title from his briefcase. BJ: "Wow, it's beautiful!" Danyon: "Thank you." AT: "It's a stupid piece of wood." Bailey and Danyon: "Conspirator!" We are mercifully saved from more of that as "Bulls on Parade" hits the speakers and M V Pete comes down the ramp towards the ring to a huge cacophony of heat. The crowd boo for all that they are worth, but Pete seems to be taking no notice of them at all, just warming himself up in the ring and doing some stretching. "The Mirror" plays, and the crowd go farkin' wild for the Champion of the Interconts, Saij! He appears at the top of the stage as the crowd cheer and cheer in easily the biggest pop of the night so far. He makes his way down to the ring, smiling at the fans but keeping one eye trained on his enemy, M V Pete. AT: "The AoD has lost several titles and has been on a bit of a losing streak recently. Jack The Ripper has brought some strength back to them by winning his match tonight. Can M V Pete go one better and take the Intercontinental Title from Saij, or is it a step too far?" BJ: "I'm telling you, Pete has this in the bag." AT: "I disagree. I think Saij just has a little bit more in the tank than Pete does." XUW Intercontinental Title Match: M V Pete v Saij © The bell rings and the match is one. The two competitors stalk each other warily as they contemplate the best way of attacking their opponent. Eventually they just do what they always do, and that is lock up in the centre of the ring. Pete starts to push Saij back towards the ropes, and then shifts his momentum, swinging Saij to the far side. The champion bounces off the ropes and comes back with a Flying Forearm, but Pete dodges out of the way. Saij flips back up quickly, and grabs the arm of Pete, whipping him to the ropes and looking for a Back Body Drop, but Pete scouts it and hits a Sunset Flip. ONE... KICKOUT! Saij moves quickly after the kickout and grabs Pete's legs, shifting his grip up them, and then falling backwards, slingshotting Pete up into the air and through the middle ropes to the outside. He lands near the announce desk. AT: "Hey, don't you go near him with that belt. Just let it be a fair match!" Bailey skulks towards Pete and tries to lure him over with the wooden title belt, but Pete just looks at him with disdain. Danyon: "I don't think Pete is good enough to get the Legionnaires Intercontinental Title." BJ: "I agree. He... ummmmm... smells." Pete slides back in to the ring, and Saij is immediately on him, looking to hit a Suplex, but Pete blocks it and hits a Suplex of his own. Saij gets to his feet and Pete straight away grabs him and hoists him up on to his shoulders. AT: "He's going for the Double B early on!" Saij wriggles out from it and rolls Pete up. ONE... FORCEFUL KICKOUT! Pete gets up and just glares at Saij, his eyes narrowing as he sets himself and then charges at the champion. Saij tries to dodge him, and Pete just catches him with a glancing blow from an attempted Spear. Both men get up again quickly and lock up once more. AT: "This is an evenly matched contest thus far." Pete hits a knee to the gut of Saij, and then hits a Gut Wrench Suplex. He goes for a cover, but Saij is moving before the count can even be started. Pete whips Saij to the ropes and hits a Big Boot as he comes back. This time Saij stays down for slightly longer, and Pete gets some of his breath back. Saij gets back to his feet, a little unsteadily this time, and Pete looks for a German Suplex, but Saij hook his leg round Pete's and blocks it. Saij runs himself and Pete towards the turnbuckle, and runs up it, flipping backwards over Pete and landing catlike on his feet behind him. From there Saij hits a German Suplex of his own, and then a Standing Leg Drop. He hooks a leg. ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! As Pete gets to his feet slowly, Saij scampers over and climbs the nearest turnbuckle. Pete stands up and looks round for Saij, and as he turns he finds himself on the end of a Top Rope Flying Crossbody! But Pete rolls through as they land, into a pinning situation! ONE... TWO... SHOULDER UP! Pete gets up slightly before Saij, and whips him to the ropes, hitting a Big Clothesline to almost behead the Intercontinental Champion. AT: "This is a back and forth match. Just as one of them gets on top and starts to dominate, it gets switched around and the momentum has changed. No matter what either of them say, this isn't going to be a short, quick match." Bailey: "These are two great athletes, but are either of them really good enough to have a shot at the Legionnaires' Intercontinental Title?" AT: "You mean the L.O.S.E.R. Intercontinental Title?" Danyon: "Shut up, conspirator." Pete looks to grab Saij and lock in the Junkyard Stretch, but Saij wriggles out of it and makes it to the ropes. Pete gets up quickly and applies a series of boots to the side of Saij, forcing him out of the ring under the bottom rope. On the outside, Danyon now gets up and tries to lure Saij over with his fake wooden belt. Saij looks at him and laughs, before jumping back on to the ring apron and then into the ring, where he is met with a few hard right hands from M V Pete. Bailey: “I’m not sure that Saij is Intercontinental Title material either. Neither of these guys wants the Legionnaire Title enough. They’re amateurs.” In the ring, Saij fights back with some right hands of his own, and slowly gains the advantage. He blocks a lower shot from Pete and hits a Neckbreaker. Pete stays down, and Saij goes up to the top rope, where he dives off and nails the Farkin’ Senton! He hooks a leg. ONE… TWO… SHOULDER UP! Pete gets his shoulder off the canvas just in time, but the frantic pace of the match is slowly starting to take a toll on their endurance. Saij gets up, and as Pete uses his hands to help himself stand up, Saij gets up some momentum and hits the Downtime (Spinning Heel Kick) to send Pete to the mat. Saij spots his chance and climbs the turnbuckle, and turns to the crowd to yell at them. Saij: “Huzzah!” Crowd: “Huzzah!” Saij dives off with The Final Huzzah (Shooting Star Press) and hits… the mat, as Pete just rolls to safety in time. Pete gets up and stalks Saij as he gets to his feet. Pete grabs him from behind and hits a roll-up. ONE… Pete grabs Saij’s pants! TWO… … TWO AND A HALF… … KICKOUT! AT: “Pete nearly got a win by roll-up! Can you imagine how Saij would feel after that?” BJ: “He should try again, that would be funny.” Saij gets up, looking furious, and charges at Pete. This time Pete can’t move in time, still groggy from the hard shots he has taken, and Saij hits a Spear, sending Pete all the way through the ropes to the outside. Saij hangs on to one of the ropes, and ends up sprawled on the edge of the ring apron. Pete slowly gets to his feet, but as he does so, Bailey jumps him from behind and smashes him in the back of the head with the wooden title belt! Pete drops down hard, and Bailey stands over him, laughing. The Canadian drops the wooden belt and picks up Pete, rolling him back into the ring. Saij hops down off the ring apron to confront Bailey… and gets nailed in the back of the head with the wooden title belt so hard that it actually smashes the belt in half! Saij falls to the floor unconscious, and Bailey picks him up and rolls him into the ring too. AT: “What the hell do the New Royal Legion think they are doing?” BJ: “Saij and Pete disrespected the belt! They deserved it!” AT: “Well, the belt isn’t worth much any more!” BJ: “I know, it’s a tragedy. I blame Saij for having a thick skull.” The ref looks down at the two unconscious fighters at his feet, unsure of what to do. He goes to call for the bell for a DQ finish… but then stops short. AT: “Hey, look at the top of the ramp!” BJ: “Nooooooo!” At the top of the ramp stand Numark, Sheri… and Mister Webster! Sheri and Numark run down to the ring and chase off the New Royal Legion through the crowd as Mister Webster gets a microphone out from inside his jacket. Webster: “This match will continue. We will have a winner.” Webster leaves as Numark and Sheri continue to chase the New Royal Legion from sight and the crowd pops huge… until they look at the ring. Pete seems to have recovered quicker from the effects of the belt shots, after the colossal impact that Saij took. Pete staggers over and drapes an arm over Saij. ONE… AT: “No, not this way!” … TWO … TWO AND A HALF… … THR… SHOULDER UP! AT: “By God, the Boomer Sooner got a shoulder up!” In the ring, Pete looks amazed at the resiliency of his foe, but gets up and brings his opponent up with him. AT: “Saij seems to be running on instinct alone here after that attack from the NRL!” BJ: “Take into account that Pete’s not feeling good either. Both men were laid out there. I’m amazed Webster allowed it continue, it’s the first good decision he ever made!” Pete hits a Suplex, and then climbs the turnbuckle, flying off and hitting the M V Drop, his own version of the Senton Bomb. He then grabs the legs of Saij and twists him over, applying the Walls of Brooklyn (Jericho). Pete yanks back on it, but Saij uses the last of his strength to move forwards with his hands and grasp the bottom rope. Pete yanks the hold back one last time before releasing him, and then shakes his head around a bit to clear the cobwebs. AT: “For the first time now we’re seeing one of the combatants start to impose their will on the match. Saij is looking in real trouble here.” Saij tries to help himself up using the ropes, but he seems to have very little left in the tank. Pete helps him to get up… all the way up on to his shoulders. Without hesitation or delay, he swings Saij round and nails the Double D (F5)! Pete drops to his knees and hooks the leg. ONE… … TWO… … TWO AND A HALF… … THR… KICKOUT!!! BJ: “NO!” AT: “YES! Saij kicked out of the Double B! He won’t let go of his hold on the title!” Pete is surprised, but he picks up the body of Saij and hoists it up again, this time taking a few seconds holding him there, getting his breath back before nailing another Double B. Saij isn’t moving, but the ref is. ONE… … TWO… … TWO AND THREE QUARTERS… … THRE… SHOULDER UP!!! AT: “How is he doing this?” Pete looks exasperated, amazed and dumbfounded. He shakes his head and picks up Saij from the floor, setting him up and hitting a Stalled Vertical Suplex, but letting Saij fall face first down to the mat, and then scooting round to lock in the Junkyard Stretch! Saij seems to be out cold. AT: “Saij has fought hard, but what can he do now. He’s caught in the middle of the ring, in one of the most fearsome submission moves in the whole federation. If Samurai Bob had to tap to this, what chance does Saij have after the beating Pete has given him?” In the ring the ref checks on Saij and lifts his arm up. He counts the number of times it falls… ONE… … TWO… … THR… NO! Saij’s other arm hovers about an inch off the floor, and his eyes flick open, revealing a look on his face that speaks of murderous rage. AT: “Saij simply will not give in.” BJ: “Kill him Pete, if you have to kill him to keep him down then just kill him!” Saij starts to lift himself up with his free arm, as Pete’s grip on the hold loosens as he tires. Saij is able to get and roll around to the ropes, and the ref breaks the hold. Pete moves in for the kill, but Saij lifts him up and nails him with a hard Spinebuster. Pete gets to his feet again, but Saij I on fire and takes him down with a Clothesline, and then another. Saij’s eyes are burning with intent, and after taking Pete down with a Jumping Side Kick, he goes to the top rope. Pete is moving, so Saij waits and then unleashes a Top Rope Topsy Turvy (Hurricanrana) to take down his opponent. Pete is down, and Saij comes off the far ropes, using his newfound energy to hit the Ker-Splat (Rolling Thunder). He hooks a leg. ONE… … TWO… … KICKOUT!! BJ: “Admit it, Pete’s no slouch either!” AT: “He’s an incredible competitor, they both are!” Saij lifts up M V Pete and hits him with a Snap Suplex, but he holds on and rolls around to hit a second. The crowd are going bezerk, with near-deafening pops for the Champion of the Interconts. Saij gets up, steadying himself on the ropes, but finding new energy from the enthusiasm of the crowd. Pete seems sluggish as he raises himself to one knee, and then up on his haunches. Saij whips Pete to the ropes and goes to the opposite side himself. Pete tries a Clothesline as they cross again, but Saij ducks it, hits the ropes and comes back with another Downtime (Spinning Heel Kick). The fans go crazy as Saij gets back to his feet, knowing what usually follows that move. AT: “It’s time for the Final Huzzah!” BJ: “I hate that move. Move Pete, move!” Saij slowly climbs the turnbuckle, almost losing his footing on the way, and turns to look at the daunting distance he will have to cover to hit Pete. He shrugs and turns to the crowd. Saij: “Huzzah!” Crowd: “Huzzah!” Saij bends down to get as much power in his legs as he can, and takes to the sky with the Final Huzzah (SSP). He soars through the air, covering incredible distance as he spins in the air, and comes crashing down… right across the rib cage of M V Pete! AT: “He did it! He nailed the Final Huzzah!” The impact knocked Saij off Pete, and the champion crawls over to make the cover, but as he reaches him he instead lifts him up and throws him hard into the corner. Pete isn’t moving, and Saij goes over with one thing and one thing only on his mind. Murderous Rage! He lifts Pete up on to the top turnbuckle and then follows him up there. The crowd count along as he nails shot after shot to the head of M V Pete, busting him open. After he reaches ten, he slides behind Pete, and to finish the move, jumps over Pete’s head to deliver a stunning Top Rope Neckbreaker, but… AT: “Wait! Pete’s caught him! What’s he doing?” As Saij dives over Pete’s head to go for the Neckbreaker, Pete stands up and catches Saij on his shoulders. With an almighty heave he swings Saij round and dives backwards, sending both men crashing down to the mat. BJ: “Top Rope Double B!” AT: “What an amazing counter!” Both men seem hardly able to move, but Pete crawls the two feet that separate them, and drapes a hand over the chest of Saij. The ref starts the count. ONE… … TWO… … TWO AND A HALF… … … THREE!!! AT: “Dear God, what a match, what a match!” BJ: “Incredible!” Winner, by pinfall, and NEW Intercontinental Champion – M V Pete!!!!! AT: “Saij gave it his all, but this time Pete just had a tiny bit more” BJ: “Unbelievable.” AT: “I thought Saij had taken everything Pete could throw at him, but he found one last shot, one last effort, and it was enough.” BJ: “As rare as it is for me to say something rational and sane, it couldn’t have been such a spectacular match without both men in it. Saij has been of the best, if not the best Intercontinental Championt that we have had.” AT: “Time will tell if Pete can emulate that fact.” The ref gets the belt and hands it to the bloodied AoD member, M V Pete, who slumps down in the far corner of the ring, holding it close to him, a dazed grin on his face. Despite themselves, the crowd give the match a standing ovation. AT: “What an ending. What a Vortex.” BJ: “Be sure to tune in on Sunday and Monday for Xposure and Mayhem. We’ll be there, you’d best make sure that you are too!” Fade to Black
