XUW House Show #4

24 Feb 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW House Shows The lights dim then flicker and an explosion of pyro technics and load music rush through the stadium. AT gets up from his position, grabs a mic and rolls in the ring. AT - “Hello, good evening and welcome! Tonight we’ve got a huge show! Kevil will face off against The Comedian Chris Blake. The Commonwealth duo of King Henrik and Hodgo will get it on with the Deadly Combination. The Technicians will be up against …. AT is cut off by Little Magic Johnson, whose jumped the barricade, sat down, put some head phones on and started going for it. LJ – Hiii ….. A-tomic Tomartoe! How you doin? AT – What the hell are you doing down there? Get the hell out!! LJ – Come now …. A-tomic Tomartoe! I’m your new Calo … cola … colour commentator! AT – My ass you are! Get the hell …. Non title match – Kevil vs. The Comedian Chris Blake . Suddenly the lights go out and Lynch Pin by Fear Factory hits the speakers. AT gets out of the ring and runs up to his desk. Red flames then shot from the stage and as they subside Kevil is left posing for the crowd. A few of the regulars to the House show’s cheer as he comes down the ramp and gets in the ring and poses on the turnbuckle. The music to Crazy Train then starts up and The Comedian steps out with none other then the Anti Christ himself Ozzy Osbourne. Ozzy has a mic in his hand and begins to sing. The Comedian pointing and smiling. In the ring Kevil is lent up against the ropes. Ozzy finishes his song as Chris Blake gets into the ring. AT – Did you see that! Ozzy performed for the fans tonight. AND NOW HE”S COMING DOWN HERE!! In the ring the two men lock it up, Blake getting the upper hand by twisting Kevil’s arm behind his back. Kevil counters and flips up and over Blake then hits him with a dropkick. Blake falls forward into the corner. He turns and Kevil runs up him then flips of Blake backwards. As he lands Blake comes out of the corner with The One liner, measured perfectly it sends Kevil flying backwards to the mat. Blake quickly follows him to the matt and drops a knee into Kevil’s chest. AT – Hey look Ozzy’s coming over! Ozzy gets to the announcers table and with out saying a word he picks up Little MJ and bites his head off! AT cheers him on as back in the ring … In the ring, Blake has gotten Kevil up and sent him into the ropes and executed a very professional looking Tilt a whirl backbreaker on his way up. Kevil is out of it as Blake pulls Kevil up again and sends him into the corner. He quickly follows him in with a low blow with his knee. The is positioned poorly and misses the blow. Blake then lifts Kevil up to the top turnbuckle. But Kevil try’s to fight out, landing a few blows to Blake cranium! Blake stumbles back, but as Kevil try's to stand Blake hits the top rope and Kevil comes crashing down heavily, but is caught by Blake before he can hit the ground. Blake re-positions himself and Kevil and hits the Last Laugh. He rolls Kevil up for the pin 1 … 2 … 3! Winner by  pinfall – The Comedian Chris Blake AT – Well, some foul play by The Comedian to get the win there and Ozzy bit the head of Little MJ. I’m going to need another announcer, thank god! AT’s eyes suddenly light up as he sees the pimply faced Hot Dog Vender again. AT runs up, grabs him and drags him to the table kicking and screaming. AT – OK everyone! Look who I’ve got! It’s Simon! Simon (All ready sobbing) – Hel … hell … AT – Come on, you can say it! Simon – Hell …. HELP ME!! THIS GUYS NUTS!!!! AT slaps Simon in the face, knocking him back into his seat. AT continues as Simon begins to cry. AT – Ok up next we’ve got the duo of The Commonwealth, going up against The Deadly Combination! Non title match – The Deadly Combination vs. Henrik and Hodgo . Deadly Combination by Tupac and Big L hits and The Deadly Combination of M V Pete and Numark step out to a descent pop from the crowd. They run down to the ring high fiving with the crowd along the way. They get in the ring and pose it up for the fans. Anarchy In The UK by The Sex Pistols hits and Hodgo come bouncing out to a chorus of boos from the crowd. He poses for a moment before Rule Britannia sweeps across the stadium and the Boo’s pick up a notch. Henrik then steps out with Naron. They stop for a second at the top of the steps, as abuse and other things are hurled at them. They walk down the ramp, as they get closer to the ring The Deadly Combination hang over the edge inviting them in. But they take their time and circle the ring. Henrik calls for the Ref to get Pete and Numark away from the ropes so he and Hodgo can get in. Ref does so and Henrik walks up the steps as Hodgo slides in. Naron walks over to the announcer’s table. PFHDV (Standing and letting the Naron sit down) – Her .. he … here you go miss take mine! AT –Sit down you blubbering idiot! Naron – Thank you young man, (Staring at AT) at least some men around here still have manners! AT – Yeah and some women still act like lady’s!! Naron (Sternly) – What are you implying? AT – Never mind the match is starting! As Henrik and Hodgo are discussing something The Deadly combination come running in and belt them from behind. Hodgo goes sprawling to the ground and Numark chases after him, laying a few kicks and punches in before catapulting Hodgo into the barricade. The Ref tells Numark off and gets him around to his corner whilst in the ring M V Pete is utilising his early advantage to the max. Sending Henrik into the ropes, landing a elbow to the jaw, then picking him up again and repeating the process. After a couple of elbows Henrik is finding it hard to stand on his feet. Pete sends him into the ropes and on the way back goes for the big boot. But Henrik ducks bounces of the opposite ropes and returns with a clothesline, knocking both men to the ground. Naron – See that resilience by Henrik! AT – I saw him getting his ass handed to him, until he hit M V Pete with a desperation move! Naron – Nonsense Back in the ring both men are crawling to their corners. But Henrik makes it first and Hodgo runs in and drags Pete away from the tag. Pete gets to his feet and try’s to land a clothes line of his own but Hodgo ducks it and pulls through with a neck breaker. He gets up and begins to lay a few well-placed elbows and knees to Pete. Numark hangs over the ropes, yelling for the Tag. Hodgo walks up to him and poses. Numark goes to get in but the ref stops him as Henrik gets in and him and Hodgo pick Pete up them level him with a duel spin buster. Hodgo slaps his hands in the air, Henrik goes down for the pin and the ref goes down for the count .. 1 … 2 … AT – KICKOUT!! HE KICKED OUT!! Naron (Looking pretty ***t ty) – He’s a lucky man. Simon – Can I go now Mr Tomato? AT and Naron – NO! Henrik looks less then pleased, but picks up Pete to his feet and then hits a arching fisherman’s Suplex. The ref goes for the count .. 1 …2 ... AT – HE KICKED OUT AGAIN!! HE KICKED OUT!! Naron (Looking at AT) – Shut … UP! Henrik picks Pete up and walks him over to Hodgo, they tag and Hodgo comes in. Henrik says something to Hodgo and the two men smile. Henrik jumps to the top rope and Hodgo goes for a DDT. But Pete pushes Hodgo up against the ropes, causing Henrik to go crashing to the floor. Pete then gets a second wind and lays a few forearm shots to Hodgo before hitting a belly to belly. Pete, still with his second wind pulls Hodgo up and catapults him into the opposite corner, then delivers a running knee. Pete Tags Numark in who hits a impressive looking Jumping DDT. The Deadly Combination then sets Hodgo up for M V Pete a power bomb from the turnbuckles. As  Hodgo starts to come down Numark runs up the opposite ropes. Hodgo comes down hard and folds up like an accordion. As he lays out on his back Numark comes flying through the air like a drunken Superman and hits The Five star to complete the ….. AT – TOTAL DEVASTAION!!!!! THAT WAS HUGE!!! Numark covers for the pin 1 … 2 … 3!! Winners by pinfall – The Deadly Combination Hodgo rolls out of the ring as The Royal Legion come down and help Henrik and Hodgo back up the ramp. They turn and run down to get in the ring but are stopped by The Deadly Combination from getting in. The two tag teams stare off as The Royal Legion back up the ramp. AT – WOW!! What a match! The Deadly Combination gets the impressive win. But it looks as though there’s some tension between them and The Royal Legion! AT – Ok, next up we’ve got a big one for you kids. When the Stoners come up against The Technicians! Non-Title Tag Team Match Up - Technicians vs. Hurristoners . Stay Together for the Kids erupts the stadium and Chase and Fatbadger come down the ramp to a huge pop! A few blokes in the front row with a far away look in their eye’s, hang over and offer deals, but The Stoners ignore them and get in the ring. Play the Game then Explodes onto the arena The Technicians step out onto the ramp with Sheri Deadmon to a pop that would make Ben Hurr cower as a few people flee with blood coming from there ears.! They walk down the ramp acknowledging the crowd. The Technician’s roll in the ring as Sheri makes her way around to the announcer’s table. AT – Now this is an honour. Miss Sheri! Hey Simon! Why don’t you offer her your chair? Simon – Um … yeah .. I’m just selling a couple of Hot Dogs! AT (With a very stern look on his face and a touch of evil in his voice) – get .. your ass .. over .. here! NOW!!! Simon jumps, spilling sauce into a dudes lap. He runs over and pulls the seat out for Sheri who smiles then sits down. The guy who Simon spilt sauce on comes over and picks him up passing him back over the crowd. Simon screams out for his mummy but he’s soon drowned out as the crowd swamps him! AT – Well Sheri, what a delightful pleasure! Sheri – Thanks AT and may I just add, what match we have here! Well for the most part it’ll be good, it’s got Horsemen in it! AT – Well you can’t take anything away from the Hurristoners! Sheri (with a cocky look on her face) – Oh! Of course not! As the Technicians talk in the corner the stoners decide that Chase will go first. Fatty pats him on the back and he goes charging in at The Technicians. But Trunks catches him with a drop toehold and Chase smacks his head down into the bottom turnbuckle. BC gets out and Trunks unloads some German Suplex’s on Chase. After 3 Trunks gets up and walks up to Fatty and gives him the one-fingered salute! But Fatty grabs his finger and twists it around then lays Trunks out with a clothesline! BC goes to get in but the ref stops him as Fatty and Chase do a little double teaming, laying the boot into Trunks. The ref quickly breaks this up and Chase gets out, leaving Fatty and Trunks to duke it out! AT – This is looking to become a great match up! Sheri – It’s always great when the Horsemen are involved! Where like diamonds you know! AT (With a “What the?” look on his face) – Yes I heard. Back in the ring Fatty connects with a few Badger Scratches before hitting the Fat Burner and going in for the pin … 1 … 2 … Sheri – NO THREE COUNT HIS FOOT WAS ON THE ROPES!!! AT – Excellent observation Sheri. Fatty looks discouraged at the fact he didn’t pin him. He drags Trunks over to the corner and tags Chase in. Who goes up top and comes down with a knee drop. But the move was risky and Trunks moves as Chase hits the ground hard on his knee. Trunks staggers to the ropes, shakes the cobwebs off and runs and dives to tag in BC, who comes in and takes off Chase’s head with a running Lariat! He turns back and pulls Chase back up and shoves him towards the ropes, hitting the big man with a power Slam as he comes back at him. But Fatty is set up to do a huge Fatty Boom Batty. He comes flying in but BC rolls over Chase and pushes him in the way. Fatty comes down hard on Chase and gets straight up to check if his mates all right. As he looks up Trunks comes from nowhere and devastates Fatty with a spear. BC goes the pin on Chase … 1 …2 … 3!! Winners by pinfall – The Technicians AT – What a match. A bit of  a mix up at the end, but still a solid performance by both teams! Sheri – Solid indeed! I see you around AT AT (Blushing) – She called me AT! Mmmm ….. WHA?! Where am I? Oh! That’s right! Up next we’ve got a couple of egomaniacs! Submission Tournament round 2 – Blake Savage vs. Rujasu. Some generic rock music hits and Rujasu comes out to some descent heat! As he walks to the ring he talks some trash with the fans. But as he’s distracted Blake Savage comes flying out and hits him from behind. The two when exchange blows down to the ring before Blake gets the upper hand and rolls Rujasu in, following up with a spring board son ton bomb. Blake gets up and poses for the crowd. Suddenly Doggy Styles music comes on and the two members walk down the ramp stopping half way. This infuriates Blake Savage who starts to yell abuse at them. They begin to walk down the ramp some more before Dave Kifper comes running out to a large pop and lays out the members of Doggy Style with a double bulldog! AT – What the hells going on! This is insanity! Dave bad mouths Doggy Style a bit before Savage calls him over, they go to shake hands but before they touch Rujasu gets to his feet and hits a huge release dragon  Suplex, the follows Savage in and puts the Philly Cloverleaf on him! Savage holds out for a few moments but eventually taps out! Winner via Submission - Rujasu Dave gets in the ring and helps Savage up, Savage is obviously mad and directs a few choice words at Dave. Dave looks a little confused and points down to Doggy Style who have gotten themselves up and are walking back up the ramp. Savage contemplates something for a moment before shaking Daves hand and rolling out of the ring and walking back up the ramp. AT – Well that  was strange! Anyways next up we got the man mountain himself Severson taking on Frank Vegas! Hardcore Tournament round 2 – Severson vs. Frank Vegas Smooth criminal hits and Frank Vegas comes out. As he poses at the top of the stage The Comedian comes out from behind him and Frank turns straight into a harsh chair shot. Frank hits the ground hard and Blake is on him quickly, pounding in a few cheap shots! AT – DAMN THAT CHRIS BLAKE!! DAMN HIM TO HELL!!! Chris gets up off Frank Vegas as Sev walks out, stunned to see his opponent laid out before him. Sev shrugs his shoulders and stands on Frank’s chest. The ref counts the three and the match is over! Winner via pinfall - Severson AT – Well never the less it’s still a win. Seversons going to be some hard competition for the up coming finalists in the Hardcore comp! Never the less, next up we’ve got the always-impressive DDT against everyone’s Alco Al Crud! Non-title match – Al Crud vs. The DDT Good ole George Thorogood’s classic hits and Al comes out to a good pop. A few drunks in the front row hand him some beers on the way down, which he accepts and carries on. Blackend by Metillaca hits and DDT walks in through the crowd. He jumps the Barricade and climbs in, only to be greeted with a kick to the mid section  and a crudderline for good measure. Al gets DDT back to his feet then sends him running into the ropes. Al waits and catches him on the return with a powerslam. AT – Al’s got the upper hand early on! It’ll be hard for DDT to come back from this! Meanwhile Al goes in for the early pin …1 …2 …KICKOUT!! Al kneels on the spot for a moment, looking disappointed with the call. But he’s back into his game quickly and pulls DDT back to his feet. He goes for sharp right and connects. But DDT stands there, looking pretty pissed. Al goes for another but this doesn’t help his cause. As Al goes for a third DDT blocks the shot and lands a couple of his own. The crowd Mark out big for this! DDT sends AL sprawling onto the ropes, then sends him flying across the ring. He then catches him on the rebound with a devastating clothesline that almost sends Al’s head flying! DDT quickly picks AL back up and hits a fall away slam on him. Not missing a beat he turns and picks Al back up, only to be levelled out again by the DDT! DDT stands in the ring and stares out into the crowd, melting a fan, wicked witch style, into their seat! The fans mark out big time as he turns back and covers Al for the pin, the crowd chant along! 1 … 2 … 3 Winner via pinfall - DDT AT – That was one of the most impressive displays of total control in a match I’ve seen in a very long time! DDT is determined to make that world title his! Tune in to Mayhem people to see more of the great action that is .. X .. U .. W!!!! The scene fades out with the fans marking out to DDT as he walks up the ramp, stops, melts a few other people then walks off!
