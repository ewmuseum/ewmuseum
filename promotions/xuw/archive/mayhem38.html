XUW Mayhem The black steel doors of the XUW arena fly open. The camera speeds through the foyer, down the hallways, briefly capturing the blurred, faceless images of the backstage crew and then it surges through the curtain that leads to the ring, travelling down the aisle, past the fans and resting, finally, on the ‘Monday Night Mayhem’ emblem that is emblazoned on the battle worn canvas. If you look hard enough, you can see some old, faded, yet stubborn bloodstains on the mat. They are a reminder of the titanic battles and bitter feuds that have enveloped XUW since it’s first ever show. The undecipherable words of the Mayhem anthem reverberate around the building. The crowd’ hyped. The pyro blasts to kick off the show and they go insane as the arena becomes bathed in light and noise. Fathers hold their sons in the air to get a better view and the camera zooms in and out on random groups of fans. Some toast the camera with a beer while others hold up hand scrawled signs. The camera finally swings round and frames the faces of the XUW commentary team. AT: “Good evening ladies and gentlemen! You’re with us live at the XUW arena for Monday Night Mayhem! It’s me The Atomic Tomato! And on my left, as always, is Mr Majestic himself! Barry Johnson!” BJ: “Stop kissing so much ass Potatohead!” demands the majestic one. “Ladies and Gentlemen wouldn’t be caught dead watching this show! Let’s get real! Good evening fat unemployed rednecks and crack whores!” AT: “I don’t think you can say that Barry!” gulps Atomic Tomato as the producer has a heart attack backstage. “I’m sorry ladies and gentlemen for my colleague’s language. Let me tell you that we love our fans and we have a great show in store for you this evening! Will the relatively new team of the BDA have what it takes to defeat the Horsemen representatives, AJ and BC? We will find out any minute.
