XUW Mayhem #82

14 Jul 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Mayhem We are off and running for another exciting edition of Mayhem! The camera pans around the arena to catch such eye popping and colorful signs as… I have a face without eyes! (Interestingly enough, this person is wearing sunglasses) Where is Hardkore? Al Crud was Robbed! And finally… Blake, I own a sheep farm! The camera now pans around and down to our announce team of Mikey and Barry Johnson. Mikey: Hey everyone, and welcome to another fun filled and action packed edition of Mayhem! I am Mikey, BJ: …and I’m Mr. Majestic Barry Johnson… Mikey: …and have we got a show for you tonight. In our first match, The Shadow will be defending his Intercontinental Title against Kata. BJ: In a match that we all know Shadow will dominate in. Mikey: The Shadow has been doing very well of late, and he has brought back some prestige to the IC title after the whole Wildlife debacle. After that we will see one half of the tag team champions, Doughboy, taking on Ric Austin. As we all know, Ric always has his crew closely in tow, and we have word that LKK is not in the building…wait, I’m hearing that Dough is just arriving in the arena, and we have a camera back there… We cut backstage to Doughboy walking down a hall (the crowd pops when they see the tag champ) with a bag slung over one shoulder and his tag team title around his waist. He comes to his door and drops his bag, then finds a note taped to the door. The camera zooms in on the note, which reads “You had better pray your friend is alone in Galviston.” The only signature is a red thumbprint at the bottom. Dough rips the note off his door and looks at it confused, then looks around to see if anyone is around before entering his room. We cut back to ringside to Mikey and BJ Mikey: What the hell was that all about? BJ: I don’t know, but it looks to me like Dough might have a few worries tonight… Mikey: Anyone has worries going into a match with Ric Austin, cause it is never a one on one contest. Anyway, after that match we will see Dave Kipfer taking on his old stablemate and friend Fatbadger, although the match will be non title. BJ: What? I thought Fatty was putting his submission title on the line. Mikey: You’d think so, but no. We don’t quite understand the logic of that call by the new commissioners, but I guess it is their decision. In our main event, we will see a rematch from what has to be one of the most brutal tag team title matches in history, as the Hardcore Icons will be taking on the Ultra Violents. Tonight there won’t be any cages or anything like that, though, as this is going to be a straight up tag team wrestling contest. BJ: I don’t know who’s gonna win, and I don’t care! I love both of these teams! Mikey: I figured you would for sure be rooting for the XUW commissioners, since they are your bosses. BJ: …Good point…GO ULTRA VIOLENTS! Just then, 'On Your Way Down' by Stabbing Westward plays, and Kata emerges from behind the curtain to a small pop. He makes his way to the ring, all business and slides in when “Violence Fetish” by Disturbed hits, and The Shadow steps into the arena. The crowd begins to boo as he poses with his IC title slung over his shoulder. He makes his way to the ring and gets in, then hands his belt to the referee. The referee shows the belt to Kata, but Shadow takes advantage of the small distraction by attacking Kata! The ref quickly hands the belt to the time keeper and calls for the bell… Intercontinental Title Match: Kata v. The Shadow © The Shadow starts off with some hard rights to the blindsided Kata. The Shadow immediately backs Kata into the ropes and sends him to the other side. The Shadow then hits a hard clothesline, sending Kata crashing to the mat and making his head bounce. Kata grabs the back of his head, but the Shadow shows no mercy in stomping on Kata. Shadow grabs Kata’s hair and pulls him to his feet, but Kata breaks the hold and immediately hits Shadow with a fury of right hands. Kata backs into the ropes and hits a dropkick on The masked IC champ, who staggers backwards and through the middle and top rope to a huge pop from the crowd, as Kata stands in the ring looking very pumped and ready. Mikey: A fast match so far. Shadow jumped Kata while he was distracted, but Kata came right back. BJ: It was a fluke. Shadow will take Kata down. Just wait for it. Kata invites The Shadow back into the ring, but he remains outside. The Shadow quickly flips off Kata, who gets angry. Shadow then turns to yell at some fans, and turns around just in time to be met with a baseball slide from Kata! Shadow flies backwards and into the guard rail, as Kata slides under the bottom rope. Kata hits Shadow with a hard kick to his body, then another from his left side. Kata then goes for a crescent kick to The Shadow’s head, but he moves and pushes Kata forward, causing Kata to come down crotch first on the guard rail. Shadow then puts Kata’s arm behind his own head, then lifts Kata off the guard rail with a hard side suplex! The Shadow doesn’t let up, however, and he grabs Kata by the hair and picks him up, then throws him into the ring. The Shadow then rolls into the ring himself and grab’s Kata’s hair and begins to hit him in the forehead with some hard closed fists. The referee warns Shadow about using a closed fist, but The Shadow ignores the warning and continues to pummel Kata. The Shadow gets up and bounces off the ropes, then leaps into the air and hits a hard legdrop right across Kata’s neck. Shadow goes for a quick cover… ONE… TWO…. KICKOUT by Kata! The Shadow wastes no time in once again hitting Kata with a few stiff and closed fist right hands to the forehead. The Shadow picks Kata up and throws him into the ropes, and on the return he hits Kata with a hard spinebuster! Shadow once again goes for the cover… ONE… TWO…. TWO AND A HALF… TH…KICKOUT by Kata! Mikey: The Shadow is hitting Kata with some serious power moves here, but Kata just keeps kicking out. You can tell the Shadow is frustrated… BJ: How? With that mask on, you can’t tell what the hell The Shadow is thinking! Mikey: Well, him slapping the mat and yelling at the referee is usually a good indication of frustration. And, just as Mikey said, The Shadow is yelling at the referee and accusing him of counting slow. During this time, however, Kata has recovered slightly and grabs Shadow from behind with a quick school boy roll up… ONE…. … TWO…. … TWO AND A HALF… TH…KICKOUT by Shadow! The crowd gasps as Kata came half an inch from pinning Shadow, but he kicked out. The Shadow immediately turns and beigns to lay some hard boots into Kata’s head. The Shadow picks up Kata and hits him with some stiff forearms to the face before backing him into the corner. Shadow then grabs Kata by the arm and throws him into the opposite corner so hard that Shadow falls down himself! Kata hits the corner with a hard crash, then immediately hits the mat. The Shadow walks over and picks up Kata, and again backs him into the ropes. Shadow once again throws Kata to the opposite corner as hard as he can, and once again Kata hits the corner hard, but this time he holds himself up by the ropes… …Which Shadow capitalizes on by hitting the Darkside Splash! Kata stumbles out of the corner a little bit, and Shadow grabs him from behind. Shadow then lifts Kata into the air, and brings him down HARD on the back of his head with the “Shadow Fall!” Shadow goes for the cover… ONE…. TWO…. TWO AND  A HALF… THREE! Winner, and STILL Intercontinental Champion: The Shadow! “Violence Fettish” plays again, as The Shadow gets to his feet and the referee hands him his belt. Shadow kicks Kata a few times and knocks him out of the ring, then begins to pose in the corner with his IC title as the crowd boos like mad. Mikey: Like him or not, that was a convincing win over a talented man in Kata. He has definitely brought back a sense of worth to the IC title. BJ: …,and you doubted him as a champion. Mikey: I never doubted him, as he is very talented. I did, however, doubt the way in which he uses weapons all the time, especially that lead pipe. We cut backstage and into the Ultra Violent’s office. There are two big desks, and sitting behind them in full wrestling gear and ready for their match is the Ultra Violents themselves, Blake and Hodgo. Blake: Hello, everyone. We are here today to answer a burning question on everyone’s mind… Anarchist: …Is Mikey wearing a toupee? Blake: Well, that’s not really a burning question, we all know he is. The real question is why is the match between Dave and Fatbadger tonight not a Submission title match. Well, the answer to that question is very simple. Anarchist: While we understand that Fatbadger is not worthy of holding that title, we also realize that Dave is above the Submission title, and is, in fact, due for another world title shot sooner or later. We don’t want to lower him to the standards of a belt currently held by someone as worthless as Fatbadger. Blake: That’s right. Dave is well above the standards of the submission title, and giving him a submission title shot would not only automatically give him the title, but then the lesser superstars such as Fatbadger would never get a chance at a title. I mean, we all saw how Dave destroyed Enigma the other night at Vortex, and we know Dave’s skills. But I digress…tonight’s match won’t be a title match, and we would also like to state that this isn’t something that will happen often. I don’t mean the non title match, but I mean explaining outselves. We are the commissioners, and you will all do what we say, whether you like it or not… Anarchist: Now choke on that, Wankers! We cut back to ringside, and to Mikey and BJ. BJ: See, I knew there was a perfectly good explanation as to why the match was non-title. Mikey: That’s not a good explanation at all! That just seemed like run around B.S. to me. BJ: You’re just upset because they told the world that you wear a wig! Mikey: I do not! BJ: Don’t deny it anymore, we all know…its okay… Mikey: It is taking every ounce of strength in my body not to reach over and smack the living sh… BJ: …don’t flip your wig… Mikey looks extremely angry and like he is about to hit BJ in the face, but then stops and counts to ten…then calms down a bit and moves on… Mikey: Our next match will be between Ric Austin and Doughboy. If this were a one on one match, it would be an excellent contest. I, however, think that Ric’s cronies will get involved, and Dough doesn’t really stand a chance. BJ: Dough doesn’t stand a chance anyway. Mikey: You are aware of his fantastic singles record and the fact that he is currently one half of the tag team champions, right? BJ: Doesn’t matter, LKK isn’t here with a chair to do his dirty work. “Cup Of Tea” hits the speakers, and out comes Doughboy, to a big pop. He has one of the XUW Tag Team Titles around his waist, and he waves to the crowd and slaps a few hands as he makes his way down to the ring. Behind him comes FilmScorpion, former XUDF Timekeeper and long-time associate of both Doughboy and his tag partner, the Latch key Kid. BJ: This is a disgrace, he's brought FilmScorpion out purely to try and interfere in the match! Mikey: No, he's brought him out to stop Derrick Miles and Bob The Temp from interfering. BJ: No, it's a disgrace, and that's the bottom line because.. it's, like, true and stuff. Mikey: Whatever. “In The End” by Linkin Park blares through the arena, and out comes Ric Austin, accompanied as ever by the other members of the Ric Austin Show, Derrick Miles and Bob The Temp. Austin laughs as the crowd erupts with huge amounts of heat for the hated heel team. BTT and Derrick stand outside the ring, with Derrick shooting FilmScorpion a look that says “I could snap you like a twig, little man”. Singles Match: Doughboy v Ric Austin The bell rings, and the two men walk towards one another. Bob The Temp walks around the side of the ring and sticks an arm into it, distracting Dough. He casts a glance towards BTT, and that's all that Austin needs to lift a quick knee to the midsection and hits a quick DDT. Dough gets to his feet, but Austin has sneaked round behind him and locks in a Full Nelson. Dough struggles to get out of it, and moves back towards the ropes, but Austin dives forwards, and uses his weight to hit his Full Nelson Face Buster, the End Game. Mikey: Doughboy can't afford to fall for the tricks of Austin's friends, or he will be in big trouble here tonight. BJ: Go Austin! Austin goes for a lazy cover, but Dough kicks out quickly. Austin brings Dough up to his feet, and then whips him to the ropes. He looks to hit a Spinebuster, but Dough uses his momentum to turn it into a Lou Thesz Press. He unloads a couple of right hands to the temple of Austin, and then rolls off, before running to the far ropes and coming back with the Jelly Roll (Rolling Thunder). Mikey: Doughboy might be looking to end this one early! Dough waits for Austin to get to his feet, looking to hit an early Cookie Cutter, but as he does so, he looks around, almost nervously looking for another assailant. BJ: Hah! Bob The Temp and Derrick Miles have got him spooked! Mikey: I'm not sure about that. It might be the note he found earlier. BJ: No, it's the Ric Austin Show. Give credit where it is due! Austin gets to his feet, and Doughboy grabs him, looking to hit the Cookie Cutter, but Austin manages to push him away. Doughboy charges the former hardcore champion, but Ric ducks under a clothesline. Dough hits the far side and Austin goes for a Back Body Drop, but Doughboy jumps right over him and lands a Sunset Flip! The ref is right there... ONE... TWO... KICKOUT! Mikey: Doughboy nearly sneaked it there! Austin gets right up, but runs into a Clothesline from Dough. He gets up again, with the same result. On the third time, Dough just grabs his opponent and hits The Blender (Tornado DDT). The tag champion, goes to flip over Austin and apply the Cookie Press, but as he does so, Bob The Temp jumps up on the ring apron and distracts the ref. Doughboy lets go of Austin and pushes BTT from the ring apron. He turns around again, but Ric has gotten back up and rakes the eyes of the fan favourite. Doughboy stumbles back, and Austin whips him to the ropes, this time hitting the Twisting Spinebuster that he calls the Spine-tingler. He goes for another cover. ONE... ... TWO... ... SHOULDER UP! Dough slowly gets to his feet, with Austin calling him on. Dough walks forwards and walks right into ... The Best Damn Headlock In The Business! BJ: It's over, it's over! Winner by headlock - ... oh, sorry Doughboy struggles in the headlock, but Ric has it on really tightly. BJ: It's the best damn headlock in the business! Nobody can ever escape this move! Doughboy's got to tap! Mikey: Ummm, are you feeling okay? In the ring, Doughboy runs to the ropes, with the headlock still on, and then throws Ric from his side to the far ropes, breaking the hold. Austin comes back and runs right into a huge Spinebuster from Doughboy! Dough picks up Ric and executes a German Suplex, before locking in the Taffy Pull (STF). Austin is struggling in the hold, but not yet tapping out. Mikey: This could be it! BJ: Bah, it's not up to the standards of Austin's Headlock. Austin tries to get out, and inches his way to the ropes. Bob The Temp sneaks over as Derrick Miles distracts the ref, and tries to place Austin's hand on the ropes, but FilmScorpion comes at him, and BTT runs like the proverbial scalded dog! This leaves Austin short of the ropes as the ref turns. Ric struggles and struggles some more... and finally reaches the ropes. The ref breaks up the hold, and Dough releases it immediately. Austin tries to shake some feeling into his arm as he gets to his feet, but Dough is on him again, and whips him to the corner. Austin hits it hard, and Doughboy gets up on the second turnbuckle and starts to unload right hands, 'Kneading the Dough'. The crowd chant along all the way to ten, and Dough drops back down and does a Monkey Flip to send Austin to the middle of the ring. Mikey: Doughboy is gaining ground now here, can Austin find an answer to it all? BJ: Of course! Dough goes to the top rope and waits for Austin to get up. As he does so, Dough flies from the top rope and hits a great Missile Dropkick. Austin hits the canvas, and Derrick Miles rushes over to drag him to the outside. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO... says the crowd. Mikey: Damn coward! BJ: It's tactics! Suddenly, FilmScoprion comes running around from the side and takes out Bob The Temp. BTT hits the floor, and then Film turns round and starts to brawl with Derrick Miles. Austin staggers off and slides back into the ring... where Doughboy grabs him and throws him into the ropes... hits a kick to the gut... and Austin pushes Dough away... hits a Drop Toe Hold... and then locks in the Clap Trap! BJ: What a move! A reverse figure four leg lock, with push ups and claps inbetween! Mikey: Hang on a second! Doughboy twists over sharply, and suddenly Austin is placed in a regular Figure Four. He screams out and gestures for BTT or Derrick to help him out. Derrick is occupied by FilmScorpion, and BTT is laid out still. Austin moves over and grabs for the ropes... and finally gets to it! However, the ref is trying to sort out the mess on the outside and doesn't see it! Doughboy, however, being the nice guy, lets go of the hold. He then picks up Austin, and looks to nail the Cookie Cutter... but pyro goes off on the ramp! Mikey: What the? The ref looks up, and then tries to stop the brawling, as nobody is coming down the ramp. Mikey: Hang on! However, there are two men coming through the crowd, one from each side! BJ: It's those damned Masked Men! Mikey: The Eyes Without a Face! Who the hell are they? The two men in masks, one tragedy, one comedy, slide into the ring, each holding a chair. They advance on Ric Austin, and swing their chairs, but Bob The Temp pulls Ric's legs and he falls over... and the chairs hit Doughboy! He crumples to the floor as Austin gets up and throws one of the masked men from the ring. The other gets his ankle grabbed by BTT, as the ref turns to see Austin pinning Doughboy... ONE... ... ... TWO... ... ... THREE!!! Winner, by pinfall, Ric Austin!!! BJ: He did it! He won again! Just as he said he would against Sheri, he delivered again! Mikey: That's crap, it was due to interference! Austin slides from the ring and retrieves Derrick Miles and Bob The Temp, before walking up the ramp, celebrating his victory. Doughboy gets up to his knees in the ring, looking around to the two masked men who now stand on the outside, and make their way back out through the crowd. Mikey: I can tell you now, Doughboy is cursing the say they invented the folding chair. BJ: Hah, I like the masked men now! They remind me of LKK! Mikey: That's not fair! We cut back to the backstage area once again, and this time into another locker room. The camera sees all kinds of electronic gadgets on the floor, and parts scattered about. Sitting in the middle of all of this is Rujasu. He is holding some kind of remote device and is tinkering with it with a screwdriver. Ruj looks up at the camera and gives it a big grin, then returns to his work as we cut back to ringside. Mikey: Well, that seemed rather…erm…odd. BJ: Well, Ruj has never struck me as being the most normal guy around. In fact, I think he is probably about nut short of a pair of testicles, if ya catch my drift… Mikey: …yea…Anyway, our next match will be between our submission champion, Fatbadger, and his former friend and stable mate, Dave Kipfer. BJ: Which, as we all heard earlier, will be non-title. “Not Falling” hits, and Dave Kipfer emerges from behind the curtain. He stands at the top of the ramp and poses while the crowd goes nuts booing the former world champion. Dave doesn’t give the crowd a second glance and he makes his way to the ring when “Mudshovel” by Staind plays and Fatbadger steps into the arena to a huge pop. Fatty poses with his submission belt secured around his waist. He makes his way to the ring and slides in, not taking his eyes off Dave (obviously not trusting him very much) as the referee rings the bell… Non title match: Dave Kipfer v. Fatbadger The match starts off slowly, with Dave and Fatty circling each other and sizing each other up. The two superstars lock up in the center of the ring. Dave gets the early power advantage, and gives Fatty a hard knee to the stomach. He then takes Fatty under his arm for a headlock. Fatty backs Dave into the ropes, then sends him to the opposite side. On the return, Fatty falls to the mat and Dave runs over him. On the second return, Fatty leaps in the air and Dave ducks under. On the third return, Fatty falls to his back and puts his legs in the air, catching Dave and throwing him through the middle and top rope, and crashing to the ground. Dave stumbles to his feet, and looks up just in time to catch Fatty flying through the middle and top rope into him with a hard suicide dive! The crowd is going nuts and on their feet, as Dave and Fatty both struggle to their feet. Fatbadger is the first man up, and he immediately hits Dave with a few kicks to the stomach to make sure he is softened up a bit. Fatty then grabs Dave and throws him into the ring. As soon as Dave is in the ring, he is on his feet, and as Fatbadger rolls in, Dave immediately hits him with a few hard kicks to his back. Dave picks up the slightly weakened Fatbadger and hits him with a hard European Uppercut, which sends the submission champion backwards and into the corner. Dave follows Fatbadger in, and hits another hard European Uppercut. Dave then picks Fatty’s chin up, and hits him with a hard chop across his chest, causing a loud slapping sound. Dave picks Fatty’s chin up and hits another chop…and a third! Fatbadger’s chest is now completely red, as Dave takes his arm and whips him to the other corner. Dave follows Fatty in with a clothesline… …BUT Fatbadger ducks, and gives Dave a big back body drop! Dave leaps into the air and somehow lands on his feet on the apron! Fatbadger turns around just in time to have Dave grab his head and fall to the floor, pulling Fatty’s neck down over the top rope, sending him flying backwards. Mikey: What a counter to a counter by Dave right there. Dave showed good agility in getting over the top rope. BJ: Dave is a great athlete, and he is only better now that he is aligned with the Ultra Violents. Mikey: But is he really? I mean, recently he has had some harsh words for his Ultra Violent friends, and from what I’ve seen, The Shadow has had some equally harsh words right back. BJ: I don’t follow… Mikey: Dave and the Ultra Violents might not be on the same page… BJ: …Where are you going with this? Mikey: Dave might not be fully behind the Ultra Violents… BJ: …Don’t use your fancy technical terms with me… Mikey getting frustrated: Forget it… Back in the ring, Fatty is still feeling the effects of having his throat dropped across the top rope. Meanwhile, Dave has gotten back into the ring. He looks down at Fatty and then walks off to the corner and leans against it. Fatty gets to his feet and looks at Dave, who continues to just stand in the corner. Fatty gets a confused look on his face, as Dave stands in the corner just looking at him. Fatty walks over and shoves Dave, who just stands there and kind of takes it. BJ: What the hell is this? Mikey: I don’t think Dave really wants to fight Fatty anymore. BJ: …Why the hell not? I would LOVE to fight Fatbadger! Back in the ring, Fatty points to the red marks on his chest and shoves Dave again. Dave starts to look annoyed, and shoves Fatty right back this time, then shakes his head at him. BJ: Bah! This is stupid…KILL HIM DAVE! Mikey: Dave really doesn’t want to fight anymore…apparently anyway…but he really has no choice. Back in the ring again, the referee tells Dave to start fighting. Dave then runs at Fatty out of nowhere and hits a hard clothesline. Dave then picks Fatty up and kicks him in the stomach, then hits “The Bootie Call!” The crowd pops big, as Dave gets to his feet quickly and looks down at Fatty… …then shakes his head and slides under the bottom rope! Dave makes his way to the back as the referee starts a 10 count… 1… 2…. 3… Dave stops and looks back at the ring, and contemplates going back… 4… 5… 6… 7…. Dave waves off the referee and continues to walk towards the back… 8…. 9… Dave walks through the curtain…. 10!!!! Winner via countout: Fatbadger BJ: …What a Screwjob that was! What kind of crap was that? Dave had Fatty beaten, and he walked away! Dave isn’t any kind of man! Mikey: Dave walked away from a fight I don’t even think he wanted to start. We cut backstage to find a bunch of EMTs surrounding someone. The camera gets in closer to reveal The Shadow laid out on the ground, with a lead pipe sitting next to him. He isn’t moving at all, and the EMT’s begin to put him on a backboard. When they lift him up, a lead pipe falls out of his coat…next to the other pipe. The EMTs make no mention of it, and continue to load him. In the background, we can see two strange red glowing circles, that disappear quickly, as we cut back to ringside… BJ: It had to be Dante! You saw his eyes in the background, watching… Mikey: As much as I hate to say it, you may be right…especially since he left that lead pipe behind, and those did look like his eyes in the background…but for now perhaps we should just move on to our main event…as we will see a rematch of one of the greatest Tag team title matches in history. "Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck" hits, and the XUW commisioners, Blake Wolfson, and The Anarchist walk out to a thunderstorm of boos. They stand at the top of the ramp, with looks of determination on their faces. Mikey : Well, here we go folks. A rematch of one of the most brutal matches in XUW history, The Ultra Violents look to "rediscover" themselves tonight against DDT and Raven X. BJ : But this time, there's no chairs or ladders. There's no triple cage. No C4. The Hardcore Icons will be without their weapons, and even that wasn't enough last time. Mikey : I don't think that'll be much of a problem....DDT and Raven X have done plenty of damage without weapons. BJ : But we're not talking about DDT's cooking. Mikey : Ass...... The UV's hit the ring, and hit the corner posts, and stare into the crowd, the intensity showing on their faces. Firewater replaces Grinspoon, and "Droppin' Like Flies" hits, as The Hardcore Icons make their way to the ring. Their faces match those of their opponents, as they lock stares with the Ultra Violents, never leaving each other's sights. Mikey : If I'm hearing right, there's a bit of cheers for the Hardcore Icons. BJ : (mumbling)one too many shots to the head....... Mikey : What? BJ : I said, let's get back to the ring. The Hardcore Icons vs. The Ultra Violents DDT and Raven X roll into the ring, and stand across the ring from Blake and Hodgo. They continue to stare one another down as the ref gives instructions. Mikey : You can feel the electricity! BJ : Electricity? You holding DDT's tazer? CHEATER!!!! The ref signals for the bell, as the four men come toe to toe...just staring one another down. The ref calls for two men to leave, but no one budges. Hodgo pushes RX, but as he goes to push back, the ref steps in front of him, leaving Blake and Hodgo free to attack DDT at the same time. He tumbles over them, and clutches his knee as he lands. Blake comes up behind DDT, and hits him with a forearm to the back of the skull. But RX comes around behind Blake, grabs him by the back of the head, and hits him with a reverse DDT on the floor. DDT turns his attention back to Hodgo, and begins to work on the knee with a few hard stomps. DDT rolls Hodgo back into the ring. Mikey : DDT's found a weak spot.
