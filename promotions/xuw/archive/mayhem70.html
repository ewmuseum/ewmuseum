XUW: Mayhem - Number 70 The New Royal Legion - Bailey, Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire are still marching in a circle, still on strike from Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. Larry has a megaphone and begins to scream... Larry: What do we want?! Bailey: The end of the conspiracy! Larry: When do we want it?! Danyon: Now! Larry: What do we want?! Bailey: The end of the conspiracy! Larry: When do we want it?! Danyon: Now! The chant and march continues, as suddenly the DDT and Raven X – The Hardcore Icons appear on the scene. The New Royal Legion stop them in their tracks. Bailey: Whoa, again? You guys aren’t going in there again are you? RX: Yeah, again. And just like last time, we’re going in! Danyon: So you and DDT are in bed with Webster? DDT: Them’s fighting words, bitch! DDT throws down his gym bag and steps up to Danyon. Bailey: If you cross this picket line, you thereby are condoning the unfair working conditions that Webster is putting forward towards the real talent on this roster! RX: ...or how about we don't! D, lets move…these bunch of clown shoes aren’t worth it. The Hardcore Icons brush past the New Royal Legion as the NRL shake their heads in disbelief. The pyro explodes in the arena and were off on another long winded and hopefully exciting edition of Mayhem… Mikey: Welcome fans to another exciting edition of Mayhem. BJ: Well, Tomato…wait a minute. What have you done with the Atomic Tomato? Mikey: You know damn well what happened. We all know that Tomato was brutally beaten by Hardkore at XPosure. Video tape is shown of XPosure. BJ: When did that happen? Mikey: Idiot. We’ve got a heck of a night of matches for you fans tonight. Numark verses Dev in a submission rules match. Blake Wolfson goes one on one against the young lion of XuW the Latch Key Kid. BJ: Don’t forget the greatest IC Champion in the history of XuW… Mikey: Saij? BJ: No. MVPete takes on former World Champion Sakie. Mikey: And in our main event the World Heavyweight Champion Henrik the King takes on the devious X-Factor. BJ: The man who beat Samurai Bob last week. Mikey: He didn’t beat Bob…the only thing X-Factor is good at is beating the bishop. BJ: Beating the bishop? Mikey: XuW’s next Pay Per View is set. We’re travelling to the land of the rising sun…to the Tokyo Dome on April sixth for Power and Prestige. Call your cable company for details. Enough talk…let’s go down to the ring. The first few powerful bars of “Smooth Criminal” plays and out walks Numark with the beautiful Sheri. The crowd lets out a huge pop when they see the young superstar. Nu runs to the ring and climbs on the turnbuckle acknowledging the crowd pop. “Build Fear” begins to play and out walks Dev to a mixed reaction. He walks like a powerful cat on his way to the ring. He rolls under the third rope and starts his warm-up. Submission rules match: Numark vs. Dev Both men lock up and Dev gets the upper hand and gets a go behind. Numark reverses and goes behind Dev. Numark lifts Dev up and slams him to the mat and he lands on his knees and elbows. Nu moves quickly and gets a front face lock applied. Dev rolls over on his back and Numark holds on for the ride. With a fantastic show of strength, Dev bridges up. Nu follows the momentum and pulls Dev over and delivers a quick piledriver. Nu goes for a cover… The ref taps Nu on the shoulder… BJ: It’s Submission Rules match…Numark looks lost. Nu shakes his head in agreement and goes to help Dev to his feet. Nu slings Dev into the ropes and delivers a knee to Dev’s chest. Dev does a 180 in the air and lands with a thud. Numark goes for the crossface but Dev grabs the ropes. Nu releases the hold. Dev stands and they lock up again. Dev sends Numark to the ropes and delivers a drop toehold sending Numark to the canvas hard. Dev scrambles and climbs on Numark’s back and goes for the Lion Tamer. Numark grabs the rope and Dev doesn’t release the hold right away. Dev flashes Sheri an evil look and smiles while pulling on her boyfriend’s vertebrae. Dev lets go and delivers a kick to Numark’s head. Mikey: Dev has a vile streak, no doubt about it. BJ: He’s got champion written all over him… Mikey: In very small print. BJ: What? Dev helps Numark to his feet and delivers a chop across the Suspects’ chest that bursts some blood vessels. Crowd: Whooooo! Dev lathers, rinses and repeats the process with another vicious chop. Numark spins him around and delivers a chop or two of his own. Numark sends Dev into the ropes, Dev reverses and delivers a violent Dragon Screw Leg Whip. He pounces on the stunned Suspect and goes for an STF. Nu grabs the bottom rope again and Dev release the hold when he feels it’s appropriate…not when the ref tells him to. Dev stands over a kneeling Numark and serves up a Flurry of Kicks. Numark rolls out of the ring and Sheri runs to his side to help him up. Nu gets to his feet and says something like “enough”. He slides back in and Dev is right on top of him, but Nu manages to stand and deliver a few shots and soon it’s one for one. Dev lands one, Numark lands one, until Numark gets the upper hand. Nu delivers a snap suplex and stands and drops a leg. Numark helps a stunned Dev to his feet and hits The Sauce (Jumping Neckbreaker). Numark goes for a sharp shooter but Dev has enough presence of mind to kick himself loose and make his way to the turnbuckle. He hoists himself up and motions for Numark to “come get some”. Mikey: That Numark has got some stones doesn’t he? BJ: Between his ears, he does. Numark charges and Dev meets him with another Spinning Heel Kick. Dev slaps on a Lion Tamer to the crowd’s displeasure. Numark grimaces and shakes his head madly, refusing to submit. Dev bares down as Numark tries to neutralize the move by pushing himself off the mat in a push up. Numark’s pushing knocks Dev forward slightly onto one knee. He’s still applying the move and Numark is still in pain, but now the Suspect can reach around and grasp Dev’s ankle. Numark applies a modified ankle lock, and now Dev is grimacing in pain. Mikey: It’s a matter of who wants it most. Who’s got the intestinal fortitude…? Dev drops to both knees and tries to modify the Lion tamer. Numark twists Dev’s ankle and both men are inches from tapping out. And suddenly, both me tap at the same time…the bell rings and it’s over. Winner: A Draw. Both Dev and Numark have to be helped out of the ring. Numark holds his back and Dev is limping. Mikey: Both men tapped out. I’ve never seen anything like that before. BJ: Dev was robbed. Sheri interfered… Mikey: What match were you watching? “School’s Out” rocks the speakers and the crowd lets out a cheer. The Latch Key Kid walks out from behind the curtain and starts his confident strut to the ring. He climbs the steps and climbs into the ring. Alice Cooper’s voice is suddenly drowned out by “Snap your Fingers, Snap your Neck” Blake Wolfson steps out and is pelted by beer cups and a chorus of boos. The Ultra Violent makes his way to the ring. Match: Blake Wolfson vs. Latch Key Kid. Blake charges the Kid and spears him almost through the mat. He’s hammering away, punch after punch. Blake gets to his feet and walks away, leaving the Kid stunned. Blake plays to the crowd, strutting in the ring. Suddenly, the Kid does a kip up and the crowd is on its’ feet. LKK climbs the turnbuckle. Blake turns around and is met by a drop kick off the top rope. Blake hits the mat hard and now it’s the Kid’s turn. He jumps on the lone wolf and starts hammering away. Punch after punch, leaving Blake stunned. Mikey: That kid reminds me…of me. BJ: Brain dead and lazy? Mikey: Lightning fast and a heartbreaker. BJ: Whatever. LKK climbs the ropes again and waits on his perch until Wolfson gets to his feet. Blake gets up and the Kid goes for a Cross-Body, only to be caught by the Ultra Violent. Blake holds the hapless Kid and delivers an over head release suplex. He goes for the cover… 1… 2… Kickout by LKK! Blake helps the Kid to his feet and serves up a devastating swinging neckbreaker and goes for an immediate cover… 1… 2… Kickout by LKK! Blake is becoming frustrated at his opponent’s resilience. He scoops up the Kid and goes for a powerbomb. As he gets LKK up, the Kid refuses to let go and holds on to Blake’s neck. The lone wolf stumbles around and falls against the ropes, actually going over the top rope and falling to the floor with the Kid still refusing to let go. Both men fall to the floor, but the Kid stays on top. Blake holds the back of his neck as the kid climbs the turnbuckle. The crowd cheers, getting ready to chant “Holy Sh*t! Holy Sh*t!” Mikey: Who the hell is that? A man in a mask makes his way to ringside. He watches the Kid climb the turnbuckle. As soon as the kid is on the top turnbuckle, the mysterious figure charges and nocks the kid to the mat. The crowd dishes out the heat to the masked man. The masked man climbs the turnbuckle and waits for the surprised kid to get to his feet. LKK gets to his feet and turns to face his assailant. The figure comes off the top rope delivering a vicious Bagwell Blockbuster. Result : No Contest BJ: I don’t know who he is…but I like him! Blake gets to his feet and sees the masked man in the ring. He starts laughing and he slides under the bottom rope. Blake points at the mysterious figure and smiles. Suddenly the figure spears Wolfson to the canvas to a good-sized pop. BJ: I take it back I hate him! Mikey: I have no idea who this guy is but he’s obviously no friend of the Lunchroom Patrol or the Ultra Violents. We cut backstage to where Mister Webster is sitting with Hot Boy D. One of D’s ladies is standing in the corner, chewing gum lasciviously, as girls of that ilk often do. Webster sighs and runs a hand through his graying hair. Webster: Well, who the hell was that? HBD: I gots no idea. Webster: Well, at least this time the Ultra Violents didn’t get away without taking a hit. I can’t tell you how much I enjoyed them losing that handicap match. HBD: I’m down wit dat. Webster: I have the perfect idea too, that might just shut them up. HBD: Oh? Before Webster can say anything, in storms Enigma, followed by his manager, Michael Baldwin Bruce. Enigma stands there, looking down at Webster, ignoring everything else in the room. His manager starts to speak. MBB: We’re fed up of being treated like some sideshow, Webster. Enigma is going to tear his way through each and every one of your so-called Superstars until he is sat at the top of the heap. We demand you put him in some matches against opposition that can even come close to hurting him. Once you do, you’ll see why he is the real deal in XUW. Webster: Well, I shall have to lo… Enigma: Enigma… … … needs fresh blood… Webster seems taken aback by this, somewhat. Hot Boy D: Yo, dawg, youse betta get to da back of da line. Dere be a lotta people wantin matches here. Enigma leaves the room, and Michael Baldwin Bruce just laughs at HBD and Webster. MBB: You have not heard the last of this, not by a long shot… The camera cuts back to the announce position, with Mikey and BJ. “Anthem” by Good Charlotte tears through the speakers and the crowd is on its’ feet. Sakie appears at the top of the ramp and raises his hands in the air. He’s motioning for a belt to be placed around his waist. He’s slapping a few hands on his way to the ring. He steps inside the squared circle and does his customary warm up, waiting for the champion. Mikey: Sakie looks great. BJ: Homo. Mikey: You wish. He mimes backhands to the booing fans ringside. He motions for Sakie to step back and let him in the ring. Sakie flips him the bird. Pete grits his teeth and rolls into the ring. The fans are on their feet as the champion grabs a handful of the stunned X-Factor’s hair and climbs the turnbuckle. Henrik springs off and hits the London Calling (top rope bulldog.). He points to the rafters and climbs the turnbuckles yet again as the fans threaten to tear the place apart. His Highness is airborn and hits a textbook Kingpin (shooting star press). He goes for the cover… 1… 2… 3! Winner and still XuW World Heavyweight Champion: Henrik the King! BJ: Damn that Samurai Bob! Mikey: The champion comes out with a hell of a win. He took everything that Factor had to dish out and came back for more. What a champion. BJ: What injustice. Where’s my phone…I need to make another call. Mikey: For Barry Johnson and myself goodnight fans, we’ll see you at Vortex…and Tomato get well soon. Fade Out.
