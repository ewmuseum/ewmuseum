XUW Mayhem #33

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem The indecipherable music is blasting, but no pyro, as the entire budget was blown on Sudden Impact. Instead, we get two fat, shirtless guys with sparklers in their mouths, shooting Roman candles out of their hands. AND MAYHEM IS ON! AT: Hello! After one of the biggest Pay-per-views in XUW history, we have one of the biggest Mayhems, doubtless to be followed by one of the biggest Vortexes, and one of the biggest Xposures, and... BJ: I'm "The Majestic" Barry Johnston, and since Cabbage is blathering like a damn idiot, I'll run down the card for ya. KsC Trunkz defends his TV Title against Michael Bolling, Chase Wilson faces Blake Savage, the Friggin' Carrot faces Kore in a Hardcore Title defense, Henrik defends his Submission title agains SupaThug Esco, the Deadly Combination faces Sidhe and DDT of the Army of Darkness, and HBK defends the Intercontinental strap against Blake Wolfson! AT: ...all leading up to a HUGE pay-per-view...I just made an ass of myself, didn't I? BJ: As always, Broccoflower. KsC Trunkz (c) vs. Michael Bolling - TV Title Match "Date with Destiny" hits as Bolling comes to the ring to moderate heat. "Umali's Bar" hits and the crowd pops huge for Trunkz. The two men face off as the bell rings, and Trunkz takes control quickly with a clothesline. He follows up with a samoan drop, whips Bolling to the corner, and hits a series of knife-edge chops. Trunkz goes for a piledriver, but Bolling reverses into a back drop, and then hits a fast elbow on the downed Trunkz. As Trunkz gets to his feet, Bolling hits a dropkick and then a piledriver of his own. AT: Bolling is looking quite impressive in this match! BJ: Anyone would look impressive against a Horseman. Bolling quickly goes up top and hits the Destiny splash, followed by the Executioner. Trunkz slowly gets to his feet, dazed, and eats a Bolling superkick. Bolling quickly hits the Destiny Slam and goes for the cover, and it's enough for a three. Winner and NEW TV Champ - Michael Bolling AT: What a way to start Mayhem! A new TV Champion is crowned. BJ: Yeah, it'd be more impressive if that didn't happen about twice a week. AT: Shut up. Chase Wilson vs. Blake Savage "Welcome to the Suburbs" hits as Chase Wilson comes out to a pop that dwarfs damn near everyone elses - which is odd, as Chase hasn't wrestled in a while. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" hits as Blake Savage comes out to a hearty pelting of garbage and a lot of heat. Blake tries to take the early advantage by rushing Chase before the bell, but Chase ducks and hits a fast spinning neckbreaker, followed by a Russian legsweep. Chase goes to pick Savage up, but takes a low blow for his troubles. Blake hits the Blake-plex and goes for a pin, but only gets a one count. Blake bounces off the ropes, and gets caught with Short Term Memory Loss from Chase! Chase goes for the pin, and gets a two. AT: Chase isn't showing much ring rust. BJ: Chase is stoned beyond belief. AT: Also true. Chase goes for the combo, but Savage elbows out and hits a Blake-tastic neckbreaker. He goes for the pin and gets a two. Savage hits another Blake-tastic and goes up top for the shooting star press, but Chase rolls out of the way, making Savage hit the mat HARD! I mean HARD! Chase hits the Mindbender for another two count, and then goes for the CT Crunch, but Blake slides over his back and rolls him up. Blake hooks the tights AND puts his feet on the middle ropes. He tries to stick a thumb in Chase's eye - so as to complete the Holy Trinity of Heel Tactics - but the ref has already counted the three. Winner via Pinfall - Blake Savage AT: Savage cheated! BJ: I didn't see anything. AT: You're kidding, right? BJ: Looked like a clean pin to me, unless you count the hooked tights and the feet on the ropes, which I don't. We cut to backstage, where Henrik is wandering around nervously muttering. Henrik: There's already been one title change tonight...bloody conspiracy...if Kore beats that bloody Carrot, I'll know something's up... Bionic Carrot (c) vs. Kore - Hardcore Title Match "Superstar" hits and Kidd Kore comes out to a moderate pop. "Bad Moon Rising" hits and Bionic Carrot comes out to a bigass pop. The two men each bring a cookie sheet with them to the ring, shake hands as the bell rings, and then begin to smack each other in the head with said cookie sheet. Carrot gets the advantage, taking Kore down to one knee, and then kicking the cookie sheet into Kore's face. Carrot goes to the outside and gets the Trashcan O' Hardcore Goodies which is mysteriously ALWAYS at ringside. He takes Kore down with an elbow smash, and then hits a hangman's neckbreaker on to a block of wood he pulled from the trashcan. Carrot goes for the cover, but only gets a two. AT: Quite a display of resiliency by Kidd Kore! BJ: Well, hitting that boy about the head and shoulders isn't going to do much good. Carrot hits a reverse shoulderbreaker for another two, and then goes for the stalling brainbuster, but Kore floats over and hits the Paranoia Anonymous. Kore gets the bowling ball from the trashcan and drops it on Carrot's "carrot", and then hits the Snake Eyes. He throws BC to the corner kicks him down, positions the trashcan, and then climbs the ropes on the adjacent corner. Kore hits the Kranium Krunch and makes the cover. It's good for the three. Winner and NEW Hardcore Champion - Kidd Kore Backstage, Henrik looks more paranoid than usual. He looks worriedly at Naron Henrik: Damnable conspiracy. I'll find a way around this... Henrik leaves and Naron watches him go. Blake Savage walks by and stops at Naron. BS: Hey Highness....didn't I look....charismatic out there tonight? We return to the announce position. AT: That Blake Savage is hitting on a married woman! BJ: Yeah, they're the best... Henrik (c) vs. Supathug Esco - Submission Title Match "Doggz" hits and Esco comes out to much heat. Then "Rule Brittania" hits and a very confused looking Henrik comes out...the mic catches some of his mutterings on his way to the ring. Henrik: Conspiracy....must not lose title.....Bailey beat the conspiracy....hmmm.... Henrik rolls into the ring and tries chewing on a turnbuckle. He looks disgusted, and turns into a clothesline from Esco. Esco beats down Henrik in the corner. Esco hits a DDT, and then a spinning sidewalk slam. He goes for the Thug Passion, but Henrik quickly gets to the ropes. Esco goes for the Assault and Battery, but Henrik rides over, rakes Esco's eyes, and then hits a dropkick to the knee, taking Esco down. Henrik rolls out of the ring, grabs his belt, and runs to the back. Esco gets to his feet, trying to find Henrik, as the referee makes the count... Winner by Countout - Esco. Henrik retains title AT: Henrik's been acting awfully strange lately... BJ: You're telling me...he tried to imitate Bailey! The Deadly Combination vs. The Army of Darkness (Sidhe/DDT) "Step Up" hits as the Deadly Combination comes to the ring to a gargantuan, nay, monolithic pop. The bizarre funeral music plays, and then "One Armed Scissor" hits as Sidhe, DDT, and Kat come to the ring to a mixed - but extremely loud - reaction. DDT and Numark start off in the ring. DDT quickly takes down Numark with a short clothesline, and then goes to work on the injured knee. DDT hits a belly-to-belly suplex, and then the Shock Treatment DDT. He hits a series of forearm smashes, kicks out Numark's injured knee again, and then drags him to the AoD corner. AT: Looks like the AoD is really capitalizing on Numark's injury. BJ: Yeah, and DDT's about to bring in the technical part of the Army. AT: Hold on...what the hell? Sidhe jumps off the ring apron and refuses the tag. Nu hits a desperation low blow on DDT, followed by a DDT. Sidhe stays at ringside, pacing. AT: Looks like Sidhe's upset about the IC title match from Sudden Impact. Numark hits a reverse DDT, and then tags in MV Pete. Pete hits a superkick, and then powers up DDT for a chokeslam. DDT looks dazed as Pete whips him to a corner and then tags in Numark. Pete sets DDT up on the top rope and starts climbing up to deliver the Total Destruction. BJ: This might be a bit early in the match to try this... AT: I was thinking the same thing...hold on...the f***? Kat jumps up on the ring apron and flashes Numark, distracting him as Sidhe knocks him off the top rope. DDT punches Pete in the head a few times, and then sets up for a top rope powerbomb of his own. DDT and Sidhe hit their own Total Destruction on Pete, but rather than go for the cover, Sidhe just starts punching Pete in the face repeatedly. DDT goes to the outside, grabs Numark, and rams him face first into the guard rail. Sidhe tosses MV Pete to the floor, bounces off the ropes, and hits a Candy Flip on Pete. AT: This is totally uncalled for! BJ: Why? The AoD has no reason to win this match! Sidhe leaves Pete on the mat and heads over to DDT and Nu, where Nu is starting to fight back. Nu lunges at DDT, who lifts him, and Sidhe completes an old-school Darkness Deathdrop (3-D). Nu comes around and jumps Sidhe from the back, and is briefly beaten on by both AoD members, before Nu collects himself and attacks DDT. The four men and Kat fight to the back as the ref calls for the bell. Referee's Decision - No Contest AT: That was horrible... BJ: I think Sidhe and DDT were just sending a message that they're just as dangerous without gold as with. HBK (c) vs. Blake Wolfson - Intercontinental Title Match "I Stand Alone" hits as Wolfson makes his way to the ring to gigantic, massive, oppressive heat. "Six Feet Under" hits and HBK comes out to a pop which is transcendent in it's majesty. The two men square off as the bell rings. Wolfson immediately takes advantage with a big clothesline, followed by a legdrop. He goes for a pin, but barely gets a one count. Blake hits a flying forearm and a Russian legsweep, then hits the Jugular for a two count. He goes for a top rope legdrop, but HBK rolls out of the way. Phoenix takes advantage with a fast heel kick and a side-suplex AT: This match is going to be a close one. BJ: It will be until Blake gets his momentum going, then it'll just be a squash. HBK hits a flying elbow for a two count, and then a butterfly suplex. He follows it up with Vertical Defiance for another two. He goes up for the Balance Point, but Wolfson follows him up and delivers a top rope belly-to-back suplex. HBK's feet hit the ref on the way down and all three men are out in the ring. AT: What action, what excitement, what... BJ: ...a smug-looking Ripper coming down from the back! The Ripper steps into the ring just as HBK gets to his feet. Jack kicks HBK in the stomach and delivers a double Ripsaw before leaving. Wolfson gets up slowly, as does the referee. Wolfson picks up the limp HBK and hits the Crying Wolf for the pin. Winner and NEW Intercontinental Champion - Blake Wolfson AT: I don't believe it! HBK was just robbed! BJ: What? AT: You damn well saw it! BJ: No I didn't, you're hallucinating. This is Barry Johnston for the Plutonium Plum, bidding you all a good night. Fade out on Wolfson celebrating to a chorus of boos.
