XUW XPosure Xposure - July 14, 2000 Tiger Mask V vs Laudust "The Natural" by Randy Newman blares and Laudust makes a dramatic entrance to mild reaction as makes his way down the ramp to the ring. As gloats to the crowd, "Pushes Me Away" by Linkin Park and Tiger Mask V sprints down the ramp and slides under the rope.  Before Laudust turns around Tiger nails him with a Drop Kick to the back sending Laudust face-first into the corner.  As the bell rings, Laudust staggers backward as Tiger has quickly made his way to the tope and hits a Spinning Huricanrana.  He jumps on the downed Laudust 1…. 2… Kickout! Frustrated, Tiger jumps up and lays Boots into Laudust’s back and head. He brings Laudust to his feet by the hair and Irish Whips him into the ropes. Laudust comes off the ropes and is met with a Spinning Wheel Kick.  Again , Tiger goes for the pin… 1…. 2…. Kickout Tiger gets up and drags Laudust up, but Laudust nails a Low Blow out of the ref’s sight. Tiger is dazed in pain. Laudust hits a series of punches to Tiger’s head. He grabs Tiger and nails a Back Breaker.  Laudust bouces of the ropes and hits a KneeDrop to Tiger’s head. He picks Tiger up and whips him into the far corner.  Laudust runs and hits a Back Elbow on Tiger, who falls back into the corner , barely standing. Laudust climbs to the 2nd turnbuckle and delivers the 30-second Trailer (30 punches to the head). After the last punch Tiger slumps in the corner. Laudust gloats and makes his way to the center of the ring. He runs towards Tiger in the corner and goes for the Sneak Peek (Bronco Buster)…..but Tiger rolls out and Laudust’s groin lands on the turnbuckle and he crumbles in pain immediately   Tiger picks him up near the corner, cradles his head in his arm, climbs the ropes and hits a Toranado DDT.  Tiger climbs back to the top turnbuckle as Laudust groggily gets to his feet near the corner. As Laudust stands up Tiger comes off the corner for a Standing Moonsault (Backflip Crossbody). The crowd is a mix of boos and cheers as Tiger gets Laudust up and signals for his finsiher. He sets Laudust up and hits a Tiger Driver’ 91…goes for the pin…1……2……..3……. Winner – Tiger Mask V Saij vs Syco "This is Not" by Static-X comes on as the crowd cheers and Syco makes it to the ring.  As he acknowledges the crowd “Want it All" by Queen plays. The crowd dies down and Saij goes down the ramp to the ring. The bell rings and the match starts.  Both men circle each others then lock up  Syco gets the advantage a slaps on a Chicken Wing. Saij reverse it into his own Chicken Wing. Syco elbows back into Saij’s head to try and break the hold, but Syco duck them and scoops Saij up for a Release German Suplex. The crowd cheers and Syco stomps on Saij then picks him up and delivers a quck DDT. Syco immediately pounces on Saij and locks the Paralyzer (STF) on him…the refs checks on Saij as he screams in pain. Syco cinches harder on the hold and the refs checks again. Syco finally gives up on the hold and stands over the fallen Saij.  He picks him and whips him to the ropes. Syco goes for  a Clothesline, but Saij ducks, hits the opposite ropes and nails Syco with a Clothesline of his own . As Syco lies prone, Saij runs to the ropes, comes off and hits Ker-Splat (Rolling Thunder). He goes for the pin… 1….. 2…… Foot on the Ropes! Saij angrily gets up and using the ropes as leverage presses his foot into Syco’s throat. He releases it has the ref hits 4 and yanks Syco up by the hair. Saij goes for a roundhouse right but Syco blocks it and hit one of his one. He follows this with two more, sending Saij backwards. He grabs the staggered Saij and nails him with the Pain Killer (DVD)..goes for the pin…. 1.. 2 1/2.. Kickout Syco gets up thinking he won.. as the ref explains to him that it was only 2 ½ Saij nails him with a  stiff Right to the Head. He whips Syco into the corner and hits a Splash. Syco falls to the mat and Saij climbs the ropes and hits Eat My Shorts (Leg Drop) ropes. He quickly picks Syco up , whips him into the ropes and nails Downtime (Spinning Heel Kick) sending Syco hard to the mat near the corner. Saij goes to the top turnbuckle and delivers Ground Zero (Senton Bomb) . He goes for the pin…. 1….. 2…. 3….. Winner  -Saij New Royal Legion vs Apostle/Bluto "Fight for Your Right" by The Beastie Boys blares over the PA and the crowd cheers loudly as Bluto , beer in hand, walks down the ring. He slaps hands as he gets in the ring. He raises his beer and toasts the crowd when “Psalm 69” by Ministry plays, and The Apostle appears on the ramp. Bluto looks confused, and crowd dies down, as The Apostle make his way down the ramp carrying a hockey stick. As the Apostle climbs in the ring Bluto goes to talk to him when "Hello Time Bomb" by Matthew Good Band plays, and the New Royal Legion come out down to the ring.  The crowd boos as they get in the ring and the bell rings. Bluto is still attempting to talk to The Apostle, when The Apostle, still holding the hockey stick, runs over to the corner where Danyon and Bailey are and begins to attack the turnbuckle with is mouth. The Royal Legion look at him and each other no sure what to make of it. Before Bluto can do anything The Apostle is in Danyon’s face with a mouthful of padding. He slaps Danyon across the face. Immediately , Dayon grabs the hockey stick and nails The Apostle over the head with it causing The Apostle to spit padding out as he falls to the mat. The ref signals for the bell as the Royal Legion both lay into The Apostle with boots and fists. Bluto looks disgusted at his partner and just slip out of the ring and up the ramp as the continue Winners by DQ – The Apostle/Bluto Kevil vs Destroyer "Lynch Pin" by Fear Factory comesoverthe PA and the crowd boos as Kevil comnes down to the ring.The crowd soon begins to cheer as "Still Fly" by Big Tymerz signaling  Destroyer’s entrance.  He looks ready to go as he climbs in the ring and the beel sounds.  Immediatle Kevil runs at him and runsrigh into a Head Scissors by Destroyer.  As a shocked Kevil gets to his feet and staggers into a Spinebuster by Destroyer.  Destroyer makes it to the top turnbickle quickly and comes off with a 450 Splash. He goes for the pin…. 1……….. 2………. Kickout! The crowd and Destroyer both groan at the nearfall. He grabs the barely awake Kevil by the hair and kicks him in the gut, doubling him over. He gets a double underhook on Kevils arms and hits The Destruction (Double-Under Hook Brainbuster)..Goes for the pin.. 1…. 2…. 3….
