XUW Road Rage 2

4 Aug 2002

XUW Road Rage Explosions happen......people die. And thus another XUW pay-per-view begins. Adults and youngsters alike simultaneously scream and cream as the house lights come on and the cameras pan the sell-out capacity crowd here in Phoenix, Arizona for Road Rage 2!!! A rainbow of colors adorn the signs that fill the arena as only the front row of fans can actually see the ring. “I can kick Jack’s ass”   “Hurrisuckie”  “It’s my Destiny to be here” “Bob = Icon”  “Cake Pulfson”  “gWo 4 a while”  “I used to be a Horsemen too”  “I came to see Laudust”  “...”  “-er Sooners”  “I shall return, after I piss”  “Deport me!”  “I like pudding with my puddin” Atomic Tomato: Hello fans, I am Atomic Tomato. Barry Johnson: And I am Mr Majestic, Barry Johnson. AT: And welcome to Road Rage 2. Wow, it’s hard to think that XUW has been around for over a year now. To have a ppv event with the number ‘2’ in it....that means that XUW is in a very elite group of wrestling federations. When you consider that the average e-fed lasts less than a month, and it’s considered very good to last 6 months.....here we are as strong as ever. BJ: It was August 28, 2001 that XUW had it’s first ppv, Road Rage. And this past Sunday marked the one year anniversary of the existence of XUW. We would be remiss if we didn’t take a minute to thank Fluid and the *censored* crew, Blitzwing, Diablos26, HBK, Blake Wolfson and all the others who helped to make XUW rise from its humble begins as without them, we wouldn’t be here today to bring you this night full of action. AT: And what a night full of action we have. Every single title is on the line tonight. We have a hardcore battle royal. We have grudge matches. We’ve got the conclusion to the IC Title tourney. Heck, we even have a pudding match!! BJ: I love pudding. And I love women. I wonder who the lucky soul will be who gets to referee the pudding match between Kat and Sheri? I would like to personally state that I am volunteering my services to be the ref for that match should something happen to the scheduled referee. AT: One match that I wouldn’t want to referee is the Hardcore battle royal. CK, Syco, Michael Bolling, Severson, Blake Savage, and Laudust will all be involved with the winner getting a shot at the Hardcore title on the first Monday Mayhem following the ppv.  Tonight we also have a grudge match between former tag team partners as Rujasu and Destroyer do battle....I’m anxious to see which Eliminator gets eliminated in that one. BJ: Don’t do comedy eggplant, no matter what corny lines they feed you in the headset.  Also tonight we have the exciting conclusion to the IC Title Tournament. This first ever double elimination tournament as been in the spotlight over the past few weeks as 8 men are now down to 4. Woof is also the only man remaining without a loss in the tournament. First we have Numark facing NFM, then the winner gets no rest as he is then thrust into a match with Kore. Whomever survives all of that, will go on to face Woof which the remaining loser’s bracket wrestler must defeat twice if he wishes to become the IC champion. AT: That sure is a tall task for all 3 men in the loser’s bracket right now. Another tall task could just as easily be a walk in the park as Jack or Jack the Ripper will show up to fight Hodgo and Apostle for the Hardcore Title. The Submission title is on the line as the dark DDT faces off with the fighter Dev. BJ: We have a first here at Road Rage 2, a ‘Pit Match’ between Admiral John and Bionic Carrot. It was AJ who competed in the first ever ppv match for XUW when he wrestled Toto at last year’s event. I’m not really sure of the details to this Pit match, but it’s rumored to be at the old Horsemen complex, which is now a pit of rubble. AT: The TV Title match tonight features two of the most impressive rookies to enter XUW since perhaps Dave Kipfer, Blake Savage, NFM, Frank Vegas, and Kevil all arrived in the beginning of 2002. The Professor Ric Austin has been trying to serve up a bit of class to XUW and certainly has that deadly headlock at his disposal. His opponent, Saij, is already being called a future main eventer here in XUW. There has been talk of this match involving a blindfold, but so far no official word has been made as to its use in this match. BJ: Tonight’s feature matches are the tag team title match and the World Title match. These two contests are expected to be very very highly competitive, and the hype and build up to them as been nothing short of phenomenal. The rest of the roster who one day hopes to hold either the tag titles or the World Title will no doubt be looking on as we could get two classic bouts at the end of the ppv.  The tag team bout is interesting to say the least. We have the champions, the Boomer Sooners, who beat one of the top teams ever in XUW the Deadly Combination last month at Liberation. Dave Kipfer and Chase Wilson have shown nothing to make us believe that win was a fluke, or that they don’t take the responsibility of holding the tag belts very seriously.  They aren’t conventional, but they are very successful. AT: Speaking of unconventional, their opponents fall under that category as well. The Beat Down Authority seemed to be an unbeatable fighting force when they formed with the birth of the gWo. The XUW tag division had to be wondering just how long it would take these two stalwarts to achieve team gold. The path to gold has proved to be a bumpy one though. First, the gWo concentrated on winning the Stable Wars event, then the destruction of the Horsemen, and now....now their plot thickens as a super-hero has entered the fray. Hurrisakie, not seen since the winter time, has burst onto the seen to warn Esco and others about the evils of the gWo and it’s leader the Grand Wizard. With Sak...errr, Hurrisakie now out of the gWo, that puts a twist onto tonight’s matchup. Hurrisakie fighting for truth, justice, freedom and the Hurri-way of life. And Supa Thug Esco fighting for the honor of the gWo. One team, two causes.  I just don’t know if the Wiz, Esco, and Hurrisakie can work together to win those tag titles. World Champion and #1 contender. They’ve been playing mind games with each other for the past few weeks. And the time for play is over. Both men are in near peak condition and ready to wage war upon each other. I can’t wait anymore. This is just going to be a great night of action and excitement here in Phoenix, Arizona for Road Rage 2!!
