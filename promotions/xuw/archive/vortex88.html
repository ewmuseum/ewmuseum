XUW Vortex #88

31 Oct 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Vortex BOO!!!!!!!! Bet that scared you, eh? No? Well maybe this will... it’s time for the XUW Costume Battle Royal! We’re in the XUW Arena, and the whole place is decked out in a theme, that theme being, surprisingly, Halloween. There are gravestones on the way to the ring, there are spooky pictures hanging around the arena, and all in all it looks a bit creepy. Speaking of creepy, most of the fans in attendance have brought their own costumes, and some have brought their own signs! “Happy Fark-O-Ween!” “I want to go as Chase Wilson!” “Mmmm, Violence.” “Ric Austin win the match? Fuggettaboutit!” “The New Royal Legion: United and unstoppable!” and “Blake Wolfson Is Gay!” …however, security quickly remove that sign as they deem it to be offensive. Speaking of offensive, we go down to the announce desk, where Mikey and BJ are waiting. Mikey is dressed as a large pumpkin, and BJ is dressed as a Hawaiian hula girl. Mikey: Hello! BJ: Aloha! Mikey: Indeed, and welcome to the second annual XUW Halloween Costume Battle Royal! BJ: Is it Battle Royal or Battle Royale? Mikey: I honestly don’t know. BJ: Oh. Mikey: Well, indeed. We’re awaiting all our participants to come down to the ring, but first apparently Sev will be coming down to ringside to make sure that no cheating goes on, and that only people who are meant to be in here get to compete! BJ: Bad news for XUDF types then! Mikey: yes it is. Well, let’s go backstage. I believe he’s preparing to come down to ringside, with April O’Neill. We cut backstage, and indeed out come Sev and April, walking purposefully down towards the crowd. Sev is wearing a Hermann Munster costume, and April is looking delectable in her yellow leather outfit, a la Uma Thurman in ‘Kill Bill’. They walk past two of the contestants as they do so… one of whom seems to be a little irritable. These two people are dressed as The Flintstones. One is dressed as Fred and one as Wilma. Wilma: I can’t believe that these were the only costumes you could get. Doughboy, I swear I will pay you back for this! Fred: Relax Latch… you make a great Wilma Flintstone! Fred Flintstone starts laughing uncontrollably, as Wilma tries to smack him round the head. He walks down to ringside, and sits down next to Barry Johnson and Mikey. Mikey: Good to have you here, sir. Sev: … Mikey: Thank you. So, is the Battle Royal going to begin now? Sev: … Mikey: Wow! BJ: What did he say? April: He said that before that happens, we’ll find out about the remaining place in the Chris William Memorial Cup! BJ: Excellent! The crowd goes absolutely nucking futs for the return of one of the legends of Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. The only living member of the XUW Singles Wrestler Hall of Fame walks down to the ring, smiling as he does so, a microphone in his hand and a T-shirt with Stu Hart on it over his chest. He steps between the ropes again, and waits for the cheers to die down. He then waits some more… …and some more… …and eventually it dies down enough for him to be heard. Bob: Wellllllllllllllllllllll… Big pop! Bob: I guess not everyone was expecting me here tonight then? Another big pop. I think you can take for granted that every time he pauses, there will be a big pop. It’s Samurai Bob for crying out loud! Bob: Well, maybe some of you have been reading the dirt sheets, or going to those smark sites these days and found out I would be here, but nonetheless, here I am. Poppage! Bob: So, what am I doing here? Am I returning to XUW? Tension… Silence… Bob: I’m afraid I’m not. BOOOOOOOO!!! Bob: I said when I retired that I was going to bow out of XUW, and let the young guns have their time. They’re doing it now. Names like Saij, The Shadow, Ric Austin, The Anarchist, Darkseid, The Lunchroom Patrol… they’re the stars now, not me. I’m just happy to be sat at home with the love of a beautiful woman and a small child I can treasure. Crowd goes all soppy for a moment. Bob: Yet… there is a reason I’m here. You see, the other day I got a strange invitation through the door. It was inviting me to represent Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling in an inter-federation tournament, namely the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. Talk about being torn. I had no idea whether to participate or not. Everyone who knows me will know that I want to do it, hell, I’d want to wrestle forever… but for my body. I don’t want to overstay a welcome, and I don’t want to end up like Tom Billington or any of those other guys… Yet… it kept on pulling me back. One night only, one glorious night. What a way it would be to end a career, what a way to show the fans the skills I had for the very last time. I weighed it up, and then looked over at Naron, and over at our baby. In the end, I couldn’t do it. I couldn’t accept the place in the CWMC. Imagine if I got injured badly. How would I face them? So it was with a heavy heart that I wrote a letter back to XUW, thanking them for the offer, but saying that I couldn’t participate. The crowd is now a bit upset… they wanted Bob in the CWMC! Bob: So I was going to mail it, but I forgot. Each day I kept on forgetting, until I received another letter. This was the list of competitors. Yet where it said Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling, there were four slots, not just three, as was said here previously. The crowd is interested now… hanging on every word. Bob: I looked down, and saw something that changed my mind. I saw one name, and it sent a chill down my spine. Entered for Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling was… Saij… Huge crowd pop from the Fark-O-Maniacs! Bob: The Shadow… Huge heat from everyone sane. Bob: … … …and The Ripper. Stunned silence. Bob: Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy has his name down to compete in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. The crowd starts to chatter incessantly. Can this be true? Can this really be true? Bob suddenly looks deadly serious. Bob: If this is some kind of stupid f**ing prank then someone is going to get more than me throwing an egg at their window… I’m gonna f***ing well bust them open. POP! Bob: Jack… If you’re really out there, if you’re not really dead, you be careful. You make sure that you’re healing up nicely… … …because this damn old man is going to come to the CWMC and make the hell sure that you are gone. You’re not coming back, Jack. I made sure you were gone, I rid the world of your rotten damn carcass, and nothing… I repeat, NOTHING… is going to stop me from doing so again. I couldn’t live with myself if I ended up seriously injured in competition… …but I couldn’t live at all if I didn’t make sure that son of a bitch is gone. ENORMOUS POP! Bob: So mark down my name.
