XUW Mayhem The XUW logo flashes across the screen and a video package showcasing XUW wrestlers, random shots of them performing moves, and some backstage antics set to Drowning Pool's "I Don't Care About Anyone". The video package closes with Sidhe and Kat embracing outside the ring after Sidhe succesfully defended his XUW title, followed by footage of X-Factor standing in the ring looking furious. That scene fades away and is replaced with the words, "Unknown Factors - Sunday October 6, 2002.... The piros explode and the crowd goes somewhat bonkers.  The camera pans around the arena, capturing various signs bearing such slogans as "WOLFSON ATE MY BABY" "IF YOU READ THIS SIGN, YOU'RE A COCKHEAD" "HUZZAH!" and "I AM AN INCONSIDERATE BASTARD WHO CARRIES A REALLY, REALLY BIG SIGN TO THE SHOWS WITH HEAPS OF WORDS ON IT, AND IT TAKES UP SO MUCH SPACE THAT LIKE, AT LEAST TEN OF THE PEOPLE SITTING BEHIND ME CANT EVEN SEE THE FRICKEN WRESTLING.....  THATS HOW DAMNED ANNOYING I AM.....  THE SIGNS SO BIG, AND IT DOESNT EVEN HAVE ANYTHING TO DO WITH WRESTLING..... I'M NOT EVEN GOING TO PUT A WRESTLERS NAME ON IT, THATS HOW LOW I AM, AND THERE"S NOTHING ANY OF YOU CAN DO TO STOP ME!  HA! HAHAHAHAHA!". The gentlemen carrying said sign is quickly escorted from the arena, as we cut to Atomic Tomato and The Majestic Barry Johnston..... AT:  Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to another exciting edition of XUW Mayhem!  I am, of course, Atomic Tomato, and with me as always is The Majestic Berry Johnson. BJ:  Hello Cocktail Olive!  Tonight, two masters of Submission wrestling face off for the TV title in what should be a classic! AT:  Kata Hajime vs Henrik The King will surely be a ferocious battle; the two have had a storied passed, having met several times before.  Both men have held the XUW Submission title, as well as the TV title. BJ:  Also, Dave Kipfer will face off against X-Factor!  X-Factor has been on a role since returning to XUW, and now that he has HBK in his corner, he seems to be on um.... On even more of a role than he was before he had him in his corner....  Its gonna be a hell of a lot for the Boomer Sooner to overcome. AT:  Even so, you should never underestamate the determination, stamina and..... Talent, of Kipfer.  And then there's the main event, a huge non-title match between Sumarai Bob and Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy. BJ:  It should be great, since these guys havnt fought since last spring.  Since then, Sumarai Bob has become doubly mean, and Nobbledy, even more sadistic. AT:  No doubt about it, it should be ultra-competitive.  And what about the status of Raven X and his Hardcore title?  After the prank gone wrong last week, there's been alot of speculation as to whats going to happen with the belt.  No doubt we'll get some word on that situation tonight, so stay tuned. BJ:  But now, lets cut to the action; its time for our Telivision title match! Telivision Title match: Henrik The King vs Kata "Guild of Mute Assassins" by Clutch plays over the speakers as Kata steps through the curtains to a huge pop.  He wastes no time making his way to the ring, a look of burning determination on his face.... AT:  As stated, Kata and Henrik have a heated passed.  When Henrik led the Commonwealth, Kata was a member of the Four Horsemen, and the two groups were bitter enemies at the time.  Kata and Henrik, in particular, had some great battles, and this shouldnt be any different. BJ:  Kata reminds me of the guy from the Three Amigos, you know, the really quiet one. Lounge Act by Nirvana hits the speakers as Henrik The King comes through the curtains along with his very very new, very very hot, very very blonde friend, Bobbi Ellis.  The vicious "King" recieves a mixed reaction, obviously the cheers are directed towards the piece of eye candy accompanying Henrik to the ring.  The Hungry Mexican suddenly comes through the curtains and tries to grope Bobbi, but recieves a swift kick to the groin for his trouble.  Henrik then picks the Hungry Mexican up and turfs him off the stage, where, for some reason, there is a huge pile of four foot long steal spikes for The Mexican to land on.  As the Mexican falls to his bloody and painful death, Henrik and Bobbi proceed to make their way to the ring..... AT:  The King is, of course, escorted by his new lady friend, Bobbi Ellis. BJ:  WOO-HOO!  PUPPIES!  YES!  YES!  YES!  YES!  THE PUPPIES ARE PEAKIN' AT ME, JR!  THEY'RE PEAKIN' AT ME!  WOO-HOO! AT:  I swear to god, if you do that again, ever, I will kill you.  I will kill you, then track down your family, and kill them too.  Then I will track down all of your friends, if you even have any, and send them all death threats too. BJ:  Yeah, sorry, I probably did go a bit far, come to think of it. The match starts off with Henrik and Kata circling each other.  Kata takes a step towards Henrik, but Henrik takes a step back, and they continue circling each other.  Then, Henrik takes a step forward, and Kata takes a step back, and they continue circling each other.... AT:  These two are so evenly matched, and they know each others style so well. I dont think either man wants to make the first mistake. BJ:  Wow, really?  I thought they just just liked walking around in circles! Kata starts the match off with a running clothesline, which catches Henrik off gaurd.  Kata begins stomping on Henrik, then picks him up for a vertical suplex, but Henrik counters the move by flipping off of Katas shoulders and landing on his feet.  Henrik stuns Kata with a The Crown Jewels (a low blow), then hits him with a series of vicious lefts and rights, followed by an irish whip into the ropes, but Kata comes off with a picture perfect running lariat. With Henrik down, Kata wastes no time going straight to work on Henriks legs, putting him in a half crab.... AT:  Thats certainly not a good sign for Henrik, being put in such a painful hold this early in the match. BJ:  Wow, really?  I thought being put in a painful submission hold in the first few minutes of the match was a good thing! Henrik doesnt take long to make his way to the ropes, but the effects of the half crab are evident, as Henrik is already slightly favouring his left leg. Kata charges Henrik, but Henrik ducks, causing Kata to go charging into the ropes.  As Kata bounces back off, Henrik sends him out of the ring with a beautiful standing dropkick.  The crowd is already hot for this match... BJ:  Kata didnt hurt himself landing outside the ring, as he is back up, but slightly dazed. AT:  But trouble is headed his way! Henrik mounts the turnbuckle and comes off with an attempted top rope cross body block to the outside, but Kata intercepts with a superkick to the gut, causing Henrik to hit the ground coughing and weaving.... AT:  That must have been extremely painful.  Henrik may have broken a rib. Kata quickly throws Henrik back in the ring, then applies an abdominal stretch! BJ:  After the kick to the ribs, the abdominal stretch must be excruciating for Henrik! AT:  No doubt.  The match so far has been fairly even, but after making such a crucial error in judgement, Henrik is in big trouble. Kata really synches in on the abdominal stretch, but Henrik fights for the ropes.  The crowd are going wild with chants of "KATA!  KATA!  KATA!".... Henrik begins to fight his way out of the hold by using a series of elbows to the gut with his free arm, but Kata wont let go.  Henrik then tries a new strategy: using his free arm to repeatedly punch Kata in the face.  After about the second blow, Kata is forced to let go.... AJ:  During his time out from XUW, Henrik competed in various bare knuckle boxing tournements, pit fights and un-sanctioned wrestling matches throughout the world, and as a result, he has the most educated fists in XUW.  Kata had to let go of that hold, or succumb to Henriks deadly punches. BJ:  In his time away, Henrik actualy killed a man, inadvertantly of course, with his punches, and while he may be brave and noble and all, Im sure Kata doesnt want to be a hole in the ground just yet. With Kata dazed from Henriks punches, and Henrik in pain from the abdominal stretch, the two men begin to circle each other again.  This time, Henrik is the aggressor, nailing Kata with a spear, then a series of left and rights.  Henrik then lifts Kata up and whips him to the ropes, and hits him with a knee to the gut.  He picks Kata up, and drills him with a snap suplex.... BJ:  Henrik is starting to gain the advantage now, and Kata will be in real trouble if Henrik can follow up. Henrik picks Kata up off the ground and tries to place him in The Stratford Strangle (Million Dollar Dream), but Kata counters it by hitting a low blow, then drilling Henrik with The Abyss! AT:  This could be all! Kata goes for the cover... 1... 2... KICKOUT!!! BJ:  NO! Kata, looking slightly frustrated, picks Henrik up and throws him into the turnbuckle, then hits him with a series of knife hand chops.  The crowd "whoos" along with Kata, and Henriks chest is left a shade of bright red.  Henrik retaliates with a series of chops of his own, then an irish whip followed by a backdrop.  Bobbi Ellis is on the apron, arguing with the referee while Henrik chokes Kata on the mat.  When the referee shrugs her off and turns back around to pay attention to the match, Bobbi buys Henrik more time by grabbing the ref's behind.... BJ:  Why the hell is it that a balding idiot in a black and white suit can get action, and for some reason I cant! AT:  I know.  Being a referee must be the best job in the world. When Henrik notices what Bobbi is doing, he stops what he's doing to scold her, telling her to stay out of the match.  Kata takes the opportunity to roll-up Henrik..... 1... 2... KICKOUT!!! AT:  Henrik is not to be denied! Kata charges Henrik and tries to hit a running lariat, but Henrik ducks and Kata hits the mat hard.  Henrik picks Kata up and hits a piledriver, then runs over to the corners and climbs mounts the turnbuckles.... BJ:  If Henrik hits the Kingpin, it's OVER! Henrik saws off the top rope and nails Kata with the Kingpin, as the referee makes the count.... 1... 2... 3!!! Winner by pinfall and still Telivision champion: Henrik The King AT:  What an incredible match between two very equally equiped atheletes. Henrik The King is simply on a role, and has yet to be cleanly defeated since coming back. BJ:  The Kings Reign of Pain continues, and dont look for YOUR King to drop the title any time soon.  That belts not going anywhere for a long time. AT:  Kata may have been unlucky tonight, but at the pay per view, he will be teaming up with Beat Down Authority, Esco and Sakie, to face Bonecrusher, Azreal and Kore, along with Admiral John. BJ:  The match, announces on Xposure last night, has a lot of history, and there'll no doubt be alot of emotions running at Unknown Factors. AJ:  And now for singles competition between two hardcore warriors: NFM and DDT! DDT vs NFM "Dead Souls" by Nine Inch Nails plays through the speakers as DDT makes his way to the ring to a reception of jeers from the crowd.  The Hardcore Icon is indifferent to the crowd as he makes his way down to the ring.... AT:  When asked which fellow XUW superstar they fear most, wrestlers almost always answer with "DDT", and  thats because he is one of the fiercest men in wrestling.  We are looking at the one true Hardcore Icon. BJ:  Yeah, he's a tough guy, too. "Fear" by Disturbed hits the speakers as NFM makes his way out to a good pop. He wastes no time running to the ring to attack DDT, but as soon as he slides under the ropes, the Hardcore Icon begins putting the boots to him. BJ:  DDT is ultra-aggressive.  You need to keep your eyes on the man at all times. DDT picks NFM up and hits him with a belly to belly suplex.  He then picks him up and goes to work on him with a series of right hands.  NFM comes back with a round of heymakers, then sends DDT to the ropes, but DDT hits him with a running Big Boot.  DDT picks NFM up and attempts a powerslam, but NFM reverses it with a hurricanrana. AT:  NFM is truly fearless!  He is not intimidated by anyone in XUW, including the Hardcore Icon! BJ:  Everyone's scared of DDT, whether they admitt to it or not.  It is fear of his opponent that's fueling NFM right now! NFM mounts DDT and goes to work on him with a series of lefts and rights.  He then attempts to pick the big man up, but DDT reverses it by throwing him forcefully into the ropes, then hitting him with a running clotheline.  DDT picks NFM up, but NFM quickly hits him with a series of chops.  He then attempts a running body press, but DDT catches him in mid air, and then, in an incredible feet of strength, lifts him up and  throws him literally out of the ring, and into the steal gaurdrail! AT:  My god!  I dont see how NFM can possibly win it from here! BJ:  It looks like things are about to get a whole helluva lot worse for NFM, because here comes the Hardcore Icon! DDT climbs out the ring and picks up NFM, then throws him against the gaurdrails.  He then picks him up and attempts to whip him into the ring post, but somehow it is reversed by NFM, and DDT goes crashing in to the steal post.... AT:  I honestly thought the match was over right there!  How in the hell is NFM capable of coming back after taking such a vicious bump? BJ:  You'd be surprised what fear can do.  NFM is so terrified of what DDT can do to him, that his body wont even allow itself to rest! AT:  .........  Right........ NFM walks groggily over to DDT and stomps on his prone body a few times, before picking him up and throwing him back into the ring.  NFM then climbs up to the top rope and attempts a leg drop, but DDT moves out the way. BJ:  Had NFM hit that, it could of been over. AT:  But that kind of mistake can be deadly, especialy when you're in the ring with someone like DDT.  It could be over for NFM now! DDT gets to his feet and proceeds to stomp NFM, then picks him up and whips him into the corner.  The Hardcore Icon attempts a running clotheline, but NFM counters with a boot to the gut, sending DDT to his knees.  NFM climbs the top rope, but DDT was playing possum!  He quickly springs to his feet and pushes NFM off the top rope, sending him crashing, once again, into the steal barricades! BJ:  HOLY s***! AT:  Surely, there is no chance of NFM coming back and winning from here.  He has been thrown from ten feet in the air into a steal barricade, not once but twice! AT:  JESUS!  How the hell does NFM keep coming back, time after time? BJ:  NFM is like the antithesis of Hungry Mexican: the bastard just wont die!!! NFM gets off the table and picks DDT up, then drills him with a knee to the gut.  NFM attemps a piledriver onto the concrete, but DDT manages to reverse it into a backdrop, causing NFM to land spine first on the concrete.  DDT is too sore and tired to follow up, and he falls to the ground right after hitting the move.... AT:  That was pure desperation on the part of DDT!  It's anyones match now! All of a sudden, and without much warning, Ric Austin comes out to the ring to a recpeption of boos from the crowd.  He ascends to the top turnbuckle, and then comes flying off with a HUGE splash to the outside, nailing both NFM and DDT! AT:  That was one of the coolest moves I have ever seen!  On the other hand, Austin has no business being out there!  Damn him!  Damn him to hell! Austin proceeds to pummel DDT, and then NFM.  Then he picks up a chair, and hammers away on both men untill a group of about five officials hold him back. He drops the chair, taunts the furious crowd, and walks back to the locker rooms.  The referee rules the bout a no-contest... Winner:  No Conest BJ:  Austin has just proven himself to be an unstoppable force, taking out two cornerstones of the XUW hardcore devision! AT:  Bull!  That was nothing more than a display of cowardice, done by a coward for cowardly reasons!  The cowardish behavior of Ric Austin is absolutely cowardiddleyesh!  But that aside, it is now time for what is probably the hottest match of the card, except, not surprisingly, the main event.  I am talking, of course about Dave Kipfer vs Sumarai Bob. BJ:  Its Dave Kipfer vs X-Factor.  Samurai Bob is in the main event, against The Ripper. AT:  What??? BJ:   Its Dave Kipfer vs X-Factor.  Samurai Bob is in the main event, against The Ripper. AT:  Yeah, no kidding, Einstein.  Anyways, without further adu, lets cut straight to the action! Dave Kipfer vs X-Factor "One of A Kind" by Point Break hits the speakers as the one and only Dave Kipfer emerges from the dressing room with a genuinely happy expression on his face. The crowd goes wild upon seeing him, and with every step he takes the crowd goes into more and more of  a frenzy.... AT:  There is no other athelete in XUW that captures the fans hearts quite like this guy does.  Hell, there is nobody else in XUW like Dave Kipfer.  The guy is phenominal. Aint no way Kipfers gonna overcome the X-Factor. AT:  That remains to to be seen. X-Factor walks around the ring area to catch his breath, as the referee counts.  After the referee makes it to about 7, Kipfer quickly darts over to the turnbuckles, climbs to the top rope and hits X-Factor with a stunning flying body press.  The fans are estatic as Kipfer hurls X-Factor back into the ring.... AT:  I dont care what you say, Kipfer is taking it to X-Factor! BJ:  But for how long? As Kipfer slides under the bottom rope to get into the ring, X-Factor nails him with a series of stomps, much to the crowds disgust.  Factor quickly picks Dave up and whips him into the ropes, hitting him with a jumping roundhouse kick.  He then drills him with a snap suplex, and ascends to the top rope. BJ:  What could he be going for, do you think? AT:  I dont know. BJ:  Well, I dont know either, but one thing is for sure: Kipfers certainly taking it to the X-Factor right now!  Ha, he's really making him his bitch, isnt he?  Gee, X-Factors getting the crap pounded out of him!  Ha!  Ha!  Ha ha! Factor goes for The Bliss (top rope Swanton Bomb) but it is ducked by Kipfer, who immediately gets to his feet and picks Factor up, hitting him with the The Showstopper!  He makes the quick cover.... 1... 2... POWERFUL KICK-OUT BY X-FACTOR!!! AT:  Both men waisting no time going for their trademark maneuvers, and taking advantage of every available oppurtunity to finish the other off quickly.
