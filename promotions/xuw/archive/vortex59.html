XUW Vortex #59

27 Mar 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Scene opens up outside of the DeSantes arena, with The New Royal Legion; Bailey, Danyon, and Larry picketing the entrance of the arena.  There is a small crowd gathered with them, who are all wearing NRL shirts and giving the New Royal Legion some encouragement.  Bailey has a megaphone in his hands while Danyon and Larry hold up signs, as the group of people walk around in a circle in front of the entranceway, some holding signs, some just chanting some non-sense… I mean, how much sense can they really make… they are fans of the New Royal Legion.  As the protesters march around, Bailey, Danyon, and Larry can be seen looking over the group while talking amongst themselves. Bailey:  How in the hell could Mr. Webster be making us fight tonight?  Doesn’t he know that we are on strike?  I mean really, what’s this all abooooot? Larry:  I don’t know, Bailey… I just don’t know what that man is thinking.  I mean, we are nearly union now!  So he won’t be able to do this for long! Danyon:  Why don’t we just boycott the match… it’s not like we care to see how Hodgo makes out. Larry:  Can’t do that, though, because then Mr. Webster will suspend you two without pay. Bailey:  Damn that Webster… Just then the picket line starts to move away in the middle as the Hardcore Icons begin to walk to the entrance and scare some of the protesters, who just step aside and let them pass.  Bailey and Danyon, however, don’t share the same feelings and just stand in front of the doorway to confront the Hardcore Icons as they walk through the crowd, sneering at the Legionnaires they pass, until they come face to face with The New Royal Legion. Bailey:  You aren’t thinking about going in there are you? DDT:  Out of the way small fry… there are some asses to be kicked and you’re standing in my way. Danyon:  You shouldn’t go in there with the horrible work conditions that are taking place in there… strike with us. Raven X:  Step aside boys, or we’ll have to start with you. Bailey and Danyon step to the side, all the while staring at Raven X and DDT as they walk into the doorway.  Bailey and Danyon begin making ‘Scab’ remarks at the two of them, until the whole crowd of Legionnaires starts up a ‘Scab’ chant, which seems to have no effect on The Hardcore Icons, as they walk in laughing, even further upsetting The New Royal Legion members. BOOM BOOM BANG CRASH THWAP BOOM BOOM…. And other random noises can be heard, just barely though, over the screaming, filled to the brim, capacity crowd!  After the pyro finishes it’s explosions and pretty colors the cameras then obligatorily (Nice big word there) scan the audience, randomly stopping on signs such as: “Rujasu Doesn’t Suck!” “POOP!” “I LOVE NFM!!!”  Sign is held by someone wearing the new big gay foam cowboy hat on sale at XUW Paraphernalia or XUW merchandise stands for only $14.95! and, “I’m the father!! I thought I told you last week!” The camera’s then go over to our announce table with some new, yet familiar faces, as we see Mikey and Lauwass sitting at the booth. Mikey:  Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to another exciting edition of XUW Thursday Night Vortex on Fox!  I’m your announcer and with me tonight is non other than the annoyingly great, Lauwass. Lauwass:  Hey, I thought I told you to just say “great”! Mikey:  Just a little improv, don’t mind me.  Anyway, we have a hell of a card lined up for you tonight folks, which features an incredibly huge main event. Lauwass:  That’s right as we get to watch The New Royal Legion and Hodgo wipe the floor with Dave Kipfer, Dante, and the Has Been Kid. Mikey:  Has been IC champ?  Has been commissioner?  Has been a great athlete for as long as I can remember? Lauwass:  No, I meant has been in a bad way, you fool. Mikey:  I know, but it’s just so easy to shoot down your pathetic insults, I just couldn’t resist. Lauwass:  You <censored>. Mikey:  Okay then, moving right along… Also on the card tonight we have the great Samurai Bob with the lovely Naron by his side, set to take on NFM in a match which has certainly seemed to raise some tempers between the two competitors. Lauwass:  I can’t wait to watch NFM shut that old fart up.  However even better news, is the chance to watch Rujasu get his ass kicked once more! Mikey:  Well, he does have a match with DDT for the Submission Title, but I wouldn’t be so quick to say that he is going to be getting an ass kicking… Rujasu is a great talent here and has every shot in the world to win that belt and prove all the naysayers wrong. Lauwass:  Yeah, how much did he pay you to say that? Mikey:  What’s this, I’m getting word that we are about to go backstage where Kata and Dev are doing a little bit of training before Kata comes out here to take on Raven X. The scene goes backstage just like Mikey had said, to a room set up like a Japanese dojo.  The two are sparring off with each other, trying out different submission moves on each other and such in a nice display of athleticism from each man.  Dev then manages to lock Kata into a half crab, completely stretching out the muscles in Kata’s leg.  A huge amount of pain can be seen on the face of Kata. Kata:  As his face begins to turn red. Okay, you can let go of the hold now. Dev, however, seems to be lost in another world and just pulls down harder and harder, causing Kata to actually groan in pain for a second, until *POP*… something goes terribly wrong and Kata yells out once again.  Dev then realizes what he did and lets go of the hold.  He stands back up to his feet and turns around to see Kata lying on the ground holding on to his leg.  The camera then goes back to the announce position as Dev looks down with his arms crossed and a smile on his face. Mikey:  That didn’t look too good for Kata right there. Lauwass:  Well, they were only practicing… it’s his own fault he got hurt. Mikey:  No way in hell… Dev just went too far. Lauwass:  Does this mean that Kata isn’t coming out tonight? Mikey:  Well, his match is next, so we are about to find out… Singles Match Raven X Vs. Kata “Liberate” by SlipKnot rips through the PA system and the fans let out a huge chorus of boos as Raven X steps out from behind the curtain.  He stops at the top of the stage and folds his arms while looking out at the crowd with a sick smile on his face, before walking down to the ring.  “Naked Sunday” by Stone Temple Pilots then hits and the crowd starts to cheer, but there is no Kata at first.  After a few seconds, Kata steps from behind the curtain and limps his way down to the ring, favoring his hurt leg. Mikey:  Well, Kata looks to be in a little bit of pain, but that’s never stopped him before.  Just like I had thought, he’s coming out here no matter what. Lauwass:  Why couldn’t he have just stayed backstage? Kata slides into the ring, but before the bell can even be rung, RX runs over to him and starts kicking him while he is down.  RX then lifts Kata to his feet as the ref signals for the bell.  RX whips Kata to the ropes and Kata bounces off the ropes, still with a limp, and gets taken down by a drop toehold from RX, which he turns into a leg lock, keeping Kata grounded.  Kata yells out in pain, as RX continues to attack his injured leg. Mikey:  Raven X is wasting no time attacking that injured leg of Kata. Lauwass:  He is a former Submission champion.  Raven X is no stranger to attacking someone’s weakness. Back inside the ring, Kata slowly managed to pull himself to the ropes and grab a hold, causing Raven X to break the hold.  RX then lifts Kata to his feet once again and tosses him over his shoulders with an overhead belly-to-belly suplex.  The crowd then starts to boo as RX lays a few more kicks into Kata while he is down.  RX then grabs a hold of the injured leg and drops and elbow on it.  He gets right back up to his feet, and repeats the move once again, but this time he holds on and stretches Kata’s leg out with his arms.  Kata yells out in pain once again, but happened to be very close to the ropes.  RX holds on until a four count, inflicting as much pain as he possibly can, before letting go.  RX then lifts Kata to his feet once again, and grabs a hold of the injured leg of Kata, causing him to hop on one foot.  RX begins to smile and say something to Kata, that can’t be heard, but before RX can finish his sentence Kata jumps into the air and plants a kick right into the side of RX’s head with his good leg.  RX drops to the canvas like a sack of rocks holding the side of his head, as Kata pulls himself back up to his feet. Mikey:  Raven got a little cocky there, and Kata made a great move to stop the onslaught on his leg.  Let’s see if he can keep control now. Lauwass:  I’m sure he can’t… Kata waits as RX gets back to his feet, and as soon as he does, Kata whips RX into the corner, with such force that it seems to move the ring a couple of inches in the direction he hit.  Kata then makes his way to the corner and sucks up the pain for a minute as he unleashes a flurry of martial arts style kicks into Raven X.  For the last kick, Kata uses the bottom rope to spring himself up a bit to land another kick to the side of Raven X’s head, sending him crashing back down to the matt.  Kata then climbs up onto the second rope and jumps off, landing an elbow across the neck of RX.  Kata then hooks one of his legs, going for the quick pin… One… Two… Kickout! Kata wastes no time getting back to his feet… well, as quickly as he can with a leg that’s bothering him, and lifts RX up to his feet as well.  Kata sets RX up, and then nails him with the Inferno Suplex (Wrist Clutch Exploder), dropping RX hard on his head.  Kata then hooks a leg once again… One… Two… Kickout! Mikey:  Close fall there, but Kata still regains control of the match, even with an injured leg… now that is impressive! Lauwass:  You really think so? Mikey:  Well… yeah. Kata looks a little upset, but still gets right up to his feet anyway.  He turns to the ref and holds up three fingers, but the ref just shakes his head and holds up two fingers.  When Kata isn’t looking, however, RX comes up from behind him and takes out Kata’s bad leg with his shoulder, sending Kata down to the mat.  RX then grabs Kata’s bad leg and lifts it up in the air.  He hits the injured leg with a couple of well-placed kicks before once again dropping an elbow and pulling up on the leg.  RX doesn’t hold onto it for too long, however, and lifts Kata up to his feet.  RX then whips Kata to the ropes and after he bounces off, RX grabs a hold of Kata and slams him hard down to the mat with a HUGE Big Bang Theory (Spinning Spine Buster).  RX then quickly lifts Kata up to his feet and nails him with a kick to the gut, followed by The Family Values (Pedigree), planting Kata’s face into the mat.  RX then goes down and hooks one of his legs as the ref slides in… One… Two… Three!!! Winner via pin fall… Raven X!!! “Liberate” hits once again and the ref goes to raise Raven X’s hand as the crowd boos, but RX just pulls his hand away from the ref and raises his own hands before exiting the ring. Mikey:  Well, Kata put up a great fight, even with a huge disadvantage going into this match. Lauwass:  Whatever… Raven X won, and that’s all that matters. Mikey:  Maybe to narrow minded people like you, but he showed great resiliency in there tonight. Camera cuts backstage, where Mister Webster is talking with a Dumb Blonde Assistant. Webster:  As much as he’s been annoying me, we’re going to have to give Ric Austin a contract. Dumb Blonde Assistant:  Who? Webster:  Ric Austin. He’s a wrestler. I figure he can do less harm when he’s subject to my rules again. DBA:  Okay. Webster:  He should arrive soon, get him to sign this contract, and tell him he’s going to have a match this weekend. DBA:  Okay. Webster leaves the room, off to run the federation and attend to important matters, such as dealing with the alarmingly low stocks of Funyuns in the XUW vending machines. The dumb blonde assistant sits there, twirling her hair and looking attractive, until a man wanders into the room. He isn’t too tall for a wrestler, if indeed that is what he is, but he is dressed in the strangest manner. He wears multicoloured jester’s trousers, and a huge black shirt, clearly about ten sizes too big for him. He carries in his hand an action figure, seemingly of himself, and he has an air of bemusement about him. DBA:  You must be Ric Austin. Man:  Who? DBA:  That’s what I said. You’ve got to sign this contract. Man:  Why? DBA:  Webster wants to let you wrestle. The man looks down at his action figure. Man:  That’s why I was interested in those wrestling shows! I’m a wrestler! The action figure dances in his hands. Man:  Yes Mister Action Figure, it’s “Ric Austin”! DBA:  So, are you going to sign the contract? I’m, like, all busy and stuff. Man:  Of course, please hand me a pen. The dumb blonde looks for a pen, as the man talks to his action figure. Man:  Isn’t this exciting Mister Action Figure? I’m going to be a wrestler again! Nick Austin! I wonder what my gimmick is? DBA:  Here’s your pen. The man signs the contract with a flourish. DBA:  That says “Nick Austin”. Man:  Yes. DBA:  Your name is “Ric Austin”. Webster said so. Man:  It is? DBA:  Yeah. Shouldn’t you know that? Man:  I guess so. DBA:  I have another copy for you to sign. Webster always gives me three copies of everything, in case I make a mistake or something. The man has another shot at signing, and gets it right this time. He smiles at the assistant and walks off. Man:  “Ric Austin, eh? Time for me to … to … ermmm …. Remember how to wrestle.” The cameras then go back to the announce position where the announcers look a little surprised at what they just saw. Lauwass:  Who the hell was that, and why was he pretending to be Ric Austin? Mikey:  I don’t know, but it sure seemed as though he thought that’s who he was… sheesh, what people will do to get a contract here. Lauwass:  It’s sickening… it really is.  Ric Austin deserves that contract, not some fool! At this point a ring hand walks up to Mikey with a note and hands it to him. Lauwass:  Hey, what do you have there? Mikey:  I just got this in… a new match has been added to the card for the Power and Prestige PPV that is set for Sunday, April 6, in the land of the rising sun, Japan.  And that match is Dante defending his hardcore title, against none other than DarkSeid! Lauwass:  Ah, now that’s a match I can’t wait to see.  I love watching Dante get his head crushed in with steel chair shots from DarkSeid. Mikey:  Dante is the champion, in case you forgot… it may just be DarkSeid that gets his head smashed in this time. Lauwass:  Yeah… heh, good one. Mikey:  Well, up next is our Submission Title Match, where Rujasu has a chance to finally prove to the world that he can hold a title. Lauwass:  I need some popcorn; this should be a fun one to watch. XUW Submission Championship Match Rujasu Vs. DDT © The title music to the game “Chrono Trigger” hits and Rujasu steps out from behind the curtain to a very nice crowd reaction… I guess now that he has entrance music they know to cheer for him when he comes out.  He makes his way down to the ramp slapping hands with a few of the fans along the way, before sliding into the ring.  “Only” by Anthrax then rips through the speakers and the crowd starts up a loud chorus of boos as DDT walks from behind the curtain with the Submission Title around his waist.  He doesn’t waste any time at the top of the ramp, and just makes his way down to the ring, sneering at some of the fans as he passes them.  DDT then slides into the ring and hands his belt over to the ref, before looking at Rujasu and laughing.  The ref shows the belt to Rujasu who looks it over, and then holds it high for all to see.  The ref then hands the belt over to a stagehand and signals for the bell. Rujasu gets set to circle around DDT, but DDT just crosses his arms and laughs as if he isn’t taking the match very seriously.  This gets Rujasu a little upset, so he runs at DDT and locks up with him in the center of the ring.  Rujasu surprisingly gets the advantage with a headlock, but DDT wastes no time pushing him off to the ropes.  Rujasu bounces off the ropes and DDT takes him down with a shoulder block.  Rujasu, however, springs right back up to his feet and DDT whips him to the ropes once again.  Rujasu bounces off the ropes, and gets knocked down with a second shoulder block.  He hops up to his feet once again, but this time he doesn’t get whipped to the ropes, instead he receives a vertical suplex from DDT.  DDT then lifts Rujasu up to his feet and nails him across the back with a couple of hard forearm shots until Rujasu falls back down to the mat.  DDT then stands over Rujasu, before locking him in the Dark Eternal (Full Nelson Camel Clutch).  The ref checks on Rujasu, but he refuses to quit.  Rujasu then slowly pulls himself to the ropes and manages to grab a hold of the bottom rope, causing DDT to break the hold. Mikey:  DDT went for the early win there, but Rujasu showing that he just won’t quit that easily. Lauwass:  He should have just given up then… would have saved himself a lot of pain. DDT steps back and lets Rujasu get to his feet.  Once he does, Rujasu turns around to face DDT and gets met with a kick to the gut, followed by a quick DDT.  DDT, the wrestler, gets back up to his feet quickly and lays some kicks into Rujasu as he tries to get to his feet.  Rujasu makes his way to the corner, and starts to pull himself up as DDT continues to kick him.  Rujasu finally gets all the way to his feet, and DDT lands a couple of punches into his head, to make Rujasu a little wobbly.  DDT then picks Rujasu up and puts him up on the top rope.  DDT climbs up and grabs a hold of Rujasu for a super-plex, but Rujasu manages to get a couple of shots into DDT’s side, before pushing him down to the mat.  Rujasu then steadies himself on the top rope and jumps off with a picture perfect High Score (Shooting Star Press) that he lands directly on target, and the crowd starts to get behind him.  Rujasu then lifts DDT to his feet quickly and whips him to the ropes.  DDT bounces off and runs right into a standing dropkick from Rujasu that practically takes his head off. Mikey:  Rujasu finally got into the swing of things here, Lauwass… now what was it you were saying about he should have given up already? Lauwass:  I’m sticking to that story… just wait and see. Rujasu then quickly grabs DDT’s legs and locks him in a Figure Four Leg-Lock right in the center of the ring!  The crowd starts to go crazy as DDT flops around the mat in pain while Rujasu keeps applying more and more pressure.  The ref checks on DDT, but he refuses to give in.  DDT sees that the ropes are too far away to grab, so he instead begins to try and turn the hold over, however Rujasu doesn’t allow it on his first attempt, which only makes the crowd cheer louder.  DDT then tries a second time, and succeeds in reversing the hold and causing Rujasu to be the one in pain now.  The cheers quickly turn into boos, as the ref now checks on Rujasu, who still refuses to quit.  The crowd then starts a chant up for Rujasu, and he manages to summon the strength to spin DDT over once again, and reapply the Leg-Lock! Mikey:  Amazing!  Rujasu is really showing something tonight… he just will not quit, I bet DDT was not expecting this! Lauwass:  Yeah, but all the rolling around is just getting them closer to the ropes. Lauwass was very correct in what he was saying, and DDT reaches out to the ropes, but is just a little too far and can’t reach them.  The ref continues to check on DDT, who adamantly refuses to tap.  DDT then once again manages to roll over, and reverse the hold for a second time!  Only, Rujasu was placed right next to the ropes, and grabs a hold of them real quick.  DDT refuses to let go of the hold, of course, until the ref counts to four.  DDT then quickly gets to his feet and begins to stomp the downed Rujasu, which sends him rolling out of the ring and down to the floor below.  DDT then follows him out and the ref has no choice but to begin counting.  DDT picks Rujasu up and whips him into the steel ring post, sending him down hard to the protective mats on the outside of the ring. One… Two… DDT kicks Rujasu again a couple of times. Three… Four… DDT picks Rujasu up and whips him into the barrier that surrounds the ring, causing him to crash into it with a LOUD thud. Five… DDT makes his way over to Rujasu and bends down to pick him up when all the fans rush forward for the chance to touch one of the superstars. Six… As the fans rush forward, the person in the front row gets knocked forward and accidentally hits DDT in the head with his sign, and DDT looks up at the fan with a pissed off look. Seven… DDT then sees that the fan is wearing a Legionnaires shirt and the sign he was hit with is about the strike, which only furthers the anger inside and DDT begins yelling at the fan. Eight… DDT stops paying attention as Rujasu manages to sneak away while DDT yells at the fan. Nine… Rujasu rolls into the ring and breaks the count at the last second! Mikey:  That was a close one there, they almost got a double count out and no one wanted to see that! Lauwass:  I would have been fine with it… Rujasu slowly pulls himself to his feet as DDT turns his attention from the fan and slides back into the ring.  DDT quickly makes his way over to Rujasu as he is still getting to his feet.  DDT gives Rujasu a quick kick to the guy and follows it up with The Shock Treatment (Quick DDT).  The crowd starts to boo as DDT gets back to his feet and turns to the fan that hit him with the sign (accidentally, of course) and begins to mock him.  The fan yells back some obscenities, which just gets a laugh from DDT.  DDT then turns around and sees that Rujasu is already on his feet waiting for DDT.  Rujasu gives DDT a quick kick to the gut, this time, and then nails him with a vicious r-Driver (Vertabreaker)!  Rujasu then quickly grabs DDT’s legs and locks him in the Philly Cloverleaf (Texas) and pushes down hard as the fans get behind him!  DDT struggles and screams out in pain, but is way, way too far from the ropes.  He slowly starts to pull himself towards the ropes, though, and manages to get within inches, when Rujasu pulls him back to the center of the ring and pushes down even harder on the hold until DDT has no choice………. But to tap!!!!!!! Winner via submission and NEW XUW Submission Champion… Rujasu!!! Mikey:  He did it!  Rujasu pulled off the upset!! Lauwass:  Oh that’s bull… that fan interfered in the match!  I think that decision should be reversed! Mikey:  DDT didn’t have to mock the fan, especially since it was an accident! Lauwass:  Oh yeah, I’m sure a Legionnaire fan did that by accident. The crowd goes crazy as Rujasu’s music hits once again and he grabs his newly won belt and quickly rolls out of the ring.  He stops on the stage holding his belt high with one hand, while holding the back of his head with the other.  DDT doesn’t even bother arguing with the ref, instead he just rolls out of the ring and yells a couple things at the fan, before making his way to the back also. The camera cuts to a section of the guard railing at ringside, and someone is seen hopping over the railing. This man, who is wearing a large brown robe and a Jason mask, slides into the ring and starts pacing back and forth. Mikey:  Hey, that looks like the guy who attacked LKK and Blake Wolfson this past Mayhem. The masked man pulls out a mic from his robe and begins to speak. His voice is distorted. Masked Man:  Well, it seems that the past few days, there has been a mystery of sorts. Just who would come out and attack both LKK and Blake Wolfson? The answer is quite simple really. That person would be the type of guy who doesn’t give a damn who he attacks. It was enjoyable attacking LKK, a foolish fan favorite, and it was just as enjoyable, if not more enjoyable, seeing the look on Blake Wolfson’s face when he thought I was on his side. The masked man reaches up for his mask as the fans look on in silent anticipation. Masked Man:  If you fools thought things were getting brighter for XUW, think again. Mayhem was just the beginning of what lies ahead for this company. <starts to pull off the ‘Jason’ mask>  It’s time to make my presence known in XUW....because I’ve grown tired of the competition elsewhere. Lauwass:  Oh s**t, it’s really him! Mikey:  I can’t make it out. Who is that? The Jason mask comes off and the boos start raining down in the arena. Underneath the mask, is another mask, a very familiar mask that has been on XUW T.V. a couple of times before. The Shadow:  Who did you people think I was, Dave Kipfer? By coming out and performing a signature move that just happened to be one of his, you ignorant people and even his stable mates were deceived. You people are fickle, and disgust me to no end! I think I will enjoy causing each and every one of you to be as unhappy as possible, now that! I’m in XUW. The Shadow starts laughing evilly as his music starts up and The Shadow exits the ring and walks to the back. Mikey:  Who is this guy again? Is he just another rookie? Lauwass:  Hell no! This is The Shadow, from the XUDF! I thought he had a match on Saturday against Joey Vendetti though for the NA title. Mikey:  I wonder what’s going to be done about that? The two announcers shrug their shoulders.  The scene then cuts to the parking lot where a furious Ric Austin is trying to get into the building, but security guards and police officers are restraining him from coming into the arena.  The scene then goes back to the announce position. Mikey:  I guess Ric Austin also saw what took place earlier today and doesn’t look too happy about it. Lauwass:  Would you be?  They won’t even let him in the building to resolve this issue! Mikey:  Well, yeah, they don’t want him coming in causing trouble now when he’s angry like that.  Enough about him, though, we have our next match starting up. Singles Match No Fear Man Vs. Samurai Bob w/ Naron “Fear” by Disturbed hits on the PA system and NFM comes out to nothing but boos from the crowd directed towards the man that had such harsh words for their hero.  He pays no attention to them and just makes his way down to the ring with a serious look on his face.  “F*ck That!” then rips through the PA system and Bob steps out onto the stage to a MASSIVE pop from the crowd.  Naron steps out right behind him, and the unthinkable happens… the pop gets even louder!  The two pose on the stage for a moment before making their way down to the ring.  Naron takes her place outside the ring and Bob slides in and he and NFM just stare each other down.  The ref then signals for the bell, as the two just continue to stare at each other. The stare down then stops and the two circle each other in the center of the ring, before locking up.  Bob gets the advantage with a headlock, and then spins around behind NFM, pulling his legs out from under him, tripping him up.  Bob then takes a step back and looks down at NFM with a smile on his face, as NFM gets back up to his feet, with a look on his face like he isn’t very amused.  The two circle each other again, and go to lock up, but this time NFM lifts a knee up into Bob’s midsection.  NFM then hits Bob in the back with a couple of stiff forearm shots before hitting him with a snap suplex.  Both men get right up to their feet and NFM grabs Bob’s arm and attempts to whip him to the ropes, but Bob counters and instead sends NFM on his way.  NFM bounces off the ropes and run right into a Steiner line from Bob, that sends him to the mat.  Bob then picks NFM up by his hair and sends him back down with a snapp suplex.  Bob holds onto NFM, though and lifts him back up, hitting him with a second one, and then repeats it for a third, before rolling over and making the cover… One… Two… Kickout! Lauwass:  Ha!  Bob should have scouted his opponent better than that.  NFM can’t be pinned that easy. Mikey:  I’m sure he knew that, but there is nothing wrong with taking any attempt to pin your opponent. Bob gets back up to his feet, and lifts NFM up with him.  Bob then sets NFM up for another snapp suplex, but NFM manages to block it with his leg, and hit a snap suplex of his own on Bob.  NFM then gets to his feet and lays some kicks on Bob before lifting Bob up to his feet.  NFM then whips Bob to the ropes, and once Bob bounces off the ropes NFM nails him with a clothesline.  Bob then gets back up to his feet and NFM gives him a kick to the gut, followed by an impressive powerbomb.  NFM then makes a quick cover… One… Two… Shoulder up!
