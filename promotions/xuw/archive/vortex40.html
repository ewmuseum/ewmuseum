XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #40

24 Oct 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex The show opens as the Ultra Violents, Henrik the King, Hodgo the Anarchist, and Blake Wolfson walk through a corridor looking menacing. Some boos can be heard in the background. AT: "There they are, the Ultra Violents... they promised to make a huge impact tonight... what will happen?" Then, the opening video for Vortex plays, followed by a huge explosion of pyro throughout the arena, which shoots into some of the upper deck seats, setting some unfortunate fans aflame. Signs are seen throughout the arena, reading such things as "Who'll get the call-up? hardkore wil, btich!", "I attacked Raven X, I did it. What of it?", a big picture of the new X-Box videogame "xUw Mayhem 2", and "MOVE BITCH! GET OUT THE WAY!" Then, the camera shows the five-star announce team of the xUw. AT: "Welcome everyone... to another action packed edition of xUw Vortex! I'm the Atomic Tomato, and with me as always, Mr. Majestic... Barry Johnson!" BJ: "It's the man that you love to hate! What a show we got tonight, as the Ultra Violents are here to make a huge impact... right in the ass of every AoD member!" AT: "What about that big InterContinental title match, as the challenger, representing the New Age Horsemen, BoneCrusher... takes on the Champion, from the Boomer Sooners, Saij!" BJ: "Saij, another target of the Ultra Violents! Oh! What about this? The battle of Immediate Impact, as KoRe takes on Cereal Killer!" AT: "But first, let's get to ringside for our first match up, this one for the xUw TV title!" Dev  vs.  Numark © xUw World Television Championship "Build Fear" by Soilent Green plays, and the crowd boos as the main lights dim, the F.E.A.R. logo comes on the UltiTron screen, soon to be replaced by the kanji for anguish. Dev makes his way to the ring admists some decent crowd heat, wearing a Explicit Violence t-shirt, the one with the F.E.A.R. logo on the right sleeve. He gets in the ring, non-chalantly, and awaits his opponent as the lights come back up. Then, "Clean my Wounds" by Corrosion of Conformity plays, and the crowd pops huge for the defending Television Champion. Numark gets in the ring, and plays to the crowd a bit, who responds with cheers. AT: "They love Numark here, the Usual Suspect who defends his Television title against the lone member of F.E.A.R." BJ: "I'm not big on the Suspects Kumquat, but Dev needs to realize that... F.E.A.R. is D.E.A.D." AT: "Well, whether you loved or hated F.E.A.R., Dev stands by his beliefs, and he still belives in the sadistic vision that Blitzwing had." Dev removes his shirt, while Numark surrenders his title belt to the referee who hands it to a ringside attendant, and calls for the opening bell. Dev stretches out a bit, before the two men circle each other. They begin with a Collar-and-Elbow Tieup. The Champ comes out of it with a Side Headlock. He spins around and gets behind Dev, then takes him down with a Double Legtrip. He then gets a Waistlock on, which Dev quickly does a sitout and escape, followed by a hard Forearm Smash over the back of the head of Numark. Dev then gets on a Front Facelock, and goes for a Vertical Suplex, from which Numark escapes, landing behind the Fighter. He then executes a beautiful German Suplex into a bridge... 1... 2... kick out by Dev. AT: "Nice mat wrestling by these two great competitors in the early going." BJ: "Ah, comon... where's the carnage?" AT: "I'm sure that will follow after this feeling out process is over." BJ: "Feeling out? I was doing that backstage with this one chick, she... " AT: "Nevermind Barry, we don't need to know." Both men are to thier feet. They get into a Test of Strength. They lock hands, then push against each other. Neither man gets an advantage, so they break after a few seconds. They then stare each other down, but with a small amount of mutual respect in thier eyes. They then go for it again, but this time, Dev takes the easy way out, kicking Numark in the gut. He then hits a hard right, and another, then uses an Irish Whip to send Numark to the ropes, Numark rebounds with a Diving Lariat, taking Dev down. Numark slowly gets to his feet, and as Dev is getting up, Numark goes to get a Front Facelock on, but Dev surprises the Champ, countering with a Northern Lights Suplex into a Bridge... 1... 2... kick out right before the 3. BJ: "SOOoo close to a new Television Champ!" AT: "Dev came out of nowhere with that great Suplex, Dev himself, a former Television Champion." BJ: "He's a former member of F.E.A.R. too, but he can't seem to understand that slight fact." Dev gets up, hitting the Champ with a stiff Dropkick to the upper chest, dropping Numark. Dev then picks him up, and sets him on the top turnbuckle. Dev climbs up, and goes for a Superplex, but Numark grabs the top rope, then he throws Dev off. Dev lands hard, as Numark gets his wits about him. He then positions himself to drop the Bombshell (Five Star Frog Splash.), which gets a massive pop from the capacity crowd. The cover... 1... 2... 3! Kickout, but a split-second too late! AT: "Numark got the three count!" BJ: "Damn, I thought Dev was gonna take it too." The winner of this bout, and.... STILL xUw World Television Champion... Numark! AT: "Numark retains his Television title, he adapted and overcame the Fighter, but give Dev credit for a well fought match. Wait, something is going on backstage...." Backstage, Fatbadger walks up to a vending machine to get a bag of Funyons. Suddenly, Blake Wolfson and Hodgo the Anarchist walks up behind him. Fatbadger has no idea until he is startled by Wolfson's voice. Blake: "Well, what do we have here... a Sooner... out of his element." Anarchist: "Tsk Tsk Tsk... you shouldn't ever be out of your element. Because when you are... " Blake hits Fatbadger hard in the face, and Hodgo follows with a couple of Anar-Kicks. Blake then takes the bag of Funyons, opens it, and eats a couple, then pours the bag out over the fallen Fatbadger. Anarchist: "Bad things happen.... Violent things happen." Blake: "Yuck... how can he eat those. Ugh!" Anarchist: "Heh." The two Ultra Violents turn to leave, but then Hodgo turns back, and hits another Anar-Kick to Fatbadger for good measure. Blake then realizes that he gave Fatty back his Funyons, so he takes them from him, and kicks him in the head before leaving the room. AT: "What was that? What did Fatbadger ever do to him?" BJ: "He showed up Potato... that's enough to earn an ass kicking." AT: "Well, next up... Immediate Impact implodes!" BJ: "This is the one I've wanted to see all week!" KoRe  vs.  Cereal Killer The sound of galloping horses can be faintly heard, until "Jackass" by Green Day starts up, and the crowd reacts with a very mixed reaction for the High Roller of Hardkore. The Horseman comes down to the ring, sporting the new "New Age Horsemen" T-Shirt from xUw Paraphenalia. (Not available on the site yet.). He gets in the ring, and waits as his music fades. Then, the mixed reaction continues, as "Name of the Game" by The Crystal Method plays, and the Emerald City Assassin makes his way to the ring. CK gets in and plays to the crowd a bit, before his music fades and the opening bell rings. BJ: "This is it! This is the match of the millenium!" AT: "Umm, don't overdo it Barry, I'd be surprised if this was match of the year." BJ: "Hey, it could happen." AT: "Yes, yes it could... but considering everything that has happened this year, it's doubtful." KoRe and CK lock up, CK comes out of it with a Armdrag Takedown. KoRe gets back up quickly, and CK goes for a Clothesline, but KoRe ducks it, and counters with a poorly executed Swinging Neckbreaker. KoRe gets up quickly, and makes up for his bad move by hitting a perfectly executed Elbowdrop. KoRe then covers... 1... quick kick out by CK. AT: "What the hell is KoRe thinking, going for a cover this early in the match?" BJ: "He probably thinks he really has broken CK's neck... you see that move he hit... brutal!" AT: "Yeah." Kore then sits CK up, and applies a Rear Chinlock. After several seconds, CK gets to his feet, and hits a series of Side Elbowsmashes to the gut of KoRe, breaking the hold. CK then goes for a Vertical Suplex, but KoRe counters with a Backslide Pin... 1... 2... 3! The fans pop at the sudden victory. BJ: "What? It's over... just like that?" AT: "Yeah Barry... that's what happens when someone gets a three count... the match ends." BJ: "Shut up Coconut." Here is your winner... KoRe! Backstage, Kata is being interveiwed by Jay 'Broni' Bronson. Broni: "We're here with Kata Hajime. Kata, you still are determined to clear the name of your good friend, Sheri Dedmon, of the attack that injured Raven X a few weeks ago. Anything to add?" Kata: "Well Jay, I have narrowed the suspects down to two... either Stungunn Ric Austin or NFM. Now, I'll deal with Austin, and the rest of his false Horsemen at a later time, but next week on Halloween Vortex... I challenge NFM to a match." Broni: "I'm sure it's more than just a challenge though, am I correct?" Kata: "You are. NFM... I don't know for certain that it was you... so I will have to simply beat the truth out of you." Kata leaves the set, as Jay Bronson looks a bit surprised. Broni: "Well... NFM and Kata next week? We'll keep you informed as things develop. Back to you Tomato." AT: "Thank you Jay 'Broni'. Now, it's time for our second title match here on Vortex. A battle of two friends for the InterContinental title." BJ: "Friends? I think not Grape. You hear what Saij has had to say about his... former colleague? I mean, I'm sure he doesn't have any hard feelings towards the New Age Enforcer, but he was hardly complimentary." BoneCrusher  vs.  Saij © xUw InterContinental Heavyweight Championship The sound of galloping horses can be heard once again. Then, as the main lights dim and the multi-colored strobelights flash, the opening guitar licks of "Here We Go! (Let's Rock 'n' Roll)" by C&C Music Factory plays, followed by the hip-hop/dance music that follows. The crowd pops huge as BoneCrusher makes his way to the ring, slapping some hands on the way down the aisle. He gets in the ring, and plays to the crowd a bit as the main lights come back up. Then, as his music fades, the arena lights up bright, in sharp contrast to the entrance of before, and the crowd pop gets even bigger as "I want it All" by Queen plays while the defending InterContinental Champion makes his way to the ring. Saij gets in the ring, and climbs to a corner turnbuckle, and the crowd yells out with him... Crowd: "...HUZZAH!" AT: "Listen to this capacity crowd!" BJ: "What? Shut up people, I'm trying to hear!" Saij climbs down, and hands his belt to the referee, who in turn, hands it to the ringside attendant. The music fades, and the opening bell gets this one underway. Bo'Cru and Saij lock up. Saij comes out of it with an Armwringer, twisting the rather large arm of the challenger in a very unnatural position. He then hits an Elbowsmash into the tricep of the arm. After a few seconds, Bo'Cru uses his power to execute a swift Armdrag Takedown, sending Saij to the mat, his momentum causing him to roll out of the ring. As Saij gets up, Bo'Cru hits the ropes, and it looks like he's about to hit some sort of Suicide Dive, but Saij sidesteps... however, Bo'Cru jumps up on the top rope, and springs backwards back into the ring. The crowd cheers a bit. AT: "Whoa... Saij saw the Enforcer getting ready to go airborne, but the challenger also seen that he wasn't gonna hit the move just in time." BJ: "Dumb ass idiot... he's a seven foot, 300 pound monster... he should act as such." AT: "Umm... he does... but a monster with wings." Saij gets back in the ring, and locks up again with his challenger. BoneCrusher effortlessly tosses Saij away and into the corner. Saij looks a bit shocked at how easily he did that. Saij hits the ropes, and goes for his Spinning Heel Kick, the Downtime. Bo'Cru catches him, and lifts him up, then he goes for a Running Powerslam, but Saij slides down the big man's back, and as Bo'Cru turns, Saij kicks him in the gut, and hits a nice DDT, planting Bo'Cru's head into the mat. The crowd cheers. AT: "Nice evasion by Saij, and a GREAT DDT by the Champion!" BJ: "Yeah, it was alright, I guess." Saij then takes a few steps back, and hits the Ker-Splat (Running Somersault Senton Splash, RVD's Rolling Thunder). Saij rolls the big guy over and covers... 1... 2... right before the count of three, Bo'Cru tosses Saij clear off of him and sits up. The crowd cheers this. Saij stumbles back to his feet, as the Enforcer gets up. Saij goes for a punch, but Bo'Cru uses the old I-block-your-punch-you-don't-block-mine routine, followed by a kneelift to the gut, and Bo'Cru lifts him up, hitting a Vertical Suplex Drop (Vertical Suplex into a Bodyslam.). Bo'Cru then bounces off the ropes, and hits a Running Somersault Senton Splash of his own, to the surprise of the crowd. Bo'Cru covers... 1... 2... kick out by the Champion. AT: "One, Two, Three, he got... NO! Saij manages to escape from that one!" BJ: "332 pounds of muscle hitting a Senton Splash. Can you imagine getting hit with that... and still kicking out?" AT: "Couldn't be any more painful than announcing a show with you." BJ: "HEY!" Bo'Cru picks up Saij, and goes for the Los Angeles Neckbreaker, but Saij breaks free from the Reverse Neckbreaker set-up, and he rolls Bo'Cru up... 1... 2... kick out. Saij gets back up, and Bo'Cru dives at him, hitting a rather sloppy, but still very effective Clothesline. Bo'Cru then gets back to his feet, and he picks up the Champ. He whips Saij into a corner, but Saij reverses. Bo'Cru in turns reverses, and in turn, so too does the Champ, turning this into almost a Square Dance... but Saij whips Bo'Cru into the corner, inadvertantly crushing the referee into the corner. The crowd responds with an "Oh!" AT: "Oh my... that official can be seriously injured!' BJ: "So what, it's just a damn referee... they're sub-human." AT: "That's horrible Barry." BJ: "Horrible, because it's true." As if it was scripted to happen, Saij is setting up for his Topsy-Turvy Hurricanrana, when out of nowhere... where it always comes from... Henrik the King comes in with a Kendo Stick. Saij actually gets to execute his move, to the delight of the crowd. The referee wasn't all that hurt actually, until from the blindside, he is nailed with by Henrik. Saij gets up from hitting the move, and he takes a shot to the head from Henrik. The crowd is booing loudly. AT: "What? It's Henrik! King Henrik! Is this the impact that the Ultra-Violents was wanting to make?" BJ: "Yes! They said it all week long on The xUw Network that they were going to make an impact... and here it is!" Saij is starting to get up, quite dazed, when Henrik goes to strike him again. However, Henrik is suddenly hurled from the ring... by BoneCrusher. The crowd pops huge. Bo'Cru helps Saij back to his feet, making sure he's alright. Saij acknowledges. The crowd pops huge. Henrik stumbles to his feet outside the ring, looking oddly at the two men in the ring. AT: "BoneCrusher did say that if the Ultra-Violents got involved that they would pay." BJ: "What the hell? You don't help out your opponent?" A couple of xUw security men come down to escort Henrik backstage, as Bo'Cru and Saij checks on the official, who is out cold. Then, another official runs down to talk to them. BJ: "Is this match over? Has it been thrown out?" The words can't be made out, but then, the offiical puts his hands together, signaling for the two men to go at it. The crowd pops huge. AT: "This match continues!" Saij and Bo'Cru look at each other, and shake hands. They then stand back, and lock up again. Saij hits a Kneelift to the big man, then, he whips him to the ropes, and he hits the Downtime, a Spinning Heel Kick... which is a set-up for the Final Huzzah. The crowd pops. Saij then gets up, holding his head which was nailed by Henrik, he's still a bit groggy. Saij climbs the turnbuckle, and yells out to the crowd... Saij: "HUZZAAAAH!" The crowd pops huge, as Saij goes for the Final Huzzah (Somersault Senton Bomb), but Bo'Cru rolls out of the way, and Saij eats canvas. The crowd pops again. Bo'Cru springs back to his feet, then runs up the turnbuckles, and looks around at the crowd. AT: "He missed the Final Huzzah! Wait! BoneCrusher is up... he's on the turnbuckle, this could be... " BoneCrusher dives off, hitting the Big Enforcer Elbowdrop... connecting into the chest of Saij with such impact that Saij almost rolls head over heels. Bo'Cru covers... 1... 2... 3! The bell rings, and the crowd pops huge! AT: "One... Two... Three! He got it!" BJ: "We have a new InterContinental Champion!" The winner of this bout, and... NEW xUw InterContinental Heavyweight Champion... BoneCrusher! Suddenly, Henrik re-emerges with his Kendo Stick, and before Bo'Cru can celebrate his win, Henrik nails him directly between the eyes, knocking him out cold. The crowd boos loudly! AT: "That bastard Henrik!" BJ: "Maybe that was the impact they was talking about.... you hear that shot?" Saij gets up slowly, and as he turns, he is also layed out by Henrik. Then, Henrik nails both men a few more times while they're down. Referees and Road Agents run out to force Henrik out of the ring. The crowd boos as Henrik leaves... and as he leaves, the camera zooms in. Henrik: "More Double Trouble... that InterContinental title will belong to YOUR King! Hahaha!" AT: "That bastard! He's obsessed with the InterContinental title!" BJ: "Bastard? That's YOUR King you speak of, knave!" AT: "Oh, shut up Barry!" Backstage, Mister Webster is watching what has transpired. An assistant walks in. Assist: "Sir... did you see that? Henrik had no business out there!" Webster: "No.. yeah? You think I can't see that?" Assist: "Well, it's just that... one could argue that Saij was screwed out of the InterContinental title!" Webster: "Yes, you're right. That is why... at More Double Trouble, Henrik wants that title so badly... fine. He will face BoneCrusher for the InterContinental title... as well as Saij... in a Triple-Threat match!" Huge crowd pop. Webster: "I'd really hate to be Henrik when those two get in the ring with him." Assist: "Umm... sir, where is the InterContinental title belt?" Webster: "What do you mean... I thought you had it?" Assist: "It was on the timekeeper's table, but through everything that happened, I..." Webster: "Hmm.... " The assistant leaves the room, as Mr. Webster looks thoughtful. BJ: "Pineapple, do you have the InterContinental title belt?" AT: "Why would I have it?" BJ: "I dunno... I ain't got it." AT: "Henrik never came around over here and took it... not that we could see anyway. Interesting... anyway, Ladies and Gentlemen, it is now time for out main event!" The Ultra-Violents (Blake Wolfson, Henrik the King, Hodgo the Anarchist) vs.  The Army of Darkness (The DDT, M V Pete, Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy) Six-Man Tag-Team Match "Lounge Act" by Nirvana plays, as the crowd boos loudly for the Ultra-Violents. Henrik makes his way down the aisle, fresh from his earlier attack, while Hodgo the Anarchist and Blake Wolfson flank him. They get in the ring, and play to the crowd a bit as the boos intensify. Then, the lights dim ever so slowly until the arena is filled with... what else? Darkness. "One Arm Scissor" plays, and the crowd gives a mixed, but extremely loud reaction, as M V Pete, DDT, and The Ripper come to the ring, the only illumination is a dim blue spotlight shining on the entering combatants. As DDT and The Ripper methodically gets in the ring, M V Pete quickly rushes in and begins to assault Henrik. Soon, an all out melee begins, but ends fairly quickly when Jack tosses Hodgo out of the ring, while Wolfson tosses out DDT, only to turn around and be nailed with a vicious Clothesline from The Ripper. The referee calls for the opening bell, as M V Pete and Henrik begin. AT: "What a match we are sure to have here. These men, already tearing into each other here!" BJ: "And it should be mentioned that this is the first match for DDT since his return." AT: "Good point." M V Pete whips Henrik to the ropes, Henrik ducks a Clothesline, hits the opposite ropes, ducks another Clothesline attempt by Pete, and hits a Reverse Neckbreaker. Henrik then picks his opponent back up, hits a Forearm Shot to the chin, then whips him to the ropes once again. This time, Wolfson pulls down the ropes, causing Pete to take a spill outside the ring. The other two Violents then attack the former Suspect. The Ripper drops from his corner and rushes over to the scene, Hodgo sees him coming, and goes for a Clothesline, but it is ineffective, as Hodgo is basically steamrolled by The Ripper, who tosses Wolfson off of his partner. The Ripper then helps Pete back into the ring, but with Henrik right there, he begins putting the boots to him. The referee counts to 4 before Henrik lets off, and plays a bit to his "Loyal Subjects", getting the desired response. AT: "It might be a good idea for Henrik to stay on the attack here instead of taunting the fans." BJ: "It might be, but that's not what YOUR King feels like doing, Onion." Henrik then turns to pick his opponent up, but Pete hits a nice hard right hand, then another, then he backs the "King" into corner. He whips Henrik into the opposite corner, and he follows him into the corner with a Clothesline. Pete then tags in DDT, and the fans cheer a little as the Hardcore Icon comes in, nailing some hard Shoulder Thrusts into the midsection. DDT then grabs Henrik and, instead of whipping Henrik to the ropes, he pulls him back and drops him with a Shortarm Clothesline. AT: "I told you that Henrik should've stayed on the attack." BJ: "Ah, shaddap!" DDT tags in The Ripper, who gets a very loud response. The Ripper lifts up Henrik almost effortlessly, and he hits a very high angle, and high impact Bodyslam. AT: "OH! What a slam! Henrik must be broken in half!" The Ripper then lifts up the King with one hand, and tosses him into his corner. Henrik quickly tags in Wolfson, and the two former xUw Champions trade blows, with The Ripper getting the advantage. The Ripper then kicks Wolfson in the gut, and sets up for the RipSaw, when Hodgo rushes in with a Diving Clothesline, dropping The Ripper. Hodgo leaves the ring, and Wolfson jumps up for an Elbowdrop, which misses as The Ripper rolls away, and makes the tag to DDT. DDT then comes in and applies a Front Facelock, backing Wolfson into his own corner... which backfires as the other two Ultra Violents attack DDT. AT: "I'm not certain of DDT's strategy here, backing himself into a triple-team by the Ultra Violents." BJ: "He's an idiot, a complete..... moron! He had Wolfson in a bad way, and then does..... this?" AT: "DDT pauses between sentences, not words.... fool." DDT manages to break free of the assault, but without the advantage he once had. Wolfson locks up with DDT, and comes out with a Hammerlock. DDT spins around, but Wolfson adjusts his hold into a Top Wristlock. DDT breaks this by hitting a Kneelift. DDT then quickly applies the Lockdown (Crippler Crossface). AT: "Lockdown! The Lockdown! This could end the match right here!" BJ: "No! Wolfson, the Lone... erm... the Wolf! He can't... he can't!" Wolfson endures the Lockdown for several seconds, before Henrik runs in and nails DDT with a kick to the head, breaking the hold. M V Pete rushes in, and spears the hell out of Henrik, sending both of them through the ropes to the floor. BJ: "OH! Man... have you ever just had air... leave your body?" AT: "I'm sure that Henrik just has! What a Spear from M V Pete, but not before Henrik breaks the hold from DDT!" DDT then tries to re-apply the Lockdown, but Wolfson blocks it, then rolls over, and manages to hit an undetected Low Blow. This allows Wolfson to get up and tag in The Anarchist. Hodgo rushes in, and hits a couple of hard Anar-Kicks to DDT. He then goes for a cover... 1... 2... Save made by M V Pete! The referee forces Pete out of the ring, while behind his back, DDT is assaulted by Wolfson and Hodgo. Jack tries to get in and stop this, but the official won't let him pass. AT: "DDT has found himself in a really bad spot! Turn around ref!" BJ: "No, don't turn! The AoD are trying to get in the ring... stop them!" Wolfson picks up DDT, and sets up, then hits the Crying Wolf (Tomikaze DDT, Unprettier, Impaler). Wolfson quickly slides out of the ring, as Hodgo pins. The AoD is still trying to get in the ring, but the referee threatens them with disqualification, so M V Pete and The Ripper slowly gets out of the ring. The ref turns around and sees the pin.... 1... 2... foot on the bottom rope! The crowd pops! AT: "Foot on the ropes! DDT got his foot on the ropes!" BJ: "NO! Dammit, that short amount of time was enough to allow DDT to get his... umm... head straight! This match should be over! Disqualify the Army of Darkness!" AT: "For what? They left the ring as instructed." Hodgo sits up DDT, and kicks him hard in the back. DDT yells out in pain. Hodgo then picks up his opponent, and whips him to the ropes, only to hit the ropes himself and attempt a Running Clothesline, but DDT goes for the same move, and both men get dropped, the crowd pops! AT: "Both men are down! DDT came out with a Clothesline, and both men are dropped!" BJ: "No! Get up! Someone get up! Hodgo!" The referee counts both men down... 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... both start to stir a bit... 7... 8... they are almost up... 9... Hodgo dives and makes the tag to Henrik, but DDT tags in The Ripper! Both men collide with a barrage of punches as the crowd pops! Jack then hits a kick to the midsection, and he sets up.... and hits the RipSaw! (Release Jackknife Powerbomb) He covers... 1... Hodgo and Wolfson rush in... 2... so does M V Pete and DDT... 3! Pete dropkicks Hodgo, Wolfson tries to make the save but is a split second too late! AT: "One... Two... Three! It's over!" BJ: "Oh, dammit! That's not the impact the Ultra Violents wanted!" Here are your winners...  The Army of Darkness! The brawl ensues between the two teams, and all hell breaks loose! Suddenly, Sidhe Nimh rushes into the fray to help out his stablemates. The crowd pops huge! Suddenly, the Usual Suspects rush in, not to help the Ultra Violents, but only to attack the Army of Darkness. All four Boomer Sooners rush in to attack the Ultra Violents. The four teams find themselves in the ring, then the real brawl continues. AT: "Look at this! The four teams are battling it out amongst each other... look!" BJ: "It's complete anarchy! Hodgo must be in heaven!" AT: "I don't know about that... it looks to me like he's in a war! Wait... it's the Horsemen!" BJ: "What?!" The New Age Horsemen come out, but as KoRe and Stungunn Ric Austin run, BoneCrusher, with a bandage on his forehead holds them back. They ask what he's doing, and you can hear him. Bo'Cru: "Just... look." As soon as the three see the other teams fighting each other, they all cross arms and watch. BoneCrusher and Ric Austin smiles, while KoRe laughs at the carnage in the ring. BJ: "What are they doing?" AT: "Apparently, BoneCrusher figures why stop them from beating each other senseless?" A barrage of Referees and Road Agents rush the ring to break up the melee. The crowd is extremely loud as this brawl comes to an end with no apparent advantage. AT: "This has really boiled over here tonight... we're out of time folks! For Barry Johnson, this is the Atomic Tomato.. good night!" The copyright graphics and such comes up as the show ends. Fade to black.
