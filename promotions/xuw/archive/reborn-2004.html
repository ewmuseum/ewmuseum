XUW: Reborn - Sunday, April 4, 2004 XUW REBORN Just over two years ago, XUW was in turmoil. It was fractured, disorganised and looked to be heading the way of many other federations as it stumbled towards a bitter and twisted end. Yet XUW was saved. XUW carried on to become the juggernaut of Sports Entertainment that it is today. XUW was Reborn. We've seen some thing here at Reborn before now. We've seen titles won and titles lost. We've seen new stars emerge, shock victories and unbelievable stunts. We've seen legends born… … …and we've seen legends die. Hopefully this year in Boston there will be no more death, but what there will be is pain, and lots of it. One half of the tag champions will be wrestling with a severely damaged left arm, which is healing from being recently broken in a car accident. The mental state of those involved in the War Games match is definitely questionable, and the man bidding to defend his PTC Global Title is suffering from cancer. So here it is, XUW Reborn, and let more words not cheapen the occasion, this day just over two years from the day XUW grew from being a good, but brittle federation to being a strong, and world class one. The fans are cheering and raising up their signs, and the announcers are ready for the action. The signs are many and varied, such as… “I came to see the cute animal!” “Fatbadger – representing XUW… SPECTACULARLY!” “You're going to be… ELIMINATED!” “Rujasu > Hogan” “Intelligent Hardkore for President!” “Skeletons of Society winning War Games? Fuggettaboutit!” “Blake Wolfson is gonna win that PTC Title!” “I am a Legionnaire” …and, as tradition dictates… “No, I'm the father!” So, let's join our esteemed announcers, former XUW World Champion, Mikey, and his helpful friend “The Majestic” Barry Johnson.
