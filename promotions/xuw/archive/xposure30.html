XUW XPosure #30

20 Oct 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW XPosure "Obsession" by Papa Roach starts playing as we see a shot of the Ulti-tron and stage all set up for Xposure.  A ridiculous amout of pyro blasts off, sending sparks flying everywhere.  The camera shows a full packed house... and no, Xposure isn't taped before another show, Xposure is it's own show, baby.  Simply because XUW is just that good. Fatbadger Vs. Rujasu "Generic Rock Music" hits and Rujasu comes out to a bit of heat from the crowd. He pays them no attention really, and just makes his way down to the ring.  "Mudshovel" by Staind hits and Fatbadger steps from behind the curtain to a large, Boomer-sized pop from the crowd.  He seems a little surprised, but happy none the less.  He poses at the top of the ramp before heading down to the ring.  He slides in the ring and the bell sounds, making our first match of the night official.  Ruj and Fatty lock it up in the center of the ring and Ruj takes control with a headlock.  Fatty pushes out of it, sending Ruj to the ropes.  Ruj bounces off and gets leveled with a clothesline.  Fatty jumps over the downed Ruj and runs to the ropes.  Ruj gets up as Fatty comes running back.  Ruj ducks and Fatty leapfrogs over him, running through to the other side.  Fatty bounces off again, and Ruj takes him down with a drop toe hold.  Ruj reaches over, grabbing Fattys legs trying to cinch in the Philly Cloverleaf, but Fatty scrambles to the ropes before he can come close to locking it in.  The ref pushes Ruj away from Fatty, and Fatty gets to his feet.  The two being to circle in the center of the ring again before locking up once more.  Fatty takes the advatage with a headlock this time, before spinning around to the back of Ruj. Fatty lifts him up, and puts him back down with a German suplex.  Fatty lifts Ruj up again and tosses him to the ropes once more.  Fatty grabs Ruj on his way back and hits a stiff twisting spine buster on him.  Fatty then walks over to the corner and starts to slowly climb the ropes.  Right before he gets all the way to the top, Ruj gets up and runs over to the corner, pushing Fatty to the outside.  Fatty flies off the turnbuckle smashing his face into the guard rail. Ruj then climbs up himself and flies off the top himself, coming down on Fatty with a flying knee drop.  The ref starts to count... 1 2 Ruj gets up and picks up Fatty, throwing him into the guardrail. 3 Ruj then stomps on the downed Fatty. 4 5 Ruj picks Fatty up again and goes to slam his face into the steel steps, but Fatty blocks it and slams Ruj into the steps. 6 Fatty then throws Ruj into the guard rail, sending him over it and into the fans. 7 Fatty hops the baracade after Rujasu and the two begin trading blows. 8 9 They continue to trade blows, oblivious to the count inside the ring. 10! The ref signals for the bell and the two just continue fighting until they are out of sight. Double Countout. We cut to the backstage area where X Factor and Dave Kipfer can be seen talking at the refreshments table. Dave is making what looks to be a cup of coffee as X Factor is talking... X Factor- These bloody females act as if they should be the center of a man's world and look what happens when they get it! They quit. They give up. I tell you, the blooming lot of them should be tied up and gagged. Dave laughs a bit, but doesn't look comfortable with the discussion. X Factor- I know how you feel. After what that female nazi did to you, turning on you, sleeping with another man. I can take that all away. I can show you how to be better then you are... make you the champion that a woman will never want you to be. Dave looks at X Factor, something flickering in his gaze. He takes a sip of his coffee and then smiles at X Factor. Dave- I'll think about what you have said. I'll get back to you about it. Dave turns and walks away as X Factor nods his head. Destroyer and "The Bastard" Al Crud Vs. Micheal Bolling and Laudust "One Bourbon, One Scotch, One Beer" by George Thorogood hits and the bastard gets a real nice pop, much to his surprise.  He chugs his beer on the ramp and makes his way down to the ring.  "Pass the Courvoisier Pt. 2" by Busta Rhymes feat. P. Diddy hits and Destroyer comes out to a nice pop of his own.  "The Natural" by Randy Newman hits and Laudust comes out dressed like Doink the Clown... so he shall be reffered to as Doinkdusk for the remainder of the match. Finally, "Date with Destiny" hits and Micheal Bolling comes out to a lot of heat from the crowd.  All four men take their respective corners, with Crud and Bolling starting things off.  The bell rings and the match starts.  Crud and Bolling lock up and Crud backs Bolling into the corner.  The ref asks for a clean break, which is given.  Before Crud can back away, though, Bolling reels back and gives him a shot to the head.  Then another, and another, before grabbing Crud and giving him a snap suplex.  Bolling gets up and gives Crud a couple of boots to the shoulder, before picking him back up.  Bolling the whips Crud to the ropes, and tries for a shoulder block, but the bigger man in Crud just knocks Bolling right on his ass.  The fans cheer while Crud looks down at Bolling smiling.  Bolling looks shocked for a moment, but then gets back up and locks up with Crud.  Bolling takes the advantage this time with a headlock. Crud pushes Bolling off to the ropes and hits him with a spinebuster on his way back.  Crud then goes for the pin... 1 2 Kickout. Crud then picks up Bolling and wastes no time hitting a Bastard Bomb (Powerbomb) on him.  Crud then goes over and tags in Destroyer.  They both pick up Bolling and send him to the ropes, knocking him back down with a double clothesline. Crud goes to his corner while Destroyer goes for the quick pin... 1 2 Kickout. Destroyer then goes to the corner and climbs up to the top.  He jumps off looking for the Self Destruct (450 Splash), but Bolling rolls out of the way at the last second.  Bolling then uses this advantage to make the tag to Doinkdust. Dust then walks over to the downed Destroyer and begins to lay a few boots into him before picking him up.  Dust hits a backbreaker on Destroyer.  Dust picks Destroyer back up and whips him to the ropes.  Destroyer bounces off the ropes and runs right into a sidewalk slam.  Doinkdust then goes to his corner and tags Bolling back in.  Bolling comes in and immediately starts kicking the downed Destroyer.  Bolling picks Destroyer up and whips him to the ropes and Destroyer runs right into a picture perfect dropkick.  Bolling goes for the cover... 1 2 Kickout. Bolling then picks Destroyer up again and attempts a suplex, but Destroyer blocks it with his leg and throws a snap suplex of his own.  Both men get up and start trading blows with each other in the middle of the ring, until Destroyer begins to gain the advantage.  Destroyer gives Bolling a quick kick to the gut, and a powerbomb before Bolling can react.  Destroyer waits for Bolling to get up and once he does Destroyer locks in the The Hot Shot (Tazzmission into pinning German suplex).  Destroyer holds the submission for a second or two, before lifting Bolling up and slamming him down for the pin... 1 Doinkdust runs to make the save... 2 Al Crud runs in and kicks Doinkdust in the gut.  Crud hooks in the Mother Crudder (Stunner) as the ref counts... 3!!  Crud hits the mat at the same time as the refs hand. The crowd goes nuts as "Pass the Courvoisier Pt. 2" hits and Al Crud and Destroyer share a beer in the ring. Winners via pinfall... "The Bastard" Al Crud and Destroyer A video package begins to air showing the time NFM took to drop the TV title due to personal reasons.  It picks up and begins playing from here: NFM: I've made this phrase famous before...and I shall do it once again. XUW... I SHALL RETURN. With that,Mr. Webster, whoever is in charge. See to it that my title gets a worthy new owner. NFM places the Television Title down onto the mat as the fans all clap. NFM rolls out of the ring and walks up the ramp. The scene then changes to Mr. Webster sitting behind his desk. Mr. Webster:  Hello XUW fans.  I'd like to take this time to address the Television Title situation.  NFM has chosen to drop the title to go take care of some personal issues, and we here at XUW wish him the best of luck.  That leaves us with a problem though.  A matches have been set, and we must go on.  Chase Wilson and Numark are both the next two men lined up for the Television Title and so they will have theor shot.  The two will be fighting tonight for the vacant title, with the winner becoming the XUW Television Champion.  That man with then go on to Mayhem and face the loser of the match for the title once again.  Basically giving both men NFM's place in the matches.  Thank you. Numark vs. Chase Wilson (Television Title Match) "Welcome to the Suburbs" by The KottonMouth Kings begins to play as Chase Wilson makes his way out to a nice pop from the fans. He scratches his head once and then shrugs, walking to the ring. He climbs up on the apron and steps between the ropes, turning as "Clean My Wounds" by Corrosion of Conformity begins to play to another nice pop from the crowd as Numark walks out, looking at the audience for a moment before he heads to the ring. He climbs in as the referee signal's for the bell, starting this Television championship match. Chase and Numark study each other, no strangers to the other. The two finally lock up in the middle, with Numark winning and giving Chase a german suplex. He follows it up quickly by pulling Chase back to his feet and hitting the Ante up (double underhook powerbomb). Numark goes for a pin.... 1... 2... Kickout! Numark looks surprised but shrugs it off and pulls Chase to his feet, hitting a snap suplex and rolling through to pick Chase up again and hit another. He goes for another pin.... 1... 2.... Shoulder up! Numark picks up Chase, tossing him to the ropes and catching Chase on the rebound, applying a t-bone suplex. Numark goes up top for the Bombshell (Five Star Frogsplash), hitting it, but it takes a lot out of him and he rolls to his side, holding his ribs in pain. Numark finally pulls himself up and pulls up Chase, giving him his finisher, Sweet Desire (cradle piledriver). Numark goes for the pin.... 1.... 2..... 3!!!!!!! Winner and NEW XUW Television Champion Numark A Mayhem commercial begins to play on the Ulti-tron.  It starts out with the end of Saij's match with Blake last week into the forming of the Ultra-Violents. Small video packages begin playing showing some of the events of recent promos on both sides of the Boomer Sooners and the Ultra-Violents, as well as Kipfer talking to X Factor backstage.  A shot of Dave Kipfer with Henrik the King on the side of him, both posing, kind of like what the WWE does for a match. Kipfer has Saij standing behind him, and Henrik has Blake.  In the middle of all the men is X-Factor with "Special Guest Announcer" under him.  It then showcases some of the antics of The Beat Down Authority as well as The New Royal Legion.  We see the same type of picture showing the BDA as well as the NRL, with the tag belts over their shoulders.  We now see Hodgo the Anrachist performing a few wrestling moves in the ring on opponents.  The shot switches to M V Pete making a couple of opponents tap, until it shows him holding the Submission Title high.  We see the two of them standing next to each other, the Submission Title over Pete's shoulder.  We then see shots of Television title holders of the past, until the shot changes to Numark and Chase posing, with the TV title on the screen between the two of them.  "Mayhem - October 21", is the last thing we see on the screen until we go back to the arena shot with the fans screaming. XUW Hardcore Title Match - Kata Vs. "Stungunn" Ric Austin © "Guild of Mute Assassins" by Clutch hits and Kata comes out to a loud pop from the crowd.  He poses for a second before making his way down to ringside.  "Back in the Saddle" by Aerosmith hits and the fans pop as equally loud when Austin steps from behind the curtain with the hardcore belt around his waist.  He poses for a moment and heads down the ramp.  When he gets to the ring, he lifts one of the flaps hanging off the ring to pull random weapons from underneath it.  He tosses in the regular assortment of a trash can and lid, a stop sign, and some of those cookie sheets they love so much.  As Austin goes to climb into the ring, Kata knocks him down with a baseball slide.  Kata then follows outside after him and the bell rings for good measure.  Kata grabs Austin by the head and slams it into the guard rail.  Kata then takes Austin and whips him into the steel steps.  Kata picks Austin up and rolls him into the ring.  Kata then slides in after him and picks him up again.  Kata whips Ric to the ropes and Ric ducks under a clothesline.  Austin bounces off the ropes and hits Kata with a clothesline of his own.  Austin then walks over to the garbage can lid and picks it up.  He waits for Kata to get up and when he does Ric slams the lid into Kata's head, sending him back a couple of steps.  Ric moves forward and hits him again with it sending Kata back into the ropes.  Austin drops the lid and whips Kata to the opposite side of the ring.  Kata runs back into a nice swinging neckbreaker from Ric.  Ric then picks up the stop sign.  Kata gets up and Austin tries to hit him with it, but before Austin and swing the sign Kata gives him a kick to the midsection, causing him to drop the sign.  Kata then grabs hold of Austins head and delivers a DDT right on the stop sign that Ric dropped.  Kata goes for the pin... 1 2 Kickout. Kata gets up from the pin and walks over to where the cookie sheets are and grabs one.  Kata uses his free hand to whip Austin to the ropes.  Austin bounces off the ropes and Kata winds up, hitting Ric in the head with the cookie sheet, sending him flying.  Kata picks up Austin again and sets him up for a verticle suplex, but Ric blocks it with his leg and throws his own verticle suplex. Austin takes his time getting up and walks over to the garbage can.  Before he can pick it up Kata spins him around and whips him to the ropes, but Austin counters and sends Kata to the ropes.  Austin bends down and hits a back body drop on Kata.  Austin then picks up the garbage can that he tried to get before and then hits a downed Kata in the back with it until it starts to fold up. Austin drops the can and rolls Kata up... 1 2 Kickout. Austin picks up Kata and gives him a DDT.  Austin then rolls out of the ring to take a chair from ringside before sliding back in.  Austin attempts to hit Kata with the chair, but Kata kicks Austin in the midsection.  Austin drops the chair and Kata grabs him and throws a snap suplex on him.  Kata then picks Ric up and hits a German suplex.  Kata gets back up again and turns around to find another weapon, but as soon as he does Dev practically takes Kata's head off with a steel chair.  Kata goes down hard and Dev slides out of the ring.  Ric sees Kata down and crawls over to make the pin.  It was all legal... being a hardcore match and all, so the ref counts... 1 2 3!! Winner via pinfall and STILL XUW Hardcore Champion... "Stungunn" Ric Austin Austin grabs his belt and leaves the ring quickly while his music plays.  The feed fades out with Kata slowly coming to in the ring.
