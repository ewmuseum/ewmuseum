XUW Mayhem #1

3 Sep 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Show begins with pyro and the titan tron lighting up the arena, as the music hits and D26 walks to the ring D26: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the first ever XUW Meyhem. Now, I, (crowd chants along) The Whole F’N Show D26: Will be running the show. On to my first match announcement. I know that there is some dissention in the Submission title ranks. There are quite a few people opting for this title. I understand Jay has thrown himself into the mix again. Well, Jay, you may get what you want. Tonight, you will have a match with Kata, and the winner will get a title shot at the next PPV. There seems to be some other pro… Music hits and Jack Nobbledy walks out onto the ramp Jack: Heheh, now I believe there is something you owe me. You see, the ripper won your little contest, and how you owe me a title shot. What better way to get back at my former partner than to win the tag team title without that no talent piece of ****. D26: Hey now, first of all, you can’t swear, this is not PPV, this is cable! Second, where do you get the BALLS to interrupt me (crowd chanting along) the Whole F’N Show! Jack: I am tired of your talking and I am tired of all these goddamn huge promo’s at the beginning of shows. It is time that we cut that crap out, and get to the wrestling. Now D26, put up, or shut up! D26: Who’s your partner pal… Jack: You’ll see…later, I will have gold! D26 exits the ring and they cut to the commentators, AT and Jobber AT: Welcome to Monday night Meyhem… Jobber: Oh shut up you JR wannabe! AT: Don’t even get me started on you pal… Jobber: Well, our first match tonight is one of my favorites, it is a submission match, between Lance and Chase. This is because of the ending to the Chase/Mr. Mikey match at Road Rage! Chase enters the ring to many cheers, and Lance enters to the boos that are customary. Lance grabs a mic. Lance: If I can be serious for a moment. I am here to wrestle. I have already beat one pot head senseless, and now I have to beat another? This is… Chase jumps Lance from behind and begins to beat on him. He does a few quick suplexes, and puts Lance into an arm bar. Lance yawns and quickly gets out of the move, and rolls to the outside. Chase makes a chase…yea…that is right…and Lance catches him with a lariat on the outside of the ring. He rolls Chase into the ring and goes for the half-crab, when Bob comes running down the ramp. Bob dives into the ring, and begins to beat on Lance and the bell rings. Chase sits with his jaw on the floor staring at Bob beating on Lance, and then gets up and pushes Bob down. Bob gets up and an all out brawl ensues. Ref’s (The Hungry Mexican, Shooter, and three midgets) hold back the men. Shooter holds back Bob, while Mexican holds back Lance and the three midgets hold back Lance. Winner by DQ and still Submission champion: Lance Storm AT: Wow, did you see those midgets holding Lance back?! Jobber: Yea, no wonder Lance hates midgets… AT: Our next match is the one that Diablos announced at the top of the show. Jay V. Kata. Here we go! Jay’s music hits and he walks to the ring to a mixed reaction. Kata’s music hits and he walks slowly to the ring, smiling the whole time… Jay: What is so damn funny Kama? Kata: Oh, you’ll see… Jay: Nooch, whatever you… Kata grabs Jay and quickly slaps on a cross-face chicken wing. Jay reaches the ropes when he looks down and sees Naron sitting at ringside. He quickly loses focus and crawls outside the ring. Kata sits inside laughing… Jay: What up, boo boo kitty ****. Hey, you wanna meet me later after the match? Kata gets out of the ring and grabs a chair and hits Jay over the back with it. He rolls Jay into the ring and quickly gets the submission with an ankle lock. Winner and in submission title match: Kata Naron gets on the mic and begins to talk: Jay, I am getting tired of seeing your face. Here is what we are going to do. Next week, right here, I am going to put the most valuable thing I have on the line against you, and that is Bob. If I win, you leave, and Bob is mine forever. If you win (snickers) then you can have Bob, and…just to make things interesting, I will go on a date with you… Jay smiles and accepts the challenge… AT: Well, that should be…interesting… Jobber: Yea, like getting a tooth pulled. One no talent girl is bad enough, but two! AT: Jay is….well, I see your point. Anyway, our next match will be HBK v. Sinner. HBK as we all know, is holding Severson’s title hostage, and now it is time for HBK to face Sinner, who faced Severson…well you get the point… The contestants enter the ring and we get under way Sinner and HBK brawl outside the ring, and it spills into the crowd. They brawl into the outside, as Mexican tries to get the match into the ring. The fight goes on outside, and ends up on the street. Sinner throws HBK into a car, and slams him through a window on the side of the building. HBK gets up and grabs a pipe and begins to beat on Sinner with it. Mexican gives up and calls for the bell. Winner: No contest Suddenly Severson comes from no where and slams on HBK with a chair. He stands over HBK and begins to talk: Severson: You made the biggest mistake of your life when you took my title. Get ready for some pain…because I will get my title back! Just wait… AT: Wow, what the hell was that? Jobber: This is the worst damn show I have ever seen. This makes Raw look like an emmy winning show! AT: I agree…now, onto our next match: and it is for the tag team championship! This is going to be interesting, considering Woof and D26 don’t know who they are facing! Jobber: Oh, you’re talking again? D26 and woof enter to massive cheers. Jack’s music hits, and he walks onto the ramp, mic in hand. Jack: Well, it seems I am going to have some gold in a few minutes. Partners are easy to come by here! Here is my choice, the king of the dark match, along with me, the king of darkness, the Ripper! My partner is The Cereal Killer! Together we will bring a new reign of terror to XUW! Cereal killer’s music hits and they race down to the ring, and the match is on. The match starts with D26 and Jack brawling in the ring. Jack hits a suplex and whips D into the ropes and hits him with a back drop. Jack tries to whip D into the corner but D reverses it and Jack hits the corner with authority and collapses. D stalks over to him, but Jack picks him up and drops him on the top turn buckle. Jack reaches over and tags in Killer. Killer comes in and begins to beat on D. He whips D into the corner and sets him up for a superplex. D reverses it, and drops Killer off. With the last of his strength, he hits a 5 star frog splash, but can’t make the cover. D crawls over to his corner to tag woof, who is waiting with his arm outstretched. D reaches for the tag, but Woof turns around. He drops from the mat and leaves the ring, seemingly confused. Killer double team D and whip D into the ropes and hit a 3-D. Jack goes for the pin and Mexican counts 1-2-3! Winner and new tag team champions: Jack and Cereal killer AT: Wow, that was pretty crazy! Hmm..I wonder what Woof was thinking? Jobber: That this show sucks! AT: Anyway, on our first show, we have new tag champs, what is gonna happen next! Blake Wolfson’s music hits and he walks to the ring, mic in hand. Blake: It seems that we need a #1 contender for the world title. Well, it seems that the two most logical men to fill this job are me and X-Factor. I would like to issue a challenge to X-Factor next week. The winner will become the NEW #1 contender for Mikey’s title! Unless your little British punk ass can’t handle it! X-Factor’s music hits and he walks onto the ramp X: Let’s face it. The Aussie’s began as British prisoners. Basically, what I am saying is that you are the crap the British did not want! So hell yea, I will beat your ass next week! AT: Well there you have it, next week’s main event! Jobber: Big whoop-de-do! Where is the Japanesse guy when you need him. Who cares about the British and the Aussies! Blitz’s music hits and he walks down to the ring Blitz: Well, do to the actions earlier in the tag team division, there will be a tourney to determine the next tag team #1 contenders. The people in this tourney will be: Raj and Tony v. the Technicians (next week) Admiral John and Toto v. Bluto and a mystery partner (also next week) The winners will face off at the show before the PPV. Well, I hope you enjoyed tonight sh… D26’s music hits and he walks to the ring D26: No, before this ends, I want to say this to Woof, I don’t know what you were thinking, but I am going to beat some sense into you next week! I am here now to ask you, Blitz, one thing… Blitz: What is that? D26: Make the winner of our match #1 contender for the IC title. Blitz: On one condition, the IC champion, Mr. Mikey, will be the referee. D26: You got it. Woof, get ready for: (crowd chants along): the whole F’N Show! fade to black, Show ends
