Bob springs off them and runs at Jack, just barely ducking under big boot from the champ, continuing off to the other side. Bob then bounces off the ropes and runs at Jack with a clothesline, but Jack runs under it to the ropes, as Bob runs through to the other side once again. Both men bounce off the ropes at the same time and head towards each other. They meet in the center of the ring with Jack hitting a HUGE spear on Bob that practically rips his body in half. Jack then makes a quick cover on Bob as the ref slides in... One... Two... Kick out! BJ: Damn! I thought Jack might have had it with that one. AT: It's going to take a lot more than that to keep Bob down, and Jack knows that. Jack gets to his feet and lifts Bob up with him via his hair. Jack sets Bob up for, and hits, a beautiful pumphandle slam on Bob, taking even more energy from him. Jack then lifts Bob to his feet once again, and grabs a hold of him from the side and lefts him off his feet, locking in the Eternity in Desolation (Side on Bear Hug). Bob begins to scream out in pain as Jack continues to squeeze the life out of him with his massive arms. Bob tries to fight the hold off, but Jack just keeps on squeezing tighter and tighter, pulling more and more energy out of Bob with every passing second, until his body starts to go limp in the arms of the Ripper. The ref comes over to Bob and grabs his arm lifting it into the air. He drops Bob's arm once, then lifts it once again as the crowd starts to get behind him. Bob's arm drops a second time, and as the ref grabs his arm, Naron starts pounding on the mat in unison with the crowd's stomping feet. The ref drops his arm a third time, but it doesn't go all the way down! The crowd erupts in cheers as Bob takes an elbow to the side of Jacks head. Jack holds on, and squeezes even tighter than anyone would think was humanly possible, but Bob fires back with a second elbow, then a third, causing the Ripper to slouch over and let Bob's feet touch the floor. Bob then hits Jack with a couple more elbows, which finally get the champ to release the hold. Bob then grabs Jack by the head, and plants him into the mat with a vicious DDT. That gives Bob a chance to catch his breath. Bob then slowly pulls himself to his feet as Jack begins to stir. Jack finally gets to his feet and leans on the ropes for a second, looking over at Bob. The first thing he sees, however, is Bob running at him. Bob hits Jack with a stiff clothesline that sends both men toppling over the ropes and crashing down to the floor below. Naron runs over to check on Bob, but doesn't stay long when she sees that not only is he okay, but Jack is picking himself up to his feet. Both men get to their feet and Bob takes a swing at Jack, but the Ripper blocks his punch. Jack then lays a shot into Bob, then a second, before whipping him into the steel ring post, busting Bob open. Jack then picks Bob up and lifts him over his head in a body press, before walking over to the guardrail, and dropping him right on top of it. Jack then rolls into the ring, and right back out in order to break the count by the ref. The XUW World Champion walks over to the timekeeper and throws him out of the chair he was sitting on, before picking it up and folding it. He walks over to Bob and lifts the chair high above his head over Bob, who has fallen forward from the guardrail and is now on his hands and knees. Jack goes to swing the chair, but it gets ripped out of his hands from behind. Jack turns around and sees that Naron is the one that took the chair from him. AT: Oh no! Run Naron! BJ: Yeah, probably not the smartest move on her part. Naron quickly turns around and starts to run from Jack, who decides to pursue her around the ring. Naron turns a corner and can be seen tossing the chair forward, and she ducks as Bob catches the chair in mid air and swings it at Jack as he turns the corner, waffling him with the chair, and busting him open as well. Bob then takes his turn to slide in the ring, and right back out to break the count once again. Bob picks the chair back up and proceeds to smash Jack repeatedly while he is down with the chair, much to the delight of the crowd... hey, the XUW crowd is a strange bunch. After about four or five hits with the chair, Bob drops it to the side and rolls Jack back into the ring. Bob then climbs to the top rope and holds his hands up as the crowd goes absolutely nuts. Bob then jumps off the top rope with a flying head butt, which connects! Bob then hooks a leg as the crowd chants along... One... Two... Foot on the Ropes! BJ: YES!!! AT: That was amazing... how the hell could Jack kick out after the beating he took from that chair, AND the head butt from Bob? BJ: Jack is no ordinary wrestler... that's why he's the champ. Bob looks shocked that Jack kicked out, as he wipes the blood out of his eyes and the crowd lets out a huge groan. Bob then pulls Jack back up to his feet and in an amazing feat of strength, Bob hits Jack with a gut wrench suplex. Bob quickly picks Jack up once again, and in another amazing feat of strength, Bob hits Jack with his new finishing move, the Old School Suspension (Cradle Brainbuster), which gets a HUGE reaction from the crowd and leaves Jack looking completely lifeless. Bob then hooks a leg once more as the crowd chants again... One... Two... Shoulder up! AT: Jack is just refusing to stay down! It seems like Bob is going to have to kill him to take that belt away from him! BJ: That's Jack for you he refuses to quit. It's not an easy quest to beat that man by any means! The crowd lets out a huge groan again as Bob looks completely shocked once again and checks with the ref. The ref reassures to Bob that the match must still go on, and Bob shakes his head before picking himself back up to his feet. Bob looks down at Jack, and grabs his legs once again, before locking him in a second figure four! This time Jack can't make it to the ropes as easily, and screams out in pain as the crowd cheers for their hero. Jack then realizes he can't make it to the ropes and slowly tries to turn over on his stomach to put the pressure on Bob, but his first try fails. Jack then tries a second time, and this time he manages to turn the hold over! Now it's Bob that is screaming out in pain, while Jack gets a sick smile on his face that can be easily seen through his crimson mask. Bob reaches out for the ropes, but it way too far from them; so instead, he just rolls over once more and gets the advantage back in the hold. The crowd begins to scream like crazy again as Naron starts pounding the mat to cheer Bob on. However, Jack wastes no time rolling over once again, and getting his advantage back! The ref checks on Bob, not even noticing that through all the rolling, it brought Jack closer to the ropes, which he is now using for leverage against the aged knight. Naron sees this from across the ring, and quickly makes her way over to Jack. Bob lifts his hand up like he is about to tap, but Naron comes face to face with Jack and slaps him right in the face, causing him to break the hold! BJ: Naron is really asking for trouble tonight, isn't she? AT: She is just making sure that this is a clean fight! Nothing wrong with that. BJ: She should be minding her own damn business! After Jack releases the hold he slides out of the ring and grabs Naron from behind before she can get a chance to run away. He spins her around and starts yelling at her, to which she looks extremely frightened. Jack then gives her a swift kick to the gut and puts her in position for a RipSaw! The crowd starts to boo like crazy and throw insults out at the champ. He just looks out at them with a sick smile on his face, which is still covered in blood from before. Jack reaches down and grabs Naron around the waist, but before he can pick her up, Bob jumps off the apron and hits Jack with a double axe handle right to the lower back, causing him to release Naron. Jack then turns to Bob and the two start trading blows once again. Jack blocks one of Bob's punches and proceeds to whip him right into the guardrail once again. Jack then pulls Bob over to the announce table and gives him a swift kick to the gut and puts him in a RipSaw position. BJ: No, no, no! Go to the Spanish announce table, Jack! AT: No, don't do this at all, Jack! Jack pays no attention to the announcers and lifts Bob up onto his shoulders, before turning to the side and sending Bob crashing through the announce table with a RipSaw! Jack looks up at the ref who is in the middle of counting nine, and Jack runs to the ring, sliding into the ring and breaking the count right before the ref could get to ten. Jack then slides out of the ring, and picks up Bob's almost lifeless body and slides him back into the ring. Jack lifts Bob back up to his feet, and gives him another kick to the gut. Jack lifts Bob up and hits him with a second RipSaw in the middle of the ring! Jack hooks a leg as the ref slides in and the crowd boos... One... Two... Shoulder Up! AT: Amazing! Two RipSaws in a row, and one was through our table, and Bob still refuses to quit! BJ: How the hell did he kick out of that?!?!? Jack looks completely stunned in the middle of the ring as the crowd erupts in cheers. Jack gets to his feet and turns to Bob, laying a few kicks into the downed hero. Jack then lifts Bob back up to his feet. Jack goes for another kick to the gut, which he hits, causing Bob to double over once again. Jack pulls him in and gets set to hit a third RipSaw on Bob, but Bob counters the move into a back body drop, which gets a HUGE reaction from the crowd. Jack grabs his back as soon as he hits the ground and pulls himself back up to his feet. Jack turns around to face Bob, and this time he gets a swift kick to the gut. Bob then grabs Jack and hits him with a second Old School Suspension! The crowd cheers like crazy as Bob goes for another cover. One... Two... Foot on the Ropes!!! But the ref doesn't see it! Three!!! Winner and NEW XUW World Champion - Samurai Bob!!! AT: He did it! Bob beat The Ripper! BJ: Nooooo! He cheated! "F*ck That!" rips through the PA system once again and the fans start to go absolutely crazy as Bob celebrates in the ring with the belt and Naron by his side, while Jack starts to argue with the ref. Voice: Stop the music! Stop the damn music right now! BJ: Thank God, Sirius is here to make things right. Sirius: Referee, you reverse that decision right now! The Ripper clearly had his foot on the ropes! The ref is in the ring expressing the fact that he is not going to reverse his decision while Bob and Naron roll their eyes. Other voice: Now hold up here a second, Sirius. AT: Good, Mr. Webster will set things straight now. Mr. Webster: Now that is an XUW certified referee in the ring and I will back his decision. The call will not be reversed and Bob is now the new World Champion. A HUGE pop from the crowd fills the arena. Sirius: Well, it seems as though a conspiracy has befallen the AoD now. Looks like you better keep your lawyers close by you, as you may just have another lawsuit on your hands. Sirius gets an evil smile on his face as Mr. Webster starts to show signs of concern at how the word 'conspiracy' has been getting thrown around lately. BJ: Exactly! It's all a conspiracy! AT: That is nonsense, I agree with Mr. Webster's decision one hundred percent. BJ: Yeah, you would! "Heroes" by David Bowie then rips through the PA system and another loud crowd pop can be heard. Henrik steps out onto the stage with a mic in his hand. Henrik: Webster, your king is tired of all these games! Will you please tell me how this is going to affect my World Title shot at Reborn? Mr. Webster seems to be looking even more concerned now, as the stress starts to get to him. Mr. Webster: I'll, umm; I'll have my final decision for you soon. Henrik: And just when is soon?
