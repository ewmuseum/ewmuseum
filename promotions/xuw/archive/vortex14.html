XUW Vortex #14 (2002)

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Vortex - Number 14 Pyro flies, the same old indecipherable lyrics play, and several women take down their tops, much to the delight of the cameraman. Yes, ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, it’s here, it’s live, it’s XUW Vortex! The camera pans round the screaming audience, and once more comes to rest upon our tried and true commentators, Atomic Tomato and Barry Johnson. AT: “Hello there and welcome to XUW Vortex! This promises to be a night full of excitement, so let’s get straight to work.” Hardcore Match: JP Blood v Rujasu “Lost” plays and JP Blood makes his way down to the ring to a smattering of boos. Some generic rock music by some generic hip band plays, and Rujasu walks down the ramp to join JP. The crowd give him a slightly stronger chorus of boos. AT: “Well, I must say, Rujujubee is looking a little more in focus than usual.” BJ: “That’s Rujajabu, and yes, he looks like he means business!” Ruj starts off by whipping JP to the ropes and catching him in a big Spinebuster as he comes back. He immediately gets down to work and locks in a Figure Four Leg Lock on the hardcore veteran. AT: “Well, Ruj is taking this to JP, whether it’s a hardcore match or not!” JP crawls to the ropes, and out under them, climbing outside of the ring. Ruj quickly follows him, and as JP gets up Ruj hits him with a Dropkick. Ruj goes underneath the ring and returns with a trash can lid. As JP gets up, Ruj smacks it down hard over his head. JP goes down to one knee, and Ruj smacks him once more, knocking him to the floor. JP staggers to his feet again, and Ruj drops the lid, before grabbing JP and hitting a Piledriver onto the trash can lid! He covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Say what you like about him, JP Blood is a hardcore performer! Ruj is quickly on JP again, and goes for the R-Driver, but JP counters and hits a DDT. Ruj gets to his feet, and JP hits a Samoan Drop. He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Ruj gets up, looking angry, and nails JP down with a Clothesline. He slides outside and picks up a steel chair. JP looks to hit a Baseball Slide to the outside, but Ruj dodges and cracks the chair down across his legs. Ruj slides back into the ring and waits for JP to get back inside. As he does, Ruj smashes the chair down on his head. JP looks out for the count. Ruj looks down at his opponents, and uses all his strength to lift him up, hook the arms, and hit the R-Driver. JP is laid out, unconscious, on the mat. Ruj looks down and then looks up at the crowd, and raises an eyebrow. AT: “Hell, the crowd are starting to applaud Rijujabababee!” BJ: “It’s reluctant applause, but dammit, he’s putting on a fantastic match!” Rujasu picks up the limp body of JP once more, and hits a slam to lay him down. He leans over and wraps up JP into the Philly Cloverleaf. The ref checks JP, and with his last strength, he taps… Winner, by submission, Rujasu!!! AT: “That’s one hell of an impressive performance by Rujasu!” The crowd starts to applaud as Rujasu makes his way to the back. BJ: “That’s a change of heart from the crowd, it’s about time they respected Rajujujabbajabba, he’s the future!” AT: “Okay, just say whatever makes you happy. The rest of us will get on with the show!” Tag Match: Doggy Style v The Technicians “We are the Champions” plays, ironically, as Wolfson and Woof Woof make their way to the ring. “The Game” plays, and two of the four horsemen come down to the ring, it’s The Technicians! Wolfson starts off with BC, and gets the upper hand early on. Irish Whip by Wolfson, and then a Swinging Neckbreaker. BC gets up quickly and hits a Twisting Spinebuster. Wolfson is down, and BC goes for the early pin. 1 … … KICKOUT! Wolfson is up, and goes for a clothesline on BC, but BC ducks and hits a Reverse Shoulder Breaker on Wolfson. Wolfson struggles to his feet, and BC looks for a Superkick, but Blake ducks. BC turns round and gets hit by the Doggy Dropper. AT: “Wow, that twisting spinebuster sure seems to be popular in this match!” Blake tags in Woof, and he goes to work on BC, hitting a Doggy Style Driver. He covers. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Woof sets up BC for the Wag The Dog, but BC powers out and hits a Brainbuster. BC crawls to the corner, and just gets the tag ahead of Woof’s despairing dive. KsC charges into the ring like a man possessed, and nails Woof with The Critical Shot, almost tearing Woofie in half! KsC goes for the pin straight away. 1 … … 2 … … NO! Wolfson enters the ring and breaks up the pin. The ref ushers him out, and KsC goes to the top rope, looking to hit the Flying Headbutt. He soars off the turnbuckle, but Woof rolls away at the last minute and KsC hits the mat hard. Woof tags in Wolfson, and he sees his chance. He waves at the crowd and looks for the CRIKEY! The crowd chant along… “C-R-I-K-E-Y” Blake tries to worm his way across the ring, and fails miserably, allowing KsC to get up and hit a series of hard kicks to Wolfson as he lies on the floor, looking foolish. KsC tags in BC, who whips Wolfson to the corner and sets him up for the Nail In The Coffin, but Wolfson powers out and shoves BC out of the corner. BC charge back in, and Wolfson runs away, BC’s momentum sending him right into the turnbuckle at high speed. He goes down and Wolfson comes back, locking in the Leg Humper on BC. BC manages to crawl over to KsC and tags him in, with Wolfson unaware. KsC smacks Wolfson in the back of the head and tries to apply the “What the f***”, but Woofie runs into the ring and hits him with a hard Lariat, and follows it up with the Wag The Dog. KsC goes down hard. BC comes in as Blake stands up, and he hits Blake hard with a Superkick. Woofie slides out of the ring and pulls BC with him, as Wolfson theatrically falls down right on top of KsC. The ref has no choice but to count… 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!! Winners by pinfall, Doggy Style!!! AT: “Dammit, they were lucky!” BJ: “Nonsense, that’s true teamwork!” AT: “Wolfson doesn’t even know where he is!” BJ: “That’s nothing new though.” AT: “True.” Doggy Style make their way up the ramp, as the XUW announcer makes it known Announcer: “As a result of winning this match, Doggy Style will go on to meet the tag champions at April Annihilation!” AT: “Wow, a chance to regain the titles, they’re gonna be stoked!” BJ: “Huh?” Tag Match: Sidhe Nimh/Kata v Hodgo/Henrik The horsemens’ music plays, and they enter the ring, and then “Rule Britannia” plays, with Hodgo, and a heavily strapped Henrik making their way to the ring. AT: “We have to remember that Henrik is still feeling the effects of the crash caused by that mad Jock!” Hodgo starts the match with Sidhe, and Sidhe quickly gains the upper hand with some stiff Forearms to the face of the Commonwealth member. Sidhe follows it up with a T-Bone Suplex, and a quick cover, his arm pushing down on Hodgo’s throat. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Sidhe throws Hodgo to the ropes and hits a Reverse Elbow as Hodgo comes back. Hodgo goes down and Sidhe quickly flips up to the top rope and hits a big Leg Drop. He once more covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Hodgo stumbles around, but as Sidhe advances, Hodgo dives forward and hits a Diving Clothesline. Sidhe goes down briefly, and Hodgo uses the respite to tag in the heavily strapped Henrik. Henrik advance on Sidhe, but is immediately hit with the Bad Trip. Sidhe looks down on Henrik and tags in Kata. Kata sees Henrik on the floor and locks on an STF. AT: “Henrik is hurt and Kata never even let him get up!” Henrik refuses to tap, and Kata soon lets him go, but looks disdainfully as Henrik gets to his feet very slowly. As he does so, Kata hits a Chop Block to Henrik’s leg, and locks Half Crab on. The crowd seem to be giving Kata heat for picking on the injured Henrik. BJ: “I never thought I’d see a horseman get this reception for doing his job!” Kata never eases up on the hold, but Henrik struggles his way to the ropes. AT: “It’s funny, but now I notice it, Naron and Sheri are here, but where the hell is Kat, she’s normally here for Sidhe’s matches” BJ: “I don’t know, but from what I’ve seen this week it almost looks as if she left him!” AT: “Ah, that’s explain the intensity of Sidhe…” Henrik gets up and aims a kick at Kata, but he catches it easily. Kata laughs at Henrik, but he hits an Enzeguri, and tags in Hodgo. Hodgo charges at Kata and levels him with a Flying Sidekick. Kata gets back up as Hodgo advances with a series of stiff Anar-Kicks. Kata is batter down, and Hodgo ascends the second turnbuckle and hits The Molotov Cocktail. Kata is down and Hodgo goes for the pin. 1 … … 2 … … NO! Sidhe kicks Hodgo and breaks up the pin. Hodgo gets up and hits a DDT on Kata. Hodgo then lifts Kata up and sends him to the ropes with an Irish Whip. Sidhe gets the blind tag, as Hodgo stops Kata and hits the Riot Breaker. Hodgo looks pleased with himself, and then turns into Sidhe, who hits the Bad Trip. Hodgo is down, and Sidhe goes up top to hit the Candy Flip. He covers. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winners, by pinfall, Sidhe Nimh/Kata Hajime!!! AT: “That was a great match, marred by the fact that Henrik was injured, but what an effort by the Commonwealth leader.” BJ: “He’ll be getting a lot more respect after that!” The men make their way to the back and the next match is all set up to start. TV Title Match: Bluto (C) v “The Comedian” Chris Blake “Fight for your Right” plays and Bluto comes down to the ring, beers in hand, smiling from ear to ear. “Crazy Train” then plays, and “The Comedian” Chris Blake comes down the ramp, to a chorus of boos. Comedian starts of with a series of rights hands to Bluto, and then whips him to the ropes. Bluto comes back hard and just runs through Comedian. Comedian gets back up, and Bluto hits The Keg Stand. He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Comedian gets back up, and hits the advancing Bluto with a few rights, and then a kick to the gut, followed by The Punch Line. He covers. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Bluto gets back out, but Comedian hits a few more rights and forces Bluto back into the corner, and hitting the Ten Drink Minimum. Bluto goes down and Comedian locks in The Killing Joke. Bluto yells, and Comedian pulls back harder and harder. Bluto edges forwards and eventually gets to the ropes. Comedian has to release the hold, and Bluto holds onto the ropes for balance. Comedian comes on the attack again, looking for a clothesline, but Bluto ducks and hits an Edge-O-Matic. Bluto then goes up top and hits The Bluto Bomb, and goes for the pin. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Comedian just gets out in time, and gets to his feet carefully. Bluto takes a run up and buries a knee to the midsection. Comedian goes down and rolls to the outside. He complains to the ref as he does so, and the ref goes outside to try and shepherd Comedian back in to the ring. AT: “Bluto looks to have the upper hand so far… Hey, look, there’s Kat!” Kat wanders down the ramp towards the ring, seemingly disinterested in the happenings around her. She slides into the ring, and Bluto looks quizzically at her. He goes to show her the way out of the ring, but she turns and viciously lashes out with a kick to the unmentionables. Bluto winces, and Kat follows up with The Ninth Gate. She then calmly rolls out of the ring and walks over to the Comedian. The ref finally notices her and sends her backstage, with security to escort her. Kat hisses, and lashes out with a knife, cutting one of the guards. AT: “What the hell was that all about?” Comedian rolls back into the ring, and much to his delight, sees Bluto prostrate on the floor. He picks up Bluto and hits The Last Laugh. Comedian hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winner, by pinfall, and NEW XUW TELEVISION CHAMPION: “The Comedian” Chris Blake!!!!! AT: “What the hell was Kat doing out here? What problem does she have with Bluto? Where’s Sidhe? I’m confused!” BJ: “I think we all are, but we have a new TV Title holder, and the AoD finally has some gold back in their camp!” AT: “Indeed they do, and who knows what they have in store for the future…” Singles Match: Lance v Samurai Bob Lance’s music plays and he makes his way to the ring, very businesslike. “F*** that” hits the speakers and the crowd trample each other to death to get a view of Samurai Bob as he walks down to the ring. Bob and Lance lock up. They try and push each other back, but to no avail. Lance tries to drop down and grab a leg, but Bob matches it. Bob tries to go over the tope and wrestle Lance to the floor, but Lance stands tall and doesn’t allow Bob to get the leverage. AT: “Man, this is a close, technical fight” They break the lock up, and Lance suddenly dives for Bob’s legs. Bob tries to raise a knee, but Lance avoids it, and soon has Bob off his feet, and an Ankle Lock applied. Bob grits his teeth and starts to edge towards the ropes. Upon reaching them Lance releases the hold, and the two men go back to circling each other. AT: “This seems to be a more in focus Lance than we saw last week against Bluto.” Bob grabs Lance and hits a Snap Suplex. He quickly capitalises, and hits another Snap Suplex, followed by a DDT. Bob goes for the early cover. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Lance gets up and aims a Superkick at Bob’s head, but he avoids it and hits a Side Suplex. Lance is down, but not for long as he springs back to his feet from down on the floor, and hits Bob with a Clothesline. Bob gets up into a Hip Toss, and Lance tries to get an STF locked in, but Bob wriggles his way to the ropes. Lance releases the hold, and hits a Vertical Suplex as Bob gets back up. Lance looks for The Serious Submission, but Bob is wise to it, and kicks the hands away from the leg. Bob gets up and hits a Gord Buster, leaving Lance lying on the floor. Bob goes up top and looks for the Kendo Killer, but Lance darts away, and takes advantage of Bob’s mistake, hitting a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. In a rare move, Lance then moves to the top rope, looking for an acrobatic kick, but as he jumps off, the turnbuckle gives way beneath him, and he falls awkwardly to the floor, smacking his head hard on the mat. Bob grabs the near dead weight of Lance and carries him up for the Saigon Snapp. He hits it and pins Lance. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner, by pinfall, Samurai Bob AT: “Man, I’d have loved to have seen how that would have gone if that damn turnbuckle hadn’t given out. I think Lance could have had him!” BJ: “It would have been a lot closer, I can assure you!” Bob celebrates in the ring, but Lance gets back up and lays into him, hitting a Spear and a series of right hands to the face, which bust Bob wide open AT: "Dammit, that's not right. Lance lost, okay, so the turnbuckle gave out, but it's a risk you take, there's no need for that!" Camera cuts backstage to where Bluto is drowning his sorrows with a cooler full of beer. Bluto (sings): “Nobody knows the trouble I’ve seen…” Supa Thug Esco comes into shot and gives Bluto a look of utter disdain and contempt. Esco: “You be so whack, knaaaaaamean!” Bluto: “Gimme a break. Mmmm drunk!” Esco: “You be no title holder, fool!” Bluto: “Don’t push me, man, I’m fit to burst!” Esco: “Oh dawg, you be so whack yo mama be savin’ up to get you shot to save da family name!” Bluto rises up and smashes the cans around Esco’s head, in a CAN-Chairto! He then sits back down and starts mumbling to himself. Esco gets up and starts trying to hit Bluto, but security has arrived and splits the two up… Cut back to the announcers. AT: “Interesting, but now for the Main Event. Talk about history! There are so many different feuds encompassed in this one match!” Tag Match: DDT/Jack The Ripper v HBK/Sakie “One Armed Scissor” plays and the Army of Darkness comes to the stage, and then down to the ring. The crowd boo them heartily, though not too much, in case they’re looking closely. Sakie’s music plays and he comes down to the ring, watching the AoD closely as he waits for his partner. HBK’s new music plays and he comes down the ring, with a pop around the same level of Sakie’s, which was big. DDT and HBK look to start off the match. DDT advances on HBK, but he hits a Drop Toe Hold and then a Side Suplex as DDT arises. DDT gets up, and hits HBK with a forearm to the face, and then from nowhere nails the Shock Treatment! He covers. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! DDT tags in Jack, who mocks HBK as he gets up. Jack hits The Break, and then casually pins HBK. 1 … … 2 KICKOUT! Jack looks slightly surprised, but then picks up HBK, whips him to the corner, and tags DDT back in. DDT goes to work with some right hands, and then a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. HBK goes down hard, and DDT covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “I’m amazed to see HBK manhandled like this!” DDT tags Jack back in, and he kicks HBK in the gut, signalling for The Ripsaw, but HBK reverses and hits a Back Body Drop. Jack gets up and walks straight into Sweet Chin Music from HBK! HBK slowly covers Jack. 1 … … 2 … … NO! DDT dives in to make the save! HBK gets up and throws DDT out of the ring, before going back to pinning Jack. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! BJ: “Man, that was close!” HBK finally tags in Sakie, who has been raring to go. Jack stumbles up, and Sakie hits a Buzzkiller! He goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Jack once more gets to his feet. Sakie attempts a Flying Cross body, but Jack catches him and hits a big Fall Away Slam. Sakie gets up slowly, and Jack sends him to the ropes, hitting a Big Boot as he returns. Sakie still gets straight up, much to Jack’s annoyance, and as Jack looks for another Big Boot, Sakie sneaks in under the leg and hits a Death Valley Driver. Sakie looks for the cover, but Jack rolls away and tags in DDT. DDT goes straight on the offensive, and hits a Short Arm Clothesline. As Sakie goes down DDT is moving already, and locks in an STF. Sakie refuses to tap, and slowly edges his way to the ropes. DDT releases the hold, and goes to tag in Jack, but Sakie grabs his leg, and gets up, hitting a Dragon Screw Leg Whip. DDT goes down, and Sakie goes up top, before hitting “The Joke’s On You”. He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … NO! Jack comes in to the ring and makes the save. As Sakie turns to face him, Jack picks Sakie up and hits an enormous Call of Destiny. The ref ushers jack from the ring, but the damage has been done. Both legal men try to crawl back to their corners, and both tag at the same time. Jack rushes HBK, but gets knocked down once, twice, three times to big right hands. HBK locks up with Jack and uses his agility to perform an Arm Drag Takedown. He holds on for an Armbar, but Jack powers out and hits a big Spear on HBK. Jack motions for HBK to get up, and as he does, Jack nails the kick to the gut, and delivers a huge RipSaw on HBK. AT: “Well, that’s goodnight for the Phoenix!” Jack smiles, and covers HBK. 1 … … 2 … … NO! Sakie comes in to make the save as HBK rolls over. AT: “Damn! I swear HBK just kicked out!” BJ: “Nonsense, Sakie made the save!” AT: “I’m telling you, HBK kicked out of the damn RipSaw!” BJ: “NOBODY kicks out of that move, your eyes need testing.” Jack gets up and throws Sakie back to his corner. HBK gets up slowly, and Jack hits The Break. He pulls HBK up again, and once more does the kick to the gut, and the RipSaw. Jack smiles and walks over to Sakie, taunting him. Sakie gets infuriated and climbs into the ring, to get ushered out by the ref. Jack picks up the limp HBK as the ref has his back turned, and DDT starts to choke him on the tag rope. Jack merely laughs and kicks HBK in the gut one more time, hitting The Break once again. Jack goes down to one knee and pins HBK easily with just one hand. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “What the hell! That’s unpossible!” BJ: “Dear God, he’s a fool! He’s a crazy, superhuman fool! Give up now, else The Ripper will destroy you!” Jack looks puzzled at HBK’s continual resistance, and picks up the nearly broken Phoenix again. He hits a Stalled Vertical Suplex, and goes for the pin again, this time hooking the leg. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Jack picks up HBK once more, looking for the RipSaw to end it all, but HBK drops down and pulls his legs from underneath him. Jack falls down and HBK locks on The Heartbreaker. Jacks yells out, but can soon power free of it, as HBK’s strength diminishes. Jack aims for a Clothesline, but HBK ducks and gets a blind tag from Sakie. HBK hits a Dropkick on Jack, and then whips him to the ropes and follows through. DDT blind tags Jack just as HBK follow on and with the last of his strength hits a colossal Clothesline that sends both men over the top rope and to the outside. Back in the ring, Sakie and DDT are going at it again. Sakie looks for a Superkick, but DDT ducks and hits a Neckbreaker. DDT looks for the Shock Treatment, but Sakie reverses it and suddenly hits a Sakbottom! He covers DDT. 1 … … 2 … … NO! From nowhere, Jack’s arm snakes out and pulls the ref into the ring post. The ref doesn’t see who did it, and slumps into unconsciousness. Jack stands up to enter the ring, but HBK summons it all up for one last push and spears the back of Jack, sending him chest first into the ring steps, and both men to the floor. In the ring, Sakie is looking on with dismay as he has seen victory temporarily snatched from his grasp. Unseen to him, Rob The Throb has run through the crowd and up into the ring. Sakie hears the crowd and turns into the Throbbing Headache! Sakie slumps down as DDT covers and the ref suddenly comes back to life. He crawls over and counts, so so slowly. … … 1 … … … 2 … … … … KICKOUT! The crowd pops huge for the kickout, but DDT gets up quickly, and as Sakie rises, he gets a kick to the gut and DDT hits the Creeping Death. He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … … … 3!!!!! Winners, by pinfall, DDT/Ripper!!! AT: “Dammit, Army of Darkness stole another one! I swear, they’ll get their comeuppance! Tune into Xposure and Mayhem this week to see what other matches will be set up for April Annihilation!” Fade to Black
