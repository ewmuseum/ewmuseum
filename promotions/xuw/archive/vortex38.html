XUW Vortex #38

10 Oct 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex The show begins as thousands of wild, insane fans crowd another nameless arena in Somewhere, U.S.A. for the latest edition of xUw Thursday Night Vortex! Crowds fill the arena, saying things like "Kata Hajime for President", "Sheri Dedmon stole my clothes!" and of course, the oh-so-true "..." Then, the announcers are shown at ringside. Atomic Tomato: "Welcome everyone to xUw Vortex, I'm the Atomic Tomato, and with me as always, my arch-rival 'Mr. Majestic' Barry Johnson!" Barry Johnson: "Ah, shaddap Cucumber... It's like this guys and gals, we're fresh off of one of the most magnificent Pay-Per-View Supercards in the history of xUw, with six titles changing hands!" AT: "That's right Barry! X-Factor, the new World Heavyweight Champion! Saij, the new InterContinental Heavyweight Champion! Samurai Bob, the new Submission Champion, will defend tonight! But first, the new World Television Champion, Rujasu defends against the one without fear, let's get this show underway!" NFM  vs.  Rujasu © xUw World Television Championship "Fear" by Disturbed hits and the crowd cheers as the challenger makes his way to ringside to a decent pop. He gets in the ring, and the crowd response turns sour as some Bargain Basement Generic Crap music hits the sound system, as Rujasu, the Future, and new TV Champ makes his way to ringside. BJ: "Check out our new TV Champion!" AT: "He's a good one, as this is his second reign as Television Champ, but will he have the skill, and determination, to fend off the fearless?" BJ: "Who the hell are you talking to?" The music fades, and the bell rings as this opening title match gets underway, as the two men start off with a Collar-and-Elbow Tieup. Rujasu quickly manuevers into a Side Headlock, then he spins around, and in one fluid motion, nails a Russian Legsweep. Rujasu is up quickly, and he applies a Rear Chinlock to NFM. NFM soon gets up, and Rujasu adjusts his Chinlock into another Side Headlock. NFM hits a Side Elbowsmash to the gut of the Champ, breaking the hold. NFM then bounces off the ropes, but Rujasu takes him down with a Shoulderblock. Rujasu then hits the opposite ropes, NFM starts to get up, but then ducks down as Rujasu leaps over, hits the ropes, and this time, NFM gets to his feet, and NFM tres to take Rujasu over with an Armdrag Takedown, but Rujasu counters, spins around, and executes a nice Hiptoss. This doesn't keep NFM down long, but as NFM gets up, Rujasu kicks him in the gut, and drops him with a DDT. The cover... 1... 2... shoulder up by the Challenger. AT: "Rujasu is doing well here in this opening contest." BJ: "Funny thing is, everytime someone says that, momentum changes." Sure, enough, as soon as Rujasu picks up NFM with a Front Facelock, NFM hits a couple of knees to the gut to break the hold, then the two trade punches for a bit, before NFM takes over with repeated unches. He then backs Rujasu up against the ropes, then goes for an Irish Whip, but NFM pulls him back, and hits a Kitchen Sink Kneelift. NFM then gets a short bounce off the ropes, before hitting a Driving Elbowdrop (Steve Austin/Bret Hart style). He then leaves his elbow on Rujasu's throat, and chokes him with it as the official counts... 1... 2... 3... 4... NFM breaks right before the count of 5, then hits an elbow to the forehead of the Champion. He stands as the crowd cheers a bit. BJ: "See Peach, what did I tell you?" AT: "And what did I tell you about calling me 'Peach' ?" NFM then picks up Rujasu, and Rujasu hits a couple of shots to the 'lower abdomen' before bounceing off the ropes, when NFM ducks down as to go for a Back Bodydrop, but Rujasu sees the telegraphed move, and kicks NFM in the chin. NFM stumbles back, and Rujasu reaches in for a Bodyslam, but NFM escapes it, then looks to be going for a Slam of his own, but instead, he positions the Champ, and NFM hits the Lightning Strike (Jumping Tombstone Piledriver), as the crowd cheers. NFM covers... 1... 2... 3! The bell rings, and the crowd pops huge! BJ: "Look! New Champion!" The winner of this bout... and... NEW xUw World Television Champion... No Fear Man! AT: "NFM, one of the top Hardcore men in this company has secured for himself a non-Hardcore title, will he be ready to defend against all challengers, every night?! Wait, what is this?" "Hello, Time Bomb" hits the arena PA system and out walk the new tag team champions accompanied by Lizzy to loud boos.  They slowly walk down to the ring, pointing to their belts around their waists. AT:  What do these guys want now? I can't believe that they are coming out here after what happened on Sunday and the shameless way of winning the titles. BJ:  Hey, you would have done the same thing to overcome xenophobia. AT:  Xenophobia, yeah, right.  And I can't believe that little bitch, Lizzy is coming down with them.  Look at the smile on her face, she seems to be happy to be walking with those two. BJ:  She should be happy, she is privileged enough to be walking out with the new tag team champs.  She is with people who actually care about her. AT:  Care about her?  Gimme a break, all they care about is the tag titles. Bailey and Danyon enter into the ring while Lizzy walks over to a kid in the front row who has a sign that says "I'm a Legionaire."  Lizzy  shakes the kid's hands and then makes her way into the ring.  Bailey grabs a mic and begins to address the crowd. Bailey:  I said we would do it and we did it.  We won the tag titles on the weekend at Unknown Factors.  I told all the Legionaires out there that we would not lose this time.  I never disapoint and always stay true to my word.  But this weekend was even more triumphant for other reasons besides the tag titles. At the PPV, we overcame all difficulties and defeated xenophobia.  Once again, I overcame a conspiracy to hold me down.  This weekend was perhaps one of the greatest weekends in the NRL's history. Our legacy as being the greatest tag team in XUW history can not be denied by anyone now.  Dave Kipfer may say that he was robbed but I will show you Dave later on tonite that we were the rightful victors on the weekend. Danyon:  Boomer Sooners, on Sunday nite, your high life came down to a big crash.  You had three people at ringside and you still couldn't defeat us. You had xenophobia on your side and you still couldn't defeat us. But we had something on our side that you didn't know about.  We had a little back up plan that no one knew about.  I think you may know who this back up plan was.....she is very familiar to you all.....especially you Dave.  I would like to introduce the newest member of the NRL family.......Lizzy. Lizzy:  Why thank you Danyon.  Dave, and the rest of the Boomer Sooners, I know some of you maybe asking why I did what I did on Sunday nite.  But you see, I had to do it.  I had to prove a point to all of you.  I think it came to the point where you all took advantage of me and didn't care for me and my needs. However, these two guys do care.  They care a lot about me. The care about everything that I do and this big guy loves me a lot.  I can only be with people who care about me.  Dave, you stopped caring a long time ago as wrestling became more important to you.  I lived with it for a  little while hoping that you would come back to me but a girl can only wait for so long.  I deserve more. And now I am getting more.  Fatbadger, I am sorry I had to hit you with the hockey stick but you have to understand, a girl has to do what a girl has to do.  But tonite, Dave, I hope that you will get what you have coming towards you.  I know that Bailey is ready to go against you and give you the beating of a lifetime.  And I will be there at ringside watching it all and enjoying every second of it.  And one more thing.......... But she never finished her sentence as Bailey cut her off. Bailey:  Do you ever shut up?  We gave you some mic time and you won't stop talking.  I am tired of all of this and tired of you. Lizzy:  Huh?  Danyon what is he saying? Danyon:  He is saying that he is tired of having to listen to you. Lizzy:  You should do something about it......especially if we are going to be on the same team. Danyon:  I will do something about it.  I will shut you up and that should do it. Lizzy:  Huh, what?  I don't get it........what are you saying? BJ:  This is getting good. AT:  I don't know what is going on here.  Are the NRL turning on Lizzy? BJ:  Why don't you shut up so we can listen, Potato Head. AT:  You are the one who spoke first............. BJ:  Shhhhh....... Bailey:  What he and I are saying is that we are tired of your antics. We put up with them for awhile but I have had enough of them.  No wonder Dave stopped caring about you.....you are so annoying.  Everything is about you....you, you, you. Lizzy:  Bbbbb....bbb....bbb...ut, Danyon what about all you said before. Danyon:  About what I said, I lied.  I didn't care about you one bit. I never have and never did.  I only befriended you to get closer to the tag titles. That is the only thing that I care about.  There is reason why I am a greatest wrestler in the Southern Hemisphere.  Time and time again I use people to get what I want.  And this time it was you Lizzy. Lizzy:  I can't belive it. Bailey:  Well, believe it honey. I probably could have put up with your stuff but the look that you gave Dave as entered the xUw Arena today was just it.  I am too afraid that you would run back to him.  So, we are going to make it so that you can never run back to anyone. Lizzy:  I don't get it. Danyon:  He is saying that your services are no longer required, you are fired. And with that being said, Danyon grabs Lizzy and picks her up.  He sets her up into the cradle piledriver position as Bailey helps deliver the Hemispheric Connection (Spike Cradle Piledriver) on her. AT:  I can't believe what happened here.  I don't believe what I saw. BJ:  It was great, she had it coming to her. AT:  But before you said that...... BJ:  Whatever.........will you shut up. "One of a Kind" hits the PA system to a loud pop as Dave Kipfer comes running out from the back with a chair.  He runs down and in the ring. Bailey and Danyon quickly exit out of the back of the ring.  They walk around to the front and start up the ramp, pointing to their belts. Dave stands in the ring by the ropes and drops the chair.  He points at Bailey and Danyon.  Dave turns around and looks at the befallen Lizzy.  She is trying to get up.  She looks up at Dave. He walks over to her.  He looks at the crowd who are all wondering if he is going to help her up.  He extends his hand to help.......the crowd lets out a negative response.........He brings his hand back in.......the crowd cheers for him.  Dave smiles. Lizzy is on her feet now, she looks at Dave.  A tear flows down her cheek. She mouths that she is sorry.  Dave extends his hand towards her, showing a sign of good faith.  The crowd boos at Dave.  Lizzy takes his hand. Dave draws her closer to him.........but then gives her a huge lariat. And Lizzy falls down on the mat.  The crowd pops huge as "One of a Kind" hits the PA system once more.  Dave leaves the ring and leaves Lizzy there in the middle of the ring, wondering what just happened.  She starts to cry as he EMT's come out to help her. AT:  She definitely had that coming to her. BJ:  But Dave always said he loved her.....I guess we know that he is nothing but a liar now. AT:  One thing is for sure, there is no love lost between Dave and the New Royal Legion.  That is going to be one hell of a confrontation later tonight as Dave takes on Bailey in a singles match. Kata Hajime  vs.  Dev "Build Fear" by Soilent Green plays as Dev comes out to the ring first, he gets in the ring, and does the anti-pose, as the crowd boos. Then, the main lights dim, and red strobelights flash as "Guild of Mute Assassins" plays as the crowd cheers for Kata. As Kata gets in the ring, the mail lights come back up, and it seems as if Kata and Dev are having words in the ring. Not everything can be made out, but there is enough to tell that Kata is questioning Dev, both about his PPV attack, as the attack on Raven X, in which Kata's friend Sheri is the prime suspect. AT: "Some bad blood here. Dev ran in and attacked Kata during his match with the New Age Horsemen last Sunday night at Unknown Factors." BJ: "Ah, he's just bitter. Dev was going after KoRe, Kata was just at the wrong place at the wrong time... kinda like he is now." Something is said, and Dev punches Kata. Kata returns the punch, and the slugfest is on. Dev gets on a Modified Side Headlcok, while hitting some hard shots to the kidneys of Kata. After a few shots, Kata toses the Fighter off, and mounts him and nails some punches. After several seconds, Dev tosses his opponent off, and this match is looking like more of a UFC brawl than what you would see on most professional wrestling shows. AT: "Nothing technical or pretty about this one, I wonder what was said that set Dev off like that?" BJ: "He's just bitter, and Dev is making it even more sour." AT: "Hey, Kata is giving as good as he's getting." BJ: "It won't continue, Kata is good, but Dev is a true fighter... something someone like you would know nothing about." After rolling around, punching and kicking, KoRe runs in, and gets in on the fun in the ring. This one is thrown out by the referee. The Result of this match due to Outside Interference... No Contest. The fans don't like the ruling, but it doesn't stop as this has now become a three-way fight between Dev, Kata, and KoRe. After a few seconds, Dev and Kata look at each other, then they go to doubleteam KoRe. Dev carries the beaten KoRe over to a turnbuckle, and slams his face into it. Kata then grabs KoRe, and does the same. They takes turns, trying to out-do each other while talking trash. BJ: "I bet KoRe wishes he had never picked this fight." AT: "This is funny... both men are trying to out-do the other by slamming KoRe into the turnbuckle even harder than the last guy did." After about 10 times a piece, they decide this is getting nowhere, so they let the High-Roller go, and he collapses, while Dev and Kata begins talking trash in each other's faces, then they once again begin trading blows. Soon, referees and road agents rush the ring to break this one up. A couple of guys help out KoRe, while two men a piece hold Kata and Dev. AT: "It finally looks as if this thing has been brought under control." BJ: "Don't be so sure... look!" Dev hits off one guy, grabs the other in an Inverted Facelock, before hitting the Devastator (Inverted Facelock into a Spinning Elbowdrop). He then grabs the first guy, puts him in a Fireman's Carry, then swings him around and hits him with the Devolution (Fireman's Carry into a MichinokuDriver). He then stands up, as "Build Fear" plays again, and the crowd boos him loudly. AT: "He just... just... damn him!" BJ: "I wouldn't be talking there Pina Colada, he might come over here and hit you with a Dev-a-something." AT: "A Pina Colada is a drink, you moron." BJ: "Well, I'm running out of fruits, Strawberry." Bob and Numark are talking backstage. Numark: "Well Bob... I just want you to know that I got your back tonight." Samurai Bob: "Wellll... I'm not too worried about it. Everyone already knows that Jack will be getting involved in some form or fashion. When he does, I got something for that son of a bitch." Bob walks off, calmly, as Numark has an odd look on his face. AT: "Tonight, the xUw Submission Champion, Samurai Bob takes on former Suspect M V Pete for the title." BJ: "That should be great. The new, dark M V P will finish the job as he breaks what's left of Bob." AT: "Bob's a fighting champion. I'm sure he'll retain the belt for the Suspects!" Backstage, BoneCrusher is sitting back in the New Age Horsemen locker room, as the new xUw Hardcore Champion, Stungunn Ric Austin walks in. Ric: "Hey Crusher. Seen KoRe?" Bo'Cru: "Yup. He got dealt with by Dev and Kata." Ric: "And... you didn't run out to help him?" Bo'Cru: "No. I didn't. Why?" Ric: "Bone, we're supposed to be a team here. You're the Enforcer of the New Age Horsemen, you need to like, enforce." BoneCrusher then stands up, as he towers over the Hardcore Champ. Bo'Cru: "OK. Then tell me this brah... exactly what did we have to gain by KoRe running out there, and stopping Dev from breaking Kata in the ring?" Ric: "We coud've taken them both out." Bo'Cru: "OK... and that gains us... exactly what? KoRe wants to handle business, he's gotta do it right, not just run out there and get involved in something that doesn't concern us. Now, if maybe he ran out and took whoever came out on top out... that's a victory for the Horsemen. Instead, we have Kata and Dev both standing, and KoRe in the trainer's office." Ric: "I see. You'd rather let them take each other out, saving us the trouble." Bo'Cru: "Exactly brah." Ric: "Then the last guy standing... he's easy pickin's for us." Bo'Cru: "You got it. KoRe's alright, just a bit of turnbuckle breath. I think he's got that xUw logo imprinted backwards on his face." Ric: "Heh. So, Are we getting out of here?" Bo'Cru: "Yeah. Let's stop by and pick up KoRe on the way. We'll regroup, and come back strong next week." Ric: "Damn Straight." BoneCrusher and Ric Austin then leave as we head back to ringside. BJ: "With Ric Austin's skills and stunningness, KoRe's... High Rollerness, and BoneCrusher's experience, it looks like the Horsemen could really be a force to reckon with in xUw!" AT: "Maybe. But where are they going to go with Admiral John out of the picture? Well, onto our next match!' Dave Kipfer  vs.  Bailey "The Animal" Steele "One of a Kind" by Breaking Point plays, as the crowd pops, and spotlights shine throughout the arena for Dave Kipfer, as the Boomer Sooner comes down fdor a singles match. Then, "Hello, Time Bomb" by the Matthew Good Band plays, and the crowd boos as the self proclaimed "Greatest Tag-Team Wrestler in xUw History" also comes down for a singles appearance. He gets in the ring, as a replay of what happened earlier with Lizzy Borden comes up. AT: "That was earlier this evening, notice that Lizzy is not here at ringside." BJ: "She got what was coming to her." Suddenly, the camera shows as Danyon has come down to ringside. AT: "What the hell is he doing down here?" BJ: "He's just making sure that Dave doesn't pull any funny business." AT: "Dave isn't the one that you have to worry about funny business with." The opening bell rings, and the two men lock up. Bailey hits a knee to the gut, then clubs down on Dave with a hard Forearm Shot to the back. Dave grimmaces in pain, as Bailey coimes up and scratches down Kipfer's back. Dave yells out. Bailey then gets a handfull of Dave's hair, and throws the smaller man outside the ring. The official admonishes Bailey, while outside, Danyon gets in some cheap shots. AT: "Comon Ref, turn around,Dave is fighting a handicapped match here!" BJ: "I know, ain't it great!" Danyon then tosses Dave back in the ring, and the official turns around, he asks Danyon if he had done anything, as if he was going to own up to it. Bailey stomps hard into Dave's gut, leaving Dave to gasp for air.  He picks up Kipfer, and whips him to the ropes, and as Dave comes back, Bailey applies a big Bearhug, squeezing the lungs and ribs of Dave. After a few seconds, Dave bgins punching at Bailey, but to no effect. Then, Dave scratches Bailey's eyes, which finally breaks the hold. Dave takes a second to catch his breath, then he bounces off the ropes, and hits a Clothesline, which doesn't quite take Bailey off of his feet. Dave then bounces back for a second Clothesline, but Danyon grabs his foot, tripping him up. The referee then walks over and gripes at Danyon, while Bailey has regained his balance, and he drops Dave with a thrusting Big Boot Kick. AT: "Dammit, this isn't right! Dave Kipfer is facing both members of the New Royal Legion by himself!" BJ: "No he's not. See? I have the card right here... Kipfer vs. Bailey. See?" Dave then picks himself up in the corner, and Bailey rushes in, hitting a big Avalanche into the turnbuckles. Bailey then looks directly at the turnbuckle pad. BJ: "Uh oh... Bailey is about to have lunch." Bailey begins gnawing away at the turnbuckle pad, as Dave rolls to the outside. Danyon comes over, and picks him up, and goes to whip him into the steel ringsteps, but Dave somehow reverses, sending Danyon crashing through them. Suddenly, from behind Dave comes X-Factor. AT: "What the? What is HE doing down here?" BJ: "He's the new Heavyweight Champion of the World, he goes where he wants... wait a second, what is he... ?" X-Factor starts to speak to Dave, who turns around, confused. X-Factor then keeps on talking, and for some reason, Dave starts to follow him down the aisle... away from the ring! AT: "Where is Dave going? He's going to be counted out!" BJ: "What an idiot!" AT: "I'm not sure about what the Champ is telling him, but this has had to have been a frustrating night for Dave Kipfer, dealing with the Lizzy situation, as well as the NRL." The winner of this bout, as a result of a countout... Bailey "The Animal" Steel! X-Factor and Dave are walking off, and a part of the converation can be heard. X: "I tell ya, frustrating women like that... they're no good tramps." X-Factor and Dave Kipfer leave the arena. AT: "Well... that was... interesting." BJ: "I know Cheddar, X-Factor and Dave?" AT: "Cheddar? Since when is Cheddar a fruit? Anyway,onto our main event, a very personal grudge match for the xUw Submission Championship!" M V Pete v Samurai Bob © xUw Submission Championship "Bulls on Parade" by Rage Against the Machine plays, and the crowd boos as the contender, and newest addition to the AoD comes down to the ring. "F**k That" by Kid Rock blasts over the sound system as the crowd pops HUGE for the two-time former xUw Champion and current xUw Submission Champion. Bob comes to the ringand the two men stare each other down for a second, before M V Pete goes for a lock-up, when Bob ducks it, and comes out with a quick, stiff jab. He then hits another, and another, before going for a big roundhouse right, which Pete ducks, then reaches back and hits a Reverse Neckbreaker. Pete then grabs Bob's right arm, and stomps on the joint between his upper arm and shoulder.  He then attempts to apply a Cross Armbreaker, but Bob struggles and manages to get free, hitting Pete with an Uppercut while getting to his feet. Bob then has Pete backed into a corner, and he starts hitting some hard Knifehand Chops, which gets "Whooo"s from the capacity crowd. AT: "It looks like, at least a good portion of this crowd has decided to side with Bob again." BJ: "Yeah Asparagus, they're back on the bandwagon, but what will happen when Bob decides to go back to his unholy war?" AT: "From what I understand Barry, he never stopped. It just so happens people don't like or respect a traitor like M V Pete." Bob then whips Pete into the opposite corner, but when Bob rushes in, Pete moves and Bob collides shoulder first into the ringpost. Bob then slowly gets to his feet, and as M V Pete gets up, he gets rocked by a series of hard right hands, then a kick into the gut, and a swift DDT. However, this hurts the shoulder, and Bob is slow to get up and can't capitalize on the situation. As Bob does get to his feet, he slowly climbs the turnbuckles. Then, as he sets up, he hits the Shogun-Bushi (Diving Headbutt)... right into the mat as Pete sits up just in time. AT: "OH! Big Move! Big Miss!" BJ: "What a moron! He's a sitting duck now! Comon Pete... take him to the Junkyard!" AT: "That would be the smartest thing to do right now!" And, as you can imagine, Pete does appliy the Junkyard Stretch (Crippler Crossface). Bob is yelling out, trying to keep from tapping. The crowd is cheering loudly, and they're getting a chant going... Crowd: "here we go Bob, Here we go *clap clap* Here we go Bob, Here we go *clap clap*" Bob is struggling hard to get to the ropes. Pete yell out. M V Pete: "COMON! Arrrggh..... TAP!" Pete really wreches back on the hold, and the official is checking to make sure Bob is still with us... he lifts the free hand... it drops. He lifts again... it drops. He lifts one more time... it dr-it stays up! Bob flips off the referee, then yells out loudly, as he struggles to reach the ropes, and... he... gots it! The official tells Pete to break the hold... 1... 2... 3... 4... Pete releases the hold. Pete gets up, stomping and kicking at his former mentor. He then reaches down and picks Bob up, but he does so at an angle to where the referee can't see Bob hit Pete with a wicked Low Blow... right in the nuggets! Bob masks it quickly by hitting a couple of left hand shots to the chest and midsection. Bob then bounces off the ropes, and Pete goes for a big Clothesline. Big, because it misses big, as Bob slightly ducks it, jumps and floats over Pete's back, and hits a sweet left-handed Hurricane DDT. Bob then slowly gets to his feet, as from the entrance comes "The Ripper" Jack Nobbledy. AT: "Oh dammit! We knew this would probably happen, but I doubt that Bob will be able to fend off both these wicked men." BJ: "He may not have to Nectarine, look!" From behind, The Ripper is nailed by Numark. Both The Ripper and the former InterContinental Champ do battle outside the ring. Bob gets up to his feet, and sees the fight outside. He then picks up Pete, and takes him over with a nice Snapp Suplex (very quick Snap Suplex). Bob then follows up with a Double Knee Drop to the neck and forehead of M V Pete. Then, as Bob sets up for the Buto Blade (Legdrop off the top turnbuckle, The Ripper grabs Bob's leg, pulling him down and crotching him over the top turnbuckle. Numark then nails the Ripper, but the damage has been done, as M V Pete gets up, and grabs Bob with a modified Fireman's Carry. He then spins Bob around, hitting his patented Brick Town Bulldog (Lesnar's F-5). He then follows this up by re-applying the Junk Yard Stretch. This time, after several seconds, the official checks his arm... it drops once... twice... three times, ending the match!! AT: "Dammit! The AoD have stolen another one from the Suspects!" The winner of this bout, and... NEW xUw Submission Champion... M V Pete!!! Numark rushes in the ring, and attacks M V Pete, throwing him over the top rope and to the floor, where he is met by The Ripper, who is holding his Submission Title belt. Numark is kneeling down in the ring next to Bob, his eyes locked on Pete, who looks back, and raises his Submission title belt as the crowd boos. BJ: "Haha! The Darkness leaves with the victory and the Submission title! Look at Bob laying there. And there's nothing that Numark can do about it!" AT: "You're enjoying this? How can you.... nevermind." Bob starts to move around a bit, as he looks down the aisle, where Pete and The Ripper have just left. Numark is helping him to his feet. AT: "M V Pete and the Army of Darkness may have won this battle, but the war is far from over here. For Barry Johnson, I'm the Atomic Tomato, see you Monday night on xUw Mayhem. Good night everyone!" The camera shows a very upset Bob and Numark in the ring, as the copyright graphics come up. Fade to black, the show is over, go home already!
