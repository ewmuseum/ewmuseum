XUW: Pay-Per-View The screen is purely black, and we start to hear the ticking of a clock. Tick tock... tick tock. The sound gets louder, and louder, and louder, driving into our minds, and suddenly it flashes up on our screen, a clock with knives for hands, about to strike midnight. Tick tock... BONG!!! It's time, it's time, it's VIOLENT TIMES!!!!! BANG BANG BANG!!! The pyro goes flying, the loud generic rock/metal music kicks in and we're taken to the Gaylord Entertainment Center in Nashville, Tennessee, where the crowd are pumped and ready for XUW Violent Times, the first Pay Per View extravaganza under the control of the Ultra Violents. The camera pans to take it all in, and we can see that it's another sell-out crowd, and many of them are indeed holding signs. Who'd have thought it? “I love the Ultra Violents!” “HBK will kick your stupid butts!” “Saij, why don't you hate Kipfer like the rest of the sane world?” “I'm the father!” “I saw The Ripper at McDonalds!” “Country Music Sucks” and “Shadow loves Hot Boy D, and you can't prove that he doesn't!” The camera then cuts to the announce desk, where our heroes, Mikey and Barry Johnson are waiting. Mikey: Hello there ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to Tennessee for XUW Violent Times! BJ: That's The Ultra Violents present: XUW Violent Times! Mikey: Sure thing. What a night of action we have planned for you here. We've got every XUW title belt up for grabs, and it's hard to pick a winner in any of them! Take the Submission Title for instance, where two of the craziest people in the federation will be trying to force the other to tap out. BJ: That match will suck. Mikey: Well, with the Wheel of Ultra Violence getting involved, anything is possible. It could even be The Fool v Fatbadger inside a Hell in a Cell! BJ: It would still suck. Mikey: Well, we have other title matches too! The Hardcore Title is on the line between the challenger, Kata, and the holder of what he calls the 'happycore' belt – The Fallen Fae. Kata has certainly been looking impressive in recent weeks, and is adapting to a new style very well, but Fae has been nearly untouchable since his narrow loss to Saij in the World Title tournament. BJ: I hope Kata wins here, as Fae has been disgracing the title with his antics. Mikey: The only way he can disgrace it is in the ring, and inside the squared circle he has been an admirable champion. BJ: Bah. Mikey: Well, if that's not to your taste, we have the friend of the Ultra Violents, NFM, as special guest referee in a match between the Hardcore Icons and the XUW Tag Team Title holders, The Lunchroom Patrol. BJ: You're right there, this could be the match of the night. The Hardcore Icons always put on a great show, and with the Latch key Kid going mental, he might outweigh the amount that Doughboy is going to stink up the ring! Mikey: Don't talk nonsense! Doughboy is one of the best wrestler in XUW, and he'll be looking over his shoulder to keep LKK in line tonight. BJ: Shadow has to be the favourite, as he will be prepared for whatever the Wheel of Ultra Violence has to offer. Mikey: True, but Dante has the edge in experience, and he's no longer injured. In another huge match we have Sheri Dedmon going one-on-one again with Ric Austin. You can't help but feel that there will be fireworks in this match, as there is such a deep-seated animosity between not only the two participants, but also their entourages.
