XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #92

4 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Vortex - Number 92 Vortex! The only wrestling show on a Thursday with added XUW! Woohoo! Pyro flies like it was shot out of a giant cannon, music blares like it was being blared through giant speakers, and the crowd cheers like it was being held at gunpoint and forced to cheer or be killed upon the spot. Thankfully, the Ultra Violents are no longer in charge of XUW, so that last one isn’t true any more. The camera swirls around the arena, taking in the signs as it always does. “Is Dante fooling us all?” “Why, Bailey? Why?” “Where’s Darkseid then, eh?” “Why, Longhorn? Why?” …and… “I’m STILL delaying the Chris Williams Memorial Cup!” …needless to say though, the man with that sign is taken out and shot. So, announcers, where are you when we need you? Mikey: Hello there one and all, and welcome to XUW Vortex! I’ve received word that this will be the FINAL Vortex of the year, so you’d better make the most of my colour commentator, Lauwass, while you can. Lauwass: That’s a disgrace. I demand an inquest! Why is this the final Vortex? Mikey: Well, with the PPV Card going out tonight, they have decided to scrap Vortex next week, and December to Dismember Three is our final card of the year! Lauwass: Other than the awards show. Mikey: Yes, other than that. Well, before we get things underway, I believe that Henrik the King has been spotted going backstage, and walking towards the office of Commissioner Sev! The camera cuts to the backstage area, as it so often does around these times. Henrik the King is walking down the corridor, a big smile upon his face and a big roll of paper in his hand. He approaches the door of the Commish, knocks it twice and then enters before he can get a reply. Inside he finds Sev and April, both doing paperwork. Henrik: Sev, April, I have the best plan you’ve ever heard of. I was looking at the old plans of the castle, and I… Sev stands up and looks irritated. Sev: … April: He doesn’t like being interrupted, Henrik. You should know this by now. Henrik looks up at the monster standing in front of him, and shrugs his shoulders. Henrik: Well, it’s your loss then, Sev. XUW’s loss. My plan was brilliant, in fact it IS brilliant. I’ve heard rumours Sev. I’ve heard rumours that means you might need something like this plan. Sev: … April: Then come back later. He’s busy right now. Henrik: Okay, okay. I’ll be back though, Sev. You can’t hold me down forever. Henrik smiles, as if he knows something that Sev doesn’t, and then leaves the room. Sev: … April: I know what you mean. I think something big is going down tonight. Something very big. We cut to elsewhere backstage, to the locker room of one Ric Austin. Well, actually outside of his locker room, where NFM is etching something in to the door, below where it says the name of the Intercontinental Champion. He turns round and sees the camera looking at him. NFM: Well, I know this will cost me more money, but it’s worth every penny to see Ric Austin’s face when he gets here. NFM walks away, laughing, as it is revealed that he has carved into the door: “Ric Austin - Your time as hunted draws to a close. Soon, it will be time for the kill” We cut back to the announce table, as Mikey and Lauwass await the first match of the night. Mikey: Well, I’m not sure what Henrik has planned there. Lauwass: It will doubtless be something that is really great. Mikey: Well, we’ll see. Lauwass: Unlike what NFM is doing. Ric Austin is going to kill him for that. Mikey: I don’t doubt that it’s going to annoy Austin. The question is, what’s he going to do about it? Lauwass: Kick the hell out of NFM… duh! Mikey: Well, now it is time to go on to the first match this evening which involves two wrestlers going in different directions. One, Dante is going up and is challenging The Anarchist for the Heavyweight Title at D2D3, while the other, The Fool,…… Lauwass: I’d hardly say that the Fool is going downwards considering he was never up in the first place. Mikey: This is definitely a tune up match for Dante and The Fool, if he has a good showing perhaps he’ll get PPV match up. Everyone’s attention turns to the entrance ramp as “What a Fool Believes” starts up and out comes the Fool to a large pop from the crowd. He slowly makes his way down the aisle to the ring and climbs up and enters in the ring. He has his trusty sidekick Mr. Action Figure with him. The crowd dies down but then starts up with the loud head as the screeching sounds from Psycho go over arena’s sound system then “Fireproof” starts up. Dante comes out to a lot of heat from the crowd. Dante runs down to the ring and slides in the ring and chases after The Fool but The Fool quickly hops out of the ring and starts talking to Mr. Action Figure. Dante waits in the ring. Then all of a sudden, X Factor’s music starts up and out comes the Submission Champion, X-Factor, to a lot of heat from the crowd. He makes his way down to the ring and walks over to The Fool. He starts talking to the Fool and takes Mr. Action Figure out of his hand. The Fool looks like he is going to cry as X-Factor takes it out of his hand. X shakes his head and points to the ring. The Fool reluctantly slides back in the ring. Dante goes right after him and hits him with a few kicks and the ref signals for the bell to start the match. Opening Match: Dante vs. The Fool Mikey: I’m not exactly sure why X-Factor is here right now for this watch. Lauwass: You fool, he’s helping out the Fool. He’s showing him how to win a match, that’s what he is doing here. Dante stomps on The Fool while he is down. He continues to do so while The Fool tries to get to his feet. On the outside X yells at Dante to get his attention and Dante looks over at X. While this happens The Fool is able to get up to his feet. He grabs Dante and whips him across the ring and nails him with a clothesline. Dante goes down on the mat. The Fool then helps Dante up and nails him with a snap suplex. He then messes up Dante’s hair and rubs his face into the mat to a large crowd pop. Mikey: Looks like The Fool has taken control of this match. I’m not sure X-Factor said to him on the outside but it sure has worked. Lauwass: I told you, he’s told him what it will take to win the match. X-Factor is a former World Champion you know and the current Submission Champion. That’s more than the Fool can claim. The Fool looks over at X for some guidance once more. X has since picked up a chair and slid in the ring and point to it. The Fool walks over to it and picks it up and looks over at Dante. He drops the chair as Dante is now up to his feet and Dante charges and spears Dante down once more. The Fool comes back to the chair and X yells at him some more and tells him to use it on Dante. The Fool starts to reluctantly walk towards Dante who is back on his feet now. Dante looks over at The Fool who is walking slowly and Dante nails The Fool with a standing drop kick and hits the chair back into The Fool. The Fool drops the chair and falls to the ground. Dante tosses the chair to the side of the ring for now. Dante points to X on the outside of the ring and has a few words for him. Dante helps The Fool to his feet and delivers a few right hands. He whips The Fool into the ropes and as The Fool comes off it he nails him with a back body drop. Dante then scoops The Fool up and slams him down on the ground. Dante runs over and climbs up the turnbuckle and leaps off hitting a 5 Star Frog Splash on The Fool. Dante goes for a pin……….. 1…………… 2……………. 2.5…………….. 2.75…………….. However, X pulls The Fools foot and puts it on the ropes. The ref breaks the count. However, the Fool takes his own foot off the rope and slides into the middle of the ring and lies down for Dante. Mikey: What is The Fool doing? I don’t understand what just happened here. X-Factor helped him out and tried to help him stay in the match but The Fool somehow just refused it and moved to the center of the ring and lied back down. Lauwass: It’s all a big ploy as X Factor has something up his sleeve here. Back in the ring Dante seems to be just as confused as to what just happened. He goes over and puts his right foot on The Fool and pins him arrogantly……….. 1……………….. 2…………………… 2.5…………………. 2.75……………….. 2.85…………………. 2.95…………………….. 2.999……………………. 2.999999999999999……………………. 2.9999999999999999999999999999999………. 3……….NO!!!!............The Fool gets his shoulder up at the last possible second. Dante is pissed at what just happened here. Mikey: What is going on? Lauwass: It’s all part of X-Factors plan. Just wait and see. Dante grabs The Fool by his hair and brings him to his feet. He starts nailing him with right hands. He whips him against the ropes and grabs him and nails a sidewalk slam. The Fool is down and out. Dante picks The Fool up and nails him with the Devils Bomb (Inverted Power Bomb). Dante quickly covers The Fool as X Factor turns around and leaves the ring area obviously disgusted by The Fool’s showing………… 1………………. 2………………. 2.5……………………. 3!!!!!!!! Winner: Dante Mikey: That was very, very odd. But Dante is going into D2D3 a winner and has some momentum. Lauwass: I’m still waiting for X-Factor’s plan to come to fruition. Mikey: He was trying to get The Fool to cheat. That WAS the plan. Lauwass: No, I think it was deeper than that. Mikey: Well, I think Ric Austin has arrived backstage. We can see what he’s going to do if there’s a camera there… We cut backstage again, and we see Ric Austin arriving at his locker room, with Bob the Temp and Derrick Miles standing behind him. Austin sees the words carved in to the door, and his face starts to grow with anger. Austin: Dammit! What do I have to do to stop this little punk trying to call me out? He had his shot and he blew it! Sure, he didn’t get pinned, but that wasn’t the name of the game. Derrick Miles looks at Austin as if to say “Well, there’s a few ways we can solve this.” Austin: Indeed there are, but I’m fed up of them. I’m not fighting NFM any more. I’ve had it up to here with him stalking me. I’m going to go where he can’t find me ever again. Bob: You’re going to Alaska? Austin: No, you fool. Watch this!” Austin walks off with a purpose, followed by a smiling Derrick Miles and a puzzled Bob the Temp. We cut back to the announcers. Mikey: Hmmm, I have a feeling something big is going to happen. I told you earlier… something is definitely afoot. Lauwass: Austin is going to get a restraining order on NFM! How great is that? Mikey: I don’t know, Lauwass… I think he’s planning something different. Something big. And now it is time to move onto our next match. We have an old set of enemies from XUDF renewing their feud of sorts in XUW. But the question has to be what exactly are Longhorn’s intentions? Lauwass: I think we are going to find out this evening. Lauwass is interrupted by the sounds of “Stupify” starts up and the crowd boos as Overkill, a member of the Skeletons of Society comes out to large amount of heat. He arrogantly makes his way down to the ring. As he makes his way down he sees a sign by a fan that says “S.O.S. Needs Help” and OK takes and rips it to shreds. He slides in the ring and awaits The Longhorn. “Ring of Fire” starts burning through the sound system in the XUW Arena and out walks Longhorn to a mixed reaction from the way he has been acting lately. He looks around at others with a sad face and sighs. LH starts to make his way down to the ring, slowly. OK decides to hop out of the ring and meet him half way and they start brawling in the aisle. LH is able to get the upper hand and whip OK against the steel railing separating them from the fans. LH is very aggressive and whips OK across to the other steel railing. He then follows it up with a big clothesline, sending OK over the railing and into the crowd. LH climbs into the crowd and stands besides OK who is starting to get to his feet. LH goes to give OK a right hand but LH left himself vulnerable and OK nails him in the stomach with a fist of his own. Then he gives him a low blow as LH grabs his mid section in pain. OK is up now and grabs LH’s head and nails it into the steel guard a twice. OK then climbs over the steel railing and reaches back over and grabs LH. He sets him up for a vertical suplex and takes him over the railing and onto the floor below. Mikey: I don’t think that this match has officially begun yet. Overkill and Longhorn are still fighting outside. The ref has not yet signaled for the bell to start the match. Lauwass: They are just playing mind games with each other that’s all. It’s a brilliant move on both of their parts. Both men are slow to get up in the aisle. LH is the first to his feet and he grabs OK by the hair and drags him towards the ring. He goes around to the steps on the outside and smashes OK’s head into the steel steps on the outside. He then tosses OK into the ring. He rolls himself into the ring afterwards. The ref signals for the bell to finally officially start the match. Match Two: Overkill vs. Longhorn Mikey: Finally this match is now official and will be recorded in the books. Lauwass: Do you really record these in the books? Mikey: Not me………someone else does…..I heard that’s what Blake Wolfson is doing after he retired. Lauwass: That’s a shame. LH helps OK to his feet and then whips him across the ring against the ropes and LH drops down to give him a back body drop but OK is able to stop in time and deliver a DDT to counter it. OK goes for the quick cover………….. 1………………. 2………………… 2.5………………….. 2.75………………… LH kicks out! Both men are back up to their feet now. LH takes a few steps forwards and swings at OK but OK ducks his arm. OK goes behind him and wraps his arms around him and delivers a Release German Suplex. LH goes across the ring and decides to slide out of the ring to recover. LH grabs the back of his neck on the outside of the ring. He leans against the railing and shakes his head. He kicks the railing in frustration. The ref continues on with his count out. He is now up to 5………. Longhorn starts walking back towards the ring. Overkill tries to go towards the ropes to get outside but the ref stops him and pushes him back much to the complaining of OK. Meanwhile on the outside the Longhorn lifts up the ring apron and looks underneath it. He reaches out under it and pulls out a baseball bat. He climbs into the ring. He walks towards OK who is still arguing to the ref but has his back to LH. LH takes the bat and swings and nails OK in the back. The ref sees the whole thing transpire and has but no choice to DQ LH. Winner by a result of a DQ: Overkill Mikey: What was Longhorn thinking? Lauwass: He’s teaching Overkill a lesson that’s what he was thinking about. Mikey: Uh-oh……of course this was bound to happen. Where there is one Skeleton of Society there are others. Joey Vendetti and Bubba Bailey are slowly making their way down to the ring. Longhorn sees them coming and stands there with his bat in hand waiting for them to come closer. He calls them on as Vendetti and Bubba wait on the outside. Behind LH, OK has gotten to his feet now. He reaches into his shorts to adjust himself…..no wait….to pull out a pair of brass knucks. He puts them on and walks up to LH and turns LH around and nails him in the head with a punch. LH goes down hard. Vendetti and Bubba hop up on the apron enter the ring with smiles on their faces. OK pulls LH back to the middle of the ring. Mikey: This just isn’t right. Who is going to come down and save Longhorn from this? Lauwass: This is so right. This is what you get Longhorn for trying to teach Overkill a lesson. Now it’s time for him to learn one. OK stands LH up and he looks around dizzily at Bubba, Vendetti and OK surrounding him. They all take a step closer to him and are about to gang up on him when they all hold out their hands and start shaking hands with smiles on their faces………..the crowd boos in major heat. Mikey: What the hell is going on here? It can’t be. Longhorn has joined the Skeletons of Society. That is just so wrong. Lauwass: Yes…….what a smart move. These guys are the best stable in XUW right now. Mikey: Better than the Ultra Violents? Lauwass: Um……..no one is better than them. Mikey: But you just said……….. Lauwass: Never mind what I just said. Look in the ring. They are so happy. We cut backstage to where X Factor and The Fool are walking around backstage. Factor does not seem to be taking The Fool’s loss to Dante very well. He is berating The Fool for not cheating, and thus losing the match. The Fool seems cowed by the man who has promised him his very own foam fingers, and says very little in response. As they walk along, they pass The Fighter, Dev. Dev laughs at the two men passing him by, as he walks towards his own match, which is up next. Factor: You think something is funny? Both Dev and X Factor stop… and after a few seconds of realising nobody is berating him any more, The Fool stops too. Dev: You are funny. You used to be something, you know that? You used to know something about the business, and you could achieve things in some form of style. Now you are just a bitter shell, holding the belt of Suffering as if it were nothing more than a prop. You chatter out nonsense towards your pet there, a once great man turned to a jobber, someone malleable at your will. You disgust me, Factor. Factor: Sweet Jesus you bore me. Dev: I bore you? Factor: I’m all for winning matches and all that, hell, look at the title I’ve got. But you… you’re something else. You’re like a black hole that sucks entertainment from my soul. Dev: Well maybe I could take that title while I am sucking. Factor laughs at what Dev just said. Dev: It matters not. You can laugh and try and train your pet there for another day. I have a former World Champion to defeat. Your belt… it can come later. Dev actually smiles as he walks off for his match, and he leaves X Factor looking uneasy. Factor: Now look what you’ve done, Fool, you’ve upset Dev! Fool: Sorry. Do I get the foam fingers yet? Factor: No, it’ll still take a bit longer… The Submission Champion and his friend walk off in search of, well, I don’t know, a drink maybe? We cut instead to a different scene, where Sev is sitting in his office, with April O’Neill. They are going over yet more paperwork, when in barges Ric Austin, followed by his entourage. Austin drops down in a seat opposite Sev, and puts his feet up on the desk. Sev immediately swipes the feet off the desk with one hand, and stands up. Sev: … April: Never do that again, or you’re in trouble. Austin: Whatever. Look, I’m here for one thing and one thing only. You see this Intercontinental Title belt I have? Nice isn’t it? Sev: … April: yes, it’s one of the most prestigious titles in wrestling today. Austin: Well I don’t care any more. You can have it back. Sev: … Bob: What? Derrick Miles gives a look that says “What?” April: What? Austin: You heard me. If NFM wants to break all my stuff because I have this belt, then he can have it. Bob: What? Are you mad? We get more money with that belt! You’re not scared of NFM are you? Austin: I just don’t care. It’s not worth having all my stuff broken, having a madman trying to ‘hunt me down’. I can live without it. Bob: But… but… but… Austin smiles like the devil himself, and turns to look Sev dead in the eye. Austin: …and you know why, right big guy? Sev: … April: …the title shot. Austin: You got it. Bob: You mean the clause in the contract? Austin: Yep. Every Intercontinental Title holder can drop the title for an immediate shot at the World Title. Bob: Yeah, but isn’t Dante the Number One Contender? Austin: Not any more he isn’t. Sev: … April: Dante won the Halloween Battle Royal. He’s getting a title shot at December to Dismember Three. Austin laughs and drops the Intercontinental Title belt on Sev’s desk. Austin: So am I. Give this to NFM, or DJ, or whoever. I’m claiming my World Title shot. Without a further word, Austin leaves, followed by his cohorts. April and Sev sit there, speechless, as we cut back to the announcers. Lauwass: Yes! That’s even better than a restraining order! Mikey: Ric Austin has relinquished his Intercontinental Title in order to have a shot at the World Title. Will Sev let him participate at December to Dismember Three? Lauwass: He has to! The title shot must be within the month, and December to Dismember Three is the next opportunity. That’s a stroke of genius from Austin! Mikey: Well personally I think he was getting a little wary of NFM, but… Lauwass: Nonsense! Austin knew what he was doing. What a clever, clever move. Mikey: I knew he had something big up his sleeve! I just knew it! Lauwass: Well, no rest for the wicked, here comes the next match! Tag Team Match: Saij and Rujasu Vs. Dev and Wildlife The title song to Chrono Trigger hits on the PA system, and the crowd starts cheering as Rujasu steps out from behind the curtain. He waves to the fans and makes his way down to the ring. “Live Again” by Sevendust then hits, and in a matter of seconds, the entire crowd yells out “HUZZAH” as Saij steps out from behind the curtain with his arms raised high as the crowd goes nuts. He makes his way down to the ring slapping hands with a few of the fans along the way, before sliding in the ring and slapping hands with Rujasu, as they each go to separate corners to pose for the crowd. “Forces of Nature” by Oasis then hits on the PA system and the crowd changes quicker then a dollar from cheering to booing. Wildlife steps out onto the stage and just looks out at the crowd with his arms on his hips smiling. He makes his way down to about halfway on the ramp, and stops to wait for his partner, staring into the ring, as his two opponents just stare back at him. “One Man Army” by Our Lady Peace then hits, and the crowd keeps the distain going, as they just boo Dev as he steps out onto the stage. He doesn’t waste any time on the fans and just makes his way down to Wildlife. They both then run at the ring and slide in as not only the bell rings, but a brawl starts the second they get in the ring. Dev and Saij immediately start exchanging blows, as do Rujasu and Wildlife. Eventually Wildlife gets the advantage, backing Rujasu up to the ropes, and eventually hitting him with a clothesline that sends him over the top rope and crashing down to the floor. Wildlife then turns to check on how Dev is doing, and can see that Saij clearly has the advantage with Dev backed into a corner, and is unloading a couple shots on him. Wildlife attempts to run over to help Dev, but the ref stops him halfway across the ring, and sends him to his corner, much to his dismay… which he lets be known through a shouting match. Saij then sees that both Rujasu and Wildlife have gone to their corners, maybe not with the methods they would have liked, but nonetheless, they are there. Saij then whips Dev to the opposite corner of the ring, and Dev slams into the turnbuckles with such force that the ring almost seems to have moved and inch or two from the impact. Saij then runs in and jumps at Dev with a big splash, but Dev just manages to roll out of the way seconds before Saij crashes into the turnbuckles himself. Dev then quickly reaches behind his back, and wraps his arm around Saij’s neck from behind, pulling him down to the mat with a neckbreaker. Dev then quickly gets up to his feet and kicks Saij a couple of times, before picking him up and carrying him over to his corner where he tags in Wildlife. Wildlife climbs to the top rope, as Dev walks to the middle of the ring with Saij in tow. Wildlife then jumps off the top rope with a double axe handle, but Saij ducks at the last second, and most of the impact falls onto Dev’s head. Dev falls down to the mat, and Wildlife leans over to check on him, as Saij comes up from behind Wildlife, and nails him with a bulldog, right on top of Dev. Mikey: Looks like that double team effort failed. Lauwass: Gee, ya think? Saij then lifts Wildlife back up to his feet, and hits a nice snap suplex in the middle of the ring as Dev makes his way back to his corner holding his head. As wildlife slowly gets back up to his feet, Saij tags in Rujasu and the two run at Wildlife, taking him down with a double clothesline. Rujasu quickly goes for the pin as the ref slides in… One… Two… Kick out! Rujasu doesn’t seem at all surprised by the kick out and immediately lifts Wildlife up to his feet. Rujasu whips Wildlife to the ropes, and Wildlife springs off the ropes and runs directly into a dropkick, which sends him right down to the mat. Rujasu then quickly makes his way over to the corner and climbs up to the top rope. The fans pop as he leaps off the top rope with the High Score (Shooting Star Press), but Wildlife rolls out of the way at the very last second. Mikey: He may have went for that a little early, but in tag matches you have less chances for big moves like that. Lauwass: You suck as an announcer… you know that? Wildlife lifts himself up to his feet, and quickly lays a couple of kicks into Rujasu as he is down, before lifting him back up to his feet, and quickly nails him with a Butterfly suplex. Wildlife then goes to the corner and tags Dev back in. The two lift Rujasu up to his feet and perform a beautiful double suplex on him. Dev quickly lays a couple of kicks into the downed Rujasu before locking in the… Lockdown. Rujasu yells out in pain and struggles as much as he can to get out, but Dev has it locked in too tightly, and the struggling just makes it worse. Rujasu slowly lifts his hand up to tap after a few minutes go by, but Saij comes in and breaks the hold, by kicking Dev. Dev gets to his feet and yells at Saij, but the ref keeps them apart, and sends Saij back to his corner. Dev then turns his attention back to Rujasu, who is just barely up to his feet, and brings him back over to his corner, tagging Wildlife back in. As Wildlife steps through the ropes, Dev lifts Rujasu up into a powerbomb position, and slams him down with Wildlife adding to the force by helping to push Rujasu down. Wildlife then goes for the pin as Dev steps out of the ring… One… Two… Two and a half… Broken up by Saij! Mikey: This match was less then a second away from being over if Saij didn’t step in there. Lauwass: That cheating bastard! Mikey: He was just helping out his teammate. Lauwass: Oh sure, when the good guys do it, it’s fine… but not when the guys you don’t like do it. Once again, one of Saij’s opponents gets up and yells at him, but the ref keeps them separated. But, this time, as the ref is making Saij go back to his corner, Wildlife uses the opportunity to choke Rujasu. Right before the ref can see, Wildlife breaks the hold and lifts Rujasu back up to his feet. Wildlife tags Dev back in, and they both send Rujasu off to the ropes. Rujasu bounces off the ropes, and runs right back at Dev and Wildlife, who are going for a double clothesline, but Rujasu ducks under it at the last second, and continues off to the other side. He bounces off the ropes once again and runs back at the two, nailing Dev with a dropkick that sends him down to the mat. Wildlife then quickly lays a couple of kicks into Rujasu while he is down, but soon after the ref grabs Wildlife and forces him to go back to his corner, with much protesting from Wildlife. Rujasu slowly starts to crawl to his corner as Saij reaches his hand out as far as he possibly can, while the crowd cheers him on. Rujasu inches his way closer and closer as Dev starts to notice what is going on. Dev runs over to Rujasu and grabs his foot, pulling him back into the middle of the ring, but it was seconds after Rujasu made the tag! Dev looks up to see Saij running at him with a clothesline that takes him right off his feet. Saij then runs off to the ropes, and springs back at Dev, who has just gotten back up to his feet and takes him down with another clothesline. The crowd cheers even more as Saij runs off to the ropes a second time, and bounces off, attempting another clothesline, but this time Dev knows its coming, and takes him down with a drop toe hold, slamming Saij’s face into the mat. Dev gets back up to his feet, and quickly drops an elbow to the back of Saij’s neck. Dev gets back up to his feet, and drops a second elbow on Saij. Dev then gets to his feet and signals to the crowd for his finisher, who just boo him. Dev then lifts Saij up to his feet, nails with a kick to the gut, lifts him onto his shoulders, and plants him to the mat with the Devolution! Dev then quickly goes for the pin… One… Two… Rujasu runs in to break up the pin… Two and a half… But Wildlife clotheslines him to the outside!… Two and three quarters… Kick out!!! Mikey: Could that have been any closer?!?! Lauwass: Umm, of course not… You saw, he was at two and three quarters there. Dev looks completely surprised that Saij kicked out, and quickly lifts him to his feet, before hitting a piledriver on Saij, which causes him to collapse back down to the mat. Dev then looks to the outside and sees Rujasu and Wildlife trading blows. Dev the slides to the outside and attacks Rujasu from behind. The two then whip Rujasu into the steele steps causing them to go flying in two pieces, in the other direction. The two then stomp Rujasu on the ground a couple times, and then slide back in the ring. Dev climbs to the top rope, as Wildlife lifts Saij into position for another piledriver. Dev then jumps off the top rope, grabbing Saij’s feet, and nailing him with a spike piledriver. The crowd boos the two heavily as Wildlife slides back out of the ring and goes to attack Rujasu some more, while Dev lifts Saij back onto his shoulder and hits a second Devolution! Dev then quickly goes for the pin… One… Two… Three!!! “One Man Army” hits on the PA system again as the ref raises Dev’s hand while the crowd boos like crazy.
