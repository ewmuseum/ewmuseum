XUW Mayhem #27

25 Mar 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Pyro blasts, the misadjusted lasers this time miraculously cure five men in the middle section of the arena of testicular cancer, the foam machine backclogs and explodes, killing the Hungry Mexican who was standing nearby having a latte, and some idiot in the truck mixed up the Mayhem theme with his daughter's Harry Potter Books-on-Tape, causing Chapter 4 of "The Prisoner of Azkaban" to be read to the audience. In addition to all this, the air conditioning for the arena is blown and the temperature inside is swelteringly hot. In a desperate effort to save face, the camera cuts to the Atomic Tomato, who has just reached the announce position and has yet to sit down, revealing he's wearing only a pair of silk boxer shirts with his jacket, shirt and tie. Barry Johnston, however, is dancing on the table in a Britney Spears style Catholic schoolgirl skirt, making AT's faux pas seem inconsequential. And....MAYHEM IS ON!! AT: Ummm...sorry about that folks...Barry, get off the table...anyway, welcome to Mayhem! We've got a big night for you lined up tonight... BJ: The cameras didn't catch that, did they? AT: Yes. Ignore that anything unusual happened. ANYWAY! We've got Kipfer facing Bluto for the TV title, Naron facing Kat in what's sure to be a vicious match, Sev, the Carrot, and NFM in a non-title three-way hardcore match. HBK is making his return to face Rob the Throb, Esco faces a previously thought dead Henrik in a Submission title match, and the Royal Legion faces the Deadly Combination for the Tag Titles. BJ: I guarantee that the Royal Legion loses that match due to some unfair tactics. Admiral John is conspiring against the Legion, I tell you. AT: You're an idiot. And now...it's TV TITLE TIME! Dave Kipfer(c) vs. Bluto - TV Title Match "Fight For Your Right" hits and Bluto comes out with his cooler to a rather subdued pop. "One of a Kind" hits, Lizzy Borden's breasts come out, followed by Kipfer's...talent, followed by Lizzy, and then, finally, Dave Kipfer comes out to a monumental pop that may rival the size of said...talent. The two men square off, the bell rings, and the match is underway. Kipfer takes the early advantage with a waistlock takedown, which he rides over into a front facelock. Bluto backs Kipfer into the ropes, forcing a break. The break is clean, and the two men tie up again. Kipfer again takes the advantage with a headlock. Bluto throws him off the ropes and ducks the initial clothesline attempt from Kipfer, but is caught by a flying forearm as Kipfer rebounds. Kipfer pulls Bluto to his feet, hits a scoop slam and follows it up with an elbow. He goes for the cover and gets a two. AT: Kipfer's showing his talent inside that ring! BJ: I sure hope he isn't! This is a family show! AT: No...his talent...not his...nevermind... Kipfer springs to the top rope and hits a hurricanrana on Bluto for another two count. As Bluto gets to his feet, Kipfer hits him with the Bootie Call, but is distracted by Blake Savage, who's run to ringside. Blake slides into the ring and he and Kipfer begin brawling. Bluto rolls out of the ring and chugs a couple beers from his cooler to refresh himself. He takes two more beers into the ring. As the ref separates Kipfer and Blake, Blake gets in one last low blow on the considerable...talent of Kipfer. While the ref sends B1 to the back, Bluto hits Kipfer with the Can-chairto and follows it up with the Keg Stand. He goes for the pin, and gets the three! Winner and NEW TV Champ - Bluto AT: That damn dirty Blake just cost Kipfer the title! BJ: Kipfer shouldn't have let himself be distracted, even after Blake entered the ring! AT: How was he supposed to do that? BJ: I could have, back in my wrestling days... Tomato gives "The Majestic" BJ a very blank look as "God Save the Queen" plays. Naron vs. Kat - Women's Match Naron comes down to the ring to a mixed reaction, waving to the crowd. The crowd preps for the drum riff, and is pleased as "Bad Girl's Lament" hits. Kat comes out to a rather large pop. She rushes to the ring and jumps Naron from the back, knocking her out of the ring. Kat follows her out and attempts to catch Naron's head between her foot and the ring post. Naron ducks, barely, and Kat catches the ringpost full on. A cracking sound is heard as the ref signals for the bell, and then immediately checks on Kat. Kat shoves him away and gets to her feet, grinning at Naron, who looks a little concerned for her own safety. AT: I think Kat broke something when she missed that kick. BJ: Doesn't seem to be bothering her. Naron slides back into the ring, followed by Kat. Naron begins stomping on Kat's injured foot before Kat can even get to her feet, but is met with laughter and a fist to the throat. Naron goes down, clutching at her throat as Kat gets to her feet, slightly favoring the injury, but not seeming to be fazed at all by it. She kicks Naron in the face with the injured foot, and then stomps directly on Naron's sternum. She goes for a cover, but Naron manages to get a shoulder up at two. Naron gets to her feet slowly, and is met with an attempted superkick from Kat. Naron catches her foot, however, and applies an ankle lock - a rather good one, surprisingly. Kat starts giggling and pulls herself to the ropes. Naron gives a clean break. Kat gets to her feet, heavily favoring the injured leg. Naron moves in for the kill, but Kat kicks her in the gut and hits a scissors kick. Kat then turns, and low blows the ref before crawling out of the ring and getting a Kendo stick from under the ring. She rolls back in and is about to crack it across Naron's face when the ref pulls it away from her. She turns and starts screaming at the ref, giving Naron a chance to get a quick roll up and the pin. Winner by Pinfall: Naron Kat gets to her feet and pulls a knife. The ref restrains her as Naron hurriedly makes her way to the back, and Kat responds by stabbing the ref in the arm. Sheri runs out from the back and drags Kat to the back before she can seriously injure the ref. Some EMTs come out and escort the ref to the back. AT: What the hell was that about? BJ: I have no idea. Maybe the heat in here is getting to Kat. AT: Could be, but that was just bizarre, and oddly senselessly violent for a member of the Horsemen. BJ: Hold on...are we talking about the Horsemen who torch people's houses and blow up cars? AT: Point taken. At any rate, we've got many more matches to get through tonight, so let's cut the jibba-jabba, fool! Next up is a little dance between Severson, the Bionic Carrot, and NFM, with the stip that Severson must win this match to get a shot at Blitzwing! BJ: Sev is going to dance? NFM vs. Severson vs. Bionic Carrot - Hardcore Three-Way (non-title) "Mr. Self-Destruct" hits and NFM comes out to blistering heat. "Play the Game" hits and Bionic Carrot comes out to a pop that can only be described as the kind of pop that Bionic Carrot gets. Then Severson comes out to a pop that isn't as big as Sev himself, but still pretty sizable. All three men stare each other down, and then Sev explodes from his corner with a double lariat on the other two, who roll out of the ring to regroup as Sev stands - looking imposing. AT: Well, this looks to be quite the hardcore three-way. BJ: What did you just say? AT: This looks to be quite the hardcore three-way, Barry. BJ: BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! AT tries to figure out what's so funny as BC gets a chair and NFM grabs the kendo stick - still outside the ring from the last match. Both men get back in the ring, start to go for each other, and then turn and begin to mercilessly bludgeon Severson. After about ten shots from each man, Sev goes down to one knee. Ten more and Sev finally goes down. The two men continue to beat on Severson for a good minute or two, and then BC smacks NFM with the chair. The two men begin to exchange weapon shots as Sev lies in the fetal position in about a quarter of the ring. Finally, NFM goes down. BC drops his chair and begins to stomp on NFM. NFM rolls out of the ring and then under the ring. BC follows him out, but didn't see him go under the ring. NFM re-emerges on the adjacent side with a cookie sheet. He rushes around and levels BC with a shot from said cookie sheet. NFM DDT's BC on the floor, goes to the time keeper, and grabs the hardcore title. He begins whipping BC with the belt on the floor AT: Say what you will about NFM and F.E.A.R, but the man knows his hardcore! BJ: Hehehehe..."hardcore three way".... Slowly, BC gets to his feet as NFM continues to beat him with the belt. BC surprises NFM with a kick to the midsection. NFM loses his grip on the belt and it flies into the ring, landing next to Sev, who slowly reaches out and grabs it, wrapping it around his gigantic fist. BC begins to turn the tables on NFM, hitting a spinning neckbreaker on the floor. He then hits a super shoulderbreaker on NFM and goes for the pin, but only gets a two. As NFM gets to his feet, he rakes BC's eyes, which only serves to enrage the Horseman. NFM takes of running around he ring, followed by BC. NFM slides into the ring and runs back out the other side. BC makes it about halfway across the ring when, with a final burst of energy, Sev raises to his feet and punches BC in the head with the Hardcore title belt. Sev falls onto BC and gets the pin. Winner by Pinfall - Severson NFM gets back in the ring and begins to beat on Severson, quickly joined by BoneCrusher and Blitzwing. BC gets to his feet and grabs a chair from outside the ring as Sue Wolfe runs down from the back. AT: It's Sue Wolfe! It's Sue Wolfe! BJ: Who? AT: One of Severson's old partners in Exodus! Holy f***! Sue and BC chase off F.E.A.R and help Sev to his feet. AT: Severson has his shot at Blitzwing! BJ: Yeah, but in the shape he's in, how's he going to make it to the match? AT: I haven't a damn clue! HBK /w Sunny vs. Rob the Throb "Your Disease" hits and Rob The Throb comes out to massive heat. He stands in the center of the ring and waits. The lights go out in the arena and the Photex remix of "Six Feet Under" begins to play as the arena is bathed in dimly flickering orange light. HBK and Sunny come out onto the stage to a pop which is so much more than a pop that words cannot describe it, although perhaps an interpretive dance could. HBK rushes to the ring and spears the holy hell out of RTT, who looks utterly shocked. RTT stumbles to his feet and into a falling side suplex from HBK. With no fanfare, HBK goes to a corner and crouches. RTT once again gets up and eats the Sweet Chin Music. HBK gets the easy pin and leaves again. Winner by Pinfall - HBK AT: DAMN! BJ: DAMN! AT: DAMN! BJ: DAMN! AT: That was how you make a comeback! BJ: DAMN! AT: Next up in the Submission title match between Esco and Henrik! BJ: DAMN! Henrik the King (c) vs. Supathug Riley Escobar - Submission Title Match "Bad Dream" hits and Riley comes out to massive heat. There're a few moments of silence before "Rule Britannia" hits and Henrik comes out to a VERY mixed reaction. Henrik is bandaged up and looks like hell, but he walks with pride to the ring. He gets in the ring and gets directly in Esco's face. The ref signals for the bell, and Henrik kicks Esco in the midsection and begins to beat him back to a corner, but Esco blocks and levels the injured Henrik with a massive right. He kicks Henrik in the ribs repeatedly, picks him up, and hits the Beatdown. Riley applies the Thug Mug as the ref checks Henrik, who is understandably unconscious. The ref calls for the bell. Winner and New Submissions Champion - Supathug Esco EMTs come out to check on Henrik. AT: Henrik's not moving...this doesn't look good. BJ: It's a shame anytime a wrestler is injured... AT: Hold on... Slowly, Henrik gets to his feet under his own power. He stumbles briefly, and then turns and waves to the crowd, which explodes into cheers. Henrik looks shocked as a "Henrik" chant starts and follows him all the way to the back. AT: Wow...it's good to see that Henrik is relatively ok. BJ: He should still probably let the EMTs check on him, though. He looks like he was in a car wreck. AT: He was. BJ: Well then...that would explain it. DAMN! I'm still getting over HBK... AT: And now the moment you've all been waiting for...the main event...the Royal Legion faces the Deadly Combination for the tag titles! The Deadly Combination (c) vs. The Royal Legion - Tag Team Title Match "Rule Britannia" hits once again and the Royal Legion comes out to massive heat. "Deadly Combination" hits and, surprise surprise, the Deadly Combination comes out to a gigantic pop. The ref signals for the bell as Numark ties up with RX. Numark takes advantage with a side headlock. RX throws Numark off the ropes and knocks him down with a standing shoulderblock. He picks up Numark and goes for a vertical suplex, but Nu spins it around into a Fujiwara armbar. RX grins at the pain, causing Nu to release the hold, looking slightly disgusted. RX gets to his feet and he and Nu circle each other slowly. Suddenly, RX springs, whipping Nu off the ropes and levelling him with a clothesline. He pulls up Numark and hits a shoulderbreaker. RX drags Nu to the corner and tags in Bailey, who had been contendedly munching on the turnbuckle. Bailey bites Nu on the forehead and then hits a belly-to-belly suplex. He goes for the cover, but only gets a two. AT: Looks like the Royal Legion is taking control here. BJ: DAMN! AT: Still stuck on HBK? BJ: DAMN! Bailey applies his patented sleeper to Numark, who starts to elbow out of it. He hits a piledriver on Nu, drags him to the corner, and then tags in RX. Bailey picks up Numark for the Down Under, RX connects with the top rope elbow and goes for the cover, and the ref slides in to make the count...which seems a little slow. One...two..........KICKOUT! AT: Was it just me, or did that count seem a little slow? BJ: DAMN! AT: You think so, too? BJ: DAMN! A frustrated RX starts arguing with the ref, completely ignoring Numark. Nu crawls to his corner and tags in MV Pete, who levels Raven from behind with a clothesline from behind. Pete whips RX off the ropes and hits a big boot, which he follows up by pulling RX up and hitting the Prime Time Chokeslam. Bailey gets in the ring and attacks MV Pete, but Numark has recovered himself and leaps into action, knocking Bailey out of the ring and following him to the floor. As RX and Pete get up, Pete goes for a right hand, but Raven ducks and hits a huge release German suplex. RX sets up for the Family Values, but Pete lifts him up and heads for the corner, setting RX up on the top turnbuckle. He punches Raven in the gut and climbs up as Numark ascends an adjacent turnbuckle. They hit the Total Devastation to a huge pop. Pete goes for the pin and gets the three. Winners and Still Tag Champions - The Deadly Combination AT: The Deadly Combination has retained the titles! But there's going to be some controversy over that seeming slow count by the referee... BJ: DAMN! AT: Well, that's all the time we have folks! This is the Atomic Tomato for Barry Johnston saying... BJ: DAMN! The show fades out on Pete and Nu posing with their belts to the adulation of the fans.
