XUW Mayhem #68

3 Mar 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Mayhem - Number 68 Pyro flies a bit, but not much really, since the pyro all broke yesterday and messed up the whole darn show. Technology eh? Can’t trust it… One thing you can trust is signs. Oh yes. “I WONDER IF THIS SHOW WILL BE RUSHED AND CRAPPY?” “L.O.S.T TITLES > XUW TITLES” “SIDHE IS GONNA LICK DANTE” “JACK SUCKS, HE SHOULD JUST LEAVE OR SOMETHING.” Without further ado, we go to the announce team of Barry Johnson and Atomic Tomato. Atomic Tomato: The technology here could go bust at any time, so if this edition of Mayhem seems at all rushed, then it’s because of that, and no other reason at all. Barry Johnson: Stupid arena. This place sucks. AT: Well, tonight we’re bound to find out some more matches for Reborn, and… BJ: You poor dumb tomato. The card was announced yesterday on the XUW website! AT: Oh. BJ: HAHAHAHAHAHA! AT: Well, then we’re just going to have some stellar matches. Unless the lights go off or something. BJ: That would pretty much be a tragedy. Triple Threat Match Hot Boy D Vs. Hodgo Vs. Raven X ”Area Codes” by Ludacris hits and HBD walks out from behind the crowd to a nice pop. He waves to the fans and struts his way down to the ring. “Anarchy in the UK” by The Sex Pistols then hits and the cheers turn into an even louder chorus of boos as Hodgo steps out from behind the curtain. He flips the fans off as he makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and the ref keeps the two separated as “Liberate” by Slipknot hits and the booing just continues. Raven X steps out from behind the curtain and looks around the crowd with a sadistic smile on his face and his half of the tag team championship belts around his waist. He makes his way down to the ring jeering back at some of the fans along the way. He takes his title off and slides in the ring as the bell sounds, signalling this one is underway. RX and Hodgo look at each other and both nod before turning their attention on HBD and bum rushing him into the corner. He does his best to hold both men off with punches, pushing RX away from him and then turning his attention to Hodgo who he punches a few times, before RX comes back with a kick to HBD ’s gut. Hodgo and RX then hit HBD with a double suplex. AT: Looks like Hot Boy D could only hold them off for so long, but not many people could have even held them both at bay for that long. RX and Hodgo lay a few boots into HBD while he is down before picking him back up again. They whip him to the ropes and HBD runs right back into a double spinebuster from his opponents. Hodgo then picks HBD up and lets lose a barrage of Anar-Kicks on HBD until he collapses to the mat. RX then picks up HBD and hoists him up onto his shoulders. RX then nails HBD with the Purity (Death Valley Driver), before rolling HBD out of the ring. Hodgo and RX then turn and look at each other before getting face to face in the middle of the ring. AT: Looks like the two former Common Wealth members are getting set to get it on here. BJ: Well, I knew Raven was a strange guy, but I don’t know about getting it on with Hodgo in the middle of the ring. AT: That’s not what I meant, you idiot. Back inside the ring, the two opponents start doing some s*** talking, until the punches start to fly. They both just keep unloading punches on each other, neither man showing any signs of backing down, until RX grabs Hodgo’s arm and whips him to the ropes. Hodgo springs off the ropes and runs right into a vicious clothesline that sends him down to the mat. Hodgo is back up to his feet in no time and RX takes him back down with another clothesline. Hodgo springs back up to his feet a third time, but this time RX doesn’t go for a clothesline, instead he grabs a hold of Hodgo and tosses him over his head with a belly to belly suplex. RX then stomps on his downed opponent a couple of times before walking over to the ropes. RX backs himself up to the second rope, before jumping down on Hodgo hitting him with a knee drop to the head. RX then goes for the cover… One… Two… Kick Out! RX picks Hodgo up again quickly and sends him to the ropes once more. Hodgo bounces off the ropes and RX grabs a hold of him and nails him with the Big Bang Theory (Spinning Spinebuster). RX then goes for the cover again… One… Two… Broken up by HBD! AT: Looks like the Hot Boy still has some life left in him after all! BJ: Hot Boy… heh. HBD grabs RX by the hair and lifts him to his feet, after the stomp to the head he used to break the pin up. HBD gives RX a swift kick to the gut, before planting him to the mat with a solid powerbomb that seems to take the wind out of RX. HBD then turns around to see Hodgo running at him with a clothesline and ducks it at the last second. Hodgo runs through the ropes and bounces off running back at HBD, but he is ready for his this time, and Hodgo runs right into a beautiful standing dropkick from XUW’s pimp. HBD then picks up Hodgo and wastes no time nailing him with the Destruction (Double Underhook Brainbuster). HBD quickly goes for the pin as the crowd cheers… One… Two… Broken up by RX! RX returned the favor to HBD with a boot to his head that time. RX then lays a couple more boots into the side of HBD’s head as Hodgo rises to his feet. Hodgo can be seen saying something to RX, and RX nods. Raven then picks HBD up and grabs a hold of him around the neck and sets him up for a DDT as Hodgo grabs HBD’s legs. Hodgo lifts his legs into the air and pushes down on his legs while RX drops back hitting him with a spike DDT. AT: Wow, these two are actually working together well. Even though they are facing off at Reborn for the tag titles… and they are beating up their ref! HBD gets planted into the mat and appears to be out of it. RX starts to get to his feet, but before he can Hodgo lands a martial arts kick right to RX’s temple, sending him back down. Hodgo then picks up RX quickly and whips him to the corner, causing RX to slam his back into the turnbuckles at full speed. Hodgo then runs in with an elbow right into the jaw of RX sending his head springing to the side and backwards in a very unhealthy looking manner. Hodgo then hits another equally devastating elbow into the jaw of RX. Hodgo then pulls RX out of the corner and backs himself in. Hodgo then climbs up to the second rope and grabs a hold of RX nailing him with The Molotov Cocktail (Second Rope Piledriver). RX slams into the mat with a sickening thud, before grabbing his neck as Hodgo pushes him out of the ring and to the floor below. Hodgo then goes back over to HBD, who is still motionless and lifts him up to his feet. Hodgo then nails HBD with the Riot Breaker (Hangmans DDT), which basically knocks HBD out cold, if he wasn’t before. Hodgo then goes for the cover… One… Two… Three!!! Winner via pinfall, Hodgo! AT: Hodgo with a brutal display in the ring tonight, taking out both his opponents, after getting beaten most of the match. BJ: That’s what the Ultra Violence is all about, baby. ”Anarchy in the UK” hits once more and the crowd boos like crazy as Hodgo rolls out of the ring and starts walking up the ramp. He turns around about halfway up the ramp and sees RX is on his feet and in front of the ring, holding his neck with one hand, his tag title belt in the other, and a sadistic grin on his face. RX tosses the belt over his shoulder and just stares up at Hodgo, as Hodgo stares back down at him. AT: What a great match that was, and we have another good one in store for you next, as Danyon takes on Sheri! BJ: Who better than Danyon? “Fight” plays, and out come Sheri and Numark to a bunch of cheering. Soon it is replaced with boos as “Hello Time Bomb” plays and Bailey and Danyon come out. While in the ring, Bailey asks for a microphone. He looks around at the crowd while they boo at him. He is given the mic. Bailey: Okay……before this match begins, we have a few things to talk about. It mostly deals with the blatant love that you have for my esteemed tag team partner, Danyon. You do know that he is the greatest international wrestler ever to grace the ring in XUW. Maybe that is why you are so attracted to him. He’s a successful foreigner……you girls love that type of stuff. So what I am suggesting is that….and Danyon has agreed to this………. If Danyon wins….he has agreed to go on a date with you. Sheri grabs a mic as well and answers to Bailey. Sheri: So, wait a second. Let me get this straight. If you win <points at Danyon> I get to go on a date with him. I don’t know what is blinder you or a bat. I don’t quite understand what the point of this stipulation is but I will agree to it. Danyon: But we have one more thing as well to offer you. I know how much you envy us and our titles. So, we have something for you……..a title belt of your own. Larry The Legionnaire walks up to Danyon and opens up his silver briefcase to reveal a title belt. Danyon pulls out the wooden title belt and hands it to Sheri. Danyon: I present you the Super Legionnaires Universal Team Title……..the S.L.U.T. Title. Sheri looks at the title belt. She then hits Bailey with the title belt and then hits Danyon with it. The ref calls for the bell as the match starts. Bailey and Larry quickly leave the ring. Singles Match: Sheri v Danyon Sheri circles Danyon and goes to kick him in the gut as he tries to get up. Danyon grabs her foot and trips her up, sending Sheri to the mat. Sheri quickly hops up and puts Danyon in a side headlock. Danyon counters by lifting Sheri up and planting her to the mat with a sidewalk slam. Danyon gets up and grabs Sheri from behind, as though he is about to deliver a german suplex. But instead, he just stands there with a big smile on his face as the crowd boos. AT: Oh come on now! BJ: Nice work Danyon. Cop a feel! Danyon lifts Sheri off the ground as though he is going to suplex her, but she counters by leaning forward and putting her legs back, hooking Danyon’s arms and rolling him in front of her, setting up a pin... 1... 2... kickout with force AT: Close one there. Danyon is being too cocky in there against a former tag team champion. BJ: Hehe you said cocky. There is something She.. AT: <interrupting> OK! back to the match. Danyon and Sheri get up at the same time and Sheri delivers a few standing kicks to the face, not really phasing Danyon much. Sheri then bounces off the ropes and goes for a clothesline. It connects, but doesn’t faze Danyon much. Danyon laughs at Sheri as she bounces off the ropes and goes for another. Again, not much effect, except this time Danyon bounces back a few feet. Danyon motions for a third time and Sheri obliges. She bounces off the ropes and goes for a clothesline, but at the last second floats around behind Danyon and hooks him up for a german suplex... BJ: Look at her fondling Danyon like that banana! She wants to know all about the New Zealand love machine! Sheri goes to lift Danyon up but only gets him up a couple of inches. Danyon laughs but he doesn’t laugh for long. Sheri kicks Danyon right in the little superstars and Danyon lets out a huge groan along with every male in the arena, along with Numark who also leans over in pain. Sheri winks at Nu and gathers all her strength. The crowd pops huge as Sheri lifts Danyon up and plants him with a german suplex and bridges. 1.. 2.. 2.75 kickout! Bailey looks relieved in the corner as Sheri hops up and the crowd cheers her on. Danyon slowly gets up, grabbing the family jewels, as Sheri gets alongside him and sets him up for a russian leg sweep. Right as she is about to do it, Danyon turns and clotheslines Sheri stiffly to the mat. Numark glares at Danyon as Bailey applauds Danyon’s efforts. Danyon gets down on the mat and takes much pleasure covering Sheri... 1.. 2.. Shoulder up! Danyon pins her shoulders to the mat again.. 1.. 2.. Kickout again. AT: Oh get off of her you pervert. She kicked out already! The crowd boos Danyon as he climbs on top of Sheri and pins her shoulders to the mat again... 1... 2... Shoulder up! Danyon goes to pin her down once more but Sheri gets her arm free and slaps the taste out of Danyon’s mouth. Danyon grabs his face as Sheri squirms free and dropkicks Danyon in the face, sending him down. Sheri hops up on the top turnbuckle and waits for Danyon to get up. He does and slowly turns around, and the crowd pops huge as Sheri goes for a top rope hurricanrana. Danyon catches her as she bends down to finish the move. The fans laugh and boo at the same time as the two wrestlers are in an awkward position in the ring. BJ: Hey Danyon, how’s the view from there? AT: Numark can’t be liking this right about now. Sheri sits back up as Danyon gives a thumbs up, which bring boos of hatred and probably jealousy as well. Danyon gets set to powerbomb Sheri but she starts nailing Danyon with lefts and rights. Danyon starts to stumble around while Sheri continues the assault, not giving an inch and trying to get out of the impending move. Sheri pushes forward and Danyon falls back to the mat, landing with a resounding thud. It’s a cover... 1... 2... Kickout with force! BJ: Uh oh. Danyon looks upset now. Danyon and Sheri get up quickly and Danyon charges. Sheri counters with a swift kick to the gut, followed by the Dream Starter(stunner) to a HUGE crowd pop. Bailey slams the mat in frustration as Sheri goes for the pin.. 1... 2... 2.95 SHOULDER UP!! AT: Sheri almost did it Barry! She almost defeated Danyon! BJ: Very good Captain Obvious potato head. Sheri goes to lift up Danyon but he nails her with a picture perfect DDT. Sheri looks to be almost out of it and Danyon shakes the cobwebs out of his head. Bailey goes over to where the S.L.U.T. title lays on the ground and slides it into the ring next to Danyon. Bailey then gets on the apron and yells out, getting the ref’s attention. AT: Did you see what that no good Bailey slid into the ring? It’s really sad that Danyon has to resort to this to pick up a victory. BJ: What are you talking about cumquat? Danyon is just presenting Sheri with her title. Danyon picks up the wooden belt and lifts it up, about to “present” it to Sheri, but Numark hops on the ring apron and grabs the belt at the last second. Danyon turns around, stunned, and Numark wallops Danyon with a massive shot to the temple with the belt. Danyon goes down in a heap and Numark quickly hops off the apron. Sheri crawls over and covers as the ref turns around... 1... 2... 3!!! WINNER: SHERI!!! BJ: What the hell is this? This is a conspiracy! Numark and Sheri cheated! AT: Give it a rest already. It serves Danyon right for the s**t he’s been pulling lately. BJ: Watch the language pomegranate. We are on Fox now, and you can easily be replaced. AT: <sighs under his breath> Sometimes I wish I was. With seamless editing, the commentators hush up and listen for the arrivals for the next match between Bob and DDT. XUW Submission Title Match: DDT v Samurai Bob “Only” by Anthrax plays, and out comes DDT to a barrage of heat and abuse from the near capacity crowd. It would be full, but some people couldn’t make two days in a row, some fetishists left after Sheri finished, and some of the scalpers outside were busy having their arms burned. Allegedly. “F**k That!” hits the speakers and the near capacity crowd makes approximately the same noise as a big bloody noisy thing that’s decided to throw caution to the wind and become incredibly noisy. Yes boys and girls, it’s Samurai Bob! He comes out and the noise gets noisier, as noises are wont to do. He comes down to the ring with Naron following behind him. He wears the Submission Title that he will be defending tonight, and Naron carries the World Title. Bob kisses Naron, and then the World Title, before taking off the Sub belt and handing it to the ref. As he does so, DDT jumps him immediately and I guess that means the match is on. DDT keeps the attack going, hitting some forearms and then whipping Bob to the ropes. Bob comes back and DDT nails him with a Spinebuster. Bob gets up and DDT grabs him, swinging him into a Short Arm Clothesline. AT: “Surprisingly good start from DDT here!” BJ: “Surprising? He’s a former champion in this discipline, in hardcore, and he’s one half of the tag champs!” AT: “He ain’t bad, I’ll give him that.” DDT hits some more Forearm Smashes, and then hits a hard Belly-to-Belly Suplex. He goes for a quick cover… and then remembers that it’s a Submission Title match, and slaps on the Strate Jackit (STF). Bob struggles to work himself over to the ropes, and finally manages it. The aged knight gets to his feet and stretches out his arms, looking DDT right in the eye and locking up with him. He pushes the Hardcore Icon back, and then nails a Snapp Suplex that shakes the ring. Bob goes for the quick cover.. but also remembers it's a Submission Title match. AT: "Bob has flawless execution with that snapp suplex." BJ: "Yeah, I gotta say, it's one of the best in the biz." Bob picks up DDT, and whips him to the ropes. He kicks the Tag Champion in the gut, picks him up and hits an impressive sit out powerbomb. Bob then rolls DDT over on his stomach, as he applies a Fujiwara armbar. DDT screams out in pain, but shakes his head defiantely, refusing to tap. He slowly inches his way towards the ropes, as he stretches out with his right foot, hooking onto the ropes, effectively breaking the hold. Bob continues to work over DDT's arm, as he picks him up and slams him down to the mat, with his arm positioned behind his back. AT: "Smart move by the double champion here." BJ: "Yeah, isolate one part of the opponents body, and go to work on it." Bob picks up DDT once more, as he hoists him over his shoulder, and drops the AoD member over his knee, hitting a devastating shoulderbreaker. Bob then picks up DDT's arm, drags it over to the corner of the ring, where he proceeds to ram it into the turnbuckles several times, as DDT yelps out in pain. Bob picks up the fallen Hardcore Icons, and whips him to the ropes. Bob attempts to go for a backdrop, but DDT telegraphs the move in advance, and hooks Bob up for the vicious Shock Treatment (DDT). DDT however can't capitalize on the move, as he is also knocked out on the canvas. AT: "Had this been a normal match, it might have all been over by now." BJ: "No doubt about it. No one gets up from the Shock Treatment." Both men slowly get back to their feet at the same time, and begin to exchange right hands. DDT takes the power advantage, as he backs the Samurai into the corner. DDT unleashes with vicious knees to Bob's midsection, as the double champion doubles over in pain. DDT turns Bob around and hits him with a devastating belly-to-back suplex. DDT rolls Bob over onto his stomach, as DDT steps on top of Bob, and hooks him up with the Dark Eternal (Full Nelson Camel Clutch)! AT: "This must be it. Bob can't escape from this move!" BJ: "DDT is weighing him down too much, he can't move. He has to tap!" Bob furiously shakes his head at the referee's pleas, refusing to give up. He tries to inch his way towards the ropes, but like the Majestical One said, DDT is weighing down the World champion too much. Bob starts to fade, as the crowd begins to cheer and chant for the Samurai, pleading for him to escape out of the move. With one last burst of energy, Bob tries with all his might to...actually power out of the move, which he succeeds at doing! Bob slowly gets to his knees, with DDT on his back! AT: "What an extraordinary power display by Bob!" BJ: "Son of a...why doesn't he just die already?!" Bob staggers around the ring, still with DDT holding onto his back. Bob picks up DDT and falls backwards, crushing the Tag champion as he falls to the canvas. Both men are out once more time. AT: "What a move by Bob!" BJ: "Bah. Bob this, Bob that. Come on DDT!" AT: "Wait a minute, look who's here! Damnit, he shouldn't be out here!" BJ: "Whoo hoo! Here comes the Ripper!" Sure enough, a huge chorus of boos emanate from the crowd as the Ripper slowly makes his way down to the ring. The referee steps out onto the ring apron, and scolds the Ripper for being at ringside, as Jack simply raises his arms, pleading his innocence. Jack stays firmly planted at ringside, but doesn't move towards the ring. AT: "Why is it that whenver the Army of Darkness have a match, the other members of the stable are always in sight to interfere?" BJ: "Interfere? The Ripper would never do such a thing, and frankly, I think he'd be insulted that you suggested such a horrific tactic." Bob gets to his feet slowly, as he turns his attention to the Ripper, pointing at the former World champion, and hurling insults at him. Suddenly DDT attacks Bob from behind with a forearm shot, sending Bob flying through the middle ropes. DDT distracts the referee, as the Ripper picks up Bob and rolls him back into the ring. BJ: "See? I told you, he didn't do anything to Bob, even when he had the chance!" AT: "I'm not buying it. Something's up." DDT whips Bob to the ropes and attempts a clothesline, but Bob ducks the move and hits a huge Steiner-line of his own, several times. Bob picks up the Hardcore Icons, and hits a running bulldog across the ring to a big pop. Bob picks up DDT, and hits the belly-to-back brainbuster. Bob signals for the end, as suddenly, the lights go out. AT: "What the hell?" BJ: "WHO SAID THAT?" As the arena remains completely dark, there's a huge blue flash in the ring, and a few seconds later, the lights come back up to reveal DDT in the ring smiling, with Bob laying on the mat unconscious! Jack is laughing maniacally at ringside, as the camera pans behind DDT to show that he's infact holding a tazer behind his back! AT: "That lying, cheating, no good son of a..." BJ: "Hey, you don't know that, it could only be there for show!" Bob is lying motionless on the mat, as DDT rolls him over and applies the Dark Eternal once more. AT: "Bob is completely out of it." BJ: "He looks extremely disorientated!" DDT pulls back on the move as much as possible, and after a few seconds, Samurai Bob taps out! Winner via submission and NEW XUW Submission Champion: DDT!!! AT: "Damnit! It's not supposed to end like this!" BJ: "Why not? I certainly didn't see anything illegal..." Suddenly, Jack the Ripper slides into the ring as he and DDT collectively begin to put the boots to the unconscious World champion. DDT picks up Bob and hits another Shock Treatment in the middle of the ring. Jack signals his hand across his throat, as he picks up Bob and with a testament to his strength, lifts up the World champion with ease, and powerfull Jacknifes him to the mat with the RipSaw. Both men pose over the World champion's beaten and bruised body, with DDT holding up his newly won Submission Title. AT: "Damnit! Someone get some help out here!" BJ: "Bob looks in a really bad way here. Seriously, get some EMT's out here or something." AT: "This does not fare well for Bob at Reborn, he's at such a disadvantage thanks to the blatent cheating from the Army of Darkness." EMT's rush out to the ring, and tend to unconscious Samurai Bob. Jack just stays there, laughing, until “Heroes” hits the speakers, and Henrik rushes down to join in the fight. Jack ushers the other AoD guys to the back. BJ: Main Event time! Woohoo! Singles Match: Henrik The King v Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy AT: These two have fought a lot recently. There’s no love lost between them, and I’m sure we’re in for a treat here. Jack starts off by clocking Henrik with some hard right hands, and then whips him to the ropes and flattens him with a Big Boot. BJ: Jack is starting off as he finished off with Bob just moment earlier! The Ripper picks up Henrik and nails him with a Pumphandle Slam. Giving the Number One Contender to the World Title no respite, he picks him up and nails him with the Gutwrench Backbreaker known to one and all as ‘The Break’. He keeps Henrik over his knee, pushing down with both of his hands and exerting great pressure on the vertebrae of the smaller man. AT: I swear Henrik looks bigger in there than before… Jack lifts Henrik to his feet and whips him to the ropes. The King is not too big to duck under a clumsy Clothesline attempt, and he does so, gaining momentum as he runs and coming back off the far ropes with a Flying Forearm, which staggers The Ripper. Henrik gets up and whips Jack to the ropes, hitting a Back Body Drop as he comes back, taking the big man off his feet. The smaller man tries to lock in a submission hold, but Jack pushes him away and gets to his feet, fire flashing in his eyes. They lock up, and Jack gets the advantage, lifting up Henrik and hitting a Stalled Vertical Suplex. The Ripper makes a cover. ONE… … KICKOUT! AT: It’ll take more than that to keep Henrik down for the count! BJ: Bah! The crowd start to chant for Henrik as he gets to his feet, but then go back to being quietened as Jack nearly tears his head off with a vicious Lariat. AT: Jack has been trying to get Henrik to doubt himself, to get Henrik to think he’s nothing unless he cheats. It’s a despicable tactic. BJ: Henrik sucks. AT: Thanks for that insight. Jack stands over his fallen foe, and laughs right in his face. He picks up Henrik, but the King reverses into a Small Package. ONE… … TWO… … KICKOUT! Jack gets up and looks furious, swinging wildly at Henrik’s head. Henrik dodges to one side and grabs him, hitting a Running Bulldog. He looks to go for a pin, but once more Jack just shoves him off. Henrik rebounds off the ropes and looks to hit a Rising Knee, but jack springs forwards with a vicious Spear to knock the wind out of Henrik. AT: As quickly as that, Jack can turn the tables on you. The Ripper picks up Henrik once more and nails him with a huge Call of Destiny (Albamaslam). He stands over Henrik and locks in The Fear (Steiner Recliner). BJ: This could be it right here! What dominance from Jack! AT: I think Henrik’s got enough strength left to fight him. Look, he’s nearly at the ropes. In fact, he’s at the ropes, and he manages to grab hold of the bottom one. Jack releases the hold, and takes a step back, waiting for Henrik to get to his feet. Instead of pressing his advantage though, he takes a step back. AT: Hey, what are they doing here? Sidhe and M V Pete run down to ringside, one carrying a baseball bat and the other with a Singapore Cane. Sidhe taps Henrik on the leg, and then passes him the cane. AT: What the hell? BJ: They’ve turned on The Ripper! Nooooooo! Henrik looks at Sidhe as if he were mad. Well, madder than usual. He throws the cane away, and charges The Ripper, spearing him all the way into the far turnbuckle. He then backs off before dashing in to nail an Avalanche, and follows it up with another Running Bulldog. As Jack is lying down on the floor, Pete passes Henrik the baseball bat. Henrik looks at it, and weighs up his options. AT: I’ve got it! They want Henrik to cheat! They want him to turn his back on the fans that have carried him this far in his career! Henrik drops the weapon and picks up The Ripper, hitting an impressive Snap Suplex, and then the Brain Biter (DDT). He hooks the leg. ONE… … TWO… … SHOULDER UP! BJ: Henrik simply can’t beat Jack without cheating. Look, now he’s getting a chair from Pete! AT: No, Henrik! Don’t do it! Henrik goes to swing the chair… and then just throws it out of the ring! He hits the rising Jack with a Clothesline that staggers him, and whips him to the corner. AT: He didn’t cheat! He believes he can beat The Ripper by himself! Henrik follows Jack in and clambers up above him on the turnbuckle, jumping off to hit the London Calling (Top Rope Bulldog). The crowd are popping hugely for Henrik turning his back on his past cheating ways. He gets to the top of the turnbuckle and signals for the Kingpin. AT: If he hits this, it’s over! He goes to jump, but as he does so, Pete jumps up and pushes him off. Sidhe slides in the ring and nails him with the Brink of Twilight, and the ref calls for the bell. Winner, by DQ: Henrik The King!!! Back in the ring, Jack is up to his feet, and he nails down a hard RipSaw on Henrik, slamming him to the mat with authority as Sidhe and Pete hold him in place. The three men look down on the battered superstar and laugh at him as they put the boot in even more. AT: Dammit! Jack is trying to make sure that neither of his opponents can make it to Reborn! Do either of them even stand a chance to leave with the World Title? BJ: No! Ha ha ha ha ha! AT: Well, we shall see as we get closer to the biggest PPV of the year. Don’t touch that dial. BJ: Or remote. Fade to Black
