XUW Mayhem #98

1 Dec 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Mayhem - Number 98 -Begin Show- Boom bang-a-bang, it’s time for Mayhem! More booms, more bangs, and less useful descriptive stuff. It’s Monday night and that can mean only one thing… XUW Mayhem is once again taking over the glowing box in the corner of the room. The fan are packed into the XUW Arena, crammed together in a way that surely contravenes a thousand health and safety regulations… but they wouldn’t have it any other way. The atmosphere is electric, the pyro is flying, and the Hungry Mexican just got killed backstage… again. So, the fans in the expensive seats, and for that read the ones that actually got seats instead of standing on an uneven surface covered in broken glass, have brought with them their signs. “2004 – The Year of the Skeletons of Society!” “Vote Ric Austin!” “Danyon > Bailey” and “I am delaying the Chris Williams Memorial Cup!” The man with that last sign then gets tackled to the ground and beaten senseless by the rest of the fans. Nice. We join the usual Mayhem commentary team, Mikey and “The Majestic” Barry Johnson, down at ringside. Mikey: Hello there everyone, and welcome to Mayhem! BJ: Why do I never get to introduce the show? Mikey: Well, it’s because you’re an alcoholic gambling addict who sucks at introductions and predictions, and really at anything to do with wrestling. BJ: Yes, but other than that? Mikey: Maybe I’ll let you do it next week. BJ: Woohoo! Mikey: Well, we’ve got a great show lined up for you tonight, with four big matches, one of them involving a title defence. We’re also sure to get more information about the happenings in the build-up to December to Dismember Three. BJ: Ah yes, the final wrestling show of the year, and the one containing the Main Event where The Anarchist will destroy Dante. Mikey: Well, I wouldn’t be too sure of that, Barry. You’ll notice that Dante cost The Anarchist the Hardcore Title on Vortex, so he’ll be gunning to make it two from two on December 14th. BJ: Bah. Well, at least the Dark Alliance will destroy the Lunchroom Patrol once and for all. We all know that the Ultra Violents have their number. Mikey: Yes they do… if you forget all the times that the Lunchroom Patrol have beaten the UVs. BJ: With my memory, I forget all the things that I don’t want to remember. Mikey: I suspected as much. BJ: The third match that has already been signed is between Danyon and Bailey, and I for one will be glad to see the back of Danyon. Mikey: What do you mean? BJ: Well surely Bailey will finally defeat the man who has been holding him back all this time. Mikey: Holding him back? The fans love Danyon! He’s as much a part of the New Royal Legion as Bailey! BJ: The New Royal Legion is no more my friend. It’s the dawn of a new age, of an age beyond conspiracies. The age of Bailey! Mikey: Well, I can’t possibly agree with that. BJ: You don’t have to. The truth of the Age of Bailey does not need your belief. Mikey: You’ve been watching the Matrix too often. BJ: Bah. Mikey: Well, enough with the talking let’s get some action here between Bubba Bailey and DJ! “Main In The Box” by Alice in Chains plays, and out comes DJ to a decent sized pop. He walks down to the ring with terry Tate tagging along behind him, and enters the ring. Mikey: It looks like DJ has brought an insurance policy with him to the ring. BJ: Well, he’ll certainly need life insurance once the Skeletons of Society finish with him! DJ waits in the ring as the lights start to dim, and “As Above… So Below” plays, and Bubba Bailey starts to walk down the ramp towards the ring. He is followed by Overkill, another member of the Skeletons of Society. Bubba reaches the ring, the boos from the crowd ringing in his ears, and proceeds to walk around it once, scoping out his opponent and the man he has brought down with him. BJ: It looks like Bubba has brought someone else out to even up the cheating of DJ! Mikey: Oh don’t be ridiculous, it’s Bubba that’s the one likely to cheat! Opening Match: DJ v Bubba Bailey Bubba sizes up the much bigger man in the ring, DJ a full six inches taller as he stands just below seven feet tall. The go to lock up, but DJ just bodily throws Bubba into the corner, and the crowd pop. Bubba looks around, almost scared, but then composes himself and rushes towards DJ, ducking under a Clothesline attempt and then hitting a Chop Block to the back of DJ’s left knee. DJ drops to the ground, and Bubba goes to work on the knee, stamping on it a few times and then lifting it off the canvas before smashing it back down. The big man holds his injured appendage, as Bubba climbs to the second turnbuckle and waits for DJ to get back up. As he does so, Bubba jumps off and hits a Thrust Kick to the abdomen of DJ. BJ: The Lethal Injection! Bubba’s doing it all on his own, he doesn’t need any help from the other Skeletons! DJ staggers back, clutching downwards, and Bubba runs off the ropes and hits him with a Shoulder Tackle, which staggers the big man even more. Bubba flies off the ropes one more time, and a big Clothesline sends both men over the top rope and down to the floor outside. Mikey: Things could get interesting here… Bubba distracts the referee away, as DJ gets back to his feet. The Skeleton of Society complains about some imagined slight, allowing Overkill to stalk up behind DJ. He hits a kick to the gut… … …but then Terry Tate comes through and flattens him with a huge tackle! The crowd pops big, as DJ gets back up on the ring apron to confront Bubba. Bubba throws a quick thumb to the eyes and then grabs DJ’s head, dropping down to smash DJ’s throat into the rope. DJ reels back but holds on to the rope and doesn’t fall. He gasps for breath, but ducks under an attempted punch from Bubba and then drives his shoulder between the ropes, into the gut of his opponent. Bubba doubles over, and DJ takes control, stepping over the top rope and sending Bubba down to the floor with a clubbing arm to the back. The big man lifts up Bubba and Gorilla Presses him, before turning it into a Powerslam! Mikey: The Heaven and Hell! What strength from DJ! He’s showing why he’s the Number One Contender for the Intercontinental Title right here! Bubba’s body slams into the mat, and DJ draws a finger across his throat, to a big pop from the crowd. They are excited, as he signals for the Torture Crack. He lifts up Bubba, but he squirms and escapes the grasp of the behemoth. Bubba quickly hits another Lethal Injection and a DDT to knock DJ down. The crowd half pops, surprised to see such tactics used by DJ, but it was against Bubba after all… DJ then brings Bubba up into the Torture Rack, and then drops to the canvas in a sitting position, slamming Bubba hard across his shoulders. Mikey: That was close there, Bailey almost locked in his finisher. BJ: Yeah, but X-Factor is too quick for him. He is the fastest man in XUW. Mikey: He is? BJ: Sure why not? Bailey turns around to where X is and gets nailed with a standing dropkick from X. Bailey stumbles backwards and down. X goes over and waits for Bails to get up. As soon as Bails is up X goes for an Evenflow DDT and nails it to perfection. X then walks over to the turnbuckles and climb to the top of them. He looks out and sees that Bails is still down. He hops off the ropes and goes for a shooting star press but Bails is able to move out of the way at the last second and X hits hard on the mat. Bails gets up and waits for X to slowly get up. Bailey then grabs X and lifts him up and locks in the Canadian Back Breaker on X. Mikey: He has it locked in. This could be it. BJ: But wait a second, looks who’s coming to ring side. Sure enough as the camera pans to the aisle the crowd pops as Danyon and Larry the Legionnaire are running down towards the ring. Larry hops up on the apron and distracts the referee as Danyon runs to the other side of the ring and grabs the Submission Title and slides into the ring. Danyon runs over and nails Bailey with the Submission Title as in the head. Bails drops down to the ground and X grabs his back in pain. Danyon quickly slides out of the ring with the belt. Mikey: What was Danyon thinking? BJ: He’s costing Bailey the match….. X looks over and sees Bailey out. He goes over and locks in the Boston Crab. Larry hops down off the apron and goes over to where Danyon is on the side. The ref turns around and sees Bailey tapping out. He signals for the bell. Ding! Ding! Ding! Winner and New Submission Champion: X-Factor Mikey: That’s it we have a new champion. The reign of Bailey is over. BJ: I can’t believe that Danyon cost him the match but I love it. Mikey: I can’t believe that either but I don’t know if I agree with it like you do. X Factor slides out of the ring and Danyon gladly hands him the Submission Title. X rips it out of their hands and holds it up in the air. He celebrates as he goes back to a bunch of boos from the crowd. Danyon and Larry mock Bailey in the ring for losing the title. Bailey kicks the ropes in frustration and looks over at Danyon and Larry and points at them. Mikey: What a surprising turn of events there I think. We have a new champion. BJ: Yes, but I’m not complaining since it is X-Factor. Mikey: You wouldn’t complain. But that’s it for tonite. I’ll see you on Thursday for Vortex.
