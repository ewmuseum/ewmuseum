The inaugural XUDF call-up match took place in November 2002 at More Double Trouble, where Latch Key Kid shocked everybody by narrowly defeating the unbeaten Doughboy and Darkseid to become XUDF's first XUW call-up. Doughboy then hung on to defend his North American Title. Last year, at Road Rage 3, The Longhorn scaled the walls and just managed to beat Joey Vendetti to the ground to earn his XUW contract, and Joey went on to defeat Overkill for the North American title. Darkseid returned at the Anniversary Show to the surprise of everyone, and emerged from the Hardcore Invitational as the new Hardcore Champion. It's a volatile situation, and there are sure as hell going to be some fireworks there. He will defend it against seven other men, conforming to the XUW vs. XUDF theme once again. Fatbadger, Rujasu, Kata, and "Eliminator" Danny Williams will be participating from XUW, while Hardcore Jon, Matt Patera, Enigma, and Rhinox will represent XUDF. It's an eight-man elimination match for the Intercontinental Title, and only one man will be left standing when the dust settles. Things continue to escalate from there, as three long-standing feuds come to a head tonight. LKK and Henrik have a long history with each other, and practically beat the holy hell out of each other at the Anniversary Show. If their brawl that night is any indication, this is sure to be a bloodbath. And speaking of blood, the next match proves that it does indeed run thicker than water. Dave Kipfer goes one-on-one with Joey Vendetti in the Family Matters match, with their rivalry stemming back to the classic Stable Wars battle between the Boomer Sooners and the Skeletons of Society last June. Ever since, Joey has targeted Dave, who has recently learned about the truth of his past. It all comes down to one match tonight! But after that, however, is the match that XUW fans everywhere have been waiting for. Two men who absolutely hate each other, who are so alike, yet so different, meet tonight for the final time. It's a rivalry that we may one day be uttering in the same breath as Samurai Bob vs. Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy -- Saij vs. Ric Austin. These two have clashed over the Television, Tag, Intercontinental, and World Titles, trading victories and leaving a wave of controversy in their wake. They've talked two years worth of trash to each other, and have played one-upsmanship on so many occasions. It all began at Road Rage 2 with a blindfold match for the Television Title. It ends tonight at Road Rage 4 in a scaffold match, with only pride at stake, and revenge on the minds of Fark-O-Maniacs everywhere. It's been ten long months since that fateful night when Ric Austin threw Saij off the top of the Ultitron and put him in a coma. Tonight, Saij gets the chance to return the favor. It's Saij vs. Ric Austin, the Showdown on the Scaffold! Whether it's business or personal, we can be sure of one thing: tonight, things will be settled once and for all between these two men. Dante defeated Wolfson at the Anniversary Show, capping off an amazing run through the eight-man tournament and scoring a well-deserved -- and overdue, to my estimation -- victory to capture the elusive belt for the first time in his career.
