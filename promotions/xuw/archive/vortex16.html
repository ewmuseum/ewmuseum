XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #16

11 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex A black screen lies dormant, until staccato gunfire hits it, ripping it to shreds, and leaving the word “Vortex” emblazoned upon it. The screen drops and we’re in the arena! Green pyrotechnics fly up into the rafters, explosions shake the floor, and “Lose Control” by Ash plays. The camera pans round the arena, taking in the rabid fans, eager for the night’s action. The signs are out in full force, “I want Dave’s … talent!”, “Bluto owes me a beer!” and, strangely, “Bolling for President”. Atomic Tomato: “Hello fans, good evening and welcome to yet another action packed edition of XUW Vortex! Boy do we have a show for you tonight? Yes we do. Lazarus will try to take the TV Title from the hands of Dave Kipfer, Lance Storm will be looking for glory in the form of the Intercontinental Title, and Bionic Carrot will try to end No Fear Man’s reign as XUW Hardcore Champion.” Barry Johnson: “Yes, but what about the other big matches? Rob The Throb takes on “The Comedian” Chris Blake, with a stipulation that the loser must leave the Army of Darkness!” AT: “Speaking of the AoD, their leader, Jack Nobbledy, will take on former XUW World Champion Samurai Bob, in what promises to be a classic!” BJ: “I’ve got fifty bucks on The Ripper. Don’t let me down big guy!” “Crawling in the Dark” by Hoobastank hits the speakers, and Lazarus makes his way down to the ring for the first of tonight’s matches. Breaking Point’s “One of a Kind” plays, and Dave Kipfer comes down to the ring, TV Title round his waist, to a big crowd pop. Dave enters the ring, where Lazarus awaits, and the bell is rung. XUW TV Title Match: Dave Kipfer (C) v Lazarus Dave starts off with a Snap Suplex, and then hits a second one. He goes for a third, but Lazarus blocks, and hits a DDT. Dave is up quickly, and reverses an Irish Whip, sending Laz to the ropes. Laz comes back off with a Flying Elbow, but DK ducks and Laz hits nothing but air. Dave is all over Laz immediately and hits a Tornado DDT, and then delivers The Eulogy. AT: “This one could be over already!” Dave pins, but Lazarus manages to get a shoulder off the canvas just before the three count. Laz gets to his feet unsteadily, and DK goes to the ropes, jumping up and going for the Springboard Hurricanrana, but Laz sees it and reverses into a Powerbomb. Laz holds him down and goes for the pin, but only gets a short two count. DK gets up and Laz lays into him with some boxing style jabs, and then takes him down with a Clothesline. Laz gets down and tries to lock on a Boston Crab, but Dave makes it to the ropes before he can get it fully locked in. BJ: “Lazarus came here tonight to fight, and he’s showing DK that being the TV Champion is no easy ride.” Both men get to their feet, and then lock up in the middle of the ring. DK whips Laz to the ropes, but the challenger ducks under the attempted Clothesline and hits the ropes again, coming off with a Super Spear that smashes into DK’s ribs. Laz goes for the pin. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! DK gets to his feet, holding his ribs, and just dodges an attempted flying clothesline from Lazarus. Laz gets up with his back to DK, and the champion locks in the Separation Anxiety! Laz starts to tire, but manages to use the last of his strength to jump backwards into the turnbuckle, causing Kipfer to break the hold. Laz slumps to the floor, but DK picks him up and hits the Viva Las Vegas. He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … FOOT ON THE ROPES! AT: “It was all Lazarus could do to get that one foot on the ropes!” Dave steps back as the ref checks on Lazarus, when, suddenly, Blake Savage crawls our from under the ring and rolls in under the ropes. He sneaks up on DK, and taps him on the shoulder. DK turns round straight into a hard right hand from Savage. DK goes down to the canvas hard, and the camera focuses in on Savage’s hand, as he gets back under the ring. AT: “Damn that Blake Savage. Why, that looks like a foreign object in his hand!” BJ: “It was just the power of the punch!” AT: “Dammit, he’s used the brass knucks!” Back in the ring, the ref looks around to see Dave laid out, but with nobody in sight, he lets the match go on. Lazarus crawls over to where Kipfer lies, and drapes an arm across him. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winner by pinfall, and NEW XUW TV CHAMPION: Lazarus!!!!! AT: “Dammit! No! Not like that!” BJ: “Lazarus really showed up DK there, not such a big shot now, are you Mr … Talent!” AT: “What the hell? Are you blind? Kipfer had it won and Savage clocked him with the knucks!” BJ: “All I can see is a new champion.” Lazarus raises the belt in the air to a chorus of boos, and makes his way to the back. AT: “That was disgusting. Let’s hope for a more sporting contest in this next match.” BJ: “The next match is Bionic Carrot versus No Fear Man, so I wouldn’t bet on it.” AT: “Why, because it’s a Hardcore Title Match?” BJ: “No, it’s those Horsemen, they’re notorious cheaters!” AT: “Will you stop?” BJ: “No! The Horsemen split, it’s the funniest thing I’ve seen since Chase ran into a door while he was stoned.” “Bad Moon Rising” plays, and the crowd seem uncertain at first, but once they see Bionic Carrot coming down to the ring they go wild with applause. He is carrying a kendo stick and a trash can full of assorted hardcore weaponry. “Mr Self-Destruct” plays, this time the crowd are not uncertain, and pour forth scorn and heat upon NFM as he strolls to the ring, hardcore items adorned with the F.E.A.R. logo upon his person. XUW Hardcore Title Match: No Fear Man (C) v Bionic Carrot BC gets straight to work, throwing a trash can lid Frisbee style at NFM’s head, but the champion ducks. BC, inside the ring, starts to throw assorted ‘solid steel’ trash cans, trash can lids and other implements at NFM. NFM dodges them all, and rolls into the ring, nunchuks in hand. He swings them quickly as he advances on BC, who finds he is out of weapons. NFM darts in to strike, but BC backs off into the ropes and comes off strong, knocking down NFM with a Shoulder Charge. BC picks up NFM and hits a Reverse Shoulder Breaker. NFM stumbles back up, but BC lays him out with a huge Stalling Brain Buster, which smacks NFM’s head down to the mat hard. BC hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! BC picks up NFM again, but the champion’s hand finds the nunchuks and slams them into the back of BC’s head. BC staggers, and NFM hits a Brainbuster. BC is down, and NFM rolls outside, coming back with a ‘STOP’ sign and a fire extinguisher. BC gets up to a blast from the extinguisher, and he walks round into a colossal SMACK from the ‘STOP’ sign. BC drops to his knees, and NFM delivers a kick to the head to put the challenger on the deck. NFM goes for the pin. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! AT: “Wow, this ultimatum from Admiral John has really spurred Bionic Carrot into some action!” BJ: “Don’t get me started!” NFM takes a swing at BC as he gets up, but BC ducks and whips NFM to the ropes. BC then drops to the floor and hits a Drop Toe Hold, sending NFM down to the floor, and smacking his head against the fire extinguisher, to a thunderous clanging noise. NFM, barely conscious, manages to roll out of the ring before BC can get across to pin. BC picks up the bent ‘STOP’ sign and follows him to the outside. BC picks NFM up off the floor, and the champion staggers away from him, holding on to the railing as he walks up the entranceway. BC catches up with him and smacks the ‘STOP’ sign over his head. NFM staggers up the entranceway even further, resting against a large speaker. BC swings the ‘STOP’ sign, but NFM ducks and hits a few elbows to the midsection. BC stops, and NFM climbs the speaker, jumping off from six feet up and nailing a huge Clothesline, sending BC down to the ground. NFM covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “Holy hell on a hand grenade! How did BC kick out of that?” NFM picks up BC and looks to hit The Lightning Strike, but BC manages to power out, and whips NFM through the curtain, to the backstage area. NFM hits a table, and rolls on top of it. BC gets on top of an opposite table and looks to hit a leg drop from one table to the other. He gets air, but NFM rolls out of the way and BC smashes through the table by himself. NFM picks himself up, and lifts BC up, hitting a Flapjack onto the other table, but it doesn’t break! NFM gets up sluggishly, and covers BC on the table. The ref is right there. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! NFM looks bewildered, but gets up and hits BC with some hard right hands. He tries to lift BC and hit The Lightning Strike on top of the table, but BC hits a knee to the midsection, and NFM doubles up. Barely aware of his surroundings, BC lifts NFM up onto his shoulders and jumps up, moving NFM in mid air and hitting the Nail in the Coffin onto the table, which finally breaks. Both men seem to be totally our of it, but BC slowly drapes one arm across NFM’s chest. The ref counts. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winner by pinfall, and NEW XUW Hardcore Champion: Bionic Carrot! AT: “My God! That was a new Bionic Carrot we saw tonight, he was motivated like never before!” BJ: “Bah, he was lucky…” AT: “Wait! Who’s that?” A huge shadow goes across the two dazed competitors, and as BC stirs and sits up, Bonecrusher smashes him round the head with a baseball bat, which smashes and splinters. BC drops, unconscious and bleeding, to the floor. Bonecrusher picks up NFM and carries him off round the corner, just as Kata, KsC and Sheri come round the corner, to find BC on the floor, bleeding from a head wound, but clutching the XUW Hardcore Title close to his chest. AT: “Well, Bionic Carrot won the title, but at what cost to himself?” The camera switches back to the ring, and “Your Disease” plays, as former XUW Hardcore Champion, Rob The Throb goes down to the ring. “Crazy Train” then hits, and “The Comedian” Chris Blake comes down the ramp, to about the same level of heat that Rob got. No DQ Match, Loser leaves Army of Darkness: Rob The Throb v “The Comedian” Chris Blake Rob and CCB square up in the ring, and then they’re going at it, hard rights and lefts being landed by both men. CCB whips Rob to the ropes, and hits a Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker on him. Rob is down, but gets up quickly as CCB misses a Knee Drop. Rob is on him quickly with a Snap Suplex, but CCB blocks an attempt at a second one, and hits a Gut Buster. Rob stumbles to his feet, but The Comedian hits The Punch Line and goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … SHOULDER UP! AT: “These boys both want to stay in the Army of Darkness, you can tell from their intensity!” BJ: “No, really? Well, what a mystery you have solved there Sherlock! Of course they want to stay in AoD, it’s the best damn stable in the fed!” Rob gets to his feet and rakes CCB’s eyes. Rob slides from the ring and comes back with a trash can lid from the previous match. CCB looks to kick it from his hands, but misses, and gets the lid smacked over his head. The Comedian goes down, but as Rob advances for another shot, Chris whips his legs round and trips him up. RTT drops to the floor, and then CCB is atop him raining down punch after punch, and Rob is cut above the eye. The Comedian gets off him, and motions for him to rise. RTT gets up slowly, and The Comedian hits a big Spinebuster. AT: “Hey, here come DDT and Sidhe Nimh!” BJ: “They’re obviously interested in who wins this match.” CCB whips Rob to the corner and sets him up for The Last Laugh, but all of a sudden DDT and Sidhe enter the ring, and pull him off Rob. The Comedian looks round, confused, but then DDT kicks him in the gut and gives him the Shock Treatment! AT: “What the hell?” Sidhe Nimh picks up The Comedian and delivers the China White, leaving him sprawled on the mat. DDT goes and rouses Rob The Throb, and he crawls across the ring and pins CCB. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winner by pinfall, and still in AoD: Rob The Throb!!!!! BJ: “It’s obvious, Chris Blake was a joke, and the AoD saw that!” The lights dim as Rob celebrates in the ring, and Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy walks slowly down the ramp. He enters the ring and greets Rob with a handshake. In the other corner of the ring, DDT hits the Creeping Death on The Comedian. Jack raises Rob’s hand, and Rob is grinning from ear to ear. AT: “Well, I guess Jack thinks Rob can pull through this tough spot.” Jack drops Rob’s arm, and beckons him closer, as if to whisper something in his ear. Rob leans forwards, and jack says something inaudible to everyone but Rob. Rob takes a step back, the smile gone, and now The Ripper grins, kicking RTT in the gut and delivering a huge RipSaw! AT: “I know I said it before, but, what the hell?” Sidhe Nimh turns round, a grin on his face, and he lifts up RTT as Jack produces a lead pipe from his long coat. RTT is in pain already, and then gets the lead pipe cracked into his ribs once, twice, three times. He is dropped to the floor, and Jack smacks Sidhe on the back. DDT turns from tormenting The Comedian, and smiles at Jack. Sidhe goes over to Chris Blake, lifts him up, and throws him at Jack. Jack picks him up easily and delivers another RipSaw, on to Rob The Throb. AT: “Oh my God, what the hell is going on?” BJ: “Hush, The Ripper is going to speak…” Jack gets a microphone and motions the crowd to silence, which works well, since they were already fairly stunned. Jack: “You may ask “Why?” but The Ripper would rather ask why not? These two men here failed the Army of Darkness, and as such are both expelled from the darkness. They had their tasks, and yet they lost to lesser men. Many chances were presented to them, but they proceeded to fail in almost every aspect. Their potential was incredible, but all for nought. Let this be a lesson to all who are still as yet unmoved by the talk of darkness within XUW. The Army of Darkness is stronger than ever, with just three members. The Ripper, Sidhe Nimh and DDT, more than just a force to be reckoned with… a force to be feared. No more can anyone beat one of these two losers and claim to have vanquished the Army of Darkness. So, everyone in XUW, this is a warning. Mock the Army at your peril, for there shall be no mercy from this point onwards…” Jack drops the microphone and walks slowly to the back, DDT and Sidhe Nimh walking close behind him. AT: “What the hell?” BJ: “Is that your new catchphrase?” AT: “Shut up, that was truly shocking stuff there. The Army of Darkness is now down to three members, but they seem to be happy about it, as far as they ever seem happy about anything.” BJ: “Well, the EMTs have finally cleared the blood away, so, next match!” “Ytse Jam” plays, and Blitzwing leads out the newest member of F.E.A.R. Dev, to a barrage of heat. “Rule Britannia” plays, and Raven X and Bailey come down to the ring, also to a barrage of heat. AT: “Not a match of the fan favourites here, oh no.” Tag Team Match: Royal Legion v Blitz/Dev Bailey and Blitz start off the match. Blitz hits a German Suplex on Bailey, and then comes off the ropes, hitting a big Powerline. Bailey is down, and Blitz tags in Dev. Dev starts off strong with a DDT, but as he goes for a Spinning Heel Kick, Bailey catches him and hits a Backbreaker. Bailey tags in Raven, and together they hit a Double DDT. Dev is down, and Raven hits a Leg Drop, then going for the pin. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Raven gets Dev up, and hits a Full Nelson Slam. RX picks up Dev once more and whips him to the ropes. Blitz gets the blind tag and advances on RX, just as he nails Dev with a huge Spinebuster. Blitz goes to work on RX, hitting some hard right hands and then a Side Suplex. Raven rolls away and tags Bailey who runs in and Clotheslines Blitz, but the FEAR member had managed a clothesline of his own too, and both men go down. Bailey is back to his feet first, and tries to pull Blitz back from his corner, but Blitz hits an Enzeguri, and tags in Dev. Dev charges Bailey, but gets hit with a Belly-to-Belly suplex, and Bailey tags RX back in. Dev gets up, but RX quickly hits the Morning Glory. Dev looks out of it, but RX picks him up again, and hits a Running Powerslam. Dev looks in a bad way as RX kicks him in the gut and nails the Family Values right in the centre of the ring. RX hooks the leg as Blitz and Bailey both rush to the pin. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winners by pinfall: The Royal Legion!!! Bailey beat Blitz there by a heartbeat, and manages to tackle him to the ground before he can split up the pin. AT: “Well, I can guess that’s why they call him #4, right?” BJ: “Do not mock F.E.A.R. or they’ll set Bonecrusher on you, and you don’t want that!” The Royal Legion high five in the centre of the ring, but who is that at the top of the ramp, on the stage? Why, it’s The Deadly Combination! They stare holes in the Royal Legion, but their stares are only returned with equal venom. AT: “Well, that’s quite a staredown there folks, I feel these two teams may well meet up again before long!” BJ: “Indeed they will, and speaking of that, here’s the next match!” AT: “Huh?” Everyone has cleared the ring, and the musical tones of “Pachelbel-Cannon” (in D major) rings through the audience, and Lance Storm makes his way down to the ring. AT: “Such sophistication! I bet you don’t know this music!” BJ: “Of course I do, it’s ‘Taco Bell’s Cannons” with Lee Majors!” AT: “You’ve been wanting to do that for a while now haven’t you?” BJ: “Yup!” “Chop Suey” by System of a Down plays, and Sidhe Nimh walks out onto the stage, soaking up the boos that reign down on to him. He walks slowly to the ring, and enters it, immediately going for Lance. XUW Intercontinental Title Match: Sidhe Nimh (C) v Lance Storm Sidhe gets the start on Lance, working him down to the ground with some clubbing blows from his forearms. Sidhe hits the X-Hangover, and then follows it up quickly with a Gut Wrench Suplex. He goes for a pin, but only gets a two count. AT: “Sidhe Nimh is, I think, the shortest wrestler in XUW, but he’s possibly the most dangerous.” Sidhe looks to hit another Suplex, but Lance blocks and delivers one of his own. Sidhe gets up, but Lance hits a Superkick and goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … SHOULDER UP! Sidhe gets right back up, but is still slightly reeling, and Lance gets behind him, and locks in a Full Nelson. Lance holds it for a few seconds, and then hits a Dragon Suplex, landing Sidhe right on the back of his neck. Lance moves like lightning and locks in an STF submission hold on the Intercontinental Champion. AT: “This is great work from Lance. Did Sidhe underestimate the prowess of the former XUW World Champion?” BJ: “Of course not, this is part of the plan!” AT: “Being in a very painful submission hold is his plan? How so?” BJ: “Uhhhh, shut up!” Lance tears back on the STF, but Sidhe manages to wave to the side with an arm and grab a rope. The ref breaks the hold, and Sidhe gets to his feet unsteadily. Lance looks for another Superkick, but Sidhe ducks, and catches his leg on the turn. Sidhe hits a Dragon Screw Leg Whip, then another, and then a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Lance is down, and Sidhe goes up top, looking for the Candy Flip. Lance gets his knees up at the last moment, and Sidhe goes down clutching his ribs. Lance gets up slowly, but is waiting for Sidhe, and hits a Rolling Fisherman’s Suplex. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Sidhe gets up at the same time as Lance, and looks angry. He hits some stuff right hands, and then whips Lance to the ropes. Sidhe goes for a vicious backhand shot, but Lance ducks it and cannons off the far side, coming back and hitting Sidhe with a Leaping Side Kick. Lance drags Sidhe to his feet and looks for the Straight Shot (Franchiser), but Sidhe hits some elbows to the side of the head and turns Lance round, locking in a Crossface Chicken Wing. Lance tries to struggle out of it, but just as he looks like he might succeed, Sidhe flips him backwards for an Overhead Chicken Wing Suplex! AT: “Hell, with these guys you get one hell of a show. I didn’t even realise such a move was possible!” Sidhe is straight over to Lance, and picks him u, delivering the Black Tar into a pin. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “This is great intestinal fortitude being shown by Lance!” BJ: “Who are you now, Gorilla Monsoon?” AT: “Careful, or I’ll clock you one on your lower occipital protuberance!” Sidhe goes up top, and hits the Reverse Shooting Star Press he calls The Shooting Gallery. He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … FOOT ON THE ROPES! Sidhe looks impressed, but still business-like. He looks to him the “Methadone”, but Lance twists the leg and rolls Sidhe into a Half Boston Crab. Sidhe grits his teeth and stretches for the ropes, but Lance pulls him back into the middle of the ring. AT: “My God, where did that move come from? Sidhe was attempting an Inverted Fameasser, and suddenly he’s in a Half Crab!” Sidhe tries again to crawl to the ropes, but Lance pulls him back in. Sidhe then lifts himself up as high as he can get with his arms. Lance has to stand a little higher, and then Sidhe quickly tucks and swings his upper body through Lance’s legs, and hits a Throat Thrust. AT: “Now that’s what I call unorthodox! Spectacular, but unorthodox.” Sidhe gets to his feet, favouring his left leg, and takes Lance down with a Clothesline. Lance gets back up, but Sidhe hits a boot to the gut and set him up for the China White… and delivers! He hooks the leg, and the ref counts. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner by pinfall, and STILL XUW Intercontinental Champion: Sidhe Nimh!!! BJ: “Whooo, score one for the new AoD!” AT: “Damn that was a good match! Those two guys put on one heck of a show. Lance Storm is back, and nearly at his best again. I see bright things happening for him again soon.” Lance makes his way to the back, shortly followed by Sidhe, belt in hand AT: “Well, that was an great match, and the next one promises to be interesting. Can KsC have as good a Vortex as his tag partner Bionic Carrot?” BJ: “You mean can he get his head split open with a baseball bat? Well, it’s possible…” Umali’s Bar” by Drunken Tiger plays, and KsC Trunkz comes down the ramp to a good face pop. “Fight for you Right” plays, and Bluto comes down to a pop of his own, beer cooler in hand and at the ready. Singles Match: Bluto v KsC Trunkz The two men go right at it, locking up straight away. KsC sends Bluto to the ropes, but gets knocked down by a Body Check. Bluto runs off the ropes again and sends KsC down with a Clothesline. KsC gets up, but walks right into the Keg Stand. Bluto goes for the early cover, but just gets a two count. Bluto swings a haymaker of a punch, but KsC dodges it and hits Bluto with a Samoan Drop. He goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Bluto gets up fairly easily, but KsC has run off the ropes and thunders into him with The Tiger Pounds. KsC goes for another cover, but once more just gets a two count. Bluto is looking gradually more unsteady on his legs, and KsC hits some hard Knife Edge Chops to send him back to the turnbuckle. KsC takes a run up and charges Bluto, but the big drinker lifts himself up the turnbuckle and avoids the impact. AT: “Now what’s this? Surely not!” Bluto looks at the crowd, gives a ‘thumbs up’ gesture, and tries to lock a Tarantula on KsC, and succeeds! The crowd pops huge, and then laughs as he can’t hold it and drops down to the arena floor. AT: “Now I think I’ve seen it all…” Bluto picks himself up and enters the ring, but KsC picks him up and hits The Intoxication (Death Valley Driver). Bluto is down, and KsC springs up to the top turnbuckle, coming flying off with The Headshot! He holds his neck for a few seconds, and then pins Bluto. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner by pinfall, KsC Trunkz!!! AT: “It’s a good day for the Horsemen!” BJ: “Ex-Horsemen, I think you’ll find.” AT: “Will you stop!” The competitors go to the back, and there is an audible buzz around the arena as the crowd prepares for the Main Event. Then the sound guys fix the buzz, and we’re ready to go. “One Armed Scissor” hits the speakers, and the arena bursts into a cacophony of heat for Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy. He walks slowly down to the ring, accompanied by DDT. “F*** That” comes on, and the Richter Scale is torn a new a**hole by the deafening pop that accompanies Bob to the ring. Bob looks closely at DDT, who stands at ringside, innocently playing with his tazer. The ref spots him, and goes outside to talk to him. AT: “What’s going on here? It looks like DDT is being forced to leave!” BJ: “Dammit! There IS a conspiracy here against the AoD! First they screw Jack out of his title shot, then they make Sidhe fight Lance, now they’re getting rid of Jack’s moral support! It’s an outrage!” AT: “Oh grow up.” BJ: “Grow up? I’ve got money on this fight!” Singles Match, Winner fights HBK for #1 Contender Status: Jack “The Ripper Nobbledy v Samurai Bob The two men circle round in the ring, and then simultaneously go for one another, locking up in the centre of the ring. Jack uses his power to force Bob back to the ropes, and then hits a hard shot to the chest as they break. Bob is rocked, and Jack whips him to the ropes, hitting a Big Boot. Jack picks Bob off the floor with one hand, and grabs him round the throat, as if to Chokeslam him. Bob kicks out and catches Jack in the stomach, and then hits a DDT as he bends over. Jack is up quickly, but Bob comes off the far said ropes and hits a Flying Shoulder Tackle to knock Jack back down. Bob goes for the cover but only gets one. Both men get up, and Bob goes for a grapple, but Jack just hits him in the head with a huge backhanded swipe. Bob staggers back and Jack is upon him, hitting a Sidewalk Slam. Jack covers, but again for just a one count. Both men back off a little, warily, and the crowd applauds their efforts. AT: “They’re sizing each other up here, this is a battle of wits as well as bodies.” BJ: “Whatever. Come on Jack, bring home the money!” Bob and Jack lock up again, this time Bob getting the upper hand with a go-behind, and taking Jack down off his feet. Jack gets up, but Bob hits a Side Suplex and then goes up top with the Samurai Star (elbow drop) off the top rope. Bob hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Jack gets back to his feet, but Bob hits a hard Clothesline, leaving him staggering backwards. Bob tries another clothesline, but Jack just grabs him and hits the Call of Destiny (Whiplash Spinebuster). Jack goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Jack brings Bob back to his feet and throws him into a corner. Jack takes a run up and charges Bob, crushing him into the turnbuckle hard. Bob staggers off, and Jack grabs him around the waist, hitting The Break. Bob lets out a groan, and Jack hits The Break again. Bob slumps down to the floor, and Jack covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Jack looks surprised, but picks up Bob and goes to hit The Break a third time, but Bob wriggles out of his grasp. Jack turns round quickly, but Bob was quicker, and he hits a series of right hands, and then a Snap Suplex. Bob goes up top, and flies off as Jack gets up, hitting his Budo Blade Flying Clothesline. Jack goes down hard, and Bob looks for the cover, but Jack kicks out shortly before the three. Bob gets up, and hits some hard shots to the back of the Ripper as he gets to his feet. Bob goes to lock up with Jack, but Jack just lifts him up and hits the Event Horizon Jackhammer. Bob is down, and Jack covers to a two count. AT: “This match is really swinging back and forth, it’s a great spectacle.” Jack lifts up Bob and hits some hard right hands, backing him up o the turnbuckle. Jack then backs off and charges in with a colossal Spear, which would have worked better had Bob not sidestepped it. Jack charges shoulder first into the ring post and stumbles backwards off it. Bob wraps Jack’s own arm round his throat and hits a Regal Cutter. Bob covers. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Both men get back to their feet, and Bob whips Jack into the corner. Struggling a little with the bulk, Bob lifts Jack to be sitting on the top rope. Bob hits a few right hands, and then sets Jack up for the Saigon Snapp. Just as he goes to execute it, Jack pushes hard, and Bob falls down to the canvas. He’s up quickly, but Jack flies off the top rope and hits a big Double Axe Handle. Bob goes down, and Jack is upon him in a flash, rolling him over and locking in The Fear. BJ: “It’s over! It’s over!” Bob grits his teeth as Jack pulls backwards, straining the hell out of Bob’s neck and back. Jack continues to pull back on Bob, as he grits his teeth and refuses to give. Bob’s resolve is strong, but he starts to weaken, and soon seems to be out of it. The ref lifts up his arm, and it drops. The arm goes up a second time, and drops. A third time, and it dro… NO! It stays up. Bob somehow finds the strength and fights to his feet, carrying the 6 foot 8 inches of Ripper on his back. Bob charges backwards into the turnbuckle, slamming Jack into it hard. Bob lifts Jack up to be sitting on the top turnbuckle, and then goes up too, looking for the Saigon Snapp. Bob pulls, but Jack is leaning back, and he won’t budge. Bob lets go and looks to hit some right hands to weaken Jack, but Jack hits a hard right of his own and Bob wavers precariously on the top turnbuckle. Jack, with surprising balance for a big man, snakes out an arm and grabs Bob by the throat, forcing him downwards. AT: “No, dear God, no!” Jack sneers at the crowd from atop the turnbuckle, and delivers an almighty RipSaw from the top rope! Somehow he manages to stay standing on the turnbuckle, Bob a crumpled mess below him. Slowly, Jack comes down off the turnbuckle, rolls Bob onto his back, and hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! If they want to take over XUW they’ll have to go through hell, high water, and some damned heroes!” BJ: “I don’t care, I won the bet, and I’m rich! Rich!” AT: “Well, we hope you enjoyed Vortex as much as I did. Two new champions, two men out of AoD, and two tired commentators bidding thee adieu!
