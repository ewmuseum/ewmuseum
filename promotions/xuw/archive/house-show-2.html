XUW House Shows I don't know where XUW is going. But I know where ever it goes, I'll follow. So without further delay heres the House Show. Singles competition - Spoonman vs Frank Vegas “Spoonman” by Soundgarden sweeps across the arena. The crowd cheers as it’s the first match and they know there about to see a familiar wrestler straight up. Spoonman bounces out, happy that the crowd is cheering him. He goes down to the ring and roles in. “Smooth Criminal” plays and a mixed reaction greets Frank Vegas as he walks out, stretching his arms as he goes. Vegas gets in the ring and the bell sounds to get the match going. The two men square off and circle each other; Frank Vegas makes the first move, lunging at Spoonman. But Spoonman steps aside and pushes Vegas past him and through the ropes to the out side. Spoonman follows him out and as Vegas gets to his feet Spoonman comes through with a knee to the head, sending Vegas back to the deck. Spoonman then picks Vegas up and goes to roll him back in but Vegas blocks it and turns around, delivering a stiff clothesline to Spoonman. Vegas then picks him up looks behind them then hits a Snap Suplex into the barricade! Vegas gets up and talks a little trash to Spoonman before turning his attention to a man in the front row wearing a Spoonman T – Shirt. Vegas try’s sneers at the man and then raise’s his fist at him, but the man doesn’t flinch and goes to jump the barricade but is pulled back by security. Vegas laughs to himself and then focuses back on Spoonman, who has now climbed back to his feet. Vegas grabs him, rolls him back into the ring and then follows him in. As they get in Vegas gets up and quickly goes on the offence, delivering some knees to the head. Spoonman stumbles back with each shot until he catches the knee and flips Vegas up and spinning it into a half Boston crab. Vegas screams out in pain but is close to the ropes so he reaches over and the ref breaks the hold. Spoonman then drags him back into the centre of the ring and goes for the figure four but Vegas kicks out of it and try to roll to the outside. But Spoonman catches him and delivers a hard Dragon Back Breaker. Vegas rolls around on the flour in pain. Spoonman picks him up and slings him into the corner. He then runs in, lays a couple of punches then manoeuvres around Vegas till he has him set up in the DVD position on the top rope, ready for the “Holy ***t That Hurt!” Spoonman jumps to perform his move turning the DVD into a pile driver but mid move Vegas clasps his legs around Spoonmans head and flips Spoonman over him, levelling Spoonman out once his head hits the ground. Vegas gets to his feet and looks around stunned. Spoonman is out cold, so Vegas rolls him up and 1 … 2 … 3!! Vegas rolls out as a chorus of boo’s rain down apon him. He quickly moves away from the ring, he looks shocked at the win and keeps looking back every so often to see if Spoonman is still down. Winner – Frank Vegas Singles Competition - Dave Kipfer vs Kevil “Lynch pin” by fear factory echoes around the arena as the lights go out, huge red flames shoot out from the stage. As the flames subside Kevil is standing there. He Poses there for a few seconds before he sprints to the ring, slides under the Bottom rope, climbs up to the top for a macho man like (both feet on the Turnbuckle, arms straight out) pose. He then back flips off, crowd mark out for this display and Kevil looks happy.  "Arrow" by Candlebox hits and Dave bounces out, the crowd begin to cheer, because they think they’ll get more of what Kevil sowed them, which stops Dave in his tracks, he looks confused. He looks around then shrugs his shoulders, smiles and then runs down to the ring. He jumps up on the apron and flips into the ring over the top rope, the crowd again mark out for the aerobatics. The two men meet in the middle, slap hands and the bell rings. They lock it up, but quickly turn it into a hip toss event, neither man giving in. This continues until during the hip toss Dave catches Kevil’s leg in mid air, causing him to flip twice. Yet the agile Kevil lands on his feet and the crowd again mark out. The two men slap hands again and lock it up, this time Kevil gets the upper hand and pushes Dave into the ropes. He comes back and the two meet in the middle, Kevil trying to side bump Dave. But Dave being the bigger of the two sends Kevil down. Dave then launches himself into the ropes bouncing back and over Kevil. On the way back Kevil reverse Leapfrogs him then runs up the corner and springs off delivering a spinning heel kick from the top rope to the back of Dave’s head, sending Dave to the mat. Kevil then jumps him and hits the Evil Sault (Lion sault) from the middle rope. The crowd are on their feet as Kevil picks Dave up and takes him to the corner to set him up for a “Welcome to the Dark side” (Scorpion Death drop from the top rope, set-up just like a tornado DDT) But as Kevil turns to pull Dave up, Dave unleashes a flurry of fists, then a kick to the gut followed by “The Bootie Call” (Diamond Cutter) from the corner. Both men are down, but Dave gets to his feet. He then stumbles over to Kevil, were he slumps over him and the ref counts 1 … 2 … 3!! Dave rolls off and out of the ring. The crowd cheer and applaud both men for their excellent performance. Winner – Dave Kipfer Tag Team match - The Deadly Combination vs Halloween/Matt Logan "Deadly Combination" by Tupac & Big L hits the speakers and the Deadly Combination come out to a pop worthy of an up and coming tag team. As they walk down the ramp, pointing and posing for the crowd Matt Logan and Halloween rush them from behind and a brawl insures on the ramp. The four men exchange blows with Numark getting the upper hand on Matt Logan with a knee to the gut. He then marches him down to the ring, rolls him in, gets on the ring apron, yells to the crowd, which produces a wild cheer, then performs an inside out leg drop onto Logan and the bell rings to start the match. Mean while M V Pete and Halloween continue to trade blows until Halloween shins Pete, causing him Pete to crumble to one knee. Halloween grabs him by the scruff of the neck and tosses him into the barrier. Halloween then runs up to his corner and try’s to get in the ring, distracting the Hungry Mexican and allowing Logan to get the upper hand with a low blow to Numark, who then drops like a lead balloon. Logan then gets the tag to Halloween, who runs in and delivers a few short kicks to Numark, before taunting the crowd. M V Pete has gotten to the ring apron by now and is calling for the Tag. Halloween picks Numark up and begins to take him to his corner. But Halloween is being too cocky and this allows Numark to launch a stiff elbow to Halloweens jaw, knocking him back slightly. Numark then turns and Tags in M V Pete, who runs through Halloween with a devastating clothesline, then pulls Logan into the ring. Pete then turns back to a groggy Halloween who’s now gotten to his feat, only to receive the Big Boot from M V Pete. The Hungry Mexican is confused and stands back. Logan gets up but is met with a super kick that knocks him back to the ropes from Numark, who seems to have gotten a second wind. Numark then gives Logan a kick to the guts and then pulls him back and performs a jumping DDT on him. Logan, in pain, rolls to the outside. By this time M V Pete has hammered Halloween into the corner and set him up for what looks to be a powerbomb from the turnbuckle. Numark seeing this climbs up the opposite turn buckle. M V Pete then delivers an amazing powerbomb from the top ropes. The impact causing Halloween to fold up and over until he’s lying on his back in the middle of the ring. Numark then performs a huge 5 star frog splash and covers for the pin to complete The Deadly Combinations, Total Devastation! Even though he’s not the legal man the HM still does the three count and The Deadly Combination come away with a strong win. Winners – The Deadly Combination Singles Competition - NFM vs Raj Patel  [TV title tournament] Raj Patel comes out to a chorus of boo’s. He looks very focussed and doesn’t take his eyes off the ring as he roles in and paces around it. The lights then fade out and royal purple lightning strikes as Creed's “My Sacrifice” starts playing. A small collection of boo’s can be heard around the arena as No Fear Man walks down to the ring with a sinister smile on his face. Before he gets there though Raj roles out and the two trade blows until Raj rakes the eyes, lay’s another punch and then runs NFM’s into the corner post. He then picks up NFM and rolls him into the ring. The bell sounds to start the match off. Raj picks NFM up to his feat and then lands a couple of stiff forearms to NFM’s back. Raj then swings NFM into the ropes and as he comes back he catches NFM and goes for the Shut Down (Sweeping Rock Bottom) and the early win. But NFM counter’s it into a DDT and rolls off, quickly getting to his feet, he rebounds of the ropes and delivers a sharp knee to the back of Raj’s head. NFM stands above the fallen Raj and smiles. He then picks Raj up and set’s lifts him up for a Brainbuster, holding him there for a while. Raj suddenly wiggles out of it, turns in mid air and slides NFM into a dragon sleeper. NFM is asked consistently if he wants out, but he says no. Then, in a desperation move NFM grabs hold of Raj’s waist and somehow flips himself backwards, connecting with a knee to Raj’s face, forcing him to fall backwards and let go of the hold. NFM gets up and drags Raj into the centre of the ring. NFM then grabs a struggling and Raj and after a small show of strength between the two NFM spins Raj over and into the Fear of Death (Walls of Jericho with shin pressed down across opponents neck) Raj fights for a while but there is no miracle move for Raj to perform, he is trapped, as he concedes to the pain and tapes out! NFM continues the hold for a while, and then lets go after being hit in the head with a projectile of some sort. He spits into the crowd, rolls out and leaves. Winner – No Fear Man Main Event – Hodgo vs Coco Hernandez [TV title tournament] “California” love by Tupac plays and the majority of the crowd stand there, turning to one another and asking whom this is and the other half is either booing or cheering. Coco and Shorty Hernandez come out, flashing their gangs sign and grinning proudly. Coco gets in the ring whilst Shorty goes to his corner. "Anarchy In The UK" by The Sex Pistols hits and the crowd stand in unison to hurl abuse, amongst other things at "The Anarchist", Hodgo. He poses for a second, only to be sconed on the head by a flying Hodgo “Vibrating” doll. He picks it up and starts shouting at the crowd, returning the abuse. He then throws the doll down and rolls into the ring. Smiling at Coco, he runs to a corner and poses, only to be booed by the crowd even more. He gets down, the bell rings and the two men lock it up. Hodgo gets the early advantage, taking Cocos arm and twisting it in unusual ways. Coco counters with some fancy flips, ending up on his feet, he catapults Hodgo into the ropes, then runs back and springs himself of the opposite ropes. They come back at each other and Coco leapfrogs Hodgo, they return but as Coco goes to leap frog Hodgo again, Hodgo launches himself at Coco with a flying kick to Cocos chest. (Much like Steve Blackman) This lays Coco out. Hodgo steps to the ropes and taps the side of head whilst smiling to the crowd. The crowd merely abuse him more, so he threatens a small child in the front row, yet he never gets out of the ring. He then proceeds to go to work on Coco, picking him back up then delivering The Anar-Kicks to Cocos head. Shorty gets agitated and slams the mat with his hands whilst yelling at the ref. Hodgo picks Coco up into a bear hug and runs him into the corner. Coco slumps to the second buckle. Hodgo steps back, holding onto the ropes he begins to deliver some sidekicks to Coco. Coco slumps further to the ground and seams out of it. Hodgo, again works the crowd, yelling to them how he’s “God’s gift to XUW” and how he’s the “…greatest spectacle in wrestling", he’s met with more boo’s and abuse. He turns back to Coco and goes to deliver another side kick but Coco catches it, gets to his feet and performs a leg sweep, spinning Hodgo over and into the corner. Hodgo lands heavily on his shoulders and falls over on to his back. Coco stumbles to his feet and seeing Hodgo down he throws himself at him with a elbow to the head, splitting him open. The ref goes down 1 … 2 … kick out! Coco rolls off, still a little groggy from the beating he’s taken, he picks Hodgo up and performs a messy belly to belly, causing both men to be down for the count. Coco rolls to the outside and is met by Shorty who gives him a small pep talk then rolls him back in. Coco and Hodgo both get to their feet and start to trade blows. Coco getting the upper hand, kicking Hodgo in the gut then bouncing back of the ropes and performing a sunset flip into a pin 1 ... 2 … Kick out! The two men are worn down. Coco gets to his feat and drags Hodgo over to the corner, perhaps setting him up for a Aye mios dio (450) Coco goes to climb the ropes but Hodgo kicks out his knee causing him to fall backwards and over Hodgo who follows him through and whilst pushing him down for the pin, pulls up the back of his pants. The HM goes down 1 … 2 … 3!! Shorty roles in and runs up to Hodgo as he roles out of the ring. Shorty shouts abuse at him along with the crowd. Hodgo is obviously warn down and surprised that Coco almost had him, yet he manages a sneering smile towards the ring as he walks back up the ramp the victor. Winner – Hodgo
