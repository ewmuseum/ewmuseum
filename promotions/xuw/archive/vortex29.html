XUW Vortex #29

25 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex "And now, ladies and gentlemen, it's time for XUW VORTEX!!!!!" The opening video package springs into life, showing numerous high flying, high impact amazing moves being performed by the superstars of XUW. The indecipherable lyrics are sung over the generic hard rock anthem performed by the latest badly dressed wannabes. The opening comes to a close, and we open not on the crowd, but on the parking area, where XUW Reporter William Holmes is waiting, microphone in hand. He looks around at the empty area, and shrugs, and then the camera cuts to the announce desk, and to the best double act since Pamela Anderson had surgery, Atomic Tomato and "The Majestic" Barry Johnson. AT: "Hello there and welcome to XUW Vortex. All our talent is here tonight except for Blake Wolfson, who has a title shot right here, looking to win the World Title in a match with Samurai Bob and Chase Wilson." BJ: "Rest assured, one of our many reporters is waiting outside, and as soon as he gets here you'll be the first to know about it. Mind you, with his visa problems, we’ll probably see the Canadian border patrol before we see the Lone Wolf!" AT: "Well, assuming he gets here we’ll be seeing a huge three-way match for the World Title, but before that, it's time for the TV Title defence by Destroyer, and his opponent will be "The Professor" Ric Austin!" BJ: "Oooooh, I feel a headlock coming on!" AT: “Oh brother, please no more…” “School’s Out” is played, and “The Professor” Ric Austin comes down to the ring, to a decent amount of heel heat. The crowd seem to have certainly decided that this new addition to the XUW roster is not a favourite of theirs. “Pass the Courvoisier pt 2” hits, and Destroyer comes down the ramp, in his new ‘Hot Boy D’ attire, to a bit of a pop from the crowd. He enters the ring, and the bell rings for the start of the match. XUW TV Title Match: “The Professor” Ric Austin v Destroyer (C) The two men lock up, and Austin executes a Front Face Lock, and then hits a Suplex. AT: “Wow, a non-headlock move to start with!” Austin picks up Destroyer, and lock in a Headlock. BJ: “There it is! The Best Damn Headlock in the Business! Ring the bell, this one’s as good as over!” Destroyer throws Austin off, and then charges him, looking to hit a Shoulder Charge, but Austin drops to the mat and hits a Drop Toe Hold. Austin locks in an Anklelock, but Destroyer makes it to the ropes quickly, and he has to release the hold. Austin and Destroyer lock up again, and this time Destroyer forces Austin back to the ropes, and then whips him hard to the far turnbuckle. Austin staggers off it and Destroyer hits a big Spinebuster. He goes for the cover, but The Prof kicks out at a short two. Destroyer grabs Austin and looks to hit a Powerbomb, but Austin counters and grabs Destroyer with another Headlock. BJ: “There it is! Lightning has struck twice! Surely Destroyer is finished now!” Austin releases the Headlock and kicks Destroyer in the gut, following up with a Piledriver. Destroyer gets up again though, but only to be set up into the Pop Quiz (Superkick). Destroyer hits the mat and Austin covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT!!! Austin shakes his head at the prone Destroyer, as if admonishing him for kicking out of his move. He picks up Destroyer, but the champion hits back with some sharp elbows to the chest of the challenger. Austin double up, and Destroyer hits the Powerbomb this time. He sees his chance and goes up top, coming off with a 540 Splash. AT: “Oooooooh, Austin gets his knees up just in time!” BJ: “That could break your ribs right there!” Austin picks up Destroyer and hits a German Suplex. He quickly follows this up with a Standing Armbar and then switches seamlessly into the Mid-Term (Rock Bottom)! Austin goads Destroyer back to his feet, taunting him, as the champion struggles up to a vertical base. Austin takes hold of an arm, and starts to whip Destroyer to the ropes, but then changes his grip and slides behind Destroyer, hooking in a Full Nelson. He viciously slams Destroyer to the mat, face first. AT: “He hit the Final Exam! This could be over right here…” Austin hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner by pinfall, and NEW Television Champion: “The Professor” Ric Austin AT: “Yes, it is over, and Ric Austin put on a flawless display. I don’t care for the man personally, but he’s got some good skills.” BJ: “Don’t forget, he’s got good skills AND he’s got the best damn headlock in the business!” AT: “How could I forget?” "F*ck That!" blares over the PA system and the crowd goes wild. AT: “Hey, what’s this?” Numark comes out first and walks to the left of the stage and starts posing as Bob and Sniper come out standing in the middle of the ramp. M V Pete comes out last with a knee brace on, and limping a little bit. The four make their way down to the ring, slapping hands with some of the fans on the way. Numark hits the turnbuckle and poses as Bob pulls a mic out of his pants. He lights a cigarette and starts to talk as the music dies down, along with the crowd. Bob: “Looking at the "talent" that we have in this very ring. I see why the name Usual Suspects strikes fear into our opponents. I see why the name the Usual Suspects commands respect.” Bob looks at Numark, Pete and Sniper. He chests up to Numark. Bob: “But one thing I won't tolerate is someone not trying. Winning and losing means little to me. I've won a few, I've lost quite a bit. But, not trying really...really pisses me off.” Bob walks over to the bulk that is Sniper. Bob: “The Usual Suspects are about sacrifice. Are about wrestling. And above all else the Suspects are about building a group of men who put the sport back into sports entertainment. We've come far. Tag Team champs, XuW Heavyweight champ...runners up to the Stable Wars. But, one of us is a weak link. One of us has stopped trying to excel. One of us has become a cancer...sucking the life, slowly...spreading throughout this stable of champions.” Bob chests up to MVPete and looks at his leg. Bob: “One of us has become weak...lame. I wonder who that could be?” Pete looks at Bob and grits his teeth. Suddenly, Numark delivers a Superkick to Sniper's head, knocking him out cold. Bob spits on the KO'd monster. Numark then takes the mic from Bob. Nu: “Look, no offence Sniper... it's all business. You just haven't been trying. Wherever we were, you found a way to not be there. It has nothing to do with your wrestling skills or your mic skills. It has to do with the fact that we are a tight unit and you just weren't conforming to our ways. Look, we can still be friends as we always were... but this is business. Now... We have big news for you. Now, the stable is three members. One of which is injured... a temporary set back, I'm sure... but none the less, we have decided to fill the void that Sniper has left. This person is a man of honor, respect, and strength. He knows what it is like to be a member of a stable in this group. More than a stable in fact... a family. That is what we are... a family. What you see here is the most elite group of athletes this business has to offer, and we had to find someone that would fit in well. Well, we have. Ladies and gentlemen... please welcome, THE NEWEST member of the Usual Suspects..... Bionic Carrot!” "Bad Moon Rising" hits on the PA system and the fans go wild as BC makes his way down the ramp with a much happier look on his face then in days past. He climbs upon the ring and poses on the turnbuckles for the crowd. He jumps down in the ring and is greeted with hugs and handshakes form Pete, Numark and Bob. They hand BC the mic. The crowd lets out a monster pop as BC takes the mic. He motions for them to settle down just a bit. But the more he tries the more they pop. Finally BC begins to speak. BC: “Before I go any further…” The crowd pops again. BC: “You fans might be wondering why BC is thinking about being a Usual Suspect. Well like Marlon Brando in the Godfather said they made me an offer I can’t refuse. It could not have come at a better time for me. Lately I have had some problems with people who I thought where friends…family so to speak. All I ever wanted was to be the best stable in XUW. AJ proved I was blind to what he was really all about.” Bob: “BC, there is no one who understands teamwork like you. We recruited you not because you need us, but because we need you. We need someone with your desire, your skills...your fire. Will you join hands with the Usual Suspects and help us kick the living sh*t out of the entire XuW roster? Will you step up and become a member of the elite stable known as the Usual Suspects?” BC: “This is an honor and I will not let you guys down no matter what the cost. You can take that to the bank.” The crowd pops once again seeing the reformed Usual Suspects standing in the ring. M V Pete then takes the mic and starts to speak as the crowd dies down to hear what he has to say. MVP: “Since this is a big night of announcements here. I have one of my own as well. You see. I injured this leg at Liberation, in the land down under. So I made the mistake of going to some Aussie hospital and getting diagnosed and treated there. Since getting back here, I met with a REAL doctor and got the REAL news. It turns out, this injury ain't as bad as those damn dingo humpers thought. And the recovery time ain't as long neither. So to those who think they can f*ck with the Usual Suspects since we're a man down. Think again. Because VERY soon, you're gonna have to f**k with…” Points thumbs downwards, a la RVD MVP: “M-V-PETE!” "F*ck That!" hits once more and the crowd basically blows the roof off of the building as The Usual Suspects pose for a moment than leave the ring and to the back. The music cuts down as Sniper finally gets to his feet. He grabs his chin and hops into the ring extremely pissed off. He picks up the mic that was dropped in the ring and begins to talk. Sniper: “Oh, so that's how it is, huh boys? I do my own thing for a bit and you throw me to the side like a piece of garbage? Well F**K YOU! F**K THE FANS! F**K ALL THE GUYS IN THE BACK! I don't need you... I NEVER needed you! You needed me. Numark... you better hope we don't meet in this tournament, because if we do... I am going to rip your F****ING head off! Do you hear me? You guys are going to regret the day you crossed me... because once I get you in my cross hairs... it's only gonna take one shot, and I will be the last left standing.” Sniper throws down the mic and the fans boo him as "Unsung" hits and he storms to the back looking extremely pissed off. AT: "Wow, Bionic Carrot is now a member of The Usual Suspects! I can't believe it!" BJ: "Me neither, why would they want a loser like that in their ranks. I mean, he used to be one of the ponies!" AT: "Hey, shut up! BC is one of the longest standing, most well-respected superstars in XUW, and they know that they can always count on him to have their back if the going gets tough." BJ: "Speaking of tough, it'll be a tough time here in this next tag team match!" AT: "That was a truly terrible link." BJ: "Like I care? All I want to know is how well Blake Savage and Kevil will be working together as a team. They've not really been seeing eye to eye over the past few weeks." AT: "You can say that again, but please don't." “Date With Destiny” suddenly hits the speakers, and Michael Bolling and Dev come down to the ring to a smattering of heel heat. “Hey Man, Nice Shot” hits, and Blake Savage comes to the ring, Kevil following closely behind, with an evil glint in his eye. Ding ding. Tag Team Match: Bolling/Dev v Blake Savage/Kevil AT: “This could be interesting as there’s a fair amount of bad blood between Kevil and Savage.” BJ: “Hell, they’re almost at war, after Savage nailed Kevil with that trash can lid!” AT: “He claimed he ‘didn’t like rhymes’, but I think it runs deeper than that.” Dev and Savage lock up first, and Savage takes the advantage, whipping him to the ropes and connecting with a Diving Reverse Elbow. Savage grabs Dev by the scruff of the neck and hits The Blake-Plex. He looks over to Kevil, but decides not to tag him in at all. He continues to attack Dev, hitting some blows to the kidneys with his feet, but the fighter soon gets up and they lock up again. Dev pushes Savage away from himself and then flattens him with a Flurry of Kicks. Savage rolls away and has to tag in Kevil. The root of all evil is on the offensive at once, hitting some hard right hands, and getting the advantage, despite his small stature. He hits some quick kicks to the abdomen, and then whips Dev to the corner, following it up with the Evil Splash in the corner. Dev drops down to the floor, and Kevil goes for a Bronco Buster, but Dev dodges, and makes it to his corner to tag in Bolling. AT: “Well, I’m surprised, but Kevil and Blake Savage have held the upper hand thus far in the match.” Bolling comes in, and block an attempted kick by Kevil. The larger man makes a grab, and hits Kevil with a Piledriver. Kevil gets up, but walks straight into a Superkick, and Bolling covers. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Kevil rakes the eyes of Bolling, and escapes to tag in Savage. Savage and Bolling lock up, wrestling one way and then the other across the ring. AT: “Hey, what’s Kevil doing? He has a chair!” BJ: “He’s going to help Savage!” Kevil slides into the ring as Savage pushes Bolling away, and Savage turns round into a big chair shot by Kevil! Kevil exits the ring, laughing to himself as he goes up the ramp. Bolling gathers himself and goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Kevil, Bolling, and the whole crowd seem astonished. AT: “Savage is fighting to stay in there, but surely the end is nigh for this match.” Bolling picks up Savage and hits a standing Dropkick. Savage goes down and Bolling pins again. 1 … … 2 … … FOOT ON THE ROPES! Bolling shakes his head, and picks up Savage, looking to hit a Suplex, but Blake fights back and hits a Blake-Plex of his own. Dev rushes the ring, but Savage takes him down with a Blake-sational Neckbreaker. Bolling gets up, but Savage nails him with a Clothesline. Dev gets up, but Bolling take him down with a Big Right Hand. AT: “By God, Bolling’s clearing house!” Kevil runs back down the ramp, and as Savage prepares to clock Bolling one, Kevil nails him with a chair again. Savage’s eyes roll up in his head and Bolling grabs him, hitting the Destiny Slam and hooking the leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winners, by pinfall, Bolling/Dev Dev and Bolling celebrate in the ring, and then leave to the back. Kevil dives back into the ring and starts wailing on Savage’s knee with a chair. Officials stream from the back and try to stop him, but he lays in a few more shots, before retreating to the back with a smile on his face. AT: "That was brutal, disgusting and unnecessary!" BJ: "Just like those nude pictures of your wife on the internet!" AT: "What?!?" BJ: "Ummm, never mind." AT: "Hmmmm. Well, ladies and gentlemen, we've just got word that the last time Blake Wolfson was seen he was being detained by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, somewhere near the border." BJ: "Well, the Mounties always get their man, though, from the rumours Harlequin has been spreading, so does Wolfson!" AT: "Speaking of hardcore, here's our next match, where Hodgo will defend his Hardcore Title against The Apostle!” "Anarchy in the UK" blasts through the arena, and Hodgo makes his way up to the top of the ramp, wearing protective sunglasses to avoid any eye-bound projectiles coming his way. He smiles as all the thrown beer cups bounce off him harmlessly, and he makes his way down to the ring, climbing between the ropes. "Psalm 69" hits the speakers, and out comes The Apostle, to a moderate pop. He makes the sign of the cross, and comes down the ramp to the ring, to wage battle for the forces of good! XUW Hardcore Title Match: Hodgo (C) v The Apostle Hodgo and The Apostle lock up, with Apostle sending Hodgo to the ropes and looking to hit a Clothesline. Hodgo ducks and hits a Neckbreaker. AT: "I'm surprised to see Hodgo start without any hardcore implements in the ring, maybe the good vibes given off by the Apostle are making him ensure it's a fair fight?" BJ: "Unlikely, he's just climbed out of the ring and got a steel chair from the timekeeper!" AT: "That always happens, we should just make that guy stand up the whole time." Hodgo swings the chair at the Apostle, but Apostle dodges, and the chair comes back off the ropes and smacks Hodgo in the face, knocking off his sunglasses and sending him down to the canvas. He gets up quickly, but the Apostle is on him quickly. He goes for a Suplex, but Hodgo blocks and reverses into a Suplex of his own. Hodgo slides to the outside again and comes back with a model aeroplane, an orange and a small wooden table from Ikea. AT: “This could be brutal!” Hodgo smacks Apostle over the head with the table, and then squeezes the juice from the orange right into the eyes of the Apostle. BJ: “Right in the eye! There’s poetic justice for you!” Hodgo whips Apostle to the ropes and then throws the model aeroplane, smacking Apostle on the head and dazing him. He stumbles into a stiff Anar-kick from Hodgo, and Hodgo goes for the pin. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Hodgo and the Apostle both roll outside of the ring and look for weapons. Hodgo comes back with a piece of two by four, and a trusty coconut. Apostle comes back with … … a large cross! Hodgo swings the two by four, but Apostle dodges and smacks Hodgo across the head with the cross, and then hits him with it … RIGHT IN THE EYE! Hodgo tries to shrug it off, but Apostle clocks him on the head with it again, and Hodgo falls down, smacking his head on the treacherous coconut! Apostle covers. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner, and NEW XUW Hardcore Champion – The Apostle!!!!! AT: "Well, to my mind we've just seen one hell of a surprise!" BJ: "Hodgo was cheated. There was clearly a double team there by God and The Apostle. He should be disqualified immediately, and the title given back to its rightful owner!" AT: "Will you stop?" BJ: "Only to tell you that Blake Savage has been tended to by EMTs in the backstage area, and he is fine. There is no lasting damage from the injuries inflicted by Kevil, but he may be walking with a limp for the next week or so." AT: "I'm also getting word that the Root of All Evil, Kevil himself, will be getting a two week unpaid suspension for his heinous act, so Blake Savage had better put thoughts of revenge on the back burner for the time being." Camera cuts backstage to where Saij is sitting, watching a replay of "The Professor" Ric Austin winning the TV Title tonight. Saij: "Hmmm, he thinks he's so perfect, doesn't he? He thinks with his little streak of wins that he can't be beaten. Well, he may have had fun showing people the error of their ways so far in XUW, but when he starts to mess with the XUW elite, we'll see if he's got what it takes." Camera pans across to see who Saij is talking to ... it's Laudust, still wearing his La Parka costume! Laudust (paying no attention to Saij): "Swooooooooooo!" Saij rolls his eyes. Saij: "He thinks his headlock will be able to beat me? He's got another thing coming, right Laudust?" Laudust: "Swoooooooooo ... LaParkaDust!" Laudust wanders away, doing the La Parka dance, leaving Saij on his own, totally and utterly bemused. Cut back to announce desk. AT: "That boy is pet coon goofy." BJ: “Well, that may be so, but here’s a much anticipated clash between Kore and Numark!” “Greed” by Godsmack begins to blare over the arena loudspeakers as out comes Kore with the Grand Wizard in tow, to a loud chorus of boos. As the pair walk down the ramp, the Wiz yells out his last instructions before Kore hops into the ring. “Soldier” by Eminem hits and out comes Numark to a huge face pop, as he acknowledges the crowd and makes his way down to ringside. The referee rings the bell and the match begins. XUW Intercontinental Title Tournament Match: Kore vs. Numark The pair lock up in the centre of the ring. Kore overpowers Nu and pushes him to the corner. The referee tries to get a clean break but Kore pokes Nu in the eye. Kore takes Nu, whips him to the ropes and hits a hip toss. He goes for the cover, but Nu kicks out at 1. Kore backs Nu back into the corner and climbs to the second rope as he begins to punch away at Nu. Nu lifts Kore up and hot shots him over the top rope, sending him to the outside of the ring. Nu follows Kore to the outside of the ring, he picks him up and goes to whip him into the ring post, but Kore reverses and Nu goes flying into the steel, as he crashes to the floor. Kore regathers his thoughts, and get back in the ring, distracting the ref, and allowing TGW to hit Kore a few times with his ever-present wand. AT: “He hit him with the wand! That’s blatant cheating!” BJ: “He did not! It’s more a sceptre than a wand!” Wiz rolls Nu back into the ring, and Kore goes to work, dropping several stiff knees to his lower back, and then hitting the Obscure Reference. He goes for a cover. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Nu gets to his feet, but Kore hits him with a hard kick to the chest and then a big Brainbuster. AT: That could be it right there!” Kore covers, but only to a short two count. He picks up Numark again, and sets him up for the Paranoia Anonymous, but Numark counters and hits a DDT, leaving both men on the canvas. The ref starts to count, but as he gets to six, both men are on one knee, and rising. AT: “Hey, ref, get the Wiz off the apron! He’s got no business being up there!” The ref goes to get rid of TGW, and as he does so Kore hits a Low Blow on Numark and follows it up with the Royal Flush, and then the Peaked Point (5* Frog Splash) from the top rope! He hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Kore looks on in despair as the referee indicates it was a fraction shy of a three count. Numark gets to his feet and tries to shake the cobwebs from his head as Kore advances. Kore looks for a Clothesline, but Numark ducks under it and Kore continues his run off the ropes on the far side of the ring. Numark looks up and hits a hard Superkick, knocking Kore down and giving himself time to regroup and plan a new strategy. Kore gets up, and Numark locks his arms in for a Cradle Piledriver, but Kore fights out and hits a Back Body Drop, and stumbles to the corner. Nu gets up quickly and charges Kore, hitting a hard Clothesline and then flipping him over, locking in an Ankle Lock! AT: “Numark was lagging to start with, but those hours with Stu Hart have paid off. He has greater endurance than before, and that ankle lock looks tighter than I’ve seen it before!” Kore screams in pain, but edges closer and closer to the ropes, until he can finally grab it and force Numark to release the hold. Kore hobbles away on one leg, and Numark is right back on him, hitting a German Suplex, and then another! He goes for a pin. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Nu goes up top and looks for The Bombshell, but Kore dodges at the last moment, and Numark eats mat. Kore distracts the referee once again, by feigning injury, and the Wiz enters the ring sneakily, and kicks Numark square in the nuts! Kore goes for a cover. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “He nearly had him there, after that obvious piece of trickery!” BJ: “Well, Bob and Numark have been going on about how good their mentor is, in Stu Hart, but they never stopped to consider that The Grand Wizard might be smarter than them all, and have some clever tactics up his ample sleeves.” AT: “Don’t justify cheating as intelligence, buffoon!” BJ: “Why the hell not?” Kore gains the advantage again, hitting a few hard right hands and then throwing Numark to the corner, and hitting a Monkey Flip to send Nu back into the centre of the ring. Kore goes for a second rope Leg Drop, and hits it! He hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! AT: “Numark clearly wants this match as bad as Kore does!” Kore picks up Numark again, and looks to hit a Swinging Neckbreaker, but Numark counters the move and lifts Kore up, hitting a Spinning Powerbomb. He covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Kore gets up, dazed, and Numark goes for the Cradle Piledriver once more, but Kore breaks his arms free and drops Numark to the floor with a stiff right hand. Kore locks in a Sharpshooter, and Numark grabs out at the nearest rope, managing to reach it before too much damage can be done. Kore charges Numark, but Numark hits a kick to the gut. Nu looks to hit a Jumping DDT, but out of nowhere Kore grabs him and hits the Snake Eyes! He covers. 1 … Foot On The Ropes … 2 … Wiz Knocks The Foot Off … 3!!! Winner, by pinfall, KoRe!!! Kore and The Wiz back off up the ramp, and hold their arms in the air as a sign of victory. Numark sits in the ring, and slaps down on the canvas hard. AT: “Well, that was an epic battle, but in the end the dastardly dealing of The Grand Wizard, knocking Numark’s foot off the ropes, ended the match,” BJ: “He was well within his rights to do that! He was just … ummm … stretching.” AT: “Yeah, sure thing.” BJ: “Well, either way, Kore faces NFM in the winning bracket, and Numark goes on to face Syco in the losing half.” AT: “Well, there’s no way you could call Numark a loser after that effort.” Camera cuts backstage to the gWo locker room Wiz: “Well done KoRe, I knew you could do it! That title is as good as y… …” The voice of the Wizard trails off as he sees Sheri Dedmon enter his locker room. He backs up, and walks into a wall, his eyes never leaving those of Sheri. Sheri looks hard at the Wiz, but says nothing, and does nothing, other than smirk at him. The Wiz looks at Sheri with a look of distrust, and stays with a wary look on his face as Sheri slowly backs out of the room, and round the corner, her eyes never leaving those of The Grand Wizard, her gaze never once faltering. Cut back to the announce position. AT: “Well, that was strange. Is she against the gWo truly, or is this some clever ruse?” BJ: “Well, I’m just getting word that some of the border patrol have arrived here, and that they’re still looking for someone, and I can only presume that to be Blake Wolfson!” AT: “Well, he’s either here or he isn’t, because it’s Main Event time!” “Welcome to the Suburbs” plays, and Chase Wilson makes his way to the ring. “I Stand Alone” plays, and nobody comes to the ring. It plays through again, and nothing. AT: “I can’t believe that Wolfson’s World Title ambitions have been thwarted by the border patrol!” “F**k That” hits the speakers, and the crowd rises as one for a monster pop for the XUW World Champion. Bob comes down to the ring, all business, and the games begin. XUW World Title Match – Triple Threat: Chase Wilson v Samurai Bob (C) v  ummmmm … nobody The two men lock up and Chase forces Bob back, using his size and strength to his advantage. AT: “I know that Bob has been primarily focused on Wolfson, but I think he might have kept some game left in store for Chase too.” BJ: “He’ll need to have, Chase can be a monster in the ring!” Bob pushes Chase away, and then connects with a hard Steinerline. Chase somehow stays upright, but another Steinerline send him to the canvas. Bob climbs up top, but Chase gets back to his feet, and Bob changes his move from a top rope Elbow Drop to a Shoulder Charge, and knocks Wilson off his feet again. The crowd pops big for the daring move, and Bob goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! Chase proves he has plenty more where that came from, and kicks out with authority. Bob looks to hit a Knife Edge Chop on Chase, but the big man blocks it and hits the champion with the CT Crunch (Burning Hammer). Chase covers. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! AT: Bob just barely got out of that incredible move by Chase. We’ve had two new champions already tonight, what price a third one?” Chase hits the Short Term Memory Loss on the champion, and goes for another pin, but Bob kicks out at 2 again. Chase follows up with an attempted Spinning Reverse DDT, but Bob just throws him bodily into a turnbuckle. Chase slumps down, and Bob goes up top on the far side, coming off with a huge Samurai Star Elbow Drop from the top rope. He hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Chase survives, but Bob is looking dominant, and he hits a few hard right hands, and then he whips Chase to the ropes, coming back with a Flying Shoulder Tackle to send Chase to the canvas. Bob pulls Chase to the edge of the mat, and then delivers an incredible Guillotine Leg Drop from the top turnbuckle. AT: “Oh my God! Chase looks half dead from that move, and Bob isn’t looking too hot down on the outside.” Bob struggles to his feet and makes it back inside the ring, pinning Chase. 1 … … 2 … … Foot On The Ropes! Bob pulls Chase to the middle and covers again. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Bob picks up Chase, and whips him to the ropes again. He looks for a Steinerline, but Chase ducks it and bounces off the ropes, coming off with a Flying Shoulder Tackle that sends both men to the mat. Both men get up again, and Chase looks to hit the Short Term Memory Loss again, but Bob swats it aside and grabs Chase, hitting a Snap Suplex. He covers again. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “You can tell that Chase is getting weary now, and that Bob is taking control!” BJ: “Hey, look at that disturbance in the crowd! That looks like an Australian on the run to me!” Bob picks up Chase and goes to whip him to the ropes… AT: “It is! It’s Wolfson! His clothes are all tattered and torn, but he’s climbing up on the ring apron!” Chase reverses the whip and sends Bob to the ropes. Blake climbs up and nails Bob on the back of the head. Bob is stunned, and Chase turns him round, looking to hit the Dying Daze (Fisherman DDT), but Bob escapes and tries to hit a Suplex. Chase blocks it and looks for a Suplex of his own. Bob hooks his leg round Chase, blocking the Suplex, and tries for a DDT, but Chase uses his weight to hold himself firm! Chase looks to hit the Dying Daze again, as the crowd look on, popping their hearts out! Chase gets Bob halfway up, but Bob counters, hooks Chase up, and hits a Dying Daze of his own!!! Bob covers. 1 … Wolfson scrambles through the ropes … 2 … He dives at Bob … 3!!! He hits Bob. Winner, and STILL XUW Champion: Samurai Bob!!! AT: “He did it, he defended the belt again!” BJ: “Wait up, there’s still a brawl in the ring!” Chase wanders off in search of headache tablets and pretzels, and Bob and Blake exchange hard right hands. Bob gets the advantage and whips Blake to the ropes, following up with a hard Steinerline to send Wolfson over the top rope and down to the floor. Blake looks to enter the ring again, but spots the border patrol guards descending on him, and makes his getaway through the crowd. “F**k That” hits the speakers once more, and the crowd pops deliriously for their champion, Samurai Bob!
