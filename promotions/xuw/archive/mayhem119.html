RX refuses the ref's requests to submit, and holds on, despite the pain from the submission maneuver. And then Eliminator baseball slides into Factor, knocking him off of RX. RX sits up suddenly, and sees Eliminator on the mat next to him. He growls at Danny, and pulls him up to his feet. He whips Eliminator into the ropes, and as he bounces back, RX catches him, spins, and takes him down with a crushing spinebuster. Mikey: The Big Bang! That's it! BJ: Nah, that's not it. Danny's tougher than that, and you can't count Factor out. Raven grabs Eliminator and pulls him back to his feet, and sends him into the ropes. As Danny bounces back, RX nails him with a huge big boot, and Eliminator slumps to the mat. RX pulls Danny to the middle of the ring and sits on his back. Factor increases the pressure of the hold, as the ref raises RX's arm again. Again, it hits the mat. The ref signals two. The ref raises RX's arm a third time, and it starts to fall.
