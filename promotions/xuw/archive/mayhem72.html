XUW Mayhem #72

14 Apr 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Mayhem - Number 72 The scene opens outside the arena before Mayhem. Bailey, Danyon, and Larry are all outside walking around with signs in their hands and protesting the XUW. There are a surprising amount of Legionnaires out protesting with the three, as they walk in a circle shouting protest slogans. One of the Legionnaires leaves the line and walks over to Bailey and asks him something: Legionnaire fan: Bailey! I’m glad to be out here protesting here for you guys! Bailey: Thanks, kid. We all need to speak out against the conspiracy! Legionnaire fan: Well, at least at stable wars the New Royal Legion will beat everyone! Danyon: Stable wars? Bailey: I don’t think we can do stable wars. Legionnaire fan: Why not? Larry: There is no one we can trust. They are all in on the conspiracy, each and every one of them. We can’t team with any of them. Besides, I have an idea that is much better than any Stable Wars… Bailey: Ohh really? Danyon To the Legionnaire: Go back to the line… Legionnaire fan: Okay! The fan runs back into the picket line as Bailey, Danyon, and Larry huddle together and we hear some inaudible mumbling as we go back inside as Mayhem is LIVE! Pyro explodes as the camera pans from the Ulti-Tron all over the XUW arena. Thousands of fans are packed in, some of which are carrying signs such as: I am entering Stable Wars! What ever happened to the Lurchador??!! Samurai Bob is gonna recycle Wildlife! And… I eat glue…Butterflies are next! The camera finally pans down to Mikey and Barry Johnson sitting at the announce table at ringside. Mikey: This is Mikey here… BJ: …and Mr. Majestic Barry Johnson! Mikey: …and we bring you MAYHEM live! Tonight we’ve got a fantastic show for you, including two title matches. BJ: Ohh, come now. You and I both know that Wildlife shouldn’t have to defend his Intercontinental title against Samurai Bob. It just isn’t fair. If anything, Bob should put HIS title on the line. Mikey: Well, the IC title IS on the line tonight, between Samurai Bob and Wildlife. That should be a great match, but what should be an equally good match will put No Fear Man against Hot Boy D in a match for an Intercontinental title shot against the winner of tonight’s main event. BJ: NFM has been around this federation since the beginning, MUCH longer than Hot Boy D. NFM shouldn’t even have to BE in this match to get a title shot. Mikey: None the less, NFM will have to earn a shot at the IC champion. Also tonight, we will see DarkSeid defend his newly won Hardcore title for the first time against Hardkore…one of very few men in this fed that are actually bigger than Seid himself. BJ: Seid is a big guy, but Hardkore is even bigger and he can dish out some serious punishment…or just set you on fire. Mikey: And last, but certainly not least, we have a six man tag with Heartbreak Kid and the Lunch Room Patrol taking on the Hardcore Icons and the Fallen Fae in what should be a classic. BJ: HBK and the entire Army of Darkness have quite the history…usually a history of HBK getting his ass handed to him on a silver platter. Mikey: In any case, he has back up tonight with the Lunch Room Patrol in his corner. Just then “Area Codes” hits and Hot Boy D emerges from behind the curtain. The crowd pops for Webster’s right hand man, as he makes his way down to ringside. As HBD slides into the ring, “Fear (The NFM mix” by Disturbed hits and NFM steps into the arena to some boos from the crowd. NFM has an intense look on his face as he walks to the ring with a purpose. NFM reaches the ring and slides in, then gets right into HBD’s face. NFM starts jawing at HBD, who just stands there and takes it. As soon as NFM is done talking, HBD gets a big grin on his face. This pisses NFM off even more, as the bell rings… NFM v. Hot Boy D …and HBD hits NFM square in the jaw! HBD starts with some more hard rights and left to NFM’s face, then backs him into the ropes. HBD attempts an Irish whip, but NFM reverses it. HBD bounces off the ropes and NFM goes for a quick clothesline, but HBD instead hits NFM with a float over DDT! HBD goes for the quick cover… 1… kickout by NFM! NFM kicks out quickly, but HBD doesn’t give him much time to breathe. HBD immediately grabs NFM and locks on a headlock. NFM turns and finally manages to get to his feet, then hits HBD in the stomach with an elbow. HBD locks the hold in tighter, prompting another Elbow to the midsection from NFM. NFM hits a third elbow and then sends HBD into the ropes. D bounces off the ropes and runs over NFM, who is face down on the mat. D bounces off the opposite side, and runs right under NFM who leaps over HBD. NFM looks to be going for another leapfrog, as HBD returns, but instead NFM just hits a Lou Thez press on Hoy Boy D and begins to pummel him with hard rights hands. NFM gets to his feet and kicks HBD in the stomach a few times as the crowd begins to boo… Mikey: The crowd is obviously firmly behind Hot Boy D. BJ: Does that even really matter? NFM is clearly the better man in this match… Mikey: How can you say that. We are barely INTO the match. BJ: Just you watch… Back in the ring NFM grabs D by the hair and pulls him to his feet. NFM hits a few forearms to the head and then places his arm over HBD’s head and hits a hard and quick snap Suplex. HBD sits up to gasp for air, but NFM gives him no time and throws D back down to the mat. NFM covers HBD, with his forearm across D’s face… 1… 2… KICKOUT by HBD!! NFM looks a little frustrated, but doesn’t let it get to him. He immediately grabs the now sitting up HBD from behind. NFM sticks his knee in the middle of D’s back, then grabs him around the chin and begins to pull his head back. D yells out in pain as NFM’s knee digs further into his back, and HBD’s head is pulled further backwards. HBD tries to pry apart NFM’s grip, but his fingers are locked tightly around his neck. HBD begins to roll to the side, but NFM pulls back harder, prompting D to stop in his tracks… BJ: I told ya. There is no way HBD can hold out in this move much longer. Mikey: Give D some credit. He can take some serious punishment. That move is very painful, as your neck is torked way back and the knee in your back digs in further…not many men can take that hold for very long… NFM pulls back further as the referee checks on HBD. D refuses to give up, though, and he finally rolls to the right and breaks the hold. D doesn’t make it far, however, as NFM hits D in the back of the neck with a hard forearm, and D crashes to the mat. NFM stands over D and begins to verbally berate him. The crowd is on its feet yelling and booing NFM, who looks to the crowd. He looks around and begins to scream at fans while still over HBD. D, however, has started to come too. HBD gets to his knees, as NFM hasn’t noticed yet. Finally, D stands up and picks NFM up on his shoulders! NFM looks around confused for a moment, then begins to hit D with some hard rights to the face. HBD doesn’t take it for long, though, as he lifts NFM up a little more and sits down as he slams NFM to the mat face first. NFM flips over to his back from the impact, and HBD makes the cover right there… 1… 2… KICKOUT by NFM!! HBD looks to be a bit frustrated, as he picks up NFM. HBD backs NFM into a corner, where he hits NFM with a hard chop across the chest. NFM grabs his chest and doubles over, but D picks him right back up and does it again…and a third time. D grabs NFM by the hand and whips him across to the opposite corner, and follows closely behind. No sooner does NFM hit the corner than he is met with a hard lariat by HBD. D whips NFM right back, and once again follows closely behind. This time, however, NFM grabs the top rope and jumps over the incoming HBD. NFM lands behind D and shoves him into the corner face first. NFM then grabs D’s feet and picks them up, as D’s upper body and arms are on the top rope. NFM looks to the crowd with an evil look, then proceeds to kick D square in the groin. D falls to the mat and grabs himself, as the referee reprimands NFM… Mikey: How can that be allowed! That was a blatant low blow! BJ: That was a kick to the midsection, and we all know that. Don’t go spouting your little theories about low blows unless you have been hit like that before… Mikey: I am a former world champion, you know. I HAVE been hit like that… BJ: Ohh…well…it wasn’t a low blow. NFM is tired of the verbal berating he is taking from the ref, and he just shoves him out of the way. NFM then grabs HBD by the hair and picks him up. NFM grabs HBD around the neck and goes for the Final Destination (Choke Slam), but as he attempts to pick HBD up, HBD kicks NFM in the gut and hits a hard DDT, and both men fall to the mat. NFM is the first to his feet, and he reaches down and picks HBD up. He goes to hit HBD with a right, but D blocks and hits a right of his own. NFM tries again, but once again D blocks it and hits another right. D then begins to hit a series of rights to NFM, then finally picks NFM up and hits a scoop slam. D climbs to the top rope and looks down on his waiting opponent, then dives off and hits the Self Destruct (450 splash) perfectly on top of NFM and goes for the cover… 1…. 2…. 2.5…. 2.99…. 3!!!! Winner: Hot Boy D Just after the referee hits three, NFM’s shoulder shoots up, but it was a second too late. HBD rolls out of the ring as “Area Codes” begins to play again and the crowd pops. HBD goes to the crowd and shakes some hands NFM looks around in awe and amazement. BJ: NFM got his shoulder up! Mikey: Not before the three, though. NFM gave it one hell of an effort, but HBD pulled it out against the veteran. BJ: That referee is corrupt I tell ya. NFM should have won this match. Mikey: Hot Boy D won fair and square, and now some time before Big Brawl, he will be getting a shot at the Intercontinental title, and whomever may hold it at the time. We cut backstage where we see Dante and Sara walking down the hall of the arena. Dante’s ribs are heavily taped, and Dante is still holding his body. They turn a corner, and run into Sheri and Numark. Nu: Hey Dante. I’d ask how you were doing, but it looks like I can already tell… Dante: Yea, I’ve definitely had better times. Sheri: Its too bad you got injured now, with stable wars coming up. Numark here was one of the best guys in it last year, and he almost had it won if not for the AoD. Nu: Stop it… Sheri: Stop what? It’s true. You did fantastic last year, and this year you’ll do even better. Sara: Stable wars? Forgive me, but what’s that? Dante: Basically, all the stables in the XUW are in a tournament, and the winning stable gets title shots. I was going to go in with the Horsemen, but obviously that isn’t an option right now. Nu: It’s probably better. You should let those injuries heal. They look nasty. Dante: A little internal bleeding and some broken ribs won’t be stopping me. Sheri: Maybe not…but not having a team might. Dante just shrugs… Dante: Who said I don’t already? I will be in stable wars, don’t worry. I looked back and saw it from last year, and I know it propelled you into the top of the fed. You’ve done a lot since Stable Wars last year, Nu, but I’m afraid there’s gonna be a new stand out this year. Nu: Well, good luck too ya. Sheri: Nice meeting you Sara. Sara: You too. Nu and Sheri continue on their way, as Dante and Sara continue their walk towards the trainer’s office, and we cut back to ringside with BJ and Mikey. Mikey: Well, it looks like Dante might have something up his sleeve for Stable Wars. BJ: Yea, but at the injury rate he’s going at, it’s probably a broken arm. Mikey: Sure, Dante is down, but he certainly isn’t out. I don’t doubt that Dante will be back in full force by Stable Wars… We cut to a shot of the Ulti-Tron, with Mikey’s voice over: Mikey: Stable wars will take place live at Big Brawl 2, Sunday, May 11th, at the Veterans Field in Philadelphia. Tickets went on sale Saturday, and were sold out within half an hour, but you don’t have too miss out, as it will be live on Pay Per View as well! We cut back to the announce table… BJ: You are such a shill for “The Man.” Mikey: You mean Webster? BJ: Yea. All you do his shill his stuff, his products. Let him do the promotional work… Mikey: You do understand that if I don’t shill the Pay Per View, then XUW won’t do as well and YOU might be out of a job. BJ thinking for a second: …Buy the Pay Per View! Big Brawl! You’ll love it! Mikey: Idiot… Just then, Hardkore begins to walk to the ring without any theme music. The crowd is more confused than anything, as there are scattered cheers and books for hardkore. He climbs into the ring, just as “Enter Sandman” blasts through the arena. DarkSeid enters the arena to a chorus of boos. He stands at the top of the ramp with his dented chair that has Dante’s name written on it in blood. He raises the chair over his head as the crowd’s boos grow louder. DarkSeid just laughs and walks to the ring. DS gets into the ring and hands his newly acquired hardcore title to the ref, who shows it to Hardkore, then rings the bell… Hardcore title match: DarkSeid © v. Hardkore Seid and Hardkore start off by circling each other, but that doesn’t last long as Hardkore charges at Seid and begins to hit him with some extremely hard forearms to the face. Hardkore then rolls out of the ring and grabs a trash can. He puts it on the apron, but Seid was waiting for it and he kicks the can into hardkore’s face. Seid then slides out of the ring himself and reaches under it. The first thing Seid grabs is a lead pipe. Hardkore gets up and leans against the ring post, as Seid swings the pipe at Hardkore’s head! Hardkore ducks and we hear a loud ringing as Seid drops the pipe and shakes the sting out of his hands. He looks up just in time to get a kick to the stomach from Hardkore. Hardkore grabs the pipe and swings it and hits Seid in the back of the head. Seid crashes to the ground, and Hardkore looks into the front row. A fan starts to scream at Hardkore. Hardkore walks over to the fan and swings the lead pipe and this the FAN in the head! The fan falls to the ground in a heap, and Hardkore laughs at him. Mikey: What is wrong with Hardkore! He attacked a fan! BJ: Do you know how many times I’ve wanted to do that? Mikey: But you never would. BJ: One of these days, I just might… Hardkore continues to yell at the fans as Seid gets to his feet. Seid walks over and grabs a chair out from under the time keeper. Just as Hardkore turns around, he is met with a steel chair to the face. Hardkore only falls to a knee, though, as Seid swings the chair again. This time, the chair is dented over Hardkore’s head, and Hardkore is wearing it like a hat. Seid laughs as Hardkore gets to his feet with the chair still dented over his head. Seid then grabs the lead pipe from before and hits the front of the chair, knocking the legs into hardkore’s forehead! Hardkore falls to the mat again, and Seid gets into the ring and poses with the lead pipe. Hardkore gets to his feet outside the ring and looks more pissed off than angry, and climbs back onto the apron. Hardkore tries to get into the ring by stepping over the top rope, but as he gets one foot over, Seid kicks the rope into Hardkore’s groin! Hardkore grabs his crotch and falls down inside the ring, as Seid laughs. Seid grabs the pipe from before and falls to the mat, and starts choking hardkore with it. Seid stops choking Hardkore and stands over him. He then takes the pipe and swings it down at Hardkore’s head! Hardkore moves at the last second, though, the pipe hits so hard that Seid drops it. Seid gets to his feet and is met with a kick to the stomach. Hardkore grabs Seid around the neck and lifts him up for a suplex, then holds him in the air! Mikey: What a display of strength from Hardkore! Seid is a BIG man! BJ: Well, Hardkore is an even bigger man! Back in the ring, Hardkore finally drops Seid on the back of his head and neck, hitting a hard brain buster. Hardkore gets to his feet and tries to get to the top rope. Hardkore purches himself on the top rope, while Seid climbs to his feet. Seid looks up and sees Hardkore on the top rope facing away from him, So Seid runs over and shoves Hardkore off the top rope… …and into the first row of the crowd! Hardkore lands in the crowd over a group of older women, and he crushes them. Hardkore gets to his feet and looks really pissed off. Hardkore looks around the crowd and sees a fan wearing a Dante shirt. Hardkore gets an enraged look on his face and grabs the fan by the neck. Hardkore the picks the fan up and choke slams him on the outside! Mikey: What is going on with Hardkore! BJ: I am liking him more every minute! I am a Hardkore mark! Mikey: He is out of his mind, someone stop him! But it is too late. Hardkore pulls a bottle of lighter fluid out of his trunks and sprays it all over the fan as the crowd around him begin to run away! Hardkore pulls out a book of matches, lights one, and throws it down on the lighter fluid covered fan! The crowd around him screams and runs away as EMTs and other emergency works charge towards the burning fan. Hardkore laughs and begins to walk make his way through the crowd again, looking for more people too burn. Meanwhile, back in the ring, Seid is standing at the corner watching Hardkore with a perplexed look on his face. Seid laughs a bit at the antics of Hardkore, and is so busy watching him that he doesn’t see Enigma sneak into the ring! Enigma stands looming behind DarkSeid with a trash can and a trash can lid in his hands. Seid turns around, and Enigma hits him square in the head with the trash can lid! Seid falls to the mat, as Enigma stands over him. Enigma takes the trash can and puts it over Seid’s head, then lays Seid down in front of the corner. Enigma leaps to the top rope in a single bound, then turns around and jumps off, catching huge air before coming crashing down with his leg across the trash can! The trash can is dented to hell, and DarkSeid looks to be almost unconscious. Enigma pulls the trash can off of Seid, to reveal that Seid is now busted open and has a very glazed over look on his face. Enigma laughs as he looks down, then poses as “Long Hard Road Out of Hell” begins to play and Enigma walks towards the back… Official decision: No Contest Seid manages to get to his feet in the ring once again, and the ref hands him the hardcore title. Seid looks down at the hardcore title, and clutches it to his chest. He turns and rolls out of the ring, then makes his way to the back. Mikey: Wow…I can’t believe what we just witnessed. Hardkore is setting fans on fire, and Enigma just attacked DarkSeid from behind for no apparent reason! BJ: That was…the single…greatest segment…in Mayhem history. Mikey: What? BJ: The carnage…the violence…the complete lack of goody goodys! Mikey: You’ve got a little drool there… BJ wipes the drool from his mouth and looks at Mikey. BJ: Why must you ruin my fun? Mikey: What was fun about that? Fans are on their way to the hospital…and let’s not forget about the strange death of Enigma’s manager Michael Baldwin Bruce. BJ: That was fun because it was nothing but carnage. That is what I love! Mikey just slaps his forehead as we cut to the back and into Webster’s office. Webster is sitting behind his desk writing something as the door swings open and Wildlife enters the office, with his friend Kidd closely behind. Webster doesn’t even look up from his desk, as he is used to people barging in on him. Wildlife looks at the paper Webster is writing on and becomes enraged! Wildlife: Is that paper recycled??!! Webster Finally looking up, and rather confused: What? Wildlife: You had better not be writing on paper that isn’t recycled! Webster: Umm…it’s recycled…now can I help you? Wildlife: I shouldn’t be in a match with Samurai Bob tonight. It isn’t right. Bob is a sad, pathetic polluter. He doesn’t deserve a shot at my IC title! Webster Even more confused: What would him being a polluter have to do with getting a shot at the Intercontinental title? Wildlife: Intercontinental title? Ohh, get with the times! This isn’t the Intercontinental title anymore…it’s now the Improving Cleanliness title! Webster Infuriated: Excuse me? You can’t just change the names of my titles! Wildlife: Well, I did, and Bob doesn’t deserve a shot at it. If by some miracle Bob should happen to win, then he would have two titles! That’s not ecologically sound! Webster: Wait wait wait…I’m still stuck on this title name change…what makes you think you have the authority to change that title? Wildlife: You are a polluter, so you have no right to name a title! Webster: Excuse me? Look, no matter what, you are defending that title tonight against Bob, and that is all there is too it, understand? Wildlife: Fine…let’s go Kidd. We’ve got things to do, plans to make, paper to recycle… Wildlife and Kidd turn and leave the room as Webster just shakes his head and returns to his paperwork and we cut back to ringside. Mikey: How dare Wildlife change that title name? BJ: He has the right, it is his title now! Mikey: That title has a lot of history behind it! It has had a lot of prestigious holders! BJ: And now it has the most prestigious holder. Wildlife is the best thing to happen to that title. Mikey: I disagree…but that is normal. Our next match will be a six man tag team match. HBK will be getting some back up from the Lunchroom Patrol to take on the Army of Darkness, consisting of the Fallen Fae and the Hardcore Icons. BJ: This is just going to be a preview of Stable Wars. Mikey: What? BJ: Stable wars. When Stable Wars rolls around we will get to see the AoD dominate it again, just like last year. Mikey: Don’t forget about the other teams formed already. The Ultra Violents will be giving the Army of Darkness a run for their money when it comes to Stable Wars. BJ: Ohh yea…The Ultra Violents… Mikey: Yea, which of those do you like? BJ: It doesn’t matter. As long as one of them win, and people like Bob and the Lunchroom Patrol don’t win, I will be a happy man. “Welcome to the Planet Motherf***er” begins to blast through the arena as the Fallen Fae steps through the curtain poses with Kat at the top of the ramp, when his music cuts out and we hear “Droppin Like Flies” by Firewater plays and the Hardcore Icons step into the arena and pose at the top of the ramp. The crowd screams out boos as they make their way to the ring. The three of them slide into the ring and pose in the corners as “Lunchlady Land” hits and The Lunchroom Patrol emerge to a big pop. They pose on top of the ramp and stare down at their opponents as “No Quarter” by Tool blasts through the arena as HBK emerges to a huge pop. HBK and the Lunchroom patrol run down to ringside and slide in. They stand face to face with the AoD members. Six Man Tag Match: Lunchroom Patrol and HBK v Hardcore Icons and Fallen Fae The bell rings, and HBK starts the match off against the Fallen Fae. FF starts by turning round to scan the crowd for shiny objects, causing HBK to shrug his shoulders, before grabbing FF and hitting a Reverse DDT. FF hits the ground hard, and rolls away from any further danger into his own corner. Once there, Kat whispers something in his ear, and his eyes flash with sudden menace. Mikey: Uh oh, I don’t know what Kat told Sidhe, but it doesn’t look like it was nice. BJ: What are you talking about? Sidhe isn’t here! Mikey: Oh, not this again… The Fae runs forwards and dives into a Spinning Heel Kick, but HBK swats it aside. Fae flips up in an instant though and catches HBK by surprise, grabbing his arm and turning him into a Judo Hip Toss. HBK rolls up, but Fae nails him down with a big Lariatoooooo! He goes to capitalise, but HBK counters with a Drop Toe Hold, and tags in the Latch Key Kid. LKK goes right to work by dropping a knee across the back of Fae’s leg. Fae yelps out in pain, and LKK keeps working on the lower body of Fae, hooking in a Spinning Toe Hold, and then doing it again for good measure. Fae is in pain, and LKK lifts him up to deliver a Shin Breaker. Fae is down again, and LKK tags in Doughboy, allowing the two of them to hit a nice Double Suplex. Mikey: Good teamwork by the Lunchroom Patrol to start this match off. Dough lifts up Fae and hits a Spinebuster, then locking in the Cookie Press (Half Crab) on the bad leg of the Fae. The man who looks suspiciously like Sidhe in facepaint crawls towards the ropes, and just manages to grab the bottom one. Dough releases the hold, but Fae just bits him on the leg, causing Dough to wince in pain. Fae dives for the corner and tags in Raven X, who runs in quickly and takes Doughboy down with a Clothesline. Dough gets up, but RX has sneaked round behind him and locks him into a Full Nelson. He drags Dough one way and then the other, before turning and delivering a thunderous Full Nelson Slam, which knocks the wind out of the former XUDF star. Raven X goes for a pin. 1… 2…. KICKOUT!!! Mikey: Raven X delivers that move with authority, but it’ll take more than that to keep Doughboy down. BJ: Bah. It was clearly three. RX lifts up Dough and rakes him in the eyes, before whipping him into the AoD corner and following it up with a Big Splash. RX threatens to punch Doughboy, and the ref ushers him away, leaving a golden opportunity for the heels to choke Doughboy with the tag rope, which they take with aplomb. The ref eventually spots it, and Dough staggers out of the corner, only to get hit with the Big Bang Theory (Super Spinning Spinebuster) from RX. The Hardcore Icon tags in his partner, and the malicious and evil DDT enters the ring. He picks up Doughboy and immediately delivers the Shock Treatment! He covers! 1… 2… BROKEN UP BY HBK!!! Doughboy staggers to his feet, as the ref clears the ring of HBK. DDT moves in for the kill, but Dough manages to grab his leg as he attempts a kick, and hit a Dragon Screw Leg Whip. Dough runs off the far ropes, re-invigorated, and hits the Jelly Roll (Rolling Thunder). The crowd pops, and Dough finally makes it to his own corner to tag LKK back in. The Kid enters the ring as DDT gets up. LKK grabs DDT by the arms and then drags him in to hit a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. DDT lands hard on his back and almost bounces back up to a standing base. LKK quickly helps up the psychotic chef and hits him with the Cheese and Crackers (Atomic Drop into Big Boot). He hooks a leg… 1… 2… KICKOUT! Mikey: Quite an even match so far. BJ: Nonsense. The Hardcore Icons and the Fallen Fae are clearly dominating the match. Look, DDT just used his skills to gain the momentum again. Mikey: He just hit the Latch Key Kid in the nuts while the ref wasn’t looking! BJ: Now that’s talent. Some other schmuck would have let the ref see it. DDT is way classier. Indeed, DDT has gotten himself back into the ascendancy through somewhat debatable tactics, and follows this up with another quick Shock Treatment DDT to give himself another pinning chance. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! DDT wastes no time in picking up LKK and setting him up for the Creeping Death, but LKK pushes away and hits a Dropkick to momentarily stop the AoD member. LKK tags in Doughboy again, who immediately comes in and hits the Blender Tornado DDT on … ummm … DDT. Dough whips DDT to a neutral corner and proceeds to Knead the Dough, with mounted punches in the corner. With DDT tired and dizzy, Dough tags in HBK, and then goes back to ship DDT into a hard Clothesline from the seasoned competitor. Mikey: HBK has been in fantastic shape since he returned to XUW, and is back to full strength now after a gruelling Hell in a Cell match with M V Pete. HBK goes up top and nails the Balance Point (Moonsault) perfectly onto the exposed midsection of the AoD member. HBK picks him up again and looks for an Irish Whip, but DDT counters it into a Clothesline, and tags in Fae quickly. Fae runs at HBK, but the ‘Showstopper’ ducks away and grabs him into a Neckbreaker. Fae gets up quickly, but HBK is on top of his game and hits a Side Suplex. He goes for a pin. 1… 2… 2.5….. BROKEN UP BY RAVEN X! RX starts to stamp on HBK, so Doughboy and LKK run into the ring and stop him. This brings DDT into the ring, and the two tag teams starts to brawl. RX kicks LKK in the gut and nails him with the Family Values (Pedigree)! Meanwhile Doughboy grabs DDT and from nowhere hits a huge Cookie Cutter! Mikey: It’s kicking off now! HBK gets up, as does the Fallen Fae. Fae looks to grab HBK and hit the Faerie Spike (Double Underhook Piledriver), but HBK hits a Back Body drop to send the Fae to the floor. At the same time, RX and Doughboy lock up, and fall through the middle ropes to the outside. Fae gets up slowly … as HBK starts to stamp his foot down.
