XUW Mayhem Welcome to Mayhem! Tonight we have a major event, with a special longer edition. (Whew…this is gonna be a long one boys!) AT: Hi all, At here as always with my cohost, Mr. Mikey…erm…Mr. Mikey, what is that god awful smell? MM: Well, someone decided it would be funny to set a stink bomb off in D’s office. You were there, remember? Was it you! AT: No, it wasn’t me; relax. Right now we have an opening match that will blow your socks off! MM: That would stink even worse! AT: Anyway, here we go with our first match! Raven X’s music hits and he walks to the ring to a chorus of applause. Sidhe walks to many boos, but seems very happy about it. It is a GOOD thing! Raven starts the match off by whipping Sidhe into the corner and following him in with a lariat. He picks Sidhe up and puts him on the top rope. Raven climbs up and sets up for a headscissors, but Sidhe counters and knocks Raven down. X gets back up and goes to spear a still kind of groggy Sidhe in the corner, but Sidhe moves and X hits the pole shoulder first. Sidhe rolls out of the ring and begins to wail on X’s arm, swinging it into the steel ring post. He pulls X out and picks him up and hits a shoulder breaker. X is rolling around in pain, and he can’t use his right arm. Sidhe rips the top off the Spanish announce table and picks X up. He sets X up for a power bomb through the table, but X reverses it with a back body drop. X turns and kicks sidhe in the head. He picks Sidhe up and begins to hit him with a barrage of lefts to the head. He sets sidhe up for a suplex through the Spanish announce table, but when he picks him up his right arm gives out and Sidhe falls down on top of X. Shooter is in the ring counting, and by now is up to 7. Sidhe stumbles to his feet and he throws X into the ring. Sidhe climbs to the top rope and goes for a cross body, but X does a standing drop kick to Sidhe in the gut and Sidhe is winded and almost unconscious. Bailey walks onto the ramp, but he us jumped by Hodgo and thrown from the stage. Hodgo charges to the ring, only to be stopped by, D26? D lays Hodgo out and an angry and confused Sidhe looks out to see what is happening. Meanwhile X gets up and he rolls up Sidhe for the 1…2…3! Winner: Raven X D walks to the ring, mic in hand, sunglasses as per usual. D: Did you really think you were gonna win via interference? Not today. Now, I’m here because I have a match with my partner John against Sue Wolfe and HBK. HBK, bring your stink bomb layin ass out here so I can kick it all over my ring! HBK and Sue Wolfe charge to the ring and begin to beat down D. Admiral John and Mikey charge to ringside as the bell rings and we begin. HBK starts with the upper hand and he whips D into the ropes and hits a back body drop. D is laid out from the beating he has sustained already. HBK stands on D’s knees and grabs his arms and turns over into a surfboard. D screams in pain but won’t give up. HBK releases the hold and tags in Sue. Sue charges in and begins to stomp away on D. She sends D into the corner and goes for a spring flip elbow. She smashes D in the jaw and sends him down. Sue picks D up and goes for a side suplex, but D counters into a Russian leg sweep. D gets up slowly and tries to tag in John. Sue grabs D’s foot and won’t let him tag out. D goes for an inseguri (sp?), but Sue raises her leg and hits D with a low blow. She laughs as she walks to the corner and tags in HBK. HBK walks in, as John tries to help his partner. Shooter detains john as Sue and HBK choke D in their corner. Mikey gets up on the ring apron and he runs at Sue, scaring her so she falls off the apron. Mikey laughs and gets out of the ring, as D gets a burst of energy and dives for his corner and makes the hot tag to a fresh John. John comes in and cleans house. He hits HBK and Sue, then he does a few standing drop kicks. He whips HBK into the ropes, and HBK goes for a lariat, but John ducks and he hits sue. D regains himself a bit and he climbs to the top rope. John picks up HBK and hits the Anchor’s away on him, then D dives off and hits his devil drop. John goes for the pin…1…2…NO! Sue breaks up the pinfall and D and John are pist. HBK is still unconscious, as John and D double team Sue. Sue goes for another low blow, but instead she gets kicked in the head, and John hits the Anchor’s away on her. John throws her out of the ring, and he looks at HBK. He picks HBK up and he puts him in the corner. D throws Sue into the stairs on the outside, and he climbs back in to help out John. HBK is limp in the corner and John climbs to the top rope. D grabs HBK and picks him up for a super power Bomb, but D keeps hold of HBK’s neck. They hit a super power bomb/neck breaker on HBK and D goes for the pin…1….2…3! Winner: John and D AT: Oh My god! What a set of matches to start off the show! MM: I guess D was upset at the lack of wrestling on the last mayhem so he added more matches! We cut backstage where Naron and Bob walk in to a huge pop. Bob: Naron, honey, can you go to the dressing room? Naron: Bob, why, I want to be with you. Bob: I know, but I have some business before my match, just please? Naron: Okay, but be quick! Bob: I just gotta talk to D about my match tonight. Bob walks into a dressing room, but it doesn’t say Diablos26, it says “Woof Woof.” You can’t see anything, just see the door closed. Bob: You think you’re a big man, taking Naron like that! (You hear a series of loud cracks and you see Bob walk out with a broken Kendo stick. He sees the camera man and looks pist as he throws down the stick.) I think you are gonna need a medic in there….GO! (the camera man drops the camera and all you can see is the puddle of urine where he was standing) AT: Wow, what has gotten into Bob? MM: Well, lemmie think here, oh yea, NARON’S PREGNANT MORON! AT: Well, that was rude. Well, there is plenty more action tonight. Our next match involves Two tag teams, but this is not a tag match, it is a 4-way! MM: When these guys get in the ring, it is ALWAYS  a four way… AT: Wait, I am getting a message over the headset. I’m told…we need to cut to the back. We go into Blitzwing’s office, and he is sitting with a puzzled look on his face, talking to Blake. Blitz: What do you mean you broke up! Blake: He wanted to go into other directons… Blitz: I’ll give him another direction. He is in the right now you say? Blake: Yea, what are you gonna do? Blitz: I got a match for him… We go to the ring where Jobbie is in the ring waiting…When KoRe’s music hits and he runs to the ring. KoRe hits a quick lariat, then another, then another. Jobbie gets up stunned and he turns and KoRe DDT’s him and goes for a pin..1…2…3! Winner: KoRe KoRe calls to the back and Jason Michaels runs out with a chair and he and KoRe beat Jobbie down. Jobber is beat to a pulp and as KoRe and Jason walk to the back Jobber is taken back on a stretcher… AT: Whoa…what was that? MM: Funny… AT: Well, yea…Our next match will be RTT v. Takerrocks on a hardcore match! Both men are in the ring as we begin RTT starts out with a stop sign and he beats TR with it. Suddenly CK and Jack sit on the stage in folding chairs watching the match. RTT whips TR into the corner. He dives at TR, but he moves, and RTT eats pole. TR gets out of the ring and begins to wrap RTT’s arm around the pole. He grabs a chair and goes to hit RTT’s arm and the pole. RTT gets out and begins to fight TR up the ramp, spilling into CK and Jack. CK and Jack get up and they smash TR with their chairs, knocking him to the floor. TR is out cold, when RTT does a huge moonsault from the stage onto TR for the pin…1…2..3! Winner: RTT AT: Damn! That looked awesome! MM: Where his this guy been? AT: Hell if I know…Our next match is: OUR WORLD TITLE MATCH! Lance walks to the ring to massive amounts of boos. KoRe walks out to small laughs… As KoRe dives into the ring, Lance jumps him. He begins to stomp KoRe. KoRe gets up and goes to kick Lance in the stomach. Lance grabs KoRe’s leg and rolls him into the straight shooter. KoRe desperately calls for help…but can’t hold out and he taps, and the match is over quickly… Winner: LanceStormDBC Jason runs to the ring again, a little late, as Lance dives out. KoRe gets up annoyed and pushes Jason and they argue their way back to the locker room. AT: Well…quick… MM: Certainly…it was…erm…quick… AT: Moving right along…Our next match will be between Severson and Lurchador for the Hermit title. Sev walks out to a chorus of boos. Lurch walks out and you hear someone in the 100023rd row say…”He’s still alive?” Lurch gets on the top rope right away and dives into big Sev...who catches him! Sev does a fallaway slam on Lurch and turns into a pin…1..2..NO! Lurch kicks out and Sev begins to stomp on him. Sev and Lurch begin to just duke it out, but obviously Lurch can’t out hit big Sev. Sev gets the better of Lurch and sends him into the ropes. He does a back body drop. Sev follows it up by doing an elbow drop then picking Lurch up and guerilla press slamming him. Sev sets up Lurch and goes for a huge powerbomb…and hits it! 1…2…3! Winner and NEW hermit champion: Severson! Sev gets a mic and begins to talk: Sev: This title has meant the world to me, and it is only fitting that being the only REAL hermit in XUW, I retire it, as of now. I am officially the first, last, and best Hermit champion ever! AT: Oh god…he doesn’t wanna defend it anymore! MM: I think that was noble… AT: Pompus jackass… MM: Yea…and proud of it! AT: Out next match is a return match of sorts between Chase and Kata for the submission title! A quick video recap of the 4-way for the sub title is shown where Kata wins the title, and Chase taps out. Chase walks out to massive cheers, and Kata runs to the ring to get the match underway Chase starts out by hitting Kata with a few quick punches. He stomps on Kata and he hits a body slam. He does a hip toss, and another, and another. Kata stops and rolls out to regain himself. Kata dives back in and he and chase lock up. Kata locks on a hammer lock, and Chase turns it into a chicken wing, which Kata reverses into a side suplex. Chase gets up and regains himself. Kata and Chase lock up one more time. This time Kata puts kicks Chase in the stomach and he DDT’s him. Chase is laid out and kata puts on an arm bar. Chase gets up and picks Kata up while still in the arm bar and he slams him to the mat. Kata is stunned and Chase takes advantage by locking on a figure 4. Kata rolls and rolls and after about 2 minutes turns it over and Chase is forced to let the move go. Kata gets up and dives and does an overhand smash to Chase. As chase is laid out on his back, Kata locks on a surfboard. Chase screams in pain, but refuses to give up. Kata releases the hold, but is clearly frustrated, as he gets in Shooter’s face. Chase sneaks up behind Kata and hits a reverse suplex on kata. Chase locks on the rings of Saturn…and Kata screams in pain. Kata is writing but he refuses to give up. Finally, after about 4 minutes of Chase rearing back, Kata passes out. Shooter calls for the bell… Winner: ???? Shooter calls to the back and the Hungry Mexican, the senior official walks to the ring. Mexican tells Shooter something, and shooter tells the ring announcer. The ring announcer stands up and says: Announcer: The winner and NEW submission champion: Chase Wilson! AT:…? MM: What a screw job! AT: Well, Chase did kinda win… MM: But Kata didn’t give! AT: Well, next we have BC v. Jack v. Tony They all get in the ring and go to work. BC starts things out by hitting Jack with a suplex. He gets up but gets leveled by Tony with a lariat. Tony begins to stomp on BC, and Jack gets up and they both begin to put the boots to BC. They both beat BC down, and pick him up. Tony picks BC up for a powerbomb, and Jack grabs BC’s neck and they do a neckbreaker/powerbomb on BC, who falls out of the ring. Tony and Jack immediately get up and begin to duke it out. Jack hits tony with a few rights, then he throws him into the corner. Jack grabs Tony’s leg and does a quick leg whip. Tony is laid out as KsC, Big Daddy, and Ck all run to the ring and an all out brawl ensues… Winnner: No contest All the men are fighting when “Bodies” hits and D walks to the ring. D: HEY! STOP RIGHT NOW! All the men stop and stare at the stage, to a seeming pist off Diablos… D: Now! Since you all want each other so damn bad, fine then. Next week, a three way for the title. Whoever gets a pin first is the champions! Now….get out of my ring! And Horsemen…I can’t help you on this one…but I know you can win…so prove it to me! AT: Wow, what a match next week for mayhem! MM: Well, since D is getting a stiff wrist, lets wrap this show up already! Lance walks to the ring, belt still in tact, with a ref shirt on. Bob walks out with Naron to a huge pop. Lopen walks out to the biggest heat of the night. Lopen starts out by hitting Bob with a few quick lefts, then a right. Lopen lays Bob out with a lariat. He picks Bob up and grabs his hand. He hits a short arm clothesline, but holds on. He picks Bob up and hits another, and another, and another. Bob is completely laid out and Lopen runs to the ropes and goes for a leg drop, but misses. Lopen is sitting down, and Bob does a drop kick to Lopen’s head. Lopen drops like a fly, and Bob goes for the skid mark chicken wing, but Lopen doesn’t allow it and gets to the ropes. Bob grabs Lopen and brings him back to the middle and picks him up by his leg. Lopen hits an inseguri (sp?) and Bob is laid out again. Lopen grabs Bob’s leg and locks on a boston crab. Bob screams in pain, but his adrenaline gets pumping and he powers out of it by flipping Lopen. Bob is still in massive amounts of pain, when lopen picks him up and goes for the Lopen-cutter. Naron gets on the ring mat and distracts Lopen. Lopen walks over to Naron and grabs her hair and Lance goes over to try to stop it. Bob looks up and sees Lance and Lopen by naron and he turns completely red. He dives out of the ring and grabs the ring bell. He dives at Lance and lays him out. When Lopen turns, he gets a face full of bell too. Bob loses it and begins to beat both men mercilessly with the ring bell. Winner: No contest… Bob continues to beat a now bloody lopen and lance until naron gets in the ring and urges Bob to stop. Bob looks at her, and drops the bell and walks away. AT: Wow! It looks like Bob has lost it! MM: Did he ever have it? AT: Well…I guess not… Lopen gets up and begins to walk away, but Lance lays there motionless. Blitz and D run to the ring and check on Lance, and they call for a paramedic, as we fade to black.
