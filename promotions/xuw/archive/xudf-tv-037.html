As the camera fades in, a small montage of tonight's events flash by.. A shot of Blake Savage winning the North American Championship back from last week's show, Joey Vendetti, Blade and his entire B&J Security squad is shown, Dracul flying by, Longhorn preparing, Bubba Bailey, Robert Cutter, Flip the Man as the camera switches to real time as the old "Saturday Night Main Event" theme blares over the P.A. System as the camera shows a small arena full of people with signs, of course.. "Flip THIS Man!" "Rope Me, Longhorn!" "Wanna be a Thug Superstah like V!" "Big Daddy AINT so Big!" "I'm 101% Sexual Charisma!" Pyro's are going off at the ramp and in the ring and even above the ring as the camera pans down to Bice and Shooter sitting at their accustomed announce table.. Bice: ..and then Tommy Thompson fell into the puddle! Shooter: Do I seem like I care? Why don't you ever tell any funny stories? Oh I know! Because you're old! Both Bice and Shooter hear a "Ahem" sound and look over at the camera with a surprise look on their faces. They fix their paperwork on the table and speak.. Bice: Good evening XUDF fans and welcome to another exciting edition of XUDF TV! Shooter: That's right Bice, tonight we get to hear from the North American Champion, Blake Savage! Who in my mind, never lost the title at all. Bice: You shouldn't overlook Overkill. He defeated Blake Savage and.. Shooter: ..then he was defeated by Savage! Savage has been saying for weeks how it was all apart of his plan, you dare doubt 100% Sexual Charisma!? All of a sudden, 'Hey Man, Nice Shot' by Filter blares through the system as some of the ladies stand and melt while most of the others just boo the hell out of the song. "Superstar" Blake Savage makes his way to the ring, strutting his stuff and showing off the North American Title. Savage grabs a mic.. Blake: You know, I told you morons that it was all apart of the plan. I give Overkill a little edge, let him think he's not Over-rated and then put him in his place! I told all of you that I am 100% Sexual Charisma! Nobody can beat me! Shooter: You tell them Sexual Charisma! Blake: So I defeated Overkill no problem. Now what? Do I face Urine Danger!? Or how about Big Daddy!? There is nobody here who can stop me, I've beaten them all! With that said, "Slow Chemical" by Finger Eleven blares as the lights cut out. Smoke fills the entrance ramp as a green light comes on and a figure is in front of it. It's Dracul! Dracul: You haven't beaten me, Savage. You want to talk about how you walked over Overkill. I think you're mistaken. Enough of that. I am now your number 1 Contender opponent, Blake Savage. After I destroy Blade and Vendetti, your blood is next! Blake: Oh I'm sorry, were you saying something? 'Stupify' Live Version by Disturbed all of a sudden comes over the P.A. System as Overkill makes his way out! As Dracul stands mid-way to the ring, Overkill stands at the entrance looking at Blake and Dracul. He has a mic in his hand as he speaks.. Overkill: You know Dracul, I gave you the number 1 Contendorship when I was champion and now that I have.. lost my title back to Savage, I want my rematch! Blake: Um, hello? Losers, up here! I am the North American Champion and I say I will face... Wait for it.. Neither of you! Blake laughs as all of a sudden, Mr. Webster comes on the Ultitron!! All three of them look over at the Ultitron as Mr. Webster speaks! Mr. Webster: You know it seems we have an interesting situation here. So I'm going to make a decision right here, right now as to who will be facing Blake Savage next and this will happen at the June 7th show! Blake Savages opponent for the North American title will be none other than... Overkill!! Blake: Oh that's fine, I'll just beat his ass again.. Overkill: We'll see come June 7th! Mr. Webster: Well I am glad you two are happy about your rematch and I am sure it will be a great one. Oh and one more thing.. Must of slipped my mind.. Savage, you will not only be facing Overkill but you will also be facing Dracul! That's right! A Three Way Dance for the North American Championship on June 7th! Be ready boys and that is ALL! Shooter: Holy CRAP! Savage will not only face Overkill but he will also face Dracul! I don't know if Sexual Charisma can handle that!! Bice: It was only a matter of time before Dracul got himself another title shot. His last one was in September for the U.S. Title and I am sure he would like to make things different this time around! Overkill: See you boys on June 7th! Dracul looks at Overkill and smiles as green energy eminates from his eyes. Dracul then looks at Savage, who is not thrilled at all and smiles and laughs as he leaves. Savage, speechless for once, leaves the ring as we go to Bice and Shooter.. Bice: Well it looks like we have ourselves a three way! Wait, good 'ol Tommy Thompson has caught up with Flip the Man backstage! TT: Flip! I'd like to be the first to welcome you back in to XUDF! What is your first order of business here since you have officially returned?? Flip: Well Tommy.. I'm going to make this short and hella sweet. It's this simple. There's this guy who's supposed to be a wrestler. His name, maybe you've heard of him.. Joey Vendetti.. Well, he's going to be taking on the Longhorn on the June 7th XUDF Show! Now here's the good part.. I will be the special guest referee for that event! I think that's enough said and now if you'll excuse me, I have some friends I want to say hello to.. Bice: OH MY! Tonight is just full of surprises! THE MAN returns and will be the special gues refere between Longhorn and Joey! Well that's not all that'll happen here tonight as the show rolls on. As a matter of fact, lets head to the ring, it's time for our first bout! Shooter: Wake me up when it's done, will ya? Bice: Oh dear lord.. As the lights dim down, 'Break Stuff' by Limp Bizkit blares over the P.A. system as one Fezz "X" Ray makes his way down to the ring playing to the fans. Fezz soaks up the crowd as he enters the ring, still apluading them as the P.A. He makes his way out as the fans throw things at him as he steps into the ring, looks over at Fezz "X" Ray and laughs. The referee looks at both men and then signals for the bell as the match begins.. ROBERT CUTTER vs. FEZZ "X" RAY Bice: Look at these two men, very dedicated, very determined, only one shall be the victor! Shooter: Oh will you shut up! Bice: He's going to hit Cutter with a Flying Spear! Oh my god! Shooter: What the hell are you, J.R. now? Doc's going to avoid this o.. Cutter kicks out with authority at the last second! [B]Shooter: HAHA! See, Fezz is nothing to the good Doc! As Robert Cutter get's up, Fezz bounces off the ropes and leaps towards Robert Cutter. Cutter makes the cover! ONE!!................ TWO!!!......................... CUTTER PULLS FEZZ UP! The referee isn't too happy about that as he warns Cutter not to do that again. Bice: Robert Cutter is having his way with poor Fezz "X" Ray! Shooter: Calm down there Bice, you're not trying to freestyle are you? Bice: What is "freestyle"? Joey Vendetti/Blade w/ B&J Security vs. The Longhorn and Dracul As the lights fade out, 'Square Dance' by Eminem begins to blare over the speakers. The fans boo the hell out of this song as Joey Vendetti, Blade and a ton of B&J Security guards make their way to the ring. He salutes all of the people that love him and tips his hat as he stands in front of the ring starring at Joey Vendetti.. 'Fever' quiets down as 'Slow Chemical' by Finger Eleven blares! The lights turn off as a green strobe circulates throughout the arena. The fans are still on their feet as Dracul makes his way to the ring! He looks at the fans and smiles to reveal his fangs as he stands next to Longhorn. The two look at each other and they both rush the ring! DING! DING! DING!
