XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #37

3 Oct 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex "Growl, growl, grrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr" scremas the lead singer from the band that plays the indecipherable Vortex theme, and we're underway once more! Pyro does nice, green fizzy things and makes the crowd pop like balloons with pins in 'em. We see the signs that the faithful XUW fans have brought to the arena with them. DEADLY COMBINATION RIDE AGAIN! X FACTOR STOLE MY OTHER SIGN HEINZ FEARS J.R. The camera cuts to the announce desk where, as always, The Atomic Tomato is joined by 'The Majestic' Barry Johnson. AT: "Hello one and all and welcome to the last edition of XUW Vortex before the Pay Per View extravaganza that is XUW Unknown Factors." BJ: "We've got some action in store for you here tonight, with The Deadly Combination reuniting for a tag match against two of the Boomers Sooner, Case Wilson and Saij." AT: "Dev and Kore will aim to finish their troubles tonight too, not to mention Henrik trying to continue his 'Reign of Pain' against Michael Bolling." BJ: "First off though, here's Fatbadger versus Danyon, the International Superstar from New Zealand!" "Mudshovel" hits the speakers, and the crowd pop for the Boomer Sooners member, Fatbadger! He comes down to the ring and has a quick headbang as he awaits his opponent. "Hello Time Bomb" plays, and Danyon comes down to the ring on his own, other than for the hoards of XUW fans screaming abuse at him. he scans the crowd and spots a lone "I AM A LEGIONNAIRE!" sign, at which point he smiles. Danyon enters the ring and the match is underway. Singles Match: Fatbadger v Danyon The two men lock up, the strength advantage allowing Danyon to push Fatbadger back to the ropes. The ref calls for a clean break, but Danyon unloads a hard Knife Edge Chop to the chest of Fatty. The Kiwi wrestler whips Fatty to the ropes and hits a Clothesline to knock Fatty down, and then a Spinning Sidewalk Slam after picking him up once more. Danyon goes for the early pin. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Fatbadger crawls to the ropes, and then hits a Thrust Kick to Danyon’s chest, following up with a German Suplex, and then another German Suplex, which bridges into a pin. 1 … SHOULDER UP! AT: “Both men starting off with some good offence here.” The men start to exchange blows, and then Danyon hits a Rising Knee to the gut of Fatbadger. Danyon grabs Fatty and hits a hard Kiwi Plex (Face First Suplex). He then picks up the stricken Sooner and hits an Atomic Drop. AT: “Fatbadger’s beginning to look in trouble now, as Danyon is mounting a dominating offence.” BJ: “Of course he is! He’s the best!” Danyon continues his dominance by hitting a DDT perfectly, and then picking up the beleaguered Fatbadger, whipping him to the ropes and hitting a Dropkick. Danyon goes for the pin. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! AT: “Fatbadger must be fuelled on Funyons to still be going!” Danyon sets Fatty up for the Southern Cradle Piledriver, but gets a Back Body Drop for his troubles. Fatty whips Danyon into a corner, and then raises him up to the top rope. Fatbadger sets Danyon up for a Superplex, and hits it! Fatty gets to his feet and climbs between the ropes. He then jumps to the middle of the top rope at comes flying off into a MASSIVE Leg Drop aimed at Danyon. BJ: “No! Not the Fatty Boom Batty!” No indeed, for Danyon rolls to the side just in time, and Fatbadger hits the mat hard. Danyon recovers quickly and this time connects with his Southern Cradle Piledriver. 1 … 2 … 3!!! Winner, by pinfall, Danyon!!! Danyon celebrates, and Bailey comes running from the back to start stomping on Fatbadger as he lies on the mat. Danyon joins in, and between them they lift Fatbadger up and hit their tag team finisher, The Hemispheric Connection (Spike Cradle Piledriver). Fatty is down, and down hard. AT: “Wait! Here’s Dave Kipfer!” Kipfer hits the ring at pace and unloads hard right and left hands at the New Royal Legion. He hits Danyon over the top rope with a Clothesline, and then does the same for Bailey. He stands in the ring with a defiant look on his face, as he tends to his fallen comrade. AT: "Despite the bad blood at the end there, I have to hand it to Danyon, he's looking impressive here leading in to the big Tag Team Title match on Unknown Factors." BJ: "Of course he is! He's the XUW Superstar from New Zealand! I predict that on Sunday night the New Royal Legion will get the gold that they deserve!" AT: "Speaking of gold, we'll now see if Henrik the King can keep his 'Reign of pain' going by defending his TV Title belt against Michael Bolling, 'The Destiny'!" "Date With Destiny" is played, and Bolling comes down to the ring slowly, to a smattering of heat. "Lounge Act" plays, and the crowd grow noisier for the self-proclaimed King of XUW, Henrik! He is accompanied to the ring by his gorgeous young valet, Bobbi. XUW TV Title Match: Michael Bolling v Henrik The King © Henrik starts by whipping Bolling to the ropes and then hitting a combination of left and right handed punches, softening Bolling up for a hard Snap Suplex. Almost as soon as Bolling hits the ground, Henrik is up again and dropping knees across his chest. Bolling rolls away to the ropes and then hits a Drop Toe Hold as Henrik comes in on him. Bolling hits a Piledriver on Henrik, and then goes up top, flying off with a Moonsault! AT: “Great air there by ‘The Destiny!’” BJ: “Air Destiny!” Bolling lands square on Henrik, and goes for a cover. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Henrik gets up slowly, and Bolling goes for a Clothesline, but Henrik swats it away. The King of XUW waits until Bolling gets up, and then locks in the Stratford Strangle (Million Dollar Dream). AT: “Bolling seems to be weakening here, and Henrik is forcing him down to the ground.” BJ: “The reign of pain continues! Bolling’s out for sure!” Bolling drops to his knees, and then to the ground, where he finally manages to get a foot to the ropes and cause the referee to break the hold. Henrik pays the ref no heed and merely pulls the weakening Bolling back towards the middle of the ring. The challenger once more struggles towards the ropes, and slowly, slowly, gets a foot locked there again. This time the ref succeeds in breaking the hold, and Henrik just dumps Bolling on to the canvas. Henrik parades around the ring, and looks over to his pretty young valet, Bobbi, with a sadistic smile. Henrik picks up Bolling and hits a Short Arm Clothesline, and then a Dropkick, but Bolling is merely staggered until Henrik runs off the far ropes and comes back with a hard Flying Knee to knock the challenger back to the mat. Henrik reaches down and grabs the legs of Bolling, twisting him into the Four Leaf Clover (Texas Cloverleaf). Bolling yells out in pain, and this makes Henrik pull back harder. AT: “The only three time XUW Submission Champion has got his favourite submission move locked in. It’s all over for Bolling unless he can get to those ropes, and fast!” Bolling pushes up with his hands, and moves across, reaching as far as he can for the ropes, and finally grabbing one. Henrik drops the hold and undoes the padding on one of the turnbuckles. The ref tries to stop him, but as all good refs never really do anything, he lets it slide. Henrik picks up Bolling and smacks his head against the exposed steel, and Bolling goes down, bleeding from a busted open forehead. Henrik goes to take advantage, but Bolling makes a last ditch Superkick connect, and both men are down! The ref makes the count. 1 … 2 … 3 … 4 … Bolling gets to one knee. 5 … 6 … Bolling stands up and lifts up Henrik, but Henrik hits the Crown Jewels (Low Blow)! Getting to his feet, Henrik goes up top and hits the London Calling (Flying Bulldog). Henrik goes to regain his breath in the corner, and the ref goes to check on the cut to Bolling’s forehead. AT: “Look out! Here comes Hodgo!” BJ: “He’s got some brass knucks!” Hodgo runs through the crowd, one member of which pokes him RIGHT IN THE EYE, but he shrugs it off and enters the ring. Henrik turns to get a punch from the bass knucks, and then Hodgo hits the Riot Breaker (Hangman’s DDT)! Hodgo slides out of the ring and looks on as both the ref and Bolling turn to see Henrik fallen. Bolling makes the cover. 1 … 2 … 3!!!!! Winner, by pinfall, and NEW XUW TV Champion: Michael Bolling!!!!! Hodgo laughs with glee and makes his way to the back. BJ: "No way! Henrik got screwed, big time!" AT: "Good grief, what will this mean for the Pay per View? Henrik and Hodgo will be wanting to tear each other to pieces in a No-DQ match. I can't help but fear for the safety of both men!" BJ: "Fear for Hodgo, as Henrik is going to tear him limb from limb after having his title taken from him!" AT: "Well, there goes Henrik, off to the back in search of Hodgo and revenge. Will he have to wait until Sunday night or will he find him before then? Only one place to find that out, and that's right here!" BJ: "Yeah, they don't have action like this on Thursday Night Football!" AT: "That's because they don't have Thursday Night Football." BJ: "Shhhh, you're ruining it!" Bolling leaves with the belt and a smile on his face, the new XUW TV Champion. AT: "I've just heard that the Three Way match between Rujasu, Destroyer and Michael Bolling at Unknown Factors will indeed be for the XUW TV Title!" BJ: "That might spur those young competitors into some more action!" AT: "Speaking of young competitors in action, next up is Dev and Kore, and these two go way back!" Soilent Green's "Build Fear" plays over the speakers, and Dev comes out to a not bad amount of heat. AT: "The fans here don't really like Dev, but they do respect his will to fight." "Jackass" hits, and Kore comes down the ramp to a crowd pop, sliding into the ring and getting ready to fight. Singles Match: Dev v Kore Dev starts off by backing Kore up with a Flurry of Kicks, but Kore manages to dodge them all and starts to unload some punches on the Fighter. Kore ducks another kick aimed at his head, and then responds with a hard Clothesline to take Dev down to the mat. Kore jumps atop Dev and starts to unload more and more punches to the face. Dev finally manages to get Kore off, and rolls away to the ropes, holding his jaw. Both men get up and square off again. AT: “These men have fought several times in the last few weeks, and there’s still a lot of bad blood between them. Both men think that they can beat the other in a straight fight.” BJ: “This doesn’t seem like much of a fight beyond the brawling though. No technique, just all out aggression.” They lock up and Dev whips Kore to the ropes. Kore ducks under a Clothesline and hits the far ropes, coming back hard with a hard Lariat that knocks Dev down to the canvas, lying on his front. Kore goes up top and hits the Peaked Point (Frog Splash) hard on to the back of Dev. The Fighter rolls out of the ring, clutching at his back. Kore follows him to the ropes, but Dev jumps up to the apron and then drops back down with Kore’s head in his hands. Kore’s neck gets smacked into the top rope, flicking him back into the ring, hard. Dev gets back into the ring and unleashes a string of kicks to the sides of Kore as he fights back to his feet. Dev hits a hard elbow smash to the back of the head, and then drills Kore with a DDT. Kore gets back up and Dev hits a series of Knife Edge Chops, backing Kore up into a corner. Kore blocks another chop, as his chest starts to well up, red and raw. Kore grabs Dev and they switch positions, Kore getting his own back with some hard Knife Edge Chops of his own. He eventually stops, and throws Dev across the ring to land hard on his back. Kore advances again, but Dev springs to his feet and throws a Clothesline of his own to take down the High Roller. Kore gets up quickly, but a Spinning Heel Kick takes him down again, and Dev goes for the first cover of the match. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! AT: “It’s getting brutal and vicious in there, of that there can be no doubt!” Both men get back to their feet, and Kore regains the momentum with a stiff Forearm to the jaw of Dev. Dev staggers back, and Kore hits another Clothesline, which just staggers Dev. The Fighter bounces off the ropes, and Kore unloads with some more punches, before turning Dev round and choking him on the top rope. The ref intervenes, but Kore is right back on the offensive, hitting the Royal Flush Flapjack and hitting with yet more punches. Dev rolls to the outside and shakes his head, looking back into the ring. The ref starts to count. 1 … 2 … Dev makes his way to the announce desk, and picks up a microphone. 3 … 4 … 5 … Dev walks round the ring to the entranceway and looks back at the ring, raising the microphone to his lips. 6 … 7 … He motions as if to talk, but just walks backwards up the ramp, the microphone at his side. 8 … 9 … 10!!! Winner, by countout: Kore!!! Dev continues to walk backwards up the ramp, and holds the microphone close to his mouth. Dev: “I may be leaving now Kore, but I can promise you one thing. Dev, The Fighter, will be at XUW Unknown Factors, and I will be there to do one thing. I’ll be there to make an impact.” Dev drops the microphone and goes to the back. After a short wait, Kore follows him, as the crowd cheer his victory. AT: “A strange ending there, but Dev seems intent on proving something at the Pay Per View this weekend.” BJ: “I wonder what capacity we will see him in?” AT: “Very probably in a fighting one, I would say.” BJ: “Speaking of fighting, here’s a really bad link to the next match, where they will also be fighting. It’s the Sooners and the Suspects again!” AT: "I've been looking forwards to this one all week, the return of The Deadly Combination!" “Right Here, Right Now” plays, and the crowd lets out a monster pop for the return of The Deadly Combination! Nu and Pete walk down to the ring, Numark looking the happier of the two, but both clearly ready for the match. “BOOM” comes over the speakers, and the crowd pop once more for the team known as the Boomer Sooners. Not the tag team itself, but two members of the stable, those being Saij and Chase Wilson. They are all smiles as they slap hands on the way down to the ring, and hand out a few free bottles of J.R’s BBQ Sauce! Tag Team Match: The Deadly Combination v Saij and Chase Wilson Numark and Saij start in the ring together, just as they will on Sunday night at the PPV. They start off by locking up, and push each other back and forth a little, until Saij uses his weight to his advantage and pushes forwards hard. Numark drops to his back and uses his legs to throw Saij over his shoulder in an impressive judo fashion. Saij gets up and inclines his head towards the Intercontinental Champion. They lock up again, and this time Saij spins behind Numark and hits a Neckbreaker. Numark quickly gets to his feet, and inclines his head towards the challenger for his belt. AT: “It’s great to see such respect between two top class athletes.” BJ: “No it isn’t! Hit him with a sledgehammer, Pete!” Numark grabs Saij and whips him to the ropes. He tags in Pete and hit a Double Clothesline to take Saij down. The Sooner gets to his feet, and Nu and Pete go for the Combination kick (Double Superkick), but Pete hits his a little before Numark does, and it isn’t quite as effective. Pete, now the legal man, picks us Saij and rakes his eyes, then going on to rub Saij’s face hard along the ropes. He hits a Suplex and turns to tag in the waiting Numark. Instead, he turns back to Saij and hits another Suplex. Pete tries to lock in the Junk Yard Stretch, but Saij battles to the ropes, and then kicks Pete away from him. Pete comes back, but Saij manages to tag in Chase Wilson. Chase enters the ring and clubs Pete down with a huge right arm. AT: “Chase is about the same size as The Ripper, so Pete had better get used to fighting the big men, and quickly!” Chase hits a Side Russian Leg Sweep, and then goes for the Short Term Memory loss (Jumping Superkick), but Pete ducks and tags Numark back in. Nu enters the ring and charges Chase, hitting a Spear on the big man, and then grabbing his ankle and applying his Ankle Lock. Chase yells, but uses his size and strength to make it to the ropes before he is forced to tap out. He holds his ankle, and tries to get back up. Nu helps him up, but then helps him back down via the Spin The Bottle (Eye of the Hurricane). Nu leans back to tag in M V Pete, but their arms miss the first time, and then look slightly surprised as they finally make the tag. AT: “They’ve missed a few steps now. Has Pete’s new, meaner image taken something away from their superb tag team form?” Pete moves in on Chase, but Chase hits a blow to the ribs, and then nails Pete with a desperation DDT. Chase tags in Saij, who comes in like a steam train and flattens Pete.  Saij hits the Ker-Splat (Rolling Thunder) and then hits the Funny Bone Elbow Drop from the Top Rope! Saij covers. 1 … 2 … NO! Numark breaks up the pin. Saij and Numark look each other in the eyes, and then Pete hits a Low Blow on Saij, from behind him. Pete looks for a roll-up. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Saij kicks out, but Pete grabs him and hits a Prime Time Chokeslam. AT: “What’s he doing here?” BJ: “He said he would be here, and he’s being true to his word!” The man of whom they speak, Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy, is walking down to the ring, lead pipe in hand. As he gets close to the ring, another man jumps the guard rail and knock him to the floor, then starts hammering away with solid shots to the head. AT: “It’s Samurai Bob! Bob’s defending the other Usual Suspects!” BJ: “Who’s this now though?” AT: “That’s Bailey and Danyon, and they’re going for Chase Wilson!” The New Royal Legion slide into the ring, and Bailey hits the Spinning DDT on Chase. Danyon starts booting him too, until Saij pulls him off and hits a Standing Dropkick. Outside, M V Pete has joined in the carnage as Jack and Bob brawl in the aisle. Bailey goes to hit the Spinning DDT on Saij, but Numark saves him, and then hit a Double Suplex on Bailey. Danyon gets back up and flattens Saij and Numark together with a huge Double Arm Clothesline. Inside the ring, as outside, it degenerates into pure brawling. BJ: "It's just turned into a free-for-all! I love it!" AT: "This is crazy!" The bell rings, as the ref has lost control, and people are just randomly brawling. Result: No Contest BJ: "Here come to officials to break it all up. Damn, I like a good brawl." AT: "Well, that's all we have time for folks, I hope to be seeing you this Sunday at XUW Unknown Factors, as it's sure to be a stellar night packed full of excitement and other such spectacular things!" BJ: "You suck at hyping things." AT: "That's what your Mom said." BJ: "Yeah, I know. Errrm....." AT: "We can't still be on air, can we?" Fade to Black
