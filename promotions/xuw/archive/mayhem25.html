XUW Mayhem #25

11 Mar 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Pyro blasts, the music blares so loudly that even if you could understand the lyrics normally, you wouldn't be able to now, some crew guy turned up the lasers too high and the light show decapitates half the people in the nosebleed seats before anyone can stop it, and to top it all off, someone fed in the intro video backwards. Which can mean only one thing - MAYHEM IS LIVE! We cut to Atomic Tomato and "The Majestic" Barry Johnson. AT: WELCOME TO MAYHEM! Have we got a show for you tonight! Dave Kipfer... BJ: ...after some of the most unorthodox training I've ever seen... AT: ...faces Hodgo for the TV title. Naron and Sheri Dedmon square off in what promises to be a vicious women's match. Blake Savage pursues a death wish as he faces Severson. BJ: I think they should handicap Severson for any match he has against a normal sized human being. Like put him in a straitjacket or something. AT: We get more Commonwealth and Horsemen action as the Royal Legion faces the Technicians. Jack is scheduled to face a man who may well be dead when he squares off against HBK, and Sakie puts his world title up against a man he's never beaten in Supathug Esco. BJ: This is going to be an exciting night in XUW, when the first incomprehensible champion is crowned! AT: Let's get the party started with the TV Title Match! Hodgo (c) vs. Dave Kipfer - TV Title Match "One of a Kind" hits and the...talent comes out, followed by Lizzy Borden's breasts, then Lizzy herself, and finally Dave Kipfer emerges to a pop that's almost as big as his...talent. He poses for the fans, causing several women to faint and a guy in the front row to lose an eye. "Anarchy in the UK" hits and Hodgo comes out to heat. Massive heat. Which he NO SELLS, acting like he's beloved by all. Until a drink cup hits him RIGHT IN THE EYE...which he also no sells. Hodgo gets into the ring and stares for a moment at the...talent, in shock. DK takes the advantage by clotheslining Hodgo out of his boots as the ref signals for the bell. DK pulls Hodgo to his feet, hits a snap suplex followed by an elbow drop. He gets Hodgo to his feet, whips him off the ropes, and hits a release belly-to-belly. AT: Kipfer has come out here as a house on fire, Majestic! BJ: He's got a lot of passion, I can tell you that! Kipfer goes for a cover, but gets a mere two count. As he pulls Hodgo up, Hodgo low blows him, stands, and hits a vicious thrust kick to Kipfer's head. Hodgo goes for the cover, but Kipfer gets the shoulder up around two. Hodgo gets Kipfer back up, and goes to work on him with the Anar-kicks. He hits a final roundhouse to DK's head, and goes for another cover. But, again, Kipfer kicks out at two. Hodgo stands, frustrated, and starts jawing with the ref, allowing DK to go for a school boy. Hodgo kicks out at one and, as the two rise, goes for a clothesline. DK ducks, and tries to apply the Separation Anxiety. AT: Looks like Kipfer is swinging... BJ: ...but we all new that. AT: I was going to say, he's swinging the momentum his way, but... Before DK can get the Separation Anxiety locked on, Hodgo runs at the ropes and ducks, causing Kipfer to fly over the top. Dave, however, catches the top rope and lands on the apron. Hodgo turns around after taunting the fans and gets hit with a springboard hurricanrana. Hodgo, dazed, gets to his feet, and walks directly into the Booty Call. DK goes for the pin...and he gets the three. Winner and New TV Champion: Dave Kipfer DK holds his new belt high as he heads to the back with Lizzy. AT: What a great opening match. BJ: I've seen better. Usually involving me. AT: How did you see a match you were in? BJ: Dissassociative drugs.... AT: Ok...umm...next up is Naron and Sheri in a women's match. Naron vs. Sheri Dedmon "Bitch" hits and Naron comes to the ring to massive heat with her Kendo stick, which the ref makes her leave at the announce table. "Four Horsemen" hits and Sheri Dedmon comes down, ready for action, to a huge pop and lots of whistling young boys. She gets in the ring, Naron goes to slap her, but finds the attempt blocked and is met with a right from Sheri. The ref signals for the bell. Sheri continues to pummel Naron back into a corner, then whips her to the opposite side, follows her in with a clothesline, and combos it out into a bulldog. She goes for the pin, but Naron gets a shoulder up. Naron gets up, dazed, and is met with a powerbomb from Sheri, who then hits the Scorpion Splash for another two. AT: This is one hell of a display from Sheri Dedmon! There are men in this fed who don't show the range of moves she has. BJ: Normally, I realize, I don't have much to say that's positive, but after seeing women like Sheri and some of the Japanese women's feds, no one can tell me that women are nothing but eyecandy at the ring. Sheri sets Naron up on the top rope and hits the Scorpion's Tail. Naron rolls out of the ring and the ref stops Sheri from following her. Naron grabs her kendo stick and nails Sheri in the head from the apron. The ref calls for the bell. Winner: Sheri Dedmon via DQ Naron continues her assault on Sheri when, silently, Kat comes out of the crowd and slides into the ring. Naron raises the kendo stick for another swat at Sheri, but Kat grabs it out of her hands. Naron turns as Kat flips the kendo stick around Highlander style. Naron hightails it to the back. Kat helps up Sheri and the two Horsewomen leave to a huge pop. AT: Getting out of there was probably good strategy on Naron's part. BJ: Agreed. Naron might carry that kendo stick around, but I have a feeling that Kat actually knows how to use it to its full potential. AT: She's a dangerous little sociopath, that's a definite. We'll be right back after this break with Blake Savage of Doggy Style going against Severson! We cut to commercials...including Dave Kipfer advertising Trojan Extra Larges, Sirius Smythe's legal advice, and a video tribute to the Flashtastics. When we return, it's in the back, with a camera on Blake Savage, trying to get ANYONE to pay attention to him. Savage: I wanted to go out there and take on Dave Kipfer, I did! Concession Stand Attendant: Ummm, sure you did. Savage: I wanted to show him that I could take down the man with the bigges...talent in XUW! I was going to show you all! Random Backstage Man: Okay, okay! Savage: So, if I can't face the biggest...talent, then I shall have to make do by defeating the biggest man, period. Severson is going to see why "Superstar" Blake Savage is the best, most talented man in XUW! Cut back to AT and BJ. AT: Blake seems... BJ: Insane? AT: Not quite what I was going for, but it'll work! XUW cuts to a brief recap of something that probably only happened three minutes ago. Blake Savage v Severson "Hey Man, Nice Shot" plays, and Blake Savage makes his way to the ring, getting some decent heat. He enters the ring and poses for the ladies in the crowd. The Immense bulk of Severson is then seen at the top of the ramp, and he lumbers down to the ring to a chorus of boos. He steps over the top rope easily, and looks down at the man who stands over two feet shorter than him. AT: I have no idea how in the hell Savage is supposed to fight this man. BJ: Which man? That's Severson in there with him, he's not human, he can't be! Savage still looks happy, and is smiling as he delivers a kick to Sev's gut. Sev just smiles and pushes Blake down with one hand. Blake scrambles back up and hits a Flying Crossbody on the big man. He hits, but it has no discernable effect on Severson, who starts to laugh at the smaller man. Sev goes for a huge right hand, but Blake ducks underneath his legs and runs to the ropes, hitting them hard and springing back to hit a full blown Spear to Sev's left leg. The big guy is taken down to one knee. Blake swings a kick high up at Sev's head, but it is caught, and Sev picks up Blake, spinning him above his head, an evil grin on his face. AT: Look at the strength, this guy scares me, he's enormous! Sev throws Blake into the corner, and swings another huge fist at his head, which Savage just avoids. Blake runs round Sev quickly, and jumps onto his back, applying a Sleeper Hold. Sev tries to reach round and pluck him off, but he can't quite manage it. Sev looks to be weakening, but then backs up to a turnbuckle and jumps backwards hard into it. Blake loses his grip and drops down to the floor. Sev starts to climb the turnbuckle, as it sags and creaks under his huge mass. He gets up to the second buckle when Blake jumps up and hits a Dropkick to the leg, causing Sev to fall off backwards. Blake nimbly leaps to the top rope and dives off, hitting a big Missile Dropkick as Sev gets back up. Sev is briefly staggered, and Savage runs to ropes and comes back with a big clothesline. Sev stumbles backwards, just keeping his feet. Sev goes for a kick, but Blake catches the leg and tries to bring him down for the Blake-Tastic, but Sev is just too damn big, and Blake is on the receiving end of a huge Backhanded Fist, sending him stumbling to the canvas. Sev eyes the turnbuckle once more, and again starts the climb up to the top rope. AT: My God, he'll kill him if he lands any sort of move from up there! Somebody stop the damn match! BJ: No. This could be funny! Sev gets to the top turnbuckle, but then, amid much shrieking of cables and metal supports, the entire corner of the ring collapses, ropes and all. The elevator cables used for ropes whiplash across the ring and catch Sev across the chest. Already falling, Sev is flung into the only remaining standing in the corner, the ring post. Sev's huge head connects against it, and he falls down back into the ring, right at Blake's feet. The dazed "Superstar" rolls over and places one hand over Sev's chest for the pin. Winner: Blake Savage via pinfall. AT: Dear God! We'd better gets some EMT's, some ring repairers, and a damned crane out here! BJ: Is the crane for rebuilding that corner of the ring? AT: No, it's for getting Severson on the damn stretcher! We go to a "Technical Difficulties" screen while they reassemble the ring. BJ: My God...that was bizarre... AT: Only in the XUW. Next we have the latest installment in a feud that's been simmering for a while now as the Technicians square off against the Royal Legion. "Play The Game" hits and the Technicians come to the ring to massive cheers. "Rule Britannia" hits and the Royal Legion comes out to massive heat. The four men face off in the center of the ring, and the situation rapidly degenerates into an out and out brawl. KsC and Bailey fall to the outside as the Carrot puts the boots to RX in the corner. BC whips RX to the opposite corner, follows him in with a huge lariat, and whistles to KsC, who slams Bailey headfirst into the ring post and then begins to climb the turnbuckle. BC lifts RX for the Nail in the Coffin... AT: They're going for the fast win with the Excellent Execution! Henrik runs down from the back and causes KsC to crotch himself on the turnbuckle. BC hits the Nail in the Coffin and goes for the pin, but Bailey recovers himself and rolls into the ring to break up the count. Henrik, looking pleased with himself, begins to back up the ramp, unaware that Sidhe has come out from the back and is standing directly behind him, cigarette in mouth. Henrik backs into Sidhe, slowly turns, gets smoke blown into his face, and then takes off running back towards the ring, where RX has set BC up for the Family Values. BJ: There's too much going on out here to cover easily, folks! Just try to follow the action on your own... RX hits BC with the Family Values just as Henrik slides into the ring, with Sidhe in hot pursuit. Henrik slides back out of the ring on the opposite side, but Sidhe dropkicks him between the ropes, knocking him back into the announce table. Sidhe goes to work on Henrik. RX and Bailey turn their attention to their fallen King, allowing KsC and BC, who have collected themselves, to get a double rollup for the win. Winners: The Technicians via Pinfall Sidhe, KsC, and BC all head to the back to massive cheers, as the Commonwealth stands confused in the ring. AT: That was...bizarre. BJ: Yes it was. I doubt we've seen the last of the confontation between Henrik and Sidhe. AT: I doubt it heavily myself. Next up is Jack against...well...a dead man. The Ripper vs. HBK The ominous funeral music hits, breaking into "One Armed Scissor", and Jack Nobbledy comes to the ring to heavy heat, but no thrown objects, as the audience doesn't want to actually call attention to themselves out of fear. He gets in the ring, looking smug...but evil. Suddenly, the now familiar Red X begins to flash on the Ultitron, and "F*** That!" hits to a pop which reverberates throughout the world, causing Israelis and Palestinians to put down their arms and embrace each other in brotherhood, and Marv Albert to regrow a full head of hair. Then Bob actually comes out, and the pop escalates in intensity and innate goodness, curing men worldwide of impotence, triggering the discovery of a cure for cancer, and ending world hunger. Bob is still heavily bandaged, and appears to be drunk, but he looks determined as he gets into the ring with Jack and waffles him in the head with his cast! The ref looks shocked, and then signals for the bell! The Ripper vs. Samurai Bob Bob continues to bludgeon Jack mercilessly with his cast, screaming alternately between blows to the head "This is for Flash" and "This is for Flex". He whips Jack off the ropes and hits him with a running cast shot to the groin, which the ref ignores. Bob undoes one of the turnbuckle pads, grabs Jack, and tries to ram him headfirst into the exposed steel. Jack blocks, knees Bob in the gut, and Ripsaws him backfirst onto the exposed turnbuckle! He pulls up the writhing Bob, and repeats the Ripsaw. Jack goes for the pin, but somehow Bob kicks out at two. AT: Good God! Bob has no business being in that ring in his condition! BJ: He's got a broken arm, bruised ribs, and is drunk off his ass, but he's putting up a hell of a fight! Jack hits Bob with the Break, and applies the Fear. The ref checks on Bob, but Bob refuses to give. Slowly, Bob battles to his knees, but the Ripper releases the hold and stomps on Bob's lower back, then reapplying the Fear. Again, the ref checks on Bob, and again, Bob refuses to give. Bob fights to his knees, and then rapidly to his feet, lifting Jack on his shoulders. Bob falls forward, dropping Jack throat first across the ring rope, but also dropping Jack directly on the ref, knocking him out. AT: The ref is down! This match is chaos! BJ: This is fantastic! As Jack and Bob get to their feet, Lance runs out from the back and slides into the ring. Neither Jack nor Bob notice him as they slug it out. Lance measures Bob from behind, looking for an opportunity to hit the superkick. Bob starts to turn and Lance rushes in. However, Bob was only turning as Jack had laid him out with a  monstrous right hand, and Bob drops to the mat. Lance's superkick catches Jack right in the solar plexus, dropping him. Bob goes for the pin as the ref recovers, and he gets the three! Winner: Samurai Bob via Pinfall Lance goes to work on the weakened Bob as Jack gets to his feet. Jack turns and looks at Lance, and then damn near rips him in half with a spear! Bob jumps Jack from behind, and all three men begin to brawl! DDT, Rob the Throb, and Chris Blake run out from the back as Lance clears out through the crowd. Bob fights to his feet, but stumbles back into a Throbbing Headache from RTT. DDT lifts Bob and hits the Creeping Death. All four men put Bob up on the top turnbuckle, and Jack hits him with a massive top rope Ripsaw! Jack asks for the mic as he stands over Bob's fallen and broken body JTR: Lance, The Ripper will remember what you did here tonight. The Darkness will find you, Lance, and it won't matter how serious you are. Destiny calls you, and The Ripper is that destiny. "One Armed Scissor" hits again as the AoD heads to the back to massive heat. EMTs come out and load Bob onto a stretcher. AT: Bob looks like he's been in a damn car wreck! BJ: Good thing he was drunk, his muscles were relaxed enough to prevent any serious injury, hopefully. AT: That was a horrific sight...but we have to keep going! Next up - World Championship Match as Sakie faces the Supathug! Sakie (c) vs. Supathug Esco - World Title Match "Bad Dreams" hits and Esco comes to the ring to massive heat. "Number One" hits and Sakie comes out to massive pops. The two men square off, Sakie goes for a clothesline, but Esco ducks under and hits a neckbreaker. Esco pulls Sakie back up, bodyslams him, and comes off the ropes with an elbow drop. He goes for the pin and gets a two! AT: Esco's new attitude seems to be paying off! BJ: I've got a feeling this could be Esco's night to shine. Esco pulls Sakie back up, who tries for a right hand, which Esco ducks. Sakie spins completely around, Esco kicks him in the gut and hits a DDT. Esco goes for the pin again, and again gets a two. He pulls up Sakie, whips him off the ropes, and hits the Bad Dream. Esco pick Sakie up yet again and hits the Drive-by. He goes for the cover, but again, only a two. Esco, frustrated, argues with the ref, allowing Sakie to get a surprise roll up for a two count. The two men get to their feet, but Esco lays out Sakie with a short lariat, stopping any momentum AT: My God, Esco is dominating this match! BJ: Yeah, and Sakie isn't even having to pay him! Not like that girl last night... AT: What? BJ: Nevermind. Esco whips Sakie off the ropes again, but Sakie reverses and goes for a clothesline. Esco ducks it and hits the Finisher! He goes for a pin, and gets a 2.975 count. Frustrated, Esco sets Sakie up for the Beatdown, but Sakie slides over his back as he lifts him up. Esco turns, and Sakie gets a small package for the pin! Winner and Still World Champion: Sakie Frustrated and furious, Esco heads to the back as Sakie celebrates inside the ring. AT: Incredible! I never thought I'd say this, but the champ just got an upset win! BJ: Esco had that match, if he had just had the focus. AT: That's all the time we have, folks! Until Vortex, this is the Atomic Tomato for the Majestic BJ, saying goodnight! Fade on Sakie celebrating.
