XUW XPosure #17

23 Jun 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW XPosure The arena explodes with fireworks and pyro as the Xposure theme blares over the loud speakers and the fans pop with delight, because Xposure is finally underway. The camera heads over to the announce position. AT: Hello everyone and welcome to XUW Xposure! As always I am the Atomic Tomato and joining me at ringside to help with color commentary is “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson. BJ: Yeah yeah yeah, I gotta meet an old friend of mine at the local pub after this so can we rush things a bit. AT: Barry, this is Xposure. People wait days for this show to air in hopes of seeing their- BJ: Yadda yadda yadda, I’ll just run down the card for you. The Eliminators and Syco take on the Stable With No Name, Bluto, Al Crud, and the Apostle. Lazarus and Kevil fight over the TV title, our special XUW’s “From The Vault” match, Cereal Killer faces Sniper, and in the main event, Henrik the King puts his Submission title on the line against Laudust. AT: Umm….lets get into things I guess. ”We Untouchable” hits as The Eliminators and Syco make their way from the back. They walk down to the ring and climb in. “One Bourbon, One Scotch, One Brew,” hits as the Stable With No Name makes their way down to the ring to a nice pop. The climb in the ring and the ref rings the bell. The Eliminators and Syco vs. The Stable with No Name Rujasu and Bluto start things off. They lock up and Rujasu gets an advatange with a go behind waistlock. Rujasu quickly lets go and goes for the Hopeless Future (Cobra Clutch), but Bluto blocks and grabs Rujasu’s arm and slams it across his shoulder. Rujasu hobbles around and Bluto nails him with a clothesline. Rujasu gets back up and gets another clothesline. Rujasu gets up and Bluto nails him with a knee to the midsection. Bluto then goes to set him up for the Keg Stand (Stall Brainbuster), but Rujasu slides out of it and gets Bluto and locks him in and delivers an R-Driver. Rujasu walks over and tags Syco. Syco enters the ring as Destroyer gives Rujasu a dirty look. Rujasu returns the stare. AT: Whoa, did you see that look from Destroyer? Looks like he’s upset that Rujasu didn’t tag him. BJ: Yeah yeah, no tag, we gotcha. Syco comes in and goes to work on Bluto. Syco picks up Bluto and sends him into the ropes. Syco goes to clothesline Bluto, but Bluto ducks and comes off the other side with a high cross body. Bluto gets up and makes the tag to the Apostle. The Apostle comes into the ring and nails Syco as he gets to his feet with the Genuflection (Forward Russian Legsweep). The Apostle goes for the cover. 1… 2... Kickout! The Apostle picks up Syco and sends him to the ropes. He goes to nail him with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, but Syco counters and lands on his feet. Syco then rakes the Apostle in the eyes and then nails him with the Head Check (Tombstone Piledriver). Syco drags the Apostle towards his team’s corner, climbs the turnbuckle and connects with the Psycotic Splash(Five Star Frog Splash). Syco goes for the cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! AT: I can’t believe it! The Apostle kicked out of the Psycotic Splash! BJ: Damn you Apostle! Get pinned already! Syco picks up the Apostle and sets him up for the Psycotic Edge (Razor’s Edge), but the Apostle slips out of it and takes Syco down with a hangman’s neckbreaker. Both men are down as they try to reach their respective corners. Inch by inch they get over and Syco tags in Destroyer as The Apostle tags in Al Crud. Crud meets Destroyer with some Crudderlines as the fans let out a pop as Al lets out a fart. Al sets up Destroyer so he can deliver a helluva Crudderline, but before Al connects, Destroyer levels him with a D-Line (Clothesline from Hell). Al is turned inside out. Destroyer goes for the Exterminator (Rolling Thunder), but Al moves out of the way. Destroyer then lands on his back hard. Al looks at the crowd and then runs to the ropes to deliver the Scent Of Victory, but Destroyer moves away from that. Al gets up holding his ass as Destroyer delivers the Destruction on Al. He makes a cover and the ref counts. 1… 2… 3!!! Winners – The Eliminators and Syco AT: What a match! Al Crud and Destroyer miss big moves, but Destroyer recovers and hits the move that matters. The pinning move! BJ: Yeah yeah, pinning move, that’s great. Destroyer’s team wins, wahoo, lets get to the next match! AT: Well in our next match, Kevil faces Lazarus for the TV Title. Will the root of all evil be able to defeat the 2 time TV champ? ”Lynch Pin” hits as Kevil makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and poses as “Sinner” hits and Lazarus makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and hands the ref the TV title. The ref shows off the title and rings the bell. TV Title Match – Lazarus (C) vs. Kevil Lazarus and Kevil tie up, but Laz gets the upper hand with some boxing jabs. He rocks Kevil back into the ropes and goes to knock him outside with a knockout punch, but Kevil moves as Laz stumbles in the ropes. Kevil turns him around and starts nailing him with some punches. He then sends Laz across the ring, but Laz halts, springboards off the ropes and goes to nail him with spear, but Kevil catches him with a DDT. Kevil makes a cover. 1… 2… Shoudler up! Laz starts to get up as Kevil goes to scale the turnbuckle. Laz gets up and Kevil goes to nail him with the Green Eggs and Ham (Spinning Heel Kick from the top), but Laz moves out of the way. Kevil hits the mat hard. He starts to get up, but Laz levels him with a Big Boot. Laz makes a quick cover, pulling back on the leg. 1.. 2.. SHOULDER UP! AT: Kevil is really showing some heart in this fast paced matchup. BJ: Just the way I like it Cucumber. Fast paced, and done within minutes. Laz sets him up in the middle of the ring and delivers a powerbomb. Kevil is flat on his back in the middle of the ring. Laz looks down and runs to the ropes and goes to deliver a Lazasault, but Kevil puts his knees up. Kevil then gets to his feet and catches the stumbling Laz with an enzuigiri. Laz falls to the canvas on his back. Kevil then runs and performs an Evilsault and connects. The ref makes the count. 1… 2… KICKOUT! Kevil looks at the ref with a frustrated look. He then picks up Laz and goes to deliver a bodyslam, but Laz counters with a small package. 1… 2… KICKOUT! Kevil goes to pick Laz up, but Laz sneaks in a low blow. Kevil hunches over as Laz puts him under and picks him up for a crucifix powerbomb. Laz backs up and sets him on the turnbuckle. Laz then goes to set him up for the Cross Powerbomb, but Laz stops as he sees Cereal Killer run down the ramp. The ref walks over, but Laz pushes him out of the way. Laz goes to punch CK, but CK ducks nails him with a shoulder to the midsection and a fist to the groin. CK jumps off the apron. Laz falls back in the corner as Kevil pulls him back in and sets him up for Welcome To The Darkside (Scorpion Death Drop from the top) and connects! He makes a cover. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner and new TV Champ – Kevil AT: Kevil has reclaimed the XUW TV title on the account of Cereal Killer’s interference! What a stellar match I must say, except for that interference by CK. Fast paced with a lot of impact moves. BJ: I knew it wouldn’t be too long until Kevil put him away. <looks watch his at />Well I didn’t think it would take this long, so lets get into our “From The Vault” match eh. AT: Well fans, today we go back to December to Dismember, where the vacated XUW World Title was to be contested between our current champion Samurai Bob, former 2 time champ Lopen, and the Horsemen’s own Kata. Lets head to the match. Kata Hajime vs Lopen vs Samurai Bob Referee : Fat Badger Kata Hajime comes down the ramp to mixed reaction. He doesn't acknowledge that there is an audience. Lopen comes down next to good heat. He gets in the ring with Kata, and the two stare each other down. "F*** That" hits the speakers and Bob gets the biggest Pop of the night. He runs down the ramp. and slides into the ring. the bell sounds. Kata and Bob lock up. Lopen sits off in a corner watching with a smirk on his face. Bob get Kata in a headlock, Kata reverses into a hammerlock, Bob reverses to a wrist lock. Lopen watches Matlock. Kata reverses to an armdrag take down. Both get up and circle. Bob and Kata lock up again. Kata goes for a suplex. Bob wiggles out and pushes Kata at Lopen, Lopen catches him and nails a Lopen Cutter. Kata falls out of the ring. Lopen aproaches Bob. Lopen locks up with Bob, Bob manages a tiger suplex. Bob hits a couple leg drops, and finishes the series off the top ropes with a spinning leg drop. Bob gets Lopen up so that he can give him a double arm DDT. Kata comes back into the ring and grabs Bob with a over-head belly-to-belly suplex. Kata picks up Lopen and applies the "Kata Hajime". Lopen struggles and Bob kick Kata's knees out from under him.  lopen lays in a pile in the near corner. A very pissed off Bob attacks Kata with an elevated boston crab. Kata, being a submission champ, refuses to tap and crawls to the ropes. Badger calls the break. Bob picks up Kata, and puts him in a shoulder breaker. Then follows up with a dragon sleeper. Kata spins around and reverses with a northern lights suplex. Lopen runs at Kata and hits a flying elbow drop. Kata and Bob are laying side by side. Lopen climbs the near ropes. And delivers a turn around moonsault to them both. Lopen covers Bob. Badger slides in. 1...2...kickout. Lopen gets up and covers Kata 1....2....Kickout. Lopen pushes down Fat Badger and begins to kick him. Bob and Kata get up and give Lopen a double atomic drop. Then they give him a tandom wishbone. Lopen grabs his crotch, and Bob grabs Kata. Bob hits a neckbreaker. Picks Kata back up and drops him with a inverted tiger driver. Bob picks up Kata and whips him to the corner. Bob picks Kata and sets him on the top turn buckle. Bob begins to climb the ropes for a Super Plex, but Lopen pushes Bob out of the ring. Lopen delivers a Super Lopen Cutter from the top rope to Kata. Lopen covers. 1.....2......3 Winner and new XUW World Champion: Lopen Bob gets back into the ring as Lopen celebrates. bob Sneaks behind Lopen and gets him into the Skid Mark Chicken Wing. Kata gets up and begins to beat down Bob. Lopen uses the opportunity to escape up the ramp. AT: That Lopen is a vile, coward. Mr. Mikey: And the 2 time, 2 time XUW champion!!! AT: I hope the next match is better. The camera heads back to the announce position with Mr. Majestic and the Atomic Tomato. AT: And what a great match that was. Now its time for our next match between Cereal Killer and Sniper. BJ: Yeah yeah, Sniper used to be managed by PTP, CK got sent a whore by PTP, and this match is supposed to mean something to CK since he’s getting his hands on something formerly managed by PTP. AT: Sheesh, you really want to leave don’t you? BJ: Yes. ”Here To Stay” hits as Cereal Killer makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and stares down the aisleway. “Unsung” by Helmet hits as Sniper makes his way down to the ring. He rushes the ring and slides in as the ref rings the bell. Cereal Killer vs. Sniper Sniper goes to tackle CK, but CK beats on the backside of Sniper as soon as he gets in the ring. Sniper then manages to get to his feet, but CK backs him into the ropes and sends him to the other side. CK then grabs Sniper as he bounces off the ropes with a single handed choke. CK goes to set up Sniper for a chokeslam, but Sniper looks down at CK and shoves his hand right into CK’s throat with such impact that CK lets go of his choke. Sniper then sets up CK and delivers Pull The Trigger (Chokeslam). CK hits the mat hard and Sniper goes for a cover. 1… 2… KICKOUT! AT: Would ya look at that? CK goes for a chokeslam on the huge Sniper and all Sniper does is chokeslam the hell outta CK. BJ: Yeah yeah, irony, I get it. Sniper picks up CK and sets him up for a suplex, but CK blocks. Sniper lifts CK up finally, but CK slips behind Sniper and pushes him into the ropes. Sniper comes off and CK connects with a spinning belly to belly. CK goes for a cover. 1… Shoulder up! CK picks up Sniper and nails him with repeated European uppercuts. He goes to send him to the ropes, but Sniper reverses and sends CK to the ropes instead. He goes to slam him in the face with a big boot, but CK ducks and comes off the other side and nails him with the Crippling Effect (Matt Hardy-like neckbreaker drop). Sniper then starts to get to his feet slowly but surely and CK then nails him with the Power Trip (Reno’s roll the dice). CK covers. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner – Cereal Killer AT: CK with an impressive win over the big man Sniper. Does PTP still want a chance at facing CK after this display? BJ: Yeah, I wouldn’t if I were PTP. Say, don’t we have just one more match? AT: Yeah and its- BJ: Well lets get to it. Submission title baby! ”The Natural” hits as Laudust makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and ‘Swoooo’s’ for the audience. “Rule Brittania” hits as Henrik makes his way down to the ring with Naron. He climbs in the ring and removes his title from his waist and hands it to the ref. The ref shows the crowd and then rings the bell. Submission Title Match – Henrik The King (C) vs. Laudust Henrik and Laudust lockup. Laudust gets Henrik in a headlock, but Henrik reverses with a go behind and a double leg takedown. He then jumps towards the front of Laudust’s body and locks in a chinlock. Laudust grabs the ropes that are near him. Henrik lets go of the hold and gets to his feet. Laudust gets to his feet and dusts himself off. They go to lock up again and Henrik catches Laudust with a standing wristlock that he soon counters into a crippler crossface. The ref checks on Laudust. Laudust holds his hand out to help steady the pain. He screams as Henrik pulls back. Laudust inches over to reach the ropes. He keeps his hand stretched out and finally grabs the ropes. AT: Boy, Henrik is having his way with Laudust. BJ: Yeah yeah, finish him off! Laudust gets up again and dusts himself off. Henrik looks at him and smiles. Laudust charges Henrik, but Henrik ducks and gives Laudust a double leg takedown and holds onto the legs and starts to lock in the Four Leaf Clover. Laudust tries to fight it, but Henrik still manages to lock it on. Laudust tries to make his way to the ropes. He inches closer and closer to the ropes. He reaches out to grab the ropes, but Henrik drags him back into the middle of the ring and wrenches back on the hold and Laudust starts to tap. The ref rings the bell. Winner and still Submission Champ – Henrik The King Henrik calls over for a mic as Naron climbs in the ring. The ring announcer gives Henrik a mic. Henrik: My loyal subjects, your King would like to speak to thee about Liberation. Your King doesn’t fear DDT in the least bit. He may get his kicks from scaring my Queen, but come Liberation, he will fear my Four Leaf Clover, and he will tap. Henrik puts down the mic as the camera heads back to AT at the announce position. AT: Well that’s it for Xposure. Any last words Bar- The camera shows the entire announce table as Mr. Majestic has left the area. AT: Well, ummm,….I guess he left. Well everyone, tune in for Mayhem. For Mr. Majestic, I am the Atomic Tomato and goodnight.
