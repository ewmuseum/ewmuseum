XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #24

13 Jun 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Pyro goes off as the unmistakable theme of Vortex blares throughout the arena. The fans are popping like crazy spotlights swerve around all over the place. Signs are shown all over the arena. Signs such as “Sidhe fears Sniper!”, “Show with F.E.A.R. = Ratings”, and “Marry me Kat, I look like Sheri.” The camera heads to the announce position. AT: Welcome everyone to another exciting installment of Vortex, live from Hambone Dome. BJ: Bow down before our hosts, “Mr. Majestic” and umm….that- AT: Lost you thought? BJ: Damn you Parsley. Shut up and let me talk. We got a great card for tonight ladies and gents. Tonight, you’ll see the man who’s been stinking up the XUW bathrooms as of late, Fatbadger, go up against the Oat Bran Assasin, Cereal Killer. XUW’s smallest male competitor Sidhe Nimh takes on the Usual Suspect’s biggest member, Sniper. The TV title is on the line as Kidd Kore goes for gold against Kata of the Horsemen. AT: Anyone want to see two women wrestle around on a mat? Well, we got just that. Mixed tag time for everyone as Naron and Henrik go up against the AoD’s DDT and Kat. In tonight’s main event, the Intercontinental title is on the line as Blake Wolfson defends against X-Factor in a No DQ match. Lets get into things with- The camera heads outside. Standing outside is DJ, Sev, and an unknown figure. After a little conversing, DJ and Sev leave. The camera heads back to ringside. AT: I wonder what that was all about. ”Make Me Bad” hits as Cereal Killer makes his way to the ring. There’s a moderate pop for him, but once he climbs in the ring, the fans explode as “Mudshovel” by Staind hits and Fatbadger makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and CK jumps on him from the get go. The ref rings the bell. Fatbadger vs. Cereal Killer CK hammers away at Badger, but Badger comes back and nails him with a Badger Scratch. CK backs down as he holds his eyes as Badger goes on the attack. Badger goes for a quick finish with the Fat Burner, but CK blocks and nails Badger with a face first suplex. CK goes for a Fujiwara armbar, but Badger counters and catches him with a rollup. 1… 2… Shoulder up! CK gets out of the roll up quickly and hammers at Badger with some double axhandles to the back. CK picks up Badger and goes to deliver a belly-to-belly, but Badger counters with a double ear clap. CK drops the hold and holds his ears as Badger grabs CK and goes behind him and delivers a vicious release German Suplex. He rushes over to the fallen CK quickly and locks on the Badger Stretch. CK screams for help, but Badger only puts on the hold more and starts to foam like a Badger. After about 25 seconds, CK starts to tap out and Badger releases the hold. Winner – Fatbadger AT: Wow. Badger picks up a surprisingly quick victory. Must be the BBQ sauce he injected in him earlier. BJ: Yeah it must be. Look at him run for the bathroom! AT and BJ have a good laugh at Badger as the camera heads to the backstage. Backstage, AJ, Sheri, and BC are standing around. AJ: BC, we got a meeting that we gotta go to with XUW management, so be sure to keep an eye out for the gWo. You know they like to fight underhandedly and whenever numbers are in their favor. BC: They aren’t gonna touch a Horsemen outside of their own matches, AJ. The camera heads back to the arena. “Unsung” by Helmet hits as Sniper makes his way to the ring. He’s receiving a moderate pop. He climbs in the ring and “Eternal Life” hits as Sidhe gets a warm reception from the fans. He walks down to the ring and climbs in. The ref rings the bell Sniper vs. Sidhe Nimh Sidhe and Sniper lock up. Sniper uses his pure strength to shove Sidhe into the ropes. Sniper goes for The Brick Wall (Big Boot), but Sidhe ducks it and catches him with a snap capture suplex. Sniper gets up and goes for a clothesline, but Sidhe ducks it and locks in Fear and Loathing. Sniper fights against the hold and uses his size to back Sidhe into a corner. He hits it hard enough that Sidhe lets go. Sniper gives Sidhe a few back elbows in the corner and stops as Sidhe falls and sits on the mat. AT: Wow, Sniper may actually be new and improved Barrry. BJ: I doubt it. His size is the only thing that’s kept him in this match. Sniper pulls Sidhe up and puts his hand on his throat. Sniper goes to deliver a chokeslam, but Sidhe counters on the way down with a cross face. Sniper uses his strength to get up from it and does a good job of it. In fact he backs Sidhe into a corner again, but Sidhe this time jumps up onto it and locks on a chicken wing and then executes a vicious tornado suplex out of the corner. Sidhe then climbs the turnbuckle nearest Sniper and executes the Shooting Gallery. Sidhe covers. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner – Sidhe Nimh Sidhe grabs a mic from the ring announcer Sidhe: Hi. The fans pop a little Sidhe: Now that victory was truly an impressive on. For the shortest guy on the XUW roster to beat a guy of that size rather easily is something to fear. As a result of the AoD winning Stable Wars, I have a title shot of my choice and I plan on using it against you……..Bob, and I want to use it on July 7th, the next PPV, Liberation. Are you man enough to accept? Sidhe drops the mic and heads to the back. AT: Amazing, Sidhe has challenged our World champion, Bob, to a title match at our next PPV, Liberation, for the World title. That could be one helluva matchup. BJ: Finally, the World title will come back to the AoD where it belongs. The camera heads backstage to the locker room of Blake Wolfson. Blake is getting ready for his IC title match. There’s a knock at the door. Blake turns to look at the door. Blake: Who’s there? No one answers. Blake gets a little annoyed. Blake: Who the f**k is there? No one answers again. Blake turns to finish getting ready when all of a sudden, DJ and Sev burst through the door and beat the living hell out of Blake. The camera heads back to the arena. AT: Dear lord, Blake just got attacked by DJ and Severson backstage! BJ: Hah! That was great. Stupid Aussie never saw it coming. AT: That’s gonna surely affect Blake for his title match tonight. Speaking of a title match, up next for the TV Title is Kidd Kore vs. the reigning champion, Kata. BJ: I think Kore is gonna get it done. With the Grand Wizard in his corner, he’s surely going to be hard to stop. AT: Due to cheating? BJ: Hey, now that’s not fair. Kore is a talented wrestler. AT: He’s an imbecile. He wants to be the XUW Ice Cream man for God’s sake. ”Superstar” by Saliva hits as Kidd Kore comes down to the ring with the Grand Wizard. The fans boos as the Wizard yells at them to shut up. They climb in the ring as the Wizard shows the fans the TV title is going to Kore. "Change (In the House of Flies)" hits and the fans’ jeers turn to pops as Kata comes from behind the curtain with the TV title around his waist. Kata stares at the Grand Wizard and Kore as he comes down to the ring. He slides in the ring and Kore goes to attack. The ref rings the bell. TV Title Match – Kata(C) vs. Kidd Kore Kata ducks Kore and starts to lay waste to Kore on the ropes. He hits him in the head with some right hands and after about nine of them, he winds up and clotheslines him right over the top rope. Kore hits the outside as Kata hypes the crowd. The Grand Wizard gets on the apron and distracts Kata. Kata walks over and before he can punch him he jumps off the apron. Meanwhile, Kore has sneaked back in the ring and tries to attack Kata before he can notice, but Kata catches him with a series of chops. He goes to wind Kore up, but Kore reverses and sends Kata to the other side. The Grand Wizard trips Kata as Kata turns around to go after the Wizard, but Kore nails him with the Obscure Reference (reverse DDT), but can’t follow up. Kata remains on the canvas a while before beginning to stir. Kore manages to gather himself together and gets over to Kore and starts to lay the boots into him. AT: Well Kore has turned this thing around with the Wizard’s help. BJ: Well he had to, he was cheating. AT: What?! BJ: That’s right and you know it. Kata was cheating and the Wizard just fixed that problem. AT: You must be influenced under alcohol cause what you’re saying makes no sense what so ever and is making you sound goofier than a pet coon. Kore picks up Kata and sends him across the ring to the turnbuckle. Kata hits hard as Kore runs across the ring and hits him with a clothesline. Kata stumbles out of the turnbuckle, but Kore pulls him back in by his hair. He then grabs his head and climbs to the second turnbuckle. He then delivers a flying bulldog. Kore goes for a cover. 1… 2… Shoulder up! Kore looks at the ref like he should have made a faster count. He then picks up Kata and sets him up for the Royal Flush, but Kata blocks. He knees Kata in the chest and goes to deliver again, but Kata takes him down with a double leg takedown and delivers a slingshot to Kore, who flies into a turnbuckle, bounces off and falls to the mat. The fans cheer as Kata has made himself an opening. Both men struggle to get to their feet, but Kata makes it to his feet first. Kore stumbles to his feet, but stumbles right into an Ipponzei (Judo throw) from Kata. Kore gets back up and stumbles into another. Kata backs Kore into a turnbuckle and starts to deliver a 10 punch count. After the 10th punch, Kore stumbles out of the corner and falls onto the mat. The Grand Wizard gets on the apron again as Kata walks over and puts the ref aside and grabs the Wizard by the collar. He holds his hand out like to punch the Wizard and looks around the audience. Out of nowhere, Kore slips in with a low blow on Kata. The Wizard jumps to safety as Kore sets up Kata and delivers a brainbuster right on his head. Kore then sets up Kata on his back. Kore then goes to the outside and gets on the turnbuckle. He looks at the audience for a bit and smiles. The Wizard yells at him to focus, but Kore just looks at him blankly. Kata then springs up on the turnbuckle catching Kore by surprise and delivering the Driving Force (backdrop driver) from the top. Kore slams into the mat as Kata goes to make the pin. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner and still TV Champ – Kata Kata gets up as the ref hands him the TV title and he holds it up. The Grand Wizard climbs in the ring and yells at Kata. Kata looks at him and grabs him by the collar, but out of nowhere, Woof has come through the crowd and nails Kata from behind. Woof starts to beat on the fallen Kata and soon enough Kore joins in as the Grand Wizard yells at Kata some more. After a while, the Wizard pulls off Kore and Woof and raises their hands as Kata remain down on the mat. The gWo walks to the back as a ref checks in on Kata. AT: Well Kata wins the match, but he surely didn’t win the war. Where was BC to help him on that one? BJ: I don’t know, but those Horsemen never were the brightest crayons in the box. They probably thought Woof came through the crowd to help Kata. AT: Needless to say, Kata really needed some help in there. He’s a great wrestler, but two on one isn’t fair when it starts with a cheap shot. The camera heads backstage as DJ and Sev are leaving the arena counting some money. The camera then heads back to the arena. AT: Well it does look like DJ and Sev were paid to do the job on Wolfson, but by who? BJ: <sarcastically />Oh, now that’s a brain teaser. The camera cuts to the backstage at a refreshment table. Out of nowhere, Bob walks over and starts to get some coffee. Also out of nowhere, Sidhe comes walking towards him. Sidhe: So Bob, man enough to accept my challenge? Bob turns to look at Sidhe as he takes a sip of his coffee. Bob: Oh believe me, I’m man enough and I gladly accept. The question is, are you man enough to take it from me? All of a sudden, MV Pete and Numark walk up behind Bob and stand there. Sidhe: I think you should be asking yourself, are you man enough to let me? Sidhe walks off as Bob turns and looks at Numark and MV Pete. The camera heads back to the arena AT: Sidhe vs. Bob, man that should be a classic. BJ: Sidhe is by far the superior athlete. He’s proven that size doesn’t matter and it won’t matter when he and Bob finally get in the ring. AT: Well from one AoD member to two others. Next up is our mixed tag match. DDT and Kat take on Henrik and Naron in what’s sure to be a classic. BJ: DDT’s been stalking Naron I guess you could say as of late and its about time the Commonwealth has a chance for payback, but will they get it? ”Rule Brittania” hits as Henrik and Naron make their way to the ring from the backstage. Naron seems a little apprehensive. Henrik turns and notices her demeanor and tries to console her and hype her up. Her look of concern turns to one of “Lets get it on.” They climb in the ring as “Another Body Murdered” hits the speakers and DDT and Kat come out. DDT and Kat look at each other, nod, and turn back to the ring and smile. They make their way to the ring and climb in. Naron and Kat go to the outside as Henrik and DDT remain inside. The ref rings the bell. Mixed Tag – Henrik The King/Naron vs. DDT/Kat Henrik and DDT circle each other in the ring. They go to lock up and Henrik gets an eye rake in. DDT backs off and holds his eyes as Henrik lays into him with several forearm shots backing him into a corner. He continues to lay in with the forearm shots, but DDT shakes it off and shoves Henrik rolling backward on the canvas. Henrik gets back up and charges at DDT, but before Henrik gets there, DDT walks out of the corner and sticks out his arm and nails Henrik with a clothesline. Henrik hits the mat hard as the crowd lets out a gasping groan. DDT walks over and picks Henrik up by the head. He brings him over to the AoD corner. He puts Henrik into the corner and starts to shove his shoulders into Henrik’s midsection. Weary and at a loss for breath, Henrik is pulled out of the corner by DDT and thrown to the ropes. DDT ducks to give Henrik a backdrop, but Henrik stops and locks DDT’s head in and delivers a facebuster onto the knee ala Triple H and a dropkick that sends DDT to the canvas. Henrik goes for the cover. 1… 2… Kickout! Henrik then picks up DDT and sets him up and delivers a vertical suplex. DDT gets back up and tags in Kat. Henrik walks over and tags in Naron. AT: In this match, the women wrestle the women and the men wrestle the men. BJ: And if Parsley were in this match, he would be wrestling both. Naron climbs in the ring and catches Kat with a double leg takedown. Naron gets on top and takes Kat by the head and takes Kat by the head and starts laying waste to it on the mat. Naron gets off and sends Kat to the ropes, but Kat reverses. Kat nails Naron with a spinning heel kick. Naron hits the mat as Kat walks over and tags DDT. DDT gets in the ring and walks over to Naron, hovering above her. Naron looks up and finally sees DDT, she backs away ever so frightenedly, but Henrik comes off the top with a flying dropkick. Naron makes her way to the outside as Henrik gets on DDT and punches him continuously. Henrik gets up and picks DDT up by the head. He puts him under and sets him up for a piledriver, but DDT blocks and takes him down with a single leg takedown and locks on half Boston. Henrik writhes in pain as he tries to escape the hold. DDT soon releases the hold and locks on the Strate Jacket. Henrik writhes in pain as the ref checks to see on a tap out. AT: Would ya look at that, DDT showing some technical skill in the ring against Henrik. BJ: DDT wouldn’t be main event caliber had he not learned some technical wrestling, Parsley. AT: Who exactly are you rooting for in this match? BJ: The AoD of course. They won Stable Wars impressively. AT: Look! Henrik just broke the hold and has locked on a crossface! BJ: Go Commonwealth! Err I mean, yeah. AT: You’re such a front runner. Henrik is still locked in the Strate Jacket as DDT continues to lock it on. Henrik is fading when Naron enters the ring. DDT sees her and lets go of the hold. He stalks her as the ref tries to get her out of the ring. DDT backs Naron into an empty corner and Naron falls into the corner scared, but DDT gets The Crown Jewels (Low Blow) delivered to him by Henrik. Naron escapes to the Commonwealth corner as both men are down. DDT starts to make his way over to Kat as Henrik tries to make his way over to Naron. They both get the tag and Naron goes at it with Kat. Naron grabs Kat and sends her to the ropes. Naron nails her with a clothesline. Kat gets back up and eats another clothesline. Kat gets up and ducks another Naron clothesline and goes to deliver The Broken Heart (Superkick to the chest), Naron ducks amazingly. Kat comes back around and Naron catches her and delivers The Royal Decree (Tombstone Piledriver) She gets back up, but backs into DDT. She turns around and sees DDT. He grabs her by the hair and walks around the ring with her. The ref gets in his face and yells at him to leave the ring. DDT keeps walking with a handful of Naron’s hair as she screams for Henrik. The ref gets mad enough that he walks over and tell the timekeeper to ring the bell, thus DQing the AoD. Winners – Henrik The King/Naron DDT throws Naron down as he turns to look at the ref. The ref holds up his hands to keep DDT at bay, but instead takes the low road and jumps out of the ring. Henrik helps Naron out of the ring and grabs a mic as he walks up the ramp. Henrik: Listen, you excuse for a human being. Stay away from my Queen! Pick on someone your own size, like me. DDT ‘Ha’s’ at how Henrik is close to 100 pnds smaller than him Henrik: Laugh it up, but are you man enough to use that Stable Wars title shot against me at Liberation? Huh? Are you? Kat gets DDT a mic. DDT: Henrik…………I think the question is………..can you protect……….your title………….and your woman? DDT tosses down the mic and looks at Henrik as he and Naron walk to the backstage. The camera cuts backstage. BC exits a bathroom. He walks down the hall to his locker room and meets up with AJ and Sheri AJ: There’s the man we wanted to see. BC: Hey guys. Come on in. They all walk into BC’s locker room to find blood all over his stuff BC: What the hell? Why’s there blood on all my stuff?! Kata storms in the locker room seemingly pretty mad. Kata: Thanks a lot man for the support out there. BC: What? Kata: Yeah, I really liked getting my ass kicked out there by Woof Woof and Kore. Kata stares down BC as Sheri and AJ look on. The camera heads back to the arena. AT: Looks like Kata isn’t too pleased about BC not watching his back tonight. BJ: He’s just sour that the Horsemen didn’t ensure his victory tonight. You know those Horsemen. All they ever do is cheat to win. AT: Oh give it a rest. Do you even watch these matches or do you just sit back and think of what you want to see happen. BJ: Oh if that were true, XUW would be better than the Garden of Eden. AT: Well next up is our main event where X-Factor has another shot to take away the IC title that Blake Wolfson has given a home to for quite a while. This may be X’s last chance to do so and its in a No DQ environment so will he be able to avoid getting the final knockout? BJ: These two had a phenomenal ladder match at Big Brawl. With the rules we have tonight, we may just see a ladder come into play again. "Killing in the name of” hits as X-Factor makes his way to the ring. With him, he carries a wire garbage can loaded with random objects such as a dingo lawn ornament, a lamp, and a coconut. He climbs in the ring as “I Stand Alone” hits as Blake Wolfson makes his way down to the ring. Blake pushes out a shopping cart with the following random objects inside of it….a beach chair, a bear trap, and a big wooden spoon. Blake gets down to ringside and removes the big wooden spoon from the shopping cart as X removes the dingo lawn ornament from his wire garbage can. Blake slides in the ring and jabs X in the kneecap with the spoon. The ref rings the bell IC Title Match *No DQ* - Blake Wolfson © vs. X-Factor AT: Since when did this become a hardcore match? BJ: Well Parsley, these guys are British and Australian. If you told them it was an I Quit match, they’d come out with bath towels and a megaphone thinking it was a Rat Tail Whipping contest. AT: Ummm…..what? BJ: Fine so it wasn’t the best reference, how bout this one? They’re just frickin’ weird. AT: Works fine by me. Blake starts to paddle X in the ass with the big wooden spoon as X hobbles around the ring, dingo lawn ornament still in hand. Blake then hits X in the back after he hobbles for a bit. X leans forward chest first as he cringes in pain. He goes on his hands and knees and sees the trash can on the ground. Blake goes to whack him again, but X meets Blake’s twig and berries with his foot. Blake hunches over in pain and drops the big wooden spoon. X then turns around and slams the wire garbage can over Blake’s head. Blake falls to the mat, but bounces up as the trash can slams into his head once he connects with the mat. Blake starts to stumble around as X picks up the dingo lawn ornament again and winds it up like a baseball bat and swings and connects with Blake’s head a couple of times. After about 6 shots to the head, the lawn ornament breaks and Blake falls to the canvas. X goes for a cover. 1… 2… KICKOUT! As Blake kicks out, his head shoots forward and catches X’s in a headbutt. X falls backward and holds his head as Blake tries to get his head out of the garbage can. X makes his way to his feet and catches Blake off guard and nails him with the Migraine! Blake hits the mat hard and remains standing on his head due to the flattened base of the garbage can. X shoves back over and makes another cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! AT: Well this match has been rather unorthodox. BJ: Well like I said before, these guys are British and Australian- Barry keeps talking Australian and British gibberish as X goes to pick up a weary Blake. Blake then gathers enough sense to start and starts to slam his head into X. After a few shoulder shots on each shoulder, Blake finally connects with X’s head as both men fall to the mat. Blake finally tries again to get the can off his head and does so. Blake then goes to the outside and goes to his shopping cart. He grabs the beach chair and throws it in the ring. X starts to come to his senses and he picks up a coconut. The ref slips on a hat like the Skipper from Giligan’s Island and shrugs as the audience laughs. Blake comes in the ring and X throws the coconut at Blake’s head as it hits him “head on.” Blake stands up as he holds his head and X goes to hit Blake with the beach chair, but Blake kicks X and DDT’s him on said chair. X bounces off of said chair due to its meshy seating and falls back on the mat. Blake looks down at the chair and then sets it up in the corner. X gets up and goes to spear Blake, but Blake moves out of the way and X goes through the meshy seating. X backs out of the corner with the chair still seated on his head and shoulders. Blake goes to give X a clothesline, but gets clotheslined by the chair due to it sitting further out than X is. Blake holds his throat as he attempts to get to his feet and X removes the chair off of his head. He throws the chair outside the ring and takes Blake and goes to set up London Bridge (top-rope hurricanrana). Blake blocks and shoves X off the turnbuckle. Blake goes to deliver a Top-Rope Legdrop, but X moves and Blake lands on the canvas. Blake gets up and X nails him with a stalling piledriver. X goes for the pin. 1… 2… KICKOUT! AT: Blake and X sure know how to put on a show. They had a stellar ladder match and now they’re putting together a great No DQ turned Hardcore IC Title match. BJ: These guys are killin me over here. I haven’t laughed this hard since you caught Clay with your wife. AT: If you want to live through this broadcast, you’ll shut up. X gets up and goes to pick Blake up, but Blake nails him with a low blow. The audience lets out a groan as Blake tries to gather himself. He lifts X up and delivers the Crying Wolf (Unprettier) out of nowhere and goes for a pin. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP!!!! Blake looks stunned as he sees X shoulder drop back to the mat. Blake goes outside and gets the last item from his shopping cart….the bear trap. Blake rolls in the ring and sets up the bear trap. He then walks over and picks up X. He winds up X and sends him off the ropes, but X hangs on and sends Blake to the ropes instead. Blake runs right into the bear trap as it shuts on his leg. Blake lets out a horrifying scream as he tries to walk around and shake off the insurmountable pain, but he turns around to receive a horrible lamp shot from X. Blake falls to the mat. X goes for a pin. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner and new IC Champion – X-Factor AT: We have a new IC champion! X-Factor has done it! The first clean pin on Blake in months. Since August right? BJ: That wasn’t clean! He had to use a bear trap and a lamp to get it done. AT: It was no DQ, so everything was legit. BJ: DQ’s are in place for a reason. The Lone Wolf got screwed…..hey wait a sec……he cost me some mad cash….BWAHAHAHAHAHA! You got what you deserved you filthy Australian! AT: Well that about wraps it up for Vortex tonight ladies and gentlemen. Hope you had as much fun as I did. Till next time, I am the Atomic Tomato and for “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson, good night.
