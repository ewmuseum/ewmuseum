XUW Cause and Effect The pay-per-view opens with Mr Webster alone in his office, the whirr of a new air-purifier the only noise to be heard. The head executive of XUW seems troubled and stressed out. Mr Webster: This night is huge. Darkness could overcome XUW completely tonight and there is not much I or XUW can do about it. The Deadly Draw has AoD in it still. Somehow members of that stable are positioned to fight for every belt in all of XUW tonight. While tonight is a great night filled with action, the night XUW puts the fireworks into 2003, it could be a night where the darkness does not yield to the light in the morning. Mr Webster picks up tonights lineup of matches Mr Webster: I pray that XUW isn't in shambles by the stroke of midnight tonight.
