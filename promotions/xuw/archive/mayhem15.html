XUW Mayhem The countdown begins and XUW Mayhem is on the air LIVE from the Kipfer arena! Pyro hits and then an opening video sequence is shown hyping tonight’s main matches which is D26 vs. Admiral John, BC vs. Blake vs. Woof vs. KsC in a 4 corners match for the XUW Tag Titles and in the main event it will be HBK & Lopen vs. The Flashtastics! They lock up in the middle of the ring again and Esco now takes the control and forces Jack back into the corner. Esco then applies a half boston crab in the middle of the ring. Jack begins to scream and tries to power out of it, but to no avail. Jack won’t give up however. Esco goes for another clothesline but Jack ducks and as Esco turns around, Jack nails him with rights. Jack then runs off the ropes but is met to a knee to the midsection sending him tumbling to the mat. Esco signals for his finisher, The Beatdown. Esco goes to hit the pumphandle slam, but Jack escapes from behind Esco and grabs Esco and takes him down with a big German suplex. Both men are now out as the referee begins to count to 10. Both superstars get up at the 8 count and begin exchanging fists. Jack takes the advantage and whips Esco to the ropes, hitting him with a devastating powerslam. Jack follows it up with a series of clotheslines followed by a big boot to the face. He goes for the pin. 1….2….NO! Riley just kicks out in the nick of time. Jack goes to a corner and signals for the spear, but Esco moves out of the way and Jack crashed into the turnbuckles. Jack stumbles forward and Esco hits him with The Finisher (Rock Bottom). The referee goes for the pin. 1….2….Just then Al Crud’s music hits and Esco immediately gets up and goes to the edge of the ring, looking at the entranceway. Al then proceeds to lower the microphone and lifts up his leg and lets one rip, to the disgust of Esco. By this time, Jack has completely recovered and is standing behind Esco. He turns Esco around, kicks him in the stomach and delivers a big RipSaw. Jack goes for the cover. 1….2…3! Winner: Jack The Ripper After the match, Al Crud runs down into the ring and stands behind Esco. As soon as Esco turns around, Al delivers a kick and goes for the Mother Crudder, but Esco pushes him off, Al bounces off the ropes and Esco catches him with the Drive-By. Flex then gets back to his feet and goes to work on HBK in one corner by delivering a series of left and right punches. Flash goes up to the top rope and comes off with a crossbody block, but Lopen uses the momentum to reverse the pin. 1….2….NO! Flash just gets his shoulder up at the last split millisecond. Flash quickly gets up and takes Lopen down with another clothesline. Flex then sends HBK to the outside of the ring, as the Flashtastics signal for the Blinding Flash! Flex hoists up Lopen, and as Flash is running off the opposite ropes, HBK from the outside trips up Flex and pulls him to the outside. HBK then rams him into the steel steps. Back in the ring, Flash whips Lopen into the corner, Flash them climbs the ropes and hits a few punches on Lopen as the crowd counts with Flash. By this time HBK is back into the ring, and before Flash can reach 10 punches, Lopen pushes Flash off who is somehow able to stay on his feet, but when he turns around he is met by a big Sweet Chin Music from HBK. Lopen goes for the cover. 1….2….3! Winner: HBK & Lopen AT: Wow what a good match, the Flashtastics gave it their all. MM: Yeah but there was never any doubt, was there? AT: What’s this? Why are they attacking the Flashtastics after the match? HBK and Lopen continue to beat down on Flash. Flex gets in the ring but he is taken out by a Lopen Cutter. Suddenly Bob comes running down to the ring and clears the ring. HBK and Lopen then slowly walk up the ramp with evil grins on their faces as Bob looks concerned as he checks on the Flashtastics. HBK and Lopen pose at the top of the ramp to a chorus of boos as the camera fades to black.
