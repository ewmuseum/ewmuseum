XUW XPosure #16

16 Jun 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: XPosure - Number 16 A few local college kids run across the stage holding sparklers while selected audience members pop paper bags. The Ladies Auxiliary of the Local Lodge of Dingo’s plays the Xposure theme on kazoos, and the theme is either “Beautiful People” or “Fly Me To The Moon”; it’s hard to tell. The camera pans to the announce table where Atomic Tomato and Barry Johnson sit as the decidedly low budget Xposure gets underway! AT: Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, and to our dozens of fans in attendance here in the Kentucky State Arena in beautiful Frankfort, Kentucky! BJ: Yes, the beautiful state of Kentucky, where the first thing a woman will say after sex is ‘get off me, dad, you’re crushing my smokes’. AT: (shakes his head) And on behalf of all of XUW, I’d like to apologize for what I am sure will be many comments from my esteemed colleague on his many comments about this lovely state. BJ: If there is a stuffed possum anywhere in your house, you live in Kentucky. AT: We have quite a show tonight! Five stellar matches showcasing some of the hottest XUW talent! Tonight Bluto takes on Syco for the Hardcore Title. Sniper takes on “The Bastard” Al Crud, Hodgo will battle Laudust in a Hardcore match, KsC Trunkz takes on Woof Woof, and in our main event, Kata defends his Television Title against Lazarus! BJ: If you've ever been kicked out of the zoo for heckling the monkeys, you’re from Kentucky! BAHAHAHAHA!!!! AT: (looks at Barry and shakes his head) Let’s get underway! Sniper vs. Al Crud “Unsung” by Helmet begins and Sniper appears on the stage, smiling at the crowd pop. He makes his way to the ring and rolls under the bottom rope, climbing the corner and posing as “One Bourbon, One Scotch and One Beer” begins and Al Crud staggers onto the stage, decked out as always in his gray “College” shirt and carrying the remnants of a six pack by the plastic can holder. He makes his way to the ring as the crowd cheers and tosses the beer to the side, climbing into the ring with slightly less agility than Sniper as the bell sounds. The two lock up in the center of the ring with neither getting the clear advantage until Al gets a side headlock. He applies pressure to the hold but Sniper is able to lift him up and drop him in a back suplex. He goes for a quick cover... 1... Kickout! Al is quick to his feet and he charges, yelling incoherently as Sniper steps aside and takes Al down with a drop toehold. Al is again to his feet and charges again, only to run into a backdrop from Sniper. Sniper again covers... 1... 2... Kickout! AT: Al isn’t having very much luck getting any offense in... Sniper is all over him. BJ: Kind of like a Kentucky family fighting over the last Possum Nugget at a family cookout. AT: Possum Nugget? What the hell is a... I don’t want to know. Al gets to his feet and this time does not charge, but slowly approaches Sniper. Sniper tries for a lockup, but Al lowers his head and drives it into Sniper’s midsection, knocking him to the mat. Al leaps for an elbow drop, nailing Sniper dead on. He covers but Sniper kicks out before the ref even gets in position to count. Al backs off as Sniper gets to his feet, then charges, hitting a clothesline and knocking Sniper to the mat. He follows with yet another elbow drop and quickly gets to his feet, coming off the ropes and leaps in the air, landing on Snipers chest. The crowd goes un-naturally silent, awaiting what they know is coming as a sound not unlike a foghorn emanates from Al. AT: ACK! Scent Of Victory from Al Crud! BJ: Whew! If I could bottle that stench I could make a bundle in this state selling it at the Piggly-Wiggly. Snipers eyes go wide as he realizes Al has just floated an air biscuit while sitting on him. The ref, after pulling his nose in his shirt, slides in to count.... 1... 2... Kickout, with authority. Al goes flying as Sniper quickly gets to his feet, a look of pure disgust on his face. He comes off the ropes and hits Al with the Brick Wall, knocking him to the mat. He bends over and picks Al up by his hair, grabbing him by his neck and chokeslamming him to the mat. Sniper covers... 1... 2... 3! Winner by Pinfall: Sniper AT: Sniper made quick work of Al... I guess he didn’t appreciate the Scent of Victory. BJ: Can you blame him? I can still smell it. Smells like a Kentucky Prom in here. AT: Things have been heating up lately here in XUW... seems that NFM may be about to make his long awaited return. BJ: Did he go somewhere? Where was the sword freak? AT: Yeah, well maybe he can get some sort of direction to Fear, who seems a bit discombobulated since their fearless leader took off. Let’s get to out next match! Hardcore Title Match: Syco(c) vs Bluto “Fight For Your Right” begins to play and Bluto steps onto the stage with a golf club in his hand as the crowd cheers. He walks down to the ring and tosses the club in, climbing in after it. He picks the club up and stands waiting, looking towards the ramp as “This Is Not” begins and Syco appears, dragging the belt behind him. He drops the belt and beelines for the ring as Bluto tosses the club to the side and climbs out. The bell sounds as the two charge each other and hit a double clothesline and both are down on the ramp. AT: Double clothesline and both are down! BJ: Good, Pomegranate... make sure you explain things to these Kentucky people. They’re not the brightest bunch of bulbs in the tree. The ref comes to the outside and begins to count. He gets to four and the both get slowly to their feet. Bluto gets to his first and scoops up Syco, slamming him to the ramp. He goes for a cover... 1... Kickout! Bluto tries to pull Syco to his feet but as he gets Syco to his knees Syco knocks his hand away and hits a lowblow on Bluto that sends him to his knees. Syco gets to his feet and grabs Bluto by the hair, landing several hard punches before pulling him to his feet. He brings Bluto back to the ring and goes to slam him into the steps but Bluto gets his hand up and blocks, reversing the move and slamming Syco into the steps. He grabs Syco and rolls him into the ring and climbs onto the apron, and Syco, in a pure desperation move, gets to his feet and hits a dropkick, knocking him to the floor. Syco climbs the turnbuckle and, as Bluto gets to his feet, he leaps for a cross body. Bluto was playing possum, though, and catches him in the air, powerslamming him to the mat. Bluto covers... 1... 2... Kickout! AT: Incredible heart from Syco! He barely got his shoulder up! Bluto drags Syco by his hair to the announce table and smiles to the crowd. He sets Syco for a piledriver, but Syco reverses, turning it into a backdrop that sends Bluto crashing through the announce table. BJ: Watch it! We don’t speak Spanish, you know! Syco covers amidst the wreckage... 1... 2... 3! Winner by Pinfall and Still Hardcore Champion: Syco AT: You know, Barry, this past week has shown DJ and Sev apparently taking sums of money from other people in XUW... what kind of racket do they have going? BJ: Blackmail? Maybe they have pics of some of the XUW roster in compromising positions with their siblings? Although here in Kentucky, that’s perfectly acceptable... OUCH! AT has just smacked BJ in the back of the head with a clipboard. BJ gives him a hurt look that AT ignores. AT: Something is definitely going on, and I’m sure that we’ll learn more in the weeks ahead. Now, let’s get back to the action. Folks, we want to remind you of tomorrow nights main event on Mayhem... we have our former Commissioner Admiral John challenging Bob for the World Title. AJ has never held a title in XUW and has decided that needs to end. BJ: Yeah, well the Pony has to go through Bob to get to his ever elusive gold, and between you and me, I doubt that’ll happen. AT: Well, you never know in XUW. Staying in the Hardcore mood, let’s bring on another Hardcore match! Hardcore Match: Hodgo vs. Laudust [hardcore match] “Anarchy in the UK” begins as Hodgo comes to the stage to a monster heat. He takes it in, smiling to himself, and begins to make his way to the ring as Laudust suddenly charges him from behind, hitting a clothesline and knocking him to the ramp. Laudust picks Hodgo up by his hair and drags him backstage. A conveniently place camera picks up the action backstage as the two emerge from the curtain. Laudust drags Hodgo to a nearby equipment crate and tries to shove him headfirst into it. Hodgo blocks the attempt and throws an elbow, catching Laudust flush in the chin and knocking him back a few steps. Hodgo picks the crate up and throws it at Laudust, who ducks in time as it smashes to the ground. As Laudust stands back up, Hodgo picks up a nearby 2x4 and hits him full in the chest with it, breaking it in half and knocking Laudust to the ground. AT: Wow! Hodgo is all over Laudust! He looks incredible! BJ: For once, Cherry, I agree with you... he could give Syco a run for the title. Hodgo pulls Laudust to his feet and brings him over to a large plate glass window that leads to the small arena cafeteria. He lands one more hard punch, dazing Laudust, then steps back, landing a hard superkick that sends Laudust flying into the window, which shatters as he flies through it and into the cafeteria. He lands on a table, which also shatters. Hodgo climbs through the window and covers Laudust as the ref comes for the count... 1... 2... 3! Winner by Pinfall: Hodgo BJ: Holy sh*t! That was brutal! AT: Yes, it was... Hodgo dominated Laudust in that match. I think Syco needs to very carefully watch what went on here tonight. At least there’s no debris in the ring to clear up since, well, they never made it that far. So before BJ can crack another comment that will make me have to apologize yet again, we want to let you all in on the condition of Jack... as you remember, he was injured at our last PPV The Big Brawl. XUW Management has heard from the hospital he’s staying in and was told that despite intensive therapy, he still has made little improvement. Our thoughts definitely with him as... BJ: Hey, get all touchy-feely on your own time, Avocado. Most of these fans don’t understand big words anyway. Let’s move on to the next match. Woof w/Grand Wizard vs. KsC Trunkz “Big Bad Moon” begins and the crowd heat soon overpowers the music as Woof and Grand Wizard appear on stage. They make their way straight to the ring and climb in, then turn and stare back up the ramp as their music fades, only to be replaced with “Umali's Bar” and the appearance of KsC Trunkz. He stands on the stage as Grand Wizard exits the ring, then, flashing the sign of the Horsemen as he makes his way to the ring. He climbs the apron and turns to the crowd, and in a flash Woof is on him. Woof grabs a handful of his hair and drags him into the ring, delivering a hard elbow to his head and knocking him down. He follows with a fast elbow drop, then another. He pulls KsC to his , scoops him up and lands The Woofinator. He again pulls KsC to his feet and, as Grand Wizard cheers him on, hits a Wag the Dog. He covers... 1... 2... 3! Winner by Pinfall: Woof Woof As Woof celebrates over KsC’s prone form, Grand Wizard grabs a mic... Grand Wizard: And yet again, the Grand Wizard Order continues to dominate the Horsemen. What you see here is just the beginning of the total dominance we will continue to exert. We will go through the Horsemen one by... He is interrupted as “Change In The House Of Flies” starts. He looks to the ramp, then without another word, leaves the ring and exits through the crowd with Woof in pursuit. AT: You see that? Grand Wizard is all talk. He talked all sorts of smack and as soon as it seemed that Kata was going to shut him up, he books. BJ: That’s because, unlike everyone in this arena, he’s smart. He knows now is not the time so he’s holding his cards until it is. AT: Who did I piss off to get stuck with you as a partner? Well, having Kata’s music playing is a nice segue into our main event, as Kata puts his TV Title on the line against Lazarus. TV Title Match: Kata(c) vs. Lazarus As Kata’s music continues to play he appears on the stage, flashing the sign of the Horsemen with one hand and hoisting the belt with his other hand. He makes his way to the ring and leaps to the apron, then climbs the turnbuckle and poses as the flashbulbs go off. “Sinner” begins to play and Lazarus appears on the stage to a decent heat from the crowd. He wastes no time as he makes his way to the ring and slides under the rope. He charges Kata and the two lockup as the bell sounds. Kata gets the upper hand, locking on a side headlock which tries to reverse by lifting Kata up into a back suplex. Kata flips over and deftly lands on his feet and as Lazarus turns Kata hits a hard clothesline and covers... 1... Kickout! Kata rolls backwards and springs to his feet as Lazarus gets to his. The two circle warily, then slowly extend their fists, touching them briefly in a shake. AT: Rare show of respect from these two... Lazarus grabs Kata’s hand and pulls him forward, hitting a short clothesline and knocking him to his back. Lazarus follows with a legdrop and goes for a cover... 1... 2... Kickout! Lazarus pulls Kata to his feet and bodyslams him to the mat, pulling him up by his hair and going for another slam but Kata slides off his back and rolls Lazarus into a schoolboy... 1... 2... Kickout! Kata runs for the ropes and as he charges Lazarus ducks and sends Kata over the ropes in a bodydrop. Kata flips in the air and manages to land on his feet and as he turns to get back in the ring Lazarus hits a baseball slide that knocks Kata back to the ramp. AT: Incredible display from both of these performers! BJ: Again, Potato, I have to agree... these are two of the best performers XUW has to offer. The ref has begun to count Kata out and gets as far as 7 before Kata gets to the apron. Lazarus is on him in a second and locks on a suplex, flipping him over and planting Kata hard. He again covers... 1... 2... Kickout! But Barely! Lazarus pulls Kata to his feet and gestures to the ropes. He flings Kata to the ropes and as he comes back Lazarus catches him in a Fall From Grace, then covers... 1... 2... 3! Winner and NEW Television Champion: Lazarus AT: Unbelievable! Lazarus has pulled of an upset and captured the TV Title! The Horsemen have lost another title! BC: I think that this may have been intentional on Kata’s part... we’ve all seen the tension between BC and Kata lately... maybe now Kata can concentrate on settling his differences with BC. AT: Things are definitely looking bad for the Horsemen... they were 0 and 2 tonight, and after the beating KsC took, who knows when we’ll see him again? Well, for XUW Xposure, this is Atomic Tomato and Mr. Majestic, wishing you good night, and we’ll see you tomorrow at Mayhem!
