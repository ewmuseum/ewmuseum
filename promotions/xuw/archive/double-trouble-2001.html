XUW Double Trouble

5 Nov 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Double Trouble Pyros erupt from the stage to the ring, Music blares from the speakers, the crowd is deafening. Atomic TomatoWelcome to the Dedman Dome! Tonight we have quite an event! Hi I'm Atomic Tomato joined, as always by Mr. Mikey. We have two main events tonight, first will be WARGAMES XUW style! Diablos and his Horsemen, versus the incomparible Heartbreak Kid and Exodus. Mr Mikey: : Then The XUW champ Mikey will defend his title from the submission master LanceStormDBC! Queen's "One Vision"  hits the speakers and Blitzwing comes out to the stage. Blitzwing: "Later tonight, Jobber was to take on Kata Hajime and I. However, since he has yet to find a partner, this match may not happen!" Jobber appears on the UltiTron. Jobber: Oh Blitz***t, I have a partner alright, he's running a little late, but he will be here! Your pampered ass is finished!" MM: Well looks as if things are underway! AT: Our first match will be Henrick and Hodgo versus Flash and Flex! Flashtastics versus Henrik the King and Hodgo Referee: Brad Bice Flashtastics music hits and Flash, Flex, and Naron come down to the ring. The crowd is content with the tag teams debut on a PPV. The King and Hodgo are right behind them, crushing any left over cheers. Ding Ding Ding Hodgo and Flex start first. They lock up, and Hodgo slips into a headlock on Flex. Flex reverses with a suplex. Flex drops an elbow, two, three elbows to the chest of Hodgo. Flex tags Flash and they begin the double team. They pick up Hodgo and double-powerbomb him. Then they go after Henrick. Henrick jumps down from the corner and grabs a chair. The Flashtastics then pose for the crowd and give kudos to Naron. Henrik slides into the ring and grabs Hodgo and pulls him out. Henrik then goes back in to take Flash on. Flash gets and early advantage, with a quickly applied armbar, Henrick twists out and dropkicks Flash after the rope toss. Henrick then begins shouting unsavory things at Naron. Naron waits till he gets close and nails him in the nads. Flex climbs to the top rope and drops a flying elbow. He then applies the Torture Rack until Henrik taps. Bice calls it! Winner: The Flashtastics Henrik gets up and looks for Hodgo. AT: Hodgo left before the match was over! What's going on here??? MM: I don't know but the King doesn't look happy. AT: ...damn things getting wierd around here. Next up is a battle of the insane, or damn near insane. JP and DDT both have disorders. TakerRocks has multipersonality schitzophania. And KoRe...well he's just KoRe. MM: I think I saw JP Blood eating a raw cow heart backstage earlier.... JP Blood and DDT versus TakerRocks and KoRe Referee: Shooter NO DQ MATCH Taker and Kore come down the ramp first, and get into the ring. They pose and jump around ready for a fight. Kore as a chair in his hand as they continue to wait. AT: Where's JP and DDT? MM: As you said earlier Tomato, Things are getting strange around here. Quietly JP and DDT climb out from under the ring. They slide very easily, into the ring, stand up slowly and sneak behind Taker and Kore not moving. DING DING Taker looks at Kore, and vice-versa. Both have confused looks on thier faces until they are both bulldogged to the mat. Kore rolls out of the ring, followed by Taker. Taker and Kore re-group, and Taker gets back into the ring. DDT goes to his corner while JP and Taker lock up. Taker gets the advantage and he body slams JP. Taker off the ropes, big knee to JP. (Kore looks around ringside for his chair.) JP rolls to get out of the way as Taker go to the ropes again. Taker delivers an elbow to the mat. JP tags in DDT and DDT gets all over Taker until Taker escapes and tags in Kore. Kore spears DDT into a neutral corner, and then baseball slides into DDT's groin. Kore goes to his corner so Taker can hand him a chair. Kore goes back to DDT and reaches back with the chair to bring it down on the motionless DDT. But JP grabs the chair out of his and and throws it out. Kore and JP begin fighting straddling the ropes. DDT gets up and grabs Kore and slams the Shock Treatment on him. DDT goes for the pin. Shooter slides in. 1-2-3! Winner JP Blood and DDT. Backstage Jobber is seen. He is pacing back and forth muttering. Chase Wilson and Riley Escobar go to Jobber. Chase: What's up Jobber? Why are you sweating like a man on death row? Jobber: My parnter isn't here yet and my match is next. Riley: Who's your partner? Jobber: Oh you know Riley, that guy with the hair, and the thing on his head. Chase: Ohhh, that guy is your partner. That's odd, he's never late. Riley: Who are you guys talking about? Chase: Damn new guys and thier silly questions! Jobber: Yeah, no kidding Chase. Riley why don't you make yourself useful and get me some towels. I'm going to need 'em to get all of Blitzs**t's blood off. Chase: Good luck out there Jobskie. Jobber: Thanks, and welcome back to XUW! Blitz and Kata are waiting in the ring when Jobber's music hits. AT: He has no partner! MM: Is Blitzwing going to cancel the match? AT: I guess not, looks like he's getting ready. Kata and Blitzwing vs Jobber and ? Referee : The Hungry Mexican Mexican signals for the bell. Kata and Jobber lock up and Kata gets the advantage. Kata whips Jobber to the ropes and then clotheslines him. Kata then straddles over Jobber and delivers 10 punches to Jobbers skull. Kata then tries to get an anklelock but Jobber kicks Kata down. Jobber sits on Kata's face and tells him "Welcome home Kata" Kata pulls Jobber off and tags in Blitz. Blitz climbs the ropes and missile drop kicks Jobber. (HBK appears on the stage.) Jobber shakes the cobwebs out of his head as Blitz and Kata argue who gets to use thier finisher on Jobber. (HBK leaves) Jobber attacks Blitz from behind and gets him in The Walls of Job. Blitz almost taps, so Kata saves the day with a punch to Jobbers face. (Blake Wolfson appears on the stage and roots for Blitz and Kata) Blitz tags Kata. Kata goes into his submission mode. He throws Jobber to the ground and hits the crossface. Jobber slowly gets to the ropes. Mexican calls for the break. Jobber gets up and DDT's Kata twice. Kata tags in Blitz and lays down on the floor, dizzy. Blitz tries to suplex Jobber, but Jobber reverses and delivers one of his own. Just then Blake runs down with a chair. AT: Blake's going to help Blitz! Blake slides in and raises back with the chair, Jobber moves out of the way and Blake begins slamming the chair down over and over on the weary Blitz. Kata dives in but Blake turns and nails Kata with the chair. Kata rolls out of the ring, and Blake follows. Jobber goes for the pin. 1-2-3 Winner: Blake and Jobber AT: That bastard! How could he? Blake is a heartless cold, calculating, Bastard. MM: What I don't understand is Jobber is the one that put Blake in a hospital for the entire month of October. AT: That Bastard! MM: I'm glad Blake saw the light. AT: You sick f***! You are actually happy Jobber won this match? MM: Oh yes, my money was on Jobber the whole time. AT: (shakes his head) Next up is the XUW Tag Tiles Match. XUW Tag Team Title Match 3XL versus The Army of Darkness Referee: Brad Bice Iron Man by Ozzy hits the speakers and the crowd goes nuts. Tony and Big Daddy appear on the stage, and some wonder how it can hold all of that weight. 3XL make thier way to the ring as the UltiTron shows a close up of a throat being cut. One Armed Scissor plays. The Ripper and Cereal Killer come down the ramp, titles over thier shoulders. Tony and Ripper lock it up. Ripper goes for a sleeper, but can't hold on to the big man's head. So Ripper goes for his knees with a drop kick. Tony buckles. Ripper tags in Cereal and the two stomp on various parts of Tony's large anatomy. Ripper goes back to his corner as Cereal drops knees on Tony's legs. Tony crawls to the corner and tags in Big Daddy. Daddy fresh and vigerous grabs Cereal and tosses him to the corner. Daddy gets a running start and hits the Big splash. Daddy then steps on Cereal's throat, then applies some wieght. Bice calls for the break. Daddy stands in the middle of the ring and raises his arm in a choke hold, indicating a coming chokeslam. Cereal hobbles toward Daddy swing his fists. Daddy grabs him by the neck and choke slams him. Once, twice..... AT: Oh My Gaawwwd! Three times. MM: Damn, that must sting a bit. Ripper grabs Cereal's foot and pulls him out of the ring. Ripper gets up on the ropes and flies down with a cross body block. Daddy catches him and powerslams Ripper. (Raj Patel runs down the ramp) Raj grabs one of the tag titles and nails Tony in the head twice. Tony finally goes down. Raj than gets up on the ring apron and gets the Brad Bice's attention. Raj "accidently" trips him with a drop toe hold. Raj then nails Daddy with the Tag belt 3 times. Daddy finally drops. Raj then drags Ripper over and lays him over Daddy. Raj helps Brad to his feet and points out that Daddy is pinned. 1-2-3. Winner : The Army of Darkness. AT: THAT was highway robbery. MM: No, that was an excellent effort by the Army. They dominated that match. AT: Next up is the Intercontental Title Match. XUW Intercontental Title match Samurai Bob vs. Woof Woof. Referee: Shooter F**k That by Kid Rock hits the speakers and the crowd roars. Bob comes out and struts to the ring. Weezers "Hash pipe" plays, Woof Woof and Naron appear to mixed review. Bob and Woof go at each other immiediatley. Woof gets Bob with a single arm DDT. Then follows up with a standing moonsault. Bob rolls and gets to his feet. Bob hits the ropes and high knees Woof in the gut. Woof doubles over and Bob hit him with a swinging neckbreaker. Bob walks toward Naron and gives her a dirty look. Woof gets back to his feet behind Bob, and nails him with The Woofinator. Bob does not move. Naron calls Woof over and grabs his head, as if to kiss him. Instead, she drop down while holding his head slamming his neck across the rope. Woof is momentarily choked then spruck backwards to a waiting Bob. Bob applies the Skid Mark Chicken Wing and Woof taps. Shooter calls for the bell Winner and IC Champ: Samurai Bob. Naron runs over to Woof and tells him: "I don't need you to like me, I like myself just fine." Naron then turns to Bob and asks: "Will you take me back?" Bob just smiles and nods. MM: Awww...I mean Ewww. AT: What are you saying. I thought that was beautiful. MM: I find Naron to be uh...uh, hmm. Maybe I'll shut up. AT: THAT is the first time I have ever heard you say that! Hey next up is a very unique match for both The XUW Hermit AND the XUW Hardcore Title belts. XUW Hermit/Hardcore Title Match Lopen Vs. Lurchador. Referee: The Hungry Mexican Lopen comes onto the stage and is booed. Beer, hot nachos, soda, eggs, and small barnyard animals are thrown at him. Lopen spits out chicken feathers all the way to the ring. Lurchador walks to the ring with a decent pop from the crowd. AT: The ASPCA is not going to like that! MM: What a terrible thing to happen to Lopen on Free Range animals night. Lopen and Lurch lock it up. Lopen gets an armdrag take down on Lurch and then begins stomping into Lurch's ribs. Lurch rolls and gets up. He crosses his arms and smiles. The Horsemen come running down the ramp and dive into the ring. D26 kicks Lopen in the gut and KSC Trunks hits a missile drop kick from the top rope. Lurchador, Diablos26, KSC Trunks, Bionic Carrot, and The Admiral take turn beating down Lopen. The Mexican tries to break it up , only to have his arm, nose, jaw, hand and chin broken, his knee and shoulder dislocated, and his hair messed up. MM: No Hokey-Pokey for the Mex for awhile. None the less mex calls for the DQ. Winner by DQ: Lopen AT:Well looks like Lopens going to need help out of the ring, and Lurchador gets to keep his belts. MM: And Hungry Mexican going to have to eat his meals through a straw for a long time. (singing) You stick your right broken leg in, you stick your right broken leg out, you bring right broken leg in and you shake it all about. That's when you pass out! AT:Very funny. Next up is the first of two Headline matches. XUW Wargames is next! XUW WARGAMES The Four Horsemen vs Exodus A STEEL. roofed cage is constructed as scenes from Mayhems past is on the UltiTron. Handcuffs are attached to the inside of the steel cage one cuff locked to the cage and the other left unlocked. The sign of the Horsemen shows on the UltiTron as KSC and BC come down the ramp to a huge pop. As they get into the cage Loose Cannon, Severson, and Sue Wolfe come down. Everyone but Sue is locked inside the cage as the bell rings. A brawl insues as soon as the bell rings. A flurry of fists and kicks makes what you are seeing very hard to follow. KSC is pounding on Cannon, Then he is getting a beat down from Severson. Slowly it is recognizable that Severson is dominating. BC is on a rampage with Loose Cannon, and manages to drag him to one side of the ring. Here he locks his hand in the handcuffs, and continues kicking his face. Severson grabs The Carrot, and smashes his face against the STEEL cage. Severson then handcuffs BC to the cage right next to Cannon. BC and Cannon have been eliminated but still manage to kick each other. Admiral John, and shortly later, HurriSakie come down the ramp and are locked in the STEEL cage. HurriSakie gets control of the tired KSC, and the Admiral is giving Severson a hard day. The Admiral hits the Anchors Away on Severson several time, but Big Sev keeps getting up. KSC is losing fast to HurriSakie, and once Sakie hits the Truth and Justice, KSC is dragged to the side of the ring and handcuffed. Meanwhile, the Admiral finally gets some progress on Severson and has him almost handcuffed when Diablos and HBK are locked in the ring. The Admiral gets distracted by HBK and Severson gets out of the handcuffs. Diablos swings in and puts Severson back in the handcuffs and locks them. Then everything stops. HBK suddenly realizes that Sakie and Diablos are shaking hands. Sakie, Admiral John, and Diablos turn to HBK. They begin thier second Beatdown of the night on HBK. After a short time the three men begin congradulating each other. Everyone getting a pat on the back until.... Diablos26: Hey, where did HBK go? As the Horsemen and Sakie were celebrating HBK climbed the side of the 20' covered STEEL cage. He monkey-bars his way to the middle of the cage hanging just above the other three men. As they stand together looking around, HBK drops on top of them, elbows and knees out to cause as much damage as possible. He drops 20 feet and collides with a sickening crunch. HBK then gets up slowly and handcuffs Sakie to the side of the cage. Diablos is crawling right behind him and handcuffs HBK. WINNER OF WARGAMES: The Four Horsemen MM: That was a whole lot of buildup for a fricking cheat! AT: What do you mean? MM: Sakie, that no good turncoat. Traitor! You think he can hear me? TRAITOR! AT: I thought that was one of the greatest matches I have ever seen. But the next one is going to be good too. LanceStorm DBC is going for it all against Mikey in the XUW Heavywieght Championship match next! Backstage Diablos and the Horsemen are drinking champagne and celebrating. Women are everywhere, just trying to touch one of the Horsemen. Blitzwing enters the room and everything stops for a second. Diablos and Blitz shake hands, and then Blitzwing starts throwing money at Diablos and the Horsemen. XUW World Heavywieght Championship LanceStormDBC vs Mikey Stipulation: If Lance wins, Mikey must retire from the ring. Referee: Shooter, cause Mexican is broken...again. "The Fighting 17th" begin to play and the crowd pops. Then changes it mind when Lance gets to the ring. Acid 8000 hits over the speakers and Mikey appears. The crowd goes berserk. Mikey calmly puts out his cigerette and runs down the ramp. Mikey slides in the ring and Lance is poised like a cat. Lance pounces him with speed Mikey hasn't ever seen from Lance before. Lance stomps him, never letting him get to his feet. Mikey finds and opening and rolls away. Mikey runs off the ropes and clothesline him, flipping him completely. Mikey then picks up Lance and hits him with a double arm DDT. Lance tries to get away buy Mikey catches him and nails him with a full-nelson suplex. Mikey walks around Lance watching him writhe in pain. Lance seems to be holding his ribs. Suddenly Lance jumps up and russian leg sweeps Mikey. He then moonsaults Mikey and goes for his ankle. Mikey realizes what is going on and gets to the ropes. Lance goes for a superkick as Mikey gets to his feet. Lance misses and Mikey Legwhips him. Mikey then begins deliveing elbows. Lance rolls away and tries to get to his feet only to be greeted with a German suplex. Mikey lights a cigerette and stomps on Lance six times. Mikey puts his cigerette out on Lances chest. Lance is awakened by the smell of his flesh burning and give Mikey a drop toe hold. Lance then rolls him into a Boston Crab. Mikey won't tap. Lance then tries a figure four, Mikey gets the ropes. Shooter calls the break. Mikey gets away and comes back to DDT's Lance. Mikey sets up the McCorpin bomb and hits it. Mikey goes for the pin. Shooter slides in 1...2...no! Lance gets to his feet and whips Mikey to the ropes and nails him with a spinning heel kick. Lance then gives him a ten punch, pick Mikey up and whips to the ropes again. This time Lance goes for the Straight Shooter. Mikey struggles to get to the ropes, but Lance pulls him back to mid-ring. Mikey begins to scream as Lance puts all of his might down on Mikey. Mikeys fist are balled as he slowly taps 1.2.3. Shooter calls for the bell. Winner and new XUW Champion: LanceStormDBC
