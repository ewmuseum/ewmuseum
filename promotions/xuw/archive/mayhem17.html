XUW Card Archive

XUW Mayhem #17

16 Jan 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem MAYHEM 1 Pyro blasts, videos roll, the music - with it's indecipherable lyrics - plays, and the first XUW Mayhem after X-Terminated is on! The crowd pops big as we cut to the announce table...until they notice Mr. Mikey and decide to start booing. Then they realize that it's unfair to the Atomic Tomato, so they cheer...but they really hate Mr. Mikey...this goes on for a few minutes before it settles into a healthy mix of heat and pop, meaning one of the announcers better start talking. AT: Welcome to Mayhem in the wake of X-Terminated! XUW has been shaken to it's very foundations and... "No Quarter" hits and HBK, Lopen, and Blitz come to the ring with much posing and throwing of objects at them. AT looks relieved that he doesn't have to deliver the cheesy intro speech that was given to him by some hack writer earlier in the day. The three members of Exodus enter the ring, and a drink cup hits Blitzwing RIGHT IN THE EYE! He shrugs it off, though. HBK's got the mic and the heat starts to melt the steel foundations of the arena. HBK: Ladies and gentlemen...your winner...and STILL XUW Heavyweight champion - LOPEN! The heat actually gets turned up a few notches. In the audience, the new drummer for Spinal Tap spontaneously combusts. HBK: Lopen, how do you feel about your hard-won victory Sunday at X-Terminated? Lopen: Hard-won? There was nothin' hard about it...who betta than Lopen? CROWD: EVERYBODY! Lopen: But that's enough about me...let's talk about the new guy on the title scene...your TWO-TIME, TWO-TIME XUW Intercontinental Champion - WHO BETTA THAN HBK? CROWD: LOPEN'S MOM! At this point, some smark tries to start a "Mikey" chant, just to annoy Lopen, but it fails to catch on. However, an "A****** " chant does start. HBK: Thanks for you kind reception. But, Lopen, at this point...the IC belt just isn't enough for me... Lopen: It isn't? HBK: No...I think it's time I set my sights higher... Lopen: Like on what? The crowd start to pop a little, as they anticipate tension in the ranks of Exodus. HBK: The XUW World Heavyweight championship! So, as of now, I relinquish this belt to Blitzwing, and thus make myself number one contender to the Heavyweight belt. Blitz symbolically holds up the belt, the crowd pops in anticipation of seeing Lopen and HBK beat the hell out of each other. We cut back to the announce position. AT: What a main event! HBK vs. Lopen for the Heavyweight title! MM: Don't jump to conclusions...there hasn't been a match booked yet... AT: Yes, but...HBK's the Commish! He's going to book himself in for tonight...his ego's too big for him not to! In the ring, Blitz takes the mic. Blitz: As the owner of XUW, I hereby declare the Intercontinental Championship VACANT! As far as the world belt goes, except for previously scheduled title defenses - of which there are none - no one gets a shot at the belt until HBK - the number one contender - takes his! HBK: And...Lopen...I will face you for that belt...at...some point in the future! The FAR future! All three men laugh inside the ring as the heat builds even more, as does the pelting of garbage... AT: I can't believe it! Lopen was, for all intensive purposes, just crowned champion for the remainder of his career! MM: Well, who betta than Lopen? HBK: As for the IC belt, there's going to be a tournament...an eight man tournament. The participants will be - representing the Ponies - Sailor John, Bionic Parrot, and KFC Sucks. Representing the Commonwealth - Bailey and his Highness, King Henrik. Representing Exodus - Mr. Mikey... MM: HE MENTIONED ME! AT: I guess he eventually awards all his sycophants... MM: What's a sycophant? AT: Nevermind... HBK: Representing values Exodus believes in - Hodgo. And foolishly standing on his own - Dish Mine...or something like that. The heat is now unbearable...people are undressing...none of them very attractive...sadly. HBK: So, with that said...HIT MY... HBK never finishes his catchphrase as "F*** That!" hits to a pop audible for a one mile radius around the arena. Bob comes out on stage, and the pop gets a little louder. This also cools off the arena, and the ugly people put their clothes back on. Bob: So...let me get this straight...you screw me out of the title at X-Terminated, and now you're screwing every hard working SOB in the back out of ever getting a shot at Lopen? Well, you've made a huge mistake... HBK: Sorry, Bob, there's nothing you can do... Bob: Oh, but there is. See, if there's a previously scheduled title defense, it still must happen, correct? HBK: Right, but... Bob: So, since in the X-Terminated contract, the loser of the World Title Match was guaranteed a return shot on the next televised XUW event...this being the next televised event, doesn't that mean I have a match already scheduled against Lopen TONIGHT? The crowd pops huge. HBK goes slackjawed, Lopen looks furious, and Blitz is on his cell phone, getting in touch with his office. He sadly turns to HBK and confirms the return match clause... Bob: I guess you left it in just in case Lopen didn't manage to get the job done the first time. Big mistake, 'cause he won't get lucky twice. Now HIT MY MUSIC! "F*** That!" hits again and the pop is now audible for a full five mile radius around the arena. Lopen is fuming as HBK leans over and whispers something to him...and Lopen smiles, laughs, and the three Exodusians head for the back. AT: What a main event! Lopen vs. Bob, one more time for the belt, and the stakes are higher than ever! MM: I can't believe it...this is awful...but HBK will have a plan. He always does...just like his plan for Sakie in this Cruiserweight title match! AT: I'd been wondering about that...isn't Esco a little - large - to be considered a cruiserweight? MM: HBK has decided...this once...to bend the rules a little. Sakie (c) vs. SupaThug Riley Escobar Cruiserweight Title match "Renegade" hits and the SupaThug comes to the ring to moderate heat. "Stay Together For the Kids" hits and Sakie enters the ring to a big pop. No sooner has he slid into the ring than Esco is on top of him with boots to the ribs. AT: Come on...a two-hundred fifty pound man in a cruiserweight title match? MM: S***, dogg, Esco be flyin' all over this bitch, knaa'msayin? Esco hits Sakie with a powerslam, followed up by a camel clutch, but Sakie makes it to the ropes and the ref breaks the hold. Esco whips Sakie to one corner and follows him in with a huge clothesline, followed by a vertical suplex. Esco goes for the pin, but only gets a two. Esco whips Sakie to the corner again, but Sakie leaps to the top and executes a diving moonsault, taking Esco down! Sakie crouches, waits, and then hits a beautiful standing dropkick, knocking the Thug out of the ring. AT: Sakie will not be denied! He will not stop, regardless of the odds. MM: Damn, G, that s*** is WHACK! As Esco gets to his feet outside the ring, Sakie flies over the top in a Stoner Press, again taking down the SupaThug. He lifts up Esco and slams him hard on the floor. Sakie rolls Riley back into the ring, climbs in himself, and waits for Riley to get back to his feet. As soon as Riley does, Sakie levels him with the Sakbottom! He goes for the pin, but only gets a two. Sakie picks Riley up again, hits a Michinoku driver, and then goes to the top rope, signalling for the Joke's On You. Sakie flies off the top rope, but Riley lifts his knees at the last second. Sakie hits hard, and lies on the mat, clutching his ribs. Riley lifts him into a pumphandle, and then sits down into a fire thunder driver. He goes for the easy pin and the win. WINNER and NEW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION - Riley Escobar MM: You peep that, holmes? Riley just laid the Beatdown all up in Sakie's area, word? AT: My God...he actually won... As Riley celebrates, "One Bourbon, One Scotch, and One Beer" blares over the sound system and the Original Bastard, Al Crud, comes out to the top of the ramp. Al: So you're a cruiserweight, huh? Well, I guess maybe usin' whatever kinda measuring system they use in France, that's the case. But, if you're a cruiserweight, then I'm a cruiserweight. And, seein' as I beat you at X-Terminated, I think I deserve a shot at that belt. Al farts into the microphone. Al: 'Cause my name's Al and I'm an AL-COHOLIC! The crowd chants along with Al and pops huge, leaving Riley looking angry. MM: Damn, bruva, Al just be lookin' fo' Riley to break his punk ass off. I mean, all dem chickenheads be freakin' over Riley! AT: What the f*** did you just say? MM: I have no idea. Next up is some hardcore action featuring Jenna Jameson and Kobe Tai...errr...new hardcore champion Rob the Throb and newcomer Mickey O'Neal. AT: What did you say at first? MM: Sorry, thinking about a movie I saw last night. RTT vs. Mickey O'Neal - Hardcore Non-title Match "Whole Wide World" hits and Mickey O'Neal comes to the ring as half the audience runs to the bathroom, as no one know who he is. "Your Disease" hits and Rob The Throb comes out to a mixed reaction, as everyone runs back to their seats. As soon as Rob gets to the ring, he reaches under the apron and pulls out a bin of hardcore toys, a footstool, and a hardback copy of the complete works of William Shakespeare. MM: What an odd assortment of items... AT: And if there's anyone in the XUW who knows how to use them, it's RTT. Rob throws a chair into the ring, which Mickey catches. Rob grabs a chair of his own and, as Mickey rushes him, Rob slams his chair into Mickey's chair, knocking Mickey's chair back into his face. Rob jumps back down to the floor and gets a garbage can and a kendo stick. As Mickey gets to his feet, Rob shoves the garbage can on his head and upper body, and proceeds to beat the can with the kendo stick before taking out Mickey's knees with said stick. MM: I'm getting the feeling Mr. O'Neal didn't know what he was getting himself into. AT: I'm getting the same impression. As Mickey lies on the mat in pain, Rob goes outside, grabs the lid to the trashcan, the footstool, and the book. He slides back in, whips Mickey to the corner, and follows him in - hitting him in the gut with the edge of the can lid. He whips Mickey to the other corner, and follows him in with a chair to the face. He whips Mickey back across the ring, and Mickey slumps against the top turnbuckle, barely on his feet. Rob sets up the footstool in front of Mickey, grabs the book, goes to the opposite corner, runs in, springs off the footstool, and slams the book into Mickey's face! AT: POETRY IN THE COMPLETE WORKS OF WILLIAM SHAKESPEARE!! WHAT A SHOT!! RTT sets up Mickey for the Throbbing Headache, but then sees JP Blood jump over the barrier. RTT goes to the ropes as JP gets on the apron, and JP grabs Rob by the head and snaps him, throat first, off the top rope! Rob stumbles around, and turns directly into the lightning fast right hook of Mickey O'Neal! Mickey covers and gets the upset pin! WINNER: Mickey O'Neal by pinfall AT: That Pikey Hook is deadly! What a debut for Mickey O'Neal! "Rule Brittania" hits and Naron and Henrik come to the ring to a chorus of boos, except for some diehard Naron fans. Henrik holds the ropes open for Naron as she enters the ring, and then he takes the mic. Henrik: My loyal servants *cheap heat*, it is I, your king, here to ring in the new year with a state of the Commonwealth address! The Commonwealth is now, and always will be, the greatest and strongest stable in the history of the XUW! Regardless of what happened last night, we have the greatest hardcore wrestler in the XUW in Raven X! We have the most loyal servant ever - Bailey! The most beautiful queen and princess in Naron and Arianne! And, despite what the Ripper would have you believe, the greatest Submissions Champion - ME! Now, my darling queen is being exhausted by you insolent peasants, so... Horsemen music hits to a huge pop...not Bob-huge, but huge. KsC Trunkz, Bionic Carrot, Admiral John, and Sheri Dedmon come to the stage at the top of the ramp. KsC: So, Henrik, you're going to f***ing come out here and f***ing run down and talk s*** to the greatest f***ing fans in the world? *cheap pop* What the... CROWD: F***? KsC: I'm not going to f***ing stand for it...hell, WE'RE not going to f***ing stand for it! BC: You see, Henrik, you're not the greatest leader of the greatest stable in history. You're not the greatest anything. If you were one of the greatest, you'd be a Horseman, and not a jackass running around in a bathrobe wearing a crown. AJ: Admittedly, the Horsemen haven't looked up to par lately, as the situation with Flashtastic proved. But that has been rectified. See, Henrik, to be a Horsemen, it means that you're the best. It means that you're a leader. It means that you're a legend. And, when we were looking through the XUW roster for a man who is all these thing, there's only one name that fit... Sheri: So, without further delay, the greatest submissions champion in XUW history and the newest member of the Four Horsemen! KATA! "Gets Me Through" hits, and Kata Hajime walks through the curtain to a gargantuan pop - not Bob-gargantuan, but gargantuan. He flashes the four fingers and poses with the rest of the Horsemen as Henrik goes red with rage inside the ring. AT: MY GOD! The entire XUW has just been turned upside down! Kata Hajime has returned to the ring! MM: That's it...I'm not talking anymore... AT: It's about time... The Ripper (c) vs. CK - Submissions Title Match Everyone clears out of the ring, "Praise" hits, and CK comes to the ring to a chorus of boos and fainting teenyboppers. He gets in the ring and poses, but it only gets him more heat. He looks very confused, and then scared as the lights go out other than a dim red glow on the ring and the funeral march begins...breaking into "One Armed Scissor". Pyro goes off, strobes flash, and the intimidating force known as Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy appears at the top of the ramp. He walks to the ring unhurriedly to a chorus of boos - other than the rather odd Ripper Fan Club - takes off his trenchcoat, and climbs in the ring. CK immediately rushes him as the ref signals for the bell. Jack catches and lifts CK and hits a high-angle spinebuster. He then backs off, and as CK gets to his feet, the Ripper nearly tears him in two with a huge spear! Jack pulls CK to his feet, hits the Ripsaw, rolls CK over, and applies the Fear. CK holds on as long as he can, but taps. Jack steps back over the top rope, puts his coat back on, and heads to the back. WINNER: The Ripper by Submission to retain AT: That was...anticlimactic. MM: Under a minute... AT: Like him or not, The Ripper is a damned beast in between those ropes. MM: Damned may be more appropriate than you think...did you see Raj Patel at X-Terminated? Doggy Style (c) vs. The Flashtastics - Tag Titles Match "Thunderstruck" hits and Flash and Flex enter the ring to a subdued pop...Flash in plain black trunks and white boots, Flex in hot pink trunks with a rainbow stripe and purple boots. Flash keeps on looking at Flex like he's a damned nut. "Control" hits and Blake Wolfson and Woof Woof come to the ring to a chorus of boos. They enter the ring, look at each other, and then rush the Flashtastics on the other side of the ring. Blake knocks Flash out of the ring as Woof Woof goes to work on Flex. Woof Woof hits a series of hard right hands, and then a fast belly-to-belly suplex. He picks Flex back up, hits a PUNCH! and a KICK! and then a PUNCH KICK! combination! He hits the Doggy Style Driver and goes for the cover, but Flash breaks it up. AT: That Doggy Style Driver is such a devastating move! MM: If only we knew the proper term for it... Woof drags Flex to the Doggy Style Corner and tags in Blake. Blake immediately hits the Spiker on Flex and goes for the cover, but, again, Flash breaks it up. Blake picks Flex back up, hits a DDT, and goes for the Code. He locks it in, but Flex reaches the ropes. Flash has had enough of seeing his partner so abused, and rushes in. Woof re-enters the ring as well. All four men begin to battle - Flash is going to work on Woof in the corner with a series of hard boots to the midsection, Blake continues to dominate Flex in the center of the ring. Blake whips Flex off the ropes and hits the Doggy Dropper. He reapplies the Code in the center of the ring. Flash turn to assist his partner, but Woof Woof hits the Wag the Dog on him and applies the Woofie Tamer. Unable to reach the ropes, and badly beaten, Flex taps out. WINNERS: Doggy Style by submission to retain AT: Doggy Style can't be taken lying down anymore...did I just say that? Tell me I didn't just say that... MM: That's not how it seemed in that movie I saw last night... Lights go out in the arena and the countdown begins on the Ultitron...3...2........1 and "Late Great Planet Earth" blasts through the arena to a mixed reaction...a good bit more pop than heat...as Sidhe Nimh - in his standard black Macgear raver pants, Airwalks, Double O Halo bowling shirt, and "Preacher" trenchcoat - heads to the ring. He poses on the turnbuckles and get the mic from Spanish Jobber. Sidhe: Alright...lemme start off by saying that tonight, Bob claims what's rightfully his when he takes out Lopen for the strap. Sidhe soaks up the cheap pop and lets the "Bob" chant die down. Sidhe: See, Lopen still being champ is bulls***, and we all know it. There was a lot of bulls*** that went down at X-Terminated. It wasn't all intentional. Derick's injury was bulls***, but no one can blame AJ for that. Hell, AJ went to the hospital with Derick, so I think that's saying something about what it means to be a decent human being. But then there was the intentional bulls***...like HBK firing Diablos, the screwing of Bob...but that's bulls*** we all expect from HBK. Then there's the bulls*** that we don't expect. For Hodgo to attack me after the match...I saw that coming. But for him to not even show up until I'd been fighting two guys for almost ten minutes...that's absolute, total, and complete bulls***! What's more, he comes in completely fresh...and he's the one who gets pinned! Now, I'm not too upset about losing the match, since the way Hodgo lost is just too f***ing funny not to laugh at. But, basically, here's the deal. I'd wait until the Intercontinental tournament to take care of this piece of business, but Hodgo's not gonna make it past the first round, and I really doubt I'll be facing him then. So, Hodgy, if you've got the balls to actually take me on face-to-face, bring your ass down to this ring so I can hand it right back to ya. The crowd pops as Sidhe stands, facing the ramp, waiting for Hodgo. "Anarchy in the UK" hits and Hodgo comes out to the stage. Hodgo: So you didn't get enough at X-Terminated? I don't have to come down there...I proved at X-Terminated that I can beat you with no weapons... The crowd boos louder... ...face to face... The heat picks up even more... ...while you're at one-hundred percent, even when I'd been in that grueling match for over half-an-hour. We all know that I was the one carrying the entire match against the Hurristoners. We all know that I'm God's Gift to Wrestling. We all know... Sidhe: You're hung like a f***ing pimple and have severe genital warts? *huge laugh pop* Hodgo, if you're so damned confident that you can take me out, what's the risk in coming to the ring? Hodgo: Oh no...I have nothing to prove to these people. If you want a piece of me, you're gonna have to come up here. Sidhe: Fine. Sidhe throws down the mic and slides out of the ring. He takes off the trenchcoat as he stalks up the ramp. Hodgo panics and starts backing up the ramp as Kat creeps up behind him... AT: Look at the boots Kat's wearing... MM: That's the most steel I've seen outside of Pittsburgh. Are those spikes on there? Hodgo backs directly into Kat and turns around slowly. She goes to slap him, but he catches her arm, so she responds by kicking a ninety-yard field goal into his crotch. The men in the arena let out a collective groan. AT: What a kick...Mr. Mikey...Mr. Mikey? Mr. Mikey is curled up on the ground in the fetal position, holding his crotch and crying. As Hodgo slowly staggers to his feet and turns to attack Kat, Sidhe tosses Kat the coat as he spears Hodgo into the Mayhem set. The two men begin to brawl on the ground as security comes out to try and break up the fight. AT: I'm sorry about this, folks...we'll try to get some kind of order...can we cut to commercial? We cut away to an XUW Shopzone commercial for Hurristoner's Glowsticks, and then to Bob eating Campbell's soup...mmm, mmm good! When we return to Mayhem, Hodgo goes to kick Sidhe in the gut to set up for the Riot Breaker! Sidhe catches his foot, spins Hodgo around, and goes for the Cure but security and Kat pull him off before he can lock it in. Hodgo keeps talking trash, but the look in his eyes is a little less than confident. Sidhe lights a cigarette and stares down Hodgo as security drags him backstage. AT: We're really sorry about that...I think it's safe to say that Hodgo has more than stepped over the line - he's tried to end Sidhe's career on several occasions...I think we can all understand... MM: Understand? Sidhe's a maniac! He could have seriously injured Hodgo up there... AT: Ummm...yeah. Aren't you supposed to referee the next match? MM: Oh f***... We again cut to commercials as Mr. Mikey goes off to prepare for the next match. This time, we get Al Crud's "Stench of Defeat" t-shirt promoted. We then cut to a public service announcement from Sidhe and Kat about the dangers of drug abuse. Then a commercial for CK's CD. When we return, Blitzwing has taken Mr. Mikey's place for commentary. AT: I'm joined by... Blitz: I think everyone here knows who I am, Tomato. Fatbadger vs. Bionic Carrot Special Referee - Mr. Mikey Mr. Mikey is already in the ring. "Mudshovel" hits and Fatbadger comes to the ring to a big ol' pop, cautiously watching Mr. Mikey. "The Game" hits and Bionic Carrot comes to the ring to a damn large pop himself, also cautiously watching Mr. Mikey. Badger and BC shake hands, and Mr. Mikey signals for the bell. AT: This promises to be a great matchup of technical skill and good, clean wrestling. Blitz: Well, we're doing the best we can here in the XUW. AT: Unfortunately, Mr. Mikey is involved... Blitz: And what's that supposed to mean? BC and Badger tie up, BC gets the advantage and applies a hammerlock. Badger reverses into a hammerlock of his own, and then performs a headlock takedown. BC turns the headlock into a pinning predicament, and Mr. Mikey counts the fastest three in history and signals for the bell. BC shoots up angrily, and Badger is still laying down in shock! WINNER: Bionic Carrot by pinfall AT: That's what I mean! Mr. Mikey just screwed the fans out of what could have been a great match! Blitz: All I saw was the official making his count. It was an even, fair count. Mr. Mikey raises BC's hand, and BC turns it into a short clothesline. He pulls up Mr. Mikey, hits a standing Nail in the Coffin, and then throws him over the top rope. BC helps up Badger and raises his hand to a huge pop! Blitz helps up Mr. Mikey and lets him return to the announce position. MM: Did you see that? The Parrot just attacked me for no reason! AT: Shut up. Well...this is it...Bob's last shot. Bob and Lopen ONE MORE TIME! MM: Bob'll choke like he did at X-Terminated... Lopen (c) vs. Samurai Bob - XUW World Championship Match "F*** That!" hits and the house explodes. The pop is big like, say, Jupiter is big. Or God is big. We're talking a huge, deafening pop. Then Bob actually comes out and the pop reaches mythic proportions. He makes his way to the ring, shaking every hand he can reach, before sliding into the ring and posing on one of the turnbuckles. Something that sounds disturbingly like "Evenflow" hits and Lopen comes to the ring with the belt and is literally showered in trash - as in, by the time he reaches the ring, you can't see the ramp anymore. There's no trash in the ring, as no one wanted to disturb Bob's concentration. Head official Hungry Mexican signals for the bell and the match is on! A "Bob" chant immediately starts, and Lopen and Bob tie up! AT: Come on...I know I'm supposed to be unbiased as an announcer, but BOB BOB BOB BOB! MM: Shut up. Lopen and HBK have this in the bag... Lopen takes the advantage with an arm twist, which Bob reverses. Bob goes for the Chickenwing, but Lopen blocks. Lopen goes for the Lopen Cutter, but Bob blocks and goes for a full-nelson slam, but Lopen wiggles away barely. The crowd pops as the two men reevaluate the situation. Lopen holds up his hand for a test of strength, and when Bob takes it, Lopen rakes Bob's eyes and punches him in the throat. He begins to mercilessly stomp on Bob, picks him up for a neckbreaker, connects, and the tosses Bob out of the ring. Lopen follows him out and throws Bob into one of the corner posts. He then lifts Bob and drops him chest-first across the guard rail. Lopen rolls Bob back into the ring, hits the Backlash for a two count, and then sits Bob up on the top turnbuckle. He hits a vicious Super Lopen Cutter and makes a lazy pin. The Mexican counts...one...two...NO! Bob barely kicks out. Lopen pulls him up, hits another Lopen Cutter, hooks the leg, and goes for the pin again...but again, Bob kicks out just before the three. Lopen pulls him up one more time and slaps Bob. Bob goes down to one knee and fights his way back up. Lopen keeps talking trash and slaps Bob again, but Bob doesn't go down and seems to be shaking out the cobwebs...the "Bob" chant restarts and starts building to deafening levels... AT: I think Bob just woke up... MM: Nonsense, Lopen's about to finish him off... Lopen slaps Bob again, but Bob completely no-sells it and stares right into Lopen's eyes. Lopen goes for another slap, but Bob blocks and lays Lopen out with a huge right! Lopen gets up and is met with another huge punch from Bob! The crowd is going nuts. When Lopen gets up again, Bob kicks him in the midsection and hits a huge DDT. Both men are down, and the Mexican starts the ten count... MM: That's it...that's all Bob had left in him... AT: I'm afraid you might be right... Lopen gets back to his feet, looks at the fallen Bob, and goes for a cocky pin, but Bob grabs his foot and applies an ankle lock! Lopen struggles to the ropes, gets to his feet, and is immediately taken up for a gourdbuster by Bob. Bob goes to the top rope, signals to the crowd, and hits the Kendo Killer! He goes for the pin, but only gets a two count. AT: Bob may be moments away from becoming the new champion... MM: Not going to happen... HBK starts running down from the back as Bob sets Lopen on the top rope. He signals to the crowd, and hits the Saigon Snap! He goes for the cover...1...2...and HBK pulls the Mexican out of the ring and throws him into the ring steps as the crowd voices it's diapproval. HBK poses for the crowd, then Bob grabs him and drags him into the ring to a huge pop. HBK starts begging off Bob, but Bob kicks him in the midsection, hits a DDT, and then throws him out of the ring. The Mexican, miraculously, is getting back to his feet and seem relatively uninjured. Lopen has gotten back to his feet and goes for the Lopen Cutter as Bob turns around, but Bob shoves him into the ropes chest-first and, when Lopen stumbles back, applies the Skidmark Chickenwing in the middle of the ring just as the Mexican gets back in! MM: THIS ISN'T HAPPENING! AT: Bob's got Lopen in the Skidmark Chickenwing! Lopen is wearing a thong! This is great! The Mexican checks on Lopen...who refuses to submit...refuses again...and then, slowly, taps three times. The Mexican signals for the bell and the crowd goes off in a pop that is heard in the most remote parts of the Amazon. Pyro blasts, and Bob is presented with the belt with tears in his eyes! MM: That didn't just happen! AT: Yes it did! Lopen tapped out! Bob is the new champ! The first time the title has changed hands on regular XUW programming! The locker room is emptying...the rest of Titans, Inc...The Horsemen have brought out champagne...The Hurristoners...Sidhe and Kat...Al Crud's brought the beer! People are putting their differences aside just for this moment...Bob has won! You folks at home can't appreciate just how loud it is in here right now! Bob has done it! Bob is the new champion! We're out of time! My God...Bob has done it... A shot of the ring is shown with Bob raised on the shoulders of the Flashtastics while being sprayed down with champagne by Kata. Bob raises the belt to a pop so loud it subtly alters the climate of the world, making summers more mild, and winters less harsh. Fade out.
