XUW Annual Awards Ceremony The scene fades in on the XUW logo. Underneath it scrolls the words… Attitude. Belief. Desire. The scene then cuts to the outside of a building, but not just any building, because tonight’s show comes from the Kodak Theatre in the Hollywood and Highland Complex, Southern California. A venue that hosted the Oscars in 2002 is now home to an even better, more important awards ceremony… though hopefully a shorter one. The camera lingers around the edge of the red carpet as a sleek black sports car arrives and out steps former XUW Commissioner Admiral John! The flashbulbs pop, and the fans cheer. Next up is a limousine carrying the Ultra Violents. Another one brings the remaining Four Horsemen to the show, and then a strange green coloured limo brings Jim Ross and the Boomer Sooners to the red carpet where they are met by massive cheers and celebrations from the amassed fans here to catch a glimpse of their heroes. The Sooners enter the building, and then up draws the Limo of Darkness, and from it comes Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy, Sidhe Nimh, M V Pete, DDT, Kat and Sirius Smythe. Jack has the XUW World Title slung over his shoulder, and is approached by XUW Reporter Alex Jefferson. Alex: “Jack, all of the Army of Darkness is up for an award tonight. Do you think that it will be a good night for your team?” Jack: “The Ripper is sure that the night shall go well, as XUW sinks further and further into the darkness.” Alex: “There have been rumours abounding that Mister Webster will be on hand tonight to end the controversy surrounding the XUW World Title situation. Do you have any opinion on this?” Sirius Smythe moves forwards and answers for The Ripper. Sirius: “There is no controversy at all, Jefferson. Look who holds the World Title. That is all there is to it.” The Army of Darkness entourage starts to leave to go inside the building, as the final limo pulls up, and out steps Samurai Bob, with Numark and Naron. Sidhe, Jack, Pete and DDT looks back at the Usual Suspects and there’s a smouldering tension still lingering between them, before Pete turns and goes back into the building. Jack stands there a little longer, his eyes locked with those of Samurai Bob, but then he too joins the AoD inside the Kodak Theatre. The photographers go to leave also, until a small moped draws up, and Rujasu gets off. He goes to dash inside, but the doormen stop him and throw him to the floor. Rujasu: “Hey, I’m an XUW Superstar!” Doorman: “Sure thing, tough guy.” In the foreground, Alex Jefferson shrugs, and the camera cuts inside the building… …where we see the sheer opulence of the Kodak Theatre, with chandeliers, beautiful scenery, and a whole load of other descriptive gunk. It’s a big posh awards venue, and it is here that XUW will be presenting the End of Year Awards. Oh yes. The cameras pan around the huge room, seeing where everyone is seated. There are XUW Superstars past and present, most of them looking pretty excited about the show. There’s an XUDF table, where the Superstars of the Development Federation sit, faces mingling with heels and looking at each other, ready to rip each other’s throats out in an orgy of violence. Or so it would seem. Somewhere over to one side, in a dimly lit area is the Table of Darkness, where the AoD are sitting, looking menacing. The Boomer Sooners table is missing a few bottles of champagne, but there does appear to be a jeroboam of BBQ Sauce on there instead, which greatly pleases the fun loving stable. The entire roster is there in one way or another, though Rujasu seems to have had problems getting past the security on the door still. As well as current and former stars of XUW there are a few celebrities from stage and screen, and towards the back there is a sectioned off area where a few lucky XUW fans have been allowed to stand, probably after winning some kind of contest which involves watching XUW promos for eight days without sleep. The Dave Kipfer v Jack Nobbledy hours took the most casualties, so it was rumoured. These fans even have some signs, but they’re not very funny, and security confiscates them and hits them round the head with a large trout, just for kicks. So the camera spins and twists and then cuts to a less drunken cameraman. The new angle show the podium up on the stage, behind which stand The Atomic Tomato, and “Mr Majestic” Barry Johnson. They look resplendent in their tuxedos, and receive a huge ovation, as Blondie’s “Atomic” blares through the auditorium. There are two giant screens set up behind them, to act as Ultitrons for the night, showing highlight packages and suchlike. For the moment they have spinning XUW logos on them, like the kind you would find on a cool website with loads of animated GIFs.
