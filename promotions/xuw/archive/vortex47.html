XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #47

2 Jan 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Green pyro flies from the stage, and golden sparks shower down from the ceiling, the crowd goes ballistic, and the first XUW show of 2003 is underway! The camera takes in the sight of thousands of fans cheering and being deliriously happy that XUW is back on air, and ready to rock 2003 the f**k on. The signs in the crowd are many and varied as ever. "Is Sakie hiding with Henrik and Kata?" "I like sheep." "Insert witty and/or humorous sign here." The camera eventually leaves the search for a non-boring sign and goes to the announce desk, where the all-new commentary team sit!!! Atomic Tomato: "Hello there and welcome to the first Vortex of 2003! With me as always is 'The Majestic' Barry Johnson!" Fooled you. Barry Johnson: "We're back with a bang, and four big matches to really get the blood flowing. First off we're going to see Sidhe Nimh take on NFM in a hardcore match." AT: "You've got to question NFM's ability to fight in that match, as he seems to have lost his memory!" BJ: "Well, after that we're going to see a brutal four way Submission Title match between Raven X, Kata, DDT and Admiral John!" AT: "Any of those men could come out champion here, and with the history between RX, Kata and DDT, expect to see fireworks!" BJ: "Ooooh goody, I like fireworks." AT: "Third match on the bill tonight is a tag team match between the in-form Ultra Violents and a new team of Henrik the King and the Latch Key Kid. I think they're just set up as a one-time tag team, but perhaps if they succeed in stopping the march of the Ultra Violents then they might consider staying together." BJ: "Finally to round off the card there's the Intercontinental Title match between Sakie and Saij!" AT: "Huzzah!" "Fear" by Disturbed hits the speakers, and the crowd pops for NFM as he walks slowly down to the ring. He seems a little wide-eyed, surprised at where he is and the reaction he is getting. "Bound" by ... Disturbed then hits, and the crowd starts to boo for Sidhe Nimh as he walks to the ring with a steel chair in his hands and a grin on his face. He is accompanied by Kat, who also holds a steel chair. AT: "Both men with music by Disturbed, and it's a good description of their mental states right now!" Singles match: Hardcore rules NFM v Sidhe Nimh The bell rings to start the match, and NFM backs away from Sidhe, who advances with the chair held in one hand. Sidhe swings the chair at NFM, and then throws it at his face. NFM catches the chair but manages to just duck under a hard Roundhouse Kick from Sidhe. NFM drops the chair and looks down at it, allowing himself to get caught by Sidhe and whipped hard to the turnbuckle. The AoD member runs in and hits a Shoulder Thrust to NFM, then picks up the former hardcore champion and places him into the Tree of Woe. Sidhe gets the chair from within the ring and sets it up behind the head of NFM. He grabs the second chair from Kat, and hits the Iron Maiden (Running Dropkick of Chair to Face). The crowd pops despite itself, and Sidhe pulls NFM down to the mat, covering. 1... 2... SHOULDER UP! AT: "Sidhe looks surprised that NFM kicked out at all!" BJ: "Hell, NFM looks pretty surprised himself! He can't remember why he's doing this." Sidhe picks up NFM and hits a Snap Suplex, floating over into another pin. 1... 2... SHOULDER UP! AT: "Look at NFM's face. He just doesn't understand it at all." Sidhe picks up one of the steel chairs from the mat as NFM gets to his feet. Sidhe swings at NFM's leg, but the shot is dodged and NFM grabs Sidhe by the throat. AT: "NFM's looking for the Final Destination!" NFM picks up Sidhe, but Sidhe reverses it in mid-air and turns it into a DDT on to the steel chair. NFM is cut open above his left eye, and Sidhe hooks a leg. 1... 2... KICKOUT! AT: "NFM is running on instinct alone here." BJ: "Instinct may keep him alive a little longer, but it won't give him a win against one of the best wrestlers in XUW." NFM gets to his feet slowly, and Sidhe closes in on him. Sidhe goes for a kick to the gut, but NFM catches his foot. Sidhe goes for an Enzeguri, but NFM ducks and pushes the former World Champion away. Sidhe starts to run and hits the far ropes. He runs back at NFM and baseball slides through his legs. NFM is slow to turn and Sidhe hits the Brink of Twilight (Stunner) out of nowhere. Sidhe drops down for the pin, but sees a chair right there, and smacks NFM round the head with it once more for good measure, before hooking the leg. 1... 2... 3!!! Winner, by pinfall, Sidhe Nimh!!! "Bound" plays again, and Sidhe holds his arm up in victory, as Kat comes to join him in the ring. AT: "NFM could still take a lot of punishment, but he just isn't himself right now. In fact, he doesn't know who he is, or how he knows how to wrestle." BJ: "Well, when he remembers I wouldn't like to be the one who hit him." AT: "Nor would I like to be fighting Sidhe Nimh any time soon. He's been taking people out hard of late, and with his new found love of hardcore matches, perhaps Ric Austin needs to look over his shoulder." BJ: "Maybe, but I'm sure it's a fight he'd love to have." AT: "Indeed. Well, next up is the Submission Title match, but first we're going to go backstage as we hear there may be an announcement about Mayhem." Camera cuts to backstage where Danyon and Bailey, the New Royal Legion are standing around outside the office of Mister Webster. Behind them stand CK and Rujasu. Bailey: "Damn this conspiracy that's making us wait around so long." Danyon: "I know, how dare anyone make the New Royal legion wait for anything? They don't respect the greatest tag team wrestler of all time, that's you Bailey." Bailey: "I know I am, and they don't respect the best Superstar to have ever come from New Zealand, that's you Danyon." Danyon: "You're too kind, but you've been an XUW Tag Team Champion three times now. That's more than anyone else, ever." Bailey: "Thank you, I couldn't have done it without you though, you're the greatest tag partner a man could ask for." The camera pans slightly to the left, and we see Mister Webster standing by the door, talking to an XUW official. Webster: "Yeah, I'm making the Mayhem card right now, I just need to fill a couple of spots. It's time to give Dante and Kore a title defence against someone, but I'm not sure who to give it to." The camera pans back to Bailey and Danyon. Danyon: "You have the most devastating Spinning DDT I've ever seen." Bailey: "Your Cradle Piledriver is easily the best one I've ever seen from New Zealand." Behind them, Rujasu leads CK around to the side, and to the door of Mister Webster's office, as the New Royal legion continue to talk, oblivious to their surroundings. Webster: "I guess we'll give the title shot to the next team that talks to us." CK and Rujasu overhear the conversation. Rujasu: "Hey, give us the shot for the Tag team Titles then." CK: "The pain is a comin' to the Horsemen!" Webster: "Sure thing, you've got your shot. Thanks guys." Rujasu: "Thank you too." Mister Webster closes his door, and CK and Rujasu high five. Rujasu: "Woohoo, we got a title shot!" CK and Rujasu walk off into the distance, and the camera pans two feet to the right, to where Danyon and Bailey are still talking to one another. Bailey: "The New Royal Legion has overcome the conspiracy before, and we shall do again, as we are the greatest tag team in XUW history." Danyon: "It's true, we're the greatest." The two men turn to look at the door of Mister Webster's office. Bailey: "He's taking too long." Danyon: "He's probably hiding away, plotting ways to avoid giving us a tag title shot." Bailey: "I'm sure I saw Scott the Intern going towards our locker room earlier too." Danyon: "Damn this conspiracy!" Bailey: "We must not lose faith, the truth is out there!" The New Royal legion rush off back to their locker room, and the camera cuts back to the stage. AT: "Those two men are loopier than a bowl of Cheerios." "Tear Away" by Drowning Pool hits the speakers, and the crowd pops for Admiral John as he makes his way down to the ring. He steps between the ropes, and flashes the sign of the Horsemen. "Only" by Anthrax is next to come from the speakers, and the demented DDT walks down to the ring, whilst pulling the arms off a Barbie doll and throwing them into the crowd. He bites the head off the doll and spits it at Admiral John as they stare each other down in the ring. "Liberate" by Slipknot heralds the entrance of Raven X. He gets a pop as he walks down the ramp towards the ring, his eyes locking on those of DDT. However, his head turns, and so does DDT's, when... "You Know You're Right" by Nirvana comes through the PA, and the crowd slightly pops for the arrival of the XUW Submission Champion, Kata. AT: "Ever since Kata was revealed as the man behind the vicious attacks on Raven X and DDT the crowd have been unsure what to make of our Submission Champion." BJ: "Yes, and he's not come out and said anything to deny the attacks, or even explain his justification for them." AT: "Still, on his day he can take just about anyone in XUW down." BJ: "Will today be another one of those days?" AT: "It's time to find out." BJ: "Before we do, I've got some news that'll please DDT, because this match has had a NO-DQ stipulation added to it!" AT: "That's got to play into the hands of the Hardcore Icon!" No Disqualification Four Way Submission Title Match: Admiral John v DDT v Raven X v Kata © The bell rings and the match gets underway. DDT and RX both rush at Kata and end up colliding with one another, and then exchanging hard right and left hands. Kata moves to the centre of the ring and locks up with Admiral John. The Horseman whips Kata to the ropes and hits a Spinebuster. He lifts up Kata and hits a Running Powerslam with huge impact. Raven X and DDT have locked up in the meantime, and DDT executes a go-behind, then hitting a Release German Suplex. RX and Kata are on the ground, and so Admiral John and DDT start to exchange blows. AJ gets the upper hand, forcing DDT back to the ropes, but when the Horseman tries to whip his opponent across the ring, he reverses it and hits a Short Arm Clothesline. AJ is down and DDT locks in the Strate Jackit (STF). AT: "That's a painful move DDT has locked in, but AJ's still looking strong, I think it'll take more than this to make him tap out." Kata gets up and breaks the hold, before Raven X approaches him from behind and hits a big Full Nelson Slam, shaking the ring as Kata's body hits it. RX looks down gleefully at Kata's body, but then gets a Chop Block to the back of the leg from AJ. The Horseman keeps RX on the mat and locks in a Half Boston Crab, but before long DDT is up and hits AJ with a Clothesline, knocking him down. AT: "The action's fast and furious in there tonight." BJ: "Look, Raven X has crawled out of the ring, and he's got one of the chairs left over from the hardcore match earlier!" DDT picks up the Submission Champion and hits the Shock Treatment (Jake Roberts style DDT), but then as soon as he gets up he is on the receiving end of a huge Tomahawk Missile (Running Spear) from Admiral John. DDT clutches at his ribs and rolls out of the ring, just as Raven X gets in from the other side. RX swings his chair and connects solidly with he head of Admiral John. AJ drops to his knees, and another chair shot takes him down to the ground. RX sets up the Horseman with his head on the chair, and goes to the turnbuckle. He ascends to the second turnbuckle and hits a Flying Elbow Drop to the head of AJ. AT: "That's the No Brainer! Raven X is in a great position to win from here!" Kata gets up and starts to hit RX with some Knife Edge Chops, but RX hits a Knee to the gut and then whips him to the ropes. Kata hits them hard and then runs right into the Big Bang Theory (Huge Spinning Spinebuster). Raven X turns round, just as DDT rolls into the ring with the other chair from the earlier hardcore match. DDT just gets his swing in first and clubs RX round the head hard. AT: "This is turning into an old school hardcore contest here! DDT and Raven X have been fighting hardcore battles for over a year now, this is probably the oldest rivalry in XUW history!" RX stumbles, and gets back to his feet, grinning. He swings his chair and cracks it over the skull of DDT. The Hardcore Icon goes down to one knee, and then gets up, a huge grin splitting his face. AT: "Can you believe that these two actually enjoy getting the hell beaten out of them? Madness!" BJ: "I think you're right on the money there." DDT smashes RX with his chair, and then RX returns the favour, as the two men start to slow down, wearied a little by the chair shots they seem to be enjoying so much. Across the ring from them, Admiral John gets back up and whips Kata to the turnbuckle. AJ lifts Kata up to the top turnbuckle and then leaves the ring, climbing to the top rope and hitting the Anchors Away (Top Rope Atomic Drop)! Kata is down, and AJ quickly locks him into a Sharpshooter. The ref checks on Kata, who is weakening after the big move from Admiral John. DDT and Raven X continue to just wallop the hell out of each other with the chairs. AT: "Kata's weakening more and more, I think he's about to tap out." BJ: "Raven X and DDT don't even seem to be interested!" AT: "Wait, they're turning round." DDT and RX walk over to where AJ has the move locked in, and hit a vicious con-chair-to on the Horseman. AJ drops to the floor and releases the hold. DDT picks up Kata and RX grabs AJ. Raven X puts the Twists of Love (Rings of Saturn) on AJ, right in the middle of the ring. Over to one side, DDT lifts Kata up and nails him with the Creeping Death (Powerbomb into a Diamond Cutter). Kata bounces off the mat and DDT kicks him to the outside. Raven X tightens the move on Admiral John, and with no rope or saviour in sight, he taps out to end the contest!!! Winner, and NEW XUW Submission Champion: Raven X!!!!! AT: "We have a new Submission Champion!" BJ: "Raven X has got the gold." "Liberate" plays over the speakers, and RX lifts the gold belt high into the air, as the ref raises his other hand. DDT is stood over by one of the turnbuckles, chair once more in hand, and Admiral John slowly gets to his feet inside the ring. AT: "Look, AJ is going over to Raven X. I think he's upset that RX had to use a chair to beat him." BJ: "It was a no disqualification match, and I think that tilted the scales in favour of DDT or the new champion, Raven X, as they're both former Hardcore Champions." AT: "The Admiral looks to be getting quite animated there." BJ: "I guess he doesn't like getting hit by chair shots." AT: "He'd better look out then as Raven X just got the chair again. He seems to be saying it was in the rules." As RX and AJ talk, DDT comes walking round to meet them. AJ turns to look at him, and Raven X throws the chair at his face. AJ catches the chair, just as DDT swings his own and nails him right in the face, the colliding chairs making a hellacious sound. AT: "Oh my God! DDT and Raven X just took Admiral John right out of there!" BJ: "That's one of DDT's favourite moves!" DDT drops his chair, and raises the hand of Raven X in victory, as the crowd starts to boo heavily. BJ: "That's rare to see such great sportsmanship from DDT, and rare to see the crowd boo it." AT: "I think they're booing DDT and Raven X teaming up to smash Admiral John in the face." BJ: "Oh, they should be applauded for that. I always thought AJ needed some surgery anyways." AT: "So, what does this mean, are DDT and Raven X a team, or was this a one-off situation?" DDT and Raven X shake hands, and then leave up the ramp, DDT following close behind the new champion. AT: "Well, they say to keep your friends close, but your enemies even closer. Who can tell what the next twist in that tale will be?" BJ: “We’ll have to find out later, because right now we’re scheduled to have some tag team action between the Ultra Violents and a new team of Henrik the King and the XUDF call-up Latch Key Kid.” AT: “I don’t think we need to refer to Latch Key Kid as a rookie or anything like that any more. He’s proved himself at the highest level of competition here. He’s going to go far in this business, of that I am sure.” BJ: “He’s not going to get very far tonight against The Anarchist and Blake Wolfson, they’re on a roll.” AT: “That they are, but maybe the train is about to be derailed?” BJ: “Before this match starts though, let me add in a word about the next XUW PPV coming to you soon, from the chocolate capital of the world!” AT: “Bournville in Birmingham, England?” BJ: “No you dolt, Hershey, PA!” AT: “Ah yes. It will be XUW Cause and Effect, and it will be coming to you live from Hershey on January 19th! Now, that’s pretty soon so we can expect to see some major developments taking place pretty soon. In fact, I’ve received word that there will be a MAJOR MAJOR announcement on Monday about the PPV.” BJ: “That’s more than just a MAJOR announcement. It’s a MAJOR MAJOR announcement, so you’d all better listen up for that one, and keep those eyes peeled.” AT: “Quite.” “Heroes” by David Bowie hits, and out comes Henrik the King to a good face reaction. He wears his black kilt with pride, and comes down to the ring to fight. “School’s Out” by Alice Cooper is played, and the Latch Key Kid makes his way down to the ring to join his partner for the night. He shakes the hand of the King, and they await their opponents. “Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck” plays, and out come the Ultra Violents. They are widely jeered, apart from a small section of the freaks that call themselves fans of the Ultra Violents. Weirdos, scum of the Earth, just the kind of people that might get on with Wolfson and the Anarchist. Tag Team Match: Henrik the King/Latch Key Kid v The Ultra Violents Henrik and Hodgo start the match off in the ring. AT: “We’ve had a night of old rivalries, and this one is as old as the hills.” BJ: “If the hills are under a year old.” AT: “Yes.” Hodgo whips Henrik to the far side and then hits him with a karate Kick to the chest. Henrik is stunned, and Hodgo sneaks in with a blatant Closed Fist Punch to the throat. The ref admonishes Hodgo as Henrik staggers to the ropes and clutches at his neck. Hodgo just laughs and hits a Suplex. He covers. 1… 2… KICKOUT! Hodgo gets up and tags in Blake Wolfson. Between them they lift Henrik up to his feet and Hodgo whips him to the ropes again. The Anarchist executes a Back Body Drop on his opponent, and Wolfson catches him into a Sit Out Powerbomb (Three Cracked Ribs and a Punctured Lung). Wolfson goes for the cover. 1… 2… Broken up by LKK! AT: “That was nearly over right there!” The ref ushers LKK out of the ring and then returns to see Wolfson picking up Henrik and hitting the Warning: Causes Headaches (Brainbuster) in the middle of the ring. He goes for a cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! AT: “Henrik just reached down there and saved himself.” Wolfson brings him up to his feet and looks to hit the Crying Wolf, but Henrik fights out of it and pushes Blake to the ropes, hitting a desperation Clothesline. Both men are down, and try to get to their respective corners. AT: “Listen to the crowd getting behind Henrik and trying to bring him back into this match!” Henrik struggles to his feet, as does Wolfson. Both men crawl to their corners, and tag simultaneously. AT: “Two fresh men!” BJ: “You sound like you’ve said that before.” Latch key Kid enters the ring to a big pop, and takes the Anarchist down with a hard Right Hand, and then with another, and another. Wolfson gets back up but LKK is on fire and hits a big Clothesline to send the Aussie back down. Hodgo gets up, but LKK is right on him and hits an Atomic Drop, followed by a Big Boot to the face. AT: “The Cheese and Crackers!” Wolfson is up again, but LKK spins him round and nails The Dead Bolt (Scorpion Death Drop)! AT: “The Latch Key Kid is really taking it to the Ultra Violents here!” LKK picks up the Anarchist and takes him to the top rope, where he hits the Powerbomb Jam and makes a cover. 1… 2… Save by Wolfson! AT: “The Latch Key Kid nearly won it right there and then!” BJ: “Come on Ultra Violents!” Wolfson hits a Swinging Neckbreaker on LKK and leaves him in the ring with Hodgo, both men down on the canvas. The crowd cheer on LKK, and he gets up before Hodgo, cutting off the tag. The Anarchist tries to squirm through the legs of his opponent, but LKK catches him and whips him into the corner with Henrik. LKK tags in Henrik and they hit a Double Suplex. Henrik looks to lock in the Four Leaf Clover, but Hodgo hits him with a Low Blow and throws him back to the Ultra Violents corner. Wolfson gets the tag and together the UVs hit the De-Humanizer (Double Team Spike DDT). Wolfson picks up the hapless King and hits the Crying Wolf for good measure. He hooks a leg. 1… LKK runs in to break it up… 2… Hodgo gets in the way, and gets a hard Clothesline for his troubles… 3!!! LKK breaks it up a second too late. Winners, by pinfall, The Ultra Violents!!! AT: “Henrik was out of sorts tonight, and though the Latch Key Kid fought bravely, it just wasn’t enough.” BJ: “The Ultra Violents are unstoppable!” AT: “I wouldn’t go that far, but they’re pretty damn impressive, that’s for sure.” The Latch Key Kid helps Henrik to the back, and then the Ultra Violents leave, after celebrating with their few weird fans. AT: “Hmmm, let’s see, it looks like Saij v Sakie next, for the Intercontinental Title.” BJ: “That could be a good one… if there’s a brutal run-in.” Suddenly, the speakers spring into life, and “Song For The Man” by the Beastie Boys plays. AT: “Whose music is that?” Out steps Mister Webster, and makes his way to the ring, as the crowd cheer heartily. AT: “Aha, it’s the MAN!” Webster gets into the ring and pulls a microphone from his sleek black suit. The crowd dies down as it waits for momentous events. Webster: “Hello there XUW. I’ve just got a few little announcements to make, and I’ll try not to take up too much of your time before the Main Event. First off, I want to talk about Mayhem on Monday night. Now, I can tell you that I will be making a big announcement that night. It’s so big that it involves EVERY SINGLE WRESTLER IN XUW and if you miss it then you’ll be kicking yourself forever for having missed it. That’ll be on Mayhem this coming Monday night. You do not want to miss this show, believe me.” The crowd pops for the mention of a big announcement. Webster: “Now I’d just like to be serious for a second here and talk about a constant thorn in the side of XUW, and that is the Army of Darkness.” Big crowd heat for the AoD. Webster: “Quite, and those are my feelings on them too. Now, M V Pete recently locked the Television Title away in the Domicile of Darkness, and that’s quite a complicated legal situation. So that’s not clear cut and we’re working on a solution as to what the position will be going forwards. What is clear cut though is the World Title picture. Now in that respect Dave Kipfer is the undisputed XUW World Title holder.” With those words the Ultitron flares into life and we see the entire Army of Darkness standing there. Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy and Sirius Smythe stand in the middle, flanked by Sidhe Nimh and Kat on the left and by M V Pete and DDT to the right. Sirius: “You were saying?” Webster: “Dave Kipfer is the World Champion. You must return to the ring now and hand over the belt.” Sirius: “That’s a disgraceful way to run a business, young man. Here you have the greatest wrestler in your federation, and yet you cheat him of the spoils of his rightful victory. The Ripper is the World Champion, and you should make it official now in front of that audience right there.” Webster: “I’ve had enough of this Jack, you come down to this ring right now and hand over that belt.” Jack and the rest of the AoD just stand there and laugh. Webster: “I’m warning you Jack.” Sirius: “Look, Webster, Raven X was a perfectly legitimate official at that Pay Per View. You know that, I know that, Jack knows that, and so does Raven X.” Sirius turns to his right. Sirius: “Don’t you, Raven?” The camera pans out to reveal Raven X standing just to the side of DDT, wearing an AoD shirt and grinning from ear to ear. The whole Army of Darkness starts to laugh at Mister Webster, including their newest member, RX! Webster: “What the hell?” The AoD continue to laugh, and Mister Webster looks absolutely furious. Webster: “Right, I’ve had enough of this. Belt or no belt, The Ripper is stripped of his Number One Contendership. Someone else will get the next shot at Dave Kipfer, and it’s gonna be soon.” Webster storms out of the ring and up the ramp towards the back. The Ultitron fades out with Jack Nobbledy holding up the World Title belt and clinking it against Raven X’s XUW Submission Title belt. AT: “Wow, Raven X has indeed joined the Army of Darkness, but it’s not all good for them, as Jack is no longer the Number One Contender to the World Title.” BJ: “What does he care, he’s got the belt in his hands.” AT: “Well, not if Mister Webster has anything to do with it.” BJ: “Combine that with the news about Mayhem having a HUGE announcement, and we’re really looking at quite a stacked schedule headed towards the next Pay Per View, XUW Cause and Effect.” AT: “It could be one hell of a show.” BJ: “Okay, enough small talk, give me the next match.” “Stay Together For The Kids” plays, and Sakie comes out to a good pop from the crowd. The XUW Triple Crown Champion eats up the applause as he goes to the ring, and awaits the coming of the champion. “The Mirror” by Dream Theater plays, and the crowd go crazy for the arrival of the Intercontinental Champion, Saij. He waits at the top of the ramp as some pyrotechnics explode around him, and then comes down to the ring with the belt around his waist. XUW Intercontinental Title Match: Sakie v Saij© The two men shake hands, and then they get underway with Saij whipping Sakie to the ropes and hitting a Clothesline as he comes back. Sakie gets right up but walks straight into a kick and then a DDT. Saij goes for the early cover. 1… KICKOUT! AT: “It’ll take a lot more than that to put Sakie away!” Sakie gets up again and ducks under another attempted Clothesline. Sakie hits a Neckbreaker, but Saij pops up quickly and hits a Neckbreaker of his own. Saij goes up to the top turnbuckle and aims to hit the Funny Bone (Elbow Drop) but Sakie rolls off to the side and then hits a Leg Drop on the champion. Saij struggles to his feet, and Sakie looks to hit the Final High Superkick, but Saij ducks under it and as Sakie turns round Saij hits him with the Topsy Turvy Hurricanrana. Sakie is down and Saij goes off the ropes and hits the Ker-Splat (Rolling Thunder)! He hooks a leg. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! AT: “Sakie’s in a bad way at the moment. He needs to get some offence in here before he gets overwhelmed.” BJ: “He’s not looked his usual sprightly self here, perhaps he overindulged at New Year?” AT: “Well, he’s been known to be a stoner in the past.” BJ: “Saij probably overindulged on the good old BBQ Sauce!” AT: “Jim Ross would be proud.” Sakie gets up and locks up with Saij. Sakie gets a Wristlock applied, and then a Headlock. He moves this into place and then hits the S**ts and Giggles (Twist of Fate) on the Champion. He goes for a cover. 1… 2… KICKOUT! Saij gets back up and Sakie whips him to the ropes. Saij ducks under a lazy Clothesline and hits the far ropes, coming off with the Downtime Spinning Heel Kick. The fans go wild as they know what is coming next. Saij leaps up to the top turnbuckle. Saij: “Huzzah!” Crowd: “Huzzah!” Saij leaps off with The Final Huzzah (Swanton Bomb) and hits it straight on. He hooks a leg. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner, by pinfall, and STILL XUW Intercontinental Champion – Saij!!! AT: “Saij did it, he pulled out the win against the more experienced Superstar.” BJ: “Bah. They both suck.” AT: “Ah, shut up. We’ll see you all again on Monday night for the BIG ANNOUNCEMENT!” Fade to black
