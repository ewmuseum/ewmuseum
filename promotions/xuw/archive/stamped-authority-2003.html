XUW: Stamped Authority The XUW logo sweeps across the screen, and we see the words 'Attitude. Belief. Desire.' before they fade out, and there is a black scree. The calm before the storm. BAMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM!!! No flashy music video starts us off this month, we're straight into the jam packed Cow Palace in San Francisco, the place that tonight will see some of the biggest matches imaginable to an XUW audience. It's time for people to stamp their authority over the federation, it's time for XUW: Stamped Authority. The fans go wild, go mad, go crazy go nuts at the fest of wrestling that will be theirs to savour. You can bet they are holding signs, and many of them...
