XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #79

28 Aug 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The XUW logo flashes quickly across the screen Ker-pow, ker-bang and ker-friggin-splat, the pyro are firing off because it’s Vortex time! Not just that though, it’s the first Vortex since the Ultra Violents lost their Commissionership. What will happen, who is in control of XUW and what will they do with it? All this and less will be answered in the next few hours. What we do have though is a big arena full of fans, who are all still happy as hell that the UVs are on the way out of power. They’re so happy they brought a bumper bag full of signs for us. “Joey in XUW? Fuggettaboutit!” “The Ultra Violents suck ass!” “Bubba Bailey murdered my wife!” “…and mine!” “…mine too!” …and the old classic… “NO! I’m the father!” The pyros finish, and we are taken to our regular Thursday night announce crew of Lauwass and Mikey. Mikey:  Hello there everyone and welcome to XUW Vortex. We’re just a few days removed from one of the most important nights in XUW history, when the team led by M V Pete and Numark regained the Commissionership from the clutches of the Ultra Violents! Lauwass:  When? When did that happen? Mikey:  Monday night! Lauwass:  Oh, I never watch that show. Vortex is much better. Mikey:  Well then order the replay, because it was one of the finest shows in XUW history, and that’s saying a lot. Not only were the Ultra Violents defeated, but Rigor Mortis and Electra sneaked out a victory over the Hardcore Icons, and Joey Vendetti finally fought his way up to Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. Lauwass:  Go Joey! Mikey:  Well, tonight we will see if he can keep up with the pace, as he faces his first test in the form of The Fool, who is still searching for his first win since he arrived here too! Lauwass:  Anything else on the show? Mikey:  Well we can expect to hear from the winning team, and maybe from the Ultra Violents too? Lauwass:  Okay then, let’s get going. Mikey:  It looks like it’s wrestling time first up, because here comes The Great Sombrero! Indeed, the sound of the Macarena fills the arena, and out comes The Great Sombrero, followed by his wonder donkey and his bitter translator, Manuel. He reaches the ring, ties up the donkey and rolls under the bottom rope. As he does so the sound of “As above… so below” by Behemoth starts up, and out comes Bubba Bailey. The crowd aren’t sure exactly what to make of this superstar, who was recently released when it came to light that he may not have murdered his wife after all. Mikey:  Whether he’s a criminal or not, Lauwass, Bubba Bailey is one strange individual. Lauwass:  Even by my standards! Bubba walks around the ring, scoping it out, and then walks up the steps and through the ropes. Singles Match: The Great Sombrero v Bubba Bailey The bell rings and the match is underway. The two men lock up, and Bubba uses his strength to drive the Sombrero backwards. He then reverses the momentum and whips Sombrero to the ropes, hitting a Clothesline as he returns. TGS is down, but not for long as Bubba picks him up and lifts him into the air. He seems to hang there for a few seconds before Bubba drops him into the Abysmal Descent (Suplex into Spinning Neckbreaker). TGS drops down hard and holds his neck, while Bubba just looks down at him silently. Pedro starts to bang down on the mat, and it pulls the vision of Bubba over for a few seconds. Mikey:  What’s that Pedro doing? Lauwass:  Who knows, he’s a weirdo! In the ring, TGS is using this to his advantage, and he gets up, and grabs Bubba from behind, trying to hit  a Reverse DDT, but Bubba fights out and hits a regular DDT of his own. Sombrero is down and Bubba kicks him in the head, following it up with a Standing Somersault Legdrop! Mikey:  The Message From Hell! Bubba runs off the ropes and jumps into a Standing SSP! He flies through the air into the Led To Believe… … …but Sombrero gets his knees up! Lauwass:  That could have been over right there! Bubba staggers off, and Sombrero grabs him, hitting a Stalling Piledriver. Bubba shakes his head and tries to clear it, but TGS is right on him again, and throws him into the ropes, softly. Bubba hits the ropes, and TGS pushes him down on to the middle one. He signals for a big move and then runs off the far side, trying to hit La 051 (619)… … …but Bubba ducks it, and TGS is stood in the middle of the ring. Bubba hits a modified Driven To Hate (Cradle Piledriver) and Sombrero is down. The psychotic superstar runs off the ropes and… … … Mikey:  Led To Believe! Led To Believe! He hits his finisher, and hooks the leg of The Great Sombrero. 1… … 2… … 3!!! Winner, by pinfall – Bubba Bailey!!! Bubba’s music plays, as the ref holds up his arm. He just looks at the ref, and then walks to the back. Eventually, TGS follows, and the arena is left in silence. Mikey:  You know, I wonder what the new Commissioners will… BOOM! Right on cue, the new Commissioners appear at the top of the stage. There they are, Numark and M V Pete! The crowd pop like they were balloons with a sharp pin thrust into them, or if you’re into slightly more violent things, then they pop like a cat in a microwave. Either way there are cheers raining down on the two members of the Deadly Combination as they enter the ring, and pose for a while, which is fair enough considering what they have just achieved. Eventually the crowd dies down and they can speak. Pete:  Hello XUW! Predictably large pop. Pete:  Well, it looks like we have new people making the rules here. No more Ultra Violents, so let’s make a quick new ruling… …The crowd holds their breath… …the new ruling is that I get a World Title shot every week until I win the damn title! … … The crowd decide to boo… and heavily. Pete:  Oh man, can’t you guys get a joke? The crowd realises it has been foolish, and shuts up. Pete:  No, we have one thing to do as Commissioners. Numark:  …and that is to book the Ultra Violents in handicap matches every week… forever!!! The crowd cheers… a lot! Pete (laughing):  Are you joking? Seriously, we’re not as bad as them. Numark:  For a start, if anybody has a problem with us, we’ll face them ourselves, right away. The two men look around, but nobody seems to be dissenting. As they stand there, the song “Blank” by Failure creeps out over the speakers, and Sirius Smythe starts his slow way down to the ring. To cut a long story short, Sirius eventually makes it to the ring, and Pete nods towards him. Pete:  The one thing we need to do, as Commissioners, is something that every member of the team will thank us for. We’re going to give it away. We’re not going to become like the UVs have been, losing their love of the game, losing their skills and their minds just trying to screw others over. No, we’re handing the controls of XUW over to one person. Sirius is beaming with joy, as he produces a microphone. Sirius:  Excellent news, you have made a wise decision to appoint me. Pete, now even the sky is not the limit. Numark:  Wait up old man, nobody said anything about you. Sirius:  But… Pete:  Nu is right. Sirius, the AoD doesn’t need the Commissionership. We can dominate without it. Sirius:  But I want… Pete:  No Sirius, it’s not going to be you. Sirius:  Bah! Pete turns back to look at the crowd, as Numark takes up the story. Numark:  We were going to go and see an old friend, someone we knew could be trusted, someone we knew would be a good Commissioner for XUW. Pete:  We decided to ask Bob if he would be the new Commish! Huge crowd pop… could it be true? Pete:  BUT… we thought again. Bob wouldn’t let the injustices stand, but he’d get drawn back into the action, and before you know it, he’d be back in the ring again, and we knpw he doesn’t want that, deep down inside. Numark:  So, sadly, we didn’t ask Bob to become the Commissioner. The crowd sighs. Pete:  We did ask someone else though, and they did accept. Numark:  He’s another long-time XUW member, and someone of impeccable credentials and standing. Pete:  He’s a man of few words, but of great morals. Numark:  No man in their right mind would stand up to him. Pete:  Ladies and gentlemen… Numark:  We give to you… … … … … Both:  Commissioner SEV!!! No music at all hits the speakers, and the giant Severson comes out dressed in a badly fitting suit. He stands at the top of the stage and holds his hands up in acknowledgement of the crowd… who are going freaking ballistic! The monster puts his hand in his pocket and pulls out a microphone. He holds it up to his mouth. Sev:  … The crowd pop huge! Sev:  … The crowd gasps! Sev:  … The crowd cheers, as Sev waves to them, and leaves for the back. Lauwass:  What the hell was that? Mikey:  Didn’t you understand him? Lauwass:  He didn’t say anything! Mikey:  I’ll tell you what he said in just a second. In the ring, Pete and Nu are all smiles. Pete:  Well, that’s some huge news to be getting on with. I’m going to go to the back now and watch the rest from there. I’d better keep my eyes peeled… I’ve got a title to win sometime soon… hopefully… Numark:  Enjoy the rest of the show! “Deadly Combination” hits the speakers, and M V Pete and Numark go to the back, followed by a disconsolate Sirius Smythe. The crowd cheer on, and Lauwass still looks perplexed. Lauwass:  What the hell did he say? Mikey:  He said that he was honoured to be the new XUW Commissioner, and that he would do his best to keep the federation as good as it has always been. Lauwass:  Anything else? Mikey:  Yes, he said that since The Shadow was sadly injured at the Anniversary Show just this past Monday, he would be unable to defend the title, and it was now declared vacant! Lauwass:  Wow! Mikey:  Yes, and there will be an Intercontinental Title Tournament starting up soon, with details of it to be announced on Mayhem! Lauwass:  Excellent, tournaments rule! Especially when the bad guys win them! Mikey:  That’s not all, he said that following his heinous actions on Monday night in assaulting his team-mate, Dave Kipfer is banned from the arena for the night, and will be dealt with this coming Monday on Mayhem. Lauwass:  Wow, stacked show already, and we don’t even know any of the matches! Mikey:  I’m sure there will be more to come, but for now, let’s get back to the action. Next up we have the first ever XUW match for Joey Vendetti, as he takes on The Fool. "What A Fool Believes" by the Doobie Brothers hits the speakers, and the fans all get up and start dancing to the funky music (because, let's face it... this music is funky).  "The Fool" Ian Fetzer makes his way out onto the stage, accompanied by the irreverent Mr. Action Figure.  The Fool holds Mr. Action Figure up to his ear as if he's listening to the toy man whisper something, and then he nods his head in understanding, as he gets into the ring. Lauwass:  This guy thinks his action figure is alive, why didn't the Ultra Violents send HIM to the funny farm with Dante? The Fool's music fades out, and what certain uncultured swines classify as "soft Italian music" begins to waft over the sound system for roughly half a minute, as the crowd switches from pop mode to incredible heat mode.  After thirty seconds, "Square Dance" by Eminem begins to blast over the arena sound system.  Joey Vendetti emerges from behind the black entrance curtain as the XUW faithful shower him in a tidal wave of boos, hisses, and some other rather nasty words that shall not be repeated here.  Joey stops at the top of the stage, closes his eyes, draws in a deep breath, and lets out a contented sigh.  With a sick smile on his face, he locks his eyes on The Fool in the middle of the ring, and makes his way down the ramp. Mikey:  This is Vendetti's first official match as a member of the XUW roster, and he's certainly going to be quite a strong addition to this company.  I don't like that look in his eyes, though... Lauwass:  Leave him alone, he's just savoring his first-ever XUW match! The Fool seems to be rather engrossed in an intellectual discussion with Mr. Action Figure regarding global economics, when Vendetti charges right into the ring and blindsides The Fool!  The ref admonishes Vendetti for the cheap tactic as he quickly calls for the bell to begin the bout. Singles Match: Joey Vendetti vs. The Fool Vendetti whips The Fool into the corner and starts laying into him with a series of vicious knife-edge chops.  A few people who, for some reason, cheer the heels, can be heard yelling "WOO!" each time Vendetti's hand makes contact with the sternum of The Fool.  Vendetti whips The Fool across the ring to the opposite corner, and follows right behind, charging in and nailing The Fool with a hard spear, driving his elbow painfully into The Fool's abdominal area.  The Fool gasps as the very wind is knocked from his lungs, and he doubles over, trying to maintain his composure.  Vendetti has a look of fire in his eyes as he grabs The Fool's head and plants him into the mat with a DDT.  The Fool is a tad slow in getting up, making his way onto his hands and knees and shaking out the cobwebs.  Vendetti looks down at The Fool and laughs, as he grabs The Fool by his hair and stands him back up.  Vendetti strikes The Fool across the jaw with a left hook, then again with a right, another left, another right... then he goes for a haymaker to send The Fool stumbling back into the ropes, but The Fool blocks it with his arm at the last second!  Undeterred, Vendetti tries to throw another big punch, but The Fool blocks that as well! Mikey:  With Vendetti dominating early on in this match, it looks like The Fool may have a chance to fight back here! Lauwass:  Joey should have crippled that worthless jobber by now! Mikey:  It's true that Joey's tough, but you shouldn't underestimate The Fool. Lauwass:  Of course he's tough!  He could take on the entire AoD by himself! Mikey:  Well, he certainly has guts for seemingly trying to pick a fight with them, but that's not a smart move when you're flying solo in these ranks.  Anyway, The Fool looks like he might be turning things around here! Indeed he is, as The Fool has somehow managed to get Vendetti into a headlock!  He looks over at Mr. Action Figure in the corner and yells a question about what to do.  After a moment, he nods, and then gives Vendetti a noogie, messing up his hair! Mikey:  That's not really an effective move physically, but being given a noogie during a match can sure be demoralizing! Lauwass:  That rotten limey bastard!  He'll pay for that! The fans are all laughing as Joey breaks away from The Fool, his feet turning red in a mix of embarrassment and rage.  He bares his teeth at the laughing Fool, and then charges right at him, hitting him with a huge clothesline that sends the goofy man (no, not Vendetti, The Fool) spilling over the top rope and to the floor outside.  Vendetti rolls out of the ring and "helps" The Fool up to his feet.  However, The Fool is ready for him, as he suddenly strikes out of nowhere with a flurry of left and right punches, and the occassional kick to the shin!  Joey stumbles backwards as The Fool unleashes a flurry of offense, until they reach the ringpost.  The Fool senses an opportunity to strike hard, as he grabs Vendetti's head and goes to slam it into the steel ring post.  At the last second, however, Vendetti blocks The Fool, and smashes his head into the post instead!  The Fool grabs his throbbing forehead, reeling from the sudden blow.  Vendetti whips The Fool hard into the steel steps in the opposite corner on the outside with a huge CRASH!! Mikey:  Good lord, Vendetti is trying to break The Fool in half here! Lauwass:  That's what The Fool gets for leaving the ring! Mikey:  He was knocked out by Vendetti, he didn't leave under his own free will! Lauwass:  Yeah... well... he still shouldn't have done it! Back in the ring, the ref has been counting to ten, and is now on seven... as he reaches eight, Vendetti picks up The Fool and rolls him back into the ring, and then follows him inside to break the ten-count.  With The Fool being rather out of it, Vendetti sets him up in the middle of the ring, and locks him in his Submission finisher, the Blood Money (Haas of Pain)!!  The Fool has been thoroughly beaten up, and with the ropes all out of reach, he has no choice... ...but to tap out. The ref calls for the bell! Winner, via submission:  Joey Vendetti!!! Joey doesn't relinquish, however, as he cinches the hold in tighter on The Fool.  The Fool pounds the mat repeatedly with his fist, and yells out in agony.  The bell continues sounding for a few more moments, but Joey continues to keep the hold locked in, laughing maniacally. Mikey:  This is sick, someone stop this!!! Thankfully, officials begin to pour out from backstage and hit the ring, and Joey mercifully lets go of The Fool.  The officials in the ring check on The Fool, who is looking rather roughed up in a bad way to say the least.  The fans boo tremendously as Vendetti makes his way to the back with a smile on his face. Lauwass:  Now THAT'S how you make an impact in your debut match!  That's probably the most impressive performance by a rookie in their debut match in XUW history! Mikey:  Impressive my eye!  That was sick, vile, heinous assault and The Fool sure as hell didn't deserve it!  Vendetti is an evil human being! Lauwass:  I think your view of "impressive" is very narrow. Mikey:  You be quiet After that ‘classic’ match, we are taken backstage to where we see Enigma stalking the hallways of the XUW Arena. He is carrying in his hands a steel chair, and he finally founds what he has been searching for… the locker room of Kata. He kicks the door in, and rushes Kata, who was putting on face paint. Enigma barrels into him, and knocks him to the floor. As Kata gets back up, Enigma swings the chair and it connects with his head. Kata goes down, but that doesn’t stop Enigma, and he gets in another three chair shots before dropping the dented metal on the floor. Enigma:  Come then. Bring your Ashura, and he too will fall to Enigma. The camera cuts back to the announcers. Mikey seems shocked, but Lauwass is just chatting with a female fan over the guard rails. Mikey:  What a despicable attack there by Enigma. He’ll do anything to get an advantage! Lauwass:  Huh? Whatever. Mikey: You should pay attention sometimes… Well, either way, up next we have someone who has decided to return to XUW after a short layoff due to injury that occurred at the hands of the Ultra Violents.  Sakie is back for his first match in over 3 months. Now he’s back and ready to go again and prove why he was an XUW legend. Lauwass:  Exactly he was one.  Now he’s all washed up and stuff. Mikey:  Sakie was nominated for the Hall of Fame, that’s more you can say about your short lived wrestling experience. Lauwass:  I never wrestled….. Mikey:  Right….sorry I forgot that was Laudust, not you.  Two different people. Lauwass:  Yep. As the two continue their bickering, hardkore starts walking out from the back since he doesn’t need them music to come out to.  He walks down towards ring and sees a commie standing in the audience.  He walks over to where the commie is sitting and hardkore reaches in and grabs the commie by its hair and pulls it over the guard rail. hardkore puts the commie who is now kicking and screaming over his shoulders and continues to walk towards the ring.  Once he gets there, hardkore tosses the commie into the steel turnbuckle on the outside of the ring, knocking him out.  hardkore then reaches under the ring and pulls out some barbed wire and ties the commie to his back.  Hardcore then climbs up on the apron and jumps off doing the Commie Dive, pretty much killing the commie. Mikey:  That was totally unnecessary and wrong for him to do that to a helpless, innocent fan. Lauwass:  The fan wasn’t innocent, he was a commie.  He deserved it. As hardkore is untying the commie from him, the EMT’s begin to rush down to the scene a loud “BOOM” is heard across the arena and the crowd responds with a –ER SOONERS as “Stay Together for the Kids” hits and the crowd pops massively.  Sakie emerges from the back to a large cheer.  He has made his return to XUW.  Sakie takes his time going down the aisle, slapping a few hands along the way.  He slides into the ring and hops up on the second rope and poses for the crowd.  He hops back down and turns around but hardkore comes from behind him, nailing him a large lariat.  Sakie goes down.  The crowd boos and the ref signals for the bell to start the match. Sakie vs. hardkore Mikey:  And we are off. Lauwass:  This is a wrestling match not a horse race. Mikey:  Fine and the match begins with Sakie going down from a shot from the big man. Lauwass:  Oooh, nice call. Sakie gets back up as hardkore comes after him and swings for another lariat but Sakie is able to duck this one.  Sakie kicks hardkore in the back a few times and nails a couple of forearms as well.  Sakie runs off to the other side of the ropes to get more speed and comes off the ropes and jumps to nail hardkore with a flying cross body.  However, hardkore catches him and slams him into the mat with the force of his weight behind him. Mikey:  Whoa…..looks like Sakie is showing some rust after such a long layoff, although, hardkore is a tough wrestler to beat because of his size. Lauwass:  Sakie should just be lucky that he is not a commie cause if he were the match would be over by now and Sakie would be dead. Sakie is quickly up to his feet once more and so far has not been favouring his ankle that was injured and put him out for so long.  Sakie gives hardkore a few rights and then some more kicks to the chest, slowing the big man down.  Sakie delivers a standing dropkick on hardkore causing him to stumble backwards a few steps.  Sakie then takes a few steps back to get more of a running start and spears hardkore, taking the big man down.  The crowd pops for Sakie as he takes him down.  Sakie gets up and poses for the audience as it pops for him. Mikey:  Perhaps, I was wrong about the ring rust. Lauwass:  That’s nothing new, you are always wrong. Mikey:  I must say that it is good to see Sakie back in action and the crowd seems to be enjoying seeing one of their favourites back in action. Sakie continues beating on hardkore with a few kicks to the chest while he is down.  Sakie hops onto the second rope and does a moonsault off it on to hardkore.  Sakie gets up once more and poses for the crowd and it returns the favour by popping madly.  Young women everywhere scream their heads off collectively for him.  A few even faint.  One may even have died.  Sakie helps hardkore up to his feet and promptly whips him into the ropes.  Sakie goes off an opposite side ropes and hits hardkore with flying forearm, knocking the big man down once more. Sakie goes over to a corner and waits for hardkore to get up once again.  hardkore slowly gets to his feet and shakes his head from disorientation.  Sakie takes a few steps forward, nailing hardkore with a Hurrikick.  The biggest man in XUW goes down hard.  Sakie hops over to the turnbuckle beside him and before he hops up he yells “BOOM” and the crowd responds “–ER SOONERS.”  Sakie hops up and steadies himself on the top rope.  Sakie leaps off nails “The Jokes on You” (5 Star Frog Splash) on hardkore.  Sakie pins hardkore as the ref drops to count the pin. 1…………. 2…………… 3……………….. The crowd pops as Sakie has won. Winner:  Sakie Sakie stands up and poses in the middle of the ring.  The crowd cheers loudly for his triumphant return as “Stay Together For The Kids” hits. Mikey:  Sakie was victorious in his return tonite.  And the crowd is definitely loving every minute of it.  Sakie showing no real signs of his ankle injury. Lauwass:  Yeah, well if hardkore hadn’t wasted all his energy destroying a commie before the match, we all would have seen how out of shape Sakie really is. Mikey:  Somehow I doubt that. Lauwass:  Well, at least hardkore did something for all of us this evening, he rid the world of another commie. Back in the ring, Sakie continues to celebrate by climbing up the turnbuckles and posing at them as the crowd pops and flashes from cameras go off.  He hops down and leaves the ring and slaps a few hands along the way to the back. As he goes to the back, so we cut there, but not to Sakie, we cut to the locker room door of the Boomer Sooners. There we find Wildlife standing, with a contract in his hands (written on recycled paper, of course). He knock on the door, and we can hear a muffled argument from within the room. Wildlife waits for a couple of seconds, and then just goes in anyways. Saij and Fatbadger look up from playing Rock, Paper, Scissors. Saij:  Well, that saves us from finishing our first to one hundred losses answers the door competition. Fatty:  You always beat me with paper. Why does nothing beat paper, why? Fatty commences shaking his fist at the ceiling, and remains doing so for the remaining length of this segment. Saij:  So what the fark do you want? Wildlife:  I’ve come to collect Jim Ross! Saij gasps. Saij:  Gasp! JR just snores, because he’s asleep in the corner on a reclining chair. The Cockjockey is drawing on the face of the fat Sooner with what looks like a permanent marker pen. Saij:  You can’t have him. Now fark off. Wildlife:  No, I won him, remember? Saij:  When? Wildlife:  It was in the contract for the time when I defeated Sakie to win the Improving Cleanliness Title! Saij:  The what? Wildlife:  The Intercontinental Title. Saij:  Oh. Wildlife:  So give me him now, so I can brainwash him into becoming my follower again. Saij:  What? Wildlife:  I mean treat him kindly, and let him play with Captain Planet. Saij:  That man is strange. Wildlife:  Captain Planet? Saij:  No. Jim Ross. Wildlife:  True, but hand him over anyway. The contract says you have to. Saij:  Is there anything else I can do to change your mind… such as kicking you in the farking nards? Wildlife takes a step backwards. Wildlife:  You could put it all on the line! The World Title AND JR! Make the match for Mayhem! Saij:  Is that all? Wildlife:  Are you scared of the Recyclatude? Saij:  Fark no! I just thought you might want me to do something hard… Wildlife:  So it’s on? Saij:  Oh yeah, it’s on! Wildlife laughs, punches the air and then runs out of the room. He then runs back in and takes all the empty plastic BBQ Sauce bottles from the floor and scurries off to recycle them. Saij shrugs and sits back down to watch the rest of the show, while Fatbadger still shakes his fist at the ceiling… Mikey:  Well, that was strange if nothing else. Lauwass:  Wildlife has a World Title shot! He’s going to be recycling champion of the world! Mikey:  Well, he’s improved a lot, but to take on Saij is another matter entirely. Lauwass:  Polluter… Mikey:  Less of that, please. Now it’s time for our Main Event, and after the vicious beating we saw Enigma inflict on Kata earlier, you can rest assured it will be Ashura coming to the ring tonight, but in what state will he be? “Long, Hard Road out of Hell” plays, and out comes Enigma to a hail of abuse from the crowd. He enters the ring and awaits the arrival of his opponent. “Crash” hits the speakers, and out comes Kata… or as the face paint and ring attire would indicate… Ashura. He walks down to the ring, holding his head in pain, and slides under the bottom rope. Main Event: Enigma v Kata The bell rings, and within an instant Enigma is on the attack, hitting a Scoop Slam and a Leg Drop which gets a short two count from the ref. Mikey:  Enigma’s trying to polish this one off early. He knows that Kata is injured. Lauwass:  It’s Ashura. Kata got injured, and Ashura is filling in for him. Mikey:  You know nothing at all, do you? Enigma whips Ashura to the ropes, and hits a knee to the gut that sends his opponent spinning to the ground. The big man goes up to the top and tries a Moonsault, which looks incredibly impressive… other than for the fact that Ashura rolled to the side and all Enigma hits is the bare mat. As the big man lies there, Ashura goes to the second turnbuckle and drops a leg to the back of Enigma’s head. Enigma struggles up to his feet, but Ashura is in control for the moment, and hits a kick to the gut, and then one to the head as Enigma doubles over. Mikey:  The Wrath of Heaven! Ashura is pulling out all the stops, despite his injuries! Lauwass:  Bah, come on Enigma! Ashura goes up to the second ropes again, dragging Enigma to his feet as he does so.
