XUW Mayhem XUW World Title Match: KoRe v Blake Wolfson © The two men eye each other up in the ring, and Kore comes over, pushing Blake back with two hands. Blake laughs and pushes Kore back. Dave is stunned as Kata whips off the ropes and lands a high knee to the head of Dave.  He whips DK to the ropes and hits a brutal powerslam.  Dave is slwo to get to his feet as Kata is measuring him up.  He locks on a full nelson and pushes Dave into his corner.  Sheri tags Kata and climbs the ropes.  She comes down from the top with a hard forearm shot to the jaw.  She lays in a couple of elbow shots and whips Dave to the ropes.  Dave reverses and stops before he hits Sheri with the knee.  He backs into this corner and tags in Chase.  Chase just looks dumbfounded for a second and tags Dave back. Kata gives Dave a scoop slam and bounces off the ropes.  He goes for a kneedrop, but misses as Dave leaps and tags in Chase.  Chase comes in and ducks a clothesline from Kata.  He gives him a kick and lands a double underhook powerbomb.  Chase climbs up to the top of the corner and jumps off for an elbow to the throat of Kata.  Kata is down as Chase goes for the pin. 1… 2… kickout!!
