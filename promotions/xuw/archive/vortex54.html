This Thursday edition of Vortex on the XUW This Thursday edition of Vortex on the XUW's new home.. the FOX Network, opens in the backstage area where we see Admiral John leaving his locker room to head towards the entrance way that leads to the ringside area for his match.  From out no where, Darkseid runs up from behind AJ and grabs a hold of the back of the Horsemen's head, ramming him face first into the door he was about to walk through. AJ stumbles back and goes down to one knee but Darkseid grabs a hold of him and throws AJ face first into the Pepsi machine in the stage area, smashing the front part of the glass out. AJ lays on the ground, clearly not moving.  Darkseid pulls AJ up to his feet again and throws him through the doors, leading to the stage area. Darkseid follows after him as the scene changes and we go down to ringside where our commentator's for the evening, Atomic Tomato and "Mr.  Majestic" Barry Johnson are sitting, looking rather stunned at how Vortex had just opened AT: "Good evening folks.. wow, I'm not sure how I want to start off the show now since Darkseid just unprovokedly attacked Admiral John in the backstage area as Vortex was going live on the air..." BJ:  "I know how to start off this show!  Want me to show you how?" AT, looking rather sarcastic:  "By all means, I just have to see how the Majetstic one would start a show.." BJ:  "Excellent choice Cabbage.. here we go... " Barry is just about to open his mouth when Admiral John is thrown down the ramp at the top of the stage.  The fans start cheering and booking at the same since they don't know whether to do each one as Darkseid walks out to the top of the ramp and looks down at where Admiral John is laying in a heap at the bottom of the ramp.  He slowly begins to make his way down the ramp AT:  "This man had a hell of a showing with the World Champion a week ago and almost defeated the Ripper." BJ:  "If he keeps up the punishment he's showing Admiral John here tonight, I'll forget that he likes to eat butterflies for breakfast, lunch and dinner.." AT:  "And for a snack before bed." BJ:   "Yeck."
