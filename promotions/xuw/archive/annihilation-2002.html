XUW Annihilation The XUW logo flashes across the screen, and then it goes black. From the silence comes a voice… “The call went out, and they came, they all came. XUW was reborn, and a new era began.” Godsmack’s “I Stand Alone” plays, and images of XUW Reborn flash up on the screen… Hodgo pinning Bluto for the TV Title. The Comedian smashing Dave Kipfer with a chair to win the Battle Royal. Henrik winning the Submission Title. No Fear Man hitting his finisher onto thumbtacks for the Hardcore Title victory. Sakie pinning HBK. DDT zapping Bob with the tazer. Admiral John flying through the air and coming down, smashing through the announce table. Lance Storm getting his new XUW contract. Doggy Style retaining their titles after a spinebuster through a flaming table. Sidhe Nimh nailing Sakie and winning the Intercontinental Title. Pictures of Sakie and DDT, footage of their match, and then Sakie hitting the ground, and becoming the XUW World Champion. “They came, and they raised the bar beyond all expectations, and now, they prepare for Annihilation.” Sakie is shown, title in hand. The picture then cuts to DDT, brooding in the darkness. “Above what anyone could have thought, XUW continued to grow, with new blood, and old faces.” The members of F.E.A.R. are shown, and then a screen split of Derick Jackson and Blitzwing “Stables clashed in an ever escalating battle for power.” Explosions are seen all over the screen, and then the members of The Commonwealth, and The Four Horsemen are shown “Feuds both new and old remain to be settled. Former friends set upon a path of destruction…” Sidhe Nimh is shown training with Ikke Mighty, and then we see footage of Hodgo attacking him with a huge pane of glass “Yet some men are just starting their roads here, unaware of the Annihilation that they may face.” Supa Thug Esco and Bluto are shown, with clips from their recent victories “And so they fight. Some for gold…” Blake Savage is shown taunting Dave Kipfer with his TV Title “… some for their honour …” The ladies of XUW, Naron, Sheri and Kat are seen attacking one another “ … some because it is all they know …” Bob and Lance are shown going at it, from several different occasions “Then there are the warriors, who fight for more than just a pinfall, who fight for their very souls.” The reborn Phoenix is shown, with a fiery backdrop, and then Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy, shrouded in darkness “For some this could be the end of the line, and a veritable legacy is at stake.” We see the many victories of Doggy Style, and then of The Deadly Combination “And then there are those who come back, stronger than at Reborn, knowing fully now what they mean to XUW, and what the title means to them. The true gladiators, putting it on the line one more time…” Sakie’s title victories are shown, but always in the background remains the elusive presence of DDT, preparing, ready to strike “But are any of them ready, for Annihilation?” PYRO FLIES, GODSMACK BLARES LOUDER THAN EVER, AND THE CROWD GOES SO WILD THAT THIS HAD TO BE WRITTEN IN CAPS! The camera spins round the huge arena, taking in the fans, and their sings, such as “Sakie is MY hero”, “I want Kipfer’s … talent”, “Commonwealth Schmommonwealth” and, strangely, “You shall all fear Sin Yun”.
