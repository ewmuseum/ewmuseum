XUW Vortex #71

19 Jun 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Vortex Last week under the start of the Ultra Violent reign there was no more pyro. This week, change of plans.... Just Kidding! Instead of pyro going off like usual, footage of pyro from old shows goes off, followed by footage of each and every Superstar in XUW... well, not exactly. Footage is shown only of XUW wrestlers getting their asses kicked by a member of the Ultra Violents, with anybody who has never faced an Ultra violent excluded from the opening video. Isn’t that a bit biased and egotistical on the part of the new commissioners? Well, yeah! Let’s move on to some signs shall we? The camera sweeps across the crowd looking for good, original signs, and massive cheering can be heard, but oddly it doesn’t look like anybody is cheering... odd. We finally come across a great set of signs, and they seem like the average, normal, everyday set of XUW signs: “I want to have an Ultra Violent’s baby!” “Ultra Violent mark!” “God save the Ultra Violents!” and of course, with a fan holding up a beer making a toast: “To the ULTRA VIOLENTS!” The camera cuts to the announcing table, where Mikey is looking down in disgust at the new ‘uniforms’ that were issued to the announcing team this week. They are polo shirts that read: “Property of the UVs” Lauwass, on the contrary, looks down at his new shirt and grins. Mikey: Welcome everyone to Vortex! Lauwass: Not just any Vortex though, one of the two greatest Vortexes off all time!! And do you want to know why? Mikey: Because it’s the second Vortex under the UV’s control? Lauwass: Yeah, but I wanted to say that, bastard! Mikey: Just to change the subject completely, in just over two weeks time on July 6th, XUW presents it’s next great PPV, Violent Times! Lauwass: And do you know why it’s going to be a great PPV? Mikey: Yeah yeah, it’s the Ultra Violent’s PPV, blah blah blah. So far there is one match announced for the card, and that is for the World Title. HBK, the man who won the World Title tournament at Stamped Authority faces a very difficult challenge when he defends the title against Ultra Violent member Dave Kipfer. Lauwass: I almost feel sorry for HBK, almost. I wonder if he realizes just what the UVs have in store for that poor sap leading up to the greatest PPV of all time! Mikey sighs. Mikey: Well, if he wasn’t sure than he will get his first taste tonight, as he faces off against Ric Austin in a non-title match, with none other than Dave Kipfer as the special guest referee. I suppose you’re now going to tell me that Dave will call the match fairly, right? Lauwass: But of course. If anybody, Dave would know what it’s like to get screwed by a referee. Dave is an unbiased, very fair individual, and he will call it right down the middle! Mikey: Yeah...... right. There’s no use arguing here, so let’s move on down the card. Also tonight, HBD and The Fool are in for a battle as they take on NFM and The Shadow. After their new found respect, it should be interesting if these two can get along and fully function as a team. Lauwass: Yeah, it should also be interesting to see just how many seconds it takes for them to get rid of HBD and The Fool. I hope there’s blood, blood I say!! Mikey: Riiiiiiight. Anyway, another match on the card should be a classic, as Kata and the new tag team champions, the Lunchroom Patrol take on the AoD, consisting of the Hardcore Icons and the Fallen Fae. Lauwass: I just want to see LKK snap and go DarkSeid all over his opponents like he did at Stamped Authority. Mikey: Whatever. And in our first match of the evening, M V Pete takes on Rujasu one on one. And apparently Pete isn’t in the best of moods, considering that he feels he was robbed of a World Title shot by the UVs. Lauwass: Sucks to be you, Rujasu. Mikey holds up his hand over his ear... Mikey: Apparently there is something going down backstage. Let’s go there now and see what’s transpiring. Lauwass: I bet you feel all important when you do that, don’t you? Anyway, the camera cuts backstage as Fae is shown, sitting at a table in the kitchen area, eating some pasta. Fae: Fae like spaghetti! Wha’ Fae like even more than spaghetti is eating spaghetti off Fae’s new shiny plate! The camera pans around to reveal that Fae’s new ‘shiny plate’ is indeed the Hardcore Title. Fae: Da. Fae smiles, as he twists and twirls some spaghetti noodles with a fork. Just then, Kata enters the frame, glancing over at Fae with an odd expression on his face. Fae looks up at him. Fae: Kata! Hello goodly Kata. Does Kata want some pasta! Kata raises an eyebrow. Kata: No, thank you Fae, but I appreciate the offer. Fae: Da. Fae continues eating as Kata looks on quizzically. Kata: Isn’t that the Hardcore Title you are eating off of? Fae: No no silly Kata. This Fae’s shiny plate, also known as HAPPYcore title. Hardcore title badly, maaaa.... Fae looks upset, but then sees his pasta and smiles as he continues eating. Kata’s expression begins to turn serious. Kata: Fae, you should not eat off of the title belt. That belt brings with it respect, and you are not showing the belt the proper respect. Fae: No, Fae respect shiny plate. Having more than one use make happycore title feel more important. Fae smiles. Kata doesn’t. Kata: You should not eat food off of the title. You should carry it with pride and honor. Fae: Da! Fae carry HAPPYcore title with honor. But greatest honor ever will come when Fae lose title to Fatbadger! Kata: What? Fae: Losing to Fatbadger... it’s a huge honor! Fae readly to lose ‘gainst Fatbadger! Kata looks visibly upset after hearing that remark from poor, innocent Fae. Kata: You do not bring honor to the title. You do nothing but bring shame upon the hardcore title. Kata frowns at Fae, turns, and walks away with a disgusted look on his face. Fae looks confused. Fae: Da? The camera cuts back to the announcers, who looks just as confused as Fae did. Mikey: Fae is an odd little fellow, but he just looks too cuddly to beat up. Lauwass: That is the gayest thing I’ve ever heard in my life. The crowd’s laughter is quickly replaced by a loud chorus of boos, as ‘Bulls on Parade” by Rage Against the Machine blares through the PA. M V Pete steps out from the back and has his game face on as he walks with purpose down to the ring. Mikey: All business tonight it seems. As Pete waits in the ring, ‘Chrono trigger’ blares, if it’s even capable of ‘blaring,’ out of the PA system and the crowd lets out a nice pop for the analytical one, Rujasu. Ruj high fives a few fans as he walks down to the ring. Mikey: Rujasu looks very determined as well. Lauwass: Against Pete, he needs to be. M V Pete vs. Rujasu As Rujasu climbs into the ring, Pete dashes over to the ropes and knees Ruj in the midsection. Pete then grabs Rujasu by the hair and yanks him into the ring as the ref calls for the bell. Pete throws Rujasu into the corner and starts to unload on him with a series of lefts and rights, finishing the series off with a hard clothesline in the corner that sends Ruj stumbling out of the corner. Pete turns around and nails Ruj again with a second vicious clothesline from behind, sending him down to the mat hard. Mikey: Pete looks very upset tonight. Lauwass: Well, if you were promised a world title shot for helping the UVs become the commissioners, but instead got the opening match against Rujasu, I bet you’d be upset as well! Mikey: So you sympathize with Pete over the UVs? Lauwass: I’m not exactly saying that. Mikey: Then what ARE you saying? Lauwass: Ummmm... hey, nice elbow drop by Pete there. The camera cuts back to Pete just after he dropped an elbow across Rujasu’s throat. Pete ‘helps’ Rujasu back to his feet, and pushes him back against the ropes, before whipping him across the ring to the opposite ropes. On the rebound, Pete goes for a big boot, but Ruj ducks under and bounces off the opposite ropes. Pete turns around and goes for a clothesline, but Ruj ducks again and counters with a neck breaker that sends Pete down to the mat. Both men are up at the same time, and this time Rujasu goes for a clothesline, which connects. Pete gets right back up, and Rujasu goes for a dropkick... ...but his legs gets swatted away in mid air by Pete, who saw it coming. Ruj lands awkwardly on his head and he grabs it, as Pete grabs both of Ruj’s legs, and kicks Ruj in the ‘midsection.’ Mikey: Hey ref, that was a blatant low blow! As Rujasu curls up on the mat after that shot, Pete, rolls him up... 1... 2... 2.5... 2.75... Shoulder up! Mikey: Whew, close call. Pete doesn’t even look up at the ref, but instead mounts himself on top of Rujasu and starts wailing on poor Ruj’s face, with very stiff rights and lefts. Ruj lifts up his arms to protect his face, and finally the referee pulls Pete off of Ruj. The crowd boos loudly at Pete, but he ignores them as he continues the onslaught, by kicking Ruj in the ribs a few times as he tries to stand up. Rujasu finally makes it to his feet and Pete roughly whips Ruj to the corner again. Pete looks around at the crowd and flips them off, before walking over to a stunned Rujasu in the corner. Pete lowers his shoulder, and begins to ram it into Ruj’s midsection repeatedly. Mikey: that reminds me of someone else. Ruj hunches over in the corner, and Pete grabs Ruj and nails him with a belly to belly suplex out of the corner, similar to Steiner’s. Pete bounces off the ropes and drops down on Ruj’s head with a closed fist, before rolling him up into a pin... 1... 2... Shoulder up at the last instance! Pete gets to his feet and slowly circles Rujasu, much like a vulture circles his prey. As Ruj gets to one knee, M V Pete kicks him in the face, sending him back down. Pete then grabs Ruj’s arm, leaps up into the air, and drops down on it with both knees. Pete repeats the process, then turns Rujasu over on his back and clubs him in the back of the neck. Pete then drags Rujasu over to the ropes by his hair, and drapes his neck over the bottom rope, before applying all of his weight on top of Ruj, choking him. Mikey: Come on ref! Break this up! Lauwass: Heh, I like it. Make Rujasu pay for what the UVs did to you! Mikey: Why? That doesn’t even make sense. Lauwass: So? Back in the ring, the referee counts to four before Pete lets go of Ruj. The ref berates Pete, but he simply walks past the ref, ignoring him, as he continues to punish the former submission champion. Pete runs to the opposite ropes with Ruj still draped over the bottom rope, and rebounds off the rope, attempting to leap on Ruj, choking him even more, but Rujasu moves away at the last second. causing Pete to almost leap out of the ring, but the middle rope stops his progress as it chokes him. The crowd cheers as Rujasu is the first man to his feet. Ruj gets ready while Pete gets up, grabbing his neck. Before Pete can react, the smaller Rujasu hooks him up and drops him with a dragon suplex. Ruj then hops up and bounces off the ropes, before landing on Pete’s head with a flying knee drop. Ruj covers and hooks the leg... 1... 2... 2.5... Pete squirms... 2.75... Ruj leans back as hard as he can... 2.85... Kickout, barely! Mikey: And Ruj seems to be taking control of this match. Lauwass: I know. I’m as surprised as you are. Mikey: Surprised? Rujasu is a talented athlete. Rujasu gets to his feet and poses momentarily for the fans, before lifting Pete up and whipping the larger Pete to the corner... which Pete reverses. Ruj bounces off the corner relatively lightly, and Pete reaches out and grabs Rujasu around the throat, signaling for the Prime Time Chokeslam. Ruj senses imminent danger, and quickly nails a few elbows to the side of Pete’s head, before breaking out of the hold and nailing a fisherman’s suplex to Pete. The crowd starts to get into it as Ruj raises his arm and lifts Pete up, before this time successfully whipping Pete to the corner. Ruj follows up by climbing up to the second turnbuckle, and punching Pete on the top of the head as the crowd counts out loud... 1!!! 2!!! 3!!! 4!!! Before we can get to five, Pete grabs Rujasu and steps away from the turnbuckle. Mikey: Uh oh, this doesn’t look good. Rujasu continues to pound on Pete’s head, trying to get free from his grasp, but Pete has none of it and executes an inverted Atomic Drop. Ruj is still standing, and Pete damn near takes his head off with a massive big boot to the face! Lauwass: Ouch. Pete grabs Rujasu around the neck while he is down and yanks him up to his feet, and then another good five feet into the air, before sending Rujasu’s unfortunate back back down to the mat with the Prime Time Chokeslam! Pete looks down at his opponent for a moment, still looking visibly upset, and then drops down for a lackadaisical cover 1... 2... Foot on the ropes. Pete doesn’t seem to mind though, as he rips up Rujasu by his hair and hoists him onto his shoulders. Pete clamps down momentarily as though this was a torture rack, and then swings Rujasu around and drops him with the Double B (F-5)!!!!! Pete then winds back, and kicks Rujasu hard in the ribs one more time for the hell of it. Mikey: That has to be it. This match is over. Lauwass: Yep. Pete makes a normal cover... 1... 2... 3!!! Winner via pinfall: M V PETE!!! Pete’s music starts up as Pete flips off the fans once more, then exits the ring and walks angrily up the ramp, leaving a disoriented Rujasu laying in the ring. Mikey: Impressive display by M V Pete there against Rujasu. That has to tell the Ultra Violents that he is deserving of a title shot. Lauwass: Wait, it’s just Rujasu. It’s not like Pete beat anybody important tonight. Mikey sighs... Lauwass:  Well, before we move on tonight, I must say that I have received a vitally important videotape. This could rock the foundations of XUW to their very core. Mikey:  What? What is it? Lauwass:  This could put us out of business, Mikey. It's footage from another federation... The Ultitron comes to life, and we seem to be in the middle of a jungle or something. The camera moves forwards, swatting aside leaves and branches, all covered in water. After a few seconds, the camera reaches a clearing, in the middle of which is a wrestling ring. We can hardly see the ring though, for the multitudes of people sitting around the outside of the ring. The camera moves over, and we see a blackboard, with chalk markings on it, telling of the card for the night: Opening Curtain Jerker Match: Hulk Hogan v Ric Flair Other Match Nobody Cares About: Goldberg v HHH MAIN EVENT Improving Cleanliness Title Bout The Masked Polluter v Wildlife (champion) Ahhhh, so it's a rainforest, not a jungle. The camera moves closer, so we can see the in-ring action of the Main Event, and hear the commentary of the Rainforest Wrestling Federation's commentators; Barky and the Radioactive Iguana. Barky:  By God, Wildlife is putting it all on the line tonight, his coveted Improving Cleanliness Title! Radioactive Iguana:  His belt is made from bits of garbage! We see Kidd holding it at ringside. They speak the truth. B: Look at the Masked Polluter go, though! The Masked Polluter tries to kick Wildlife, but WIldlife reverses, and then hits the Greenhouse Effect! The crowd pops huge as he goes for the cover! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! Winner, and STILL Improving CLeanliness Champion - Wildlife!!! He falls to his knees and accepts the rapturous applause of the crowd, as both commentators faint through excitement, and the cameraman makes a hasty exit, as someone spots him wearing non-recycled clothes. The Ultitron turns off, and we cut back to the announcers. Mikey:  Well I'll be damned. Lauwass:  Wildlife! Wildlife! He's off in South America, defending his title wherever he goes! What a hero! Mikey:  I wonder if he'll ever make it back here? Lauwass:  He can come back now, since the Ultra Violents got rid of that fraud Webster... The camera cuts to backstage, around some boxes and equipment, as the Boomer Sooners are shown, looking rather serious... yep, including Fatbadger, who just might be pretending to look serious. Saij: I don’t get it. He just seems to be changing for the worst every day. JR: By God Saij, good ol’ JR agrees with you! Fatbadger: Uh, hey dudes. Who are we talking about? JR: You silly pet coon Badger! Of course we’re talking about that no good Dave by god Kipfer! A loud negative reaction emanates from the crowd at ringside. The Sooners look around as though they don’t know where that came from. Fatbadger: Oh yeah, Dave. How’s he been doing anyway? I haven’t seen him around the locker room lately. JR: By God Badger! That rotten turncoat Kipfer turned his back on us and joined the Ultra Violents! Saij: Joined who? JR: The Ultra Violents. Saij: I’m not sure who you’re talking about.
