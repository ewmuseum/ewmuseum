Suddenly they start exchanging blows back and forth until the ref finally makes some sort of order. Dev and Nu are left in the ring to start the match. Nu immediately runs to the ropes and tries a shoulder block but only mildly affects Dev. Dev goes back for his own shoulder block but has the same effect. They both back to the ropes but Dev goes for a clothesline that is ducked. Nu kicks him in the gut and delivers a swift ddt. AT: Neither team is backing down. This could be a real clash between two of the most winningest teams in xUw. BJ: Well the Deadly Combination haven’t show me anything impressive in a while, but Explicit Violence is poised to take over the tag team division. AT: That’s going to be harder than they think BJ. They still have to get past this match. Nu grabs Dev’s leg and drags him to his corner. He tags in Pete who immediately comes in stomping on the leg. Pete throws Dev into the ropes and goes for a suplex but is reversed into a suplex by Dev. Dev looks to be tired as he goes to tag in BoCru. BoCru comes in and lays out Pete with a clothesline and hits Nu with a shot knocking him off the apron. He gets back up as BoCru throws Pete into his corner. Dev takes a few shots before BoCru lands a superplex on Pete. He tags in Dev who immediately drops a knee to his sternum and throws him to the ropes giving him a spinebuster. Dev goes to tag BoCru but Pete leaps and tags in Nu. Nu comes in and hits Dev with a backdrop but is attacked by BoCru. Pete hits BoCru with a clothesline and they both pick up Dev for a powerbomb. AT: This match has all the intensity it should have. Wait…is that Raven X and Bailey coming from the back. BJ: Yeah, what are they doing down here. They aren’t scheduled to be here tonight. Total chaos has taken over the match as Nu tries to help Pete but is only attacked by BoCru from behind. BoCru is attacked by Bailey, but Bailey hops back outside the ring to finish off Dev. BoCru is leaning on the ropes as Nu takes the time to roll him up in a schoolboy pin. 1…2…2 and three fourths The referee calls for the bell to the surprise of both teams. Nu starts to celebrate in the ring but is still in shock. The referee give s the announcer the official decision which is a no contest. The format will be a tournament that is single elimination for each team consisting of 4 individuals. The first person to be pinned or submit in each match eliminates his ENTIRE team. In the finals, the 8 person tag team match will be decided as each individual must be pinned, and the final remaining person or persons from one team will win Stable Wars. The official brackets will be announced next week. AT: Wow, Big Brawl seems to be shaping up quite nicely. I'm pretty excited for it, as I'm sure each stable in XUW is. BJ: That is June 2nd. AT: Bob looks great and he should be in the best position to win this match since it is a no DQ. BJ: Well, he does have that over Jack but this is the Ripper we are talking about. BJ: See Tomato, that is a true champion’s champion. He is gonna show you tonight why he is called “The Ripper”. He’s just vicious. AT: BJ, I have no arguments with you there. BJ: That is true, but Jack hasn’t needed any help thus far. He should be good for this by himself.
