XUW XPosure #25

8 Sep 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW XPosure The waiting has ended and fans who have been in the arena since Thursday (and not showered since before that... the arena kinda smells..) come alive as generic Rock Music chosen specifically to avoid having to pay the unknown artist royalties fills the arena as the ring announcer jogs to the ring. We are treated to your standard spat of rules... no lasers, no garbage tossed in the ring, yadda yadda yadda and we are OFF AND RUNNING BY GAWD! Bolling w/Rujasu at ringside vs Destroyer Bolling and Ruj make their way to the ring first, followed by Destroyer. Bolling and Destroyer lock in the center of the ring, but Destroyer gets the quick advantage, tossing Bolling to the ropes and hitting a hard clothesline. Destroyer goes to the rope, maybe trying for a legdrop but Ruj grabs his leg and trips him. As the ref admonishes Ruj Bolling goes to work on Destroyer, scopping his for a hard slam and cover, but Destroyer kicks out at 2. Bolling continues the offense, pulling Destroyer up by his hair but and trying for another slam but Destroyer rolls him in a small package for 2. Destroyer is able to go on the attack, throwing Bolling to the ropes and hitting a backdrop. As Destroyer once again goes to the ropes Ruj pulls down the top rope, sending Destroyer to the floor. Ruj lands some hard stomps on Destroyer as the ref tries to keep Bolling from leaving the ring. Finally Ruj rolls Bolling back into the ring as Bolling pushes the ref aside. He picks Destroyer up by his hair and suplexes him. Bolling climbs the corner as the ref counts, launching himself in a frog splash, but Destroyer rolls out of the way, making Bolling eat mat. Destroyer is quick to follow up, picking Bolling up and setting him on the top turnbuckle, climbing up after him and hitting a hard superplex. Destroyer covers and gets the three. Winner by Pinfall: Destroyer Destroyer leaves the ring, and shortly after Ruj helps Bolling out. The ring annoucner and another ref make their way to the ring and the next match begins. XUW TV Title Match: Dev© vs Kore Dev comes to ringside first, and as he poses in the ring with the TV Title KoRe runs to the ring, slides under the ropes and the two begin exchanging punches as the ref calls for the bell. KoRe is first to come up short and is knocked to his back and Dev follows with a legdrop for a quick cover and kickout at 1. Dev is quick to his feet and Kore is just as quick and the two charge, locking up again in the ring and jockeying for position. This time Kore comes out on top, tossing Dev to the ropes and hitting a dropkick that sends Dev to the ropes. Kore follows with a hard clothesline, spinning Dev out of the ring and to the floor. Kore tries to dollow but the ref stops him, admonishing him to the other side of the ring as he begins to count. Dev slowly gets to his feet and is in the ring at a six count. Kore charges but Dev ducks, pulling the top rope down and now sending Kore to the outside. Dev rolls out of the ring and pulls Kore to his feet, picking him up and dropping him across the security railing before picking him up and pushing him back into the ring. Dev pulls him up and tries to set him up for a Devolution when Austin runs down to the ring and tries to get into the ring. The ref goes over and tries to restrain him as Dev lets go of Kore and tries to go after Austin. As the two argue, Kore grabs Dev's TV Title Belt and, out of ref view, belts Dev in the head, dropping him like a brick. Austin finally gets off the apron as Kore covers. The ref turns and counts the three! Winner by Pinfall and NEW XUW Tv Champion: KoRe KoRe celebrates in the ring as the crowd seems torn... why did Austin come to KoRe's aid?? Only time, and properly written shows , will tell. KoRe leaves the ring, stopping to hold his belt over his head before leaving the arena. The ring announcer and yet another ref jog to the ring as Dev slowly leaves the ring and our fourth match of the night is underway! New Royal Legion vs Saij/Laudust The Royal Legion make their way to the ring, followed by Saij and the enigmatic Laudust, who is dressed up as Meng, even including the horrendous hairdo. Danyon and Saij start out, and Saij gets the upper hand, hitting several trademark moves, such as the Topsy Turvy and the Ker-Splat. Danyon tags out and Bailey gets even with Saij, getting in some stiff forearms and a splash in the turnbuckles. Saij manages a Suplex that leaves both men down, and then finally managed to tag out simultaneously. Laudust comes in looking impressive, with a Hip Toss and the 30 Second Trailer. Danyon reversed the Sneak Peak into a Powerbomb that left both men dazed. Both men again exchange tags, letting Saij get the advantage on Bailey and nail him with a Suplex and a Missile Dropkick. Saij tags Laudust back in, but Bailey hits a few clubbing blows to the head, before going off in search of turnbuckles to eat. Laudust comes round and whips Bailey to the ropes, hitting a Shoulderblock and then another Hip Toss. He connects with a massive Matinee Inverted Powerbomb, and then gloats for the crowd. He plays up his part as the Munsterous Mengdust, and as he does so Bailey rolls him up! Danyon stops Saij getting the break-up, and Bailey get the pin, 1-2-3! Winners by Pinfall: New Royal Legion Danyon and Saij trade a few blows after the bell, before Saij helps his partner to the back, and the New Royal Legion celebrates their win. The ring announcer comes out and announces a brief intermission, and doesn't fail to remind the crowd this is a PERFECT time to take in the concession stands and load up on some GREAT official XUW merchandise. The fans file out to buy said offical merchandise and relieve themselves in the official XUW restrooms before our main event. They all file in just in time to see the ring announcer and yet another nameless ref run to the ring. The ring annoucncer tells the crowd this match has not been sanctioned by XUW and Ric Austin has not been cleared to wrestle after the horrendous fall he took at Dingos and Darkness. With that, the match begins... Raven X vs Ric Austin Raven X comes to the ring first, followed by Austin, who has seen better days... he climbs into the ring and the bell sounds as the two lock up. Raven immediately takes it to Austin, leveling him with a hard clothesline. He pulls Austin up by his hair and slams him hard to the mat, going to the ropes and trying for a leg drop but Austin rolls to the side. Austin is to his feet and tries for a clothesline of his own, but Raven ducks and Austin goes to the other rope. Raven ducks for a backdrop but Austin hits a facebuster, sending Raven to the mat and covering him for a quick 1 count. The force of the kickout sends Austin flying and Raven is quick to capitalize, picking him up and hitting a hard backbreaker, then he locks on a Boston Crab. The ref asks if Austin wants to quit and Austin fiercly shakes his head. He crawls, trying to reach for the ropes. He is able to barely get his hand over the bottom rope and the ref calls for the break. Raven gets off Austin and stands back, waiting for him to get to his feet. As Austin does, Raven charges and hits a dropkick, spinning Austin over the top ropes. He hits his back across the arpon as he falls to the mat and lies not moving. The ref follows Austin to the floor and takes one look at him before calling for the bell. Winner by Submission in a non-sanctioned match: Raven X And thus, the show ends, and some fans leave, but most stay, knowing they have an entire day of inring promos to catch before Mayhem... sick freaks...
