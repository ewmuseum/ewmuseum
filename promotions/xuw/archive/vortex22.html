XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #22

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Pyro goes off as the unmistakable theme of Vortex blares throughout the arena. The fans are popping like crazy spotlights swerve around all over the place. AT: Hello everyone and welcome to another stellar edition of Vortex. As always, I am The Atomic Tomato and I’m joined by “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson. We have a great show for you tonight. BJ: Do you always start the show off the same boring way? AT: What? BJ: You say the same old crap week in and week out. Be more original! AJ: Umm….ok. We got a- BJ: Great card tonight ladies and gentlemen. See! Too darn predictable. I’ll run down the card so the audience doesn’t fall asleep. In a hardcore match, NFM takes on ‘Sexual Charisma’ Blake Savage. In a six man tag, the tag team champs, The Royal Legion, and Hodgo take on The Boomer Sooners and Fatbadger. Our greatest TV champ ever, Michael Bolling, goes up against God’s messenger to XUW, The Jehovah’s Witness! AT: Umm….that’s The Apostle. BJ: Whatever. In a submission title match, OUR King Henrik mixes it up with the likes of Woof Woof. In a battle of AoD vs. Horsemen, AJ and KsC take on former Horseman Sidhe Nimh and the Hardcore icon DDT. Tonight’s main event is a doozy, ladies and gentlemen. The co-Intercontinental champs, Supa Thug Esco and Blake Wolfson, go at it to determine who the real Intercontinental champ is. Anything you want to say, Parsley? AT: Umm…..lets head to the ring for our first match. BJ:<Buzzer noise> Wrong answer! Big Brawl comes to us live on June 2nd and you don’t even mention who’s in Stable Wars?! Well I guess I will. So far, the teams announced competing in the Stable Wars tournament are The Four Horsemen, FEAR, Blake Wolfson, The Usual Suspects, and The Boomer Sooners. More teams will be announced on Xposure and Mayhem. NOW, lets head to the ring. ”Hey Man Nice Shot” hits as Blake Savage makes his way from the backstage to massive heat from the crowd. He makes his way to ringside and goes over to the time keeper and shoves them out of their chair and takes it and brings it into the ring. “Du Hast” hits and NFM makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring and Blake swings the chair, but NFM ducks. The chair bounces off the top rope and whacks him in the head. The ref rings the bell. Hardcore Match – NFM vs. Blake Savage Blake falls over as NFM laughs at him. Blake stumbles to his feet and NFM grabs him by the throat. He then gives Savage the Lights Out (chokeslam from hell) onto the steel chair. The ref checks on Savage as NFM rolls out of the ring and goes under the ring. He grabs a sledgehammer and rolls into the ring with it. Savage rolls over face first onto the canvas. NFM puts down the sledgehammer and picks up the chair. He then sets it up so Savage’s knee is wedged in-between the seat and the chair. NFM then picks up the sledgehammer, wields it up high, and comes down across the chair as the crowd lets out a huge gut-emptying gasp as Savage screams in pain. AT: Oh….my…..- BJ: Sh**! Now that’s hardcore! AT: I think Savage is hurt, Barry. He’s screaming like his kneecap was just shattered. NFM goes to pick Savage up, but Savage falls to the ground. Savage attempts to get up, but struggles on one leg. NFM then picks him up and sets him up for the Lightning Strike (jumping Tombstone) and connects. NFM hooks the leg. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner – NFM Explicit Violence runs down to the ring and puts the boots to Savage. Dev picks up the injured Savage and gives him the Devolution and BoneCrusher follows with the Big Enforcer Elbowdrop. Savage is out on the mat as the three members of FEAR leave the ring. AT: Dear lord, they picked him apart! I don’t like Savage, but he was hurt way before those two goons came out here! BJ: FEAR is proving that they need to be taken seriously. I’d be very afraid of them come Stable Wars. I mean look at this. EMT’s are having to cart off Savage. AT: He’s still screaming over that leg. He could have a broken kneecap, torn ligaments, who knows what. The camera heads backstage and manages to find Sakie and Kidd Kore Sakie: Kore, you have no idea what being saved will do for your career. YOUR hero will help you grow to become one of the elite. Kore: Just like you? Sakie: Well, not so much, but believe me. Being saved has helped me, and it will surely help you, Kore. The camera heads back to the arena. AT: Well that was quite odd. Well everyone our next match is a doozy. This match was setup on account of the mishap on Mayhem. The Boomer Sooners accidently bagged Sheri instead of Naron when trying to pull a prank on The Commonwealth, and RavenX didn’t take that too light heartedly. BJ: The Royal Legion are the tag team champs. They’ll be victorious in this match like always and that’s because they defy the odds. They beat the conspiracy! ”Rule Brittania” hits as The Royal Legion and Hodgo make their way down to the ring to massive boos. RavenX looks like he just wants to hurt somebody. They climb in the ring and pose to the boos. “Real American” hits the speakers as the fans erupt in cheers for The Boomer Sooners and Fatbadger. Resistol hats come flying through the crowd as they make their way to the ring. Fatty slides into the ring as Chase and Dave hang out in the corner. RX starts to pound away at Fatbadger. The ref rings the bell. The Royal Legion/Hodgo vs. The Boomer Sooners/Fatbadger RX picks up Fatbadger and sends him to the ropes. Fatbadger ducks an RX clothesline, but gets nailed with a spinebuster. Fatty rolls around on the canvas. RX looks down at Fatty and walks over to him with a purpose. RX then shoves his hand down Badger’s shorts as the crowd gasps. AT: What the hell is RavenX doing?! BJ: Look! He found Rosie. Thank the maker! RX puts Rosie on his hand and jumps around the ring with much joy. Fatbadger rolls over as he blocks his crotch after being somewhat violated by RX. Badger starts to get up and RX awaits for him. Badger turns around and RX goes to shove Rosie into his mouth ala Socko, but Badger ducks as RX creams the turnbuckle. RX stumbles back into a belly-to-back suplex. RX rolls from the move and tags in Bailey. Badger tags in Dave as the crowd pops as he and Bailey meet in the middle of the ring with fisticuffs. Dave backs Bailey into the ropes and sends him to the other side. Dave hops onto the ropes and nails an already coming Bailey with a springboard hurricanrana. Bailey flies through the middle rope and to the outside. Dave climbs the turnbuckle and goes to leap onto Bailey on the outside, but Hodgo jars the rope and Dave falls spread eagled onto the turnbuckle. Dave falls backward and ends up in a tree of woe position. Bailey makes his way back into the ring. RX gets in the ring as well and goes to the other side. RX winds up Bailey, but Bailey reverses and they nail Dave with the Power Play (Double Splash into the turnbuckle) as he remains in the tree of woe. Dave falls over after both RX and Bailey connect with the double team. AT: Now that had to have hurt even more than the regular move. Damn Hodgo. BJ: Great teamwork from The Commonwealth, as expected. Bailey picks up Dave and nails him with a running powerslam. Bailey goes for the cover. 1… 2… Kickout! Bailey picks up Dave again and nails him with a big time bodyslam. Bailey then climbs the turnbuckle to the second rope. He goes to deliver the knee drop from the second rope, but Dave moves out of the way. Bailey lands hard on his knee and hobbles around. Dave stumbles to his feet and catches a turning around Bailey with The Showstopper (superkick). Both men are down as the fans cheer the ongoing battle. Bailey and Dave make their way over to their partners and tag in Chase and Hodgo. Chase and Hodgo meet in the middle of the ring, but Hodgo takes an early advantage with an eye rake. Hodgo goes to kick Chase in the head, but Chase ducks and sends Hodgo to the ropes. Dave pulls the rope down and Hodgo flies to the outside as the fans pop. Dave yells at Hodgo as to in reference to what he did earlier in the match. RX then comes charging across the ring at Dave and knocks him off the apron. Badger comes in the ring and picks off a charging Bailey with a spear. They roll to the outside. AT: This match is getting way out of hand. Its complete mayhem. BJ: This is Vortex you kook. As RX and Dave fight it out on the outside, Hodgo grabs a pane of glass from outside the ring and slides in the ring as Chase watches Fatbadger and Bailey go at it. The ref is trying to break them apart, but having some problems doing so. Hodgo goes to nail Chase with the pane of glass, but Chase nails him in the gut with a kick to the midsection. Hodgo drops the glass and Chase delivers Dying Daze (Fisherman’s DDT) on the glass! The pane shatters and Hodgo is busted open. Chase makes the cover. The ref decides just to give up on keeping Badger and Bailey apart. He looks in the ring and sees Chase with the cover. He goes in and counts. 1… 2… 3!!! Winners – The Boomer Sooners and Fatbadger AT: And Chase pulls out with a victory for his team! BJ: He cheated! He used an illegal object to take out Hodgo! AT: Its about damn time Hodgo got what was coming to him. "Right Here. Right Now" hits and the crowds go nuts as the Deadly Combination step out from behind the curtain. M V Pete and Numark are followed by Sniper and PTP, who is carrying a duffle bag with him. The four make their way ringside where they pose, much to the delight of the camera owning fans. Nu: Thank you, but first we'd like to introduce you to a friend of ours. PTP: Wait, put these on first. PTP removes four ear protectors, like the ones used by airline workers. Numark, M V Pete and PTP put theirs on while Sniper tosses his to the crowd. MVP: And now, the New Champion of the world. SAMURAI BOB. The arena darkens and "F*ck That" hits. The pop is deafening as the Usual Suspects still have to cover their ears. Bob steps out from the back with a million dollar smile. With the belt draped over his shoulder he makes his way to the ring. At this point, the Usual Suspects are deaf and blind from the pops and camera flashes. Bob removes the mic from his pants and begins. Bob: Weelll! Huge crowd pop...people in Finland are saying "What the f**k is that?" Bob: Some people have been asking what I've been doing since Mayhem. Well to be honest. I've been s**t faced and sooooo f**ked up I couldn't tell you. This win has been a long time coming...the sun hasn't set on my career....and the darkness is without gold and without direction. Crowd pop! Bob: Now our focus moves on to Stable Wars. The greatest the XuW has to offer battling for bragging rights...battling for glory...battling for the future of XuW! The men in this ring are the best of the best, supreme athletes who know and live words like sacrifice...selflessness...and honor. At Stable Wars this ethic...this philosophy...is gonna bring the glory to the Usual Suspects! Tell em' Sniper! Sniper: You see, I am here to destroy the XUW. And to the stables, let me give you a demonstration. This mic<holds up the mic> This mic is the other stables in the XUW. This is what I'm gonna do to you. Sniper begins eating the mic. Before he can do any damage to it Bob grabs the mic and passes it to Numark. Nu: June 2, 2002... a date which will live in infamy. A great war is scheduled for that day. There will be a lot of blood, a lot of sweat, a lot of broken ego's, and probably some broken bones to go along with it. Bragging rights?... nah, it goes much further than that... this is what it is all about... people align themselves with others for power, strength in numbers and what-not... the sh*t talking will come to an end and the comedy will come to an end, it will be time for skill and adrenaline to take over... who has it in them? No one really knows... are we going to win it?... I don't know... What I do know, is that we are going to go out there, and give it our all... if we lose, hopefully it will be to the better team, if it is because of cheating, well, the other team will have to live with the fact that we were the better team. So come Big Brawl, you better hope that not only you are ready, but also your team mates are ready... because we are going out there as one in the form of four bodies, and we are going out there to win. Nu tosses the mic to M V Pete. MVP: Well said.... Just a warning to any that get in our way. The fire's been lit. I look at my three partners and all I see is INTENSITY. Bob is a reborn Champion, Sniper, well just look at the crazy bastard, and Nu, my tag partner, our greatest days have yet to come. The fun and games are over, The Usual Suspects are on a mission. We know what it takes to win. We've proved that already. And on June 2. The world will find out just how strong The Usual Suspects are. M V Pete throws the mic down. F*ck That hits and the crowd loses it as The Usual Suspects exit the arena. AT: Strong words from The Usual Suspects. They look determined to win Stable Wars. BJ: You could say the same about every other stable ya nincompoop. AT: Well, up next is our TV Title matchup, but before we head to the ring for that, here’s some footage of what happened backstage during our last match. The Ultitron shows Michael Bolling walking around backstage. All of a sudden, Lazarus jumps him from behind and they start to fight with Laz having most of the upper hand. Security and officials come and break the two apart. ”Psalm 69” hits as The Apostle makes his way from the backstage and to the ring. The fans don’t quite know how to react to the ex-Comedian, but pop anyways since he’s still quite humorous. He climbs into the ring and “Date With Destiny” hits as Michael Bolling makes his way down to the ring. He runs down to the ring and he and the Apostle lock up as the ref rings the bell. TV Title Match – Michael Bolling (C) vs. The Apostle Bolling gives The Apostle a kneelift and then winds up and sends him to the ropes. Bolling goes for a dropkick, but the Apostle hangs onto the rops. Bolling hits the mat and when he gets up, the Apostle nails him with a clothesline. Bolling gets right back up and sent back down with another clothesline. Bolling then gets up and is dazed. The Apostle takes him by the head and throws him into the turnbuckle. The Apostle then turns Bolling around and starts to deliver The Blessing (slam the head 10 times into the turnbuckle). Bolling falls back onto the canvas and The Apostle goes for a cover. 1… 2… Shoulder up! AT: The Apostle is making quite an impression with this match, Barry. BJ: Who knew Jehovah’s Witnesses would be so violent. AT: I don’t think he’s a Jeho- BJ: Lets just get back to the match. The Apostle picks up Bolling and sets him up on the turnbuckle to deliver the Tree of Salvation (Tree of Woe). He backs up and goes to deliver the dropkick to the face, but Bolling somehow lifts himself up just high enough that the Apostle slams into the ringpost groin first. Bolling falls off the turnbuckle and attempts to gather himself together. The Apostle starts to gather himself too, but before he can, Bolling nails him with a superkick. Bolling makes a cover and hooks the leg far back. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! Bolling can’t believe that the Apostle kicked out and shows his frustration by hitting the mat with his fists a couple of times. Bolling then picks up the Apostle and delivers an inverted atomic drop. Bolling then sets up the Apostle a couple feet from the corner and scales the turnbuckle. He goes to deliver the Moonsault, but the Apostle moves out of the way. Bolling hits the mat hard and gets to his feet due to the impact. The Apostle gets up and out of desperation he nails Bolling with the Genuflection (Forward Russian Legsweep). He drapes his arm over Bolling. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! AT: Bolling just got a shoulder up! What a match! BJ: The Apostle can’t put Bolling away. I don’t care if he’s a messenger from God, you can’t beat destiny! The Apostle gets to his feet as Bolling gets to his. For some reason, the Apostle is a little more wobbly. Bolling goes to nail him with a superkick, but he ducks and the ref takes the blow. The Apostle goes to give Bolling a clothesline, but gets nailed in the gut with a kick. Bolling then sets him up and delivers a piledriver. Bolling looks at the Apostle and signals for the Destiny Slam. He picks up the Apostle and puts him onto his shoulder. All of a sudden…… AT: What the hell is CK doing here?! BJ: I don’t know Parsley, but he’s up to no good. Bolling doesn’t even see CK as he has the Apostle on his shoulder. CK hops onto the ropes and pulls the Apostle’s feet down. Bolling falls backward as the Apostle lands on his feet and reverse the Destiny Slam into The Redeemer (Rikishi Driver). CK runs to the back as the Apostle makes the cover on Bolling. The ref slowly makes his way over and makes the slow count. 1… 2… 3!!! Winner and new TV Champ – The Apostle AT: CK just cost Bolling the match! BJ: And he just cost me $50. AT: Is there any logical reason to why CK would do this? BJ: Well Bolling did have the title for a very long time. AT: The streak! That must be it. CK wanted to remain the longest TV champ ever. BJ: Yeah, but that doesn’t even count. CK defended the title like three times that whole time period before Reborn, if that. AT: Well what the record book says is all that matters. A tech guy runs from the backstage and gives AT a sheet of paper. AT: Whoa, this is big. Fans, I’ve just received notice that this coming Monday on Mayhem there will be a tag team title match between The Royal Legion, Explicit Violence, and The Deadly Combination. BJ: You mean after the match the DC and EV had on Monday, they actually plan on giving the DC a shot at the tag titles that EV have had since their victory at Sudden Impact?! AT: Well, the match never ended with a clear cut winner. I’d say that this is a great match for both teams. The DC and Explicit Violence both deserve shots at the tag team titles and The Royal Legion deserves to have a challenge thrown at their way to prove that they’re worthy champs. BJ: Its that damn conspiracy again! The Royal Legion are getting screwed worse than- AT: Hey hey hey, this show is still actually on cable. We don’t need to be thrown off due to your foul language. BJ: F**k you. ”Rule Brittania hits as Henrik and Naron make their way out to the ring. The crowd boos heavily as Henrik and Naron ignore their jeers make their way down and climb into the ring. “Paranoid Android” hits as Woof Woof comes from the back to a pretty good pop from the crowd. He walks down to the ring and climbs in. Henrik gives the ref his Submission title and the ref holds it up and then rings the bell. Submission Title match – Henrik The King (C) vs. Woof Woof Henrik leans outside to talk to Naron and Woof takes advantage by jumping all over him. Woof starts wailing away on Henrik against the ropes as Henrik reels backwards. Woof then sends Henrik to the ropes and back body drops him. Henrik gets up holding his back as Woof picks him up and body slams him. Woof climbs to the top turnbuckle and goes to deliver a moonsault, but Henrik moves out of the way. AT: Woof appeared to be a little over zealous there. BJ: How dare he disrespect the king, but thinking he could put him away with a moonsault with such minimal offense. Henrik gets up and nails Woof in the back with a double ax handle. Henrik picks up Woof by the hair and goes to deliver the Stratford Strangle by surprise, but Woof counters and throws him over with a Judo throw instead. Henrik gets up and Woof charges at him, but he ducks and takes Woof’s legs out from under him and locks Woof in the Four Leaf Clover. Woof winces in pain, but manages to reach the ropes after about 15 seconds. Henrik releases the hold. Henrik shoves Woof into the corner and starts to kick him in the midsection numerous amounts of times. Henrik then shoves Woof to the canvas. Henrik scales the turnbuckle and looks to deliver the London Calling (top rope bulldog).Woof stumbles to his feet. Henrik comes off the top with the London Calling, but Woof grabs his leg on the way down and Henrik lands chest first onto the mat and Woof locks on the Anklelock! AT: What a counter to the London Calling! Woof may have Henrik beat! BJ: My Queen! Do something to help our King! Naron looks on with pure horror and runs to the other side of the ring. The ref looks down at Henrik to make sure he sees a tap. The ref has his back to Naron as she reaches into the ring and grabs Henrik’s hand and bring it to the ropes. The ref sees Henrik grab the rope and tell Woof to release the hold. Woof protests that Naron helped Henrik get to the rope. Woof and the ref engage in a very heated discussion as Henrik makes his way back to his feet. Henrik sees Woof distracted and catches him with the Stratford Strangle. He works his way down onto his knees as Woof struggles to get out of it. After a while, Woof can’t hold out any longer and he taps. Winner and still Submission Champ – Henrik The King AT: Dammit! Woof had that match won and that miserable little crumpet Naron had to cheat to get Henrik the win! BJ: Hey, Woof didn’t have to tap out did he? No, that’s what makes this title great. You could be knocked out cold and not know you’re being pinned, but you have a choice on whether you tap. What a glorious title. AT: Look what’s going on backstage. The camera heads outside where Michael Bolling is leaving the arena. He looks to be pretty pissed off. All of a sudden, out from the shadows steps Lazarus. Lazarus: Hey Bolling! How bout you and me have a little match at Mayhem? Oh, but there is one little problem. I can’t compete in an XUW ring, so how bout we take it to the streets? Bolling: You’re on, freak show. Come Mayhem, you’re gonna get your ass kicked by yours truly because its your fate and MY DESTINY! Bolling gets into his car and leaves as Laz laughs and makes his way back into the shadows. The camera heads back to ringside. AT: Looks like we got another match for Mayhem if you can call it that. I wonder when that suspension of Laz’s will finally be up. BJ: That’s a part of the conspiracy so he’ll probably have to get Bailey and RavenX on the case. AT: Oh brother. ”Eternal Life” hits as Sidhe and DDT make their way to the ring. A wave of boos, then cheers, then boos fluctuates around the arena as Sidhe and DDT look around trying to figure out what the fans want to do. They climb into the ring and “Gets Me Through” hits the speakers as the fans erupt in cheers as Admiral John and KsC TRuNKz make their way down to the ring. They run down to the ring and get into things with Sidhe and DDT. The ref rings the bell and the match is underway. Sidhe Nimh/DDT vs. Admiral John/KsC TRuNKz DDT and KsC start off as the legal men, but AJ and Sidhe are fighting in the ring as well. DDT takes advantage of KsC with some hard forearm smashes into the back. DDT throws KsC into the turnbuckle and starts to give him short clotheslines into the turnbuckle. He winds up KsC to send him across the ring to the other turnbuckle, but KsC reverses and DDT slams into the other turnbuckle, but instead of staying there, DDT takes the impact and goes after KsC, but KsC reverses and hits him with an ipponzei. DDT falls back into the turnbuckle as KsC scales it to deliver a 10 punch count, but DDT shoves him off. KsC runs back at DDT, but DDT catches him and delivers a belly-to-belly suplex to the outside of the ring. DDT goes over to his corner and tags in Sidhe. KsC climbs back in the ring and Sidhe catches him with a snap suplex. Sidhe scales the turnbuckle as KsC is down and he nails him with the Candy Flip. He goes for the cover. 1… 2… Admiral John makes the save! AT: Sidhe and DDT look to be dominating this matchup, Barry. BJ: The Horsemen are nothing compared to the darkness. Sidhe picks up KsC and goes to deliver a dragon suplex, but KsC flips through. Sidhe goes after KsC, but KsC catches Sidhe with a spinning spinebuster. Both men are down as their partners try and motivate them to get the tag. KsC makes his way over to AJ and gets the tag. AJ rushes the ring and gets his hands on Sidhe before Sidhe can make the tag. AJ nails Sidhe with the Tomahawk Spear. DDT rushes the ring and gets met by KsC. AJ picks up Sidhe and sends him to the ropes. Sidhe reverses and Admiral John comes off the ropes, ducks a Sidhe clothesline and bounces off the other side with a huge shoulderblock. AJ then sets Sidhe up on the top turnbuckle. AJ motions to the crowd for Anchor’s Away. He sets Sidhe up for it, but Sidhe counters with an unbelievable top rope bulldog. Both men are down. Sidhe struggles over, but makes a cover. 1… 2… 3!!! Winners – Sidhe Nimh/DDT AT: Sidhe and DDT barely escape with a victory, but Sidhe showed AJ why he is one of the best damn technical wrestlers in XUW today. BJ: That bulldog was incredibly vicious. Back in the ring, AJ and KsC try and gather themselves together when the Ultitron lights up with the shadowy figure. The figure says "You're on the trail, Horsemen, but the trail grows oh so very cold..." , and the lights go out. When they come back AJ's flat on his back while KsC looks about in confusion. "Sad... but true.." is said as the ultitron goes out... AT: AJ is out cold again and nobody knows why except for that damned shadowy figure. BJ: Well AJ is showing what’s going to happen to the Horsemen at Stable Wars. End up flat on their backs’. AT: Well now its time for our Intercontinental title matchup. Co-champs Blake Wolfson and Supa Thug Esco will battle it out to see who the rightful IC champ really is. ”Doggz” hits as Supa Thug Esco makes his way down to the ring. He gets in the ring and climbs the turnbuckle and motions that he’s going to walk out tonight as the champ. “I Stand Alone” hits as Blake Wolfson comes out from behind the curtain to the crowds boos. He yells at the crowd as they continue to boo. Some ever throw boomerangs at him for some odd reason. He climbs in the ring and the ref rings the bell. Intercontinental Title match – Blake Wolfson (C) vs. Supa Thug Esco(C) Blake and Esco tie things up and Esco takes the advantage with a headlock. Blake throws him off and gets taken down with a knock down. Esco goes off the ropes as Blake ducks to the canvas. Esco runs over top, comes off the other side and gets caught with a belly-to-belly suplex. Esco flies across the ring and gets back up as he holds his back. Blake charges Esco, but gets backdropped out to the floor. Blake gets to his feet and Esco jumps over the top rope onto Blake as they both hit the floor. AT: Holy cow, Esco’s a big guy to be doing that. BJ: Blake didn’t see that one coming at all. That’s what its going to take to win this match. Plenty of surprises. Blake and Esco get up, but Esco takes the advantage and slams Blake’s head onto the apron. Blake stumbles backwards as Esco grabs his head again. He goes to throw Blake into the ringpost, but Blake counters and throws Esco instead. Esco hits hard and falls to the ground. Blake picks up Esco and sets him up for a Russian legsweep by the apron. Blake executes it, but holds on and delivers a forward Russian legsweep to follow. Blake then picks up Esco and rolls him into the ring. Blake goes for the cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! Blake picks up Esco by the head and sends him to the ropes. Blake ducks and Esco stops real quickly, puts Blake under, and delivers The Drive-By. Esco goes for a cover. 1… 2… KICKOUT! AT: Both men are down. What a bout! BJ: Both men deserve to be the Intercontinental champion, even if Wolfson is a dirty Aussie that cost me a load of money. AT: Both men look to be digging down in their reserves. You gotta remember folks, these two guys had a stellar matchup on Mayhem. Fatigue has to be working its way in over the adrenaline rush. Blake and Esco get up and meet each other with lefts and rights. Blake takes control, but Esco ducks under a right hand and jabs him in the gut. Blake hunches over and Esco ducks down and picks Blake up by both legs and delivers Platinum Front. Esco goes for the cover. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! Esco takes Blake and goes to whip him into the ropes, but holds onto his arm and catches him with the Finisher, but Blake counters and catches Esco with a dropping reverse DDT. Blake hooks the leg. 1… 2… 3!!! And a shoulder up right after the 3 is registered! Winner and sole Intercontinental Champion – Blake Wolfson AT: And Blake escapes with the Intercontinental champion in a classic bout with Supa Thug Esco. I would not be surprised if these two guys met down the line and had another classic bout. BJ: You couldn’t tell who was going to win this match, it was that close. Just a great match. AT: Well that about wraps it all up for Vortex. We’ll see you on Xposure. For “Mr. Majestic” Barry Johnson, I am the Atomic Tomato and good night.
