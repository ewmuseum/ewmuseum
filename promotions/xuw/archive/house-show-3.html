XUW House Show #3

17 Feb 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW House Shows The lights dim then flicker and an explosion of pyro technics and load music rush through the stadium. AT appears at the top of the ramp to a huge pop. He walks down to the ring, rolls in, grabs a mic and begins to speak to the crowd AT: “Hello, good evening and welcome! Tonight we’ve got a great line up for all you die hard XUW fans out there in the arena! Starting off with La Familia taking on the un likely duo of Mr Mikey and Kevil. But before that match gets under way I’d just like to inform you, the fans, of a small competition we’re having here tonight. Since Mr Mickey’s found his wrestling boots again I’ve been left to hold this ship together with the likes of Harry Windsor, Elton John and even … Kat! So I’ve decided that beginning tonight were searching for a new commentator! So if you’d like to become a commentator and call the matches tonight then come on down!!! The crowd goes silent as AT searches the crowd for a hand. But there is nothing! AT: Anyone? Anyone at all? AT rolls out and continues to look into the crowd. His eyes light up as he reaches over the railing and grabs a young, pimply-faced hotdog vendor. AT: Here! You’ll do! As AT drags him to the announcer’s table the YPFHV kicks and screams. Tag Team competition: Mr Mikey and Kevil vs. La Familia They become seated and suddenly “One Step Closer” hits the speakers and Mr Mikey walks out with Ms Calender to a mixed reaction. He gets down into the ring and begins to pose it up. But before he can really start the lights go out and “Lynch” hits the speakers to moderate POP. Huge flames fly from the stage and as they subside Kevil is left standing alone. He poses for a second, and then runs to the ring sliding in. As he goes to run up a turnbuckle, Mr Mikey gets in his face and the two begin to argue. AT: WOW! Looks like some tension there! What do you think .. um .. sorry what was your name? YPFHV: my .. my names Simon. Did you want a Hotdog sir? AT: HOTDOG? Listen son, your working for me now! You do a good job here and you won’t have to sell those hotdogs no more! Simon: But I like selling hotdogs! AT slaps his forehead as “California Love starts to play through the speakers to some mild heat as La Familia strut out onto the ramp, flashing some weird signs and bouncing down to the ring. They roll in and strike some poses before Shorty gets to the outside. At the opposite end Mr Mikey is standing on the outside with his back to the ring. Kevil continues to talk to Mr Mikey before he turns around into a dropkick by Coco, knocking Kevil back into MM, causing MM to fall down onto Ms Calender. The crowd boos heavily at the fact Ms Calender’s been hurt and MM gets back onto the ring, infuriated at what just happened. AT (Nudging Simon): Ooohhh, it looks like Ms Calender’s going to need some attention, hey Simon? Hey? Simon merely blushes as, back in the ring, Coco starts to go to work on Kevil, much to MM’s delight. Coco lands some stiff forearms to Kevil’s back, before lifting him up, bouncing off the ropes and collecting Kevil with a clothesline. Coco leans over the ropes at the crowd and yells something at them. The crowd may not have understood but they boo heavily anyway. Coco turns back to Kevil who is now getting back to his feet. Coco delivers a knee to Kevil’s head then lead’s him over to Shorty where he Tags him in. Shorty quickly goes to work on Kevil with some knees to the forehead and some stiff forearms to the back. AT: It certainly looks as though La Familia is cornering Kevil. He’s going to have to get out of there or he’ll be in real trouble! What do you think Simon! Simon (Sobbing to himself): I .. I .. I just want to sell hotdogs!! WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA! AT: GET A HOLD OF YOUR SELF, MAN!!! Simon gets up and runs off much to the crowds delight! Back in the ring Shorty is really taking Kevil to school. Shorty picks Kevil up and then slams him down to the matt. He slowly picks up Kevil and sends him into the ropes. But as Kevil gets near the ropes he jumps and springs off them, hitting Shorty with a Evil Sault, which sends Shorty tumbling backwards. The last move took a lot out of Kevil and he struggles to crawl his way over to MM. Shorty, feeling the effects of Kevil’s knees smashing into his face just moments earlier, is also crawling and trying to get to Coco. As he gets closer MM tags him and runs in but is met with a cross body block by Coco, which sends MM down. Coco gets to his feet and runs up the turnbuckle he spins around and as MM gets to his feet Coco hits a Hurricanirana on MM send him into the ropes near an exhausted Kevil. MM gets up and slaps Kevil on the back and ducks out of the ring. AT: That damn coward! I never! After all the things you go through with a guy you think you know him?!! Kevil, feeling the slap, looks down at MM being attended to by Ms Calender and raises his arms in the air as if to say “What the?” But as he’s doing so Coco runs up, spins him around and flips him in. Shorty rush’s the ring and quickly set’s him up for a pile driver. He executes the La Razza with Coco pushing down for added impact! AT: LA RAZAAAAAAA!!!!!!! Shorty slides out and Coco covers for the pin 1 … 2 … 3!!! Kevil shrugs out but it’s to late. Coco and Shorty slide out and give each other a hug, before going back up the ramp, passing MM and Ms C. Kevil gets up on his arms and glares up at MM. Winner: La Familia AT: Oh man, you gotta know those two are going to mix it up again soon. And when they do Kevil will be looking for revenge!! Ok, now during that match I’ve gotten word there’s someone on the staff that wants a shot at calling the matches with me. So Rik, I know your back stage, come on down!!! As Rik comes out he runs down to the announces table, smiling all the way. AT: Well hell Rik you look …. Rik: AT, I can call you AT can’t I? AT I’m stoked at being given this chance!! AT: Well that’s great Rik! Next up we got … Rik: Single competition AT! SINGLES!!!! Between Dave Kipfer and Rujasu! WHOOOOO!!!! AT: Ok? Singles Competition - Dave Kipfer vs. Rujasu “One of a kind” hits and Dave comes out, package and all. The girls in the crowd scream and cheer. As he walks down to the ring, pointing and blowing kisses at them. Rujasu then comes rushing out. He pause’s on the ramp for a second whilst he taunts the crowd. Then continues into the ring where the two men lock up straight away. Sending each other to the mat with a series of arm bar takedowns and fancy flipping. Rik: THAT’S IMPRESSIVE! THOSE TO MEN HAVE SCOPED EACHOTHER OUT WELL!! AT grabs a near by phone and begins to dial. Back in the ring Rujasu gets the upper hand by sending Dave into the ropes and then levelling him out with a dropkick to the head. Dave gets back to his feet, eager to keep going, but is meet with a sharp Snap Suplex. Rujasu then quickly scale’s the ropes and then launches himself into a flying knee drop directed at Dave’s face. As Rujasu comes down Dave rolls out of the way and Ruj lands heavily, rolling and clutching his knee. Rik: oooooohhhhhhh, that’s gotta hurt! AT: Not as much as this …. AT nods and several shots are heard as Rik collapses in a bloody mess. AT smiles and nods approvingly before hanging up the phone. Meanwhile, Dave has shaken off the cobwebs and has Ruj up on his feet. He combines some quick kicks to Ruj’s knee and a few hard rights to the head, backing him back into the corner. He then lifts Ruj up to the top rope and then climbs up in front of him. As he climbs a little higher Ruj kicks out Dave’s leg, sending Dave sprawling to the matt. Ruj slumps off the corner and falls onto Dave. The ref goes down 1 … 2 … AT: KICKOUT!!! Dave kicked out! Dave gets to his feet and lands an elbow into the back of Ruj’s head. He then picks Ruj up and hits a Tornado DDT. Ruj looks out of it and Dave covers him. 1 … 2 … AT: KICKOUT!!! Ruj Just kicked out!! Dave gets up and walks to the ropes, he has a look of frustration on his face, but doesn’t look overly concerned. He turns back Ruj sits up and legs Dave, flipping him onto his back. He then gets to his feet and puts the Philly Cloverleaf on Dave. Dave reaches for the ropes, but Ruj drags him back towards the centre. He keeps the hold locked on until Dave can take no more and is forced to tap! Winner: Rujasu AT: Well that was a shocker. I thought Ruj was dead and buried! Now, since my first two candidates for the new announcers position have been failures then I think I might suspend this little event. Just then a small African American man jumps the barricade, wacks on some headphones and in a very high-pitched voice … Little Johnson: Hi! You haven’t given me a chace yet … A-tomic Tomartoe! AT: And you are? LJ: Whose am I? Whose am I? I thought you’d reconise one of  the NBA’s greatsh? AT (With a smirk growing on his face): NBA greats? LJ: YESH! I’m Magishic Johnshon! AT: You’re a fruit loop! LJ: Come now … A-tomic Tomartoe! Les just be friends! Singles competition: Blake Savage vs. Frank Vegas Before anymore of this ridiculous conversation can be heard “Hey Man – Nice Shot” comes on and a chorus of boos sweep the stadium. Blake Savage appears at the top of the ramp. He struts down, posing to some of the Ladies in the front row. Several become offended and begin to puke. Smooth Criminal hits and Frankie V comes out to moderate heat. He poses for a second before coming down the ramp. But he’s a bit distracted by all the vomit and doesn’t see Blake get to the top turnbuckle and execute a cross body block, which knocks Vegas down. Blake, quick on his feet, but a little sore from the high risk move, locks in the Blake – tastic and begins to shout into the crowd about how he is truly 100% pure sexual charisma! Vegas frantically taps the ground and screams in pain. The ref drops out and pushes Savage from behind, sending him hurdling into the barricade and knocking him out. LJ: Thash was down right desh-pic-a-ple! AT: He could’ve done serious damage with that move if the ref hadn’t off broken it up! Vegas gets to his feet, picks Blake up and rolls him into the ring as the ref sounds the bell. Vegas climbs up the corner and executes a flying elbow, landing heavily on Savage. Vegas covers for the pin 1 … 2 … kickout! Vegas rolls off, frustrated he couldn’t get the pin. He turns back to Blake and picks him up, but then sends him crashing back down head first through the use of a Wild Card (Piledriver). Blake stirs a little as Vegas covers him for the pin again 1 … 2 …….. kickout! AT: That was long three count! Vegas, extremely frustrated at the fact he couldn’t cover Savage, again picks him up. But this time goes for a Triple Sevens. But as he spins Blake up, Blake counters and the move is reversed as Blake delivers the Triple Sevens to Vegas. Blake staggers to his feet, still dazed from the Wild Card. He shakes it off and with an evil grin rushes up to Vegas and locks the Blake – Tastic in. This wakes Vegas up out of his concussion. Blake leans back into the move, causing Vegas to scream out in pain and eventually TAP!! Winner - Blake Savage AT: He tapped out!! What a win for Savage! LJ: I taut he was a going to! But jusht to my de-light …. he woone! AT: Well up next we got two monsters in Psycho Kinetic and Severson! TV Title Tourney: Psycho Kinetic vs. Severson Chic hits and Kinetic walks out, throwing bread sticks and French fries into the crowd. He gets in the ring and Severson storms out. He gets to ring and climbs in. And the match is off and running. Kinetic offers a bread stick to the monster Severson. But Severson lets him know what he thinks off his bread stick and gives Kinetic a huge boot to face sending him to the outside. Severson quickly follows him out and picks him up. Throwing the 6’8 man over the top rope!! Severson follows him in quickly, picks Kinetic up and then lands a devastating punch to Kinetics face! LJ: I believe thash move ish called The Black Eye’s! AT (Looking through his notes): That’s right! How’d you know that! LJ: I do my homework! Severson covers for the pin and scores an easy victory over the mental Kinetic! Winner: Severson AT: Well that was a easy win for the big man Severson, wait who’s that up on the ramp! Up on the ramp Hodgo can be seen applauding the win before smiling and walking off. AT: Well that was strange? Anyways up next we have The King Henrik taking on Spoonman! Submission Title tourney: Spoonman vs. Henrik the King Spoonman hits and no other then Spoonman walks out! But as he begins to walk down the ramp Henrik rushes him from behind knocking him forward, so he stumbles. Henrik continues the pursuit and knocks Spoonman again. The two men get to the ring and Spoonman turns around to block a sharp fist from Henrik. The two-man trade blows until Spoonman whips Henrik into the corner post. Henrik stumbles to the barricade. As Spoonman walks over to grab him, Henrik spins around and rakes his eyes. Henrik walks of towards the announcer’s table, but Spoonman isn’t down for long as he runs up behind Henrik, grabs him and tosses him into the steps. Henrik flips up and over them. Henrik gets back to his feet as Spoonman waists no time and comes around the corner, grabbing Henrik and planting his head into the announcer’s table. Spoonman does this thrice more before Henrik drops to the ground and rolls over near the stage. The ref and is down telling the two men to get back in the ring as Spoonman steps over to Henrik. Henrik then pulls something from under the ring and hits Spoonman squarely between the eyes with it. AT: DID YOU SEE THAT?? DID YOU SEE THAT?? Henrik just laid Spoonman out with a foreign object!! LJ: All I saw wash our King, ushing his strengh to other .. othe … overcome his opponent! AT: You’re not right in the head are you? LJ: no. The ref is blindsided by Spoonman and doesn’t see what happened. Henrik slides what ever it was back under the ring and gets to his feet, delivering a few kicks to Spoonman for good measure. Henrik then gets Spoonman to his feet and rolls him into the ring. Henrik stops and waves to the crowd as they boo him more, his face turns sour and he rolls into the ring. He lifts Spoonman up, then lays him out with a clothesline. He picks Spoonman up again and then executes a release German Suplex into the corner. Spoonman hits the corner heavily before staggering back and dropping down into the centre of the ring. Henrik stands and waves some more, but the crowd boo him and begin to throw things. Henrik’s face again becomes sour and he shouts something out into the crowd before reaching  down and putting the 4-leaf clover on Spoonman. Spoonman is in agony and tries to reach for the ropes, but can’t. Blood is running down his face from the shot that Henrik gave him with the object from under the ring. Spoonman screams out in pain but refuse’s to tap. Eventually the pain becomes too much and he is forced to tap out. Winner: Henrik the King Henrik stands up proudly and waves again. This time he gets hit in the head by a bag of crumpets! He becomes irate and shouts out into the crowd. He then leaves, stopping momentarily to pick up the crumpets. AT: Wow, what a night! Two men furthering themselves in their quests for gold and a few slobber knockers in between. I’ll see you all next week folks! LJ: And show will I! AT: No you won’t! LJ: I shink I will? AT: I don’t think so! The scene ends with Atomic Tomato and the mental who thinks he’s Magic Johnson arguing.
