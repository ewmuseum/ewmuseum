XUW Card Archive

XUW Mayhem #42

11 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem “BOOOOOOOOOM!!!!” go the pyrotechnics, “Mumble mumble mumble” go the indecipherable lyrics of the Mayhem theme, and “WHOOOOOOOOOO!!!” go all the fans crammed into the XUW Arena for the first show since the big XUW Liberation PPV! Yes, boys and girls, guys and dolls, blokes and lasses, it’s that time again, it’s XUW MAYHEM! The camera spins round fast enough to make a figure skater dizzy, and then slows down as the operator stops for a quick vomit. He regains composure to look at a few signs strewn around the arena. “BBQ Sauce fears Ketchup”, “Henrik fears promos” and “Jack fears everything” are the only ones that actually make it into focus before the camera swings down once more and comes to rest by the announce desk. Atomic Tomato: “Hello there and welcome to our first Mayhem show since our Australian Pay Per View extravaganza, XUW Liberation! I, sadly, am joined here as always by “Mr Majestic” Barry Johnson.” Barry Johnson: “Well, what a PPV it was too! We had a new Submission Champion, a new Hardcore Champion and new Tag Champions.” AT: “Yes, but what we did not have was a new World Champion. Samurai Bob defended his title successfully against Sidhe Nimh, scoring the first clean, one on one pin on Sidhe since XUW was Reborn.” BJ: “You can’t underestimate what that match cost Bob though, he looked truly dreadful afterwards, beaten to hell and back … but still a champion.” AT: “Speaking of which, I believe Sidhe Nimh has an announcement to make at some point during this show. I’m sure he’s gonna be after a rematch with Bob after their incredible match at Liberation.” BJ: “We also saw the comeback of NFM, he did eventually return.” AT: “Not only did he return, but Jack Nobbledy made another appearance in an XUW ring, though not too well, his Ripper persona still being lost to the world. I’m actually starting to feel sorry for that poor guy. Still we have a show to do here, and to kick us off we’re gonna see the debut of a new talent in XUW, “The Professor” Ric Austin. He will take on Sniper in a match that has seen a lot of talk between the two competitors.” BJ: “This is certainly the most we’ve heard out of Sniper since his arrival in XUW. He seems determined to ram Ric Austin’s words right back down his throat.” AT: “It’s certainly going to be a clash of personalities, and of style.” “Unsung” by Helmet breaks through the crowd noise and the impressive bulk of Sniper makes his way down to the ring. “School’s Out” comes over the PA and “The Professor” Ric Austin comes out to the stage and smiles down at the crowd as if they were his students. They respond with abuse and a hail of plastic cups, which infuriates the Yale Graduate. He hurries down to the ring and looks Sniper up and down before tutting to himself. Singles Match: Sniper v “The Professor” Ric Austin Sniper charges Austin, but Ric ducks under, spins Sniper round and puts a Headlock on the bigger man. AT: “Ric Austin is a wrestler decidedly of the ‘old school’. He prides himself on his basic wrestling holds.” BJ: “Sniper, on the other hand, relies on power rather than wrestling prowess, but boy it can work sometimes!” Austin grinds in the headlock, and finally Sniper manages to send him to the ropes. Sniper looks to hit The Brick Wall (Big Boot), but Ric ducks and grabs Sniper, fastening him tightly in with another headlock. Sniper once more whips Ric to the ropes, and this time connects with a Clothesline. Ric goes down, but Sniper picks him right back up and hits him In The Crosshairs (Rikishi Driver). Sniper goes for the cover. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Austin rolls to the corner and tries to clear his head. Sniper advances on him, but Ric dodges to the side and grabs Sniper, locking on … a headlock. BJ: “Looks like Austin was right, he really has got that headlock down to a fine art.” AT: “I’m beginning to wonder if he actually has any other moves.” Austin grinds in the Headlock, and smiles to the crowd, raising a whole bunch of heat. Sniper counter with some punches to the ribs, and hits a mean looking Suplex. Sniper lifts up Austin and goes for another Suplex, but “The Professor” counters it and hits a Suplex of his own. Sniper gets up slowly, and Austin locks on … another Headlock! AT: “I think that suplex may have been an accident, he was probably looking for another headlock.” BJ: “He’s the undisputed master of the headlock.” AT: “I think Sniper might actually just submit out of boredom!” Sniper lifts up Austin and runs to the turnbuckle, leaving Ric sat on the top of them, still refusing to let go of the Headlock. Sniper pulls Austin off the top and goes to slam him, but Austin holds on and spins round, connecting with a Tornado DDT from the turnbuckle. The crowd pops huge for the daring move, and Austin springs to his feet, admonishing them for responding to such a move. He looks frustrated that he was forced to do that, and picks Sniper up, holding him in, you guessed it, a picture perfect Headlock. Sniper is growling with displeasure over the constant barrage of headlocks, and executes a beautiful overhead Belly to Belly Suplex, which causes Austin to let go of the hold. Sniper charges in at Austin, as the Prof gets up, and sets him up again for the In The Crosshairs, but Austin is wise to the move this time and slips out, grabbing Sniper’s arm and putting him in a Hammerlock. AT: “Oh my God! Can you believe it! He’s doing a non-headlock move!” BJ: “What a display of variation, and technical prowess. This man is a legend. I could watch him for hours.” AT: “As could I, but only if he was being beaten over the head with a stick the whole time. C’mon Barry, this is the dullest wrestler I’ve ever seen!” BJ: “Blasphemy! You just can’t appreciate it as you didn’t graduate from Yale.” AT: “Neither did you!” BJ: “True, but I did get a degree.” AT: “Only on mail order.” BJ: “… Shut up!” Sniper swings back a big elbow and catches Ric around the head with it, forcing him to let go of the arm. Sniper grabs Austin immediately, and hits Three Times a Charm (triple powerbomb) into a pin. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Sniper stays on the offensive, whipping Austin to the ropes and this time connecting with The Brick Wall. Austin rolls to the outside and shakes his head to clear away the cobwebs. AT: “Well that’s a smart move right there, perhaps his next move will be to try a move other than a headlock!” BJ: “He’s the master of the headlock, and you know it!” Austin rolls back into the ring, and Sniper immediately connects with a kick to the gut and goes for the Three Times a Charm again, but Austin knows about it this time and rolls away, spinning Sniper round and applying, yes, your guessed it, a Headlock! BJ: “The Headlock! The Headlock! It’s as good as over!” AT: “Will you stop?” The crowd are getting very frustrated, and a chant of “BORING, BORING” breaks out. The Professor just smiles at them all, and keeps grinding in the headlock. AT: “Please God, end this. I submit! I submit! Do you have a towel, I want to throw it in!” BJ: “You just don’t appreciate a good headlock.” Sniper manages to lift up Austin and then throw him down to the floor. Austin springs up and rushes Sniper, hitting the surprised big man with a Spinebuster. Austin drags Sniper back to his feet and looks to hit The Final Exam, but Sniper powers out of it and hits The Reload (Belly to Belly Side Slam). Sniper picks up Austin from the floor and goes to Gorilla Press him, but Austin wriggles out and slips behind Sniper. The big man spins round and looks to Clothesline Austin, but he ducks and rolls Sniper up. 1 … … 2 … …. 3!!! Winner by Pinfall: “The Professor” Ric Austin BJ: “I knew it! The headlocks wore him down!” AT: “Austin steals one there with a crafty roll-up!” In the ring Sniper looks enraged, as The Professor looks smug and points out the roll-up showing on the Ultitron. Sniper comes across as The Professor tries to talk him through his mistakes. The big man grabs Austin and smashes him with Excessive Force, leaving Austin face down on the mat. AT: “Woah, I don’t think that Sniper took well to Austin’s reaction there.” BJ: “He’s a sore loser! The Prof beat him fair and square with some old-fashioned mat wrestling, and then Sniper just destroyed him with some brutal new style force. It’s just not fair.” AT: “Oh, shut up, now next we…” Suddenly, like a bolt out the blue, or an Idea from an Australian, "Smells like Teen Spirit" Hits the PA system and the lights go down, to be replaced by Blue and Purple lights. With the smoke machine working overtime, Factor emerges from behind the curtain coughing and spluttering. Walking down the aisle, the camera picks up on the fact that he's dragging the IC Belt along the floor. Picking it up and looking at it, he throws it over the top rope and into the ring.  Moping around the outside towards the Ring Announcer, he grabs the Microphone and crawls in under the bottom rope. Settling on his knees, he begins to speak. X: “It seems to me that xUw doesn't seem to know what to do with me. First they give me an opponent so worthless, so weak in Fatbadger that he gets beaten up by a fat, rancid, southern accented simpleton like Jim Ross. Then, when he gets taken out, I get placed before me the biggest piece of Homoeroticism since the last Deadly Connection Promo I can't understand it. All my life I've been very deprived, and when I finally claw myself back up to the heights from whence I came..I get shafted by the very organisation I work for. Is it any wonder the doctor said I'm "Clinically Depressed"? I work hard to claim the IC belt, and I hardly wrestle on a show. Then when I do get an opponent, it's wrestlers of the level of Fatbadger and Michael Bolling. But fear not… for I have a plan. Actually, I have two.” Factor crawls over to the IC Belt and picks it up, showing it to the Crowd. X: “ This...this piece of Gold Plated tin and leather is my key. My key out of obscurity, my key to the top, my key to leapfrogging all comers, all mid-carders, and yes… all the Main Eventers. Y'see, I realised that the company was not going to help me… so once again in my miserable existence called life, I was left to rely upon myself. So, I looked through that xUw Rulebook. I searched high and low. Then I found it. I was so happy I almost smiled... almost. Title shots in xUw mostly come to those who have pinned the champion… mostly. But there's an old xUw rule. A rule that states, and I Quote: ‘The Holder of the Intercontinental Championship may choose to vacate his Title belt, in return for a shot at the World Champion, at a time and a Date of his choosing. So, In accordance with the rules and regulations of xUw… I hereby do vacate THIS Intercontinental Championship Belt from this moment forwards… in return for a shot at Bob's World Heavyweight Championship, this coming Monday Night at Mayhem.” Factor simply drops the belt in the middle of the ring...and the crowd pop at the idea of Bob Vs Factor on Monday Night X: “Onto my seconds piece of news. For the last few weeks I've been talking about my mission, my calling if you will. Well, after I become the World Champion on Monday, my mission will enter its next step.” While Factor is saying this, a whole bunch of men in Black suits come down the aisle and surround the ring. One of them, a middle aged, grey haired man enters the ring X: “Excuse me… but who the hell do you think you are?” Man: “Mr Hughes. Can you come with us please?” X: “No… I will not come with you… who the hell do you think you are anyway…” Man: “That wasn't a question sir... that was an order” With that, the man knocks the Mic out of Factor's hand and the two start talking. It quickly escalates into a shouting match, with Factor shouting "NO" very loudly, and frequently. The man tries to grab onto Factor to drag him out the ring. Factor pushes him away..then receives a push himself. Out of nowhere, Factor strikes the man with the Evenflow DDT. He then grabs the mic and runs to leave the ring...but he's surrounded. Factor starts to speak X: “No... no, I'm not leaving.” Another man gets in the ring and talks to Factor Man: “Look, we understand you're upset..but you do not have the necessary Visa to work in the United States, so we have been sent here to deport you.” X: “No...this is just another ploy...George Bush is in on it. You all are....you'll never take me...NEVER!!!” Man: “You are welcome to apply for a visa when you get back to Britain...now MOVE.” With that, all the men pile into the ring and grab hold of Factor. He struggles against them, but there are too many of them. They grab him and cuff him, before they hoist him onto their shoulders and carry him out the ringside area. All the time Factor screaming obscenities that would make a Docker blush. AT: “Well, that was weird.” BJ: “I think that might be the last we see of him for a while, but he’s a slippery devil, he may well escape their clutches…” AT: “So Barry, what’s the next match?” BJ: “It’s Bonecrusher, defending his title against Blake Savage, and BoCru is coming off a good win at Liberation.” AT: “Indeed, he overcame the odds to retain his TV Title in a Three Way Cage Match, but not before some money was spent by NFM to hire DJ and Sev to take out BoCru’s opponents.” BJ: “Well, here he comes again, to lay it on the line, as he has to on every single televised XUW show.” AT: “Well, the belt didn’t get its name for nothing BJ!” “Hey Man, Nice Shot” by Filter roars through the speakers and Blake Savage comes down to the ring and climbs between the ropes. He looks focused, and doesn’t even bother taunting the crowd. “Here We Go (Let’s Rock ‘N’ Roll)” blasts out and the current TV Champion appears at the top of the ramp and poses with his belt. He makes his way down to the cheers of the crowd, steps over the top rope and gets ready to fight. XUW TV Title Match: Blake Savage v Bonecrusher(C) Blake starts strongly with a DDT and a Russian Leg Sweep, but Bonecrusher gets up from them, seemingly unharmed, and slowly walks towards Savage, towering over the returning Superstar. Bonecrusher picks up Savage as if he was a rag doll and hits a Stalling Vertical Suplex into a Slam. The ring shakes with the impact of Savage’s body, and Bonecrusher looks to capitalise with a Figure Four Leg Lock, but Savage rolls away and takes BoCru down with a Drop Toe Hold. Savage goes up top and catches BoCru with a Diving Thrust Kick from the turnbuckle, sending the big man back down to the canvas. AT: “What with the string of matches, and the beat-downs we’ve seen over the past weeks, it’s a wonder Bonecrusher is still holding himself together. Could this be Blake Savage’s night to take advantage of the weary Bonecrusher?” BJ: “I hope so, I hate Bonecrusher.” AT: “Why’s that?” BJ: “I had a lot of money on him dropping that title at Liberation, damn him!” Savage goes for a cover. 1 … … 2 … SHOULDER UP! Blake continues his surprising dominance with a Blake-Plex and a Standing Leg Drop. He climbs to the top turnbuckle once more and comes off with a Shooting Star Press, but BoCru raises his knees and Savage gets them planted right into his ribs. He drops to the floor in pain, and Bonecrusher rises back up. He picks Savage up with one big hand and whips him to the ropes, hitting a Jumping Side Kick as Savage rebounds. It connects heavily, and BoCru pushes his weight down in a pin attempt. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “Savage just barely kicked out there, and it must expend a lot of energy getting the 330+ pounds of wrestler off your chest.” BJ: “I’ve heard you know a lot about that Aubergine!” Bonecrusher hits a Running Neckbreaker Drop on Savage, and then goes up top for the Big Enforcer Elbow Drop. He flies off with amazing agility for a man his size, but Savage rolls out of the way at the last minute and tries to take advantage by locking in the Blake-tastic, but BoCru’s legs are still too strong to get it fully locked in, and Savage gets kicked off to the ropes. BoCru gets back up to his feet, but is staggered by a Clothesline from Savage. He reels back, and again as Savage hits another Clothesline. Savage tries for a third to get the champion off his feet, but BoCru straightens up and smashes Savage with a Pulling Walk Slam. BoCru with a lateral press. 1 … … 2 … … FOOT ON THE ROPES! AT: “Savage is in trouble now, he barely escaped that pin attempt.” BoCru picks up Savage and puts him in a Torture Rack, but Savage gets one leg free and kicks the big man in his head. Savage slides to freedom and hits the Blake-sational Neckbreaker. BoCru is down and Savage once more tries the Shooting Star Press… this time connecting with it! Savage grabs the big man and locks his legs up in the Blake-tastic (Texas Cloverleaf/Walls of Jericho combo)! AT: “That’s incredible strength for a man his size and build. He’s got his submission finisher locked in. Will the TV Champion tap out?” Slowly Bonecrusher moves himself towards the ropes, seeming forever out of reach, but getting gradually closer and closer … and … yes! Bonecrusher makes the ropes, and Savage has to release the hold. BoCru gets unsteadily to his feet, and Savage immediately goes down for the legs again, but gets a hard Karate Kick to the head for his troubles, felling him instantly. Bonecrusher ascends the turnbuckle and flies off, hitting the Big Enforcer Elbow Drop! He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner, and STILL XUW TV Champion: Bonecrusher!!! AT: “All credit to Blake Savage, he really held his own in there, this was another tough victory for BoCru, but a victory it was and he’s fast becoming one of the most impressive TV Champions we’ve seen recently in XUW.” The competitors all make their ways to the back, and the crowd gets a little restless, before "Hello, Time Bomb" by Matthew Good Band hits and out come The Royal Legion to a chorus of boos. Bailey is walking down the ramp, followed closely by the newest member of the Commonwealth, Danyon, who is still sporting the popcorn vendor uniform. They make their way into the ring and Bailey grabs a mic. Bailey:  “Hello, Commonwealth fans.  These are strange times in the Commonwealth these days.  Naron is out indefinitely, Henrik lost the Submission Title are on the bad side.  But on the good side Hodgo won the Hardcore Title and in process become the only wrestler to hold 4 titles in XUW.  And of course, the reason why I am here, The Royal Legion was resurected on Sunday with a new member Danyon.  I told you all that I would not disapoint on Sunday.  The Royal Legion would win.  I never, ever had any doubts that I wouldn't find someone as talented as Danyon.  I would never let the Commonwealth fans down.  I would never do that.  I am the greatest tag team wrestler of all time in XUW, I knew what I was doing the whole time.  And without further ado, I want to give the mic over to the man of the hour, the reason you all came here tonite.  I give you the newest member of the Royal Legion and the Commonwealth..........Danyon.” Danyon:  “Thank you Bailey for the warm introduction.  First off, I want to tell you all about myself.  I am the best wrestler to come out of New Zealand. I have won every major title in New Zealand.  If I didn't win it, it wasn't worthy enough for me to win.  I have also won many titles all over the Southern Hemisphere.  There was nothing really left for me to do down under.  I was getting bored with wrestling.  But then I heard about Bailey's search for new recruits and of course, I came to help out.  I am very loyal to the crown, so how could I say no.  I thought that this was an excellent opportunity for me to wrestle some new people.  I finally had a chance to go up North and wrestle, to show off my great skill.  And what better way to go, then fight with the greatest tag team wrestler of all time.  I couldn't say no.  The best wrestler in New Zealand with the greatest tag team wrestler of all time, such a amazing combination.  We will be unbeatable, unstoppable. Because who is better then Danyon?.........nobody.” Danyon hands the mic back to Bailey. Bailey:  “So, there you have it Commonwealth fans.  The Royal Legion is back, better then ever.  We are not going to stop until we are at the top.  No tag team is going to stand in our way.  DC, Boomer Sooners, Technicians, "...", Team with no Name, it doesn't matter who it is, we are sending out a warning to everyone.  Watch your backs.  The Royal Legion is hungry. Sunday was the beginning.” Bailey drops the mic.  "Hello Time Bomb" hits once more as Bailey and Danyon exit the ring. AT:  “Wow, that was a new Bailey we saw here tonite.  He looked more serious. It looks like he means what he says.  And this Danyon character, he shouldn't be taken lightly.” BJ: “Of course, he shouldn't be taken lightly.  He is a member of the Commonwealth.  Do you think that Bailey's partner would be some lowlight.  He has to be elite just to qualify as a Commonwealth member.” “Big Bad Moon” hits the speakers, and Admiral John comes down to the ring to a cacophony of heat. He is accompanied by The Grand Wizard, who seems all smiles about the encounter that will soon take place. “Change (In The House Of Flies)” plays, and Kata gets a good reception as he comes down to the ring. The bell sounds and the match is on. Singles Match: Admiral John v Kata AT: “Well, talk about history here! Kata and AJ were both Horsemen, but those days are gone now, and AJ has become selfish under the watchful eye of The Grand Wizard. AJ cost the Horsemen their match at Liberation, and maybe even their very existence!” AJ starts strongly, whipping Kata to the ropes and then hitting the Tomahawk Missile Spear. Kata rolls away from the clutches of AJ, and then charges him, sliding under his legs and then applying a Sleeper Hold from behind. As AJ weakens, Kata releases the hold and hits a Bulldog to take the Admiral to the floor. Without missing a beat, Kata applies a Hal Crab to the former XUW Commissioner. AJ is close to the ropes though, and manages to grab one, forcing Kata to release the hold. AT: “Hey, what’s this? Sheri Dedmon is coming to ringside!” BJ: “It’s always good to see Sheri here, but which side will she be cheering on? Her friend Kata, or her ‘friend’ Admiral John?” Kata whips AJ to the ropes, and then hits some hard Knife Edge Chops on the newest gWo member. The crowd gets going as Kata backs AJ up into a corner and continues to chop the hell out of him. Sheri seems agitated by the side of the ring, unsure whether to cheer or to boo, to laugh or to cry. AJ finally fights back by grabbing Kata by the throat and throwing him into the turnbuckle. AJ hits a few Reverse Elbows and then tries for a Monkey Flip, but Kata just pushes him off and locks on the Fading Memory (STF with a Chicken Wing). AJ is struggling, but as the ref checks on him, The Grand Wizard smacks Kata round the ankles with his cane. Kata releases the hold and glares at the gWo leader, allowing AJ sufficient recovery time to get up and nail Kata with a Running Powerslam. AJ covers. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! AT: “That damn turncoat Admiral John almost stole one thanks to his nefarious manager there!” BJ: “Yeah, but look at Sheri, she doesn’t know whether to go over there and smash The Wiz one in the face, or thank him for saving AJ!” AT: “I’m sure she would rather have Admiral John win by fair means if he is to win at all.” BJ: “You’re such a goody goody aren’t you?” Admiral John whips Kata to the ropes and goes for a Short Arm Clothesline, but Kata ducks it, kicks AJ in the stomach and hits The Edge of the Night (Inverted Piledriver). AJ is laid out, but before Kata can cover, The Wiz gets up on the apron and starts to complain to the ref. Kata goes over to get involved too, but Admiral John gets up and smacks him with a huge Low Blow. AT: “Ouch.” BJ: “Ouch.” AJ grabs Kata, who is doubled up in pain, and hits a big Atomic Drop. He rolls up Kata, and grabs a handful of clothing for good measure. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner, by pinfall: Admiral John!!! AT: “Oh, that’s just disgusting! The old AJ would never have resorted to such tactics to get a win. Kata was cheated here, plain and simple.” BJ: “Hey, I’m starting to like this new Admiral John, I could get to like him!” Everyone leaves to the back, and the crowd gather together in anticipation of something happening. Would you believe it, something happens. "I Don't Mind" hits as Sidhe and Kat make their way out to the ring to the normal, very mixed, and very loud reaction. Sidhe looks around for a moment, and then signals to the truck to cut the music. He's got the mic. AT: “Sidhe said he had some kind of big...” Sidhe gives the Tomato a dirty look. Sidhe: “Thanks. As I'm sure you all know, Bob and I beat the hell out of each other on Sunday. It was the kind of match that makes you remember why you're in this business. Why you drag your ass out here every night to entertain the unwashed masses, so to speak. And, for me at least, it reminded me of how far I've gone from when I was having fun out here.” The crowd's gotten quiet. Sidhe: “Remember Beyond The Mat Specials? Remember Kat and I screaming lines from Pulp Fiction at Hodgo in a Burger King? Remember when you never saw me in the gym...unless I was smoking in there or making fun of someone working out?” Sidhe grins. Sidhe: “I'm sure some of you even remember the original Fear and Loathing promo that mysteriously disappeared. Kat and I used to push the envelope of what was acceptable in promos...now we sound like every other wannabe badass chasing Bob. We used to make people laugh...now we sound like broken records. Some people, of course, would do the logical thing - go talk to creative, rework their character, come back next week completely repackaged. But...well...doing the logical thing would kinda go against the entire point of us, wouldn't it?” Kat: “So, we came out here to say we're leaving for a while...and going to the Interdimensional Circuit with Ikke. Kind of a personal bildungsroman.” Sidhe: “If anything can restore our previous insanity, that would be it. We'll be back...eventually. In some unknown form, possibly.” Kat: “So...for the last time for a while...rock..” Sidhe: “Hit our music.” U2's "Children of the Revolution" hits as Sidhe and Kat leave to a nicely sized pop...off to unknown adventures. AT: “Did you...” BJ: “Well, he's nuts.” AT: “I suppose so. Well, we have a #1 contender shaped gap in the roster now, but I’m sure that there are many deserving wrestlers out there eager for such an opportunity.” BJ: “Indeed there are, but on with the show!” “Fear” hits the speakers, and as it yells “Fear something again!” there is a huge explosion of pyro, and through it comes NFM, ready for battle against his former stablemate, Dev “Build Fear” hits, and Dev comes out, all business, and hurries to the ring to do battle with NFM. Singles Match: NFM v Dev BJ: “Why do they keep mentioning the word FEAR in all these songs? I just don’t get it.” AT: “Uhhh, because they were both in the F.E.A.R. stable!” BJ: “Oh! That spells FEAR! I always did wonder…” In the ring, NFM starts by whipping Dev hard to the ropes, and then locking on a Sleeper Hold. He takes Dev down to the mat and tries to lock in the Fear Of Death, but Dev wriggles out and takes NFM down with a Drop Toe Hold. Dev grabs NFM with a Headlock on the ground, to the groans of the headlock-saturated crowd, but then he inverts it and locks his legs round the body of NFM, performing the Lockdown. AT: "Wow, some great technical wrestling skills there from Dev. He may even have it won already, this is a debilitating hold he's got locked in here." NFM slowly struggles to the ropes, and finally grabs hold of the bottom one. Dev reluctantly releases the hold, and NFM staggers to his feet while clutching at his throat. Dev grabs him quickly and hits a DDT, then going for a cover. 1 ... ... 2 ... ...KICKOUT! NFM gets back to his feet, and Dev runs off the ropes coming back with a Spinning Heel Kick, but NFM ducks and swats him away. Dev comes back up, but NFM stops him in his tracks with a Big Uppercut, and then a Jumping Roundhouse shot to the chest. Dev staggers into the ropes and comes back right into the Last Rites (Alley Oop Powerbomb). Dev somehow gets back to his feet, but NFM hooks him with an Inside Cradle. 1 ... ... 2 ... ... KICKOUT! AT: "NFM almost returned with a victory then. He's certainly looking refreshed in this match now." BJ: “He accomplished a rare thing in XUW at the last PPV: A surprise return that was actually a surprise!” NFM is relishing it in the ring, and he grabs Dev round the throat, hitting the Final Destination (Chokeslam). NFM picks up the groggy fighter instead of going for a cover, and tries to hit The Lightning Strike, but Dev has just enough strength to push himself away from NFM. The No Fear Man approaches Dev, and gets a kick sliding narrowly past his head. NFM backs off slightly, and then charges at Dev, who dodges to the side, allowing NFM to smash shoulder first into the turnbuckle. Dev hits a big Release German Suplex that ‘folds him up like an accordion’. Dev goes for a pin. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! AT: “Dev almost got back into the game with a big win there. It’s too hard to call right now. Will NFM get the big comeback win, or will Dev restore some long overdue pride to the ranks of F.E.A.R?” Dev and NFM get back to their feet, and Dev whips NFM to the ropes. Dev looks to hit a Superkick, but NFM dodges and hits a Spinning Clothesline. NFM picks up Dev and looks once more to hit The Lightning Strike, but Dev hits a short Throat Thrust, and gets NFM up into a Fireman’s Carry and then hits a Michinoku Driver, thus completing the Devolution! He hooks the leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! Winner, by pinfall: Dev!!! AT: “Wow, Dev gets this one! I must say Barry, there’s been a lot of very close matches here tonight that could have gone either way. It’s been a night of tense finishes. BJ: “Hold on their Fruitcake, I'm being told by my director that we have a live outside feed coming through!” We are taken outside the arena, where the men are carrying X Factor on their shoulders still. One of the men leave to open the back of the Wagon they are in. Inside the wagon are people from many different nations... although from the looks of it, mainly Mexicans and Cubans. They remove Factor’s handcuffs and throw him in the back. Factor lands on a little boy whose Father takes exception to Factors rude landing. Mexican: “¡Hey, el señor, consigue de mi muchacho! Usted pedazo de piss arrogante de scum..I en sus madres graves.” X: “No....You can't do this. THESE ARE MEXICANS! NO... NO!” The men lock the Wagon tight shut and start to drive off. Blake: “I should have won that match! Damn Bonecrusher! Damn him!” Just off to the side sits Kevil, with his Dr. Seuss hat perched firmly on his head. He laughs at Savage and begins to speak. Kevil: “This Savage man is such a punk. It seems his confidence is sunk, And all he needs is just to chill. Of antics, his, I’ve had my fill!” Kevil laughs like a madman, and the camera cuts back to the announce desk. AT: “Well, I don’t know what that’s all about, but I do now one thing, and that’s the next match is going to be our Main Event for the night!” “Greed” by Godsmack plays, and KoRe and Woof Woof come down to the ring. Kore looks quite hyperactive, but Woof looks almost sullen, and not too into the prospect of a match with the Sooners. “BOOM” by P.O.D. plays, and the crowd erupts with chants for the Boomer Sooners and their newly won XUW Tag Team Title belts. Dave Kipfer and Chase Wilson pose for the crowd at the top of the ramp, and then run down to the ring, throwing bottles of BBQ Sauce into the crowd to make themselves even more rabidly popular. XUW Tag Team Championship Match: gWo (Kore and Woof Woof) v The Boomer Sooners (C) Kore starts the match off with Chase Wilson. They lock up, and Chase uses his size and strength advantage to push Kore back to a neutral corner, and he then looks to grab hold of the self-proclaimed High Roller, but Kore slips past him and hits the Obscure Reference (Reverse DDT). Kore lays the boot in as Chase tries to get in, and then tags in Woof Woof. Woofie enters the ring just as Chase has gotten up, and Woof finds himself on the wrong end of the CT Crunch (Burning Hammer). Chase picks up Woof and tags in Dave Kipfer, allowing the Boomer Sooners to hit the One Legged Man In An Ass Kicking Contest. AT: “I love that move, but it’s just so darn hard to call properly. Remind me never to have a comeback against these two young BBQ guzzlers!” DK hits Woofie with the Viva Las Vegas (Blockbuster) and then a Flying Elbow Drop from the second turnbuckle. Kipfer covers. 1 … … 2 … … NO! Kore enters the ring and breaks up the pin by stamping on the head of Kipfer. The tag champion looks angry, and whips Woof to the corner of the gWo, allowing Kore to tag in. Kore goes straight for Kipfer, and the two men lock up. Kipfer hits a Snap Suplex out of nowhere, and goes up top for a Five Star Frog Splash, but Kore moves at the last possible moment and DK lands hard. Kore picks up the ailing DK and hits a Piledriver, and then an Elbow Drop to the back of DK’s head. Kore poses, and taunts the crowd, asking how good the Sooners are looking now against the High Roller. He soon lives to regret the cocky gesture though, as Kipfer flips back up to his feet and grabs Kore bodily, hitting the Eulogy. Dave jumps to the turnbuckle and hits a picture perfect Split-Legged Moonsault on Kore. He hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … NO! This time it’s Woof Woof who breaks up the pin and gives the gWo a lifeline. Chase picks up his opponent as he comes back, and hits a huge Twisting Spinebuster, which Dave grabs and Diamond Cutters to the ground, hitting The Slobber Knocker! Dave hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!
