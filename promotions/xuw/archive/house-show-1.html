XUW House Show #1

19 Jan 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW House Shows DDT vs. Halloween Hardcore Rules "The God That Failed" by Metallica hits the pa system. Halloween walks out from behind the curtains to a crowd of boos and he taunts the fans as he walks to the ring. He rolls into the ring and then stands up and walks to his corner. "Creeping Death" by Metallica hits and DDT comes to to a mixed crowd he walks to the ring. As he gets to the ring he walks around grabing chairs and throwing them into the ring. He then gets under the ring and pulls out a garbage can with some other things in it and he throws it into the ring. He also pulls out a couple of tables and leans then against the ring. As he does this Halloween grabs one of the chairs and rolls out of the ring on the opposite side and kneels down. He stalks over to the side where DDT is sitting tables against the ring and when DDT turned around right into a hard chair shot from Halloween. DDT stumbles back and gets hit with another very hard chair shot. DDT falls to his knees and Halloween hits him a third time with the chair. DDT falls back on his knees and Halloween raises the chair and plays to the crowd. He then throws the chair down and pulls DDT up. He pulls him to the ring and throws him in Halloween gets on the apron and climbs to the top rope waiting for DDT to stand up. As DDT is getting up he glances behind him and see's Halloween perched on the top rope, so as he gets up he also grabs on of the chairs he threw in. Halloween jumps off the top rope as DDT stands. Halloween goes for a flying clothesline but is met with a stiff chair shot to the face. DDT looks at the crowd and then begins to repeatedly smash the chair onto Halloween. After about six chair shots, he throws the chair down and then begins to repeatedly stomp onto Halloween. He then stops stomping and drops an elbow onto the back of Halloween. DDT rolls out of the ring and sits one of the tables up outside of the ring. He picks up another one and sits it ontop of that first one he setup. DDT then pulls them to where they are diagonal from the corner of the ring. Halloween begins to sit up in the ring. DDT gets in the ring and goes over to Halloween, he goes to pick Halloween up but gets a low blow. DDT falls to his knees and Halloween gives him an uppercut to the jaw and DDT falls back. Halloween stands up and grabs the garbage can, that DDT put into the ring, and sits it in the middle of the ring. Halloween picks up DDT and whips him into the ropes and gives him a spinning sidewalk slam onto the grabage can. DDT grabs his back and rolls over onto his stomach. Halloween runs over to the turnbuckles and climbs to the top rope, he looks over and see's the tables sitting there, then back at DDT, jumps off and gives him an elbow drop to the back. Halloween pulls DDT to his feet and punches him a couple times. Halloween then drags him over to the corner by the tables. He picks DDT up and crotches him into the ropes right by the turnbuckles. Halloween gets out of the ring and climbs up the turnbuckles. When he gets onto the second rope he leans over and pulls DDT closer and then Halloween puts one of his feet up on the top turnbuckle. He pulls DDT in between his legs and tries to lift him up into a powerbomb. DDT grabs the top rope and blocks it, he then low blows Halloween. Halloween loses his balances and gets crotches onto the top rope, this also causes DDT to lose his balance and falls into the ring. DDT lays in the ring as Halloween stands back up on the top rope and gives him a Guillotine Leg Drop. Halloween then covers him and only gets a two count. Halloween gets up and argues with the ref about a slow count. As he is doing this DDT starts to get up. Halloween turns around and see's this and kicks him and then pulls him up. He grabs DDT and goes to whip him into the ropes but DDT keeps ahold of Halloween's arm and pulls him in for a Short-Arm Clothesline but Halloween ducks it. He stands behind DDT and turns DDT around. When DDT turns around Halloween goes for a kick to the midsection but DDT grabs his foot, spins him around and then kicks Halloween in the gut and delivers The Shock Treatment and pins Halloween for the win. Winner: DDT Al Crud vs. "Psycho" Kinetic "Chic" by Le Freak hits and "Psycho" Kinetic comes walking to the ring smoking, shaking his head at the fans. He then gets in the ring and stands in the corner smoking. "One Burbon, One Scotch, One Beer" by George Thorogood hits and "The Bastard" Al Crud comes walking out. On the way to the ring he claps a couple of the fans hands but at one guy holding an "Al Crud's Workrate Sucks" sign, he turns around and farts in his direction. The guy falls back into his seat and Al laughs and then continues to walk to the ring and then gets into the ring. Him and Kinetic walk to the center of the ring and start talking trash to each other. Kinetic takes a drag from his cigarette and then blows the smoke into Al's face causing Al to start coughing. As Al is coughing Kinetic grabs Al and gives him a bodyslam. Kinetic drops an elbow on Al and then picks him up. He whips Al into the ropes and goes for a clothesline, but Al dunks. On the rebound Kinetic bends over for a backbody drop but Al stops and kicks Kinetic in the shoulder, as Kinetic stands up, Al hits him with a Crudder Line. Kinetic falls back onto the mat. Kinetic sits up but Al kicks him in the back and Kinetic falls back holding his back, Al drops a leg drop and then walks over to the ref and farts in his direction. He then goes back to work on Kinetic. He picks up Kinetic and whips him into the ropes Al goes for another Crudder Line but Kinetic dunks it and then stops a couple steps behind Al. As Al turns around he is met with Le' Guillotine! Crud falls to the mat hard. Kinetic pins Al and only gets a two count. He pulls up Al, he gets behind Al and locks on Le' Dream. Al starts to fight to get free but after about a minute or so. Al goes down to one knee. Kinetic locks it one harder and begins to jerk Al around for a minute until Al is on his side and Kinetic is laying above him. The ref is down checking on Al. As the ref is checking on Al, Kinetic puts his feet on the middle rope. The ref stands up and looks just as Kinetic puts his feet down. The ref goes back to Al and raises his hand once, it falls. He raises it again but Al keeps his hand up. Al starts to move around and he sits up and then gets up on one knee. He elbows Kinetic in the stomach twice and then runs into the ropes and goes to give Kinetic a Crudder Line but Kinetic raises a knee into Al's gut. Kinetic then grabs Al and gives him Ze' French Kiss and goes for the pin but once again at two Al gets his feet onto the ropes. Kinetic gets up and argues with the ref about a slow count and as he is doing this Al is getting up. Kinetic turns around and see's this, he walks over and goes to punch Al but Al blocks it and then kicks Kinetic in the stomach and gives him a Belly-To-Beer Belly Suplex, Al goes running to the ropes and comes back jumps into the air and lands in a sitting position onto Kinetics chest and farts performing The Scent Of Victory. Al gets up and Kinetic starts to roll around on the ground and then gets up swatting at the air trying to get the smell to go away. Al walks towards Kinetic but Kinetic comes running at Al and goes for a clothesline but Al dunks and turns around. When Al turns around Kinetic also turns to look at Al, Al kicks him in the gut and then gives him The Mother Crudder and goes for the pin getting the win. Winner: "The Bastard" Al Crud Matt Logan vs. Rujasu "Back In Black" hits and Matt Logan walks out. He walks down the ramp and starts shaking fans hands on his way to the ring. When he gets to the ring he starts walking around the ring shaking fans hands. He stops at one of the fans and signs something as he is doing this Rujasu walks down to the ring and gets into the ring and crouches in the corner as Logan continues shaking hands of the fans. After a couple minutes Logan gets into the ring. Matt and Rujasu lock up in the middle of the ring, but Matt gets the upper hand and pushes Rujasu into the corner. Matt lets go off Rujasu and does a clean break as Matt is backing up with his hands up Rujasu kicks him in the gut and then pushes Matt into the corner. He then kicks Matt a couple times and then gives him a chop. He does another one and then Matt grabs him and throws Rujasu into the turnbuckles and gives Matt a chop and then they start to exchange chops. After a couple of them Rujasu finally just kicks Matt in the stomach and gives him a DDT. Rujasu goes to the turnbuckle and climbs it waiting for Matt to get up. Matt starts to gets up and turns around. Right when Matt turns around Rujasu goes off the top ropes and goes for a missle drop kick but Matt jumps up with a standing dropkick and hits Rujasu. Matt then backs off and waits for Rujasu to get up. When Rujasu gets up Matt goes off the ropes and hits Rujasu with a Flying Forearm and then goes for a pin only getting a two count. Rujasu stands up and kicks Matt in the face and then goes over and climbs the turnbuckles. When Rujasu gets to the top rope he then jumps off and goes for a Flying Knee Drop but Matt moves and rolls out of the ring. Rujasu grabs his knee and kicks it a couple times. He then stands up and walks over to where Matt is just now getting on the apron. Rujasu goes to punch Matt but Matt dunks rams a shoulder into Rujasu and then does a sunset flip but Rujasu grabs the top rope so that he doesn't fall back. Matt pulls for all he is worth and it pays off becuase Rujasu loses his group and falls back but only for a two count. Rujasu and Matt both stand up at the same time Matt kicks Rujasu in the gut and whips him into the rope, Matt then hits Rujasu with a backbody drop. Matt then grabs Rujasu and sets him up for The Unprettier but somehow Rujasu overpower's Matt and turns it into The Rujasu-Driver and hits it. Rujasu then gets up and puts Matt in the Philly Cloverleaf. Matt fights for a minute and tries to reach the ropes but he can't take the pain and he taps out. Winner: Rujasu Bailey "The Animal" Steele vs. "The Comedian" Chris Blake. "Hockey Night In Canada" Theme music hits and Bailey comes down to some heat. He walks to the ring and gets in as "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne comes on. "The Comedian" Chris Blake comes out to a mixed reaction. He walks down to the ring and then gets in. Chris Blake gets on the turnbuckles and plays to the crowd. As he gets down Bailey comes running and splashes Chris in the corner. Chris falls back and onto the mat. Bailey walks over to that corner and starts eating at the turnbuckle. As he is doing this Chris gets up comes running and dropkicks Bailey in the back. Chris then grabs Bailey by the head and gives him The Ten Drink Minimum onto the half eaten turnbuckle. Bailey stumbles back and shakes the cobwebs outta his head. When he looks up he is met with a clothesline, Bailey falls down to one knee but gets back up. Chris climbs to the top rope and jumps off and clotheslines Bailey. Bailey goes down and Chris goes for the pin only getting a two count. Chris gets up and whips Bailey into the ropes and on the rebound Chris goes for a backbody drop but Bailey kicks Chris in the face and then when he is standing straight up Bailey kicks him in the stomach, and then gives Chris a DDT. Bailey goes for the pin and only gets a two count. Bailey picks up Chris and whips him into the ropes and gives Chris a powerslam and then goes for the pin, again only getting a two count. Bailey gets up and looks upset so he walks over and takes a bite out of one of the turnbuckles. He starts chewing on it as he walks back over to Chris. Chris grabs one of Bailey's feet and pulls it out from under it. It causes Bailey to start coughing on the turnbuckles piece in his mouth. Chris stands up and leg drops Bailey and gets a two count. Chris pulls Bailey up and whips him into the turnbuckle but Bailey reverses it at the last second and Chris hits chest first into the corner. He stumbles back right into Bailey who puts him in The Sleeper Hold. Chris goes to grab the ropes but Bailey picks him up off his feet and steps back into the middle of the ring. Chris tries to fight it for a minute but is soon on the ground. The ref raises Chris's hand and it falls, he lifts it again and it does the same thing. The ref raises it for a third time and it falls again, ending the match. Winner: Bailey "The Animal" Steele Raiden vs. Severson "Control" by Puddle Of Mudd hits and Raiden comes walking out. He  gets in the ring and poses to the crowd. Severson comes walking to the ring as Raiden is posing to the crowd and he gets in the ring. Raiden gets down off the ropes from posing and looks up at Sev. Sev looks down and laughs to him and tells him to take a shot. Raiden goes running off the ropes and clotheslines Sev. Nothing happens, Sev tells him to do it again and he does, the same thing happens.  Sev tells him to do it again, although this time when Raiden comes back he doesn't go for a clothesline instead he does a dropkick right into the knee of Sev. Sev stumbles back and Raiden does it again. Sev goes down to one knee and then stands, Raiden goes against the ropes and goes for a cross body. Sev catches him and starts to laugh, he takes a couple steps and then picks him up over his head and gives him gorilla press slam. Raiden lays on the ground holding his back. Sev  then walks over and picks up Raiden and gives him a powerbomb. Sev puts his foot ontop of Raiden and goes for the count. At two he takes his foot off of Raiden and picks him back up. Sev whips Raiden into the turnbuckles. Raiden stumbles out of the corner and then Sev grabs him arm and does it again, but this time Raiden climbs up the ropes and jumps off doing a moonsault onto Sev, completely catching him off gaurd causing him to fall back. Raiden grabs one of Sev's legs and pulls it up. The ref goes for the pin and Raiden actually gets the three count. Winner: Raiden The Deadly Combination vs. The Technicians "Got Yourself A Gun" by Nas hits the pa system and M V Pete and NuMark, The Deadly Combination, come walking to the ring to boos. They talk to each other and ignore the fans as they walk and get into the ring. "The Game" by Motorhead hits and the crowd pops for them, on the way to the ring they clap a couple of the fan's hands and then walk to the ring. As The Technicians walk beside the ring NuMark and MVPete run and baseball slide into BC causing him to fall into KsC, causing them both to fall over. NuMark grabs BC and throws him into the ring and MVPete stays on the outside fighting with KsC. NuMark  grabs BC and pulls him up. When he gets BC up he kicks him in the gut and then gives him a DDT. NuMark then gets up and stomps on him a couple times. He then puts BC in sitting abdominal stretch. On the outside KsC has just got whiped into the apron. MVPete runs at  him but KsC side steps him and then knees MVPete in the stomach. KsC then rams MVPete's head into the apron and looks into the ring. He then steps up onto the apron only to be pulled back down off it by MVPete. MVPete then grabs KsC and rams him into the steel steps. As KsC stumbles back into MVPete, MVPete grabs him and gives him a russian leg sweep on the outside. MVPete then gets on the apron and says something to NuMark. NuMark lets go of BC and picks BC up as he is doing this NuMark climbs the ropes. NuMark outs BC between his legs and goes to pull BC up but BC blocks and then gives NuMark a backbody drop, BC then jumps and hits the top rope causing MVPete to lose his balance and get crotched. KsC gets up on the apron and goes to the corner where MVPete is crotched. KsC climbs up the ropes and then steps over the top rope and puts BC in a headlock. KsC then signals something to the crowd and jumps off the top rope giving MVPete a top rope bulldog. BC then gets out of the ring, as does NuMark and they both go to their corners. KsC stands up and pulls MVPete to The Technicians corner and tags in BC. BC and KsC then preform a double team suplex onto MVPete. KsC gets out of the ring and BC pulls MVPete back up and whips him into the ropes, MVPete rebounds and is met with a clothesline and then an elbow drop. BC plays the crowd a little then goes back to work on MVPete. He pulls MVPete up and whips him into one of the corners. BC goes running and then goes running for a splash but MVPete side steps it. MVPete then  gives BC a stiff clothesline to the back of his head. BC falls to the ground holding his head as MVPete goes over and tags in NuMark. NuMark then goes over to BC, picks him up and executes a suplex. NuMark then goes and climbs the ropes he gets on the top rope and jumps off for a five star frog splash, but gets only canvas becuase BC rolled out of the way. BC slowly makes his way over to KsC and gets the tag. MVPete comes running in only to be met with a clothesline, he gets back up and is met with another one. NuMark gets up and KsC gives him one as well. BC is standing and gives MVPete a superkick, right when BC connects with the superkick, KsC comes running and gives MVPete The Critical Shot (Goldberg Spear). MVPete falls out of the ring. BC then walks over and picks NuMark up and puts him on his shoulder. BC then backs up into the corner and walks up then and sits on the top turnbuckles, as he is doing this KsC is climbing the other corner. BC then see's that KsC is on the top rope and he jumps off and gives NuMark the Nail In The Coffin and as son as BC stands up KsC comes flying off the top rope connecting with the flying headbutt, thus performing The Execution. KsC then goes onto getting the pin for the win. Winner: The Technicians Yeah.. Well that was the first house show back.. I will be back here later with a match listing for the next one...
