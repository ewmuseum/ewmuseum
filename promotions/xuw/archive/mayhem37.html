XUW Mayhem #37

6 Jun 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem As is traditional immediately following a PPV, the effects budget for the week is blown, and we get the Official XUW Fat Shirtless Guys with Kazoos and Sparklers, kazooing some indecipherable song. So Mayhem is on, kinda. We cut to the Atomic Tomato and Barry "The Majestic" Johnston. AT: What a show we have lined up tonight! HBK, The Ripper, and Raven X have all been shelved for a while, sad to say. We wish them all the best of luck in their recoveries. Anyway, coming off of the incredible Big Brawl pay-per-view, we've got MV Pete against Blake Wolfson for the IC Title as the main event. BJ: Yes! Blake Wolfson! Who single-handedly won stable wars for the Army of Darkness! AT looks at Barry like he's seeing a herring in a privet bush. AT: Yeah....sure. In other post-Stable Wars activity, Sidhe faces Numark, looking for revenge from the Stable Wars finals, and looking to send a message to world champion, Bob. BJ: Kevil faces CK for the TV Title, as the pain comes to the root of all evil. AT: Syco faces BC for the hardcore title, as something something something. I can't think of anything to say. BJ: Neither can I, by God, so let's get the action underway. Or something. AT: I really don't care tonight. BJ: Neither do I. I hope there's some fantastic in-ring action, though. Or not. Bionic Carrot (c) vs. Syco - Hardcore Title Match "This Is Not" hits as Syco comes to the ring to a mild pop. Then "Bad Moon Rising" hits and BC comes out to a massive, ground-splitting pop only ONE TENTH the size of Bob's. BC gets the Trashcan O' Hardcore Goodies out from underneath the ring, tosses it in, and hits Syco RIGHT IN THE EYE! Not being Hodgo, Syco is unable to quickly shrug it off, and is met with a chairshot from BC for a two count just after the ref signals for the bell. BC picks Syco back up and goes for a stalling brainbuster, but Syco reverses into a brainbuster of his own, directly onto the Trashcan O' Hardcore Goodies! Syco gets a two for his troubles, and then begins to take control of the match with a series of stiff right hands followed by a DDT for another two. He gets a Cookie Sheet  O' Death out of the Trashcan O' Hardcore Goodies and hits the Head Check on it for a near three. Syco sets the nearly unconscious BC up on the turnbuckle and then.... AT: NO! BJ: THAT'S NOT.... AT: IT IS! IT'S THE PLASTIC CUP OF DOOM! Syco goes for the Pain Killer on to the PLASTIC CUP OF DOOM, but BC reverses and hits the Nail In The Coffin on the PLASTIC CUP OF DOOM for one...two....KICKOUT! BC is shocked. The ref is shocked. Hell, Syco's shocked. BC picks Syco up to try another Nail In The Coffin, but Syco slides off his back and hits the Jaw Jacker as BC turns around. Syco lays the Trashcan O' Hardcore Goodies on top of BC and hits the Psychotic Splash, but hurts himself in the process. Suddenly, there's some motion in the crowd, and Rob "The Throb" The Throb jumps the railing and slides into the ring! AT: Oh my God! It's RTT! WITH ANOTHER PLASTIC CUP! BJ: I think he was just having a beer in the crowd... RTT hits Syco with the SECOND PLASTIC CUP OF DOOM...to the expected effect of a slight stinging from the punch. Then Syco grabs a chair as BC grabs the Trashcan O' Hardcore Goodies and they WAFFLE RTT completely out of the ring. BC turns around, smiling, into a chair to the gut followed by the Psychotic Edge. Syco gets the pin and the win. Winner and NEW HARDCORE CHAMP - Syco AT: What an upset! What a victory for Syco! What the hell am I talking about! BJ: I don't know, in the grand scheme of things, it doesn't matter. AT: You're right...damn angst. Backstage Blake Wolfson is seen taping his ribs in preparation for his match against MV Pete later in the night. He looks to have a pained look on his face. AT: Well, Blake took a hell of a beating at Big Brawl. Will he be able to hold on to his title? And, really, does his title truly make him happy? BJ: Having held many titles in my life, Brussel Sprout, I can tell you that while it does bring you a temporary feeling of accomplishment, in the end, it leaves you empty and depressed, driven to strong drink. AT: I figured as much. Anyway, X-Factor said he had an important announcement to make. Hopefully, it will be truly important...such as the reason behind human suffering. But, I doubt it. CK (c) vs. Kevil - TV Title Match "Lynch Pin" hits And the fans have fits For the entrance of Kevil The root of all Evil! "Make Me Bad" hits and the crowd explodes in boos for Cereal Killer. The two men square off as the ref calls for the bell. CK quicly overpowers the smaller man and backs him into the corner before hitting repeated knife-edge chops. CK turns and postures, giving Kevil a chance to jump to the top and hit the Green Eggs and Ham, taking CK down. CK looks shocked at the speed of the tiny Evil One. Kevil hits some quick kicks to CK's gut and then a stiff clothesline, taking the big man down. AT: It looks as though CK wasn't thinking about Kevil going into this match. BJ: Probably considering the larger issue of the nature of Evil. AT: Alright...this IS CK we're talking about. BJ: Good point...and Kevil is undefeated, after all. You know, without evil, there can be no good, so it must be good to be evil sometimes... Kevil goes for the Evilsault, but CK catches him in mid-air and carries him to the corner. CK sets Kevil up on the turnbuckle, but gets kicked in the face and turned around. Kevil hits the Welcome to the Darkside for the pin and the title win! Winner and NEW TV Champ - Kevil Bolling and Dev are seen walking down the hall. Dev: Tonight's going to be interesting...I'm teaming with Bonecrusher, and I have no idea what's up with the guy. Bolling: I'm sure something will work out. The pair come upon NFM's dressing room, which is empty, with no sign of NFM. Woof Woof /w The Grand Wizard vs. Admiral John /w Sheri Dedmon "I Hope You Die" hits as Woof and the beturbaned Wizard come to the ring. "Gets Me Through" hits for the entrance of Admiral John and Sheri Dedmon, and the accompanying MASSIVE pop and wolf whistles. The two men square off, cautious of getting too close to the ropes for fear of some trickery by the outside participants. They lock up, and Woof pushes AJ over near the Wiz. AJ quickly breaks the tie-up and gets himself back to the center of the ring. AT: AJ is doubtless looking for revenge after Woof's cowardly attack. BJ: But, really, what good is revenge? What does it accomplish? They tie up again, and AJ pushes Woof over towards Sheri, but - Woof being Woof - he doesn't notice and Sheri holds the back of Woof's trunks as AJ pounds on him. The ref notices Sheri's activity and warns her not to get involved again. AJ hits Woof with a DDT for a one count, and then a short-arm clothesline for a two. AJ goes for a running powerslam, but Woof slides off his back and pushes him face first into the opposite turnbuckle. Woof then tosses AJ to the outside and goes to antagonize Sheri while the Wizard gets some shots in with his wand/scepter thingy. Woof goes to the outside and rolls AJ back in, going for the pin, but AJ gets a foot on the ropes. Woof pulls him up and hits the Woofinator for a two, then the W-Bomb for another two. He goes for the Wag the Dog, but AJ rides over him and hits a powerbomb of his own. AT: What a match! It almost made me forget about the agony of daily life... BJ: Almost...but the pain is far too great... The two men get to their feet at the same time and start exchanging punches, with AJ getting the advantage. AJ takes Woof down with a short-clothesline, and then backs into the ropes, looking for the Tomahawk Missile. The Wizard clocks AJ in the small of the back with his scepter-wand-thingy, and AJ stumbles forward into the Wag the Dog. Woof picks up the pin Winner by Pinfall - Woof Woof The Wizard gets in the ring with a mic, and is just about to speak when Sheri jumps in the ring and chases him to the back. AT: Well, at least we were spared the pain of The Wizard's ceaseless ramblings. BJ: But not those of the X-Factor, sadly, who's about to make his important announcement. "Killing in the name of" comes over the Speakers and the usual Pyro shoots off. Factor clutching his "Certificate of Sanity" walks down the aisle and hands it to the nearest xUw official. Wearing his newly "borrowed" 3 quarter length leather jacket, he enters the ring, with a really unhappy look on his face. X: Now, I know I promised you a huge, HUGE announcement. But as usual in my life, something's gone wrong...bloody pathetic lowlife's letting me down.... The fans start to boo X: What...I'm sorry..what more do you want.. He looks around and see's a sign saying "Who Cares about X Factor?". He tries to laugh it off and starts to talk again X: You know nothing..nothing about me.. Factor looks once again at the sign, laughs it off, then looks away X: Nothing at all... He once again looks at the sign X: Screw it..I don't need this.... With that he drops the mic and leaves the ring, with a solemn look on his face AT: Well, that was interesting. BJ: He didn't get to his major announcement, on account of these dumb fans. AT: Anyway, next up is Sidhe against Numark. And I understand Bob will be watching this in the back. Cut to Bob, watching a monitor in the back. AT: And I was right! Numark vs. Sidhe Nimh /w Kat "Right Here, Right Now" hits as Numark comes out to a Usual Suspects-worthy pop. "Eternal Life" hits as Sidhe and Kat come out to their usual extremely confused, and horribly loud reaction. The two men face off, circling each other carefully. They tie up, and Numark executes a go-behind and tries locking in the waistlock for a German suplex, but Sidhe keeps him from locking his hands and twists his arm into a standing armbar. Nu rolls out of it and hits a jumping DDT for a one count. Nu takes the advantage with some right hands as we see an inset of Bob watching closely. Nu backs Sidhe into a corner and whips him out to the farside. Numark hits a spear for a two count. On the inset, Bob gets a look of "Don't do it" on his face as Nu positions himself for some mounted punches. Suddenly, Sidhe's legs hook up and he applies a body scissors to Nu, pulling him away from the face and removing all leverage. Sidhe pops a few kicks into Numark's kidneys, as Nu tries to figure out how to extricate himself from this position. The crowd boos as the stalemate begins to last for over two minutes with no obvious offense from either party. AT: What the hell? BJ: This is definitely a message to Bob...that's the ground guard perfected by Royce Gracie in MMA competitions. Sidhe's doing a fairly good job of keeping Nu from getting any offense in while he waits for an opening...like that one Nu is creating right now! Bob just shakes his head grimly in the inset as Numark tries backing out of the guard, but Sidhe grabs his arm and locks in a triangle choke as Bob nods, appreciatively. Nu gets to the ropes and stands, holding his neck. Sidhe stands too, grinning, as Kat just sits on the barrier, nodding. Numark goes for another tie up, and Sidhe gets the go-behind into a leg trip, and rides up into a mount, hammering punches into the back of Numark's head. The ref makes Sidhe break the position, and Sidhe waits as Nu gets to his feet. Nu fakes for a tie up, but kicks Sidhe in the stomach and hits a spinning powerbomb for a two. He follows with a Reverse DDT, and then goes up top for The Bombshell, but Sidhe rolls out of the way. AT: Critical error on Nu's part. BJ: Sidhe's good at acting more hurt than he is to fake his opponents into making rookie mistakes. Which works most of the time. AT: But not on someone like Bob? BJ: Probably not. However, like Numark, Bob is from New Jersey, and they are dumb out there. Sidhe gets to his feet and sees the breathless Numark. He moves in as Nu gets to his feet and hits him with a quick punching combination, followed by a vicious kick to the face. Nu goes down and Sidhe goes to work on his knee, hitting a spinning toehold, and then a crucifix leglock. Bob continues to watch in the inset, impressed, as Nu gets to the ropes and then to his feet, favoring his knee. Sidhe grabs the injured leg and starts hitting rolling dragonscrews, getting up to a count of five before pulling Nu in for a snap capture suplex. He goes for the pin, but pulls up Nu at the two count, responding to the referee's warning with "The boy's tapping". Sidhe begins to hit very stiff roundkicks to Nu's injured leg, when Nu catches Sidhe's foot, spins him around, and hits a reverse DDT! AT: Numark's making a comeback! BJ: But you've got to wonder if that leg is going to hold up... Nu gets to his feet slowly, as does a stunned Sidhe. Nu hits a German suplex and goes for the pin, but only gets a two. Nu sucks it up, and goes for the spinning powerbomb again, his knee giving out on him as he turns, but he connects...only to have Sidhe kick out right before the three...and lock Nu back into the guard position, giving himself a chance to catch his breath. Frustrated, Numark tries to stand out of the guard, but opens up the injured leg in the process. Sidhe quickly grabs the bad wheel and takes Nu down as he wrenches the leg into a kneebar. Nu holds on, but Sidhe adjusts for better leverage, and Numark taps as, quietly, Bob applauds the match. Winner by Submission - Sidhe Sidhe and Kat stalk to the back as officials help Numark out of the ring. AT: Well, I have little doubt that Bob got the message. BJ: That was beautiful...that was old-school, Morel Mushroom. AT: And now from the beautifully violent to the just bizarre...how will the former members of F.E.A.R coexist? BJ: They won't. Explicit Violence vs. The Boomer Sooners "Boom" blares over the loudspeakers as the Sooners enter to a pop that makes Bob's look like, well, Bob's on a bad day, in a small gym. All is silent, and then "Build Fear" hits and Dev comes to the ring to massive heat, looking very grumpy. "Here We Go (Let's Rock and Roll)" hits and Bonecrusher comes to the ring to a very subdued pop. Almost immediately, Dev gets in his face, and BoCru doesn't back down an inch to his partner. Chase and Dave look at each other, and then run over and pants Dev to laughter from the crowd and BoCru. Dev angrily pulls up his pants and goes to the apron as Chase and Bocru - both still laughing - square off as the bell rings. The two big men tie up, and BoCru backs Chase into a corner...and breaks clean. They tie up again, and Chase backs BoCru into the corner and reciprocates the clean break. The two men eye each other respectfully, and tie up again. BoCru gets Chase up and hits a vertical suplex drop, and hits a jumping sidekick for a one. BoCru twists Chase's arm and tags in Dev, who gets in the ring, punches Chase once, and then tags BoCru back in. BoCru turns and has words with Dev as Chase gathers himself. Chase looks at Dave and shrugs. Kipfer just shrugs too. AT: All is not well in the F.E.A.R camp! BJ: Well, of course not. Bonecrusher has turned into a loser jackass. Bocru turns into a Chase Wilson clothesline, followed by a side Russian legsweep. Chase gets a two, and then tags in Kipfer, who comes off the turnbuckles with the Viva Las Vegas for another two. Kipfer hits the Eulogy for another two, and then tags Chase back in. BoCru hits Chase with a drop toe hold, but when he reaches for the tag from Dev, Dev doesn't reach back. BoCru stands and gets in Dev's face as Bolling climbs over the barrier. Bolling punches Bocru low as Dev guillotines the big man over the top rope. BoCru stumbles back into a rollup by Chase for the pin. Winners by Pinfall - The Boomer Sooners Post-match, Dev and Bolling beat down Bonecrusher. They whip him off the ropes and go for a double clothesline, but Bocru ducks and hits a double running neckbreaker drop on the return. Bolling gets groggily to his feet and turns to see a very enraged Bocru. AT: Uh oh...looks like F.E.A.R just woke a sleeping giant. Bocru LAUNCHES Bolling out of the ring and then turns into an attempted spear by Dev, who just bounces off the big seven-footer. Bocru grabs Dev, presses him, and tosses him onto Bolling to a HUGE pop. F.E.A.R backs up the ramp as Bonecrusher challenges them to come back. AT: Bonecrusher's getting cheered again! He can hardly believe it! BJ: Ummm...let me just say that any comments I made earlier about Mr. Bonecrusher being, say, a loser jackass are not meant personally. "Material Girl" hits as Naron makes her way to the ring to MASSIVE heat, which she seems completely oblivious to. She steps in the ring. Naron: Why, I'm so heartened to hear such a warm reception, even if it is from peasants like you! MASSSSSSIVE HEEEEAAT! Naron: I just wanted to reassure you all that your King, my darling Henrik, will have no problem taking his rightful title away from that ruffian Bob... MAAASSSSIIIIIVVVE HEEEEAAAATTTT! Naron: And, as a further treat, I challenge that strumpet, Sheri, to a match for the title of Miss XUW...if she's not too busy breaking in a new Horseman. I'm sure she'll be too worn out from her work on the corner to possibly beat me. And with that said, ta-ta! "Material Girl" hits again as Naron somehow manages to remain oblivious to the massive heat. AT: Strong words from Naron. BJ: I was just thinking...with Explicit Violence broken up, and Raven X in the hospital, the Sooners might be the top contenders to the tag titles. And that thought frightened me. AT: As well it should! But, now! The Main Event! MV Pete faces the man who got him eliminated from Stable Wars, Blake Wolfson - for the Intercontinental Title. Blake Wolfson (c) vs. MV Pete - Intercontinental Title Match "Right Here, Right Now" hits as MV Pete comes out to a MASSIVE pop. "I Stand Alone" hits as Blake, taped ribs and all, comes to the ring to blistering heat. The two men square off as the bell rings. MV Pete goes right to the ribs with a body shot, then a suplex, angling Blake as he falls to hit on his side, doing more damage to the ribs. Blake flops on the mat in pain, then gets up to eat a big boot from Numark, who goes for the cover, and gets a two. As he pulls up Blake, Blake rakes his eyes and hits a lowblow. Blake hits a DDT and then applies a rear chinlock, resting. AT: Blake is at far less than one-hundred percent right now. BJ: Which should make this match about even. MV Pete battles to his feet and hits a back bodydrop reversal to the chinlock, doing more damage to Blake's ribs. Pete locks on the Walls of Brooklyn, causing Blake to cry out in pain...but he refuses to tap, slowly making his way to the ropes. Pete hits the Brick Town Bulldog for a near fall, then the Primetime Chokeslam followed by the MV Drop for another near fall. AT: I know I'm normally very hard on Blake, but that he's taking all this abuse and still kicking out is amazing. BJ: Especially since he's, y'know, Australian. Blake rakes Pete's eyes and hits another DDT, then a lowblow. As Pete gets up, Blake hits the Jugular, but only gets a one. Blake hits the Side Russian legsweep, then goes up and hits a top rope legdrop...but it hurts him just as much as it does Pete as the shock rips through his ribs. Pete stands and goes to lift Blake to the top rope for the Devastation, but Blake stops him short with a lowblow and rolls to the outside, crouching near the timekeeper. Pete follows him out and reaches down to pull Blake up, but is distracted briefly as he notices DJ sitting in the front row, allowing Blake to get a hold of the IC Belt and CLOCK Pete right in the noggin. Painfully Blake rolls Pete into the ring, climbs in and hits the Crying Wolf for the pin. Winner and Still IC Champ - Blake Wolfson AT: PETE WAS ROBBED! BJ: What? AT: You saw what happened! BJ: Yeah. Blake hit his finisher and won the match. AT: I hate you.....so much. Anyway, this is the Atomic Tomato saying goodnight. Fade out.
