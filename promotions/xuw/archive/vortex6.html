XUW Card Archive

XUW Vortex #6

31 Jan 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Pyro explodes, lights flash, and the XUW music hits! The fans around X Factor arena go wild, it's Thursday night, it's VORTEX TIME! Atomic Tomato: "Hello there grappling fans, and welcome to the first Vortex of the new era! And what a start to this era it was, with Sidhe Nimh and Diablos26 taking over the ownership, and the leadership, of Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. With Hodgo, Jack The Ripper and the Army of Darkness behind them, what can the rest of XUW do to stop them taking Total Control?" Scott Burton: "Well AT, they could listen to me more, it's clear from where I sit that to stop Total Control they'll have to..." Scott is cut off as "Hurt" by Nine Inch Nails starts to play, and Kat O'Shea makes her way down to the announce table. Kat: "Scott, clear the hell out of here." Scott: "You can't just do that to me, I'm..." Kat: (interrupting) "Orders of Total Control. Now get to the back or do I have to kick your ass first?" Scott Burton makes his way up the ramp, and as he leaves Eminem's "Quitter" hits the speakers, and CK makes his way down to the ring. Grabbing a mic, he gestures for his music to be cut. CK: "Now, recently CK has been on a losing streak here in XUW. CK hasn't been taken seriously by the fans, the other wrestlers and by the management. Being a teen heartthrob isn't an easy business, but now I have my focus back, and I'm challenging Hodgo to come down here and put his title on the line against the hardest working superstar in XUW! All I want is another shot, another chance to prove that CK is NO JOKE!" "Anarchy in the UK" rips through the speakers, and Hodgo comes to the top of the ramp, with Sidhe just behind him. A chorus of disdain rains down upon them. Hodgo clears his throat and goes to speak... Hodgo: "CK, people think you're a joke because you ARE a joke! I mean, look at you! What the hell is a teen heartthrob doing in a wrestling ring? You're the laughing stock of XUW! Why the hell don't you listen to your music and just quit? So, you want a title shot? Let's get it on! Sidhe, you can stay in the back, this should only take a minute!" With that, Sidhe wanders to the backstage area, and Hodgo struts towards the ring, a large smile on his face. Entering the ring, the bell sounds and the match is on. XUW Cruiserweight Title Match Hodgo (c) vs CK Hodgo saunters over to CK and laughs in his face. CK responds by throwing Hodgo to the ropes and catching him with a knee to the gut on the way back. Hodgo goes down in pain, but CK allows him no respite, picking him back up and hitting him with some hard chops to the chest. AT: "Whoooooooooooooooooo!" Kat: "Shut up!" CK keeps up the offense on the stunned Hodgo by hitting a Dragon Screw Leg Whip and then stamping down hard on his chest. Giving Hodgo no time to catch his breath, CK hits the Cherry Popper (swing into twisting neckbreaker) and goes for the lateral press. 1 ... ... 2 ... ... KICKOUT! Hodgo reaches down and just gets a shoulder up. Bewildered by the turn of events, he reaches a vertical base, and turns to see CK grinning at him. Hodgo aims a sluggish kick at CK, but it is easily blocked. CK then grabs Hodgo and brings him up for a suplex, but then turns it into a body slam piledriver through his legs. AT: "The Bam! CK hit The Bam!" Hodgo lies motionless in the ring. CK rolls over and hooks a leg. 1 ... ... 2 ... ... 3!!! WINNER AND NEW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION: CK!!! AT: "Wow, CK was as impressive as hell tonight!" Kat: "Damn Hodgo, he never pays enough attention." AT: "Wait, CK wants the microphone back." CK walks around the ring, microphone in hand, still high on adrenalin from his title victory. He kicks Hodgo out of the ring and starts to speak... CK: "Tonight I proved to you all that I am no joke! Do not take CK lightly! I have been training hard, because CK HATES LOSING! So now, I need to prove to the whole of XUW that I AM the hardest working wrestler in the business. To do this, I am changing this belt from the Cruiserweight Title, to the XUW TV TITLE!" A big crowd pop greets this news, as CK rolls out under the ropes and makes his way to the back. AT: "Can he do this?" Kat: "Yeah, we'll let him." AT: "Man, this is huge news! XUW has a TV title, to be defended at each and every XUW televised event! CK is going to have to live up to his words as the hardest working wrestler in the business if he wants to keep a hold of his gold! I think this sends out a message to everyone else though, CK is not a man to be trifled with lightly!" Kat: "He looked pretty damn good tonight, I'll give him that. Hodgo is no slouch in the ring when he pays attention." AT: " Hey, look, here comes Kata!” Kata Hajime makes his way down the ramp to no music at all. He enters the ring, lays his XUW Submission Title on the mat, and picks up CK’s discarded microphone. Kata: “Against all the odds, and after a vicious attack upon my person, I have reclaimed my Submission Title from The Ripper. It is not he, however, with whom I have an issue. To the members of the so-called Iron Fist, I challenge you. I challenge any one of you to come down here to this ring and take me on. Take me on in a simple submission match. I will even make it a title shot if that’s what you want?” AT: “Kata is calling out The Iron Fist!” Kat: “Who are they anyway? They seemed odd, and yet strangely familiar.” AT: “It doesn’t look like the Fist is listening.” Kata:  “I repeat. Any one member of The Iron Fist can come out here, now, and get a title shot. There’s a belt here waiting for a challenger. Are you man enough to face me in a fair fight? Nothing happens for a few seconds, but then a figure is seen atop the ramp, and the sound of Metallica comes into the arena. AT: “Wait, who’s that? It’s… It’s DDT! Is he in the Iron Fist?” DDT’s music cuts out and he stands there, staring down at Kata in the ring. DDT: “Kata, I don’t know anything about The Iron Fist, but there’s one thing I do know about, and that is pain. I know the feeling of crushing an opponent with my bare hands, of making their whole word be swallowed up in pain, until submission is the only option. If that title shot is still open, you will not find a more worthy contender than I…” Kata nods his head slowly, and motions for DDT to come to the ring. AT: “Wow, these guys are both seasoned veterans, this could be a classic!” Kat: “You’re very excitable aren’t you? Maybe you should try some sedatives, or hanging around with the Hurristoners…” XUW Submission Title Match Kata Hajime (c) vs DDT Both men start by circling the ring. Kata edges forwards and the two men lock up. Kata does a go-behind and tries for a German Suplex, but it is blocked by DDT, and the two men resume their circling. DDT goes on the offensive as the two men meet again, using his weight advantage to force Kata back towards the ropes. They reach the ropes and DDT swings back to the center, whipping Kata to the other side at speed. Kata come back off the ropes and ducks under the clothesline attempt, hitting the other side ropes and coming off with a flying forearm, which takes DDT off his feet. Moving quickly, Kata gets atop DDT and grabs a leg, pulling it backwards into a Half Crab. DDT writhes in pain, but refuses to tap. AT: “Great move from Kata, this one could be over right here!” Kata keeps the pressure applied as DDT slowly edges towards the rope. After what seems like an age, DDT makes it to the ropes, and the ref calls for Kata to break the hold. Getting up, DDT hobbles over to the corner, but as Kata approaches him DDT spins on the ball of his good foot and levels him with a hard clothesline. DDT turns Kata on to his front and tries to apply the Lockdown (crossface). Kata struggles, and makes it to the ropes before DDT can get it fully locked in. Both competitors get back to their feet and lock up once more. DDT whips Kata to the ropes and goes for the Back Body Drop; however, Kata sees the ploy and catches DDT with a rising knee to the face. As DDT goes down, Kata grabs his legs and locks them together into a Sharpshooter. DDT grimaces in pain, but refuses to tap. Slowly but surely, he makes his way to the ropes. Ever closer, ever closer the ropes draw. DDT reaches out, and just as he’s going to grab the bottom rope, Kata drags him back to the center of the ring and wrenches back. DDT tries to stop himself, but eventually has to tap. WINNER AND STILL SUBMISSION CHAMPION: KATA HAJIME AT: “Wow, what a match we had there! Kata is a great fighting champion!” Kat: “He’s going to need to be, who’s that behind him?” AT: “My God, it’s The Commonwealth!” As Kata holds his belt aloft, Raven X, Bailey and Henrik rush the ring. They start to charge Kata, who throws RX into the turnbuckle and lays out Bailey with a clothesline, before Henrik hits a kick to his gut and nails Kata with a piledriver. RX and Bailey get back up and kick Kata while he is down. Henrik grabs a mic and turns to address the crowd. Henrik: “Now, you all listen to me. The Commonwealth has had enough of all you American morons and your idiotic ideas. I, your King, am here to tell you that I have had enough of your ignorance, and that XUW will be my stage to prove the superiority of The Commonwealth over fools and commoners such as this “Car-tar-hay-jee-mee…” Suddenly, the music of the 4 Horsemen hits and the Technicians run down to the ring. AT: “It’s Bionic Carrot and KsC Trunkz!” BC and KsC enter the ring and quickly take it to the members of The Commonwealth, throwing them one by one over the top rope, and then tending to their fallen comrade, Kata. AT: “My God, I don’t believe it! The Four Horsemen are back together! Horsemen for life!” Kat: “There’s only three of them, dumbass.” AT counts on his fingers, but gives up after two… AT: “Who the hell cares, The Horsemen are back! There’s going to be trouble for whoever stands in their way now!” The Horsemen stand reunited in the ring to a big crowd pop. AT: “Well, our next match could be another interesting one, it’s that Ebonics loving scoundrel Riley Esco taking on the veteran Sinner. Sparks are sure to be flying here!” Kat: “Riley be layin’ down da beats on Sinner, knaaaaawamsayin’?” AT: “Erm, quite frankly, no.” Riley Esco vs Sinner Sinner comes down as “Down With The Clown” plays, and he soaks up the crowd’s polite applause. Then Busta Rhymes hits the sound system, and Super Thug Esco comes to the ring to a chorus of boos, and a small “We Want Al” chant. Esco enters the ring, and the match is on. They lock up, and Sinner uses his weight and strength to back Esco up into the corner. Esco goes for the cheap shot as they break, but Sinner was waiting for it, blocks the fist and responds with some hard chops to the chest. Sinner then gets up on the turnbuckle and starts to pound away on Esco. AT: “1 … 2 … 3 … 4 … 5 …” Kat: “Hey, you’re not meant to cheer along with the crowd!” Back in the ring, Sinner whips Esco to the far ropes and goes for a clothesline, but is met by a hard diving shoulder tackle from Esco, and both men go down. The ref starts a ten count, but both men struggle to their feet at six. Esco is the first to go back on the offense, grabbing Sinner and hitting a DDT. Esco goes to cover. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Kat: “Supa Thug’s be flamin’ all over dat sucka!” AT: “Errrrr…” Esco pulls Sinner up and looks for the Chokeout, but Sinner kicks him in the shins until he lets go. AT: “Sinner’s unorthodox, but it seems effective!” Sinner whips Esco into the ropes, and hits the Sinner’s Sole (Big Boot) as he rebounds. Esco hits the floor, and Sinner follows, looking for the three count. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Sinner slams the mat with one hand, and waits for Esco to stand up so he can hit the Sin-Sation. Esco spots Sinner’s stalking though, and stays down for a moment longer, before exploding into a tackle that sends both men into the turnbuckle. Sinner stumbles from the corner as Esco sneaks behind him, grabs the pumphandle and hits the Drive-By (Rikishi Driver). Down he goes, hooking the leg, and… 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! WINNER BY PINFALL: SUPA THUG ESCO! AT: “I don’t like that guy, but I’ll give him his due, he can be one mean mutha!” Kat: “That’s the spirit!” AT: “Now, our next match will see the Hurristoners take on a team new to XUW competition – La Familia!” Mexican style music plays and out come La Familia, Shorty and Coco Hernandez. Kat: “We just signed these guys from a Mexican fed, and we think they’re gonna make a big splash here in XUW!” AT: “Well, they have their work cut out for them because they’re facing one of the best teams we’ve seen in our time here, The Hurristoners!” Fatbadger and Chase Wilson come out to a big pop, as Blink 182’s “Stay Together For The Kids” reverberates around the arena. They trot down to the ring, pausing only to throw a few glow sticks out to the crowd. Tag Team Match: La Familia vs Hurristoners Referee: Brad Bice The match starts with Chase Wilson facing up to the taller of the Hernandez brothers. AT: “Is that Short or Coco?” Kat: “It’s Shorty. Shorty is tall, Shorty’s not short, Coco’s the shorty. Get that?” AT: “Errrrrrr, anyway, back to the action…” Chase is getting the upper hand in the ring, and hits a Side Russian Leg Sweep. Pulling Shorty up, he sends him to the ropes and connects with the Short Term Memory Loss (jumping superkick). Chase goes for the pin, and Brad is right there. 1 … … 2 … KICKOUT! AT: “Ummmm, the tall one is showing some resilience here.” Chase holds Shorty, and brings him over to the corner where he tags in Fatbadger. Together the Hurristoners send Shorty to the ropes and go for a double dropkick, but Shorty hangs on and avoids it. As the Stoners get to their feet Shorty runs and executes a perfect double clothesline, and all three men are down. Chase rolls to the outside as the other two men get groggily to their feet. On the apron, Coco Hernandez is jumping up and down, desperate for the tag. Shorty staggers over and slaps hands with Coco, just as Fatbadger arrives at their corner. Coco goes to work with a series of right hands, and then runs off the ropes and hits a bulldog. Fatty stays down as Coco waves at his brother, who is recovering in the corner. Coco lays a couple of boots into the midsection of Fatbadger, and then brings him up, only to scoop slam him back down to the mat. Coco smiles, and goes to the top rope, looking to hit the 450 Aye Mios Dio on Fatty. Coco goes airborne and gets incredible height, which is a shame for him as Fatbadger rolls out of the way and Coco eats mat! The crowd pops and chants “Fatty, Fatty!” Fatbadger gets to his feet and grabs Coco from behind, suplexing him upwards and then dropping him into the stunner that is known as the Fat Burner! Coco is down on the floor, seemingly out cold. Fatbadger climbs over, hooks the leg, and Brad Bice is there for the… 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Shorty Hernandez enters the ring and stamps on Fatbadger, breaking the pin. As Fatbadger gets up to complain to the ref, Shorty catches him by the throat and delivers a big chokeslam, leaving Fatbadger and Coco side by side on the floor. Brad forces Shorty out of the ring and then starts the ten count. On five, Fatbadger starts to get to his feet, shortly followed by Coco. Fatty whips Coco to the corner of the Stoners and tags in Chase. Chase executes the Dying Daze (Fisherman’s DDT) on Coco, and then tags Fatbadger back in. Fatbadger goes flying over the top rope and connects with the Fatty-Boom-Batty (Over the top rope legdrop). Fatty hooks the leg as Chase runs over to stop Shorty interfering. Brad Bice is right there. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!! WINNERS BY PINFALL: THE HURRISTONERS! The music of the Hurristoners plays, and Fatbadger headbangs in the ring, while Chase looks for munchies in his pockets. AT: “Well, they’re certainly ‘out there’, but you’ve got to love those Hurristoners!” Kat: “No I haven’t.” AT: “Well, we’re getting word from the back that the Flashtastics want a word with your bosses!” Kat: “Hey, they’re your bosses too, don’t forget that!” Cut to… Backstage, where The Flashtastics are being interviewed by Scotty Spalzutof. Scotty: “So, boys, I hear that you have some harsh words for ‘Total Control’?” Flash: “So, those fools in charge of XUW think they can do what they want? They think they can just boss people around as they see fit? They think they can just dominate us all?” Flex: “Mmmm, dominate.” Flash: “Stop it! We need to have more focus, we need to get ready, we need to kick these guys!” Scotty: “You wish! Total Control is gonna kick your asses!” Flex and Flash turn as one, and Scotty runs off as fast as he can Flash: “It’s time. Time to give Total Control a Flashtastic beating, for what they did to Bob on Mayhem” Flex: “I love it when you’re all manly!” Cut back to the main arena, where the lights start to dim, and red laser beams sweep the audience. A calm, controlled voice comes over the speakers. “There is no need for alarm. We are in TOTAL CONTROL. Do not adjust your set, this IS normal service. Welcome, to TOTAL CONTROL!” Music hits, and Sidhe and D26 appear at the top of the ramp, to a chorus of abuse. They make their way to the ring, and then The Flashtastics ‘come out’ to a big pop. They jog down to the ring, slapping hands as they go, and the match is now on. Tag Team Match: Total Control (Diablos26 and Sidhe Nimh) vs The Flashtastics Referee: Brad Bice Sidhe and Flash start the match. Sidhe starts off the some right hands, and then grabs Flash, hitting a T-Bone Suplex. Flash gets up and Sidhe hits a Snap Suplex. Flash arches his back in pain, and Sidhe mugs to the crowd. Going over the Flash, who is getting to his knees, Sidhe grabs him for a German Suplex, but Flash spins round out of it and hits Sidhe with a series of forearms to the face. Sidhe is staggered, and Flash manages to make it to the corner for a tag. Flex hops through the ropes and grabs Sidhe, setting him up for a Gut Wrench Suplex. He connects, but then gets distracted staring at Sidhe’s ass as he lies on the floor. D26 creeps up behind Flex and locks him with a Full Nelson, before slamming him down face first to the mat with a Leg Sweep. Brad Bice ushers D out of the ring, and the men inside both struggle to their feet. Kat: “Get up Sidhe!” Sidhe is first to his feet, and leans over the ropes to smile at Kat. Kat waves back at him, but by then Flex has got up and grabbed Sidhe’s legs. Sidhe holds on the ropes as his legs are dragged away from him. Flex flashes a big cheesy grin to the crowd, and then kicks a field goal into Sidhe’s midsection. Sidhe goes down, and Flex tags Flash back in. AT: “Ooooh, that’s gotta really hurt!” Kat: “Shut up!” Flash walks over to Sidhe and whips him to the ropes. D26 gets the blind tag and vaults the top rope. Flash levels Sidhe with a clothesline, but then sees Flex dramatically pointing behind him and turns into a big superkick. D goes for the first pin of the contest. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! D gets up and pulls Flash into an Implant DDT. Signaling for the Devil Drop, D goes to the top rope and taunts the crowd. However, just as he takes off, Flex darts through the ropes like lightning and pulls Flash out to safety. D26 hits the mat with a tremendous ‘Smack!’ Seeing the opportunity, Flex grabs D26 off the ground and gets him in a big Bear Hug. Flash spies the set-up and runs off the ropes to deliver a hard clothesline to D. AT: “The Blinding Flash! That’s great tag team continuity for you! Hey, where are you going?” Brad Bice goes down to count it, as Flash pins D26. 1 … … 2 … … NO! As Brad moves his hand down for the third count, Kat kicks Flash hard in the head. Flex turns to see this happening. Flex: “Nooooo! My Flash!” Running over to Kat, Flex backs her into a corner, before Sidhe Nimh grabs him from behind and hits a big Release German Suplex. Meanwhile D has gotten up and hits Flash with another superkick. Kat pulls some brass knuckles from her coat and hands them to Sidhe. Total Control starts to beat down The Flashtastics badly. “F*CK THAT!” Bob’s music hits, and the arena almost explodes with an enormous crowd pop! AT: “Aarrgghh, my freaking ears!” Bob runs down to the ring with a chair and lays Sidhe out cold with a vicious blow. D26 turns round from beating on Flash to walk straight into a hard blow to the gut from the solid steel chair, and then gets the chair bent out of shape across the back of his head. AT: “Wow, looks like Bob’s Totally out of Control! Geddit?” Bob tends to his fallen allies, as the music of The Flashtastics plays. WINNERS BY DQ: THE FLASHTASTICS!!! AT: “Wow, that’s gotta be seen as an upset by my reckoning! Score one for the Flashtastics!” Bob leads The Flashtastics to the back to a huge pop, after which D helps Sidhe up and they leave to many “You suck” chants. Kat checks that Sidhe is okay, and then returns to the commentary position. Kat: “Damn that Bob, but he’ll be getting his soon enough. You don’t cross Total Control and get away with it…” AT: “Well, he seemed to be doing pretty well. Anyways, it’s time for the Main Event! HBK takes on Lopen! There’s a lot of history here, these former friends going at it. I sure hope we get to see a fair fight!” Kat: “I wouldn’t put it past that sneaky HBK to cheat like he always does!” Lopen’s music hits, and Lopen makes his way down to the ring to a cavalcade of boos and jeers. He spins around in the ring, and asks the crowd Lopen: “Who betta than Lopen?” Crowd: “Everybody!” “No Quarter” hits, and HBK poses at the top of the ramp, to a good pop. He is accompanied to the ring by his valet, Sunny. AT: “HBK seems to have changed his ways of late. He’s working hard, and playing fair!” Kat: “It’ll never last. Lopen will beat some sense into him.” Lopen vs HBK Referee: The Hungry Mexican The two competitors start off by walking towards each other until their eyes are mere inches apart. Lopen seems to be talking some kind of trash to HBK, but is only getting a wry smile in return. Lopen seems infuriated by this and slaps HBK hard across the face. HBK retaliates with some stiff right hands, and the match is really on now. HBK follows up this offence with a hard kick to the calf, and then hooks Lopen into a butterfly suplex and executes it perfectly. Lopen is quick to get back up, and ducks under an attempted kick from HBK, grabbing his head on the way past and executing a great neckbreaker. Lopen springs back to his feet and leans over the ropes, taunting the crowd. HBK manages to get up and pulls Lopen off the ropes before hitting a Falling Side Suplex. AT: “Damn, do you heels NEVER learn about that?” Kat: “It’s a tradition, or in the Constitution, or something…” HBK drags Lopen a little nearer the turnbuckle and goes upstairs for The Balance Point (Moonsault). HBK flies like one born to it, and lands squarely across Lopen’s chest. Mexican goes down to count the 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! HBK pulls up Lopen, but gets hit with a low blow while the referee is conveniently looking the other way. Lopen takes advantage and hits the Backlash (Edgo-o-matic) for the 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Lopen goes over to the ropes again and starts taunting a bunch of Young Republicans. AT: “Hey, maybe he’s not that bad after all!” HBK once more creeps up on Lopen, but he’s waiting for it this time and hits HBK with a Flatliner. Lopen now circles the grounded HBK, taunting him, and waiting for an opportunity to hit the Lopen Cutter. HBK finally rises, and stumbles towards his waiting adversary. Lopen grabs HBK by the neck and goes for the Lopen Cutter, but HBK pushes Lopen off into the ropes and hits a swinging neckbreaker, leaving both men down. Lopen is first to rise, but instead of capitalizing, he leans over the ropes and taunts Atomic Tomato! AT: “Seriously, will this guy EVER learn?” Kat: “It’s probably contractual…” HBK sneaks up once more on Lopen, smacks him round the head and goes for the quick Schoolboy Roll-up. Mexican is right there. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! WINNER BY PINFALL: HEARTBREAK KID!!! AT: “Wow, that’s an unexpected ending! Lopen looks furious! Wait, it’s Sue Wolfe!” Kat: “Can you end any sentence without an exclamation point?” AT: “No!” In the ring Sue Wolfe of Exodus stands up to HBK and offers her hand in friendship. HBK smiles, not noticing the immense bulk of Severson clambering over the ropes behind him. As he reaches out to shake with Sue, Big Sev hits him around the back of the head with one enormous fist. HBK goes down quicker than a student on a first date. Sev raises Lopen’s hand in the air in celebration as HBK lies in a heap at their feet. “F*CK THAT!” Bob’s music hits for the second time that night, and this time the walls start to shake from the crowds’ enormous pop, dust tumbling down from the ceiling. Bob, accompanied by the Hurristoners, rush the ring and take it to Lopen, Sev and Sue. Suddenly the lights go out, and the calm voice of earlier comes back on the speakers. “Do not panic. We still have TOTAL CONTROL” The lights come back up to find Jack The Ripper, Diablos26, Sidhe Nimh, Hodgo, Rob The Throb and Raj Patel surrounding the ring. As one they dive in and an almighty fight ensues. Sev and Lopen beat down HBK, Sue kicks Sakie in the nuts, and RTT and Raj throw him over the top and follow him out. Jack and Fatbadger lock up, and still wrestling together they fall through the ropes towards the announce table. D26 wrestles Bob to the ground, and they too roll out of the ring. Hodgo and Sidhe keep the beatdown going on Chase Wilson, with the last Hurristoner down in the corner, having seven shades of … hell stomped out of him. Hodgo poses for the crowd on the turnbuckle, but gets pelted with stuff from the crowd, and falls off to the outside. Sidhe and Chase are the last men in the ring, Chase receiving one hell of a beating, when… … ACID 8000 hits the speakers, and Mikey appears at the top of the ramp to a monster pop! AT: “My God! He’s back! Mikey’s not Lost In Cleveland any more!” Kat: “Oh hell, I hate him almost as much as Bob.” Mikey runs down to the ring, Wal-Mart cola in one hand, a packet of Marlboro in the other. Throwing his accessories to the floor, he enters the ring and confronts Sidhe. Sidhe smiles and rushes Mikey. Mikey smiles right back at Sidhe as he grabs the smaller man and hits a powerslam. Mikey motions for Sidhe to get up, and then grabs him, setting him up for the McCorpin Bomb. Mikey is about to nail Sidhe, when a mischievous grin crosses his face and he walks over to the ropes. Mikey summons up the rage and frustration he felt at being Lost in Cleveland, the hurt he suffered in the asylum and his general hate for corrupt powers, and McCorpin Bombs Sidhe Nimh over the top rope and into the crowd, where the Young Republicans are sat and The Hungry Mexican is cowering. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!!!!!!!!!!!! As Sidhe lands there is an enormous explosion, and three rows of seats are sent flying into the air. Kat: “NOOOOOOOOOO!!! Sidhe! Sidhe! NOOOOOOO!!!!” AT: “Oh God! Somebody stop the damn match. Oh, wait, there’s no match. Somebody call the damn paramedics!” Kat rushes to the explosion site, but there is no sign of Sidhe other than a big crater, and the dismembered limbs of the Young Republicans. Kat goes to her knees and cries, Kat: “Why God why?” Mikey picks up his Wal-Mart cola again, and stands in the ring, happy to have rid the XUW of one half of the corrupt and evil owners. Acid 8000 plays once more, and everyone else just lies there in shock, as small fires burn themselves out over the arena. AT: “Well, that was one hell of a bang to end this weeks edition of XUW Vortex! Be sure to tune in again for Monday Mayhem, where we’ll see what the hell Total Control can do about this! This is The Atomic Tomato, signing off…” Fade to black
