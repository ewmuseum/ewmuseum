XUW Vortex #18

25 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Pyro flashes, the foam machine is spitting out what amounts to soft cannonballs at the audience as someone's finally gotten it to work properly, the laser show is going off flawlessly, everything seems to be going flawlessly...when the Hungry Mexican falls off the rigging into the foam machine, causing bloody, slightly meaty foam cannonballs to be launched at the audience. People are screaming, there's general chaos, and we cut to the announce position, where the Atomic Tomato is in shock at what's just happened to the Mexican, and "The Majestic" Barry Johnston is taking it all in stride, as he saw this happen at a show in Japan back in his wrestling days. AT: Did you...what.... BJ: WELCOME TO VORTEX! AT: THE HUNGRY MEXICAN JUST WAS SKINNED AND PROCESSED BY THAT DAMN FOAM MACHINE! BJ: I know, wasn't it cool? Anyway, we have quite the show tonight...Sheri faces Lazarus for the TV Title, Explicit Violence faces ... AT: What? BJ: ... AT: I'm doing the rest of the card, dammit. Kore faces Michael Bolling, the Phoenix faces Hodgo, Al Crud and random fellow face the Deadly Combination and...what the hell? "Gets Me Through" begins and Admiral John walks out to a massive crowd pop. His normal jovial expression is replaced by one of uncharacteristic seriousness as he climbs in the ring. He mounts a turnbuckle and poses briefly to a sea of strobing flashbulbs. He catches a mic that is tossed to him and climbs back down. He makes a chopping gesture and his music cuts out suddenly. AJ: As everyone knows, Sudden Impact is coming up... May 5, as a matter of fact. The crowd cheers briefly at the mention of XUW's next PPV AJ: And in keeping with the great PPV's throughout my time as Commissioner, Sudden Impact will showcase the BEST talent in XUW. It is my pleasure to announce two matches. We have three top contenders for Sidhe's Intercontinental Title. HBK, Bob and DDT. Over the past weeks these men have battled each other trying to prove who is the better man. Well, they're ALL worthy of challenging Sidhe for his title. So, I'm putting all four of them in the ring at the same time. At Sudden Impact, it'll be HBK vs. Bob vs. Sidhe vs. DDT in a Fatal Four Way for the Intercontinental Title. The crowd cheers hard as Admiral John pauses... once the cheers die down he continues... AJ: I've also decided that X-Factor needs to be dealt with. He comes in here after a self-imposed vacation, like that other chump Lance, and disrespects the XUW. Not only does he run his mouth about his glory in some dead federation but he's even taking watches off one of our cameramen recently. Well, he needs to be taught a little respect for the XUW and I've decided that Kata, a Horseman, a XUW vet and a man who's proud of it, will be the one to do it. The crowd again pops huge, but Admiral John holds up his hand to quiet them down. AJ: I've also been doing some thinking. When I took over as XUW Commish, it was and has been the best thing that has ever happened to me and to this fed. Never in my wildest dreams did I think that XUW would take off like this. When it looked like XUW might be over I worked my ass off to not only keep it afloat but make it better and I did. I created tournaments to give all XUW wrestlers a shot at glory. The tournaments culminated at the first PPV under my reign - Reborn, the largest XUW PPV in fed history. New champions were crowned. Sakie emerged as XUW World champ and still defends it and XUW with honor. New faces and talent have grown to join XUW's already great veterans in the history books.  I have seen my beloved fed grow in numbers and excel in quality has I held the office of Commissioner...BUT.. We've had our share of troubles, as well. He pauses for a moment AJ: While XUW on a whole has flourished, within its ranks some things were quite wrong  -. The Army of Darkness, F.E.A.R and other such punks are running roughshod with no sense of respect for me or XUW;  the Commonwealth and their "king" rambling on about a "conspiracy" , questioning MY honor and integrity,..and worst of all, in my eyes - a once great stable, the one group all others aspired to be -my "family", the Horsemen -  have been in turmoil...... Now, I have to admit, some of these problems are my fault. I thought my years as a wrestler prepared me to handle the business side of this "sport". I tried to run XUW as a business, but I've realized that my business isn't behind the desk. It's here, in the ring. This is where I can best handle these problems. With this in mind, I've decided to step down and resign as XUW Commissioner. The crowd boos fiercely at this announcement. Admiral John tries in vain to quiet the crowd. After several minutes, he is able to succeed. AJ: I've taken steps, though, to insure that XUW doesn't suffer because of my decision. And to explain how I've done this, I'd like to introduce Mr. Ronald Webster. The XUW theme plays and a sharply dressed man holding a folder makes his way down the ramp. The crowd gives a collective "Huh?" as he steps in the ring. He nods to Admiral John, they shake hands and AJ hands him the mic. Ronald Webster: " Good evening ladies and gentlemen, My name is Ronald Webster and represent the board of directors of XUW, Inc. Before I continue, please join me in giving this man, Admiral John, a well deserved round of applause for his work and commitment as XUW Commissioner" RW  claps as the crowd cheers and begins a "AJ" chant. Admiral John, is obviously taken aback by this as he waves to the crowd in acknowledgement. After a minute or two the crowd dies down. RW: " Allow me to explain who XUW, Inc is and what has transpired between us and AJ over the past the few weeks. Following the unfortunate downfall of the last regime in the XUW head offices. Admiral John stepped forward to take over as Commissioner to run the federation. At that time, XUW the company was also in financial straits due to the mismanagement of its officers. Seeing this, AJ sought out partners to aid him in the financial business-side of the federation. This group, who I represent, acted as silent partners. While AJ ran XUW  day-to-day operations from his office , the board of directors ran XUW, Inc and all its financial dealings. This system worked well with both sides separate but equal in their success and control. Recently, though, the events in the ring have crossed over to affect those outside of it. This is not to lay blame on Admiral John. He did his best and handled the office well. Understand folks, while he was a fine Commissioner , in his heart AJ is a wrestler whose heart for competition never died. Though he strove to stay impartial and not be affected by his fellow wrestlers, who he was now in "control" of, his desire to return to the ring and deal with the problems first hand was too great.  Admiral John understood that he could not properly run the fed as Commissioner AND be a wrestler in the ring. This is when he came to us and resigned as Commissioner" The crowd grumbles slightly. RW: The board of XUW, Inc. while surprised, understood why he did this and accepted. Immediately, AJ and the board discussed his replacement. A number of names surfaced, all fine XUW wrestlers in their own rights. Finally, though, it was decided that though these men understood "wrestling" , XUW is first and foremost a business and needed to be run as such.. Therefore, the board and AJ choose to not replace him as Commissioner, but that XUW, Inc. would take the reigns." The crowd boos slightly. AJ motions to them to quiet down and they do. RW: Please, folks. Let me elaborate before you judge. For you, the fans, XUW will remain the same. You may see slight differences but no change in the quality and performance you come to expect from XUW. We, the board, will run XUW the same way it has run in the past..with one main exception - we will be doing so with complete impartial less visible manner. For the XUW wrestlers, though, things will change. From this day forward there will be no "Commissioner", per se.  No office for wrestlers to burst into thinking the can demand or threaten to get their way. No one man in power trying to use the federation as his own little chess game. Just wrestlers doing what they do best. Shows and PPV's will be created the same way BUT the board and its recently hired experts will handle XUW from afar  - faceless and nameless figures doing their best for XUW. On occasion I, as XUW Inc's spokesperson, may address certain problems that arise, but for the most part we shall remain out of the spotlight. We feel that this is best way to handle the company's in-ring product and it's financial responsibilities as well." AJ (borrowing the mic):" Trust me folks. This solution will work. I know that, Mr. Webster and the board will do all they can to keep XUW at the forefront of the wrestling world as you, the BEST damn  fans in the business , deserve " The crowd cheer at the cheap pop attempt. AJ smiles and motions to the crowd to applaud Ronald Webster, to which the crowd gives minor props. RW: "Thank you , AJ, and thank you (motions to the fans). Now, as the first order of business done by XUW, Inc. (opens his folder) We have some matches for Sudden Impact. First, when AJ resigned he requested he be able to get back to doing what he does best...wrestle. Well, as of this moment Admiral John is full reinstated as a wrestler (Crowd cheers) He also had one more request that we feel he deserved based on his excellent work as Commissioner - Admiral John will take on Henrik the King in non-title match at Sudden Impact!!" The crowd explodes at the announcement. "AJ" chants begin. As the volume dies down RW speaks RW: Also, as I mentioned XUW is a business and we will run it as such. A business needs to have all it's top people doing their part to make it work. In XUW this means our various Champions we need to act like such, in and out of the ring. Recently, our Hardcore Champion, Bionic Carrot, has publicly stated he will not wrestle until he sees fit. Well this will not do. If XUW is keep it standards high our titles are the keys to this. So, Bionic Carrot WILL wrestle and it will be at Sudden Impact He will defend his belt in a rematch against NFM of F.E.A.R." The crowd cheers. Signs for the Horsemen and people flashing the Horsemen hand symbol pop up in the crowd. RW: And while discussing belts, numerous wrestlers have vied for the XUW TV Championship belt. They hope to join it's past champions in a tradition of hardwork and supreme talent. Two such wrestlers will get their chance in a 3-way dance for the TV Title at Sudden Impact. Crowd again cheers. RW closes his folder. RW: "Again, thank you, Admiral John, for your work and thank you, the fans for you support of this new and exciting era in XUW. As AJ stated we at XUW, Inc promise to give you the best product we can because you deserve it. Good Night." RW and AJ both acknowledged the crowd who cheers and both men escort each other out of the ring and up the ramp. AT: Wow! The Admiral has stepped down as Commissioner! BJ: About time. He's been running this fed into the ground. AT: Have you been paying attention? BJ: No. Let's get things going with a TV Title Match! Sheri Dedmon /w KsC Trunkz vs. Lazarus - TV Title "S*** List" hits as Sheri and Trunkz come out, hand in hand, to a gigantic pop. The camera cuts to the back as Lazarus prepares to leave the Commonwealth locker room. Laz: So, anyone want to wish me luck? Bailey: Shh....the NHL Tournament is on. Laz: Anyone? Henrik: Don't disturb your King...I have to consider the ramifications of that dratted Admiral's announcement. How does this fit in the conspiracy... Hodgo: Good luck...but I've got to get ready for HBK. He shouldn't be a challenge, but I want to completely dominate. Laz: Ok. Well, I'm going to go beat up a woman now. I'll be back in a few. RX: Mmmmm....Sheri.... "Sinner" hits and Lazarus comes to the ring to massive heat. He looks at the comparitively diminutive Sheri and starts laughing...until she kicks him in the face, and whips him off the ropes. He knocks her down with a shoulderblock. Laz goes off the ropes again, but KsC trips him up. Laz gets to his feet and turns to leave the ring and attack KsC, but Sheri rolls him up for a surprise two count. As they get to their feet, Sheri hits an arm drag and locks in an arm bar. Lazarus easily fights to his feet, but Sheri goes behind him and, somehow, gets him over in the Fun House german suplex /w bridge. Laz kicks out after two and rolls out of the ring, frustrated. AT: Sheri's being fairly dominant in this match. BJ: Lazarus went into this taking her too lightly. She's a capable wrestler...what am I saying? I'm acting like a face, Plutonium Persimmon. Help me. Laz rushes in and goes straight for Sheri, who starts running from him. They begin just running around the ring. Sheri slides to the outside, followed by Lazarus. Sheri gets back in the ring, and the ref starts to reprimand her. While the ref is distracted, Trunkz levels Lazarus with a chairshot and rolls him into the ring. Sheri goes for the cover as RX comes down from the back. KsC has surreptitiously slid the chair into the ring as the ref questions him as to what happened. There being no ref to make the count, Sheri breaks the pin and goes for the chair. When she turns, Laz grabs it and takes it away from her and goes to hit her. Raven, seeing Sheri being threatened, lariats Lazarus from behind. Sheri rolls Laz up in a small package just as the ref turns around, and he counts the three. Winner and NEW TV Champion: Sheri Dedmon Sheri and Trunkz celebrate in the ring as Raven X hightails it to the back before Lazarus figures out what happened. AT: Did you see that? Raven just cost his Commonwealth teammate the match and the TV Title! BJ: Well, Raven needs to stop obsessing over Sheri and get his mind on business. AT: Agreed, but Lazarus is a beast for daring to even think of hitting a woman with a chair. BJ: Hey, we've all done it...err...NEXT MATCH! Kidd Kore vs. Michael Bolling "Superstar" hits and Kidd Kore comes out to a mild pop. "Date With Destiny" hits and Michael Bolling comes out to mild heat. The two men square off and then lock up. Kidd Kore takes the early advantage with a spinning neckbreaker. He goes for the Obscure Reference inverted DDT, but Bolling blocks. Kidd Kore goes off the ropes, but is caught with a samoan drop by Bolling. Bolling backs up and hits a superkick and then goes for the Destiny Slam, but Kore reverses into a back body drop. Kore kicks him in the gut and goes for the Snake Eyes, but Bolling reverses into a 110th street slam. Bolling goes up top and hits the Destiny Splash for the three. Winner by Pinfall - Michael Bolling AT: Impressive display by Bolling as he picks up the win! BJ: The kid's got potential. Dev /w Bonecrusher vs. Severson /w DJ "Build Fear" hits as Dev comes out with BoCru to massive heat. Then....nothing, save the massive footfalls of Severson and DJ as they come to the ring, and the huge pop that accompanies them. Severson and Dev square off. Dev goes for a spinning heel kick, but Severson catches him and tosses him into the corner, following it up with a series of vicious right hands, not that any right hand from Sev isn't vicious. Sev pulls Dev out of the corner, and then goes for a power bomb, but Dev goes over the top and hits severson with a low blow. He then begins to pepper the big man with kicks, finally bringing him down to one knee. Dev goes for a clothesline, but gets caught in a bearhug from Severson. Dev rakes the eyes, and then hits Sev with a DDT. AT: Dev's managing to hold his own in this match. BJ: Of course he is...technical skill always proves superior to brute force. Dev tries to apply the Lockdown to Sev, but can't get his legs around Severson. Sev hits a snapmare, followed by a low dropkick, which is a freaky sight to see. Sev goes off the ropes, and gets tripped up by BoCru. DJ rushes around and hits BoCru with the Flatline lariat. Sev turns and prepares to launch his massive body over the top rope onto Sev, but then sees that DJ is in the way, and so returns his attention to Dev, who hits Sev with a big martial arts kick. Dev goes for the cover, but DJ pulls him out of the ring. BoCru takes DJ down as Dev gets on the ring apron. Severson goes to suplex Dev back into the ring, but is tripped by BoCru, causing Dev to land on top of him for the three. Winner by Pinfall - Dev The four men begin to brawl, with Explicit Violence seeming to get the advantage, until Severson lays out BoCru with a big boot and DJ nearly chokeslams Dev through the mat. The FEAR members roll to the outside and grab a mic. Dev: You guys want a fight? Meet us at a match at Sudden Impact. Sev and DJ nod their agreement. AT: Wow...I wonder if we'll be able to get that signed. BJ: Hopefully. I'd love to see Severson get manhandled like that again. AT: What? Nevermind...I know you watch your own version of all the matches... BJ: Are you kidding? Dev just press slammed Severson! AT: You're a very strange man. BJ: Thank you. Backstage, Al Crud is looking for a tag partner for his upcoming match. He finds Woof Woof. Crud: *farts* Hey, you wouldn't happen to wanna be my tag partner tonight or nothin? Woof: Sorry, I've got...ummm...other things to do. I've got a dog collar match in ten days...I need to find a comfortable collar. Crud: Oh... Crud wanders on and happens upon KsC and Sheri. Crud: Hey, Trunkz, would you mind... KsC: Sorry man, but that f***er Lazarus f***ing hurt Sheri out there, so I've got to f***ing take care of her. Crud: Oh... Crud, distraught, wanders into the coffee lounge and finds CK. Crud: Hey! You! CK: NO! Crud grabs CK and drags him, unwillingly, to the curtain. "One Bourbon, One Scotch, and One Beer" hits as Crud and a protesting CK come through the curtain. Deadly Combination (c) vs. Al Crud and CK - Tag Title Match CK seems a bit upset as he and Crud enter the ring. "Deadly Combination" hits as Numark and MV Pete come out to a huge pop. They enter the ring as Crud steps to the apron, leaving a very confused CK in the ring. Numark and CK tie up, and CK takes the advantage with a European uppercut. He follows up with a belly-to-belly suplex, and then tags in Crud. CK holds Numark for Crud to take a free shot at him, but Crud just burps. CK gets out of the ring as Crud looks confusedly at him, trying to figure out why he was holding Numark. Nu rolls Crud up for a two count. Crud's still looking rather confused by CK's tactics as Numark tags in Pete, who quickly hits Crud with a suplex, followed by the Brick Town Bulldog (TKO Diamond Cutter). Pete tags in Numark and sets Crud up in the corner. Nu goes up top as Pete hits the Devastation top rope powerbomb, and the crowd chants "T-D" as Nu hits the Bombshell frog splash to complete Total Devastation. It's enough for the pin, especially as CK doesn't seem too concerned with breaking up the count. Winners by Pinfall - The Deadly Combination AT: Poor CK.. BJ: He didn't even want to be out here. But he passed up a shot at the tag titles. The fool. AT: I just got word from the back that ... against Explicit Violence has been signed for Sudden Impact. Hodgo vs. HB "The Phoenix" K "Anarchy in the UK" hits as Hodgo comes out to massive jeers. As he gets in the ring, a drink cup hits him RIGHT IN THE EYE, but he shrugs it off. "Six Feet Under" hits and HBK comes to the ring to a pop that splits the heavens and at least two pairs of pants in the audience. The two men face off, and then Hodgo takes the immediate advantage with the Anarkicks. Hodgo hits a DDT and then goes for the Ankle Lock, but HBK gets to the ropes. HBK gets to his feet, and Hodgo levels him with a Karate kick to the face for a two count. Hodgo follows up by applying the La-Choke-Em. HBK seems to be fading, but manages to get to the ropes. As he gets to his feet, he manages to catch Hodgo with a fist to the gut and a falling side suplex. Both men are down as the ref makes a count. AT: This is a much more competitive match than I was expecting. BJ: Well, you can never underestimate Hodgo. He is God's Gift to XUW, after all. Both men get to their feet, and Hodgo goes for a kick, but HBK catches it and spins him around and hits a calf kick. He follows it up with a Butterfly suplex, followed by the Balance Point, and starts signalling for the Sweet Chin Music, but DDT runs out and distracts him. Sidhe jumps the railing and slides into the ring and rushes HBK, but HBK turns just in time and backdrops him over the top rope. Sidhe catches the top rope as HBK hits the Sweet Chin Music on an attacking Hodgo. HBK gets the pin as Sidhe flips himself back into the ring. Winner by Pinfall - HBK HBK turns into a capture suplex from Sidhe. DDT slides into the ring and the two AoD members start stomping on HBK. DDT hits the Creeping Death on HBK, followed by China White from Sidhe, as "F*** That" hits and Bob comes running down from the back with a chair. The AoD clears out quickly as Bob stands over his fallen comrade. AT: Wow...that four way at Sudden Impact is looking to be an incredible match. BJ: Yeah, but can Bob and HBK stand together against the AoD? For that matter, will DDT not be gunning for Sidhe? And can anyone possibly beat Sidhe one on one? He's been a machine for the past few months. AT: We'll have to see. But now...the main event...a submission... BJ: These three men are going to go at it until one of them just can't go anymore! AT: That...was wrong. Henrik (c) vs. Lance Storm vs. SupaThug Esco - Submission Title Match "Doggz" hits as Esco comes to the ring to massive heat. "Canon" hits and Lance Storm comes out to more heat. Then "Rule Brittania" hits and Henrik comes out to the most massive heat of all. The three men cautiously look at each other, and then Lance and Esco both lay into Henrik. They manage to maintain a fair amount of teamwork with double clotheslines and even a double suplex. Then Esco grabs Lance and throws him out of the ring. Henrik goes for a clothelins, but Esco ducks and hits the Finisher. Esco goes for the Thug Passion, but Lance breaks it up. Esco and Lance begin to brawl as Henrik rolls to the outside. Henrik makes his way over to the time keeper. AT: What's Henrik doing? BJ: What any sensible man would do. Running. Henrik is heard muttering about AJ's "conspiracy" as he grabs his belt and starts running for the back. Esco and Lance continue to brawl in the ring. Lance manages to apply the Half Crab, but Esco gets to the ropes. Esco limps to his feet and ducks an attempted Lance superkick, hitting the Platinum Front, followed by the Ice Grill (Steiner Recliner). Lance battles out of it and applies a sleeper, but Esco powers him up and throws Lance out of the ring. He follows Lance out and the two men battle to the back. Decision - No contest. AT: What a night it's been in the XUW! AJ steps down, we have a few great matches set up for the PPV, and Barry said more horrible things than I can remember him ever saying before! That's Vortex for the night, and this is the... BJ: Uranium Rutabaga! And I'm calling Lance against Esco for #1 contendership to the Submission title at Sudden Impact! AT: ...ATOMIC TOMATO! Ahem, for "The Majestic" Barry Johnston, saying goodnight! The scene fades on Lance and Esco battling at the top of the ramp. Fade out.
