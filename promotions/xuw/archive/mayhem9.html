XUW Mayhem Welcome to MAYHEM! We are 24 hours removed from XUW Double Trouble where we crowned a new WORLD CHAMPION and the old champion retired. D26 is on his way to the ring Mic in hand, limping from last night’s grueling match with Exedous D26: Last night, I proved who the superior faction is in the XUW! FOUR HORSEMEN! Now, we have a few surprises up our sleeves tonight, so first things fir… ***k That By Kid Rock hits as Samuari Bob and Naron walk to the ring, IC title in hand. Bob: Last night I beat woof woof for this title. Now, there is a little known clause in the contract of the IC title holder that states that the person who holds that title automatically gets a world title shot! Naron: Yea, so hand over Lance for our title shot now! D26: Just a minute, guys. There is one small problem, it also states that you must give up the title for you to get that title match! Naron and Bob look through he contract, and Bob looks kind of upset, but drops the belt. Bob: Then Give me Lance! D: All in good time, my friend, all in good time. Later tonight, in the main event, LanceStormDBC v. Samuari bob for the WORLD TITLE! AT: Welcome to Mayhem. We are 24 hours removed from Double Trouble where one man retired and another became the new world champion! MM: That’s right, last night was quite a night, and tonight will be just as big. Wait, what’s this? D26 is shown getting backstage with the IC title and he walks into Blitz’s office. D: Blitz, we have a problem, no IC champion anymore. Blitz: Okay, well, what do you wanna do? You are the commish, you make the matches. D: Well, I was thinking we could have a… Blitz: If you say tourney so help me I will slap you in the head…that angle is a little played out! D: NO NO NO. A battle royal, 15 men. Blitz: Tonight? D: Oh hell yea! AT: Wow, a 15 man battle royal tonight! It is gonna be quite a slobberknocker! MM: Gee, thanks JR! Can I have my tomato report now too! AT: Cute…well, on to our first match, it is Hodgo v. Bailey… Hodgo walks to the ring… with a big f’n pain of glass… Bailey follows him, no glass though. AT: What is that? MM: It looks like a big f’n pain of glass… Bailey starts off by attacking hodgo fast and furious. He begins to maul Hodgo and beat him mercilessly. Hodgo escapes and gets out to regroup. He slides into the ring and he does a quick drop kick. He does it again, and again. Bailey is reeling when Hodgo hits a suplex. He whips Bailey into the ropes and he hits a back body drop. Hodgo hits Bailey’s head on the corner, but Bailey doesn’t feel it. Bailey goes nuts and begins to slam his head on the mat. Suddenly Hodgo slides out of the ring and grabs the pain of glass. Sidhe Nimh runs to ringside with a big f’n pain of glass and they dive in the ring. They kick Bailey in the stumach and they smash the glass together around Bailey, who is laid out. Out of nowhere, Raven X dives into the ring to save Bailey. Winner: Bailey via DQ Hodgo grabs a mic on the way back…: There you see it folks! The conBigF’NpainOfGlassTo! AT: Wow, I think bailey needs some medical attention! MM: I’d say so…well, while this mess is cleaned up, here comes Cereal Killer on the ramp… CK walks onto the ramp, mic in hand. CK: Last night we proved once again that the Army of Darkness is the best tag team in XUW history! There is no team that has ever defeated us, and none ever will. Last night we showed 3XL the… The technicians jump CK on the ramp and throw him off. Suddenly out of nowhere Jack runs out and nails BC with a chair, as he is also flung from the ramp. 3XL attacks Jack and KsC and they are both thrown from the stage, and 3XL stands on the ramp and poses victoriously. AT: Wow, that was nuts! Can we get them some medical attention too? MM: Is everyone gonna be taken out in an ambulance? AT: Well, there is gonna be no one for the Battle Royal. MM: Our next match is about to get underway. It will be Lurchador v. DDT for the Hardcore title ONLY! Lurchador walks to the ring with both of his belts still in tact. DDT walks to the ring to a mild crowd reaction. Lurchador starts off by hitting DDT with a trashcan lid. He pummels DDT with the lid until he falls. Lurch lifts the ring apron to pull out a lot of toys. DDT regains himself and runs at Lurch, but Lurch sprays him with a fire extinguisher. Lurch hits DDT in the head with the extinguisher and takes a minute to pose for the crowd. DDT gets up and he grabs a stop sign and nails Lurch in the head with it when Lurch turns around. DDT picks up a garbage can and throws it and Lurch into the ring. DDT whips Lurch into the ropes and does a drop toe hold onto the garbage can. Lurch is laid out on the garbage can when DDT grabs a chair. He climbs to the top rope and he does a sit down leg drop, but he puts the chair under himself as he is dropping, so he hits Lurch in the face with the chair. Lurch is busted wide open as DDT goes for the count…1…2…NO! Lurch somehow kicks out. DDT gets up and grabs the chair again. He throws it at Lurch, but Lurch catches it and throws it back and hits a van-daminator and DDT falls out of the ring. Lurch climbs to the top rope and he does a corkscrew ploncha off the top rope and hits DDT. Lurch throws DDT onto the Spanish announce table and climbs up himself. He sets up DDT for a piledriver, but DDT back body drops Lurch to the floor. DDT Picks up a chair and hits…SPANISH JOBBER??!! DDT Picks up Spanish jobber and holds him like a baseball bat, beating Lurch with him. He throws Lurch into the ring and hits the shock treetment on Lurch for the 1…2…3! Winner and NEW hardcore champion: DDT AT: Wow, what a match! MM: I have never seen anyone use Spanish Jobber as a weapon! AT: There is a first for everything! We go to backstage where HBK is talking to X about last night. HBK: Last night, we had a small setback. That damn do-gooder, Sakie, interfered in our plans. Well, no matter. Sev…I want you to take care of Sakie in that battle royal, understand? Sev: …. HBK: Exactly! Don’t worry, I have a special ace in the hole for good ‘ol Sakie…superhero my ass! Now, other than that, I want you all to remember, this battle royal is EVERY MAN FOR HIMSELF! This means we are against each other, so no hard feelings if we end up against each other, got it? Good, now, let’s go take them out! We go back to the ring where the flashtastics are getting ready for their match, when “It’s raining men” hits on the speaker and Blake Wolfson and Jobber step onto the ramp. Flex: Wow, I love this song! Flash: Are we facing them? Flex: I think so…hey…the one guy has some really short shorts! Flash: I think that is Blake! Flex: What is with the blow up doll, is that legal? Shooter: I’ll allow it! Jobber and Blake reach the ring (finally) and the match begins. Flex starts off by hitting Blake with a lariat. Blake bounces right back up and gets another, and another. Flex tags in flash. Flash runs after Blake and they get into…a slap fight?! AT: What the hell is this? MM: I don’t know…where is RTC when you NEED them! Blake gets the better of the slap fight and he tags in Jobber. Jobber gets in and he goes for a pump handle slam on Flash. For some reason, jobber stalls before the move, but hits it with some authority. Jobber begins to pose for Blake, when Flash gets up and sets Jobber up for a powerbomb. He picks Jobber up and holds him there…and for some reason Jobber has a rather large smile on his face…Until he goes down. Suddenly out of no where, Blake nails Flash with a bottle of vasoline and it gets all over the ring. They begin to slip and fight, until some officials break it up. AT: Well, that was…erm…different. MM: I’d say different is definitely the word for that. AT: Well, the next match is out 15 man battle royal. The 15 men are: HBK Severson Woof Woof Admiral John Loose Cannon Raj Patel Takerrocks Sakie KoRe Kata JPBlood D26 DDT Big Daddy Rob The Throb The match starts off with everyone in the ring brawling. You notice that Severson, HBK, and Loose cannon have all teamed up on Sakie and are beating him down. On the other side, KoRe and Takerrocks eliminate each other when KoRe lariats TR over the top and goes with him. Meanwhile, Sakie gets knocked out by HBK, Sev, and loose cannon. Suddenly Sev and HBK nail Loose cannon and they throw him out too. Big Daddy then makes his presence felt by picking up DDT and throwing him at Admiral John which knocks DDT and AJ out. RTT is fighting with Woof when D26 steps in and he stops RTT from knocking out his former tag parter. Raj runs at Big Daddy, and Raj, Kata, RTT, Woof, and D26 all team up and they knock BD out of the ring. Raj turns and he nails Kata and they being to fight. RTT and Woof woof begin to fight again. D turns around and h is leveled by HBK and severson. Out of nowhere Woof woof and RTT knock out Severson. It is down to woof, RTT, D26, HBK, Kata, and Raj, JPBlood. RTT turns and knocks JP out, then RTT is thrown out by woof woof. Kata and Raj patel battle in the corner, until Raj Back body drops Kata out. Raj stands up only to get leveled by a super kick from HBK, knocking him out of the ring. Woof Woof, D26 and HBK stare each other down. D and HBK nod, and they both begin to beat on Woof. They pick Woof up and throw him out. HBK and D stare at each other, then they begin to fight. D hits a DDT on HBK. D goes to pick HBK up, but HBK hits D in the stomach and he whips D into the ropes, which are pulled down by Sue Wolfe, so D goes over the top! Winner and NEW IC champion: HBK AT: Wow, what mayhem! MM: What a match! A new champion! AT: What a cheap ass way to win! MM: I don’t think that D will take that sitting down. Anyway, on to our final match of the night. The world title is on the line between Samuari Bob and LanceStormDBC. D26 gets back in the ring, mic in hand. D: HBK, don’t think I won’t get my hands on you! Your time is coming! Right now, though, I will introduce the newest member of the Horsemen….and oh yea, tonight he is gonna be the special guest referee in this title match! Our new manager: MIKEY! MASSIVE CROWD POP! Mikey walks to the ring, smiling and happy. Lance comes in to a chorus of Boos. Bob comes out to the second biggest pop of the night (only to Mikey. Bob and Lance lock up and there is a quick arm toss from bob to Lance, and another, and another. They are feeling each other out. Lance finally gets the upper hand by kicking Bob in the stomach. He tries to power bomb bob, but he counters with a back body drop. Lance then put bob in camel clutch. Bob refused to tap and finally made it to the ropes. Lance won’t break, so Mikey kicks lance in the head, making him break the hold. Lance gets up and he slides out of the ring and grabs the belt. He dives back in and goes to level Bob with it, but mikey stops him. Lance turns and nails mikey with the belt, busting him open. Out of nowhere, Lopen runs to the ring, and he DDTs bob on the title belt, then he DDT’s Lance on the belt. He drapes Lance over bob as mikey has blood in his eyes and can’t see who he is counting for…1…2…3! Winner and still XUW champion: Lance StormDBC AT: What a show! That was unbelieveable! MM: Well folks, that’s all, we’ll see you next week on Mayhem. Spanish Jobber: Aidios! AT gets up and spears Spanish Jobber cause he is tired of any Jobber in general. MM: GORE, GORE, GORE! Cut to the back, where HBK sits laughing and talking to someone HBK: Did you see that? That is what I want from you. I want you to do that to Sakie! Mystery man: You got the money…I got the job done. HBK (handing an envelope to the man): Excellent, thank you, Riley… Riley, the Ultimo Supervillian: No problem, I always wanted to kick that do-gooder’s ass. Now is my chance! Bob walks back into the dressing room.  Naron is in the bathroom throwing up.  Bob rushes in Bob:  Baby, are you all right? Naron:  Please get me a wet washcloth.  Bob wets a washcloth and puts in on her forehead.  Naron finally stands up but her knees are weak.  Bob picks her up and puts her on the couch. Bob:  Do you want me to call a doctor? Naron:  No, Bob, I know what is wrong. Bob:  Tell me. Naron:  (bursting into tears)  Bob, I'm pregnant.  And I don't know if it's yours or not. Bob:  What the f*#k! Naron: I don’t know what to say…I’m so sorry honey… Bob: … Bob puts his arm around Naron as we fade to black
