XUW Vortex #19

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex The lights in the arena hit and the lasers nearly behead a few people in the front row.  A lady has just given birth to a baby boy in the second tier and the usual indecipherable xUw music plays signaling one thing………xUw Vortex is on the air. Atomic Tomato and “Majestic” Barry Johnson make their way down to ringside and take their usual positions at the announce table. Atomic Tomato:  Welcome once again xUw fans to Vortex.  We are only three days away from Sudden Impact and tonight’s card is huge.  We have Sheri defending her TV Title against Hodgo of the Commonwealth, a main event with the unlikely duo of Dave Kipfer and Supa Thug taking on Lance and Blake Savage. Also tonight Raven X looks to take revenge for the loss of Bailey on Monday. “Majestic” Barry Johnson:  You talk way too much.  Anyway, the revenge you speak of was part of the conspiracy that is holding the Commonwealth down. Bailey wasn’t beaten…he was robbed.  Tonight RX can try and take it out on MV Pete. AT:  Well, we will see about that.  Let’s get to the action. BJ:  You still didn’t introduce me.  Man you suck. MV Pete vs. Raven X “Liberate” by Slipknot hits as Raven X comes out to some crowd heat. AT: He has a mic in his hand.  I think he has something to say. BJ: What, you figure that out all by yourself? RX climbs into the ring and stands in the middle with a strange smile on his face. RX: MV Pete…guess what…my training was successful.  Do you know why?  Do you know what I found?  The conspiracy that beat Bailey?  No?  The plot…the plot to ruin…the plot to destroy?  No?  The wenches with the paddles?  No?  The inner voice…the one that told me to beat you?  You have no idea do you?… Just then “Step Up” by Linkin Park hits as MV Pete makes his way to the ring.  The bell sounds as he slides into an attack by RX.  He is flung to the ropes as RX delivers a power slam and begins to smile at the crowd.  MVP dodges an attempt at a falling elbow then starts to lay punches to RX and backs him into the corner.  Super-plex delivered by MVP and the cover for 2. AT: RX could be in trouble this time.  He’s already been weakened by an onslaught from MV Pete. BJ: Yeah, that was a conspirator’s super-plex.  Everyone is against this guy. MV Pete throws RX into the ropes and delivers a Prime Time Chokeslam and goes for the cover.  Only a two as RX kicks out and rolls out of the ring.  MVP follows but is met with a fierce clothesline and RX grabs him and throws him into the guard railing.  MVP is rolled in the ring as RX climbs the turnbuckle and hits a big elbow.  Two count as MVP is rolled over into the Twists of Love. MVP gets a foot on the ropes and the ref breaks the hold.  RX throws him into the corner and delivers a few boots to the midsection and sets him up for the Morning Glory.  MVP fights it and pushes RX back.  He climbs to the middle turnbuckle and jumps for an axe handle smash. BJ: “The Man” is holding down RX. AT: The conspiracy isn’t real. BJ: Whatever…the members of the Commonwealth told me you would say something like that.  You are probably in on it too. MVP goes for a standing suplex but is countered with a boot to the midsection by RX.  He delivers a double under hook power bomb and goes for the cover.  The ref begins to count as RX puts his feet on the ropes for more leverage. 1…2…3! Winner by pinfall: Raven X AT: The ref had to be blind not to see his feet on the ropes. BJ: He didn’t have his feet on the ropes.  You just want to help keep him down. He won fair and square. AT:  That’s not the way I saw it. As RX makes his way backstage he is grinning at the upset MV Pete who is arguing with the ref in the ring. AT: Well the conspiracy theory will be put to the test at Sudden Impact. Whether he is mad over Sheri or not, RX has to face the music Sunday. BJ: Speaking of music… ”This is Not” by Static X hits as Syco makes his way to ringside to a mild face pop.  “One of a Kind” by Breaking Point blares through the system as Destroyer comes down to the ring and waits for a second before entering the ring to the awaiting Syco.  The bell rings as the match is underway. Al Crud vs. Syco vs. Destroyer The two men exchange a few blows until Syco gains the upper hand and sends Des to the floor.  They continue battling on the floor as “One Bourbon, One Scotch, One Beer” hits and Al makes his way to the ring.  He immediately joins in the battle and lays in a few punches to Des before throwing Syco into the steel steps. AT: This match is just a preview of what we are going to see at Xposure.  All three of these men want to be the next TV champ, but there are 7 other guys waiting to be crowned the next champ. BJ: Don’t forget there is also one lady in that group also. AT: True, Kat is no slouch in the ring and she hopes to be the next woman in line to hold the title. Des climbs back into the ring and executes a suicide dive to both men fighting on the outside.  All three men are down and start to recover slowly. Al and Des cooperate and throw Syco back into the ring before climbing in themselves.  Des climbs the ropes for an attempt at the 450 Splash but Al catches him and throws him to the mat with a press slam.  Syco gets to his feet as Al throws him to the ropes for a Crudderline.  He plays to the crowd before going for the cover, which is broken up by Des.  Al delivers a swift DDT to Des and looks to be winding down.  As he turns around Syco from out of nowhere hits all with the Jaw Jaker.  Des stumbles to his feet and goes to clothesline Syco but is reversed into the Paralyzer.  Des taps out just as Al tries to come from across the ring. Winner by submission: Syco AT: Now that was a great display of showmanship.  Any one of these guys could have taken it at anytime, but Syco came out the winner in the end. BJ: I have to agree with you…the battle royal will be great on Sunday and the TV Champ should be watching just to see what he or she is up against. AT: Speaking of the TV Champ…she has a match up next.  Wait who is that coming down to the ring. Rage and Kaos make their way to the ring area and all three guys start pummeling Al and Des.  They hold Des and signal for Syco to go for the Psychotic Splash but just then Azrael comes to the ring area and pushes Syco from the top rope.  He climbs in as Bolling and Rujasu come down to the ring area.  All eight men battle it out exchanging blows for blows. AT: This is the battle royal a little early. BJ: I guess these guys couldn’t wait until Sunday. RTT and Bluto make their way to the ring and join in on the melee.  They begin to clear house until Kat comes from the crowd and clocks RTT with the kendo stick and runs back out the ring.  Bluto and Al are the only ones left in the ring and they both turn to face each other in a heated stare down.  Al signals for his cooler, grabs two cans and gives one to Bluto.  Al cracks open his can and raises it in toast to the crowd.  Bluto looks puzzled and eventually cracks open his beer and they both toast. AT: One thing is for sure, now they are buddies, but come Sunday it’s every man for himself. BJ: Or even every woman for herself in that matter. Sheri w/KsC vs. Hodgo *TV Title match* “Anarchy in the UK” rocks the speakers as Hodgo comes to the ring to massive crowd heat.  He is talking to the ref when the bell rings and “Four Horsemen” comes through the speakers for the arrival of the TV Champ Sheri and KsC.  She climbs in the ring and plays to the crowd before putting down her title but is turned around by Hodgo.  Hodgo gets in her face and starts to berate her and Sheri delivers a few punches to Hodgo, which catch him off guard. He goes to hit her back, but she ducks and pushes him towards the ropes.  KsC grabs his leg making him turn around and Sheri goes for a schoolboy for only two. AT: Now that would have been a major upset if she would have pulled that off. BJ: Why is it an upset…that is Sheri of the Four Horsemen.  She is one lady I wouldn’t mess with.  In the ring of course. Hodgo gets up with anger in his eyes as he goes to grab Sheri.  She rolls out of the ring and runs around to the waiting KsC.  He nails Hodgo with a clothesline and throws him into the steps.  Sheri climbs back into the ring as KsC pushes Hodgo back into the ring.  She delivers the Scorpion splash and covers for only two.  She goes to apply the House of Pain but is pushed off and sent to the corner.  Just then Lazarus runs down the ramp with chair in hand and smacks KsC in the back of the head. AT: What is he doing here?  He has no business down at ringside. BJ: Yeah right, and KsC is supposed to be helping. Laz picks up KsC and throws him into the railing.  He follows with a running knee to the midsection and lays in more kicks.  Security makes their way to ringside to break up the fight.  Sheri is on the ropes looking at the fight, but doesn’t realize that Hodgo is just waiting on her.  Laz is finally sent to the back and the refs are checking on KsC’s condition.  Sheri turns around and is caught with the Riot Breaker and a cover. 1…2…3!! Winner and new xUw TV Champ: Hodgo AT: That was just wrong. Lazarus had no right to be at the ringside. BJ: Like I said before, neither did KsC.  You see my point? AT: KsC has a match up next…do you think he will be in any shape to take on the Submission Champ Henrik? BJ: Well, if he wants to win he has to suck it up and go for it. AT: We’ll see in just a moment. Cut to the Commonwealth locker room where Henrik is warming up for his match and talking to Naron with footage from the last match playing in the background. Henrik: Well it looks to me that poor KsC ran into a little trouble.  Still even in his weakened state I will not give the unknown conspirators a chance to take this title away from me unjustly.  I don’t have to, nor will I, put this title on the line tonight.  It will not have it taken by a plot to bring the Commonwealth down.  To bring, me, Henrik the King down. RX and Bailey enter the room and bow to Henrik. Bailey: Did you see, did you see?!  Hodgo is bringing more gold to us.  The conspiracy of Sheri holding the belt has been beaten. Henrik: Yes, I saw the footage.  That doesn’t mean the conspiracy is over. It just means they have someone else to go after besides me.  We will show them though come Sunday.  You and Raven X will win the titles and there will be only two more goals to achieve. Henrik looks over at RX who is strangely blank in expression. Henrik: Raven what the devil is your problem?  You should be a bit happy by the win of your fellow comrade. RX: Sheri… Henrik: Are you still pining over that Horsemen wench.  Snap out of it and join the rest of your team.  Your desire for her should not be as great compared to the loyalty you have for your king.  Do you understand? RX: I…I…think so. (thinking to himself)How could I let this happen to my love?  Sheri you will be mine.  Hodgo shouldn’t have… Henrik: You think so?  You better get it straight.  You know so.  This team is one power combined under the leadership of your king and queen.  Make sure you understand that.  Focus on winning the titles and not some pasty wench. Understand?  Naron my queen, let us go to fight the demons now. Henrik and Naron leave the room as RX and Bailey stand back watching.  RX still has a confused look on his face as the camera cuts back to the announce position. AT: It looks as if RX has some trouble deciding where his loyalties lay Barry.  He may be a weak link at the tag match this Sunday. BJ: He is far from a weak link.  RX is as tough as they come.  You will see, he will prove that to everybody. ”Rule Britannia” blares through the system as Henrik makes his way slowly to the ring with Naron by his side.  The heat is high yet Henrik waves to the fans as if they adore him.  They stand in the ring and Henrik grabs a microphone. Henrik: Due to uprising conspiracies, your king will not be putting his title on the line.  Yet he is gracious enough to give KsC Trunkz a chance to prove himself.  I apologize to the king’s fans but rest assured you will all be rewarded with a great display of the king’s wrestling abilities.  Thank you. Henrik puts down the microphone to massive crowd heat and continues to wave until…”Umali’s Bar” hits the speakers and a battered KsC makes his way to the ring to a large crowd pop.  He rolls into the ring and the bell rings for the match to begin. Henrik w/Naron vs. KsC Trunkz [non-title] Henrik begins the offense with a few blows to the temple and dropkick.  He takes his time over KsC and locks him into an armbar.  Henrik lets go of the hold and throws KsC into the corner.  KsC tries to fight him off with a kick to the midsection but Henrik is barely phased by the weak shot delivered and gives him a monkey flip from the corner.  KsC gathers enough to reverse a whip to the ropes and catches Henrik with a Samoan drop. AT: KsC looks to be in bad shape.  He’s going to have to dig deep to come up with a victory here.  Wait…is that someone coming to the ring area? BJ: Yes, it looks to be AJ.  Those Horsemen can never have a fair fight. AJ comes down to the ring just as Naron is about to slap the dazed KsC and catches her arm.  He turns her around and raises his fist as the crowd goes wild.  Henrik is back up and sees AJ and starts to yell at him making AJ pause and argue back.  Henrik is leaning on the ropes as KsC gets to his feet and rolls up Henrik in the schoolboy. 1…2…3!! Winner by pinfall: KsC BJ: Look at that, another conspiracy.  Didn’t the ref care anything about the blatant attack by AJ to a defenseless woman? AT: Naron is anything but defenseless. BJ: I see all of you conspiracy guys stick together.  Henrik was robbed of his rightful victory.  He could have won that match one on one if AJ hadn’t interfered. AT: You may be right, but don’t forget one of his members had a hand in KsC’s condition. BJ: KsC was in top condition what are you talking about.  He looked better than ever. AT: Whatever you say.  Next up we have a triple tag match with BC and “…” teaming up against three members of F.E.A.R., NFM and Explicit Violence. Bionic Carrot and "..." [DJ and Sev] vs. NFM and Explicit Violence [Dev and Bonecrusher] “Rammstein-Du Hast(NFM Mix)” hits the speakers as the three members of F.E.A.R. walk to the ring.  They stand in the ring in wait as “Bad Moon Rising” comes on and BC makes his way down the ramp.  He stops halfway to wait for his partners to come out.  “How You Remind Me” blares through the sound system as DJ and Sev come out to join BC.  They all approach the ring together and a brawl ensues as they enter the ring.  Bc and NFM battle it out as the two tag teams take the fight outside the ring. AT: This isn’t gonna be pretty at all.  BC is hardcore and so is NFM. BJ: Yeah this will be a little test for his title.  NFM is hungry and wants that belt back. BC delivers a scoop slam to NFM and drops down for a knee to the chest.  NFM is thrown into the opposition’s corner and BC tags in DJ.  DJ comes in with a few shots to NFM before sending him to the ropes.  NFM ducks a clothesline and hits a reverse neckbreaker.  He drags DJ to his corner and tags in Bonecrusher who drops a knee into DJ’s leg.  Bo Cru lifts up DJ and tags in Dev.  They immediately go for the Combined Violence and Dev goes for the pin.  Only a two as BC makes the save.  DJ is dazed at this point as is thrown to the ropes, but he stops and delivers a desperation ddt.  DJ lunges for his corner and tags in Sev.  The crowd goes wild as Sev delivers a clothesline to Dev, then an interfering Bo Cru, and gives NFM a shot off the apron.  Dev sneaks up behind Sev and gives him a belly to back suplex. AT: This match is really fast paced and both teams are tagging in and out with great continuity. BJ: Yeah, and they are tagging well. AT: That’s what I s…..never mind. Sev is barely phased and gets back to one knee as Dev tags in NFM.  NFM comes in and delivers a dropkick to the face of Sev but it doesn’t knock him completely down.  NFM backs back once again but is caught with the Black Eyes.  Sev picks up NFM for a gorilla press slam and tags in BC.  BC comes in and hits two straight clotheslines on NFM followed by an elbow drop. He goes for the cover but only gets two.  Whip to the ropes into a Spine Buster by BC.  BC picks up NFM and sends him into the neutral corner.  He gives him a few kicks and then goes to tag in Bo Cru.  Bo Cru puts BC on the top rope and looks for a super fallway slam but BC jumps off of his shoulders and executes a power bomb. AT: BC just won’t give up. He is on fire tonight. BJ: Yeah, he should be fired from the cheating Horsemen. AT: I said on fire, not be fired. Wait… I just received a message that Lazarus has been suspended. BJ: Are you sure?  I mean, he is one of the Commonwealth’s most valuable members.  That must be a mistake. AT: We will have more details on this news after the match. BC makes it to his corner and tags in DJ.  DJ runs over to Bo Cru on the ropes but is thrown over the top rope.  NFM and Dev take the opportunity to lay in a few punches to DJ and throw him into the announce table.  They throw him back into the ring and Bo Cru tags in NFM.  NFM picks up DJ and gives him the final destination.  He goes for the cover but only gets a two due to both BC and Sev.  Dev and Bo Cru climb into the ring and a brawl ensues.  Sev throws Bo Cru outside of the ring and follows after him.  Those two brawl outside of the ring. NFM picks up DJ and gives him the Lightning Strike at the same time as BC giving Dev the Hangman’s Neckbreaker.  NFM goes for the pin and Dev grabs BC’s foot to stop him from breaking it up. 1…2…3!! Winners by pinfall: NFM and Explicit Violence AT: NFM and BC at Sudden Impact is gonna take down the house.  Both of these men are impressive, but only one can wear the belt. BJ: Well, they are both truly hardcore, but I don’t think the belt would fit both of them at the same time. AT: Funny.  Like I said folks we will have an update later tonight on the state of Lazarus in the xUw.  For now let’s go to our next match with the unlikely team of Esco and DK. BJ: Esco still ma dawg, knaamsayin. AT: Actually I have no clue half the time, but that’s beside the point. Mr. Webster comes down to the ring area with mic in hand. He stands in the middle of the ring and tries to quiet the crowd. Webster: It has come to the board’s attention that Lazarus can’t seem to stay out of other’s concerns.  So effective immediately…he is hereby suspended from the xUw. The crowd erupts at the announcement as the camera goes to the Commonwealth locker room to show Lazarus Laz: All of these conspiracies.  I knew it had to go higher than AJ.  This isn’t right! Henrik: Calm yourself, this will be rectified in due time.  Trust your king. The camera cuts back to Mr. Webster in the ring. Webster: In addition to that announcement I have another.  A TV Title match will be on the card for Sunday.  Hodgo will defend the belt against none other than KsC Trunkz. The crowd erupts again as Mr. Webster leaves the ring AT: What an announcement.  Sudden Impact is shaping up to be one of xUw’s biggest events. BJ: All of these conspiracies. ”Hey Man, Nice Shot” hits the speakers as Blake Savage makes his way to the ring followed by Lance.  They stand in the ring and wait until, “One of a Kind” hits the speakers and Dave Kipfer comes out on the ramp along with Supa Thug Esco.  The bell rings as the four men designate their starters. Dave Kipfer and SupaThug Esco vs. Blake Savage and Lance Dave starts the match against Lance. Lance gets in the first offense with a whip to the ropes and a snap suplex.  He follows with a quick elbow and a cover. Only a two as DK kicks out.  Lance throws DK into the corner and goes to deliver the ten punch, but DK counters and hits a running sit down power bomb. He goes for the cover but only gets two.  He picks up Lance and throws him to the ropes for a vertical suplex but Lance reverses and hits a release German suplex.  Blake is tagged in and runs straight for DK who ducks and flips him out to the floor.  Lance charges DK but his momentum is used to throw him over the ropes also.  DK backs up to the ropes and hits a suicide plancha to the floor. AT: Dave is not holding back at all.  He better be careful cause he does have that injury that he sustained on Monday. BJ: Yeah the Ripsaw is nothing to take lightly. DK makes it to his feet and rolls Blake back into the ring.  He whips him to the ropes and hits a spinning side slam, then goes for the cover.  Only a two from the Lance breakup.  Dave tags in Esco who throws Blake into his corner.  He charges towards the corner but stops and points at Lance.  He signals for him to come into the ring but Lance looks around for a second before tagging Blake. Lance and Esco circle each other for a few seconds then the lock up.  Lance backs Esco into the ropes and whips him to the opposite ropes.  He drops down for Esco to jump over him and connects with a spinning neck breaker. AT: Well Barry, looks like your boy is down.  He may be in trouble if Lance snaps on the Straight Shot. BJ: Yeah, if you say so dawg.  I give ma boy props.  He gots dis, knaamean. Lance goes for the cover but only gets two.  He tags in Blake who goes for the clothesline but is reversed into the Finisher.  Esco gets to his feet and knocks Lance off the apron before tagging in DK.  DK hits a springboard hurricanrana and signals for the finish.  Lance comes into the ring to hit DK from behind.  Esco rushes Lance and ducks the superkick.  He gives Lance a belly to belly as DK catches Blake for the Bootie Call. Pin attempt. 1…2…3!! Winners by pinfall: Dave Kipfer and Supa Thug Esco AT: Now that was a wild match.  Neither team was really a team, nor did they act like it.  Sudden Impact should be good. BJ: It better be, I have a family to feed. AT: You live by yourself. BJ: Oh yeah, that’s right.  Never mind. Blake gains his composure and rolls into the ring with a steel chair. Dave doesn’t see him coming and turns around into a massive chair shot.  He begins beating on DK and working especially on his injured neck.  He picks him up and delivers a brain buster to further injure DK.  He picks him up for the Blake-sational Neckbreaker and goes to do it again until Chase Wilson comes down to the ring area.  He runs Blake off who is smiling at the damage done.  Chase and DK stand in the middle of the ring with the crowd going crazy. AT: Retribution will be dealt at Sudden Impact and Blake better watch out. BJ: Where did Chase come from? AT: He’s been here the whole time I think.  He saved DK and now Blake may need saving at Sudden Impact.  DK looks to be mad. BJ: Well I would be too if I had a sore neck. AT: That’s all for tonight but tune in Sunday for Sudden Impact.  It’s going to be huge.  Contact your local cable or satellite provider for details.  Goodnight from Atomic Tomato. BJ: and Barry Johnson.  See you all Sunday.
