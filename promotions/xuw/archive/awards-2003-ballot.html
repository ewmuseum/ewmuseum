2003 Awards Nominee Ballot

circa December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, here we are approaching the end of yet another year. It hardly seems like twelve months since we last did this, but it is. So, without much further chit chat, here are the awards nominees. There are a few new categories in there, and a few have been altered slightly from their appearance last year. To vote, simply write down a list of numbers from 1 - 31 down the side, and write next to it who you think should win each award, like this: 1 - Raj Patel 2 - Takerrocks 3 - Cereal Killer 4 - Kore etc etc Include in your email the name of the XUW or XUDF character you have played or are currently playing. We recognise most email addresses, but there might be one or two others. The address to send the nominations to is: [detail withheld] So, here we go: The XUW In Character Awards 1 - Best Past Wrestler (must not be in the Hall of Fame already) Who's your favourite wrestler not currently active in XUW? - Blake Wolfson - Dave Kipfer - HBK - M V Pete - Numark - Sheri 2 - Most Immediate Impact by a Newcomer Who was the quickest to send shock waves through the federation? - Darkseid - Doughboy - Joey Vendetti - Mister Amazing - The Shadow - Wildlife 3 - Best Wrestler Never to Hold a Major Title (Tag, World, IC) Who looks to be the next one to break this hoodoo? - Dev - DJ - Hot Boy D - Joey Vendetti - NFM - The Longhorn 4 - Best Catchphrase Which one do you find yourself using when with friends?
