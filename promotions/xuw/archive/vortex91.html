XUW: Vortex - Number 91 What does XUW do on Thanksgiving? Do we eat turkey? Not really.. Do we surround ourselves with our families and say what we're thankful for? Nah. So what do we do? We beat the living s*** out of each other on XUW Vortex, that's what we do! HAPPY THANKSGIVING!
