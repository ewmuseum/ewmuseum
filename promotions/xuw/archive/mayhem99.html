XUW: Mayhem - Number 99 The pyro flies higher than a stoner on a bong full of really strong weed… or some other simile designed to entertain. Whizz it goes, and bang and pop and splat, where it hits the occasional spectator, but he shouldn’t have been standing in the way, should he? We’re in the ever-familiar XUW Arena, where the crowd sit, stand and generally jump around with excitement for the latest edition of XUW Mayhem. It’s episode 99 of the best wrestling show in town, and with anticipation for Mayhem 100 getting greater and greater, they are so tightly sprung that you could turn them into a bunch of toy kangaroos. Toy kangaroos with signs though. Oh yes. “I want to be entered in the Deadly Draw!” “Woof Woof + Anyone = Deadly Draw Champions.” “I Submit to Cleanliness” …and making a surprise return to the fan sign circuit, the ever popular… “No, I am the father!” So, with all that fun, we go to the announcers, and it’s Mikey, along with “The Majestic” Barry Johnson. Not Lauwass this time, oh no. Mikey: Hello there ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to the ninety ninth edition of XUW Mayhem! We’re getting ever closer to that all-important Mayhem 100, but to some of these wrestlers here tonight, number 99 is going to be even more vital for them. BJ: Yes indeed, Mikey. As much as I hate to agree with you, not everyone will be in a match for the 100th edition of Mayhem, so this show could be very significant for them. Mikey: Just take Dev. He’s scheduled to defend his title in a week’s time against an as yet unnamed opponent… but that all changes if Wildlife can beat him tonight! BJ: As if that could ever happen… Mikey: Well, I wouldn’t… oh hang on… I’ve gotten some word about an attack that has been perpetrated backstage! We cut backstage to the view of a cameraman running down a corridor. He turns a corner and we find a few onlookers looking on at the body of former XUW World Champion, Sakie. He is lying on the floor in a pool of his own blood. He looks like he has been savagely attacked from behind with a blunt instrument of some description. The camera looks around for a clue as to who could have done this, but nobody can be seen… though perhaps we can just about hear a voice softly speaking. Voice: One by one they fall, and then they can’t stop me! The voice trails off into quiet laughter, but the cameraman cannot find the source of the voice, or indeed the laughter. With little further to do, the camera cuts back to the announcers as EMTs try to do an emergency patch up job on the embattled Boomer Sooner. Mikey: Well next up we have a scheduled match between Bubba Bailey and Sakie, but after seeing that, I don't think Sakie will be able to compete. BJ: Bah, he BETTER get out there and compete if he knows what's good for him. Mikey: And he'll be out there with the SoS... poor Sakie... They are interrupted as "As Above...So Below" by Behemoth hits the speakers and the boos rain down from the crowd. On top of the stage, Bubba Bailey walks out accompanied by Joey Vendetti. The massive Bubba Bailey just sneers at the crowd as he is in his ring attire, while Joey smiles his normal cocky little smile and he's wearing a fine Armani suit with his hair (that has just grown back) greased back. The two of them march to the ring and slide in. Mikey: I don't see why it's necessary for Joey to come out here with Bubba. BJ: To make sure Sakie doesn't try anything funny. Mikey: Right... Joey says something to Bubba then exits the ring and stands at ringside. Bubba's music fades and is soon replaced by "Numb" by Linkin Park. The fans pop massively for Sakie, but Sakie comes out onstage with a blood stained bandage around his head. As he walks down the ramp, he stumbles a little bit and falls down, having to pull himself back up. On the outside of the ring, Joey is laughing and on the inside, Bubba is gesturing for Sakie to GET IN THE RING. Mikey: This is unbelievable. I can't believe Sakie still has to compete. The heart of the man is incredible. BJ: Yep, he could leave if he was a sissy girl I suppose, but that stubborn Sooner won't admit it. Mikey: But who would attack Sakie? That just doesn't make any sense! Sakie finally makes it to the ring and slides in. The ref calls for the bell... DING!!!DING!!!DING!!! Bubba Bailey w/Joey Vendetti vs. Sakie Bubba rushes forward and quickly grabs Sakie and lifts him up in the powerbomb position. Bubba spins around three times quickly then jumps into the air with a jumping sitout powerbomb. The ref makes the count as Bubba holds the legs.... 1... 2... 3!!! DING!!!DING!!!DING!!! Winner by Pinfall: Bubba Bailey BJ: HA! That was fast! Mikey: Sakie is absolutely beaten to death here! This is unfair! Bubba gets back to his feet and beckons for Joey to enter the ring. Overkill walks down the corridor, and as coincidence would have it, he finds himself walking towards Kata, his opponent one week from today and the current Hardcore Champion. No words are spoken as they pass, but their eyes lock, and they both know that this will be a test. For the experienced veteran it will be a chance to prove that he is still the man who has defeated the likes of The Ripper and M V Pete, and for the rookie he can get a golden belt before he even loses a single match. Is this the breakthrough the Skeletons of Society have been looking for? Thoughts rush through the minds of the two soon-to-be adversaries, but before Overkill can think too much, he passes a side corridor, and sees a door swinging shut. He’s looked more and more stressed as the night has gone on. BJ: He must miss the competition. He’s nearly eight feet tall and is a former champion here in the federation. The eco-warrior walks up to Sev, but the Commissioner has turned away and is going to the backstage area. Wildlife won’t take no for an answer though, and uses all his strength to turn the giant Commissioner around. Mikey: Uh oh. Sev does not look amused here. Sev tries to tell Wildlife to shut up one more time, and turns round to leave… but Wildlife grabs him again. It’s all too much for the Commish, and he grabs the Submission Champion and lifts him high above his head, before dropping him down into a Gorilla Press Slam! The crowd pops huge for it, and Sev allows himself a smile as he goes to move to the back. Or at least, he tries. That’s the point that DJ arrives, and he smashes Sev around the head with his fist… which happens to have a massive chain wrapped around it. The Commish is staggered, and then another blow sends him to the ground in a fountain of blood. April tries to get in DJ’s way, but he just stands above Sev, taunting him. Heat from the crowd rains down on DJ, their former idol and current Intercontinental Title holder. The changed man just grunts, and moves away from the fallen Commissioner. A little too late, NFM rushes to the aid of the Commish, and helps him to his feet. NFM grabs a microphone. NFM: I’ll get him for trying to pull this kind of crap. You don’t turn your back on XUW like that, DJ. Your time is coming to an end. NFM goes to continue on, but Sev puts one huge hands across the chest of NFM, and gestures for the microphone. Sev: … … … April then grabs the microphone. April: Wow, now that is emotion. NFM, I’m afraid you no longer have an Intercontinental Title shot at Mayhem 100. NFM: But… April: Instead you get to go after the man you hate more than any other. You get to wrestler Ric Austin for the XUW World Title! The crowd pops huge, and NFM nods his head with a smile. NFM: Then who fights DJ? April smiles, and turns to point at the man mountain himself… Commissioner Sev! The crowd explodes with poppage for the news that Sev will be making a one-off return to the ring. Mikey: This isn’t about a title… this is about revenge, and about DJ turning his back not just on Sev, but on XUW as a whole! Slowly, Sev makes his way back to his office, accompanied by April, NFM and a trail of blood. Everyone else has long since cleared the ring, and it’s time for the Main Event. Mikey: Well folks, we’ve come to the final match of the evening, as the X-Factor is set to take on the XUW World Heavyweight Champion, Ric Austin... in a non-title match. BJ: You hate having to say that, don’t you? Mikey: Say what? BJ: Saying that Ric is the World Champion. It must eat you up inside! Mikey: I may not be fond of Austin, but he worked hard for the title, and when it was all said and done, he weaseled his way into becoming the champion. Good for him. BJ: I sense a little hostil... Mikey: Shut the f*ck up! BJ: ... ‘Smells Like Teen Spirit’ by Nirvana blasts through the speakers, and X-Factor steps out from the back. He looks out at the fans, and strolls down to the ring. Several old ladies at ringside hold their purses extra tightly for some odd reason as he passes by, and they give him dirty looks. Factor slides into the ring and stretches a little bit, as he awaits the champion. Mikey: You wouldn’t think it to look at him, but that young man in the ring is one of the founding fathers of XUW, and a seasoned veteran. BJ: Yep, he has been, not to mention X-Factor is a former World Champion. Mikey: Lately though, Factor’s been toiling around in the lower ranks, but tonight is his big opportunity to make an impact, by defeating the arrogant, pompous ass, Ric Austin! BJ: Hah! There’s that hostility again! ‘In the End’ by Linkin Park replaces Factor’s music, and the man everybody loves to hate... scratch that... that man who everybody just plain hates, Ric Austin, strolls out from the back, accompanied as usual by his associates Bob the Temp, and his black, mute bodyguard, Derrick Miles. The trio walk down to the ring whilst being booed tremendously, and Ric hands the World Title to Derrick to hold onto, as Ric looks at his reflection in the belt and then kisses it. He then forces Derrick to wipe the smear off of the belt as he begins to walk up the steps to the ring, but not before forcing Bob to wipe off each stair before his feet touch them. Mikey: Oh brother. I didn’t think he could get more conceited! BJ: Conceited? He just knows what he deserves! Hell, that ring should be spotless before Ric steps onto it! And just as BJ says that, a cleaning crew run out to the ring and begin to clean the ring in front of a grinning Ric Austin, but the ref quickly forces them out to Ric’s disappointment. And finally the two men are alone in the ring, and the match can finally start. Main Event: The X-Factor vs. Ric Austin (non-title) The two men circle each other slowly, and then lock up in the standard collar and elbow tie up, with Ric Austin quickly getting the advantage by placing Factor in a side headlock. Factor squirms somewhat, and then whips Ric to the ropes, but Ric drops to a knee and holds on, cinching in the headlock even more. BJ: Very impressive headlock there by our World champion, if I do say so myself... not the Best Damn Headlock in the Business, but close in stature. Mikey: What’s the difference between them? I mean seriously, what’s the difference? It’s a headlock, a HEAD-LOCK! BJ: You are obviously not keen on your wrestling maneuvers then, if you don’t know the difference. Mikey: I’m not keen on my wrestling maneuvers? You’re not serious, are you? BJ, you don’t even know what a snap mare is! BJ: Of course I do! It’s.... it’s... well.... Mikey: Thought so. Back in the ring, the Ric still has the headlock in place, and Factor lifts Ric up into a side suplex, but Ric leans forward, causing him to drop down and Factor to flip over him, and to the mat, with the headlock still cinched in. Factor feels around, trying to figure a way out, and he finds a handful of Austin’s hair. He yanks on it, and Austin releases the hold immediately as he gets up and starts yelling at the ref about Factor pulling his precious World Champion locks. Factor takes advantage of Ric not paying attention, and rolls him up for a pin attempt! 1... 2... 2.5... NOOO!!!!! Austin barely squirms a shoulder loose, and he hops up and runs for the ropes. On the return, Factor goes for a standing dropkick, but Austin holds onto the ropes. Factor lands hard on his head, and grabs it as Ric drops his knee directly over Factor’s head. Ric then helps Factor up, and quickly nails him with a reverse DDT with a spin! BJ: Yes! A Snap mare! Mikey: No, you idiot. That was Ric’s signature Break Down. BJ: Well yeah, I was just testing to see if you knew that! Of course I know all of Ric’s main moves..... Ric struts around the ring arrogantly for a few moments, as Factor begins to sit up, and then Ric charges at him, and cinches it in, the wrestling maneuver to end all wrestling maneuvers, the Best Damn Headlock in the Business! BJ: Yes, Snap....wait... Mikey: What was that BJ? were you about to call that a snap mare? BJ: No! Of course not. Sheesh. I was just being a hip dude by saying ‘snap.’ That word's all the rage, man. Mikey: Ugh... Ric locks the headlock in tightly, but Factor is able to twist around and contort himself to where he can flip himself over and be laying on top of Austin... for a pin! 1... 2.... Shoulder up by Ric, who lets go of what turned into a front face lock, and he starts yelling at the ref again, about how Factor shouldn’t be allowed to counter out of the Best Headlock in the Business, because it’s so great. Therefore, Factor must have cheated. Mikey: Okay, this is getting ridiculous! Ric backs the ref up to the corner, yelling and screaming at him, and Derrick Miles joins the fray, frowning and glaring. With the ref distracted with Derrick, Ric grins and walks toward X, only to discover that Factor is waiting for him, and knocks him for a loop with solid lefts and rights, stunning the world champion. Factor grabs Ric, and executes a quick snap suplex, and then follows up with what looks to be another, but stops all of a sudden and drops Ric with the Evenflow DDT! Ric is down now, and X-Factor senses the opportunity to steal a victory, pun intended. Mikey: Factor’s going to the top rope fans! This could spell the end for our wonderful champion! BJ: Oh dear god I hope not! Factor gets to the top turnbuckle, but he took too long to nail his Shooting Star Press finisher, since Ric has started to rise to his feet. but Factor waits on the top rope for Ric to get to his feet and turn around... waiting... waiting... Factor leaps off the turnbuckle, executing a picture perfect top rope Hurricanrana!!!! .... or at least that was his intention. Too bad he ended up being powerbombed viciously to the mat... twice, as Ric countered the move. Factor grabs his head, and appears to be in dire straights, as Ric Austin signals for the end... picks up Factor... AND HITS THE IDOLIZER!!!! Mikey: Well, Factor might have had Ric there once or twice. Better luck next time I suppose. Ric leans over X-Factor for the cover... 1... 2... 2.5... 2.75.... Shoulder up! Well, not really. Ric pulled Factor up... Mikey: What the hell is he doing? Just pin him you arrogant prick! BJ: No, not yet. I still need to see a snap mare, so I know what it is! Austin picks up X-Factor and lifts him up for the Idolizer a second time, and holds him for a few moments while smugly laughing at the ref and the fans. But Ric eventually drops Factor on his head again, and this time pins him completely. 1... 2... 3!!!! Winner: Ric Austin!!!!!! Ric rises to his feet, and the ref rises Austin’s hand in victory. After the ref finally lets go, Austin screams at him again, making him lift Austin’s hand up once again, as he makes Derrick present him with the World Title. Mikey: Ric Austin is so arrogant, so egotistical, I don’t see how anybody could possibly like him... even you. BJ: He’s my hero, and I’m so happy that he hit that snap mare at the end. Mikey: Umm, yeah. Great snap mare.... anyway, good win by Ric Austin tonight, but as we heard earlier tonight, there’s no rest for the asshole champion, as next week he is forced to put the world title up for grabs against NFM. NFM is finally getting the rematch against Austin that he never received, and the largest prize in the company is up for grabs! BJ: Ugh, don’t remind me. Ric beat him once. Ric will beat him again.
