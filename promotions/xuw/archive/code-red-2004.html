XUW Code Red

XUW: Attitude. Belief. Desire. XUW Code Red - Monday, May 17, 2004 On Sunday, April 4th, at XUW Reborn… Mikey: April has stepped down, and has named a new XUW Commissioner! BJ: Bah, I'm sure whoever it is will be reasonable enough to do the right thing, and put Austin in the main event! April waves goodbye, and the Ultitron fades out. Austin: Fine! If you want to be a coward and run away, then so be it! I don't care who the new Commissioner is, because I'm still going to get what I want! I'm still going to be in tonight's main event! I don't even care if the new Commissioner is... Austin is cut off, as suddenly all the arena lights go out... … … "HUZZAH!!!" The fans cannot believe it. They go absolutely berserk. Ric Austin cannot believe it. He nearly soils himself. Mikey cannot believe it. He starts marking out. Mikey: MY GOD!! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!! IT'S SAIJ!! BJ: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Mikey: SAIJ IS BACK!! SAIJ IS THE NEW COMMISSIONER OF XUW!! BJ: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! …and so a new era was born for Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. An era not based on fear and intimidation, like the Ultra Violents. An era not based on a giant mute, like Severson. An era not based on a woman with the same name as some broad who hung around with Ninja Turtles, like April O’Neill. An era not based on whatever the hell Mister Webster used to do, like Mister Webster. No, this new era would be based on fun, games, playing pranks on Ric Austin… and above all. FARKNESS! On Monday, April 18th, at XUW Mayhem… The camera switches over to the locker room of the Boomer Sooners, in Saij's "office." The XUW Commissioner is sitting on the couch, and drinking from a two-liter bottle of Mountain Dew: Code Red. He takes a swig of the beverage, then looks into the camera. Saij: Huzzah, everyone! First, I'm happy to announce that it was just been learned that one of the (supposedly) best female wrestlers in the PTC has been beaten... by a midget! The sound of a crowd pop can be heard in the background. Saij: But that's not what I'm here to talk about. I'm here to talk about XUW's next... PAY-PER-VIEW!!! Saij takes another drink of his Code Red. Saij: Now, I'm still getting a few details about the PPV sorted out. I've been trying all day to think of a really farkin' cool name for it... a name that smacks of Fark-O-Mania! I've come up with such titles as "XUW: Farktoberfest," "XUW: Farkapalooza," "XUW: Farkstravaganza," "XUW: Prick Austin Sucks," but none of them really quite seem to feel right to me. And to be honest, I still haven't quite thought of the name... Saij goes to take another drink from his Code Red bottle... then he stops. He stares at the bottle for a moment. His face lights up, and he grins broadly. Saij: THAT'S IT!!! I've got it! XUW's next PPV show shall henceforth be named... XUW: CODE RED! Saij nods. Saij: That's right ladies and gentlemen, in less than four weeks, XUW will present the first-ever PPV under the New Era of Farkness... Code Red! Code Red will be held at Camp Randall Stadium in Madison, Wisconsin, on Sunday, May 16th! …and thusly, from the mind of a ‘genius’, XUW: Code Red, was envisaged. Questions still remained as to who would be there, though. Who could challenge Darkseid for the World Title? Rumours were being whispered that The Fallen Fae might get a title shot, but after his tazer shot to the head, nothing of Fae remained but a body going by the name of Sam Stellars… A pacifist by nature, Sam didn’t want to fight Darkseid, until Kat was placed in the line of fire. On Thursday, May 6th, at XUW Vortex… Sam stares down the ramp at Darkseid, and speaks. Sam: Darkseid... I don't want to have to resort to violence. I don't want to have to fight. However... after what you did to Kat, I may have no choice. So... Sam pauses, as if thinking over his decision one final time. Darkseid waits patiently. Sam: I accept your challenge. The crowd pops! Darkseid smiles and nods. Sam: That doesn't mean I'm going to fight, though. It just means I'm going to show up. I don't want to resolve this by fighting, but if you force me to... ...then Darkseid, I can't be responsible for what happens. Darkseid chuckles bemusedly, as he picks up his mic again. Darkseid: You can't be responsible for what happens, eh? An interesting claim. But you're a shell of the man you once were, and at Code Red, YOUR PAIN WILL BE MY PLEASURE!!! Darkseid smiles, and licks his lips in anticipation. Sam eyes his opponent warily from the top of the stage. The two men keep their gaze locked on each other, as the camera fades to black.
