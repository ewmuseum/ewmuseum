XUDF TV Tapings #2

c. late 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Begin Live Satelite Feed The following program is brought to you by P.A.E. Productions, Nintendo Game Cube, Saltrates, and King Cobra Malt Liquor "Song 2" by Blur hits and an obscene amount of fire works explodes at the first Whoooo Hoooooo!  A video montage of last week's highlights and lowlights rolls as the packed convention center goes nuts. Bice: Welcome to the second edition of the XUDF.  We're at...uhhhh we're at... Shooter: Shut up you old senile fool.  It doesn't matter where we're at.  As long as we get the F in.  We'll be fine. Bice: Get the F in?  What's that supposed to mean? The two are interupted as "Ditty dum ditty do" hits and the confused crowd pops. Bice: What's the meaning of this? Shooter: Who is it?  Don't tell me it's Film Scorpion. Pete Tha Pimp and eight scantily dressed women apper at the top of the runway.  The girls dance along as PTP makes his way to the ring.  He grabs a mic and waits to begin. Shooter: What's this bum doing here? Bice: I don't know.  Who is he? PTP: Well!!!!!! Welcome to the show.  If no one noticed.  This program is brought to you by P.A.E. Productions.  Pimpin Ain't Easy Productions!  That's right, ME!  So I'll be producing a show every couple of weeks.  So without further aduie.  Let the show begin!!!! Wildlife vs. Tim Clover The theme from the Animal Channel hits and out comes Wildlife.  He gets some boos for his effort as he makes his way ringside, picking up trash and telling people to recycle. Bice: And here's our first matchup tonite.  Wildlife is entering the ring first. Shooter: Where did we find this guy? Bice: I don't know.  I just don't know. "Boom"  by P.O.D. hits and out comes Tim Clover to a mixed reaction.  He wastes no time and quickly heads towards the ring. Bice: And here's Tim Clover making his in-ring debut.  Last week he beat down Wildife but the favor was returned by the nature boy later on. Shooter: That's what I like to see.  Two guys who hate eachother. This should be great. Suddenly, "Dogz" by Redman hits.  The crowd marks out as Supa Thug Esco struts down the runway. Bice: What's he doing here? Shooter: Geez, I wonder.  Didn't you notice the extra headset. Bice: Ohh.  I guess the Supa Thug is joining us. Clover wasts no time admist the confusion as he slides in and the two begin to exchange rights. Bice: No love lost between these two. Esco gets to the table and sits down. Supa: Wazzup!!! Bice: What do you want? Supa: Just here fo the show. Shooter: Good to see you brother. Clover gets the early advantage and sends Wildlife to the ropes.  He hits Wildlife with a power clothesline, sending the nature dude to the mat. Clover quickly hits the mat as well and puts Wildlife into an armbar submission. Bice: A little early for a submission huh? Shooter: It's never too early.  I hope he taps! Bice: Why? Shooter: So we can get home earlier. Supa: Please.  These rookies. Much to Shooter's dismay, Wildlife refuses to submit.  Clover pulls harder on the submission, to no avail.  Wildlife slowly reaches for the ropes and is able to get his right foot on the bottom rope.  The ref calls for a break but Clover holds on for a little longer until the ref warns him multiple times. Bice: Clover's here to hurt the guy isn't he? Shooter: Hurt him.  Kill him.  Whatever.  Just end the match. Supa: Just like what I'm gonna do tomorrow night to those Usual Suspect Fools. Clover waits for Wildlife to get up.  Wildlife, using the ropes, slowly gets to his feet only to be kicked in the gut for his troubles.  Wildlife keels over and Clover gets behind him and readies to hit a german suplex. Wildlife, realizing his trouble, gives Clover a kick to the junk. Bice: Low blow.  Low Blow!  How did the ref miss that!!! Supa: That's dope! Clover steps backwards, grabbing his groin.  Wildlife seizes the opportunity and hits a diving headbutt.  Clover hits the mat hard.  Wildlife gets to his feet, shakes off the cob webs and smiles as he looks down at the fallen Clover.  Wildlife hits a springboard moonsault and both men are on the mat. Bice: What a great matchup.  Two guys giving it all. Shooter: Please.  I can do that much better. Supa: Gonna have to go with my boy Shooter on that one. Wildlife gets to his feet and signals for the end.  Clover seems to be unconcsious as Wildlife picks up the big man.  He stands him up and hits a Full Nelson Russian Legsweep. Bice: The Eco-Challenge!!!  That's it.  It's over. Shooter: Thank God! Wildlife goes for the cover.  The ref slides in and the count begins. 1 . . . 2 . . . Kickout!!! Wildlife can't beleive it.  Wildlife goes for the Canopy Snap but Clover reverses it and puts Wildlife into modified Sharpshooter. Bice: The 4 Leaf Clover! Supa: This one's finished. Shooter: Thank God. Clover has Wildlife in the middle of the ring.  Wildlife has no choice but to tap out. Winner by submission....Tim Clover!!!! The ref holds up Clover's arm as the crowd slightly pops for the victor. Wildlife is visibley upset as he heads over to the announce table.  Esco gets up and tosses his chair to the big man. Bice: What did you do that for? Supa: I dunno.  He's a bad guy.  I'm a bad guy. Shooter: Finally some excitement! Clover is still posing victoriously as Wildlife waffles him from behind and knocks him out.  The crowd boos the big man as he throws down the chair and leaves. Supa: Looks like I'm out too.  No chair.  Gotta run. Shooter: Later Thug.  Take out the Suspect's for me.  What a great guy. Bice: Yeah.  A great humanitarian.   Well, that ends the first match. Clover made an impressive debut as he made quick work of Wildlife. Shooter: Not quick enough. Bice: Did anyone ever tell you that you're annoying? Shooter: Yeah.  My mom. Bice: Figures. Shooter: C'mon.  Let's get this card moving. Bice: That's right.  Next on the card.  An interview. "Two Ton" Tommy Thompson is in the middle of the XUDF ring with a microphone in his hand. TT: Fans, you've been witnessing some great XUDF action here tonight, but now it's time for a change of pace. Please join me in welcoming one of XUDF's superstars. From Galveston, Texas, the Latch Key Kid! The crowd pops as Alice Cooper's "School's Out" blasts out of the PA system and LKK jogs down to the ring. He jumps up onto the apron and climbs through the ropes. TT: Welcome, Kid. Can I call you Kid? LKK Sure, if I can call you Double T. TT: Deal. So anyway, Kid, how does it feel to finally get that first XUDF match out of the way? LKK: Well, I'll tell you. It feels great. It was a tough match, and Sean Winstrom put up a great fight. I've gotta hand it to 'Shooter,' he obviously came prepared for that match. I'm just glad to get the match out of the way, and it's just icing on the cake that I was victorious." TT: Yes, I can imagine. Now let me ask you something. DDT came down to the ring during the match. Do you know why? Have you been in touch with him over something? LKK: Double T, I honestly have no idea why DDT came down there. I've never met the guy. Heck, I've never even talked to him. So I don't know what that was all about. Frankly, though, I don't like it. DDT, if you have something to say to me, say it. Don't walk out like you're going to interfere in my match. I don't need your help. Stay away, you hear me? TT: I think he heard you, alright. Now, Kid, in your promos you come across as a fairly quiet guy. You don't seem to get too hot-headed. How do you control your temper, or do you even have a temper? LKK: Double T, it's like this. Anger is a strong emotion. Most of the time, if you let anger in, it will get the better of you. It clouds your head, your mind, your thought process. I've learned in my life that if I can keep my temper from manifesting itself, my judgment will be much clearer. And I can use that to my advantage in my matches. Now, that's not to say I don't feel anger at all. I know what anger is, I know what rage is, and I know what it's like to let that loose. I know how to control it, and I know when the appropriate time to let it loose is. Sean Winstrom found that out last week. My next opponent will very likely find that out, as well. The camer changes backstage where Kata is watching, the crowd pops TT: Right. Moving along, then. You seem to talk a lot about your past. Why is that? LKK: Look, Tubby. I don't talk about my past to make myself look like avgreat person. I talk about it because it's made me who I am today. There were a lot of tough times. The roller coaster had more downs than ups, if you know what I mean. I took the tough things, though, and instead of letting them defeat me and drive me into the darkness, I rallied against them and forged a fighter, a warrior, a survivor. And that's the person you're talking to now. TT: Yeah, uhm...wow, would you look at that. We're out of time. Great talking to you, Kid. We should do it again sometime. LKK: You know where to find me, Double T. I'm glad I could be a part of this XUDF show. I'm looking forward to next week's show. And I'd like to thank all the fans for coming out tonight. See ya later, Tommy. "School's Out" blasts over the PA system and the Latch Key Kid makes his way out of the ring and back up the aisle, slapping hands and giving high-fives along the way. Meanwhile, backstage....... Film Scorpion is watching LKK's interview as Darkseid comes up from behind.  Film doesn't realize until Darkseid comes right next to Film's ear and says. DS: Enjoying the show? Film jumps a bit and turns around to see Darkseid.  No words are exchanged as the two stare eachother down.  LKK comes through the curtains and sees the two at eachothers throats and jumps between the two. LKK: Break it up fellas.  Let's let the guys on the card get the spotlight tonite.  Let's not mess around backstage. DS: No problems here.  Was just asking my friend a question. LKK: Yeah sure.  You're nothing but trouble. Doughboy enters the scene to a crowd pop. DB: What are you doing here? Darkseid just smiles at the trio as he slowly backs away. LKK: Get out of here bro.  Before any trouble breaks out. Darkseid continues to smile as he backs away and exits the scene, leaving the three to just stare at him. Shooter: What was that?  Darkseid causing problems!  I love it! Bice: That's what makes this place so damn exiting.  Now to our next match.  This one started at Flip "The Man's" interview last week.  That dastardy Papa Lazarou attacked him and ran. Shooter: Yeah and we haven't heard from him since. Flip "The Man" vs. Papa Lazarou "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourn hits and Papa Lazarou comes out to a series of boos. Bice: Not a popular guy, is he? Shooter: I don't see why?  He seems like a nice guy. Bice: Nice guy? Papa Lazarou gets in the ring and waits.  He does not wait long as "We are the Champions" hits and Flip bumrushes the ring. Bice: Looks like The Man is in a rush to get Lazarou. Shooter: Huh.  What a cheater.  Hey I got an idea. In the ring, Flip is unloading on Lazarou. Bice: What is it? Shooter: Let's take a break and just watch.  No commentating. Bice: Fine.  Anything to shut you up. In the ring.  Flip is fast at work getting revenge on Papa Lazarou.  Flip punches Papa with a left, then a right, and then delivers a knee to the gut. Papa Lazarou keels over as Flip runs to the ropes, springs back, and nails Papa with a running DDT.  Papa is down on the mat as he tries to pull himself together.  Flip takes the opportunity to play to the crowd, as he gets up on the bottom rope and strikes a pose.  Papa recovers and nails Flip with a superkick, sending Flip over the ropes. Bice: The inexperience shows, both men are down and out. In the ring, it seems the superkick took as much out of Papa as it did from Flip.  Flip recovers first and crawls back into the ring to seize the opportunity.  Flip picks up his opponent and executes a Fisherman Suplex. Bice: WOW.  He's really taking advantage here. Shooter: Blah blah blah.  When is the main event starting? Bice: Shut up.  I thought you were going to be quiet. Inside the ring, Flip continues to have his way with Papa.  Flip sends Papa to the ropes but Lazarou reverses it.  On his return, Flip is met with a spear from the big man.  Flip no sells it and gets up just as fast as Papa does.  Papa doesn't beleive it.  Flip clothesline Papa, sending the big man hard to the mat.   FLip picks him up and sets Papa up on the top rope.  The crowd cheers as they sense the end coming.  Flip sets Papa up and begins what appears to be a suplex but he swings Papa around and executes a tope rope piledriver. Bice: Tope-Rope Piledriver!!! Shooter: Whooop de frickin do.  Wonder how you came up with that name? Bice: Be quiet! Papa is knocked cold after that one and Flip goes for the easy cover. 1 . . . 2 . . . 3! Winner by pinfall- Flip "The Man" Bice: Impressive debut for Flip.  Not so for Papa Lazarou. Shooter: Not at all.  Maybe he should rethink this whole wrestling thing. Bice: Well, it's time for the main event.  This one's been boiling up since last week.  It should be a doozy.  Wait, something tells me there something going on backstage. Backstage cam..... Camer zooms in on Blade, who is roaming the backstage area.  He looks pretty pissed off as he finally will get his hands on Fezz tonite.  As he's about to turn the corner, a voice at the far end of the hall calls him out. Hey Blade! Blade turns to see Beamer at the opposite end of the hall. Beamer: Like how I embarrassed your punk freind Jonny Postal?  He's lucky Fezz used a bat and knocked him out of the game.  Saved himself the pain that Fezz is gonna rain down on you tonite.  See ya in the hospital. Blade just shoots a pissed off look at Beamer and then resumes his walk to the ring. Bice: Damn.  He's on pissed off guy.  I'd hate to be Fezz tonite? Shooter: Hate to be Fezz?  Didn't you hear what Beamer said?  I feel bad for Blade. Bice: I'm just gonna ignore you from now on.  But now, the main event. MAIN EVENT- Blade vs. Fezz "X" Ray "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit hits and out steps Fezz "X" Ray to a mixed reaction.  Fezz makes his way down to the ring, yelling back at some of the more vocal fans.  Fezz hops over the top rope and awaits his opponent. Bice: Fezz looks confident and focused.  It looks like he's on a mission tonite. Shooter: He should be confident.  He's gonna thrash Blade.  I got money on it. Bice: You gamble on this? Shooter: Yeah.  Why not?  It makes it more interesting. "Bad Boy For Life" hits and out comes the monsterous Blade to a decent pop.  The 6'11 beast heads towards the ring, eyes never moving off of Fezz. Bice: WOW!  He looks crazy, this should be a doozy. Shooter: It better be.  Or I want my money back. Blade slides under the bottom rope, the bell rings, and the main event is on.  The two circle eachother, neither side willing to make the first move. Fezz puts his hands up, challenging the giant to a strength test.  Blade goes along but gets a roundhouse kick to the face for his troubles, sending him back into the ropes. Bice: Fezz with the trickery. Shooter: Trickery?  I call it strategy. Blade grabs his jaw as it seems that Fezz has stunned the big man.  Blade moves in on Fezz only to get a russian leg sweep for his troubles.  The 300 lbs. beast hits the canvas hard.  Fezz looks to seize the opportunity and hits the turnbuckles early.  He stops at the top and poses for the fans. Bice: Fezz definately has been aggresive so far.  Will it pay off? Fezz soars into the air and hits a 1080 degree senton bomb. Bice: Wow Shooter: The Fezzton Bomb!!!! Bice: Did you just make that up? Shooter: No.  I swear. Fezz lands hard on the big man, putting Blade in a world of pain.  Fezz rolls over and goes for the early pin. 1 . . . 2 . . Shoulder up! Bice: WOW!  Fezz threw up his shoulders in the nick of time. Shooter: Damn.  I though he had it. Fezz can't beleive it and begins to argue with the ref, claiming there was a slow count.  The ref's not buying and Fezz turns right around into a Terminator (Chokeslam) from Blade. Bice: A lot of rookie mistakes tonite.  He shouldn't have lost track of the Big Man. Fezz is down and Blade begins his work.  He picks up the smaller Fezz and holds him over his head.  He completes the gorilla press slam, accidently throwing Fezz into the ref, knocking him down and out. Bice: Uh oh.  The referee is down.  We're gonna need another one down here. Blade checks on the ref only to see that he's out cold.  He turns around to find Fez leaning against the far ropes.  Blade charges him and knocks him out of the ring.  The crowd is loving it.  Blade climbs out of the ring and joins Fezz outside.  On his way over, Blade sees a chair and sees an opportunity to avenge his freind.  He grabs the chair and heads over to Fezz.  The big guy holds the chair over his head and as he swings, Fezz recovers enough to kick the chair back into Blade's face. Bice: Wow, this kid's got guts! Shooter: That's why I bet on him. The chair shot stunned the big man and Fezz takes advantage.  He whips Blade around and sends him into the steel post of the turnbuckle.  Blade goes down hard.  Fezz reaches under the ring and pulls out a baseball bat! Bice: Oh no.  It looks like he's going to give Blade matching injuries!!! Shooter: Excellent.  Finally some entertainment. Fezz goes to swing as he brings the bat over his head.  He's about to swing down when the bat is stopped from behind.  He turns around.  It's PTP!!!!  He never left the ringside! PTP: Not on my show. Fezz doesn't take too kindly and pushes PTP away.  PTP is succesful as he takes the bat with him.  Fezz turns back to the still down Blade.  A few stops to the head later, Fezz heads over to PTP and grabs the bat.  On his way back to Blade, Fezz is intercepted by a new ref who informs him that they are about to be counted out.  Fezz angrily picks up Blade and rolls him back into the ring.  Fezz springs to the ropes and nails a split leg moonsault onto Blade!!! Again, he rolls over for the cover. 1 . . . 2 . . . Foot on the ropes!!!! Bice: Oh my God!!!! Where did that come from!!!!! Shooter: Dammit!! Fezz can't beleive it.  He pounds his hand 1...2...3...onto the mat, arguing with the referee.  The crowd comes alive as Blade slowly recovers. Fezz, now smarted, gets up and turns to attack Blade.  He baseball slides into Blade's face, knocking the big man down.  Fezz once again pulls him to the corner.  As Fezz climbs the ropes, Blade slowly gets up.  Fezz gets to the top and jumps, attempting to hit Fezz with a Body Splash. Bice: He CAUGHT HIM!!!!! Shooter: Ahhhh ******* Blade actually caught in mid air.  Now the big man hits a brackbeaker, sending Fezz bouncing off of the mat in pain.  The crowd pops BIG!!! Bice: It looks like it's over now. Before Blade can do anymore damage he is distracted, Beamer has come halfway down the ramp and is watching the match.  He distracts Blade enough so that Fezz can recover and hit Blade with a running bulldog.  Beamer smiles as he watches on. Bice: Why the heck is Beamer down here?  What's he want to accomplish? Shooter: Hey! He works here.  He can go there if he wants.  He just wants to see Fezz beat Blade. Bice: I don't know about that. Fezz hopes to capitalize once and for all on his opponent.  He once again climbs the top ropes and hopes to hit one last finishing move.  But at the same time Blade is getting up as well.  He sees Fezz climbing and charges the ropes, causing Fezz to crotch himself.  As Fezz is in pain.  Blade punches him a couple times to the back of the head and climbs the rope as well.  He gets to the top and picks Fezz up and executes a Brainbuster off the top rope. Bice: That's it!!!! The Death Drop.  It's definately over!!! Blade hooks Fezz's leg and goes for the cover. 1 . . . 2 . . . 3!!!!! Winner by pinfall- Blade!!! Bice: Blade has won.  Blade has pinned Fezz for the win!!! Shooter: No **** Sherlock. Blade gets up and gets his arm raised as the place pops as much as a XUDF arena can pop.  Beamer smiles and heads back at the ramp, staring at Blade. Blade returns the favor as the two eye eachother up from a distance. Bice: There you have it folks!  Tune in for tomorrow night's XUW Pay Per View, Dingos and Darkness.  Where today's stars battle it out. Shooter: And don't forget to play Gamecube, use Saltrates, call PTP, or drink King Cobra. End Transmission
