XUW XPosure #20

21 Jul 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW XPosure The Ring Announcer runs to the ring and takes in the numerous cheers. He does the normal opening speech, you know, no video cameras, no laser pointers, no tossing stuff in the ring, before spending five minutes pimping the concession stands filled with GREAT xUw merchandise before announcing the first match of the evening... Sheri vs. Laudust Sheri makes her way to the ring, slapping hands as she does, then slides under the ropes and poses in the corners. Laudust is soon to follow, resplendent in his black fur lined parka as he gestures for a mic, which the announcer hands him. Laudust: “ ‘What was sundered and undone, shall be whole, the two made one.’ Augura, The Dark Crystal, 1982. And just as Augura meant the healing of the Crystal would start a new era, so will my defeat of you tonight lead into a new era. The era of... swoooooo... laPARKAdust!” Sheri looks at him with her head cocked to the side as he climbs into the ring and the bell sounds. Laudust goes for a lockup but Sheri steps to the side. Again Laudust grabs for her only to have Sheri sidestep him. A third attempt and he gets a kick to his stomach. He doubles over and Sheri bounces off the ropes, only to be caught in a powerslam. Laudust covers... 1... Kickout! Laudust pulls Sheri to her feet and tries for a punch but Sheri blocks and lands a punch of her own. Another punch, another block and return, and Laudust tries for a dropkick, connecting and sending Sheri into the ropes. She slides under the ropes and lands on the floor and the ref begins to count. Laudust goes to a corner and rips a turnbuckle pad off, tossing it to the crowd as he walks back to the ref. He shoves the ref aside and goes outside, grabbing Sheri and sliding her back into the ring. Sheri is quick to her feet and as he climbs back up to the apron, Sheri grabs his head and drops to her knees, sending Laudust neck first into the ropes and to the floor. This time it’s Sheri who goes to the outside, pulling him to his feet and tossing him back to the ring. She follows him in, again pulls him to his feet, and irish whips him to the corner... the same corner that, surprise surprise, is missing the turnbuckle. Laudust stumbles and goes head first into the exposed bold as Sheri runs up behind him and rolls him into a schoolboy. 1... 2... 3! Winner By Pinfall: Sheri On the Ultitron (yes it’s a house show, but let us worry about that, ‘kay? Thanks.) we see Clay Hamilton catch up with Kevil... Clay Hamilton: Kevil! Kevil! It’s been awhile since you’ve been at an xUw event... where have you been? Kevil grabs the mic away from Clay and takes a breath, preparing to bust one lose, but before he can Blake Savage runs up behind him and clocks him with a trash can lid, knocking Kevil to his face. He looks down a Kevil, picks the mic up, looks at Clay, and says... Blake Savage: I hate his dumb-a$$ rhymes. He hands the mic back to Clay and walks off as Kevil begins to stir... Back in the ring, the announcer announces our next match of the evening... KsC Trunkz vs.  Tiger Mask Tiger Mask makes his way to the ring as the announcer announces him, and KsC follows. The two waste no time as the bell sounds and the two lock up. Tiger gets the side headlock but KsC powers out, lifting Tiger in the air and dropping him on his back. KsC comes off the ropes and tries for an elbow but Tiger rolls aside and KsC gets nothing but mat. The two get back to their feet at the same time and size each other up. KsC extends one fist slowly to Tiger, who does the same. The two touch fists, then lock up again. This time KsC gets the better half, running and hitting a bulldog and covering... 1... 2... Kickout! KsC gets Tiger in a front facelock, wearing him down on the mat. KsC pulls him up and sets him up for a suplex, but Tiger slides over the top and shoves KsC into the ropes, hitting a dropkick and sending KsC again into the ropes. KsC comes off the ropes and lands a hard clothesline on Tiger, knocking him to the mat. KsC is quick to capitalize and locks in the D.N.M.E (Crippler Crossface) and leans back, forcing Tiger to tap. Winner By Submission: KsC XUW Blast From The Past Match: Sakie vs. Lopen from December 24, 2001 Vortex Linda Robin introduces Santa who gets a large pop as he comes out with a bag full of toys. Santa walks up towards the ring, going "ho ho ho" "merry christmas". Stopping to greet the fans and shaking hands he walks over to the announcers table. Burton: How ya doing Santa Santa: Ho Ho Ho Scott Burton, pretty good. You having a good christmas? *HBK cuts them off* HBK: Look fatty, the XUW commish asks that you just go sit down shutup and ring the stupid bell when told and there won't be any more incidents like we had at the mall the other day, got it? *Santa stops laughing and turns around, going over to the time keeper's chair.* Linda introduces Sakie who comes out to a big pop, Sakie comes down in a new set of tights that are slick black with Hurristoner's Rule written down his tights in silver. He has the cruiserweight belt slung over his shoulder and he stops near the announcers table to glare in HBK's direction who acts as though he isn't seeing him and merely scoffs. Sakie smirks and rolls into the ring raising his belt high into the air. Linda then introduces Lopen who comes out to massive heat, the world title fastened across his waist, Lopen points down to it and then grins towards Sakie as he makes his way up the ring steps and into the ring. Sakie points at the world title and Lopen shrugs. Mexican grabs a hold of Sakie's belt and places it before Lopen who in turn mocks the belt with a scoff. **Sakie wastes no time and hits a running dropkick on Lopen sending him back into the turnbuckles, Sakie gets up and hits a knife edge chop against his chest, the skin slapping and echoing across the arena as the fans react. He goes for another chop leaving a red mark on Lopen's skin. Sakie calls out to the fans as he raises a fist in the air, but Lopen grabs a hold of the back of his neck and twists away from the turnbuckle reversing the situation and tossing Sakie into the corner, Lopen responds with a set of his own knife edge chops, before giving Sakie a blatant thumb to the eye. Mexican warns Lopen of such a move as Sakie holds a hand over his eyes. Lopen gives the ref a confused look and goes back to work on Sakie with a blatant choke hold, Mexican breaks the attack and sticks a finger in Lopen's face. Lopen pushes the ref aside and grabs a hold of Sakie's arm irish whipping him into the far turnbuckle, finishing out the attack with a rushing lariat crushing Sakie against the turnbuckles, as he steps away Sakie stumbles out from corner and Lopen hits a bulldog, slamming Sakie's face hard into the mat. **Lopen poses and says its over as he rolls Sakie on the back going for an arrogant cover with just one foot on his chest. Mex doesn't even get to his knee to count a 1 before Sakie has kicked out. Lopen looks a bit irked as he goes to drag Sakie back up on his feet, but Sakie catches him by Surprise pulling him down into a small package! Mex hits the mat going for a count 1...2..NO! Lopen gets over the initial shock and kicks out, the fans begin to chant Sakie's name as he rises to his feet around the same time as Lopen...Sakie spins on his heel hitting a sidekick to Lopen's midsection, then backing off a few steps landing a spinning heel kick across his chin and knocking Lopen down to the mat. Sakie springs off the nearby ropes and hits a falling senton splash, Lopen jerks once and reaches down holding his ribs in pain as Sakie gets up and poses for the crowd who returns a big pop! He turns around and picks Lopen up sending him into the ropes and leap frogging over him, pivoting back around to catch him in a belly to belly release suplex upon his return sending Lopen flying up and over landing hard on his back! Lopen quickly rolls out of the ring to break Sakie's momentum and looks shocked as Sakie refuses to take his eyes off him.... Burton: Looks like your boy is having trouble! HBK: That's nonsense, look at how easily Lopen has Sakie just where he wants him...its like taking candy from a baby! **Lopen takes his attention of Sakie and gets into a verbal altercation with a few fans at ringside, Sakie utlilizes the opportunity and springs off the ropes showcasing his agility and speed by flinging himself through the top and middle ropes catching Lopen with a suicide dive just as he turns around! Lopen falls hard, the back of his head hitting the steel barricade! Sakie rolls over and picks himself up to one knee, the move obviously effecting him as well. Lopen rolls over on his side, his hands across the back of his head from where he landed. Sakie is the first to both feet and he returns right back to a full assault, grabbing a hold of Lopen's hair turning him back towards the apron and rolling him into the ring. Sakie looks to the crowd and signals for the top rope turnbuckle as he hops back on to the apron and leaps to the top turnbuckle, Lopen is stretched out on the mat, belly up. Sakie leaps off the top with a guillotine leg drop, but he misses! The savvy Lopen rolls out of the way, showing he wasn't quite as hurt as led to believe. He quickly gets to his feet and takes advantage of a downed sakie by executing a fist drop square on to his forehead. Lopen picks him up and twists him around with an inverted face lock, he thrusts downard driving Sakie's head right into the edge of his knee. (think Christian) and Sakie bounces off and hits the mat with a thud. **Lopen goes for the pin on the fallen Sakie and hooks the leg. 1...2..NO! Sakie puts up a shoulder! Lopen slaps the mat in frustration and begins to pummel Sakie with a flury of rights and lefts before getting back up to his feet and spinning around to pose for the fans as they boos him heavily. Sakie rolls off to his side as Lopen turns around grabbing a hold of one of his arms, spinning a leg around and converting Sakie into a mahistrol cradle pinning maneveur! 1...2 NO! Sakie still kicks out and Lopen gets up furious, stomping the mat, he leans down to grab Sakie by the hair and pull him to his feet but Sakie shows his determination driving the base of his head into the midsection of Lopen rising up to nail him with a hard right, Lopen attempts a right of his own but he's blocked as Sakie levels him with yet another right, a left jab, a right hook...Lopen goes falling backwards into the ropes...Sakie crouches and waits for him to return, hooking him up for the Sakbottom!! **Silent Bob comes running from the back and towards the ring to a massive pop!! Lopen begins to respond with hard elbows of his own getting out of the Sakbottom and pushing Sakie back towards middle ring, Silent Bob disregards any warning given by the Mexican as he slides past and barrels his way through the attempt to block by the ref. Lopen has his back turned and is focused on Sakie as Bob sneaks up right behind him and immediatly applies the skid mark chicken wing!!! Lopen gasps in shock and goes down with Bob as he cranks on the hold refusing to let go. Mexican calls for the bell! Winner via DQ: Lopen **Silent Bob continues to drain the life out of Lopen refusing to let go of the hold, once again HBK drops his headset and runs into the ring, Sakie now over the confusion goes to stop HBK from advancing into the ring with a rushing lariat but HBK ducks right under it and spins around sending a sweet chin music square into Sakie's face sending him out of the ring!! HBK then focuses his attention back towards Bob who has already broken the hold, he and HBK stare down as a crumpled Lopen struggles to grasp for air, rolling quickly of the ring and collapsing on to the floor below. HBK grins and asks for a mic as one is tossed to him HBK: Go ahead and just try Bob...I'm the commish and you can't hit me! **Bob doesn't give HBK a chance to say anymore as he nails him with a right knocking the mic out of his hand, Bob goes for the skid mark chicken wing but Lopen crawls back in grabbing a hold of his heel and sweeping him off his feet dragging him out of the ring, the two men begin to brawl down the ramp as HBK sighs in relief, fixing his tie once more. Burton: Hey what's this?! It looks like Santa Claus is entering the ring.... **Santa has climbed up the ring steps with his bag of toys slung over his shoulder and he enters the ring with a jolly smile on his face, HBK has his attention focused on the recovering Sakie getting to his feet on the outside. Just then Santa Claus swings his bag of toys around nailing HBK straight across the back to a MASSIVE pop!! Sakie slides into the ring and picks HBK up delivering the Sakbottom, while Chase Wilson runs down and also dives into the ring...both men begin to celebrate as Santa removes his hat and beard to reveal..... FATBADGER!!! Burton: Holy crap!! Its Fatbadger!! Fatbadger has returned to XUW...he is with the Hurristoners?! **They answer Burton's question as all three of them raise each other's hands and do a mini bow. Celebration is far from over as the Riot Squad comes running down the ramp and into the ring meeting both Chase and Sakie as they begin to brawl, Fatbadger meets a slap to the face from Kat, but he responds by grabbing her by the hair and tossing her out of the ring, Badge then turns to help Chase out with Hodgo, the action spills fully out of the ring and heads to the back as the fans are going wild. HBK is left stammering to his feet spinning around in a daze, the crowd gets up and begins to look around as Diablos once again comes running through the crowd!! He hops the barricade and slips into the ring just waiting like a panther for HBK to turn around, HBK stumbles and spins around still trying to make sense where he is when D delivers a huge spear knocking him out cold!! The crowd pops HUGE and D stands over the motionless HBK with no expression. Burton: Fans what can I say?! I'm as speechless as you are! We saw the return of the Badger, D leveling HBK, Sakie retaining his title! What a night.....till next week this is Burton signing off!!! *End show and credits roll with D standing over HBK being the last image* Back to the present, as the announcer announces the next match... TV Title Match: BC vs Destroyer Destroyer runs to the ring first, then BC makes his entrance, still flashing the sign on the defunct Horsemen as he makes his way down to the ring. He climbs in the ring as the bells sounds. BC charges Destroyer, landing hard punches that sends him back to the ropes. The ref calls for a break, which he gets, and the two lock up. BC shoves Destroyer to the mat and follows with a quick elbow drop. He covers... 1... Kickout, with authority! BC rolls to the side as Destroyer gets to his feet. Destroyer bounces off the ropes and BC tries for a clothesline, which Destroyer ducks. BC turns and hits a drop toehold as Destroyer comes back off the ropes. Destroyer hits face first as BC locks on a front headlock. He pulls Destroyer to his feet and slams him to the mat, then goes to climb the corner. He looks to the crowd and back towards the entrance and sees, standing there in a gWo shirt, Admiral John. BC jumps to the mat and walks to the ropes, pointing to AJ and yelling something to him. AJ slowly walks to the ring, then jumps to the apron. He’s nose to nose with AJ as the two yell back and forth to each other and finally AJ slaps BC in the face. AJ jumps down to the floor as BC grabs for him. He continues to yell at AJ as Destroyer quietly sneaks up behind him and rolls him into a schoolboy... 1... 2... 3! Winner By Pinfall and NEW TV Champion: Destroyer BC seems totally oblivious to his loss as the ref hands Destroyer the belt. BC begins to run to the back after a departed AJ as Destroyer celebrates in the ring. On the Ultitron, we see Professor Ric Austin watching a tape of Saij’s match from Vortex. He makes notes on a clipboard and shakes his head occasionaly, at one point sayin “Saij, what were you thinking?” Back at the ring, the ring announcer calls for the next match... IC Tournament Match: Woof vs Sniper Woof makes his way to the ring first, then Sniper. The two meet in the center as the bell sounds and the two lock up. Woof Woof shoves Sniper to the mat and Sniper does a back roll and lands lightly on his feet.  Sniper charges him and hits a hard clothesline, knocking Woof to the mat. Sniper pulls Woof to his feet and hits a standing suplex. Woof lands hard and doesn’t move. Sniper comes off the ropes and hits a hard legdrop, then goes for another, but this time Woof moves out of the way as Sniper lands hard. Woof slowly gets to his feet and hits a clothesline of his own. He covers... 1... Kickout! Woof kicks out hard and springs to his feet, again charging and hitting a clothesline and knocking Sniper to the mat. He pulls Sniper to his feet and locks in on him from behind, hitting a hard german suplex. He pulls him again to his feet and hits a DDT, burying him headfirst. He covers... 1... 2... 3! Winner By Pinfall and advancing to face either Bonecrusher or Kata: Woof Woof The crowd is apparently getting restless as the announcer calls for the main event of the evening... Intercontinental Tournament Match: NFM vs Syco NFM makes his way to the ring first and is followed shortly by Syco. The two stand at opposite corners as the bell sounds and the match gets underway. The two slowly circle each other then, as seems to be the ‘in’ thing to do this evening, lock in the center of the ring. The two break the hold after a time and stare at each other. Syco holds his hand up, calling for a strength test against NFM. NFM slowly reaches for one hand and locks a grip, then before he can lock his second hand he turns the hold into a reverse wrist lock, then into a reverse hammerlock. Syco throws an elbow and hits NFM squarely in the head and NFM loses his grip enough for Syco to break lose and come off the ropes. NFM bends over to try for a backdrop and Syco flips over his back. As NFM stands and turns Syco tries for a superkick but NFM drops and kicks Syco’s other leg from under him, sending him to his back. NFM covers... 1... 2... Kickout! NFM pulls Syco to his feet and puts him in a front facelock but Scyo powers out, shoving NFM to the ropes and dropkicking him to the outside. As the ref counts, Syco climbs the corner and as NFM gets to his feet he jumps and hits a double axe handle ala’ Macho Man and again knocks him down. Syco pulls him to his feet and rolls him into the ring. He follows and covers... 1... 2... Weak Kickout! NFM is looking bad as Syco pulls him to his feet. Syco sets NFM up for a vertical suplex but NFM blocks, then rolls Syco up in a small package... 1... 2... Kickouot! Syco pulls NFM up by his hair and pulls back for a punch, but NFM blocks and pulls Syco into a shortarm clothesline. He pulls Syco to his feet and sets him up for a Lightning Strike (Tombstone). He buries Syco into the mat and covers.. 1... 2... 3! Winner By Pinfall and advancing to face either Numark or Kore: NFM The show ends as the well satisfied fans file out...
