XUW Liberation A shot of the XUW arena outside is shown in Sydney, Australia.  The people are lined up around the arena and many of the people have on some of the new XUW merchandise and waving their favorite signs. Video package is shown of some of the recent feuds of XUW: The paths of a few aren’t as defined as others Bailey is seen trying out different partners and grabbing the popcorn vendor on Mayhem. A shot of the Eliminators trying to find harmony during training. Some are destined to become greater than their peers Scenes of Syco, Hodgo, and BC are shown training for the hardcore match. Some have been chosen to be great and must prove it A shot of the steel cage is shown spinning as overlays of Dev, Bonecrusher and Bolling are seen looking up at the sky in different ways. At times good meets evil in what proves to be an epic battle A shot of Kevil paying off Sev and DJ is shown, and shots of The Apostle Chris Blake getting some retribution is shown. Those who are bitter enemies by fate must face each other in a classic struggle. A shot of X Factor winning the IC title, and having a match with Fatbadger as the special referee is shown.  A shot of Fatbadger’s return and a few of his antics involving X Factor are shown. The times of retribution sometimes lead to a clash of egos Shots of wins of both GWO members and Horsemen members are seen. Torment from Wiz and company on many different occasions and retribution of the Horsemen. To the victor goes the spoil Shots of DDT and Henrik are superimposed over a shot of the submission title in the background. But spoils are not always as they seem Shot of the Sooner’s press conference, and their trip to Australia. Shot of Blake, and his quest to defeat two teams at once. Shot of the Deadly Combination preparing for their match.  Then a shot of the titles are shown in the background with all five faces over the front. Sometimes it is all a dream. A shot is shown of Kat hitting Numark and Sidhe’s battles with Bob.  A shot of Bob throwing the title in the trashcan and then telling the Deadly Combination it was all his plan.  A shot of the title is shown with an overlay of both men in front. The video package fades out and is replaced by a dark screen and silence. Suddenly in the darkness a flicker can be seen on the Ultitron. “Main Offender” by the Hives hits the speakers and the Ultitron lights up completely. The house lights go on and the fireworks blaze from the lighting rig down to the entrance ramp. Four loud explosions are heard and seen, and the stage pyro goes off signalling xUw Liberation is now on it’s way live from Sydney, Australia.  The camera pans the crowd and sees signs such as “I sold my kids to get here”, “HA”, “PTP has Skank Mart beat”, “I luv KAT”, and “Bob, have you seen my belt?”.
