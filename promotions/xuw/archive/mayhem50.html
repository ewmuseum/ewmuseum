XUW Mayhem The scene opens up with a black screen and white bolded lettering spelling out "Happy 50th XUW Mayhem!". Metallica's "The Memory Remains" begins to play as various highlight packages begin to play. The original UCW logo and credits is shown, as is some of the original figures in the federation including Fluid. The logo crashes out of the picture and is replaced with the earlier XUW logo, with the figure heads of Blitzwing, Diablos and HBK being shown. That logo crashes out of the picture also and is replaced with the current XUW logo and Admiral John and Mister Webster are shown. Suddenly the picture cuts to highlights of in-ring action, starting with forgotten superstars like Toto, Jay, Raj Patel, Takerrocks, Rob the Throb and the Lurchador. More footage is shown of some more well known superstars who have left XUW in the past, including Flashtastic E&C, former World champions Lopen, LanceStormDBC and Mikey, and most recently, Naron, CK and the Comedian Chris Blake. As the picture and music slowly fade out, the chorus blares up again and as quick as the picture faded out, it fades back in with pictures of the current roster. Newcomers like the Professor and Saij, as well as long standing wrestlers like Bailey, Kore, NFM, Sheri Dedmon, Numark and M V Pete, the Anarchist, BoneCrusher, Sakie, Henrik, The Ripper, X-Factor, Samurai Bob, the returning HBK and then the scenes fade into a shot of Blake Wolfson and Sidhe Nimh staring down each other before tonight's Iron Man Challenge. The screen slowly fades to black with the bolded white lettering once more saying: "XUW - Attitude. Belief. Desire!" BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The pyro explodes as the live feed cuts to the XUW arena where the crowd are hyped up for what's set to be an action packed edition of Mayhem, even more so than usual! Generic rock music plays over the arena as the pyro stops exploding and the fans let out a loud chorus of cheers! The camera pans around the arena to reveal such signs as "Hey Mom, Huzzah!", "The Anarchist loves the Queen", and "The Ripper fears NFM". The camera flies around to ringside where the Atomic Tomato and the Majestical One Barry Johnson are waiting!
