XUW Mayhem #48

19 Aug 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! ...Goes the sound of the pyro exploding in the XUW arena as some generic rock music begins to play through the loudspeakers as the fans get up on their feet and scream for what is sure to be a huge night of XUW action. The camera pans around the arena to show such fan signs as "I love Sheri", "I love Sheri", "I love Sheri", "I love Sheri" and surprisingly enough, "I love Sheri". The cameras then cut to the announce position where the Atomic Tomato and Barry Johnson are awaiting our company! AT: Greetings fellow squared circle fanatics! Welcome to another jam-packed night of XUW action! Barry, welcome. BJ: The pleasure is all yours Tomato. Tonight we will witness one of the biggest Mayhem main events in recent history, when the XUW Heavyweight Champ, Blake Wolfson, will fight for survival when he takes on the entire Army of Darkness in one match - Jack the Ripper, Sidhe Nimh and DDT. Never before has one man like Wolfson shown so much... AT: ...stupidity? BJ: Yep. I know he's the World Champ for a reason, but he's got to be kidding himself if he thinks he can beat the AoD by himself. AT: Stranger things have happened. But remember that there is a much more important stipulation at stake in this match, if one of the AoD members gets to pin Blake or make him submit, then that person will become the automatic #1 contender to Blake's Title. And if somehow Blake if able to overcome all the odds and defeat the AoD, then no member of the AoD will be able to get a Title shot whilst Blake's still the champion. Confusing enough for ya? BJ: Not only do we have that big main event Tomato, but we also have what is set to be a huge match-up when Raven X takes on M V Pete. AT: For sure. And you'd have to think that the winner of this match would be in line for a shot at the current Submission champion, DDT.  Both men have bad blood with not only each other, but also DDT, so the outcome should prove to be interesting. BJ: And what about the huge 4-corners match involving Kata, Chase Wilson, Danyon and Supa Thug Esco. AT: Usually these 4 men as of late are more suited towards tag team competition, but they'll have to adjust to being in the ring alone tonight. Their teammates will still be at ringside, but won't be allowed to have any bearing on the match. BJ: We also have a huge street fight set for tonight, when past teammates Michael Bolling and BoneCrusher lock it up. AT: Besides a cage match, you won't be able to find any match more brutal in this industry than the street fight. No rules, no regulations, and plenty of weapons. Both of these men have bad blood and a score to settle, and what better way than beating the hell out of each other in a no DQ environment! BJ: But first up Tomato, we have the customary TV Title match, pitting the current champion Kat, against the equally dangerous Dev. AT: Ah yes, who is able to forget last Thursday night's TV Title match when Kat pinned Sidhe in a...well, unusual position. BJ: You didn't hear him complaining... AT: But tonight Kat will face a very tough challenge in Dev, who is known as somewhat of a Submission expert. We all know Kat's tough, but can she take down Dev? BJ: Enough of the small talk, let's head up to the ring to find out! XUW TELEVISION TITLE MATCH: KAT © vs. DEV "Build Fear" blasts over the loudspeakers as out comes the challenger, Dev, to a loud chorus of boos. He slowly makes his way down to the ring and stretches on the ropes, awaiting his opponent. "Big Gun" then begins to echo throughout the arena as out comes Kat to a huge pop, as she blows a kiss to the crowd at the top of the ramp. She runs down and slides into the ring, hands the referee the TV Title, and the contest gets underway. The pair lock up in the center of the ring, and Dev takes the power advantage by shoving Kat to the mat, as a wry smile surfaces across his face. Kat gets back up to her feet and they lock up again. This time Dev takes Kat in a headlock, whips her to the ropes and hits her with a shoulderblock. He runs to the adjacent ropes and follows it up with a clothesline, and then another clothesline. He picks Kat up and delivers a snap suplex, and goes for the cover, but only gets a 2 count. AT: It looks like Dev's intentions are clear early Barry, he's trying to win this one from the start. BJ: You mean as opposed to not wanting to win at all? AT: Shut up. Dev whips Kat into the corner and follows it up with a vicious clothesline at the turnbuckles. Dev climbs up on the second turnbuckle and poses, as the crowd heat escalates. As he's doing so, Kat gets back to her feet and dropkicks Dev in the keister, sending him over the top rope and to the floor, as the crowd lets out a large pop. Kat follows Dev to the outside and unleashes a couple of chops to his chest, whilst he's leaning against the security barrier. Dev reverses one of Kat's chops and manages to whip her into the ring steps, making a loud thud as he does so. He picks Kat up and rolls her into the ring. Dev gets back in the ring, picks up Kat and whips her to the ropes, nailing her with a spinning heel kick as she rebounds back. Dev manages to pick Kat up and applies an abdominal stretch in the middle of the ring. Whilst the referee is checking on Kat to see if she's giving up, Dev reaches behind him and grabs the ring ropes, to apply more pressure. The crowd screams out to try and get the referee's attention, and as he stands up to check the other side, Dev lets go of the ropes. The referee goes back to check on Kat and Dev grabs the ropes once more. This time the referee sees it and Dev refuses to let go, so the referee kicks Dev's hand away and Kat uses the momentum to hit a hiptoss on Dev. Both are down on the mat as the referee begins his count. AT: Good move by the referee! BJ: What are you talking about? Kat should be disqualified! AT: Because of interference from a referee? BJ: Exactly! Both men...err...humans get back to their feet and begin to exchange fists. Dev starts to get the upperhand with his power, but Kat levels the score by kicking Dev in the 'you know what'. Dev falls to the mat in pain, grabbing his crotch. Kat then is able to hit a handspring elbow and goes for the cover. 1... 2... KICKOUT! Kat picks up Dev and using amazing strength for a 112lb'er, she picks up Dev and nails him with the Kat's Kradle (Brainbuster). She signals for the end as the crowd lets out a huge pop. She climbs to the top rope and jumps over for a flipping leg drop, but Dev sits up in the nick of time and Kat hits the mat with force. Dev slowly gets to his feet and whips Kat to the corner, where he unleashes with a vicious flurry of kick to all around Kat's body. He then proceeds to take Kat down to the mat with a Dragon Screw Legwhip, and follows it up with an elbow drop to the inside of Kat's thigh. Dev then locks in a modified STF, but it's near the ropes, and after a few attempts to do so, Kat is able to struggle free and make it to the ropes. AT: What a match thus far! Both Kat and Dev aren't giving up, and Kat's doing remarkably well in there. BJ: What do you expect? I wouldn't want to meet her in a dark alley. AT: Why, because she's not a male prostitute? BJ: Exac...HEY! Dev signals for the end and picks up Kat for a Fireman's carry. He tries to go for the Devolution (Michinoku Driver) but Kat is able to escape around the back, and as Dev turns around, Kat nails him with a kick to the midsection and follows it up with a big time DDT. As Kat gets back to her feet, Sidhe runs down and into the ring. Kat turns around and starts to talk to Sidhe. AT: I wonder what Sidhe's doing here? BJ: Gee, I wonder. AT: Yeah but Kat doesn't look like she wants Sidhe's help. As the pair are discussing the situation, "I Stand Alone" bursts through the arena as Sidhe and Kat quickly turn their attention to the entranceway as the crowd heat escalates. After a minute however, the music stops playing and Wolfson is nowhere to be found. Sidhe keeps his attention on the entranceway, and as he does so, Dev quickly rolls up Kat from behind as the referee makes the count. 1... Sidhe turns around. 2... Sidhe makes a dive to break up the pin. 3!!! Too late! WINNER AND NEW XUW TELEVISION CHAMPION: DEV!!! AT: We have a new Television champion, and Blake Wolfson has cost Kat the match! BJ: He didn't interfere though! He outsmarted the AoD yet again. AT: But give full credit to Dev, he looked great in that match and managed to dominate Kat for a majority of the match. Kat tried her best, but in the end Dev was too strong. BJ: But now we head to an alleyway near this arena, as it's time for the street fight involving Michael Bolling and BoneCrusher! Let's head there now. STREET FIGHT: BONECRUSHER vs. MICHAEL BOLLING The scene opens up in the alleyway where BoneCrusher is standing, with a large piece of wood in his hand. He shouts out for Bolling to face him, but he doesn't appear. After a few moments, BoCru begins to walk past a dumpster when suddenly the lid pops open and out comes Bolling and smashes BoCru from behind with a baseball bat. Bolling jumps out of the dumpster and picks up BoCru, ramming him into a brick wall, causing BoCru to fall to the concrete. Bolling goes over to a trashcan, picks it up, and smashes it across BoCru's head. He goes for the cover, but BoCru kicks out at 2. AT: Just as we suspected Barry, this match is going to be brutal. BJ: Now this is what I call a good fight! Bolling picks up another trashcan nearby and charges at BoCru, but BoCru gets a big boot up and knocks the trashcan into Bolling's head. BoCru picks up Bolling, kicks him in the stomach and hits him with a stalling vertical suplex onto a trashcan. He goes for the cover as the referee tries to scamper around the debris and makes the cover. 1... 2... KICKOUT! BoCru picks up Bolling and procees to whip him into the brick wall a few times. As Bolling is standing against the wall, BoCru charges at Bolling who moves out of the way and BoCru crashes into the brick wall head first. Bolling capitalizes by jumping on BoCru and going for the pin. 1... 2... BoCru gets his shoulder up! AT: Ow, that hurt me just to look at it. BJ: I can't believe he just ran full speed into a fricking brick wall and he kicked out! Bolling crouches as he waits for BoCru to get to his feet, and as he does so he goes to give him a superkick, but BoCru catches his foot, spins Bolling around, picks him up for a back suplex but crotches him on the side of the dumpster! Bolling screams out in pain as BoCru picks up another piece of wood, and breaks it across Bolling's back. Bolling falls back in the dumpster, and BoCru places his head over the side of the dumpster. BoCru then proceeds to slam the door shut several times, right on Bolling's head. AT: That's it, he's dead. BJ: This is insane! Bolling manages to climb out of the dumpster, as BoCru positions himself on top of the dumpster. As soon as Bolling turns around, BoCru jumps off the dumpster and nails Bolling with a thunderous Lightning Kick. The camera pans to the far end of the alley to reveal Rujasu standing there, watching on with interest. AT: What's he doing there? BJ: Maybe he's there to get revenge on Bolling for costing him the match last week. AT: Good point, and after all, this match has no rules. Bolling climbs up on the dumpster once more and goes for the big Enforcer Elbow drop, but Bolling moves out of the way and BoCru crashes into a pile of trash. Bolling takes advantage by climbing up on the dumpster himself and hitting BoCru with a moonsault. Bolling goes for the pin as Ruj looks on concerned. 1... 2... KICKOUT! Bolling picks up BoCru for the Destiny Slam, but BoCru escapes behind Bolling and executes a big Pulling Walk slam onto the concrete! BoCru goes for the cover. 1... 2... Rujasu flies out of nowhere and nails BoCru with a 2 x 4! AT: What the hell? BJ: Why did Rujasu attack BoneCrusher?! Rujasu picks up BoCru and hits the R-Driver (Vertebreaker) on another pile of trash, as Ruj picks up the groggy Bolling and places him on top of BoCru. The referee makes the count. 1... 2... 3!!! WINNER BY PINFALL: MICHAEL BOLLING!!! AT: Damnit, this match deserved to be settled one on one, not like this! BoCru had control and was about to win the match until Rujasu interfered. BJ: Looks like Ruj and Bolling have formed a handy little alliance here. AT: Anyway fans, it's now time to head backstage, let's get a camera back there! Ask yer Mom! BWAHAHAHAHA! Kata picks Danyon up and goes for a neckbreaker, but Danyon reverses through the momentum and hits a neckbreaker of his own!
