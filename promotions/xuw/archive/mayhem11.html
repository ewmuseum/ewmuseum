XUW Mayhem #11

20 Nov 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem We are two weeks away from December to Dismember, and we are LIVE from the Flashtastic E+C arena! AT: Hello everyone, good ‘ol AT here, with always is Mr. Mikey, and we got one helluva show for you tonight, including a main event so secret that the owner Blitzwing doesn’t even know about it. MM: Yea, what the hell is that? Why doesn’t anyone know what the main event is!? “Bodies” hits and D26 walks to the ring with two folding chairs. He sits at ringside on one chair with the other in his hand. He grabs the Ring announcer’s mic. D: Tonight, there will be NO interference in ANY match, do you all understand that? If anyone comes out here to interfere, they can expect some MAJOR PAIN! Our first match begins as KoRe walks to the ring to a chorus of boos, and out walks henrik the King, and he stops on the top of the ramp. Henrik: Allow me to introduce my new valet….NARON! Big Crowd pop as Naron and Henrik walk to the ring and we get underway Henrik and KoRe lock up and Henrik throws KoRe into the ropes and hits a lariat. He whips KoRe in again and this time he hits a big back body drop. Henrik turns and he goes to mat work on KoRe, locking on a half crab. KoRe struggles and gets to the ropes, and Henrik breaks the hold. Henrik begins to get in shooter’s face for making him break when KoRe hits Henrik from behind. He begins to just hit henrik and beat him down. KoRe gets on the top rope and hits a big splash on henrik for 1…2…NO! henrik kicks out and KoRe is amazed. He picks Henrik up and puts him in a sleeper hold, but Henrik drops to his Knees and pulls KoRe’s head down, almost breaking KoRe’s jaw on Henrik’s head. KoRe begins to call to the back and Jason tries to run out, but D waffles him with a chair. D picks Jason up and he hits a DDT on Jason on the chair outside the ring. KoRe is looking in shock, and he turns into a huge lariat. Henrik drags KoRe to the corner and gets on the top rope and hits the Kingpin for the 1…2…3! Winner: Henrik the King. AT: Holy s***! Did you see that? MM: What part…D making Jason into road kill or Henrik beating KoRe… AT: The Jason thing. Someone beating KoRe isn’t a big deal… Sakie starts to the ring to a huge pop, followed by Loose cannon and Sue Wolfe. Sue gets halfway to the ring but D stops her and sends her back. In the meantime, the match begins Loose cannon mocks sakie and challenges hurrisakie to a strength contest. They lock hands and Loose cannon begins to get the better of sakie, but sakie’s hurripowers kick in and he gets back up and begins to put down Loose cannon. Cannon then kicks sakie in the stomach, and keeps holding sakie’s hands. He turns and sakie locks his arms as Loose cannon picks up sakie and slams him to the mat. Loose cannon puts Sakie in an arm bar, but sakie gets to the ropes. Cannon gets up and as sakie is getting up, LC goes for a drop kick, but Sakie dodges it. Sakie picks up a stunned LC and begins to slug it out with him. They exchange rights until finally sakie gets the upper hand and he lays LC out with a big big left. Sakie drags LC to the corner and puts him on the top rope. Sakie gets up and hits a hurriconrana (Sp?) from the top rope. He kicks LC in the stomach and hits the Truth and justice (twist of fate) for the 1…2…3! Winner: Hurrisakie AT: What a match, and there was absolutely no interference! MM: Yea, it was amazing. It’s been a while, I guess seeing jason’s brains scrambled scared the people in back. we go backstage to see Riley standing somewhere…but where? Sakie walks through the curtan and he is jumped by Riley and beat down. Riley grabs a steel pipe and begins to beat on Sakie until security breaks it up. Blitz walks up and sees this and get’s irate! DDT walks to the ring to mixed reaction, then RTT walks down to a round of boos, with a kendo stick in hand. RTT begins by whaling on DDT with the kendo stick. He beats DDT down, then RTT dives out of the ring and lifts the ring apron to find some more goodies. RTT pulls out a guitar and goes to get back in the ring, but DDT baseball slides into the guitar and it hits RTT in the face. DDT gets out and he grabs a stop sign and hits RTT in the head with it. They begin to fight to the backstage area, with shooter and the belt in fast pursuit. DDT throws RTT into a wall, and into another wall. DDT puts RTT on a table and DDT climbs up with him. DDT sets up RTT for a piledriver, but RTT backflips DDT off the table to the concrete floor. RTT grabs a pipe and begins to hit DDT. He picks DDT up and throws him through a window to the outside of the arena. RTT climbs through the broken window and picks up the bloody DDT. He goes to throw DDT into the production truck, but DDT stops it and reverses it. DDT then turns, and he is hit by A CAR! CK gets out of the car and begins to laugh as RTT goes for the pin…1…2…NO! Somehow DDT kicked out! CK is furious and he is about to begin to stomp on DDT when out of nowhere KsC jumps him and beats CK down. Meanwhile RTT is arguing with shooter and DDT rolls up RTT from behind for the 1…2…3! Winner And STILL hardcore champ: DDT AT: jesus H. Tapdancing Christ…he got hit with a  car and got up! MM: DDT is truly hardcore… HBK walks to the ring and sneers at D sitting at ringside. AJ walks down too, to a round of pops. HBK begins with a baseball slide to AJ as he tries to get into the ring. HBK then does a suicide dive onto AJ on the outside. HBK gets up and throws AK into the ring. He begins to go to work on an already woozy AJ. He whips Aj into the corner and follows him in trying to hit a shoulder to the gut, but AJ moves and HBK hits the pole. AJ Pulls HBK out and he starts to work on the shoulder. AJ picks HBK up and hits a shoulder breaker on the IC champ. AJ grabs HBK’s arm and jumps over the top rope, pulling HBK’s arm with him and almost dislocating it. AJ gets back in and he picks HBK up and hits a body slam. AJ climbs to the second rope and goes for a leg drop, but HBK moves. HBK tries to hit AJ with his injured left arm, but it has little affect, so he uses his right. He hits AJ and whips him into the ropes. HBK hits a lariat on AJ with his left arm, but it hurts HBK too, and they both go down. HBK rolls out of the ring and tries to grab a chair, but D stops him. D grabs the chair away from HBK and the two exchange words. HBK begins to mock D and tells D to hit him. D refuses and sits down, and HBK turns around and he is hit with a suicide dive from big AJ. AJ throws HBK into the ring and he goes for the anchor’s away…1…2…NO! HBK kicks out showing his resilience. AJ picks HBK up and goes for another one, but HBK drops to his knees and hits a low blow on AJ, which shooter misses. When john gets up he turns into a superkick from HBK for the 1…2…3! Winner and still IC champion: HBK AT: D looks pist off right now! MM: What gave you that impression? AT: Look at his eyes! MM: He is wearing sunglasses, moron! AT: SO! He is mad, I can tell… We go backstage where the flashtastics are talking to bob about the match tonight. Flash: Who is his partner? Flex: jobbie? Flash: I hope so, I mean that guy lost to KoRe! (Both laugh, as bob walks in and they immediately stop) Bob: Just go win your match, then I’ll win mine, and Titans Inc. will PROVE we are the best in this federation. Flash: I love him when he is so dominant! Flash and flex walk to the ring to mild cheers, but more confusion than anything. Blake walks to the ring as well, with no partner! Flash and Flex take advantage by beating blake down when all the lights  go out. When they turn on, Flash and Flex are both laid out and Blake is on top of Flash…1…2…3! Winner: Blake and ????? Blake gets a mic Blake: Haahaaa, my plan worked perfectly…meet my partner…s! From under the ring Woof woof and Sheri Dedmon slide into the ring. They smile and raise their each other’s hands. AT: So his partner was Woof woof? MM: No, it was sherri… AT: Woof MM: Sherri! Spanish jobber: Naron! They both turn and look at Spanish jobber and begin to beat him down. They each grab a chair and do a conchairto on Spanish Jobber, who is out cold…D gets up to deal with it, but then just laughs and sits down. By the time this is all over the three tag teams are already in the ring and ready to go, but D has a mic… D: This match will have new rules…To win you must be on the team that is left. If one man get’s pinned, then the team is not eliminated. To win you must me the last man in the ring, and your team will win. I personally will escort the pinned man to the back to see that he doesn’t screw anyone over…we WILL have an undisputed tag team champion after tonight. Oh yea, no tags are necessary; this will be an all out brawl, NO RULES! Jack and CK jump BD and they beat him down. KsC begin the beat down on Tony too. J and CK each grab chairs and begin to go to work CK goes to slam it down on BD, but KsC takes the chair and waffles CK, who falls down out of the ring. BD gets back up and he grabs jack and throws him into the corner. BD grabs KsC and throws him into Jack. Tony gets the upperhand on BC and throws him out of the ring, then he grabs BD and whips him into the corner and he smashes KsC and Jack, who both fall out of the ring. The only men left in are 3XL. On the outside, both teams regain themselves. They dive into the ring and an all out brawl ensues. Somehow the army gets split up and they are getting beat by two men each. 3XL is taking care of Jack while the Technicians are taking care of CK. The technicians whip Ck into the ropes and they hit a 3-D on him! KsC goes for the pin and shooter counts…1…2…3! (CK is eliminated). 3XL grabs the chairs and they swing them at jack, but he ducks and they get stingers on their hands from the chairs colliding. KsC and BC jump a stunned Tony and they throw him out of the ring. Now three men team up on BD. They all begin to just beat him down, but BD goes nuts and throws them all off. He grabs KsC by the neck but Jack gave him a massive low blow. KsC and BC give BD a double DDT. Jack jumps off the top rope and he hits a big splash onto BD and Shooter counts…1…2…3! (BD is eliminated). Meanwhile tony rolls into the ring and he grabs a celebrating jack and rolls him up for the 1…2…3! (Jack is eliminated). AT: No matter what now we have NEW tag champions! MM: Good observation…NOW BRING OUT THE PUPPIES! AT: What? MM: Sorry, Lawler came back…so I am doing my Lawler gimmick now… AT: Sheesh… (Meanwhile in the ring…) KsC and BC both run at the only man left in their way, Tony. Tony ducks the double lariat and he kicks BC in the stomach and he hits a DDT. He throws BC out of the ring, and turns into a massive lariat from KsC. BC rolls into the ring and they throw tony to the ropes and they hit a double back drop. They set up the execution. BC goes for the nail in the coffin but Tony reverses it and KsC does a headbutt onto the mat. Tony rolls over and goes for the pin on KsC…1…2…3! (KsC is eliminated). BC gets into the ring and he spears Tony as he is getting up and he goes for the pin…1…2…3! Winner and NEW tag team champions: The Technicians AT: Holy crap! That was nuts! I can’t believe that happened? We have new tag team champions! MM: Puppies! AT: Will you shut up! D gets into the ring with a mic Well, it is time for me to make an announcement. You see, as commissioner I have some contractual obligations, which include crap like playing fair, not cheating, and not granting myself title shots at every turn. Well, I have been a busy man lately. First thing I did was write myself a new contract…as only a wrestler…but it also reserves me the right to take back the commissionership at any time before the PPV. All this means is Blitz…you are on your own for running things. If people wanna hurt each other…I DON’T GIVE A DAMN ANY MORE! Don’t think that I didn’t make a main event for tonight first though! Severson and Bob, come out here! Severson walks out to a chorus of boos, while Bob walks out to the biggest pop of the night D: Tonight, your match will be for the #1 contender to the world title! BUT…There will be two other men in there…two men who I feel also deserve this title shot! Lopen and Kata, get out here now! Lopen walks out to massive boos, then kata to a mild pop D: Let this match begin! 4-way for the #1 contender to the world title… Kata and Sev pair off, as do lopen and Bob. Kata and sev lock up and Kata whips sev into the corner. Kata follows him in with a lariat. Kata grabs Sev and moves him out, then he sits on the top rope and he hits a tornado DDT from the top rope. Meanwhile, Bob and Lopen are beating each other down in the ring. Bob picks up Lopen for a body slam. Bob goes off the ropes and he hits a leg drop. Bob picks Lopen up and he throws him out of the ring and they begin to just fight outside. Shooter tries to get them back in the ring, but they refuse. D tries to break it up, but they just push him out of the way. Lopen turns and gives D a Lopen Cutter, knocking him completely out. Bob and Lopen fight all the way to the back. AT: Bob and Lopen left! MM: Wow! That was nuts! AT: I’d say so! They hate each other! Meanwhile, Severson got the upper hand in the ring and he throws kata into the ring. Out of nowhere, Lance runs to the ring in a hospital gown and he nails Severson from behind with a kendo stick. He rolls Severson into the ring, but Kata saw the whole thing and refuses to cover. When Severson gets up, Kata and he lock up. Kata puts Sev in a hammerlock, which sev reverses. They break up and lock up again. Sev throws kata into the corner. Sev follows him in and goes for a shoulder to the midsection, but kata lifts himself out of the way and hooks his legs under Sev’s arms and turns it into a pin…Bob and Lopen rush back to the ring to stop it…1…2….3! Winner and # 1 contender: Kata Lopen and Bob get into another battle and they are fighting as we… Fade to Black…
