XUW Mayhem *Video Package* Woof kicks out hard and springs to his feet, again charging and hitting a clothesline and knocking Sniper to the mat. He buries Syco into the mat and covers.. 1... 2... 3! The screen goes black then shows a video package of XUW stars and other random events as some undecipherable rock music plays.  The screen then emblazes with fire and goes to a shot of the sold out crowd in the arena.  The pyro next to the stage goes off and fireworks shoot from the lighting rig over the ring down to the ramp and back up again.  The camera pans the crowd and shows the enthusiasm from the people.  Signs such as “Hurrisakie is MY hero”, “Ripsaw me Jack”, and “The TV title is MINe” are shown.  The camera switches to the announce table to show Atomic Tomato and Barry “The Majestic” Johnson putting on their headsets in preparation for the show. Barry “The Majestic” Johnson: I thought you would never stop talking, Avacado.  I’m doing fine by the way, but not only do we have the IC tourney matches we also have Destroyer taking on Rujasu for the TV title.  Jack takes on CK in what looks to be a good match, and the GWO tries to iron out it’s problems as Hurrisakie and Esco take on Bailey and Danyon.  This looks to be a good card going into Road Rage 2.  I for one can’t wait, how about you Nuclear Lettuce? Destroyer vs Rujasu  **TV Title match** ”Generic Rock Music” plays as Rujasu makes his way onto the stage.  He looks around at the booing crowd before making his way down to the ring.  He slides into the ring and heads straight for the corner to pose. AT: It’s safe to say that Ruj isn’t winning any brownie points with this crowd tonight.  He does look confident though. BJ: Yeah and that confidence is most likely what will carry him through this match.  He’s got skills so this match shouldn’t be too much for him. AT: Well we’ll see about that. BJ: Well this should be good.  Both of these guys have been doing a lot of talking, but who can be the winner?  I got my money on Rujasu. Rujasu gains the upperhand and lands a knee lift to the midsection of D.  He then follows up with a snap suplex and poses to the crowd. AT: Ruj is a bit confident.  It may be the downfall for him in this match though.  D is not a light competitor. BJ: Yeah he weighs like 215. BJ: Ruj is playing it smart during this match.  He’s trying to use every advantage he can against D to gain the win. AT: Well, the match is still early and this is just a little impatience by D. Nice dropkick by Ruj to the jaw of D.  He goes down hard and Ruj looks to be making his move now. BJ: Yeah, he’s going for the cover but only gets a two count.  The ref has to be counting slow. Ruj complains to the ref for a second about the count then picks up D and whips him to the corner.  He charges in for an elbow strike but misses as D hits a neckbreaker to send him down hard.  D bounces off the ropes and hits a huge leg drop to the throat of Ruj and goes for the cover.  He only gets a two count as Ruj kicks out at the last second.  D picks him up and throws him to the ropes.  He drops his head and Ruj tries to reverse the move into the R-Driver. D escapes before he is turned over and attempts a pin. AT: Nice escape by Destroyer on that one.  He almost saw the end of the match coming. BJ: Yeah, that would have been the end.  D couldn’t have gotten out if Ruj was to hit the R-Driver. D picks up Ruj and delivers standing scoop slam to the mat.  He then goes to the corner and climbs to the top.  He goes for the 450 Splash and lands a little off. AT: D looks to be going for the finish now.  He’s picking Ruj up to try for The Destruction. BJ: Yeah, this doesn’t look good for Rujasu.  Wait…he reversed the move and hit a nice facebuster on D.  D is down and Ruj is straddling him for a headlock. AT: That looks more like a choke hold to me. BJ: Maybe you need new glasses, Avacado.  It’s a perfectly legit hold. The ref breaks up the hold and Ruj starts to argue with him.  At the same time, he is laying his leg over the neck of D in a blatant choke.  The ref immediately breaks it up and Ruj starts to argue again.  He starts to get in the referee’s face before turning his attention back to D.  D tries to make it to his feet as Rujasu whips him to the corner.  He follows up immediately with a few closed fist shots.  The ref catches the last one and holds his arm.  Ruj begins arguing with the ref and getting in his face.  He shoves the ref and the ref shoves him back.  D starts to get up again and Ruj lays in a few more closed fist punches before the ref stops him again. AT: Ruj is really doing a number on D now.  He’s cheating in any way that he can. BJ: I keep telling you that he’s not cheating.  He’s improvising.  Ruj wants to beat D at any way he can.  He’s a stand up guy. Ruj gets in the ref’s face once again and starts to argue again.  The ref stops him from landing another closed fist to D and Ruj pushes him down to the mat.  He whips D to the ropes once again and gives him a kick to the gut.  Ruj then lands the R-Driver and goes to cover.  The ref gets to his knees and calls for the bell.  Ruj starts to celebrate in the middle of the ring.  He goes to the outside and grabs the belt and celebrates a bit more, but the ref grabs the belt and hands it to the weary D. Winner by DQ and still TV Champion: Destroyer AT: What a close one D just pulled out.  Even after all he’s went through he’s still the TV champ. BJ: Well he only won on a technicality. ”Pass the Courvoisier pt. 2” plays as D celebrates in the ring.  The camera switches to the back where a strange beautiful dark haired lady is sitting in the back.  She has on an XUW backstage pass as she looks at the monitor and nods approvingly. AT: Who was that? AT: Jack doesn’t really look like he’s up to this match.  I wonder if he can pull it together to beat CK? BJ: Well you don’t have to wait long because CK said he’s ready.  We’re gonna find out in just a few moments. AT: CK doesn’t look to be having any problems.  He’s ready for action and is letting Jack know just what he’s getting into. BJ: Well Jack may have a surprise up his sleeve.  You never know. CK vs Jack AT: It looks as if Jack is backing off a bit from CK.  He’s just standing in the corner trying to defend himself. He lands another with the same results then CK lands an overhead belly to belly on Jack.  CK goes for the cover but only gets a two count. AT: CK seems to be a bit frustrated by the whole match. BJ: Jack needs to mount an offensive if he’s going to win.  CK whips Jack to the ropes and he lands a beautiful Chokeslam.  He’s going for the cover. Only a two count as Jack kicks out in the nick of time.  CK whips Jack to the ropes again and lands a dropkick square on the chin.  CK goes to the corner and climbs to the second rope.  He jumps down and lands an elbow to the head of Jack.  CK looks like he doesn’t know what to do as he locks on the Fujiwara Armbar.  Jack is screaming in pain as he gets to the ropes in a fair amount of time.  The referee breaks the hold and CK lays in a few boots to Jack. BJ: Jack may be in real trouble now.  He’s got to do something.  CK is just toying with him now. CK picks up Jack and lands the Crippling Effect to Jack.  He looks to go for the cover but doesn’t as he picks up Jack once again.  Jack is dazed as CK lands a tiger Driver to Jack.  He goes for the cover but Jack kicks out at 1. AT: The Ripper Just Kicked Out!!!! BJ: Now that was amazing.  With all the punishment he’s been through, that last move should have been the end.  Look now Jack just landed a kick to the midsection of CK. AT: He’s going for the Ripsaw. Jack lands the Ripsaw on CK and goes for the cover.  The referee gets in position and counts the 3!! “Blank” hits the speakers as Jack almost runs to the back.  CK is stunned in the middle of the ring as the announcer makes the official announcement. Winner by pinfall: Jack “The Ripper” Nobbeldy AT: I still can’t believe what just happened.  That was truly amazing. BJ: Well believe it, Passion Fruit.  Jack just won the match and CK is looking like he doesn’t know what happened. AT: That’s true.  But away from that we have another IC tourney match between Numark and Kore up next. *commercial* They have put in blood, sweat and tears to get to this point.  They have sacrificed everything to make it to the top.  They have weathered the test of time to become the greatest IC champ in the XUW.  This Sunday, they will have the chance to go home as the champion.  This Sunday, they have the chance to make a name for themselves.  This Sunday it all starts again.  Road Rage 2 comes to you live from Phoenix, AZ.  Live at the America West Arena.  Be sure to call your cable company or satellite provider for more information.  Tickets are available now from all Ticketmaster outlets.  Don’t miss it. Nu vs Kore [IC tourney match] AT: Welcome back folks, now it’s time for the next IC tourney match between Kore and Numark. BJ: Let’s see who gets to go on to the next round and who sits in the loser’s corner.  Personally I think Kore is going to win, but that is just my opinion. AT: When did your opinion ever count? ”Soldier” hits the speakers as Numark comes down to the ring with a determined look on his face.  He slides in the ring and poses for the crowd before hopping down and waiting for Kore to make his entrance. AT: Well Numark is ready for this, but is he really ready for the Grand Wizard on the outside also? BJ: The Wiz isn’t any trouble at all.  He’s just a manager.  He doesn’t get involved in matches. AT: Well if that’s what you want to believe. ”Greed” hits the system as Kore comes out with Grand Wizard.  They both stroll down to the ring to a chorus of boos as Nu comes over to the ropes facing the ramp.  Nu and Wiz exchange a few words before he slingshots off the ropes and lands a flying body cross to both Kore and Wiz on the outside. AT: Looks like Numark is starting off the action and wasting no time in doing it. BJ: That was just completely uncalled for.  I can’t believe he did something like that. Nu picks up Kore and slams his head into the guard rail.  He then throws Kore into the ring as Wiz finally makes it back up.  Whip to the ropes on Kore and Numark hits a vertical suplex sending Kore down hard.  Kore tries to get to his feet as Nu whips him to the ropes once again.  Kore ducks the clothesline attempt and bounces off the opposite ropes to land a flying forearm shot.  He picks up Nu and slings him to the corner and goes in for the kill. BJ: Like I said Kore isn’t going to lose.  Numark may have gotten the first shot, but Kore is going to get the last. AT: BJ, the match is just starting.  Shouldn’t you wait a bit before calling the end? BJ: I don’t like to wait.  I’m impatient, can’t you tell? Kore lands a few forearm shots to Nu in the corner before whipping him to the opposite corner.  Nu reverses and sends Kore into the corner and lands a ddt as Kore bounces off the turnbuckles.  Nu bounces off the ropes and goes to hit a legdrop, but misses as Kore rolls out of the way.  Kore locks up Nu in a headlock as the ref comes over to check on him.  Nu starts to try and get to his feet but Kore increases the pressure and the ref looks at Numark for a submission. AT: Nu is almost down for the count.  Wait the ref drops his hand and Nu stiffens up.  He’s starting to get back to his feet. BJ: Kore can’t seem to keep him down.  He’s almost to his feet now. Numark makes it back to his feet and lands two elbow shots to Kore’s midsection.  Nu bounces off the ropes and hits Kore with a shoulder block.  He whips off the ropes again and jumps over Kore and reverses a hiptoss with one of his own.  Kore gets up staggering and leans against the ropes.  Nu comes in with a charge, but Kore ducks and sends Nu flying to the outside to the Grand Wizard’s feet.  Kore starts to talk to the ref as Grand Wizard lays in a few hits to Numark with the wand.  Kore climbs to the outside and whips Numark into the steps.  He sets up Numark on the post and goes for a spear but misses and hits the poles.  The referee is counting on the inside and the Wiz is instructing Kore to get back into the ring before the count out. AT: Almost a double count out there.  Both men need to watch what’s going on inside the ring. BJ: Kore has it under control.  If not the Wiz is looking out for him too. AT: Both men are now back in the ring and Kore still has the advantage. Kore whips Nu to the ropes and misses with a standing dropckick.  Nu takes the opportunity to lock on an Ankle Lock.  Kore struggles to get to the ropes as Grand Wizard pulls on his hand to get him to the ropes.  The ref sees this and warns the Wiz about his interference in the matchup.  Wiz backs up as if he’s done nothing wrong.  Kore is smarting from the submission move as Numark whips him to the ropes and lands a Superkick to the jaw.  He covers for a two count as the Wiz pulls his foot on the ropes.  Numark starts to complain to the referee about the Wiz on the outside, but to no avail.  Kore grabs him from behind and hits the Obscure Reference.  He goes for the cover but only gets a 1 and a half. Time Limit Draw AT: Well it looks as if Kore and Wiz are livid. BJ: True, but…wait!! Woof is coming through the crowd!!
