HBK then bounces off the ropes and hits a hard lariat on the former tag team champion! HBK turns and tags in Kata. Mikey: Whew... HBK had been in this match for a long time, thus far. I was waiting too see how long it would take him to tag Kata back in. BJ (Yup, you guessed it): All it took was a bloody thumb to the eye... what a mess. Mikey: ...concentrate Mikey... don't kill the man... you don't need ANOTHER homicide conviction... BJ: What? Mikey: ...nothing. Back in the ring, Kata has gone to work on Blake with a few stiff kicks to the midsection. Kata backs Blake into the ropes, where on the return he goes for a hard clothesline, but Blake ducks under it. On the second return, Kata leaps over Blake. On the third time around, Kata doesn't turn around quite in time, and when he does he is hit with a vicious spear from Blake. Blake grabs Kata by the hair and picks him up to his feet. Blake then hits a quick belly to belly suplex on Kata. Blake gets to his feet again and looks frustrated at something. Blake points at HBK, then walks over to Kata and begins to choke him! Mikey: What is this all about! BJ (still in British accent): You know bloody well what it is all about! Blake and The Anarchist are upset with all the cheating that HBK has been doing, so now they are flaunting the rules. I tell you that HBK is a Neanderthal. Mikey: I need a new job. BJ: I should say so, old chap, and from where I am sitting and what I smell I dare say you need a new pair of knickers too. The Referee makes a five count to get Blake to stop choking Kata, and finally Blake relents. Blake walks over and tags in The Anarchist, who is more than eager to get into the match. The Anarchist quickly goes to work on the downed Kata with some hard rights. The Anarchist stands back and waits, holding his ribs, as Kata gets to his feet. As soon as Kata is on his feet, the Anarchist begins to hit Kata with a series of Anar-kicks of his own. First one to the right leg, then another to the left shoulder, then a third to the right shoulder, and a fourth to Kata's left leg, then a final round house kick straight to Kata's head! The Anarchist quickly goes for the cover... ONE... TWO... BROKEN UP BY HBK!!! Blake runs into the ring and gives HBK a hard lariat and sends both men out of the ring and crashing to the floor. They begin to roll around and brawl as The Anarchist gets up and is distracted by them. Meanwhile, behind him, Kata is getting to his feet.
