XUW Card Archive

XUW Mayhem #61

6 Jan 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW: Mayhem - Number 61 BOOOOOOOOOOOOOM! BOOOOOOOOOOOOM! BOOOOOOOOOOOOOM! (er Sooners) The pyrotechnics fly high into the air in as many colours as you can think of, and then some, making that Audioslave video seem positively firework-less. A HUGE capacity crowd is in attendance for the first Mayhem of the New Year, and they all cheer heartily as the indecipherable lyrics of the Mayhem theme play, and the camera swirls and swirls and swirls. "Jack steals titles like a 14 year old scouser" "Rujasu + CK = goldage" "I have Legionnaire's disease" "Kipfer is the true champion" ...are just some of the signs that litter the crowd like a giant bundle of multicoloured litter. With writing on. And stuff. Without further ado, the camera cuts to the announce position, and we have the usual boys doing their usual thing. Atomic Tomato: "Hello there ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to the first Mayhem of 2003, and when I say we have one of the most stacked Mayhems in modern memory, I'm not even joking around." Barry Johnson: "You never joke around. You're duller than a Bill DeMott v A-Train match." AT: "Who?" BJ: "Exactly." AT: "Well, that aside, we have some tantalising matches on the way here. First up will be a non-sanctioned match between The Ripper and two mystery opponents. This will have a special guest referee, and really, none of us know anything about this at all." BJ: "All I know is that The Ripper is going to win." AT: "It's possible, and I smell some sort of set-up there. This will be followed by Rujasu and Cereal Kiler trying to take the Tag Titles from the Four Horsemen. Can this unorthodox team actually upset the champions." BJ: "I'd love them to, but face it, they're jobbers." AT: "Well, Rujasu has been working very hard this week, it could be an interesting situation." BJ: "No, it could be a squash match." AT: "Talking of interesting, after that will be the first XUDF match to my knowledge to be scheduled on a Mayhem! Doughboy will defend his North American Title against the two top contenders, Darkseid and Darkseid... I mean Darkseid and The Shadow!" BJ: "Ah, forget the matches, what everyone really wants to hear is the big announcement! I hear they're going to sack some of the dead wood here, and give me a big pay rise! Woohoo!" AT: "How can you forget about the matches? After those three there are still two huge ones to go. Firstly M V Pete will continue to wage his war against the Usual Suspects as he takes on their leader, Samurai Bob. He's beaten Bob before, but will Bob have something new up his sleeve this time?" BJ: "No, Pete will kick his ass for the eighteenth time." AT: "Well, whichever way that one goes we have a real humdinger of a Main Event, as the other Suspect, Numark takes on our World Champion, Dave Kipfer!" BJ: "Kipfer's needed the break to get over his savage beating from The Ripper." AT: "Yes, and I'm sure Jack will be keeping a watchful eye on this one. I just hope they can have a fair fight with no interference at all." With that, the feed cuts to backstage, where Bailey and Danyon are standing around near a hot dog stand. Bailey: "I see they have a big announcement scheduled for tonight." Danyon: "Yeah, and you just know what that's going to be about." Bailey: "Exactly. It's going to be about the greatest tag team in XUW history, the New Royal Legion!" Danyon: "Of course it is!" Bailey: "I'm looking forwards to it. After all those months and months of injustice, Mister Webster is finally going to apologise for his conspiracies and give us back our Tag Team Titles." Danyon: "It'll be nice to hear how he says sorry for the trouble he has caused us." Bailey: "Yes. Let's celebrate with a hot dog. Brings back memories, eh?" Cut to the announce desk. AT: "Well, the New Royal Legion seem to think they know what the announcement is about. Could they actually be right?" BJ: "He said 'eh'!" "Blank" by Failure drifts through the speakers, and the crowd furrow their brows trying to remember when they last heard that music. After a few seconds, Sirius Smythe walks down the ramp and slowly, so slowly, gets into the ring. The crowd boo him for taking so long, and for being a lawyer, and a miserable swine, and a member of the AoD. Quite a bit of booing. Sirius: "Yes, thank you, thank you. I have come here to announce the special stipulations of the handicap match tonight. However, before that, let me introduce to you the holder of the XUW World Title belt, Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy!" "One Armed Scissor" hits, and Jack comes down to the ring to a barrage of heat. He enters the ring and takes his coat off, smiling at Sirius. Sirius: "Let me now introduce the special guest referee for the match... Raven X!" "Liberate" plays, and down comes Raven X to more huge heat. The latest addition to the Army of Darkness is wearing a ref's shirt, quite possibly the one he wore a December to Dismember Again. Sirius: "Now, I'm sure you're all wondering who he could be up against." More heat for Sirius. BJ: "It could be anyone!" AT: "I smell a ruse here." Sirius laughs. Sirius: "They are both former XUW Title holders, both in the tag division, and one in the Hardcore division too. The other has held the Submission Title, and both are formidable competitors." A buzz runs through the crowd, this sounds good. Sirius: "Ladies and gentlemen, I give you... Chase Wilson and Bionic Carrot!" The crowd pops, and then remembers that neither man is in too good a shape, and shuts up. BJ: "What a coup!" AT: "It can't be true." BJ: "Nice rhyme from you." AT: "Shut up." "Y.M.C.A." by the Village People hits the speakers, and out come two midgets dressed as Bionic Carrot and Chase Wilson! They pose atop the ramp, and try to get cheers from the crowd, but the crowd is having none of it and boos them mercilessly. BJ: "Look, they're just like the real thing! Other than, like, the wheelchair." AT: "Oh dear." The midgets rush to the ring, and Sirius exits it. Both little guys run at The Ripper, who laughs and kicks Mini-BC in the head. He goes down, and Jack picks up Mini-Chase with one hand, holds him above his head and hits a massive Chokeslam. Mini-Chase looks dead, and Jack picks up Mini-BC from the floor, placing him between the Ripper's... well, ankles, and hitting an enormous RipSaw onto the body of Mini-Chase. Both midgets look pretty much dead. Jack steps on them as a cover. Raven X makes the count, nice and slow. 1... ... 2... ... ... 3!!! Winner, by pinfall, Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy! BJ: "What unbiased calling by Raven X! Nobody can accuse him of any impartiality!" AT: "What a crock!" The camera cuts backstage to the Boomer Sooners locker room. The fans take time out from booing the AoD to pop huge as we see Dave and Saij watching Jack's antics on the monitor. Dave shakes his head. Dave: "..and he's the one that said I cheapen the belt..." The camera cuts to the locker room of the Usual Suspects, and we see Samurai Bob and Numark watching the pummelling of the midgets. Bob: "Ah, that's enough. Someone's gotta do something about those cranks." Numark nods, and then the camera cuts back to the announce position. AT: “Next up is the tag team match for the titles, but before that we’re going to nip backstage again for a minute.” Camera cuts backstage to where The Latch Key Kid is talking with a random stagehand. The crowd pops big, presumably for the former XUDF Superstar rather than the stagehand, though he does look pretty cool. LKK: "Ah, it's good to see the XUDF guys get on XUW television again. It'll give them another taste of the big time and make them more determined to get called up here." Stagehand: "You got a pick to win tonight?" LKK: "I've got to go with Doughboy. At least, I'll be cheering him on. He's a good friend, which is more than I can say for Darkseid, or Dark... I mean The Shadow." Stagehand: "Well, Doughboy's never been beaten before, has he?" LKK: "He's never been pinned before, that's true, but I can remember a match that he didn't quite win..." Both LKK and the stagehand laugh, and then one half of the Ultra Violents, Blake Wolfson, comes into view behind them. Wolfson: "What's so funny?" Blake walks towards LKK in a threatening manner. Wolfson: "Come on, let's spread the humour around. I love a good joke." LKK stands his ground, but motions for the stagehand to get behind him. LKK: "What do you want now?" Wolfson: "Oh, nothing much." As he says this he takes a step back, and LKK visibly relaxes... which is when the Anarchist attacks him from behind with a stiff Anar-Kick to the head. LKK stumbles forwards and Wolfson hooks him up for an Implant DDT. Hodgo grabs the legs and they execute the Double Team Spike DDT known as the Dehumanizer. LKK's head smashes into the concrete, and the Ultra Violents look down at him and laugh. Anarchist: "Now THAT is funny!" Camera cuts back to the announce desk. AT: "Why, those no good Ultra Violents! I hope somebody soon teaches them a lesson!" BJ: "Ah, come on, LKK deserved it." AT: "What?" BJ: "He was being boring. All that compassion and friendship stuff. Yuck." AT: "You sad, strange little man." BJ: "What did I do now?" Before Atomic Tomato can reply, "Whatever" by Godsmack plays, and out comes Cereal Killer. He does a few twirls on hi way to the ring, gets dizzy, and then falls over. Behind him we see Rujasu walking down to the ring with a look of quiet composure on his face. CK gets up and climbs into the ring, ascending the turnbuckle to a small crowd reaction, and Rujasu just stands on the mat, shaking his arms and legs out. AT: "This is a more focused Rujasu than I think we have seen before. We'll have to see what happens when he and CK face up to Dante and Kore!" BJ: "They're gonna get squashed. How many times?" "Bodies" by Drowning Pool is played, and out come Dante and Kore, to a decent pop. They walk down to the ring and slide under the bottom rope together. The bell rings to start the match. XUW Tag Team Title match: Rujasu/CK v The Horsemen (Dante/Kore) © AT: "It looks like CK is insisting that he starts the match in the ring with Dante." BJ: "He's going to have try to carry Rujasu here to prevent the squash." AT: "Will you be quiet." CK and Dante lock up, with the Horseman whipping CK to the ropes. On the way back CK tries for a Clothesline, but Dante easily ducks it and picks up Cereal Killer, hitting a Sidewalk Slam. Dante wastes no time in lifting CK back up and then scooping him into the Hells Bells (Vertebreaker). Dante hooks a leg. ONE... TWO... Rujasu makes the save! Dante gets up and hits a Snap Suplex on the young superstar, and then ref forces Rujasu to exit the ring once more. Dante waits in the corner and then explodes as CK gets up, and Dante hits him with the Impaler (Spear). CK is down, and Dante tags in Kore. Together they hit a Double DDT, and then Kore goes up to the top rope. He jumps off with a Moonsault to the chest of CK. AT: "The 1-800-Gambler! Kore and Dante are in control here." Kore goes for a cover. ONE... TWO... SHOULDER UP! AT: "So close!" Kore picks CK back up, but CK pushes him away and then hits a Belly-to-Belly Suplex on the champion. BJ: "Well, that made an Immediate Impact!" AT: "HAH!" CK stumbles over to the corner and dives to tag in Rujasu. The crowd pops for the newly focused superstar. Rujasu goes to work immediately with Dropkick to stagger Kore, and then a Neckbreaker to take him down. Rujasu goes up top and comes off with a Flying Knee Drop that connects hard! Rujasu hooks a leg. ONE... TWO... TH... Broken up by Dante! Dante drops an elbow to the back of Ruj's head, and buys Kore some time. The fallen Horseman tries to get up and crawls to his corner. Rujasu gets up to stop him, but Kore dives and makes the tag. Dante comes in and levels Ruj with a Clothesline. Rujasu gets back up, but Dante hits another Clothesline and back down he goes. Rujasu gets up a third time and now Dante whips him to the ropes. Ruj comes back and Dante goes for a Back Body Drop. Rujasu turns in mid-air and grabs Dante from behind, locking in and delivering the R-Driver (Vertebreaker)! AT: "Great move from Rujasu!" Rujasu covers. ONE... TWO... TH - KICKOUT! AT: "Dante with just enough energy to get out of it." Rujasu grabs Dante's legs and twists them into a Cloverleaf variant. He then turns Dante over and places him in a Lion Tamer type hold, with the legs still locked. AT: "The Philly Cloverleaf! That's Rujasu's old finisher!" BJ: "Dante himself throws a similar move, called the Devil Stretch." AT: "True, can that help him get out of this painful submission hold?" Dante edges closer and closer to the ropes, and reaches out to grab them, but Rujasu drags him backwards into the middle of the ring. Dante grows visibly weaker and weaker. AT: "Is this the end for Dante?" Kore runs in and hits Rujasu in the back of the head. Ruj releases the hold and hits a Piledriver on Kore, before kicking him out of the ring. Rujasu turns round and gets hit with an Impaler Spear from the tag champion, Dante. AT: "So quickly the momentum shifts, but I can't help thinking that Dante underestimated Rujasu here." Dante hits a few hard shots to Ruj's head, and moves him to the corner. He ascends the turnbuckle and dives off, hitting a sweet Tornado DDT. AT: "Dante's Darkside Spin there is leaving him in control of the match. He's going for a cover." ONE... TWO... SHOULDER UP! Dante looks surprised, but picks Rujasu up and sets him as if for a Powerbomb. Dante motions for the Devil Drop, but Rujasu finds the strength to hit a Back Body Drop. Both men are down, and get up at the same time. Dante makes a grab for Rujasu, but walks into the reset Button (Sidewalk Slam)! Rujasu goes to the corner and gets to the top turnbuckle. He jumps off with the High Score (Shooting Star press) and nails it perfectly! He hooks the leg. ONE... TWO... THRE - SHOULDER UP! The crowd pops huge for the near fall! AT: "It really doesn't get much closer than that!" BJ: "He's got to look for his new finisher now, the System Error." AT: "Indeed, Rujasu seems to be stalking Dante as he gets to his feet." Dante gets up and Rujasu immediately locks a Half Nelson onto him, Ruj goes to ad a Dragon Sleeper with the other arm, but Dante battles back and hits an elbow to the side of Ruj’s head. Ruj staggers off and Dante goes for a Clothesline, but Rujasu ducks and hits a Flapjack, dropping Dante throat first across the top rope. Dante clutches at his throat, and Rujasu hits a German Suplex into a pin. ONE… TWO… THR – SHOULDER UP! AT: “So close again! Who’d have thought it?” BJ: “I predicted Rujasu would win!” AT: “What?” BJ: “Hey, who is that?” Rujasu and Dante lie exhausted in the ring, their partners urging them to go for the tag. However, at the top of the ramp stand DDT and Raven X, the newest member of the Army of Darkness. They walk down the ramp and point at Kore, laughing to themselves. In the ring, Rujasu and Dante struggle to their feet. AT: “DDT and Raven X seem to be taunting Kore.” BJ: “They’re pretending to mop the floor!” Kore looks furious, and dives off the ring apron at them, but they both duck and he sails over them into the railing, landing awkwardly. Back in the ring, Rujasu and Dante are starting to exchange hard blows. AT: “It looks like Kore landed badly there, he’s clutching at his leg.” Kore is yelling out, and his leg is twisted at an unnatural angle. DDT and Raven X seem to find this very funny, and we can just about hear them. Kore: “Argh! My leg’s broken!” RX: “Does it hurt when I do this?” RX stamps on the broken leg. Kore: “AAAARRRRGGGGHHHH!!!!” DDT: “Ha.” DDT and RX pick up the fallen Kore and drag him to his corner. AT: “What are they doing? The guy’s got a broken leg?” BJ: “They’re helping him get back into the match!” AT: “He’s got a broken leg!” BJ: “Maybe he can run it off?” In the ring, Dante whips Rujasu to his corner, but Ruj reverses it and Dante is sent into the turnbuckle. RX lifts Kore up and DDT grabs his hand, slapping Dante on the shoulder. AT: “What was that?” BJ: “A tag from Kore! That’s how helpful the AoD is!” DDT and RX throw Kore over the top rope and into the ring, as Dante looks on, astonished. The AoD members drop back down to the floor and look on, smiling. Dante gets out of the ring and charges them. He hits an Impaler Spear on DDT and then unloads with hard right hands on RX. In the ring, Rujasu looks down at Kore, who is still yelling in pain and clutching at his leg. Rujasu shrugs, and goes down to pin him. Dante turns round and sees the pin being made. He rushes the ring as CK rushes to stop him. ONE… Dante gets in the ring, CK runs at him… TWO… Dante goes to break it up, CK falls over. THRE – Broken up by Dante! AT: “Oh my God! So close again!” BJ: “Here come RX and DDT, eager to help again! Such nice men.” DDT and RX storm the ring, and RX nails Dante with a kick to the gut, and then hits the Family Values (Pedigree). DDT picks him up and nails him with the Shock Treatment. RX picks Dante up one more time and hold him in the air, allowing DDT to run off the ropes and hit the Darkness Death Drop (3-D). By now the bell has been rung quite a lot. Winners by DQ, and STILL XUW Tag Team Champions: Dante and Kore!!! AT: “The Horsemen retain, but at what cost? That’s a brutal attack from the AoD!” BJ: “Rujasu looks angry at them, but he’s not going to start a fight with the two of them if he only has Cereal Killer as back-up!” The camera cuts backstage to where Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy is standing with M V Pete in the Army of Darkness locked room. Jack switches off the monitor with a smile. Pete: "Nice match you had out there. Glad to see you could manage to overcome the odds." Pete laughs. Jack: "Yes, it is a good thing that The Ripper spent the week in training, else it could have ended differently." Pete: "Training..." Jack: "The Ripper notices a cloud on your mind." Pete: "It's Numark. I beat him at December to Dismember Again. I took his title, and I was the last man standing." Jack: "That is true." Pete: "So why the hell does he get the title shot?" Jack: "The Ripper sees it as an attempt to slap the Army of Darkness in the face. Webster is frustrated that the AoD will not play his little games, nor jump through his hoops." Pete: "Dammit, Numark does not deserve that title shot. I should have one before he does, all he's done is beat jobbers for the last few months. When he fought a real challenge, he lost. How does it make sense?" Jack: "It does not, but they fight for nothing. This major announcement is sure to be Mister Webster acknowledging that he cannot defeat the Army of Darkness, and officially making The Ripper the XUW World Champion. Numark's little match will count for nothing." Cut back to the announce desk. AT: "It seems that a lot of people have their own ideas about the big announcement." BJ: "The Army of Darkness must have the inside track. They're never wrong!" AT: "I thought you said that the New Royal Legion were correct." BJ: "They are. Webster will end both conspiracies in one fell swoop!" AT: “Well, now it is time for a first, it’s the first ever XUDF match on non-PPV XUW programming!” "Enter Sandman" by Metallica starts to blare over the arena speakers, and XUDF's United States champion, Darkseid, appears on the staging area to a loud, yet mixed reaction from the crowd. He seems indifferent to both the cheers and the boos as he makes his way down to the ring. He's wearing a brace on his still-injured knee. AT: “Sounds like the US champ has some supporters out here, but with an equal number of detractors as well.” BJ: “He's an idiot for coming out here with an injured knee!” AT: “I agree it might not be the smartest thing in the world, but only Darkseid knows for sure how much he'll be able to do on that knee. The North American championship is something that all three men in this match would be willing to suffer a broken leg for, I'm sure.” As Darkseid gets into the ring and hands his title belt to the referee, his music is replaced by "Undertaker's Theme, circa 1998" as the arena erupts into a chorus of boos. The Shadow sneers at the crowd as he makes his way down the ramp to the ring. He climbs through the ropes, and gets right into Darkseid's face, staring him down. Darkseid stares just as intensely back at The Shadow. AT: “Geesh, these two men are about to explode right now!” The ref gets between Darkseid and Shadow, and both men back off of each other. They turn to look back up at the entrance ramp. "Cup O' Tea" by the Newsboys begins to play, and the fans pop loudly for the North American champion, Doughboy. AT: “The XUW faithful undoubtedly remember Doughboy's classic matches on PPV, as has already garnered quite a following here in the big leagues!” BJ: “Pffft, if he's so great, how come he's not in XUW yet? AT: “Because the Latch Key Kid fell to the floor just milliseconds before Doughboy did...he could just as easily be on the XUW roster right now!” BJ: “He's a loser and a failure, because he couldn't fall down fast enough!” AT: “Oh forget it.” Doughboy remains at the top of the ramp for a second, playing to the cheering crowd. Then, he looks toward the ring, and stares directly at Darkseid and Shadow, pointing to the NA title belt strapped around his waist. Both men in the ring shout profanities toward Doughboy, who in turn shouts back at them. Finally, Doughboy just rips the NA belt off from around his waist, and charges toward the ring. He slides in through the ropes, and the ref calls for the bell to start the match. XUDF North American Title Match: Doughboy © vs. The Shadow vs. Darkseid Before Dough can get to his feet, DS and Shadow start stomping on him to keep him down. After a few moments, Shadow pushes DS aside so he can work on DB himself. DS, however, doesn't take too kindly to the gesture, and blindsides his fellow challenger, taking him to the mat with a clothesline. AT: “Looked for a moment like Darkseid and Shadow were on the same page, but I guess that's over now.” BJ: “Darkseid's gonna pay for turning on Shadow like that! He'll rue the day! RUE!” DS gives Shadow a couple more kicks for good measure, then turns back to DB, who's slowly getting to his feet. DS lays into DB with a huge punch...and a second...and a third...and a fourth...no! DB blocks the fourth punch, and starts laying into DS with a few lefts and rights of his own, as the crowd cheers him on! AT: “The Champ is going on the offensive!” BJ: “I'll say he's offensive.” AT: “...” DB grabs DS and gives him an irish whip into the ropes. DB goes for a clothesline, but DS reads it and ducks under as he passes by. DB executes a standing missile drop kick, but DS hangs on to the ropes and DB hits nothing but air...and the mat, hard. AT: “Ouch, tough break there for Doughboy.” A dazed Doughboy starts to get back up to his feet, and DS moves in to strike...when Shadow sneaks up from behind and locks DS in the No Escape (cross-face chickenwing/Tazzmission combo into an STF)! BJ: “Shadow's going to win! Darkseid has no choice but to tap out here!” DB stomps Shadow to break up the submission hold. AT: “Nice call there, Sherlock.” DB pulls Shadow back to his feet while DS is still reeling on the mat from the No Escape. After a few punches, DB whips Shadow into the corner, and hits him with a tremendous splash! DB follows Shadow into the corner, and introduces his face to his fist, ten times over, with Kneading The Dough (10 punches in the corner)! The ref admonishes DB for the move, but the crowd just cheers and counts along with the NA Champ...7...8...9...10!! Shadow falls down to the mat, with the fans roaring their approval. DB looks out at the crowd, then signals for his finishing move. AT: “Doughboy's going to try and end it right here! It's Cookie Cutter time!” BJ: “Shadow, you idiot! Look out!!” Alas, Shadow cannot hear the pleas of BJ, as he gets up in a daze and turns around. Doughboy boots The Shadow in the stomach, and nails him with the Cookie Cutter (Diamond Cutter)!! He rolls up Shadow as the ref counts... 1... 2... Thr...no! Darkseid breaks up the pin at the last second!! AT: “Good lord, Doughboy nearly retained his title right there!” BJ: “You didn't really think it was going to end THAT easily, did you? Ha!” Shadow is slowly beginning to stir on the mat, as DB gets back up and locks up with DS. DB locks the big man in a side-arm takedown and pulls back, applying pressure to his shoulder. DS winces in pain, but doesn't seem to be terribly affected by the move. He manages to reach up and toss DB right over his body, slamming him to the mat. DB quickly gets back up, and the two men lock up once again. DB whips DS into the ropes this time, and rolls him up on the rebound into the Cookie Press (half-crab roll-up)! The ref counts the pin... 1... 2... Darkseid reverses it into a pin of his own!! 1... 2... NO! Doughboy reverses it back!! 1... 2... Darkseid kicks out at 2.99!! AT: “Good lord, what impressive reversals by both men! The crowd is starting to go crazy!” BJ: *gasp* “Where's my asthma medication!?” DB and DS both look somewhat frustrated at their failures to pick up the pinfall as they get back up to their feet. The crowd cheers at their display of reversals and near pinfalls. AT: “These men are all giving the fans their money's worth so far, but who's gonna walk out as XUDF's NA champ?” BJ: “The Shadow is! Look, he's back on his feet!” Indeed he is. While DS and DB go to lock up with each other again, The Shadow charges in and takes them both down with an impressive double clothesline. DS tumbles over the ropes to the outside, while DB manages to stay in the ring. With fury in his eyes, Shadow whips DB into the opposite ropes, and hits him with the Shadow Stop Spinebuster (spinebuster a la Farooq) on the rebound. He waits for DB to get back up to his feet, then whips himself into the ropes and nails the NA champ with a Fear Maker (float-over DDT). The Shadow rolls DB up for the pinfall... 1... 2... Kickout! The Shadow bangs the mat in frustration, and picks DB back up to his feet. He sets up the champ, and connects with his finishing move, the Shadow Fall (Scorpion Death Drop into a pin)!! AT: “This could be it for The Shadow!” BJ: “New champ! New champ!!” The ref counts... 1... 2... Thr...NO!! Darkseid comes out of nowhere to break up the pin!! BJ: “DAMN IT!!!” AT: “I don't know how Darkseid managed to make it back into the ring to break that up, but he sure as hell is quick for someone his size! Guess desperation does that to a man.” DS, filled with renewed energy, begins to lay into Shadow with lefts and rights, with DB still out on the mat. Shadow tries to fight back, but the momentum has already shifted over to the US champion. DS grabs Shadow by the throat, and nails him with a hard chokeslam near the corner! Before Shadow can recover, DS picks him up in the corner and drills Shadow with The Dark Cloud (top rope piledriver)! DS then sets Shadow up in the corner again... AT: “Darkseid's going for his finisher now!” BJ: “Doughboy's still down! If Darkseid hits this, it's all over!” DS nails Shadow with his finishing move, the Total Damnation!! DS hooks a leg as the ref comes in to make the count... 1... 2... Doughboy dives in and breaks up the pin at the last possible instant!!! BJ: “WHAT!?” AT: “Holy...! Where'd Doughboy find the strength to get back up like that!?” The crowd is on its feet, cheering Doughboy's eleventh-hour effort to keep DS from winning. Shadow is out cold, while DB struggles to get to his feet. DS, in a word, looks pissed. AT: “Doughboy may have saved his title for the moment, but it looks like he's about the suffer the consequences!” DS turns and glares at DB, who is now on his feet, but groggy. DS marches out into the center of the ring and grabs the NA champ, whipping him into the corner where Shadow was...unfortunately, the ref was checking to see if Shadow was alright, and gets his clock cleaned by a charging Doughboy! AT: “The ref is out now!” BJ: “All three men have tried their finishers and couldn't come up with a victory, and with the ref out cold, anything can happen now!” AT: “I'll say...good lord, it looks like a tornado hit that corner!” DB gets back on his feet, and looks out at the crowd. The fans are applauding his efforts to stay in the match, and cheer for him to retain his title once again. The cheers fill DB with a renewed vigor, as he stands upright and points at DS with a grin on his face. DS motions for DB to bring it on, and DB obliges by charging into the center of the ring. DS and DB quickly lay into each other with a series of lefts and rights, with neither man gaining a clear advantage. Nu picks up Dave, and delivers a devastating German suplex, and follows it up with a running legdrop. Nu picks up Dave and delivers a sit out powerbomb in the middle of the ring, as Kipfer looks to be out of it.
