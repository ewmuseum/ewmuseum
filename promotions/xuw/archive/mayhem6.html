XUW Mayhem #6

16 Oct 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Welcome to Mayhem, live from the Brad Bice arena! Things start off with Bob and Naron coming to the ring to a massive pop. Woof woof walks to the ring to very little fanfare. Bob starts things off quickly by attacking Woof as he gets into the ring. He hits woof woof a few times, and then proceeds to pick him up and whip him to the corner. Bob then pulls out….a BONE! Bob runs at woof with the bone and hits woof in the head with it. Naron just sits on the outside with her hand over her head. Bob whips Woof to the other side of the ring, then he runs at woof, who moves and Bob crashes into the corner. Woof grabs the bone and hits bob with it. Woof hits a big suplex on bob, then picks him up and hits a flying elbow. Woof then climbs to the top rope, and goes for a big splash, but Bob moves. Bob locks on the Skid mark chicken wing and Woof taps! Winner and NEW IC CHAMPION: BOB! AT: Welcome to Mayhem, as we start things off with a title changing hands! I never expected that! MM: Well, expect the unexpected here at Mayhem. And als… AT: Well, speaking of unexpected, why is Takerrocks coming out here? Takerrocks walks to the ring with a  mic and begins to talk Takerrocks: Tonight I have a match with Lance. If I win, I want to get my hands on Mikey again. I am tired of Mikey getting all the attention. I want a title shot! LanceStormDBC walks to the ring calmly and the match begins. Takerrocks tries to hit lance with a kick to the face, but lance catches Taker’s foot and rolls him into the half crab that Lance calls the “Stright shooter. Takerrocks taps to the pain. Winner: LanceStormDBC From nowhere DDT runs down to the ring and jumps Takerrocks. Lance grabs DDT from behind and rolls him into the straight shooter. DDT begins to tap as well. Winner: LanceStormDBC Takerrocks and DDT fight to the back as Mikey walks onto the ramp. Mikey: I called in a favor from the front office to set up this match. The man who TOOK the Submission title from you, Kata! Kata walks to the ring, generating cheers only because he is fighting Lance Kata starts things off with a few quick Lariats. He picks Lance up and gives him a body slam, followed by a body splash. Kata throws a sleeper on Lance until Lance hits the mat. Kata gets up and picks up lance. Kata goes for a neck breaker from behind, but Lance avoids it and turns it into a side suplex. Lance climbs to the top rope and does a frog splash onto Kata and goes for a pin…1…2…NO Kata kicks out. Lance picks Kata up, and he rolls Kata into the straight shooter. Kata refuses to give, and finally makes it to the ropes. Kata, slow to his feet from the pain, gets up, but gets a superkick to the face. Lance goes for the pin…1…2….3! Winner via PINFALL: LanceStormDBC AT: What a showing by Lance. He wants that world title! MM: Yes, I noticed, and he also beat three men. AT: One thing that is going to bother Lance, though. He didn’t beat kata by submission. Kata has never given up to Lance. MM: That is a good point. Lance won, but not by submission. Backstage Bob and Naron are celebrating Bob’s title victory over woof woof. Bob is talking on the phone Bob: Yes, I know mom…yes I beat him. No mom, he didn’t bite me…yes mom, the bone worked…it was a good idea…. Out of nowhere Woof hits Bob over the back of the head with Naron’s Kendo stick. Woof then clocks Naron and carries her off. Bob looks around angry and chases after them, but woof gets in a car and drives off. AT: It looks like Woof wasn’t to happy that he lost his belt tonight. MM: Speaking of Woof, here comes his former tag team partner, D26. D26 walks to the ring with a mic in hand. D26: Tonight, I am making a match. Not for 1 title, BUT FOR 2! That is right, tonight it will be Severson v. Lurchador for the Hermit title AND the hardcore title. Sev walks to the ring, as D26 joins the announcers. Lurchador walks to the ring to a huge pop. Lurch starts out by hitting Sev with a garbage can. Lurch then grabs a chair and beats Sev with it. He goes for a quick pin…1…2…nope, Sev kicks out. Sev gets up and reaches under the ring and finds a stop sign. He hits Lurch with it. He then motions for someone to come to the ring. Loose cannon tries to run to the ring, but D26 picks up a chair and beats Cannon down with it. Sev. Calls again and this time HurriSakie comes down, only to suffer the same fate as Loose Cannon. Sev gets really mad and calls down more people. Suddenly HBK, shows up on the ramp, with Sue Wolfe and Lopen. All three run down to the ring and beat D26 down. Meanwhile, in the ring, Lurchador regains himself, and rolls up Sev, who is watching D26 get beat…1..2….3! Winner and new Hermit/Hardcore champion: Lurchador! The beating of D26 continues as the rest of the horsemen run to his aide. They run off HBK and his crew and stand in the ring, giving the sign of the horsemen. AT: I think the bigger story is that Lurchador is now a double champion! MM: I know, what an upset! AT: I think that Sev. Is gonna be lookin for some revenge now! MM: But Lopen and Sue Wolfe have joined HBK, it looks like we are going to be in for a long ride with those guys all together. Hodgo walks down to the ring followed by KoRe. KoRe starts by throwing Hodgo into the ropes and hitting a somoan drop. He then body slams Hodgo a few times, and follows it up with a lariat. He picks Hodgo up and gives him a suplex. KoRe climbs to the top, and does for a sentan bomb. Hodgo moves and KoRe is down. Hodgo grabs a chair and nails KoRe with it. Winner: KoRe via. DQ Henrik runs to the ring and begins to beat down KoRe. Takerrocks limps down to the ring, and he pulls KoRe out. Hodgo and Henrik stare them down from the ring when the flashtastics attack Henrik and Hodgo from behind. KoRe and takerrocks dive in and join the brawl when “Bodies” plays and D26 walks out, holding up the 4 horsemen sign. D26: It seems we have a small problem. You guys want to beat each other down, good. Next week, we will have a 4 corners match. The winner will get a shot at the Army of Darkness for the tag titles. I know the flashtastics would LOVE that shot, as would the rest of you. The problem is this though…you are also going to have to compete with Raj and Big Daddy, 3XL! Now, get out of my ring before my horsemen take you out! HUGE crowd pop as D stands up top with the sign of the horsemen and he walks to the back. AT: What a main event next week! A four corners match! MM: Yea, I know. It is gonna be a great event! Spanish Jobber: Si, es muy bueno! MM: Would you shut up. Spanish Jobber: Si, no soy bueno… AT: Well, our next match involves the submission title as well, and it is our main event. It is Blitzwing v. Jobber, where if Jobber wins he gets a shot at Kata for the submission title. Blitz walks to the ring to a large pop. Jobber walks and gets the most boos of the night, but he seems to feed off of it. Jobber and Blitz start by locking up. Jobber hits a hip toss on Blitz, and another, and another, and Blitz finally backs up. They lock up again, and Blitz puts Jobber in an arm bar. Jobber gets up and rakes Blitz’s eyes, then he runs off the ropes and hits Blitz with a lariat. Jobber gets back up and he climbs to the top rope and hits a 450 splash on Blitz. He goes for the pin…1…2….no, Blitz kicks out. Jobber gets annoyed, and he goes out to grab a chair. He brings it into the ring, but Blitz kicks it back into Jobber’s face, a la RVD. This makes Jobber even angrier as he was hit with a RVD move. Blitz gets up and he hits a face buster on Jobber. Blitz picks Jobber up again, and picks jobber up like a powerbomb, but jobber reverses it and turns it into an X-factor. Jobber gets up and picks up Blitz. H puts Blitz on the top rope and sets him up for a superplex. Blitz counters and hits Jobber in the stomach a few times. Jobber falls, and Blitz sets up for a swantan bomb, but HBK runs to the ring and pushes Blitz off. Jobber rolls up Blitz for a quick 1..2…3! Winner: Jobber AT: Well, that was a fluke! MM: Yes, it was. Jobber has a mic. Jobber: It seems that I get a title shot next week! BRING IT KATA! D26 once again walks onto the ramp, mic in hand, flanked by the horsemen. D26: Jobber, I am a man of my word, you get your shot at Kata. However, the man you just cheated, he will be the special guest referee! Jobber: No problem! Kata, you are going down! Fade to black
