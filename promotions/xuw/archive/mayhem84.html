The crowd give him a mixed reception of cheers and boos, perhaps more on the cheers than anything, which seems to surprise him. Mikey: DDT has been in great shape recently. He was a huge figure in the Hardcore Icons defeating the Ultra Violents, and then he came within milliseconds of upsetting Saij and becoming XUW World Champion. BJ: He should have used the damn tazer! "In The End" hits, and out comes Ric Austin, accompanied by Derrick Miles and Bob The Temp. Austin looks focused, and doesn't showboat in the way he normally does. What has not changed though, is the tremendous heat he receives, as one of the most hated men in the entire federation. Mikey: Well, he seems to have gotten back in touch with 'The Professor' side of his psyche. I'm not sure whether that is better or worse than before. BJ: Better! More headlocks! More headlocks! Singles Match: DDT v Ric Austin The bell rings and the action gets underway immediately. Austin enters the ring and DDT starts on him with some clubbing forearms to the back of the neck area. DDT then throws Austin right into the ring post, where he nails his shoulder! Audtin cries out, but DDT is relentless and pulls him out, before slamming his head down hard on the turnbuckle. DDT charges him and looks for a Clothesline, but Austin ducks and uses his shoulder to lift DDT over the ropes. The Headlock will not be defeated so easily! DDT goes to capitalise, but Austin quickly rolls away to the corner and then springs out to hit a Spear takedown. The Professor rolls over into an Armbar, but can't get it totally locked in, and DDT hooks the ropes with his right leg. The ref tells Austin to let go, and he does so, on a count of four. DDT picks up Austin and whips him to the ropes, but Austin reverses and sends DDT to them. The AoD member hits the ropes, and Bob The Temp grabs him by the ankle, stopping his momentum. The crowd boo, as DDT turns around to see who stopped him. He reaches over the ropes and grabs BTT by the head, lifting him up for a second, before Austin grabs him from behind and hits a Release German Suplex. DDT gets back up and runs at Austin, but the Prof uses his momentum against him, twisting him into the Spine Tingler (Spinning Spinebuster). DDT hits the mat hard, and Austin covers. ONE... ... TWO... ... KICKOUT!!! BJ: Dammit! Slow count! Mikey: It was nothing of the sort! Austin pulls DDT to his feet, and tries an Irish Whip, but it is reversed. DDT is first to his feet, and he looks around before grabbing the nearby timekeeper's chair, and folding it up to use it as a weapon.
