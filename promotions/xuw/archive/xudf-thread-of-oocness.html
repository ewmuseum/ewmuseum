XUDF Thread of OOCness!!!!11

Oct 13–17, 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Not a roleplay — the standing out-of-character catch-all thread for the XUDF Out of Character Discussions board, subtitled "Haha... 'OOCness' rhymes with 'penis.'" Page 6 of 15, meaning this was already a long-running default hangout space by this point. It is the single complete piece of forum text to survive XUW's 2005–2006 forum loss (see Storylines, "The ampersand tragedy"); no roleplay or promo text survived that loss. Served here in full because it is a genuine, privacy-safe slice of the community's own day-to-day voice — including the board's own in-character/handler conduct rule, quoted verbatim mid-thread by a member enforcing it on another.
