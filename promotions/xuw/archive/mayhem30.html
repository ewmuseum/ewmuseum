XUW Mayhem #30

15 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Pyro blazes, lyrics indecipherable blare, and someone brought back the damn foam machine and it's causing massive problems with drowning a few rows of the audience. Add that one of the pyro cannons misfired and has set a man on fire and you have the boys in the truck desperately trying to cut to the announce table. Which they do. Except, of course, Tomato and Barry aren't there, as they're still battling through the foam at the top of the entrance ramp. So they cut to the audience where signs such as "Bob is MY Hero!", "Die Sakie Die!", and "The AoD Sucks Goats" are clearly visible. Finally, Tomato and Barry, covered in foam, make it to the announce position. AT: Welcome to Mayhem! BJ: Can someone get this damn foam out of my ears? AT: Welcome to Mayhem! BJ: Hello Cleveland! AT: Welcome to Mayhem! BJ: HELLO CLEVELAND! AT: We have quite the show for you tonight! Esco faces King Henrik in a Submission Title Defense... BJ: HELLO...CLEVELAND! AT: Will someone get the foam out of his ears? Blake faces former partner, Woof Woof. The Deadly Combination - with Big Sev - faces the combo of the Royal Legion and Blitzwing... BJ: Hello Cleveland? AT: ...Lazarus faces Syco for the TV title, Sidhe faces Kata in an IC title matchup... BJ: WILL SOMEONE GET THIS FOAM OUT OF MY EARS? AT: Sakie faces Blake Savage in a World Title Match, and HBK and The Ripper square off one more time in the epic battle for good and evil...and the Number One Contender's spot! BJ: It's about damn time...alright, we have quite the card tonight... AT: I already did that, Barry. BJ: DAMN! Supathug Esco (c) vs. King Henrik - Submission Title Match "Rule Brittania" hits and Henrik comes to the ring with Naron, to massive heat. "Doggz" hits and the SupaThug comes to the ring, also to massive heat. The two men circle each other as the ref signals for the bell. They tie up. Esco gets the advantage and whips Henrik off the ropes, but gets caught with a clothesline on the return. Henrik pulls up Esco and goes for a suplex, but Esco blocks it easily and hammers down on the back of Henrik's neck. He picks up Henrik and hits a spinning side slam, and then begins to stomp on Henrik's knee. Henrik battles to his feet, only to take the Platinum Front (110th St. Slam) from Esco. Esco goes for the Thug Passion (reverse figure four), but Henrik quickly battles to the ropes before Esco can lock it in. AT: Esco is taking full advantage of the size difference here. BJ: It's just smart wrestling on his part, Brussel Sprout. Esco pulls Henrik up again and hits The Finisher (RockBottom). He goes for the Thug Passion again, but Henrik makes it to the ropes. Frustrated, Esco whips Henrik to the corner and then follows him in, but is caught with a boot from Henrik, who then hops up to the top rope and hits the London Calling (top rope bulldog) on Esco! Esco's dazed, and Henrik leaps upon the opportunity and applies the Stratford Strangler (Million Dollar Dream). Esco tries to make it to the ropes, but Henrik drags him to the mat. Esco holds on for almost two minutes, but finally is forced to tap. Winner and NEW Submissions Champion - Henrik the King AT: What a match! BJ: I knew Henrik would pull through in the end. He is royalty, after all. Blake Wolfson vs. Woof Woof "I Stand Alone" hits and Blake comes to the ring to a huge amount of heat. "We Are The Champions" hits and Woof Woof comes to the ring to a huge pop, which seems to confuse him. He slides into the ring and is immediately met with stomps from Blake. The former friends begin to slug it out in the center of the ring, with Woof getting the advantage. He begins to pepper Wolfson with vicious martial arts kicks, and then hits a belly-to-belly suplex. Woof goes for the pin, and gets a two count. As he pulls up Wolfson, Blake hits a low blow. Blake bounces off the ropes and hits a flying forearm, followed by a twisting gutwrench powerbomb. He goes for the cover and gets a two. He hits the Jugular (swinging neckbreaker into a pin), but again, only gets a two. AT: Wolfson is looking frustrated. BJ: That's not frustration, that's concentration. Blake goes for the Last Legs sharpshooter, but Woof rolls him into a small package for a one. As he and woof get to their feet, Blake goes for a big right hand. Woof ducks and catches Blake in a full-nelson, which he turns into the suplex he calls "The Woofinator". Woof follows up with a Doggy Style Driver (tiger driver '91) for a two count. Blake rolls out of the ring. Woof follows, only to get waffled by a chair by Blake. Blake continues the beating as the ref calls for the bell. Winner by DQ - Woof Woof Blake continues beating on Woof Woof. AT: This is totally uncalled for! BJ: This is what happens when you cost your partner the tag titles. AT: But Woof didn't...well...at least not on purpose...can we just go to the next match? The Deadly Combination + Severson vs. The Royal Legion + Blitzwing "Rule Britannia" hits and the Royal Legion comes to the ring to massive heat. "Ytse Jam" hits, and Blitzwing also comes out to massive heat. "Deadly Combination" hits and the self-same group comes out. Then nothing hits, except the sound of Severson's massive feet hitting the ramp. The ref signals for the bell and the match starts with Severson tying up with Blitz. Blitz tries to slam Severson, which Severson responds to by pressing Blitz with one arm. Severson tosses Blitz to a corner, and rushes in with a body avalanche in the corner. He pulls up Blitz, who low blows Severson and then rakes his eyes. Blitz takes the opportunity to hit an inziguri on Severson, taking the big man down. Blitz tags in RX, who immediately begins to stomp on Sev. Sev battles to his feet and RX tries for a belly-to-belly suplex on Severson. Sev blocks the first attempt, and then, in an incredible display of power, RX gets the big man up and over. Drained, RX tags in Bailey. Bailey body checks Severson, hockey style. He signals to RX, who distract the ref while Bailey clocks Severson with a hockey stick. Bailey goes for the cover, but Numark breaks it up. AT: Who would have ever expected to see Severson dominated like this? BJ: Well, it's easy to forget in comparison, but Raven and Bailey are big men themselves. Around 300lbs each. Or, as they'd rather be described, around 118 kilos each. Bailey goes to his corner, apparently to tag in Blitzwing, but gets distracted and begins eating the turnbuckle. Severson starts crawling to his corner. Blitz tags himself in and rushes to stop Severson, who performs a freakish act of agility and turns a front somersault to his corner, tagging in Numark. Numark comes in and spears the holy hell out of Blitzwing. Things quickly break down from there, Severson getting beaten with a hockey stick by Bailey, MV Pete and RX going at it outside the ring, and Numark basically manhandling Blitz inside the ring. He hits the spinning powerbomb and goes up top for the Bombshell (5 Star Frog Splash), but Bailey knocks him down with the hockey stick as Raven distracts the referee. Blitz goes up top for the Megawatt (450 Splash), but Pete has pulled Raven off the apron and rams him into the ringpost, causing Blitz to crotch himself. MVP quickly climbs the turnbuckle, setting up for the top-rope powerbomb as Nu climbs the adjacent turnbuckle. The crowd starts the "T-D" chant as Nu and Pete hit the Total Destruction (super powerbomb/frogsplash combo). Sev holds Bailey back as the ref counts the three. Winners - The Deadly Combination and Severson AT: That was quite the six man tag! BJ: If only it'd been six women...and I'd been in the ring... Lazarus (c) vs. Syco - TV Title "This Is Not" blares as Syco makes his way to the ring to some heat. "Crawling in the Dark" sounds as Lazarus comes out to a good bit of heat. Syco jumps Laz with a clothesline before the bell. He follows up with a DDT followed by a pin for a one count. Syco hits the Pain Killer DVD and then applies the Paralyzer (standing dragon sleeper). Laz tries to make it to the ropes, but Syco drags him back to the center of the ring. Laz seems to be fading, and then, in a sudden display of power, lifts Syco into a tombstone position from the dragon sleeper and hits a fire thunder driver! Suddenly, two men rush out from the back, only to be stopped by JP Blood and Rob the Throb in the aisle. The four men brawl to the back. AT: What the hell? BJ: That was Rage and Kaos...associates of Mr. Syco, as I understand it. Don't ask me what JP Blood and Rob The Throb were doing. Now...how about that dragon sleeper reversal from Lazarus? AT: That was damn impressive. BJ: I'd be impressed with these rookies, if only they weren't rookies. AT: Did that foam get to your head? Laz stands and leans against the ropes. He then springs forwards and hits a huge spear on Syco. He whips Syco off the ropes and hits a big boot, followed by the Lazasault (tope rope lionsault). He only gets a two, but whips Syco off the ropes and tries for the Fall from Grace (Chokeslam spinebuster), but Syco blocks and rakes Laz's eyes. Syco bounces back off the ropes, but telegraphs the clothesline and gets hit with the Fall From Grace for the three! Winner and Still TV Champion - Lazarus AT: What a strange match... BJ: Very strange match... AT: And from the strange to the downright twisted...Kata gets a chance at revenge against ex-Horseman Sidhe. Sidhe Nimh (c) vs. Kata Hajime - IC Title Match "El Phantasmo and the Chicken-Run Blast-O-Rama (Wine, Women, and Song mix)" hits and Kata comes to the ring to a Horsemen pop. "Chop Suey" hits and Sidhe comes out with Kat - in a Catholic schoolgirl outfit -  to massive heat. The ref takes one look at Kat, who's obviously carrying a knife at the very least, and quite possibly a gun, and sends her to the back. Sidhe seizes the opportunity while Kata's distracted and hits a high knee, knocking Kata back into the corner. Sidhe begins to hammer on Kata with stiff kicks, but Kata turns the situation around and begins to hit Sidhe with a series of hard kicks and chops. Kata goes for an Ipponzei (judo shoulder throw) out of the corner, but Sidhe lands on his feet, blocks the attempted chop by Kata, hooks Kata's other arm, and hits an underhook belly-to-belly suplex. Sidhe rides over and begins a brutal series of punches to Kata's head. Kata works his way to the ropes, forcing the break. Sidhe measures Kata as he gets to his feet, and tries to cannonball Kata on the ropes. Kata moves, causing Sidhe to crotch himself and get rolled up by Kata for a two count. Kata pulls up Sidhe and goes for a piledriver. He connects and gets another two count. AT: This is a great looking match. Kata seems to have really taken control BJ: Shut up. I bet on Sidhe, so if he doesn't win I'm in big trouble. Kata applies a half-crab to Sidhe, but Sidhe pushes up, rolls under his own body, and kicks Kata in the face with his free leg. Sidhe goes for a lariat, but Kata catches him with a northern lights suplex for another two count. Kata applies the Grim Sleeper (abdominal stretch with Dragon Sleeper), but Sidhe keeps fighting off the dragon sleeper part.  Frustrated, Kata goes for a wakigatame armbar, but Sidhe rolls through, frees his arm, and kips up. Kata gets to his feet, and turns into a kick to the midsection followed by the Needle and the Damage Done (Tiger Driver into shoulder/neckbreaker). Sidhe whips Kata off the ropes and hits the X-Hangover (forward driving spinebuster) for a two count. Sidhe hits a fisherman's suplex for another two. Sidhe hits a capture suplex and then goes up for the Candyflip (twisting moonsault into swanton), but misses. Kata jumps on the opening and again attempts to apply the Grim Sleeper, but Sidhe again blocks. Kata whips Sidhe off the ropes, frustrated, and goes for a back body drop, but he telegraphs it badly. Sidhe drops to one knee and uppercuts Kata. Sidhe stands, kicks Kata in the midsection, and hits the China White (Dominator/DDT combination) for the pin. Winner and Still IC Champ - Sidhe Nimh AT: That damn China White...one of the moves that has, sadly, put "The Comedian" Chris Blake out of action indefinitely. The extent of Chris Blake's injuries after his assault at the hands of the Army of Darkness is unclear, but it's currently not known when or if he will be returning to the ring. BJ: Blake had it coming. All the Blakes. AT: Including Blake Savage? BJ: After he beats Sakie, yes. Sakie (c) vs. Blake Savage - World Title Match "Hey Man, Nice Shot" plays as Savage comes to the ring to general heat and nastiness. "In The End"  hits and Sakie comes out to a MASSIVE pop. The two men square off as the ref signals for the bell. Savage and Sakie tie up, Savage tries for a russian legsweep, which Sakie blocks. Sakie tries for a fire thunder driver, but Savage slides over Sakie's back and smacks Sakie in the back of the head. Sakie turns, smirks, and goes to work on Savage with right hands, then bouncing Savage's head against the turnbuckle ten times as the fans count along. Sakie turns to pose as Savage clutches at his face. BJ: It looks like Savage may be hurt...disqualify Sakie! AT: Isn't that a bit... BJ: BAN SAKIE! The ref checks on Savage, who's indicating that his eye may be hurt. Sakie moves in, but the ref pushes him back. Savage feels around in his tights and finds a pair of brass knuckles. Before he can slip them on, though, Dave Kipfer jumps the guardrail to a gigantic pop, punches Savage in the chin, grabs the knucks, and... BJ: OH MY GOD! AT: Kipfer just hit Savage right in the... BJ: ...CHARISMA! Savage stumbles around as Kipfer backs up the ramp. Savage walks right into a Sakbottom, as Sakie picks up the easy pin. Winner and Still World Champ - Sakie BJ: And Savage had Sakie right where he wanted him, too! Disqualify Sakie! Ban Kipfer! And bring me some coffee-flavored coffee! AT: I think you need to lay off the coffee... BJ: DAMMIT! AT: Next up is the main event...for the right to face the world champ at Sudden Impact...The Phoenix - HBK faces the leader of the Army of Darkness - The Ripper... HBK vs. Jack Nobbledy - #1 Contenders Match "Six Feet Under (Remix)" hits as HBK and Sunny come out to a pop which seems to illuminate the arena. The orange lights flicker over the ring as he poses for the crowd. Funeral music plays, and then "One Armed Scissor" blasts through the darkened arena as The Ripper, Jack Nobbledy comes to the ring. The two men stare each other down as the ref signals for the bell. The staredown continues, neither man wanting to make the first move. Finally, HBK hits JTR with a stiff elbow to the jaw, and then stands, judging the Ripper's reaction. JTR smiles at HBK, and then levels him with a huge right hand. He, too, waits, quietly judging his opponent. HBK gets to his feet, runs off the ropes, and hits a huge calf kick on The Ripper, knocking him off balance. HBK quickly follows this with a punch to the midsection and a butterfly suplex. HBK goes for the early pin, but barely gets a one count before JTR powers out. As they get to their feet, HBK tries for a kick, but his leg is caught by the Ripper, who pulls him in for a short lariat. JTR picks up HBK and whips him off the ropes, going for the Call of Destiny spinebuster. Jack connects and goes for the pin, but HBK also kicks out quickly...before a two count. The two men stand and reappraise each other. They tie up and JTR applies a side headlock, which HBK reverses into a falling side suplex. HBK pulls up the Ripper and whips him off the ropes, hitting his own spinebuster on the return. He goes for the cover, but barely gets a two before the Ripper powers out, bench-pressing HBK out of the ring. The Ripper rolls out to the floor to follow HBK. AT: This is everything we were hoping for, folks. BJ: Yeah, except I have money on the Ripper, so I want this over with and soon. HBK blocks an attempted punch by the Ripper and hits a modified Jawbreaker. He picks up the Ripper and slams him on the floor. HBK goes up on the ring apron and leaps to the top rope. He comes off with a big flying elbow on Jack on the concrete. Both men are down and slowly get back to their feet. HBK backs off and goes for the Sweet Chin Music (superkick), but JTR ducks. However, HBK quickly switches directions and catches the Ripper with Sweet Chin Music as he turns. HBK is still clutching his ribs from his leap from the top, but he makes it onto the ring apron. He looks down at the Ripper on the floor and attempts Vertical Defiance (shooting star press), from the apron to floor. Suddenly, as HBK is in midair, Jack's hand shoots up and grabs HBK by the throat. The Ripper gets to his feet and tosses HBK into the guardrail. JTR clears off the Spanish announce table and hits HBK with the Event Horizon jackhammer through said table. Jack rolls HBK back into the ring and slowly follows him back in. BJ: YES! I'm gonna be rich! AT: HBK was just about broken in half by that jackhammer! Jack goes for the cover...but HBK somehow kicks out right before the three. JTR hits the Break (gutwrench into backbreaker), and repeats. When he goes for the third, HBK powers him over in a back body drop and hits a standing moonsault on the Ripper. Both men are down, but Jack gets to his feet first. He goes for the Ripsaw, but HBK, again, powers out into a back body drop and then applies the Heartbreaker inverted STF. JTR slowly manages to pull himself to the ropes. HBK, having gotten a chance to rest while applying the submission, quickly goes to a ring corner. As the Ripper stands, he's caught with a huge Sweet Chin Music, which HBK follows with a Balance Point moonsault for a pin attempt...but Jack kicks out just before the three. HBK gets up and starts measuring for a third Sweet Chin Music, but Jack ducks, and then ducks the next attempt and kicks HBK in the midsection. He grabs HBK's head and pulls him in for the Ripsaw. He hits one...but then powers HBK back up and hits another non-release Ripsaw. He powers HBK up again and hits a third...and then a fourth. Finally, he snaps HBK down to the canvas in a final huge Ripsaw. He goes for the cover and gets the three. Winner and #1 Contender - Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy As HBK and Sunny clear out, the Ripper stands in the center of the ring, collecting himself. BJ: I'M RICH! AT: What a match! What a damn spectacle! Well...that's all the time we have tonight...so, for the Majestic BJ, this is the Atomic Tomato. Goodnight! The scene fades out as the lights over the XUW ring slowly go out, enveloping the Ripper in darkness.
