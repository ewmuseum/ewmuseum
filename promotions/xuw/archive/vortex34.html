He comes off the top turnbuckle and Saij moves just out of Nu’s reach. Numark hits the mat hard as Saij rolls onto his stomach to catch his breath. Nu gets onto his stomach and then on all fours get catch his breath as well, but he’s obviously better for wear. Saij gets to his feet and nails Nu with the Downtime (spinning heel kick). Saij shakes out his head and sees Nu down. He runs over to the turnbuckle and slingshots himself to the drop. He looks at the crowd and shouts “HUZZAH!” and leaps off and hits Nu with The Final Huzzah! (senton bomb), but injures himself on the end of it. Saij holds his back before making an attempt to even cover Nu. He slowly slides over and drapes an arm over Nu. 1… 2… SHOULDER UP! as Pete breaks up the count at the same time. Pete starts to stomp at Nu as Dave comes into the ring and tackles Pete through the ropes. Fatbadger and Bob also get in the ring and tackle each other to the outside, leavin Nu and Saij in the ring. AT: Hell has broken loose here. Badger walks up behind Bob and simply picks him by his feet and rolls him over the guardrail, because Bob hit that Steinerline with such impact, he himself hunched over the guardrail and wasn’t fast enough to get his gut off it.
