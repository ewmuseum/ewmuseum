XUW: Triple Trouble 2003 First there was Double Trouble… Then there was More Double Trouble… Now, it’s time to step things up a gear… Welcome to… TRIPLE TROUBLE!!! TRIPLE TROUBLE!!! TRIPLE TROUBLE!!! The pyro flies around the arena like a deranged army of fireflies. The crowd cheers as if there lives depend on it, and in front of them a man holds up a sign telling them that if they don’t cheer loudly enough… they will all be killed. The Pyramid, in Memphis, Tennessee is packed to the rafter tonight, another great sell-out for Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. The ring is set up in the middle of it, and the entranceway is lit up by some nifty laser work in red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and indeed violet. The fans are wearing a similar cavalcade of colour, and are holding up signs, some of which are witty, and some of which are s***ty. We’ll let you be the judge of which are which. “Johnny Ramos for the World Title!” “Legionnaire 4 Life!” “This sign is made from recycled card.” “This sign is made from trees that were cut down as painfully as possible.” “Welcome back to The Longhorn!” “Why isn’t Vendetti being prosecuted?” “Why isn’t my sign funny?” “Dante is loopier than my Cheerios.” “Sending out an SoS to M V Pete!” “X Factor stole my sign.” …and the all-time classic… “It’s my baby! Mine!” Enough of that frivolity, let’s get going. We cut down to the announcers for your show this evening, former XUW World Champion, Mikey, and former Playboy TV subscriber, “The Majestic” Barry Johnson. CRUSH HIM! Mikey: We’ll also see Dante, the winner of the Halloween Battle Royal, taking on Henrik the King and the Latch Key Kid in what promises to be a stellar match.
