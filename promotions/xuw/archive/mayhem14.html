XUW Mayhem #14

20 Dec 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Welcome to Mayhem, LIVE from the Atomic Tomato arena! Tonight we will be seeing our main event of Bob v. Kata for the number 1 contendership for the world title! We will also see a battle royal, and the Hurristoners pulling double duty in two matches each. Here we go! AT: Welcome to Mayhem where tonight we will have a huge, great card! MM: That’s right, where tonight we will see new tag champions, and maybe a few surprises too. AT: I don’t know about any surprises… MM: Well then they wouldn’t be surprises would they! The Hurristoner’s music hits as Chase Wilson makes his way to the ring to a loud pop. Jack’s music hits and he walks down to a lot of heat, as he threatens to hit the people in the crowd. Chase Wilson vs. Jack Nobbledy As Jack is getting into the ring, Chase jumps him. Jack and Chase begin to just beat each other down. Finally, jack gets the upper hand and he sends Chase into the ropes. He hits a big back body drop, and then picks Chase right back up and he sends him to the corner hard. Chase hits the corner with such authority he just falls to the ground. Jack walks over, but Chase pulls his legs out from under him. Chase grabs Jack’s legs and he falls backwards, sending Jack into the pole. Chase grabs Jack and begins to hit him in the stomach. He sends Jack into the ropes and hits a lariat. Chase then kicks Jack in the stomach and picks Jack up and hits a big pile driver. Chase gets down and puts Jack into an arm bar. He wrenches back on Jack’s arm, but Jack refuses to quit. Finally, after struggling, Jack reaches the ropes. Chase gets up and argues with the Mexican. Hungry Mexican then pushes Chase…but breaks his arms and runs to the back screaming. Chase, looking confused, waves it off then picks Jack up. He kicks Jack in the stomach and goes for a power bomb, but Jack reverses it into a back body drop, but Chase holds on to Jack’s legs and tries to pull jack over into a sunset flip/pin combination. Jack is struggling to hold on, when out of nowhere, Henrik the king dives into the ring and nails Jack in the face with his cast, sending him backwards. Jack falls and Chase goes for the pin and another ref dives into the ring…1….2…3! Winner: Chase Wilson After the match, Chase runs to the back and Jack is laid out in the ring for about 5 minutes… AT: Wow…hmm, what was that? MM: That was Henrik with a foreign object. AT: Jack is the one who broke his arm. Jack brought it on himself! MM: Is Henrik even cleared to wrestle! We go to the back where HBK is trying on his referee shirt… HBK: This doesn’t, pop, like an Armani suit. I need something new…how about an Armani ref shirt! Assistant: I don’t think they make those… HBK: Well…MAKE THEM MAKE THOSE! Assistant: I can’t do that… HBK: Well then YOU can’t buy Christmas gifts for your kids! You’re FIRED! Assistant: In THAT case, you are an ignorant, stupid slob! HBK: Ugh…stupid people… AT: Well, our next match will be between two cruiserweights…Sakie and Cereal Killer. Sakie walks to the ring to a big pop. Then out comes Cereal Killer, to some mild boos. This match is for the cruiserweight title. XUW Cruiserweight Title: Sakie vs. Cereal Killer Sakie starts things off by running at Cereal Killer. He jumps and does a quick hurricanranna then he turns it into a quick pin…1…2…no. CK kicks out and he gets up quickly and lariats Sakie. CK dives out of the ring and gets a chair. CK gets into the ring and throws the chair at Sakie and kicks it into the champ’s face, and the referee calls for the bell. Winner: Sakie via DQ After the match CK gets upset that he lost. He picks up the chair and begins to pummel Sakie with it. CK picks Sakie up and sets up for a piledriver onto the chair, but Chase Wilson runs to the ring and knocks CK out. Just then Henrik, Bailey, and Hodgo dive into the ring and they all begin a beat down of the Hurristoners…when… HBK’s music hits and he steps onto the ramp wearing his Armani suit, and a special Santa hat… HBK: Ho…Ho…Ho…the only Ho’s I see are in this crowd…but enough of the small talk. Commonwealth, I like your work down there. I have a Christmas gift for you now…I want you to take the Hurristoner’s place in the battle royal today, seeing as they look like they are in no condition to do so. Well, have fun boys… HBK walks to the back as the cage begins to lower… AT: Well, it looks like we are getting right into our next match, this one will be quite interesting. It seems that due to lots of interference, they will have this one in a cage. It will be Admiral John v. Severson… MM: And tonight, Severson is gonna prove he is the superior athlete! The cage is lowered, the contestants are in and we are ready to go! Cage Match: Admiral John vs. Severson The only way to win this match is to climb through the steel door or exit over the top of the cage, no pinfalls or submissions are allowed. Both men tie up and AJ gains the advantage. He hits a shoulderblock followed be a clothesline. AJ then counters an irish whip to the corner and pounds away on Sev in the corner with rights and stiff boots. AJ then hits a vertical suplex and follows up with an elbow drop. AJ then begins to whip Sev into the cage a few times and signals that he is going to climb up. He begins to climb up the left side of the cage, but Sev recovers and shakes the ropes, crotching AJ on the top rope. Sev then back suplexes AJ and follows it up with a splash in the corner. Sev then hammers away on AJ in the corner and gives AJ a backbreaker. Sev climbs up to the top rope and hits a top rope elbow. Sev then picks up AJ and rams him into the side of the steel cage, several times. AJ is busted open. Sev begins to climb to the top of the cage but AJ gets up and follows in pursuit. They straddle on top of the cage exchanging fists. They begin to climb down into the ring when AJ knees Sev in the gut. AJ then hits a top rope superplex on Sev to the ring. Both wrestlers are out. A minute later, both recover and start to exchange fists. AJ gains the advantage and hits a backdrop followed by an atomic drop and a clothesline. AJ then hits his trademark powerslam and signals for the Anchors Away. As he is about to hit it, Sev knees him in the head and hits a DDT. Sev goes up top but AJ crotches him on the top rope. AJ climbs up and hits the Anchors Away off the top! AJ starts to walk towards the door when Derick Jackson runs out from the crowd. AJ is almost out the door but DJ slams it on AJ, and knocks him out. A few seconds later, Sev climbs to his feet and climbs the cage, and manages to escape the other side, giving him the win. Winner: Severson After the match, DJ and Sev celebrate on the ramp while a bloodied AJ looks on. AT: Damn that Derick Jackson! He screwed AJ again! MM: You know what I just realized? AT: What? MM: Both Derick Jackson and Admiral John’s last names begin with J! How freaky is that? AT: Ummm…anyway, now we go to our next match, it’s D26 vs. Flashtastic E&C for the XUW IC title – and don’t forget, HBK will be the “unbiased” referee. Yeah right… HBK’s music hits and he stands at the top of the ring with a classic HBK smirk as he makes his way down to the ring. Flash E&C comes down to a big pop, and D26 comes out to a mixed reaction. XUW IC Title: D26 vs. Flashtastic E&C Flash and D lock up and D takes Flash down with a headlock. Flash comes back with a series of arm drags followed by a shoulderblock. Flash goes for a backdrop, but D kicks Flash and hits a reverse neckbreaker. D goes for the cover and HBK gets a very slow 2 count. D gets up and starts to yell at HBK, but Flash recovers and takes D down with a clothesline. He picks D up and gives him a powerslam and follows it up with a elbow drop. He goes for the cover and HBK also gives him a very slow 2 count. Flash gets up and goes over to HBK but HBK just points to his referee shirt and smirks. Flash takes D down with a flapjack and goes to the top rope. He flies off the top rope but D recovers and nails him with a dropkick on the way down. D then goes over to the corner and pounds away on Flash, and smashes him into the turnbuckle 10 times. He backs up and goes to charge at Flash, but Flash moves out of the way and D smashes his shoulder into the ringpost. Flash then takes the advantage after a backbreaker and he then applies D in the sleeper. At this point HBK goes over to the corner and sits down, obviously not worried about the fact that D could submit. D then manages to fight out of the sleeper and runs at Flash, but Flash hits him with a knee to the midsection. Flash goes for the covers and HBK pretends to hurt his knee and walks over to the other corner so he can’t count the pinfall. Flash goes over to HBK and shouts at him. By this time D has recovered and clotheslines Flash from behind. He then hits a backdrop, a powerslam and a spinebuster. D signals to go up top and he hits the frog splash. He goes for the cover and HBK counts 1….2….HBK then stops to count and stands up, as he smirks at D and once again points to his referee shirt. D then pushes HBK and HBK pushes him back. D goes to clothesline HBK, but HBK ducks and he lays him out with Sweet Chin Music. Flash then gets up and HBK gives him the superkick as well. Winner: No Contest HBK then climbs out of the ring and raises his hands in victory as he walks up the ramp to a chorus of boos. AT: Damn HBK! He was supposed to be unbiased! MM: Well he was unbiased, he knocked out both guys! AHAHAHAHA! AT: Well next up we have the tag team battle royal involving 5 teams! The last two men in the ring will be the new tag team champions, no matter if they are teammates or not! XUW Tag Team Title Battle Royal: Riot Squad vs. 3XL vs. Technicians vs. Doggy Style vs. The Commonwealth All men make their way down to the ring, with the Technicians getting the biggest pop. The bell rings and total chaos erupts. Woof and KsC pair off and begin to exchange fists in the corner. Woof tries to life KsC over the top rope but BC comes over and pokes woof in the eye to break up the attempt. Bailey comes over and low blows KsC, and follows it up with a DDT. Sidhe pounds away on RavenX and gives him a clothesline. Sidhe walks back and bumps into Hodgo, and when they both turn around they were going to hit each other, but then decided not to as Sidhe went over to BC and Hodgo began to kick Blake. Bailey goes and tries punch Tony but Tony just stands there and shakes his head. Tony then kicks Bailey and hits a massive powerbomb in the centre of the ring. Big Daddy then comes over and press slams Bailey over the top rope out onto the concrete! Bailey is eliminated and seems to be injured. EMT’s come down and place Bailey on a stretcher. Raven looks on concerned and woof clotheslines him from behind, knocking him out of the ring as well so that now both Commonwealth members are eliminated. Raven X helps Bailey to the back. Sidhe comes over to woof and hits a vertical suplex. He then picks up woof’s feet and goes to slingshot him out of the ring, but woof grabs onto the ropes and stands up on the ring apron. He then headbutts Sidhe in the abdomen and goes for a sunset flip but Sidhe moves and woof hits nothing but canvas. Big Daddy then goes over to the Technicians and nails them both with kicks to the face. He sets them up on the ropes and goes to clothesline them both, but they duck and when BD turns around, both Technicians clothesline him out of the ring eliminating him. Tony then gets angry and begins to take out his frustrations on Blake including a chokeslam. Everyone then runs over to Tony and starts pounding on him. They corner him and everyone tries to elevate him over the top rope but to no success. Woof then runs off the ropes and dropkicks Tony in his backside which forces him over the top rope, eliminating him. AT: Wow what a match! MM: Yeah this is great! Only 3 teams can win the titles now! Each team then takes a corner and stares at each other for a few seconds, taking a breather. Hodgo then runs over to KsC and takes him down with a spear as chaos once again ensures. BC begins to pound away on Sidhe and takes him down with a neckbreaker. BC picks up Sidhe for the brainbuster, but Sidhe escapes out the back and hits BC with a reverse DDT. Woof immediately turns Sidhe around and hits the woofinator (Angle Slam) as woof celebrates. Woof turns around and gets the Riot Breaker (Hangmans DDT) from Hodgo. Hodgo then gets woof and sends him over the top rope. Woof once again grabs the ropes and holds onto the apron, but Hodgo knocks him off with a dropkick, eliminating him as Blake is now really angry. Blake then takes out both Riot Squad members with clotheslines, until the Technicians take him out with a double clothesline of their own. Blake gets back up and KsC gets ready for the spear. He charges at Blake, but Blake moves and sends KsC over the top rope, eliminating him. Blake goes to one corner, as does BC with the Riot Squad at another corner. Sidhe and Hodgo look at each other and grin, as they rush Blake and BC. Hodgo nails Blake with a piledriver while Sidhe holds BC. Hodgo then goes to hit BC with a clothesline but BC ducks, and Hodgo knocked down Sidhe. Sidhe then gets up and yells at Hodgo. Hodgo pushes Sidhe, and Sidhe pushes him back. By this time, Blake and BC are recovered and they rush the Riot Squad and hurl them both out over the top rope at the same time. Winners and NEW XUW Tag Team Champions: Blake Wolfson & Bionic Carrot After the match, Blake and BC begin to stare each other down as the referee gets in the ring and hands both wrestlers their title belts. Meanwhile, Hodgo and Sidhe are now fighting on the outside of the ring, as security comes down to break it up and they separate both wrestlers. Blake and BC make their way to the back trash talking each other at the same time. AT: Wow, what a match. Who would have thought two wrestlers who weren’t a team winning the tag titles? MM: Yeah I wonder how woof and KsC are going to feel. AT: You’d have to think that the partnership between Hodgo and Sidhe is almost over too. MM: Almost? AT: Good point. Next up we have the main event which is Bob vs. Kata for the #1 contendership for the XUW Title! Bob makes his way out to the ring to a big pop, and Kata comes out to a mixed and more quieter response #1 Contenders Match: Bob vs. Kata The bell rings and the two lock up. Bob sends Kata off the ropes and hits a hiptoss. He follows up with an armdrag and he continues to apply pressure to Kata’s arm. Bob then hits another armdrag and gives Kata a legdrop on the arm, and applies a leg grapevine. Kata then gets up and puts pressure on Bob’s shoulders which gets a 2 count before Bob rolls back down, re-applying the pressure on Kata’s arm. Kata then brought his legs up and legdropped Bob several times until he broke the hold. Kata then took down Bob with a neckbreaker and followed it up with a running legdrop for a 2 count. Kata went for a dropkick but Bob hit a sunset flip for a 2 count. Kata quickly got back up and took Bob down with a dropkick to his left knee. Kata then began to stomp on Bob’s knee and wrapped him in a figure 4 in the middle of the ring. Bob tried to inch towards the ropes but he couldn’t make it. He lay down and got counted for several 2 counts. He then tried to roll over and after several attempts he did it, applying the pressure to Kata now. Kata then made it to the ropes and the hold was broken. Bob went for a clothesline, but Kata ducked and hit Bob with a chopblock from behind. Kata went for another figure 4, but Bob rolled up Kata. 1…2…NO! Close fall but Kata got back up and took Bob down again with a clothesline. Kata propped Bob in the corner and set up for a tornado DDT, but when he was about to hit it, Bob stood still and threw Kata off face first into the canvas. Both men were out and the referee began to count, with the crowd chanting along. Both then got up and started exchanging fists, with Bob gaining the control. Bob then hit a few clotheslines, followed up with a powerslam and a DDT for a close fall. Bob then propped Kata on the top rope and went for the Siagon Snap, but Kata leapfrogged Bob over the middle rope, and when Bob turned around, Kata hit the Fisherman buster. Kata then signalled for his finisher and applied the Stretch Plum in the middle of the ring. Bob started to fade, and the referee lifted up his hands. 1…..2…..NO! Bob then began to wave his arms as the crowd began to clap along. Bob then lifted Kata up on his back and fell backwards on Kata, knocking the wind out of him. Bob then placed Kata on the top rope and finally hit the Siagon Snap off the top rope. Shooter slides in the ring and counts 1…..2…..3! Winner: Bob to become the new #1 contender for the XUW Title AT: What a night in XUW! A new #1 contender, new tag champs and a lot of controversy! MM: Yeah but what’s new? Just wait till Vortex… After the match, Bob climbs up on one of the corners and raises his arms in victory as the crowd cheers. Lopen then comes out on the stage and starts to laugh, as Bob and Lopen stare at each other when the cameras fade to black.
