XUW Vortex #11

7 Mar 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Vortex Some random Nu Metal nonsense plays over the speakers, the crowd goes wild, the pyrotechnics explode in a particularly pretty and expensive way, and yes, ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, readers and … talent, it’s VORTEX TIME! The camera swirls around the auditorium, taking in such fan signs as “Bluto fears sobriety”, “Ikke Mighty fathered my children”, and even “We miss Matt Logan”! The cameraman eventually calms down from laughing, and goes to the announce desk, where our usual hosts for the evening, AT and BJ, are ready to kick things off. AT: “Welcome to XUW Vortex, and boy, do we have a show for you tonight?” BJ: “Well?” AT: “Well what?” BJ: “Do we?” AT: “Do we what?” BJ: “Have a show for them?” AT sighs, and then pyro erupts for the arrival of Hodgo. “Anarchy In The UK” plays, and the newest Commonwealth member makes his way to the ring, looking cocky as ever. He goes to pose in the corner, and gets pelted with rotten vegetables, and other assorted produce, before a plastic cup flies and hits him RIGHT IN THE EYE! He shrugs it off, and soon enough his opponent for the night comes down the ramp. It is JP Blood, and he looks ready for action! TV Title Match: Hodgo (C) v JP Blood Hodgo starts off the match by attacking JP as he enters the ring. He connects with some boots and then sends JP to the turnbuckle, and unloads with lefts and rights. JP is reeling, and Hodgo hits a suplex to leave JP down in the middle of the ring. Hodgo climbs the turnbuckle to the second rope, but it’s merely to taunt the crowd. Hodgo climbs down and turns to resume his assault, but JP has gotten up, and throws him to the ropes, hitting a Samoan Drop on the TV Champ as he comes back. They both get up, and Hodgo runs straight into a Spinebuster. JP climbs atop the corner, and goes for the Bad Intentions, but Hodgo gets his knees up. Hodgo flips up to his feet, and unloads a series of Anar-Kicks to JP’s head. JP goes down and Hodgo goes for the cover. 1 2 KICKOUT! JP shakes off his daze to kick out of the attempted pin. Hodgo goes for a Running Thrust Kick, but JP ducks, and Hodgo crotches himself on the ropes, much to the amusement of the crowd. JP takes advantage and hits a big Reverse DDT back into the ring. JP Blood looks for the pin. 1 2 SHOULDER UP! Hodgo gets to his feet, and JP Blood looks to hit his finisher, the Bloodshed, but as he grabs Hodgo, the champ drops to his knees and nails him with a low blow. JP doubles over, and Hodgo hits him with an Axe Kick to the back of the head. Hodgo picks up the weakened JP Blood and hits the Riot Breaker. He hooks the leg. 1 2 3! Winner, and still XUW TV Champion: Hodgo! Hodgo celebrates in the corner, and leaves to the back as smug as ever. AT: “That damn Hodgo, he’s acting like he didn’t cheat to win that match!” BJ: “He didn’t cheat!” AT: “That was a blatant low blow!” BJ: “No it wasn’t! It was a Martial Arts Chop to the upper thigh. It can be a devastating technical move!” AT: “You’re so full of it!” Cameras move backstage, and we are greeted by the impressive sight of the giant, Severson. He stands there, impassively leaning against a wall, as Blitzwing walks past. Blitz couldn’t fail to notice Big Sev, and turns to face him. Blitz: “So, big man, you still here?” Sev: “…” Blitz: “Ah, so, still keeping with the silent routine?” Sev: “… …” Blitz: “You can stand there, all imposing and silent, but we all know you’ll be afraid of F.E.A.R! You cannot stand against F.E.A.R!” Sev: “…” Blitz: “Insolence! You shall pay for this, silent man! You think you’ve seen it all here in this fed?” Sev raises an eyebrow Blitz: “You’ve not seen a damn thing! F.E.A.R. is the greatest thing this fed has seen since … since … well, since the last damn time I was here! You’re going down, big man!” With that, Blitzwing storms off on his way, leaving Severson, who looks as impassive as ever… XUW Hardcore Title Match: NFM (C) v CK AT: Well, here we are waiting for the appearance of CK, though Lord only knows what music he’ll be using this week…” ”Furious” by Ja Rule plays, and CK comes down towards the ring to a big pop. AT: “No wonder he’s getting cheered, he kept the same music for a whole week!” CK poses in the ring as the cheering continues, and then NFM’s music hits, and he comes down to the ring. His ‘Blade Of Fear’, is noticeably missing. The bell rings and the match is underway. CK starts off the brighter, grabbing NFM and hitting two Overhead Belly-to-Belly Suplexes. NFM gets back up, but is hit with a Mafia Kick to the face. CK sees that NFM is down, and leaves the ring in search of hardcore objects to use to full effect. He starts off with a steel chair, and rolls back into the ring, looking to cause damage. CK swings the chair, but NFM dodges away and kicks the chair back into CK’s face. NFM pulls some brass knucks from his pocket and smiles an evil grin at CK, as he gets up from the chair shot. NFM swings the knucks at CK, but he ducks and grabs NFM in a Full Nelson. CK then hits the Fear Factor. He goes for the pin. 1 2 KICKOUT! CK slams his hand down on the mat, and then prises the brass knucks from NFM’s fingers. CK stalks NFM as he rises, but NFM is wise to it and sweeps CK’s legs from under him. NFM rolls out of the ring, and comes back carrying a cookie tray and a baseball. CK is on his feet, and ducks under the baseball thrown by NFM. This leaves CK off balance, and NFM is able to sneak in and hit him with a cookie tray to the face. CK goes down, and NFM goes to cover. 1 2 SHOULDER UP! NFM picks CK up and whips him to the ropes. NFM goes for a Clothesline, but CK ducks, spins round and nails him with the brass knucks to the head! AT: “He calls that the ‘As If………..Whatever!’” BJ: “Huh?” NFM rolls out of the ring and is lying on the outside. CK leaves the ring and tries to lift NFM back into the ring, but NFM is a dead weight, and it takes CK some time to lift him up and roll him back under the ropes. CK climbs back into the ring and drops an elbow across NFM’s chest. He then hooks a leg. 1 2 KICKOUT! CK starts to hammer his fists down on the canvas after NFM just barely kicked out of the pin. CK gets up and starts to yell at the ref, stamping both feet down at once to emphasize his point. The ref just backs off as CK continues his tempter tantrum, and NFM sneaks in from behind, hitting CK across the back of the head with the cookie tray. As CK tries to keep on his feet, NFM reaches into another pocket and brings out … … a BUTTER KNIFE! AT: “That’s what CK has been calling the ‘Blade Of Fear’!” CK turns round and NFM smacks him with the butter knife right between the eyes. He catches CK as he falls, and hits The Lightning Strike onto the unforgiving cookie sheet. NFM hooks the leg. 1 2 3!!!!! Winner, and still XUW Hardcore Champion: NFM!!!!! AT: “Wow, what a match. Some great innovative offence there from NFM to snatch the win!” BJ: “On to the next match!” Singles Match: Bluto v Supa Thug Esco Bluto comes to the ring with his usual cooler of beers, chugging away on a couple as he goes. Then “Bad Dreams” by Bustah Rhymes plays and the Supa Thug makes his way down to the ring. He looks focused and intense as he steps in the ring and waits for Bluto to come back in. Bluto seems a tad sluggish as he enters the ring, but the bell sounds, and the match is on. Bluto swings a lazy right hand at Esco, but the Thug easily steps back out of range and then takes Bluto down to the floor. Esco tries to turn Bluto over and apply the Thug Mug, but Bluto powers out. Both men get up and Bluto grabs Esco, hitting a Suplex. However, he can’t capitalise as he’s still a little sluggish. AT: “I’m not sure if Esco is really hurting Bluto tonight, or if Bluto’s just wasted!” Bluto goes for a Leg Drop, but Esco rolls out of the way and then hits the Drive By as Bluto gets up slowly. Bluto is almost out, but rolls under the ropes and lands on the floor by the announce desk. He crawls over to the cooler and gets out two beers. He stands up and sees Esco coming over, and moves for the CAN-chairto. He swings the cans together, but Esco neatly steps back, and Bluto’s poor aim causes him to hit himself in the head with the cans. Bluto just drops to the floor, and Esco catches him with a backhanded fist on the way down. AT: “Man, he just isn’t himself today…” Esco rolls Bluto into the ring, but he seems to be hardly moving. Esco locks on a Reverse Chin Lock, and the ref checks Bluto’s arm. It drops… 1 2 3! Winner, by submission: Supa Thug Esco!! Esco looks quite puzzled, but then looks at him fists and smiles. AT: “Well, that was weird. I think either Esco has gotten incredibly strong, or Bluto needs to cut back on the drinking!” BJ: “You can say that again.” AT: “Well, that was weird. I think either Esco has gotten incredibly strong, or Bluto needs to cut back on the drinking!” BJ: “I didn’t mean that.” AT: “Oh.” BJ: “Next match! Next match!” Tag Team Match: Jack The Ripper and “The Comedian” v The Hurristoners While the commentators were babbling, Jack and Chris have made their way to the ring, surprisingly quietly. Then the Hurristoners come out to “Stay Together For The Kids”, and the crowd goes wild. The men run down to the ring and soak in the crowd pop before the match begins… Jack and Fatbadger start the match together. Jack grabs the Hurristoner and pushes him back to the ropes. The ref goes to break it up, but Jack pushes him away and whips Fatty to the other side, connecting with a Big Boot as he returns. Jack picks up the prone Badger, and hits a big Stalled Vertical Suplex. Badger tries to get up, but Jack is on him in an instant, flipping him over and looking to get The Fear locked on. Fatty fights it, and rolls Jack over, hitting a few right hands to slow down the big man. Fatty gets off and hits a stiff Roundhouse Kick to Jack’s head, sending him stumbling towards the corner, where Chris Blake tags himself in. TCCB flies at Fatbadger and takes him down with a Spear. Fatty gets up quickly, but is levelled as TCCB hits The Punch Line. Chris hooks the leg. 1 2 KICKOUT! Fatty gets up and stumbles to the corner, tagging in Chase Wilson. The fresh man goes to work on Chris, hitting a series of right hands and then the Short Term Memory Loss. Chris is dazed, and Chase picks him up to hit the CT Crunch. TCCB seems out of it and Chase goes for the pin. 1 2 NO! Jack steps between the ropes and pulls Chase off his partner, turns him round and hits The Break, to leave both men down on the ground. Fatty comes back in and hits a Clothesline on Jack, sending both men to the outside. Back in the ring, Chase picks up Chris Blake and tries to hit the Dying Daze, but it gets countered, and hits a Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker from nowhere. He goes for the pin. 1 2 KICKOUT! Chase kicks out, and then sends TCCB down with a hard Lariat. Back outside of the ring, Jack throws Fatty into the ring steps, and then walks over to the announce desk. AT: “Hey! What’re you doing? Give me my chair back!” The Ripper grabs the chair out from underneath Atomic Tomato and proceeds to smash it over Fatty’s head. Fatbadger goes down, hard. Back in the ring, Chase has heard the chair shot, and turns to see The Ripper enter the ring, chair still in hand. The Comedian turns Chase round, and goes to hit a Spinebuster, but as he throws Chase down, Jack connects with a huge uppercut with the chair! Chase is left unmoving on the canvas. AT: “Oh my God! That was horrific!” Jack throws the chair down, and tells Chris to help him pick up Chase. He complies, and Jack hits a RipSaw right onto the hard-edged chair. Jack starts to smile, and shakes the hand of ‘The Comedian’. Fatbadger has gotten up, and Chris warns The Ripper. Fatty charges the two men, but Jack sidesteps him and Chris hits a dropkick on him. Jack steps to the side and finds some lead piping conveniently in his discarded coat. He throws it to Chris, and then grabs Fatbadger from behind. Chris Blake smashes the bar into Fatty’s ribs, and then down across the back of the head. Jack smiles at his comrade, and together they pick up the dead weight of Fatbadger’s body, before Jack proceeds to give him a RipSaw onto Chase Wilson! Somewhere along the line, the bell got rung. Winners, by DQ: The Hurristoners! AT: “That’s a damn disgrace! He should be banned! I want my chair back!” BJ: “It’s rare that I can agree with you, but this Ripper goes too damn far.” AT: “Wait, he wants a microphone!” Jack gets a microphone and addresses the baying crowd, and one man in particular. JTR: “Sakie, you like what The Ripper did to your friends? You think this is all an act now? Sakie, where have you been living this past week, for you have not seen the power of the Army Of Darkness. HBK has been disposed of. Bob has been taken out of the equation. The Flashtastics have been … … well, they met with an untimely accident. Now, your Hurristoners join the list of casualties. Sakie, how fitting that your music is now proclaiming you to be ‘Number One’. You are now the number one target for the Army Of Darkness, and for The Ripper. You now stand alone, one man facing the might of the darkness. Still think this is an act Sakie? Still think The Ripper cannot intimidate, and destroy? Ask HBK, ask Bob, ask your friends here … if they recover…” Jack drops the microphone and leaves the ring as “One Armed Scissor” reverberates through the arena. Chris Blake follows behind him, a kind of glaze drawing over his eyes... AT: “That Ripper is a heinous creature, but at least I’ve been given a new chair…” BJ: Now, once the bodies are cleared away, it’s time for another title match!” Intercontinental Title Match: Sidhe Nimh (c) v Raven X Sidhe Nimh enters the arena to a huge pop, as “More Human Than Human” plays. Raven X then comes down to “Liberate”, and seems to have some lipstick on his face. A lady in the crowd takes careful aim with her drinks cup, but then realises that she already hit Hodgo with it. Sidhe Nimh starts on the offensive, hitting a few right hands and then a T-Bone Suplex. Raven X gets up, but gets put right back down again with the Bad Trip. Sidhe goes for the quick cover. 1 2 KICKOUT! Raven gets back up, but Sidhe levels him with a Clothesline and tries to tack on an STF on the floor. Raven wriggles his way out, and catches Sidhe with a hard kick to the face. Raven capitalises with a Release German Suplex and then a DDT. Sidhe gets up more slowly, and Raven runs at full tilt to hit a huge Clothesline that nearly knocks Sidhe’s teeth out. Sidhe get up to one knee, and Raven takes another charge, this time hitting a big Running Knee to the head. Raven hooks the leg. 1 2 SHOULDER UP! Sidhe gets his left shoulder up as Kat looks on rather worriedly. Raven locks on “The Twists Of Love”, and pulls back with all his strength, trying to get Sidhe to submit, but the little man won’t give up. Sidhe uses his leg strength to push forwards, and get to the ropes. Raven reluctantly releases the hold, but then drags Sidhe back to the middle of the ring and tries to reapply it, but Sidhe moves quickly and gets a Small Package hooked on. Raven screams, and Sidhe releases his genitals. Sidhe then goes for the wrestling move, the Small Package, and the ref starts to count. 1 2 KICKOUT! Raven gets up and runs at Sidhe, but Sidhe hits an Arm Drag Takedown and holds on, looking for an armbar. Raven rolls through, and the two men are locked together, when Bailey and Hodgo run down the ramp to ringside. AT: “Hey! They shouldn’t be here! They’d better not try to take my damn seat!” Sidhe hits a couple of Snap Suplexes on Raven, and turns round to see the other Commonwealth members. Bailey distracts the ref and Hodgo sneaks into the ring, only to get knocked down by Sidhe. Raven, however, grabs Sidhe from behind and hits an Edge-O-Matic. The other Commonwealth members leave the ring, and the ref counts. 1 2 KICKOUT! Sidhe still has fight left in him, and Hodgo and Bailey start towards the ring again, only to have Bionic Carrot and KsC Trunkz run through the crowd to confront them! The Technicians chase Bailey and Hodgo back up the ramp and out of sight. Raven watches his comrade go, and Sidhe moves in from behind and locks in the Fear and Loathing. Raven goes down, and can’t get out of it. In a few more seconds it is all over, and Raven is choked out. Winner and STILL XUW Intercontinental Champion: Sidhe Nimh AT: “That’ll teach that damn Commonwealth to try and interfere!” BJ: “They were just there to try and make the fight fair, but did you see the way Sidhe attacked Hodgo? Totally unprovoked! Disgraceful, he should be banned! Or shot!” AT: “Hmmmm. Anyway, we have our final match next. It’s for the XUW Tag Titles, and it should be just great!” XUW Tag Title Match: Doggy Style (c) v The Deadly Combination “The Dealy Combination” hits, and Numark and MVPete run down the ramp to a good pop. They are soon followed by “We Are The Champions”, and Doggy Style come out onto the stage to HUGE heat. They are accompanied by two of the “ladies” sent to them by TDC. Blake and Nu start off the match, and Blake gets the first move in, hitting a Doggy Dropper, and then a Spinning Leg Drop. He tags in Woof Woof, who goes to work with some hard right hands, and then hits a Woofie Bomb. Woof goes for the pin. 1 2 NO!!! MVPete gets into the ring and breaks up the early pin. He hits a Suplex on Woofie, and drags Nu a little closer to the corner before he leaves the ring. Nu gets to his feet slowly, but is dragged back to the centre by Woofie. Nu goes for the Enzeguri, but Woofie ducks and looks for an Anklelock. Nu rolls through and dives for the tag but just comes up short. Woof whips Nu back into the corner and tags in Wolfson. Wolfson vaults the ropes and hits a Spinning Heel Kick on Nu. Nu goes down, and Wolfson looks for the pin. 1 2 KICKOUT! Nu just kicks out, and Wolfson looks annoyed. He tags back in Woof Woof, and together they hit a Double Suplex on Numark. Woof goes for a pin. 1 2 KICKOUT! Woof now looks annoyed, and tags back in Wolfson. They hit a Double DDT and then stand in the ring, looking very pleased with themselves, as Blake Savage struts down the ramp to stand with the ‘girls’. Wolfson picks up Nu, and sends him to a neutral corner, before rushing in the slam him in harder. Nu sees it coming and gets a boot up. Wolfson drops back and Nu goes for the tag, but Blake Savage grabs his leg and he can’t make it. AT: “That is low tactics from Doggy Style, but then I guess that’s their style…” BJ: “Mmmmm, Doggy Style. Speaking of which, don’t their ladies look lovely tonight?” AT: “You mean you don’t know?” BJ: “Know what?” AT whispers in the ear of his fellow commentator, who promptly leans over to vomit… Back in the ring, Wolfson has recovered, and looks to hit the CRIKEY on Numark, but Nu pushes him off, and finally gets the tag. In comes MVPete, looking to kick some ass! He floors Wolfson with a hard right, and then Woof Woof. MVPete picks up Wolfson and hits a Prime Time Chokeslam on him, and then repeats the process on Woofie. The champions are in disarray, but as Woofie holds the ref, Blake Savage comes in and levels MVPete with a Superkick. The crowd starts to boo uncontrollably. AT: “Hey wait, someone’s coming through the crowd, it’s Dave Kipfer! He’d better not be after my chair!” BJ: “Nobody cares about your chair, but this is Kipfer, he’s got … talent!” Dave Kipfer grabs Blake Savage and throws him into the guardrail. He unloads with a series of right hands and then hits The Bootie Call right on the outside. The crowd goes wild as Kipfer gives the thumbs up to The Deadly Combination, and walks over to where the ‘women’ are standing. Back in the ring, MVPete has scrambled to the corner and tagged in Numark. He hits Wolfson with a Clothesline, and then hits a Superkick on Woofie. Numark rages around the ring and looks to nail Wolfson with a Superkick, but the Aussie ducks and hits a hard Neckbreaker. Nu goes down, but manages to roll over and tag MVPete back in. MVPete goes for Wolfson, but is levelled with a Clothesline. Blake looks to set him up for the Crikey, but then hears an ear-piercing whistle, to see Dave with the ‘ladies’ lined up together. The ‘ladies’ then lift up their skirts to reveal most unladylike bulges. Woof Woof and Blake Wolfson both scream in horror at the same time, allowing MVPete to smack their heads together and hit a Suplex on Wolfson into the middle of the ring. Numark was waiting, ready, and flies off the top turnbuckle, getting incredible air and hitting a huge 5 Star Frog Splash. MVPete goes for the cover, as Numark watches his back. 1 2 3!!!!!! Winners, and NEW Tag Team Champions: The Deadly Combination!!!!! AT: “New Champs! New Champs! Oh My God!” BJ: “I don’t know what has shocked Doggy Style the most, losing their titles or finding out their gals are really guys!” AT: “Looks like it was Daniel and Christian all along! Ha ha ha!” Back in the ring, Numark and MVPete celebrate, as their music fills the arena. AT: “Well, what a night, what a Vortex, what a show, what? It’s late? Okay, we gotta go. Be sure to tune in to Xposure this weekend and then Mayhem on Monday. Until then, this is AT, and I’m still amazed!” Fade Out
