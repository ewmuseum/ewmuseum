XUW Road Rage Welcome to Road Rage!!! We are in for a hell of show here at the packed Kilpatrick Forum!!! Our referees for the evening Takerrocks, Brad Bice, and Shooter. tonight's announcers are Atomic Tomato and Jobber. Pyros blast deafeningly, XUW music hits, and what ever hearing you had left after the pyros is erased by the crowd screaming and cheering. The lights go dark...a Battleship imerges on the Ultimo-Tron. Pyro's go off as the Battleship fires... Admiral John vs, Toto : Underused Talent match. The Admiral's music plays and plays, but nary is John to the stage. he suddenly appears in the ring in full SWAT/SEAL fatigues. The crowd seems confused. "Crazy Train" hits the speakers at a deafening tone. The crowd goes nuts...until Toto walks out onto the stage. He makes his way down to the ring, and the match begins. Admiral and Toto shake hands, smile and both go for a short arm clothesline at the same time. Toto is first get and delivers a double Az handle smash to the still-stirring Admiral. Off the ropes into a leg drop. Then a rapid succession of elbows. Toto then poses for the crowd, but can't even get any heat. Admiral gets up as Toto poses, and gets Toto set for the Neckbreaker, Toto reverses and throws Admiral into the ropes, catches him on the rebound and gives him an Armdrag take-down. Toto then climbs the ropes and drops on the Admiral with a Big Splash. BAM Toto rolls Him up for the pin...Takerrocks slides in ...1...2 Kickout. Taker gets a huge pop for the quick count??? Oh god. Now I KNOW these two are old school, but geez. Toto goes for his finisher, Kansas Cyclone, but the Admiral reverses and hits the DDT. The Admiral then gets Toto in the Boston Crab, but Toto grabs a rope, Takerrocks counts the three, getting another huge pop. AT: Who knew Takerrocks was so popular. Jobber: You know it's a crappy match when the ref carries the match. No way could that happen in Japan. Admiral pulls Toto into the middle of the ring and nails him with the Anchors away dropping both feet into Toto's groin. Admiral Rolls him up, and...1....2....3 Winner by pinfall, Admiral John Admiral and Toto get together in the middle of the ring. Toto raises Johns hand in victory. AT: Old school or not, that was a pretty good match! Jobber:For backyard maybe. How about that Ref?? Jack Nobbelty and Bluto VS Tony Mendolia and Raj Patel The lights go out. A single red light points at the stage. A pipe organ plays a macabre tune over the speakers. The UltiTron shows a close up of a throat being cut. Jack steps out onto the stage to a hail of boos. Jack raises his arms and makes several slashing motions. Bluto steps out from behind the curtain next, beer in hand. Strangely, Bluto gets a nice pop. This enrages Jack who pulls a knife and begins to slash at Bluto's direction. Bluto hands Jack his beer, distracting him. Thunder and lightning are fed through the UltiTron and speakers. And Blitzwingsteps out onto the stage. Instantly the whole arena erupts. "The following match, Jack and Bluto, will be a No DQ match!" Blitz turns and exits. FEEL THE POWER!!! Tony and Raj step out and run toward the ring. Jacks pulls a blade, and Bluto smashes his beer bottle. Tony jumps into the ring, grabs Jacks hand with the knife in it. Tony wrestles the knife out of his hand and throws it out of the ring. Tony hits the vertical suplex on Jack. Jack crawls out of the ring, and grabs a chair. Bluto hits Jack with the ring bell. Raj starts to grab Bluto while outside, realizes what is going on and smiles. He takes the chair from Jacks limb hands, and nails him repeatedly. Raj rolls him up. Bice slides in. 1...2...3! Winner by pinfall, Raj and Tony Sinner vs. Severson : Non-Title match STIPULATION (If Sinner wins, the Hermit Title will equal 200 posts and below. If said poster has been with Ultimo in excess of 6mos.) Sinner comes down the ramp, to a barrage of cheers. He jumps into the ring fresh and ready for a wicked fight. Severson saunters down. Quietly. As a True Hermit does. Severson attacks first grabbing and DDTing Sinner. Severson follows up with a huge elbow. Rolls into an Armbar. Sinner refuses to tap. The Hermit then stomps him into the mat over and over. He picks up Sinners now flacid body, and puts him into a sleeper-slam. Sinner hangs on. Shooter picks up Sinner's arm and lets it fall, ...1..2 Sinner raises his arm. Sinner delivers elbows to Severson, getting away. Sinner takes off and hits the running drop kick, which staggers the emense Severson. Then Sinner hits him with a clothesline. But Severson refuses to go down. Sinner gets wise and goes for the knees with another dropkick. Severson drops and grabs his knee. Severson get back to his knees, and Sinner nails him with a running boot to the head. Goes for the pin and Shooter gets 1...2...Power kickout, and Sinner flys off of Severson. Sinner flys through the middle rope and onto the floor. HBK Runs down the ramp, chair in hand. he flies into the ring and nails Severson repeatly in the head with the chair. The bells rings. HBK runs off with the Hermit Heavywieght Title. Winner by DQ, Severson. A.T.: HBK just made a HUGE mistake. Jobber: Ya, he showed up at this disgrace of a PPV. Then, the PPV dies... Goddamn, AT&T cable just cut out again. So after 25 mins of screaming at some operator.... ....Five star Frog Splash by the "Whole F'n Show" Diablos. He rolls KsC trunks up for the pin. Bice slides in 1..2...3! Winner and still XUW Tag Champs, Diablos and Woof! (Backstage Blitzwing is talking to Fluid) Blitz That had to be the best tag match I have ever seen! Fluid No kidding, Jeez, I can't believe KsC and BC, dominated the whole match and still somehow lost! HBK vs Blake Wolfson HBK dances down the ramp with pyros blazing. He doesn't have the Hermit Title with him. Jobber giggles to himself. HBK poses and the crowd throws garbage at him. Blakes music hit and the crowd goes berserk. Blake races to the ring and slides in. HBK meets him while Blake is still on his gut. HBK poses, stomps, poses and hits an elbow drop. HBK then goes for the Figure 4 Leglock. Blake reverses, HBK breaks the hold, HBK tries to walk off the cramp. Blake bounces off the ropes and nails HBK with a flying clothesline. Blake picks him up, and irish whips him into the corner. Blake takes off and nails the running Splash. Blake climbs the corner to the second rope and goes for the ten punch, the crowd counts for Blake, as Blake cannot count. After the "10" Blake Monkey flips HBK out of the corner. Blake goes to the top rope and attempts a cork screw splash. HBK rolls out of the way. Blake lands HARD. HBK picks up Blake, whips him into the ropes, and gives him a Hurricanrana. HBK then hits a Short leg drop. Poses. Blake staggers to his feet, and HBK stomps his foot 3 times, and levels Blake with Sweet Chin Music. HBK rolls Blake up. Takerrocks drops 1...2...Kickout. HBK gets up stunned. "What the...?" HBK grabs Blake, and gives him a Snap Suplex, climbs the ropes, and follows up with a Shooting Star Press. Once again he rolls Blake up. 1...2...3! Winner by pinfall: Heartbreak Kid A.T.: But where is the Hermit title??? My Gawd, what has that bastard done with Severson's title? XUW #1 Contender Submission Gaunlet Entry number 1 is Kata Hajime. Entry number 2 is Ty Copas Referee is Brad Bice Kata comes down the ramp to mixed reactions. He doesn't notice that there is a crowd, as most Submission Wrestlers don't. They cause pain. Ty comes down to a pop. he gets into the ring and plays the crowd. Getting them rallied behind him. Kata sneaks behind him and hits the Kata Hajime. Ty slips out of conscienceness. Bice lifts the arm three times, ty's arm falls three times. WINNER Round one: Kata Hajime. 3rd is X-Factor. X-Factor gets The Amazing Pop...Like TNN pop. X-Factor runs to the ring. Kata catches him with a single arm DDT. Kata applies the cross arm breaker. SNAP! X-factor screams. The paramedics come down and help X-Factor to the back. Brad Bice looks like he is going to be sick. WINNER Round 2 Kata Hajime 4th: DDT runs out to his backwards music (which proclaims that Mr Rodgers is the devil.) DDT catches Kata off guard. DDT goes for the dragon-sleeper, but Kata gets to the ropes, and Bice calls the Break. They circle, get into collar-elbow tie-up. Kata tries the Hammer lock on DDT, but DDT reverses the Hammer with one of his own. Kata reverses this with a snapmare. DDT pulls out a bloody sock, he calls Mr. Sycho and hits the mandible claw. Kata bites down on DDT's fingers. Kata gets to the ropes, Bice calls for the Break. Kata tries the cross Arm-breaker, but this time, DDT reverses it with a Texas Clover leaf. Bice sees Kata's hand poised to tap. Kata reaches behind him and grabs DDT's ankle, pulling, causing him to fall. They both are up, and tie up. Kata slides around and nails the Kata Hajime, DDT slowly passes out. Bice calls it. WINNER Round 3: Kata Hajime LanceStormDBC's Music hits. He is wearing a ref shirt and carries a mic in his hand. Lance:"I think this final battle should be ref'ed by a true Submission Champion. Someone who knows how to cause pain and win Submission matches Kata. Mr. Bice, you are excused." Lance enters the ring just as Silent Bob's music hits. The crowd roars for Bob as he makes his way to the ring. Kata stands poised. A little grin on his face. Bob charges Kata and nails a diving fore-arm smash. Kata springs to his feet and runs at Bob, grabs and gives him a Bulldog. Bob rolls out of the ring and collects his thoughts. Kata smiles at Lance. Lance motions that the match must continue. Bob re-enters the ring, and is caught by Kata's Somoan Drop. Bob rolls away from Kata, this time slightly hurt. Kata follows closely. Kata picks up Bob, and whips Bob with a belly-to-belly over head suplex. Bob gets up slowly. Kata hits him with a cross body block. Kata grabs his ribs, showing pain on his face. Kata then put's an Ankle lock on Bob, Bob crawls to the ropes, and Lance calls the Break. Kata says:"This ends Now." And gets Bob into the Kata Hajime. Lance is distracted by Naron who has appeared at ringside. Bob has his power shut down, and is unconcious. Kata looks for Lance, releases the hold and begins to make choice comment at Lance. Lance is still yelling at Naron. Kata comes over and sees Naron has a smile on her face. Bob shakes out the cobwebs. He approaches Kata, and hits the crossface-chicken wing. Kata steps backwards passing out. Lance calls it. Winner of the Submission Gauntlet, and #1 Contender: Silent Bob. Big Daddy comes down to the ring. He is booed and called A**hole. He gets a mic from Jobber. "BLITZ! you've messed with me and messed with me. You had me on the card, then like the little bitch you are, you go and throw my career down the toilet because some freak tried to escape from an institution. This is a pile of B.S.! Anyone want to take me? Anyone dare? I'll take on whoever has the sack to face me!" A battleship appears on the UltiTron, "Crazy Train" Plays. Admiral John and Toto appear from behind the curtain. "We'll take you on Big Butt! I mean Daddy." says John. Toto screams. "The underused can still Abuse!" They take off running down the ramp. Both hit flying forarms on B.D. Daddy staggers, but remians on his feet. Then the Old Schooler's give him a double kick to the gut, and go for a tandem suplex. Big Daddies 450lbs prevents this and B.D. reverses and suplexes them both. Big Daddy picks them up. Grabs one in each hand, and gives them a simultanious spinning choke slam. He puts The Admiral on top of Toto, for the double pin. Shooter counts the 1...2...3. Winner of this match, Big Daddy by pinfall. Don't hurt me, I'm just an announcer...hey...ow...
