XUW Mayhem - Monday, July 12, 2004 XUW MAYHEM - JULY 12 2004 BAM! POW! ZAP! FLASH! OTHER NOISES! Yes… ladies and gentlemen… boys and girls… animals and alien beings composed of pure energy, it's the moment you've all been waiting for… THE FIRST XUW SHOW SINCE BIG BRAWL THREE! Oh yes. Oh yes indeed. The fans are in attendance, with their signs and their trademark vacant stares, as they wait for the federation known as Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling to fill their minds with moral guidance… and shiny things. Mmmm… shiny things. Fade to black... ...but that's not quite all for the night... A Final Farewell The scene opens in the locker room to find a man who over his time here in the XUW has changed. Once nothing more than a pawn in another man's game of destruction, he matured into his own man – a man who took his own pleasure in the pain of others. He never expected to achieve the greatness that he did…he never expected to be a man who made the history books. XUDF The first US champion… North American Champion He slowly opens his locker, he begins to remove his personal belongings… XUW The first from the XUDF to hold a title… Defeating Dante, he has been hardcore champion… Alongside a man with an equal thirst for violence, he became tag team champion, defeating long time rivals the Lunchroom Patrol He even became world champion, defeating Fatbadger as well as his one time tag team partner, The Shadow… He shrugs, recalling these great times, but they weren't all filled with such violence, some were filled with fun…he removes the hood which he has worn more times than not during his tenure in the XUW/XUDF He thinks back to the time spent when he lost his memory…the time when he was allowed that twinge of happiness… He thinks back to the Deadly Draw victory…he thinks back to the commradery formed with the Lunchroom Patrol's own Latch Key Kid…a man with whom he has shared a love/hate relationship…more times than not focusing on the latter, not the former. He smiles, almost all the memories he has of this place are good in some way or other… He smiles thinking back to the events of a recent evening, doing battle with a man who he should have wanted to destroy, doing battle with a man who destroyed one of the few friends he had while he was here. He was not a friend of convenience, he was instead a friend just for friend's sake… He smiles… It would seem that somehow, he has found a way to be happy away from the ring, away from the madness, away from the violence, away from all that has kept him going all this time…He has found a way to no longer be defined by the actions in the ring, but instead by what he does outside of it. He packs the final bits and pieces of his things, there was hardly anything there to begin with… He takes a deep breath in and he closes the locker door. Turning to his left, he takes a few steps forward finding a mirror. For the first time in a while, he looks at himself in the mirror, no mask, nothing hiding his identity. People have seen him like this for a little while now, as he removed his mask in a fit of rage, they were able to see the monster for who he truly was. They were able to see within the darkness, they looked in his eyes, they saw the madness… The scars on the outside of his body may never go away, they are in fact a part of who he is, each one a part of the story that is his… The scars on the inside of his body have begun to heal. He looks in the mirror, somewhere the hate has faded, somewhere the eyes have found a way to hold a bit less hate, somewhere he has become the man his parents always thought he could be… For the first time, he looks in the mirror and doesn't see a monster…he just sees himself. It is finally gone as he turns and walks out the door…the desire to hurt people, the desire to cause pain and suffering to anyone who crosses his path. It has been replaced with an acceptance of who he is, who he has become, and the potential of who he can still be. No more will anyone's pain be his pleasure… The lights are turned off in the locker room as he walks out – the walk down memory lane is over – the walk to make new memories has begun…
