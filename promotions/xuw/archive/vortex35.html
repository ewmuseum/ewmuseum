XUW Vortex #35

19 Sep 2002

XUW Vortex The camera pans round the arena in hushed silence, and then the Vortex theme plays, and they go banana in anticipation of the excellent Vortex show in store for them, or at least they hope so! The pyrotechnics fly, fly, fly and sends sparks into the air, causing all the girls in the crowd to go “Ooooooh, pretty”, and all the feminists to get upset about it. We see signs, oh yes, we see signs in the arena. “Kore will feel the REIGN OF PAIN” “Pete is gonna tap!” “Bob is 100 years old” “Are the New Age Horsemen a bunch of Hippies?” The camera then decides to get on with the damn show, and spirals down to the announce booth like a demented ballerina. Atomic Tomato: “HELLO! Welcome to yet another edition of XUW Vortex, and we have a stacked show for you tonight, with three titles on the line, and all the members of XUW’s newest stable, the New Age Horsemen, in action.” Barry Johnson: “Shut it, fruit-boy. Welcome to Vortex, I’m ‘The Majestic’ Barry Johnson and I know for a fact that the New Age Horsemen will lose every match tonight, as they face superior athletes in every respect.” AT: “Nothing to do with your accumulator bet on Henrik, the BDA and NFM?” BJ: “I told you to shut it, now let’s have some action. ACTION, dammit!” “Lounge Act” plays, and Henrik The King comes down to the ring, looking mean and ready for action. He gets a fair degree of heat from the crowd, his return to action not changing the crowd’s opinion of him. There is a brief pause, and then Green Day’s “Jackass” plays, heralding the entrance of Kore, the XUW Television Champion. The crowd pops for him, and Kore seems to enjoy his newfound popularity. XUW TV Title Match: Henrik The King v Kore © AT: “Kore’s certainly been one of the most dominant TV Champions we’ve seen here in XUW. He seems well on the way to challenging for the longest reign so far for the title.” BJ: “That was before he faced Henrik, the most deadly submission expert in the history of XUW!” Henrik and Kore lock up, and Kore starts to push Henrik back to the ropes, then whipping him to the far side and hitting a Swinging Neckbreaker. Kore tries for a Piledriver, but Henrik powers out into a Back Body Drop. AT: “Impressive strength from Henrik there, he’s giving up a lot of height and weight in this match. He’ll have to use his speed and quickness to his advantage if he wants to make an impact.” Kore gets to his feet, but Henrik is on him in an instant. Henrik hits a Snap Suplex and goes for an early cover, but Kore kicks out at one. Henrik grabs Kore and hits a series of left and hand hands, a combination of punches that sends Kore back to the mat. Kore gets up and ducks a kick, grabbing Henrik to deliver the Royal Flush (Flapjack). Henrik gets up but walks into a kick to the gut and a Piledriver. Kore goes for a cover, hooking Henrik’s leg. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Henrik gets back to his feet and rakes the eyes of Kore. The ref admonishes him, but he shrugs it off and goes on the offensive, with a stiff kick to the chest and then a Dropkick. Kore is staggered, and Henrik goes up top, jumping off the top turnbuckle and grabbing Kore on the way down with the London Calling (Top Rope Bulldog). Kore is down and Henrik uses this opportunity to lock in the Four Leaf Clover (Texas Cloverleaf). AT: “Good God, that’s the move he used to make many a man submit!” BJ: “He wasn’t the Submission Champion three times for nothing. Kore has no chance now. No chance.” Kore screams from the pain, but doesn’t tap … yet. He works his way forwards and manages to grab hold of the ropes, forcing the ref to break the hold up. Henrik releases it and waits for Kore to get back up, stalking him from behind. Kore gets to his feet and Henrik attacks him, looking to lock in the Stratford Strangle (Million Dollar Dream), but Kore is wise to it and drops to the floor, hitting a Stunner type move. Henrik rolls over on to his front, and Kore decides that it’s time to go for the aerial attack angle, jumping up to the top rope and launching himself off with the Peaked Point (Frog Splash), hitting down hard on to the lower back of Henrik. Kore rolls the former Commonwealth leader on to his back and goes for a cover. 1 … 2 … SHOULDER UP! AT: “Some bad things have been said about Kore and his past, but he’s been in great form of late, and he can’t be seen as anything other than a tough competitor and a good champion.” Kore sets up Henrik for the Snake Eyes, but Henrik rolls up Kore into a Small Package. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Kore looks frustrated by the pinning attempt and goes for a Clothesline, but Henrik ducks and hits a Drop Toe Hold to drop Kore once more. Henrik drops a few elbows to the back of Kore’s head, and then locks in a Full Nelson on the ground. Kore manages to spring up using his legs, and float over, placing Henrik in a pinning predicament. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Henrik gets up quicker than Kore, and hits a hard right hand that knock the champion back to the mat. Kore gets up and Henrik grabs him from behind, ducking down and hitting a Low Blow that the ref doesn’t see. AT: “Right in the Crown Jewels! Henrik is looking more vicious since he returned to XUW, there’s a real fire in his eyes here.” Henrik hits a Dropkick that staggers Kore, and then runs off the ropes and hits a knee to the guts. Kore bends over, and Henrik hits a vicious elbow shot to the back of Kore’s head. Kore falls down on to the mat, and rolls flat on to his back. Henrik looks down at him and then goes up to the top rope and comes off, nailing the Kingpin! He hooks the leg. 1 … 2 … 3!!!!! Winner, by pinfall, and NEW XUW TV Champion – Henrik The King!!!!! AT: “Wow, that was an impressive display at the end there by Henrik, dominating the bigger man and starting his ‘Reign of Pain’!” BJ: “I told you, those Horsemen are gonna lose everything tonight!” AT: “Even with that loss they have two more matches, and I can’t help but that think we’re going to be seeing more of Kore soon, he’s been one hell of a champion, and has really made people and sit up and take notice of him. He could go far in this fed.” BJ: “Bah, shut up about him, look at Henrik, he’s the champion!” “Lounge Act” plays as the two men go to the back, Henrik first and then Kore. AT: “Well, I hear we have some cameras backstage, with the Usual Suspects!” Scene cuts to backstage, where we see Bob and M V Pete in the locker room of the Usual Suspects, preparing for Pete's big Title Match later on tonight. Bob: "You've trained hard for this, Pete. You can beat his fat, dark ass." Pete just paces round the room, impatient to start the match. Bob: "He's a damn tough competitor, but if he tries to cheat you out of that gold, then don't let him. For every dirty trick he pulls, pull one right back. No Mr Nice Guy." Pete looks at Bob and smiles. Pete: "I've come too far now, I will join him in the darkness and make him feel pain." Bob smiles right back at Pete. Bob: "That's it. Remember you're a Suspect, and we don't take s*** from anyone." Camera cuts back to the announce desk. AT: "Wow, Pete and Bob look focused on getting that title from The Ripper, and they will stop at nothing to accomplish their goal!" BJ: “It might be the only way to defeat the darkness. We can’t be sure though, as it doesn’t happen often enough to form much of a trend.” AT: “One man who overcame the darkness last week was Raven X, the new XUW Hardcore Champion. He has a match later on, but before that we have tag team action, pitting the beat Down Authority against the New Age Horsemen.” “Here We Go (Let’s Rock ‘N’ Roll)” plays, and Bonecrusher comes to the ring, followed by Azrael, and Admiral John, their manager. “Krwlng” hits the speakers, and the crowd is on their feet for the ever-popular Sakie and Supa Thug Esco. The beat Down Authority enter the ring, and the teams decide that it will be Azrael and Sakie starting the match together. He goes for a cover. 1 … 2 … NO! Bonecrusher enters the ring and breaks up the pin. Sakie gets up immediately, but BoCru levels him with a Jumping Sidekick. Esco hits the canvas, and Bonecrusher goes to the top turnbuckle, and as Esco gets to his fee the big man flies off and hits a Moonsault Body Block, slamming Esco back down to the mat. AT: “Every time I see Bonecrusher I am more and more impressed with his performances, and the way he can still play the high-flyer after all these years.” Bonecrusher tags Azrael back in, and Azrael hits the Blade Of Fire on Esco (Clothesline From Hell). He covers Esco. 1 … 2 … KICKOUT! Azrael is right back on the attack, nailing Esco with a Backbreaker, and then lifting him up into a Torture Rack. Azrael dives in and makes the save with an Elbow Drop to the back of Sakie’s head. He follows it up by nailing Sakie with the Demon’s Wings (Pedigree)! Azrael rolls BoCru on top of Sakie and the ref counts it. 1 … 2 … SHOULDER UP! Azrael is shocked, and goes back outside, calling for the tag. Esco is doing likewise on the other side as Sakie gets back to his feet. He makes it to tag in Esco as BoCru gets to his feet. Esco charges him, but Bonecrusher hits a Spinebuster. AT: “He’s going for the aerial attack again!” Bonecrusher perches atop the turnbuckle and dives off with the gravity defying Off-Ramp Hurricanrana, flipping Esco around like a rag doll. Bonecrusher goes back up one more time and flies off, hitting the great impact of the Big Enforcer Elbowdrop! Jack ducks away from a big right hand, hits the ropes and nearly takes Pete’s head off with a Big Boot of his own. Jack looks down at Pete on the floor, and waits for him to rise. As he does so, Jack grabs him and hits The Break (Gut Wrench into a Back Breaker). He holds Pete in the Back Breaker position, then lifts him up and hits The Break once more. Pete is straining to escape, but Jack hits The Break for a third successive time, and pushes down, trying to snap Pete in half. Pete is in obvious pain, but manages to use his right arm to lay in some punches to the side of The Ripper. Jack drops Pete to the floor and walks off a little way, rubbing his side. The pain soon forgotten, Jack approaches Pete once more, and picks him up, setting him in place for a Suplex type move, but MVP blocks it and hits a Suplex of his own. As Jack gets up Pete grabs him and uses incredible strength to put him on his shoulders, and deliver the Bricktown Bulldog (Lesnar’s F-5)! As his opponent is down, Pete tries to lock on the Junkyard Stretch (Crippler Crossface), but Jack struggles out of it and makes the ropes. AT: “That Junkyard Stretch is a deadly move. If he gets it fully locked in then it’s pretty much game over.” Both men get back to a vertical base, and circle round the ring, looking for openings. AT: “The Ripper took this match very seriously indeed, and it looks as though he had good reason to, for Pete is matching him in the ring at the moment. We could even be in for a bit of an upset.” The two men lock up for the umpteenth time, with Jack once more forcing Pete back into a corner, but this time instead of breaking the hold he kicks Pete hard, and sets him up on the top turnbuckle. He follows Pete up and comes off with a big Superplex. Jack scoots over to the body of the fallen Suspect and locks in a grounded Sleeper Hold. He grinds it in to the neck and throat of Pete, and he seems to weaken almost immediately. The ref checks Pete’s arm to see if it drops. It drops… Once… Twice… BJ: “Three times a lady!” Thr… NO! Pete comes back to life, as if by magic, and fights back up to his knees. AT: “Great fighting by M V Pete! Terrible commentary by Barry Johnson!” Pete is drawing strength from the crowd, which is going crazy go nuts. He gets back to his feet and hits the chest of The Ripper with a series of elbows, forcing the big man to release the hold. Pete slaps Jack round the face, hard, and then hits him with the Prime Time Chokeslam! Jack is down and Pete locks in the Walls of Brooklyn (Jericho)! Jack is in pain, and far from the ropes! AT: “That’s another tough move from Pete, his training is paying huge dividends here!” Jack slowly moves towards the ropes, but as he reaches them Pete pulls back harder, and then drags him back to the middle of the ring. The ref asks Jack if he wishes to give in again, and Jack’s hand hovers above the mat, but he grits his teeth and pushes upwards to relieve the pressure. His face grows more determined, and he moves forwards, grabbing the bottom rope. The ref tells Pete to release the hold, but he waits a full four seconds before he does so. Jack’s legs drop to the ground and he grips the ropes and tries to get back to his feet. Pete is relentless, running off the far set of ropes and hitting a Big Clothesline as Jack turns round, sending them both over the top rope. Jack falls to the ground, but Pete hits the apron hard on the way down. Both men remain on the floor as the ref begins to count them both out. 1 … 2 … 3 … Jack stirs, and sits up. 4 … Pete uses the guard rail to get to his knees. 5 … 6 … 7 … Jack throws Pete back first into the ring apron, and then hits him hard in the face with a Forearm. 8 … Jack enters the ring. 9… Pete half climbs and half drops into the ring, stopping the count just before the end. Jack is on him in an instant and picks him up, draping him over his shoulders and then hitting the incredible impact of the Call of Destiny (Bob Holly’s Alabamaslam). Pete bounces off the canvas hard, and lays there, crumpled up. AT: “One move. One move is all it takes for The Ripper to go from being dominated to being right back in control.” BJ: “He’s probably the most dangerous man in XUW, and Pete is finding that out the hard way.” AT: “I think Pete already knew that, he’s been preparing for this match his whole time here in XUW.” Jack picks up Pete and hits the Event Horizon Jackhammer, sending Pete down on his back incredibly hard one more time. Jack smiles at the fallen Suspect and slides outside to the floor, where h roots around under the ring and finds a piece of lead piping. AT: “Ref! Stop him!” Jack slips the pipe in the back of his waistband, and re-enters the ring. The myopic referee has seen nothing, and Jack picks up M V Pete, hitting a DDT and looking to lock in The Fear. Pete has enough energy left to get to the ropes though, and the ref breaks up the hold. AT: “Hey look! It’s Samurai Bob coming down to the ring! He must have seen jack with that damn lead piping!” BJ: “The ref’s coming to stop him getting in the ring though!” The ref is distracted, and Jack looks over to see Bob being prevented from entering the ring. He turns and waits for M V Pete to get up, and then swings the lead pipe like a hammer blow at Pete’s head… but Pete dodges out of the way and takes Jack down with a Facebuster. The pipe rolls out of the ring and M V Pete locks in the Junk Yard Stretch, this time getting it fully locked in, right in the middle of the ring! Jack grimaces in pain, trying to move to the side and reach the rope with his free arm, but Pete has it locked in tight. He tries to move, and tries to move, and tries some more, but Pete is like an animal, tearing at Jack’s arm and shoulder. Jack raises his left arm a little of the mat… and taps out!!! AT: “Jack tapped! Jack tapped! New Champion! New Champion!” BJ: “Wait, the ref is still trying to get Bob to leave the ring, he hasn’t seen it!” AT: “Dammit! Jack tapped out!” BJ: “Nonsense, he was … ummmmm …. Testing out the acoustics of the canvas.” Jack struggles further towards the ropes, pain etched into his face, and his arm finally reaches the ropes, just as the ref turns round. The ref immediately breaks the hold, as the crowd hurls a ton and half of abuse at him. AT: “M V Pete won the damn match!” BJ: “He still might, Jack is in trouble, his arm nearly got ripped from his body!” Pete goes on the offensive, laying in blow after blow to the injured arm of The Ripper. Jack flinches off the attack of Pete and tries to back up into a corner. Pete continues to assault him, until Jack reaches up with his good arm and rakes Pete right in the eyes. Pete stumbles back and Jack launches himself with a Spear born of desperation. It take Pete down with him, and Jack rolls back to his feet, glaring down at Bob with a smile on his face, and holding his arm, then shaking it out. Pete gets back up, but Jack is right on him, and hits a kick for the gut, then thrusting the head of the Suspect underneath him, lifting him up, and hitting the inevitable… AT: “RipSaw! RipSaw!” BJ: “Jack hit the RipSaw! Now the mood is changing!” Pete lies half entangled in the ropes, but Jack just flips him over and locks in The Fear (Steiner Recliner). He wrenches back on it, and unbearable pain shoots through the battered body of M V Pete. He still refuses to tap, and then Jack pulls back even harder. AT: “Pete’s feet are actually by the ropes there, now if only he can hook them back he can break the hold.” BJ: “It’s his only possible way out!” Jack pulls back even harder on the move, and Pete somehow manages to last long enough to get his foot on to the ropes. The ref is too concerned with checking if Pete wants to give up, and Jack wrenches back even harder than before, the ref asks Pete if he needs to submit one more time, and Pete has no option but to agree. The ref calls for the bell, and Jack throws the body of M V Pete to the floor. Winner, by submission, and STILL XUW Submission Champion – Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy AT: “NO! Dammit! Pete got screwed again, he had a foot on the ropes!” BJ: “It doesn’t matter, The Ripper did what was needed to get the win!” AT: “Pete looks broken in half in the ring there, but Jack doesn’t look much better either!” Bob slides into the ring as Jack leaves between the ropes. He lifts up his XUW Submission Title as “One Armed Scissor” plays, and makes his way to the back, leaving Pete and Bob in the ring. AT: “What a show! One new champion, and four classic matches.” BJ: “I can assure you, for Pete, Bob and The Ripper, this is far, FAR from over!” Fade to Black
