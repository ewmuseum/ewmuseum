XUW Mayhem #13

10 Dec 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Welcome to Mayhem, as we are now 1 week removed from December to Dismember! Tonight we have a card that is…well, no one knows that tonight’s card is except that D will be defending his IC title against both Admiral John AND Severson. AT: Tonight, we have a completely unknown card, and HBK has yet to arrive in the…wait a minute…we are getting word that…cut to the back! We cut to the back where HBK is arriving, clipboard in hand, in a new Armani suit. His music hits and the camera follows him to the ring, where grabs a mic. HBK: Stop my music! Tonight, we have a DOUBLE main event. Lopen asked me for a main event so I made one. Tonight, we will see Lopen v. Samuari Bob for the World title. But that’s not all folks! Kata will be the referee for that match, let’s hope that they are ready! Also tonight, the submission champ will be defending his title against The ripper. Also, those little ponies have been a thorn in my side for a long time, and tonight, I have a surprise for them. Technicians, you boys will be facing none other than…Hodgo and Sidhe, the riot squad for your tag team titles. I will PERSONALLY be refereeing this match to make sure that everything goes well. Tonight will truly be a night to remember, my first Mayhem as the new commiss… The music of the horsemen hits and the three remaining horsemen step onto the stage, Admiral John has the mic. Admiral John: Tonight ladies and gentlemen, and HBK, whatever the hell you are, we will introduce the newest member of the horsemen. He has quite a history, and I am sure you will all not only be surprised, but quite happy as you see our replacement for D…and speaking of D, later tonight, I will take that IC title from you. We were partners, and you threw it away just because you were a little pissed off. Tonight, I avenge the name of the horsemen, and tonight…we will become the #1 force in wrestling again! All three men hold up 4 fingers and we go back to the ring, where HBK has a very angry look. He turns around and gets speared by D who came from under the ring! Huge crowd Pop as HBK is laid out and the horsemen stare that their former leader from the stage. The horsemen walk to the back as D leaves through the crowd… AT: What a way to start off mayhem! Tonight a double main event seeing the IC title and world title defended! What a night! MM: What a night indeed! Tonight we will see a new IC champion, a new submission champion, and new tag team champions, I GUARANTEE IT! We go to the ring where DDT is in the ring getting ready for his hardcore title defense, but against who? Then the music hits and out walks…CEREAL KILLER! Match 1: Hardcore title, CK v. DDT Cereal killer walks to the ring with a shopping cart full of goodies and he throws them into the ring. CK dives into the ring and grabs a stop sign. He runs at DDT with the sign over his head, but DDT kicks CK in the stomach. CK drops the sign and DDT begins to beat on CK. DDT picks up a garbage can and puts it over CK’s head. DDT grabs a chair and smacks CK on the head and the garbage can. CK throws the garbage can off his head and grabs his ears as they are ringing like mad. DDT picks up…a hamster wheel? He throws the hamster wheel at CK and hits him in the head. He throws CK into the corner and beats him down. DDT gets out of the ring and brings a table in. He sets up the table in the middle of the ring and goes back over to CK. Ck has regained himself though and he low blows DDT as he comes over to him. CK begins to beat down DDT and turns him into the corner. CK puts DDT on the top rope and sets him up. CK hits a hurriconranna (sp?) on DDT onto the table. CK quickly crawls over to make the cover…1…2…NO! DDT kicked out! CK is amazed and he goes over and grabs a guitar. He swings the guitar and lays out DDT. He throws DDT out of the ring and begins to beat him down. CK looks under the ring and finds a ladder. CK puts the ladder in the ring and sets it up. CK climbs to the top of the ladder and he stands on top. DDT on the outside gets to his feet. CK takes a huge dive off the top of the ladder to the outside, but DDT moves out of the way and as he is moving, he swings a chair and nails CK in the face as the crowd starts a HOLY S*** chant. DDT quickly falls on top of CK and goes for the pin and the Shooter counts…1…2…3! Winner and STILL hardcore champion: DDT AT: Holy s***! Did you see that jump! That was without a doubt the craziest thing I have seen anyone in the hardcore division do! MM: That was NUTS! AT: What will happen next??!! A limo pulls up outside the arena, and the license place says 4 hrsmn on the front and 4ever on the back. AJ, BC, and KsC all walk out to greet the man in the limo, but he doesn’t get out. BC sees the cameraman and walks over and takes the camera and turns it off as we go back to the ring where Doggy style is sitting and waiting for their opponents. The flashtastics’ music hits and they run to the ring, ready to go, as they have taken up Doggy style’s challenge! Match 2: Doggy Style w/ Sherri v. the flashtastics Woof and Flash start the match off by locking up. Woof body slams flash and bounces off the ropes and hits a quick leg drop. He tags in Blake, who comes in and they both pick up Flash. They whip him into the ropes and do a quick double lariat, and follow it up by doing a double leg drop on them. Blake picks up flash and throws him into the ropes and he hits a big back body drop. Blake turns and he goes to pick up Flash, but Flash kicks Blake in the head and Blake is stunned. Flash begins to hit Blake with rights, then he kicks him in the stomach and puts him in a side head lock. Flash whips Blake into the ropes and hits the mat, and Blake jumps over him. Flash then leapfrogs Blake and falls onto his back. Flash uses his feet to catapult Blake clear out of the ring. Flash gets up and he tags Flex. Flex climbs to the top rope in his own corner, as Flash turns and nails Woof onto the ground. Flex dives off the top rope onto Blake on the outside. Flex and Blake are both laid out on the outside. Sherri gets on the top rope and hits a hurricanranna on Flash in the ring. Sherri dives to the outside before Mexican can see. On the outside, Flex has gotten up and he throws Blake into the ring. Flex gets on the top rope again and goes for a senton bomb, but Sherri pushes him off the top rope and Blake moves out of the way, and Mexican doesn’t see because he is checking on why Flash is still in the ring. Blake gets up and he locks the Code on Flex who begins to tap franticly, but Mexican is still checking on Flash. Blake let’s go and is really mad at Mexican for not paying attention. Blake gets into an argument with Mexican and Flex rolls him up from behind…1…2…NO! Blake kicks out. Flex gets up and he kicks Blake in the stomach and he hits a big powerbomb, but it took a lot out of Flex too. Both men are tired and they begin to crawl to their corners. Blake gets the tag to woof as Flex gets the tag to Flash. Woof comes in and lays out Flash, but turns around into a lariat from Flex. Flex crawls out of the ring and Flash picks up woof. Flash throws Woof into the ropes and hits a go-around DDT (a-la the rock). He goes for the pin…1…2…NO! Blake breaks it up. Flex gets up and runs at Blake and gives him a lariat and they both go over the top rope. Flash gets up on the inside and woof hits the woofinator (angle slam) and goes for the pin and Flex dives in to stop it, but Blake stops him…1…2…3! Winners: Doggy Style AT: This has to move Doggy style up the ladder when it comes to those tag titles! MM: Yes, but the technicians have the Riot Squad to worry about tonight! The music of the horsemen hits as the Technicians walk to the ring to a loud pop. Riot squad walks down to some heat. Match 3: Riot squad v. Technicians (tag team title) Before the Riot Squad can get to the ring, they are jumped from behind by the hurristoners. Riot Squad and Hurristoners are beating each other down as the Technicians in the ring stare and laugh. Behind them, out of nowhere, Blake runs up and slaps the Code on KsC and Woof woof jumps BC. A brawl erupts in the ring, and the flashtastics run to ringside and they begin to brawl with everyone in the ring. 3XL run out next and now the hurristoners and the riot squad have spilled into the ring. HBK’s music hits and he runs onto the ramp screaming HBK: HEY HEY HEY HEY HEY! HBK holds up a kitten and a gun and begins to scream again… HBK: KEEP FIGHTING AND THE KITTEN GETS IT! Everyone in the ring stops dead… HBK (pulling the trigger on the gun and a flag pops out that says bang): Did you really think I would shoot a kitten?! Jesus, how sick do you think I am! Listen, you all want that tag team title so damn bad, okay. Next week on Mayhem, all of you will be in a battle royal. The last two men remaining will be the tag team champions! If KsC and Sidhe are left, they are tag team champs! Whatever happens happens…now hit MY music! The brawl in the ring begins again as an army of security and refs run to the ring. HBK shrugs them off and walks to the back petting the kitten (aww…he has a soft side ) AT: Wow! So much can happen at that match, you never know what will go down! Meanwhile…the bell rings? HBK runs back to the ring with a referee shirt on. KsC and BC are walking to the back when Sidhe and hodgo jump them and throw them into the ring. The match is finally underway. Sidhe begins by whipping BC into the ropes and hitting a big lariat. He picks BC up and holds him by the arm and tags in Hodgo who jumps to the top rope and does a double ax handle onto BC’s arm. BC falls to the mat and Hodgo picks him up. Hodgo whips BC into the corner and follows it in with a big lariat. He whips BC to the other corner and goes for the lariat again, but this time BC moves and both men are down, from the match and the brutal brawl they were just in, both men are tired. They both crawl to their corners, and Hodgo tags in Sidhe, and BC hot tags KsC who comes in on fire. KsC cleans house and takes Hodgo out of the ring completely. BC gets up and they both pick up Sidhe and whip him to the ropes. They hit a double backdrop and then pick Sidhe back up and hit a double DDT. BC gets up and picks up Sidhe like for a spine buster and KsC goes off the ropes, but is tripped and pulled out by Hodgo. Hodgo then dives into the ring and dropkicks Sidhe in the back, and sidhe falls on top of KsC and HBK makes a fast count…1…2…3! Winners and NEW tag team champions: Riot Squad KsC and BC are in the ring in disbelief as the riot squad runs to the back… AT: Wow! I think the Riot Squad stole that one! MM: Well, next week, I think we will have at least one new tag champ, so the riot squad had better not hold their breath about keeping those belts. AT: Our next match was made by HBK at the beginning. It is Henrik v. Jack for the submission title! Henrick walks to the ring with naron and his title, shining it. Jack walks to the ring to massive boos. Match 4: Jack v. Henrick for the submission title Jack and Henrick lock up and Henrick kicks jack in the stomach. He whips him into the ropes and hits a back body drop. He immediately follows it up by putting Jack into an arm bar. He wrench’s back on Jack’s arm and Jack finally reaches the ropes. Jack gets up and shakes his arm out and they lock up again. Jack goes for a quick roll up, then remembers he is in a submission match and lets go. Henrick steps back and laughs at jack, who drop kicks Henrick out of the ring. Jack looks really pist off because he was laughed at, so he climbs to the top rope and dives off into Henrick. He throws Henrick into the ring and then grabs a chair. Mexican tries to take the chair away by grabbing it, but Jack pulls back and Mexican’s arms fall off. Mexican screams for the bell… Winner: Henrick via DQ Jack then waffles Henrick with the chair. He opens the chair and puts Henricks arm in it and closes it back up. Jack runs off the ropes and stomps on the chair and Henrick begins to scream. Jack gets a sadistic smile and goes off the ropes again, but Naron dives into the ring and covers Henrick. Jack looks down at her and blows her a kiss, then leaves the ring as the paramedics run in to check on the condition of Henrick, who is clutching his arm. AT: Damn! I think henrick’s arm is broken! MM: I would think so! That was so vile, so mean, so evil…GOD I LOVE IT! AT: Shut up…Now we have one of what could be THREE main events tonight! The first will be D v. Severson v. Admiral John for the IC title! D walks to the ring first, to a mixed reaction. Severson comes out to big heat and Admiral john comes out… Before AJ reaches the ring, Derek Jackson jumps him on the stage. Derek begins to beat down Admiral John and goes to throw him from the stage. Admiral john reverses it though and DJ goes flying from the stage and through some tables. Just then HBK’s music hits… HBK: Do you think you can just get away with that! You are out of this match! This will be 1 on 1 now! You did this to yourself! Admiral John (ripping the mic from HBK’s hands): That’s okay. I got this covered. You will get yours by the end of the night…don’t worry! Admiral john walks to the back as the Severson in the ring jumps D from behind as the bell rings. Match 5: D26 v. Severson: IC title Sev gets the quick upper hand by jumping D from behind. Big Sev beats down D and he picks up D over his head and guerrilla press slams him to the mat. Sev bounces off the ropes and hits a big splash on D and goes for the pin…1…2…NO! D26 kicks out and Sev picks him back up. Sev whips D into the ropes and goes for a back body drop, but D jumps over him. When Sev turns around he gets a big spear from D26. D gets up and climbs to the top rope and goes for the devil drop, but Sev gets his knees up and D is now laid out from landing on Sev’s knees. Sev picks up D and throws him out of the ring and follows suit. Sev puts D onto the announce table and gets on top himself. Sev grabs D around the neck with both hands and picks him up to baldo bomb him through the table. D swings his legs and kicks Sev in the groin and Sev puts D down. D grabs Sev by the Head and DDT’s Sev through the table. D picks Sev up and throws him into the steel steps, and the steps go flying into the third row, hitting some vendor in the head. D grabs Severson and goes to throw him into the steel pole, but Sev reverses it and D hits the pole with massive force. D hits the ground and is now busted open. Sev grabs the ring bell and when D gets up, he is nailed with the bell. Mexican calls for the bell, but no one can ring it, so the match continues. AT: Damn that Severson! MM: Heheh…good thing I am time keeper, ehh?? (MM begins to laugh) Severson throws D into the ring and hands the bell to Mr. Mikey and rolls back in the ring. Sev picks up D and throws him into the corner. Sev goes for a spear, but D jumps to avoid it. D puts his legs under Sev’s arms and rolls him over into a pinning combination…1…2…3! Mr. Mikey refuses to ring the bell, so AT taps him on the shoulder and when MM is distracted, he grabs the hammer and rings the bell for him… Winner and still IC champ: D26 AT: Take that! Haahaahaa! MM: You think that is funny? AT: What, you gonna make me kiss your ass! MM: No…we do have more class than that But we will get the last laugh! AT: Our next match is for the world title. Leave it to HBK to make his own match the main event! MM: My real question is where is this surprise Admiral john of the horsemen promised…and who was in that Limo? AT: Maybe we’ll find out now… We cut to the back where the horsemen go to the limo and open the door. A man gets out in a suit with his back to the cameraman. He points behind his back to the cameraman and Admiral john walks over to him. He kicks the camera man and hits a DDT and the camera falls and breaks and we see static for a while until we go back to AT and MM. AT: Who was that?! MM: I don’t know! Why are you asking me! Bob walks to the ring to the biggest pop of the night, followed by Lopen to a chorus of boos. Kata follows suit with a referee shirt on. match 6: Bob v. Lopen: World title, Kata referee Bob and Kata start by having some words and Kata looks over at Lopen and winks. Bob gets a confused look on his face. Lopen runs at Bob, but bob moves and Lopen hits the corner. Bob locks on the skid mark chicken wing right away and Lopen desperately reaches for the ropes. He finally gets to them and Kata makes Bob break the hold. Bob gets up pist off and gets in Kata’s face. Kata just smiles and Lopen turns bob around into a lopen Cutter and goes for the pin…1…2…NO! Bob kicked out of the Lopen Cutter! Lopen goes to argue with kata, who once again just smiles and stands there as Bob low blows Lopen. Lopen and Bob begin to brawl, and kata breaks them up. Both men look at Kata annoyed and kata just smiles smugly…Until both men nail him. Both men begin to beat down Kata, then they fight each other as the bell rings franticly… Winner: No contest All three men continue to brawl when HBK’s music hits and he walks to the ring. HBK: Would you guys stop it. (Slaps Lopen on the back and sends him to the back) Now for you two! You two are being quite immature about this whole thing. I want you two to face each other next week where the winner will be the #1 contender…got it? No get out of my ring so my match can begin. Where Is that damn janitor! KoRe walks to the ring in a jump suit and dives in… Match 7: KoRe v. HBK for the commissionership KoRe is holding a plunger and he runs at HBK and begins to beat him with the plunger. HBK is laid out and KoRe turns around and holds up the broken Plunger, and when he turns around, KoRe is hit in the face with a massive Chin music and Mexican counts…1…2…3! Winner: HBK HBK picks up a mic HBK: You aren’t in my league…kid! Go clean my toi… HBK turns and is speared by D. HBK and D begin to fight when the music of the horsemen hits and the horsemen step onto the stage. Admiral John: Meet the NEWEST HORSEMEN! From behind a masked man begins to beat down both HBK and D. He cleans house and gets in the middle of the ring…and rips off his mask to reveal… FLASHTASTIC E AND C! The rest of the horsemen run to the ring and get in with Flashtastic E and C and they smile and raise the sign of the horsemen as we fade to black…
