XUW Card Archive

XUW Mayhem #36

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem XUW programming starts abruptly with The Atomic Tomato and "Mr Majestic" Barry Johnson making their way down to ringside, as the theme from "The A-Team" plays at deafening volume. The crowd pop like party poppers with extra pop in them, and Mayhem is underway! The camera does a quick pan around to take in a few signs, such as "Wolfson smells of goats", "Sidhe Nimh 3'16''" and "We miss Matt Logan". Barry Johnson: "Hello there and welcome to Mayhem!" Atomic Tomato: "We have an exciting line-up of matches here, such as..." BJ: "I'm going to have to interrupt you there, Cucumber!" AT: "Why's that?" BJ: "I just find you annoying..." "Make Me Bad" hits the speakers and Cereal Killer makes his way down to the ring, looking hungry for a fight. "Psalm 69" by Ministry comes over the speakers, and the TV Champion, The Apostle comes down to the ring, and the match gets underway. TV Title match: Cereal Killer v The Apostle (C) Cereal Killer goes on the offensive, hitting a European Uppercut, and then an impressive Spinning Belly-to-Belly Suplex. The Apostle is shaken, but manages to avoid the incoming CK and skip around to the other side of the ring, buying himself some time. CK advances again, and the two men lock up. The Apostle uses his height and weight advantage to push the challenger back, and deliver a hard Gutbuster. The Apostle picks up CK and locks his legs to the top turnbuckle, leaving the challenger hanging. AT: “The Apostle putting Cereal Killer in the Tree of Woe there.” BJ: “Correction, fruit-boy, that’s the Tree of Salvation, you sinner!” Apostle comes in with a Baseball Slide to kick CK hard in the face, and the former champion falls to the ground, clutching his face. Apostle takes the advantage by picking up CK and performing the Genuflection (Flatliner). Apostle hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Apostle gets up, closely followed by CK, and the men lock up again. Apostle whips CK to the ropes, but CK ducks under an attempted Clothesline and bounces off the far ropes, coming back with a hard Forearm Smash. CK then grabs the Apostle and gets him with the Nuclear Winter (Bearhug into Spinebuster). CK covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! The Apostle rolls over to the ropes, and gets to his feet, then exploding forwards to take down CK with a Spear. Apostle wastes no time in picking up CK and whipping him to the ropes, then hitting a Running Neckbreaker. CK goes down hard, and Apostle picks him up one more time, placing him across his shoulders, and then delivering The Redeemer (Rikishi Driver). The Apostle is quickly up to his feet, making an exuberant sign of the cross with his right arm before going down for the pin, however, his flailing arm catches the ref in the eye, and he stumbles off, clutching at it. The Apostle looks concerned, and goes over to apologise to the official, and to comfort him. The ref seems to have lost a contact lens, and The Apostle sets out to help him find it. AT: “Someone should tell him that the match is still on!” Cereal Killer stirs, and grabs The Apostle, rolling him up into a pinning predicament. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winner, and NEW TV Champion: Cereal Killer! AT: "The Apostle was too good for his own good!" BJ: "Does that even make sense?" AT: "Yes it does. Now, as I was going to say before you interrupted me earlier, we have a great show lined up tonight. There's a lot at stake tonight, but still, the buzz around is beyond tonight, at the upcoming Pay Per View, XUW Big Brawl!" BJ: "For once I agree with you, Melon. This will be the centerpiece of the night, culminating in a four on four elimination match as the Main Event, to decide who will be crowned the first ever winners of Stable Wars, and then that team can truly lay claim to being the best stable in XUW!" AT: "By that time they'll be tired, they'll be weary, but they will fight on, two teams going at it, until one team has had all its members submit or be pinned. I can't wait. Who do you have your money on, Barry?" BJ: "Ah well, that's a secret for now. I'll be taking notes throughout the night, looking for strengths and weaknesses. I could make a killing!" AT: “You’ll not be the only one making some sort of killing, if you don’t quieten down!” Before any further toot can be talked, the camera cuts to the street outside of the arena, where Michael Bolling and Lazarus are standing face to face. Bolling: “No rules, no suspensions, nothing out here but us two, Lazarus.” Lazarus: “You only held that TV Title because I wasn’t here to kick your ass!” Bolling starts to push Lazarus, and Laz responds by hitting Bolling with a few stiff right hands. Laz continues with the boxing jabs, and then grabs Bolling, whipping him into a nearby streetlight. Bolling thunders into it and then gets caught in a Full Nelson. The member of F.E.A.R. struggles in the hold, but then lifts a leg backwards and catches Lazarus in the unmentionables. Laz winces and drops to his knees, allowing Bolling to hit him with a Superkick to the head. Laz drops to the floor, and Bolling looks to capitalise, but the Commonwealth member rolls away from him and then hits a Drop Toe Hold, sending Bolling down to the hard concrete sidewalk. Laz gets up, steadying himself on the streetlight, and then whips Bolling into the nearby wall of the arena. Bolling hits the wall, and rolls off it towards the entranceway. Lazarus follows him and hits a Chop Block to the leg, knocking Bolling down again. Lazarus gets on top of him and starts a flurry of blows, before Bolling rolls him over and starts to unload with stiff punches of his own. Bolling gets off of Lazarus and waits for the suspended man to stand up. As Lazarus advances on him, Bolling sneaks up to the arena entrance and darts between big security guards. Lazarus goes to follow him, but is restrained by the guards. Laz: “Hey, let me in!” Guard 1: “You’re still suspended until the end of the month, so you can’t come in.” Laz: “But Bolling’s in there, and I need to kick the hell out of him. His arse is mine!” Guard1: “You’re not allowed in.” Laz curses, and then grabs the first guard, hitting him with a DDT onto the concrete. Just then, Bolling rushes back outside and smacks Lazarus across the head with a heavy sledgehammer! Laz goes down hard, and starts to bleed from the head. Bolling: “Hah! Now who’s the man, eh Lazarus?” Laz tries to get himself up, but can barely move after the huge blow to the head. Just then, Cereal Killer walks past, his TV Title around his waist. Cereal Killer: “Heh heh heh… I am the man, and I have the XUW TV Title! I am the longest standing TV Champion ever!” Bolling turns round, and Laz gets up, both staring daggers at CK. CK starts to run off towards his waiting car, as Bolling and Lazarus give chase. CK just makes it in time and starts to drive off, waving the TV Title out of the window and laughing. Bolling: “CK! This isn’t over! I want to destroy you at XUW Big Brawl! Put your title on the line on Sunday!” Lazarus catches up, his eyes still glazed over from the sledgehammer shot. Laz: “Me too! A three way, at the PPV, for MY title!” CK leans out of the window and nods his head, still grinning, but then gets back in the car to swerve and narrowly miss a streetlight. Cut back to the announce position. AT: “What the hell was that all about?” BJ: “Looks like we have a TV Title defence at the PPV now too!” Suddenly the arena lights go down and the opening chords of "Killing in the name of" strike and the X shaped pyro goes off signalling the imminent arrival of The X Factor into the arena. As if on a magical cue he enters the arena carrying Owen in one hand, a white piece of paper in the other and two chairs under his arm. He reaches the ring, and slides the chairs in before him, then climbing up the steps and between the ropes. Placing Owen in the corner of the ring, he sets the chairs up facing each other in the centre of the ring. He then fetches a table and sets it up between them. X: “Sorry for the wait, but I wanted the scene to be perfect. Ladies and Gentlemen, tonight, on Mayhem, the bracket for Stable Wars is going to be set. As of this point in time, Blake Wolfson is entered. The little bastard thinks he has a chance. But Blakey, I'm here to give to a quick reality check. See around my waist right now, is the Intercontinental Championship belt. This might not be MY belt, but as of this minute, possession is nine tenths of the law. Blake, you want this belt back, you need to get your ass out here right now.” He looks towards the ramp..no Blake X: “I REALLY do suggest you bring your arse out here..or your precious belt will be melted down into a collar for the dogs. This is your final warning. GET OUT HERE NOW.” Still no Blake X: “Right, that's it then, no more IC bel…” Suddenly, interrupting Factor comes Godsmack and "I stand alone". Blake Wolfson, the real Intercontinental Champion, appears at the top of the ramp. In his hand is the puppy, Michael and in the other a microphone. BW: “Just for your information, unlike your bloody dogs, I don't obey your commands. I come out here when I want, not when you want. On the other hand, we both have something the other wants. You must want this mutt back, and I want my IC belt back. So since we're both here, with the object of the others’ desire, I suggest we do it now.” X: “Y'know what Blake that's not a bad idea. It really isn't. But I have one more stipulation to add before we do our little exchange. Blake, you handed me my first defeat. I'm not too thrilled at that. Add to the fact that your feet were on the damn ropes to pin me in the first place, and not only do I feel pissed off but I also feel slightly pissed on. So Blake, if you want this belt back, you have to agree to give me a title shot, this Sunday at the Big Brawl.” BW: “Hey, I'm the one with the puppy...You don't call the shots I do. Now I'm not one to shy away from a match, but this Sunday, at Big Brawl. I'm already involved in a big tournament, you may have heard of it, Stable Wars. This Sunday the smallest stable in xUw is going to win it all...not facing some loser from Liverpool for MY belt.” Factor takes off the belt and lays it on the table, followed by the piece of paper. He smiles smugly X: “Really..you're gonna win it all! Wow...I guess we must all have underestimated the Lone Wolf. But Wolfson, I think you better listen to logic. The way Stable Wars is set out, means you have absolutely NO CHANCE IN HELL of winning. Sure, you may get through the first round this Sunday, especially if you face someone crappy like FEAR or Beat Down Authority, but what if you face the AoD or the Usual Suspects...” BW: “That's OK, cause in the first round, you only have to pin one guy! That’s easy...” X: “True, but what about after that? In the final you have to pin each and every member of the team. What happens then Blake? What if you're drawn against the AoD then? You have to pin DDT, then Sidhe...then the former xUw world Champion, a sadistic son of a bitch called Jack the Ripper. Or The Commonwealth. That's four tough bastards, whoever they choose to compete. There’s Laz, the Tag Champs the Royal Legion, Hodgo and the current Submission champ, and after tonight, possibly the xUw World Champ, Henrik. Hell even Naron might be involved. They only have to pin you once, and you'd be gone. They could lead a four on one attack, it would be perfectly legal, then a simple 3 count from Naron, leaving you pinned once again by a female, and a bloody, battered, and more importantly embarrassed, Intercontinental Champion.” Blake gets a concerned look on his face, he seems to be thinking over what Factor is saying. X: “Think about it Blake. I know, that somewhere in that head of yours you realise that the numbers, and the format of the competition is against you too. Blake, instead of all the pain, all the anguish, all the possibility of embarrassment that this Sunday could hold for you, why not leave it. Leave the Stable Wars to the real stables. Let them kick the crap out of each other. Why not just face me, this Sunday, for the Intercontinental Championship. I have the contract right here. All you have to do is sign it, and we're on. One more time, you and me can dance the dance, kick each other’s arse and make these fans give us a standing ovation. Blake, instead of embarrassment, you could move higher up the card. We could have the match to end all matches. Solidify your position as THE INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPION OF THE WOOOOOOOOOOOOORRRRLD!” BW: “Erm....there isn't an intercontinental champion of the world...Y'see the Intercontinental part already implies the world part...” Factor looks embarrassed X: “Yeah, well...whatever. Come on Blake, just come down here and sit on these chairs and sign the bloody contract.” BW: “Okay. We'll come down there...BUT, you even think about hitting me, and Mikey gets it. This isn't a yes either, I'm just going to read the contract.” X: “Scout’s honour. I wouldn't do anything to put Michael in trouble would I?” Blake makes his way down to the ring, as X Factor paces anxiously around it. Blake cautiously climbs between the ropes, and starts to read. BW: “You know if I DO sign this and not compete in Stable Wars, it doesn't mean I wouldn't have won Stable Wars.” X: “Oh, of course, I mean, you're just out numbered. You'd be doing the smart thing.” BW: “Yeah, and the system is just totally unfair to me. It wouldn't be so much as me leaving Stable Wars, it would me being FORCED out through bad booking concepts on behalf of the booking team!” X: “Exactly.” Blake spends a few minutes reading it, while Factor looks on impatiently X: “Look, are you just going to sign it or not?” BW: “Hey, didn't anyone ever tell you that patience is a virtue.” X: “I think someone tried, but I got bored so didn't listen to him.” Blake takes a pen out of his pocket and signs his first name BW: “HEY! What’s this in the small print?” Factor shifts uncomfortably in his seat X: “Really? Where?” BW: “There, you idiot!” X: “Oh, THAT bit. That’s nothing. It just explains that the match is to take place under Ladder Match rules…” BW: “A ladder match?” The crowd upon hearing this stipulation start to cheer like Pee Wee at a XXX cinema X: “Yes, a ladder match. When you beat me on Mayhem a few weeks ago, you cheated like an Argentinean footballer. You had your feet on the ropes for the pin. I want to take out the risk of you cheating this time. Just you, me, a ladder and this belt hanging 15 feet above the ring.” BW: “I dunno...” X: “Come on Blake, where's your spirit? Ladder matches are brutal, bloody, and most of all, great fun! You want people to remember you, to make an impact, to show the rest of the xUw that you mean business, that the Lone Wolf can do it all, then the ladder match is the only way to go.” BW: “Fine. Just don't expect any mercy when I send you to hospital.” X: “Fine!” Blake finishes signing the rest of his name. He passes it over to Factor, who having written the contract, automatically signs it. X: “It's on then. June 2nd, Big Brawl, Blake Wolfson versus The X Factor for the Intercontinental Championship... in a ladder match!” The crowd pops hugely. BW: “There I've signed your little contract, you have your match, now give me my belt back, NOW.” X: “You want your belt, you have to give me my dog...” BW: “Good, take the little bastard, he cost me a hundred and fifty bucks in police fines. But first, I want my belt.” Factor picks up the IC belt, and hands it over to Blake. Wolfson smiles and goes to leave the ring. Factor grabs Blake and turns him around. The crowd, sensing the tension, start to pop. X: “That's not the deal Blake. GIVE ME MY DOG!” BW: “You want the dog? Have him!” Blake throws the dog in the air over Factors head. Factor, being an athletic chap, turns and catches him. As he turns round, happy to be reunited, he is met with an almighty smack in the head from Blake and the Intercontinental Championship belt. Blake takes both Michael and Owen and places them in the corner away from the chaos. BW: “You wanted the belt. There, you just met it head on.” He turns around and takes a steel chair that Factor had been sitting on. He picks it up and strikes Factor again, this time on the back of the head. He hits him again, and again with the chair, making audible smacking noises throughout the arena AT: “Whoa, Blake’s flipped, he's going mental with that chair!” Blake leaves the ring and looks under the banner. As is always the case, there just happens to be a ladder kept under the ring. Blake pulls it out to a loud series of boo's from the crowd. BJ: “The crowd are going nuts, listen to them cheer for Blake” AT: “Cheer for Blake? They're booing the hell out of him!” BJ: “That's where you’re wrong Jalapeno, they aren't saying Boo..they're saying Boolake, Boolake!” Blake slides the ladder into the ring and goes around to the timekeeper and commentators. He takes all their chairs and throws them in the ring. BJ: “Hey, I need that to sit on! Lousy Aussie convict!” Climbing  in the ring Blake picks up one of the chairs and hits Factor again, just to make sure he isn't getting up. Dragging Factor to the corner of the ring he stands him up against the turnbuckles. Moving quickly, he drags the table in front of Factor lengthways so he doesn't fall down.. AT: “What the hell is Wolfson doing here?” Blake picks up the ladder and lays it down of the table. Taking one of the steel chairs, he places it on the edge of the ladder by so it's stood up right in Factors face. Picking up the rest of the chairs, he places them over the gaps in between the steps of the ladder. Picking up the last chair he has left, he sets it into the seated position at the end of the construction. Walking rather nonchalantly back to the opposite corner he turns around to face Factor. Without warning he starts to run across the ring. Using the chair he set up as a sort of step up to the table/ladder construction he leaps onto the ladder. Running along the ladder, using the chairs as stepping stones across the gaps he leaps into a running dropkick, right into the chair, crushing Factors face with it. AT: “Oh my GOD!” BJ: “It's the Wolf Terminator! And I don't think Blake's finished...” Blake, although slightly hurt from his daredevil move, gets up and moves all the debris of chairs and pushes the ladder off the table. Lifting a now unconscious Factor onto the top turnbuckle, he turns the table round and stands on it. He hooks Factor into the position for the Crying Wolf (Unprettier) Smiling at the thought of what's about to happen, Blake falls down performing the Crying Wolf through the table. Amidst all the remains of the table, Blake gets up and searches for his Belt and the Mic. Meanwhile, the Officials and Medical team run down the aisle BW: “You wanted to bring ladders into the game Factor? So let’s play! Come on Factor… Come out and play!” The xUw officials get between Blake and Factor and order Blake to the back. Blake begrudgingly goes, but not without his belt and a huge smile on his face BJ: “Holy crap, these guys really hate each other, and they’ll be fighting in a ladder match for the Intercontinental Title at XUW BIG BRAWL on Sunday! This is shaping up to be the greatest XUW PPV we’ve ever had!” “Four Horsemen” by Metallica plays, and Sheri Dedmon goes to the ring to a big crowd pop. “Anarchy in the UK” hits the speakers and Hodgo comes out onto the ramp. He ducks the plastic cup headed for his eye, and even remembers to block the Frisbee as it makes its way for his head, but he didn’t notice the kid sitting in the front row with a water pistol filled with ‘dubious liquid’, who squirts it RIGHT IN HIS EYE! Hodgo shrugs it off anyway, and gets to the ring. Singles Match: Sheri Dedmon v Hodgo Sheri takes advantage of the distraction with an Arm Drag Takedown and a Moonsault. She then looks for the Toy Box (Stunner), but Hodgo block it and whips her to the ropes. He hits a Karate Kick and then a DDT on her. He covers. 1 … … KICKOUT. Hodgo laughs at Sheri, and she slaps him hard across the face. He merely smiles at her, and lunges forward to grasp her, but she dodges to the side and pushes him into the ropes. He bounces off and she hits a German Suplex. Sheri goes to the top rope and tries a Hurricanrana, but Hodgo reverses it into a devastating Powerbomb. Hodgo picks up ‘Miss XUW’ and smashes her in the head with a series of stiff Anar-Kicks. Sheri is reeling, and Hodgo grabs her, wasting no time in hitting the Riot Breaker (Hangman’s DDT). Sheri is laid out, and Hodgo makes the cover. 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Hodgo smiles sadistically as he pulls Sheri’s shoulders up off the canvas. He lifts her up high and delivers a big Spinebuster. Hodgo picks up Sheri and looks around to see where the ref is. He then twists and whips Sheri hard right into the ref. He goes down quicker than Monica Lewinsky in the Oval Office. Hodgo starts to laugh, and goes under the ring, where there is, as ever, a big f’n pane of glass. Hodgo grins like the maniac he is as he brings the glass into the ring and waits for Sheri to get up. She slowly gets back to her feet, and Hodgo swings the glass, smashing it over her… NO! From nowhere, Raven X runs through the crowd and dives into the ring, getting between Hodgo and Sheri, and taking the pane of glass to the head for her. RX goes down in a shower of glass and blood. Hodgo looks at his fallen comrade in shock, and bends down to see if he’s okay. Sheri stumbles over to him and rolls him up quickly, just as the ref looks over. 1 … … 2 … … Should-3-er up!!!!! The groggy and half-blind ref calls for the bell. Winner, by pinfall: Sheri Dedmon!!!!! AT: “Holy hell in a handbasket, Sheri has beaten Hodgo!” BJ: “He had his shoulder up!” AT: “The ref didn’t see it! Sheri’s beaten Hodgo! Twice!” Sheri goes to the back, to a big pop, and then Hodgo reluctantly helps RX to the back, and the ring is cleared. The camera cuts backstage to where Henrik is preparing, with Naron nearby. Henrik: “I have a World Title match tonight, dearest.” Naron: “Of course, and you will win, as you’re far classier than that commoner, Bob.” Henrik: “I shall win, but just for tonight, if you could stay back here and watch on the monitors, that would be marvellous. I don’t want XUW to say that I won the title unfairly.” Naron: “Well, I’d love to be with you, darling, but if you wish, I shall stay here and await you, with champagne.” Back to the announcers AT: “Is Henrik actually … being honourable???” BJ: “Of course! He’s royalty!” “No More Tears” plays, and Kata comes down the ramp, to a big Horsemen pop. “In The End” plays, and Sakie makes his way to the ring, enduring abuse and chants of “Die Sakie Die”, though he seems to b quite happy and at peace with them. Singles Match: Kata v Sakie The two men enter the ring and lock up quickly as the bell sounds. Kata pushes Sakie off and hits a few hard Chops to Sakie’s chest, and then follows up with an Ipponzei judo throw. Sakie goes down, but rolls through well, and gets back to his feet, his eyes perhaps showing some more respect for the former Submission Title holder. They lock up again, this time Sakie pushing Kat away and hitting a few hard right hands and then hoisting Kata up, before delivering a Death Valley Driver. Sakie covers quickly, but only gets a short two count. Sakie maintains the offence, whipping Kata to the ropes and hitting a Spear. Kata goes down hard, and Sakie tries to lock in a Figure Four Leg Lock, but Kata makes it to the ropes. Sakie advances on Kata, but the horseman moves out of the way and hits a German Suplex. Kata moves like lightning to get atop Sakie and lock in an STF. Sakie cries out in pain, but doesn’t tap, and edges closer to the ropes. Sakie slowly, but surely, makes his way to the ropes and finally manages to grasp the bottom one. Kata releases the hold, but as Sakie gets up he hits a kick to the gut and then a Piledriver. Kata covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! AT: “This is a very close encounter. Sakie’s a former World Champion, but Kata’s looking more focused than ever out there, and he’s giving as good as he’s getting!” Kata remains on the offensive, with a whip to the ropes and a Hip Toss. Sakie gets up, but Kata grabs him again, hitting the Northern Lights Bomb, and hooking a leg. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! Sakie is showing his famous resilience, and stays in a corner, trying to get his breath back. Kata advances, but Sakie springs out ferociously and Clotheslines Kata down to the mat. Kata gets up, but Sakie now has the momentum, and he hits the Sh**s and Giggles (Twist of Fate). The former World Champion goes up top and looks for “The Joke’s On You”, but Kata moves away at the last second and Sakie eats mat. AT: “This could go either way right now.” BJ: “You know, I don’t trust Kata. There have been these mysterious attacks against Admiral John, and some are saying they think it’s one of the Horsemen. If it is, who better than Kata, using his inside information to take down AJ. I mean, he’s never been there to help his ‘friend’ has he?” AT: “That’s nonsense, Kata’s a horseman for life!” Back in the ring the two men get to their feet and exchange right and left hands. Sakie whips Kata to the ropes and they both try for a Spear at the same time, the two men crashing together and falling to the mat. The ref begins to count… AT: “Hey, it’s Esco! What’s he doing here?” Esco slides in to the ring and hits the rising Kata with The Finisher (Rock Bottom). The ref calls for the bell. Winner, by DQ: Kata!!! Esco continues to attack Kata, stamping on his prone figure, and then locking on the Thug Passion (Reverse Figure Four). AT: “Hey, now here come The Technicians, BC and KsC!” The two horsemen run down and enter the ring, attacking Sakie and Esco, the members of the Horsemen seem to be getting the upper hand, until Kidd Kore runs through the crowd with a trash can lid, and nails Bionic Carrot with it. KsC is still hitting Esco and doesn’t see him coming, and gets a trash can lid for his troubles too. AT: “Hey, here comes the Admiral!” The crowd pops huge as AJ comes down to the ring and starts to clear house. He throws Kore straight over the top rope, and then BC and KsC are with him, and they throw Esco out of the ring. Kata is now back up, and he whips Sakie into Admiral John, who throws Sakie into a Back Body Drop over the top rope and down to the floor below. The Four Horsemen celebrate together in the ring, raising the sign of the Horsemen to a HUGE crowd pop. AT: “They’re together! They’re a unit!” BJ: “Bah, humbug!” AT: “The Four Horsemen are a dangerous team to come up against, one of many entered in the Stable Was event of XUW Big Brawl.” BJ: “Speaking of which, we’ve had an announcement about one of the teams entered in the Stable Wars. All of the teams entered in the Stable Wars have a roster of four, as the rules state … apart from one of them. The powers that be are threatening to throw this team out if they do not sign up a fourth member before the Pay Per View on Sunday.” AT: “Which team?” BJ: “The Army of Darkness!” AT: “AoD! Good grief, they’re one of the strongest teams in XUW. Could they really be thrown out of Stable Wars? Who could they get to join up with them? Is there anyone sick enough, dark enough to join with Sidhe, DDT and Jack The Ripper?” BJ: “This could be huge, this could have an enormous effect on my betting strategy!” AT: “Well, Jack’s seemed too occupied with The Phoenix, HBK, recently. Can he concentrate enough to gather himself a fourth member before the deadline?” BJ: “I’m hearing that the fourth member need not wrestle, or even show up at the PPV, they just need a fourth name on the team-sheet, else they’re not allowed in.” AT: “Wow, big news there, as most people thought the AoD were going to be entering as a band of three!” BJ: “Well, speaking of big news, here’s some more, it’s the Tag Team Title match!” “Build Fear” by Solent Green plays, and BoneCrusher and Dev, collectively known as Explicit Violence, make their way to the ring to a chorus of jeers and heat. “Right Here, Right Now” by Ice Cube hits the speakers, and the crowd goes loopy for the former champions, The Deadly Combination! “Rule Britannia” plays, and the tag champs, the Royal Legion, come down to a huge outpouring of scorn and boos. Three Way XUW Tag Team Championship Match: Explicit Violence v Deadly Combination v Royal Legion(C) Dev and M V Pete start off in the ring, and the other stand round the outside, staring at each other. Dev starts with a Snap Suplex, and then hits a DDT and goes for a cover. 1 … …2 … SHOULDER UP! AT: “The Royal Legion are going to have to be careful here, as they don’t even need to get pinned to lose the titles!” BJ: “They lose all advantage they had as champions! It’s not fair, in fact, it’s a conspiracy!!!” MVPete battles back and hits a Prime Time Chokeslam, and then tags in his partner, Numark. Together they hit a Double Superkick on Dev, and then Numark whips the FEAR member to the corner where the Royal Legion are standing. Bailey tags himself in and charges Numark. Nu sidesteps and grabs Bailey, hitting a German Suplex. Bailey springs up as if nothing happened, and grabs Numark, throwing him to the neutral corner and hitting a Big Splash on the turnbuckles. Nu staggers off, and Bailey hits a Running Power Slam! He covers, but Bonecrusher comes in and breaks up the pin. Numark gets up and tags in the big man from FEAR, and he picks up Bailey, hitting a Vertical Suplex Drop, and then a Jumping Sidekick, showing great agility for a man of his size. Bailey tags in his partner, and RX comes in to the fray. Bonecrusher hits him with some hard right hands, and then whips him to the ropes, hitting a huge Clothesline. RX goes down, but Bonecrusher lifts him up and hits a Powerbomb. BoCru hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Bailey enters the ring and breaks up the pin. He then kicks BoCru in the gut and hits his Spinning DDT! RX covers Bonecrusher, and Numark enters the ring to break it up, but Bailey grabs him and hits another Spinning DDT! 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Dev has sneaked into the ring and kicks RX off his partner. Bonecrusher gets back up and goes to the top rope slowly, before flying off with the Big Enforcer Elbow Drop! He covers RX. 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Numark comes flying off the top rope with a 5 Star Frog Splash, breaking up the pin. The ref tries to get some semblance of order back into the ring, and everyone is sent out other than the prone forms of RX and Bonecrusher. The two men get back to their feet at about the same time, and start to exchange blows. Bonecrusher gains the upper hand, and whips RX to the ropes, where Numark gets the blind tag in. Bonecrusher hits a big Spinebuster on RX, but then gets nailed with a Superkick from Numark. Nu goes for a pin, but BoCru kicks out at two and tags Dev back in. AT: “This is an incredible match! It’s fast paced and frantic, just how I like it!” BJ: “Please tell me you didn’t just say that…” Dev goes to town on Numark, hitting a flurry of Stiff Kicks, and then a Dragon Screw Leg Whip, setting Numark up for a Sharpshooter! Dev gets it locked in, and Nu screams out, but uses the resilience he learned back in Calgary, to push forwards and crawl towards the corner. Nu can’t quite reach the ropes, but he reaches his tag partner, and gets a tag just before he gets pulled back to the middle of the ring. MVPete springs over the top rope and comes in with a hard Clothesline to the back of Dev’s head. MVPete picks up Dev and hits the Brick Town Bulldog (TKO). He hooks a leg. 1 … … 2 … … SHOULDER UP! MVPete tags Numark back in, and they whip Dev to the ropes, then hitting him with a Double Dropkick. Nu goes for a Suplex, but Dev reverses it, and tags Bonecrusher back in. The big man throws Numark into a corner, and unloads on him with measured punches, chops and kicks. Numark is looking pretty beaten up, and then BoCru whips Nu to the ropes and hits the Pulling-Walk Slam. Bonecrusher goes to the corner for the Big Enforce Elbow Drop, but Bailey tags himself in with a blind tag! Bonecrusher flies off and hits the Elbow Drop, and goes for the cover, but Bailey pulls him off and tries a cover himself! 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Bonecrusher pulls Bailey off and goes for a Chokeslam type move, but RX comes in and jumps on his back with a Sleeper Hold. Bonecrusher weakens, and Bailey whips him, still with RX on his back, into the ropes. The two men collide with Dev, on the apron, and Dev goes flying off into the guardrail. RX lets go of the hold and goes to the outside as Bailey hits another Spinning DDT on Bonecrusher. The big man goes down and rolls to the outside, as RX gets up on the apron. Numark tries to tag in MVPete, but Bailey catches him and hits a Hip Toss. Bailey then wanders back and tags in RX, before starting to eat the nearest turnbuckle pad. RX hits a Full Nelson Slam, and then a Spinebuster on Nu. Numark looks to be in trouble, but just manages to reverse an Irish Whip, sending RX to the Deadly Combination corner. MVPete smacks RX in the head, and he turns round into a Superkick. Numark uses the last of his energy to climb the turnbuckle and fly off, hitting the Bombshell (5 Star Frog Splash) on to RX. He hooks the leg as Bonecrusher, Bailey and Dev all dive to break it up. 1 … … 2 … … … NO!!!!! All three men reach the pin at the same time, thundering down and breaking the count. AT: “Oh my God, could that have been any closer?” BJ: “NO!” AT: “Poor Raven X! He’s been smashed over the head with a pane of glass, and now he’s been literally crushed under a huge pile of bodies!” BoCru, Dev and Bailey all stand up, and Dev hits the Devolution (Fireman’s Carry to Michinoku Driver) on Bailey. MVPete comes in and hits a Flying Crossbody on Bonecrusher. Numark is STILL pinning Raven X. 1 … … 2 … … 3!!!!! Winners, and NEW XUW Tag Team Champions: The Deadly Combination!!!!! AT: “My God, they were too busy fighting to realise that Numark was still pinning RX!” BJ: “Hell, my Grandmother could pin RX, the shape he’s in right now!” AT: “I still can’t believe it, The Deadly Combination are Two Time, Two Time Tag Champs!” Back in the ring they are still brawling, Bonecrusher and MVPete are exchanging right hands, Dev and Bailey are wrestling on the mat, and Numark is, well, just looking down at RX, and then being handed the tag belts by the ref. Numark puts the belt to good use, smacking it on Bonecrusher’s head, which allows himself and MVPete to leave the ring and celebrate their momentous victory. Bailey and Dev fight a little longer, until some XUW officials come down and break it all up. AT: “Wow, we’re seeing some shifts in the balance of power right here. If Bob can keep his title in the next match then The Usual Suspects will surely be favourites to win the Stable Wars, having the World Champ AND the Tag Team Champs, and Sniper too, who’s coming off a win over Laudust!” BJ: “Yet, if Henrik can pull a win out here, then the momentum will perhaps lie with the Commonwealth, and the new World Champion.” AT: “True, but it’s all just speculation until we see the match take place, which will be any minute now.” BJ: “Hang on a second, I’ve just heard that HBK, the Phoenix, is to respond to The Ripper’s challenge that he made earlier this week!” AT: “Jack has been consumed with thoughts of revenge ever since HBK cost him his XUW World Title, and challenged HBK to a match at Big Brawl, even though he’s already fighting in the Stable Wars!” Scene cuts to a nondescript room, brightly lit by countless candles. HBK, The Phoenix, is sat in the middle of all the flames, his eyes glowing brightly as he looks at the camera. Phoenix: “So, the darkness is challenging the flames eternal. Many times the snakes of the darkness have sought to extinguish the Phoenix, yet many times they have failed. I still sit here, still aflame with the power to destroy the darkness. Ripper, you wish to have one last match, one final confrontation to end them all, where one of us will truly be destroyed, either light or dark triumphing over the other? So be it. At XUW Big Brawl we shall meet for one final cataclysmic time. It will be our last match, for at least one of us must be destroyed, and be no more. Our final battle, our Ragnarok, our Armageddon. So, how more fitting could it be than to have a match to truly settle the score, a match that links us both… a match made in HELL…” The Phoenix stands up, and the candles all seem to burn brighter, their flames giving him strength, and new intensity. Phoenix: “Do not take my silence for weakness, Ripper, for that would be your mistake. You may not see me at all times, but I am there, the light that casts your shadows. I may not see you until Sunday, at the PPV, but when I do, I will see you in hell… Hell in a Cell…” The candles burn even higher, and then the camera cuts back to the announce position. AT: “Oh my God!” BJ: “Could tonight get any bigger? Any more monumental? First off Blake Wolfson nearly kills X Factor as they set up a ladder match for the Intercontinental Title, then The Deadly Combination win back the Tag Team belts, and now HBK will take on Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy at XUW Big Brawl in a Hell in a Cell match!” AT: “Remember, Jack is already wrestling in potentially three matches that night in Stable Wars. The Army of Darkness leader has got his work cut out for him this Sunday, that much is for sure…” BJ: “And we haven’t even gotten to the Main Event yet!” On cue, “Rule Britannia” hits the speakers, and Henrik comes down the ramp to enormous amounts of heat, but, true to his word, Naron does not accompany him. “F*ck That” comes over the speakers, and the roof is well and truly raised, as the crowd pops its’ socks off for the arrival of the XUW World Champion, Samurai Bob! He makes his way down to the ring, acknowledging the fans as he does. XUW World Title Match: Henrik the King v Samurai Bob(C) The two men start off with a stare-down, looking each other in the eyes for any sign of weakness. AT: “Bob only won this title last week, defeating Jack The Ripper after HBK interfered on his behalf. During that match Bob took a fair old beating, and he might not be back fully at 100%, but nothing short of being dead could keep Bob away from an XUW ring!” BJ: “I’ve heard he has a deal where his ghost might come back and haunt us later on too, so even death might not stop him!” AT: “Well, I don’t know about that…” In the ring the two men lock up, and Henrik executes a go-behind and then hits a Bulldog. He immediately tries to lock on the Four Leaf Clover, but Bob powers out of it and gets back to his feet. The men lock up again and again Henrik gets behind Bob, this time hitting a German Suplex on the bigger man. Bob rolls away, and as Henrik advances on him Bob executes a Single Leg Takedown. Bob lifts Henrik back up by the head and hits him straight away with a DDT, picking up Henrik and repeating the process with a second DDT. Bob hooks a leg. 1 .. … 2 … SHOULDER UP! AT: Henrik has been cowardly in his defences of his Submission Title, but now tonight, when it isn’t on the line, he’s showing a lot more courage.” Henrik almost responds to AT’s words immediately by levelling Bob with a Clothesline and then going up top and trying to hit the Kingpin (450 Splash), but Bob gets his knees up, and Henrik falls to the canvas in obvious pain. Bob picks up the Submission Champion and hits a Snap Suplex, and then a Side Suplex. Bob sets Henrik up on the edge of the apron and then hits a Guillotine Leg Drop across his throat. Bob climbs back into the ring and pulls Henrik away from the ropes, performing a lateral press. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Henrik once more digs deep and comes up trumps. Bob scales the turnbuckle, and comes off with a Flying Shoulder Tackle, which nails Henrik back down to the mat. Bob covers again, but once more only to a two count. Henrik struggles to his feet, but manages to hit Bob with a Drop Toe Hold and get a Front Facelock applied on the floor, slowing the match down and allowing him to regain his breath. Bob gets to his feet, but Henrik swings round and hits a Tornado DDT, but doesn’t release upon impact, keeping Bob in a Headlock. Henrik gets to his feet again, and swings in behind Bob, locking on the Stratford Strangle (Million Dollar Dream)! Bob starts to weaken almost immediately. The ref lifts his arm to see if he’s able to continue. 1 … … 2 … … NO!!! Bob gains some strength from somewhere, and the crowd pops huge as he fights back with a series of elbows to the midsection of the King, and then a big Clothesline off the ropes. Henrik goes down and Bob goes to the top turnbuckle, flying off with the Samurai Star (Flying Elbow Drop). He connects right into the chest of Henrik, and hooks the leg of the prone Submission Champion. 1 … … 2 … … … SHOULDER UP! AT: “Unbelievable! I would have thought that was it for sure!” Samurai Bob picks up Henrik and takes him to the corner, setting him up on the top turnbuckle and looking to hit the Saigon Snapp. Henrik reverses at the last minute, into the London Calling (Top Rope Bulldog)! Henrik looks down at Bob, then climbs the turnbuckle and hits the Kingpin! Henrik covers. 1 … … 2 … … KICKOUT! Henrik holds his head in his hands in amazement that Bob kicked out, and the crowd yell Bob on to get back up and beat the leader of the Commonwealth. Henrik lifts up Bob and tries for a DDT, but Bob uses his leg strength to just charge Henrik back, right into the turnbuckle. Bob hits a DDT of his own, and goes to climb the turnbuckle, but Henrik grabs one leg and executes a perfect Dragon Screw Leg Whip, sending Bob down hard to the mat. AT: “Hey, here comes Naron! She was meant to stay backstage, not come down here to ringside!” Naron makes her way down to the side of the ring, accompanied by a referee, who wants to take her back away from the action, but Naron is clearly just ignoring him. In the ring, Henrik grabs Bob’s legs, and wraps them into the Four Leaf Clover (Texas Cloverleaf)! Bob is in pain, but refuses to submit, and crawls over to the ropes. He finally makes it there, and grabs the bottom rope … but Naron swats his hand away! The ref didn’t see it, and Henrik pulls back on the hold. Bob still refuses to tap, and makes his way to the ropes, grabbing the bottom one again… and Naron knocks his hand off again! The ref in the ring still doesn’t see it, and Henrik drags Bob a little way back into the ring, Bob clearly weakening from the submission hold. The World Champ grits his teeth one more time and grabs hold of the bottom rope for a third time. Naron tries to knock it off the ropes, but Bob’s grip is tighter this time. Bob slaps the mat, trying to attract the attention of the ref in the ring to the fact that he’s made it to the ropes, but the ref only sees the tapping hand. He calls for the bell… …but it doesn’t ring, as the ref Naron brought with her stops the call from being made and attracts the in-ring ref’s attention to the hand on the ropes. Henrik drags Bob back into the middle of the ring, but releases the hold, as the refs argue amongst themselves. Henrik goes over to argue with the refs, saying that he should be the bloody champion. Naron sneaks into the ring, chair in hand, and swings at Bob. Bob moves out of the way, and then grabs the chair. He fights for it with Naron, and wins the tug of war, but yanking the chair out of her hands sends it swinging backwards, and CLUNK into the shoulder of the ref. The ref sees the chair n Bob’s hands and calls for the bell for real this time. Winner, by DQ: Henrik the King!!! Henrik looks distraught, despite winning, for the belt remains with Samurai Bob. Bob himself throws down the chair, and leaves the ring with his title. AT: “Good grief, I thought that Naron was going to cheat Bob out of his title then!” BJ: “It was close, but all credit to Henrik, as he matched Bob move for move and hold for hold.” AT: “True, he did, and they may well meet again this coming Sunday, at XUW Big Brawl.” BJ: “The final card will be posted soon, but we know the eight teams that will enter Stable Wars!” AT: “Indeed we do, and here they are: The Commonwealth The Usual Suspects F.E.A.R. Total Anarchy The Four Horsemen The Boomer Sooners The Stable-With-No-Name, and The Army of Darkness (pending a fourth member).” BJ: “It’s a hard pick, but I’ve studied the form, an I’ve made my pick.” AT: “Dare I ask?” BJ: “Well, in the absence of Blake Wolfson’s one man team I’m going to have to go with Total Anarchy!” AT: “Really?” BJ: “They’re a lock, I’m sure of it.” AT: “Well, I think the momentous events of the day have finally gotten to him. They’re getting to me to, so we’ll wrap it up here and say adios until next time!” Fade to Black
