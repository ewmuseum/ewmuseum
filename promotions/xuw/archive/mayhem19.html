XUW Mayhem #19

28 Jan 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XUW Mayhem Welcome to the DeSantes Arena…where Mayhem will change the course of XUW forever! Intro video is shown of HBK and Bob’s feud. Clips of Bob and HBK fighting it out are shown, with the finale of Bob holding the title and posing in a corner as the Pyro goes off and we are at MAYHEM! AT: Hello everyone, I’m AT here right along side Mr. Mikey as always… MM: Jesus, it is always “Mr. Mikey with AT. I say it is AT with Mr. Mikey! AT: Erm…’kay. Anyway tonight we have an explosive card…topped off with a world title match between the commish of XUW, HBK and the world champion, Samurai Bob! We cut to the back and see Exodus entering the arena from their limos, and HBK riding in a separate limo. HBK: Heheh…tonight will be my night. Not only am I the only man in charge of XUW…I have a title shot! And of course, I have “X” to back me up…right guys? With you all here, XUW won’t be going anywhere! Lopen (looking rather angered): Man, you know that I am the world champion. Bob ripped me off! And now you are going to sit there and tell me that YOU deserve that title. I wish I were the commissioner so I could give myself titles every day! HBK: I got this title shot fair and square! What, are you jealous! We can’t let these petty differences get between us! We need to stick together! I’m going to get ready for my match! Lopen (grabbing HBK and screaming in his face): I AM THE CHAMPION! YOU KNOW IT! Security pulls Lopen away from HBK and HBK stands back in amazement…as Jack arrives in the arena… Jack: Hi boys…Lopen, no worries. There is a greater force at work in XUW now. HBK and Bob is still goin down tonight. But believe me when I say some changes are gonna happen! HBK: Jack, maybe you don’t know who I am? I am HBK! I am the commissioner! I am the man! Jack (walking away): Okay…If you say so…heheh. We go back to ringside where a messenger is handing AT a note. AT: Mr. Mikey? This is the card for tonight. Didn’t we get one of these before? MM: Yea, like 3 days ago. What does it say? AT: Umm…I can’t really read it. This thing reads like stereo instructions! MM: Gimmie that! God, I swear you act like Gomer Pyle sometimes! Mr. Mikey gets into the ring and gets on the mic… MM: I have been handed this note with the new card for tonight. No bitching from you viewers! There is a disclaimer at the bottom of your tickets that says “card subject to change!” NO REFUNDS! Now, here is tonight’s card: 1)Esco is hereby stripped of the CW title! It will be fought for in a match between Sidhe and Hodgo. 2)Raven X will be facing Sakie for the IC title 3)Jack Nobbledy will defend his Submission title against Kata 4)The tag team titles will be defended as Doggy style faces off against the hurristoners: Fatty and Chase 5)The hardcore title will be fought over between RTT and DDT. 6)And the main event will be HBK v. Bob for the world title! There is a large pop at the announcement of the last match as MM gets back on the headset and Hodgo’s music hits and he walks to the ring. MM: You see that! They love me! At least someone around here appreciates me! AT: Oh, I am sure they love you. Everyone loves a good hernia! MM: Excuse me! AT: Ohh come on! You are the biggest pain in the ass I have ever met! Sidhe’s music hits and he and Kat walk to the ring. Sidhe wastes no time diving into the ring and going after his former partner! Match 1: Sidhe v. Hodgo: CW title Sidhe dives into the ring and spears Hodgo to the ground and begins to beat him in the head. The ref goes to pull Sidhe off, but Sidhe pushes the ref down. Sidhe continues to pummel Hodgo with a series of rights and lefts while Hodgo just tries to block. Finally the ref manages to pull Sidhe off of Hodgo and Sidhe turns and begins to argue with the ref. When Sidhe turns around, Hodgo tackles him and begins to pummel Sidhe mercilessly. After knocking the ref down, Hodgo is finally pulled off Sidhe. Hodgo picks sidhe up and whips him into the corner. He follows Sidhe in hard with a knee to sidhe’s stomach. He picks sidhe up and puts him on the top rope and goes for a top rope hurriconranna, but Sidhe pushes Hodgo off and turns around and does a moonsault onto Hodgo and goes for the pin…1…. 2…Kickout by Hodgo. Sidhe wastes no time and gets right back on Hodgo and picks him up. He hits Hodgo a few times and then does a standing drop kick right to Hodgo’s face, which sends Hodgo right to the outside. Sidhe turns and runs to the opposite side and bounces off the ropes. He runs at Hodgo and dives through the ropes, but Hodgo moves and Sidhe missiles himself into the announce table! AT: Ahh…crap! I spilled my coffee! MM: Looks like AT wet himself! HEY EVERYONE! AT WET HIMSELF! Hodgo takes advantage of the situation by climbing into the announce table and bringing sidhe up there with him. Hodgo faces the crowd and smiles sadistically and he goes to put Sidhe through the table with a pile driver. Hodgo goes to pick up Sidhe, but sidhe reverses it and back body drops Hodgo into the crowd! Sidhe stumbles to the floor as Hodgo tries to get to his feet. Hodgo looks to his left and sees a kid standing with a sign that says “Hodgo fears Kat” and he tears the sign. The little kid starts crying, and Hodgo gets into the ring. Kat walks over to the little kid and gives him a big hug for losing his sign…which the kid seems to like VERY much! Hodgo climbs back over the railing and Sidhe has stumbled to his feet and is leaning against the steel pole. Hodgo Runs at Sidhe, but sidhe moves and Hodgo runs face first into the pole. Sidhe picks Hodgo up and throws him into the ring. Sidhe climbs into the ring and he picks up Hodgo. Sidhe hits Hodgo with a sit down power bomb and goes for the pin…1…2…kick out by Hodgo! Sidhe gets up and begins to argue with the referee again, and Hodgo quickly rolls up Sidhe…1…2…3! winner and new CW champion: Hodgo Sidhe looks up in disbelief as Hodgo grabs the title and a mic. Sidhe begins to walk away from the ring when Hodgo begins to talk… Hodgo: Sidhe, wait! Sidhe turns around and stops Kat. Hodgo: Listen…I know we have had our differences, but you showed me tonight that there is no one I would rather have in my corner than you. I want to go back to the days of the Riot squad! This title doesn’t mean crap to me! I consider it retired; it has caused too much trouble. All I want is for us to be a team again. For us to go back to kicking other’s butts, instead of each other’s. Sidhe walks back to the ring, and Kat follows suit…Sidhe rolls into the ring and stares at Hodgo. He grabs Hodgo’s hand and raises it as Kat gives Hodgo a hug to a big face pop for the newly reunited Riot Squad. AT: That was so…beautiful! MM: Does anyone have a tissue? The little kid from before hands MM a piece of his sign and MM blows his nose. Then Sakie’s music this and he walks to the ring for his IC title match. Raven X walks out afterwards and the match quickly begins. AT: Well, we aren’t wasting anytime are we? Match 2: IC title: Raven X v. Sakie The match begins with Raven X and Sakie circling each other. They lock up, and Sakie gets a headlock on RX. RX elbows Sakie in the ribs, and sends him into the ropes. RX ducks as Sakie leap frogs him, then RX lays down as Sakie runs over him. Sakie on the way back goes to leapfrog RX again, but RX catches him and hits a huge spine buster. RX picks up Sakie and kicks him in the stomach and body slams him. RX goes to the outer apron and he waits for Sakie. Sakie stumbles to his feet, and RX grabs his head and pulls his neck over the top rope, hanging Sakie out to dry. RX grabs Sakie and pulls him out of the ring. He goes to whip Sakie into the steps, but Sakie reverses it and sends RX into said steps. Mexican gets out to check on RX and Bailey comes from the crowd and whips Sakie into the steel post. Bailey quickly dives under the ring before Mexican can see and RX takes advantage of a downed Sakie. He throws Sakie into the ring and climbs to the top rope. RX goes for a splash, but Sakie puts his knees up! Sakie gets up and begins to gather his thoughts and gets some offense going. He begins to pummel RX into the corner. He climbs onto the second turnbuckle and begins to pound on RX as the crowd counts along. 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 Sakie rears way back for this one…10! The crowd is getting really into the match as Sakie picks up RX and throws him into the ropes. He kicks RX in the stomach and runs off the other ropes and hits a rocker-dropper. He goes for the pin…1…2…kick out by RX. Sakie gets up and waits for RX. RX stumbles to his feet and Sakie goes to spear RX. RX moves though, and Sakie spears Mexican, causing the Mexican to Explode. Sakie looks at the smoking pile of body parts that used to be the referee stunned and RX takes advantage by hitting Sakie from behind. RX holds Sakie as Bailey comes out from under the ring and grabs Mexican’s leg. He dives at Sakie, but Sakie ducks and Bailey hits RX in the face with Mexican’s foot! Sakie throws Bailey over the top rope and when RX gets up he hits him with the Sakbottom and goes for the cover, but there is no referee! Sakie screams for a referee to come down there, and finally one does…1…2…kick out by RX! Sakie quickly climbs to the top rope and he goes for “the Jokes’ on you” but he sees that Bailey has gotten up so he dives to the outside and takes out Bailey! Sakie dives into the ring and turns around into a lariat by RX. RX goes for the family values (pedigree) But Sakie back body drops him! Sakie climbs onto the top rope and hits “the Jokes’ on you” for the 1…2…3! Winner and IC champ: Sakie! A huge celebration ensues as the hurristoners rush to the ring to congratulate Sakie on his win. They go to pick Sakie up on their shoulders, and they are carrying him around the ring when Chase trips and Sakie falls to the mat. Fat Badger begins to laugh at them and they all go to the back. The music of the horsemen hits and Kata walks to the ring for his match with Jack. He gets on the Mic and begins to talk: Kata: I am a horseman! Horsemen stick to their word! I will stick by XUW, no matter how low it gets. I don’t care where we go, I will stick by XUW! Out of nowhere, a loud megaphone declares, “FEAR THE IRON FIST!” and three men dive into the ring and begin to destroy Kata with sledgehammers! They beat Kata down and quickly get out of the ring. Kata is laid out in the ring for a minute when Jack’s music hits and he runs to the ring! Match 3: Submission title: Jack v. Kata Jack wastes no time going for the submission on a decimated kata. He locks a crossface chickenwing on Kata, but it only seems to wake Kata up and he screams in pain. Kata finally reaches the ropes as the referee makes him break the hold. Jack goes to work on Kata’s Arm. He grabs Kata’s arm and runs and dives over the top rope, pulling Kata’s left arm with him. Kata screams in pain! AT: Man! It looks like Jack is going to rip Kata’s arm clean off! MM: Oh…we are still here?! I didn’t know I got to talk anymore! AT: It has been oddly long since we talked, hasn’t it… Jack dives into the ring and he takes kata and puts him in an armbar, stretching Kata’s arm to its limit as Kata screams in pain. The referee asks kata if he wants to tap, but Kata refuses. Finally after about 2 minutes of ripping at Kata’s arm, he finally lets the hold go. Jack gets up and he pulls Kata up, but out of nowhere Kata begins to fight back! Kata hits a few quick rights then he locks on the Kata Hajime on Jack! Jack screams, but Kata won’t let the hold go! Finally Jack begins to tap, and the ref calls for the bell… Winner and new Submission champion: Kata Jack gets up and looks really upset, and he rolls out of the ring and grabs a chair. He gets into the ring and begins to just beat down kata. He throws Kata over the top rope and hits him in the head with the chair again. By now Kata is busted open and Jack throws him onto the Spanish announce table! Spanish Jobber: Ayy…no quiero! Spanish jobber dives under the table to hide and Jack sets up Kata. Jack hits the ripsaw on Kata through the table! Kata and Spanish jobber are both laid out Jack smiles and grabs the mic… Jack: I am done with that pitiful little title! I have bigger, better things to worry about! Jack walks to the back and he is passed by Kat, who is walking to the announce table… Kat: Umm…Mr. Mikey? I was given this note to replace you. MM: What! Kat: I’m sorry. I don’t know what’s going on either. Just please, don’t make this hard! MM: I will not leave! Security comes down and 5 big guys grab MM and drag him to the back as AT is snickering. AT: Welcome Kat! What brings you here? Kat: I wish I knew! Through all the confusion of changing announcers, the participants in the next match have already gotten into the ring. Match 4: Hardcore title: DDT v. RTT Match starts out by DDT diving out of the ring and grabbing a bat from under it. RTT sees this and he dives out of the ring and grabs a bat from the other corner. They both get into the ring and swing at each other, and their bats collide, shattering. Both men grab their hands and scream in pain. DDT is the first to get up and he grabs a chair and goes to hit RTT in the head with it, but RTT kicks it back into the face of DDT. RTT goes for the pin…1…2…kick out! RTT gets out of the ring and he grabs a fire extinguisher. He gets into the ring and goes to fire it at DDT, but it won’t fire! DDT smiles and begins to walk towards RTT who is trying to get the fire extinguisher to work, but finally gives up and as DDT gets close enough, RTT hits him in the groin with the fire extinguisher. DDT is laid out and RTT gets a chair form the outside and puts it on top of DDT. RTT grabs another chair and gets on the top rope. He dives off the top rope, doing a splash, while holding one chair in front of him and landing on the other chair. Both men are now laid out, and RTT is the first to his feet. He sets the chair up in the corner between the ropes and picks DDT up. He whips DDT into the corer with the chair. RTT follows DDT into the corner and goes for a shoulder block, but DDT moves and RTT smashes face first into the chair. DDT rolls out of the ring and grabs RTT and throws him into the crowd. They begin to fight in the second row, when DDT grabs a beer that a fan is drinking and throws it at RTT. The man gets furious and hits DDT in the face, sending him down. RTT then takes advantage by taking a chair from a fan and hitting DDT over the head with it as the crowd begins to “ooo” and “ahh.” RTT and DDT fight into the lobby, where RTT throws DDT into the women’s room. You hear a loud shriek and Naron comes running out of the room and she sees RTT laughing! She kicks RTT in the groin and walks away. RTT recovers and goes into the bathroom where DDT is waiting. DDT waits for RTT to come close enough, the he swings the bathroom door closed into RTT’s face. DDT grabs RTT and brings him into the bathroom. DDT Throws RTT into the drying machine. DDT then grabs RTT and throws him into the mirror. RTT is bleeding and DDT goes for the pin…1…2…kick out! DDT gets frustrated and he grabs RTT and brings him into a stall. DDT tries to put RTT’s head into the stall wall, but RTT blocks it and instead he low-blows DDT. DDT is laid out and his head is over the toilet when RTT slams the lid down on DDT’s head, breaking the toilet lid. DDT falls in a lump on the floor and RTT goes for the pin…1…2…3! Winner and Hardcore champion: Rob The Throb AT: What a match! That was as hardcore as it gets! Kat: And men…that is why you ALWAYS put the seat down! We go to the ring where the music of the tag champs hits and doggy style walks to the ring to massive boos. Then the hurristoner’s theme hits and the crowd goes nuts! AT: Well, I wonder who the favorite is in this one? Kat: It is the hurristoners…ya dumb hillbilly! Match 5: tag titles: Hurristoners (Chase and Fat Badger) v. doggy style Match starts out with Chase and Blake in the ring. Chase and Blake circle each other, and then lock up. Neither man gets the upper hand, so they break it. They try it again, and this time, Chase pushes Blake into the corner with raw power. Blake gets up and the lock up again. Now Blake powers chase into the corner, and he gets up. Blake goes to shake Chases hand, and despite the crowd’s heavy reaction against it, Chase goes for it anyway, to which Blake responds by kicking Chase in the stomach and giving him a DDT. Blake picks Chase up and he tags in Woof. Woof kicks punches Chase in the head and pushes him into the corner. Woof then runs to the other corner and he hits FB. FB tries to get into the ring, but the ref stops him, and in the other corner Woof and Blake double team Chase with a series of kicks and punches to the head. Blake gets out of the ring and Woof whips Chase into a neutral corner. Woof follows it up with a lariat, which puts chase sitting in the corner. Woof goes for a bronco buster, but chase gets his foot up and Woof gets a low blow. Woof is laid out holding himself and Chase crawls over to his corner. Blake distracts the referee, while Chase gets the tag! FB gets into the ring, but the ref stops him, saying he didn’t get a tag. Meanwhile, Blake slaps his hands together and rolls Woof out of the ring. He begins to attack Chase who is still groggy. The ref questions Blake, but Blake says he made the tag and continues to fight. Blake hits a sit-down power bomb on Chase and goes for the pin…1…2…broken up by FB! Woof comes in and an all out brawl begins. The ref tries to get FB out of the ring while on the other side, Blake has a chair! Woof holds Chase while Blake waffles him with the chair. They throw the chair out of the ring and Blake goes for the pin while Woof knocks FB off the apron…1…2…3! Winner and still Tag Champs: Doggy Style AT: What a rip off! Kat: That was horrible! AT: Of all the cheap ways to win a match! We cut to the back to Exodus’s locker room. HBK: Hey guys, I am gonna go get ready for my match. Lopen, you try to calm down! Lopen: You’re right HBK. I was jealous… HBK leaves the room… Lopen: Jealous of your stupidity… Sev: …Huh? Lopen: Whatever. Listen, We all know I should be champion. He is only getting this shot cause he is the commissioner! I say we throw HBK out! Sev:… Lopen: I know it seems bad, but let’s vote! If you think HBK’s head is too big and you want him out, say “I” All: I!! Lopen: If you want HBK to stay, say “nay.” Small voice from the back: Nay… Lopen: Who said that? All the members of exodus move to the side to reveal Shooter??!! They all get annoyed and lay shooter out, then leave with him in a bloody mess as we go back to ringside… Kat: I was just handed a memo. It says that if no one wins tonight, then the belt will be held up. AT: How can no one win though? HBK’s music hits and he walks to the ring to a chorus of boos. Then the lights go out and Samurai bob’s music hits, and the crowd goes nuts as a spotlight shows Bob on the stage. He walks to the ring and poses with his belt and the bell rings… Match 6: World title: Bob v. HBK HBK starts the match off by locking up with Bob. HBK goes for a quick small package roll-up…1…2…reversed! Bob goes for the pin…1…2…kick out! Both men get up and begin to circle each other again. This time they lock up again, and Bob goes for a side headlock. HBK picks Bob up and hits a suplex though, but bob quickly gets up and stares at HBK. HBK runs at Bob, but bob does a hip toss into an armbar. HBK gets up while still in the hold and he jumps up and grabs Bob in a head scissors takedown and puts bob into a scissors submission. Bob turns and flips onto HBK, holding his legs and pinning him…1…2…HBK bridges out of the move and turns it over and picks Bob up for a piledriver, but bob kicks his legs and reverses the piledriver by picking up HBK and going for a piledriver of his own. Bob hits his piledriver and goes for the pin…1…2…kick out by HBK! HBK gets to his feet quickly and they begin to circle again when Exodus runs out to the ring. They dive in and all begin a group beat down of Bob! HBK looks on in amazement, when they all turn and Lopen begins to laugh. He points at HBK and gives a thumb down, then they all jump HBK! Out of nowhere, the riot squad runs to the ring and fends off Exodus. The riot squad stays at ringside to make sure no one else interferes. After about 2 minutes Bob and HBK both stumble to their feet. HBK suddenly hit the sweet chin music…1…2…KICKOUT BY BOB! HBK can’t believe it! He begins to argue with the ref, while Bob shakes off the cobwebs. HBK turns around and Bob grabs HBK and this a belly-to-back suplex. Bob whips HBK into the corner and sets him up. Bob hits his finisher, the siagon snapp! Bob goes for the pin…1…2…KICKOUT BY HBK! Bob is stunned at this! He begins to argue with the ref now. HBK gets to his feet and falls in the corner, barely able to stand. Jack runs to the ring, and grabs Bob and hits the ripsaw on him! HBK gets out of the corner and begins to argue with Jack! HBK (yelling as loud as he can): I can do this by myself! Jack: This wasn’t for you boy! Sidhe rolls into the ring and hits a side Russian leg sweep/inverted DDT on HBK! Kat: It’s the “fall from grace!” AT: How did you know the name of that move? A man from the crowd climbs onto one corner and Sidhe climbs onto the other corner. The mystery man stands up tall to reveal…DIABLOS26! The crowd pops huge at the sight of D26! Then D goes for the devil drop, and he hits…BOB! Sidhe hits the Candyflip on HBK. D looks over at the referee and demands that he end the match. The referee refuses, so D kicks him in the stomach and goes for what looks to be a powerbomb, but when he has the ref in the air, he bridges back, dropping the ref on his face. D calls for the bell and it is rung… Winner: No winner: belt is held up Kat dives into the ring with a mic… Kat: Meet the new owners of the XUW: Diablos26 and Sidhe Nimh! Sidhe: Ya see, when XUW went under, double O halo bought up as much stock as it could. The other 50%? It went to my man right here…the REAL XUW commish…D26! the Hurristoners run to the ring to try to stop Sidhe and D26, but are quickly decimated by Hodgo, Jack, D, and Sidhe! AT: XUW will never be the same! New owners! And how disgusting could they be! AT continues to rant and people begin to throw beer cups and other trash into the ring as we fade to black…
