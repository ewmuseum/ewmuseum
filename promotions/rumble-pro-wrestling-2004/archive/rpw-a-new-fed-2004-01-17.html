A New Fed

Hi, I'm Pete K. I'm opening a new federation, hopefully a little bit different than anything else out there today.
This will be a true ROH/MLW/Indy style federation. We will run 1-3 shows a month, summary style except for the BIG shows, which hopefully will by PBP. We will be closed flash.
We will be moving out of the "feud with a guy during TV shows for 2 months and then blow it off at a supercard" mold and getting into a different format that allows for multiple feuds at once, titles changing hands at any show, etc.
The FAQ is below. If you have any questions, write me at [detail withheld] and make sure to join our yahoogroup at groups.yahoo.com/group/rumblepro - and thanks!
Pete K -Former FSW fedhead, handler of Rum Barton, Debonair, Derek Irvin, etc. ============================================================================== Who are you? ------------- I'm Pete K, I've been in e-w for a few years in tons of feds. I've handled Derek Irvin, Debonair, Rum Barton, and a few others. I've run the Superslam supercards as well as Five Star Wrestling for a brief period.
What is this? (OOC) --------------------- An e-fed. Duh. It's basically going to be a ROH or MLW type of indy fed, summary style, (unless I suddenly get the time on my hands to put together full PBP cards, which I may do for the "big" shows of the year.) closed flash, an email fed.
What is this? (IC) --------------------- This is the newest product from Chris Chronic, who had to close Five
Star Wrestling due to monetary reasons. He has now assembled a new promotion with a new way of making money that will hopefully allow it to thrive. He has formed Rumble Pro Wrestling in the hopes of bringing
America the best independent wrestling action around.
What do I need to know? ---------------------------- RPW is going to be unlike any other federation. We are really going to go with the "indy" feel of things. Show dates will be announced, and matches will be added to the card as the date comes closer. We will range from 1-3 shows per month, depending on by schedule and other factors, so we won't get into the bi-weekly rut. Shows will come out on the date they are supposed to. The fed will be summary style, except for 1-2 major shows a year, which will be PBP. Depending on the time between the update and the show, you will either do 1 or 2 flashes per show. For example, if we have two shows, 2 weeks apart, you will do one flash for that show, but if we have only one show in a particular month, you will probably do 2 flashes for that show. Trust me, you'll get the hang of it.
Winners will be decided on flashes, strats, and angles when necessary. This won't be a purely angle based federation. If you want to do an angle with somebody, by all means do it, but I'm not going to make people do angles. Also, we are going to get out of the typical fed layout in which there are 6-7 weeks of TV shows followed by a PPV show or supercard. That is too strict and doesn't allow for more than one feud at a time, taking away from the excitement of the shows. In this format you can carry on several feuds at once, and each show is as big as the last. Titles can change hands at anytime. It should be a lot of fun.
