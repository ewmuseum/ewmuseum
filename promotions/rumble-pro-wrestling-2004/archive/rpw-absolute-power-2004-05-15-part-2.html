[Cut back to the locker room area where RPW's own Kristy Millard is standing by with the 6'8" tall Werewolf Gregorson. Dressed in his usual ring attire of black tights with three claw marks up the sides of each leg and a black t-shirt with the words "I Hunt Therefore I Am"

KRISTY: "Ladies and gentlemen, I'm backstage with Werewolf Gregorson, the man who pulls double duty tonight here at Absolute Power by first wrestling 'The Outlaw' Travis LaGrange in a qualifier for the Rumble
Pro Strong Style Cup and later _teaming_ with LaGrange in a tag team battle royal to determine the number one contenders to the RPW Tag
Team Championship currently held by Real Xtreme. Werewolf, do you think the possible fallout from your match against The Outlaw will hurt your chances of winning the number one contendership later tonight?"

Regardless of the outcome, though, I intend to honor my commitment to our partnership and get back in the ring later tonight
_with_ Travis to claim the number one contendership that's rightfully ours."
KRISTY: "So you're saying this match between yourself and The Outlaw was fated to happen?" WEREWOLF: "Kristy, have you ever heard the expression that states
'What doesn't kill us makes us stronger'...because that's what I believe about this match and my partnership with Travis LaGrange. You see, when I was looking for a tag team partner, I wasn't exactly checking character references. I wanted someone with a 'take no prisoners' attitude and that's what I got. I knew from the start that
Travis was no saint but I still called him and asked him to come to
RPW...and I don't regret that decision one bit. In fact, I'd have to say that asking Travis to be my partner and being given this opportunity to wrestle him tonight are two of the crowning moments of my career because, win or lose, I'm going to come away from this match and our partnership knowing a hell of a lot more about this business than I do right now."
KRISTY: "But does The Outlaw share your enthusiasm, Werewolf? Will
Travis LaGrange honor his commitment to _you_ if you beat him tonight in the Strong Style Cup?"
WEREWOLF: "I can't speak for Travis, Kristy, because it's not for me to put words in his mouth...but I truly believe that he and I have a lot more to accomplish in Rumble Pro before we go our separate ways.
In fact, whether we win or lose this battle royal tonight is actually irrelevant because I guarantee you haven't seen or heard the last of us as far as Real Xtreme is concerned."
KRISTY: "Speaking of Real Xtreme, I've heard rumors that The Outlaw received a _stiff_ fine for throwing that fireball at Marty Lyons. Any comment on that?"
WEREWOLF: "As a matter of fact, yes. You see, Kristy, Marty Lyons was warned time and again to keep his nose out of our business and chose to ignore that warning every time it was given. You know the old expression that goes 'When you play with fire, you get burned?'
"Well, Marty Lyons was apparently under the misconception that _he_ was fireproof." KRISTY: "Wow. That doesn't sound _anything_ like the Werewolf Gregorson _I_ remember...the Werewolf Gregorson who won his Rumble Pro contract right here in Duluth just three short months ago. Has teaming with Travis LaGrange really jaded you that much?"
WEREWOLF: "Kristy, my eyes have been opened in the past three months more than you could ever know and the fault for much of that can be laid squarely at the feet of Marty Lyons and Real Xtreme. So, if you want to blame someone for the changes you see in me, blame them. In the meantime, I have a very busy night to finish preparing for so..."
KRISTY: "I'm sorry. Any final thoughts before you go?" WEREWOLF: "Yes. To 'The Outlaw' Travis LaGrange, I look forward to our match, may the best man win, and know that, win or lose, I've got your back."
