LaValle Cup (interfederation tag-team tournament)

"Interfed Crockett Cup Results (Now the LaValle Cup)"

10 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In late January, I began soliciting teams for a Crockett Cup-style tournament to be run on WLS. After three posts, 16 teams from a variety of feds, many current or ex-champions, signed up, and the tourney was conducted via e-mail. The bonus for the first-time winner was that I would name all such future competitions after the handler of the winning team- look fo announcements for the LaValle Cup II this summer.
Here's how the tourney went: *The Trailer Park Assassins defeated Mayhem, Inc. in a stunning upset *Dragon Bomb (my pre-toruney favorites) defeated DOA Jones and bodybag Jones *The Kauffman Brothers won by submission over the Maloney Brothers (my own team) *Hardcore Heaven, debuting as a team, I believe, over the Ghetto Boyz *The Twin Terrors defeated the Ghetto Assassins *The Masters of Motion defeated Amerocan Patriot and American Boy *The American Dreams pinned the American Twins thanks to a poorly-aimed attack by the Dreams' arch-enemies, Mayhem, Inc. The Dream Police also took a few shots at the Dreams.
*The Dream Police squashed Elemental Force *In a long, hard-fought 35+ minute match, Dragon Bomb got by the Trailer Park Assassins as Mayhem, Inc. executed another run-in *Hardcore Heaven upset the Kauffmans, again requiring nearly 40 minutes (BTW, don't tell HH that these were "upsets"- they take a good deal of umbrage over their underdog status)
*The Twin Terrors defeated the Masters of Motion, but a figure-fourleglock execued on Tonnage would come back to haunt the TTs *The previous three matches averaged over 35 minutes, but the Dream Police advanced after a mere 8 1/2 minutes for two matches combined, as their opponents, the Amercican Dreams, were victimized a third time by a run-in (again, Mayhem, Inc.- Mayhem and manager Josh Brown were escorted to the local precinct house by the gendarmes)
*In the semis, Hardcore Heaven continued their surprising run, when Dragon Bomb's Brazilian Bomber was dqed for an (uncharacteristic) illegal chokehold *The Dream Police won what was essentially a handicap match over the Twin Terrors, as Tonnage's left leg could not support his body-weight, following the figure-four he suffered in the previous round (the night's one Pyrrhic victory)
*In the finals, the bruised, bloodied, inexperienced, and exhausted Hardcore Heaven defeated the rested, healthy, squash-win-after-squah-win-victors, the Dream Police.
I have since been informed that Hardcore Heaven have signed as a team with their first fed,. and it bears mentioning that this team beat current or former champions in each round. As a result of their win, the tourney, previosuly (inconsistently) referred to as a fantasy Crockett
Cup or the Interfed Tag team Tourney or the Interfed Crockett Cup is now
The LaValle Cup Tag Team Tournament
The next LaValle Cup TTT is scheduled for July-August, and announcements to that effect should be made in June. Congratulations to the winners, and thanks to everyone else who participated, roleplayed, or just lent advice (or, in the case of Tommy Smith from the AEWA, his announcing team).
