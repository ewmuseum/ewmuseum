A recurring interfederation tag-team tournament simulated on WLS, drawing entrants from AEWA, UWF, GIWF and other e-feds, renamed for the handler of its first champions after an April 1996 debut. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This interfederation tag-team tournament began in late January 1996, when its organizer started soliciting teams “from a variety of feds, many current or ex-champions” for a Crockett Cup-style bracket run on the WLS simulator and conducted entirely by e-mail; sixteen teams signed up across three recruiting posts.

Results posted 10 April 1996 traced five rounds of upsets and interference — Mayhem, Inc. running in on the American Dreams three times, a figure-four leglock still hobbling one wrestler two rounds after he took it — before Hardcore Heaven, debuting as a team, beat current or former champions in every round and took the final over the Dream Police. The tournament, until then “ inconsistently referred to as a fantasy Crockett Cup or the Interfed Tag Team Tourney,” was renamed after Hardcore Heaven's own handler.

Its second edition filled twenty-four entrants into three pools by 8 December 1996, each pool to run its own single-elimination bracket before a round-robin final between the three winners — two of the entered teams both called themselves the Machines — with AEWA's own announcing duo, Matt Wilson and Big Daddy Michael Hurt, billed to call the brackets.

Its surviving record runs through that second edition's pool announcement; no third edition or closing notice survives.

American E-Wrestling Association (AEWA)

Member (1996)
AEWA's own West Coast Connection (its three-time AEWA World Tag Team Champions) and Graveyard Shift units entered the LaValle Cup II bracket, with the West Coast Connection winning Pool C and reaching the tournament's own round-robin final; AEWA's own announcing duo, Matt Wilson and "Big Daddy" Michael Hurt, called the tournament's Pool B and Pool C rounds. AEWA's own announcing talent is separately credited on the tournament's first (April 1996) edition.
