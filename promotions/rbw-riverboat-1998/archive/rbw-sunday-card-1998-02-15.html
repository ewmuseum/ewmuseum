On the ring apron, Khan steps over the top rope and enters the ring. <Perris> Our final match of the evening pits the biggest man in the RBW against the smallest man here. Brute strength versus the speed and high flying antics of Iko Kimura. ***Ding ***Ding***Ding*** A true David and Goliath match as Khan moved to the center of the ring and waited for the diminutive Kimura to make a move. Kimura quickly circled the mighty Khan looking for a weakness as well as an angle to attack from. Hitting the ropes for a bit of speed and momentum, Kimura dipped his shoulder and struck Khan . . no reaction . . . the big man just stood there. Again Kimura hit the ropes before ducking his shoulder. He did this two more times . . . each time with the same results. Stepping back for a second, Kimura took a deep breath before hitting the ropes one more time. This time, Khan flicked out with his large arm and knocked the much smaller Kimura through air, who was dumped on his rear several feet back. Khan moved with the same ambling gait towards the still downed Iko. Grabbing him by the hair, Khan whipped Kimura into the ropes and dropped the light-heavyweight with a well placed boot to the chest. Kimura struggled to his feet but was still quicker than the slower Khan. Racing up the ropes, Kimura let fly with a picture perfect Backflip. Easily 15 feet off of the ground, Iko Kimura was plucked from the air by Khan. In one fluid motion, Khan tossed the smaller Kimura five rows deep into the crowd. Stepping over the top rope, Khan followed after Kimura who was struggling to get back to his feet. Rather than step over the metal railing surrounding the ring, Khan hefted a six foot section over his head. Kimura never saw what hit him as the giant brought it down on his head, instantly busting him open. Dropping the railing, Khan reached down and jerked Kimura up by the neck and into the air for all to see as the blood flowed freely from the gash on the young man's head. A hushed silence came over the crowd as they watched the lifeless body of Iko Kimura dangle limply in the giant's grasp. With a bang against the metal railing, Kimura was brought down with a Choke Slam. It was over. Khan picked himself up and tossed the limp body of the valiant Japanese wrestler back into the ring, then rolled back in for the 1 . . . 2 . . . 3 count. ***Ding ***Ding***Ding*** <Liza Hopner> Your winner . . . at 5:42 . . .
