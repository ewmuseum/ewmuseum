LWA (LaRose, 2000) — Roleplay

The Super LWA Infomercial

11 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen goes black, and the words "The LaRose Wrestling Alliance proudly brings to you the LWA Information Hour!!!!" appear on the screen, and then fade away as the scene changes to your standard infomercial set. There is a live crowd who are all obviously paid to be there, and a stage with several items on it, namely a chair, a pyramid of microwave ovens and a bazooka. An announcers voice begins to speak...]
Announcer: And here is your host...Liquid Hot Johnny Texas! [The audience begins to applaud as Liquid Hot Johnny Texas makes his way out from stage right...Johnny is dressed in blue jeans, a denim shirt, and a brown leather vest...]
Johnny(Enthusiatic to the point of absurdity): HELLO PEOPLE!!!! HOW Y'ALL DOING? Great to hear!!! Now I'm here to tell you about something I've just discovered!! Now this something is so great that I promise, you all will not believe your eyes or ears!!!
[The crowd ooos and ahhhs...] Johnny: I bet you all are saying to yourselves, "What's this guy got that's so great? Cause if it's some kind of scheme, then he's not gonna fool me...cause
I'm too smart for that...." Well, rest assured friends, this is no scheme, this is genuine Top-of the-line amazement that I've got for you all today!
It's true!!! [The crowd starts to look around at each other all excited like...] Johnny: That's right friends...What I'm talking about is a rather new federation called the LaRose Wrestling Alliance!!! You want to know how amazing this fed is people? C'mon do ya?
Crowd: Yeah Johnny, we want to know! Johnny: I can't hear ya people!!! Crowd: YEAH JOHNNY, WE WANT TO KNOW!!! Johnny: OK then, since you all asked so nicely, I'll show you... [Johnny goes over and picks up the bazooka that was laying on the stage, aims it directly at the pyramid of microwave ovens, and fires, causing the
Microwaves to explode! The crowd begins to applaud loudly...] Johnny: You wanted to know how amazing the LWA is? Well that's how amazing the LWA is!!! [The scene cuts to your typical living room in your typical apartment, where a lady about mid-forties is sitting waiting to talk...]
Lady: Hi, my name is Vanessa Ventamin, and before I discovered the LWA my life was in shambles, I hated my job, I had a no-good marriage, and my dog was dying. But then the LWA came along and showed me the correct way to live, now
I couldn't be happier!! It gave me the confidence I needed to quit my job, and pursue a meaningful career as an Airline Stewardess. It showed me that I didn't need to spend my life in a dead end relationship with a fat, lazy bastard, now I'm seeing a 25 year old Italian Stud. And It also cured my dog!
Thanks LWA, you really work! [The scene cuts back to the studio...] Johnny: Wow, as you saw from a real live person, the LWA works wonders for people...Now I'm gonna bring out a very special guest...Ladies and gentlemen, the LWA President, Mr. Kevin RockStar!!!!
[The LWA President, Mr. Kevin RockStar enters stage right, wearing his purple vinyl pants, and a black t-shirt. He is a young man, with long black hair, and a stylish beard. He's got a big phony smile on...]
Johnny: Hey Kevin! How's it going? Kevin: Fine man, how are you? Johnny: Kevin, I am Fan-damn-tasitic!!!! I'm telling you, ever since I discovered the LWA I've been in such a great mood! So what do you have to say to all these fine people about the Federation? You know...give us a bit of background...
Kevin: Well we've been up and running for a good three months or so...We hold one weekly card, and a monthly PPV. We are a booked fed, all our shows are hand written. We have three titles, the LWA World Heavyweight Championship, the LWA North American Championship, and the LWA Tag Team Championship...We accept all level handlers, from beginners to experts...We're basically a place for characters to grow, develop, and have fun.
Johnny: Interesting...So do you have any plans for adding any weekly shows, or titles? Kevin: Yes Johnny, we do have plans to add another show within the coming weeks, and at the next PPV, Sex, Drugs and Wrestling, we will be introducing a new belt...The South American Championship!
Johnny: Wow! Did you hear that folks? A new belt! [The audience begins to clap...] Johnny: We're gonna take another break, when we come back we will wrap things up...Stay tuned!!!
[The scene cuts to someone's swimming pool, in the backyard of a large mansion. Sitting poolside is a dirty old man, sipping a martini, and surrounded by three beautiful females...]
Dirty old man: Hello, my name is Sam Morie. Now I'm not gonna lie to you, before Liquid Hot Johnny Texas introduced me to the LWA, I was, plain and simple, a bum. I lived on the streets, and I begged every day for people's change so that I could buy booze, and maybe a muffin or something...But when I found the LaRose Wrestling Alliance, all that changed!!! Now I'm a multibillionaire thanks to the inspiration of the LWA, and I am no longer begging for change! Thanks LWA, you really work!
[The scene cuts back to the studio...] Johnny: You see people, what I'm trying to get you all to realize is that the
LWA really does work! Now Kevin, is there anything else you want to say to all the future LWA prospects out there?
Kevin: Yes Johnny, there is...Now, I admit, there are a lot of great feds out there, and I understand why so many would want to get into them...but what I want to ask you is this...What would be more rewarding as a wrestler, to go into an already "established" and "great" fed and get lost in the shuffle, or to go into a growing, up and coming fed and be one of the people that helps to make it great? Think about it, and when you've come to the right decision, we'll be here for you...You can visit us on the web at...
http://go.to/LWA Johnny: Powerful words, Kevin...I want to thank you for joining us...the address again is...
http://go.to/LWA We're outta time folks...I'll leave you with this...The LWA is helping millions achieve all their hopes and dreams...Now it's your turn...Let the LWA help you!
Thank you, and good evening. [The crowd begins to applaud, as Liquid Hot Johnny Texas is seen shaking members of the audience's hands, and the camera fades to black...]
