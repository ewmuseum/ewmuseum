Introduction ----------------------------------------------------------- Sundevil Wrestling Promotion is a relatively new booked e-wrestling league. Hopefully you're reading this FAQ because you're somewhat interested in becoming a member of the SWP. The SWP is dedicated to it's members, and promises a unique and fun e-wrestling experience.
As you can tell by the name, the SWP is regional federation meaning that we have a low amount of money to start out with. As a total fantasy feeling, the
SWP will try to gain more exposure around it's home state of Arizona. To sum it all up, to begin with, the SWP will have no camera men, barely ANY fans, a low number of wrestlers, and nothing spetacular that would set it away from any other federation.
But, as times goes on, the SWP will start to gain more fans which means making more money, which means touring out of the state, which means television contracts, and etc. Everything in the Sundevil Wrestling Promotion will be a total chain reaction. You will totally enjoy this unique experience.
The people behind the federation have extensive experience within the game of -e-Wrestling. We're trying to create a friendly, relaxed enviroment for people to participate in. We're a federation focused on fun. If something isn't working out, please tell us, and we'll try to make your experience that much better. So without further ado, let's get on with the show.
2. The Committee [Fantasy Purposes] ------------------------------------------------------------------ The Committee is the brain, the driving force behind a federation. The SWP is made up of two people who run it all and other greatly appreciated people who are always willing to help out by writing weekly matches.
President Jessica Falcon - The Sundevil Wrestling Promotion is headed by e-wrestling mid-vetran, Ms. Jessica Falcon. Overall, she has a general knowledge of the game and is considered to be a masterful fed head when she uses help. And, this time, she got some help.
Vice President Tim Straka- Tim Straka has been in the game well, not for that long. But, Jessica and Tim both are very optimistic that he'll be able to pull his act together for the long haul to help the SWP out.

4. The SWP Style ------------------------------------------------------------------ The SWP Style is what we refer to as our modus operandi for the the SWP. We employ an ABS system (Action in Brackets), which has been used by many federations, the IIWF being one of the premiere ones. Our idea is that quality is far more important than substance. We write matches that are clear, and well written, rather than trying to flood you with a bunch of useless garble.
The second thing that SWP is aiming for is the regional fed atmosphere. This meaning, as explained way up there in the introduction, that we start out extremely small and work our way up. We start out with hardly anything and, a few months later, end up with EVERYTHING! In a comparison, we're like the
"Little Train That Could." The SWP has a lot of good workers, and wants quality applicants. It's very angle based, along the lines of the WWF, but we also stress in-ring action as well. In essence, we have something for everyone.
5. The SWP Staff ------------------------------------------------------------------ Donald Swearingean-Interviewer Donald Swearingean is a backstage interviewer. This is your heel man. He's always around the heels back stage and is always on their side. Whoever is hated more, that's who he likes. He refuses to do in-rings after his previous attempts, in which he was pelted with a variety of foreign objects from the crowd.
Kristen Mercer-Interviewer Kristen Mercer is the neutral interviewer of the trio. She interviews in-ring or backstage. She tries not to side too much with anyone, whether they're neutral, heel, or face.
Walter Ramsey-Interviewer Walter Ramsey, interviews in-ring or backstage. He is the exact opposite of Steve Williams . He's a face interviewer. He sides with whoever he interviews, most of the times out of fear, but he refuses to side with heels. He has very low self-esteem and has been attacked multiple times backstage by antagonistic wrestlers.
Paul Dodd-Ring Commentator Paul Dodd is the heel ring commentator. He's the color man of the two announcers, and is always ready to fire off a quick retort to Rothwell's comments. He's usually dressed in fine armani suits, with a cuban cigar in one hand, and a Budweiser in the other.
Greg Rothwell-Ring Commentator Greg Rothwell is the face commentator, and play by play man. He's been with the SWP since the beginning and is one of the backers of the federation. He sides with the faces on a consistent basis, although he calls matches right down the middle and will praise any wrestler for his talent and skill. He is usually found at ringside wearing jeans and plaid shirts. Very Grizzly Adams-like.
John Wright - Ring Announcer John Wright is the number one Ring Announcer. He only introduces the wrestlers in the beginning of matches and announces who wins at the end of the matches.
He's liked by the fans and sometimes gets face pops for just standing in the ring. Kristen Bioux - Ring Announcer Kristen Bioux is John Wright's back-up in case he gets injured. She's not seen very much. 6. Match Determination ------------------------------------------------------------------ The SWP resolution system works on the "heat" concept. Heat is based on how much draw there is for your wrestler. This is created by doing in-rings, flashes, and getting involved in angles (which is a HUGE draw). Most important in match resolution is the angle. In reality, angles are what make wrestling interesting. If you're in this federation just to win a belt, you're in the wrong fed. This isn't to say you'll never be world champion, but you'll probably lose before you win.
Usually, an angle determines what the match is going to be resolved as. If Wrestler A has planned with Wrestler B to make the first match be a no-contest, it will more than likely be a no-contest, as to drawing "heat" for the second match, where Wrestler B will seek revenge by winning, and the feud continues.
This, however, assumes a "planned" angle, which is only one type, where match determination is resolved by the handlers.
The "unplanned" angle is an angle that is either simply set up by the handlers, or is created by happenchance, with the bookers in control. This is where in-rings and flashes, etc. come into play. The heat that is being generated
(whether heel or face) will determine who wins or loses the match. If you need a percentage breakdown, 60% will come from Heat, 30% from overall participation, and 10% from strategies (covered later).
7. Long Term Involvement ------------------------------------------------------------------ SWP likes to plan out storylines way in advance. The development of storylines entails handler cooperation to create huge, exciting detailed angles, critical to the success of any federation. Honestly, what do you remember more? Stone
Cold's intense feud with Vince McMahon, or the in-ring action? You remember the story more, rather than just bits and chunks of the story. Getting in touch with other handlers and working with them is one of the highlights of e-Wrestling. If you need help finding a long term storyline, please, contact the fedheads, we're always willing to help.
8. Roleplaying ------------------------------------------------------------------ Some quick points about roleplaying... No spamming. The maximum RPs we want to see from you (per week) is two. This should allow you enough time to develop two quality roleplays, rather than ten mediocore ones. Remember, quality over quantity.
Don't break kayfabe. It's unprofessional. Don't talk about "jobbers" and "heels" in your roleplays and in-rings. We don't like it, and if you think it is cool, we'll kindly show you the door.
Concerning the length of flashes, we like tight concise flashes. We don't want huge 15k stories. Say what you have to say, and leave it at that.
Swearing is fine, but don't over do it, and use it sparingly. If we find a flash is too profanity laden, expect it to be rejected and began in your email box with REWORK plastered on it.
The WWF, WCW, ECW, NJPW, AJPW, NWA, nWo, lWo, DX, The Rock, and Stone Cold don't exist. We don't want to see references to real wrestlers or real federations. If you'd like to use it in the background to your character on your application, we'll allow it, but that's it. However, if you need to describe something, such as X-Factor, please ask us before you put "X-Pac's finisher" in your flash. We're willing to help, really!
Don't use other wrestlers in your flashes unless they give you consent. That's it. If you have problems with the above points, talk to us. Don't try to loophole around it. We have common sense, and will let you know if there's something wrong with your flash.
9. Strategies (kindly lifted from the AGW Players Guide :)) ------------------------------------------------------------------ A strat should describe your wrestler's behavior/attitude/intentions for a match. It should contain information on how your wrestler will be dressed, any special actions at the start of the match, e.g. will they push or shove their opponent during the instructions, attack before the bell etc. It should contain a list of the moves that they'll try and use during the match, any special combination of moves they'll use to try to get a win. Any special areas of attack they'll try, e.g. they attack their opponents knee because they know it was injured in an earlier match. Aspects of their own or opponents character/style that they'll try and exploit (e.g. they're bigger than their opponent so they'll concentrate on power moves, or they're smaller but faster so they'll concentrate on high flying moves). You may even consider a series of moves in response to what you know about your opponent and their ring style for example.
Also, a nice touch and the sign of a good worker/seller is to describe some possible counters to your own moves, that your opponent may or may not have thought of. This helps make for a great match and it helps the writers write the cards. This type of attitude also shows me that you are not doing this only out of ego, but that you care about the show.
Note there is no promise that any or all of the strat will be used, although we will do our best to use as much as is possible in the circumstances. Please don't get frustrated if it is the case that your angle/roleplay/strat/or challenge doesn't seem to get used. For a variety of reasons each is constrained by the 'big picture' and what others want as well.
10. General Rules ------------------------------------------------------------------ 1. Don't whine to us. We know what we're doing, really :) If you have an issue with something, figure out what you want to say, then tell us. Going off on fedheads without thinking first is a quick way to jobberdom :)
2. Stay active. Otherwise we'll be talking to you about your position on the roster. 3. SWP has instituted a new way to bring on "rookie" talent. A three week probationary period occurs once a new handler/wrestler has been brought in. The SWP expects an angle to be worked out between the fedheads and another wrestler or wrestlers within two weeks of being in the federation. This is to your advantage, because it'll keep you interested :) The SWP brings a cut and dry formula to the table. If you don't participate within your first three weeks, you get cut (unless you have a legitimate reason worked out with the fedheads).
This was primarily instituted to avoid overcrowding and keeping the roster small. 4. Don't use other people's wrestlers in your flashes without their consent. 5. It's a game. Have fun dammit :) 11. Weekly Shows ------------------------------------------------------------------ The SWP weekly show will be called CACTUS and that card will be held on Sunday nights. At the moment the card will hold from 3-5 matches a week. PPVs will be held bimonthly, and will be held on Sundays only. They will be held in the months of January, March, May, July, September and November. PPVs will hold more, and longer matches than the weekly. There will be a flash packet in which flashes come out on. It will be held twice a week, and is named "Bringing The
Heat." 12. Titles ------------------------------------------------------------------ SWP State Title The biggest title you can hold in the Sundevil Wrestling Promotion. This title will be defended once a month.
SWP Regional Title The SWP Regional Title is the second best title you can hold. Rookies and newcomers to the federation may have a shot at this title in their first match and whatnot due to the fact that this title will be defended every other week.
13. Singles Application ------------------------------------------------------------------ You may have a female or male wrestler. You can also have one of each or two of one if you want to. Right now there is no tag team division, although there will be tag team matches every now and then. This fed will not be as hardcore as others neither. There may be the occasional hardcore/extreme match, but the
SWP will not be based on it.
