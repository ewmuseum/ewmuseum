A New Unexpected Chapter Begins

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-14 There I stood in The Void, my month long trek was a success, I had succeeded in opening a gateway to The Void, the very place where Gods refused to go, a place where beings of infinite power dwell, and the place where I would attain the power I needed in order to storm the citadel of the Gods and take from them what I have tried to do for the last two hundred years. Their seat of power. But I couldn’t do it on my own, I needed help, thus why I was here. Here and only here could I get not necessarily the allies I needed but the assistance I was in dire need of. Now I stood before the most powerful beings that all of creation had to offer, my plans so far had been a success, now all I had to do was take that one last step and this war was all but over. Looking up into the darkness, all I could see of them was a vague outline of their dark forms, all of them were looking down onto me, as if in judgment, and that’s probably what they were doing, judging if I was worthy of not only their attention, but worthy of the intrusion and annoyance I had caused them by appearing suddenly out of no where. Them looking down on me was something I didn’t enjoy, for the first time in my existence I felt truly weak, it was not something I was accustomed to, something about it didn’t feel right, something about all of this didn’t feel right, though I couldn’t quite place my finger upon it. The silence was then parted, as if Moses were here himself and it was the red sea, it was split in two as finally one of the beings spoke. “You come here for assistance in your ongoing battle with the Gods….but you yet….I mean….you….” the words were stumbled over. “CUT!!!” --*****-- With that the “Void” lights up and a man walks onto the scene with a glass of water handing it to Tarrant. In front of Tarrant stand four men and a woman all are now pulling out books from their back pockets and begin reading them feverishly, and from the left a fat man walks into the scene, yelling up a storm. “NO, NO, NO!! You would think after a week of rehearsing you should be able to get your opening line right correct wouldn’t you Jessica!?” The women in black looks up from her book to meet the eyes of the fat man, who is standing there with his hands on his hips glaring at her with all the anger and frustration he can seem to muster up at that moment. She almost seems to brush him off and looks back down to her book. “Well maybe if the part wasn’t re written seven times I would be able to get it right Randolph.” Her response was said in a matter of fact tone, very snobbish and you could tell she didn’t much care for this Randolph person. “How dare you talk to me like that, I am the director, you will treat me with RESPECT!!!” It didn’t help his case any that he was beginning to squeal like a pig. Tarrant just looked on and watched as the two began to scream at one another about who was right and who was wrong, from the looks and sounds of it, this wasn’t the first time these two had gotten into an argument, Tarrant just shook his head and turned around to walk away and off what now appeared to be nothing more then a set. “And where do you think YOUR going?!” Stooping in mid stride Tarrant turned slowly to address the director who had decided to take a small break from Jessica and now squeal at him. “I am going to my dressing room to relax, we have been at this for eight strait hours and have gotten no where, I am tired, hungry and am going to relax.” Turning around Tarrant continued walking in the same direction he had before, all the while he could hear squeals of protest from behind him, as he walked further the got fainter, thank god for that he thought to himself as he came to his dressing room door, opened it and walked in. It was nothing special really, the life of a wrestler was grossly over exaggerated, only a chair, couch, small TV. and some bottled water in a bucket of ice awaited him as he closed the door gently behind him and made his way to the bucket of ice and water, grabbed a drink and unceremoniously plopped down in his chair, closing his eyes he lay his head back and remained there unmoving. Until that is, a knock came at the door. Letting out a sigh of annoyance Tarrant took a drink from his water, tilted his head towards the door and opened one eye. “Come in” The door opened and walking through was a women who was quite familiar, she was the one who played FATE in all of Tarrant’s promos, the women he had killed and who had returned from beyond to extract her revenge upon him, however, everyone in real life knew her as Amber. “Why did you leave the set, Randolph was none to happy.” Her voice was soft and soothing to the headache that had been forming in Tarrant’s head for a few hours now, her voice always did that for him. “I left because I was tired of their bickering; I left because in a matter of days I have a match against Blaise McClancy in the GTT4 tournament and at this rate will never be able to finish one video package let alone the three I had planned to hype the match. Do you realize I am the underdog going into this? The fans are actually cheering for HIM, I have to get these done if I have any hopes of going into my match a fan favorite, otherwise I won’t be picked to win by management.” Tarrant rolled his had back to its previous position facing the ceiling and closed his eyes not seeing that Amber had walked up behind him, reaching out she began to rub his shoulders. “Who would have thought that one day, wrestling stars and match winners would come down to who could bring in the most money and hype, and NOT actual wrestling skill eh?” Grunting in response Tarrant took a drink from his water, careful not to spill any on his face which was covered with white makeup. “I didn’t that’s for sure, but that’s what the business has become, so that’s what I have to adapt to.” The room was then taken over by an uncomfortable silence as Amber continued to rub Tarrant’s shoulder, opening his eyes Tarrant looked up to her. “You know that no matter what I love you right?” “Well I should hope so.” She said with a playful smile on her face. “What makes you ask such a silly question?” Sitting up Tarrant put his hands to his face and rubbed his eyes a bit before looking up and staring off into space. “I don’t know, its just…well, I have been at this for years Amber, carrying the Tarrant name, bring a new element to the Goth character, taking it places it had never seen. All the while doing so, taking MYSELF places I had never seen. These past twenty years have been kind to me, but I haven’t been kind to them.” “What do you mean?” you could hear the confusion in her voice, Tarrant shook his head and continued. “I took everything for granted, that’s what I mean. For years I sat at the top of this business convinced that I was the best it had to offer, and at one time, that very well may have been true, but now, in these present days, I’m not so sure anymore.” “Your not thinking of leaving it all behind are you?” Shaking his head, Tarrant rubbed his eyes again, this time he didn’t do so out of frustration or tension, he did so to wipe away a the tears that were beginning to roll down out. “I can’t say the thought hasn’t entered my mind, as I look at the business now, and compare it to what it once was, I know they are nothing the same. It has changed, and over the years, I have refused to go with that change. Now…..now I am nothing more then an old man, clutching to the remains of what I once had, and what I was once able to do. My name used to be synonyms with wrestling, now it’s all but forgotten except by those who are old enough to remember what the name Tarrant truly meant.” “And what did that name used to mean?” Amber asked, never once stopping with the shoulder rub. “It used to mean respect. It used to mean recognition and admiration, but over the years it has become know for a man clutching on to the remains of what made him famous.” Standing up, Tarrant puts the cap on his water and tosses it down onto the spot where he was just sitting, and looks into Amber’s eyes, her ever beautiful eyes. “If ever there was a person in my life that I couldn’t be without, it would be you my dear; this is why I asked if you knew that no matter what, I loved you. For years you have stood by me....” “And for many more shall I ever be by your side my love” she interrupted. Smiling softly, Tarrant nodded. “It bring me joy to hear those words, but in a few days, I face Blaise McClancy, a man I have never faced before, a new generation of super star, a man whom I have great respect for, and although young has a great head on his shoulders. He’s but only twenty five, and me, nearly forty three, I fear I may have finally met my match, and if that’s true, so be it, at least it would be to a man whom I have a deep respect." As Amber tried to voice her protest, Tarrant waves a hand to silence her and she indeed quiets down in response. “I know my dear, you would have me believe that I have a chance, and you may be right, but if I am to have a chance at this, it can’t be, and it wont be as this Goth character that’s well past its prime, it wont be as Ehlexander Tarrant, for he can no longer fight my battles. No longer will I allow myself to be known for Tarrant’s morbid ways, I will be taking myself back to the days that built my career, to actual wrestling, I say screw the business, I say screw the hype, screw it all. I hate what this business has become, its nothing more then a monstrous hype machine, long ago was the true wrestling element taken away. Ehlexander is a product of that hype machine; no longer can I allow him to continue on." With that Tarrant turns and heads for the door, just as he pouts his hand on the knob, Amber finally speaks up. “But I don’t understand any of this, if you won’t face him as Ehlexander Tarrant, whom will you face him as?” Standing there a moment, Tarrant then turns the knob and cracks the door open. “Simple, I fear that the only way I can progress any further in this tournament, the only way I can win my match is to be true to myself. No longer can I hide behind the face of Ehlexander; no longer can I let him fight my battles. Its time I do that myself, its time the world see what I myself can do, and not a name and reputation.” “I will face him not as Ehlexander Tarrant, but as myself. It’s time I let this business know just how it was I came to be who I am today, and why it was that I used to rule the circuits in Japan, Puerto Rico and Mexico. I’ll show them all, and finally prove to myself that I have what it takes to succeed without the Ehlexander name, and to win with my real one, no gimmicks, no tricks, nothing but my own ability and my own name.” “Chris Tarrant.” “Its that, or I go out in one hell of a bang, either way, it’ll be without the Goth, it will be without the scare tactics, death, darkness and voodoo, at least if I fail, I can say it was me, and not someone else that gave it their all, because that’s what Blaise McClancy will be getting…my all.” With that Tarrant opens the door and shuts it softly behind him, leaving Amber alone in the room. Still looking at the place where Tarrant stood she softly whispers to herself, “Good Luck.” then exits the room as well. -End Scene.-
