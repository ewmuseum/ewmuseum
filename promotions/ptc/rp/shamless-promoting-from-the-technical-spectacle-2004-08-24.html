Shamless Promoting from the Technical Spectacle

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 -Begin Soliloquy- Good evening, let me introduce myself for the imbeciles over in Prime Time Central who have been living under a rock the last eight years. My name is Jerms, The Technical Spectacle, and the Greatest Technical Wrestler in History. And….the reason why 4WW is what it is. I’m sure you’ve heard some harsh things said about me over the last few weeks from my so called “teammates”, but you must believe me when I say I’m the good guy here! Sure I am better than everyone else, does that make me a bad person? Jealousy has stricken the 4WW and that’s why I dislike it so much. They treat me like crap over there, I deserve better. Fnord, DeAngelo, and Biddle have all been beaten relentlessly by me (some on numerous occasions) that they cannot fathom the though of teaming up with me. Let’s Go Core! Let’s Go Now! At least you guys can show some sort of Team Unity! Oh who the hell am I kidding? Screw this stupid PTC Survival, I don’t need it. I already look good without this big event. But the man, however, says I have to do it. I am obligated to compete in this, or so states my damn contract. Which I need remind the 4 Winds Wrestling association that it expires next month and I have no intention of resigning with them! I’ve got my movies to work on, people to see, and places to be. Who needs wrestling anymore? One more thing… Go See My Movies! -End Soliloquy- “Nobody move or I’ll pick you off one by one!” Downtown Baltimore where a jewelry store heist is in progress. Three masked crooks stand in the jewelry store holding AK 47’s waving them around in a panic. “What’d we do Jack?” one of the crooks asks. “I’m thinking you idiot,” replies Jack. “We’re going to get out of this thing, just keep your cool!” The crooks began the heist only five minutes prior but before they could make their grand escape the Police and SWAT teams swarmed the building, blocking off all the exits. A booming voice is heard coming from the street. “PLEASE COME OUT WITH YOUR HANDS UP! RELEASE THE HOSTAGES OR PREPARE TO FACE THE CONSEQUENCES!” The crooks stand there aghast; they did not expect this to happen. They thought this only happened in movies. What were they to do? The cops were using the megaphone now issuing threats. “No! No! No!” yells Jack who is obviously the leader of the trio. “We make the threats here. Give me that phone!” Jack, who is the tallest and must muscular looking of the three takes a phone from one of his cohorts, he begins to shout demands into the phone. “You listen here coppers,” he says into the phone. “You don’t tell us what to do! We’ve got twenty hostages in here and we’ll do whatever it takes to get out of this thing alive!” A police negotiator is heard coming from the other line trying to calm down the criminal. “If any pig storms this place we pick off the hostages. We want you to back off and get those TV Cameras the hell out of here,” he continues to yell into the phone. “Alright? We’ll issue a list of demands in five minutes. Send an unarmed citizen up to the door to retrieve the list and we’ll release some hostages!” Jack tosses the phone back to the man he got it from. He walks over to the jewelry stores cash register where the twenty hostages, a mix of employees and customers, are huddled together in sheer panic. Jack paces back and forth in front of them. “You cooperate, you live. It’s real simple!” Jack explains. A man’s voice is heard coming from the back of the store. “He boss, come check this out. Look at the gem I found baby!” “You stay here Joe. I’m going to see what the hell Lou is talking about.” Jack says. Joe nods his head and stands before the hostages as Jack runs to the back of the store to see what all the fuss is about. “Ya…You heard the man. Don’t Don’t do nothin’ nothin’ stupid aight?” Joe stutters. The hostages tremble with fear. A security guard sits in front of the rest of the hostages trying to devise a plan to save these innocent people. He looks around the large jewelry store for an answer but to no avail. The blinds have been drawn except for the one on the thin front door. There is, however, a gigantic glass roof overhead. The sun hangs directly overhead, causing you to shield your eyes if you look up at the window. The sound of arguing from the back becomes audible. Joe, who has seemingly forgetting his task, races to the back to see what the fuss is about from his two partners in crime. The security guard stands up. “Alright people let’s go!” whispers the security guard. An elderly Asian man crawls his way to the front. “Are you crazy? They would kill us! As the manager of this store I ask of you to stay calm and situated. The police are coming!” The security guard begins to shake the Asian man yelling. “This is our only chance you fool!” he says. This arouses the three criminals who stop arguing and rush out to see their hostages. With their guns pointed, Jack begins to open his mouth when all of a sudden an enormous shadow is cast down on them from the rooftop. A shadow of a man is seen. The sun is still peaking in around the man making it impossible to get a good look at him. The crooks shield their eyes when all of a sudden the man pulls out a gun and shoots downward breaking the glass. The crooks run for cover underneath some desks as the man crashes to the floor in front of the hostages. The three crooks begin to open fire towards the hostage area. The man ducks and rolls out as the bullets whiz past his head. “Who the hell is this guy?” shouts one of the crooks. An announcer’s voice kicks in. “Against all odds comes a man who has owned the competition in the Wrestling Ring. Now you can see him on the big screen for in many Feature Films from Talesmith Productions.” The intruder pulls out two 9MM and begins to fire back. He wears a sleeveless police uniform, badge included, and he is very muscular and tan. This massive man continues to dart around the jewelry store until he picks off each of the criminals one by one. “He saved us!” shouts one of the female employees. “Who are you?” asks the security guard. The man, who has now bent down to check on Joe stands with his back to the hostages. He wipes his brow before slowly turning around. “It’s…It’s Jerms!” shouts the Asian man. The announcer’s voice begins to talk again. “Even though you can now see him on the big screen, for one night only turn on Pay-Per-View to see different Worlds Collide!” Jerms nods to the hostages before slowly walking to the front door. He stops, turns back and looks at the hostages, before exiting. Police swarm the building to secure the Jewel store as Jerms slowly makes his way down the street unnoticed. “The Technical Spectacle…..movie star…and wrestling legends joins the Team of 4WW for one night only at the Prime Time Central event….SURVIVAL!” The screen fades out. -The End-
