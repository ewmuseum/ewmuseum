TTD - The Rodriguez Brothers -v- Metallo Zords

12 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene 1 (Chivo) The scene opens in a room that is empty aside from a mirror on the wall and a man sitting on a chair with his back to the camera, half of his head is totally shaved, and the other has a slight stubble beginning to grow. The reflection in the mirror shows this man to be Chivo, but his appearance has changed slightly, aside from the hair that is beginning to grow on the left side of his head, his left eye does not have the black paint around it. The right eye however still has the black paint around it, and the right half of his head is still completely shaved. He begins to shake his head and turns so his right side is facing the camera and the left side is reflecting in the mirror. A sinister grin falls over his face and he begins to speak. Chivo: So Metallo Zords, in only a few short days you get to find out exactly what we have planned for you, and those plans don’t include you walking out of the arena under your own power. Thus meaning you will not be leaving with hopes of one day been PTC Tag Team Champions either. So you best think about the match in hand…. Chivo pauses and turns toward the mirror. Chivo: What are you doing here? I don’t need you around right now, I have work to do. He continues to look at the mirror almost as if he is listening to someone speak, he does this for about five seconds, then begins to shake his head. Chivo: That does not matter, I have them right where I want them, I don’t need you around to screw things up again. So I suggest you leave now, or at least keep your mouth shut, and let me take care of business. Chivo then turns back away from the mirror and is looking at the wall with his right side facing the camera. Chivo: Metallo Zords you have now come to the point where your arrogance has become overconfidence. You may think we have no chance, and thus you are making a very grave mistake, you are underestimating us. Chivo pauses and an angered look falls over his face as he again turns to face the mirror. Chivo: You made me say that, that is something you would say, quit making me say things like that, I have told you once, now keep your mouth shut, you are not needed here. I am trying to say something and you just keep getting in my ear, unless you want to end up like The Metallo Zords I suggest you stop now. Chivo turns back to face the wall and is about to speak again. He then stops and a frustrated look falls on his face. He holds up his hand towards the mirror while still looking at the wall. He holds his hand up for a moment and then lets it drop and begins to speak Chivo: As much as I hate to admit it you may be one of the best tag teams in LOW. Now that does not mean you are allowed to talk, so don’t bother. Metallo Zords, what my annoying friend here just made me say about you underestimating me, may just have a shred of truth to it. You do seem to think that you are unbeatable, and you may think that we would not stand a chance against you, which does sound a lot like you don’t think we can beat you. Which would indeed mean that you are underestimating us. I could be wrong about this, and I am just taking your thoughts out of context, and I hope I am, because stepping into the ring with a team that wants to tear your heart out is bad enough, but stepping into the ring with a team that wants to tear your heart out, and not being prepared for what they are going to do, is a mistake that would be more than fatal. So Metallo Zords, now that you know what a huge mistake you are on the verge of making, will you change your thought process or will you continue to approach our meeting with ignorance. The choice is yours, and yours alone, I suggest you think it over real well, as this may be the biggest decision of your lives. So don’t just keep playing your little games, because you will never get inside my head, as I have told many worthy adversaries before you, trying to get into my head is not the way to go about taking me down, because in doing so you are fighting a battle you will never win. Chivo turns to face the mirror again. Chivo: I know that, I don’t need you to tell me, just let me get to that, there are more important things that need to be said. Chivo stops and looks into the mirror again as if he were listening to someone talk to him. He begins to shake his head and then turns away from the mirror to face the wall again. Chivo: The only time wits will even come into play in this match is when you come to and collect yours after we have dropped you on your heads, and pinned you for the one, two, three. Even if it were a battle of wits, I would not be coming in unarmed, I am much more intelligent then you may give me credit for, and you will find that out come this week. Now while we are talking about lack of wits, what makes you so smart, because you can look up a word in a thesaurus and use against someone who you think will not understand it, well if that is what shows intelligence then by alls means you are the smartest men in the world. So Me…. Chivo turns to the mirror again and a shocked look falls over his face Chivo: That is a good point, that is the first intelligent thing you have said all day. Maybe I will keep you around after all, you might come in handy. Chivo turns back to the wall Chivo: Let me ask you this Metallo Zords, who do you have to face first? Me, so why are you so concerned about winning the titles already? Now don’t get me wrong, you need to worry about the other teams, very much so, but worrying about them now is not a good thing to do, because if you are looking down the road come this week you will not even make it to the next round. Chivo stops speaking and begins to clap his hands, then begins to speak in a sarcastic tone. Chivo: I must applaud your little act Metallo Zords. You want to see multi tasking Metallo Zords, then that is what you will get. This week, we will look down at you and laugh at the same time. How is that for multi tasking? He drops the sarcastic tone and continues. Chivo: Metallo Zords you have learned a lot in your career, but you still have a lot to learn, and we will be glad to teach you a lesson or two, when we meet for the second time this week. I will teach you that nothing comes easy, and that you have to be more than just a kid with a brain to win matches in the wrestling world. Then I will teach you that no matter how much something hurts there is something else right around the corner that is going to hurt even more. Then Finally I will teach you that people like you never beat people like us, unless there is a reason. So I again come back to my plan, that you seem to think is just me whining and making excuses. You go ahead and think that, because then when things come full circle and you find out that my plan was not a joke at all. But until then you just go right on thinking that I am making excuses. I told La Envidia Mata about a plan that was set up for him and he did the exact same thing you are doing now. Review the tapes Metallo Zords, and find out what happened to him. Chivo again turns to the mirror and sits as if he is listening he begins to nod his head and then still nodding turns back to face the wall. He stops nodding as he does he begins to speak again. Chivo: Take a good look Metallo Zords, because from this point forward this is as close as you are going to get to the PTC Tag Team Title. The Rodriguez Brothers will stop you on your quest to the PTC Tag Team Titles like we did to you in BDW. I still remember BDW's last program. The Metallo Zords laying on their back, with The Rodriguez Brothers standing over them. So Metallo Zords, once the time for talk has come to and end. We will be ready, because we know that you will be ready to fight, and I know that you will not go down easy. Unless you change your views on a few of the things I have discussed with you, you are headed for obscurity courtesy of yours truly. We are ready for you Metallo Zords, are you going to be ready for us? Chivo turns back to the mirror and appears to be whispering towards it as if he were talking to it. He then stops for a minute and then almost evil grin falls over his face and he breaks into devilish laughter, the laughter continues to intensify them stops suddenly, Chivo then drops his head to look at the floor as the does the scene fades to black. Scene 2 (Nicky Jam) [ The screeching sound of large rubber tires hitting the black asphalt of the John Fitzgerald Kennedy International Airport can only signal the landing of arriving Boeing 747 licensed to Delta Airlines. The sounds of roaring engines from departing and arriving planes fill the air as inside of the airport terminal the hustle and bustle of businessman and women tourists alike fill the air. The Delta Airlines flight 7832 from Miami slowly comes taxiing into the docking bay as it heaves to a stop. Inside of the terminal as young women in a navy blue business jacket and skirt that stops right above her knees, steps behind the terminal desk. The light from inside of the airport reflect off the somewhat dull set of golden wings on the wing of her jacket as she approaches a small microphone.] Woman:"Now arriving Flight 7832 nonstop service to Indianapolis from Miami...arriving at gate D-12." [The plane stands stationary as the black nose of the large aircraft sits a feet from the Plexiglass window. A Delta attendant slowly opens the door of gate D-12 as passengers slowly begins to disembark from the plane. After a few businessmen and a few tourist step from the loading gate carrying on there carry-on luggage, the UPWA's very own Nicky Jam steps out of the loading gate with his black Nike duffel in his hand. He starts walking from the terminal wearing a basic UPWA T-shirt, grey sun visor, baggy blue denim jeans with white stitching on the side, and a pair of black Adidas Superstar Cells. He reaches up with his free hand adjusting his dark gray visor slightly over his eyes but not hindering his eyesight one bit for himself but cutting off his eye contact from everyone else. He continues to walk as he reaches an escalator moving down towards the baggage claim. He runs his tongue over his front teeth as his head perks up. He looks around swearing to himself that someone is calling his name. Unfortunately, he was correct. He looked up the escalator and there he was, in the flesh. Mr. Green. Nicky Jam cursed under his breath as he watched Mr.Green excuse himself through people on the moving escalator as he makes his way towards him. Nicky Jam shakes his head as he looks over at Mr.Green underneath the bill of his visor.] Nicky Jam: "To be totally honest I never would have thought that I would ever see you again. I figured I shook you up enough to the point where you would probably put in your resignation the next day." [Mr.Green gave a weak smile, rubbing the back of his neck.] Mr.Green: "Yeah well even though our last meeting wasn't exactly my dream interview but my bosses loved it. They though it was so real." Nicky Jam: "Hey news flash, suit. It was real. Everything I said. Everything I do. It isn't some little gimmick. This isn't some little game or an act. This is all real. Besides, what do you want this time? I got to head to the hotel and I got a taxi waiting for me. Boy, us UPWA superstars travel first class." Mr.Green:"Well, I was hoping to be able to grab one more interview with you before you step into the ring against The Metallo Zords." Nicky Jam: "It's not going to happen today. I got things to do and places to be. Besides, you guys aren't going make this worth my while, then I'm not interested." [Mr.Green smiles as he places a hand on Nicky Jam's shoulder. Nicky Jam, in turn, looks over at him quickly with a nasty glare. Mr.Green slowly removes his hand from Nicky Jam's shoulder, placing it back into the pocket of his suit jacket.] Mr.Green: "Well instead of a taxi, how about a limousine ride to the Ritz-Carlton?" Nicky Jam: "For real?" Mr.Green: "And of course the same dollar amount that we discussed in our first meeting." [Nicky Jam looks over at him, raising up the collar of his gray visor. He looks at Mr.Green with a smile on the side of his mouth. He nods his head in approval.] Nicky Jam: "Okay, you got a deal. Now where is this limousine you're talking about?" Mr.Green: "Right outside...follow me." Nicky Jam: "Then lets roll." [After grabbing another bag from the baggage claim. They finally make their way over to a Lincoln stretch limousine awaiting them outside at the nearest curb as a driver stands in waiting holding the passenger rear side door open. Nicky Jam tosses his bags inside as he steps into the limousine with Mr.Green close behind. Nicky Jam takes a seat in the rear of the limousine as Mr.Green sits directly next to him. As usual he pulls out his tape recorder but this time no legal pad of questions. He is going at them in the dark.] Mr.Green: "You are a part of the UPWA, a point that had been quite apparent. This week in the PTC Tag Team Title Tournament you and your brother Chivo face a team you have wrestled before The Metallo Zords. You two teams will go head to head?" Nicky Jam: "Whoa, let's calm that down right there. You know I was finally able to catch a glimpse of The Metallo Zords a little while back. I've seen a lot of freaks and crap like that, but this ones take the cake. I mean they are a mixture between Power Rangers and Star Trek. Just add a few wrestling abilities, throw it all in a blender, set on puree and chill for ten minutes. Afterwards you have The Metallo Zords...serves twelve. Hell this guys are more of a damn piece of disgruntled art than they are wrestlers. I'm so tired of this bullsh*t, man." Mr.Green:"What are you saying? What you don't want to fight The Metallo Zords in the Tag Team Title Tournament?” Nicky Jam: “What can I tell you? I don’t deserve this SHT. I mean for Gods Sake, we’re fighting a team that we have already defeated! We deserve better than this! You know how we are. We're The Rodriguez Brothers, damnit! We're the sh*t without the toilet paper! Understand this.... I don't give a damn who in the hell The Metallo Zords are, what they have done, or what they will do. I've gotten notes from some other wrestlers about this and that, who is who in the PTC, and you know what? I don't give a flying f*ck! That's right! I'm a grown-ass man! I do what I damn well please and until someone comes in the ring and TRIES to shut me up. I'm going to keep talking until the damn day I die. Damnit, I'm the UPWA United States Champion, the most unpredictable bastard that will ever grace a damn PTC ring." [ Mr.Green nods his head, checking the tape making sure its still recording.] Mr.Green: "Well it has been mentioned, that you will be quite the opponent for any wrestler in the PTC and quite the unpredictable factor in the ring. You've claimed to be hard-core. You've claimed to be in matches that would break the limits of any normal man. Granted in the Independent circuit you have been in quite the hard-core match, but now that you are in the UPWA, things are different. This is no longer the independent circuit, you are now in a well maintained and organized federation." [Nicky Jam looks at him shaking his head.] Nicky Jam: "Moron. You say you've seen matches before, right." [Mr.Green nods his head.] Mr.Green: "So have my bosses." Nicky Jam: "Yeah right. So if you saw some of my matches, what in God's name makes you think I'm claiming all of this bullsh*t for? I'm dead on when I say I'm hardcore as the next guy. The matches I've been in have all come close of ending my career. And you know what? If I could go another route I would turn around and go down that same damn street once again. If this match goes DQ, that let me go balls out from that point forward. Anything goes and that's exactly what is going to happen. Anything and everything, screw the normal weapons of a chair or a table. This is will pull out all the stops and make sure that the fans of the PTC have one hell of a time...what the hell am I talking about? Hell screw the fans I get hardcore for me. Besides, Mr.Green, this is my body and I do what I damn well please." [Nicky Jam looks out of the tinted windows to his left as the limousine comes to a slow stop in front of the Ritz-Carlton in downtown Indianapolis. A bellhop comes to side of the limousine opening the door. Nicky Jam grabs his bags, then hands the young man his bags as he looks back at Mr.Green.] Nicky Jam: "Check this out Mr.Green. This week will mark the beginning of something great. This will mark the day that The Rodriguez Brothers step into the UPWA full circle and destroy The Metallo Zords dead into the ring. Wanna see hardcore? Watch how hardcore I get on The Metallo Zords and the rest of the PTC when I spill their blood and mine in the middle of that damn canvas. By the way, thanks for the room and the ride. Tell your bosses that I said Hi." [Nicky Jam steps out of the limousine closing the door with a loud slam. Mr.Green looks out of the tinted window as Nicky Jam looks around with a large smile on his face. He walks into the glass revolving door of the five-star hotel as the scene fades to black.]
