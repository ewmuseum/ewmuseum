[The Shadow Riders] The Getaway pt. 1 (TTT Finals RP 1)

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Shadow Riders Affiliated Pimp Joined: 08 Apr 2003 Posts: 4 Location: Over here now. Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 7:03 pm Post subject: [The Shadow Riders] The Getaway pt. 1 (TTT Finals RP 1) (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... "The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.". The scene opens to a shot of the interior of long sleek black Cadillac. The change of car suggests that the Shadow Riders and Johnny D have decided that it was best to drop the stolen police car from the bank heist in favor of a less conspicuous vehicle for their getaway. As the three men continue to drive along doing their best to remain undetected, a conversation breaks out between Russo who is sitting in the passenger side of the car still holding his gun from the robbery and Kage who is occupied behind the wheel.) Russo: "I can't believe we actually pulled that off . . . what a rush! We finally have something go our way. We're home free!" (Kage responds without taking his attention off the road ahead to avoid any minor occurrences that could end up causing a major problem at this juncture in time.) Kage: "Don't get ahead of yourself man. We're not out of the woods yet." Russo: "What the hell are you talking about!? We got away from the bank . . . we got a new car . . . how the hell are going to get caught?" Kage: "I'm just saying we can't stop taking things seriously yet. After all, we still have to make it over the border and into Mexico which isn't gonna be a walk in the park. All I can tell you is I'm through with all this adrenaline rush against the law crap after we get done with this one." (With a look of disbelief on his face, Russo responds waving his gun in the air haphazardly.) Russo: "You're through with this!? Why? We got away with it." (In yet another attempt to get the point through to Russo that they still have a long way to go, Kage increases the tone in his voice stressing his words.) Kage: "Not yet we didn't! You can't be waving your gun around like that either! Why can't you understand that!?" Russo: "Because we are home free . . . we're doing the speed limit. The guy we took this car from sure as hell isn't gonna be reporting it missing. Not to mention that the cops have no clue who in the hell we are. They probably still think Sal and Seymour were the only two people involved. So just lighten up." (Annoyed that Russo is still not getting the message, Kage responds.) Kage: "I told you not to do that. If you keep assuming things like that, you're gonna end up making a mistake and getting us all caught." Russo: "Did you take your meds today man because you're freaking the fuck out. You're getting all paranoid and shit for no reason whatsoever." Kage: "No I'm not . . . I'm just trying to tell you to keep cool until we get over the border. Can you just do that one little favor for me?" Russo: "Fine . . . but do you promise to just chill out a little once we get down to Mexico?" (Relieved that Russo has finally given in, Kage responds as he relaxes a little bit.) Kage: "Sure I will. Hell, I'll even spring for a couple bottles of Tequila and a few senoritas for us from my cut of the money." Russo: "Still, I'll bet you nothing will even come close to going remotely wrong before we hit the border." Kage: "I hope you're right. I hope nothing does go wrong because in case you forgot, once the heat dies down we still gotta come back into the states for our tag match against Homo/Phobic at New Era." (Russo face fills with a look as if something long forgotten has just re-entered his mind.) Russo: "Oh yeah . . . I almost totally forgot about that." (Russo now turns around to the back seat casually resting his hand with the gun in it on the back of his seat in order to speak with Johnny D. who is staring off into space with a glazed look in his eyes.) Russo: "Hey Johnny, what do you make of those two ass jockeys?" (The sound of Russo talking snaps Johnny back into reality.) Johnny: "Huh . . . what?" Russo: "Homo/Phobic, what do you make of those two jackasses?" (Showing his indifference to the world of wrestling, Johnny responds.) Johnny: "What about them? I could care less . . . all I'm thinking about is what I'm gonna do with my share of the money." Russo: "Come on Johnny . . . I really wanna know what you think of them. You've seen them on TV, do you think we can take the . . ." (BANG! Russo's gun fires off hitting Johnny dead in the throat. As Johnny begins to slump down onto the seat, he tries to speak but only gurgles escape his mouth. Slowly dying on the back seat, Johnny begins to go into convulsions as his gurgling becomes louder. Hearing the sound of the gunshot sends Kage into a panic. Still trying to keep his attention on the road in front of him, he yells out to Russo.) Kage: "What the hell was that!? Didn't I tell you to keep cool!? What the hell are you doing firing a gun off in the car!? (Still in a state of semi shock, Russo responds.) Russo: "I just shot Johnny . . . he's dying. It was an accident, I didn't mean it." (Hearing this sends Kage into an even deeper panic.) Kage: "You didn't mean it!? How the fuck did you shoot Johnny and not mean it!?" Russo: "Man I'm sorry . . . I told you it was an accident. Why would I purposely shoot Johnny when we're trying to make a clean getaway?" Kage: "I can't believe this! Only you could screw something up this bad . . . I told you not to wave that gun all over the place like that! You know what, I bet you did it on purpose. You probably want his share of the money! Don't you?!" Russo: "Just calm down . . . you must have hit a pot hole or something. It's not my fault you bounced the car." (Becoming more pissed off at Russo's utter refusal to take responsibility, Kage responds.) Kage: "There wasn't any pot hole in the road! You're just a careless jackass . . . why did you have to pull this crap now!? Out of all the possible times, why now!?" Russo: "I said it was an accident. Seriously, I didn't mean to shoot him. The gun just fired. I don't know how, it just did so let's just drop it. What we really should be thinking about is finishing him off . . . it's not like we can just take him to the hospital." (In a state of disbelief, Kage responds.) Kage: "You already shot him once and now you wanna do it again!?" Russo: "Well he's dying anyway, I'm just gonna speed up the process so he doesn't have to go all slow and painful. It's the decent thing to do." Kage: "No, the decent thing to do would have been not to shoot him in the first place!" (As the debate continues between the two Riders, Johnny lies on the back seat listening to their argument, slowly dying as a pool of blood forms around him.) Kage: "I swear, something like this could only happen to us. Why can't we catch a break? Damnit! I guess you're gonna have to do what you have to do." (Russo turns around to the back seat and puts the barrel of his gun against the side of Johnny's head. Johnny's eyes grow wide at the site of the gun coming towards him. He attempts to make a plea for his life but his voice is once again garbled by the blood filling his lungs and throat from the first bullet hole. Russo cocks back the hammer of the gun and prepares to shoot. Suddenly, Kage speaks up and begins speaking to Johnny while still keeping his eyes on the road.) Kage: "Johnny man, I'm sorry we gotta do this. We cant risk taking you to a hospital though judging from your past with Joey. I just want you to know though, if there was anyway I could change this I would. Sorry." (Russo now looks at Kage and responds to his comment in a sarcastic manner.) Russo: "Ok, are you at peace with yourself now? Do you think we can get on with this?" Kage: "Well excuse me for not being a heartless bastard." Russo: "Whatever, just honk the horn on three ok? Ready, one . . . two . . . three!" (Kage slams his hand down hard on the horn as Russo pulls the trigger of the gun so as to mask the noise of the shot. However, a new unexpected problem arises with the echoing blast. The bullet to Johnny's head causes it burst open spraying the entire car including both Russo and Kage with a thick coating of blood, brain, and skull fragments. Realizing that he is now covered in blood, Kage exclaims.) Kage: "Holy Shit!" (Russo also mumbles under his breath.) Russo: "Fuck . . ." (Kage takes his attention of the road for a quick moment to glace around the car. He then turns his focus back to the road as he speaks.) Kage: "Look at this car, this is definitely gonna draw some unwanted attention to us! We're on a freaking major highway in the middle of the god damned day! Russo: "I know, I didn't take splatter into account. I thought he would just go clean but no, he has to try and screw us over even in death. What an asshole!" Kage: "Didn't take splatter into account!? Well you better start taking things into account, this is the second thing you pulled today that can end up blowing our getaway! We gotta get this car off the road, cops tend to investigate cars that have windows covered in blood and brain!" Russo: "Can't we just ditch this car on the side of the road ad pick up a new one from somebody driving by?" (In a tone that indicates that Kage thinks Russo's idea is one of the stupidest things that he has ever heard, he responds.) Kage: "No, we cant just drop this car on the roadside. It's kind of covered in blood and our prints, ass. Besides, who the hell is gonna stop for two guys covered in blood hitchhiking on the side of the highway?" Russo: "Well don't look at me, I'm all out of ideas on what to do." Kage: "Figures . . ." (At this point, Kage takes one of his hands off the wheel and reaches into his pocket. He pulls out a small cell phone and flips it open as he begins dialing a number. Kage then places the phone to his ear. Seeing this Russo begins to question him.) Russo: "Who you calling?" Kage: "Unfortunately, we have a serious problem and it needs to be dealt with immediately and the only person I can think that is in a position to help us out is Mike." (With a look of despair on his face, Russo sighs as he speaks.) Russo: "You don't mean Roberts do you?" Kage: "The one and only, he just got a house over in Escondido." Russo: "Where in the blue hell is Escondido?" Kage: "It's on the other side of the valley by San Marcos. I'll tell you though if Mike's screening his calls and doesn't answer us, I don't know what we're gonna do. He's the only friend we have in this part of California. So you just better hope he answe . . ." (Kage quickly takes his other hand off the wheel and raises up his finger. He then quickly puts his hand back onto the wheel as he begins speaking into the phone.) Kage: "Mike, it's Jason . . . how you doing buddy? (Kage pauses as the man on the other end of the phone responds.) Kage: "Really, that's good to hear. Listen, I was just calling to see if you would mind if Russo and myself stop by to visit you for a couple hours. See, we kind of have a little problem and we really need to get off the road and maybe park our car in your garage. It's really important that we find a place to lay low for awhile. It would be really cool if you could help us out a little just like old times." (Kage pauses again as the voice on the other end of the line answers. He then starts speaking again.) Kage: "Mike you know I can't really talk about our problems on a cell phone, who knows who may be listening to the conversation. The only thing I can tell you is that if we don't find a place to hide out soon, the Shadow Riders are gonna be taking an extended vacation from wrestling as we tour the prison circuit. So, do you think you can help us out?" (Kage pauses once again as he listens to Mike's response. A smile now crosses Kage's face as he begins to speak once again.) Kage: "You've always been a great friend Mike, we really appreciate you doing this for us. You're really saving our asse . . ." (Kage is cut off by the voice on the other end of the phone. Once Mike stops talking, Kage begins.) Kage: "Don't worry Mike, we'll be long gone by then. We just gotta clean a few things up." (Mike cuts in once again for a brief moment. Kage now responds.) Kage: "Look Mike I fully understand where you're coming from, I promise we'll be gone before the time even comes close. Your new arrangement is safe with us man, we would never put you in any kind of jeopardy. You're like family to us . . . they'll never know we were even there in the first place." (Pause once again.) Kage: "Ok, we'll be there in ten minutes. Talk to you then." (Kage flips the phone shut and slides it back into his pocket. Russo quickly perks up and begins to question him.) Russo: "What's up, is everything cool?" Kage: "Yeah, we're cool. Listen though, we gotta do whatever we're doing quick. His doctors are gonna be paying him a little visit in two hours to see how he's adjusting to his new environment and the last thing he need is to be caught with us and a headless body. So, we gotta move fast and be out of there by then." (With that the picture fades out as the screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... "The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.". The screen now fades to black.) _________________ "You are nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders." - "Superstar" Eric Russo
