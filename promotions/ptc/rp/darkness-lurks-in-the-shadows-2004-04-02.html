Darkness lurks in the shadows

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 An old building lays peacefully between two twin gigantic structures. Ominously it sits in the darkness between these two steel giants, a shadow that many people would ignore for this is not a building that many would pay attention to. The small building, silent where it rested, is a bit ragged compared to these giants that surround it. Cracks in the structures stairs, matching white pillar posts and frame are deep, ugly. A new coat of paint is needed desperately for the old white wash has long since faded to a dingy gray that could never hope to be washed off. A statue of a gargoyle, it’s stone tongue hanging out as it glares down evilly upon the world from it’s perch on the roof seems out of place. As out of place as the thousands of cobwebs, yellow grass and garbage strewn about. There is a closed sign hanging in one section of the two glass doors that make up the front entrance. A smaller white sign above the closed one proclaims that this is a “Public library” but that is far from truth. This is not a library that many people would dare to walk into. In fact? Not many people do dare to walk into this library except for those that believe in what these books contain. Neat rows are filled from bottom to top with books of different sizes, shapes and topic. Some are books filled with lives. The lives of those people who have come before and are either no longer here or where the soul we seek has herself been once before. Tables in neat rows are empty, the chairs pushed in because the hour is late and even this library must close. Save one chair of course that is far back in the dark recess of the library. A solitary light comes from the laptop that is set up in front of a black-cloaked figure. The only sound in the library is the soft tapping of the keys as they are pressed in a rhythm that says the figure is in a hurry to finish whatever it is that they are doing. Piles of books are everywhere upon the table and the floor, some open, most closed. Sheets of paper with short notes written in a language probably long forgotten by many people are everywhere as well. With a careless gloved hand even more tumble from the table to land on the floor, unnoticed. One look at this figure says that it could only be one person. There were few that would dare to tread where they should not and there were few who had little time to finish preparing for what could quite honestly become a dance in the darkness for them. The gloved fingers left the keyboard and disappeared beneath the table as the head was lifted from the glowing computer screen to stare blankly towards the moon that shone in through one window. There was much upon the mind for her this evening. Much that she wanted to discuss, to sort, and there was much that she had yet to answer for her own self. A vague, harsh laugh fell from unseen lips as the hands raised from their hidden position to push the hood of the cloak back from their face in mild agitation. Her dark hair was held back by a single hair band that was not of course seen in the thick mass of curls. The lips, stained crimson red were thin with annoyance for much had annoyed her this evening. Black lashes lowered to brush pale cheeks as she looked to a corner where a sleeping figure that had been ignored till this point could be made out. ‘Ah but the innocence of youth,’ were her thoughts. This was her only companion now. A seventeen-year-old boy. The only one that she allowed into her darkest thoughts. He knew what her dark agenda was for the most part. What her purpose in existing was for now. No, it was no longer for the master that had once dominated her. It was no longer for the fans that had once cheered for that other half of her. ‘Simple little fool. Did she really believe that she would be able to overpower me? Hrmph, the Fallen Angel will not be supplanted so easily. Never so easily.’ Ahh but the Angel could be supplanted. Even she knew such a fact. As powerful as she was, the Angel was not all powerful. She was not a God. The thought gave her pause and made her debate over things that she rarely now allowed herself too. Arcane Asylum. A home for the mentally disturbed and imbalanced. Just a thought of that place drew the slanted dark brows together and that crimson mouth twisted into an ugly sneer. She would never return to that place. Jacob had made certain of that. Yet the possibility was always there. Always Rage was still lurking. Trying to find her so that he could again lock her away for what he had deemed was her “own good.” The thought nearly made Angel laugh. Doing what was her own good had never been a motif of the Fallen Angel and nor of Sheri, the woman who many considered to be the Fallen Angel. That was something that ill wise miscreants who did not pay attention thought anyway. Rage seemingly understood that she was not Sheri and thus had decided that the situation had to be resolved. He had an ability to figure out her next move and that there was a danger for the Fallen Angel. She could end up once more in a place like Arcane if she was not careful. ‘How best to describe that rat infested hole called Arcane Asylum,‘ the Angel mused silently to herself. Arcane was more fitting a name for the “Batman” like Asylum, Angel decided with a cold snort. The doctors attempts at “psychoanalysis” had proved futile for the Angel would say nothing to them. They asked questions she would not give them answers to. She would not beg for her freedom. She would not lower herself to such a point. She asked them for nothing in fact. That had angered the doctors. Greatly she thought with a smirk. Repeatedly they tried to get her to talk. Electric shock therapy had been applied a number of times but had gained no result but for the Angel to laugh. A psychotic masochist? ‘Perhaps,‘ the Angel agreed silently. Pain was something that the Angel was not afraid of. The doctors had learned that and had not liked it. They knew they could not break the indomitable will that made the Angel who she was still to this day even. Nothing that they had done had gotten her to talk. Ahh but the Angel admitted silently that being locked in a padded cell had almost broken her resolve. For someone like the Angel, freedom was a key. She had to be free and never closed inside of a small space for very long. It was one of many idiosyncrasies that was apart of the Angel. Enough of the recollections she thought a few minutes later. They did nothing but relive horrors that had made her stronger, not weaker. Turning back to the computer that sat in front of her still on a screen she had been staring at for the last couple of hours, the Angel debated over what words she wanted to use to express her true feelings. Idea’s flew into her mind and putting her fingers back on the keys, Angel began to type. Journal entry 345: From the diary of the Fallen Angel: April 1, 2004 ‘So they are wondering who it is that the Angel is? It would seem some are upset because the story of the Angel was told by someone other then she, herself. Foolish mortals. You looked no further then the mere words you heard spoken. How best would someone learn who the Angel is but from those who know her best? She understood that many whispers were made that Jacob’s story was not her own. Ahh foolish one’s, Jacob’s story shadows the Angels. What is it that we both know about best? Addiction. Pain. Anger. If you had been less simplistic then you fools would have seen that. Ahh but you mortals, you look only for the obvious of that which is beneath a situation and is to complicated for you to handle, to see, to find. That is what makes the Angel most happy. Making you mortals look more the fools that you are. Where there was one “Fool” in Ian Fetzer competing in this Cruiserweight Tournament of the PTC, there are now plenty. Ian must not feel quite so alone. Poor Fool. Can’t remember that he is a man of once greatness for a fed that has slowly died since his untimely adventure into the darkness that bore the Angel. Now he has become nothing short of a joke. A clown that is laughed at for the idiot that he is. It would almost be a moving moment if the Angel of course cared about any of you. The Angel of course cares for no one but for her young protégé. She doesn’t care about your simple little lives or even simpler little problems. Psychologists and Psychiatrists are paid the money they are per hour to deal with mortal lives and problems. Take all those boring and senseless problems to them and do not lay them at the feet of the Angel for she cares not to hear your pathetic little sob story. The only thing she cares about is that you stay out of her way. Do not interfere in the things that fate has decreed must happen before the Angel become the living force that she desires to be and you will live to see the next tomorrow dawn. It is of course highly suggested that you obey such a command. The repercussions of failing to do so are intense, that is guaranteed. They wonder if the Angel is able to bring the same destruction as Alex Caine can. The answer is simple. Wait and see what it is that you face. Syko? Well, he could prove to be interesting to the Angel….. If she was here to of course look for things of interest. Things are quite simple with the Angel. This PTC tournament was always about one thing… making a point. Now will they understand? This is not the Angel that was seen before in either XUW or even briefly in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. No, that Angel was a puppet. A marionette that was danced in front of the masses at the amusement of a man that taught the Angel the greatest lesson ever needed to be learned. What lesson is that you ask dear one’s? Why the lesson of war. It’s art form. It’s simple planning. It’s simple preparation. In short? The techniques of being a capable leader. Do not doubt that the Angel is not now a capable leader for the lesson learned physically will not be repeated ever again. The Angel is not the foolish doll anymore. Nor will she be again. Pity? Sorrow? They are useless wastes of emotional time that the Angel both loathes and finds disgusting. Respect? Mundane concept. Honor? The Angel needs not such a pathetic emotion. What does honor get? It gets a knife to the back. Honor lays a body out cold inside of a ring and causes them to lose sight of the goals that they have planned. The Angel will not lose sight this time of that which she desires to do the most. It is as simple as that. They wonder who this enigma, this mysterious is. There is not an answer that the Angel will offer. They wonder what motives, what goals, what secretive and devious plans that the Angel has concocted in the darkness. Do they honestly think that they could fathom the plans of the Angel? Could they understand that the Angel is not beyond doing anything and everything that is needed to get her point accomplished? In the Angels own words, and in the Angels own way, she will show you, tell you. All in time will the things desired to be known be revealed. Not before. Not after. The Angel does nothing for anybody but herself. The Angel is not like other women. She plays no sorts of games to get that which she wants accomplished. The former days where her lighter half would bare her chest to the world has come to an end for the Angel will reveal nothing but that which she desires to. Everything is easy to understand. At least the Angel believes so. Those who are to simpleminded turn away for the words that the Angel is typing right now upon this computer screen will flutter your hearts and cause you to have a case of the vapors. The Angel is many things. Poetic is the first amidst them. She will speak in rhyme if it suits her to do so or she will not. It is that easy. She is also quite intelligent and will display when it suits her that intelligence that makes her the most deadly. Do not forget that. She is also ruthless. She will stop at nothing to get a job done. If that requires slitting someone’s throat, then blood will be spilled and the Angel will laugh after she has done so. Do not count the Angel out for even though she is small, she is still deadly. Much like a scorpion, as much as the symbolism irritates the Angel, that is poisonous is still deadly, so is the Angel still a force to reckon with. You continue to read? How amusing. You aren’t as easily terrified as the Angel had before thought. Let the Angel sum into simple poetry just exactly what she is thinking, what she is feeling, and most especially, what terrible evil she is planning to unleash upon the whole of the Cruiserweight Tournament that begins soon. Syko and Alex Caine should mark the words of the Angel well for they are the first of many that shall feel the wrath of the Angel and fall at her hand.‘ The Angel sits back and reads the words she had just placed upon the screen in front of her. A smirk, pure benevolence curves those lips for they are the true sentiments of the Angel unleashed. She was not the same Angel that she had been before her trip to Arcane. She was now twice as angry as she had been before that. She saw that as no problem really. For this anger would be expelled upon the bodies of those that dared to get into the ring with her. Pulling up a document that had been resting at the bottom of the screen which contained the rhyme that the Angel had written specially to give a cold warning to those that she would face first brought a sadistic pleasure into those dark eyes. Softly, so as to not disturb the sleeping figure of Jacob she repeated those words allowed before she would add them to the bottom of the journal entry she had just typed. “Below my calm surface, you don’t know what is there, Some may call me mysterious, but are you truly aware? They think they know, what lurks inside my mind? Ah but they are foolish, they should run and hide. Is this my shield from the world, because hope was lost? To understand the enigma that is me, your death is the cost. I feel no pain, I’m not a living soul, Hatred is a friend, rage hot as a coal. I‘m not an actor, on your pathetic stage, You will never again lock me, inside of a cage. I refuse to rid myself, of the evil infesting my soul, With a knife I’ll pour, your blood into a bowl. Smiles are elusive, true amusement rare, On the inside I'm laughing, at your hopeless stare. You want to understand, this being that is me? I warn you once more, you should all turn to flee. You’ve invited me to a party, of which I’ll become the host, Somewhere in the middle, you will all become a ghost. Cut me with a knife, my blood will run free, Ah but the wounds always heal because, I’m not what you see. Below the surface, is where the answer lies, In fact if you look closely, the answer is in my eyes. So come play with me, if that’s what you dare. Understand I know no pity, I have no care. I’ll see you all soon, of that I am sure, For the pain you‘ll be in, there is no cure. This is no game, for the Angel doesn’t play, You will be beaten, if you get in my way. And remember in the end, when you lie near death, That it better be my name uttered, on your final breath.” Finishing the chilly and ominous words on her own final indrawn breath, the Angel sits back in her chair, thoughtful and almost peaceful in the same moment. Her softly uttered words, meant to not awaken the one sleeping so sound had indeed awakened him. Jacob had felt a chill as those hate filled words had fallen from the Angels lips. He knew what she was feeling as well. She meant each thing she said. The Angel was many things but ill prepared was hardly one of them. Her mistake against her Master had taught her to be even more thorough. Her mood was growing foul and that meant that she was angry. Deeply angry. Sitting up from the slew of chairs he had made into a temporary bed while the Angel worked, Jacob ran a hand through his mussed hair. Since the night she had requested his presence at the graveyard they had traveled. Long days of walking with little rest to get to a plac that Jacob had not known existed. He had never known that Sheri had used to come here, that this was a place she had done much of her recollections and studying of Pagan belief in. Here, at this library he was not sure he liked being in was where the Angel had brought him. ‘To prepare,’ she had said but he had seen little but reading done as the preparation. He needed to remind the Angel that she had not seen physical action for a few months but doubted that his speaking would do little good. The Angel would do as she had said she would, no disturbances at all keeping her from that exact path. Pushing himself to his feet with a sigh, Jacob made his way towards the woman that sat there so still looking at the computer screen. He was still a couple steps from her when the Angel said softly, “You should rest more my young friend. The journey has been arduous upon you and taxing to your strength.” Jacob was momentarily stunned until he remembered that the Angel did not need to look to know he was approaching. She would know anytime from the sound of his footsteps that he was nearing her. “Angel, it is past three o’clock in the morning. You should rest. Your first round tournament match will be taxing upon your strength.” The dark head did not turn. Those dark eyes did not fall upon him, cutting him to the quick with the heat within her glance. It was as if the Angel had fallen into another dimension mentally. In fact, he noticed, she seemed to be staring sightlessly at the computer screen in front of her. “Do they understand that the Angel plays no game with them Jacob? Or are their ego’s so much so that they fail to see that danger coming for them in the shape of a five-foot five and a half inch female is real?” Came the soft question a few minutes later. Jacob was rocked by the dark reflection that laced her tone. Was this what was angering the Angel he wondered? This seeming fact that it was felt she played a game that could easily be beaten? “I am not sure that I quite understand what it is that you are saying Angel. Who likens what you do to a game?” The Angel rose then, like a cat from it’s bed after a full night’s rest. Her lithe body stretched, palms upwards to the ceiling. She was more prepared for this then he had thought. Mental preparation was as important as physical. That was what she always told him. She turned to face him a few seconds later, curiosity tilting her head slightly to the side. The tense anger that had been at her lips had faded back to the tranquility she had seemingly enjoyed for the past few days. It was an odd mood now he realized silently and quickly decided that he would play along with it till it had passed. “They do not believe that the Angel is a force. That she could never become anything more then the single-minded puppet. They do not know that the World is changing. It is preparing for some dark terror that is about to fall upon it. The waves are stormy. Threatening to consume those that get in their way. The wind now blows speaking to those that will listen to it’s words. The Earth shifts in an almost undetectable amount of movement as if it wishes to run away and hide. A thick smell, much like sulfur permeates everything. There is a sense of foreboding. It is as if the world has suddenly awakened to the darkness surrounding it and has become lost inside of the cataclysm that it has become. Much that once was my young protégé is lost. Innocence has no place in this new world for it will be destroyed. Those that are still naïve will become knowledgeable once that wishes to rise does so once more. We have waited long enough to return to the mortal world after all. Yet it draws us closer into it‘s cold grasp with every morning that dawns and every evening the sun sets. Desire makes it hungry. Anger makes it stronger.” Jacob was perplexed at the deep wisdom that was inlaid into every breath that the Angel had used to speak the words she had. That thoughtful expression, that near curious tilt had faded to allow the one he was most used to into reappearing. The philosophical thinker. This was the Angel at what he knew best. The one that imparted knowledge like running water. He looked at her, pondering the words she had spoken as he knew she expected him to do. Before he could answer though the Angel moved off towards him. Like a wisp of something delicate the Angel walked in his direction, her booted feet never once appearing beneath the hem of that cloak she wore. Jacob knew that it was a figment of his imagination. The cloak merely hid her feet from view and there was nothing delicate about her. Nothing at all. “What it is that you speak of Angel?” He finally asked when he saw that the Angel was not likely to say anything further. In fact, the Angel swept past him on her way to where only she knew. It was a few seconds in fact before the Angel would speak and only then it would be from the middle of the winding staircase she gracefully ascended. “It is the same thing as I have said numerous times before to the one we used to try and make believe such words Jacob. You know that answer well.” Jacob turned to watch as the Angel slowly made her way down one of the many dark aisles in the library. She looked for another book he thought in silent dejection. They were going to be here all night apparently. Until she found whatever it was that she was looking for seemingly. A diary she had told him was what she sought. A diary she had said would explain much about the things she was still learning. Jacob was ready to head towards a hotel so he could get a full nights sleep and a shower personally. “It is what fuels us to be the competitors we are but it also serves as a strength we can never hope to understand Jacob. The darkness is not a simple thing that people can turn on and off like a light. It is what grows in our hearts, festers in our minds. It is what leaves us weak and yet strong at the same time. The darkness is a tangible mistress, much like the raging oceans that lay a distance from us. The darkness is cold, hateful itself of those that trespass amidst it. Yet it is also forgiving. Understanding. Everything that the Angel is herself but yet not always able to be. The darkness is what will see the Angel through these questions and troubles in her mind. The darkness is what will help her defeat the foe’s, Alex Caine and Syko. Soon she will rise Jacob… and soon, every living soul shall be burned alive by the living flame that she will become. May their pathetic God have mercy upon their worthless souls.” Jacob was startled by the sheer passion that laced the words that had fallen from his unseen now Mistress. He had never heard such a declaration fall from the Angel’s lips, had never heard any emotions in her voice save hate and anger. Perhaps one day he would truly be able to understand that who the Angel is and even though he thought that he did now, he realized that he sadly did not. He ever doubted if he would truly understand the woman that the Angel was. At least, he would never understand her as he had done with Sheri. There was a thought that he decided he would not pursue thinking about. Her wrath would be terrible if she knew he had been thinking such a thing. Seeing the Angel move from the shadows with a thick bound book in her hands got him to sigh. It was definitely going to be a long night. He had better just go back to sleep because it didn’t look like the Angel was going to do anything of importance that involved him. With a sigh he headed back to the shadowy cove he had been resting in before. It was going to be a long night indeed.
