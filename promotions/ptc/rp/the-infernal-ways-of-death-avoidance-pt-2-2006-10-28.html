The Infernal Ways of Death Avoidance (pt 2)

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-28 The Eegors were standing around outside the Mirage hotel in Las Vegas, trying to avoid the temptation to go inside any of the nearby casinos and maybe partake in a little gambling to pass the time. One checked his watch and muttered something to the others about Doctor Curiosity being 'so fucking late'. The temperature outside was not cold, but the anticipation of the crime they would be committing was making several of their number shiver through nervousness. "I thought he said this place would be closed when we got here?" said one of the Eegors, a tall guy with muscles that far outstripped those found on the doctor himself. "Yeah, how are we supposed to get in and do whatever it is we're supposed to be doing without getting seen? They're bound to have loads of security, and video cameras and… and frikkin' tigers!" replied a second Eegor. "I'd leave right now, but this seems the quickest way to get into the business… but if he tries to pull some crazy shit and get us killed then he can just go walk." The Eegors all nodded, until they were interrupted by a voice, the German accent cutting through the clear air. "Vhy vould you ever tzink I would put you in mortal danger, my Eegors?" said Doctor Curiosity, sauntering into the group with a smile on his face and a large bag slung over his shoulder. The hopefuls all stopped talking and started careful examination of their shoes. "Jah, so now zat I am here, ve can begin ze task ahead of us. Do you all know vhat ze task is?" Silence from the Eegors. "Very well. Ze task is to study a group of white tigers. Ve vill be interacting vith them, as you know, and I vill eventually subdue one and all vill be glorious." One Eegor raised his hand. "Jah, vhat is it, Baby?" "Baby?" "Jah! I have named you after ze Spice Girls, as it is easier to remember zan your actual names." "Ummm… well, that's weird… but, I was wondering why you needed to learn about tigers. Shouldn't you be more worried about your match with Vangelus Olsig?" Doctor Curiosity paused. There was no doubt that he could not tell the Eegors his true motives for wanting to learn about tigers and how best to cope with one attacking him, but perhaps there would be a way for him to turn it around. "Of course I am concentrating on zat match. Vhat you fail to understand, my trainee assistant, is zat Olsig is a vicious competitor. He could come at me from any angle, and be as relentless in attack and deadly as a tiger. So vhat better vay of training for my match vith him than facing off against a tiger?" "Well…" replied the Eegor, thinking of many different ways. "Exactly!" yelled the doc, not allowing the youngster to get another word in. "So, let us go in and cause some havoc!" Another Eegor raised his hand, eliciting a sigh from Curiosity. "Vhat now?" "Well, how are we to get to the tigers? This place is still open. I think security will have a few things to say about it." "Aha. I have thought of this, of course, as I am Doctor Curiosity!" The doctor dropped the large canvas bad from his shoulder and opened the zip. From within he pulled a couple of large yellow signs and three rolls of yellow and black tape. "If ze zoo vill not shut for us… ve vill shut ze zoo." *** The signs were put up in double quick time at an appropriate time when nobody was looking. The security camera had been taken care of via a surprisingly effective Curious Camera Breaker device produced from the bag of the doctor. Three of the Eegors stood outside, warding off potential visitors to the tiger habitat and offering them free entry should they return tomorrow. A fake stamp on a piece of paper was sufficient to placate most of the evening visitors to the attraction, though none seemed too keen on coming back. The local security guards responded to the fake police identification, and a mixture of veiled threats and bribes of a sexual variety (not from the Eegors, but from some friendly hookers that Doctor Curiosity just 'happened' to know). Posh Eegor looked to Scary Eegor and shook his head. "Are you really sure we should have gone with the sign the doctor suggested?" "Well, it's working," was the response. "Yeah, but…" Both men surveyed their handiwork. Tiger Habitat Closed Until Tomorrow Due To Outbreak Of Rabies "Hmmmm," mused the third Eegor (Ginger Eegor, for those keeping score). "I think their ticket sales might be down tomorrow too…" *** Inside the attraction, Doctor Curiosity and the two remaining Eegors were walking down towards where the tigers were kept. The tiger environment at the Mirage is quite exceptional, or so the brochures had told the doctor. It looked as good as he had been promised though, with simulated mountain terrain and pools for the tigers to swim in, should they so desire. The walkway for the customers was separated from the tigers' environment via a sloped window made of some form of reinforced glass. Through the window the three men could see one white tiger prowling around, its muscles moving majestically under the pristine white fur. The sinuous grace of the motion left all of them speechless for a moment, before Curiosity broke the silence. "Vell, ve'd better get in zere and kick its ass, jah?" "Ummm… are you sure? That thing looks pretty formidable," said the mild mannered Baby Eegor. "Jah, it does," replied Curiosity, swallowing hard as he realized how much was at stake. "But ve vill do it anyvays." "Why?" said Sporty Eegor, butting in. "There are other ways to train, and this is just dumb. It's dangerous and I didn't sign up for it. Hit me over the head with chairs all you like, and slam me through a table on to a bed of thumbtacks, but I'm not going to try and wrestle a white fucking tiger!" Curiosity nodded his head, bravado in front of his pupils for once being overcome by a deeper understanding of the situation. For all of his confidence about his inability to die until the upcoming match, seeing such a lithe animal, capable of tearing into even a trained handler and leaving them for dead, he was worried. Not just for him but for his trainees, neither of whom had seen anything that the doctor would have termed as 'action'. Still, as long as he could protect them for the time being, the experience could very well save his life… and his soul. "I vould duck down if I vere you," muttered Curiosity. The Eegors looked at him with a bemused expression, until the doctor withdrew something that looked like a cross between a slab of Jello and a hand grenade from his bag, and hefted it towards the reinforced partition. The grenade, for that is what it was, struck the glass towards the top and stuck there lazily, the pink gelatinous substance coating it giving it strong adhesive qualities. The resultant detonation proved that it also possessed fairly good explosive qualities. The inch thick reinforced grass cracked like an eggshell, and frosted around a section roughly three feet wide in diameter. Curiosity smiled at the success of yet another one of his devices, and made a mental note that if he ever survived the upcoming week or so he would happily try to sell on his patent to the armed forces. "Men, it is time to do vhat ve came here to do." "No way… fuck this, you're a lunatic," said Sporty Eegor, shaking his head. "Vhat?" Sporty continued to shake his head, and then just turned on his heel and left, walking away with his hands running through his hair and a stream of softly spoken swear words passing through his lips. Though he would never have admitted it, Doctor Curiosity was shaken. He was not used to having people bail on him; his previous full time Eegor had been his brother, who had gone so far as to kill someone who was threatening him, and had taken the subsequent life term in prison stoically. For one of his followers to quit at the first sing of danger was alien to him, and going in, almost literally, to the lions' den without protection felt wrong. Very wrong. "Why… why isn't there an alarm?" asked Baby Eegor. "I disabled it, Baby." The men looked at one another. "That sounds so wrong. Vhat is your name?" "Errol." "People are still called Errol?" "Yes… but please don't start. I get enough of that from the guys at school." Curiosity scratched his head. "How old are you, Errol?" "Ummm… twenty one!" "How about in reality?" "Sixteen," said Errol, again apparently studying the floor. Anything but looking at the face of his interrogator. "Vell, you vere meant to be eighteen to enter ze contest to become my Eegor." "I know." "But I see zat you are here, and your elder friend has left us, so maybe you deserve to stay." Errol nodded, but he was clearly scared out of his wits. "Errol, to answer your qvestion, ze alarms are not vorking because I earlier disabled zem vith a Curious Device of mine. It is vhy I vas late, as despite appearing to be somevhat haphazard in my actions, everything is moving to a plan zat I have concocted. Do you understand?" "Yes, but…" "Do you have faith in me, Errol?" "Yes." "Jah!" "Jah!" Curiosity smiled, and his heart rate slowed by a fraction, so it felt like there was no longer an insane percussionist pounding a bass drum in his chest. At times like this it was important to have somebody to help you. The doctor walked up the glass panel and jumped over the perfunctory barrier to actually stand on the edge of the slanted window. He removed a small metal hammer from his pocket and swung it with great force at the frosted area. The glass shook and deformed, revealing an incredibly fine wire that ran through it at intervals, holding it together in case of such an explosion or other form of attack. A few more hits from the hammer, though, and the glass was broken, with a large enough hole to allow entry to the tiger enclosure. Curiosity's heart rate was right back up there. "Grab the bag. Let's go in." *** Merrick was sitting in the Security Control Room of the Mirage hotel. He was very happy with proceedings as they had turned out thus far, with Curiosity being allowed entry to go and try to wrestle some tigers. Whilst he was by now an accomplished wrestler, having trained with some of the greats in the business, he was ill-equipped physically for such a confrontation. The demon watched the security cameras, which were all perfectly functional, and how they tracked the progress of the doctor and his friend. Four men sat in front of him, all apparently asleep in their chairs. They had been perfectly aware and in charge of the situation, until Merrick had decided to let them have a little more rest. As usual, Doctor Curiosity's plans had been doomed to spectacular failure, but then that wouldn't have helped Merrick at all. Curiosity needed to die, and whilst the demon could not affect him directly, he would be happy to do all he could to indirectly ensure he killed himself. So Merrick had helped with the cameras and with security, but he was perfectly happy to stand there and watch the end of the contract come to fruition. A distinct smell of sulfur accompanied the arrival of two more men in to the room, and then a fourth and a fifth. Each of them had a similar look to the first demon, and they were all arrayed in suits that looked good enough to catch the eye, but were not so impeccably tailored as to make you think that they were too far above you. Salesmanship is a fine art, and these had all mastered it. "So, you got him to break in to the tiger enclosure by himself?" asked one of them. "Oh yes. For a man who believes himself to be so clever, he can be quite a fool. This will be even more fun to watch than the time I got the Bishop to jump off the top of the Cathedral." Laughter echoed around the small surveillance room, but none of it had any joy, or any life. *** The second pair of feet hit the verdant grass, and Doctor Curiosity was suddenly very aware that he could not see the tiger. He motioned to Errol to be still, and slowly moved forwards, bringing out a pistol from his bag, complete with tranquilizer darts. This was to be a last resort, since the whole point of the mission was to be able to take on a tiger without resorting to any devices, curious or not. The doctor padded forwards, keeping both eyes out for any potential trouble, and was soon rewarded by spotting the tiger. It had taken a few steps back to retreat from the noise and confusion of the grenade that had allowed Curiosity access to the enclosure, but it was still standing, unbowed and magnificent in the artificial light. The black stripes across the fur seemed only to increase the impressive nature of the creature, and even unmoving it stood in a manner which seemed impervious to trouble. The doctor's heart rate increased once more. He tucked his pistol into his belt, and moved forwards slowly towards the tiger. Its eyes turned without a single movement of the head, to focus on the approaching doctor. His blood ran cold all of a sudden, and he backed away a single step, before whispering to his one remaining Eegor. "Jah… now is ze time for you to approach ze beast. I have you covered vith ze tranquilizers." Errol looked terrified, but he had evidently decided that the night was one to do or die, and he advanced past the doctor to come near the tiger. He was shaking, but the tiger seemed to pay him no heed at all. Soon he was within four or five paces of the beast, and then closer still. His hand descended, and Errol was touching the white tiger. "It's… it's purring!" said Errol, in a disbelieving voice. Curiosity readied his pistol and moved forwards slightly, the grass somehow having become dew-laden, making his leg feel wet. Very authentic, thought the doctor. Underneath Errol, the tiger was dropping down to lie on the rock it had been standing on. Errol's terrified frown had become a smile, and he was stroking the back of the tiger. Could this become an exercise in futility? Were these tigers tamed beyond attacking strangers? Errol looked around and caught the eye of the doctor. "Errr… doctor." "Jah?" "Look down. Look down now." Curiosity complied, thinking nothing of it, only to find that there was a tiger between his legs, licking at his trouser leg and purring. "Aaarrrggghhh!" screamed the doctor, diving backwards and firing his tranquilizer pistol aimlessly into the air. The loud noise pricked the ears of the tiger next to Errol, and it stood up quickly, scaring the poor Eegor wannabe and making him fall over backwards. The tiger followed him, apparently concerned that he was okay and displaying no aggressive tendencies. This was not so for the tiger next to the doctor. It roared and bared its claws as it moved towards him, rearing onto its hind legs. Curiosity aimed the pistol, and was exceptionally annoyed to find out that he had fired all of the shots already. The tiger looked as if it was about to land right on him, and as it descended he could have sworn he heard five different voices laughing at him, all at a remove, like a bad stereo system being broadcast from thirty feet away. Curiosity shut his eyes and tried to shuffle forwards, only to hear the sounds of something missing his head by scant inches. He opened his eyes to see the tiger with its paws either side of his shoulders. "Get it off me!" he yelled, rolling away and getting back to his feet. Curiosity ran as fast as he could towards the hole in the window, and he was almost there before he felt the heaviest weight slam into his back, and he was half stunned as he hit the base of the wall very hard. Looking up, Curiosity saw the white tiger imperious above him. He realized then that perhaps he was not invulnerable. Perhaps the demon had been lying all along. He had made a mistake and the tiger could gut him here, a simple as if he were the prey and the tiger was the predator. And indeed maybe they were fulfilling those roles. The tiger's paws came up, and with a roar it brought them down. No matter how hard he wished it, Doctor Curiosity's arms could never have protected him from the full force of the enormous tiger crashing down upon him… …and yet it never hit. After a couple of seconds, Curiosity dared to open his eyes, and he saw the tiger directly above him, suspended as if by an invisible thread from the distant overhanging ceiling. It was scant inches above the doctor, and yet it was not touching him. He was unsure what was going on, but then drops of blood hit his shoulder, and he looked around to see the rest of the scenario. The tiger had come down upon him, claws at the ready, but in the meantime, Errol had sprinted back to defend his master, and had grabbed the nearest thing from his bag. As such, he had placed a shovel upright in front of Doctor Curiosity, and the tiger had landed hard upon it. The blade had stood fast for a couple of seconds, before the sheer weight of the tiger had forced itself through the shovel and on to the shat. The enormous blade had cause untold internal damage to the white tiger, but it had prevented it from coming in contact with the doctor, and he looked up at it, still in awe and in terror. "I tzink… I tzink it might be a good idea to get ze hell out of here." Curiosity rolled out from under the tiger and grabbed the stunned boy who had just saved his life. They both clambered up to the hole in the window, as the remaining tiger investigated the corpse of its friend, and possibly its mate. "Jah! Ve escaped!" proclaimed the doctor as he hit the ground on the safe side of the glass. "I… I killed it." "Jah! But ve von! Ve escaped!" "But… it's an endangered species. I'm going to get in so much trouble." "Nein! I disabled the cameras." "But… but… the CSI guys work around here. I'm sure to get caught!" "You'll be fine!" yelled the doctor, grabbing Errol by the shoulders. "Now get a damn grip and let us get the hell out of here!" And so they ran off together, the skinny kid having done more for his cause to become the next Eegor than a lifetime of merely helping out during wrestling matches. Both of them had reasons to be cheerful, and yet neither of them did. *** It was minutes until the match would begin, in the unfamiliar territory of Portugal. Neither contestant would know whether the crowd would be behind them or not, but ultimately it wouldn't matter. Curiosity knew that tigers were dangerous beasts that could end his life… but it was just something that Merrick had told him, just like the lie that he was unable to be killed. He knew now; there was something inherently wrong in the assumptions and the promises of the demon. He should have been smarter earlier, but now the die was cast. He didn't believe that there was a tiger coming for him, and he was going to go in to the match focused, with all of his impressive brain brought to focus on getting past his opponent. The moves were memorized by his muscles and the strength was dormant, but ready to erupt if it would be needed. Doctor Curiosity shook himself down and put his hand into his pocket. He's stolen something from his sister at the start of the week, at a time that now seemed a life or two ago. Yet this was the thing that would ultimately protect him. This was the object through which he would be free, and through which he would save his own soul. He wasn't going to die fighting Vangelus Olsig, but what was to happen afterwards would be his real struggle. He'd sold his soul to a demon, and this could not be overturned by winning a wrestling tournament. This was a sideshow. This was a prelude. Curiosity tightened his grip on the tiny metal object, and knew that he would need more than just its intersecting bars to save himself. He'd need belief as well, but for the time being… It was his only weapon in his struggle with Merrick, and he knew that in the end it would allow him to win. Not now, but forever more. Curiosity lifted it up to his mouth and kissed the little silver cross. The crucifix by which he could be saved. And he went out, to face his true destiny. Unadorned of any demon's shackles. To win.
