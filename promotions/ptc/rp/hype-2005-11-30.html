Hype

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Hype (n)- 1. Excessive publicity and the ensuing commotion 2. Exaggerated or extravagant claims made especially in advertising or promotional material 3. An advertising or promotional ploy 4. Something deliberately misleading; a deception So they said they wanted hype. That want to cause a stir, a little shameless self promotion. Think I need it? GCW World Champion, three time and current Infinite Gauntlet Champion. Not good enough? Almasy called me one of the men you DON’T want to face in GTT5. Current number two wrestler in all of PTC, don’t believe me, check the rankings. Yeah, that’s a little better, I thought you’d like that one. Of course I’m talking to you, and you know exactly who you are. I could name names, point fingers, but it’d all be wasted, because the sick feeling in your gut betrays you. You’re worried, anxious, upset, maybe just a little bit unsure of yourself. You’re good and you know it, but right now you start to wonder if you’re good enough. You’ve had all the people telling you that you’ll do well in the tournament, getting to round two was going to be a lock. Too bad you came up against a great big fucking road block. If you couldn’t tell I meant me. It’s ok though, you’ll talk your big talk, and it’ll all be well and good, but face the facts kid, I’m just better. Maybe you can make people chuckle, I’ll make them bust a gut. Want to talk suspense, feel free to put them on the edge of their seats, I’ll knock them out of their chairs. Tug on their heart strings for all I care, cause I can make grown men weep. Three months ago no one knew who the hell I was, the dark horse. Now mine is the name whispered in those hushed tones. The name that makes people quiver, makes people get that cold chill in the base of their spines when it’s heard floating on the wind. Thing is I’m still the new blood, people want to see how I compare against the greats, a category I don’t see you falling into any time soon. Sorry, it’s not that you’re not a worthy competitor, and it’s nothing personal, but after round one you’re going to be watching the show from the sidelines. I just can’t let you win, I’ve got to give people the show they came to see. Scared yet? You should be, in fact I’m not sure you’re telling the complete truth. For all the bravado, all the bluster, I think inside you’re just a scared little kid. Sorta like the guy at the end of the bar picking a fight with smallest weakest guy he sees. Sure, he’ll win the fight, but have someone real step up to him, someone who isn’t completely defenseless and he folds like a house of cards. That’s you, and yes I’m still talking to YOU. Did I forget to mention I’m one of the top contenders to the PTC Extreme title? Sorry, things like that slip my mind sometimes. That’s right though, the PTC Extreme title, and the thing is I’m not even a hardcore type guy. See I’m a wrestler, I twisted bodies, I make people tag out, I don’t hit them with still chairs, I don’t do flying twisting sommersault corkscrew moon drivers through twenty flaming tables. But after I’m done turning someone into a human pretzel they’ll long for the days of those flaming tables, at least it’s over quick, you’re unconscious and you don’t have to feel it. With me you’re awake for every second of abuse, you feel every little bit of torture I put you body through. If you don’t think that’s extreme, I’ve got some real nice land for sale, it’s cheap, must sell. Leave you A game at home, for that matter forget your A plus game. You’re going to need to be at your very best, the absolute pinnacle of your own personal achievement to even step foot in the same ring with me. If you’re lucky, it might even be enough to survive. And really, that’s the best you can hope for, not a victory, not advancement, pure and simple survival. What I’m trying to say, is you can’t win, nope, no chance in hell. You might as well give up. You’re thinking about it now aren’t you? How easy it would be to just not show up. Sure you’ll have to deal with a few morons calling you a coward, but really won’t it be worth a little humiliation to spare yourself a life time of pain? If I were in your shoes, I sure as hell know what my answer would be. So you want hype? You just met him, my name’s Michael "Spitfire" Stevens, I’ll be the guy handing you your ass in a week or two. Nothing personal, it’s just business kid, hell maybe you’ll learn something. And that's hype you better damn sure believe
