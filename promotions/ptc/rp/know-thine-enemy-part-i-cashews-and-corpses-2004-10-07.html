Know Thine Enemy: Part I: Cashews and Corpses

7 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-07 Joey Capone was a weasel. Worse than that, he was a nobody from a long line of nobodies, the littlest grease-ball on the Mafia totem pole. So, when he wound up dead in a gutter, nobody really cared much. He had no family; he was a wannabe wise-guy with ties to the DeNucci family. Nothing more, just a lackey. Second page news, a one line obituary. Small time. The cruiser screeched to a halt at the mouth of the alley, cherries ablaze atop the dark sedan. Brian Davis tossed a handful of Tums in his mouth and stepped out into the chilly night air, turning his collar up against the wind. It felt like snow, they were already calling for an early winter. He stood there for a moment, collecting his thoughts, giving the chalky-sweet antacids time to quell the hot acid in his chest. The wind whipped through his hair; hair that was shot through with more and more gray each morning. With a sigh that was more irritation than exhaustion, he stepped away from the car, his footsteps echoing in the hushed silence. So many uniforms and flashing lights, but the volume seemed to be dialed down. It was weird, creepy even. He nodded to the uniform that stood watch and stepped over the crime scene tape, his breath steaming as he spoke in clipped tones, his New York accent creeping through just a little with his irritation. “Lay it on me, what do we got?” He scowled down the alley, watching the lights wink on and off, bouncing off the wet concrete. The meds were already here; simply a formality, and the coroner would be showing up soon no doubt. “We got another gangland execution killing, that’s what we got.” “Fuck me. That’s the second one in a week. Who is it this time?” Davis started walking, leaving Jim Foster of the crime lab no choice but to follow. “I’m not sure, most of his face is gone. I was hoping maybe you could shed a little light on that. This is your bailiwick, not mine.” Davis chuckled, the sound unkind. “Yeah. Knowin’ the greasy pieces of shit, that’s my specialty. Well, let’s have a look see.” He hunkered down next to the body, looking at the huge hole in the back of the head. Blood stained the ground, making a puddle that glistened in the garish wash of lights. The smell was sickening in the close confines, the stench of copper, and human waste. Davis nodded to the techs, which rolled the body over, giving him a look at the remains of the face. “Sweet Jesus. One shot would have been enough. But two in the face? God, look at these burns, he must have had that firestick shoved right up his nose…” “Yeah. Same as last one, wasn’t it?” Jim rubbed his chin, looking thoughtful. “You know, it just occurred to me, this guy we’re looking for, I think there’s a pattern. Maybe we just can’t see the big picture.” Davis was only half listening as he looked at the face, trying to fill in the missing pieces. It was a jigsaw puzzle, and a bloody mess. The only thing there was the chin and bottom lip, intact. A bit of greasy forehead. Some hair. Trace elements, not much to make a positive ID on. Something else caught his eye, something small and silver, glittering on the neck. With a gloved finger, he delicately lifted the chain, looking at the medallion. “St. Christopher.” “What?” Foster leaned closer, squinting at the tiny silver charm. “Yeah. St. Chris. Patron saint of something or other. Travel, I think. You know who had one of these? Joey Capone… heh, this looks like Joey… bad dresser… stinks like shit. Probably have to ID off his dental records… not much else left here, Jimbo.” “What do you make of that, Brian? The glass, in the eyes… that’s something I just don’t get. Creeps me out.” Davis shrugged, looking again at the ruined face. The eye sockets were intact, the eyes missing, replaced with jagged shards of mirror. “I dunno. Maybe this whack-job likes to see his face in his victim’s eyes before he offs them. Maybe he likes to check his hair before he pulls the trigger. Who knows what makes these freaks tick? Hey, you hear about that arson at the old amusement park? Some firebug torched the place, roasted a coupla squatters alive. How’s that for a cozy campfire.” “This is a mad, mad world we live in, Bry. Forehead shot is definitely post mortem, look at the lack of blood.” Davis shook his head, turning his back on the grisly display as he stood up slowly. “Same as before. He shoots ‘em once in the back of the head, the money shot. Then he plays his sick little games. The shots in the head, or the torso… and the glass. I don’t think we’re playing with our garden-variety psycho here; this guy is smart. Bust out the can of cashews, Jimbo, we got ourselves a real nut here.” Foster snickered, a sound that was more hysteria than amusement. “Smart and nuts. Sounds like a healthy breakfast. Total Raisin Nut Bran… smart, with nuts.” “Cute, Jimbo, real cute. Don’t quit your day job. I don’t think you’ll be making that appearance on Laugh-In any time soon. This stopped being funny tonight. We better not have another goddamn serial on our hands, that’s just what I need.” Davis walked back to the waiting sedan, pulling open the door just so he could bask in the heat that poured from the vents. He tossed one last thing over his shoulder before sliding behind the wheel. “Keep me posted, boys… you find anything as much as a pussy hair, I want to be the first to know, capeesh?” They don’t understand me, not that it surprises me much. Most people don’t. Why do I even bother, trying to state my case here? Who is going to read this if and when I pass on? I have no family, no friends, no legacy. I am nothing more than an outsider now. Looking in. Still, it soothes my conscience to set down this record, to at least speak my piece. Everyone wants to have his or her story heard. Why was I so different? Well, I had made it thus far. Golden Turnbuckles Tournament, round 3. Strange, I had never really fancied myself a fighter, and now I found myself falling into that role with ease. Perhaps it was the anger. Perhaps it was simply the anonymity. I could be whoever I wanted to be, and they didn’t know. They didn’t know me at all. I liked it that way. Let me mold their opinions in the ring, with my skill… with my size, as I crushed my opponents. That much was logical, and I was after all, ever the pragmatist. My first opponents were nothing, the victories coming as a surprise to me nonetheless, only due to my sheer lack of experience. And now, I faced my toughest opponent. Angelo Deville. The Deville. A shark, in human form. A cold-blooded killer. He fancied himself a hand of the devil, or so I had heard. Sadly, I doubted that. Having been to hell and back, I really didn’t see that particular taint on his soul. I didn’t fear him, as that particular emotion seemed to have faded away when I confronted my last demon. They didn’t understand what my father had to do with things, or even what she had to do with it. Everything. Nothing and everything, all at once. They made me who I am, molding me into something I had grown to hate. Enough about them, I would be forced to deal with Deville soon enough and they no longer existed. I knew nothing about him, other than the circles he ran in. Organized crime. Masterminds of chaos, my brethren, in another form. They bred evil, and he was their henchman. That much I understood. So, why the glass, you ask? I’ll tell you. I wanted to see myself in their shoes. The criminal element, the greasy gangster type. So, I killed them, with their own methods. And then I placed the glass in their eyes, all the better to see myself with. Yes, I wanted to understand. I wasn’t any closer to epiphany… Jackson Namenlos-Duisternis sat atop the roof of the university dorm across town, holding a string of silver rosary beads in his hand, absently stroking their smooth contours with his cold fingers. His hair blew around his face, almost stroking him tenderly, the way a lover would. He missed her touch, hell, truth be told he missed ANY touch. The cigarette burned away in his mouth, ashes falling down the front of his leather jacket, unnoticed. He watched the lights blink out, one by one, listening to the sounds fade away to silence. Darkness fell over him, diffused only by the feeble glow of the moon behind the drifting clouds. “Where do I go from here?” he mused aloud, his words ripped away by the rising wind. It felt like snow, winter’s chill was already creeping into the air. That was a good question. Where indeed? “I can’t very well go on randomly killing people, can I? I mean, it’s all well and good for people like Deville, who make a solid living on it, but let’s face it, I can’t get a tax break for that. Something about that pesky death certificate… makes it hard to get a regular pay cheque. Murder for hire, not a career choice I would ever have considered, although, I suppose in comparing, what I did wasn’t much different. Espionage and trickery on a different scale. I’m sure the innocent blood is all the same, when you come right down to it. Hey, a job’s a job, right?” His voice faded away into another introspective silence, and that was when he became aware of the trembling in his extremities. His hands shook, his legs trembling so that his knees were almost knocking. “Fear?” He sounded almost shocked, but yes, he was afraid. Terrified, in fact. He could feel the dryness on his cheeks, where the tears had dried in the brisk wind. A grown man, and here he was, shaking and crying like nothing more than a girl. For shame, what would his father think? “Fuck him,” he muttered, his voice vibrating with anger. His eyes glittered with suppressed fury, their blue depths darkening slightly, like the sky before a summer storm. Lightning flashed in his eyes and he let loose a sound that could best be described as a growl. “I fear no one. Nothing. I am invincible. Unbeatable.” Lies, all of them, but if you couldn’t lie to yourself, who could you lie to? How far would you go to deceive yourself? He would go to the ends of the earth… to hell and back, if you will… all to satisfy that one desire. For… “Revenge. Vengeance is mine, sayeth the Lord. Well, tonight, God’s taking a little vacation… and he left me in charge. Very sorry all,” he stood, letting the rosary fall over the edge of the roof, listening to the sound of it clattering against the concrete below. “Welcome to hell.”
