Love is a four-letter word. So is Sage.

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-11 * * * * * Isis Hollinger sat with her face buried in her palms, sobbing. Nathan’s old friend, Warden of Pennsylvania State Penitentiary, Leo Levandowski sat by her side, a gentle and consoling hand placed across her back. When she sat up and looked at Leo, her face was a mix of tears and snot, eyes puffy and cheeks reddened, "Why is this happening?" Leo could tell it was a rhetorical question, and didn’t attempt to try and rationalize the situation with a shoddy answer. He ran a hand back through his gray hair, and sat in the waiting room with Isis, who was still clad in her bloody wedding dress. Nathan had been scheduled for surgery to graft skin from his thigh onto the palms of his hands. Smaller surgeries over the course of the following week would provide skin for his fingers. With the sudden shattering of Nathan’s kneecap, the surgery was also going to open up that wound and remove the splintered bone. He would be in traction for weeks, and physical therapy for much longer. Leo Levandowski was only in Denver for the day, after having flown out from Pennsylvania to see the wedding, and would be catching a flight back later in the evening. Since Nathan and Isis were supposed to be on their way to the Caribbean immediately after the wedding, there was no reason for him to remain in Denver any longer. Besides, he hated the cold. But what he would hate even more is leaving a longtime friend – a man who earned his respect and friendship while incarcerated in the prison he was Warden of – bedridden and hospitalized with injuries that seemed to have manifested themselves out of thin air. Leo stood up to stretch his legs, leaving Isis sitting in the chair, still sobbing. Kissing your husband at the alter, only to have blood cover your cheeks and skin missing from his hands was one thing. But having him be laying in a hospital bed, and a few dozen clean cuts appear on his legs was another. A person could only take so much, and when Nathan’s kneecap shattered itself into oblivion, and he chomped down with such force that his front tooth cracked and rocketed out of his mouth sent Isis into a small state of shock. The doctors had given her a small sedative. Nothing major, only something to calm her nerves. Leo was on his second lap around the waiting room when Nathan’s doctor walked in, and whispered into Leo’s ear, "I’d like to see you out in the hallway." Isis looked up, her face full of sudden hope slightly overwhelming the initial fear of seeing the Doctor who could possibly have brought bad news with him. She nodded towards Leo in a quick approval of the Doctor’s request. Both men exited the waiting room and stood in the hallway. The doctor had a small smudge of blood on the collar of his shirt. His hands were in his pockets, and his green eyes seemed to be thinking ahead of what he was about to say. "Mr. Hollinger’s surgeries were a success, he’s currently in post-op and we’re currently awaiting to see if the sutures heal. I just want to ask you something about the nature of the injuries." "Go on." "How did they happen?" "I’d like to know that, myself, Doc." "Mr. Levandowski, I’m serious. The wounds to the hands are some of the most severe cases of burns I’ve ever come across. The small cuts on the leg carry the traits of being self-induced, although they aren’t as old as the wounds on Mr. Hollinger’s hands. Which means that someone else put them there." The doctor waited, letting the last sentence sink in. Deep down, both men knew where the conversation was going. Leo leaned back towards the door to the waiting room and peeked in through the small window, towards Isis. She was thumbing mindlessly through a magazine, ironically enough, about vacation destinations in the Caribbean. The doctor continued, "And when Mr. Hollinger was admitted earlier today, his knee was in perfectly good condition. In fact, I do believe the cuts on his calf weren’t present at the time of admittance as well. Considering your profession, Mr. Levandowski, my question to you is this: Are we dealing with spousal abuse?" Leo was shocked that the laugh erupted out of him before he could respond, and the wide eyes on the Doctor’s face only strengthened that feeling, "Abuse? Hardly. Doctor, with all due respect to the situation, the wounds appeared to manifest themselves. Isis would never harm Nathan in any way." "Are you positive of that?" "Positive? I’m absolutely positive of that!" Leo commanded, his voice deepening, "What you don’t understand is that Nathan saved her, Doc. He means everything to her, and she would never harm him in any way. In fact, considering the extreme nature of the wounds, I think the fact that she is still here and holding it together shows just exactly how much she loves him." "His knee couldn’t have shattered by itself. That would take a great deal of force." They both stood in silence for a moment. A nurse directed a patient sitting in a wheelchair past them, towards his room elsewhere in the hospital. "Look elsewhere, Doc. That poor girl is going through the worst possible wedding day in the history of wedding days. I’d be more worried about her ripping that magazine she’s reading to pieces more so than her crushing her new husbands knee into itty bitty bits." The doctor nodded, realizing that the line of questioning would probably not lead to any clues, "Mr. Hollinger will be kept in the hospital for quite some time as he recuperates. I’m going to recommend that he be placed in ICU, and have a guard watch over him. Considering his checkered past, I’m going to be making the documents and admission of his stay here at the hospital kept secret for the time being. The last thing I need is a media circus swarming around my hospital." Leo Levandowski smiled, and placed a hand atop the doctor’s shoulder, "Thank you." "Now, it’s your job as a good friend to go deliver the good news to Mrs. Hollinger. Her husband is already on the road to recovery, just as long as no more wounds seem to manifest themselves." The doctor’s eyes narrowed as he said the last sentence, somewhat leery and skeptical of the explanation of the injuries Leo had given him. Leo noticed it too, and failed to shake the doctor’s hand before making his way back into the waiting room. Just as Leo passed through the swinging door, a nurse rushed into the hallway from the post-op ward. Her apron was covered in smatterings of blood, and there were a few flecks of it across her face. "Doctor, something’s happened to Nathan Hollinger. While we were wheeling him back to ICU, something went wrong. Something went very wrong –" "Slow down, slow down. What are you talking about? Did the sutures not hold?" "No, no, dear God I wish it were that. He’s got new wounds across his chest, deep lacerations stretching all the way across. Except they just appeared there, letter by letter!" Doctor McDaniel stood, mouth agape and unsure of what to say, "Did you say…letters?" "Yes, sir. Four letters," The nurse exhaled heavily, finally able to catch her breath. She gently wiped away a few of the blood splatter across her face, which smeared across her cheek, "S-A-G-E." McDaniel stood there for a moment, contemplating what to do. He swung the door open, barely peeking his head into the waiting room where Leo Levandowski was giving Isis Hollinger the good news about her husband. He sighed, "Mrs. Hollinger, Mr. Levandowski…I’m going to need you both to come with me." * * * * * Leo Levandowski stood next to Isis Hollinger, his arm wrapped around her shoulders, holding her close. They both watched as Nathan slept, his newly shattered knee now surgically repaired and in traction. But what they stared at were the bloody bandages that were taped across his chest, where the word had appeared. The wounds required nearly 200 stitches per letter. He would wear it for the rest of his life. Isis, now clad in a pair of baggy sweatpants and a Denver Bronco’s sweatshirt that was three sizes too large, stared at her sleeping husband. They had been married no more than 20 hours, but it felt like an eternity. Trauma slows down the passage of time, and minutes crawl like they were days, and hours seem like a lifetime. "Has Nathan ever told you about his…power?" Leo asked, his voice gruff with age, but also empty and confused. Isis turned and stared at Leo, curious. At first she said nothing, and was ready to respond, but held the words in her mouth. They both stood there for another moment, watching Nathan sleep as the machines continued to monitor his health. Outside of the door, an armed guard stood watch. "During the six years he was incarcerated in my prison, we became friends. Better friends than a prisoner and warden ever should be. One day, about four years into his sentence, he confessed to me that he had a power. Something that neither you, nor I, possess." Isis was shocked at this revelation, even though Nathan had only been in her life merely a year, they had shared so much truth and honesty with each other that anyone would have mistaken it for a lifetime, "I don’t know what you’re talking about. Since the day I met him, the only power he’s had was the ability to win me over. I love him, Leo, I really do. But I think he would have told me about anything extraordinary." She turned away, and watched Nathan. Wondered what dreams might be going through his mind. She wondered if he had felt the new wounds on his chest manifest themselves, splitting skin and tearing muscle. Leo continued with the story, "He told me that sometimes his dreams came true. So I guess it really isn’t a power, per say, but more of precognition. And since that day in August, aboard that airplane, he hasn’t been able to remember any of his life that occurred beforehand. The doctors call it retrograde amnesia. But I don’t know, and Nathan started to believe that whoever had framed him – these ‘Eyes of Fear’ loonies – that they had somehow changed him." As Leo talked, Isis thought back and suddenly the pieces of the puzzle fell into place. She did remember Nathan, back when they had first met, coming into the diner where she worked at the oddest hours of the night, looking sleepless. Nathan had told her about a dream that he kept reliving night after night, about green eyes and a menacing howl. And then on the night before their wedding – last night – as the green meteorite streaked across the sky, Nathan had said something that startled Isis: "I’ve seen this before." "When these injuries first started happening to Nathan, deep down inside I feared that he had started to do this to himself. I don’t know how, and I don’t know why he would, " Leo paused and sighed. To Isis, he looked like a loving father who didn’t know what to make of a trouble-laden son, "But since he had claimed this extraordinary power so many years ago, I never doubted him. The man never lied to me, and gave me no reason to believe he ever would. So in the darkest corner of my heart, I really hoped that it was Nathan doing these horrible things to his own body. But that all changed when that word etched itself across his chest." Isis finally tore loving and affectionate gaze away from Nathan, and stepped between her husband and Leo Levandowski. To her, the warden looked like he had been through twelve rounds with a moral dilemma. His saggy face seemed pale, and his gray hair was disheveled, "What changed?" "Do you know what the word means? Sage?" he asked. "Somehow, in this particular case, I doubt it means what the dictionary says." "It’s a name. A last name, Clinton Sage’s last name." Leo paused, and finally met her gaze. The whites of her eyes had pinked with misery and crying, but the blue stood out in a stark contrast, "Clinton Sage is the asshole wrestler who had unknowingly freed Nathan from jail a year ago. He had found all the evidence the government would need to set him free, and reveal that the true people behind the murders aboard Flight 1020 were none other than the ‘Eyes of Fear’." Isis didn’t know what to say. In her mind, she could have sworn she knew the name Sage from somewhere else. From something that Nathan had told her. She let the warden continue, though. "So when I saw the name across the chest, I knew it wasn’t Nathan doing these horrible things to himself. I knew it was that bastard Clinton Sage. I know it makes absolutely no sense, dear, but I’m most certain of it. And when I get back to Pennsylvania, I’m going to make sure I pay Mr. Sage a visit." The sternness of his voice reassured Isis, and the narrowing of Leo’s eyes as he stated his intent gave her some mild hope. But she couldn’t help but search through her mind, trying to figure out why the name Sage seemed so important to her, and why Nathan had mentioned it. Leo continued, "I don’t know how much more he can take. My flight was supposed to leave tonight, so I had booked another in two days. But I think I’m going to change it back, so I can put a stop to this." Then it hit Isis, and she remembered where Nathan had mentioned Clinton Sage’s name before, she looked at Leo and added to the story, "During his wrestling run as Spartan, Nathan had to qualify for last years GTT tournament, and his qualifying match was against Clinton Sage! Apparently Nathan had absolutely destroyed Clinton in the ring, and it drove Clinton crazy. Sage apparently seethed that he couldn’t know who had been under the mask, he needed to know who had beaten him. After Nathan had won, he offered to help Clinton off of the mat in a show of sportsmanship." Isis turned back towards her husband as tears started to well up and shimmer in her eyes. She bit her bottom lip to prevent it from quivering, and continued, "Sage refused, and stood up himself. Nathan told me that Clinton said only one thing to him as they locked eyes, and stood inside of the squared circle. He told me that it was the only thing that ever troubled him about his return to wrestling, because he was baffled that people would claim to go to such distances…" Leo leaned in, placing a hand on each of Isis’ shoulders, "What did he say?" She looked up as the first tears fell down her cheeks, "That he would stop at nothing to find out who was under the mask." Leo turned away from Isis, looking over her shoulder towards Nathan’s chest. His gaze hovered down to Nathan’s shattered knee, and then back up towards his shredded hands, "It seems he’s found a way to live up to that promise." Isis Hollinger sobbed into Leo’s shoulder for a moment, her tears mixing with snot and disgust, and she placed a hand on Leo’s left cheek and pulled him down towards her, "Book another ticket to Philadelphia. We leave tonight." "What? That’s crazy, I can’t let you come with me! It could be dangerous, and Nathan needs you here!" "He saved me a year ago by refusing to give up on me, and give in like everyone else had done! He was the one who sat next to me until I drank myself stupid, and passed out, and then he drove me to the rehab clinic and checked me in. And he waited for three months as I cleaned my life up, just so he could hope of a future with me." Leo realized there was no winning this argument, and said nothing. "So I’m not going to sit by and watch some asshole turn my husband into his own personal voodoo doll!" she exclaimed, her voice still quaking from the anger and sadness. She wiped away the tears and shot a quick glance back towards Nathan, "So book another ticket, Leo, please. He did everything he could to save me, and I’m going to return the favor. He is my husband, Leo, and I love him." Leo Levandowski sighed, and nodded. "I’ll inform the nurses and doctors of my plans. I want them to prevent Nathan from seeing the wound across his chest. I don’t want him to know where we went." She stopped, lost in thought for a minute, her gaze reaching far beyond the hospital room and out towards wherever Clinton Sage might be, "But before we go, I need to stop back at the house. There is something I have that I think Clinton Sage will want to see." Isis Hollinger wiped the last tear away from her eye, and walked over and placed a kiss on her sleeping husbands lips. He saved her a year ago. She was going to save him. * * * * *
