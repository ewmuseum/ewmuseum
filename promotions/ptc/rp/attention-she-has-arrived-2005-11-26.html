Attention : She has arrived!

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Channel 4 FOX Breaking News Flash. Hello, I am Madeline Strikes. Earlier this evening it was confirmed that the PTC infederation has indeed opened its doors for this year's interfederation tournament, GTT5. This is the fifth year for the prestigious tournament and like the ones of the past it has not been a disappointment. Wrestlers have already begun to arrive from thier home federations such as GW, AWC, PRIME,UWF, and even GCW. Among the GCW wrestlers rumored to have entered one name stands out. Eva VanLorne, the first female wrestler to make a name for herself in GCW has entered GTT5 in hopes of creating a sensation in the tournament. Ms. VanLorne was caught arriving on the scene earlier and this is what she had to say. * Cut to taped arrival scene* " Ms. VanLorne! MS. VanLorne! Is it true that you could face some of the top wrestlers ever to have entered the wrestling arena? And if so how do you feel about that?" " It is true that I have the possibility of facing some rather large names, but this is not something I am unaccustomed too. I feel that I will make my mark here, just as I have done in GCW by kicking a little tail first then taking names later." " It is said the first round opponents have already been drawn and you are to face Xin Xin Xiong from FWO. How well do you think you will fare against him?" " Xin Xin Xiong, from what I have heard and researched on my own will be a tough competitor to get past, but I am fully confident that I will slip past him and move on in the tournament. I didn't come here to lose. I came to win and that is exactly what I am going to do." " Ms. VanLorne!" " If you wish for any more answers you may watch the Tonight Show with Jay Leno this evening. Thank you, no further questions." * cut scene back to the news room* Others to arrive have been named as Seymour Almasy, Lestatt Knight and Johnny Lexicon of GCW. Tyler O'Neil of PRIME, Shadow of Global. The news 4 team will follow the tournament closely with updates after each round. Good luck to all the wrestlers competing. I am Madeline Strikes, GTYX Channel 4 News. Thank you and good night.
