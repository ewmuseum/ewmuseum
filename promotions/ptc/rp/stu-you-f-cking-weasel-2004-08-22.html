Date: 2004-08-22 Foreword I’m sure that everybody in PTC has seen Stu ([name withheld]) on the forums. Just keep him in mind. On to my rp… ***** He still can’t believe it… he has to go on a camping trip with Joey Troy, Ian English, and Thug roC as part of a “bonding” experiment set up by PRIME officials in order to unite the elected team for Primetime Central’s Survival Interfed pay-per-view. Yeah, sure, Joey Troy was his best friend, but the other two… far from it. And it wasn’t that Rolo and Thug roC hated each other… it’s just that if Rolo had to pick someone to go on a camping trip with, it wouldn’t be roC. And as for Ian English… well, let’s just say that English knows how to grate on Rolo’s last nerve… that red-headed prick. Rolo zips up his huge camping bag and throws it on the ground. From out of nowhere, his eight year old son comes running into the room and leaps into his father’s arms. Tony Rolo: Woah, Vinny! You’re getting to big to be jumping at me like that. Vinny Rolo: Can I pwease come wit, Daddy? Tony Rolo: You have to stay here and watch Mommy for me. Vinny Rolo: But I wanna come wit you. Tony Rolo: How bout this… before summer’s over, me and Uncle Joey will take you camping. Like all children, Vinny wants what he wants now… not later, but he’ll look all sad and settle for that just for effect. Vinny Rolo: Okay… you promise? Tony Rolo: Yeah, I promise. At this point Tony’s wife, Allison, enters the room. Allison Rolo: All packed up? Tony Rolo: Yeah, I’m all ready. I still can’t believe I actually have to go do this stupid camping trip thing with Ian English, of all people. Allison Rolo: Maybe it’ll be good for you guys as a team… who knows. The cell phone in Tony’s pocket begins to ring. The caller ID says “fuckface,” aka Joey Troy. For those who don’t know, Tony Rolo and Joey Troy make up the tag-team of Crudely Civilized. Many say their success as a tag-team is derived from the fact that they are the best of friends outside of the ring. Tony answers the phone and if this were a movie, the screen would go split, with Tony on one half and Joey on the other. Tony Rolo: Hey. While trying to squeeze the phone between his ear and his shoulder because both hands are tied up rolling a joint… Joey Troy: Where are you, you fucking faggot? You were supposed to be here ten minutes ago. Tony Rolo: Don’t get your panties in a bundle… I’m leaving now. Joey Troy: Well hurry the fuck up… I don’t have all day to be waiting for you. Give your wife a quickie and get the fuck over here. It’s sort of a love/hate relationship that Tony and Joey share. Some don’t understand their relationship, citing their differences in lifestyle… Rolo being the devoted family man and Joey being the young, swinging, alcoholic stoner. But somehow, way back when, they found enough common ground form quite a partnership… and we’re not talking about anything homosexual either, unless you bring up Ian English and his undying love for Elton John. But that’s another story for another time. ***** Hours and hours later… Ian English: I can’t fackin' read this shite. Wot’s wrong wif youse Americans? Don’t youse arse-wipes know how to draw a bloody map? Tony Rolo: Hey jackass, it’s you fucking Europeans that thought the earth was fucking flat… moron. Ian English: I can’t make out this fackin' thing. Joey Troy: Gimme that! Joey snatches the map from Ian. Ian English: Oi! Joey Troy: You dumb fuck… you had it upside down. Ian English: How the bleedin' 'ell am I suppose to know which side is up? Thug roC: Maybe by the side that has the N on it for north. Ian English: Wotch it mate - I ain't the minority here! Joey Troy: Well, according to this map, we have to hike about another two miles east. That’s where the camp should be set up. ***** Roughly forty minutes later, the foursome stumbles upon the already set up campsite. There is one large tent, big enough for all to share… a fire already going, sending a trail of smoke straight up… and foldable camping chairs surrounding the fire. On each chair is a packet of papers that none of them have noticed yet. Ian English: Wot the...? They can fack 'orf! Only one tent? I aint sleeping in the same tent eif youse two lovebirds (Ian points towards Troy and Rolo) and yer homoish ways . If I and get any kip in there with youse, I probably won’t be able to walk right fer months because me arse will be sore. And you (turning towards roC)… well I guess it doesn’t matter, I left all me valuables at home. Tony Rolo: Do you think I give a rat’s ass where you sleep? You don’t have to sleep in the tent with us… sleep out here on the fucking ground. Thug roC: Yeah, maybe we’ll get lucky and a bear will come through during the middle of the night. Ian English: Now 'ang on, on second thoughts lets not be hasty; I better sleep in the tent too, roC, to make sure they don’t take advantage of youse. To this point, Joey Troy has stayed out of the conversation. Tony notices and looks towards him, and finds him thumbing through the papers that were left on each of their chairs. Tony Rolo: What’s that? Joey Troy: Oh fuck this shit! PRIME’s board of directors has left us instructions for the evening… this is like an itinerary. Thug roC: Let me see that shit. roC grabs the packet from Joey and starts skimming through it. Ian English: (mumbling to himself) Well fack me, I didn’t reckon he could read. Thug roC: This is… gay. Tony Rolo: What’s it say? Joey Troy: They want us to do a bunch of touchy/feely horse-crap to help unite us as a team. I didn’t sign up for any bullshit self-help group shit… we’re not a bunch of friggin pansies. Well, Ian is. Ian English: Oi, I ain't the one that stick wif Bony Tolo like a fly to shite. (Ian points over at Tony). Tony Rolo: How long was your Global Title reign? Ian English: I bloody beat you fer the title, you spaghetti-breathed wop bastid. Tony Rolo: Who’s the champ right now… huh? Thug roC: Jesus Christ! Is this all the three of you are gonna fucking do? Bicker the whole damn time. You white people are like fucking children. Either beat the shit out of each other or shut the fuck up because I’m getting a headache already. Tony Rolo: He’s right, guys. Joey Troy: Yeah, probably. Alright, Ian… let’s go. Ian English: Wot!? Joey Troy: Let me beat the shit outta you now and we can shut up after that. Ian English: You think I’m gonna fall fer that? You just wanna get me sweaty and roll around in the dirt so youse can sodomize me arse. Tony Rolo: I didn’t mean we should fight… I mean we should just all shut the fuck up. Everyone unpacked their shit or whatever… we’ll set up the grille and chill out for a while. Ian English: I tell you wot Troy yer bloody lucky… I'd 'ave paralyzed you wif an uber-flick of me little finger. Joey Troy: You would have paralyzed us with your uber-breath is more like it, moss-jaws. Joey, with his back turned to Ian at this point is hit in the back of the head with a stick the Limey grabbed from the ground. Joey Troy: Ah! You motherfucker! Joey charges at Ian. English uses all his ring experience from PRIME to come up with a cunning startegy...he runs off howling like a taunting gibbon. Ian English: AAAHHHHH!!! ***** A half an hour later… They’re all sitting around the fire. Ian has a black eye while Joey has scratches on his face and mud around his eyes, obviously attacked by one of Ian's dirty tricks as if then mauled like a rabid animal. Tony Rolo: Okay, maybe we should at least try some of these bonding techniques they want us to do. Thug roC: What do the suits want us to do? Tony Rolo: Let’s see… Rolo starts fingering through the list of bonding activities. Tony Rolo: This one looks easy enough… or maybe not. Say one nice thing about the person to your right. Rolo looks to his right at Thug roC. Tony Rolo: You… uh… I like your shoes. Thug roC: I think they want something with more substance. Tony Rolo: I like your socks, too. roC shakes his head and looks at Joey to his right. Thug roC: I bet you roll a fat joint. Joey Troy: You can say that shit again… Jerry Garcia ain’t got shit on me. Joey looks to his right at Ian. Joey Troy: You’re ugly. Ian English: Oi, that aint exactly nice, you twat! Joey Troy: Aw fuck it! I can’t do this… let’s smoke this. Out of the pocket in his hooded sweatshirt, Joey pulls out a bag… a bag which contains a fat-ass joint. Joey Troy: They want us to bond, then this is how we’re doing this. Fuck their pussy shit… this is what bonding is about. ***** Fifteen minutes later, after much convincing to get Rolo to join in on the festivities… The PRIME superstars are sitting in a circle silent. Ian English: I don’t feel anything. You sure you didn’t buy oregano from some scamming hoodlum like roC? Joey Troy: Nah, this is the real shit… grade A quality Kentucky Blue Grass. We may not feel anything yet, but it’s gonna hit you like a ton of bricks. Thug roC: Kentucky Blue Grass, huh… I knew you had good taste. Ian English: I still think it was fackin' oregano. Joey notices Tony staring off in the distance. Joey Troy: Hey Tone… Tone… you okay? He doesn’t break his stare… doesn’t even acknowledge his partner, Joey. Ian English: Oi you Mario Brother wannabe shite-face! Ian jumps up and waves his hand in front of Tony’s face. Finally the trance is broken. Tony points off into the distance. Tony Rolo: Do you guys see that? What is that making its way over here? Thug roC: Is that a skunk? Joey Troy: No, I farted… sorry guys. As the little creature gets closer and wanders his way smack dab into the middle of the four chosen ones of PRIME, Ian screams… Ian English: IT’S A FACKIN' WEASEL!!! The word “silence” is spoken forcefully… but not by any of the PRIME wrestlers. Tony Rolo: Did that thing just fucking talk!? Joey Troy: Shit, I told Charlie I didn’t want this shit laced with anything… damn it! Hiding from under his chair… Ian English: Okay, maybe it aint oregano. Weasel: I said SILENCE!!! And on the eighth day, God created a talking weasel. Okay, not really… we’re not sure where the fuck this thing came from. Either way, Rolo, Troy, English, and roC were either so stunned or so scared, that they listened and didn’t say a word… or move a muscle either. The silence is broken by Joey Troy leaning over and whispering to Tony. Joey Troy: You distract him… I’m gonna go get the bug spray from the tent. Tony Rolo: It’s a talking weasel… I don’t think bug spray’s gonna work. Thug roc joins their conversation. Thug roC: Maybe we should try being nice to it. Speaking up towards the animal… Thug roC: Um, you got a name? Weasel: I go by many names… Hidden Moniker… Sharpe… El Zeus-daddy… but you can call me Stu. Joey Troy: A weasel with an identity crisis… what a fucking jackass. Tony Rolo: So Stu… uh… Ian English: KILL IT!!! LETS DO IT NOW!!! Weasel: Do not be frightened. I come in peace. Ian English: Bollocks! That’s wot the aliens always say right before they stick a probe up yer arse! Joey Troy: That explains a lot. Weasel: I come to you as a prophet. Joey Troy: Aw fuck! The weasel’s a Jehovah’s Witness. Listen, pal… Stu, whatever the fuck your name is… I get enough of those bible beaters at my door every week. I’ll tell you the same thing I tell them… blow me! Weasel: I think you are mistaken, Mr. Troy. I come to offer my knowledge to you in your quest… to all of you. Thug roC: Are you trying to tell us you have better weed than Joey brought? Weasel: I am not referring to your quest to get inebriated. I speak of a more meaningful journey… one in the name of honor… one in the name of pride… one in the name of PRIME. Tony Rolo: In the name of loooove, once more in the name of love…. oooOOOOoooo oooOOOOooooooo… oooOOOOoooooooo… early morning, April falls, shot rings out in the empty skyyyy. Joey Troy: Hey Bono, shut the fuck up! Nobody cares that you know the words to every Goddamn U2 song ever written. Weasel: I was told offering my wisdom to you four would be pointless. Ian English: Fack you, Stu! We don’t need no piece of shite weasel telling us… telling us whatever the fack you were gonna tell us. Weasel: I hold insight needed to unite the four of you as one, rise up against the competition, and claim victory at PTC Survival! For that is why you are here right now… are you not? The four sit (well, Ian’s still in the fetal position strategically placed under his chair) contemplating the words spoken by Stu. Luckily, the effects of the Kentucky Blue Grass enable four grown men to weigh the words of an animal in their heads. Tony Rolo: So you’re saying that you hold the key to the interfed pay-per-view. Weasel: That is what I’m telling you. Ian English: Sounds like a load of bollocks to me. Weasel: Bollocks… no. There is an entire company resting on your shoulders. This is your opportunity. The four of you are each special in your own way. Together, you can become an unstoppable force. Thug roC, your toughness derived from years on the street is an invaluable asset. Joey Troy, your handle of The Enigma is as true as ever… use your seemingly effortless abilities to leave your foes in a state of wonder. Ian English, you are the most underhanded individual ever to set foot into the squared circle, always finding the advantage by means of trickery. And you, Tony Rolo, you are a man not measure by physical size or in-ring ability, but by the size of your heart… for your heart and determination is unmatched. Four men so different, but four men who can come together and represent PRIME and forever etch their federation’s name in the history books as one of the greats, to be remembered beyond it’s years… which were the aspirations of Sebastian Starr when he gave birth to this company. At this point, they’re completely mesmerized. They’re hanging on every word spoken… even Ian. Weasel: Your opponents will stand in your way, though. The teams of WMW, LOW, and WWA also are in their own quests for Glory… Stu goes on yapping as only Stu can… ***** Ten minutes later… He’s still yapping, but surprisingly, he’s still got them in a trance, as if he were a witch and the four PRIME stars were under his spell. Weasel: … Yes, those opponents will stand in your way… in your trail to glory. But they are not the biggest obstacle you will face. The absolute key to success is discovering a way for there to be coexistence between the four of you. Balance must be obtained. For the executives in PRIME were on the right track, the means to their end was faulted. You must not unite as one on their terms, but on terms of your own. This must not be forced, but instead nurture itself from within. This is the wisdom I have for you. Inside, each of you know I speak the truth. Act on this truth… become one… and become victorious. POOF Ian English: Oi! Where’d he go? Vanished… in thin air, leaving nothing but a cloud of smoke. Joey Troy: Stu? Thug roC: Joe, you got any extra weed I could buy from you? Tony Rolo: That was strange… but the stranger part is, that fucking weasel was right… about everything. ***** The following morning… Finally, the rain has stopped long enough for the four PRIME combatants to step outside of the tent. Unfortunately, the rain throughout the night brought in a cold front and the ground is all soggy. Tony Rolo: Damn, it’s fucking freezing. Thug roC: No shit. We should try to get that fire restarted. Ian English: Yer not gonna stert no fire. The wood is bloody soaked. Joey Troy: I’ll get it started. Ian English: How? Yer no fackin magician. Only magic youse do is hidin yer tiny wanger in Bony Tolo’s arse. Joey ignores him and goes inside the tent. Ian English: Yer ‘spose to stert the fire out her. Oi! Joey comes walking back out with a Zippo. Tony Rolo: You’re not gonna be able to start that shit with just a fucking Zippo. Out of the pocket in his hooded sweatshirt, Joey pulls a rusty green canister. Thug roC: That shit looks dangerous, bro. Ian English: Wot the ‘ell is in there? Joey Troy: This, my friends, is known as Chernobyl fuel, army issue. You can call it “the shit that’s gonna light our fire.” Tony Rolo: More like “the shit that’s gonna blow us the fuck up.” Thug roC: That wood’s still soaked though… I don’t care what you put on it, it won’t light. Joey reaches into his pocket for more toys. Out come small white cubes Joey Troy: Walla… fire starters. Watch and learn. He lights the cubes and a small flame comes up as he skillfully gets the kindling lit. He wipes his hands off and opens up the cap of the Chernobyl fuel. Tony Rolo: Be careful with that shit… don’t use too much. It looks dangerous. Instead of putting just a small amount on to try at first, Joey turns the bottle upside down, and the fluid comes out like a facet being blasted. Before the three can get out “oh shit,” or as in English’s case, “oh shite,” a huge cloud of fire explodes over the fire pit. The flame instantaneously shoots up into the bottle. Troy: MY HAND’S ON FIRE!!! Troy throws the bottle of “Chernobyl fuel” through the air towards the tents. It lands on its side… the contents continue to pour out onto the ground and with each pour follows more ignition as a straight line of fire ten feet long spurs across the campsite. Ian English: AAHHH!!! A FLAMING HOMOSEXUAL!!! Joey “acting quickly” heads over and attempts to kick the bottle upright. The result is him sending the flaming bottle along with a stream of flames 3 feet from the tent. Tony Rolo: STOP DROP AND ROLL!!! Joey begins to stomp on the fire as it creeps closer towards the tent … suddenly, his boot ignites. Joey Troy: AHHH, MY SHOE IS ON FIRE!!!!! Thug roC: Shouldn’t we do something!? Tony is totally panicking. Thug roC: Hey! Shouldn’t we do something!? roC obviously isn’t too eager to get near the fire. Joey’s still hopping around screaming. Ian English: Fack no!!! I’m enjoying this too much. Finally Tony snaps out of it. He charges Joey and spears him to the ground and begins rolling over and over, trying to smother the fire. Ian English: Look at the faggots!!! He’s trying to arse fack im in the one more time before he burns to death!!! The screaming stops, and so does the fire… at least the one on Joey. Tony Rolo: Joe, you okay!? Joey Troy: Fuck no! I was on fire! Fire fucking burns, damn it! Ian English: Ahhahahahahahaha!!! The scene fades away as Ian continues to laugh like a hyena. Joey starts to stare him down. And while they do not mention the weasel thus far this morning, the phenomenon is close in thought. They will remember the words of Stu, that fucking weasel.
