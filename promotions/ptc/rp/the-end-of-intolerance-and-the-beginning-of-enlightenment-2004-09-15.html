The End of Intolerance and The beginning of Enlightenment!

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 -Six long years I have spent in that hell he created for me. Six long years away from the plane of existence of reality because he couldn’t accept the fact that I had my own dreams and ambitions. At nights I can still hear the howls of the insanity calling out my name. All of this because the intolerant bastard just couldn’t live with a son that had a goal that wasn’t his own. Life is funny that way, how one mans intolerance could affect one little boy’s life so much. I battle many demons on an every day basis, but his words and actions that still burn the very fabric of my soul is a battle I still have yet to win.- Location: Arlington Heights Mortuary, Riverside California Time: September 11, 2004 12:00 AM The scene opens up to a cold, dark and dreary night. The air is still very fresh with the stench of the souls that were once living. Near a lake in the middle of the grounds a lonely toad tries his best to sing with the crickets that are in quite a chatter during this midnight hour. The trees and bushes around the complex are also lifeless like there neighbors in the ground. Through the sacred resting place the attention is pointed to a tombstone on a lonely hill. The hill has one patch of grass by itself away from the others alone in the distance. Clouds start to move in as a man from the shadows starts to move forward. -I am your light!- Inside the man’s heart is so much hurt and pain but it’s hard to even fathom the life he has been through. As he gets closer we notice he is about 7’3’’ and approximately 350 pounds. He has a goatee, long black hair, trench coat, and a pair of sunglasses. Some may say “Why does he need sunglasses at midnight? There is no sun out where he would need them!” Well the light of the sun is not what he is shielding his eyes from but more of the intolerance of life that the world has always showed him since birth. After all who could ever understand the way he wanted to live his life unless you yourself have lived it. Who could ever accept his lifestyle or the values he believes in unless you yourself have walked in his shoes? Born and raised in this eternal damnation that he calls life, he had to achieve the strength inside to escape the intolerance and live his life the way he wants to live it. -I am your salvation!- When he finally reaches the tombstone that reveals ---Here Lies Frank Adams. May wings of Devils carry him to the hell he deserves! --- -I am your savor!- Words he has felt for the man all of his life. The man now known as John Adams has had a personal vendetta against the very memory of the man who once was known as his father. A black as the night crow lands on top of the tombstone squawking as he peers into the very soul of John Adams. -I am your means of survival!- John Adams: So father, how long has it been? How long has it been since you killed my mother? Thought I blocked that out? I can still see the brain fragments that came out of her head after your gunman shot her from behind! Then if that wasn’t enough you had me committed to a mental institution till I was 18! Why Father, why did you do that to your only son? -Take my hand!- The crow that is still on top of the tombstone seems to squawk back at John Adams in response to the answers. A creeping fog rolls in from the lake that reaches knee high. And the background music of sympathy of wolves sends a chill down any normal mans spine. -Accept me into your life!- John Adams: Father, all my life I wanted to be a wrestler! It was my dream, but that just wasn’t good enough for you! All my life I believed that I had a greater destiny, a Prophecy if you will, but you just laughed in my face! All my life I had my beliefs and my dreams, and all you ever did was keeping on persecuting me because I never wanted to be like you! -And you will be saved!- After two hours of cursing his fathers name to the crow and heavens above, he starts to walk back to his bike. Heavy in thought he thinks about all of the words his father ever said to him……………. -The intolerance shall disappear!- {Father you used to ask me what you always wanted to be when I grew up, and I told you a Wrestler! Instead of comforting me and telling me that it was an admirable dream, you told me that my dream was stupid! You told me that a Wrestler did nothing at all to be proud of! And I was a stupid sniveling little bitch for having such a dumb dream!} -And the light from The Prophecy shall cleanse your soul!- {My Mother always supported my dreams, and admirations! And you hated that, you always hated that to the very fabric of your black heart! No father, look at me! Look at your son the Wrestler! So much pain that I have channeled it into something positive in my life! A life I wouldn’t let you take away from me like you did to my Mother, you bastard!} -This friend is the promise I give to you!- ----Flashback---- The flashback opens up to John Adams house where he grew up as a kid. We see a young John Adams there talking with his father who once again is drunk. Frank Adams: So son, I will ask you again! What do you want to be when you grow up? A young John Adams: I told you father, I want to be a wrestler! Frank Adams: Stupid……stupid……stupid! What type of stupid idiotic dream is that? What type of fool do you take me for? You actually think that I will let any son of mine become a Wrestler? You stupid idiot! As long as I have a breath left in my body I will never…NEVER let you become a Wrestler! With fire and passion in his eyes he proceeds to whip a young John Adams with his belt. But this time is different. This time instead of pleading for his safety, John Adams just stares at his father. But the sign of bravery by John Adams only makes it worse for him as his father continues the beating more and more furiously. He didn’t stop until John Adams was a bloody mess. After that incident Frank Adams ended up putting his son in a Mental Institution for six long years. ----Flashback ends.---- John Adams reaches his Harley Davidson with a renewed sense of faith that the way he is living his life today, is the destiny he was dealt in life. But as he reaches for his keys to the bike, a new thought comes bursting into his head……GTT4. -The Promise of The Prophecy!- {The GTT4 is a competition where legends of the Prime Time Central get together for the biggest competition in history! Some of the biggest names in our business will be there, Inphino Blitz, Cyrus Skar, Eric Russo, Noah Hanson, and others! But out of all of them one name sticks out the most Tessa Windsor, Mother of two and one impressive career in RPW! Well Tessa the promise I give to you is one of pain and anguish! It is what I have to offer you, it is the only thing that will set you free and it is The Prophecy!} John Adams gets on his bike and revs it up. He knows what he must do, and how he can accomplish it. All of the doubters and non believers that feel he couldn’t do it, all of them that feel he would never get passed the first round are in for an enlightening experience. For at GTT4 The Prophecy shall be revealed and his time, John “The Prophet” Adams time has come. The only other question is will they be ready. John Adams rides off into the night he has felt at home with and his destination will be his destiny. “To know a person's religion we need not listen to his profession of faith but must find his brand of intolerance.” Eric Hoffer (1902 - 1983) Intolerance itself is a form of egoism, and to condemn egoism intolerantly is to share it. George Santayana (1863 - 1952), Winds of Doctrine (1913) CH.4 -Fin-
