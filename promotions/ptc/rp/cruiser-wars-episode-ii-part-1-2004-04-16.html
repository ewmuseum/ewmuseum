Cruiser Wars - Episode II (Part 1)

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-16 Out Of Character Note - This RP takes place slightly before XUW Reborn, while Saij was still out of action. Otherwise this will not make much sense. Actually, there's a chance it may not make sense anyway. Carry on. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- On the previous episode of "Cruiser Wars..." JR addressed Cockjockey's inability to speak... JR: Wait a minute, JR remembers now! You can't talk, so you use a strange form of sign language that involves flailing your arms! But JR has no idea what the hell your language means! The pressing issue was discussed... JR: The Cockjockey is incapable of speech, and nobody can understand his weird form of sign language! Fatbadger: Dude, Saij can totally understand his language! JR: HE'S IN A COMA!! Fatbadger: Oh... right, that... And a solution may have been found... JR: By God, Cockjockey can communicate with Cardboard Chase!? Fatbadger: I just had an idea! My brain nearly split in half when it happened, but I managed to pull through. Anyway, I know how we can help Cockjockey, and use Chase's assistance as well! And now, we bring you Episode II of "Cruiser Wars..." A short, short time ago, in a galaxy close, close by... As before, the scene opens up in the cold recesses of space. Hundreds of thousands of stars puncture the otherwise black, infinite cloth of the lifeless void. The "Star Wars" theme heralds the arrival of two big, yellow words that pull quickly away from our view, and fade away into space: "CRUISER WARS!" EPISODE II Hot Lesbian Asskicking! The first round of the PTC CRUISERWEIGHT TOURNAMENT has passed. Great evil still looms in the shadows, threatening to overtake and destroy civilization as we know it. Dark times are in store for the galaxy, yet HOPE remains. The fate of mankind rests upon the tireless, unrelenting shoulders of the heroic Midget Wunderkind, THE COCKJOCKEY. Having vanquished the dastardly Jason Starr, the Cockjockey now turns his attention to an even greater villain -- nay, villainess. CORICIDON, a wicked Jezebel indeed, blocks the path to ULTIMATE CRUISERWEIGHT GLORY, as well as peace and harmony throughout the universe... Can the midget hero triumph once again? Or will the lesbian villainess seal mankind's ULTIMATE DOOM for all of eternity? The words slowly scroll off into the darkess of forever, and the scene fades out. We fade back in on the office of XUW Commissioner, Sev. A former XUW superstar, Sev is a very, very large man, standing at seven feet, eleven inches tall and weighing five hundred pounds. Sev also very seldom speaks. When he does, however, he simply mumbles inaudibly. Like this: Sev: ... Anyway, Sev has just been released from the hospital after suffering injuries from a match a few months prior, and is busying himself with some paperwork that piled up in his absence. Enter April O'Neill, Sev's personal assistant. (No, this is not the April O'Neil from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. This is April O'Neill -- completely different.) She is also one of the few people capable of being able to understand and translate everything Sev says. Needless to say, she is a valuable conduit to the goings-on in XUW. Little do either Sev or April suspect that they are about to become instruments in a plan conceived by the man with the lowest IQ in XUW... Fatbadger. A plan that can only have hilarious consequences. April: Sev, did you finalize the contract for the Eric Lockmont/Kata match? Sev: ... April looks a bit sheepishly at Sev. April: I know, Eric... erm, I mean, Lockmont has a long record of cheating in the past, and if he cheats this Sunday then he'll be fired as a part of the match stipulation, but... well, I think he's changed. I believe he has. Sev cocks an eyebrow. Sev: ... April looks indignant. April: HEY! What are you implying!? Sev: ... April: I do not, don't be ridiculous! Sev: ... April: Don't "yeah, whatever" me, bucko! I'll have you know that... But before April has Sev know anything further, there is a loud rapping upon the office door... to the rhythm of "Shave And A Haircut." April: I wonder who that is? Sev: ... April: Yes, come in please! The door flies open, and what was once an orderly, quiet office suddenly transforms into a veritable circus of nonsense. Fatbadger bursts into the room, with the life-sized cardboard cutout of former XUW superstar Chase Wilson tucked under his arm. Right behind Fatbadger is our hero... the man... the myth... the legend... The Midget Wunderkind... THE COCKJOCKEY!!! The pint-sized protagonist sallys forth into the room, riding upon his legendary giant inflatable penis in the same fashion that a child rides open a toy horse. He circles once around the room upon his noble steed, gallantly galloping to the wonderment of all. Well, wonderment for some, befuddlement for others. Anyway, the Cockjockey comes to a stop in front of Sev's desk, who looks curiously at the midget. It is a very strange sight to see Sev and The Cockjockey so close together, as it is certainly something akin to Verne Troyer standing side-by-side with Yao Ming. Sev leans over the front of his desk and speaks. Sev: ... April steps in to translate. April: Erm... Sev congratulates you on your victory in the first round of the Cruiserweight Tournament against Jason Starr... Sev: ... April: ...and he's curious what brings you here to his office. Which really goes without saying... Sev: ...! April: Sorry! No more editorials, I promise! The Cockjockey looks up at Sev with child-like curiosity, and then flails his arms wildly. Sev: ... April remains silent. Fatbadger looks over to her. Fatbadger: Uh... what'd Sev say? April: He didn't say anything that time... he's speechless! Sev stares at the Cockjockey for a moment, then slowly looks to April. Sev: ...? April: I have no idea what he's saying, either. Fatbadger, what's going on? What business do you and the Cockjockey have with Sev? The camera moves over to Fatbadger, who is just staring blankly into space, the futile cogs of his brain slowly turning. Fatbadger's Thoughts: Whoa, wait, did April just ask me a question? I like, totally wasn't paying attention... what'd she ask me? Oh man, I'd better say something fast, or I'm gonna look stupid! But I don't know what the question is... hm, what is it that you're supposed to do when you don't know the answer to a question? Wait, I know! Whenever I took a test in school, I'd always choose letter C if I didn't know the answer! Yeah, that's it! I'll just say... Fatbadger looks at April and smiles. Fatbadger: The answer is C! The fat Wisconsinite folds his arms and grins to himself. Fatbadger's Thoughts: Oh man, I rule. April looks oddly at Fatbadger. April: Uh... that wasn't a multiple choice question. I was asking what you and Cockjockey are doing here. Fatbadger raises his eyebrows, alarmed. Fatbadger: Damn! I hate essay questions! The Cockjockey rolls his eyes, then takes up his giant inflatable penis... and BLUDGEONS Fatbadger over the head! Fatbadger: OOOOOOOOOOOWWW!!! Fatbadger rubs his poor head for a few moments, and then his face lights up! Fatbadger: Wait! I remember now! April, Sev, we need your help! The Cockjockey can only communicate by means of a strange form of sign language, and the only person in the world who could understand him was Saij... April: Hm, and he's in no capacity to translate right now... go on. Fatbadger: Right, so, as it turns out, Cockjockey can communicate with Cardboard Chase here, by means of telepathy! Or something. Fatbadger sets Cardboard Chase Wilson upright. The life-size cardboard cutout, strangely enough, almost seems to be grinning proudly. April looks incredulously at Fatbadger. April: A telepathic link? He's a piece of cardboard!! Fatbadger: Well, duh. Sheeze April, you're about as quick as a frozen turtle. April: But you need a brain to communicate telepathically! This makes no logical sense at all! Fatbadger: Dude, April, logic is sooooo overrated. April screws up her face, almost as if she's trying to maintain her sanity at this point. Fatbadger: Anyway, Cockjockey can communicate with Cardboard Chase. It occured to me that since Cardboard Chase is a pretty silent person, and that since Sev is also a silent person... then perhaps they can communicate with each other in some sorta weird, silent language! And then, Sev can translate whatever Cardboard Chase says into Sev-speak, which you, April, can translate for us into English! April blinks. April: Wait... so what you're saying is, you need Sev and myself to help Cockjockey speak to the world? Fatbadger: Yeah! Cockjockey will speak to Cardboard Chase, Cardboard Chase will speak to Sev, Sev will speak to you, and you'll tell us what was said! I totally saw this on "I Love Lucy" one time, so it can't possibly fail! April: But that involved actual languages... Fatbadger: Sheesh April, quit trying to use reason! There's no time for that! Will you help us or not? April, still very, very confused, looks over to Sev. Sev just shrugs. Sev: ... April: He says it couldn't hurt to try... Fatbadger: Great! Alright, let's get lined up! Cockjockey walks over to Cardboard Chase, and stands to his left. Sev gets up from his desk, and joins Cardboard Chase on his right. April stands to the right of Sev, ready to receive whatever messages make their way down the most peculiar pipeline. Fatbadger stands off to the side to survey the action. Fatbadger: Alright, go! The Cockjockey turns to Cardboard Chase, and flails his arms wildly. Strangely enough, Cardboard Chase's eyes seem to shift from the Cockjockey over to Sev. The big man stares silently at the inanimate cut-out for a few moments, then turns to April. Sev: ... April turns to Fatbadger. April: "Purple monkey dishwasher?" The Cockjockey glares at April, and frantically flails his arms, almost enraged at what she said. Fatbadger: Uh... somehow I don't think that's what Cockjockey said... maybe you got a wire crossed or something. Try again! The Cockjockey sighs, and then flails his arms a bit more slowly, like a parent speaking to a child who's having a hard time understanding. Sev and Cardboard Chase stare silently at each other again, and then Sev speaks to April. Sev: ... April: "April is a stupid ugly bit--" HEY!!! The Cockjockey jumps up and down with glee, clapping his little midget hands. He gives Fatbadger a thumbs up. Fatbadger: Sweet, it totally worked! Alright little dude, now you can talk about your opponent and stuff! The Cockjockey nods, excited that he has a means of communicating with the world once again... unorthodox and logically impossible, yes, but a means nonetheless. He flails his arms to Cardboard Chase for what seems like a good minute. Cardboard Chase and Sev stare at each other for what seems like three minutes. Sev then speaks to April. Sev: ... April turns to Fatbadger. April: "Greetings, world. As you no doubt have already guessed, I am the prolific Cockjockey, the smallest entrant in the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament. True, I am... merely, as a few droll fellows might put it... a midget. However, do not judge my ability by my miniscule stature. Just because I stand at one vertical yard, give or take a few inches, that does not mean I cannot compete in the ring. My opponent, Coricidon, seems to think I do not belong in this tournament. It would appear that she thinks a midget is not worthy, nor capable, of being a serious competitor. But did not people once say something similar about women? Was there not a time, and is there not still that time for some, when women were not taken seriously as wrestlers? Why was this? Because on the average they were smaller than their male counterparts, and viewed as much, much weaker. And now, Coricidon... a woman who has the privilege of wrestling in a day and age when female wrestlers are becoming more commonplace, and are proving to the world that they can compete with the toughest males in wrestling today... is showing prejudice and ignorance by outright jumping to the conclusion that I cannot be a serious wrestler, simply for the fact that I am a midget. How sad is the irony of this? That someone who had to fight to earn respect is now disrespecting a fellow competitor for the very same reasons that women weren't taken seriously? Shame on you, Coricidon, for displaying the same narrow-mindedness that you women have struggled so long to overcome. I shall show you the error of your ways, by defeating you in the second round of the Cruiserweight Tournament... and it shall be one small step... nay, one giant leap forward, for all of midget-kind! To quote Quail Man, from the animated television series Doug, 'I may not be big... but I am small.' And I shall prove to you, Coricidon... that size doesn't matter." The room remains quiet for a moment. Everyone is astonished at the stirring words of the Cockjockey. Then, Fatbadger breaks the silence. Fatbadger: Dude, I thought you were gonna talk trash! The Cockjockey looks oddly at Fatbadger, then just rolls his eyes, and flails his right arm once. Cardboard Chase and Sev stare at each other. Sev speaks to April. Sev: ... April: "Coricidon, I will beat you because I'm better than you, and you suck." Fatbadger: That's more like it! Fatbadger grins like an idiot, as Cockjockey just shakes his head sadly. His message delivered, and his mission complete, we do not need to hear from the Cockjockey any further. On that note, the scene fades to black.
