Sirius......Get the Zanex

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A young boy sits on the floor. He clutches a worn teddy bear. His blue eyes a swollen with tears. All around him, fire rages. He doesn’t know what’s going on. Where’s mommy? Where’s daddy? A baby is crying. There’s yelling all around him, but it’s drowned out by the sizzle and roar of the flames. He’s so confused. It’s not supposed to be this way. They were so happy. But this isn’t happy. This is wrong. The smoke is heavy. He begins to cough. The yelling has stopped. He can’t hear them anymore. They’re gone. He lays his head to the ground and clutches his teddy bear. Sleep....it’s safe there. Mommy told me the monsters can’t go there. Maybe the fire monster will stay away too. He can’t breath. Maybe sleep will make the smoke monster go away too. Why? Why him? They were so happy. He feels himself drift into sleep. He feels like someone’s carrying him away. Mommy was right. Mommy’s so smart.... Mommy? ________________________________ We begin again in flame. The blue dances with the orange, swirling, twirling. It’s poetic in it’s deadly life. But this flame is giving...not taking. DDT : People just mess it up, and you wish you’d just done it yourself in the first place. The flame, under a saute pan. Next to the pan...a beef filet. DDT : I don’t eat out much. In fact, I don’t eat out at all. You realize what they do to your food? Exactly...you don’t. DDT places the filet in the hot pan. A loud hiss and sizzle escape it, causing oil to spatter over DDT’s hand. He doesn’t flinch. DDT : It’s not that I’m afraid, it’s that I don’t need someone else to do it for me. I know how to cook. I know how to take care of myself. Why bother paying someone to do it for me? He flips the beef. The pan lets out another loud hiss as the fresh side hits the hot fat. DDT adds some chopped shallots to the pan. DDT : I learned a long time ago, no matter who you think is there for you, they can be gone in a heartbeat. Another turn of the steak. Sliced peppers and garlic to the pan. DDT : Your friends, your family. They all either die or leave. It’s the way of things. A splash of red wine. An iridescent flame dances in and over the pan. DDT : They are there. DDT blows out the flame. DDT : And like that....gone. DDT adds a brown liquid to the pan. He removes the filet, and swirls the pan. He puts the filet on a plate, and pours the reddish brown sauce over it. He smells it. DDT : Mmm.... Much like this steak, they can be gone in moments. But soon, it will be forgotten. That too, is the way of things. DDT sits down at the table. He cuts into the steak. Bloody juice mingles with the sauce. He brings a piece of the rare beef to his lips, and takes a bite. He chews it slowly, savoring each flavor, each texture, and swallows it down. DDT : I can’t stand the “poor me” people. The people who think life owes them something, just because shit didn’t all go like in the stories their nannies read them as children. It makes me sick. DDT rips off another chunk of the bloody meat, and chomps it down. DDT : I’ve always wanted to give some of those people a smack. So it seems I will. Before DDT can take another bite, a voice from behind him rings out. Sirius : Is that so? DDT smiles. DDT : I’ve been waiting for you old man. A old man he is. Sirius Smythe, the patriarch of the Army of Darkness. He shuffles into the room, dressed in a black suit, wearing a black derby. His wrinkled face gives the impression that even in he had the reason to smile, he’d be hard pressed to. He looks at DDT, and shakes his head. Sirius : Just like usual, running your mouth or stuffing it, not doing anything useful. DDT : And what would you have me do Sirius? Starve? Sirius : Bah! I’d have you do less of the talking and more of the doing. DDT : You know as well as I do.. everything has it’s reasons. Sirius : Such as this so called “extreme” match you’ve entered? DDT : Of course. I nice little warm up I think. Sirius : It would be nice if you would have thought about a “warm up” before the Deadly Draw match you and Overkill lost. DDT : That match was doomed from the onset. Unlike last year, the rookie I had this year not only had a chip on his shoulder, but a stick up his ass. I swear, I hear that kid say “you don’t know what pain is” one more fucking time, I’ll shoot myself. Sirius : A bit whiny that one. DDT : All of them.....including their newest member. Silence for a minute after the mention of the newest member. Pete. He was once someone welcome in this place. He’s be welcome to sit at this table. But like the steak DDT takes a final bite of...he’s gone. Sirius : He’s just where he belongs it seems. DDT : Yes....drowning in his own self loathing, along with the other cry babies. That why I entered this thing Sirius. I’ve been watching this group for some time. I’ve heard them all talk about this Extreme Title, and the prestige it carries. Sirius : Seems like a bunch of prima donnas with nothing better to do. DDT : Not all of them...but enough of ‘em. Sirius : Hmph.....seems like a waste of energy. DDT : Same old Sirius. Sirius : I have work to do....plans to finalize. DDT nods, and off shuffles the old lawyer. DDT : Everyone goes away you see, but it’s how it’s handled that makes us who we are. I look at so many of you here in the ETD. You want to call yourself extreme, you really do. Then you all say and do things that prove opposite. Guns? What’s so fucking extreme about a blood coated shotgun? Putting a revolver to another mans head while he’s tied to a chair? Ooh...real extreme there pal. It called gutless where I’m from. An easy fix to life’s problems. Pull a trigger, and it’s over. That’s your philosophy, right? Any legal issues, you toss old money at it. That’s extreme? Pfft... DDT gets up and tosses his plate and the pan in the sink. DDT : If you haven’t figured it out yet....I hate whiney rich kids. I hate people who have everything, yet cry about how they have nothing. I knew once upon a time what having nothing was....... DDT stops moving for a minute. DDT : I had the happy life. Mom and Dad who loved me, a brother and sister who were my best friends, little home with the white picket fence. It was perfect. WE were perfect. Then the fire. Siblings died in the smoke...Dad died from 3rd degree burns later that day.....Mom from a great many things days later. But not me. Not a scratch...not a burn. It’s why they said it had to have been me. He started it. Found him with matches they said. I don’t think I knew what a match was yet....I was too young. But they said it was me. I didn’t talk for a long time after that. Seeing your family die can fuck you up! So they sent me away. ME! THEY BLAMED ME! AND WHAT HAPPENED?!!? THAT DAMN DOCTOR TRIED TO HELP ME. HELP ME! OH,.HE HELPED ME. HELPED ME BECOME A FUCKING PSYCHO!! SHOCK TREATMENTS FOR A LITTLE BOY!!!! A LITTLE BOY!!!! WHAT THE FUCK!??! DDT’s face is red. His breathing heavy. He takes a deep breath......and rubs his hand across his face. DDT : But I got over it. DDT walks out of the kitchen, and through a series of dimly lit hallways. He turns, and walks down a staircase, coming to a large black door. He walks into an empty gray room. The walls, the floor, all the same drab concrete color, with a lone dark doorway. You cant see what’s on the other side. DDT stands in a corner of the room, almost hiding in the shadows. DDT : The match is soon.... The doorway lights up. An eerie blue light begins to glow from within the room, and s light shadows begins to dance on the walls. A whispery laughter emanates through the entire area. Voice : Good.....very good. We barely see DDT now. He’s slid down into the corner to converse with HIM. He saved his life, but now it seems, DDT has come in servitude of him for it. HE says it’s for DDT’s best interest. HE says it’s all for rebuilding the AoD, and getting DDT back to what he once was....an Icon....a Hardcore Icon. DDT knows that....but still feels something is....wrong. Voice : DDT does well......DDT does very well. DDT fight in a match with many...many who want to hurt DDT.....want to maim DDT.....and they don’t even know DDT. This make DDT ready.....ready for the Skeletons.........ready for Overkill. These men you fight.......when it’s all over.....they go their own way......they looks for a title.........not for DDT. Skeletons look for DDT.........they want to end DDT for themselves.......because they fear DDT.........they fear AoD and DDT together. This match.......this for fun DDT.....this is what DDT finds fun no? Silence. The shadows cease their danceand the light fades out, leaving us in darkness again. Darkness and silence. Then DDT begins to laugh......
