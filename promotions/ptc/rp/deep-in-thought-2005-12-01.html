Deep in thought…

1 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-01 If you're looking for a bunch of flowery prose describing the metaphysical state of the mind of the man known as Maggot, if you want a deep insight into the thoughts and feelings of the former MBE World Heavyweight Championship, if you want to get in touch with The One Man Mafia's inner child in an attempt to figure out his neuroses, well, you ain't gonna find it here. What are you going to find here? Well, you're going to find darkness, dankness, dampness. You'll find a quite large man with scars all over his body. You'll find… solitude. Silence. You'll find a man with a one track mind, thinking the same thoughts over, and over, and over again. "Catch the vampire. Grab the vampire. Break the vampire's freakin' skull into a fine paste. Repeat with other bones in vampire's body." If you're looking for deep, Freudian thought, well, then you're looking in the wrong place. Not much else is passes through his head. Water drips down on his head from the overhead pipes. He doesn't care. A rat scurries over and nibbles on his boot. Not even a twitch. I bet you could probably sneak up behind him and draw a penis on his back, and he probably wouldn't flinch. But tell him that he won't be able to smash the vampire into itty bitty pieces, well, then you'll probably raise his ire just a little bit. That is the only thing running through his mind. Well, not the only thing. There are some other thoughts. NFW business, representing NFW and MBE, bringing the MBE name and legacy to another corner of the wrestling world. The prospect of facing Angelo Deville two rounds after this one, barring losses by him to Rich Rollins, Über Judge or Paddy O'Shea or an unfortunate bracket reshuffling. Maggot/Deville, the match that was supposed to happen week four of the NFW season, but was put on the kibosh because of Uriel. Uriel. Why couldn't he be in this tournament? But he doesn't get too far off track. Uriel, Deville, Miles, O'Shea, Rollins, everyone else took a backseat to Ambrosi. Virgil Ambrosi. The Vampire. The Global Platinum Champion. The proven quantity in PTC circles. And to many, the odds-on favorite to win this match. Hell, nearly everyone who picked on that fancy Internet board said so. But the role of the underdog is one that the One Man Mafia relishes. No one thought he could amount to a hill of beans in MBE, but he left his mark as one of its greatest Champions. No one paid him much mind in NFW, but now, he's one of its rising stars. And now, it was time for him to make himself known to the denizens of the PTC world. By destroying the vampire. Not with garlic. Not with holy water. Not with a stake through the heart. But with cold-hearted, old-fashioned aggression and violence. That thought puts a smile on Maggot's face as he leans back against the cold brick wall. He's ready now. Ambrosi is toast. And that's that.
