Just Another Night: That's Not Good.

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-14 Like water cascading off the edge of a waterfall, the running shower soothes our sense of sound as the scene springs to life. Jayce Bradley stood there as stiff as a board, his head tilted back to allow the near-scalding water to splash effortlessly across his face; washing away the copper scented crimson flow that slowly trickled from the open wound on his brow. Jayce had been in a grueling match against Sean Galen, more difficult than Jayce had expected. For everything that Jayce attempted to do to gain the upper hand, Galen knew a counter that ended Jayce’s attempt as quickly as Jayce had thought of it. It had become a bit of a struggle for Jayce, his lack of experience in situations like this getting the better of him, along with his quick temper. The anger and rage that had built up within Jayce had driven him insane, but he tried desperately to keep it contained; which went as well as trying to contain an oil spill in the ocean. A stray elbow from Galen had connected with the edge of Jayce’s eyebrow, splitting the tender patch of skin and causing him to gush more than Cytherea. That was all it took for Jayce to lose control, and though it nearly cost him the match, Jayce now believed that it was what started his slow and steady gain of momentum. Which eventually lead to Jayce coming out victorious in the match, successful for at least this round. But what awaited Jayce in the second round, would the competition be as difficult as the round before? Could Jayce end up with the unfortunate luck to draw yet another bonafide legend of the sport? The rapid bursts streaming out from the showerhead held none of those answers, but at that moment the sensation was enough for Jayce to forget all about what was going on. Soothing as a gentle massage, the water lifted Jayce away from his problems, away from everything that kept racing through his mind, for at least a moment. It was not long, but it was enough. To Jayce, it felt as if a giant weight had been lifted from his shoulders, and yet it was nothing more than a warm shower. Much like any other he had taken before, yet very different at the same time. The crimson tide that he was washing away belonged to him for a change, and not some poor hapless sap that had the displeasure to cross paths with himself and his mentor Russell Braun. Like a deer in the headlights of a fast approaching vehicle, their lives were pretty much left in the hands of Braun and Bradley. And more often than not, that usually resulted in death. That’s putting it mildly too, as death is not something that happens quickly in the hands of Braun and Bradley. It is more of a slow and agonizing death, tortured to the point where their hapless victim would scream out and beg for mercy…beg to have the pain end once and for all. If they had a tongue, of course. That was always one of the first senses they robbed of their victims, making it impossible to scream. With the precision of a surgeon, Braun would then watch the fear in their eyes as they heard the cordless drill puncture their flesh without severing any major arteries. It was a fun game in his mind, listening to the muffled screams as snot merged with the salty flavored tears that streamed down their faces. He gained a sickening pleasure from the suffering of others, because in his eyes that was what they were there for. For his own sick and demented pleasure. For the most part, Jayce Bradley shared the same feelings as Braun. He felt that those that were un-pure, deserved to fester in their own excretions, watching life slowly fade away like the sands of time through an hourglass. However, unlike Braun, Jayce enjoyed the thrill of the hunt more than anything else. The sensation sent shivers down his spine, as if he was standing in a cold winter’s night without a shirt on to keep him warm. Like a tiger stalking his prey, Jayce would sit in the driver’s seat of the old beat up Caprice for hours; awaiting the right opportunity to stalk up from behind and snatch his victim. He found himself smiling constantly, as he plunged the unsanitary needle into someone’s neck. Watching wide-eyed as the clear fluid that filled the syringe was slowly injected and mixed with the blood coursing through their arteries; instantaneously impeding the ability to control the nervous system. The feeling left a lasting impression, something that you cannot just toss away like last week’s garbage and forget about. Yet, there was something about the feeling he had when he was in the ring with Sean Galen that he could not erase from his subconscious. Something that he was not sure he wanted to forget, as it was something new. Success. Sure, Jayce had been a bit successful during his run in Global Championship Wrestling, but somehow this was entirely different. Wrestling in GCW was great, and overcoming Varsity to win the Cruiserweight title was even better than that, but at the time it felt as if it was just a job. Something he did to have an alibi for his whereabouts. It wasn’t where his heart was, if you could call that dark lifeless muscle that beats within his chest a heart. Until now. That victory over Sean Galen breathed new life into Jayce, injecting a burst of adrenaline into his veins. He started to think about what would happen if he advanced even further, moved on past the second round and maybe even past the third. With the amount of talent in the tournament bracket he was in, he knew it was going to be something difficult to accomplish. But if it could be done, if he could find some way to overcome the odds and advance to the bracket finals; maybe even facing off against fellow GCW alumni Rich Rollins, wouldn’t that account for something. Would that be enough to change people’s opinion of him, maybe even garner him a bit of respect for his in-ring ability. For a moment there, it was as if he actually cared for something again. That was when the phone rang. The polyphonic sound of Rammstein’s "Du Hast" was enough to snap Jayce out of his trance. He fumbled around with the knobs to turn off the water, before he cast the shower curtain aside and grabbed a towel from the rack mounted to the wall perpendicular to the shower stall. The sound of wet feet slapping across the cold tile of the motel room’s bathroom was rhythmic, as he sprinted toward the kitchen where he had left his cell phone; leaving a damp trail of moisture behind in case he got lost. Grabbing the cell phone, Jayce flipped it open and pulled it to his ear. "Hello." He stood there, one hand holding the cell phone to his ear while the other clutched the towel around his waist, as water down his inner thigh and onto the hardwood floor of the kitchen; forming a bit of a puddle around his feet. It was a nice day out, the rain had stopped for a change, allowing the sun to pop it’s head out from behind the gray muted clouds long enough to shine down from the Heavens. Detective Marcus Pride strolled along the waterfront, his black leather jacket draped over his shoulder; exposing the twin nine millimeters that were holstered above his hips. With a lit cigarette dangling loosely from his mouth, Pride allowed a cloud of smoke to seep through the slight separation of his lips. "How’s it going, detective?" Pride turned to find the medical examiner; Dr. Gregory Schwann knelt down before the body that had washed up on shore sometime before dawn. "Not particularly excited about this mess, to tell you the truth," His only reply as he made his way over to where the body and the doctor happened to be. Grabbing the cigarette between his forefinger and thumb, he flicked it into the ocean. "You know you just killed a dolphin, right?" Pride laughed it off, "Serves the bastard right for picking up such a nasty habit. So what do we got here, doc?" "Honestly," stumbling to find the right words to describe the situation. "I’m not sure what to make of it." Pride’s smile soon fades away. "There are puncture wounds of varying sizes, the teeth seem to have been drilled out one by one, by the grind marks left on what is left of them, and…" Interrupting, Pride leans in closer. "There’s more?" "The skin has been scalped, for lack of a better word." Pride gets an actual view of the body now, if it could be called that. In biology, there was this one page that showed the inner workings of the muscular system; how they connected and worked to help you do various things in life like walk, jump, and type. Well, that picture is what Marcus Pride just happens to be looking at. The muscles and tendons were the only thing holding this carcass together. Turning to his left, placing one hand on the edge of a jagged rock as he leans forward and lowers his head, Pride discovers a unique way to show us what he had for breakfast. Pride had never seen anything like it before, and it just wasn’t the fact that the body had no skin. The way the body had been mutilated beyond recognition just made him sick to the stomach. A failed science experiment, the one where you open up a frog and take out certain pieces of its internal organs, is what the scene looks like. "Where’s the skin," Pride managed to ask as he used the sleeve of his shirt to wipe away the moisture on his lips. "No sign of it yet," came the response. "Then I guess a wallet is out of the question as well?" "Pretty much." "Any idea what the cause of death was," Pride’s face was pale, and losing more of it’s color by the moment. With a slight grin and a muffled chuckle, Schwann turned his attention to Pride. "I don’t know…blood loss, maybe. The poor guy was tortured for crying out loud." "It was just a question." "A stupid one, too." Pride attempted to smirk, but the sensation of his own bile rising up within his throat caused him to jerk slightly - a gag reflex. "Let me know what you find out," he managed to mumble as he swallowed to keep it down. "Easy for you to say," Schwann responded. "You don’t have to fumble through all of this mess." "Yeah, but that’s why you get paid the big bucks." "Yeah, I wish." Detective Pride draped the leather coat over his shoulders, before placing his arms through the sleeves one at a time and pulling a pack of cigarettes from his inside coat pocket. Slapping the hard pack of Marlboro Red 100’s against the back of his palm, before slipping the single cancer stick -- that had popped out like a jack in the box -- between his lips. "Hey Pride," Schwann yelled out to grab his attention. "You may want to take a look at this." Detective Pride turned heel to toe, to find Schwann holding a small chunk of razor sharp stainless steel between his latex gloved fingers. "I think it may be a piece of the murder weapon," Schwann corrected himself, knowing that he could very well be wrong. "Or at least one of the murder weapons." That's not good, at all.
