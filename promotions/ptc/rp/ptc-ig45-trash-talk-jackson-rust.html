IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

31 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
31 May 2008 Hi, Christopher. Phew. I gotta tell you, buddy: it was such a relief to hear you again. Your poorly crafted insults and even poorer grammar were like big welcome mats. (Bee-tee-dub, how are the Berlitz classes going?) Made me nostalgic and teary-eyed, really – oh, not because you burned me with the “haha, you must be fat now” insult (trust me, you didn’t), but because it feels like I never left. Things don’t change, huh? Years later and still you’re baiting me. It’s flattering, kinda. And yeah, man. I do love bragging bout how you’ve never beaten me. Three wins to your goose egg. And look at you, being all modest. “PCW or whatever that place was called.” Come on, Chrissy. We all know it grates on you. How I’m such an inferior wrestler and yet you’ve never been able to get that win over me. I dunno why. Tons of people have. I mean, you tried it as a Schmoopie. (Have you been able to live that gimmick down yet? Was just curious.) Then you tried it as the overeager suplexing workhorse. That didn’t fly either. (Have you been able to live that gimmick down either? Was just curious.) And here you’re trying it again. Things don’t change, bud. Whether I used to be a wrestler of loftiest prestige or of most abject failure, that doesn’t make a difference. If I stayed active those years I was gone . . . that doesn’t matter, either. Fact is, I’d still beat you. That’s the gospel, plain and simple there. I would beat you still. So go on and call yourself a king. You king of the jungle, you. Guess that makes me the thorn in your paw, huh? When I’m gone you can roam free and mighty (oh, I’m not really applying that word to you), but when I am there, you’re completely disabled, miserable, looking for help and finding none. So congratulations on winning a round of the Gauntlet. And for holding a tag belt in a division more barren than the Sahara. It’s a tremendous accomplishment, truly, and Tessa must beam with pride when she tucks you in at night. ---- 31 May 2008 Hate to break it to you there, Gabby, but Time flew by your little schtick around 2005. So. Yeah. That little "i r future!" gig you got going on . . . I'd work on it, if I were you. I don't wanna sound like I'm defending Our Friend Christopher -- I'm not -- but we both lived through the Dark Gimmick trend. It kind of expired for a reason. Go on, though, should you feel compelled to spread fear unto those on whose radar you're not yet even a mere blip. (Like the cryptic-sounding language? Man, I do. Brings back memories.) I, for one, have popcorn. But then again, I do less-than-three bad flicks. ---- 1 June 2008 If you're here to impress Gabriel Kael, please raise your hand. That's settled. For serious, who writes your shit? It's natural as the San Fernando Valley. I don't see how you can stand there and actually read it off. Totally amateurish, you know? How it sounds, I mean. I've had wedgies more comfortable than your little rants. And your trial-and-error test with humor! My God, man. "But I digress, I will not waste any more breath on the fools rapidly falling into the distance in my rearview mirror." Really? Who speaks like that? This is not books-on-tape. Stop it. So, no one can back up big words like you can? Cool. Cool. What have you done? Exactly? Hmm? I mean, maybe you did something. I couldn't tell you. I don't think you could either, really. I'd hate to use the n-word, but you sound like a goddamn noob. The von Doom guy said that nobody gives a fuck about you anymore. Anymore. I'd cut that word in the final draft. We're here to honor Chris Storm by dragging his name through the mud once more. What the hell are you doing? Insulting Tessa? Anyone with the word whore in their vocabulary can do that -- and have been doing that for years. It's been unfunny for just as long. Put a twist on it. Call Storm a knobslobber. He's used to it. It won't hurt his feelings. You are wasting everyone's time. Go back to your thicket of solitude or wherever it is the dark, brooding type hang out these days. Ponder. Genuflect. Brood some more. Think of something that sounds intimidating (when you're fourteen). Then don't say it. Keep the stupid out of your mouth. End. Oh: Chris Storm's gay. Tessa's his beard. To keep the fire stoked, you know? ---- 1 June 2008 Do you think you could go over everything you've already said, please? I love hearing the same bullshit the fourth time around. It's like microwaved steak. You're just chewing on this rubbery garbage and it isn't good. You'll beat Chris Storm. No one stands a chance against you. No one will forget the name Gabriel Kael. Greatest wrestler ever. No one could take you on their worst day. Do you think you hold any credibility here? You haven't done a fucking thing, man. You're in EPW? Wrestling's starter kit? Come on. That alone should eliminate you from all contention. Me, though? I'm Jackson Rust. I ran this place. I headlined the most competitive fed in what used to be PTC. And I know. I know. "Yeah, well, that was, like, the past, man. And Time whispered in your ear that your run was over. I will break you." I said it before: Things don't change. The truth of the matter is that I've actually proven myself, bud. I've done more than talk. I've done more than you'll ever do. I could come back and go to the top of any respectable fed within an arc -- in EPW, probably two, three days. And everyone who knows me knows it. But tell me again that Chris Storm could beat me with both hands tied behind his back. It gave me the chuckles. Do you know your history, man? I've beaten him three times straight. He's yet to get the W on me. In fact, it seemed like he was fighting me with both hands tied behind his back. It was like cow-tipping. (Hi, Chris!) So yeah, anyway. It's fine that you're sitting here, waiting for your next chance to snap back at others that have proven themselves as mainstays -- yes, even James von Doom and his legendary IG record; what is it now? 0-40? -- and I'm perfectly fine slapping you back down. I mean, you just leave openings everywhere. I quote you: "Do you honestly think that is possible [to forget me]?" Well. Yeah. I think it'll be one of the least challenging feats I've undertaken, actually. (That was too easy and I don't feel as good about it as I thought I might. Like playing I-Spy with the blind.) Bee-tee-dub. Do you have some sort of obsessive mancrush on Storm? Cuz it seems like you do. "You want to face me. You need to face me. I so desperately want validation. God I'm hard!" You mouthbreathing stalker, you. Everyone's heard everything you have to say now long ago. And maybe when everyone here goes away for good in a few years -- provided that a new flux of talent doesn't roll in -- you might be the best guy here. You'll see your name everywhere. Lights, cameras, clichés. And it will be swell in a it's-really-not sort of way. But until then you'll have to take the backseat, if I can build upon your horrendous car metaphor. ---- 6 June 2008 If there's anything here getting accomplished, it's hitting all the demographic angles. Women, moms, lesbians, the very-well-might-be-retarded, the druggies, the incontinent (hi, Jamester), and, most impressively, the exclusive group in which 4WW was regarded as a legitimate name in the wrestling biz. All we need is a black guy. But pretty much I haven't missed much else than that, I see. Von Doom's still making a valiant but ill-advised and -fated attempt. I'm not sure how to take you, honestly. I don't know if it's low self-esteem on your part, or maybe it's a comically warped public perception of James von Doom, but when you say losing to you will be a nightmare it doesn't come across as intimidating. See, most times when you hear a guy say something akin to that, it's supposed to put doubt in our minds, fear in our hearts. What you've got me doing is thinking, My God. What if I do fall up the ringsteps? It's just pathetically funny, your brand of dickwaving. But it's on par with Darek DeAngleo's. For serious. "Dark Angel"? That might be worse than Gabriel Kael's routine. I was going to casually dismiss you, but then I just kind of wanted to review with you something that dribbled out your mouth. Said something about taking the Ramone girl to school, teach her what she needs to have a chance in the PTC. Yeah? Yeah. Kay. Uh. No? You've actually had to have accomplished something here before you can do that, you silly goose. Oh, and hi, Tessa. Long time and other clichés. How's it? ---- 6 June 2008 I think you've stretched what little quality you had to begin with a little too thin here, Jim. It's like you're a fucking child, man. How many times have you made the same lame joke or insult now? Implying we're all gay and whores will only get you so far, and I can assure you it won't get you to the Infinite Gauntlet. You'll run out of gas far before that. Do you think the person who talks the most is going to win? I mean, if that were true -- it isn't, bee-tee-dub -- wouldn't your Gauntlet misfortunes have been reversed a long time ago? We've all got our Waterloo, James. Mine? I can't throw a spiral. Shameful, yes. Yours? Wrestling. And I never tried a career in football. You, on the other hand. ---- 6 June 2008 ~sigh~ Jesus Christ.
