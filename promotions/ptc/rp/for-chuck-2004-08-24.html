For Chuck

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 Inside the old shack of a bar is nothing but full on, utter, and pointless chaos. The bar is like any other bar on a Saturday Night. Loud, drunk, and violent. The customers yell and shout and scream, throwing around beer bottle and pool sticks. However, among the pandemonium... ...lays one man. On the bar, in his usual seat, is a black haired man with his navy blue cloak. It looks like it came straight out of midevil England, but of course he doesn't seem to give a damn. The old bartender moves towards him as he absently mindedly scrubs a shot glass. Chuck: Alright, Scotty, lets here the excuse this time. Tami show up? A little kid kick you again? Hm? What bad excuse you got for getting drunk the night before a match this time? The man known as "Matrix" to the wrestling world, but "Scotty" to the few people that really know his name, looks up into the misty blue eyes of the old man. He then gives a serious look, and the moment seems like it could be emotional. Chuck begins to realize that Matrix may actually be hurt, and that Trix could really have some sorrow in his life. ...At least till he opened his mouth. Matrix: Shut the fuck up, Chuck, and get me another damn beer. The bartender scoffs a laugh. To think, for a moment he actually pitied the bastard. He didn't know why he stood up for Trix all the time. The man was a heartless prick and the whole wrestling community knew it, but then again, Scotty Kames wasn't Matrix. Two seperate people. Matrix: What the fuck are you standing there for? I said, another fuckin beer! Chuck: I was just think, Scott, that you should cut down tonight. The WMW Midwest Champion reals back from the sudden shock. His "mentor" had never said that before, in all the years he had known the man. Chuck shrugged a flashed a small grin to let Trix know he wasn't joking. Furrowing a grey eyebrow, Chuck turned to walk off, but needless to say Matrix would have none of that "disrespect" Matrix: Hey Old Man, I pay just like everyone else. You've seen what fuckin happens when people say no to me! The bartender stops in his tracks, and turns around dramatically slow. He casually walks back to Scotty...passed him, and then around the bar. The old man then moved to Trix, smiling. Matrix: Well, gonna get me a fuckin beer or wha- Chuck: No, not YOU shut the fuck up this time. I've known you over ten years now, I followed you when you moved to Cleavland. I helped you when you were fired from UHW. I did all that, and here your a fucking legend in WMW! Your the Midwest Champ, your the Matrix, and EVERYONE fuckin knows you, Trix. A good year you've been fightin. Now, I heard the rumors, I heard your retiring. I dont want to hear that shit from you. Matrix by now is speechless and takes a seat. His jaw hands as Chuck continues to rant. Chuck: Now if this is your last match in WMW, or in PTC, then goddamnit I wont let you go into it drunk. You hear me, Trix? If you want to walk in and waste your fucking life here, then do it, but you do it SOMEWHERE ELSE. This is MY bar. I was here for you when Hanson trashed it. I was here for you when Tami left you. Hell, Trix, I was here for you when you wanted to get drunk every damn night of your life. Here's what I want you to do, and your gonna do it or I'll kick your damn ass myself. Your going to walk out that door, your going to the gym, and so help me God, your going to WIN that fucking match. Matrix: I-...Chuck...I... Chuck: Now shut the fuck up, and get the hell out of my bar. ---------------------------------------------- Trix fumbled with the keys and shoved them into his jeep's hole, and away he went. The bar vibrated as he looked to the camera. Matrix: A promo? You want a promo NOW? No, fuck you. Here's my promo. LOW, WWA, PRIME, and anyone else I gotta fight. I'll fight you, and I expect to lose. But thats what I do. I always make second best and thats my life story. I wont fight you for WMW, or my partners, or myself. I'm not gonna win, sure.....but for Chuck, I'm gonna make sure that you NEVER forget the night with Matrix. You may win...but when you enter my world...you'll never be the same. So, PTC...Welcome to Cyberspace.
