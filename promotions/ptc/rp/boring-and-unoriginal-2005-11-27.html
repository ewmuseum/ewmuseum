Boring and Unoriginal.

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 I spent all day yesterday thinking about what I could do to hype myself for the upcoming Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. I thought about talking about what this tournament meant to me… But Shadow did that. So then I figured, hey why don't I put together a little video package with a cool song playing in the background. Maybe I could make myself a bit mysterious, really make people think there's some drama in my life that I need this tournament to help me solve. But then I saw countless of the Free Agents doing it…And who really wants to be associated with them, you know. I didn't think about it, but that Seymour Almasy plug in the toy store was real original… Wish I'd have done it. Some made me laugh, and some made me throw crap at my television. Alot of them made me throw crap at my television. I figured there was no way I was going to make a hype that would make people uninterested in me once the tourney began. But how was I going to do that? How was I going to make sure no one thought I was boring and unoriginal? Easy. I wasn't going to hype myself. Seriously, what's the point? You want to know a little about me, go watch NC-17. I want you to go watch GCW's pay per view and watch me beat the crap out of Varsity. I want you all to see me in action, because that my friends is the biggest hype of them all. Then again, like the song says… Don't believe the hype.
