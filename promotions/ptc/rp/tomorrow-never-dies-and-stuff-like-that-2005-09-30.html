Tomorrow Never Dies And Stuff Like That

30 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-09-30 “The sun'll come out tomorrow…” The radiant arms of the sun reach up over the urban horizon. It sneaks around the buildings and inches up the steep incline of the planet. God, its tough… but the climb must be done every day. Or else the hopes and dreams of the children will die… or something. “bet your bottom dollar that tomorrow…” The suns’ hands softly touches the window of the thirteenth floor apartment of the Windy City. The dark and dank apartment is slowly burnt by the rays of the sun… light drips into the room. “there'll be sun…” Just a puddle at first… but soon the puddle is taking over the room. Now it’s like a indoor pond of smiles and happiness, minus the scum and methane. “just thinking about tomorrow…” We see bottles of strawberry and blueberry dackery on a small table. Suddenly a hand shoots up and swipes the bottles away. They fly across the room and land with a crash upon some unseen surface. “clears away the cobwebs and the sorrow…” Peaking up from the far end of the table is the head of Kenny Mercury… but not the Kenny Mercury that we’ve seen before… this one… is… well… ugly. And not just ugly inside, as he is… but uncharacteristically… the ugliness is on the outside as well. “Turn that friggin’ music off!” He screamed. … “Sorry.” “Damn you…” Kenny scowled. His face was covered in scraggily black facial hair. His eyes were bloodshot and sat upon two big puffy bags… and his hair… his beautiful… golden… flowing… God-like hair… was greasy and mangled. “Good Rod… the voices… I ‘m speaking to myself again… Damn it, Kenny, get a hold of yourself… You’re the definition of precision damn it!” Kenny attempts to stand up… and fails miserably. He stumbles as if his legs were made of rubber. Kenny scrambles as his butt hits the floor. He latches onto the coffee table. Kenny’s cheeks puff up with air as he struggles up. There… wait… not quite… okay now. He’s there. “Good… good…” Kenny complemented himself. “Ugh… What the frick day is it…” Kenny scratched his head. “Well… what the hell month is? Er… how about… year?” “Aw, Christ… gotta take-a piss… Bathroom, bathroom… gotta make it to the bathroom.” Kenny bumbles down a nearby hallway. He dives into the water closet… well, he grips the toilet. He pulls down his wrestling tights and takes aim. “Come on, Kenny… You’re the definition of precision…” Moments and a flush of the bowl later… Kenny falls into the hallway. “I’ll get that later…” He decrees with a burp. The UWF’s self proclaimed “shooting star” made his way down the corridor to the living room. A pile of letters and envelopes lay untouched upon the couch. He plops down on it and snatches up the pile with a sigh. “Crap.” Kenny tosses the first letter over his shoulder and onto the floor behind him. “Crap.” He tosses another. “Crap.” You get the picture… “Crap.” “Crap.” “Shit.” “Crap.” Then he stops… he brings the letter up to his face and squints his eyes slightly. The envelope is addressed to one Kenneth Hayes… from the Universal Wrestling Federation. Kenny sits back against the back couch cushion… Kenny closes his eyes… It’s been a couple months since Kenny had been seen on UWF television… He was on vacation. The last time he appeared in public… was at the UWF pay per view FANdemonium DOS~! The Elimination Chamber… it was his last challenge… his last match… and he failed. He had lost not only the match but the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. The six man title match had been stacked… four of Kenny’s arch enemies against Kenny and his partner and only friend, Jamie Shock. But it wasn’t Misfit’s hatred that did him in… it wasn’t Malcolm Kennedy’s angst or Slasher’s insanity… nor was it Killer Dude’s lack of hygiene… pube-boy… but it was Jamie Shock. Kenny had trusted him… and Jamie had betrayed that trust. The allure of World Championship gold and glory was too much… left the federation soon after. On vacation… or so the illusion was. Kenny made it seem like he was off to the Bahamas for the summer… or Key West or something… but it was a charade. Kenny had been sulking in his apartment for weeks on end. He had become a recluse, a hermit… God damn, if his ass wasn’t white enough already, now it was really white. The sun scorched him… and he poured the salt on his wounds. The UWF? What was it now… Kenny tore open the letter and quickly unfolded it. His eyes darted back and forth as he took it all in… “Primetime Central?” “Super Series?” “Chris Storm?” Kenny’s first reaction came as somewhat of a surprise to himself… He smiled. Well, first he coughed, but then he smiled. He hadn’t smiled in a long time. And it wasn’t a smile of happiness or glee… but that infamous Mercury smirk… that smirk that just told you that the wheels were turning… the smirk that just screamed Kenny Mercury… Kenny Mercury the egomaniac… Kenny Mercury the masochist… the misogynist… the jerk-ologist…. Kenny Mercury the asshole… the OLD Kenny Mercury… “Heh… heh… heh-HA! HAHAHA!” Kenny tossed the letter into the air and sprang to his feet. “This is perfect… This is beautiful…” Kenny exclaimed as he ran back to the bathroom. “Chris Storm… Chris Storm… Chris friggin’ Storm… HAHA! That’s right… Chris… You’ve got it… You’ve got Kenny Mercury.” Kenny looked deeply at himself in the mirror. He frowned. He had deteriorated far beyond anything he could have ever imagined… I mean, this is the guy who used to throw a hissy fit because of a hang nail… He once refused to leave the locker room because his cuticles had advanced a little too far… This is the guy who could have run a bath and body works out of his apartment. “I have a lot of work to do… and it’s all for you, Chris Storm. Chris… you’ve been hiding from me for YEARS… The Original Wrestler… puh-lease… This is a dream match… and you’ve been running away from me ever since the 2003 P.I.T… Everyone’s been asking the question… Who’s the best technical wrestler alive? Well, Chris… let me tell you… that it’s NOT a question of technical prowess… To be quite frank… I’m just better than you. PERIOD. And now its time to pay the piper… you can’t run away to Japan this time. It’s just going to be you and me… and… Sock-boy… Shit…” Kenny thought to himself for a moment… “Argyle as special guest referee? Huh… interesting… the very man I retired… The guy who wrestled his last match against none other than yours truly… and lost decisively… What’s the point of this? Is this a set-up? No matter… no matter… Argyle can eat a wiener. Chris Storm can eat a wiener. Everyone can eat a wiener.” “Chris Storm is going to eat a wiener… plain and simple… The fact of the matter is, Chris… that you don’t hold a candle to the Shooting Star… the Submission Exhibition… the Technical Wizard… the very DEFINITION OF PRECISION… KENNY MERCURY!” … “Yes… yes…” … “Yes…” And with that… Kenny brushes his teeth. … With Colgate. … And receives a check a few weeks later. … Product placement is fun. The End.
