Pain In The Arse Royale - Parts II & III

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-11 ATLANTIC CITY, NJ - THE STARDUST PEARL CASINO & RESORT THAT EVENING IMPOSSIBLE TO TELL WHAT TIME IT IS O'CLOCK After Jimmy Bong got himself cleaned up, and changed out of his sweaty, old tuxedo into a fresh, clean, new tuxedo, he was off to Atlantic City with Majorly Shafted and Iva Gunnonmi. They were given further details about their mission en route; their rendezvous point with their contact man -- a British operative by the distinctive name of Tomorrow Knight -- was the Stardust Pearl, one of several casino resorts to be found in Atlantic City. Once contact with Knight was made, they were to track down CIA agent Walter Durnstrom and take him into custody whilst being as discreet as possible. During their flight, Majorly Shafted kept muttering something under his breath about promising God he'd attend church every Sunday for the rest of his life if he just got through this whole thing without Bong landing the lot of them in prison for the next three hundred years. Within a few hours, they found themselves pulling up in front of the glamourous Stardust Pearl in a limousine. The outside of the building was a veritable symphony of color and light, with the words "STARDUST PEARL" blinking on and off in giant purple neon letters on the marquis. The streets of Atlantic City were bustling with traffic, and the sidewalks were crowded with tourists and residents alike. It made Bong feel all the more important to be getting such excellent first-class service, especially with the red carpet greeting the three of them were receiving from the hotel staff. The limo driver stepped out of the front seat, and opened the back door for Bong, Shafted, and Iva. As they emerged from the limo, the hotel concierge, followed quickly by two eager, young bellhops, approached them with a broad smile on his face. "It is my joy and privilege to welcome you all to the Stardust Pearl!" greeted the concierge with enthusiasm. "The foremost fictional casino and resort in all of Atlantic City!" Bong raised an eyebrow. "Did you just say 'fictional' casino and resort?" he asked, puzzled. "Of course not, sir!" the concierge answered with a smile. "That would just be downright nutty! Come, let me show you to your room! Oh, don't worry about your bags, sir," he said to Shafted, who was grabbing the briefcase containing their satellite communication console. "We can get them for you." Shafted seemed a bit wary of that proposition, but reluctantly agreed to let the bellhops do their job. "Very well," he said. "But take great care not to drop this one, or else you'll likely be owing us about a million dollars or so." Bong gulped, and leaned over to Iva. "Don't tell Shafted," he whispered, "but I was carrying that briefcase around the airport earlier today -- I think I owe him about six million dollars." Iva just rolled her eyes and shook her head, and walked into the hotel. --- "Here it is, room 3015!" The door opened, and light from the hallway poured into the room, tainting the purity of the total darkness that shrouded it only moments before. One of the bellhops, who brought up everyone's luggage from downstairs, stepped in and flipped on the lights. Bong, Shafted, and Iva stood in awe at the sight before them. This was certainly a V.I.P. room, there was no doubt about that. The windows along the back wall were reminiscent of something out of The Jetsons, boasting a matchless view of the starry night sky and the city lights below. To the right was a cheery brick fireplace, complete with a gas valve for instant flames. A leather couch and two leather reclining chairs were situated around the fireplace, as well as a sixty inch big screen television. On the left side of the room was a spacious kitchenette and dining area, as well as a pay-per-drink liquor cabinet ("I'm really going to need that, being cooped up in here with Bong," Shafted said to himself when he first saw it). Also to the left was the adjoining bedroom, featuring two handsomely-crafted six-drawer bureaus, a roomy walk-in closet, and a bathroom that was furnished with a sample basket of fancy, expensive herbal shampoos, lotion and bubble bath. The room's coup de gras however was the set of two king-sized beds, which in particular were a beautiful sight to Bong. "Oh goody!" he said as he walked into the bedroom. "At last, I can get a decent night's sleep! No more sleeping on..." He froze as he saw that there was also a crudely constructed cot sitting on the floor between the two beds. "A cot!?" he exclaimed. "Why the hell do I need to sleep on a cot!?" Iva clicked her tongue a few times at Bong, with a sly grin on her face. "What do you mean 'Because you need to be a gentleman and let the lady have the bed'!? Why can't Shafted be the one to give it up instead? Scratch that, if I have to be the one to give up a bed, wouldn't it be easier if I just slept on one of the couches out in the other room?" "It's against hotel regulations to sleep on the couches, sir," said the bellhop, setting down their luggage just inside the door. "Against hotel regulations!?" Bong repeated in disbelief as he walked back into the first room. "What sort of daft rule is that?" "The kind one can only expect from a fictional casino and resort, sir," replied the bellhop. "Again with the 'fictional' stuff!" Bong said. "Why are you people calling this place fictional!?" "I said nothing of the kind, sir," the bellhop replied. He then coughed, smiled, and held out his hand. "Whatever," Bong mumbled. "Anyway, thanks for bringing our bags up for us." "Ahem," the bellhop coughed again, looking a bit irritated this time. "You might want to see a doctor about that," said Bong, who proceeded to close the door... right in the bellhop's face! "AAAAGH!" screamed the bellhop from the other side of the door. "MY NOSE IS BROKEN!" "I believe the young man wanted a tip, Bong," sighed Shafted, who was busying himself with the fireplace. "D'oh, of course!" Bong said, shaking his head. "How could I have forgotten?" He opened the door, to reveal the young bellhop still standing there, his hands covering his now-bloody face. "In the future," Bong said, "it's a wise idea to stand clear of the doorway when other people are shutting the door. Have a pleasant evening!" He then closed the door once again. "That's not quite what I meant, Bong," groaned Shafted, who now had a roaring fire going. "What? I thought that was a rather good tip," said Bong proudly. "Although I suppose it was a lesson learned just a shade too late. Oh well, what is life anyway, if not just one lesson after another? Anyway, I'm famished. I'm going to call and have room service bring something up." Just as Bong started to walk over the phone, there was a knock-knock-knock coming from behind him. "Room service!" came a voice from the other side. "Goodness, that was fast," Bong mused, as he turned around to open the door. "Bong, wait!" cried Shafted, jumping up from his seat and running toward the door as well. "Don't open the door! It could be a trap!" Bong chuckled. "Oh Shafted, there's no need to worry! It's only a room service caterer that we didn't even ring for. What danger could there possibly be?" He opened the door, and found himself staring down the barrel of a gun, being pointed at him by the caterer. "Well, that answers that question," Bong muttered, embarrassed. "Back in the room," barked the caterer, with a distinctly British accent. "Quickly!" "Look, if this was about my slamming the door in the bellhop's face, I'm sorry!" Bong pleaded as he stepped back into the room. The caterer just ignored him, taking a quick glance up and down the hallway before entering the room himself, pulling the food cart along with him. "Way to go, genius," said Shafted sarcastically. He stepped in between Bong, whose hands were raised above his head, and the intruder, whoever it was. "Look, you're wasting your time if you think you're going to kill this idiot," Shafted said to the caterer. "I'm a human shield, genetically engineered by some of the top scientists in London, so..." "So I'll just shoot you with the latest 'superbullets' that just came out on the market," finished the intruder. "I know who you are, Majorly Shafted, and believe me, I'm quite prepared to kill you as well." "Oh, that's just bloody brilliant," growled Shafted, stepping aside and raising his hands above his head now. "Well done Bong, well done. Now I'm going to die in New Jersey! I hope you're happy!" "Well, I am a little happy," said Bong. "I'm don't feel hungry anymore!" "And soon you won't feel anything at all," said the caterer gravely. "Goodbye, gentlemen." He cocked the safety of his gun, and took aim directly at Bong's forehead... BANG! "OW!" The caterer yelled in pain and dropped his pistol, shaking his hand. Bong and Shafted looked around the room wildly, then saw Iva Gunnonmi standing in the bedroom doorway (wearing only red silk robe, and some rather scant lingerie underneath), pointing a smoking pistol of her own at the would-be killer of her two allies. "Gadzooks!" Bong exclaimed. "Iva Gunnonmi! She has a gun on her!" "Imagine that," said Shafted, rolling his eyes. Iva stepped forward into the main room, keeping her gun pointed squarely at the mystery caterer, who was yelping like a child now. "Alright! Alright! I give!" he sniffled. "Good lord, getting shot in the hand really hurts, you know!" "Serves you right!" Bong spat. "You were about to kill us!" "Oh come on, I was going to do no such thing!" the caterer replied, slowly getting up to his feet now. "I was just having a bit of fun with you blokes! That wasn't even a real gun I was holding! I picked it up at a novelty store just the other day. Go on, see for yourself!" Shafted cocked an eyebrow, then walked over to the pistol that was now lying on the floor, and picked it up. He pointed it at the caterer, who looked entirely unconcerned by this action. Shafted pulled the trigger. BANG! ...is what the flag read, that was now hanging from the gun barrel. "What in the world?" asked Shafted. "I told you! Hmph!" scoffed the caterer. "I just needed to have a few laughs is all!" He glared at the frowning Iva, who was slowly lowering her gun now. "Hm," Bong thought to himself, looking down on the floor at the shell that was expelled from Iva's gun. "No sense in leaving that just lying around." He walked over and picked up the shell, putting it into his pocket. "After all," the caterer continued, "it's been dreadfully boring the last few days, just following that Durnstrom fellow around everywhere he goes." "Durnstrom?" Bong perked up. "But that means you're..." "That's right," said the caterer, bowing. "Tomorrow Knight, British intelligence liason. And you must be Jimmy Bong, Agent Double-Oh-Nothing." "But of course," Bong smiled. "Pleasure to meet you." "Indeed," said Shafted, with a slight tone of annoyance. "So Knight, since you're finished with your tawdry prank, I take it you're here to brief us on the current situation?" "Ah, right to the point, you are," Knight chortled. "Right, let's get down to business, then." Knight wheeled his cart into the center of the room, and swiftly removed the white covering cloth in one majestic motion, revealing what appeared to be a blueprint of the Stardust Pearl casino underneath. Bong, Shafted, and Iva all gathered around as Knight began the briefing. "Durnstrom has been living it up all over Atlantic City the past few days," he started. "It seems he has quite a few... indiscretions, shall we say... that I'm sure his wife would have a fit over. That is, if she were with him, but... well, anyway, that's not important. I've been following him around, sometimes managing to get close enough to overhear his conversations. I can tell you with one hundred percent certainty that he's going to come to the Stardust Pearl casino." "When?" asked Bong. "Tomorrow night," Knight replied. "Oh!" smiled Bong. "Pleasure to meet you!" Shafted covered his face with his hands and looked incredibly depressed all of a sudden. Iva just rolled her eyes and looked at Bong with disgust. Knight, however, returned Bong's smile. "And you as well, sir!" he said gleefully. "Wonderful, there's two of them now," Shafted mumbled to himself. "Anyway," Knight continued, "Durnstrom will be visiting the casino around nine o'clock tomorrow evening. That's going to be our best chance to grab him." "How will we pull that off without anyone noticing?" asked Shafted. "It's going to take all of our combined efforts, but I have a plan that should make things go smoothly," answered Knight. He reached underneath the cart, and pulled out a small box containing what appeared to be four earpieces. "We will be communicating via these standard earpiece two-way receivers," said Knight. "Double-Oh-Nothing will be our eyes and ears inside the casino. He'll determine Durnstrom's location, and make contact with him. Once we have a lock on Durnstrom, it will be your turn, Shafted." Knight reached underneath the cart once more, and pulled out another small box, this time containing a tranquilizer gun, and a single dart. "Your job is to render Durnstrom unconscious with this tranquilizer," said Knight, showing Shafted the contents of the box before handing it to him. "Bong will keep Durnstrom occupied long enough for you to get a clear shot -- and you must be absolutely discreet about it, mind you. One shot is all you have, but one shot is all you'll need. That dart will make Durnstrom look as if he's naturally feeling sleepy. Once he's hit, Bong, you will excuse yourself by offering to help Durnstrom get outside for some fresh air, or whatever else sounds good to you." "So that's when we'll have Durnstrom in our grasp," said Bong. "Where do I take him though?" "That's where I come in," smiled Knight. He pointed to a spot on the blueprint. "I've also been observing this casino for the last few days, and I've noticed that this particular room is relatively unguarded. I'll be cleverly disguised as a janitor and keep an eye on things back there to make sure everything is safe. Then, when the time comes, you will bring Durnstrom to that location, where we can secure and detain him for questioning." "And Iva?" asked Bong. "Ah yes, Ms. Gunnonmi," said Knight. "She will be your date inside the casino." Iva looked horror-struck, and shook her head no while wildly clicking her tongue in protest. "Hey, I don't say things like that about your mother!" exclaimed Bong. "Just go along with it, Iva," sighed Shafted. "You know that Bong is likely to screw this up somehow. We need someone competent to go along with him to make sure things go right." "Wow!" said Bong in amazement. "You couldn't be more on the ball if you tried, Shafted!" "Right then," said Knight. "Well, that's about it. Be sure to get a good rest, everyone. Remember, Durnstrom will be arriving at nine o'clock... tomorrow night." "Pleasure to meet you!" said Bong. "And you as well, sir!" Knight replied. Shafted wailed loudly, and quickly walked off into the bedroom. Iva looked about ready to rip her own hair out by the roots, and followed right behind Shafted. Bong and Knight looked at each other oddly, and shrugged. --- ATLANTIC CITY, NJ - THE STARDUST PEARL CASINO & RESORT TOMORROW NIGHT... ER, EVENING JUST A BIT AFTER NINE O'CLOCK "Phew, I guess I do blend in after all," said Bong. "For a moment I was worried that I was going to stick out in this tuxedo." Jimmy Bong was standing just inside the entrance to the Stardust Casino, which was packed nearly to full capacity. Atlantic City's upper crust was in attendance as always, and it was quite obvious as to why Bong blended in with the crowd -- every man was wearing a tuxedo. Iva Gunnonmi was -- reluctantly -- accompanying Bong, and was dressed in a shimmering, strapless, full-length white dress that made her sparkle like a diamond. "Why the bloody hell would you think that?" crackled the voice of Majorly Shafted over Bong's two-way radio earpiece. "You always wear a tuxedo! And people wear tuxedos in casinos on a daily basis! Your wardrobe was tailor-made for a mission like this!" "Yes, but this is only my casual tuxedo," said Bong. "I feel quite out of place with it in a fancy place like this. But whatever works, I suppose." "Right," said Shafted. "Now get in there and find Durnstrom. We have no time to waste!" "Easier said than done," said Bong despairingly. It was easy to see why he'd say this -- the casino was, to put it mildly, huge. The room extended as far as the eye could see. There were dozens of tables hosting such familar games as roulette, blackjack, poker, craps, not to mention the rows upon rows of slot machines. Factor in the gargantuan crowd, and finding Durnstrom was quite a task, indeed. "Knight, do you know anything about Durnstrom's habits?" Bong asked. "Are there any games he particularly favors?" "Well, I understand he fancies roulette," replied Knight over Bong's two-way. "Right, I'll head for the roulette tables, then," said Bong. "Oh, no, not THAT kind of roulette," Knight interjected. "I was talking about Russian roulette, actually. He plays quite often, and has only lost once or twice." Bong and Iva looked oddly at each other. "What are you going on about, Knight?" asked Shafted. "You can't lose Russian roulette more than once! In fact, you can't even lose Russian roulette and LIVE!" "Oh," said Knight. "I didn't know a card game was so deadly." "Russian roulette is played with GUNS, not CARDS!" cried Shafted. "OH! exclaimed Knight with realization. "Of course! I was talking about blackjack, not Russian roulette! My goodness, I always get those two confused. No wonder nobody ever wants to play blackjack with me anymore!" Shafted just sighed audibly over the two-way. "Hang on," Bong said, peering over the crowd. "I think I've spotted Durnstrom. Yes, that's him! He's playing at the blackjack table located in the northeast corner of the room, with a woman in a green dress." "Nicely done, Bong," said Shafted. "Knight, is your position secure?" "Quiet as a mouse back here," came Knight's reply. "Roger that. Iva and I are initiating contact with the target," said Bong. "I copy," said Shafted. "I'm moving that way as well. Give me about ten minutes or so to get into position." "Come on Iva, let's go!" Bong grabbed Iva by the arm and practically dragged her through the sea of humanity towards Durnstrom's table, her tongue fiercely clicking with indignation the whole way. Within a few minutes, Bong and Iva found themselves sitting at crowded the blackjack table, across from CIA agent Walter Durnstrom. Durnstrom, with a lit cigarette hanging from his mouth, cast a suspicious eye at Bong as the British secret agent took an empty chair, while Iva stood behind him. "You seem to have an impressive haul this evening, Mr. Durnstrom," said Bong, indicating the large stack of chips sitting in front of the American. "What're you doing here?" he asked rudely. "Aren't you supposed to be in some stupid wrestling tournament back in Washington or something?" "Oh... yes, that," Bong stammered. "Well..." "Bong!" said Shafted in Bong's ear. "Be careful what you say to him! Whatever you do, don't blow your cover!" Bong cleared his throat, as Durnstrom was busy looking at two cards that were just dealt to him. "Well," he continued, "Yes, I am in a wrestling tournament. In fact, I've been winning quite a lot." "Yeah, I know," said Durnstrom gruffly. "Because the moment you lose, yer outta Washington for good. I'll see to that personally." He looked up at Bong, quite serious now. He leaned forward and spoke in a low voice. "Frankly, if I had things my way, you'd have been history a long time ago. I don't believe that cock-and-bull story that N fed me for one second about you being in Washington on civilian business. I've always suspected that yer snoopin' around, tryin' to mess up our search for Garfeld. Yer just lucky I don't have any proof of that." Bong laughed. "My goodness, you're astute! That's exactly what I've been doing this whole time!" "BONG!" yelled Shafted in horror. "I said DON'T blow your cover!" "Oh, don't blow my cover," Bong repeated to himself. "I thought that sounded odd at first. Erm," he turned back to Durnstrom, who thankfully had only been paying half attention while he was looking at another card. "What'd you say?" Durnstrom asked. "I uh," stammered Bong, "I said, 'My goodness, you're a fool! Rats are hacking up live hens, throwing missile slimes!" "Uh... huh," said Durnstrom. "Now look wise guy, unless yer here to gamble, you'd better just get the hell outta my sight." "Darling," said the woman in the green dress sitting next to Durnstrom -- who was quite ample in the chest region. "Who is this man you're talking to? You seem to know each other. How rude of you not to introduce me!" Bong smiled at the woman, who in turn smiled back. "Bong," he said to her. "Jimmy Bong." "Ah, it's a pleasure to meet you, Mr. Bong," she said. "My name is Gloria... Gloria Srack." "Gloria Srack," Bong repeated. "Well, that goes without saying I suppose." "Enough of this!" said Durnstrom. "Are you gonna gamble or what?" "Uh..." said Bong, looking around. "Hang on, Bong, I'm just about in position," said Shafted. "Well," Bong continued, "the thing is, I don't have any chips, so..." "Alright, I'm set. Just waiting for a clear shot..." "Oh for the love of..." scoffed Durnstrom. "Here, maybe you'd like to have one of my chips!" He mockingly flipped one of his many chips to Bong's side of the table. "Almost..." Bong looked down at the chip, and smiled. "Oh, no thanks," he said politely. "I couldn't possibly take something that wasn't mine!" Bong flipped the chip back at Durnstrom. However, he made a slight miscalculation, and the chip bounced off Durnstrom's chest and onto the floor. "I've got him! Taking the shot now!" "You stupid Brit," mumbled Durnstrom. He leaned down from his chair to pick the chip up off the floor. THWIP! "ACK!" Durnstrom sat back up, and looked at his companion, Gloria Srack. Gloria was beginning to look rather tired. "SON OF A BITCH!! THE BLOODY BASTARD MOVED!!" screamed Shafted. "I KNEW YOU'D SCREW THIS UP, BONG, YOU IDIOT!!" "Blast it all!" Bong cursed to himself. "I can't believe it! We were so close! What the hell are we going to do now?" He turned to Iva, who gave Bong a glance that said "Don't look at me!" "Hey, Bong," said Durnstrom, seeing that Bong was looking at Iva. "Is that your girl?" Bong turned back around. "Uh," he said, "yeah! This is Iva. Yeah, Iva's my girl." Iva looked positively shocked. "Hm," thought Durnstrom with a smile on his face. "I tell you what..." He looked at Gloria once again, and she was fast asleep on the table. "It looks like my date ain't gonna be in a partyin' mood tonight. So how about we play a round of blackjack for yer woman instead?" Bong blinked. "I beg your pardon?" "You heard me!" said Durnstrom. "One round of blackjack. If you win, you get all of my chips... there's at least five hundred thousand dollars here. But if I win, then I get your woman! How does that sound?" Bong looked at Iva, who clicked her tongue as if to say "Don't you even dare think about it." But after a moment, Bong turned back to Durnstrom with a sly grin. "Okay, you're on!" he declared. Iva smacked her own forehead with her hand. "Alright, that's what I'm talkin' about!" said Durnstrom excitedly. "Deal!" The dealer slid a face down card to Durnstrom, then to Bong. Then, each man got a face up card. Durnstrom had an ace, and Bong had a king. "I'll stand," said Durnstrom. "HAH!" Bong laughed in triumph. "I'll stand as well! There's no way you can possibly beat this hand, Durnstrom!" Bong turned his card over, to reveal that he had... a two! "Mr. Bong has twelve," said the dealer. Durnstrom smiled. "Looks like I win, Mr. Bong," he said, as he turned over his card to reveal... a nine! "Mr. Durnstrom has twenty," said the dealer. "Mr. Durnstrom wins." Durnstrom jumped up from his seat, and pumped his fists into the air in victory. Bong was shocked, and Iva was just plain dumbfounded at Bong's stupidity. "Hang on!" Bong complained. "That's not possible! An ace is only worth one! Durnstrom only has a hand of ten!" "An ace is also worth eleven, sir," said the dealer. "What?" asked Bong incredulously. "When did that become a rule?" "Come to papa!" said a leering Durnstrom, who was coming around the table now to claim his prize. "Oh baby, you're gonna keep me warm tonight, I can tell! You seem like the experienced type to me, even if yer stupid enough to hang around a clown like Bong here..." Iva had taken just about all she could stand from any member of the male gender this evening. She reared back, and socked Durnstrom across the jaw, knocking him out cold. Bong looked down at Durnstrom lying on the ground, then back up at Iva. "Genius!" he said. "Pure genius!" "Well done, Iva!" said Shafted, who had been observing from his hiding place. "Knight, are you copying this? We have Durnstrom!" They all waited for a moment. There was no reply. "Knight?" Another moment. Still no reply. "Knight, are you there?" asked Bong, with growing concern. There was still no answer. "Bong, you and Iva get Durnstrom out of there now! I think something may have gone terribly wrong!" --- Bong and Iva quickly dragged Durnstrom out of the casino area, explaining to curious bystanders that the CIA agent had consumed way too many Long Island iced teas and had unceremoniously passed out. They met up with Shafted just outside the entrance, and they all quickly made their way to the back corridor where Knight had told them to meet. But when they got there, Knight was nowhere to be seen. "I wonder where he got off too," panted Bong, who was exhausted from hurrying out of the casino while carrying Durnstrom on his shoulders. "There's no telling with that crazy fellow," remarked Shafted. "This had better not be another one of his pranks." "Well, it would certainly be an inappropriate time for it," Bong said off-handedly, looking around. "As if it was appropriate last time," Shafted muttered. "I say," said Bong, pointing at a janitor's closet. "That door is slightly ajar. Aren't all of these doors meant to be locked?" Bong walked over to the closet door, and opened it. A body fell out of the closet, and onto the floor. It was Tomorrow Knight. "JIMINY CHRISTMAS!!" Bong yelled, jumping back. "Gadfry! Knight!" Shafted exclaimed. He hurried over to where Knight was laying, and stooped down to examine him. "This isn't good," said Shafted, looking up slowly at Bong. "Tomorrow Knight is dead. Somebody killed him." "You've got to be joking!" Bong said, horrified. "I thought Tomorrow never dies!" Shafted reached into his coat pocket and withdrew a pistol, then quickly stood up. "I have a feeling somebody just rewrote that particular script," he said under his breath. "And chances are... they're still here." Bong looked frantically up and down the hallway, and noticed a shadowy figure approaching them from the far end. "Shafted, look!" he whispered, pointing at the approaching figure. "Who the bloody hell is that?" Shafted asked Bong. The figure came ever closer, and eventually stepped out of the shadows to reveal himself fully. He was a muscular man, standing about seven feet tall. He wore dusty, dark brown overalls, and a faded white dress shirt. His short black hair was slicked back, and his face looked as if it had been run through a blender. The tall man looked down at Jimmy Bong, and smiled an evil smile. "I know who this man is," growled Bong, through gritted teeth. "He's one of Garfeld's goons -- one of the top killers under his employ, in fact. His name... is Ears." Ah yes, and the man also had giant ears made completely out of reinforced steel. Hence the name. "Iva, hand me your gun, quickly!" Bong ordered the Russian agent, who was tending to the still-unconscious Durnstrom. "Bong, have you forgotten?" Shafted quickly asked. "You're not licensed to kill! You're only certified to harm!" "Damnit!" said Bong. "I knew that was going to come back and haunt me someday. Well, looks like the only thing for me to do is blindly charge him." "Uh, Bong, I don't think that's..." "BANZAI!!!" screamed Bong as he lunged at the giant Ears. "...such a good idea. Well, nice knowing you I guess," mumbled Shafted. Ears simply stood there, as Bong charged right at him, and threw a quick left hook... directly into his left ear. "Ow!" cried Bong, as his fist made contact with hard steel. "That smarts! Let's try that again..." This time, Bong threw a right punch... directly into the left ear of Ears! "Blimey!" Bong wailed in pain. "Can nothing daunt this behemoth!?" "Bong!" yelled Shafted. "Why the bloody hell are you punching him in the ears!?" "I don't know," Bong cried. "I just don't know!" "Bah, stand back!" barked Shafted. "I'm taking him out with my pistol!" Bong dove out of the way, as Shafted fired his pistol directly at Ears... and, inexplicably enough, the bullet ricocheted off of his left ear! "You've got to be joking!" exclaimed Shafted, shocked. "I was aiming at his foot! What's with those ears, anyway!?" Ears just kept smiling. "He's not very big on words, Shafted," said Bong. Shafted fired his pistol again. And again. And again. He kept firing until he had no bullets left... and every one of his shots somehow managed to ricochet off of Ears' ears. "Blast it all," said Shafted. "I can tell this is going to get worse before it gets better." This was indeed true, as Ears now took his turn, and rushed at Bong and Shafted. He grabbed both men by the scruffs of their necks, and bashed their skulls together... --- Bong awoke a bit later to find himself lying on the ground. He was still in the back corridor of the Stardust Pearl. Lying next to him was Majorly Shafted, who was just coming to as well. Walter Durnstrom was slumped against the wall, still quite sedated. Ears, thankfully, was gone. "Ooooh, my head," Bong mumbled, rubbing his aching skull. "Goodness, that hurt like the dickens... where's Iva? Iva?" Bong looked around for her, but she was nowhere to be seen. She was gone. "Iva!" Bong yelled, getting to his feet. "Where are you!?" "Good evening, Mr. Bong," echoed a voice throughout the hallway. Bong froze. "Garfeld," he whispered. "Where are you!? Show your--" "As you may or may not have guessed by now, I now have the lovely Ms. Gunnonmi into my fiendish clutches," continued the shrill voice of Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld. "HEY!" Bong yelled. "Just because you're hiding somewhere doesn't mean you can interrupt me when I'm talking!" Shafted groaned where he was laying. "Re... cor... ding..." he managed to get out. "I must express my congratulations, Mr. Bong," Garfeld went on. "You have managed to foil me to this point. However, I'm afraid there is so much more to my plan that you don't know. For example, you think that I have no way of obtaining the operating codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle. The reality, however, is that they are very close to coming into my possession." "That's impossible!" said Bong. "We found out that U was the double-agent! He's in custody! There's no way..." "As to how my plan will succeed, I'll leave that to you to figure out," said Garfeld. "That is, of course, if you can survive the blast of the bomb that will detonate once this recording runs out." "Recording?" Bong asked, looking quickly around the hallway. After a moment, he spotted a tape player sitting about ten feet away from him. "EGADS! DUCK AND COVER!" he yelled. He quickly grabbed the limp figure of Durnstrom, and slid underneath the human shield, Majorly Shafted, as much as he could... "Until next time, Mr. Bong..." laughed Garfeld. Then, the tape player stopped... BOOOOOOM!!!! ... "Well, that was fun! Let's do that again!" "Ugh, shut up, Bong..."
