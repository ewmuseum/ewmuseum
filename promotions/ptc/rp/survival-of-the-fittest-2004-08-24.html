Survival of the Fittest...

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 Fnord: THIS IS JUST GREAT! [Fnord storms through a door, slamming it into the wall behind it. He storms straight towards the office of his uncle, the Vice President of Operations of 4WW… Daniel Garrett. Fnord brushes past a new 4WW superstar and almost knocks him to the ground with the bump from his shoulder. The new 4WW superstar is about to say something when Fnord turns around and jumps into his face.] Fnord: What are you gonna do? Really! [Fnord turns back around and makes his way to the office door with the VP OF OPS sign on it. Fnord bursts through the door, surprising Garrett.] Fnord: Okay, enough is enough! [Garrett quickly throws something into his desk drawer, hiding it, and sits straight up.] Garrett: What are you doing here, Wesley? [Fnord calms down and smiles. He slowly walks to Garrett’s desk. His face turns back to rage as he slams his fists down on the desk and then clears the desktop with his arms. Garrett jumps up to his feet and stares at his nephew, not quite knowing what to do.] Garrett: What do you want? Fnord: I need to know more. Garrett: More about what? Fnord: Don’t give me that shit. [Fnord walks over to the leather couch and plops down. He rubs his eyes with his hands and looks up towards his uncle, who has risen to his feet.] Fnord: I need to know the rest of the story. I need to know more about my father and his father before him. Garrett: I’ll tell you a small bit more. I can’t tell you too much at once… It would be too much for you… Fnord: Whatever, let’s just hear some more! [Garrett pulls up his chair and sits down as the scene fades to black.] [Back in the past. Sauriel, an angel that fights for what is right, lies beneath the fallen snow. The group of inhuman Germans, made up of The Grigori, going by the name Colonel Zephon and others like him, known as Nephillim, come upon the snow covered carcass.] Zephon: Nisroch, have some men uncover one of our brethren. [The one known as Nisroch, Colonel Zephon’s right hand man walks closer to Zephon.] Nisroch: Brethren? How can you be sure that— Zephon: Uncover him. [None of the other ‘soldiers’ move. They seem afraid. Zephon walks towards the carcass of the fallen Sauriel.] Zephon: The Sword is not here. That explains the separation of light we witnessed. This only could have occurred if he was locked in battle with… [Zephon rubs the snow off of Sauriel’s face.] Zephon: …one of the archangels. Here lies proof once again that The Holy One reigns not through righteousness, but through the force of arms and— [All of a sudden, the eyes of Sauriel lights up. He jumps out of the snow and grabs Zephon by the throat.] Zephon: —fear— Sauriel: Grigori! [Sauriel tosses Zephon a good thirty feet away. The other ‘soldiers’ pull out the guns. Sauriel puts each of his fists through the chest of two of the ‘soldiers’ as the others start firing at him. Zephon gets up out of the snow.] Zephon: Semicircle on me! [They get into formation and start to fire away at Sauriel.] Zephon: The archangel is weak—vulnerable—it loses light!!! [Sauriel falls down to the snow on his knees, exhausted. Zephon walks over to him and grabs his hair, pulling his head back up.] Zephon: It is time for your kind to pay for choosing the easier path of obedience… [Zephon pulls a glowing knife out of his belt.] Zephon: …and turning a blind eye to our brethren’s… [Zephon takes the knife and slices the front of Sauriel’s neck with it. Lightening-like light starts to go everywhere.] Zephon: …pain! [As Sauriel struggles with his neck, Zephon moves towards Sauriel’s wings. He holds them up.] Zephon: No need for these cumbersome things since your flying days are over. Once I have regained my own, I will sour to your master and describe in detail how you screamed as I ripped them from your broken body, archangel. [Sauriel lets out a cry of agony as Zephon rips and cuts the wings off of him. Zephon, still emotionless, walks away holding the wings. Sauriel exhausted and almost dead look sup at Zephon.] Sauriel: You will discover that possessing The Sword and wielding The Sword are as different as night and day. The Sons of Light will beat back your darkness, Grigori. It will… be… brief… [He falls down to the snow dead, this time for good. One of the ‘soldiers’ walks up holding a bullet.] Soldier: We found these, sir. American-issue casings. Nisroch: Could they have been corrupted? [Zephon takes the bullet from the ‘soldier’ and examines it.] Zephon: We must assume so. If the archangel implanted them with knowledge, their newfound awareness might jeopardize our plans. Nisroch: It’s unlikely a stray American patrol would be this far east for a probe of enemy lines. Zephon: Unless someone among them is not what he appears to be. Who has been a constant thorn in our side? Nisroch: Last word has the Centurion crusading in the Americas… [Zephon looks out as if searching the horizon for something.] Nisroch: …where he killed many of us. [The sun starts to rise.] Zephon: He may have enlisted. Taken part in the Normandy invasion. There is every reason to believe he is receptive to The Word. [Nisroch walks over to a ‘soldier’ with a radio on his back. He listens to the headphones.] Zephon: That would explain his many surprises these past millennia. He may know of our current destination. [Nisroch stops listening to the headphones and looks over towards Zephon.] Nisroch: The other unit has found The Sword. [Zephon walks towards the ‘soldier’ with the radio and takes it from him. He listens for a short while before responding.] Zephon: Well done. Meet at The Flame. Let nothing stop you, Allied or German. Any tanks or vehicles, take them by whatever means necessary. We will do the same. Out. [Zephon hangs up the radio and then looks up in the sky with a smile on his face.] Zephon: I see us tasting what should have been shared with us from the beginning, Nisroch. Soon we will be able to remind him of the ones he left behind. That we too are capable of showing no mercy. Can you see it? [Nisroch just stares at Zephon with seriousness.] Nisroch: I’ve always counted on you to supply the vision, sir. Zephon: Yes, of course you have. [Frank and Eddie are scouting ahead of the group. They see an old farm where a tank has driven through the barn and got stuck. The tank had been left in place. The two men walk by the barn, keeping their eyes open.] Eddie: Do you think souls meet, Frank? Frank: I don’t know. I guess, right? How can they not? We know there’s a Heaven now. A Hell. Eddie: But that means we—our souls—if we die, meet the souls we killed. How’s it gonna feel meeting some German guys we knocked off after D-day? I mean some of ‘em were just like us, putting on a uniform, told where to go… always wanted to know if Heaven existed, but… well… Frank: Yeah. Knowing kinda… hurts… Eddie: So, whaddya think? [Frank and Eddie come up to a field where some cows are digging through the snow with their mouths to try to get some grass.] Frank: I think if I live though all this, I’ll never miss church ever again. Eddie: I was talking about the cows. Frank: What about ‘em? [Eddie holds up his canteen.] Eddie: Canteen of milk would wash those c-rats down easier. [Two more soldiers come out of the woods near Frank and Eddie.] Pete: What’s up? Spot something? Frank: Cows. Eddie: Don’t see any dead ones, so the field’s probably clear of mines. You guys cover us. Gimme your canteens if you want milk. Frank: “Us”? [Frank and Eddie take their canteens and make their way to the cows. The rest of the soldiers come up to Pete and Jesse. Of those soldiers is Fnord’s grandfather, Wesley Williams Jr.] Wes: What the hell are they doing? Simon: Milking a cow. Wes: Get those numbnuts ba— [Out of the corner of his eye, he sees some people deeper in the woods.] Wes: Steady. Hold your fire. [All of the men get ‘battle ready’.] Jesse: Mark and David? Wes: No. They’re scouting east. Jesse: What do we do if they’re— Wes: Nephillim. I don’t know. Shoot, I guess. Jesse: Challenge ‘em, Mark. Mark: Halt! Hands up! Who plays shortstop for the New York Yankees?!? [A voice comes out of the woods.] Voice: I don’t know! Jesse: Maybe they’ll be better on a National League question, Mark. Mark: Who was the National League’s most valuable player last year? [The voice comes again.] Voice: I don’t follow sports and I don’t live in New York. I could challenge you, and ask you to name the author of ‘The Rights of Man.’ Jesse: Buncha college assholes. [The two groups comes together slowly, watching each other closely. The people from deep in the woods are wearing white clothes over their camouflage in order to blend into the snow. The leader of those in white takes his helmet off to show his soft blue eyes.] White Leader: Hey. Good to see some friendly dogfaces. Pete: If you can’t sleep on white sheets, I guess you might as well wear ‘em. Nice camouflage. White Leader: You got that right. Wes: Sorry to bust your hump, but you guys are pretty deep into the ol’ Rhineland. You have orders. White Leader: Sure. [The white leader takes out some papers and hands them to Wes.] White Leader: We’re with the 345th Intelligence and Reconnaissance Patrol. We’ve been probing German positions. Rumor has it they’re running short of petrol. On our way back to command. [Mark walks up next to Wes.] Mark: All check out Wes? Wes: Yep. Fine, Hal. Here. [He hands one of the papers to Mark.] White Leader: Not to bust your hump, but what are you guys doing way out here? Wes: Let’s just say it’s important to the war effort and leave it at that. White Leader: This preoccupations with sports… [The white leader looks to be gripping his gun a little too tight.] White Leader: …weaving it into everything, presuming everyone you meet knows as much as you do. Very annoying. Mark: So’s not knowing anything about baseball. [Out of nowhere, all of the men bring up their guns and start firing at each other. The battle lasts about as long as it started. Bullets even reached one of the cows that Frank and Eddie were trying to milk. When the battle is over, the good guys are on top… or still alive. Pete rushes forward from the back and gets in Wes’ face.] Pete: Are you crazy?!? Those were our own guys! You coulda killed us! Mark: Wesley knew what he was doing. Eddie: Yeah? And what was that, Mark?!? Wes: Killing Germans, Eddie! [The ‘White Leader’ or German Leader sits leaning against a tree. He is at his last breaths.] German Leader: H-how… did you… know…? [Wes walks over to him carrying the identification cards.] Wes: Your forgers figured that being stupid Americans we couldn’t spell the word ‘identification’ right, so you idiots corrected it. Guess what? German Leader: Y-you did… it on p-purpose… Wes: Give the kraut a gold star. German Leader: How… obviously… German… of us… [And he dies. Mark takes off his helmet and lowers his head. Jesse walks over to Frank and Eddie who were trying to get the milk.] Jesse: Hope ya got some ovaltine to go with the milk. Frank: It sucks knowing there’s a Hell now… ‘cause I can’t kill idiots like you. [Hal just stares at the dead bodies.] Hal: These two could see it was me who killed them… they’re still looking… Guess I better start digging. Ground’s pretty hard. Wes: C’mon Hal, you don’t have— Hal: Yeah. Now I do. Want me to bury your guys, Wes? Wes: Um, sure. Okay. [Hal gets out a small shovel and starts to dig at the ground. Mark walks up to Wes.] Mark: We were lucky that they were not part of The Nephillim. Our weapons would have been useless. That’s why our first stop is so important. The next time they might not be just Germans. Wes: How did you know I was doing the right thing? Mark: David and I decided it was best to remain hidden in the bushes and keep our weapons on them. I heard the German talking about the ‘petrol’ rumor. Americans I have met call it ‘gas.’ [Mark walks up to a thin tree and brings out his knife.] Wes: yeah, and what do angels call it? Mark: Rest assured, Wesley, I am no angel. Wes: Whatever you are, we’re all in this together. [Mark leans over and starts to cut the small thin tree down with his knife.] Wes: Whatever that angel did to our minds, learning about things in each of our pasts, Hell, I may not like everybody on this thing we have to do knowing what I know about ‘em now, but I trust them because I know them. You, we know zip about. You gotta level with us. About everything. Mark: Yes. You deserve the truth. All of you. But as you said, Wesley, you know ‘zip’ about me, just as I know ‘zip’ about you. Wes: You know all there is to know. Wife dead. Son alone. Me here. And if there wasn’t a chance of seeing my wife like Sauriel promised— Mark: I don’t remember Sauriel using the word ‘promise.’ If I recall, Sauriel used the word ‘opportunity.’ Wes: Only reason why I’m here. To take it. [Mark cuts the tree completely off and then starts to trim the branches off.] Mark: Can I ask you something? Wes: No. Mark: Why are you so afraid? Wes: ‘Afraid’? Afraid of what? Mark: Life. [There’s an awkward silence between the two men for a few minutes and then Wes walks over to a ledge, looking over a valley.] Wes: Mom died of cancer. I was ten… My pop and me sat by her bed and watched her waste away. Blue a lot of good Sundays kneeling for nothing… But hey, God said: ‘What the hell, I’ll give this kid Wes another two years with his dad… Let them get real close, then make sure a couple of steel girders fall on his father’s head in a construction accident. Wouldn’t want his faith in me to get too strong now.’ I was twelve years old and I found out that there was nowhere, no matter how safe I thought it was, that I could trust… ‘cause in the blink of an eye it’d be gone. Mark: And you blame God for all this sadness in your life? Wes: No. After my father died I stopped blaming God because I realized there wasn’t a God. Mark: And now? Wes: Well, I’d say now I believe in God. But I sure as hell don’t trust him. Mark: And this is the kind of life you exposed your wife and son to, a life of fear and mistrust? You should be ashamed for squandering those years. [Wes turns around to face Mark.] Wes: I should be ashamed?!? If anyone should be ashamed, it’s Him!!! If God really cared about us, this war’d all be over and I’d be home in my wife’s arms. That’s Heaven, my family around me. Not some other place we should be looking forward to when they finally shovel dirt on our faces. [Wes walks closer to Mark, who has finished cutting off all of the limbs and starts to sharpen the edge.] Wes: Some ‘reward’ for time well served. Our reward’s all around us. Now. We’ve been letting all this religious crap get in the way. Finally knowing that there’s a God after all doesn’t change that fact. We’ve let so many things get in the way… I’ve let so many things get in the way… [Mark sharpens the edge of the piece of wooding, making a makeshift spear.] Mark: Think of what awaits us if we can do the job we have been blessed for, of the changes we can make, Wesley. [Wes turns from him and starts to walk back towards their group.] Wes: I’m not gonna go counting my messiahs before they’re hatched, Mark. [Back with the Nephillim and Grigori posing as Germans. They are walking along some train tracks. Nisroch and Zephon take the lead of the group.] Nisroch: At this rate we will never find the Americans, Zephon. We should turn and meet those with The Sword. [Zephon keeps his eyes searching the horizon as Nisroch continues to speak.] Nisroch: What if the archangel planted this nonsense about the ‘sons of light’? Those footprints could simply have belonged to Americans after witnessing the archangel’s crash. Humans. Weak humans who scattered and ran. Zephon: Believing that humans are weak has always been one of your weaknesses, Nisroch. You tend to conveniently forget that you are half human. Nisroch: No, thanks to you, that fact is never far from my mind. Zephon: I am the last of The Grigori, and nothing will get in the way of reclaiming my rightful place and having the offspring of those lost in the flood sit at my side. Nisroch: I am here to serve, Zephon. I have never questioned that. I have only the best interests of The Nephillim in mind when I speak out. Zephon: Then I suggest you all take cover. American plans are approaching. [American plans swoop down close to the ‘troops’ and fire at them. Bullet wounds flutter through all of The Nephillim and it doesn’t seem to bother them. One of The Nephillim picks up a limb that was shot off and reattaches it just by placing it back in place.] Random Nephillim: They’re turning for another run! [Zephon takes off his coat and his hat. He starts to rapidly climb a tree at superhuman speed.] American Pilot1: Let loose with the fifties again. Make sure they don’t get too cozy hidin’ behind that tree line. American Pilot2: Roger that! [They go flying in again firing away.] American Pilot1: Okay, let’s take another pass and drop our last two hundred-pounders on their sauerkraut asses! [All of a sudden, a thump on his plane startles the first American pilot.] American Pilot2: What the hell was that? American Pilot1: Think I caught some ground fire! [Another thump.] American Pilot1: Damn! Caught another one! How the— American Pilot2: Throttle down; I’m swinging by for a look-see! You’re not gonna believe this… There’s a goddamn kraut on your back! [Zephon is holding onto the back of the plane. He is tearing the plane apart little by little with his hands as he crawls closer to the cockpit.] American Pilot1: What?!? How the hell--?!? American Pilot2: I don’t know! All I know is he’s going for the cockpit! American Pilot1: Open up on him with the fifties, for god sakes! American Pilot2: He’s too close! I’ll hit you both! [Zephon grabs the glass hood of the cockpit and rips it off the plane.] American Pilot2: Bail out! Now! Before he— American Pilot1: Jesus!!! Zephon: You know… I almost forgot how beautiful it is up here. American Pilot1: W-what are y-you? Zephon: Your end. American Pilot2: NOOO! [Zephon breaks the pilot’s neck and throws him out of the cockpit. Zephon gets in the cockpit and takes control. He fires on the other American plan, riddling it with bullets. The other American’s plane explodes and Zephon takes off straight up into the air. On the ground, the other Nephillim start to get worried.] Nephillim1: Where is he going? Nephillim2: He’s forsaken us! Nephillim3: No… Nisroch: I think he just needs a reminder of how high he used to fly. [In the plane, Zephon is getting higher and higher. As he gets nearer and nearer to the sun, he takes off his goggles.] Zephon: I told you… I would return… one last time... [After a short time, Zephon comes zooming down flying right over the heads of the other Nephillim. He lands a small distance in front of them.] Zephon: It would be a shame not to make use of this wonderful American plane. Who was it that came along with me to suffer though that gasbag Goring’s tour of his almighty Luftwaffe? [One of the Nephillim steps forward. He is Balel.] Balel: It was I, Balel. At Lieutenant Colonel Mayer’s Jagdgeschwader 2 Unit. He allowed us to train on the new Messerschmitt 109. Zephon: You must find an American airfield. Your new plane needs a great deal of ammunition. I would also suggest refueling, of course. Balel: Of course. [Zephon walks over to Nisroch and puts his arm around his shoulder as Balel takes off with the plane. He actually looks happy.] Zephon: Nisroch, you were correct. I’ve allowed myself to be distracted. We will acquire some half-tracks, American or German, and proceed directly to the flame. [Back with the Americans. Hal is still digging the graves as the others just take a rest and wait.] Simon: Wish Hal’d let us help him. David: We tried. He feels he killed them, he buries them. If that helps him sleep, then screw it. Maybe we should all start burying every guy we kill. [Eddie flips a rock onto the frozen skull of one of the dead Germans.] Frank: Will you cut that crap out? Eddie: He can’t feel nothing anymore. Frank: After all you’ve seen lately, you sure about that? Maybe he’ll be waiting to kick your ass once you beg your way into heaven. Let the kraut rest in peace. Eddie: You mean pieces. [Simon goes to put away the comic book he was reading and notices something.] Simon: Hey! I’m missing my ‘All-Star Comics’ number 11! Frank: Was that the one with that hawkguy swinging a mace at a bunch of Japs on the front? Simon: Yeah. [Frank lights his cigarette.] Frank: ran outta toilet paper few miles back. Leaves were too soggy. Sorry. Good story, liked that bit with the starguy who zapped those Jap zeros. [Simon lunges at Frank.] Simon: You asshole! [Before they can start rumbling, David gets up and separates them.] David: C’mon, cut this crap out! [Simon walks away and then David leans in close to Frank.] David: Don’t touch his goddamn comics again, Frank. You do, I’ll make sure you also use ‘em to wipe your face. [Mark and Wes come back from the overlook and Mark puts his hand on Hal’s shoulder.] Mark: Finish this later. [Hal does so and climbs out of the hole as Mark goes to speak to the crowd.] Mark: It is time to tell you why I am here. I ask you to remain silent until I finish what I need to… have to tell you. My name is Marcus Longinus. I was a Roman Centurion. [Mark goes into his satchel and pulls out what looks to be a blood-coated end to a very old spear. He takes it and puts it on the sharpened end of the wooden spear he made.] Mark: This iron spearhead is what I used to pierce Jesus’ body. Eddie: Well yeah, that figures. ‘Course you did. Mark: The Holy Blood that stains it calls out to me each minute, of each hour, of each day. It reminds me why I have walked this Earth for so long… and why I must continue to do so until he finally decides that I have atoned for my deed… It began for me with orders from Pontius Pilate… [Another flashback, but the bloody details will be left out at this time.] It was just another one of many dirty jobs that I had grown cold to. A prisoner put on trial and punished for his crimes against Rome—I had lost count of how many men I had helped put to death. The Nazarene shook as each lash ripped away at him. Of the hundreds of flagellations I took part in, all of them uttered piercing screams. Most passed out quickly, some died… But he did not cry out. Not a sound, not a whimper. Only the noise of the lash filled the praetorium. Our orders were to insure that Jesus arrived alive at the mount and suffered as much pain as possible. Crucifixion is not about bleeding to death or exposure… I learned that by positioning someone with their arms outstretched and higher than the rest of the body, it squeezes all the internal organs, makes it almost impossible for the lungs to expand… The Nazarene’s effort was mind-boggling. He was like a person drowning, coming up for air only to strangle with each breath. This ‘drowning’ on the cross lasted for three endless hours… The other centurions went about breaking the two thieves’ legs… Because if the thieves had no way to push themselves up for breath, they would die quicker from suffocation… And our long day would be over. The mutilation of the other two repulsed me. For some reason I wanted to protect the Nazarene from those hideous blows. I stepped forward with my spear… And pierced the right side of his chest between the fourth and fifth ribs. My right eye, useless thanks to a battle wound, was now as it once was. Perfect. He healed me even though I helped kill him. His blood across my eyes made me whole again. From that moment on, my life was not my own… [Back to the real world, Mark is holding the spear with both hands to show everyone.] Mark: I went home. Did not eat. Did not wash the blood and dirt from my body. I would let it mark me until I was shown the way. I held the spear. My wife could not pry it from my fingers. I sat in the shadows and waited for days. [All of the American soldiers look freaked out.] Mark: Then I saw the light. It told me that I must walk the earth and do God’s bidding. I would never rest until The Grigori and The Nephillim were no longer a threat to mankind. This would be the punishment for my deed. Eternal rest would be forever denied to me. I would never see my loved ones again until I wiped the earth of the last Grigori. [Wes looks over to him.] Wes: Nice to meet you… [Wes smiles as he places a hand on Mark’s shoulder.] Wes: …Marcus Longinus. [Garrett stops with the story and leans back in his seat. Fnord looks up at him.] Fnord: You can’t stop there. I need to know more. I need to know the whole story! Garrett: I can’t tell you the whole story at once, if I was to do that, it would be too much for you to handle. Take what I gave you and use it to help you though decisions I see you troubled with. Remember, you have but one purpose… [Fnord jumps to his feet.] Fnord: What the fu— [Garrett cuts him off.] Garrett: I have a lot on my mind right now. You aren’t my only business here in 4WW. We will talk after tonight’s show. Now, go get ready for tonight. [Fnord, with his jaw dropped in shock and still a tad confused walks to the door and leaves. Garrett shuts the door behind him. Fnord walks down the hall and down to the KnightMare Brigade locker room. He walks in and, being that it’s very early, he is the only one there. He looks up to the sky as if searching for answers to questions. Suddenly, out of nowhere, a bright blue light shines in the mirror. Fnord covers his eyes, but looks through his fingers to try to see. Something within the light speaks.] Light: At the end, you will see the truth. When your troubles are over, you will see what your plan is. Until then, keep searching… [With a bright flash, the bright light goes away. Fnord looks around the room, but sees no one around and nothing out of place.] Fnord: I think I might be becoming crazy again… Aw, shit… You know what’s going to happen at the PTC IPPV Survival Match? 4WW is going to win. There is nothing anyone can do to stop us. We are the four most dominant men in the wrestling world today. I bash my teammates because we don’t get along, but I do respect them. Jerms IS the most technical wrestler I have ever seen. Mike Biddle knows the ring like he knows the back of his hand. Darek DeAngelo, only shows a minor fraction of the power that lays inside him. And then there’s me. I’m the wild card. I’m the guy that doesn’t care about his body or anyone else’s for that matter. I’m the guy that will die before I lose this match. We make the perfect team. With the four of us on one side of the ring, the winners have already been declared. 4WW Survival Champions. And with this win will come respect. Respect from those who don’t deserve it back. They will respect us. They will fear us. They will loath us. And they will want to be us. We are 4WW. We are the most dominant federation in PTC. We’ve hidden in the background for way too long. It’s our time to shine now. It’s our time to show not only PTC, but also the whole world what we are made of. Blood… Guts… Glory… It’s all there. Core, you will feel how much of an impact we can make first. Then it’s down to the other teams. Whoever these other teams may be… It doesn’t matter. They will lose to us. They will bleed and be in more pain than they’ve ever felt. 4WW will SURVIVE.
