Over-Achievin' Ain't Easy

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-22 Round 4 of the GTT4. “Hollywood North” Dallas Reeves would be the first one to admit that he didn’t expect to make it this far. No one, himself included, could foresee the string of victories Reeves has managed to rack up in this tournament thus far. The likes of Scott Vyper, Kenny Mercury and the previously Unstoppable Eugene(~!) have been ousted from the GTT4 by the upstart from the NGW. Now Dallas Reeves faces yet another formidable foe…the leader of the WWA’s Devil’s Own…Dave Harley. A brutal man, with a sharp mind and a heavy hand, Harley has spread his special brand of chaos and terror throughout the corridors of Primetime Central. Indeed, his performance in the GTT4 has turned some heads that hadn’t previously been turned, and has even impressed the tricky to impress Reeves. But Dave Harley may soon find out what Kenny Mercury found out…just because Dallas Reeves comes from the NGW, it doesn’t make him a pushover. Nay, Dallas Reeves seems to be quite the overachiever. Although Reeves isn’t among the upper echelons of the talented performers within the confines of Primetime Central, he has been making the most of his success as of late. His home federation of the NGW is set to change hands after the next pay-per-view blowout, and Reeves has been taking this down time to focus all of his energies on the GTT4. Well, perhaps not all of his energies. He’s kept himself busy apart from his wrestling duties. As I say, he does seem to be quite the overachiever. The scene opens in a dusty yard, with a small, rugged looking abode just behind. The sun beats down hard, and a slight breeze stirs up some of the sand. Scattered in the yard are several cacti, and a few shot up cowboys, too. Horses are heard whinnying off camera. Kneeling front and centre, his back to the camera, is a man, hands on head. His blue jeans and dark poncho are caked with blood, sweat and dirt. He has a belt with empty holsters around his waist, and his tan cowboy hat lays on the ground to the right of him. His long curly blond hair is a mere plaything for the wind, blowing about his head with reckless abandon. A dirty, sleazy looking cowboy leans in close, with two equally sleazy lookin’ gents keeping focused on the kneeling man, hands on their holsters. Cowboy: Folks ‘round here are getting’ mighty tired of seein’ your ugly face. Ya keep gettin’ in tha way of what’s best for the town. Ya’ve been carryin’ on like ya run the place, takin’ out some of our best men in the process. Ya’ve done enough damage. We’re here to put a stop to it…put a stop to you. The three cowpokes begin to laugh, an evil laugh reminiscent of baddies that are about to get shot up real good in those old spaghetti westerns. The main cowboy turns his attention back to the kneeling blond man, and slowly draws his weapon. He levels the gun towards the man’s face, smiling a semi-toothy grin, what few teeth are in his mouth covered in tabacky juice. Cowboy: Any last requests, over-achievin’ stranger? The stranger on his knees pauses a moment, then speaks real calm-like, without a waiver in his voice. Over-Achievin’ Stranger: If I’m gonna die, I wanna die with my hat on. The cowpokes begin to laugh again. Cowboy: Oh, yer gonna die, stranger! Make no mistake about that! Over-Achievin’ Stranger: I wasn’t. That’s why I want my hat. Cowboy: ‘Cause I’m gonna shoot ya! Over-Achievin’ Stranger: Yeah, I gathered. Cowboy: Shoot ya ta death! O.-A. Stranger: Right. Cowboy: I’m gonna shoot ya, an’ yer gonna- Annoyed beyond belief, the stranger can’t hold his temper any more, lashing out. O.-A. Stranger: Can I put my effin’ hat on or what!? The cowboy’s smile disappears, and he cocks the hammer on his antique Colt. He motions with his gun for the stranger to reach for his hat. Cowboy: Slowly now… O.-A. Stranger: Slowly? Or what? You’ll shoot me? Cowboy: Darn tootin’! O.-A. Stranger: Well then, I wouldn’t want your shooting me to get in the way of your shooting me, now would I? Cowboy: Jus’ get your hat already… The stranger reaches his left hand across his body, and grasps the top of his tan hat. He slowly begins to lift it off the ground, being careful to only raise the brim that isn’t facing the three cowboys. Beneath the hat, we can see a handgun hidden. Clint Eastwood cowboy whistle. With lightning quick movements, the over-achievin’ stranger uses his left hand to place his hat atop his head, and his right hand to pick the gun up and fire a round into the unsuspecting lead cowboy. His face turns to shock, then his body hits the ground, a plume of dust rising into the air as it lands. The other two cowboys draw their weapons, but nobody fires a shot. The stranger on his knees glares from one cowboy to the other and back. Goofy Cowboy: He can’t get tha both of- Bang! He grabs his chest, then falls forward, wasting a round into the air. The lone remaining cowboy has a look of sheer fright cross his gaping maw. Bang! Bang! The last dirty cowboy crumples back in a heap, and slams against the wall of the tiny house. He slumps against the wall, then slowly his body slides down, leaving a red spatter on the wall. Director: Cut and print ! Great work guys! That’s a wrap for the day! The stranger gets to his feet, as the previously shot and killed cowboys do as well. They shake hands with the stranger, who finally turns around. We can now see the smiling face of the clean cut “Hollywood North” Dallas Reeves, as a production assistant hands him a bottle of imported water. He spins the antique handgun around in his hand, then shoves it into the empty holster at his side. He peels the dark and soaked poncho off of his athletic body, and hands it off to the same p.a. before being joined by the middle-aged director. Director: I tell ya Dallas…you make a heck of an over-achieving stranger! Dallas: Thanks. I’ve been practicing. Reeves winks at the camera, then walks off towards his trailer. The camera shoots him straight on as he walks. Dallas Reeves’ foray into acting had made for difficult scheduling tasks, what with his run as the NGW American Champ taking up a lot of his time. Rather than wrestling at the final PPV of owner Scott W.’s run with the federation, Reeves instead opted to film this guest spot on a television show that can not be mentioned, due to contractual stipulations. Now, his shoot done, he leaves this location for the quasi-friendly atmosphere of Washington, DC, and looks forward to his match up versus “The Extreme Machine” Dave Harley in the Pyrenees Bracket Semi-Final. Dallas: Okay, so the script could use some work, but am I going to complain? Hell no. Do you want to know why? Reeves is stopped by a couple of crewmembers, each brandishing sharpies and glossy photos of the NGWer. Without hesitation, Reeves signs them, then shakes the hands of the crewmembers. He then continues to walk on, past the cameras, past the craft services tables, and past the production vehicles. Dallas: Because my performance in the Golden Turnbuckle has given my, for lack of a better word, stagnant acting resume a jolt. I’ve had more gigs thrown at me than I can handle, even with my non-existent NGW tour schedule. But this weekend…oh, this weekend it’s all about the big match-up against Dave Harley. Whoever wins goes on to face Tarrant in the Bracket Final. Many out there had me counted out way back in the second round. But I persisted, stepped it up a notch, and managed to drop the almighty and heavily-favoured Kenny Mercury. Reeves polishes off the remainder of his imported water bottle, and tosses it into a nearby recycling receptical. He stops, takes his cowboy hat off, and wipes the sweat off his brow with his forearm. Dallas: In the third round, I faced the colossal man-child Unstoppable Eugene(~!). Again, the naysayers nayed, figuring that my victory over Mercury was just a fluke. But I showed them, for the second round in a row. Eugene’s ‘tard rage could not power him by me, and I stopped the Unstoppable. So that brings me to you, Dave. You’ve got it into your head that me and your middle aged secretary may have a little thing goin’. I’ll tell ya what. I’ve just finished filming in the deserts of Nevada…so you can put two and two together. Vegas ain’t too far away, no. But alas, Mr. Extreme Machine, my heart belongs to another as it is. She said “yes”. Reeves holds his hand up to the camera, and he appears to be wearing a wedding band, something that is very much new to his hand. Dallas: Not too much longer, Dave. Devil’s Own be damned, they can’t help you now. I’m a fan of the WWA, and if its owner would return my calls, perhaps we may come face to face again in the future. However, all I’m concerned about right now is the GTT4. It is my only wrestling engagement on the agenda. I’m the black sheep, the longshot, the star-killer…and I’m your next opponent. I’ve beaten Mercury, I’ve beaten Eugene. You may be the “Extreme Machine”, but, as you’ll soon find out, I’m the “Over-Achieving Machine”. If and when I beat you, maybe then I’ll be seen as a legitimate threat around the hallowed halls of Primetime Central. If not, well, I’ll just keep on truckin’. Reeves arrives at a relatively new camper, his trailer/dressing room. In front of it, his hulking and wooly bodyguard Beastor Brandt sits making desert sand castles. He looks up at his charge and smiles, before Reeves disappears within the trailer. The camera fades to black, with the words “REEVES vs HARLEY” appearing in gold lettering, then fading out as well. This promo has been released through Dudesy Productions. MMIV. Note: No cowboys were harmed during the making of this promo.
