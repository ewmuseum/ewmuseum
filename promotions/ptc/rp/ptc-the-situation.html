The Situation

30 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There was actually a time in Tyler Houston’s life when he was not a jerk or conceited, or egotistical. There was a time when he was a nice good who was very humble and always tried to help others. That was a long time ago and Tyler Houston has long forgotten those days. He has forgotten them because of the abuse he took when he was younger for being a so-called nerd and he never stood up for himself until he chose his career as a professional wrestling. It still makes him angry, at himself, at everybody when he thinks back to the days when he got bullied and got his lunch money taken from him in high school. “I only have five dollars…” A fourteen year old said in his voice, which is still developing. “I know you got more than that, dog.” A dark skin boy said. His speech was slurred because he has been into drugs for a few years and he has been into heavy drugs like cocaine, speed, and LSD for a year or so. His voice makes it sound like he is a tough kid who thinks he runs the school but he has not met his maker yet. “Sorry man, but I this is all I have.” The fourteen year old, which was a younger version of Tyler Houston, said with a voice that was getting a little desperate. He wanted this moment to end and he wanted to make sure none of his friends saw he was getting bullied. This same scene has happened every day since he walked into high school. Tyler thought that only if this kid would go away that he would have a great life. “Aight dog, I betta get five tomorrow.” The bully walked away. Tyler sighed with relief as the kid walked away. As he walked away Tyler started to fill with anger but he just started to walk to his next class. He met up with his friend, Sarah but when he talked to her he acted as if nothing had ever happened and he never got bullied. “Do you have a pencil I could borrow?” Sarah said in a high-pitched voice just like the other girls. She had a great body; nice breasts and a big butt. Tyler could not help but to stare at them whenever she walked away but he could not make a move at her because she had a boyfriend. He would have mad a move if he did not know her boyfriend but he lived down the street from him and they were friends. “Nope, sorry.” He said with a huge smile on his face. He thought that she knew that he was interested in her and she knew why he was not making a move. They were best friends so it was complicated. Tyler thought that if he became more than friends that the great relationship they had as friends would end if they broke up. Then again, he also had another girl named Jess who was extremely beautiful that he messed around with in school. He did not want that relationship to become known but it did. This relationship spread around faster than an STD in a brothel. “Crap! We need them for mid-terms and I can’t find one!” Sarah said. Tyler was really into her now and he couldn’t get his eyes off of her breasts. “My eyes are up here.” She lifted his hands with her hand and he began to smile, she laughed. “You’re no help,” she said sarcastically. “Bye Sarah!” Tyler yelled down the hallway with a huge smile on his face and she turned around smiling. He could not help but to stare at her butt because, to be blunt, it was perfect. He continued to walk down the hallway giving handshakes to various friends and saying hi to people he was acquaintances with. Tyler went along with the day not thinking about him until lunch. Tyler sat at a table with his friends that were football players. The table was wooden and was right near a wall so nobody would be able to walk behind it. Tyler sat here so that kid could not see him. He always scanned the lunchroom to find out where he was incase he wanted to get up and say hi to his other friends. He had nothing else to do during lunch because the kid had taken five dollars from him, which was his lunch money thus; he had no money left to buy anything. His mother gave him three dollars for lunch and he provided two dollars of his own to equal it out to five dollars. It was a tough situation and it caused him a lot of stress. He knew he was not much of a fighter but one of the reasons he would not get into a fight was because he was in a group called the Anti-Violence Coalition. He was also on the baseball team and in student government. Tyler thought to himself that it would not be a great influence or impression if he got into a fight. He knew he would lose and he would probably get kicked out of the AVC (anti-violence coalition), suspended from the baseball team and also kicked out of student government. It was a horrible thing especially for a fourteen year old. He wondered, with all of the expulsions how come this kid was not caught? He knew he did drugs and how come no teachers could tell? He always smelled like smoke and he always seemed to be high off of something. It could have been fate and Tyler could not help but think of this every night. He decided the best way to deal with this situation was to give him the money he wanted and by next year he would not see the kid at all. Tyler would be in the smarter classes and the kid would be in the not so smart classes. He could not help but second-guess himself all the time. The five dollars always went to the bully though. His mother did not know that he never ate lunch and neither did his dad or his brothers. It was something that he wanted to tell them but he could never get the balls to do it because he would be thought of as a wimp by his brothers and would let down his parents. He had to come to some kind of decision soon or he could just let this ride out. Either way, something had to happen and a decision had to be made. Tyler had to take the initiative.
