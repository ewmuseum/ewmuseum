[IG26] "The New Shit" Bobino - That Little Something

5 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“This isn't over...” A voice spoke over a tape playing in slow motion, showing Vivica J. Valentine barely pulling herself over Bobino to eliminate him from his chance at the Extreme Title. She pulled herself to her feet, astounded she was able to survive long enough to get the victory. “The facts are clear. You've never beaten me on your own.” The tape rewinds quickly, showing Bobino wrenching on Valentine's back, and her hand in the air, only moments from taping out. Bobino leans back, and closes his eyes, putting everything he's got into the hold. He yells out, not from the stress of the hold, but from the solid piece of steel being wrapped around his skull by the Extreme Champion, Archibald MacGreggor. “If it weren't for him... you were gone. You need help at every turn. I am independent.” Random clips of GCW matches flash by the screen, Shaman hitting the Desert Spike, Blackeagle hitting an Asia Moonsault, and finally, Vivica's hand tapping the mat as Virgil nearly snaps her in half. “You're an embarrassment. Not only to your 'friends' in GCW, but the entirety of wrestling. You're nothing but a leech. You latch onto anything that looks successful, and suck it for all it's worth. You earn nothing.” The scene changes to Garbage Bag Johnny, swing Dusk down with the Tragically Hipbuster, and making the academic pin. “Dusk. So young, so naive, so pathetic. You were man handled by a filthy, disgusting hobo, and then easily pinned by him.” The screen fades into Dusk, getting his revenge smacking a superkick under the jaw of his failed tag tam partner. Then scurrying down to make the cover. “You have the heart to get what you want done... you just don't have the direction. Obviously, you could beat that man, and get 'revenge', but was it worth it? Did he really cost you the match? Or could you have spent that week, eliminating the carelessness that got you beaten in the first place?” The camera pulls back, revealing our narrator to the very few people who hadn't figured it out. He stands next to the monitor, his black trench coat less than an inch from the ground, and his sadistic grin telling the story of confidence across his face. “Both of you are embarrassing, pathetic, and simply too weak to contend. You refuse to progress, and eliminate what drags you down, and thus, you shall perish. I refuse to tolerate anybody that cannot cope with weaknesses within themselves as any kind of 'champion' and I refuse to lose to them. I'm taking this match from you, because I feel it's time to bring respect back to wrestling, and to Primetime Central. It cannot be done by anybody like you. People that are too weak to survive on their own, or too foolish to properly manage their efforts.” Bobino lifts the remote, pointing it at the screen, and hitting a button. It illuminates to a picture of our narrator, Bobino, destroying his FUSE-Wrestling co-worker, Damien Cruz with a chair, allowing him to be pinned by Red Rock in the PTC Vs Archibald MacGreggor I-PPV . “You have to have no emotions, you have to be heartless and have no friends to truly evolve. You need to let everything go, and become something that most people hate. You need to be able to handle the pressure of being a champion.” The screen fast forwards to a still shot of Vivica Valentine, grasping the PTC Extreme Title, openly weeping. “Unlike this waste of human flesh you see before you. Look at her. She's crying, at success. She can't even believe herself that she did something right. She's showing her weakness, she's showing emotion.” CR-AAAAACK!! A Steel chair destroys Valentine's face, causing her to fall lifelessly to the mat, as her “destiny' slips away. “That's what happens when you let the moment get to you, when you let emotions take over. Valentine, until you can achieve anything on your own, and cure your obvious weaknesses, you're nothing more than a nuisance.” The screen fades in on a picture of Dusk, microphone in hand, pointing to something off-screen. The anger in his eyes almost illuminates his entire face. “Dusk, on the other hand, I know relatively little about. I know that he's angry, I know that he's mis-guided, but I know not of what his hopes, his dreams, nor his future is. Dusk, is the pathetic wildcard that to a lesser person could be just a big enough surprise to take this match from an unprepared man. I, however, am prepared. I know that Valentine is nothing, and that you are the only one I need be concerned about.” Bobino flips the monitor off, as he steps closer to the camera, his face looking into our living rooms, as he speaks. “I can show you all the videos and pictures I want. It's nothing more than a visual aide to what's going to happen once two people that indulge and ignore their weaknesses takes on a man that has done nothing but searching for his own weaknesses, for the purpose of destroying each and every one of them. Dusk, Valentine, neither of you have what it takes to hold that championship, not yet. I'm going to take it from you, and bring meaning back to wrestling and the championships here, not because you're both pathetic, not because I've figured as much as I possibly can about you both, and not even because I'm more a wrestler and competitor than either of you could ever dream of. No, I'm walking away with the belt, simply because... Bobino smirks as he walks off camera, and the scene fades to red smoke, with one last line lingering in our ears. “This -IS- The New Shit.”
