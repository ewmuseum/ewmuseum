One by one The Corporation is going down (XWF Title #1)

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
C.R. Affiliated Pimp Joined: 06 Feb 2003 Posts: 4 Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 3:09 am Post subject: One by one The Corporation is going down (XWF Title #1) (The scene opens at the breakfast bar at a Holiday Inn in San Antonio, Texas. It's quite late on Tuesday morning, with most of the customers of the hotel having already been and had breakfast, before moving on. However, there is one man left dining in the bar, with a plate with a couple more pancakes left on them sitting and contemplating as he eats. The man is obviously C.R., the XWF World Heavyweight champion. The cameraman then moves around another table to get closer, knocking a chair to accidentally catch C.R.'s attention. C.R. turns around to see what the commotion is, but a smile breaks out across his face as he tells the man to...) C.R.: Hold up a.... just let me finish my breakfast, and then I'll speak to you guys all you want. (C.R. then turns back to his plate and finishes the last remnants of his breakfast. Before pushing it in front of him and then turning his chair around so that he faced the camera. The World Champion made sure to lean forward slightly, resting his elbows on his knees and looking at the camera as if he had something up his sleeve....) C.R.: Well it looks like Limp Bizkit got half of want he wanted. He did beat me in the middle of the ring, a long time ago. But he didn't get my title. See Limp Bizkit you will never get near my title and neither will anyone else. I will hunt you down and kick your a$$ if you get near it. I know it is safe at the Photo Shoot because I know my brother is making sure that it is ok. And that is P.R. (C.R. smiles some more before carrying on.) C.R.: That's enough of talking about a piece of sick sh!t. Now moving on to The Corporation.... See Corporation, I don't know you and to tell you the truth, I don't care about you guys. I will continue running over all of you until I get Asesino at Payback. That's all there is to it. You guys are about the same number as P.R., and me so we are all about even because you maybe around 8 members but compare to us its like 2 on 2. So it should be a very good war. But like I said we will still win. This is how the war is going to end. I'm going to hit Asesino, the so-called Killer with the Rigido Stunner. And we win. That is what's going to happen. And there is nothing you guys can do about it. So Corporation you can't shake the shock because we are The Rigido Brothers!!!!!!!! (C.R. takes a sip of his drink as the camera fades to black.) (The scene opens in a park, somewhere in Little Rock, AR. The camera looks out across the park, to see a few kids playing in a playground. They ran about in their innocent way, with the boys chasing the girls, and the girls giggling with delight. It then pans across to show a man sitting on a park bench, looking pretty laid back. The man is C.R. He has his hair tied back behind his hair, and wears a ' C.R.' t-shirt, as well as some Adidas jogging pants. C.R. watches the children play caught up in his thoughts at the same time. The camera moves closer, and suddenly C.R.'s thoughts are broken. He turns to face the camera and his trademark smile is there to greet the viewers. He wastes no time in speaking.) C.R.: It looks like I may lose my title but I'm still on top of my game. Everyone still wants a match with yours truly, C.R. From Limp Bizkit, La Envidia Mata and everyone knows that Asesino still wants a match with the true champion. See Asesino you are getting old, pretty quick. Yea, you might be the number one contender but you still suck. I mean come on... You’re getting your Corporation friends in the action to try to soften me up for Payback but it won’t be enough for you to get my title at Payback. And you know it's true... (C.R. shakes his head in disgust, as he leans forwards to get closer to the camera.) C.R.: So I thin.. No, I know I will win.... I WILL get back on track and I WILL prove that C.R. is more than just an arrogant son of a bitch. I once said that not only I could talk a good fight, but I could fight a good fight as well! Well, here's where I prove that. Here's where I make a mark for myself. Here's where I continue to climb the ladder of success.... And Asesino one way or another, I will get you at Payback. That's a promise... And it also looks like Charles Stevenson is going to stir things up a little. He put me in a match against Limp Bizkit and guess who is Limp Bizkit he is Asesino’s Xtreme Fusion partner. Non-other then the sick team, themselves.... Limp Bizkit, I know you don’t want this match right down the middle. But I have one thing to tell you. If you get in my way, I will kick your A$$ all over the arena. I don't care if I lose that match. I don't care if I lose to that piece of crap, rock star want to be, Limp Bizkit. I just want to get my hands around your neck, Asesino! (C.R. lets out a sigh, before leaning back again, resting against the bench as if he can't hold his own weight.) C.R.: So Asesino and Limp Bizkit I will prove everyone wrong and I WILL win retain the XWF World Heavyweight Title! (With that, C.R. turns to face the camera one last time, finally flashing that trademark smile. He then puts his hands in his pockets and walks off up the path with his back to the camera, as the screen slowly fades to black, with the innocent screams and laughter of the children echoing in the background....) _________________ XWF Xtreme Champion January's Best Wrestler Award
