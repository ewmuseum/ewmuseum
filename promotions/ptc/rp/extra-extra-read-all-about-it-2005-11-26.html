Date: 2005-11-26 The Aurora Post November 26, 2005 Written by the Masked Slammer Every sport has its season and everyone adjusts to it. You don’t see much football fanfare during the summer months, and you don’t see much baseball fanfare during the winter months. And while all of those sports, and more, lose a bit of interest during their off seasons they are still on the news. But there is one sport that is only whispered in the ears of the truly faithful. There are no such things as casual fans here. Others may come and forget, but these will always remember it. And soon, as the winter months kick in, the roar of that sport beckons loudly, and soon the whole world is fixated yet. If you don’t know what I’m talking about, I suggest you take a look around. That’s right folks. I’m talking about the GTT5. Originally, it was criticized by critics as being nothing but another poorly organized amateur fighting tournament. Yet, over the years the GTT has become more and more profitable, enjoyable, intriguing, and exciting. It has opened the way for a thousand athletes, and that only hides the real story of the GTT saga. Forget about the WWE, the TNA, ROH. Hell, forget about Ultimate Fighting, the Contender, or any show you think is out there that details fighting. They all may be on the television set year-round and the GTT may only be here during the winter months, but none of them can compare to the amount of story and competition that the GTT brings. Since its pinnacle season in GTT3, the GTT has boasted hundred of the top fighters in the world. And for whatever reason, those fighters remain etched in the minds of fans alike. This year the GTT starts a bit late, but it’s going to be as big as ever. Take a look at the amount of names released at press time and you get a feeling that there is no sure bet in this thing to win it all. There are a dozen names you could look at and think "this guy would win it all" and that alone testifies the amount of competition in it. You look at fighters like Spartan, Deville, Bong, Rollins, Shadow, and McFarlane, and you still cannot decide who to put your money on. But while most of the attention is on such people such as the aforementioned, I remind you that this year the tournament is LOADED. There is no favorite. My case in point brings me to our city’s recently arrived Dr. Colin Gilchrist. Dr. Gilchrist is legendary among the science community and brings a sort of celebrity status with him wherever he goes. And while I for one hope that his presence here is more along the lines of working with our doctors, we cannot overlook one of the most unlikely contestants in this year’s GTT. Scientist, doctor, and researcher aside, Dr. Gilchrist proved that he can compete in the world’s most brutal and demanding competition by outlasting nearly ten men in a qualification match. If there is anyone in this tournament who can make an impact, it’s Dr. Gilchrist. Already proven that he’s as capable as anyone, he now seeks to win the entire thing. As the city’s lone entrant into this year’s GTT, the city must stand behind the good doctor. Dr. Gilchrist has bought to this city a kind of attention and aura it never had before. The city now remains in the news of nearly every major media coverage, and for the first time it isn’t for any tabloid stories. Dr. Gilchrist brings in a sense of hope for our science community, and as a competitor in the GTT5, brings us a sense of intrigue. Dr. Gilchrist will be competing and I assure you he will be domineering. But even with his impressive fighting techniques, and even with him being a favorite in his bracket, we still need to stand by him. Colin Gilchrist is one of the best fighters in this thing and would advance far with or without our cheering. But if we stand by him, we would as a city finally get our attention and our motivation. Dr. Gilchrist has come to Aurora to represent us in the GTT5, and as citizens we owe him the distinction of unbiased fanfare. So, go out and buy tickets, order it on PPV, do something, because this year the GTT is bigger than ever. One hundred twenty-eight of the best fighters in the world have gathered to compete and find out who the best is. By the time it ends, we will all know that, that distinction belongs to Dr. Colin Gilchrist.
