Prom Night

Date: 2005-12-05 "So, This is Her? I wouldn't even bother showing up, It's obvious you have no chance!" She was young. Her youthful features and flowing brown hair made Jason Warr reminisce about countless women he'd become acquainted with in the past. Her quirky good looks and determined expression reminded him of endless beauties he'd seen in or around the ring during his career. There was, However, one defining feature amongst the mass of conformity that stood before him. Her Eyes. They were standing on the corner of 9th and Broadway. Their personal decor blended with the freezing temperatures outside, as both were bundled in heavy jackets. They Stood staring at a brick wall, which had a poster plastered to it's durable side. Featured was GTT5 Competitor Amy Silveira. She posed in a common 'Battle' Stance. Jason Warr and long time significant other Angela Torchev had come across the portrait on their way back from breakfast. At first the two had shared a laugh at the posters un-authentic appearance. Frayed edges and various graffiti made the scene even more entertaining. Ever the skeptic, Torchev begin laying into her with the kind of vigorous sarcasm only a woman can muster. Warr however, studied the photograph. She looked Average, normal. Nondescript. To him, anyway. "Whats with you? Do I have something to worry about here?" said Torchev. She brushed her fiery red hair to the side revealing a mischievous smile. Warr was caught off guard. "Hmpgh? No, of course not. I've just never seen her before." He said. "Looks a little young to be wrest-" He cut himself off as his stare wandered to her eyes. I've Seen Those Eyes Before "So What if you've never seen her before. She's a girl, You can't beat an 'ickle girl?" She said. They resumed their walk down the street, Torchev consumed in her enthralling baby's voice impression, and Warr deep in thought. After a few more seconds consideration, he'd dismissed the theory. "Oh Wait, I get it, you can't HIT a woman! Muahahaha I knew it, you're just too chival." He responded to the accusation with a playful blow to her arm. "Abuse!" she echoed as he placed his arm around her waist and they continued their jaunt. "I wouldn't undersell the ladies, M'dear," he said. "Women have a strange effect on men, make them do strange things. I've seen it first hand." He didn't even get three more steps in before it hit him. ============ May 23rd, 2001. 003 Nights Until Senior Prom. She flipped through the air with Grace and Precision. As Captain of the [detail withheld] High School Cheerleading team, Sarah Miller demanded flawlessness, not just from herself but from her squad. Her team were two time State Champions, both years under her military like leadership. Even Though her last game was only one night away, She went through her heats just like any other time. As usual, she'd gathered quite a crowd of male spectators, but with chestnut hair other girls would kill for, fantastic features, sparkling girlish eyes, and a large chest to boot, she'd become accustomed to the attention from a young age, and it no longer bothered her. Four Young Men stood about 100 feet away, under the shade of an old oak tree. Two of them enjoyed the 'view'. One of them Didn't care to pay attention to a girl he considered 'beneath' him, and the last just stared. Transfixed with her beauty and skill, he'd often found his thoughts drifting towards Sarah during long sleepless nights. She was his obsession. This was Jason Warr's crew. Warr stood under the tree, cracking jokes and engaging in meaningless Conversation, like he did every afternoon after school as he and his chums waited for their respective busses. His hair was a deep black, a far cry from the shocking blue of present time. The style was however, no different. Standing to his right was his best friend, Seth Donahue. In a sharp Contrast he sported bright, nearly bleach blonde hair. A Marlboro hung lazily from his mouth, both hands dug into the pockets of his faded levi's. He wore Converse all starts and a worn looking Guns N' Roses T-Shirt. To Donahue's Left was Gregory Stevenson. He took on more of a 'Punk' ensemble, with multi-colored spiked hair and various facial piercings. All three gentleman could be considered 'Popular'. Never bad with the Ladies, had plenty of other friends at school. All three regarded the spiraling Cheerleader in the same fashion. She's Hot. But she's also about as knowledgable as a brick. A really dumb brick. "SPEAR-it SPEAR-it SPEAR-it say it loud so I can hear it" Said Donahue. A Smile formed on his face at, what he thought, was a catchy innuendo. "Shut up man, you don't know her!" Wait, did I forget our 4th Character? His name was Gerald Porter. He was the shortest of them all, at 5'6". He was also the heaviest, at 255. He was the nerdy loser-ish type, who couldn't get the time of day from anyone of the female persuasion. He had short hair, and his knack for wearing blue knit caps had garnered him the nickname 'Portman'. " .. So Just.. just.. SHUT UP!" yelled Portman, beefy hocks balling into defiant fists. "Jesus Portman," said Donahue, taking a drag of his smoke "You need to calm down. Besides, isn't she a little out of your league?" Portman blushed and turned his back on his friends. Warr and Stevenson snickered. "You've been obsessed with that chick for what?" Said Stevenson, counting on his hands ".. 3 Years? Since we were FRESHMAN! Now It's time to graduate and what, nothing? At least go ask her out." "No." Said Portman. "No, Seth is right she's out of my league." "Whose to say Whose in which League? Are you the relationship Commissioner?" Warr cut in. "Just Be Cool, be yourself. Even though I have to note, you have awful taste." The four chuckled, but Portman's usual lively laugh was absent. Now it was hollow, lacking of any emotion. He'd asked Girls out before, and he'd been turned down. Each and every time, with each rejection wearing down not just on his confidence, but on his very soul seemingly. One More Shot He thought to himself, and he walked off. Tomorrow he'd take his friends advice and ask out the girl he'd had a crush on for his entire tenure at [detail withheld] High. ============ 002 Nights and Counting As Was Tradition, Warr, Donahue, Stevenson, and Portman had gathered outside the Darkened [detail withheld] Stadium. It was a small Football Field that local school teams used for their home games. Each week during Football season, the gang would gather on the outside, and do a shot for each point the visiting team scored. Just CHOCK full'a school spirit, these four. Together, they had raided their parents liquor cabinets and come up with half a bottle of SKYY Vodka, a near full bottle of Christian Brothers Brandy, and quarter a bottle of Bacardi 151. They waited patiently for the game to begin. "START THE FUCKING GAME YOU WHORES!" yelled Donahue, err.. Patiently. The big Three were so preoccupied with shouting obscenities at the preps from their hidden drinking spot that they didn't notice Portman slink off into the night. Eventually after the three had downed a few shots, Warr noticed. "Hey.. Where it Portman go?" "Dunno Man, Who cares? More for me" Said Donahue, taking a swig of Vodka. "No No.." Said Warr "He drove you cock slave. He's the designated driver, we need to find him." And So, the three set out into the stadium area. Shielding their eyes from the bright lights of the game, Donahue began chuckling uncontrollably and pointing at the field. "Pfft.. Look! There he is, The doughboy is walking towards Sarah!" "Maybe we should go Stop Him before he makes a fool out of himself?" Said Warr, beginning to walk down the steps towards the field. He was stopped however, by Stevensons arm. "No Man, let him do it. Worse thing she can say is no, and then after that he can go back to being the normal Portman from Jr. High." Warr stopped, as Portman slowly crossed the field towards where the Cheerleading team was going through their motions. --- He'd never been so nervous. Teeth chattering, hands shaking in the pockets of his hoodie. Portman was tired of admiring her from afar, he needed to do this, he needed to know. He approached her, about to make one of the biggest mistakes of his life. In the future he'd say to himself 'If Only I'd confronted her while she was on her own, and not in front of her friends, None of this would have ever happened.' But as it was, she was there, with her squad. The epitome of snootiness, she shouted orders at her team. She didn't feel the tap on her shoulder at first, but coupled with the throat clearing, she turned on her heel. "And WHO are you?" she said, taping her heel against the soft turf. "I'm Uhh- I'm Jerry Portman.. We've had Chemistry together for two years now." Said Portman, resisting the urge to stutter and run away. "AND What is it you want?" she said. He began to sweat. Just Leave. No. I can't do this, I'm going to throw up. No. Stop running away from your problems "I.. I.. I wantedtoknowifmaybeyoudliketogotopromwithme?' "Excuse me?" She said. "I.. Wanted.. To.. Know if you'd err.. Like to go to the prom with me?" She Could Have Said No. She Could Have Said She'd Been Asked Earlier. She could have lied to him. Anything but what happened next. --- Perched atop the stadium, Warr, Stevenson, and Donahue couldn't hear their pal build up the courage to ask her out, but they saw the fallout of the query. Everyone did. She Began to laugh. Behind her, the cheerleaders began to laugh. Everyone in the front row began roaring with laughter, a few flashbulbs went off. He didn't cry. He stood there, red cheeks. He turned and he ran. He had no idea where he was running, and he didn't care what he'd do when he got there. He Just wanted to be far away from this place, this life. ======== 001 Night and Counting Chuck Anderson Was a Bad person. Sure, at this point he didn't know it. But in 24 hours he would, and he'd be constantly reminded of his mistake. His one mistake. It destroyed his small business, and forced him to move out of state, and oh.. How he regretted what he'd done. Chuck Anderson owned 'Big Chuck's World of Firearms' the self proclaimed 'House' of Weapontry in Albany. Chuck liked the hunt, so it seemed like a good idea. His only vice? Greed. He'd do just about anything for a little extra green, 'What could it Hurt?' he'd say to himself on a nearly nightly basis. The Answer? A lot. He barely heard the bell of his door as he thumbed through the morning paper. It was mostly boring, a lot of prom announcements and ads for Limo rentals. After sensing movement he set his paper down ready to greet his first customer this morning. What he saw didn't impress him. A Chubby kid in a blue knit cap. "Hey, Hey Son. Ya Ha've ta' be Eighteen ta' come in here, Thats the Law." The boy said nothing. He simply pulled the wallet from his pocket, and showed his identification. Chuck realized he was just old enough, and began to apologize. "Sorry about that, Er.. Sir. I didn't know you were that old. How can I help ya?" "I Need something small" said Portman. "Something easy to conceal that packs a real punch." He browsed the display case before him. "So, Ya want power? Somethin' Small? Why don'tya check out this stylish .9 I've got. Just came in, Semi-Automatic, the works. Not the most powerful piece we have but easy to conceal." He removed it from the case handing it to Portman. He held it, pointing it. Easy to Handle. This'll do nicely, this'll.. work. "I'll take it. How much?" "Ya'hve got a great piece there. I'd say.. Since I Like ya.. 250 American" He fished in his pocket, pulling out the crumpled bills. "Pleasure Doin' business with ya. You can pick it up Monday at Noon" He turned to leave, before whipping back. "What?" Said Portman "What do you mean Monday? I need it now. It's important." "Sorry Son, Three Night Waiting period." He dug in his pocket, pulling out another fifty. "Would this change your tune?" Chuck Considered throwing the guy out from showing the kind of attitude he did. But he didn't get a lot of business, and an extra 50 would be useful. He took the piece and two clips of ammo, and set it on the Display case. He took the money and pocketed it, and just before Portman left the shop he turned back to his paper. "If I don't see it, Ain't illegal, right?" ============ 000. Prom Night. Often Saturday Afternoons were filled with Prank Pulling and cruising for Warr and Company. Behind the wheel of his Jeep, he could plainly notice the fourth of their party has opted not to attend. Instead, Donahue sat in the front seat, and Stevenson laid in the back. That was all. "Hey, Have you guys seen Portman?" Said Warr. He was worried about the guy, afterall. "Nnnnnope." said Stevenson from the back. "I Saw him yesterday, oddly enough. I was in a hurry so I couldn't stop to see how he was doing. He was in the Tux rental shop, standing in front of a mirror, modeling a black number to himself." Said Donahue. "He was renting a Tux? Why?" Said Warr "No I-clue" Said Donahue, "But it was him. He was all smiles, weirdest thing I've ever seen in my life. Must'a had his heart broken at the game, maybe he's still in shock." "Are we going to check out Prom?" Said Warr This set off the others in the car with laughter. "ARE YOU SERIOUS?!" said Donahue and Stevenson in close unison. "We don't have tickets, and we're not.. Cunts" Said Donahue, affronted. "We can check out the after party though, maybe spike the spiked punch with some ex lax." "You Know" Said Warr "Portman. He doesn't have a ticket either. Why the hell would he rent a suit? Kind of a waste. But right, the party. What time does it start?" --- He Looked Good. For Six Years, he'd saved. Every penny he'd earned, in a savings account. Every lawn he mowed, newspaper he'd delivered at 5 in the morning, all accounted for on his bank roll. He'd accumulated a little for 800 Dollars, and he'd blown it all. The Suit. The Ray-Bans. The Gun Concealed in his jacket. I'm Slick. He'd been saving the cash for a Rainy Day and In the eyes of Gerald 'Portman' Porter, it stormin' out there. If He was going to go Out. He was going to go out in Style. He was standing in the bathroom of a large mansion, which belonged to his once beloved Sarah Miller. How he enjoyed the time they'd spent together. It was a shame it had to end. Of course, she was never his beloved. They'd NEVER spent time together. But after the Incident, Portman lost a little more than he'd bargained. He lost a little of his sanity. Billy Idol's 80's classic 'White Wedding' Began to blare over the sound system the living room. He posed in front of the mirror for few seconds. Slick. Sharp. No One Can Stop me now. He Pulled the Expensive Sunglasses from his pocket, before leaving the bathroom. --- Warr knew something was wrong. As he pulled the Jeep into the only available parking space he could find, he hopped out and broke into a run. No one else had, but he'd seen Portman, smiling. Walking into the house, and Warr wanted to know why. He knocked people over, trampled on things he could NOT identify, and eventually busted through the front door. But, he was too late. She was standing in the middle of the dance floor, with her Boyfriend. Captain of the football team, tall, great looking' guy. Warr saw him just in time. Raising the Weapon. Boom. Screams, ducking. Warr did no such thing. Rather he broke out into a run towards Portman, caution to the wind. Portman had eyes only for the victim, however. The first Shot plunged deep into her upper left Arm. Boom. The Second, into her right shoulder. The 3rd Never quiet made it, as Warr speared Portman to the ground. The gun went flying across the room. More Screams, frantic running. Portman attempted to fight back, but Warr cocked his fist back, and plunged it into Portman's jaw, knocking him out cold. He turned his head, just in time, to see the look in Sarah's eyes. A Look Warr Had never seen again. Until The Morning of December 5th, 2005. --- She'd been fine. Wounded, but not badly. Gerald Portman was sent to an Insane Asylum, where he resides to this time. They say he's making progress, but the fact remains that Warr would never speak to him again. Some things can't be forgiven. ===== Warr was pulled from his day dream by the honking of a Truck horn. He'd apparently dazed off in through while standing in the middle of a crosswalk. He felt himself being pulled onto the sidewalk by Angela, who looked surprised. "Whats with you all of a sudden?" She said. Warr shook off the 'oogies' the flashback had given him. "Nothing. Just a bad memory, triggered by that damn poster." --- Women do strange things to men. They can cause, Rage.. Distrust, Love, Even Insanity. They can ensnare a man's emotions more than anything on this earth. I Know. I've Lived it. So in the future, when someone asks just why I'm choosing not to make Amy Silveira lightly, no matter how down about herself she is. I'll know what to say. Don't Undersell the Opposite Sex, M'Dear. Women have a strange effect on men, make em' do strange things. I've seen it, first hand. -Fin-
