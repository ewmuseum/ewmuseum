To the Next Level: Part 2

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 "Ugh..." Welcome back. "Where am I?" Asleep. "Oh, you're a great help..." Shouldn't that be 'I'm a great help'? "Smartass. Anyhow, it's time to get out of here." You sure? Things are gonna get a lot tougher. "I have to, don't I?" You sure do. This body isn't going to train itself. "Do you think I can do it?" No use in asking me. "Oh yeah... that's right..." Time to wake up. Brace yourself... ------------------------- "AAAAAAAAAAAAAGH!" The Muppet Man awoke with a scream, abruptly sitting up. Everything was blurry, and he didn't quite know where he was. He didn't quite know anything. Then, quite suddenly, the realisation kicked in... and the pain. He fell back down, clutching his right shoulder and wincing, his eyes watering, doing his best not to scream out again, even cry. He couldn't see straight, partly because his vision was clouded by the welling tears, but mainly because he didn't want to. If he saw where he was, then he would be fully awake, and he would have to face the pain. If not, then he could drift off back to sleep, back to peace... "Come on! Get up! I have no time to waste." The voice was strikingly familiar, and for a moment, Muppet forgot his pain as he pondered over who it was. Of course... it was Jason Masahiro, the man who was teaching him, tutoring him, preparing him for the greatest task in his career. But there was something different about it... it was was full of fire, not fitting in at all with his look, for on the outside, he seemed like a feeble old man. For a few more seconds that seemed like an eternity, he lay there on the ground, curled up in a foetal position, clutching his shoulder, and silently sobbing at the pain. However, he managed to wrench himself out of this pathetic state, and pull himself to a sitting position, and finally to his feet. He was shaking, but at least he was standing. "What the hell... was that?" he asked slowly. "I lost focus, and I dislocated your shoulder. I had to put it back in." Muppet Man winced at the thought, counting his blessings that he hadn't been awake when it had been done. "We have no time to waste," Masahiro repeated, replacing the 'I' with 'we' for emphasis. Muppet was shocked. "I've just had my shoulder dislocated and forced back into place, and you want me to continue training?" "You have to. You don't exactly have much time, do you?" "Yes, but..." He was stumped. It was true, he had to continue with his training, or he would fail at Survival. For a fleeting moment, the Muppet Man actually considered pulling out of the event. He was not ready, and he had gotten his shoulder dislocated a few days before the event. Why shouldn't he pull out? Masahiro seemed to see this in his face, and immediately slapped him with the back of his hand. "The next stage of the training is less physical, but it is still incredibly taxing... much more so than the first stage." Muppet shivered at the thought. More taxing than the first stage? And, it was mental training? He stood absolutely no chance. But, wasn't that what he thought about the first challenge... "Are you listening to me?" Masahiro's words ripped him out of his state of thought, and he quickly nodded. No use in turning back now, so he was going to go for it. Whatever Masahiro had in store for him, he would overcome it. He had to. "In fact, I think this little injury may do you some good. It will be very hard to relax your mind, to get into an appropriate mental state, but you can overcome it. You must overcome it, otherwise you have failed, and you are not worthy of a place in this match, let alone to be trained by me." Muppet found these words harsh, but he knew they were perfectly true. That was what made him so mad. As hard as he worked, as hard as he pushed himself every day, if he did it for the rest of his life, he would never be as good as this man. But, that wasn't what was important. He didn't have to overcome Masahiro, just the best PTC had to offer... "Oh, that's a comforting thought," he thought, a little annoyed, but now he had to focus. The next task was coming right up. "Let's get started, then," Masahiro said, expressionless. Muppet Man grimaced, forcing down the pain, and braced himself. Nothing came. Instead, Masahiro sat down. Muppet was confused, but of course, he had no time to waste being confused. So, he sat down as well. Masahiro moved into a typical meditation position. Muppet sighed... he had never had much luck with meditation in the past. But... maybe that wouldn't be the task after all. Eyes closed, Masahiro spoke up. "Get yourself comfortable. This will require extreme mental concentration." Muppet Man obliged, getting into a comfortable position where he could barely feel his body, save the roaring pain in his shoulder. He knew what was coming... he would have to leave his physical body altogether. The next battle would be in his mind. "Get yourself comfortable," Masahiro repeated, "And close your eyes. You know what to do next. Take all the time you need, and I'll meet you on the other side..." Masahiro's final words echoed through the Muppet Man's brain as he concentrated with every fibre in his body at achieving his goal... but that wasn't what he had to do. He let out a sigh. This was one task he had never been able to overcome in his life, and it was only the beginning of the final stage of the training. The realisation hit him like a speeding train. The one thing he had never been able to do, he would have to do right now, as if it was nothing. This was going to be like nothing else... "What the fuck are you doing?" he asked himself angrily. "You're meant to be clearing your mind of all thought, not falling into thought. There's a big difference." But, try as he might, he could just not do it. This was it. His journey was over. All the training, all the preparation was for nothing if he couldn't overcome this one obstacle... and he couldn't. Slowly, it sank into him, pushing him further down into that unescapable abyss. He had failed, or was going to fail. There was nothing he could do, and no one he could turn to. This time, the Devil's Own weren't there for him to consult, or hide behind. He was alone, completely and utterly alone in this desolate wasteland with no escape. He would have to fight his way out, alone... however hopeless it was. What the hell was the point in even trying? Why shuoldn't he just stop, and go home now? The Muppet Man opened his eyes, and stared at Masahiro. He was motionless. This was his opening. Masahiro wasn't there, or was as good as not there... he could escape, he could get up and walk out, walk away from the pain, walk away from the trials, walk away from the torture. It would end... all of it. All he had to do was get up... and leave. A young boy, not more than 13 or 14, was punching repeatedly into a large sandbag. He was clearly exhausted... he was punching so slowly, with very little actual power, and the sweat was pouring from him like there was no tomorrow. The trainer, master, was pacing up and down behind him. "You're doing well, Andrew. Only a little more," he said softly, speaking words of encouragement. It wasn't enough. "Sir... it hurts... so much..." "There's only a little way to go. You're doing so well," he repeated. "I... can't..." "Keep going Andrew!" But the boy had already stopped. "KEEP GOING!" "NOOOOO!" Before the master could do anything, the boy named Andrew was sprinting away. The Muppet Man grimaced at the memory. He couldn't. He couldn't bring himself to do it. Not now, not while there was at least one moral fibre left in his body. He couldn't leave, he couldn't walk away. Not now... not like this. This time, he wouldn't run. This time, he wouldn't back away. This time... he would succeed. He closed his eyes, and let all conscious thought flow from his body... as easily as that. The time had come. His time had come, and he wasn't going to throw it away. ---- The Muppet Man let out a deep breath that he didn't realise he had been holding in the first place. Was breath even necessary in this place? He didn't know quite where he was. He didn't really know anything... this was a new experience for him. Glancing around, he saw nothing. No, wait, there was something... but what? A bunch of swirling colours. What the hell? A minute ago, he had been trying to get to this special place, sitting in the middle of a large hall, a dojo. Now where was he? Without really meaning to, he began to picture the place he had just left. Well, who wouldn't? The strange thing, though, was what happened next. The colours that he had been pondering over began to swirl faster, more violently, as if they were trying to do something... and they were. Slowly, the rapidly fluttering colours began to settle on particular ones, and form into familiar shapes. Before he knew it, he was back in the dojo. With a twang of pride and surprise mixed together, he realised that he had reached depths in his mind that he never knew existed. He had full control over his surroundings. "Interesting, isn't it?" The voice pulled him out of this state, and once again he was back in the undecided borderline of... well, everywhere. He glanced to his left, and noticed another figure. It was Jason Masahiro. But... how was he here? "What the... isn't this my mind?" "It is whatever you want it to be," Masahiro said in his typically vague manner. "That doesn't answer my question," Muppet said, confused once more, and a little annoyed. "We are not here to answer questions." Muppet Man remained silent. Masahiro, of course, was right. "At least answer me one question... what am I here to do?" "To perfect your mentality, to learn to use your surroundings to your advantage, to have control over yourself when you fight. You have none of these. By the time we leave, you will have them all... or we won't be leaving." It was a chilling threat, and Muppet knew Masahiro meant every word. He gulped, making the walls around him resonate. Were there even walls? "Then, if you're ready," Masahiro said, "Let's begin." In a flash, Masahiro darted forwards, and planted a punch right in the chest of the Muppet Man. It was the most devastating thing Muppet had ever felt, and he fell down immediately, no longer breathing, unable to see straight. He might as well have been dead. "Get up," Masahiro muttered. "There is no pain. Now, get up." Just like that, the pain disappeared. Not even bothering to question this, after what he had already seen, Muppet got back to his feet. He was caught immediately by a crushing spinning heel kick, which sent him hurtling to one side in a completely unbelievable fashion. There, he crashed into an invisible wall, and fell into another crumpled heap. "Why was that wall there?" Masahiro asked. Then, without even giving Muppet time to think about it, he continued. "I am not meant to be here, I don't belong. Take control, take charge, and drive me from this place." Muppet lay perfectly still, and spoke to himself. All this pain is an illusion. There is no pain. Get up. He echoed the words Masahiro himself had said, and got to his feet. Already, though, the trainer was upon him, and caught him with an elbow to the side of the head, which had the same effect as the kick. This time, though, Muppet knew what to do. He shot through the air like a bullet, and hit another wall... but this time, he landed like a feather, and fell on his feet. "Very good," Masahiro noted with a smile. "But you will need more. Drive me from this place," he repeated. And so Muppet Man launched his offence. If was ceaseless, relentless, never stopping for even a moment. Yet, all of it was blocked, all of it was stopped, as if Masahiro wasn't even trying. This combat was timeless, unequalled anywhere in the physical world... but he couldn't prevail. One slip up, one momentary lapse, and Masahiro hit him. He managed to stop himself again, but this time he was shaken. "Is that all?" Masahiro asked, sounding quite bored. "I don't think you get it. Drive... me... from... this... place... now!" But how? Muppet asked himself. There must be a way! And then it hit him. Of course. He had full control over his surroundings. This was his mind, his creation, his home. He didn't know how Masahiro had entered it, and he didn't care either. All that mattered was getting him out again. The master darted forwards, and Muppet decided to put his theory into practice. Immediately, Masahiro was stopped in his tracks. Two strands of... something were wrapped around his arms, holding him back. What were they? It was simple... they were the fibre that his mind was made up of, that he could shape into anything he wanted. In this moment of realisation, Masahiro esaped, and reached Muppet, flying at him with a kick. But, then, all of a sudden, Muppet Man was no longer there. Masahiro was genuinely shocked, and he looked around. There was Muppet... right behind him. Their eyes met, and Muppet smirked. "Not this time. You have no right to be here. Get..." Masahiro felt himself being pulled back by some unknown force. "...OUT!" And with this, the same substance that had held Masahiro back before overwhelmed him. A milisecond later... he was gone. The Muppet Man was on his own. He had succeeded. For the first time in his life, he had won. With the a sigh of pure joy, he let himself fall backwards... ---- The Muppet Man's eyes opened abruptly, and he fell backwards, shocked. "Wh... what the hell did you do?" he asked, unable to catch his breath, despite not moving a muscle this whole time. Masahiro smiled, glowing with pride. "Me? I didn't do anything." The old master got slowly to his feet, and began to walk away. "Am... am I ready?" Muppet Man asked. He already knew the answer. Masahiro did not reply, simply walking away. He opened the door to the room where Muppet had originally woken up, and glanced back, before going in and closing the door behind him. In that momentary look, an understanding flashed between the two of them. All of that, everything he had just experienced, had been inside his own mind. Masahiro had never been there. No, that was impossible, ludicrous. The whole time, he had known what to do. He had been searching for his whole life, to find that he had known the answer all along. But he was not discouraged. After a lifetime of searching, he had found what he was looking for, and now came the greatest test of all. In his own mind, he was ready, and that was good enough for him. Without a look back, the Muppet Man got up and walked out of the dojo, heading for the airport. He had a plane to catch.
