Wrath of the True Gods

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-16 A war of the gods. Cloaked in the darkness of night, a small, modest building stands within the Japanese countryside. Bamboo walls and rice paper windows and doors are the things that construct this building, a traditional Japanese dojo. For hundreds of years men have trained in these, for combat, for physical improvement, for spiritual betterment. This time is no different. This place is the training ground of one who seeks to become a champion, not in title, but in spirit. The door to the dojo is open, and we see two men standing inside the small building. One stands with his back to the camera, his head bowed in silent prayer. The other stands still against the wall, waiting patiently. The first man raises his head and speaks, a deep, commanding voice in Japanese. The words appear below him in English. It is but by the grace of the gods that I now stand here. My elimination was imminent... the words spoken ages ago and the events determined to occur by the gods were all but rendered null and void. In the first contest, I was unaware of the subtleties of this body, and this mind. In the time that has passed between the last match and this one though, I have learned much more about this human form I have taken. That which escaped me then might have allowed my victory, but now it does not matter. My fate is not to be denied. What had been ordered so far in the past must now be carried out, and I will let nothing stop it all from coming to pass. I have now acquired those things that I was missing, and they shall lead me to victory in this battle I now enter into. This man - Raijin - stands and motions to his follower - the one known as Kenzo. Kenzo nods and brings with him Raijin’s silver and black mask. Raijin takes it from him and slips it on his head, then reaches back and ties the laces that hold it secure. He turns his head and looks over his shoulder. This tournament has truly been full of... surprises. Circumstances that I would have never even imagined possible have come about. I was not defeated, and yet I did not win. In heaven this would be an impossibility... this is not heaven though, and so this tournament has proven to be full of strange situations. Strange matchups with even stranger competitors... a diminutive man with an inflatable phallus... a jester-like man with an action figure... and my opponent, a disciple of a false religion. He slowly turns around, his face now hidden by the mask, and at the same time augmented by its colors and design. This mask is more necessary than any on this earth except for Kenzo know, for Raijin’s face is that of a god, one not meant for any of this world to see. Kenzo steps away, leaving Raijin alone. He stares forward, focused, set on one thing and one thing alone: the defeat of his opponent, the one known as Ethan Knight. In the previous round of this tournament, the only motivation he had to defeat his opponents was that he had the chance to prove himself to the world, to show those who had never seen him exactly what it was that he could do. He now has a greater purpose in mind. He must stop Ethan, the man who seeks to end his existence. That fool worships beings he calls the “Old Ones”, follows a plan set forth by somebody known as the “Outer Gods”... he will soon find out it is all false. He follows those so-called deities, but I know that they are not all powerful, and they only seek to command those without a strong mind, weak-willed individuals such as himself who have no capacity to think on their own. I have come from Heaven, a place not even those entities could know, a place I know to truly exist. I will prove to Ethan Knight the existence of the true gods and goddesses. I shall show him the power of Tengoku and Ama-Terasu-O-Mi-Kami, for I am Raijin, the god of lightning. He shall be struck down, not for holding a belief not rooted in the heaven I know, but for actively seeking to destroy those he does not believe in. I accept those that do not believe in my heaven, but he embraces none but those who believe his own faith... he has no compassion, and no tolerance, and that is unforgivable. That is a transgression against my heaven, a transgression against the gods who dwell there, and a transgression warranting divine retribution. Even if I do not defeat him here though, he shall be struck down by those watching him from Tengoku. He shall not be allowed to continue spreading lies throughout this world. Raijin’s eyes begin to glow as they had before, the same ethereal blue, the color of his soul. This time, though, they glow with more than just his divine fury. They glow with hatred. Hatred for Ethan Knight. Hatred for his ideals, hatred for everything he stands for. All that he professes to be true goes against all that Raijin knows, against his very existence even. If there is a man on Earth who can prove without a shadow of a doubt that Ethan’s beliefs are false, it is Raijin, and he will do all in his power to terminate the infidel. The traditional faith of those who believe in me, in us, holds that there is no sin, only evil deeds which tarnish the soul, and only misogi, rites of purification, can eliminate those. Those who do not seek misogi are doomed to become evil kami, and that is their punishment. This we have accepted for ages, but now, we have chosen to actively punish the one known as Ethan. One so twisted and depraved cannot be permitted to attempt to take this world for his beliefs. His sacrifice will have been made in vain... none can destroy me with the powers of heaven on my side. Ama-Terasu, supreme goddess of the Sun. Hachiman, god of war and protector of Japanese people. Fuujin, my divine counterpart, the god of wind. And Ebisu, the god of good fortune. With these kami behind me, I shall not fail. Ethan Knight shall find himself alone, without support, his “Old Ones” having long abandoned him, seeing the true hopelessness of this situation. He stands silent. He bows his head once more, as he had in the beginning, and begins reciting something inaudible, something only he knows. The air grows eerily still... the calm before the storm. In his mind, thoughts of the coming war rage, but he hides them underneath a calm exterior. His lies shall end here. This shall be his day of judgment. Raijin closes his eyes, and begins to breathe more heavily. He clenches his fists tighter and tighter, his rage building within him, welling up within his soul. His hands shake, his breathing becoming even more intense. He thrusts his hands into the air, and looks upwards to the heavens, the source of his strength. The light in his eyes becomes blindingly intense. He screams, the words left untranslated. Yaaaaaaahhhh!! Ethan Knight... kami no sabaki wo ukeru! Lightning flashes outside the dojo... thunder begins to rattle the tiny building. The scene fades to complete black... darkness as Ethan Knight would see after his demise at the divine hands of Raijin. Divinity shall prevail.
