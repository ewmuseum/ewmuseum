Date: 2006-02-05 The throne had been abdicated. The once and future prince had been torn down from his pedestal, cast out of heaven. In the nebulous silence, his throne looked tarnished and cracked. For too long, the prince had swept problems under this throne, praying that they would evanesce quietly in the shadows of his crown. Finally, there had come a problem that had no solution. An expression with too many variables. And just like that, there was a new prince. But he was already building a castle of greater grandeur, a throne without the patina and vitiation of this one. In fact, the new prince would tear down this castle, brick by brick, as he worked towards the next logical step. First, there was the matter of a certain peasant. Word around the castle was that despite the fall of the former king, a peasant was attempting to lead a revolt. Seeing an impotent new force, this peasant was going to force the new prince into a move. The prince had tussled with roguery of this nature before. And one didn’t become royalty by making it a habit to lose tussles. Nonetheless, this was not the time for military strategies and defensive fortifications. It was a time for debauchery, ale, and lewd encounters for every member of the court, including the prince himself. He watched the preparations for his victory dinner, secretly cracking a recognizable smile. This was undoubtedly the face of triumph and forthcoming supremacy over all in his domain. * * * * I’m the fucking king, I think as I walk up the stairs to the brilliantly carved russet-tinted door, a trademark of this apartment complex, which might as well be called Perfidious Acres, with all the very rich, very indifferent to law residents. Coming to places like this makes me feel like I’ve been scrubbed clean; that’s how murky the waters are among these wealthy and bored idols. Today is different, though. After my shared win with Angelo Deville over a month ago in PRIME, I was keen on getting another shot. I did and I’ve been on cloud nine since. Fuck Salaok, I already beat the guy last round; Deville was just icing on the cake, in fact. And now’s not the time for ring strategy and studying tape, it’s time for gloating, getting shit-faced, and using my growing mainstream recognition for copious amounts of ass. I knock thrice on the door, as voices inside ebb to tenuous, feeble whispers. Inside, something is unfolding that the common apartment resident shouldn’t know about. Still, I hear no footsteps moving towards the door, so I issue three more knocks, progressively louder. Within, there is repeated, stale silence, but it dissipates as feet softly lead the way to the door. Eyes brimming with anticipation, a middle-aged man with big-time brown hair answers the door. It’s a bit of a shock and I’m caught, slightly aghast at seeing his face in the small crack of door that’s open. "Umm…is Shari here?" The words come out without thinking, although it seems as though I’ve been mislead, more subterfuge from the queen of backbiting and what she, in her sweetest voice, would call "not lies, terminological inexactitudes, baby." Then she would make your toes curl, your eyes roll back, and force to start saying Hail Mary’s right there. But right now there’s no Shari, just some bug-eyed dude who looks seriously confused to see an Asian guy standing at the door. "What do you want?" That doesn’t quite answer my question, so naturally my suspicion grows and I try to peek inside the apartment, wondering if he’s got her tied up. If need be, I’ll bust in and save her from this predator; the new prince will do what he never- "I said, what do you want?" Caught up in my thoughts, I didn’t respond to his first question and now he’s starting at me like I’m the dubious one here. "Is Shari here or not? My business is here with her." "Is that all I am to you, Chandler? Business?’ The voice comes, cloying and dulcet, from somewhere inside and the door swings wide to reveal her angelic form. The middle-aged man falls out of my mind and all I see is the purple satin knee-length robe, covering what I know to be breathtaking physical features. In the shadows, I can’t see her face, but I know it by heart, can connect all the dots in my head to their ideal points. "Shari, can I come in?" Chandler Tsonda? Cut to the chase? Say it isn’t so. Stepping over the threshold and thoroughly intimidating the man, in just his boxers and an undershirt, I take stock of the apartment. It hasn’t changed much, but there are slices of Shari everywhere; the way that she changes the location of her TV and couch for every different man she’s been with. I’ve never seen it this way. "Excuse me," gripes the man, stepping between my mark and me. "Just who the fuck are you?" He puts his hands on his hips and looks laughable, standing there in his skivvies, attempting to look authoritative. "I’m Shari’s ex-boyfriend." Yeah, he had to figure it out sooner or later, so might as well tell him now that I’m the ex who treated her like shit and was treated like shit and we fucked like minxes. "I need to talk to her." He doesn’t back down, despite the fact that I’m bigger than him and certainly more battle-tested. "Well, Shari’s kind of busy right now." "How about this? You grab your clothes and get out of here within ten seconds and I won’t go call your wife." Putting it all together, I realize that he’s a local TV personality, whose ring finger is very much occupied. He grumbles and grabs his things, scurrying out of the apartment. The door shuts, terminally and with force, on him and Shari and I are alone. At first, she ignores the fact that I’m there, heading into the kitchen. Her legs are the fine line between emaciated and chunky; that’s what’s so amazing about her. There isn’t a middle ground for women’s legs. She’s got the only perfect pair. Slinking up, her ass just barely teases out of her robe in the back as she sashays out of sight. I’m content to sit there in silence if she is. And she is, fiddling with something, probably trying to wait me out, or just to test me. I don’t pretend to understand her. She walks back out, coffee in hand, and peers at me with those deadly brown eyes. "Do you think I’m going to ask you how you’re doing and the conversation will eventually swing to how much we’ve both missed each other and then we’ll spontaneously have at it on the floor right now?" God, she’s hot when she gets ornery. And her cutting cynicism was always one of her greatest gifts. She looks genuinely mad, but I know her mind is racing, trying to figure out what the situation is and how she can gain an advantage. "I was just hoping for dinner." Rarely do I find myself being modest, but it’s a symptom of being around her. If there was ever a woman who could rein me in, it’s the one standing in front of me, teasing with the low cut of the robe that she fingers lightly, still able to make me salivate despite my overflowing knowledge of the content within. "Fine. Tonight at seven here. Bring a nice wine, since you’re such a big shot now, beating Angelo Deville." So she does know. * * * * The prince’s feast was the greatest seen in almost a hundred years. Some in the castle rumbled that this type of celebration was pre-emptive, noting that the unpleasant thorn in his side was gathering power, consolidating the force in numbers of serfs across the countryside, preparing for all-out assault on the castle within a few weeks time. A castle hand carried in even more food to feed the dozens of men and women seated at the table (although the women were no higher than dogs to him, he did not force them to eat scraps from his hands). Underneath the table, a disturbing amount of hands crept into nether places, then even more disturbingly returned to the vast, heaping plates. Most everyone would soon be settling down for a night of eupepsia and penetration, just as the prince had hoped. He partook in the celebratory mood, although the advisor at his side talked his ear off about the logistics of a castle defense and just how important it was to establish preeminence over the entire territory. The peasant, one of those pesky Goths (Visi or Astro or he couldn’t keep track), continued to nibble at his mind, but he was never sufficiently concerned that the peasant leader took precedence over the heaping cow’s quarters on the table. Having had his fill, the prince decided to retire upstairs with the fair future princess. Despite his lust, he was overcome by terrible fatigue. He would lie down while his lady prepared herself for his gluttonous desires later in the night. The king would merely lie down and enjoy an hour’s sleep to return with rancorous and puissant love. A castle hand approached the king’s door, but the lady of the castle cut him off. "I have urgent news for the prince," he stated mildly, but urgently. "The prince is sleeping and can not be bothered until he awakens." The castle hand knew better than to try his luck with a grumpy prince, but he knew that more news of the peasant would be important to the prince. Against his better judgment, he strolled off to bide his time until the prince could receive him. * * * * When I reach the apartment this time, the door swings open with ease and patience, which are certainly not Shari’s strong points. She looks dazzling and formal and full of life that she never had when we had been together. Hoop earrings are like small moons to her small planet-shaped head, covered in shining brown hair that tells me she hasn’t forgotten what I like. "You bring the wine?" she asks nonchalantly. With an uncaring aura, she accepts the wine from my hands and I don’t catch even a slight graze of her warm, olive skin. "It’s nice to see you, too." I don’t know what to say now, after all this time, all this thinking about what exactly there is to say. "What are we eating?" "I just did rice and stir fry, you still like it?" I nod happily, at the thought of her first efforts at rice and stir fry. She had failed miserably and gotten drunk on wine in the process. After bitching me out for being so childish about my favorite food, she had promptly made it up with oral skills that would make Jenna Jameson blush. It’s not that’s she’s the prettiest or the freakiest, but she’s a top-notch combination of the two, as well as attitude that will scalp you if you’re not careful. Sitting down at the table, I notice that she has no candles lit. Perhaps I’ve read the signals wrong, but I know that Shari isn’t one to skirt theatrics. "No candles? I’m hurt." "Listen, Chandler, if this is going to be an indictment of who I am as opposed to who I was-" The annoyance barometer is rising and I settle down. "It’s not. What’s exciting in your life? "Very little," she groans. "Why do you think there was a fucking weatherman in my bedroom?" "If I remember, that’s the best way you know how to get an accurate reading on the dew point." She stares at me with the "watch it, punk" look and we both hit the food hard, the wine slightly less hard. We mumble about things in our lives and soon the food is gone; the wine endures. "Did you come back to me because you’re getting famous again?" I’m not sure what she means, at first; PWC certainly wasn’t any sort of key to national fame and PRIME is my first spotlight. "You’re almost back up where you were before you fell off the map in modeling." How strange it is to think about life as a wrestler/model, instead of a model-turned-aspiring-wrestler. "You think that I’m that shallow." The last piece sits on the fence between question and statement, with just enough upturn of her quiet, serious voice, that I’m not sure how to response. "That was part of it. I thought you could finally accept me again. Plus, you’ve always been a sucker for celebrities," I deadpan. A grin grows somewhere in her throat, but her cold demeanor and stare abort its life. "I only said yes to dinner tonight because I wanted to tell you that we’re not going to do all this again." The glint in her eyes deceives me, betraying withheld confidence. "We’re both old enough to know that it’s both irrational and painful to think otherwise." I don’t know how to respond; if I know Shari, she wants me to fight…she’d certainly want to end things more explosively. "So then that’s that?" I get up and move towards the window, but before I can take another step, I feel her confident, prodding hands on my stomach. She whirls me around with little resistance and pulls my mouth to hers. This is what I meant by explosive. * * * * The prince was now awake, to a thumping cacophony. First, he heard the crush of wood on wood, as the peasants brought their battering ram to the castle’s ramparts. Next, he heard the shrieks of terrified maids and hands who now scurried about, rattled by the ongoing chaos. Finally, he heard the voices of the peasants themselves. They were not far outside his window; slinking over, he poked his head up to window level and looked out to see the mass of plebes. At their head was the silver-haired fiend, this calculated hero of a man, shrieking for the peasants to continue taking up arms. Before there was time to assess the situation, the floodgates opened. The splintering wood brought the prince to his senses. He immediately grabbed a frightened castle hand and sent him off to find the prince’s most trusted advisors and send them to his tower. Meanwhile, he dispatched another hand to ensure that the castle’s defenders were ready. Within moments, the response had been completely organized. And in that instant, the prince realized that the time for celebration was over, and that the time to rule was upon him. He hurried off, ready to wait out any offense that this peasant could mount. And like he had before, he would quell this revolt and the next…until there was no one left stupid enough to challenge the power of his house. * * * * I pull my pants on quietly, not wanting to disturb Shari’s sleep in that way that you don’t want to wake up a pit-bull if you’re sneaking through its yard. With all my stuff on, I proceed on the soft carpet, perfectly content with the explosive ending we’ve just agreed upon. There’s not time for a Shari right now; and yet if that were true I wouldn’t have come here in the first place. Don’t take my word on focus; just know that the prince has his mind on Salaok, this beautiful tournament…and well, maybe Shari, when there’s a moment to spare.
