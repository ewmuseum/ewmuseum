Genesis (Edited For Excitement's Sake)

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 In the beginning when God created heavens and the earth, the earth was a formless wasteland, and darkness covered the abyss, while a mighty wind swept over the waters. Then God said "let there be light," and then there was light. God saw how good light was. God then separated light from darkness. God called- BO-RING. How about a real story? A barn-burner? Mine. If it’s not more exciting then the Biblical back-and-forth about God creating "the ox and the tree and the rabbit and the echidna and morning wood and those little spikey things that live in the ocean and…"Well, you get where I’m going. I. So in the beginning where Mom and Dad made nasty sex in a thatch hut somewhere in the heart of Vietnam, they conceived a beautiful child. And Dad left because he was from the West and weak of heart. And the being known as Mom decided that this was okay because she was going to bring her strong heart and her strong child to the West. And she did. He grew. He was vain. But it was perfect. There was something in Him that gave Him a devilish curling smile on the inside every time someone admired His body or His perfectly symmetrical face. To these herds of cattle, He was the act of rediscovering the essence of humanity. Even if they didn’t know it, they treated Him like they would a piece of art. And in turn, He judged them and placed them all beneath Him. And they made Him an idol. And in time, they faded like the memory of a father, of a homeland that represented the West and their wars more then the East. II. He remained perfection. Underneath the physical perfection was a lingering disease, a pollution of the soul that could never be brought to convalescence. The sins were not countless, but they echoed even more because He could count them and taste them and feel them every time He walked. And He was flawed and He could never see that so He left the land of perfection behind. He voyaged out of this false paradise into a land of further scorn, disdain, hatred, and Italian people. This, of course, was the inferno where the devil resided: New Jersey And there in the inferno was a Starr. The Starr led Him to his new life, to the life inside the canvas square. He fought without scruples or honor for glory, gold, frankincense, and myrrh. Here in the inferno He did meet the Deville and the son and the wolf. But then the inferno released its inhabitants and the flames ceased. III. And it was cold for Him, who had no place to go but had to wander in the deserts for moons and moons. And maybe, just maybe He had to do some bad commercials for Bod body spray during that time, but hey We all make mistakes. Then he found the golden land. There was a tremendous urn, built by the gods, it was said. And it was also said that the one who held this urn could kill any man. And He wanted that power, not to kill any man, but to SAY that He could kill any man. For then He could return to His land and slay the Deville and the son and all who stood in His path. And he was told that he would also get a website and some other cool stuff, but that it was an anachronism to speak about the internet in this Biblical tone, so He didn’t. IV. But before He could walk out of the golden land, He would have to surpass many men. But He was cunning and clever and even if He was not the strongest of arm, He was the strongest of mind. So He would put the ground up poison weed in the drinks of other men and nefarious things of that nature. Because His eyes had seen the glimmer of gold that they could not resist.
