Interlude - Beginnings of Beginnings

16 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-16 [You open your eyes and see that you are in a dark and snow covered forest. The trees are black and gnarled. You feel the encompassing cold around you as your breath is a white fog before your eyes. You smell the thick night air. You ponder what the aroma you sniff is. You come to the conclusion that it is the smell of death. You take a step forward listening as the snow crunches as it is compacted under your boot. The sound echoes off the tree and you stop to look around to make sure that no one is following you. You quicken your pace dodging fallen tree branches and trees as you try to find away out of the forest. No matter what direction you turn you feel lost. You hit your knees and pray that you can get find the right direction to get out of the forest. The deep black clouds that hang in the sky part allowing the moon to shine upon you. You look up at the full moon and then down at the tree line. It is only then you notice the man standing in the distance. His face was white as the snow and his clothes were as black as the darkness. You feel the urge to run to him to see if he could help. As you run the moon falls back into the clouds. You keep your mind on reaching the man. You reach the spot where he is but he isn’t there anymore. The moon appears again and there in the distance you spot him again. This time he looks at you and extends a finger and beckons you to follow him. You wonder if your desperation and the moonlight are playing tricks on you. You look back at the figure and he is still there. You run, leaping over, and ducking under anything that gets in your way. You reach the spot to where the man is. Once again he is gone with the moon as it ducks beneath the clouds. You can feel the worry and the panic start to rise now. Your breathing is becoming more rapid and your pulse is starting to quicken. You try to calm yourself down by taking deep breaths. The moonlight this time takes you by surprise and there you see him again to your right and only a few yards away. It seems as though he is hovering a few inches above the ground. You turn your back on the figure deciding that he is just a figment of an overtaxed and over panicked imagination. Once your back is to him you look up and there he is again. He is closer this time and that is when his haunting green eyes stare right through to your soul and you begin to back away. The man holds up a finger and points to his left. You look and there you see a clearing with in the forest and in the middle of the clearing you see a roaring fire. You are cold and shivering and run to the fire. The warmth feels so good on your hands as you hold them up to the dancing flames. After sitting in front of the fire for quite awhile you notice that there are tall stones standing up right all around you. You notice that the stones surround you in a circle. You walk to your left towards the largest stone. You wipe the snow away from the rock and realize that they aren’t big rocks but headstones. Half scared and half curious you read the name on the stone. “Lourdes Lucero”,] Voice: She was my wife. [You jump back at hearing the voice coming from behind you. You quickly turn and see the figure you have been following sitting by the fire on a rock. His arms are propped on to his knees and he stares into the fire. You study him for the first time as he stands up and walks toward you. The man is tall and muscular. He has shoulder length white hair. His face was a mask of white with black tribal lines around the eyes and mouth. He is wearing long black leather pants, a black mesh shirt with a coat of arms of a crucifix on a shield and twin crossed sickles behind it, black boots, and a long black hooded leather duster. You fix your eyes back to his piercing green eyes. He stands by your side and then crouches. He takes a red rose from inside his duster and plants it into the snow. The red on the stark white background blurs your eyes for a moment. He stands back up and walks back over to the fire and sits down. You follow and watch him. The man speaks again,] Syckle: Welcome to all that remains of the old ways. The old ways I used to do things. The old ways that I will never again use. The old ways for me always led to one of two things. Either someone ended up hurt or someone ended up dead. I used to believe in balance and I lived my life by treading that line between life and death, black and white, good and evil, light and darkness. But now I have learned that balances like rules are made to bend and in some extenuating circumstances broken. To know what I am talking about however you need to hear my story. Be forewarned that my story is… [Syckle holds up his hand and a green energy surrounds it,] Syckle: Anything but ordinary. [Syckle dowses the energy from his hand and stares back into the fire,] Syckle: My story begins with one of three names. That name is Anubis. I was born into creation over 5000 years ago to my mother Nephthys and my father Set. I was raised to be the Lord of the Undead and Ruler of the Underworld. As I grew in my role I was depicted as a man with the head of a jackal. In those times jackals had the tendency to prowl around tombs. As the Old Kingdom grew I was worshiped as the inventor of embalming. I took upon myself the task of embalming the dead Osiris, preserving him so that someday he may live again. Once Osiris rose from the dead he challenged me for the crown of the underworld. Osiris and I fought for many days and many nights. On the fortieth night I was bested in combat and I relinquished my crown. Osiris commended me on the way that I fought and gave me the position of Guardian to those that have passed on to his kingdom. I was to guide and protect the dead souls that passed through the gates of the underworld. Isis, Osiris’ wife presented me with the Scales of Truth on the one year anniversary of my defeat by Osiris. I was to monitor the scales and protect the dead from the second death in the underworld. It was also I, that conducted the souls of the dead to their judgment by Osiris. Historians would later parallel me to Hermes the messenger of the Gods in the Greek Culture. But any of what I just told you can be found in a book. Now here is where the untold part of the story takes place. [Syckle gets up and walks around the fire,] Syckle: I watched the Scales of Truth and carried souls to their judgment until little over a century and a half ago. After watching the world grow and change I went to Osiris and asked him to grant me one wish, * * * 150 Years ago, The throne room of Osiris [Anubis walks into the great throne room where the Lord of the Dead, Osiris sits with his wife, Isis by his side. Anubis reaches the throne and kneels before Osiris and Isis. Osiris looks down at the man who has long red hair and green eyes. The man carries his ceremonial jackal headed, golden mask by his side. His short toga skirt and golden sandals are everything they should be. Anubis speaks with his head still down,] Anubis: For give the intrusion my lord. But I have a favor to ask. Osiris: Anubis, you have not intruded. What is this favor that you ask? And please stand. Anubis: Yes my lord. I ask you to grant me a wish. Osiris: Wishes are different the favors Anubis but I shall hear your wish. Anubis: My wish, sir is to find out what it is to be alive. [Osiris was not expecting this. He sat up as did Isis,] Osiris: Anubis, why the curiosity with mortality? Anubis: I have seen and been a part of death for over 5000 years. I have never known anything else. I ask to explore what the other side holds. Osiris: Anubis, I cannot grant you your wish. Not in the capacity that you ask of. If I were to let you be born as a normal mortal child you may not retain any knowledge. Anubis: I understand. Osiris: However since you have been faithful to me and have watched the scales of truth I can let you send your essence into a mortal child as it is being born into the world. This way you may see what is like to be mortal and still retain the knowledge when the mortal body is brought to my kingdom. Anubis: Thank you my lord. Osiris: Anubis, I will do this for you but you must be made aware of three conditions and the consequence of breaking any one of them. One, you are never to directly interfere in the mortal child’s life. Two, you are to never to take over the being’s body and use it for your own purposes. Three, you are never to let the child know the true knowledge of a God for no mortal could comprehend it. If you break any of these rules you are to be banished to the body that you will inhabit. Are these rules understood, Anubis? Anubis: Yes, Osiris. Osiris: Very well. * * * Present Day Campfire in the Cemetery Syckle: Osiris implanted my essence with in a mortal boy named, William Alexander Roberts. Through that boy’s eyes I watched the world evolve around me. Every now and then I tapped into his thoughts and emotions. The boy evolved into a man but his fate was one that even I didn’t expect. William met a man named Nathaniel Essex and his sister Annabelle. William came to find out that Nathaniel was a vampire. One night while William slept Nathan bit into William’s neck and William was turned into the living dead. For over three years every night both William and Nathan hunted and drained his prey as he traveled the English country side. Nathan and William hunted together one night as they came upon a house that housed a family who were fast asleep. William and Nathaniel stalked into the house and found the adults and drained them of their lives. William then followed Nathan as he walked into the adjourning room. The room was a nursery. It housed a small infant girl. Nathan picked up the small child and bent its head back. He was about dig his fangs in when I felt something stir in William. I felt humanity. * * * 130 years ago House on the English countryside. [William watches as Nathan was about to bite into the neck of the infant,] William: STOP! [Nathan stops and looks at William confused,] Nathan: What? William: Put the infant down and I won’t have to hurt you. [Nathan just laughs,] Nathan: Stop fooling around William. [Nathan starts to bring the baby to his fangs when William rips the infant away. Nathan roars but then stops to smell the air and his eyes grow wide,] Nathan: You, you reek with humanity? But how is that possible? William: I came to my senses. * * * Present Day Campfire in the Cemetery Syckle: The little bit of William’s soul that I was wrapped around had survived. William sprung into action and started to fight Nathaniel. Nathaniel and William crashed through the window. Nathaniel stalked off into the thick and dark forest. William was about to follow but was caught from behind by something. [Syckle looks up the sky and then back into the fire,] Syckle: William had been hit by a silver bullet. Silver bullets won’t kill a vampire but they will hurt like hell. As William was being dragged off by his unknown assailant. I, Anubis was called back into the chambers of Osiris. * * * 130 years ago Throne room of Osiris [Osiris looked down at Anubis with a stoic stare,] Osiris: Anubis, you broke one of the three rules. You let your essence free and the humanity you were protecting take over. Anubis: I couldn’t let the child die. Osiris: Regardless if your act was noble and selfless you still broke the first rule. You directly interfered in William’s life. You know the penalty. Anubis: Yes, Osiris. I am ready. [Osiris stands,] Osiris: Anubis, guardian of the graveyard, you are here by sentenced to life in the mortal world in the form of the mortal William Roberts. Anubis: Yes my lord. Osiris: However, since your reason for breaking rule was of a noble cause. I will appoint you guarding of the mortal world. You shall retain all knowledge, powers, and immortality of a deity. Go now and start your new life and new purpose. I will be watching. Anubis: Thank you Osiris. [Anubis was transported back into the body of William.] * * * Present Day: Campfire in the cemetery Syckle: I was banished to William’s body but this time I had no rules to follow. In essence I had become William. I am William Roberts. It was three days before I awoke to find myself shackled to a stake and before me stood Annabelle. I studied her as she studied me. Annabelle told me that I was a prisoner of the Faceless Order. The Faceless were a sect that hunted, judged, and executed demons. Annabelle told me that I was charged with the murders of those that I had bitten. By the orders of the Faceless I was to die by the first morning light. She asked if I had any last words. I stayed silent as I held my gaze into her eyes. She didn’t flinch as she returned my gaze. We stared at each other silently until I spotted the first signs of dusk through the stone hut that was to serve as my jail and tomb. She stated the last rights, but as she was saying the words I could hear her heartbeat. Apparently I still was a vampire. Her heartbeat was going faster and faster. That was when I saw it in her eyes. I saw the regret that she had to kill me. But she was in for a surprise. I was now engulfed fully in sunlight and nothing happened. Annabelle looked confused and for one moment caught off guard. She asked how I was able to stand in the sunlight. She walked over and inspected me. She placed her hand on my chest. That was when she felt the heartbeat. She didn’t know what to do. So she undid my shackles and told me to come with her. She reached into a pouch on her belt and threw a purplish colored powder against the wall of the hut. A black void appeared out of thin air and she stepped through it as did I. We found ourselves outside a large chamber, * * * 130 years ago Inner sanctum of the Order of the Faceless [William and Annabelle stood outside a large chamber door with the marking of a large compass rose on the front,] Annabelle: This is the door that leads to the inner sanctum of the Faceless. Only hunters and council members are allowed to pass through these doors…usually. But then again you are unusual. William: It took you this long to figure that out? [Annabelle turns to look at the door,] Annabelle: Very funny, I am the hunter, Annabelle Essex; I seek an audience with the council. [The door opens with a loud groan and both William and Annabelle enter. The inner sanctum consisted of large stone pillars and with three torches on each. There was a row of seven chairs with cloaked figures sitting in them. Before them on the floor was a large compass rose. Annabelle stepped in the middle of the rose and kneeled. The figures in one voice told her to rise,] Council: What is it you wish for us to witness? [Annabelle brought me forth,] Annabelle: This is the vampire you sought me to kill. But I bring him forth before the council to tell you that this is no vampire, but a human being. Council: BLASPHMEY! YOU DARE BRING ONE OF THE UNCLEAN INTO OUR CHAMBERS. INTO THIS SACRED PLACE? WHAT TYPE OF THRALL DOES THIS VAMPIRE HAVE OVER YOU? Annabelle: None. I bring here of my own free will. He is a man. No vampire I have slayed has ever had a heartbeat. He stopped my brother from feeding on an innocent infant. Council: Only after feeding on the parents. He is not a man but a monster! Hunter’s seize the monster and the traitor! [Out of the darkness four fellow hunters restrain both William and Anna. The hunters were about to take William and Anna away when Annabelle yelled,] Annabelle: I as a hunter of this Order invoke the seventh rule. I invoke the trials of Redemption! Council: The trials are for mortals not creatures of evil! Annabelle: I say once again that William is a man and not a monster! [William has remained still and silent. The council turns their voices to him,] Council: Hunter Essex has invoked the trials of redemption for the sake that she believes you are man and not creature. You have remained silent. What say you? [William looked up and stared at the seven figures in their hooded cloaks,] William: You claim that this Order is of holy nature. You claim that you wash the earth of the dirty and impure. You call yourself the Faceless. Is that because you hide your faces underneath the hoods or is that because you are cowards that happen to have numbers on your side? Council: We are called the faceless because we are to remain hidden from the world as we wash away the sins of your kind. William: Call it as you will but in the end you are looking for the same thing, the domination of the world. Because when my “kind” are wiped out then what? You will take it upon yourselves to act as judge, jury, and executioner against other mortals? Council: You waste our time vampire. William: Correction, you waste my time. Yes, I am a vampire but I am so much more. Those people that I am being charged with killing did something that made them guilty at one point in time. So if you want to shave it close to the skin. This Order and I are very much alike. Council: HOW DARE YOU! William: I do dare. The only difference between me and this Order is that you hide behind the excuse that you are on a mission from God! But as to these Trials of Redemption I shall participate and make it through. If the only reason of doing so is to have a clear conscious when I tear this place down brick by brick and if necessary…body by body! [William’s eyes glow green and the hunters let him and Annabelle leave.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle stares into the campfire and you can see the fire in his eyes differs from the one on the ground,] Syckle: The trials of Redemption were just that. They were a series of seven tests that took place over the next decade. Over those ten years Annabelle and I came to find that we had deep feelings for each other. We had grown close and while going through the trials we had slayed many demons. One week before the final trial was to begin Annabelle and I had tracked a particular nasty demon to a moor near Sussex, England. We cornered and killed the beast after a vicious fight. We traveled for a day or two across the cloudy moor and came upon a town. By that time we had learned that my final trial was to occur in three days time. That night Annabelle and I fell asleep under a full moon. I had awoken the next morning to find wolf tracks around what was our campsite. I thought the wolf had just simply wandered, sniffed, and then went away when it found no food. Annabelle and I traveled to the nearest town and found the local pub. We order drink and food. As we ate I overheard the bartender talking about a wolf attack. He said that it was no ordinary wolf. He had said that it walked on two legs instead of four. I asked Annabelle if she knew of any werewolves that prowled in that part of England. She wasn’t aware of any. We decided to check things out and sure enough the man had been attacked by a werewolf. The man’s throat had been ripped out. On the eve of my final trial Anna had decided to stay in town as I tried to track down the werewolf. I went back to where the man had been attacked but could find nothing. So I came back to the town to see a large crowd of townsfolk standing in the town square. In the middle of the people was a large fire. That is when I heard the screaming and recognized it as Annabelle’s. * * * 120 years ago Town square of the village [William rushed up to the fire as Annabelle screams echo against the buildings. The townspeople cheered as Annabelle burned. William wanted to jump up to help her but he knew as long as he was a vampire he would be burned to death. He looked around the crowd and then saw a face that he hadn’t seen in over ten years. Nathaniel was staring at his burning sister and smiled. Hearing nothing but Anna’s screams in his head he attacked,] William: YOU BASTARD!!! [William and Nathan went tumbling. William vamped out and started to hit Nathan with all he had. He continued to hit him until the world was silent. William looked back and saw nothing put smoldering embers and ashes. He stood up and started to attack the townspeople. He caught one young woman and wanted looked to sink his teeth in when he heard a voice in his head. It was the voice of Anubis,] Anubis: Annabelle wouldn’t want you to do that. You are stronger than your hate. Resist. RESIST! William: URGH! Who are you? Anubis: A friend. Now find Nathan he is the one that did this. [William looked around and spotted Nathan chowing down on an old man. William threw the girl to the ground and started to attack Nathan again. William pinned Nathan up against a wall and started to ask questions,] William: Why was Annabelle killed? [Nathan smiled,] Nathan: That is for me to know and you to never find out. [William reached down to his boot and pulled out a wooden spike. He jammed it into Nathaniel’s shoulder,] Nathan: Ahhhhh! William: One more time, why was Anna killed? Nathan: Because she was the same as you and me. A killer! William: YOU LIE! [William drives the stake into Nathan’s other shoulder,] Nathan: No I don’t. She was a werewolf William. She was born one. Just like I was born a vampire. William: She never told me that. Nathan: She didn’t want you to know. [William takes the stake and stabs Nathan in the knee,] William: You still lie! Nathan: No. All the members of my family that shares my father’s blood is either born a vampire or werewolf. Males are born Vampire and Females born werewolf. The females of the family were meant to keep the males in check. William: That doesn’t explain why she was burned at the stake. [Nathan just smiles again. William turns the stake,] Nathan: One of the townies saw her change. The captured her and then killed her. There are you happy. [William rips the stake out and drops it to the ground. Nathan gets up and smells the air,] Nathan: It’s like the old days. Everyone running away from us. I love the smell of fear. It gets the blood pumping. Feel like a meal? William: No. Nathan: Suit yourself. [Nathan grabs a hold of a young woman and bites into her neck. She screams and looks at William,] Woman: HELP ME! [William looks on without emotion,] Nathan: Are you going to stop me? William: I don’t see how it is my problem. [With those chilling words William walks away into the night.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle’s fist clench and unclench,] Syckle: That was the darkest day in my life. I never completed my trials but I did learn that Anubis had the power for me to never forget Annabelle. He turned me into a werewolf to find and seek out Nathaniel after I found out that it was actually he who orchestrated the burning at the stake. I had found him again but could not kill him. But I did give a fate worse then death. I placed my hand on his head and let him feel the pain of every victim that he had killed. After that incident, Anubis thought it would be better if I had a clean start. So he blocked my memory of my past and let me sleep for over a century and change. I could believe what I would find when I awoke that fateful day. To be continued…
