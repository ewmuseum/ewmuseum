Rejection Doesn't Come Naturally

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 It is a dark, dreary night outside of Omaha, Nebraska. A park, near 56th and Sorensen Parkway, can be seen off in the distance. A dark figure, wearing a black hoodie with jeans, walks up to a bench at the end of the park. The figure is holding something. All of a sudden the screen cuts to black. The view comes back into focus, except on the opposite side of the street. The camera suddenly moves around and shows that figure sitting on the bench. “Sensational” Sean White is smiling. “Wow!” Sean exclaims. Sean takes a pause and examines the street. “It’s been quite an eventful week. First, Neon closes and now it is time for GTT Four. GTT has quite a history behind it, the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament or is it the Global Title Tournament, there is a lot of mystery behind the name of the tournament so I’m just going to call it GTT Four,” Sean inhales after the sentence, pauses and stares straight ahead of him as a car speeds past him. Sean leans back in the bench a little further. “You know, finally I am getting some recognition after busting my ass for Neon, but I’m not really sitting here to talk about that,” Sean smiles. “It’s the Primetime Central debut of the Sensational Cam!” Sean smiles again as a car speeds passed him. Sean stands up off the bus bench and begins walking away from it. The Sensational Cam looks straight ahead of Sean as he continues to browse around the street. The park has some people still in the vicinity. The camera looks down as does Sean with his watch on his left arm. The time reads “9:01.” Also near the park is a car dealership, the main building is closed with the lights off, a lot of cars are in the parking lot that they have. Right behind the car dealership is what looks like a school, the building has a baseball field right next to it, and a scoreboard with it’s back to the dealership in left field. “Northwest High School,” Sean calmly says, “the place of defeat for my former school Benson High School.” Sean shifts slowly and uses the camera to look around. Sean turns the camera around to face him and press a button on it. The light on the camera turns on. Sean turns the camera back around to face the school. “Every game, every football game that Benson played Northwest we lost. We were always shutout.” Sean pauses again slowly breathing in, “every fourth and goal we tried, rejected. We were not allowed in the end zone again them. Hell for two years I went to Benson we didn’t win on game, we didn’t even score a single point. Of course rejection was normal for the Benson Bunnies.” Sean cringes at that name. “I hated going there. Although the school had some decent people friends wise but the Bunnies?” Sean pauses as he hears some screaming from the basketball court. Sean turns the camera to face the men that are playing, six dark figures are playing three on three basketball. “Look at these men,” Sean spouts out. “These guys, I’m sure that they have tried numerous times to get into the National Basketball Association, the NBA. They weren’t playing for fun then, they were playing for a spot on a team, make millions of dollars, get arrested and charged for something they didn’t do.” Sean pauses and uses the camera to zoom in on these six men at one of the hoops. A man makes a basket as the rest of the men, except one start to go down court to try and score. “They could be playing for fun, but of course not, those six men are thinking about making it in the NBA, what they could have been, and what they aren’t. It doesn’t take a smart man, but by all means I have the intellect of more than some people, to realize that these guys had their dream rejected from them,” Sean pauses again and starts to move closer to the basketball hoop. Sean reaches a park bench right in between of the basketball court and the street. He sits down and points the camera back at the basketball players. The guys are now standing around just trying to dunk while another gets up to try and block them. One guy goes up and slams the ball through the hoop while the over five shout out some sayings. “Oooooooooooooooooo,” two of the men scream. “Man, that was off da chain yo,” another says. “See, they talk like that to fit in. They don’t want to be rejected. Rejection, heh, that seems like the word of the day for me. I don’t want to be rejected again,” Sean stops, listens to the bouncing basketball again. “Rejection, in my own words means getting stopped from doing something, not being allowed. According to Webster’s Dictionary is the...” Sean pulls out a pocket dictionary from his back pocket. Sean flips through some pages. “The action of rejecting, the state of being rejected*” Sean closes the dictionary and sticks it in his back pocket, “but of course that does not help me on bit. It is kind of a vague definition but that is what I get for buying a seventy-two cent pocket dictionary.” Sean adjusts himself and the camera. The camera turns around as Sean spots the six men leaving the basketball court. Sean takes the camera and points it at his watch. The clock now reads “9:11.” Sean speaks up to himself again, “There is a reason I entered this tournament, to make a name for myself, to show everyone how ‘Sensational’ I really am. Although by the looks of it, it seems that everyone in Primetime Central seems to have an awful relationship with their family, but I had a good relationship with my family, my friends, and everyone I’ve been around, but there is a new man trying to take my throne, one Mr. Ryan Nox from XUW.” Sean pauses as a gust of wind swirls around him. “I’m briefly going to talk about you Mr. Nox, just because I don’t want to spend my whole night in a park alone by myself. I don’t know much about you, and like you don’t know much about me. I’m not going to bad mouth you at all, because that really isn’t the way I should be speaking about a person I have never even seen before,” Sean stops talking and shifts himself in the chair. “I will guarantee one thing though, I will not be rejected again. I will not be rejected by you, PTC, or anyone else. Rejection is not an option like it was for Neon Wrestling, those six men, and my old high school. I will not be rejected by the PTC, GTT 4 Tournament participants or you Ryan Nox,” Sean looks around and then at the time on his watch again. It reads “9:30.” Sean pauses for a couple of seconds, “Some of you are comedians, serious people or religious atheists and then there is ‘Sensational’ Sean White. I am none of these, I’m just a laid back say whatever is on my mind kind of guy. Make sure you keep your eyes on me, you’ll need to.” Sean speaks up again, “I know I haven’t talk too much, but I’m not in the mood to talk, I’m not in the mood to do anything right now, I’m going to go home and sleep. Whether I win or lose though, Primetime Central you have not seen the last of me.” Sean stands up and starts to walk away from the park bench towards his home. He places his hand by the camera and turns it off, a beep is heard and the screen goes black. *Definition from webster.com
