When you take that sip, whether you take it with one sugar, two creams, a “regular” for us Bostonians, black as a motherfucker, whatever, however, you are not YOU. YOU are not you. Vice versa, visa vie, and whatever else. When you drop a tab of acid and you can't find the space button on your computer, youarenotyou. YouarenotYOU. You're in a world of reality that is so true to itself and the nature of the beast, but it isn't you. It isn't someone else. Who is it? When you are with that girl, the one that makes your stomach giggle, that one where you said that stupid fucking thing to and you're puking up the regret into a toilet because why the hell did you say that, you are not you.
