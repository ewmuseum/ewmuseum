Redemption

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-27 The placid night sky rolls gently across the treetops, gusts of whistling wind caressing the distant hilltops. A dreary site is beheld as we see the silhouette of a man on his knees, sprawled out in front of an old, cracking tombstone. A bottle in hand, he trembles, a shaky hand slowly grazing the inscription. "Robin Walker...a loving mother... a dear friend..." The figure read aloud, in somewhat of a drunken slur. "January 2nd.. 1967... to March 25th..1999..." Slowly, the tone of voice changes, as he becomes filled with anger, pain and sorrow all at once. "Dearest mother...you left me... you left me, here alone. My brother...I lost him, too, mother. It ate me alive inside, too. But I didn't give up... I didn't let it destroy me completely like you did...You had another son, mother...You had another son. You... you abandoned me, to be with him. You left me here, alone, because of your pain and suffering. You pushed your burden of grief onto me like a bastard child on my doorstep, and the fool that I am, I took it inside. I let it live and grow inside of me until I too could no longer take it. Amy died while you were gone, mother. Everyone around me seems destined for death... my existence is a plague upon mankind, but so I've come to accept it." Justin lowers his head, tears dropping from his eyes, falling to the ground, ending their life with a glorious splatter across the drying yellow grass. "These tears...its as if these tears were my very soul escaping me, the burning inside my heart flooding out into the world, searing the flesh and scarring my mind forever in a way that no physical pain can ever come to match. But mother, I will not be like you. I will not allow the rage and the pain destroy me. No, because I'm stronger then you ever could be. I rose up from those ashes you burnt me into oh so long ago..I rose, as a phoenix, charged with the blazing fires of hell, and I've grown to have a purpose. I have to prove to you, mother..I have to prove to myself. I have to prove to everyone that ever doubted me, everyone that never gave a damn about me, I have to prove to them that my name is not a name to be forgotten, that I am above all else destined for greatness. And step by step, I'm doing just that. I climb the ladder to the top slowly but surely, and nothing can hamper my ascent to glory, nothing." A burst of life comes into Justin as he rises to his feet, his eyes now cast upon the twilight above. "One by one they all fall down. Their names, what promotion they come from, none of this matters, none will ever matter. I will write the pages of my history with their blood, stopping only once I have climbed the ladder and claimed what is rightfully mine. Mother, I will do what you could never do. I will overcome adversary, I will persevere. But don't get me wrong..I do this not for you..not for my brother..not for Amy...not for 'the fans'. Oh, no... I do this for myself." With that, he drops the empty beer bottle at his side, slowly shifting away from the dreary site, murmuring to himself. "Robb Hardy...'The Fool'...welcome to history."
