Massacre of the Mind '09 Chapter 1: Council of Kadosh

1 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2009-12-01 “Our Father, who art in heaven, Hallowed be thy Name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done, On earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our trespasses, As we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, But deliver us from evil. [For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever and ever.] Amen.” --The Lords Prayer “Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil walks about like a roaring lion, seeking whom he may devour.” --1 Peter 5:8 I felt like a Rolling Stone song as I pulled into the Garden State. Thanksgiving was the past and so where the 9 homicides and five suicides is Cook County (Chicago) Illinois over the Thanksgiving holiday. Satan was moving in, and hoss time was ticking. I was thankful for a lot of things, but I never once thought life to be one of them. Especially in this cluster of fucks that has become my life. What happened to the Fat man, how I obtained his car, and what was real and what was insanity is as unclear as the proposed health care plan put forth by the President and his men. My only instinct (one of the few things I still trust) is telling me just to push on. So Jersey it is, and so the adventure rambles incoherently on. Jesus was indeed in Jersey --which would make for a great Bruce Springsteen song. I’m not a fan. You either love Bruce, or you just don’t get him. I don’t get him. Never have, never will. Anyway Jesus was indeed somewhere around me, but where? Who had him? Who was leading me to him? Where can I find a steak at this time of night? The answer to the last part of my self questioning was answered by a dirty road side diner, just outside of Trenton. It was the kind of place where the waitresses look tired, old, gritty and full of stories. It was the kind of place where the hash browns are flawless, and the pie is even more perfect, putting the whole debate of the flawlessness of the hash browns in question. The perfect circle of the perfection equation created and solved using pie. It was the kind of place people look at you when you enter, because they know everybody who comes in there, and you are not one of them. It was the perfect kind of place to find satisfaction in a warm savory steak especially if you’re a lost savior on a journey of lunacy. I ate the steak fast like a rabid dog; and soaked up the remaining blood into the bread representing: life, redemption, and the body of Christ. If only he was as easy to find as he was to taste. The crusty old waitress asked me if I wanted some pie, and I asked her when she got off. This made the old lady’s yellow teeth light up bright as the sun. She gave me a free piece of apple pie, I obliged. American charm still exists but the question is; does heaven? Jesus was never harder to find then in the lords year of ’09. Two years before the end of time and he was being a dodgy elusive little shit. Last time I saw him he was a hot chick hanging on to me on the back of my Harley in the middle of the wasteland. I guess the man knows how to blend, but the problem is I really don’t know what I’m looking for. That’s the same problem faced by most of the people seeking out Jesus. It’s easy to know his name, and where he’s been; but it’s another thing to know where he’s going and where he will be finding the meaning in it all. With a belly full of questions and a head full of satisfaction I got back into my stolen blood stained Cadillac with the goal of figuring something out. Why did nine people have to die over the course of the one holiday that’s based on fellowship and family? The devil was a real motherfucker but I enjoyed a good challenge; and really another day locked up in the slavery of complacency that is everyday life didn’t appeal to me. I was born to be wild and the fire burned deep, as hell itself. I knew this would end over my dead body, but I was ready to make the statement. As the speedometer reached a hundred and nine I felt as free as a spaceman floating through our galaxy in an instrument of travel that is so far beyond our understanding. So much so, that frustration is the only emotion we can produce when thinking of it and its splendor. Fuck it. I had nothing to die for, and everything to live for, so much to the point that life itself was no longer is an issue, but merely a factor. The world whizzed by as I sat and wondered how flawless our language is, giving us woozy as a word and justification wasn’t hard to find. Just look under J. Don’t mind if I do. The fat man had funny cigarettes in the ash tray --how very sub-genius of him. I smoked myself into a woozy state of speed, sanity, and slow. “Will the Circle Be Unbroken” played on the radio for the last time coming from some strange station’s signal, beaming to the masses and falling on the deaf ears of progress. Radio was dead. It had been sick for so long we all saw it coming, so when the day came, nobody shed a tear as the news of its demise appeared on the fading ink of the morning paper. That was when I spotted the red lights of the friendly New Jersey state highway patrol. With blood on the steering wheel, dope in the ashtray, insanity in the air, and the worlds well being teetering on the edge of a total toppling of morals and dogma the friendly interference of Officer Friendly is always welcome! Hell it’s even encouraged. When you’re being raped by a gang of petty thieves perpetrating a life shattering felony there is never a cop around. Sure the old days of fearing a police beating are gone; now something much worse existed. Bureaucracy. Back in the day, a beating was the end of the line and that healed fast. Today you will be handled with soft hands, and rubber gloves. But once in the system you don’t know what you’re going to get, judged by a stranger who may or may not be having a bad day. Plus with computers it all follows you now and forever, amen. No more vanishing and starting a new life. I wish I lived in the good old days when the bad times were a changing. “Gentle On my Mind” is the most perfectly written song in the history of American Music. My situation was opposite my previous statement in every possible way. The cock, I mean cop, approached the door cautiously like he knew something I didn’t. Which given the fact I really didn’t know what the hell was going on in my life, the afterlife, or the Life of Riley for that matter; he very well indeed may have know something I didn’t. I kept my hands on the wheel in plain sight as I didn’t want any Jersey hotshot taking a cheap shot on me. “Grazie, Prego, Scusi” by Dean Martin was on the radio as the cop opened my car door with his heat pointed straight at me. I didn’t mention the song for frivolously reasons or due to some deeper meaning or importance. I only mention it because it’s the kind of thing I always want to know and nobody can ever answer. What song was on the radio, just before you got into that car crash? Maybe the song is cursed? Maybe I was just fucking cursed. This is all going to shit in a taco. I blew a kiss to the pig to set the mood with the oinker outright. ‘I’m the son of God’ I ranted loudly and with large hand movements as he and his cock sucker partner started to panic yelling orders and scary terms of compliance. I unzipped my pants and started to spin my pecker like a helicopter blade which the pink boys didn’t find to funny as I would have guessed in such a tense and awkward situation. Nobody has a sense of humor anymore, let alone a sense of justice. I was tackled, pecker out and all --which to me seemed like a cheap shot so I fought back. They fought back harder, with their pants still around their waists. Two hits to the head from the batten helped me find that lost generation of the 1960’s just as I faded back to the blackness of the Stone Age. I woke up on the cold stainless steel cot of conformity in the New Jersey department of corrections. A far cry from the Sterns & Foster bed a man like me is accustomed to slumbering on. I was sore and beaten down a mirror image of how my opponents usually feel. HA HA HA feed the ego and the pride when it’s all you got left. That’s why gangs and rappers exist. They threw the book at me. I ducked. I was charged with everything but positive energy leaving me feeling very negative to the whole damn situation. I need to change my disposition and underwear as it had been a very long and draining day. They took my shoe laces, and my freedom, and I needed a nap so sleep is what I did. I awoke to find myself trapped in the past facing mirror images that were not my own. Sorry a Quantum Leap moment. Anyway I work up when the friendly police officers were adding another addition to my already way to small studio apartment in the Jersey’s Department of corrections. I protested as they ignored me slamming the cell door on me and my new found friend. “Hi I’m Hoyt Williams,” I exclaimed! The man was frazzled and it showed in his droopy old eyes. He was an elderly wino with a big white beard, messy hair, cigarettes stained teeth, and a way to skinny body for any man to have. He ignored me as he just went straight for the bottom bunk. “Need some rest?” I questioned. He just closed his eyes and soon was snoring as was the whole world to the real happenings of the drama that surrounds them. I closed my eyes too, only what I pictured was destruction and chaos which wasn’t as fun as having my eyes wide open so I sat back up. Jail is boring, but nightmares are out of my control. “What the fuck is up with those Bears,” I conversed, “they need to fire Lovie Smith tomorrow. The guy is a bum. A bum I tell you.” “What did you call me?” The wino came to life. “I said Lovie Smith is a bum.” “Who, now?” “Lovie Smith. “ “Lovely Smith? Is that a black prostitute on twelfth?” “No, Lovie Smith. He’s a black head coach in the NFL,” I corrected him, “of the Chicago Bears.” “He’s a bum? I don’t know him!” “He runs in small circles,” I joked. Talking to the old homeless man was better than talking to nobody at all. Sure I couldn’t discuss politics or the 30,000 troops going to Afghanistan but an hour on the best canned beans at this time was brilliant and all I needed. “I like the beans with the bacon fat in them,” said the Wino. “They give me the shits,” I farted. I’m rambling off track because I was born a rambling man. It’s all bullshit. Everything and anything you think is real, is nothing more than gibberish. Footprints in the sand is God walking upon us as we are nothing more than grains of sand in somebody’s paradise. Always a servant, never a master we are the bee’s of the universe. A few queens, a few drones, and a ton of workers who work and exist with little to no understanding as to why. What’s the big picture for them? But they keep right on dancing along keeping the world pollinated in the process. Another damn gear in a much bigger machine we simply can’t see. “I like that Bumble Bee brand tuna. I eat it right out of the can!” Smiled the wino. “No shit?” I lied. Don’t you see man? Just as the ant has no concept of humanity there are things so big and different we don’t understand them. Some are the same size as us but different, that’s when the weird gets too strange to accept, yet too interesting to ignore. Like Mormons and their magic underwear. You see I’d rather sit here and chat with you then that old stinky bum. The wino in the cell with me I mean, not Lovie Smith. “I wish blue balls were as fun as they sounded.” He laughed. “I wish balls were more fun then they sounded, period! They hurt like a mother fucker and are WAY too sensitive.” I yapped. You can’t find Jesus until you identify him without him prompting you to do so. If Jesus was an ant you would have to see past his antness witnessing faith in its basic form; and only then would you see him. Once you see him if you let him know, you know, he talks to you and another rebirth is achieved. It’s a real pain in the ass but if the system ever fully developed everybody would be treating everybody else like Jesus in the process of searching out the real thing. Like most great ideas the execution wasn’t as good as the plan sounded. “Jesus?” I asked. “My own brother and it took almost two day to get you to see me,” the Wino responded,” some kind of blood you are!” “You really want to do this right now?” How lame could a man be to not see the obvious? EVERYBODY finds Jesus in prison. This is when he is the easiest to find. Once a man is stripped of his pride and freedom the only thing left is his faith. All I had left was my faith, and now here I am finding my long lost brother. Jesus was a dude once again thankfully. “You need to get out of here and get to the next round of the tournament.” Jesus stated. “I’m starting to wonder why I don’t just get a hotel near the tournament since every one of these adventures always ends up back there,” I bitched, “if I just stayed in my room and ate room service somebody would either lock me up crazy, or the world would end, and at last I would be at peace.” “Hey man,” Jesus muttered, “you have no idea the shit I’ve been through over the course of the last week. You think nails are bad? You think that Mel Gibson movie showed the hell that was my life? Just wait until the sequel comes out explaining my adventures here back on earth! I’ve been a woman, I’ve been raped, I’ve been an old man, I’ve been a cat, a caterpillar, Santa Clus, and now a wino. Ok? It’s not so swell being Jesus you know? Sure Jesus Christ made you number two and yes that sucks but my stories never end up with happy endings for me, so maybe a little less attitude here. I mean you know, I did die for your sins.” “Jesus Christ…enough with the theatrics over here…huh? I mean for your own sake cut that out,” I demanded, “we are on the brink of destruction and our father who enjoys art in heaven is in trouble!” “Our Father, who art in heaven,” Jesus corrected. “I know he likes Art. You know something I think the GTT is the wrong place to go. I think it’s a waste of time and my plan is garbage. We need to start thinking like the enemy.” I yelled like Patton. “Your plan is working you mad dolt! Karina Wolfenden out, Alias in.” Jesus informed me. “What?” I heard him but I had him repeat it just to clarify and make sure I wasn’t hearing things. Surely Wolfenden didn’t want to get in the ring with me. I understand that. She heard the good lord a knocking, and she went a packing. But Alias? Alias is a common name for Satan, aka The Abaddon, aka The Accuser, the Adversary, the Beast, Beelzebub, the Deceiver, the Enemy, The Roaring Lion, The Devil. So he took my bait and now was my chance to reclaim heaven and save my father and my own existence. All very heavy goals for a guy sitting in prison with his brother next to him not able to bail him out thanks to the stupid vow of poverty he took in some silly hippie phase. All things work out in the end, and I really had that positive belief. I’m a negative thinker but that doesn’t mean deep down I don’t think the world is good and that most of my problems do indeed find solutions. This was one of those very moments. God was still alive. Jesus was ok, and it was comforting having him beside me, even if we were in the same jam. We needed a miracle and we were the two guys who could make that happen. We bickered over a prison meal trying to come up with an appropriate plan. The best involved Jesus actually dying then coming alive yet again in the morgue and quickly escaping somehow finding a way to set me free. In the end though we settled on turning all the toilet water into wine creating a mass riot of lunacy at the very melting point we would turn into doves and fly away. Turing an entire prisons toilet water into prison wine is a taxing miracle that takes at least a day of meditating to achieve. I tend to use my miracles as cheap parlor tricks to gain free drinks and pick up sinners. So I was nowhere near qualified to help out. I hummed a sad Mickey Newburry song while I waited. The lesson was simple: A man who forgets his duties to god, country, and himself was indeed on the brink of destruction. It took a lot to get there but I’m feeling it and understanding the mission I am on. I had to walk away from the temptation of my past in order to achieve the ability to be here now to help protect the future. If I didn’t’ care, I couldn’t beat the devil. The lord works in mysterious ways, this situation just happens to be all logical and strategic. Everything was real, and everything that was happening to me had an effect on something greater then my own self interest. A real beauty in the making. In order to understand the madness I had to: feel, taste, and consume the madness. It was, and is, the trial of my life; just as Jesus carried the cross. To accept it, is to pass it, and that fact was comforting. I noticed the toilet water starting to turn a purple ting as Jesus worked his family magic. Once out of here I knew Alias was mine for the taking. The devil no matter how bad of a mother fucker cannot beat me in the ring. I’m that damn good, and I’m so damn angry I want to make him pay. I was happily retired before Satan had to go and mess everything up, and he will die, and I will kill him. At least that’s what I thought. “Williams you are out of here,” yelled the guard. Jesus snapped out of his trance just as the toilet water turned to wine. “What about me?” asked Jesus. “Just Williams,” responded the guard. “See you later brother and stick to the plan,” I said through a grin. The grin vanished fast when I saw who was bailing me out. With a bandage on his face and a satanic look in his eyes the fat lawyer had returned. “Fuck,” I sighed. “Indeed,” said the fat man.
