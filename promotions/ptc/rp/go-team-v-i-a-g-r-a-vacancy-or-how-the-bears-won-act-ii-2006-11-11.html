Go Team V.I.A.G.R.A. - Vacancy or How the Bears Won(Act II)

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-11 INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH – DAYTIME [Eddie Scott Poser sits in a chair in an empty room, still crying into his hands. He throws his head back and lets out a blood curdling scream of despair.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Why would he do this! WHY would he LEAVE us? I can’t get home from Hong Kong. I don’t even know where Hong Kong is. I know it’s not New Jersey… that’s a start. INT. HOTEL ROOM – DAYTIME [TONY DAVIS tosses a few clothes into his duffle bag, and reaches into his right pants pocket to pull out his cell phone. Seeing no calls, he zips up his bag, and leaves the room Moments later, the young brunette from the earlier evening walks out of the bathroom, and looks around cautiously. She shrugs her shoulders, and returns into the bathroom, closing the door behind her.] INT. AUTOMOBILE – PORTUGAL – DAYTIME [High Flyer is currently driving on what looks to be a highway. Edwards is in his front passenger seat, still admiring himself in the mirror on his passenger visor. Klein, sitting in the back seat, continues to see random bits of technology, cell phones, video games, PDA’s, and shrieks because of his startling fear. He scans the backseat again, and sees everything once more, shrieking for each before raising his head to see Jack’s eye line in his rear view mirror.] KLEIN: They’re… they’re surrounding me… HIGH FLYER: Klein. Shut up. They aren’t surrounding anything. I don’t think they’re even moving. [Just then, Flyer has to slam into the breaks, sending Klein and the various elements of technology flying around the back seat. A PDA lands on his lap, which Klein quickly tosses off to the side by flailing. He shrinks slightly, as he sees Jack’s cold hard gaze directed at him.] KLEIN: Are we there yet? HIGH FLYER: [in disbelief] I’m… still driving. EDWARDS: You gonna call Tony? HIGH FLYER: No. He’s a little bitch. EDWARDS: I’d say you’re the little bitch, little bitch. Are we there yet? [CUT : A small woodsy area appears behind the off ramp of a road. Suddenly, Flyer’s car comes into view, screeching to a halt. The passenger side door opens and Edwards is thrust outside, landing on his keester. The back door opens and Klein appears, running away from a cordless hair curler that had attached itself to his shirt. The tires screech over the concurrent traffic as Flyer pulls away and back onto the main road, both doors still wide open. Edwards stands there with mouth agape, before looking over his shoulder to his friend and tag team partner racing around the woods looking like a man with a cartoon dog biting his ass. Edwards turns back to the road disheartened.] EDWARDS: Now how are we gonna get home? [Edwards pauses for a second, as if he’s trying to listen to something. His eyes widen.] EDWARDS: Wha-… was that a wolf? INT. AUTOMOBILE – PORTUGAL – DAYTIME [High Flyer cranks up the tunes on his ride, the soundtrack to Team America, and then swerves his car in just the right way to use physics to shut both car doors without missing a step.] HIGH FLYER: They’ll be alright… they all will be. INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH – DAYTIME [Eddie Scott Poser continues to sob into his hands.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: WHY!?! INT. AUTOMOBILE – PORTUGAL – DAYTIME [High Flyer nods.] HIGH FLYER: They’ll be fine. EXT. WOODS – NIGHT : LATER [Josh Klein has caught his foot in a bear trap. Edwards is currently using a large scepter like stick to attempt to pry it open.] INT. AUTOMOBILE – PORTUGAL – DAYTIME [High Flyer looks off to the side slightly.] HIGH FLYER: Right? INT. AIRPORT – DAYTIME [TONY DAVIS steps up to the nearest open counter, and literally rests on its edge. The attendant types a few things into her computer and then looks up toward Tony.] ATTENDANT: Welcome to Lufthansa. Your ticket please? TONY DAVIS: See, that’s the thing. I need to get to Portugal. ATTENDANT: Okay… [She begins to type a few more keystrokes into her computer.] ATTENDANT: I’ve got a flight leaving in two hours. TONY DAVIS: I… I don’t have any money though. ATTENDANT: … Excuse me? TONY DAVIS: Can I like, wash dishes for you or something? ATTENDANT: Uhhh…. Carl! TONY DAVIS: Or I could drive the plane! That would be my first choice, but I figured I shouldn’t lead with it. Y’know, cuz who knows how many drivers you got here. ATTENDANT: CARL! TONY DAVIS: Yeah, CARL! I’ll be your Carl…. Uhhh… name me a famous Carl. ATTENDANT: [Over the intercom] Would Carl Jackson please come to the attendant desk? TONY DAVIS: Carl Jackson? C’mon. Who the hell is that. [Sternly] There aren’t any famous Carl’s, are there? [Just then, a man in a business suit walks up. He looks quite prim and proper. Flanking him on either side are two other men dressed in more generic suits, each with walky talkies and hand guns.] CARL JACKSON: Sir? Would you come with me? TONY DAVIS: Are we going to Portugal? [He immediately begins to walk off with Carl, placing his hand over his shoulder. Behind him, his flanking security force draws their pistols on an unsuspecting Tony.] TONY DAVIS: You know any famous Carl’s? Now that’s just gonna bug me… INT. CAR – PORTUGAL – NIGHT [Flyer pulls off the local highway after quite a bit of driving and enters what looks to be an airport. He pulls his car up to the side of the strip and places it in park. Tossing the keys into the dashboard, Flyer leaves his car and grabs his duffle bag, throwing in his PDA and video games and assortment of products that have been sprawled along the backseat. When he grabs his cell phone, it suddenly rings, and it’s a call from none other than Tony Davis. Flyer hits cancel and tosses it into his bag, only to be startled by a hand clamping down onto his shoulder. He turns around quickly and strikes, punching Tony Davis square in the jaw. Jack’s eyes grow wide for a split second until he realizes who it was, and then continues on his packing.] HIGH FLYER: Sor… wait, what am I sorry about? TONY DAVIS: Listen, Jack. I had a tough day. Cavity exams aren’t exactly dentally hygienic in a police station, so let me get through what I’ve been thinking about saying all day to you. [Flyer slams his car door shut and begins to walk away, ignoring Tony in the process.] HIGH FLYER: You’ve got no right to talk to me Tony. TONY DAVIS: Why, because I supposedly slept with a girl you wanted? HIGH FLYER: C’mon Tony. Do you think I’m that self-centered? TONY DAVIS: Well… yeah? [Flyer sighs, rolling his eyes in the process.] HIGH FLYER: Okay, do you think I’m self-centered when it comes to my family? TONY DAVIS: Well… yeah. You gave up Kate because the business was too important to you. HIGH FLYER: That’s what she thinks? TONY DAVIS: Dude, that’s what happened. I saw it. I watched Cyberslam last year y’know. It is a Wrestlemania type show. HIGH FLYER: Still, I didn’t care that you slept with her instead of me Tony. TONY DAVIS: Then what’s the problem?! HIGH FLYER: I care because you’re engaged to my sister. TONY DAVIS: … oh. [Davis throws his hands through his long dreadlocks.] TONY DAVIS: Welp, we didn’t sleep together anyway. HIGH FLYER: Huh? You told me you woke up with her. SHE told me. Hell, I SAW you that day. I came earlier to wake you up so we could see Hong Kong some more but… TONY DAVIS: Yeah… dude… all true. But I woke up with my pants on. And if I was as drunk as you were, I wouldn’t have taken off my pants. And if I DID take them off, I certainly wouldn’t have put them back on. [Flyer pauses, biting his bottom lip in the process before rolling his eyes. He then swings his head and begins to start walking in step with Tony toward the airport gates.] HIGH FLYER: So… how was Hong Kong? TONY DAVIS: It was Kongly. It kinda felt like watching King Kong… HIGH FLYER: Speaking of Kings… Where’s Eddie? TONY DAVIS: You know, that’s a good question. INT. AIRPORT – NIGHT [Eddie Scott Poser walks up to the attendant’s desk from earlier and throws a cooler into view. It’s labeled "Blue Poser" He then reaches down and places two more on either side, with the words Red and Yellow placed on them.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Hi. I’ve got to get to Portugal to get to Greece it seems. Uhmmm… I’d like to pay you in organs. ATTENDANT: CARL! FADE OUT TO CREDITS FADE IN : POST CREDITS TEASER : EXT. WOODS – EVENING [Edwards is still attempting to pry Klein’s foot free from a bear trap… when a Bear walks up behind Klein. Edwards stands completely still for about three seconds.] EDWARDS: Well, if this isn’t ironic. KLEIN: What is? EDWARDS: A bear gaining food from a bear trap. KLEIN: Yeah.. wait, what?! [Edwards BOLTS away, speeding as fast as he can. Klein slowly turns around, and sees the roaring bear behind him. He audibly gulps.] FADE TO BLACK
