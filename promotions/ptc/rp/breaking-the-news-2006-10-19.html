Breaking The News.

19 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-19 no brand Wrestling was making their rounds across america as one of their stars got the call about the Global Title Tournament that was being held by Prime Time Central. This would be the sixth edition of the tournament and one of nbW's best was asked to be involved. Brock Metzer was shocked and surprised by the chance to take part in the event and his cohorts in nbW were anxious for some answers. Sitting backstage was Brock Metzer; cool, calm, and collected. Seated around him in chairs and on a near by bench were Max Hopper, Austin Advent, Indy Third, and Shawn Jessica Hart. Most were close friends of Metzer, though Indy Third stuck out. Metzer had made his way through the Legacy Of Champions roster before heading off to ply his trade on the Independent Wrestling Scene, where he met Indy Third. Both men wound up in nbW and Metzer had opted to take the youngster under his wing. "So, who are you even facing first round?" Indy Third asked as he looked over towards Metzer who was in deep conversation with Max Hopper. "NOVA." Metzer said. "GUY!" Max Hopper replied with a tone of enthusiasm. "Yea, I know. He's a tough cookie, but I think I can crack him." Metzer said as he chuckled. "What do you even know about him?" Indy Third asked yet another question as his pseudo teacher thought for a moment. "He's a hardcore sadist, who likes to smoke mad blunts." "And get jiggy with the grand master." Austin Advent said finally chiming in. "Or something like that." Metzer said with a grin. "Honestly, I don't know a lot about my opponent which could be a hindrance, but what do they know about me? NOVA wrestles in PRIME, I know that much. He's been a former Extreme Champion which means our styles will surly clash. Pure Wrestling is the epitome of this great sport, not garbage. He will realize that too." "Dude, page." Shawn Jessica Hart commented. "What GUY!?" Max Hopper replied. "I've got to go." came out of Shawn Jessica Hart's mouth as he jumped up out of his seat and headed for the door. There was a loud clanging noise as the door slammed shut and the rest in the room just kind of looked on in confusion. Neither man really knew what that was all about, but the conversation continued. "Do you remember when we did the El Phantasmo XLVII shtick in Legacy Of Champions?" Max Hopper asked Metzer. "Of course I remember all of that man. It was one of the greatest times in my life. I came in working with Adam Lazarus as a tag team, then got paired with you. We had the best times in our vignettes. The low-riders, the pinata's, everything." Metzer responded. "Guy, our pinata party rocked. But nothing was as cool as my shades, I can't believe I lost my freakin' boots though." Max Hopper said as he reached down to rub his lucky white boots for well, luck. "True, but it was a good storyline. Now I have to concentrate on making it out of the first round in this tournament." Metzer said as he sighed. Was it a look of bewilderment on his face, or was he simply weighing the odds of his chances. One thing was playing through his head and that was his horrific clash with Troy Adams on nbW's Full Effect 29. Blood was the color of the night in their no-ropes barbwire match. Out of his own element, Metzer lost the match but proved he can stand toe to toe when it comes time for war. "So how are your scars?" Indy Third asked as if he was able to read Metzer's mind at that exact moment. "Funny you should ask, I don't think I'll ever get rid of these damn things. Though it does prove that I can stand tall in any playing field, or so I'd assume. I went toe to toe in razor sharp barbwire, never before and definitely never again though." Metzer said as he chuckled, rethinking and reliving the pain the match caused him. "Dude, you know what we should do?" Austin Advent piped in. "What's that GUY?" Max Hopper replied. "Girls." is all Austin Advent was able to utter. "Indy Third and a girl, damn sounds good." came the response from the green horn in the locker room. "Sounds good, I can't keep worrying about this tournament all night. Let's go out and party and live up the life a bit. Soon I'll be off on the road to get this thing started. Let's all wish me a bit of luck, as I try to do no brand Wrestling proud." Metzer said as he stood from his seat. "You'll do fine GUY!" Max Hopper said as he slapped Metzer on the back. Austin Advent stood from his seat and threw his arm around the shoulder of Brock Metzer as he announced his support. Indy Third chimed in as well, with some encouraging words. All four men made their way towards the door of the locker room as they disappeared into the hallway. Global Title Tournament Six was next on the plate for the Independent Icon, Brock Metzer. Would he succeed or fail, it was up to him. Pure Wrestling would prevail.
