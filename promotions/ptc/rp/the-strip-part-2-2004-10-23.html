The Strip (Part 2)

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 Night had crept slowly over Dave Harley as he sat in the taxicab. But up ahead, the bright lights of the Las Vegas strip beckoned him. Dave felt like he’d been sat in the cab his whole life. They had driven almost the entire length of Route 66 – from Chicago all the way to Vegas in search of Harley’s missing Personal Assistant, Jill. Days ago, Dave had sent his ever-suffering secretary on a reconnaissance mission to NGW Headquarters to spy on them and bring back detailed knowledge of his next PTC opponent, Dallas Reeves. However, the next day he discovered she had eloped (with the money he gave her for expenses) and had travelled to Vegas to get married. Harley figured NGW must be behind this in some way. He was certain that this was some kind of ploy on behalf of Dallas Reeves to catch him off guard. Of course, Dave knew it would never work. He wasn’t concerned for Jill, only the few hundred dollars he’d given her that he wanted back. He was also interested in finding out just what kind of person would want to marry Jill… “Here we are. Where next?” That was Rick, the cab driver. He may have been essentially kidnapped by Dave Harley and forced to bring him all this way, but he seemed quite excited to be part of this strange little road trip. “Umm…I dunno…” Dave wondered where Jill might head. He tried to place himself in her shoes, but the thought of being his own secretary made his head spin. “The Casino?” “Which one, idiot?” From here they all looked pretty inviting. The Las Vegas Strip looked like something that had come straight out of an epileptic’s nightmares. “How about we check out a few of these hotels. Maybe we’ll find a lead.” Rick smiled as he turned the cab in to the driveway of The Mirage. He imagined the look on his wife’s face when he pulled up in a limousine after winning big on the tables. Dave gazed in awe at the splendour of the hotel and casino. He knew his ex X Corp team mate Chance owned a casino in England – he bet it looked nothing like this. They both strolled in to Reception and Dave casually leaned over the counter and caught the eye of the receptionist. “Can I help you, sir?” “Yes, I’m looking for a Jill. She was my secretary.” “What’s her surname?” He honestly had no idea. He’d never paid that much attention to her. Realising that this avenue of investigation was futile, he headed for the Casino floor. Harley’s eyes gleamed as he looked at the Roulette wheel. Problem was, he’d spent his last hundred bucks on Route 66 souvenirs at the gas station. He turned to Rick. “Hey Dick, how much money you got?” “Rick.” “Whatever. C’mon, how much?” “Umm…” Rick rifled through his pockets. He pulled out a handful of change and began counting it. “That’s all you’ve got?! Give me the keys to your cab.” Rick handed Dave the keys. Harley pushed his way over to the Roulette table and threw the keys down on 26. “What the hell are you doing?!” “Relax. I’ve got a good feeling about number 26.” Harley looked reassuringly at his temporary teammate. Rick listened as the ball rolled around and settled on a number. “Seven!” Dave put a hand on Rick’s shoulder. “Easy come, easy go.” He then rushed over to a passing waitress and grabbed a drink from her tray. Rick stood there devastated. He’d just lost his car and his job…all because of Dave Harley! Rick was distracted by the sounds of an argument over at the Craps table. He heard security yell, “This isn’t real money! Look at it…real money doesn’t have a picture of a monkey on it! …That’s a man?! I could have sworn it was a monkey! But still, there’s no such thing as The Bank of Harley!” Rick tried to get closer and saw a man in his mid fifties being dragged from the table by security. He chased Dave, who in turn was chasing a Showgirl. “Hey wait up! I think I’ve found your secretary!” Harley stopped, “Oh yeah?” “A guy’s just been dragged away by security for having fake money with your picture on it.” “Hey! That money is not fake, alright? Just because I print it myself doesn’t mean it’s not legal tender!” “Well they took him off that way.” Harley grinned as a plan formed in his twisted head. “OK, let’s go. Follow my lead.” The man had been dragged to a holding cell in the back of the Casino. A security guard stood watch over him as he yelled that the money was not his – that it had been given to him by his fiancée. The guard wouldn’t listen. He’d heard all these excuses before. Suddenly, the door to the holding area burst open and Dave Harley stormed in wearing his sunglasses and leather jacket. “Hey! You’re not allowed back here!” The security guard yelled as Rick stepped through the door behind Harley. Harley reached in to his pocket and pulled out a toy police badge. He flashed it to the guard. “I’m Agent Cool of the FBI, this is my partner Agent Ass. We’ve come to interrogate the prisoner.” “Interrogate? Why have they sent you guys out? And why didn’t I hear about it?” Harley sighed, “We don’t have much time! This man knows where a nuclear weapon is hidden! We need to get the deactivation codes! Now wait outside!” The flustered guard ran outside and Harley approached the cell. The man looked at him quizzically. “You’re not FBI, you’re that jackass on the fake money. Jill told me all about you. I ought to kick your ass!” “Shut up asshole. Where’s Jill and why did you kidnap her? Is Dallas Reeves behind this?” “What? What does Dallas Reeves have to do with this? I’m one of the NGW Road Crew. I met Jill at NGW Headquarters a few days ago. I didn’t kidnap her, we ran away together!” So that was her plan. Run off and leave him to do his own chores! “Where is she?” “Room 208. Now are you gonna help me get out or what?” “Hell no. You try to steal my secretary you pay the price. Adios chump.” Harley walked out and turned to the security guard. “He says he hid the codes up his ass. You’ll need to perform a physical. Hurry up! No time for lubrication!” “Yessir!” Harley and Rick could still hear his screams as they reached Jill’s door. They knocked and she answered – she looked far from thrilled to see her employer. “Mr Harley! I…I…” “No need to explain Jill, it’s all taken care of. You’ll never hear from that loser again.” Jill looked heartbroken, but it was futile to argue with him. Not only was he incredibly stupid, but he was the leader of Devil’s Own. “Let’s head home Jill. We’ll take your car, this guy lost his on a game of Roulette.” Ando so Harley headed back to Chicago with his secretary in tow and…that cab driver he picked up along the way. He still hadn’t found out any dirt on his next opponent, Dallas Reeves, but he knew he could beat him. He was Dave Harley – nobody else even comes close.
