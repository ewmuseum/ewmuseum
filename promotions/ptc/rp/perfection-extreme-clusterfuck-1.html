After a while Canadian Perfect regains his composure and stares down the camera) (Canadian Perfect) Sally, Sally, Sally! I feel sorry for you. You are battling so many battles within yourself. Your emotions are getting the best of you. You said it so well yourself when you said, "I can't think how I can hurt my poor Perfect baby!" You are obsessed with Canadian Perfect. You are probably sitting in your dressing room, performing the two-finger manipulation to yourself, as you gaze upon my model perfect face. You want me sooooo bad that it hurts you. Your one desire is to get close to me and soon all your dreams come true. Too bad you won't be able to remember it! (Canadian Perfect has a sadistic grin on his face) (Canadian Perfect) You see PTC wrestlers, you can spill out all your emotions all you want, but the fact of the matter is... You have no chance. You don't have a chance at New Era, not just because you are a inferior, but because I am Canadian Perfect. It doesn’t matter if you have a sausage roll or an oven in your pants; the fact is that I will beat you. Why? Because I am the rising star of the LOW, and everyone knows it. The media is buzzing around the PTC because of me. The people have my name on the tips of their tongue as they await the anticipation of my debut. But people are yet to realize that I went back home. Yes I went back to Canada and was in an intense training schedule. So what does that mean to all the PTC wrestlers? Well you thought I was good before, well just wait until New Era. You will be the example. You will not only be a message to LOW, you will be a message to the entire PTC. After our match, when my arm is raised in victory, you will be the message. The message to them all that if they want to mess with Canadian Perfect, that they are going to get run down and then run over. I am the new superstar of the PTC and the Canadian Perfect era is almost upon us! (Canadian Perfect leans forward so he is right up close to the camera) (Canadian Perfect) And Sally as for your little question about your words being a trick or you wanting a relationship.... Well for your sake, it better be a trick. Why? Because you will end up with a whole lot of egg on your face if you want a relationship. BECAUSE JUST LIKE MY OPPONENTS AT NEW ERA, WITH ME YOU HAVE NO CHANCE! I don't have a normal life anymore. I can't walk down the street now without being mobbed by either the paparazzi or mobs of my adoring fans. I don't think you people realize how hard it is to be me. It is quite hard to be this good-looking, this talented and this perfect. You all saw it as I beat The Stechinnos into a state of unconsciousness. It felt real good squeezing the life out of them. I could feel all the oxygen squeezing out of their body, and then they went limp. But then people thought I should have just left it there. "You had made your point Canadian Perfect," they all say! But had I? If you remember back earlier in the week I clearly stated that I was not going to treat this as a match, I was treating it as a message... A message to President Meyhu. And the bell meant nothing, cause I was planning to finish the job. I told them that they were nothing but a message and I told them not to get into the ring with me, but they defied me, and look what happened to someone who defied my advice! I could see the rage emulating from their body and I loved it. Why? Because I know I have gotten to them. Everyone talks about how I am nothing but a Canadian Freak, but it looks like Canadian Perfect has gotten inside everyone's head and I will beat them at their own game. That no matter what you throw at me, you won't knock me down. I am just going to keep coming and coming and when I get my hands on everyone in the Extreme Clusterfuck, you are history. You should count yourselves lucky that security will probably drag me away, because I will have my turn to have my shots at all of you. You will probably have your shots but then I’m going to get my revenge. Consider yourselves lucky you could have the chance to see "the day after New Era" because if there was no security, I would have ended your miserable life’s right there and then. But I will just have to wait. The time will come where all of you will go one on one with Canadian Perfect, and rest assured you will feel the wrath of the beast they call Canadian Perfect! Their job is to take care of themselves as I take care of myself. If they play by the rules then there won't be any problems. But if they have a problem with this rule, then there will be a lot of problems... FOR THEM! I don't see them as partners in a match; I just see them as keeping the other goons from stopping me from winning. I think that is pretty clear and easy enough for even them to achieve. This match will be another stepping stone to the top for me because I am the greatest technical wrestler in history, I am Wrestling and above all else I am absolutely Perfect!
