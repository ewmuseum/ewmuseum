A Prophesy

14 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cold dank air of the abandoned dojo creeps into the nose of Mike Marvelous, causing a look of disgust to come over his young face. He begins to wonder why he even bothered to return to the old dojo, there’s nothing left of the interior except for rotting walls and some broken exercise equipment. He carefully steps around the damp floor, not wanting to ruin his Italian made shoes or his navy blue designer suit with the water dripping down from the ceiling of the dark building. The now abandoned dojo is the place where Mike was trained by a mysterious Asian man known only as Mao before his stint as an underground pit fighter. Mike had been Mao’s top student for the year that he had spent under his tutelage, that distinction added to Mike’s already growing ego so much that one day Mao simply refused to continue the lessons. “An ego is the greatest weakness that one can possess, one day yours shall be your downfall and you will be too complacent to even notice it.” Mao had said before banishing Mike from the dojo. Mike wasn’t upset at all with being cast out, he got all his money back and he knew the true reason for Mao refusing to continue teaching him...the old man was jealous! That thought brings a smirk to Mike’s face as he comes to a stop in the center of the dimly lit main room of the dojo. He looks about the room while shaking his head as the memories of the dojo come flooding back to him. “I’m a God!” Mike whispers to himself with more than just a hint of arrogance in his voice. He begins to look about the dojo like a king surveying his kingdom when his dark blue eyes happen upon the door that leads to the room that was once Mao’s office. He would have just passed over it without giving it another glance if it wasn’t for the light flickering out from underneath the door. Mike approaches the door, practically strutting like a peacock. He pauses at the dirt-covered door and reaches into the inside pocket of his jacket to pull out a white handkerchief made of pure silk, he uses it to open the door. He steps inside the room and what he sees surprises him, an elderly Asian man that is sitting cross legged upon the bare wooden floor with his eyes closed as if he’s in deep meditation. The man has a “Fu-Manchu” style goatee that is gray in color and he wears a blue robe of fine silk that has Japanese characters etched upon it’s surface in many different colors, he also wears a skull cap perched upon his head that matches the robe in color. Why, it is Mao himself! “I’ve been expecting you Michael Biddle.” The old man says using Mike’s given name without opening his eyes. “Don’t give me that mystic crap you old fool.” Mike says as he steps before Mao with his hands in his pockets and a self satisfied smirk glued to his face. To him the old man is just like everyone else in this world...a weak charlatan that’ll never achieve the level greatness that is possessed only by Mike. The old man smiles internally at Mike’s response as it is clear to him that the younger man has not changed in the least bit since the last time their paths crossed. “Tell me Michael Biddle, has your visit to this old dojo done what you hoped it would do?“ Mao asks with a bit of curiosity present in his voice. “If you mean it helped to remind me why I’m superior to you and everyone else, then yes.” Mike replies with all the cockiness in the world. A smile slowly forms upon the face of the old Asian man. “I see that you’re still arrogant. Tell me Michael Biddle, how do you expect to defeat someone such as Fathom when you can’t even overcome your own weaknesses?” Mike looks down at the old man and begins to chuckle over the other mans stupid comments. “I’m not weak you pathetic old bastard and you will see that when I make Fathom cry out for his Mommy to come save him from me in the Lion’s Den.” Mike answers, still chuckling to himself. “You have already lost Michael Biddle, soon your world will begin to crumble all around you and you won’t be able to do anything but watch.” Mao prophesies to Mike with his eyes still closed. The arrogant smirk on Mike’s face quickly fades to an expression of anger and it almost seems as if a fire begins to burn within his dark blue eyes. Mike kicks over a few of the candles that are in front of him and then spits upon Mao’s face. Mao does not move or say anything; he just sits motionless with his eyes still closed. “How dare you say that to me you old queer! No one can beat me...no one!” Mike says through clenched teeth, obviously seething with anger. Mike spits in Mao’s face yet again, hoping to provoke the old man so that Mike can have the pleasure of slowly choking the life out of him. After a few moments he realizes that Mao is just going to sit there like a coward and do nothing. He then decides to leave before he ends up getting his hands dirty by committing a murder. Marvelous turns to Mao just before he walks out the door, “I’ll prove you wrong turd snap...I’ll show you why I’m called Mr. Main Event!” Mike yells at the top of his lungs before slamming the door shut loudly behind him. “As the prophecy has been given, it will happen.” Mao says quietly to himself. -To Be Continued?-
