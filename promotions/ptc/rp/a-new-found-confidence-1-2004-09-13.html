A New Found Confidence (1)

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 What is an insult? Could I refer to someone as insulted after calling them a name? After making a comment about their color? Or possibly putting down their intelligence, or skill at a certain activity… A certain activity such as…wrestling? I mean, I’m certainly somewhat new around these parts…so you, as the person on the receiving end of this message; riddle me this. If I were to say to you “I scorn you, scurvy companion. What, you poor, base, rascally, cheating, lack-linen mate! Away, you mouldy rogue, away!” (Shakespeare, Henry IV Part 2, 2. 4) would you be offended? Or would you simply take me as an over educated, witty, nonsense-speaking fool who simply cares about belittling his opposition through mere intelligence alone? Thought so. However, I, Christopher Shackleton would not do such a thing to any of my peers. Since the day that I was brought to America, to follow my dream, I have appreciated and honored everyone who has assisted me along the way, from the manufacturer of the sleek gold pen I used to sign my first contract, to each and every opponent I have faced in the ring…there is a certain respect between myself and all of them. We are all competitors here, whether we like to put ourselves on a level playing field like that or not. Think about it, I have not trained significantly more, wanted much more or dreamed of anything else other than the man who stands across from me seconds before the bell tolls. No, this is not the case. Who am I to say that I am better than someone else, if there is one thing in this world that is base, and that I hold contempt and scorn for it is arrogance. Do not tell me you are better than me, do not even tell me that I am better than you. Show me. One of the few phrases I have learned while in America is one of the most important I will ever hear, “actions speak louder than words”. I hold this sentence true to ever letter that makes it up. Show me. There isn’t much that I hate, they say I have a cold heart and cold blood. This is simply from the cold weather, and has nothing to do with how I act as a person. Would I call myself a people person? To some extent, however sometimes people take my kindness for weakness, and that is when I take advantage of them in the ring. Scorn, hatred, contempt for just one thing, one idea…scorn, hatred and contempt for someone else, belittling them to nothing. Why must one whittle away his opponent with words, when he may do it with his hands. Why? ‘Can’t the world see that war will never solve any conflict’, Said I, peering out over the skyline that was just outside my window. It had been a long night, and I was restless in my awakening. Who wouldn’t be, on the eve of one of the biggest moments of my life. ‘Don’t get started on this again, Christopher. Where along the line did anyone give YOU the right to interfere in American politics.’ Responded the other man in the room, my training partner and close friend who has recently arrived from my nation of origin, Antarctica. ‘Sagat…’ (his name) ‘What could we compare this situation to other than a simple modern day paradox. Think for a moment without speaking. There is conflict in the world, correct?’ ‘Correct’, he replied. I snapped back, ‘I said not to speak.’ ‘Anyways, there is conflict in the world. But conflict over what? Money? Power? Oil? All of the things that make sense when you are inside the head of a great political leader, but to someone like you or I…the greed involved in every transaction is ridiculous, agreed?” ‘Agreed.’ ‘Please…’ I said under my breath, ‘Just hear me out without interruption.’ ‘Anyways, yes, well we have out of the way that there is conflict. Now what ways do we use back home to solve conflict. Certainly not…conflict. Right?’ ‘Right’, said he. Almost out of spite he smiled at my distaste for his decision to interrupt me once again. ‘SAGAT!’ Beads of sweat poured down my face, usually I was not this short tempered…however this morning had already taken its toll on me. Was it stress? Or was it frustration. Or was it something down inside me, some uncovered contempt. Some unsolved mystery of pent up anger, of caged scorn. ‘Please, please, please. I want to speak now.’ ‘ Well anyways, think of it in terms of science. A magnet, for instance, positive attracts to negative, and negative to positive and so on. However, when one places the negative poles together and visa versa, what happens?’ I looked at Sagat almost expecting a response this time, and just to frustrate him further I waved my hand as a motion for his response. Now his face turned bright red and through his teeth he nearly whispered, ‘They repel.’ ‘Correct my friend! Wow, you certainly have been brushing up in preparation for your big trip.’ My sarcasm was limited these days, after all, there had been so much business to attend to since my injury. It was roughly two years ago, and whilst working for the then-thriving RUSH wrestling, I was involved in a match with Chris Matthews… Matthews was a favorite to win the match all along, after all, he had bust onto the RUSH scene without looking back. The Canadian quickly skyrocketed to number one on the top wrestlers list and was looking to take more and more names in his journey to the RUSH title. I, on the other hand, was closely following him…and I had the support of President Jeremy Howard, and my stable mates (El Spiffy! And G.O.D) backing me up. There was more to the mix, Howard had promised a legit shot at the title to the victor, and I was driven for success. Of course, this was early on in my career and I knew very little about the American, or then again Canadian, style of the sport. My efforts always seemed to barely propel me into victory, which was why during this match there was no excuse, no reason to lose. None. Of course Matthews was slotted to win. Why shouldn’t he? There was so much heat surrounding his situation that you could fry an egg on it, but I knew in my heart that there was a future for me in this world…and it wasn’t going to be crushed now. My finishing maneuver is called the “Chilling Wind”, a submission hold that I invented where the opponents legs are thrust powerfully towards the area of the mat above his head, and I tweak his arm around upside down. Unbearable pain shoots through you from three sections of the body…and I had Matthews locked in. He was the first ever not to tap out. Afterwards, I was exhausted; as was he…he stood up and looked me in the eye. He said something to me with a look of disgust on his face, staring at my leg, but I could only hear and see a blurry mess. That was when it clicked in my head…my knee was at almost a 90 degree angle, and my leg had snapped due to the intense pressure I applied the move with. At the sight, I collapsed…and I’m not very sure what happened afterwards. But there were months, and years of recovery. From such an injury, many people do not return to the sport…but I was destined; I was prepared. I feel like my downfall came from the first taste I ever had of contempt, hatred or scorn. The emotion that went through me on the thought of Chris Matthews was unmatched in ultimate passion for demolishing him. For the first time, I saw someone who was ranked above me…who I treated below me. There was no pent up anger inside me, as far as I was concerned…but the thought of this one man could turn me, a respectable young fellow, into a burning ball of hatred, of scorn. Now that I look back, I should’ve felt this for all of my opponents. And I should now feel it for only one man, him being Jake Salos, but for some reason…that spot that should be a cloud floating over my heart is empty. What is this feeling? Why have I no experience with my fluctuating emotions. Why?… Walking through the city on a most unfortunate reunion, I am reminded of what I have experienced through my short lived American past. I arrived here in the year 2000, just before the fateful events of September 11th 2001…and today is the 3 year anniversary…I was in the city that day, I sat in the training offices of Primetime Wrestling Academy and awaited physical clearance for the first match of my career when the terror struck. I had never seen something like this before, such outright hatred and scorn for one group of people against another. The terrorists that attacked mainland America sacrificed their lives for something higher than the concept of living and dying….they sacrificed themselves for a purpose. Right or wrong, this put things in perspective for me. Hatred is stronger than it seems, meaning that one person can murder 3,000+ innocent ones with one decision…just because of their hatred towards one concept. The cracks in the sidewalk show me that nothing in this world in concrete, nothing is completely smooth, and their will always be a crevasse every few feet. My crevasse is Jake Salos, a thorn in my side…a crack in my sidewalk, a bridge that must be crossed. Back in the hotel room I flipped on the news, and low and behold it was the one man who I could honestly say I hated in the media. The words that I would like to express toward him would be in explainable and inappropriate…His name was Ricky Slickwater, a prominent columnist and talk-show host in the wrestling circuit, and he hated many things: myself included. He hated the way that I played “the game”, and hated my baby face image, the way I wrestled and everything that I stood for. Not to my surprise at all, this week’s episode was on “Top 5 Reasons to HATE a Babyface”. I listened in: “Hello honored viewers!”, Ricky said with a smirk on his face. “I am Ricky Slickwater; welcome to my world. By touching that dial and flipping to this channel, having your eyes become submissive to the creative rants and raves of Ricky Slickwater, you are under my control. I’ve got it all ladies and gentlemen, and no matter how much you hate me, I have you right where I want you. This is a weekly torture rack of all of those great people, places and things you know and love. This week’s topic: baby face wrestlers today. Who likes them? Everyone. Who hates them? Ricky Slickwater. Take a look around your wrestling world, the posters on your walls. Are there any smiling faces? Are there any people giving you the thumbs up? There needs to be a stop to these fake, somewhat uplifting wrestlers. This is why today we will learn the top 5 reasons why to HATE a baby face. Many have been successful just look at LLB, Christopher Fox, Brand Frontier and others…have been unsuccessful, Christopher Shackleton included, these baby faces are tarnishing the look of wrestling as a whole. This needs to come to a complete stop.” He turns and sips from the coffee to his left. “We go to reason number one to HATE a babyface. 1. The baby face’s ability to be…pretty…in an ugly sport.” Ricky wiped his mouth with the back of his sleeve, and left a small bit of coffee stain on his white shirt. “Look at some from the past, Mikey Marvelous of FSW and OSW…he had his teeth shining, his hair perfect his sunglasses shimmering. His opponents were huge, slightly clothed, sweaty and brutal. Wrestling is a man’s sport. Contact is involved. How are you to worry about your hair, your face when it is being beaten slowly and hard into a non-plush surface? My man Shackleton, he got off to a bad start. How could you ever be a fan of someone with that pale of a complexion? Come on now fans, lets get realistic here…Shackleton is pure gimmick. Oh and by the way, he’s facing Jake Salos this week in the PTC’s GTT…looks like he’s about to get a reality check. Baby faces have the undying ability to do this, to draw your attention. Or, in some unfortunate instances the attention of his opponent.” Ricky paused again, as I thought of how he belittled me, his hatred and contempt for me were unmatched by anyone I had ever encountered; Ricky and myself had barely ever met. “And now onto number two” He continued, “The one thing that irritates me maybe more than anything else in modern wrestling. The babyface beats out the heel. A monster heel brings heat and recognition to any sizeable federation. A babyface draws no attention, corporate decisions are made to replace the top man in a federation from heel to babyface. Wrong? Yes. Hateable? Most certainly. If a heel who works hard, wrestles hard, talks smack and wins…gets overbooked by a babyface, this should dictate you hating the babyface. Who likes a babyface over a heel? Merely the babyface himself.” My digust for him, was certainly unmatched. Ricky looked at the sheet on his desk and continued on. “Now number three”, he said with a grin on his face. “Is my personal favorite. I hate them. Yes, the number 3 reason you should hate a babyface is in complete following of me. I hate them, loathe them, wish to kill them all. This, in fact, does not support your chances of living if you choose to support their evil and corrupt efforts. Call me a hypocrite, a liar, an un-appreciative bastard…but I hate them. Therefore, you should.” He laughed and touched his ear as if he was receiving something from an earpiece. “Well, I’ve just received word to move on. Fans, we have a special guest with us today by the name of Fred Green, he’s a pro wrestling analyst who specializes in the area of prediction of big matches. Come on out Fred!” A small, skinny man with greasy black hair and a thick pair of glasses walked out wearing the usual attire of someone described to be an analyst, he had on a blue and white striped collard shirt tucked into his khakis which were a bit too short for him. Fred sat down with Ricky, and began to predict matches for the upcoming GTT. “I think we’ll see a lot of surprises”, said he to Ricky. “However, nothing really out of the norm. Look closely at Inphino Blitz’s match, there could be an upset from Morocco there. Also, I’d say Tessa Windsor, Doctor Curiosity, Seymour Almasy and Jake Salos move on quite easily through the first round.” Ricky looked at him with a smirk, and when I saw it I knew what he was about to bring up. “You mention Salos as a shoe in for victory, why?” Asked Ricky, almost knowing somehow that I was watching from a far off hotel room. “Well, his opponent Shackleton is most likely washed up from his injury years ago. Of course Shack was a pretty good RUSH superstar, at one point he was the Wrestling-Tattler #2 wrestler of the month, and he also was rumoured to appear in OSW during its hay day…however that injury really put him out. With a knee the way his looked, I didn’t think he’d every move again, let alone wrestle.” Ricky stared him down. “Interesting,” He questioned. “You say that he will lose because of the injury he suffered, but nothing to do with his skill as a wrestler. Think about his strategies, his persona…how do you expect him to win with that?” Frank looked flustered. “You know I can’t comment on that Ricky…he’s just a bit low down on…” CLICK I’d had enough of it. This whole tournament, this match, this return everything was too much for me. The last thing I needed was for someone to have contempt and scorn for me. Now what I really needed to do was to have the same emotion for my opponent. P.R.E.P.A.R.E Y.O.U.R.S.E.L.F S.A.L.O.S Rub your rabbit’s foot. Take your clover with you, because with the drive and determination that I’ve found since my injury it might be hard to stop me, especially in the first round. I’m not about to lose this thing. Not at all. I know that confidence like this can’t be healthy, but maybe I should listen to Ricky. Maybe modesty isn’t the best policy. Maybe it’s time to get nasty. SCORN
