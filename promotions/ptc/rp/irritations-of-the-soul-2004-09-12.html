"Irritations of the Soul"

12 September 2004

Date: 2004-09-12 *** It’s extremely simple. I don’t know who I am. Understood? *** Ir’ri*ta”tion, n. [L. irritation: cf. F. irritation.]: The act of irritating, or exciting, or the state of being irritated; excitement; stimulated, usually of an undue and uncomfortable kind; especially, excitement of anger or passion; provocation; annoyance; anger. (Taken from www.dictionary.com) I sit in a rather comfortable armchair in my extremely generic hotel room, feeling distinctly like a prisoner with access to special privileges. Alone. Perpetually alone, with the only thing that I have from my past, firmly fixed at the front of my mind. Wrestling: an art of combat, which can be dated back to the time of the ancient Greeks. Looking outside, I can see the breathtakingly beautiful glow of the moon pounding down upon the cheap outdoor swimming pool. It is nighttime and therefore the torment will soon begin anew. I check my watch. 23:10pm. It is nearly time for another bout of simulated sex noise from the sleazy moron over in the next room. Just another irritation to plague this tortured soul, or at least that’s what I’m supposed to say…right? Just another irritation… Funny thing about irritations…in my mind there are two kinds of them; to types of mental torture for a person must endure. The first is the easier kind; it is the irritation that you can do something about, the kind of irritation that you can at least wrestle some kind of control over. For example, the kind of irritation you get in the movies when your trying hard to watch a movie and you find yourself next to some moron who must question everything they see on screen, with a second moron who indulges the first with more than obvious explanations. However, at least with this kind of irritation you can do something about it. You have the option of nudging the morons and telling them to shut the fuck up. Or, you can explore the extremities of your options and hit the morons up side the head with a really heavy plank of wood. However, with this second option, there could be certain repercussions, which are sometimes best avoided. The second kind of irritation is a little more difficult to deal with; you have no control over it and the only way to combat it is to try and cope with it. Since waking up in a cornfield a couple of months back, I’ve been trying desperately to cope with such an irritation: my memory. Not being able to remember your past definitely qualifies as this type of irritation. There is nothing that I can do to alleviate my pain: I can only endure it. The most irritating thing about having amnesia is how I can remember certain things about myself, but when I try to expand on such thoughts, it’s like I hit a brick wall that I can’t get around. It then gracefully degrades from irritation to frustration, something that, eventually, I will not be able to overcome. Everything always comes down to the numbers. Everything. I researched the amnesiac condition and calculated the odds of my memory returning. It did not look good. If I was going to even get partial aspects of my memory back, I needed to constantly work at it. Even then, there were no good odds to bet on. I heard a quote on the Television the other day by George Orwell that triggered thoughts about my past: “He who controls the past commands the future. He who commands the future conquers the past.” (Taken from Quoteland.com). Were my memories close enough to the surface for me to remain the same person that I had always been, or were my memories buried so deep that since I’d become a completely different person? Without a past, could I ever hope for a future? If this was true could I ever hope to build myself a life for the future? The answer is a simple one: hell yes. I have come to learn that there is one person, just one, who I can trust, who I can depend upon: Me. I have no memories. I have no friends. I have no family. I only have me and, frankly, that is enough. Some say that looking after “number one” is no way to live and I agree with them, but I’m not “living” by anyone’s standards, I am only surviving and to survive I need to look out for myself more than anything else. I will create my past so that I can command my future…I don’t need anybody else to help me with that. The only thing I do know is: I love wrestling and I know how to wrestle. To what extent, is unknown to me. I have no idea how good, or bad, I am on a competitive level. Essentially, I am an untested entity within the tournament, pitted against a very talented veteran, Cyrus Skar. However, will being an untested entity work for, or against me? I had no experience to speak of, no previous matches for my opponent to base our future confrontation on, he didn’t know what he was getting himself into, whereas I can watch some LOW tapes and figure the guy out. Make no mistake about it; I have the advantage in this one. Before making any decision, I always ask myself: What will I gain by doing this? Granted, there will always be the possibility that I bomb out in the first round, but I am against a very talent opponent there will be no shame in that. By entering the tournament I will be able to gauge how good of a worker I am, I will be learning more about myself, it might even jar loose more information from my past. Essentially, entering the tournament had the potentiality to benefit me more than it would cost me. The choice is a simple one. The noise of a woman screaming and moaning in ecstasy violently shook me out of my reverie. I checked my watch. 23.15pm. Simulated sex time. This is the third night in a row and although it isn’t the same kind of irritation as my amnesia, it is extremely frustrating nonetheless. However, the thought had occurred to me that this is the first kind of irritation; meaning I could assert some control of the situation and alleviate, to a degree, my irritation. I had to ask myself another question: How will this action benefit me? If I am successful I will get rid of the irritation and get some rest. However, there are too many things to take into account, too many invariables that could spring up at any time. The odds could not be calculated for this, but I decided to go through with it anyway. It was getting almost intolerable. I stand from armchair, make my way past my bed and left my hotel room, finding the noise of the simulated sex boom through the offending door almost a full decibel louder than it sounded in my room. I had to admit to myself that I’m slightly amazed by the fact that nobody else had complained about the noise. However, this hotel isn’t exactly five-star calibre. It’s the kind of place where mayoral candidates took their most ‘loyal’ supporters away from prying eyes...most notably their wives. Usually, there were no questions asked and no complaints made, but today was a different day and a different kind of person. I knock on the offending door, hard and loudly making sure that the morons inside can hear me. I hear the sound clattering within the room, and heavy footsteps pounding upon the hollow floor, edging closer to the door. Finally, the door opens to reveal a small, dumpy, balding man, with a stupid looking pair of glasses hanging from his overly round face. The man is just wearing a pair of ordinary brown slacks, the sight of the pervert made me want to retched all over him and I suspect that if I did so, it would only improve his look. “What the hell do you want?” The grubby little imp said, absently scratching his crotched. “Turn the porno down.” I demanded, with a certain fiery edge that I’d managed to master over the past couple of months. The imp chuckled to himself, undeterred by the threat. I will make him pay for the insolence later. “Why the hell should I?” “Because,” I said, leaning on the door, my muscles flexing as I applied pressure to them. “I can think of at least fifteen different ways I could take you down right now…two of them involve permanent brain damage. Though I can see that somebody’s already beat me to it!” “Oh, is that so?” He turns his back on me and shouts to somebody else in his room. “Rick, we’ve got a real prick with a problem out here!” On the outside I remain calm. I don’t want to show my hand too early. However, on the inside I am furious. I want to rip the little worm’s windpipe out of his damn throat, but fortunately I am good at keeping my composure…apparently. Perhaps it is a skill I carried over from before the amnesia, or perhaps I have developed the skill since? Either way, I’m glad to have it. I watch on as the little imp backs away from the door, to allow a massive guy the size of Gibraltar to come to the door. The man isn’t much taller than me, but his width is…considerable. This is one of those variables that make the odds difficult to calculate… “What the fuck do you want?” He asks, attempting to size me up. “What the fuck you doin’ in there, giving him a hand job? Turn the fucking porno down…now!” I reply. “What the fuck are you gonna do if we…” I’ve had enough. I don’t know who this prick thinks he is talking to, but I’m not standing for it any more. I snap. With a quickness that I don’t know I possess, I reach up with my right hand and slam the big prick’s head into the wall, causing a rather large thud. Plaster fell to the ground, as did the sizeable moron, with blood pouring down from the side of his head. The grubby little imp stands there in bemusement, clearly not knowing what to do. I glare down at him for a good ten seconds, letting what had just happened sink in for the full effect. “Turn the porno down…” The little imp nods nervously, hoping that I’d already forgotten what he said to me earlier. “Okay…” The simulated sex noise will stop now. I controlled the situation and overcame the irritation…the question is: can I also use the same tactic to overcome my amnesia…or even to win the tournament? Again, there are too many variables to calculate...but the odds look good.
