The Chronicles of Muppet VIII - The Never Ending Screwup

23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-23 "Um... is this story going anywhere?" The greasy haired kid, probably around 18 or 19, was a little hesitant to interrupt King Muppet from telling this... ahem... vastly interesting story, but he did it anyway. From working with him for only a few days, he was sure... King could go on for hours. King was a little taken aback, but quickly regained his composure. "Yes, of course it is!" "..." "Ok, maybe not, but you can't blame me for trying to lighten the mood around here, can you?" The kid shrugs, lowering his head and getting on with his work. He didn't have a smile on his face, not even the mind bogglingly stupid, yet strangely entertaining, stories from King Muppet could cheer him up. Hey, working part time in a fast food 'restaurant' isn't a very stimulating job, y'know. King turned with a sigh, not able to avoid a slight eye roll. He glanced down at the McDonalds uniform that he was forced to wear, the sheer embarrassment weighing him down a good deal. Still, he managed to survive a few days, mainly because he was ducking and diving as much as he could, trying to find a way out of everything even resembling work. "No, but I can." The manager's stereotypically deep and serious voice snapped him back to attention. King looked up to see the man walking towards, tutting, as if trying to flaunt his obvious superiority and position of power, possibly in an attempt to get to King. Although King couldn't work out why he would want to do that, it was sure working. He slowly clenched and unclenched his fist, as the manager came to a halt in front of him. "I'm sorry, but I'm going to have to fire you," he said. The sincerity in his voice would have been almost believable, had there not been a big fat grin plastered over his face. He's just jealous of my natural charisma, King told himself. He was half right. "It's not that you're... incapable. I'll level with you. You're a perfectly capable worker, why you would even consider working here is beyond me. The qualifications on your CV say more than enough. It's just your attitude that's the problem. So far, I haven't seen you serve one customer, or do one thing even resembling work." He was quite observant, it seemed. "In fact, all I've seen you do so far is distract my other workers. I'm sorry, this just isn't going to work," he continued, the smile fading. They shook hands, both nearly crushing each other's hand, before the manager turned to leave. "Bad luck," he added, winking in a complete act of disrespect, before turning and walking off. That was the final straw. Had the kid not have held him back, King would have launched at his former manager and 'beaten some respect into him' the old fashioned way. But it was not to be. "Well," King began, turning to face the kid, attempting to start some sort of goodbye, "it's been..." Fun? Interesting? Worthwhile? "...An experience. Good luck with everything, and... well, I'll see ya round." "Later, Muppet dude," the kid said. They shook hands, in a much more friendly manner, and King ripped his uniform off, throwing it to the ground and spitting on it, before storming out. It wasn't the big exit he had been hoping for, but at least he was out, and that was all that mattered. If only that were true. What was King Muppet even doing working in a McDonalds, you ask? Quite simply, he was being gnawed away by guilt, from the inside out, as each day drifted by. He had to get some quick cash, but since he had no particular musical or artistic talent, that was easier said than done. King needed the money desperately, to pay off the ever increasing debt that he and the Muppet Man were in. If they didn't pay off the money within a week, they would lose their house, Muppet Manor. Whatever King did, however much he managed to earn within that week, if he wanted to keep it legal, there was no possible way he could get all the money. Their only hope was for the Muppet Man to advance through to the bracket final of GTT4. That would give the required amount in sponsorship alone, no doubt, but to do it, he had to get through Doctor Curiosity, the biggest test of Muppet's career. King was feeling increasingly guilty at placing such a large burden on Muppet's shoulders, but there was no turning back now. He was going to see this through to the bitter end. He just didn't know if he could say the same for the Muppet Man. It was with a heavy heart that King Muppet returned to Muppet Manor on Friday night. He had been doing all he could, all week long, to get as much money as he could, but it was all so futile in the scheme of things. The worst part was, he hadn't heard a word out of the Muppet Man since he had broken the news to him about having to win against Dr. Curiosity. King couldn't be sure whether Muppet was psyching himself up for the biggest fight of his life, or sinking into another pit of despair and self-hate, and that was what worried him. As King walked up that oh-so-familiar driveway, he looked up at his beloved house. He had only been there for a little over two months, yet already, he had fallen in love with it. It was perfect. He wasn't going to lose it, not now, or ever. He was going to get the Muppet Man ready for this match, if it killed him. There, staring up at that house, the first smile in a long time appeared on the lips of King Muppet. As you've most likely come to expect, it wouldn't last too long. BANG! What is it with Muppets and huge explosions? "Holy shit, WHAT THE FUCK?" King screamed, aghast, charging towards the house at top speed. Needless to say, there had been a massive explosion, somewhere near the back of the house. With absolutely no clue what the hell was happening, King burst in through the front door, almost smashing it in, and sprinted through the maze of doors he now knew like the back of his hands. He saw smoke coming from the end, and heard an all too familiar voice shouting. "Fire, fire! All hands on deck! Let's go, people!" Skidding around the corner, King had no time to observe the bizarre scene as he was forced with a wall of fire. On the other side, yet still clearly visible, was the Muppet Man. He was dressed oddly, though. Yes, I realise he's always dressed oddly, but this was different. He was wearing a long, pure white labcoat, and glasses. How he could see properly with glasses, King didn't quite know, but this was no time to be wondering about such things. "Good to see you back," Muppet screamed over the flames. "This is hardly the time, Andy," King shouted back, looking around desperately for something to put out the flames with. "We need some sort of liquid to put out the flames! Ah, this should do," Muppet commented, picking up a bottle and throwing the contents over the flames. Even from the other side, King could still read the label. It wasn't the best choice, really. "No, not that, that's..." BOOM! Having dived to the ground and covered his head, King rolled back to his feet. The whole entrance to the room had been completely decimated. Well, at least the flames were out. "...Explosive," he finished, a little angry. The Muppet Man was still in one piece, although a little shaken, the whole front side of him blackened from the second explosion. Still, King rushed forwards to check on him, if only to make sure he wasn't injured. It was imperative that he was in top form for the match. "Aha," Muppet started, as if nothing had happened, moving to embrace his partner in a strangely eastern european manner. "Velcome back, my dear friend. As you can see, I've just been concocting a few experiments of my own..." King Muppet looked around. Clearly, Muppet had been 'concocting a few experiments of his own'. For what purpose, King couldn't even begin to imagine. It appeared, in the past few days, that he converted this room into his own personal laboratory. Where he had got the equipment from, King had no idea, especially as they were practically broke. That wasn't quite what worried him, though. The Muppet Man hadn't the first idea about science, engineering or mechanics. Hell, this guy couldn't even handle flashing lights. Goodman, eat your heart out. There were a load of gadgets and gizmos scattered around on the tables. King couldn't even begin to imagine their uses. He settled on the thought that he didn't really want to know. Still, he was probably going to have to find out, sooner or later... "Imper-essive, huh?" asked Muppet, beaming. "I have no idea what you've done, but... hey! Why the hell are you talking in an accent?" "Vhat are you talking about?" Muppet said, seemingly confused. It was a mask... hidden behind... another mask... oh forget it. "You're talking in some German accent, or something. Y'know, German, Russian, it's hard to tell, they're practically the sa... OH! I see what you're doing!" King said, his tone inflated a little, with a note of triumph. "I'm afraid I have no idea vhat you are talking about, and you are distracting me from my studies," Muppet said dismissively, trying to brush King away. It wouldn't work, not that easily. "You're pretending to be Doctor Curiosity, aren't you? You're doing some sort of mental 'roleplay' to get in the mood for the match. I'll bet you're trying to get in his mindset, so you know what to expect. I applaud you for trying, but..." "Curiosity? Who is zis man of which you speak? I know of no 'Doctor' named Curiosity." "Andy, this is ridiculous," King said, a bite of impatience in his voice. He was getting a little irritated. "I don't know vhat has got into you today, Aegor, but I guess I should show you some of the beauties I've been working on for the past few days," Muppet said, moving on to one of the tables. "Aegor?" King said, mostly to himself, confused. "Oh, right. It's Eegor, you know." "Huh?" "His assisstant. His name is Eegor, not Aegor." "Vhat?" "EEGOR!" "Stop your unintelligable babbling, Aegor, ve have work to do! Some people..." Muppet said, again turning to muttering to himself under his breath. King slapped his hand onto his forehead. This was going to be harder than he thought. If Muppet could give Curiosity this much trouble, he'd take the win for sure. "I know my opponent iz an accomplished inventor, and will have tons of tricks up hiz sleevzes, so I knew I had to counter it vith some of my own!" "Hang on, hang on, hold up a minute..." King said, now totally and utterly confused. "Just a minute ago, you said you didn't know a Doctor Curiosity, and now you know of his 'work'?" The Muppet Man looked at him, looking for a minute to be about to come clean. There was a pause, before Muppet simply cocked his eyebrow. "I think it vould be better for all of us if you just shut up, today, Aegor. You're going to give me plutosis if you continue on like zis." "Plutosis... THAT'S NOT EVEN A DISEASE! IT'S NOT EVEN A WORD, YOU JUST MADE THAT UP!" Again, Muppet simply looks at him, seemingly nonplussed. "Tut tut, poor Aegor, poor poor Aegor. You must have been watching too many Shaman promos (*Shaman has a reputation for nonsensical sayings and insults in the WWA. Hey, it's the character! XP) for your own good. Still, nothing zhat can't be cured by a blast from my new..." He pauses, possibly for dramatic effect. He even does a stupid pose. Trust me, you don't even want me to describe it to you. It'd be too painful. Finally, he spins around, and picks up what looks like an oversized toy ray gun off the table. "...DISCOMBOBULATOR 5000 36XX52 MARK IV... um... TURBO!" ...And there was a long silence. "Discom...bobulator? That don't sound good," King commented, not quite sure what else to say. Plus, he was a little intimidated, what with Muppet pointing it right at him. "Oh, don't worry, I couldn't think of a name, so I just picked ze first tzing that came out of my head and added a bunch of numbers and X's on ze end." As you do... "What... exactly are you going to blast me with?" King asked, more intimidated with every tense, yet mind numbingly stupid, second that passed. "Nuzzing much, just a mixture of substances vhich vill free you of, among other tzings, ze horrible curse of Shaman-itis." "This is getting more ridiculous by the minute. And what are those 'other tzings'?" "Oh, just your... *incoherentmumbling*..." "What? You didn't answer me, you just trailed off," King pointed out. Muppet looked a little flustered. "Yes, yes, I kinda did..." "Well? Answer me. What are those 'other tzings'?" "...Just your hair, a few layers of skin, and your..." he winced. "Your..." "DON'T even say it!" King shouted, knowing exactly what was being implied. He quickly darted forwards, kicking the 'discombobulator' out of Muppet's hands. In a split second, it flew across the room, landed with a slight crack, and fired directly into the wall, before anyone could do anything about it. Well, it would have fired directly into the wall, had there not been a large filing cabinet in the way. The cabinet teetered, as King and Muppet dived for cover, before falling over onto one of the tables. Annoyingly, the cabinet did not contain files, but chemicals, which proceeded to spill all over the table in question. There was a moment of peace. The calm before the storm, if you will. The Muppet Man looked up from his little curled up ball, raising his voice. "I think... I tzink it vill be ok. Lucky none of zose chemicals reacted, or we could be in some major..." He had to say it, didn't he? As if on cue, the mush of liquid from around and under the toppled cabinet began to bubble and froth, spitting violently. "...Trouble. EEP!" "Oh shit, oh shit, oh SHIT! What the FUCK is going on?" King screamed, backing against the wall. Muppet rushed over, attempting to hide behind his partner. "You're the 'expert' here, what the hell are they doing?" King screamed again, as the bubbles and froth had now reached a metre in height. "Vell, they are... vell... vell... EEP!" Muppet screamed like a little schoolgirl, ducking behind King as a particularly large spurt of the substance blasted out across the room. The volume of it was so great that it actually began to lift the cabinet off the ground. "It's... it's..." Muppet began, almost afraid to say it. "It's ALIVE! RUN FOR ZE HILLS!" "Don't be ridiculous, Andy! We've gotta stop it! Which one of your inventions can do that?!" "Um... um... ah..." he muttered incoherently, looking around at the table with all of his 'creations'. "Well... we could use... no, no, not that..." "FOR GOD'S SAKE HURRY UP MAN!" "You can't rush a genius!" Muppet shouted, this time rushing over to the table, and scrambling franticly through the gadgets. "Help me, Aegor, help me! Ze time for reason iz now over! Now it iz ze time for... PANIC!" King Muppet rushed over, grabbed any piece of metal he could find, felt around for an on switch, and hey presto. Annyoingly, the item he picked up was not designed for cleaning up chemical spillages. In fact, it was strangely... hammer-ish. A hammer, maybe? Yes, but with one special difference... "Andy... "Why... "Did... "You... "Make... "An electric... "Hammer?!" King shouted, being dragged around the room by the sheer power of the object. "I saw it on ze Simpsons and thought it vould be cool," Muppet said, not even turning around to look. "It's no use, all of zis is USELESS! Vhy, oh vhy, did I make zis pile of CRAP?" "Um... "Andy... "A little... "Help?" "Vhat?" Muppet said, looking around. "Oh, yes, of course." The Muppet Man quickly rushed over, leaping on King and wrestled the hammer out of his hand to throw it clean out the window. What would happen to that hammer? No one knows. "Vhy didn't you just let go of ze hammer?" "It... never crossed my mind," King said, perplexed. "And you call me stupid..." "Hello? CHEMICAL SPILLAGE, PEOPLE!" "Oh shit, oh shit, oh shit..." Muppet said, getting to his feet. "Zhere's nuzzing left! Ve've exhausted our resources!" "WAIT! We have one last hope," King shouted, elated, finally looking up. "What's that, over there, in the corner?" Muppet squinted his eyes through the glasses to look where King was pointing frantically. "Zhat... is a rubber duck. Not quite sure how that got there." "Oh great..." For a moment, all was lost. For a moment, they lost all hope of ever getting out alive. For a moment, nothing else mattered. Until, King noticed something... "Hey... it's stopped!" Muppet looked up. Indeed, the substance had stopped frothing and growing unnaturally. There was a momentary silence, before they exploded. "HOORAY!" they shouted in tandem. "WE'RE SAVED!" ...And then it exploded. Ka-BOOM~!~! With the biggest blow of the night yet, the substance exploded with a horrendous force, blowing both the Muppets across the room and slamming them hard into the back wall. Slowly, they crawled back to their feet. "I hate to say it, but... at least that's over," King said, wiping sweat from his brow. How wrong he was. "Oh my God! What the hell happened here?" Both Muppets look up at the same time, to see a neatly dressed man running into the room, looking as shocked as they felt. In fact, they couldn't even begin to imagine what they looked like. "What? Who the hell are you, and how did you get in my house?!" "The door was wide open. Basically the whole town is outside, y'know. The police are here too. We heard an explosion..." "Oh shit," came Muppet's voice. That couldn't be good. "Hey, where did you get all these chemicals?" said the man, looking around the room. "Oh SHIT!" He looked around. Basically every inch of this room could land him another year behind bars. "Aegor, vhat are ve going to do?" ... "Aegor?" "I'm waaaaay ahead of yoooouuuu..." came the cry from far off in the distance. Muppet looked to his right, to see the broken window that had been smashed by the electric hammer, and King Muppet running up the hill on the outside. Glancing back at the man, who was still frozen with shock, Muppet dived out of the window and began to run after King. "I'll get you for zis, Aegor!" "It's Eeeeeeegoooor!" ---- I have to admit, that was one of the biggest screwups of my life. I just can't do anything right, can I? But I have to. I have to go down to Washington D.C. for the fourth time. I have to take on Doctor Curiosity. I have to BEAT Doctor Curiosity. All of the odds are against me. Nothing is going right, whereas for him, everything is taking a turn for the better. Can I use that? I have to. The game goes on, and it's time for one final, decisive roll of the dice. Except, there's one difference. These dice have nothing to do with luck. I've been a screwup all my life. This time, though... This time, when everything is on the line... This time, if it's not too much trouble... I think I'm going to win. ---- That was the last that the Muppet Man and King Muppet would see of Muppet Manor... at least for a very long time. Would they regain the house? Would Muppet triumph over Dr. Curiosity? Only time would tell. But who was really behind the case the police were dubbing 'the dumbest case in American history'? Was it really the Muppet Man, or... No, it can't be... But, it IS.. The rubber duck sits, unharmed, unnoticed, in the corner of the room, looking so innocent with it's beady little eyes and happy little smile. Dum dum DUMMM~! ...Ok, maybe not. What? I'm not Dave friggin' Hurst. Rubber ducks aren't real! That's why they're called rubber ducks... dumbass. Well, until next time, I'm outta here. The duck's eyes begin to glow, a menacing shade of demonic red... Fade out. NOW.
