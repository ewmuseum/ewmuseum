Survival #2

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-22 As the scene fades in from blue our blurred vision begins to focus in on our unknown surroundings. As we sharpen our sense of sight we see a place that is not too different from a place we have seen time and time again, although something is definitely missing. We are under water and in the ocean no doubt, we know this because we see the fish that we’ve seen before close to the shores of The Asesino Cove, but there are no coral species to speak of. A large Pink Tail Trigger passes us by and we take note of the fact that we haven’t seen this particular species of Trigger too many times before around the inlet of the Cove. Our interest in the Trigger quickly fades as we see a seven foot Black Tip Reef Shark swimming beneath us and we realize that we are in the back waters of The Asesino Cove Reef. Our first instinct is fear, but then when we see that the shark has no interest in us our thoughts begin to move towards the direction of confusion. We turn around and looks straight down to see a man in scuba gear pulling meat out of a bag and feeding dozens of hungry sharks, sharks of all sizes and shapes. The man's hand feeds the sharks, and they seem used to the presence of this provider. The man’s bag seems empty and as he throws the last piece of meat to the sharks we follow the man as he swims back towards a steep cliff. The cliff is only a few feet from where the man was and he swims to the top of the ridge to expose the unmistakable landscape of the Asesino Cove Reef. The man swims through the reef with ease leaving our sight many times, the only way that we can keep up is to take advantage of the stopping that the man is doing to feed some of the fish with food in one of his pockets. After sometime we reach the black sand beach of The Asesino Cove and follow the man to the shore. The man takes off his scuba mask to reveal a face, the face of The Assassin. Asesino removes the rest of his gear and we see that he is wearing a pair of blue board shorts and no shirt, not an unlikely thing for Asesino to be wearing on this brilliant summer afternoon. Asesino stretches a little then takes a seat on his favorite lava rock, the one that resembles a chair very much. He puts his feet up on the smaller rock that is in front of him and looks out at the ocean letting the tide creep in under the rock that his feet rest on. Asesino takes a drink of water and then looks into the camera. Asesino: Sharks seem to get a bad rap from the media, but they really are magnificent creatures and deserve to be treated with love, though also with respect as they are the most deadly predators in the sea. A lot of people compare me to the sharks. They see that damage that I can do in a wrestling ring and hear the name Asesino and the man that they think of is the Great White Shark . . . being a fish enthusiast that is a great compliment, though I don’t see myself as a Great White, no, I think of myself as more of a Blue Ring Octopus, I may not strike fear into the hearts of my opponents at first glance, though I should. My small body size is often misinterpreted as weakness, this is also a mistake made when judging the Blue Ring. The Blue Ring though small in stature possess a bite strong enough to kill a full grown man twice over, so many people are killed by this animal each year because of it’s unintimidating appearance, and it’s deadly reality. Asesino: A man that knows the sting of The Assassin all to well is my former nemesis Xavier Matthews. Xavier, we’ve met in the past and hatred between us, but on PTC Survival we team as friends and athletes. Perhaps you have something to prove to me, the man that you’ve never beaten in a fair fight before, and the man that your career has been so closely linked to over since you started. I know that you're trying to get your career back on track teaming with me, Chris Johnson and Big Bad Brad in Team NGW, and that you are a great athlete, but I also know that it’s a tragedy if your path has to cross my path to The Extreme Title, because I’m not going to let anyone stop me from winning that gold, not you or otherwise. Asesino: You know some people in PTC can live with being a name though. Face it guys, you're nothing, you're just over hyped talent-less pricks that will never beat NGW. Asesino: And I want to let the Assassin-Fanatics know this, even though Jeff James won the Untamed Title, and even though John Hitchin is the World Champion, it’s only a matter of time until that World Heavyweight Gold is around my waist. We have a match soon for the Extreme Title and when I give you your first loss then I will get my shot at your other title the NGW World Title, Hitchin you're a Champion who I don’t fear one bit. If you want we can do it title versus title because I am that confident that I will take the Extreme Title from you so I’ll wrestle title for title any day, and I’ll win too, because I am the heart of NGW and I am the best wrestler in the world today, period. Cut. Later... (As our scene fades in from blue we find a different sensation come over our body than those that overtake us when we enter The Asesino Cove. It’s night out and the cold crisp air bits at our noses. We see a busy city street with all sorts of different people walking along. We see an old hippie walk by followed by a big burley looking biker. Soon after we see two familiar faces come into view. Asesino and DVD walking along the street not looking too out of place. DVD is wearing a tie dye sweat shirt on this occasion as well as some plaid shorts despite the cold weather. Asesino is wearing a curious t-shirt that says "Asesino Unprettier" on it as well as a small denim jacket that just reaches his waist and a pair of tight black jeans that lead to his white Converse All Stars. Asesino would look just another guy if it weren’t for the unmistakable face. As the two continue to walk a group of four teenage boys stops them.) Boy 1: Excuse me but are you Asesino and DVD? (Asesino and DVD greet the boys as if they were old friends and talk about nothing in particular for sometime obviously making the boys day. Asesino signs some autographs as DVD puts his arm over one of the boys and proceeds to talk philosophy with the boy, this goes on for a little while.) DVD: And that’s why you always live your life by what you will learn and not what you have learned. (The boy just stares at DVD.) DVD: You’ll get it when you have to. Boy 3 (Not the one DVD is talking to.): Well, um, thanks for your time and good luck winning the Extreme Title, Assassin-Fanatics Unite! Asesino: Ha, Right on, nice meeting you. (The two groups part ways and the Xtreme Fusion continues on their journey, where ever it may be too. Asesino and DVD reach a shop with a sign that says, "Bill's Tattoo And Piercing" the two stop in front and look at each other.) DVD: You're sure you want to do this? Asesino: Yeah man, it’s going to be cool. DVD: Then let's Rock n Roll. (The two walk inside to the place which is small but nice, a heavily tattooed and pierced woman walks from behind a desk and greets the two with a smile.) Tattoo Girl: Hey! Asesino and DVD right? (Nods all around.) Tattoo Girl: I’m Trica and we’ll all ready so let's get going. (Trica leads Asesino and DVD past a curtain to a room with three reclining chairs, one is occupied and the other two are vacant, assumably for Asesino and DVD.) Trica: Okay guys, have a seat, Asesino, Bill is going to do your Tat since it’s a little more complicated and DVD I’ll do yours. Asesino: All right. DVD: Cool. A man walks into the room who is old and overweight with a bunch of really hellish tattoos all over his body, he is balding but has a full gray bread. He speaks in a deep raspy voice. Bill: I’m Bill, nice to see you again. Asesino: Hey, yeah, I’m finally going to do it. Bill: All right, there’s nothing to worry about. (Bill flashes Asesino a rather comforting smile.) Asesino: Oh Bill, if you don’t mind while you work I’m going to talk to the folks at home. Bill: Go right ahead. Asesino hands Bill his left arm and he starts working on his forearm. Asesino: I’m getting this tattoo in memory of a good friend who’s past away, but to be honest I have too much on my mind to linger on the tattoo for too long . . . Asesino: Guys, no one questions my passion because everyone knows that at two hundred pounds you don’t win World Titles without passion. I didn’t lose anything after Death Wish, I gained. After I was cheated out of the Untamed Title by Jeff James I could finally see clearly. I could see the things that I hadn’t accomplished in my career, and I could now see myself accomplishing those things. You see Hitchin ever since our last meeting I’ve only gotten better, and you’ve only gotten worst. (Asesino winces in pain a little.) Asesino: PTC our careers have taken completely different paths, and it’s only now, after months of separation that our paths cross once again, but know this, when I go into a war I go in with the mind frame of victory, and that will be the mind frame I come in with on Survival. (Asesino winces again, we can tell that this really does hurt.) Asesino: The PTC roster can ignore me for as long as they like, because I know that it's only a matter of time until I'm at the top of PTC. Bill: All done. (Asesino sits up with his back to us so we can’t see the tattoo yet.) Asesino: He was special, and having this reminder of him on my arm all the time means a lot to me, thanks Bill. Bill: No problem. (Asesino gets up and we see his new ink for the first time, it’s a Lionfish that covers a good part of his forearm.) Asesino: He sure was a good fish. (Asesino walks back to the front of the store and DVD is waiting, the two say their goodbyes then walk back out into the cold night.) Asesino: What you get? DVD: A red cichlid on the base of my hand. Asesino: Ew, fresh water fish make me sick. (The scene fades to blue.)
