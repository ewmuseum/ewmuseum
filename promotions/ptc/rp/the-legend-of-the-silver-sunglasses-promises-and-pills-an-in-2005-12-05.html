The Legend of the Silver Sunglasses: "Promises and Pills -- An Introduction to Craig's Acid-Based Quest of Darwinism"

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 A slow stream of snake-like smoke crept away from Craig Miles' lips. He leaned back in an old wooden chair, which let out a longwinded creak under the pressure. His dirty barefeet broke the tension as they landed with a thud on a nearby table covered with nothing more than dirt, one handle of Jack Daniels and two packs of Newports. Except for the dim spotlight shining on the silver sunglassed Dean of Thermodynamics and the dusty furniture, the room felt abandoned. "Alright Craig, we're ready." Miles nodded and flicked away his cigarette into the corner. "Let's try not to start a fire in the soundbooth this time either…" "You're the one who wanted to know why they call me the Dean of Thermodynamics," Miles yelled back to the sound technician, his arms opened widely as if to feign a lack of judgement on their part. "Mister Miles, I understand our contract with the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament stipulates your rights to direct and produce a Bonus DVD feature by yourself and a man actually called Beef…" the technician mumbled something about his paycheck quite inaudibly, "but it does NOT give you a right to burn, piss, legdrop, powerslam, or have sex with your wife on any of this company's property." Miles audibly sighed, the technician could've sworn this lunatic was rolling his eyes at him…meanwhile, Miles plopped down from his position and grabbed his pack of smokes. "Well, we've tried having fun," Miles started smacking the pack against his wrist, "apparently, you're just a fucking douche. So, let's just get me out of here so the Legend of Pete can remind us once more how 53% of male America can't find their dick cause they're too fat." "Excuse me?" "Are we rolling?" "Umm…no. We were hoping you'd let us know when you were ready to begin." Miles opened up the pack and tossed a cigarette into his mouth. He popped off the bottletop of the whiskey, then leaned back to his original position. "Ready." The technician blinked a couple times before realizing Miles wanted him to start rolling the tape. Of course, he wasn't going to do this right away. Instead inside his booth, where Miles couldn't hear, he looked to his co-workers and exclaimed, "We can't waste two hours of tape on a man getting plastered off Jack Daniels!" A female shrugged saying, "Just ask him questions." "I don't know anything about wrestling, or this Golden Turnbuckle!" "Oh, its all fake anyway," a male chimed in. "He'll probably splice whatever he says in here with a highlight reel." "Are we just gonna tape me getting wasted, or should I break out my lighter?" Miles asked loudly enough for them to hear. The Technician quickly avoided that repeat scenario, "Ummm…no, that won't be necessary Mr. Miles. I'm, well to be honest…I'm not even sure what to ask you right now." Miles took a swig of his whiskey and contemplated this deeply for a moment before asking loudly, "What's your name?" "Uh, its Tony. Now Mister Miles," Tony quickly tried to change the subject, but Miles laughed loudly to which Tony was surprised. "What's so funny, Mister Miles?" "Well first off…just call me Craig…or Miles. Second…" Miles laughed again, "I think I knew a Tony the Technician once…in my business." "Ah, yes very funny…Mis--Craig. I'm still not sure what to ask you. I don't exactly follow wrestling." Miles shrugged, "Neither do I." "Oh, well…ok." Tony rubbed his temples as the tapes had now started rolling for two minutes. Miles lit up his Newport and exhaled a smoke ring. "How 'bout this Tony…you just ask whatever you want, and I'll do my best to answer." "Ok…" So…Tony thought for a moment. What did he need to want to know about this man? "…who is Beef?" Miles exhaled a smoke ring, grinning widely. "A personal acquaintance of mine. A friend if you will. He's part of a force greater than most people see with their hearts. Alot of people don't understand that we are stronger when connected, but weaker when alone. That is the beauty of Mega Job." "…Mega Job?" "Yes, otherwise known as Beef. El Janito…and STEVE." "Are those their real names?" "They're getting drunk in the back of my RV if you want them to come in." "Um…no…that's ok…you're driving? …nevermind." Tony's mind raced, "We don't need any more …people in here, but why don't you tell us a little about your friends." Rolling, mexican-styled nylon guitar fingerpicking. A sense of foreboding mystery, the pains of love… "Well, nobody's really sure where El Janito came from. He's one of those magic tumbleweeds that roll from the desert and blossom to a star. Some say that there's some of Zorro's bloodline in him, others think its a twinge of Casanova. I won't forget the first day I met him. He saved my life…" "When I was young, I was brash. Ignorant…" A young, brown-skinned young man walked through saloon doors , guitar case in hand. His eyes shot wide open, frightened beyond belief. "Listen man, I didn't know that was your daughter!" Craig Miles shouted with his hands held up in hopes of calming an older gentlemen that just happened to be pointing a shotgun at Miles' chest. Panic overtook the young guitarist, his nerves shaking and causing him to drop his guitar case. The older man looked towards the noise just slightly, but enough to convince Miles to jump. The old man swerved and fired, blasting through the table Miles ducked behind with a somersault roll. "I DON'T WANNA DIE! SAVE ME! SOMEONE PLEEEEEEEEASE!" The young guitarist stumbled around the room frantically screaming and sobbing uncontrollably, seemingly unable to find a hiding spot. The older man looked at him with a sneer, "You're pathetic," he said watching the now quivering young man… CRACK~! "WHOOOOOOOOOO~! Gimme, gimme, gimme the honky tonk blues!" The man's guitar was shattered, the older man unconscious and Craig Miles smiling victoriously. "Hey you…you saved my life right there…what's your name?" "J-J-J-anito. El Janito." "Janito…I shall never leave your side until I repay this debt." "But you saved my life…" "Then we both bound as crusaders forever, for that is the Way of the Masons." El Janito didn't know what to say, his greatest day of reckoning stood before him. His father had always said his greatest fear would be answered on that hallow day, so trying to control the desire to piss himself, he asked…"Have you seen a guy carrying a snake in a bag lately?" "Yes, I know of STEVE. He has been waiting for you." El Janito nodded. Then he ran…ran as far as he could. "Wait, wait, wait," Tony the Technician interrupted. "Isn't Steve the midget they hang out with? What the hell was he carrying around a snake in the middle of Mexico for?" "Oh, it wasn't a snake." "It wasn't?" "What was it?" "You wouldn't understand." "Why not?" "You've never heard of Biaakazatron." "Right…right, I've never heard of Biaakazatron. CUT!" "What?" Miles exhaled a waft of smoke towards the ceiling, both arms extended in the air. One happened to be holding that handle of Tennessee's finest, perhaps influencing the MILESwide grin on Miles' face. "Craig. There is no such place as Biaakazatron. In fact, there will never be one. I doubt it even exists in alternate dimension if THEY exist." Tony the Technician sounded demeaning to say the least. "I will now stop your stories with Mega Job…" "Only for now, but you will believe by the end" Miles retorted, inhaling off his Newport. "I can only hope you lose in this first round of the tournament, so I don't have to do this EVER again." "Don't make me inspired, when I'm inspired I'm capable of great things." Miles grinned widely. "From my notes here, I see your opponent's name is Armaan…" Tony the Technician paused, "I'm sure you'd have some words for him, am I correct?" Miles shrugged, "Not really…" Milex blew a smoke ring towards the ceiling, "…I mean…what do you say to someone you're probably going to knock out for three days? Sorry your skull got turned to jelly again, maybe this time you should really reconsider walking into the ring with a man nicknamed 'The King of the Blindside Chairshot'? I mean c'mon, the one thing that enables me to be the man I am and the quivering piece of tenderized meat he is…its not only physical, it's mental." Miles shot back some whiskey, then took another drag off his cigarette. He let out a loud belch, slammed the bottle on the table and flicked his cigarette to the ground, stamping it out with his barefoot. "This kid…if you haven't seen it once, you've seen it a thousand times. His head's been spun around to the point its hanging by its last bone. He doesn't know if its worth it, he doesn't know if he'll make it. Its supposed to be romantic, maybe some testament to how those that get are virtuous should survive. I say fuck that, let the heads roll off one by one cause this isn't a sport or business that gives two shits and a nickel about your life the minute your last foot leaves the door." Miles paused, thinking hard and Tony could've sworn the sunglasses shimmered for a brief moment. "What a freak," the female beside him whispered. "I love fucking Darwin because he's the one that explained it all so easily. Survival of the fittest. If you're man enough to admit you've got one foot out the door cause you're afraid of the future, I'll be man enough to knock your ass the fuck out of the way because this sport means too much to me at this point." Miles sighed out loud, "Its too bad, the kid sounds like he could be the real deal. It sounds like he's got some heart, but he's got fear…and that's something I just don't have anymore. I can win, I can lose…but I still know that tomorrow, I'll be ready to bust my head again. To not know that…and get in the ring…you're playing with fire…and I love playing with fire." Tony seemed surprised at the words, was this guy for real? …was it a show? Nobody could be this completely nuts. "If you don't care about winning or losing, why are you here?" "I made a promise to not only Steve, but many beings…to save wrestling from extinction." "Just like this kid, I was laid up in a hospital. I heard he's out from a freaking brainbuster on a chair…I've eaten more of those than pancakes. I've been stretchered out only once…I got thrown off a twenty foot cage…long story short…my friends and family are very fucked up. Every doctor told me I got lucky…that I could've broken my neck, my back, gotten paralyzed, whatever…I was fine. Not many people visited me, but god bless that little bastard…Steve did…" Beep. … Beep. …Beep. Yup, a hospital room. A morning sunrise brightened it with an orange hue, the sounds of cartoons accompanied by the laughter of Craig Miles. His food tray possessed a cup of coffee and a half-eaten bacon, egg and cheese biscuit. The door opened, in walked a woman in her late 20's, brunette, wearing a hoodie sweatshirt with sweatpants She skipped up to Craig, giving him a kiss on the forehead. "Someone's here to see you," she said gently rubbing her hand through Miles' hair. Craig turned off the television, "Aw Nix, I don't wanna be both--" Miles stopped talking, his face lit up with a huge smile as a rather diminuative subject walked in. "STEEEEEEEEEEVE~!" "WASSUP." Miles cocked his head to the side, as if seeing something in Steve that didn't seem right. "You look a little upset man…are you alright?" Steve looked up and down at Miles, a twenty foot drop and this man was acting like there wasn't a scratch on his body. Steve pointed at him angrily and shouted, "WUSS." Miles seemed offended, "Oh come on! You don't mean that!" Steve shook his finger and shouted even louder, "PANSY." "Nix…I think you better leave," Craig responded with a deadpanned expression, "it seems someone has something they need to get off their chest." "Does this guy really keep saying one word in this story?" "Shut up and pay attention, Tekkie the Tiger. Steve was pissed, seemed let down or something, he spent the next fifteen minutes just calling me names angrily…as if my actions befuddled him. Then he said something really weird…" "RISK." Steve said smacking Miles' head. "Risk!!?!? What'd I risk of yours!?!?" Steve grunted, he pulled out a pill container and opened it. "HERBAL?" he asked Miles. "All right Steeeeeeeeeeve!" Miles popped the pill immediately, "I knew you'd sneak me some grass, you're the man." Steve didn't respond, instead patted Miles' head and shook his own head disappointedly. Miles' eyebrow arched, "Its not grass?" Steve nodded, confirming what Miles found to be a buzzkill. Until…his head started like lighter…like a balloon… "What the fuck, Steve…" Miles slowly turned his head, his pupils decreasing and increasing, trying to focus on Steve who was already at the door. The midget looked cold, yet strangefully hopeful. "SEVEN." Steve said with a nod. "What's seven?" "TRUTH." "And then I blacked out." "Craig…wake up…up and at 'em…" "I'd taken some good tranquilizers, painkillers, uppers, downers, acid, mushrooms, weed, keef, nitrous, boom, smack, pow, coke, white horse, white dragon, hash, black tar and Tylenol, but whatever Steve gave me must've been fucked up. I woke up, in the hospital room…but in outer space, like two points in space had been merged in some weird force field…" "I am sorry this has happened without much warning, but seeing as your lack of fear of consequences nearly got you killed, I find it very important to reveal my true nature, your destiny and why your wrestling career is vital to the survival of Biaakazatron." Craig Miles looked shocked, slightly pale. "S-S-S-S-Steve?" "Yes…and no…" "W-W-What happened to your face?" "Wait, that's the midget?" "Yeah, talking in a British accent." "I thought he only said one word at a time." "I was as shocked as you are." "And what did his face look like again, just for clarification?" "It opened up to reveal a cockpit…and a small alien inside it…" "That's some pill." "You're tellin' me." "The one you know as Steve serves as a host and cameraman for the Biaakazatronian people. We are a peaceful people…and well, we are big fans of this entertainment you earthlings call wrestling…and from time to time we like to aid those that have made great sacrifice in the sport." Miles blinked slowly, "You're British." "Your brainwaves have indicated this the least threatening of accents to your psychological profile. If we would've chosen French, I believe you might have attacked right away and accused the country itself of some very strange alien science. On the other hand, you also have never met a British person you deemed intelligent, so you don't actually believe I'm from England right now. "You talk alot more than Steve does…" "He's actually only saying one word. The portal we use only works after seven hours under the pill's influence, then for one hour we can communicate to the chosen one through Steve's vocal resonation." "I am soooooooooo fucked up right now. Is my wife out there?" "She is here, yes…" "Isn't she wondering what's going on?" "She's sleeping. You have kept many people awake the last several days. Many Biaakazatronian people believe you have proven your dedication enough and wish for your trial as Chosen One to begin." "Chosen One?" "Of Seven." "Of what?" "To try and find the Light of Biaakazatron." "Why?" "Do you wish to help the sport of wrestling, or to demolish it?" "Dude, I gotta say that whatever you have got there Steve…just sell that shit dude. You'll make crazy cash cause I am hearing you say some FUCKED UP SHIT RIGHT NOW." "Find the silver sunglasses and you can save the sport, Craig Miles. If you don't, the other six will be given the power of the Light." "And then…just like that…he was gone." "DUDE." smack! "Don't slap me motherfucker!" Miles grunted as he swatted Steve off his bed. Miles held his head, as he watched his wife Nixon turn over on her cot still asleep. "DROOLING." "Another fucking side-effect from the pill that made you sound British and talkative…" Miles said as he wiped his chin. "SUNGLASSES?" Steve said aloud, which made Miles pause for a moment. Craig contemplated the word Steve said, his eyebrow arching slowly, "Was I talking to myself, or was I talking to you just now?" Steve thumbed his chest, "ME." Miles leaned back, a little worried where this was going. "And what were we talking about?" Steve put his hands on his hips angrily, "TRUTH." "What truth?" "BIAAKAZATRON." Miles leaned back in his bed and looked towards the ceiling with a defeated sigh, "I am going to try and go to bed. I am going to pretend I am very fucked up right now and that this is the greatest drug ever invented. If you are here and say the name of that planet when I awake, I will join you on your quest to save wrestling for…those people." Miles stopped talking, trying to take in what he just said. Steve climbed up on his bed and gave him an impassioned bear hug. "AWESOME." Craig Miles finished the last drop of whiskey in the bottle, holding it up in the light and looking at it with a wide smile through his silver sunglasses. "Steve didn't say anything more that night, I'm glad he didn't. I didn't fall asleep for a good two hours," Miles put the bottle down and lit up another cigarette. "I was wondering what my life would mean if I woke up and heard him say that word…and what that meant…" "So, those silver sunglasses that you're wearing right now are from another planet?" Tony tried to hold back his disbelieving laughter. "What do you think?" Miles said with a laugh. "I think you're from another planet, I think its time to go home, and I feel very fearful for this Armaan guy that is most likely about to step into the ring with a delusional, raving psychotic that believes his great friend of a midget is a host cell for an alien race enraptured by wrestling of all things." "We're done?" Miles asked aloud disappointedly, "I was just about to get into the morning and the map!" "Craig….Mister Miles, the only way I'm hearing anymore of this drug-induced crap is if I'm forced to next round. Good bye." Miles' silver sunglasses shimmered for a moment, he exhaled a smoke ring. "Sounds like a challenge," he whispered with a MILESwide grin. Craig pulled out his cell phone, clicking the walkie-talking button, "YO STEEEEEEEEEEEVE." After an electronic chirp, Steve responded, "YO." "Can you drive?" "WORD." "Badass. Let's go get some tacos and margaritas downtown, then bet on Janito mud wrestling at The Crazy Horse." "BURRITO."
