Perceptions

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 "WHERE THE FUCK IS HE?" Universal Wrestling Federation Public Relations director, Tuck Tucker screamed as he slammed his fists down on his desk. "He was supposed to be here five days ago! He’s supposed to be filming hype videos for the GTT!" "Err… I don’t know, sir." Answered the timid intern. "Fuck… we’re so fucking screwed… Fucking Kenny Mercury… Fucking ass hole…" Yeah, that’s right. Just days away from one of the most anticipated matches of the first round of the GTT… and Kenny Mercury was nowhere to be found. Calls had been made… messages had been left… but Kenny Mercury… he’s an illusive one sometimes. Perhaps he was off in Russia, training his firm pale ass off for one of the biggest matches of his entire career. Perhaps he was in the UWF’s NPW gym, punishing his body… preparing for the very punishment Youngblood would deal him in the future. Perhaps he was taking this match seriously. Perhaps he took the GTT seriously. PSSSHHYEAHRIGHT! Kenny Mercury is off on a yacht somewhere in the Caribbean. His unmistakable golden hair framing his face, covered by a pair of black shades and topped off with a smile. Kenny reclined back in his deck chair as the warm ocean air danced over his sculpted abs. "Hype…" Kenny giggled slighty. "What is there to hype? Brandon Youngblood thinks he’s a loser… well, reality is perception. If he thinks he’s a loser… if he thinks he’s mediocre… then he will be." Kenny lowered his sunglasses to the tip of his nose, exposing his cold blue eyes. "I, on the other hand… I know what I am… I know who I am… I am Kenny Mercury. I am the shooting star… the technical wizard… I am the definition of precision. I know I am the best bet in this entire tournament. Call it cliché… call it boring… call it whatever you want… But I know… it’s the truth." … "Um… Who are you talking to?" Inquired a deck hand with a quizzical look on his face. "Me? Oh… no one…" Call it professional-wrestleritis.
