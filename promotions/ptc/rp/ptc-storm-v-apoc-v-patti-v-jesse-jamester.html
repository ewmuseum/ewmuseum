24 March 2004 The Chronicles Of Jesse Jamester: Part 1 Prologue. It’s been 12 years since he first stepped into the squared circle. Never did he think he’d get this shot, nor did he believe he’d be the best. As the once upon a dream soon became a reality, Jesse Jamester started to realize it too. He’d worked hard over those twelve years and now the chance for a shot to be the most respected man on the wrestling circuit was only seven days away. Jesse Jamester started wrestling at the age of twenty-three, when he was known as ‘The Kid’. Over the years that have passed he has grown, developed, learned, and become the beast that is now the most terrorizing force in Global Wrestling. But his main challenge come Sunday is his obstacle of beating the Global Champion Chris Storm along with Patti Anderson and Apocalypse. Jesse has the strength and the courage to give this match everything, but will he be able to overcome the week of stress and emotion that hits him leading up to it? Will ‘The Kid’ be able to tell everyone he finally accomplished his dream? Or will Jesse Jamester, fall to his face and crumble when the pressure is upon him at it’s strongest hour? Story Begins. The trees quickly passed as the black dodge 4x4 truck drove down a gravel road so bare it had to be years before it’d been paved. Inside the truck sat a man, his eyes focused on the road, but his face telling a whole different story. His hair hung down over his shoulders and went just a tad past his collarbone. It’s brown pattern of straightness was not perfect but showed the man took care of himself. As the hair went up it worked well with his face, giving him an appeal that many would see and not forget. The goatee he sported was nothing out of the ordinary, but gave the man an appeal. That appeal would be the one he used during his job every night, for this man was Jesse Jamester. His eyes a hazy blue showing he’s been on the road for a long time as Jesse sits their just staring at the road ahead of him. It’s been a while since Jesse traveled this road. The years have been numerous and so have the months following. For he’s been a busy man traveling around the world to places likes Japan and Brazil to wrestle for Global Wrestling. The history of his past memories on this road go through Jesse’s head. The time he spent with his best friend Ryan Bladez in his early days of wrestling was one of the more frequent and enjoyable ones. The two had so much fun doing this sport, it almost seemed surreal to them at first. Jesse and Ryan always wondered, what other job on the earth was as great as this one? Where else can you get paid for doing something you love to do and be able to travel all over the world and meet new people? The two would end up touring near the whole world except the north and south hemisphere’s to find out how true that statement was. Jesse and Ryan were known as the Mystical Mix, Jesse being the smaller of the two, and yet somehow Ryan being the man who did more of the insane top rope high risks. They always loved putting on a show for people, even if against each other. But after seven years of wrestling the thirty year old Ryan had taken a very large toll on his body and he’d have to retire due to a knee injury. This left Jesse in a state of unrest. He’d been motivated by Ryan and the same vice versa. But now the duo would have to split, and Jesse would have to pave his own road. It seemed odd to Jesse at first. For seven years he’d been a main tag team partner, winning many titles, and though capturing other gold he really didn’t hold onto it for long. Jesse was as they say, a lone ranger, or lonesome to be frank. His life took a whole new turn though when he met his future to be wife, Julie. Julie became this joy that showed Jesse not everything neither had to be jokes nor had to be complicated. Even though Jesse wasn’t a big time wrestler, Julie always inspired him to do his best no matter where he wrestled. She was the replacement in Jesse’s life for his best friend Ryan. Julie and Jesse got married a month shy of Jesse’s thirtieth birthday. A month after they were married however, Julie was killed in a bank robbery gone critically wrong. On August 24, 1999, Julie was claimed dead and Jesse had once again taken a turn for the worst. The road became longer in front of Jesse for he’d began to remember even more that altered his life, but shook it out of his mind. Looking ahead still he began to worry, his eyes could show the worry for they were wondering on the road as if looking for something. But nothing was to be seen but gravel that Jesse drove over in hope to reach his destination. Jesse could see life changing before him. First the global title shot, and now his past somehow filling in all the questions Jesse asked before going to sleep at night. Questions like, why did Julie die? Can I ever overcome that obstacle? God will you help me achieve my goal? I wonder where my father is? Questions like these flowed through Jesse’s mind and though some were of worry others would be questions that Jesse answered in the ring with aggression and tactical presents. It was a game Jesse played, a game that he grew to love, a game he would soon have to defeat or be defeated by to answer some of those questions. The Message. Stumbling onto the porch Jesse is tired, his eyes bloodshot from the twenty some hour drive and his hair now not so straight as it were before. Carrying only that of his luggage of dirty clothes that he wore nights before, Jesse searches his pockets for his keys when he reaches his doorstep. The door however is already open, and with the twist of the knob Jesse walks right in. He looks around at first, cautioned of what or who may be inside waiting. Shutting the door he places his bag behind the door and turns to walk down the hallway to the living room where a bright light seems to be coming from. Pacing slowly so he will not be heard, Jesse steadily makes his way nearer to the light. Turning through the archway with his right shoulder pressed against the wall and fists clenched for preparedness. He takes a final step into the living room and puts his fists up ready to swing. Looking around not a soul is there, only a lit fireplace and a chair that faces it. Jesse puts his fists down and shakes his head, as he goes to turn around. However as soon as he turns he runs straight into a small petite figure that he instantly recognizes as Nikki his girlfriend. Jesse’s face alters so quickly from his face of shock to a state of bliss it’s almost unnatural. Nikki kisses Jesse on the cheek and he kisses her back, the two showing some tenderness for they’ve been dating a long time. As the two finally sit down in the chairs that face the fireplace, Jesse sees Nikki’s sparkling eyes and wonders what she is doing in the house. ‘How’d you know I was coming up here?’ hesitantly asked Jesse as he wondered from Nikki’s face to the fire in various moments. ‘It’s the weirdest thing. See, I was sitting at home, you’re apartment and I got this phone call from a man. His voice was real deep, almost like he was disguising it at first. However when I answered, he asked for you. I told him you weren’t in and then his voice broadened, becoming a natural speaking voice if you will. Anyway, he said he was a friend of yours and told me to come here and tell you he’s in town,’ Nikki answered calmly, as if it were no big deal. But on the contrary, Jesse’s face had begun to show awe, for he could only imagine who this person was. Standing up he kissed her hand and told her he’d be back, but little did she know it’d be a long while before she saw him again. Old Friends Reunite. The sky a musty cloud as it hung in the sky like never before. The headlights of the black dodge truck pierce the dark like stars on a gray dual night, as the truck pulls up in between two trees to a driveway. On the right side tree a sign that has faded slightly over the time reads “Hilltop Drive Cemetery.” The lights go out and the door opens as Jesse steps out and with a hooded sweatshirt on walks up to the gate and opens it to go in. The creeks in the trees, the howls throughout the rural area, and even the slithering of the leaves don’t scare Jesse as he walks with hands in pockets down the driveway to the cemetery. Reaching the end of the driveway Jesse comes upon yet another gate. This one opened however, and past the gates are thousands of burial sites. Even on a night like this, where the cloud shows signs of rain, and the coldness is bitter, Jesse will visit the cemetery. A glare of light is seen about halfway down the cemetery, fire possibly, but from the place Jesse stands he can’t be sure. He wanders down through the headstones getting closer as he does, and as he comes upon the light he was right in mind it was a fire. But the person sitting there who made the fire is the reason Jesse came. The man stands up, showing a dominant figure of gigantic proportion that makes Jesse look small. Staring into the man’s deep green haze like eyes, Jesse smiles and hugs the man. The man pats Jesse on the back and they smirk after Jesse goes back to standing in front of the man. ‘It’s been a while…’ says the man in a deep voice that shows a much broader age than one would expect. ‘Almost too long,’ replies Jesse looking up at the man. The two still wearing smiles as Jesse sits on the head stone and the larger man does so opposite of him. ‘So how you been Ryan? I’ve seen you fight for the heavyweight belt over ten times now in three different companies, and each time you get this feeling you’re going to fail,’ starts Ryan looking into Jesse’s eyes with meaning, ‘I’ve come here to make sure you don’t lose grip Jess. You were always there for me. When I got my leg operated and you were in Japan, you called me and we talked for hours! I want to repay you for that, and because man, you’re like a brother to me.’ Jesse just stares at him with eyes dripping mist from the words Ryan shared with him. ‘You’re like a brother to me too man… Really, you are. I could have never been so successful if it hadn’t been for you pushing me along man,’ mutters Jesse as he begins to let tears out, but tries to hide it by wiping his eyes with his sleeve quickly. ‘Just like this place, we share memories, and I’ll never forget you or you’re wife Julie,’ stated Ryan and Jesse followed him up with a nod. The two looked at the tombstone of Jesse’s late wife, Julie Fristik, the woman of his dreams, and the woman he said ‘I do’ to. Inside of Jesse’s mind it was like yesterday, but with Ryan standing there, he didn’t want to show him the sadness. The two sat and talked for a while. Around one o’clock in the morning the rain began to fall and Jesse and Ryan left the cemetery to go back to their homes. As Jesse walked the path back to his truck he had a small tear run down the side of his cheek. For even though he had dealt with his wife’s death many years before, she still was a vital part of his mind after all these years. The Nightmares Still Exist. When death had changed Jesse’s life other demons followed. It wasn’t just a dream of hopeless comings that pushed Jesse into an insane state of mind, but it was the things he did, and the people he was around. Jesse had been a demon of his own mind for a good eight years, and when his wife had died, it was true that the demons would be relentless on his body. Jesse could not withstand a good deal of stress, never could, and possibly never would unless he faced the stress reasonably. Instead he dealt with it in the worst way, a way that would eventually kill him if he didn’t stop. As Nikki left the house to go to the town for groceries Jesse was sitting in the living room watching the Television as a thought crossed his mind. ‘What if Apocalypse turns on me come Sunday? Would he do that? And Storm, he has that Ryoplex move, where he gives you a German suplex from the torture rack. Can he get me up in the air? He hangs up and dials again, still no answer. Jesse slams the phone down and his fist shakes, how could he be so stupid, how could a man who’s trained for 12 years to get this shot forget the first rule to winning a match, studying the opponent! An irate temper steams in Jesse but it’s not the temper that will hurt him, oh no, it’s something much worse. Jesse walks around the house pacing as he talks to himself. ‘What was I thinking; I’m not champion material! I’ve gone above and beyond in so many things but I know for a matter of a fact that this match, this night, will be the night of my demise! I should have said no when Christina McCoy asked me to participate in the Global Contenders match. Damn it, now I will fail… in front of the world! How can I be so stupid!’ rambles Jesse as he stares at the floor with his body trembling from the frustration. With a glare to the side he has an idea, or some sort of plan. He hurries through the living room and into the kitchen where he grabs the handle on the fridge and swings it open. Almost blasting the door off its hinges Jesse stares inside looking for something. Then with his face suppressed in awe he comes along his favorite friend, Liquor. Jesse stares at the bottle with tender. His eyes gaze at the bottle as if it’s the answer to all his questions. Jesse reaches for the mirror but at the same time stops. Looking straight into the mirror he eyes himself down. It’d been so long since Jesse looked at himself, for with the busy schedule he never had time. His long hair was now untidy, and his goatee showed to be not so groomed. Jesse looked at his eyes, the fear that had once raged about being in this match soon grew and he could tell it was time for something he had done so long ago.
