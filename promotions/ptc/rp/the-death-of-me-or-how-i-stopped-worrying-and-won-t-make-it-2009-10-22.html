The Death of Me or how i stopped worrying and won't make it to the second round p.s. dont read this if you don't enjoy crappy roleplays. p.p.s i did work very hard and i have and while i would like you to read it you dont have to at all

Date: 2009-10-22 PREVIOUSLY ON GCW WORLD-WIDE - SurReal approaches the ropes from the outside, trash talking Quinton. But Quinton isn't a man of words. He reaches through the middle rope and grabs a hold of SurReal's hair, looking to pull him into the ring and teach him a lesson. SurReal struggles to escape Quinton's grasp. All of a sudden, he spits a thick, green mist into Quinton's face. * * * * My eyes, I can't see anything. The last thing I saw was SurReal in my face blowing a green mist into me. Again, again I lost the will, the drive to succeed. I lay in my bed with my strength near depleted. Tossing and turning, the blurring of my vision didn't help either. I keep rubbing my eyes, the pain, even will all these pain drugs, I can't seem to make this all go away. Was is psychological? What exactly is wrong with me, not physically, I know what is wrong physically, but mentally, I am at a loss. I want it gone. I need it all gone. I am a fighter, I am a champion, yet I am here laid in my bed like I was a terminal patient, I couldn't go anywhere, I felt locked in place. I felt like I was chained to my own bed, like a prisoner. I wanted to break free. I felt like someone who didn't have the drive or the will to do anything, I felt like a man without passion, without a heart, but the only thing that kept me from my bed is my eyes. I felt like dying, I felt like not caring anymore, but this wasn't the way I wanted to go. I want to go with a bang. I always thought I would go out doing something I loved. I thought I want die a proud and glorious death, but being here stuck, helpless, with a rope around my neck slowly tightening one knot after another. My mind is a mess. I need to get out. * * * “Tossing and turning, Quinton lays in his bed, famished from a night of fighting, yet he could not see where he put that fork, he could not find where that fork goes to his mouth, it was an enigma.” A strange voice echoes throughout the room. “Quinton sits up, looking all around him, not know what is going on, he tries to put on his reading glasses, but suddenly remembers he does not remember ever owning them. His mouth, that mouth of his opens up, suddenly he emerges with a voice of his own, the one he lost after all these years.” “What the bloody fuck is going on? Who the hell is there?” Quinton yells. His vision is still blurry, he was finally able to get some rest, but now awoken to a strange man sitting in the room. “Who is that?” “Hello Lucas, I want to play a game. Can you guess who I am? Lucas cocks his head and looks at me, he doesn't know who I am, he doesn't know that I am James von Doom, one of his 76 allies.” James von Doom sits there in the chair relatively close to Quinton, he is wearing a black shirt with white lettering that says NARRATOR on it. “Sitting there next to Quinton is James von Doom, wearing a shirt with the words narrator on them, since Lucas cannot read, let alone see, it was the idea at the behest of President Steven Caldera that von Doom become Lucas Quinton's narrator until he become unhandicapped.” “James von Doom? Is that you? What the hell are you doing in my house?” Quinton clenches his fists. He quickly searches for a drink of water, he dips his finger in the cup and starts to rub his eyes trying to get even the tiniest of glimpses of the man sitting next to me. “No, It's Skylar Montgomery, nah man, just kidding around, it's me James von Doom, and not Skylar Montgomery as you once thought, or did you not say Skylar Montgomery, I think I did, or was it you? I don't remember and that's not the point! I am here to be your guide dog, to be your seer, I am the period to your comma, do you feel me dog? Lucas Quinton gives me a blank stare, even though the stare is accompanied by glaring over those glossy eyes, he can't see me, but he sure can hear what I am saying. He shakes his head and continues to drink the water that was set upon him.” “I don't need a baby sitter, von Doom, I am fine sitting in this bed by myself, slowly dying.” Quinton takes the comforter and sheets off his bed, he turns and puts his feet on the floor, he parts his hair back with his hands and sighs. “As long as you are here, you might as well get me something to wear, because obviously I am not going to sleep while you are in my face doing whatever the hell you do.” “Yippy! I yell as I am excited for this opportunity, I grab Lucas up by the arm, trying to get him settled, his feet on the ground....” “What the hell are you doing?” Quinton asks, he is already getting irritated by von Doom, it didn't take long, not at all. “What do you think I am doing? I am narrating your life, Mr. Lucas Quinton. Seeing as how you cannot see, I am and will be your narrator, I will narrate every single thing you do until in which time you receive your eyes back.” Quinton shakes his head, he knew this wasn't going to go as well as von Doom hoped. He tries to walk, but collapses down on the floor. “Damn it, Lucas, are you okay?” von Doom quickly jumps down to the floor and helps Lucas back up. “You are weak man, you need to get back into bed.” Quinton shakes his head. “I will stay weak if I stay in that bloody well bed, mate. Don't be so daft, I need to get some brekkie before I kip on the floor from being so knackered.” von Doom eyes Quinton "What the hell are you saying? Your accent is funny, are you Korean or something? What is a kip and a brekkie? Daft? Are you sure you are alright? All these made up words are hurting my head." “Never mind, just help me towards the dining room, von Doom. I don't know what I am going to do with you.” Quinton sighs once again. * * * What am I going to do now? I don't need a babysitter, I don't need someone to watch over me, this is bollocks. I don't care about seeing at the moment, but I have to deal with this sycophant carting me around my own house making sure I am comfortable. He tries so hard, but all he is-is a glorified nurse. I don't need my eyes to see that he is there not just to guide me around, he is there to observe my mental state. All these losses have put me in a spiraling depression, there is no arguing these facts, but they didn't need to send him to evaluate me. I want to be strong. I need to be strong, I have been battered down and beaten like a lamb before the slaughter. A simple minded man would have already ended it all, but I know I was better than that. I sat there at the dinner table draped over some hot cereal, the cinnamon and the brown sugar invaded my nose and mouth, as I take a bite, it was like a symphony had invaded my mouth and start to play an opera piece. I smile, that was the only thing I could smile about, a good mix of porridge is what sets me from the others. “Huh? Oh you like that, Lucas?” von Doom asks, now that wasn't a stupid question he asked, surprising actually, this day might just turn out for the better. “I saw that you had some of that instant oatmeal in your cupboard, but I decided what the fuck and made something from scratch. My nan made it many moons ago when I was a wee von Doom and I thought it would be a great opportunity to show off my culinary magicks. How are you feeling by the way? I don't think it's just your eyes, man, I think you have the flu or something, we should probably get you to a doctor.” I shake my head. “No, no, I will be fine for now.” I clasp the spoon in my hand and move it towards the bowl as the trickle of oatmeal overbears the spoon. “This is spectacular, how did you make it?” “I took it from the cupboard, it was on of those instant oatmeal packets.” von Doom can be heard move his chair backwards and getting up from said chair. He walks over to Quinton and pats him on the back. “I am glad you liked it, my grandmother would be so happy.” He laughs at me, trying to play me stupid. “James...that boy ain't right, listen, take me to the living room, I have something to talk to you about.” I stand up and he guides me to the living room. I find my way onto the couch and sit down. I wring my hands and scratch my forehead. “James, why are you here?” “What do you mean why am I here? Didn't I tell you? I am here because Mr. Caldera sent me to help you with whatever you need, to be your guide dog so to speak. I don't know what the problem is, is there one, Lucas?” Suddenly I sense von Doom is getting defensive, like he is hiding something. Although I couldn't see his facial expressions, I knew when I was being played like a dog. “That's what you want to me to think, isn't it? You and I both know why you are here, maybe Caldera sent you here, maybe he didn't, but I know for a fact you are hear to see how much damage was really done to me. We all know G.T.T is here and we are both in the running, you might have advanced to the next round, but we all know you are afraid of me, so is everyone else in that little tournament. We might both belong to GCW, but I know more than you think, von Doom, and if you are here to pull the wool over my eyes, than you can just get the hell out.” I was not above to playing anymore games right now. I might feel paranoid, but it is a good reason to feel suspicious nonetheless. I can hear him getting up from his seat, he was speechless, a persons demeanor can change so drastically in a short amount of time. I can hear the pacing back and forth, back and forth until... BANG! BANG! THWACK! “HEY! What is going on? von Doom? Are you alright, mate?” I yell, I don't know if he fell unconscious or what, I had no way of knowing what happened. “DAMN! My HEEL just turned! It hurts.” I can hear him crawling, getting back on the couch. I start laughing. “Oy, what is so funny, Quinton?” “It is like the blind leading the blind, what exactly did you do to turn your HEEL? You alright there, mate?” I can hear him laughing too. He was almost exactly in the same situation as I was. We both then started to laugh together. “Yeah, lucky I didn't fall on my FACE....tweener. I'll be alright for sure, don't worry about me. I realize you have your suspicions, and you have every right to question why I am here, you have every right to be mad for something that was totally out of your control. I am here, and even though you don't know me that well, and I don't know you that well either, I am here because I am concerned for your health, I have seen most of your matches and you don't have a very good track record, and I volunteered to get your spirits up, because I know you have something in you, but you don't take control of the situation and capitalize on it, that is why I am here, I am your guide....I am your narrator so to speak. I am pretty sure we went over this, Lucas.” “Don't be such a waffle, von Doom. As I have said many times before, I am fine. Caldera had no reason to send you over, this will eventually go away. The only thing I care about is giving payback towards that insect they call SurReal. He bitches on and on about how I injured him, it is not my fault he is weak and can't take a hit to the ground, now he pulls this green mist garbage in my face, IN MY FACE, and now I can't see, I know it will return and I know I will be fine, but I want him to feel my pain.” Dejavu. I fell like this conversation I am having keeps on going on and on, repeating it self again and again. What is going on? This pain isn't just beyond the physical, I feel lost, I feel as though I keep going around in circles like I missed something, a clue that will help me along my path. I wanted to reach out and ask for help, but I just couldn't do it. I don't know why. The realm of reality is slowly beating down on my head, splitting apart my brain and picking things to throw out and mangle. I feel as though as if I am some sort of science experiment that has gone wrong and I am paying for the sins that I have committed. I sigh. “What am I doing to myself? Really?” “What do you mean? It wasn't your fault this happened, you have immense talent, just you need utilize your strengths.” He says. “Damn my HEEL hurts.” “I realize that it is not my fault, but I feel like I am paying for something, the things in my past that I couldn't control, the things that were out of my hands, the things that I could not stop and now I have to face them and take responsibilty for them.” “So, are you saying that god is dishing out pain to you because of the things you did in the past?” “Exactly. I may be the stunning young buck you see right before your eyes, but on the inside burns a deep hatred for people. I am an asshole, I am a jerk, everyone else is just daft if they think I am really their mates, I would rather die alone than live in a group. That's the way it's always been, I have always surrounded myself with others to make myself feel important, but it can't happen that way anymore.” I pause for a minute. “Not anymore...” “Dude…deep.” * * * von Doom had left long ago. I had taken the liberty to as best I could to escort him out of my house. Indeed I was grateful for his generiosty, but I did need time to myself, time to recover. Twenty times over I go through my mind thinking about all the wrongs I have done, wishing I could have gone in the past and changed things. That was never going to happen. I needed a change of course. A new direction. It’s all I ever asked for, it’s all that I wanted in life. People don’t understand me, I am a complex machine. In and out I weave, depression on a line graph, as far down as it goes I feel happy, but with that the line grows, up and up it goes shooting past the point of no return. Hope is long past gone for me, but I need to keep going. I don’t know why. I don’t care why. I am not going to take the easy way out. I don’t have a lot to live for, not yet, I need to keep my dream alive. I need to do it for all the people before me that haven’t gotten the chance. I need to live through them. Was this a new calling for me? I have no idea. I have no clue what is coming, and I don’t understand what is happening. I get up from my chair, my eyesight has gotten a little better. I can see clearer than I had a little while ago. I slowly make baby steps as if I learned to walk right then and there. THWACK “FUCK!” I slam into the wall in the hallway. My heart begins to race, my arms droop down, I have no strength left, I can’t grab ahold of my heart and not let go, they were like 10 ton dumbbells. I fall to my knees, crashing down onto the floor like meteors. All my other organs begin to fail, one by one, except for my eyes, I can see clearly now, the mist from my face finally subsided, but the only thing I see is the ceiling. All the little speckles on the ceiling, they come in clearly, each one had their own personality, their own damages, they were like me, they were not perfect, far from perfect. Hundreds, not if thousands of speckles in their own little community. They had one another and not one would stray far from the pack, they were their own little world. I was in my own world, and it was about to collapse. As I lay on the floor with my eyes wide open, I could feel the cold trickle down my entire body, but there was no breeze, the last thing I remember was closing all the doors and windows. I was slowly getting colder and colder, did the temperature drop? I only knew of one thing that could be happening to me. I finally got out. This was the death of me. * Portion of match taken from the match written by Shane (a.k.a [name withheld]) for Global Championship Wrestling
