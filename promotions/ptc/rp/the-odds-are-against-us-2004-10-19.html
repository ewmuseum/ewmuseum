Date: 2004-10-19 Elia Smith Vs. Sebastian Moraco In the greater scheme of things, that semifinal match up in the Rockies Bracket is probably one of the more unlikely match ups in the entire tournament. Sure, you could make a case for matches competitors like Dave Harley, Johnny Lexicon, and Shadow. Then again, they all proved before that their success is anything but unlikely. Dave Harley: WWA icon, and former Global Title contender. Johnny Lexicon: Well, he just does everything bigger, badder, and better than the rest. A bye helps too. Shadow: Helping Wrestling Midwest a lot in the tournament after knocking out one of the favorites. So while others may think that there are more unlikely fighters still left in the tournament, none are more unlikely than Elia Smith and Sebastian Moraco. Elia Smith joins Karina Wolfenden and Shadow as the last three remaining female fighters. However, she is hardly spoken in the same sentence as those two. Karina is synonymous with GTT winner, and the Shadow is making her way up there after big wins. Yet, Elia Smith finds herself in the same situation as them. One more win and she gets a ticket into the bracket finals. All this after she was told that she could never be a successful wrestler. After she was told that she was too young, skinny, and weak to compete with the men. Tell that to her first three opponents. Even more impressive was that she did this after losing her father. A loss that would devastate any other person. On the other side of the pairing we have another unlikely hero. Sebastian Moraco, the only amateur wrestler in the tournament, finds himself in alien waters. Sebastian Moraco is probably the most unlikely success of the two. First off, he is fighting in his first matches ever. Secondly, he was nearly out of the tournament after his first round loss, before the Committee had a change of heart and gave him a second chance. A chance he took like none other and dismantled P. Vetra, before clawing and scratching his way to victory against Digital Mortality. Two unsung heroes, and almost certainly names that few people had penciled in on their brackets at home are now here fighting for a chance to go to the bracket finals and take on either Doctor Curiosity, another tournament favorite, or the Muppet Man. Unfortunately, only one of them will advance and go through. Elia Smith has enjoyed great success thus far, but her run will come up short. Sebastian believes that, and he's intent on making sure that his second chance doesn't get wasted. As was stated before last round, this is HIS tournament. Sebastian hung up and tossed his obsidian-colored cell phone into his duffelbag as he walked into the gym. Again, the gym was slightly empty save for a few people comprised mostly of the custodian and engineering crews of the GTT Coliseum. The gym was provided for the competitors to use to strengthen themselves up before matches, but only Sebastian had used them thus far. Makes sense, since he's also one of the least wealthy of the competitors. Sebastian's first stop in his quest to get in shape was going to be on the bike. He didn't like to lift weights, and thus all of his excercise routines were purely cardio. Sebastian set the bag aside taking out a small towel and hanging it on the bars. He set the bike for a moderate pace and began to slowly pedal before speeding up moderately. Sebastian looked around as he continued to pedal in an easy manner. The gym was both majestic and state of the art. It was clearer way better than any gym he had been in before. This coupled with his suite was the luxury that the GTT Committee was prepared to give their fighters for a moment of glory. There was no telling how much money was going into this, and how much money was coming out of it. Yet, all Sebastian could see were backstage workers trying to keep up with their routines at home. A few of the other people he recognized from his appearances in the backstage of the Coliseum, and while a few approaced him like he had celebrity status, none asked for his autograph. After all, he wasn't a celebrity. Hell, he wasn't even a professional athlete. He just managed to get lucky and now his luck has carried him into the fourth round of the GTT. Sebastian thought his luck would bring enemies, but the other workers seemed more intrigued than upset. After all, if this young, barely legal to drink, kid can win so much, why can't they?
