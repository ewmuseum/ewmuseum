Nothing to Lose...Everything to Gain

14 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2009-10-14 “I have nothing to offer but blood, toil, tears and sweat.” That phrase kept on pounding through my head as I looked around the sweltering hot desert. There was no one in sight as I began to walk barefoot through the golden coloured sands towards the top of a huge dune. I wasn’t sure where I was going but I soon realized this was all a dream. I looked down and noticed I was wearing a white tank top and pyjama pants as I started to run up the huge sand dune. When I reached the top I fell on my knees with exhaustion but was pleasantly surprised when I looked back up. There were a couple of white dirty looking tents setup beside a small shrub where a camel was tied off to. I seemed relieved to see some sort of civilization as I rushed down the huge dune towards it. When I got close enough I approached one of the tents with caution almost to the point where I was tip-toeing. As I got closer I could here some sort of tribal or religious chanting, I wasn’t quite sure what the hell they were saying to be honest. But when I poked my head through the opening in the tent I saw two men wearing the same coloured blue turban and brown robes. They were saying some sort of prayer as they bowed their heads and continued to chant. When they were finished one of them poured some sort of hot liquid that looked like tea into two cups and the men drank from them. Suddenly some shuffling in the corner from a third man who I hadn’t seen drew attention from the others. The man who was sleeping had woke up and noticed me. Before I knew it all three men were standing in front of me growling. Then I heard some sort of ringing in the background. RING…RING… RING… It was my cell phone that was ringing and it woke me up from what was turning into a horrible dream. I lifted my head quickly from my pillow and reached over to the nightstand beside me to answer the call. When I looked down at my cell phone I immediately recognized the number. “Hello” I said in a tired voice. “Hey, S.G it’s me Jimmy. I’m supposed to shoot your promo for the GTT7 tournament with you, I’m the cameraman.” The man said with a patient tone. “Oh yeah that’s right we were supposed to meet in my hotel lobby at ten this morning.” When I first answered I wasn’t sure who it was but it was now all coming back to me. I really wasn’t sure who the guy was but one day I got a call from him saying he was an up and coming wrestling promoter and broadcaster and offered to shoot my GTT7 promo for me. I thought it would be a great way to give the guy some experience and it would certainly help me out financially. “Well, Mr. Martins it’s ten right now and I’m actually downstairs waiting in the lobby. Are you still in bed?” The cameraman asked in curiosity. “To tell you the truth I am. But I’m going to throw something on and come down right away.” I told him with a regretful voice. “Alright man but please try and be as quick as possible I got a couple other appointments today.” "Be down ASAP." I hung up the phone and jumped out of bed right away. I opened up my brown suitcase that sat on the floor beside the bed. I quickly took off my PJ pants and exchanged them for a pair of blue jeans. I left my white tank top on and just through my leather jacket over top of it. I then went to the bathroom to splash some cold water on my face and then headed out the door. When I reached the ground floor lobby I could already see Jimmy staring at me from one of the many white leather couches. I walked over and took a seat beside him. “Sorry about that Jimmy!” I felt bad for being late and wanted to apologize to him just as an ice breaker. “No problem. So are you ready to start shooting”? Jimmy asked “Yeah, let’s do this.” I told him trying to sound as excited as possible “Alright do you have anything in mind as for location.?” Once again Jimmy asked another question. “Anywhere will do. I gotta lot to say and I don’t think anyone will be paying any attention to the scenery when I’m talking.” I explained trying not to sound too arrogant Jimmy smiled at me and then burst into a chuckle. “I like your attitude S.G. I’ll tell you what though. I seen a nice brick building outside. I say we shoot you in front of the brick wall speaking your mind about this match. It’ll really add to your character, I’m sure of it.” “Alright Jimmy I’ll trust you on that one.” Jimmy Immediately got up and walked outside as I stayed closely behind him. Once we stepped outside I knew exactly which brick building he was talking about. We crossed the busy intersection and sure enough it was the brick building I had in my mind. He signalled to the building and I stood in front of one of the brick walls. “How’s this Mr. Martins?” Jimmy asked trying to make me feel comfortable. “ It’s Perfect and I’m ready so let’s get this started.” I told Jimmy pushing him to get things moving. “Okay, I’m going to start with a little intro about you and your match at GTT7, then you can start as soon as I give you the signal.”. “Sounds far I told him” As I leaned up against the wall behind me and even put my left foot against it as well. Suddenly I seen the red light on his camera turn on as he pointed the piece of machinery right at me. Right away I tensed up as Jimmy began to speak. “One of the most anticipated matches of the GTT7 tournament will be Number One seed Jonathan Rhine of the SCCW going up against the Unseeded TFWF Superstar S.G. Martins. Most people look at this match and there first thought is "Squash match." But let me tell you all something that is very far from the truth. Jonathon Rhine maybe the Number One seed in this tournament but S.G. Martins has quite the wrestling background himself. He was brought up in a suburb just north of Toronto where his decorated amateur wrestling achievements speak for themselves while attending High school. It was weeks before he graduated High school when he actually learned how to Professionally wrestle. He was taught by an old retired Japanese wrester named Eizi Ezaki who had opened up a wrestling school in Downtown Toronto after his retirement from the sport. After nearly a year of training Martins began to wrestle under a mask all across Canada and even a brief stint in Asia. That only lasted awhile as he was scooped up by Texas based G-2 wrestling where he’s competed for almost a year now and has captured the prestigious G-2 Open Weight Championship on one occasion. In Recent Months he was also signed to a very lucrative deal that has brought him to the Grand Organization known as the TFWF. So here standing before me now is The Golden Boy… The Evolutionist… S.G. Martins!” During the whole time Jimmy was speaking the camera was pointed at me while I wasn’t doing much but lean up on the wall and stare at the camera or rather stare at Jimmy who was holding the camera. Nonetheless I knew it was my time to speak and that I did. “ No need for that introduction, these people already know who I am. But thanks anyway.” I said sounding as confident as ever and letting the nervousness seep away from behind me. “ Once again in my career I am being tagged the underdog. Actually I’m not sure what you call a guy that nobody thinks has a chance on this great green earth of beating winning. I guess you call me more of a Dead man walking.” I seen the expression on Jimmy’s face change as I continued on. Although I wasn’t sure if that was a bad or good thing. “But that’s alright because I’m coming into this match determined to win . I have ninety nine problems but unlike Rhine a bitch is not one of them. Well he’s running around picking flowers and playing she loves me-she loves me not I’m training hard in the gym for the biggest match in my career. That’s right I said it. THE match of my career!” I stood up strait from the brick wall as I said that last phrase one last time “The Match of my career.” “Jonathon let me give you a little advice on the girl problems. It doesn’t matter which one you pick she’ll screw you over in the end anyway. Your on the road a lot and we all know your feeling lonely. But for once in your life be a man and stop worrying about the ladies. Don’t try and ignore your first round match at GTT7 because this Dead man walking actually has a pulse. Unlike you Jonathon I’ve been preparing for this match and if I don’t win at least I’ll know I’ve given the fans their moneys worth.” I started to walk away from the brick building and Jimmy followed still pointing the camera at me. That’s exactly what I wanted him to do as I crossed the intersection still with him behind me. I looked back at him with a smile on my face as we reached the other side of the street. “It’s fights like this one that I thrive on. Ever since I was kid I loved to fight the bigger more badder kids. That’s why this match is perfect for me. Everyone has already counted me out and the match is still a few days away. I know I’ll be bringing a whole lot more then my A-game let’s see what Jonathon decides to bring.” “…And that's a wrap” Jimmy shouts as he puts the camera back down by his side. Before he leaves we exchange a handshake and he promises he’ll have the promo ready for me for tomorrow. Then he took off as I went back into the hotel and upstairs to my room. When I got there I couldn’t help but jump back into bed to catch up on those couple of hours of sleep that I missed. When I put my head down on the pillow it happened again. “I have nothing to offer but blood, toil, tears and sweat.” The phrase pounded through my head again as my dream from before seemed to be continuing. Although now I was laying down with the three turban wearing men seated around me. One of them held up a necklace full of what looked to be human teeth. The other man beside him held up a bracelet with a human tongue hanging off of it. The blood dripping off of it and still looking fresh. That’s when I put my hand beside my mouth and felt my own warm blood around it. I realized the teeth and tongue that the men held in their possession were mind. This can’t be happening I thought, this is all a dream and I’ll wake up any second now. But I didn’t I just laid in shock as the blood continued to trickle down down my mouth and drip off my chin. The three men just laughed and pointed, they really seemed to enjoy my suffering. Then bombs could be heard in the distance. The three men scattered as the bombs and sounds of tanks and helicopters came closer. I sat up still in shock about what had happened to me. Before I knew it I was surrounded by American soldiers. I tried to talk but I only yelped like a dog. I heard one soldier say to the other. “Look at that funny looking terrorist piece of shit. Shoot that Jihad right between the eyes” BANG!
