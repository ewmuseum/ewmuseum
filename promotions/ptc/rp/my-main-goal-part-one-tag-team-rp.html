Shawn Fox/Mark Devlin Seasoned Network Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 237 Location: Vintage Wrestling Posted: Wed Apr 09, 2003 8:34 am Post subject: My Main Goal: Part one (Tag Team Rp) .::Voice::. Shawn Fox is a proud member of Vintage Wrestling and The World Heavyweight Champion. Although Vintage left Primetime Central he still remains in both, continuing with his flawless ranting's. As most of you may know, Fox has the next couple of months off due to the Vintage Wrestling World Heavyweight Tournament. The winner of the tournament faces the man himself, Shawn Fox but in the mean time Shawn needs something to keep his skills toned up so he and Mark Blaze, another member of Vintage also a free agent of Primetime Central will face a numerous of fantastic superstars in order to get a number one Contendership for the Tag Team Titles of PTC. Shawn has did a tremendous amount of work within the past few months and he has shown a true amount of potential within the ring. He fears no one and no one fears him only because no one really knows him.... Within the PTC community, wrestlers from a various of Federation's taunt him and try to make him look like amateur along with the entire club of Vintage. It seems ever since Vintage Wrestling opened, everyone had been somewhat jealous, why? Maybe because the talent within Vintage beholds the talent within PTC itself there for causing the entire community to pile on top of us applying tons of pressure on our back bone trying to make us crumble and fall apart, piece by, piece. Although they did succeed, in a way.... They did what the set out to accomplish, they strived Vintage out of PTC therefore giving the wrestlers of the community there power back. Vintage received the network position faster than any other federation but soon after receiving it, they evaded the PTC Community. Although Shawn Fox remains within these grounds, he continues to strain to become the best and soon enough, the words of Tristan Belmont, Cyrus Skar, Annihilation, Chris Storm, and Chryste will soon be put to shame by his name...... .::Fade::. Here I was, seated within my glistering red limousine with its black, glimmering interior. A slightly tinted window separated me from he driver as we inched our way to the Aladdin Resort and Casino. It was the place in which I and my partner, Mark Blaze would be reposing for the Tag Team Tournament hosted by Primetime Central itself. As me and my driver continue to pace ourselves down the crowded highways, my cell bursted out with a loud uproar, rumbling calling out for me to answer. I searched the bottoms of my pocket's but there was no phone within. I then reached underneath my butt ox as I continued to desperately search for the ringing phone and with luck, I found it. Clinching firmly onto it I placed it within centimeters from my left ear "Speak!" "You haven't forgotten, have you?" along with the dozen's of other thoughts I wondered, wondered who this man was and what he was talking about. His voice seemed familiar to my ears but I just couldn't picture the person's face within my head and this annoyed me very much. "Who is this?" The man loud out a deep breath as if I should know who he was "You arrigant little fool..." he brought his hateful sentence to a halt "You remember being in the back of my car with a pair of handcuffs strapped firmly around your wrist as you as you continued to ask the same question every criminal does." he changes his tone as if he was me "What the hell, what have I done?" he switches back to his normal tone "Remember now?" The mans face appeared within my head, his name was Agent Davidson and we had a little deal, he'd keep his mouth shut about my secrets if I helped him out, it was a top secret mission in which acquired me to kill someone. Sure, killing someone wasn't a problem considering I was in jail for several years due to that but I was trying to change and as always it was impossible and as the saying goes, 'If you can't beat them, Join them' so I joined there little team of super heroes as I would call them. Trying to serve and protect the riotous world of human beings who do nothing but pollute the world with tiny fragments of trash. In was meaningless in my mind but then again, if it doesn't involve me it always is meaningless. Putting my thoughts to a halt, I replied "Agent Davidson, glad you called to remind me. I almost forgot." I laughed at the end of the sentence only to angry the man "Keep laughing, if you don't get this job done the truth behind you will be told across the world and spoiling your career withing the wrestling community." That bastard, he had me right in the palm of his hands and I couldn't do a damn thing about it "I'll get the damn job done, its what I do best, eh?" "It better be or else..." and in an instance I got the sound of an operator "If you'd like to make a call please hang up and dial again, if you need assistance hang up and dial the operator." it continue to repeat itself over and over again "Stupid bitch!" the furious words of mine released from my mouth as I directed them towards the women's voice but the reply from her was still the same "If you'd like to make a call please hang up and dial again, if you need assistance hang up and dial the operator." I let out another deep breath as I put the women's voice to an end. My fatigue was beginning to run low as well as my patients. I pushed a button, sliding the tinted window to the side allowing me to speak to my driver "Driver, how close are we?" "We like five minutes sir." he replied and I closed the window back to its original position. This was just enough time to get a few things lined out within my head for instance, My mission. Yes, that's right I have a mission and if you haven't already guessed my goal then your just plane out stupid but for all you feebleminded fools out there I'll go into great detail or some detail. My main goal is to assassinate a man everyone called, Nefarious; Infamous by way of being extremely wicked. The definition of his name explained his way of living very well, he killed adolescence just because he was annoyed by there voice's. He hanged women just because they wouldn't go on a date with him. He killed his entire family except for his father, the death of his father was brought by me. His father also lived the same way as his son but he just happened to mess with the wrong child, that's right, my child. He killed him right before my eyes..... .::Fade::. I found myself hiding behind a small crate in a warehouse and the sound of gunfire was heard going off in the background. It was a few hours after the meeting with my former boss who had threatened me with my kids life because I had failed to pay him the rest of his money in which I owed. I placed my back up against the crate and peaked out beyond it to see who was firing at me. I seen three of four men with 9mm guns aiming at me, waiting for me to make one wrong me so they could blow my head smooth off but I was smarter than them. I had a 9mm of my own in my hand and I seen a empty coke can laying on the ground, I picked it up and tossed it to the other side of the room for a diversion, once the can was in site, there eyes followed the can along with the barrels of there guns. I used this as an advantage and fired five rounds at the four men. The first bullet fire pierced one mans torso, causing him to yell out in pain is he came in contact with the cold concrete. The second bullet fire, went through the skull of another man, killing him instantly. The third shot missed the third man in line but the fourth bullet pierces his torso as well and the last bullet I fired hit the arm of one man, causing him to lose grip of his gun. After all these shots were fired I found myself laying on the ground with the barrel pointed towards the fallen bodies, knowing one man still survived I rush to him to receive some answers. The man who was shot in the arm was panting. I approached the man and I could see the fear that tormented his soul. I then grabbed the mans neck and began asking questions "WHERE IS HE?!?!" the man looked at me and laughed "Its to late, Shawn. You done fucked up and now your son dies, whether you like it or not, HE DIES!" I balled up my fist and hit him one good time "If you don't tell me where he is, you'll be the one dieing!" blood began to gush out of his mouth from the last hit "There holding him at the warehouse on 54th Street." I released his neck and began walking away "But...." I stopped and turned around "you'll never save him!" the man began laughing. My body filled with anger and without thinking I fired another shot, killing the man. .::Flash::. I now found myself on 54th street, in search of my lost son. His life was in severe danger, all because of my mistakes. My mind was focused on saving him, I was determined to save him but the question was, could I? I didn't have a clue but I was soon to find out. I crept along the sidewalks of the silent night. Street lights caused small circles of lighting on the streets and where there was no street lights, it was nothing but pure darkness and in the darkness is where I lurked. I walked silently and cautionly to the warehouse on 54th street, avoiding anyone in site. My goal was to rescue my son and kill the man behind it all, Reese Flowers. As I came within yards of my destination, I seen guards, heavenly armed with a various of weapons. It seemed impossible to make it through there alive but I had no choice, I had to rescue my son. I searched for several minutes but still, couldn't find another possible route to enter the building. I knew what I had to do next, it was the only thing left to do. I walked into a spotlight caused by one of the street lights and put everyone's attention towards me 'Hey, you sorry bastards!' it was the only thing I could come up with at the time but it did its job. All the security guards quickly turn in my direction, surprised. Knowing who I was, they quickly raised there weapons, pointing the barrel in my direction 'Hold your hands up and don't move!' I couldn't make out all the figure due to the darkness that gave them stealth but I could see the total number of figures. Two slowly walked towards me as the other three covered them. The two slowly confronted me, one stopped in front of me putting the head of his pistol against my skull as the other slowly walked to my back side 'Your done with Fox, its over. You should've never shown your face.' I looked into the eyes of the man that confronted me as I begin to laugh with an evil tone 'You have no idea, boy.' he took a few steps back as the man behind me began tying my hands together with some plastic straps 'Ok, lets go!' the man in front waived with the guy still pointed towards my skull as we began progressing towards the warehouse. I now stood at the entrance of my future. Something bad was bound to happen and I was soon to find out what. The door opened as I was the first person through 'Welcome, Mr. Fox' as soon as I entered, the voice of Reese Flowers soared through the air, connecting with my ear drums 'Glad to see you could make it, I was beginning to wonder if you was going to leave your son out to dry.' I raged with fury as I began to run towards him but before I could get away something pounded up against the back of my head 'Urggg...' I moaned as my vision begin to get blurry, everything in my site was blurred, even the little boy that dangled from the ceiling . .::Fade::. "Sir, we're here!" The mans voice brought my horrible flashbacks to a complete stop. I looked out the side window from where I sat a notice hundreds of wrestling fans behind a pair of red leather ropes on each sides of the walk way. They all cheered as I escaped from the limo. I situated my black polo shirt; until I felt comfortable, in which was tucked into my black khaki pants as I smiled from head to toe. Nodding to the fans and evading there hands I began walking towards the entrance and the figure of Mark Blaze could be seen from a distance within the buiding..... .::To Be Continued::. _________________ Ranked Numer 3rd in PTC Vintage Wrestling World Champion Also Mark Devlin of LOW
