29 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
29 June 2006 The scenery illuminates into a pale grey hallway severely lacking in color and life. The white ceramic tiles are the only things that prevent the pastel grey from engulfing the entire backdrop. A slight screeching is heard as a nearby door is pushed opened and a young stud in his mid twenties with a toned-body, tanned, and murderous blue eyes steps out locking the last notch on his belt. A woman’s voice from inside the room cries out, “Hey wait…what’s your name?” The young stud turns around in the middle of the hallway and looks over at the woman he was just with. An extra large Philadelphia Phillies Bobby Abreu jersey covers her otherwise nude body. Her overblown jersey hides her otherwise mundane features. Her hair is blonde, her skin light-tanned; her features seldom distinguish her from other woman. The young stud’s look of dismay is clear evidence that he’s had better. She was boring. She was dull. Her name’s probably heather. “Bye,” the man tells her. “Wait, you never told me your name,” the woman persists. “I’ll tell you when I call you.” “Really?” her eyes lit up, not accustomed to any attention after the bump n grind. “Yeah,” the man replies, hiding his disdain. “I promise I’ll call you.” Scene fades out. A few months later, the girl we can only assume is called Heather for her indistinctive features, calls the man after months of stalking. “Who is this?” the man answers the phone. “Heather,” she replied. “You never called.” “I meant to,” the man lies. “I was just busy.” “I’m pregnant.” “And…” “I told my boyfriend it’s his.” “And…” “You asshole!” She slams the phone down and the scenery fades back out. Scenery illuminates gently into the present day. The young stud steps out of the hallway in his house and walks over to the front door. He greets a young brunette at the door in his shorts and sandals. He looks her over, approvingly. “I’m sorry Mr. Moraco,” the brunette apologizes. “Is this a bad time for you?” “It actually is,” Sebastian tells her. He hands her a business card. “Here, give me a call later and I’ll schedule a time to bang that ass into oblivion.” He tilts his head a little bit, envisioning her without her slacks. “I’m sorry?” the brunette asks, dumbfounded. “You’re not here for…never mind.” The brunette clears her throat. “I was just interviewing people on Archibald MacGregor and I wanted to see if I could get your opinion.” “Oh him,” Sebastian mutters in disgust. “Why the disdain?” “Oh, I have no problems with Archibold.” “It’s Archibald.” “That’s what I said.” “No, you said Archibold, with an “o”. “Right.” “It’s okay,” the brunette, tells him, flashing an index card before him. “I have to use this index cards to remember how to spell his name myself.” Sebastian blinks. “Well, do you want my opinion?” “Yes,” the brunette exclaims. Sebastian takes the microphone and, possessing the omniscient powers that all wrestling camera crews possess, they all miraculously appear in front of Sebastian’s home ready to record the promo. Sebastian takes a moment to catch his breath and to wait for the audience in their own personal homes to stop cheering. “I’m not worried about any man that no one cares enough to spell his name right. What the Hell have you done, MacGreggor? You’re sporting that Extreme title like it means something. How long was the title in hiatus? Exactly. And who did you beat to get it? Oh that’s right, an overrated, aging, self-deluded person who thinks he’s better than he really is. And to add to that, you had another man in your match disqualified for cheating = using steroids. I’m supposed to be impressed by that? What exactly are these people thinking that you’re some kind of unbeatable juggernaut? You prey on opportunity. It’s as simple as that. Let’s look at the facts.” Sebastian pauses to show his hand to camera as he begins to count down many of Archibald’s flaws. Unfortunately, he only has two hands. “You’re never around when the going gets tough. You only show up in small glimpses of competition when the competition is a watered down version of mid-carders. James von Doom, Hoyt Williams…who are these people? And let’s not forget that the other plaque of success you have, the CW2, is marred with people who refused to even show up and fight each other, much less you. Rayo Azul II? He would have wiped the floor with you if he wasn't getting sloppy seconds from your mother. Then you had the audacity to let a woman - Tessa Windsor - do most of the fighting in the finals. And even when she managed to knock down Cereal Killer long enough for you to steal a pin, it STILL wasn’t enough. Give it up Archibold. You’re nothing but a glorified opportunist. Now some people might say ‘but he’s willing to face all challengers in the next PTC card’. Okay, let’s run down whom they are. Red Rock. Congratulations, you’re fighting a guy probably making his first PTC debut. I could be wrong, but I don’t care. Tigera. What in the world? Are you addicted to drawing the scraps from the bottom dwelling feds? Karina Wolfenden. Oh, yes. You draw a multi-colored daredevil whose record in competition is a lot worse than yours. Yeah, everyone loves her in her home fed. But where is her success in PTC? Every time the going gets tough, she bails out. Just like you, MacGregor. You two should get together and have a baby…unless you think Heather had YOUR baby?” Several gasps escape from some of the technical crew present. They are immediately shot dead for interruption. “Let’s continue shall we? Vivica J Valentine. The Black Mariah. I bet she could beat you just by singing. Kyo Kushinaga? I have never seen someone with so much promise and hype have so many failures since the XFL. You’re salivating at facing him, aren’t you MacGregor? You can’t wait to beat this over-hyped wrestler and then lay claim that you’re unbeatable. And finally, we have Cereal Killer, finally a person with some kind of success. So of all the people that could face you, you only draw one person actually decent enough for me to give a damn about? Congratulations on drawing a lot of over-glorified midcarders.” Sebastian keeps up with tradition by slamming the microphone to the crowd and his theme song blasts across the neighborhood on cue. ---- 1 July 2006 14 Real, Actually True, Facts! Sebastian is seen slouched on his couch in a state of despair and boredom that is usually only associated with reading a Dan Brown novel. A quick scan of the area reveals that no such poorly written pieces are in the vicinity, so Sebastian’s boredom must be from somewhere else. “I’m bored!” Sebastian cries out. A pigeon taking flight off the windowsill is the only abruption. Sebastian blinks and sits up straight. He realizes that now is a good time as any to conduct a promo. “This is a great time to conduct a promo!” he exclaims. He quickly gets up and walks towards the front door because he is already dressed – he’s awesome like that. The opened door reveals a young blonde in her early twenties wearing a white tank top and a very short skirt. She has high heels to boot and proudly displays her breasts with exuberance. “Why you must be the random girl,” Sebastian says. The girl – named heather as any random girl in a storyline or promo is usually called – nods automatically. “I was just picking up some groceries when I got transported here. You must be a wrestler.” Indeed, Sebastian is a wrestler. And like all wrestlers, he possesses the uncanny ability to make random girls appear out of thin air and fall in love with him. “Now that you’re here I can continue with my random promo,” Sebastian winks at her. Heather peers into the room. With a seductive smirk she replies, “I bet the bedroom is pretty big.” Scene fades. Ten hours later – because Sebastian is such a stud – he and Heather walk back outside. Heather walks with a slight limp – she’s bruised. “I can’t believe you’re still hard after all that” she exhales. Sebastian nods. “It’s part of the job requirement. All wrestlers are required to have large cocks and exceptional stamina.” “Your parents must be proud.” “Actually, I wouldn’t know. I killed them. That’s another part of the job requirement.” “I see…” “I’m done with you,” Sebastian remarks with a flick of his wrist. In an instant, Heather is blinked out of existence as quickly and as randomly as she first appeared. Having drained the pipes, sowed his oats, and freed his seeds Sebastian walks back into his huge mansion – courtesy of a regular wrestler’s salary. He picks up a book from the coffee table. Sebastian turns to face the camera that was always there. “Some people have been complaining to my agents that I was wrong in my comments about some of their favorite wrestlers and also about Mr. Archibald MacGregor.” Sebastian nods understandingly. “I can understand that one or two of his dozen fans might be upset. That is why I decided to prove to you that my statements about Archibald being a sorry, underrated, glorified opportunist who preys on decorated, aging mid-carders is no lie. I have proof!” He flashes the book in front of the camera. The book is titled 101 Real Facts About Real PTC Wrestlers. *Ahem* “Fact 14. Once, Tigera went to a blood and sperm bank to donate blood. After learning that she gets triple the amount of tips from donating semen than blood, she returned the next day with a mouth full of… Fact 13. The FUSE wrestling federation hired Tigera as an act of affirmative action. They figured she could clean and cook, or at least sew her own sneakers. Fact 12. Josh Young – better known as Red Rock – is actually an Englishman who wishes he were Irish. Enough said. Fact 11. So determined was Red Rock to become Irish that at the St. Patrick’s Day parade, he dressed up in queer outfits and nearly got a complete sex change after injecting himself with estrogen. Sources close to Red Rock were quoted as saying, “Sex change? No that’s how he actually looks.” Fact 10. During their RPW stint, Magnum Wolf was so disappointed with Karina Wolfenden’s blotched spots that his contract was reworked to include incentives. Apparently the blowjobs Karina gave to him after each match, as an apology was not part of the deal. Fact 9. Karina Wolfenden once jobbed to a wrestler with the name Wolf in his name. That fiasco was so abysmal that it is now the standard for crappiness and anything Karina ever accomplishes will be forgotten. Fact 8. Vivica J. Valentine is such a shit stain to the wrestling community that she only received one spot in this book. She’s a virgin who secretly masturbates to pictures of PTC president, Argyle. Sources at GCW were quoted as saying, “Well this does explain why all her moves are kicks that involving doing splits in mid air. It’s like she’s trying to advertise something.” Fact 7. Kyo Kushinaga has now wrestled in the 4WW and Global wrestling federations. After having finished reading that, children everywhere start crying. Fact 6. The XUW federation refuses to admit to having employed Cereal Killer. Therefore, all trace accounts regarding Cereal Killer have been wiped clean off the XUW records. When questioned as to why they would deny Cereal Killer, an XUW official was quoted as saying, “We are a proud and storied federation. We will not have hired Cereal Killer. Even a blind, dead child wouldn’t do it.” When informed that Cereal Killer won CW2, the official replied, “Yeah, against a midget bimbo and Archibald MacGregor.” Fact 5. The reason Jack Nobbeldy keeps Cereal Killer close is because sheep are too obvious. Fact 4. Archibald shit his pants when he heard rumors that Sebastian Moraco might enter GTT5. Moraco never did, but it was not enough to convince Archibald who fled the continent. In his place, a new fellow named Spartan appeared and proceeded to show us new levels of suckitude. Legend has it that only Archibald can surpass it. Fact 3. Archibald uses a penis pumper. Currently, he aspires to be Asian. Fact 2. After seeing God be successful in creating humans, the Devil sought to emulate him. His creations were a failure and he decided to wipe his ass with them. The dingle-berries that were left on the floor sprouted to the gene line known as the MacGregors. Fact 1. Every night before he goes to bed, Archibald checks his closet for the boogeyman. Rumor has it, the boogeyman is actually Sebastian Moraco.” Sebastian Moraco slams the almanac tight with his hands. “I could go on about you Mr. MacGregor. Apparently, there is a whole chapter on your failures and shortcomings. You aren’t even a wrestler for crying out loud. You have no moves. You have no evidence at all to support your claims as a decent wrestler, let alone a perceived great one. You think beating Hoyt Williams was a big feat? Aimz could have done that. But now you inexplicably have that extreme title I could care for less. I’m not worried about the extreme title. I don’t revere it. I don’t covet it. It means nothing to me. I came to this sport for the competition, not for the flashy, gimmicky domains of an extreme title match. I also didn’t come to see people like you feign off their accomplishments as great. You don’t deserve the extreme title. You’re merely a pathetic opportunist who preys on jobbers like James von Doom, Jason Spire, and Hoyt Williams. Don’t get me wrong. I don’t want the title. I just find it nauseating that a person like you can actually possess something that translates to success. You don’t deserve an accolade of any status, period. That’s all I really want to do. I want to take that extreme title away from you, because someone else deserves it more. That someone is me. I hope you’ve enjoyed your little false reign as extreme champion, Archibald. I hope you’ve prayed to whatever gods you believe in, because at the next PTC event you will be a lamb being led to a slaughter amongst the wolves. I’m taking that extreme title away from you and in the process I will give PTC something to be proud of and I will rid the federation of your overrated, ugly, no moves, can’t wrestle, beats on women, beats jobbers, depends on von Doom (a person who somehow thinks he’s still in the NWC) for a victory, ass. This is the end of the line for you, Archibald. See you at the mainevent.” Scene fades to red.
