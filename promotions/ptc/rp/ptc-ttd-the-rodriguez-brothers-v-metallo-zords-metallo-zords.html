TTD - The Rodriguez Brothers -v- Metallo Zords

12 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Battle Begins ::The screen fills with a black sky sparsely speckled with white stars on the backdrop. The scene of outer space is calm and silent, but that is soon interrupted by the sound of rolling drums. A classical march of royal sounding origins plays loudly now as rolling yellow text begins scrolling from the bottom of the screen and moving up, slowly creeping farther away like the text at the beginning of a Star Wars movie. The text continues to scroll, as a deep powerful voice speaks the words as they work their way up the screen.:: Voice- The Metallo Zords are a mysterious pair of intergalactic superheros who have been given the task of defending the earth from all menaces that may threaten it. Week after week they battle the forces of the evil Queen Mogombu and her nefarious husband King Nanahaboomboom, a ruthless couple bent on world domination, in a battle that may very well decide the fate of the entire earth. Even with all of this responsibility, the Zords still try their best to carry on a normal career as professional wrestlers for the Lights Out Wrestling promotion of the Primetime Central network. You are about to witness an account of their trials and tribulations. Watch on and now and witness the phenomenon that is the . . . Metallo Zords! ::We open in the control room of the Metallo Zords in which the Gold Ranger is busily pounding away at a large red heavy bag that is attached to the ceiling by a long silver chain. The Silver Ranger kicks and punches at the bag in an almost unsettling synchronicity with the hum of electricity as it courses through the various computers and machinery located throughout the metallic room. Suddenly a portal like door slides open as the Gold Ranger runs into the room in a very excited fashion as he waves a telegram above his head.:: Gold Ranger- We are in! We made it in! ::The voice of his partner breaks the concentration of the Silver ranger as he turns from the bag and begins to question the Gold Ranger’s excitement.:: Silver Ranger- Wait, just calm down for a second. What did we make it in to? ::From his body language, we can tell that the Gold Ranger can barely contain himself. Quickly he holds up the telegram and begins pointing at the writing on it as he begins to explain.:: Gold Ranger- We made it into the Primetime Central Tag Team Division! ::Now fully understanding his partner’s excitement, the Silver Ranger grabs onto the Gold Ranger as he hugs him and begins jumping up and down. All of the noise coming from the Metallo Zords seems to gain the attention of another of the command center’s occupants. A large translucent cylindrical tube situated at the center of the room fills with the familiar blue face of Bobbabu, the mentor of the Metallos.:: Bobbalu- What is all of the ruckus you two? ::The Metallos immediately stop their celebrating as they turn to face Bobbalu. The Silver Ranger is the first to begin explaining the situation.:: Silver Ranger- Sorry if we woke you Bobbalu, it is just that we are a little excited. You see, we entered our names into the Primetime Central Tag Team division and we have just found out that we were accepted. We just are so excited and cannot believe that it is true! ::The gigantic face of Bobbalu fills with an even bigger smile as he responds.:: Bobbalu- Well I can see then that there is cause to celebrate. It makes me very proud to see you guys finally making some headway with your professional wrestling career. As much as I hate to bring you down though, you must not forget that your duties as intergalactic superheros must be put first. You cannot be preoccupied with basking in a championship hue. ::The Metallo Zords nod their heads in agreement as the Gold Ranger reassures his mentor that they have their priorities straight.:: Gold Ranger- Fear not, we swore that the safety of earth would always be put before our personal goals no matter how few. ::Bobbalu nods in acceptance of the Metallo Zords assurances. The Silver Ranger now begins to speak.:: Silver Ranger- However for the time being, let us party heartily! Come and let us go to the Community Center Gold Ranger so that we can inform our friends of this joyous news. ::After the Gold Ranger nods to the Silver Ranger in agreement, both Metallo Zords widen their stance and grab onto their belt buckles. They are instantly engulfed in a gold and silver beam respectively and transported out of the command center. As the Metallo Zords head for the Community Center to tell their friends of their acceptance into the PTC Tag Team division, the scene fades out.:: ********************************MEANWHILE******************************** ::The scene now reopens in the dungeon like throne room of the evil arch nemesis of the Metallo Zords, Queen Mogombu’s moon palace. The light from the moon that pours through the balcony door reflects off of the cool smooth surface of the marble floor causing the room’s jagged rock walls to illuminate with an almost supernatural blue glow. The picture slowly pans to right, weaving it’s way through a slight fog which hangs in the air giving the entire scene a dreamlike feel. The picture finally settles on a view of the evil Queen Mogombu as she stares into a green glowing orb. She furiously raises her glare from the globe as she exclaims.:: Queen Mogombu- No! Why do those metallic morons always seem to luck out in every given situation!? ::Hearing the frazzled exclamation of his wife, the nefarious King Nanahaboomboom rushes into the throne room through an open doorway.:: King Nanahaboomboom- What is all the fussy wussy about honey buns? ::Queen Mogombu looks to her husband who she notices has some type of green gunk covering his usually yellowish skull like facial features. As she looks at her husband in confusion, Queen Mogombu’s anger subsides for a moment as she questions him.:: Queen Mogombu- What exactly is that stuff? Have you been ordering products from those infomercials again? ::King Nanahaboomboom pauses for a moment as he tries to decipher what his wife is talking about. Once he realizes that she means the substance on his face, he quickly begins wiping his face off with his hand as he responds.:: King Nanahaboomboom- Oh, you mean this . . . no this is just some guacamole that I whipped up earlier. That Fab Five from Queer Eye for the Straight Guy said it really helps with dried out skin. Anyway, what was all that yelling about? ::As King Nanahaboomboom continues to clear his face of all traces of the guacamole, Queen Mogombu quickly regains her focus and the anger that goes along with it. She begins to explain.:: Queen Mogombu- It’s those metallic menaces again! They’ve been accepted into the Primetime Central Tag Team division. It’s not bad enough that they have unlimited success as intergalactic superheros, but now they have a chance to make a name for themselves wrestling too? It’s just not fair, when do we get a chance to win? ::Queen Mogombu’s spirits now seem to sink as she sulks in the unsaid answers to her questions. Seeing this King Nanahaboomboom calmly walks over to his wife and throws his arm around her in a consoling fashion.:: King Nanahaboomboom- It’s ok my snookie wookie. You’ll get those Metallos sooner or later, you just have to be patient. ::Queen Mogombu’s anger now returns at an even higher level due to her husband’s suggestion.:: Queen Mogombu- Patient!? You’re telling me to be patient!? How dare you even suggest patience to me?! I have been nothing but patient! It is time for those Metallo Zords to lose! If I cannot beat them in intergalactic warfare, then I will beat them in the sport that they love oh so much! ::King Nanahaboomboom looks at his wife in complete and utter confusion as he questions her statement.:: King Nanahaboomboom- I don’t understand. What are you talking about? You want to become a woman wrestler? ::King Nanahaboomboom’s demeanor quickly changes as a large smile crosses his face.:: King Nanahaboomboom- Are you going to wrestle other women with like oil and stuff? I can deal with that, maybe I’ll even join in from time to time. ::Queen Mogombu suddenly snaps back at her husband in response to his idiotic questions.:: Queen Mogombu- No you blithering perverted fool! I have a plan that is much more sinister in mind, one that will destroy the chances of the Metallo Zords winning the PTC tag team titles for good! ::Queen Mogombu and King Nanahaboomboom now tilt their heads back in a villainous laugh as the scene fades out. What will this mysterious threat mean to the future of the Metallo Zords? Will the Metallo Zords be able to focus on the Primetime Central Tag Team division of will their attention be needed elsewhere? Will Queen Mogombu and King Nanahaboomboom finally derail the Metallo Zords career? Does guacamole really help to combat dry skin? Only time will tell, time and the . . . Metallo Zords!::
