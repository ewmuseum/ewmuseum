Misunderstanding

22 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-22 Steve Watson had never even considered wrestling with valet at ringside before. And, as he walked down the ramp for his 3rd round GTT6 match against Tigera, he wasn’t sure he liked the concept of it right now either. But there she was, "The Mysterious Miss M", matching him stride for stride – whether he liked it or not - as he made his way down to the ring. To be honest, Steve knew he was in no position to complain about anything. Heck, he felt lucky just to be headed to the ring at all. Especially given where he had been sitting just a few days prior when the prospect of ending the week in a body-bag seemed significantly more likely than that of ending it in the ring. But then again, there were still a few hours left until midnight. * * * To say Steve had been having a crappy few weeks would probably have been the epitome of understatement. He had not seen his home or his wife in close to ten days and – given the circumstance – he could not safely say when he might see them again. Lisa Somers had taken Steve directly from the Village Café to the expansive estate of her uncle Nobert Bagley. Steve had been there once before when Old Man Bagley had, for some reason, deigned to host the company Christmas Party there several years ago. Ed Dannon had insisted on an open bar – which apparently resulted in an antique clock sustaining some heavy damage at some point shortly after midnight – and the parties have been held at the Radisson ever since. Steve remembered being impressed with Bagley’s calm and composure in the face of the clock calamity back then. And, due to the fact of neither of those qualities being in evidence in the old man this time around, Steve had to assume that what he had stumbled into was worth far, far more than some antiquated timepiece. "Do you think I am stupid, boy?" Bagley hissed the moment that Steve was escorted into his presence. Steve looked around the elegant, old manor library; but it was the three burly "enforcers" stationed around the room, rather than the old tomes and ornate woodwork, that caught his eye. One was stationed next to where Bagley sat "enthroned" behind a large table, the second stood near the windows along the east wall, and the third was presently moving into place behind him and Lisa to block them off from the door. Steve knew then that flight as probably not an option. "Do you really think," Bagley continued< "that I got to where I am by being a stone cold imbecile?" "I…I am sorry, sir," Steve stammered, thinking it best to maintain a certain formality, "But I really don’t know what you are referring to." The man near the window started to close in on Steve, until Bagley pulled him back with a gesture. "Don’t you?" Bagley said as he arose from the table, "For some reason I think you know exactly what I am referring to." Bagley walked around the table and got right up into Steve’s face. Steve thought bout how easy it might be to snap the old man’s neck right then and there; but was almost immediately shocked that he would even consider such a thing. And not just because he would have immediately been beaten to death by Bagley’s three thugs. "Listen Watson, the game is up. We are on to you," said Bagley, "We know exactly what you have been doing." "Oh," replied Watson, "Would you be so kind as to fill me in then. Because this whole damn thing is quickly becoming a complete and total mindf**k to me." Steve quickly found his body hurled to the ground via a body check from one of the thugs. "Please, Steve," Lisa chimed in, "Just answer him. There is no reason to make things any worse than they have to be." Steve had to admit, as annoying as Lisa could be, there was a lot of sense to what she was saying. He didn’t have anything to hide here. "Look, Mr. Bagley," said Steve, "I found a few odd discrepancies when I was going through the expenses last month. I’ve been following up on them. That’s my job. And, to be completely honest, I am still not at all sure what it is that I ran into." "Please don’t lie to me, Steve." said Bagley, "The only think I like less than extortionists is liars." Two of the thugs hauled Steve up by his arms and flung him into the table. Steve gasped to recover his breath - feeling lucky none of his ribs had cracked. "I swear, sir," groaned Steve, "You can ask Mr. Hainesworth about what I know. It is really not much." "That’s strange," replied Bagley, "Frank Hainesworth is the one that informed me of your demands." "Demands…?" But before Steve could get any further two of the thugs were upon him again. They held him there as Bagley grabbed his chin. "You really do think I am doddering old fool, don’t you, Watson?" said Watson, "I didn’t become the kind of success that I am doling out millions of dollars to every single two-bit blackmailer that shows up at my door." "Hainesworth told you I wanted millions of dollars?" "Hainesworth told me everything," replied Bagley, "Hell, boy, I figured you’d have been smart enough to back off after your accomplice’s unfortunate motor vehicle ‘accident’, but I guess you just thought it dumb luck just telling you that you didn’t have to split the money." "Accident?" Steve was stunned, "You’re talking about Tim Forbes?" "Who else would I be talking about? Do you have other accomplices?" "No…I…" "Did you really think you were too slick for us to catch you ducking out to Denver at the last minute and then slipping back into town under cover of night?" asked Bagley. "Look, Steve," Lisa spoke up again, "Everybody knows about all your sneaking around. Skipping out early. Disappearing for hours at a time." "I am not a man who likes being played, Steve", Bagley finished as he let go of Steve’s chin and the thugs dropped him to the floor. Steve’s mind raced. This was not a good situation. Bagley was obviously in no mood to hear him out, but the looks on the faces of the three thugs told him that he might not have a lot more time to plead his case. Steve decided to take a gamble. What he was about to say might strike home with Bagley, or it might just hasten his end. "No offense, sir. But I don’t think I am the one who has been playing you. And I don’t think you are the only one being played." Bagley turned back to look at him. His eyes flared like red hot pokers. "Sir," said Steve, "How much do you know about GTT6?" The blank looks on both Bagley’s and Lisa’s faces gave Steve all the answer he needed. Hainesworth’s disclosure had obviously not been as complete as Bagley had been lead to believe. Steve then quickly recounted everything he could about his secret life as a masked wrestler – from the day he first signed up on a lark at Larry Sharpe’s Monster Factory in Bellmawr, New Jersey all the way to his clandestine flight back from Denver to face "The New Shit" Bobino and subsequent rescue from the police in Hainesworth’s limo. Steve didn’t leave out a single detail in the hopes that the complexity of his tale would reinforce its veracity. Even so, Bagley remained skeptical – especially of Frank Hainesworth’s implied undisclosed foreknowledge of Watson’s double life. There was no way that his trusted right hand would have held back such vital and useful information about one of their potential nemeses. But, if it was true, perhaps Hainesworth was not to be as trusted as Bagley had presumed. "That’s a thrilling story there, Steve," said Bagley, "But why exactly should I take the word of a man trying to bilk me out of fourteen million over a member of my own Board?" "But Uncle Norbert," inserted Lisa, "that would explain why Frank wanted Len to where that one specific mask for the Forbes hit." It seemed to Steve that this struck a cord with Bagley and he was giving it some thought. "But still," said Bagley, "this whole wrestling accountant thing seems rather fanciful…" Steve saw his ray of hope, "Look. Let me prove it to you then." "And how do you plan do you that?" ask Bagley. "He has a match scheduled for GTT6 this weekend," Lisa guessed, "Don’t you, Steve?" "No," said Bagley, "No way." "Don’t worry, Uncle Nobert," said Lisa, "I’ll escort him there. It’ll be safe. He won’t try anything tricky with me." "How do you know that?" said Bagley. "Because he loves his wife." Steve wheeled around and stared at Lisa. "Oh come now, Steve," said Lisa, "You don’t really think Frank actually sent her away to stay with her folks, do you?" "If you people…" "We haven’t done anything, Steve," replied Lisa, "and I am sure we won’t have to either. Just so long as you prove yourself to my uncle in the center of that ring this Sunday." * * * And speaking of that ring; that was exactly where Steve was in the process of getting his ass kicked by the FUSE-W Universal Champion, Tigera. Steve’s mind kept flashing to Elise and wondering about her safety – realizing full well that it was this exact lack of focus that could be jeopardizing it. But regardless, that wee bit of self-awareness did little to change the fact that – with 3 near falls in the last 5 minutes – Tigera had been whipping him from pillar to post for almost the entire match and Steve needed to do something, anything, to reverse the flow. Steve tried to concentrate. He tried to get his focus fully into the match. But it wasn’t there. Not tonight. And Lisa Somers’ constant carping from the ring apron didn’t help much either. Steve noted that the mask over her head did nothing to mute the grating effects of her voice on him. But finally it was that exact same voice that made him snap to. To block it out, he gritted his teeth closed off his mind to all outside noise – both real and metaphysical – and just went into full wrestling autopilot. From out of nowhere, he reversed and Irish Whip and sent Tigera hurtling into the ropes right where "The Mysterious Miss M" stood in a move that sent Lisa toppling to the floor and the FUSE Champ rebounding right back into a Tilt-a-Whirl Power slam. Steve realized halfway through the move that something was wrong, Tigera’s form had gone limp before they even hit the mat and there was not even a twitch of a kickout attempt as the ref counted to three. Steve had pulled off the biggest upset of his career. And he felt horrible about it. "Miss M" was into the ring almost as soon as the ref raised Steve’s hand and leaping up into his arms for a victory hug. "What did you do to her?" Steve said as he dropped Lisa to the mat. "Yeah! It was awesome, wasn’t it?" "No," Steve said as he rolled out of the ring and Lisa followed, "It wasn’t. I’ll ask again, what did you do to her?" "What do you care? You won, didn’t you?" came the slightly perturbed response, "And it’s not like you didn’t need it." Steve glanced around until he saw the small single-shot taser unit lying of the floor at ringside where Lisa had fallen. "Look," Lisa continued as the walked up the ramp. "I was watching that Ember guy that won earlier tonight too, so if you need me to…" "I don’t need anything from you. I don’t win my matches like that," said Steve, "If I am going to take pride in a victory, dammit, I am going to earn it." "Ha!" laugh Lisa derisively, "The only thing you were earning in that ring tonight was a direct ticket to my uncle’s bad side. I mean, you hardly looked like a ‘Professional’ in there at all, if you ask me." "I didn’t ask," said Steve through gritted teeth as they passed through the curtain and into the back. As soon as they were safely in back and away from public eyes, Lisa grabbed Steve by the shoulders and planted a kiss right on his mouth. After a moment’s shock, Steve shoved her away - landing her on the floor, backside first. "What the…?" exclaimed Steve. "I’ve seen how you look at me around the office, "said Lisa, "You know you want it." "You’re out of your mind." "Look, Watson, I don’t think you know who you are messing with here" "Let me give it a shot," said Steve, "A spoiled brat with a rich, but crooked, uncle? Am I at least close?" Lisa snarled at Steve’s mockery. "I am not your plaything, girl," Steve finished. Lisa rose from the ground and brushed herself off, "You have no idea what I am capable of." "What?" said Steve, "Do you have another taser tucked into your tights there?" "No." said Lisa, "I have a cell phone. And all I have to do is call my uncle on it to tell him about your ‘escape attempt’ and the men keeping an eye on your wife will immediately be put into action." "You’d never…" "Watch me," Lisa retorted. Steve glared daggers at Lisa. If he had never truly hated anyone before in his life, he did now. "Steve," Lisa said, softening a bit when she saw the cold steel in his eyes, "You don’t need her anymore. You have me. And with Hainesworth out, my uncle is going to need a new CFO." Steve saw what appeared to be tears welling up behind the eyeholes of Lisa’s mask. But he didn’t feel an instant’s pity for her. She was mad. "Mister Watson, Miss Somers; I am going to have to ask the two of you to come with us." It was now that Steve and Lisa realized that they were not alone. The speaker was a nice looking man in a dark blue suit. He and the nine men accompanying him all had their side arms out and pointed at the two masked characters. They were surrounded. Steve took one hate filled last look towards Lisa as the armed men started to herd them towards the fire exit.
