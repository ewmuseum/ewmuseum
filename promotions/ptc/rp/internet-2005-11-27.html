Date: 2005-11-27 Hype [hype] ~ noun 1. To stimulate. 2. A drug addict. Montiero begins to laugh. "Drug addict? What the…" Seated at his computer, Christian Montiero bashes the keyboard effortlessly. A fraction of a second after pressing the return key, he swivels his chair around. As the chair completes it’s 360* rotation, something shocks Montiero. He turns the chair another 180* and smiles innocently. "Pornography?" Rudolph Polenski, Montiero’s talent agent and managet, stands over Montiero and the computer with a stone cold glare on his face. "You told me you were researching Bryan Arson!" The tone of voice used indicates Polenski is not pleased. "Well, I decided I’d try and look up a couple of words first for their meanings." Montiero speaks confidently without pausing. "So, I went on ‘dictionary.com’." He sighs, believing he has completely worked around Polenski’s question. "Well, I need you to hurry up on that. We’ve got to be out of here by the end of the week." Polenski responds with a look of urgency. He always was one to obsess over deadlines. "Okay, I’ll be a minute." Montiero turns back to the computer. "Fine. But seriously, what are you doing on porn? You never told me." Polenski seems intrigued as to what response Montiero can muster. "See…" Montiero starts. "I heard something from a fan about Bryan Arson being a, and I quote, ‘penis’. So with this information in mind, I thought it was essential to search for that." "But you’re on LESBIAN porn!" Polenski responds. Montiero’s face turns white. "Am I?" Many people believe Christian Montiero can take out Bryan Arson, that our rising star has a chance against one of Prime Time Central’s biggest prospects in what looks set to be an explosive Round 1 bout. But in reality… "I’ll leave you to get on with it then. If you think looking at lesbians will give you an insight into the career of Bryan Arson, then go for it." Polenski seems content with his role-model speech, expecting Montiero to immeditately get to work on some real research work. "Okay, thanks Rudolph." Montiero smiles as he browses the page in more detail. Polenski bows his head in shame, leaving Montiero to his business as he returns to his packing. …his immaturity means he doesn’t. But, with that said, he can damn sure try. Montiero shiftily skims the apartment, slowly transferring his hand from the desk to his waistline. "And don’t think about masturbating!" Polenski shouts from the other room in what seems like a telepathic warning. Maybe…he can try…
