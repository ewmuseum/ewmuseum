The Chronicles of Muppet VII - Game On

21 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-21 "What the hell..." Rushing up the driveway to Muppet Manor, the Muppet Man couldn't quite believe his eyes. No, the house hadn't been completely demolished by one of King Muppet's genius plans. No, the house was perfectly fine, not even a scratch to be seen. Muppet didn't notice, though. He didn't even set eyes on the house itself. It was what was outside the house that bothered him so much. There wasn't a huge crowd of people, waiting for his arrival so they could hurl abuse at him. No, in fact, there wasn't anyone to be seen. There was just a large, white truck. The Muppet Man didn't want to look, he didn't want to read the big, black lettering on the side of the truck, but he couldn't stop himself. By the time he even had a chance to look away, to delay the inevitable, he had already read it. There it went. All of the confidence he had managed to scrape, to build up, disappeared in that one, horrible instant. All thoughts about GTT, Dr. Curiosity, WWA... hell, all of wrestling, were immediately catapulted out of his mind, as he read those words. 'Repo Van' Moving slowly, Muppet walked right past the van, totally ignoring it, and up the steps leading up to the house, still in a state of shock. It wasn't that he was having something of his, that he had earned himself, although he didn't know what, repossessed. It wasn't the fact that he was obviously in some sort of financial trouble that he hadn't been aware of previously. Actually, it was, partly. The fact that he hadn't known about it, that King Muppet, his best friend, had kept it from him. He had lied, trying to cover his tracks. He hadn't changed at all. The Muppet Man reached the unnecessarily large door to his home, Muppet Manor, arranged and paid for by King Muppet. His hand went out to clasp the handle, but something made him stop. He was so totally and utterly overcome by an emotion he hadn't felt strongly for a good, long, while. Anger. It was an emotion that had been so big a part of his life before, that now he detested it above all else. There he stood, motionless, not knowing what the hell to do. He was divided, torn, and despairing. Why... Muppet just couldn't work it out. Why had King spent all of this money? Actually, that didn't really matter. Maybe he had just got caught up in the heat of the moment, and gone a little overboard. That was understandable. But, then, he had continued to spend lavishly, as if nothing was wrong. Most of all, he had hidden it from the Muppet Man. He had pretended not only that were in a solid financial state, but they were considerably wealthy. He could have come out in the open, and they could have resolved it together. But that wasn't how King Muppet did things. He couldn't accept blame for anything. It made Muppet so angry, he could haved kicked that door in right there, walked in and beat his partner to a bloody pulp. He wasn't that kind of guy, though. Not anymore. He was past all of that, and he wanted to stay away from his less than friendly past as much as he could, at all costs. There was no way he was going back down that road, not as long as he lived. But... was King Muppet really giving him a choice? No. No, I can't think like that. Slowly letting his arm fall to his side, the Muppet Man turned, his eyes firmly shut. For a moment, he teetered on the top step, the final debate raging over in his mind. Finally, it subdued, and he began to walk down the steps, one at a time. What was he going to do? Where was he going to go? He had no idea, but he had to go... somewhere, anywhere. Taking one look back at his house, the Muppet Man walked away, dropping his bag without even really noticing. He didn't care, either way. Suddenly, wrestling no longer mattered. The WWA, the place he had spent that last year a bit of his life, his true home, was no longer of any concern. GTT4 and Dr. Curiosity were quickly fading specks on the horizon, so that he could barely make them out even if he chose to. The towering mountain was now no more than a pebble. I can't give up hope, but... What the hell am I going to do? ---- "I'm not going to take anything this time, Mr. Bobson, so count yourself lucky," the repo man said, bringing a smile of gratitude to the face of King Muppet. "Thanks, I can't tell you..." "Can it. You have two weeks to pay up, otherwise... you know what." "Yeah, yeah I do..." King said hesitantly. "Don't worry. You'll get your money." The man nodded, and they shook hands. King meant what he said, but he just didn't know if he could deliver. He would have to do some pretty drastic things to get the money within the two weeks. He wasn't afraid of that, though. Well, he wouldn't have been... if the Muppet Man wasn't coming back. He was overjoyed when he heard that his friend had defeated Horazon in round 3 of the GTT, not having enough time to watch the match on PTC television himself. He knew, though, that Muppet would be on his way back, and he had to do something fast. He hadn't. "I don't want to have to do this, you must understand that. I hate doing this, but... it's my job," the repo man continued, trying to scrape a little bit of respect. "Yeah, I understand," King replied, hiding the bitterness. I understand you, you fat son of a bitch. Get the fuck out of my house, you shit-faced cockmaster, not even being able to avoid rip offs in his own mind. "I'll be on my way, then. Remember... two weeks." "I remember, you PIECE OF SHIT! You'll get your FUCKING MONEY, now GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY HOUSE!" Heh. It had come out after all. The repo man backed off, not wanting to challenge King Muppet head on, King being a trained and seasoned wrestler himself. The man exited the house rather more loudly than he had intended, and nearly fell down the stairs on the way back to his van. Half way down, he shouted back up at King. "You're gonna pay for that. One week, now. Motherfucker..." Too busy muttering to himself, he didn't notice the discarded bag at the foot of the stairs, tripping over it and slamming face first into the concrete. King Muppet was too irate to laugh, instead he spat in the man's direction as he made his way to his feet. The man wiped the blood from his face, giving King the finger, before getting in his truck and making a noisy exit. King let out a breath he didn't know he had been holding. "Why me..." He moved forwards, taking a seat on the uppermost step, and gazing out into nothingness. Just as his mind was beginning to formulate some excuses for when the Muppet Man arrived back, he noticed the bag at the bottom of the steps that the repo man had tripped over. Getting up, he rushed down towards it, fearing the worst. Lifting up, he looked inside. There it was. Glaring up at him was the Muppet Man's WWA United States title. Suddenly, King realised that Muppet had already got back... which meant he had seen the repo van, and realised everything. King hadn't even had a chance to break it to him, he had learned it the hard way. Not only was King in deep shit, he realised, but Muppet was seriously demoralised... and that could just be the only way out of this financial hole down the toilet. "God damn it... I have to find him." ---- The Muppet Man sat, restless, on the park bench. It wasn't the most comfortable or pleasant of places to be sitting, freshly damp from the midday shower, and with the paint chipping off it here there and everything, but like with so many things in his life, it seemed, he just didn't care. He stared up at the blue sky above. A different shade of blue than you would normally expect, though, a particularly dark navy blue, with depressing grey clouds floating over from the horizon. Yet, it was blue all the same. For a few moments, Muppet was lost, simply staring up into the sky. When did everything get so hard? For as long as he could remember, the Muppet Man had been put through hell by everyone around him, by the great fates, and most of all, by himself. Really, he knew that. Really, he wasn't that naiive. After anything had gone wrong, he had been too weak, too depressed to fight back. He had succumbed to all of the temptations put forwards to him, each one dragging him further down, each one holding him back even more. And the whole time, he believed he was on the road to recovery. What happened to me? Why am I so weak, so pathetic? These were all questions that the Muppet Man had no answer to. Little did he know, he would have no further time to work out why, as everything was about to get a lot more complicated. Little did he know, things were about to get that much harder. King Muppet entered the park through the main gate, a towering mass of iron, expertly worked and sculpted. Normally, King took time to admire these things. Unlike most other people, he took pleasure in human crafts, and enjoyed art and such things. This time, though, he couldn't care less. His mind was too full of doubt, there were too many desperate excuses churning over inside his brain for him to think of anything else, even where he was going. A few times, he almost tripped over for no apparent reasons, getting a few awkward glances from passers by. But what did they know? Walking slowly, he kept a vigilant eye out for the Muppet Man. He made his way down that path, almost physically shaking. He could have delayed that moment for the rest of time. He could have continued walking down that path until he withered and died, if it meant missing this encounter. Of course, though, that wasn't going to happen. Sooner or later, he was going to have a lot of explaining to do. Sooner or later, he was going to encounter the Muppet Man. Sooner or later... he did. Maybe on a different night, in a different setting, the Muppet Man would have run up and torn King's head off. Maybe if King hadn't caught him in such a desperate mood, Muppet would have taken out all of his frustration on the only friend he had in the world, and lost him forever, condemning himself back into that pit of self loathing. Maybe he was just lucky that the hands of fate had decided to smile down on him for once. In any case, there they were, and it was by no means an easy situation. "Welcome back," King said, subdued and solemn. "It's good to be back," Muppet replied, still seated, smiling at the irony of his last words. King smiled back. They weren't happy smiles, though. More... smiles of acceptance. "I trust you've seen it all," King said. It was more of a question than anything, though he already knew the answer. Muppet's response came in the form of a slight nod. It was enough. "Well, then I guess there's nothing more to say." "Hmm... so this is the part where I fuck you up?" "It appears so. Well, let's get on with it then," King said almost jovially. They were past 'depressed'. Muppet got to his feet, and walked over to his manager. King closed his eyes, bracing himself. Nothing came. Slowly opening his eyes, King Muppet looked straight at the Muppet Man, with his hand outstretched. Breaking into the first real grin in over a week, King clasped his hand, and they shook... friends again. "I'm sorry, Andy," came his voice, dripping with genuine concern. He really meant it, and Muppet realised this. He didn't smile, though. He had more pressing matters to attend to, they both did. They had to pay off the debt, and fast. But together, he knew they could do it. "We better get this over and done with as quickly as possible, eh? I've got a match this weekend, and it's going to be the toughest match I've ever had to compete in," he said, remembering and thinking about the mammoth task ahead of him for the first time since he had arrived back. "Dr. Curiosity... despite what he may lead you to believe... is no joke. Trust me, I've seen him. Now that we're getting this settled, though, my mind will soon be at rest. I think... I think I can take him." King Muppet's face, though, remained decidedly cold, as he stared down at the ground. "Um... Will?" "They gave me a week to make the payments. I'll do all I can, but..." Suddenly, it dawned on the Muppet Man. He couldn't listen, he couldn't bear to hear it, but... much like earlier, he had no choice. "Andy, you have to win this match. If you lose... we lose the house." ---- Yeah. He broke it to me. Just as I was beginning to think I could maybe take this, just as I was beginning to believe in myself for the first time in who knows how long... Suddenly, things get a whole lot tougher. I'm not just fighting for myself, for my own personal satisfaction... I'm fighting for my existence, and I'm fighting for Will. I feel, if I lose this one, then... well, I don't know what. Fuck. Fuck, fuck, fuck... Why does this always happen to me? Why am I the one to suffer, when all of these other people can simply go about their daily lives without a real fucking care in the world? Why must I be continually tormented, with freedom dangled so enticingly over my head, only to be dragged even further away? It's all a game. It's all some sick, twisted, fucking game... But, I'll tell you what. I'm going to win this game. I'm sick and tired of being fucked around all my life. It's time to finally take things into my own hands, it's time for me to finally take control of my own damn life. That's it. It's over. All of my failures, all of the hardships, everything... it's all going to end. I'm stopping it, right here, right now. Why? Because I have no other choice. I'm going to win. I'm going to beat Doctor Curiosity. I'll find some way. I'll do whatever it takes. I have to...
