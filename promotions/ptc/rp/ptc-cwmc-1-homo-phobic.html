CWMC #1 Homo/Phobic

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, I appreciate that that most of you are spectators in this sport and that even avid fans sometimes miss a game or two, so let me recap. Almost a year ago now a great tournament was held in the legendary realm of PTC/WO. It was a contest of grand proportions that saw friend, foe and former lovers reuniting to battle adversaries from other Federations. After months of hard work, waiting, dedication, waiting, and tenacity topped with a little more waiting finally the victors were crowned. Homo/Phobic emerged as the undisputed Champions! It was a joyous occasion for many, and a nightmare come true for a reluctant partner, Shane Stone, one half of Homo/Phobic. Now however, they have a unified bond far stronger than anything ever before, stronger than the Gimp's zip locks, stronger than their previous marriage, stronger than even the silk scented scarves that they tie one another to the bed with. With two successful defences already behind them in the guise of vanquished foes, Vice Squad and X-Corp, they now move on, proving their worth, raising their game, each one with their own agenda that is far removed from simply defending their titles, for as Shane Stone tries tirelessly to escape his captors the Bum Boozler tries just as hard to complete their suicide pact and share their eternal afterlife together… forever… This is the continuing Saga of Homo/Phobic! I guess the rumour mill has been churning, I guess the fans have been scouring the net in search of any hint that might explain just why Homo/Phobic haven't yet shot a promo. Most of you probably think that like some other poor and unfortunate souls that they're still watching the 50000 hour long PTC Tag Title tenuous Black Lotus promos with a Bounty Hunter… but they're not that silly… or that interested. Some of you probably think that IMProductions didn't have the resources to allow BOTH Luca and the Boozler to shoot promos at the same time… and you might be right. [Possible crisis averted! … carry on.] The Bum Boozler, Shane Stone, Tom and the Gimp sit upon the floor, forming a tight little circle whilst looking around the room with odd expressions set upon their faces as they look around for the source of the uncharacteristic narration. The Boozler shrugs. Boozler: "These walls are so thin... I think PTC are still shooting those Trash Talk promotions next door." Tom nods in agreement. The Boozler reaches into the middle of the circle and scoops up some Farm Animal Snap Playing Cards, a Trademarked Product from Barnyard Wrestling© no less. Shane leans back in his cushion formed make shift chair, content and happy for once as he plays his third most favourite game in the world. Shane: "I'm leaning back in my cushion come make shift chair right now." Everyone looks at Shane, somewhat bewildered. Shane: "The feathered centre is soft, comfortable, yet firmly set into the curvature of my lower back to give me the support I need and prevent any stress upon my poor spine… It is an ergonomic wonder and a Chiropractor's nightmare… it's red too, which is nice. *Sigh* Yes… I feel at ease… at peace." Boozler scratches his head. Tom: "Never mind him, he's being silly, he's been watching that Bounty Hunter come furniture advertisement, come 'apparent' Black Lotus promo on TV." Shane disregards the disparaging remarks and places his hands back around his head; he shuts his eyes getting even more comfortable, which helps him deal with this whole situation. Boozler: "Who is Black Lotus?" Tom: "Exactly! But I can tell you heaps about Myasato." Bobby: "Your what so?" Tom shrugs. Bobby begins to deal out the cards once more. Tom now takes his turn to sigh, and does so heavily. Tom: "Is anyone else bored of this yet?" Shane's eyes suddenly open, fearfully wide! Gimp nods, his tiny zippers jangling as he does so. Shane: "NOOOOO!" Shane snatches up his few cards mid deal, hoping to continue the game. Bobby looks at Shane. Bobby: "Yeah, this game is boring, sweetie." Shane: "What would you have us do?" Bobby goes to answer, his gaze moving towards the bed already. Shane: "Gah! This is fun, honest~!" Tom: "Let's have a vote. Who votes we stay here and play cards whilst poking fun at the Criminal Activities of a glorified generic Assassin from a Jeffrey Deaver Novel and two 'for all intents and purposes' mute Wrestling' Criminal Overlords that are aired on National TV?" Shane puts his hand up, alone. Tom: "Who votes we take a leaf from Black Lotus…" Tom smiles, proud of his last comment, he continues: Tom: "… and disregard our opponents totally and do something more interesting instead?" Bobby, Gimp and Tom throw their hands into the air. Shane: "NOOOO!" Bobby gleefully grins. Bobby: "New game! New Game!" The Boozler claps his hands together excitedly. Bobby: "Let's play Murder!" Shane falls backwards in defeat and onto his cushions, burying himself in them. Bobby: "Shane and I kill each other, and then the rest of you have to investigate…" Shane: "NO! I'm not killing myself… I'll kill you!" Bobby frowns. Bobby: "But then who'll kill you so that we might spend our damnation together?" Shane scrambles back up, throwing the cushion off of his face. Shane: "I'll kill myself afterwards." Boozler: "Honestly?" Shane: "Honestly~! I'll err… I'll use this Pillow of DOOM~!" Shane smothers his face with the Pillow of DOOM~! again. Bobby merely eyes him suspiciously. Bobby: "I have a great game that we can play." Bobby pulls out a shoebox from beneath the bed. Tom: "What?" Bobby: "Roulette!" Tom: "And I suppose you have a roulette wheel in that shoe box?" Bobby: "No silly." Bobby opens the box and pulls out a revolver. Everyone scampers back in shock… obviously not as accustomed to deadly firearms as your usual Novelist Wannabe-slash-Assassin. Tom: "Jesus Bobby! Where'd you get that!?" Bobby: "I took it from a highly secret underground gun outlet, called Guns R Us." Tom: "How did you know where to look?" Bobby: "It was featured in a Black Lotus promo." Tom: "Oh. Yeah. Right." Bobby offers the gun around. Bobby: "Russian roulette anyone?" Shane: "One bullet, everyone puts it to their brain and fires?" Bobby: "Yep. That's right, Shaneypoos." Tom: "Count me out!" Gimp: "Mhand mhe!" Gimp and Tom shuffle away, cautiously. Bobby looks at Shane, inquisitively. Shane: "One bullet… either you die, and I'm free… or I die and I don't have to watch any more Black Lotus promos?" Bobby smiles with an added nod. Shane: "It's a win, win situation!" Boozler: "Yeah baby!" With reinvigorated enthusiasm Shane shuffles up. Bobby hands Shane the gun. Bobby: "You first, sugar." Shane takes it… he positions it to his brains. Bobby: "Head sugar… place it at your head." Shane: "Oh. Yeah. Right." Shane removes it from his foot and points it at his head. His finger coils around the trigger… he stares at Bobby… the smiling Bobby… the oddly grinning Bobby that might lose his one true love any second now. Shane's brows furrow suspiciously. Shane: "Wait a minute!" Shane lowers the gun and opens the revolver. Shane gasps! Shane: "It's fully loaded you bastard!" Bobby: "Everyone's a winner, baby!" Shane tosses the gun onto the bed. Shane: "We're NOT doing a suicide pact!" Bobby frowns. Bobby: "Spoil sport!" Tom graces the scene again. Tom: "Ok, ok… so what are we going to do?" Shane: "Talk about our silly Lotus opponents whilst playing Farmyard Snap!" Bobby, still sat, cranes his head up towards Tom. Bobby: "Maybe Shane is right… we are going to be fighting them, and others, soon after all, it would make sense… what did they get up to in their promo?" Tom: "Not sure… most of it was about that MyFatso fellow, but it did involve some travelling around the World." Bobby leaps up! Bobby: "I have a great idea!!!" Shane's, Gimp's and Tom's eyebrows rise with interest. The scene fades momentarily. Trans-Atlantic Airspace: October 13, 2003 [DANGER~! DANGER~! DANGER~! Possible Black Lotus Promotion!] The scene opens up in a humongous hot air balloon high in the sky. [Possible crisis averted! … carry on.] Tom: "There's that voice again." Ignoring his buddy Bobby leans against the reinforced basket, his foot placed firmly on the food hamper as he stares over the edge and into the distant sky with disinterest. Boozler: "When I said we should go around the world… I meant as a precursor to sex, foreplay sugar… not literally." Tom shrugs. Tom: "You should be clearer next time, I thought you meant like a vacation." Bobby scowls. He then looks over to the inanely grinning Shane who tugs on the fuel line, igniting the gas with a huge burst. Shane looks about the balloon like an excited child looking for some recognition. Tom: "Come on Bobby, get with it… join in, everyone else is enjoying it." Bobby looks over at Gimp who runs around the large square basket chasing a seagull. Bobby: "Can we have sex?" Tom steps back. Tom: "Bobby… I'm not gay!" Bobby stares at Tom. Bobby: "This trip is soooo not super." Tom: "Bobby, we're high in the sky…" Bobby: "Low in spirits." Tom: "The wind is rushing through our hair." Bobby: "Spoiling my doo!" Tom: "Free as a bird." Bobby: "Jail bird that is." Tom stops. Tom: "You're not enjoying this at all are you?" Bobby: "No, sugar! This is awful… and we'll never get back in time to promo or defend our titles against Black Lotus and the others." Tom: "Come on… Simonkey wouldn't hold the bout without the PTC Champions, would he?" Bobby allows his stubborn silence to answer for him. Tom: "See. He'll give us an extension… hell, he's always giving you his extension." A small subtle smile escapes Bobby's pursed lips. Tom: "… he loves us, we make him popular." Bobby returns to sullenly staring ahead. Bobby: "I want to go home now." Tom: "Come on, cheer up." Bobby screws his face up with repulsion. Bobby: "This is the worst vacation EVER~!" Tom: "… … I think you've been hanging around with Shane too long." The Boozler suddenly straightens. He points over the basket's edge and into the cloudy distance. Bobby: "Hey, look!" Tom and the others spin around, following the Boozler's indicating finger. Bobby: "Another balloon!" Tom: "Yeah… this is a popular stretch, especially with Ballooners trying to circumnavigate the globe in record breaking time." In the distance, just pushing through the tops of the clouds, appears a Red Coloured Hot Air Balloon, and even though it's partially obscured it's clear to see that it's at least twice the size of Homo/Phobics balloon. Bobby: "Hey! His balloon is bigger than mine!" Everyone still watches. Bobby gasps! Bobby: "AND LOOK AT THAT!!!" The huge Balloon now emerges from the clouds, its large bulbous Red Canvas displaying the word: VIRGIN Bobby almost chokes. Bobby: "He's not happy with just being bigger than me, oh no! Mr prim and proper over there is even rubbing his virginity into my face like I'm some cheap two nickel whore!" Gimp rushes over to support his loving Boozler. Gimp: "Mheah!" Gimp shakes his fist at the gloating antagonist over in the distance. The man in the other balloon waves back. Tom leans forward for a better look. Tom: "Is that Richard Branson?" The Boozler leans over his basket and screams out his venomous insult. Boozler: "WHATEVER HONEY!" The Boozler slinks down into the basket. Boozler: "Worst vacation EVER~!" Tom eyes the sorry looking Boozler up, failing to recollect a time that he's seen him so sad. Tom suddenly smiles to himself, a thought dawning on him. Tom: "You like music right, Bobby?" Bobby nods with lacklustre. Tom: "So let's have a sing along." Tom goes over to several large bags on the floor and digs around in them. Soon enough he emerges with an old Ham Radio. He sets it down on a small plastic, pink, Barbie Table and begins to tune it in. Bobby sits down, his interest now growing. The radio suddenly screeches out static annoyance… Bobby clasps his hands over his ears exaggeratedly. Bobby: "ARGGGHHHH!" Tom quickly scans another wave band. Tom: "Sorry about that, we must have picked up a lot of feedback…" Tom smirks. Tom: "Which is more than Black Lotus did, hehehehe." Bobby: "We didn't get either, sugar." Tom puts a hand to his lips. Tom: "Shhh." Tom continues to tune his radio in. Tom: "I can't pick up any music on this PTC Wavelength… let's try this!" The music finally kicks in and Bobby suddenly beams with joy. Bobby: "Ooo!
