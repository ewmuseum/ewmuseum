Watch the quiet ones

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 When one looks into the eyes of others, it can be seen that they are afraid. It can be seen that they do not want to accept the cicumstances that have been placed before them. Faces turn red, eyes swell up and voices turn from sweet soft touches to violent rages. Some people just don't know how to control themselves when it comes to different situations, they do what they think's best without even considering what will come out of it all. Though, even through all this it is easy to spot those who are loud and outgoing. It is easy to know what they are going to do. It is those who are quiet and shy that you have to look out for. For those who are shy don't reveal to much about themselves, they plot things while they watch you. Lori knows this because he is one of them, his mind filled with riffs of hate and scorn. No-one knows until he unleashes his pain upon them, then they suffer... Going back in time The year was 1992, the year that Lori Equinox was at his young age of 14 years. He stood totally straight, his eyes fixated on something set infront of him out of view. He wore a white shirt accompanied with a school tie of sorts. After a few moments of dazing he lifted up his hands and fixed the tie into position before giving a quick loving smile. On his bottom half he wore black pressed trousers and black shoes, it evident he was getting ready for school. He was standing in the kitchen of his home. The counters looked to be made of solid white marble and the compartments doors were furnished with a fine wood. Facing Lori was the window of the kitchen which looked out onto the large area of greenery. The garden was awash with all different colours, from greens to reds and from blues to yellows. In a way this facinated Lori as he always wondered how such beauty could flourish on such a defected planet. In a way, his mother had brainwashed him about the way of the world. They told him that he could trust nobody that he came into contact with, especially older people. They told him to stay away from all strangers because they would hurt him. The truth was that they just didn't want anyone taking their "baby" away from them. [Flash] Creating a miracle Going back further in time, the date was the 27th April 1978, the birthdate of Lori. At this point Lori didn't even have that as his name, he was only a couple of hours old. Laying in the incubator at the hospital he was in good condition with his father, Jacob, watching over him. A smile grew across Jacob's face. It really started to kick in that he had become a father for the first time. He saw that there was a shine across his son's eyes, telling him that he would be special and perfect in every single way. Jacob blew a kiss towards his son and turned to go back to his wife. On arriving she was wide awake, a look came across her face filled with warmth. The two embraced before Jacob sat on the edge of the bed. With ultimate happiness he touched his wife's hands and spoke to her softly... "Priscilla, this is one of the greatest days of my life." Priscilla smiled with all the energy she had left and responded... "Now we have a beautiful baby boy and I don't ever want to let him go." Jacob moved closer to Priscilla and embraced her with a kiss before exclaiming... "I'm a father! I'm a father!" Priscilla let out a stiffled laugh before wrapping her arms around her husband... "And a great father you will be..." The two embraced once more before Jacob moved to the chair beside the bed. He watched his wife drift off to sleep. Within a few minutes he too faded into rest, unaware of what was about to happen. Kidnap the baby At the reception of the hospital, Rosie and Dorethy sat awaiting any calls or patients that needed their assistance. Their day had been very boring and they just could wait to get off work. They were talking to each other when the barrel of a black hand gun caught Rosie's attention. She jumped back in absolute fear and closed her eyes. Dorethy hadn't noticed the gun but turned to see what Rosie was scared of. On seeing the gun, Dorethy jumped back too, grabbing the hand of Rosie beside her. Facing them was a large man, his face covered with black cloth and his eyes covered by dark shades. He was fully dressed in black and no point of his body could be seen. The gun started to wave back and forth between the two receptionists and the gunman began to yell at them... "Where are the incubators!" Dorethy's eyes glazed over as her lip quivered with fright. She couldn't respond, her brain wouldn't let her no matter how hard she tried. The gunman saw this and knew he wouldn't get an answer. He moved the gun in the direction of Rosie and yelled out once more... "You! You there!" Rosie kept her eyes closed, in fear of what was before her. She feared for her own safety but not as much as she feared for the safety of the children. She slowly opened her eyes as he yelled at her once more... "Where's the babies!" She knew that she had to protect the children in the hospital. With a tone as polite as any receptionist she replied to him... "I'm sorry sir, but only the parents of the children are allowed to see the children." The man tilted his head to the left, his eyes behind the shades staring a whole through Rosie. With a shift movement a baseball bat moved into view, held in his left hand. Before she was able to moved, Rosie, received a straight shot of the end of the bat the the face. She fell backwards and hit the ground hard. Dorethy's sense came alive as she saw her friend hit the ground and she lunged forward, only to be stopped in her tracks by the gun pointing straight at her head. She knew that being behind a desk would not help her situation but she just had to try and help. The gunman retracted the safety and cocked his weapon... "You have 5 seconds...em..." The man took a look at Dorethy's name band... "Dorethy...5...4...3...." Dorethy couldn't take it anymore... "Ok! Take the hall infront of you and it's the fifth on the left." Without saying a word the gunman darted off down the corridor to the fifth door on the left. Looking through the window he sees a doctor sitting behind a desk reading. Off to the left of the doctor, all the incubators were, each beside one and other. Slowly, the gunman opened the door of the room. This caught the attention of the doctor who stood up to question the man... "Who the hell are you?" The man made no respond but instead ran at the doctor. With a slick movement the man pointed the gun at the doctor's right leg and fired. The gun was silenced, the only sounds heard were the doctor hitting the ground and his cries of pain. The gunman laughed just enough to be heard and looked down on the doctor... "I'm sure you'll be able to deal with that...Doc!" Laughing again he turned towards the incubators. He rubbed his hands together before thinking to himself. 'Hmmm, what shall be my prize.' His eyes moved along the line of incubators that lay before him. When his eyes finished off at the far left, a card ontop of of the incubators caught his attention. He moved forward and grasped it with his left hand. He opened up the card and read the message inside... "To my new born son, may you live well all throughout your life. You are a gift given from god and I will love you always. Jacob...your loving father." The gunman laughed before changing to his tone to one of a sarcastic master... "Awww, how cute. Unfortunate for him that his father will never see him again." He moved forward and looked down at the baby inside the incubator. He removed the covered of the incubator and removed all the aids that were provided to help Lori survive his first days. While he was doing this he was unaware that the man who wrote the card, Jacob, had been watching the whole time. Jacob's face turned to one of pure anger as he watched the man lift Lori out of the incubator and into his arms. Without thinking, his rage unleashed with a roar of hate... "What the hell do you think you're doing!" His eyes began to glaze as he moved towards the gunman who had now turned around. Within a split second, Jacob found himself with a barrel aimed straight at his face. This made his move back a number of steps and put his hands up. This was the least of Jacob's trouble as the gunman removed his glasses, then his mask. A smile came across the gunman's face as Jacob looked on at horror at the sight infront of him. Holding his stomach to try and ease the sickness swelling inside, Jacob let out all the words he could... "Steven?" Steven grinned and looked towards Lori who was laying asleep over his shoulder... "Beautiful kid you've got here Jacob, pity he'll never know who you are." Jacob's expression turned to one of hatred as he lunged forward. It was too late though as Steven pulled the trigger, Jacob was stopped in his tracks with a bullet through his head. He dropped to the ground and his life was over. Steven decided he had no time to wait around and started towards the door, unfortunately for him a number of armed police entered. Now surrounded by 6 officers, Steven played his trump card. His weapon slowly turned away from facing the officers and the barrel rested against the head of Lori... "You let me go or the kid is blown apart!" The officers put down their weapons but still stood in the way of the exit. Steven once more shouted out to them, he knew that he would get his way... "Now get out of my way! Do you want this child to die?!" The officer in charge motioned for his team to stand aside. Once the way was clear, Steven stolled towards the door. For him it was all clear from here, with his gun to Lori's head he felt that he was unstoppable. The officers watched on as he nearly stepped out the door, only to be stopped in his tracks by a sharp pain in his back. The grip he had on his gun loosened and it fell from his hand, he then dropped to his knees. Behind him stood the doctor who he had previously shot in the leg. The doctor had forcefully drove an operating knife into the back of Steven. One of the officers grabbed Lori and held him over his shoulder as Steven hit the ground. The doctor knelt down and looked into the dying eyes of his victim... "You were right my friend...I was able to deal with that..." The doctor stood up and took Lori from the arms of the officer. He placed him back in the incubator and reattached all the needed equipment. He then turned to the officers and spoke kindly to them... "Thank you all...thank you all so much..." The officers then escorted him from the room and closed it off for investigation. Jacob and Steven both lay motionless. In a way it could be said that they died together in blood, as blood brothers, for indeed they were brothers...twins... [Flash] From that point forward his mother would do anything to keep him from talking to others. She would even stop him going to school if she thought he was getting too friendly with people. She also told him that if he was to become loud and outgoing, she would disown him and end their mother, son relationship. That though, was only the beginning... [To be continued]
