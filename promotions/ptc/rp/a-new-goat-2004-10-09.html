A new goat

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 Once sure the kid fully understands his instructions and knows exactly what to look out for, Big Dave hands over the fifty dollar bill and a card with his cell phone number scrawled on the back. Twelve down, only one more to go. He crosses the street, enters another Starbucks, a mirror image of the one he just left, orders an herbal tea and takes a seat by the window overlooking the main drag. He's angry with himself - angry that he let his guard down. A consummate professional, he was shocked to wake up that morning with a note stuck to his forehead … Dear Dave, You've underestimated me by not hiding her well enough. So, I've taken Nurse off your hands. I'll try not to hurt her… too much. But fuck it! You know she deserves it after all she's done to me! Take care, Craig The funny thing was, he did know better than to underestimate Craig. After all, Craig had been Nurse Wretched’s apprentice for over a year, more than enough time for some of her twisted morals and strange ideologies to rub off on him. He had tried to warn Nurse to be careful, that this time Craig really wasn't kidding, that there could be no easy reprieve, but she just laughed and insisted they stay in her old locker room at the XUDF Arena; a place Nurse had often escaped to when in need of respite. Dave knew it would be one of the first places Craig would come searching, but he didn't realise that he would be so easily able to navigate his way through the myriad of booby traps and alarms he had rigged. Sighing, Dave drags himself away from his thoughts and concentrates on his vigil. From here, he can see three other coffee shops. Sooner or later, Craig will surface from whatever hole he's dragged Nurse to get his fix. God bless the predictability of addiction. Not far from where Dave is sitting, Craig is waiting for Nurse to wake up. Looking around the dank basement, he smiles at the appropriateness of it all. The building has been owned by his family for a century but has lain derelict for most of it. It had been the site of some of the most grisly murders the state had ever seen back in the twenties. His great uncle, Charles Goat, had lured women here and brutally slain them. The thick walls of the granite faced basement and its location below a busy shooting gallery make this more than an ideal place to bring Nurse. No one will hear her scream. Thinking about his uncle, and imagining the anguish of the scores of women he brutally assaulted here, used to cause great feelings of shame to well up in his gullet, but after seeing Nurse at her best – or worst depending on your point of view – Craig has become almost immune to the world’s evil. He has often wondered if he is anything like Uncle Charles, and it seems that soon he shall find out. Craig pries one of the floorboards loose, and peering into the darkness, finds what he's always been told was there, but never really believed… Good ole Uncle Charlie's tool kit. Dave orders another herbal tea; lemon and ginger this time. His phone rings and he eagerly answers it, expecting it to be one of the many coffee shop workers reporting a sighting of either Craig or Nurse. However, the voice on the other end of the line belongs to a third rate stage hypnotist named El Fantasmo. “Mister Big Dave, I’m calling to check how the ummmm, patient is.” “Listen, I don’t have time for this, Craig’s disappeared and taken Nurse with him. Your check’s in the post, so…” “Please, don’t hang up! If Craig’s gone missing, then there’s perhaps something you should know.” “Go on…” “Well, as you know, I was hired to help Craig through sleep hypnosis with his little problem where he can’t wrestle effectively against anyone other than women he finds attractive. But, well, that’s not an easy thing to do. I may have inadvertently caused him to switch his affection to any man he wrestles. But, in making him attracted to his male opponents, I may also have made him unattracted to women. In fact he may hate them. A lot. Especially ones he used to like. At the time I thought it was a rather genius thing to do. Damn whiskey.” “What! You turned my wrestler gay?” “Look, you only have to pay me half if that helps?” As Craig polishes and arranges the equipment on a bench, his mind wanders to his upcoming match against Flyin’ Brian Calvin. He’s looking forward to this one more than any of the others. He’s proven he can beat a novice like Jonathan Borealis and managed to get one over on Jenna Ryder, porn star extraordinaire, but Calvin is different. He’s been in this game for years and is a seasoned professional. He may be forty years old, but he still fills out a pair of wrestling tights admirably. Craig finds himself getting strangely excited by the thought of pinning Calvin, but before he can ruminate further on this, he’s distracted by a groaning sound. Nurse wakes and finds herself securely strapped to a solid metal chair with the type of leather restraints she is well used to from her days working the more violent wings of a psychiatric asylum. She smiles. “Oh Craig! Have you sunk so far as to kidnap me to get my attention and win my affections? Trixie may have fallen in love with her captor, but I guarantee you I won’t!” “Wrong. I don’t want you to love me.” “Yes you do!” “No, I don’t! “Oh yes you do!!” “Just shut up Esme! That may have been true before… right up until yesterday in fact. But I’ve changed. I woke up with such a feeling of freedom, like a huge weight off my shoulders. I was almost happy and it was because I realised that I definitely don’t love you anymore. In fact, there’s not a glimmer of anything but contempt for you in my heart.” Nurse laughs, “You’ve claimed before that you didn’t love me. Remember the night you tried your best to stab me? Why was it that you couldn’t go through with it? Wasn’t it because no matter what I ever did to you, you couldn’t exorcise your misguided feelings for me. And what about Trixie? You may delude yourself by saying it’s all my fault that she left you… but did you even try to win her back? Admit it. You’re glad she’s gone; you can focus on me again!” “Look at me Esme, really look at me. Do I look like some stupid kid with a crush?” As Nurse watches his face, she sees that what he says is true. He’s different in some way. Are his eyes a colder, harder blue, or is it because it’s the first time he’s ever met her stare without quickly looking away? Examining him closer, she sees not the man-child she’s been able to use despicably and control with a twist of his emotions, but a wrestler – a wrestler with a table full of torture implements laid out in front of him. Big Dave is reeling from El Fantasmo's news. “Fantasmo..” “That’s Mr. El Fantasmo, if you please.” “Listen, you ruined my wrestler and my boss is in danger because of it. If I want to call you Tinkly Pixie Woo Woo, I will. Now, tell me absolutely everything you did with Craig over those three nights… and don’t miss a single thing.” “This could be a long phone call… do my usual rates apply?” …Silence “Ok, I get the picture. Now the thing you need to understand about hypnotism is that it’s like, really hard to do! In order to change the minds perceptions about one thing, you have to mess about with lots of other bits. The brain is kind of like a computer…do you know anything about computers?” “Not really.” “Damn! Me neither.” Getting more infuriated by the second, Dave demands, “just tell me everything you do know.” “I think, no… I’m sure, almost positive in fact that I made him a tiny bit more aggressive.” “Aggressive in what way?” “Hmm… some little kids like to pull the wings off flies. I wouldn’t be surprised if Craig liked pulling the legs off little kids.” Nurse knows she’s in danger, and it’s exciting! There are very few men in the world with the power to beat her at her own game and stand next to her as an equal, so it’ll be interesting to find out if Craig is one of them. This game of torture is one Nurse has played many times, albeit usually as the one wielding the instruments. Torture is usually a very one-sided game. It’s kind of difficult for the victim to be the victor, but Nurse figures it’s about time all that changed. “What are you giggling at? You’re about to be tortured for Chrissake!” “Really? I don’t think you can walk the talk Craig. In fact, I’m willing to bet on it!” Frustrated by Nurse’s apparent lack of fear, Craig grabs the first thing he lays his hand on and waves it in her face, “Oh yeah? Well, let’s see if you laugh once I’ve used this…” He glances down to see just what it is that he is brandishing… “this wobbly plastic thing on you!” “A dildo? But I thought you didn’t like me in that way anymore!” This is the last straw. With an anguished screech, he bludgeons Nurse with the leopard print dildo. Once again, the lights go out for her and she slumps back into unconsciousness. As he stands over her panting from the exertion, tears start to fall freely from his bloodshot eyes. What is he doing here? Why is he doing this? Strange emotions have been jostling for his attention all day, and now they finally explode with force to the fore of his thoughts. What has Nurse done to him? Is the man in the black cape who Trixie mentioned part of this? He must be; there can be no other explanation for why he feels like an almost entirely different person than he did a mere few days ago. But how? Why? El Fantasmo is scared. Very scared. Big Dave has demanded that he meet him face to face. As he enters Starbucks, his cloak swinging about his ankles, he stops and fills the doorway, almost as if he owns it. “I am El Fantasmo and I own this doorway!” His bravado quickly dissipates at the sight of Big Dave glaring menacingly over at him. With a nervous squeak, he joins him at a table littered with empty tea cups. “Looks like you’ve been here all day. You know, for a small fee, I could cure you of your tea addiction, bad for the bladder… I think.” Dave grunts, “I’ve seen the results of your work, so please don’t be offended if I say no freakin’ thanks. I’m not an addict of any sort, but Craig is. That’s why I’ve been sitting here since sun up. He will surface soon enough to feed his coffee craving, and when he does, I’ll be the first to know.” El Fantasmo pulls on his collar and gives a tiny cough, “Oh dear, did I not tell you that I may have cured your friend of his caffeine dependency? It was all part of the two for one deal. So what’s plan B?” Incredulous, Dave just about resists the temptation to rip the insignificant two-bit stage hypnotist from the land of the living. “Are you sure? There’s no chance that you failed at that one too?” “I’m positive. Unless of course he has a new allergy to rhubarb, in which case, he’s still a caffeine junky.” Big Dave finds it hard to identify a new emotion coursing through him. It’s the feeling you get when you fail, and Dave has never failed, and failed so spectacularly before. Like a lot of men confronted by the sight of a grown man beginning to cry: El Fantasmo starts to babble uncomfortably, “Goat eh? That’s an unusual name. Never heard that one before, except of course for the notorious Charles Goat… Any relative d’you think? In fact, I think he used to own the building next door. Read about it in the Enquirer. They say his torture kit is still there somewhere… If we ever do find this Craig kid, do you think he’d give me a tour?” Dave grabs El Fantasmo by the lapels and plants a sloppy kiss on him. “You genius!” Craig screams over and over as he ransacks the basement, injuring himself accidentally as he overturns the bench holding his uncles torture implements. The sharp pain and brightness of the free flowing blood from his wound calms him slightly. Pain is a familiar feeling, and hence good. He slouches down beside Nurse Wretched and stares up into her vacant face. She’s still alive, but barely. He can see her pulse throb weak and threadlike at her neck. Once she wasn’t awake and sneering at him, it was a lot easier for Craig to hurt her, even though he still can’t understand why he hates her so much. Yes, she has done her best to destroy his life and push away everything important to him, but he let her do it. Is it himself he’s more angry with? As these new thoughts mingle with the thousands of others racing through his brain, Craig too begins to lose consciousness. The last thing he sees is two men, one large, one short and fat, burst through the door. As Big Dave checks on his boss and her protégée, El Fantasmo looks at his patient appraisingly. “Wow! I really did turn him gay, didn’t I? Unless of course, he always wore chaps and leather vests. It may take me a few days to reverse it all so he gets back to his usual wimpy woman wrestling self. Hypnotism can be a very complicated thingy-me-bob.” Dave’s mouth twists into a sardonic semi-smile, “How about we just let him wrestle Calvin first?”
