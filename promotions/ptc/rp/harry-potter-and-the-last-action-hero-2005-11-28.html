Harry Potter and The Last Action Hero!

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 Harry Potter and … The Last Action Hero! After walking all through the first day back at Hogwarts, the two friends, Harry and Ron finally got to the class they had been waiting for. Mad-eye Moody was supposed to be their new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher, but unfortunately he had stomach problems… if ya know what I mean. So Harry and his friend finally reached the class. It was full of press and Rita was there just gushing out emotion at the prospect of the new teacher. They both sat down and waited for the new teacher to arrive. Ron: Have you seen Hermoine? Harry: Yeah, I think she went to the bathroom… she is probably PMSing. I can’t wait till her boobs start to grow! Ron: Hey! Don’t say that… ok… me too! Is there like an X-Ray spell I can use on Fleur though? Harry: I haven’t found one yet. Hermoine: (Out of nowhere) Damn. I just saw Fleur… when am I gonna get the boobs!? Ron: Harry and I were just asking ourselves the same thing. Harry: I wonder when this new teacher will arrive… I hope he teaches me something to torture animals with. A fog starts from the room of the professor. From it appears a big muscular man. His muscles are a bit saggy because of the man’s age, but he still looks like he can kick your ass. He is dressed in a grey suit and has a campaign button on his jacket. The man is none other than Arnold Schwarzenegger. Ahnold: Good mohning. I am tha new defense against tha dahk ahts teacha. Everyone sits there with a strange look on their faces. They couldn’t believe that the famous Arnold was the new teacher… did he know anything about the dark arts!? Ahnold: I know what ya must asking youhselves. Does ‘e know anything about tha dahk ahts? Well… I do. I am not only tha best action ‘ero in ‘istory, but I am also tha gov’noh of Califohnia. Harry: This will be interesting… Ahnold: I ‘ave defeated tha best monstahs there is. I was in Conan The Barbarian, The Terminator 1-3, Commando, Predator, Total Recall, Kindergarten Cop, End of Days and Batman & Robin… wait… could ya scratch that one? Harry: Oh Lohd… shit! Now I’m stahting to talk like ‘im! Ahnold: Tha fihst spell I will teach you is the Cruciatus. ‘On, come ‘ere. Ron stands up scared like always. Ahnold takes out a little stick with no form whatsoever. He aims it at Ron but it doesn’t seem to work. Ahnold: Dammit! Stupid thing. Harry: What a moron! Ahnold starts to mutter curses to see if one of them works. Ahnold: Avada Kedabha. A green ball shoots out of the stick and heads directly towards Harry. Ahnold: Get down, ‘Arry! Get down! Harry throws himself to the floor and the ball hits some unsuspecting cameraman. He drops dead. Hermoine: Nicely done! Got him dead. Ahnold: Ok kiddies, class dismissed. Don’t be concehned… I’ll be back! Everyone starts to walk out of the room with surprise on their faces. Arnold tells Harry to wait. Harry picks up his backpack and waits for everyone to leave. Ron and Hermoine were flirting with each other and I’m pretty sure I heard them say something like ‘sex’ and ‘my room’. After everyone had left, Arnold grabbed a box and gave it to Harry. Ahnold: ‘Arry, this should ‘elp you in tha battle against the dahk lohd. Harry opens the box and inside is a very big gun. Ahnold: I am su’e that will ‘elp mo’e than crappy wands. The scene darkens with Harry shooting a hole through the wall and Arnold laughing. We interrupt the great movie that you were just watching, Harry Potter and the Last Action Hero, to bring you this important announcement. A mad man is on the lose. This man that you see right here is wanted dead or alive for murder on the first, second and err… third degree, grand theft auto and worst of all… posting a hype in Spanish in an all-English forum! The madness! The travesty! Thank you for your time. Now, back to your regular programming. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule]
