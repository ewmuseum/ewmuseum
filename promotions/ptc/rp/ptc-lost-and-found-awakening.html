Lost and Found Part One: Awakening “Men are not prisoners of fate, but prisoners of their own minds.” - Franklin D. Roosevelt Location: Leesburg, Florida Date and Time: July 1st, 2006 @ 1:30AM[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] Errrrrrr![FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] The back door to the local drinking bar opens ever so loud. A man stumbles out of the door loudly, unable to catch his balance for the life of him. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] STOMP![FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] He continues to stumble around as he almost falls down the three cracked stairs leading out of the bar. The man, wearing a pair of brown Lugz boots, smacks the concrete hard with his feet as he walks down them, still trying to catch his balance. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] SPLAT![FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] A drop of blood drops to the concrete right next to him. A closer look at the man reveals that the blood is from his face. There are four cuts covering his face, with blood slowly trickling out of them. However, the cuts don’t seem to faze the man as much as the alcohol has. As he reaches the bottom of the stairs, he falls down, but seems determined to continue on. He continues to move towards the nearby graphitized wall opposite of the bar. He crawls over towards the wall and sits with his back against the wall.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] VROOM![FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] Around him is an ambience of sound. Cars driving by the alley, loud music playing through the streets, the sirens of police cars, fire trucks, and ambulances fill the air, but he can’t hear any of it. He’s in a daze. His brown hair, short and dirty, his clothes though look like he could be a model for Banana Republic as he is wearing a black, long sleeve wool sweater, and a pair of nice blue jeans, both from the overpriced store. The man, through the blood, dirt, and sweat, is attractive. The first thing that you would notice about this man is his eyes though. Even through the grit and grime, through the pain and daze, his eyes light up like a Christmas tree on Christmas Eve. There’s a certain fire to them that would make you think the man is focused on something, but what it is, you have not the slightest clue.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] Scrape.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] His head moves against the brick wall, causing a faint scraping sound to be heard. The man is quickly losing consciousness and can barely keep his head up any longer. A cloud has formed inside of his head, causing him to look at things slowly. The injuries he’s suffered, while not severe, have taken their toll on him, but he continues to fight. He tries to break through the haze because he does have a focus. He has to get up and continue with what he was doing before. His arms try to push him up, but they fail. They’re weak. He’s weak. He tries again and fails yet again. Thinking that the third time would be a charm, he tries once again, and not only fails, but the exertion wears him out. As he thinks about how all he wanted was a drink, his eyes suddenly close as he blacks out to the world around him[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] Unfortunately for him, the man who had caused the damage to his face was just stepping out of the door to finish what he had started. [FONT=Verdana] ---- [/FONT] Location: Los Angeles, California Date and Time: June 30th, 2006 @ 8:30PM[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] Click. Click. Click. Click.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] A vivacious woman is seen walking down the street. The heels that she is wearing are clicking against the concrete sidewalk with each step that she makes. She’s walking with a purpose and ignores the people that continue to walk past her. As they walk past her though, they have to do a double take because of the sheer beauty of this woman. Her long brown hair curls just perfectly all the way down her back that would make women envious of her. Her face has a certain innocence to it that make men crumble with just one look. Her blue eyes would make men melt and her smile would make anyone weak in the knees. She knew she was beautiful and she loved to show it. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] On this particular night, she was wearing a long black silk skirt that went right to her knees, and a red sleeveless shirt with enough cleavage to show off her nice breasts to turn a man on. She had it all. The scary part was that she knew it. She was confident about herself. At times, too confident, but she could care less. However, on this night, something preoccupied her mind. Her left hand continued to fiddle around with a silver necklace that was somewhat tight around her neck. This necklace was deeply important to her because her father had given it to her on the day she was born, and she hadn’t gone a day without wearing it since that day. As she continued to walk down the street, her cell phone began to ring, and after a few seconds of fishing around in her small black purse, she found it, and answered it.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Hello?” she sternly answered. The person calling her had broken her concentration, and she wasn’t happy about it in the least bit.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Ms. White, this is Shawn,” the man replied.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Yes, Shawn? Have you found him?” she questioned him. This was an important phone call after all, and was happy that her concentration had been broken after all. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Yes, Ms. White, we have found him. He’s currently in a small town located in Florida called Leesburg,” he answered with a certain pride in his voice. She smiled slightly as she heard his response. Not only because of the fact that he had found who she was looking for, whomever that may be, but because of that very pride in his voice. He took pride in doing his work and she not only respected that, but loved it. Not often enough had she found a man that could not only do his job, but did it well and with pride to boot.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Well done, Shawn. I’ll be there as soon as I can. I’ll call you when I land,” she finished before closing her cell phone and placing it back in her bag. She then stopped in the middle of the sidewalk and turned around. The latest bit of news had proven to be more important then the place she was currently heading. She had to leave the city immediately and head towards Florida to meet with a man that had long been elusive.[FONT=Verdana] ---- [/FONT] Location: Leesburg, Florida Date and Time: July 1st, 2006 @ 1:20AM[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “You haven’t called…,” a rather muscular man said to the bartender for the evening. She glared at him, before turning her back to him, not really wanting to have a conversation with the man. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “I haven’t called Ricky because this relationship or whatever it is because it was never a relationship to begin with, is over! How come you just can’t seem to get that? You call and you call, yet I never pick up! So stop calling me!” she berates him while fixing a drink for another customer. Not only do the words sting him, but the fact that she won’t look him as she says them infuriates Ricky. He places his hand on his chin. While Ricky is not the most charming man in the world, he does okay for himself in the world. Ricky is your standard looking male in the world, around six feet tall, but in good shape, though with some fat on him. However, Ricky does care a little about how he looks and has some muscle definition to him. Yet, the drinking doesn’t exactly help him in the long run. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Then let’s fix this! I know you want to be with me Rebecca!” he tells her sharply. If there’s one thing that Ricky doesn’t lack, its confidence. He’s full of it, so full of it, that it blinds him to really understand what anyone is saying, including Rebecca. As he tells her this, she scoffs as she hands the man sitting three stools down from Ricky a drink. The man doesn’t even look at her as he takes the drink and just downs it. If she hadn’t been a little scared of the guy, then this would’ve done, but everything about the man just seemed off to her, especially his red eyes. Never in her life had Rebecca seen red eyes before, but right here before her sat a man who had red eyes. A man that also didn’t like to speak as all he said when he came into the bar was that he wanted a drink. When she asked him what kind, he didn’t respond, so he took it upon herself to make him any kind of drink and see what he liked and didn’t like as if he were a guinea pig. He had had seven drinks in the span of twenty minutes, and didn’t seem the least bit stunned yet. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Then stop acting as if you control me Ricky. You can’t tell me where to be, when to be there, and what to wear. That’s why I don’t want to be around you. If I wanted someone to control my life, then I’d go back to my mother’s house, and let her run my life for me. Trust me; she has my husband picked out, how many children I’m going to have, and a shack out back for me to live in. If you ask me, that’s God’s way of showing me what hell could be like, and I, frankly, don’t feel like going to hell! And you know what you are? You’re my mother!” she yelled at him once again, going off the cusp as she pulls out a white hand towel from underneath the bar top to clean off the counter. For a moment she stops and looks around the bar to see how full it is tonight. She sees nobody else in the establishment though, more than likely due to the fact that it was fifty minutes before it was time to close up shop, and makes a mental note of that. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “That’s what you need in a man Rebecca because you have no direction in your life. Look at you, you’re thirty years old, and you’re a bartender in a small-ass city. This is Leesburg and you should be living a much better life then this,” he tells her knowing full well that he also lives in Leesburg, and doesn’t have the glamorous life himself either.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Yeah, and like you’re going to change all that for me?” she retorts before chuckling at him.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Are you laughing at me? Are you laughing at me, bitch?!” he yells at her before shooting up out of his seat and kicking his stool away. She steps back in horror as he stands there, ready to leap over the counter, and beat her.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Ricky, calm down! You’ve had a few drinks, so let’s not do something here. Not again…,” she begins before he smashes his mug into the counter. As she is seem with a fleeting look on her face, the other man sitting at the bar looks up at what’s going on, particularly at Rebecca. As he looks at her, he notices two distinct bruises on her left arm that she’s tried to hide with makeup or something of that sort. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “You’ve beat her before…,” the man says to Ricky calmly. Ricky glances over at the man in disbelief that he even has anything to say to him and is sticking his nose into their business.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “What?” he strikes back, trying to feign disbelief and total innocence. However, the man isn’t fooled as he is quickly shaking his head while slightly chuckling at the foolish man for lying to him.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “There are two distinct bruises on her left arm that she’s tried to cover, and you did so horribly I might add,” he points on the woman. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “From the way you’re acting now, it’s only safe to assume that you’ve been beating her, which is the real reason that she doesn’t want to be with you. You beat her. Just think about, why would a woman want to be with someone who beats her? It’s common sense, idiot. Plus, even if you aren’t beating her, you’re attitude isn’t exactly helping the situation. Am I rambling? I’m rambling, aren’t I? It’s the alcohol. My apologies. So, let me end this by saying the cliché, leave her alone,” he finishes in a low tone that doesn’t change the entire time he’s talking, realizing that the alcohol may be affecting him even more then he would care to realize. He’s hoping that the man will leave, because he’s not liking his odds in a bar fight. Ricky begins to inch up closer to the mysterious man.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “How about you stay out of my business?” Ricky tells the man, more so than asking him. The man just sits there, both elbows up on the bar, and his drink glass in between them. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Sounds good…,” the man begins before smashing his elbow in the throat of Ricky. Ricky quickly grasps his throat as he stumbles backwards as he feels his airway starting to close off, but not quite feeling the pain yet. As he begins to stumble backwards, the man slowly gets up to finish off the fight, but is quickly met with an elbow in his midsection. Ricky had recovered much quicker then the man had anticipated, and before he knew it, Ricky was placing his hands on the back of his shirt and pants, and tossing him up over the bar top and into the cabinet full of alcohol behind where Rebecca was standing moments before. The man crumples instantly on the ground, trying to keep his wits about him, but the blow he took plus the alcohol isn’t helping matters very much. Rebecca bends down to attend to the man who is already starting to pull himself up.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “That wasn’t part of the plan,” he mutters loud enough so that Rebecca can hear him. He starts to pull himself up, but before he can even find out where Ricky is, he feels a palm on top of the back of his head before it smashes the man’s face into the bar top. The man starts to stumble himself, reeling from the impact of that blow. He’s quickly trying to recover himself, but it’s getting harder by the second as the alcohol acts like a drug activating inside of his system. The cloud in his head begins to get bigger by the minute, blurring his vision and reactions. He turns around to see Ricky coming at him and goes to punch him in the midsection, but misses by a mile. Ricky returns the favor by head butting the man in the face hard. He quickly goes down, but is trying to pull himself back up immediately. The man’s hand comes up to his face to see how bad the damage is and feels blood starting to trickle out above his left eye. Definitely not part of the plan the man thinks to himself before placing an elbow in Ricky’s midsection.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] However, Ricky seems less fazed then the man does and immediately knees him in the face. Both fighters are ignoring the constant yells from Rebecca to stop. They don’t even register in their minds as the fight continues on. Ricky then grabs the man by the back of his shirt once again and notices the door leading to the alley out behind the bar. He then picks him up and pushes the mysterious man towards the door. The man is then seen trying to pick himself up, but stumbles out of the door trying to regain control of his body.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Ricky, stop! Don’t you think you’ve done enough to him?!” Rebecca pleads with the unrelenting Ricky. Ricky just turns around and smacks her in the face hard enough to cause her to start bleeding from her lip. Rebecca, who is now on her knees on the ground, begins to cry from the sheer force of the slap from Ricky. Her emotions are all over the place and she has just broken down now.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Stay here Rebecca. I’m coming back for you after I finish off your little knight in shining armor,” he yells at her before walking towards the door to finish his fight.[FONT=Verdana] ---- [/FONT] Location: Leesburg, FL Date and Time: July 1, 2006 @ 1:32AM[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] The mysterious man laid there unconscious. The mixture of alcohol and the preceding fight had taken a lot out of him, and his body was trying to shut him down so it could basically reboot him. The alley was filled with trash everywhere as the bar mainly used it to store their trash, but also crack addicts and drug dealers loved to use the alley to either get high or to finish up a drug deal without the public around them knowing about it. Littered throughout the alley were used needles, dropped paraphernalia, and other little trinkets. As the man laid in the filthy alley, little did he know that trouble was on its way in the form of Ricky, the man who had done the damage to him. As Ricky stepped through the door and walked down the three concrete steps cockily, he noticed his opponent was laid out in the street, and smiled to himself. He slowly walked over to the man and lifted him up by his nice, fancy shirt. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Told you not to get involved in my business, boy. Now, you’ve gone and done woken up a little demon inside of me. See, I was living a nice, quiet life here in Leesburg, but do you know who I am? Do you want to know what my real name is? It’s not Ricky; the name is Wayne Arthur Silsbee. Do you know what I do to people? Sure, they know about the children I raped up in Oregon, but what they don’t know about are the women I’ve raped and murdered over the years as well. Ya see what you done got yourself into, boy? You’ve gone and opened up a can of worms that you weren’t really ready to open now, were ya?” Wayne reveals his sordid past to the dormant man. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] Wayne then began dragging the man through the back alley, to where, no one knows. However, he wouldn’t be moving for long as before he knew it, he felt a large bottle crash against his back. As he felt the pain go across his back, he relinquished his hold on the man to turn and face his new assailant. He laughed slightly as he saw that it was just Rebecca before he wrapped his large hand around her throat and lifted her up high above the ground.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “You don’t know who you’re messing with, Rebecca! What I’ve done to you is nothing compared to what I’m going to do to you! Do you understand that?! Do you fucking understand that?!” he yells at her as the expression in her face begins to turn white. Her body continues to squirm, trying to pull away from her, and her eyes continue to get wide as the fear begins to set in on her. She feels like she’s going to die, and it’s going to happen right now. The life that she has had will have been for nothing. The future she wished she had, the future she was trying to build towards, she will never know about. Her arms start to flail as she tries to weaken the grip that Wayne has around her to no avail. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “P-p-please…,” she says the words barely able to escape from her throat. The grip around her throat is so tight that her vision is starting to get blurrier by the minute. However, she’s saved as the grip on her throat is unexpectedly released. As her vision begins to come back, she sees that the man who’s been trying to help her is back up, groggily, but up nonetheless and is fighting back. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] The forearm to the back of Wayne’s back didn’t do much damage, but it did its job as it took the attention off of Rebecca, and back on the mysterious man. As Wayne turns around, he looks at the man who is roughly the same size as him, but more built. The fact that Wayne is lucky that he’s kept the upper-hand on this man doesn’t escape his mind, and knows he has to keep him down if he wants to walk out of here alive. Wayne goes for a punch at the mysterious man, but the man ducks the punch and nails him with a right hand of his own to his jaw. Wayne stumbles backwards from the sheer impact of the punch as he felt the knuckles from the man’s hand crush against his jaw. Wayne can feel the blood in his mouth, and that motivates him enough to throw another punch, but the man is able to duck it again and nails yet another punch to Wayne’s face. As Wayne stumbles even more, the man places a knee to his midsection before slamming his head into the nearby stained wall. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] As he feels the tide changing, the man places a swift kick to Wayne’s face to keep him down for just a little bit longer. The man then drops to one knee, still feeling the effects of the alcohol and the beating he took just minutes ago, but his body had shut down so it could essentially reboot itself so that he would have a better chance at living through this ordeal. As he tries to regroup himself, he feels a hand on his shoulder. He looks up to see Rebecca sitting before him, checking him out to see how he’s doing. She sees the blood above his eye and pulls out her white hand towel, and begins to wipe the blood off of his face.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “We don’t have long before he gets back up, but thanks for your help. What’s your name?” she asks her mystery hero.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “I don’t know…,” he responds, searching his mind for the very answer he’s been searching for what seems forever.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “You don’t know? How can’t you know?” she questions him, perplexed by this startling piece of information. As he goes to respond however, he feels his world explode into a million pieces as the overpowering Wayne punches him right in the back of his neck. The force from the shot drops the unknown man right onto his chest, and he loses his breath. The pain in his abdomen area is mind numbing as he tries to suck in some air, but is unable to. As he continues to breathe in some air and gain his strength back, he is plucked up into the air and has his face smashed in by Wayne’s large, hairy, and sweaty forearm. A large crackling sound rings throughout the air as the man yells out in grotesque pain as he feels his nose break from the power of the blow. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Time to say good night,” Wayne says with a strain of excitement and glottal blood thrust to his voice. [FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Good night,” a voice coming from behind Wayne says in a low tone. Wayne instantly lets go of the unknown man and as he turns around he’s met with a roundhouse kick to his face, taking him down. As Wayne is going down, the beaten man is seen crawling towards the wall, feeling the pain throughout his face, and the blood flowing out of his nose. He sits up against the wall, and looks up at the man who’s helped him who is walking over to him.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Craig, are you okay?” the new man asks him as if he knows him. He looks back at him with a perplexed look on his face as if he has no idea what he’s talking about.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] “Who’s Craig? Who’re you?” the man asks in response, trying to get answers to this. The new man though is shocked by this. He stands there aghast at what he just heard from his friend. The man sitting before him, beaten and bloodied, was one of his best friends in the entire world.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] And he didn’t even know who he was.[FONT=Verdana] [/FONT] To Be Continued.
