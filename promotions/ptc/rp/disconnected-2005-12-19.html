Disconnected

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 December 10th Lexington, Kentucky The Original Pranksta was pulling into a small parking lot. The weather cold as always. This time of the year sucks in short. Parking his car in front of a tiny building with a sign reading Gags & Gifts. "This is it" Grest mutters to himself as he gets out of his car. He sighs as he pushes open the door. The Global Superstar sees no one in sight and just looks around at the array of whoopee cushions, collapsible chairs, and other great prank items. He walks up to the counter and rings the bell next to the cash register. "One moment please" A woman says coming from behind a partially open door. Grest recognizes Karen’s voice and childishly decides to continue ringing. "One moment please" She says again still in a nice sweet tone of voice. Grest smirks as he continues to ring the bell to test her patience. "I said one moment" She says very agitated. Holding back a laugh Grest stops for a moment. "Thank you, Sir" She says her voice still sounding agitated. He goes back to ringing immediately after that. You hear a snarl like noise coming from behind the door as she slams a box down. She turns around and opens the door and as soon as she sees The Grester a smile comes across her face and a middle finger in the air. "Asshole" The tall lovely red head says. "I’m happy to see you too." He says as he walks behind the counter and hugs Karen. Swinging her around and letting her go. "Glad you to see down here. What’s new with you?" Karen asks. "I entered a tournament called GTT5. I beat Jay Ramey and next round I face off against a guy name Jorge Samuelsson I should do pretty well." Grest says with a smile. "Isn’t that what you always say?" She replies in a condescending tone. Grest raises an eyebrow and tilts his head….. confused. "What are trying to imply Karen" Grest asks. "You haven’t done well lately with wrestling." Karen remarks. "I know I haven’t exactly had the best of luck but why should that stop me from continuing to wrestle…. My dream." Grest says in hopes of explaining his actions. "You aren’t exactly one of the best in the business and you’re suppose to be reaching your prime. If that’s the case…. You haven’t reached very far." Karen says showing no confidence in Fred. "That means I should just give up?! People have went years in this business without winning a world title and because they stuck around they finally did it. They did it because they did not give up." Grest damn near shouts…. Intensity showing in his eyes. Anger. "Grest…… don’t get so angry I am just saying maybe you are better suited for something else. Maybe you should come into business with me?" She suggests. "Are you kidding me?! Work for you? ….What the hell were you doing shots back there?!" Grest showing more intensity. Unable to believe what he is hearing. "Partners Freddy…. Partners." She says backing away from Grest. Fearing this usually kind and gentle man. Grest takes a moment to collect himself taking a deep breath. "I can’t believe this." He mutters quietly. "I’m sorry… I didn’t mean too." She tries to apologize for her actions. "I have to…. I have to get out." Grest says as he bolts for the door trying to get out of the store quickly. He pushes the door open hard as it swings open he heads directly to his car getting inside and slamming it shut. He pulls out of the parking lot… Driving off. Fred Grest couldn’t believe the conversation he had with his high school sweet heart Someone that always believed in him no matter what the circumstances were. She believed nothing was too great for him to overcome. Even if he appeared to be nothing more then your average guy to the rest of the world she helped him believe he was more. He already knew it but very few others saw any sort of potential. If she did not see it anymore did anyone else. Could anyone else? Could he just be a 2nd round exit in a tournament meant for the best? Grest is not Rich Rollins, He’s not Seymore Almasy, He’s not Christian Zenith. He had no reputation to fall back on if he were to falter because that’s all he does. If she did not believe he was sure nobody did. He wasn’t even sure if he had enough confidence… Enough strength to believe in himself. After all when is the last time he’s done anything important. Never… December 15th Toledo., Ohio Cold… Snow… Christmas Cheer… Everywhere you look this time of year you see tons of houses strung with lights and decorated Christmas tree. Well atleast one Global Wrestling superstar was an exception to this…. Fred Grest. The usual jovial Grester was anything but at the moment. He seemed disconnected from life because of what happened in Kentucky. The only thing Christmas about him at the moment was the fact that he was shoveling the side walk. Tossing the snow to the side of the street as the ice cold wind blew in his face. His face turning red and his cheeks feeling as if they were turning to ice. He hurries to finish the side walk so he can get inside to get warm. "Karen…" He mutters to himself. GTT5 was on the back burner of Grest’s mind at the moment. More worried about a woman then he was crushing on then his opponent Jorge Samuelsson. "How… Could she?" Saying to himself as he walks up the steps . He stomps his boots free of snow before opening the door and heading inside. Walking to the back porch and setting his shovel there. He takes off his coat and tosses it to the floor uncaringly. He walks over to the sofa crash landing on it laying there lifeless. "Isn’t that what you always say?" Grest says trying to think back to the conversation he had almost a week ago. How can win if he barely had enough in him to shovel the snow. He is sure his opponent is doing more then moping over some girl and dealing with confidence issues. Jorge has probably watched tons of tapes on him already. Grester on the other hand hasn’t seen a one. "I’m doomed." Words of doubt escape his mouth as usual. Doubt that will destroy him against Jorge Samuelson like they have against most of his opponents. Grest once heard that in this business if you let the slightest doubt enter your mind you’ve already lost the match. That’s what seems to be Grest’s biggest problem for the past 2 years. His rookie year was simple amazing but after Wrestling Midweset sucked the life out of him he thought a change of scenery would help him out. So he arrived in Global with the ring gear of one of his arch rivals alter egos past gimmicks. Grest wanted to play head games with Syberus when he could not return to being The Incredible Y as he planned. However you can not play mind games when you haven’t even won match against him. Syberus had the confidence to climb up the ladder and grab the gear. Grest had nothing but doubt and self pity lingering in his mind to do so. Just as he does now. Grest knows he will fail… therefore he will.
