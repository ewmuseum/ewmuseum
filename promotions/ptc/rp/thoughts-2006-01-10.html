10 January 2006

Date: 2006-01-10 Shanty Jones sat in the hotel room. His mind was closed to me this time, as if by finding out the truth about his nature I had closed a door. No more revelations were forthcoming, but I could still see him. I could still gaze upon his quiet demeanour and ask questions like how, or more importantly, why? I’ve done some bad things in my time, I really have, but nothing like that. I was always loyal to my team, to my group, to my unit. Shanty Jones was one of them. What could have turned him away from that? Then there’s Doctor Curiosity. He’s within the requisite twenty yards of Shanty, but not in the way he had imagined he might be. I must commend his restraint though, as he’s stopped yelling and even whimpering, just sitting locked inside the closet, biding his time. I’ve sensed he has more importance here than Shanty gives him credit for. He’s no fool, despite his appearance. I’ve not seen his past, but everyone has heard the rumours of just how far he went last year to claim the crown. Artemis Atkins. I’ve no idea how he ended up with such an unusual name, after our parents had called me Steven, but that’s parents for you. I’ve been seeing flashes of him recently. He’s still living in London, working in an office. He keeps the nine to five going, or more accurately the quarter past nine to a quarter to five, with a nice long lunch break. He’s drifting aimlessly, much like my mind is right now. I don’t know why I’m being shown his life again, because they’re nothing of interest in it, but maybe it means I’m branching out. Maybe it means that I’m doing my observing job well, for whoever wants me to see these things. Perhaps I’ll be able to see everyone else I left behind? Paul Goodman… now that’s a new one on me. I’ve heard the name, of course, and I’ve seen him from when he was in the prison with Shanty. I don’t know why I… ah hell, I sound like a stuck record now. Even so, he sits in his prison cell, looking at the wall as if he can see straight through it, as if he can appreciate the fine sunset outside. He doesn’t look like a caged man, like someone who has lost their liberty. Perhaps he’s dreaming of Shanty Jones, and how his protégé is faring? Perhaps he’s wondering if introducing Shanty to his brother, Doctor Curiosity, and getting him entered into a wrestling tournament was a good idea. Then again, Paul Goodman doesn’t seem to be wondering about anything. His face is set. He knows his place in the world, and maybe that’s all he needs. "Almost," he says. How curious. Then I’m gone from seeing others, and I’m alone with my thoughts in this abyss, this simple absence of space and sensation. I still can’t believe what I found out about Shanty. If I had skin it would be crawling, and even now I can imagine that I have a body, and every molecule is screaming at my betrayer, at the one who denied me of life, who turned on his own kind and got away with it. I want to scream into every ear that this man is a killer, a terrorist, a man seemingly capable of anything. I want to make a difference, to pay him back for what he has done to me, and to whoever else he has ruined. I think back, and there was one memory, a few weeks ago. I saw him, Shanty Jones, and he was having an argument. I was there, in a manner of speaking. Not only as an observer, but also as a participant… but this time it was not the past I was seeing, it was the future. … There must be a way back.
