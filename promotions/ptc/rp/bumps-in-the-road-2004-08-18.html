Bumps in the Road

18 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-18 WE OPEN IN THE BOOMER SOONER LOCKER ROOM!!! ….. Wait, where the hell are we? Damnit, what a waste! After years of intros, that is the only time I’ve gotten straight to the point of where we were without any fuss or having to explain anything or just plain break down in tears. I was so eager to get it done, I didn’t even realize that we didn’t even open in in the Boomer Sooner locker room like usual. I mean, I was just going with the odds. It should have worked. Now look, I’ve slipped back into my old routine. *Sigh* Oh well, should have just expected it. Fine, let’s get on with it then shall we. We open on a nameplate on a dark wooden desk. The name displayed is one that used to only invoke minor feelings of pity and annoyance from many within the XUW organization. Recently though, the name on the plate has begun to invoke some stronger feelings. Feelings like confusion, uncertainty, fear, and anger. Confusion in his return. Uncertainty of the future of the company. Fear of what he may have planned. And anger at what he most certainly does have planned. The name on desk is none other … than Mr. Webster: XUW Commissioner. The camera begins to pan up, revealing a few empty fifths of various alcohols lain across the desk, some random papers, and a freshly poured crystal glass of bourbon on the rocks. We continue up until we rest on the man himself, sitting in a large red leather chair behind the desk, looking rather pleased with himself. He’s sitting forward in the chair, elbows resting on the desk with his hands cupped together, trying to shield his grin. He looks from side to side, eyes focusing on some things in his office with him. At first glance, he appears to be a well dressed man. At second glance, his shirt is stained and wrinkled like it’s been slept in, and his tie is pulled undone. His eyes are crinkled and bloodshot, and he’s got a five o’clock shadow … from about a week and a half ago. So at third glance, he looks like a drunk who just rolled out of the gutter after a binge, except for one thing. The smile. He finally cannot hide his glee anymore. His hands leave his face as he falls back into his chair. Being a smaller man to begin with, having a large, high-back chair doesn’t do much for his stature. He laughs and holds his hands out in front of him in a “ta-da” motion. The camera begins to pull back into the room. Webster disappears into the shadow of the chair as the large wall of windows behind him pour light into the camera, as well as the room. We continue pulling back between two chairs in front of the desk. In the chair on the right lounges Woof J. Woof. A calm, cool, and crazy adventurer, his leg is thrown over the arm of the chair, and his arm hangs behind it. He looks around the room, his face showing mostly boredom at the current lack of events. In the chair on the left sits XUW’s most lovable jobber, Fatbadger. He has been chosen for this team for just that reason. Sitting uncomfortably in his chair, eating handful after handful of Funyuns, his nervous gaze never leaving something on his left. As we continue to pull back, we see what cannot take his eyes off of. There, stands a man against the wall, his arms crossed and face obscured by shadow, but his eyes fixed on the man behind the desk, cutting like knives. This man has long been assumed dead, and quite possibly has been. He is a man who has haunted the dreams of Fatbadger for just as long, and now towers before him. He is The Ripper, Jack Nobbledy. As the camera finally pulls all the way back, we see the final piece of Webster’s puzzle to the right of Woof. His Doggy Style partner … wait. That doesn’t sound right at all … or does it?! No, not at all. The man standing along the wall on the right is the Lone Wolf of XUW, and its current movie star, Blake Wolfson. He is the man who might be most responsible for where the other four men sit now. He and another man who is referred to as the Anarchist, a little over a year ago, used their Stable Wars victory with the Ultra Violents to challenge Mr. Webster for his commissionership, and forced him out of XUW until his recent return. Now Blake, along with Jack, Woof, and Fatty, look across the desk of the man who's main goal seems to be the downfall of Xtreme UItimo Wrestling. Mr. Webster: Gentlemen ... BEHOLD!! Heh! Heh! Heh! Jack: What was the purpose of this gathering, Webster? Mr. Webster: Come now Jack. Live a little. Heh! Get it? Live? HA! That's gold. Blake: *sigh* What a surprise, drunk again. Mr. Webster: Now, now ... *hic* ... my perception might be a tad altered, but my eyes are working perfectly. And what I'm looking at is one pretty picture. Fatbadger: Oh dude, did you get the new Playboy too?! The room goes silent as all eyes fall on Badger, causing him to slump back into his chair frightened and pop a few more Funyuns in his mouth. Mr. Webster: Not an actual picture, you stupid stoner. The four of you. The XUW team that I've entered in the PTC Survival Free For All pay per view. Heh. The team that will fail miserably in that pay per view, and *hic* make XUW look like the federation of losers that you are. Webster slams a fist on the desk. Blake rolls his eyes. Webster smiles and looks over at Badger who's sheepishly got his face buried in his bag of Funyuns. Mr. Webster: And not only will you lose, you're gonna lose ... Badger perks up like a dog hearing a dog whistle and pops out of his chair. Fatbadger: ... SPECTACULARLY!!! Woof looks annoyed and kicks Badger's chair into the back of his knees, causing him to sit back down and grab some more Funyuns. Mr. Webster: Heh! I used to hate that! Now, for some reason, I can't get enough of it. XUW is going down in flames in a spectacular fashion, and I'm gonna love every minute of it. Blake: Look Webster, I don't have time for this. I had to cancel a very important shoot this afternoon. Woof looks confused. Woof: No you didn't Blake. Tonight was when they were shooting the shower scene, and you had to be off set cuz of that court order remember? Blake looks embarrassed. Blake: Right. Thanks ... buddy. Blake firmly pats Woof on the shoulder, as Woof looks plenty pleased with himself. Woof: I mean, how could you forget? It was after you got caught putting that mini-cam in her toil-- Blake: YES! I remember Woof. Thank you! Mr. Webster: Pa *hic* pathetic, yet beautiful! This is the kind of thing I like to see in a team going down in flames: inner conflict. Blake: Look Webby, this is just normal banter between us here. You really don't know what you're doing here do you. I mean, you expect a team containing the best tag team in XUW history to lose? Mr. Webster: Yes, yes I do. And I'll admit I might now know what I'm doing, but I'll tell you what I do know: this group of nobodies are gonna embarrass this company beyond repair. Blake stops resting on the wall and takes a step toward the desk. Blake: Nobodies? Look around Webster. You've got three Hall of Famers sitting in front of you buddy! Webster slowly scans the room. Woof flips him off, Jack remains ever stoic, and Fatty just smiles and waves. Fatbadger: And me. Blake: Right, and him. Mr. Webster: Hall of Famers? You think I care about who the fans voted into the Hall? Bunch of *hic* internet geeks who sit around on thier computers all day and look for naked pics of Sheri? Webster peers down at his desk and quickly shuffles a few pieces of paper into a top drawer. He peeks around to make sure nobody saw that, even though everyone did. Fatbadger: Boobies!! Webster clears his throat and tries to make like he was going for a sip of his drink. Mr. Webster: As I was saying, I'd put more faith in a Florida senior citizen vote than some wrestling fans. Blake: Hey! You leave that smart humor to Woof! Woof nods. Blake: But, granted, the XUW fans are a pretty big bunch of morons, ya know, except for voting me into the Hall of Fame. So how about accomplishments? You're looking the best World Champion ever. Woof here is probably the best IC champ ever, and together we were the most dominating tag team ever. And I guess Jack won the World title a few times too. Fatbadger: Hey! What about me? Blake: What about you? Fatbadger: I was World champ once. Blake: Huh? Fatbadger: What? Jack: What did he just say? Blake: Nothing, don't worry about it. Fatbadger: Who? Jack: Did the tiny one just say he was actually World champ? Blake: Huh? No. Well, maybe. It was a fluke. Jack: If the Ripper were still dead ... he would be spinning in his grave. Blake: Nothing to get upset about, he was just an accidental co-champ. That doesn't friggin count. Fatbadger: Dude, it soooooooo counts. Blake: No way. You have to hold the title by yourself. Fatbadger: But you don't hold the tag titles by yourself. So are you, like, co-tag champs, uh, or something? Blake looks angry and flustered. Blake: What?!! No you idiot. Each "dude" on the tag team gets his own belt. Blake makes the finger quotes as he says "dude". Fatbadger: I had my own World title belt dude! Blake: No, you had half of a belt because you cut the greatest prize in out sport in half with a sword! Fatbadger: Yeah! So uh, you're saying you have have your own belt? Blake: Yes, finally. Fatbadger: You mean like the PTC Global Title? Blake: Yes exactl--, you little bastard. Badger laughs because he knows that his stablemate in the Boomer Sooners, Saij, did what Blake could not in winning the Global Title. Webster rubs his hands together. Mr. Webster: Excellent. Now *hic* Blake, I'm well aware of everyone's accomplishments. Thanks to Jack's little ... suprise ... entry to our little team here, I had to do a little scrambling, make a few quick deals. Let's just say that the most of the PTC heads like to do a little drinking too. That's how you ended up going against UWF in your opening match, guaranteeing that you won't be sticking around too long. They're unbeatable. Jack: Wait, did the Ripper hear correctly? Mr. Webster: That's right, unbeatable. Jack: No, about ... this one. The room quiets and cools as Badger slowly looks over his shoulder. Jack: This little one ... defiled The Ripper's belt? Jack's eyes focus on Badger, who only now realizes that there might be repercussions for what he did. Jack: The Ripper's ... blood ... and soul ... were on that belt. Jack finally moves from the wall, stepping toward Fatty. Jack: You will be finding yourself beside Raj Patel. Have you ever heard of Raj Patel? Exactly. Which is what causes Badger to react the way he does. Fatbadger: NOOOOOOOOOO!! Yeah, like that. Mr. Webster: Stop right there Jack! As much as I'd *hic* love to see this, you four will be going to the PTC pay per view ... unharmed. There will be no excuses when the four of you take an early exit and every other federation sees what a bunch of useless, pathetic curtain jerkers XUW has. Besides, Fatbadger here is the key to everything. You might have all been born losers, but he has made a career of it. Not only does he lose, he loses ... Badger perks up and ignores Jack for a second as he pops out of his chair and says on instinct. Fatbadger: ... SPECTACULARLY!! Badger looks around and again slumps back into his chair and goes back to cowering in the presence of Jack. Blake: Uh, all the more reason to let Jack tear into him. Mr. Webster: Simple. If he touches him before the pay per view ... I will shut down XUW. The three superstars ... and Fatbadger ... sit there stunned. Blake: Excuse me mate? Mr. Webster: You heard me *hic*. I might be bent on embarrassing XUW as much as possible, but the second things aren't going my way, I have no problem just shutting the whole thing down ... oh, and holding onto your contracts until they're expired. No more tv coverage means no more movie deals Blake. No more Boomer Sooners Fatty. No more Army of Darkness Jack. No more ... whatever it is you do Woof. Everyone exchanges glances. Jack steps back from Badger and steps toward Webster instead, hovering over him. Jack: The only reason the Ripper does not end you now is so that he can watch that drunken smirk fade from your face after he single handedly tears through the PTC. Then, and only then, will you receive a Ripsaw through this desk. Webster simply smiles. Mr. Webster: A toast then. Webster sloppily retrieves four glasses from his desk and slopishly pours some liquor into each one. He pushes them toward his team. Jack has already left. Blake scoffs and heads out the door. Badger, relieved that Jack is gone, has a few more handfuls of Funyuns before heading toward his locker room. Woof looks around and sees that everyone is gone. He thinks for a second and takes a seat on Webster's desk, grabbing the glass that's in front of him and taking a swig. Mr. Webster: Ahhh, cheers there Woof! Webster holds his glass up to Woof, who looks at it for a few seconds before putting his glass on the table. Webster shrugs and finishes his off. He starts to pour himself some more as Woof rubs his hand over the desk. Woof: Say Webster ... these sure are some fancy digs you got here. Mr. Webster: Top of the *hic* line. Everything is brand new. Woof: You wouldn't mind telling me where you ... ya know, scratched up the cash for all this? Mr. Webster: *hic* We're saving money left and right! Cutting out the show intros paid for this desk alone. Hell, with the money I'm saving on Vortex salaries, I'll be getting a new house! Heh! How about a toast to that! Webster holds up his glass again toward Woof, who just stares back with his hand on his glass. He picks up his glass and slowly pours it over the new desk, never breaking his stare with Webster. He gets up and walks toward the door, pausing and turning slightly. Woof: Toast is for Nazis. Mr. Webster: What *hic* what is that supposed to mean? Woof: I hate toast … and I hate Nazis. Woof turns back toward the hallway door, leaving Webster fumbling with some paper towels and trying to make sense of Woof … just like the rest of us try to do. Fade.
