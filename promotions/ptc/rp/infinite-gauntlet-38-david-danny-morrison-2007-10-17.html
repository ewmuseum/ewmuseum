Infinite Gauntlet 38 - David Danny Morrison

17 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To: PTC Representatives From: Chuck Flannery I am not the best talker in the world and I hate using computers, but I thought that it would be better to speak to you guys directly before bad news came your way. We have had dealings in the past. As much as I would love to have some PTC backing or talent on one of our shows, you never returned my phone calls. We sell merchandise including the DVD sets from the Golden Turnbuckle Tournaments, as well as some of the individual one off shows like the Archibald v. World show. So we do bring you guys some money, however small it is. But the thing is, I would like to get some PTC talent around. If you actually give this any thought, please get in touch with me. I spent five hundred dollars out of my own pocket trying to buy a spot in a round of the Infinite Gauntlet, as you well know. You said that I would have to wait until a certain time before I would get a talent in it. Now I sent to you the name of this competitor, as well as a tape of his work to show it was completely legit. Well, at a show in Charleston, IL, Richie 'Ripper' O'Connell suffered a ruptured MCL in a main event match. O'Connell has a bit of a buzz going for him, as he's been touring some across the US scene, and did a brief tour in Japan. I thought that I could get this kid some exposure to get him his big break, but apparently that ain't going to happen. I do not know how long he is out for yet, but I imagine it will not be for a while. But we had a transaction, and I would like to collect on it. I know Ripper was the talent I showed you, but I got another kid. He's probably good for a punching bag, to get the Illusionist over. I think this way, I give you some enhancement talent, he gets to do some moves, and we go on our way. When Ripper is able to go, I'll send another check out of my own pocket, even though it is hard to come by all things considered. The guy replacing Ripper is David Danny Morrison. I don't know how this kid got up here to Chicago, but he's got a bit of a drawl. He's from Alabama, so you might not want to put him on the mic. Then again, Wade Elliot has that country charm and it's working good for him in PRIME, who we also carry merchandise of. Morrison is a young kid, but he has some tools. He's only been doing this for just over a year, so feel free to rough him up a little if you want to help get Illusionist over. Hope to hear back soon. Chuck Flannery Great Lakes Wrestling Association
