An (un)pleasant surprise Part 2

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 Sometimes it’s quite ironic how the course of the future is. You would seem to think that Mr. Darkness is someone lurking in the shadows telling some pervert stories. But the opposite is trough. The eye of the media is fixed on Mr. Darkness. Every move he does is being closely watched by paparazzi. All of them waiting for a story about him. Nick Persia however was not once bothered by the press this week. First of all, because in Holland the sport is very small. Second of all because he isn’t that much of a star. Sure, he is having great matches in the Universal Wrestling Federation and has had great matches there. But the tournament was just that much bigger. So many wrestlers participate there that only the biggest and the best can get all the attention. And Mr. Darkness was now considered to be just that. He’s the favourite. The people are seeing a new star in him. He’s in the bright spotlight now. While Nick Persia is now the one lurking in the shadows, waiting to catch his prey off guard like a lonely, hungry wolf. When he isn’t with his nephew, of course. Nick Persia held his hand for the eyes of his nephew, guiding him into a big building. The iron door was pushed open by his right hand. Nick and Nathan walked in and Nick closed the door. He removed his hand from the face of the boy. The boy blinked once so he could get used to the sudden amount of light. Slowly, his eyes started to scan the area. It was a corridor, with a few pictures on the wall. A photo of the chief executive officer and a few other people who have worked there for a long time. Nathan started to walk but not too quick since he was eager to find out where he exactly was. They turned to the right. At the end of the corridor a black curtain could be seen. The young boy still took his time to take a good look on everything. As they came closer to the curtain the bigger his excitement became. He had a suspicion of what he would see behind the black curtain. A chuckle appeared on his face, revealing his content to Nick. Nick noticed the chuckle and was glad to see it. It was a sign he appreciated it. Finally, they reached the curtain and they walked through it. Nathan couldn’t believe his eyes as he saw all the seats. He was in an actual arena. His eye immediately went to a banner which reads “Primetime Central is proud to present GTT 4 Round 3.”. “Is this is were you are going to fight, Nick?” the boy asked. “Yes, this is the place,” Nick answered with a smile. “Cool, the ring is already been set up,” Nathan said enthusiastically. “It’s much bigger than I thought it was.” “Nothing is what it seems, Nathan! Always remember that, and you’ll do just fine,” Nick guaranteed him. “Can I get in the ring?” the youngster asked impatiently. “Sure, you can,” Nick said and the boy ran to the ring. He slides into the ring at all speed. Nick quickly followed him, but didn’t rush too much. The boy was bouncing from the ropes time after time until he finally stopped. “I just realised something,” Nathan spoke like he had seen the light. “In this very ring you are facing that no-good Mr. Darkness.” “Very true,” Nick admitted instantly. “And I’m sure we’ll have a great fight.” “I can’t wait to see it on television,” Nathan said full of enthusiasm. “Don’t you be so sure of that!” Nick exclaimed. “Follow me, Nathan.” The boy climbed out of the ring, after some struggling with the ropes. They walked towards the front row of the seats. Nick sat down on seat number eight and Nathan took the place right next to him. “Do you think you can see the ring good from here?” Nick asked. “Yeah, it’s really close to the ring,” Nathan replied. “Take that envelop from underneath your seat,” Nick instructed him. The boy dropped his knees on the ground and looked under the seat. With his hands he searched for the envelop as he couldn’t see it. He pulled it off the seat and showed it to Nick. “Well, why are you looking at me?” Nick asked. “Go ahead and open it.” The boy tore the envelop apart with barbarian methods, almost making Nick laughing out loud. But he knew to keep the laugh inside. The boy found a ticket inside. He started to read it, but didn’t understand all of it though. “Is this a ticket for tonight?” he asked full of enthusiasm. “Yes, and for the place where you are sitting right now!” Nick said with a bit of pride. “You didn’t actually think I forgot about your birthday?” Nick picked the boy up and gave him a hug. “I’m not a little boy anymore!” Nick laughed about this comment and put Nathan back in his seat. “I’m sorry, Nathan,” Nick apologised. “But that ticket is your birthday present.” “Cool, so I can see you kicking that bastard’s ass.” He continued with telling his thoughts. “Live and Loud.” Nathan started to laugh about his own joke. Even Nick appreciated this link to his own federation, UWF. “But watch that language, Nathan,” Nick warned him. “You know what your mother says about that.” “But she isn’t here!” Nathan replied naughty. “But I will watch it. Just like I am going to watch you. You are going to beat Mr. Darkness, right?” Although Nathan was absolute sure of that fact, Nick wasn’t. Not everybody has beaten Inphino Blitz. Nick was just the underdog. A biting underdog. “Hold your horses, Nathan,” Nick said. “He is a very good wrestler and has a lot of success in WWA! He’s been a Television champion there!” Nathan laughed about it before he reacted. “Well, what does the WWA mean in the wrestling world?” Nathan wondered. “When you compare it with a huge federation like UWF?” Nathan looked at Nick for a moment, and replied to his own question: “Nothing.” “But you can’t say Mr. Darkness is a bad wrestler just because he is in a lame federation,” Nick said. “It’s all the same for me. If he really was that good he could have worked somewhere else as well.” Nathan rambled on. “It’s in the bag already.” What can you say against that? That he shouldn’t put so much faith in him? It was kind of flattering to hear it. Above all of that, it felt good that someone believed in him. “Of course it is,” Nick agreed. “And after the match they also know you are my nephew I am afraid. I think this will be a night to remember.” For the seven-year old it was as Nathan was still staring at the ring. His pupils were dilated after seeing all of these amazing things. Nick just smiled for the boy, but inside he knew what lay ahead of him. A future that is full of pain. Mr. Darkness would do anything he can to beat Nick Persia. He would do whatever he has to. But Nick would do more than anything. Everything that should be done in his opinion will be done. Nick was now just taking a big deep breath before the storm. Because when the match would burst out tonight there will be no salvation for either. Nick Persia would do anything he can. Nick Persia is in this to win it. Mr. Darkness will have his own motives. Good for him. But to be honest, you aren’t going to make it with best intentions and your own motives. You can compare all you want. But in the end, you can only compare two equal products. Nick and Darkness aren’t just equal. Although in Nathan’s mind there was no doubt, we mustn’t forget something. Never say never.
