Being Sick Sucks.... Being sick totally sucks, the sneezing, the congestion, the blowing of my nose and everything else in between makes this experience totally anal… Date: Monday, April 11, 2005 Time: 10:00 a.m. Place: Kansas City, Kansas Where: My House My head was pounding; my nose was sore and was running all the time almost like a faucet at times. My body felt like an incubator for every known virus. I was not in the mood for this tag match, but we had committed to it. I have to go out there and prove why Wrestling Midwest deserves more recognition than we usually get. This tag team title match will do just that, it will be a chance for the whole world to see what a real team like Soul Purpose can really do. I know that Unholy Matrimony is probably the early favorite, simply because of the name Seymour Almasy. I know that everyone is so impressed by the fact that the former two time Global Champion is now in the tag division. It is so weird that Almasy is that selfish that he wants the tag belts too. I have nothing but admiration for him, but I have to say that is very greedy to try and capture the tag team belts. He wants to be some kind of greatest hit I am sure. He wants everyone to remember him for his accomplishments in the divisions and all the tournaments that he has won. I guess winning all those tournaments proves something to himself. Some kind of self gratification, making himself a legend in his own mind might actually transcend everything else. I understand that in his own fed he is just mediocre at best. Being mediocre is ok to; there is nothing wrong with that. Therefore, if you lose Seymour I am sure that there are plenty of little tournaments that you can enter yourself in and continue your path to self righteousness. I sneezed and grabbed some tissues and wiped my nose. “Owww, my nose…” It hurt so much; I know that if someone punches it, I will scream my damn head off. Maybe I will sneeze on them too, would serve them right for hitting me when I am sick. I shivered as if I was could even though I was bundled up under a bunch of covers and just laying around watching TV. Being in the tag tournament had also drained me. My match with Luther Jackson against Dess and CMack did nothing short of drain me physically and emotionally. I had done that match extremely sick. I was told by my doctor that I should not have even wrestled and that I should have taken some time off. I could not do that; Noah Hanson does not miss a show and does not “no-show”. That is simply not an option, I came to the tournament to try and become a three time champion and that was what I set out to do no matter what. I wanted to be something more than just a wrestler that was inducted into the Hall of Fame just because everyone else felt sorry for me. I honestly hope that Dess really did not mean that because I never wanted to be inducted into the Hall in the first place and if that was the reason I was inducted than I would rather they take my spot and shove it up Garvin’s ass. He was one of the original fighters in WMW and he actually deserves to be there more than I do. Whatever, I guess being in the Hall means that I am some kind of legend…A LEGEND KILLA! Seymour, make no mistake about it this is no damn video game. I know that you have some sort of weird fascination with Final Fantasy. I hate that damn game it eats ass, give me a good survival horror game any day not some bullshit game that has dancing bitches that are just badly ripped off from the Matrix as far as the wild bullet time moves. I really hope that Seymour does not think that his smoke and mirrors crap will actually work on someone of my talent and charisma. I will exploit every weakness and every flaw that he has. He makes a mistake; I will make him pay dearly for it. He needs to be better than he has ever been and will need to be on the top of his game for this match. Hell, he will need to be better than the best he has ever wrestled. He will need to elevate his game to a whole different level. He will need to tap into the deepest, darkest recesses of his soul and then reach just a little bit farther before I will concede any victory to him. I may not have ever held a world title in two years of being in WMW, but I have become the wrestler that no one else wants to face. I can rattle off the names of people that have underestimated me and have paid with their WMW careers. Guys like Jackson Dre, Matrix, Jeff Hardkore, Justin Evers, Stevie Vertigo, Scott Vyper, The DT and Micky Burling they all fell at my hands, some have made it back and some have disappeared from WMW forever. The difference between them and you is that I plan on wiping you from the very plane of existence of Prime Time Central itself. I plan on ridding you from any tournament that will surface. Seymour make no mistake this match will be a war like none you have ever experienced before… “I need a drink…” I mumbled as I pressed my button that called for a servant. A few minutes later Kristina arrived wearing a very sexy French maid’s outfit. My eyes sort of bugged out and she had my attention… “How may I serve you master?” She asked, as a seductive smile slithered across her mouth. “Master…” I kind of laughed at the thought, but she looked real good in that outfit and I was slightly aroused by her desire to turn me on even though I was sick. “Well, you could bring me a soda and my lap top. And then later maybe you could sit on my lap.” She giggled as she bent over revealing her panties to me. “Yes master, your wish is my command.” “Well, we will have see about that…” I said as I wiped my nose with some tissue. I sneezed again and this time it blew straight through my tissues. “Ohhhh, poor baby so sick.” She remarked as she placed her hand on my forehead, feeling for my temperature. “You feel really warm, I am going to go get the Tylenol and see if that helps any.” “Honey…” I remarked. She turned and looked at me, smiling the whole time. “Yeah babe, what can I do to make you better?” “Just having you here is doing wonders for me. Thanks, I really am lucky to have you.” I said, forcing a smile out of my germ infested mouth. “Sweetie, it is no big deal. I would do anything to make sure that you are healthy as possible. Maybe next time you won’t go down to Global, because ya know that is where ya got sick. Hell, ya never know what they have down there. They always say don’t drink the water, hell there must be more than that in the air around Global…” She said as she patted me on the head and then kissed me on the forehead gently. “Yeah, I guess sometimes ya have to learn first hand. Next time I will heed your warning.” I said as I coughed a few times and then took a sip of my apple juice. “How about I bring you some Orange juice too. They say that OJ is better for you when you have a cold like this!” She said as she started to walk out. “Oj never helped Ron Goldman…” I said as I was trying to be funny, but even as I said it I knew it wasn’t really that funny. “That was horrible…” she remarked as she rolled her eyes and the flipped her ponytail over her shoulder. I watched as she walked out of the bedroom and I picked up the file that I had acquired on the tag team champs. They call themselves Los Machos Muchachos or something like that hell why not have a name that rolls off the tongue a little better than “La Cucha Racha”. All I know is that one of them is called White Lion and other is El Para De La Pascado or something like that. God, I have always hated luchadores just for the simple fact that their names are always something that sounds fucked up and I can never get it right. The fact remains they defeated the team that I thought would have been able to hold onto the title for a long time. I thought “Click, Click Boom” would have been the dominate tag team like the Metallo Zords or The Souls of the Black Lotus. Teams that can truly dominate are very rare, but that is what I thought Tessa and Karina were. I was really impressed by their ring skills and it would have been fun to fight them. However, the Los Grande Impalas ended the reign of the best chick team to ever grace PTC. It really is too bad to have such a successful team break up right after their loss, guess it shows that PTC is full of posers and pretenders. Throwing together a team just for the sake of saying “I won the PTC tag belts!” is shameful at best. Being sick like this had also made me very irritable and touchy. I was angry at the fact that Unholy Matrimony had slithered themselves into a title shot like proverbial snakes in the grass. I can only hope that Seymour’s little wife or whatever she is can handle herself in the ring because this match is one that I will not hold anything back. I really hope that Los Chapos Muchachos are capable wrestlers and not just some silly luchadore flying around the ring and not even able to stay on the mat for five second scams. I will do whatever I can to ground those two from being able to fly around the ring even if I have to go as far as giving them this blasted cold I have… I kept coughing and sneezing and then saw Kristina open the door again. She was carrying a large glass of fresh orange juice and a bowl of chicken noodle soup. I could see the steam rising from the bowl and I could smell it kind of. I mean my nose was pretty stuffed up, making it very hard to breath. “Look what I have for you my love!” She said with a long smile on her face. “You’re always taking good care of me….” I said with a weak smile. “That’s my job, babe…” She said confidently. She placed the bowl of soup and the glass of juice on my small table next to my bed. She then walked back out and returned a few minutes later with my laptop. I flipped it open and then powered it up, I was going to head over to the Core website and see who I would be facing in that too. Figured I might as well do some homework on whomever I was to face at the Atlantic City show. It would be my first ever match on Core soil, so I wanted to make sure that I knew what I was getting myself into. I looked over the card and saw my name across from Core legend Rzarector. I had to admit it was a very big challenge, Rza is a very large obstacle but one that I am willing to face head on. I don’t know much about him, but that is why I have to study him much like I have to study my opponents in the tag team match. That match will have to wait till I can give it my full attention and at the moment that is not possible. “You need to get something in you some juice and some food. Need to get something in you that will do you some good.” She remarked as she pushed the bowl towards me. I leaned down slightly, looking at the bowl. It looked good I can say that, so I grabbed the spoon and took a few crackers and then crumbled them into the soup before I took a spoonful. The warmness of the broth was soothing to my throat, it felt great. I took a spoonful and then another and another and soon the bowl was empty. I took a few sips of the juice and then placed the glass back on the table. I then sneezed real hard, almost felt like I had dislodged something from my head. Then I got this sudden rush of pain in my temple, almost like when you get a brain freeze from eating ice cream to fast… “You ok sweetie?” She asked as she sat next to me. “I dunno, suddenly I got this tremendous headache.” I said as I grabbed the side of my head. “Man it hurts so bad…” Kristina then grabbed a bottle that was nearby and popped it open, handing me three tablets. I looked at her strangely… “What are they?” I asked. “Tranquilizers…” she replied. “To help you get some sleep. Because you need it baby.” My eyes felt heavy, like sandbags were sitting right on my eyelids. I was feeling very drowsy; I was getting tired of listening to Almasy. He was so kind to point out that he did in fact beat me in the P.I.T. Yeah he did that was like over two years ago or something, all I will say is that I am a very different person than I was when you beat me then. In addition, you can relay this message to your little trollop of an ex-wife that I will, without a shadow of a doubt get plenty of offense in this match. Almasy I see what kind of game you are playing, you’re trying to get into my sickness filled head. You should be careful though, getting inside of my head is a very dangerous thing to do. You see Seymour my world is far more dangerous than any RPG that you have ever played. My mind is a death trap into itself, it has a vice life grip and when it closes it jaws on you it will not release you till you are drained emotionally. I could feel the sweat rolling down my forehead; my fever must be breaking from the way I felt. The bad thing is my fever won’t be the only thing that gets broke when I get my hands on the tag team champs. The thing is they rely probably on a lot of high flying moves, being sick and all I have not really had a chance to do much scouting. For once I guess I will have to learn them as I go, not a real big deal but I usually like to know the team that I am facing beforehand. This will be a great match make no mistake Los Muchos Macho Mascadas and Unholy Matrimony this will be a match of biblical proportions. Like Moses, Adam and Eve and Noah’s Ark this will be a match that should be recorded for the future to see…. “Now if I could just get over this cold…”
