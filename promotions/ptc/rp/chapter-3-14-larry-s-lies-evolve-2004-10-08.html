Chapter 3.14: Larry's Lies Evolve

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 Sometimes lies can come back to bite you in the butt once the truth is revealed. But what happens on those odd occasions when the person who lied didn’t really know he was lying. Is it still considered a lie? Or is it just over looked and forgiven as it may have been based on bad information in the first place? But what happens when that lie puts people’s lives in danger. For instance what happens if you invade a country based on a lie……. …. “Who is going to help a poor lady with her bags,” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom bellows while standing at the doorway. “I’ve been here for a full two minutes and none of you four have even volunteered to help me out. Four strong young men, albeit one of you is in a penguin suit, standing there in front of me and not one of have volunteered to help me. What is wrong with the world today? Where have all the gentlemen gone?” “Paris, France?” remarked Bailey. “What did you say young man?” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom responded with her voiced raised. “umm, nothing….just that I will gladly help you with your bags.” Bailey glances over at Danyon and Larry before walking to the door way of the New Royal Suite and helping Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom with her suitcases. “That’s very nice of you. It’s nice to know that my son Larry is hanging around fine young gentlemen, it’s just unfortunate that he isn’t enough of one to help his own mother out.” Such was the scene a few days ago in the New Royal Legion Suite located at the Howard Johnson Hotel in downtown XUWville. This had taken place before Larry the Legionnaire was to wrestle his second round match in the GTT against Cyrus Skar, which somehow inexplicably Larry won. That is only Larry’s 4th win of his career and the 2nd without the help of the New Royal Legion. Despite the protests of Bailey and Danyon, Larry went on to fight his matches in the first and second rounds of the GTT. The days leading up to Larry’s second round match were much like the scene where Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom first arrived. She was always on Larry’s case about every little thing. Nagging him and nagging him relentlessly. It was probably the best that Larry left after a day to go to Washington to fight his match as he could not stand being around his mom any longer. The strange thing about when Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom was there was that not once did they discuss wrestling or anything related to wrestling. Also, not once did the topic of her supposed death from tuberculosis come up in a conversation either, which seems really odd given that Bailey and Danyon only found about it just before she arrived. Although, no one really ever claimed the New Royal Legion were normal anyways, after all they do have a cult following of one million and one Legionnaires. ____ The door to the New Royal Legion Suite slowly opens up a crack and Larry the Legionnaire peek’s his head around the door and looks in and around the room to see if there is anyone there. Larry just arrived home from Washington where he won his 2nd round match in the GTT. However, much like the previous round he didn’t tell Bailey or Danyon about this. He wrestled a match for only the 2nd time in his career without their supervision and miraculously he won. Larry also didn’t bother telling his mother about the match, instead just choosing to leave without notifying anyone. Unfortunately for Larry, there were some people in the New Royal Legion Suite as his mom caught him peeking through the door crack. “There’s my favourite son,” she calls out. “Now where have you been for the past few days? I’ve been worried sick about you. Next time you take off like that tell me where you are going.” Larry reluctantly comes into the room with his head hanging down in shame as if he disappointed his mother. Slowly, he brings his head up and looks at the people in the room. Standing in front of Larry were Bailey, Danyon, his mother and his best friend Stripes the Penguin. “Sorry mom, I’ve been out of town on ….umm…business for a few days, next time I’ll tell you where I’m going. I had to head to Washington for a few days.” “That’s okay. I’ve just been worrying to death here on where you could be. I thought you may have injured yourself or something. Luckily your friends here assured me that you were fine. You have some nice friends Larry, I’ve gotten to know them pretty well over the last few days since you vanished.” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom smiles as she looks over at Bailey and Danyon in the room. “Although, this young one here in the penguin suit doesn’t really talk too much. He stands there all day long and doesn’t say too much. I don’t even know how he can stand being in that suit all day long as it must get so hot in there.” A puzzled looks comes over Larry’s face as he looks at his mother and at Stripes. “Mom, that’s a real penguin……..” “That’s what your friends tried to tell me Larry but why would you have a real penguin hanging out with you?” Larry looks over at Bailey and Danyon with a confused look on his as he really didn’t know how to answer that question. Luckily Larry didn’t have to answer that question as Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom decided to keep on talking. “Enough of that Larry. The real question is where did you get that black eye Larry?” “Oh, this,” Larry answered pointing to his black eye. “I had it the other day when you were here. It’s nothing new. I just uh… fell the other day, that’s all.” “I know you had a black eye Larry the other day. You had a black eye on your right eye but now it is your left eye that is all purple. What happened? Did someone beat you up Larry? It’s okay you can tell your mother this……..” Larry is taken back a bit by this comment as he realized that indeed his new black eye is on his left eye and not his right eye from before. He looks over at Bailey and Danyon for help with this as the last thing Larry wants to do his tell his mother that he was wrestling. However Bailey and Danyon are not offering any advice in this situation as they themselves are trying to figure out exactly how Larry received the black eye. Bailey whispers something in Danyon’s ear and Danyon nods. Bailey turns back towards Larry and asks, “Did you say you were in Washington Larry?” “Umm yes… that’s where I was.” Bailey looks back over at Danyon and nods his head. He then turns his attention back towards Larry and takes a deep breath and lets it out so that everyone can hear it. “You continued on in the GTT didn’t you Larry?” “Wwwwhy..” Larry stutters out his answer, “why would you ask that?” “The black eye…..Washington for a few days…….. Last time you wrestled you came home with a black eye. I warned you about what could happen if you wrestled Larry. You are lucky that you came home with a black eye only. You could have died in the ring Larry. You’re not immortal you know that. Larry when are you going to listen to our advice and pull out of the GTT before you get seriously injured?” “I’m not…I told you this. I want to win this thing. I’ve two matches so far and I am sure I can win another.” “Larry you are playing with the odds here. Every time you step into that ring the chances of you getting injured are slightly greater as the competition gets tougher and tougher as it goes along.” “No way, Bailey, I think the sky is the limit for me. I have pulled some upsets already as everyone out there is underestimating me. But I’ll show them what it means to be the greatest Antarctican wrestler of all time. It will be the last time that they underestimate anyone or anything from Antarctica,” Larry proudly responds. “Wait a second here,” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom interjects in the conversation. “What’s all this talk about wrestling and tournament and Antarctica? What’s going on here?” Danyon looks over at Bailey and mouths “oh crap” as he just realized the reason why the tournament was never really discussed around Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom. To her, Larry is still a lawyer and never lost his mind believing he was from Antarctica. Now, however, things have changed. Bailey, Danyon, Larry and even Stripes the Penguin all exchange glances in the room as no one really knows what to say next. “Umm….” Danyon is the first one to give it a try. “Mrs……” but then he pauses and leans over towards Bailey and says something that only Bailey can hear. “What is Larry’s last name?” Bailey’s eyes widen as he realizes that he doesn’t know it either. “I have no clue.” For almost two years Larry has been associated with the New Royal Legion and apparently not once did it ever occur to them to ask what Larry’s last name was. To them he was always Larry the Legionnaire. Danyon leans back away from Bailey and bites his lip as he pauses before continuing on, “like I was saying, Mrs……Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom, that Larry here has decided to take up wrestling and is wrestling in the most prestigious tournament there is. Against our better judgement might I add.” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom stands up out of her chair with a look of disappointment on her face as she looks out at her son Larry. “I spend all this money on you to go to university and to the best law school in the nation and you go and throw it all away on being a wrestler. Is this the kind of thanks I get? Is this anyway to treat your mother? Here I was thinking you were in Washington arguing some sort of case in Washington but now I find out you were really wrestling. You are breaking your mother’s heart here Larry.” Dumbfounded, Larry responds, “Best law school in the nation? There are no law schools in Antarctica.” Danyon looks over at Bailey once more and says “oh crap.” He then turns his attention in the direction of Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom. “Ummm Mrs….” Danyon pauses once again and thinking that he really does need to find out Larry’s last name. “Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom, umm… this was probably something that we should have said earlier to you. Larry, for one reason or another, neither of which we know ourselves, believes he is from Antarctica and is a professional wrestler. And if I may add once again, we had nothing to do with this. He just woke up one day and believed he was from there.” “That’s not true Danyon,” Bailey interrupts. “I may inadvertently have something to do with it.” “Oh?” “I seem to recall a few months ago I joked that Larry needed a new gimmick and that he should pretend he was from Antarctica and a wrestler. I never thought he would believe it. He woke up the next day believing it and since then he’s been living that life with total disregard for reality.” “Isn’t this interesting,” quips Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom. “When did this chapter start? Is there anything else I should know about?” “According to Larry you died of tuberculosis…………” Danyon says and no sooner did he finish did it clue to him that perhaps he should not have said that. “Dead? Larry said I died from tuberculosis? Does it look like I’m dead?” “Not to me it doesn’t…..Mrs……… Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom,” Bailey responds quickly. Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom stands there with her arms crossed across her chest and looking none too happy. “So are you telling me Larry for the past two years you’ve been down here hanging out with these two bumbling idiots and a guy dressed in a penguin suit?” “Hey now I thought you said we were fine young gentlemen,” Bailey wonders out loud. Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom turns towards Bailey, “I lied.” “Fair enough,” Bailey responds. “Larry,” she continues on without missing a beat, “I’m disappointed in you. When you told me two years ago that you were going to leave Toronto to be a lawyer for two men that were being discriminated against by their employer, I thought it was admirable and that’s why I let you leave. Now I find out that for the past two years you’ve been chasing some dream of wrestling. Is this what has happened?” “If I may say something in his defence,” Danyon politely interrupts. “It really isn’t the past two years that he’s been wrestling. It’s only very recently. For the first year or so he was our lawyer and a good one at that.” “Although, Danyon, there was that one point where we forced him to wrestle as we were on strike,” Bailey reminds him. “Oh, good point Bailey. I forgot about that. So besides those few matches he wrestled, which are over a year ago, he was our lawyer.” “And there was that PTC Cruiserweight Tournament that he entered too because I forced him to,” Bailey says. “We can’t forget that one as remember he got screwed out of the victory.” “Right, and the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament,” Danyon adds. “Like I said besides that tournament and those matches over a year ago, he really hasn’t wrestled.” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom stands there not looking any happier shaking her head. “Are you two done yet?” “I think so,” Bailey answers. “So, Larry, what do you have to say for yourself?” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom demands. “I’m confused about everything. I’m from Antarctica. I’m a professional wrestler from Antarctica. I don’t know what you are talking about in regards to going to law school and being a lawyer,” Larry answers with a puzzled look on his face. “If this is some plan by you guys to confuse me and my past it’s not working. I know my past and my history unlike some other people. I was born in Antarctica. I grew up in Antarctica and survived the great storm of 1978 when we lost power for over two months and had to live in the cold dark. I remember all of this. It happened despite what you guys say.” Danyon takes a step towards Larry and asks, “What about your mom Larry? You said she died. If she died then who is that right there?” Larry remains silent for a moment as he stands there thinking about who exactly this lady was in front of him. “It’s my mother ………but…..she died……well maybe she didn’t die……. maybe she just moved out of town. A lot of people moved out of town after that tuberculosis outbreak. She could have moved. We never did have a funeral for her or anything of the sorts. I just assumed she died as I never saw her after the outbreak.” “Oh brother,” Danyon says shaking his head in disbelief. “This is getting entirely silly. Sorry about this Mrs…….. Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom. Bailey and I are at fault here, we should have let it get out of hand. It was fun at first but now it’s gone too far and someone, probably Larry will get hurt.” Danyon walks up to Larry and puts his hand on his shoulder and looks at him in his eyes. “It’s okay Larry. The game can end. You’ve proved your point about being able to wrestle now by advancing two rounds in the GTT but you should pull out now before you get seriously injured.” A dumbfounded look comes over Larry’s face as he looks back at Danyon. He tilts his head so he can see the rest of the room and looks over at his mom and then at Bailey, both of which are nodding in agreement to what Danyon said. Larry sighs, “but I have no clue as to what you mean by game. I’m a wrestler from Antarctica.” Danyon takes his hand off of Larry’s shoulder and raises them in the air, “I give up. This is hopeless. Larry, if you want to be in the GTT then fine. You can wrestle. But if and when you get injured don’t come crying to us as Bailey and I have warned you plenty.” “Why would I get injured? I’m a professional……..” “Yes Larry we get it. You are professional wrestler from Antarctica,” Danyon says obviously flustered from this argument with Larry. “Listen Larry, stay in the tournament but do us a favour. If not for us, do this for your mother, quit the Antarctica bit. You are a lawyer from Toronto.” “I’m still confused as to why all three of you think I’m a lawyer. I’m from Antarctica and became a professional wrestler.” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom walks up to Larry. “It’s painfully obvious Larry that you are under a lot of stress. I think you should relax and come home back to Toronto.” “But…but….home isn’t Toronto. It’s Antarctica.” “Larry, this is getting awfully repetitive,” Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom says. “So, are you saying that you don’t remember Antarctica mom?” “No, Larry, I don’t remember that. I’ve never stepped foot on that continent in my entire life and why would I? Canada is cold enough as it is.” “But…but…but…..”Larry stammers for a second…….and then he hangs his head down looking at his feet. Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom turns and walks away seemingly satisfied that Larry will stop portraying this character and go back to being a lawyer. “I can prove it,” Larry says as he brings his head to look at everyone in the room. Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom spins around. “What did you say?” “I can prove it to you that I’m from Antarctica, despite what you all I believe.” Danyon, Bailey and Larry the Legionnaire’s Mom all look at each other. “And just how do you plan on doing that Larry?” ask Danyon. “By taking you all to Antarctica. I’ll take you to where I grew up to prove that I’m really from Antarctica.” “I repeat my question again, how do you plan on doing that Larry? How are we going to get to Antarctica?” Larry pauses and reflects for a split second, “on my snowmobile.”
