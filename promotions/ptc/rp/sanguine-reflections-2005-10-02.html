Sanguine Reflections

2 October 2005

Date: 2005-10-02 The darkness is like a living blanket of entwined, shadowed ghosts writhing with perpetual torment, waiting eternally for light to shine upon them and release them from their torture while the rain smothers the city like a pillow being held oppressively over a sleeping enemy’s face. Moving through the darkness like an embodiment of the malady that plagues the street, Deszaras hates the town more and more with every step, and hates himself for being in this position. The Resident Evil was a successful and feared warrior, until his rivals and enemies clubbed together to usurp his power. They almost succeeded, too, in driving him out and tearing him down from his throne, but he managed to destroy them and outlast them. However, his body was broken. His mind was ravaged. For what seemed like decades he wandered around looking to pick fights with anyone and everyone, deciding that everyone on Earth was guilty for his sufferings. He charged emptily into any battle he could find, looking to punish the entire world for the sins of only a few. Worse still, he meandered through the world lacklustre and bored and angry, little better than a delirious and rabid arch deviant, until by some twist of fate his path crossed with a certain woman… one that destroyed his mind. His obsessions grew with each passing day, fixations on pain and payback and of other, more malign emotions. As his mind dwindled, his maliciousness augmented and the loss he felt from the things that kept him calm made his insides roil. Too many times his necessities, ambitions and desires turned to ashes in his mouth, and too many times has he had to turn his stony countenance backward and inward to look for answers that should only come from without. In the end everything succumbs to pressure. Even the strongest of minds can only take so much corrosion of emotion. ~ Law two in the Code of the Hammurabi states something along the lines of; “If any one brings an accusation against a man, and the accused go to the river and leap into the river, if he sinks in the river his accuser shall take possession of his house. But if the river proves that the accused is not guilty, and he escapes unhurt, then he who had brought the accusation shall be put to death, while he who leaped into the river shall take possession of the house that had belonged to his accuser.” [Note: Don’t understand what we mean here, kids? Tough shit. Think about it or join the queue.] Deszaras sits alone in his house, but never lonely. Always his mind screams at him whether he wants to hear it or not. His whiskey isn’t strong enough and there are no lights on, making the room as dark as the soul of sin. Dhar sits on the kitchen counter behind him singing ‘You are my sunshine, my only sunshine…’ softly. The malignity in its eyes and the smirk on its mask can somehow still be just as clear in the gloom. ‘You make me happy when skies are grey…’ ‘Get fucked.’ says Deszaras coldly. ‘Ouch!’ shouts Dhar theatrically, jumping from his perch. ‘Still haven’t finished feeling sorry for ourselves, have we? King of only that which is rotten.’ With an almost ethereal purr, the phone rings behind him, yet he lets it ring several times before he can bring himself to stand up. His chair croaks as it is pulled backwards and then it stops and is silenced like a victim as his throat is sliced and butchered. Deszaras answers the call, finally, dispassionately. ‘What?’ he says, ever so enthusiastically. [Note: I know what you’re thinking, you’re thinking “and he wonders why he has no friends.” aren’t you? Well… fuck you.] ‘Yes, Klaus, I- what? No, no, it’s not evil spirits, mate… it’s just rain. No. No, you can’t- …okay, good luck trying to kill them.’ Nonchalantly he hangs up the phone and sits back down to his disappointingly anti-climactic double-whiskey. His desire is to get drunk, not marinade his fucking steak with the shit. Yet he casts his mind back, and away from such disgruntled thoughts to earlier in the night when it was still young… ~ “If a judge tries a case, reaches a decision, and presents his judgement in writing; if later error appears in his decision and it be through his own fault, then he shall pay twelve times the fine set by him in the case, and he shall be publicly removed from the judge's bench, and never again shall he sit there to render judgement.” So states law five in the Code of the Hammurabi. “Take It Easy” by the Eagles laments over the juke box while a bar full to bursting with hillbillies and rednecks talk loudly, shout, laugh and more. Yet sitting in the corner, as per usual, knocking back beer after beer in the darkest and most reticent corner he can find, Deszaras’ look is cast down upon the table before him as if he dares not lift his chin for fear of providing his usual pall of strength. He feels that here, in this place, doing this activity, he shouldn’t need to. Yet he always does, somehow. For whatever reason he needs always keep his game face on. And why? Childish peers. Brainless rivals. How often must he listen to narrow-minded, hollow-headed men and women talk about his systematic physical destruction? How often must he be forced to listen to the same uninteresting portentous threats that are never realised in actuality? For too long has he been subjected to this, and made to sit through such drab comments. For too long. And for too long he has been getting laughed at by two ugly piece of shit at the bar as they look back and giggle like horny schoolgirls at him. Wearied by this, he decides to go to the little boys room to relieve some tension from his loin department. [Note: Yes, his crotch. Shut up.] He enters the lavatory and sighs at the familiarity. Stained, graffiti-ridden walls, doors hanging off their hinges and unlockable, vomit on the floor… and the smell of cheap disinfectant. Standing over the urinal, reading a message on the wall talking about how skilled some unknown girl named “Tanya” is in the act of fellatio and then stating her phone number and bra size, he begins to take his well-deserved piss. During the exploit, the two schoolgirls from the bar appear through the door and stand at urinals either side of the hero of our story. Smirking with an imbecilic smugness betraying drunken confidence, and too much of it, they begin to talk some serious shit around him. ‘So,’ begins the taller and fatter of the two morons. ‘You think this small-cocked freak-job’s seen the shit written about that bitch of his in stall three?’ ‘That fuckin’ Kiri slapper?’ The adipose fucker laughs. ‘Yeah, that goo-’ The snap and crack of the door as it explodes outward sends a soothing calm through Deszaras’ body as the fat fuck falls through it, sending the entire bar into a shocked serenity. Meanwhile, back in the toilet, the other of the two men, shorter, less wide and more shabbily dressed tries to cover his face with his hands as Dez’s fist slams down upon him time and time again. Over and over. Relentless. Merciless. ‘You are my sunshine…’ sings Dhar. ‘…My only sunshine…’ Punch after punch. ‘You make me happy…’ And then all fades to nothingness as Deszaras’ feral rage overtakes him. ‘…When skies are grey.’ ~ Law twenty-six of the Hammurabi. “If a chieftain or a man (common soldier), who has been ordered to go upon the king's highway for war does not go, but hires a mercenary, if he withholds the compensation, then shall this officer or man be put to death, and he who represented him shall take possession of his house.” So he simply sits here now, resting his punching knuckles. Listening to the singing.
