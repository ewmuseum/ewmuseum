All Up In My Blood

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 [UWF Headquarters. Chicago, Illinois.] [Two men sit within the thick haze, a haze that fogs not only their vision, but their thought. The two men sit at a desk, racking their brains… pushing their mental capacities to their limits. Smog pours from their lungs and fills the room… the men wallow in their own cancer and despair… the time of their death is fast approaching.] Man #1: "Bah gawd…. Ah can’t believe this is happenin’…" [The smoke in the room is so think that all that can be seen of each of the room’s inhabitants are the glaring reflections off the lenses of their glasses… that and the glowing red tips of their cigarettes. They speak in gruff voices, belched out from their sandpaper throats.] Man #2: "I knows it… I knows it… After all these years… it’s finally over…" [He tapped his cigarette lightly over a small ceramic tray… a pinch of ash danced as it fell.] Man #1: "Aftah all these years… all those memories… all those great moments… the Universal Wrestling Federation is closing." [Two dark men appear at the doorway. They walk swiftly, yet silently towards the center of the room. They are the jackals. They squat down… pick up the desk… and carrying it off, out of the room, and into oblivion. One of the reminisces now holds the ash tray in his hand and sets in on his lap. He takes a long drag from his cigarette… the kind of drag that had only previously been performed by Clint Eastwood and Bill Hicks] Man #2: "You know… this place deserves to go out with a bang… something that the world of professional wrestling can remember it by." Man #1: "Yah sayin’ that the last ten years didn’t mean jack shit to yah?! The legendary matches… the rivalries… the blood, sweat, ‘n tears that we all put intah this company… it didn’t mean shit!?" Man #2: "No, no! You misunderstand me… look… the UWF was the pinnacle of professional wrestling for… how many years… and it was great, it really was… it was the HIGHLIGHT of wrestling… but now, it’s about to be over. Yeah, we’ve got farewell show coming up… and I’m sure there’ll be reunion shows… but really… in its purest and truest form… the UWF is dead. And even though it lives on in the memories of the fans that shelled out their hard-earned dollars for a little bit of violence and entertainment… they’ll fade away too, in time… so what does that leave us?" Man #1: "Leaves us… with a…. myth." Man #2: "I’d say about so… you think the rest of world gives a damn? The world of wrestling is moving on… it’s turning into something ugly… it’s not about competition… it’s not about the personalities… it’s about catering to snot-nosed GEEKS! Have you watched any wrestling on television lately?" Man #1: "Can’t say that Ah have… Ah can’t even bare the thought of being involved with another promotion." Man #2: "It’s complete horse shit, Mack. HORSE SHIT. The same cookie-cutter brownnosers licking the assholes of the same dumb ass prima donna cocksuckers. It’s not trying to put on a show for the masses… it’s not trying to be the best it can be… it’s catering to a minority of stuck up little white geeks wearing pampers. It’s not professional wrestling… it’s HORSE SHIT." Man #1: "That’s right… we had wrestling’… we had it all… we had a chimpanzee." Man #2: "Say what you will… but it made sense in context. You think any of those cybernetic twits gave a second thought to what the chimpanzee meant? Or how it got there? Or WHY it was there? Hell no…" Man #1: "It meant a whole lottah lawsuits added to the legal laundry list." Man #2: "The show was doomed when people stopped watching and started listening to some little shit with a keyboard and the delusion that his opinion mattered… and he only told half the story." Man #1: "Ah get it… but what’s the point?" Man #2: "We’ve got to show those motherfuckers what the world lost when UWF closed its doors… We’ve got to show them what they missed when they formulated their opinions from some other ass-hole without a clue… and we got to show them that the UWF was the best damn wrestling promotion in HISTORY!" Man #1: "GTT?" Man #2: "GTT…" [The jackals return… hungry for more… but alas… the room is now empty… save to the two chairs the men are sitting on.] Man #1: "Fuckin’ repo ass holes…" [The jackals usher the men out of their chairs and lifts the seats. They quickly exit the room, warm ass-grooved chair under their arms.] Man #1: "Well… Ah don’t see no problem in puttin’ on a show the people will never forget at the GTT… Who we got?" Man #2: "Ha… who you want?" Man #1: "You kiddin’ go with the PTC tournament hosses! Seymour Almasy! ‘The Renegade’ Rich Rollins! They can’t be beat!" Man #2: "Almasy’s wrestling under a GCW banner… And Rollins for PRIME." Man #1: "Sonuvabitch… Well… how ‘bout… the active roster?" Man #2: "All booked up with independent shows or enjoying their premature retirements… except Sledge and Axel Action… they’re in." Man #1: "It’s a start… but they’re only Television title level! They ain’t proven on sucha big scale!" Man #2: "I know… and we’ve contacted Digital Mortality, and he’s agreed to sign on…" Man #1: "Good… good… and?" Man #2: "Erm… Plucky the Cock Fighting Duck." Man #1: "He won his qualifying match?" Man #2: "Pecked both the guy’s eyes out and gave him a mean case of Salmonella…" Man #1: "And that’s all we got?" Man #2: "Who else is there?" Man #1: "Tsunami Oni! He‘s the best in the business!" Man #2: "Retired! Crippled by Kenny Mercury, surely you remember that… can barely walk, last I heard." Man #1: "Gawd damn it! That bastard! Oni was the best this company ever had… why, if it ain’t for that Kenny… Mer… cur…y…" Man #2: "No, no… we can’t. We just can’t!" Man #1: "And why not!?" Man #1: "He’s working for XUW! He’s their T.V. champ! XUW owns the rights to the Kenny Mercury name now… it’s IMPOSSIBLE for Kenny Mercury to wrestle for UWF. PERIOD." [The jackals return… they pluck the smoldering butts of the cigarettes from the debating men’s mouths… they strip them of their shirts and pants… they take the lint from between their toes. They leave.] Man #2: "That’s right… KENNY MERCURY can’t wrestle for UWF…" [The man takes a deep breath… the room’s haze tumbles into his lungs… swirling through each of his lungs and flowing through all his veins. He exhales.] Man #2: "But I know someone who CAN." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Kenny Mercury. Six foot one inch and two hundred forty-five pounds of man chiseled from marble, bathed in molten gold, and dried with the silver. Hair like the fleece of Aries… biceps like Pop-eye’s triceps… triceps like Pop-eye’s triceps… buttocks like the pistons of a Maserati… and all created with a divine precision… one that only Kenny Mercury exhibited.] Kenny Mercury: "SUCK… MY… NADS… YOU OVERGROWN… DICKWART…" [And the mouth of a sailor. Forgot that.] Kenny Mercury: "I HOPE YOU GET A RUSTY TROMBONE FROM A EBOLA INFESTED WALRUS TITTIED WITCH AND YOU DIE FROM ANAL EXCAVATIONS BY A TEAM OF MICROSCOPIC PROTIST ARCHELOGISTS DIGGING THROUGH YOUR ASS, SEARCHING FOR YOUR BRIAN!" [Sailors on PCP.] Kenny Mercury: "Scrotal-remora…" [Kenny Mercury storms out of XUW corporate headquarters. For reasons unexplained, and just accepted… Kenny isn’t wearing a shirt… and is wearing his black and red wrestling tights and boots… though he’s not booked to wrestle, nor is he even near a ring. The XUW Television title rested comfortably around his waist.] Kenny Mercury: "Fucking Konkey Dongs… WHY THE HELL would HOT water come from the WHITE spout… WHITE IS THE COLOR OF COLDNESS! Ice is white… Snow is white… SO WHY ISN’T COLD WATER WHITE?! NINCOMPOOPS!" [The twin glass doors of XUW Headquarters close shut… Kenny stomps along a concrete path that runs perpendicular to the street. Just then, a rust-colored van pulls up and abrupty stops right in front of Kenny. Kenny nearly walks right into it.] Kenny Mercury: "Get this piece of shit vehicle… OUT OF MY WAY!" [The door slides open and inside is middle aged man wearing a brown sports coat and aviator sunglasses.] Van-Man: "Kenny Mercury, I presume?" Kenny Mercury: "The same Kenny Mercury also known as the ‘Shooting Star’?" Van-Man: "Yes." Kenny Mercury: "The same Kenny Mercury also known as the ‘Technical Wizard’?" Van-Man: "Yes." Kenny Mercury: "The same Kenny Mercury also known as the ‘Ring Doctor’?" Van-Man: "Yes." Kenny Mercury: "The same Kenny Mercury also known as the ‘Submission Exhibition’?" Van-Man: "Yes." Kenny Mercury: "The same Kenny Mercury also known as the ‘Sultan of the Ottomatically-You-Tap-Out Empire‘?" Van-Man: "Er…" Kenny Mercury: "The same Kenny Mercury is the VERY DEFINTION OF PRECISION?!" Van-Man: "YES!" Kenny Mercury: "Yeah… That’s me." Van-Man: "Good, we’ve been searching for--" Kenny Mercury: "I am indeed the shooting star, the technical wizard, the ring doctor, the submission exhibition, the caliphate of come-get-some, the VERY DEFINTION OF PRECISION… look it up if you don’t believe me… I’M KENNY MERCURY!" Van-Man: "Yes… got that part…" Kenny Mercury: "What it is that you want, peon?" Van-Man: "Well… I’ve heard that you are a man of taste…" Kenny Mercury: "Only the taste of victory." Van-Man: "And that you have interests in… physical preservation." Kenny Mercury: "Of course I have interests in it! I’m a god! I’m DIVINE! I don’t age… my body doesn’t deteriorate… there’s no sign of wear or tear on me… I have interests in it so far as for me… my body is naturally preserved. I’m KENNY MERCURY. I’m IMMORTAL." Van-Me: "Right, but… have you ever heard of…" [The man leans forward, his head peeking out of the doorway… the aviators staring blankly into Kenny’s eyes… peering deeply into the vast emptiness of Kenny’s soul.] Van-Man: "Complete cosmetic blood transfusions?" [Kenny’s eyes light up and he leans towards the man.] Kenny Mercury: "Are you SERIOUS!?" Van-Man: "Yes… I thought you might be interested…" Kenny Mercury: "I’ve always wanted to have that done… like Keith Richards… I hear it can lengthened your life by like… twenty years!" Van-Man: "But I thought you were immortal?" Kenny Mercury: "Can’t hurt to have a few spare years to tag onto infinity, now can it? Where do I sign?" Van-Man: "Just get in the van." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Man #2: "You cannot be serious… that plan won’t work! He ain’t gonna just… step right intah the back ovah stranger’s van!" Man #1: "You obviously don’t know Kenny Mercury… He’s a glutton for new-age beautification shit… the man’s ego is the size of his hair. Like Dee Snider. He charts the advancement of his cuticles, for chrissake." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Kenny is lain out on a thin flat table, strapped down securely, in a small, immaculately white room. Dozens of clear tubes weave in and out of his blood vessels and attach to a large tank that sits behind the table. Kenny Mercury smiles as he stares up a the ceiling lights.] Kenny Mercury: "Alright, jackillacs… let’s get this show on the road… I’m not getting any prettier! … or am I? In any case, my blood… it feels old! I need new blood! This old blood sucks. Can my new blood be green? I always thought that’d be cool. Or maybe just replace my blood with mercury, so when I’m in my match I can bleed REAL mercury… that’s not poisonous, right?" [Kenny is ignored. Suddenly, his red blood begins to flow through the tubes… exiting his body…] Kenny Mercury: "Whoa… it’s working!" [A new substance runs down a portion of the tubes and into Kenny’s body.] Kenny Mercury: "Ya know… New blood doesn’t really FEEL like blood… I guess I’ve just been missing out on the joys of fresh blood… Are we in Switzerland? Not that it matters… I just heard you could only get this done there… Hey, you sure blood is supposed to feel like this? Wait a second… that’s NOT blood! THAT’S…" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Man #2: "100% pure Tetrahydrocannabinol." Man #1: "Pure T.H.C.!? You’re gonna replace his blood with pure T.H.C.!?" Man #2: "Precisely." Man #1: "Won’t that KILL ‘em?!" Man #2: "Perhaps… but damn it… that’s just one risk I’m willing to take. We can’t let the UWF’s GTT hopes ride entirely on the backs of a Puerto Rican and a waterfowl." Man #1: "But Anatidae make great wrestlers!" Man #2: "Yes but… the public just WON’T get that… We need someone they CAN get… we need to get someone they can relate to… sorta… we need someone they can look up to… kinda… we need someone who can WIN GTT6… But most importantly… we NEED to get Kenny Mercury HIGH AS FUCK!" Man #1: "Mah Gawd… You’re absolutely right…" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Kenny Mercury: "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! YOU MOTHERFUCKING SONS OF BITCHES! YOU GUANO SUCKING TIT MOUSE CHOADS! I HOPE YOU EAT ELEPHANT TAINT IN HELL YOU CUNT-BUBBLES! FUCKING TWAT CRICKETS! YOU BASTARD ASS RECTAL SMURFS! I’LL GET YOU FOR THIS!" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Man #1: "So where’d you find the old ring attire?" Man #2: "At the bottom of an five year old box of zebra-cakes in Kenny’s pantry." Man #1: "Motherah Gawd… he kept dirty wrestling tights in there?" Man #2: "Among other things…" Man #1: "Well… let’s see if it worked…" Man #2: "Right… Kenny! Kenny, can you here me?" [The men circle around Kenny’s body, still strapped to the table… the tubes are gone, as are his old clothes… instead Kenny’s body now bears purple tights and boots… and his long, beautiful golden locks are hidden behind a violet mask… His eyes slowly open, and he looks around the room, in a state of confusion and bewilderment.] Man #1: "Kenny, can you hear me? Man #2: "Can you feel me near you?" Man #1: "Kenny? …Kenny? … KENNY!?" [He sits up.] Kenny Mercury: "Whoa, maaaan… I once knew some dude named Kenny, maaan… he was all like… pissed off about bat poop or somethin’, man… and he smelled like flowers. Cleanliness is Godliness and God is Kenny… or somethin’, man… I dunno what ever happened to him though… Hey, what‘s with the unexplained bondage, dudes?" Man #2: "We’ll untie you… but quickly! What’s your name!?" Kenny Mercury: "…My …name?" Man #1: "Ah’ll give you a hint… The Shooting Star!" Kenny Mercury: "Er… you mean… The Haze-man?" Man #1: "The Technical Wizard!" Kenny Mercury: "The Master of Haze-aster!" Man #1: "The Ring Doctor? The Submission Exhibition?" Kenny Mercury: "The Haze-Meister! The Professor of Haze-Onomics!" Man #1: "A local term for monarch followed by some obscure and ludicrous compliment for yourself!" Kenny Mercury: "Whah…?" Man #1: "The Definition of Precision!?" Kenny Mercury: "What’s a definimition, man? The Haze-ster is confused and would find it very cool if he was to be released from the clutches of said binders… if not… that’s cool, too, man. Purple Haze abides." Man #2: "Did you hear that?" Purple Haze: "The simultaneous cries of millions… of… Chicago… Bears… fans… WHOOOAA! DUDES! DUDES!" [Purple Haze, with a burst of unforeseen strength and energy, RIPS through the straps and leaps onto the table top.] Purple Haze: "THE HAZE-MEISTER CAN FEEL IT! THE VIBRATION FROM GEORGE HALAS’ SUBTERRANEAN GRIN! THE BEARS! THEY’RE… THEY’RE ON THEIR WAY TO THE SUPERBOWL! RIGHT ON! RIGHT ON MY BROTHERS! GO AND VANQUISH THE FORCES OF EVIL AND RIGHT THE WRONGS OF THE PAST, DUDES!" Man #1: "This guy… is weird." Man #2: "Yeah, but as I was saying… did you hear what he called himself… Purple Haze! Purple Haze LIVES! Do you know what this means!?" [Purple Haze reaches down into his purple speedo-esque tights and fishes around with his hand, tongue out in concentration. Aha! He’s found whatever he was searching for… Purple Haze pulls out a zebra cake from his crotch.] Purple Haze: Equus zebra… delicious maximus, man!" [Purple Haze scarfs down the age old groin cake.] Man #1: "It means Ah’m gonna be sick?" Man #2: "No! It means that Purple Haze can compete in the GTT under the UWF banner! XUW might own Kenny Mercury… but they don’t own Purple Haze!" Man #1: "Ah think we mightah been better off without ‘em…" Purple Haze: "The Secretary of the Haze-on wealth wishes to know what year it is…" Man #1: "2006." Purple Haze: "Whoa, man… let’s do the time warp again, man!" Man #2: "Alright… now let’s get him to registration…" Man #1: "And what if they piss test ‘em?" Because the Haze-man doesn’t… That’s probably the last thing the Haze-man would want to do… ya know, man? Because violence against women is wrong, man… and the Haze-man would never do such a thing… that’s not righteous, man… no… no it’s not. The Haze-o-rama would rather like… not do that, ya know, man? You thought the Haze-ster was going to let Haze-amania run wild… but nah, dude… that’s just like what the Haze-man wan’t you to think… April fools, man. Yeah. Snoogins." [The End.]
