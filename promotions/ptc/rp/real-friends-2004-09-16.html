Real Friends

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 [Jonathon Banner is in his promoter’s office. Mike, his promoter, walks in and finds Banner in his chair.] Mike: Okay buddy, the name on the door says Mike Jerkins. Now unless by some far off chance that’s your name too, your ass better get out of my chair. [Banner swings the chair around. He’s smiling at Mike.] Banner: Mikey, this is a nice feeling. I mean I could find myself doing this some day. Mike: Very funny Jon boy. [Jonathon gets out of the chair, as Mike goes and sits down. He takes a drink of water.] Jonathon: Mike there’s no easy way of saying this, so I’m just gonna blurt it out. I need time off. [Mike chokes on his water, as he spits on himself.] Mike: Wh-why-Why? Jon. You-you can’t do this to me. I mean last week, last week you just won the heavyweight title. I mean your the core of my promotion. Jonathon: I know, I know. That’s what makes this so hard. Mike: Your telling me?! Jonathon: Mike, I’ve talked to a wrestler and he think it’s the best thing for me to do. See I just got accepted into the GTT tournament sponsored by Primetime Central. I mean this can be huge for me. I’m gonna need all the time to train for the wrestlers I’m gonna be competing against. Mike: But Desert Storm Wrestling. I mean, this is great for you, I know this is like world wide stuff. But this little promotion, DSW has it’s little following too. I mean just because we don’t even air on T.V., we’re still a real promotion and. . . Jonathon: Mike, no. Man, this is a great place. But I told you last week I was sending in my resume’s to some other promotions. Mike: I know, but I mean, you just won the title and everything. It’s just sudden, that’s all. Jonathon: I’m sorry. I worked hard for this title, but this is a shot of a lifetime, so I have no problem dropping the title at our next show. [There’s a long pause. Mike is certainly not pleased about this. After Mike let’s out a couple large sighs, he stares at Jonathon.] Mike: Alright Jon boy. You’ve earned everything here in DSW. I can understand this. Jonathon: Great!! Great Mike. Thanks a lot. [Banner gets up and heads out of the office as Mike looks on. he leaves, shutting the door and is confronted by a wrestler and a girl right away. The wrestlers name is Jackpot and the girls name is Sarah. She gives Banner a hug.] Jonathon: Hey! You don’t even know what he said yet. Sarah: Sorry, we were listening thru the door. Jackpot: Congrats again man. Jonathon: Thanks man. Jackpot: Hey man, we were talking to the guys in the locker room and. . . well just don’t let them give you any grief. Jonathon: What? [Sarah glares at Jackpot] Sarah: (under her breath) Jackpot, be quiet. Jackpot: Listen man, you’ve got to know. Your young. What, 25 years old? DSW is your first wrestling experience. And we have fun but we ain’t the big boys. Guys get jealous. And people don’t think you got what it takes. Jonathon: What are you talking about? Jackpot: Just don’t listen to them. You get in this tournament and do your best. [Jonathon looks at Jackpot, a little confused.] Jonathon: Okay. [Jonathon shakes hands with jackpot and heads down to the locker room. He goes inside to get his bags and all the wrestlers are in there, kicking back before the show tonight. A locker room full of conversation, quietness falls over them all as Banner walks in.] Jonathon: Sup fellas? [No one answers. He shrugs it off and heads to his stall. Then one guy stands up.] Wrestler: Banner! So you win a little gold and head for the hills huh? Jonathon: Trent man, it’s not like that. Trent: No, it IS like that! Kid, your just a little punk that’s gonna get eaten alive out there! You think your some kind of God send or something to this sport. Your heading up there and facing true, high profile professionals, that get paid millions of dollars to get in front of the crowds. Jonathon: Alright, I’m not the oldest or wisest, but. . . . Trent: Damn skippy you ain’t the oldest or wisest. I’ve been there man. I love this sport. I’m 51 years old and I’m here now, making 50 bucks a night, performing in front of crowds as little as 25 people, because I love this!! You? Your just some young son of a bitch thinking you can conquer the world. [Jonathon’s grabbed one of his bag, but immediately drops it. and turns face to face with Trent.] Jonathon: I don’t think I can conquer the world, but I do feel I’ve earned respect. It might just be a little respect but I’ve earned it here, doing what I’ve been asked and beating guys. I beat you! Trent: Banner, I’m double your age. If you didn’t beat me, then you don’t even belong in this business. Your good, don’t get me wrong. But your small time. And your gonna get your ass handed to you. [Banner turns around.] Jonathon: Whatever man. [Just then another wrestler, Steve Johnson, stands up.] Steve: Banner, do you even know who your facing? Jonathon: Not yet. Steve: Well we do. Latch Key Kid from XUW. That’s a premier organization and he is a multi-time tag team champion. Jonathon: Chill out, I’m gonna do my research. I’m not going to go in this and . . . . Steve: Kid! Face it. You shouldn’t even bother your time in this thing. They are just gonna eat you up and spit you out. [Jonathon’s grabbed his two bags and starts heading toward the door.] Jonathon: Gee’s guys, thanks for the pep talk. You know, I know I’m an underdog in this but this is a uphill battle I think I can do well in and. . . [Another wrestler stands up.] Wrestler: Banner!! Once you leave don’t bother coming back. Because after your done in the tournament, your gonna be a nothing, a no one, a has been. All at 25. [Banner drops his bags and gets in the wrestlers face.] Jonathon: You know what? I could give a rats ass what any of you guys think, especially you! I guess I was blind for a long time but I’m starting to see that each and every one of you aren’t friends. Your all wrong and I’m gonna prove it to you. Jackpot told me about this. I should’ve known you’d turn on me just because this good thing’s happening to me and not to you. I’m taking my shot and running with it. You can talk all the crap you want about me. Start all the rumors and I’m sure you already have. And I’m sure those wrestlers in XUW, Neon, NGW and anywhere else are going to look at the little brackets next to my name and see “free agent” in there. They’re gonna see it and think ‘oh here’s some little no name rookie of a wrestler that’s gonna it ousted in the first round.’ Well I’m gonna prove them all wrong. Wrestler: Whatever. Get the hell out of here and go get your ass handed to you, you little bastard! [Just then the emotions break loose and Banner lunges at the wrestler and they are rolling around on the ground punching each other. Finally the wrestlers break them up and pull them apart. Only a couple wrestlers keep Banner from the wrestler as the rest stay on the other wrestlers side.] Jonathon: Fine. I see how it is. I called you guys friends not just one week ago. Heading to this tournament is the best thing for me I guess. Because when your friends betray you, all you got left are strangers. [Jonathon picks his two bags up again and walks out, slamming the door behind him. He stands there by the door and all of a sudden, his head starts twitching. He’s trying to keep steady, but his head twitches side to side, as he looks around to se if anyone’s there. no one is.] Jonathon: N-n-n-no, the-th-the-they’re not-not--n-n-ot right. I-I-I-I c-c-c-can-can-can win. [Banner starts to grumble as he reaches into his pockets and pulls out a little bottle. His hands are shaking as he undoes the lid and swallows down about four pills. He puts the bottle back in his pocket and he’s still twitching, as he walks down the hallway. The twitching subsides as he walks out into the car garage.] Jonathon: I know I can win. [He walks away to his car and drives off.]
