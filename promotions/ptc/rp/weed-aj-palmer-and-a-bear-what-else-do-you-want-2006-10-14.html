Weed, AJ Palmer, and a Bear…what else do you want?

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 Intro Sometimes it takes a little for me to wake up in the morning. I like to stay up late, not really doing much. Training, watching some film, most of the time smoking a joint or two. Recently though, the mornings have been much better. It has been a lot easier for me to wake up and enjoy the moment. Living the life I used to live, the life of a new school mobster, killing and stealing at will. When you had the money in the bank I did, anything was possible. Now though, things have changed. The life I left behind me seems to stay with me though, the friends I left have not forgotten the 'switch' I made. I say it's for the better, I say the life I have a head of me is nothing but pure, nothing but roses and sunshine. I have to thank one person, a person I care for deeply, but I don not completely trust yet…. Chapter 1: Not so good morning With my former partner and best friend, Angel Gennaro, hot on our trails trying to get back at me for what he considers a back stabbing. We decided to go someplace that he would never know of. Hell, a place no one would know of unless they needed to go directly there. Maine, the most northern part of the United States. Known for it's good pot and cold winters, a place you can really go and hide. I had never been there before, Ashley's family had a house there we were going to stay in. The spot could not have been better. The road leading in was a dirt one lane road that almost never is used. A hand full of houses were scattered up this steep incline which went for a little more than two miles. Her house was the on at the very top, standing two stories over looking a giant blueberry field the seemed to blend in with view of hills lasting for miles. Thus the name of the road…Blueberry Hill Rd. We lay in bed on that cold fall morning, fire going in the fire place and Ashley's half naked body tight against me. The fur blankets seemed to make a warm seal around us, not allowing us to leave. "Wow," Ashley began "I love nights like that." I smirked and yawned, cracking my jaw. "What can I say, us Italian's are animals in the sack." "Oh, YOU don't have to tell ME that." Ashley replied, nudging my ribs with her elbow. "What a place," I began "No way Angel's gonna find us….." "Shhhhh" Ashley butt in, putting her finger to my lips. "You need to relax, no more thinking about past issue's. You're a Global Wrestler…the Platinum Champion, on to bigger and better things." She was right, I knew deep down that ditching Angel like that was the best thing, it wasn't like I actually ditched him anyway. I went up to him like a man and told him I wanted out. I told him I just wanted to be a wrestler now and nothing else. HE'S the one that took it to far, he's the one that should be running from place to place. But he doesn't have a chick to think about, he doesn't care about someone besides himself. "You really need to stop thinking about that." She said, reading my mind. "Babe, you got Johnson in a barn coming up, you got the GTT6 in just a few days, your wrestling career is about to soar." "You know you're right Ash." I said rolling on top of her and brushing her dirty blond hair out of her face. "You and me girl, we make a good team." Ashley kissed me on the cheek "I think we'll always make a good team." I knew where she was going with this, she always wanted to talk about love and try and get me to use the word, I acted as quickly as I could. "Better get some early work out in…" "Noooo," Ashley wined "Not yet, come on we were talking." "Yeah but like you said, I got a lot of matches coming up." I went on, picking up self up off the bed and stretching out my arms, feeling the cold dead air in the room. "Wanna be nice and ready for them." "Why do you always gotta avoid the subject?" Ashley said, sitting up and wrapping the blankets around her. "Avoid what?" I said, acting like I didn't know what she ment while pulling on a heavy black 'Globalite' sweat shirt. "You know exactly what Anthony." Ashley said in a pissed off tone. I grabbed a silver and black backpack, trying not to make eye contact with Ashley. "Be back in a couple hours, think I'm gonna take advantage of the country and go for a hike, got the weed?" Ashley just glared at me from bed. "I got some on me…" I finished rushing out the door. Chapter 2: What a hike Outside wasn't much warmer than the inside, walking through the frost covered blueberry bushes, I could see my breath. Made me really glad I grabbed one of me beanies to cover my head. This hike wasn't really for training, actually this had a lot to do with me needing to get out and figure out things. For those of you that don't know, I've been diagnosed with Depression, you know, the chemical imbalance shit in your head. In my opinion it's a bunch of shit, damned if I needed a pill to make me happy. It was a great time for a hike though, walking down the rocky trail really relaxed me. It may sound weird to some, but after growing up on the beaches of Southern California, the woods of Maine really helped. Never in my life I had been up this way and it just amazed me. The red, yellow, and orange flowers made the trees look like they were on fire. Different from the Palm Trees I was used to. Hiking, I found out real quick, was not easy. It was just a simple stroll down the road, no, not here. The trail I followed seemed to go both up and down hill at times, when your there though it seems more up then down. The terrain was very rough, ruts running along each side. Giant rocks and pot holes just waiting to snap an ankle. Finally I decided to take a rest and a small smoke break. I sat down along side the trail, leaning my back against a giant oak tree. By this time the sun had came up more and being in the warmth of it made this a perfect moment. I reached into the small bag I had brought with me, pulling out a baggy full of fresh pot. The one thing that always seemed to make me at ease. In this case, Sage, a beautiful blue colored bud with a very fruity smell. As I set the bag on my lap, breaking up the buds, the thoughts seemed to flood my head, not only am I "depressed" the quacks say, but I also of a touch of ADD. Something that makes it very hard for me to focus on things, this is where the pot comes into play. It seems to help a little, but still worries and doubts fill my head from time to time. I had a lot to worry about that day. Entering into a the sixth edition of PTC's Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Something I had always seen happen but never had the guts to enter till now. Eight years in the game and finally I decide to really test myself, why did I wait so long? I have wins over some of the people in this thing, I should have believed I would do better. Rob Johnson, that name comes into my head a lot to. The man whom I have had two matches with and both being ties. Only the second one being for the Global Platinum Title, making us Co-Champions? Bullshit, I know I'm better than that crazy bastard. In a couple days we face off in a barn match? What the hell was is that? The in-ring problems where nothing compared to my outside though, a girl I had deep feelings for, the first in a very long time. Some reason I just couldn't bring myself to believe in the feeling I was having, something was telling me to hold back. I didn't know what to do, I knew how she felt. I could just feel it and see it in her eyes. I know how I feel too, just, don't want to let her know that. I have never been in a big time relationship, Ashley really had gotten to me. And the worst? By cousin and best friends for life, Angel Gennaro, a man I used to trust is now on my case trying to kill me. A subject that hurts very badly and I really try not to think about. One last deal, that's all he said, then the knife went into my back. Setting me up like that, planning to kill me in the middle of no where. Well there's one thing I will promise right now, AJ Palmer kills no one from now on! Well, by this time the joint was half way smoked. The THC really seemed to calm my restless mind down. A slight wind came into the air which brought me out of a zone, and it was a good thing. Just as I turned my head, looking down the trail, I saw a big black object strolling my way. I wasn't sure if it was the weed or what, but at that second I would have sworn up and down I was just seeing things. No way in hell was I seeing a…..BEAR! My heart jumped into my throat, I had never seen a bear and here I was about to come face to face with the thing. He was still far away enough, I grabbed my head and slowly slid around to the other side of the tree, never taking my eye off the animal. He was big, REAL big, a black bear. He was searching for something, I could tell from the nose being on the ground moving quite quickly. He got just in front of the tree, a spot where I could see him perfectly, when he stood up on his back legs, opening his mouth and letting out a hugh roar. I'll admit I was scared shit less, right then and there I made my peace with god and hoped Ashley would be alright. I knew I was dead, I knew I had my last smoke. I watched the bear as he came down, sniffing and snorting on the ground. He took a couple steps back and let out another roar, which interested me. He didn't know I was there, if he did he would have made a move by now. No, he was more interested in something on the ground. Very slowly, I inched my way to the other side of the tree, trying to focus on what the bear was looking at. Another roar lit up my ears drums, making my heart pulse just a little bit faster. Then, I realized what it was. A small hint of white smoke was coming off the ground, or should I say…off the joint. In my haste to get around the tree, I must of dropped the joint on the ground. He must have been a distance behind me the whole time. Then when I began smoking and the scent from the joint must have attracted the bear. I bit my lip to stop from laughing, how was I to know smoking a joint in the middle of the woods would do this? What seemed like an hour of watching this bear sniff and paw at the joint was really only about twenty minutes. After the first five it wasn't funny anymore. When your in un familiar woods, hiding behind a tree to avoid a black bear, twenty minutes IS a long times! Finally the bear did in fact lose interest and keep walking down the trail. I didn't move an inch until I was perfectly sure he was gone. When I was though, please believe I grabbed my bag and ran. And experience like that really makes you just want to get home to what's important, and I knew exactly what that was….Ashley. Chapter 3: Love over Lunch When I came home I snuck through the back door, I still wasn't ready yo talk to Ashley and seeing that bear really had my nerves on edge. I knew just what to do to calm them down though… "Ahhhh" I let out, eyeing my dark green bong. I wasn't sure where Ashley was at the moment, so quickly I snatched the bong and headed into the down stairs bathroom. I wasn't afraid of her catching me smoking, hell sometimes the girl smokes more than I do. I just needed some time to relax and hide out. Like I said earlier this place was amazing, the house she lived in and the place her parents gave her when she died. To her it wasn't just a house, but to me I could live in the bathroom. Phone, stand up shower, his and her sinks. I mean the damn thing even had a flat screen in the corner. A tub you could swim in, I mean I came from money. I've had all this, but in Maine? A place where you'd find naked hillbillies and sheep loving farmers. I turned the shower on, hoping it would make Ashley think I was busy AND steam up the room helping out my high. I took one giant hit, letting the smoke and steam expand in my lungs. It felt good…too good. Knock Knock "AJ, you back already?" Ashley shouted through the door. "Yeah.." I said startled and trying not to cough. "I'll be out in a second…" "I'll be out on the balcony." Busted, maybe turning the shower on wasn't such a good idea. I took another hit, trying to cash out the slide. The hit was big, thick and yellow. The kind of hit that crosses your eyes and lets you know you had enough. I turned the shower off, letting the extra water drip in my hand so I could wet my hair. Had to make it look right. The balcony was off the Master Bedroom. It had an awesome view, suspended on the second floor over looking to mountains and the fields. A place Ashley likes to come during the fall and look at the colors in the trees. "Good hike?" Ashley asked as I shut the glass door behind me. "Yeah," I chuckled "Real good hike." Sitting down I see she had lunch already. Some grapes, one of my favorite. A couple sandwiches and a good glass of milk. She likes to keep me eating healthy, knows its important if I want to live my dream in wrestling. Things like that really make me wonder why I don't just let go…trust one more time. "I love this place.." she began, looking out over the mountains. "Always seems to relax me." I looked up at her over my sandwich, the wind softly blowing her dark blond hair. I knew then, biting into a fresh cucumber sandwich she was the one, sounds stupid, sounds weird…but cucumbers made me realize I love this Ashley Woods. A couple minutes went by, me still eating and Ashley still watching over the view. I finished my sandwich and popped a grape into my mouth, leaning back in my chair. "What's wrong?" I asked. "……." "Look, I know I avoid certain things." I began looking down at the table, "Just, when you lived like me…things never come easy. And this…." "Has been easy? FOR WHO?" Ashley snapped "Every time we get into a real conversation, you bail on me AJ. You back away." "I know, I know…" "No you don't know, you don't know how it makes me feel. I want to be loved AJ, I want some one to be there for me…to be HERE for me. And you…you just hide." I didn't know what to say, and she could tell that. Staring at me with those beautiful green eyes, and still I try to hold back. Why? "I…I…" Nothing came out. "What AJ? What? Talk to me for once, please!" She begged me. "I Love you ok? It's not that easy for me you know?" I said standing up and walking over to the railing looking over the view. "My past is bad, very bad. I've killed, I've kidnapped, I've extorted. Hell, there's things and people I don't even remember. And just like that I tossed it away, over night I went from a killing machine to a wrestling machine…YOU don't know what THAT'S like." "AJ, I don't care about those things…" "Yeah but I do, you need someone whose kind…gental. Someone who's a good person, not an animal." "Is that what you think? That I deserve better than you? Are you kidding me? The man who lights up a room, the man who's taking Global by storm. The fucking Platinum Champion no less. AJ, you're every girls dream. You got a great GREAT personality now that you left Angel and Joe. You NEEDED to do that, it gave you life." "Yeah, life can be taken away very easily, with them lurking out there. Following us around, hunting us like deer. You can do WAY better.." "Hush AJ," Ashley said, sneaking behind me and wrapping her arms around my waist. "AJ we're gonna be fine, Angel will give up…eventually. YOU are going to be amazing, and we…." I turned in her arms, looking her in the eyes and holding her face with my hands. "WE will be forever…" Chapter 4:In The Air Tonight That night I couldn't sleep. Usually after hours of love making I sleep like a log. Tonight, my feelings were getting the better of me. For almost the first time in my life I was feeling good. I was standing tall amongst SOME of Global's superstars, GTT6 was inching it's was toward me and I was geared and ready for that. Not to mention the best thing in my life, the love of a beautiful woman. The night air was crisp, cold, but tonight it didn't matter. Shirtless, smoking a joint out on the balcony…nothing could touch me on this night. I was on top of the mother fucking world! In my hand held another path in my wrestling. Another chance to taste victory and challenge myself against the greatest fighters in the world today. An annual tournament, one I have watched for 5 years but never had the guts to try, this year…this year it's different. This year AJ Palmer makes his mark among the PTC. The white envelope had my name on it, the official PTC stamp on the back. I held it sense yesterday, not wanting to see whom I was against. "Now or never" I mumbled to myself, butting to joint on on the wooden rails and tossing it into the breeze. As I watched the joint sale, something caught my eyes in the darkness. I stuck the letter into the waistband of my silk pajama pants, leaning forward and looking out into a field. Something was definitely coming, no…someone was coming. Very quickly but trying to be un-seen at the same time. One name came into mind….Angel Gennaro!! Every time I get a high, no pun intended, but every time I get a high in life, the man feels like cutting my nuts off. Some friend huh? Well, ex-friend you could say now. I wanted to be cautious, not really let the figure know I was on to him. Very calmly I stretched my arms yawning, letting out a mist from my breath. Took one last glance, watching the man as I walked back into the door, shutting the glass behind me. Once inside I knew I didn't have much time, Ashley was fast asleep. Sex and weed really take the girl out for a night. No time to put a shirt on, I simply sprint toed my way down the stairs. (Sprint Toed is like a running tip toe, ya know?). Instead of ending in the living room, I took the long way around and came out in the kitchen. A perfect view of the field, although by now I had lost sight of the figure. Being in the kitchen, I had plenty to choose from for protection. My first instinct of course was for a good sized butcher knife. Quickly I grabbed it, looking out into the field before realizing which I had chosen. "No killing…" I whispered to myself glancing around the kitchen. My eyes fell on a carved walking stick leaning beside the door. It was about three to four feet long, plenty of strength still left in it. Will the stick in hand slowly I opened the door to the outside, searching franticly around me for any sign of movement. Nothing, all I could see was shadows bouncing around from the trees and the full moon. A bad night for hunters, but shitty if your the one being hunted. I crouched, slowly making my way to a big rock just outside the fields. The cold air made it hard to breath, something I would just have to deal with. To my right I could see an airport, which would explain the constant noise heard from above. I reached the rock, able to stand a little and pear around looking at the field. Nothing at first, my eyes needed more time to adjust. Just off to the left I saw the man, he had lost sight of me as well when I went back into the room. My heart again was beating rapidly, about ready to burst out of my chest. This was the feeling that kept me going, the feeling that let me know I'm alive. I laid on the cold grass, crawling alongside a old rock wall. The figure was stuck behind some bushes, not sure which way to go. I could just barely make him out as I made my way past the wall and into the field, the blueberry bushes scrapped and poked at my bare chest as I tried my best to navigate around them. The figure had begun it's movement also, ironically moving directly at me. Seeing this, I stopped, looking more like a snake ready to attack it's prey. The figure was about ten yards away from me. I could now make out the figure, it was a man…a rather scrappy looking man. No doubt one of Angel's goons sent to take me out yet again. The man, dressed in black leather, held a small pistol in his right hand. Another sign of Angel, the man always liked guns for some reason. When the man got about 2 feet from me, he stopped, looking around him. I knew this was my chance, in a quick motion I swung the stick and hit the man directly on the chin. "Arrggg" The man let out, dropping to a knee and grabbing his shin with his free hand. I jumped to my feet, again swinging the cane and knocking his gun into the darkness. The man rolled backward on the ground, pulling a long knife out of a holster on his hip. "Sneaky bastard aren't ya, Angel sends his regards." The man whispered to me, launching forward with the knife. I side stepped him, giving him a shot in the back with my stick as he went by. The man went chest first into a bolder, not fazing him much though. Seeing the advantage I took a step onto a lower rock and jumped at the man, bring my stick down with all my might. CRACK The man rolled along the rock, causing me to miss and my cane to break on impact with the bolder. A laugh came from beside me as knife swung, just missing my bare chest. Another swing with the knife but this time I caught the mans arm along my side. He struggled, trying to free his arm, but no good. My grip was far to good to let go now. I swung my head back and hit him directly on the chin with a head butt. It hurt me, made me a little daze. I wasn't the only one, the blow rocked Angel's goon and I released his arm, giving him a right hand to the chest for good measure. The man took a couple steps back, trying to regain some composure. Something seemed odd, something seemed out of place behind him in the field. I had a very bad feeling like we were not alone, and then…we weren't. Just as I was about to take another charge, a clawed paw came from the darkness and hit the man in the shoulder, toppling him over with great force. My eyes grew hugh as I saw what had done it. With the man laying on the ground, a giant growl pierces the air, revealing my friend from earlier had returned….the Black Bear!!! The man was sprawled on the ground, screaming in agony and grabbing at his torn shoulder. I was frozen stiff, face to face with a snorting bear. It was weird though, the bear looked at me for maybe a spilt second, then took his time with man. It was horrible, I don't know why I couldn't take my eyes off the site. This guy, a person I had no idea who he was. Tried to kill me, probably was after Ashley as well, and now I was watching him be torn limb from limb by a bear…woods of Maine, very dangerous place I guess. It didn't take long for the screams to die down. Blood was all over, bits and peaces of flesh were scattered on the ground like a human jigsaw puzzle. It I was a normal man I probably would of either shit myself or passed out, but AJ Palmer is no regular man. The bear never paid attention to me after that, just went on his way dragging what he had left of the body into the woods with him. I was still frozen, didn't dare move or blink. I must of stood in that same spot for at least thirty minutes after the bear left, nothing thinking just standing in awe. All I could think, was that I had kept my promises, at least I didn't kill the man! End GTT6…here I come baby!
