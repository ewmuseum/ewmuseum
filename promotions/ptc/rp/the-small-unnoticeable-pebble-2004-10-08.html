The Small Unnoticeable Pebble

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 There was no doubt they were breathtaking. They broke through the skyline and stood tall and mighty, as if it had muscled it’s way into the sky. They looked strong and intimidating, like a giant. At the top though is why they were famous, the white snow covered the top like icing on a cake. If it was not for the snow no one would care about them, but the snow added that extra factor that made you want to challenge it, made you want to climb to the top and look down upon the world. In a way it is rather appropriate for the bracket Mr. Darkness is in to be named after the breathtaking mountains. When a competitor first stopped to look at it they found it intimidating, no matter who they were and what they had achieved the idea of conquering the mountain wasn’t a very appealing one. But then the eyes would wonder up to the very top, and there would be the goal. To say that you reached the final six of the biggest tournament known to wrestling, a target that if achieved would be simply breathtaking. But each person that climbs a mountain must do so under their own route. For the competitors in the GTT it was chosen for them, Mr. Darkness himself must have thought lady luck was on his side at first, after all he eased up the mountain to the second level without so much as a challenge. And then it came. An avalanche, an avalanche named Blitz. Imagine wanting something so bad only to find that bad luck has cost you that goal, that’s what Mr. Darkness had to endeavour through. Most would have given up on their journey and ran to the bottom. But Mr. Darkness wasn’t like that. Some would call it foolish arrogance. After all he had no chance of fighting through an avalanche so big, but regardless, Mr. Darkness tried it. Armed with only a trust pickaxe he continued on his route, onwards and upwards. And boy did the avalanche hit him, and in all truth it nearly killed him. But it didn’t. Call it luck or call it skill but Mr. Darkness persevered, he took the avalanche head on and he defeated it. And while it is obvious Mr. Darkness took some punishment from the avalanche, and while he took a beating, perhaps the old saying was right. What doesn’t kill you, makes you stronger. But now if Mr. Darkness is to continue on his journey, he must take on Nick Persia who is also an extremely talented athlete. However, there is no doubt in my mind that after beating that damned avalanche Mr. Darkness can get pass any obstacle that steps in his way. “Yet another person predicating I will win despite not knowing a damn thing about me. Stupid media hogging peasants, that is what these damned journalists are!” The Butler suddenly looked up from his book on “Landscape Gardening” and glanced at his weary and slightly agitated master. It had only been a couple of days since Mr. Darkness’ battle with one of the favourites to win the tournament, Inphino Blitz, and while Mr. Darkness may have come out the victor he still took a hell of a beating himself. It seemed that the talk of the last few days had been about how Mr. Darkness had caused the upset of the tournament and all of a sudden a small time player had become one of the most talked about competitors in the tournament. “You have to understand master, that you achieved something no one was expecting,” said the ever wise Butler, who was trying to make sense of something so obvious yet something Mr. Darkness was blinded to. “Sometimes Butler, it is wiser to stay out of the spotlight, something so far Mr. Persia has done,” the inevitable reply that made the Butler look both inexperienced in these matters and of course, stupid. Until the third bracket matches had been made the closet wrestling fan that was the Butler had no idea Nick Persia had entered the GTT. It was odd, here was one of the bigger players from the UWF yet he had slinked in the shadows the whole time, not attracting any attention. “Master, there is no doubt in my mind that you can beat Nick Persia,” the inevitable statement from a Butler to his master in an attempt to boost his master’s confidence. Mr. Darkness snorted at the idea and a smile spread across his face. “The problem is Butler, that in these type of situations, in these big tournaments…anyone can beat anyone. There are no favourites, the past results have to be forgotten, the future is the only thing that matters.” The Butler seemed somewhat startled by the reply, he had expected a rather arrogant reply from his master, something along the lines of, “of course I’ll win” yet that was no where near it. The Butler decided to take a long look at his master, the man now dubbed by some as a “Legend Killer”. Physically the current WWA TV champion looked more or less the same, sure there were the odd bruises and wrinkles that showed tiredness but overall Mr. Darkness looked like the same old Mr. Darkness. But the Butler knew from past experiences that it was the eyes that told the story. Normally Mr. Darkness’ eyes would have been filled to the brim of that all to familiar glint of confidence that so many rich people had. Mr. Darkness’ eyes however seemed ravaged, like the eyes belonging to an old and wounded soldier who had seen victory and seen defeat so many times that he could no longer tell which one was which. Something about this made the Butler realise perhaps it was the best to allow his master to reflect back on things ,after all a lot had happened and he’d been far too busy with interviews and the like to really relax by himself and let such a big win sink in. “I apologize Master for my departure but I must finish off some of the polishing in the kitchen.” The reply was subtle and Mr. Darkness instantly recognised it for what it was. Normally a master would have never allowed a Butler to talk to them like they were equal but Mr. Darkness preferred it. It was something about his Butler, the charitable and compassionate man that he was. It was something that seemed to keep Mr. Darkness from cracking, he needed the complete opposite, he needed someone that was completely different from him. “You are excused Butler, afterwards you may retire from your duties for tonight, I highly doubt I will require your services anymore.” And with that the Butler nodded and after taking one last glance at his weary master he left the room in pursuit of the kitchen. Mr. Darkness seemed to smile warmly after the butler left, as if showing a great deal of affection for him. Regardless Mr. Darkness himself would admit that he needed time to reflect on the latest results and his future match. In a way it really did feel like a climb and in a way it reminded him of a climb he did about four years ago after repeated begging from his Butler that he take a vacation. It was one of the biggest benefits of being rich, you could go anywhere you wanted at anytime and that time Mr. Darkness took advantage of that… The small mountain rose up out of the ground and seemed to blossom in the clouds. For the past few weeks Mr. Darkness had been trained and taught in the art of climbing mountains, or at least that’s what he’s instructor said. In reality Mr. Darkness had climbed a mountain ever since that fatal day when his life changed forever. No matter what people might have thought it was never an easy task to deal with the monster you have become, never an easy task to look in a mirror and realise that you are evil now, and you are destined to always be. There had come a time when Mr. Darkness had realised just that, and that was perhaps the worst time in his life. Now though, that was over, all that lay ahead was the mountain, nothing else mattered. The days would seem endless and the nights short, at first it felt like a mere stroll in the park but gradually the task grew more difficult. Mr. Darkness began to tire and while he had a tough time admitting it the words “Give Up” spun round and round in his mind. Physically his body gave up a quarter of the way into the climb, the same point when the ever faithful Butler had to give up after nearly dieing from the pain. But it wasn’t to about a third of the way up, round three if you will, that Mr. Darkness mentally began to weaken. He looked down to the bottom and with snug satisfaction found himself very pleased with his progress. At this point in his life he wasn’t the fittest of people and had a still recovering injury from that depressing time when he saw no good in continuing to live. An injury that of course, he would never let onto anyone else. In so many ways Mr. Darkness had climbed further than he should have done, and had achieved more than he hoped he would. He contemplated quitting just so he felt no despair but there was that thing biting into his mind. The thing that told him he could conquer the mountain, the thing that told him to beat the prediction. And, Mr. Darkness being the damn stubborn fool that he always had been, he surrendered to that thing in his mind. Then, just as he started to climb a small pebble under his foot broke away, and the ant sized pebble would surprisingly topple Mr. Darkness. Despite all the struggling Mr. Darkness fell back, rolling along the rocky mountain and picking up scratches and bruises, not just physically but also mentally. Soon Mr. Darkness reached the bottom, somehow he had come away from the floor without any serious injuries which was very fortunate but Mr. Darkness had an injured ego. A small unnoticeable pebble caused him to fall a third of the way up. A small unnoticeable pebble had knocked him out in the third round… Mr. Darkness suddenly chuckled to himself. Once again he faced a small unnoticeable pebble a third of the way up. Once again he faced something that e hadn’t even known was there in round three. This time however…it would be the pebble that fell and Mr. Darkness that climbed higher.
