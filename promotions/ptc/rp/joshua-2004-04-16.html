The beasts surge forward was halted as Joshua slammed his foot directly onto the handle of the knife, leaving the beast to back elbow the ten year old. Josh disappeared back into the light and surged forwards after Carys, leaving Chryste wide open. Chryste's eyes settled on the creature as it turned to her, and then they slowly found themselves staring down upon Carys. That smirk still remained, even as the beast started forward towards her... And even as she spoke... "T'was nice knowin' ya, Car..." They came past her lips, almost in a choked up cry, but more or less seeming content, as if she was ready for this... Too bad she may have been the only one ready, however. Following a deep shout of Chryste's name, the door to the room was flung open, and through it rushed Bobby, the flame-dye haired head of security for Chryste, and a proven, abnormal human being... "Mother fuck..." He silently spoke to himself... "This again..." No need to argue with the specifics of his job... He had a deep care for Chryste, and had a deep honour to fulfil in his duties. Like a comic hero on his way into a fierce battle, Bobby's teeth gritted and he lunged forward... The demon was halted in its tracks as it was struck in the chest, that flame-tattooed arm with much power behind its actions! "Get the fuck outta here!" He shouted with demand to the three, just before he drove a second fist into its chest, and a third one, an uppercut to the sunken chin of this wild deformation of life. Before Bobby had a chance to say it twice a flash that could have been mistaken for the glare reflected from glass hit the room. Carys disappeared before the boy, Chryste milliseconds after. “Hi,” the kid popped up behind the rigorously beaten demon, which was still standing against all the punishment. Bobby didn’t have time to second guess the kid who now seemed six feet tall – and in fact hadn’t changed size, just floated – because his hands were ripping the demon to pieces from inside the beast. "What the..." Bobby questioned as he stepped back a few paces, his widened eyes taking in the scene at full. A first time of something like this with Chryste was freakish to him... A second time confirmed that he'd either watched too many Tarantino flicks, or he needed a raise. The demon began to feel a raising effect; the blood that travelled from its mouth’s blood was coagulated before it even left. Its chest began to shrivel down in size. Soon Joshua’s fingers began to pour out from the front of the animal’s chest, causing further satisfaction in the child’s eyes. A great, confusing look of innocence still clear in his jade eyes. Carys’ knife fell from the beasts back as Josh continued to look blankly at Bobby. Bobby had already taken another step or two back towards the door. He lifted his shoulder sin a brief shrug and nodded to Josh, his voice holding an heir of uncertainty as he spoke... "Eh, you got this covered kid? If so, I'mma go check on the girls... And my raise..." “They’re on the lawn.” That animal was now dead, there was no doubt of that, but it wasn’t gone – about the size of a burnt out teddy bear would have set the picture more appropriately. Bobby found himself running back out of the room, a brief glance through the stained glass windows confirmed Josh’s statement and he lumbered down the stairs. A loud crackle from the room made the doorframe to Chryste’s bedroom creak, then a flash of light lit the gothic style bedroom before everything was as it was, clean, tidy. Back out on the lawn, Chryste was standing leaned against the framework of a small gazebo on the lawn. She was saying nothing much, if anything at all as she stared down through matted coal locks... "Take the book and read it... You'll know why I didn't fight..." She finally spoke, softly, quietly, to her friend Carys who stood only a few feet behind her. Josh popped on the lawn, he lay down tiredly on the trimmed grass and wiped at his eyes. “Two in two days,” Josh mumbled, “that’s not a good thing.” Carys was too distracted with the kid who’d just happened onto their sights than to quiz her obviously pained friend. Bobby sprinted across the lawn and into sights of the two girls, Josh had plonked himself back on the grass, not having much sleep had worn him out nearly as much as the fighting. "Everyone okay?" Bobby huffed out as he was on his way to Chryste's aid. It took very little coaxing to bring her into his arms, and he held her tight while he watched the other two, a flat expression upon his features. "You okay, Miss Fellini?" He quietly whispered, and received nothing more than a faint shake of Chryste's head in response. He rocked her lightly, accepting her answer as all the while, his eyes never left the young boy who lay across from them all. “Listen, C…I’m gonna have to blaze. The kid’s flat out, and I still need to get in touch with the police over who he really is.” Carys lay her head on Chryste’s shoulder, her seventeen year old body seemed as drained as the kids, but she had no choice but to be responsible. "Fear him..." Chryste had quietly whisper in response, before reaching out to lightly pat Carys' hip. "And get some rest; don't let this ruin your chance in your career like it has in mine..." Carys chuckled as she limped back over to the ten year old, a dismissive wave as she did so. She had a lot to deal with. Rest, train, fight, Josh still ranked at the top. Not that the tournament wasn’t important to her, but right now that kid had no-one, well…no one before Carys.
