Comfortably Numb...

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 - Copyright Credit... opening lyrics are from "Comfortably Numb" as performed by Pink Floyd. All writing credits to them and thanks for inspiration. - Hello…. Is there anybody in there???? Just nod if you can hear me…. Is there anyone home???? Come on now…. I hear you're feeling down…. I can ease your pain…. And get you on your feet again. I walk down the road, everything that surrounds me is like a blur. I haven’t been like this in a long, long time. There are a few women huddled together on the corner, they murmur something as I pass by them to cross the street. I shake my head and snicker, I can’t help but feel sorry for them as I walk, the mere thought of them disgusts me, leaving a sick feeling in the pit of my stomach and a sour taste in my mouth. His footsteps echo throughout the street. The night was beginning to fall, the air that was once warmed by the golden rays of the sun had gone cold. It was April, but Toronto still had a few more weeks before it warmed to summer temperatures entirely. The nights would still run cold for many more nights. The streetlights were beginning to illuminate the streets, the roads were beginning to empty, the bars beginning to fill as the buzz of the large city nightlife began to invigorate the spirits of the city’s restless spirits. Christian Darke was walking down a familiar street. The abandoned buildings; once predominant stays for teenagers who had abandoned hope and had turned to substance abuse and alcoholism, they were all finally beginning to be repaired. The majority of the buildings were now surrounded and protected by tarps, barrier and “Under Construction” signs. Tools, wheelbarrows and crumpled brick and mortar littered many of these sites. “Has it been that long?” I ask myself, as I look around and continue on my way, trying to keep my mind from wandering into the dark depths of my past. But, I can’t help it, my mind races down memory lane as I pass by the rundown hideaways of my youth. All of them, now being torn down for probably what will be another government building or corporate headquarters. But, how fitting it actually is, these buildings that housed much of my troubled past, now being torn away and rebuilt as I continue on my path to rebuild my own life. Christian nears the corner of the street. The cycle of humankind displaying itself right in front of him, the buildings had once been strong, thriving pieces of a commercial community of the large Canadian city. Then, in a twist the commercial neighborhood began to deteriorate, began to fall apart. Businesses closed down and folded, bankruptcy was a common theme and even those that were successful relocated, escaping the troubled area. That’s when the kids took over. Troubled kids… and with them came the drugs, the alcohol, the fights, the violence, the crime. The pain… I turn away after a final glimpse of the rundown area of Toronto. The city’s revitalization fund must have finally come through, nearly ten years too late to save me, but better late than never I suppose. I can’t help but wonder what my life would be like now if that money had come through all those years ago. It could have saved me so much trouble, so much pain and suffering. My emotions hit me like a tidal wave, I think back on all my painful memories of the city. The random girls, the heroine, the cocaine, the violence, the rage and hatred, the terrible hopelessness of life, it all comes back. The rush of all the pain of an orphaned teenager growing up in a pool of hopelessness hits me like a Mack Truck, bringing tears straight to my eyes. Christian breathes out a sigh, and wipes the tears from his face. He puts his head down, allowing his long black hair to fall in front of his face. Tucking his hands into the front pocket of his black DWO hoodie, he begins to quicken his pace. He didn’t really know why he was back in his hometown; something had lured him back here. And in the midst of all his memories, and the rush of returning home, he had already returned to his old habits. He taps the pocket of his blue jeans, he feels the small bottle of pills and hears the soft rattle but refuses to allow himself to take them right now. I can feel the bottle in my pocket, and I can hear the rattle of the drugs. I still haven’t figured out what has brought me back to Toronto, this city of terrible memories has a voice, the voice of the temptress at times. Unable to ignore her calling once again, I feel a sudden urge to return to the hopeless places of my youth, my hideaways, my retreats, my shell… Christian stops on his heels, he turns around staring at the old abandoned buildings. He looks around, there were people heading to bars all over the place, everyone seemed too busy to pay attention to him, a feeling all to familiar to Christian Darke. He shrugs the thoughts of regret and fear from his head and begins to make his way back towards the abandoned construction zones. Night was beginning to fall, replacing the golden twilight upon the horizon. The rush of the streets was distracting, but there was a something that was drawing Christian into a place where he knew he shouldn’t have been. He ducks under the small barrier protecting the construction site. It was empty, the workers had long since returned home to their wives and families. Christian continues to make his way into a building, he makes his way up the stairs, surprisingly this building was familiar to him, and was for the majority unchanged since his teenaged years. Christian smirks as he makes his way towards the loft area of the building where he and the others had once spent much of their time. Yeah, I refer to them as the others. I could call them friends, but that’s not what they are. Although they were always in company, and always friendly, they were not trustworthy, they were not loyal. They were like dogs that had lost their way, sick and confused, angry at the world for their own faults and they would never be friends to another human being as long as they lived. And for a long time, I was exactly like them, one of them. Christian finally gets to the loft area, a scene all too familiar to him. The serenity of it all takes him back to his youth, a terribly un-humbling place to be. Christian plops himself down onto one of the couches, the workers had not yet removed them, why would they? A great place to sit and relax when they should be working… your hard-earned tax dollars going to good work. He glances around the room, a sickening feeling comes over him. He cannot contain it any more, the sheer idea of being here disgusts him, it isn’t where he wants to be. But, alas here he is and here come the memories. The memories rush back at me like bullets. I try to dodge them, but I can’t… there are too many, coming at me too fast. I lean back, I need something to take them out of my mind. When I was young and weak, there was only one way I could escape the terrible thoughts. And unfortunately, as a grown man sitting here, I feel just as weak, I have only one choice. Christian reaches into the pocket of his jeans. He can’t reach them entirely right now, he sits up, arching his back against the couch. Finally, the pills come into his grasp. He pulls the bottle out and stares at it into the light. The label read Prescription, but it was not in the name of Christian Darke. That did not matter, the capsules inside this bottle were far from prescription, far worse than anything you could find in a pharmacy. They aren’t my true preference, I was never a big fan of pills. But, they’ll have to do, it’s a lot harder to get heroine nowadays. I remember back when I was 14, it was the only escape for me, the only way to escape the thoughts of abandonment, the terrible memories of that rainy, midnight in September, when that drunken motherfucker stole my parents from me. I never really got over it, how could I? My family, everything to me at the soft age of 12, had been robbed from me. In one swift blow some idiot, who shouldn’t even have been behind the wheel in the first place, smashed into my father, and killed them both on impact. I was in the backseat, and my seatbelt kept me safe, but it was a fate worse then death. To be left…alone. With no one who cares. I never managed to deal with it, never managed to get over it. Heroine, that was the only way out for me. After running away from the foster homes, I found my salvation here. In amongst the sea of other lost souls. That was a long time ago, I was over that now, I had found a new salvation. Shit… the drugs have kicked in. I feel the poison of the capsules rushing through my veins, robbing me of my true self and sanity, but bringing my higher up. After all of the pain and suffering I had received from drugs, I was back to it. After all the counseling and hours of withdrawal inside that rehabilitation center, I was back to my old tricks. Christian’s serene look disappears, his face contorts in a disturbing manner. He lunges forward, flinging his body from the couch. He lands on his hands and knees in front of the couch on the floor. He begins to convulse, and vomit. Shit!! I should have known better then to buy a batch of X off some punk kid in the mall. They’re fucking bad! This is trouble for me, I won’t get out of this state for a while. I had better get comfortable, the vomiting won’t stop for a while, and I’ll convulse for a long time. Shit!! I should have known better… Wrestling is my retreat now. It has been for a long time. I knew the first time I stepped into that ring it was my new retreat. The rush of the combat… it soothed me. The thrill of the fight… it relieved me. The feelings of pain…mellowed me. Inside that ring, even in the most pain of my life, I felt more alive and better then I had ever felt in my life. Since my parents had died, I have always been a lost, confused little boy. I have never truly grown away from it. But, it has always been how I handled it. How can I escape from this pain? It was always the question on my mind. I found my answer in running, then in drugs and now it was fighting. The physical pain relieved the emotional pain of my scars. I should have let that be enough, I should have waited for my chance at PTC’s CW2 to get my chance at relief and salvation. But, waiting was never what I did best. And look at me now, paying for it. Throwing up all over the place, feeling worse then when I started out the day. I can’t get away from it, ever. Patience is a virtue of the strong, I am not strong, I am weak. I am a weak and confused little boy. But, goddamn it can I be a vicious motherfucker. And soon, all this rage will come out. I will get my true salvation. In PTC’s tournament I will experience great physical pain and it will enthrall me. I will experience the rush of combat, the sight of another human being, pitted against me, with many cheering for me to hurt him more then he hurts me, and it will be with sick satisfaction that I give it to them. But, I have nothing left. Now, I have been reduced by my former love. These drugs have crippled me, the numb sensation warms me, but I feel no better. Christian vomits again, a pool of spit, blood and bile forms on the floor in front of him. His body continues to tremble and shake, he is not well off at all. The numbness has washed over me. Everything is beginning to fade, I truly have become…. Comfortably Numb.
