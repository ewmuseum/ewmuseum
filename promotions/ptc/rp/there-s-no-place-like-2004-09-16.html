There's no place like

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Nick Persia looked at his mail. He received three letters, the first one was a blue envelope, which means it's a tax assesment. He payed no attention to it as he knew what they wanted. They are the Dutch gouvernement. They want his money, for the well being of his country. While he wasn't even there. He hasn't been there for the last year and a half. All his time he spent on travelling and wrestling. It started to take its toll on Nick Persia. The desire to go home grew in him, intensified by the knowledge his father was dying. He sighed for a moment when he saw his second enveloppe. It was just some special offer from a financial institute, offering a lower interest rate. Why do they always bug you with this crap? The third enveloppe was marked with a black mark, which means its priority mail. His thoughts immediately went out to his dying father, and he opened the envelope with amazing speed. He read the letter and a feeling of comfort fell upon him. His girlfriend sent the letter to him to notify his father was getting surgery on Wednesday evening. He put the note down as he walked to the window. He looked down, but didn't see anything special. Just a few people walking on the pavement, and a car passing the pedestrians. He turned around, as he heard someone knocking on the door. He walked to the door in a slow pace. While he opened the door a smile appeared on his face. The man standing in the corridor got a warm welcome as the long arms of Nick closed around him, hugging the man.The response of the man was suprising; he just frowned. Man: "You may let go of me now, Nick!" Nick stepped back and released the hold. Nick: "Sorry Raymond, I am just pleased to see you. It has been a long time since the last time we spoke each other." Raymond: "Have you already heard how dad is doing?" Raymond asked the question with legitimate concern. He was extra careful since he didn't know what Nick already knew or didn't knew. Nick: "I just received a latter he will have surgery." Raymond: "They found a perfect match for him. The chances he will make it have increased dramatically, Nick!" Nick answered with a smile. Nick: "I hope he does. I wish I could have been there. That I could can support him when he needs help. I really start to begin to hate the travelling. I don't even have the time for doing anything else but work. Like visiting my own family! Do you even know when I was in Holland for the last time?" Raymond: "Well, no. But that's not really important. As long as the people you love are in your heart you will always be with them. And correct me if I'm wrong I believe it has been a long time dream of you to wrestle in a large arena with a lot of people paying attention to it. Now you have the chance and you decide not to take it. What's wrong with you? Is there something that needs to get of your chest?" Nick: "I am sitting here in a Hotel room spending a few nights here. First I have to do a few promotional interviews and then I wrestle a match here and I take a flight the same night so I am in time for the next show. I should be taking a flight the same night, but not to the next show! I should be taking a flight and go to see how dad is doing. To see how my little nephew Josh is doing! To see how the family is doing! To see how Holland is doing in general." Raymond gestured with his hand he should calm down. He replied with a calm voice. Raymond: "After your match you will have five whole days to spend with the whole family before you need to get on the road again." The expression on Nick's face was definitely a happy one. Nick: "That's good news, have you already bought a ticket for me?" Raymond: "Not yet, I am going to the office later today because I will have to buy a ticket for myself as well since I am heading to New York later this week." Nick: "So you will not stay here until we head to Holland?" Raymond: "No, I've got other things I have to attend. A few boring business dinners, that's all." Nick: "Good to hear your company is still running." Although Nick tried to hide his disappointment, but failed miserably as he spoke a bit softer than normally. Raymond: "Get a hold of yourself! How long have you been on the road now?" Nick: "I've been on the road since my three-day-break I had with Christmas!" Raymond: "Then what do a few days matter for you? I mean, it won't make much difference if I had to stay nine months away from my family or nine months and three whole days." Nick knew he had a point here, but he didn't say a word about it. In fact, he didn't reply to it at all. Raymond: "Besides, it's always better to go and train then sit on a bed crying out loud that you haven't been able to visit your family since Christmas. You will think of something else then! It will do you good." Nick: "Like my match with DeMarco?" Raymond: "Like your match with DeMarco." Raymond confirmed his question with a chuckle. Nick: "Speaking of DeMarco, did you find any information on the guy?" Raymond: "Not much. I found out he was born in Italy and he has had a rough youth. He grew up as a part of the maffia and is a real gangsta, at least so it seems." Nick: "A nasty fellow if he's like you talk about him like that. You're always so subtile in your descriptions. It looks like he's a bit away from home as well. So I am not the only one travelling around the world. Italy is not that far removed from Holland though. I mean, in absolute numbers Italy is just as far removed from Holland as New York from Washington." Raymond: "You know what really bothers me avout this whole thing?" Nick: "Actually, I don't." Raymond: "You can't find many information on the guy. It looks like he's been nowhere. I searched in archives, on the internet and I asked several insiders of the wrestling business like you requested, but he's nowhere to be found. A little... weird by a lack of a better term. Like he's been covering his tracks." Nick: "Yeah, Mr. Conspiracy Theory. The fact that he's not been in much wrestling federations doesn't say anything. He might broke into wrestling yesterday, but maybe like you say he's been there a whole long time. But does it really matter? I think not. You see, the most important thing for me is to show the world what I got. Not what he got. I am not there for him, but for myself." Raymond: "So you're looking to find your grove in that place?" Nick: "Maybe, but as we all know.....there's no place like HOME!" With that they both get a big smile on their face. ~finish~
