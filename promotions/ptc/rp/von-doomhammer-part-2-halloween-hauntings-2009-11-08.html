Date: 2009-11-08 “In order to appease the gods, the Druid priests held fire rituals. Prisoners of war, criminals, the insane, animals... were... burned alive in baskets. By observing the way they died, the Druids believed they could see omens of the future. Two thousand years later, we've come no further. Samhain isn't evil spirits. It isn't goblins, ghosts or witches. It's the unconscious mind. We're all afraid of the dark inside ourselves.” - Dr. Sam Loomis, Halloween II (1981) 10/29/2009 Night… Instead of training for my big GTT7 match against Buck Fledger, I've decided to have a night of fun. I’m at my buddy Andrew’s house in North Las Vegas for a Halloween party. I’ve brought my girlfriend Laurie and my wrestling buddy Rapmaster Rick, who‘s most likely high on drugs by this point, to the party since they already were planning on going anyway so we carpooled together. My mask is on the top of my head. I’m dressed as Jason Voorhees with khaki pants, a black Halloween (2007) T-shirt, and a navy blue jacket over my shirt. Rick is dressed as Skeletor from ’He-Man’ and Laurie is in a sexy female vampire costume. So far it’s been pretty boring at the party. Of course a lot of it has to do with me being the youngest person here. Another factor in this equation is the fact that it’s almost all older guys who only want to talk about sports and sports betting. It’s always like this at Andrew’s house, though. I should’ve known better. I’ve been standing near the kitchen fpr what seems like forever as my buddy Vince comes over. He’s wearing a red T-shirt from his high school and a Wolverine mask. He looks bored out of his mind. “This shit is fucking boring,” he says to me, shaking his head. “Remember when I told you earlier we was going to take some pictures for the Youtube?” "Facebook," I reply. "Whatever," he says to me. "But do you remember me saying that?" I nod my head, remembering the camera in my pocket that I brought with me. “Well come on,” he says, motioning toward the front door. Looking around, the party’s still pretty dull right now so I follow him out the front door. The cool air hits me immediately upon coming outside. He walks across the street as I pull the camera out. He sneaks across the street to look for something to pose by. Turning the camera on, I follow him as he hides behind a car, pointing his finger at an invisible criminal as I get the camera focused and take the first picture. He laughs and then comes over to see the picture to see how it turned, which I show him on the back of the camera. He looks pleased with the results. “Looks good, kid,” he says with a smile. “Now what kind of pose can we do next? I want to post this on Facebook tomorrow.” We both start looking around for something to take the pictures by but then he suddenly remembers something that is going to make for some amusing pictures. “There’s a park a few streets over,” he says with a mischievous grin. I start smiling deviously at this point too. We’re both obviously plotting and scheming. Suddenly Vince starts laughing and says, “Let’s go get Rizzolo.” “Let’s get Malloy too,” I tell him as I start laughing at this point. Rizzolo is this guy who’s a pathological liar and Malloy is…well, Malloy. This is going to be fucking hilarious if we get those two to come with us. He giggles and replies, “Yeah.” Acting like excited little school kids, we job back to the house grinning like Cheshire cats as we enter the house once again. I follow Vince toward the backyard where he looks for Rizzolo. He finds him over by the grill talking with some older guy as I notice Malloy drinking a Bud Light next to the bar that is located on the right side of the sliding door. “Rizzolo!” Vince exclaims as he motions for him to come with us. “Come on!” “WHAT?!” Rizzolo yells back. “Come on!” Vince exclaims as he grabs the Burger King mask that Andrew was wearing earlier in the evening off of the air hockey table to our left. I reach over and grab my fake Jason machete off of the air hockey table. We were taking pictures that made it look like someone cut off the Burger King's head earlier. “Malloy!” I yell out so that he can hear me over the loud party chatter. He looks over and sets his beer down after I motion for him. He sets his beer down and comes over toward me after he grabs his mask off of the bar. “What’s going on?” Malloy asks me as Rizzolo now joins the three of us. “We’re going to go to the park with our costumes and take some pictures with us acting out fight scenes,” Vince says as he heads into the house. “And you’re going to be the Burger King, Rizzolo.” “Oh brother,” Rizzolo says as the four of us walk past everyone who’s huddled inside the living room to stay out of the cold Vegas night. “Where are you guys going?” someone asks as all four of us ignore it and sneak out the front door. Once outside, we follow Vince down the street. Our footsteps echo off the pavement as we walk across the street and onto the sidewalk. It’s colder than a witch’s titty outside as we head down the street toward the intersection turn ahead. Thinking ahead, I hide the fake machete down my jacket sleeve just in case any police are on patrol in this area. “Where the fuck are we going, Vinny?” Rizzolo asks as we continue toward the park. “We’re going to the park down the street,” Vince replies as he continues to be our fearless leader on the way to the park. Malloy starts laughing for some reason and I have a big grin on my face as I notice that we can all see our breath now when we breathe. It’s getting colder outside now. Must be in the forties. “Fucking A,” Rizzolo replies as now we reach the sidewalk and walk south beside the street. We walk past some trees as now Vince is way ahead of Rizzolo, Malloy, and myself. The three of us try to keep up as Vince points down the sidewalk. “It’s just a little bit further,” he says before noticing a tree in the grass to our left. He then walks over toward the tree and climbs it, hanging down from one of the branches. “Take my picture, Jimmy.” I roll my eyes, pulling out the camera once more and opening the cover before focusing the lens. After snapping the picture, Vince climbs out of the tree to see how it turned out. He seems very pleased with the result. Behind us, Malloy and Rizzolo are watching him as they both collectively shake their heads. “Ok, let’s keep moving,” Vince says. “Didn’t think it was this far away, guys.” “Where the hell are we going?” Rizzolo asks in a loud voice. “Are we walking to Primm or something?” Vince keeps walking and we notice some parking spaces past the next set of houses. “We’re here,” I tell them both as the four of us walk down the sidewalk. We walk over to the playground set and stop in front of the slide. Observing what is in front of us, me and Vince start scheming. “Ok, Rizzolo,” Vince says as he points to the slide. “Put on that Burger King mask and get on the slide.” “What the hell for?” Rizzolo asks, a bit annoyed for some reason. “So we can take pictures of us kicking the Burger King’s ass,” Vince replies as both Malloy and myself start laughing. “This is fucking great,” I chuckle out as Rizzolo puts on the Burger King mask and starts climbing onto the playset. “Ok, Jimmy,” Vince says in a director’s tone. “You and are going to be beating up the Burger King. Ricky will take the picture.” I pull the camera out of my pocket once more and get it set up as Vince’s phone is ringing. Showing Malloy how to take the picture, I hear Vince mutter out, “It’s Andrew. He’s probably wondering where we are.” Vince puts his phone back in his pocket as I hand Malloy the camera. Walking over to the slide, I see Rizzolo sitting down at the top of the slide with the Burger King mask on. Vince, still in his Wolverine mask, is fake punching the Burger King face as I bring out the fake plastic bloody covered machete I brought with me. Pulling my Jason mask on, I then make it look like I’m about to stab the Burger King with it. Malloy focuses and takes a picture. Pulling my mask back up, I walk over to Malloy who then hands me the camera. Vince starts laughing and comes over to see the picture. “Awesome Jimmy!” he exclaims just as his phone starts ringing again. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out his cellphone to see who is calling. Glaring at the name, he growls in frustration. “It’s Andrew again,” he snarls out. “This is worse than being married,” I reply as Malloy starts laughing his ass off. He probably snorted some cocaine at the party or something and now it’s taking effect. “Now Jimmy,” Vince says. “You take a picture of us kicking the Burger King’s ass as he’s trying to get up from the ground. Then we’ll take a picture of us chasing the Burger King.” “We need one of Jason fighting Skeletor too,” I reply back. “And…” My thoughts cease at this time as I hear some footsteps walking toward us. Turning in the direction I hear them coming from, I see four shapes walk over toward Vince as I sneak around the other side of the playset. The four men walk over to Vince, Rizzolo, and Malloy as I look on from the shadows behind the playset. As the men walk into the lighting, I recognize them immediately. It’s the same four guys from the independent wrestling show that I screwed over before GTT7 started. American Hero, “Viper” Vic Creed, “The G Playa” Jamarion Williams, and “Dirty” Diego Sanchez are all in their wrestling gear with black jackets on over their costumes since it‘s cold outside. I also notice that they are armed with weapons. Hero has a baseball bat, Sanchez has a crowbar, Creed has a knife, and Williams has a handgun in his belt. My three buddies look over at them as they walk over to them. “Which one of you guys is that jackass von Doom?” Creed asks with a scowl on his face. Rizzolo, Vince, and Malloy look at one another before looking around for me. None of them seem to know where I’m at. After looking around for me, they collectively turn back to the four wrestlers. “Where’s James?” Malloy whispers to Rizzolo who only shrugs his shoulders in response. “He’s not here,” Vince replies to them. “He’s still at the house.” “I know you’re lying!” Vic yells at him. “He left the house with you three. We know he’s out here somewhere!” Just then I sneak out from behind the playground and run up behind Creed. I grab his knife and hold it to his throat. “Put the weapons down!” I yell at them as all three of them grumble as they put down their weapons. Just then two large black hearses drive over and parks next to the curb by the park. The hearse doors open and some of the Church of the Unholy minions come out of the hearses. They appear to be the ever infamous Unholy Druids, but they could just be some of the organization‘s executioners. Running over, they grab Williams, American Hero, and Sanchez before I let them grab Viper Vic as I drop the knife on the ground. The four wrestlers scream in terror as the Church members pull out some body bags and put them inside the body bags. After doing so, two druids take each body bag to the back of the hearses with two body bags being put in each hearse after they open up the trunk doors of the hearses. Once the body bags are thrown inside, the Druids close the trunk doors before getting back inside the hearses and driving off. The plan worked. I set this whole thing up knowing that they would probably follow me to the party to try and take me down. Suckers fell into my trap. That’s why they just learned a big life lesson: never mess with somebody who’s more of a bad ass than you are. * * * 10/30/2009 Morning… It’s around noon as it’s turned into a regular club meeting at my house. Turning toward the next door, to my right I see his assistant who has a big screwdriver in his hand. He also is wearing a blood splattered apron and kind of looks like Dougie Howser, M.D. “We need more body parts,” the doctor says as I‘m marking out like a wrestling fan. “Maybe you all would like to donate some parts?” “Yes,” the assistant responds in a mildly squeaky voice. “The doctor needs more bodies for his experiments.” Upon leaving this room, we enter into a forbidding hallway with blood on the walls and flickering ceiling lights. It’s like something out of the Texas Chainsaw Massacre. “The Church needs an evil lair like this,” I say, motioning around. “This is so awesome. I need to get decorating like this at my house.” After going through the next door, we’re in another darkened hallway. It’s pitch black. It leads to another corner. We turn and hear some noises. This is the strangest thing I’ve ever experience. This room is clearly meant to disorient and for people to experience what it’s like to be Rick Malloy on a daily basis. “HOLY SHIT!” I exclaim in a high pitched voice I try to figure out where the hell I‘m walking as the white flashing light continues to blind me. “I CAN‘T SEE!” “ME EITHER!” Laurie yells out, grabbing a hold of my jacket to do that. “WHAT THE HELL?!” Malloy exclaims loudly. The walls are covered in white coverings to make it even more confusing. “Shhh…I hear something,” Malloy says acting like Ray Stanz in the ‘Ghostbusters‘ library scene. I look around and barely see two figures in the distance yelling things to confuse us. They continue this and split up, which only confuses us more. “THIS WAY!” one of them says. “NO THAT WAY!” the other one says. This is just fucking great. All three of us are lost in a big room with a white flashing light that’s blinding us so we have no clue where to go. “I can’t see a fucking thing,” I snarl out, clearly not in one of my best moods. “Where the hell are we going?” “I don’t have a clue,” Laurie replies. “I think we’re lost,” I respond as I continue to look around for the exit. I think I see something so I start walking toward it. Once I get to it, I realize it’s the door. The figures then turn and stop following me as now Laurie and Malloy are the targets left for them in the room. “AHHHHHHHH!” Malloy screams out. “I CAN’T SEE! AND THE BUGS! THE BUGS ARE ATTACKING ME! PULLING ME INTO THE LIGHT!” I look into the room and see him running around in circles scratching at his arms as the two figures continue to mess with his direction. “Oh crap…” I mutter out as I’m currently in a state of disbelief over what is going on. Laurie hears me from inside the room and walks in my direction to get out of the room. “What is it, James?” she asks. “Malloy’s gone apeshit,” I reply, shaking my head as now I am looking into to whitened room as watching as he is pushing and shoving at air. “GET AWAY! GET AWAY FROM ME!” he exclaims, scratching at his arms and clawing at the air in alternating motions. I start to yell at him to get his attention to get him out of the room. “RICK! RICK! OVER HERE!” I yell out to no avail as he continues to act like a psychotic. “James!” Laurie exclaims, sounding very relieved as she is now out of the room. “Thank God I found you. Couldn’t see anything. Where’s Rick?” “Running around like a fucking cokehead idiot like he usually does,” I growl out, motioning toward him as she looks on in amazement. I again try to yell his name to get his attention toward the exit door. “RICK!” I exclaim as loud as I can. It doesn’t work. He’s going the other way now. “Wrong way,” Laurie says in his direction. “You have to go this way!” I yell out to him. “STOP THE VOICES! STOP THE VOICES!” he yells out frantically. I can only shake my head. “Dammit,” I snarl out. Knowing I have to do something, I head back into the room and immediately get confused. Walking through this room is like being on drugs. However Rick already is on drugs so now he’s really bugging out. Somehow I find him, grab his arm, and drag him over to the door where Laurie is waiting patiently for us. Once out of the room and into the darkened hallway, he seems to really be confused about what just happened to him in the room we were just in. “Huh? What happened?” he asks, seemingly confused about what just happened in the white room. “You got lost and were yelling like a psychotic maniac,” I reply in reference to his question. “I was?” he asks, scratching his head. “Yeah cokehead, you were,” I reply, again annoyed by what is going on. “Now let’s keep moving so we can get out of here and get you home.” We start to head down the hallway and immediately I hear something once we reach the next corner. “I hear something again,” Malloy says in a paranoid tone. “You always hear something when we come around a corner,” Laurie responds. Something then shoots air up from beneath the floor through an open grate. Malloy jumps up in fright as Laurie grabs onto me. “OH MY GOD!” Malloy exclaims, almost freaking over what is going on. Instead of being scared, I smile at the ghoul and decide to respond with a sarcastic comment. “Hi, how’s it going?” I ask politely. A bit annoyed by my response, the ghoul moves out of the way so we can pass into the next area of the haunted house. Laurie can only shake her head at my comment. “James, why are you so non-chalant about this place?” Laurie asks me. “It’s because if we were all the Ghostbusters,” I say in response. “I’d be Peter Venkman and you two would be everybody else.” Laurie rolls her eyes in disgust as Malloy is shaking and cowering with fear. “Yeah, that’s great James,” Laurie replies. “You just compared me to Dan Aykroyd. That makes me feel so sexy right now.” Malloy suddenly starts scratching at his arms and trying to brush something off of his clothing. “BUGS!” he yells out loudly. “THE BUGS ARE CRAWLING ON MY SKIN!” Instead of trying to help him get the bugs off, I roll my eyes in response. “Dear lord.” I grab him and motion for him to be quiet so we can get out of the haunted house before he goes on a homicide spree. I lead the way with Laurie and Rick behind me. There’s also another patient ghoul in the middle walkway blocking my path. It’s really hard to walk after that last room. AWESOME!” I exclaim, quickly getting on the walkway and jumping on it like a little kid. “You can’t leave!” the ghoul exclaims trying to scare me. “We don’t want you to leave!” “Dude, I SO have to get a room like this at my house!” I yell out in a giddy voice. “This Is and the white light room are the two coolest things I’ve ever seen in my life!” “I can’t even walk, James,” Laurie snarls out. It works. “Hold onto the rails,” I tell her and Malloy as I start making my way down the ramp way. “Yeah, that’s easy for you to say when you’re up in the front,” Laurie snaps back at me. “You can’t leave!” the ghoul says to try and freak me out. “We want you to stay!” I no sell it and respond back to Laurie’s snippy comment instead. “I’m up here because I have no fear of death,” I tell her as I smirk. “And I also have one of the patients trying to scare me and if one of you two were up here, you’d probably faint.” “Asshole,” she snaps back, rolling her eyes as she says this. “Quit your bitchin’ and let’s keep moving before one of you two gets motion sickness,” I growl out and try to keep moving. The ghoul meanwhile repeatedly asks me, “Where are you going? Where are you going?” After about the fifth time of him asking and blocking my way, I’m finally annoyed to the point where I smile at the annoying ghoul as he’s trying to scare me to try and get him to stop. “Nice weather outside today,” I respond to him. He looks at me funny before moving out of the way so we can pass through the walkway into the next section of the haunted house. Upon leaving the walkway, we enter into a short, darkened hallway which then leads to a forbidding looking courtyard area complete with tombstones and a creepy looking fence. A husky female ghoul patient confronts us upon entering this area. “Where you going?” she asks in a squeaky voice. “Where you going?” “Somewhere…over the rainbow,” I reply with a sarcastic grin. She clearly almost breaks character which to me is hilarious since everyone knows that the undead have no sense of humor. “He’s going to get you!” she exclaims followed by shrieking laughter. “I just hope he’s not gay,” I reply back. Now both the female ghoul and Malloy are laughing. “AHAHAHAHAHA! James, knock it off!” Laurie yells at me with a laugh. We continue past her and into another area of the haunted courtyard where another female ghoul is waiting for us. “Don’t disturb him…“ she says in a quiet, almost peaceful voice. “Or he’s going to get you.” “Great,” I respond with a smile. “He sounds like a fun guy. He sounds like he knows how to party.” Laurie grabs a hold of me tighter as Malloy begins to use me as a human shield for the eight hundredth time since we’ve entered the haunted house. “James, I got a bad feeling,” Laurie says as she is clearly trembling beside me. We walk past a coffin with a dead body in it. This small area reminds me somewhat of an above ground graveyard in New Orleans. Suddenly we hear the noise of a chainsaw starting up. As we walk into the last area the chainsaw murdering ghoul runs at us with the chainsaw as Malloy and Laurie once again freak out as I no sell the whole thing like the bad ass that I am. I’m being ‘avant-garde’ but they don’t know that. “OH SHIT! OH SHIT! OH SHIT!” Malloy exclaims as he panics over the chainsaw. Laurie starts screaming too, pushing me toward the exit. “IT’S HIM!” she yells out again and again as she continues to push me along. Both Malloy and her run out of the haunted house as I calmly stroll out like nothing is going on. Once outside I meet Laurie but Malloy is nowhere to be found. Smith walks over and motions toward someone running around the parking lot screaming like a banshee. Of course the person is none other than Rick “Cokehead” Malloy, my best friend and the bane of my existence. “Oh crap,” I mutter out, motioning toward him. “Now he’s running around the parking lot.” Smith looks on helpless as Laurie is now getting her wits back together after surviving the chainsaw wielding maniac at the end of the haunted house. “von Doom, what should we do?” Smith asks. Great. After going through all that, now I have to take the lead and deal with this shit. This is just great. “Go get the car,” I tell Smith as I reach into my right pocket and hand him my car keys. “Take Laurie with you.” Smith and Laurie head for the car as I try to scheme up a plan to catch my druggie friend before he does something really stupid. The ghoul’s face is all scarred up. However that is not why I came over to talk to him. I have to go catch my coked up friend before he humps a tree or something.” “Be my guest,” he says in a scary voice before handing me the net. “Thank you,” I reply with a nod. Yeah, my training for GTT7 round two has been replaced by this crap. Just another day in the life of von Doom.
