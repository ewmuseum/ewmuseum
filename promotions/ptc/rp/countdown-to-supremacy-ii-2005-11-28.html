Countdown to Supremacy II

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 It was a day that will live in annals of wrestling history. The day a superstar fell and a PRIME legend was born. Slowly, but surely the referee begins to get his senses back together thanks to some prompting from the champion. Karina goes back to repeat the pin, drawing a slow count from the referee. One! . . . . Two! . . . . . Thre… NO! Nick: Good Lord how close was that! Killen Sirrajin somehow managed to kick out at a count of about two and nine tenths! Richard: That's if you ignore the other twenty eight seconds he was down for. Resisting the temptation to bang Bernie Robert's head against the mat, Karina yanks Sirrajin back to his feet and doubles him over with a strike to the gut. As the Supreme Machine leans over, the K-Wolf hooks both of his arms and begins to spin the Intense Champion around. Nick: Wait Richard, I think Karina Wolfenden is preparing to deliver the C4! We've heard a lot of ruckus about this extremely dangerous move during the week and now it looks like we're going to see it first hand! Karina goes to run to the ropes, but as she tries to flip into the seated position, she rolls straight through due to Sirrajin latching onto the middle rope. Not to be deterred, Karina quickly tries to grab both of his arms again, but Sirrajin spins around and seems to plant his thumb straight into the Negasonic Lupines eye. Nick: The C4 is countered, but here Karina goes again…. Richard: THUMB TO THE EYE! DQ! DQ! Nick: I… That was inadvertent I'm sure… A moment of confusion takes over as Sirrajin realizes that the K-Wolf is temporarily without the power of sight. Seeing that the referee is still trying to shrug off the effects of the earlier collision with Karina's foot, Sirrajin drills her with a second Supreme Justice without further delay. Nick: SUPREME JUSTICE NUMBER TWO! Clambering on top of the K-Wolf, Sirrajin leans back with a hold of the near leg, begging for the official to count. One! . . . Two! . . . . Three! The bell rings, causing an eerie moment of silence as the fans try to register just what exactly has happened. It was something no PRIME-ate had seen before… the sight of Karina Wolfenden's shoulder being held down for the count of three. WINNER: Killean Sirrajin The sound of The Perfect Circle confirms it however, prompting the crowd as Killean Sirrajin is announced as not only the winner, but the Universal Champion. Nick: I don't believe it… the streak has been ENDED here at Ultra Violence! The K-Wolf has gone down and we have our THIRD Universal Champion in the space of just a few weeks! Richard: This is almost like the Global Title Curse all over again! There you have it Nick, a title win that Ian English would have been proud of! Nick: I don't believe that eye poke was deliberate at all and despite your attempts to discredit this victory, Killean Sirrajin is the new champion and the euphoric expression on his face says it all. Handing the Supreme Machine the belt, the referee steps back as Sirrajin clutches not only the Universal title, but the Intense title to his chest. As with most Universal Title changes now, confetti begins to rain from the rafters to add to the Kodak moment in the ring. Nick: Killean Sirrajin joins the elite group of Dual Champions by… Nick pauses as Karina sits up, nursing her throbbing neck. Her eyes strained with anguish she looks across to the Canadian holding her belt… her streak was gone and so was here title. While she might still have another time ahead of her, this was Sirrajins night and keeping to her principles, she slips from the ring, hunching up against the barricade. Ultra Violence 2005 was the night. The stage was set. One of the mainstays of the PTC community was set to defend her Universal Championship against an up and comer in the PRIME ranks. No one thought that Karina would ever be defeated. Surely she couldn’t lose to a man who still couldn’t reach his peak after over seven years in the business. Karina was too good and too loved by the millions of fans that adored her mere existence. But that night in the Boston Fleet Center, history was made. Not just in PRIME, but in the entire wrestling world. Karina Wolfenden, formerly having 10 wins without a loss in her PRIME career was beaten, tired and defeated. All this coming from the twenty-seven year old man who people thought would never get the job done. History was made that night when no one thought it was possible. Now, history will be made again at GTT5. This time, only one person doubts his own destiny and doubts his opponent. The countdown is on and the fans are waiting. The Supreme Machine is coming to PTC.
