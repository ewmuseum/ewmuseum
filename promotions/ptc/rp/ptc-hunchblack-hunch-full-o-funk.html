IG31- Hunch Full O' Funk

13 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: Hunchblack Fed: Free Agent Present Day I’ll be God damned if I can remember anything pleasant from my childhood. I never went to school, never had the luxury of friends, never even had the luxury of a loving family. My name? To tell you the truth I have no idea. My parents, if you can call them such a thing, abandoned me when I was very young. I wasn’t placed into a foster home or an orphanage, no. I was picked up by the circus, and that’s all I’ve ever known. You can’t tell why that happened by reading what I write. If you could see me, you would understand. I’m a fly-ass nigga with a birth defect, waiting for those lights to dim before I get on the flo’. For now I just slip in between the disco lights, wowing the crowds gathered to admire my moves, and wishin’ they were their own. The hump on my back is one made more refined through the years of toil and torment I’ve had to endure. Toil and torment have been replaced with groove and high levels of funk. The hump with the funk. Two Years Earlier The arrogant ring master stepped from behind the curtain to the roaring approval of the crowd. Ring Master: And now, the main event! He held his breathe as the crowd grew ever more excited. They knew what was coming. It was the only thing they came to see, what everyone was talking about. I was behind the stage, in my box. The stagehand thought it was a good idea to poke me with a pointy stick before I went on. As if being the centre of attention for this drooling, blood thirsty mob wasn’t humiliation enough. If they were born a couple of centuries earlier, they would have been marching up to Frankenstein’s castle carrying flaming torches. But Frankenstein’s monster is merely literature. I am real. At least, to me I am. Ring Master: First welcome our heroic champion; Victor! Victor. He was our strongman. He could lift a Cadillac with his arms and pull a truck along with his teeth. Although I’d never seen him do it myself. Every day, they forced me to wrestle Victor. Of course they would claim they were forcing him to wrestle me, thinking that I liked it. Victor is as strong a cracka as I’ve seen, but even he knows he can’t beat me fairly. Unfortunately, I’ve never been given such a chance. Holding for applause, the ring master continued in his shrill weasely voice; Ring Master: And to challenge the might of Victor is a beast raised in the fiery pits of hell... At least the part about where I was raised was always right. Ring Master:...A monster so terrifying, so wild, that he has to be kept behind bars for your own safety... The audience clapped and laughed. They knew the ring master was fabricating, although they already decided to play along and side with Victor. Just as they always did. I was never given the chance to plead my case or show them I was more than a sideshow. I know now that it takes a lot more from me than it does others to earn respect. I learned that the hard way. Ring Master: ...Be ready to gasp with wonder and recoil in fear as we introduce Victor’s opponent for this evening... The stagehand continues trying to get me angry. Taunting me and poking me through the bars of the cage like I was an animal. To them, I suppose I was. Sure there were people who complained about the way I was treated, but when someone shows up to check, they make out it’s a guy in a suit. Like it’s a joke, and they are given no evidence to suspect otherwise. But I was past angry. I don’t remember anything before I was taken to the circus, so I have little knowledge of how much better or worse my existence could be. The only person who has every spoken to me in a civilized way, as a person, was one of the gymnasts; Maria. She tried protecting me since we were young, but always fell on hard ground. She once threatened to report the circus for cruelty, but was met with a counter threat that her and her family would lose their jobs and homes. But I was always, and still am, grateful for everything she helped me with. She read me books, she played me music, she taught me to dance and taught me to read and write. However brief these releases were from the autocratic regime of the circus, I was never given the chance to use them practically. But they made me into a better person. A person, like I always said I was. Ring Master: ...The evil...The wild...The uncontrollable... He called me these words every day, and I almost let myself believe him. Ring Master: IGOR! The crowd erupted in boo’s. They made their choice, and it seemed there was nothing a humble nigga could do to change their opinion of me. Each day I was given a new name. It had been ‘The Creature’ or ‘The Freak’ in the past, but at least at this one was a real name. Not that I liked it. The stagehand opened the cage door and pushed me through the curtain. I looked at the crowd and they looked at me. I could hear them talking to themselves, about me. Things that I couldn’t understand, but I knew they were about me. I walk to the middle of the stage and stand in front of Victor, who smiles at me. Not the kind of smile you would consider to be friendly. The bell rang. This was the most exercise I was allowed to get. They even had security on standby with tasers, in case I got ‘out of control’. They knew that I could destroy Victor, given the chance. Nevertheless, I play their game. It’s either that or I’ll be abandoned again. The crowd all cheer on Victor as we circled each other on the derelict stage. He lunged in for the grapple, his hands on the back on my head on my shoulders. This is the part where I was supposed to let him pick me up and throw me to the ground. Instead I spun round behind him landed a German suplex, much to my delight. So began the chorus of boo’s from every man, woman and child in the arena. They all hated me. They wanted Victor to hurt me, to hurt the guy in the suit, as I was to them. Maybe if they knew the truth they wouldn’t have shouted ‘freak’ at the top of their collective voices at me. Maybe some of them still would have. Victor stood up, giving the ring master a glare, as if it was his fault I was too quick for him. We circled again. This time Victor ran at me like a Rhino, wanting to crush me. Again I move out of the way, sending him to the floor. The chorus of boo’s restarted their vicious symphony. Victor stood up in front of me and began throwing punches towards me, all of which I dodged. Agility is one of my strong points. Another is my strength, which I demonstrated by throwing Victor, a two hundred and fifty pound man, into the weak wooden wall at the back of the stage; sending him through it. The crowd grew ever more savage, this time throwing bottles and coins in my direction. One of the stray bottles, given by a man to his young son, hit me square in the temple. This knocked me off balance and sent me towards Victor, who in his sneaky wisdom decided to knock me to the floor and pin me. And that was it, it was over. Victor had one, just as they wanted. I often wondered what it would be like to look like him, to borrow his appearance for a day or day. He acts like a fucking prick and gets the respect and admiration of the people who treated me like a piece of shit on their shoe. Look at us both on the inside, and you can really see just who is deformed. That night I spoke to Maria, and decided that it would be best if I were to leave. She agreed, although she needed me to talk her out of leaving with me. I knew it would be best for me to travel alone. At night my cage was locked and kept inside. Not to keep me warm, but to put me where nobody could see me. This time I finally had the courage to pull out the bars and make my escape. Maria had taught me so much about the world, and this was the time that I would see it for myself. She had given me a cloak to hide myself, as not to drawn any immediate attention to myself. She told me if I stuck to the alleyways and shadows that I wouldn’t stand out as much. After that, I would need somewhere to say. Someone open-minded and kind enough to offer a hunchbacked black man some refuge. Present Day The time has come for me to prove I still have that animal instinct, even if I have evolved since then. No doubt that IG will be a star studded event. All I want is a chance to prove to those motha’s that I deserve the chance to compete with honesty. Asking for a fair chance has been the story of my life. One Year Earlier I wasn’t aware of what city I had been living in all these years. Only that the people were accepting of difference or change. As I sat in an alleyway, a kind gentleman even gave me some money. Perhaps if he had seen my face he would have given more, or taken it back altogether, I guess I’ll never know for sure. Another gentleman I came across saw my face. Homeless Guy: Hey man, what happened to you? Hunchblack: ... Homeless Guy: Come on nigga. We all brotha’s here! Like me he was seeking refuge in this alleyway, but I was unsure what he was hiding from. Like me, he was an African-American; I believe he called us, before referring to me as his ‘nigga’. I was not sure what this meant at the time, but he was friendly towards me, something that I greeted. As night grew to its darkest, I decided to brave the wrath of the normals and walk down the open street. Few people were around, and all doors in the various structures were closed and locked. I found one building that was not locked a grand spectacle of masterful architecture. I walked inside. There was a long aisle with a big wooden cross at the end, soft welcoming candles lighting the way. To the left of me was a large box covered by a curtain. Being used to sleeping in safe confinement all those years led me to believe I could spend the night there. I entered and sat inside. Another man was on the other side. He said he was a priest, and he could help me. He saw my face, and still offered to help me. Priest: Do you have something to confess? Hunchblack: ... That was when he leaned forward enough to see me in detail. Priest: Good Lord. My son, do you need help? He was kind enough to send me to one of his friends who could offer me shelter and a warm bed. I couldn’t remember the last time I’d slept in a bed, and was immediately sold on the idea. I was given an address, and I had decided to walk. This particular street had many distractions, but none more so than the bright lights shining on the road from inside a discotheque. I exchanged glances with the doorman before deciding to find another way inside. But I had already caught the groove seduced me. The doorman was thrown aside like a ragdoll as I slid inside and unloaded a nigga’s hunch full of funk. The darkness just making myself anonymous enough to avoid the glares and stares of my fellow groovers. They were watching me. They were watching me bust it old school like a mo’fucka. I had found my home. Present Day I was born with a birth defect causing abnormal growth on my dorsal or upper back, resulting in a hump. My face? More growth abnormalities in my jaw, my nose, mouth, ears and cheeks. There was a time where just being a black man was enough to be shunned by society. But now, it doesn’t matter what colour my skin is, because in their eyes I will always be different. Ask me my name again and I’ll give you another answer. My name is Hunchblack, a groovy nigga with a hump. Deal with it sucka. The Infinite Gauntlet is my opportunity to show the normals what I can do. After all, who is really the three hundred pound hunchbacked nigga? Not I. Hunchblack: Now watch me jive it out like a mo’fucka. Ooh, yeah, come on baby.
