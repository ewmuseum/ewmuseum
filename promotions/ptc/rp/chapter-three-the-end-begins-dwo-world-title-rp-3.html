Chapter Three: The End Begins (DWO World Title RP #3)

Donny Diamond Emotionless Monster Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 59 Location: My own personal Hell. Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 8:36 pm Post subject: Chapter Three: The End Begins (DWO World Title RP #3) Chapter Three: The End Begins Lots have happened since the first day. Not the first day since this match was signed, lots have happened since his first day as a signed member on the roster of DWO. He has gone from a wrestler that dominated the independent circuits to a being of superstardom in a DWO ring. In his time within this federation he has become the most hated individual to walk down the aisle. During his reign as DWO Extreme Champion his true shade of darkness was saved as he ended one career, and nearly did the same to another. Though his time as Champion came to an end his soul grew more as his body was vanished into the pits of Hell where his soul would be reborn and his eyes fully open. Now the time of the destined battle has come, and everyone expects to see a winner. Donny Diamond versus Kaiowas, this is going to be one for the ages. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~ ~`~ Hell ~`~ | ~`~ There is no time for the dead ~`~ ¤Listen to that noise Do you hear it? The sound of your heart beating faster Faster every minute¤ ~†~ Pools of flames and flowing lava can be seen everywhere as we see the canyons inside of Hell. Inside the city limits all of the tortured souls from yesteryear remain, continuing to follow through with their eternal torment. Sitting on a stone someone can be seen; this being is dressed in a robe that matches the color of Khiku’s blood. Nothing can be seen of this being except for his hands, which hang out from the arms of the robe, and they seem to be made of stone. Raising his hands to the hood of the robe he pushes it back, and immediately his red eyes burn a hole into the center of your soul. Just like his hands, his face also seems to be made of stone with a metal plate covering it for protection only allowing his eyes to be clearly seen. After a moment he places his hood back over his head to hide his face once more. Looking down he continues his walk through the city of the damned; soon he comes to the bridge that is created over the sea of flames. Finally he arrives at the gates to the castle as two long swords cross each other in front of him permitting him from entering. Emerging from the shadows of the castle are two guards, both shadows themselves. ~†~ Shadowed Guard #1: Who are you? Shadowed Guard #2: What reasons do you have for entering the castle of our Lord? Shadowed Guard #1: Leave at once, or we will be forced to extract your soul into eternal punishment. [Standing before the two guards, this being shows what is hidden behind the hood and sheltered by the darkness, as the hood is pushed back for a moment. Immediately they retract their swords and fade back into the shadows of the castle. The hood of the robe is once again placed over its head as it walks forward into the castle. Entering the throne one, it takes a kneel as the throne is facing the opposite direction.] ¤The pace will quicken Each time a day passes Listen to the clock ticking away As the final hour nears¤ Unknown Being: My Lord I have arrived as you had requested. [After the sentence from this unknown being ends, the throne starts to turn towards it. Sitting in the throne is the Lord of the Underworld. Each arm is placed on its rightful side of the throne as he looks down to the robed being before him.] Satan: So I see you have shown your loyalty, and came as I ordered. So tell me Sorrow, how has your studies been going? Sorrow: My studies have been going good my Lord. I have learned many things while I was away. Why do you ask this my Lord? Satan: Because soon it will be time for you to prove exactly what you learned thus far. First however, left us dine. [He gets up from his throne, and the two servants beside him grab his midnight black silk cloak. Quickly they place it around him, and snap it with the crystal chain that holds it together. He walks down the stairs, and starts to make his way down the hallway to his left as Sorrow follows from behind. Arriving in the dining hall, he takes his place in the chair at the front of the table as Sorrow takes his seat in the chair beside him. Satan snaps his fingers, and two towers of flames come forth and start moving in a circular motion. Soon they fade away and two skeletons appear.] ¤When it finally comes to the final hour Fear will be seen inside your eyes You will begin to fear for your reign As I am coming to start my own¤ Satan: Get us food so we can feast. [At the sound of their order the two skeletons go off to do as they were told.] Satan: So Sorrow, where has your lessons taken you? Sorrow: I have traveled many miles during my lessons. And I have come across many different cultures in doing so. Recently my studies have taken me to live in the wilderness, inside the jungles of Africa. There I studied human behavior, and watched as they worshipped various Gods for various reasons. I even watched as they forced their children into doing several trials to prove to the entire tribe that they were prepared to accept adult tasks. Satan: So you have learned on how much a human body can withstand. Sorrow: Yes, even when faced with tremendous amounts of pain placed in a task before them, they would continue to do it. To them it showed dedication to their tribe, it also proved their worth. Satan: Ah. Maybe this information of yours could prove to be helpful for my son. Sorrow: Your son? Excuse me master, but when did you become father of another? Satan: For being the one that knows all you sure do not know much at all. I was paid a visitor a while ago, it was a soul filled with complete darkness, hate, and pain. He was sent here before his time, normally I have no problem with that, but this time however he was different from all of the rest. So I showed him the true pain of his life, he accepted it and acknowledged me as his father. Thus I gave him the chance to be reborn as a part of me, and blessed him with a soul created of complete darkness. I allowed him to relive his life on earth, but only if he did my bidding while completing his goals. Sorrow: I see. However my Lord, please excuse my stupidity, but I do not see how this has to do with the information I have been gathering. Unless of course you plan having him follow me during my travels, which would be an honor. Satan: Hardly. Your information can be of great values to my son, during this time. ¤Using your reign as my payment I will end it As I end you Let my domination begin¤ [The skeletons from earlier come back into the room with carts following them. Together they place the different sets of dishes from the cart onto the table. After doing this, one sets up the plates as the other uncovers the meals. At a snap of the fingers from the King, they vanish even quicker than they appeared.] Satan: He is scheduled to face Kaiowas. This is his chance to prove to the world that he has what it takes to dominate, and to take control. Since this is such an important time for him, I order you to flee from this realm, and to find my son. There you are ordered to teach him your lessons in torture, and to make him understand as to what he must do in order to be successful against Kaiowas. Go now. [No wasting any time, so that his Lord is not angered, Sorrow gets up from his seat. Without taking time to even catch a bite of food, he bows down before his Lord, and proceeds to walk out of the room. Soon after he exits the castle, and makes his way back onto the bridge. He places his hands together, and starts to speak to himself, his lips barely moving. Everything around him begins to get blurry, as his body starts to vanish.] ~`~ Rundown Asylum ~`~ | ~`~ April 8, 2003. 7:48 PM ~`~ ¤I show no mercy Not even to the weak I’ll show none to you Because ending you is what I seek¤ [Outside the weather is gloomy as nightfall starts to set in. Cars can be seen passing on the highway at full speed as no police cars are in sight. On the right of the highway is nothing, except for tall grasses and cane fields that are never kept up. However on the right side we do see something, other than what is on the right side of the highway. Past the ditch is a long fence, and it goes as far as the eyes can see. Inside of the fence is a large building, which seems to have been closed down for a very long time.] [That is where we see him at. Standing on the final step is Donny Diamond. Like always he is dressed in complete black, and the wind can be seen tossing his coat around behind him as it continues on it with its steady breeze. He walks up the final step, and then opens the door to enter inside. He looks to the side where he sees a switch, he flicks it up, and the lights come on.] Donny Diamond: Interesting. Obviously someone cares enough about this place to keep paying the electric bill. Does places like this scare you Kaiowas? Not me. I have paid many visits to places like this. I use to come here every Sunday, and pay a visit to Ron. Of course all of this was done when I had him placed inside of here. You can’t blame me though; after all he was trying to kill me. If you were to ever come inside a place like this, and take a look around for a few hours when you leave you would never be the same again. [He walks around the entrance area, looking at all of the dusty items around. In the far corner by the door leading into the main room of the asylum we the word “death” written in what appears to be blood. Using his leg he does a big boot, which forces the door to open, he walks inside and notices all of the different hallways.] Donny Diamond: When my brother was placed in here we were more of a family, excluding the facts that he wanted me dead, but it wasn’t his fault. His head just wasn’t right. His favorite activity to do together was to read the bible. He had a strong belief in God, but as you notice upon my return Kaiowas, God could not save him from me. Just like the Ktulu cannot save you from me Kaiowas. You know that’s why you keep them around, because you know soon your reign will end by my hands. So for some odd reason you think having this tribe stand behind you will stop me from coming after you. Wrong idea Kaiowas, because nothing is strong enough to stop me from coming after you. ¤I laugh at the mentioning of your name Because the intensity of your fear humors me Then your tears fall Because you know everything I say is true¤ [He starts to walk down the hall, but suddenly the scenery changes. No longer is he inside of the asylum, now he is walking out from a cave and seems to once again be in Hell. Looking around he sees no one in sight, but he gets an eerie feeling that someone is behind him. Slowly he reaches inside of his coat, barely moving his arm as he walks forward not to make anything look suspicious. His hand grips firmly on a handle inside of his coat, with one swift movement he turns facing the figure behind him, his small sword was pulled out during the swift turn, and is a hair away from cutting the neck of the hooded person now before him.] Donny Diamond: Tell me who you are, or I shall slice your head off from your shoulders. [The person behind Donny slowly tilts his head up and red eyes can be seen. Slowly Donny’s arm holding the knife starts to move away from the neck of this person and starts curving in towards Donny. He is then forced down onto his knees before this being, and the knife is pressed into Donny’s chest, over the center of his heart. He immediately closes his eyes, and clears his mind allowing himself to focus on his surroundings. Quickly he passes his left hand behind this person’s legs and forces him to fall to the ground. Now being able to use all of his limbs, Donny rolls to his side and is now over this person. Since his fall the hood is pushed back, and Donny quickly removes this metal mask, which unveils a third red eye. Using his sword Donny places it over the center of the eye with the tip of the blade pointing down.] ¤I fear not the monsters The monsters of my past Because inside your mind I am the monster you fear¤ Donny Diamond: A telepathic guardian. Interesting. Tell me what are your reasons of wanting to kill me, and I just might show mercy and allow my father to deal with you, instead of me ending you here. Unknown Guardian: Your father is my reasons. Donny Diamond: How dare you say such a thing. I should end you here just for saying that. Unknown Guardian: No you shouldn’t. I speak the truth; your father is the reason I am here. Think of our little brief encounter just now as a test. Donny Diamond: A test? Explain yourself, I demand you too. Unknown Guardian: The name is Sorrow; I am one of your fathers most trusted allies. He sent me to look for you, so that I could teach you some of my knowledge. Donny Diamond: Why does my father believe I need to be given more knowledge? Sorrow: Perhaps if you got off of me, we could answer that question. [Donny places the small sword back inside of his coat, and removes himself from atop of Sorrow. He climbs back to his feet as Sorrow puts his metal mask back on, and uses his telepathy to levitate himself back to his feet.] ¤Pain is the only feeling Left When we collide you will feel it Feel it deep within¤ Sorrow: So you think your unstoppable? Donny Diamond: I just stopped you, didn’t I? Sorrow: I’m five hundred thirty-two years old; do you think that is all I am capable of? For my entire life I have traveled all across earth, learning the different cultures. Learning about how people put themselves into tremendous amounts of pain to please someone else. Donny Diamond: Neither your age, nor your job concerns me. Sorrow: Then perhaps it should Donny. After all knowledge is the key to winning any battle. Your father, my Lord, told me something about you was different than all of the other souls that have passed through here. Now I see for myself exactly what he was talking about. Let me ask you something Donny. Why did kill Khiku? Did you enjoy it? Donny Diamond: It is my job. His death was payment to my father, for him allowing me the chance to be reborn so that I could achieve what I my goals. Sorrow: Yeah it was a job. However you enjoyed doing it, even if there is no smile on your emotionless face there is a touch of happiness inside of your soul. It shows itself every time you kill another. Donny Diamond: So what if I killed another, and enjoyed doing it? I thought you were here to teach me a thing, or two? Sorrow: That’s exactly what I am talking about Donny. In a normal situation death bothers whoever is close to that person, or thing. You however are different in this situation; you welcome death as if it was something glorious. You also enjoy forcing others to feel massive amounts of pain before they see the final flash of life. Somehow it drives you on. Just like when you pressed the face of your twin brother into the flames, or when you set your father on fire to die. Donny Diamond: You’re damn right I enjoyed making them suffer. They deserved it; try to tell me otherwise I dare you. Sorrow: Their fate is not for me to decide. Not only do you enjoy making others suffer, but also you do it for the pure enjoyment of havoc. Do you know what happens to most when they are encountered with the death of someone close? It burns them deep down inside. If their death comes from the murder of their enemy it eats away at their soul for the rest of their life, or until they’re able to end the life of this person and force them to feel pain as well. Donny Diamond: So what your trying to say is, the death of Khiku and Lecood is eating away at the soul of Kaiowas? Sorrow: In a way, yes, because he knows in some shape or form you had something to do with the death of both. That’s what I wanted to show you Donny. I wanted to show you how to hurt someone without even making physical contact. Follow me. [Sorrow starts walking away from Donny, and towards the backside of the cave from which Donny came. After thinking about it for a moment Donny starts to follow Sorrow. Around the other side of the cave, they walk towards the edge of the small island surrounded by an ocean of lava. A table can be seen, and screaming can be heard, as they near it with each step. Soon they come up on the table and we can see several demons surrounding it, most of them have their flesh missing in big blotches. On the table is a very bloody, and bruised Khiku. He is chained down to the table forced to remain still as he stares up at an illusion of Kaiowas begging for him to save him as the demons surrounding him are pushing needles into various parts of his body over and over again.] ¤Blood Pain Misery All of these things shall be shown once we do battle¤ Sorrow: Here he lies for the rest of eternity. Being forced to feel the pain you gave him until time ends. Look at him. Still he begs to Kaiowas, even though he knows his soul cannot be saved. Is it dedication, or is it foolishness? Donny Diamond: It’s both. Even past death he is dedicated to Kaiowas, because he believes that somehow he can still be saved. However it is also foolish, because his soul can never be saved from this eternal torment. Sorrow: Ah, you’re right to a certain extent. He is not begging to Kaiowas, so that his soul can be saved. He is begging for Kaiowas, his Lord, to end your life so that your soul can be tortured along with his. Donny Diamond: Let him beg for something that will never happen. Kaiowas can never defeat me, no matter how strong his tribe becomes. I have my eyes set on achieving immortality, and I will do it at any cost. Even if that means I must do to him, what I did to Khiku. Sorrow: Glad to see you show some determination Donny. Just remember Donny, in order to achieve victory you must also force him to feel pain in a physical and mental way. Force him to feel the pain that you were forced to live through. [As Donny turns to face Sorrow the scenery starts to fade slowly. Soon nothing can be seen for a moment, and then an old room starts to come into view as the blurriness subsides. Remaining in the same spot, Donny looks around for a moment at the different things in the room. He kicks over the mattress in the corner of the room, and a roach comes crawling out. Using his boot he plants it firmly down right over the insect squashing it as its insides leak out.] Donny Diamond: So this is it. The very room I came to every Sunday for two years, here is where I came to visit my brother. He had faith that maybe one day we would actually come together as a normal family. For two years he had hope in himself, and in me, do you know what hope got him? The only thing that came out of him being hopeful was a burned face at the hands of the one he loved most, me. I then take a look at you, and your little tribe Kaiowas, and you know what I see? Donny Diamond: I see them having hope in you. Hoping that they can give you the strength that is needed to defeat me in an asylum such as this one. That’s all you have going into this fight, hope, because you know this will be your first real test as Champion. Never have you stepped into the ring with a force as strong as myself, but this time we’re not stepping into a ring Kaiowas. We’re stepping into an asylum, where death may be the only option for you to take if you have any hopes of walking out still known as Champion. Donny Diamond: Do you have enough determination set in yourself, and enough honor for your tribe to come forth, and win this match by ending me? That’s the type of dedication I have to myself, so understand something Kaiowas. I have no problem coming into this match knowing that I may be forced to kill you, just in order to win and achieve my goals. I’ll be seeing you soon Kaiowas, sooner than you think. ~†~ Slowly he tilts his head down, as he looks towards the torn pieces of paper scattered across the floor. The lights in the asylum start to flicker, as the laughter coming from an escaped patient can be heard. Donny’s own laughter can be heard mixing in, as Kaiowas is the selected thought coursing through his mind. ~†~ ¤I’m coming forth Forth with full force This is your chance to hide and save your life Since there is no ending the damned¤ _________________ DWO Record: 9-5-0 PTC Record: 1-0 -I only mark for those deserving, and you know who you are.-
