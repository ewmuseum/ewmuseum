Championship Material pt. 1

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-19 ~~~{ When we last left off with the Shadow Riders, they had just managed to reawaken the ruthless spirit of a team long thought gone. In a true test of their will to succeed, the Shadow Riders pulled through a situation that even their own mentor, the Snake, did not think them capable of. It was with the drive and self confidence of that moment that Eric Russo and Jason Kage used to fight their way through the teams of The Pain Syndicate and Shoudou & Ukyo Kushinaga. Now with their careers back on track and their path set toward a PTC Tag Team title match at the upcoming PPV Survival, nothing could bring the mood of the Shadow Riders down. Then again if it didn’t, what kind of story would this be? }~~~ ::The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “Life may end but legends never die.”. The scene opens in the locker room of the Shadow Riders sometime after their grueling contendership match for the PTC Tag Team titles. The luggage of the duo is piled and ready to be taken to the car in order to travel to the next event. “Superstar” Eric Russo is sitting off to the side of the room on a metal folding chair rummaging through a duffel bag. His hair is still slicked from the sweat that he worked up during his match. Russo is shirtless wearing a pair of jeans a white towel which hangs down around his shoulders. Over at the other side of the room, a wardrobe girl is busy ironing out the wrinkles on the dress shirt of Jason Kage. The girl looks over at the other shirtless Shadow Rider with a starry-eyed grin as she begins to speak.:: Girl: “Uh . . . Mr. Kage, I’m almost done with your shirt sir.” ::Kage looks up at the girl with a playful smile from his seat then goes back to playing around with his Gameboy Advanced. There is now a knock on the door of the locker room. Hearing this, Kage speaks up without taking his attention off of the gaming screen.:: Kage: “Come in.” ::The locker room door swings open as Luke Justice walks in with a bottle of champagne in each hand. The monstrous Shadow Rider closes the door behind him with a back kick as he exclaims.:: Justice: “I told you guys you could do it!” ::As Justice looks at Russo and Kage, he notices that neither man is fully dressed yet.:: Justice: “C’mon you two, get moving. You just won a big match, lets go hit the town.” ::Russo looks up at Justice with a slight frown as he responds.:: Russo: “It’s a no go on the clubs tonight. LOW decided to put some kind of bull$h!t no partying rule into effect for us. They say that we get into too much trouble when we go out. They’re afraid we may end up doing something illegal and missing out on the match at Survival.” ::Flicking the power switch on his Gameboy Advanced to the off position, Kage looks up and begins to back Russo’s statement up.:: Kage: “They even mentioned something about getting us some kind of babysitter. You know, someone to keep us in line and out of trouble.” ::Hearing this seems to somewhat annoy Justice as he begins to rip apart the scenario set forth by the LOW.:: Justice: “LOW can’t do that. They don’t control what you do away from the ring. Just think back, when have you two ever not partied after a big win? If you ask me . . . by not going out tonight, you may end up hurting yourselves. Maybe going out and hitting the clubs is what gives you guys your edge. Did you ever think of it that way?” ::Never being ones who need much justification for throwing the company policy out the window, Justice’s reasoning is all Russo and Kage need to spark their enthusiasm. As the two Shadow Riders quickly finish getting dressed, Kage begins to speak to the wardrobe girl.:: Kage: “Ok look, I’m going to need you to do us a small favor. There’s gonna be a guy from LOW’s public relations department knocking on the door in about an hour or so. When that happens, I need you to tell him that . . . Ah hell, just make something good up. You think you could do that for us honey?” ::The young girl sheepishly nods at the Shadow Rider as she hands him his shirt. With a smile, Kage reaches out and grabs his shirt as he responds to the nod.:: Kage: “Thanks a million babe. I’ll be sure to look you up next time I’m in town to give you a call. Anyway I’m gonna have to be going, don’t forget to tell that guy something good.” ::Now fully dressed, the three Shadow Riders head out of the locker room, closing the door behind them, and take off down the hall. As soon as they are out of sight, a mysteriously dressed man steps around the corner at the opposite end of the hallway. With his identity concealed by a black trenchcoat and cowboy hat, the man walks down the hallway with a rigid precision. Finally he comes to a stop directly in front of the Shadow Riders locker room door. The man turns to the door in a military like fashion, not wasting any movements, as he reaches out and turns the door’s handle. As the door opens, the man is taken aback to find nothing but the young wardrobe girl inside the room. Seemingly angered by the lack of the Shadow Riders presence in the dressing room, the man quickly turns and heads off down the hallway in the same direction that the Riders had just left. The scene now fades out.:: ********************************LATER********************************** ~~~{ Paying little attention to LOW’s requests for them to keep a low profile, Eric Russo and Jason Kage decide to hit the town with fellow Shadow Rider, Luke Justice. Being that they are in New York city, the three Riders can think of nothing better then to hit the famed Scores strip club. Little did they know however that at the very same time, their appointed babysitter from LOW was hot on their trail. }~~~ ::The scene reopens to a shot of Eric Russo, Jason Kage, and Luke Justice as they sit around a table which is littered with empty beer bottles and glasses; inside the famous New York strip club Scores. A cloud of smoke hangs around the table from the cigars that the Shadow Riders are smoking. The shot now pans around the pulsing nightclub showing numerous celebrities such as Howard Stern and Dennis Rodman before finally settling back on a shot of the Shadow Riders. As the shot moves closer we can now hear that the Riders are in the middle of a conversation. As usual Russo is the one doing most of the talking. The view begins to move in even closer on the three LOW wrestlers as their conversation becomes audible.:: Russo: “So that’s one match down and only one more to go.” ::Casting off the importance of the upcoming PTC Tag Title match and the opponents the Riders will have to go through to win it, Kage pipes up.:: Kage: “Yeah, and it’s gonna be a cake walk. I mean seriously, we’re going against the New Royal Legion. Those guys are a bigger joke then Q-Factor was.” Russo: “Tell me about it, we nearly destroyed them while we were doing that whole Metallo Zord thing. To think, we went easy on them then too.” ::Kage lets out a little chuckle as he thinks back to the last encounter with the New Royal Legion. He then responds.:: Kage: “Those two don’t have any idea what they’re in for this time around. They have about as much chance of matching up with us as Jamester does of regaining the Global title from Saij.” ::Hearing the mention of Jamester’s name seems to pique the interest of Luke Justice as he jumps into the conversation.:: Justice: “Did you two really have to bring that jackhole up? If I never have to hear that name again I’ll be a happy man.” ::Reassuring Justice, Russo responds.:: Russo: “At least he’s finally out of LOW.” Kage: “I’m actually looking forward to see . . .” ::Kage is cut off as the front door of the club sings open with a loud thud to reveal the assumed babysitter that has been appointed by LOW. The dark figure makes his way into the club and immediately begins scanning the room for Russo and Kage. Once he has spotted them he man removes his hat, finally reveling his identity at that of the Shadow Riders’ mentor, The Snake. We can see the look of anger in the grizzled veteran’s sunken eyes as he storms towards the table. The Snake immediately begins yelling at Russo and Kage, giving no explanation for his presence.:: Snake: “WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TWO DOING!? WHAT ARE YOU THINKING GOING OUT AND ABOUT CAVORTING, AT A STRIP CLUB NO LESS!?” ::Baffled by the sudden appearance of his mentor, Russo responds in a questioning tone.:: Russo: “Snake? What are you doing here?” ::With a sneer crawling across his face, the Snake responds.:: Snake: “LOW brought me onto staff for the time being to keep you two in check. They knew that you weren’t gonna listen to anyone else so they figured they would give me a crack at the situation. Wrestling schools don’t bring in as much money as they did during the boom in business a couple of years ago, so I agreed to help out.” ::Bringing up his feelings from the Shadow Riders last encounter with their mentor, Kage speaks up.:: Kage: “Tell me Snake, what makes you think we would give you the time of day? You turned your back on us when we came to you for help. Why should we help you out and do what you say now?” ::A stern look fills the Snake’s face as he responds.:: Snake: “Because I can get you a win. The reason I didn’t help you when you came to me is because you two needed to get confidence back in yourselves. That was up to you two and only you two. I couldn’t help you there, but this time is different. This time you need me to make sure that you are prepared. If it was left up to you two, you would not even think about the match with the New Royal Legion until it was too late.” ::With Russo and Kage’s interest now piqued by the Snake’s words, Russo begins to speak.:: Russo: “OK Snake, lets just say for a moment that we were interested in you getting us ready for our match. Exactly what would that entail on our parts?” ::His anger building at Russo and Kage’s stalling tactics, The Snake exclaims.:: Snake: “THIS IS NOT A NEGOTIATION! YOU TWO EITHER AGREE TO MY TRAINING REGIMEN OR YOU DON’T!” ::Still trying to play of his mentor’s seriousness, Kage begins to speak.:: Kage: “Just sit down and chill out Snake, it’s not like we killed anyone.” ::The Snake snaps an evil look towards Kage as he responds.:: Snake: “Don’t even get me started with that one Jason.” ::Testing his limits as well, Russo now chimes in.:: Russo: “Yo seriously, you have to clam down. There’s a vein popping out of your forehead, you’re gonna give yourself an aneurism if you don’t calm down.” ::It seems that Russo’s comment is the last straw as the Snake explodes in a rage.:: Snake: “SHUT UP AND LET’S GO! IF WE’RE GONNA DO THIS, WE HAVE TO LEAVE NOW! IF WE’RE NOT THEN TELL ME AND STOP WASTING MY TIME!” ::Realizing that the Snake is not in a playful mood, Russo whispers into Kage’s ear. The two men then look at each other and give a nod. They then stand up and begin to walk towards the exit of the club behind The Snake and Justice who has also started towards the exit. As they reach the front door, both Russo and Kage are grabbed by a couple of stripers and pulled towards the back room as The Snake and Justice continue their way out of the club oblivious to what is happening. Kage and Russo exchange a smile as they follow the women. The picture switches to a shot outside of the club where Snake and Justice continue walking towards a limo that is parked in the lot. The Snake begins talking to Russo and Kage as if they’re behind him.:: Snake: “I suggest you pack warm clothes, we’re going to be going on a little trip. The next week is going to push you two further then you’ve ever been pushed before. Do you think you can handle that?” ::The Snake turns to see why the two men are so quiet only to find nobody behind him. Snake flings his hands up in the air as he says...:: Snake: “I can’t believe these guys, they’re like a couple of two year olds! I don’t know how their ever gonna get ready in time acting like this!” ::The Snake quickly turns to Justice as he continues.:: Snake: “Well... what are you waiting for? Go get them out of there!” ::Justice turns and walks back into the club as The Snake waits by the limo. Moments later Russo and Kage come running out of the club followed closely by Justice. The three men run past a confused Snake and jump into the car. The Riders then quickly begin yelling at The Snake from within...:: Collective Yell: “GET IN!!!” ::The front door of the club swings outwards to reveal a large fat man with a double barrel shotgun. When The Snake sees this he dives into the limo as it speeds of with him still half hanging out. As the Snake finally slithers the rest of his body into the limo with the help of Russo and Kage, Russo pulls the car door shut. Unfortunately of Kage however, his right forearm is caught between the car door and the frame of the car. With a loud crack the door bounces off of Kage’s arm as he falls back into the car with a scream of immense pain. Back outside the club, the man with the gun fires a few shots in the general direction of the car but it is already out of range. As he watches the car speed of he yells at it.:: Man: “WHEN WE SAY NO TOUCHING, WE MEAN NO TOUCHING!” ::The shot fades back to the logo of the Shadow Riders with the usual “Life may end but legends never die.” voice-over before the picture goes to black.::
