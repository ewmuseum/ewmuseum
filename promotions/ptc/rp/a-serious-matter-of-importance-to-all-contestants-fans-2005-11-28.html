A Serious Matter Of Importance To All Contestants & Fans

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 Yeah, the whole "my hype is that I don’t need hype" thing sucks. It doesn’t make sense and it makes me want to hit you in the face…hard. Also, saying "believe the hype" really means the same thing as "don’t believe the hype." That whole arena of sayings grinds my gears and gets my goat at the same time (don’t try that at home, let me tell you). Glad we got that out of the way because it’s time to hype me. I live off of it. I’m like one of those algae eater fish. If you give me hype, I’ll expand my stomach to fit all of it in. I probably won’t explode, but hey if I do there’s comic potential there that I can run with. If you don’t give me the hype that I so preciously desire to keep my inner workings fit, I will wither away, but not before killing you and your whole family via a secret and ancient ninjitsu method known as "pouring ammonia down your throats." I’m not sure what the English translation is, but it’s been known to be both deadly and fatal. So listen to those other superstars who say they don’t need the hype because, apparently, they don’t. Give all your unnecessary hype to me and me alone. I will bask at my luxurious apartment in a large tub full of hype and perhaps a bit of bubble bath mix if the right lady comes over, yaknawhatI’msayin, eh? Eh? Eh? Yeah, you catch me. Those who need no hype will need no hype for their exit, but ME? If I were to ever lose (of course it would be via cheating, treachery, deceit, corruption, fraud, and sabotage), the fans and competitors would go up in arms. You wanna know why? It’s because I’m going to get all the hype (it would be pretty ironic if I won the whole tournament but no one read this hype HINT HINT HINT oh fuck you’re already reading you idiot). The things I’ve done in the past twenty four hours that are hype-worthy are as follows: invented the piano, cured AIDS, divorced Nick Lachey, read the Da Vinci Code, watched Ghost Whisperer, subsequently vomited, beat up the Devil without provocation, swiped your ATM card and PIN number without you seeing, remarried Nick Lachey, and finally came to a complete stop at the terminal, thank you for flying Southwest. Every single one of those things got me on the front page of CNN.com. At one point, I had replaced myself three times in a row, before some lady was, unfortunately (both for her and my newsworthiness) mauled to death by dogs in Texas. So don’t bother with whether to believe or not to believe is the question. There is no question, but a command: give all the hype to Chandler Tsonda. And while, I’m commanding: someone do my laundry. So fuck those people who need you to either believe or not believe the hype. They’re wrong. I’m all the hype you need.
