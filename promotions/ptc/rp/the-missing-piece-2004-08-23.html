The Missing Piece

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 The lighting in the building was dim, although still dim enough to not give shape to every object in the beige-colored hallway. A bald man dressed in a pair of baggy black jeans and a black t-shirt, with the, with the logo from Slayer's "Reign in Blood" album on the back, walked down the wooden-paneled hallway. He turned right, then made another slight right to walk down a staircase. As the stairs led downward, the wall color suddenly changed from beige to an almost pitch black. The screen continued to follow the man until he reached the bottom of the steps, where a lone white door stood at the end of the short hallway. The words, "Life Begins Here," were written in big black lettering, sticking out against its plain background. The bald guy raised his fist and gave the door three powerful knocks, the sound echoing in the hallway surrounding him. For a few moments, he stood out there, just waiting for a response from the other side of the door. When the door did open, a man dressed in all black was on the other side, barely standing out in the darkness that enveloped all sides behind him. He had a black hood on as well, covering all the way down to his nose, concealing his eyes. The man didn't smile, or make any other facial expression for that matter. He merely stated four words. Hooded Man: "He who is holy…" We couldn't really see the expression on the bald man's face from behind, but his response to the other man was similarly stoic. Bald Man: "Shall have no salvation." Without another word, the hooded man stepped back from the doorway to allow the bald man to cross into the threshold. The camera followed closely behind, but it was barely visible inside. Only the small illumination that gave off from a few candles lit the camera's path. It stumbled as it followed the man down another set of stairs, about thirty in all. As the stairs went down, the hallway and the room below was more lit up, with more candles and eventually a dim light bulb hanging from a cord on the ceiling gave away some of the room's features. There were about seventy people here in all, a few of them in the front wearing a black hooded robe, the kind that a druid might wear. Another man gave the bald man a robe as well, and as he put it on, the camera shifted its focus from him to the rest of the room. The walls were yellow from the dim light and candles, and there was a wooden altar against the far wall of the room, placed atop a five-foot pedestal. There were no sounds coming from the room, no talking whatsoever between the ten men that stood around in a semi circle that connected to the altar. Suddenly, the sound of a succession of footsteps interrupted the silence. The source of this noise was coming from another man, who was slowly approaching the altar, wearing the same black hooded robe as the others were. The only difference was that on the middle of his robe, a white symbol stood out, appearing to be some kind of twisted form of a dragon. He was slightly above average in height as he stood before the podium, wearing a pair of glasses with red lenses. He had no facial hair on his tanned skin, but as the hood was lowered off of the top of his head, it revealed medium length blonde looks. The man gave a half-smile as he gazed upon the men that were situated in front of him. Slowly, he raised his arms up a little, palms up, beginning to address the small crowd. "You sir, why are you here?" The man he points to doesn't really seem surprised that he has been called on, and simply says. "For the purpose of enlightenment." The speaker at the podium seems to like this answer, as he smiles and claps his hands together. "And enlightened you shall be. My friends, my followers, my children…I have summoned you all here today, because this is indeed the rebirth! We have lie dormant for far too long…we have just sat back and watch as the filth in this world have taken over. The rich, the supposed noble, the religious, they all must be put to a stop, and today is the day in which we begin on our mission. We must spread our message of darkness and anarchy to everyone, and we must also embrace all who wish to follow the three simple rules, in which I, Markus Warfield, have set in stone, by way of the great prophet, Anton LaVey." Suddenly, the camera shifted from the man on the podium towards the crowd. It roamed past a few rows to the back of the room, where two men were leaning there against the wall. The man on the left was probably the only person in the room not wearing black, as he had on a white t-shirt and a pair of blue jeans. He was a short guy, with a little bit of a gut and straight brown hair that was patted down on his head. His right knee was up while his foot rested against the wall behind him, shaking his head in wonder as to why he got dragged here. The man next to him was the dragger though, wearing a black and red t-shirt and black baggy jeans. His brown hair was tied up behind his head for once instead of hanging over his face and shoulders, as he listened intently to the man at the main stage. This was Eddie Sanders, Neon Wrestling star and East Coast Champion, although right now, he was merely a listener. His other friend, a guy named James who had become somewhat of his lackey in the eyes of others, just rolled his eyes and hit the back of his head on the wall. James: "Remind me again exactly why the hell we are here?" Eddie didn't really acknowledge the question for the for ten seconds or so, as he was concentrating on the now distant words of Mr. Warfield. Eddie: "I think the one thing I'm missing from my game is inspiration. So I'm just trying to find something that might help to complete my performance in the ring. Wasn't it you who said something about inspiration?" James: "Yeah but I didn't mean religion, or whatever the hell this is. I mean, how the hell did you even find these guys?" Eddie: "Some pamphlet in a convenience store window…shut up, I can barely hear this guy as it is." James: "You don't need to hear anything from this guy, because I know what this is all about. God is evil, Satan is good, Satan is our pal…it's just the same old zombie bull crap that gets you to drink the green Kool-Aid Eddie. I'm NOT drinking the green Kool-Aid and killing myself in a field, so I suggest we get the hell out of here." Eddie: "Well I mean that Botwin guy I'm teaming up with is a Nazi or something, maybe this can help me see where he's coming from." James: "These aren't Nazis…those two guys in the front row are black, how the f*ck can they be Nazis? And besides, even if they were, why should you lower yourself to the level of such a bastard like Botwin?" Eddie: "It's still a win James, and that's what is most important. I could careless about defending the honor of Neon, but if I get made to look like a loser, then I'm going to be extremely pissed off." James: "Same old egotistical bull…" Suddenly, the voice of the speaker rang through the hall towards the back, causing the entire crowd to turn around to the men standing against the wall. Since they weren't paying much attention at the time, Eddie and James looked back to the audience with a look of mild bewilderment. It eventually comes to their attention that Mr. Warfield on the podium was looking directly at them. Not only was he looking at them, but also pointing at them with his finger. Warfield: "It seems as if two of my children have some questions…so please, share your thoughts with all of us here." Eddie just nudged James on the shoulder, motioning with his head that he should speak up. James turned his head from Eddie to the robed preacher in the front, and clearing his throat, he decided to do what was probably going to be a mistake. James: "Umm, yes I was just having a talk with my friend over here about some of the values that your prestigious faction holds in regard." Warfield: "Oh, well why didn't you just say so in the first place. You guys must be first timers, am I correct?" Eddie chimed in. Eddie: "Yes you are, and my friend was just telling me how much he was eager to learn. Actually, I came with him and have to get to a lunch meeting, so I'm going to get going." James was about to speak up, but Markus beat him to the punch. Warfield: "Alright, have a good day sinner…bring the interested one up the front." James looked back to Eddie as Eddie turned around and started to quietly laugh. A few rather large people led James to the front with little incident, seeing as he would probably get like raped in he tried to run. Eddie managed to quietly slip out the side door a few feet away as the camera followed him to the hallway. Eddie started to laugh now that he was out of the room, shaking his head slowly from side to side. Eddie: "Serves his bitching ass right…hope he doesn't get like, molested though. Oh well, what doesn't kill you makes you stronger." Eddie started laughing again as the camera followed him down the hall and eventually to another door. As Eddie pulled it open, it revealed the extremely sunny outside world. In fact, the intense glare in the lens made it hard to see anything for a few moments and the camera was forced to look to the ground to get a clear shot of anything. So for a few moments, we followed the black and white Adidas sneakers which belonged to Sanders. Eventually the feet stopped moving, and the camera traveled back up to where Eddie is standing in front of another building, this one looking a little bit decrepit, with a few loose bricks on the front. The sign on the front was in big black letters, and read the word "Sully's". Through the glass windows, we could see a few treadmills and stationary bicycles, including a cross trainer machine, indicating that this was most likely a gym. As Eddie pulled on the door handle, a little bell sounded that was barely audible from about ten feet away. Once inside the place, we could see more gym equipment off to the right, including one of those universal machines with four exercises in one. It was being occupied by two different people at the moment, as were one of the stationary bicycles that looked as if it might have been the first stationary bicycle in the history of mankind. The centerpiece to this gym however was the huge wrestling ring towards the back, where two young guys were currently practicing hammerlocks, while another smaller man watched from the outside. A smirk came across Eddie's face as he started over towards the ring. With the camera getting closer, we can see that the smaller man is most likely in his fifties, with black hair that was thinning in a horseshoe type pattern. The man turned around quickly to see who it was, and stopped talking when he saw the familiar face. Man: "Well I'll be a skeleton's bitch…Eddie Sanders, how ya doing?" Skeleton's bitch? Eddie started cracking up as he didn't know where Sully, the man who owned and operated this gym, pulled these expressions out of. The guy gave him a quick hug as Eddie's eyes wandered to the ring. Eddie: "These guys any good?" Sully: "Not bad, not bad at all really. Hey, did I f*cking tell you guys to stop?! Keep going at it!" They listened to him without question, as did most people who wanted to train here. It had only been four years since Eddie went back to this place, but the look of it had changed a little bit. The universal machine had been there since he could remember, but the treadmills were new, and the ring looked like it had actually been cleaned for once. Sully: "So what brings you here today, young Edward?" Eddie: "Just in the neighborhood, wanted to see how things were going?" Sully: "Same as usual for me, how about you? You're in that Neon place right? How's that working out for you? Get a title shot yet?" Eddie: "Actually I already hold a title, the East Coast Championship. So I guess it's working out for me alright." Sully: "East Coast is nice, but you're good enough to fight for the main gold. Did you blow your opportunity, or just not get one?" Eddie: "What opportunity, they make me fight bums there. I already had a match with someone who just became the Heavyweight Champion and I beat him. But I don't get a title shot, I get to represent Team Neon in some War Games with like twelve other federations." Sully: "Oh that Prime Time Central thing that they do, right? That Survival Series or something like that. Well shouldn't you be training for that then, it's like later on this week I think." Eddie: "Why should I bother training? I've done my training, doubtful any of my teammates have though. They're all slackers, they don't do sh*t compared to me. Two of the guys, I don't even know if they're going to be ready for the match." Sully: "F*ck them Eddie, you only have to worry about yourself. If you guys lose the match, it's more than likely not going to be your fault then right? Your teammates will come along eventually, everyone has their own pace. Just remember that and worry about your own ass in there. Still a cocky son of a bitch I see…" Eddie: "Hey, you had a hand in teaching me, just remember that." Sully laughed a little bit, looking back towards the ring to watch his two new students. Suddenly, he wasn't laughing anymore. Sully: "HEY! What the f*ck is that piss poor execution all about?! That's a good way to break someone's arm jackass!" Sully looked back to Eddie, but Eddie shook his head a few times. He knew that Sully was going to ask him to get in there and give an example, but not today. Eddie was still a little full from that substandard chicken salad sandwich from the deli a few blocks down. The thought that there was something not quite right about that sandwich still lingered in the back of his mind, but as for now, he was going to have to let his stomach settle. Besides, why get into the ring with these two rookies when he was supposed to be saving his strength. Sully: "You guys want to take a look at a real wrestler? This here's Eddie Sanders, otherwise known as Shocker Sanders, or whatever the hell nickname he's going by this week." Eddie: "Awesome." Sully: "Whatever, let me tell you a little something about this man. Started coming here when he was seventeen years old, left here at eighteen. Has wrestled in about ten or fifteen different countries already, and has held titles in Japan and England as well. Already has held eleven different titles over his lifetime for eight different federations. You can learn a lot from this guy, and he's only a year older than you two clowns. And he's a rich bastard who didn't have to get into this business, but he loves it so much that he can't stay away. He's going to be a champion someday soon, going to have all the women and publicity that you two desperately want. Keep that name in mind, you'll be hearing it a lot someday." Eddie kept a grin on his face during the whole speech. He loved it when people talked about him, which is why he got into the business in the first place. He wanted to be mentioned in the same breath with all the other great superstars of the past, and soon enough, he was hoping that dream would come to fruition. Eddie: "Thanks Sully, but listen I gotta go. I think James might be getting ushered into a Satanic cult." Sully: "Yeah, yeah, whatever…go, get out of here. But come back soon, you hear? I would like to use you as a demonstration one day to show these guys what a real wrestler should look like." Eddie nodded his head and gave a wave over his shoulder as he headed out of the gym. It was good to reminisce sometimes, but he probably shouldn't have left James with those weirdoes. Outside the sun wasn't as vicious anymore, but it was still enough to cause a mighty glare in the lens. As Eddie walked for about twenty more seconds, he reached the building in which he came out of before. It was conveniently close by, which was good, as watching him walk for a little while could have been quite boring, unless he picked up a chick maybe. So Eddie opened the door and went into the building, but for some reason, the camera didn't follow. It just stayed there, watching other people pass by on the sidewalk, watching little kids riding by on bicycles, unaware of what might have been going on in this building. A cloud blocked out the harsh sun after another half a minute, just as the door swung open and two figures came running quickly towards the camera. It was Eddie, followed by James, who wasn't wearing any pants at the moment, but he was wearing one of those black robes. He kept pulling the robe over him as he ran so people on the street wouldn't see his underwear, but that was the least of his worries right now. The Warfield guy, who was speaking on the stage came out from the building, watching them run down the sidewalk with a furious look on his face. Warfield: "Come back here, you ungrateful sinner!" The shouting did nothing but draw attention to himself, something he didn't want. He quickly went back inside the building and closed the door behind him, as Eddie and James were slowly disappearing up the block. The screen started to fade out to black as the two friends got further and further away, and eventually could not be seen.
