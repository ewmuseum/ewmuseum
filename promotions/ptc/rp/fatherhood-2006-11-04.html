Fatherhood

4 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-04 The jingling of keys. The click of a deadbolt. The creak of an opening door. "Daddy’s home!" Saij stepped into his apartment and slammed the door shut behind him. The place was an absolute disaster area. Toys were scattered all over the living room floor: letter blocks, dolls, a push-and-ride car, a set of Thomas the Train Engine tracks, plastic dinosaurs, tiny musical instruments… everywhere. It looked as if Toys R’ Us had come over for a party, gotten wasted, puked all over the place, and left. The kitchen was worse off than the living room. A mess of dishes piled high in the sink, and the garbage disposal was stopped up. The trash bin in the corner was spilling over, attracting several flies. There was a carton of long-expired milk sitting in the fridge that Saij hadn’t bothered to throw out yet. Stacks of empty pizza boxes and baby food jars lay on the counter, dusted with crumbs and dried bits of who-knows-what. The place was a bacterium’s amusement park, and admission was free. Eating something off a locker room floor would have been more sanitary than eating something from Saij’s kitchen. If he’d had anything in the place to eat, that is. But the decrepit state of his apartment was far from Saij’s mind. At that moment, all he cared was how his baby was doing. "Leanne?" Saij looked around as he set his keys down on the dining table, which was covered in old newspapers and three months of unopened mail. The place was quiet. Too quiet. "Leanne?" Saij asked, his voice growing worried. "You there, sweetheart?" He listened silently for a reply. There was none. At least not at first. A few moments later, Saij could hear the pitter-patter of tiny feet, as a small figure came running up. Saij looked down, and smiled warmly. "Hey, thanks for watching Leanne for me," he said. The small figure, a midget, flailed his arms wildly in response. He was mute, and not at all proficient in American Sign Language, so the arm-flailing was his only form of communication. Exactly how Saij was able to interpret what he was saying was beyond anyone’s guess (and when it came to Saij, it was often best not to think long on certain things for the sake of one’s sanity), but they had enough of a rapport to understand one another. He was Saij’s long-time friend and sidekick from his XUW days: Hancock Jockensen, more famously known as the Phallic Phenom, the Midget Wunderkind… The Cockjockey! -~-~- BRIEF ASIDE IN WHICH THE LIFE STORY OF THE COCKJOCKEY IS DESCRIBED IN GREAT DETAIL* I. The Short Version The Cockjockey is a midget who carries around a giant inflatable penis with him at all times. He rides around on it like a child rides a toy stick horse, and uses it often to bludgeon people over the head. He carries fury in his eyes, piss and vinegar in his veins, and a song in his heart. II. The Long Version The Cockjockey is a midget who carries around a giant inflatable penis with him at all times. He rides around on it like a child rides a toy stick horse, and uses it often to bludgeon people over the head. He carries fury in his eyes, piss and vinegar in his veins, and a song in his heart. He rules and you suck. * (The words "brief" and "great" may or may not be juxtaposed) -~-~- "So is Leanne asleep?" Saij quietly asked, peeking into his daughter’s bedroom. The Cockjockey nodded. "That’s good. She behave herself?" The Cockjockey scowled, and held up his arm. It was covered in teeth marks. Saij grasped it gently and examined the tiny bites. "Huh, whaddaya know," Saij said finally. "She’s grown another tooth! Awesome!" The midget roughly pulled his arm away, and flipped off his taller friend. "Sheesh, sorry," said Saij, holding up his hands defensively. "I didn’t think she was that hungry. Although I guess there’s nothing to eat around here, huh? Crap, that’s right… I meant to go to the grocery store today. Ah well, maybe I’ll remember to do that tomorrow." The Cockjockey scowled, and flailed his arms. "I know I’ve been saying that for the past three days! I’ve been busy! Sheesh, gimme a break…" There was a knock on the front door. Saij frowned. Who was stopping by at this hour? He didn’t think anyone even knew he was back in town… not anyone that cared, at any rate. He approached the door, and peered through the peephole to see who his visitor was. It was a red-haired woman. A beautiful one at that. Saij seemed delighted. "Hey, it’s a chick!" he whispered excitedly to The Cockjockey. The midget shrugged, and wandered back to the bedroom to check again on Leanne. Saij’s face slid down the door, to a second peephole that was situated a foot below the first. He was now afforded a close-up view of the woman’s chest. "Booooooobies…" he drooled. The woman knocked on the door again, giving Saij a bit of a fright. "Fark!" he yelped as he jumped back. "Uh, hang on!" He unbolted the door and pulled it open for his visitor. -~-~- Brenda Clark’s nose wrinkled in disgust as she caught the first whiffs of Saij’s malodorous apartment. Her eyes immediately trailed to the toys scattered about the floor—nothing unusual about that, she thought. But what was that smell? "Do you have a cat or something?" was the first thing she asked. She looked up at Saij to see him simply gawking at her, his mouth stupidly agape. He grinned a cheesy grin and ran his hand through his hair, trying to look smooth. He wasn’t doing a very good job, she noticed—his gaze was inexplicably fixed right below her neckline. Trying to stay casual, Saij lifted his arm to lean coolly on the doorpost. He missed. Saij lost his balance and crashed onto the floor. Brenda sighed. Great, another schmuck who thinks with the wrong head, she thought as she smoothed her grey slacks and adjusted her blouse. "Uh, what’d you say?" Saij asked, scrambling back to his feet. "I said…" she started. She halted in mid-sentence as she looked at him. A spoon was sticking to the front of his face. "You uh…you have a…" she said slowly, then tapped her forehead. "Huh? Oh," Saij replied, grasping the spoon and pulling it off. He examined the back of it. "Thanks… wow, this thing’s pretty sticky. What is this, peanut butter?" He licked it, which caused Brenda to grimace. "Hm, nope… pudding. At least it was at one point. Man, I really should get to washing the dishes one of these months." He unceremoniously tossed it back on the floor. "Anyway… hey, are you alright?" Brenda’s face had quickly turned a pale shade of green, and she looked as if she might be sick—and unfortunately, she wasn’t going to puke up toys. "I think I need to sit down," she said finally. "Oh, sure." Saij motioned her inside, and pulled a chair from the dining table for her. "Thanks," Brenda replied weakly. She lowered her head and took a few deep breaths to calm herself down. "Hang on, I’ll get you a glass of water." Saij went into the kitchen area, and sifted the dishes around to get to the faucet. "Thank you," Brenda said once more. "I’m sorry to say it, but there’s something that doesn’t smell quite right in here—that’s why I was asking if you had a cat or something." "A cat?" Saij replied as he wiped down a glass with his shirt. "Nope, no cats here. I dunno what smell you’re talking about either. I guess it might be the kitchen, but I’m pretty used to it so I don’t really notice anything." "The kitchen…?" Brenda looked up, and saw the horrific mess that was Saij’s kitchen. She was appalled. "Ugh, oh God…" Brenda was positively disgusted, and unfortunately it wasn’t anything she wasn’t used to seeing in her line of work. Such filthy conditions… Brenda had half a mind to grab Saij’s daughter and leave right then and there. But, she had to go through the formalities and at least ask a few questions, so she could gauge what neglect or other maltreatment was taking place—although she was already getting a clear and scary picture of the conditions that poor child lived under. She was also beginning to think Saij wasn’t even mentally fit for parenthood, and she’d only been in his company for two minutes. Then again, that was everyone’s reaction. Saij smiled as he brought the glass to her. "Uh, thanks," she said, not at all thankful. She set the glass down as far away from her as possible. "So, what’d you say your name was again?" Saij asked, pulling out a chair for himself and sitting down. "I didn’t. Brenda Clark. I work for the state child welfare office." Saij wasn’t really paying attention—his gaze had returned once more to her chest. "The what? The booby welfare office? Well that’s good, we always need people looking out for the welfare of boobies!" "No, Mr. Jeffries, the child welfare office!" Brenda growled. "I’m here to ask you a few questions about you… and your daughter." "Me and Leanne, huh?" Saij grinned devilishly. "Interested in the stepmother position, eh? Well I’m flattered, but I hardly even know you…" "Mr. Jeffries, please. This isn’t a social call. This is government business." She dug into her briefcase and pulled out a pen and legal pad. "I don’t intend to take up much of your time. I just need to ask a few questions regarding the upbringing of your daughter, and her environment." Saij sighed. This woman was no fun at all. "Alright, fine," he said. "Truth be told, I’m too busy to be romantically involved with anyone right now." "I see," said Brenda, rolling her eyes. "Well, that brings me to my first… uh, who is that?" She pointed in the direction of Leanne’s bedroom, as The Cockjockey emerged once more. The Midget Wunderkind stopped in his tracks at the sight of his best friend sitting down at a table with a total stranger. "That? Oh, that’s Cockjockey," Saij said off-handedly. "He was watching Leanne for me while I was out. Hey Cockjockey," he motioned to the little man, "this is Brenda! You know, the chick who was knocking at the door! Yeah, she’s from the child welfare office or whatever. Cool, huh?" The Cockjockey looked very worried at the mention of "child welfare office." Unlike Saij, he was no fool—those words didn’t bode well with him in the least. "So he was watching your daughter, was he?" Brenda asked. "While you were out?" "Well, part of the time anyway," Saij said, as The Cockjockey joined them at the table. "I had to give him a call when I was thrown in jail earlier tonight." "Ah yes, you were in jail," Brenda nodded, making a note. "I know. I was in the building not too long after you were let go… Anyway, you said your friend here was watching Leanne for part of the time. Who else was watching her?" Saij looked confused. "Huh?" he asked. "Who else was watching her? Before him?" "Well, nobody." "Nobody?" Brenda asked, astonished. "You mean you left her here all alone!?" "Well, not alone," Saij explained. "I mean she had her toys and the TV and stuff. She wasn’t gonna be bored or anything while I was gone." The Cockjockey clapped a hand on his forehead, as if to say "you fucking idiot." Brenda quickly scribbled a few more notes down. "Mr. Jeffries, what in the world made you think it was okay to leave a little girl of eighteen months home by herself? And in these horrible conditions… for God’s sake, when was the last time you cleaned your kitchen?" "Let’s see…" Saij leaned casually back in his chair and thought. "I’d say… about… three…" "Days?" "Months." "Months!?" Brenda was aghast. "Yeah, three months," Saij confirmed. "We only got back a few days ago. We’ve been traveling all over since August, since I had to look for work and everything. So I just left the apartment and we lived out of hotels and whatnot. I guess I probably shoulda cleaned up before taking off like that… but, oh well." "How can you be so nonchalant about this?" Brenda asked, surprised. "Do you realize what kind of situation you’ve put your daughter in? You clearly lack a suitable, clean environment for raising a child," she motioned around the messy apartment. "The kitchen is a filthy cesspool. Looking around this place, you don’t appear to have any child-proof locks on the cupboard doors, or child-proof covers on the electrical outlets, or child-proof… anything. You left your child alone for a whole evening…" "Only part of it!" Saij protested. "…and on top of all that, you got yourself thrown in jail! From what I understand, it was for causing a public disturbance." "Huh? Oh yeah, I got in a fight with a duck." Brenda blinked. "Did you say a… duck?" "Yep," Saij said matter-of-factly. "A duck. Why, is there a problem with that?" "You don’t find getting into a fight with a duck to be an unusual occurrence?" Brenda asked. "Not really," Saij shrugged. "I mean I’m flying out to Singapore in a few days to wrestle it in a match." "Wrestle… the duck?" "Yep." "The same duck?" "Did I farkin’ stutter or something? Yes it’s the same duck!" Brenda made another note. "Well, on top of everything else, you strike me as being mentally unstable too…" "Yeah, I get that a lot," Saij smiled. The Cockjockey buried his face in his hands and shook his head. "There’s one other thing I don’t quite understand," Brenda continued. "You’re not hurting for money, from what I’ve been told. You could easily afford childcare for your daughter while you’re on the road. You could easily afford maid service for your apartment, for that matter. In fact, you could be living in a nice house instead of this dump. Why haven’t you done anything to improve your living conditions?" She fully expected another half-assed reply, but once again Saij surprised her. He didn’t say anything at first. In fact, he seemed a bit… somber. Saij looked thoughtfully at Brenda for a moment, then leaned back in his chair and sighed. He fiddled with a small gold chain that hung around his neck for a bit, then answered quietly, "I just gotta do things my way. Take care of certain things myself. I have my reasons." "Well, whether or not the reasons are good, your actions do not seem to be in the best interests of your daughter. I’m not even sure if you realize this, but you’re not giving Leanne the care she needs to grow up to be a healthy, happy girl." Saij frowned. "What’re you saying, exactly?" "I’m saying that I’m going to have to take—" Before Brenda could finish her thought, The Cockjockey leapt up onto the table and kicked over the glass of water that had been sitting there… and it spilled all over Brenda! "AGH!" she screamed, jumping out of her chair. She glared at The Cockjockey, who grinned innocently. "You little shit, you got that filthy water all over me!" Brenda hissed. "Cockjockey!" Saij scolded, getting up from his seat. "What the fark did ya do that for? Sorry about that, Brenda. There’re some…" He stopped. The water had soaked her blouse, which he could now see through completely. "Booooooobies…" "MR. JEFFRIES!" "Right, sorry! There’re some towels in the bathroom, you can dry yourself off in there." He pointed down the hall and sat back in his chair quickly. Brenda stormed off, mumbling obscenities under her breath. "Geez, dude," Saij shook his head at The Cockjockey. "What got into you? We were just having a pleasant conversation, then you go and farkin’ kick water on her!" The Cockjockey smacked Saij across the face. "OW!" Saij coiled back, rubbing his reddened cheek. "Holy fark, what was that for? Did somebody piss in your Cheerios this morning?" The Cockjockey flailed his arms desperately. "Wait, what? Brenda’s gonna take Leanne away from me?" The Cockjockey nodded. Saij looked baffled. "But uh… isn’t that like… kidnapping or something?" The Cockjockey smacked his hand on his forehead then flailed his arms again. "She’s allowed to take her away from me!? But she’s from the child welfare office! How is taking a child away from its father good for the child’s welfare!?" The Cockjockey rolled his eyes, and flailed his arms less fervently. "Oh, that’s child welfare does? Fark, I didn’t know that. If I did I probably would have kept my mouth shut about leaving Leanne here by herself, and all the other stupid stuff I said. Man, I can be an idiot sometimes." More arm flailing. "Okay, pretty much all the time. But forget all that. I need your help…" -~-~- Brenda was outraged as she did her best to dry off with a towel, which she had carefully inspected before using to make sure it was actually clean. Of all the cases she’d handled, she’d never come across someone as bizarre as Saij. He wasn’t by any means the worst human being she’d ever encountered—there were some real deadbeats she’d had to deal with in her career. And she could tell that he did have a kind heart and meant well. But good intentions weren’t enough to keep a child safe. She knew that Saij was incapable of properly raising a child, and it was time to take action. She was going to have to take Leanne away, and get her into the foster care system as quickly as possible. She looked at herself in the mirror, and straightened her frazzled hair. It was time. This was probably the most unpleasant part of her job, separating a child from its parent… but it was for the best. Brenda turned and opened the bathroom door. She walked out. The front door was wide open. Saij was nowhere to be seen. "Mr. Jeffries?" she beckoned. No answer. "Mr. Jeffries, are you there?" She stepped forward toward the living room, looking around the apartment. There was still no answer. "Shit," she said to herself. He couldn’t have gotten far, though… maybe she could still catch him… She turned to leave, but something was blocking her way. It was The Cockjockey. He was standing in the doorway, with a sash tied around his forehead. The Phallic Phenom brandished his giant inflatable penis, holding it like a Samurai wields a katana. "Um… could you please step aside?" Brenda asked tentatively. The Cockjockey smiled… and charged. -~-~- Saij ran from his apartment building with his car keys clenched between his teeth, and carrying Leanne. She was wrapped up in a warm blanket, and fast asleep. A scream came from his window. "YEEEAAGH!! GET AWAY FROM ME, YOU LITTLE PRICK! STOP HITTING ME WITH THAT THING!" Saij grinned slyly to himself. Perfect. This would give him just enough time to get away. He reached his car and quickly unlocked it. He secured Leanne in her baby seat, then got in himself and started the engine. He looked back at his precious little girl, who was dreaming sweet dreams. How peaceful she looked… not a care in the world. "It’ll be okay, baby," Saij said gently. "I won’t let them take you from me, no matter what it takes." He fastened his seat belt, and put his hand on the gearshift… then stopped. He clutched at the gold chain around his neck once again, and pulled it out from under his shirt. Dangling from the chain were two wedding bands, linked together. He kissed them, and looked upward. "No matter what it takes," he whispered. He shifted into drive, pulled out, and sped down the road and into the night. There was no question that Saij was fiercely loyal to the ones he loved. But Brenda Clark was right… Leanne was going to be far better off without her father.
