Never Gonna Stop...

21 September 2004

Date: 2004-09-21 [The GTT logo explodes across the screen over the quaint urban streets of Santa Barbara, California. It is Saturday morning, about 9 AM, and the sun is already blazing showing its dominance over the town. Some people sleep in late, either getting over from the hangover from the following Friday night outing, or relaxing after the grueling weekly grind. Some children are planted in front of the television being taught the lessons of Saturday morning cartoons, or sharpening their skills in front of their favorite X-box or Play station 2 games, one shows a child playing the latest hot new wrestling video game to hit the market today, pretending to live the action of his favorite superstars in the ring through the help of high tech CGI programming. And as he goes from level to level living in his imaginary video world, he’s unaware of what it actually takes to live that life, the work, pain, and sacrifice the actual people go through which to him seems as simple as tapping the button on his controller; more so is he unaware that outside his door is a person that actually is living that life he fantasizes over. Out side in the suburban streets, where except for the colors, the homes look all alike, where little by little the inhabitants of the community scamper out to do the daily chores of maintain their abodes, or their vehicles; they stop to notice that one of their own isn’t exactly like one of their own. The first one notices is the powerful and curvy bare long legs covered in sweat as they stride like a gazelle taking three quarters of a block at a time, which go all the way up to the skimpy red stain running shorts from Everlast. Higher up is the washboard 6 and a ½ pack abs which tense up every time she hits the pavement showing the steel like definition they possess, next are the very toned an powerful biceps and forearms, which are softened by her short manicured pink fingernails showing that power and femininity that co-exist together. Her black Nike Sports bra top becomes darker from the sweat which soaks it, as her 36D’s softly bounce up and down to the rhythm of her run, while her erect nipples form a mold in her top from the morning breeze still in the air catching the attention of the males her age and older as they watch with awe. From the back one could see her mini cobra which put a lot of her male counterparts to shame, that flowed down to her curvy half Brazilian tight rear the cause of a young man being BITCHSLAPPED by his girlfriend for taking too hard of a look. Facial wise she had a very strong jaw like, but that was the only thing strong about her as her lightly tanned skin glistened from the sweat pouring down her face, with her bright green eyes, soft pink lips, and flowing red hair held down with a blue bandanna. She looked as if she could be a model for a teen magazine, or one of those girl next door videos. At 7 foot 245 lbs, Caitlin Fairchild was the freakish mixture of beauty and power forever to be a spectacle for the mere mortals around her. The young Amazon was cursed with acromegaly, a disease that results in an over abundance of growth hormones, also known as Gigantism, something the late great Andre the Giant had to deal with for the majority of his legendary yet short life span. At the age of 16 Caitlin had already reached way over the 6 foot mark, and viewed as a freak in her young adult life, loving adoptive parents who refused to see her crushed were her support in carrying the heavy burden, along with medication, and a strict training regime and diet to keep her weight in check. All of this proved to be more of a reward as her body matured shaping itself into something people would marvel at for years to come. As Caitlin continued her morning run through her neighborhood her mind wandered through different turning points in her life. Finding her love in wrestling, being the first female on her high school and college wrestling team, becoming the first female NCAA champion in her weight class, her journey to Canada where she had the privilege of training the in the late Stu Hart’s dungeon, her first professional gig in the FCW which flopped, the embarrassing release from her contract. Nights of living in run down hotels, and sometimes in her car with her best friend/manager as she refused to run home, dedicated to living her dream. Her second chance given by VSE owner Tommy Craze, where she became not only the first woman to hold the VSE People’s Champion, but the first female VSE World Tag Team Champion, and through all her hard work and dedication given the chance of a life time, to become the first female to hold the VSE World Heavyweight Championship, her heart would continue to pound as she remembered that big day at of VSE PPV Obsession, for the World title where too rookies, a top contender, and the champion himself would battle it out in a 4 way Elimination Ladder match, two would be eliminated, and the last two would battle it out ladder style for the belt hanging for the world to see, a war which came down to her and the most Sadistic man in the company, the current World Champion known as the Dawg, who had no desire to lose his title to a “little” girl. Caitlin would remember her body being driven to the mat every time she attempted to reach the belt, first by a superplex off the ladder followed by the Spiral Powerbomb. She would remember the chair shot that would bust her open causing her to take her own blood in the first time in her career. How her adrenaline level exploded as she could hear the screams of the people while the Dawg went up to claim his title causing her to fight on pure instinct as she rushed up the ladder after him, and brought him down with a Russian Legsweep that would almost break both their bodies. The Burning Hammer: Reverse Death Valley Driver head first into the steel steel chair, a brutal desperation move she would execute to put the Dawg out of the match for good, and grant her safe passage to the World title, the tears that would fall as she fell off the ladder to the floor clutching the belt close she knew that the young age of 19 she had reached the top of the mountain, the highest pinnacle of this sport; it was terrifying and exhilarating at the same time, but what truly brought her to tears what that through all her hard work, all the pain, blood, sweat and tears her burden was finally been lifted…people didn’t look at her any more as a freak, but as the Heavyweight Champion of the World. Eight months after that eventful night Caitlin still held the mantel as World Champion defeating all who would dare try to take her title, opportunities would begin to open for her left and right as younger women and girls would want to be her, while men would just want her. She would travel to distant lands, compete in the largest arenas, eat as the most expensive restaurants, and sleep in the most lavish of hotels, buy a little home for her and her partner in crime, her best friend in the world, and drive a vehicle (fit for her of course). At the age of 20 Caitlin would have it all, accomplish what took many in her profession a life time to do if they could even achieve it. So why wasn’t she slowing down? Maybe because she had no intention of slowing down…maybe because she knew she owed something to all those who were still sleeping in the parked cars, and run down dirty hotels, working high school gyms, in front of fans of 20 to 30 people, getting paid 250 dollars a show which barely took them throughout half the week, all for the opportunity to get a taste of what Caitlin had right now. So she had a new burden a much lighter one though that would reap bigger rewards, remaining on top of the mountain as long as possible, and as she remained on top of her mountain she would learn to fly and take to the stars to reach the level that only a hand full have the privilege to become…immortal. That’s why when Caitlin heard about GTT4 from her long lost older sister Patti Anderson, she took the chance to decided to enter the competition, what better way to learn to spread her wings than to come out the champion in a world-wide competition, to be known world-wide as the young Amazon that shook the foundation of the GTT. Where her sister had failed, she wouldn’t, round one gave her a BYE which allowed her to observe and study the competition as well as her opponent for the next round, a cruiserweight style wrestler by the name of Horazon from the now defunct federation known as FAW apparently retired, returning with one last try to make a name for himself. Caitlin knew that coming in he would have ring rust, that it would take close to 6 months to a year for him to become a 100 percent again; she had the advantage, and although she pitied his attempt knowing what it’s like to be at the bottom, and having to fight her way to the top, she knew she could not show any mercy in that ring, because in this business mercy could kill your career…besides, she knew that if reversed he wouldn’t show her any either. Caitlin slowed her stride as she neared her beach house home stopping at her drive way which housed Roxy’s supped up Fast and Furious type purple and white stripped custom Mini, and her custom red and black Humvee 2. As she rested again her vehicle, she checked her sports watch on her wrist while she took her pulse, 1 hour and 30 seconds flat, better than her last time of an hour and 3 minutes. Her pulse slowed down to a smooth rhythm as she began to breath easy. As Caitlin turned to enter her home she’s met with a flying blue towel covering her face.] Caitlin Fairchild – HEY!! [Caitlin removed the towel to see her manager/best friend in a Spiderman 2 XL shirt with matching boxer shorts, and a black G-Unit bandanna covering her purple and lavender hair with furry green dinosaur slippers that make roaring sounds when she walks. Her eyes half closed trying to adjust to the sun suggesting that she just woke up.] Free Fall – Had a good run? Caitlin Fairchild (Wiping her self down.) - Beat my old time. Free Fall – Makin breakfast… Caitlin Fairchild (Smiles.) – Cool…need something before I hit the gym. [Caitlin walks up the steps as Roxy shakes her head in disbelief.] Free Fall – Girl…don’t you EVER stop? Caitlin Fairchild (Smiles looking at her.) – Why would I? [The GTT logo explodes across the screen as Caitlin heads into the house followed by Roxy as her slippers proceed to make the disturbing roaring noise as the scene fades to black.]
