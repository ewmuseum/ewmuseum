To which flag do I raise up high?

16 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-16 Solitude, the state that was but is no more. What once was a darkened corner has been lit upon by a heady yet vital source. It would be none other than my beloved Television title. This simple piece of gold and leather has brought so much into my life, more than you will ever know. For once, I could sleep peacefully, I didn't have to constantly submit in defeat against this ravenous mind of mine. My dreams are now pleasant, ones of hope and future aspiration, not like before where I would have to scream, only to be heard by no one. My existence was basically futile until I found the key to unlock the door of this enigmatic, and with that releasing the past to be forgotten forever. This is a new beginning for me, my life was reset on the day that I won the WWA Television Championship. And since there, new found respect has found me, people actually acknowledge me now. Not like back in the day where I was left behind by the conformist hypocrisy known as ... "Society" If only they could see me now, climbing the mountain of success and getting closer and closer to the summit of glory. The journey is defined and the result is inevitable. So what are they doing now? They are imprisoned in a life that has no purpose and no direction. They confide in cubicles and are stationed at desks for the majority of their existence. And what for? What's the point? I cannot understand it. That is why I am here and what I have done. I have achieved more than them now then they ever will. Best part about it is that there is still more for me to reach for. As the saying goes ... "The sky's the limit" Only in the great industry of ours can such a feat be achieved. This is the greatest thing in the world and only those who love it, are the ones that understand it. The rest of the world will never understand it as long as they remain on the outside. We are different in some way and that is why we congregate within that squared circle. We are different to the rest of the world. We are unique, we are better. That is why now it is them who are on the outside looking in. "Fate is not without a sense of irony" But enough about them, what about us? This industry is greater than ever before, once was confined but since has expanded to the outer regions of this earth. But just like anything else that exists, conflict lies within. Spanning the world over, federations, alliances, companies, groups, whatever the organisation, they are all trying to be the best. Pure talent, dirty tricks or just a beefy wallet are some of the tactics used in trying to out shine one another. But there is only one was to determine who is the best and that is done by what is being done. "Survival" Putting the best stars head to head in a series of matches to settle all the talk and feud. Now as I represent the WWA by being their TV champ, it was only fitting that they assign me to their official team in this contest. They made the right choice. Things are finally coming round for the Juce. My journey has mainly been about one simple thing. "Being heard!" What better way to do it by taking it to other feds and showing them that the Green Plague can be something to fear. What better way for the sickness to spread than unleashing it upon many different feds. There survival will then appear to be impossible. But I am not alone in this contest. While I have a different purpose than my combatants, we share one thing. We represent the World Wrestling Alliance. Myself, Mr. Darkness, Muppet Man and the Pain Syndicate have been chosen. Why? Several reasons are possible but only those who chose us know. Are we the biggest stars? The best wrestlers? The great up and comers? Or maybe, the sickness has spread through out the ranks of the WWA? As I said, many a reason is possible. But one thing is for sure, the WWA is going to take all these other feds to the limit. I've never heard of any of these places before and I'm not sure of who they are or what they can do. Frankly it doesn't worry me. Nothing does. But one thing has my concern, a conflict within lets say. "Which one will it be?" I realize that I am a part of a team here, I've been forced to be for sometime now. To be honest, it is driving me a little insane. The issue of trust and reliance was never a problem for me. But now, I'm not so sure anymore. First it was Global Warfare, then a tag match at Meltdown and now this. I don't want to play with others. I am the Juce. All I need to rely in is myself. I was yet to be pinned in a match until GW where the "team" let me down. "Which one will it be?" But I guess now is a little different as we all fly the same flag. But that is a decision I have yet to reach. "Which one will it be?" I DON'T KNOW! IT'S DIFFICULT TO DECIDE! JUST GIVE ME SOME TIME! " ... " Deep breathes ... deep breathes ... Each journey does not exist without a fork in the road. A decision to be made that leads to a brand new path. Which one is right? You never know until the destination has been reached and by that time there is no turning back. Every decision could be your last. Always remember that there are two sides to everything. The WWA has given me some great opportunities to achieve my goals and has given me a home. They also gave me the chance to claim this TV title and for that I will always thank them. But then, teams have failed me in the past. It has been something I have never fully understood. The team atmosphere is just so different than when your by yourself. More concerns and more demands. On the other side, if I stand alone and prevail than even more opportunities will present itself. I know I have what it takes, I trust the sickness to assist. But if I fail ... hmmm. There is a fine line I tread, the difference between standing alone and acting alone. Most people think by what I just said is that I'm going to go out there and screw my team mates over. You are too quick to judge. As I have said previously, I'm not like that. I do things for me, it's the 'tear away' concept. "I don't care about anyone else but me" I am apart of this team but it is still undecided whether I will be a part of that team. This I will need to think over, more so than the match itself. The feds we rival have been nothing to fear me. But they should consider if I am something to fear them? Normally I would return to the darkness from which I came, but that is a thing of the past. There is only one place that I am destined to go to ... "The Summit Of Glory"
