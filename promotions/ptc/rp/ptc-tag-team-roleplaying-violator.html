Tag Team Roleplaying

6 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The rain beats down upon a nearly abandoned cemetery. Above an open grave, dug swiftly and with a vengeance, we glance. Inside of this quickly formed resting place, a pair of tangled beings flail. "Why you gotta be like that man!?" "Prepare for total violation!" Like zombies returning from the hereafter, their hands reach up, clutching the edge of the grave. Pulling themselves up, Scourge and the Violator moan. Scourge: That guy don't treat us right, man! Violator: I am the Violator! Brushing dirt from himself, the eternal bestiality loving jobber known only as Scourge looks on into the gloom. Scourge: We come out here to this cemetery, and what thanks do we get? Violator: Prepare for total violation! Scourge: That's right! We get pushed into a damn grave by Clyde! Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: Well, I've had enough, man! Violator: (Shaking his fist) Prepare for total violation! Scourge: I've had it! Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: Today is the day it stops! Violator: PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION! Both Scourge and the Violator look at each other, as thunder booms overhead. Scourge: ......so yeah, how're we gonna stop it? Violator: (Shrugging) I am the Violator. Scourge: Gah! Thrusting his hands deep into his pockets, Scourge begins to walk. His brow furrows, as he thinks deeply....and finally, he raises his head as a light bulb appears over it. Scourge: I've got it! Violator: (Tilting his head at the bulb) Prepare for total violation? Scourge: What? No man, I know what we have to do! Scourge grins broadly, turning to face his longtime friend. His smile fades though, as he sees the Violator more focused on examining the mysterious light bulb which he has now fetched from midair. Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: (Snatching the bulb) Gimme that! Violator: Prepare for total violation!!!! As Scourge tosses the glass bulb over his shoulder with a crash, Violator sighs. Scourge: We need to enter UWF too! Violator: ......I am the Violator? Scourge: Yeah, man! See, I've got this all planned out. There ARE no tag teams in UWF, man! Pretty much, we can just walk right in and we WILL be the champs! Violator: Prepare for total violation. Scourge: ........what do you mean we haven't won a match in ten years? What's that got to do with anything? Violator: (Waving his arms) I am the Violator! Scourge: Why you gotta be like that man? Even we can't lose a match if we're not up against anybody! Violator: Prepare for total violation! I am the Violator! Scourge: ......okay, fine, we might at that. You just gotta ruin all my fun, don't you? Violator: Prepare for total violation. Scourge sits upon the rain soaked ground, holding his head in his hands. Scourge: Haven't won a match in ten years. Have we even been a damn team for ten years? Violator: I am the Violator!!!! Abruptly, Scourge looks up again, as another light bulb appears over his cranium. Scourge: I've got it! Violator: (Freaking out and pointing) PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!!!!! Scourge: What.....oh forget the damn light bulb, man! This is important! Reaching up and pulling down the second bulb, Scourge tosses it away and looks to his friend. Scourge: We're gonna get YOU to enter this tag tourney coming up to get us started. Violator: .......I am the Violator. Scourge: Think about it, man! We may have not won a match in ten years, true. But YOU, I'm pretty sure you've won at least one this decade. So, we put you into the tag team circuit by yourself. That way, our chances of winning go up by at least fifty percent! Violator merely stares at Scourge, attempting to process his logic. Before he can though, his friend continues. Scourge: And besides, think of it man! The fame! Violator: Prepare for total violation! Scourge: The money! Violator: I am the Violator! Scourge: The horny french poodles waiting to satisfy your every sexual whim! Violator: I am the Viola...... The big man pauses mid-sentence, once again staring. Violator: ......tor? Scourge: So yeah, I'm gonna sign you up! We can do this! Do it for us, big man! Do it for America! Do it for little Timmy who isn't gonna get a Christmas tree this year! Violator: (Wiping a tear and nodding) Prepare for total violation!!!! With a mighty bellow, the Violator throws his arms in the air and charges off into the night, in the direction of God only knows where. Scourge: ......hey, wait for me man! Wait, WAIT! And as a man gives chase after a mentally challenged friend who may or may not actually top him in the brains department, we mercifully fade to black.....
