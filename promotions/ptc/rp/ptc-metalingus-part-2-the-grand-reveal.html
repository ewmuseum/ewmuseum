Metalingus (Part 2: The Grand Reveal)(ETD Title Shot)

Chapter Four: A DEAL WITH THE DEVIL Tick, Tock Tick, Tock Tick, Tock Staring up at the clock, The Black Mariah waited patiently as time ticked away. Vivica took a deep breath and blew the blonde hair with baby blue tips out of her face, with her feet kicked up on the massive mahogany wood meeting table in front of her. So many times she'd been in this room over the years, it was certainly a room that she never thought she'd see again. Behind her was a glass window that took up the entire wall, on the other side was a breath taking view of the city. The cerulean eyes of The Fearless Phenom shifted over towards the window, where the sun was rising behind a wall of gray. Slowly snowflakes trickled down from the sky, dancing in the wind. The image took Vivica to a much simpler time at home in Highland Park. The holidays gone by were really the only good memories of her youth. When you still believed in magic, the tooth fairy, the Easter bunny, and above all else Santa Claus. Waking up Christmas morning before the sun rose and looking at all the presents under the illuminated Christmas tree. A time where she was so jaded. The saying was true, ignorance was bliss. Now is seemed like her world was full of lies, deceit, and frustration. So much so that she'd almost forgotten that Christmas was under a week away. She hadn't bought a thing... "Viv?" The sound of a voice snapped her back into reality again. Looking back towards the table she saw Christian Zenith in the doorway. Always dressed for success, he wore a different custom made black business suit. His eyes matched the color of the clouds that floated across the sky in front of him. "Sorry... I just have a lot on my mind." Vivica answered. "I've pulled some strings and it looks like PrimeTime Central was more than willing to give you a rematch for the Extreme Title Division." Christian paused before going on, "You really didn't need me at all, you would've gotten it anyway. They must really have a problem with that Archibald MacGregor. Maybe even more than you." The name still made her blood boil. Sitting up straight, Vivica began to take notice. Beautiful green-blue eyes narrowed, turning into spiraling balls of scorn. Arms once relaxed across her chest balled into fists. "I doubt that." Vivica mumbled under her breath. Missing Rich Rollins wasn't the biggest disappointment of PRIME's Jewel In The Crown tournament, as you might be lead to believe from Steven Caldera. Rich Rollins was just a pawn on her way to Archibald MacGregor, another goal that seemed to slip through the cracks. "All the paperwork is finished," Christian said, "You're well on your way to becoming the next PrimeTime Central Extreme Title Division Champion." "The championship don't really mean much to me..." Vivica finally fessed up, "It was all about the championship the first time. That sure as hell didn't work. I wouldn't care if he would've pinned me in the middle of the ring like a man. That asshole deceived me... he crossed me over and made me look like a fool. Embarrassing me for the entire world to believe." "He really DID get to you... didn't he?" Christian question as he approached meeting table. Pulling out a chair he sat down. Reaching his arm across the table as if to comfort The Black Mariah. Deep down inside, she didn't want to trust him... but it was so hard to resist. After so many years, she'd grown accustomed to seeing his face. As much as she might've hated to admit it, she almost missed the bastard. She answered in silence looking away from Christian toward the window, trying to go back to the happy place she was just a few moments ago. "How's Markus Blair holding up?" Christian asked, trying to change the subject before he lost Vivica's interest entirely. Markus... how could she forget?! "He doesn't go by Markus Blair anymore." Vivica replied, "It's Markus Eaux D'Artifice." "My mistake, I had forgotten since that whole Total J Cup fiasco." The Midnight Messiah responded, "Is he getting proper nourishment? Is he taking to real blood?" "He isn't eating." Vivica answered, "He doesn't know what's wrong, I don't know what's wrong. I think he just needs to make an adjustment to his life. He's having trouble accepting..." "That he's a vampire?" Christian answered almost as if he knew already. There was a reason he was one of the richest and most powerful men in Chicago. He may be arrogant and he may be a bastard, but he was a smart bastard. Every move was calculating, every word was well planned ahead of time. He wanted to hear the right things, and get the answers he needed. "I don't know... what he is." Vivica put her hand on her head, looking down towards the ground, "Although, that is the only thing that would make sense to me. How did that happen Christian?! That's not what he signed up for." "I wouldn't say completely that he's a vampire..." Zenith said walking around the table behind the bewildered Black Mariah, "He's unstable, he needs to be monitored so his condition can be stabilized. With my equipment I can help him, bring him back to health just like he was. Maybe even make him human again." "I don't know if that's wh..." Vivica tried to respond before she was interrupted. "You do care about him... don't you?" Christian answered, knowing what Vivica's response would be. She'd grown quite fond of him, ever since she was forced to go get him down in Mexico. He was genuinely a nice guy... it was a shame that they had to have met under the wrong circumstances. Vivica couldn't possibly imagine being in his position, wandering around life not knowing if you're dead or alive. Not knowing what you are when you wake up in the morning. It must be devastating. "He's at the McFarlane Estate." Vivica answered without even giving a second thought. After the words had left her mouth she had realized what she'd done. Was this all really just a trap, a trap to take back Markus? Had revenge consumed her so much that she'd rat out possibly the closest friend she had left? "Do you know your way around? Could you escort him back to the medical facility?" Christian asked immediately, without even giving Vivica a chance to think about her reply. She'd already spoken too much, there was no turning back now. "I'll take him back... under one condition." Vivica answered boldly, "I want The Hunting Grounds inside the arena during the ETD match. Archibald MacGregor is going to get the ass kicking that he deserves, I want him mentally and physically destroyed in the middle of that ring. Just exactly like he fucking did to me." A deal with the devil. Her actions couldn't be described as anything more or less than that. A smirk crossed the face of the quick witted, raven haired business man next to her. His answer would follow shortly afterwards. "You have yourself a deal Miss Valentine." Chapter Five: THE DESCENT Outside the McFarlane Estate, in Toronto, Ontario, Canada... the crisp air blew through the golden blonde hair of Vivica J. Valentine. Standing outside the door sighed, and watched her warm breath float freely through the air. Taking a glance back at Jason LeMaster and six other members of The Hunting Grounds, she knew there was no turning back now. Her friends that she had taken in may very well be gone forever. Everything that she had worked hard for since leaving Global Wrestling was gone, and she was back to square one. Back to where she started... living in the shadows of Christian Zenith. What had she become? "A winner." She said outloud to herself. That was what she was going to become... maybe even Shadow would be proud of her. She wouldn't go out to the ring and lose every night anymore. No more smart ass comments from Virgil Ambrosi in the locker room. She was going to be a winner, she was going to be famous. She may even one day be the greatest of all-time. People would forget the night Archibald MacGregor won the PTC Extreme Title. They would remember when Vivica J. Valentine made it her own. "Let's go." Vivica pushed the door open after a bit of convincing herself to go through with it. On the other side was the place that she had called her home for the past couple of months. She tried not to look around too much... she didn't want to see the look on their faces as she did what she had to do. But destiny certainly wasn't going to come to her, she had to take it for herself. As they walked up the stairwell, almost everyone seemed to be gone or asleep. She had planned it as such, but didn't believe it would work so well. It made everything a little bit easier. The small army marched their way through the dimly lit, narrow halls. Halfway through Vivica pointed at a door on the right, inside was Markus Eaux D'Artifice... probably trembling in pain from his diet. "It's for his own good..." Vivica said under her breath as she pushed the door open. Inside two pairs of eyes shot up at them. The eyes of Markus Eaux D'Artifice laying down in bed, sprawled out in pain next to Lia (known to Shadow in most circles) sitting in a chair next to the bed. She looked up in confusion before standing up and walking over to Vivica. "What's going on here?" Shadow asked, almost challenging The Black Mariah. "I'm here to take back Markus." Vivica answered shying away from eye contact. "To where Vivica... where are you taking him." Lia questioned The Fearless Phenom who just looked away. She couldn't look her in the eyes, it was too painful. "Markus is going back to Zenith Corporation... where he belongs." Jason LeMaster answered for Vivica, who was completely lost for words. Lia looked back at Markus, he was obviously sick... but no one deserved the animalistic treatment that was surely on the other side of the Zenith Corporation walls. Plus genetically... he was the next line. Almost like her own son. "Please Lia..." Vivica answered finally looking into the stern eyes of her tag team partner, "It's for me... it's for his own good. Look at him, he's sick. He needs our help." The look of Shadow could've burned holes through any man who had the guts to stare her in the face. But reluctantly she stepped aside and let Jason LeMaster and his worthless grunts pick Markus Eaux D'Artifice off the bed and carry him outside the door. Vivica J. Valentine watched as they carried him down the hallway... he went, against his best interest. He was in no shape to fight. "You know what he's going to do to him don't you?" Shadow asked Vivica, who was in the room alone with her. "Yeah..." Vivica said, "But it's something I have to do." The Black Mariah walked out of the room and closed the door behind her, leaving Shadow alone in the room that once belonged to Markus. She looked down at the sheets and simply shook her head. Chapter Six: INTENTIONS CLEAR Click... Click... Click... The sound of footsteps resonated through the hallway. It had been a few days since she had made a deal with evil personified, but the dirty feeling hadn't really gone away... regardless of why she made the decisions that she did. Each step down the hallway she grew closer to the room of Markus Eaux D'Artifice. He was just moved back into the manor last night, and it was the first time that she'd had an opportunity to sneak away to see him. Her conscious, what was left of it anyway, couldn't let her sleep without talking to him. She had someone she needed to let him in on... but it might be too late. She was hoping with every fiber of her being that it wasn't. Putting her hand on the doorknob she paused. What if he didn't understand? There was no turning back now. How many times would she have to tell herself that? It would be so hard to wake up and look at herself in the mirror in the morning. Then, she'd be just like Markus. Then, she'd know what he went through each and everyday. This pain she thought would be temporary was becoming very real. Opening the door Vivica snuck in and closed it behind her. Turning around she saw Markus sitting up on the bed, eyeing her. He knew she was there all along and was waiting... "Before you say anything let me explain..." Vivica stated hoping to talk Markus out of any predisposed ideas he might've had. "I know everything already." Markus looked up at her. "But ho..." Vivica's question was interrupted immediately after it started. Click... Click... Click... The sound of footsteps echoed throughout the room, and a figure began to appear from the other side of the room. A pair of eyes looked back at her from the Shadow's as a figured stepped forward into the moonlight. Raven colored hair hung over her porcelain white face. Certainly a beautiful and marvelous being she was... it was no wonder man and woman alike fell victim to her looks and charm. "I told him." Lia answered looking over at Vivica before patting Markus on the back. The Black Mariah's uneasy feeling quickly grew... normal. "Good." Vivica answered, taking a sign of relief, "You're a little early aren't you?" "Eh... I finished up a little early." Shadow answered with a smirk, "It wasn't nearly as hard as you put on. What about The Hunting Grounds?" "Jason and crew are in the limo waiting to go, I told them it was a long trip and we needed to leave early... before Christian woke up." Vivica answered walking over to the corner, "How are you feeling Markus?" "Confused... but well." Markus answered before standing to his feet. He looked over at The Black Mariah rooting through 2 coolers and a backpack in the corner. Inside of them there were frozen packs of blood, and in the backpack was an IV unit. Just the thing they needed to sustain Markus. "Better than seared meat huh?" Vivica said as she closed the lid. "How do you think Christian is going to react when he..." Shadow began to speak before getting cut off by The Black Mariah. "Realizes that he's not half as fucking brilliant as he thinks he is?" Vivica answered behind a mischievous grin, "I wish I could see the look on his face when Marques wakes him up in the morning to find Jason and The Hunting Grounds gone... along with Markus and all his supplies to be maintained." "Ah, after his comments it's a moment I'm going to miss myself." Lia answered, throwing the backpack over to Markus, who caught it before putting it on. "Just because I have blonde hair and blue eyes everyone takes me for a fool..." Vivica said, "Missing the look on Christian's face is going to be nothing compared to the look on Archibald MacGregor's face when he's beaten and broken on the ground. He may have outsmarted me before... but two can play that game. Now with an army at my disposal... we'll see how much of a tough fucking man he is." "Let's go..." Shadow says holding the coolers in her hands, "you have to get to bed Viv. You have a title to win." Vivica chuckled a little bit before she spoke under her breath... "It's like taking candy from a baby." "I've been defeated and brought down Dropped to my knees when hope ran out The time has come to change my ways On this day I see clearly everything has come to life A bitter place and a broken dream And we'll leave it all, leave it all behind" -- "Metalingus" by Alter Bridge
