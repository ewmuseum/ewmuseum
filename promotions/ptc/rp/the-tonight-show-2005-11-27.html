The Tonight Show!

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Jay: So how many of you have been tuning into the preshow of GTT5? Audience goes crazy with applause. Jay: Well tonight my first guest is one of those who have entered the interfederation tournament. She is one of the top wrestlers of GCW. She has her own clothing line and she is in the midst of getting her first book published dubbed " A theme for E.V.L." Welcome Eva VanLorne to the show folks. Eva walks out. Audience applauds loudly. She hugs Jay before blowing a kisses then sits down. Eva: Hello there Jay. The audience continues applause as the camera pans to Jay staring at her legs and cleavage. Jay clears his throat a bit as the audience continues applauding. Eva just shakes her head and laughs. Eva: They seem to appreciate.. She is interrupted be even louder applauding. Eva: Wow, they really love you Jay. Jay chuckles. Jay: It isn't me they are applauding for. More applauding. Jay: Can you blame them? Those are the nicest set of twins I have seen. Eva: Thanks, I think? Jay: You are very welcome. So you have a clothing line. E.V.L. You have a wrestling career and now you have written a book? Eva: Yes. Jay: How long did it take to write this novel? Eva: It has taken me about three years. I started the project one summer, then life got the better of me. Recently I became aware of the fact that I never relax thanks to a certain friend of mine… Eva looks into the camera lifting both hands to her lips and blowing a kiss. Eva: Love you Lex! He told me to find an outlet, so I did..by picking up the book and writing again. Jay: Did it work? Eva: Yeah, it did. Since completing the novel I have found myself inspired to write more. I find inspiration anywhere and everywhere I look. Some of the characters in the book even share similarities with people I know in my personal/professional life. Jay: Like? Eva: Well, Lex is in there lending a bit of life to Evie's best friend and associate. Jay: Evie? Eva: Evelyn Victoria Larson aka E.V.L., the main character of the book. Jay: You've found a publisher as well. Eva: Yeah. Jay: Who did you get? Eva: Pocket Books. In fact publishing has already begun. Jay: Wow, they work fast. Eva: Yes they do. I am really proud of this piece of work. Jay: I bet. So tell us a little bit more about the book. Eva: Well, as I said the main character is E.V. She is a woman who was inspired to be a private investigator all of her life and has been discouraged from it because it is a 'man's' world. It doesn't stop her and she opens her own shop. From day one trouble walks through the door. E.V. takes this job to find a young woman who has gone missing and finds out that there is more to the story than she was told. So she calls her best friend from college asking him to help her. Together they find themselves following a weak trail, facing danger,etc. In the end there is a huge climax ending. Jay: You like huge climaxes? The audience goes wild over the innuendo. Eva: Something like that. Jay: Now isn't there more to the whole story than meets the eye? Eva: Oh yes, there is a few twists to keep you guessing. But I can't tell you anymore. Jay: Why not? Eva: Because I would have to kill you. Classified information. If you want to know you'll have to read the book. Jay: I need to buy the book. Eva: I can help with that. One of Jay's stage hands brings a book to Eva who places it on Jay's desk. Eva: There ya go. Jay: Ask and ye shall recieve. Maybe I should ask for .. Eva: (laughing) Not on your life buddy. Jay looks at the audience shrugging his shoulders. The audience laughs as he picks up her novel. Jay: This is a nice cover! Jay turns the book towards the camera instructing them to get a close up. Eva: Thank you. I designed it. Jay: I can tell. Mostly black and white. So I am curious..why only black or white for your wardrobe? Eva: Why not? I feel I don't need a bunch of colors to enhance my beauty. Everyone has always said there's a grey lining to everything, well red and blue don't make grey. So I chose black and white. Jay: I suppose that makes sense. You have stuck to that color strictly even in your wrestling career and clothing line. Eva: I sure have. Jay: Speaking of wrestling. You will be in GCW's biggest PPV of the year tomorrow for the U.S title. Eva: Yep. Jay: What do you feel your chances are of winning that since you are GTT5 too. Eva: I think my chances are pretty good. My opponents are Mike Savige, Typhoid Mary, and the champion, Johnny Borealis. Despite the fact the others are in there my focus is on the belt and I'll take out anyone who gets in my way. The focus'll shift after that show for GTT5. Jay: You are a tough woman. I wouldn't want to face you, however, there are some legends who've entered the tournament. How do you think you'll fare? Eva: Let me put it to you this way. They don't call me the nutcracker for nothing. Jay winces then laughs. Jay: Well good luck Eva. Eva: Thank you Jay. Jay turns back to the audience. Jay: My next guest tonight is the man who says he excerises by lifting a beer mug to his lips. He is known as one of Hollywood's sexiest men..next to me of course. Colin Firth will be joining us after this commericial break. Cut to commericial.
