Behind the Scenes! (PT 1)

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-21 ~ The darkness in my soul has awakened, The people in my life have only taken, Taking the last bit of sanity that I have, Weird part is that all I can do is laugh, Forever forsaken in this solitude of damnation, Pain, suffering, and drama our the leaders of my frustration, Now trapped in the hellish reality of torture, Slowly letting my flesh being picked away by the many vultures, I scream to the heavens above to let me out, They lead me in many directions with the same outcome of doubt, How can this be, What was there that I didn't see, Trapped with no apparent way to go, The breath inside of me is getting low, Colder inside I get, Hate and anger is the only comfort where I sit, As my last breath is about to leave this dark dynasty, My only hope is the world will remember The Prophecy. ~ -John "The Prophet" Adams ~ If I had a dollar for every time I was an underdog in PTC…..hell I would be richer than Bill Gates! But that’s a different story for a different time. Incase you all have forgotten, my name is John “The Prophet” Adams, and yes the last time you have seen me was GTT4 where I got knocked out by that cheating bastard Blaise "Hazard" McClancy of 4WW!!! But know my time has arrived again as the federations of PTC have picked there elite in a competition known simply as “7 Of the Best!” But I ask you what makes you the best? Is it because you have more wins then the next, or maybe it’s because you’re the biggest opponent in the ring! No, it’s because when you walk into that ring and face your opponent in the eyes and realize that the reason he is shaking in his boots……is because of you. He fears you, and that’s what makes you the best! ~ 12-10-94 Time: 7:00 PM Location: Culver City/ Los Angeles.CA {It has been a couple of months so far since John Adams has confronted his father Frank. Since then John has been out on his own trying to live out the one thing that has been eluding him for all these years since his birth……. his dream of being a wrestler. John Adams found a job in Culver City working for an Indian woman by the name of Kathy Chaudhry, fixing up her house, not a bad set up he though; work during the day, and go to Wrestling School at night. But what he was about to learn was the hell that he has been living in was far from over. The lady offered to have Adams live in the house with her while he was doing his work, well how could Adams resist an offer like that. He had no idea that the smartest thing for him was to just walk away.} ~ To experience his greatness you have to know his hell……. ~ 12-11-94 Time: 3:00 PM Location: Culver City/ Los Angeles.CA {It has been 14 hours straight of complete work and Adams can feel it every where in his body and soul. As he is painting the inside of the house you can actually see sweat dripping off of his brow and forming a puddle down by his feet.} ~ To experience his power you have to know his weakness……… ~ Kathy (coming inside the room.): John is this all you have done? John Adams: What do you mean? Kathy: I mean you have been on this job for a day and all you have done is this painting! {John Adams has been on the job for a day but there was a lot more done then that. He already had put up dry wall and fixed the cracks on the remaining walls also. Who was she to judge how much work John has been doing? She is the lady that is paying him money to get the job done, that’s who she is. So with that thought in Adams head he puts away his feeling of anger for know.} ~ To experience his courage you have to know his fears…… ~ John Adams: Listen Kathy, your right and I am very sorry for that…..is there anyway that I can get some food? Kathy: What do I look like John!!! Do I look like some type of food bank? Do you think I am made out of money? I’ll get you food but that will cost you one day of pay! {My God, one day of pay just so Adams can get something to eat…..Adams wonders if this is how children are treated in a sweat shop. As Kathy leaves Adams goes into the kitchen of the house, he wonders if he will make his first day of wrestling school…..he only has three days before it starts. After all the hell and torment that John Adams been through in his life he starts to think that maybe it just happens to follow him everywhere he goes. Inside the house is a whole decoration of Indian art and a blanket that is apparently where Kathy kneels and prays. But there are no clocks and no TV’s in the whole house…..not even a radio, talk about primitive. The mood inside this house is that you are literally trapped; John Adams was hostage inside the place that he worked. He went back to work for 3 more hours until Kathy finally came back with a Del Taco bag.} ~ To experience his dream you have to know his nightmares….. ~ Kathy (walking into the door.): Well I hope you appreciate this food! {She threw the bag down by Adams feet, but Adams does not care as his animal instincts take over due to the hunger that he felt. He was disappointed to find out that all there was in the bag was two tacos. Disappointed and hungry he ate them almost in an instant.} ~ To experience his rise you must experience his fall……… ~ Kathy: What…..no appreciation? You pig; do you realize how much that cost me? John Adams (with food inside his mouth): Yeah 99 cents each at the most! {Kathy very angrily walks back to her room and slams the door. John Adams wonders if this is really worth all of the trouble that he is putting himself through, especially on the first day. But that soon resides as he is comforted by the thought of how close he is to achieving his dreams.} ~ To experience his destiny you must know his ……….PROPHECY! ~ ~ To be continued ~
