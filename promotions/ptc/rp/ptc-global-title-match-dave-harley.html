PTC — Roleplay

Global Title Match

20 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
20 January 2004 [The neon sign above the door reads ‘Comedy Club’. Not the most inventive name for sure, but that’s not important right now. Stood outside are two men familiar to WWA fans – they are the founding members of X Corp (the Extreme Corporation) Mal Somers and the WWA Intercontinental Champion Dave Harley. Dave has his gold wrapped around his waist and is wearing a ‘Too Hot For Monday Night’ T Shirt. Mal has a ‘Laying the CRAXXT Down!’ T Shirt on.] Harley: Knock, knock. Somers: Who’s there. Harley: Tank. Somers: Tank who? Harley: You’re welcome! Somers: Dave, that is the lamest joke I have ever heard. Your repertoire sucks! I’m gonna wow them in there with my ‘Yo momma’s so fat’ jokes. You don’t stand a chance! It’s just another thing that Mal is better than you at! Harley: We’ll see Chairboy. First, I’ll beat you on ‘Open Mic Night’ at the Club, then I’ll beat Seymour Almasy for the Global Title and then I’ll beat you at Rage In The Cage! Somers: In your dreams, Dude. [Both men head inside with big grins on their faces. We cut to the interior of the club. It’s the typical comedy club setup – a microphone stood in front of a brick wall. The place appears to be pretty busy. Dave and Mal have both taken seats at a table near the stage as the host makes his way onstage.] Host: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the Comedy Club. Tonight’s first act is a star of the World Wrestling Alliance. He’s facing the PTC Global Champion in a few weeks. Give a warm hand to the ‘Extreme Machine’ Dave Harley! [The crowd applauds as Dave climbs up on to the stage. He grabs the microphone in one hand, whilst waving to the crowd with the other.] Harley: Hey folks! How are you all doing tonight? Somers: Heh heh. Dave’s routine will suck. Then it’ll be my turn! Harley: So in a week or two I’m taking on Seymour Almasy for the PTC Global Title. It’s a big match for me, but I’m confident I’ll win. In fact, I know I’ll win. There’s no way I can lose when I’m facing a guy as stupid as Seymour Almasy. Seymour Almasy is so stupid, he thinks a quarterback is a tax refund. [The crowd starts to laugh.] Harley: In fact, he’s so stupid, he hears it’s chilly outside so he goes to get a bowl! [The crowd laugh a bit harder.] Harley: Seymour Almasy is so stupid he took a ruler to bed to see how long he slept. [The crowd starts laughing even louder. The smile is suddenly wiped from Mal’s face as he suddenly realises…] Somers: That’s my routine! Harley: But enough about Seymour. We all know he’s stupid. He’s almost as stupid as his momma is fat! [You can almost see the steam coming from Mal’s ears.] Harley: His momma is so fat that when she turns around, people give her a welcome back party! His momma so fat that she jumped in the air and got stuck! Seymour’s momma is so fat she wakes up in sections! She’s so fat that when she broke her leg, gravy poured out. [The crowd is in hysterics…all except Mal of course.] Harley: His momma so fat her blood type is ragu! [The crowd applaud Harley.] Harley: But I’ve gotta give the lady some credit. It’s not easy having a son as ugly as Seymour. He’s so ugly she had to be drunk to breast feed him! In fact Seymour is so ugly he made an onion cry. It’s true. Seymour Almasy is so ugly even Rice Krispies won’t talk to him! [Another round of applause and cheering.] Harley: Thank you, thank you! And now I hand you over to my X Corp buddy…MAL! [Dave walks off stage grinning as Mal gets up. By the time he reaches the microphone, the room has gone absolutely silent. If this were a Western, tumbleweed would roll by any second…] Somers: Umm…knock, knock… [We fade out with Mal stood staring out at the deadpan crowd…] ---- 21 January 2004 [Before the scene opens, we can hear what sounds like a cat being strangled. We open on the scene anyway, confident in the knowledge that if an animal is being tortured we can turn over and watch a non-offensive program. We see a crowded bar. Stood in a circle of young ladies are X Corp members Venny LeTrav and Chance. Dave Harley stands off to the side, a beer in hand grinning as he watches the figure on the stage. You see, it turns out that strangled cat is none other than Mal Somers on a karaoke machine. Surprised? Well, you shouldn’t be…] Somers: Aaaaaaaall by mmmmmyyyyysseeeeee-eellllllff! Don’t wanna be, all byyyy myyyyyseeeelllllffff! [Dave takes a swig from his beer and turns to the barman.] Harley: Two more Buds bud. Barman: You’re not gonna have a go are ya? Harley: What? The karaoke? Don’t I always! Barman: But usually, you and Mal do that Morcambe and Wise act… Harley: Bring me sunshine? Barman: …Either that, or the four of you do YMCA… Harley: Meh, we didn’t fancy dressing up for it tonight. And Jack said he’ll never do it ever again after we made him dress as the Indian. So tonight, I have something a little different in mind! [Harley flashes his trademark ‘I have something stupid planned’ grin as Mal finishes up on the karaoke. The crowd cheer out of politeness as he makes his way back over to the bar.] Somers: WOO! I’m rockin’ tonight! Harley: [Passes Mal a beer] You were awesome Dude. But tonight…I have an ace up my sleeve. Somers: Don’t be stealing my routine Dave. You did that at the comedy club! Harley: Relax Chairboy, how could I possibly rival your rendition of Like A Virgin? Somers: I know, it’s pretty cool. Harley: No, I mean ‘How could I possibly rival it?’ With this song right here! [Dave pulls a ragged piece of paper out of his pocket and unfolds it.] Harley: Watch and learn bud, watch and learn! [Dave takes a last gulp from his beer before heading up on stage and taking the microphone.] Harley: Ladies and Gentlemen, it’s great to be here tonight! [The talking amongst the crowd stops and they all stare at Dave. A few murmur ‘Who’s that guy?’ Somebody even asks the guy next to him if it’s King Kong.] Harley: As you all know, it’s not long now until I take part in the PTC Global Title match. Now I could stand here and tell all you guys about it, but I know you’re all here to hear some kick ass karaoke! [There is a small pop from the crowd.] Harley: So without further ado, The Extreme Machine Dave Harley presents to you…Gloria Gaynor’s ‘I Will Survive’…the Mad Monkey version! [Venny and Chance – who have obviously had a little too much to drink – let out a huge cheer from the corner. The crowd applauds lightly and Mal looks completely bewildered. The music strikes up and Dave starts to sing…] First I was afraid I was petrified Kept thinking ‘bout when Homo/Phobic really whooped our hides But then I spent so many nights thinking about PTC And I could see How I could beat Almasy! And so I’m back, to grab some Gold I’ll just walk in and take it from him if I may be so bold I should have trained for this match If I’m to beat him that’s the key If I had known for just one second he’d be tangling with me Go on now go, walk out the door Just turn around now Cos Harley’s gonna beat Seymour! Weren’t you the one who said that he could beat me down? You think I’ll crumble? You think I’ll lay down and die? Oh no, not I I will survive As long as I know how to pin I’ll kick his scrawny hide I’ve got all my life to live I’ve got all my strength to give And I'll survive I will survive! It’ll take all the strength he has Not to fall apart Because my Harley Bomb, when hit, is a work of art And he’ll spend so many nights Just feeling sorry for himself He’ll even cry And I’ll raise the belt up high And you’ll see me Somebody new I’m gonna take that Global Title far away from you And so you think you’ll get the pin And keep that Title from me But I’m gonna kick your ass Best two out of three! Go on now go, walk out the door Just turn around now Cos Harley’s gonna beat Seymour! Weren’t you the one who said that he could beat me down? You think I'll crumble? You think I'll lay down and die? Oh no, not I I will survive As long as I know how to pin I’ll kick his scrawny hide I've got all my life to live I've got all my strength to give And I'll survive I will survive! [The crowd lets out an almighty cheer. Even Mal has to applaud him on that one. Dave takes a bow and walks off stage as we fade out. An on screen graphic reads;] DAVE HARLEY KARAOKE CHAMPION AND FUTURE GLOBAL CHAMPION ---- 24 January 2004 [The scene opens at a McDonalds. Does it matter where? Sitting in a booth in the corner is Dave Harley. He has a Happy Meal in front of him. In his left hand, he holds a half eaten burger. In his right hand, he holds a pen and is scribbling in to a notebook. Dave’s tag team partner, Mal Somers walks past the window. He sees Dave sitting inside and walks in to join him. He sits down at the table and smiles at his buddy.] Somers: Hey there, my Global Title challenging amigo! How’s it going? Harley: mmfffssmmfff… [Dave has a mouth full of burger as he speaks. He points to his notepad.] Somers: What’s that you’re writing there…another song? Harley: muh-uh Somers: Well what? [Dave swallows the last remnants of his burger down before he attempts to answer his partner.] Harley: …I’m writing Haiku…It’s about beating Seymour…For the Global belt… Somers: Dude, that was awesome! Harley: …I haven’t told you it yet… Somers: You made it sound so convincing! Harley: Huh? Somers: What you said, it was a haiku. Harley: It was? Then what the hell am I writing? Somers: Let me see… [Mal examines the notepad.] Somers: Umm…Dave, this is just a picture of you holding the Global Title. Harley: Yeah, I didn’t know what a haiku was…sounded intelligent though… Somers: A haiku is a piece of poetry written in three lines. The first line has five syllables, the second has seven and the third has five. For example; Mal should have your shot At Rage In The Cage he’ll win Dave is a monkey Harley: Oh yeah? Well, umm…how about this… Harley is the best He will kick Seymour’s fat ass Then he will be champ Somers: That’s not too bad. I like the one I wrote better. Harley: Your one sucked Chairboy. Ooh, here’s another! Almasy is champ That will all change very soon That belt will be mine Somers: Better. But how about this… Dave will beat Seymour Lets face it anyone can But Mal can beat Dave Harley: In your dreams Dude! It’s more like… Dave is the coolest He will use the Harley Bomb Mad Monkey Goes Ape! Somers: No no no no, it goes like this… Dave is a monkey Mal is the coolest X Corp guy And that is a fact Harley: Oh yeah? We’ll see who is the coolest when I win that Global Title. I’ll have the whole of PTC talking about X Corp…hey wait, I’ve got it… When I face Seymour I’ll make X Corp proud of me When I take his gold Harley: How’s that? Somers: Meh, it’ll do…So what made you write all these haikus anyways? Harley: I dunno. I got this notepad out of the Happy Meal. [And on that highly intellectual note, we fade out for the final time for this contest.]
