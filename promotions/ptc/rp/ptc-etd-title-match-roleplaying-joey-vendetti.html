ETD Title Match Roleplaying

20 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Going to Extremes... Joseph Marciano Vendetti... At the PTC Survival pay per view, Joey Vendetti became the Primetime Central Extreme Title Division Champion. A few months ago, the above statement would have been written only in the funny papers. But in present-day professional wrestling, Joey Vendetti does indeed define extreme as the champion of PTC's extreme division. More than that, Joey proved himself against five other men to acquire the title. For a while, Joey took time off and kicked back, prepared to rest. He wrestled in an XUW match every now and again, but for Joey, what was the point? His heart lied under a belt now, a belt that no other XUW superstar had ever acquired, despite their attempts. At the Vendetti Estate on the rocky shores of New Jersey, Joey had taken ample time to rest himself before his first title defense. With a newfound respect for himself and a belief in his abilities, Mr. Vendetti was hoping for a challenge, and preparing for his hopes to be dashed. Not to say that Vendetti was, by any means, cocky...... Joey: Shadow? Dave Harley? I've never even hearda these mooks! I'll put 'em down with one hand tied behind my back! Ok, so maybe Vendetti was a bit cocky. But when your home is overrun with guests of all walks of life, being humble is just not an option. While you stand in the presence of men who have achieved more than a Harvard graduate could ever hope to achieve, you have to have a serious level on confidence. This was more true for Joey on the day of his house party than any other day in his life. The palatial Vendetti Estate looked as though it could rival the Taj Mahal. The stone walls that protected the mansion within were deceiving from the roads below, but once inside the house there could be no doubt that Joey Vendetti had done very well for himself. From the dinosauric dining hall to the enormous lounge, the Vendetti Estate was definitely a spectacle. In the ballroom area, Joey had been soaking it up for a good twenty minutes with all the people that were surrounding him. In his armani suit and with his Extreme Title Division Championship draped over his shoulder for the occasion, he looked much more than impressive. The complex conversation began with none other than The Practice's Donald Trump near Joey's bar in the ballroom. Joey: So I've decided on going into that match with just my set of brass knucks. I won't need much else to pull out a win over this Harley guy, and this Shadow character doesn't seem much different. They all advanced further than me in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, but that doesn't mean anything. Trump: Accomplishments and success mean everything Joey, and members of your specific division have accomplished much more than you have. Joey: True, but none of them have accomplished what I now keep on my shoulder. I feel much more confident in my abilities than I used to, and I'm not afraid to take a risk to keep this title. Trump: That's good, because to truly be successful in any industry, risks are inevitable. Joey: Well Donnie, as the Extreme Champion, risks are something I take everytime I step into the ring. But not just as an Extreme Champion, as a wrestler. It's just... I think I have a little more room to take these risks than the other two guys do. Trump: Oh? Joey: Yes. Actually, I'm getting ready to make my speech, so if you'll excuse me. Donald Trump nodded as Joey dismissed himself to walk to the front of the room where a microphone stood on a slightly elevated platform. As he stepped up on the stage and tapped the microphone, the elegantly dressed party all took their seats and prepared for the speech that Joey brought them there for. Welcome, ladies and gentlemen, to the Vendetti Estate. Before we get started I'd like to recognize a few guests and let you all know that this is being televised by Primetime Central television. First, I'd like to thank Donald Trump for attending, everyone please give him a hand. The crowd burst into applause as Donald Trump stood to his feet and nodded before sitting back down. As the crowd noise began to die down, Joey began again. Tonight, via satellite, I would like to thank Senator John Kerry for joining us. The crowd immediately burst into applause and Joey smiled as the screen behind him revealed the face of the losing candidate for the 2004 presidential race. And thank you to everyone else who decided to attend. As you all know, we're here tonight to place bets on my Extreme Title Division Championship match. It will be a Weapon of Choice match, and I have chosen brass knuckles. I will be taking on Dave Harley and Shadow, and the odds have been listed in your program that my associate Toro Columbo distributed at the door. Now that all that is out of the way, I'd like to try and convince you to place a bet on me, Joseph Marciano Vendetti. After listening to my speech, I believe a choice for the winner of the match will not be very difficult. Consider this. In the extreme title division, each wrestler must push his or her body to the limit to try and achieve success. They have more at risk than anyone else in any other division. The fact that I hold this belt should be more than enough proof for anyone that previously doubted me, but for fun, I'll tell you why else I will not fail. Ladies and gentlemen, a wrestler is not just a wrestler, a wrestler is a person as well. And a person has things in his or her own life that must be guarded and protected. They have families to live for, they have friends and lovers to be with, and they have jobs that they love to work for. So ask yourselves, how far can a man push himself? How extreme can someone possibly be? Joey paused to toss his belt directly in front of the crowd. The loud thud it made as it made contact with the floor caused a few people to jump. That belt is nothing ladies and gentlemen. A collective gasp ran through the crowd. That belt means nothing to me. It's a piece of gold and leather. It is absolutely nothing. Look around you! This palatial estate is mine! I live better than either of my opponents could ever hope to. So why do I even bother to defend this championship? The crowd grew silent. Because it's not the belt I defend. Robert Kennedy once said that it's not a man's actions that make him extreme, but his words. If Shadow and Harley want to fight over a ridiculous looking piece of gold, let them fight over that. But that belt is not what I fight for. I am a champion of the extremists, and there's a reason for that. In that elimination chamber, no one was willing to go further than me to win that title, and you know why? Because those people have things to go back to. Joey began to increase the volume of his voice. February of 2003, Geopetto Joseph Chonci Vendetti was laid to rest after a massive heart attack. My father passed away, leaving the fortune that bought my home in his wake. My father made me the man I am today. He made me ruthless and cold-hearted. He made me unafraid. I don't know Shadow and Harley, but even if I did, breaking their bodies would mean nothing to me. They are pawns in my game ladies and gentlemen. They are obstacles that are easy to overcome, if dealt with in the correct fashion... Papa taught me that, and Papa passed away. I have no father. August of 2003, Maria Yacavone, formerly Maria Vendetti, confessed that she was not my birth mother. I left her, and have spoken to her only once since that incidence. I have no mother. I am an orphan. October of 2003, after a car accident that sent her car into a lake, my girlfriend April drowned and died. The love of my life passed on and left me for the realm of heaven. I have no love ladies and gentlemen. November of 2003, my real mother, a prostitute, revealed the true nature of my father's death, and then disappeared herself. No mother. No friends. No father. No family. I am alone. I have nothing to live for. All of this, this mansion, this money, these parties. They are nothing! I am hollow, ladies and gentlemen. I have nothing left to lose. Harley and Shadow will fight, to an extent. But they will not fight to their deathbed, like I will. Ladies and Gentlemen, your bets are safe with me. Good evening! The crowd stayed silent for a moment, before bursting into applause. Joey took a step forward to step on the belt he worked so hard to win, before walking out of the room to prepare for the challenge ahead. L'Estremita...
