Training for destiny!

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-24 **Prologue** A single vessel had approached these majestic cliffs a few nights ago. It lone passenger climbed these cliffs with a drive that could only be compared to the mythical stories of gods long gone. Defeat was the last thing on his mind. Victory was first and foremost. He knew his opponents were very good yet he was not going to be deterred from achieving his goal of moving onto the next round. He contemplated on how to defeat each and every man n their terms. Yet felt it would be best to defeat them his way. They do not respect him as an opponent each is very vane in his honest opinion. Each of those men claim to be better than the other by simply stating the fact. Samuelsson isn’t claiming to be the better man. He is merely claiming that he is going to hurt them. Before we move forward and follow what Samuelsson is doing I will read to you something that was written by someone in Samuelssons family. Destiny is a path less defined. Each step brings you closer to destiny. Every stone upon the path tells a story. Each turn leads to a new discovery. As the sun sets on each day time begins again. As the moon rises amongst the stars each one tells a past story. As each rain drops falls a lone sound is heard. As each sound is heard it tells a solo tale. Setting yourself upon the path is honour. Finding the path is gratification. Travelling the path is humbling. Finding destiny upon this path is the future. This is the way of our people. This is the life of our people. These are the ways of our people. These are the paths of the Vikings. These words ring true for all of those that fight onwards. These are the words of the Vikings. These are the familiar aspects from which Jorge Samuelsson defines himself. With each battle Samuelsson battles onwards and fights on and on until there will be no more fighting only the long dark road to Valhalla. Is this the road you want to travel or is this the road you wish to avoid when a man like Samuelsson comes walking down his path to destiny. **The Story** As the sun rises over the trees upon the cliffs called Thor’s Hammer birds chirp and call in the peaceful morning. The suns rays glide through the cracks in the cabins walls as they shine down upon the large sleeping form who slowly begins to stir. Seeming to have a had a rough evening he sits up wiping his eyes and then strokes his beard looking about blinking a few times to get his bearings. Turning his head from side to side a few loud snaps are heard as he stretches his large arms upwards towards the heavens and lets out a long and hard yawn. Slowly he gets to his feet pulls the leather covering over the window to the side and looks out over the harbour below. Watching each wave as the slowly and lazily roll into the harbour and crash upon its beaches he smiles as he has found private peace something he has not had in a long time. Slowly he begins to dress himself as he looks about grabbing each piece of clothing and choosing carefully. He begins speaking to himself as if everybody is listening. "Time to train and bring yourself to ultimate heights and glory and finally Time to prove to the PTC and everyone in the GTT5 that you should not be ignored, Yes there are great people in this tournament, People who have proven themselves worthy of the great battles to come. You want to be part of these great battles. When will all of the people look upwards and wonder in astonishing fear. Why do they think they are invincible and I do not mean business?" Clearing his head of thoughts he exits and walks out to a fountain outside of the cabin. Reaching down he scoops up to hands cupped together full of water and splashes his face The crystal clear cool water helps awaken him and sharpen his senses. The water seems to cleanse the thoughts he was having and become focused once again. Knowing the time is dwindling for him to fight a major fight, a fight of his life. Four men and only two can be victorious. "The day comes closer for four men to enter a ring and beat each other senseless. It is a script for a great story to be told. I am glad I am part of this story. Many men and people will consider me to be the lesser man in this battle. I consider myself the odd factor in this. Each one of these men claim to be the greatest. Each man is more concerned with their personal life rather than pay attention to the dangerous people who wish to hurt them. One of these men even hold onto the meaning of a word term simply put Fuck You! Is this the seriousness of a group of men who I should fear? A man prepares for war then battle in different ways, but never ignores those who they will face. Bringing doubts to yourselves and ignorance to your thoughts as you always turn a blind eye to whom you really face. Would you be as confident as my hands were wrapped around your throat squeezing the life out of your body? No I don’t think you would really do that. Then again it would be so much fun to watch you struggle uselessly against the grip of my hands." Staring intently forward as he walks he comes across a large pile of rocks and he reaches down and begins throwing them to the side. Each one rock varies in sizes and color as he tosses them to the side much like waves would do with flotsam against a beach. Grabbing each stone he seems to become more driven to throw them to the side. His hands become dry and scraped as he tosses the rough stones. His anger seems to sedate the pain from the hands as he works even harder to complete his task he had set before himself. "Doubt is something that very seldom comes within my mind. Truthfully I am more than just a huge muscle bound monster bent on conquering. I am a man who is intelligent and knows how to work the weaknesses of my opponents. All of my opponents current glaring weaknesses are displayed before me as if they were an open book. Dwelling within them is a problem I will use to my advantage. I don’t care damn it. Your pathetic little self worth is useless in a war. Your useless in this battle. I am coming to fight better be ready." Grabbing the last stone he throws it with enough force that when it hits the other rocks a few of them shatter and splinter. He stares over at it for a moment his anger growing as he turns and stalks off towards the wooded portion of the cliffs, touching a large oak tree as he passes it seeming in great reverence of the ancient tree. Then he slowly walks up to a stoned circle in the middle of a clearing where two men are attempting to throw each other out of it. He watches with interest as one man is tripped and then falls out of the circle. Samuelsson steps into the circle as both men look at him. "I am training and I wish to enter the circle. Sven you and I know each other very well and have known each other for years. Ragnar I do not know you that well, but rest assured I will not harm my kin I will use this circle as a training tool only. Come lets begin I do not wish to waste anytime." Samuelsson walks the edge of the circle as Ragnar is first to charge at Samuelsson. Reaching under with one arm and over with the other Samuelsson hoists Ragnar to the side and easily tosses him out of the ring as Ragnar spins from the circle. Turning Samuelsson faces Sven as they grab each other in a powerful hold. Samuelsson easily reverses the hold of the smaller man. Turning quickly he lifts Sven up into a suplex position and then steps forward and drops him onto his feet outside of the circle as he stops and looks at Sven. "As I have said my friend there are ways to defeat your opponent where there does not have to be violence. Then there are other ways that deem violence." Turning at the last minute Ragnar hits Samuelsson square across the face and begins to punc at samuelsson as Samuelsson stops and grabs Ragnar by the throat. Squeezing with all of his might he begins to choke Ragnar as he drops to his knees. "Stupid move young Ragnar, very stupid move. You are going to understand that my rage does not easily go away. I will make you an example for all of those who will face me in the near future. You will understand why I hate to be underestimated and ignored. You cheap attacked me and by this your suffering will begin now." Dragging Ragnar to his feet he throws him to the ground just as fast. Ragnar stands and stares at Samuelsson with anger and rage as Samuelsson smiles and allows Ragnar to come in charging. Suddenly Samuelsson delivers a punch to ragnar’s temple dropping the man where he stood. Looking down at the man Samuelsson looks at him with contempt. "The younger ones always look to achieve a high goal with no experience or learning they require, much like the older ones who have acquired their positions through earning them. Then when they get there they are too afraid to allow the younger up and coming talents and warriors the chance prove how good they have become or really are. They are truly afraid of defeat, Much like my three opponents." Spitting on the ground in disgust he raises an eyebrow and then looks to the sky. "Salaok, the man of many problems and so few of words, Fuck you is such an intelligible way of speaking. Much more befitting his stature as a champion and a man who thinks he should over look everyone, but himself. Do not look in the mirror of vanity too long it might break on your face. You stare too long you will go blind from the reality of things that are really happening. Things you should truly pay attention too. Maybe concentrating less on your verbal communications and more on your opponents might give you a real chance at winning the match up. I do not play games with any of my opponents so hope beyond a shadow of a doubt that you do not get beaten and bloodied by my fists upon your face. So in your own words Saloak Fuck you!" Growing a bit more agitated and a lot angrier, He stares at Sven as he drags the unconscious Ragnar away from the stoned circle and back towards the cabins. Samuelsson reaches down and lifts a stone from the circle. Looking at the stone he makes a small motion as he blows the dirt from it. "Fear Salaok is simple. True fear should be of oneself and what you are truly capable of. You and Tsonda both are concentrating on Deville and looking past me. That is your greatest mistake. Are you truly ignorant enough to think I cannot defeat you?" laughing deeply he smiles. "You must be. Only ignorance would cause you to look past me." Returning to the seriousness of his statements he watches as the old man in the robes he met once again shows up. The man nods and motions for Samuelsson to continue. "You complain of being overlooked and ignored. You and I have something in common there Salaok. You’re a man removed from his past. I am a man who lives with my past and is bringing back to glory. You are going to live in my past very soon. Ignore me all you wish Salaok for you and I will meet very soon." Samuelsson points to his head as he looks at the old man. He knows that his destiny and his training will come full circle when he exercises his mind. Knowing that his opponents are all the training his mind needs as he begins to get into theirs. "Chandler Tsonda you grasp on things that should be ignored and let go. You hold a grudge against Angelo Deville and a man named Vendetta. Does it really make a difference if you hold the grudge when someone else may surely surpass you on your path to greatness? Your anger your darwinisms are simply useless in the ring. The ring is a battlezone where you must survive. You have Salaok here a social pariah, Angelo Deville the silence is disturbing and his words would be greatly welcomed compared to the two of you complaining and wallowing in self misery and pity." Stopping he looks at the old man and nods. "You know what forget you three. I am going to fight for everything in that ring and I want to win this damn match. You three can do whatever it is you want. Just remember one thing I am tired of waiting, Tired of you’re crying and moaning about injustices done to you. I bet the greatest injustice would be me beating the hell out of you three who have graciously overlooked me. I am done with all of you idiots overlooking me and ignoring me. I am going to gladly hurt and take you three apart in the ring. Go find your little holes to crawl into and cry about who and what wrongs have been done to you. In the end nobody cares nor listens. Welcome to the true reality where this man the Viking warrior comes to you and smacks your face bloody." Looking around he walks to the old man and motions him onwards. "Let’s get the hell out of here. These three idiots are beginning to enrage the hell out of me. Why is it these three think they are walking gods. Somebody has to shut them up and finally shut them down. I am going to enjoy being the one to do this." Slowly they disappear into the forest as the sun blazes down upon them. The skies begin to show signs of clouds and the sun rises higher into the sky as the world underneath it begins to heat up. Animals begin to become active again as silence replaces the place where a Viking warrior continued his quest towards destiny.
