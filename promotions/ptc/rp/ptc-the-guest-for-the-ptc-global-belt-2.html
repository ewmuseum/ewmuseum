The Guest for the PTC Global Belt - #2 - Producing the belt Miami, Florida Sunday 11th September 2005 11:00 am “Time to deliver on a promise…” grumbled Jason as he clasped the phone together hanging it up. He promised PTC the Global Title belt and now they had rang to grant him an Infinite Gauntlet match, under the assumption he was ready to hand over the recovered PTC gold. Tossing his cell phone on the Miami motel bed, Jason perched himself on the end of the bed and cupped his head in his hands. To say he hadn’t found the belt was an understatement. Since the first initial promise, Jason hadn’t thought about it. The week after “looking” in Madison Square Garden’s lost and found, the Missing Ideal got his contract with PRIME and since then the Infinite Gauntlet and more importantly the PTC Global Belt has been at the back of his mind. “Maybe if I fashion something…” thought Jason as a light bulb appeared over his head. Standing up, Jason left the motel room with a great idea filling his mind. ==== Miami, Florida Sunday 11th September 2005 11:23 am “I need PVC glue…” demanded the PRIME-ate as he took his first step into a small stationery store. The shop felt very confined as the woman behind the counter, “Mary” on her badge looked up from the morning’s paper and eyed the large wrestler entering. Mary moved her glasses from her eyes, making sure she was seeing correctly and then proceeded to point towards the back of the oversized corridor she called a workplace. Some people just don’t have great customer service anymore. Regardless, Jameson followed her finger and went to the back of the store to get the PVC glue he required. Stopping in front of the shelves that were filled with glue, Jason surveyed the situation momentarily and then obliged himself to the biggest container he could eye. Gratefully plucking it from the shelf, Jason spun on his heel and caught the attention of the shopkeeper and she suspiciously watched the oversized man in her store. “You got any paint?” questioned Jameson as he peered at different shelves, attempting to find it himself. In the same manner as before the shopkeeper obliged Jameson by pointing at the paint shelf. Not a word was spoken by the woman however her gaze at the wrestler was unrelenting. Jason nodded a thank you to the woman and surveyed the many colours of acrylic paint that were held on the wooden shelves. Lifting down yellow, white, black and red bottles into his waiting arms, the Missing Ideal spun on his heel and dropped the bottles onto the counter of the store. “Got a bit of project?” questioned the woman as she began to total the amounts of the glue and the paint. “Something like that…” ==== Miami, Florida Monday 12th September 2005 03:23 am With the desk lamp on, Jason Jameson wiped some of the sleep from his eyes as he sat over his new project. It really shouldn’t have taken him this long to complete it, especially when he realised how shoddy it looked. Lifting the papier-mâché creation in two hands, the PRIME superstar lifted his makeshift into the air with a grin across his face. It wasn’t perfect; in fact it was the furthest thing he had seen from perfect since Meanstreak had given the PWA students a lesson in selling. The letters PTC were poorly embroidered across the front “face” and delicately covered in gold coloured paint. Okay so delicately is a lie. After combining yellow, white and a bit of black paint together, Jason continually mixed it for minutes to get a colour that resembled gold and then he just slapped it onto his papier-mâché in the area that was supposed to be the gold plate. Add to that some black paint that covered the leather strap, Jason Jameson held a wet dripping mess that he would try to pass off as one of the highest valued belts in the world. But then again, if the PTC Tag Team belts can still hold some prestige after being carried by a team managed by James von Doom, Jameson knew miracles can still happen in this modern world. What Jason needed was a convincing lie that would explain the “poor” condition he had found the belt in. He could pretend that JEW got a hold of it and degraded it. Nah, I’ve already used that joke. I could mention that Deville and K-Wolf continuously did the dirty deed atop the title belt; nope I think I’ve done that joke. James von Doom joke, done. Dark Ages joke, done. I think I’ve covered all the jokes in e-wrestling. Oh hold on… Jason could tell the PTC that this was the original PTC Global and that the one they had used for the past few years had actually been the “original” work of The Educator… “I like it…” thought Jason. The PTC Global Belt was in his possession. He had the perfect excuse for its poor condition. The adulation, many thanks and subsequent Infinite Gauntlet shot could wait until a respectable hour of the day… Right now, the Missing Ideal needed to sleep.
