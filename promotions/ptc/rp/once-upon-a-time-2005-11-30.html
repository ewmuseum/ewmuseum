Once upon a time…

Date: 2005-11-30 …In a kingdom far far away, there lived a lonely young prince. This young prince lived in a castle on the edge of the Knowitall Woods. Deep in these vast woods lived a horrible beast, which caused the young prince much headache. One day the prince decides that he can no linger live in fear of this horrendous beast, and swears on his famliy crest he shall slay this beast. The young prince digs deep into the weaponry of the castle and fetches his weapon of choice, and sets off. He is met by the people of the village showering him with gifts and items of good luck. The prince knows in his heart he doesnt need these trinkets, as fate is on his side. He waves a farewell to his faithful followers and passes through the gates, which may very well be the point of no return. The brave young prince searches day & night, relentlessly for this savage beast that has tormented his family, his friends and his followers for the past many moons. Thirteen days he spent wandering through the darkness of Knowitall Woods, when he stumbled up on a cave. Not just any cave, this cave was dark. Like most caves. The prince decides now is the time, to draw up the courage and find out for himself if this is the home of the beast he has come in search of. The young prince nervously enters the dwelling, as heads to the deepest, darkest, dankest (and any other adjectives starting with "d") part of the cave he can find. He thinks that he may be alone, and have entered the wrong cave when a roar, is heard from behind him. The young prince spins and attempts to draw his sword, but fails. He slips and falls as the shadow moves through the dark, and lashes out at him. The Prince rolls to one side and manages to draw his sword. He shoves it into the air and somehow drives it through the heart of the dark beast. The young prince cuts the heart from the beast and returns to his kingdom. The streets are filled with joy, and peace has been brought to the kingdom after moons of darkness.
