Covering your heart and soul with every pass, embedding into your very soul like a knife into your life. Twisting till your very life bleeds out of you. The very image that shown up, filling with life with the colors that it held, was lovely. Red, green, white. All the colors that the earth was made up of. "I told you Mr. Bong, I warned you that she was going to be left behind. A vivid of your imagination." The sky was light up with little points of light. Almost like sharp points in a piece of paper, trying to make the world into a global image rather then a little light bulb in a camera. The camera pans upward, crossing the very start of a what looks to be an old church. The very sounds of churchly singing can be heard from it. "HOLLLY NIGHT! TISSSSS HE’LLLLL COME! THENNNN IT’LL DONEEEE!" Not common of the people of this land. The church. Common to the people of one land, Jerusalem. The land of Jesus Christ. The greatest man in both the living and dead world. The man that brought life to me and to this world. As the camera pans a little more you can see the cross that stood over the church of Nea or "New" in Greek. The oldest and most important church after the Church of the Holy Sepulcher. Credit: Eva Vanlorne’s Handler… --------------------------------------------------------------- The scene switched to a holy cross that stood high. Dark, pitch black as can be with blood stains on it. It was covered in a glass case. Pressed into a wall that stood high. "The cross of Vinaca. My soul that god so took from me. The very man that all of man kind takes into your heart. Mr. Bong, you are the same. You play as if god is going to save you from my wrath. As if your playful antics…." Pressure. Torment. The inter soul that we all possess deep down inside. The part of us that will always be raged out from his collective being. The real Elia Smith. Not this Reamond to a leap of faith, a leap of righteousness towards god and the rest of the world, trying to get away from the demon that has took over the family that she once knew before. Killing the race that was once bold like herself. "…STAY! STAY BACK IF YOU KNOW WHAT’S GOOD FOR YOU. YOU WILL NEVER TAKE BACK CONTROL…..NEVER!" A bolt of lighting struck across the sky. Striking the cross that stood so tall in the land that God himself walked. Striking the cross of Nea. And as it did, the shadow of Reamond disappeared in the night, only to be forgotten for this one night, for this one sudden moment. "It’s almost complete. The death of Elia Smith will be complete…" The very voice of Reamond spoke out… "…And the birth of Reamond will be replaced!"
