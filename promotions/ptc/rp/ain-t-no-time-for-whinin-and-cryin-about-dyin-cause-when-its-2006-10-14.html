ain't no time for whinin and cryin about dyin cause when its your time, its your time, but right now I feel so good

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 "So what's your take?" One guy says to another, both displaced from the public behind the tinted windows of a vehicle. "I think it's doable. It'll take a lot of fucking planning and consideration. There're so many x-factors that can play-in, so it'll be hard to come up with a concise plan that'll keep our asses out of jail. If we're going to do this, we're going to have to pull pull it off with fucking style, grace, and a grip-shit of anonymity." He says, taking drags between phrases which fills the four-door sedan with thick smoke. The other looks on longingly from the passenger's seat, but attempts to regain a hold of his focus. "What do you propose? How should we go about doing this?" "It's too early to say." Another drag. Before continuing, the man in the driver's seat peers through the bright sun. He squints towards an elementary school playground, crowded with children on the basketball court while what is assumed to be the gym teacher watches on. "It'll take three of us. We go with more than that, it'll become muddled and a mess. Just us two, we won't have enough manpower to pull it off. We'll be the first thing on Katie fucking Couric's mind tonight and we won't be able to watch it from behind prison bars. Now this is very important, dude, so you have to concentrate on me…" The man in the passenger's seat's eyes drift back from his friend's to the joint that dangles between his fingers. A column of ash stays intact to the cherry but continues to grow, leaving only a sudden movement between it and the upholstery. He's too busy eyeing the spliff to notice the children shuffle back inside the school. "We need two people to do the pick-up and someone else on getaway. Without a driver or an extra pick-up-dude, we'd be scrambling our asses off to pull this job. We're going to have to get an unmarked, inconspicuous vehicle to make sure no one can find a trace back to us, and make sure we do it at an opportune time. Even a single fucking witness can throw our entire plan off-track. It'll probably take a few more days of reconnaissance before we get this entirely mapped out, and get our third guy. That reminds me…" The driver shuffles towards his pocket as the passenger's eyes go wide, watching the ash and cherry plummet to his lap. It creates a rather unnecessary mess. "What are you doing!?" He yelps. "Getting out my fucking cellphone! Fucking christ! Calm your fucking nerves." Another drag, only the joint is diffused. The driver keeps 'smoking', not noticing he's inhaling mere air. "I'm going to shoot a text to our third guy; tell him about the plan. Text messages fly under the man's radar, so that's the only way to talk about shady business. Plus I just love the neat little predictive text feature; it's like it's reading your thoughts! Anyways, this guy is the perfect dude for this job, so it should go off without a hitch. IF he's involved, that is, which this plan's execution is TOTALLY dependent on." Another futile drag. "Dude, it's out." The passenger interrupts. Immediately, the driver takes offense and removes his attention from the text message he was sending. "What's out? I'm out?! You're out, bitch! You don't tell me I'm out. This is MY fucking ransom-plot!" "I'm talking about the joint; it's out." "Well fucking speak up, mother fucker." The driver lifts a lighter from the middle console and re-ignites the joint. He takes rather large drags that suck it down to get it started, and the passenger's eyes have sunken so low that he makes the perfect inspiration for a Precious Moments figurine of a homeless person. "Adam?" "What?" The passenger pleads. "Can I please hit that shit?" "You fucking hobo-mooch." Speaks the driver behind his colorful vinyl mask. He takes three more gigantic drags before passing it off to the current Transatlantic Champion of AWC, Garbage Bag Johnny, who immediately takes a deep breath accompanied by a marijuana-filter. "You bums are all about the 'Can I?, Can I?, Can I?', feeding off of my hard-work!" Exhale. "Dude, it's my weed." "BUT I ROLLED IT! WITH MY FUCKING ZIG-ZAGS, BITCH." Before GBJ can take another drag, The Illustrious Face-Eater rips the joint from his fingers and begins to suck it down again. As it hangs from his lips he takes a moment to finish his text message and send it out. Finished, his full concentration is on the school. He notices all the mini-vans and SUVs that pull-up through the parking lot and also that the time is 3:07pm, indicating that school was just about finished. "We've got to be super dee-el about this, John. I'm talking fucking ninja. You saw what happened to Rollins; dude was sent to FRY for fucking with someone close to Seymour Almasy. And I don't know about you, but I'm too famous to go to jail. Plus, people think I'm dead." "Well I'm an AWC Champion, so I really can't have a criminal record--" "Dude." Facey interrupts. "It's AWC. That, like, doesn't even count as a fed. So stop fucking worrying about your career, and start worrying about the one that matters: mine. Yeesh, you're so fucking selfish." GBJ retreats to his corner of the vehicle to sulk for a moment, feeling a bit guilty for his one-sided attitude. He decides to be a team-player and contribute to the plan. "Have you had any ideas on how we're going to pull this off? I'm pretty sure there are jet-packs still laying around from AWC's last Pay Per View." Facey smokes the joint as it burns to its end, waving GBJ's plan away with an extended index finger. "nononono, dude, I come up with the plans. And I've already thought of an escape route involving a helicopter, the Grand Canyon, dropping eight tons of Napalm on the Hoover Dam to provide an elaborate distraction for our getaway, and three pounds of dank for the ride." GBJ rolls his eyes. "Isn't that going a little over-board, Facey? I mean, come on, THREE POUNDS?" "What the fuck? Now that you mention it, I'm pretty sure we're going to need MORE. I should send another text to Nova and tell him to bring more weed." The name-dropping of one former PTC Extreme Champion piques GBJ's interests. "You asked Nova to be the third guy?" But the question takes Facey off-guard. "DUDE. DO YOU HAVE TO LISTEN TO EVERY SINGLE FUCKING WORD I SAY? Jesus, you could at least PRETEND to act surprised when I try to do something nice for you--" He's interrupted by the sound of "Buddha Lovaz" by Bone Thugs, a song that indicates Nova is contacting him. Time- 3:09pm. Date- 10/3/2006. RE- coolest half-baked scheme ever Subject- Are you fucking kidding? You seriously have to be joking. KIDNAP Seymour Almasy's cousin? So he'll pick you to be his first round opponent? You're seriously more screwed up than I thought… ps No more pinching off my sacks >:0 Face-Eater slams his flip-phone shut and lets out a loud sigh. "Okay, Nova's out because he's going gay. I changed my mind anyways. Forget about Nova and let's just do it right now." Facey glances towards the digital clock in his display, noting the time of 3:10pm. Across the street, the school's bells ring for a moment. The quick succession of events takes GBJ off-guard as Facey gathers things into his pockets, readying his exit from the vehicle. "Wait, what?" GBJ asks. "Forget about Nova? I thought you said we NEED three people or else this plan won't work! What happened to precision and planning and perfection and all of that?" As Facey finishes the end of the joint, he doesn't acknowledge that GBJ wants to squeeze one last hit out of the roach and just tosses it outside his window. Suddenly, children begin pouring out the double doors that lead inside the school, all of them heading towards bicycles or school-busses and their parents vehicles. The I.F.E. watches intently out the wind-shield, as if scanning the children for a specific person… "Listen, I don't have time to dick around with LOSERS. I'm a man of action! A man of prosperity! You think I'm going to puss out because Nova called me crazy!?! IS THAT WHAT YOU THINK?!" Facey digs his finger into Garbage Bag Johnny's chest. "Nova called you crazy?" "ARE YOU MOCKIN--LOOK! IT'S HIM!!" Facey frantically begins slapping GBJ's shoulder with one hand while pointing onto the street. What Facey points at, in general, is a group of children that walk along the sidewalk. Specifically, Facey is indicating a young, tall African-American boy that stands out amidst them all; Tyrone Almasy. "That's the kid! That's Seymour Almasy's closest living relative: his cousin's cousin's cousin." GBJ looks confused. "You're sure that's Seymour Almasy's cousin? He looks pretty dark, and Almasy is pretty… you know… white…" "OF COURSE I'M SURE!" Facey responds, offended at such an accusation. "I'M HEAD OF THE RESEARCH DEPARTMENT AND I DO MY JOB DAMN WELL! WHAT, YOU THINK I JUST SAID THIS KID IS HIS COUSIN JUST BECAUSE THEY HAPPEN TO HAVE THE SAME LAST NAME AND I DECIDED TO BANK ALL OF THIS PLANNING ON A COINCEDENCE?!" The Hobo stares at his grandiose friend blankly for a moment. "I really wouldn't put it past you." "Look." Facey tries reasoning. "That is Seymour Almasy's cousin's cousin's cousin; I wouldn't lie about something like that! And after we kidnap him and send Almasy a ransom note, he'll have NO CHOICE but to pick me to be his first round opponent in GTT! THEN I'LL SLAY HIM LIKE GOLDBERG STARRING IN THAT STRAIGHT-TO-DVD HORROR-FLICK, SANTA'S SLAY, WHERE HE GOES ON A MURDEROUS CHRISTMAS-THEMED RAMPAGE! THEN WE'LL SEE WHO'S HALLS ARE DECKED!" "Shouldn't we, you know, do the grab?" Hmmmmm, the thought rattles in his brain for a moment. "Yyyyyyyeeah, let's go. But don't use real-names. I'm wearing my Wrestling mask for a reason." GBJ nods and the two get out of the car. They ignore the crossing guards and soccer moms and teachers all littering the school's parking lot as the children walk past, as well as ignoring any previous notion of remaining anonymous. Quickly approaching the child, the Face-Eater and Garbage Bag Johnny quickly become surrounded by their large numbers. The kids have learned of stranger danger before, and half of them grow really nervous at the sight of a skinny guy wearing a cape and wrestling mask accompanied by an even skinnier hobo who honestly should be wearing a mask. While few people watch the scene, no one really takes immediate notice to what is happening. "YOU, CHILD." Facey says. "Me?" One kid asks. "No, the black one!" "Teacher says we aren't s'posed to say 'black'." Comes from another. "Yeah, I'm African-American!" speaks the child designated as Tyrone Almasy with what pride he could muster. "Argh, are we going to get into this?!" As if being dragged in an opposite direction Facey lets out a sigh. "Look, if we weren't allowed to say 'black' then we make the general assumption that EVERY 'black' man in America is either part African and-slash-or part American. WHAT HAPPENED TO THE JAMAICANS AND THE TRINIDAD AND TOBOGOIANS!? What happened to the ISLANDERS?! Are we to forget them and label them 'African-American'?! Or do we need to split even more hairs and give everyone their own neat little label!?? Sorry, but I'll stick to the term 'Black' and piss EVERYONE off instead of just a choice few. That way, it's easier to figure out who my enemies are. And then I don't have to deal with that whole white-South-African dude crap, because that's an entirely different paradox to wrap my brain around! ANYWAYS, BLACK BOY, YOU ARE THE ONE WE ARE HERE FOR!" Facey pushes his way through the children and once finally reaching him puts his hands on Tyrone's shoulders. "You are coming with me, for it is YOU who is the ONLY LIVING RELATIVE of Wrestling Legend SEYMOUR ALMASY!" Tyrone's eyes grow real wide with shock. "I am?" He says with a tone that masks his regard. "You are." Garbage Bag pipes. But Tyrone's face quickly turns sour. "Who the fuck is Seymour Almasy?" Facey immediately gasps. "Do all you kids talk like this!?! God damn, have you no fucking tact?!" Tyrone interrupts Facey before continuing his rant. "He'd better not be that fucking bum." "Hey." GBJ says with a frown. "I resent that." "We don't have time to deal with these shit-heads! Johnny: take care of the witnesses so we can escape!" Facey barks orders at his partner. But Johnny remains a bit hesitant. "Uh… are you SURE this kid is related--?" "JOHNNY!" "I just think we should be sure--." "JOHNNY! KIDS! RIGHT-GODDAMN-NOW!" GBJ's shoulders drop, defeated. "Well, okay." He responds, then grabs the nearest kid to Tyrone Almasy and knees him in the stomach. "OOMPH!" Some forced air exits the child and he tumbles over. Then GBJ turns to the next kid, and delivers unto him the very same fate. "OOMPH!" So goes the next child, and he falls as well. Luckily, Facey intervenes before GBJ can knee another child in the stomach. "dudedudedude, WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?!? USE THE PEPPER-SPRAY, IT TAKES LESS TIME!!" With a toss, Facey delivers GBJ a small vile with the very substance. Wild sprays of peppery residue begin soaking the remaining children, and they immediately keel over in tears. "Don't forget the ones you kneed!" Facey reminds him. Garbage Bag Johnny bends over to make sure his aim to their eyes is spot-on. "Alright, let's get the fuck out of here before someone sees us!" With the stunned and shocked Tyrone Almasy in his grasp, Facey drags the child towards the vehicle until he notices that GBJ isn't following. "Come on!" "Oh, my bad." He says, pulling himself away from relentlessly spraying a child's eyes as he lay on the ground. The child has since stopped moving. The make-shift crime duo enters the vehicle, forcing Tyrone Almasy in the back, and then speeds off down the road. Facey drives frantically, swerving to keep within the lanes. "HELP! HELP! FUCKING HELP!" Little Tyrone screams and jumps wildly within the car, becoming the biggest fucking nuisance either two had ever encountered. "Calm down and let us kidnap you, kid!" GBJ says, but the little boy kicks him in the cheek. "YOU KILLT MY FRIENDS YOU ASSHOLE!" "Alright, Kid, we'll let you out as soon as your uncle Seymour cooperates." Comes Facey's assurance. "Now give me his phone number so I can make the Ransom Call!" "I don't even know who you're talking about!!" The kid pouts, so frustrated and scared that he's crying at this point. Facey stares a hole through Tyrone as he collapses into a ball, clutching his knees and sobbing. "Well, fuck. Johnny, get the screw-driver and blow-torch ready. When we get back to the hotel room, we're going to have to resort to torture to get the phone number!" Tyrone jerks himself up, looking particularly no-where, but the look on his face reads horror. "Dude. I am NOT torturing a twelve-year-old kid." GBJ protests. "Oh, but you'll spray ten of them in the eyes with pepper-spray?" "That's different; that was like a carnival game." "Well, so is this." Facey says. "Think of it as, like, pin-the-smoldering-metal-on-the-kid-with-the-uncle-who's-ass-we-want-to-kick." "Hmmm, well, when you put it like that…" ………………………………………………………………………………a few hours later… With footsteps quiet enough to rival a ninja's, Facey steps into the low-budget, incognito hotel-room he acquired earlier in the day. It was supposed to be used for the actual planning of the kidnapping--a place to escape the public eye--but when your ADD cripples your attention span so that the only thing you can focus on is Ebaumsworld.com, more often than not the course of action will change. "Hurry the fuck up! We don't want anyone to notice that we're dragging a small child into a shady hotel room; who knows what people will think!?" Garbage Bag, struggling as he hold's Tyrone over his shoulder with his arms secure while the kid kicks wildly into his back. "Maybe if you'd just hold his legs like I asked you too, I wouldn't feel like Christopher Reeves trying to walk." "Johnny, if you can't handle a twelve-year-old child, tell me. I won't invite you to shit like this next time." With a look towards the heavens, GBJ sighs and mutters to no one in particularly, that's all it takes? He tosses the child on the bed, who immediately bolts towards the open door. But the Face-Eater's reflexes are lighting-mother-fucking-fast and he shuts the door with a dead-bolt before the kid even takes two steps. "SORRY, ALMASY, BUT YOU ARE NEEDED. ALIVE." The kid sighs in relief, knowing that he wasn't about to die. So Facey decides to shake things up. "ALIVE, FOR NOW! MWAHAHAHAHA!" Then the child begins crying so Facey can leave him alone, work finished. He approaches GBJ who stands at the front-window to make sure no one can see through the cracks of the shut blinds. "So are you sure no one followed us?" Facey whispers into his ear, a panic somewhat taints his speech pattern. "For the last time, I was watching the kid the whole time! There's no way I could have even noticed." "Well, dude." Facey's pitch begins to get a bit squeakier than it normally is. "Come on! If we get caught for this, we're fucked!" OOOOOH, Deep Breath. Calm yourself. With the tension somewhat eased, Facey battles off the sudden motion sickness that hits him along with the paranoia of being caught. He feels sturdy for the moment. Somewhat anxious. While the terror of being convicted for attempted kidnapping and extortion still lingers, Facey knows everything will turn out alright. "Don't panic, don't panic. Everything is cool. Everything is cool. Now all we have to do is wait and the money will roll in!" Assurance in his tone. "EVERYONE OUT OF THE ROOM, THIS IS THE POLICE! RELEASE THE HOSTAGE, RELINQUISH YOUR WEAPONS AND SURRENDER YOURSELVES; NOW!." "What the FUCK?! HOW DID THEY FUCKING FIND US?!" "Well, we DID kidnap a kid in front of a school while a grip-shit of parents and teachers were still there. Watching us. Oh, and then we pepper-sprayed a few of his friends." With a raised eyebrow, GBJ ponders over the recent events in recap style. "I think that's it." "Pft, YOU sprayed kids with pepper-spray. I didn't even DO anything! Wait, maybe we can talk our ways out of this…" With light bulbs lingering brightly over Facey's forehead, he cracks the blinds open and peaks out the window of their floor-story hotel room. He sees four squad-cars, a fire truck, two EMT dispatch teams, and a gaggle of fucking pigs. Fire-arms un-holstered and aimed at face-height, it doesn't take long for Facey to realize the drastic nature of this situation. "Uh, excuse me, officers? I think this is a misunderstanding. See, me and my homeless man-servant were going to pick up my adopted son from Uganda because well, I don't trust man-servants at my home alone because we all know how sticky their fingers are especially when they are bums. No, seriously, his fingers are really sticky--!" "IT'S OVER, ADAM DICK. GIVE UP THE BOY, AND WE'LL MAKE THIS AS PAINLESS AS WE CAN!" With the mention of his name, Facey gets confused. He glances back out the window to examine everyone present once again, as if someone he knows would be out there. "Dude, did you sell me out?" Comes the accusation of GBJ. "I would NEVER sell you out, boss. You're, like, a brother to me." "Thanks, Johnny, that means a lot. Unfortunately, I don't fucking trust you." Tucked behind in the back of his pants, Facey withdraws the Tec-9 he stored for desperate measures and points it at GBJ's head, who immediately wets his pants (for probably the third time this roleplay). "FACEY, DUDE." Rings a familiar voice from outside. Again, Facey peeks out the blinds and scans the squad-cars, where sitting inside is the very man he asked to take part of this plan: NOVA. "Dude, come on, you don't want to do this! You've got so much going for you--why are you resorting to kidnapping kids?!" Nova asks from over the police-intercom, echoing out the mounted speakers and into the room. "I can't believe you fucking sold me out!" Facey screams. "You should have just stayed out of it! BUT NOOO, YOU INSIST ON BRINGING THE BACON." "This is CRAZY, Face! Come on, let's just get stoned! What are you even doing, anyways? Kidnapping a small child--?" "THIS CHILD." Facey interrupts. "HAPPENS TO BE THE LAST LIVING RELATIVE OF THAT BASTARD, SEYMOUR ALMASY, AND WHEN HE FINDS OUT I KIDNAPPED HIS COUSIN, HE'LL HAVE NO CHOICE BUT TO CHOOSE ME AS HIS FIRST ROUND OPPONENT IN THE GTT COMPETITION! THEN, I WILL ELIMINATE HIM AND FEAST ON HIS HEART AND GAIN HIS STRENGTH SO I CAN REACH THE GTT FINALS AND ACHIEVE ETERNAL GLORY." "Dude." Nova begins shaking his head, and pulls his head away from the microphone. "Where do I begin?" Wondering about every aspect that Facey fucked up about his plan. "Dude, FIRST of all, Seymour Almasy already forfeited his first-round-choice! SECONDly, the bracket draw already happened! Almasy isn't even in your bracket! THIRD of all, your first round match has already been decided! You're wrestling the FUSE commentator, Hunter Jones. FOURTH, I don't think there's a single fucking percent of possibility that, whoever that kid you have in there is, he is NOT a relative of Seymour Almasy." As Nova says this, Facey glances at the crying Tyrone, then at GBJ. "Are you sure you didn't just pick this kid based on coincidence of their last names?" Nova calls out. "Well, fucking shit! Who else has such a stupid last name! WELL, IT DON'T MATTER! I WANT ALMASY IN THE FIRST ROUND! AND UNLESS PTC WANTS A PSYCHO MURDERER ON THEIR ROSTER, THEY'LL GIVE IN TO MY DEMANDS!" "Uh, dude?" Nova begins to light up a cigarette. "Something tells me PTC won't CARE if they have a psycho murderer on their roster… Apparently, most of them get immunity or something…" After getting it lit, he takes a mighty drag then speaks back into the microphone. Facey shuts the blinds and rests his back against the window, taking a moment to rest and think. With the barrel of the Tec against his temple and his face deeply entranced in thought, Tyrone and even GBJ begin doubting the ring-leaders frame of mind. "You're telling me that all of this… ALL OF IT.. WAS FOR FUCKING NOTHING?!?!" Facey screams. Not exactly what he or the cops wanted to hear, Nova shrugs. "Pretty much, yeah." A cop quickly rips the microphone from Nova's hands. "That's the LAST time we let a civilian try to address a hostage situation!" Nova begins to smile. "I was stoned and it looked easy. Come on, it's, like, the easiest job in the world!" With a trained professional now taking hold of the microphone, the Cop begins to speak with a much more direct tone to the two perpetrators inside the hotel room. "Alright Son. We know you made a mistake. Just release the hostages, you and your partner can exit unarmed, and we'll all go home. it's not the end of the world for you yet, Son. You still have the power to help us. But if you do anything stupid, by God, we won't be able to help you. Do you understand? It isn't over. You still have a chance. This isn't the end." Haha, he laughs. But something so casual slowly slips into a sinister form similar to a hyena cackling. "This isn't the END? YOU DON'T FUCKING KNOW ME, PAL! THIS WAS ALL FOR NOTHING! ALL OF THIS PLANNING, WASTED! AND FOR WHAT?! BECAUSE I'M A FUCKING STONER AND CAN'T SEEM TO REMEMBER ANYTHING CORRECTLY!?! NO, SORRY OFFICER, BUT IT IS OVER!!" The police are all getting anxious on the outside, they look to their captain who stands in the back with a worry on his face. The captain glances to the man holding the microphone, who attempts to continue. "No, Son! Listen, all you've got to do--." "No, YOU FUCKING LISTEN! BECAUSE THAT'S ALL I EVER WANTED ANYONE TO DO! THAT'S ALL I EVER WANTED, WAS SOME FUCKING RECOGNITION! FROM ALMASY, FROM ANYONE! BUT NO ONE COULD OFFER IT! NOT EVEN NOVA, MY SUPPOSED FRIEND, WOULD BE THERE FOR ME!!! I'M SUPPOSED TO BE SOMEONE, GOD DAMMIT! I'M SUPPOSED TO BE A FUCKING SUPERSTAR!!! BUT I GOT TO FACE THESE FUCKING LOSERS!?!? FROM FUSE?!? I'VE HELD CHAMPIONSHIPS IN COUNTLESS FEDS AND I'VE BROUGHT COUNTLESS FEDS TO THEIR KNEES WITH MY WRATH, BUT I'M WRESTLING A DILDO WHO'S ONLY COMMENTATED A FEW MONTHS WORTH OF SECOND-RATE WRESTLING PROGRAMS!!! I'M SORRY, COCKHEADS, BUT IT IS OVER!!! IT'S OVER FOR THE FUCKING REST OF YOU, BECAUSE FACEY ISN'T GOING TO BE DICKED OVER ANY LONGER!!!" Face quickly grips Garbage Bag Johnny by the hair, kicks open the hotel door, and walks out with his arm around GBJ's neck and the barrel of his gun pressed against his head. Even through the mask, you can see the desperation and craze plastered across his face. Or maybe the mask just accentuates it; it's hard to tell. With a new hostage, Facey faces the police squad (and Nova) with a renewed sense of valor.
