5 June 2008 question: why am i here? answer: chris storm is IG champion. question: chris storm is a fagot. answer: that's not a question. question: why do i care? answer: because even a three-legged dog with autism has a better chance of winning that belt than chris storm does at retaining it. question: can i kill von doom? answer: after you come through on your promise of killing almasy. question: i'm gonna kill von doom. answer: too easy. he's burying himself with image and youtube tags. like anyone in this dig has read more than five words that he's posted. also, that wasn't a question either. question: why is von doom so fucking stupid? NO ANSWER. question: can i shit on chris storm? answer: *checks the status of bobino* yes. yes you can. question: tell me, how can one man think his shit is so hot when that title has been passed around more times than Lindsay Troy in FWO, EPW, and PRIME combined? and, like Lindsay Troy, it doesn't stay with one person for long. answer: because Lindsay Troy is a D-word. question: a dick head? answer: no. question: a dung beatle? answer: quit acting a von doom. question: a dyk-- answer: YOU DON'T WANT RJ TO RETURN AND BAN YOUR ASS, DO YOU? question: what am i, chris storm? answer: hmmm, good point. ---- 6 June 2008 so, i've been sitting here. thinking. thinking, "why waste my time with this shit?" i mean, it really wouldn't matter if i got the shot. it wouldn't matter that i go down to the ring, wait for chris to join me, and beat his ninny ass all across the mat. it wouldn't matter that the thousands of fans in attendance and the millions watching from home are praising my shit-stomping skills as the ref graces my waist with those fifteen pounds of gold. none of that really matters. what does, is that chris storm held the infinite gauntlet championship for the grand total of five fucking minutes. and he'll continue to run his mouth as if he's holding it - as if it meant something when he held it. great. we're sinking to new depths on a consecutive basis - cassandra paragon, danny morrison, fucking hunter sabuani. if i were a david lynch fan, that list might be obscure enough to make my balls tinge all the way to high heaven. you aren't hot shit, chris. you held the global title for a fucking month over four years ago, yet you claim it heralded the title's rise to prominence. you'll probably do the same for the gauntlet. let me tell you, that ain't happening. let's face it, you're another rung in the ladder of one-shot champions. we all know that, without a partner, you can't keep a strap around your waist past a first defense. so let's just get it over with. drop the belt to a formidable champion - one that PTC can throw it's weight behind and support. one that PTC KNOWS can carry on past the one defense stigma. step into the ring with good old face-eater - if you dare. i'd say it's an easy feat, but i'd be lying. just ask Kimbusa (but have your partner be there. y'know, for your safety). i can't say it won't be scary, chris. honestly, you'll be so fucking terrified, you'll wish you were jon benet ramsey. after our match, you'll be so horrified, the chris benoit story will seem like a nursery rhyme. you'll be so fucking battered, you'll wish you were in the spaceshuttle challenger. we know you're losing the belt the first chance you get*. don't keep trudging what's left of this establishment in mediocrity by giving it to someone like Seymour Almasy (yawn) or Jesse Jamester... again. lose it to me. let's thrust this bitch into the limelight, like only a face-eater can. *a match with von doom excluded. ---- 6 June 2008 i'm sorry, i guess i forgot to remind you that THIS ISN'T 2004. i also forgot to remind you that THE UWF WAS A PISS-POOR EXCUSE OF A JOKE. so you can shove your "historic" accolades and your "record long singles champion reigns" down your own fucking throat, because that shit don't matter. you won every single title in WIW. OVER FIVE YEARS AGO. guess what? did the same thing in PRIME and AWC (sans the Uni strap, but fuck that shit), less than two. and i haven't stopped spitting that hot fire, son. so go on and tout the horn you love. go on with your "greatest tag-team ever" schpiel because that argument is the most valid, and it's still a bold face lie. i've been on two teams, and between them i myself shit on every single one of your accomplishments. you're proud of your year long reign when your company is wearing itself thin in their search for "talent" to put up against you. von doom? please, i blast my toilet bowl with pieces von dooms on the daily. kimbusa? well, we already know the tale of facey and the whale. you've got your categories all wrong, chris. see, you've divided them perfectly; it's just that they've got the wrong labels. on one side, you have the people who can see right through the shit you love to air out on these here boards (and, boy oh boy, there are a lot of us), and on the other you have the people too fucking blind to see you for the arrogant shithead that you are. either way, a person on either of those sides is going to wreck your shit. why not me? :) ---- 6 June 2008
