Date: 2004-10-07 ~ Begin Scene ~ “PUSH! PUSH! BREATHE! BREATHE! PUSH!” A woman in a white nurse’s gown hollers at Rachel while her legs are propped up in the stirrups. “I CAN’T! IT HURTS TOO MUCH!” Rachel shouts back at the nurse. Suddenly the surgery room door flies open and Arch Angel bursts through, wrestling gear still on. He rushes over to the table and clinches onto Rachel’s hand. Caressing her head Angel assures her everything is okay. “It’s okay baby, I made it. You don’t have to worry...I won my match.” Angel whispers into Rachel’s ear. The nurse continues to coach Rachel along as sweat pours down her face. After a few moments the nurse nods for the doctor to come over. As the doctor approaches Rachel lets out a relieving whelp. Her belly deflated and her pupils dilated as the nurse cut the cord. As Angel continued to caress Rachel’s hair a doctor grabbed his arm and pulled him outside of the room. Sitting Angel down the doctor pulled out a chart and rubbed his head. Seeming to have a blank mind and can’t find the words he wants. “Mr. Staton...she’s not going to make it.” The doctor sighs. An expression of despair and shock comes over Angel’s face as he jumps up in front of the doctor. “What the hell do you mean she isn’t going to make it?” Angel screams. “She lost a lot of blood and there is no way she will be able to recover...I’m sorry.” The doctor regrettably puffed. Angel fought the tear that rolled down his cheek as he forced out a more painful sentence, “And what of my son?” “He is very weak and is unlikely to survive through the night. I’m sorry, I really am. These things just happen sometimes.” The Doc held back tears as well. “Bullshit! There has to be something that can be done. I don’t care the cost just do it!” Angel shouted. “Really it isn’t a matter of money sir, there just isn’t anything we can do at this point.” The doctor replied. Angel falls to his knees and in a matter of second’s flashbacks of his life with Rachel run rampant through his mind. Their first time meeting in the school library. That time he couldn’t sum up the courage to ask her out to the prom. Their first kiss in his old broken down 85’ Chevy Blazer. Finally coming to a halt at the time Rachel stepped out of the bathroom and wrapped her arms around him then whispered into his ear, “I’m pregnant...”. Snapping out of his incoherent mindset Angel gathers himself and opens the surgery room door to see Rachel lying on the table, barely able to move. Angel walks over to the table and places his hand in hers. Letting out a painful sigh Rachel turns her head to face Angel. “Hey baby, I don’t feel so hot...” Rachel cries. “I know sweetie, I know.” Angel tearfully replies. Falling to one knee Angel almost collapses. Pulling his chain cross from around his neck he places it in Rachel’s palm as they both hum a silent prayer. Angel lowers his head as tears pour out seeming to never end. As a hand is felt on his head he looks up and Rachel is beaming what seems to be the most beautiful smile he has ever seen. “I...Love...” Rachel hisses out with her last breaths as her head falls back into the pillow. “Why me...why now?” Angel moans as the heart monitor drops to a flat line hum. Beep... Beep... Beep... Booooooooooooooooooo! Angel covers closes Rachel’s eyes with one hand as he pulls the covers up with the other. Exiting the room and walking down the hallway at a steady pace Angel is road blocked by GTT4 Reporters and a camera crew. “Excuse me Angel, after your victory over Johnny Blaze how do you feel? Angel...what are your thoughts on your upcoming match against Train? Excuse me Angel....” The reporter boasts at Angel as he walks past. Suddenly Angel turns grasping the shirt of the reporter with both hands and lifting him up into the wall. Breathing heavily Angel stares deep into the eyes of the reporter. Tears still streaming down his face as he releases the reporter and exits the hospital. The reporter turns back towards the camera, “Well I guess we all can sum up that this is not a good thing for Train. With only a monstrous glare Arch Angel is coming! David Sumner for GTT4 News Coverage is out!” The reporter signals for the crew to cut the camera’s and approaches one of the men holding a notepad. “Man what was his problem?” The man holding the notepad asked David. “I don’t know but it can’t be good.” David replied. ~ End Scene ~
