Global Title Contenders Match

26 January 2005

"The Road to the Global title begins" The symptoms begin I remember it as if it were yesterday. I had gone to bed early that night, around 2 am which is pretty early for a partier like myself, with plans of waking up early to get some business out of the way. I wanted to get an early jump on things so that I could enjoy the rest of my day. There was only one problem when I swung my feet off the side of my bed and attempted to get in an upright position; I felt like all the air had been sucked out of me. “Come on Justin, you got your butt kicked at Blizzard but that is no reason to be a sissy about things.” I say to myself to try to give myself a reason to get out of bed and stop being such a pansy. I put the back of my hand up to my forehead and yelp in pain. I’m burning up. I finally manage to get to my feet after quite the struggle and stumble into the bathroom, nearly tripping over my computer chair as I move along. I can’t figure out why I’m so weak. I know I took a pretty severe beating, but that was a few weeks ago and my body should have had the time to heal and rebuild itself by now. I close the mirror on the medicine cabinet that I don’t remember opening in the first place and I see a look of horror reflecting back at me. I put my hands up to my face and push my index fingers into the puffy area below both of my eyes. Before I know what is happening, I quickly reach for the toilet seat and lift it up, spewing last night’s steak and potato dinner into the porcelain waterway. The camera continues, for some God forsaken reason, to focus on the remnants of my delicious meal. My hand can be seen disappearing from the shot and suddenly the sound of running water takes over any other noise in the room. I get up from the toilet and step into the shower, throwing my clothes out over the top of the curtain. I peep my head through the shower curtain and tell the camera man to cut for now. Feeling a little better The camera cuts back on as the camera man is focused in on my reflection that is coming from my bedroom mirror as I adjust my tie and wink at the camera in the reflection of the mirror. The camera man gives me a thumbs up, knowing that I will be much more pleasant to be around now that I am feeling a little bit better. Hopefully that prescription medication I took will keep what appears to be a flu in check. I will try to get to the doctor after I leave the office, but who knows if that will actually happen. The twenty minute car ride from my Cleveland Penthouse to WMW Studio B in downtown Cleveland is only about twenty minutes away. I put the aforementioned Flu medicine into my brief case in case I need it while I’m taking care of my Vice Presidential business. The first ten minutes went pretty well. I was listening to Pearl Jam’s greatest hits album “Rearview Mirror”. Most people should understand that I really don’t think they should have released this record. It’s not really their style, on the contrary, I knew that they had to do it to get out of their record contract with Sony Music. Anyway, that first ten minutes was perfectly fine, then I pulled up at a stop light and it was all downhill from there. The next thing I know there is an impatient jerk behind me laying on his horn because the light has turned green and I’m not moving. You would think he would have just a tad bit of sympathy for me since I’m leaning out of my rolled down window puking my guys out. Not this guy though, he just had to keep laying on that horn. I finish puking, reach into the glove compartment for some napkins and breath mints and then decide this guy has to pay. I slowly get out of my car and raise to my full size, hoping that he will back down. I feel like crap, I have a lot of work to do and I really don’t want to have to deal with some ‘billy badass’. As I approach the car, he makes rude hand gestures and appears unflappable. “Is there a problem sir?” I ask him as I lean on his car door. “Yeah, I’m trying to get somewhere and you are stopping me. If you have to puke, pull over to the side of the road. If you’re going to be an asshole, at least be a considerate asshole.” This guy is just smiling from ear to ear, he is really annoying me and I don’t have to mention for the millionth time that I’m just not in the mood for this. Ok, so it wasn’t the millionth time I would say that but that’s not the point. Lately I’ve been having anger management issues and this guy is about to find that out first hand. You have to realize that even with him sitting down I can figure out that I have a quite a few inches and probably seventy five pounds on him. So, whatever is about to happen to him is out of my hands. Before the guy even has time to react I quickly open up his car door and yank him out of his seat. The only thing that I didn’t realize was that once the initial adrenaline fizzled out, I was going to be out of energy just like I have been since I woke up this morning. I feel this weird sensation when my entire body goes numb for a split second followed by blood squirting out of my mouth as random jerkoff begins to knee me mercilessly in the abdomen. I decide that in my current state, the only thing I can do is fall to the ground and curl into a ball. It turns out that random dipshit isn’t much for beating someone when they are down, so he hawks a friendly loogie at me and then gets back into his car and speeds off. With the little bit of energy level that I have, it takes me about five minutes to actually get to my feet and begin to walk towards my car. I tell the camera man to stop rolling until we get to my office, I can already tell that this is going to be a long unpredictable day. At the office, finally This particular scene begins as I am walking into the headquarters of Wrestling Midwest. I walk a few feet to the elevator and push the “2” button. My office just so happens to be on the second floor for those of you idiots that can’t figure that out. When I finally get to the second floor, my personal Assistant Kenny quickly shuffles up to me. “Good morning Mr. Evers, you aren’t looking too well, must be that Flu that is going around.” I simply nod my head and let him say whatever it is that he is going to say next. The key with Kenny is to just let him get his talking out of the way. If you converse with him then he will never let you go about your business. “We are ordering brunch right now. I already have an order from Syberus and AWS Man, do you know what you might want from McDonalds?” Ugh, why does this guy always pick the worst restaurants to eat at? He knows that myself and my fellow front office members have all the money in the world, but we let him pick where we eat to make him feel like he is actually needed. We only hired him because we got sick of him begging us for money outside of the McDonalds. In case you are wondering, AWS Man was the one who chose McDonalds before Kenny was around to make that idiotic decision for us. Sometimes I think he gives the money we give him to his fellow former bums and just steals the food. That would explain why we never get what we order. “I don’t really feel all that great Kenny, McDonalds will probably make me even more sick. I think I’m just going to walk across the street to that Soup joint and get a bowl of soup or something. Thanks though.” “Okie dokie, your loss.” Kenny says as he walks away and I roll my eyes. I decide to pay AWS Man a visit before actually getting to my office so I get back into the elevator and take it down to the first floor. As I approach his office, I heard some wretched singing, apparently the Insane Pres thinks he is Frank Sinatra today. Every day he picks a new old time singer to impersonate. Good thing most of them are dead and don’t have to hear his ‘vocal stylings’. I knock on the door and a “It’s freakin open, enter at your own risk” is the response that I get. I attempt to turn the door knob before realizing that the door is in fact locked. I suddenly get that uneasy feeling back in my stomach. I know that AWS Man has a bathroom just off of his office, but I have to get inside the office to use it. “Listen man, I’m not feeling too well and this door is definitely locked. If you don’t let me in, you are going to have a steaming pile of puke outside your door.” AWS Man took his sweet ass time getting to the door and by the time he did, it was too late. Before he even had time to react, he was wiping partially digested carrot from the eye holes of his paintball mask. “That’s just freakin’ grooooooooosss.” AWS Man yells out before running into his office and grabbing a towel of his the nearby gym bag that was laying underneath his desk. I quickly run to the bathroom for another rousing round of puking my guts out. AWS Man hands be a bottled water as I get up from emptying my already empty stomach and I nod as if to say “thank you”. “So, have you freakin thought about cutting a promo for your PTC Global title contenders match yet?” I raised an eyebrow to AWS Man. Lately he had been better than me at remembering important things like matches. I had been on cloud nine lately and I really needed to come down from that if I was going to have any chance of having success in PTC. “I’ve been so sick lately I completely forgot about that match dude.” I respond to him to which he replies with, “Well, I don’t think you are going to have all that tough of a time anyway. After all, in the last few months you beat Sonic and myself. If you can beat Sonic who made it pretty far in the GTT4, then you should have no problems disposing of these guys.” I nod my head in approve at the confidence that my stable mate and current best friend is giving me. “I agree with you, well at least I would if I knew who the heck it was that I’m fighting.” AWS Man rubs his chin, well at least he rubs the chin of his paintball mask. “I know I got a freakin fax around here some the freak where.” AWS man begins to rummage around on his desk before finally finding a sheet of paper with a PTC letterhead. “Ah, here the freak it is.” As he was talking I could feel the temperature begin to rise again. I reached in my briefcase and pulled out some of the medicine that I had brought along and took a few pills getting them down with the water in my hand. “Lets see here, we got Cameron Worrell, Emilio Estevez and Summer Rain. Yup, they all sound like easy freaks that you are going to slap around in no time. GTC title match here you come!” I snatched the fax from AWS Man and read out the real names of the guys that I would be fighting. I didn’t recognize any of the names listed, but I knew that I was really going to have to train hard to win this match. I thought about doing individual trash talk but I was leaning towards not bothering on that and just focusing on my training for the match. All of my thought processes were ruined when I heard a “tally hooooo!” coming from across the room. I walked to a spot in AWS Man’s office where the carpet read “secret entrance to the office of the Head Janitor, Syberus. I had no idea that Syberus had a secret entrance to his office, I had been there a thousand times. I shrugged my shoulders and pulled up the carpet, then climbed down the ladder that lead into the dungeon where Syberus office was located. When I got down there Syberus and a few other janitors were using weapons of janitorial importance to fight off this evil dragon that was trying to set Syberus pool table on fire. “First of all, there is no secret entrance to my office and second of all there are no fire breathing dragons.” Syberus interrupted my narrator as I entered his office and he was playing a rousing game of skee ball with Matthew Perry. “So how you been Syb? We haven’t gotten a chance to talk lately since I’ve been off of Wrestling Midwest TV the last few weeks.” Congratulations on being Fury by the way. Syberus looks at the Midwest title sitting on his desk and shrugs his shoulders, getting back to his game of skee ball while replying to me. “It really was no problem. We have both beat Fury in big time matches, the guy is a major choke artist.” ”Yeah, he only beat AWS Man for the Great Lakes title because I wanted to beat Fury more for the title so I cheated to help Fury win.” “So I hear you have this big match coming up in PTC. Do you have confidence that you can win even though you haven’t been in a competitive match in quite some time?” “I believe that if I train properly and find a way to get my head back into this thing, I can definitely perform at the level to win this four way. I think I showed in GTT4 that if I would have worked a little bit harder I could have actually made it out of the first round. That is in the past though and that is exactly where I am going to leave it. I will watch some film from that match and see what I did wrong but I’m not going to live on what ifs. I’m going to learn from my mistakes like I always do and then prepare for this four way match. I only have to beat 2 guys to make it into the match for the Global title, but I want to be the last man standing. I want to send a message to whomever the Global champion ends up being at the time that Wrestling Midwest is the real deal. Despite the fact that AWS Man lost when he had a few shots at the title, he truly opened the eyes of a lot of people in PTC. I strongly feel that he opened the door for me and now it’s time for me to repay him by bringing home the Global title to the greatest stable in wrestling history, The Powers That Be (Kicking Your Ass).” That stable consists of AWS Man, Syberus and myself for those of you who don’t know. Two of the greatest Great Lakes Champions of all time and the current Midwest slash up and coming great Syberus. Syberus shakes his head in agreement and I head out into the hall and board the elevator to finally go to my office. I really should get this Mono taken care of. Fade to the color of your choice.
