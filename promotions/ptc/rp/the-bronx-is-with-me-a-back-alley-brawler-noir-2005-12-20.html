The Bronx Is With Me (A Back Alley Brawler Noir)

20 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-20 [name withheld] "Hi, Joe." And he thought his night couldn’t get any worse. A two hour walk through the light devoid streets of the Bronx and a liter of Jack Daniels were the cure for the terrible night the Brawler had been having thus far. Mere hours earlier he was coughing up blood into a toilet and racking his brain, wondering if he had imagined the presence of Death or really held a conversation with the Grim Reaper. That was always the inherent problem with being drunk, reality and fantasy often melded into each other. "I know you Joe. You’re too headstrong to back down from GTT. And it seems like God and Satan seem to only want people for shock value the past few years. How else could you explain Richard Pryor? I’m going to tell you this information now….if you win the Global Title Tournament, you’re going to die. Between all the stress, the pills you’re popping, and the punishment you’re absorbing; your celebration will be cut short by an untimely death." The entire walk home, when he wasn’t concocting different ways to a land a hard right hand to Seymour’s visage, he reflected on that statement by the imaginary/real entity that was Death. Had the alcohol and Percocets really taken that much of a toll on his mental health? Unfortunately, it wasn’t a question he could answer clearly at that moment. All he wanted was to open the door to his shithole of a room at the Trailways Motel and call it a night. It didn’t matter that his current living situation made a crack house look like the Hilton, at least he wouldn’t be using the BronxBar bathroom toilet as a pillow tonight. As if he didn’t have enough problems to reflect upon in his already troubled sleep, a very curvaceous one presented itself the moment he opened his hotel room door. Karen Balboa. The mother of The Back Alley Brawler’s child, Carmen, and the only ex-wife he ever loved. He had no clue how she found her way into his temporary haven, but she looked completely out of place in a motel room filled with emptied bottles of alcohol and stray newspaper articles. Like a white rose in a garden of snapping Venus flytraps, Karen’s had no place in the world of depravity the Back Alley Brawler could not escape from. The last time he saw her, she ejected the Brawler from her and their daughter’s life. Choosing the Infinite Gauntlet and a Global Wrestling contract over being a battle-tested family man, Karen’s verbal assault and goodbye kiss hurt more than any chairshot the Brawler had taken in his career. But there she was, angelic, the white light in his world of darkness. He could tell she had thrown some clothes on, because she never wore jumpsuits unless she had to run the quickest of errands. It was a testament that even so "thrown together", the Brawler would have given into her desires as easily as one could breath…. ….if it weren’t for the sheer hatred her harbored for her. "Aren’t you going to say anything?" He wanted to say everything and nothing. Let her in and shut her out. Karen proved to be the ultimate killer of his alcohol buzz as the Brawler eyed her delicious figure, the limited light streaming from the blinds only accentuating what he knew she offered. His worn eyes met her cinnamon colored pupils, Karen still awaiting anything to come from her ex-husband’s mouth. Finally, the Brawler stood to the side of the door and pointed to the stale hallway outside the confines of his motel room. "Get da fuck out." "I’m not going anywhere Joe, not until we talk." "We’ll talk mah next court-appointed visit ya spiteful bitch." Karen folded her arms, doing her best to stay calm despite Joe’s vicious barbs. The Back Alley Brawler studied her finely aged features, cursing the fact that the years always showed kindness to Karen’s natural beauty, leaving him forever attracted to her. She took in a soothing breath, batting a strand of her brunette hair out of her eyes as she looked to her former lover once more. "Stop holding that against me. I told you that you couldn’t have us and wrestling Joe and I stuck by it. I didn’t come here for that." "Den’ what is ya hea’ fo then? Why da fuck is you messin up what’s left uv mah damn night?! How’d ya even get in hea?" "I told the desk clerk I was a hooker. Compliments of one of the local bosses, hoping you would make the Bronx proud in GTT." She always was resourceful. Things like that made Joe want to rethink his stance on hating her, but he had to hold his ground. "Give me one good reesun(reason) why I shouldn’t boot ya ass out mah room mahself?" Her reply was simple, snatching up a bottle of Percocet on the rickety dresser, having found it in the room prior to the Brawler returning. The evidence was cold and hard as she thrust it out to him, causing the Back Alley Brawler to shut two things with that action. The door and his mouth. The room was now devoid of light, the only exception being the thin slices of moonlight seeping through the blinds of the window. Karen took slight steps towards the hulking bruiser, her lips quivering for reasons yet to be known by the Brawler. Karen’s steps continued until she was right before the Brawler, escaping the shower of light she once stood in and sharing the darkness with her ex-husband. The Brawler could feel the plastic bottle pressed against his chest now, Karen looking up to him and in his eyes. "I don’t hate you, Joe. I hate what you do to yourself. I cried for days when I found out about your knee inju-" "It musta not hurt dat much, since you neva even visted me at da hospital." "YOU THINK I WANTED TO SEE YOU LIKE THAT?! Unable to walk, broken down like I feared? I wouldn’t have been able to take it Joe and you know that. As much as I want to write you out of my life, I still worry about you…" Her soft right hand gently caressed the Brawler’s battle-worn cheek, the Bronx Barbarian wanting to turn away, but falling victim to her satin touch. He closed his eyes and drifted into memories of when she was his. The days when her naked right hand used to brandish a simple gold ring, representing the union they shared. Two months of shitty pay at the fish market was worth it to see her smile, the glow she emitted eclipsing the luster of his symbol of marriage. "I watched your first round match, me and Carmen. She was so proud to see her daddy win…and admittedly, I was too. But then I saw your face Joe. I could feel how much pain you were in, I just knew everything wasn’t right with you. I was hoping you’d come back through the Bronx before your round 2 match and you did. You’ve been the talk of the town lately, so you weren’t hard to find. I promise, I just wanted to see how you were…then I found these Joe." She tapped the Percocet bottle to his chest, breaking him from his pleasant memories of what used to be his life. "The knee brace was one thing, but pills Joe? It’s all going too far. You don’t know the meaning of quit and I fear that the day I turn on that TV….they’ll…they…." He could see it in her eyes and knew what was coming. Instinctively, he opened his arms for her and she crashed into the wall that was his chest. The pills fell to the motel room floor to join the other wreckage strewn about the room. Karen didn’t have the strength of will like her ex-husband had and like always, even with how strained things were; he was there to comfort her. Joe could feel the tears flowing from her eyes and onto his wifebeater, her breaths frantic and desperate. "Joe I still love you. I always have. I…I just want you to…-sniffle- -sniffle-…I want you to be alive. I want you to come home with me tonight, please. I keep hearing about wre-wrestlers dying and getting injured beyond repair, I’ve hated you, but never enough to wish that on you….not you. I just know if you ke…-sniff sniff-.. keep fighting, something I going to happen to you…and we’ll lo…I’LL LOSE YOU. Don’t fight anymore please, just come home Joe…we can fix everything I promise." She still loved him. It took him reaching "dead man walking" status for her to finally admit that truth, but at least she admitted it. It was the sweetest symphony he had ever heard next to, "It’s a girl Mr. Balboa," and it filled his heart with the warmth it had lacked for so many cold years. "Joe, I’ll do anything, just please…please come home before something happens." Karen was right. The buffets of alcohol and painkillers were becoming too much for even the Brawler to handle. He could feel the years of constant punishment each new day, always in fear of going to sleep because he was never sure he could wake up. Death had mentioned that if he quit, the Brawler would probably regret the decision knowing he could have won. That choice would have lead to an eventual suicide. But would he really have made that choice knowing he would return to a loving family? A wife and a child eager to accept him back in their lives? Karen had braved the rough streets of the Bronx, putting herself at risk all in the name of making a plea for the Bronx Barbarian to save himself before it was too late. Was the quest for the Global Title really worth it, knowing that there would be more days like earlier, with the Brawler puking crimson into a random toilet? Even in victory, he would die alone, with no family to celebrate his impossible win with. The Brawler was tired of hurting. Tired of having no one to love him. It was at that moment that he surrendered to reason. For once he would make the sane choice. Quit. He pulled Karen’s face up from his chest, looking her deep in the limpid pools of brown that were her eyes. A salty trail of tears was visible on her face, the Brawler wishing he could shed his share of tears along with her. It was a pity he forgot how. Even crying made him want her more. This was Karen with her defenses totally dismantled. She was giving herself to only man she had ever loved, pleading for his return to her life. He knew what needed to be done. Only three words could start the process of completion for the estranged lovers. "Karen. I’ll quit." He could feel her cheeks curving into a smile which eventually formed on her face. She could feel her fear being stripped away and banished to a far off land with those three words. The hulking bruiser had finally surrendered to the power of her love. Carmen would have her father. Karen would have her husband. Their family would have a second chance again with Joe Balboa making the choice to end his life as The Back Alley Brawler… ….and resume his long lost role of Joe Balboa, father and husband. Swept up in this beautiful moment, she could care less if she was in a shitty motel room or on top of Mount Everest, love returned to her life. She leaned the Brawler’s head down, pressing her lips to his with all the passion she could muster. This was unlike the kiss she gave the Brawler before he left on his odyssey for Global Wrestling. This kiss was a promise of a new beginning. A promise of better memories to replace the fractured, spiteful ones they recently created. This kiss was as fulfilling and real as the day she said, "I do". The kiss turned from an innocent press of the lips to a furious melding of the two as one. The electricity between the estranged lovers could have powered New York for decades. This was everything and more then they could have imagined, like making love for the first time with someone you truly love. Or a drink of ice water on the hottest day of the summer. No matter what any dictionary defined it as, this would forever be the definition of "fulfillment" for Karen and Joe. Karen released the kiss long enough to look Joe in his eyes, both releasing the flood gates of lust for each other. Her arms wrapped around his muscular neck, her caress sent tingles across every muscle of the Brawler’s failing body. "Carmen’s got a babysitter until morning. If you want…" The Back Alley Brawler smirked, pressing his hand to the small of her back. She responded by pressing her healthy frame to him, forgetting just how pleasurable the warmth of a man could be after four years. He delicately ran his fingers through her hair, treating Karen like the fragile flower he knew she was. Her eyes closed as she slipped into a private ecstasy, the want that would appear when she opened her eyes a clue that she wanted Joe to join her. "I kno’ what I want from you. And I don’t think ya gonna fight me, is you?" "No." Karen took careful steps backwards, the Brawler being led by the hand to the sorry excuse for a bed behind her. She took a seat on the edge, sitting the Brawler next to her as well before releasing his hand. This was pure happiness the two were experiencing; giggling slightly at the situation they found themselves in. It was their honeymoon all over again. And they loved every second of it. Karen took this opportunity to make the first strike, moving in for the kiss on her brute of a lover. However, she was stopped mid-way, the Brawler taking her in his arms and pulling her down with him on the bed. His grasp about her was secure, Karen returning in his embrace in kind as the two stared into each others eyes horizontally. She admittedly was confused, her mind racing with thoughts of making love to the Bronx Barbarian after so long. Karen awaited his move, but none came. "I..I thought that we were going to…well, make love." "We are, Karen." She didn’t get it until he laid her head on his chest, his embrace yet to falter. The epiphany hit her and made their situation so clear. Having sex, as wonderful as she imagined it would have been, would only ruin the pure innocence of this movement. Joe could find pleasure, legal and illegal, anywhere. But, it was innocent fulfillment that the Brawler truly coveted. Their quiet embrace, with the moonlight to blanket them was that innocence. And it was absolutely perfect. This moment wasn’t the birth of his daughter, or his Global Television title win….but it was damn close when it came to the greatest moments in his life. With each passing moment in her arms, the Global Title and the fact he was quitting seemed to matter less and less. Who knew bliss could be found in the Bronx? The Next Morning …….If only it were that easy. NOTHING was ever easy for the Back Alley Brawler. Especially lying. Karen was fast asleep in his motel room, the Brawler having managed to escape her embrace around 5 o’clock in the morning. Before leaving, he burned the image of her sleeping peacefully in his mind along with the blissful slumber they shared. It would be that moment he would rely on whenever he needed a reminder of what happiness felt like. The painful nights when he contemplated jamming a switchblade in his temple, he would think of their loving embrace at the Trailways Motel and have faith life was worth living. Yes, he told Karen he’d quit….but he never said right away. One day, if she would forgive him for the harsh lie he told her, he would honor his backhanded promise and be the husband and father to Carmen she needed. But for now, the Bronx needed a hero…a symbol to represent them in the Global Title Tournament. So despite not being the flashiest or most eye-catching symbol ever, he would strive to at least be effective. So there he was, back on the streets of the Bronx with a New York Giants hoodie on. Braving the frigid weather, the Brawler was jogging down the street, 3 Percocets for breakfast aiding him in this doing this task without accustomed pain that followed. What was worth noting was the Walkman clipped to his pants, a yellow relic that played cassette tapes instead of today’s standard of CD’s. It looked like a clock radio was bolted to his side, but it served the purpose of playing the song he needed to hear with his back against the wall. The whole run, only one question repeated through his mind without fail. "What would Rocky do?" Rocky Balboa, his middle-namesake, never backed down from a challenge no matter how stiff. So it would be an insult to back down from the man who everyone pegged to oust The Back Alley Brawler from the tournament, namely Seymour Almasy. If not the Brawler, who else would go the distance to show that the Bronx had more to offer the world than drugs, crime, and ghettos as far as the eye could see? It was up to Joe Balboa to prove that the Bronx could produce winners too. So with that, he turned his Walkman up to full blast and proceeded into a rare full sprint down the grime-infested streets of the Bronx. He could feel his muscles pushing harder than they had ever been worked as his speed picked up, the Brawler refusing to let the fire in his heart die out. It was then, taking in the raw energy of his hometown, that these words escaped his mouth. " Risin up / back on the street / did my time took my chances / went the distance now I'm back on my feet / just a man and his will to survive!" It was "Eye of The Tiger" playing on his Walkman, the same fight song that drove Rocky Balboa to greatness in the famous series of movies. He bellowed the words to the song loud for all to hear, releasing his fears of death, failure, and regret through Survivor’s inspiring words. "so many times/ it happens too fast / you trade your passion for glory / don't lose your grip on the dreams of the past / you must fight just to keep them alive!" His speed increased as he rounded Steinwell Apartments, but something else increased too. At that moment, a group of five young boys (8-10 years old) in their winter coats began to follow the Brawler, running for dear life to catch up to the battle-tested vet. Caught off guard by their presence, the Brawler turned down his Walkman while still in sprint. "What are you lil’ scamps doin round hea?" They smiled, steadily running by his side. Not knowing any other way to put their intentions to the 21 year veteran of the sport, all they could do was show him. "It's the eye of the tiger its the cream of the fight risin up to the challenge of our rivals and the last known survivor stalks his prey in the night and he's watching us all with the eye of the tiger!!" The Brawler smiled, flattered deep inside that the children would join him on his run. Why stop them? Turning up the music on his Walkman, now a group six strong sang "Eye of The Tiger". As the singing continued, The Brawler began to notice a stampede of working class men abandoning the 7th Street strip of local bars and putting on their winter coats. Some jetted directly to the front, others were content with a brisk pace that placed them in the back. The point was, they were also running with the Brawler… …And singing. "Face to face out in the heat hanging tough stayin hungry the stack the odds still we take to the street for the kill with the skill to survive!! He was amazed by their presence, wondering how a simple 6 o’clock run could draw the ire of a force of now 30 people, all singing the song on unison. The Brawler had now passed 5th Street with his force, his speed climbing as he could feel their energy racing through his own veins, providing warmth regardless of the temperature. He could see the 5TH Street mainstay of Foster’s Gym coming up on the horizon…and something else… ….A group of 30 boxers, all in their winter clothing and jogging in place. As the stampede of joggers came their way, they began to seamlessly meld into the group as well. The entity was now large enough to take up the entire street as the entire block now echoed with the following words. It's the eye of the tiger its the cream of the fight risin up to the challenge of our rivals and the last known survivor stalks his prey in the night and he's watching us all with the eye of the tiger!!!! It took a while, but he realized what was now happening. It was the Bronx looking out for their own. It was no secret the Brawler was a broken down man, far from the prime of his life. But he still had the respect of the Bronx and all its populace. It only proved its strength as tenants from the 4th street ghettos, a group of 100 strong made up of all races and creeds, awaited the wave of runners. They had yet to get in step, but were already singing along. "Risin up straight to the top had the guts got the glory went the distance now I'm not gonna stop just a man and his will to survive!" These people were the heart, the pulse, the life blood of the Bronx. These were the losers, under-class, dreamers, gangbangers, whores, hopeless, and even the derelict that the news and TV categorized as the population of the Bronx. To the world, the Bronx was just a shithole of a city in New York. The place you pass over on your way to Manhattan. The spot gangsta rappers mentioned in their rhymes to sell albums, yet were too scared to ever call their true home. This mass making a beeline straight for city hall were the underdogs of the world, who couldn’t quite agree with that assessment no matter how true it was. Fuck what the world thought, this was the Bronx united in a common cause… …This wasn’t just Joe Balboa attempting to win the Global Title Tournament. This was THE BRONX trying to take the prize home. As they neared the steps of City Hall, more bodies joined the mass until the streets were covered with runners, all of the same mind and body and all with the same goal of the man who was in the lead, Joe Balboa. He once believed his battle with Seymour to be a lonely one, an obvious defeat on paper. But he was not alone. Little did Seymour know that his round 2 match was a handicap match. The Final Fantasy vs. Joe Balboa & THE BRONX. All of it. From the crack babies, to the honest grandmothers working as church secretaries. They would be Joe’s legs if he couldn’t run and his blood if it failed to pump. They were the strength that Seymour could never tap into. The run had finally reached its climax, the steps of City Hall. At the top, the marble statue of Rocky Marciano, the famous boxer that the character Rocky Balboa was inspired by. His lungs burned from the strenuous run, but looking back, he could see the supportive faces of all the Bronx behind him. For once, the chaotic neighborhood was in sync as they all sang… "It's the eye of the tiger its the cream of the fight risin up to the challenge of our rivals and the last known survivor stalks his prey in the night and he's watching us all with the eye of the tiger!!! He could feel his heart racing with those words and his feet followed as well. The rows of steps were an easy task to traverse with the Bronx behind him and so would victory. Who cares what Death said. Who gives a damn about his age? It wouldn’t matter when he would achieve victory and show the world that you don’t fuck with the Bronx. And with that, he finally reached the top, the Bronx waiting with baited breath on his touch of the statue. Placing a hand on it, the crowd roared in sync as the Brawler raised a fist in the air, the adrenaline high and his fighting spirit intact. He was the tiger the song spoke of….and he hoped Seymour was watching somewhere. Fear no longer lived in his heart…only the will to survive, The eye of the tiger.
