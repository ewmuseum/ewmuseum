Date: 2004-08-21 All right, then. Team XUW is presently seated in their assigned locker room in whatever arena it is that this here PTC Survival deal is going down. It's a number of days before the actual show and, if the silent darkness that seems to have gripped the rest of the backstage area is any indication, they're the only people here. Well, except for Old Mr. Mopbucket, the ancient janitor hired God Knows how many years ago to mop the floors and whistle songs that have been lost to the past forever to himself. But other then Team XUW, Old Mr. Mopbucket, and the ghosts of the 33rd Fighting Infantry (who died valiantly while trying to protect a Parisian hooker from drunken British troops. 'Hold on, Sophie.' One of the 33rd had said, 'We'll not let those rotten tooth bastards soil yo--' He never got to finish that sentence, having been run down senselessly by a Lexus full of drunk teenagers on their way to a Box Spring Social) the arena was really quite empty. So, just why? was Team XUW sitting around in a locker room in an empty arena? For lack anything better to do, really.
