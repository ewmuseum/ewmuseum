Spanish Sunrise

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 Serpenti who was dressed in loose fitting cotton workout pants and a towel across his neck, and his ever present mask; stood on the balcony of his home that was located on the coast of Spain; A short distance from La Coruna, Spain. It had been a long time since he graced the home of his childhood and the home his parents left him. The thoughts of his parents brought a deep sigh from Serpenti who stood there soaking the fading dusk. He had arrived late in the night, much to the surprise of the house staff. It had only been a few days since he face his GTT4 first round opponent, Dusk. Just as the Dawn ends the Dusk.. So did He. Many thoughts raced though his head about that match. The match was just not to Serpenti’s liking. For some reason Dusk just did not seam to be in the match. Serpenti has scouted and seen tapes of his work. Dusk was better then that. Sure got the win, but not in a fashion he liked to win his first round match. Serpenti sighed and took a drink of his morning juice. Serpenti laughed a little. Maybe the match was not as bad as he thought. Serpenti smiled again thinking about the good accomplishments. Although personally not happy with the match, everyone had congratulated him on advancing and his match; many even said he was a favorite in round two. Everyone was talking about him now. He caused people to look at him and see a gifted fighter. Serpenti looked out at the stars and moon that shown in the clear night sky, the moon reflecting off the surface of the water. The air; cool and crisp as a slight breeze blew in off the North Atlantic Ocean. Fall waged its annual battle against summer, causing the weather to be extremely hot in the day and very cool at night. Serpenti looked at his niece golden Rolex, It was almost time for sunrise, time for his every day morning ritual. Serpenti sipped on his juice, and waited for the sun to rise in anticipation. Anxiously Serpenti ran his hand though his still wet hair from his shower not to long ago. Slowly the sun started to rise… As it did Serpenti stretched his arms out to soak in the early morning sunrays. The sun warmed his cool skin as he felt the Spanish sun rise since he was thirteen years old. Slowly he opened his eyes to watch the magnificent sight before him. The sun made the blue water sparkle and shine. In the distance a pair of dolphins leaped and arched before splashing back in. “Picture perfect.” Serpenti thought. Serpenti drank the rest of his juice and walked back into the house. The white plaster walls were adorned with a few things. Pictures of lands far away, a few swords hung here and there, an accessional shield, a few paintings of family members on his father’s side of the family. Over the large fireplace was an enormous hung a painted portrait of his farther and his mother. They looked about the same age as he was now. The happiness on there faces apparent. Serpenti lowered his head in a silent prayer to his parents wherever they may be, after the prayer he walked down a hall and opened a wooden door. Everything was still in great condition and the house staff kept it that way. Serpenti walked into the room to a large table. The table contained three pieces of a map made on leather, a folder with the Global Logo on the cover, a bail of clear fishing line and a pincushion with a few pins in it. Serpenti looked over at the map, tying to figure out the location of the Golden monkey. He shook his head. With out the last part it was useless. Who ever tore the map up made sure you would need all three pieces to make sense. Serpenti mussed at the words leading to the last part given to him by King Willy. “Seek the one who would be king, but is denied. Seek the wielder of the king of swords. For it is not in this world that the last piece lays waiting and resting.” King Willy had told him. “Cryptic indeed.” Serpenti thought “Strange how fate plays its hand.” Serpenti lifted up the folder and opened it up. “Has to be him, No other logical option in the entire world, only one king of swords existed. Excalibur; its wielder, Current Global Television champion Vennemen Pendragon.” Serpenti thought. A knock came at the door and an old Spanish man stood at the door. “Señor, Usted tiene correo.” The old man says, and pulls out two letters. “Gracias Juvian.” Serpenti says as he takes envelopes, three in all. The Global Logo on one, The PTC – GTT4 logo on the next, and the last bared a strange design depicting a red dragon on a shield. ”Pendragon.” The name rang out in Serpenti’s mind. “At last response from Lord Vennemen Pendragon.” Serpenti opened the letter and pulled out sheet of paper written in an Old English font. Master, Serpenti. Your formal request to have audience with Lord Pendragon has been accepted. Lord Pendragon shall convene with you at Castle Pendragon in two days. Be the not late or ye audience with thy lord Pendragon will surely come to pass with out fulfillment. Ribald Pendragon Realm Scribe. ”Well, it is something, Word in the Global locker room was that Pendragon was a respectable and honorable man.” Serpenti thought to his self as he placed the letter back in the envelope and placed it on the table next to the map. Serpenti slowly opened the letter from Argyle; the GTT4 Tournament Director; while wondering what Argyle would need to send him a special letter. Serpenti knew he had advanced past Dusk, and was already informed that he was facing John Hitching in round two. “What could this be about?” Serpenti wondered pulling the letter out. Señor Serpenti, I am sending you this letter to inform you that John Hitchin of NGW has dropped out of this tournament, do to medical issues. To keep you from having a bye into round three, I have awarded Fnord of 4WW his spot. Good Luck in your match. Sincerely, Argyle Von Smackdown PTC Owner and GTT4 Tournament Director. Serpenti blinked a few times and re read the letter from Argyle. And nodded and slowly replaced the letter into the envelope and set it down next to the one from Pendragon’s scribe. “Fnord Huh?” Serpenti mused. Serpenti had heard talk in the locker room of Fnord at the last event. Fnord was a large and impressive fighter, who was known for his brutality in matches. “This is going to be a true test of skill. If I can get past Fnord, I Just might have a chance in this tournament. It will not be easy though.” Serpenti thought to his self dryly. Serpenti looked at the Envelope with the Global Logo on the front. Thinking of Global caused a whirlwind of emotions to flood though his mind. Kitty…. He was falling hard for the vixen known as Kitty Mac, he knew it, but could not help it. Looking into her eyes turned him into jelly, and made him fly though the clouds. The Vergomovz…. Serpenti’s face, hardened under the mask, and his eyes changed from a dreamy look to a look of a man ready to kill. There was nothing but brutal and unadulterated rage for those two men. They had hurt him, hurt Kitty and hurt a new ally and friend in Adam Myers. Myers… Serpenti lowered his head in Sadness, for his fallen friend. The doctor’s did not know how long he would take to heal. Do to Adam’s medical condition, his lack of immune system his body took longer to heal then normal. Serpenti sighed and opened the letter… Serpenti! Bubba here. Serpenti My boy! You have been Placed in the World Cup Global Elimination Match for Maximum Carnage. You will be Figihiting NINE other Global Stars. YOU asked for a chance to Shine… Well SON. This is it! Dont Let Me Down! Sadly There is only 1 Vergo in the Match, Zergo. Arefieve is in another important Match, And I am baring you from interfearing in that Match. If you do, I will have NO choice. I will PULL you out of the World Cup Match! Do I MAKE MY SELF CLEAR ON THAT? …. GOOD! See you in Chicago! GOOD luck against Fjord in the GTT as well. Bubba McCoy GLOBAL OWNER! “Man. Bubba really needs a secretary to do this for him. Arefiev will come in time. But now I have a chance to beet Zergolov up again. Winning. Not important. I want Zergolov.” Serpenti thought to his self-tossing the letter down. Serpenti slammed his fist into the table causing the stuff on tip to move with the vibration. “What has you in such a foul mood on such a wonderful day?” A familiar female voice rang out from behind Serpenti. Serpenti spun and everything seamed so peaceful. The Raven-haired beauty that had stolen his heart stood in the door way, her suitcase sitting on the floor next to her... Se stood there dressed in a tight pair of ripped blue jeans with her fishnet stocking showing in the large holes in the jeans. The pink top of her thong popped over the low cut waist of the paint, a white cami hugged her chest tightly almost too small but not quite. Serpenti melted into her emerald green eyes. Strange he thought. How one person could just make everything better on sight. “What? Cat got your tongue?” Kitty said with a mischievous smile.. “No! I… I... I just was not expecting this chica.” Serpenti sputtered the words out clumsily. “Well! If I am not wanted, I will go!” Kitty said her smile turning into a frown. “No.. I… I am Happy you have come mi cristal se levantó.” Serpenti said flashing a smile, causing Kitty to smile her self. The two crossed the room and embraced in a embrace of lovers, and kissed each other passionately. Regretfully Serpenti pulled back and looked into the face of his love. “What made you come here mi amor?” Serpenti asked. Kitty shoved off Serpenti and looked at him saddened and turned away “Do you really have to ask that?” Kitty asked obviously upset. Serpenti walked behind Kitty and wrapped his arms around her. He breathed the sent of her hair. Of her perfume, and was lost. “Forgive me, Mi amor. I should not have asked that, why does not matter, all that matters; is that you are here.” Serpenti said softly into Kitty Mac’s ear. The soft words and breath of Serpenti cause Kitty to shudder and she hugged his rooms and leaned against him. “Well, if you really want to know, it was your letter.” Kitty said closing her eyes. However Serpenti’s eyes went wide… wide in fear. “How could I resist?” Kitty asked. “Ahh yes… The letter. Apesadumbrado mi amor.. In the rush of the past week, I forgot I mailed it. Serpenti said trying to sound convincing. “Perdóneme por favor? Please forgive me?” Serpenti asked. Lying to Kitty hurt him. But until he was sure of things no need to cause he any un-needed worrying. Kitty spun around and looked in to his eyes seeing his sadness, not knowing the truth behind the sadness she tilted her head sideways. “All is forgiven. Now! How about showing me around!” Kitty said with a flourish of her hands and spinning around. Serpenti sighed and laughed. “But of course. Su deseo es mi comando, mi amor.” Serpenti said with a bow. “ Let me put some things away and I’ll be right with you.” Kitty tapped her foot as Serpenti looked around and quickly rolled up the map and placed it and Pendragon’s letter into a safe and locked it. “Okay. Ready, let’s go.” Serpenti said ushering Kitty out the door. Serpenti looked around once more. Trying to see invisible eyes, eyes that were not there. Serpenti shook his head and closed the door. The woman known as La Máscara Carmesí sat sideways on a black iron chair with crimson velvet cushions. Dressed in a red and black leather body suit, with a deep plunging neckline exposing most of her ample cleavage with out any inhibition. A small crimson mask covered only her eyes showing off most of her beautiful face her lips painted in a deep yet glossy red. She twirled the handle of a long black bullwhip in her hand. In front of her, a man in solid black clothing; a crimson red flame adorned the sleeve of his long sleeved button up shirt, kneeled on one knee head bowed and one hand on his knee and his other in a fist on the ground. This was how all of La Máscara Carmesí’s pirates kneeled when in her quarters. “So the letters have been delivered?” La Máscara Carmesí asked in a demanding tone. “Yes my captain. I have delivered the letters as you asked, and waited to make sure they reached their destinations.” The man said with out moving a muscle. “Good. You may rise up then Sanchez. You have pleased your captain for now.“ La Máscara Carmesí said with a alluring tone in her voice. The man known as Sanchez stood up off his knee. Sanchez looked at his captain, and smiled. He knew the reward for pleasing his captain very well. Never once had he failed. Failed and died like many others. La Máscara Carmesí climbed out of her throne like chair and walked over to the window of her captain’s quarters. She looked past the horizon off in the distance. “Enjoy your Kitty, my Serpenti. Enjoy her while she last. Soon you will suffer and die.” La Máscara Carmesí said out loud and lifted her head back and laughed, a laugh that belonged to the insane. Her plan was slowly coming together. Serpenti would lead her to the golden Monkey, and She would have the honor and privilege of killing not only the fames El Serpenti De La Muerte. But she would also lay claim as the woman who was able to destroy and kill the one known as Vennemen Pendragon in one fell swoop of her hand. He name would become infamous and remembered forever. La Máscara Carmesí smiled and looked over to the large man known as Sanchez. She motioned him closer with her finger. Lust filling her eyes. “Come Sanchez. You truly have pleased your Captain. For this you deserve rewarding. Plus I am in an un-natural good mood” La Máscara Carmesí said lying down on the large bed. Sanchez said nothing, only complied. To resist was death, but who would resist such a voracious woman like La Máscara Carmesí. Sanchez shook his head slightly as he climbed onto the bed and lowered his mouth to the exposed neck of La Máscara Carmesí. Resisting the furthest thing on his mind.
