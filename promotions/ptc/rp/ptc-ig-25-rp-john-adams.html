Ig 25 Rp

8 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The dream sequence opens up to a brightly lit room with white walls all around...very clean except for the slight dirt stain near the bottom bridge that connected the floor and the wall. The emptiness and sorrow can be felt inside; the despair felt like knifes slicing away the very humanity of the human soul. The lowliness is not for long as we see a young boy enter into the room crying his eyes out in the right hand corner. The walls of the room suddenly start to crack and split open with each booming and piercing cry from the young boy, as fresh maggots are seen coming out of the walls. A tall middle aged business man with brown hair and piercing red eyes enters slowly after that. As he enters the maggots on the wall start to fill in the room more heavily. He takes off his belt as he begins to speak. “I thought I told you to clean your room! And look at you, you crying pathetic bastard! Get up! Get up now or I'm going to beat the pansy out of you!!! I said GET UP!!” As the scared little boy gets up to look at what is revealed to be his father, he knows it is already too late. The thundering crackle can be heard as the belt is used to teach the boy a lesson, a lesson that he would learn over and over again. The frightened boy try's to run away, but fails each time as he is met with the cruel instrument of torture. The walls are starting to buckle in as the beating is getting worse. Blood from the little boy splatter amongst the split and cracked walls as the scene melts away from the reality that is witnessed…an outside garden is what is seen. Brightly lit sun shining with all of its rays, the sweet aroma of the roses attracts all sorts of buzzing bees that decide to stay and collect there daily dose of pollen. Innocence is in the air as the boy is working in the garden with his mother....It's a bountiful day we see as the two are picking tomatoes out of the garden. The Mother, a picture of perfection is humming a lovely tune "Irish lullaby". She had golden hair that was not matched unless next to the sun. As she continues her hum of comfort, the little boy starts to cry. “What’s wrong John?” With the little boy now revealed as John Adams, he turns to his mother with so much hurt and pain in his eyes…his mother could see all the way to John’s soul and could tell he was in pain…so a Mother’s love could heal anything but could it heal this? She decided to give her son a huge comforting hug. “Mother, why does daddy hate me so much? Why does he punish me all of the time? Why?” With the words of wonder and sorrow spoken his Mother’s face of perfection is no more as it starts to melt off her head and onto the ground which they were working on. A loud thunderous and booming gun shot is heard as we see the perfectly shaped bone of her skull revealed. After the horrible and gruesome sight the scene once again starts to melt away from the reality that is shown, to reveal a dark hallway with a light at the other end of it, the walls that surrounded them were damp and built from a very old stone. Down the very dark hallway it begins to illuminate all around revealing doors at each side of the hallway. Each door that is passed reveals a window inside, showing men and women in coats of containment from the very maddening sickness that plagued them. Each one of them is staring in the great lonely emptiness of there life. Salivating is the only pass time they now have left in the world, then deeper into the hallway there is a heavily boarded up steel door with no window. Concentrated on the door there is noises of the most unfortunate souls as they start to holler a song of anguish and despair. A gargantuan sized roar can be heard from the other side of the door. Fear is felt as the door buckles inward from the raging beast on the other side. Looking down we see the maggots and various other bugs scurrying all over, prophesizing the creatures forthcoming. Looking back at the door we see splinters of wood and parts of the steel coming off as the beast continues his attempt to come through. Trying to run away is useless as the walls start to crack and cave in. The song of the damned gets louder as the door finally flies off the hinges to reveal................. John Adams wakes up in his hotel room covered by the blanket of sweat and blood…a dream…it was only a dream and then nothing more. But what can be expected of this dream? One can only hope for the best…even when they have no hope.
