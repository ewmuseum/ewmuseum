Pain to come

8 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-08 It had been three weeks since Salaok had paid the visit to his father which would change the way things were done in his family and The Lair forever. Chris D. had been the last remaining member of the older generation that haunted the Lair. Chris D. however had finally been taken care of and there wasn’t a person happier than the man in whom killed him. His son. Sitting back in his office at the casino, Salaok over looks the flocks of people as they sit in front of tables hoping and praying with their credit cards that something will finally go their way. It never would though. His long black hair falling over his face as he leans down and picks his packet of cigarettes out of the bottom draw of his desk, blanketing his face. It was a bad habit, but a habit none-the-less that keeps his hands from being idle. Idle hands do the devils work. Within a few seconds the cigarette is lit and the TV remote has found his hand. Life comes to the large flat screen TV which is mounted on the wall and despite what he was hoping to see, the first channel that comes on features nothing more than the events of the PTC’s GTT4 tournament which Salaok had foolishly entered. Wrestling, or fighting at least had been a big part of his life for as long as he could remember, so when this arose again, he couldn’t pass it up. He had sat by for the past three tournaments and done nothing but watch. This time would be different. No longer contracted to any federation, Salaok was a free agent and when the invite had landed on his doorstep it was hard for him to say no. Forever, or at least as long as he could remember, his life had always found comfort in the land on pain. The World of Hurt in which he found inside that ring, or simply in the street. He didn’t need a wrestling ring to stop him from having a brawl as bars and nightclubs had often given him a place to show off what he could do. But he was getting older now. No longer was it the place for him to hang out at a nightclub, hoping that someone would bump into him and look at him the wrong way. Plus with all the legal costs involved when found guilty of causing Intentional Bodily Harm to another human being, it was far easier to simply allow himself to compete in things such as this. But wrestling…fighting wasn’t his life and he refused to let it dominate it. Salaok was and has always been a business man and with casino’s and restaurants to run, there was very little time to concentrate on getting into shape to get into the ring and fight some worthless no-body from some federation that basically meant shit all to him. But alas, with it being broadcast on TV so much, Salaok was finding it hard not to pay it the attention that he otherwise wouldn’t have. His opponent for this round would prove to be somewhat more of a competition than the last two rounds, however it didn’t matter. Win or lose, Salaok would still be able to enter that ring and cause as much pain as he possibly could, and better yet, would be able to take as much pain as he wanted. And that was the major factor in things… It was all about what he wanted. There wasn’t a man who has been able to dominate Salaok since he began this tournament in the world of wrestling and this David whoever sure as hell wasn’t going to be any different. If Salaok didn’t want to win this thing, then quite simply, he wouldn’t. Not going to lay down on the job of course, but would simply decide that he can’t be bothered anymore and simply not give it his all. All this no-body to inflect as much pain as he was willing to take and then it would all be over. Right? Wrong. Even after this match is over and done with, there will still be that need. The need for pain. Whether it be giving it or getting it, there was something about Pain that gave Salaok comfort. Salaok attention however wouldn’t be allowed to stay on the television set for long as a group of his security guards suddenly began to run through the casino below and knowing of what will be coming up next, Salaok stood up and watched as they found their target. A target for pain. Sure, in some part of the world security guards have to comply with regulations and so on, but when the man who runs the casino has more money than the entire town does, it is soon easily forgotten that rules do actually exist. Who would this fool be and what would become of him? The grin began to stretch across Salaok’s already distorted face as they pounced and fists began to fly. It doesn’t take long for a mob of oversized men to over power some desperate money hungry moron who hasn’t eaten as fear of leaving his table is simply too great. The fear that while he is away his numbers will finally come up. They didn’t. And soon Salaok would be the proud owner of whatever this man had. It’s a cruel world, but one that we all enjoy being apart of so much.
