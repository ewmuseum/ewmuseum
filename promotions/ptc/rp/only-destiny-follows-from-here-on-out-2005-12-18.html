Only Destiny Follows From Here On Out.

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 "Man, remember, until an hour before the Devil fell, God thought him beautiful in Heaven." –The Crucible by Arthur Miller The wind howled through the night, the leaves picked up by the wind bristled softly against the grass. The residence of the Jackson Family estate was dimly lit as Aiden Jackson, a man of decent stature, sat in the study with a man known as Reverend Tobias. Reverend Tobias was a shy man, although when he grew comfortable around you, he would open up more. Reverend Tobias was not only a shy man, he was a deceitful man. What is this you say? A Reverend? Tobias was never truly a pure man, he has plotted murders, escaped convicts, and yet he shares his knowledge of God. How do I know all of this? I’m the man he helped plot a murder for, I’m the man he helped escape from prison, and I am the man he shared his knowledge of Christ with. I was drawn closely to the conversation with the Reverend, it was apparent that I held a respect for the man. While, I respected the man, I didn’t trust him one bit. I murdered a man nearly five years ago. Although, Reverend Tobias helped me out of the situation, he drove me to the insanity that I live to this day. "Have you been studying for your exam?" Reverend Tobias questioned me, as he took another sip from the coffee sitting in front of him on the large wooden desk. "Yes," I said to him softly as I rotated my chair back and forth, hands behind my head in a relaxing position. "You’ve completed the full reading of the bible?" He questioned me once more. "No, I’m in John," I replied. "Ahh, Yes, how far along are you? I clearly remember a great part of that book, one of my favorite parts of the bible in fact. It’s John 1:51…" "And he says to him, Verily, verily, I say to you, Henceforth ye shall see the heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of man," We said together in unison. "You shall do well, my son," Reverend Tobias noted to me with a smile across his face. I hesitated then smiled back at him "I hope so." I mouthed to him; no sound came from my mouth though. "Aiden, I must go. You will do great things in the world that you are coming into. I expect to see you at church tomorrow. I must go prepare the wine and bread for tomorrow," He smiled as he closed the door behind him. For a moment, I sat there thinking about the words exchanged. What is with that man? He creates the evil of the world yet he can seem so righteous and pure. I pulled open the drawer in front of me, searching between the pens and stapler; I pulled the small box from the desk and sat it in front of me. I grabbed a cigar from the small box, lit it and watched the smoke disperse from my mouth. I wasn’t sure exactly why I extracted the cigar from the box or even smoked it; usually the cigars were there for important visitors. I truly wasn’t much of a smoker myself. I slowly rose from the comfortable red chair, walking from the large office I traveled straight up a large stairwell into my bedroom. The room was dimly lit by a small lamp in the corner of the room on a black metal end-table. I opened the closet, there sat at least twenty black suits; I pulled from the hanger one of the suits and hung it on the door handle of the bathroom. I then sat on my bed and pulled the bible from the end-table’s drawer. Pulling the cigar from my mouth, I blew the smoke out once more, and then threw the cigar into the trashcan. "1On the third day there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there; 2and both Jesus and His disciples were invited to the wedding. 3When the wine ran out, the mother of Jesus said to Him, "They have no wine." 4And Jesus said to her, "Woman, what does that have to do with us? My hour has not yet come." 5His mother said to the servants, "Whatever He says to you, do it." 6Now there were six stone water pots set there for the Jewish custom of purification, containing twenty or thirty gallons each. 7Jesus said to them, "Fill the water pots with water." So they filled them up to the brim. 8And He said to them, "Draw some out now and take it to the headwaiter." So they took it to him…" I read to myself softly. My eyes grew heavy, I lay on the bed drifting, and my eyes snapped back open as they tried to close. I stared at the sheet but everything blurred. I glanced over at the clock "1:32 A.M." I need to go to sleep if I ever want to get up tomorrow, I thought to myself. My eyes fluttered shut once more, they snapped open, I finally gave into the darkness that over came me. BEEP…BEEP… BEEP I snapped into an upright position on my bed, frightened by the alarm clock. Regaining my composure I pushed the button on top of it to make the beeping stop. I looked down at myself, still dressed in the same attire from the night before, the bible sitting there on my lap. I hopped up from the bed, grabbed the suit from the bathroom door handle and began to put it on. After I was prepared, I walked down the stairs greeted Mabry, who was my butler, and I was off on the expedition to church. Nearly ten minutes later, I arrived from my Saint Louis home to the large church. The lovely pane glass windows reflected the light inside in a seemingly beautiful manner. I greeted the tons of smiling faces with a hand shake, a hug, or a smile. These are all of the people who were here for me when my parents died. They are the closest thing to family that I have, I hold on tightly to them. Everyone piled into their seats, I sat alone in a pew, and I picked up the Bible and listened to the sermon conducted by Reverend Tobias. I gazed around looking into the faces of everyone in the church; they sat with smiles across their faces, eating up the words that Reverend Tobias spoke. It is sad that they didn’t know the true Reverend Tobias, I lived a lie, not telling my "family" of the lies and treachery this man lived. "Children of God, I now invite you to take part in the Holy Communion of Christ," His voice resonating through the church. Each row rose, one by one walking up to Reverend Tobias’ altar, kneeling taking the bread and wine and being blessed by him. I walked up to the altar; Reverend Tobias spoke then allowed the group of eight to kneel on the padded altar. As we prayed he walked to me, being the furthest on the left. He picked a piece of bread from the tray… "Take and eat, for this is the body of Christ." He smiled at me softly. He then removed one of the small glasses of wine from the tray and handed it to me, I drank from it and he spoke once more… "Take and drink, for this is the blood that Christ shed for you to be here today." I set the small shot glass which contained the wine from before, down and I walked slowly back to the pew I had been in before. I sat there for a moment, things began to blur, and I lost attentiveness. I looked around me at the other people, their faces still glowing with the smiles from before. My body began to convulse slowly at first but then growing rapid and violent. My body shaking, the scene around me, burning in and out of my mind. My body shaking, I hit the floor. The violent shaking continued, the crowd poured in around me, everything ran in slow motion. The words from people’s mouths were not truly words, but only me reading their lips, I felt my body shaking once more. Shaking…Shaking…Shaking… "Aiden," I saw Reverend Tobias mouth this over my convulsing body. I tried to talk; the words from my mouth never came. I was silenced by groans and grunts, I truly wanted to scream "You bastard you poisoned me!", although I wasn’t sure why he did it nor was I sure that he actually did it. Or Was I? My thoughts jumbled quickly into believing that Tobias was truly innocent. The people in a joint effort brought me to my feet and sat me onto the pew. I tried to talk once more…only a grunt could be forced out. "Aiden, my son." The booming voice echoed in my head. "Who…who are you?" I said to the voice. "Your creator, Aiden." "God?" I questioned, at this point I was so oblivious to what was going on that I wasn’t sure what to believe. At this point, I had come to realize that I had gained back mobility of my body, the ability to speak, and the voices around me grew apparent. A wave crashed over me as this voice from above held precedence over the other voices. I heard an outcry of "Aiden are you okay."’s. "For a moment Aiden, Ignore them. I would like a word with you." The voice roared and everything fell to silence around me once more. "That’s better, Aiden, the path that you have led in life is a righteous one. The life that you are leading has been sidetracked by the demonology of the man known as Reverend Tobias. You must avoid this man or he will bring you down to the pits of hell, you are a pure man Aiden do not fall into temptation." At this point the voice was filled with truth. "I understand." I confirmed to him. The truth was in my head, I was dead to what was being said, my mind was un-absorbing of the words going into my head. I wanted to escape this nightmare that had all progressed around me. A snap back to reality, the voices heard for the second time, the voice boomed one last time. "Keep it in your mind Aiden, Do not fall unto evil." Suddenly the room around me began closing in and I faded to darkness. A shiver ran through my spine, the cold set in around my body. The darkness still there, I began rubbing my face, poking myself in the eye. I reached around searching for a light or something of sort. The least I found out was that my eyes were open and this wasn’t a dream. "Ouch," I said loudly to the darkness and myself after the poke to the eye. Suddenly an influx of light left me rubbing my eyes and attempting to open them after nearly two minutes. The tall, lanky Reverend appeared before my eyes standing half way down the stairs. The contents of the room finally became apparent to me; it was the basement of a house, the room decorated finely and a bed in the corner of the room. "I would have thought that you might have found your way to the bed. Would you like some coffee?" He said with an innocent smile running across his face. "No, I need to get out of here." I rubbed my shoulders in a desperate, yet unsuccessful attempt to gain heat. "Aiden, Please stay for a while. Have a cup of coffee with me," he spoke with a convincing nature. I remembered the voice; I remember what he told me. I trusted and confided in the lord always but I was unsure of if the lord had really spoken to me. I convinced myself that Reverend Tobias may have the answers if I keep my intentions of kicking his ass unapparent. "Alright, fair deal," I said as I faked a smile. I walked up the slightly narrow staircase straight into the kitchen; he poured a cup of coffee into a glass. I was weary about drinking the drink, as I already believed he was the one who caused…whatever it was that happened to me. I took the glass from his hand and sat at the table with him. "Sugar or cream?" He said politely. "No." "What did you do to me?" I muttered. "Aiden, What are you talking about? I can’t explain your actions at Church; you began speaking in a different tongue and everything. You now have people at that church believing you’ve been possessed by some demon," He complained. "I can’t explain what happened and I’m trying to get the answers can’t you see that? I know what you’ve done," I had the coffee glass reared in my hand; I was ready to throw the hot coffee class at him. "Aiden, it was something to calm you down. It caused adverse side effects that I wasn’t aware of okay?" His voice grew colder, a more chilling fashion came from his mouth, and Reverend Tobias was a dark man indeed. "If you say so." I replied. But in all actuality, I was thinking to myself. What are you saying Aiden!? You can’t honestly believe the bullshit that is spouting from his mouth. Are you actually going to trust this man after an obvious higher being came to you and warned you of his evils? Yes. I thought to myself. "Now Aiden, now that we’ve settled this dispute. You need to lie back and take things slow for now. Let things around the church die down before we have you attempt a job at preaching a sermon. That is of course, the next step in your job," He said to me with that mischievous grin across his face. "Alright, I understand," I jeered back with a smile. By this point, I had relaxed, lulled myself into a false sense of comfort and I had set the coffee on the table. Internally, I felt unstable as though I were rearing to puke everywhere. The thought of trusting this man sickened me, but my mind told me to, the shit it was feeding me sounded so perfect. "I will see you later," I said as I got up from the large wooden table. "We’re fine, correct Aiden?" "Yes." He saw me to the door, and I walked out of the large wooden door, just as wooden as his table. I was unsure of how exactly I got to his house, but there sat my car. Confused, I hopped into the car and drove away, in search of the Jackson Estate. Music pumping through the speakers was ignored by my mind as the thought of Reverend Tobias ran through my head. I couldn’t get him out of it no matter how hard I tried…Reverend Tobias stuck in my head, he was a man to trust, he was a man of honor, he was a man of truth…Trust him. You’re truly not okay, I thought to myself as the constant reminder of the "hallucination" I had was there. Zoning from reality, warmth came over my body, everything around me slowed once again. Not again…The convulsions never came, only silence, I turned the speakers up, no sound came. "Aiden, I told you not to trust him," The booming voice arose once again. "I… I…Lord have mercy on my soul…" "Do not speak Aiden, you only thought I as a figment of your imagination. I am not Aiden; you must realize this here and now. Aiden, Reverend Tobias will come to you with three temptations, YOU WILL DENY HIM. Aiden to follow the right road and reveal this man’s evil you will have to follow my instructions. Aiden he used a mind altering agent on you that has created a mentality for you to reside in him and trust him. He will drive you to your grave in the end Aiden." "Lord…Please forgive me." "Aiden, you know what you have been given from me when Christ’s hands were hammered in such a gruesome nature to that cross. When he was stabbed in the ribs by a spear. Aiden, you know why Jesus Christ died for you. Aiden, do your deeds for me and Jesus Christ sitting by my side. Conquer your fear, and conquer the evil one, conquer the fate that you will hold under this man’s guide." The truth said to me. Reality snapping in once more… "Wow, that has to stop," I said to myself slightly shaken up by the event that had just occurred. My mind was racing faster than before; I wasn’t sure who to trust now. The louder voice sounded as though it were more convincing and true though. Deny the temptation that will be brought before me from the demon of hell. The louder voice had gained control over me; the voice of God, the voice of enlightenment had brought just that to me. Although at this moment it seems apparent that I’ve forgotten of the one known as Alec Lancer, let it be known that he is in the back of my mind in spite of all of the chaos and confusion. Alec Lancer is a symbol to me, he represents power and greed, he represents the things that the Reverend wants most. This tournament is a lifeline to him; he is striving off of GTT5. The story has brought deeper ties than just some tournament, this has brought a story that you WANT to read, and it has become an addiction, something that you have to have…so go head keep on asking me for more. ~ This is my fate and the downfall of many, those who are in my path of destiny ~ Above is something said all too often, every wrestler in their career says it at least once as some sort of intimidation factor but I’ll be sure to fulfill the desire of the fans. The guarantees of victory aren’t certain but the fact of the matter is I’ve left you guessing and yearning for more, and that was my sole purpose. "To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven: A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pluck up that which is planted." (Ecclesiastes 3:1-8) The seed of Aiden Jackson has been planted. But I trusted in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God. "(Psalms 31:14) Credits: The Crucible by Aurthur Miller, King James Bible.
