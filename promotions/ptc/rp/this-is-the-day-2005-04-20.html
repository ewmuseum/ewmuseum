This Is The Day

20 April 2005

Date: 2005-04-20 -(The sickening sun burnt a hole in Drake's mind. He was stuck on that thought, stuck there as he sat on that park bench. It shined off of his black trench coat, his hat pulled over his eyes. The park was green and beautiful, and there Drake was, sitting there like a storm cloud in a beautiful blue sky.)- -(Drake won his match against Jack Morgan. He put down the opponent, dismantled the critics, and overall it was a successful mission. Things were not right, though, as expected. Drake was now in the spotlight, a man who could win a match. People recognized Drake, and it was always the same…"Hey, nice job, bud!"…"The freak wins one!"…"Wooo, Drake!"…despicable. The shadows were Drake's best friend at times, and even though he won, he embraced the anonymity.)- -(Last match, though, was currently out of Drake's mind. Drake needed out of this damn sun, away from the people. The bandages were starting to heat up, but that didn't really matter. Ever since the injury, Drake has lost feeling in his first black layer of skin. The only problem was further burns, something Drake didn't need. He got up and started making a quick pace out of the park. The exit seemed miles away, with people staring as Drake rushed past them. Freedom was close.)- -(Today, though, seemed to not be in Drake's favor. At the exit of the park, right before the street and the dark alley way Drake desired to be in, was a carnival. Not just a regular carnival either. This had all the clowns, animals, and little kids you could ever imagine. Drake stopped dead in his tracks, his eyes bulging. He gulped the air in his mouth and shook his head before proceeding through them.)- -(Pulling up his collar around his face Drake started to squirm through the sea of people. All things were going well until, suddenly, Drake tripped. He fell right on his face, his hat flying off. People helped him up, and Drake's face was revealed.)- "Oh my God, what's wrong with you? What's on your face?" "It's nothing! Let me go! Please!" Drake was trying to make his way through, but the people circled around him. "Look at him! What a freak!" "What happened to you, King Tut? Hahahah!" "Oh my God! This is the funniest thing ever!" -(They kept on saying things as Drake was standing there, looking around and spinning from side to side. He didn't know where to go; he was stuck. Quickly, he saw which way the exit to the park was. Without hesitation Drake sprinted towards it, running through people. On the sidewalk, Drake made a mad dash through traffic. Everything was fine until…)- *HONKKKKKK, SMASH!* -(Up and over Drake went, landing hard on his back on the pavement. A short gasp came out of Drake's mouth, but he managed to pull himself back to his feet. The people all stared, some laughing and some asking if he needed help, but Drake didn't make notice. He dashed into the alley way, making his way through the familiar darkness he grew to love.)- -------------------------------------------------------------- Through all my years of being like this, I've seen it all. I've seen the proud, the disgusted, the angry, the pity, everything. Everybody has a different reaction because everybody does not know how to react to the unknown, so they go with their instincts. Even though I was in the alley now, my mind was stuck in that crowd, in the middle of it all, surrounded by the type of things that could make a man go crazy. From child to adult, they were laughing at me, tormenting me. It didn't matter that I had just won as an underdog in a match at the PTC CW tournament in my first round…I was still a freak, still something they could pay $5 to see at the circus. I leaned against the wall of the alleyway and sat there, growing nauseous. Without warning, I began to hurl, the blood flowing out of my mouth mixed with the eggs I had that morning. Now, the pain kicked in. It pulled me down into a ball, my arms around my legs as I sat against the alley building. I could still the impact from my body flying up and smashing into the ground…what was I thinking? Had I lost that much control? Something was wrong, that was for certain. The tournament was changing me. Everything about me now feels different. Usually, I would just walk right through a group like that, just shrug it off like it was nothing. But after my win, I felt vulnerable. I felt like people now knew who I was, that I couldn't hide behind the mask of mystery. I was now out there for everyone to see, for everyone to notice and say "Hey, that's Drake Coleman, I saw him do this or that," or "Hey, Drake Coleman was HORRIBLE at blah blah blah." Today, I didn't know how to react to the people staring at me. What was I going to do? Just stand there and die like a man while they joined hands? Or risk a potential battering as I trudged on through them? My mind was racing, my body was sore from being hit by a car, and overall I was confused. I needed to go for a walk, to stay in here until night hits and just stay in the shadows. I started to slowly make my way through the alley. All over the place was trash, its stench staining the air with an off-color green. I tried not to think about it, which was easy enough considering what was happening. I just kept on going. As I turned down one way, I started hearing screaming. At first, I thought nothing of it. As it drew closer, though, I could see the little girl in the distance with a man right on her. My pace quickened and my blood started to boil, if this man was doing what I thought he was doing. The two became more visible, and now, I was angry. No, that word is too tame; I was furious. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] I broke into a full sprint towards them, and before the man could make a reaction, he received a knee to the side of the head and was knocked off her. I shot a glance at the girl. "Get up and go, NOW!" She heeded my advice and mouthed the words "thank you," as she got up and scampered the other way. The man watched her over my shoulder and started to walk towards me, but I put my hand out. "Stop," I said, "You will not take another step towards her." Suddenly, a burst of laughter. "HAHAHAH! Shit, son, you serious?" The man was disgusting, his only tooth on the top yellow to match his long, greasy blond hair. He had a big belly that stuck out under his black sleeveless tee. The legs were fat too, and the tight, stained black jeans did them no justice. His arms, though, were big and strong. One hit with those would hurt. I was now in my soldier state of mind. In seconds, I was making my analysis on how to go about dismantling and defeating this man. He was big and strong, but not fast. I figured I'd let him through the first punch and make my move a counter so I could see how he fought. I stayed quiet after he asked his question, still analyzing him, which made him mad. He grunted and spit off to the side, his saliva thick, brown, and horrible. Quickly, he glanced me a smile as he tightened his belt. "Son, that there was one of 'em big mistakes ya tend to try not to make." From his pants he pulled out a switch blade knife. Something else into the fray. Now, Drake needed to make sure he had the counter attack ready; a first strike by Drake gave the man a good chance to strike back. "No," I said, staring right at him, "I think you're the one who made the mistake." Without hesitation, the man rushed at me and made a stabbing motion towards my stomach. Using the Russian martial arts, I stepped to the side of his arm and push his arm in, using his own knife to stab himself. The man stopped short now, his own knife stuck inside him with my hand pushing it into him. I then pushed the man away, which forced him right onto the ground. Standing over him I grabbed the knife out of his body, and held it in my hand. As I walked away, I heard something… "Please, mister, save me…" That pushed me to the edge. A rapist asking me to help him. A dirty, no-good nothing, asking me to save his ass. I turned around and looked at him, standing right above him. I got close to his ear, and whispered the magic words… "Like hell." I smirked as I got up and walked away. About ten feet away, though, I heard the words that would be the end of him. "Fuck you, freak." Without thinking, my body made me turn around and go over to him. My leg kicked him in the ribs, my mind having no control over me anymore. I was acting on primal urges, kicking him again and again. To finish him off, I got on my knees next to him and start plunging the knife in and out of his torso. Stab. Stab. Stab. Stab. I continued to do it well into his death, the blood splashing up against his face. My eyes were red with fury and my mind filled with anger. After a while, though, I stopped to see what I was doing, and shook my head. What have I become? Even though this human was vile, did I stoop down to his level? Was I now just a beast, like him? I got up and made my way out of the alley way, pulling the trench coat close around me. I looked around and, thankfully, there was a hotel across the street. Just my kind, too, as it was nice and scummy. Right before that door, though, there was that girl. Didn't she run the other way? Why would she be over here? This had been one of those days where your head hurts from the confusion…and now…what the hell? "Hi." She said, forcing a small smile. I could see where the tears had fallen from before. She was small, no older than 14, wearing a cute little dress with flowers and such on it. Her glasses were thin rimmed, and her once-beautiful brown hair was now torn into a mess. Before this happened, I'm sure this girl was beautiful, was amazing. Now? On the inside, she was miserable. She looked at herself like she was nothing, like she was ugly. She reminded me of something. She reminded me…of… Me. I was at a loss of words as I stared at her, slowly pulling down the sides of my trench coat to let her see my face. She didn't wince, she didn't flinch, she didn't make a face of disgust. The only thing on her face was that same forced smile. Bowing my head down, I walked to the other side of her and got on the ground, sitting against the hotel with my legs straight out. Almost immediately, she did the same and sat next to me. "Charlene." She introduced herself without a handshake, without a look towards me…just a name. "Drake." I did the same. We sat there for a few more minutes, the silence more than comfortable as I felt that I had found me in someone else. I was not attracted to her…I'm sure a 60 year old man being attracted to a 14 year old would bring up lots of trouble…but I felt like she was part of me, that she was me. "What were you doing down the alleys?" She asked me that solemnly, looking at me out of the corner of her eye, staring for a moment. It was like my mother, God have mercy on her soul, was sitting next to me. "I could ask you just the same." That part of the conversation was officially over from that response. There was another period of silence, but, now, I felt that I had to ignite conversation. "Follow wrestling?" A simple question that could lead to hundreds of conversations. Maybe she hated it? Maybe she loved it? Shit, maybe she even knew who I was. "No." She wasn't much for words right now, but that was understandable. The poor thing just got raped, so I didn't expect a fine conversation. "Well," I started, "I'm a wrestling, believe it or not. I'm quite capable under all these bandages. In fact, I just won a match in a tournament…" "Congratulations." She said that with a smile as she looked up at me, wiping away one of the tears. "Thanks." I said. "The thing is…its changing me. Earlier today, people were laughing at me, hundreds of them, and I didn't know what to do. Usually, I'd run away immediately…but I froze…" "People were laughing? Why?" This question shocked me the most. It was like she didn't even notice my deformities, like I wasn't even covered with off-white bandages at all. I looked at her right then, and pointed to my face. "Look at me. Look at all this. What isn't there to laugh at? I'm a freak." I looked down at the pavement between my legs, shaking my head. Hearing these words from my mouth hurt the most; it was like I was admitting defeat. "Its not so bad. I think its pretty cool looking." … Never before has someone called the bandages "cool" or "neat" or anything of the sort. I was confused, yet satisfied. Charlene was something else…but all things must past, and I stood up. Tipping my hat towards her, I turned around and walked towards the hotel. But I was stopped. "Drake," She said, "Take this." It was a necklace. Too small for my neck, but the gold pendant read something… "Love Yourself Before Others." I stared at it for a moment, and then looked at the girl, smiling. I didn't say a word, and nor did she. Turning around, I headed into the hotel, never to see Charlene again… -------------------------------------------------------------- -(The lobby was a dark tan, though the stains and dirt all over the walls made it seem like a rainbow of brown. The red carpet was now a dark, dark maroon, looking more like a dirty shade of blood. Except for a few chairs and a receptionist desk, the place was empty. The man behind the receptionist desk was an black man, leaning against his arm as he read something. He was a little overweight, but that was about it.)- -(Drake walked up to the receptionist desk, smiling, leaning against the counter. He tapped the bell, staring at the man. He only received an unwilling pair of eyes, though, staring at him like he was a hinderance.)- "I'd like to take a room for the night." -(The man spun around and grabbed a set of keys. Spinning back around, he slapped them on the counter, looking back at the magazine.)- "Pay me in the morning." -(Drake was ignored again, nothing. It was back to the usual. Drake, though, had better fish to fry than this. For now, this was not a hindrance. For now, this was just the normal thing that happened in Drake's life. Remembering this, Drake proceeded up the stairs to his room, the camera fading to black.)- -----([([([END FEED])])])----- "This is the day, your life will surely change, This is the day, when things fall into place." --The The
