Extreme Division Match

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everyone loses now and then—after all, every competition must have at least two participants, and one of the two (not the winner) is bound to be a loser. But there’s a difference between simply losing, and being a Loser, and it’s all a matter of cause and effect. A lower case loser (that’s most of us, at one time or another) plans for an outcome and, in the process, accommodates the possibility of various levels of failure. Sometimes we win, other times we lose, but even in failure we can console ourselves with the idea that we “did out best” because the loss is something we’ve foreseen and can deal with. Upper case Losers (people that I wrestle a lot) are a whole other matter. Here, the concept of considered cause and effect is thrown out the window—here, actions have consequences no one could ever have foreseen, and they’re usually catastrophic, to the point where the Loser’s life itself takes a disastrous—and permanent—turn. As often as not, these are the Losers whose lives, but for the losing, are usually forgotten. The everyday Loser’s only legacy is that moment of supreme failure, a massive blemish on an otherwise empty (as opposed to clean) record. We’re tempted to laugh at their folly, to take comfort in their misfortune. But it always helps to keep in mind… …it could happen to you. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [The room is dark. Nothing can be seen except for the blinking of the red light on the camera, which signifies that the camera is on. All of a sudden, a flash of light blasts through the camera lens and the rest of the room. Fnord is now seen sitting in an old beaten up Lazy Boy reclined and looking as joyful as ever. On the table next to him is a lamp that is missing a shade, a huge book, a pack of matches, and a pipe. Fnord is dressed all fancy in one of those ‘smoking robes’, which makes him look very distinguished. He removes his goggle-like sunglasses and tosses them across the room into the vast darkness that is the rest of the room.] “Why hello out there in PTC world.” [Fnord picks up the pipe and the matches. He puts the pipe in his mouth, resting it on his lips, and goes to strike a match. He pulls one off and goes to strike it, but it breaks in half and he just tosses it to the ground. At this time, the light bulb on the lamp begins to fade a little bit. Fnord smacks the lamp the light comes back on. Fnord grabs another match, and this time is successful at lighting it. He puts the lit match in the end of the pipe and goes to inhale…] “COUGH COUGH COUGH!” [Fnord spits out the pipe into his hands and then tosses it into the vast darkness of the rest of the room. He has a look of disgust on his face as he tries to wipe the nasty taste away from his lips.] “Who the hell smokes a pipe these days? That was nasty!” [Fnord finishes wiping off his mouth and spitting out anything left from the horrible pipe incident. He brings out a pack of Marlboro Menthol Greed Midgets along with his trusty Zippo 4WW brand lighter. He takes out a cigarette and lights it up, inhaling the sweet menthol that he calls fresh air. He smiles as he blows out the smoke towards the camera and puts his Zippo away.] “There, that’s better…” [Fnord reaches over and lugs the huge-ass book onto his lap. On the front of the leather bound book it says ‘LOSERS’ really large. He opens the book to the beginning and starts to look through it.] “I have the perfect couple of stories to show how I feel about Limp. Our Bathroom Brawl is going to be outstanding. I can’t wait to give that guy a swirlie!” [Fnord brings a little remote out from his pocket and presses a button. The lamp fades down to a very dull brightness as a projector shines on the opposite wall of Fnord. The first picture is of an old-looking man. He looks very happy as it looks as if he is sitting in the clouds. He looks down to see a naked man running around with complete joy in his face.] “Who was the original Loser? It would have to be—Adam!” [You know who Adam is. He’s from the famous duo of Adam and Eve. They blew Sonny and Cher out of the water! So, the picture changes to show Adam leaning back on a lion in a garden and eating grapes that a red robin brings to him. He looks as happy as ever.] “He had it all, no death, no taxes, the Garden of Eden, rent-free. There was just one condition—not to eat the fruit from the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil.” [Fnord hits the remote and we see an old looking tree with fruit all over it. There are signs posted all around the tree that say “DO NOT EAT HERE”.] “God did all he could to make Adam happy. He even fixed him up with a fabulous babe.” [The screen clicks over to see ‘God’ introducing Adam to Eve, who just happens to be a hot babe! I remind you, they are all naked! Their hair and their surroundings are covering up their unmentionables as to not shock our young viewers.] “The Devil tempted Eve, and she and Adam ate The Forbidden Fruit.” [A click of the screen and we see a stupid-looking Adam eating a piece of fruit next to The Tree of Knowledge. Eve and a snake hanging down from the tree are laughing at him.] “God really lost it.” [A click again and a big giant head appears before Adam and Eve, who are now covered in leafs and flowers to cover up their nakedness.] (Using best ‘God’ voice)”I ask you not to do one thing—just ONE THING! Do you listen? NO!” “The rest is history…” [Click to the final screen image where ‘God’ is holding a torch and chasing Adam and Eve away.] (Again in ‘God’ voice.) “I’ve had it with you two! No more free lunch, Bubelahs! You’re outta here! Yesiree Bob, I’ve got plans—poison ivy! Hangnails! Traffic James! Alcohol-Free Beer! TV Evangelists! Disco! Brady Bunch Reunions! [And with that, the screen clicks off. Fnord smacks the light again and it pops back on to full brightness. He takes out another MMM (Marlboro Menthol Midget) and lights it up. He sets down the remote and looks down at the book.] “Adam was a Loser. He got kicked out of the best life anyone could have. Hell, if he would not have fucked up, we could still be there! But who would want to wrestle naked? I mean, I don’t need anyone’s johnson flapping all over me during a match. Speaking of johnson, let’s talk about Limp. He is my opponent in this Extreme Title Division. He is the man that I picked out of the crowd to attack. I want a shot at that PTC Extreme Title and I have to work my way up. Mr. Limp Johnson is next on my list. And you know what, Limp can’t win for losing. I always said luck is the bastard child of fate. He will be extremely lucky if he beats me.” [Fnord looks down at his book and chuckles a little.] “And fate can indeed be a cruel mother, giving with one hand while taking with the other. And as you’ll see, some people can’t win for losing. Limp, you will lose to me. Let me tell you a few stories of people that thought they were winners…” [Fnord turns the page in his book and then smiles.] “An Oklahoma district judge candidate lost by 91% to the incumbent—who died months before Election Day. However, his name remained on the ballot and the bar association endorsed the dead man. You might beat me if you die first… Maybe…” [Fnord looks on the opposite page.] “A Boston man died of a heart attack two weeks after winning $3.6 million in the lottery. The stress of being hounded by accountants apparently resulted in the fatal attack. An Arizona woman became a 25% owner of her ex-husband’s $2.2 million lottery prize because a paperwork error delayed their divorce for 11 days, during which time his number hit. Limp, you’ll need to do more than pay me off in order to win the match. I don’t come cheap.” [Fnord turns the page to the next page and laughs out loud.] “A California woman was celebrating winning the football pool at the cannery where she worked by passing out doughnuts, when she was snagged by a machine and pulled to her death. A West Philadelphia man’s car slammed into two utility poles, plunged down a 20-foot embankment, and crashed through a fence. He walked away without a scratch—only to be struck and killed by a passing truck. Lastly, the funeral of a man in Palermo, Italy, was halted when the deceased woke up and climbed out of his casket. His mother was so startled she died on the spot of a heart attack. Limp, unless I die tragically before our match, I will be kicking your ass.” [Before Fnord can say another word, the light bulb in the shade-less lamp explodes.] “Aw shit, I guess this promo is over.” ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Let’s look at it this way, Limp. I don’t know you and you don’t really know me. All I know is that I need to become the PTC Extreme Champion. I also know that you are in my way of that prestigious Championship. Therefore, you must go down. I must jump ahead of you. I must and will endure our battle and come out the winner. In order to win our match, all I have to do is pin you in that bathroom. Of course, I will be beating the crap out of you, maybe literally. There is a toilet if you need it. Think of the stall door smashing into your head. Think of your head going down into a dirty and probably used toilet. The shit and urine all over your face and hair, you will probably like that. Don’t worry; I’ll use the automatic dryer to dry you off. Maybe even burn you a little. I can’t wait to get that plunger in the janitor’s closet and pound away on you. All this talk is making me giddy. I’m ready to fight you. I’m ready to beat you in that bathroom… I’m ready to win…
