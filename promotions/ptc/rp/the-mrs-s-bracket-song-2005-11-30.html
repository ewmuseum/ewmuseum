The Mrs. S. Bracket Song

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 *Copy and paste either link. http://www.geocities.com/massicrist/gregkeeper.mp3 OR TRY http://media.putfile.com/Greg-Jackson Linking to it isn't working. The lyrics are below* Greg: There’s still Shanty and Williams and Zenith and Shadow, Archer and Farmer and Somers and Killean Sirrajin. But can you recall the most dangerous wrestler of all? Nobody knows Greg Jackson, he’s truly a cut above, But when it came to respect, no one showed him any love. All of the other wrestlers, who were all of greater mass, They all would mock Greg Jackson, if he fought back they’d kick his ass! Then one night at GTT, Jackson stomped some guy, Sledge said, "I can’t take no more, muscles weak my neck is sore!" Then all the wrestlers feared him, victims screamed in agony, Jackson the God-like black man, you’re the best in GTT! Just tellin’ it like it is, that’s all… Strings. At this point, I’m quite certain you’re all trembling at the thought of facing me, And for that I am sorry, but it could happen. Keyboard. (Instrumental break) Greg: Pay no attention to my ignorant background singers. This changes nothing! My message is clear, and that message is, "Greg Jackson equals certain death to all that oppose him!" They’re, uhhhh, kinda, ruining everything… Then one foggy Christmas Eve, Santa came to say, Rudolph with your nose so bright, won’t you guide my sleigh tonight? Then all the reindeer feared me, as they shouted out with glee, Gregory the awesome Jackson, go and win that GTT! … There… if that doesn’t scare ‘em off, nothin’ will. *Lyrics by me, vocals by me… except that one part. Original lyrics by Robert May, originally performed by Gene Autry.*
