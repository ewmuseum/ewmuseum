Darth von Doom versus Itachi Kobayoshi

1 October 2008

Unholy Revenge, Phase 3 - Attack of the Unholy and Other Stupidity (IG50 Promo #3 of Title: Unholy Revenge, Phase 3 - Attack of the Unholy and Other Stupidity (IG50 Promo #3 of 5) Word Count: 999 Wrestler: Darth von Doom Yesterday… In Melbourne, Australia, Prime Time Central’s headquarters are bustling with activity because of the three big events they have coming up. There’s the STRIKE MMA tournament, the PTC Elite Championship title defense, and the historic Infinite Gauntlet 50. However, one of the participants in the IG 50 contest is the notorious murdering Sith Lord Darth von Doom and he is a member of the infamous organization known as the Church of the Unholy. This group had vowed to attack if their offers for peace were not answered. Two days later, the first part of their revenge plan is unleashed. Inside the PTC building, screams are heard in the bustling lobby. Everyone turns and sees a woman screaming as she runs through the lobby from the outside. The people in the lobby turn to look outside through the front windows and they see Zombie Robots marching toward the building. A FedEx courier who is in the lobby looks closely and notices that one of the Zombie Robots is carrying the Evil Bong. The courier gulps upon seeing that. Courier: Oh bugger… * * * Two hours later… After the Zombie Robots were sent to attack the PTC Headquarters with the Evil Bong, the Amphibious Ninjas of Doom squadron begins their invasion of the California port city of Bodega Bay which is the hometown of the Church‘s arch nemesis superhero team the Fishermen. After the first attack wave hit the coastal parts of the city, a Sith X-Wing flies in and lands on the beach. The ship’s entrance ramp opens up and out walks Darth von Doom onto the beach. Some Ninjas of Doom come over and he motions for them. Darth: Attack formation. Ninja #1: We haven’t been able to break through their defenses. Darth: Leave that to me. Darth pulls out his light saber and ignites it. The crimson blade flickers in the ocean breeze as he walks up the beach. Just then Water Woman and Surf Boy attack the Ninjas with Sonar Guns. The Ninjas go down like flies but then they try to fire at Darth. Darth blocks the sonar shots and then Force pushes the two of them hard to the ground, knocking them both unconscious. Darth looks down at them both and shuts off his light saber before simply walking over their bodies as he heads toward the main part of the town. * * * The Bodega Bay docks… At the docks, the sea boat owned by Fishermen superhero team leader Aquadude is where an ambush has happened. The Evil Fish Construction Company’s assassin attacked the boat but unfortunately only Aquadude’s sidekick Fishboy to tend to things. However, he was ambushed and tied up by the assassins Raper and his girlfriend Piranha Lady. They briefly interrogated him about the whereabouts of Aquadude. Raper points a gun at Fishboy’s head and laughs. Raper: Do you think not telling us where he is will really change anything? Fishboy: You bastards! Raper laughs evilly and then starts to leave the boat before turning around to point his gun at Fishboy‘s chest. Raper: When we catch the rest of your friends, we’re going to have some fun with Beach Girl before we kill her. At least she won’t be able to walk away before we deliver the killing blow. Fishboy: Go to Hell! Raper: You first. Raper then pulls the trigger and fires two rounds to Fishboy’s chest. Fishboy falls over in a heap as Raper and Piranha Lady sneak off to their car. * * * Ten minutes later… Fishboy is still on the boat but is on the verge of death. Just then someone walks on the boat. Blood comes from Fishboy’s mouth as he tries to call out to whomever it is. Fishboy: Aquadude… A shadow then comes over him and he soon realizes that it is not Aquadude but is in fact the evil Sith Lord Darth von Doom. Darth looks down at him and smiles. Darth: Not exactly. But how would you like chance at revenge? How would you like to get back at your killers? Aquadude: Revenge… Darth gives him a knowing look. Darth: This is your lucky day. * * * Today… In the living room of the Castle of Blood, some of the main leaders of the Church of the Unholy are sitting on the dusty red couch and the ancient recliner right next to it. The living room is dank, dusty, and very forbidding to be in. However it’s where the large screen HD-TV is located so that’s where Dr. Acula, Darth von Doom, Wolfen, and C.G. Murder are watching the world news about the events that have happened over the past few days. Dr. Acula: I can’t believe what happened. I mean, how in the hell did those Yori Yakamo, Jr. Dildos of Death get loose in the PTC Headquarters? Wolfen: *snarl* If it wasn’t for that, we would’ve taken over the PTC. Dr. Acula: (grumbling) Fucking dildos. Darth: Look on the bright side. At least the Evil Bong is still in our possession. C.G. Murder nods his head. Murder: True. And our Zombie Robots did kill people. (licks his lips) I like to kill people. Dr. Acula points to the HD-TV screen where the news is showing the U.S. Army shooting at the zombies that the Church illegally immigrated into the United States from Mexico to attack. The battle is taking place in Tuscon, Arizona. Dr. Acula: You idiots! We lost the battle in Australia, the Fishermen destroyed our Ninjas in California, and our zombies are getting slaughtered in Arizona. What the hell are we going to do now, huh? What are we going to do now. Behind them, someone clears their throat loudly. Everyone turns to see the Horny Mathematician with a big grin on his face. Mathematician: The experience wasn’t entirely wasted. Thanks to Darth bringing me the specimen, we now have a third Horseman of the Apocalypse to unleash in the next attack wave. (To be continued)
