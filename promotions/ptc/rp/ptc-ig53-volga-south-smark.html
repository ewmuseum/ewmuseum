South Smark Despite the three inches of snow that covers the ground this morning, the sun shines high and bright in the sky overlooking small, simple South Park, Colorado. The sun is enough to blind young Stan Marsh, although at the particular moment he’s wishing something could make him deaf, too. “Shut the hell up, Cartman!” Stan yells at his “friend” as he mindlessly hugs himself to shield him from the cold. His other friends, Kyle and Kenny, subsequently denote their frustrations as well. But Eric Cartman does not listen. “You’re just jealous ‘cuz I got to stay up late last night watching the PTC pay-per-viiiiiiew and yooooou didn’t!” Cartman shouts, waving his flag that says “VOLGA” on it. “No one’s jealous, fatass! Wrestling’s fake!” Kyle shouts back. “IT’S NOT FAKE!” Cartman screams, spittle flying from his mouth. “IT’S SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT!” “The only people entertained by it are drunken rednecks like Kenny’s parents!” says Stan, and is retorted by a blistering muffling sound coming from the orange-parka wearing fifth grader. “I am not a redneck! You wouldn’t be saying that shit if VOLGA was here right now!” Cartman begins waving his flag more emphatically. “VOLGA would choke all of you to death and I would let him.” “What, is GOLGA your best friend now?” Kyle asks as his friends laugh. “HIS NAME IS VOLGA! And I’ll have you know he IS my best friend! Much better than you, you filthy Jew! In fact, he’s coming to my house today!” Cartman pauses as, for one second, he drops the act and looks horrified at what he has said. After a moment, though, he smiles again. Kyle and Stan scoff, but Kenny’s eyes widen as he sprays an incredulous and incomprehensible rant the fat kid’s way. “That’s right, Kenny! I DO know VOLGA, and we’re going to hang out and backyard wrestle at my house, and yoooou can’t come!” “You don’t know a professional wrestler, lardass!” Stan shouts. “Do too!” Kyle looks up ahead and sees the bus coming, then sneers back at Cartman. “Then prove it! Tell VOLGA to spend the night on your couch and then bring him to the bus stop tomorrow.” Cartman shifts nervously. “He can’t sleep over. He’s a very important wrestler with…” “Bring him to the bus stop or we’ll never believe you and call you a Fat Lying Assweed for the rest of your life and make you get a tattoo that says FLAW right on your fat lying assweed ass!” Cartman stares at Kyle and Stan for a long moment before shaking his head. “Screw you guys, I’m going home!” Kyle squints, then looks at the bus. “What are you talking about? The bus is getting ready to take us to…” “Screeeeeeew…you guys. I’m going home.” Cartman repeats, then waddles sideways away from the bus stop. As Kyle, Stan and Kenny board the bus, Stan sighs. “I hate that lying assweed – that was a good one Kyle!” “Thanks.” -------------------- While the other boys are listening to Ms. Garrison prattle on about fundamentals of education, Eric Cartman is knocking on the door of a large house frantically. “OPE N THE DOOR!” Cartman yells, pounding heavily. After a moment, the door is opened and Jesus stands in the doorway, his arms open in acceptance. “Yes, my son, how may I serve?” “I need you to grant me a wish!” Cartman says impatiently. Jesus sighs. “Genies grant wishes, Eric Cartman. I perform miracles, but I can’t help everyone with what they need. What is it that you need?” “Make the wrestler VOLGA come to my house and stay with me so I can impress my friends!” Jesus stares at his loyal follower for a few moments, puzzled. “I’m not sure this is something I would feel comfortable doing.” “Oh come on, Jesus! I’m doing it so I can rub it in a dirty Jew’s face! His people killed you, I thought you’d be sympathetic.” Jesus responds by slamming his door in the little kid’s face. Cartman closes his eyes and starts shouting once again. “BUT YOU’RE A CHRISTIAN! YOU’RE SUPPOSED TO HATE OTHER RELIGIONS!” -------------------- VOLGA usually meets most problems head-on and without fear. However, he doesn’t quite know how to react when he’s facing 547 people in the middle of a New York hallway. So he simply stares. The one in the middle, wearing a silly Darth Vader helmet, laughs maniacally. “Behold my army of side characters. They are the only reason I have any relevance whatsoever.” One of the characters in the back laughs, pushing up his glasses and wrapping the lab coat around him tighter. “We also make it look like we’re the Verizon Network. Can you hear me now?” He then giggles very annoyingly. VOLGA raises an eyebrow, and the leader laughs. “You are supposed to compete in the IG match against the former King Blueberry. I cannot stand to see you and that fruit in a match that reminds me how many times me and my crew have lost every important match we’ve ever been in. So I’m going to use Unholy magic to banish you to a place that even a scary person like yourself will fear.” From behind the leader steps a robed figure. The robe covers every part of the (man? Woman? Goat?) figure except for its clawed hand, which is wrapped around a small bag. Without any words, the shrouded figure throws the bag and a flame erupts. From the flame VOLGA feels himself slowly fading, and before he can do anything, he faints. When he wakes up, he finds to his utter horror he is seemingly animated out of construction paper. Then he faints once again. -------------------- “Aren’t you supposed to be in school, sweetie?” Cartman’s Mother asks as he waddles through the doorway. “Shut up, mom!” Cartman shouts, then makes the way to his room slowly. He thinks about what Kyle said to him at the bus stop, and even though Kyle is a stupid Jew who couldn’t do anything like that, Cartman still doesn’t want to face the guys the next day. Thankfully, there is a caped wrestler sitting on Cartman’s bed when he enters the room. “What? SWEET!” Cartman shouts as he bounds towards the bed. His sudden motion makes VOLGA jump up and arm himself in a defensive stance. Cartman stops and approaches the wrestler slowly. “No, no, VOLGA…I’m not a bad guy…I’m a friiiiiend. Friiiiiiiend.” Cartman holds his hand out for VOLGA to sniff, and he does for one moment before grabbing Cartman by his neck and starting to squeeze. “MOM! A PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER’S CHOKING ME!” Within moments, Cartman’s mother enters the room to view the carnage. “Did you invite him to have ice cream?” “NO I DIDN’T INVITE HIM TO HAVE ICE CREAM, HE’S CHOKING ME!” “Well, professional wrestlers don’t respond well to impoliteness. Apologize to him.” “But mooooooooooooooom…” Cartman whines. “Eric!” “Fine, fine, sorry,” says Cartman, who was able to talk the entire time while simultaneously being choked. It’s probably because his throat is so large. VOLGA drops Cartman and sits back down on the bed, growling. After collecting himself for a few seconds, Cartman smiles. “This is awesome! Wait til Kyle sees this!” -------------------- The next day at the bus stop, Kyle, Kenny and Stan are waiting anxiously for Cartman to arrive. Kyle is grimacing, and Stan slowly approaches his buddy. “Uh, Kyle? Don’t you think you’re being a bit hard on Cartman about this VOLGA thing?” Kyle shrugs. “I don’t know. I guess. It’s just that professional wrestling can make people do crazy things, and I’ve always been told to stay away from it. It might be fake but the things people do for it are pretty real and scary.” “But still, you don’t have to tattoo FLAW on his ass just because he was being a jackass about it. Cartman’s a jackass about everything.” “I wasn’t really going to, I was just…” Kyle stops suddenly as he sees two figures plodding towards the bus stop. On the right is a large man wearing a cape and tights, shielding himself from the cold (as that is, of course, all he is wearing). On the left is a short fat kid singing his theme music. “There is this guy who’s really super sweet! Doesn’t talk much but he’s good with his feet, VOLGA! VO-VO-VO-VOLGA! He’s come to South Park to show what he can do, he’s gonna choke out a condescending Jew, oh my VOLGA! V-O-L-G-A VOLGA!” That is certainly not his real theme music. Though it probably should be. “Holy shit!” Stan yells as Cartman and VOLGA walk up to the bus stop. VOLGA just stands there angrily, but Cartman is all smiles. “Guys, this is VOOOLGA! VOLGA, these are the assholes who didn’t think I was friends with you. Say hi, guys!” Kyle eyes the big man skeptically. “Are you really VOLGA?” VOLGA predictably doesn’t respond. “See? He doesn’t respond because he can’t talk! Just like the real VOLGA!” “Lots of people don’t talk!” Stan says. “Do your finishing move if you’re really VOLGA.” The big man looks down at the children uneasily, and Cartman nudges him. “It’s okay, best friend! You can show them.” VOLGA shrugs and picks up Kenny, choking him. Kenny’s face turns blue before the big man releases and throws him into the snow. Kenny doesn’t move. “Oh my God, you killed…” Stan starts to say until Kenny dusts the snow off of him and standup. “No, nevermind, you didn’t kill him.” “That’s because wrestling’s fake,” Kyle says, then looks up to the large wrestler and gulps. “So what are you going to do with him, Cartman?” Cartman laughs. “In three days, John Cena is going to be in town for the 4th annual Smarkinson’s Disease benefit, signing autographs. I’m going to walk in there and make him fight VOLGA at the civic center!” “John Cena? He sucks, dude! He only knows, like, five wrestling moves.” Stan waves his hand in front of his face. “You can’t see me? What the hell is that?” VOLGA nods and appears to be chuckling, but no sound comes out. Cartman growls. “You won’t be laughing when VOLGA wins me ONE MILLION DOLLARS!” Kyle’s eyes go wide. “A million dollars? How?” “That’s going to be the prize money for beating Cena! And if you guys help me get him ready and train him, I’ll give you guys each a thousand!” Kenny muffles something, and Stan is quick to agree, “Yeah, how come you get that much?” Cartman puts his arm around VOLGA’s legs. “Because he’s myyyyyy best friend! And a thousand dollars is a lot of money! It’s more than Kenny’s parents make in three years.” Kenny starts yelling again, but Kyle cuts him off. “Okay, what do we have to do?” -------------------- A few days later, Cartman, Kenny, Stan, Kyle, and VOLGA all stand outside of the event building. “How do we do this?” Stan asks. “One of us needs to pretend to have Smarkinson’s Disease so we can get inside. Kyle, I think it should be you.” “Why me?” asks Kyle. “Because scientists have found that money-grubbing big-nosed kids have Smarkinson’s more than any other ethnicity,” Cartman replies. “Fuck you, fatass!” Cartman hands Kyle a laptop and a 2-liter bottle of Mountain Dew Code Red, then whispers, “Okay. Just twitch a lot and shout things like ‘I hate John Cena!’ every couple of seconds. I’ll do the rest.” Cartman knocks at the door, and a skinny gentleman answers. “May I help you?” “Hi, my name is Eric Cartman, and this is my friend Kyle. He has Smarkinson’s Disease. We’re here for the benefit.” Cartman shoots a meaningful glance at Kyle, who twitches and shouts “I hate John Cena!” The lean man nods. “Oh, he certainly has it all right. Look how big that bottle of Code Red is! Come on in.” As the kids and VOLGA walk through the hallway, the lean man sadly shakes his head. “This is a very sad disease, Eric. Your friend is very brave.” “I know, sir,” Cartman says as Kyle shouts “CENA USES STEROIDS!” “They’ve done research, you know, and they’ve discovered that it’s largely genetic. These poor victims can’t help their obesity, their addiction to Mountain Dew, or their undeniable hatred of John Cena, Hulk Hogan, and other wrestlers that normal people have no problem with.” Cartman nods as they spy John Cena towards the back of the room, handing out a meal to an overweight, laptop-wielding man in a wheelchair. The man slaps the food out of Cena’s hands and shouts, “You suck!” “There he is, guys. Let’s go talk to him.” Stan and Kenny stay back as Cartman, VOLGA, and Kyle walk up to Cena. “Hey, what’s up, guys?” Cena says, instinctively putting his hands up as a bowl of spaghetti sails over his head. “Nice arm-strength, Tommy! Next time, we’ll work on your aim.” After watching the scene awkwardly for a few moments, Cartman speaks up. “Hi, Mr. Cena. My name is Eric Cartman, and this is VOLGA and Kyle. We have come to you with an offer.” Cena adjusts his baseball cap and sits in a chair so he is looking eye-to-eye with Cartman. “All right, let’s hear it.” “My wrestler here, VOLGA,” Cartman stops here to point to the caped grappler, who growls, “would like to face you in a wrestling match tonight at the local civic center. If you win, VOLGA will give you a million dollars.” Here Cartman avoids the angry gaze of his wrestler and continues. “But if you lose, you have to give US a million dollars.” John Cena laughs and stands to his full height, looking at VOLGA. “That’s a nice offer, kid, but I can’t participate in matches outside of my company. My boss wouldn’t like that.” “Screw your boss!” Cartman shouts. “If you won’t do it for the money or for the pride of beating the best mute wrestler in the world, at least do it for Kyle here.” Cartman puts an arm around Kyle, who recoils. “Kyle is stricken with Smarkinson’s Disease and he really just wants to see you wrestle live…so he can boo you.” “Please help us, Mr. Cena,” says Kyle. Cartman shoots an angry look at him as Cena looks confused. “You have Smarkinson’s?” Cena asks. “Yes, sir.” “But you’re not fat,” Cena muses. Then, after a beat: “And you called me sir.” Cartman steps in and pushes Kyle to the side. “He just came down with it, and he’s fighting it so hard, Mr. Cena. But it would really make all of us very happy if you had this wrestling match with VOLGA.” After a look long at Kyle (who twitches and sips his Code Red), Cena nods. “All right, kid. You’ve got yourself a goddamned deal. Tonight at the civic center.” Then he looks at VOLGA and nods. “And you? You can’t see me, punk.” Dropping his twitching act, Kyle looks up, straight-faced. “Yes he can. You’re right there.” Cena stares at Kyle for a long moment before walking away. “YES!” Cartman says, high-fiving Kyle. “You were awesome, you're a better retard than Timmy!” “Thanks, I – hey!” -------------------- Word travels fast, and the civic center is filled with wrestling fans and Smarkinson’s patients, all ready for the big fight. VOLGA leans against the ropes while Cartman, Kyle, Stan and Kenny all stand outside of the ring behind him. “This is great, guys! We’re gonna win lots of money!” Cartman shouts, clapping his hands. “I just hope it doesn’t get too violent,” Stan says, looking up at the angry crowd, many of whom are holding up signs such as JOHN CENA SUCKS, I HATE CENA, and KANE WEARS LIFTS. “Who cares about violence, dude?” says an exasperated Kyle. “It’s a thousand dollars!” “I thought you hated wrestling.” Kyle shrugs. “I did until I found out it could make me rich. Now I want VOLGA to kill the dude!” YOUR TIME IS UP MY TIME IS NOW John Cena’s music suddenly hits and the crowd goes wild (or furious). Half of the crowd is cheering, but the other half is booing loudly. Two fans stand next to each other shouting different chants and wearing different shirts: one says “I love John Cena” and the other says “I hate John Cena so much I spent a ton of money supporting his career just to tell him how much I hate him.” VOLGA nods at him and punches his fists, ready for his first action since winning the Infinite Gauntlet match. Then, as he looks out at the crowd, he sees the robed figure from the Unholy army that sent him here. The robed figure pokes a hand out of the robe and waves to VOLGA. The caped wrestler’s eyes become slits and he begins to crack his knuckles. “All right, guys, let’s have a clean fight,” says the referee, then calls for the bell. As soon as the bell is called, Volga rushes forward and grabs Cena’s neck, squeezing it until it collapses. Within seconds, John Cena falls to the ground, no longer breathing. Volga steps on him for the three count. “That wasn’t clean at all!” shouts the ref before running out of the ring. The kids look on in shock as VOLGA stands over the dead body of John Cena. The Smarkinson’s patients all watch in silence until one of them says, “Who do we boo now?” After a moment, another says, “He wasn’t that bad of a wrestler, really. He could carry a match with anyone, even big dumb guys like Umaga.” A few voices ring in agreement, and someone else says, “Yeah, and you have to respect someone who comes back from injuries and works hard for the company.” “What’s wrong with us?” asks one. “We’ve been monsters to this man!” The wheelchair-residing Smarkinson’s patient suddenly stands and says “We’re not the monsters, guys! VOLGA is the monster! He killed him in no time at all!” “Yeah, that was an easy victory. Too easy. VINCE MUST BE PUSHING HIM TOO MUCH!” Then, all of the Smarkinson’s fans boo VOLGA, throwing hamburger wrappers and beer bottles at him. Over the booing, maniacal laughter rings out as the robed figure enters the ring. “This is exactly how I planned it,” says the figure, pulling back his hood. Cartman points in surprise. “AXEL ACTION?!” Axel laughs, rubbing his hands together. “YES! It is I, and my plan worked perfectly!” Turning to the dead body of John Cena, Axel begins shouting. “THIS IS WHAT YOU GET, CENA! THIS IS WHAT YOU GET WHEN YOU FUCK MY MOTHER ON CAMERA!” Giggling, Axel doesn’t notice VOLGA creeping up behind him. Clutching him by the throat, VOLGA chokes the Church of the Unholy member until he too falls to the ground. Suddenly, “The Flyin’ Hawaiian” Bryan Dawkins enters the ring and looks down on the carnage. “Lawl. That’s some twisted shit, bruh.” He then spits pineapples on the bodies and walks out. Suddenly, the robe that Axel Action was wearing starts to glow red. VOLGA looks at it, confused, and gives it a little kick. The robe jumps up and becomes a swirling portal that VOLGA is sucked through, leaving only the kids and the dead bodies in the ring. Cartman looks from Cena to Action, then begins crying. “What about my money?” Kyle stares off into the distance, and Stan pats his shoulder. “Kyle, are you mad about the money too?” Kyle turns around, smiling. “No, I’m not, because I learned something today. I learned that wrestling is something that is meant to be enjoyed, not analyzed and judged. These people with Smarkinson’s are sick, sad people, because instead of liking this simple thing presented for their enjoyment, they get so blinded by hatred for simple characters that they let it get in the way of their fun. They might claim to be wrestling fans, but they don’t enjoy it anymore; it annoys and upsets them because they got so caught up in hating Cena and other guys that they forgot to just sit back and enjoy the show. And we were the same way, not caring about wrestling for anything but money. I think it’s best if we just enjoy something for what it is, not for what some of us think it should be.” Stan nods, then looks at the robe and shrugs. “Plus that VOLGA guy was a total asshole.” “Yeah, whoever came up with the idea for him to come to South Park is the biggest idiot in the world.”
