Twistodium: Commercial

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 The shot opens up in a popular gymnasium in New York City. People of all body sizes are working out all over the gym. Some are on treadmills, some are lifting weights, some people are doing push-ups, and some are using Nautilus machines. On a table in the center of the gym there is a bottle of Twistodium and a large bowl with 100 white tablets sitting inside for anyone to take. A large muscular man walks up to the table and grabs the bottle of Twistodium in one hand. He’s wearing black work out pants and a black T-shirt that reads "Got Goat?" on it. Around his waist is a large gold wrestling title belt with the letters GCW in scripted. "Hi," the man begins. "I’m the Global Championship Wrestling champion, Twizzy and I’m here to talk to you about how I got to the peak level of fitness and success I’m at today. I owe everything I have to one little white pill: Twistodium." He holds up the bottle and smiles brightly. Twizzy picks out one tablet from the bowl and holds that up to the camera as well. "The perfect work-out tool." He pops the pill and smiles. Then he grabs a big handful of Twistodium and downs all of those pills as well. His eyes bulge and then twitch. His whole body begins to spasm for a brief three seconds before Twizzy takes a deep breath and calms himself. "Mmm… T is for Twistodium and that’s good enough for me!" He starts to walk around the gym, bottle in hand, and supervises all of the people working out. "Using Twistodium to help you is a great idea. Before I used Twistodium I was just a boring wrestler. I didn’t have anything going for me. I even developed a severe knee injury called Jobberitis. It was pretty painful. But then my doctor recommended a new product he thought could help me. You guessed it: diet and excersize. I said I already did those things and so he gave me the easy way out: popping pills. Now Twistodium is my very best friend. We go to the zoo together, we work together, we play catch together. I use Twistodium in my cooking. You haven’t had eggs benedict until you’ve powdered them with crushed Twistodium." Twizzy licks his lips and rubs his stomach. The camera goes in close on Twizzy’s face, which grows a little more serious. "Fact is that Twistodium is the number one most effective steroi- I mean, fitness helper on the market today. So buy it! Take it! You’ll love it as much as I do. Boy-o, the power of Twistodium compels you!" Twizzy points at the camera sternly. A graphic for Twistodium comes up on the screen. It’s a goat with a very muscular white furry human body in a wife-beater smiling hard and holding a bottle of the steroid. He has veins popping out of his neck and looks inhuman, especially since it’s a goat. Twizzy’s voice comes on over the graphic. "When you see Twistodium always remember that Goat… is… Good!"
