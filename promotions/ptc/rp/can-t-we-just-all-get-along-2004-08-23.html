Can't we just all get along?

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 "This sucks." The defeatist words of Blake Wolfson resounded throughout a spacious gym, where Team XUW had set up their base for the next few hours, in their attempt to develop a game plan for what lied ahead in the weeks to come, that being the daunting task of defeating thirteen other PrimeTime Central feds in the Survival tournament. Blake gazed around the empty gym, remembering that The Ripper requested a special closed training session so as the foursome wouldn't be bothered by any other wrestlers. As he stared down at the ground looking at nothing in particular, he couldn't help but wish that he was anywhere but here. Normally, most wrestlers involved in this sort of tournament would feel honored to represent their federation in such a highly regarded competition as this. But not Blake Wolfson. After his Global Title defeat at the hands of Hessian back in May, Blake vowed that he wouldn't compete in another PTC event, simply because he publicly believed he didn't need to lower himself to compete against other filth from different federations... although secretly, he didn't want to compete in another PTC event incase he was to get defeated and embarrassed on the biggest of stages once again. He was never motivated for this event, because XUW's tyrannical commissioner -- Mister Webster -- placed him on the team to disrupt his preparation for the XUW World Title Tournament, which is currently underway. Webster made no effort to hide his hatred of Wolfson, or for that matter most of the XUW roster. He had no option but to place Wolfson in the World Title Tournament simply because he was the previous #1 contender, but now that he's in it, Webster's trying to make sure that Blake has to go through as much adversity as possible, and what better way to make sure he's not the flagship of XUW by placing him in one of the most physically demanding tournaments in recent times. Blake's thoughts are immediately interrupted by a booming voice that echoes throughout the entire gym. "The Ripper wishes to spar with you now, Wolfson." Blake looks up, noticing the imposing figure of Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy standing inside the squared circle on the opposite side of the gym, with the height of the ring exaggerating The Ripper's size, making him look larger than normal. "Yes sir." Blake mutters under his breath sarcastically, annoyed with The Ripper's constant demands. Blake has an issue with authority and he doesn't like being told what to do, but really, you shouldn't be surprised there. Blake makes his way over towards the wrestling ring where the imposing figure of The Ripper is standing. Blake slides underneath the bottom rope, refusing to take his beady eyes off his teammate. "Mmmmph... dudes, these Funyuns are like, the best Funyuns ever." proclaims a voice behind the ring, and upon further inspection it's revealed that it is indeed Fatbadger. "What are you on about? They're in a packet. They're always the same as every other packet of Funyuns!" replies another voice from off-camera, as the scene pans around, revealing woof woof who is casually sitting on a steel blue chair. "Nah-uh! I can't remember the last time I had a packet of Funyuns as good as these!" says the Badger of Fat, nodding enthusiastically. "But that's because you have a bad memory." retorts woof. "Who are you?" poses Fatbadger, scratching his head, as woof simply shakes his. "Enough of this tomfoolery." exclaims The Ripper. "It is important that you pay close attention to The Ripper at all times. You might learn a thing or two." "Like how to look fucking ugly?" quips woof, as he bursts out laughing along with Fatbadger and Wolfson. "Silence! The Ripper's looks will not come into question at this training session. We are here to focus on much more important things." replies The Ripper. "Is this really necessary? I mean, seriously. We're all competent wrestlers here." Blake pauses, looking at the direction of Fatbadger. "Okay, except retard-boy over there." "HEY!! I resemble that!" cries woof woof, as Blake shakes his head. "Okay, almost all of us are competent wrestlers. Do we really need to train together? Because I really am fairly busy at the mo'. I have to get to the movie set in a few hours and I could really do with some extra preparation time." Blake pauses, thinking about his latest movie, 'My Guide Midget And Me', which is a comedy based on the life of a scientist who goes blind from an experiment gone wrong, and because he's allergic to dogs, instead of a guide dog being assigned to him in everyday life, he's been assigned a midget to take on the role of a sight aid. But I digress. "Not only that, but I really should be getting started on plotting woof woof's doom for when we meet in the next round of the XUW World Title tournament." Blake continues, referring to the match between himself and his Doggy Style partner. That sounds wrong, but the team name is Doggy Style, honest. "The Ripper will not hear such excuses. The Ripper would rather be reading in the Library of Darkness than spending time with you three cretins, but The Ripper is still here." states the former Army of Darkness leader. "Dude... this thought has just come to me. At the Domicile there's a Library of Darkness, there's a Laboratory of Darkness, and there's even a Casino of Darkness. Is there a Brothel of Darkness?" poses Fatbadger, arching an eyebrow, contemplating the possible answers. "Yes. But the women are hairier than normal, and they radiate a strange odor." replies The Ripper. "Do they dress in Nurse's outfits?" queries Fatbadger. "No comment." The Ripper answers. "Can you take me there?" asks the Badger of Fat hopefully. "No. The Brothel of Darkness is for no one's satisfaction except for The Ripper's. Now stop distracting The Ripper and stop asking such bizarre questions. The Ripper is here for one reason and one reason only, and that's to win." he states bluntly. "Really? Because I thought we were here to lose." quips Wolfson. "Sarcasm is the sign of an unimaginative sense of humor." replies The Ripper. "Blake's sense of humor must really suck, then." states woof with a smile. "Shut up, tosspot." Blake retorts, before turning back to The Ripper. "What I'm trying to say is why do we have to train together? I have no problem with you teaching Fatbadger the ways of kicking ass, but I'm a little insulted that you think you need to do likewise to myself, who is arguably the most decorated wrestler in XUW history." "While your exaggeration is a little over the top, The Ripper's intent is not to insult you, unless he says likewise to your face. To truly have a chance in this tournament, we need to work together as a team, even though it pains The Ripper a lot to say so. If you look around at the various federations taking part, The Ripper would guarantee that hardly any of them would be training together as a team, because for various reasons most teammates would hate each other. The situation is the same in our case. However, The Ripper advises that it would be in our best interests if we put past vendettas aside. The title of the interfed event is appropriate, as it is Survival that we will have to achieve if we want to win this. If we are to survive Mount Saint Hell's, we'll need to work together as a unit." states the Ripper calmly. "Dude... so that's why you've come back! Because we're fighting in Hell, and who knows more about Hell than you!" exclaims Fatbadger. "Not quite, tiny Badger. While we won't be fighting in hell, we'll be fighting in hell." retorts The Ripper. "Sheesh, and you guys call me stupid." says Fatbadger, who shakes his head extremely confused. "Well, yeah, we do." woof resounds. "Oh." "What's in this for you, Nobbledy?" asks Wolfson, stepping right up to the behemoth known as The Ripper. "It is none of your concern what is in it for The Ripper. Besides... don't you want to win, Wolfson?" questions The Ripper. "Well sure, but..." Blake is cut off once more. "No buts, then. If you want to win, sacrifices must be made." states The Ripper clearly. "WHAT?! YOU'RE GOING TO SACRIFICE ME TO THE GODS?! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!! YOU WON'T TAKE ME ALIVE!!" screams Fatbadger, who leaps to his feet, but he then pauses suddenly. "Well, actually... yeah, if you're going to sacrifice me, I guess that means you won't really take me alive, will you? I suppose that was, like, a pretty stupid statement on my part." reasons a confused Fatbadger. "Tiny Badger, you will not be sacrificed." replies The Ripper sternly. "Phew. Because I thought you wer..." "... Yet." interrupts The Ripper. "Crap." Fatbadger drops his packet of Funyuns, before frantically starting to pick them up off the floor and shove them into his mouth as quickly as possible. "Enough foreplay already." booms The Ripper. "Don't you mean small talk?" asks woof woof. "Quiet you. Now Wolfson, let's go." says The Ripper, who once again begins to circle Blake inside the ring. It was a first. Jack 'The Ripper' Nobbledy and Blake Wolfson... in the ring... one-on-one. Wolfson has been a staple of XUW ever since its introduction in 2001, The Ripper likewise... except for the last year, 'cause y'know, he's 'dead' and all. They were, in their peak, arguably two of the biggest forces in XUW history. Yet not once have they ever fought one-on-one in a competitive match. They have had their confrontations in the past, but never have they tested their limits against each other. The Ripper lunges towards Wolfson, applying him in a side headlock. He synches in the move, using his power to do so. Blake counters by backing him into the ropes, before whipping him to the opposite side of the ring. The Ripper's massive frame bounces off the ropes, and completely flattens Wolfson with a big shoulderblock, sending Blake flying to the mat. The Ripper smiles deviously, as Blake gets back to his feet, still glaring at his counterpart. "Do not rely on your strength and power, but rather your balance and agility." states The Ripper bluntly. The Ripper applies another headlock to Wolfson, and once more Blake whips him to the ropes. The Ripper rebounds and once again attempts a shoulderblock, but this time Wolfson staggers backwards, obviously from the sheer force and weight of The Ripper, but he is still able to maintain his vertical base. "The Ripper is not such a fool after all, is he?" The Ripper smirks, proud of his advice. "Shut up." snarls Wolfson, upset that he was taught something by a fellow wrestler, his pride being wounded in the process. Wolfson charges at The Ripper, as the two XUW legends clash once more. Wolfson uses his speed to duck behind The Ripper, applying him in a reverse bear hug. The Ripper uses his awesome strength to pry Wolfson's hands from around his waist, turning him around and attempting to deliver a clothesline, but Wolfson ducks, and in turn taking down The Ripper with a short-arm clothesline of his own. Blake gets straight back to his feet, proceeding to dropkick The Ripper's right knee, trying to knock him off his feet. The Ripper staggers backwards, as Wolfson runs off the ropes once more to land the 'killer' blow, but The Ripper telegraphs what's coming as he bends down, low-blowing Wolfson who immediately falls to the mat, grabbing his crotch. "Y... you son of a bitch." screams Wolfson, who has turned red after the low blow. "Our match at Survival is a No Disqualifications match. You will have to deal with a lot more than just a low blow, I can assure you." warns the cold, harsh voice of The Ripper. "Yes, but this isn't fricking Survival yet, is it!?" screams Wolfson, still angry at the cheap shot. He brushes himself off before slowly getting back to his feet and getting in The Ripper's face. "Besides, the rules also state that our match will be held under Lucha Libre rules. Does that mean you're going to perform a hurricanrana on me? I think not." "Are you sure about that?" The Ripper queries. Before Wolfson can even move, The Ripper leaps up with all his strength, hooking Wolfson's head with his legs, taking him down with a modified head-scissors takedown, as opposed to just a regular hurricanrana. "FWAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Dude... you just got owned." laughs Fatbadger, who's looking straight at Wolfson. "You know, if you weren't my partner, I'd probably disown you for that piece of stupidity." states woof woof. "Dude... but then if you weren't his partner, you couldn't disown him, because like you wouldn't be associated with him in the first place. Why don't you disown him now?" asks Fatbadger, scratching his temple once more. "You're making too much sense. Go and eat another gallon of BBQ sauce or something." retorts woof. "Mmmmmmmm... BBQ sauce." drools the excitable Fatbadger. "Shut the hell up!" yells Wolfson, outraged by The Ripper's one-upsmanship. "Bring it, bitch." Wolfson motions towards the Ripper, as the pair lock up once more. "You know what I'm thinking?" asks woof woof, turning to Fatbadger. "That you'd like to lick BBQ sauce off of Elisha Cuthbert's body? And then after that, you'd like to have a bath with her, but instead of water you'd be like sitting in a tub of Code Red and the tub would be full, and I don't mean like half full like most people have it, I mean like really full, up to your nose which would be kinda hard to breathe although it'd be funny to see you snort Code Red out of your nose, it's be all frothy and stuff and I... what was I saying?" asks the confused Fatbadger. woof pauses momentarily, trying to re-gather his thoughts. "... Anyway, like I was saying... look at these two. They're so consumed by bettering each other -- in other words they're trying to compare penis sizes -- and their egos are too big to stop fighting now. They'll probably be here for a few hours until they get too tired, or someone dies. Either way. So, do you want to go and do something else?" asks woof woof. "I dunno dude, that sounds like too much work, and my ass is just molding itself into this steel chair nicely." says Fatbadger, with a wriggle of his ass. "Oh Jack... I think Fatty wants you to RipSaw him!" mocks woof, crying out a little louder than his normal tone. woof turns around, noticing that Fatbadger has completely disappeared. "Meh." exclaims woof with another shrug of his shoulders, as he gets to his feet and walks out of the gym. Wolfson and The Ripper continue to brawl, neither noticing that their two training 'buddies' have suddenly disappeared. Even though common sense would suggest that the pair should conserve their energy leading into such a brutal tournament, it was in both of their competitive natures that they'd try and beat the living crap out of each other at that very instant in the gym. It's that same competitive nature which drives Blake to compete and give it everything he has, despite the fact that he was forced into the PTC Survival event from the beginning. It's the same competitive nature that everyone in XUW has, and it's why -- in Blake's mind anyway -- that he was certain they would prevail. He was certain they would survive. The Ripper gives Wolfson a backbreaker, as the camera slowly fades to black.
