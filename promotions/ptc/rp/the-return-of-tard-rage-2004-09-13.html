The Return ... of Tard Rage

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 Quote of the Day (From a Help Wanted ad in a Vermont newspaper) “The University of Vermont is an affirmative action employer and does not discriminate for reasons of national origin, race, gender, class, sexual performance, or religion.” (The scene opens up inside of … c’mon, guess … you can do better than that … OK, pencil’s down … here it comes: … a playpen. Yeah, don’t even try to act like you called that. Scattered on the playpen’s cigarette-burnt carpet are lots of children’s toys, mostly wrestling figures. There are even a few toy wrestling rings, each containing the participants of a PTC dream match: one with Seymour Almasy and Jay Phoenix in it, another containing Angelo Deville and Inphino Blitz. The last ring, which holds the participants of the July PTC Global title defense, rests in front of the lone occupant of the playpen, who even sitting Indian-style towers way above his miniature playmates. And I mean way above. The former PCW wrestler known as Unstoppable Eugene, clothed in an oversized pair of children’s overalls with duckys on them, reaches down and scoops up two of the toys in the ring, AWS Man (also known as Bill) and Saij, which look the size of toothpicks in his massive hands. A childlike grin crosses the sizeable distance from ear to ear – which isn’t saying much, since this grin is basically a mainstay on Eugene’s gargantuan face. He smacks the Saij doll into the AWS Man one, dropping it back to the ring. He accompanies the “move” with an emphatic nod of his head, sending his long, stringy hair flying everywhere.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : SAJ SMACKS ABC MAN LIKE A BIOTECH! (Eugene now moves Saij to stand above one of the ring posts, before raising him back in the air to flip head over heels forward onto the AWS Man figure with a shooting star press.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : THE FINAL HURRAH! 1, 7, 3! (On a side note, Eugene always did have trouble with that elusive number in the middle of the three count.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : ASS MAN ARE TEH LOOSER AGAIN, AND THE SLYSTER RETAINS WATER! HOORAY! Annoyed Female Voice : Damn it, Eugene, will you keep that racket down?! Mommy has a headache again. (At this point, the camera finally pans out a little to reveal the rest of the room in which the playpen is located. The pen occupies the corner of an apartment living room – and not a particularly nice apartment. As it turns out, it’s not just the carpet in the playpen which is stained and marked with unidentifiable flecks of white everywhere and cigarette burns: which is good, or else it would appear that Eugene has taken up a nasty habit at a rather young age – mentally, at least, if not physically. A half-wall separates the living room from the kitchen, which doesn’t look too much better. The walls are brown, the tile floor is brown, and the counters are a lighter brown; and even though I’m the one narrating this piece, your guess is as good as mine as to if that’s their original color. Along one wall, doors open up to two bedrooms and an untrustworthy bathroom. Back in the living room, a ratty-looking couch, with probably more stuffing showing than pillow cover, occupies the back wall, facing a sixteen-inch television that’s easily the nicest thing in the house, and probably the only thing of value. A dark-haired woman is lying on the couch, clearly nearing middle-age yet having kept herself in good shape and still pretty attractive: the woman, that is, not the couch, which has really let itself go even as couches are concerned. She has a hand over her eyes, clearly not watching the TV, which is on but on MUTE.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : SORRY, MOMMY! (Lowering his voice by perhaps an insignificant fraction of a decibel level) HOORAY! THE ERA OF FARKA- FARKITY- FARKORA- UM, FARKNESS CONTINUES! COME, LET US CELEBEHRATE WITH THE COCKJOCKEY! (Eugene’s mother’s eyes open at this, and she sits up and gazes down with displeasure at her son. Well, not technically down, since on a size level, Eugene sitting on the floor is still about as tall as his mother sitting on the couch. But somehow she still manages to glower at him in a manner that makes her seem higher above him.) Eugene’s Mother : Eugene, where did you hear that word? Unstoppable Eugene~! : UMMM … FROM YOU, MOMMY! (Eugene’s mother’s brows narrow in confusion.) Eugene’s Mother : From me? Are you sure, Eugene? Unstoppable Eugene~! : YEAH! LIKE WHEN YOU SAID, “MOMMY’S GONNA CELEBEHRATE DADDY’S CHILD SUPPORT CHECK COMING BY GETTING TORE UP AND FINDING A DADDY OF HER OWN!” Eugene’s Mother : (Irritated) No, not “celebrate.” I mean that last word you said. Unstoppable Eugene~! : …OWN? THAT COMES FROM YOU TOO, LIKE HOW YOU ALWAYS SAY YOU OWN ME! YAY, MOMMY OWNS EUGENE! Eugene’s Mother : (Sigh) No, Eugene, not the very last word that you said, after I started questioning you about what you said. I mean the word that you said you and your dolls would celebrate with. (Eugene’s smile momentarily disappears as he screws his face up, trying to remember this far back in the chain of events. Eugene normally doesn’t think about what just came out of his mouth at all, let alone two sentences ago. After a good thirty seconds of Eugene remaining lost deep in thought, his mother rolls her eyes and realizes she’ll have to repeat the word herself in order to refresh her son’s memory.) Eugene’s Mother : The word was … well, it was “cock,” which is a very naughty- (But Eugene has already been distracted by the familiar word, which returns him to his former train of thought. Well, “train” might be a little too strong there. It’s more like one of those pushcarts they used to have on railroad tracks, the ones where you’d pump the handle up and down to make it go forward. Ehhhh, don’t act like you don’t know what I’m talking about.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : YAYYY! LET’S PLAY WITH THE COCKJOCKEY! (With that, Eugene reaches for his Cockjockey action figure, miniature even compared to the others. Eugene looks disconcerted for a moment while still searching around, until he finds the XUW toy’s companion: the Giant Inflatable Penis. Of course, the toy version of the Cockjockey’s mount is neither giant nor inflatable, but it is quite unmistakably a penis, a detail which Eugene’s mother does not fail to miss, and which elicits a gasp of displeasure from her.) Eugene’s Mother : Eugene! Did you get that out of Mommy’s closet?! … Wait, no, that’s way too small. Where on Earth did you get that thing? Unstoppable Eugene~! : MOMMY BOUGHT IT FOR ME! NEEDED IT FOR COCKJOCKEY! WAS SOLD SE-PA-RATE-LY! … FRANKS AND BEANS! Eugene’s Mother : I can’t imagine that I would buy that for you … Unless, well, was I drunk then? Unstoppable Eugene~! : MOMMY HAD A HANGNAIL! LET EUGENE BUY WHATEVER I WANTED! Eugene’s Mother : The expression is “hangover,” Eugene. I haven’t had a hangnail in … well, a lot longer ago than I was last hung over. (Hastily) Though it’s not like that happens that often either! Anyway, I don’t want you playing with your midget doll anymore. Or his little … friend. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OKAY, MOMMY! (Eugene obediently tosses both the Cockjockey and the penis action figures over his shoulder, where they go flying through the glass window in the wall behind him. Eugene doesn’t seem to even notice the damage, despite the fact that broken glass from the window showers over his shoulders. His mother, on the other hand, does notice, but doesn’t seem to particularly care. Having a sixteen-year-old son with the mind of a three-year-old and the body of at least two well-developed twenty-year-olds put together has desensitized her to a large degree to casual property destruction. It also probably helps that the landlord, along with all the other employees of the apartment complex, is petrified of Eugene and his mother’s absolute control of him to such an extent that their broken windows, leaky pipes, holes in the floor, and other frequent mishaps are fixed promptly. So instead of scolding her son, the matriarch smiles sweetly and changes the subject.) Eugene’s Mother : Eugene, honey, it’s time to put your toys up. We’ve got to get ready to go soon. Unstoppable Eugene~! : GO WHERE? CITY HALL?! (Eugene’s mother rolls her eyes and shakes her head no. A little explanation: Her son, an avid Powerpuff Girls watcher, is convinced that he and his mother live in Townsville, despite the fact that there are no tall skyscrapers anywhere nearby, and of course he has never seen a character from the show in person. He also asks an average of 22.3 times a day if they’re going to City Hall to see the Mayor, who Eugene wants as a pet. At this point, she no longer tries to explain the difference between reality and fiction to her son.) Eugene’s Mother : No, not today. We’re going to the sparring gym, actually. You do remember what Mommy told you yesterday, don’t you? … And the day before … and the day before that … and every day for the past week. Unstoppable Eugene~! : UMMMM … UNKY TYLER IS A DOUCHEBAG? Eugene’s Mother : …Well, probably, but I tell you that most days. I mean, something important. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OH YEAH, HE’S IMPOTENT TOO! Eugene’s Mother : Can we get off the subject of my dirtbag brother? Although I do still hate his guts for alerting the authorities to your real age and abruptly ending my- that is, our stay in PCW, finally we can put that behind us. Now do you know what I’m talking about, Eugene? Unstoppable Eugene~! : YES! (Eugene continues to sit on the floor with the same witless smile plastered on his face, and drool beginning to collect at the side of his mouth.) Eugene’s Mother : …By which you mean no? Unstoppable Eugene~! : YES! Eugene’s Mother : (Finally snapping at her son) Damn it, Eugene, I’m talking about your return to wrestling, and to Primetime Central! You’re finally old enough to wrestle legally, with my supervision at ringside, of course. And you will be, in the GTT4, a really big tournament with lots of your favorite wrestlers in it. Unstoppable Eugene~! : YAAYYYYY!!! I’M GOING TO WRESTLE AGAIN! YAYYYYYYY!!! I CAN BRING TWO FRIENDS! (His mother’s face sours at her son’s display of excitement.) Eugene’s Mother : Eugene, of all the television characters for you to emulate, Special Ed is the worst choice. I don’t think I should let you watch Comedy Central anymore. In fact, I should probably get rid of your TV time altogether. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OKAY, MOMMY! (Eugene suddenly lumbers up and steps over the playpen, which only reaches up to about his shin. One might question then what the point of Eugene’s mother putting it up is at all, but then again, one can also hardly blame the woman for losing a little bit of sanity raising Eugene by herself. Before she can properly react, Eugene stomps over to the wall and rips the TV out of its sockets, before hurling it out the window and shattering the remaining glass.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : ALL GONE! Eugene’s Mother : (Screaming) God damn it, Eugene, that’s not what I meant! (Eugene seems struck by his mother’s anger almost as if by a physical blow, actually reeling backwards and covering his ears with his hands. Soon his bottom lip begins to quiver, and shortly after his eyes begin to fill with tears. Within moments, Eugene has sunk to the floor and degenerated into a full-grade bawl. His mother sighs and immediately begins to speak in a more soothing tone of voice.) Eugene’s Mother : Eugene, sweetie, Mommy didn’t mean to raise her voice. Come on now, honey, I’m not mad. Don’t worry about the TV, we’ll have enough money to buy a new one soon. Come over here to Mommy. (Sniffling, Eugene crawls over to his mother and rests his massive head on her lap, which is obviously quite a weight from her grunt. She strokes Eugene’s hair as she continues to coo to him.) Eugene’s Mother : Eugene, your mother remembers the few short months you spent in PCW very well, a lot better than you do yourself. I remember how much fun you had, the most fun you’ve ever had in your life, in fact! I know you have to remember that much, at least. (Eugene slowly nods his head and sniffles.) Eugene’s Mother : I also know that nothing would make you happier than to return to doing what you love more than anything in the world, wrestling. And your dear, loving mother wants nothing more than your happiness. You know that, right, Eugene? (Eugene nods again, his still snotty nose glistening.) Eugene’s Mother : Of course you do. And I know one more thing: that my sweet little Eugene wants nothing more than to make me happy. So when it’s time for your first GTT match, I want you to have lots of fun in the ring. That will make me happy. But I also want you to help Mommy send a message. (Eugene speaks again, though a little more quietly this time – although still quite above normal speaking voice level.) Unstoppable Eugene~! : WHAT MESSAGE, MOMMY?! Eugene’s Mother : Well … how should I put this? OK, Eugene, other wrestlers, even ones that you may know and love … well, they’re not nice people. And I know you like making new friends, but none of them want to be your friend. Unstoppable Eugene~! : (Distraught) NONE OF THEM?! Eugene’s Mother : No, Eugene, none of them. I’m sorry, but it’s true. Unstoppable Eugene~! : BUT HOW DO YOU KNOW? Eugene’s Mother : Well, ah, because I’ve spoken to them, Eugene. Unstoppable Eugene~! : ALL OF THEM? Eugene’s Mother : …Yes, all of them. Every one. Unstoppable Eugene~! : AND NONE OF THEM LIKE EUGENE? Eugene’s Mother : (Starting to get irritated) No, Eugene, I’m sorry, they all laugh at you. Unstoppable Eugene~! : EVEN FATBADGER? Eugene’s Mother : Especially Fatbadger! They all hate you, now stop questioning me! Unstoppable Eugene~! : SORRY, MOMMY. Eugene’s Mother : It’s OK. Now, the message I want you to send is this: that you don’t care what any of them think. The only person that should matter to you, Eugene, is me. Unstoppable Eugene~! : CAN I MAKE THE MESSAGE IN MACARONI? Eugene’s Mother : No, Eugene, it’s not that kind of message. You see, everybody else in the tournament, they’re bad people. And they deserve to be punished. So you’ll send that message with your actions: by hurting them. Just like your play wrestlers hurt each other. Only a lot, lot worse. Understand? Unstoppable Eugene~! : I … I GUESS SO. Eugene’s Mother : Don’t guess, Eugene, do it! Unstoppable Eugene~! : YES, MOMMY. Eugene’s Mother : Good. And should any other wrestlers manage to talk to you, which shouldn’t happen because I intend on being by your side at all times, don’t listen to them. They’ll try to tell you things about Mommy: bad things. They’ll call me words like … well, I don’t know if you know what this means, but they might call me cruel. Unstoppable Eugene~! : MOMMY IS COOL! Eugene’s Mother : (Slowly and carefully) No, not cool, crrruuueeelll. It means really, really mean. But you know Mommy’s not mean, right? Unstoppable Eugene~! : NEVER! Eugene’s Mother : Of course not. They’re the cruel ones. They want to hurt you and keep you out of wrestling forever. So that’s why you’ve got to hurt them first. Hurt them bad. It’s not cruel, because it’s no more than what they deserve. You shouldn’t have any problem doing that. Especially not against your first opponent, some little jobber from that one federation you seem to like so much, XUW. Theodoro Gutierrez is his name, not like it matters. Unstoppable Eugene~! : YAYYY, EUGENE’S FIGHTING TEDDY RUXPIN! Eugene’s Mother : …Yeah, close enough. I researched this guy a little, definitely nothing special. You beat way better guys in PCW with ease. The one promo I saw by him archived on his website was a blatant rip-off of a Friends episode, the one where Chandler and Joey take a nap together. You know Mommy never misses an episode of a good sitcom; but this isn’t a sitcom, it’s wrestling! How low can you possibly go? Sickening… So for making fun of you behind your back, as well as stealing his ideas from popular sitcoms and being stupid enough to think no one will notice, you have to hurt Theo- … Teddy. Unstoppable Eugene~! : OK, MOMMY! (Eugene promptly reaches out and pulls an old Teddy Ruxpin bear from under the couch, ripping its head off and throwing both parts out the window which he apparently uses as an alternative to a garbage can.) Eugene’s Mother : Yes … just like that. Just like that, sweetie. Now let’s change you into your workout jammies and go get you ready for doing that to the real Teddy. (The scene fades out to Eugene standing up and prancing off to his room, shaking the apartment which each step and sending more bits of plaster to the floor, which explains the white specks all over the carpet.)
