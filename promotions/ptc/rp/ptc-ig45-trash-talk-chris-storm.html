And just like when I won the Global title and everyone boo and hooed, you can just learn to deal with it and accept that I am the best thing to happen to PTC since Angelo Deville learned how to put his feet into someone's crotch. Shit like that doesn't get a man ready to face someone like me. But maybe when Noah Hanson gets his round, you both can step up to the plate. For now, stay on the sidelines and watch the big boys play. And McKail, I know you saw me put a whooping on Hunter after he just smacked you around and you thought to yourself "oh snap, I'm not on that level" so it's cool. Maybe if you're lucky I'll let you carry the IG around backstage at GCW. Maybe. There is one person I was shocked to see wouldn't be joining us though. Ah come on Rust, what's the deal? Normally you would love to brag about the 3-0. Are you finally realizing that luck can strike 3 times and your luck may just be out? Look, yes, you beat me in PCW or whatever that place was called. I can't remember now, been so long. But that was so long ago and you probably got fat and out of shape. You wouldn't even last one round with me anymore. That's ok, at least you can be proud to know that you could do leagues better than Chryste. That hack is so pathetic she's probably crawling around the backstage of some slum bar on the arm of a 300 pound biker named Biff who has a really fucking lame mustache. Oh how the mighty has fallen. Actually I've been predicting this for years. So come one, come all, and try to dethrone the King. But it's easier to talk the talk than to walk the walk.
