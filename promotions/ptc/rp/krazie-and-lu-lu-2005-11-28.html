Now my math has never been that great… But after consulting with Lu Lu she has put it in lament terms for me… If this were Survivor my torch would be snuffed! If this were Amazing Race I would be the last team to check in! If this were The Apprentice, I would be Fired~! But I have one thing to say! This is neither Survivor, nor the Amazing Race or the Apprentice! This is the 5th Annual Global Title Tournament and this is where Under Dogs, like me, beat the odds! This is where I, Kid Krazie, wipe the floor with Ethan Frost to go on to the next round! You may be saying to yourself, "Yea right!" or my favorite, "Krazie!? Who’s that?" My response? I’m Kid Krazie bitch! Ethan Frost has nothing to prove! He is probably sitting their in his igloo rubbing noses with his snowman and eating yellow colored snowcones thinking to himself, "I have got this in the bag!" To All Those That Doubt Me: I hope you underestimate me! I hope you believe the hype surrounding you, because when you see me next I’ll be walking on into the Next Round and you’ll be forced to think about the 6th Annual Global Title Tournament and what you can do next year! Ethan Frost! I will see you in the ring! Now I know the rules stated no nudity, but come on, the rules were made to be broken! Thus I provide you with a shot of the ever-beautiful Lu Lu!
