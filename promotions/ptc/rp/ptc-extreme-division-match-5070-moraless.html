22 April 2004 Moraless RP#1 vs Joey Vendetti A search for greatness SPOILER WARNING If you don't know who was victorious in the Extreme Title Defence don't read the roleplay just yet as it makes reference to it towards the end...thankyou Times were changing in Moraless’ life. He now held a certain sense of urgency about himself and everything that he did. Life wasn’t good, lets be honest life is never good. We are all slaves to the wage, having to work every hour life sent just to survive. Moraless always believed this and he always would, there was no respite in the evening or the weekend as they left far too soon and Monday bought about the daily trudge again. Yet something had changed for Moraless. Job satisfaction. Years of crappy second rate federations had led him around the world and back again, never settling in one place, being forced to live out of suitcases in hotel rooms. He had always thought that just around the corner he would be spotted, by somewhere respectable, a federation who would take him with arms wide open and look after him. Instead he found the Universal Wrestling Federation…again. This time though there was a plan of action. He was going to do something about it and change the world around him, through the resurrection of UWF his career would be given an injection in the arm and he would finally be able to proud of himself. He would be able to over throw his brother’s dominance over him and feel as though something had been accomplished. He would be able to look at himself in the mirror in the mornings, without looking at the large scar on his face and thinking to himself ‘failure’, he would finally be able to look at himself and see the scar to mean hard work, effort and success. However he knew that the take over of UWF would take time, the foundation has been laid and they were stronger than he could have imagined, the numbers flocking to the resurrection’s cause had shocked Moraless but none the less there was a long battle ahead of them. Claiming the UWF tag team titles had been the first step of many but Moraless needed to affirm his position as a main stay in the wrestling world and there was once channel which remained open to him. The Extreme Title in Primetime Central. His last bout had been less than successful, DDT had somehow gotten the better of him. He wished he could say it was because he was injured, he wished he could say that it was because his mind was on other matters. Sadly it wasn’t. The fact of the matter was that DDT had beaten him fair and square, the two had gone to the limit and DDT was the victor case closed end of discussion. He still had to try though. It was who he was, a fighter. “It’ll be easy enough, I’ll just walk in and see what the situation is, then I’ll let them know I want to participate and all will be sweet” spoke Moraless to himself, his eyes remaining forwards and focused on the road ahead of him, the leather steering wheel clutched tightly in his hands. He pulled up outside a large marble building, the Primetime Central insignia engraved on the wall penetrating the skyline for miles. Stepping out of the car Moraless removed the sunglasses from his eyes and looked up the building in awe. He had heard about it before, but had never seen it with his own eyes. Just by looking at the building he knew that if he could pull of something here, then he would be made. Slamming the door behind he and pushing a small button on the car keys to lock the car Moraless strode through the revolving doors into the main foyer of the building. The foyer itself largely represented an expensive hotel, a small fountain in the centre of the room, which barely caught Moraless’ attention as he walked towards the reception desk. “Hello there I’m looking for a fight please,” said Moraless looking down at the brunette receptionist, smiling in a manner, which may very well have been conceived as ‘homicidal’ maniac. It was The receptionist nodded frantically, before moving her hand under her desk slowly, Moraless looked at her curiously not entirely sure what was going on. Moraless was never the brightest man, in the ring he had a presence he knew the space and confines well enough, but anything else was very much touch and go, despite his attempts to be all manly and broody, he just couldn’t pull it off in society. In a burst of activity the receptionist suddenly jumped backwards and swung violently for Moraless, brandishing a large shiny aluminium bat, which missed Moraless by inches as he instinctively darted his head backwards to avoid a concussion. “You want a fight huh, come on then I’ll fight ya” screeched the receptionist, who now bore an amazing likeness to an Amazon, swinging once again trying to indent Moraless’ skull. Reacting in a state of panic Moraless raised his hands, shaking his head, constantly stepping backwards. “Whoa calm down you’ve got the wrong end of the stick here crazy lady, I’m a wrestler. UWF I work there I’m looking for some more work here though…” Moraless trailed off as he dodged one final desperate swing from the receptionist. She paused momentarily, looking at Moraless, looking him square in the eye before nodding in approval, adjusting her blouse and sitting down at her computer once more. “Name” spoke the receptionist, in a manner that did not represent someone who had just attacked the man she was addressing. Moraless stood dumbfounded not knowing how to react entirely. He remained silent for a few moments before the receptionist gave him a darting glare, which quickly forced him to speak. “Moraless” replied Moraless, stepping backwards, being extremely cautious not to annoy the woman who could quite happily belong in an asylum. “No sir I require your full name for the database, not your moniker” The receptionists statement was met by only a sigh of distain from Moraless, he hated his name, always had done always will. “John Edwards” spoke Moraless, once more taking a cautionary step towards the desk as the receptionist typed quickly at her keyboard. She waited for a few moments before her screen burst into activity as information flooded the screen about Moraless. “Ah yes here we are John Edwards, goes by the ring name of Moraless. Has one sibling currently wrestling in RPW under the name of James Edwards, ah I see he was a recent member of our cruiserweight tournament being eliminated in the first round” Loser, thought Moraless as he listened intently to the receptionist, as she reeled off information about his life that even he wasn’t fully aware of. It scared him in a way, how all of this technology held the information to the world, scary stuff. “Currently wrestling for UWF, acquired the tag team championships with his stable member and hall of famer Digital Morality this past Sunday and currently competing in the Extreme Title Division for Primetime Central. So how can I help you Mr Edwards” the receptionist stopped abruptly as Moraless stood with his jaw agape, he’d only won the tag team titles three days ago and already it was on the system. “I’m just looking for a match in the extreme division, I know that my last one didn’t go to plan. A sort of self-redemption thing if you will. Just looking to push myself up the ratings a bit more” Moraless smiled, he thought that it was a better course of action than being aggressive or she might have decided to go all Xena again. “Ah yes” she mumbled in reply as her fingers turned into a flurry of speed as they travelled over the keyboard, until another screen popped up on the monitor clearly displaying all of the information she required. “Here we go, well it seems that at the moment with conflicts with our Cruiserweight tournament and federation matters, the only person currently available is Joey Vendetti from XUW…but I should point out sir.” Moraless cringed at the three small letters at the end of the sentence, his current run going against XUW was nothing to one and he didn’t relish the chance to be mocked once again by them. Running his fingers through his hair Moraless fumbled about the dilemma in his mind, he knew he had to take the match really. He couldn’t let his self-doubt get in the way. “Ok I’ll take it. I’ve got to do this” Moraless spoke as the receptionist tapped more buttons on her keyboard, looking at the screen perplexed, she opened her mouth to try and talk but Moraless simply continued to ramble on, delivering a self motivational speech of sorts. “So what if I lost against DDT, it’s not like I’m facing him this time. A new opponent, a new beginning and most importantly of all, a new drive up the extreme title division” “That’s what I’ve been trying to say!” the receptionists voice bellowed out from her tiny lungs, causing Moraless to step back once more, the thought going around his head that maybe she had wrestled somewhere before, she did work for a corporation of it after all, Moraless eyed her carefully with an air of curiosity. “You don’t need to climb the tables of the division. You are the number one contender” Moraless stared at her before grinning and turning away laughing; it had to be some sort of joke. He had driven for two hundred miles to try and put himself in better standing, to put himself in a better position in Primetime Central. When all along he was at the forefront of one of the divisions. He stopped in the middle of the foyer and scratched his head, he simply didn’t know how to comprehend this, turning around he walked back towards the desk and leaned on it looking at the computer screen. It was true the details were there in black and white. Well colour but you get me. “How did this happen? I came third in the battle royal and lost to DDT, how the hell can I be the number one contender?” asked Moraless, the confusion in his voice was genuine he honestly had no clue how this could have happened. “Well there are a lots of up’s and downs and we would have to describe the whole system we operate on for you to fully understand, but basically it goes like this. When Mike Marvellous lost the extreme title to Sean McClancy, the stipulation added to the match was that he would leave wrestling. This would ultimately have ended in a Sean McClancy vs Shadow match. However due to personal reasons, Shadow asked to leave the division leaving you Mr Edwards as the number one contender” The words flew over Moraless’ head like a jet hitting mach one. “So there is no need for me to have a match, I just wait until my scheduled bout against McClancy and then go for the big one” Moraless spoke grinning from ear to ear and began to pace through the foyer. This had made things a lot simpler. He had firepower now, something to work towards and build up. He could hype himself in the magazines and websites, soon people would know the name of Moraless to mean something “Oh dear” The words stopped Moraless’ thoughts in an instant as he turned to the receptionist with a worried expression on his face, an expression that was reciprocated by the receptionist. “Now you have to promise not to be angry, or tell my boss” Moraless took a few steps towards the desk as the receptionist reclined into her chair, refusing to make eye contact with Moraless, who was looking more like a lost child than anything intimidating. “I’ve err well I’ve done something on the computer and it’s booked the match, the match with Vendetti.” The words from the receptionist barely audible as she spoke. Moraless’ eyes widened with shock and a tiny smidgen of horror. “You have to be kidding me, fix it push some of those buttons and fix it” Moraless said frantically leaning over the desk and tapping random buttons on the keyboard, which resulted in nothing. “I can’t Mr Edwards it’s already been processed, at this minute regulations, stipulations will already be taking place there is nothing I can do to reverse the progress, you face Joey Vendetti next week” ---- 25 April 2004 Everyone's a winner Ever made a big mistake? Have you ever done or said something that you looked back on and just wondered why you did it. Ok so there may have been reasons for it, whether it is something as simple as, you simply didn’t know the ramifications of what you were doing. How you didn’t understand that your decision to do something, could in theory ruin everything forever. Oh well, you’ve gotta laugh haven’t you. That was exactly what Moraless had chosen to do; he had gone and booked himself a match with Joey Vendetti, an Italian dude who apparently had trouble sleeping. It must be something in the water over at XUW, as DDT couldn’t sleep either. Normally there would have been nothing wrong with this. Moraless had never backed down from a challenge in his life, it was just that he’d found something out afterwards. Though a bizarre number of random events he had somehow found himself to be the number one contender to the extreme title, with a guaranteed shot at it against Sean McClancy in May, however he now had to take care of Vendetti. This is what had begun to worry Moraless for many reasons. The first that came to mind was a simple matter of fact, Joey Vendetti had beaten DDT before, DDT had beaten Moraless and as such that would indicate that Joey should in theory not have any trouble in kicking seven shades of shit out of Moraless. Having said that though, there were plenty of theories that haven’t worked out before so why should this one apply. It would apply because Moraless was thinking it and as such he had already subjected himself to the loss. He was a man who up until recently had relied on his self and his self-alone. If things were going well, it would be down to one person and if things were going badly then it would be his own fault. He was still having a hard time breaking this belief. He knew that Joey was going to be more than capable of taking him apart the odds were stacked greatly against him it was what he liked to call a lack of forwards thinking. You see Moraless knew that Joey had beaten DDT anyone who had watched wrestling knew that, they would have seen him multiple times beating DDT week in week out, getting the one-two-three and leaving him in a pool of his own blood. The sad thing about this was that Moraless didn’t watch wrestling, he didn’t like to go around scouting opponents liking the thrill and the draw of not knowing what was going to be coming the following night. That was why he had signed up to the division in the first place. Then he heard Vendetti’s promo. Then he went and watched some old XUW tapes and then he laughed. It wasn’t a nervous laugh either just a simple laugh, what else was there to do? He would go out there and give it his all against Joey Vendetti but what more could he possibly hope to do? To beat him? He didn’t have a chance in hell the odds were totally against him, in a match that he had not even begun to think about preparing for, think about it objectively for a moment. It’s a submissions match, the word submission to Moraless may as well mean piledriver or tap the opponent on the forehead until the get so annoyed they quit, he didn’t have a submission move in his repertoire that he could truly call his own. His only few were moves he’d seen from his brother and on that principal alone he despised using them. Joey had everything going into this bout where as Moraless had nothing, so Moraless had formulated a plan. A plan so evil and genius that you could have called it Hitler. Moraless knew he was going into this match with the odds against him, so he was going to turn the odds in his favour. Or something like that anyway. At night the city came to life, the neon signs blacked out the night sky, a man looking up simply wouldn’t be able to see the stars, or a large neon cowboy smoking on a cigarette or signs that simply read Casino filled the skyline. Moraless stood and looked around his eyes wide with wonder, this was Vegas baby, where anyone could become a winner. And Christ knows Moraless needed to become a winner and soon. You could see the doubt in Moraless’ eyes as he walked down the streets, when he was facing DDT there was a small glimmer of hope, he felt as though he could possibly just about take him to the wire and win now though, things just seemed bleak. He was walking like a beaten man already, his head hung low and his feet scraping across the pavement as he walked various members of casinos dressed up as roman centurions vying for patrons custom, tempting them to part with their money with promises of free drinks and hot buffet. You would have to be stupid to fall for that right? “You look like a winner sir, care to enter and try out our roulette table or maybe craps is your game it doesn’t matter sir because everyone here is a winner and do you know why sir, because tonight if you enter our casino not only will we give you free drinks for the duration of your stay, not only will we give you free access to our buffet bar but we will also give you ten no, twenty dollars worth of chips to get you on your way to becoming a winner” A large man spoke the words extremely quickly flowing off his tongue, clearly having said them countless times over, so often that there was no feeling in the words anymore and they were just there. Moraless looked at the man and shrugged and took an immediate turn right and entered the casino. It wouldn’t turn out to be that bad he had thought to himself, it wasn’t as though he was a compulsive gambler and the promise of free drink proved too good to be true, this was Vegas after all it wasn’t like they were going to be ripping him off any time soon. He walked straight past the numerous gambling machines most of which were video poker in this part of the casino, collecting his free chips on the way and headed straight to the bar, finding a vacant stool he propped himself on it and turned to watch the television, idly taking a drink as a waitress carrying a tray full walked past. You know how sometimes things just seem to happen for a reason and fall into place…yeah this is one of those times. On the screen was the image of Joey Vendetti, cutting his latest promo for the forthcoming match up, Moraless watched with interest, taking in every word he said right until the very end. He then placed down his glass, the clear fluid within it remaining untouched, rose to his feet and left the bar heading into the main casino itself. Something had changed though, for some unknown reason Moraless was smiling, maybe the thought and promise of being left in a pool of blood sounded like something Moraless was into. “Do you know what really intrigues me Joey?” spoke Moraless his voice was totally calm as he spoke, the hubbub of the casino trawling around him, all too eager to empty their pockets of their hard earned money. “ The fact that we are so alike and yet so very different. We all live both live for the fight for violence, we both know that we could go anywhere we wanted and that the skies would be no limit for us but our backgrounds are so very different”. Moraless turned and walked slowly through the casino, a number of flashing lights exploding around him and sobs of grown men who would be forced to walk home this evening instead of driving their car home, foolishly betting it on seventeen in blackjack. As he passes the roulette table he stops and gazes at a couple, in their late twenties. The man is carrying a suitcase looking somewhat lost and perturbed by their surroundings, the woman is simply stood their smiling, glowing you might say, he stomach protruding from her stomach clearly pregnant, a last minute holiday before their lives are taking over by a child something to love and cherish. “Family…heh its funny really, you can live and love but at the end of the day the only people who will really love you are your family, the ones who are there at the beginning to the end. Except you are the exception to that rule. You were sold by your own father to increase his financial gain or standing. You as you said were a puppet so regardless of what happens when we face in the ring Joey I have to tell you something.” Moraless spoke as he continued to watch the woman, not taking his eyes off the bump and the life that was brewing inside. “Thank you. I know that this maybe hard to explain but I owe you thanks. I grew up without a family, my true family anyway, being shipped from foster parent to foster parent from the age of six. You made me realise something though. Before then, before the foster family, I was with my parents and my brother a proper family. We argued as families do, but it was never bad. Even when dad was ill it was never bad. A family to the end.” Moraless’ face had hardened now or at least tried to, his eyes were welling up and his bottom lip shaking as he turned away from the couple and began to walk through the casinos labyrinth of one-armed bandits. “I always hated them for leaving me, I didn’t care that they had died I hated them. They left James and me alone in a world we barely knew. I hated them everyday for leaving me with so much pain, that I tried to cut it out of me, thinking and hoping that I would be able to be with them if I just ended it. Then you came along Joey and made me realise something. At least my parents loved me. I can still remember some one Christmas, it had been snowing for days, me and James had a snowman building competition, I won but James swore he did, it was getting colder as dad called us in. We sat round the fire and opened up our presents. I don’t remember what they were but that’s not the point. The point is that we were together and we were happy. But you I guess you don’t understand that Joey. I’m not going to explain it to you, because you haven’t been in a position to know. I lost my family, you didn’t you were sold off to the highest bidder. My family loved me until their very deaths, yours didn’t even care enough to get a receipt.” Moraless let the words rest for a few seconds, considering what he had said. In some peoples eyes it would have seemed harsh but this was real life and Joey had given Moraless a lifeline, something to stop the hurt to stop the pain. “The truth hurts doesn’t it Joey.” Said Moraless as he slowly sauntered towards the roulette wheel, a smile on his face now the weight of the world lifted from his shoulders. “Regardless of what happens when we face each other, should I lose I know that the loss that I feel will be nothing to the pain that you feel inside. The lack of love, I don’t know if I could sleep at night.” Moraless looks down at the table and watches the ball spin around, placing his twenty dollar chip on number twenty-four. “No more bets please” the croupier called as Moraless watched the ball spin around and land snugly in the pocket of number twenty-four. Moraless simply grinned, collected his winnings and left the casino. I guess some things are true. Everyone’s a winner in Vegas.
