Sidewalk Leper

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There was something very wrong about what he had done. Studying the sidewalk leper, the world didn't seem so important anymore. Broken glass and a splintered pallet was this man's legacy. - Dead leaves and trash only another fragment of his narrow ambition. He turned as he pulled a newspaper over his body with the crunch of a page. The news print was now old and faded as Johnson studied to read it, he knew after he was gone he'd derive much the same fate. The day's next chore meant little and had him questioning the essence of a bloom. It was all more temptation than it felt like tangible substance. As if beauty were a hologram and we were merely contending for a piece of the unrealized enticement. The bum grunted in drunken splendor before he disappeared into his lackadaisical slumber. An archaic member of the gallery of lives lost to displeasure and dissatisfaction. Reposed next to the vagrant was Johnson's yearning. It lay in front of the next trash can deflated like a collapsed lung. It was nothing more than another piece of the human debris that made the stymied eye shot. And it all so reminded him of his own inheritance from life's little bag of misfortune. His betrothed misplaced, his psychosis a broken mirror and his rawest sacrifice yet - his arm - a reminder of his dirtiest sentiments. For the sake of conformity it was how he was viewed. To a lesser extent it was how he felt. Johnson stepped over the booze hound and entered the backdoor of a clinic. With its door cracked it took him little effort to pull it and enter. He frowned after realizing it had been ransacked most likely by stoners who broke in stealing what syringes and medicinal samples were there. A scale laid under a dented file cabinet as charts and graphics on the walls were torn or left hanging by their edges. The scent made Johnson pause as he casually paced inside. He smelled what he concluded was a cocktail of piss and feces on the floor. After his grandmother found him, at the always curious age of twelve, smoking his first cigarette he learned that not only was he in the wrong but he was going to be eternally punished had he did it again. Even though it tasted good he was obliged to be accommodating to his subjugation. "What are you doing boy?" She exclaimed as she rubbed her hands on her apron. "Don't you know if you do that again you're going to hell?" He was content that at least she didn't see what he had done to Ashley, A.J. Palmer's ex-girlfriend. Even though he felt a sense of shame in taking Palmer’s girl he didn’t feel for Palmer himself. This was a man who had attempted to smear his name in Global and in the “community” or black market. Johnson felt his contribution to A.J.’s disgrace was just, despite the means used. It was the proverbial eye for an eye syndrome that drove him insane as he waited hours after each show resolved. During this time he contemplated how his enemy would be equaled in his discourtesies. He did so in front of a mirror plotting betwixt his ingestion of his Neuroleptics medication. This was a psychiatric drug that kept delusions at bay. Regardless as he glanced at his shell he detected outside of his palpable reflection he no longer felt occupied by anything fond or genuine. Softness wasn't a feeling any more than it was an irretrievable noun that made him angry. Palmer only added to the angst as the avalanche of emotions’ return to the pit that bred his worst sensations. “To go back now would be an incredible contradiction.” Eventually, Rob would thumb the orange bottle into a pocket and wash his face with cool water. He’d tell himself the next day would improve upon his last and he wouldn’t have to venture beyond the tolerance of the public. He would fake a smile before grabbing his duffle bag and exit the building without consequence. All he could do was ignore the anxiety and cut another inch off his body. He knew what would lay under the bandages. And like a warm child he wanted to hide inside its cool interior and fantasize. Maybe he would remove it and realize he was mistaken or even forgiven by the supernatural hand of God. He wanted the shadow of his pain to relieve him of the confusion. He reached the final layer of fabric before a reluctant pause. The scissors were ready to deliver him the news as they glinted from the weak sunlight that crawled inside the room. He palmed the old layers in his hand as he extended his arm out as if to give it to someone. Had he, in his bitter zeal, been right all along? Or was he now the outcast? "I want you to hold this." He whispered in his solitude. The thought evoked his best of intentions. If only there were someone to tell him everything would be okay. A white lie would be enough for Rob. Perhaps a magical hand would show him he wasn't alone in his trial. The unoccupied room consisted of little more than bloodied medical instruments that had been cast astray on the top of a black lacquer cabinet top. Cotton swabs and syringes found themselves recluse in partial darkness as a stainless steal microscope looked on indifferent. He was unwashed and still as he had appeared in the conclusion of the Dallas barn save the medical tape on his right arm. For most of the elapse time he took shelter between the drifters and vagabonds. He stayed with them until confusion passed and he was able to find a clinic where he could use some of its instruments to get him back to where he needed to be for his next contest. He grumbled as he turned his head placing the bottle of alcohol on the cabinet. It was a trinket from those he hid amongst. Johnson tried to remember the last time he felt so alone; So removed socially that he had to create his own world and subvert his opponent's loved ones. It wasn't his regular routine obviously, though it became a technique that would be difficult for him to drop. As he cut the gauze, Rob noticed part of the creme linen of his toga had matted to his skin. He delicately peeled it away noticing little pain. Toughened blood fell away like red and black flurries as his arm felt the air kiss his skin. One agonizing moment crawled by hopeless minute as the tape was removed. As he poked his scarred hand with a loose index finger, he found himself amused by his peers. While he was left behind considering the implication of his arm, the Prime time elite was out there preparing for their next match. GTT6 offered him a stage. Though it was more than that it offered him the chance to show just how far outside he could be. The appeal of being the greatest villain in PTC history was the sincerest of accolades. The task would be rising from the obscurity of PTC fandom and upsetting the much-hyped ego masturbators. It wouldn’t be his favorite of pursuits but he would enjoy the task nonetheless. The tournament was obviously lacking a Rob Johnson. “I’ll poison their tropical fish with a laxative.” The gauze was finally freed and he allowed it to fall to the ground. Rob just wanted to see what he had become. What he had created and if there were any way for it to be fixed again. His wrist was the first part of his arm to undress as he noticed the scaling of the skin. White turned to pink and redness took growing precedent. He turned away knowing what he tried to deny since his heat with Palmer commenced. He was without regard - even for himself. A bum scampered through the back door as he swished a Listerine of gin around in his mouth. He spit it on the floor disdainfully in the entrenched clinic. Despite noticing Rob he only grabbed the caramel bottle of Jack Daniels. He dared Johnson to say anything as he wiped the lip of the glass with the faded denim of his jacket before he drank as if it were his last before his scheduled execution. Johnson reached for a single box of medical gloves. He removed a blue one before pulling it onto his burned hand. The latex stuck to his skin like an adhesive encouraging him to stand again and make an exit. He hid his arm inside a fold in his tunic as he approached the door. The bum seized the opportunity to reclaim his bed as he crawled on top and drifted to sleep.
