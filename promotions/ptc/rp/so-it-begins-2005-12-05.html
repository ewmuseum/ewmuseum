So It Begins…

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The crusade begins… The road ahead appears so far from where I stand… But I can grasp the glory as if it were right in front of me… Outside the warm cozy atmosphere slowly changed into a dark eerie cold sensation that sent a thrill through the body of Devil D. Graves. A day before debuting in the opening round of the GTT5 Tournament, Graves makes his last few hours in New York very dull as he walks through the city as the sky begins to cry snow. Placing the hood from his sweater over his head, Graves reaches into his pocket and retrieves a pack of smokes and a lighter to enjoy one last smoke before heading off to the airport. The sweet taste of nicotine…nothing beats its. To make a day gone sour turn around, all I need is a Player’s cigarette and the history written before passes me by. Stopping near the McDonald’s awaiting Fredrick to swing by any minute to take him to the airport; Graves reaches once again into his pocket and pulls out a sheet of paper. On that paper shows the "Hype" for Grave’s opponent Burkhard Diederek Nazi symbol and all. Grave’s begins to shake his head and begins to laugh appearing to open his mouth before a limo pulls in front of him. Enjoying one last sweet drag, Devil tosses the butt into a small puddle on the road and stands up. Opening the door Grave’s does not find the empty backseat of the limo vacant, but a large man sitting in his usual spot. Peeking into the limo Grave’s catches a glimpse of non other than long time business partner and ring leader of a crime organization in New York, The Advocate. Stepping inside the limo, the Advocate smiles as Graves takes a seat next to him, recently a few months back the two were disgusted at each other not talking nor arranging any business matters. Though after a bitter sweet loss at Global Conflict, The Advocate arouse once again as a deep companion of Devil, joining the side of a long time friend in hopes of regaining championship gold in the CwA. So he has decided to join my efforts towards walking away with the GTT5 Championship, I knew the other offer was to tempting for him to pass up. Looking into his eye I can tell the excitement and adrenaline boiling deep inside of him. The Advocate was very tall man with a sinister look to his face. Only having one eye as a result to a teenage accident in a factory, the Advocate wears a patch over his eye giving him a pirate type look along with small scars around his bald head. Along with being very tall and quite strong, he is also a master of negotiation…convincing anyone of any nature to do his bidding or whatever he requests. A man with a passion of poetry and literature The Advocate can be explained as a man with two opposite personalities. Advocate: So…now we add this to our list of goals? Graves: Yes, the work to achieve this so called honor will be a long trial; errors are something we cannot afford. Shayne Stylez has also decided to grace himself into this tournament and I’ll be damned if shows me up. Advocate: There will be no problem there, you have defeated him before I am positive he will no be a problem with you’re plans of domination. Graves: But with new faces come new mysteries and challenges, much like my opponent I am facing first. Advocate: Burkhard Diederek, A German fellow associated with the Nazi party. Graves: You know of him? Advocate: I did some researching last night, tough fellow but low when it comes to thinking and brain skills. Graves: I’m surprised they allow such a character of beliefs caused by a man who was jealous of Jews. A joke I think it is, the Nazi’s being cowards it could only make this much oh so easy for myself. If you look at the Nazi’s you realize all they were was an army of brainwashed fools following a small skin and bones man with a bad hair cut because he felt threatened and scared. All that I can imagine that Burkhard is the exact same, a man demising another race to feel superior because he lacks in other skills, whatever they be this must make him feel powerful and respected. But to me it makes me think of himself as a shallow, shallow man. Advocate: Disgusts me. Graves: I know I am an asshole, I know that you are not so easy going around the edges yourself. But one thing that makes somebody lower than anybody is a man driven on the advice from Adolf Hitler. I promise him after tomorrow that he will be better off shaving his head and drawing swastikas on paper. Advocate: Care for a drink? Jack on the rocks? Graves: Sounds good… As The Advocate hands over a glass to Graves, Devil begins to look off onto the street and starts t think. This tournament will be mine…there is no one that I fear entering this tournament and no one that can stop me from achieving my chance at eternal glory. No overgrown giant, no skank of a woman, nor no Nazi to get in the way of my path at becoming the GTT5 Champion. With the Advocate accepting my offer to join me outside of the CwA and join me on my crusade, I feel that much more confident. Though it is only the beginning the mastermind device being planned will be kept quite…words shall be spoken in later dates…and more actions will be revealed as time progresses. The real Devil D. Graves has not shown his total aggression just yet, what is coming for each individual placed in front of me will feel pain that they have never felt before. Burkhard, I know tonight you will fall asleep very confident as I am sure most of you’re kind is…but as you drift off into the land of nod I know the vision of my face will haunt you. The sight of looking into my eyes may just frighten you as much as looking into the eyes of Lucifer himself, though I am sure being a Nazi asshole like yourself meaningless dreams like that will not affect you. But when you step into that ring and realize that anguish you are feeling at the hands of me…you shall wish that the dream you had was a sign to stay away from me…to quit while you have the chance and not be embarrassed in front of you’re fellow brethrens. The mockery I will make of you will amuse myself and others while you’re Nazi buddies will look down on you with shame. The clock will soon strike, and the GTT5 will begin, all that you can hope for is an oh so clever upset over me, being considered an underdog myself I know what the feeling would be like. But as Round 2 rolls around I will make an impact like no other…GTT5 and PrimeTime can only wish they got their hands on me a very long time ago.
