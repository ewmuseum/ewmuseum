Date: 2006-01-08 We find ourselves today with the view of what seems to be an old fish tank completely consuming our field of vision. It is positioned on a breakfast bar surrounded by various pieces of clutter like torn open envelopes with threatening letters from finance companies emerging from them, old TV guides, news papers and celebrity gossip magazines along with a dead plant. The water of this fish tank is dense and murky but we can still make a slight outline of a plastic Victorian diver figure in its silly copper diving helmet. The pump filter makes a warming hum that creates bubbles, which makes the water comfortingly ripples. As we become aware of the surroundings it becomes obvious that once again we're in the hovel that is Red Rock's flat. It is dark and although it is only 2pm in the afternoon the room is almost in complete darkness, this is due to the thick clouds accumulating outside shielding any light from Red Rock's only window in his living room. A very cold atmosphere brings a dark and depressing feeling lingering through out the whole open space. As our attention is drawn back to the unclean murky fish tank we see Red Rock and Boolie bent over so their eyes are level with the rim of the tank. Red Rock as well as Boolie seems to be staring at something afloat on the surface. It looks like a poor little gold fish that has died from neglect on our anti hero's part. Red Rock sustains an expression of sorrow as well as Boolie who appears equally saddened at their loss…or so it would seem. Red Rock: And you're SURE this is only food in the house? Boolie: Absolutely. Red Rock claps his hands together then rubs them against each other just as a hearty grin replaces his sadden expression. Boolie: So shall we bake it or fry it? Red Rock: Well…we don't actually have any gas or electricity. The shit faces… they cut it off this morning. Boolie: How much do we owe them then? Red Rock: Probably enough to get gang raped by British gas. Red Rock sucks his lips in and taps his chin as the cogs and gears in his head chug along to think of possible alternatives to cook the fish. Then unexpectedly an idea shoots through Red Rock's head like an arrow fired by a careless child with a crossbow. Red Rock: AH HA! I know! I got that old camping stove some where in my room, I think it still has some gas left in it. That'll work better than any Lean Mean Fat inhaling fuck machine…or what ever it's called. He disappears through his bedroom door and pushes it shut behind him giving us a view of a "The Cure" poster. Boolie stands patiently by the fish tank but it's not long before he gets bored. So to cure his boredom he begins trying to cup his hands around the dead fish to retrieve it from the tank. He splashes about trying to get hold of the slippery body but he can't quite manage to emerge the decomposing pet and all he gets is fish shit all over his hands. On that note Red Rock flings his bedroom door open before stepping out holding a 25 year old gas fueled camping stove proudly under his right arm. Red Rock: Shazam! I found the little bastard! I'm so proud of it, I'll put my fucking name on it. Now let's get George Foreman on that dead fish! Boolie: Can we make fish Fingers? Boolie doesn't seem convinced that this antique will provide satisfaction judging by the way he is blankly staring at it. Red Rock secretly is thinking the same thing but is trying his best to maintain an ounce of optimism. Red Rock: Righty-O, pass me a pot! Boolie dithers over to the sink where there is STILL a repulsively large pile of filthy moldy plates stacked on each other. Boolie's face creases up at the thought of putting his hand anywhere the rancid blue fury mold growing on them. Red Rock: HURRY UP BOOLIE! Fucks sake I think my stomach is actually beginning to digest it's self! His stomach begins to turn and his cheeks blow up followed by a verse of prominent wretches. How ever Boolie manages to refrain from puking everywhere and pulls the rancid pot out from the pile. The cold water raps on the bottom of the metal pot, filling it up. It then comes to Red Rock's attention that the color of the water. Red Rock: Awww bloody hell! Red Rock doesn't quite earn enough at AWC to make ends meet and with all his arrears and county court judgments on his name he had no choice but to seek a second job. He has been searching for a second job for quite a while now, but because of his wrestling commitments there wasn't a place around that would touch him when they found out how much he is in and out of the country. However with this second job there is no office or counter to stand behind. All he needs is himself and his mobile phone. But it does have its draw backs, he does get woken up quite frequently in the early hours of the morning by sad lonely repressed husbands who are trying to hide their homosexuality from their wives, he also gets the odd wives phoning up wondering what this £3 a minute number is showing up on their phone bill. Red Rock has now officially "outed" 4 repressed husbands. Red Rock: He was a nice man. Right where were we! Red Rock takes hold of the little knob on the side of the prehistoric contraption, then his attempts to rotate it but it appears to be jammed from years of rust. Red Rock: Piece of shit, It's fucked! It won't move! Boolie: Stand aside mortal and let me have a go! Boolie jokingly pushes Red Rock and flexes his muscles, and for a man of 7'7, 450lbs his muscles aren't really all that toned. Boolie takes some humorous large deep breaths before grabbing the jammed knob. He attempts to turn it but Boolie doesn't get much joy either. Boolie: Wow, that's one stiff knob! Red Rock: teh heh! You said stiff knob! Red Rock giggles with his hand cheekily over his face. Boolie: He, he, he! So I did! Red Rock and Boolie giggle a little bit but only for a few moments. Boolie positions his feet a suitable distance apart and grabs hold of the knob once more. He takes one more deep breath and with one burst of strength and power… SNAP! Red Rock: FUCK! Boolie: whoops… Red Rock's jaw drops as his slaps his hand against his forehead. Any hope of a fish dinner has been destroyed. Red Rock: You fucking broke it! YOU COCK! Red Rock throws his hands around the now broken gas stove and hurls the piece of junk furiously across the room. It connects with the adjacent wall with a huge thud then it bounces off then lands on the floor on top of a pile of pizza boxes and old dated newspapers. He mutters some inaudible sentences to himself, which Boolie can't quite hear but it is obvious that Red Rock has entered one of his infamous little girly bed wetter sulking fits. Don't forget that you're in GTT5, now you don't want people to find out that you really are a pussy little bastard do you? Red Rock shakes his head from side to side to gesture the answer no. Oh I HATE that faggot with his good female relations ability…and good luck! Red Rock folds his arms and pouts his lips as he ponders about his round 3 opponent. You see Red Rock has never met William Gwynn, but he is secretly grown envious of the man. He is better looking and is better with the ladies; Red Rock hasn't had a proper date in years. Avatar is said to have memberships with fancy country clubs, where as Red Rock can't even afford the gas to cook a dead fish.
