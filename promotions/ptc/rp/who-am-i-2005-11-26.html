Who am I?

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Who am I? That's something that some of you will ask. That's something that my first round opponent in this tournament is probably asking. The simple answer is: Danielle Lynn Northsky, the daughter of Daniel and Lynn Northsky. I was born on the Rosebud Tribal Reservation in the Black Hills of South Dakota. I'm a full blooded Sicangu Oyate, or as you would call them the Sicangu Lakota Sioux. But that's the simple answer. The hard answer is that I'm a woman, and a wrestler. But most of you wouldn't know that. Especially some of them in my home federation, Global. You see, as far as Global is concerned, I'm still almost a nobody. I've a former Platinum Champion. I've got some impressive streaks, and then again I've got some that aren't all that impressive. I've had a little over a 1 year career since making my break into the CCWA from the lower, independent federations. When the CCWA folded I was called their rookie sensation. In the course of a short month I had not only gained their North American Title, but I never lost it. It still sits on the mantle over my fake fireplace in the house in Las Vegas that me and my boyfriend have been shacked up in. I held Global's Platinum title for over a month, even winning the match that merged it with the Television and Cruiser Weight Titles… The best night of my career. I beat 5 other people including Mike Biddle, Dawn MacFarlane, Elia Smith… and a couple of others that are really not for me to remember. But all in all, that night I proved to myself that I have what it takes for the big leagues. But people like Zenith, who sit on an ill-gotten title at the top of Global, still haven't noticed who I am. That's all fine and dandy. I'm talking about a guy who runs around with a blonde on his arm, but pays more attention to his male body guards then her. Sometimes you have to wonder about that. I was hoping that by entering this tourney, I'd be able to get away from Global and it's politics for a while. Guess I was wrong. First round I get a chance at something that I've always wanted. A shot at a world champion. And since he's in my own fed, it'll be even sweeter. Showdown in Tokyo comes up, and the prize that night would be a shot at the World Champion too. A nice workup, a nice build up. Now all I need to do is come off those ropes, high over the ring. Twisting and turning in the air… Until I land on Zenith's chest. As I find my momentum stopping, hook his leg and lay there and pray that the referee hits that magic third count. It's not just about climbing the ladder of GTT5, it's also about proving myself. And my first test is a world champion. I've got the tools, I've got the abilities. Now I just need to do it.
