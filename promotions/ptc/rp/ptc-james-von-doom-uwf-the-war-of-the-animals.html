James von Doom (UWF) - The War of the Animals

2 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, July 31, 2005 The scene opens in a plush meadow somewhere in the Northwest. It looks like he is in Yellowstone National Park. James von Doom and his mysterious girlfriend who is, of course, hidden via a shadow. James looks up and the shadow is coming from altocumulus duplicatus clouds that are hovering overhead. Girlfriend: James. James turns to his mysterious girlfriend. JV: Yes honey? His girlfriend turns to him with a smile. Girlfriend: Where are all the animals? James laughs out loud over hearing this. JV: (points) They're out there. She looks and, sure enough, there are animals out there. There are mountain lions, black bears, grizzly bears, coyotes, and other various assorted mammals. She turns back to James. Girlfriend: But they're men in costumes! JV: No they're not! His girlfriend shakes her head. Girlfriend: No. LOOK! She points and James looks over. The mammals are all on their feet and talking to one another. James smiles and turns back to his girlfriend. JV: Yeah, so? Girlfriend: But animals don't speak! This is obviously one of your stupid tricks, isn't it James? James shrugs his shoulders. JV: No. Not really. Girlfriend: So you're not playing me for a fool? You didn't set all of this up to amuse me or to amuse other people? James shakes his head. JV: No. James' girlfriend puts her hands on her hips. Girlfriend: Really? So this isn't just another of your stupid wrestling gimmicks? James smiles and motions toward the animals. JV: Well, why don't we go ask them if I had something to do with this? James' girlfriend nods her head. Girlfriend: That's not a bad idea. James grabs his girlfriend by the hand and they start walking over to the animals. They walk across the meadow to where the animals are talking but they get stopped by a badger and a raccoon. They are armed with slingshots and AK-47s. Badger: HALT HUMANS! Raccoon: Why are you here? James scratches his head. JV: Uhm, because we're on vacation and we... Badger: Want to see the animals, eh? James nods his head. JV: Yes. The Raccoon growls in frustration. Raccoon: Puny humans. Do you know how much people gawking at us upsets us? James shakes his head. JV: No. Badger: We feel like sideshow freaks! Raccoon: YEAH~! We are not freaks! We are animals! And we deserve respect! James points to the AK-47s. JV: What are those for? The Badger looks at his AK-47 and smiles. Badger: These are for the war. James raises an eyebrow. JV: What war? The Raccoon smiles. Raccoon: The war between the animals. James looks excited. JV: Oooooo...animal war. Badger: For thousands of years the four tribes of animals have waged a secret war. The tribes of the land, sea, sky, and the reptiles/amphibians have been living in relative peace for years but now the tribes must go to war. There has been a desecration of the woods. No one knows who did it. It wasn't your human species though. Now, the creatures of the world must fight until only one tribe remains. The strongest, the wisest, the most cunning. That tribe will then battle the all-powerful humans for the planet Earth. James nods his head. JV: Can I watch? The Badger thinks for a moment and then nods his head. Badger: Of course you can. James gets all excited. His girlfriend, however, looks unamused. Girlfriend: James, you're fucking crazy! Only you would want to watch animals blow each other up! JV: Well, they show this kind of shit all the time on the Discovery Channel and I didn't hear you complaining about it then. His girlfriend growls in frustration and storms off toward their picnic blanket. James waves goodbye to the animals and heads off after her. They sit down and then watch the animals prepare for war. JV: This is so going to pwn! James' Girlfriend rolls her eyes. Girlfriend: Shut up nerd boy. Then, it begins. A giant flock of many various kinds of birds flies over head. They starts dive bombing the mammals! The Raccoons and Badgers take up flanking positions and start shooting at the birds with their assault rifles. JV: Wow! This is great! The fish then rise up out of the river behind them and the flying fish start diving bombing the mammals. Flying Fish: Dive bomb formation! The other fish start attacking both the birds and the mammals. The fish have assault rifles too and are being led by the sharks, who have bazookas and are blasting at the bears, who are firing back on their position from bunkers with RPGs and heavy machine gun fire. The birds drop poop bombs onto both tribes but are bombarded by heavy cannon fire from the fishes who have cannons set up on the side of the river. JV: HOLY SHIT~! IT'S LIKE WORLD WAR III! The Bears starts commanding the mammals. Black Bear: All batteries fire! Fire! The reptiles then slither out of the woods and the amphibians flank attack the fishes from behind. Anaconda: STRIKE NOW~! The turtles throw shell shaped grenades at the fish and the snakes choke out any creature that comes near. Some kamikaze seagulls try to dive bomb James and his girlfriend but get hit by mortar fire from the bighorn sheep. The mammals use a giant slingshot and fling a giant whale shark into the air. The creature opens its mouth and gobbles down birds like it was nothing. Girlfriend: This is really exciting. JV: You see! I told you! Girlfriend: But what does this have to do with wrestling? They start to cuddle. JV: Nothing. Nothing at all. The sound of gunshots and the deaths of many animals do not phase them. The sounds of kissing, a gentle breeze, and the sound of a pigeon being incinerated by a chainsaw fill the summer air. Great White Shark: Flank'em! Flank'em! The fish try to flank the mammals but get Shit Bombed by the birds! *BOOM~!* Suicide bomber Marmots have just taken out the Amphibian/Reptile Command Bunker! Rattlesnake: Those bastards! We must eat their rodents! The snakes go into a rodent feeding frenzy and eat every rat, mouse, squirrel, and small rodent in sight. But then, a silence hits the battlefield. The four tribes forgot about the fifth tribe. James' girlfriend freaks out when she sees... Girlfriend: EEK~! INSECTS! The tribe of the Insects rises up from the ground and attacks the other tribes. Army Ants go after the mammals, beetles buzz bomb the fish, and tarantulas are slingshot into the air to eat the birds. JV: WHOA! This is fucking awesome! Girlfriend: No it is not! I want to go home! JV: And I'm going home because I'm not going to win! James and his girlfriend watch the mad orgy of violence until it is done. A few hours later, there are only three animals standing. The bugs are all squashed, the fish are fried, and the birds have had their heads cut off and their bodies have been sent to the nearest KFC factory to be consummed by the American public. All that is left are a Grizzly Bear with an RPG Launcher and 2 small Coral Snakes who coordinated a grenade assault on the fish. They got stuck behind enemy lines. They team up and circle the bear. They then fly through the air and try to bite his face! But the bear claws them both at the same time, slicing them into pieces. The mammals have won the war and will face the humans for control of the world! The bear raises his paws in victory! Grizzly Bear: HAHAHA! Soon the world will belong to the mammals! Just then, a hunter shoots the bear in the head, thus winning the war for the humans as James wakes up. He looks at his clock. JV: Dude, how the hell am I gonna explain this to PTC management? He then pulls off his dream helmet and stops the VCR. The helmet is attached to the VCR via a wire. James then presses rewind and goes back to sleep. The promo you have just seen is the video of his dream and is now released for your enjoyment. The scene fades out. Cut.
