Night Sweats & Insomnia

23 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-23 Night Sweats and Insomnia Foreword: Enclosed Thoughts Ever since the beginning of round one of the GTT4, I have been feeling something inside of me, something taking over. Every time I try to breathe, it gets harder and harder to catch it, every time I try to ease it away, it feels like a boulder of pressure putting more and more weight onto my shoulder blades. I feel like I can’t stop it, but in a way, the pain is gratifying. In round one, the boulder was just small enough to annoy me like a gnat buzzing about in my private dwellings. It bugged me (pardon the pun) so much, that I had to take care of it, and thus I swept the rug from underneath the body of Venneman Pendragon in an exciting duel to the demise of one of our hopes of advancing. But now round two has emerged, and I’m starting to see that I’m climbing ever so higher above sea level. Soon enough, when the actual fight arrives, I will be at step two of this experience. But with the next level, a new weight gets put onto this boulders pressure. As the excitement and adrenaline sets in, so does the pressure of knowing that I have to perform at my best to sweep the rug under this new opponent as well. I was the underdog by a long shot in round one, but did that stop me? No, it didn’t make me shudder one bit. So what would be so different about this time around? Nothing, I was still not thought of as a threat in this chain, but slowly and surely maybe all of that would change into an advantage. The more they kept downing me, the harder I would fight and the stronger I would emerge. Soon enough, the boulder’s pressure would become even harder, and I would fight to survive it and conquer it. But, for now, this was only round two. I couldn’t get too far ahead of myself, for focusing on the future could make a disaster of the present. I had to focus on this match with Jonathan Sail. Surely, if I underestimated him like everyone underestimates me, he could easily take me out of commission and advance. And when I look at Sail’s biography, I see a load of star quality behind him, so I couldn’t take him for a fool, unless of course I wanted to make a fool out of myself. Sail had tournament experience dating back to 1998, around the same time I actually started getting into the wrestling business. But after that fact, we had nothing in common as it came to tournament experience. He had more of it, it was that plain and simple to see. It brought a question to my mind, could my match experiences equal and perhaps even be better than his tournament ones? All of this thinking was surely putting me into a daze. I needed to somehow get my mind off of it, but also couldn’t lose focus. I needed to get some sleep, but damn this adrenaline for keeping my eyes peeled open and my heart racing. Damn this headache, I need some aspirin. Scene One: A Caffeine Rush “Ag, my head is killing me.” :: The scene opens up with a sleep deprived looking Iron Lung. Bags hang under his eyes, looking to the point where someone could claim that they gave him a cheap punch. His eyes were bloodshot, although you couldn’t tell as much with his fluorescent red contacts in. His hair was a mess, his clothes disorderly and wrinkled, and the yawning of his jaw told more than just a tale of fatigue. He had been keeping himself awake for hours now. He originally started getting his first encounters with the sandman around 9 or 9:30 Prime Meridian (PM), but now the time had stretched almost into the witching hours of midnight and 1 Ante Meridian (AM). Surely a cause for aggravation, Lung snaps his fingers, hoping to perform some sort of magical sleeping fix on his holistic self. :: “Snap out of it dude, it’s only a match. Why am I having such a hard time getting to sleep?” :: The fact that earlier in the night, Lung drank a couple of Double Shots, didn’t really help the situation. Now he was trying to sleep off what energized him in the first place, and despite his lack of effort to defeat the caffeine rushing through his blood stream, the superstar kept focus on trying to gain some much needed shut eye. :: “Damn it! I can’t believe I’m putting so much stress on myself. Maybe it’s because that it’s a bigger stage than I’m used to, maybe it’s because I have no damn clue about my opponent or what he is capable of doing to me and my shots at moving on to the next level. It could be a lot of things, but whatever the deciding factor is, it sure as hell is driving me crazy!” :: Snapping his fingers more and making a fist to lightly tap it against his forehead, trying to drive the rush out of his brain, our subject is becoming more and more frustrated with every moment of waking that passes by. Nervousness might have also come into play as to why he couldn’t get sleep, for he wanted to gain the edge on his opponent so badly, that he didn’t want to waste any minute devoted to what he called his “personal time away from work”, or “p-time” for short. :: “Okay, enough is enough. I’m going to close my eyes, and by the time I open them again, I better be asleep and in a dream. Someplace, somewhere, with some hot babe next to me. Yes brain, I’m shutting you down for the night and letting my imagination run wild, so you’d better cooperate! This is a one-way ticket to la-la land, and I ain’t talking about Los Angeles!” :: Finally, a peaceful silence falls over the room as Lung begins to meditate. Soon enough, the space around him is transformed into a Zen-like aura, with our main character’s breathing becoming more eminent. Slowly taking breaths in and out and trying to channel out the pressure of the headache and the caffeine fix, Lung is finally able to close his eyes and enjoy serenity. But was it for the good of the night, or would more disturbances be in the mix? :: Scene Two: A Sugar High “Son of a bitch!” :: Awake again moments later, and not a moment was wasted on a dream. In fact, as soon as Lung’s eyes were closed, they shuttered and opened wide open again like a dysfunctional door that you would see in cartoons. It was almost no use, Dreamland seemed an eternity away for our hero. It seemed as if he were just floating on the highway for mile after mile, but his destination was still days away. The best way to describe his feeling right now, like a road trip from coast to coast, a long and never ending winding road. And when he finally got to the finish line, it would be time to turn around and come right back to his homeland again. :: “Why are you doing this to me? Why can’t I shake this off?” :: The questions kept running through Lung’s mind as he interrogated himself, all that was missing was the spotlight shining on his face to make him sweat. But it wasn’t needed, for his eyes were already so full of passion and his heart full of speed, that his breathing became quickened and a bit of sweat glistened from the top of his forehead near his hairline. He was overstressed and overworked, to put it briefly. But would this come to play in a matter of days when he had to step inside of the ring, or would he conquer this quest and actually advance to the next stage of this tournament? Only time would tell. But for now, Lung was concentrated on one thing and one thing only, advancing to the sack. :: “Ugh, I’m so tired. Why can’t I close my eyes? Am I that worried about Sail, or am I just playing mind games with myself to make me more ready for him? I can’t believe that I’m not asleep yet!” :: His thoughts were becoming irrational, as his eyes seemed to zigzag back and forth across his confinement. They widened with intensity, but Lung kept trying so hard to shut them, only to find out that they wouldn’t close under his power, not naturally anyway. :: “That’s it, a sleep aid! Why didn’t I think of that? Surely, that will let me get to sleep. Huzzah for the miracles of science!” :: The situation was getting desperate. Not only was our subject not making a bit of sense, but he was also making up words that would seem to only be found on some sort of Medieval Times show. But what would result from taking this swiftly acting drug. Perhaps he would finally dream, or would the dreams become hallucinations once again for Iron Lung? :: Scene Three: A Quick Fix, The Final Result “Here’s to finally getting some shut eye.” :: Looking at a box of sleeping solutions entitled “Doze-Away”*, Lung chuckles a bit as he reads the bottom of the brand’s logo, which hilariously quotes the motto of “It’ll knock you on your ass.” :: “Hmm, maximum strength. I like the way you were thinking when you thought up this product, whatever your name was.” :: Taking the bottle out of its box casing, Lung unsnaps the “child’s proof” cap and shakes one gel cap out into the palm of his opposite hand. Putting the capsule into his mouth, he presses the cap back on and places the bottle back into his cabinet, making sure to dispose of the box in his trash bucket. :: “Look out sleep time, here I come!” :: Finally swallowing the gel cap, Lung’s vision before us starts to get a bit fuzzy and blurred. Waving his hand across his eyes, we see it moving in slow motion as a slurred “Oh, shit” took it’s time to our ears. Now seeing waves of different colors in front of him, we start to sway from one side to the other as our hero tries to make his way to a nearby couch. But the two feet that he has to step seems to keep stretching like a never ending hallway. :: “(Slurred) Oh man… this is some heavy shit.” :: With his speech continuing a slur and his walk becoming drunken, Lung starts to violently sway from side to side. The couch is so close to being reached, but would he ever be able to conquer this fast-acting drug? :: “(Slurred) Must… make it… to couch.” :: It was no use, his body was moving too slow for its own good, and soon enough, the expected happened. The eyelids of Iron Lung grew heavier and heavier, as he tried to fight it in all of his power, he tried to hard just to make it to the couch. It was ironic, just hours before he was trying to go to sleep and kept a wide eye, but now he wanted the opposite. But it was too late, the effects of the fast-acting gel cap took control of his blood stream, and as the lights went out on the camera, Lung’s body was seen falling down and looking right at the floorboards with the couch inches away, a sharp thud raining out in closing. :: *Doze-Away is a fictional product used only for the purpose of hilarity in this piece. No one should go out and actually attempt to buy Doze-Away, for it’s not available anywhere to my knowledge.
