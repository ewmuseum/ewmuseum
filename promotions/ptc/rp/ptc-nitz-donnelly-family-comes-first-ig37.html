(IG 37 - Nitz Donnelly) Family Comes First In Most Cases

2 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday, September 22, 2007 @ Boston, MA Dear Nicholas, I watched you this past weekend on TV. You were in that match with PTC to win the right to take ok Tchu. I was pulling for you. But I’m not really a major wrestling fan. Matter of fact I was pulling for Nicholas Donnelly the person and not necessarily the wrestler. You’ve worked hard. I’ve seen tapes of your Indy matches and I have seen all your PRIME appearances. You always put in the effort and no one can take that away from you... don’t let them. I know you Nicholas, I know how you live, how you eat and how you sleep. Y*ou’ve had too many sleepless nights... He crumpled up the letter and threw it to the side. It was the third one he had found in a week. Someone was claiming to know him, trying to get under his skin. It was working. Curiosity got the best of him and he picked the letter off the ground. Unrolling it, he continued to read from where he left off. ... you need to sleep Nicholas. Rest assured that I’m not a stalker, nor a sick minded fan. You know me Nicholas and I know you. I’ve been right under your nose the whole time. Chalk my lack of physically talking to you up to nerves and a low self-esteem. I would just love to see you face to face, instead of those wonderful eyes looking down at paper. I would love to tell you that I have fallen for you Nicholas and I know that you feel the same for me too. Perhaps soon we can see each other again and get everything out in the open. Please just trust me, I have lost faith in plenty of people so don’t force me to lose faith in you too. Best Wishes and Love, Dani. He folded the letter and opened his car door. He didn’t know a Dani at all. He had no idea who was writing these letters but they were capturing his interest. Normally, he would treat any of this as a possessed stalker but he had a gut feeling it wasn’t. Maybe the name wasn’t clicking with him. Either way, he was not about to report anything. It was a female, and she sounded innocent enough. This letter was under his windshield wiper. The first one was slipped in with his mail four days earlier and the second was slipped inside the pizza box delivered by the pizza guy. The kid claimed to know nothing about it and rejected a tip. He was paid by someone, enough to keep his mouth shut. That was only two days ago. It bothered him that the first note was slipped into his mail. There were only three people he knew that knew where he lived. His best friend Scott Hannon was out of the equation for information because he was currently busy in Alabama living up to his football scholarship. He really couldn’t see Mikey being involved because the poor guy has enough to worry about, working fifteen hour days at the restaurant it seemed. The only other person was a far reach, but there was no one else. Nitz trusted his sister. Hailey had never done anything to deceive him and now was not the time to start. But deceive was a little too harsh. If she did say anything to anyone, there had to be a good reason for it. She had the same beliefs in family as Nitz, besides the whole “Chad is a fucknut” story. So what was he to do to find out the identity of this mystery person? |~o~| One Hour Later... “You mean to tell me you know nothing about this?” Nitz put the spoon down in his chowder and wiped his mouth with a napkin. Mikey just shrugged, trying to figure out exactly what his friend was trying to say. He put his tray down on the table across from The Ego and leaned on it. “Of course not, I’d tell you if I did. There aren’t many other people I’d take into consideration over you Nick.” Mikey said while fixing his messy hair. Nitz took another sip of his chowder. “Besides, this sounds messed up to me... like a stalker issu...” “It’s not a stalker that much I can tell you. I can feel it inside, this is someone I know. This person knows me, knows how I operate and knows how I think. It’s someone who has been around me enough to know almost everything and for the life of me I can’t put my finger on who it is. It’s not really my thing to dawn the retarded Sherlock fuckin’ Holmes hat and do some digging around. I just figured I’d ask you. Cause you are one of three people who knows where I live and this person knew. My parents don’t even know where I live because I’m always over there. Sorry for reaching but it was worth a shot.” “No worries my man.” Mikey said as he picked up the tray from the table. “I understand what you mean, but it still sounds like a crappy stalker issue to me man... until you prove me otherwise.” He smiled and made his way back to the kitchen. Nitz was left to stew in his thoughts. That was two out of three down. Scott was not part of any of this since Nitz hadn’t seen him in a couple months at least. So where did that leave him? |~o~| One Hour and a Few Hard Drinks Later... Nitz was sitting in his living room, only about thirty minutes after calling his sister. She was now standing before him, with the image of football flashing on the television set in the background. She looked a little nervous, almost like she knew why she was there. “This is killing my nerves Hail. I’d like to think you aren’t telling people shit about me causing some sorta crazy psycho person to follow me. It’s fuckin’ creepy if you ask me.” “Look, here’s the deal...” Hailey came to sit beside her brother, putting a hand on his shoulder. “I didn’t say much. All I said was where you were, what you were into and what you have been doing. That’s it.” She shook her head innocently, but she didn’t seem to be getting the point of it all. Nitz sat up where he sat, with a large funny looking grin on his face. He couldn’t believe she didn’t get it. “I have someone named Dani leaving me notes and letters on my car, at my door and in my fuckin’ pizza for god sakes!!! Fibre is good for you but come on, I don’t need this!” He threw his arms into the air and got up off the sofa. He paced around a little, mostly upset about Hailey doing what she did. “Nicholas, I’m sorry. I was asked some stuff about you and I told. Based on who was asking, I trusted her.” She explained with a twinkle in her eye. “So it is a she... that’s a relief. I guessed and was hoping that some rainbow loving freak wasn’t creepin’ after me. But you or I don’t know a Dani, unless you are going around with your fitness club femmes lookin’ at tight ass and talkin’ about brothers and how hot each others is. You got a new booty call that you’re writing letters to now as well? Or am I the only lucky fuck? Who the hell is Dani?” He was practically begging without actually doing it. He was not about to beg. Hailey wouldn’t put him in danger and he did still know that deep down. It could be something that started innocent enough and some crazy lady decided to take it over board. He just put his head in his hands. His mind was mixed and his back was sore. It got that way through a bit of stress. It was a nag mostly. “I promised not to say anything about ‘Dani’, as you call her.” “As I call her? Look at this note!” He tossed the letter he dug out of his pocket to her. It was the one he found on his car, signed by Dani. “It plainly says right there that her name is Dani.” “She is using that name as a decoy Nicholas. You’d know exactly who it was if she gave her full name.” “No fuckin’ shit I would! Isn’t that obvious? I wanna know who it is now... not later, not tomorrow and not next week. I wanna know now at this second who this is, so I can move on and forget this ever happened.” “But I promised little brother.” She stated bluntly. “You promised shit. Family comes first, you know that!” “But she is family...” BOOM! He felt like he had been hit by a ton of bricks. He had some demented thoughts floating around in that head of his but before he sunk to any conclusions that he really didn’t want to think about, he reacted. “Ok, if you tell me some second cousin has a crush on me, I’m carting your ass down to the nut house right now.” “No Nick, not that kind of family. Extended family.” She said matter of factly. “Extended fam...” He paused, reality suddenly dawning with him. There were few people outside of the actual family that everyone considered close to them and most of them were males. Scott, Mikey, a couple friends of Hailey’s and a couple that his parents have known for countless years were really all of them. Except for one... “You don’t mean...” Nitz tried to ask. Hailey nodded. “This isn’t a joke?” Hailey shook her head. “You aren’t pullin’ my fuckin’ leg?” “Nick, why the hell would I lie about this now? You figured it out.” Now it was Nitz’s turn to shake his head, rubbing his hands over his face in a bit of shock. “Does Chad know anything about this?” “You kidding Nick?! You think things are bad with him and you now? How bad would they get if he knew?” “Jesus Christ Hail...” “I know. It seems that she was only with Chad a short time ago. Hard to believe it was over three years ago now. He left her high and dry, but you stuck up for her when he took off.” Back and forth he went in his head with the new information. On one hand, she was extended family. On the other hand, she hadn’t been with his brother in three years and obviously she was trying to express herself. In the past, she always seemed to have problems talking to Nitz and now it was much of the same case he guessed. He looked up at his sister and smiled. “You still have Rachelle’s number in your phone Hail?” She returned the smile. “Chad?” She asked as she handed her cell over. “Fuck Chad.”
