A Happy Beginning

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 [In the heart of a sweltering Los Angeles spa, a well-dressed, four-foot Italian man shuffles his feet in designer loafers. Wiping sweat from his forehead, he glances at his tacky Rolex rip-off and gives a groan of resignation. He begins walking down a long, well-lit hallway, stopping at a thick wooden door halfway down. Knocking three times with a small, pudgy fist, he presses his ear to the wood to listen.] Mr. Biggs: Yo, Mitch! I've been waiting out here for a goddamn hour and I'm sweating my goddamn nuts off! You comin' or what?! [A dramatic, booming voice emanates from the other side of the door, provoking a surprised and frightened expression from the small, peevish man.] Mitch: What did Mitch say?! Mitch is not to be disturbed during his facial cleansing! NOT TO BE DISTURBED! [The heavy wooden door swings open to reveal a muscular, 6-foot young man wearing a pink bathrobe and a green facial mask, his long blonde hair done up in a loose ponytail. Upon seeing Mr. Biggs, his expression melts to one of complete submission.] Mitch: Oh…sorry boss. I thought you were… Mr. Biggs: It doesn't matter what you thought. I don't mentor you for your thoughts. Get the hell out here, wouldja? You've got a photo shoot in half an hour and an autograph signing at five. I've got better things to do than to sweat my ass off in [Mitch looks down at his bare feet and nods amidst Mr. Biggs' flurry of verbal abuse.] Mitch: Yes, sir. I'll be right out. Mr. Biggs: Good. [The door inches closed and Mr. Biggs sighs and glances at the camera, walking with it back down the hallway.] Mr. Biggs: Those of you who lucky enough to have tuned in tonight have just witnessed the second coming of professional wrestling, my friends. A finely-tuned athletic machine, capable of everything and more within a squared-circle. A man whose class and potential knows no bounds. A man whose charisma and good looks have earned him the title of "Sexiest Man Alive" amongst his large and devoted fanbase. A man who is the next Champion of the World. And that man's name is… [At high-pitched, child-like voice erupts from behind the door] Mitch: But whaddya MEAN Mitch doesn't get a "happy ending"? Come oooon… [Mr. Biggs glances at the door and presses his fleshy palm to his forehead. The camera pans back as Mr. Biggs runs back to the wooden door and flails his arms and legs against the frame, screaming obscenities until his face turns red.] MITCH MITCHUM.
