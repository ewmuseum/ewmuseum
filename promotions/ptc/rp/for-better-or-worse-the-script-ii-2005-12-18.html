For Better or Worse - The Script II

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 ‘Jack?’ The light flickered on in the office. I was still hunched down in the corner of the room, clutching the script close to my chest, not willing to let go, not willing to let anyone see. I curled up even tighter, hoping that whoever it was would go away. There was nothing that I’d feel more ashamed of than being stared at while the tears rolled down my face, thinking of the pain and the misery that the script aroused in me, all the locked up information that could do nothing but hurt me. And all the while the journalist’s words spun round me, reminding me that for all intents and purposes it would be very likely that things like this could reach the media and the general public. I didn’t want or need that happening but how was I supposed to stop it? ‘Jack?’ The word was more pleading this time, hoping that I would respond and turn to look at whoever it was. But I couldn’t. All they received was the faint sobbing of a tiring old wrestler who didn’t know what was the path for him anymore. And it was true. The encounter with the journalist had made me think all the way home in the car, wonder exactly what I was doing these days in the wrestling business. It had taken me nigh on fifteen years to make it in any sort of way in the business, fifteen years that could have been so different if I’d just decided to stay at home, in Ireland and become the farmer everyone expected me to be. I could have been a local celebrity and nothing more, happy with myself and my family. But I never could grasp on to that idea, I was always the dreamer at the back of the class, conjuring up images of me fighting the latest evil villain from television, battling them in a duel of wits, each event very likely the last for the superhero Jack Murphy. It didn’t matter though because inevitably I would win out and save the girl, a peck on the cheek all the praise I needed. It was all so simple back in those days, life cloudless and pure. There was a given path lain before me and all I had to do was follow it. But I never did. Instead, I looked to the overgrown path, the one which had many cracks and pitfalls but ultimately what looked like a bigger prize, at the time anyway. The foolishness of innocence. But now…what was I dong this all for? I really wasn’t sure… A warm hand brushed over my shoulders and rested on my neck, lightly rubbing the back and trying to invoke me into moving. I didn’t dare. The person then knelt beside me and with their arms outstretched, cradled me into their body, letting me feel the warmth. It was Jessica. And as I sobbed inconsolably into the faded blue pyjama top that Jessica wore, she sat patiently and quietly, merely rocking me back and forth slowly, speaking sweet words of comfort. But in the context of the evening…they were hollow. All I could feel though was the warmth…something more than I had felt before, but similar all at once. *** A quiet evening in a park somewhere, the place unimportant. The sun is setting slowly and everything is calm about me. Rua sleeps like a baby just steps away from me and I can hear the exhalations whispering across the cool air and they placate my soul. There is nothing like knowing your child is at peace, absent from any kinds of distress. Distress is the last thing I would ever want him to feel. To my right is my wife, my Laura. She is picking some flowers from the ground beside us, creating a chain to wear across her head. Her brown hair flows down her naked back and is full and bountiful. When she twirls the hair moves as one, moving entrancingly in time with her body, swivelling and swirling. There is nothing I want to see more than her face…which she obliges over. Seeing her soft face, the warm smile, the golden brown eyes that beam back at me warmly. I feel a sense of…happiness. Just looking at her I call her over and she flops into my arms, the silky smooth skin rubbing against me and sending me into skipping heartbeats of joy. There is nothing greater than this I thought… …nothing… ‘I love you Jack…I always will…’ ‘I love you too.’ *** Both of them gone, linked inextricably to the scenes in the script. Despite only seeing that first page of the film, I knew what else would be locked within, that’s why I couldn’t come to reading it. It immediately reminded me of booming lights and sounds… ‘Jack?’ She whispered it so quietly that I was shocked she was still there. It was only then I realised she still had me cradled in her arms, rocking me back and forth beneath her dainty frame. Despite my weight she was keeping me up easily, supporting it with little effort from her own body. Several moments had passed now since she whispered now and I really didn’t know what to say. ‘Hmm?’ ‘You were thrashing about a little there…bad dream?’ ‘In a way.’ There was nothing I wanted more right now than to be tucked away in the past, free from all this pain with the opportunity to start over. Knowing what I know, lived what I knew would hurt so much…I didn’t want any of it, I wanted it gone. So maybe I was an insecure prick, someone who just couldn’t deal with myself but this was the way I was. And all I felt like doing right now was crying…bleeding my heart out drip by drip until I could siphon off the caustic pain trapped within. If that meant dying in the process…then so be it. I curled even tighter into a ball and tried to forget about it. *** A giant rush of the crowd greeted my arrival, even if the arrival was a shower of boos. I strutted down the aisle, taking it all in my stride and lapping it up. Despite the size of the crowd being only three hundred people or so, it felt like I was the world’s whipping boy, I was the one who would suffer the abuse of the common man and woman, taking out their crappy day jobs and lives on me, venting their frustration in one single effort. I was merely the conduit for them to do so. I didn’t feel any remorse or regret doing this, there were simply some things that I had to do as part of my wrestling and being hated was one of them. I know they didn’t hate me, they merely hated the character, the outlandish idea that I represented. It wasn’t me, the person behind the stereotypes that they despised. So I revelled in the task of providing and giving as much ammunition as they could throw at me. This would continue, night in, night out at the smaller spots of the city, plying my trade and invoking the sense of mob mentality wherever I could, inviting them to try and out do me. It became quite the fun game. Good times… *** I opened my eyes for the second time tonight and now the light was beginning to dawn a little behind the drawn over curtains in the corner. It presented a dull illumination that I could see several things better. The mess of my desk, still strewn about like carelessly kept secrets, the script propping up on the floor and looming. All very similar but one thing had changed since I had fallen asleep again. Where was Jessica? I could feel no warm arms wrapped around me, nothing at all except the cold floor of the office and I immediately widened my eyes in the hope of seeing her. In the background of the atmosphere I could hear the clacking of keyboard keys and the pale blue light of a computer screen impacted on the periphery of my vision. I turned to see her sitting, working at what I think was five in the morning. Her hair was tossed all over the place, though not at all like her usual self and so it looked…nicer, more natural to me. The lack of makeup too was a change but it suited her, able to show off her pale complexion, the porcelain effect exaggerated by the light and despite the aforementioned light, her eyes still shone… ‘I see you’ve woken up. How are you?’ She had a cheeky smile on her face and it was only now I realised I was under a full blown duvet cover, toasty and warm. I chuckled slightly, noticing the pink fluffy elephants dotted on the cover. ‘I’m…a bit embarrassed.’ ‘Why?’ ‘Because I’m under a duvet cover and you’ve definitely changed my clothes for sleeping.’ It was true…I was now pretty much naked except for boxers that I had worn but all the same I felt like she’d done way more than she had to. It wasn’t that I was uncomfortable about her seeing me like that…in fact it was… ‘Well, I wasn’t going to let you crease your clothes now was I? You feel a bit better?’ Her interjection was welcome and I now thought about what I should do. There was only one thing that came to mind and instinctively, irrationally I blurted it out. ‘I feel lonely…would you, just lie with me?’ I bent my head in shame, definitely embarrassed now by question. I fully expected her to ignore me… And she did, she walked right out of the room. My heart sank. What was I doing? I had never considered any of this before, none of these thoughts had ever crossed my mind. It all made no sense to me. I was afraid that I was acting out in my less than stable state and I wasn’t sure what it meant to her. I’m glad she left, I’m glad she didn’t come near me, leave me to think about things… I felt the press of a body up against my back and I inhaled sharply, feeling her smooth skin caressing against my own coarse back. Her arms wriggled between my own and tightly hugged me. I turned without thinking and saw her there, lying, in a simple nightdress, nothing expensive or seductive, just a plain one. That was all I wanted though… ‘Thank you.’ *** The morning arrived in full gloom, the clouds hanging overhead and leaving the world in perpetual moodiness. My hair lay across my face and I blew it up into the air in the faint hope that it would drop to either side of my face and allow me to see. No such luck. So I brought my hand up instead and brushed it aside, pulling myself up. I looked around the office…and Jessica was nowhere to be found. My head throbbed slightly from discomfort of sleeping on the floor with only a pillow covering it. Grumbling to myself about having wooden floors throughout the entire house, I struggled to my feet and began to wander to my room. The quiet of the house was all too clear and I knew then that Jessica was probably gone. Since the onset of the tournament, I had offered to Jessica for her to sleep in my house and stay nearby since we would be doing a fair bit of travelling together. She had no problems with this and neither did her father and my own trainer Dan O’Grady. So I had given her my spare bedroom as hers for the completion of the tournament, whether I lost out in the first round or in the last. So it would be no surprise if she felt like giving me some space and letting me sort out my head right now. I certainly would. I pressed on the door to her room and looked on in to find Jessica curled up on her bed, no covering to keep her warm. I crept back to the office and got her duvet cover, sprawling it over her once I returned and made sure it covered her entire frame. Sitting down beside her I sat and waited, holding her idle hand by the side of the bed and looking at her strong, but innocent face. ‘Thank you Jess…’
