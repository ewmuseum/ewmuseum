IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

30 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jason Snow is living proof of context. Jason Snow works for criminals, and by being slightly more pitiable than them, comparatively looks like an angel. Jason Snow is also surrounded by idiots, and by being marginally more intelligent, comparatively looks like a genius. Jason Snow, in his “work life”, is surrounded by cowards and weaklings, and by being marginally proficient in defending himself and intimidation, he becomes a “Lion” among mice. But Jason Snow is truly exceptional in other ways. Between working for bad guys who kill you if you ask for day off, and working for a wrestling company where you are prominently featured on television, it seems to push the Clark Kent can’t possibly be Superman bullshit top a new level. Witnesses? Witnesses wearing a Jason Snow t-shirt failing to recognize a mob enforcer in action? But in Jason Snow’s little world, everybody must be corrupt already, because thou shalt not be more sympathetic than Jason Snow. It’s true. Any character that is more sympathetic dies. Dead wife? Check. Dead whore replacing wife with no sexual contact? Check. Other characters that are basically the same over and over again that I couldn’t be bothered to read about because that shit was too long? Check? Well, probably. Well, Jason, fantasy land is now over, since in our shared reality this time, we’re trash talking you to death so you finally let go of the IG title, so we can make the new policy that Jason Snow can’t ever have it again. In this reality, you don’t get to pretend that all your competitors will be stupid/corrupt/weak to make you feel like a big man. You’re going down, you’re too cocky to be allowed to continue to hold both prestigious awards, as that cuts potential fictional t-shirt sales in half. … I’m not sure what this about anymore, but I guess this doesn’t matter. You, Jason Snow, are part of an extensive wish fulfillment fantasy, which is the number one reason that you’re plagiarized so much. Weak liars who steal others’ writing want to be Jason Snow instead of themselves. … I’m not even sure this is trash talk anymore. Dammit, this is all your fault, Jason Snow! I hope your bosses order you to execute someone really nice so that you feel like the genuine killer that you are, and not the slightly-less-evil scumbag with delusions of lowered standards!
