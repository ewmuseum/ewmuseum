Death and Life Pt. 2

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Life Dead. He was dead and I knew it. Yet for some strange reason there he was right there sitting next to me, He was just sitting there standing out into the nothingness of space as if there was nothing wrong. What in the world was going on…? I took a moment to take in everything that had just happened. I was about to wreck. Then I started to wreck, and then everything just…stopped, paused in time. Nothing was making any kind of sense at the moment. I looked up at the scene that lay before my eyes. My black Toyota truck and the small red compact were suspended in mid collision. I could see the metal of my truck bending from the force of a combined speed of somewhere in the range of 140mph. I saw a single rain drop on the dirty windshield of my truck about to make contact with the glass, yet not quite there. What in the world was going on? I brought my attention quickly back to the person sitting next to me, the man that was not there a minute ago, the man who was dead. I turned my head slowly towards him. He was no longer staring off into space, but looking directly at me. I could do nothing else but gape. This man who had been like a father to me, this man whom I had hated for such a long time, this dead man was sitting right next to me staring. “You’re dead.” I stammered because nothing else would come to my mind for me to speak at the moment. My mind was still reeling from these events that had hit back to back. This was impossible I knew, yet it was happening right before my eyes. Was I dreaming? “This isn’t a dream Jack” said my father figure almost as if he had read my mind. I was still frozen from the chaos of my mind taking it all in. I tried to say something, but all I got was my mouth opening and closing a few times making me look completely retarded. Not that it mattered at this moment. I wouldn’t blame anyone for being dumbfounded at a moment like this. Memories of Richard flooded my mind at that moment. I remembered when Richard found me in my isolation of the jungle. I remembered Richard and I growing closer together. Memories of the good time kept winding their way into my mind. Memories of Richard being the man I loved him being. A man who was like the father I don’t even remember. Yet then also came the memories I would have given anything to forget. The ones that made me want to hate him for everything he was and ever had been. The ones that made him the person I distrusted the most. The way Richard had betrayed me, and the way he had used me for money. Yet most of all though, the memory of me finding out what he was doing came flooding back into my mind. It’s one thing to not train hard enough to the point that they would be no good in a wrestling ring. It was another thing to poison them. That was it. The single most horrible thing he had ever done to me. He had poisoned me in order to make me perform at my worst, and just because he could win a cheap $50 bet. He had nearly taken my life in order to make a cheap buck. He risked my life and my wrestling career in one instance and it cost him everything where I was concerned. He would regret it. I had made sure of that. I wouldn’t even speak to him after I found out. If I would have seen him I probably would have ended up with my hands around his bulging neck. That is why I had moved out to Oklahoma in the first place. IT was quiet, secluded, and Richard had no idea I was there. I found out from friends that he was saying he was sorry about the entire ordeal. Not that it mattered. He knew where I stood on it and that was that. He later even had the audacity to try and ask for my forgiveness. It had taken me about a half a second to slam the door in his face, or I should say one of his trainees faces. He didn’t even have the guts to face me. Yet, it was still his last words that I would always remember ‘I’m sorry’. “I would imagine you are wondering why I am here and for that matter how I came to be here?” I simply nodded, my mouth still hanging. He looked at me like a father looks at a son, that sort of warmth in some connection that only they would have. I was mesmerized by it. It was a look I had longed for what seemed like an eternity. It was something that I had purposely driven out of my life, love. A single drop of sweat rolled down from my dark blonde hair onto my nose before dripping off. It was enough to jerk me back into reality, if you would call it that. I ran my fingers through my now damp hair as I began to calm down. “Unfortunately I have no answer for you. You see it would just be too difficult for you to understand,” said Richard in a quiet secluded tone that was nothing like I was used to. I just stared at him, though I had calmed quite a bit down. I was still unsure of what to say. “Therefore,” began Richard, “I must first say this is what you might call a wake up call. I am not here to stay. I am here to deliver you a message.” A message? Why would Richard, a dead guy of all things, be here to deliver me a message? What in the world was going on? “Go…Go on then.” I managed to say. I surprised myself by being even able to speak a few words. Richard simply continued giving me that same loving smile. I nearly cried just by looking into his eyes as I saw love there that I had not seen in years. “You have much left to do. Yet you waste your time. You are better than what you believe yourself to be. You have a purpose in this life. Now you must figure it out. You must forget about me and everything I once stood for.” His words weren’t making much sense. I was receiving too many things to register at once. “My purpose?” I asked quizzically. It was something I had often wondered about. We all have a purpose in life. I had honestly come to the conclusion long ago that one’s purpose in life was to live and then die. As uninspiring as that may sound it was what I believed at the time. No one had ever proved me other wise either. People die all the time for no reason whatsoever. So what other reason would there be for living if it was not to meet the ultimate end game to every living breathing organism in the universe, death. Yet, now I was questioning myself about my belief. I was not just questioning myself about the purpose of life, but about what is in death. Things that only those of religion care to think about. Was there a god? Was there an afterlife? Where would I go when I died? Questions never proposed by myself were now flooding my mind. “What is my purpose?” I asked hungrily. I needed to know the meaning of it all. He had said this was a warning. A warning for what though? What was the meaning of all of this? I needed to know. I wanted an answer. I craved it and felt it throughout my yearning body. I had never become as passionate as I was in that moment in my life. I needed answers and he could give them to me. “That is not for me to answer, but for you to find out. I was sent to give you a message. What you do with the message is up to you. I was chosen for this assignment because of the emotional attachment I share with you and you with me. Nothing else.” Said Richard in a cold hard tone that more than uncharacteristic of him. Once again I was brought back down to earth by the reality that nothing was making sense to me. “It is time for me to go. I have delivered the message and that is all I was required to do. Have a good life,” said Richard in a somewhat softer tone than earlier. “No don’t go. I have so many questions for you. Don’t leave me like this wait a second. I said don’t go!” I screamed wanting to reach over and grab him. “Live you life to the fullest. Do what you were meant to do,” replied Richard to my pleas. “No, no don’t go.” I was nearly in tears now. I didn’t want him to leave, but not because of the fact that I wanted answers. It was because of the fact that it was nice being close to him again. It was almost as if all the bad things had been wiped clean. “Goodbye” With that he began to fade into nothingness. I tried to grab at him but got nothing but air. I started to cry in the agony of being away from him again. “I’m sorry. I forgive you!” I screamed as the last remnants of him disappeared and for one last time his voice was heard with two simple words. “I know.” With those last words I knew it was over. The impact of the two vehicle I had forgot was coming jerked me and I knew nothing else. It would be twenty minutes later before I woke up. It was then as I was dazed and partly confused; trying to get out of my totaled truck that it was time for me to change. Time for everything to change. ‘Live you life to the fullest’, he had said. I intended to just that. I would become more focused. I had to focus on the one thing he had taught me to do well, wrestle. I had to make it through my challenges, especially my current one of Caitlin Fairchild. I had to get as far as I could in the GTT. It was my destiny I said to myself. I had just been reborn anew. Everything had changed. Again…
