Chapter Six: Making Moves

3 January 2010

Date: 2010-01-03 It was the smell of hibiscus that finally caused Alanna Marshall to stir. She squirmed lazily, her hands stretching out above her as she yawned slowly. Alanna had always liked the smell of hibiscus, from back when she was growing up on The Big Island. It helped that you could find them practically everywhere, sure, but she found the smell comforting and relaxing as well. She shifted slightly on the unfamiliar surface, marveling at the ability sand had of getting into places where it had no business being. Seriously, no matter what you wore… Wait a minute? Sand?! Alanna woke with a start, sand falling from her dress as she propped herself up on her elbows. Ahead of her stood the bright blue expanse of the Pacific, waves washing lazily against the shore. The sun shone brightly, feeling warm against Alanna’s skin. There was no sign of anyone else being present. Not a beach chair or towel or pair of water wings…nothing. “Bumbai bachi,” Alanna muttered under her breath. “Actually,” said a female voice from behind her, “I’d say this is the first decent break you’ve had in a few days.” Alanna jumped at the sound of the stranger’s voice, clambering to her feet as gracefully as circumstances would allow. She turned slowly, not quite sure what to expect. The stranger was tall, looking just slightly taller than Alanna’s own five feet, eleven inches. She was wearing a long, white diaphanous dress which seemed to flow down her frame like water. A single gold bangle hung from her right wrist and long red hair spilled down her back. Alanna fought the urge to label the woman as a danger just because she had red hair. Just because redheads tended to be a health and safety issue in SCCW, it doesn’t mean it’s that way all the time. “Um, hi?” The stranger smiled back at Alanna; a warm, welcoming smile. She waved her right hand slowly, the bangle clattering slightly against her wrist. “I can see why you come here,” the stranger responded. “It’s very calm, very peaceful. Very relaxing. Trust me, relaxing is very much what you need right now.” Alanna laughed lightly in response. “Well, I know this is a dream.” The stranger smiled. “What was your first clue?” “Last thing I remember I’m in the ICU and now I’m on the beach? Trust me, there could not possibly be enough drugs in my system to make me think this is real.” “What’s the last thing you remember?” asked The Stranger. “I was waking up after the surgery,” Alanna answered. “And there was…there was this guy…” Now, I think it’s time that you and I had a little chat. G-d, Alanna thought, why did her head suddenly feel so bad? Like someone had taken a hot knife and was slicing it through her skull. “What else do you remember?” asked The Stranger. I’m the one who took your son away from you. I’m the one who did whatever I wanted to him for an entire week. “He told me,” Alanna answered, “that he was the one who had taken Zach. That he was the one who had hurt my baby.” Alanna gasped, clasping the sides of her head with her hands as she cried out in pain. “I know this hurts,” said The Stranger, “but we have to keep going.” The Stranger walked slowly and deliberately towards Alanna, coming to a stop just inches away from her. Alanna was in too much pain to do anything. “What else happened?” “I don’t…” “What else happened?” “He,” stumbled Alanna, her tongue suddenly feeling thick and dry in her mouth, “he tried to hurt me…he tried to… Say goodnight, Alanna. “He tried to kill me.” At the touch of the stranger’s hand, all went still again. Alanna’s head suddenly stopped hurting as The Stranger placed her hands against Alanna’s temples. “I know you’re scared and I’m sorry for that. But you have to try and remember what happened to you as best as you can. It is very important that you do.” “Figures you’d say that,” Alanna answered. “Oh,” said The Stranger, “why is that?” “Because we’re in my dream, so you have to be some aspect of my subconscious or something, someone that I created in my head.” The Stranger just continued to smile. “If I am just a creation of your mind, then you know I’d never do anything to hurt you, right?” Alanna simply nodded. “Okay, then,” replied The Stranger, “I need to you to close your eyes.” Alanna’s eyes closed slowly, as if she was falling into a trance. “Remember, I will never hurt you. Do you trust me?” “Yes.” “Then relax,” said The Stranger. “On the count of three. One, two..” With just the slightest push, The Stranger sent Alanna falling towards the sand below. She fell slowly, as though gravity had decided to take its leave of her. It was mere inches before impact before The Stranger added, “Thrr..” “AAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!” The first thing that registered in Alanna Marshall’s mind is pain. Every part of her body hurt, nerve endings flaring with an agony that she didn’t have the words to describe. She could vaguely feel something coursing through her bloodstream, doing what it could to stop the pain and failing miserably at it. The second thing she noticed were two sets of hands trying to hold her in place, trying to keep her from moving. She struggled against them, trying as hard as she could to get free. “Lani! Hey, baby, it’s me. It’s me, baby, okay? Just relax.” Alanna stopped thrashing, looking upwards now into the face of her husband, Lance. His strong, solid face creased with pain and worry, every ounce of his feelings visible upon his features. “G-d, Baby, you gave us a hell of a scare.” The initial surge of adrenaline over, Alanna slumped back down onto the bed, spent. “Lance? What happened? Where am I?” This time, it was the owner of the other set of hands who replied. “After what happened to you in the ICU,” answered the nurse, “we have you moved as soon as possible to a private room.” “They found you unconscious in the ICU”, added Lance, “a syringe full of something stuck into your arm. We were lucky that a nurse came by to check on you when she did. If she hadn’t, they tell me…” He trailed off, unwilling or unable to finish the sentence. “As it is,” continued the Nurse, “you’ve been in a coma for the past forty-eight hours. We’re surprised you’re awake now, to be honest. All of a sudden, your vitals just took a sudden jump up.” “I’ve…never been one for subtlety,” replied Alanna. The nurse chuckled. “Glad to see you still have your sense of humor. Now,” she added, “I need to step outside for a few moments, let them know that you’re up and running. I shouldn’t be too long.” Once the door had closed behind the nurse, Lance turned to his wife again, his smile threatening to stretch off of his face. “Thank G-d you’re okay, baby. Thank G-d.” “Lance, I...someone attacked me.” “We know, baby,” Lance answered, “Detective Bentley and his people think it was one of the hospital workers. Some kind of weird of angel of mercy or angel of death thing, I don’t know. I wasn’t listening all that closely.” “No,” Alanna interjected, “I remember…I remember he told me something…” I’m the one who took your son away from you. I’m the one who did whatever I wanted to him for an entire week. “It was him. It was the son of a bitch who took Zach. He’s the one who did this.” Lance’s face hardened. “Are you sure?” “He gloated about it. He laughed about it. He...he enjoyed the fact that he hurt us.” “Can you remember anything about him,” Lance asked. “Height, weight, anything?” Alanna closed her eyes tightly, willing her mind to bring up the images of that evening, to let her see clearly what had happened to her. But her mind…refused. “No…baby, I’m sorry, I can’t,” said Alanna, tears forming in her eyes. “I’m trying but I can’t hold onto it. Just when I think I’ve got it, it slips away.” “That’s okay,” Lance assured his wife. “You’ve been through too much lately, I shouldn’t have eben asked you to try.” “No,” said Alanna, “I wanted to try. I want to find this bastard and make sure he pays for what he’s done to us.” “And he will,” answered Lance. “The cops just better hope they find him before we do.” Alanna nodded weakly. “How the hell can I be this tired when I’ve been doing nothing buy sleep for two days straight?” Lance took his wife’s left hand in his right, brought it to his lips and kissed it. “You did just have surgery, baby, remember? If you need to get some sleep, go ahead. If you want, I’ll stay here with you until you go to sleep.” “Please?” Lance smile at his wife. “Of course, baby.” So, with her hand nestled safely in his, Alanna Marshall allowed herself to sink back into the arms of sleep. Before she slipped away completely, she saw her husband looking down at her as though she was the most precious thing in the world. She took hold of that image, burning it into her memory. It was the same image that would greet her when she woke up again the next morning. It had been just twenty-four hours since Alanna had been allowed home from the hospital and, as life tended to do, nothing had actually bothered to stop while she’d been away. There were bills to be paid, calls to be returned, matters to be addressed. And with the way she was feeling now, she’s consider it a miracle if she wasn’t destroyed by the daily running of the household. Thank G-d for Lance’s parents, Mom and Dad and the help of friends, she thought, I do not know how we would have managed this one without them. Then, as always, there was work. Not for her, no; she had taken a leave of absence once she had learned of her condition and she didn’t expect to be back for some weeks yet. Someone still had to be the breadwinner and, even more than usual, that responsibility fell upon Lance. Not only was he being regularly booked in SCCW but he was also doing very well for himself in the GTT Tournament. This, as you might expect, placed a rather large demand on Lance’s time…necessitating more time away. He had volunteered immediately, of course, to withdraw from the tournament and take a leave of absence himself in order to be by her side. She’d refused, assuring him that everyone would be okay, that work was the best thing for him, that it would keep him occupied and not focused on them twenty-four hours a day…even though she knew, while saying it, that it was a lie. This particular week had seen a rather busy traveling schedule. First, Lance had gone up to Ottawa for an SCCW show. It had been less than a day since he’d returned from that day and there the suitcase was on the bed again, getting ready this time for a trip to Miami and the bracket finals of the GTT tournament. “Damn,” said Lance, “from eighteen degrees yesterday to the seventies today to Miami weather tomorrow. I don’t have the time to be sick.” “You might want to cancel packing the shorts,” said Alanna as she walked into their bedroom. “Miami’s supposed to be having a cold front this week.” “Define cold front.” “Swinging between the 40’s and 60’s till the weekend. For Miami, hon, that’s cold.” “No packing the shorts and t-shirts, then,” Lance muttered, removing a small pile of clothing from the suitcase and setting it aside. Alanna walked over to it, taking it in her hands. “Don’t worry about it, hon, I’ll take care of it.” “Don’t you dare,” Lance replied, “you’re supposed to be resting. And that means no work. At. All. So go. Read a book, go out by the pool, sit in front of the TV and eat ice cream. Just as long as it doesn’t fit the definition of “strenuous activity”.” “Yes, sir,” Alanna answered, giving a mock salute at her husband. She headed out the door, stopping as she saw a small plastic bag on the dresser. Stopping to point to it, she asked “What’s this?” “Just some stuff I picked up from ThinkGeek. Some of it’s for Zach.” “I really hope this one isn’t,” Alanna responded, taking out and holding up a stuffed toy from the bag. The toy looked like no animal she’d ever seen. The body was a pale green color, with two cloth lined wings sticking out from its back. The head seemed to be in the shape of an octopus, with tentacles swimming down from it, and the eyes were a bright, burning red. “Oh no,” Lance replied as he saw what his wife was holding up. “That’s something for Violence Jack. I saw that in the shop and couldn’t help but think of him.” “Why?” “Oh, you great and terrible elder gods that man would turn into children’s toys.” Wait a minute, thought Alanna. That sounded a little familiar. Something about the research they’d done on Shanahan…then it hit her. “Oh my god. Is this a Cthulhu plushie?!” Lance nodded. “They had one in silver and one dressed in a Men In Black uniform. But Bruce? I figure him to be a guy who appreciates the classics. What do you think?” “I think,” said Alanna, giggles starting to escape from her mouth, “that he’ll love it.” The Voice kept a close watch on the police station entrance. He’d been observing the building for two days now, keeping a close and careful watch on the comings and goings of the officers inside. Now, he knew, would be the time to act. Lance was out of town, he knew this already. Even cutting it close, he would need to have left by now in order to be in any sort of condition to wrestle in the current round of the GTT. Perfect. The attack on Alanna Marshall at the hospital had gone just as his employers promised, doing her injury but not resulting in her death. That was fine. There would be no to spread word of his coming had Alanna Marshall died; The Voice very much wanted Lance to know what was coming next. The Voice straightened up from his hiding post, making himself look as presentable as possible. The next step in the plan required a little bravery, a little daring…and that he could do. He walked up to the building and made his way inside, slowly heading towards the on duty desk clerk. “I’d like to speak to someone, please.” The policeman was in his forties, roughly thirty pounds heavier than he should be and looked as though he’d rather be self-administering a lobotomy than working today. “And what is the nature of your emergency, sir?” “Oh, no emergency. You see, I’ve been having some conflicts with a neighbor of mine. You may even have heard of him, his name is Lance Marshall.” The policeman nodded briefly. “The wrestler on TV. Go on.” “Well,” continued The Voice, “it started civilly enough. Heated exchanges of words, that sort of thing. But lately, it’s gotten worse. He’s threatened to kill me, repeatedly, and I’m starting to be afraid for my life.” The policeman nodded. “So, you wish to file charges against him, sir?” “I’m not sure. While his reaction to our conflict has been rather extreme, I can’t say it’s been completely unjustified. After all, he does have his reasons.” “And what would those be, sir?” “Most likely, the fact that I sexually molested his son and attempted to murder his wife.” The policeman blinked. The man standing in front of him had just confessed to some fairly horrible things and, yet, not once had the smile ever left his mouth. “Excuse me?” “You heard me just fine, you dumb fucking cumstain,” The Voice responded. “[withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] And just because of that, I have some roided out lunatic wishing me dead. So, if you are capable of processing any information at all, I want you to get these next two things down very clearly.” “My name is Martin Weston… …and for my protection, I want you to arrest me.”
