IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

3 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
3 July 2007 Easton Hall - Kevin Smith Filmed This Black-and-white. Easton Hall sits in an otherwise empty bar. His long hair is pulled away from his face. He's dressed in a black sleeveless tee emblazoned with a Mastodon logo; "Hunters of the Sky" plays faintly in the background. He shakes his head, a self-indulgent smirk shaping the thicket that is his beard. Easton Hall: The people who come into these things are a buncha gloryhogs, all of 'em. Second-rate guys, trying to make a name for themselves. I'm not saying I don't have a long way to go myself, because I do. I'm on the cusp right now and for the sake of my career, I should be clamoring at every chance like this, I should be ready to go 24/7. But I'm not in this for any of that. I'm here because of one man. Easton's smirk becomes a scowl. Easton Hall: Jason Snow. The Canadian Dragon exhales, his nostrils flaring as he fixes an iron gaze on the camera. Easton Hall: At ReVolution 131, for those of you who weren't watching, Jason Snow thought it was a good idea to blindside me. He hasn't given his reasons, and I don't care about them. I don't care what Jason Snow thinks, I don't care what his goals are, what he's after, 'cause what matters is that he attacked me unprovoked, and that means I've gotta do something about it. Easton leans forward and the camera zooms in, closing in on his face a bit more. Easton Hall: Every ReVolution that Snow is backstage, I'm gonna haunt that motherfucker. If you think that Chainz is going to get in my way, you are dead fucking wrong. The camera cuts to a clip of Easton at ReVolution 126, putting the boots to Chainz backstage, before returning to Easton's face. Easton Hall: Every time Snow shows his ugly mug, he'd better be looking over his shoulder. I'm gonna flush him out, I'm gonna run him down, and I'm gonna fuck - him - up. Easton clenches his fist, bringing it just under his nose as he collects himself. He shakes his head, lowering his hand. Easton Hall: But that's not all of it, that's not the end of the story. The reason I'm here is to wipe Jason Snow off the map. Jason Snow has become a dominant force here in Primetime Central, and that's gotta end, and I'm gonna end it. Easton shrugs his shoulders lightly. Easton Hall: Beating a shit-ton of schmucks doesn't matter to me. The Canadian leans forward confidentially, pointing at the camera to punctuate his words. Easton Hall: What matters to me is getting in the ring with Jason Snow and beating him to a pulp. What matters to me is ripping those five pounds of gold from around his waist and shoving it in his face. What matters to me is taking everything that he's earned for myself, lighting his legacy on fire. The last sentence is accompanied by a rending motion of Easton's hands. Unexpectedly, Easton chuckles, the sound still bearing the weight of his anger. Easton Hall: I know they gave the Unified Title shot to that nutjob Adam Dick, but that's okay. If you're listening, Jase, I hope you beat the Face Eater, 'cause I'll be right there, breathing down your neck, ready for my fuckin' shot. Every accolade you've won, I'm gonna win, and I'm gonna do it ten times better. And when you're sitting all alone, wondering what happened, wondering where your career went, I'm gonna say I told you, I motherfucking told you so! Easton's face is flushed with rage, but he takes a second to calm himself. The camera closes in tight on his face. He bares his teeth, wolflike in fury. Easton Hall: It starts here, Jase. It starts with Aye-Gee Thirty-Three. It starts with me taking this belt. It ends in PRIME… with me taking your goddamn dignity. Easton inhales deeply, his face shaking. Easton Hall: You're gonna be an example. No matter who you are, no matter what you've done, or what you're doing, or what you're gonna do, you DON'T FUCK WITH EASTON HALL. Static. ---- 6 July 2007 Easton Hall in Delware! Black-and-white. The scene is the side of a mountain, a small clearing surrounded by pine. Easton Hall stands on the side of the hill, a bottle of water in hand. He is dressed in a sturdy pair of cargo pants and a baggy t-shirt. His hair is held away from his face by a bandana. Hiking gear is settled against a rock nearby. Easton takes a deep breath. Easton Hall: God, mountain air... so cool, crisp. Fills your lungs, makes you feel alive. I could be anywhere else... maybe Mexico, getting drunk, looking at topless women, but that'll just get you tired, hungover. This? This is invigorating. Easton takes a few steps down the hill towards the camera. Easton Hall: Maybe some of you should take this in, because as it is, you're going in shitfaced and pretty much blind. All of you are walking into Infinite Gauntlet cocksure, chests puffed, looking at that prize. You're all talking shit with raised eyebrows and Hollywood egos like you've come straight from Hayward, California. But the thing you're all missing is that YOU'RE NOT FUNNY. Easton rubs his head, his fingers digging into his scalp for a bit. His face contorts and he lets out a sigh, raising his eyes back to the camera. Easton Hall: All of you are running the same damn gimmick here: "Look at me, I can be on a sitcom!" You expect me to believe that Tchu, that fuckin' bleeding heart in PRIME, the long-suffering, revenge seeking son-of-a-bitch is suddenly a fuckin' comedian? Easton tilts his head back, wincing in disbelief. After a second, he glares back at the camera. Easton Hall: You take a good long look in the mirror, Tchu, 'cause you're not fooling anybody. You know, I respected you. You were someone I thought brought an ounce of credibility into this fucking business. You're not Sonny Silver and the fact that you're trying to be him, trying to be that talentless, spineless, good-for-nothing scumbag shows me how insecure you are about this match. Easton gave a single breathy laugh. Easton Hall: You've got all the talent in the world, Tchu, and you're still pissing yourself about this. Easton takes a swig of the water and spits the vestiges out, stepping towards the camera again. Easton Hall: James von Doom has lost this goddamn thing so many times nobody wants to keep count anymore, but we're supposed to believe that he's suddenly a fucking god? Easton makes a sweeping, inclusive gesture. Easton Hall: Look at the mouths that the greats have, von Doom. They earned that. As much as they're still dicks, they've accomplished something that they can brag about. You haven't earned shit. Every night, I know you're quaking your damn boots. I know you're watching those tapes, trying to figure out what you did wrong, trying to figure out how you can fix it. But you can't, and the reason why is because you're a poser. You think by talking the talk you'll suddenly be able to walk the walk? Laughing, Easton shakes his head. Easton Hall: You're a fucking headcase, man. Easton rubs his nose, taking another step towards the camera. Easton Hall: Most of you haven't given me more than a few words. You continue to pretend like I'm not here. "Show me somebody I've heard of!" "Who's that guy?" That says two things to me. Either you're too stupid to realize that you never, EVER underestimate your opponent, or you're scared. You're scared of what I can do, you're scared of the fact that walking into the Infinite Gauntlet, I've got a mission and I'm ready to see it through. You're trying to push it away, like if you deny it it'll disappear, but I'm right here. Easton's jaw sets tightly as he closes into the camera. His nostrils flare. Easton Hall: This whole act, the smoke and mirrors that you're putting on, it's for nothing. I see right through this bullshit. This is the Infinite Gauntlet. You're not making pilots for Comedy Central. If you want to win this thing, you act like it. You make like you're walking into a wrestling match. You talk like you're abuot to fight somebody. Easton sneers. Easton Hall: Right now, every single one of you looks lost, staring open-mouthed up into a thunderstorm. Easton lifts an eyebrow and takes a long pull on the water bottle. He then turns, starting slowly back up the hill. He looks over his shoulder, pointing back at the camera with his water bottle hand. Easton Hall: And I ain't got a problem carving you up. Easton Hall walks up to his hiking gear, shouldering his backpack and picking up his hiking stick. He starts up the mountainside again, walking offshot. The camera turns to static. ---- 8 July 2007 Easton Hall Sings! Color! Easton Hall stands in the middle of a wrestling ring in full wrestling attire. His hair is pulled back away from his face in a ponytail. Kneeling beside him seem to be two musical theater types in black turtlenecks and berets. Suddenly, Easton points straight at the camera, and in his best falsetto, belts out: "BOBIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINOOOOO, YOU SUUUUUUUUCK!" He switches his posture, pulling clenched fists to his sides! Easton Hall: BOBINO YOU SUUUUUUUUCK! He thrusts his fist into the air triumphantly, eyes squeezed shut. Easton Hall: BOBINO YOU SUUUUUUU-UU-UU-UUUUUU-UU-UU-UUUUUUUUUCK! Dead silence. The two musical theater types start to rise, walking around Easton and snapping their fingers. In hushed voices, they fill the air. Musical Theater Types: bobino you suck. bobino you suck. bobino you suck. bobino you suck. Louder. Musical Theater Types: Bobino, you suck. Bobino, you suck. Louder! Musical Theater Types: Bobino, you suck! Louder! Musical Theater Types: BOBINO, YOU SUCK! All together now! Everybody: BOBIIIIIIIIIIIIINOOOOO, YOU SUUUUUUUUUCK! BOBINO YOU SUUUUUUUCK! Cue the gosepl choir, coming down the ramp and clapping hands. Gospel Choir: BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK! OHHHH LOOOORD, IT'S TRUE! BO-BIII-NO, YOU SUUUUUCK! Easton Hall: Tell it on the mount-aaaaaaain! Gospel Choir: BO-BI-NO, YOU SUUUUUUUUUUUCK! Easton Hall & Choir: OH PLEASE GOD HEEELP USS! BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK! WE'RE TRYIN' TA TELL YAAAAAA! BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK! OH LORD IT'S TRUE, BO-BI-NO! BO-BI-NO, YOU SUUUUUUUUCK! The choir is in the ring now, swaying and clapping hands. Easton Hall appears to be doing a fan dance, sans fans, in the middle of them. Everybody: BO-BI-NOOO, YOU SUCK! TELL IT ON THE MOUNTAAAAIN! BO-BI-NOOO, YOU SUCK! OH PLEASE, GOD HELP USSSS! BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK! OH LORD, OHHHH LOOORD, IT'S TRUUUUUEEE! Easton Hall bursts from the midst of the choir as multicolored pyros shoot up from behind the ring. A spinning mock-up of the PRIME 5-Star title appears in the sky. All voices are raised now, trying to give glory to telling people that Bobino sucks. Everybody: BO-BI-NOOOO, YOU SUCK! TELL IT ON THE MOUNTAAAAAIN! BO-BI-NOOO, YOU SUCK! OH PLEASE, GOD HELP USSS! BO-BI-NOOO, YOU SUCK! WE'RE TRYIN' TA TELL YAAAAA! BO-BI-NOOO-OO, BO-BI-NOO-OO, BO-BI-NOOOOOO! Easton steps out of the crowd, thrusting his fist forward mightily. Everybody: BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK! BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK! BO-BI-NO, YOU SUUUUUUUUUUUUUCK! Everything comes to a sudden halt and Easton makes a sharp pelvic thrust. Easton Hall: SUCK MY NUTS, MOTHERFUCKER! Easton storms out of the ring, ripping the tie that held his hair back out and tossing it away. The musical theater types and the choir are left standing, bewildered. Easton Hall: (off-screen, muttering) I stayed up all night smoking some potheads up trying to come up with a Sonny Silver-ish routine, and that motherfucker has the nerve to say I don't have something original to beat him? Fuck that motherfuckin' guy. Choir & Musical Theater Types: BO-BI-NO, YOU SUCK!
