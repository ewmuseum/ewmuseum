Cheating The Reaper (Part 3: Miranda Rights)

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 VII. The Snake Under the dim ceiling light he sat waiting. The stack of papers that lie in front of him was a constant reminder that there were things to get done, but he couldn't concentrate. Something much more important to him was taking place as he sat, fingers drumming on the desk in front of him. He adjusted the rubber band that tied his pony tail back, trying to ease the tension a bit as he stared at the door. Hopefully this time things went a little more smoothly than their last adventure to get what they wanted. Continuing to stare at the door… he wondered where she went off to, and what she was doing. Thankfully she had to be far, far away from here. The drumming of his fingers still echoed through the room as he took a look down at his watch. Ten minutes had gone by since the last time he checked. Maybe something was wrong? Almost as if on cue, the door opened on the far side of the room and a shadowy figure stepped into the darkness and out of the light. He knew what type of sources his target had, and there could be anyone or anything around… it's best that he stays out of sight and out of mind. "It's done Mr. Hathaway." The figure said from the darkness. Hathaway took a deep sigh of relief and responded with a smile. As he adjusted his paperwork he looked into the darkness. "So did anyone see you?" Mr. Hathaway asked. "Just our 'FBI agents', but they obviously didn't suspect anything as did anyone else. It's only natural I'd walk into that room." The person responded, "No one else was around. Christian Zenith has absolutely no idea what's going on. He seems very on edge, I've never seen him like this before. Finally to see the emotionless hard ass crack is a bit entertaining. He doesn't seem to do so well under the manipulation he does to others he cherishes so dear. I suppose it isn't quite as entertaining or satisfying when he's the puppet." "When is he leaving Japan?" Hathaway asked, intrigued by his target's plans. "Christian Zenith is leaving Japan in the morning," The figure responded, "He will be on his way to GTT5 to face off against Danielle Northsky. If he loses it'll be a little easier to make our plans possible. He'll be isolated in Chicago since Global will be taking a small leave for the upcoming holidays." "When will you be arriving in Chicago?" Hathaway asked before pulling out a palm pilot with a look of sheer joy spreading across his face. "Two days." The person responded still shrouded in darkness. "Great." Hathaway jotted something down on his palm pilot before looking back up, "I'll certainly make sure to have a surprise waiting for Mr. Zenith. I need you to hold up your end of the bargain though." "Oh don't worry…" A response came from the shadows, "I'll keep my eyes on him. He won't get very far from me or those 'agents.' He never does. Don't you worry Mr. Hathaway… everything is going exactly as planned. I'm enjoying every moment of watching him squirm." "Good…" Mr. Hathaway responded as the snake in the grass left the room. Things couldn't be better… the only thing he had to worry about now was the wild card. She didn't even know who she was anymore, how much trouble could she possibly cause? VIII. The Right To Remain Silent High above Chicago, Illinois… four people inside of a personal luxury jet liner couldn't have their minds farther away from the cruel reality of what was happening in the city below them as they made their descent. It was just about time for a reality check for The Midnight Messiah. Glasses were filled with champagne after the GTT5 first round victory. Christian Zenith sat in a chair suited for a king of the sky. Next to him on the table sat and empty glass and a manila folder with details on his next victim. Vivica J. Valentine say across from him, still with a smile on her face from watching that bitch Danielle Northsky suffer. What big words for such a little girl, obviously she was in over her head against The Midnight Messiah. Vivica spoke up as Demitri and Jason sipped champagne from the back of the aircraft. "So whose next?" Vivica said with a smile, "Maybe some guy who doesn't know who he is and joined the tournament to find out his identity. Or perhaps a big, dark, and brooding mysterious guy who drinks blood and 'strikes fear into the heart of everyone who crosses his path.' I really do love this place… everyone has to make up some silly story to make people think they're something special. They can never even come up with their own make-up story. Just recycling the myths they heard from the people before them, hoping it'll work again and strike horror into the minds of the ignorant." "You want to know the truth…" Christian said grabbing the folder that lay on the table next to him, "I didn't even take the time to look at this until just now." "Excuse me passengers, I don't mean to interrupt but I have a reminder for you all." A voice spoke over the loud speaker, "We will be landing at a private air-strip here shortly. Remember to buckle up as we approach, and a limo has already arrived to pick you up. Thank you, have a nice trip home." Home… it used to be a place Christian looked forward to going to. Not anymore; it had became some sick and twisted version of what the Catholics would call Hell. Hell on earth, if you believe in that in that religious nonsense. It had just struck him how much he really didn't want to land. If you could believe it or not there seemed to be less drama in fighting these days then there was at home in the city. He couldn't get that note out of his mind. What did they mean they'd get back to him later? Who could've possibly pu… "Well…" Demitri Miasnikov said from the back, "Are you going to open it?" Zenith looked down at the folder in his hand. No wonder he had not opened the information folder yet, his mind had been elsewhere… even if the others didn't feel the same. Most times it seemed they cared much more about his GTT5 advancement than he did. They didn't have a clue about what was really going on. They weren't on that rooftop, and none of them had a clue about the note. "Yeah." Zenith said before opening the folder, "Kasidy Drake?" His announcement of the name brought a blank stare from his Hunting Grounds partners. Either they were in shock, or had absolutely no clue who that was. Zenith was guessing the later. "I have no clue who that is…" Vivica said aloud. You could always count on Viv to be brutally honest about the situation. "Well… does it have his information?" Jason LeMaster spoke up to break the silence. "I'm sure it does somewhere…" Zenith flipped onto the next page where background information and statistics were to be listed, "No Information Provided." "Well that's a fucking waste of a tree now isn't it?" Vivica spouted off almost immediately. "No tournament history…" Christian Zenith continued on, "He's a free agent." "So what… they just picked him up off the street while he was fighting a homeless guy for food and threw him into the tournament as a numbers buffer?" Vivica said, "Or maybe he was too busy trying to screw executives to make it into the tournament that he forgot to fill it out?" That reply brought a chuckle out of Demitri and Jason, who listened on as Vivica rambled. "I don't think so." Christian said before folding up the useless folder and dropping it into a nearby trashcan, "I just don't think he has anything important to say about himself. He's a coward and he doesn't even belong to have his name next to mine on the tournament match listing. The PTC executives just saved themselves some money but not wasting their time typing out statistics and information for some half-ass wannabe professional wrestler who won't make it past the second round." The buckle seatbelt light came on inside and they all reached down and fastened their belts just moments before the plane landed on the airstrip. As they came to a halt, the giant Russian bodyguard looked over at Christian Zenith and spoke. "Last time you didn't know anything about an opponent it was Smiley. Remember that Atlantic City pay-per-view you joined to get information about Dafoe?" "Heh…" Christian Zenith glared back at Demitri, "Even blind squirrels find nuts every once in a great while Demitri. I couldn't give a damn about Smiley or that match… I was only there to get what I wanted from Reno. I did, and I left." Just as that sentence escaped his mouth the door of his private jet busted open and 4 Chicago Police officers in riot gear boarded the aircraft with guns drawn. Everyone in the plane threw their hands in the air accept for Zenith, who closed his eyes and took a deep sigh. "Speaking of blind squirrels…" Zenith said aloud. "Put your hands in the air Christian!" The leader of the police force shouted, "You're under arrest. You have the right to remain silent…" The police force ripped Christian Zenith out of his chair and tossed him onto the ground. Pulling hard they forced his arms behind his back and cuffed them while Vivica, Demitri, and Jason watched on in dismay. Violently they jerked him up to his feet and forcibly pushed him out of the aircraft towards an army of squad cars that surrounded him limo below. "What in the hell is this all about?!" Vivica shouted from inside the plane, but received no answer. She watched helplessly, restrained by 4 officers inside the plane as the door was opened to the squad car. "You know damn well I didn't do a thing…" Christian said as he was shoved into the back seat of the car as uncomfortably as possible. "Yeah right." The officer said before closing the door, "Tell it to the judge. I can't wait to see you get the chair you sick murdering bastard." IX. Memoirs Of A Life That Was Meanwhile in New Mexico… With a deep breath, she pushed open the glass door in front of her and stepped into the vaguely familiar structure. Looking around confused, she walked across the blue carpet. How did she know this place? Bits and pieces were starting to return to her memory, but she was still very lost. At this point in time she felt lucky to be alive and well. The only way to learn more and be safe was to get the hell out of New Mexico… and with the money she stole from those two fuckers who kept her locked in a box. It was now a reality. Where could she go? Did she have a family? A million questions crossed her mind as she walked past a vending machine. Suddenly she stopped and looked back at her own reflection. Her long raven black hair was long and straight, just barely not falling in front of her cadet blue eyes. Hair may be able to cover her eyes, but they could never cover her scars. She ran her hand up and down the side of her neck, feeling the raised surfaces of scars caused by the collar. That collar, she could still even feel the burning in her sleep. That was certainly something she'll never miss nor forget any time soon. A family nearby looked at her. Quickly she acted as if she was fixing her hair, trying to conceal the scars from them. The less questions she was asked the better. Most of them she didn't have any answers for, but she certainly couldn't have anyone getting suspicious. She was still on the run afterall… if someone found out they may catch her and lock her back up in the box. Trying to force the memories out of her mind she started looking around the airport for something to read. Anything to keep her from crying. CHICAGO Once she saw the word she just couldn't get herself to look away. Suddenly images of tall buildings and terrible storms crossed her mind. The water playground at Millennium Park on a sunny day, O'Hare International Airport. An ambush of information flooded through her mind with just the sight of the word. One building in particular stuck out in her mind. A tall black building, made mostly of glass. On the door to the building was a golden Z inside of a circle. The two words beneath it were blurry in her mind. There was something about this place… she knew it. So much she didn't know, this could only be the taste of the information she could learn back in Chicago. Perhaps people knew her… perhaps she had a family waiting for her there to take care of her and tell her about herself. All the answers laid in front of her very eyes in the form of a one-way plane ticket. "Chicago…" She said to herself aloud before looking at the nearest ticket window. Quickly she made her towards it. With no line at all she got to the teller fast. A beautiful red-headed girl stood behind the counter with a blue vest and a smile as she approached. "Hello, welcome to United Airlines." The lady said, "Is there anything I could help you with?" "Yes." She responded, "I'd like a ticket." "Where to?" "Chicago…" She said to the red-headed lady, who quickly began typing. "Okay." The woman said with a smile, "There is one departing in about 2 hours to Chicago, Illinois. Will this be a one way or a round trip flight?" "One way… please." More typing followed her answer. "Your name?" The nice lady asked with a smile. "Tera…" she said with hesitation, "Tera Zenith."
