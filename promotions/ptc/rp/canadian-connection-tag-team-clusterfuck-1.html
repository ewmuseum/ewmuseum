Canadian Connection (Tag Team Clusterfuck #1)

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Canadian Crippler Affiliated Pimp Joined: 21 Mar 2003 Posts: 3 Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 9:19 am Post subject: Canadian Connection (Tag Team Clusterfuck #1) We open up in a lounge type room. There is a big screen TV, Leather chairs and a table. The table has nothing but a phone on it. As we look around the room music is heard coming from another room. The music is Cleaning Out My Closet by Eminem. Seconds later Canadian Crippler appears through the door. He is wearing a Gold’s Gym t-shirt and black jeans. He is also wearing a NY cap. He holds a bag in his hand. He throws the bag on top of the table and walks up to the crew. Canadian Crippler: How’s it going boys? Sorry I took my time before calling you but I had a very special week. First off I am called to a Canadian Connection meeting where i hear some good news. Second I returned home from a house show to hear of my first-born. It’s a boy we named him Brian. He and my girl are still in the hospital right now but don’t worry they are both fine. I told my wife I would stay but she told me I got to go to New Era and win the Tag Team Clusterfuck. So here I am at home picking up my stuff. Canadian Crippler points to the bag on the table and then walks over to it and opens up the zip. He pulls out a very shiny replica XWF championship strap. He then looks back at the camera. Canadian Crippler: This is what it is all about. My XWF championship. I go into New Era and believe me I’m walking away with Rey as number one contenders to the PTC Tag Titles. I may have a rookie for a tag partner but that’s just what happens when you are so good. It don’t matter how good Rey is I make him look even better with my skills. Rey is my partner and I’ll have to work with it, for now at least. I suggest after New Era when Rey and me win that we get the respect that we deserve. WE have teams like DJ and Raven members of The Corporation thinking they can combine to make The Monte Brisas Outlaws and take over. There is no way DJ and Raven are taking away my future title shot. As for The Rodriguez Brothers I can’t say anything. These men are legendary and they made the tag teams what they are today. If I had to lose to any team in the match it would be Chivo and Chezina. DJ and Raven you are the wrong guys in the wrong place at the wrong dam time. Your in for a battle, believe me your in for a war, There is no way in hell I’m being pinned by either one of you guys. The Canadian Connection tag team will reign supreme over all PTC teams. This will be our actual first match as a team. And with Rey we have new plans in the making. All will be revealed...But for now I got to be out. I got to catch a plane Canadian Crippler places the XWF title back in his bag and walks off out of the room as the scene fades. We open up to a thunderous ovation from XWF fans hanging around outside of the official XWF Hotel. It is called XWF Hotel because most of the XWF Superstars are staying there while they are in town. Karen stands outside talking to the crowd. Karen: Well hello everyone. I’m here to get a word from any of the XWF stars that show up. Just then a Dark Match wrestler walks out of the door as Karen approaches him. Karen: Oh I don’t know you. Who are you and why are you here? ?: Well my name... Karen: Ok thanks bye. The wrestler walks away not looking very embarrassed, as Karen turns to see non other than Rey. Karen: Rey...Rey...Any chance of a few words Rey: Sure Karen, Go ahead Karen: Well Rey we are only days away from New Era, What are your thoughts going into the Extreme Clusterfuck Match? Rey: Well Karen knowing I’m the King I know I’m going to be a marked man. I am going into this match as a King and I’m walking away as a King and number one contender for the PTC Tag Team Titles. Karen: Canadian Crippler thanked you for what you had to say... Rey: Did he, That’s great. I was telling the truth. He is the greatest World champion in XWF History. Except for me when I win it. You see I’m already a King. Together with my Canadian Connection partner Canadian Crippler we won’t lose. There is only one thing wrong and that is that we have a lot of hungry tag teams looking for us. They don’t deserve to wear the tag gold. Everyone should just resign for the match and forfeit the win to us. Then all we had to do was wait for the winners of Homo/Phobic and Shadow Riders match to face us. Then we win the titles. It’s as simple as that. Karen: We know you don’t like DJ, Raven or Chezina and we know you dislike Chivo but the question is, Can you really beat these men in your way to winning the Extreme Clusterfuck? Rey: Your damn right I can beat them. Since i arrived in the XWF as a Fucking devil worshipper I have changed an awful lot. If you had asked me that question 6 months ago I would of said Probably but now I’m a Superstar. I have experience, Other than The Monte Brisas Outlaws no other single or Tag team has more experience than me in tag team matches. Karen: Well thanks for talking to us Rey and good luck. Rey: Thanks Karen! Can I borrow that microphone as I got some nuts to say? Karen nods her head and hands over the mic. Rey: Chivo you have had quite the successful career in XWF. Beating people such as La Envidia Mata in one of your biggest matches. On record you have a win over C.R. although you did use the ropes to your advantage. You have been a World champion, Now you want to be a PTC tag team champion for the first time. It isn’t going to happen boy. I will make sure of it. As for your partner Chezina. Chezina you as well have had a very sucessful career in XWF. Corporate champion, you also hold a victory over C.R. Now you also want to become a tag champion. Now don’t get me wrong you are both pricks, you are both pathetic and you are both dreaming. There is no way I’m giving you my win. I might have the greatest partner in the world but I have got my own skills. If need be I’ll take both you guys out on my own. Get ready Boys because the storm is brewing and Rey has arrived. With that Rey drops the microphone and walks out of view of the camera. The scene fades as Karen approaches a different superstar. We open up to the sounds of Without Me By Eminem {No matter how many fish in the sea} {It'll be so empty Without Me} {Hum Dei Dei La La La La La} We can hear Canadian Crippler singing along with the tune that is coming from the radio. Minutes later he emerges from the bathroom wearing only a towel. Seeing the camera's he quickly walks back into the bathroom. You can hear him cursing the camera crew one by one. Seconds later he returns from the bathroom wearing a pair of Blue jeans. We are now standing in the living room of Canadian Crippler’s flat. He is renting the flat for the weekend. Maria walks into the room as Canadian Crippler is brushing his hair. Maria: So you looking forward to your match? Canadian Crippler: Yeah it’s a chance to show what I can do as a tag team wrestler. Maria: I thought you liked tagging with Rey? Canadian Crippler: I do...Its just Rey has been in a real mood lately. Pissing people off for no reason. He must have something on his mind. But there is an upside to me being in singles competition. I can show what I can really do. Maybe i can break more records than I already have. I mean as a tag team I will be PTC Tag Team Champion with Rey. But maybe I should be going for more singles titles and working my way up like the guys. Maria: Well if you’re sure that’s what you want. Aren’t you going out with Rey and Koko B. tonight. Canadian Crippler: Probably yeah...They still pissed at the Beer drinking contest being cancelled. So we are out on the town for one last drinking session before New Era. Maria: OK. Thinking about it that way I’ll be expecting to see you in around 4am at the earliest. Canadian Crippler: Don’t worry I won’t be too late. Koko B. has an important match tomorrow. So he is probably going back to the hotel early. Speaking of which you sorted out that hotel for tomorrow night Maria: Yeah, Its a bit expensive but they are the only hotel that has a room available. Canadian Crippler: Shit i forgot. Maria: What? Canadian Crippler: I had to meet the guys at the gym. I got 10 minutes to get there. I’ll be back at about 5. But I’ll be back out for 7. Do me a favor and cook me a quick dinner before I go out Maria: OK but all we have is beans on toast. Canadian Crippler grabs his keys from the table and runs out the front door. He jumps into his rental car and takes off down the road. The scene comes to a close.
