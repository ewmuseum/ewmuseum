However, when nothing grave happens then people will go on with their own lives thinking about themselves like the selfish people they are. What? You don’t believe me? Then let me ask you this… When is the last time you gave a financially challenge person (a bum in lamest terms) some spare change? Or when is the last time you help your best friend or a family member on a sever problem in life? Like I said selfish… There’s no true pity in this world especially in the world of a wrestler. As a wrestler we cannot show any sign of this feeling toward the one you are facing in the ring even if is a brother, a sister, a friend or a lover you cannot show it and if you do then you will be easily defeated. The only way you can be victorious is to think and fight for yourself rather then fighting for others and others’ purposes as this is the true law as a wrestler. Without pity this world is a harsh and cold one well with it the world is still this way… As most of you already know I’m in this year’s GTT and I’m facing the Exceptional Dave Hurst in the first round who is quite the favorite to advance the first round and probably to advance to the final rounds in people’s eyes. God, you people are worst as moths drawing on to Dave Hurst because of his brilliant reputation in GTT 3 and other tournaments that Primetime Central held over the years. I have news for you moths… All of Dave Hurst’s highlights happened in the past so you all need stop being slaves of the past and be more worried about one’s future. Dave Hurst, you may have been Exceptional in your once glorious career but this time around you will not come out on top instead you will fade away in the shadows like others before you. Try to escape your chosen shadowy path if you want I mean it will be meaningless but yet entertaining to show the moths that you’re nothing more then a tire wrestler who thinks this would be his great chance to comeback in the world of wrestling. Delusions, Dave. They’re just delusions for a feeble-minded wrestler to believe to give them hope on defeating a challenging obstacle. Man, you’re going need a whole lot more than that for our match. It is truly a pity that all you moths must follow one whose spotlight had dim out since he became a free agent.
