Flying the flag for the final time. - Part I

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 To whom it may concern, It has come to my attention that at the end of 2006, the UWF will be closing its doors for the final time. This has come as a shock to many people, because many thought that the UWF was the greatest wrestling promotion in all of the PTC. I am both saddened and disheartened to hear of this most unfortunate turn of events, because I myself had purchased tickets for my son and I to attend UWF Live and Loud: Fandamonium as part of the live audience. Now that I know it's the final event the UWF will ever have, I'm not sure we're going. I'm not sure that I will be able to contain my tears, my heartbreak, and my disappointment. I can't even bear to think about how my son would feel about it. He's only ten years old, and the UWF's been such a prominent part of his life. It's hard to believe that it is all about to be over, and I'm sad to see it go, but it's like they say. "All good things must come to an end." I just wish things didn't have to be this way. In closing, I would just like to say, from the bottom of my heart, thanks for the memories, UWF. You'll soon be gone, but not forgotten. Sincerely, John Jablonski "Well said, John Jablonski. Well said." ___________________________________ [The scene opens inside the foyer of Axel Action's home in Hollywood, Florida. The archway and walls are painted an off-white color. The hardwood floor shines in the sun. Moving further inside the house, we can see that every piece of furniture is polished and dusted. There isn't a speck of dirt on anything. The house is completely spotless. In the background, some music can be heard.] When there's trouble, you know who to call… (Teen Titans? What the Hell?) "Magic finger, my ass!" [One could assume that someone like Axel Action wouldn't be watching something so trivial and meaningless as the Teen Titans. One could also assume he wouldn't be watching cartoons, period. Moving further into the house, we come to the living room, where Axel is sitting on his couch watching the television.] "Kick his fanny, Robin!!!" [Axel sees the camera out of the corner of his eye and jumps to attention.] "Uhm… You didn't see that, okay?" [His eyes widen.] "You mean, you… were taping? Oh God! What were you thinking?!" [Axel becomes frustrated with the camerman's unwillingness to respond.] "Answer me, Roddamn you!" [There's still no response from the cameraman. Axel lets out an angry yell.] "You're hopeless." [The cameraman finally makes a response, but it isn't the one Axel was wanting to hear.] "Axel, you ARE aware that I'm here to get your thoughts on GTT6, right?" [Axel mocks the man.] "Axel, you ARE aware that… Ah, shut your mouth! Do you know that you've got a face that only a mother could love? And yes, I am aware that you're here for my thoughts on GTT6." [A long pause ensues.] … … … … … … … … [Still paused.] … … … … … … … … … … [They pause some more.] … … … … … … … … … … … … [After the long pause, Axel finally speaks.] "The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, an event where anyone can achieve greatness at anytime. GTT is a tournament in which a perpetual nobody can come from out of nowhere and win the whole thing, earning himself a shot at the PTC Global Championship in the process. That's the prize that ninety-five other people are in it for. I, on the other hand am in this tournament for one reason, and one reason only. I'm in this for the UWF. I'm in this to give a good showing in its honor, because as many people are well aware, the UWF is in its final days. I, for one, intend for it to go ut in a blaze of glory, and what better way to do that, than by having one of UWF's roster members win the GTT tournament?" [He strokes his chin.] "I know what you're thinking. You're thinking, 'But Axel, you're an underdog in this tournament. So many people are placing their bets against you.' Do you know what I think? I think it's a bunch of malarky. That's exactly what it is. Anyone with at least half a brain knows that in this tournament, odds don't mean anything. Sure, I'm an underdog. Sure, my chances of winning this tournament aren't great, but I thrive on that. I live for that. I love a good challenge, and GTT will provide just that. My entire career has been an uphill struggle, while others have been coasting along, having everything handed to them based off of past accomplishments, or name value alone. I've had to earn everything I've gotten. I've earned it all, and I will earn my victories in this tournament just the same. " "See, I'm a two time UWF Television Champion, I'm the longest reigning Television Champion in UWF history with over two hundred days to my championship reign. My accolades speak for themselves. So I'm taking that into GTT6, and I'm confident that I will be victorious." [Axel grins slyly.] "And as for my first round opponent, "The New Shit" Bobino…" (I bet you're thinking, "Oh no. Not the trash talk again. Well, pay close attention, because I'm trying something new.) "He's been very impressive as of late in the PTC v. Archibald MacGregor competition, and in other PTC-related events, and I look forward to facing him. He should provide a great challenge." (Here it comes.) "It's really too bad he's not going to make it past me. I have worked too hard in my career to lose this tournament, and I'm not about to let him or anyone else stand in my way. I'm flying the flag for the final time, and I want to make everyone proud." [The scene fades to black.]
