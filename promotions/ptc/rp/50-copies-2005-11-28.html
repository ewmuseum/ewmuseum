Date: 2005-11-28 Audio On Video On Recording June 12, 2002 Hello wrestling world, or better known as the world of sports entertainment. My name is Christopher Kaden Calix, most people just call me Kaden, and I am currently twenty years of age. For as many years as I've been alive, I have known only one thing, wrestling. No matter what type it might have been, I've been apart of if. My father has been pushing me since I was born "to become this great countries best wrestler, and future Olympic gold medalist." Sure, I enjoyed working towards that dream while I was still young and impressionable, but for the last 4 years, or so, I've come to realize that it really isn't my dream at all, instead my fathers. You see, he was scheduled to wrestle a qualifying match to become apart of the Greek Olympic wrestling squad to go the 1984 summer games in Los Angeles. As he would tell the story, he was injured in that qualifying match, and couldn't compete that year, nor could he wrestle or do anything else for the rest of his life. True, he got injured in that match, but it was all staged. He was caught using steroids a few days prior. Although at the time the use wasn't that big of a deal, a steroid user entering the Olympics would have been a big deal. He was threatened, and had he won the match, something would have happened to him. Instead he allowed himself to get into all the wrong positions, and eventually injured in his match, screwing his career up big time, as if it already hadn't. Anyway, back to the fact, despite my vast knowledge in freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestling styles, I've been apart of an underground wrestling (sports entertainment) promotion here in Athens for many years. I've been told my talents could get me on TV, and I've taken it to heart. At the tail end of this tape you will find all the footage that I have of myself inside the ring. After the footage, my contact information will be given. I really hope to be contacted in the near future, and I don't think I'd let anyone down if you give me the opportunity. Oh, and just so you know, this is not a young man's attempt to get away from his deranged father. I love my mother to death; I wouldn't just leave her behind like this. Sure, my dad is out of control, and yeah, it would be nice to have a reason to get out of here and visit the homeland of half my ancestry, but yeah, this, um, yeah, my dad isn't the reason I want to get the Hell out of here. So yeah. I hope you enjoy the footage, and I hope to hear from you. Thanks. Video Cut Audio Still Recording God I hope making 50 of these tapes is enough. Oh shit, I didn't get that thing turned off all the way. Stopped
