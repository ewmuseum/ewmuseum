I'm Just Suggesting Pt 1

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 A loud annoying beep, the sound of an alarm clock blares through a room. The screen comes into focus and shows a woman rolling over in the bed hitting the alarm to shut it off. She sits up with her long dark brown hair covering her eyes. She starts rubbing her eyes and looks at the clock that reads “11:45.” The woman stands up out of the huge king sized bed. She folds the bed covers over and makes the bed. The woman walks away from the bed and walks into another room. She comes out of the room about ten minutes later, her hair now put up and she looks better than she did when she woke up. The female is about five foot six inches tall and maybe one hundred and five pounds maximum. She is now wearing a white t-shirt and tight dark blue jeans. “Doo doo doo,” She hums. She walks over to another door and opens it taking an immediate right out into a hallway. She walks down the hallway into a big living room. A man is sleeping on the couch in a white t-shirt and blue jeans. He has on a plain black hat covering his eyes. The woman walks over to the couch and climbs on top of the man who is asleep. She lifts his hat up and plugs his nose. The man pops up immediately throwing his hat off his head in the process. The female immediately jumps off of him laughing. The man can be distinguished now as “Sensational” Sean White. Sean stands up rubbing his eyes as the woman is laughing at him. “Damn it Emily, I’m going to stop letting you sleep here anymore if you keep doing that,” Sean says. “I can’t help it, you just lie there sleeping, such an easy target,” she says smiling. Let’s go back a little bit and tell you a history about Emily. Emily Marie Jansen is and has been Sean White’s longtime friend. They have known each other since middle school and really became good friends in high school. Sean recently caught up with Emily at a party a couple of months ago. About two weeks ago Emily came over to Sean’s house with a black eye, Sean asked her what happened. Supposedly her boyfriend at the time, named Troy, hit her. This wasn’t the first time that he had been violent towards her. Sean and Emily ended up going to Troy’s house the next day where it was revealed that Troy has befriended Sean’s ex-bodyguards Damien Andre Davidson and John Anthony Michaels. Emily broke up with Troy when Sean was with her and the resolution was ended peacefully. Sean has let Emily stay with him because she did not want to move back in with her parents. Sean has been sleeping on his couch and letting Emily sleep on his king size bed showing that he is a true friend. Some flirting has gone on between the two of them but it has never turned into anything serious, yet. “So what’s on the agenda today?” Emily says. “Nothing,” Sean replies, “I’m probably going to sit here and do nothing all day and night. What about you?” “I was planning on going to the mall, you know, I feel like spoiling myself,” Emily says with a smirk. Emily sits on the couch next to Sean. “Yeah well don’t get used to it, I still need to find a new job,” Sean says disheartened. “I know what you mean, ever since Neon closed down,” Emily looks at Sean who hasn’t said a word since job. “Maybe if I win GTT 4 or get pretty far in it then things will start to pick up,” Sean still doesn’t look too happy after mentioning Neon. “How far are you?” Emily asks. “I am in the second round right now, I defeated Ryan Nox in the first round and it won’t be any different when I defeat JaZon King in the second round,” Sean now gets a smirk on his face. “Who is he and where is he from?” Emily questions. Sean walks over and grabs his hat, he puts it back on his head and sits down on the couch again. “I don’t know, all I know is that he’s not in a PTC federation, but neither am I right now,” Sean stops smirking. “Oh stop that. You know that you’ll find work, you are ‘Sensational’ Sean White,” Emily tries cheering him up. “I know, but I’m pretty much feeling like I should concentrate on GTT 4. It wouldn’t make sense to look for another job right now when I still am representing Neon Wrestling,” Sean seems to be getting very serious. Sean stands up off the couch and walks across the room into his kitchen. Emily stands up after Sean leaves and follows after him. She walks into the kitchen and Sean is grabbing a bowl out of one of his cabinets. “You aren’t mad at me are you?” Emily asks. “Of course not, it’s just a stupid job, hell I’m still making money every minute of the day,” Sean shrugs off Emily’s question after his answer. Sean opens up another cabinet door and pulls out some generic cereal. He opens it up and pours some into the bowl. “Because, I don’t want you mad at me,” Emily adds. “I’m not mad at you,” Sean comments. Sean leaves the cereal down out of the cabinet and opens up the refrigerator door, which is right next to the cabinet. Sean grabs some milk and sets it on the counter while Emily grabs a bowl down from the cabinet. Sean opens up a drawer and grabs a spoon. Sean pours milk into his cereal and sets the milk on the counter. He walks over to his kitchen table, sits down, and begins eating. Emily pours cereal into her bowl and closes up the cereal box. She puts it back in the cabinet and shuts the door. Emily grabs a spoon out of the drawer and puts it into her bowl. She takes the milk from the counter and pours some into the bowl. She closes the milk and puts it back in the refrigerator. She sits down by Sean eating her cereal. The whole time while Sean and Emily are eating silence surrounds them. Sean does not say a thing while Emily doesn’t want Sean made at her even after numerous times telling her that he is not. Sean finishes his bowl, backs away from the table and picks his bowl up. He walks to the sink and turns the water on. He rinses his bowl out and sets it in there. He turns the water off. “What’s in the past is in the past Emily,” Sean says. “What? I didn’t hear you,” Emily wants him to repeat what he said. “What’s in the past is in the past and it should be left there,” Sean repeats himself adding a little bit extra onto it. Sean walks into his living room still engaging in a conversation with Emily. “And what is that supposed to mean?” Emily questions. “It means that I’m leaving things behind me, I’m not going to reflect on those and blame someone for them, that’s what it has been like with me. I’m not going to be a new person, I’m still the same old ‘Sensational’ Sean White, not some Nick Nobody.” Sean finishes talking and walks back into his living room. “That’s good to hear,” Emily says. Sean looks at the clock in his living room, it now reads “12:05.” Sean walks back towards the kitchen door and leans in, “You might want to get going, don’t want to be out to late.” “But it’s only noon,” Emily says. “So? You’re all ready to go. I just figure why don’t you get it out of the way?” Sean states. “You’re kicking me out aren’t you?” Emily questions. “No not at all. If you want to stay you can, I’m just suggesting it,” Sean says. “Fine then I’ll go,” Emily says with a cocky attitude. Emily gets up and walks out the back door, right next to his kitchen table. Sean doesn’t think anything of it and he continues doing things around his house. First, Sean grabs Emily’s bowl and rinses it out placing it on top of his bowl in the sink. Sean then pushes her chair in and shuts the back door locking it in the process. Sean heads out of his kitchen, passing through his living room and down the long hallway. Sean takes the first left and goes into a bathroom. The door shuts right behind him. About fifteen minutes later Sean comes out with a towel around his waist. Sean takes a left right when he exits the bathroom and walks to his room. Sean turns right at the end of the hall into the room. Sean takes a right at the door to his dresser. He pulls out some boxers, socks, shorts, and a black t-shirt. Sean puts all the clothes one in the order of boxers, shorts, shirt, and socks. Sean walks over to his closet and pulls out a pair of white Adidas shoes. Sean sits on his bed and puts the shoes on. After the shoes are put on Sean lays down in the bed, feet hanging off. “JaZon King, I’m not really one to trash talk but when I have to I can. Are you supposed to be extreme JaZon King? Do you get off by brawling and being hardcore? You have never met someone with the technical caliber of ‘Sensational’ Sean White. You will meet your destiny, the destiny of defeat. I will run you over...” Sean stops talking as his phone begins ringing. Sean checks his caller ID, “Cell Phone” is what it says. Sean picks up the phone. “Hello...what the...yeah...they haven’t done anything have they? Good....I’ll be right there.” Sean hangs up the phone, throws it down on his bed and speeds out of his room. He runs down the hall, taking a left in his living room going down his stairs. Sean takes another quick left jumping down his smaller set of stairs and then takes a right at the door. Sean opens his door and shuts it behind him grabbing his keys off the shelf. Sean pushes a button and his garage door opened. Sean pops a button on his key and his suicide door on the Dodge Viper pops open. Sean gets it, turns his car on and backs out of the driveway. The garage door closes automatically after Sean pushes a button on it. Sean speeds down the street out of sight. TO BE CONTINUED...
