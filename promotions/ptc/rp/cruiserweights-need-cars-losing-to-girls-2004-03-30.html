Cruiserweights need cars & losing to girls

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-30 Promo Begin For those that don't know I am the narrator of the promos done by XUW superstar Ric Austin. Yea it's a living. Ric Austin is the former XUW world champion and self proclaimed most hated man in XUW. However, surprisingly enough these series of promos are not about Ric Austin. Well not entirely, because the name on the sign up sheet was not that of the 250 pound egotist. The name on the sign up sheet was of his manager and sidekick Robert Theodore Temporary more commonly known as Bob The Temp. But why Bob The Temp who has never wrestled a singles match in his life? Why the 180 pound member of the Ric Austin Show who is in the worst physical condition of the three? To just put it the question in the simplest terms, WHY BOB THE TEMP? Hopefully all these questions will be answered in this promo. Now we rejoin the present as we are inside the locker room of the Ric Austin Show. The three me inside the locker room are: Ric Austin the egotistical jerk who is the only member of the group who actually wrestles, Derrick Miles who is the token black man who is also the tokken disabled person because he is mute and communicates by looking at people, and finally Bob The Temp who is the reason we are all here. Like usual the three are sitting inside the Ric Austin Show locker room doing absolutely nothing when there are matches to train for. Austin: Oh great it is that narrator guy again. That means it time for us to talk about my match this Sunday with Mr. Amazing for the Tag Titles. Bob: Actually Ric I don't think that narration was for you. You do have a tag match on Sunday with Mr. Amazing at reborn but I think he was here to do a promo about my first round match in the cruiserweight tournament. Austin: Bob, who's name is that on the front door before the word "show"? Bob: Do we really have to go through this again? I mean we know the whole series of questions the answer is going to be yours so... Austin. Right, now who's the one who is a former XUW world champion? Bob: Look you are but this is really getting... Austin: Thank you, so I think that they are going to come and do a series of Role plays about Ric Austin who is the former world champion before they do a series on Bob The Temp who is wrestling his first singles match. Ok? Derrick gives Ric a look that says, "Actually if you want my opinion..." But he is cut off in mid-look by Ric Austin. Austin: I don't think I did ask for your opinion now did I Derrick? Bob: What's wrong Ric, are you perhaps jealous that for once the spotlight is on some one else in the group and not on you? That while you are just teaming with Mr. Amazing for a shot at the tag titles in XUW, I am in a tournament for the new cruiserweight title here in the PTC. Technically if I win this tournament my cruiserweight title would outrank your XUW world title. Austin: How do you figure that, I was champion of the entire world? Bob: Simple, you were just champion of the entire world as it pertains to XUW. I will be champion of all the cruiserweights including those of XUW, RPW, UWF, and all other federations in the PTC. Austin: Yea but how can you be a cruiserweight with a loser car? I mean come on Bob you drive a mini-van. You can't cruise in mini-van. To cruise you need something like a Dodge Viper, a Ford Mustang, a BMW Z3, and other cars that are like that. Derrick gives Bob a look that says, "I actually have to agree with Ric on this one. Unless you pimp out your mini-van it is very hard to cruise in it. You have to tint the windows really dark and you have to put in some 15 speakers. And I mean even then it is still hard to pick up girls in a mini-van." Bob: Why would I want to pick up girls in my mini-van? I'm married and have kids Derrick. That is why I own the mini-van and I don't own a viper or a mustang. Austin: Well if you win the tournament you need to get rid of the mini-van and get a faster car that you can cruise in. Bob: Again I have a wife and kids Ric. If I get a small car I won't be able to fit them all in it. Austin: Well my advice is that if you win, you need to divorce your wife and marry a hotter woman anyway. I mean lets face it dude, chicks dig gold and if you win that title they will be all over you. Derrick and Bob just stare at Ric for a second as if they can't believe what he just said. Bob: Do you think before you say anything or do the stupid and insensitive things you say just pop right out of your mouth naturally? Austin: Huh? What do you mean? Bob: Well you see for most people we think before we say things. For example I wouldn't tell someone that they should divorce their wife and marry someone hotter. I have something in my brain called common sense that stops my stupid impulses. Do you just not have that impulse or do you ignore that little voice that says, "Don't say that Ric, it will piss Bob off"? Austin: Hmmmm. You know I have never thought about that before. I guess it all has more to do with the fact that I just really don't give a damn what you think and I could personally care less if I piss you off. Bob: And for some reason your answer does not surprise me. Derrick gives both Bob and Ric a look that says, "Look guys we need to stop arguing and we need to focus. Bob has a match this Sunday with Tessa Windsor and McFarlaine. Shouldn't that be our main focus?" Austin: You have a point my silent associate. Now since both your opponents are female I have only one piece of advice for you Bob. If you lose, don't come back to the Ric Austin Show locker room because you aren't welcome. Bob: What? That isn't fair. Why am I not allowed back in the locker room if I lose this match on Sunday? Austin: Well Bob to put it simple, women can do anything men can. Men can run for political office, they can compete in the business world, and some are outstanding athletes. Bob: You have yet to answer my question. Why can't I come back to the locker room on Sunday if I lose the match? Austin: Hold on, I'm getting to it. I just want to make sure I am cover all of my politically correct bases here so I want get a bunch of Ric Austin is a chauvinist e-mail. Now like I said, women are equal to men in many areas but that doesn't make it okay to lose to them. Losing to a woman is perhaps that most emasculating thing that can happen to a man. Bob: Well, what about that time you lost to Sheri Dedmon in the world title tournament. As I recall you didn't make a big deal about that now did you? Infact all you did was say how you were going to beat her next time. Austin: Well that was different. You see Bob, though I might have lost a match to Sheri look what she lost in return. I took away her Boyfriend by crippling him, I cheated her out of the tournament later on, and I also put her though a flaming table later on. So yes she beat me in a match, but I ended up beating her in the end. Really I beat her, she didn't beat me. If you lose on Sunday there is no rematch and no chance for revenge so you are automatically the loser. Do you understand? Bob: The only thing I understand is that you lost to Sheri and now you are trying to say that if I lose in what is essentially a situation where I don't have to be pinned to lose, then I am worse than you. Your logic is stupid and it makes no sense because you don't justify the actions of others you only justify your actions. Austin: Such is the logic of Ric Austin. I make rules to suit myself and when they no long apply to me, I change them so that they do. Derrick gives Ric a look that says, "Yes but doesn't that make your opinion less effective. What I am trying to say is that if you are always using hypocritical logic, then people are less likely to take your opinion serious. You can't just make rules that suit you when you need them to. Imagine if our government did that, it would be utter chaos in our country with only a small minority of the population holding all the wealth." Ric and Bob just look at Derrick wondering how he gave a look like that Austin: You know eventually your face is going to freeze and you are going to be stuck with that same stupid expression on your face for the rest of your life and then I am going to laugh. Bob: He does make a good point though Ric. Since I can't remember what it is I guess we should just go and get something to eat? Derrick just gives them both a look that says "But you both were pissed at each other because Bob might lose to a girl. We never resolved th conflict. This makes no sense!!!!" Austin: Fine if Bob loses to a girl he can stay in the group but I get to mock him endlessly. There happy now mutey? Now I'm hungry lets go get some Wendy's. Derrick gives Bob a look, "But this makes no sense!!!!" Bob: If it made sense then it wouldn't be a Ric Austin Show promo now would it. Come on lets go to Wendy’s. The Three men leave the Ric Austin Show locker room and head out to the limo that is waiting out in the parking lot so that they may go to the local Wendy's to get something to eat. Once again they leave the audience with nothing resolved. The promo begins to fade to black on the Ric Austin Show locker room as we go to commercial. Promo End
