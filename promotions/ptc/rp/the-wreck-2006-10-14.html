The Wreck

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 I woke up in my hotel room at about nine o’clock in the morning with an alcoholic haze still holding a firm grasp over my senses. A beam of light shone through the smallest of gaps between the room’s curtains right into my eyes. For a brief second the thought of getting up and closing them crossed my mind, but fuck it, I was too vexed – I’d squint and bare it. I’d need to get up soon; I had an appointment with Dr. Keaton in a couple of hours, but at this point in time it was going to take a lot more than an appointment with the good doctor to drag me from bed. My head was pounding and my vision was blurred – all I wanted to do was lay low for an hour or so. Alcohol was a poison I had only recently developed a taste for – having spent the first twenty seven years of my life completely sober, I’d taken up drinking last year when the damned back injury that has followed me around for the past five years decided to rear its ugly. It’s not like my exile from professional wrestling turned me into an alcoholic – far from it, I knew what I was doing, and I could usually keep things under control. But there was occasionally the odd night where everything just went crazy… last night one was one those nights… I could tell from the headache that was currently pounding through my head. I could tell by the fact that all I wanted to do was shy away under the cheap sheets of this horrendously uncomfortable bed. I could tell from my memory of the previous evening’s events – or the lack there of. What the hell happened last night? … and then she reached across from here side of the bed, draping her arm gently across my chest. Patches of the night came flooding back into my memory. ----------------------- Was this really Andy M. I was waking up next to? The very Andy M. who I’d seen on AWC television those months ago? Wow! This was great… wait till I tell all my girls… Even though I’d just woken up, I felt great already… and almost safe, with my arm draped across his still muscular chest. Last night was great! So many good memories… out in the club having a great time with the girls, and then leaving with a genuine sports superstar! My eyes closed, I smiled at the thought of this, and slowly drifted away again… ----------------------- I remember starting off in one some nondescript bar, nothing special, just a couple of beers with a couple of old friends, just chilling out, catching up… clearly, what started off as a "couple of drinks" escalated into something greater. Next thing I remember, several drinks down the line, I was in some shitty nightclub, dancing to some dreadful 1980’s synth-pop bullshit. I must have met this girl – damn, I can’t even remember her name – there, and, well, you know the rest. I turned around a little and sat a little more upright, resting the back of my head against the padded headrest at the top of the double bed. I took a good look around the room, lit only by the beam of light creeping through the gap in the curtains. It was a lot, well, "prettier," than I had remember… tastefully decorated, cream and deep navy blue being the dominant colours. Shame I hadn’t kept the place in better knick – there was clothes, newspapers, all kinds of stuff lying around. So I looked down at this nameless girl I had spent the night with. To be fair, I could have done a lot worse – even through my alcohol-induced haze I could see that she was FAR from being an unattractive girl. Long, black hair, tanned, clear complexion… and with the sheets being quite far down on the bed, an anything-but-quick glance downwards revealing a figure that most woman would love to posses… Wait a minute, what the hell was I thinking!?! ‘Snap the hell out of it, Andy!’ I told myself in my head. I took what seemed like a long, hard look at myself in the mirror directly across from me on the dresser. What the hell was I thinking? I was a father, for God’s sakes… I used to have a strong, promising wrestling career and here I was, hungover, lying in a bed in a hotel room next to a girl I don’t even know the name of! Immediately and somewhat forcefully I removed her arm from her chest – my clouded mind suddenly became a lot clearer. Unsurprisingly, she stirred, seemingly waking and raising her hair-veiled head from the pillow. Squinting, she looked up at me with a somewhat accusing glance. All I could do was shake my head… ----------------------- What the hell just happened? One minute I’m lying here in bliss, the next Andy’s practically throwing me arm off of him? Something wasn’t right. "Hey…" I managed to croak, my voice was ragged from the previous night’s extravagances. "What was that for?" I could see him shaking his head, something was definitely up. "Nothing, just leave…" he almost whispered back at me, a stern look strung across his face. I was still a little bit under the influence, but I could tell from the tone in his voice and the look on his face that he was being deadly serious. They’re all the fucking same. ----------------------- I could tell that by telling her to leave I’d upset her, but frankly, I didn’t give a damn. I needed to get out of this; these debauched nights were getting me nowhere. By God… if one year ago I could see the situation I’m in right now I’d have been disgusted. "What?" she barked back, almost demanding some sort of explanation. If that’s what she was looking for, she certainly wasn’t going to get it, I simply did not have the energy to explain anything to her – I was shattered. She sat up, grabbing most of the sheet and holding it closely against her person. She was displeased, and clearly didn’t want to give me the pleasure of gazing upon her near-perfect figure… Fuck, there I go again. Snap out of it! "Leave, please…" I reiterated in a louder tone of voice this time in effort to get my point across. Shaking her head, the nameless woman let out a deeply disappointed sigh – obviously, last night had meant a heck of a lot more to her than it did to me. I didn’t care. I was sick of the sight of her. ----------------------- What a jerk. Andy M. was an asshole! I didn’t even want to talk to this miserable piece of trash, and as I let out a sigh, I turned round immediately, dropped the sheet from around my body, and stood upright. At this point I didn’t give a shit if that scumbag was gonna watch me get dressed – I was gonna get out of this place as soon as possible. This isn’t something new to me. The first thing I came across whilst rummaging around the floor was my frilled, white blouse, which I grabbed and threw around my shoulders – really, I had no energy to take the time to find my bra, it was unnecessary. Soon enough I was dressed, high heels and everything. Stepping up from the bed, I walked across towards the dresser and grabbed my handbag, without a pause to even glance upon that vile pig of a man, I headed across the room and straight out the door, making the extra effort to slam it particularly hard. This wasn’t the first time I had been in this situation, but one thing was for sure – it certainly would be the LAST time… Fuck Andy M. He’s no different from all the other scumbags out there after all. ----------------------- Thank God she was gone, and I was left alone in the room with nothing to keep my company but my reflection in the mirror. This is what my life had become over the past half a year or so. I’d become a shadow of my former self… sleeping around like some kind of drunken frat boy. I’m better than this… well, at least I used to be… I need to get back into wrestling. I need to get my life back on track… but I can’t just shake off this damned back injury. This was going to take one hell of a push.
