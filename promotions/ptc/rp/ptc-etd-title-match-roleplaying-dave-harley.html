ETD Title Match Roleplaying

19 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Embracing Extreme (RP 1) I like to think of my basement as an archive. My life’s work documented. In the far corner, I keep a trophy case. Before I joined the WWA it was pretty bare – I had a few Tag Team Trophies from my time in Japan and the EWA Extreme Title. Since joining the WWA, I’ve added a lot to the shelves – 2 Tag Titles, the GenX Title, the TV Title, the Intercontinental Title… In the centre of the trophy case is a large space. Of a night, this is the resting place of the WWA World Heavyweight Title. At the moment, it sits snugly around my waist – my crowning achievement in the world of professional wrestling. I won the Title at last years Birthday Bash Pay Per View. Since then, my career has reached unprecedented heights. I owe most of this to stabbing my long time friend Mal Somers in the back. Since assaulting him and leaving him for dead in the centre of the ring I have become new leader of the Devil’s Own, WWA World Champion and have even managed to persuade WWA President Matt Warr to jump on the Devil’s Own bandwagon. My goal of achieving control of the entire company draws ever closer and I owe it all to my ego. In the centre of the far wall sits a projector screen. In front of it, a single chair awaits the feature presentation. Glancing around the room, I take in the countless shelves filled with Dave Harley archive footage. Every time I have ever been in front of a camera I have brought the footage back here and catalogued it. I may not be the tidiest guy in existence but when it comes to my own videos I am meticulous. The archive starts long before I began wrestling. Hell, there are tapes here that date back to my childhood. My parents bought one of those old chunky-looking video cameras when I was just a small boy. Even at such a young age I would pose and play up to the camera. Further along the shelf are tapes of my training sessions in the local Chicago gym. I would later meet my future tag team partner (and the first person to ever feel my wrath) Jonny Carnage there. He recommended me to his father and I trained at their family gym in Detroit until I was ready to hit the ring for real. My footage then jumps to my first tour of Japan, with Jonny as my partner. I learnt an awful lot on that first stint in Japan. Most notably, I learnt the strong style of wrestling. I learnt how to take some serious punishment and deal it out in return. You could say I started to learn how to be extreme. But of course, we weren’t touring with any ‘extreme’ Japanese companies. We never participated in Deathmatches or anything like that. But I discovered what it was to be a legitimate tough guy. My footage of my independent work in the US is pretty sketchy. I could do with fleshing out this area, but getting hold of tapes from defunct promotions can be tough. Of course, I have the footage of myself intentionally crippling my partner Jonny Carnage. Hell, that footage is now legendary. In fact, I’m convinced it was that footage which landed me a job in Phil Flaws’ Euromerican Wrestling Alliance. In the EWA, I was allowed to let myself loose extreme wise. From my EWA debut, I was often paired against the likes of ‘Extreme Icon’ JC Swinger or my future associate (who I’m proud to say I betrayed) ‘ChairMan’ Mal Somers. The highlight piece of EWA footage for me was their final show, ‘Wholesale Aggression’. It was here that I cemented my position as a hardcore icon by becoming the last ever EWA Extreme Champion. The countless shelves that follow are my WWA collection. Every promo I ever cut, every in-ring segment I ever participated in and every match I ever graced…it’s all right here. The Ultimate Dave Harley Collection. I peruse the shelf, looking for inspiration. As a contender to the PTC Extreme Title I’ll need to find my extreme muse. Many consider the final match in the X Corp v The Cell series as one of my crowning achievements. It’s a match which has come to be known as the Burning Hell In The Cell. Put four former EWA Hardcore participants into a cell coated in a flammable gel (of course, we didn’t know that at the time). Add an exploding golf cart (I guess you had to be there…) and you have Extreme magic! Personally, I find that particular match contains far too much Mal Somers for my stomach to take. Glancing down a few shelves, I find my GenX Title reign. This belt would later become the WWA Extreme Title (although it was originally, but it’s a long story). My biggest title defence involved me competing in an Inferno Match, a Ladder Match and a Cell Match all in one night. I called it ‘Three Stages of Extreme’. Unsurprisingly, I came out on top. They don’t call me the Extreme Machine for nothing. I would lose the GenX Title in another gimmick match – a match of my creation, one which would later win a WWAmmy, voted ‘Match of the Year’. It was called the Tokyo Showdown. Christ, I must have been out of my mind. It involved a Godzilla suit and a scale replica of Tokyo. The less we mention it, the better. I decide to abandon my quest for hardcore inspiration and instead find myself drawn towards an old photo album of myself and my parents. They were a middle class family who wanted their son to go on to Law School. They were disappointed when I chose a life of wrestling over my Legal studies. I haven’t spoken to them in years. I thought about contacting them when I became World Champion…but then I realised just how much I hate speaking to members of the public. Hell, they might even ask me to sign an autograph for my little cousin Jimmy and his three friends. I just couldn’t go along with that. My eyes are drawn towards a battered old videotape which sits in the top corner of my oldest tapes. It looks as though it hasn’t been played for years, so I decide to throw it in and see what it holds. If it’s nothing special, I’ll be able to use it tomorrow night for the Baywatch Marathon. I dim the lights as the video whirrs to life. The projector screen is lit and a grainy picture flickers on to it. Voice: Wave to the camera Davey! Aw Christ, baby footage! It’s a good job there’s nobody else around to see this. It’d be pretty embarrassing. My mother was a simple woman. Perhaps a little too simple. My Dad on the other hand… Dad: C’mon Jan, you’re wasting perfectly good video tape! …Yeah. He was always interested in watching me perform. I watch as a gorgeous little boy toddles on to the screen. Hey, it’s me! What a surprise! Dad: What’s that you’ve got in your hand son? In Baby Harley’s hand is a toy gun. Sticking out of the barrel is one of those little toy darts with a sucker on the end. You know what I’m talking about. Dad: I’ve told you, I don’t like you playing with guns! Mom: Frank! Don’t get so angry dear. Come on sweetie, give it to Daddy. An evil smile creeps across Baby Harley’s face as he looks his mother square in the eye. I’ve seen that look before. It’s the same look I had in my eye before I Harley Bombed Jonny Carnage through the mat. It’s the same look I saw in my eyes on the video of my attack on Mal Somers. Mom: No Davey. Give it to your Father. Oh, I give it to him alright. Right in the ba… Dad: AAAAAARRRRRRRGGGGHHHHH!!! OH, FOR THE LOVE OF GOD! So that’s why I never had any brothers or sisters. That evil smile is still plastered all over my face. I clearly enjoyed his agony. I relished in his pain. That’s when I realise that what I’ve been searching for this past hour was under my nose the entire time. Forget my various hardcore matches. Forget ring success. To be hardcore, to be extreme, is a state of mind. For all my past accomplishments wrestling an extreme style, the most basic principle of extreme has been within me since I was a child. To achieve my goal of conquering the PTC Extreme Division, I need to harness that power. I must embrace my most primal urge – the urge to harm. I used it on Jonny Carnage. I used it on Mal Somers. Hell, I even used it on my old man! And now, the best that PTC has to offer will fall under the Extreme Machine. The WWA already knows. PTC is about to find out… The Devil Will Have His Day.
