YORI YAKAMO JR vs. THE TERMINATING DILDOS FROM THE FUTURE v. APEMAN HITLER

Date: 2006-11-03 (cue up the theme music from underrated 80's sitcom, Soap) "When we last left Yori, things weren't going so well for our plucky pervert. He was entered in the prestigious GTT6 tournament, but ran into Y-2000, a very large and very angry terminating sex robot from the future, sent back in time to kill him. MIKE RANDALLS arrived just in the nick of time to save Yori from a very unseemly end at the hands of a spiked vibrating dildo, and the two escaped into the past by means of the YOR-ABBITTO, the Mexican cut-rate equivalent of Yori's infamous time traveling vibrator, the YOR-abbit. There they met JESUS CHRIST who was busy hunting vampires. Will Yori get back in time for the second round of the tournament? Will the army of rampaging sex toys overwhelm our heroes? And will Smitty ever find out who her real mother is? The answer to at least one of these questions await you next in YORI YAKAMO, JR VS. THE TERMINATING DILDOS FROM THE FUTURE VS. APEMAN HITLER --- It was the early 80s, and Yori was in Bangkok on vacation with the rest of his family. It was here that Yori first became fascinated with the musical possibilities of a Thai Hooker marching band. But that is another story. He was visiting various street markets with his mother, who was looking for a nice throw rug to put under the coffee table in their modest apartment. There under a strange looking pergola in the corner, Yori saw it sitting in a mason jar. HITLER'S BRAIN- 500 BAHT Now, Yori didn't have the faintest idea who Adolf Hitler was, or that purported Hitler brains were a staple of 50's tag sales in America Suburbia. All he knew is that it was the coolest thing he had ever seen. So he did what any six-year old without 10 Baht to his name would do. He stole it. It ended up in the family garage, under Yori's old red wagon (The Lady-OH! Flyer), piles of newspapers, and the remnants of a ten year old Yori's first, and tragically unsuccessful, attempt at making a life size sex robot fashioned in his own image using old pizza boxes, copper wiring, a 256 count box of Crayola crayons, and the motherboard from his parent's Apple IIGS. The YOR-OBOT ZERO didn't live very long, thankfully, though long enough to try and violate the family car before Yori's older brother Hida put it out of its misery upon coming home from cricket practice that afternoon. Then one day, while searching through the garage for the boxed of Thai Hooker Monthly that his mother had thrown out, fourteen year old Yori came upon that old brain, still sitting in its Mason jar, now cracked and dusty. Yori had a science fair coming up next week, and he hadn't even started on his project. There had to be something he could do with that brain. The next week, Yori proudly presented his exhibit in his junior high science fair, APEMAN HITLER. He got a C-. It was his best grade ever. Of course, he probably would have been failed if his science teacher knew that he had paid a disgruntled and Quaalude-addicted Tokyo Zoo Vet to steal him an ape, and then transplant the brain into it. Or if the teacher had still cared enough about her profession to seriously question the scientific merit of transplanting Hitler's brain into a primate. But Yori's was the last exhibit she had to grade, and she really wanted to make Happy Hour in Rippongi. But now Yori was stuck a super intelligent ape that just wanted to sit around and paint watercolors all day, and occasionally pleasure itself. Yori wasn't entirely sure what to do with it. He tried to return it to the zoo, but that lasted about a week. Sure, the ape got the little baboon train that circled the primate exhibit to run on time, but only through the forced conscription of a number of marmosets. And then he threatened to invade the Marmot exhibit, so the zoo vet had to send him back to Yori. Yori got bored with Apeman Hitler pretty quickly. Sure, he got to learn some German, which would serve him well when trying to ply co-eds when he spent a Spring in Munich trying to find himself, but he spent way too much time trying to clean the animal's feces off the wall. He could never bring a girl over when Apeman Hitler was around either. The damn dirty ape kept trying to ply Yori's lovely ladies with blackberry brandy and get her to have a threesome with him and Yori. Now granted, Yori past was littered friends like that, but in an ape with a Hitler mustache it was kind of creepy. Eventually, just before Yori's twentieth birthday, he donated Apeman Hitler to a local college that was doing animal research. It was a bit of a bittersweet moment for Yori. Apeman Hitler and he had some good times together, but he just couldn't keep taking care of a racist ape bent on world conquest. He didn't envy what was going to happen to Apeman Hitler now, but the bastard shouldn't have tried to ethnically cleanse his Thai Hooker cabaret act. He gave away all the ape's watercolors to an outsider art gallery that was fascinated with the primate's use of his own feces in his media. The early reviews for the exhbit were quite good, with the art editor of Toyko's alt-weekly calling it "boldly primitive and accessibly avant-garde." Yori couldn't bear to go to opening night. He just sat in his room, drinking blackberry brandy, and weeping softly while watching Escape from the Planet of the Apes. What Yori didn't know is that a few weeks later, Apeman Hitler escaped from the research lab. Or that a passing trucker would stop and pick him up, thinking he was a very ugly woman, what with his fur shaved and covered in cosmetics. Or that he would escape into the mountains of Hokkaido, enslave a small village of fisherman and build an iron fortress 50 miles under the earth where he would plot revenge against the man who had tried to sell him into ape slavery. A former friend. Yori Yakamo, jr. --- Yori was getting tired of time travel. Sure, a sexual liaison with Catherine the Great was interesting in theory, but she had proven to be a bit of a cold fish. Yori got tired of having to complete an equestrian course to get her all hot and bothered every time he wanted to bed the Russian Tsarina. Plus, she wouldn't loan him any Cossacks to help fight this terminating sex robot that kept following him around. Randalls had suggested that they visit the dinosaur people that live in the center of the earth after that, but Yori just thought he was popping on ludes and didn't actually here what he said. He was popping on ludes, of course, but Randalls was serious. "When I was working for the CIA in the sixties, the dinosaur people were common knowledge. We reverse engineered some alien technology to try and create the perfect spy plane, but when I went for a test flight over Antarctica, I detected a human settlement underneath the ice. We went down for a look and discovered a settlement of dinosaur men who had escaped the massive climate change by finding passage into inner earth through a chasm in Antarctica. There they evolved into bipeds and developed their own language and society. They are incredibly fearful of outsiders, and when they discovered us they butchered my partner Murphy. Ah, poor bastard. He was a good man. A drink for Murphy!" Seven quarts of vodka later, Randalls passed out on the floor of Yori's 18th century Russian hut. When he woke up a few hours later, he continued his story. "The dinosaur men captured me and held me for days in one of their gulags. They forced me to fight other criminals in gladiatorial combat, like human, and dinosaur-man, cockfighters. After five years I won my freedom, though I had to kill my best friend in prison, Igni. Poor Igni, a true warrior and a true friend. Even if he was a reptilian bastard. Anyway, as luck would have it, a few days later I was passing through the village square when I stumbled upon a group of T-Rex hoodlums accosting a young lady. I let out my pent up rage against these poor thugs, and their green, viscous blood flowed everywhere. The woman thanked me profusely and I soon found out that she was the governor's daughter, who had snuck into the casbah to see what the common dinosaur man's life was like. The governor gave me a commendation for my valor and said if I ever needed a favor, to come and see him. So I'm thinking an army of dinosaur people could totally take an army of rampaging sex robots. What do you think?" Yori pondered this for a moment. "I think anything is better than getting saddle sore from having sex with Catherine the Great." With that proclamation, the YOR-ABBITO was fired up, and Yori, Randalls, and the still severed head of ROBOYORI headed back into the green portal, and back to the present day. --- The duo couldn’t believe their eyes. The underground Antarctic city of the Lost Dinosaur People was in ruins. Devastation surrounded them on all sides. The Dinodome, where Randalls had fought dinosaur men in gladiatorial combat every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. (Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday were flea market days) had been reduced to rubble. Inside, the enslaved dinosaur men and women labored to create a giant concave dish made out of metal and glass, while under the oppressive yoke of an army of lobotomized Hokkaido fishermen. Yori’s cellphone began to play the midi chorus of "You Give Love a Bad Name" signaling that he had a text message, but it also signaled his presence to the overseers and dino collaborators. They were on Yori and Randalls in a flash, electrified pikes gleaming in the thin rays of light that made it through the icy surface. Randalls took down one, two, three, before he was shocked into submission. Yori did as Yori does, cowering behind a rock, and waving an eleven inch dildo at any who drew near. The dinosaur men quickly surrounded him and hurled invectives at him in their foreign tongue. Had Randalls still been conscious he would have translated them as "coward" and "pink scum" and one phrase that had no clear counterpart in human language, but approximated a feeling of both revulsion and superiority. They were about to strike a fatal blow to Yori before one of the Hokkaido Fisherman stepped in and stopped them. He shocked Yori into unconsciousness and Yori faded away into dream land. --- "Ahhhhhhhh, RABBIT!" Yori was having a bad trip, the multi-colored, green rabbit-owned discothèque that existed in his usually Quaalude-addled brain was melting before his very eyes. He was powerless to stop it, and he watched in horror as the giant green rabbit who may or may not be God was slowly reduced to ash, by the green-grey fires that consumed the dance floor. The rabbit’s haunting cries pierced through the soundless landscape and Yori begin to run, but he didn’t seem to be getting anywhere. After what seemed like an eternity, he found himself at the edge of the landscape, with nowhere to go. He sat there for days, thirst and madness consuming him. Finally, a visitor arrived in a 1984 Cutlass Supreme. "Meow." "Not now Toonces." "Meow? "Wait a minute. TOONCES! Get me out of here Toonces." Yori jumped into the passenger seat and the car began to float into the empty sky. "Yori, you are ze biggest fool in der world. Zu shood have killed me when you had ze chance." "Toonces?" Slowly the head of the Cat Who Could Drive a Car morphed into an equally familiar visage. But one far more horrifying. "APEMAN HITLER? AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!" Yori awoke with a shudder. He lay on the cold, dank floor of a prison cell. "Phew. It was only a dream." "It was no zuch thing, Herr Yakamo, I assure you." Standing at the prison bars was an old friend, with the same awkward looking ape mustache he had when they had parted some ten years ago. "Apeman Hitler. I thought you were dead." "Oh, if only it vere zo, Yori. If only it were zo. Soon enough, you vill vish that I was. Almost as much as you vill vish that you vere." Apeman Hitler’s ape-like, yet still maniacal, laughter filled the corridors of his underground gulag. A sound Yori had not heard in years. A sound that filled him with unmitigated terror.
