Further origins of Fae, Happycoreosity

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-01 Once again, we don’t open on Fae. Well, we do, in a way...we open on the same man, but not Fae. Not the scruffy, disheveled man we saw previously, but a lean, muscular man, with long, black hair, a black goatee, a tight black t-shirt, black bondage pants, and a leather trenchcoat that looks like something a priest might wear in some kind of weird movie. From the setting, we can assume that this is the Domicile of Darkness. From the timestamp on the film, we can guess that this is also early 2003. Which means that this must be Sidhe Nimh. One of the most decorated athletes in XUW history – rookie of the year, former Intercontinental champ, former Tag champ, former TV champ, former Hardcore champ, and former World champ. A man once knows as Sam Stellars, and now known as the Fallen Fae. He also happened to be a nut, but no one really realized to what degree. What began as a slight predilection towards the bizarre turned into a love of the out and out surreal, and a descent into a world so far from what we perceive as reality that perhaps he now sees things more clearly than most who are thought of as sane. He sits in a director’s chair, smoking a black clove cigarette, looking over some notes. Enter a young Japanese girl, with buzzed hair, in a plaid bondage skirt, black shirt, and knee-high boots. This is Kat. She looks a bit skinnier than she does now, in part because she was coming off a methadone addiction at the time of this footage, but it’s definitely the same girl. She leans over Sidhe. Kat: Are you ready? Sidhe: I...I...I think so. Kat: I know you haven’t felt well lately...we don’t have to cut this promo now. Sidhe: I’ll be fine. I have my notes down. And just in case, I’ve pasted a false cover on to a copy of the script. If it comes down to it, I’ll... Sidhe stops...he starts to panic... Kat: Just breathe...it’s okay...no one is going to make fun of you. Sidhe takes a few deep breaths. Sidhe: Okay, if it comes down to it, I’ll just use the script and cut the promo looking like I’m reading a book. Sidhe takes a few more deep breaths, and stands up. He rolls his head around a few times, stretching his neck. He lowers his head for a moment, and Kat grabs his arm. Kat: Hey...you did remember to take your medicine this morning, didn’t you? Sidhe stops...and then panics. Sidhe: I...I...I forgot... Sidhe sits back down, and starts rocking back and forth. Kat: It’s okay...I’ll go get your pills, and we’ll just do the promo in a couple hours when they kick in. Sidhe: No no no no...I can do this...I’ve got the script...I want to get this done... Kat: Are you sure? Sidhe: Yes. I’m sure...I don’t want to have this camera crew around any longer than necessary... Kat kisses Sidhe on the forehead. Kat: I’ll go get you some ice cream...do you want ice cream? Sidhe: And some juice... Kat leaves, and Sidhe takes a few deep breaths, and something about his being changes slightly. He seems a little more relaxed, and he steps out in front of the cameras. He lays down lackadaisically in a large, wingback chair, and opens up his copy of the script, which has a false cover on it to make it look like an Aleister Crowley book. The scene freezes, and we pull out to see Fae watching the footage in the Gym of Darkness. Fae: Fae don’t like watchin’ those things, but Fae knows it important that he do, ‘cause Fae don’t wanna end up like that ‘gain. Fae nods. Fae: Not many people believe this, but Fae ALWAYS takes his medicine now! First thing in the mornin’, Fae takes his medicine. Fae feels bad fo’ the Sidhe, though. Sidhe always had all this stuff on his mind, an’ was worried that pooie mean peoples was gonna make the funs of Fae. So Sidhe always had to do everythin’ perfect, jus’ to prove that he could, an’ he made himself cwazy doin’ it. Fae don’t worry ‘bout bein’ perfect, Fae jus’ wanna be the best Fae that ever Fae’d his way into wrestlin’. An’ Fae IS the best Fae to ever Fae! When Fae came to XUW, Fae saw lots an’ lots of hypocrisy...Kat taught Fae that word...an’ it ANGERED a Fae. Seemed to Fae like the good guys an’ the bad guys was only different in that they made fun of each other. Wasn’t no one even tryin’ to be nice...the good guys made fun of the bad guys, an’ the bad guys made fun of the good guys. The bad guys jumped the good guys from behind, an’ the good guys jumped the bad guys from the front. Wasn’t no different, jus’ how people saw it. Then enter Fae. An’ Fae started callin’ people on how they was actin’, like sayin’ that Saij was jus’ a bully, even if he was popular, ‘cause he jus’ made fun of people. An’ no one appreciated it, an’ people booed the Fae jus’ cause he was in the AoD, ‘cause they can’t tell the difference between goodly and badly. Even when the AoD started to stand up ‘gainst the Ultra Violents when no one else would, people still said there was some funny motive behind it. The AoD didn’t do no attackin’ people from behind anymore, an’ we didn’t really make fun of people, we jus’ said “We better than you, an’ we gonna beat you.”, an no one liked that for poo. Then the AoD gots hurt bad by some Skel’tons of Suzie, an’ we all had to go away for a while. An’ when we came back, people finally ‘ppreciated us. But it didn’t make no difference to Fae, ‘cause Fae is as Fae was, an’ as Fae ever will be. An’ now, it the cruiserweight tournament in PTC, an’ we gots us some funniness goin’ on, ‘cause the XUW bein’ represented by two lawyers, a schlong-ridin’ midget, the Fool, an’ Fae. ‘Gainst the Ultra Violents, the AoD stood up for XUW when no one else looked like they was goin’ to. Now, it bit different. Ain’t no one serious standin’ up for the XUW in the PTC cruiserweight tournament. Jus’ a bunch of silly people. Jus’ so happens that Fae ‘bout the silliest of them all. But Fae ain’t a midget, an’ Fae ain’t a lawyer, an’ Fae ‘members all his wrestlin’ moves...so it looks like Fae jus’ ‘bout the best chance the XUW gots. Fae here to represent for XUW, an’ that means there gonna Fae gonna bring the Xtreme Ultimo attitude... Or somethin’ like that. That’s what April said Fae should bring to the tournament, but Fae ain’t ‘bout to go skateboardin’ or no other Xtreme sport wearin’ his Ultimo Dragon mask...Fae can’t see in that mask to begin with, an’ skateboardin’ in it jus’ seems like a good way to get hurt really bad. So Fae thinks he gonna bring the virtues of happycoreosity an’ mightyness to PTC. Fae nods, then stands and walks over to his dog, Puppy...though that’s not really a good name for the dog. “Killer” or “Hellbeast” or “Man-Destroyer” or “The Great Beast That Is Called DRAGON”...those are the names you give a dog like this, some kind of mutant Staffordshire Pit Terrier/Hellhound mix, weighing right around 200lbs, brindled in black, with jaw muscles that visibly flex, and can possibly crush steel. However, like any Pit Terrier that was raised with a gentle hand, Puppy is a big baby. At the approach of his master, Puppy rolls over onto his back for a belly-rub. Fae plops down next to his dog to rub said belly. Fae: Happycoreosity is summed up in Puppy. See – Puppy don’t need to be a nice puppy. He can get whatever he want by force. He could kill Fae an’ get to run free. But he don’t. Fae be nice to Puppy, an’ gives Puppy treats, an’ walks Puppy. Puppy plays catch with Fae, an’ sleeps at the foot of Fae’s bed, keepin’ Fae’s feets toasty warm. Puppy nice to Kat, too. An’ Ikke, an’ Lawrence, an’ DDT, an’ Ray, an’ even Sirius! ‘Cause they all nice to Puppy. But if you ain’t a nice person... Fae points to a training dummy. Fae: Puppy...POOHEAD! 200 pounds of muscle shoots off like a bullet at the dummy, launches into the air, and removes the throat without effort. Puppy drops the torn cloth, trots back over to Fae, and plops down. Puppy: WOOF! Fae: Good Puppy! See? That’s the negative side of happycoreosity in action! You ain’t goodly...you get the kickin’. Fae touch on the positive side later. Fae wanders away from Puppy, who lies back down, and goes to sleep. Fae don’t know who his opponents be, but Fae know one of them calls himself a “heel”, an’ Fae knows that that’s wrestlin’ for “bastard”. An’ that means that they ain’t a good person, an’ they gots the active manifestation of happycoreosity comin’ to ‘em. The active manifestation of happycoreosity is the Fae Ripsaw, ‘specially in this tournament, where there ain’t no one too big for it. Fae nods. Fae: Fae’s Ripsaw ain’t got the power of Jack’s, but it still a decent Ripsaw, Fae thinks. Bunches of people who been hit with it also think it a decent Ripsaw. Fae nods again. Fae: Of course, Fae realizes he still gots some work to do for this match comin’ up, both his match in XUW an’ his match in PTC. In strides the 7’5”, 356lbs., manbeast of Iceland, called in some circles “The Mighty” Ikke Mighty “The”, but who prefers to be addressed as “The Icon, the Mighty” Ikke Mighty “The Icon, the”. Or just as Ikke. But never by his middle name of Elizabeth...unless you’re getting to know Ikke in the Biblical sense. Ikke: HAH! We must train! ‘Tis a time for great training, and perfection of many a move! Fae: YAY! ‘Cause Fae was really wantin’ to work on Fae’s Ripsaw, ‘cause Fae knows that you gots a nice powerbomb, an’ was thinkin’... Ikke: HAH! Nay, not so puny a move as that! Lo, no, we must work on ye no-selling skills. Indeed, ‘tis the mightiest move in existence. Pause. Fae: Firstlywise, you a big freak, an’ secondwise, Fae think you wrong. Fae not think the no-sell gonna benefit Fae in any lastin’ way. Ikke: Indeed, and you would be correct. Fae: Then why you makin’ Fae work on it? Ikke: Because it is the mightiest move in existence. Fae: Fae not understand, but Fae not understand lots an’ lots of things. Though Fae does understand the films of David Lynch. Ikke: Yea, verily, for ye are mighty. Fae: No...Fae think it ‘cause Fae cwazy. But, anyway, Fae can already no-sell anything. Ikke: Lo, even death? Fae: Da. Fae ‘sploded one time, an’ Fae came back. Ikke: HAH! Likely story! Kat walks in. Kat: No...really. He exploded, took two weeks off, then came back. Ikke: Lo, he still needed two weeks to recover! HAH! Fae: No...Fae spent the whole time playin’ monkey curlin’. Just slidin’ monkeys ‘round on the ice... Kat: Which, if I recall, you once called the mightiest sport in existence. Ikke: Indeed it is. Did ye ever get a monkey on your back? Fae: Da! But Fae an’ Kat went through a thirteen-step program an’ got better! Kat: Yep, a thirteen-step program, because twelve just wouldn’t do. Ikke: It took ye thirteen steps to get a monkey off ye back? Ikke normally just throws himself backwards, and then shoots the offending baboon. See? Two steps! HAH! Kat stares at Ikke for a moment, then kicks Ikke in the balls. Ikke no-sells. Ikke: HAH! Kat looks over at Fae, who nods, then rears back, and MIGHTILY kicks Ikke in the yarbles. Ikke tries not to no-sell, then screams and falls over. Kat nods, and turns to Fae. Kat: Right. Now, we have a lot of work to do, and not much time to do it. Fae: Fae pretty sure we gots lots of time to do workin’. It not like Fae got any matches comin’ up. Kat coughs. Fae: Fae gots one match comin’ up. Kat: Two. Fae: One of them is ‘gainst the Skel’tons of Suzie. The other one is ‘gainst a very unmighty Christian Crawford an’ a guy who calls himself “Primetime”. Kat does the math. Kat: That’s actually slightly less than one legitimate match, but we can round up. But we need to work on preparing for the PTC match. Fae: Fae gots a match? Kat: Yes. Fae: Really!?!? Kat: Well...about two-thirds of a match. The thing with the SoS in XUW is only about a quarter of a match. Grand total, you really only have about eighty-five percent of a match, but we’ll train for a whole match, just so you’re well prepared. Fae: Good. Fae can’t concentrate for more than ‘bout one match worth of trainin’ anyway. Ikke slowly stands back up. Ikke: HAH! And the best way to train is cliff diving in Acapulco! Fae pantses Ikke, then gives him a mighty unprotected crotch kick. Ikke collapses. Fae: You jus’ wanna go check out tanned, hardbodied, vacationin’ college students! Kat: And not necessarily girls, either. Fae: You wanna go check out girls... Kat: That’s why we have the internet. Fae: Da. But how best to prepare for these matches? Kat wanders off, and returns with a sippy cup. Fae: Fae’s sippy cup! Kat: Yep, and you must be like the sippy cup! Fae: That easy! Fae already like a sippy cup. Fae ain’t transparent, so people can’t see what Fae’s motives are. Fae throws the sippy cup down. It bounces. Fae: The sippy cup don’t break when you drop it, much like Fae. Fae nudges the sippy cup, and it doesn’t fall over. It pops back upright. Fae: Fae don’t fall down for no one. An’, most importantly, Fae don’t spill stuff everywhere, an’ Fae’s shatterproof. Fae nods. Fae: DDT showed Fae that! Fae takes his sippy cup and gnaws on it for a moment. Then he pulls it from his mouth and cocks his head to the side. Fae: Fae ain’t the sharpest grapefruit spoon in the silverware drawer. Fae aware of that. But Fae ready for war this week. Fae gonna play War Games with the Skel’tons of Suzie, but they not really games. They more like bludgeonings. And it ain’t really playin’. It more like workin’. But it all the same in the end. Fae gonna bludgeon those Skel’tons into submission. ‘Specially Joey, ‘cause he hitted a woman, an’ Fae not ‘bout to stand for that. Then Fae gots the PTC cruiserweight tournament to deal with. An’ Fae not know neither person he’s goin’ up against in that. Fae not worried though, ‘cause winnin’ ain’t the important part to Fae. The important part is the fun-havin’, and the enjoyment of what Fae does. Lots of people forgets that this is somethin’ we all started doin’ ‘cause it was fun. That we enjoyed it. Fae don’t forget that, though. Fae ain’t got no bigger mission, no perception that he gonna change the world. But Fae does believe he can have a good time. An’ if Fae can have a good time, an’ make one other person have a good time, then that’s two people who are havin’ fun. An’ if that person who has fun watchin’ Fae goes out an’ has fun with another person, that’s three people...but what if he goes an’ has fun at a party, an’ he’s in a better mood for watchin’ Fae. He gonna make bunches more people happy. An’ Fae don’t just make one person happy when he wrestles, he makes a bunches of people happy. An’ those people go out an’ make more people happy. An’ it adds up, an’ eventually comes back around to Fae. That’s positive happycoreosity. You make people happy, an’ eventually people gonna make you happy, too. It ain’t changin’ the world. It’s changin’ one blank face to a smile. Fae nods and then wanders off to play with Puppy. Fade to black.
