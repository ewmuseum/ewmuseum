IG 67 RP THREAD

12 July 2009

12 July 2009 [Scene Begins] Moraless is stood in the darkness of an alleyway, the similar haunts to where he had been 9 years ago now though he was a little more grey. A lot stiffer and truth be told not entirely sure where he was. Moraless: So you call me out here, thinking you have control over me? Well let it be known i only show up here because i want to be here you understand? [Cameraman peers out from behind the camera looking confused] Cameraman: Are you talking to me? Moraless: You summoned me after all. Cameraman: No i didn't.....i was told to show up here. Moraless: I see through your lies, the same old lies you tried to fool me with nine years ago. Why else would i be hanging around an alley perched on a crate. It makes no sense? Cameraman: Nine years ago, i've only been working for the company for three months. Moraless: I see, so why am i here? [Cameraman shrugs and turns to the director who is not visible on screen] Director: You're here to promote the infinite gauntlet, round 67 now shut up and get on with it. [Laughing like a mad man Moraless leaps off the crate towards the cameraman who yelps slightly] Moraless: At Infinite Gauntlet 67. I will be victorious, no matter who you throw at me, i shall swat them aside like flies. Why in my day i was a PrimeTime Central Extreme Champion, my body is a tapestry of the wounds i've taken in the form of entertainment. Now as i make my return after three years in the wilderness i shall be win against whoever you throw at me. [Moraless steps backwards into the darkness of the shadows, only his eyes showing] Moraless: Believe me when i say that i will be the world champion! Director: This isn't for a world championship! Moraless: Extreme championship? Director: There is no championships involved! Moraless: Let me get this straight! You call me out here in the dead of night, making me dwell in the shadows of an alley in the dark and there is no championship involved? This is out of the question! Don't you know who i am. I am Moraless and i deserve a title match, sure no-one else know's who i am but what does that matter! I am Moraless and i have won everything they've ever thrown at me! I demand a title shot now!. Director: There is no title to fight for! Moraless: I demand to speak to the fed-head. Director: Who? Moraless: The fed head, give me their AIM messenger and i will bitch at them till they bleed! I will get this title shot if it kills me! The camera cuts [Off Screen] Moraless: That went well don't you think? Cameraman: The sound is still on they can hear you. Moraless: That was the plan wasn't it? We have this private moment after when everyone thinks it's a joke? Cameraman: I dunno i just point the thing. Moraless: Either way. I will be champion regardless of not knowing anything about what's going on! ---- 25 July 2009 [Scene Opens] It's an empty arena, the show had long since ended but a single solitary spotlight shines down into the middle of the ring. In the center of the ring stands a twenty foot ladder and atop that sits Moraless staring out across the empty arena. Moraless: Time's sure do change don't they? There once was a time when there would be insurmountable amounts of ammunition to come back with, to tear into the enemy and rip the words to pieces. However with age becomes a lack of ambition. Who have we got that have dared to show up? Next to no-one. For a prize that was so profitable, that would lead to the possibility of greatness. Yet no-one bothered to show up. [Moraless jumps down from the ladder and lands in the ring, his knees bend awkwardly as he lands and he grimaces, the ages of time evidently taking their toll on Moraless as well.] Moraless: The difference is, as time goes on one of two things happens. You either fade and disappear or you stand up and fight for one last hurrah. Sure my body is broken and my mind even more so, but lets be honest here. No-one else even cares. Our industry is dying and i have to ask the question what am i? Am i the resurrection or am i a historical figure, simply clutching on to what remains. Being a stubborn bastard and refusing to become little more than a memory. [Feeling uncomfortable in stood in the ring, Moraless dashes to a turnbuckle and perches himself atop one.] Moraless: For ten years i threw myself around rings like these and for what? Nothing no-one cares. I came back thinking that people might remember the good old days. That they may just revitalise the spirit of 90's but this wasn't to be. Unless it's full of corporate sponsors or appearance fees. People don't care. [The light in the arena clicks off leaving Moraless alone in the dark, a voice eminating from the darkness] Moraless: So prove me wrong people, show me you do care. Show me that this business is still worth fighting for, that it's worth putting my body through one last punishment. Show me you aren't all cowards.
