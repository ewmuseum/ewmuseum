Theres no place like home

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Todays Promo is brought you by the word Loyalty. We open up inside Ryan Noxs apartment. The dim light of an old lava lamp casts shadows along the walls. The walls that were once covered in posters are bare. There are still some scraps of left hanging around the push pins. A grandfather clock ticks the seconds away as Ryan Nox walks in to turn the tv on. The faint glow of the tv lights the room up. As the rest of the room comes into view you can see most of the furniture is turned over. The kitchen table on its side. The splintered remains of the chairs lay in a small pile. The standard color test and monotone beep emit from the surround sound. Ryan sits on the floor and watches as the whole image changes to the XUDF promo channel. Suddenly the phone rings. "Hello. Yes this is Ryan Nox. Yes I am a professional wrestling. Who is this? I have a job already. No I dont want to hear your offer. I am quite content with-Main Event? Big Money? Fine. Ill come and listen. All expenses paid? Good." Changing scenes we cut to a sparsley decorated office. Outside the sounds of big city traffic pass by and police sirens blare. Seated behind a desk is a slightly balding man. In front is a name plate that tells us his name is Mr Henry Reabrun. A loud knock startles him as he looks up from his paperwork. "Come in." He says while standing up and straighting up his cheap suit. "Ahh Mr Nox please do come in and have a seat. Im Mr Henry Raeburn. Despite what my name tag says. Got it cheap and I guess thats what I get. How are you doing?" "I am fine. I am here. What is this proposal you have for me?" He says sitting down. "Well here it is. The Inquisitor." "The what?" "Okay hear me out on this. For my company Rogue Championship Wrestling you wiull play a silent hitman type. The backstory is that you are the hand of judgement. You deem who is worthy of continuing wrestling. You would be one of the top heels. Or Faces. We are still working out the basics. I have a few sketches for you." He holds up a few pieces of white skeching paper. Some of the images are in color while others are just black and white ink drawings. "This one here is of a possible mask you might want to wear. And this is what you would wear to the ring. It would be a long burgandy velvet robe with a large purple Q on the back. The mask I particulary like. Kinds like a thinner version of Mick Foleys mask from his Mankind days. But with this added Q on the forehead. IU like this gimmick. I think this is one of the best we have come up with." "You want me to wear a jockstrap on my face with a big Q on the front. You want me to wear a velvet robe with a Q on the back. What is wrong with who I am right now?" "Well to be honest you just arent interesting to out viewers. But we do see alot of talent in you. You do have a unique style. Not many wrestlers will attempt to crossover into that many wrestling styles." "My wrestling style and my name are what make me. Changing my name and giving me some 80s era gimmick wont help me get over. You might as well call me Aldo Montoya. Im sorry but no ammount of money can change my mind sir. It was a nice offer but I doubt you could pay me enough to wear all that. Thank you but I doubt I would want to hear how much you are going pay." "Half a Million a year plus travel." "Pheew thats alot." We change venues agaion and this tie its outside the an office building. The XUW Random Interview Guy comes running up to Ryan Nox. Who must of just finished with his his meeting. The RIG who has been patiently waiting outside the building begins to ask Nox a question. "Rhinox so did you make a descion as to which company you chose?" "Yes I did. Out of loyalty to XUDF and XUW. I chose to stay. I have no reason to leave. In a few days I go to battle Brian O Connell for a chance in the Punk Out Invitiational. Why give that up for a jockstrap and the letter Q. Besides. This is my home. And there is no place like home." Fade To Black
