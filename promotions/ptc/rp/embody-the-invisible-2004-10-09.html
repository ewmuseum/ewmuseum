Embody The Invisible

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 There is no such thing as peace without there being such thing as war. After all, if war did not exist then what would peace have to be the opposite of? If war did not exist, then how could Peace be known as happiness, when it would be the 100% guaranteed living status. If war did not exist, then peace would be considered, well, life. You see what I mean? ----------------------------------------------------------- "Inner peace is a mask, hiding the true inhibitions of the weak." - ?? [And we fade into a dark room, of what appears to be a large bedroom under the early night's unforgiving eyes. A soft melodramatic melody is wafting into the bedroom from the neighbouring bathroom.] # We used to swim the same moonlight waters # Oceans away from the wakeful day [The melody now takes on a hard electric guitar, but still remains of what would be an opera.] # My fall will be for you # My love will be in you # If you be the one to cut me # I'll bleed forever # Scent of the sea before the waking of the world # Brings me to thee # Into the blue memory [The bathroom door then opens, leaking light into the once dark bedroom, and the song gets louder. A disgruntled man makes his way to the bed, and plops down; head first into the pillow. His vision, now black. He takes in the sounds of the Neo-Metal song, as it gets quieter as he embarks on 'Ye Olde Journey to Dreamworld'.] {The dead hold all the answers. It would take days, but we would find the key. The only problem is that when the dead start talking, they never shut up. Never shut up. Never. Shut up. Never. Shut up. Never. Shut up...} "SHUT UP!!!!" [Odium’s voice rings through the resounding black; as the voice that was once there is now gone. A small light appears in the distance, and a very quiet dripping noise echoes through the black. Odium takes a few steps forward, and looks forward. The light appears to be further away, and the dripping has gotten louder.] "Turn off that damned water!!" [Nothing responds to Odium's voice; now weakened. The dripping increases in tempo and in loudness, but a slight trickle can be felt on Odium's face. It is no water. Blood is streaming from the nose of Odium, and after scaling down his lips, over the rough patch of hair on his chin; it hits the ground with immense force. Odium raises his arm under his nose to cup the blood.] {The dead hold all the answers. It would take days, but we would find the key. The only problem is that when the dead start talking, they never shut up.} [He dashes toward the light, and it engulfs him as he crumples onto the floor immediately; his body streaming a sea of red.] {The dead hold all the answers.} [A silent gust of wind is enformed, and he finds himself floating in the sea of light. He looks down, and he can embody the invisible. His body; now a pale white, looks like art in the large circle of red.] {You came here for an answer, did you not?} [Odium opens his mouth to respond, but the nothingness inside him halts his voice.] {He will betray you. This isn't a game, or some written scripture. What was once a friend will now be an enemy. Is this your line of work? Embody the invisible. It is there. Go.} [A door appears in the wall of white, and Odium's spirit; almost out of instinct, races toward it. It opens before him, and he gets a foothold as he gazes forward into another blackness. He leans forward, and falls into the black, into nothingness. A light orchestral melody can be heard, getting louder.] "UGH!" [Odium springs up, looking around, in the bedroom, now full of light. His pillow is soaked in blood as he rushes down his house stairs to the kitchen to find a cleaning flannel. He grabs one from the kitchen sink, all the while muttering.] "Why in god’s name do I keep getting these damn forsaken dreams. I'm trying to concentrate on Mine and Fred Grest's match, let alone stopping these bloody dreams..." [He runs up to his bedroom, two steps at a time. But as he walks into his bedroom, he says a woman with brown hair facing away from him all the time. Nervously, he taps her on the shoulder. She turns around, and to Odium’s surprise, a very pale and deeply cut body of his mother is sitting there, and she speaks to him in a strangely echoed and harsh voice.] {You wanted answers, didn’t you?} *Blink* *Blink* [Odium opens his eyes a third time, and to his surprise he is laying down in his bed, facing the blue ceiling above him.] "Oh bloody hell..."
