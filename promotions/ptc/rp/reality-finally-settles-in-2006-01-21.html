Reality Finally Settles In.

21 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-21 The pain coursed through his body like a runaway train, clutching at his chest as its sting almost dropped him down to a knee. He grabbed a hold of the refrigerator door handle for support, hesitating before moving again. Jayce Bradley’s match against Evan ‘Damage’ Jackson had left him a little worse for wear, but it was just something he was going to have to suffer through. He had made it past the FUSE Universal champion after landing a picture perfect ‘Final Solution’; a modified flipping DDT using the top turnbuckle as a springboard that Jayce uses as a finishing maneuver, and he would be damned if he was going to take it easy on himself with the match he had coming up in the fourth round. And what a match it was going to be, as the usual one on one matches that take place within a tournament like this one were thrown out on their ear like a fat man at a buffet table, in favor of a fourth round fatal four match up. Global Championship Wrestling’s Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins; who just happened to be the man who eliminated Angelo Deville in the first round of the tournament, PRIME and Atlantic Wrestling Club’s Paddy O’Shea; who just happened to defeat GTT favorite the Uber-Judge and has been on fire since then, and last but definitely not least, the World Wrestling Alliance’s Jonathan Winters, who had earned an opportunity to a second round match after falling at the hands of Seymour Almasy. Three men that had blazed a trail through the tournament, while Jayce slipped into the match with little to no fan fare. He was a virtual unknown, even after the fact that he had made it this far into the tournament, no one had even bothered to find out who he was or how he had managed to make it this far. He had taken out a virtual legend in Sean Galen in the first round…Sure, Galen was only a legend in his own mind, but the man had history compared to Jayce with titles that Jayce could only dream of ever holding. And speaking of champions, Evan Jackson is the current FUSE Universal champion. The best that federation has to offer, and Jayce eliminated him from this competition. It doesn’t matter how he did it, because that only goes to show how far Jayce is willing to go to make it even further into this tournament. Determined isn’t even a strong enough word to describe how much Jayce wants this, because this is what he has needed in his life lately. It gives it meaning. But no one cared. In the second round of the tourney, he faced some guy named Joe Miller that had traveled all the way from the future with a monkey… A freaking monkey with a jetpack was at ringside, and no one realized that Jayce was even in that match. Oh, but they remembered the monkey though. They always remember the monkeys… Or was it chimpanzees that they remember? It does not even matter which primate they remember, because after it is all said and done, Jayce is the one still in this tournament. Sure, he may not be a big marquee name like Rich Rollins, he may not have beat any of the favorites to win this tournament before it had even begun in Angelo Deville or the Uber-Judge, and he may not have fought and clawed his way back into this thing after losing in the first round… But that does not mean he does not have the want or the need to win this thing, and it does not mean that any of the others want it or need it as badly as he does. All it means is that people know them, that people can relate to them, and it is going to make the whole thing that much sweeter if… No wait, scratch that. It is going to make the whole thing that much sweeter when Jayce Bradley moves on to the next round, because he does not plan on being eliminated yet. No one is paying him any attention, and that may be a blessing in disguise for GCW’s Cruiserweight champion. There is no added pressure on him to win. Well, not compared to the rest of the wrestlers left in his bracket anyway. With the pain slowly subsiding, Jayce stumbled his way into the living area; practically falling onto the couch in front of the television. He winced in pain as his body slapped against the beige faux leather cushions, groaning slightly as he tried to adjust himself to a more comfortable position. With no shirt to cover his upper torso, you can see the bruises and welts that dot his chest, yellow at the point of impact and fading into a slight purple before blending into his natural skin tone. The bruising happened to be a bit more severe in the area around his ribs, more so on the left side of his trunk. Laying on his right side to hopefully subside a bit of the pain, he bent his arm bent at the elbow to prop his head up, then lazily fumbled through the three manila folders tousled about the glass and wrought iron coffee table that was placed between the television and the couch. He grabbed one and laid it out in front of him on the edge of the couch, before fumbling through the various sheets of paper that were within. Jonathan Winters was typed out on the top of the page, a brief history of absolutely nothing as not much is known about the World Wrestling Alliance’s United States champion. "Selective amnesia," Jayce whispered to himself in a mocking tone, since the file mentioned that Winters did not remember anything before waking up in a cornfield in Kansas. Funny, how someone can forget everything that makes them the person that they are, but they can remember the years of training that they undertook to become a wrestler. "What a crock of shit. Next thing you know, the guy will have super powers or be some sort of fucking vampire." His comments fell on deaf ears, as there was no one around for him to talk to but himself. He continued to glance over the file, making mental notes here and there about the guy and making sure to remember that he had an eleven match win streak going on. Either he had been in quite a few matches lately, or he just did not count the loss to Almasy since it did not happen in the WWA. Either way, the guy was not impossible to beat. =¤=¤=¤=¤= =¤=¤=¤=¤= Tears slowly trickled down her cheek, her eyes irritated from the brackish fluid that would not cease to build up within her eyelids. Her arms were tied in front of her waist, the thin braided twine digging into her wrists as she tried to wriggle her petite hands free from their restraints. She was only eleven years old. She had captivated Russell Braun from the moment he had seen her jump into her father’s arm. He was fascinated by the way her golden brown hair glistened under the radiant rays of the sun, stimulating him in a way he had thought was lost. The kills were becoming more and more convenient lately, too simple to extract any emotion after having done it for so long. It was a breath of fresh air, the way the adrenaline coursed through his veins, invigorating him. Just the thought of a small innocent child’s life being strangled away brought a smile to Braun’s aging features. He felt like God again, a feeling he looked forward to on the eve of every hunt; or at least he used to. This one was different though. She fought back, and had yet to simply give up in her pursuit of freedom. There was something inside of the little girl that would not let her simply cave in to the possibility that she would not be making it out of this alive; it was as if she knew something that Braun didn’t. Licking his lips like a wolf about to pounce on its next meal, Braun reached for the cellular telephone he had purchased at the convenience store around the block. It took all of his energy not to slit the throat of the turban-bearing cashier that snatched the money from his hand, even before he had even mentioned the total of the purchase. Later, he had thought to himself as he tossed his bag onto the passenger seat, I’ll be sure and pay that rag-head a visit. Right now though, right now there was something even more important to take care of. He finished dialing the number into the cell, pointing the telephone toward the child with a sadistic grin on his face. "Say hello to Mommy," he whispered to her as his heart began to pound within his chest. That was right before she yelled at the top of her lungs of course. "MOMMY!!" =¤=¤=¤=¤= =¤=¤=¤=¤= Jayce tossed the Jonathan Winters folder back onto the coffee table, watching it slide across the spotless glass before coming to a stop a quarter of the way over the opposite edge. He pushed the top folder aside with a brush of his hand, lifting the other folder that rested on the table and dropping it down in front of him as he did the one before. He flipped through the pages, looking through information from both the AWC and PRIME. Again, nothing real important to note about Paddy apart from the fact that he was a drunken brawler from Ireland with a past of parental neglect. As a matter of fact, apart from beating the Uber-Judge in the first round, O’Shea really had not done anything to garner much attention. That victory pretty much made him a household name, and defeating Jason Warr and Doreen Pepperpot solidified him as a true possibility to win this entire thing. It was strange how one victory could change the way people view you, but Paddy became the poster boy for the old phrase ‘Never judge a book by it’s cover.’ Paddy became an overnight celebrity, much like his fellow PRIME-ate, Clyde Walkins. That’s the thing though, no one ever realizes that they are not the only ones that can pull an upset victory out of thin air. The luck eventually has to run out, paving the road for someone new to waltz in and take their place. It may not seem as if it could happen to Paddy at the rate he is going right now, but that was probably the exact same thing that was said about the Uber-Judge about a month ago. Probably the same thing said about Clyde Walkins before he stepped into the ring with Seymour Almasy, too. And everyone knows how those two events turned out. "He don’t look like much." Jayce closed the folder and tossed it back onto the table. There was only one left to look through, the one he had been dreading like the plague since the day the match was announced. =¤=¤=¤=¤= =¤=¤=¤=¤= Detective Pride sat with his phone pressed against his ear, his head hanging low in exhaustion from not sleeping for the last few days. His free hand was balled into a fist to prop his head up, keeping it from slamming against the cherry oak veneer. The events of the last few weeks had been disturbing on so many levels, so much that his wife had been talking to him about taking some time off. Of course he wouldn’t hear of it, this case was too important to just leave in the hands of someone else. Especially after the Sapphire murder. He did not understand how someone could be so cold and heartless, taking such a slow and methodical approach to ending someone’s life like Lacey and Sapphire’s killer had. The man must have been a sick and twisted individual, if it was even a man at all. Pride nodded as he managed to force out the word, "Yes," to answer the person he was having a conversation with, as if it hurt him to have to speak. Pride had been blaming himself for Sapphire’s murder since the morning he had received the call, he felt he had led Lacey’s murderer to her apartment. He did not know it -- maybe he never would -- but he was right in his assumption. Russell Braun had actually been following him throughout the day. "Look," Pride started, "I understand what you’re saying, but you need to understand that I don’t have the luxury of waiting for the damn results until Monday." Pride lifted his head as he slammed his fist down onto the desk in frustration. "Thank you, anything you could fax over would be appreciated." Pride placed the receiver back onto its base, closing his eyes to relieve some of the stress if only for a moment. The bags of sand beneath his eyes seemed to drag his entire face down, as if the sands of time were swiftly descending, the hourglass of his life; informing him that not much time was left. Pride rubbed the bridge of his nose with his forefinger and thumb, in an attempt to massage away all the problems in his life. It didn’t work. The ring of the telephone snapped him out of his trance, eliminating any chance of drifting off into a temporary slumber. "Pride," he said as he pressed the telephone receiver against his ear. The once weary look on his face suddenly disappeared, as if someone had something that took him by surprise. "Who is this," as curious as a cat, Pride listened intently to whatever the person on the other end of the line was saying. He motioned for another detective to come over to him, quickly jotting something down on a piece of paper. He held the paper up for the other detective to see, the words; get someone to trace this call, ASAP, were scribbled messily on the yellow sheet. "Look," Pride scolded, "I don’t know who the hell this is, but I don’t appreciate this one bit. Don’t you realize that you can be brought up on charges for prank calling the police department?" The look on his face changed once again, and his face suddenly became pale as if someone had removed the color from his face. He no longer appeared fatigued, for some reason…he was scared. "Listen you sick fuck, if you harm one hair on her head…" Pride paused as tears welled up within his eyes. "I swear I will hunt you down and make what you did to Lacey and Sapphire seem like an everyday occurrence." "I’m listening," was the response he gave to whatever was said on the other end of the line. Pride began to take deeper breaths, his chest heaving to and fro in a rhythmic motion as he listened sullenly. Whatever was being said had caused him to become enraged, the blood vessels within his eyes had become inflamed, giving them a scarlet tint. It wasn’t even a minute before Pride calmly placed the receiver back down on its base. He wiped a few stray tears that had managed to trickle down his cheek like early morning dew on a window. "Detective Pride," the detective that had received the note to trace the call pretty much screamed as he came racing into the office. He was breathing heavily, but still managed to finish what he had to say. "Nick…they traced the call to…to your house." "I know." "I already dispatched a patrol car that was in the area." "It doesn’t matter," Pride replied placidly. "He’s not there." "But the call came from your house," the detective replied, his face contorted in bewilderment as he cocked his head to the left. There was clearly something that Pride was holding back from his colleague, something that had struck fear into his heart in a matter of seconds. =¤=¤=¤=¤= =¤=¤=¤=¤= Sitting there; dragging the tips of his fingers along the letters of the name sloppily scribed across the front of the folder, Jayce could not help but feel a bit intimidated. Unlike the other two participants of the match, Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins had a whole hell of a lot of credibility. The numerous accolades that the man had received over the last year almost classified the man in a level all his own. Beating Angelo Deville was just the icing on the cake that Rollins had devoured whole, cementing himself as the man to beat in not only the Mrs. H. bracket, but in the tournament itself. There were things in that folder that Jayce did not even have to open to know about, and that was what made it even more difficult to flip through. Still, hesitating slightly as he lifted the folder from where it rested, Jayce managed to flip open the front of the folder. And there it was. Red bandana pulled tightly around the crown of his head, his dark hair messily draped over his shoulders as he peers into the camera that had taken the portrait. Almost as if Rollins was staring into Jayce’s heart, picking out the doubt and hesitation that had as much control over his life as its steady rhythmic beat. "Those eyes," Jayce mumbled to himself as he stared at the photo, the beat of his heart steadily gaining in momentum the more time he spent gawking over the man that he felt knew everything that had ever happened to him. Jayce closed the folder and threw it across the room, the papers scattering in the air as they floated across the room and onto the gray carpeted floor. "Fuck," he screamed out in frustration as he rested his head in the palm of his hands. His elbows firmly planted on his knees for support, Jayce knew that he was thinking about it too much. Rich Rollins was just like any other man in this tournament, and stood just as much chance of losing as anyone else as well. Sure he was better than Jayce, and sure he had done so much more then Jayce could even dream of doing… "I’m so screwed." Reality had reared its ugly head, and it was at that moment that Jayce realized what so many others already knew… He is going to lose. Badly. The worst part about it, they may all be right. =¤=¤=¤=¤= =¤=¤=¤=¤= He had been anticipating this moment since early this morning, salivating over the possibility of toying with the emotions of someone who has the authority to hunt him down and end his life without the repercussions that he himself would have to endure if he were ever captured. It’s strange how something like this would get him so excited, but he was running on pure adrenaline right now. If there was anything that could stop him from what he was about to do, he honestly doubted it would even be enough to slow him down at the rate he was going. After a few moments of silence passed, as he impatiently lingered on hold, he had finally been directed to the man he wanted to speak with. The sound of his voice on the other end made him quiver in anticipation over what exactly would happen next. He was as giddy as a schoolgirl, his eyes lighting up before he had even muttered a word of his own. Now that the time was upon him, everything he had planned just flew out the window. But it was too late to turn back now. He needed to wing it. "Hello, Detective," Braun held a voice distorter between his lips and the cellular telephone he held in his hand, "A shame little boy blue got all choked up the way he did…you two seemed to be getting close." He had been wondering all day what his first words would be, and after trying out one witty barb after another, he felt he had nailed it with his quick on the fly adjustment. Braun smiled as he listened to the immediate change in Detective Pride’s voice. The twelve inch serrated blade reflected the dim light of the overhead bulb, and Braun took pleasure in shining it directly into the eyes of his underage captive. "Who I am is really of no concern," Braun beamed, "Since it will ruin the ending of the game if I give you all of the answers too soon. All you need to know is that the two queers suffered an agonizing death at my hands, and the fun and games are far from over." Pride’s lecture on the effects of making a prank call brought a smile to Braun’s lips, a trivial snigger escaped through his tightly pressed lips. He grabbed the auburn haired child by the neck and pulled her close, whispering something in her ear before saying something into the phone. "Anne Marie," two words from Braun that changed the entire direction of the conversation, just happened to be a name. It was the name of Detective Pride’s wife. Russell Braun grinned wide as he listened to Pride’s wife inform her husband that he had possession of their daughter. He then proceeded to laugh out loud when Pride began to threaten him. Considering pride was in no position to be threatening anyone, least of all the man who has the power to take his only child’s life. With a firm grip around the back of the child’s neck, Braun applied a little pressure. Enough pressure to cause the child to yelp out in pain. "Are you ready to listen to me now," Braun said into the phone in a raised voice, "or do you need to be sent a body part first?" "Very well," Braun began, "I’m sure you’re eager to get your daughter back, so it would be in your best interest to listen and listen good. I’m going to be leaving the country very soon, and I want you to be a good father and find a way to slow down any investigation taking place. I doubt I left behind any evidence, but seeing as how I need to patch up a few things before I leave…I need you to give me that time." Braun smiled, satisfied that his point was getting across. "Like any good father would." And with that, he pressed a button on the phone to end the call. "Your father’s a good man, Audrey," Braun said softly into the little girl’s ear. She whimpered as he ran his rough and calloused hand along her spine. She jerked away slightly as he began to gently massage her back, which only made him chuckle softly to himself. She was scared, and that was exactly what he wanted her to feel right now. Fear. "It’s okay," he assured her, "as long as your daddy does what he’s supposed to, you’re going to be back home in your room in no time. You can cuddle up with your little yellow Care Bear, and get tucked into bed with a lullaby from your mom." It’s sick to think about, but Braun had been planning this for the last few days. He had watched Pride and his family live out their everyday lives, all the while taking mental notes about who did what and when. The thrill had indeed returned.
