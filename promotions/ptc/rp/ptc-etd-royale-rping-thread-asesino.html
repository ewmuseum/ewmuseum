ETD Royale RPing Thread

26 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: This is what Asesino send me on his floppy to post here: Sorry about the shortness but I have to go to sleep to go to work in the morning where I have double shift to then go to my friend's wrestling show to go back home which I should be arriving early Sunday Morning, since by then the deadline will be posted I'll throw what I had done thus far since I said I would be participating and I am a man of my word. As the scene fades in from blue we find ourselves in the cave portion of the famous Asesino Cove. We look at the smooth brownish red walls and the many posters of fish and wrestling events that line it. We look up to the ceiling and see cave formations that look like brown icicles way above the black sand floor. We pan straight back and see a shot of The Assassin sitting in the Lazy Boy that once belonged to DVD, but due to the odd camera work the shot is upside down. The camera operator fumbles with the camera then gets the shot he wants looking directly at Asesino and the chair. Asesino is wearing a pair of blue board shorts with colorful fish on them and a white Jerry Edwards shirt. He repositions his legs from the crossed position to flat on the floor and begins to speak. Asesino: After all this time NGW your finally starting to understand. After all the time that you’ve spent bragging about this and that you’re finally starting to get it. You understand now that without the fans, we're nothing. You get that victories are hollow if there’s no reason behind it. Your starting to see that the fans make this business what it is, and the fans are the reason we do what we do. Asesino: I like that out of you NGW. It’s a new side that I’ve always known was somewhere inside. When you're doing it right, you don’t burn out. The fans don’t let you burn out, the fans are what make me get up every day and go for the gold. Without them I couldn’t give all that I give, and do all that I do. If the World Title meant nothing to them, it would mean nothing to me. But it does mean something to them, it means that, that man is the best in the world. I want to be the best in their eyes, and I feel that in order to achieve that I have to win The NGW World Heavyweight Title. Asesino: People must have been unconscious or something when I went against John Crigger and Benjamin Williams on Tuesday. I didn’t win the match, and I’m not the number one contender for the tag team titles, John Crigger and Benjamin Williams are, and just like you fans I too wish it were me. Although I’m pretty sure that Scottywood will make the right decision and let me get a shot at John Hitchin and the NGW World Title. Like you people I think NGW too is making a turn for the better. John Hitchin saw what he could accomplish by staying inside the rules and I think he is good enough to put me in the match at the next PPV if he retains the title at Great Canadian Bash. Asesino: You're right NGW, I am at the top of my game. In the last few months I’ve taken my skills to a different level. I’ve stepped up and made people take notice. People are seeing how good I really am, and most know it’s just a matter of time until that skill pays off, pay off with the World Title. So I do know I will be at my best for the Extreme Division Royale. Are my opponents trying to get me to think they're the best, or are they trying to make themselves believe they're the best? You may say that you're at the top of your game, but I’ve been watching the beatings that you’ve been taking, physically and mentally and I know that you can’t possibly be at your best. I know that you're doubting yourselves, that you're not too sure about what you can do on Sunday. Asesino: And I don’t blame you at all, guys. Like I said, I think that you need to take a break, not be in an over competitive match like you will be on Maelstrom. But I also know you're men of pride, and you WILL be there on Sunday and you WILL be there at Maelstrom. So all I can do is this. I can tell you to be prepared on Sunday, and wish you good luck at the pay per view. The rest, is up to you. Asesino: Your future at Maelstrom is clear, but mine is not. I guess all I can do is prepare, and hope that you see my logic, and when the time is right, you’ll decide to show or no show at Maelstrom. I’ll be ready to get closer to the PTC Extreme Title, and I’ll be ready to sit at home and wait for my show at the NGW World Title. I hope the Extreme Division stars make a wise decision. (Suddenly we hear a loud crashing noise coming from outside the cave. Asesino jumps up out of his chair to see what the commotion is about. He runs outside and we see a dirty man laying on his back with his eyes closed on the black sands of the Asesino Cove. The man has long unkempt blonde hair and a huge beard. He is wearing a filthy shirt that is torn to shreds that reads "Rio de" it would a appear there used to be more, but it can’t be made out. We look at the man and there is something eerily familiar about him, he opens his eyes to expose bright blue. Asesino looks down at the man in shock, and confusion.) Asesino: DVD? Later... (As the scene fades in from blue we find ourselves in the inner cave of The Asesino Cove. We are walking through the somewhat new, at least to our knowledge structure. We are at the entrance of the cave and we walk down the dimly lit corridor for at least a minute before we reach the well lit more open room portion of the Cave. We see Asesino sitting in his lazy boy recliner. Asesino is wearing a white "Cream" T-shirt and blue jeans. He is also wearing his blue Adidas Rod Lavers which we haven’t seen in a while. Asesino has a confident look in his eyes, the kind of look that we saw before his XWF Title Match, or the match that he won the XWF Title in with C.R. Asesino cracks his neck on either side the looks into the camera lens. He still remains silent for a few more moments then speaks.) Asesino: Man it sure is good to have my best buddy DVD back. He’s come back just in time to help me get ready for my Extreme Division Royale match up on Sunday. He’s had a rough time, I mean not being able to get out of Mexico then getting sold into the Texas slave trade only to be rescued by Walker Texas Ranger isn’t exactly the easier way to spend months and indubitably he is a little out of shape due to it. So he just left here to head for the gym a few minutes ago. I’m supposed to catch up with him a little later, but first I need to get some stuff off my chest. (Asesino crosses his legs and gets into a more serious mode.) Asesino: Destiny. That brings back memories. Memories of years ago when I made my pay per view debut in the JICWF. I was booked in a match with a man by the name of La Envidia Mata. I beat Mr. Mata soundly and went on to start a month of success. While I was wrestling La Envidia Mata I never imagined that I would find myself in this position years later. The idea of being in a battle royal situation in the Extreme Division, let alone an extreme division royale was inconceivable. But now I am in that match, and I know that I deserve to be here. On Sunday I will go up against the most extreme wrestlers in all of PTC. Asesino: There’s nothing that I will let stand in the way of me and the Extreme Title. I’ve fought too hard throughout this year to let this chance slip away. I’ve done too much to earn this position to not make it worth while on Sunday. I know my abilities in this kind of match, and I know that I have the skill level that may be just a little higher than my opponents in this field, and I plan to use that to my full advantage. If there is one thing that hanging out with The Corporation the last couple of years has done for me it’s given me that extra confidence that I need to be a World Champion. They're men that are as good, if not better than my opponents, and they know that I have that same potential. All this years they've been telling me that I had what it takes, that I am as good as anyone. Asesino: You can think that you're better than someone your whole life, but once other people start telling you it, it’s different. Because when only you know it, it’s only a thought, but once someone else knows it, it’s just about a fact. And when that some else is someone that has been there before, someone that was once in your position, it only means that much more. And not only do they know it, NGW knows it. why else would they let me represent them? If I was just some average Joe why would they even worry about what I do? They wouldn’t. A chance to face me face to face. I don’t think it’s what you wanted, but it’s what your getting. You have to look me in the eyes on Sunday, and you don’t know what you're getting into. (Asesino stands up picking up his gear as the scene fades to a promo hyping the Extreme Division Royale at Maelstrom.)
