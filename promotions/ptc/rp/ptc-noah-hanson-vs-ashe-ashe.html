Noah Hanson (WMW) #8 vs Ashe (MVW)

13 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
13 March 2004 In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. The earth was void and empty; darkness was on the face of the depths; the spirit of God hovered above the waters. God said, "Let there be light" and there was light. God saw that the light was good; God divided between the light and the darkness. God called the light "day" and the darkness God called "night"; there was evening and there was morning, day one. God said, "Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters and let it divide between the waters and the waters." God made the firmament and divided between the waters below the firmament and the waters above the firmament, and it was so. God called the firmament "heaven"; there was evening and there was morning, a second day. God said, "Let the waters be gathered below the heavens to one place and let the dry land be seen," and it was so. God called the dry land "earth" and the gathered waters God called "seas"; God saw that it was good. God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation -- grasses which produce seed, fruit trees which produce fruit, according to its kind, in which is its seed -- on the earth"; and it was so. The earth sprouted vegetation -- grasses that produce seed according to its kind and trees which produce fruit in which is its seed according to its kind; God saw that it was good. There was evening and there was morning, a third day. God said, "Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens to divide between the day the night; they shall be as signs, as appointed times, as days, and as years. They shall become lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light upon the earth"; and it was so. God made the two great lights: the great light for ruling the day and the small light for ruling the night, as well as the stars. God put them in the firmament of the heavens to give light upon the earth, to rule the day and the night, and to divide between the light and the darkness; God saw that it was good. There was evening and there was morning, a fourth day. God said, "Let the waters swarm with creeping living things and let birds fly over the earth, across the face of the firmament of the heavens." God created the great sea-fish; all living beings which creep, which the waters had swarmed, each according to its kind; and all winged-birds, according to their kinds; God saw that it was good. God blessed them saying, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the water in the seas and let the birds multiply on the land." There was evening and there was morning, a fifth day. God said, "Let the earth bring forth living beings according to their kinds: domesticated animals, creeping things, and beasts of the earth"; and it was so. God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the domesticated animals according to their kinds, and all the creeping things according to their kinds; God saw that it was good. God said, "Let us make humanity in our image, according to our likeness, and they will rule over the fish of the sea, the birds of the heavens, the domestic animals, all the earth, and all the creeping things which creep on the earth." God created the human being in God's image; in the image of God, God created him; male and female God created them. God blessed them and God said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply; fill the earth and conquer it; rule over the fish in the sea, the birds in the heavens, and all the beasts which creep on the earth." God said, "Behold, I give you all the grasses which reproduce by seed which are on the face of the whole earth, and all the trees which have fruits of the tree and which reproduce by seed -- they shall be food for you and for all the beasts of the earth, for all the birds of the heavens, and for all the creeping things on the earth -- that which has life in it -- all green grass shall be food"; and it was so. God saw all that God had created and behold it was very good; there was evening and there was morning, the sixth day. The heavens and the earth and all their hosts were finished. On the seventh day, God finished the work God had done and God rested on the seventh day from all the work which God had done. God blessed the seventh day and proclaimed it holy for, on it, God rested from all God's work which God had created to do. And on the eight day … … there came Ashe! . . . . . . . . . . . . . . sorry, what is that you say? A little on the egotistical side was I? I suppose that you could be right there my friend, I mean it looks like I just compared myself to the genesis of the universe and this little ball of rock and mud that we lovingly call home. Ok, yes you are right, it doesn’t just LOOK that way, that is exactly what I did, you know it and I know it. … but I thought that that was what was expected upon entrance into the Extreme Title Division. Don’t you suddenly have to be the next best thing to the Messiah himself, or perhaps even the new and improved model, upgraded for the 21st Century? Sort of like the small ads you see in the local papers, you know where it says single white male, good sense of humor, seeks single white female? I thought that I was meant to come out with my own version of a wanted ad, or an MTV generation hype video. Was I wrong? … where did I get that idea? Oh, that is easy, I simply looked around at some of the other people who have thrown their name into the hat for this division, and realised that I must not have got the memo with the guidelines on it, because all I saw was ego stroking, boasting and border line fantastical hot air blowing. In a moment of weakness my normally pretty healthy ego …or is it id, I never remember my psychology you know … deserted me and I turned into a lemming, basically following the path laid out before me by my so called peers. Instead of telling the truth, instead of simply being me, instead of hoping that my skills in the ring, my experience, my natural talent and everything else that is REAL about me would be enough, I followed the herd and became the next J. K. Rowling and started down the path of fantasy. I mean take my opponent in this division, Noah Hanson. … no I mean REALLY take him … Sorry, it seems that fantasy is not my strong suit and neither is a stand up comedy routine, I should perhaps leave that to people like Dave Harely or AWS Man instead, and stick to what I do best. … what is that? Good question, but don’t distract me, I was talking about Noah Hanson, so let me continue there. You see, I had the pleasure of checking out his words of wisdom about this upcoming match, and it became very clear that Hanson is one of those people, one of those wrestlers, that lives in a small town just the other side of reality. Yeah, I know that it isn’t on any maps, but trust me on this, the place has to exist simply because there are so many people that reside there. I mean it is fair enough to be confident, it is fair enough to believe in yourself, but Hanson goes so far as to state that even though he doesn’t know me, even though he doesn’t know much about the type of match we are to have that I am going to get my ass handed to me by him. By the instant ratings boost no less! Should I take a few moments here to feel privileged, perhaps even to stand in awe of his presence? No, I didn’t think that I should either, but thank you for agreeing. I have to be honest at the onset here, and say that I am not like other wrestlers. God, now I sound like a teenage kid trying to get his first date with a corny chat up line don’t I? … you don’t need to answer that one. The point is that I really am not like other wrestlers though, I don’t feel the need to come out and state that I am better than anyone else, I don’t feel the need to belittle or put people down, I don’t even feel the need to make unfounded boasts about how good I am. Hanson was right in a couple of things though … and no I am not talking about him being the rising star in Midwest … or the hardest working man in Midwest, I am sure that there are quite a few wrestlers in his home territory that would argue with him about that. … and I am certainly not talking about the fact that he thinks that he is the man that no-one wants to face, as I think that he needs to sit down, let the blood rush away from his brain for a few minutes and then do the one thing that he doesn’t seem to do very often. Think. Hanson, I know that you realise that you have been challenged to a match, and I know that you have accepted it, so perhaps you need to take a few seconds and think about that one little aspect. Challenged … no-one wanting to face you. No-one wanting to face you … someone challenges you. Steady there guy, I didn’t mean to shatter your whole faith, but I thought that it was only fair to wake you up and make you realise that in my eyes at least you are not this big, unstoppable hardcore machine. In my eyes you are not the instant ratings boost that I should be worried about facing. In my eyes you are simply the first step in the journey, simply another opponent, simply my next match. The one thing that you got right though is that I am an unfamiliar opponent, an unknown factor so to speak. Only one match in wMv so far, and no other information known about me. Oh dear I do seem to be fitting a stereotype in wrestling now don’t I? Let’s see. Mask? … check … Parts unknown? … check … No known history? … check … Damn it, and I was doing so good as well, but I suppose I will have to admit that perhaps I am not so different from other wrestlers after all. Then again I can still hold true to some differences. I prefer that my actions do the talking, I prefer to let the truth speak for itself, I don’t care about what people think about me. That is why I wear the mask Hanson, that is why there is no history about me. It doesn’t matter how many years I have been wrestling, how many titles I have won, who I am under this mask. Do I really need to know about your title defences? Do I really need to know about the people that you have beaten before? Do I really need to know about what is happening in the federation that pays you? … do I even CARE? The answer is no Hanson, to all of those questions. All that matters is the here and now, the past is gone, the future unknown, the only time that we have is the present. The only thing to think about is you and me … Hanson and Ashe … in the ring, fighting until only one man is still standing. All that I need to know is already known. You are a vainglorious boaster who relies so much on the sound of his own words that he has forgotten that sound without meaning is nothing. Your words are as empty as the hope that you seem to have in yourself to win this match. I know that I am now leaning towards making the same threats as you have done my friend, and to some that may appear as hypocritical, but you see there is a difference here. You talk about me being a newbie punk that plays with childhood toys. … I am not so new Hanson, you just don’t know me, and I never had a childhood. You talk about not being concerned about losing the match and your place in the rankings. … that should be your only concern, your lack of care simply underlines the fact that you do not believe in yourself, and probably realise that your chance is slim. You talk about branding me like a steer in this match to ensure I know you. … I know you Hanson, I know you quite well actually, you just don’t know me at the moment, but trust me on this very soon you will. You talk so much Hanson, but at the end of the day you SAY nothing of value at all. … as I was saying there is a difference between what you have said and what I am saying now. What is it? I will back my words up. ---- 17 March 2004 I wonder what Aristotle was thinking when he said that all human actions have one or more of seven causes behind them? Chance Nature Compulsion Habit Reason Passion Desire I mean of course it goes without saying … though I will say it anyway … that Aristotle, more than any other thinker, determined the orientation and content of Western intellectual thinking. … but I have to think that no matter how intelligent or forwarding thinking he was, or how much he shaped the way that modern man actually thinks, he never thought about human actions and their causes in the context of a modern day phenomenon that we lovingly call sports entertainment. However, seeing as I recently had the gall to compare myself to the miracle that is the creation of the Universe itself, I thought that I would rein my ego in slightly and this time simply do no nothing more than use an Aristotelian argument to consider my upcoming match with Noah Hanson, the self proclaimed ‘instant ratings boost’. … join us next week as I decorate the PTC’s offices in the style of Michelangelo’s Sistine Chapel … … or not. Anyway, as I was saying, if Aristotle was correct … and trust me on this, I may seem to have some problems with my ego, but arguing against Aristotle is not something that I feel qualified to do … then I should be able to look at Noah Hanson’s recent actions … namely accepting my challenge and his comments thereafter … … as well as looking into the causes behind them, exploring each of the seven aspects that led him to do, or say, what he did. Still with me? I am impressed … not a lot admittedly, but mildly … to see that someone who is a fan of the modern day gladiatorial games that stretch the mind no more than a generic MTC advert would still be here as I discuss millennia old Greek philosophical comments, stay with me to the end and you may even raise my impressed level to one of awe. Isn’t that good enough reason to stick with it? … don’t answer that, it was rhetorical. Chance is the first of the causes for Hanson’s acceptance of my challenge, and this is one of the more simple ones, for you see Hanson has found himself in an arena in which he does not seem to be comfortable. You see he is admittedly a hero in his own mind … oh, apologies for that, I meant to say in his own federation of course, I would never disparage his achievements, though I suppose that it would be fair to say that Hanson is a big fish in a small pond in the WMW, and now that he has stepped out into the big bad World of the PTC Extreme Title Division he has realised just how a minnow in the Atlantic Ocean feels. So looking around the division itself, and realising that he is not going to be able to bluff his way through these matches, what does he do? He sees the one name on the list that he doesn’t recognise, does some research and finds out that Ashe is a newcomer to both mVw and the PTC, finds out that Ashe has only one professional bout under his belt, and finds out that Ashe is not even ranked in the division at all. … he finds out that Ashe has made an open challenge to the whole division and what does he do? He takes his chance. He accepts the challenge, taking the chance that he will be able to defeat this poor little rookie of a wrestler, earn some kudos, and move up the rankings. He takes the chance and accepts MY challenge. It isn’t his fault though, it is just his nature after all. … did you like that? The little segue into the next cause of Aristotle’s seven … I was quite proud of it myself, seamless you have to admit. True as well though, Hanson seems to be a perennial grappler, a man who has lived his life either on the street by his wits … and one has to feel sorry for him, because if you could compare that to a salary then his wits would only be equal to minimum wage with no pension … or in the ring. … and even in the ring Hanson doesn’t seem to have been able to rise to the very top of the game, instead languishing in the mid-card territory that so many wrestlers seem to end up in. Again though it seems that his nature is to delude himself into thinking that he is greater than he actually is, that everyone else is lesser … and if that is what gets him through each day, then fair enough, who am I to intrude into his little world that is one stop away from reality? Oh yeah … I am his opponent, that is who. I HAVE to intrude here, I HAVE to argue against Hanson, you could almost say that I am compelled to do so … almost but not quite as we are not delving into my psyche here, we are not using Aristotle to pry into my brain here, no more than I would allow the teachings of Jung, Freud or Rorschach to be used against me, so it is Hanson’s own compulsion that we are looking into, not mine. … never mine. I am not even sure if Hanson is consciously aware of what he is doing, one would hope that he isn’t because while animals are put out of their misery when they are beyond help, we humans are not that compassionate with our own kind, and Hanson would just have to keep on suffering. I think that deep down he is simply compelled to reach out and attempt to gain some recognition, attempt to gain some notoriety, attempt to gain ANYTYING that elevates him above his peers. … or maybe he has accepted this match simply because he is stuck in a rut, simply because he doesn’t know any other way, simply because he is in the habit of rushing in head first, not pausing to think, not pausing to consider the ramifications of what he does. From what little I know about Hanson, he does seem to be in the habit of repeating the same mistake time after time … of making the same assumptions time after time. Who am I to break this habit of his that leads him to defeat simply because he cannot see outside the cycle of destruction he seems to be in? Well as I said I am his opponent, and trust me on this, I don’t intend to break this habit of is. I don’t have any reason to do so, just as Hanson’s reasons for accepting my challenge are as clear and obvious. I have already stated that Hanson is deluded enough to equate me being unknown with me being untalented … that simply because he does not know what I am capable of means that I am not capable of anything. Aristotle being a teacher would appreciate that fact that at journey’s end … when Hanson and I face each other in the ring … I will at least teach Hanson the error of his ways, and who knows next time Hanson is faced with a similar predicament to the one he is in know he will not make the same mistakes. You see his reasons for accepting this challenge … his thoughts of me as some form of cartoon watching joke, his views that I am someone not in his league simply because he doesn’t realise what league I am in, and all of his other misguided reasons are ultimately no reason at all, as he will no doubt realise in the very near future. … which will be too little too late. You see one thing that I cannot argue with is that Hanson is allowing his heart to rule here, he is reacting to my challenge with the adrenal fuelled organs that primitive man used so well to survive in prehistoric times. I could almost see Hanson in furs roaming around a cave looking for small mammals to skewer for dinner, I think that his mindset would serve him well there. … only problem is that we are no longer living in those times, we are no longer simply fighting to survive daily. Passion is simply one approach to things, and for me it is not the right one … why let the heart rule the mind? Why let emotions get involved in matters that require nothing more than logical thought … cold calculation … evaluation? Hanson can keep his heart, I will keep my mind, and another philosophical thought can be tested … is it survival of the fittest, or survival of the calmest? Will the man with the hottest head and blood win out … the one who boasts and jokes the most? … or will it be the cold, calm, calculating man who doesn’t joke … who doesn’t boast, and to be honest doesn’t even care? Will it be Hanson or Ashe? … perhaps it will be desire after all that decides this match, because that is the one area along with passion where Hanson has me beaten, fair and square. You see if this match was to be judged on desire … on who WANTS to win the most, on who HAS to win the most, on who DESIRES to win the most, then I would put my money on Hanson as well. If the match was decided on the emotions and passion that came out of this, on who felt the blood run through their veins as the bell rings, on who gets that rush of adrenaline as the audience cheer, on who lives and breathes for this, then I would again have to say that Hanson would win this in seconds. … what a pity for him then that this match won’t be decided on who gets that fastest hard on when they think about the fans. … what a pity that for him then that this match won’t be decided on who smiles the most … cares the most … feels the most. This match will be decided on one thing and one thing only. Who is still standing at the end. This match will come down to who can give … and take … the most and still keep their body in one piece, on who is willing to put it all on the line knowing that it may be the last thing that they do. It won’t come down to who wants it the most … it will come down to who cares the least. … who cares the least about hurting, about the pain they feel, about the agony they will continue to feel in the days after the match has finished. … who cares … Well, I don’t. You see I have held Hanson up for inspection, and I have used Aristotle’s methods in trying to figure out the cause … the reason … for his action … for why he accepted my challenge. Do you know what I have found out? Of course you don’t, I haven’t told you yet. I have found out that it is not one of Aristotle’s vaunted seven causes … it is not chance, nature, compulsion, habit, reason, passion or desire … that has led Hanson to this choice, instead it is something different, something new. Something that I like to call Ashe’s two causes. Stupidity, and delusion. Harsh? … perhaps. True? Undoubtedly so, as Hanson … the so called hardcore icon himself … has evidenced for us. Every woman fantasizes about and every man fears but wants to be? Really Hanson, clichés like that would be laughable if it wasn’t obvious that they are all you have, all that you can cling to in this world. Your life itself seems to be one long cliché and you don’t even seem to realise it! I mean come on Noah, I have already told you that I am not only not impressed by your past, but I literally do not CARE about it. You keep repeating what you have done before … last week, last month, last year, even your very first match. Why? It doesn’t actually matter Hanson, all that matters is the one match that is coming up, the match that I challenged you to and the match that you accepted. The last man standing match. Thank you though for explaining to me what that match means, I mean just think if I couldn’t figure out for myself what it entailed simply from the name ... … last … man … standing … how else would I have known? Oh yeah, I know … because I CHALLENGED you to it you fool, I am the one that requested it. What did you think … did you think that I sat with a wrestling encyclopaedia and looked up interesting names … or perhaps opened the book randomly and stuck a pin in it? Oh dear … have I just revealed the secret to your so called success? Seriously though Hanson your grasp of the obvious here was nearly as inane as your fascination with trying to pretend that you KNOW what I am thinking or what I am feeling. Trust me on this if you somehow DID get a sneak peek into my head and managed to see how my mind works you would NOT be waiting for me to meet you in the ring. You would either be running as fast as you can without looking back or looking for a priest to confess to, finally realising that all the old crap that you were taught in bible class really is true. So don’t try to tell me that I am afraid of you Noah, or that I am nervous about facing you. Don’t try to tell me that I am going to face a world of pain when you take your little brass knuckles or a steel chair to my head. … I have faced much worse Hanson, I have felt the pain of losing my mother and the fear of going with her. I have felt that pain and much more my friend, so try your little word games on someone who not only believes them but cares about them. I am not afraid of pain Hanson, in fact it is one of the few things that reminds me that I am alive. … did no-one tell you that Hanson? … did you research not point out that while I have entered the Extreme Division I refuse to actually fight to so called hardcore rules? You pick up a chair and hit me with it if you like … punch me with your brass knuckles … do what you want to. I won’t use a weapon Hanson, that isn’t my style. I will face you though, I will fight you, I will take whatever you can dish out and keep coming back for more. … I will do all of that and one thing more. I will beat you. Hear that Hanson, that isn’t fear, that isn’t nervousness, that isn’t even the self deluded voice you hear every time you open your mouth. That is me Hanson. That is Ashe. That is the truth. So come on Hanson, come an prove yourself ‘hardcore’ if you can. I said that you were either stupid or deluded didn’t I? Well you seem to be waiting for something of a biblical nature to happen, so I don’t want to disappoint you, so how about this … … how about a little REVELATION? Ashe’s two causes for you accepting my challenge, and your actions since then aren’t that you are stupid or deluded. They are both.
