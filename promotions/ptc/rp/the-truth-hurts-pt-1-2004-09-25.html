The Truth Hurts pt.1

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... "The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens in the plush interior of the Shadow Rider’s South Beach home. We can see that it is some time in the mid afternoon hours of the day by the streams of light shining through the large glass sliding door windows looking out to the deck. Inside the room, Jason Kage sits on a red leather sofa playing one of the many video games on the Playstation 2. As Kage continues to play his game, the door to the deck slides open as Eric Russo enters the house carrying some sort of paper in his hand. With a slight chuckle, he begins to speak as he looks at the paper.) Russo: This has to be a joke. (Kage looks over to Russo in an inquisitive fashion.) Russo: Me taking on Kris Kanton!? The poor kid isn’t even close to my level. This match is gonna be a massacre. (Kage goes back to his video game as Russo continues his rant.) Russo: To think, he even calls one of his moves the Falling Star. What a blatant rip off of me, it’s kind of sad really. He can’t even come up with an original name for his move so he steals one of mine. (Without ever taking his attention off of his game, Kage interjects.) Kage: Well Eric, they do say that imitation is the greatest form of flattery. (Russo quickly pops back with an answer to the age old saying as he drops the paper onto the glass top table in front of Kage.) Russo: That’s bull$h!t Jason. It’s only flattery if they do it well. Kanton ripping off my material does nothing but drag it through the mud. (Kage simply shakes his head at Russo’s total dismissal of his statement as he continues playing his game.) Russo: I mean seriously, how’s it gonna look when the name GTT winner’s finishing move is being used by some jackass like Kris Kanton!? It’s like a slap in the face. (Realizing that Russo is not about to stop anytime soon, Kage presses the pause button on his controller. He then tosses the controller onto the table and turns to Russo as he responds.) Kage: Jesus Eric just let it go. It’s a friggin’ move name. Is someone using the name of one of your moves really gonna damage it’s effectiveness in the ring? (Russo’s face fills with anger as Kage continues.) Kage: I swear, you spend so much time worrying about this detail and that detail instead of getting your head where it should be. (Kage’s last comment is all that is needed to set his friend off. Russo responds in a questioning tone that is riddled with the obvious anger that he is trying his best to conceal.) Russo: Getting my head where it should be? (Knowing that he has started something, Kage quickly tries to diffuse the situation.) Kage: Just forget about it Eric. (Not allowing Kage to go back on his words, Russo plops down next to him on the couch as he speaks.) Russo: No . . . I wanna hear this. I wanna hear where you think my head should be at. Kage: Dammit just drop it Eric! I don’t wanna get into this with you! (Kage’s attempt to get off the subject once again is to no avail as Russo continues.) Russo: Get into what Jason!? You said my head is in the wrong place, I wanna know what you meant. (With a glare in his eye, Kage finally explodes.) Kage: You wanna know what I meant Eric!? I’ll tell you what I meant! I think you should put your head where it really belongs and that’s in LOW! You’re the world champion for crying out loud! Do you think about that though, no! You worry about the GTT, you worry Kris Kanton, you worry about Jason Starr! You have a world title match coming up in LOW! You should be worrying about that, you should be saving your energy for that! (Kage stands up from the couch as he continues.) Kage: I hate to break it to you but we’re not as young as we used to be Eric. It’s time to slow down and concentrate on what’s really important. Right now that should be you holding the LOW world title, not some tournament. I’m telling you this as a friend. Just it slow down a little and enjoy. (Russo looks up at Kage flustered and responds.) Russo: This is what you think? (Kage quietly answers in a clam and even tone.) Kage: I’m sorry, but yes Eric. That is what I think. (Still slightly agitated from the conversation he has just had, Russo pushes himself off the couch. Then in a disgruntled fashion he stomps off towards the back hallway of the house as he mumbles under his breath.) Russo: Then think whatever you want Jason but the fact remains that the choices are still mine and mine alone. (Russo continues off down the hallway and enters the bathroom. Once inside, he slams the door shut behind him and starts for the toilet, still mumbling to himself under his breath. As Russo passes by the sink, he catches a glimpse of himself in the mirror out of the corner of his eye. The sight that he sees is enough to stop the Shadow Rider in his tracks. He turns to take a better look at himself only to be further confronted by the facts.) Russo: Is that really me? (The face that stares back at Russo from within the mirror is not the one of youthful exuberance that he remembers. He is instead greeted by the face of a grizzled veteran, a face that has witnessed many battles and partaken in too many wars. It is then that the words that he has been hearing for the past few months finally begin to sink in.) Russo: Maybe I am pushing myself too much. Maybe Jason is right. Maybe it is about time that I slow down. (Russo turns the water faucet in the sink on and places his hands underneath the running water. As the water cascades through his fingers, the Shadow Rider cups his hands together allowing them to fill with water. He then quickly brings his hands up and splashes the cool water against his face. This action seems to be enough to dispel the feelings of doubt that have crept up inside of him as he now looks back into the mirror and speaks to himself with a smirk.) Russo: Who am I kidding, there’s no rest for the wicked. I’ll have plenty of time to slow down when I retire. (Russo twists the faucet handle to the off position as he turns away from the sink. As he now reaches for the hand towel which is hung on the back of the door, Russo begins to become strangely unsettled. Within an instant the room around him is spinning as a nauseous feeling fills his stomach. Then nothing . . . all of the spinning and nauseating feelings gone into the darkness that now takes hold. As Russo falls to the floor unconscious, his head smacks off the side of the porcelain bathtub. Hearing the thud from the other room, Kage is quickly on the scene as he barges in through the door. Seeing Russo’s body laying prone on the floor as a small puddle of blood begins to from around his head is enough to severely panic Kage who quickly screams out.) Kage: Luke call an ambulance! Eric is hurt! (As Kage now turns back to his friend to try and revive him, as the screen cuts back to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which states “Life may end but legends never die.”. The screen now fades to black.)
