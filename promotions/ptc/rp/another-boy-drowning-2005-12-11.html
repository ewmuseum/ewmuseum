Another Boy Drowning

11 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-11 I won. After months and months of nothing, away from it all, an outcast from the sport, I come back. I was an underdog, a miniscule freak going against a current fighter. I wasn’t supposed to win. But I won. I won. And the thought kept on hitting me, over and over again like a wind up car going straight into a wall. I’d try to have my mind somewhere else, but I was stuck on that one God damn thought. I won. Here I was now, stuck. I already checked out of my motel room, the scuzzy attendant in the lobby giving me a look. I thought I lost, so I just thought I’d save some time. And now the bandages covering my burnt body were dirty and bloodied up, needing a change. But I couldn’t…my mind was not ready. Not only did I no longer have no where to do it, but I was still stuck on that thought. I won. Usually when someone wins, they feel ecstatic. They want the world to know. Or they feel humbled, fulfilled. And I used to be that way. I was that warrior, that beast, that monster that ached for that next kill. I used to be a true competitor. But not anymore. After the Vietnam War, after my accident in which I was encased with a sea of fire from a bomb, I didn’t want it anymore. People stared at my burnt body, thought I was dead. They thought I was a goner. I survived though…unfortunately. From head to toe I’m mummified. I’m covered in sutures, super strong ones at that. They protect me from getting wet, from damaging my skin…they’re really a God send. There are problems with them, though. They’re a bitch to take off…hurt like hell. Worst pain in the world. And, the public are horrible. I’m an outcast with these, I can’t fit in anymore. I can’t just be that every day Joe. I’m a sore thumb in a sea of fingers, sticking out like nothing else. The darkness is my only savior. That shady part of cities where nobody gives a fuck about who you are or what you do is where I go. The motels with no door handles on the rooms are where I stay. I live in solitude, because everything has left me. "The world is accepting Drake! No, really! They’ll love you!" Fuck you. I’ve heard it all before. I’ve heard all the hookers and the drug dealers and the scum tell me that they’ll accept me. But don’t think I haven’t heard it all. "What’s wrong with him, mommy? Why is he covered in bandages?" "Fucking freak, get out of here!" "Don’t stare…it’s rude." So that’s why I’m the way I am. I’ve become a creep, afraid of mankind while still wanting to go back. I live a pitiful existence of hiding from the world while trying to jump back in. Tonight, though, standing outside of the motel, draped in my black trench coat, I felt something different. I won. I was on top of the world. I felt like that maybe, just maybe, people would love me again. That I’d go back to being that kid I was before the war, that All-American teenager. Maybe I’d be that man I always dreamed about growing up to be, not this freak I currently am. In my current state, I decided to roam the streets, the dark alley ways, my usual stomping grounds. It was late, and all the scum was out. The prostitutes, the hood rats, the muggers…all roaming like me, looking for their prey. After a few blocks, I noticed a couple of them stalking me. They were two big guys, fellas you wouldn’t take home to mom. I knew they were going to mug me, without a doubt. You’d think they’d stay away from the freaky looking guys…pathetic. I was on top of the world tonight, though. I felt like I could do anything, crush anything, and defeat reality itself. In my cocky state of mind, I turned around and waited for them, not moving a muscle in my body except my calmly beating heart. They stopped. They were shocked I would do such a thing, but I think it was mostly that they got a good glimpse of me. They saw the dirty bandages, peeling off at parts, covered in blood and grime. It was a scene out of a horror movie, the beast stopping for its prey. But these guys were hood rats…they were "hard", not afraid of anything. "How’s it going buddy?" One of them called out to me as he began creeping towards me, trying to catch me off guard. He had a slimy voice, sloppier then a salamander that attempted to cajole me into dropping my guard. I replied with nothing. I didn’t want them to think I was soft. I had my poker face on, waiting for them to come and get me. I was ready, ready to defend myself. "What’s the matter, buddy? ‘Fraid to talk?" They got closer and closer, and I could almost feel the fight. The punches, the kicks…I didn’t think victory was out of the picture. Soon, they rushed me, and I rushed back with a kick to the head, knocking one down. Unfortunately, though, there were two, and I was hit hard. Very hard. "ARRGHHHH!" My head whipped to the side as he hit me square on, sending my body flying into the concrete wall. *RIPPPPP* "NYUUUUUUUGH!" I could feel it…oh the horror! I should’ve changed those bandages! They had grown so weak that, with simple contact, they ripped, with my skin doing the same against the wall. I could feel it peeling off my body, going down far enough to make me bleed. I was paralyzed with pain and agony. "Mother fucker kicked me in the head! Why I oughta…!" *BAM! BAM! BAM!* He laid into me with his boots, but I couldn’t feel a thing. It was like death…there was no more pain anymore. The last thing I saw before I blanked out was the two men pulling my trench coat off of my limp body, flipping me over into darkness… -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- In a room. By myself. Darkness. Where was I? What was happening? Why was I here? The door creeps open. *CREEEEEEEEAK* Light outside. Blasting into my face. It’s blinding. ARGHHHHHH! There’s someone outside though. Who could it be? I peer towards… Is it her? Charlene? But I thought she left me! She’s here! This is my day! I get up and start walking towards her, and she starts walking towards me. My lover! My former reason for living! But…what’s that? She’s changing…blood…guts…gore… Oh no! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- "Hey, wake up buddy! Rise and shine!" My eyes peeled open, breaking the crust that held them together. I was lying on my stomach, my body numb and a felt paralyzed. I couldn’t move a thing except my mother and my eyes. "Jesus, you look like a fucking MESS, Drake! What the fuck happened to you?" It was him. The man who has been part of me for who-knows how long. He came back last week, haunting me, giving me those oxycontin pills for my pain. And now he was here, wherever the fuck we were. Me from the past…my reoccurring hallucination. "You’re lucky I was around, Drake. Saved your ass!" He crept in front of my eyes, crouching next to the bed. His smile glistened, forcing me to squint for a moment before I could get used to it. His eyes gave me a look of "Glad to see you!", piercing my own. It was painful. It was awful. It was, technically, me. "Hey there." My response was blank, my jaw barely being able to move. "How you feeling, you dumb fuck?" He laughed in my face and stood up, pacing off to somewhere else in the room. I was still unable to move, so I could only imagine what he was doing, where he was going, and the likes. "Uh…I don’t feel a fucking thing…" And it was true. I was numb. There was nothing. It was as if I just had a mind and a sack of bricks attached to it. I was being held down by dead weight, and I couldn’t tell if I liked it or not. "And you remember why, right? Don’t’cha, buddy?" "Of course I do. Muggers." "DING DING DING! We have ourselves a fucking winner! I’ll show you what’s behind door number one, ok?" He walked into my sights again, dropping two pills in front of my. They were the oxycontins. "I don’t need these." "Oh really?" Suddenly, a shooting pain shot throughout my body. I gained all feeling back, writhing on the bed. I rolled off the bed and started clawing at the walls, the pain too much. "ARGHHHH!" I couldn’t force out any real words, everything so unbearable. It was the worst feeling I have ever had. Without notice, I felt myself being pulled up and dragged. Once I was taken to the mirror, I saw him standing next to me, smiling and shaking his head. "Hurts like a bitch, doesn’t it?" No words came out of my mouth as I clenched my jaw. I would clench my teeth, but I lost those years ago. Well, most of them, that is. It was only fitting, really. He started fumbling on the table and grabbed a mirror, placing it behind me. From the reflection I could see my back—disgusting. It was soaked in blood, the bandages being strewn and mangled. My burnt skin mixed with the red, making a mess. It looked like a chocolate Sunday with strawberry sauce—all puked up by some little kid. "Ugggghhhh…" My stomach churned as I could feel myself beginning to wobble. Luckily, he was there to keep me up. I don’t know how that worked, since he didn’t exist, but I didn’t bother questioning it at the time. All I could think about was the pain. "Yeah, Drake, you see, those guys got you…real good. Man, didn’t you feel it? You hit that damn brick wall puh-rettyyyyy hard. I mean, hell, look at this!" He placed his hand on the back of my head, smiling evilly. Once he pulled it away and held it to my face, I could see it was covered in little shards of black skin and drenched in blood. "BLOOOOOOORGGGG!" He dropped me and I fell to my knees as the puke flew everywhere. It was in the carpets, on the walls. And the smell—imagine dragging a human at 150 mph against the concrete. That smell of burning flesh… "BLOOOOOOOORG!" "Awww…poor little Drakey!…" "BLOOOOOOOORG!" "What? Can’t handle the pain?" And that was the truth. This was something horrible…worse than the initial pain from the accident. These muggers tore me up, good. I needed help. I needed something. I remembered the pills, the oxycontins. I stood up, staggering and almost falling right back down. Clutching onto anything I could, I made it back to the bed, seeing two of everything. Through it all I saw the pills on the bed and lunged towards them, falling straight into the bed. "Hey, Bud, what’re you doing?" He knew exactly what was happening, but he wanted to hear the words come out of my mouth. I couldn’t say a thing, though. I laid there on my back, holding the pills above me and stared at them, quietly. "Heh…I thought you said you didn’t need them! Oh, look at me! I’m Soldier Drake Coleman, tough guy of the millennium! But now, WAAAAA! I’m a little fucking baby! I shed tears day in and day out! WAAAA! Gimme pills, WAAAAA!" "Shut up." The words were stern, but I didn’t mean them. He was right…what was I doing? Was this me, actually thinking that these pills would be a good idea? He laughed at me aloud, shaking his head all the while. I could see him out of the corner of my eye strutting towards me, eventually taking a seat next to me. He grabbed the pills out of my hand, catching my eyes as well. I followed the pills into his pocket then, as they went out of site, I glanced at his face. "You son of a…" I attempted to lunge towards him but quickly stopped, remembering just how much pain I was in. My head begin to pound with everything as well as I sat up, trying to be still. I breathed heavily, trying to keep myself conscious. "I see there is a change of heart, eh Drake?" "No…it’s just…" "It’s just, it’s just…it’s just there IS a change, buddy, and that’s the bottom line, ok? I know it, and it’s sure as hell obvious that YOU know it as well." And I did. I had miles ahead of me. Clyde Walkins from PRIME was my next opponent and, after beating LeStatt Knight, I knew he wasn’t a pushover. I was an underdog who needed that boost. I needed the energy, the relief…but I wasn’t about to admit it. As I turned to deny him, though, he was gone. Vanished with the two pills sitting there being the only trace. I stared at them…it was almost as if they shone like stars. Each breath I took was painful. My chest expanded and shrunk, a flash of pain to accompany each. I was feeling woozy, sick…it was all horrible. All the while I knew they were there. The pills. My future. It was inevitable I would take them. I could no longer go on living like this. This pain…this… Most people try to find something else when they’re in pain. They look for a diversion, something to draw their attention away from what really causes them agony. It turns into a search, a journey, an adventure for comfort. Michael Crighton said it in his book The Lost World: Jurassic Park perfectly when he said that "We are stubborn, self-destructive conformists. Any other view of our species is just self-congratulatory delusion." We look for something to grasp onto. We need that something to be a part of when we are in pain, whether it’s an actual group of that idea of this or that helping us. Everyone has different things they look towards. Some people have food. They enjoy a nice meal to cheer them up. Some like entertainment. A funny movie will lift feelings, or a romantic movie will make themselves feel good. It all varies. The most prominent diversion is religion. Every day there are millions—no, BILLIONS of people who have faith. They go to church, ask which way is west, avoid the pigs. They believing living this or that way will ultimately save them. I used to as well. Before it all happened, I thought there was a God. I went to church, I owned a Bible which I constantly read…hell, I was even part of the choir. But then it all happened. The war. The accident. The burns. I prayed to my God that I’d never feel so much pain again. But the agony had just begun. I was alone. Nothing. No one. For years I believed that I still had God. But no God would put me through such agony. No being would let a man like me survive an accident like the one in ‘Nam, allow me to keep on breathing. So now, here I am, alone. There is no savior to save me from my pain anymore. I have nothing to look towards… Except these pills. … I lost. -------------------------------------------------------------- You pray to your God that you’ll never feel so much pain again.
