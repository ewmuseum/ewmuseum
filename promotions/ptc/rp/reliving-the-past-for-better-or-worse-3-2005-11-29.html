Reliving the Past - For Better or Worse #3

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 We hear the crackling of flames as the screen is lit up again (this time pretty visually) for our third look into the past of Jack ‘The Bull’ Murphy. Whilst the preceding two have been in calmer, more relaxing climbs in sunny California, this one seems to be a lot more dangerous and urgent. And ridiculously hot. As the camera pans out we’re revealed to the picturesque view of a building on fire. People are frantically rushing about, hoses spewing water everywhere the eye can see and there are several TV cameras from the local stations covering the event, hurriedly retelling a horror story. They interview residents of the apartment block it seems and they are in tears, lamenting their lost possessions but thankful they are simply alive. However in one corner of the scene there is an altogether bizarre looking prayer circle going on. In this, the 80’s, surely they must be worshipping the almighty dollar? Is perhaps Gordon Gecko standing tall in the centre of the circle, waxing lyrical on the effects of losing possessions on our economic climate? No, it’s ‘The 8th Wonder’ Jack Murphy. Huh? There, standing in the centre of a group of men stood Jack Murphy, clad in a dashing shocking green and grey tracksuit, speaking to each man in turn, telling them various things. As we close in we begin to hear the voice of Jack. Jack Murphy: …you may have lost something that is material and worthy of your money my friends, but you have gained the opportunity to realise something far more important. Just think Larry, (points at a man) just think if the firemen hadn’t been in the building to carry you out, do you honestly think you could’ve got out of there on your own with your…lacking physique? The man is clearly obese, rolls of fat actually covering further rolls. Larry: No… Jack Murphy: Exactly! And you Clarence…do you think you’d be out of there on your own with your chronic phobia of fire? Would you have cowered in your room until the fires took you and confirmed your own fears? Clarence it seems is a middle aged black man, coincidentally looking over his shoulder every five seconds to make sure the fire hasn’t gotten any closer to him since the last time. He simply looks at Jack for a moment, nods his head hurriedly and then returns to his watch. Jack Murphy: My friends, this is no way to live and the fires have opened your eyes! Look at yourselves. Do you want to be a liability to yourself anymore? Do you? Man: No… Jack Murphy: Louder Harold louder! Harold: NO! Jack Murphy: That’s the attitude that I’m looking for! That’s the kind of person who wants to do something with their lives! And my friends…for only a small long-term loan, all of you can achieve your peaks physically and never be afraid of anything again…even fire Clarence. Saying this Murphy plants his arm on Clarence’s shoulder who looks up at him expectantly. In true cheese fashion, a light seems to be on behind Murphy, planting a halo behind his head. Jack is smiling, beaming as usual and winks towards the camera. Jack Murphy: It took a life changing even for these men to realise that they are not fulfilling their potentials, will you be likewise and open your eyes? Or are you going to be just like Jake Robertson, blind being led by the blind. Don’t be stupid, look for the light my friends, the light that is calling to you. Murphy now steps beyond the group and heads towards the beacon of fire in the background, the apartment that is pouring forth flames despite the work being put into it by the firemen. Jack stops in front of it, addressing the camera more formally. Jack Murphy: For two years I have been a resident of the NCWA, two long years that have seen me toil and scrounge deep in the undercard of the place…but no longer. Tomorrow night at Sunday Night Revenge I will have the chance, a chance I will take, to stake my claim as the Heavyweight Champion. NCWA has been standing on the foundations of an old, tired wrestler for too long. The building now seems to be crumbling in the background, bits of smoke leaking out of the ground floor. Jack Murphy: It starts with a murmur but before long it builds into a shockwave and tomorrow night I set the NCWA ablaze…it is time for it to be built anew, and I am the foundation. Without another word ‘The 8th Wonder’ walks off the picture and the camera focuses in on the building itself, slowly falling apart from the ground up…
