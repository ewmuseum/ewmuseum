James von Doom - Getting High on von Doom At a home somewhere in the city limits of Duluth, Minnesota, two reefer smoking friends have been puffing from their bong for the past half an hour when one of them gets his laptop going and decides to show his friend some videos he found on the video sharing site Youtube. The guy with the laptop, whose name is Tom. He is your average metalhead stoner in America. He’s dressed liked a nineties Grungie and has pictures of weed on the walls of his living room. The other guy is his loser buddy Paul. He’s wearing a Stay Puft Marshmallow Man T-shirt, ratty jeans, and dusty old Vans shoes. He has a joint hanging out of the side of his mouth. “Ok dude,” he says to his buddy Paul. “When this loads up I want to show you some videos on Youtube of this wrestler guy named James von Doom who is like a complete psychopath!” “What?” Paul replies, clearly out of it at this point. “This dude is like out of his mind,” Tom says with a grin. “He fights fans, he shoots on other wrestlers all the time, he pisses off management, he calls people ass kissers all the time, and he just creates chaos. He’s basically the Joker from the Dark Knight without the make-up.” “Are you fucking serious or are you pulling my chain?” Paul asks with a raised eyebrow. “Is it just the weed talking?” “Yeah, I am fucking serious,” Tom responds. “This guy says some crazy shit. He’s like the Mike Tyson of wrestling.” “That’s cool,” Paul says. “I can’t wait to see this shit now.” “I think you will love this fucking shit,” Tom replies. “This guy also almost took a piss on the ring one time. It was fucking awesome. And that was with his boss standing next to him! This guy fucking rules the universe and all dimensions in it!” The laptop finally loads up and Tom quickly doubles on the Internet Explorer icon before it comes on in a new window. Tom clicks on the address bar and types in the address for Youtube before clicking Go and the Youtube home page comes on the screen. “Now just let me get on my favorites list and I’ll show you some of the stuff this guy does,” Tom says. “Awesome,” Paul says with a grin as he takes another hit. He clicks on his Youtube username, which is SuperWeedTowlieMan420. A home page screen comes on and he clicks on the word Favorites. Then a whole bunch of videos on there. “Ok,” Tom says as he starts looking at the videos he has saved. “I’ll just show you the good shit and not the crappy shit. I‘ll show you the best of the best, the most wild stuff you will ever see.” “Dude, this von Doom guy sounds fucking awesome,” “He sounds like he’s completely out of control and doesn’t give a shit.” The video then starts playing of the press conference for GCW Warpath 2008 of James von Doom talking about his match with AXEL Action. Von Doom does the basic question and answer session with the reporters in the video. He can be heard saying that AXEL Action can “lick his asshole” and that he’s “going to make him his he-bitch man-slave.” Then he starts ranting and raving about the backstage environment as the camera pans over to show some officials shaking their heads. “WHOA!” Paul exclaims with a chuckle. “This is like the coolest video of all time!” “I KNOW!” Tom replies. “I mean, he just told the entire GCW locker room and most of the wrestling world that they were ass kissing brown nosers and could love the underside of his balls,” Paul says as he starts laughing. “I heard this guy sucked but man, but he fucking rules.” “He does suck,” Tom replies. “Everybody hates on this dude all time but he takes it. That makes him one bad mother fucker.” Paul is staring at the laptop screen and watching the video with a look of shocked amazement on his face. “DUDE!” he exclaims. “He just told AXEL Action that he would eat his unborn children and shit in his future wife’s mouth! I’m so loving this guy right now!” The video then finishes up with von Doom starting a fight with AXEL by von Doom slugging him in the head before AXEL can even speak and they start fighting. Von Doom’s dragged out and the video ends as Paul turns to Tom. “It’s official,” Paul says as he claps sarcastically in approval of what he had just seen. “Fuck Flair, Fuck Hogan. This guy is my favorite wrestler of all time now, period. He may suck compared to most wrestlers but he fucking rules the universe and all dimensions in it just for this shit. These Youtube videos are the greatest things I’ve ever seen in my life.” Tom then grabs the laptop and clicks the back button to the go back to the favorites list. He then scrolls over to a video entitled ‘von Doom fights with Backstage Officials.’ He clicks on it and hands the laptop back to Paul as the video starts up. “Watch this dude,” he says. “This one is fucking awesome.” Paul starts watching the video and in it von Doom is walking up to some officials back when he worked at GLOBAL wrestling. He goes up to one of them and pushes him. He then starts yelling in his face. Paul is about to mark out over this stuff. “HE JUST TOLD THAT GUY TO FUCK OFF!” Paul exclaims loudly. “THAT’S HIS BOSS!” “I love that video,” Tom replies. “Shows just how out of his mind this guy is. He doesn’t give a shit and that’s why he rules all.” In the video von Doom starts choking the official and then a ton of refs come running over to break it up as the video ends with von Doom yelling across the hallway at the official. “HOLY FUCKING SHIT!” Paul yells out. “He just told that official that he was going to have the Cave Hulk rape him in the ass and make him his HE-BITCH MAN-SLAVE!” Paul turns to Tom who is lighting the bong again on the couch. “You got any more videos?” Paul he asks. “Yeah,” Tom replies. “I got one where he stuffs a hot dog in his opponent’s mouth and yells that he knew the guy was a homo.” Paul starts laughing his ass off. “That’s awesome,” he responds with a chuckle. “When I first saw that I thought that I had seen the greatest thing in the history of mankind,” Tom says with a smile as he takes a hit from the bong. “Seriously, there’s more funny ass cool shit on there. Just go back to the favorites video menu and click on one.” Paul goes back and finds a video entitled ‘von Doom making fun of Banned and Exiled~!’ He starts watching it and it shows a Church of the Unholy interview from mid-April from GCW when they were a team. AXEL starts off talking first and then it comes to von Doom’s turn to talk. Von Doom goes into his usual rant but this one is more colorful than usual. “Oh my God! He just told them to eat his asshole!” Paul yells out. “And before that he told them to suck each other off like they do backstage before each match! HOLY FUCKING SHIT! That was awesome!” “I know,” Tom replies. “Yeah,” Paul says as he starts getting mesmerized by what he is seeing. “Wow…this guy just called a guy a fucktard and an ‘ass ramming fuckstick’ in one sentence. That alone deserves an Academy Award.” “I KNOW!” Tom replies. “See everyone thinks that everything about wrestling is just about in-ring stuff not this kind of stuff. This guy is legendary just for this kind of out of control behavior. I love it.” “That’s very true,” Paul says. “This guy is probably the most underrated wrestler of all time.” “I think so too,” Tom responds. “He’s won like thirty something championships but he keeps getting shit from everyone.” “OH MY GOD!” Paul yells out. “He told them that he would chop off their dicks and make them fuck each other in the ass! DUDE! That is the greatest thing I’ve ever heard in my life! That’s some deep ass shit right there, man.” “He’s like a psychotic philosopher like Dr. Giggles or something,” Tom says as he starts laughing. The video then ends and Paul goes back to the menu to find another video. He then clicks on one that says ‘von Doom Fights Fans.’ He starts it up and the video begins with the announcers talking about von Doom sitting on the announce table. “What the hell is this?” he asks Tom. Tom looks over and takes a peek at what Paul is looking at on the laptop. “Keep watching,” he replies. “This gets really good and fast.” “Ok,” Paul says and he goes back to watching. As he watches a fan throws a cup at von Doom. Von Doom then gets up off the table, leaps over the barricade, and starts punching the fan in the head! Von Doom then starts fighting other fans as AXEL joins in and now it’s both of them against each other and the fans. “HE JUST PUNCHED THAT FAN IN THE HEAD!” Paul exclaims as he watches on in awe over what he has just seen. “Told ya,” Tom replies with a grin. “NOW HE’S BEATING THE SHIT OUT OF LIKE FIVE FANS!” Paul yells out. “THIS IS THE MOST BAD ASS THING I’VE EVER SEEN ON A WRESTLING SHOW!” “I agree,” Tom responds. “The rest of the match kicks ass too. It’s like forty or fifty minutes and he beats up more fans later on.” “COOL!” Paul responds with a grin. The video then ends and Paul goes back to the favorites video menu where he finds another gem of a video entitled ‘von Doom calls Wrestler a Homo.’ He clicks play and the video starts up. “This should be interesting,” he says as it starts up. “Which one are you watching?” Tom asks, a little curious as to what his stoned out buddy is watching. “The one where he calls another guy a homo,” Paul replies with a laugh. Tom starts laughing. “That’s one of the best ones,” he replies. “Just wait till he gets the hot dog.” Paul keeps watching. It starts off with von Doom and AXEL fighting in the concession area. It’s from the same match as the crowd fight. Von Doom somehow ends up in the concession stand. They then fight to the food area. “Dude, he just started kicking the other dude’s ass in a shirt stand and now they’re fighting in the food stand,” Paul says. “Keep watching,” Tom replies. Paul keeps watching as they start tearing up the food stand and now von Doom has a hot dog. He stuffs it in AXEL’s mouth and yells ‘I KNEW HE WAS A HOMO!’ before turning around and getting smacked in the jaw by AXEL. “HOLY SHIT!” Paul exclaims. “THAT FUCKING RULED ALL!” “I love it when he turns around and AXEL punches him in the head,” Tom says with a chuckle. “That part is awesome,” Paul replies as the video ends. “This was pretty cool. This guy is a fucking hardcore bad ass. How can people not like this guy?” “Well he is controversial,” Tom replies. “And he does a lot of crazy shit. Like he’s the reason for Koopa Troopa and the Giant Sperm. He’s like a psychotic maniac and an evil genius. People hate this guy for all this stupid shit he causes all the time. He has like a circus of sidekicks in the business.” “Oh yeah,” Paul replies, stroking his chin. “I remember some of that from the television. Like the Killer Pirates and all that crazy shit.” Tom nods his head. “Yeah,” he replies. “That’s von Doom’s shit. He’s a fucking idiot with some of this stuff. But he’s still a bad ass. I will give him that.” “Oh, I agree after seeing this shit,” Paul responds. “This guy is probably one of the best sports figures of all time. He’s the Mike Tyson of wrestling. He’s hardcore. No matter how much he acts like a retard he’s still a bad ass no matter what anybody says.” “I agree one hundred percent,” Tom replies. “But he always loses.” “He’ll win someday,” Paul says. “Hopefully soon. This guy’s too insane to lose.” “Yeah but that also gets him into trouble,” Tom replies. “He’s always pissing off people and that makes him a chaotic element in the sport. Imagine how bad ass this guy would be if he was sane.” “True,” Paul says. “That is very true indeed.” Paul hands the laptop back to Tom and Tom clicks off of the Internet Explorer before shutting down the laptop. “Well I think that’s enough crazy shit to watch for one day,” Tom says with a grin.” Paul nods his head. “Hell’s yeah!” he responds with a laugh. “That was some crazy ass shit. I’m so glad we were high seeing that shit. Made it even more cool.” “von Doom is the baddest mother fucker on the fucking planet Earth,” Tom says as he closes the laptop after it shuts down. “He just don’t give a shit. He just does whatever he wants and doesn’t care.” “What is von Doom up to now anyway?” Paul asks. “Nobody knows,” Tom replies. “He got knocked out by Koopa Troopa in the NFW and just disappeared.” As Tom puts up the laptop in the other room Paul gets up and finishes his joint. He puts it out on the ashtray sitting on the table as Paul comes back in the room. “Dude, I’m getting a serious case of the munchies,” Tom says grabbing at his gut. “I am too,” Paul replies as his stomach growls. “Where are we going to go eat?” Tom asks. Paul then looks around for some inspiration about what to do when he looks over at the DVDs laying on the floor beside the television stand and sees exactly what he is looking for. “I’ve got it! I know where to eat at!” he exclaims with a grin. Tom looks at him with a raised eyebrow. “What did you have in mind?” he asks. Paul points to the DVD on top of the pile and Tom looks down to see ‘Harold and Kumar Go to White Castle.’ Tom turns to Paul with a big grin on his face. “WHITE CASTLE!” Tom exclaims as Paul nods his head. “Let’s go!” he yells out as they run for the door. Paul and Tom head for the door. Tom pulls out his keys as Paul skips out to the car. Tom turns off the lights and then locks the door. Their journey is about to begin before the craving for munchies becomes too much to bear.
