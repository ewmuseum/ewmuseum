IG 28 - Cassandra Paragon

The Adventures of Cassandra Paragon, Soldier from the Future. Part One “The biggest surprise for the Post-Apocalyptic world was that it even existed at all” - Albert Charity, author of 'A History of What Came After', published in 26 WCA (approx 2068 AD) PRESENT My name is Cassandra Paragon and this is, I guess what you'd call a diary. Some of the things I tell you in here will seem strange, unbelievable even. It's not your place to judge their authenticity though, merely think of yourself as an observer, a blank page that can act as a witness to my attempt to change the world. FUTURE In thirty-some years the world will change. For most people inhabiting the globe, it will end. It won't be by global terrorism or by a rogue agent infiltrating a nuclear bunker or anything so spectacular. It'll be a simple mistake made in a military research facility in California. The scientists within it will be researching a weapon called a Concussion Bomb, or Conc Bomb. It's a new spin on an old theme; trying to create a conventional explosive device that can do more damage than before, but without leaving behind pesky radiation and fallout. After all, if you're planning on invading a region, you don't want to end up a cancerous mess. Needless to say, it will go wrong. The men and women working on the project will be the first to die, but it'll be a long line that follows them. The first experiments with the bombs will work well, and the controlled environments will contain the blasts, but as they increase the force of the explosions they will pass a threshold and the critical bomb will explode with a force exponentially greater than planned for. The facility will not hold the blast, nor will the Earth itself. The concussive forces generated by the device will tear the ground to pieces, rending it asunder and causing the San Andreas fault line to become the San Andreas Great Big Fucking Hole in the World. All previous records for volcanic eruptions, earthquakes and tidal waves will be destroyed as comprehensively as the buildings and bones they consume. Both International Space Stations will watch in awe as a gaping hole appears in the western coast of America, and all hell breaks loose. Anything within a couple of hundred miles will be pretty much destroyed as the blast radius expands at the speed of sound. Millions upon millions will die, and that's only the beginning. The earthquake created by the explosion will be powerful enough to level buildings on the far coast of the United States, and shatter windows in Europe. The ash, rock and assorted crap that lurks under the crust of our fair planet will be ejected into the atmosphere, and it will blanket the entire sky, no matter where you live. The tidal waves caused by the explosion will be the largest seen in history, towering so high that it will seem as if the entire ocean has decided to move inland en masse. Anywhere not a couple of hundred feet above sea level will be flooded, and those so-called lucky souls living above that level will be too busy choking on the poisonous gases to remark on the preponderance of new lakes nearby. Governments will fall, and the recriminations will be bitter. The survivors will do a good job in killing each other with the conventional weapons available, and a few world leaders will make their dying acts to casually tear apart the remnants of the world. Nuclear, chemical and biological weapons will be launched, leaving most of Asia and parts of Europe and Africa utterly uninhabitable. Technology will, temporarily, be taken back into the Dark Ages. That is until necessity, the famed Mother of Invention, will cause a scientific renaissance. I will be born, and I will be sent back to change everything. PAST I've lived here for just under a year now. I've done things that I'm not proud of to get to where I am, but the mission is critical above all else. I woke up that first time to find myself standing in the middle of an abandoned warehouse in Wichita. My first emotion was one of relief and surprise that I was actually alive. Once I was sure that this wasn't the afterlife, I checked all of my equipment as planned, my mind easing into the military training 'mode', where everything becomes routine and task-based. My clothing, a hypoallergenic and skintight suit of a polymer that resembles leather, had come through unscathed. It clung to my nimble frame in the way it was designed to, with the microcircuits embedded within it regulating temperature, atmospheric composition, airborne pathogens and a host of other information that could be shunted into my brain. The plastic-based guns containing darts and an array of different toxins appeared to have made it to the past without a hitch, as had my bag containing various documents, high denomination notes, and cards that were forged so well that short of very accurate carbon dating, it would be impossible to tell that they were not legitimate. Finally, a compact disc containing mission information rested with the other items in the tiny bag attached to my shoulder. It was not believed that hard stored data would pass through from the What Came After so well, hence not taking any memory sticks or Info Wands. The location of the warehouse was exactly as I had been told. I walked out through a side door and hiked across a couple of gardens to make it to the road I wanted to be on; a popular place for hitchers to get themselves to the next town along. Questions would be asked by the truck driver, especially seeing as I was going to need to find somewhere I could buy some clothes, but I have always had a way with men, and it was enough for me to make it to somewhere I could stay the night. From there, it was a case of following the leads I had been given. I needed to be in different places at different times. I've crossed off most of my assignments, but the big ones is still to come. I've only had to kill seven people so far. FUTURE I'll finish top in my class in, well, basically everything, and they'll recruit me to the Military. At the age of sixteen I will graduate from their training program, and will be sent out to do fieldwork. This will involve traveling around the remnants of the United States, looking for enclaves of humanity and trying to get them to rejoin society. In many cases they'll want to be left alone, but we won't have the resources to keep tabs on them all. Some will have to be eliminated, for the good of the country. For the good of the world. I'll be seventeen when I kill a man for the first time. I'll be one year older when I'm forced to kill a woman. I'll been lucky enough to avoid needing to kill a minor in the line of duty... at least until I leave the What Came After. At twenty two I will be put forwards for a job which will likely result in my death. Having gathered intelligence and information about the time leading up to the Conc Bombs and the Disaster, our finest investigators will notice something of a thread running through many of the developments in the world. They'll be hard to spot at first, like strands from one rug woven into many others, but eventually they will become easier and easier to find. A name will emerge, and then another. Several of them will repeat over and over again, but one will be there every time the destiny of the world is changed. The Dark Cowl. Their story will unravel like the very tapestry we will try to put together. We'll find that they were behind the scientific breakthroughs used for the Conc Bombs. We'll discover that they lurk like some kind of arcane secret society, forcing political appointments and manipulating the media. They'll seem to be the enemies of sanity, and we will find their works still happening now. The Dark Cowl will be the second strongest enclave in the What Came After. At least, we'll hope that it will only be second. This job, it will involve ten of our most highly trained and successful operatives. They will... we will go into the mission knowing that nine of the ten will die before they have a chance to do a thing. The way that the scientists will manipulated time and space, giving us the ability to send someone back... it'll come in the form of collapsing a very complex quantum wave function. It will have ten possible outcomes, but until the 'trigger' is fired, all can exist as reality. When the wave form collapses, one of the ten will be sent back, to before the What Came After. The others will die, their atoms simply burned out of existence. I'll keep my bearing. I'll stand up, clothed and ready, primed with the information I will need to change the past, and alter the What Came After. I'll look my fellow operatives in their eyes, and they will look back at me. We'll only have the one shot at it, and it will use up all of our energy reserves to rip the fabric of time apart. And just as I hear the countdown reach one, I will see something approaching a startled look from everyone else in the room, their bodies reacting to the change far too slowly. They won't have the time to react consciously. They'll be dead, be extinguished, and then I'll be thrown backwards, never to see any of them ever again. PAST Some of the members of The Dark Cowl have known that I was coming for them. How this could be possible I don't know. None have revealed to me any secrets, and I have to hope that the small alterations I have made to this reality can take heart from chaos theory, and work as a butterfly causing great storms in the way that the world pans out. I can never know, though. I can only cross off each objective on my list, and move on to the next one. Political aides and businessmen have been my victims this far, but now I have moved on to the media. More specifically, the world of professional wrestling. It seems so odd, so trivial a place to look for the greatest conspiracy in the history of man, but my orders are clear. There are many who have to be investigated. Some may prove to be innocent. In fact, all of them could be, but they must be tried. PRESENT So here I sit, waiting to be called forwards to take my first step on this objective. I need to become known in this world. It shouldn't be difficult, since my training stands me in fantastic stead for the kind of physical combat I am about to take part in. Anyone underestimating the small woman in the strange looking cat-suit will be struggling to walk back to their locker room afterwards. None of these men I face are on my list. Winters, French and Adams are nothing to me but the first step in the correct direction. There are other, larger fish to fry in the grand scheme of things. Names on our list read off as the great and the good of these circles. I must investigate the one called Violence Jack, and his ties to dark rituals and cults. There is a woman called 'Shadow' who participates in this world, and she too must be looked at. A man by the name of Clinton Sage has to be checked out, as does the improbably named Angelo Deville. All connections must be explored, and where necessary... eliminated. This is my work. This is my purpose. I do this so your future does not become What Came After.
