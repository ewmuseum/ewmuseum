We've Only Just Begun

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-15 Clank Syko slammed the door to his midnight black Nissan Skyline shut. He picked up his black duffel bag and slung it over his shoulder and looked straight ahead at Del Fuego Wrestling Gym. Syko took a quick glance at his watch, making sure he wasn’t too late. He wasn’t worried though. He had told Jacob, that middle-aged friend of his, to take care of the gym while he ran some errands. Errands in the form of a doctor’s visit that is. Syko was victorious in his first round match with Alex Caine and Angel. However, victory never comes without a price. Alex Caine was a bit of a disappointment for Syko. He had few moments where he showed much promise. Yet for the most part, his martial arts skill proved to be of no asset. Angel, on the other hand, was very impressive. Impressive in fact, that Syko was completely unprepared for her. It didn’t matter though. Syko found a way to win, like he always does. Angel put a scare on Syko, and perhaps on anyone who was foolish enough to bet on him. That match was over, and Syko came out victorious. He came out a bit fatigued and a visit to his private doctor put away any concerns that he might have injured his back once more. He didn’t. “Alright.” Syko muttered to himself as he opened the front mahogany colored double door to his gym. “Jacob got his money, there’s no more reason for me to fight.” As soon as Syko stepped inside his gym, he was bombarded by a flock of mean mugs and the silent treatment. Syko through his duffel bag against the wall, by the entrance, and looked around at twenty of his students all looking at him shaking his or her own head. “I know, I know.” Syko smiled. I tell you don’t be late, and here I am breaking my own rules. “Why didn’t you tell us?” Amanda, a blonde in her early twenties, asked Syko crossing her arms in front of her white sports tank top. Syko cleared his throat. “I’m sorry, but my personal business is really not up for discussion. If you must know, I had a medical appointment to attend to.” Syko started to make his way through the crowd of students. But none of them seemed eager to move. One student, a tall slim man in his late twenties, stepped forward. “That’s not what we are talking about here, Syko.” He said. Syko shook his head. “Okay, then what” Syko stopped for a moment not believing what he had heard. He turned to face the student who had just talked to him and gazed at him, and the rest of the students in curiosity. “What did you just call me?” “Syko” The kid said. Everyone of the other students each nodded his or her own head. The air was soon filled with whispers of “yes”, and “Syko”. The students began to mumble amongst themselves, while letting Amanda, and the other boy who dared to speak, take the initiative. Syko was at a lost for words. He didn’t want any of his students to realize, or find out who he was. Then again, he didn’t want anyone to realize who he was. He had enough of the spotlight. Believe him, it wasn’t as glorified as it was advertised to be. He made some money, but he lost his life. Not literally, but he lost friends and relationships, and without them you have no life. Syko sighed. “How did you guys find out?” he asked them hesitantly. The kid who referred to him as Syko stepped forward. “Michael said he had some tickets to a fighting tournament. A couple of us took his offer and we went to see it. He explained.’ Syko finished his thoughts for him. “And you saw me’ “Yeah” Amanda said. “How come you never told us? What’s wrong if you’re famous? People like famous people.” Syko turned away and the crowd of students finally made a gap for him to walk away. “Maybe I don’t want to be famous. None of you recognized me without my long hair. If it weren’t for my mistake of fighting again, none of you would have realized who I was.” Syko said as he walked past his students. The kid who dared to call him Syko, called after him. It’s not that bad man! You were great out there. But we all know, those fighters were a joke. I mean a guy who thought he was Jet Li, and a girl? Even I could have beaten them.” That caught Syko’s attention and he turned around with a smirk. “You think you can be successful in that tournament, Jack?” Jack nodded, and a few of the students agreed with him. “Yeah, I mean all those people were like cruiserweights. Anyone fit enough can beat them. I bet I could easily win a match or two with those guys. Oh and I’m not forgetting about the girl. How you let her run all over you, I wont understand.” Syko just nodded in agreement. He knew better. Jack was one of his better students. He was in great shape and was learning the ropes quickly. Yet, he still had no concept of what it was to be in a real match. “Jack, you are a fool if you think you can win a match in that tournament. Those fighters are better than any you have ever seen. They are better than anyone you have ever faced.” “If you think you have a chance at beating any of them, including me, then you are a damned fool that needs to go back to playing role playing games with his action figures.” Syko said as he walked away from the crowd. “Then again, sign ups are over now, so it’s too late to sign up. Maybe next time.” <<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Syko opened the door to Jacob’s office and let himself in. His abrupt arrival startled Jacob who seemed to be falling asleep on the job. Syko ignored that. “Jacob, I need you to take care of the Gym tomorrow.” Syko said standing halfway out the doorway.” Jacob cleared his throat and, in a frantic manner, tried to clear his desk clean. “Oh, sure no problem. You take care of your business and I’ll take care of the gym.” “Thank you.” Syko responded solemnly. He nodded in thanks and caught a glimpse of Jacob’s right hand. His right pink and ring finger were bandaged up heavily, all signs indicating to them being severed. Syko noticed Jacob trying to hide it and thought something was wrong. “What happened to your hand?” Jacob looked at his hand and chuckled a bit. He was acting rather strange, Syko thought. Then again Jacob had been acting strange all week. “Oh this?” Jacob asked rhetorically. “It’s really nothing. We just had a little accident with the grinder at home.” Syko closed the door shut and approached Jacob. “That doesn’t look like a little accident. Did you get it checked out?” Jacob nodded. “Thank you, Syko. You know most people would think I’d be looking for you, not you looking over someone of my age. I got it checked out, but the doctors said the fingers were too severed. So far now, I’m going to have stumps on my hand. It’s not bad though. I can still live normally.” Syko nodded. “I’m glad you’re okay. You be careful Jacob, don’t do anything stupid.” Jacob smiled a false smile. He wanted to make good on that promise, but he knew already that it was broken. Syko opened the door and proceeded to let himself out. “I hope the money you won for the cruiserweight tournament is enough?” Syko asked hoping to hear that he won’t have to fight for money again. Jacob stood up and laughed nervously. “Um…Syko….” He began to reply while rubbing his temple. A habit he does whenever he has something to say that he doesn’t want to say. “I didn’t tell you everything…” Syko slammed the door shut. “What is it now? Are you saying I have to fight…fight…whoever the hell it is I’m supposed to fight?” Syko shouted. Jacob shook his head. “No, I mean that I am completely bankrupt. I didn’t tell you before because I thought I could think of something in the mean time. I’m sorry Syko. I feel really bad asking you to do this for me. Just keep on…” “Heh…” Syko smirked. “I don’t get it. You could have said something to me earlier. You know damn well this was not what I wanted to do, and you still went ahead and threw me into it. You got your first round winnings. You want money from me? Work overtime. I’m not fighting in the tournament again.” Syko turned and walked out the door. Jacob tried calling after him, but his pleas fell on deaf ears. Jacob slammed the door shut and slumped back down on his chair. He shook his head, and pounded his left fist on the table. “Goddamn you, Syko” he muttered under his breath. He took a glance at his severed right hand. “Goddamn you…” Earlier that day Framed by grey apathetic walls, Jacobs stands in the middle of the damp room; his suitcase at his side. He sighs and breathes in slowly, trying to calm himself down. After a few moments of him being alone, the door at the far side of the room opens up and a group of four burly men walk in. Jacob takes a seat by a lone desk, as he is told and puts the suitcase on the table. “You have the money this time, old man?” One of the men asks, who obviously appears to be the leader of the group. Jacob nodded, too scared to say anything. He opened the suitcase up and rotated it, so that his new found friends could see the money inside. “One-One-One hundred thousand dollars, just as advertised.” Jacob said in a lowly voice. The leader of the bunch smiled and nodded. He grabbed some bundles of cash from the suitcase and flipped through it making sure it was legit. “It took you a while, old man.” He said. “But you eventually got it done.” Jacob smiled and nodded. “You made good on your word and paid back what you owed.” The leader continued. Jacob smiled and nodded. “For that, we should clear all debts in your name.” The leader suggested. Jacob smiled and nodded. “Unfortunately, we can’t do that…” The leader said with great glee. “You see, you wasted too much of my bosses time. You cost her money as well. You had a chance to make good and you blew it. This money here?” Jacob didn’t smile. The leader took the suitcase and through it down to the floor, causing all the money to erupt out of the suitcase like an active volcano. “This money means nothing. As far as our boss is concerned, you still owe us plenty. The price is now five hundred thousand dollars.” Jacob shook his head and quickly rose up from his feet. He was extremely frightened and it showed. “Why?! I owed her a hundred grand only, and I paid it! Why?!” The leader of the men shook his head and snapped his fingers. The other three men grabbed Jacob and bent him over the table. Jacob tried to fight back, but his old age body was to frail to fight against those burly men. One of the men on his right grabbed his right hand and laid it like a pork chop on the table. Grabbing a large sharp knife from his back pocket, the leader smirked. “You should have known better than to bet on our boss’s casino. You should have known better than to keep her waiting.” He remarked. “Now you have one week to get her the money. Here’s a little reminder not to be late again.” With that, he grabbed Jacob’s two right fingers and began to slowly severe them. Jacob screamed in pain and tried to free his hand frantically. However his screams were muffled, and Jacob soon lost his right pinky and ring finger. The burly men finally let go of Jacob and threw him onto the floor. The leader of the men stood over him, wiping his knife clean. “We’ve only just begun. That’s just small fries old man. Next time, your wife and daughter lose a limb.” Jacob shook his head, pleading with them. He didn’t want anything to happen to Maggie, his wife, or Amanda, his daughter. Yet, he knew unless he could get the money, there wasn’t anything he could do. Damn his gambling habit…damn it to hell. The leader had no remorse for Jacob’s pleading or his family. “There is something the boss wanted me to ask of you. You see, anything that goes with gambling she’s made aware of. Interestingly enough, she came about this very interesting bet on a certain tournament. The fighter who won the winnings was someone by the name of Syko Arsonist.” Jacob nodded in agreement. Now he feared that he had bought Syko into harm. “Don’t worry about him,” the leader continued. “He’s not going to get harmed, yet. The message is simple. Obviously you have some sort of connection with him. Keep him fighting, and the boss will lift up the debt. She apparently likes the kid. Don’t ask me why. But you keep him fighting and winning, and the debt is gone.” Jacob smiled from the floor. Finally a break, he thought. The leader motioned for the other guys to leave the room. “His next opponent will be someone named The Fool.” The leader said with a chuckle. “You better hope he wins, or u better learn how to write with your feet.” Having said that, the leader walked out the door with the suitcase, leaving Jacob to his severed hand. Do you like to play with toys, or something? You’re not ready. You won’t ever be ready for a long time.” Jack shook his head in denial. He felt he was ready. He was the best wrestler in Syko’s gym, and that success gave him an ego. “What are you talking about? Those wrestlers there were walking toothpicks. I could beat them. I mean you beat them. A washed up wrestler beat two fighters there. Now what does that say?” Syko sighed in disgust. “Trust me Jack. You are not ready for them. I could beat you.” Jack wasn’t going to let Syko shoot him down. “No, you’re just afraid that someone is going to surpass you and take the spotlight away from you. You know whom you’re supposed to be fighting next? A skinny kid by the name of The Fool! What kind of name is that? That guy is a walking joke.” Syko picked up his clipboard and walked away. He didn’t want to argue about fighting anymore. He didn’t care what Jack thought. He didn’t care who he was supposed to fight. “The Fool?” Syko muttered to himself. “He is right. What kind of man calls himself The Fool? A name like that could be misleading. On the other hand, it could fit him to a T. Whatever.” Congratulations Fool….you may just have won a bye to the third round… ….bastard…
