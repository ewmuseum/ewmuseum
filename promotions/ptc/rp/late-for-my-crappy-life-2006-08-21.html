Late For My Crappy Life

21 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-21 "You know what the worst feeling in the world is? Waking up late for work. Especially when it's a job you hate. When you wake up late for your crappy job, it's like you're late for you crappy life." - Dane Cook This is the exact feeling that ran through Bobino's head as his eyes snapped open, looking at the digital alrarm clock next to his bed. The red, blinking numbers read 3:17am, but they lied. Apparently, a local storm had caused a slight power surge, and made the power in Bobino's apartment to flicker, offsetting the honesty of his alram clock. Bobino jumped from bed, in the frantic way you only do when you -KNOW- you've woken up late. He looked for a shirt on teh ground as he searched for something that would tell him the true time. He was supposed to arrive at the arena at eight in the morning. This evening was one of the biggest moments in FUSE history: the Road To Glory tournament. The winner recieves a championship shot, but more importantly, the recognition of being better than the other participants. Bobino was supposed to be in that tournament. Rewind to two weeks prior, Bobino, whom had let the younger talent get wins over him to advance them into the tournament, was supposed to be repaid by winning a battle royal to earn the last vacant spot. Instead, in a last minute decision by FUSE management, a debuting star won the battle royal to earn her spot. Yes, -HER- spot. Tigera, the woman whom had -NEVER- stepped into a FUSE ring, was given the spot that belonged to the man that had dedicated his entire career to this comapny. Bobino had arranged to make an appearance at Road To Glory. By "arranged" I mean he was going to do it no matter what, and by "make an appearance" I mean he was going to invade the ring, with a microphone, and let the fans know how he was truly treated. He had every detail planned. He was going to slip inside the building at eight in the morning with the rest of the roster, help the participants warm-up as usual, then, he was going to watch the show intently, to mark where they left the live microphone. Every detail was perfect, he may lose his job, possibly even be arrested, but it was all worth it. He was tired oflaying down for people who hadn't earned his respect, he was tired of having his career spat on, and he was tired of not being recognized as the talent he truly was. He'd give up everything, just to set the story straight. As he searched the unorganized room, he finally found his cell phone, neglected and left in a pocket. I time looking back at him as his eyes widened in disbelief. ------------------------------------ "You never actually wake up late either. It's always like two mintues before you need to be there. Like, if you need to be at work at nine, it's always like 8:57. That's not even enough time to have an English muffin!" - Dane Cook ------------------------------------ The small display screen of the phone told the story. 7:59am. Bobino literally had one minute before he was late, and before his plans were ruined. He shook his head, and let out an audible groan, as he gathered his things. Bobino's trench coat was thrown over his shoulders as he prepared to walk out the door. He slid his phone out of his pocket another time, to check the time again. 8:02am. It takes bout half an hour to travel to the site of FUSE's Rise To Glory Pay-Per-View. Bobino began to slow down, realizing that no amount of rushing would get him to the event on time. He took a deep breath, as he walked out the door, his eyes watching his every step, as he made his way to the parking garage. Bobino pulled into the parking lot of the event, and saw no production truck. Maybe he wasn't the only one running late? He investigated further, noticing that everything looked like it was missing -SOMETHING-. He finally found the answer, in the form of a memo taped to the locker room door. The investers had pulled their money from the show, and it's television slot was revoked. FUSE's biggest event thus far, had just become a dark match. Bobino scoffed, realizing his efforts would be in vain, and turned to head back home. Bobino eventually talked himself into staying to watch the show, as he hadn't missed an event in over two years. FUSE torn the house down with a Pay-Per-Veiw worthy event, although only the thousands in attendance would ever know. ------------------------------------ "Biding My Time… Until I'm Strong Enough… To Fight Back…" - Lyrics From Mushroomhead's "Solitaire-Unraveling" ------------------------------------ Over the next few weeks, the future went from looking non-existant for FUSE-W to looking brighter than ever. Bobino had found the fortitude to control his outbursts for the good of the company. The last thing a near-death company needed right now was for it's longest employed member to talk about how horrible it was. Bobino had fallen back into his usual rut of losing to anybody management would tell him to, and making them all look good in the process. For alittle while, he had actually convinced himself that management planned on repaying him. Bobino had recieved some phone calls from his old friends and partners. Most of them wanted to know what was up with him, wanting to see him break his losing streak. Slowly, the pieces were falling into place. Slowly, a plan began to unwind in Bobino's mind. He just sat, and waited for the right moment to strike… or until he lost his cool, which ever came first. KillZone 05. Bobino lost his cool. His entire career, Bobino prided himself on one thing. He may hurt innocent people, he may hurt women if they've been assigned as his opponent, but he had -NEVER- hurt an innocent woman. After an embarassing loss to Jorge Samuelsson, Bobino wanted to have a quick on-air interveiw, mainly to try and start -ANYTHING- that could lead to a win. When he found the company's interveiwer, she blew him off, leading him to do something he's never done. As Dominique tried to walk away, he grabbed her arm, his hands clasping her forearm so hard it would be impossible for her to move. He went on to talk about how he was tired of being embarassed, and everything else I've told you earlier. The thing that scared Bobino as he re-watched the segment at home, was the look of pure terror on Dominique's face. She was honestly afraid of being hurt by Bobino, and that's a reaction he had never wanted to get from an innocent woman. Bobino came to the conclusion that the constant disresepct he had been the victim of was eating away at him. Every loss made him a bit less sane. Everytime he was forced to look inferior, his mind slipped a bit. Bobino's losing streak had irked his anger, and formed a weakness within him. Here's the part where you might need a bit of background to understand. Bobino is a Darwinist. In fact, he's been called "The Master of Darwinism". He believes all beings evolve, and that the only true key to success and strength is to eliminate a weakness. You could watch a different Bobino match everyday for a year, and never see the same match, because Bobino prided himself on chaning every match. This also means that he was very hard on himself when he found a weakness within himself. He'd do anything to eliminate that weakness, and that brings us back to the story. As Bobino watched the replay of KillZone, he saw a weakness. He looked into Dominique's eyes as she listened to him, and realized that he had lost all pride and respect. Not from others, he had never cared about what others thought. He never tried to impress fans, because he didn't care what they thought, for one reason. He always had respect for himself. Until now. For the first time in his career, when he watched himself on television, he saw through the fans eyes. He had no respect for "The New Shit",and that hurt him inside. At that very moment, he swore he'd regain his self-resepct. He wasn't gonig to let himself get mistreated anymore. He wasn't going to lose to people whohadn't earned their spot on the roster. He wasn't going to work management's way anymore. Bobino turned that video off, then deleted it from his TiVo. He didn't want to relive that day ever again. Bobino walked through the locker room with a renewed vigor before the next episode of FUSE's KillZone. He had just come from letting FUSE management know he refused to lay down in his match tonight. Obviously, they disagreed, but they eventually gave in, not wanting to lose anyone on the roster at the moment. Bobino made his way down to the hallway, wanting to get to wardrobe, making sure he looked good for tonight. He was all smiles, grinning at everyone as he walked through the halls, untill he came to one person. The grin drained from his face, and his confidence left, as he looked at her. She was dressed in her street clothes, and a purple bruise was visbile on her upper arm. The shame filled Bobino as Dominique realised he had entered theroom. Dominique blatantly avoided eye contact with Bobino, as Bobino did with her. He slowly approached her, as he began to do something that was a rarity for him. "Dominique… I… I just just needed to say something. I'm sorry. What I did was foolish, and I let my temper get to me. I'msorry it ever happened, and I promise it will -NEVER- happen again." Bobino stood, waiting for a response. He waited quite a while, as she never spoke a word. He finally nodded his head, and turned to leave. He choked back his shame as he began to walk away. "It's ok, Dominique. I wouldn't have forgiven me, either." Bobino walked towards the exit, his head hanging in dissapointement. He Was about to leave, when he was frozen to the spot, by two simple words. Dominique finally spoke, and her words confused him. "Thank You." He turned, and looked at her, making sure he heard correctly. He couldn't quite grasp why she chose to say that, of all things. "Thank you? For what?" She lifted her head, looking at him for the first time since the incident. "THank you for the appology. Thank you for explaining yourself. Thank you for giving everything you've given to help everyone keep their jobs, to help me keep my job. You've worked hard, and I can understand frustration. Nobody else respects you, because they look at the win-loss records, but as an announcer, all I ever see is effort. Thank you, Bobino." She cracked a grin, as she left the room. She left no opportunity for a response. She walked away, as Bobino processed her comments. She'd never hear it, but as she walked away, Bobino responded, barely whispering to himself. "No… thank you…" She had no idea how much her words meant to him on that night.
