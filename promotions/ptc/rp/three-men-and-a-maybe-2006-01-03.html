Three Men and a Maybe

3 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-03 With its recent Birthday Bash Pay Per View bringing 2005 to a close, the World Wrestling Alliance celebrated its ninth year of operation. The show was another success, the event was sold out and merchandise sales hit record levels. But as the amped up crowd slowly trickled out of the arena, nary a thought was spared for the technical staff and road crew who worked long into the night. Backstage, a thick curtain, as black as the dead of night was being hung from the rafters by three members of the dedicated road crew. All shared the same physical attributes, being vertically and folically challenged and well compensated for horizontally. They all had faces for radio, and a dedication that belied the minimum wage that they were being paid. "That should do it!" exclaimed one of the men, wiping the dust off his hands. "Recording equipment set up?" enquired another, before guzzling a can of cola. "Yep" replied the third, wiping his nose with his hefty forearm. The three then stood there for what seemed like an eternity, looking around backstage. "So where is he?" +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Earlier that night, former World Wrestling Alliance World Champion and PTC Global Title also-ran Mal Somers stood backstage, just behind the curtained entrance to the ramp, an area sometimes referred to as the 'Gorilla Position'. It afforded him an opportunity to watch both the goings on in the ring as well as the reaction of the fans in attendance, and he was having an enjoyable time, munching on a McDonalds Happy Meal as he observed. It had been a year of highs and lows for Somers, he had been reunited with his long lost parents earlier in the year, and had challenged his mortal enemy and PTC favorite Dave Harley for the World Title. In that match he was injured and put out of wrestling for almost six months. During that time he would quite often sit in this position and long to be out there in the squared circle just one more time. He got that opportunity in a rematch against Harley… and was beaten once again. Life's a bitch sometimes…then you wrestle one. Somers winced as he looked on and saw another high risk move being performed. He looked to his sidekick, his beloved retired Title belt called "Rookie" who lay draped over his left shoulder and shook his head. Rookie didn't say a word. Mal then turned his attention back to the arena, and the crowd in attendance. He focused on one man in particular, obviously the patriarch of the family. The man's wife sat there talking to the daughter, and their young son seemed to be the only one who was engrossed in the match. The father stood there, holding a video camera, taping the events, as he looked aimlessly around the stadium. "Dad! Did you see that powerbomb! Tracer Bullet just slammed that guys head almost through the ring!" The father shrugged, looking up into the bleachers. "Nah, I'll watch it later on tape." And the match went on. As it did so and Mal looked around the arena some more, something dawned upon him. This kind of activity seemed to happen more and more, and it did not sit well with the founder of MALcoholics Anonymous. That man is right here, watching this match happen right now, but he isn't even experiencing it! Somers thought to himself.He is so busy recording it so he can relive it later…that he isn't even living it now! As that dawned upon him, a wry grin crossed his face. Mal realised that he was sick of watching GTT5 from the sidelines, sick of waiting to experience things later. This time, in Round 3, he was ready to experience a match against Arch Angel as soon as he could. And with that, Somers threw the rest of the Happy Meal in the waste basket, span on his heels, and patted Rookie on his head as he departed the arena. "C'mon Rookie, I have a match to wrestle!" +++++++++++++++++ Backstage, the three members of road crew slowly dismantled the rigging that held up the black curtained backdrop. "How ironic" said one of the men, as he scratched his ample behind. "I know," began another "Mal's first two opponents didn't front, now he has the chance and he doesn't show up!" The curtain fell to the ground leaving the third man to pick it up. "The kids of today…no respect! No respect at all!"
