IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

8 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
8 July 2007 Tony Gamble - PRIME [IG:33] You know, I was going to stay out of this. I figured, there's already plenty of people coming out and talking absolute nonsense that I shouldn't even bother. I mean seriously, what's the point in competing against bottom of the barrel - let me get in on this open challenge because I'll never earn one of these shots on my talent alone - pricks that think they have what it takes to face off against Jason Snow. I saw no point in taking time away from my busy schedule of sitting in front of the television, picking my toe nails, and smelling my fingertips. Then I had the unfortunate accident of coming across Bobino's pathetic attempts to trash talk. Seriously, for someone that claims no one cares about PRIME, you sure do have quite the hard on for the place and it's roster members. What's the matter, your application to join get turned down because you just didn't have the talent needed to be anything more than a dark match contributor? I know, you'll probably say that you would never waste your time applying to some federation full of has beens and overhyped, no talent, bastards that sit around in a circle jerk for no other reason than they can. Or maybe you feel that we couldn't handle the extreme amount of talent that has brought you the many accolades of entry level titles that you have won over the years. Yeah, I wouldn't be proud to brag about only being able to win the lowest tiered title in a federation I was in for ten years either. Whatever the case may be, Bobino, it seems to me that you have a grudge against the members of the PRIME roster, and quite frankly I think it has to do with the fact that every time you've had to face someone from before mentioned roster, you've come up short. Oh, and please, tell me again how beating Axel Action to get to the second round of GTT6 somehow makes you better than a man like Tchu, who was defeated by one of the finalist; Violence Jack? Your damn arguments have more holes than swiss fucking cheese, Bobino. So why don't you do us all a favor and quit whining like a twelve year old girl that the big boys didn't allow to play football with them because they knew the first time she got hit she'd run home crying foul, and tuck your fucking tail between your legs and run away like you do everytime a real fucking challenge is in front of you. And yes, bitch, I said a real challenge, because as good as you think you are, you're still miles fucking behind if you expect to catch up to anyone in PRIME. I'd say have a fucked up day, but shit, that's just another day in your life. ---- 8 July 2007 I'm sorry, did I give you permission to address me? I mean, I know it's in your contract somewhere to talk about every single person in the match, when you're in this sort of match... But did I ever give you any sort of idea that I even cared you were here? I mean hell, the empty screen of darkness that you're staring at should have given you the hint that I didn't want to waste my time in front of a camera, and yet you still have to find some way to fit me into your conversation and pathetic attempt at sounding big and tough. News flash, Neanderthal, I could care less about your stupid little competition between little yellow bus riders. And no, it was not a yellow SUV. I think your parents just told you that to keep you from hurting yourself trying to figure out the truth. Truth be told, Monsoon, I'm not here to rain on your little Special Olympics parade. I'm here because some guy that's been calling himself "The New Shit" for the last few years has yet to realize that the shit is actually very old, and very tired. I mean, when you can't even get a gig in a place like FUSE, something has got to be wrong. Then again, it must hurt to watch new guys like Covel, Sage, Raynes, Rhine, and Cruise just waltz in and climb right up past you on that ladder you've been keeping steady for so many years. No, Monsoon, I'm not here to steal your thunder. I'm just here to make sure that the man screaming for originality, while being as redundant and pointless as James von Doom, realizes that it's hard to bring up new points against a man that keeps doing the same damn thing over and over again... Which actually amounts to absolutely nothing worth discussing. So, Big Bear, you and the other helmet wearing chumps can go back to spinning yourself on the merry-go-round until you spew up more utter nonsense not worth mentioning, and I'll be content to send in these audio clips that take me all of about ten minutes that I could have spent fondling myself uncontrollably. Ten minutes that I'll never get back. Shit, thanks a lot. ---- 9 July 2007 Tony Gamble - PRIME[IG:33] You know what I find really funny, Bob? The fact that you hit the nail right on the tip of the head when you said I babbled on and on about nothing anyone really cared about. Considering all I did was talk about you, I guess you'd have to say that it was fitting. You see, while you sit there and claim to have been sitting there waiting for a response, I've been sitting here waiting for you to show up. What's the matter, did you miss the bus? You want me to talk about something original, but you still say the same tired ass shit you've been saying all day. You know what Bobino, no one has been hiding from you, you just don't have the courage to wipe away the shit that's been covering your eyes so you can see everyone right here waiting for you to finally show the hell up. You're a joke, man. And to be honest, not even a funny one. You're like that little kid that always needs to be at the center of attention. You don't really deserve it, but you think if you raise your voice and annoy the shit out of everyone, eventually someone will pat you atop your head and say 'Good Job.' Well good job, Bob. You've managed to get more attention than you'd ever actually deserve. People are talking about you left and right, saying your name and praising it like the second coming of the XFL. Though, I actually have to give you a round of applause for actually managing to get here in the first place. I know it must have been hard for you to actually show up for anything. Oh wait, there I go being unoriginal again. I tell you what, how about you actually go find a small bit of credibility, do something, anything, worth mentioning, then come back and play with us, because right now all I hear from you is: "Come on and get me, try your best to prove you can find something that I have done that I don't deny. I suck, I know. I got fired and accomplished nothing in my ten year career, I know. I will never amount to anything more than what you would scrape off the bottom of a shoe, I know." Bob, it's kind of hard for us to prove you're a failure... If you've already admitted to it.
