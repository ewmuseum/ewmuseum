The Day Of The Underdog

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 The Day Of The Underdog “Sometimes in life Butler, an opportunity comes along. I learnt that in the business world. As you make the way up in whatever area you find yourself colliding with someone better than you. Someone who everyone predicts will push you aside because they have done that to others so many times. And when it comes down to it, no one expects you to fight. They expect you to back down, to give up, to accept defeat. It is what happens at this point in time that will change your course forever, for good or for worse.” A very different master than normal informed me of this as we dined together in the banquet room. Physically there was a very noticeable difference, his normally straight hair was wild, even his custom made suits which are normally worn with delicate care seemed as if they were merely thrown on. More disturbingly there was a significant mental change, most would not have seen it, but I could. I know my master inside out, perhaps more than I should, but I have never seen his eyes look so determined. It worried me that any individual facing the legend that is Inphino Blitz would be so certain of himself, so confident. “As you know Butler, my business is not exactly normal. You know of the deals I have made, of the strange artifacts that I have sold and of the tricks I have pulled over my competition. But what you do not know, what in fact no one else but me knows, is how I reached that level. I wish my opposition were still around to tell the story but unfortunately they vanished from sight soon after they dared me to step up to their level.” I finished my last square of pheasant and pushed my plate away, signalling that my master had my full attention. My master took one last sip of red liquid from his wine glass and swished it around, moistening his mouth so the words would merely flow out, this made it apparent that the tale would be a rather interesting one. “In my line of business there are very few contenders for the top spot, normally there is just one with the rest either being fakes or perhaps companies that have unfortunately been branded fakes. Mine when I first started was of course the second. Though I was fortunate enough to gain a few loyal customers who were impressed at the quality of items they could purchase many thought I was another person who had simply stumbled into the line of business and sold fakes. At the time there was one huge company, led by a man who was infamous at his trade, an explorer named John McWayne. Mr. McWayne would travel world wide searching for items that did not belong to him and stealing them. Through mysterious ways that no one can be sure of he managed to find huge amounts of information about the stolen goods. He was the most trusted man to buy from.” Suddenly something clicked in my mind. It was the name of John McWayne that had caused me to stir, while I was not sure how the name seemed somewhat familiar. My master seemed to recognize this as he gazed in my direction and nodded, “I imagine the name John McWayne would be familiar to you, a while back he reached front page news.” I nodded my head, somewhat relieved my master put me out of my misery. I always kept a name, my father taught me that it’s best to remember these people in case you have to deal with them. Memory aside, my mind refocused on the tale of my master’s past. “The national newspapers stumbled upon the biggest scandal of all time. Or at least they thought so at first, but they never really discovered the whole truth. Apparently a news reporter had been handed contact details to reach McWayne from a mysterious person. Nobody knows who of course, though there are many speculations regarding that for those that still remember the scandal. The news reporter figured from the information that he had that Mr. McWayne was apparently insane and believed in witchcraft. The next day after the supposed scandal was first revealed the plot thickened. It was then they discovered that McWayne had supposedly escaped from a prison for the criminally insane and was attempting to start his own cult which included human sacrifices. Suffice to say the news was huge. Then a mere matter of days later the whole incident was forgotten about.” I nodded my head, enthralled in my masters tale. But then a thought popped into my head. “Surely after that your line of business should have been exposed?” I asked confused, unable to believe that it would have all been blown aside. “Many feared that, even I did at first. But the truth was many were disturbed and deeply concerned. As far as they were concerned, they did not want to know. Though some foolish people did get involved, they supposedly had their eyes open by the news discovery. In reality it was more of an expression. A cult like following rapidly grew. The best ever time for the industry, even some of the smaller companies rose though they were soon pushed aside. McWayne Inc. was the top of the pile, and always would be according to the majority. And that is what makes it even more amusing. When you are at the top, when you have proven yourself to be the best, you make a mistake. You get confident, and with confidence comes the feeling that you can beat anyone. And then along comes a small time businessman with a company many ignore, and you try to take them out of business. And at first it works, everyone is on your side and you use that to your advantage. Or at least McWayne did. His first shots shook my entire company, I nearly pulled out and stopped right there…” My master paused for a few seconds, perhaps remembering the time very well. I summed up the courage to bring him back to the present. “But, you didn’t?” “No, though I came close. Something happened at that point, something which perhaps will shock you. The voice from so long ago, the voice that made me turn against my family…it returned.” I stared, gaping at my master. Unable to believe the words that had been uttered, this was the first time I had heard of the voice returning. That evil voice which he describes as elegant and beautiful that led him to turning against his own family! The voice that led him to burning down his life up to that point! “This time the voice was singing a slightly different tune. It seemed to be looking down at me in disapproval, unable to believe that I was contemplating quitting. It urged me to rise to the challenge, it filled my heart with confidence and made me courageously fight back. I did. Imagine a wounded deer fighting against a powerful lion, that is the equivalent to what happened. Somehow, someway I overcame the odds. Somehow, some way I had beaten McWayne Inc.” I nodded my head though my thoughts were still on how the voice returned. It worried me, if that voice returned then, surely it could return once again… “Do you have any tales like that Butler? Any tales from when the odds look down at you? Any tales of you fighting back courageously?” The questions broke my concerns instantly, they echoed through my mind and then it came back to me. Something that I had left in my past… “Hey kid, what the hell are you doing?” I gazed up, staring down at me was him, the bully with no name. I scampered to my feet, using the support of a helpful boy who was amongst the now gathered crowd. I foolishly offered HIM my hand, he looked at it for a few seconds before slapping it away. Before I could react he grabbed me by the collar and pulled me nearer. All I could see was the look of insanity in his eyes as his stinking breath swept into my skin, sending shivers down my spine. Somehow I managed to utter out a small prayer, knowing my time was almost over. I begged for the answer to what I should do. Then suddenly and rather coincidently a chant rose up from the gathered crowd of students who seemed to decide they had been bullied around one too many times. Each one of them began pumping their arms in the air and yelling “fight” at the top of their lungs. The bully chuckled to himself before shoving me to the ground. As I gazed up, scared out of my wits, the excited faces stared at me, willing me to get revenge for them as well. None of them knew me for I kept mostly to myself but here they were, believing in me. That excited me, urged me on and even made me believe in myself. Recreating a move I had seen my favourite wrestlers do I nipped to my feet. The small crowd cheered as I stepped up to the bully. Anger and rage pumping through my veins. We stared in each others eyes for what seemed an eternity, he dared me to make the first punch but I was paralysed with fear. I saw his eyes flicker but I merely flinched as he struck out with a true right hand which not knocked me off my feet. Everything seemed to stop as I lay there, my face burning. He stood over me, chuckling at how easily I had fallen. Everyone in the gathered crowd seemed heart broken yet they still held hope for me. They hoped that I would leap to my feet and bravely knock the bull down but I couldn’t, nay, I wouldn’t. I walked home that school day with my head down and tears rolling into the small puddle of blood that came from my broken nose. I admitted my defeat, not because of how I was a physical wreck but because mentally I gave up. I believed there was no chance that I could fight the intimidating monster. I gave up to his reputation, not to his brutality. “So Butler, any tales you wish to tell?” The master stared at me, like a parent who knows their child is keeping away something. Like a child I trembled, then I froze, like I did that day in the fight against the bully. Eventually I gulped and came back to life. “No master, nothing of the sort has happened to me. Now if you do not mind I must return to my activities,” I said, already making my way to the door. The master glanced at the plates but then seemed to read my mind and knew I wouldn’t clean them anyway. He nodded his head and waved me on. I left the room and shut the door behind me, why back then did I give up? That is perhaps the worst decision I have ever made, to give up. No matter what the odds were I should have fought against them, tried to beat them. Instead I surrendered. No, forget it. There is no use in reliving the past when I have a duty to do. In I go again to collect those plates. “It is time.” Wait, where did that voice come from? It is a female’s voice yet there are none currently in the mansion, unless…no wait, my master is in the dining room which is quite a way from the television in the lounge. It’s odd, the voice seems so familiar, as if I have heard the beauty, the elegance before. “I am not so convinced, perhaps this is as far as I am meant to go.” My master’s voice this time, it sounds as if he’s chatting to someone, but who? Dare I enter the dining room? It is not my place but then that voice is drawing me, it seems to have grabbed me by the throat and is pulling me towards it. The dining room door is left slightly ajar…perhaps I should take a peek. Standing there in the middle of the room opposite my master is a tall woman, she is clothed in nothing but a cloak, the colour of pure white. Long golden locks flow from her hair down to her shoulders, the skin which is revealed is a pale white colour. She offers out her hand and reluctantly my master clasps it. “I understand, the odds are stacked against you. No one believes you have a chance, no one would bet against the legend that is Blitz. But then no one would have bet against McWayne Inc. Always fight, never surrender. Perhaps the day you face Blitz will go down in history as the Day Of The Underdog.” For once my master looks uncertain and somewhat nervous of the female’s presence. Seconds pass and then finally my master nods his head as if accepting what this mysterious woman has to say. “But…how?” The tension increases, the woman steps closer to Mr. Darkness and he seems frozen on the spot as if willing to move away but unable to. Suddenly the woman thrusts her head back and she laughs. “How? Did you not ask me the same question when I revealed to you that your friends and family were traitors?” My master seems transfixed by her but then again, surely he remembers what this woman made him do. She made him betray his family and now she is using that to tempt him into whatever evil intention she has in mind now. I clear my throat trying to attract the attention of both, I receive none from my master but the woman does turn to face me… The hideous evil stares at me, daring me to move. The beautiful woman has transformed into a devilish creature that looks to be from the depths of hell. It is a skeleton, levitating in the air with a blue glow surrounding it. It cackles at me and then suddenly points in my direction. Suddenly I feel a force crash into my ribcage, I am sent flying into the wall of the hallway. Suddenly a wind destroys the dining room, opening the door further so despite my dizzy state I can still see the events which are taking place. The woman has returned her attention to my master. “How will you defeat the odds? The simple facts of life. The greats like Blitz may dominate those weaker but the evil dominate the greats. That is the path you must follow like you did so long ago. Listen to me, the only way to beat Blitz is through…EVIL!” The woman suddenly cackles, the hideous sound echoes around both the dining room and the hall. And then suddenly she vanishes, and with her goes all the mess and chaos. The pictures which were flung off the walls by the wind are replaced, the vases that were shattered are now back in their place. Everything is as it was. Except for my master. In his place is…is…evil personified.
