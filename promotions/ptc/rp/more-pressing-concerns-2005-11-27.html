More Pressing Concerns

Date: 2005-11-27 The screen flickers brightly, fading in and out static. Slowly, black begins to overtake the white, and the contrast of the shot begins to adjust, revealing the fuzzy outlines of uncertain surroundings. A street light cuts on, and a pale, but tall figure walks underneath it, squinting slightly at the sudden illumination. As he walks past, flickering neon signs and flashing lights can be seen above the tops of the apartment flats in front of him. A small block of white text appears at the bottom of the screen, resembling a subtitle in a foreign film. A few months ago, another stranger became a part of Global Wrestling, something not uncommon… He stops to look at the visual onslaught that is downtown Las Vegas for a few moments, before continuing, a sudden gust of wind blasting in from the desert catching his long, black hair, and pulling it behind him. His impact has been undeniable. A quick shift of perspective shows him dropping Danielle Northsky to the mat with his patented move, the Cocytus Drop. But Virgil Ambrosi remains a mystery. He holds the Platinum Championship belt in his hands after winning his match against Northsky, looking at it curiously. His motivations remain unclear. Back in Las Vegas, he slowly climbs the steps outside the Global Arena, glancing up at the glass doors, and smirking. There are even rumours that he is something not of this world, a being of legend. A shot from his second match with Danielle Northsky plays now, with a slightly more tired Virgil going through the same motion, his Cocytus Drop serving him well once again. Once more, he becomes the Platinum Champion. Who is he? What ends does he pursue? A quick succession of shots shows him finishing off several of his opponents with the same move shown twice previously. His opponents should concern themselves with other matters. Once again in the warm, dry city, he walks away from the camera, toward the arena. Such as how they plan on tarnishing his impressive record, which he looks to improve further at GTT5… They may need more than a week. The Global Wrestling logo flashes into view, before the shot once again descends into static.
