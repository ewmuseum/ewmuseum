Dave Hurst versus King Blueberry

Destiny Has Brown Eyes (IG50 Promo #4 of 5) Destiny Has Brown Eyes I can’t let him down. Lying there with the covers over his back is Ducky. He looks so peaceful, the greatest friend anyone could have. How could I be so selfish? This contest isn’t about me, it’s about him. He’s kept with me for these years not because he’s got nothing else to do, but because he believes in me. He knows that I’m capable of achieving so much more than I have in life. He can be content sitting and watching documentaries on The Discovery Channel, but he knows there’s something better planned for me. I sometimes feel that he knows of my destiny. Deep down inside, he knows that some day I’ll get my just rewards. I’ve been doing this for twenty years now. I was a wrestler from the age of sixteen. I would lie about my age so that I could get into wrestling events. I’ve been in a GTT final, I’ve been in world title fights, but have nothing to show for it. I’ve been second-best in everything I’ve ever done. But I know that someday, I’ll do just enough to get a title. Maybe it’s this time? -----=====I=====----- I can’t stand sitting around this hotel room much longer. Ducky is sleeping, but I can’t sleep a wink. I’m too anxious about this fight, but not in the same way I was two days ago. I need to know. Is this my destiny? Am I here for a reason? I leave the hotel room and head down to another one of the bars they have here at the hotel. It’s a little smokey, but it’s a quiet atmosphere, it’ll help me relax. I sit down at a table and wait to be served. The bar is somewhat empty, but at this time there are only a few stragglers left who’ve had one too many. I hear a big noise to my right. A group of men walk in to the bar. I assume that they’re tourists, but you can never really tell. They’ve obviously been out drinking all night and are making their last stop for the night. I doubt many of them will be served. There is a loud smash across the room and I jump. The group have broken a glass that was lying on the table. I’m a bit on edge, due in part to a lack of sleep. One of the waitresses moves over to the tourists to ask them to leave. Is that… her? One of the tourists gets up and grabs her firmly by the wrist. That’s it. She shrieks at the man for grabbing her, and the guy grunts with fake machismo, I walk over to them and call. “I think you need to let go of her, mate. Don’t you?” The guy’s smiles and grunting stop, the group turn to look at me. “This doesn’t concern you, pal. Mind your own business,” he says. What a courageous statement that is. These guys have seriously pissed me off. Not only have they spoiled my quiet drink to gather my thoughts, they’ve decided to finish their night on the tiles by harassing a waitress. These kinds of people are the scum of the Earth. When the guy doesn’t let go of her wrist, I look him in the eyes with a stern, unwaivering stare. I’m about ready to take this guy’s head off. “I don’t think you heard me. I said, let go of her.” “Alright, alright, take it easy, man. I was just having some fun, okay?” He lets go of her wrist and she immediately shuffles away from them. The group then decide that they aught to make their move out of the bar, and get up and leave. I sit back down at my table. As I wait to be served, or possibly get ousted from the building by the manager for causing a raucus, I hear her voice. “Don’t I know you from somewhere?”
