"Wolfie, that’s absurd-" "Don’t you dare fucking call me Wolfie," Wolfgang warned her. "You don’t have that right. You can’t deny to me that you and Abigail never spoke either. She came to you. When she found out she couldn’t have any children, she saw you. And I want you tell me what the fuck you did." Karla let out a belly busting laugh. Wolfgang was infuriated. He jerked Karla, in all her naked glory, to her feet, pulling her up by the arm without any concern for whether or not he was hurting her. "You’re going to tell me why she went to see you." Karla looked Wolfgang straight in the eyes with a smirk. She was getting turned on more and more by his anger. "You’re not ready for the truth." Wolfgang grasped Karla’s fragile neck with a firm chokehold. "Try me." Karla smiled before Wolfgang tightened his grip. The grip became more and more suffocating until Karla started to believe that Wolfgang would really kill her. She tapped Wolfgang’s arm frantically until he let go. Gasping for air she explained, "Yes, your wife did come to see me." "Why?" "She wanted a second opinion on whether or not she could have children." "What did you tell her?" "I told her Dr. Erin Haskell was right." "Bullshit!" Wolfgang cried out, "Angela’s living proof of that!" "No, it isn’t," Karla explained sternly. "It’s the truth. Abigail couldn’t have any children of her own. But then I explained to her that there was a cure. I told her there was a way she could have a child of her own, if she was willing to accept the circumstances." Wolfgang stood aghast, "A miracle of science…" "Damn right," Karla said. "Your precious little Angela is here because of a miracle of science. If it wasn’t for me, she wouldn’t be here. I’m her mother, Wolfgang." Wolfgang shook his head, not in denial, but affirmation, "No, you’re not. Abigail is her real mother." Karla snarled at Wolfgang. It was the first time in many years they had known each other that Wolfgang ever saw Karla at her most angry. "If you don’t tell Angela the truth about her mother, then I will."
