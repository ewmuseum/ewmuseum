IG45 Trash Talk Open Invitation thread (all vs Chris Storm)

31 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
31 May 2008 God, I'm sorry. Because, you know what? I'll own up to this. This is my own fault. It's my fault because three plus years ago, I could not stop Chris Storm from pinning AWS Man (also known as Retired), thus giving Chris Storm the PTC Global Title and reinforcing the notion in his head that he actually matters. But let's see what's happened since then, eh? I won multiple world titles. Had my very own Infinite Gauntlet round named after me, which I won. Had not one, but TWO GTTs premised on my elimination. Made it to the semi-finals of GTT5. And what has Storm done? He's won the IG title and been tag team champions for a year. Whoopie. He's GCW tag champions. I didn't know we had tag belts, and I WORK for GCW! Congratulations, you beat Kimbusa in a handicap match. I didn't need a partner to beat him. We've been in the UWF together, Chris. We've been in GCW together. And yet, I know damn well every time Lew Spectre or Steven Caldera bandied about the idea of Chris Storm vs. Seymour Almasy II you most likely crapped yourself. Because the fact remains simple all these years later: one on one, you stand no chance against me. You never have. You never will. And for a man who proclaims his hatred of me so much...isn't it odd that you've never stepped up to the plate? Never took so much as a swing at me? You're a coward. But hell, you've got a built in out. Two challengers for your title next round. That way, when I pin you, you can blame the other person in the match. You're very good at excuses. But don't worry, Chris. After I take your title, you'll inherit a new one. Second most hated PTC Infinite Gauntlet champion. ---- 2 June 2008 Everyone, everyone, everyone. Stop fighting! You're all missing the POINT! The point of this is to mock Chris Storm for his entirely overinflated sense of self-importance. Fact of the matter is, other than Chris Storm, I don't *care* about any of you, not right now at least. With that said, Chris...let's be honest. You know you want to knock your teeth down my throat. You've wanted to do it since 2003. And yes, maybe we met at a UWF show. Sorry if I forgot, but I was busy winning World Championships while working for my old home out of the goodness of my heart. More important things on my mind back then, such as temporarily performing Charm on your fiancee, but Hell, I'll leave her out of this, because it seems mocking Tessa is the best most of the Rabites around here can do. Your GCW career's a joke. A year long title reign in a division with no talent. You're facing LeStatt Knight in a handicap match at High Stakes. A HANDICAP MATCH, against a man who has NEVER BEATEN ME! Chris, this is your chance. Your chance to prove that it all hasn't been a fluke. You beat me, and it doesn't MATTER what comes out of my mouth. We've known each other for five years. I've hated you, you've hated me. So when PTC staff comes to you and asks you for two names, pick anyone you want for the first. I don't especially care. But for the second, Chris...write down Seymour Almasy. You know you want to. ---- 2 June 2008 Jealous, much? Demean my title reign as much as you want, but the fact remains that like everyone else in what remains of Primetime Central, you followed me out of the place when you went to Legacy of Champions in some effort to gain even one-FIFTH of the fame that I have in this sport. In All-Star Championship Wrestling, I beat Max Danger. Violence Jack. Brandon Youngblood. Coral Avalon. Alias. Khristain Keller. Names that you, to be frank, would piss yourself at hearing announced against you. And best I can tell, the goal of being champion is to hold onto the title...and until ACW's last night, I did exactly that. Far as I'm concerned, that's a success. You can stew in your own juices all you want, and yet, at the end of the day you STILL hate me for my success. You wonder why you're not the one with multiple Global Title reigns, and the name recognition that I have every time I step into the ring. Since you insist on bringing up Tessa...does it matter? *shrugs* Maybe I slept with her. Maybe I didn't. The fact of the matter is simply that, with proper education, I had her pointed at you like a nuclear warhead. Seems familiar to me, because it occurs to me that one LeStatt Knight is probably doing the same thing as we speak. I will agree with you on one thing, I do enjoy the thought of Tessa and Dawn together. That may, of course, be the ONLY thing we ever agree on, but small steps. Let me underline this for you, Chris, so that if I decide not to say one more word to you, you'll know the score. You don't pick me...and everyone's going to wonder why. "Man, that Storm guy hates Seymour, but he was too much of a pussy to fight him." That's what the world's going to be saying, Chris. Because amongst the Charlotte Ramones, the James von Dooms, and the Jerichoholics Anonymouses of the world...there is Seymour Almasy. And when I'm done with you, Chris, you will *wish* the Dead Man's Hand ended your career. ---- 2 June 2008 *I'M* not listening to what's coming out of my mouth? The man who's spent the last YEAR OF HIS LIFE doing nothing but comedic segments backstage is trying to be hard? THAT is the only comedic thing going on, Storm, I assure you of that. You can laugh at me all you want. Make all the jokes you care to make. But the fact remains that I'm better than you. I've done more than you. More people know who I am than you. You can try to cover this up by claiming that my accomplishments "don't mean shit" to you, but they mean shit to anyone with a brain who's not trying to puff himself into King Shit by citing his own meager accomplishments in this sport. You took my Global title by pinning a retard. You then immediately lost it to Jesse Jamester after I'd successfully defended it twice, setting the title into a tailspin that didn't recover prestige until, surprise, I won it again. JESSE FUCKING JAMESTER, Chris. Youngblood's off, by his own admission, sipping cocktails on the beach, enjoying his retirement, so obviously he didn't quite get the job done. There's a lot you don't understand. I refuse to go away. All that people like yourself do is simply inspire me to stick around longer, make more money, win more championships, and annoy people. Oh, before I go -- mocking me for laughing at the fact that I cry because my wife died? Classy, Chris. You want the honest truth? I probably cry about it twice a week, at night, when I'm lying in bed and realize that I'd trade it all to have her back. You want to make fun of that, Chris? Tessa drops dead tomorrow, you're telling me you WOULDN'T cry? If the answer is that you would cry, you're a hypocrite. If the answer is no, you're something far, far worse.
