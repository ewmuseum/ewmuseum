School and such...

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This contender’s match for the Extreme Title isn’t about some worthless fool like Fathom crying about being Belmont’s bitch, or Chris West blubbering about being picked on as a child! Hell, it’s not even about that idiot Ice complaining because he’s not good enough to be noticed. This match is only about one thing and one thing only...”Mr. Main Event” Mike Marvelous cleansing this division of all it’s dead weight and then going on to the CWMC to save the Extreme Title from that chicken chested queer Tristan Belmont! Yes, this match is just the beginning of me saving this division and making that Extreme Title prestigious once again! Quite frankly, I won’t blame any of you if you decide not to show up for the match...I know facing the hottest star in 4WW history can make people rather nervous and cause them to quit right at the beginning. ***** Boarding schools, when mentioned people always think of some preparatory institute that is perhaps in Great Britain. They think of schools that are either specifically for just boys or just girls, places where the students have to dress in their “Sunday’s best” each and every single day. The students in these schools are always the children of European royalty or rich American businessmen; the only “commoners” allowed within the walls of such an institute are the janitors who clean the hallowed halls on a daily basis. When one pictures such a place in their mind they might see a tall structure resembling a castle that is perched high upon a hilltop, perhaps located somewhere in the English countryside. Our story takes place in a location not much unlike the one that is described above; it is a boarding school known as The Brookfield Preparatory Academy for Boys. Brookfield is located within the heart of England’s countryside, resting upon a tall tree lined hill with the sounds of the country peacefully playing in the background. This story isn’t about Brookfield though, it just happens to take place at the centuries old school. The story is about a young man from Beverly Hills, California that attended Brookfield...his name is Michael Biddle but you all know him better as “Mr. Main Event” Mike Marvelous. The year is 1993 and Michael Biddle is 15 years of age, he has been at the Academy since he was 12...just a few months after his parents died in a mysterious skiing accident in the Swiss Alps. Most children become introverted and withdrawn upon the death of their parents, but the exact opposite happened with Michael; he became arrogant and opinionated. The counselors at Brookfield theorize that Michael became this way due to his parents never having faith in him and that his behavior is just his way of proving them both wrong once and for all. The time is four o’clock in the afternoon on a crisp fall day, the Academies classes have let out for the day and the students are at this time either studying for their classes the next day. Or they are practicing for one of the various sport teams that are sponsored by the school. A few even practice playing classical instruments for the schools orchestra. Michael Biddle is doing neither of these things on this particular afternoon; he is wandering the halls with two brutes that could be considered his “hatchet men”. The three of them are dressed in khakis, a blue blazer with the schools coat of arms on the left breast and red tie hanging around the collar of a crisp white shirt. They are looking for a fellow student named Nathaniel Burns, a young man that turned the three boys in for forcing him to do their daily assignments. “There’s the little weasel!” Michael says quietly to the two other boys while pointing to a boy that is small in stature with wildly messed up brown hair and thick black glasses clinging to his face. The boy is obviously Nathaniel Burns and they are looking for revenge. Michael and the two brutes approach Nathaniel quietly as he enters the lavatory. They pause outside the door for a few moments to make sure that no one is watching before they enter. They see Nathaniel standing in front of a sink washing his hands, Michael clears his throat loudly and Nathaniel looks up into the mirror and sees who is standing just a few feet behind him; he suddenly becomes frozen with fear. “H-hey g-guys.” Nathaniel says in a timid voice; afraid of what might happen next. His worse fears are realized as the two brutes look to Michael for the signal to attack; Michael gets an evil smile on his face as he looks over in the direction of Nathaniel, he then nods his head and the two brutes close in on the smaller boy. One of the large boys lift Nathaniel by his neck and slams him hard against the wall while the other one takes off Nathaniel’s glasses and steps on them, crushing them beneath his feet. The other boy releases his grip on Nathaniel’s throat and he falls to the ground with a loud thud; they are careful not to leave any visible marks or bruises so that the headmaster doesn’t become suspicious and begin to ask questions. Michael walks over towards the wall and motions for his two thugs to lift Nathaniel to his feet. They hoist up the smaller boy and hold him by his arms up high enough so that he is eye level with the slightly taller Michael. Michael looks at Nathaniel with pure disgust showing in his dark blue eyes, he slicks back his blond hair with his hand before spitting on the victim of the assault by the two brutes. “You know going to tell the headmaster was a very stupid thing. Now, because of your stupid ass my two friends and I are suspended from the wrestling team.” Michael says, the arrogance in his voice growing with each word that leaves his lips. “If you had just kept your mouth shut and did what you were told to do like a good little bitch none of this would have happened.” “Sod off...” Nathaniel says in a small and weak voice. Michael’s eyes narrow in anger and his breathing becomes deeper, he’s angry now. Without thinking he punches Nathaniel square in the nose with all the force that he can muster. The two large boys holding Nathaniel up release their grip right after Michael’s punch connects. Nathaniel slumps down to the ground totally unconscious and blood gushing from his nose. “Crap! We weren’t supposed to actually hurt him Michael!” One of the large boys says in an extremely worried voice. “We were supposed to just scare him mate!” The other one says in an equally worried tone. “He did this to himself.” Michael says as he walks out of the lavatory, leaving the two brutes alone with Nathaniel. “Besides, it’s not like the headmaster will do anything, we‘re the best wrestlers in this school and he needs us.” ***** It’s a nice fall morning the very next day. The time is nine o’clock sharp and Michael Biddle is sitting in the office of Headmaster Chipping. Headmaster Chipping leans forward in his chair and gives Michael a stern look. Chipping is a tall man, standing at about 6’5”; his graying hair is receding into a horseshoe pattern on the top of his head and his wire frame glasses rest impeccably upon his crescent shaped nose. He clears his throat in order to catch Michael’s attention before he speaks. “I don’t appreciate you and your two little thugs assaulting my students Mr. Biddle.” Chipping says this while trying to keep his composure. “It’s what he gets for getting us suspended from the wrestling team.” Michael says in a cool manner, if he’s nervous it’s definitely not showing. Chipping removes his glasses and rubs the bridge of his nose with his thumb and forefinger while heaving a heavy sigh. He carefully places his glasses down on his oak desk and rises from his chair. The Headmaster walks around to the other side of the desk and leans against with his hands tucked inside his pockets while he looks down at Michael, who just stares back at him like nothings wrong. “You were only given a warning and suspended from practice for a week, which is not a severe punishment considering what you did. Nathaniel only suffered a bloody nose so I’ll be lenient this time as well, but only if you stay away from him! Can you do that for me Biddle?” Headmaster Chipping asks in a forgiving tone, the only reason he’s so lenient with Michael is because he feels sorry for the young boy...losing his parents in an accident and all. “Yes sir, I can do that for you.” Michael says, amazed at how easily the Headmaster caves in. Chipping motions with his hands that Michael may leave the office and go back to class now. Michael quickly rises from his seat and walks towards the door, hoping to exit the room before Chipping changes his mind. “Oh, and one more thing Michael.” Chipping says just as Michael is about to walk out the door. “Yes Mr. Chipping?” “You can return my kindness by winning the wrestling tournament in your weight division next month.” Chipping says as smile forms on his face. “I’ll definitely do that Mr. Chipping, thank you very much sir!” Michael says while walking out the door and out of the room.
