A Meeting With The Mob (4)

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-22 It was a simple party of Washington D.C.’s finest criminals. The ball room was extravagantly elegant, a crystal chandelier hung down from the ceiling while the tables were outfitted with the finest china. The oak tables glistened from a fresh dusting, the red silk table clothes flowed over them perfectly. The oak chairs shown like the sun in the sky, the glare from the lights cascading off of them made this event seem like a party for royalty and nobility, and to some people that’s what it was. The nobility of the next class of hell all sat silent. In the room were such esteemed known criminals as Joey Merlino and Michael Ciancaglini from the Philadelphia family of La Cosa Nostra. Or in other words the American Mafia. Quiet Dom Cirillo of the Genovese family of New York sat silently with these two men, everything seemed to be peaches and cream. Sitting a few tables back sat various leaders of the rival gangs of Washington D.C. You could see various men sitting in the tables that surrounded these two esteemed groups, many of the closet casino operators and various other Mafia organization operators sat around them. Far away from them sat a group of men with their sun tanned bodies, bright white smiles, flowing long hair all dressed in suits and ties. In the middle of this table sat Token Weed. This dinner was called for a reason, as business needed to be discussed between every single person in this room. The gangsters and the Mafia had been doing business for years, and these California punks had came in with a better product and pushed hard. This had ruined the day of many a drug dealing gangster, and in turn had affected the Mafias profits in it all. Token sat at his table silent, everyone at the table had hardly touched a thing. Token looked around and smiled, he truly wasn’t enjoying himself here but for some of these guys he needed to be reassuring. They were young guys in the crime game and weren’t exactly familiar with how to act at a meeting like this. You kept a large smile in the face of impeding danger, you showed yourself to be one cocky mother fucker. Token glanced to his right to Mark and began talking ‘Mark, how’s it been going so far?’ Mark looked at Token and smiled, ‘I’m pushing around forty percent.’ Mark stated as Token smiled. ‘Good, good, I got my cut the other day your doing very well.’ Token stated bluntly. He couldn’t say much in front of these guys and he signaled to Mark to get up with his eyes as both of them got to their feet and walked over to the bar stationed beside the food table. ‘Listen Mark, I understand why you wanted me here. Your going to have to be with me at all times to get respect around here. I want you to input on everything these fucks gotta say, alright?’ Token stated as Mark nodded his head. ‘You know these Italian bastards are going to fuck with us right? I heard they want me to give them a cut and all kinds of stupid shit like that.’ Mark stated as Token looked at him and nodded his head. ‘I expected as much, these fucks think because they have the niggers and the spicks working for them that they’ll get the same out of us Californians. We’ve pushed the mob almost entirely out of Los Angeles. They know what were capable of and want to cut us off at the knees. Keep an eye on your boys tonight, make sure they aren’t getting too friendly.’ Token said giving Mark advice, he had been doing this for years and Mark would take quick note of any utterance Token made. ‘Lets get back to the table I’m a bit worried about this New York fuck.’ Mark stated as Token looked at him and smiled. ‘That Genovese fucker is just here for back up. The Philadelphia guys want some one from New York to show us the Mafia still has power. Fucking cunt bags.’ Token stated as the two walked back to the table. He and Mark went back to socializing with the fellows as a few tables over a heated discussion was raging. ‘Dom, those Californian fuck bags are trying to run us the fuck out of here.’ Joey Merlino stated as Dom nodded his head. He had heard of this drug groups power out west, they were the kings of kings out there. He had to tread a bit softer than usual with these guys. ‘I mean we all know what they did in LA. Hell they ran the mob out of there and into Las Vegas. They aren’t your run of the mill drug dealers.’ Michael stated as he filled in for Joey. Dom continued to nod his head. ‘Maybe you guys should get out of Washington?’ Dom stated with a slight smile, he had a reputation for being a ruthless smart ass with his own people and it had started show through. Joey and Michael both sneered at Dom, Michael was quick to reply. ‘Fuck that, that’s a good percentage of this families profits.’ Michael stated bluntly not noticing Dom’s sarcasm. Both of them looked at Dom for an answer, and after a few moments he finally gave one. ‘Listen have a meeting with these west coast scum bags. Say you want a cut of whatever they make off of it. Keep cool and don’t get out of control. That guy sitting beside him is going to go with him. That’s basically his capo for this city.’ Dom stated as he went back to eating his stuffed shells. The two heads of the Philadelphia La Cosa Nostra smiled as Michael tapped one of his capos on the arm. ‘Get Token and his shit stick buddy over here. Then I want all of you guys out of here, and keep the morons behind us occupied.’ Michael said as the capo instantly eyed everyone up and word began to spread. The men at the table stood up slowly leaving in intervals as they walked over to the other tables and began speaking with their own men. The Capo in question walked a few tables back, he looked at Token and smiled. ‘Dom Cirillo and his associates Mr. Joey Merlino and Mr. Michael Ciancaglini would like to speak to the both of you.’ The Capo stated as he walked away to a table full of his own men. Token smiled to Mark, this is how it always started. Token and Mark both got to their feet and nodded to their men as they walked over to the table. Joey and Mike both quickly got to their feet and walked away from the table. They wanted Dom to sit alone in case they needed some ‘help in negotiating.’ The two Mafia bosses both headed towards a back room as Token and Mark quickly followed the two men back through a set of double doors. Mike and Joey both began to glare forward at the two men, trying to read exactly what mood both of them were in. Were they nervous? Scared? Frightened? Token looked at the two and cocked an eerie smile, instantly both men froze as he reached to the inside of his jacket. As he pulled his hand forward Joey flinched. Token let out a loud cackle as he popped a Marlboro Red into his mouth. ‘Got a light?’ Token asked Joey who instantly sneered. Token smiled at the older individual as Mike quickly piped in to take the stress off of Joey. ‘Listen were here to talk business, not worry about this petty bullshit.’ Mike stated bluntly as Token cocked his head back sarcastically and kept the grin going. ‘Really, I thought this was a nice sit down dinner shit bag.’ Token stated, his smile turning to a cold stare of anger. He wasn’t in the mood for this tough guy shit. ‘Anyway we want a cut of what your making!’ Joey shouted in anger. Token looked at him quizzically, he smiled and then reached down and grabbed Joey’s hand and flicked out a knife. ‘You mean like this?’ Token stated as he ran the blade across Joey’s skin. Joey quickly pulled his hand back and stared straight forward. ‘Listen fuck bag, we want forty percent of your profits,’ Michael stated glaring forward. He was the bad ass of the two lovebirds Token thought. ‘How about this, you get down on your knees and suck my cock. Maybe then I’ll think about giving you each a percent in this game. So until your willing to do that, get the fuck out of here!’ Token shouted angrily as Joey and Michael sighed. ‘We’ll go get Dom Cirillo, and see if he can sort this shit out.’ Michael stated as the two disappeared. Token grinned and looked at Mark. ‘Now this is the fucking dego we want to talk to.’ Mark nodded his head in agreement. A ticket to New York sure did sound like fun.
