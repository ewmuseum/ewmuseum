Munson VS Vicious Despair RP Thread

9 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the world of wrestling today everybody has turned to the ways of sports entertainment. That is really how everyone sees the wrestling world these days. It has all come down to the people who are in the ring and what they say instead of actually what they do in the ring and how good they do it. This is where Vicious Despair comes in. VD has decided that he does not like the world of wrestling with sports entertainment in it. Sure it can be fun at times if you have good characters running about but VD wants something different as he walks into PTC as one of the many newcomers to the business. Watching wrestling on TV always got to VD, he sat there and was always able to predict what was going to happen during the matches. He had decided that he was going to do something about it and decided that he was going to try and join the LOW. Vicious Despair (Now) Date: May 8th 2004 Standing in a large parking lot, VD and his father Stephen sit on the hood of their pontiac firebird gulping back their bottles of budweiser and relaxing. Stephen turns to face Vicious. "How did your interview with that wrestling place go earlier in the week son?" A wide smile appears across the face of Vicious and his eyes widen. His tone was sweet and enlightened. "I got in! I can't believe it but I got in!" Stephen moves across and embraced him with a hug. "That's good to hear. You finally get to live out your dream huh?" The embrace concludes and Vicious' mood changes slightly to a more somber outset. "It's not really a dream dad, it's just something that I have to do. I have to do it so that I can pay you back all the money I owe you and I have to set the wrestling world right!" Stephen turns away and looks straight out infront of him. He had planned this day with his son so that he could tell him an important secret that he had locked away for the whole of his son's life. He began thinking to himself. "Here I am, sitting with my son. Well I wish he was my son anyway. I wish that I had told him earlier in his life but I've just been enjoying parenting so much. I must tell him though, the lies have gone too far. I have to tell him that he is not my son." Vicious Despair (Then) Date: Febuary 18th 1985 VP never had the joy of having a mother throughout his life. He had lived with Stephen for 18 years, the entriety of his existance...well apart from a couple of weeks. Living life in a trash can Two o'clock in the morning. Stephen Gracey walked through the backstreets of New York City on his way back from work. He was an independent funernal director in the centre of the city and was finishing up a few details on recent deaths in the city that he had to take care of. He moved as briskly as he could as it could be quite dangerous at that time of night. He was stopped in his tracks though as he heard the cries of a child coming from a side street. "What the hell?" He turned quickly and entered the lane swiftly moving towards the cries. His eyes became fixed on a large metal trash can at the end and he ran over. Opening the lid he moved back in complete shock. "Sick bastards..." Anger had began building up inside him as he bore witness to a small child, maybe only a couple of weeks old laying inside the trash can, still attached to it's baby seat. He lunged forward and lifted the child out and started towards the end of the lane again. Thoughts began to flutter through his head. "This isn't right! Whoever done this needs to be put against a wall and shot until they are no more. Worthless pieces of sh..." He thought he had been moving quick before but now he was almost running. Another couple of metres and he reached his front door, slamming it behind him. From there on in he would become the child's father...he named him Ben... Back to the present "Dad!?...Dad!!!!" Ben shouted to his father over and over again and finally got his attention which scared the hell out of Stephen. "Sorry son, I was in a different world there." Ben rolls his eyes. "You're always in a different world dad. Something on your mind?" Stephen knew that this was the time to tell him. He didn't know how but it would have to be now and not later. "There is son." A frown quickly comes across his face and Ben's expression turns to one of worry. "What is it?" Stephen's stomach twists as he moves close to his "son". "Ben, you're not my son..." Ben throws down his bottle of Bud and it smashes all over the concrete. His eyes burn with a firey passion. "What do you mean I'm not your son!?!" Stephen stands and tries to embrace Ben but gets shoved against the car. He know begins to worry also as he feels he is going to lose Ben as a friend. Stephen's eyes start to swell up and his head drops. Ben begins to push for the answer he is looking for. "Well?" Stephen looks up, tears now coming from his eyes, his stomach wrenching he starts to explain. "I found you eighteen years ago. You had been abandoned by your parents. Maybe your mother or your father, I do not know. I was coming home from work one night and heard crying coming from a side street so I decided to check things out. You were there...in a trash can." Ben's eyes widen with pure anger and his tone of voice tells Stephen that he was not at all happy with what he has just heard. "A trash can?! You have got to be fucking joking me!" Stephen's body sinks and he sits against the car, his breathing becoming erracting and his mind tangling with worry. "I had no choice Ben! You would have died if I hadn't of brought you out. I was going to take you to an adoption centre but then decided that I would take care of you. All my life I had wanted to be a father and that was my chance, so I took it" Ben's eyes start to grow with sadness now too and he falls to the ground, his mind in torment. SIlence ensues as he thinks to himself. "This can't be happening, it isn't happening! This man is my father and spouts out this bullshit now, now after being my inspiration for eighteen years. This is bullshit and I won't stand for it. I won't stand for being a bastard, I am not a bastard and never will be!" Looking up to Stephen, Ben can hardly speak but he is able to get some words out. "Why the lies? Why not tell me the truth about all of this earlier and not now when I am on a total high?" Stephen's head drops. "I don't know Ben. You are stronger now, you're older, wiser and thought you have been able to take the news better, but I was wrong" His fist slams again the front of the car with a fuirious force. "This isn't the way I imagined things to be happened. I just didn't want you going off to the LOW and not seeing me as much without know the truth. In the past I was happy with knowing that you thought that I was your father but then I got a feeling that somehow you could find out that I wasn't." Pulling himself up off the ground, Ben stands and moves over to stand infront of Stephen. His face becomes red with fury and he grabs him by the throat. Pushing Stephen's head against the windscreen of the car it smashes and he starts to yell. "This isn't the ways thing were meant to go. This is not how my life is meant to work out. I refuse to believe that I was abondoned by my parents! What have a done to deserve this father? Have I done something terrible to you that would make you say these things. If you are telling the truth I want you to get into the car and get the hell away from me. If you are telling me the truth you are nothing bu a stranger to me!" Stephen sits up slowly, blood coming from the back his head from the impact with the windscreen. His eyes flood out tears like a tidal storm and he stands face to face with Ben. "I am not your father Ben. I never was and never will be. I can not change that now. I only wish I could. Ben's mouth straightens in shock and he turns his back to Stephen. "The only good thing you have done today Stephen is stab me in the front. If you want to make one wish come true then get away from me right now. This is a wish of mine, for I do not want to hurt you...but I will. So make this wish come true Stephen or you won't be wishing anything for a very long time..." Stephen holds his head in his hands, his soul ripped apart by the man who he had raised and giving a chance at life to. "I will Ben, your wish has just come true. I hope I'll see you around" Stephen stares at the back of Ben, hoping for some response but none is given. He decides it's all over and moves slowly to his car. Starting the engine he reverses back then drives off. Ben is left standing in the parking lot all alone and drops to his knees holding his head in his hands. He quickly opens his arms out full stretch and looks up to the sky. "Why God why!? Why have you put this Despair upon me. Why have you changed my life so dramatically in a matter of moments. You have unleashed the vicious side within me which I never knew I had. You have released the darkened side of me...HUMAN KIND BEWARE...THE TIME HAS COME...FOR VICIOUS DESPAIR!!!" Waiting for the first onslaught Date: 9th May 2004 Whilst at the PTC building Vicious ran into a member of Core Wrestling named Munson. A heated of exchange of words happened which led to the booking of a match a week from now on BDW. When asked about this match up by a PTC reporter LOW's newest start had these choice words. "The mountain man Munson is going to feel the wrath this has been put onto me these past couple of days. I have the advantage going into this match as it will be my first appearance in professional wrestling. He has never seen me in action before so he will not know what to expect. On the other hand I have checked out Mr Munson himself and I can see that he can be easily taken out by someone as impressive as myself. He will be confident no doubt because I am a newcomer but this will be his flaw and I shall snap his confidence like a twig and corrupt his mind as easy as it is to take candy from an unwatched shop stand...that is all for now..."
