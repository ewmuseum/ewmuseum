Guess Who?

28 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Can the ends justify the means? A simple statement that could spark a lengthy debate on morals, a debate on what is wrong and what is right. Then again, what is ‘wrong’? What is ‘right’? How can we define an action to be wrong? Through morals? Yet how can we come to a definition of a moral? Where do our morals come from? Some claim they are ‘built-in’ so to speak. They are just there, no questions. Others say you learn morals and that depending on where you were brought up you have different morals. Let’s take the second argument to be true. If morals are learnt, then who is allowed to say what morals we should and shouldn’t follow? Why is it not decent to kill somebody? How can we prove what is and isn’t precious? How can we justify doing what is supposedly the ‘right’ thing. By a book? By religion? No. Morals cannot be proven to be right; therefore no one can say what is right and wrong… This leads on to whether the ‘ends’ justify the ‘means’. If one has no morals, then one would not care what had to be done to accomplish a certain goal. If murder was involved one would just do it, no regret and remorse. However, if one was religious, or had morals for some other reason then they would think twice about what they were doing. However, take this real conundrum. One man possesses both of these qualities. How is this possible? Through the fact he has two personalities, his normal personality, one with few morals, but morals none the less and then the personality with no morals. The personality with no remorse, the personality that doesn’t think twice. This could be seen by some people as being a hindrance, however in Cyrus’s line of work it is not. PTC Extreme Title Division A name can say a thousand things. A name can tell you so much about something; a name gives meaning to what it names. Shakespeare knew how much a name meant, anyone whose read Romeo and Juliet will know how important a name can be. How a name can cause a feud, how a name can cause two people to die. Cause so much destruction, how a name can cause a war… A war is brewing. A war between all the participants in the PTC Extreme Title Division Rumble. Plans are being drawn; victories are being celebrated before the battle has even begun. Countless fools are already proclaiming to become the victor of this chaotic match. One man knows that this isn’t going to be a matter of skill, this isn’t a wrestling match. It’s a brawl, it’s who can survive the longest in a fight. Cyrus believes he has a high chance of surviving, he has good reason to, he’s 6ft8. Not many people match up to his size. He has a clear advantage over the smaller people in the Rumble. He hopes to gain something from this match, if he comes out with a low ranking in the division he will not be happy… --------- The hot coffee trickled down the back of his throat causing him to flinch in slight pain as it singed his skin; Cyrus placed the cup back down on his mahogany table and put his feet up next to it. He leant back in his leather sofa and took a deep breath. It’d been a lazy day for Cyrus, he’d been to the gym and had a pretty comprehensive workout. Now that he was back, wrestling on a weekly basis, he’d regained the fitness that he lost since LOW had closed its doors. Cyrus was back to his best and was starting to rise to the top of the EWA already. The Extreme Title Division interested Cyrus. He wanted to have an excuse to go that little bit further in the ring. To get just beyond the line, to cross the white chalk and then have his opponent spit blood all over it. Cyrus was hungry for blood; it’d been a while since he’d made another man bleed. His quench for the crimson liquid had grown stronger and stronger the longer he’d been away from LOW. LOW was known for it’s ‘Extreme’ matches and that was one of the reasons Cyrus joined it. The EWA did not have as many ‘Extreme’ matches so Cyrus felt that it was time he quenched his thirst for destruction. He signed up for the PTC ETD as soon as he was able. The rumble match, his first match in the ETD since LOW, would be his first real taste. A test of his endurance mainly. He was entered near the beginning, so if he wanted any chance of winning this match he had to stay in for a long time. He knew that he was capable of it. It made him laugh when he thought other, more feeble men honestly thought they had a chance in this match. This match would sort the men out from the boys, this match would show the future King’s from the pretenders to the throne. This match was an ideal way for Cyrus to stamp his authority all over the ETD. If he dominated the match like only he could, he would reassure the critics in PTC that he was back and he was back to take over. But Cyrus was not just doing this for the critics; he was doing this for himself. Whenever he’d had an opportunity he’d taken it. Though not many opportunities had arisen for Cyrus. This was not because he could not wrestle, no, he had the talent, it was just he wasn’t as famous as the ‘Belmont’s’ of PTC. Now though he was far more recognized than the same time the year before. Cyrus really was starting to go somewhere; he’d achieved a few things in his career and had been doing well in all competitions. He’d left LOW with a bang being the last ever Realm Champion and just before that had been in the final eight of the P.I.T tournament. Cyrus had accomplished a lot in a short space of time, but a title in PTC had evaded him, Whilst he was in LOW he tried to enter the ETD but due to time restrains and demands from LOW he had to drop out. This frustrated Cyrus and led to the critics saying he could not run with the most ‘Extreme’ wrestlers in the PTC circuit. Cyrus wanted to prove to himself that he didn’t back out, Cyrus wanted to show himself he could dance with the best in PTC. Cyrus wanted to have brutal blood baths; Cyrus wanted to cause some mayhem. After all it was fun to him, it was like a hobby, plus he got paid for it. “Excuse me Sir, but it appears that you have a parcel, should I sign for it or would you like to go and personally sign for it?” The sullen monotone voice of Geoffrey burst Cyrus’s daydream like a pin obliterating a balloon. Cyrus turned to face his rather small butler and looked at him with that same expressionless look. Geoffrey guessed that Cyrus would want to sign for the parcel himself, thus why he’d asked. “I’ll take care of it, you can be getting off now, if you stay any longer you won’t be paid.” Cyrus showed no compassion towards the man he now spent most of his time with. He showed no friendship towards him, not even any manners. Cyrus regarded Geoffrey as being lower than him, so thought that he deserved to be treated like an object rather than a human being. “Thank you Sir.” Geoffrey was not one to hide his emotions, he replied sarcastically and then proceeded to get his coat and leave as quickly as was humanly possible. Cyrus eased himself out of the comfortable sofa and walked towards the door, he opened it to see a small, fat man with a Parcel Force uniform on. He took the clip board off him and signed what he needed to and then accepted the parcel. It wasn’t very big, Cyrus unwrapped the brown paper carefully to reveal a box of chocolates and a note. The note read: “Hey sexy, it’s nearly Valentines Day, will you be mine?” Cyrus walked over to his dustbin and placed the chocolates and note inside. He then shut the lid and went and sat back down. Something felt strange about that not, he recognized something about it, maybe the handwriting. No, that was stupid. Cyrus flicked his television on with the remote control and put on a video tape of a few wrestlers competing in the ETD. He thought he might as well not waste his free time, studying his opponents may give him a slight advantage in the match…yet that note, how hard he tried to push it to the back of his mind…it was still there…making him wonder…who? ----------
