Arresting Action...[GT rp1, rp 2/6 The Final Saga]

Kaiowas Cable Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 52 Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 5:06 pm Post subject: Arresting Action...[GT rp1, rp 2/6 The Final Saga] Pain, Anguish and Loss.... Kaiowas had known these things for far too long and for far too long these emotions had hounded him. Kaiowas had never been one to openly create human bonds with others.... Being such a loner and wanderer he felt it right that if one was going to have comrades, then the bond should create itself..... Therefore making that partnership so much stronger, being based on real respect and real honour rather than free drinks and drunken promises. Kaiowas had never allowed anyone he did not trust to feel in anyway related to him..... Unfortunately this always sets you up for a fall, a fall from grace, a fall from a great height. No matter what you do you cannot stop or change everything. There are just some things beyond your control, some things that even the Lord of the Ktulu, a small Brazilian Tribe that had to relocate to the Iowa Forests, cannot do. One of those things is bringing back a fallen comrade..... Just hours ago Kaiowas went through one of the biggest battles of his life. After a shape shifting wizard infiltrated the highly secure village of the Ktulu, and opened the gates to let in a bunch of attackers, Kaiowas and his old comrade, a sixty year old Ktulu Hunter, Lecood were the only ones close enough to launch a counter offensive in time. Charging forth, the quick and nimble Hunter, extremely fit for his age, and the Young Tribal Warrior armed with his trusty staff drove back the first wave of attacks, staving off ogres and other such weird beasts. These beasts were here for a reason. Kaiowas and the Ktulu had praised a god named Pultra for a very long time, generations in fact. When Kaiowas ascended to be the official servant to Pultra the Lord of Vengeance, he was given the title Angel of Retribution. Kaiowas was soon sent ot the USA to pose as a professional wrestler, while collecting as many souls and victories for his god as possible. But soon everything got very strange as someone began to wage war on Pultra. A silent secretive force was wearing down the god of the Ktulu and Pultra relied on Kaiowas to stop it.... And stop it he did... but for only a while. For the words of the enemy, known only as the ethereal beast "the Slayer", spoke truth to Kaiowas when he proved to him that his god was a fake... and that he should denounce the fake and destroy him. Kaiowas was lured by the power that was offered him if he succeeded. Forced to the cause by the spiteful and evil bastard Pultra, Kaiowas proceeded to destroy all of Pultra's advantages before soon he will move to destroy the impostor finally. But mere days before Kaiowas will make the steps to finish off a long and legendary war on his fake former god, Pultra sent this attack force to hinder or even stop him in his tracks. They were sent to kill Kaiowas or at least wound him so that Pultra could finish the job much easier than before. Unfortunately they failed in their task of wounding Kaiowas badly, he only obtained minor cuts and bruises to his head, forearms and legs. One thing that he had not expected though, was the mental impact of this battle. After the swift and victorious destruction of the twenty or so beasts Kaiowas found Lecood the old hunter had fallen to a blast of magic. The tragic and underhand death of the respected battle genius hit Kaiowas hard. Kaiowas rushed over to the lifeless body of the old blade master and removed his custom wrist blades from his arms, desperately searching for a pulse or a sign of life, finding none the pain inside began to flare.... Now he walks alone, miles away from his village, mindlessly ruing the mistakes he made in battle to have let this old man die... [Quickly and aggressively the young brazilian who just entered into such power over his Tribe strides without purpose through the land around and about his newly built village. As usual the long wooden staff that he uses in battle accompanies him, strapped to his back by fine tanned leather straps. But now there was a new addition to this old weapon that had served him so well. In honour of the old hunter Lecood that had died saving the Tribe, Kaiowas had taken the weapons that the old man had used so deftly in battle time and time again. In reverence and in respect for the man's honour and honed speed and battle skills Kaiowas had had the most skilled member of the Ktulu attach these huge foot and a half long blades to either end of his staff. Now Kaiowas would fight as if he had old Lecood at his side, and it made him a hell of a lot more dangerous as he now combines his natural speed, the heavy blows of the toughened wood and the quick slicing cuts of the super sharp blades. But for all that new effort that Kaiowas had put into remaking his weapon so that he could face Pultra, Kaiowas felt no better about the death of his comrade. The anger rose and fell in him, patterning itself, and timing with the bouts of self loathing and hatred..... Hatred for himself, for the sneaky wizard who used the underhand magic attack to kill the extremely skilled blade master and hatred for the one he has, for so long now, planned to denounce and kill.....Pultra. Many a tree has felt the full force of the Tribal Warrior on the warpath, as his long walk around his encampment has been to soothe this anger and the other conflicted emotions. It still hasn't worked and Kaiowas is striking out at anything and everything that gets within ten metres of him. Many of the old oaks now have six foot long scours along their bark, many of the large old rocks in the area now lie split in half and many of the beasts of the forest have scampered away scared and frightened by the foul mood and obvious aggression in all of Kaiowas' movements. His extremely hardened demeanour that he normally wears has fallen away like a snakes skin and has been replaced by the extremely worried and conflicted face that scowls and snarls around the forest floor. The night has now even fallen and Kaiowas has lost none of his impetus, still rushing around, still trying to calm the great need for combat and finally...trying to sort out why he feels so guilty about the death of Lecood. The night beasts are out and the moon shines full in the bare cloudless night sky. Kaiowas still hasn't really noticed the fast paced changing of time because he is so absorbed in his self accusations. His usually stealthy approach to everything has been flung aside in this time of tension and stress. Kaiowas is feeling the full mental blow of Pultra's surprise attack and he knows that Pultra is probably laughing that evil laugh at him right now. And that is what pushes him on all the time.... Knowing that if he does not shape himself up for this demi god encounter he will fall short of the expectations of his people and himself. He cannot lose, he cannot die.... just because he cannot let himself...] *** Why did I let him fall to that evil bastard's force? Why did I even endanger anyone else in this whole business? Did I have to pull the Ktulu into this whole thing? Did I have to bring the wrath of a fallen Angel upon all these people that have supported me through my time as a Tribal Warrior? Why did I even come back to the Amazon? I could have saved myself and the others all this grief by plunging my blade into Pultra's heart all those months ago! I failed then and there is nothing now to say that I won't fail again! How can I act like a Lord of the Ktulu when I have been away serving the one who enslaved them for generations? I served Pultra! I followed his orders....... grudgingly...... spitefully....... and unwillingly but I still played a role in keeping the Ktulu under wraps! I was part of the problem! I was part of his regime! I was the one who was handing out his Vengeance! And the whole time I was blind to the real truth! Now I have paid for my ignorance in the cost of other's lives! And it is all down to my failure in battle! Lecood should never have died....... I should have been the one on the end of that magic flare! I should have been the one who took the hit! I have resistance to that type of magic, Pultra "blessed" me with it years ago! It is all my fault that the oldest hunter and most experience Ktulu blade master lies in a shallow grave! It was he that trained me, gave me my speed! It was his wish to be at my side fighting Pultra! Now he cannot even know if I am successful or not! I may be using his blades to pierce the blackened heart of the tyrant but it does not bring him back........ It will never bring him back..... *** [Snap........ a twig snaps behind Kaiowas and his ears spark into action along with his arms and his body. Grasping the staff and sliding it out of it's sheath with his right hand he becomes wary of where he is for the first time in about six hours. Snapping into his pre set defensive crouch he scans all around him, looking to make out any minor movements or any sort of sign of an intruder.... He waits and waits but nothing shows. Not lowering his guard at all Kaiowas does not replace his weapon. Holding it tightly in both his hands he does not move but his mind begins to race once more, allowing the bloodflow to slow overcoming the shock...] *** If that is that foul underhanded wizard that caused that whole ambush back at the village I will have Lecood's vengeance here and now..... Now sly trick can fool me now... I will have his head and intestines on a stick within the hour and no-one will stop me! The bastard will fall to Lecood's blades and the old man shall smile over me from above! This must end here with this wizard, I will slice his robes open and cut out his heart while he screams to the night air.... but nobody will hear him! Because nobody is here.......... *** [Rustle....] *** Here he is... the vile beast comes back to finish off the job his master sent him for.... Here he comes through the darkness... not showing his face! I will destroy the bastard! *** [Stepping out into the moonlight the figure in the bushes stands at six feet five inches tall. The man walks slowly out into the shining rays of the white moon above. The more he moved towards the young Brazilian clutching his weapon tightly, the more Kaiowas seemed to be transfixed with who it was....] Kaiowas: You.....? [Now just fifteen feet away stands the Vampire who saved Kaiowas at the battle that ended Lecood's life. He is now without the strange protective mask shading him from the deadly sun's rays but Kaiowas can tell it is the man that beat back Pultra's forces as they threatened to overwhelm the Tribal Warrior.] Vampire: Yes.... it is I... [Cloaked in a long flowing trench-coat which ends at his ankles, the arms ending at his wrists, displaying the pale skin underneath the man with a full head of long thin blonde hair nodded towards Kaiowas wisely... His lithe features did not hide his physical abilities in the slightest. Under his clothes his veins stood out and his muscles rippled. By his right hand was a sword in a sheath attached to his waist by a thick belt around his waist. As he walked it knocked off his thigh. Kaiowas became wary of it and pointing at him with the end of his staff he shouts out...] Kaiowas: Demon! If you wish to talk civilly then take your hands from your sides! [Raising his arms away from his weapon and his thighs the Vampire stands stock still, calm and calculating, waiting to gain enough of Kaiowas' trust to speak up. The laced shirt he wears lies open slightly and it flutters in the breeze of the night. His hair too brushes over his face. Kaiowas relaxes his guard once more but does not lower his weapon just yet.] Kaiowas: Who are you demon? Why are you here? Why do you dare even infiltrate the realm of the Ktulu? I have dealt with your kind before and dispatching you is just as easy as any of those beasts from that attack party this morning... Tell me quickly! Or you may number one of the dead too! [Kaiowas' threat hardly sinks in when a cold chilling laughter utters forth from the lungs of the tall pale man. Keeping his sight transfixed on Kaiowas he laughs and claps as if it were all supposed to be a big joke. Kaiowas seems to be offended by this and raises his weapon, bringing it to bear in case it was needed. The last thing he wanted now was to be mocked by a vampire who had saved his life just this morning.... The vampire on seeing the rage burning in Kaiowas' eyes quickly puts an end to his laugh and clears his throat. Raising his hand in a gesture to Kaiowas to lower his staff once more he speaks clearly and eloquently.] Vampire: I.... am the one you have sought to find, the one you have pondered over. In fact I am only one of us... for there are many. You have met many of us before, they have tried to track you.... Only to meet their untimely demise at your hands... Something we have come to expect from such a person as you... full of aggression and a ruthless rebellious streak... I myself am called Felrig, but during the day they call me Congressman Bull. We on the other hand, are called the Dracons. You may know us to be...... "The dragon in full flight" as I recall you called it.... [Kaiowas' jaw drops in shock.... For the last two months Kaiowas has not just been hounded by Pultra but also by a group of people he thought to be a special group of the FBI looking after supernatural beasts on our world. Up until now Kaiowas had believed that they sought to destroy him, and part of him still held this belief the other part of him only being slightly intrigued in what this person was saying. The Vampire Felrig brings his hands together and pulls something off his right middle finger. Holding it up letting it glimmer in the moonlight he showed it to Kaiowas before lobbing it underhand at the Tribal Warrior. Kaiowas catches it and examines the coin ring. It matched those that he had found on the previous FBI officers, the dragon in full flight emblazoned on its gold surface. Looking at it cautiously and then throwing it back Kaiowas becomes wary once more.] Kaiowas: The Dracons? Who are the Dracons? Why are they here? What are they doing? Why in the hell did you save me? What makes you think I am glad you helped me? [Felrig notices the great intrigue that he has awoken in Kaiowas and he starts to work on it. He edges around the questions noticeably though. His dark features seem to light up as he talks and he steps closer to Kaiowas gradually.] Felrig: Kaiowas? There is much that you ask that you do not need to know..... At least yet. But there is much that I can tell you... here and now. Now you ask, why did I save you? Why wouldn't I save you? Didn't you ever think of that? If we are after you then why would we allow some foul and disgusting huge spider? Even if we wanted you dead for some reason do you think that it would suffice people that have followed you for so long and so far would give up the chase and allow someone else to finish off the job? Or maybe there is something else being all this Kaiowas.... Maybe something you could never understand because your mind thinks along the simple route when you are in "battle mode". If you are being hounded then destroy the hound, right? Well maybe that doesn't always work.... We knew you would ruin our first contacts with you and we knew you would escape our grasp.... And we even knew that at sometime you would need us..... Just as we need you Kaiowas.... [Need him? Why did they need him, Kaiowas' heart beat jumps up to well above resting and sweat begins to seep from the deep pores in his skin. His palms begin to grip the staff even harder to make up for the lubricating salty liquid that his body is producing...] Kaiowas: Need me? You need me? You send men to kill me and you "need me"? (mocking) Please Felrig, do explain to me how all this works out, how this makes sense to you. Tell me why your "group" needs me... Explain to me why the Dracons need to bother a Tribal Warrior especially when he is... [Felrig comes in breaking up Kaiowas' sentence.] Felrig: About to slay his god? How did I know that? I know a lot of things about you Kaiowas, sometimes I know too much about you... But right now I'm asking you to follow me... Kaiowas: No..... [It doesn't seem to phase Felrig at all but his vision shifts from Kaiowas' face to just over his shoulder. Dropping into an offensive position drawing his sword, Kaiowas also readies himself for combat.] Felrig: Very well, little warrior.... [Suddenly a strong hairy hand falls on Kaiowas' should grasping it tightly, it shocks Kaiowas and the Tribal Warrior turns swiftly to get a glimpse of his new assailant. Behind him is what can only be described as a hybrid of a human man and a wolf, a werewolf! The pointed nose is but inches from Kaiowas', who turns his whole body, bringing his bladed staff to bear, swishing at empty air as the nifty agile Wolf dodges skilfully. The "schlinggg" of Felrig's sword leaving his sheath is heard behind Kaiowas and again Kaiowas is caught in two minds.... Which should he attack or defend from? The momentary flinch is all that is needed by both the attackers as Felrig and the werewolf take full advantage of the distracted Tribal Warrior. Luckily still having the instincts that he had honed during his training in the Amazon, Kaiowas ducked out of the way as both the attackers collide in the air space where Kaiowas used to be. Felrig is the first to his feet and goes after Kaiowas, sword raised in a well practiced combat technique. The wolf follows soon after, but now Kaiowas has a much better chance.... Both assailants are infront of him!] Felrig: Give up Kaiowas! [Felrig charges and ends up on his face as Kaiowas deftly side steps and trips him by placing a sharp blow to the vampire's ankle. Turning to face the huge werewolf Kaiowas finds that he has launched himself into the air and he has to react quickly. Digging the spear into the ground and putting his weight on one end by standing on it he angles the sharp point of Lecood's blade upwards hoping to allow the supernatural creature impale itself on the long staff by the miracle of momentum. Unluckily Kaiowas hadn't reacted quick enough and the blade only slashed the arm of the beast who roars bestially. It lands and rolls towards Felrig who is picking himself up once more. Rising to its haunches it holds the wound with one clawed hand. Felrig drops his sword on the ground, letting it clatter and clang.] Felrig: Enough! Duddy.... your ears! [Both Felrig and the werewolf cover their ears with their hands and Kaiowas looks at them in total bewilderment.] *** Flash *** [All of a sudden there stands a beautiful woman all dressed in white robes with wispy blonde hair. Within seconds, to Kaiowas' horror, the woman ages and turns to a floating wraith with an empty face and torn dress, the hair turning black and thick, cockroaches crawl from eye socket to eye socket as she opens her mouth....] Kaiowas: NO! [Too late! The floating creature that hovers before Kaiowas is a Banshee! Her mouth opens to supernatural width and soon the long whining pours forth, it crescendoes quickly, the pitch also rising dramatically. Soon the whole forest is alive with the sound of the deadly beast. Kaiowas' ear drums nearly burst and he attempts to pull his hands to his hears but he just cannot. Struggling badly he falls to the ground and soon the expression leaves his face and consciousness leaves him. The noise of the Banshee falls away and the white woman emerges from within the wraith once more. Felrig is the first to react, removing his hands from his ears he shouts over to the woman...] Felrig: Thank you Mary... [He hurries over to Kaiowas and throws him over his shoulder, pointing to his staff he shouts at the werewolf...] Felrig: Duddy... get the staff and help the lady along her way! [Felrig, Duddy and Mary all pace out of the clearing laden up and purposefully as above the moon still shines full and bright.] _________________ DWO World Champion and Most Improved 2002, PTC no1 january 8th and 14th and no7 too!
