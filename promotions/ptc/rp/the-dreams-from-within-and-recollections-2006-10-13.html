The dreams from within and recollections!

13 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-13 A huge longhouse looms before you looming larger than any building in legend. It seems to be swirling snow in thickening drifts and sheets of blinding white. The cold air is chill biting right to the bone. Footprints being covered by the heavy falling snow can be seen leading to the Long house. The foot prints seem long and laborious. As if the person who was walking had had a long journey to this place. The footsteps begin to disappear as the snow falls harder and the drifts become deeper. The Long houses’s main doors open as a stranger dressed in furs enters fully claked within the confines of that thick fur. The men around the table look upon the man with suspicion and disdain. One large man sits at the head of the table and looks down at the stranger with one eye as the other is covered with a patch. The large man leans forward as he runs his hand through his mighty white beard as he taps his spear three times as all in the great hall grow quiet. The man stands and points at the stranger his mighty hand still grasping the spear readying it to be used at a moments notice. The deep thundering voice of this older yet seemingly wise man speaks as he stares at the stranger. "Whilst come amongst the halls of the Aesir? These halls are reserved for the honoured and revered amongst the gods! You are welcomed here, but ye have come to soon my son. Is your task complete?" The stranger looks at everyone as another red bearded man with a large hammer stands and holds his hammer before him as the thunder rolls outside as if to worship its master as the stranger merely sneers under his hooded head and then turns and looks at the larger wiser man. "Nay great father I am not finished. I have come for I have lost thy own path. I needed to breath the airs of my people once again. I am a warrior born great father. Whilst I sit here and dine amongst thy kin I prepare for great deeds of war once again. The games of mortal men hath intrigued even the greatest of us. Though none of ye can walk the lands of men thou hast a warrior who fights for our greater honour. The world beyond is scary and hath become much changed since thou hast influenced the great minds of man. Ye are no longer cherished by men. You are legends and history and mere comic book fantasies." Looking at the mighty aesir with the hammer he sneers staring at him as the hammer is raised into the air and is slammed down upon the table. The thickly furred man does not react he merely laughs deeply watching the man. "Thor still easily angered I see. Thou art but a figment of history and you’re enraged that you are thrown to the side as flotsam is upon the waves of the oceans. Ye are a sad excuse for a god of thunder aren’t you? You’re scared of the present and live within the past." The old wiser man stands cutting off everyone as the furred cloaked man turns his gaze and stares at the old man and listens as Thor sits down staring bullet holes through the stranger. "Enough for enow! I hath a request of you young one. Thou shalt cause the men of midgard to remember that we do remember them and we do exist as we are embodied in you. Regardless of whom you are or what we be. We are the past and you are the present and future. Go now to the world of midgard and return the glory of the aesir to the world of mortals. Your ancestors and brethren are awaiting your return to the mighty halls of Valhalla. Go now be gone for thee hath worn thin thy own welcome here." The stranger grins just under his cloak as he removes his hood revealing that he is Jorge Samuelsson Viking last of the Aesir amongst the mortal men. "As you wish Odin!" The noises become confused and distinct as the area suddenly changes as the area is swaying back and forth as the view becomes clearer through somewhat of a fog. A cramped area on a plan sits Samuelsson. He casually sits up and reaches for a magazine as someone sits next to him looking at him with disgust. It so happens to be it is a woman who is simply leering at him. Samuelsson narrows his eyes at her and raises an eyebrow. "What lady?" She looks at him and then points a finger at him beginning to scold him. "You groped me most of this flight!" Samuelsson grins and smiles. "Really lady it was only a dream. I didn’t do anything!" The woman seethes with anger and yells at Samuelsson now. "Must have been some dream you groped my right breast for three hours." Samuelsson looks at her and laughs heavily. "Only three hours I am losing my touch." The woman groans and gets up stomping away towards the back of the plane as a man stewardess walks up to him and speaks. "Uh sir there has been some complaints by the other passenger about your rude gestures and snoring." Samuelsson looks at the little stewardess of a man. He chuckles slightly and then leans forward and spits on the man. "There that should help." The stewardess looks at Samuelsson and walks away as Samuelsson looks at the man in front of him smoking a cigar as a kid next to him is slamming the seat back against his knees and Samuelsson looks at the kid as the kid sticks out his tongue at Samuelsson. Samuelsson looks at the kid and makes a deep growling sound as the kid slowly slips back beneath the seat as Samuelsson reaches forward and grabs a wrestling magazine as it shows Gtt6 on the cover as he opens it and looks at it. "They forget who I am. What a shame if you ask me. My improvements from last year and my return to GTT6 after my magnificent run in GTT5. This the prophet John Adams or John Adams the prophet of even the profitable reject John Adams I don’t care. He is just another toady to be squished by me. They always underestimate me." Samuelsson shuts the book with disgust and flings it out into the aisle way as he stares forward at nothing and yet everything at the sametime. His eyes focused on some unknown point and he clears his thoughts and begins to speak. "My dreams lately seem to be telling me and teaching me things. My lessons of the past and present are becoming intermixed. I have become more worldy in the ways of mere mortal men. This isn’t about winning or losing it is about conquering everyone who has crossed my path. You Prophet have come into my domain and will have to face me in a way you have never faced before. I am not scared of you nor will I ever be. Why do my dreams continue to balance around this match these days." Samuellson rubs his eyes and then looks at the magazine before him. Reaching again he picks up a Time magazine reflecting the Iraq war. He picks it up and thumbs through it. His eyes stare at each weapon and each explosion and each portion of bloodied body. He grins sadistically as he leans back closing the magazine. "That is much better it has opened a huge path within my destiny. Blood shall flow again in the hallowed halls of PTC. Starting with you John Prophet do not look beyond me I am going to make you bleed and hurt. Your mistake was entering GTT6. Their mistakes was making me the underdog once again." Standing up he walks through the isle way of the plane looking at people and then he observes a newly married couple. "Pathetic truly pathetic that I shall be suffered the stupidity of people with no reality aspect of what is watching them. I could simply trash talk this time around, but I want people to see the inner beast as well." Stopping abruptly he looks over at a young boy he notices that he is playing with small vikign actions figures. "What you got there young man?" The young boy looks up at him and grins and proudly shows off the toys. "They are Viking warriors and I am going to be one when I grow up. Dad says I am Norwegian or it runs in the family whatever that means. That is how I got this fiery red hair he says. I will be a mighty Viking one day and nobody can stop me. I will be as big and strong as Samuelsson the wrestler. You know you look a lot like him." Samuelsson grins and stands up looking down at the kid. "I get that a lot and I am sure you will be a strong man one day. It is a good dream." Samuelsson rubs the boys hair and walks away as the boy glares at him and turns back to his toys. Samuelsson walks up to a woman who is reading tarot cards. The woman seems to be dressed much like a gypsy as she looks up at him and motions for him to sit. "Come sit sit I want to show you the cards." Samuelsson nods a bit amused as he sits. She flips over the cards one at a time in a set fashion as he looks on. She speaks staring at the cards. "You have a pact with the gods for some reason. Your path is not defined yet it is set before you in a very intriguing. Your destiny is unfolding and there is some sort of contest where you will be facing many odds that will overwhelm you and you will get a chance to defeat many a mighty opponent." Samuelsson reaches into his pocket and pulls out a piece of paper. "Let me read you this little poem I wrote. Maybe you can make more sense of the words than I can." The woman nods to Samuelsson as he begins to read. "Stand before me but do not judge. Stand beside me and join me. Stand Around me and do not crowd me." Breathing a deep breath he looks voer at the woman and then looks back to the paper. "Look with me but make no sound. Look ahead with me and not despair. Look forward and then try not to look behind." Trembling a bit as he breaths deeply steadying himself. "Breathe a deep breath of cold air. Breathe a deeper breath of depressing air. Breathe in the air of despair." Gaining confidence as he readies himself for the reading of this poem he had written. "Love that which you cannot have. Love that which you want more and more. Love those who are below you and whisper." Samuelsson sneers as he reads the love part and quickly hides the fact that his demeanour has changed. "Show some pride in others. Show people that you are not shallow. Show that you stand and watch over all." Narrowing his eyes he thinks forward to the outcome of his first match in GTT6. Knowing he is going to have to perform at the best of his ability and beyond. "Watch the world come full circle around you. Watch and realize that the world is a cruel place. Watch your own self and despair." Closing a fist as he looks at it. His muscles rippling in his huge arm as he slowly looks forward looking at the people on the plane. "End what has become the beginning. End the worlds staring upon you. End the hatred inside yourself." His mind fades back to when he was beginning his career as a wrestler a Viking Warrior choosing a popular battlefield. Suddenly he flashes back to his performance of last year in GTT5. Knowing he wishes to top it this year. The time ahs come for him to get a little pay back. The beginning has ended now. Embracing the hatred he holds towards everyone and uses it as fuel for his rage. He looks down at the paper again and begins to read. "Bring yourself to the pinnacle. Bring yourself the pride you deserve. Bring your own place in the desolate world." Samuelsson folds the paper and hands it to the woman and grins. Looking at her he eyes her up and down. He then narrows his gaze at her tarot cards. "You keep the poem it is the last I will write. You’re a seer of sorts. I will seek you out again in the near future if I am victorious at the tournament I am in. I am looking to succeed and conquer all doubters and those who think they need to look beyond me. I am going to prove once and for all last years tournament was not a fluke it was a precursor to a warrior coming to destroy the competition." Looking at her he goes to stand up as she reaches out and takes his hand and turns it over setting a small silver necklace in his hand. The necklace has the symbol of Odin upon it as she looks up at him. "The All father is always watching regardless of what you think. You are one of his children and before you go to war you must cast aside all doubts." Samuelsson looks at the necklace and then turns and looks down the aisles of the plain and then turns to look back at the woman and notices she is gone only thing where she was in a single white feather as Samuelsson looks around as if nobody had ever seen a woman there before as he stops the rude stewardess of a man. "Did you see that woman that was here?" The stewardess looks at him and shakes his head as he speaks. Walking away as he does so. "Stupid Norwegian threatens us and then loses his mind." Samuelsson looks at the man and follows him to the back of the plane and grins grabbing the man by the throat and slams him into a small storage closet as he looks the man in the eyes. "It is only the beginning of the suffering you shall see. One warning little man! You threatened the wrong person and then screwed up making fun of me. Have a good remaining flight. When they ask me where you are I will simply state you wanted a better view of the clouds." Slamming the man’s head against the wall he simply stuffs him into the storage area and shuts the door. Walking back to his seat he looks at the magazines and picks up the wrestling magazine again. "Might as well read this, you never know when your opponents will be waiting to fight you again and again. Well GTT6 is the place to bring the fight. Now the time has come to begin my run again. How will it go? Only time will tell." Looking out the window he sees a vision of Odin sitting upon his throne looking at him with strong piercing eyes. Samuelsson stares at the vision his mind is producing on the clouds as he places his hand upon his strong chin. He gently strokes his beard as he watches and his mind begins to ponder which battles must be fought and how hard they will be fought in the coming weeks.
