GW's - Souls of the Black Lotus Tag Title Division Week 1 RP Title: The Key To Winning - Unity - .::The world as we know it revolved around one thing, teamwork. If the world could be run by one person alone, then I have no doubt that every person would live in their own world, in solitary confinements forever. The truth of the matter is that we all need someone else. It is a measure of help, a sort of security blanket for the entire human race. It is the basic need of the human race, to be able to fall back on someone. And it is the basic human instinct to rely on another It is the ability to do so that makes the world go round, it is what has made we as humans so strong. From this concept comes many more questions, but very few answers. Why is this statement so? Why have we allowed ourselves as a race to fall into this position? How can it be that such a powerful species; although free, has lost all of its independence on an individual basis? Why must we rely on others? Why? And so we arrive from a look at this predicament at a global scale, to a much smaller scale. From the real world, to the world of professional wrestling. Although much reduced in size, wealth and population, this world follows the same basic ideas as the real world. Here in the wrestling world, we can see this reliance just as strong, if not stronger than in the real world. 1) A governing body. In the real world it is the president or prime minister and the government. In the wrestling world it is an owner and a board of directors. 2) The stars. In the real world these are your role models. These are your actors, your musicians, the athletes. In the wrestling world these are your superstars, your wrestlers. 3) The rest of the people, the basic population. In the wrestling world, your combined mix of staff, road crew and fans. And from all this what do we see? The same trends in both the world and the world of pro wrestling. Team work, and interdependence are the keys to success::. The room was dark and damp. Lonely, although merely a room the sheer sense of it’s being conveyed a deep and utter depression. The room was essentially bare, alone in the room was a television set and a small chair. The chair was tattered and torn, with a slight tinge of mold growing on the foam that was coming through the rips in the fabric of the chair. The television screen was filled with static and was currently emitting two noises. The soft hum of the electric current running through it and the loud cackling of the static. There was a small click and the entire screen went black. Soon the static noises were replaced by voices… u•ni•ty -The state or quality of being in accord; harmony. -Singleness or constancy of purpose or action; continuity: “In an army you need unity of purpose” (Emmeline Pankhurst). He whose ranks are united in purpose will be victorious. --Sun Tzu In order to have a winner, the team must have a feeling of unity; every player must put the team first-ahead of personal glory. --Paul Bear Bryant The two brothers were inside the training ring. They were dressed in their typical warm up suits; muscle shirts with sweat pants. Of course, Ichiro was clothed in white and Jushin in black. They stood engaged in combat. They took turns lunging forward and attacking one another with combos of kicks and punches. A small bell signaled that it was time for a break from their training. They sat down in the corner of the ring, each taking occasional sips from their water bottles. “That’s good, you’re staying quick even though we’ve almost double our normal workout length,” said Ichiro. “You know I can keep up, why are we still doing this?” asked Jushin. “We’re just getting started little brother,” Ichiro started. “I want to try something a little different the next time we get going though.” “What is it that you have in mind?” asked Jushin. “Well we’ve been training against one another, but we fight alongside each other. Lately we have been performing well, but if we want to win the PTC Titles we have to step up our game that extra little bit, make our in ring strategy completely flawless. We need one more competitive advantage over the rest of them, Jushin,” said Ichiro. “Ichiro, we’re brothers. We already have a leg up on the competition, we trust each other to no ends do we not? We are solid fighters individually, not to mention together. We have confidence. And we are the GW Tag Champs already, what more do we need?” asked Jushin. “Unity.” “What the hell are you talking about?” demanded Jushin. “Unity,” said Ichiro. “The one thing that will make or break a team. The ability to function as a unit for a collective goal, without cares of personal gain, merely for the betterment of the unit.” “Okay, dictionary boy. How about you talk sense and tell me what you mean and exactly how we are going to achieve this,” Jushin said as a smirk crept over his face. “Unity, our overall ability to move, think and act collectively as one. I believe that if we can think alike, and move alike, then we shall ensure our victory, and none shall be able to defeat us,” answered Ichiro. “Alright then, how are we going to do this?” asked Jushin. “Ever played follow the leader?” laughed Ichiro. ”You’ve got to be kidding me. This is an important match man, we can’t waste our time playing stupid kid games!” yelled Jushin. “Relax little brother, it will help. Just follow what I do for five minutes, then soon after I will follow you. Then the next time we will try and go at the same time, predicting each other’s movements. Moving together, united,” answered Ichiro. “Alright fine, let’s give this a shot,” said Jushin. Jushin stood and walked to the far side of the ring. He turned and faced towards Ichiro, who was still seated on the ground. Ichiro shook his head and took another sip of his water bottle. He took the bottle from his mouth and began to stand up. Muttering one sentence from his mouth as he stood. “Yunaiteddo itsumademo,” he mumbled as he stood. Roughly translated it meant “United Forever.” It was a common saying of he and his brother. Ichiro now stood facing his brother. He made a slight bow which was met by his brother and then he began the exercise. He moved slowly at first, making it easier for his brother to keep up. After a few minutes, Ichiro began to pick up the pace. He threw a large combination of kicks and punches together, as Jushin mimicked his movements about a second later. They continued at this, several times, switching back and forth between the leaders. Their eyes stayed focused and their faces stayed stern. Their minds did not stray, there were only two important things and that was the other person and following his movements. They continued at this for a good half an hour, finally it began to happen. They stopped worrying about who was leading and who was following. The thoughts of such things faded from their minds. All that was left was a feeling of what was happening next. As their minds began to lose focus on the rest of the world, their souls took over and guided them. They were soon moving together, completely in sync, each knowing the others next moves well in advance. “We have done it,” said Ichiro and he stopped his movements. He picked up his water bottle and stepped out of the ring. “Strange, I felt as if I could see inside your head, like I knew what you were thinking,” said Jushin. “Thinking with one mind,” said Ichiro. “It is that act that will help us out in our matches, we will know what should be done next, and we will know when to act and when to stay uninvolved.” Jushin slid out of the ring after his brother. Together they made their way back to the change rooms of their personal gyms. They quickly showered, before they were ready to go about their days proceedings. The two brothers each emerged from their personal dressing room about twenty minutes later. They were freshly showered and cleaned up. The two brothers dressed in their traditional business suits. Ichiro in white and Jushin in black began to make their war towards the exit. Jushin pushed his way through the door, the sunlight almost blinding him at first as he stepped into the parking lot. He walked down and Ichiro was still shutting the lights out behind them. He made sure everything was shut down and safe, then as he pulled the keys for the warehouse gym from his pocket he heard the engine of a car roar to life. He looked up as he was about to leave, his mind raced as he read the words that were painted on a banner over top of the doorway. “Yunaiteddo itsumademo!” – Unity Forever Ichiro’s mind raced as he read the words. They enthralled him, it seemed as if every time he read the words he was brought back to another point in his past where the words meant something to him. Another point in time, when the words had a strong relevance. And this time was no different. Ichiro looked at the massive building in front of him. The large thirty story building was his next place of attack. The evening twilight of the sinking sun reflected off the windows, and it’s light was enough to light up the next three city blocks. He stared at the building and to be truthful it scared him. He knew what his mission was tonight, a quick in and out assassination, but it made him nervous because tonight Jushin would not be going in with him. The building was much more secure than any they had ever infiltrated before. Now, because of this Jushin was going to have to stay outside the building and monitor the security features of the building, many of which were unnoticeable to the naked eye. Jushin was going to have to be Ichiro’s eyes for him. The night was early still, he would not be back until later on that evening. He had the schedule of his target well planned out, and from this he had drawn out a plan. Very detailed and completed far enough in advance to account for any changes that might have occurred already. Such changes had not occurred and so the plan was to go on as normal. Ichiro walked back towards the small hotel across the street. He walked through its hallways and eventually came to Room 106. The room where Jushin had set up all of his gear, the room he would leave in about an hour and a half in order to go kill, some big business man or something. He saw Jushin and the two exchanged looks. Ichiro walked past, he walked into the bedroom and fell asleep. He needed to rest a bit before he went to do this next job. It would require an undying trust in his brother, he had to trust him with his life, and no matter who it is, it takes a lot of time to do that. With that thought in his mind, he fell asleep. An hour later he woke up, he walked into the sitting room of the hotel where Jushin was going over plans of the building, and making a rough time schedule for the night. He handed it to Ichiro as he walked out, once again no words were exchanged. Ichiro read over the papers Jushin had handed him, then he handed them back and walked into the bathroom. When Ichiro walked out he was dressed entirely in black. He walked over to Jushin, for the first time in many hours the two brothers exchanged words. “Are you ready?” asked Jushin. “Yeah, about as ready as I can be for this. I’m trusting you out there, I need you to be with me at every second bro,” said Ichiro. “You know I’ll be right there with you. Just pretend like I’m walking right in front of you, just follow my movements and my instructions and I’ll have you in and out of there,” said Jushin. “But, I’m telling you, we have to do this right. There isn’t enough time to argue or to fuck around tonight, I have it planned out and as you can see it’s going to be tight. So don’t ask questions, don’t doubt me, not tonight.” “Not ever!” said Ichiro. He then smiled and placed his hand on top of his brothers. Jushin smiled back and nodded. Jushin then reached into his pocket and pulled out a small earpiece, he handed it to Ichiro, who immediately put it into his ear. He adjusted the volume, as Jushin spoke into it. It was quiet enough so only he could hear it, that’s how it would have to stay. With that Ichiro kissed his brothers forehead and walked out the door. He quickly crossed the street and clicked on the small fiber-optic camera that Jushin has installed in the suit. It would be the only way Jushin could see what Ichiro saw. The two of them were as one now. They would have to act together, work together and be at harmony with one another if Ichiro was going to accomplish this assassination and escape with his life. As Ichiro walked across the street, he shook all the negative thoughts out of his head, they did him no good now. He just walked across to the spot where he knew he had to begin from. Once there, he stopped. It was a small garbage chute, not used at night time, but the perfect way into a secured building. However, he was still a good twenty feet away from the disposal, just outside of the security camera’s view. He stopped and listened, waiting for Jushin’s instructions to come from the earpiece. “Okay, when I tell you, run straight underneath that chute, the shadows will cover you for the next sweep of the cameras,” said Jushin. “Okay, go now!” And with that Ichiro listened and he ran quickly across the twenty foot gap. He ducked, into the shadows and waited as he watched the cameras sweep past, not noticing him hiding in the shadows. Jushin came back onto the microphone and told him it was time to go again, and with that Ichiro leaped up and steadied himself in the disposal chute. Ichiro climbed his way up and saw the first exit, he tapped his ear piece and waited for instructions. “Not this one Ichiro, you need to go up two more,” said Jushin through the earpiece. With that Ichiro pushed his way up forward, and finally when he arrived on the third floor, he snuck out of the disposal quickly. He landed quietly, he looked around the darkened room, and realized where he was. “You’re in one of the small offices, you’re on the third floor. You need to get up to the thirteenth floor, it shouldn’t be too hard, your only big problem is that the twelfth floor is where the security offices are. Other then that, I see no guards patrolling anywhere near you, if you move fast, and wait for my signals, I will get you up to the fourth floor, as the guards come down to the third. So, get your ass to the stair way, quickly!” said Jushin. Ichiro didn’t have time to think, he quickly racked his brain, remembering where the staircase was located from this office, and he made his way there. And that was the trend, finally he was on the eleventh floor and situated in another small office cubicle. There was a lot of patrols being sent out, and it was starting to get sticky. “Alright, you’re in the middle of a small shift change. A couple guards are about to head home, but they do a routine sweep all the way down to the bottom, there is a big group of them, about seven. So get into an office quickly, hide out and wait until I tell you,” the instructions came through the earpiece and Ichiro followed them. He waited inside the room, and finally the order came, he moved on it immediately and bolted up the stairs. “Shit, you’re faster than I thought, keep going you can get up to the thirteenth floor quickly right now, just duck into an office as soon as you get there, security will be there soon,” said Jushin. Ichiro did as his brother instructed and when he reached the thirteenth floor he ducked into a small office just outside the staircase. He waited for Jushin’s instructions, it did not take long before Ichiro heard his little brother. “Okay, Rishahiro is still in his office, he is doing some work at his desk, in order to get into the office you have to go through a secretary’s office, but it looks like he sent her home for the night a long time ago, so you should be alright to sneak right up to the door. But, once you’re in you’ve got to be quick, he’s got an alert so get in there, off him, and get the fuck out of that building.” Ichiro did as his brother instructed him. He quickly recalled the floor plans in his mind and broke for the office. He quickly opened the door, and snuck inside, his target was so focused on his work, that he did not even notice Ichiro sneak in. Ichiro took his silenced weapon from his side holster, and took aim. He quickly made the shot, and his victim fell limp. However, on the way down, his head bashed the small emergency button, setting off alarms throughout the building. “SHIT! The alarms are going off, that room is going to be swarming with guards pretty soon, they are already running for the stairs!” said Jushin. “Just get me out of here, Jushin. Tell me what path to take and I’ll take it,” said Ichiro into the small mouthpiece. “Okay, it’s a long ass fall, but u should end up inside the garbage dumpsters outside. Look to your right, there is a painting, pull it off the wall and there will be a disposal chute, take it now, here they come!” yelled Jushin. Ichiro bolted for the side and threw the painting off the wall. He ripped the chute open and slid down it, he fell and fell, almost weightless as he was falling. Finally, there was a loud thud, and sharp pains ran through his entire body as he hit the garbage dumpster. The bags broke his fall, but he was still in a lot of pain, as he ran across the street. He snuck into the hotel, and quickly gathered Jushin. His thoughts were interrupted by the sound of the car horn, and Jushin’s yelling from the driver side window. Ichiro shook his head, so impatient was his brother. This month in PTC Tag Title competition would be a true test of their strength as a team, but they had worked together many times. Jushin had been his eyes before, and if need be, he was ready to trust him to the end of the earth. The brothers could work united, and with that none should defeat them. Ichiro stepped out the door, and locked the warehouse door behind him… The room was now completely dark, the television was off and quiet. There was nothing alive about the room now, nothing left to it at all. It was a silent, dead space. However, words still seemed to echo throughout it’s walls, and they spoke great words of wisdom to all who listened. IN UNION THERE IS STRENGTH. –Aesop
