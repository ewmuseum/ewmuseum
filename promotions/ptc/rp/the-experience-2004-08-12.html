The Experience!

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-12 A blackened river looms before this man, a river of questions and doubts. The landscape is brutal in ones mind as he stands in the turmoil of doubts. His mind only reaching a heightened conclusion that he and he alone must do what is necessary to win a title away from his stomping grounds. Doubting himself guides his path to causing pain and discomfort for his opponents. None of them he knows. He only knows one thing. That thing is to make sure he is the last man standing with the ultimate prize. He knows he will be a marked man if he wins it. Everyone will gun for him. He stops and listens as he ponders for a moment. He must concentrate on the task at hand, the survival to gain the ultimate prize. He can train physically all he wants to. In the end there is only the mental preparation that matters. The doubts begin to become clear messages to him. He turns and watches the black river before him. “There is a path here. I must find this path to gain what it is I want and desire. I look out across this wide expanse of river and realize that what I desire is the PTC Extreme Title. I have watched and learned and watched some more over these past few months. Concentrating on what could be my finest defining moment in the PTC. Do I have what it takes? Will anyone underestimate me like always? Then I want to know is it simple? Is the answer sitting before me and all I have to do is look down and grasp hold with both hands tightly. The questions are there, yet the answer still eludes me.” Quietly watching each wave and ripple upon the rivers surface this man before you begins to think of the path he is searching for. Has it always been here before him? Why is he so blind to it? Does he not have the fore thought of what is to come? All these questions toil through his mind as one constant remains. Survival of the strongest! He is one of the strongest and he wishes to bring it to his opponents first hand. His hand rubs his chin roughly as he watches the river once more. “My opponents are men each established and understood in each of their respected organizations. Each has strengths that should never be underestimated. Do they look at me the same? Do they begin to think I am an easy victory? Better yet do they think that I will simply cower before them? I say they have much to understand about me. To learn more about me you must understand the paths I have walked in my life. I believe none of them are willing to search and dig deep into my history. They may find things that would make a grown man cower in despair. I am watching closely and doing my homework. I will know who they are and what they have achieved. In the end the achievements mean very little. I will know my opponents regardless.” Stopping in silence he watched the waters, causing a ripple effect obvious only to him. Like a blow had been struck from an unseen enemy. He turns and grins sadistically. “Now the simple question has been answered for me. My opponents believe that I will be a man of no consequence in this match. Someone they will hold no fear towards. Do they truly believe that I as a man am an easy target? No gentlemen and I use the term loosely. Destiny holds many cards for me. The beginning card is dealt soon to be followed by the winning hand. Play your hand well boys. I am playing for keeps this time. There is something at the end of this path. Come hell or high water I will get this prize. “ Reaching down he lifts a small stone. Tossing the stone out into the river as its ripples become mixed and disappear in eddies and backflows of the river. Turning he watches the distant sky as his mind thinks ahead of what is to come. “Why do I do this I keep asking myself? Eight years I could have left the game. Ten years ago began this quest against all odds. What were the odds of me being here today? I say five years ago slim to none. Then again fate is a cruel mistress is it not? Perhaps it is time to address each individual in my match. Perhaps it’s best to understand their abilities and their own thought processes. It may bother you, but I have never been one to give a damn!” Pressing a thumb into the palm of his hand leaving a white mark he grins and then looks skyward. Suddenly someone comes to mind as he lowers his gaze leveling it across the river. “Sean “The Irish Bastard” McClancy a former champion in this match. Looking to regain past glory I believe. A man so desperate to regain his past he re-enters to be beaten and bloodied and hopefully to come out on top once again. Sean have you considered perhaps this could have been a very dangerous mistake for you. There are four other very hungry competitors coming after you. The rest of us have nothing to lose, but you have everything to lose including your dignity. Would it have been best to remain as former Champion and get the accolades for that? We shall soon see Mr. McClancy!” Smiling he looks about. “It is so nice to be able to be noticed by my opponents. I am sure I have your attention now. Each and every one of you is listening. Waiting to hear what I have to say. Next two I speak of are from the XUW I believe. DDT aka Damien D. Teri the first of two, Yes Mr. Teri I am not afraid to use your real name or to even draw your ire. Am I afraid of you? Not in the slightest inkling of your mind. I have a plan for you. Pain comes in full force against you and yours. The second of two is more or less like the first. Why do I even bother it is simple. I wish to make it known to my opponents on a name basis that I recognize who they are and are prepared for them. Joey Vendetti aka Joseph Macianno Vendetti. Your record seems to speak for itself. It is impressive, But not that impressive. You two gentlemen will deserve some extra special notice in the ring. I will enjoy giving the extra attention with a side order of pain for you.” Looking at his hands again he turns. Seemingly lost in deep thought he ponders. “Next we have men from 4WW jumping on the band wagon. Two more who have to bare close observation. ICE you seem to be a certain mystery from 4WW. You are simply there as an image and yet your nothing at all. You have a certain quality about you. Can you be a dangerous adversary or one who will simply be used as fodder to feed the hunger? Konichiwa Kyo Kushinaga the Masked Avenger. You do realize there is not much to avenge here Kyo. This is pure survival in this contest. You may have skills and abilities, but it may not be enough for you. Where do both of you gentlemen fit into this puzzle of survival? Only time can tell!” Wading out into the water Shaman places his palms flat upon the surface. The currents flow around his massive frame. Quietly he feels the waters rushing caress. “The WWA’s very own hardcore legend walks among you gentlemen now. I tend to show you why I have excelled in being who I am. Why I have been around so long. Why it is they call me the most sadistic son of a bitch to walk the WWA’s halls. You will come to know why the fans like to call me the Native American bad ass. In the end there will be one simple truth for you four. You will find no heroes here!” The camera fades as he disappears below the waters surface.
