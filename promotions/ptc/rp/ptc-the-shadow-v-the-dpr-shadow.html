The Shadow (XUW) v. The DPR (RPW)

24 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You are a cold, bitter man who holds a grudge for far too long and hates the world accordingly... Long ago, you used to be what people would consider normal. You were respected and maybe even admired by your peers back in the day, when as a youth aspiring for a grand and glorious future you were a hard and determined worker. But for you, those great things just never came. For you it seemed that greatness and happiness were always one step away, just one... but for some reason unknown and unexplained, every time you took that needed step, you still came up short. You are more successful than most in this world, but for you it means nothing unless you can have it all. You are incomplete in many ways. Every time your goal was taken away... every time you almost achieved success and missed it by the smallest of margins, you became even more bitter than before by your perceived failure. Whenever tragedy struck you or those close to you, you took the negative approach to dealing with it, in fact never truly dealing with it at all. Events that took place over a decade ago still scar you mentally and physically, and since you’ve never come to terms with the past, everything that happens now, even things of little to no importance, impact you in tremendous ways. In this world that you have forsaken and are convinced has forsaken you, you are rarely accepted anywhere and have lived with loneliness for so long that you actually prefer it over being in the company of others, even those who would desire your presence. You are a brooder in the truest sense of the word. But you have a talent, perhaps your only one. You have a masochistic violent streak. For whatever inner reasons or motivations you might have, you simply lust for violence whenever you can get it. To some people, getting high or drunk can be a release, a way to escape from the overbearing and often times unforgiving reality of life... at least for a short time. To some people, bungee jumping off of a two hundred foot bridge or skydiving would classify as providing a huge rush, causing the adrenaline to flow freely throughout their bodies and send an overwhelming feeling of excitement through their senses. For you, immersing yourself in violent acts provide both at the same time. You are at peace when you look down and see the blood of some poor bastard on your hands. Maybe because subconsciously you envision that blood to be a symbol of all you despise in this world, including yourself. Who knows, especially as far as you’re concerned. There was a term coined for your lust of brutality, and that term is the name of a faltering group of like-minded men you are aligned with, the ‘Ultra Violents.’ Being a part of that allegiance was the one time in your life that you felt like you were a part of something special. You were part of the group that dominated XUW for months, but at the height of your power tragedy struck and sent you into a state of bitterness all over again. That seems to be the way things go with you. You build up momentum for yourself. You crush the competition and defy any and all odds. You work up to a point in your life when you think that you can maybe once again become normal, and then as soon as you reach out to grab greatness by its neck, squeeze the life out of it, and claim it in your name, it is as though the good lord himself snaps his fingers and sends you once again plummeting back down to where you belong... with nothing, nobody, and even more problems to torture your already clouded and troubled soul with. Your stable is in shambles. The one person you had the most in common with in terms of bitterness of the world was able to overcome his hatred and become a better person, but in the process leave you down in the gutter, depressed and even lonelier than before. The more things change for you and the more you make good things happen for yourself... the more they stay the same. Nothing has really changed. Life for you is a miserable existence. You are a bitter man. You have isolated yourself from all others, and are now at a boiling point within. You need a release, and you need it now. You need to forget about your problems, at least for a brief time because right now in your life, the pressure is higher than ever before to be successful. You are so close... so close, yet so far away. You need one night of pure Ultra Violence to clear your head and calm your senses. You need the blood of some poor bastard to cover your hands so you can smile again. You are The Shadow. ................................................................................... “It was the best of times. It was the worst of times.” The familiar and haunting voice of The Shadow reverberates throughout the large, oppressive, dimly lit facility that The Shadow calls home. The Shadow sits on the grimy wooden floor while leaning back against a wall that has more than one hole in it the size of Shadow’s clenched fist. The Shadow is lightly tapping the back of his head against the wall, and he sits there assuming the posture of a defeated man, a depressed and frustrated creature who has no energy at all except for the small amount it takes to rap his head against the wall. “It can never simply be the best of times, can it? There always has to be a factor involved that balances the equation, doesn’t there? I have gained nothing... nothing.” The Shadow turns his head to the right and looks down, glaring at exactly one half of the XUW World Heavyweight Title which is placed beside him on the raunchy ground. Apparently having the belt placed in a muddled pile of garbage and fungus that reeks of excrement is an indictor as to how prestigious he considers the belt nowadays. But then again, The Shadow is also sitting in the pile of garbage and fungus build up, so that must be an indicator as to how he feels about himself as well. At one point in time not too long ago, this title was the entire focus of The Shadow’s life. He became obsessed with it so much... he was so convinced that if he were able to acquire it and hold it with honor, that he would be able to overcome the dilemma that is his miserable existence. But apparently it wasn’t meant to be... entirely. “I won the title. I emerged victorious and pinned the World Champion. That one moment in time was destined to be mine in the spotlight, my moment of glory when I knew that my life could be different, when I knew that I wasn’t a failure, but no... An idiot had to pin him simultaneously. An idiot would have to subsequently take a sword and literally cut the XUW World title in half. And the part that stings more than any other is that this idiot is not a credible name in XUW. This man is not known in any circumstance for winning matches, let alone World Title matches. This idiot was a nobody! My dream, my glory was taken away from me not by a warrior, not by a name to take seriously, not by a name I shouldn’t be ashamed of letting get away with this fiasco. No. He is a nobody!” The Shadow’s voice intensifies to the point of mindless rage as a grabs his long, damp hair with his hands and begins to pull several strands out in frustration. His glaring eyes never move away from what it is they are focused on, the .5 XUW World Title. When he looks at that title he sees nothing but embarrassment, his own personal embarrassment for coming so close to his obsession and yet failing... and also the embarrassment of knowing that the foolish Fatbadger is off somewhere, enjoying his half of the title. “Perhaps I simply wasn’t good enough to seize the day. Perhaps what my competition says this week rings true. I am but a ‘half champion, a man not to cause worry in a wrestling match...’ a wrestling match.” The Shadow mutters those final three words in a half mocking tone, discontent ringing out clearly in his haunting voice. He takes a very deep breath in an attempt to calm himself. “I joined PTC’s Extreme Division to escape from all of that. I joined this division in the hope of getting my violent fix and being accepted by other like-minded souls, much like I was in the Ultra Violents at one time. I wanted to drown out all of my sorrows in a pint... a pint of my blood intermixed with another’s on the canvas that would eventually dry, stain, and forever leave an aftermark of a consensual violent act done well. But I just couldn’t resist when the opportunity presented itself. I was so close that more thoughts of glory flooded my senses.” The Shadow’s eyes move away from the XUW Title and instead simply stare straight ahead blankly as he motions his hands slowly over the black mask that covers the majority of his face. “Thoughts of being normal again have even tarnished the simple joy I could have found here!” The Shadow continues feeling his mask which is a direct representation of something not normal in The Shadow’s life. He rarely ever removes that mask because he can not stand to look at his own face. He wears colored contact lenses not because he has bad eyesight, but because he cannot look into his own pure eyes. A man who is a failure in life and knows it... a man who thinks he completely ruined his life years and years ago and will continue to think so until he convinces himself otherwise, is a man too ashamed to look upon the man whom he let down so much, himself. And he will not consider himself otherwise until he knows that he is not a failure, until he is a true success. “The Extreme Battle Royal was my opportunity to immerse myself in Ultra Violence. It was my way out of dealing with life for a while, but I found myself surviving while those around me were slowly being eliminated. Thoughts of glory returned when they shouldn’t have! This was the one thing I had where I could be at peace... but now even THAT has been mangled. It turned into winning. I had to win the Extreme Rumble. Sean McClancy was the favorite. He entered last. I entered third... I had to decimate him and emerge victorious, not for any honor of consensual violence... but for the glory.” The Shadow shakes his head, almost in embarrassment of the statement he just made. “And I failed there as well... The battle didn’t even matter any more. Ultra Violence didn’t help me then. Thinking of the pain I inflicted on others and the massive amounts of strain I endured throughout that strenuous event, sadly, was the last thing on my mind. I just... I can’t help it anymore.... Which brings me to the here and now. Soon, I have my opportunity at redemption on both fronts. I have the chance within a month’s span to win both titles fully. Imagine that... then I would know... I would know that I’m not a failure. I would just know it!” The Shadow’s voice is elated slightly as he speaks of winning the titles. He is closer now to his dreams and fantasies than he’s ever been in his life. The next thirty days could be The Shadow best in over a decade, or they could be his worst... and the pressure is getting to him. Who knows how long The Shadow has sat in that same spot on the floor just thinking about the possibilities and daydreaming about how his life could change for the better if he were to become a champion in both XUW AND the PTC. But his elated tone drops quickly as he remembers just who he is. He is the man who let an idiot ruin his dreams in XUW. He is the man who came so close to eliminating the favored Sean McClancy and become the number one contender to the Extreme Title. He failed on both counts and he sighs profoundly. “But before I must do any of that, I am granted one more opportunity to escape from it all. I have you Roberts, all to myself. Win or lose, it is of no consequence. There is nothing on the line here for me... nothing... you have the chance to be my salvation for a short time Roberts...” It is unclear just when the Shadow stopped talking to himself aloud and started to talk to Roberts, but it all just segued together oddly and The Shadow’s cold and calculating gaze is focused right into the camera, right at Roberts whom The Shadow hopes is watching intently. “You may have been dead on with your witty little summary of me Roberts. In a wrestling match where stress can be high, I’ve been known to fail lately... You would not need to worry about me then... surely...” In a complete change of mood, The Shadow grins slyly and there is a sparkle in his evil eyes. One hand reaches into his leather jacket as the other one begins to convulse uncontrollably. “But Roberts... this is not an ordinary match, is it? Is it?” The Shadow slowly removes what has always been his trusty weapon of choice when going into a raging war, a lead pipe that is dull after the many years of bloodstains that have accumulated on it. This lead pipe isn’t really a weapon of The Shadow’s, but really an extension of himself, his own personal instrument of Ultra Violence. His hand is still shaking is he brings it up near his face. “This is my realm Roberts. This is an Ultra Violent playing field you have accepted to be a part of. Get this one thing clear... You picked the wrong division to be in to have Chris Storm as your trainer. He may be many things worthy of being called great. He may be a phenom when it comes to technical wrestling, but look into my eyes Roberts and believe me when I say that there will be very, very little technical expertise being tested in our little affair. Will the mighty Chris Storm be able to teach you how to attempt to mentally deal with the prospect of laying in the middle of the ring, helpless, while a mad man in a mask is wielding a lead pipe and pummeling you senseless with it while the referee, the oh so sad referee can only look on in pity? Is there a reversal or a suplex to counter that, Roberts! This is not the Global Title Division, and if Chris Storm were to face me in an Ultra Violent environment, allow me to proclaim here and now that he would be decimated ruthlessly. This is my realm Roberts, for me and those who are like me. He would not survive here... and if you would or not... that remains to be seen.” This is The Shadow of old. This is the man who has laid waste to countless souls. This is the man who came in fourth in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, only to be eliminated by disqualification because he was just a little too violent for their liking. This is The Shadow that the Dread Pirate Roberts gets to face in just a matter of days, this psychotic bastard who has only accepted this match because he needs an escape from the stress of life... because he needs to immerse himself in violence again, in blood again. The Shadow licks the disgusting lead pipe while clenching his fists. He then slams the pipe hard into the wooden floor, creating yet another of the many holes that have already been created in the rotten, decaying wooden floor. “But please, continue to indulge yourself and allow Chris Storm to train you. It couldn’t hurt in the slightest. Let his negative words fuel your fire to prove yourself on Sunday night. Continue to say that one sentence to yourself repeatedly. Wait, what was it again? Oh yes, I remember.” The Shadow cocks his head to the side as he mockingly quotes Robert’s words of inspiration. “We triumph without glory when we conquer without danger. I like that little phrase Roberts, I really do. But there is no glory to be gained by defeating me. I am not a champion... I am not the Extreme Champion. I am most certainly not a leader of XUW willing to lead a charge of battle into the realm of RPW. You will get no glory by defeating me... there will be no honor in besting a man who simply wishes to make you suffer... But there will be plenty of danger.” The Shadow’s sick grin intensifies as he continues to sit on the floor and stare into the camera. He has no need to sit up and do pushups, for he knows deep down how this whole affair of Ultra Violence is going to turn out. This is going to be the most fun The Shadow has had in months, since the Ultra Violents were in the peak of power and he had no worry in the world. He has no worry here, only an opportunity to lose himself in the drug he loves so much. The Shadow looks down at his infamous lead pipe, and then back at the camera once more as it slowly fades to black. The last image is an eery one, of a twisted and knowing grin from one of the most vicious men to ever step into an XUW ring... perhaps soon to be one of the most vicious men to ever step into an RPW ring. “Oh there will be plenty... of danger....”
