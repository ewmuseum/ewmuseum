Date: 2004-11-25 Craig stretched and limbered up half-heartedly. Getting out of bed that morning had been difficult. Showering and brushing his teeth had seemed an almost impossible challenge and the short walk through the corridors of the XUDF Arena to the ring had been almost torturous. Each day that had passed since his mother had died in a bomb attack on his mansion, a bomb set by Nurse Wretched, his grief had been dragging more and more at his soul. It was only his dogged determination to win and desire to briefly forget his pain that had brought him to the ring that morning. A figure stepped out from the shadows. “You showed up. I hope you’ve stopped all that crying and crap, you kept me awake half the night you know.” Craig turned to Nurse Wretched and with disgust dripping from his voice said, “Perhaps it was guilt that kept you awake. You must have a lot on your conscience now.” Exasperated, Nurse threw her hands up in the air, “Oh give me a break! Are you still going on about the bomb? Jesus, no wonder you never had any friends before me, you’re so boring, always on and on about how hurt you are. Get over yourself Craig, it was only a bloody house.” “And my mother!” Incomprehension creased Nurse’s face. She had never been one to value family and had never once cried over the father whose murder she witnessed or the mother whose eyes she gouged out in revenge. “Can’t we just forget about it and get on with the training? Tarrant ain’t going to pin himself for you, you know. That’s if you’re still serious about winning this thing.” Packing away his pain deep in his soul, Craig nodded. He wanted to win GTT4 so badly, or else all the heartache and loss would have been for nothing. There was a great emptiness inside him that needed filling, and this was the only way he knew how to do it. Nurse’s eyes wandered over Craig’s body as he completed his warm-up. How had she not noticed until recently that he looked so good? He was still on the thin side, but the muscles he had were as hard as rock and rippled gently as he moved. His hair had grown longer so that it flopped over his eyes - more due to lack of time for a visit to the barber than a style choice. As he bent over to tighten his laces, Nurse grinned with pleasure. She wanted him. The only thing that marred his appearance were his red-rimmed eyes and doleful expression. Nurse knew she was the cause of his desolation, and did feel a tinge of guilt about it. This was the man she’d fallen in love with, and seeing him like this hurt her. She wanted to comfort him, to protect him, to heal him, but every time she opened her mouth, some scathing remark escaped instead. “Are you ready Craig?” With a resigned sigh, he nodded and stepped into the ring. Craig had been Nurse’s sparring partner all through her rise in XUDF and until the beginning of GTT4, all his experience in the ring had been taking beatings and bumps for her. He knew what it was like to be pinned under her and held in her infamous thunder thighs hold, but now he was the one executing the moves, and she was the one taking the bumps. As Craig Irish whipped her into the ropes, he mused that this was maybe one of the reasons he had stuck with the tournament; the opportunity to throw her beautiful body around in the ring, to break her as she had done to him. The endorphins that this exercise released into his system lifted his mood. Feeling invincible and awash with adrenaline, he dropped an elbow on Nurse, almost crushing her face accidentally… or perhaps on purpose. Immediately remorseful, he rolled over to check on her. Angered, Nurse caught him and twisted her body until she held him firmly pinned beneath her. “What the hell did you do that for?” In pain, Craig moaned, “I’m sorry, it was an accident, I lost my concentration.” Breathing heavily from the exertion of the session, Nurse pressed her face close to his. She could almost taste the sweet scent of his sweat. Her face was an agony of protesting flesh. Nothing appeared broken and she knew she’d be lucky to escape with no more than a hideous swollen bruise. But what had really shocked her was the fact that Craig was capable of damaging her like this. Once, many months ago, when she had pushed him too far, Craig had crept into her bedroom with a six inch blade and the intention of killing her, but Nurse hadn’t been concerned then; she knew at that time that he wouldn’t be capable of the deed. Now that he had changed and grown so much stronger, would that still be true? A warm trickle running down her face from her temple told her that her skin was split. Craig had drawn blood, and that was unacceptable. Her face contracted into an ugly snarl as she slowly squeezed her hands tighter and tighter around Craig’s neck. This wouldn’t be the first time she had rendered him unconscious, but the emotions that rushed through her immediately after were brand spanking new. As the light dimmed in his eyes and his breathing slowed from frantic to that of a deep sleep, Nurse let out a high pitched wail and pulled his limp form close to her chest. Covering his face in wet kisses and salty tears, she apologised and screamed, “Why can’t you just love me!?” Pushing him away, she blindly ran from the ring, a desperate plan forming in the back of her mind. When Craig came to, he was still lying in the middle of the ring. Shivering, he slowly sat up to a rush of dizziness. As he waited for his body to stop shaking, his mind busily sorted out the memories of the events preceding his sudden nap. He really hadn’t meant to hurt Nurse, for even after all the pain she had caused him, there was still some small part of him locked deep away that usually stopped him from seeking his just revenge. He hated himself for being unable to purge her from his life, but also hated himself for hurting her. His face fell as in a moment of honesty he finally admitted to himself what was really going on – he still loved her. Despite her driving away his last girlfriend; despite the recent events that had left him motherless and homeless; despite her manipulation of him at every turn, there was still a niggling part of his soul that insisted Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched was its mate. His mind still reeling, he left the Arena to wander the dark city streets aimlessly. He had a lot of thinking to do. Nurse knew she should have brought Craig back to the locker room with her, but she couldn’t risk him finding out her dark secret. She knew there was only one way to tame Craig and protect herself from the rage that simmered so transparently in his every expression. She would make him love her again… whatever it would take. Opening the locked metal box she always had near to her, she arranged the tools she would need for this task, this communing with the dead, on the floor. Opening the first three buttons of her still pristine uniform, she took the key that hung from a chain around her neck out. With it, she opened the locker she had been welding shut just twenty four hours ago. “Well, Mrs. Goat, are you enjoying the afterlife?” Nurse laughed at her own joke as Craig’s elderly mother glowered at her from her metal prison. Her clothes were singed at the edges and her face streaked with soot and grime, but otherwise, Mrs. Goat looked very much alive and extremely vexed. Nurse ripped the tape from her mouth but left her hands and feet bound. “You Bitch! Where’s my husband? What have you done with my son?” Nurse smiled, “Your husband is fine. He’s in a nursing home recovering from the shock of your death. I think he likes it there, they have cable TV.” Shocked herself, Mrs. Goat exclaimed, “They think I’m dead? My family think I’m dead? How could you do that to me… to them!” Nurse shrugged and made herself more comfortable sitting on the floor by the open locker. “Oh hush! You’re barely missed! Craig cries on occasion, but then he always has been the sensitive type.” Mrs. Goat shook her head furiously, “He can’t still be with you! He must know you tried to kill me, he’s not a total idiot.” “Does it hurt to know that he thinks so little of you? That even though he blames me for your death he still can’t bare to be parted from me?” “My son’s not mentally well. He must have forgotten his medication, that’s all.” One corner of Nurse’s mouth lifted in a twisted grin as she slowly shook her head. “Mrs. Goat, as much as I like these cosy little chats with you, I’m a busy woman with a lot to do. So, let’s get down to business… tell me how to make Craig love me.” Mrs. Goat burst into wheezy laughter. “So, things with Craig aren’t all as fantastic as you make out! If you think I’ll help to deliver my son further into your clutches, you’re sorely mistaken, missy.” Nurse sighed and shrugged, “Well, I offered you the easy way first…” Rummaging in her case, she quickly found what she sought; a small vial and a syringe. “This stuff is very expensive, but very effective. It might interest you to know that your son bought it for me for my birthday. He’s so considerate.” Worried now, Mrs. Goat couldn’t help but ask, “What is it? What are you going to do to me?” Nurse tapped the syringe to expel the air bubbles and held the injection up to the light, “Why it’s a truth serum of course. Many people believe that drugs like these are merely a fictional tool used in spy movies, but if you have the right contacts, anything is possible.” Mrs. Goat pressed her slight body further back into the locker as the needle advanced towards her, but couldn’t escape its sharp sting.
