My Gold Medal is Gone… Big Deal

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 An arena full of freestyle wrestling mats, which none-the-less, are all full of young men working their asses off to make it to, from the looks of the bracket on the far wall, the next round of this tournament. The video footage is easily taken from a home video recorder, as the time and date are in the lower right hand corner of the footage reading August 9, 2000, and the picture is none too great. The camera focuses in on one match at hand, and then stops. A fairly large man steps in front of the camera, all smiles. 'Mr. Chairman, members of the Greek Olympic Committee, it is my honor to supply you all with this footage of my son, Christopher Kaden Calix, in his finest, on this here wrestling mat.' He says, with the smile on his face still plastered there. 'He's just recently turned 18, and I think it would be in all of your best interests to allow him to, in the near future, tryout for our fine countries Olympic team in the heavyweight freestyle division for the 2004 Olympic games right here in Athens.' The large man turns around after finishing watching a moment on his son's match. A few moments go by, and two men in business suits come into picture. 'Are you Alec Calix?' One of them asks. Stunned, the large man looks around, and with a worried smile says, 'Yes.' He takes a large gulp. 'Have I done something?' The other man in the black suit takes his turn. 'Of course not sir. We just want to ask you a couple simple questions. If that is OK with you?' He takes no time in cooperating. 'Sure! Shoot.' His face still filled with fear. Suited man number one then opens his coat and grabs something from the inner pocket. He unfolds the piece of paper and hands it to Alec. 'Have you seen this before?' The man asks. 'Why, of course I have. It's my son's birth certificate.' He says with no hesitation. Number two suited man then does the exact same, takes a piece of paper from his coat, unfolds it and hands it to him. 'What about this one?' He asks. Alec looks at it and seems confused. 'This is his birth certificate too. Is there something wrong here? What's the deal?' He takes another large gulp. 'Well, as you can see, the first one is his official birth certificate. Born May 2, 1982, which would have made him 18 years of age as of May of this year.' He finishes his statement, and Alec starts biting his finger nails on his left hand. The other man then pipes up. 'The second one is a duped copy of your son's birth certificate. It reads that he was born May 2, 1984. A whole two years younger than he really is. Now why on earth would you have needed to change his birth date?' The man asks. 'I… That… When… Um…' Alec stumbles. 'I'll tell you why Mr. Calix. Because you wanted to enter your son into the Junior Olympics held here over the summer, and being 18, he wasn't young enough to enter. Not to mention the fact that he wasn't old enough, long enough, to tryout for this years summer games in Sydney. You were in a bind, and desperately wanted to see your son compete. So what did you do?' The man asks. Before letting Alec explain himself, the other suited man answers for him. 'You duped his birth certificate, and for some reason the Junior Olympic committee here in Greece accepted your son with his fake birth certificate, and let him compete in the games, and even win that gold medal. Unfortunately for you, Mr. Calix, that gold medal will have to be confiscated now. So if you would please come with us, we've got a few things to straighten out.' The two men grab Alec, one on each arm, and drag him out of the shot of the camera. He doesn't go without a fight, as he flails his arms as much as he can, and starts to kick his legs. He knocks the camera over in the process, and after exiting the shot, we see nothing but a shot of his son, with a big smile on his face, watching his father be drug from the arena as his arm is raised in victory after his match.
