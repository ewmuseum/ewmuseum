TTD: Invincibleness~! -v- The Corporation

19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So let us recap, shall we? This whole mess had started not too long ago. Everything seemed all right within the house of Gabriel Brown and his best friend, Scott Matthews. Scott, for the first time in his life, was happy with Alyssa Sanders by his side. Gabriel Brown was not. No, he wasn’t gay or anything, Gabriel was all about the chicks. The young 19-year-old had his fair share of romps in the sack, but that’s really not the point. Though it’d probably be a more eye-grabbing topic than this, those are other stories for other days, mind you. In his eyes, this woman, a complete stranger to him, comes out of nowhere and changes the dynamic of he and Scott’s very relationship. Gone are the days of just the two traveling up and down the road together. Now SHE had to ride their coattails. This wasn’t some personal vendetta, some personal conquest for glory that Gabriel “Invincible Gabe” Brown was going through, heavens no. This boiled down to nothing more than jealousy. Though sometimes, the green-eyed monster was all it took to break up even the tight-knitest teams. Let’s face it: Invincibleness~!, given their tension right now, were very lucky to have made it this far in the PTC Round Robin Tag Tournament, but can they keep the last threads of the team alive one more time against the Corporation? Well, read the blasted promo, ya mook!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It's not easy when the world crumbles around you. It's not possible for everything important to you to come apart at the seams, is it? For the members of Invincibleness~!, it's not only possible, but it's true. Scott and Alyssa spent two hours looking for Gabe after he had stormed out of the Waffle House. Scott wanted to know what happened, Alyssa wanted to reconcile. Gabe? Gabe was so angry he probably didn't want anything but someone to blow up on. That's not unreasonable, but definitely not a healthy option. Scott drove along, looking, trying to pick out spots where he knew his friend might be. In the end, a bar seemed like a reasonable place. So Scott found him, sitting at the bar, looking intently at a glass of brown whiskey in front of him. Scott didn't say anything to him; it wasn't time for that yet. But he did need to know what had gone on. He waved the bartender over. "What'll it be, big fella?" the rotund man asked. Scott shook his head and motioned to Gabe, on down the bar. "How much has he had since he got here?" the man known as Rampage asked. "Just that one drink," the bartender replied. "He's been sittin' there for an hour and a half lookin' at it." Well, THAT’S nice, Scott thought to himself. This lout should be sent up the creek, given the fact he gives Gabriel some booze and HE’S ONLY 19 YEARS OLD! Ah, the powers of a fake ID. Scott nodded at the bartender, though. Right now, all he could think about was hoping to end this petty nonsense right now. But Gabe as the bar in the first place: not a good sign, nevertheless. Gabe had come in intending to drink, but instead, was too angry even to do that. He was just sitting there, almost as if waiting to calm down to start. Scott didn't want to waste time, angry or no. His big hand took the Invincible One on the shoulder. "Let's go, Gabe." "Not if that dumb cow is still with you," came the sharp reply. "Now," Scott said, using a tone that would allow no argument. His hand turned Gabe around and pulled him up off the barstool. Knowing his limits, Gabriel Brown walked out the door with the big man behind him, his head down and his hands clenched. What awaited him outside only rekindled the smoldering fire that was about to begin. Alyssa got out of Scott's Wrangler, her expression soft as she took a step forward. "Gabe, I..." she began. Sadly, that was as far as she got. “How many times do I have to spill it out?” Gabriel shouted. “I. HAVE. NOTHING. TO. SAY. TO. YOU.” There it was, as succinctly and harshly delivered as possible. “Just stop it, Gabe. Stop it!” She fired back. “We’re hurting Scott, you know this.” “WE’RE hurting Scott? No. It’s not like that at all. Me and Scott were doing just fine until YOU,” he poked her above her chest after that last word. “…Came along and ruined it all. You tainted our working relationship badly. You know how much trouble I nearly got in because of you ruining us? I was so damn mad from the damn bickering between us, I nearly stiffed the fuck out of one of those Lunchroom Patrol guys in the PTC Round Robin match…and I don’t even stiff people, PERIOD! I get smacked the fuck around until Scott runs in there and Hulks it up so don’t turn this on me at all. I’ve done nothing to Scott. ” Alyssa sobbed, trying her best to control whatever jealousy-filled flames had enveloped Gabe inside. “GABE, STOP IT! NOW!” she shouted. “I mean, I don’t know what your deal is. I come here, date Scott, you go nuts. The hell is your deal? Jealousy? Suck it up and get over it! I’d never do anything to hurt your relationship, either professionally or personally.” Scott stood back and watched this shouting match unfold until he heard words that he didn’t want to hear come out of Gabe’s mouth. “Damn it, I don’t know why I ever agreed to keep this damn argument on ice for so damn long! It’s obvious this ‘be kind’ shit ain’t working! Rampage, at that very instant, interjected himself into the conversation. “Wait, wait, wait,” he asked, demandingly. “What was that supposed to mean, Gabe? You’ve argued long before this?” Alyssa and Gabe looked at each other with a guilty look, then both folded their arms and turned away from Gabe. “Well…” Alyssa spoke up quietly. Scott knew the next words were gonna hurt with this new revelation coming to light. “We tried. Oh, how we tried to keep this on ice until we could get the chance to talk. But every time we did, the same damn argument happened. An endless shouting match full of everything from childish insults to some crazy, irrational rhetoric.” “It’s not irrational in the slightest!” Gabe shot back as he turned to Alyssa again. “And we don’t need this shit right now! We have a damn match to worry about, apparently against some fucking group from a WWE time warp called the Corporation, and all we’ve done is sit here and fucking argue!” As they continued to bicker, Scott thought that Gabe was right about one thing: the fact that the whole time this tournament was going on, there was no celebration. Just arguing. More shouting, more bickering. The tourney was the farthest thing from their mind, it seemed. Scott was hit the most with this excess baggage, and even the walls of a 6’6” 350 lb. man had to be torn down sometime as it was all that he could stand. Scott let out a primal bellow that hadn’t been heard out of him by Gabe’s ears in ages. The shouting match ceased instantly as both Gabe and Alyssa turned to him. “Oh, no,” was the only thing Gabe uttered. “What’s ‘oh, no?’” Alyssa asked him. “His damn rage complex. It eats the guy up, but I haven’t seen it out of him in such a long time.” Scott had heard this argument enough and now it was finally taking its toll on him. Every word exchanged between Gabe and Alyssa had made him reach a breaking point inside and now whatever was around him would feel his wrath. He spotted his Wrangler. His only mode of transportation, not to mention Gabe’s and Alyssa’s. He let out a scream as Gabe and Alyssa watched on in horror. He ran full speed at the jeep, holding his arms out like he was wielding a sledgehammer. The man known to many as a Manbeast in the ring smashed his massive arms through the glass, shattering the full windowpane into nothing more than rubble. He let out a low, feral growl as Alyssa let out a scream and Gabe, showing the first bits of protective instinct for his fellow man, stood in front of her. “SCOTT! STOP IT, YOU’RE FREAKING US OUT, MAN! CALM DOWN!” Scott was in full rage now. Throwing and smashing stuff was easy, but at the moment, he wanted to do something bad. He knelt, grasping the bottom of the dumpster at the end of the building and heaved, trying to tip it over. Then, the unthinkable happened: Scott "Rampage" Matthews cried out in pain and collapsed to the concrete. His shoulder, the one that had Violence Jack and blocked his spear on, had simply given up on him. Scott felt all too human again and it broke him. In that instant, his spirit broke and he sat down on the pavement, his shaggy head bowed, sobbing. Both Gabe and Alyssa moved towards him carefully. Alyssa hadn't ever seen this before. Gabe hadn't seen it in years. One thing they both did know is that like this, they had no clue how he might react. But as they neared, his reaction was an incredible shock. "I can't take it. I can't," came the gasping words. The man called Rampage wasn't so much a beast anymore. "You two, all the time. I've got nothing, you hear me? Nothing except the two of you." He stopped and took a trembling breath, tears falling from his cheeks. "I wanna go back in there and drink, Gabe," he said. That stopped Gabriel Brown cold. Scott only drank when it seemed like his mind was ready to collapse, when he needed to escape. He’d regressed to this level before, but now, he feared, it could be the end of everything they’d worked for. "I wanna go drink until I can't think, until I don't know what's going on." Alyssa's heart cried out for him, but she didn't dare go closer. His arms were wrapped around him, and trying to approach him now might result in a violent outburst. "You two, all the time. I'm so damn sick of my friends fighting each other. I'm so damn sick of the only two friends I have hating each other. I'm so damn sick..." he trailed off for a moment, "... of being me. I don't wanna be me anymore. I wanna drink 'til I don't know who I am. I don't wanna be me anymore." His head rose a bit. They could both see the tear-streaked face gazing at them, back and forth. "Let me go," he whispered. "Let me go." Slowly and shivering as well, Scott picked himself off the ground and looked at Alyssa and Gabe. Both of them, intentionally or not, had hurt Scott and now they were paying for it. He looked to the inside of the bar, his safe haven at this time he thought. His mind swirled around, trying to make light of the situation, but all he could see was black. Black and a pitcher of the coldest booze he could suck down out of a mug. Scott walked into the bar, almost trance-like. Leaving Gabe and Alyssa standing out there, all alone by themselves. It had seemed like the longest eternity before any one of them spoke. “What have I done?” was the only thing the 19-year-old Gabriel Brown uttered. And as Gabriel and Alyssa looked on in horror as Scott walked into that bar, the scene faded out.
