The poor Irish Man

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-30 It has been only but a few days since I escaped the flames. Despite liking the screams, I could not take being in the land of brimstone and fire for much longer. I needed to get out. I needed to smell the wretched stench of humanity once more. Luckily, there was one who was open for my presence. His faith was not yet strong enough and neither was his mind, so I slipped in easily through the television as he watched his favorite Saturday morning cartoon. I kept my location a secret until I had complete control of the boy. Once overpowering his soul, I put on the whole wacko possession shtick. I made the child act like Linda Blair on crack. It was a major spectacle that got the attention of all the local churches, including a young priest named Pietro. He was the pinnacle of physical fitness and wrestled in the independent wrestling promotion put on by his church. He became my prime target once he stepped in to perform the exorcism on the juvenile. Unfortunately for Father Geist, his will faith was not as strong as it seemed, as the lessons learned from his atheist father lurked in the back of his feeble mind. Therefore, when he finished the incantation to expel me from the boy, I quickly found a new home inside him. Unlike the child, I wasted no time corrupting Pietro. I would haunt his every waking hour until his soul finally crumbled from the force of my will. Lucifer does love when we send the soul of such a devote follower to hell. He thinks of them as prisoners of war. Anyhow, I played the role of Father Geist for a year before leaving the church on a “sabbatical” to try my hand at the pro wrestling business. The fools actually believed that I would go out and wrestle to show the power of the almighty god to the nonbelievers. They would believe the sky is baby shit green, if I told them so. Let the truth be told that I am using wrestling to expose the power of Father Pietro Geist and his church of Judas. The sheep will be herded with ease, as so many of the simple-minded tune in without fail to watch every explosive minute of their favorite promotion’s television show. Millions upon millions will watch me and with no trouble at all, follow me right into the flames. So, now I walk down this street and I wait for the moment when I must engage in battle with two souls. My mind would normally be pondering various ways I could pummel the pair, but the mouth-watering aroma of depression is in the air. It is like walking into a five-star restaurant and your nose obtains the scent of your favorite dish being made. You have to get it or your body would rebel against you. That is the best way I could describe the sensation of a sorrow-ridden soul to someone who would never know. My pet’s nose catches the scent just moments after and she looks to me with her bright blue eyes. “You know the drill, Dinah. Find me that soul,” I instruct the blonde female and she rushes away. Part of me wishes to smile, as the beautiful woman scours the area for the scent. She was one of the first “converted” to the church of Judas. She was such an easy target that I almost laugh now when thinking about it. She was that dumpy teen who was too obese and horrid looking that all of her peers viciously mocked her every day. Dinah had grown to the point where she believed that suicide was the only way out. I would have preferred the school-shooting thing myself, but beggars cannot be choosers. I stepped in one night and gave the same offer that I give everyone. She was so eager to accept that I thought her soul was going to race to the flames before I could grant her wish. However, in her haste, she forgot to read the fine print. You see, with her soul being sent below, her body in this realm becomes nothing more than a meat puppet for me. I usually just let them live their lives as normal with them never knowing that the pearly gates will never open for them. Yet, there are some that I keep for personal pleasure or service. Dinah is a prime example of that. Her beauty and loyalty is unrivalled and with her by my side, the promotions will have to give me more camera time due to the male demographic drooling over her. That, my friends, is appealing to the target audience. I know what the coffin stuffers want and I give it to them each and every time I am seen on the television. Sex and violence, a greater pair than peanut butter and jelly. “Master, the soul is located in the pub on the corner,” Dinah informs me with a devilish smile. “Then that is where we shall go,” I grin evilly and place my hand on the top of her head, as she bows slightly. We march down the sidewalk and soon make our way into said pub. My young servant opens the door for me and I remove my sunglasses. The place is emptier than Jessica Simpson’s cranium and the bored bartender jumps up with an excited look on his face. He looks like he has seen better days, but has not seen many people enter this establishment. The portly man quickly cleans the portion of the bar before him with a towel, as we walk over to him. Once brushing the back of my jacket away from me, I sit down upon a stool with Dinah quickly sitting alongside me. I tuck my sunglasses into the inner pocket of my long black jacket and lock eyes with the smiling barkeep. “What can I get ya two?” The red-haired bartender asks in a perky manner with a strong Irish accent. “She will have a bloody Mary and I will have a scotch on the rocks,” I reply with a quick fake smile. The chubby bartender rushes to make us our drinks and I notice how eager he is to please the two of us. In no time at all, he places a pair of glasses in front of us. “A scotch on the rocks for the lad and a bloody Mary for the lass,” he announces with joy. “Anything else I can get ya?” “That will be all, sir,” I respond. “Nah, none of that sir shite. The name is Frank,” he informs us with a grin. “Very well, Frank. Now you tell me. What can I get for you?” I say nonchalantly before downing a large gulp of my scotch. “What do ya mean?” He quizzes me with a look of utter confusion. “I asked what I could do for you,” I reply and lock eyes with the red-haired man. “Telling from how happy you are to see customers and the lack of customers in here, I think you could use some of my help.” “What could ya do? Ya just a bloody priest,” he responds, probably thinking I am just playing with his head. “Looks can be deceiving, Frank,” I inform him before downing the rest of my scotch. With a simple snap of my fingers, the bell dangling over the front door of the pub jingles and several men and ladies enter. Frank rushes out from behind the bar and right up to the herd. Ray Charles could see the joy on his face. However, it is short lived, as another snap of my fingers makes the mass of humanity vanish into thin air. Frank looks back to me in shock and his jaw drops wide open. I turn on my stool to face the portly man and look down to the floor with my fingertips pressed together like I am Mr. Burns. “It is that simple, dear man.” I remark quietly. Hook… “But… But… But how?” he stutters into a question. “The higher power blessed me with the gift to help others,” I answer and stare deep into his eyes. “You too can reap the benefits of my gift. I could make this place the most popular tavern in the entire state. You just need to do one small thing.” Line… “Name it,” he responds quickly and scurries over to me. “I haven’t had a customer in weeks! If I do not get people in here soon, I will lose everything! I will do anything to keep this place going!” And sinker… “Very well. Come, pray with us and all shall be given,” I instruct the desperate man. “That all?” he asks with a shocked look upon his face. “Small things mean the most, Frank,” I reply. “One must always remember that. Now, shall you join in our prayer?” “I have tried everything else,” he explains, “so what have I got to lose?” What else to lose indeed… I can barely hide the smirk that wants to appear on my face, as he drops to his knees and closes his eyes with his hands pressed together palm to palm. I motion with my hand for Dinah to join him and she does without even giving it a second thought. I place my one of my hands on each of their heads and I lower my head. An evil smile slowly creeps onto my face as I now have this sucker on the verge of throwing his soul into the flames for money. Knowing that you have the soul in your grasp is almost as pleasurable as actually ripping it from their wretched body. This was surprisingly not difficult at all. However, he is Irish, so he would have swapped his soul for a lager and a sack of potatoes. Anyhow, a soul is a soul and I shall take them however I can. Hmm… I wonder if McClancy would just give me the win, if I make him such an offer. Perhaps I shall try it before our battle. There is no harm in buying a victory when vegetables are the payment. Alright, enough with the Irish stereotypes and back to this experience. “Now is the time,” I announce to the pair kneeling before me. A low growl begins to bellow from deep inside me and my eyes fade into nothing but darkness. Every lightbulb in the bar explodes and the only light that can be seen is a crimson glow radiating from my hands. A warmth teases my fingertips, as my growling becomes louder and louder. The glow from my hands expands, so it covers the two people before me. I unleash a deafening roar and pull my hands from their scalps with all of my power. My left hand feels nothing, as I already have Dinah’s soul, but my right hand feels as if it is sitting in a lake of scorching hot magma. The sensation is nearly unbearable yet I would not trade it for anything in this realm or the next. I can feel the man’s soul slithering up my arm and into my chest. My body slowly rises from the ground in a crucifixion pose and then, the energy of the bartender’s soul shoots into the ground like a cannon blast. The ground rumbles and splits, sending fiery light up through the cracks in the ground. It is done. His soul now resides in the flames. My body slowly lowers back to the ground, as the two lost souls rise back up to a vertical base with their eyes focused on me. With a wave of my hand, the entire bar returns to normal and I step to the bartender. “You shall have unrivaled success with your bar thanks to our lord,” I inform the “converted” man and place my hand on his shoulder. “Now, live your life and bask in the happiness my lord gives you.” The pub owner nods like a mesmerized zombie and with the blink of his eyes, he is returned to normal. My jacket is straightened with a single tug and I make my way towards the exit. Dinah rushes to open the door for me and I am about to step through it when the bartender stops me. “Wait a moment, Father,” he commands and I turn around to face him with an eyebrow raised. “What is it?” I ask callously. “Where do I go to pray and for services?” He answers with a question of his own. “Poor simple man, the church of Judas is not a place,” I explain to the portly man. “Unlike the other faiths, we do not need guidelines to live by or specific destinations where we have to pay homage to the lord. Do what you wish and pray where you like. My lord will give you what you deserve.” “Thank ya again, Father,” he says with a large smile, as a flood of people rush into the bar, and I reply with a quick nod of the head. The mass of humanity rushing in the door, parts before me like the red sea, as I head out of the door with Dinah in tow. We continue our trek down the sidewalk with renewed energy. There is nothing more invigorating than claiming a new soul. This brings my total to eleven for the day. Mmm… And just my luck, the sweet scent of sorrow is discovered in the air once more. It appears that I will make quota for the day. Dinah points to a church at the end of the street we are walking along. Looks like our next stop will be in enemy territory. That is just the thing to wrap up a good day at the office.
