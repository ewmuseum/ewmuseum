Blue gum, four smoked cigarettes, a jolly rancher wrapper, and what appears to be human urination

10 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-10 New York, New York! The capital of the world they say. The lights, the love, the money, the power, its the city that never sleeps. But when you’re East of Ave B and South of 14th you better fucking sleep. The night is rough and the hustle is hard. Learn young or be gunned. Opportunity is something New York City is famous for, but not when you hit the Alphabets, not in Alphabet City. You’re greatest opportunity will be full possession of a single street corner where one day you will either be arrested or have your brain splattered on a dirty stain glass window where the owner of that store will send the bill to your house before your family even knows your dead. Sucks don’t it? Nobody ever leaves the Alphabet, five blocks away from a new world but five decades away from even giving it a glance. Where does this leave me? I don’t know, I just am. Eighteen years old and I’ve been doing the same shit since I was seven. I go to school, then I go to work, then I go to sleep. That’s all there is to do unless you want to fill your time selling E, slapping bitches, and doing time. I’m satisfied; my only complaint is that I’m alive. I won’t kill myself though; my mother would die in awe if I dare do a thing that goes against the words of almighty Jesus Christ. I wouldn’t kill myself anyway, that’s the type of shit them spoiled suburban white kids do for attention. Well fuck them cause they dead. When you die you die, nobody remembers you, well not here at least. Gangs try and make it seem like you’re their boy or something when you in, but they won’t wait till a day after you die before they start fucking your sister and living in your room free of charge. You can’t trust anybody here. All you can ever do is hope that something will eventually change, but in the back of your mind you know it never will. You just are and you got to deal with that. I did. I’ll give you my story, it isn’t much of a story but I’m sure you’ll listen. I mean come on; this is the shit people live for. Listen to someone else talk about their fucked up life so you can go home and feel good about yourself. Well guess what, you’ll feel like gold after I’m done talking. I don’t care though I can’t change it. From as long as I could remember I’ve been living in the same house, with the same people, with the same problems. When I was younger I was happy I guess. I would play with my friends, ride my bike, play all the sports, I didn’t know any better. You can stick a young kid is a fucking war zone and they’d make a good time out of it. But as I got older I lost those feeling. I didn’t feel joy or happiness cause there was nothing to be happy about. I always told myself I would never go into the drug scene or be involved in any crime, and I haven’t. What I have done is watched everyone I’ve ever known do it. They rake in the cash, they become too good for you, or on the other end they become users and they grow weak, fully dependant on you for survival. Both ways they eventually die and with each death I lose tears. I don’t even care anymore. I watch TV sometimes and see these kids my age so happy, ready to go to college and to start their own lives. I don’t even know what that is, that to me is fiction. I go to school, hell I’ll probably even graduate. That doesn’t mean shit though. You see once I have a diploma I could either do one of two things. I could look for a job in the Alphabet where every naïve uneducated shop owner will look down on me for trying to make something of my life. Or I can go to the big city and look for work, even though nobody will look past the third line of my resume before it hits the bottom of a garbage pale accompanied by a smoked cigarette and Big Mac wrapper. Nobody wants anybody from the Alphabet, which is why nobody leaves the Alphabet. Now my family is all I really have, but even they I don’t care about much. My father never existed, must be a real surprise eh? The only fathers in the Alphabet are the ones that don’t know it yet. My mother thinks she’s the Virgin Mary. Her room has more icons and saints in it then the Sistine Chapel. She never really talks to me; she just kind of speaks to the Lord in my presence about me. She’ll do something like: Dear Lord, Show this boy the way, let him be with you in his hard times and pierce your wisdom in his heart. She’ll say it in Spanish of course, and at a rate of 20 words a second. Oh yea, I guess I forgot to mention I’m Columbian. Yea I know, coffee and drugs. My sister is the other end of the spectrum. She’s two years older then me and has probably slept with any guy I know. The only times she’s home she’s having sex on my bed and trying to steal the little money I have. She got three sons under four, all destined to a world of nothing. I also live with my uncle, one of those real right off the boat fuckers. Always tells me how I’m not proud of who I am cause I’m not representing my people. Yea he’s the type who bullshits all day at the bar and thinks the Alphabet is a great place, when in his heart he knows he’s living in piss. What do we do for money? Well we get a check from the government periodically and that’s about it. I work at a Keyfood Supermarket where my pay is equivalent to one of those fucking starving Indonesians making Puma sneakers. I have nothing going for me at all. And I never have. I’ll never have a happy ending and I’ll never get that storybook romance. Only bitches I’ve ever cared about are dead, pregnant, or suck dick for a living. Men here use bitches for sex, nothing more. I can’t say I don’t take part in it either. I’m down for a quick fuck and some meaningless head. You’ve got to be, cause that’s all you’ll get. I live for the title, cause it sound better then dead. I sleep, I eat, I breath. I just am…. I’m Javier Mendez, the name don’t even matter. *Sigh* Javier backs away from his keyboard and takes a heavy breath as his eyes quickly scan through the text that lies in front of him. The fuck you doing! Her voice sounds abrasive and you can hear the d sharply roll off her tongue. I’m typing Angie, what chu want? I don’t want nothing, I’m just surprised you doing somtin other than looking at the Big Tittie Patrol. You afraid I’ll see you in it again. Go get laid motha fucka! That’s how our conversations usually went. Then she storms out and tries to think of something vindictive to do to me. I didn’t care though. I never care. So I shutdown my laptop… No I didn’t fucking steal it, it fell off a truck. I’m playing that’s just too much work. I bought the shit for twenty dollars from this crack head transvestite with a glass eye. You see some crazy shit in the alphabet. The laptop doesn’t do much, the shit is like ancient, but I can type on it. That’s all I need. I then decide to go sit outside for a bit and get some fresh air. My room was hot as hell and I was getting that crammed feeling. It just is not your fucking day when you start getting a dry sweat in your own damn house. Blue gum, four smoked cigarettes, a jolly rancher wrapper, and what appears to be human urination, mixed in with an assortment of debris that I can’t even name. So much for sitting on my stoop. So I go for a walk, walking is good, just watch your step cause New York City Sanitation don’t clean THESE streets. I remember a teacher in school once told me about how in Medieval Times everything always smelt like shit and piss and people became used to it. I don’t get used to it. As I walk I pass the basketball courts near my apartment. You can’t miss the big green “Environmental New York: Keep the City Clean” sign with “Suck my dick, Zinasia” tagged right underneath. Gets me every time. Not enough to laugh though, shit takes too much energy. You gotta love these courts though, I don’t know why they even mention basketball on them; it’s everything but. See the city thinks they can make an environment to tame the animals, but the animals can never be tamed. They put a cage net on the rim so nobody hangs on them. They don’t either, that was until some motherfucker ripped it off last week and beat a hobo with it. They paint the courts nicely with all the lining perfect, just so some niggers and spics can tag their own colors on them and make half a court their “territory”. Not like anyone plays basketball anyway. It’s just used as a meeting place for drug trafficking and occasionally the local public school will have their recess there. You gotta love when little kids are walking around holding sterile needles and thinking that a used condom is just a balloon with a treat inside. It’s pretty fucked up. I wouldn’t even take a deep breath inside that cage without getting tested for syphilis afterwards. The best are the New York City college students who come in the courts to get their weekly fix of weed and oh gosh sometimes even crack. I feel bad for them though; they come in seeming all tough and confident when you know that they’re really shitting in their pants. They all make one mistake. They pull out their wallets. Anyone in the alphabet knows that a wallet is something you just shouldn’t have. If you got to carry money you carry it in your deepest pocket, the same pocket with the knife you better plan on using if anyone touches that money. When a dealer sees a wallet, he sees an opportunity to teach you a lesson. I don’t say shit to them though; I don’t give a shit about them enough to. You see everything is a lesson in the alphabet, your probably thinking “What lesson are a bunch of no good spics, niggers, and drug dealers gonna teach me.” Well you’d be surprised. There isn’t one fucked up thing that they won’t do. And don’t depend on police to help you out, they don’t bother us at all. They probably figure “Let the animals kill their own”. It’s a jungle, full of nothing. Well it’s getting pretty damn hot out here also. This fucking sun is just killing me today. I guess I’ll get my ass back home. Try and get some sleep, maybe I’ll forget where I am. Doubt it though, but I don’t care.
