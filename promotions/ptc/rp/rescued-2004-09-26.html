Date: 2004-09-26 “You stupid son of a bitch.” Tony Stecchino snaps awake, looking up into a blinding sun, with a figure standing over the top of him. “Wha?” Tony replies, he begins to sit up, he grabs his head, it feels like he had been drinking all night and then some one slammed his head into the brick wall. “Damn, I wont ever drink that much again.” Tony stammers as he tries to stand up. A hand pushes him back down to the ground. “Sit!” The voice says again, Tony begins to wonder who this is talking to him, voice sounds familiar, but there is this buzzing or chopping noise in the background. “What the hell is that noise, it’s killing my head. Is it the air conditioner? Turn it off, god damn.” The voice chuckles at Tony’s last comment, there are some other scattered laughs behind the dark figure in front of him. Tony closes his eyes, his head is swimming, his whole body feels like it is swimming, like he is going up and down with the surf just off the beach in a little boat. Speaking of his body, it’s all tight and stiff, like it just had one hell of a work out. He tries to move his arms up, sharp pain goes through his shoulders, he gives up. Figures he will take a hot bath and do some stretching. “You know you could have died?” The voice says to Tony. “Yeah, but I didn’t I just drank too much and then went on a weight lifting binge or something. Damn I hurt.” There are a bunch of laughs from his statement. “Where the hell am I at?” Tony asks to the people who are laughing at him. No one has laughed at him in a long time, he would usually shoot some one for laughing at him. It’s a sign of disrespect, but obviously these guys either know him really well or they are gonna kill him. “Well Tony, you are on your little private island out here in the Atlantic. You came out through here in a middle of a hurricane in your sixteen foot ski boat. Not a smart move. You left me treading water in the marina.” The voice kind of chuckles. “Stevie?” “Yes Tony, we figured you would be here, but we couldn’t get out into the water until most of the hurricane had gone through, so you had to sit on this island probably for a few days.” Tony opens his eyes again trying to adjust to everything that is going on around him. He focuses on the dark shadow standing in front of him. He knows it’s his friend Stevie now, couldn’t really tell with the ringing in his ears, the noises in the background, he was confused. “Hey Stevie,” Tony whispers to his friend, his voice course, full of pain. “Yes Tony.” ”Was she here? I know it’s a long shot, but was she here?” Stevie looks down at his friend, looking over his cracked skin on his face and up body, where the salt had dried him out. “She could have been here, it looked like someone came here, made a little fire. There was a picture of you and her, wedged under a rock.” Stevie takes his eyes of Tony looks back over the sea; he sticks his hands in the pockets of his Dockers. Tony looks up at him, tears welling in his eyes. “She misses me Stevie, I knew that she did, I wonder how much I missed her by.” Tony closes his eyes, he could be close, she could have left days ago. She could be looking for him or just missing him enough where she could be ready to come back to him, they could restart their life together, everything could get back to normal finally. “Are you good enough to walk?” “Huh?” Tony replies as he looks up at Stevie who holds a hand down to help is friend up. Tony reaches up and grabs his friend’s hand up and pulls himself up. He stumbles a little bit as the blood rushes to his head, Stevie wraps an arm around him from keeping him from falling down. He nudges Tony forward through the Coast Guard EMTs toward the raft that will take them to the boat. Tony closes his eyes letting Stevie guide him down the walkway of his former residence. He had some great times there with Melissa, though they only owned it for a month before she left and he burned it to the ground. But it felt like a home. He should have never burned it down. Tony collapses into his former flower garden, Stevie looks worried, he yells for the EMTs to come and help him, Tony sees Stevie’s face right in front of his. It’s fuzzy and gets fuzzier. He closes his eyes and opens them again. It’s black. He closes his eyes and shakes his head, opens them up again. It’s still black. “Tony?” A distant voice of a woman beacons him from the darkness. Tony pulls himself up from the ground; he dusts off his black sweats and looks down to where he was. Black. He walks toward where he thought the voice had come from. WHACK!! “Son of a bitch!” Tony reaches up and grabs his nose with his right hand, he just ran into something, but what? He pushes his left hand out in front of him. Feels smooth, like glass, he tries to peer through it. The surface is black; he smirks and pushes back, looking into the black around him. “Hello” Tony calls out. His voice echoes. No answer. Tony shrugs, turns to his right and starts walking forward with his left hand touching the black surface to his right. This has to be another dream he thinks to himself. He closes his eyes, trying to concentrate. “Tony.” The voice calls from the darkness again. “Tony.” Tony spins around, the voice coming from behind him this time, he opens his eyes. Nothing, just black. With his right hand on the surface he walks back to where he was going. He swallows the lump in his throat, not knowing what to expect. Dream or not, fantasy can easily be scarier than reality. “Who is out there?” Tony calls into the blackness ahead. “Why am I here?” Silence surrounds him again, its force heavier than the blackness that consumes him. Unsettling, but soothing at the same time. He continues forward, still not knowing what to expect. So many images have been burned in his mind through his twenty five years of life; anything could be in front of him. Victims of his past hits, people he drove to suicide by destroying their family, or Kurt Cobain. He closes his eyes again, focusing, trying to figure out where he is going, what he is doing, why is he here. He opens his eyes again. It was bright. He looks up at the ceiling of a boat, it rises and crashes over the surf. He moves his head slightly to the right, looking at Stevie who is looking out of the window. He closes his eyes again. “Tony.” The voice calls again. “GOD!!! Who are you!? What do you want!?” Tony screams into the darkness, unknowing, almost unwilling to be here any longer. “Why do you keep torturing me like this?” Tony sobs, dropping down to his knees. He places his face in hands as doubles over, his hands and face inches from the floor. A hand touches his shoulder softly and then disappears. Tony shoots up and looks around, nothing, he swipes the air around, touching nothing just the air and space surrounding him. He falls and lies flat on his back. Looking up into what he assumes is infinite darkness. He covers his face again with his hands for a moment, sighs and lets his arms fall out perpendicular to his body, forming a T in what could be zero gravity. Nothing made sense anymore. “Mom?” Tony inquires. “I’m sorry I killed you, but you nearly killed me hundreds of times.” He says softly into the darkness, almost patronizingly. You tried to drown me in scalding hot bath water you bitch! You deserved to die!” He voice cracked, with passion and remorse. He loved his mother, she did good too, it wasn’t all bad. She tucked him at night, scared the monsters away from under the bed, sang him to sleep, cooked him meals. Can’t complain about a woman who cooks a good meal. Especially a good Italian meal. “You’re wrong.” The voice. Tony shoots up propping himself up with his arms behind him. He looks around. “What do you mean I’m wrong?” “I am a family member, but not your mother.” “Why don’t you tell me who you are?” ”You killed me and I don’t want you to ever know who I am. You deserve the torture for the rest of your life.” The voice says almost crying. The voice having distinction for the first time. The voice of a little girl. “But who are you? The only family member I have killed that was a woman was my mother, that was it. Never met my either of my grandmothers. Never had a niece or a nephew, or an aunt. Or a sister.” “You think about that for a long while. Wish you could see me on the other side so we can actually meet, but too bad.” “Too bad?! Too bad?! That’s bullshit!” Tony screams at into the space. He closes his eyes as tears begin to well up in them again. “Please tell me who you are.” He says gently. “No.” The voice soft and subtle, soothing him. Tony opens his eyes. He looks to his right and sees Stevie Nickels still looking out the window at the sea. Stevie senses that some one is looking at him and looks over at Tony lying in the middle of a bed, IV tubes running out his right arm. He turns around looking at his friend. “How was your nap sleepy head?” ”Stevie, do you know if I ever had another female family member beside my mother and grandmothers?” He squints looking into his friends eyes, begging for an answer. “No, I don’t think so. Why do you ask?” ”This little girl in my dream, said she was a family member that I killed. I don’t know who she was.” Stevie looks down at his friend, knowing that he was probably a little crazy, he had been through a whole lot over the past three days. So mentally he couldn’t all be there, physically he couldn’t all be there, especially with Kasper Sky tomorrow night in the GTT tournament, and Tony was not in a good spot right now. “What did she look like?” Stevie asks. “I don’t know what she looked like. She was shrouded in infinite darkness, there was no light. It was all dark, couldn’t see a damn thing. Though I wish I could have seen her.” Tony rolls his to the left, watching the clouds pass by above him. “Hmmm. I don’t know what to say.” ”Don’t worry about it Stevie, we’ll know one day.” Tony says as he closes his eyes. To Be Continued.
