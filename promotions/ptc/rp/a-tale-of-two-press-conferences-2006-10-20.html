A Tale of Two Press Conferences

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 FUSE KillZone PTC Los Angeles, CA He’d done it. The cameras had clicked off, meaning FUSE’s KillZone PTC broadcast was off the air, but the celebration continued nonetheless. It wasn’t Almasy’s usual celebration, done solely to rile up the crowd after a big win. No, this one was as genuine as they once been, before Seymour had become jaded. Before he’d joined Dies Irae. Before he became a joke who lost to rookies in GCW. Almasy tagged hands with fans as he staggered up the aisle way, letting them touch the richest prize Primetime Central had to offer. It didn’t quite seem real yet. The woman still recovering in the ring had owned him like no one else in his career ever had. Once again, he had thrown the book at her, and she had survived it all…with a smile, no less. And then the punishment continued, to the point where he was done. Finished. A few months ago, the Texas Cloverleaf taught to her by G. Ian Childress would have been it. A few months ago, he would have been hung on the Fool’s Gallows…but not tonight. And then, Levin Bolt caught and turned into a move he hadn’t used in two years was enough to finally, FINALLY keep Lia Radilu Vlaszy’s shoulders down for three. "I did it! I really did it," he screamed, to a young child who was happily patting Seymour on the shoulder. As much as Seymour hadn’t wanted to admit it, he missed this. He missed the adoration of the crowd, missed people cheering when he won. In GCW, he wouldn’t get a reaction like this: not now, and probably not ever again. Still, that wasn’t enough to cheapen this victory, this moment. As he continued to celebrate, the sweet sounds of "Highbrow", Motoi Sakuraba’s epic final boss theme from Star Ocean: Til The End of Time filled his ears. It was one of his favorite songs, and having it here for his victory only made things all the sweeter. Finally, he reached the stage area, reluctantly parting from the fans. There was much to be done now behind the scenes. Having held the title twice before, he knew well the media blitz that was about to begin. For once, he was actually looking forward to it…but reporters and press conferences couldn’t compare to this moment. Pausing just before disappearing behind the curtain, he turned to face the ring and the crowd in the jam-packed Staples Center, before taking a deep bow and raising the Global Championship overhead one last, glorious time. The Final Fantasy soaked up the cheers, before finally leaving the friendly confines of the arena, and walking through the curtain. There, as he knew there would be, were the backstage employees, applauding. Almost immediately, one stepped out from the crowd, grabbing Seymour by the elbow. "This way," the FUSE tech said, "the press conference will be held in one of the media rooms." "Hell yeah!" Almasy exulted, the atypical language indicating just how pleased he was right now. "Did you see the match, man?" "I did," the tech responded. "It was a great one." "She HAD me," the still slightly incredulous new champion said, clutching his prize ever-tighter. "She fuckin’ had me dead to rights, and I still won!" "I thought it was over when she had you in the middle with that Texas Cloverleaf," said the technician, while guiding Seymour through the labyrinth of passages that was the backstage area of the Staples Center. "When she dragged you back to the middle, I mean." "So did I," Almasy replied, shaking his head. Really, he HAD thought it was over, then and there. He’d thought himself doomed to lose to Lia, again…and worse yet, to a move that she had used for a purpose. Lia thought him a fool for his actions…and she had come within about two seconds of proving it…had it not been for those who still wished to see Seymour triumphant. "The fans saved me," he admitted, finally starting to come to grips with it all. "Their cheers…it helped me dig down, fight off the pain just long enough to make the ropes. And then I saw her go up…she’s beaten me with that damned Levin Bolt before, so it was just a matter of catching her, and then I basically pulled the Pulsar outta my ass." The technician was excited, perhaps inordinately so. Maybe he was an Almasy fan. Maybe he simply enjoyed good wrestling. Or maybe, like so many of the techs that Seymour had come to know over a five year career, he lived vicariously through those who could make a living in a career that they simply lacked the physical tools to achieve success in. Whatever the case, Seymour enjoyed talking about his work, especially with those who enjoyed it as much as he did. "We’re here. Knock ‘em dead, Seymour." With a sharp pat on the back, the tech was gone, and Almasy stepped into…pandemonium. Flashbulbs went off, nearly blinding him as he stepped into the media room. Thankfully, another technician was there to guide him to his spot at the podium. Once properly situated, even more cameras flashed. Like the true pro he was, Seymour shifted the belt to one shoulder for some pictures, then to the other for more, finally holding the belt aloft for the final set before the din calmed enough for him to speak. "Wow…three times," was the first brilliant thought out of Almasy’s mouth. "If you guys don’t mind, I’ll just talk a bit about tonight to start, and then answer any questions you have. Sound good?" Given no objections, he took a deep breath. What he said would be published in wrestling newsletters and hidden crannies of newspaper sports sections the world over. How could he sum up the joy he felt? How could he properly express the cornucopia of emotions running through him? He didn’t think he could, but he had to try. So he simply began to speak. "First off, let me just say I’ve never been so happy to be 1-6 against someone in my life." The line gets a few cheap giggles, as Seymour shrugs. "Tough crowd. It’s the truth though. To be perfectly honest, I was nervous as Hell out there tonight. She’s beaten me every time we’ve been in the ring, and pretty badly too, so I was surprised when I got in control early. Then she came back and did her usual killing of me. I got off Naught’s Approach, and damn near cried when she kicked out of it. You all saw what happened from there. She put me in that damned Cloverleaf, and I was done. The fans helped me keep alive just long enough to survive, and make the ropes. Levin Bolt attempt, caught, Shockwave Pulsar, done. Finally." The relief in his eyes was evident. No matter how many times he related the story of the match, he still didn’t seem to believe it. The match was supposed to end with him losing, continuing his downward spiral. He knew it, Shadow knew it, and it seemed like the entire world except the FUSEiacs in the Staples Center knew it. But it was those brave souls who had given Seymour what he needed to win. "Shadow’s an incredible wrestler," he continued, nodding his head. "Every time I step in the ring with her, I know it’s going to be an absolute war. We may not especially care for one another anymore, but I will always have respect for her as a competitor in the squared circle. Again, thanks to the fans for basically willing me onto victory, and out of this dry spell I’ve been having lately. Up until Infinite Gauntlet 23, I had zero momentum. With that, and now winning the Global Title again, I feel like I’m on top of the world. I‘ll take questions now." Almost immediately, one man stood, the PTC logo emblazoned on his chest. "Steven Saunders, PrimeTimeCentral.net. With all of this momentum behind you, are you ready for GTT6?" Patting the title belt on his shoulder, Seymour shakes his head. "Geez, you cut right to the chase, don’t you? Can’t I have some time to enjoy this? No? Okay then…" The Final Fantasy chuckled. In spite of his little pathos play, he had expected the GTT6 question to come up early. After all, he had never made any big secret about his love for the annual tournament, and this year, he could choose his own destiny. "Absolutely I’m ready for GTT6. I have all the momentum I could want. Two huge wins in the space of two weeks, and most of the doubts that I had earlier are gone. I was debating even entering this thing a few months ago, and now I feel like I can win it. Just like in GTT4, I’m going in with a gigantic target on my back as the PTC Global Champion. Hopefully, this year goes better than GTT4 did." GTT time, for Seymour, tended to mix professional success with personal tragedy. Though, the more he thought about it, that seemed to be true for everyone really. He’d never understood it himself, always chalking it up to the stress that surrounded such a hugely important event in his line of work. Well, he’d left the door open. Seymour scanned the crowd, waiting to see who would ask the only other question that really needed to be asked. As it turned out, it was a man named Star Reynolds, from WrestlingTattler.com. "Seymour, have you picked your first round opponent yet?" "Actually," Seymour replied, smiling as he saw the crowd of reporters reach for their pencils and pads of paper, "I have." He gave the crowd the proper amount of time to prepare, and then spoke, a slightly full-of-himself grin on his face. "At first, I figured I’d do what Rich Rollins did last year…and pick a big name to take out. As of two days ago, I was going to announce that I’d face that family-murdering, wife-stealing waste of humanity round one. But then, I realized something. Something that had been bothering me ever since that fateful Rollins/Deville match last year." Whether it was true anger at Rollins’ decision or simply retconned hatred of the Renegade being used to justify his position didn’t matter. Seymour was angry, and when emotional Almasy could go on tirades with the best in the business. "Rollins made his pick months in advance! He’d been preparing for Angelo Deville for MONTHS, without Deville having the same chance. I’d certainly HOPE Rollins could beat Deville if he had months to prepare for him! The fact of the matter is that if Rollins had fought Deville over the course of the tournament, with no special preparation, he’d have gone down. Hard." Predictably, the pencils began writing out the newest stage in the Almasy/Renegade war. Seymour knew that everything he said was being recorded (and possibly inflated in the process), but he simply didn’t care. "So you know what? I’m not going to be a coward like Richard Rollins. I’m not going to give myself an advantage over someone. I’m too good for that. So you know what I’m going to do? That chance I won in IG 23…consider it null and void. I’m putting myself back in the draw. It does not MATTER who I’m facing first round. My tournament record speaks for itself. Whoever it is had best be prepared for an early exit, though, because as I’ve told you ever since GTT4…I simply don’t lose in tournaments. ESPECIALLY not early." "But what about Archib--" "Finish that question," Almasy stated, angrily, "and you’ll be walking home with a camera shoved up your ass. Now, any other questions? No? Good. Take care, gentlemen." With that, the Final Fantasy stomped down from the podium. Inwardly, he reproached himself. Even a year after the fact, the gashes that Rich Rollins (and Archibald MacGregor, for that matter) had left in his life were still fresh and angry, still welled up with blood. Rich Rollins had ruined a perfectly good evening for him, an evening in which he’d been able to embrace those that still cared for him, and had been able to capture lightning in a bottle just long enough to reclaim the PTC Global Championship. As it turned out, though, it would be his last good night for quite some time… ----------------------------- GCW Dangerous Games Thomas & Mack Center "NA NA NA NA, NA NA NA NA, HEEEE-EEE-EEEYY! GOOOOODBYE!" "NA NA NA NA, NA NA NA NA, HEEEE-EEE-EEEYY! GOOOOODBYE!" This…was NOT. FUCKING. HAPPENING. Not after everything he’d earned in Los Angeles. Not after becoming the PTC Global Champion again. He was NOT being shitcanned on worldwide PPV by Steven Caldera. GCW security, lead by Toad, had him dragged halfway out a side exit before he could do much but glower at the figurehead of Global Championship Wrestling. "What about my title," Almasy demanded of the men dragging him beyond the curtain. The PTC Global Championship was still at ringside, and he’d be damned if anyone would rape him of any more than his dignity tonight. "We’ll bring it outside for ya," one of the guards sneered. "Along with yer shit. Mista Caldera’s told us you’re to be taken outside the building." So this was it, then. Not only was he to be removed from the ringside area, he was to be thrown out of the Thomas & Mack Center entirely. Left on the streets of Las Vegas like some homeless person with a gaudy belt…which was what he supposed he was now. UWF wasn’t running shows. Effectively, Seymour was out of work. He didn’t resist any further as they dragged him to the door, and threw him out bodily onto the steps. Battered bones screamed as they bounced off the pavement, the pain adding to the damage already done by one Brandon Youngblood. As the guards headed back in the building, Almasy pulled himself up to a seated position, screaming up at the gigantic structure. "YOU WILL PAY FOR THIS, YOU OVER-INFLATED, SELF-IMPORTANT SON OF A BITCH!" Seymour shook his head. Ironically, that was probably a comment Caldera would use to describe him. The two men had never gotten along before, not from the day Caldera hired him, and sure as Hell not now. "Fucking asshole," Seymour sneered, glaring at his bruised form. "All he gives a fuck about is his business, and he tries to play some righteousness card on me? HE BAILS OUT A FUCKING MURDERER, AND FIRES MY ASS? WHO THE FUCK DOES HE THINK HE I--OWWWWW!" The PTC Global Championship came flying out of the door, courtesy a security guard’s toss, and pegged Seymour squarely in the back of the head. His duffel bag followed, bouncing down a few stairs before coming to a stop at Almasy’s side. "Yer shit," the guard pronounced, laughing to himself as he closed the door once more and left Seymour alone. The Final Fantasy picked up his beloved title, and looked at the face of it, gold scratched up from its trip off his head and bounce of the pavement. "Those fucks," he swore, glaring at the title, "tarnished my belt. They tarnished everything I achieved last week, and all because Steven Caldera’s an idiot." Some of the things he said might have been true a few months earlier. Self-pitying? Yes. Self-centered? Sure. But to accuse Seymour of lacking passion for his sport…that was a mortal insult. And one, that if Caldera cared about PTC, was one that he’d proven to be utterly false in Los Angeles when he’d beaten Shadow for the belt he now cradled in his hands. He was Seymour Almasy again. The man who was nigh unbeatable. Brandon Youngblood annihilating him had come because of Seymour’s earlier idiotic decision to make things personal with the Only Diamond in Our Sport…well, and the fact that he was still recovering from the Shadow match. But if that was true, why was everyone so happy to see him go? Did GCW fans really hate him that much because of his association with Dies Irae? Or had he become what Caldera seemed to all but call him: an out and out liability? Seymour didn’t know. But he did know that he had a lot of soul searching to do. As much as he wanted to be able to write Steven’s comments off as the owner of GCW speaking out of his ass…a lot of people seemed to be agreeing with him. So, thinking would be necessary. And when Seymour needed to think…there was only one place that he wanted to go. ---------------------------------------------------------- Treelawn Cemetery Long Island, NY No matter how many times he came back here, he never quite believed the inscription on the stone, nor would he ever. Laura Marie Winters 1983-2005 "Twenty-two years old," Seymour said, sadly, sinking to his knees in front of the grave of his ex-wife. A fresh bouquet of white roses lay in front of the headstone. He had brought them with him as he always did, a small token to assuage the guilt that he felt for not being able to save her. She had always liked the white roses best. "It’s so unfair. I should be the one gone, not her." Indeed, Rollins had given the pistol to Laura a year ago with the idea being for her to take Seymour’s life. That hadn’t happened…though more and more, he wished it had. At least she would still be alive, lighting up the world with her smile. What did he have to offer? The fans hated him for the most part, save seemingly for the ones in FUSE. Sure, he was a champion, but all he could do was compete. He wasn’t especially smart, nor was he especially strong. He was nowhere near as good of a person as Laura was, nor would he ever be. She was kind, and gentle, and sweet… "I’m sorry," he managed, voice choked. "I’m so sorry…" Every time he came here, everything else seemed so insignificant. Titles, his firing…it all paled in comparison to the fact that the first woman he ever loved would never draw another breath again. Never laugh, never cry… "I don’t know what to do," he whispered to the hallowed ground, letting his face rest in the dewy grass. "I’m tired of it all. As much as I love being the Global Champion again, it’s almost not worth it. So many people hate me. So many people think I’m irredeemable. Sometimes, I just wish it was over…" The last thing he heard before stress-born unconsciousness claimed him was the sound of a turtledove, cooing overhead. "Shh, sweetie. It’s okay." I am dreaming again, and yet, not. This is more than any dream could ever be. Perhaps it is simply my memories of her that fuel them, but they feel more real than anything does nowadays. My head is pillowed in her lap, her soothing hand in my hair, and I never want to leave. Forget Kimbusa, forget Steven Caldera, forget the PTC Global Title. For this to never end, I would give them all up gladly. I would proclaim Caldera’s greatness to the stars, admit to every negative thing everyone’s said about me, if only it would mean that I could stay here forever. "Wrestling is what you do, Seymour. It’s what you love. You’ve let yourself forget that, let yourself get lost. Compete not because of others. Compete for yourself." How can I? No one will forgive me. It seems that the world has turned its back on me, as I have to it. Seemingly, there can be no redemption for me. Save from her. Perhaps that is why I enjoy these moments so much. She loves me unconditionally, so much so that I cannot understand it. How could she care for me after all I have put her through? How could she care for me after I caused her to take her own life? "You worry too much," she says. It is amazing how she can cut through all of my tension with a few simple, heartfelt words. I look up into her beautiful eyes, and my breath leaves me. How can I bring myself to question one as angelic as her? Yet, I must know. "Why do you love me so unconditionally," I query, only to look on helplessly as she smiles, and elevates me to a sitting position. I look into her eyes and am lost. "Because, you’re mine," she says, matter-of-factly. There is no hint of possession or jealousy in her even tone. It is as if she is merely stating something that is, something that always will be. "You were way back in kindergarten. You were when we were in high school. You were when we were together. You were when we were apart. You still are." She scoots closer, to the point where she is practically on top of me, a feeling of both utter bliss and utter guilt. "We’ve been over this before. It was my own fault for pulling the trigger a year ago. Not yours, Seymour." "I really wish you wouldn’t have," I manage to get out, nervously. "It’s rather lonely without you." At that, Laura chuckles, a knowing smile on her face. "Well, I TOLD you that you and Tessa wouldn’t work out, but would stubborn Seymour listen to me? Noooo…" Perhaps she feels sorry for me because of the rather sheepish look on my face, because she pulls me into a tight embrace, resting my head on her shoulder. "You don’t have to say anything," she whispers, her breath stirring a stray wisp of hair at the nape of my neck. "I know how you feel. I always have. I always will. And I’ve always felt the same way." I freeze, stunned, until her hand wraps around my neck. I know what is coming, and I pray that I won’t wake up, at least not until this part finishes. Please? Pretty please? And then, her mouth is on mine, and everything I’ve forgotten comes rushing back to me. How soft her lips are, for example. The way I always used to hold her: one arm around her back, the other tangled in her hair. The way she kisses me: until I’m breathless, then holding it just a second longer because she can. Soon, far too soon, it is over, and I can tell by the faraway look in her eye that our time together nears its close. "Don’t go," I whisper, in yet another effort to defy the fate that I have suffered through for over a year now. "I have to," she responds, "but I’ll see you again…" "When," I ask, desperately, trying to cling to this fantasy for another few seconds. "Soon," she replies, the corners of her mouth tilted upwards in a strange, knowing smile, before I am rudely returned to the realm of consciousness. "Uggghhh…" Almasy rolled to his back, panting heavily. He felt as he if he had run a marathon, limbs laying heavily in the grass, body unwilling to move. The sweat exuding from seemingly every pore clung to him, shrouding him in a dampness that made him shiver. "Not again," he moaned, shoulders slumped in defeat. Similar dreams had come to him before, and yet, none had been as deliciously tormenting as this one. Even in a clammy, confused daze, he couldn’t help but shake the feeling that something wasn’t quite right. He reached fingers up to his mouth, and green eyes widened to the size of watermelons. His lips were still wet. --------------------------------------------------------------- PTC’s American Headquarters Atlantic City, NJ "Mr. Almasy, what are your thoughts on being fired from GCW?" Once again, Seymour Almasy stood at a podium, staring down the assembled media. The last time he’d done this, it had been in the afterglow of his Global Title victory. Now, he addressed everyone after a firing and Caldera’s continued vengeance: namely a giant cash donation to PTC to secure his client, Kimbusa, a PTC Global Championship match in the first round of GTT6. "I told you when this press conference started," an exasperated Seymour replied, "that I’m only taking questions on GTT6. Nothing on PRIME, nothing on AWC, nothing on GCW, just GTT6. Now, if anyone has any tournament-related questions…" Almasy shook his head, patting the championship belt on his shoulder. He knew what was next, someone would ask him a question on Caldera or GCW disguised as a tournament question. And lo and behold, it happened. "Seymour, how do you feel about facing Kimbusa, a man who some are calling Steven Caldera’s hired gun in the wake of recent events?" ‘Recent events’. Seymour almost chuckled. With that two word euphemism, the speaker had opened up anything and everything. Still, Mr. James of the Wrestling World News had played the game properly, and Seymour could appreciate that. "First of all, facing a 6’7", 500 pound man is never fun. Ever. I don’t care if it was the finals, I’d STILL not look forward to facing Mitsuhiro Kurokame. Beyond that, am I happy about the circumstances? No. I personally think it’s a sham that a corporation like Primetime Central has bowed to a $600,000 gift from a man who could give that sort of gift three times a month and not miss it. The fact of the matter is, though, that I don’t run Primetime Central, so it’s entirely out of my control. I’m going to do the one thing that I can do under these circumstances: get in the ring and fight with absolutely everything I have." The crowd of reporters seemed restless as they scribbled on their notepads. "Come on," the first reporter suddenly exhorted, "Caldera fired you on pay per view, and you’re not going to fire back at him?" Clearly, the reporter was trying to goad Seymour into a response that would make front page news. Trying to make Seymour say something in a moment of anger that could sell newspapers. "Fine," he acquiesced. "I’ll talk about Caldera. When I first decided to do this press conference, I was going to sit up here and rant about him the entire time. But you know what? That’s what he wants. He wants me to be focused on him. He wants me to rant, and rave, and lose my focus, so that Kimbusa squashes me like an insect, takes my title, and ends my GTT run in the first round." Really, it wasn’t a bad plan. Considering Caldera’s shrewdness, Seymour wouldn’t have been surprised if it had been in formulation from the moment he won the Global Title. "The thing is…Caldera’s issues with me aren’t personal, as much as everyone would try to make them sp. He’s a businessman who thought, for whatever reason, that I wasn’t good enough for his company. Does that piss me off? Yeah. Does that make me want to shove the contract that I signed for him a year ago when he was all but begging me to sign up his ass? Absolutely. But I’m not going to give into base emotions. I’m going to take a relatively high road on this. Right now, the best revenge I can get is to beat Kimbusa, and eliminate one of Caldera’s umpteen chances to get the Global Title back." "On that note," another voice interjected, "what do you think of your bracket?" Another trap. Almasy remembered his own past very, very well. The last time he’d had a bracket this difficult on paper, it had been CW2, The Tournament Which Was Never Again To Be Mentioned. And back then, he’d let himself get obsessed with the enormity of his task, and he’d fallen in the first round to PTC’s favorite enemy, Archibald MacGregor. Not again. NEVER again. "It’s a lovely bracket. Though, to be honest, it has the worst name. Kanga’s such a boring character. Couldn’t we at least have been the Roo Bracket?" It was an attempt to make people laugh. Some did. Others didn’t. "Seriously though," he continued, "Kanga’s got a lot of good talent in it. A lot of good talent that I can’t afford to worry about right now. Kimbusa is the only opponent on my mind. Kimbusa is the only man I am focused on. If I worry about Haze, or Shadow, or Hoyt…I’m going to lose. I’ve got a new approach this year, gentlemen. One match at a time. I’m not going to overlook anyone, because now, one loss ends my tournament career. There will be no next time, no chance to make up for a loss this time around." "What about Sage’s victory over Rich Rollins in the first half of round one? Are you happy Rollins is gone?" The look on Almasy’s face was deadly serious as he replied. "I am, but I think Steven Caldera’s even happier, which makes me sort of torn on the issue. I would have loved the chance to face Rollins down the road, but again, I’m not really thinking about it. Kimbusa is MORE than enough for one man to worry about." Seymour’s shoulders slumped forward, and he took a deep breath, a clear sign that he was exhausted. The weight of his situation pressed down heavily upon him; after all, this was IT. His final tournament. His final chance, depending on how his match with Kimbusa went, to address the world. Just in case, he had to take it. "Before I go," he said, suddenly straightening, "there’s a few things I’d like to say." Closing his eyes, he went over the words in his head. How could he sum up five years in five minutes? How could he cover everything that had happened to him? It was odd to him, somehow, that his entire career would soon be reduced to a sound byte, to a column in a wrestling newsletter. But if that was his fate, he wanted to make that sound byte or column as good as it could be. "I’ve been doing this since the UWF hired me in 2002, when no one else would," he began, opening his eyes slowly. "Since then, I’ve won the PTC Global Title three times, won two PTC tournaments, and become far more than I ever thought I’d be that summer day I first stepped into a UWF ring. I’ve also done a lot of things that I regret. In the course of my fame, in the course of everything that I’ve done, I’ve left casualties in my wake. Places like my Global Wrestling before my return as Emoticon. Places like Global Championship Wrestling. Places which deserved my full attention, and never got it." The memories were potent. As good as he was, and as known as he was, there were dozens of opportunities he’d left on the table. Chances to cement his legacy. Times when he didn’t perform up to his expectations. "People like Brandon Youngblood, whose life I attempted to ruin for no good reason. Even people who I once counted as my friends, like Lia and Dawn. I’ve come to realize that Seymour Almasy is many things. He is fiercely loyal and highly competitive. He can sign five hundred autographs for a crowd, and stay until the last one is signed. But he is also extremely vain. He is also emotionally needy, and yes, a bit of a skirt-chaser. He has an ego, one that is probably quite a bit bigger than it should be. And so, to anyone whom I have done harm to, from the fans of GCW to Youngblood to Dawn to Lia to anyone else, I’m sorry." The pencils were scribbling furiously now. Whatever he said would probably be spun, but he didn’t care anymore. It no longer mattered. One way or another, by the time GTT6 ended, his time in the public spotlight would be mostly over. "I’m going back to basics. Once, I carried a sword with me to battle. Once, my hair wasn’t white, but a brilliant purple instead. Once, I fought to take down the mightiest bosses this game had to offer. Enemies come no bigger and no more dangerous than Kimbusa. Kimbusa is coming for my title. Kimbusa is coming for my tournament career. And, last but not least, he is coming on behalf of Steven Caldera. You know, when he fired me, Caldera said one thing that stuck with me. He said that I didn’t have what it took to be in Global Championship Wrestling. He said that I’d lost my passion." Almasy removed the PTC Global Title from his shoulder. It was, in all likelihood, the final championship he would ever hold in his illustrious career. He stared into the plate of the belt, looking at the fine craftsmanship, at the image of the PTC logo. Below all of that, at the nameplate which bore his name. He liked the sight of the words "Seymour Almasy" there. "Kimbusa" wouldn’t look nearly as good.
