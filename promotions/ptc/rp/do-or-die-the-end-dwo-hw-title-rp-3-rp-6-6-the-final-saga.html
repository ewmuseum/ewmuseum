Do or Die, the End[DWO HW title rp#3, rp 6/6 The Final Saga]

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kaiowas Cable Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 52 Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 4:31 pm Post subject: Do or Die, the End[DWO HW title rp#3, rp 6/6 The Final Saga] Give Up? Give in? Let go? Let fall the chance to immortalise your deeds? Denounce your claim to greatness? Step down? Step back? Retreat? Withdraw? Walk away from the climax of all your effort? Let something as small as this upset your whole plan? Open your clenched fist and drop the thing most precious to you? Release your claim to freedom? You claim to be right? Your claim to have saved a race of people? Why? Why now? Why now, when you have worked so much to get this far? Why would you throw it all away in frustration? Why would anyone do such a thing? Why would anyone let this obstacle break them down and tear their confidence to pieces? Why would a man with the most stubborn attitude in the world back down just because he cannot find that which he seeks? He is so close, why would he stop and walk away now? He stands at the gateway to his future, yet you think he will give up just because he cannot find the gateway? He is strong and skilled, why should he back down now, at the crossroads of his life, where the wrong turn would lead him to the "final" destiny? Why would he force himself to take the wrong turn? Why on earth would anyone choose death over life, victory, glory, freedom and above all.... power? So many lies, so many half truths.... That's why.... Lies? How many lies have passed the lips of his masters? His "friends"? His "servants"? How many times has he missed the truth because of human sentiment and trust? How many times has he failed to be right by succeeding to be a human? Why did they lie to him? Why? Because they wanted him... His power, his strength. The lies... The lies... They changed him.... didn't they? They swayed him, they pushed him, they forced him to things he would not think of in the right mind, didn't they? Didn't the lies of Pultra, that hated Lord of Vengeance, push him, nudge him and boulder him into things that he would have thought.... Pointless? Stupid? Idiotic displays of power? Wasn't he told that the punishment that Pultra meted out to him was.... Justified? Correct? Proper? What lies! What pack of useless, truthless and cruel..... lies? Where they lies? Does he know even now? After he has passed the point of no looking back, no turning back, no stopping now, does he even know if the reason he is here is not just some....lie? Is it not just a vicious circle? Was it from one liar to the next? Was the Slayer lying too? Was it all just a legend of lies? Was it just two men trying to out-lie the other? Were both men just trying to get what they want from an "innocent" pawn? Was he just the victim of lies? Which was the better of two evils? Had he chosen the right one? He already knew, he knew he would never be free of the lies, but he already knew that.... That he was standing in the throne room of the greater evil.... [Anger, frustration and hatred all well up inside of Kaiowas as he feels the grip of failure tightening around the once shining light of victory... He felt the success once so close slip slightly as his task becomes just that little more impossible. The plush room that is slowly destroying itself is empty. The walls and all the luxurious furniture are decaying and rotting before Kaiowas' very eyes, every trace of the huge ten foot fallen Angel, Pultra are fast escaping his sight. Angry and extremely tense, Kaiowas grabs a huge wall drape that hangs to his right and tears it down in rage. It billows as it falls sending a whoosh of stale air and dust mites into the stale atmosphere. It hits the ground like a wet towel, limp and without structure. Kaiowas stares down at it seething with anger. Stamping on it in his frustration, Kaiowas lets out another scream of pent up aggression, allowing the whole demolished palace to know about his torment...] Kaiowas: Pultra! YOU COWARDLY INFIDEL! [Charging in his aggressive state towards the platter of once extremely good food, Kaiowas thunders through the throne room. Staff raised, he snaps it down on an unfortunate roasted pig which has turned green in its accelerated rot. Slicing it and its plate in two, the staff strikes the marble surface beneath. Instead of bouncing back, clanging as the surface reflects it, the blade cuts deep into the crumbled, weathered stone and dust flies everywhere. Kaiowas rage increases further when he finds he cannot pull the huge staff from the stone it has now embedded itself in. Tugging did him no good what so ever. Frustration and tension flows once more as he kicks out at a wooden bowl which contained glorious fruits of summer. Now all it contains is a collection of rotten balls of mush and disease. The bowl makes a hollow sound as it makes contact with the floor and the rotten things inside are catapulted across the room and make a horrible splat noise as they strike the far wall. Kaiowas looks up and breathes calmly once more. He sees the squashed fruit remains on the wall and he sees their foul liquid juices roll down the tapestries and once fine paintings. One thing in the whole room remains completely contact, and the sight of it catches Kaiowas' attention. A huge mirror, formed in the shape of the symbol of Pultra is attached to the wall mere feet to Kaiowas' left, only partially hidden by a fallen curtain. The sight of such a symbol of his enemy boils Kaiowas' blood. The only thing in the room which has not decayed beyond recognition is the last symbol of the Lord of Vengeance's power...] Kaiowas: (whispered) Bastard..... [Stepping towards it with intent Kaiowas reaches out with both hands instinctively. He would destroy every symbol of Pultra until Pultra himself showed up.... to be destroyed. Seven years of bad luck or not... This mirror would soon lie in many pieces over this ruined floor... Pushing away the curtain which hides the gold gilded mirror, Kaiowas sees the full worth of this mirror. All over it, it is covered in little gems, little jewels. This mirror was a artifact of Pultra's reign, a gift long ago from his people. A show and display of his sway and control over the people of the Amazon. Kaiowas saw and understood. He saw and detested this final sign of Pultra's dominance.. Was it jealousy? Was it hate? Perhaps a mix of the two most strong emotions in the world. Reaching out Kaiowas wrenches the luxurious reflective object straight off its wall fixings and pulls it close to his face. In the mirror he sees the man who came to kill a god, the man who came to free a people and the man who came to take that one step closer to immortality... But he also sees a man. A human man, not always capable of what they set out to do. That is man's fatal stereotype... Overconfidence. The man staring back at Kaiowas hasn't shaved for a month due to one reason or another. His dreadlocks are not their usual pristine selves and his eyes have bags under them... This would be tough on him...] Kaiowas: RAAARRGGHHHH! [Pulling himself from the gaze of the man in the mirror Kaiowas hurls the huge golden object to the rubble covered ground. Seven years of bad luck may kill him now, but he could not stand to look into it any longer. The large decorative mirror smashes off a fallen piece of lumber and the shards of reflective glass are thrown everywhere. The light glances off each one as they plummet to the ground once more. Fortunately the seven years bad luck has not yet come into effect and every shard misses Kaiowas, leaving him unscathed and with a huge evil grin on his face, feeling relieved after exorcising that demon... His mind turns to the task at hand again and he walks towards his staff, preparing to yank it free with renewed vigour and strength. On his way... He glimpses to one of the larger pieces of the mirror on the ground and is forced by his curiosity to look again...] Kaiowas: What the.... [He stoops and looks at the glass in more detail. In its reflection he can now see a huge blue ball of swirling energy above him... Silently it whirls in the reflection. Maybe it was a trick of Kaiowas' eyes. He looks deeper into the fixating energy whirl and he can see into another plane of existence.... Suddenly the image draws him through a portal in his mind, and he can see it all... Pultra and their last meeting place... Kaiowas snaps himself out of the sleepy image and drops the glass in shock. It shatters on the ground, but Kaiowas pays no attention to it at all. Bending backwards and looking vertically upwards he sees what the reflection had shown him. The whirlwind of blue and black energy merging and meeting making a huge portal above Kaiowas.... The gate to his destiny. He must have missed the huge thing when he walked in the doorway and could not see his enemy. He must have let the rage and frustration blind him to the portal to his final showdown with the fake god that had tormented him so...] Kaiowas: (whispered to self) Here I come.... At last. [Ducking quickly to his left, he reaches out with both hands, grabbing hold of his great bladed staff and tearing it free of the grasp of the crumbling masonry that had held it in place. Charging at a nearby wall he kicks off it and, using his staff for extra leverage, pushing himself further into the darkened sky towards that blue black ball of energy. It looks as if he will fall short until a wave of suction catches him and pulls him with force into the huge portal above his head. The once silent energy mass now whines and screams as it's maw opens to give access to the Tribal Warrior. Kaiowas is last seen readying himself in mid air as he is sucked into the blue ball.] Kaiowas: Prepare yourself, impostor, The Betrayer has arrived.... [The young Brazilian finally disappears into the portal and it snaps shut behind him with a pop. Soon all is silent and the ruin is again empty. A brick falls off a wall and we leave this place to rot....] *** FLASH *** *** THUD *** Kaiowas: Eurgh! [The heavens open up and Kaiowas is hurled at the ground below. He lands on his ass and makes a sickening thud on contact. Sitting up, the world around him seems to be the most intimidating thing the young Brazilian Ktulu Tribal Warrior had ever had to encounter... Yet his persistence did not let him give way to the horrors of this place....] Voice: Get up! [Who? Turning his head slowly Kaiowas sees who is ordering him about in this weird world.... Standing above him is the ethereal being that caused Kaiowas to be here this very moment, the Slayer. Kaiowas held a feeling of contempt for this man also... Kaiowas never liked the thought of someone who felt superior to him without even proving it... Kaiowas would now prove to the rest of existence that Pultra had flawed in his attempt to be superior... And the Slayer was next if he did not watch out...] Slayer: Get up Warrior... The hour has come... I have waited for you. [Brushing himself off and standing up in this chamber of horrors, Kaiowas faces the cloaked ethereal Slayer who had been the first to attempt to topple Pultra... He now promised all his powers as a semi-deity to Kaiowas if the Tribal Warrior could destroy the man who plagued the Ktulu.] Kaiowas: (spitefully) You waited for me? Could you not have brought me with you? [Laughing quietly the Slayer seems to brush off the comment...] Slayer: I wanted to get you angry and hungry before you came here... That is why I let you roam that palace.... I see you have the boon's of the gods. [He was referring to the semi-permanent tattoos that Kaiowas had emblazoned himself during a short ritual before he teleported to this place. Small symbols of all the characteristics and skills that he will find exceptionally useful in the quest ahead...] Kaiowas: And I see you are as manipulative as ever.... Slayer. [The words were bitter as they left his mouth and the Slayer recognised the pent up anger that may soon be released. Worried that it may be released on the wrong target he attempts concilliation..] Slayer: No need to become angry with me little Warrior.... [That phrase... again! Kaiowas cannot stand those words, "little Warrior", used in such a condescending tone. It set his nerves on end. It was like someone running their nails down a blackboard. It sent that feeling of worthlessness down through Kaiowas, even though Kaiowas knew that he was a great Warrior. It seemed so idiotic to be condescending to a man who was about to fulfil the task that you were set out to do.... and failed.] Kaiowas: Then do not talk to me as your inferior.... Slayer... [More bitterness, Kaiowas' tone reeks of it. Thinking of it all he decides to leave this encounter until another time.... After all he was promised powers if he succeeded, powers he would not receive from a dead Slayer...] Kaiowas: Enough of this.... Show me the way... And no more! [His cloak wafts open as a bony armoured finger swoops through the air and points to the north. Kaiowas turns, following the pointing finger. He takes in all which lies around him with a sense of awe. He sees a long twisting staircase stretch out into the visible foreground. Around it are walls. Walls that move, walls that squirm. Walls that seem to live.... Kaiowas clutches his staff tighter and without a word, he leaves the presence of the Slayer, striding off into the distance, walking the last mile towards his destiny. No feeling of dread seems to fall yet, only determination and courage. His heart does beat triple time and he does know that this next task will either be the first step of the rest of his life of the last move of the whole of his life. These next few hours determine his life and the lives of many others... Many others who won't even know that they are being freed and saved.] Kaiowas: Onward and upward, the Beast shall fall and be driven forth to where real punishment for his sins can be reaped.... For now... I am the reaper, the betrayer, the assassin, the murderer. But I am also the liberator, the rebel, the revolutionary, the saviour, the one to lead the people from the tyranny and the Lord of the Ktulu... You Pultra however... You are but one thing.... You are the doomed! [Placing his first foot on the staircase he is enveloped by the horrible walls that surround him. The walls are like a lump of living flesh, all squashed together from many fallen victims and remoulded like clay to form this wall. It moves and reaches out, trying to touch Kaiowas. Muffled voices scream out to him, they tell him of dangers and of their horrible tragic deaths at the hands of Pultra... They warn him: "go back, turn or be killed". They attempt to block his passage by melting into a barricade over the staircase. Kaiowas however is in no mood to retreat and swiftly puts an end to the foolish barricade by slicing it in half, carving a way through. The screams grow louder at the pain and the warnings soon change to threats and evil promises of vengeance. Kaiowas still ploughs his way through this staircase, pummelling through the fleshy wall when it offers up resistance of any sort. The walls become more and more aggressive in their attempts to stop him and soon knives and swords start bursting out of the walls, aimed for Kaiowas' heart, neck and stomach. Kaiowas' quick reflexes save him, raising his staff well in time to ward off the flying projectiles. They clatter harmlessly to the floor and Kaiowas marches on... He seems to have been walking and moving for such a long time, defending himself against the attacks and threats of the dead souls that Pultra has captured over the years. Kaiowas soon sees an end to the tunell and finally he escape the lamenting and groaning of the dead souls. He steps out from a hellish place into his worst nightmare...] Kaiowas: Pultra you bastard! [All around him is the most horrible hellish scene that anyone could have evoked anywhere. For Kaiowas this was hell. All around him his new village lay in ruins. All of its members lay impaled on stakes and burning in huge body piles.... It was like a freeze shot of it all. It was like Pultra's final attempt at scaring off Kaiowas... To show him the effect of any failure he may have... Kaiowas closed his eyes and shook the image from his head, clearing everything but his goal from his well trained combat mind.... This was it... He was not going to let anymore mind games destroy his chances of saving himself, his tribe and taking all the power that the Slayer promised....] Voice: Ah Hah hah hah hah! [The chilling laughter reverberated around the bleak place. Kaiowas opened his eyes to find himself in a new place... Without the burning village and without his comrades dying or dead on spike or in pyres... This was the ultimate arena where Kaiowas would bring the vengeance or be sent to find his own punishment in the depths of hell. Readying himself, he grips his staff and stays alert, waiting for the unannounced arrival if the evil bastard himself. The Lord of Vengeance.... Pultra...] Voice: And of course Kaiowas! We meet again! [Kaiowas looks all around him but can find no source of the bodiless voice. As he turns and twists to find Pultra he takes in the scenery even more. Huge pillars rise everywhere in this arena. Huge white stone pillars. They are cold and lifeless, just as the spikes which line the walls of the arena. This was Pultra's home territory and Kaiowas was sure he was playing on Pultra's rules from the very start so he kept himself on alert.] Voice: As you pitiful humans would say..... In the red corner! [Suddenly the ground trembles and a hatch opens fifty feet away from Kaiowas, the ground opening up and a huge platform burrowing out if the red dusty soil beneath. On top stands Pultra, confident and oozing his lax "i'm already victorious" attitude. He stands a full ten feet tall and is humanoid in structure. Two arms, two legs, a torso and a head. His skin is covered in thick layers of red and black armour leaving only his legs and his forearms un-armoured. He is a colossus and as he ascends from the pit he raises his huge sword to the sky. It gleams a deep evil red as the sunlight catches it. His shield, held in his left hand, is held loosely by his side as he finishes the mocking build up to the battle of his life....] Pultra: The reigning champion AND Lord of Vengeance! PULTRA!! [Kaiowas, as usual is unimpressed by this show of power and is itching to get this whole battle underway. He shouts up at his opponent in challenge.] Kaiowas: Pultra! Let us fight! NOW! Pultra: And in the BLUE CORNER! [The ground directly under Kaiowas rumbles and shakes and soon another platform lifts him off the level ground and pulls him up to the same altitude as Pultra where it stops. Kaiowas finds his balance and readies himself to leap.] Pultra: The unlucky Challenger from the Amazon Jungle! The puny angel of retribution! The comical betrayer! Kaiowas! Kaiowas: Enough games Pultra... I challenge thee.... to Mortal Combat! [Before Kaiowas could blink the battle was on, furious and swift. Pultra had leapt feet into the air, far above the top of the pillars and had drawn back his sword arm. Pointing his sword directly at Kaiowas he released a sharp burst of electricity at Kaiowas. The long blue white stream of energy raced towards the unfortunate Tribal Warrior and Kaiowas only just about avoided the energy bolt by rolling out of the way at the last second.] Pultra: Nice move! Pity you won't do it again! [Pultra falls gracefully and lands directly on top of one of the pillars, using it as a stepping stone in his journey to Kaiowas' pillar. Kaiowas knew automatically that he was the underdog. The only magic he had was the magic he had instilled in the tattoos that shine brightly on his skin. Turning to his foe, Kaiowas spies another lightning bolt head straight for him. Rather than ducking, Kaiowas somersaults skilfully from the platform to the ground, landing in a cloud of dust and sweat. Pultra curses his bad aiming and soon descends to the ground where Kaiowas awaits with his bladed staff at hand. Pultra hits the ground running and charges headlong at Kaiowas. Kaiowas stands his ground and smiles evilly as the huge beast of an angel rushes him.] Kaiowas: Just a little closer.... [As Pultra nears, Kaiowas leaps to his right and kicks off one of the pillars sending him over the top of the advancing impostor god. Kaiowas lands crouched and lunges forward, tackling the ten footer from behind sending him into another of the pillars face first. Kaiowas is back on his feet moments later and retreats a few steps to avoid any attempt by Pultra to reinact Kaiowas' smart move. Pultra stands, dizzy and soon clears his head. Although a trickle of blood flows from under Pultra's spiky helmet, Kaiowas does not take any confidence from the first clash of bodies.] Pultra: You think mere blows to the head can take me down... little warrior? [Pultra swings his sword around again and aims the blade at Kaiowas, releasing the blue bolt again. Kaiowas was to close to react quick enough and the bolt strikes him, forcing him to stagger.... Then another strikes him full in the chest and Kaiowas is sent flying back twenty feet, also ending up striking a pillar. Kaiowas sits up and assess the damage. He looks at his body and feels only the pain of the impact with the pillar. Slowly he looks at his legs and sees one of his tattoos glimmering and shining like a light.] Kaiowas: The magic! [The magic he had imbued in himself had resisted the pure energy of that lightning bolt, but the tattoo was fading fast... You could see the tattoo dulling as he sat there. Jumping up Kaiowas picked up his staff which had rolled as he had impacted on the pillar. Turning looking at the pillar, he sees that he has left a great indent where he had struck. He turns about face and falls into a defensive pose, awaiting the next attack.] Pultra: You little useless human... You think your tattoos can save you forever? [Pultra fires out three more bolts of electricity but in his rage they all overshoot and Kaiowas remains unscathed.. Pultra however is attempting to force more magic out of his sword.... And it isn't happening.] Kaiowas: And you Pultra! Do you think your useless magic will last forever now that you have no power source! [It was true, as Kaiowas had talked the Ktulu over to his side, Pultra had no everlasting power source that he could just dip into now and again. And it seemed as if all the magic had just run out....] Pultra: Curse you! It does not stop me removing your head with my sword! [Pultra charges once more, in a more warrior-like way. Kaiowas makes a counter charge, hoping to soak up some of the momentum with counter movement. Kaiowas had been no mathematics scholar, but simple principles of motion always helped in combat. Finally the two bodies clash in a fury of blades and wood. Flashes of light reflected of shined surfaces and sounds of two metals clashing reach us. Kaiowas and Pultra swap blows and parries as both man and angel battle to their full trying to better each other. It soon comes down to a stand still, with both men's faces mere inches apart in their struggle to finish each other off. Kaiowas' huge muscles are bulging under the strain and Pultra is heard groaning in his struggle against his ex-servant. With one mighty heave Kaiowas is flung back by a huge effort of strength from Pultra. Kaiowas staggers and is again on the defensive as Pultra follows up with a fury of swipes and overhand cuts aiming for Kaiowas' neck and head. Further notching his staff, Kaiowas fends off the huge strikes and soon he is safe again. The two men fight as they have been and soon Kaiowas is pushed back against a pillar again. With a strike of genius he turns, breaking off from combat and rushes headlong at the pillar, using it again as leverage. He flies through the air spinning and twisting, his body and his bladed staff becoming as one... A huge spinning twirling flurry of blade and flesh. Striking Pultra like a whirling dervish, Kaiowas exacts a whirlwind of blows to his helmet, breast plate and bare left forearm. The last strike digs deep and Pultra screams out...] Pultra: AAAHHH!! [Kaiowas had nearly maimed Pultra, cutting all the way down to the huge bone structure of the ten foot tall fallen Angel. Pultra drops his shield and it hits the soil causing a plume of dust to billow upwards. Unluckily it causes both men to become disorientated and it takes them both seconds to regain their sense of direction. Kaiowas opens his eyes to the sight of a huge Angel standing over him, blade raised above his head in both hands, waiting to strike... Blood dripping and pouring out of the open wound on his left arm....] Pultra: Drop your weapon.... And I shall make it swift... [Kaiowas, stalwartly holds on to his trusty weapon silently, probably his last act of rebellion against Pultra, against anyone...] Pultra: No problem... You were always stubborn Kaiowas, you were always going to be trouble to me! I knew from the first time you had to be punished for insubordination, yet I stuck with you because I could see the qualities in you that could develop! I could see that you could become One of the best warriors.... Unluckily because of your stupidity you have failed to develop into that man, and in your own destruction you have taken the lives of two hundred Ktulu people with you... yes they know they fight for what is right... But right does not always win... Does it....? You can sit there and feel all the guilt in the world... You can feel the failure.. But where I am sending you... Those feelings... They will be multiplied by a hundred! You will suffer for your transgressions against the Lord of Vengeance "Little Warrior"!! [That was it! Kaiowas snapped..... He could stand the taunting, the condescending no longer! The words rang through his head like an alarm bell. Before even another drop of Pultra's blood could fall from his wrist and join the pool of red liquid mingling with the dirty red brown dusty soil on the ground Kaiowas acted...] Kaiowas: FOR THE KTULU!!!!!! [Snapping up in a leap that no man could have performed in normal circumstances Kaiowas brought his weapon to bear, smashing Pultra's other wrist and spinning the huge sword from his hands. The blade hits the soil and spins off into the distance.... In one fluid motion Kaiowas lands facing the opposite direction to the extremely shocked Pultra and with all his remaining strength he forces the blade of Lecood and his staff in a sweeping motion deep into the impostor god's heart. Pultra's eyes widen and he topples onto the dust. Kaiowas keeps hold of the staff and stands triumphantly on the fallen angel's chest with one foot.] Kaiowas: For Lecood.... [Kaiowas pushes the whole weapon into the chest cavity of the god and wrenches his heart free. The blackened organ is held pumping and shaking on the end of Kaiowas' blade. Lecood has had his vengeance and now so will Kaiowas. Dropping to his knees Kaiowas pulls the heart from the blade and brings it to the face of Pultra where he holds it tauntingly..] Kaiowas: For me.... [With one foul swoop Kaiowas rips the heart from any arteries and veins right in front of Pultra, himself. He then tears the heart in two and throws one half on one direction... and the other is hurled as far as he can in the other direction. Slowly Kaiowas stands, keeping his eyes on the giant tyrant who had ruined his life for so long. Now he lies dead... Dead in the sand of his home... Soon, suddenly the body begins to evaporate, the spirits came to haul Pultra's soul to the final judgement. Kaiowas smiles as the little beings go about their work... Soon nothing is left but the blood on Kaiowas' hands and the sword that was spun away in the sand... Kaiowas picks it up and straps it to his back... Lifting his staff high in the air he screams in victory....] Kaiowas: I HAVE MY VENGEANCE!!! I HAVE MY VICTORY!! I WILL HAVE MY POWER!! [Slowly Kaiowas lowers his weapon and everything fades to black.] _________________ DWO World Champion and Most Improved 2002, PTC no1 january 8th and 14th and no7 too!
