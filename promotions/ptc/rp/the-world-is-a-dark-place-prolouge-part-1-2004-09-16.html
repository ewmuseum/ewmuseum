The World Is A Dark Place (Prolouge/part 1)

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 The world is a dark quiet place, with only a few individuals able to produce the fame and the sin around them to shoot like a red streak across the skies. Men such as Caligula and Nero set the standard for these individuals that are made with debauchery and murder. Through the middle ages Prince Vlad ‘The Impaler’ Dracula, and Mary the 1st. Two people with supreme power over a land, but who could not control their desires. One who was viewed in Europe as a great Christian man. At the same time he’d have 20,000 human beings sent to a ravine and impaled. While the other, preserving her faith as the true faith of England instituted public burnings for heresy. Both Vlad's and Mary's conniving ways streaked across the sky in a crimson blaze of glory, similar to that of a shooting star. In recent times we’ve had such masters of genocide as Adolf Hitler, Pol Pot, and Idi Amin. Adolf Hitler took it upon himself to return the ‘wealth’ back to the white anglo saxon protestant Germans. Which in turn meant butchering a religion that gave birth to Christianity. He also managed to enslave 5,000,000 people of other countries. Pol Pot was another mast of genocide, he chose to execute the educated and the rich, creating a new society that was totally dedicated to his goals. Idi Amin took it upon himself to destroy any of his political enemies, and to generate money for private people gave them a license to kill. In Amin’s culture the family would give anything for the body of a deceased loved one, the rich would kill people and then say they had found their body, getting money and everything the people owned in return. All of these people were the worst of the worst, all were tyrants. All of these people had their own six individual ideas and goals, from debauchery and murder, to genocide. Some of these individuals are considered icons by modern day interpretations of them, others are viewed as the most evil men to ever roam the world. Their red streaks of terror shot across the sky in a brightness that no other streak had shot before. Until now. It is my desire to become as hated and as vile as these men. I watch as sons and daughters steal off their mothers to give me money for my mind altering substances. I watch as mothers rape their children’s college funds to give me money for a substance that they can not stop taking. I watch as peoples futures and lives fall into a dependence on substances I take and distribute out into the world. I watch as people fall into debt with myself and others over these drugs, I watch at what we do to them, as I take a gander back into history some of these men seemed to have the right ideas. The Impaler, he made it simple, you fuck with him you died. That’s my goal to have people terrified, from the sound of my attack entire nations defecate themselves. Am I a tyrant on the level of these few icons of evil? This is why I’ve entered this tournament. To show people on a one on one level what type of damage is possible, what kind of pain and suffering is identifiable. To show people what I do behind closed doors, right in front of them in an arena, slowly crushing their heroes and villains into submission. Their fear and hatred will sweep the world as my streak of red light rides off into the night, larger and brighter than any other human has came before me. My reign of blood will never end, people will remember the drug baron ruler of the world. My opponents will symbolize mass executions as their fans stop cheering when I destroy their ability to ever step into a ring again. I will be their executioner, and they will slowly realize my superiority above all others. They will realize why my claims mean so much. I am the greatest. No, not the greatest wrestler. No, not the greatest fighter. The greatest human to ever walk the face of this earth. That’s what I’ve become, the most advanced, the smartest, the most amazing. My all around ability is unequalled to. I am perfect. My reign of blood will streak higher and farther than all other humans, I desire it and I will make it so. The world is a dark place (1) Everyone slowly expands outwards, your social bearing increases as your life continues on, your mind and body become more in tune to what happens around you. That’s why Mr. Fredrick Jones was now fearing for his life. The man in front of him stared onwards into Mr. Jones’ eyes. The tears in his eyes welled up and proceeded to explode from his eyes. He stared up and glared into the stone blue eyes of his maker, he knew there was no hope for him and no turning back for the man with the gun. ‘But… my family…’ Mr. Jones stuttered out as his tears slapped against the concrete. Token Weed glared down at him, his sadistic smile grew wider with this statement. ‘Your family will find you in the afterlife’ Token stated as his smile grew even wider. He cocked the hammer of his gun back, and quickly three men came through the door to the room. Each man carrying a bag that was kicking and screaming. Slowly each man ripped the bags open to reveal a young boy, no older than six or seven, a young girl no older than thirteen, and a woman who was about thirty five years old. Instantly Mr. Jones began weeping even harder as the three all looked up into the face of their captors… There was no remorse to be found in their faces. Token pulled the trigger, shooting Mr. Jones’ wife in front of him. Once again the sobs escaped Mr. Jones to no end as he tried to get in front of his children to shield them. Token once again squeezed the trigger sending Mr. Jones’ daughter flying backwards, the back of her skull flew out and her brains impacted Mr. Jones straight in the face. He looked over at his son, the two’s eyes instantly locked. Token squeezed another round out of his .45 magnum that sent the young boy straight to the floor, the eyes Mr. Jones’ was looking at, one was missing as his son’s body instantly stopped breathing. Token passed the .45 over to one of his henchmen who looked down at the gun and smiled. He produced a white towel from his pocket, giving the gun a quick wipe down and handed it to a now gloved Token Weed. Token smiled as he walked over to the kneeling Mr. Jones and picked him up, standing him in front of his family. He knew his fate, he knew what would happen to him now. Token grabbed Mr. Jones’ hand, placing the gun in it and taking a firm grip around Mr. Jones’ hand. He placed the gun to Mr. Jones’ head, Token grinned as he pulled the trigger, blood and brain matter exited Mr. Jones’ head on the other side. The man dropped to the ground, the gun crashing down, still in his hand. Token smiled, the perfect crime had been committed. Token turned and exited the room walking down the stairs to the street. Two cops awaited Token at the bottom, he tossed one his gloves the man quickly set the gloves on fire as he looked over at Token and smiled. The other cop asked ‘so, what did this one do?’ Token smiled. ‘Wrong Place, Wrong Time. Saw the wrong shit.’ Slowly Token regrouped with his three thugs as they all stood in a circle. One of them was totally silent, he kicked at the ground and then looked up at Token. ‘So, what now?’ the man asked as Token looked around the circle blankly. ‘Do you three want to know why we killed that man in there?’ Token stated, letting the words float into the air like they were a puff of cigarette smoke. The three men looked up at him a bit anxiously. What had this one done? Did he catch Token selling a few assault rifles to a few minors? Did he owe Token money for his product as most of the scumbags they killed did? ‘It’s pretty simple, the fuck bag stepped on some toes some where he didn’t belong. Let me put it simply for you guys, remember our friend up at Soft? This is a favor for him. This fuck had the balls to testify against them in court, he asked me to take care of him. I decided to up the stakes a bit myself, take care of his family, keeps future versions of snitches from ever walking the earth.’ Token said sighing. ‘It’s cuntfaces like this that shouldn’t be allowed to reproduce, the snitches of the world. I say see the fucker in there, that’s what the rest of them fucks will look like.’ Token stated as he pivoted and turned away. One of the thugs followed Token away from the scene, the two others spun around and walked the other way. ‘Now listen,’ Token said as he continued walking, trying to keep it down and not tip off anyone of his talking. ‘Those two, I want them both dead. They sucked the wrong pigs cock this week. Make sure it’s long and painful. Make sure your away from it as fast as possible.’ Token stated as he began to walk a bit faster. His thug pivoted and instantly went the other way. Token walked off into the night, his hold on the Los Angeles drug scene remained intact. Or so it seemed, the two thugs from earlier both began to walk a bit faster after Token was long out of sight. They rounded a bend and headed straight into a small diner. It was a dimly lit diner with only a few people sitting quietly at booths. One man sat in a suit and tie, he appeared very out of place. He sat quietly, his coffee in one hand and a lit cigarette in his other. He nodded to the two men who quickly approached him and sat down, the man closest to the window pressed his back up against the window, to help conceal who was talking to this man. ‘What do you have for me?’ the man said as he glared across the table, now wasn’t the time for games. ‘Man this shit is wack!’ one of the thugs shouted. ‘You better start forking over the cash beforehand, I aint down for this candy cane shit. I want the money, this is my fucking life if this gets out.’ The thug said as he slammed his fist on the table. ‘Well if you shut the fuck up and could manage to be quiet for a minute we wouldn’t be having this problem. For fucks sake man, I am not a candy cane bull-shitter. For fucks sakes I work for the DEA so watch your damned mouth before I break it.’ The man said the look on his face was anger. ‘Now listen, we want Token, what do you have on him?’ the DEA agent said as he glared forwards, he wanted answers and he wanted them now. ‘We got what you need to bring Token down,’ the other thug stated confidently as the DEA agent pulled out a black briefcase and slid it across the table. ‘Ten grand is in that case, don’t pull it out and count it. Trust me.’ The agent said as the two men both shrugged and slid the briefcase back under the table. ‘Now listen we heard about something big going on over in Token’s direction. Four dead and it looks like the father of the family shot his two kids and his wife…’ The agent said somberly. ‘Does this tie into Token in anyway?’ Cue the sighs of the men. They thought this would be some random drug charge to put Token away for a few years. Fuck no, this was going for the max on the controversial man. ‘Naa we don’t know anything about it,’ one of the thugs piped up quickly as the agent instantly reached over the table and grabbed the throat of the thug. ‘Ok… ok… It was Token. He did the murders, him and a fellow named John. They set it all up to look like it was a triple homicide plus suicide.’ The thug stated as the agent instantly began to grin as he wrote the information down. He had what he wanted now it was time to leave… Or so he thought. Call Token Weed the man with a thousand ears if need be, he had been keeping tabs on his individuals as of late and had a hunch. Sending the thug to observe and if needed to take out was a perfect plan. Cue up the automatic weapon fire. Instantly a bullet ripped through Mr. DEA Agent’s skull sending brains and blood splattering across the diner onto the bar. The two thugs had time to look over, they looked into the eyes of their once friend. The moment seemed to last an eternity to them as bullets began ripping through their mid-section and head. Token’s loyal soldier put the automatic weapon in a trash can and walked away, he picked up his cell phone and called the boss. ‘It’s done.’ On the other end of the line some where lurking in Los Angeles, Token Weed grinned a sadistic smile. ‘Kill their families too, mothers, brothers, sisters, hell even take the grand parents out.’ The reply from Token Weed rang chillingly in the warm night air in California. ‘By the way, expect to be rewarded heavily for this.’ A simple blunt statement showed the night in LA what it had become with a man like Token Weed lurking in the shadows. His desire to rule with an iron fist and scare tactics echoed through out the world. Is the UTT an amazing way to start a cross country drug traffic? We shall see.
