Somebody to Love

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 A very good friend of mine once told me: "The principle for meditation is simple, but difficult to accomplish. As you strive for perfection in the most complicated of your activities, so should you also strive for that same perfection in everything you do. Each day is started and ended with one breath, and that breath can set the tone for how you will live the rest of that day, or how you will sleep the rest of that night. Focus on achieving the same perfection in breathing as you strive to achieve in wrestling. Let each breath be more then simply breathing, let it suck in all your concerns and worries…and then expel them into the air…so that you may seem them more clearly. Focus on that Purity of Breath, and the rest will follow." That was how Ronin lived every minute of his life, focusing on that one principle of Purity of Breath and letting all his other principles flow from there. He even tried to teach me once, ungrateful brat that I was, and spent the better part of a year trying to help me batter past my infamous impatience. But for all of his teaching, and for all of my trying, I’ve never been able to master the ability to see past the distractions of the day and focus on one thing…always my mind is flying around like some chaotic whirlwind. I’m exhausted. I think I spent basically all of today saying hello to people I’ve never met or hugging old friends I’ve not seen for close to a year. It’s nice to back in Japan again, nice to be under the same roof as Master Mifune again…but even after all my time away I need some time to myself, and I almost thought that long string of greetings was never going to end. Just another reason to wonder… …have I done the right thing? There’s no doubt in my mind that I needed to get out of the Estate, get out of the country even…and stretch my wings a little, give myself a chance to get away from all the…memories I’ve built into that place. But I miss it just the same…that building has been home for as long as I can remember, and even since I started having "odd" guests it’s remained my home. To be away from it now, especially when I’m waiting for…for Lia, is just so odd. To say that I’m not thinking about GTT5 would be ridiculous, but it’s equally ridiculous to think that I’ve got nothing else on my mind right now. I want to go out there and prove to everyone that I’ve still got it, that I can be the wrestler my old man was…but at the same time I want to be with her again, to rest in her arms and feel the press of cool skin against mine. I’ve thought I was in love before you know? With various boys I met in school or in sports or professionally…even managed to date some of those boys, though it never lasted obviously. I even thought I loved CV for a while after…after our fathers passed away so close to each other…but that was puppy love mixed with desperation, we both felt like we had no one else. After CV I never thought I was the type to fall for another woman, especially not one whom I’ve had every opportunity to come into conflict with about everything from wrestling styles to personal hygiene… Am I risking everything we’ve managed to build by focusing so hard on GTT5? I’ve seen her name on the roster, so I know she’s going to be out there too…fuck she’s in my own bracket and that’s a nightmare I don’t even want to think about thinking about. But I want to prove to her that I’ve still got it, at least as badly as I want to prove to everyone else…maybe even a little more. Since when did one person’s thoughts about me matter so much? Gods, does she feel it like I do when we’re apart for so long? Does she have mornings when she wakes and wonders if she can face the day without me there? Does she wonder how crazy things have gotten between us that she can’t stop thinking about me? Does the thought of never seeing me again…kill something inside her? Am I terrible person to hope she feels these things? Have I put too much of myself into her, without even knowing if it runs as deep for her? The winter air here in Japan seems that much colder without her here. No blanket or comforter or heavy coat can take away the chill I feel when I go to bed alone…Gods, why didn’t anyone tell me that it would hurt like this? The only thing I know for certain anymore is that I have to find her, have to at least see her one more time…and hopefully more. No matter where she is, or what she’s doing…I have to find her, and I have to finally tell her the truth…all of it. On nights like tonight…the moon looks so much like your pale face, the stars like your eyes on a raven coloured curtain. Come back to me Lia Radilu Vlaszy, I love you.
