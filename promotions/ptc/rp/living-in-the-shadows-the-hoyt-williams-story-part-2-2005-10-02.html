LIVING IN THE SHADOWS: THE HOYT WILLIAMS STORY (part 2)

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-10-02 [A slide show of pictures depicting Hoyt Williams slamming guys around and preaching the bible is shown in rapid fire with a dirge playing in the background. The pictures are all current showing Hoyt only in PRIME and as a champion.] Narrator: When I left you we had discovered the past of Hoyt Williams. A fatherless child raised by his mother until her tragic death. We heard of his transformation from good student to violent racist. We learned of his alleged vengeance of the men who killed his mother and his belief that he is carrying out God’s will. We revealed his father sent him off to learn to be a wrestler as an alternative to a life of crime. His past has been a hectic rollercoaster of pain and retribution. Now he is an adult with a successful career on the brisk of attempting what only a few have managed, winning the PTC Global title. Tonight we talk to the love of his life Mary. We explore his political action organization the RISP. Plus we finally speak with Hoyt Williams where all questions will be answered in a no holds barred interview. MAIN TITLE SCREEN: LIVING IN THE SHADOWS: THE HOYT WILLIAMS STORY (Part 2) by Al L. -->Fade to: TITLE SCREEN 1: THE MOTHER MARY, THE WOMAN BEHIND THE MAN Narrator: Since his debut in PWC Hoyt Williams has always been graced by the company of a sweet blonde clad in make-up and supporting his every move. Her hair stands so high when it’s teased it seems to reach the heavens. The old country saying goes the higher the hair the closer to heaven and Mary lives by those words. She’s been known as “The Virgin Mary”, “Mary With Her Cherry”, and most currently “The Mother Mary”. No matter what you call her she is a kind soul. She is Hoyt’s fiancée and soon to be mother of his child. Now lets meet Mary White. [The camera opens up inside the north shore Chicago mansion of Hoyt Williams. Sitting on a sofa with our film maker Big Al, is the delicate and obviously very pregnant fiancé of Hoyt Williams, Ms. Mary White. The room has obviously been decorated by Mary as its feminine touch shows with vibrant colors and modern furniture. The only thing that speaks of manliness is the chess table by the window a favorite game of Hoyt’s. Our filmmaker smiles as he begins his interview with Mary.] Big Al: So you are no longer the Virgin Mary? [Mary laughs a shy southern giggle as she waves her hand at the filmmaker before rubbing her large belly.] Mary: Well shucks Al, I guess the secrets out. Big Al: Congratulations. Mary: It was God’s will and Hoyt’s seed. Being as he is the son of God that makes this child a very special child. [Big Al raises an eyebrow as he sips on his hot tea.] Big Al: Is Hoyt the son of God? Mary: Oh no question about it. My soon to be husband is the real deal I’ve seen him work miracles. Big Al: Such as? Mary: Turn water into wine, make handicap men walk again, heck you know Seymour Almasy was born retarded? Big Al: No, that’s news to me. Mary: Oh yea at the age of 20 Almasy had the IQ of a second grader. When Hoyt found him he was eating his own snot and coloring outside the lines in his Elmo coloring book. Look at him now Al when he talks he sounds intelligent, almost! That is a miracle of God! Big Al: Well I didn’t know that I’ll have to confirm it with Seymour. Mary: Oh don’t bother he’s in denial and poisoned by the devil. Once weak minded always weak minded even with my man’s Devine intervention. You see Almasy is the Final Fantasy while my man is the final savior the second coming of Christ the real deal. Big Al: I have to insist on asking HOW DO YOU know he is the real deal? Mary: I have shared my soul and body with him. I have looked in his eyes and have asked him and I can see he was telling me the truth. Nobody can lie to me I have an ability to detect deceit and sniff out dishonesty. How long is this going to take cus Mr. Ed McMahon mailed me a letter saying I was going to be a millionaire I’m hoping he shows up today. [The filmmaker looks at his watch and smiles.] Big Al: Not much longer I’m sure. We’ll get out of the way before Ol’Ed shows up with the prize patrol. Have you ever seen Hoyt cry? Mary: I’m sure he wouldn’t want me to talk about that but yes. I have seen an emotional side of him most folks have never, and will never see. He is a sensitive man. You know about his mama? [Big Al shakes his head acknowledging that he does.] Mary: Yea when he thinks about her on the holidays and what not he cries. He understands why God had to take her from him but it still hurts. He may be a savior but he’s still human. Jesus felt pain when those nails went through his flesh. Flesh and blood feels pain regardless of the soul inside the shell. My Hoyt is no different. Big Al: What makes him laugh? Mary: He enjoys the same things you and the rest of the sinners do a funny movie, a good clean joke, the stupid hair cut of Michael Stevens I mean the list goes on. Big Al: Is there anything Hoyt Williams can’t do? Mary: Get Michael Moore to lose weight. That guy is so full of Satan even Hoyt couldn’t save him. [Mary laughs loudly at her obvious attempt at a joke.] Big Al: What drives your fiancé? Mary: Love. No question about it L-O-V-E. He is a man who loves the world and everything and everyone in it. He fights for the little guy and does what he can to turn the tide on Satan. Hoyt is God’s son and God is love so together they go hand and hand don’t they? He cares for everybody and wants to see the world a better place. Look at what he did with Killean’s wife. Some would see that as evil or mean digging up her body and taking the wedding ring off her dead finger. But you have to understand Hoyt spoke with Killean’s dead wife in heaven and she saw him cheating on her with that red haired tramp. She asked Hoyt to get that ring back and send a message to Killean and that’s what he did. What a man! Big Al: Have you ever seen him talk to the dead or God? Mary: Oh yea we had Jesus over for dinner the other night. He’s a vegetarian which is a pain to cook for but I manage. He and Hoyt had dinner and talked about childhood memories and what not. Jesus helps Hoyt as best he can since he’s experienced this world before. Big Al: Wait a minute Jesus was here in physical form? Mary: Oh yes. Big Al: Does he look the same? Mary: I didn’t see him as I’m a sinner. Hoyt has me cook for Jesus and himself and I leave the plates in a private room. An hour later Hoyt comes out and both plates are finished. Who else ate the food? Big Al: Could Hoyt have eaten both plates? Mary: Oh no, he’s on a strict diet he would never do that. It was Jesus who ate the food. Clearly. Big Al: Hummmm, have you ever heard conversation? Mary: I’m not a busy body I don’t spy but there was this one time I heard Hoyt talking, actually it was yelling while Hoyt was watching a football game. He was in his study watching the Bears and he was cussing up a storm. Now I don’t go for that kind of talk so I went in the room to confront him and he explained to me that he and God were fighting over Hurricane’s and who to strike down and what not. I guess God is a Packer fan because Hoyt kept damning them folk. Big Al: Hoyt and God work together to take people out? Mary: Evil people. Hoyt prayed on TV for God to take out Boda and a week later he got hit by a truck ending his career. Proof once again my man is a powerful and spiritual man. I’m not supposed to say anything about this but I’ll let it slide for your little film. You know Karina Wolfenden? Big Al: One of Hoyt’s opponents in the scavenger hunt match and fellow Prime combatant. Mary: She’s not going to be able to compete if she even shows up. Big Al: Explain? Mary: God and Hoyt have it worked out that the day of the match she will come down with a terrible flu that will leave her unable to wrestle. Even if she pushes through it she won’t win. I can promise you that in the name of GOD himself. Then you can replay this tape and see how right and true we are. Blind faith Big Al I sense you doubt my husband’s legitimacy like most of the other nay sayers out there. That is fine but when you are burning in the depths of hell you will have plenty of time to remember the chances you had at believing in blind faith. Big Al: I’m just making a film giving all the facts. It’s up to the audience to decide if Hoyt is the Son of God or a quick tongued charlatan. Mary: Armageddon is coming and Hoyt owns the stable of the four horses of the Apocalypse. They are his horses that will carry out the end of evil and the rebirth of spiritual bliss. You know how the story ends with good conquering evil why not join the winning team? Hoyt is the top champion in the top wrestling federation in the world. Many have doubted him since day one but those who believe will be reward when Saint Peter opens the gates of heaven and we march in together while those who disbelieved are raped in hell. [Big Al takes a deep breath.] Big Al: Soooooooooooooooo have you come up with any names yet for the baby? Mary: Jesus after his brother or Mary after his mother. Big Al: It’s odd to name a daughter after the mother. That’s not custom. Mary: Custom is out the window when you are talking about the grandson of God. Big Al: Point taken. I’ll leave you be in case Uncle Ed shows up with your check. Thank you for your time. Mary: Thank you. -->Fade To: TITLE SCREEN 2: INSIDE THE RISP [We peer in what appears to a boardroom in a large office building. Sterile, professional, and tactfully decorated this is obviously a very well off organization. A large painting of an eye in a pyramid sits high on the wall over looking the back of the head of the table’s seat.] Big Al: Half religion, half political group and totally profitable this organization the RISP is a mystery to most. A secret society with its roots rumored to be buried deep in history from the Pyramids to US Presidents. What it produces and what it sells is a mystery and given its’ religious status by the Untied States Government we have no access to its tax records. I was given a tour today of the facilities and all I can say is I’m stunned. We were not allowed video access for security reasons but I can tell you what I saw. RISP headquarters is a triangle shaped building with the cap cut off (top tip of the triangle). It has 33 rooms made up of offices, phone banks, research departments, and a very odd military planning room. The all seeing eye of God is everywhere along with statutes of owls and two headed eagles. What I’ve seen is amazing what I wasn’t allowed to see can only make one wonder. What and who are the RISP we find out now as we speak with Hoyt’s right hand man and chief of staff here at the RISP headquarters Jim Pike. [The filmmaker sits at a side seat as the head of the table chair is now filled by a middle aged white man in a perfect fitting suit.] Big Al: What does the RISP stand for? Pike: Humanity my friend. Big Al: Revival Independent Socialist Party is what the web site rumors say. Pike: And they are wrong. We are a hierocracy not socialists so that is absurd. Big Al: You believe religion should dictate action. Pike: It’s not a new concept after all we are one nation UNDER God are we not? [Pike smiles a devious smile as he sips down some water from his clear glass.] Big Al: So RISP stands for nothing? Pike: Again we stand for humanity and brotherhood. Our members know what it means and we are happy with that. Knock Mr. [name withheld] and the door will open for you. Ask and you shall receive knowledge. Big Al: Hoyt Williams has been using wrestling to recruit members for the RISP since day one of his arrival in the wrestling world. It seems he aims for white, middle-class to rich men, who feel cheated by a different world. Pike: There was a time in this country when a black man was the minority. Now with affirmative action, a moral decline of values and general chaos the white man is now at a disadvantage in this backwards world. Big Al: That sounds almost racist. Pike: No, you misinterpreted as our doors are open to all black, white, brown, or yellow men. A brother is equal to another brother and skin color means nothing to God. What we ARE against is the fact a man can call a women a whore and degrade a women on TV and nobody says anything. NOTHING. Not a peep. People on their cell phones using the “F” word in public and nobody even cares. Our values have fallen to Satan and we are here to correct that. Big Al: Is it true that you guys the RISP are a division of any of these secret society’s such as the new world order? Pike: No. Big Al: Ok, how about the order of skull and bones? Pike: No. Big Al: The Illuminati? Pike: Never heard of them. We are simply a group of men who share common values of a traditional world. No gays marrying as sodomy is a sin. No destroying your body with drugs, piercings, or tattoos we are pure and clean are our bodies are our temples to God. We are what the outside world calls “lame”, “out of touch”, “ignorant” because we don’t think children should be exposed to filth, violence, or other tools of Satan. Big Al: Music you hit on, but what are some other tools of Satan? Pike: Fried Chicken holds no nutritional value yet KFC is on every corner. As is McDonalds and nobody cares!! Mothers no longer teach their kids to eat right and what do we have a fat lazy society. My God its nothing for somebody to haul their large asses to the buffet Sunday morning but God forbid they get up to go to church. Why is God now a bad word? Big Al: I don’t think it is. Pike: Well the courts do as you can’t say “Under God” in the pledge. You can’t look at the Ten Commandments at the court house. The ACLU will defend a perverts right to look at child pornography but they won’t defend somebody’s right to look at a cross in a court room. Strange isn’t it? A new world order is on the horizon and no paleo-libertarian can stop us. The RISP is huge and we already control more then you think. Big Al: How big? Pike: Huge. Big Al: Do you think Hoyt is the son of God? Pike: Are we not all son’s of God? [Big Al takes a moment to think to try to throw this man off his game.] Big Al: Is he the Second coming of Christ? Pike: If I said he was would you believe me or mock me? The question is not important when the answer is already in your heart. Open it and see for yourself and all questions will be answered. We are five years from full mobilization of our army that will defeat the evil in this world. You have five years to find that answer before you become one of the evil and are ravaged by our tanks painted with the eye of Rah and commissioned under the grace of God. Armageddon is coming and if you’re too blind to see the light then I can’t enlighten you. Big Al: War? Pike: Read your bible to understand may I suggest Revelations. NOBODY can stand in the way of our goals our military is top of the line working behind the scenes. Hoyt Williams is a skilled athlete but don’t kid yourself before he steps in the ring we know everything about who we are facing. The research is done and the game plan is written and executed. Ms. Wolfenden, Mr. Almasy, Mr. Rollins, The Uber Judge, Mr. Edwards, The Shadow, The Mime, Mr. Stevens, and Mr. Youngblood have all been under surveillance and we have done what needs to be done to even the odds. The Uber Judge has plans of world domination but he doesn’t have the resources and will fail. This is just wrestling can you imagine what else we control? Big Al: Is it true President Bush is a member of the RISP. [A big smile comes over Mr. Pikes face as he winks and takes a sip of water.] Pike: We don’t give out information on other members you’ll have to ask him. I can tell you right now what The Mime is doing, his temperature, where his mother is at, what he ate for breakfast this morning, his fighting style, and when the last time he got laid was. Mr. [name withheld] I know this about you as well. We know all and see all because we are under the direction of God. Mr. Clause has a list that he checks twice to see who was naughty and who was nice. Who do you think gives him that list? Huh? You think it just falls from the sky? DUH the RISP! The owl is watching my friend. Big Al: So Hoyt will win the Global title? Pike: We don’t fail Mr. [name withheld] we are directed by God himself. The deity is done playing games with a worthless culture of sinners. Remember that Mr. [name withheld] it was you the people who walked away from God not the other way around. It was you the sinners who molested tradition in favor of an easier life. For it is been done so the sinners shell reap in the pain of a vengeful God. Big Al: Some frightening words. Pike: Indeed. Big Al: Are you covering up ANY illegal activity here? [Pike slams his water down on the table hard.] Pike: Now we’re not playing nice anymore are we? No. This is strictly a religious organization on the up and up. We play fair Mr. [name withheld] as that is God’s will isn’t it? So mote it be, now will you excuse me I have some paper work to catch up on. Big Al: Sure thing. Pike: Mike will assist you out. [Shots of our filmmaker being escorted out of the building followed by him searching the streets are flashed across the screen. A montage of him searching the internet for information and chat rooms for loose lipped members is shown. Finally we find our filmmaker in a very bland location that looks like a hotel room sitting with a man whose face has been blacked out.] Big Al: For lack of a better word after being thrown out of RISP headquarters I decided to do some investigative reporting. After four days of searching I found an ex-high ranking official of the RISP who has since gone into hiding. He spoke with us only under the promise of anonymity so for this segment we will call him Alex although that is not his real name. Alex what say you? Alex: Thank you for giving me a chance to speak as I’m not sure how much longer I can hide. Big Al: Are you in danger? Alex: Very much so. The RISP controls the mafia along with most of the gangs in the country. Big Al: They engage in illegal activity? Alex: 80% of all drug trafficking comes from one of their organizations. Big Al: They don’t sound like a very spiritual origination. Is it the money? Alex: No. [The answer catches Big Al off guard as he shakes his head like come again.] Big Al: Then what do they do it for? Alex: To poison those willing to be poisoned. They control the banks and the money already they don’t do it for the money they do it for the zombies. Big Al: Zombies? Alex: People strung out on drugs are so much easier to control then somebody free of addiction. The RISP wants a weak society so that when they concur our world the control will come easy with little uprising. They create to oppress companies like Nike and Sean John. You see they hold down low income people by having them spend their money on culture instead of necessity thus rendering them useless as dependents of the Government. The less educated, the less functional, and the less motivated a society is the less threat those in control with the money have. Who makes up the congress? RICH PEOPLE. They are not making laws to help the people they are making laws to nurture and mother the people as a good parents or masters do. Do you understand the RISP is behind a global conspiracy and they will not stop until they create a one world government under God and headed by Hoyt the chosen one? These men have been working the system long before Hoyt just waiting for his arrival to begin Armageddon. Big Al: Are they a member of the order of skull and bones, the Freemasons, the new world order, or the illuminati? Alex: Members of? You don’t get it they ARE the Illuminati!! The RISP is the militaristic branch of the Illuminati where as Imperium is their executive branch. Order of Skull and Bones are the political fraction of the Illuminati. Do your research and you will find both John Kerry and George W. Bush are members of the Skull and Bones. Hoyt controls them all as the alleged son of God. Big Al: Do you think he is the son of God? Alex: No, but they sure do. I’ve seen pentagrams and other symbols of Satan I’m not sure what side the Illuminati is on but I know one thing they are hell bent on taking over the world. This war in Iraq is just a small sample of what’s in store. Big Al: Will they succeed? [The man takes a deep breath to think the question over.] Alex: Yes. Big Al: You think so? Alex: The RISP is ready to fight, both the US and British army is at the control of members of the Skull and Cross Bones, the Supreme Court is made up of hand picked Illuminati members. This thing goes deeper then you can even imagine Trump, Greenspan, Letterman, Turner, Republicans, Democrats, it doesn’t matter most are members of the one of the fractions in the RISP and all are Illuminati. Hoyt’s continuous success in the wrestling world is a gauge symbolic of success in the real world. Global Champion? That sounds dangerous. Big Al: What can be done? Alex: Nothing. Join them or be consumed by them they are everywhere and know everything. Big Al: They haven’t found you yet? Alex: Just a matter of time my friend. I’m a dead man walking. Big Al: Thank you for your bravery. -->Fade to: TITLE SCREEN 3: THE MAN/SAVIOR KNOWN AS HOYT Narrator: This documentary was meant to be a look at a wrestler. A fictional character in a sport of titans dancing in a ring. What I have found is something deeper. Possibly a killer, a leader in a secret organization, and maybe something more. Could he really be the son of God or have I been worked over this entire show. No man has EVER interviewed Hoyt one on one who wasn’t a member of the RISP. I have to admit when I started this journey I thought this would be the cool part now I find myself scared to face this man. Here it is my unapologetic interview with the PRIME Universal Champion he is known as “Your Personal Jesus” he is Hoyt Williams. [Sitting at a chess table in his home is Hoyt Williams dressed in a sparkling robe of jewels. Not your typical house coat but this is one strange man. Playing chess with him is the filmmaker Big Al in a 20/20 style interview shot.] Big Al: Thank you for allowing me to speak with you. Hoyt: No problem my slightly overweight sinner. You know God has a weight limit in heaven and if your ass is just the slightest bit saggy you’ll sink right through those clouds straight to hell. I recommend a diet for you my man. Big Al: Well thanks for the advice but let’s talk about you. [Hoyt grabs the filmmaker’s wrist and ties a W.W.H.D. shoestring bracelet around it.] Hoyt: Now remember before you ask a stupid question examine, stare, and study that arm reminder and ask yourself WHAT WOULD HOYT DO? Big Al: Ok, first question is a straight forward one. Are you the second coming of Christ? Hoyt: Yes. I grew up as a spirit in heaven and was given this earth shell to defend its glory as the leader of a revolution. Big Al: Many think it’s a wrestling gimmick. Hoyt: Many people just don’t get it. I spoke to God and he tells me you don’t believe and I’m sorry for that. I’m the King of Kings, the Pontiff of PTC, the Savior of Man, and Your Personal Jesus. I have the PRIME universal title do you? No, you are not good enough born of original sin and stinking up my world. You don’t believe, then may I hear your cry’s and smell your burning flesh from your spot in the depths of hell to my throne at the right hand of God. [Hoyt moves his bishop and takes out one of Al’s pawns. He then reaches into his pocket and pulls out a Polaroid photo.] Hoyt: Sorry for snapping at you it’s been a rough day. My friend Roy Obrecki was murdered last night. He was a good guy and a trusted advisor. Somebody slit his throat this morning such a terrible world. [Big Al looks down at a photo of the man he interviewed just a night ago as a cold chill shakes down his spine. “Alex” is laying on a sidewalk in a pull of blood with a cut throat. Al hands the photo back to Hoyt who winks at him.] Big Al: Oh God. Hoyt: Hoyt’s fine. What’s a matter it looks like you knew him? Just a commentary on the world we live in. This is what I’m talking about a Godless society that would allow this to happen. You think we, after all these years of alleged civility would have evolved into a land of compassion and brotherhood. Oh well. Big Al: Like the war the RISP is planning for a one government nation of “values”. Hoyt: What? Big Al: That’s what Jim Pike said. [Hoyt lets out a hardy laugh.] Hoyt: He was messing with you. Pulling your leg the RISP is a merchandise company that I use to sell wrestling and religious perihelia. A military? [Hoyt laughs hard as he takes another Pawn with his knight.] Hoyt: Listen all kinds of rumors surround me and that’s why I allowed this film to be made. I’m just a wrestler who also happens to be the son of God. No big deal. Big Al: What about the Illuminati? Hoyt: What’s that, I have never ever heard of it? Big Al: Come on. Hoyt: Come on what I don’t know what you are talking about. Big Al: Tell me about your father. Hoyt: He was a bad man who is being born again. He left me to fend for myself as a child but I forgive him because I’m a good man and that’s the RISP thing to do, forgive. Big Al: He works for you now? Hoyt: He does and his in ring experience has been an asset. Again I forgive him for his past and the wrongs he caused me. Big Al: Tell me about your mother? Hoyt: She was a perfect women and I miss her very much. Big Al: She died a violent death how did you deal with it? Hoyt: Like any young man I fell off the biblical wagon and became a bad kid. But I recovered and that’s what matters. Big Al: I talked with a former gang member who said you got revenge on those who did wrong to your mother. Hoyt: Gang member? I was never in a gang and the only revenge I ever got was when I heard the guys who killed my mother were killed themselves in a gang shooting. I don’t know who you’ve been talking to but you are way off. [Big Al’s bishop takes Hoyt’s Knight] Big Al: I have also heard tales of violence and vengeance against those who have done you wrong. Hoyt: I’m a pro-wrestler you must be talking about my actions in the ring. That is what I do. I carry out the lords work IN THE RING. Big Al: What about the real world. Hoyt: Politically maybe but not in the same fashion I do it in the ring. Big Al: You scout your opponents? Hoyt: I do being the son of God I to get to know all and see all. I know Almasy picked on some idiot street preacher. WOW what a statement you can shake up some worthless scab of society who thinks he’s the voice piece of God. The difference is I’m not a voice piece I am the voice. I am the voice of God with a direct link. I’m not some phony preacher trying to make another buck. Money means nothing to me. I am the last hope of humanity. God is pissed off right now and when he gets pissed bad things happen. The ark is being built as we speak if you get what I’m saying. My words and my actions are the last chance for humanity before the world is washed clean again. Big Al: Will you succeed? Hoyt: I will not fail my fathe. The Uber Judge is talking about being a supreme being a judge of man while he plays games, I do it. It’s not an easy job nor do I get health insurance but it’s a rewarding job none the less. SO MANY, SO MANY false prophets out there trying to be me and they can’t do. They can’t achieve the things I have like winning PRIME’s top prize or running off people like Boda, Killean, and some even say IVAN retired because he heard of my footsteps in OSW. Big Al: The Global title scavenger match how do you feel about it? Hoyt: How can you hide something from somebody who knows all? This little game fits right into our master plan as I snag the PTC global title. Ten men and two titles is playing against the odds but you can in the name of God place a bet that I win it. It’s not that I’m that damn good it’s that I’m that damn blessed. While the baloney tit’s of the Harvard fans wiggle like Jello I’ll be taking the rest of the field to school. Listen Jesus never went to Harvard he was a carpenter but he got the job, almost done. I mean they did send him to boarding school but that was because he was a hippie. I’m his younger jock brother and I will finish the job. I’ve put my mind too it. Big Al: So you’re not a member of a secret organization or anything else. Hoyt: Let’s not worry about that kind of stuff on a said day like this where my friend…our friend…”Alex” was so grossly killed. As a matter of fact I’m going to win the match for Alex and all the other people of principle out there watching. That my sinning friends is the Gospel Truth. Big Al: Thanks for your time. Hoyt: Check Mate. [Hoyt takes Al’s king.] Narrator: Who is Hoyt Williams? A man NOT living in the shadows of his father but rather a cloud of mystery. We’ve given you the facts and asked the questions now you must decide if Hoyt is the real deal or not. Thank you for your time and I hope you enjoyed The Hoyt Williams Story. I’m Al L. =w=
