A toasts to all us underdogs…

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 "Last call, last call for alcohol." As the lone bartender calls out Reverend Blaze quickly pounds one of his last remaining shots that are lined up in front of him. He slowly raises his arm to get the bartender's attention, as he does he pounds yet another shot. By the time the bartender arrives to him he has downed the final shot in front of him. "Captain Morgan's private stock, line 'em up." The bartender takes a long look at him. "I think you have had enough buddy." "I'll tell you when I had enough! Now don't make me come over this bar!" The bartender turns around and begins to walk away but Reverend Blaze pulls out a hundred dollar bill and tosses it at the bartender who bends over and picks it up. He pause for a moment before grabbing the bottle off the shelf behind him and heading over to Reverend Blaze. "Here pal, take it and get the hell out of here." Reverend Blaze grabs up the bottle and a nearby shot glass and slowly stands up to leave the bar. He pauses by the door before turning around and looking around the bar. All that's left at this time of night is people trying to drown their sorrows and other searching for their personal demons at the bottom of a bottle. Reverend Blaze grabs a nearby chair and stands on it, he clinks the shot glass and bottle together to get anybodies attention but nobody pays him any mind. "Here's a toasts people, a toasts to the underdogs in us all. Raise your glass with me and drink you fill, raise your glass with me cause the world never will. The world will never love us or think we have a place, will I say screw the world and spit me liquor in its face!" The bartender reaches under the bar and grabs his axe handle to handle the unruly partons at his bar. "Hey pal that's enough of that, now get the hell out!" Reverend Blaze chuckles a little bit and jumps down off the chair and stumbles for the door. As he reaches the door Reverend Blaze looks back at the bar and knows that in the upcoming GTT5 and tonight is his first and last night to indulge in things of this nature. Come this time next week, Reverend Billy Blaze will show the world that the underdog should never be taken lightly.
