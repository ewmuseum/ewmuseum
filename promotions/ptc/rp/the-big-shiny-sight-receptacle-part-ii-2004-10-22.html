The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle - Part II

22 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-22 WASHINGTON, D.C. - JIMMY BONG'S HOTEL ROOM LATER THAT EVENING NOT-SO-BROAD DAYLIGHT As the skies darkened over the streets of Washington, D.C., so too did the mood of Jimmy Bong. Back and forth, he paced the length of the hotel room he was sharing with Majorly Shafted, frequently glancing at the human shield with growing anxiety. Iva Gunnonmi was now safely with Bong and Shafted, and at that moment was taking a long, hot shower in the adjacent bathroom. Shafted was busily hooking up what appeared to be some sort of long-range communications device that had been built into a bulky silver briefcase. "Are you quite finished yet, Shafted?" asked Bong impatiently. "I want to get in touch with N as soon as possible." "Not yet," mumbled Shafted in reply. "Oh, fine," said Bong. He paced the length of the room once more. "How about now?" he asked no more than a moment later. "Just a few more minutes!" Shafted snapped back. "Egad man, you've asked me about a dozen times already! I told you, it takes time to set up this equipment! I have to calibrate this thing and then I can hook it up to the telly, but it's going to take a bit longer, so please show some bloody patience!" "Bah, very well," groaned Bong. "I'll just step out onto the balcony for a moment then." He turned around and made his way to the sliding glass door that led out onto the balcony of their hotel room. "Why don't you throw yourself off while you're at it..." said Shafted under his breath. "Eh? What was that?" Bong asked, looking back at Shafted. "Nothing, I was merely thinking about how wonderful it would be if you would just kill yourself," replied Shafted in a much clearer voice. Bong chuckled. "Haha, my goodness, I've heard that several times before," he said as he stepped out onto the balcony. The night sky was just as clear as it had been during the daytime, and the moon was shining in its fullest this evening. Bong leaned forward with his arms folded against the railing, taking in the breathtaking sight of the Washington skyline. The room was about ten stories up, which gave Bong an excellent view of the city streets, which were bustling with activity. The headlights of cars snaked their way through an urban maze of pavement, enhancing the illumination of the streetlights that lined each city block. The British secret agent stared fixedly at the scene before him, taking in everything and allowing it to momentarily wash away the qualms in his mind. His eyes trailed away from the cityscape, down to the alley below where Dmitri Kolehnkov had tried to kill Iva just hours before. The alley was illuminated by the moonlight, and as far as Bong could tell, the scene hadn't been disturbed since he and Iva had been there. Bong let out a small sigh of relief -- at least that meant the police didn't know that a man had been killed there, he figured. Kolehnkov's blood had dried on a wooden crate and on the far brick wall, as well as on the ground directly in front. "Hm, maybe I should go down there with a mop and some rags and clean up that mess," Bong seriously considered, becoming desperate to somehow alleviate the waiting. He looked back through the glass door to see if Shafted was finished yet. Alas, he still appeared quite busy, and Bong thought better of bothering him. But then, something just inside the door caught his eye... "Hello, what's that?" Bong asked himself curiously. He stepped back through the sliding glass door, and examined the thing that had captured his attention. It was a sniper rifle, leaning upright against the wall -- no doubt the same one Shafted had used to take down Kolehnkov earlier that afternoon. "Oh goody, an actual gun!" Bong said happily. "I never get to hold one of these things! Do you mind if I have a go, Shafted?" He picked up the rifle, not even waiting for Shafted's response. Bong slipped the attached strap over his shoulder, and struck an action pose. "Bong, what the bloody hell are you doing!?" wailed Shafted, looking up from his work. "Put that thing down now!!" "Oh come on!" Bong almost whined. "I just wanted to look like one of those fellows you always see in those motion pictures, with all those explosions and guns and whatnot. You know, like that Mohatma Ghandi chap." "Ghandi was a pacifist!" exclaimed Shafted. "Oh," said Bong. "Well, nonethelesss, those action movie stars are quite muscular, aren't they? I wish I was as well-built as Ghandi was." Shafted clapped a hand over his face. "Do you even listen to yourself, Bong?" he asked. "I try not to," Bong answered. "It gets very confusing otherwise." "Just put the rifle down, will you?" Shafted asked, trying to calm himself. "I'm about finished hooking up this thing." Bong sighed with disappointment. "Fine," he mumbled. "But can I at least shoot you once?" "What!?" exclaimed Shafted, shocked. "You're a human shield, it's not like it's going to hurt you!" Bong said, taking aim at Shafted with the rifle now. "NO! ABOSLUTELY NOT!" yelled Shafted. "Put that down! Don't waste any bullets! The ammo clip in that rifle is the only one I have!" "Fine," said Bong, lowering the rifle. "Thank you," Shafted said, quite irritated. "Now, I'll be done in a moment..." He removed the shoulder strap and turned to set it against the wall, when the rifle accidentally slipped from his hands and dropped to the floor! "Agh!" yelped Bong, diving behind one of the hotel beds, half-expecting the rifle to discharge. Fortunately however, there was no gunshot. Bong peeked up from behind the bed, and sighed heavily in relief. He made his way back over to the rifle, and saw that the ammo clip had fallen out. Bong picked it up and examined it curiously for a moment, then nonchalantly tossed it a few times into the air and caught it with one hand. "Hmph, if I'm not allowed to play with guns, then he shouldn't be either!" Bong said to himself. He slipped the ammo clip into his pocket with a sly grin. "Bong, you are a master prankster," he thought. "Alright, I'm finally done," sighed Shafted. "Oh goody!" said Bong with glee. "Hurry up then, let's make contact with OHMCS!" "I'm sure N just loves the fact that we're holding this meeting in the middle of the night over there," said Shafted sarcastically, as he switched on the power. Bong sat down at the foot of his bed, watching the television intently. Shafted was seated on the floor to Bong's left, fiddling with the console controls and trying to find the right signal. A video camera was set up on the right, no doubt to allow N to view Bong and Shafted face-to-face from her London office. The television hissed and crackled as the screen initially showed nothing but static. After a moment, however, the face of N, head of OHMCS, came into clear view -- she was wearing only a thick, pink woolen robe, and her hair was all done up in curlers. There was no make-up on her wrinkly face, and there were large bags underneath her eyelids. "Horrors!" Bong shrieked at the sight. "It's the boogeyman!!" "Shut up, Double-Oh-Nothing!" snapped N. "You know perfectly well what time of night it is over here! Now, if you gentlemen could please wait patiently for a just one moment more, U will be joining us shortly." "But we're already here," said Bong. "Not 'you,' Double-Oh-Nothing, U!" N said angrily. "But..." Bong started. "Don't even say it," N said, cutting him off. "Say what?" Bong asked genuinely. "Why does everybody get so angry when I try to explain myself? I'm just..." "An idiot. Yes Bong, I know," N interrupted again. "...I was going to say 'confused,' but that also sounds about right," Bong mumbled. A small picture-in-picture box suddenly appeared in the bottom right corner, heralding the arrival of U, the head of OHMCS Weapons Research & Development. He was clad in blue pajamas with white pinstripes, and a floppy nightcap on his head. "Good evening Shafted, Double-Oh-Nothing," U yawned. "How did the exchange go with Kolehnkov and Gunnonmi? Did you get the information they had for us?" "Well, yes," Bong started. "Although there was a slight complication." "A 'slight complication!?'" Shafted scoffed. "Kolehnkov tried to kill Iva!" "What!?" exclaimed N and U at the same time. "Kolehnkov tried to kill Iva," repeated Shafted. "They had arrived at the drop-off point, when Kolehnkov drew his gun. I had to take him down from our hotel balcony." "Why on earth would Kolehnkov turn on one of his own agents?" asked N. "Our records indicate has a remarkable service record since the collapse of the Soviet Union. He isn't the sort who would betray his own country. It doesn't make any sense... did Agent Gunnonmi have any idea why Kolehnkov tried to kill her?" "Iva had no idea whatsoever," said Bong. "Maybe those disks will give us some answers," muttered U. "Bong, give the disks to Shafted so he can send us the data on those disks." "Right," said Bong, reaching into his pocket. He procured the two red disks that Kolehnkov had dropped in the alley, and handed them to Shafted. Shafted slipped one of them into a disk drive on the side of the briefcase console, and typed a few commands. "Sent," said Shafted. "Got it," said U after a moment. "Shouldn't take too long to see what's on this little devil." U appeared to be typing something on his end of the transmission, and then his eyes looked as if they were reading a computer screen off-camera. A few moments passed, and his eyes widened with shock. "I don't believe this," he gasped. "It's Project Perestroika!" N gasped. Bong and Shafted gave each other quzzical looks. "What the bloody hell is... 'Project Stroganoff?'" asked Bong, confused. "Not stroganoff Bong, Perestroika!" N corrected. "Wasn't 'Perestroika' just a Soviet economic policy during the presidency of Gorbachov?" asked Shafted. "Yes, but it's a little-known fact that it used to mean 'blow up the Americans,'" stated U matter-of-factly. Shafted screwed up his face with confusion, and Bong cocked a curious eyebrow, as U continued explaining the situation. "At any rate, Project Perestroika was rumored to be the Soviets' trump card over the American nuclear arsenal... but as far as we knew, the project never got off the ground. We didn't even know what the project was, to be honest, and there didn't really seem to be any cause for concern. It was really little more than a fairy tale. But this data indicates otherwise..." "Are you saying Garfeld has re-started Project Perestroika?" asked Bong. "No," said U. "I'm saying that Project Perestroika was already finished. The Soviets had a fully-developed prototype of the weapon, and they had launched it into space." "You mean there's an abandoned Soviet doomsday weapon just floating around above the planet!?" Bong exclaimed. U nodded solemnly. "And what's even more outrageous," he continued, "is that the Americans apparently knew about this, and they never told any of their allies! Including us!" "Absurdity!" said N. "Why on earth would the Americans keep us in the dark on something like this!?" U just shook his head, as he typed a few more commands and read some new information that came up on his screen. "Astonishing," U muttered. "Simply astonishing. There's a schematic layout of the weapon, and colored photographs. I'm transferring them to your screens now." A second later, a photograph of what appeared to be a satellite floating around in space appeared on the television. Bong looked at it closely. The satellite, which he presumed to be the weapon in question, had a brilliant luster. It was shaped rather like an almond, and had a large glass window in the center, which looked remarkably similar to a pupil. "Wow, it looks golden," Bong commented, "and it's shaped like an eye! Why, this is..." "No Bong, no it's not," U snapped. "Are you trying to get us sued!?" "Sorry," whimpered Bong. "Anyway," said U, "you're actually on the right track with the name, Bong. The Soviets called it... 'The Big Shiny Sight Receptacle.'" Bong blinked. "What kind of stupid name is that for a doomsday weapon?" he asked. "The name doesn't matter, Double-Oh-Nothing," said U. "What matters is that it has incredible large-scale destructive power!" "What does it do, exactly?" asked Bong. "Is it some sort of machine that fires some kind of death ray from space?" "That's the most unusual bit of it," answered U. "According to this, it does fire 'some kind of death ray,' as you so eloquently put it. But it's not a machine... it's a human being." Everyone was silent now. They were all wondering the same thing. What did U mean, "a human being?" "It seems," U continued, seeing that his audience was completely shocked, "that during the height of the Cold War, the Soviets were heavily into biomechanical research, trying to find ways to augment the abilities of a normal human being. I don't know if any of you are familiar with the character 'Cyclops' from the X-Men, but..." "Ooh! Is he the guy with the magic wand and the thunderbolt scar on his head?" Bong asked excitedly. "That's Harry Potter, Double-Oh-Nothing!" exclaimed U. "Oh," said Bong. "Anyway," U went on, "the Soviets discovered a way to alter the genetic makeup of the human eye, to allow it to absorb solar energy, and later discharge it in the form of a deadly ray of light, burning everything in its path. There was one scientist in particular who was quite unbalanced, and envisioned using that biotechnology on an astronomical scale. He was the one who began Project Perestroika, and oversaw the development of the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle. The weapon is designed as a small, eye-shaped capsule, containing a person who's eyes have been genetically altered to capture the rays of the sun, and discharge the energy in a single blast. The glass window at the "pupil" of the capsule is actually a special kind of magnifying glass, which greatly amplifies the light beam, increasing its destructive power exponentially, giving it the ability to wipe out an entire city with a single blast!" Bong scratched his head. "So wait, are you just saying that the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle is a man floating around in space that can blow up whatever he looks at?" U sighed. "Yes, Double-Oh-Nothing, the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle is a man floating around in space that can blow up whatever he looks at." "Well that's not very encouraging," said Bong. "But I wonder, if he -- well, whoever it is -- has been floating around up in space all this time, how come nothing has been blown up?" "My first question would have been 'how is he still alive?'" grumbled Shafted. "Well, I'm not sure how to answer your question, Shafted," U admitted, "but to explain how the weapon has never been used, the answer is simple. There was a special security visor designed to fit over the person's eyes. It can only be unlocked with a proper clearance code down here on the ground. After that, it's open season on the entire planet." "So," said Bong. "That's Garfeld's plan, then. He's going to take over the world by threatening to unleash the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle on civilization." "Indeed," said N solemnly. "Hm, what's on the second disk, I wonder?" asked U. "Let's find out," said Shafted, removing the first disk and inserting the second. After a few keystrokes, the information was relayed to U, who quickly analyzed it. Once again, his reaction was complete and total shock. "Inconceivable!" U exclaimed. "This disk! It contains the clearance codes for operating the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle!" "WHAT!?" everyone shouted. "That's unimaginable!" U continued. "What in the world was Kolehnkov doing with the clearance codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle!?" "That might explain why he tried to kill Iva," Shafted offered. "Maybe he was secretly working for Garfeld. The only way Garfeld can access the codes for the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle is to get them from the Russian government, and clearly he would need an inside man to accomplish that. Maybe Kolehnkov was only pretending to help us. After all, why else would the Russian government allow him access to such precious information?" "Good point, Shafted," said N. "And if that's the case, then that means one thing... Garfeld doesn't have the codes yet! That means his plan can't move forward!" Bong smiled. "And that means all we have to do now is find him, and it will all be over!" "Yes, and the CIA knows where he is," N continued. "So Bong, you need to pay them a little visit." Bong's face sank. "What? Are you saying I have to break into the CIA now?" "Exactly," said N. "Bloody hell, I'll be killed if they catch me!" "Indeed," said N with a smile. "That's just great," mumbled Bong.
