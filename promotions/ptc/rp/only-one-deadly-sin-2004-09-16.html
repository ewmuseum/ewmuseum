Only one deadly sin?

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 A member of the seven deadly sins? Jakob sat back in his chair and stared at the people moving on the streets below him. So much like ants, with their own miserable little existence and nothing to show for it. He was different, he had what he wanted and still was after more. Greed would do that to a person. Making them wish with all their hearts that they had this and that and when they do have this and that, making them want the other too. Standing up, he let his arms fall to his sides in a relaxed pose, nothing aggressive to the stance, more one of not caring about the world at large, only his little part of it. His long black hair tied tightly back in a braided tail hung to the middle of his back, his hands clad in the familiar tight fingerless gloves scrunched as he made a fist. His legs though muscular, are clad in comfortable jeans, his upper body is bare. His eyes though are cold and vacant to the first look, but should one peer deeper, they would see a fire for those things that make a man wealthy in many ways. Letting a slow sigh escape from his lips, he speaks aloud knowing as usual, that there is a camera somewhere in the room taping him. “ I stand here before the world a man that is both poor and rich. I have money, houses, cars and any woman I want. So then why would I be poor? Because I haven’t the fame that goes with it all. In some places I am revered as a destructive force in the ring and beyond. Other places I am nothing more than a nobody to those around.” Stepping back from the window, he walks to the fireplace and looks at the flames licking at the tender wood that was placed inside the grate for an offering to the gods of ash. “ This Tournament, with so many entrants, this is the place for me to be known, this is the place to quench my thirst for fame, but I still intend to take what I can from the simpletons that come to these matches, these tournaments. I want their money, I want their adulation and I want my fame. DO YOU HEAR ME? I WANT MY FAME!!” Breathing hard with a raw look of want on his face, he turns to the camera. “ YOU HEAR ME? Erector, Rzarector or whatever the hell your name is, YOU will be the one to give it to me. Its mine you hear me? IT”S MINE!” Moving past the camera, he slams the door behind him as the camera fades.
