PTC — Roleplay

Delta Upsilon Iota/The Metallo Zords/Lag Sverige

25 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The screen fills with a black sky sparsely speckled with white stars on the backdrop. The scene of outer space is calm and silent, but that is soon interrupted by the sound of rolling drums. A classical march of royal sounding origins plays loudly now as rolling yellow text begins scrolling from the bottom of the screen and moving up, slowly creeping farther away like the text at the beginning of a Star Wars movie. The text continues to scroll, as a deep powerful voice speaks the words as they work their way up the screen.:: Voice- In a time of great need on the planet Earth, an intergalactic defender known throughout the universe by the name of Bobbalu, traveled to our planet in order to build a new base of operations for his mission of interplanetary defense. Along with his robotic helper and confidant, Bobbabu, the once great hero searched far and wide for a new force to face down the tyrannous forces of the evil Queen Mogombu and her nefarious husband, King Nanahaboomboom. Once he found two capable of such a task, the great Bobbalu bestowed to them tremendous powers and abilities, transforming them into and unstoppable force for truth and justice. In addition to their newfound powers, the two young heros were also able to call upon a pair of long forgotten ancient robotic assault vehicles. Since that time, the identities of the two heros have remained a closely guarded secret from the villains they fight and ones whom they protect alike, the stories of their exploits however have not. The tale of their trials and tribulations have become the lore of legends and has spread their name far and wide, the name of the . . . Metallo Zords!! ::We open in the control room of the Metallo Zords in which the Silver Ranger is busily working away at a large red heavy bag that is attached to the ceiling by a long silver chain. The Silver Ranger kicks and punches at the bag in an almost unsettling synchronicity with the hum of electricity as it courses through the various computers and machinery located throughout the metallic room. Suddenly a portal like door slides open as the Gold Ranger runs into the room in a very excited fashion as he waves his arms above his head.:: Gold Ranger- I cannot believe it! The new LOW management team has loaded us down with work again! ::The voice of his partner breaks the concentration of the Silver ranger as he turns from the bag and begins to question the Gold Ranger’s excitement.:: Silver Ranger- What did they do this time? ::From his body language, we can tell that the Gold Ranger can barely contain himself. Quickly he holds up a piece of paper with some printing on it and begins pointing at it as he begins to explain.:: Gold Ranger- Our shot at the tag team titles is not the only match we have this Sunday at Pulse. We are set to take part in an interpromotional triple threat tag team match as well. We are going to be facing Lag Sverige from Core Wrestling and Delta Iota Upsilon from Global Underground! ::Now fully understanding his partner’s excitement, the Silver Ranger reaches out and grabs onto the Gold Ranger’s shoulders as he tries to calm him.:: Silver Ranger- It is fine, LOW probably just needed a team that they could rely on to represent them. Who did you think they would look to . . . the Sakio System? ::The Gold Ranger nods his head in understanding as he responds.:: Gold Ranger- I guess you are right, it is our job to make sure the LOW name is upheld. I just hate to expend our energy when we have the chance to make history by becoming the first ever two time LOW tag team champions later that night. ::Shrugging off the true challenge that this extra match is going to be for the Metallos, the Silver Ranger fires back in a reassuring tone.:: Silver Ranger- Do not worry about it my metallic mate, we are the Metallo Zords. We can handle anything they want to throw at us. I mean at least we don’t have to worry about that evil Queen Mogombu this week . . . ::Suddenly the room is filled with the ear shattering wail of some type of siren as its shrill sound slices through the air. The walls of the room flash red with light reflecting off of their metallic surface from some unseen source. Just then the large cylindrical tube situated at the center of the room fills with the familiar blue face of Bobbabu. He is soon joined by the Gold and Silver Rangers as they race over to the blue glow of the viewing orb closely followed by Bobbalu. As the Metallo Zords stare into the hypnotic glow of the viewing orb to see what has caused this disturbance, Bobbalu quickly scurries about pressing buttons on the numerous control panels. Judging from their body language, the Metallo Zords seem to be in quite a panic at the situation. The Gold Ranger calls out in his robotic voice.:: Gold Ranger - What is going on?! What is the problem?! ::Now joining in with his panicking partner, the Silver Ranger calls out to Bobbalu.:: Silver Ranger - Have you found anything yet Bobbalu?! What should we do?! ::In a frenzied state as he continues rushing back and forth between the computers, hitting buttons almost at random, Bobbalu responds.:: Bobbalu - I cannot seem to get a lock on the source of the disturbance. The only information that I have been able to find is that something is going on in the town park. I am trying to bring up a picture in the viewing globe as we speak you two. ::Hearing this the Metallo Zords rush into an open area of the room as the Gold Ranger speaks.:: Gold Ranger - We do not have time for that Bobbalu. ::The Metallo Zords turn to face Bobbalu as the Silver Ranger continues the statement.:: Silver Ranger - Yes, people may be in danger as we speak. ::The Metallo Zords now look to each other as they realize that their hopes of a calm and quiet week have once again been shattered.:: Metallo Zords- Here we go again . . . ::Without a second thought, the Metallo Zords grab onto their belt buckles and are instantly engulfed in a gold and silver beam respectively and transported out of the command center. As our heros head to the park, ready to do battle with whatever evil forces may be lurking there this week, the scene fades out. What kind of threat awaits our heros? Will the Metallo Zords have enough energy to carry out two matches this Sunday at LOW’s Pulse? Can a hero ever truly have a quiet week without any trouble? Will the Metallo Zords make history by becoming the first two time LOW tag team champions? Find out all this and more, next time with the Metallo Zords.::
