The Bounty Hunter Saga (Part 2)

26 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dallas Days Inn Hotel: October 14, 2003 9:00 am The sun shone through the cracks in the curtains that cover the hotel room’s lone window. The curtains shine and glimmer in the morning sunlight. The rays of sun shine through and onto the sleeping body of Myasato Hyasaki. He stirs slightly as the bright sunlight, begins to waken him from his deep sleep. He finally becomes completely awake, and wipes his face down. He is sweating slightly as the room is now much warmer than when he had gone to sleep. He began to sit up, and wiped his eyes with his forearm. He also wiped his face clean of the sweat that had begun to build up throughout the course of the night. He glanced at the small alarm clock radio that was on the night table. It read 9:00 a.m. He shook himself, and could not believe that he was up so early. Myasato stood and walked over to his small duffle bag. He pulled out a new shirt, a new pair of boxers and socks, and a fresh pair of jeans. He walked into the bathroom and shook off the boxer-briefs that he was currently wearing. He quickly hopped into the shower, and cranked up the hot water. The steam rose off of his body as it made impact with his skin. It burned slightly but it felt good, almost as if each drop was a deep tissue massage. Ten minutes later Myasato emerged from the bathroom. He was now wearing a pair of blue jeans, a fresh Armani T-shirt, and a slickly gelled hair-do. He walked out of the bathroom, and quickly through his shoes on, he looked at the small clock reading 9:45 a.m. as he left, it didn’t bother him he was up this early. He needed to do some shopping, and it was like the saying goes, the early bird gets the worm. He walked out of the door leaving a small pile of laundry on the floor. He left the bed messy and the table unorganized; it was still covered with papers about the Souls of the Black Lotus. As Myasato left the room, he shut the door behind him, just after checking his pockets to make sure he had the keycard. He shut the door as he went, closing it and making sure to see if it was locked. He began to walk towards the elevator; the taxi was already waiting outside. Secret Munitions Depot Just Outside Dallas: October 14, 2003 10:50 am “Alright then, so let me see what you have in stock,” the voice was that of Myasato Hyasaki. He had been told by Ishuzu that this underground gun outlet existed; he had not believed it at first. But now, now he was seeing it for himself, and he had no choice but to believe it. He was standing outside a small metal re-enforced door and was awaiting the man inside to allow him access. He needed inside the gun shop, because in order to take out the Souls of the Black Lotus, he was going to need some serious firepower. He waited patiently as he heard the familiar sound of several locking mechanisms being turned off. The door swung open in front of him, but no figure emerged from the shadows behind the door. They were smart enough not to show their faces where anyone could see them. He stepped inside slowly and cautiously, and the door was immediately closed and locked back up again. He felt slightly nervous and began to feel his chest for his Glock 9mm, he felt the handle of the gun, and was instantly reassured. He followed the shadowy figure that had let him in, down the long flight of stairs. He walked slowly and cautiously, even though Ishuzu had recommended him to this place, it did not settle his mind or heart any due to the small lighting in the building. He walked down into a small room where the lighting got slightly better. The walls were made of bricks, but carried water stains and mould, the sure fire signs of time. Myasato stood at the doorway as the man who had led him down the stairs walked over to the far side of the room. He leaned over and spoke to an older man who was seated on an old couch on the far side of the room. He watched as the two men whispered back and forth with each other for a few moments. Myasato began to grow impatient. Myasato stood staring at the two men impatiently. He looked at the two men, what was there to discuss? All he was here to do was purchase a small quantity of firearms. He had cash to pay for them, just as Ishuzu had instructed him. He waited for the two men to finish their conversation so he could see their selection. Finally after five minutes of long, quiet conversation the man who was seated on the couch before rose to his feet. He stood tall and proud, and was significantly older than the other man. He had strands of grey hair, falling in front of his aging eyes. His face was lined with wrinkles, the signs of his age. “Are you a cop or something?” he asked Myasato. “No,” replied Myasato. “I’m here on business from another country. So, I just need some firepower and if you don’t want to sell them to me, then I’ll be happy to take my business elsewhere!” “You sure you’re not a cop?” asked the man, his voice was cocky and arrogant. Myasato had had enough. He turned his back and began to walk away, he was sick of this guy’s games. He kept walking but replied as he walked away, “No, I’m not a cop!” “Well then, where the fuck are you going?” asked the man. Myasato turned back to see the old man smiling. The man pulled a small remote control from the back pocket of his jeans. He pressed a three button combination on the controller, and then pocketed the remote. Just as he placed the remote in his pocket there was a loud cranking and the sounds of gears turning. Myasato watched on in awe, as before his very eyes, the brick wall slowly began to rotate. When the wall had finished turning around and the noises had stopped, the wall was completely different. Where there once was brick and rusted wall, there was now a stainless steel backdrop emphasized by racks of guns. Several different kinds of guns, fully automatics, semi-automatics, pistols, auto-pistols, sniper rifles, you name it and it was there. Myasato’s jaw must have dropped, because the man was now laughing. “You like our selection?” asked the man. “It looks as if it shall suffice my needs,” answered Myasato as he began to walk towards the stacks of guns. He walked up to the wall, and instantly began to take pieces off of the wall, he tested them out. He felt the guns for weight, durability, performance, sighting and a wide variety of other factors. By the end of his twenty minutes in heaven, Myasato had racked up well over three thousand dollars worth of guns and ammunition. This was pretty expensive, as the guns were at a low black market rate. Myasato did not care; to him being in a gun shop was like being a fat kid in a candy store. Myasato walked out of the warehouse building and with the assistance of the two other men, he loaded ten black duffel bags worth of guns into the trunk of his small white rental car. He slammed the trunk closed hard, and then turned back towards the two men who were smiling as he turned. He shook hands with each of them and wished them his best and then he went to the side of his car, and hopped inside. He sped off into the city streets, the time was drawing near. Warehouse Complexes in Dallas, Texas: October 14, 2003 3:17 pm The two brothers were standing beside their car; they had just arrived, and were prepared for some intense training before their match at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. They were grabbing their stuff from the car, when they heard the distant roar, of a car’s engine. They disregarded the noise instantly; a revving engine was nothing new to the big city streets of Dallas. Little did the two brothers know, how close or how significant that revving would actually be. The two brothers continued to unpack their bags. Finally they had everything they needed, their workout clothes, their shower supplies, and of course the one thing they never left home without, each man was carrying their twin 9 mm pistols. They were hand crafted, special gifts from their father upon entry into his service several years ago. Both Jushin and Ichiro had cherished the guns, and they rarely ever went anywhere without them. In there line of work, they had no choice. The two brothers walked into the small warehouse building side by side. Each man was wearing a black business suit, and a pair of black shoes. They walked simultaneously; their strides almost at the exact same pace, they walked into the training facility, and shut the door. Ichiro continued to walk towards the unlit ring on the far side of the warehouse, while Jushin turned right and went to restore power and light to the warehouse building. He got to the fuse box, and with a few slip switches he restored the lights over the ring, and their entire training set up was now visible. Outside of the wrestling ring were many weapons, all of which were scattered across the floor and the mats. Most of the weapons were ancient forms of combat taught to them at the Shao Lin Temple where the two brothers had spent their childhood. They walked into separate dressing rooms and quickly got changed; when they came out they were each wearing a pair of workout shorts, and a muscle shirt. However, unlike much of their training they were both wearing running shoes, today was going to be more of an endurance workout than a workout of style. They had come to the conclusion that their endurance was what was killing them; during their matches their body was becoming too exhausted to maintain their focus. So, the only thing to do was to train and try to change it. They were planning on doing a lot of cardio, and so they began to stretch their muscles for the task at hand. Meanwhile: Just Outside the Warehouse Myasato pulled in and quietly turned the key, killing the power to his small rental car. It had not been too hard to find the brothers, they left a trail wherever they went, and it seemed they eagerly shared information with the people who lived in their building, a big mistake for them. He slowly got out of the car, and quietly shut the door. Myasato walked to the back of the white Civic which he was renting. He moved quietly, he didn’t want to give away any advantage that he had right now, so he slowly crept to the back of the car. Quietly he inserted the key into the trunk’s key hole, and turned. As he turned, he pulled the trunk open and revealed the duffel bags still sitting inside of the trunk. He smiled as he began to slowly and quietly unpack the baggage from the trunk. Myasato placed the bags down on the cement behind the car; he left the trunk open as he knew he would soon have a couple of extra bags to go back inside the trunk. He opened several of the bags and sorted through them to find the supplies that he would need. He opened up a bag which had several pistols inside of it; he pulled out four of the similar 9 mm Glock pistols, and began loading clips from the same bag into them. As he loaded the guns he also checked them, and made sure the safety was engaged before placing them inside of his jacket. He clipped every gun into place using a small leather holster which ran throughout the length of his entire body. He threw the few bags full of pistols and pistol ammunition back into the trunk of the rental car. He then began to search through the rest of his bags for the disassembled sniper rifle which he had purchased. Finally, he found it in one of the last bags he checked. He began to silently assemble the sniper rifle down behind the open trunk of his car, after a few minutes he finally had the gun assembled. He grabbed a clip out of the bottom of the bag and loaded it into the gun; he grabbed the separated pieces of the sniper rifle and began to assemble them. He sat for a few minutes behind the car. First, he twisted the barrel and the handle together, they snapped, and then he grabbed his next piece. He attached the end part of the barrel, the nozzle of the gun and twisted it into place. Myasato grabbed the scope and laser sight, and adjusted them onto the top of the rifle; he glared through the magnifying lens, and adjusted the dials, and sensors. He made sure his distance readings were correct and manageable; finally he was satisfied and loaded a clip into the rifle. Myasato put the rest of the bags back into the trunk of his car, he wouldn’t need them right now, and if he could complete this mission successfully he would never need them. He walked to the small overhanging ladder, and slung the sniper rifle over his shoulder. He jumped up and grabbed hold of the ladder, he pulled himself up, and quietly began his ascent to the roof. Myasato finally made it to the top of the roof, and began to creep around to the other side. He looked over the edge and saw the Nakazura’s car was still there; he smiled to himself, and went about his next task of finding a viewpoint to shoot from. He looked around the top of the roof and noticed the small sun roof window. He smiled sadistically to himself, and he began to creep towards the window. He looked down inside and glared down into the ring. “Perfect!” he thought to himself. Meanwhile: Inside the Warehouse The two brothers continued their training. They were working on a round system, the concept was simple but it was effective for building cardio. Each round lasted three minutes, and during those three minutes each man would push himself to his limits on one area of cardio training, then there would be a one minute rest between each round, before starting another round of new training. After ten rounds they would rest and talk for a while and then begin again. The two brothers were in the seventh round and they were both covered in sweat. The sweat poured through their pores, their hair was wet and sweaty, their palms sweaty and their muscle shirts beginning to dampen from the perspiration. They trained on, and continued into their break. During the minute break both of the brothers took a chance to take a drink, and sit down. They chatted slightly before Ichiro looked at his watch and signaled to his little brother that it was time to start their next workout. Ichiro begins to do some ply metrics; he bounced on his feet slowly, before picking up his pace. He saw his little brother standing in the middle of the ring. Jushin was circling around the ring; in his hands were five pound weights, one in each hand. He thrust his arms out, and it was apparent that he was shadow boxing. Ichiro continued doing his workout. A minute or so into his workout, he heard a small creaking sound. He looked around the warehouse, but saw nothing. His brother had not heard the noise due to his own panting and heavy breathing, maybe he was imagining things. He heard the creak again slightly louder this time, but again Jushin made no signs of noticing the noise. Ichiro looked around the warehouse again, he checked by the door, saw no sign of anyone. He checked one of the holes in the abandoned warehouse building; again he saw nothing. Jushin looked over and saw the concerned look on his brother’s face, and then he began to wonder what was going on. He looked down to his brother. “What the fuck are you looking for?!” he demanded. “I don’t know, I thought I heard something,” answered Ichiro. He was still looking around, but was satisfied that nothing was amiss and continued his training. Jushin shook his head, and went back to his shadow boxing. Ichiro began his steps again, but something was telling him all was not right. He began to glance around the warehouse again. Jushin noticed his brother looking around once again, but this time paid no attention. After a few moments, Ichiro finally caught clue of why his brother might not have heard the noise. The noise was coming from above him, so his panting was making him incapable of hearing the creaking. But still what could it be, he looked around and looked at the far wall. He saw a layer of sunlight shining through the sunroof, and reflecting onto the wall. SUNROOF! That was it! He couldn’t believe he had missed it. He looked up towards the ceiling and looked at the roof for the sunroof. He saw the glass tiling of the sunroof, and noticed one place where the hatch was let up, and there was an opening. He saw something coming into the opening but could not make out what it was. He then caught sight of it, and realized it was the barrel of a gun, he then saw the shadowy figure hovering above the gun staring through the zoom lens. Ichiro knew that the assassin was focused on his brother, otherwise he would surely have noticed Ichiro staring back at him. Ichiro’s mind raced, his brother was circling and moving slightly, but that wasn’t much, and the round was about to end. Ichiro couldn’t decide what to do, he needed to take action and he did; when he did he was just in time. Meanwhile on the Roof Myasato glared through the scope of his rifle. He moved the dials around slightly, trying to get a perfect view with his lens finder. He scanned the ring, he followed the small man’s movements as well as he could, from what little information he had; he knew the man in the ring, the smaller of the two brothers was Jushin. He focused hard and waited for the perfect moment to strike. Suddenly out of the corner of his scope he caught Ichiro, the older and larger of the Nakazura brothers slide into the ring, underneath the bottom rope. He quickly gained his focus on Jushin as he knew his cover was blown. He hurried his shot and managed to get a shot off just as Ichiro knocked his brother out of the way. ”SHIT!” yelled Myasato, frustrated at himself for not staying out of sight, and pissed off at himself for blowing such a great opportunity. He guided the sight of his sniper rifle back towards the brothers and shot off another couple of shots as the brothers scrambled out of the ring. The two Nakazura brothers, disappeared from sight, and for several minutes they were gone from sight. Myasato didn’t know what to do next. He decided to take his chances in a gun fight, he could tell from their reactions that the Souls of the Black Lotus were tired and in no mood to put up a good fight. He carried the guns down the stairs, he threw the rifle into the backseat of his car, and quickly pulled out two of the pistols located underneath of his arm pit. He still had four more pistols stashed throughout his body, just in case. He turned the safety off and raced around to the other side of the building, he needed to cut the Souls of the Black Lotus off before they got away. Meanwhile Inside of the Warehouse The two brothers ran to their jackets. They each grabbed their twin pistols from the pockets and quickly loaded them with their custom built magazines. The mags held thirty bullets in each; they loaded and pulled the safety off. They were ready to go, but they were fatigued. They weren’t ready to fight this guy, whoever he was. They weren’t ready, surely they would lose if they were to try and fight. Yes, they needed to get to their car and get the hell away. They left everything where it was. They left the weights scattered, the weapons and other training devices were lying everywhere across the floor, also they left their suits, they had several, one didn’t mean anything. And besides, if it was the difference between life and death it wasn’t worth it. The two brothers ran side by side, their guns held pointing up in the air as they ran. The sweat in their palms made it hard to hold the guns steady, and the perspiration on their clothing made it less than comfortable to run, but they pushed on. They got to the small opening, make shift door that they had constructed. Jushin ran ahead and kicked the door down. He had left the keys in the ignition, and was damn glad he had now. Upon impact the small piece of paneling that covered the opening to get in flew out of the way. Jushin ducked and ran out, followed closely by his older brother. The sunlight hurt their eyes at first, and it took a few seconds to adjust to the light. They both ran towards their car, and just as Jushin was opening his door he heard the sounds of two guns clicking their safety off in the background. Jushin swung the door open, and awaited the sounds of gun fire. He looked at his older brother and in a way that only brothers could do they mapped out a plan. The seconds seemed to last forever. “I’ve heard stories about you, telling you as great street warriors. ‘The most feared men in Tokyo’ they called you. But still, you don’t seem all that impressive to me!” yelled Myasato from his stance about twenty five yards behind the car. “Sweetheart, you aint seen nothing yet!” said Jushin with a cocky tone of voice. Jushin and Ichiro both heard the reaction, they heard the guns cock, and they heard the click and crack of guns being fired from their attacker’s 9mm pistols. Jushin jumped into the car and ducked down to avoid being hit by any stray bullets. Ichiro jumped and slid around in front of the car. After ten seconds of constant shooting their attackers clip finally ran dry. They heard the gun click showing it was empty, then they heard metal against pavement as both pistols fell to the ground and hit with a loud clink. The two brothers took their opportunity. Jushin’s head flew up from the driver’s seat and began to fire off both of his pistols at their attacker. The back windshield shattered after a few shots, and then Ichiro joined in. He slid onto the hood of the car, and also began to fire upon their attacker. Myasato pulled two more pistols from his waist line, he drew them out and his coat flared around his arms as he brought the guns out and began to take aim. Suddenly however, his right bicep was pierced, and he heard the splatter of his own blood hitting the pavement. He dropped the gun in his right hand, and gripped his arm with the other. He began to apply pressure to minimize the bleeding. He felt the warm trickling of his own blood running down his arm and it infuriated him. They would pay for this! Ichiro saw his shot had landed its mark and ran around to the passenger side of the car. He yanked the door open, and jumped inside just as Jushin was steadying himself in the driver’s seat. They both slammed their doors shut, and Jushin turned the ignition. The car roared to life, and instantly the tires squealed as Jushin hammered the car into gear and peeled out of the parking lot and out onto the Dallas Texas streets. The two brothers looked at each other with a sigh of relief as they drove. They were headed for home; they needed to shower and figure out what the hell was going on. “Who the hell was that?!” demanded Jushin. ”I have no idea, but whoever he was he was from Tokyo,” answered Ichiro. ”How the fuck do you know that?!” asked Jushin. “Did you not see nor hear him. Pay attention to detail Jushin, they are the very essence of defeating your opponent. He was dressed similarly to how we usually dressed, and was obviously Japanese. Furthermore, he talked about hearing stories of us, how else would he hear stories of the Nakazura family had he not been from Tokyo,” answered Ichiro. “My guess is, he is one of our father’s enemies, a bounty hunter contracted to kill us.” “True, but what does that mean?” continued Jushin. “It means that someone who dislikes our father knows where we are, and wants to get at our father through us. Whoever they are, they are trying to send a message to us,” answered Ichiro. “Well then, I guess all that’s left is what do we do about him?” said Jushin. ”We have to eliminate him, but first we must find out all we can about him. Perhaps father knows something about him, and can help us out. We shall call him when we return home, and then we will go from there,” answered Ichiro confidently. Jushin was satisfied with that, he liked when his brother spoke. Ichiro always had a way of being confident, and just being around him made you feel confident. He continued back towards their apartment building. Meanwhile Still At the Warehouse Complex Myasato was searching through the warehouse complex. He had walked through the small opening to get a better glance of the inside. He wanted to see what they did, and it was also on his mind to find something to stop the bleeding until he could get the bullet out. He walked past the ring, and then behind the ring. He looked around the ground; he saw all of the weapons littered across the floor. However, Myasato had no interest in the weapons; he needed some information and something to stop the bleeding in his upper arm. Strangely enough on a bench near the back of the warehouse he found both. He walked over to the bench and found the suits that the Souls had been wearing before they started working out. He reached into the pockets and found nothing, except for a business card, a business card for an apartment housing complex. Myasato smiled as he pocketed the card, and then tore one of the Armani dress shirts apart in order to heal his wound. He wrapped the strands of the shirt around the bullet hole and pulled tightly. He tied several knots, and the pain in his bicep was excruciating, but it was for the best if the wound was no longer bleeding. He grabbed one of the jackets and wiped off most of the excess blood on his arm, however, some of the blood had dried and stayed on his arm. He then used another strand of the silk Armani shirts to make a sort of sling to keep his arm immobile. He fastened the sling, and began to go about his business of cleaning the crime scene. He piled the clothes with blood and anything else which may have traces of his DNA into a small aluminum garbage can in the middle. He opened up a pack of matches and lit a single match. He touched the flame to the rest of the matches and they all simultaneously erupted into a single flame. He dropped the matches into the trash can and the can caught ablaze. He smiled and began to walk away. Myasato exited the warehouse and walked back to his car; he piled all of his guns into the trunk, and made sure everything was clean. He felt satisfied and hopped into the car, he turned the key and roared the car to life. He heard the sound of sirens on their way to the gun shots, as he turned the corner out of the warehouse lot and drove off back towards his hotel. The Souls of the Black Lotus were going to pay for this, he would not accept defeat. To Be Continued…
