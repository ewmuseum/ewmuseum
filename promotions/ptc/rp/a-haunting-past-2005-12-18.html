A Haunting Past

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 Sweat flew from Dawn’s arms as she laid into once of the many punching bags owned by the Mifune School of Wrestling Excellence. It dripped from her bangs and the tip of her nose, ran in rivulets down the small of her back and collected in a sheen on her belly and chest…but she did not relent in her assault. The Steel Rose danced around the heavyweight bag, launching a seemingly endless series of punches, high and low, left and right…occasionally breaking up her assault with a savage kick that rocked the punching bag back on its chain. All in all, she had been at this almost all morning. From her place by the door, Alexis Key watched her one time hostess turned friend savage the punching bag for the umpteeth time, her face a mixture of concern and anxiety. Dawn had been largely quiet since she had woken half the compound with her screaming the night before…but Alex wasn’t buying that she had just been having a nightmare. There was something driven in the way Dawn attacked the punching bag, something almost…desperate. Dawn stepped back for a moment, measuring the bag for another assault, and Alex decided it was now or never to speak to the other woman. She cleared her throat nervously, stepping in through the training room door. "Hey Dawn." The Steel Rose looked back over one shoulder, smiling a little wanly at her sudden guest. "Oh, hey Alexis didn’t think anyone else was around at the moment." Alex moved to one of the nearby benches, setting down a small brown bag and a thermos. "I brought you some lunch…Michi said you hadn’t eat since you got up, I thought maybe you could use a bit of a break." Dawn shook her head, turning back towards the punching bag. "Thanks but I’m not really all the hungry right now…if you wan to just leave it here I’ll get to it a little later." Alex sighed; obviously Dawn wasn’t going to make any of this easy at all. "You’ve been at that for a pretty long time…training for your next GTT5 match?" "Something like that…" Came the noncommittal reply. Irritation flashed through Alex’s mind and she folded her arms across her chest with an annoyed sigh. "You know…just clinging to whatever’s bothering you like a wild dog isn’t going to help anything." Dawn paused for a moment before shrugging her shoulders. "I’m not clinging…I’m working it out." "What by driving yourself to exhaustion and starvation? Bullshit. I’ve seen you the day after you have bad dreams, and you’ve never, EVER behaved like this before." She cocked her head to one side, her voice softening a little. "What’s going on Dawn?" Dawn stared at the punching bag for a long moment, not even looking over her shoulder at Alexis. "I…don’t want to talk about it." That was it then. Alex knew better then to try and force the "Stubborn Rose" into doing anything, so she simply sighed and pushed herself off the bench. "Alright…but if you change your mind and you wanna talk, I’ll probably be in my room or in the courtyard." As she reached the door, Dawn’s voice stopped her. "Wait." Alex turned around to find Dawn had done the same and was staring at her intensely, her green eyes seeming to be searching for something in Alex’s face. After a long moment of silence Dawns shrugged slowly and moved toward the bench Alex had been occupying, plopping down with a sigh. "I suppose there’s a fairly decent chance you might believe me…as opposed to thinking I’ve totally lost my mind." Her face scrunched up in surprise, Alex moved back to the bench and sat down next to the other woman. "Lost your mind?" "Yeah…it wasn’t really a dream I had last night…" Alex cocked her head to one side. "What do you mean?" "I…god this sounds so stupid to say…I was visited by someone, although I suppose attacked is probably a better descriptor for what happened." "Attacked?" Alex stared at her friend wide eyed for a moment. "Dawn what are you talking about? There isn’t a mark on you." "I know! He…came in my dreams." She put her face in her hands, resting her elbows on her knees. "Marcus came to me in my dreams." "Marcus?" "My ex-boyfriend…sort of. He…" Dawn hesitated, her fingers digging into her forehead to stop from shaking. "When I was with WMW I had…some fairly major problems. After my big fallout with my sister, and my failing to get anywhere in that promotion…I sort of turned to other places to take the edge off…it’s not really common knowledge, but for a long while I had a really big problem with drinking…and some harder stuff on the side…" Alexis couldn’t find the words, simply staring in shock at the woman beside her. Dawn continued, seemingly unaware of her friend’s surprise and discomfort. "Marcus was like a knight in shining armour when I met him. He came to me when I was at my worst and he helped to drag myself out of the hole I had created. He had me sober again in a few months, with the drug habit having been kicked about a month before…for a while he was the center of my life, the sun my world revolved around." Dawn lowered her hands to rest on her thighs, looking sidelong at Alex as she did. "I should have known that it was too good to be true really, that kind of fairy book stuff never happens in real life. Once I was clean, he…changed…a lot. It was like he’d built me back up again just so he could break me down on his own terms; make me totally a slave to him. At one point I banned him from my rooms, but that didn’t make any difference of course…he came anyway and just…took what he wanted." "Oh god Dawn…" Alex rested one hand lightly on the back of one of Dawn’s, feeling the other woman shaking through that small contact. "…I didn’t know." "Almost nobody does…I don’t really like to talk about it. I tried to break away, but he was stronger then I was, faster, tougher…and nobody would believe me. Anyone who came to talk to him always walked away saying I was a liar, that I was lucky to have someone who cared for me so deeply. Not surprising really, I should have known he was more then just a monster emotionally." "A monster? What do you mean?" Dawn turned her head to look at Alex for a moment, as if trying to find the words to explain that wouldn’t make her seem…crazy. "You and your "Den" probably had more experience with his kind then I have…" Alex’s eyes went wide and her hands clapped to her mouth in shock. Dinner plate sized eyes stared for long, horrified, minutes at Dawn before she could find her words again. "He’s a…" "…bloodsucker." Dawn finished for her, looking back down at her shaking hands. "I probably would still be there now if I hadn’t found someone who could fight him on his own terms. It was like Déjà vu really, Corvus was like some dark knight sweeping out of the night to slay my enemies. He never tried to hide what he was from me, and when he found out about Marcus…well that was the end of his little charade. I don’t really understand what makes one vampire more powerful then another, but Corvus was more powerful then Marcus by far…he beat Marcus so badly I thought for a moment he was going to kill him. It might have been better if he had." "So…what happened next?" "I was kind of like a vampire bed warmer I guess, since I pretty much fell right into things with Corvus. He told me he loved me and then I let him move in…first time I ever broke one of my cardinal rules." "What rule is that?" Alex asked quietly, almost afraid of the answer. "Never sleep with a co-worker." Alex nodded vehemently, her long hair bouncing around her face. "Never turns out to be worth it in the long run you know. Men are always trying to play you for something, even just sex." Dawn chuckled, "Wish I’d had your brains at the time, maybe I could have seen right through Corvus then. I didn’t though, and he dropped me pretty much as quickly as he’d picked me up…for Lia." Alex’s eyes nearly popped out of her head and she worked her mouth for a few minutes with no sound, looking like a fish that had very suddenly found itself out of water. "Corvus…and Lia…" "I don’t know the full details; I guess there was some kind of vampire necrohoodoo involved. They hate each other now, but at the time it was anything but hate." "So…what happened with Marcus then?" Alex asked, trying to gently steer the conversation back on track. "He just vanished." Dawn threw her hands up in the air, "Took his little batch of vampire slaves and disappeared. I was really hoping I had seen the last of him." "But…last night you dreamt of him?" Dawn shoved herself up off the bench violently, stalking around on the floor and tossing her hands out in frustration again. "That’s just it, it wasn’t a dream! He was talking to me like he was right there, and he threw me around just like he used to…god it was like WMW all over again Alex, I was powerless to do ANYTHING! I was just too fucking weak to even fight back!" Alex winced at the venom in Dawn’s tirade, mostly disturbed by the fact that it was directed predominantly inward. "Dawn…if that really was him, he’s a vampire…when they start the fight on their terms, it’s basically impossible to keep up." "But what am I supposed to DO Alex? I can’t tell anyone here, they already think I’m totally batshit crazy! I can’t go to the police for the exact same reason…but how do I fight back against something like that?! I didn’t stay with Marcus so long because I wanted to be abused you know, I just couldn’t fucking get away!" Dawn’s whole body had started to tremble and her eyes glistened slightly, threatening a torrent of angry tears if left unchecked. Alex dropped her face into her hands, scrubbing her face for a moment while she made up her mind. When she looked up again, she took a deep breath to try and steady her courage. "Well…I can help you." Dawn stopped short, turning to look at Alex in astonishment. "What? How?" Alex chuckled a little at Dawn’s surprise, grinning sheepishly up at her. "You’re the one that brought up the "Den" earlier Dawn…I do have some experience in finding and killing creatures like this…though I normally work as part of a team." "And don’t you normally have some pretty heavy hardware? How could we go up against him without any of that?" "Just because I left the Den doesn’t mean I don’t remember a few things about it…like where some of our weapons caches are hidden. When I was with them we scattered caches of weapons and supplies anywhere we thought we were going to need them…even here in Japan." "And you think you could still find one of them?" Alex nodded firmly. "Positive. There aren’t any here on Hokkaido, but if I can take the railway back to the main island then I can find something we hid there." Dawn stared at her for a spell, and then shook herself as if coming back to her senses. "And you’re sure you want to help me? We both know that he’s very dangerous…and I think he wants to kill me." "Don’t get me wrong, it’s going to be difficult…but I’m not just going to leave you all alone here Dawn, it’s not how I operate. I’m going to help you any way I can." "And so am I." A deep voice rumbled from the doorway. Both women jumped at the sound, Dawn spinning to face the large black man who’s frame nearly filled the entire doorway. "Michi! You scared the shit out of me." "Sorry Kaiden (Little Dragon), I didn’t want to interrupt either of you." "How long have you been listening?" Alex asked. "Most of the conversation." He turned to Dawn, his expression somber. "I wish you’d said something to me Dawn…I wouldn’t have thought you were crazy." "Are you sure? I’m talking about being visited in my dreams by vampires Michi…that’s about as out there as it gets." Michi shrugged his massive shoulders nonchalantly, leaning against the doorframe and folding his arms over his broad chest. "I suppose that really depends on how you look at things. In the worlds rush to modernize, we’ve managed to convince ourselves not to believe in the things that used to haunt our dreams in the past…even when some of them are still real. During my stay with Master Mifune, I’ve learned that there are a lot of "things" our there that you just can’t explain away logically. I may not have any of Miss Key’s experience in fighting these vampires…but I think I have enough knowledge to be careful…and I’m still a big guy." He said the last with a smile, trying to crack some of the tension in the air. Dawn nodded slowly, the corners of her mouth twitching up into a smile. "Well…I’d be lying if I said the thought of you backing me up didn’t make everything seem just a little easier. Are you sure about this?" "Dead sure. We’ve known each other for a long while now Dawn, I’m not just going to leave you to the wolves." "Okay then." Alex stood up, rolling her neck from side to side. "I’ll go see about recovering one of our caches…and then I guess we should plan what the next step is." Michi nodded, indicating the brown bag on the bench. "First step is for Dawn to eat something. Even if you’re not hungry, Alex made you lunch and it’d be rude not to eat it." Dawn flushed at the rebuke, limping over to the bench to plop down, her aching muscles starting to stiffen from the relentless workout. "Yeah I guess you’re right…not much use to anyone otherwise." She dug into the brown paper bag for her lunch and then paused, her head coming back up slowly. "Do either of you hear that?" Alex and Michi exchanged glances before looking back to Dawn. "Hear what?" "I think I hear…singing."
