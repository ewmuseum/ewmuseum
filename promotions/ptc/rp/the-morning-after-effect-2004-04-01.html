The Morning After-Effect

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-01 Taped fists fly through the air, crashing into a large, swinging punching bag. The knuckles bleed through the paper tape around the hand, sweat splattering with each throw of the arm. Justin angrily thrusts left and right, left and right, his eyes focused on the invisible opponent in front of him. The punches just keep coming, nonstop, the muscles in Justin's arm rearing to explode, veins ripping outward. A woman in a black halter-top walks into the dark, grungy basement from upstairs, a towel wrapped around her hair, waterdrops rolling off her cheeks. Her leather pants make a stretching noise as she steps, her bare feet barely gripping the rickety old stairs. "Jeeze, Tiger, why don't you save up some of that energy for me?" She asked, with a sly grin. Justin stops dead in his tracks, a wild and dangerous look in his eye, as his attention whips over to her. He lets out an unappreciative smile, in disbelief of what he just heard, as he walks over her slowly, heavy, condescending footsteps leading him only inches away from the girl. "First of all, what the hell are you still doing here? And why the hell did you take a shower here? This is my house, woman, who the hell do you think you are?" he said to her, his nostrils flaring. "I'm..I'm sorry, I should just let you get ready for your match..." "Yeah, you should do that. But you didn't. Why? Because you're a pretentious little bitch.. now get the hell out of my house, before I remove you myself." "I...I...I thought what we shared last night...was special, and I thought, I just thought that I, I..." "You, you, you what, bitch? No...don't even bother explaining to me, it doesn't even matter. We didn't share anything 'special', tammie...georgia...sarah...ah, whatever the hell your name is. I was just getting my rocks off, no more, no less. Now if you don't mind, I have things to do here. The first match in the tournament is in a few days and I don't have time for sluts like you. My focus from here on in is on Robb Hardy and Ian Feltzer, it doesn't involve you." "Jesus Christ, you ASSHOLE! You..you..you son of a bitch!" she yelled, as her hand reached out and swung full-force for his face, only for him to catch her by the wrist and twist it. "You don't want to hit me, that would be a mistake, girlie. And with me, you don't want to be making mistakes. You might end up... unhappy. To say the least." "You...you psycho... let go of me! Go back to your stupid...wrestling...thing..." With that foolish comment, his eyes widened and his face went crimson red, veins ripping forward as he yanks her closer to him. "Stupid? STUPID? I'll tell you what's stupid...a 24 year old woman with nothing better to do then follow guys home and have sex with them when they barely know them, just so they can cling on to them the next morning... my wrestling 'thing', is not stupid, it's my LIFE. You are nothing but an insignificant speck, where as I am molding myself into a GOD, here. You'll see, just like everyone else will, when I come out on top, slapping that wanna-be gangster and that half-witted ****-for-brains pile of horse **** out of existence and carving my pathway to success through their mangled bodies. Enscribing my name into the memory of every living person who witnesses my triumph, redeeming myself for all the hell that's been at my hands since the day I was born. Victory, for once in my life. To be on top, to know that nothing can stop me... to destroy everyone who steps in my path...that is my destiny! You...are just a whore. Nothing more...nothing more. Walk out of this room. Get out of my house. And never come near me again, or I'll snap your neck where you stand. Got it, bitch?" Her eyes swelled up with tears borned from fear, as Justin's grip let loose and she slowly backed away, running up the stairs without another word. Doors slam, and a few minutes later, Justin silently turns to the punching bag, and without another word or thought about it, he begins pounding away again.
