Date: 2004-09-14 Don’t flex baby/ I wanna see you touch your toes in that dress baby/ Bounce it up and down like we havin’ sex baby/ Give me the head and you can give them tricks a rest baby/ And nothing less baby/ The erotically blunt sounds of “Don’t Flex” by 8-Ball swarmed the dim nightspot, The Rush. The dance floor was a sea of bodies of all colors and styles, all partaking of the kind of vice meant for the nightlife. An eye in one direction, you could catch police officers illegally dealing the same drugs they would bust dealers for. An eye to the other direction, sex being sold at the starting rate of a cool grand. However, the focus would be upon a well-groomed, suited man of spiked honey-blonde hair grinding rhythmically upon the epitome of a vixen. Her hair was a mix of shadow black and blazing red, her “painted on” dress matching perfectly with her cinnamon skin tone. It would seem completely irrational to picture a man of his obvious stature with a woman of hers….but in the city of LA and at the time, it makes perfect sense. Their sensual grinding continues, but unbeknownst to the two a pair of eyes have remained locked upon them since the moment they made eye contact and have yet to leave ever since. That man sits at the bar, enjoying a glass of Hypnotic as he sits quietly with a closed grin, adorned in a snow white Gucci suit which could have easily been bought with the average two month salary. This man is Jamar Gordo, the eccentric millionaire martial artist and movie star, and although it wouldn’t seem out of place for a man like himself to enjoy an evening at a nightclub….his actions can only be described as peculiar. “You’ve been sitting here all night playa, haven’t even danced with not ONE of these fine dimes in here tonight! What’s on your mind?” Jamar’s eyes never left his intended vantage point, namely the suited man and his lovely female companion, but following a trademark Cheshire grin he gladly acknowledged the 20-something bartender cleaning a glass with a look of interest on his face. “Let me answer a question with a question then my boy.” The bartender leaned against the counter, obviously interested in what Gordo had to ask him as he replied jovially, “Shoot.” “You ever met the woman you knew was right for you? Were you absolutely CONVINCED the two of you could share your world together, forever?” The bartender looked slightly perplexed by the question posed to him, his youth barely allowing him to know what chest hair was, let alone true love. Not even able to fake an answer even if he tried after a moment of silence, the bartender sheepishly replied, ”No. Not a clue.” “Good. I wouldn’t wish that on anyone….well, maybe a few people.” These words were spoken from the mouth of Gordo as he continued to eye his “prey”, more specifically the suited man within the duo. Jamar’s face construed from his usual cocky grin to that of a pained wince. The kind of face where regurgitation could have come at any moment…but never did. Reaching into his suit pocket, the now restless Gordo placed a crisp, flawless Ben Franklin down on the glass bar table, almost shocking the bartender into cardiac arrest behind the table. “You keep bringing me Hypnotic until that Benjy’s no good…and when that’s no good, you’ll see another. Too much for you, or should I forget the tip,” asked Jamar without turning any eye to his server. The bartender’s eyes locked upon the 100 dollar bill between Jamar’s fingers the way a tiger would a lamb, immediately pouring up a glass of the movie star’s requested drink. It would be a long night for Jamar…but there certainly was a purpose why he was here. 3 Years Ago / Anywhere, USA / The Beach “I can’t. Jamar I can’t.” This was not supposed to happen. The mood was right, the salty water of the ocean slamming into the golden sand. The moon was high and regal in all it’s glory, the stars and night sky their blanket. The ring had set him back 8 months of TERRIBLE pay at Oscar Gordo’s restaurant. HOW COULD THIS HAPPEN?! Why wasn’t her answer the yes he had envisioned in his dreams over and over, which would end in passionate love-making on the sand with the ring he purchased on her and nothing else. Why did Jamar have to look into those emerald green eyes and not receive tears of joy, but overbearing sadness. Fate had played a cruel trick on the then wide-eyed and hopeful Gordo, merely filming scenes for an independent fight film and working hours at his sensei’s restaurant. Not enough money for college and unaware of any financial security...all he needed in the world was Erica. The felt ringbox sat in his hand, unaccepted by his target. His eyes slowly looked over the splendor that was Erica. Her long brown hair, the cherry lips he’d die to kiss, her wonderful personality and spirit…and it would never be his. “Why,” was all that could be asked, lacking passion or even anger. It simply was stated as monotone and plain as drywall, reminded of his dreams being dashed by a hard wave against the sand. “You and I want different things Jamar. You are so deadest on chasing this “movie” dream and becoming some great fighter, but how do you base a family around that? I need more, I need security! I need to know I can look over at night and know who I need is right there and not have to worry about his health day in, day out. Marriage is not the answer Jamar...” Wow, who knew breaking his heart would come out so easily. Barely a stutter or mis-used word in the entire spiel, Erica made her feelings known to the hopeful youth. “Erica, I don’t have the clout or cash for college. All I have is a dream and desire…the only thing I love more then my dream is you and losing you would change me. Change me forever. You can’t end it like this, you just can’t. -sniffle- I need you,” the outpour continued, Erica’s head hanging as more tears left her eyes and smashed into the sand, “I don’t have the cash to bring security, BUT I GOT NOTHING BUT LOVE FOR YOU GOD DAMN IT! I’d do ANYTHING for you! I went through hell to make this happen…and you’re just going to, leave it all?” He expected an answer, but the hard winds blowing that night on the beach signaled the turn this moment had taken. There would be no answer for Jamar…merely a punctuation. Her punctuation of choice, after a deep breath and wiping the tears from her eyes, was a satin soft kiss to the forehead of Jamar. He clenched his eyes tightly and needed no psychic to predict where this was going. He was the rape victim who never saw the violator. He was sick with no cure in sight. Most of all…with that kiss, he asked a question that was never given the deserved answer. His body would not even allow him to raise up from his Indian style seat, Erica standing up and turning her back to leave the crushed Gordo on the beach that should have been the greatest night of his life. Instead, Gordo was forced to settle with this moment as the reaper that would pick at him forever. It was there he sat, the ringbox pressed to his head and ready to die inside as she became smaller and smaller, disappearing in the horizon. Something died in Jamar that day, and was never given a proper funeral. The Present / The Home of Lyle Stevens “SAY IT!” “I’M A WASTE OF FLESH!” “LOUDER!” WHAP~! “I’M A WASTE OF FLESH!” “LOUDER!!” Those sounds could be heard audibly upstairs, but merely an afterthought to an uninvited house guest in the form of Jamar Gordo. He seemed unnerved, even oblivious to the borderline torturous, yet strangely erotic sounds upstairs. There he stood, in the home of the District Attorney of Los Angeles, Lyle Stevens. The best of the material world surrounded Jamar as he eyed hand-crafted grandfather clocks, Faberge’ eggs, and even exquisite Oriental tapestries. However, only one thing drew his eye…and no price tag could be placed upon it. Jamar approached an oak stand with a metallic frame encasing a picture of what Jamar considered the most priceless artifact in the entire living room of Stevens… Erica. Stevens that is. But still his Erica. “I didn’t understand it then, but I do now. I know you needed security, and you got it…but you also got an unfaithful asshole, a problem of my own. A problem I’ve been ready to fix for some time,” Jamar stated to Erica’s likeness, the sounds from upstairs growing stronger and more intense. “By the way, your husband is a waste of flesh apparently,” joked the movie star with a smirk as he ran his fingers over the picture…in a way, settling an unknown score with Stevens and avenging her. The pieces were strewn, but able to be put together with patience, like a jigsaw puzzle. Let us fill in the final piece. 5 Days Ago / The Los Angeles Home of Jamar Gordo / Seaside Dojo “....And in other news the Los Angeles District Attorney, Lyle Stevens, intends to follow up on assault charges regarding the millionaire playboy and movie star, Jamar Gordo. Gordo, who allegedly assaulted club patron Jessie Caan, the 26 year old bartender of the Escape as well as 3 other yet to be identified patrons, is looking at serving some serious time due to previous charges as well as being on probation for similar charges. Word is that this could put Gordo’s Global Title Tournament plans in jeopardy. We have pre-recorded comments from D.A. Stevens in just a mom...” Those words, as related by a cookie-cutter beautiful newscaster, blared over the private, seaside dojo of Jamar Gordo. Glass surrounded the dome structured dojo, the suns rays seeping in and delivering a soothing heat to the ebony warrior as he devastated a fire engine red heavy bag in the center of his dojo. His hakama pants flailed wildly with each kick deliver, his muscles pulsating and sweating as ever intense strikes continued, still listening to the broadcast. “...The city of Los Angeles will not tolerate bad boy antics, especially from uncontrolled eccentrics like Jamar Gordo.” These words came from the mouth of Stevens himself, alongside his lovely wife, Erica Ste...” It took no time at all for Jamar’s head to shoot to the flat screen TV hanging on the wall, his breath not gone from his workout, but the sight on the screen. There she stood, aged like a fine wine and even more beautiful then he could remember. She had found her security…Stevens, the man from the club earlier in our story. He wanted it to be a coincidence more then anything and was shocked and appalled when it wasn’t. The sparkling ring resting on her finger did not help matters at all. Jamar came to one conclusion the moment he witnessed Stevens with his love. He had to be crushed. Walking over to a pair of SouthPole pants strewn outside the sparring area, Jamar pulled out a Nokia cellular phone from the pocket and dialed 7 digits with no hesitation. The phone was too his ear, waiting on an answer until finally Jamar had received one. “Ricardo, yea…listen….fuck this whole court thing with the charges. The gloves are off, I want this motherfucker buried. What? GTT is still on, period. I’m not about to let Matrix just slide without taking his ass-whipping. I hated that movie anyway. Just have my ticket ready in 6 days for the GTT, alright? I know I'm the very best in the fighting world and I can't believe I was gonna forgo that over this shit. Oh...tell me...does Stevens still have a thing for leather and nasty girls? Good...let’s just say I intend to fuck up his re-election chances. Find me Nikki’s number will ya...” Back To The Present / The Home Of Lyle Stevens How hard was it to make this plan work? Unbearably. How did Jamar plan it? With the utmost pleasure. He quietly ascended the steps, the screams of Stevens growing ever louder and louder, keying him to how close he was coming. Stevens intended to attack Jamar’s freedom to cement his chances for re-election. Stevens married the woman he loved, and didn’t even have the decency to be faithful and love her the way she should have been loved. As the cliché goes, two wrongs don’t make a right...but Jamar certainly was hoping they’d cancel each other out. There Jamar stood, in the doorway of the bedroom where husband and wife would lay together. Obviously, Stevens didn’t see monogamy the way Gordo did...especially with the stories he heard. There Stevens was, in all his glory, the honorable DA strapped by the wrists to his regal headboard by chains with his back to the door. Judging by fit of the leather mask snuggly fit over his head and the leather thong…he had no problem providing his own equipment. His posterior and bare back were red and sore from the beating administered by the cinnamon skinned beauty from the club earlier. She stood beside him, bedecked in a short lather dress with thigh high “fuck me” boots and her instrument of erotic torture...a studded paddle. Her eyes caught Jamar’s and she shared a secretive smirk with him, the entering movie star mouthing, “Thank you Nikki”. She smiled, pointing to the bookcase at the right of Jamar as he nodded his head, understanding her signal. His steps made no sound, a boon coming from years of martial arts training as he crept to the bookcase, his eyes lighting up like proverbial gems as he took hold of a Sony mini-video camera placed discreetly on the shelf. “One more time Lyle,” seductively asked Nikki as she patted the paddle in her hand, awaiting an answer from the sexually explicit district attorney. “YES YES, PLEASE, I’M BEGGING YOU,” he exclaimed, thirsting for more domination from Jamar’s pawn in his grand scheme. “Alright, but you may not like what you get,” warned Nikki as she stepped back as if to paddle Stevens. The cameras power was switched off, the red light fading to nothingness. He had what he needed. Stevens wouldn’t be able to prosecute a TV court case, let alone prosecute for the city of Los Angeles anymore...this was just extracurricular now. His mind raced with the thoughts of Stevens attempting to take his freedom...Stevens marrying his one true love...Being unfaithful to her...Trying to make an example of him...he had to pay, and the price would come hard and costly. SMACK~! A round kick from Gordo sent jolts of searing pain through the spine of Stevens, no pleasure derived from it at all as he screamed in excruciating pain. He exclaimed, “GOD DAMN, THAT HURT TOO MUCH”. Jamar merely replied, “I know”. Nikki unlocked the chains on Stevens’ wrists, the DA falling back first on the bed and staring up at the mastermind behind his inevitable ruin. “GORDO?! WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DO…” He was quickly shut up with a scathing kick to the face, Stevens’ falling to his side as he ripped the mask from his face, the fantasy over and Nikki grimacing in amusement. “You shut the fuck up you piss ant, I have all the cards now,” demanded Jamar, not even wishing to hear Lyle’s voice. “You realize if you were faithful to your WIFE, this wouldn’t be happening. Fact is Stevens, NOBODY makes an example of Jamar Gordo, and the fact that you tried to test me in that fact got you here today. You wanted to fuck around, so you got fucked and I have no sympathy for you…especially considering who your wife used to be to me. I’ve watched and waited for this….waited for you to play into everything...Nikki, the club, going home...all of it.” Jamar held up the video camera, Lyle’s face turning ghastly pale as Jamar relished in Stevens’ weakness. “Seeing as how I have all the money I’d ever need…who should I just GIVE this tape too, huh? UPN, ABC, FOX...take your fucking pick, either way…your career if fucking over Stevens. Well, unless some certain charges on me get dropped…and in short fucking order. Maybe I can persuade you by showing highlights from your “performance” just a minute ago,” taunted the ever cccky martial artist, Stevens’ breaking down right then and there, this moment everything Jamar wished it to be and more. Just days ago, Stevens held the one thing he once held dear and his freedom in his hands….now Jamar had all the power and more. “So I guess you’re ready to negotiate.” It was a wonderful thing when a plan came together...
