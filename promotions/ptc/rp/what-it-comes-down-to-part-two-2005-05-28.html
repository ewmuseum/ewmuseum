What It Comes Down To (Part Two)

28 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-05-28 “Don’t worry, everything’s going to be okay.” It‘s one of those lines that are much easier to believe when you aren’t in the victims shoes just as I was . How was I to know that the one person who promised to put my name on the map would do it in such a manner that brought me all the fame I could ask for, but in doing so drowned out all the respect I ever had. On one hand you had the Tessa Windsor who was nothing more than a sidekick to the stars. Chris Storm, Jackson Rust, Karina Wolfenden, all former World Champions who I had one time or another teamed with. Difference between them and I is they had what they needed to succeed, me, on the other hand needed any one of the three just to keep my head above water. That was until now. Now, I was on my own and shining in the ring. At least that’s what I had thought had gotten me this far, until the speculation grew otherwise. Now, yet again it’s supposed that I had to resort to someone else’s help to win this thing. This time that someone else was Christian Zenith. I’d never asked for his help, nor was it all that wanted, but somehow is his and others mind having a Tessa Windsor look alike star in some adult rated film meant wins, which meant money for him, considering it was him who had dragged me back into the game. So how was playing lackey to someone like Christian Zenith going to put me back on the map? To tell the truth, I wasn’t too sure of the answer myself, I simply trusted Dawn’s judgment. She too had the need to get back on the top of her game, and though she was a few months younger than I she had much more experience in the business. Neither of us really knew what we were getting into, but if it got us both back on the radar, than we though ‘what the hell, why not give it a shot?’. Thing is, now it’s got me thinking, how much is it really worth? Here I am, at one of the highest points in my career and yet I’m still not happy. How much is it going to take to feed this need for success? It almost seemed as if the roles had reversed in the Windsor household. Before I was the mother to two adoptive children. Now since I’ve been gone it almost seems as if they’ve grown up and now I’m the one crying to them on the few days I’m home. [b[Before it was Chris who’d obsess his performance in the ring. It was Chris who lived, breathed, and demanded a ring twenty four hours a day. NowI’d almost turned into a female version of him, minus all the obsession over being the greatest technical wrestler. Before I was simply Tessa Windsor, an average housewife who just happened to wrestle as well. Now I was Tessa Windsor, the woman who was on the verge of becoming a star. It made me wonder, did I really want this? Or was I bound to become a selfish superstar who wanted nothing to do with the real world. Right now I had everything, and I simply wanted more. But how much more could I take before the world would eat me apart. I had a man and two kids at home who loved me, even if at times we were at each others throats. And money was hardly an issue with how much we had pulled in over the past few years. So why risk it all when I had nothing more to gain and everything to lose? Because I wanted to prove to the world I could be the star everyone doubted me to be. Call it selfish, but who doesn’t want to be seen for their full potential? It’s as simple as that. And even if it meant having to play along with Zenith’s antics, I would. He had the one things others were never able to give to me and me alone, success. And while I cant say I respected his decision to make a phony tape to increase my fan base, I can understand not only his want but his need to see me succeed. Now, it was time to seize the opportunity and prove that I could finally win the big one, not only for me, Zenith, Dawn, the kids, or Chris, but for anyone who had ever believed in my ability to pull it off.
