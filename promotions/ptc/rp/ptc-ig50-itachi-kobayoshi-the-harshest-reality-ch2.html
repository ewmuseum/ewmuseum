“The Harshest Reality is the Truth” Featuring: Itachi Kobayoshi Written By: [name withheld] Word Count: 920 “Kisame-san?” his name slipped from Gai's mouth more as a question than as a statement. He ran forward, and fought back tears as he needed to validate that he was truly seeing the truth. “It's me, Gai. You think I would let Itachi leave for America with you, and not come along?” Kisame chuckled a bit as he spoke, almost ignoring the fact that a few moments ago, Gai spoke of his death. “How are you... I mean... I saw you... You're alive?” Gai could hardly form an entire sentence. “Like I said, Kisame was in the same position as I... if you had known about either of us... you would've been in danger... and we couldn't do that to you.” Itachi's words were aimed at trying to calm a now frantic Gai. “More importantly, you need to prepare yourself. If you want to help Itachi in his journey, you must first embark on your own.” Kisame placed a firm hand on the shoulder of Gai, trying to reassure him. “You're right... you'll have to forgive me, been a busy weekend for me... two people I had been told by police and everyone else had passed away... are standing in front of me right now, healthy as ever. Let's focus on the present, though... where do we need to go?” Gai's response was a lot more rational and controlled than Itachi had expected. Perhaps he had matured a lot more than he had imagined in the few years they had been separated. “Tonight, we rest. Tomorrow, we leave for Tokyo. That's where we will begin.” Itachi's voice was firm and commanding. It felt good to have his team together again, and he knew at this rate, there wasn't anything that could shake them. ~*~ Itachi didn't sleep much that night. It might have been the noise from outside, it could've been the small amounts of dust still in the room. More than likely, though, it was his thoughts racing, as he knew this journey was going to tear Gai apart... and it pained him to bring this upon someone he had come to think of as a son. Before he knew it, the light and warmth of the morning sun was beating down on his face, signaling the beginning of a new day, and the first step in a long journey for the Rising Sons. Itachi stepped from his room and into the cooking area, where Kisame and Gai had already finished their meals. “You slept late, Itachi-san. We were just about to wake you up. Ready to get going?” Gai's voice seemed more full of energy and excitement than last night. “You sure seem to have changed your mood.” Itachi remarked, barely ready to leave. “Gai said that he's excited to go. He wants to discover what he needs to help us.” Kisame shot a concerned look to Itachi as he explained Gai's mood. “I just want to help you, and if going to Tokyo with you will help me do that, then I'm ready to go.” Gai tried to rationalize to his mentor. “Very well then,” Itachi looked between the pair, “It's time we left.” ~*~ The train between Osaka and Tokyo isn't what you would call a luxury ride. Dirt and grime could be seen on the walls and floors, men who were down on their luck requested change of passengers, and the years had not been kid to the train's lighting. The group tried to ignore the filth of Japan, as they had seen it for far too long, and accepted it as part of what they were working to save. Once they arrived in Tokyo, Itachi led the group through a smaller, more rural suburb of the city, past an abandoned hospital. “This place feels weird. Like I know it.” Gai broke their silence with. Itachi and Kisame looked at each other, finding no reason to lie to him. “It should feel familiar... this is where you were born, Gai.” Itachi told him with his usually level of bluntness. “Wait, I thought I was from America. You mean I was born here?” Gai questioned, running a bit to get in front of the duo. “You're parents were both American, but you weren't born there. That's why you have no memories beyond the dark streets of Japan... you've been here your entire life, just in different cities. The group had walked onto a large grassy hill with trees and beautiful flowers around them. They were pretty far from the hospital they had passed, and even farther from any other buildings. “Why bring me here? Why show me these things, the past isn't what's important. Why are we here?” Itachi and Kisame didn't utter a word, ignoring his questions. When they reached the top of the hill, Gai gazed upon a beautiful group of flowers and bushes, gathered around a stone tablet. He couldn't help but stare at it, as the central point of all the foliage around them. “Wow... it's beautiful... what is it?” Gai asked, still fixated on the tablet. “Gai... that is a grave. It marks the resting place for a very caring woman who wanted nothing more than to make people happy and bring joy to Japan.” Kisame answered with a smile on his face. Gai felt Itachi's arm on his shoulders, as he tried to comfort him before delivering the next statement. Itachi's usually stoic voice quivered a bit as he spoke. “Gai... this is the final resting place for your mother.” Chapter 2 of 4 Chapter One: “The Longest Journey Begins With the Smallest Step” Chapter Two: “The Harshest Reality is the Truth” Chapter Three: “The Gift of Life” Chapter Four: “Where One Ends...
