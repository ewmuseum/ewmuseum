Silent Dreams

6 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April is the Cruelest Month, Breeding Lilacs out of the deadland mixing Memory and Desire, Stirring Dull Roots with Spring Rain. Winter Kept us Warm, Covering Earth in Forgetful Snow, feeding A little life with dried Tubers. TS Elliot - The Wasteland (Line 1-7) 1922 A gentle Breeze escapes into the room from the partially opened window on the far corner of the room. Velvet Drapes hang majestically either side of it, encasing...enshrining the only outlet to the outside. A flickering light...perhaps a candle...gives a slight light to the darkness that otherwise clouds the four walls of this prison...or sanctuary. Shadows dance, given life by the waving majesty of this candle's light. The candle sits, alone, giving its breath of heat to the seemingly dead room. The wallpaper, peeling off of the walls, can be made out to be a faint lime colour amidst its dark surroundings. Patches where the paper has completely faded, reveal the stone casing of the room...this is an old place...old memories...old desire. Two of the walls lie bare, except for a closed wooden door, scratched and creased with the mark of time; and a window, its adjascent brother. A third wall plays host ton the candelabra, on which the single, lone candle dances with the moonlight eminating through the opposing window. It is accompanied by two boxes...one filled with unused white candles, the other with red ones. Some significance? Or perhaps just a simple pratcicality. On the fourth wall, hangs a frame. The frame holds a portrait...half worn away by time...almost unrecognisable. Under this window to an ancient photograph sits an aging Mahogony Desk. Scratched, beaten...the desk is missing one of its drawers, and all it seems that is holding the desk together is its frail frame against the wall. A 17th Century Louis XIV chair is set before the desk, upon which sits the figure...the figure of the prisoner of this old, dark sanctuary...Jason Spire. This, our hero, or perhaps villain...depending on perspective...sits, staring into a mirror that sits on top of the desk. Parts of the mirror have been etched away, decayed through the journey it has no doubt passed through. The camera peers into the reflection that greets Jason Spire. His frame, well proportioned...toned; flexible; strong...seemingly a good specimin of an athlete. His hands, though somewhat worn...betray his innocence...inexperience to the wrestling world. Laying flat out on the Desk, they seem to grip onto it, as if holding for some inevitable push or nudge. Broad Shoulders frame his body, and though obscured by a sleeveless ripped tanktop...it is obvious Jason Spire is in good shape. But, all this does not define him. What defines our prisoner...is his portrait, held preciously, fragile, in the reflection of the mirror. Flowing, if scruffy, blond hair frames his face. The hair almost reaching his shoulders...straining to rest upon them. Dirty, yet clean at the same time. But its true condition obscured by the darkness of the scenery. Jason Spire's face though...is what provides the interest to his character. One side of his face, perfectly formed. Chiselled features. Glowing Green eye, high cheek bone...his features well defined...almost rugged. The other side of Spire's face though...although further obscured in shadow...is evidently not of such a perfectly formed nature. Through the dark reflection, one can make out the slanted eye...as if deformed...and the lip smiling, even though that is not the intention of the expression. It is a forced deformity. Spire leans forward suddenly, plunging his face into the light. What greets our noble viewers is a portrait of pain, and anguish. A large, deep scar runs from the bottom of his left eye, right down past his nose, to the tip of his lip...forcing it ever upwards. Little burn marks aspire beyond the scar, moving across the entire canvas of the left side of his face...deforming the skin...cursing it with bent and rendered flesh. His eye, slanted downwards, is kept open by the scar's pulling on it. It's size is unnatural, and belies the dissymetry of his face. This, deformed creature, is Jason Spire. He sits, patiently, as if waiting for some inevitability to occur. His hands still lying flat on the mahogony of the desk, his eyes peering silently, yet deafeningly into the mirror. What does he see? What is it that captures his attention...perhaps the deformity of his otherwise perfectly proportioned face? Or perhaps he searches for something else...something invisible. Whatever it is, Jason Spire sits back again, into the half darkened blanket over his face. His hand goes into an open drawer, and withdraws what appears to be a mask. A black mask. He draws his hand back, and into the darkness...applying the mask to the deformity of his face. Attatching it, setting it on...he once again leans forward into the light created by the solitary candle. Again, he peers into his reflection...the mask covering the left side of his face...revealing only the bottom of his chin, but otherwise obscuring everything up to his scalp. Spire blinks a couple of times, before returning his attentions to his reflection. Jason Spire: "And...so...here I am." His voice...shaky yet prominent...as if from some battling mind, trying to realize some truth, but unable to find the key to its cage. Jason Spire: "But where have I been?" The rhetorical question seems to strike Spire by surprise. He ponders for a moment, and lifts his hand to his face, running his fingers across the mask obscuring half of his face. Jason Spire: "And what...what is this face that greets me? This deformed portrait...this creature." He edges his hand again into the open drawer, withdrawing a card. A driving license. The camera peers in on the license, and it shows Jason Spire's face...as it once was...perfectly formed. Smiling even. He doesn't smile any more... The card is from the state of Illinois...and tells us Jason Spire's age. He is 24. But beyond his name, age and state...we are denied any information. Spire stares deeply at the card, his expression sad...still...broken suddenly by a conviction to tear himself away from this image of the past. He slams the card down with his palm onto the desk, and stares at his reflection in the mirror. Jason Spire: "God Damn it! Why don't I remember!" It would appear that Jason's memory betrays him... Jason Spire: "Where are my memories? Who is behind this mask?" Questions posed to an invisible audience...but questions none the less. Jason Spire: "Answer me damn it! I deserve that, at least. Locked here, in this darkness...why have things transpired to this?!" Spire stares into the mirror. The camera focuses on the reflection, and something is changing in Spire's character. Unable to pinpoint what discernible change has come over our hero...something is certainly different. The eyes seemingly deeper. The expression, harder, more confident perhaps? The eye underneath the mask seems to respond most though, edging itself to life from its otherwise slumbering position. It looks, peers at the mirror...as if laughing, smiling, or perhaps both. A noticable change though, comes over the irratic breathing of Jason Spire. It now becomes smooth, slow, rhythmic. Jason Spire: "You ask questions that you know full well, I cannot answer. I found you in that alley...downed...beaten. The scars across your back and face, removing you from humanity. I found you Jason. I picked you up...and carried you here. Here, to your...our new home. The past remains locked...and I am thusfar unwilling to render you the key. Just feel assured in the knowledge that it was me who saved you...brought us here." Jason's expression returns to its previous, frightened...unprepared grimace. He shakes his head slightly, the words from his own lips like poison to his ears. Jason Spire: "You lie. You guard my secrets because you are frightened of what I may realize...what I might discover. You keep these things from me, when it is no right of yours to do so!" The solid, dark expression once again falls over Spire's face. Jason Spire: "Insolent Child! I have every right to do as I wish with anything of yours! You damned fool. If it weren't for me...there would be no more Jason Spire! Now, listen to me Jason. I do these things for you. I am your ally. I am your only ally. In that world...you do not exist any more. You are not welcome, my friend, in the place outside these walls. People will take one look at you, and judge you. And they would be right to...because you are a creature now of darkness." Jason Spire shakes his head again, closing his eye. Jason Spire: "No! It is you who are not welcome. I'm still a person. I'm still alive aren't I? Or is this some damnable hell you've brought me to? Yes..I smell the air. I feel the breeze against my cheek. The heat from the candle. I AM Alive. And in being alive...I exist outside these walls as much as anyone else. And you WILL tell me what has happened. Why I am so much deformed. Is it your mark upon me? Is my tainted skin some metaphor to an existence that always bred within me? Tell me! Radakast!" The name ushered from his lips betrays the identity of this other...personality perhaps. Existing within Jason Spire...another part of himself maybe? But the name gives a title to the target of Spire's questions. Radakast: "These answers will come in time, my friend. For now, all you need to know, is that those responsible are everywhere. You must realize first that I am your friend...your ally in this. It was I that gave you a new life...a new home. A sanctuary from the world. And it was I, who gave you a new outlook...a new future in life. This, wrestling business...it is ideal for us. It is why I have strained to get you into an organisation known as Core...the world will hear our story Jason. They will hear your pleas...and listen to our vengeance! There is no other way!" Jason Spire: "You...You've given me this? A life of confrontation? To stand in the ring? To be condemned by all those who watch? What kind of a life is that!" Spire's expression changes to a deep smile. An accusing look comes over his face...staring, conviction brewing in his eyes...into the mirror. Radakast: "Ah...you do not see the whole picture Jason. I give you an opportunity! Yes, this will be a life of confrontation. But, you will stand against those who did this...deformed you. They are all responsible. Do not ever think otherwise. All those you face...they were all responsible. It will give us a chance at revenge. Give us a chance to put wrongs right. What right do they have to have unblemished skin? What right do they have to live their lives of decadence? What damn right do they have, Jason, to condemn you? This is why I put you here...vengeance. And, you will see...you give these observers the pain...the suffering...the torture you were forced to endure...and they will love you for it! Humanity is fickle in this way. Trust in me Jason...they will adore you!" Jason Spire seems to ponder this last point. He seems distressed by the entire debacle...but something convincing in Radakast's statement. Could he redeem himself? For surely...to be this deformed...must have been a punishment. And what if Radakast was right...what if these people could love him? Jason Spire: "You tempt me with words of poison! But, alas...I see no other choice. Yes, I will stand in that ring. Yes, I will win. But it will be me...not you...who carve a future out of all this. I am more than you make me out to be Radakast. I am so much more. I will remember! I will make you tell me! When the people roar my name...when applause deafens you...you will have to tell me!" Radakast: "All in good time Jason. All in good time. For now, feel blessed that I am by your side. You will see my point of view Jason...eventually. But yes...goade the love you so desperately seek. Take it. But never forget who...and what you really are...Jason Spire!" ********** The scene reopens in a bar...quiet, local, and full of character. Sitting at the bar, two men, drunk on whiskey, are having some inaudible discussion. Behind them, seated at a table, two young girls sit. They are sharing perhaps their first drink. But these are no ordinary girls...they are in love. Their eyes meet, and betray their feelings. Sipping at their wine genially, and exchanging the odd few words of practical dialogue...the recent telephone bill...last week's party. These verbal exchanges a poor mask for what is really on their minds. One of the drunken men is turned on his chair, looking at the two girls. He nudges his friend, who also turns. He lets out a grunt, and the first man whispers into his partner's ear. They seem to agree on something, and the first man rises to his feet, his friend looking on. The standing man, dressed in a red checkered shirt, with baseball cap and blue jeans, edges forward. He reaches the girls' table, and hooking his thumbs into his jeans, pulls them up. He kneels down, resting his elbows onto the table. Then, in a coarse southern accent, begins to speak. "Hey Ladies...can I get ya a drink?" The girls pay little attention to him, merely a glance, before returning to their conversation. The man s******s to himself, and looks back at his friend, pushing his thumb up as if in some kind of apprasal for his efforts. "You know, it's rude to ignore someone when being spoken to? I aksed you if you'd like a drink? Me and my friend over there wanna get you one. And you're gonna say yes, and come over and share some nice, friendly chat with us...right?" One of the girls stops her conversation, turning to the man. She has piercings in her nose, and all over her left ear. Jet Black hair and lips compliment one another, as she looks the man up and down. First Girl: "Erm...sorry hunny...we're kinda in the middle of something. Why don't you just go over and get your buddy a drink. He looks kinda lonely over there." Her friend lets out a giggle at the comment, and the man kneeling by their table seems taken aback by this. He wasn't expecting that. Man #1: "What? What the...? What the hell's wrong with asking you to get a drink?" First Girl: "Well, hunny...in case you didn't notice...we don't like, swing that way. We appreciate the sentiment...but we ain't really into the whole old pervy men thing...so, no thanks!" The man stands up, again offended by the ferocity of her rebuttle Man #1: "F'ckin Dykes! Oi, Bob...we got ourselves a couple of girls who play for the other team over here!" Bob gets up, his blue t-shirt betraying his pot belly, as its tucked into his jeans. He comes over...about 5 inches over his friend. He stands over the two girls. Bob: "See, I don't think they got what you were askin' Joe. Maybe we should rephrase it?" Joe: "Yeah. Look, "girls"...I don't give a damn what you think you want...but me an' Bob know what we want. You're comin' with us, and we'll convince you to convert back to the christian way." Bob snickers. The two girls look frightened. They look at the barman for help...but he disappears to the back, pretending to be busy...but he knows full well the situation. There seems to be no one else around...and the two girls look at each other, and seem to panic. Suddenly, the first girl jumps to her feet, and knees Joe in the groin. Bob grabs her, and throws her face into the table. Her girlfriend gets up, and Bob slaps her down. Joe rubs his groin, and grabs the first girl from behind. Just then, someone approaches from a dark corner. Carrying a glass of malt whiskey...his boots wet, and treading squelchingly on the tiled floor. He stands but four feet away from the two men, a trenchcoat obscuring his face. He takes a swig of his malt, and motions to the two men. This is Jason Spire...our prisoner of sanctuary. Jason Spire: "it was them! They did it!" Spire shakes his head, as if the words came from elsewhere. Jason Spire: "Can I buy you guys another drink?" Joe lets go of the girl he was holding, and pushes her back into her seat, turning round, facing Spire. Joe: "Just who in the hell are you?" Jason Spire: "A concerned friend..." Bob: "Hey Joe...look at that guy's face. He's wearin' some sort of mask" Joe: "F'ckin freak! Get the hell outta here boy...before you get yourself hurt." Joe turns around again, and gropes at the first girl. Spire grabs him by the shoulder, shunting him back. Jason Spire: "I don't think you heard me Joe." Bob: "He heard you fine freak!" With that, Bob slams his fist hard into Spire's stomach, forcing him to double over. Spire falls to his knees, and Bob boots his face hard. Bob lays a couple of boots into Jason's chest, who holds his gut in pain. Bob: "Next time, mind your own f'ckin business!" He turns his attention back to the girls, as Jason lies on the floor, grimacing in pain. He seems to hear something, and turns his head around fast. He turns his head back. No one there...except the two men and the girls. Radakast: "I told you you weren't welcome here Jason. But you just had to go out didn't you? Had to see the world?" Jason Spire: "Shut up!" Radakast: "You're going to have to listen to me Jason, if you want to carve this new glorious life of yours. I am your deliverance." Jason Spire: "I said Shut up!" The two men turn around, looking at the fallen Jason Spire. Joe: "Who the hell you talkin' to boy?" Bob boots him in the face, sending blood across the floor. Bob: Keep your mouth shut kid!" The camera peers in on Jason Spire, who again grimaces in pain. Something slowly comes over him though. He stops grimacing. His eyes deepen, and his expression changes. Spire gets to his knees, and stands up. He pats his lip with two fingers, and looks at the blood. He then flicks it over at Bob, staining his shirt. Bob turns around, to see Jason Spire standing, staring at him, smiling. Bob: "Who you little..." Jason Spire: "Freak?" Bob looks furious. He walks over to Spire, squaring up to him. Spire though lunges his fist into Bob's gut, and knees him hard in the face. He kicks him in the face, sending Bob to the floor. Spire grabs Joe, and throws him face first into the bar. spire then grabs a chair, and breaks one of the legs off of it. He walks over to Joe, and holds his face up...peering into it. Radakast: "Now...Jason...watch what happens when you get involved in the outside..." Jason Spire slams Joe's face into the bar, sending blood everywhere. He slams his face in again. Joe falls to the floor. Spire slams the chair leg over the back of his head, hard. He turns, looking at Bob, who is on his knees. Spire runs over, and slams his foot into Bob's face. Blood splatters on the seats. He brings the chair leg across the face of Bob, and brings it down again over his back. The first girl, realizing what's going on, comes over, and grabs Spire's arm. He swings his arm back, and knocks her to the bar. Bringing the stool leg down over her face, she topples to the floor unconsciouse. He walks over to her girlfriend...a grin on his face. She holds her arms up, terrified...when Jason Spire finally comes to his senses. He drops the stool leg, now covered with blood, to the floor, and looks over at the three bodies on the floor. He looks back at the terrified girl, and falls back into the bar. Jason Spire: "No...No...it wasn't me! No...I'm sorry. I...I...Radakast You bastard!" Spire looks over the fallen bodies again, and at the terrified girl. He sees the barman in the back, on the phone, calling the police. Spire thinks for a moment, before running out of the bar. As he runs...a voice comes out of his lips...a voice quietly suggesting... I Told you so...
