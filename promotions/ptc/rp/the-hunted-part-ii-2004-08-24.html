The hunted: Part II

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 I stood on the edge of the forest I had occupied merely a few nights ago when I had been the victim of a vampire attack. The scene was familiar save for a few exceptions. The stars that had been dazzling with such beauty had now retreated behind the clouds as a violent rain drenched the earth soaking into the soft soil beneath my brown Doc Marten work boots. This downpour cast an eerie glow about; the rain, seemingly luminescent painted a haunting glow upon the face of these woods. Trees, grass, rocks, plants, and even the dark soil itself seemed to absorb this shimmering liquid, looking down upon my arm I couldn't help but notice the strange shade my skin had taken on, differing from it's normal color it now seemed to glow with a bizarre silver color. The sleeveless black trench coat I wore now glimmered of silver and turning my bluish gray eyes to the sky I saw a strange silver hue amidst the heavens. The stars may have not been visible but the moon most certainly was and it was decorated with a ring, this ring wasn't of the same silver as the rain but rather a shade of red…blood red. Something evil was amidst. A clove of garlic rested in the pocket of my frayed jeans and strapped to my side was an iron stake, this iron stake was sharpened to a deadly point and its handle was a crucifix of sterling silver. Bound to my left hip by tight leather thongs it remained unmoving as I turned to look at the shadow man, Xavier Crysto. "I'm ready." I said, without a moments hesitation. "This rain makes my old bones ache…don't make me play savior tonight." Xavier warned, in complete sobriety. "I can't do this on my own Xav…" I sighed as I made this statement, it truly pained me to admit that I was helpless in such a situation. "You won't have too, I'll be there to guide you in your quest." Xavier softly replied his aged voice cracking. "I thought that your age hindered you from taking such action against the forces of evil." I said, neglecting my tact momentarily. "I never said that I would be physically there, but I will guide you in this journey nonetheless." The wise Xavier replied softly as he leaned against his all too familiar walking stick, the rain rolling off of his black cloak. "This is no time for riddles, I'm prepared to embark on a difficult mission." I returned with a snort of contempt in my voice. "Go your way my child and you shall have my guidance…" Said Xavier his voice soft as I strained to hear it above the torrential rainfall. Looking into his cold and haunted blue eyes I saw something in them that I had never before seen…reassurance. For a brief second I could almost see the Xavier Crysto of old, from his days as a hero and defender of the forsaken. Yet this brief look of kindness quickly faded as the wrinkles around his eyes were crunched together and his kind look returned to the haunted features that he will be best remembered for, without another word I turned my back to Xavier and stepped into the forest. The drenched grass and mud gave way beneath my feet, sinking in and allowing my boot to be coated in mud only for the silver rain to wash it away instantly. The rain seemed to fall harder now as I stood within the realm of good and evil, the forest haunted by vampires, witches and warlocks. My right hand crossed my body and my palm quickly adhered itself to the sharpened stake on my left side, wrapping my fingers around this beautiful piece of silver I pulled it free of it's makeshift scabbard and held it tightly in my hand. Putting forth my left palm I raised the crucifix stake and pressed it's sharpened edge against the tender skin of my palm. Biting down on my lip, I winced slightly as I jerked my arm back and drew the sharp stake across my palm, slicing it open in the center. The skin turned a sickening shade of white, tainted by the silver rain, before the warm crimson flow began and a pool of blood was in my hand. Looking down at my hand I slowly wiped my hand across the soft white tank top I wore, staining it crimson with my very own life's essence. The blood continued to run and I wiped it upon my jeans, trench coat and once more on my shirt. Reaching up to brush the shaggy locks from my eyes a stream of blood ran down my face staining my left cheek with the rich red liquid. The blood continued to flow as I allowed my left arm to hang limply the blood steaming from my palm and dripping down my fingertips before it hit the ground in perfect synergy with the rain. Returning my focus to the moon I couldn't help but make the realization that the red circle seemed to shine even brighter, even going so far as to cast a haunting look upon the thunderous heavens above me. Taking a step forward I began my journey into the woods following the same trail that I had taken the other night, only reminding myself to be on the lookout for trees this time around. The mighty trees that I had my eyes set upon housed no bats or owls tonight, but rather hanging bows that gleamed with the silver hue of the fallen rain. Raindrops fell from these branches to the ground giving a faint illumination unto my path. The strange glow allowed me to spy the footsteps of a few nights ago, and I simply followed these as my guide. "Danger lurks behind every corner…" The voice said, causing me to jump. I recognized the weary croak easily for it was the voice of Xavier Crysto. "Xavier?" I asked, whipping around, my eyes looking in every direction to see if he had followed me into these woods. No answer met my ears, and I was forced to voice my query once more. "Xavier, where are you?" My voice was now filled with confusion as I looked for him throughout the shimmering forest. "I am with you, as I said I would be." Came his patient answer. "No! Don't play games with me…I don't have time for your riddles…where are you?" I asked, angered by the complex enigma that he was. "Foolish mortal." Hissed the vampire, his voice silent and inaudible above the rain. "Look at him, talking to himself, or is he seeking guidance from God? It makes no difference to me for I shall dispose of him…" The calculating vampire held back a laugh as he sat perched twelve feet above the ground in a large tree merely twenty feet away from our hero. His sharp incisors shined in the gleaming rain, his full red lips pulled back in an arrogant smile. "Tonight will not be your night of victory, for tonight I shall drink your blood and consume your flesh." The vampire whispered, shaking with anger as his drenched hair fell over his face and hung to his shoulders in ratty strings. "My son, I have told you…I am with you." Xavier responded, his voice more kind and gentle than I had ever heard it. "I don't see you…I can only hear you, what is this Xavier, are you playing with my mind? Where are you?!" I angrily snapped, holding my voice to an angry hiss as I continued to search for the shadow man knowing full well that in his cloak, black as a ravens feather, I would be unable to see him in these conditions. "Playing with your mind? Foolish child, can't you see that I am helping you as I said I would? I am your guide on this path of great turmoil and danger." Said Xavier, his voice now taking on an amused tone. "Get out of my head! Get out of my head, now! Now, Xavier…now!" I spat out, furious at his invasion of my thoughts and mind. "And leave the hero to die? You have much to learn my child, much to learn." Xavier replied in a cynical tone. "I wont listen to you…" I said in defiance, walking at a quick pace towards a large tree that loomed ominously before me, it's black branches now shining of silver. "Closer human…closer." Begged the vampire as he now began to salivate, his mind being overtaken with an insatiable lust for blood and the scent of this mans blood drifting into his nostrils. Unbeknownst to him, our hero had cut his own hand to draw the vampire into his trap. He could almost feel the blood…the human would scream as he sunk his teeth deep into his neck and bit down. The human would thrash about to no avail and the vampire would suck the very humanity from this man. He had escaped once, but not again…this time it would be over and the vampire would taste his blood. His rich, thick, sweet blood, so full of iron… The vampire could not contain his own drool as it ran down his chin. It would only be a matter of seconds now, mere seconds. Closer…closer…" The vampire urged, as the young man unknowingly walked towards him and the vampire crouched on his branch, his body coiled tight as a spring with every muscle tight and ready to explode into action. "Be careful…" Xavier warned, his voice cutting into my thoughts as I shook my head violently trying to rid myself of his "I can't hear you…" I replied in a singsong voice, ignoring Xavier's warnings. "…danger lurks…" Said Xavier, only to be cut off again. "I can't hear you…" I responded for the second time. "…above you!" Screamed Xavier, a tortured wail in his voice as he invaded my psychological sound system. My eyes lifted to the sky… "Ahhhhh!" Screamed the vampire, his shrill cry of hatred piercing the night air as he leapt from the tree to the body of the human below! He landed atop the human and impaled him violently into the terra firma crushing his body and driving the oxygen from his lungs! "Kill him, kill him!" Screamed Xavier as I felt my body slamming into the ground and the air escaping from my lungs. I thrashed about desperately as the vampire's hands locked onto my wrists; this resembled our last encounter, only…Xavier wasn't there to help me. The silver stake had been sent out of my hand upon landing and now I had to defend myself against a virtual killing machine as best I could. Twisting my body violently I couldn't escape his grasp and as he leaned down to bite into the tender skin of my neck I did the only thing I could. Pressing both feet against the wet sod and thrusting upwards I managed to buck off the vampire and send him rolling away as I rolled over onto my stomach. At least now I could defend myself to some extent… The vampire; dressed in old-fashioned black clothes landed on his back and immediately rolled over as well, he was now facing me as we both squared off on our hands and knees. He licked his lips before making the first move as he dived towards me, I used my superior speed to roll away but he managed to snare me in one of his strong arms as he threw a powerful limb around me and drew me close to him. I kicked desperately at him as he held onto my left ankle and stood to his feet, rolling over onto my back I kicked him hard in the stomach and rolled backwards, head over heels before coming up into a kneeling position. My hand rested against a metallic object…the crucifix handled silver stake! The vampire charged at me and I quickly stood to my feet, brandishing the stake as I held it before me. He couldn't stop himself and his eyes widened, an expression of terror crossed his pale face as he ran directly into the stake… The stake hit him dead center in the heart and stuck, collapsing onto his back he didn't move, yet I did. Jerking the stake out of his chest I raised it high above my head and with both hands upon the handle I drove it down into his heart! Raising the stake above my head I drove it into his heart again…and again…and again! His blood showered over my face, chest and hands as I pulled the stake out of him and collapsed onto the forest floor, gasping for precious oxygen. "Welcome to the world of vampires, consider this your introductory course to slaying a vampire." Xavier said, a hint of pride in his voice. "One down…how many more to go?" I asked in dry sarcasm, which seemed all too ironic for such a serious situation as this. "All of them. But first on that list is the most powerful vampire of all…Leinad Soma." Said Xavier, in a sincere tone. "Soma is next…" I whispered to myself. "Soma will feel my wrath…his reign of terror will be ceased, the monster must be stopped." Xavier, in turn, replied. "The monster will be stopped…"
