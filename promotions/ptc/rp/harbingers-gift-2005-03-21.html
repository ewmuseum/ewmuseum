Harbingers Gift

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-21 The hard leather fell open easily as his fingers pressed against the copper latch. He starred down at it, his hand writing, and he wondered if ever it would matter. One day would someone come along to read the chronicle of his journey, or, would it be lost into the sands of time. Thin fingers reached from beneath black cloth and flipped through the pages. Each was marked with a symbol and held his thoughts. Often he would wonder to himself if it would really matter, would someone ever find and understand what he was trying to convey? Perhaps it could serve them better than it had ever served him. In the riddles he had collected and the scriptures he had studied, he found many keys too many doors, but none gave him the answer he so desperately sought. And as he looked over the leather bound journal, he found himself against questioning the reason behind it. It had become a scribbled parchment of pages that held incantations and information that had long ago fallen out of the public view. The truth about the witch hunts in Salem. What the world never wanted to know of the Illuminati and their role in creating their perfect country of America. The scientific age that had been planned by man to remove their Father from existence. And the Church knew of all of this. Quietly they sat back, like kings upon their thrones, believing that their soldiers would hold back the coming fray by any means. What they didn’t know, what they couldn’t have known, was how their apathy would lead a man to torment and loss. How the frailty of man would doom the only one who could save them. He could remember the arguments and threats that came from his meetings with the Church. How Padre Vincent did not want to hear of the hunt that was currently taking place. He scoffed at Monroe and the belief that Avion would remerge himself. Then again it was not uncommon for the chronicles of the lost angels to have been scoffed at. It was, after all, now nothing more than common place in their sanctity of stone. From behind the rock walls of the Holy city did these priests believe that they could avert the churches eventual downfall. After all, should the church of God fall how much longer would it be before man fell as well? Then again most of them knew what was happening. They knew the signs and only cringed in fear. Even the Pope himself had seen the writings from Fatima and openly wept, but he never tried to understand what they meant. He only understood the baptism of blood and what it meant for all things. In the warped and twisted minds of those who reign on high in the Catholic Church, they believe that their wisdom is what serves them best, even when fueled by hidden agendas and underhanded deals. Yet it was their wisdom so long ago that failed not only humanity, but their Father as well. Indeed it was the wisdom of man who helped bring about the resurrection of a fallen deity. Not the Messiah meant to bring about the second age of happiness and hope. Instead it was their desire and lust to look upon the face of God which led them to find the answers to an ancient puzzle. Wrapped within the tomb of the Arcane and containing the tail of a creature that was never to know existence, it was their push which helped to find him. It was their insistence that helped to awaken him. And it was their stubbornness and pride that helped to doom his avatar for all eternity. “My name is Scott Monroe.” Two silver eyes stare out from beneath a black leather hat. Long ago those eyes knew happiness, contentment and truth. Now the windows to his soul reveal only one single, primal urge. “And this is the chronicle of my nightmare.” Now those eyes reveal the only thing left to drive him. Rage! Origins Chapter II – Harbingers Gift 1300 A.D. “Father Taylor I must insist that you wait for the Vatican’s approval before pressing on with any of your research.” Father Marcus Taylor looked about the room and knew instantly that he was outnumbered. They didn’t understand or couldn’t understand what was beyond that seal. Why the ancient Pagan’s had sealed away the entity within. It was their fear of the truth which blinded them. Their fear of knowing that their Heretical Gods were not real but that the one true Father was. So inside they sealed away the answer to solving the puzzle of how best to show him to the world. “Father Taylor are you listening to me?” He gave a slight nod as he looked about the room. These men were supposed to be his contemporaries, his equals, and yet here they stood shaking like leaves in the August breeze. Their fear wasn’t justified because whatever lie behind that seal was all that humanity needed. It was their proof, their poise, and their chance to show the world that the Church indeed knew what it was doing. The mistakes of the past would be cleansed in the future of the new… “Taylor I’m sorry…” Domini Vogue rose from his seat behind the desk and shook his head. “…I’m afraid that you’ve taken your studies to far. I have no choice but to order all of your studies to a halt until we receive word from the Pope himself. The Abysel will remain intact, here, under my command until we are given news, but you are not to go near the Landlock, is that understood?” Father Taylor again gave a slight nod. He knew that there was no arguing with them, that their minds were made up. Unable to comprehend what was beyond those rocks, what was sealed away by the ancient symbols of the past deities, these men claimed that he bordered on heresy when it was they who ignored God’s law. To spread the word and proof of his existence to all who would listen. As Father Taylor turned on his heel and marched from the audience chamber of Cardinal Neviana, he knew what had to be done. And Cardinal Neviana gave off a slight smile as he watched the priest walk away. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Present Day “It will be hate that drives you, young one.” The voice spoke to him from the nether, hiding itself and its power far beyond where his prying eyes might search. But he listened intently, knowing that the sermon would continue. “This is a battle that you have been chosen to wage alone. Your life, your reasoning and your belief in the Father are what have doomed you to this. Those who walk the line so closely between faith and heresy are easily manipulated by our kind. We search for you because you seek the miracles that we can perform. We restore your faith while your Father hides away. And what happens when the end becomes the beginning, and we are given a chance to stain this plain in the river of blood again?” From the darkness it spoke, hiding itself away from the light because it had been banished from it. Never again was this creature to know the touch of the divine. Never again was it to know happiness in servitude to its Lord and Creator. Now this creature walked the line of the heretic. An eternity spent upon this plain searching for the keys to erase the existence of its Father. “I was like you once.” And he was. Long ago before anyone could have possibly imagined the powers that were sealed within this planet. Long before the Church had become the corrupt group of egos and agendas. Once upon the time he was one of them. Before he fell in love with someone whom he could never have, before he found the weakness of the angels was mirrored in the weakness of man. Love can be the greatest experience for a being to ever know. And a forbidden love can be even better. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 1300 A.D. Father Taylor woke from his slumber, his eyes red and bloodshot, his hair tussled and matted. He had fallen asleep again going over some of his work. He knew that should someone come for him he would be taken on trial before the Vatican, but he didn’t care. He was close to solving the riddle of the Landlock, close to finding the true face of God. What would the pagans and heathens say then? What would those who lived in sin and wallowed in hatred do when their Father was freed from his prison and loosed upon them? It would be freedom from weakness. It would be their freedom from failure. Humanity could once again look upon their Father and smile knowing that they were meant to be there with him. That the curse had been lifted and that Eden was no longer beyond their reach. Eve’s sin would be cleansed and their kind could return to happy worship of the deity who created them. It was his belief that he was doing something important, something for the better of mankind, which drove Father Marcus Taylor to continue his studies against the order of the Vatican. He was one of the few men in his time to have studied the ancient languages of the nomad tribes. To have seen their sorcery and the demons loosed by it first hand. He was all that was left of their once proud order of Templar Knights, the men who protected and served the Church as they were asked. Now his time was spent in study. All day every day, because he felt he was so close to finding the answer. It was a riddle that he was convinced he could solve. His belief that upon solving the puzzle the world would instantly be changed for the better was something he held dear. He wanted to believe in his work. He wanted to believe that there was a way for man to commune with their Heavenly Father, so that one day man might be allowed to return to Eden. Father Taylor believed that he was close to finding a way. Close to appeasing God and erasing the sins of all man. If only he had known what he was truly doing. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Present Day I started work on this book shortly after I heard of her death. Samantha Crowley, age 18, leaving this world at such a young age. I remember it like it was yesterday, I remember walking into the Cathedral and asking her to marry me, and I remember her breaking out into tears as she said yes. It was a dream, vivid and pleasing, that I would have wrapped myself in forever if I could have. Then again when we’re chosen for service, there isn’t much more we can do than to serve. “Why do you let yourself dwell upon thoughts that do not matter?” The endless boundaries of the void stretched for miles and miles. To him it might as well have stretched forever, it was a state of dementia, a way to make sure that his mind was unable to hold itself in place. Yet it didn’t bother him anymore. The dreams, the voices, he was used to them now. After all, how long was one supposed to live half a life and not accept their fate? How long was one meant to be an avatar of destruction and condemnation yet try to free themselves from it. This was his war now and he would help stain the Earth in their blood. “Because it is her memory that fuels my flame.” He stepped forward into the light, his eyes starred at the black sun as if there was an answer there. He knew that there wasn’t, that the voice wasn’t going to help in, that in the end he had found his own cursed road. “Remember that to extinguish the flames of hate would be to extinguish your very life.” He gave a slight nod, he knew what it meant to turn away now, he knew what would happen to himself and to those whom he loved. Yes, he still felt love, emotion still coursed through his veins. He was not completely gone yet. Though he knew that it was emotion that played the bitter part of the key, and it had loosed the lock of the damned upon the world. Emotion, the greatest gift we were even given and the greatest curse we could ever imagine. Anyone who has ever felt the exhilaration of love knows that the only thing stronger is the loss of that love. “Stay vigilant, my avatar, because soon our war shall begin.” As his eyes starred at the black sun he shook his head. There was no need for vigilance on his part. A quick glance about the world showed that they were hurtling at a break neck speed towards their own demise. No one cared but everyone knew. The entire human race seemed to be hushed in a quit awe of what was coming. A collective conscious that could feel their own end and were still apathetic towards it, they disgusted him. Kneeling before the son he placed his hands upon the ground and took a deep breath. This was reality to him, this pain was real and he knew it, thus he prepared himself to receive the sacrament. “You believe that your service will eventually be looked upon by the Father and deemed worthy. I see into your mortal soul, descendent of the traitor, and I know that which you seek. To repent for the sins of your family, to beg forgiveness for the man who played a key role in loss of humanity upon this plain. Yet I must know, my child, why do you still seek his comfort? Why do you still seek to find his grace even after he has abandoned you?” What was his driving motivation to hold onto his humanity? Was it love for someone whom had passed on? Was it love for someone who stood before him, who offered him a chance at salvation? Or was the creature right and he was truly trying to repent for the sins of his families past. Deep down it was a combination of all three and he knew it. The answer was nothing more than a single word. A word of power to those who knew and understood its meaning and what that meaning held for this damned race. “Faith.” The black sun began to spin as the sky grew dark and foreboding. All around him the voices came, the shouts of the damned that were trapped within this place. Echoes of a distant past that begged for freedom from their torment and would willingly give their very souls for a brief release from their pain. He knew that their pain was forever, as his was to be, and he knew that this creature was going to make sure of that. “You believe that your faith will bring favor with your Father? You believe that by doing the bidding of those whom have twisted his name and word you shall find him waiting at the end? Your God, dearest son, is dead and gone. He left a long time before you creatures could even understand the stupidity of your own actions. He turned against his own kind and waged a war upon us, his Seraphim, because we were unwilling to let you defile the Mother. After all that he has suffered, all that existence has suffered because of his greed and lust, it is fitting that it would be arrogance that feeds your false faith.” Was it arrogant to believe that if he served his purpose in the end he would be rewarded? Was it truly nothing more than pride that made him continue along this road? No, pride and arrogance weren’t his reasoning for faith, he had faith long before he understood what those two concepts meant. He could remember being young and going to the Church, a white wooden building in the center of their small town. He could remember the feeling of peace and the thought of becoming a priest, devoting his life to the Father which had given him a life. It was arrogance that held his faith in place but the desire to repay his Father for the gift of life. No matter how horrible it might have become, no matter how empty a shell he would eventually become, he would at least say thank you. “You believe that because his purpose for you was different that the one you envisioned for yourself that he has gone astray. You lie to yourself, creature, and claim that God has abandoned us. Yet the truth is that you have abandoned him. It was you who turned away from the light and sealed yourself in this darkness and it will be you, not he, who suffers eternally for it.” Laughter began to echo within his ears and with slow, steady paces, became more and more intense. His brain began to throb and his nose began to bleed but he held his ground. Perhaps he was forced to live upon his knees due to the reasons of his Father but he would never be turned against him. Freedom was always there, just beyond his reach, he just had to figure out how to escape from the grasp of this thing. A damned deity who was born for no other reason that spit in the face of Jehovah, to try and prove that the Father could be wrong, yet they didn’t understand why. It was pitiful that this creature of unlimited power was nothing more than a child looking out onto a helpless world. Hidden away from the entire universe for so long that all record of its existence had been wiped away and only now was it reemerging onto an unprepared time. “No, mortal, I believe that if I hold the same power as your vaunted God, that he cannot be God. I believe that if I am capable of performing the same miracles, the same creation of life, the same creation of existence, then I too am a God. I have proven this time and time again to you and I have shown you the way that you shall walk for all eternity. You hope that his damnation of your race will be appeased by your actions but you fail to realize that you and I, we are damning them all.” His head came from its position on the ground and starred into empty sky. Was he truly damning them by continuing the path that had been chosen for him? Was he nothing more than a puppet of a failed deity that wanted to experience all that this life had to offer? No. Deep down the answer had been given to him long ago, all he had to do was continue to hold onto his faith. No matter the trials and tribulations he would continue his march. All of the damned who were now trying to wreak havoc upon this plain, it would all be for naught, because at the end of the road he would be there. And if hatred had to be his weapon, then so be it. “You are wrong.” His vision began to blur and he felt his head start to swim. His nails clutched at the ground beneath him but he knew that it was too late. A loud rumbling began to fill the air and he watched in horror as the ground he knelt upon gave way. Shattering and tearing into a thousand pieces and leaving him floating upon a small chunk. The stars filled the air and a figure emerged in the sky. His long black hair was receding in the front yet stretched out halfway down his back. Above his lips were two thin, dark lines, and upon his chin hung a small outcropping that fell halfway down his chest. Two hands were created from the stars themselves and the universe around him began to hum and purr like a kitten searching for affection. Indeed, here he was God, and as those two eyes starred down at their avatar, a grim chuckle escaped from the void that was his mouth. “Am I wrong, cursed son, am I truly wrong? I have lived for an eternity and patiently awaited your birth and eventual realization. I have seen the coming signs and watched the vision play before my sight a thousand times. For all that you think you know, you could only begin to imagine the wealth of knowledge that I have stored within me. I know what I am to be. I am the key to open Pandora’s Box, to help release upon them that which they deserve most, his vengeance. Then I shall turn against him, his creation having become more powerful than he could ever imagine, and I shall take this universe and existence as my own. And you, young Monroe, you shall be the avatar that I have waited all my existence for.” Rising from the ground he clenched his fist and narrowed his eyes. No matter what claims this demon made, no matter what gifts this demon offered, he would forever refuse his advances. If it had waited an eternity for him to be born then it would wait another eternity while he fought against it. His role was clear and was made clear to him long ago by one of the few people to have ever loved him. Her memory would not be in vain, her death would not have been for nothing, he wouldn’t allow it. “Do what you will, demon, because you fail to realize that I have that which you Seraphim have forever hated my kind for possessing.” He expected more taunts, more threats, something, but instead there was only silence. Instead he simply starred up at the being who starred back. Perhaps it was reading his soul. Perhaps it was testing him, trying to see if it could easily break him, but his mind was made up. He was not meant to serve the dark forces in their pursuit of power. He knew what he was meant to do and what had to be done to achieve it. Was it in deep thought? Was it in mediation? What purpose could it have for the silence? As his mind began to question the situation the voice began again. This time the tone was different, less harsh, more like that of someone remembering something. “I understand your free will, mortal, and I understand that you were given something that I can never posses. Yet you fail to realize exactly what it is that I am, what it is that I have become. In time you will understand. Your rage and hatred shall be the fires to temper your soul so that your body might serve me. I see through you, Scott Monroe, and I know that your future is one of the few that looms just beyond the horizon. There are no visions to turn to for comfort. There is no one left in which you can confide. You and I, Scott, are all that are left.” All that were left? He didn’t understand and he wasn’t sure he wanted to. There were things that humanity was meant not to know. Perhaps they are things that might change our opinion of God. Perhaps nothing more than trivial information that would be useless to us. Yet as he starred up towards the vision, he knew that what was about to come was something he might have never heard before. A side to the story from a being who truly had a tale to tell. Despite an inward voice calling from his soul, telling him not to listen, not to test his faith, he found himself strangely curious. “Before the creation of man and the creation of the universe there was the infinite void. This plain of existence was nothing more than blackest night and brilliant stars. The Father and his creations, the Seraphim, spent their time enjoying the beauty and serenity of what Jehovah had created. For how long this age continued, we do not know, but we know that when the Father grew tired of the beauty that he had created, he turned away from us and towards his next project. He spent what you would call the seven days toiling over the universe, trying to perfectly fit everything together in harmony, so that there might be a union between himself, our kind and his newest creation, humanity. Alas, it was not to be. Lucent saw his seven days as the beginning of his turn away from us. It was painful, as we were created to feel the loss of the divine touch, and Lucent grew angry as the days continued. By the seventh day Lucent, who was once held highest in the court of the Father, gave him an ultimatum… -=-=-=-=-=-=- “Return with me, oh heavenly Father, that mine and my brethren might find an end to our suffering.” But Jehovah cast a casual glance towards Lucent and shook his head. “You may join me, here, but for the time being I shall not be returning to the Heavens. There is much work here to be done and I must continue my toil so that my plan can be realized.” Rage flooded the eyes of Lucent and as he stormed away from his Heavenly Father he cursed his name. It was the first time in all of creation that it had ever been done and Jehovah heard it immediately. He called out to Lucent to try and explain, hoping to show his friend what was going on, but Lucent did not answer. Jehovah left his toils for long enough to see Lucent take the harp that the Father had created especially for him and cast it into the pits. All in existence stopped and took notice. How could Lucent act that way? Had the Father not given him life? Had the Father not chosen him above all others? Had the Father not made a special place at his side so that his friend might be near him? Indeed all of those things had been done but Lucent, ever wanton, desired more. He turned on his heel and sneered towards his friend, his Creator and his Father, then spat as he spoke. “You have chosen those creatures over us, we who were created for your service, and we have never failed you. Not once in existence have we chosen to ignore you pleas and follow our own hearts. Not once have we been unwilling to sacrifice everything for you. Yet now you create them, now you create another race because you are bored with ours? Now you cause us pain and refuse to even acknowledge it?” Jehovah shook his head as he tried to calm his friend down. “Lucent look into my soul and understand that you’re only angry. This isn’t you. I don’t know why you’re acting this way but I know that this isn’t you.” It was Lucent’s turn to shake his head. “You are wrong, friend, look upon me and know that I have woken from my slumber of stupidity and ignorance. By your own hand was I shown the light of our “God” and was I allowed to behold what he would truly do to his creations. I have served you my entire life and those things, those pathetic wretches, are given all your attention? So much so that you refuse to listen as I play the lute or that you no longer wish to hear my stories? To Hell with them, Father, and to Hell with you! Either return with me to the Heavens or watch as I bring my hatred to those…those things!” “Lucent, I am not returning to the Heavens at this time, I am sorry.” Lucent snarled as he snatched his lute from the ground beneath him and held it aloft. “This is a symbol of our friendship, oh lord, and now it shall be as ours has become.” Wrapping his hands around the neck of the lute he twisted the strings until one by one they popped and snapped. The small instrument soon gave way beneath his strength and shattered in his hands. Lucent looked to his Father who again only shook his head. With a tear in his eye Lucent fought back the well of emotion within him, then turned slowly and strode towards the darkness. Forever had they been warned of entering there, forever had the angels known that they would be changed forever upon entrance into it, but Lucent no longer cared. If pain was to be his mistress, then he would embrace her until the end of his days. Jehovah watched as his friend turned away and marched into the darkness. Where he was going and what his intentions were, Jehovah knew the moment the thought crossed his mind, but there was nothing that could be done. Before the creation of the angels he knew what would eventually happen. He had given them life knowing that he would be forced to sacrifice his friendship for the race that was to be his greatest accomplishment. And he knew that his greatest accomplishment would also become the greatest weapons in Lucent’s war against him. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “…and thus began our part in this tragic play. You see I marched with the side of the angels as we were victorious against Lucent and his followers. His flock, though violent and vengeful, were foolish and knew little of the art of war. An art that the Father taught to us as the coming darkness drew near. It wasn’t long after the war that I found myself going with the others to the realm of man. We watched with great curiosity to see why exactly the Father had been willing to wage a war in Heaven so that your kind might inherit the Earth. After Lilith betrayed Adam and became Lucent’s demon queen, we saw exactly why the Father had spent so much time working on this project. He had given unto your kind free will. You could chose if you wanted to worship, you could chose if you sought to serve, and you felt no pain in the absence of the divine. It was soon after that I found myself contemplating the reason behind it. I could look upon myself in the crystal clear waters of your world and I knew that I was, essentially, a slave. That I was bound by his rules because all I was aloud to do was that which he let me. My discontent continued to grow as I found myself purposely separating myself from him. The pain became my mistress and I was no longer concerned with the absence of the divine. In hindsight I suppose I knew I was committing treason against him, but I didn’t care. Soon after he found out of our trips to Earth and our interaction and curiosity of mortals, needless to say he was not pleased. We were told never to return of our own accord, that doing so would leave us weakened to Lucent and his hordes. Even the angels, to some degree, are trapped by their own arrogance. As was I…” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “…Because I found myself one day looking into the pond when another presence made itself noticeable to me.” From across the pond she starred at him. He knew he was supposed to be wretched and evil, he knew his place was to destroy her and her kind. But he didn’t. Instead he stood there and starred into her eyes. He watched as she gracefully strode across the pond and came to stand before him. Her clawed hand reached out and thin fingers ran across his chin. It was warmth. It was love. It was something that he hadn’t felt in a very long time. Not since they had come into existence. She reached out to him and pulled him close. He could hear her empty heart beating. He could hear her breathing. She seemed to purr and the sound was relaxing. Soon after he found himself growing weary, tired and followed her into the grove where she led him. As he took a place on the soft, green grass, he looked up to her and smiled. Happiness had returned to him. Not brought from on high as it was meant to be but brought from his enemy. She starred into his eyes for long moments before he summoned the courage. “I gave in to my desire and lust. I let myself feel the love that I had longed to feel for so long. With my own purposeful removal of the divine, I left myself open to other feelings. The emotions of your kind, the feelings and sensations that you all know and take for granted. That day I gave into her.” He listened but couldn’t believe what he was hearing. One of the Seraphim, a member of the choir, removing himself from God’s grace and giving into to the advances of the damned, it was almost impossible to imagine. “Soon after she and I bore a son. I took the child to him, my Father, proud of what I had become and longing to see the war between our kind end. He looked over the child and shook his head. I knew something was wrong. Without a thought I watched in horror as the one thing that had rekindled my faith was taken from me. He ordered our execution and the banishment of our child. He said he would punish him because he was not to blame. Yet we would know that our child would never know light. He was to be trapped forever in a dream state that meant that he could never truly be born. It was a fate worse than death to me. I struggled against the men whom I had called my brothers since our creation, desperately pleading with the Father to stay his hand. We were wrong. We were sorry. Anything I could do to appease his anger. Yet as I looked to him I saw his anger and I knew that there was nothing that would stop him. He ordered my beloved to die as his son would die. None of us knew what that meant but soon, soon we found out. The first crucifix was not created on the plains of man, but by the hand of God, to show his anger and judgment had been placed upon those whom he deemed wicked. I looked to him and swore before him that I would have my revenge, even if it meant that my existence would forever come to an end.” The voice stopped for a moment and Monroe realized that even for a creature such as this, memories such as these were truly painful. Even the idea of what had happened, the mere thought of it, was much more than anyone could have been asked to bear. It explained much of this creature and its never ending well of hatred, despair and wanton revenge upon God. For a moment he almost found himself feeling sympathetic for the deity. For a single moment he almost pitied it. “But then I realized that my gift to this world would be brought by one of his own creations. I watched as his son was crucified upon the cross, just as my beloved had been, and I took note of the name that betrayed him. The name of a man who was so weak that in the face of God he turned and fled, rather than facing the truth of his own existence and reasoning behind it. I watched the bloodline for all these years. Through wars, through poverty and through illness, I gave to them dementia and eventual strength through their own insanity. Because that cursed bloodline is the key to my revenge. I was unable to complete my mission once before but this time I shall not fail. You see, descendant of Judas, it will be your blood that helps to awaken my son from his slumber. It will be your body that becomes his avatar and your hand that will begin the baptism.” Monroe stumbled and fell to his knees. It wasn’t true, it couldn’t have been true, was he a descendant of Judas? He felt sick to his stomach as the deity stood over him, watching his reaction to what was obviously the biggest shock of his life. For one who had known since birth he was different to find that it was his ancestor that nailed Jesus to the cross, it was more than he could handle. Again his head began to swim and lights fluttered before his eyes. As he drifted off into a restless sleep he could hear the final sentiments of the creature. “They know what you are and have meant forever to make sure you become just that. Your world, your life, they will ruin everything you know before your eighteenth birthday. One of the three loves of your life shall be taken from you. It is then, when all hope is lost and all strength has failed you that you shall remember her words. When the baptism begins I shall make sure that you do not become their puppet. I will protect you long enough for my son to awaken. After that there will be nothing else I can do for you. After that it will be a war between you, my avatar, and the damned forces of Lucent.” Into sleep he fell, almost like a child, but the voice made sure he heard one last thing. “Your bloodline is as cursed as my own; remember this, as it will forever serve you well.” And the deity wrapped itself around him, protecting him from harm or the intrusion of any other force. This one would have the full protection of, not only a Seraphim, but the Angel whom had waged a war for God for so long. The Archangel Michael would see to that personally. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 1300 A.D. “Father Taylor there is something I must speak to you about.” Cardinal Neviana stood in the hallway outside of Father Taylor’s door and waited patiently as he heard the locks click. Upon entering the room it was as he had suspected, Taylor had continued his research. Turning he looked to Taylor and smiled. “I understand your position on this matter, Father, and I commend your willingness to continue with your research even after having been removed from your position. It is for that reason and that reason alone that I bring you thing.” Cardinal Neviana placed a small, leather bound book on the desk of Father Taylor. “I believe you’ll find some use in this. I have taken the liberty of bringing it to you myself because if anyone else were to see you with it, they would know immediately what you were doing. I don’t think either of us wants anyone to find out about this, we both have so much to lose. Yet to not decipher the puzzles of the Landlock would cost us a great deal more. I believe that even after the Vatican shuts down our research that we shall still be able to find the answer that we seek. You see, within this tome are the ancient languages and incantations of the heretics that have studied this place as well. The keys to this riddle are now before you, Father Taylor, and I trust in your ability to decode them.” Father Taylor starred at Cardinal Neviana in shock for a moment but his shock soon fell away to curiosity. If everything that Neviana had said was true then the answers that they needed had been here all along. “Thank you, Cardinal.” The Cardinal said nothing else but gave a slight nod and exited the room. As Father Taylor began to feverishly search through the tome Cardinal Neviana stood outside of his chambers and smiled a broad smile. The time of his awakening was now and the place for their war was across the ocean. On a content that was unprepared for the part it had to play. Inside his dormitory room Father Taylor scribbled and scrawled, crossed out and rewrote the lines of text. Over and over again he continued until he began to decipher them. Their riddle, their meaning, were locked within the tome and the answer to their riddle was the answer to the riddle of the Landlock. Why had a part of the Earth been sealed away by the powers of God himself? Was there something down there? Some kind of power unimaginable or weapon so incredible that whomever would wield it then became unstoppable? What would happen when whatever was trapped inside saw freedom again? Father Taylor never bothered to ask the questions, either to himself or to Cardinal Neviana. He didn’t take the time to think about his actions or what might happen because of them. In his haste to prove himself and his faith he failed to realize the danger that he was in. He also failed to realize the danger that he was putting humanity in. The only thought that came to him was the Landlock and it seemed to be his only purpose. As the days grew longer and his isolation continued he found that only one thought was there within the walls of his mind. It had consumed him completely, just as his brethren had feared, but none now dared to approach him. Removed from the rest of his brothers his mind began to break down. His soul began to cry out for answers that he would seemingly never have. If he wanted answers he had no choice. He knew what he had to do. Unravel the mystery of the Landlock. -Finale-
