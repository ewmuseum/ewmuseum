Run, Kari, Run.

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-01 Oh Kari, Where Art Thou? #02 : Run, Kari, Run. RP 2/2 vs. Limp. Jeff's watch, meet Jeff's impatient glare. Jeff's impatient glare, meet Jeff's watch. Just getting everyone acquainted. Pacing back and forth, sighing as he snapped his red braces, RPW's creative director - Jeff W. - began his illicit affair with the slowly passing time. Each second that ticked by was logged, cumulating and creating a heavy burden on his already tested patience. Was he being stood up on a date? No, but not that anyone could blame the guilty woman... Or man. At 8:30am on a Monday morning, Jeff W. was waiting for Karina Wolfenden. Sipping from his white coffee... No latte, no frappacino, no flavourings... Ward just stood beside a row of comfortable chairs and pretended that he didn't half expect it. In his passion-free mind, Ward ran over - word for word - the memo he made sure Karina received, having made a runner force it into her hand as she abruptly left Adrenaline the previous night. "Karina, Please be at the Halton (Oh, and that's the Dale F. Halton Arena, University of North Carolina (Charlotte), Charlotte, North Carolina, the United States of America, Earth, the Sol System, the Milky Way, the Universe - just in case you try and plead ignorance over location) at 8:30am (that's the MORNING) to record promotional material for the PTC Cruiserweight tournament. Attendance is NOT optional. - Jeffrey Ward." Hardly open to interpretation, was it? The soles of overly polished black dress shoes slapped against the tiled floor, creating a rhythmic annoyance for the technicians Ward had drafted in especially. Twiddling their thumbs and checking their equipment for the 3rd time, the sound and video techs just watched Jeffrey, trying to usher forth a decision to let them go. But alas, they weren't going anywhere... And deep down knew that the demanding Ward would find something else menial to do should the K-Wolf phone it in. Ah, speaking of Karina... Unbeknown to Ward, she wouldn't be turning up. No, she hadn't slept through her alarm. No, she wasn't in bed with the flu. The impoliteness of not informing one of her bosses aside, she did have a pretty good reason; she wasn't actually in the country. Again, Ward checked his watch, seeing that just over a precious minute had elapsed since his last reading... Not knowing that where Karina was, she was over 5 hours late. "Dammit," he seethed, more out of frustration at being defied than the inconvenience. "I knew we should have never kept that troublemaker on." "Boss," whined one of the techs through his thick, grizzled southern accent. "We actually gonna be doing anything here?" Ward's head slowly turned to face him in eerie fashion. The moment his cold eyes fixed on the worker, it was clear that they would be staying for as long as the creative director decreed. "Just check the set-up," Ward snarled, even though it was all the inconvenienced workers had been doing for the past 20 minutes. "Man," whispered the sound-tech as his head shook in contempt. "Now I know why Kari decided to stand the jack-ass up." "Where is she anyway?" his video-tech colleague questioned back, their words hushed so as not to disturb or provoke their highly strung boss. "Who knows?" the sound specialist shrugged, keeping a close eye on the brooding Ward. "But anywhere's better than here, right?" The employees exchanged an all knowing nod, before returning to the monotonous task at hand. A little over 10 metres away, in stark contrast to the relaxed banter of his employees, Jeff W. had set up a permanent watch on the door. The next person to arrive would be met by a fuming glare from the stony-cold man clad in grey pinstripe. But that person wouldn't be Karina. With a furrowed brow, and to through grinding teeth, Ward forced his frustrated words out. "Where are you?" Ward observed his watch ticking towards 8:50, while it approached 2pm where the K-Wolf ran through the mild, western-European afternoon. With leaves returning to trees, and winter coats being disregarded, a clear blue sky no longer meant a sharp cold. Tables and chairs came out of hibernation and littered the pavements outside the small cafes and bistros... A sure sign that spring was blossoming in the Parisian suburb Karina and her sunken emotions had eloped to. Karina Wolfenden needed to forget. Needed to purge her senses and jettison her thoughts. Having checked the prominent clock face displayed on the breath-taking, Gothic architecture of the local church, Karina reminded herself of where she was meant to be. But only her apathy reacted, figuring that if Jeff W. had arranged it, she didn't want to be there. Everyone has their own way of relaxing, their own tips to unwind, their own passions and loves. At that moment in time, people taking a day off could be sliding deep into a hot bubble bath, to crawling back under the covers for an extra hours sleep. Gardening could be done, paintings could have been produced, and cakes were undoubtedly baked. It was all so mundane. Wanting to put her body on auto-pilot, needing to let her heart override her head, Karina Wolfenden was indulging in her newest pursuit. With salty sweat stinging her eyes, and matting her red fringe against her sheening forehead, Karina could feel her heart throw a fit inside her chest. Like a car's knackered old engine resonating with each revolution, the organ’s beats were felt throughout her entire, slender body. Adrenaline had come into play, and been spent accordingly... ... And she was only just getting started. Running hard, she blinkered her senses and just focussed in on the feeling, that slight bounce of her soles as they pushed off the dusty Parisian paving slaps. Passing afternoon joggers and leisurely paced cyclists, Karina was taking a far less pedestrian route. With the attention of the normally sedate locals following the brightly dressed young woman, Karina crossed to the neighbouring street... The entrance to which was a 100 metres or so down the sleepy, Ash-lined street. Onto the back of a bench Karina leapt, placing her foot in just the right position to balance herself. With momentum and desire ensuring her every success, the K-Wolf then sprung to the Ivy clad wall behind the bench, before hopping down into the dark grey, stony crust of the cul-de-sac's tarmac. Taking just the briefest of seconds to heave air into her baked lungs, Karina felt the humid air neutralise the lactic sting attacking her calves, before shooting off again. Immediately settling into a speedy, yet graceful pace, Karina shunned the path of least resistance... There was no fun to be had there. Tired with customers from the plethora of small, family-owned shops using their sleepy hide-away as a car park, residents had erected a row of pale stone bollards... Functional for traffic control, and even better as an obstacle. Hardly breaking stride, Karina sprung up onto the first, and still running - rather than hopping - Wolfenden used them as a walkway. Week in and week out, she'd listened to wrestlers boasting about their latest bench press, or comparing which gym had the best lifting machines. That wasn't exercise, that wasn't fitness, it was posing. It was synthetic. People were given a body, given freedom, why spend it in some lifeless gym? The birds were singing, the sun was shining, people were going about their daily business all around her. This wasn't training for Karina, this was living. Flinging herself from the final bollard, Karina hit the concrete nearly 5 metres away, blending the landing seamlessly into her stride. With beads of sweat filtering down through her eyebrows on their way to irritate her emerald eyes, Karina followed an arc as she rounded the corner... ...Only to be met with a dead end. But then again, it wasn't a if she didn't know it was there. Adding extra spring into her strides, asking much of her already roasting muscles, Karina bounded towards the angle between walls. Then, as if gravity was treated with mere contempt, and friction were ruler of physics, Karina hopped up and walked the walls. Using brief moments of contact between her heavily treaded sneakers and the aged stone bricks, Karina hopped between adjacent walls until her leather gloved hands could grasp the rough summit. "Ugh," Karina groaned as her weakening arms just managed to hoist her slender form up over the feeble barrier, before once more she dropped to the ground below... Feeling the landing cascade up her aching bones and fried muscle fibres. Finally letting her body catch up, Karina rested her hands on her thighs, leaving clammy marks where her sheened legs met thin, green trouser. With deep, satisfying breaths, Karina refuelled her respiratory system, paying off some of her oxygen debt before launching herself into highly animated action once more. Through a random assortment of locals going about their daily business, Karina followed the human slalom course, breezing within millimetres but never so much as grazing them. Feet touched down at just the right degree to launch Karina on her next bearing, total control being assumed over her often awkward seeming frame. Tall, sleek, yet riveted together by tougher-then-leather muscle and tendon. Skipping past an elderly Parisian wheeling her ancient-looking bike, Karina then took 3 extended paces towards a black, decorative lamppost before leaping at it's pole. Allowing her momentum and grip to take over, Karina span around the beacon-style lamp's support, showing awareness and control to humble the most experienced of pole-dancers... ...Before jamming a warm, gripping sole onto one of the post's ridges, pushing herself off and onto the neighbouring bench. Where normally she would have used the seat as a platform or prop, Karina instead just dropped down and slumped onto the hard, yet welcoming frame. With 2 rips of velcro, Karina removed her gloves - looking old well before their years - and dropped them beside her. Moist, steamed hand ran through her matted-down hair, sleeking it back as her breaths brought her bodily functions back into safe parameters. Then, to a breathless chuckle from the K-Wolf, a local-service bus pulled up barely 10 meters down the sleepy street, reminding her there were easier ways. Behind Karina was a row of small, local shops. The kind of businesses that were handed down from father to son, and always received with pride and honour. Produce was displayed on the same, sturdy wooden shelves they had always been displayed on, and recipes had not changed since the first loaf was baked. The words "franchise" or "chain" had no meaning... And neither should they. "What took you so long?" an olive-skinned, almost deliberately scruffy young man enquired in his native tongue. Upon hearing the voice, Karina's face lit up, and her head shot around as if her neck were boneless. Without saying a word, Karina hopped over the bench and - smiling all the way - jogged to meet the young man walking out of the grocer's. Dressed in a retro French football shirt, paired with scuffed and worn brown corduroys, the young man could have easily dressed himself at a charity shop... But somehow carried the look with an element of style, albeit one better suited to Paris' extremities as opposed to it's bustling centre. "I ran into a few obstacles," Karina beamed back with flawless gallic gusto, her pure smile enhancing her cheekbones. The young man got the joke, after all, it was he who introduced Karina to her exhausting new passion. With a tight hug that hadn't a single romantic undertone, the friends made their greeting physical, before the man took a step back. "I thought you'd appreciate this?" he winked, before producing an ice-cold bottle of Evian from his loose back-pocket. With a highly dramatic groan, emphasising her need for liquid, Karina gratefully took the bottle and made short work of the cap with a sharp twist. "The finest Champagne has nothing on this," her dreamy words praised after quenching her arid thirst. "So, what've you got planned for me?" Maybe instead of worrying where her fleeting Parisian visit would take her, Karina would have been better to think of what awaited her back in Charlotte... After all, Jeff W. is far from Ali Sibat. "That's it," Ward seethed, blood vessels becoming more prominent against the whites of his eyeballs. "Go home, you've had enough overtime as it is." Not even turning to face the workers by the promo wall, Jeffrey Ward just listened to the pair leave without any complaints... After all, they'd been paid time and a half to pretend to check their equipment for an hour. "I don't know where you are, Missy," Jeff forced through tight and thin lips. "but like it or not, you belong to RPW... And as long as this Cruiserweight Tournament lasts, you're going to belong to me." There was a sharp snap, shredding through the brooding silence, as the pencil gripped between Ward's tense fingers gave in... Collapsing under Jeff's pressure, just as he planned Karina would.
