Survival #1

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-22 The scene opens on the busy streets of Piccadilly Circus in non other than London Baby yeah! Through the mass of human life a man stands out in partial. He is wearing a pink T-shirt with the words "love and peace" on it and a pair of baggy brown cord trousers capped off with a pair of sandals. It is of course Limp. As Limp ambles down the street a man in a suit with a clip board in hand stops him. The cameraman waddles up to the them to see what is going on, as he gets there the smart looking young chap begins to speak in the usual salesman type of voice. Smart young man- Hello sir, today we are doing random surveys of people in the street for the Falling Western Sun Corporation. Would it be possible to take up 10 minutes of your time at all to partake? Limp stands there looking at the Smart young man with a big smile on his face then begins to speak. Limp- Sure thing sport. I think I can find ten minutes from somewhere. The smart young man smiles back and begins the questions. SYM- Ok sir could I take your name first? Limp- That would be Mr. Limp. SYM- Ok occupation? Limp- Oh I fight big men in trunks for gold. The man looks at him funny. SYM- Errm ok, is that some sort of sport...I'll just stick sport. Ok your next question is about relationships. Do you have a partner? Limp- Oh yes, I have had many. Currently it's with The Monte Brisas Outlaws. SYM- Outlaws....Right, is that a nickname or their real name? Limp thinks about this one. Limp- Hmm you know I don't know, that's something I should ask them. SYM- Right. So are you currently married? Limp looks baffled. Limp- Now why would I marry them, that's just silly, they just help me against the big muscular men. There's this one called Chezina, I don't think he likes me that much. He went on TV you know and said I talk too much or something like that....You think I talk to much clip board man? SYM- Ok lets just move along with this yes? Lets talk estates. Do you own, rent, house, apartment? Limp- Oh I have lots of homes. Lets see there's a flat in the docklands, I can see the river! Then I have a big pink country house in America and oh a house boat in Amsterdam. That's in the Netherlands you know. Did you know it's not actually called Holland, the Netherlands that is. Holland is old province in in the Netherlands. It's like calling the USA Texas. Man that's a scary thought. The Smart young Man seems to boiling up inside, then explodes. SYM- What the hell has that got to do with anything? So lets see you have three homes all over the world which you pay for with what? You appear to have no job, what you do sounds like you fight bums on the street for money and people go on TV to tell you you talk to much oh and yes I can see you must be rich, your clothes just speak for themselves. I have had enough of this, if I wanted to talk to winos I would work in a off license. I don't need idiots like you wasting my time! The smart young man storms off. Limp just stands there with a dream like smile on his face, looks at the camera and begins to speak with people glancing over as they hurry past to see what all the fuss is about. Limp- What a nice young man. Bit of a problem with the old anger though. You know like a lot of the guys in the UPWA, Chezina seemed a little jealous to be honest doesn't he? Saying I walk around as if I own the place, well no I didn't put any money in. I just think I'm better then you. But come on what's the problem I apologized for saying mean things about you. Let it down, let it out my friend. There's no shame in being beaten by the best, ha going on some TV show to whine about it all. I think someone needs a hug. But there will be plenty of time for that when I'm sure we will meet again. As I have a match with Team NEON and Team EPW first this week. The objective, to eliminate your opponents. Should be a match and a half, maybe even two. So I guess that leaves only the match. Let the better team win as they say, but if we say that we have already given away the ending of the match. So we won't...Well again anyway. Love and peace my friends, love and peace. Limp finishes speaking. Takes out a cigarette from a packet in his right trouser pocket and lights it. People suddenly get tired of crushing up to get out of the way of Limp and the cameraman and plough straight through them. The scene fades from the groin area of some man rushing along to replays of past UPWA matches.
