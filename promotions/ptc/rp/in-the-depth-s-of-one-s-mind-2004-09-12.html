In the depth's of one's mind...

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 (Open Scene) [As the camera fades in Zane Ozz is seen sitting on his bed at the hotel he currently resides in, due to the location of Four Winds Wrestling's Maelstrom V:Winds of War supercard. Sporting an Opeth shirt and his baggy jeans his sits on his bed, with his elbows on his knees and his hands coming together at his chin. He appears to be deep in thought. Zane's long hair covers the majority of his face. The window curtain that leads out to a small balcony is open so the city of Tokyo is seen, still busy despite the fact that it looks like it could start raining at a moments notice. What is seen of Zane's face looks troubled. He seems distant as if he is trying to remember something painfull. His eyes seem glazed over. (End Scene) (Open Scene) [Flashback-10 years ago.] [The scene reveals a younger version of Zane. Zane is currently laying down on his bed...he looks to be around 15 years old. Alice in Chains "Dirt" is blasting over a stereo syestem. Numerous posters or the various grunge bands of that time are strew along the wall. Suddenly the door opens a man of equal height and build to Zane steps in. He looks to be in his mid 30's.] Man: Excuse me Zane... [Zane not noticing the door open and the man speaking continues to listen to the song.] Man: Zane.... [Still Zane does not stir.] Man:....ZANE.... [Zane just keeps listening to the song.] Man....thats's it. [The man steps over a pile of trash and walks over to the stereo and promptly turns it off.] Zane: HEY! Can't you tell that I was listening to that. Man: I have something inportant to tell you. [Zane gives a long sigh.] Zane: I know....you're marrying mom. Man: How did you know? Zane: I saw the whole chessy routine..."You complete me..." that is by far the chessiest line I have ever heard. Man: Hey now....I'm gonna be you father. Zane: Do you really think I care...I couldn't give a flying f*ck about what you are or aren't and neither does Landen. Man: HEY! I said thats enough of that. Zane: No you didn't you asshole. Man: Well I'm saying it now....and watch your laungage. Zane: Fine then. [Zane flips off his step-father.] Man: That's it! I have tried to be nice but I am fed up with you! Landen may not talk to me but at least he seem's supportive. Zane: You think Landen is supportive....lets go ask him. [Zane starts to get up but is thrown back down by the man.] Man: You listen to me right now...after the wedding me and you are gonna have a little talk. I will be the head of this house hold and I will be the discpline. And I think you need a haircut. [The man grabs Zane's then shoulder-lenght hair and gives it a swift tug.] Zane: HEY NOW! That is it I am f*cking fed up with your bulls*it! Man: And I'm tired of your sh*t too. I mean look at you room. [The man looks around at the posters.] Man: These have gotta go. Zane: Oh f*ck no! This is my f*cking room not yours bitch! Man: I said thats enough of that! [The man backhands Zane across his mouth. Zane's head turns to the side, his eyes beginning to swell up with tears.] Man: Now lets do something about these poster shall we. [The man goes around the room ripping off several posters along the way.] Zane: That is it f*cker! [Zane jumps up on his bed and pounces on the man. The two fall in a heap of puches and swearing. Zane's fists fly at the man's face as the camera goes to black.] (End Scene) (Open Scene) [Flashback- 19 years ago.] [A very young Zane is seen sitting in a run-down house. He is currently occupied with a toy tractor. Off to his left a clerly drunken father is seen sitting in a recliner. The man is holds an acholic beverage of some kind. The man notices Zane and then calls over to him.] Father: Hey boy.... [Zane looks up.] Zane: Yeah dad? Father: What was that? [Zane's face grimaces.] Zane: sorry....sir. Father: Damn right. Now come over here. [Zane gets up from his spot and walks over to his father. Zane's face bunches up in disgust as the man smells badly.] Zane:Yes sir? Father: You wanna drink? [The father motions the acohol over to his 6 year old son. Zane: No... [The man's eyes snap open from their drunker stupor.] Father: Oh....so now your to good for me. Well maybe I should just drop you off in the woods. Or maybe I should give you to someone who you like. Is that it? Why am I not good enough for you? Zane:..... Man: SPEAK UP! [The father slaps Zane.] Father: Huh? What you wanna say something boy? Zane: No sir. [Zane rubs the red spot across his cheek.] Father: Then why do you think I am not good enough for you. Are you special? Zane: That's what mom says. Father: Oh figures...that whore is why your here. This is all your fault boy. [The father gets up and pushes the boy down.] Father: You wanna say something? Zane: No sir. Father: Thats right you f*cking problem. You f*cked my life up and now your gonna pay. Zane: What? [The father jumps upon his son as the scene goes to black.] (End Scene) (Open Scene) [The camera opens up to reveal the hotel room seen earlier. The only difference is that now outside a heavy storm blows. Lightning flashes and thunder crackles as the rain pours down. The camera moves over so that only Zane's face is seen. Zane's eyes then return to their normal state. Zane then brushes his hair out of his face and glently rubs his cheek, and then across his mouth. Zane then gets up and walks over to the window where he peers out at the rainy city. He opens the window and walks onto the balcony. He starts to talk as the rain pelts him unrelentingly.] Zane: All my life I have been held down. I have been told that I was not worthy of life at a very young age. Does anyone know what that can do to one's mind? Having someone tell you that you should have never been born at the age of 6. It's tramutazing. I was always insecure about everything about me. I have been told that I should die. But now...I relize that I do deserve life. I relize that I am better then that. I know now. I know that those whom oppress me are the ones that are wrong. I have begun to think for myself. And now insecurity is just an afterthought. I will come into this tournament both guns blazing. I will be unrelenting...just like this rain. [Zane is now commpletly drenched. His hair falls forward only to go back via a quick head snap. Zane's face is now completly revealed.] Zane: I know what I am up against. Tony Aliso...he was a champ. He was one of the best of his federations. But that is not important. I will stand tall against those who try and keep me down. I will prevail. Tony....there is a storm on your horizon...and I am not talking about this rain. [Zane looks off into the distant as the camera fades to black.] (End Scene)
