Fear The Beast Within…

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Tick…Tock… Tick…Tock… The clock of destiny ticks, the clock that decides where my future lies, the clock that determines the path I shall follow. Will I be on the road to failure, heart-ache and depression, or the lighter path to hapiness and success. Only time can tell… Only time knows… Is it my fate to be nothing but a downbeat loser, nothing but a has-been, a want to be, a never was? Is it my fate to be nothing but a dsitant memory, nothing but a glazed dream in the back of someone's mind? Fate will have an answer… Fate will determine that… Or will I be a success, leading my legacy on the golden path that is Round Two of GTT5? Can I do better than last year, where my match meant nothing, where my legacy was engulfed by the ever-growing tumour that is defeat? Can I do better, and avoid the obstacles, dodge the hazards and climb the ladder of success? That nasty tumour of defeat shall spread this year, but not to me, it shall grow across the GTT5 tournament, it shall swallow the young prince that has cut away the heart of the beast, it shall send them to the perilous underworld that is hell. Years on from now, I shall look back on a legacy, a legacy that takes me from my early childhood fear and depression through the darkness and toward the light, toward success. For a past so scarred as my own canot be reversed, it can only be hidden away, prevented from tormenting my already tormented soul. A horrible beast lives in these woods, a horrible beast that people should fear, the horrid, twisted soul of Antonio DeMarco. Torn apart by murders… Torn apart by guilt… Torn apart from years of defeat… Waiting to be repaired… Waiting to be unleashed… Fear the beast within…
