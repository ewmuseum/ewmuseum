Date: 2004-11-09 Journal Entry #743 Oct. 9th 2004 You know, I thought I was so ready for what was to be the next step in my life, the first step of many that I would need to take to finally achieve something in my life that I have strived to attain for as long as I can remember. Self Satisfaction. Don’t get me wrong, I have my beautiful wife Amber, my two wonderful children, Garion and Tanis…even my pet millipedes Goliath and Lynx, but no matter how much love I feel for them, no matter how complete my life is with them, I have never been happy with myself, and when I was young, and first got into the business I am now in, I thought Tarrant would help me take those steps to achieve that self satisfaction I so badly needed. And it did, or so it did in the beginning at least, Ehlexander Tarrant was far more successful then I had ever dreamed he would be, from Japan to the land of Kangaroo’s my name was known, my skills were world renown and my streak of hatred, malevolence and cold-heartedness was something unmatched by anyone else in the ring. It was a great time to be a wrestler, a time where skill in the ring defined when you won or lost a match, unlike now a days where writers in the back who don’t know jack about wrestling decide your fate when it comes time to decided who will get the pin fall. Politics were there, but no where near they are now, I hate what this business has become, and miss what is was in years gone past. Which is why I dropped my gimmick, and am now simply wrestling under my real name, I want fans to see what a true wrestler is, and when they see it, I want them to know who his real name is. Ehlexander Tarrant turned nothing into a giant hype machine, he had to, in today’s business you simply can’t climb the ladder of success if your aren’t. I want fans to come to a show, and I want them to see what wrestling is truly about…..and its simple as the name states, WRESTLING! No hype, no promos, no trash talking, I will bring nothing to my matches and events from this moment on except my physical body, my skill and the drive to perform to the best of my ability. Why? I know my career is nearing an end, I have been doing this for near twenty years, I’m in my early forties now, I know I haven’t much more time, and in today’s world, in today’s business and in an era where wrestlers come and go like the tides of the ocean, I want people to remember not Ehlexander Tarrant the dark God, but Chris Tarrant, the wrestler’s whose skills were unmatched by anyone whom he locked arms with in the ring, then and only then can I truly achieve self satisfaction. Was it a mistake? Only time will tell… --Chris ~~~~*****~~~~ “Why didn’t he show?” It was a question I had been asking myself for over a week now, I was set to face off against Blaise McClancy in GTT4 last week, and he never showed, no reason given, he just never showed up to the arena. All my preparation, all my training and all my time spent studying his matches was wasted, they were all for nothing… But if anything, at least it did secure my entrance into the next round, and the bracket finals against a man whom I know only a little about, Dave Harley. All I have seen of him is some cocky interviews and a few tapes of his matches, beyond that, I have only word on his skill, and the fact he has made it this far into the GTT tournament, that right there tells me how serious of a competitor I am to square off against in a matter of days. “Well….I hope he shows to the match at the very least.” Just then there was a knock at my study door. “Come in.” Turning away from the window I was greeted by my wife Amber whom was walking through the door with a slight smile upon her face. “Day dreaming again my dear?” “Day dreaming? I don’t know about that exactly, I was just thinking of how things have been going since I dropped my alter ego and have become a virtual unknown in the wrestling world.” Her only response was wrapping her arms around my upper body and embracing me in a gentle, but very loving hug. I took in the fragrance of her soft auburn hair and gently pushed her away from me so that I could look her strait in the face. “Has it come yet?” Nodding she called for out for our Son Garion to bring in the mail, something I had been checking each day in the anticipated arrival of the new PWI magazine. I did an interview with them a short while back about my gimmick drop, and how I had hoped it would be for the best, that fans would get to see whom I really was, and not who I pretended to be. They told me they would Fed Ex a copy as soon as it began its print. Garion smiled and handed me the magazine which had me on the cover, it was a nice picture actually, it had my face on it, split in half, one side was Ehlexander Tarrant, the other Chris Tarrant. It was startling to note the difference in physical appearance between the two. I shook my head clear of the thought and opened the magazine to the index, saw that my story and interview were on pare seventy three and flipped to it at once. The beginning was all about how I got started in the business, how I evolved over the years as Ehlexander Tarrant, my long stint in 360 wrestling, classic feuds and then was the interview which was pretty much word for word from what I remember it to be, however it what was what was after the interview that disturbed me. “So in conclusion, Ehlexander Tarrant, Chris Tarrant, whatever you want to call him decided that turning his back on his fans was more important then anything else at this stage in career, its my belief that fans could really care less about him wanting to be known for Chris Tarrant. Over the years people have paid to see EHLEXANDER Tarrant wrestle, paid for his EHLEXANDER’S merchandise, cheered EHLEXANDER day in and day out, and he could care less for any of it. Instead, he finds himself going through a mid life crisis and decides that he is the one who wants to be remembered, the man he started out to be, not the man whom people came to know him as. Well to you Mr. Tarrant, I have this to say. You can take your old soon to be retired ass out of the ring for good, because I don’t go to events to see an old man wrestle, I go to see Ehlexander Tarrant wrestle and destroy. Today’s business is about gimmick’s, not skill, either get over it, or get the hell out of the ring. Bottom line.” I couldn’t believe it…the words stung like a million bee stings. I dropped it on the desk and turned back to the window. Amber must have sensed something wrong for she picked up the magazine and read it. “This is complete garbage and you should know it.” Her tone was flat and angry. I let out a sigh and without turning away from the window I responded, “Are you sure? Maybe I am being selfish, it was after all the fans that made me who I am.” “Non-sense, it was YOU who made you who you were, if not for your skills, the fans would never had caught on to you what so ever, it was your skills that brought the fans to you, thus it was your skills that made you famous, and you know it Chris.” “None of this is going as I had planned Amber, not even close. If I loss the fans, I loss my matches, you know how it works. I need the fans, without them, I wont even be allowed in the ring to show them who I really am…….its all been for nothing….” “STOP IT!!” Her words cut into my like razor blades and I found myself flinching back from them as if in pain. She didn’t stop there however. “How can you say its all been for nothing?! You have bleed for what you love to do, you have shed tears for it, you have put your well being on the line time and time again, you started training Garion over a year ago to carry on the family name, and now you say its all been for nothing!? Look around you, when you first started, you had nothing more then the clothes on your back and you were living at the YMCA!! Now you have a wonderful home, a loving family, and those damned foot long bugs that your refuse to get rid of, what more do you need to show you that your life has been a success, regardless of whether people remember you or not!?” It hurt to hear her words, because I knew they were all true, and its not what I wanted to hear, not right now anyways. But I had no other choice but to listen, a screaming wife who’s only 3 feet away from you give you little choice BUT to listen to every single word they say. I turned to look at her once again and with a complete strait face I had but one thing to say to her words. “Say what you will Amber, but leave the millipedes out of it.” She seemed taken aback for a moment and stood there with a blank face, blinking a few times her features then softened and a small smile manages to creep across her ever youthful face. Slipping her arms around me again, I heard her muffled words and she spoke them into my chest. “I love you.” I hugged her against body a bit harder in response and there we stood for what seemed like hours, yet at the same time, only seemed like seconds, it was always that way when I had her in my arms, I loved this women so much, and without her, I would have surly been lost long ago. I felt her push away from me gently, and wiping a tear away from her eye she took a step away. “We have to get ready for your annual physical, you need to be there in forty-five minutes.” “Surly I can do it another time?” I said waving off her comment, I HATED going to the doctor. “Nope, sorry, you can’t compete until you get it.” “Says who?!” “Wives orders, now get your butt in gear, you know how I hate being late.” I began to sputter out a few protests, but she then arched her eye brow at me and gave me the look of “don’t bother”. Over the years I had come to know that look very well, and knew it was pointless to argue any further. -Sigh- “I’ll go get my coat.” Patting me on top of the head she smiled. “Oh that’s a good little Chris, I really should write a book on how to train your husband, I think I did an excellent job with you, don’t you think?” Not responding I walked for the door, knowing if I turned around, all I would get was laughed at, and she knew it as well, which made it all the more fun for her in the end I think. As I exited the room I heard her laugh one last time as we both made our way to the other end of the house where we then entered the garage, got in the car and began to make our way to the place I hated going the most. I had faced off against some of the scariest men in wrestling, but none were scarier then the sixty seven year old doctor with a white rubber glove on one hand that was going to tell me to bend over and cough in a matter of minutes. What was this world coming to.... -End-
