$2 Bill

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 (With his brother spotting him from behind, Desolation struggles to bench press the barbell above him. The camera doesn’t let us see the amount of weight attached to each end, but, from the vein in Des’s rouged forehead, one can plainly see it is quite heavy.) Agony: Lock it out! LOCK IT OUT! You have to go that extra mile! This is PTC Survival! (With his arms nearly extended, Des fights to lift it the last few inches. Three men come up behind Agony as he motivates his tag partner. One is short with gray hair, wearing a pinned striped suit. The other two flanking him stand over six and half feet tall, weighing around three hundred pounds. In the crime world, these two would be called “goons”. The short man taps Agony on the shoulder and the TPS member turns around, startled. Agony’s expression changes once he realizes who it is.) Agony: Vinnie Manchino… is that you?! (Agony gives Vinnie a manly hug. The weight Des is holding drops down to his chest.) Desolation: Help… me… Vinnie: Boys [snaps fingers] (The two goons grab the bar by each side and lift it off of Des’s chest, placing it back on the rack.) Agony: Vinnie! I haven’t seen you since… since… Vinnie: … since we rumbled with doze Giovanni boys, down on the pier. Five years ago, I guess. Somebody told me you were in town for some big event. Heard you were a big shot wrestler now. Agony: Yep. I’m a WWA Tag Team Champion. And, you remember my brother, don‘t ya? He’s a part of the WWA’s Survival team. (Des waves, but is too out of breath to speak.) Agony: So what brings you here, V? Vinnie: Well, I’d like to say I’m here to revive old times, but, as you can see by the muscle I brought along with me today, I’m here to do a li’l business. (Agony cocks his eyebrows curiously) Agony: What kind of business? Vinnie: Do you remember a while back when I loaned you that money? Youse two was down on your luck and I said I’d help you out. Remember? Agony: Yeah, you said someday I could repay you with a favor? Vinnie: Right. Well… now, I’m here to collect on that favor. (Agony looks over at his brother, who squints suspiciously. Agony looks back at Vinnie. Agony: Were is this going… Vinnie: I’ve got a little errand for you and yer friend here to run. A special errand. (One of the goons presents a suit case to Agony. Agony begins to open it. But, Vinnie slams it shut, nearly taking off the tag champ’s fingers.) Vinnie: Don’t open it. I just need you and your brother to deliver it to this address, and nothing more (Vinnie hands Agony a note card. Agony reads it, memorizing each word.) Agony: Well, Desolation and I are a little busy with Survival and everything, but… Vinnie: …but… Agony: …but, if this alleviates the debt we owe to you… Vinnie: Which it will. Completely. Agony: …then I see no reason why we can’t help you out. Vinnie: Good boy. (Vinnie and his goons go to leave, but Vinnie turns back towards TPS.) Vinnie: I almost forgot to give you this. (Vinnie hands Agony a green bill. Thomas Jefferson’s face is printed on the front, and a mural of the signing of the Declaration of Independence is drawn on the back.) Agony: A two dollar bill. What do I need this for? Vinnie: The cops around here… well… we sort of own them. But you’re an outsider, you see. These cops ain’t never seen you before. This will sort of let them know… you’re with us. If a cop pulls you over and asks for your license, just hand him your license and the two dollar bill at the same time, and he’ll let you go, no questions asked. (Desolation stands to look over Agony’s shoulder. They study the bill closely as the scene fades.) (The scene opens with Desolation driving his car and Agony sitting in the passenger seat, holding the suitcase.) Agony: I wonder what’s in this thing. Desolation: Maybe it’s comic books. That would be sweet. Agony: I highly doubt that. Desolation: It’s probably money. Agony: Or drugs. Desolation: Or bitches. Agony: Exactly how many bitches do you think would fit in this suitcase? Desolation: Touché. (Red and blue lights flash behind the Pain Syndicate’s car.) Agony: It’s the fuzz! Desolation: Oh crap, oh crap, crap… Agony: Just pull over and get that two dollar bill ready. (Des pulls over to the side of the road. He takes his wallet out of his pocket. Two cops exit the police cruiser behind them.) Desolation: There’s two of ‘em! Agony: I know, I know! You better give them a five dollar bill, just to be safe. (Desolation rolls down his window as the officer’s approach. One walks up to Des’s window and the other approaches the passenger side. Before the first cop can ask for his license and registration, Desolation hands him his ID with a five dollar bill folded up behind it.) Desolation: There ya go, two for each of you, plus a little extra. Cop #1: [gasps] They must be part of the five dollar bill gang. Desolation: No, we… Cop #2: And there’s probably drugs in that suitcase, I’d bet. Agony: No, I… Cop #1: Sir, please hand over the suitcase. Agony: I… I… sorry, I can’t. Cop #2: Give me that suitcase. (The second cop reaches through the passenger window and grabs the suitcase handle, but Agony won’t let go. ) Cop #2: Give it to me! Agony: No! (They continue to struggle over it.) Agony: Fine! Take it! (Agony lets go of the suitcase. It flies at the second cop and impacts with his face knocking him unconscious. This surprises Desolation, who opens his door and looks over the roof of the car to see if the second cop is okay. His door connects with the first cop’s groin.) Cop #1: Oomph! (The first cop doubles over. Desolation turns towards him to see what happened, but accidentally bumps the door again. The door strikes the first cop in the head, also knocking him unconscious. Agony and Desolation look at each other in shock.) Agony: Ummm… Desolation: Yeah… (TPS hurriedly get back into the car and speed away, leaving the two cops lying on the side of the road.)
