Airports are fun!

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 Bright florescent lights shine down in the stark, white room. Several figures garbed in airport security uniforms, hands resting uneasily on the butts of their guns in the holsters at their waists, surround the man sitting in a metal chair in the middle of the room. His hands, which are currently handcuffed behind him, are covered in a pattern of old circular burn scars. The handcuffs are the only article of clothing these security guard have allowed him. Various crisscrossing scars show through the light mat of hair covering his torso. His head is leaning forward, his brown hair barely covering the top half of his face. His eyes are downcast, staring at his bare feet. The only sounds that have been heard in this room has been the shuffling feet of the guards and the occasional cough. The silence is suddenly broken as the door opens. Everyone in the room, including the prisoner in the chair glances over, all thankful that the waiting game might now be over. The precise footsteps of the woman who enters the room are all business. Ice blues eyes behind her thick, horned rimmed glasses stare coldly at the man in the chair. Her immaculately pressed black slacks barely wisp as she strides to a table on the other side of the room. Sitting in a chair behind the table, she folds her long, elegent fingers with their short clipped nails on cold white metal surface. A quick jerk of her head brings in a couple of assistants, both wearing the black suits and sunglasses that just scream federal agent. They place a few pieces of luggage on the table before taking their places just behind the handcuffed man, who has continued to watch the pageant before him without uttering a sound.. "Mr. Kreigman, I am Agent Joan Harvest, and I'm with the Department of Homeland Security. Do you know why you've been detained?" The naked man shrugs silently in response, his eyes quietly watching her through the slight curtain of his hair. "I'm afraid that won't do, Mr. Kriegman. I want this to remain civil as long as possible. And the civil thing to do is to respond to a civil question, isn't it?" Perfectly even, almost blindingly white teeth show in a small smile as the Trashcan Man shakes his hair out of his eyes and meets the gaze of the federal agent. "The civil thing for me to do would be for me to stand up and shake your hand as you walked in. My present circumstances aren't really ideal for that. Matter of fact, I figure being yanked out of line, dragged to this room, my clothes yanked off, and being handcuffed and stuck in this cold metal chair for the past hour ain't very civil. I think you guys tossed civility out the fuckin' window a while back, what do you think?" One of the beefy agents standing behind him takes a step forward, raising a clenched fist, only to lower it and resume his position at a quick shake of Agent Harvest's head. "I can see why you'd think this is uncivil, Mr. Kriegman… May I call you Michael?" Not waiting for an affirmative response, she continues in a light, pleasant tone. "Michael, these are nervous times. The war on terror is escalating at an alarming rate. Now that North Korea's performed it's first successful nuclear test, we have to take even more stringent measures. Don't you agree?" "What's that got to do with me?" "We have to be ever vigilant against threats to this great country, Michael. And when less than a week after North Korea's first nuclear test, the son of Alan Kriegman decides to hop a plane to China, well, we have to ascertain the risk involved. And ANYTHING to do with that sick genius makes us concerned." The Trashcan Man's eyes narrow in anger as he responds in a cold, clipped tone, "Adopted. I was adopted. He is not my father, he's never been my father, he'll never be my father." His voice rises as he struggles to hold in his rage. "Hell, I helped one of your agents bring him in and have him locked up!" "Yes, that is true, you were instrumental in aiding Agent Anderson in bringing in your father. Of course, we have no idea if that was some part of his master plan or anything. You never know when you're dealing with a psychotic like Alan Kriegman… and you yourself spent some time in some mental institutions, even as recently as a few years ago." "Kriegman had me committed, dammit. I wasn't crazy, I was just in his way!" "Yes… and you were released into the custody of one Howard Porter… who is also known as one of your father's compatriots. That is, of course, before their oh so convenient parting of the ways. Oh yes, we're definately keeping Mr. Porter under surveillance as well. So far he hasn't attempted to make any contact with Mr. Kriegman… unlike you. Just over a year ago you received a letter from your father… and you sent one back shortly after that." "Yeah, I told him in no uncertain terms to fuck off and leave me the hell alone." "Quite… that was an interesting letter, Michael. All about how you were better than your father, how you had defeated your "demons" and chips in your head and all sorts of interesting statements." "Read that, didja? And after reading that, what makes you think I'd have anything to do with that sick fuck?" "Well, shortly after that you resigned your job, sold your house, your stock portfolio, and started traveling the world. Took some legwork to follow some of your tracks.. although once we caught onto your new name it made things a lot easier. "Jumpin'" Jack Flash? You were definately the social butterfly then, weren't you?" Now that was a great time, wasn't it?" Trashy heard a chuckle in his head. "Remember that senorita in Madrid? The one we danced the night away with? She had the most inventive way of licking our…" "Shut the fuck up, Jack. I don't have time for this right now." Trying to ignore the inner monologue describing the oral skills of some unnamed Spanish woman going on his head, the Trashcan Man answers the agent with a noncommittal grunt. "Is it true that you won the copyright to the song "Jumpin' Jack Flash" from Mick Jagger and Keith Richards in a card game?" Trashy sighs and shakes his head. "Not quite. I just have their promise to never sue if I use the song in any projects I'm involved in." That'll teach Mick not to try to draw on an inside straight… "Now from what we can understand, you owe the IRS a pretty big sum of cash, and you're currently unemployed. You hardly have any money in any of your accounts that we can track, and you can see why we're nervous about you leaving. How do we know you're not travelling to China to sell one of your father's inventions?" "Have you looked in my bags? What possible inventions could I be trying to sell?" "Admittedly, we didn't find anything of that nature. We did find something else though…" With that, she rises to her feet and unzips a long, flattened suitcase. She gingerly reaches inside and pulls out a steel chair. That in itself is unusual, but this particular chair is unique as it appears to be wrappted in barbed wire and has short nails hammered through it in various places. "What on earth would you need to bring this to China for?" Trashy sighs. "That's Bob." "Bob?" she asks, eyebrows raised enquiringly. "Yeah, Bob. Listen lady, I'm a professional wrestler. That there is Bob. It's part of my gimmick." "Your… gimmick?" "Yeah, wrestlers have gimmicks. Some are patriots, some are vampires, some are skateboarders, some are Cthulu worshippers. My gimmick is a crazy guy who's really good at using weapons." "And this?" She pulls out a square piece of yellow cardboard with a couple of holes cut out and a rubber band attatched. A big smile can be seen drawn under the holes. "Yeah, that's part of my gimmick too. I pretend I have another personality who's even crazier, he runs around laughing and pulling stupid pranks." Trashy hangs his head and closes his eyes for a second as high pitched laughter erupts in his head. Stupid pranks? My pranks aren't stupid, they're carefully constructed for maximum giggles. And I gotta resent being called a gimmick. Since when did you PRETEND you were me?" "You want me to tell her what we really are, Smiley? That I'm an actual pyromaniac with multiple personality disorder, and that you suffer from constant hysteria, and Jack's a… whatever the hell Jack is?" "I'm the man, that's what I am." "Shut up, Jack! Smiley, keep Jack quiet so I can try to get us out of here." The Trashcan Man opens his eyes and focuses on the blonde agent who's staring at him curiously, unaware of his mental dialogue. "Listen ma'am… You want explanations… fine. Here you go. I'm an alcoholic. I fell off the wagon over a year ago, and I fell off hard. I started drinking, partying, and I blew all my money. I've been trying to get back into the wrestling business, but my last run wasn't a great success. Sure I won a belt, but that was short lived, and my title reign wasn't anything to write home about. I'm joining this world wide wrestling tournament hoping to look good enough to get a decent signing bonus so I can pay the IRS the money I owe them and put my life back together. And that's it. No conspiracies. No inventions. No selling secrets or anything like that. I'm just a drunk that's trying to get his shit back together. Please… just let me go. Let me get on that plane to Hong Kong so I can go wrestle my match. You can contact Primetime Central in Melbourne, Australia, they're the ones that bought me the airline tickets, and they're paying for my room in China. Their number's in my address book in one of my bags there. Call them, confirm what you need to do, and let me get out of here." Agent Harvests gazes at the Trashcan Man for a silent moment, before nodding her head and reaching into his bag. "Very well, Michael, I'll call and confirm your story. While I'm gone, we just have one last search we need to do. I'll send Agent Baumgartener in here to take care of that while I'm making the call." She stalks out of the room as the Trashcan Man leans back in his chair and tries to get as comfortable as a handcuffed naked man can. "See guys, I told you I'd get out of this." Trashy sent the thought down to the other personalities riding in his skull. "Should've let me out to talk to her, Trash Man. You see how she was staring at us? I would've flashed her the pearly whites, turned on the ol' Jack charm, and covered those glasses with some ol' Jack juice, if ya know what I mean." Mr Smiley Face's laughter echoes in their mind. "She was probably wondering where our dick was. It's so cold in this room I think our outie's became an innie." "I'm just glad we're getting out of here. I never thought airport security would be such a pain in the ass…" His inner dialogue is suddenly interrupted by a woman walking into the room. Wearing much the same outfit as Agent Harvest before here, this woman filled it out a bit more impressively than the slim blonde before her. The words brick shithouse come to the Trashcan Man's mind as this wall of woman enters the room, pulling on a rubber glove. "Um… what the hell is that for?" "What do you think, Mr. Kriegman. Now stand up, walk over to the table, and bend over. This won't take long." "I wonder if she'll buy us dinner first" laughs Mr Smiley Face as the agents behind the Trashcan Man stand him up and walk him over the table. "It doesn't matter. I'll do anything, anything to be able to see my son… FADE TO BLACK
