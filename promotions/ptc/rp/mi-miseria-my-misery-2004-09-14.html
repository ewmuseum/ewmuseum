Mi Miseria [My Misery]

Date: 2004-09-14 "Hello..." ........ "Yes, you. Are you with me now? Good, because I want to talk. No no, not converse, but talk, so shut up and listen to me." "I am walking down the hall of this mansion I now live, on my way to wrestle my first round match of the fourth Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. These lights are so bright, I requested them dimmer, but who the fuck listens to a leather-clad Amazon, with burns and cuts all over her skin? Definitely not these God-fearing servants of my master…" "Yes, these wounds are real, and no, no one did them to me... Well, not all of them. I kind of liked the pain, so I did more myself... I love to revel in masochistic hedonism; I love to feel this misery that I once dreaded... Yes, yes; it grows on you..." "Yes, I said misery, Lestatt, do you know what misery is? Well, in a few short hours, when your chances of moving along in this tournament are nil, you'll have a little taste. Enough though, I mean it's obviously a bore to hear this... Perhaps if I my rack was exposed, or my legs were tanned, it'd be a bit more fun, but while I make my way, let me tell you a bit about myself, and how I came to be... You see, my name was Dawn, it's all you really need to know at the....." +++++ July 21st, 2004... “So beautiful…” he whispered into her ear before placing a soft kiss to the light-tanned skin of her neck just below. A first of his hands gently raking itself through her coal locks, he used his second to explore her well-cared for body… She would have commented in return on his own personal charisma, the light brown hair, bedroom green eyes, and the right muscles in just the right places… Oh yeah, and the Lamborghini Murcielago that every woman in the club was witness to his arrival in. She did not speak though… She merely gasped… She could not speak as a matter of fact… She was too far gone… There was a light tug at her hair, and she soon found herself on her back, two hundred pounds of desire crawling atop her body. Days of rigorous training in martial arts and the gym put a harsh limit on the kind of fun this night was bringing her… She had never tried XTC before… She had never even met this man before… That’s called 0-2... One more strike, and she’s out… ‘Dawn’ cared not, however. So many days had she been miserable, training, wearing herself out to the brink of extinction, and for what? A trophy, a busted lip, and five thousand dollars? What a prize for someone who has come so far, only to fall… *creeeeeak* The tiniest sliver of light made it’s way into the apartment from the hall outside as the door slowly creaked open. Figuring it perhaps to be her neighbor Cindy, or her ex-lover, Jack, Dawn tried to warn her partner to cover up… But as she tried to speak, it suddenly came to her realization, that she didn’t even know his name. She squirmed a bit, but barely enough to make notice; it was trouble enough to keep herself conscious. The tug at her hair was suddenly much tighter now, nearly to the point of pain… Probably was pain, were it not for the extreme altered state that told her it was pleasant. And as the door opened further, the silhouette of a man slowly stepped in, seeming to completely ignore the naked bodies upon the sofa. He stood quietly for a long moment, before brandishing a pair of gloves, which he slipped onto his hands. Slowly his head bobbed in a slight nod, before he turned slowly, and suddenly slammed the door shut! “Son of a bitch!” the naked man shouted as he freed himself from occupying Dawn’s lust to jump to his feet in alarm! As he span about to meet the intruder, he was stopped in his tracks. *clap clap* The intruder’s hands played God, and soon there was light in the rather massive, and well-decorated studio apartment. The mystery lover remained quiet as he watched this man, whose attire was reminiscent of an 80’s horror flick about a murderous game show host. Puke green and polka dots, topped off by messy, carrot-orange hair and coke-bottle glasses; what a sight to behold. “Clapping lights…” the man’s squawky voice was just a hideous as his appearance as he spoke. “I shouldn’t have interrupted you two, by the time you got in full groove, it’d be like a rave in here!” Well, he found it funny at least, following it up with a girlish titter of a laugh. Slowly shaking his head, the lover responded in a frank tone. “Jesus fucking Christ, Nybbas! I thought you were some pissed off boyfriend or something! What’s wrong with you man, slamming doors and shit when we’re supposed to be discreet!? Oh yeah, and nice timing too…” ‘Nybbas’, as we now know him simply rolled his eyes and started towards the kitchen area of the apartment. “You forget who the boss is, Isaac, I enter however I want to. And if you don’t like it, I can take you off girl’s asses and put you back in the pit, anytime…” Isaac growled, fighting till near death just to make a small crowd squeam would never compare to… Well, what’s on the sofa right now… “Well, can I at least finish, man?” he asked in a now calm tone, “I mean, look at this bitch; she‘s beautiful!” Opening the refrigerator, Nybbas chuckled before he spoke, “I told you she was undeniable, exactly why I chose her over that Fellini broad. But eh, whatever, she looked like she was having fun. You two kids do whatever, I’m gonna see what kind of cash she has laying around…” Isaac smiled, and said nothing, he only started on his way back to the sofa where the exotic Hispanic beauty laid, barely conscious, and unaware. With a carton of milk in hand, Nybbas made his way towards a small bedroom-type area of the apartment, and spoke lightly as he walked. “Don’t fuck her up like the last one Isaac, remember, we need her in good health if she‘s gonna be ready for this Golden Turnbuckle bullshit.” ++++++ “Ungh…” Dawn moaned with pain in her voice as she slowly awakened. The first thought in her mind was to bring sparring pads if she ever got in bed with this man again… The comedy of the thought was dry, however, as with each movement of her adjusting body, she wanted to cry. “What the..?” she quietly whimpered as she attempted to push her hair from her face, only to find her arm was shackled to a steel bar..Above this bed in this room that she had never seen in her life. Her head hurt, from the swollen eye to the hangover, and now fear was taking effect, only to magnify it worse. It was all coming back to her now, the opening door, the conversation between the two, and then completely forgetting it to be lost in the lust that the XTC brought. “Wakey wakey, little Flakey! Bahaha!” that horrid voice screamed out, followed by the even more horrid laugh as Nybbas swung open the door to the room and stepped in, wearing a variation, but not much less horrible version of his suit from the night before. Her fear heightening to panic, Dawn squirmed and attempted to free herself from the shackles. She glanced to one hand, noticing that it was covered in dried blood, and then released a light cry at the sight, unsure of the origin. Nybbas chuckled and sat himself on the corner of the bed before speaking. “Waking up with bloodied hands… I hate when that happens!” once again, a joke that only he found funny. Dawn stopped her squirming to stare at the speaking man; she knew she wasn’t getting free, so why bother? Go with the flow and look for the first opportunity. “What do…” she started to speak, but was cut off by Nybbas, who waved his hand and spoke amidst her words. “You can ask questions later babe, for now, I want to talk, to you. Guess I could give you the low down of what’s going on.” He smiled what might be a charming smile, had he even an ounce of charm anywhere in his soul… “My name, well, you can call me Nybbas. I’m an underground fight promoter who works mainly around the U.K. and Russia. My goal, to make violent sports everyday media. I’ve run into significant problems though. One of course being the censors. Two, which is even more complicated, is that I have yet to find a face for my promoting arts. Anyone can have a bad-ass fighter, Mike Tyson, Tito Ortiz, blah blah BLAH!” He lifted his finger and pointed to Dawn. “You…” “Me?” she questioned, following the swallow of the lump in her throat. “That was a question… Ask another question before I’m done, and I’ll have to slit your tasty little throat, okay darling?” Dawn’s eyes widened noticeably, and she simply nodded her head. Nybbas grinned in a freakish manner and continued. “As I was saying, you. You have the body of a Goddess. I caught your fight last month in Hong Kong; you fight better than so many of the fighters I promote. And you’re a girl.” She tilted her head in question, Nybbas reaching forward and gently tracing his finger in a cutting motion across her bruised neck silenced any thought of asking… “What does being a girl have to do with it you ask?” he moved himself a bit closer on the bed before continuing. “It has a lot to do with it baby, because despite the women’s rights movement, your sex is still considered weaker in physical sports. Thus it is my plan to use that to my advantage with the American media.” Nybbas gave of one of those horrifying laughs again, and leaned back on the bed. Smiling at Dawn, he spread his arms a bit and began to speak once again. “Oh, I imagine you’d like to talk now… Ask away…” Her response was quick, and desperate, almost as quick as the tears that began to pour from her eyes… “You can’t get away with this, you know! Someone will notice me missing!” “Ohhhh?” Nybbas rolled his eyes and chuckled with sarcasm before continuing, “You have to understand girl, I did my research on you. Oh yes, I am soooo sure you’ll be saved by someone… Like your dead parents, or that pathetic ex-boyfriend Jack, who only comes around when you call on him crying? Oh wait, there’s those other two friends… Steroids and cocaine! They’ll save you from this, from this…” Nybbas paused, before giggling fiendishly and slapping Dawn’s leg. “Miserable little girl, why are you even worrying? I’m doing you a favor in this. I’m going to help you not die of some stupid overdose between pathetic ass tournaments! You should be thanking me for having you raped, as well as what’s soon to come. I mean, after all, you did take out my best man’s eye last night anyways!” Unattentive to all other words for just a brief moment, Dawn glances to her blood-caked hand with widened eyes… She felt sick, instantly. “Oh God…” she quietly whimpered as a brief flashback of the night before flooded into her mind. Somewhere along all of the action, whether it be one of Isaac’s slaps, punches, or just a wearing off of the drugs, she came to life and tried to escape from the two, her first action being to put her thumb right into her lover’s eye! Her thoughts were soon interrupted by Nybbas’ horrid voice. “Oh God is exactly what I sad! It was beautiful! I knew at that moment that I made the right choice in you to be my Disciple of Violence and Misery! You’re gonna hurt so man people, and make us all very, very rich!” “Um, no… I’m not going to do this.” Dawn quietly replied, much to Nybbas’ amused dismay. “Oh, but I must argue, dear…” Nybbas responded with a smirk on his lips, “You have no choice in the matter… I’m going to go grab a bite to eat, and when I come back, let us hope your attitude has changed. Conditioning you to hate isn’t going to be a pretty sight, so you’ll need to be strong…” Dawn’s eyes widened visibly, this obviously wasn’t a party she wished to attend. As Nybbas stood to take his leave, she whimpered, and began struggling with her binds, to no avail… “Calm my dear,” he whispered, in a voice unlike his usual. “You’ll feel miserable for a while but in three months, you’ll thank me…” +++++ “And now here I stand, about to move on to where I’ll face Lestatt Knight. Will I be a success? I am unsure even, as I am untested. The last three months have been Hell for me. Misery has been my name… Beatings, torture, brainwashing…” “I love it now, though… Pain is my excitement, Misery is my lover. I want to hurt, and I want to hurt people. It appears Nybbas has won over me… I cannot, and do not want to escape… Shh, what is that?” “Ah, do you hear that music? Resurrection is its title; a rather brutal piece that Nybbas sought as fitting for the new me… A rather brutal piece, centered around a young girl‘s misery in growing up… Only to become hateful to all; a hate she eventually begins to take out on them all… It fits me perfectly… Are you ready to find out the ‘Legend of El‘, Lestatt? Too bad, because you have no choice either way…” +++++ August 11th, 2004... *CRACK!* The sound of boot hitting flesh… A most sickening thud of something possibly breaking mingled in. Followed by a hefty heave and a sharp cry… “Aww, look, the poor girl is going to cry some more!” The familiar voice of the handsome Isaac rang out through the basement cell of the unknown home of Nybbas. He towered over the naked (no doubt to teach humility) Dawn, who was balled up on the ground, covered in blood, sweat, tears, and bruises. Her coal black locks were matted to her face, and she whined as she laid in place, clutching at her ribs in obvious pain. One of Isaac’s eyes is now covered in a black patch, almost pirate in appearance… “At least you’re taking it better than you did on the first day though… Progress is good. You’ll soon come to love this…” Isaac chuckled, before reaching down and forcefully dragging Dawn up by her hair… Only to slam his fist into her jaw! Without the energy to stand, Dawn just crumpled to the floor wordlessly… From behind Isaac, walked Nybbas, once again not failing us with another horrid suit. He leaned down beside Dawn, a pleasant smirk on his lips. “Ok good, you’re still awake…” he quietly spoke after checking her out to ensure she was awake. “Perhaps a good dose of saltwater will make sure she pays attention though, aye Isaac?” Isaac grinned, and moved to take up a bucket in the corner. A foggy colored water, no doubt mixed with a heap of salt… Isaac chuckled, just before he dumped the bucket over Dawn’s cut and bruised skin! That really woke her up… She sat up, hands clutched into fist, and she cried out, unable to fight away this pain. Nybbas jumped back to a safe distance, just in case, and watched on until she finally collapsed again, whimpering and breathing heavily. “At least she’s not doing the baby cries anymore…” Nybbas chuckled as he moved forward and kneeled down before Dawn. “Congratulations Dawn, guess what today is? Today is your first day of re-education…” Nybbas and Isaac shared a silly chuckle, too many humorless jokes… Nybbas turned back to Dawn and wiped a few strands of hair from her face before he continued. “We’re going to also consider today, your new birthday… Because today, you will no longer be known as Dawn. Today, you’re name becomes… El.” He smirked, and nodded once to the prone girl. “Yes, that’s El, as in the Spanish word for, The. El was an old Spanish legend about a serial killer. It‘s one of my favorites, you want to know why? Of course you do, hehe… Because she made violence in the media, or whatever it was considered to be at the time, popular!” Nybbas emphasized his final word with an outstretch of his hands. He seemed insanely entranced now, as if this story was his life obsession, and nothing would break his telling of the story, not even Dawn, err, excuse me, El’s raspy whimpers… “From all I’ve researched in my life, she was a scorned lover. Scorned by a popular theatre actor in Madrid. It is said that one night, she came home from he parents farm, to find him bedding another woman, her own sister to be exact! She fled from the home in disbelief and rage at the discovery of the affair, and two days later, her clothes were found, torn and bloodied…” Nybbas licked over his lips and continued, “Exactly a year later from that date, they found her sister, most brutally murdered… She was found in what we would recognize as a position of bondage, and her extremities were carved out… On her forehead, an upside down pentagram was carved in… And that isn’t even the good part yet…” His following chuckle sent shivers down both Isaac and El’s spines… “Two months later during an open field performance, a war play done in dance, a mysterious figure danced onto the field… She was not dressed to fit in, covered in a black gown and a gray, featureless mask; she twirled her way across the field, wielding a small sickle in hand… A part of the play was all most figured, and she danced her way right to the woman’s former lover…” “And right there, before them all, she castrated him!” he shouted out, which caused Isaac to jump, and El to flinch… “All were in shock, no one knew what to do! And right there, she mutilated that man, and carved the very same pentagram in his head that she carved in the sister’s… And then she just danced her way back into the woods, cackling insanely! It was pretty obvious who it was… Rumors were abound that she was taken in by pagan witches after her trauma, and given the power to avenge, in exchange for her soul.” “This continued for five more years… Random assaults on actors, castration or the like of what was done to her sister. Finally she was caught though, and when they uncovered her gown, she was covered in burns and scars, and her eyes were fleshy, like that of a zombie would probably be… They burned her at the stake for heresy, and murder… And to the fear of the people, as she burned that night, she laughed, the entire time; not once did she show pain…” Nybbas slowly stood and took a few steps back. Dawn was now wide-eyed, watching on. She knew the plan he had in mind for her now. She whimpered defiantly, and tried to stand. Nybbas merely chuckled, and shook his head. “Don’t worry, child, I don’t plan on making you murder anyone in the ring, that would make my conquest a mere single match. I do, however expect you to become the most brutal, and bloodiest fighter to ever exist in a wrestling ring. I expect you to carry that insane fighter that the original El did… I won’t even care if you lose every fucking match you fight in, so long as some kid at home is watching in awe as you shed someone’s blood all over the mat…” Dawn groaned, and slumped back to the floor, burying her face in her hair and hands. She knew there was no escape; all she could do now was accept it, and hope to one day remember herself enough to escape this misery that was now her life… “And Isaac…” Nybbas quietly spoke, to which Isaac responded with a lift of his gaze. “Don’t break anything more than her spirit, you moron. She has to be healthy enough to fight soon; I’m entering her into the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament… It has quiet a following, the perfect place to unveil my new creation…” “You got it, boss.” Isaac calmly responded as he moved to stand back over El, leaving Nybbas to make his exit. +++++ “Yes, he is quite insane, isn’t he?” “It is okay though… I enjoy it now. Look out past this door that will take me to where destiny or fate one awaits me. This is what the past three months have been all about. The miserable brainwashing I’ve succumbed to. I now enjoy pain, in fact, I inflict upon myself daily… There will be nothing in that ring to make me wince in pain; especially not anything Lestatt Knight will bring to the table…” “See you soon, my friends; for more of my brutal ways when I prepare for round two…” ~fin
