No Choice

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Michael Benson’s thoughts… GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. GTT5. Benson: GTT5… It’s on its way and I will be waiting for that man, the man who I first will meet during this tournament. I have yet to hear him speak; I haven’t even had a glimpse of his face. All is well, though. When the time comes, I believe he will show his face and try to make an impact, sadly, it won’t be enough. I see all have much faith in my friend, but what is right is wrong and what you believe is never true. You all should have predicted an upset, for I will walk away victorious and an upset it shall be for this man has experience and tales to tell about his career. I have nothing, yet I still will affirm my victory because in the end, who really can overcome a true warrior like myself? GTT5, be there. The event of the year is closing in, you won’t want to miss it. But to fight…
