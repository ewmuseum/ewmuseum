An Uncertain Future.

11 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-11 Journal Entry #744 November 11th Well, here it is, the morning of what could be the defining moment in my long career, the bracket finals in the GTT tournament. This was the day that above all others in my career was the most important, for if I won tonight, it would show the world that I didn’t need a fancy gimmick, it would show people that even with my age, I still had what it took to “hang” with the new generation of wrestlers, and above all else, it would prove to myself just what I am truly capable of. I did some research on Dave Harley, looked back through his matches in the GTT so far, and I have to say he’s been quite impressive, handing victories to such people as Robb Hardy, Salaok and Dallas Reeves, all names that were pretty well known in the wrestling world, and a list of names that could easily add one more to them as well should I even make a single mistake during our match...my own. And while I should be confident of my own abilities, I have to admit I am not, sure I have made it this far, but that was riding the wave of a series of no shows. I have heard rumors flying around that there are a lot of people not happy that I made it this far, that had I faced someone that would have showed, I would have easily been beaten, that I didn’t deserve to be here, and many others before me did. These same people have even went so far to send me threats against my life, my friends and family, all I can assume is the threats are empty, tighten security around my home and carry on with what I feel needs to be done, and that’s entering the next round in the GTT. It’s been a weird week leading up to this event to say the least, amid the wave of threats against mine and my families safety, I had to go in for tests at the local memorial hospital. I hate tests and all they do is take up valuable time from my training, but my wife had insisted and she has her methods to get her own way, I found out years ago is just best to give in to what she wants instead of fighting it, it makes my life a lot easier in the end. Other then that, its been a normal week pretty much, my time training my son Garion has been decreased a bit, but he dosent need me to be there all the time, he catches on quick and I can tell he will do well in the business in the coming years. Yesterday he made the attempt to try my 720 finisher and knocked himself out landing on his head. I told him not to try it, but that’s like trying to tell a fish to jump out of a lake and swim around…its impossible. God bless him, I know some day he’ll make us all very proud, not that he hasn’t before mind you, but from a wrestling stand point, I know he will go a long way. My daughter Tanis has just started dating; Lord I am not sure if I am ready for THIS yet. It seems like it was yesterday when I was holding her in my arms and now…now she is seeing some guy named Derek. Amber assures me he is a good kid, but call it fatherly instinct, I just don’t like the fact that she’s dating; she is only 15 after all. Again my wife ensures me that that is a perfectly acceptable age to begin dating, so I can only go with what she says and trust her judgment. I suppose I should get into the gym and do some light weight training before I go to the arena tonight, I’m not sure if I am cursing myself or not by saying this, but I feel in great shape and have a feeling that today and tonight are going to go MY way. I am prepared physically, mentally and am ready for anything that could be thrown at me, so bring it on Dave Harley, bring it on world, there’s nothing you can do to stop me, there’s nothing ANYONE can do to stop me. -Chris ~~~~*****~~~~ Setting my pen down I leaned back in my chair and closed my journal. It was early in the morning, about 8:00am or so, this was my favorite time of the day because the kids were still sleeping, which meant the house was quiet. I closed my eyes to take in the silence when it was all ruined by the telephone that began to ring annoyingly to the left of me, cracking on eye open I looked down at it in annoyance. I was relived that after only a few rings a light on the phone lite up indicating that someone in the house had answered it, which relived me, I didn’t feel like talking to anyone this early. I closed my eye and let myself relax in the leather chair that was specially made for the contour of my back, it was very comfortable and after a few moments I felt myself begin to slowly drift off, or at least I was until there came a knock at my door. Opening my eyes back up I sat up in my chair, “Come in” The door opened in response, and in walked my wife, I hadn’t even known she was awake yet and had assumed it was one of the maids who had answered the phone, perhaps I was wrong. Usually the first thing I took note of when my wife entered any room that I was in was her beauty, which no matter what was almost always overwhelming to the senses, but this morning, she had something else about her, something that didn’t sit well in the pit of my stomach for some reason. “Good morning hun, is everything ok?” I looked down and she held some papers in her hand, her face still showed signs that something wasn’t right in the world, and I was now becoming genuinely worried. “Amber?” I could see the tears in her eyes as she made her way closer to the desk that I sat behind. When she go to it, she placed the small stack of papers upon it, walked around and embraced me in a hug that was surprisingly tight. I responded by doing the same as I heard her break brake into tears. “Hun what’s wrong? This should be a happy day! Today is the day of GTT, the day where I finally get to prove myself to the world, what on earth could be so bad that it would cause you to break into tears?” Her only response was more crying and I knew that I would get no where if I tried to ask her questions. What on earth could cause such a strong women to break down so easily? I kissed her on the top of the head as I looked up to the small stack of papers she had set on my desk. Was it something about those papers that was upsetting her? I tried to reach out to get them, but when you have a women clingingly tightly to you, it make sit hard to move, and it took a few moments for me to get them in my hands, but mission accomplished, I had them and brought them closer to read. Looking at them, they appeared to be a series of fax’s received about half an hour ago, which wasn’t out of the ordinary, we were receiving fax’s day and night but these appeared to be from the hospital of all places. I glanced down the paper and read what appeared to be the results of my tests I had taken earlier in the week “...felt it best to send you the results so you could read them personally, but as you can see, they all come out positive. We recommend you get second opinion, but all of our tests were conclusive, we regret to inform you that our tests show a growth in your prostate, that from all tests so far show, is indeed cancerous. When this fax goes though we will be calling you to ensure that these documents have gone through and we will explain the next step of action we feel needs to be taken now that you have been documented with Prostate Cancer. The first thing we need to do it get you in for hormonal therapy, hormonal therapy is designed to it slow the cancer's growth and reduces the size of the tumor(s). We feel that this is the first step before we take any other action until we can run more tests to see just how far you have progressed. If you have any questions please feel free to contact us or to contact your family doctor who has also been informed of your illness and a copy of the same test results that we have sent you have been sent to him...” It continued on for quite some time, but it got the point across that I needed to know, the most important point of all. I had cancer... Instantly I questioned how I could have it, I had always taken care of my body, I had always done everything to ensure that I was healthy, how and why could and did this all happen!? Next my thoughts went to the illness itself…people died from this illness, my father had become victim to it when he was in his late seventies, now it appeared I was befall the same fate... Shaking my head clear of such thoughts my next thoughts went to that of my family, and my wife who clung to me so tightly I felt as if her fingers were going to piece the skin on my shoulders. I thought of my children, would I get to see them grow up, would I see my daughter get married, would I see my sons success in the ring, would I be able to watch them grow old, have families of their own and be able to play with my grand children? And what of Amber...what would become of her should I not make it... I pushed back my own tears the best I could and hugged my wife even tighter to my body and held here there for a moment. There were so many thoughts running through my head right now, so many things, I didn’t know how to process them all. Kissing my wife one last time, I whispered to her that everything would be ok before I gently pushed her away and stood up. “Where are you going?” She said, her eyes red from crying. I looked at her for a moment before I picked up the papers on the deck and handed them to her. “Call our doctor and get the information necessary on what I need to do on my end for all of this, and then see if we should bother to get a second opinion.” “Where are you going?” she asked me a second time. “Down to the gym, I need to prepare myself for tonight.” “But you can’t wrestle!” “And why can’t I, I have obviously has this for a little while, and I did fine. I have to continue on with my every day life Amber, I have to work as long as possible, for who knows how long I will be able to continue to do so.” I know it sounded cold, but I didn’t know how to process all of this, it was entirely to much for me, and I think she knew it for she offered no more arguments but only stood there in silence. “Don’t tell the kids just yet, not until after you talk to the doctor. Once you talk with him, come down and let me know what he said, hopefully this was caught early enough…” “I love you.” She said at almost a whisper from across the room. “And I you, my love.” I walked out of the room, I felt dead inside, all emotion was draining from m body and my legs suddenly felt weak. Here I had though Dave Harley would be my toughest opponent so far…how wrong was I? My thoughts went back to the journal entry I had made earlier this morning. “I am prepared physically, mentally and am ready for anything that could be thrown at me, so bring it on Dave Harley, bring it on world, there’s nothing you can do to stop me, there’s nothing ANYONE can do to stop me.” How ironic, I had no idea God would be on that list. I entered the gym where no one but myself went, and I knew I would be alone for a little while at least until Amber made her way down here to tell me what the doctor said. Once I shut the door behind me I went over to a bench and sat down, and in moments I broke down into hysterical, uncontrollable weeping. It appeared time was closing in upon two different things now….my career AND my life...I just hoped I had the strength to keep both, for with out one, the other was pointless… I don’t know how long I sat there crying, but it must have been awhile as I now felt my wife’s arms wrap around me in an embrace, and there we sat, holding one another, crying, praying and drawing on one another’s strength. “I love you” she said softly “And I love you.” -End-
