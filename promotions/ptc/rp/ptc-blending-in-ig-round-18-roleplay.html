Blending In (IG Round 18 Roleplay)

4 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler: Cave Hulk Federation: GLOBAL It is a busy day at the local Unemployment Office in Atlanta. The large room is dimly lighted and smells like cleaning fluids after the janitors cleaned it the night before. There are long lines of people waiting in line to see if any jobs are out there or to collect their unemployment checks. Some of them are using the system as they do in all countries, but some are just trying to survive and find a new job. There are people of all races here. Latinos, African Americans, Anglo-Saxons, Asians, and even a few Native Americans but one of them stands out more than the others. He is wearing a brownish hat, a tan colored trench coat with a white tank top underneath, and white pants that are tattered and torn. The only things discernable about him are the fact that he has no shoes on, is about a foot taller than anyone else in the room, and has a big club hidden under his trench coat. This gray skinned man is the beastly creature known as the Cave Hulk and he has finally decided to try and rejoin society as a law abiding citizen. In his right hand he holds the Classifieds section of the local newspaper that he pulled off the top of a trash can. Cave Hulk realizes he's taking an awful big risk coming here. He knows that he has entered the country illegally by stowing aboard a merchant vessel that landed in the harbor about an hour earlier. When found stowing away, he worked off his ride and was given food by the crew who genuinely liked him. But now, he is here standing in a line with people gawking at him. Cave Hulk snarls as some teenage girls dressed like extras from 'Romy and Michelle's High School Reunion' take pictures of him with their Cingular cell phones. He growls at them and then shriek before backing away. Cave Hulk then turns his attention back to the classified section where he tries to find something that will fit his unique abilities. "Computer tech?" he mumbles aloud. "What the hell is that? Something that the 'Lone Gunmen' do when they move out of their parents' basement when they turn thirty-five?" The old lady standing behind him giggles when she hears this. This brings a smile to the Cave Hulk's face. Smiling is something that he hasn't been able to do ever. He starts to remember things from his past. When he was a child, the other children used to call him names and throw rocks at him. His whole life from his first day of school on, he felt ostracized and alienated by the society he wished to embrace. He knew then that he would never have a normal life. When he grew older, however, the taunting turned worse. They eventually led to a witch hunt by the religious right of the small Slovakian village which he grew up in to create a witch hunting mob captured him and exiled him to the caves of the Hills of Satan, otherwise they would torture and kill his parents. He had no choice but to do what they asked. He then lived his life in almost total isolation, growing more and more hatred toward humanity. He then began destroying mountain hamlets and villages like a modern day Genghis Khan until he was found by a wrestler on vacation named James von Doom, who brought him to America. But by then, Cave Hulk was too wild and feral to be in society so he was exiled yet again, labeled a "dangerous freak" and a "joke" by his wrestling peers for his outlandish behavior and tendency to eat referees and ring announcers at the shows he was at. It wasn't until he met his first friend, an exiled werewolf named Wolfen, that he truly gained the appreciation of what it is to be "humane." Against Wolfen's wishes and his own conscience, he decided to return to America and try and live a normal life. That is what brought him here. As Cave Hulk is daydreaming, he doesn't seem to notice that a few people waiting in line are chatting on their cell phones with the police telling them that there's a "monster standing in line at the unemployment office" and that "he looks like that guy from Sin City only uglier." Cave Hulk then snaps back to reality and shakes his head. "I must not be held back by the past," he says. "I must live for the future and that alone." Cave Hulk then returns to reading the classifieds. He sees some more job listings. "Exotic dancer?" he asks quixotically. "What the hell is that?" He then takes a closer look. "Females only," he says kind of mystified. "What kind of society is this when gender bias still exists when it comes to the local job market? What next? Will I not be able to be a lawyer because I don't look like an asshole nor will I not be allowed to be an actor because I don't follow L. Ron Hubbard's cult of Thetans?" Suddenly, the police burst in with guns pointed at the Cave Hulk. Everyone gets on the ground or runs out the door. The Cave Hulk doesn't notice but instead continues to read the classifieds. "FREEZE!" one of the officers yells. Cave Hulk looks around and notices the police officers around him. "Hello there," he says with a sarcastic little smirk. "How can I help you fine gentlemen on this humid summer day?" "Halt Cave Hulk!" another officer says. "You have the right to remain silent. You have the right to an attorney. Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law. Now, get on the ground and this can all be over with peacefully. Cave Hulk looks perplexed. "Okay," he says with a smile, folding up the classified section as he speaks. "What did I supposedly do this time?" Another officer says "You are wanted for crimes against humanity you vile beast! You are wanted for everything from cannibalism to bestiality you sick bastard! Now get on the ground now or we will take you down by force!" Cave Hulk cannot believe this mindless tripe. "Why do you treat me different than the rest of the human beings?" the Cave Hulk asks the police as they slowly surround him. "Why must I be ostracized from society just for being different? Why must you treat me like a terrorist when I'm just a normal, law abiding citizen like everyone else? Well, I'll tell you why. Because you are merely feeding off of unrealistic propaganda put forth by your president that makes anything remotely different than the norm seem like a threat or Unchristian." One of the police officers replies "This is not about God. This is about justice. You are wanting for being a menace to society. You are wanted for the beating and assault of a citizen here in Atlanta. So what I need for you to do is put your hands up, get on your knees, and lie down on the ground with your face down so we can put the cuffs on your hands and take you to jail." "But I am not a criminal," Cave Hulk replies. "I am nothing more than something you fear. Why must everything you people not know anything about be referred to as a thing? You called me a thing earlier. I am a person. My name is Cave Hulk and I demand to be treated like a normal person. I'm not just some barbarian Neanderthal from the Pleistocene Epoch. I am a dual citizen of the United States and of Slovakia. I am not a terrorist. I am not a criminal. I have been falsely accused by your government because they wish to bring witch hunting back from the Dark Ages. My pagan beliefs make me a target of right wing political propaganda." "This is not about politics Mr. Hulk," another officer chimes in. "This is about you committing crimes in our jurisdiction and you having a date with a judge to talk about these crimes. Now, what we would like for you to do is calmly and quietly get on the ground, put your hands behind your head, and let us arrest you. It's as simple as that. We will use force if deemed necessary." Cave Hulk scoffs at such nonsense. He knows he can take these guys down without much trouble, but that would be defeating the purpose of his visit to the unemployment office. He doesn't want to isolate himself anymore from the rest of humanity. He wants to embrace the natural human qualities that reside in his heart. But these people take him for a freak. They look at him like he is a common criminal. Now, he is just another statistic to be added to the number of citizens in jails in the United States. "But why treat me like this?" he replies with a snarl. "Why treat me differently just because you view me to be a freak...a monster...a creature with no feelings. But yet I have feelings. I'm just like you, but yet you treat me like a monster...but you're really the monsters." He starts to have a revelation. "You are the ones who fear anything that you cannot control. You fear the freaks, the degenerates, the deformed, the rebels, and anyone who doesn't match up to your societies norms. You've killed off numerous indigenous tribes. You've killed millions of innocent civilians, you participated in the willful killing of Native Americans by giving disease ridden blankets, and yet you call me the monster? Look in the mirror Americans. You are the monsters of your reality, not me. But yet you treat me as such. You don't see me and the Hulk family fucking each other over in order to make an extra buck. You don't see us cheating on our wives and husbands with the next door neighbor or our spouse's best friend. We're not the freaks. You're lunatics. You're living in a world that revolves around fear. You fear anything and everything. Your paranoia is a weakness and will lead to your society's eventual demise. Nothing can stop the downward spiral...not even me." "What is he rambling about?" one officer asks. "What kind of criminal is this?" another ponders aloud. "I am not a criminal," the Cave Hulk politely replies. One of the officers scoffs at the remark and quickly dismisses it as the mindless jargon of a mad beast sent to this Earth from the fiery depths of Hell. The officer quickly states "But you are a criminal who disobeyed the laws in these United States. First, you entered this country illegally from Slovakia without gaining a Green Card or having a passport. Second, you have numerous criminal battery and murder charges against you in more than five states. And thirdly, you are on the terrorist watch list. You are a beast, an ungodly creature that must be sent to where he belongs. A maximum security prison for the criminally insane, such as yourself." 'Criminally insane?' Cave Hulk ponders in his mind. How to respond to such an accusation by one such as him. "Why must you fear what you do not understand? You Anglo-Saxons are all alike. Whenever you see something that is not normal, you freak or decide that it is a ghastly, ungodly creature that must be burned like the witches of Salem. Am I not like any other immigrant coming to your country for a better life? NO. I'm a freak in your eyes. I am nothing but just another thing that must be destroyed because you cannot control it. That is the power that I possess over you Christians. You believe me to be a Bastian of Satan's Hell." Cave Hulk laughs at the stupidity of it all. "This is what I get for coming to the Bible Belt. I am more educated than you well know. I am not from Dante's Sixth Level of Hell as described in the Inferno. I am not from a place where heretics are placed in flaming tombs forever and ever. I come from the Slovakian mountains, in a place called the Hills of Satan. Watch Hostel, you might see some of the nearby villages. But alas, I do not practice torture of any kind unlike your fascist government. All you do is bully the world around with your so-called message of peace. Why should I listen to you when you don't listen to me?" One of the police officers, a real cocky SOB wearing shiny, silvery lensed sunglasses and has a Southern accent, interrupts with his jargon. "Mister," he says in a harsh tone. "I don't care where the hell you come from but this is the United States of America, home to good Christian people like myself. Now I wouldn't want any of these God believing people to see us have to hurt you. Now please, put your hands on your head and lie down on the ground now." Cave Hulk now knows that he cannot reason with those who cannot be reasoned with. With his highly intellectual logic based mind, he realizes that he cannot communicate effectively with the dim-witted police officers he is speaking to at this very moment. He sighs and then realizes what he must do. "I see there is no reasoning with you grunts of the corrupt government," he says with a gloomy sense of hopelessness in his voice. His hand grips the club in his hand. "So therefore, I see no reason in speaking to you anymore. The only way I can convey my thoughts to you now is in my actions. For I am Yum Cimil, ruler of Xibalba and my actions will speak for me since you won't listen to the reasoning of a sane man." Cave Hulk then starts swinging his club wildly at the police officers. He knocks two of them into the far wall to the right with the first swing. He then turns to his right and grabs an officer before he can fire his weapon. He then hoists him up into a Gorilla Press and then throws him into the other officers to his left before running for the door. Once outside, he sees more police officers running his way. Cave Hulk has to think fast. He sees a red VW Beetle parked in front of the office. He quickly runs over and picks it up with ease. He then throws it at the officers running his way, causing them to scatter like ants under a magnifying glass. Cave Hulk quickly vanishes into the bushes and into the alleys of nearby buildings before they even realize what's going on. Later in the day, the Cave Hulk is walking through a deserted alley. He kicks a rock into a puddle in the middle of the alley. He has his hands in his pockets and is looking at the ground, almost seeming depressed. He then notices a comic book lying on the ground and looks at it like it's a puzzle, a conundrum of some sort. He picks it up off the ground and has a revelation. At this moment, he realizes that humanity is too sick to survive and he must do something to help his cause. He smiles as he looks at the cover of the Incredible Hulk number 94 but more importantly what it says on the top of the cover. "Planet Hulk," he whispers to himself before he walks down to the end of the alley and turns the corner, blending in with a horde of people heading to the baseball stadium as the scene fades out. Cut.
