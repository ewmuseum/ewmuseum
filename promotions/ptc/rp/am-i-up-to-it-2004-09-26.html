Am I up to it

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Last night was an amazing night for WMW superstar Fury, as he went into a match with one of his biggest rivals, Fury faced off against Matrix at Darwin's Revenge 2 in an anywhere falls match. Fury and Trix would do battle going back and forth giving blow after blow. They both lost big amounts of blood they both put their bodies on the line and only one came out with the victory. Fury got the pin fall after his manager Tasha got her revenge on Matrix by knocking him out with a bat. Fury has captured the WMW Midwest title for the 3rd time but things are only going to get harder from here when he gets a nights rest then has to go on to where the GTT4 event is held and face one of his biggest challenging to date. Fury made it past the first round with ease but with new more talented opponent things are going to be much harder. (The scene opens to a shot of a door being opened, in walks Fury's manager Tasha in her hand is her brief case and on her shoulder is Fury's WMW Tag Team title. she sets the bag down and shortly behind her follows Fury he has a big grin on his face and the WMW Midwest title around his waist. He gets in and drops his bag on the floor then unstraps the Midwest title he looks at it for a few moments before draping it over his shoulder. He walks over to the couch and slumps down in it, placing the Midwest title in his lap.) What a long day it was. Last night had to be one of the toughest battles I have had in a long time. Matrix and I went everywhere. Outside the ring to the inside of the ring, We both gave blood for this title and I was the one that walked out with it around my waist all thanks to that beauty over there. (Fury stares at Tasha as she makes her way to the bedroom, he smiles and then looks at the Midwest title.) Going into that match I didn't really know what to expect. I was thinking Matrix would have played dirty that he would have let the powers that be come out and stomp me alittle bit was thinking he would have them go after Tasha but none of that happened. Trix played it right down the middle but me on the other hand I played dirty. (Fury frowns for a moment but he looks at the Midwest title and a smile comes across his face.) I brought Tasha to ringside with me so that she could be there to help me. I brought Tasha to ringside so I could prove to myself that I could have someone I cared about at ringside and not worry about what is happening to them or what is going to happen to them. And I proved that. Shit and Tasha proved to me that she is a big girl that she can handle herself when she helped me win this baby. She took a bat to Matrix's head and I took advantage. Am I upset with the way I won the title? Not really I said from the beginning that there was a new Fury in town a Fury that will do whatever it takes to win and I did exactly that. (Fury rubs the Midwest title, he then stands up draping it over his shoulder, and he walks to the room Tasha went into, he turns the corner into the room and there she is sitting on the bed watching t.v Fury just stands at the doorway watching her.) No matter how much I want to rest, no matter how much I want to just sit back and relax, there is no way for me to do that when I have my match for GTT4 coming up. One tough battle after another, can a brother ever get a break? (Fury walks in the room finally and sits down next to Tasha, she smiles and gives him a kiss on the cheek and she smiles and kisses her back.) Tasha: What’s on your mind champ? While looking in her eyes the only thing I can think about to say is. What isn't on my mind I am the Midwest champion and I have to worry about GTT4 to be completely honest I'm fuckin drained I have no energy right now and the match is drawing closer and closer by the minute. Fury: I’m just alittle tired that's all. Tasha: Really I would think you would be all full of energy after winning the Midwest title last night, I mean you made history being the only wrestler in Wrestling Midwest history to hold the title 3x that’s nothing short of amazing. Everything she is saying is true but I am so tired, so drained I don't know how I am going to be able to compete for my match. Fury: Yeah it is a very special time in my career but I'm so damn tired, maybe its the stitches I had to get cause of the match at Darwin's. I don't know whatever it is needs to go away cause I have to face off against one hell of an opponent in the next round of GTT4 this week. (Tasha puts her head on Fury's shoulder and smiles at him.) Tasha: Don’t worry about it baby. But whom are you facing anyway? The little I know about the guy is he is nothing short of an amazing talent. Hell I looked at the polls and the odds are against me, once again little ol Fury is the underdog and that’s just going to fuel me even more. Fury: Some guy by the name of Angelo Deville, from what I know about him he is pretty damn good. (Tasha shrugs) Tasha: Well so are you, So what are you worried about? Fury: Nothing but the simple fact that I don't know if I am going to be able to give him the fight that he deserves or put on the match that everyone deserves to see. Tash my head is killing me from that match with Matrix and I think I tweaked my damn knee again. I am nowhere near 100%. I just went through one hell of a fuckin battle and I feel like shit. Tasha: Well you look like shit too but that doesn't mean anything, Your just going to have to bite the bullet on this one babe. Winning the Midwest title was good and all but winning this tournament could get you even more eyes on you. This could make you a household name in PTC. Dee you might not feel up to it but I know you can win this match hell I have faith in you that you can win this whole damn tournament. (Fury nods) Fury: Yeah maybe your right, I'll do my best but I'm not promising anything right now my main focus is holding on to those titles I have over there. Tasha: I understand. Fury: I’m going to go watch some t.v. For a bit I'll be back in a bit. (Tasha nods as Fury gives her a kiss he walks out in to the living room turns on the t.v. he flicks the channels until he comes to The Batman, Yes Fury watches cartoons he leans back and watches.) I think that's why I feel in love with Tasha she knows how to keep me grounded, she knows that no matter how I am feeling I can do whatever I have to do. GTT4 is big and I am surprised I made it this far, this is the first time I have made it to the second round in a PTC tournament and I will admit it feels good to know that I had what it takes to make it to the next round. To move up one rung closer to getting and reaching a goal I set for myself. (Fury takes his eyes off the t.v. and looks at the camera.) I face Angelo Deville a man that is yet to be defeated in any tournament a man that has some amazing skill. I am not afraid to admit that Angelo you are good hell maybe great even but when you look at it, when you break it down I am great as well I have everything it takes to be the first one to knock you out of a tournament. I have everything it takes to knock you out of this tournament. (Fury clears his throat.) I will admit I am alittle sore, bruised up cause of the battle that I had last night but I am going to come into this match with all the intentions of winning I am coming into this match with the will and desire to win. Deville vs. Fury in the second round who will come out on top? I guess we will just have to wait til we meet in the ring. In other words see you in the ring. (Fury turns his attention to the t.v and the scene) FADES TO BLACK
