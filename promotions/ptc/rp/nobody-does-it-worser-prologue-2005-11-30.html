Nobody Does It Worser - Prologue

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 OHMCS HEADQUARTERS - N'S OFFICE ONE YEAR AGO THAT FATEFUL MORNING "Well, Double-Oh-Nothing, I hope you’re satisfied. You’ve been eliminated from GTT4 and have completely blown any chance we had of discovering what the Americans were hiding from us about the Big Shiny Sight Receptacle." With the rising sun at her back, the grandmotherly figure of N was cast in shadow. (Well, grandmotherly without the kindness. So mother-in-law-ly.) It was just as well, seeing how the mood of the OHMCS director was quite foul. Jimmy Bong was sure that he didn’t want to see her face at this point in time. Clad in his trademark tuxedo, he sat nervously in a small chair at the foot of her desk as she stood a few feet away and continued to berate him. "I suppose you’re happy with yourself? You recklessly assaulted an agent of the CIA, you allowed one of our Russian allies to be kidnapped, and caused extensive damage to that casino resort in Atlantic City…" "That wasn’t my fault!" Bong started. "It was Garfeld! He planted a bomb and tried to kill myself and Majorly Shafted!" "SILENCE!" N hissed, seething with ire. "You’re just lucky Shafted was there to get you out of that situation alive!" Bong opened his mouth to protest, but there was nothing to say. N was right. After all, when one works side-by-side with a man that was genetically engineered to be an indestructible human shield, one generally has safe protection against almost all forms of projectile weapons and explosives. It was indeed due to the presence of Majorly Shafted that Bong was able to shield himself from the explosion that most certainly would have killed him. But while the explosion was not fatal to him personally, it put the nail in the coffin of his investigation—he sustained a few injuries that undoubtedly contributed to his ensuing loss in the GTT4 bracket final against Craig Goat. "On top of that, your failure could have jeopardized the entire world!" N went on. "And it’s very, very lucky for you that things just happened to work out in the end!" "Indeed," Bong mused. "Who would ever have guessed that Garfeld would end up conveniently dying of a sudden heart attack? Why, the odds of that must be next to impossible!" "Oddly enough, so are the odds of you keeping your job," N replied. Bong blinked. "Wh… what’s that supposed to mean?" A wide smile crept over N’s face. She had obviously been waiting for this day for a very long time. "Double-Oh-Nothing," she said slowly, taking pleasure in every letter, "you… are… FIRED!" Bong’s mouth was agape with shock. "Fired!?" he stammered. "But why!? I mean, just because I failed at saving the world… and, well… everything else I’ve ever done in life…" "Exactly," said N. "You’re a failure, Bong. A complete failure. Why I’ve kept you on staff all these years, I’ll never know. Hiring you was the greatest mistake the British government has ever made." "Greater than naming its secret service ‘On Her Majesty’s Catering Service?’" Bong asked in reply. "Shut up, Double-Oh-Nothing!" N spat. "Oh, I beg your pardon… I meant to say, ‘Shut up, Mister Bong.’ Because you are hereby stripped of your status as a Double-Oh-Thing! You have until the end of the day to clean out your office! You must also go down to R&D and relinquish your pistol. I daresay that U will also be pleased about your dismissal." "Who?" Bong asked. "U," said N. "And if you ask whether or not I’m actually talking about yourself, I swear I’ll have you assassinated," she added quickly as Bong went to open his mouth. "Oh," said Bong. "Well, I wasn’t going to ask that. I was just curious because I thought U had been sent to prison for treason." "For your information, it turns out he’d been framed," N said tonelessly. "But we were unable to find out by whom." "Well, that’s good I suppose," said Bong. "Yes," replied N. "Now get the hell out of my office. I never want to see your face in here again!" Bong stared dumbfounded at his now-former superior. Was this really happening? Were his days with OHMCS actually coming to an end? He couldn’t believe it! What in the world was he going to do now? He was no good at anything! What place was there in the world for a man like Jimmy Bong? "What I ask you!?" he cried. "What are you going on about!?" N demanded. "Oh, nothing," said Bong after a moment. "Good. Now LEAVE!" And so, Jimmy Bong stood up from his chair. He adjusted his black bow tie, dusted off his sleeves, and raised his chin with dignity as he turned around to walk out of the office. Then he tripped and fell on his face. N just sighed. "What an idiot. I sure hope the next Double-Oh-Nothing turns out far better than this one… of course, I’d take a bloody turtle over this lousy jackanapes." "I’m still here, you know!" Bong cried indignantly. But a moment later, he wasn’t. He picked himself up, limped out the door, and exited OHMCS headquarters for the last time. He had no job. He had no skills. There was nowhere else for him to go. He had nothing. For one year, he had absolutely nothing. Well, he had a healthy wardrobe of tuxedos, a decent flat in London, and a reasonable amount of severance pay to last him for a few months. Other than that he had nothing. Then one day, not too long ago, everything changed… …and that’s where the next chapter of Jimmy Bong’s story begins. Stay tuned Episode One of… NOBODY DOES IT WORSER.
