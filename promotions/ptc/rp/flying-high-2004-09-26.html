Flying high

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 “Are you sure that this is what you want to do?” Rick James looked earnestly into the apprehensive face of Jay Phoenix as they stood almost nose-to-nose. Both men are dressed warmly with their jackets buttoned to the top, Phoenix himself also has a black scarf wrapped around his throat. As they both breathe air is visibly expelled from their mouths in ghostly clouds. “Of course I am Rick, I have come too far to turn back now” Phoenix gulped, his voice anxious. “OK then let’s get started” Rick said with a smile as he suddenly almost floated away from Phoenix, frictionless. Watching his friend move away Phoenix wobbled slightly as he attempted to keep his balance. Gliding backwards on the thin bladed skates at his feet, Rick moved across the ice-rink with the grace of a professional skater leaving Phoenix to watch in awe. Turning on his heel Rick almost seemed to forget himself, to forget his surroundings, forget everything as he became lost in the moment. A fluid pirouette spun Rick full circle once, twice then three times before he launched himself into the air, as if flying on unseen wings. Landing on one foot he spun full circle once more before skating easily back to Phoenix and coming to a stop beside his friend, breathing slight heavier. “That was awesome Rick, truly amazing my friend” Phoenix stated breathlessly as if he had held his breath throughout his friends performance. “Nothing all that special Jay, and nothing compared to what I was able to do before I hurt my knee of course” Rick said self-depreciatingly with a shrug of his shoulders. “What do you mean nothing special Rick, it looked like you were flying out there!” Phoenix pointed out as he tried to make his friend believe in himself. “Well maybe but as we both know Jay you can only fly so far, or so high, before you fall again.” Rick said with a small half smile as he bent down and rubbed at his right knee. Four years previously Rick had been on top of the World. On top of his own private World anyway. A successful career as a journalist had brought him the wealth and prestige that always followed such things; Armani clothing, fast cars, parties every night with new friends that never mind the names he didn’t even know the faces, all this and more had been his. Always shay and insecure, Rick had handled his newfound wealth and popularity badly. Used to a small community with only one real, trusted friend the influx of people now claiming that title, one that he KNEW had to be earned or granted, had unbalanced him. While the one true friend in his life was busy touring the World with the wrestling federation known as the PWW as their figurehead, the World Champion, Rick found himself being drawn deeper and deeper into a World that he not only didn’t recognise but one that his innocence had left him ill-equipped to deal with. His new friends of course were not the sorts to tell him to slow down. They were not the sorts to tell him to watch his step. They were not the sorts to stop him from getting into his car after telling him that he should have ‘just’ one more for the road. … and they were most certainly not the sorts to drop everything, to risk financial penalties if not losing their job entirely, and fly halfway around the World to be by his bedside when he woke up after wrapping his car around a tree. That last one for the road being nearly his last one … ever. The doctors had told Jay … who had told them one of the few lies ever spoken by him when he said that yes, of course, he was a relative of Rick’s; brother by adoption was the ‘white’ lie that had slipped so easily from his tongue … that it was still touch and go. It had taken the emergency crew nearly three hours to cut Rick out of his car, so severe had been the accident and when they had reached the hospital it had taken another two hours of surgery to stop the bleeding internally. The next twenty-four hours would be crucial; if he made it through that then there was a chance. … a chance only. The nurses who checked Rick’s vitals every thirty minutes during that first twenty-four hours came to grow accustomed to Jay’s presence at Rick’s bedside. They didn’t ask him to leave as they made their checks, tell him that he would be better off going home and getting some sleep, or try to make him let go of his friend’s hand. At least not after the first attempt. The nurse, who must have been at least twenty years Jay’s senior, had gently but firmly told Jay that he was in the way of her doing her job; on top of that she had pointed out that in his state Rick probably didn’t even know that Jay was there anyway so he should go home and let them do their job. While Jay never once threatened the nurse, never once raised his voice, something that he said … never releasing Rick’s hand from his own, never taking his eyes of the still, grey face … convinced the nurse that he job could in fact be done around Phoenix. … and it was. Throughout the first night Phoenix sat by his friends beside, his only company the electronic hum of the machinery that breathed for Rick … the machinery that kept his heart beating and his blood flowing. In the midst of all of this modern paraphernalia Phoenix withdrew to the only place that he could go to; the teachings and beliefs of his childhood coming back to him now in his time of need. In Rick’s time of need. Calling upon his Ancestors he begged them to intercede with the Spirits themselves on behalf of his friend. Using the medicine … so different from that practised by the men and women in the hospital but yet they both shared the same name, and had the same goal of healing people … taught to him by his grandfather, the Shaman, Phoenix did the only thing that he could do. He prayed. Eventually, as the Sun rose and sent it’s light through the closed curtains to illuminate the room in shades other than the neon green of the monitors … as the tears had long dried on his face … as his voice had broken to nothing more than a pale shadow of it’s former timbre … Phoenix felt the miracle that he had been waiting for. Rick’s hand, so tightly grasped for so many hours … a duration that had felt like nothing less than a whole lifetime to Phoenix … twitched slightly, and then with more force. Watching in awe Phoenix’s eyes widened as Rick’s fingers slowly curled around his own to grasp his own hand tightly, to hold onto him as tightly as Phoenix had held on to him throughout that long, lonely night. The doctor’s faces told the tale that their words had not. They had said that Rick had a chance to make it though if he could simply hold on for twenty four hours … but their expressions of shock at seeing the improvement in Rick belied that. It was clear then, Phoenix knew that they had expected Rick to fail … to fall … to die. Something hadn’t allowed that though. It could have been Rick’s one desire to live. It could have been the intervention of the Ancestors and the Spirits. … or it could have been Phoenix himself standing alone, frightened yet resolute on the bridge that he knew all had to cross on the journey between life and death. A journey that he knew Rick was not ready to make … a journey that HE was not ready for Rick to make. Whatever it was, for whatever miraculous reason, Rick James survived the horrific car crash that should have killed him. Three months in hospital and two operations on a knee that would never quite function as well again and he was deemed fit and well enough to go home. “Are you ok Jay, you look like you have seen a ghost?” Rick asked as he reached out and gently touched Phoenix’s arm. Shaking his head to clear the memories that had engulfed him Phoenix smiled at this friend. “I am fine Rick, not seeing ghosts … just remembering a different time and a promise made” Jay said gently as he wrapped his arms around his body as if warding off a chill. “What promise was that?” Rick asked, his eye sparkling with curiosity. “Well let’s just say that I promised something for something … a balance if you like … but that doesn’t matter right now my friend. Today YOU promised me that you would teach me how to skate, and may the Ancestors doom me but I am going to try my best to do this forsaken thing that you call a sport!” Phoenix retorted with a grin, turning away before Rick could see that his humour didn’t quite reach his eyes, that a shadow of something alien on Phoenix’s face was there. Fear. “I don’t see why you are worried Jay, I mean you make your living flying from ring post to ring post, putting your body on the line each and every time that you step foot in the ring so why should a little ice bother you?” grinned Rick. “ … because at least there I KNOW what I am doing Rick, I KNOW what to expect!” Phoenix replied through gritted teeth as he fought to keep his balance. “Even when your opponent is someone that is as vague and unknown as this Tex guy Jay?” asked Rick. Reaching out both hands, Phoenix grabbed hold of Rick’s own outstretched one and for a fleeting second everything was again as it was four years ago yet this time it was Rick holding Phoenix up … it was Rick in control and Phoenix falling. Their eyes met for the briefest of seconds and then the moment was gone again as Phoenix’s eyes widened in shock as he feet slipped out from under him. As Rick stood laughing Phoenix went from vertical to horizontal in less than three seconds and with an out rush of air his back slammed into the ice. Standing over him, reaching down as he again offered his hands to his friend, Rick smiled. “Maybe you SHOULD stick to wrestling Jay” he pointed out as he fought to keep his face under control, his laugh swallowed. “Yeah, well at least there I KNOW that someone is gunning for me, I KNOW that people are going to be trying to hurt me and beat me. In the ring, and especially in the GTT the whole idea is to fly as high as you can and NOT fall down afterwards Rick … and unlike before I don’t intend to get nearly high enough … I don’t intend to fall right at the end” Phoenix said slowly as he gathered his breath, lying flat out on the ice like a living snow angel. “Tex may be so confident that he doesn’t see the need to prepare, to train, to even speak to anyone about the tournament … but I have been here before Rick … I have fallen before. He may think that he has what it takes to bring me down, to break my wings and watch as I descend out of sight … but I doubt it. Vampir burned me a few years ago, he burned me like the Sun did to Icarus himself.” he continued as he reached out and grabbed Rick’s hands, hauling himself to his feet and regaining both his breath and his balance. “ … but not this time Rick, not this time. Tex is not going to stop me, and friend or not neither is Dave Hurst if I have to face him next. I don’t intend to fly to close to the Sun you know, I don’t intend to repeat the mistakes of the past. This time I am going to be like Daedolous, I am going to be a little more cautious and take every step one at a time” Staring into the face of his friend Rick said nothing, a little shocked by the intensity in the eyes of the man who had been part of his life for as long as he could remember. “ … and what then Jay?” he asked quietly. The familiar grin crept across Phoenix’s face as he looked out around the deserted ice-rink grateful that fame and money brought some perks to his life, one such being able to book a whole ice-rink when he wanted too. Reaching behind him and rubbing at his sore back, wincing as his hand encountered an area that was sure to be a bruise come morning, he suddenly laughed as he turned to look again at Rick. “What then? It is simple my friend … the Phoenix is going to fly … I am going to KEEP flying until I can go no higher, until I reach the pinnacle itself” he said exuberantly as he winked at his friend “I am going to win the GTT” Pushing away from his friend Phoenix glided across the ice, his body barely moving as he allowed his momentum to propel him effortlessly forwards with his hair streaming a blaze of red, yellow and brown behind him.
