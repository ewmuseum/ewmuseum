A rush of emotions.

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 It’s almost time. The UWF’s Pay Per View is almost done. We know who is the top man. Tsunami Oni has fallen, a former UWF World Champion and Hall of Famer has fallen. And you can also count on the fact that a good 60 other men have fallen also. They’ve fallen to a force that no one else could understand. A man who is a former champion, a current champion and the one that everything feels the need to mention. It’s also almost come to a head though. For now it’s time to concentrate on another accolade. Another great achievement. Becoming the first ever two-time PrimeTime Central Extreme Champion. First ever. Do you understand how much that can drive a man? Sure everyone of you have said that Sean McClancy is something of a fluke. But the fact of the matter stands. He’s one of the best. He’s always been considered one of the greats. And now, that match is coming up. 6 men to climb into the ring, and one will leave with the now vacant Extreme Title. Sean has been here before. He’s been there many times. 28 years old this coming October. And he’s already been a 4 time World Champion and is looking strongly at a 5 time holding one. What is the problem of these other men when they sit there and try to claim that someone is now washed up when they’ve held a title once? Why is it that some of these competitors out there, some prime examples being Shaman, Vendetti and Kushinaga, why is it that they feel they are the ‘shit’ when it comes down to it? Shaman has a history, you’d figure he’d know better. Vendetti is a rookie from the looks of it, eventually the School of Hard Knocks will have its way with him. Eventually he’ll crack under the pressure and he’ll be gone from the sport. And Kyo, Kyo, that is a man who has a date with destiny coming up soon. And that date means he’ll meet a man he doesn’t know and get his ass handed to him for what he’s done to a woman. This is a sport. You don’t take things personal. And that’s something that Kyo Kushinaga did. He took his fight with Patti Anderson in the 4WW personal, and that means he got Sean involved. That’s a dangerous place to be. The “Celtic Knight” Bres is a dangerous man to deal with, there are many you can ask. Some of them you have to go find where they went off to after they realized they had no career. This isn’t a fallacy. It’s not a made up story such as Shaman’s heritage or Vendetti’s ‘family’ contacts. This isn’t Grizzly Adams, or the Sopranos. This is wrestling. We could go into full details here as to the history of Sean McClancy. From growing up in the Seelie Courts of Tir Na Nog, a place that others call Ireland. We could talk about hos his blood line goes back through generations of both Fomorian and Dryad royalty. We could talk about how he’s technically a prince, and the reincarnation of Bres Mac Elatha. We could talk about how he’s one of the greatest warriors, at least his spirit is, to have ever lived in it’s many thousands of years. His ‘mortal shell’ but 28, but his spirit having been around since the epic battles of the Tautha De Dannan. But thos facts are not for the wrestling ring. In fact it’s an elimination chamber. Do these guys even know that that will entail? It’s a huge steele construction that fits around a ring. 6 guys come in and get put into individual cells and then one at a time, every few minutes, another one enters the ring. Everytime someone is pinned or they submit, they go to the back and leave the chamber. Last man will be crowned the new PTC Extreme Champion. But this man will have been in the second most brutal match that Sean has seen in his career. The most brutal he’s seen take one life and almost take another. This match will be fought on a playing field of glass and steele, the canvas is only in a twelve foot diameter square in the middle. Think about it boys. Is this what you want? Are you extreme enough to face off in this? Are you man enough to get in there with those that have described themselves as being even hungrier then the next one? Sean McClancy doubts you. He dares you. And he is coming to beat you. Get used to it. It’s the way things are. The rain didn't help Sean's mood It never did, He walked with no jacket on. He had left that behind in the restaurant with Annie, Right now he wanted his time to think. The rain pelted him; cold and unrelenting. Reminding of him of his home land in that manner. Dublin wasn’t the sunniets port on the ocean. But he knew Tokyo. He’d been here many times before. Soon he would head back to the states for both PTC and for the UWF. But not before he found his way into one more night with Patti. Soon… She was but a half hour train ride from here now. Well she was supposed to be. She’d unded up in Tokyo for a bit. She was staying here with him, but had to do a photo shoot for the 4WW today so he had decided to do a little recon work with Annie. Patti and Annie still hadn’t been in the same room outside of the wresting ring in months. And he didn’t want to test the possibility of them being in another one at this time. The rain still came down, ruining his day a bit more. Sean used to walk with his girlfriends in the rain, a different one each week. But now things were different now he was with one and just wanted to stay with that one. The urge was getting to him. He needed to figure something out. His face still carried the marks of two hard fought matches: One with the former UWF World Champion and Hall of Famer, Tsunami Oni, and the Regal Rumble itself. Both matches he gave his all in. The extreme match would be no different. He would give his all once more for the fans of UWF, The fans of Wrestling, for the honor of the opponents themselves, and god damned, he’d do it for himself… and her. Things don't change just because he is the only returning Champion in this match. If he went in there half hearted, then what honor would the winner have? There is no honor in beating someone that is not giving one hundred percent of his effort. Even though the UWF had hinted at him moving up to the Global Title Division, it was the Extreme Title he was gunning for right now. That didn’t change things. I couldn’t. Sean's black T-shirt was slick from the rain, and clung to his well cut upper body showing off what it was that he had to offer. The sloshing of his shoes hitting the wet ground seemed to be the only sound that could be heard for miles. Sean couldn't believe it was Kyo that sent those videos to Jamie and Miguel. He was sure Patti had set him straight on who planted the camera, either that or Ian did so. Fey wouldn't get upset, right t now that wasn't something she needed. She was due in a month to deliver twins: one boy one girl, the same as their mother and father. Most likely the girl would come first. So it had been for generations before that. The Female child always born before the male child, Then, if she kept on she would have two twins of the same sex. Blaise, Sean’s half Native American cousin, was the only one to break the cycle, He had two girls first, but then again he hadn't fully awakend to his heritage as a member of the Tautha De Dannan yet, he fought his bloodline until he couldn't fight any longer. Sean would be the same in that match as Blaise had been for years. He would fight until he couldn't fight any longer, then he would fight even more. He couldn't give up. He couldn't just lye down and allow the first timers to just walk all over him, without doing something about it. Sean stopped and looked at himself in the front glass window of some store that was advertising the various wrestling shows coming up in Tokyo, His face had a few cuts and bruises on it still. No surprise after Terror in Tokyo was over, he had been in two matches. One of those matches had 60 people in it, two of them had been his huge, baby brothers. It was a good warm up match for the extreme Title match coming up in just a litte bit of time, But would Sean be one hundred percent? Did his opponents take the weeks off before hand, making sure they would be fresh and uninjured for this match? If so, then they had a slight disadvantage for Sean would be ready for them. He had Annie pull every match they had been in for their federations so he could study them. Technical, powerhouses, high flyers he had faced them all no surprises for him. But was he truly ready? As he walked he mumbled like a mad man to himself, people crossed the street to avoid the raving redheaded Lunatic gaijin that just kept walking and talking to his feet. Sean: I can’na believe ‘e did this, what kind o’ man would go and do somethin’ like this? Na even a week before an event, ye KNOW yer goin’ to face someone in. At least you ga’ yer facts riogh’ after ye made an arse ou’ of yerself the first time. Sean's mind hadn’t fully been on the UWF matches either. This would explain the reason for him not being on top form. He allowed Oni to get the upper hand once or twice, but Sean deserved it, he knows to go into a match with his mind on it nothing else. Distraction can lead to mistakes, Mistakes lead to injuries or even deaths. There would be no Mistakes this time. Sean's mind was on one thing that belt... The belt that Moraless vacated without so much of a whimper. Moraless was one of the ones now on Sean’s list but he has so far avoided asking for a match against Sean, Even though they were now in the same federation. Was he afraid of him? Then again it shows you want people think of Sean as most of the new guys are saying he is the man to beat. The question is, would he be the one? The only true one to beat is yourself. You can only cause yourself to lose, it would be no other’s fault Sean’s Advice to the new guys come ready for a fight, and don’t under estimate anyone because he wouldn’t be under estimating you. It is always the silent ones you have to watch because they can be like a thief in the middle of the night. You never hear them coming until it is to late and they have struck and have robbed you. The one thing you can’t take from a man willingly, at least, is his honor. Sean knows that the match will be in some time still, but he’s going to give it his all. He would expect no less from any other man getting to that chamber. Sean didn't stop walking for a few hours but Annie finally found him after following his trail of bent trashcans; Standing out side a jewelry store, looking at something in the window. His face said it all he was a man with something on his mind. Annie knew what was going on. There was becoming to much stress for the Celtic Prince of the Fomorians, it was starting to show. The past weeks had been a lot for him, with the tape showing up in the hands of thos miscreants, seeing his sister as pregnant as she could be, Seeing Patti again and knowing his cousin finally accepted the bloodline he was from. Some good, some bad, but things none the less that add stress to a man’s life. And we can’t forget the three scrolls that he had sent out to his three superiors. The second scroll had returned today from her own, Unseelie Court. They had accepted the terms. There was only one more to go, Sean’s own king and lord. Annie slowly walked up on Sean, she knew better then startling him out of his thoughts. He was a kind man and gentle when he could be. But if he was angry and startled, like anyone, he could and possibly would swing before looking. She had left the car at the restaurant and walked to try to find him hoping he didn’t go far. It still amazed her on his speed when he was mad. He had covered a lot of ground in a short amount of time, only two hours or less. She had found him standing the rain. As he had continued, the rain hadn’t give up it’s relentless task of soaking him to the bones. Annie: Me prince, are ye ok? Sean: I wanted to be alone. I figured ye would ‘ave realized this an’ went back t’ th’ ‘otel, an’ waited fer me return. Sean spun around. Annie stood there, her silk blouse clung to her chest. The outline of her darker colored undergarments shown through it. Her long black hair was slicked down, sticking to her face. Her green eyes almost glowed as she looked at him. She gave him a slight smile. Although with someone who really had no heart at all, it didn’t quite look right upon her pale white face. Annie: I followed ye, because ye’d left yer jacket behind. With the rain, I didn’na think ye would want to get sick. Otherwise ye wouldn’na be allowed t’ meet yer new niece an’ nephew if they are t’ come early. Providing yer ever so stubborn sister doesn’na follow doctor’s orders, and keeps going to work, instead of staying home and resting. Sean: I’ve other things t’ think of. Like th’ fact tha’ one o’ me opponents fer this PTC match is a man who I should castrate an’ maybe ensure tha’ ‘e was drawn an quartered. Let along beat th’ living shite ou’ o’ ‘im. The man took mine an’ Patti’s personal things an’ sold them t’ a couple o’ morons tha’ ‘e knew would get ‘er attention, an’ mine. Mr. Kushinaga is a dead man. Ye know tha’. Why did ye na’ want me t’ see th’ fact tha’ ‘e was the one who ‘ad done it. Annie: I was tryin’ t’ keep ye from findin’ ou’ jus’ yet there Sean, because of jus’ this. Ye don’na need th’ added stress o’ it all. I was goin’ t’ save it fer the nigh’ o’ the match. Instead o’ ye steamin’ fer weeks, I was goin’ t’ let ye know tha’ day an’ let ye really get boiling. But a short term outburst, na’ a long standin’ one. Sean: Patti needs t’ know. Annie: She knows. That video played on 4WW TV. I ga’ a call from Blaise th’ other day. E’d sat down long enough t’ talk with ‘er abou’ it. She was’na goin’ t’ tell ye either. Na’ yet. Sean stared her in the eyes and saw the truth. The women of his life had moved to keep ti from him for his and Kyo’s safety. However, Annie would be evil enough to pull him aside right before he walked out to that Elimination Chamber and let him know… And then watch him almost commit a homicide in the ring. Annie: By th’ Way. One more opponent issued a statement. Well, a second one. I ga’ a call tha’ Shaman ‘ad gone off again. I could show it t’ yet. Sean: I saw it as I was passin’ one’ o’ the electronics stores. The gobshite don’na know anythin’. ‘E took th’ shot tha’ ‘e fokin’ knew more then me. Bu’ ‘e still says the Mojave is in New Mexico. Funny, when I went t’ southern Nevada, t’ be with me family when me half Paiute cousin, Blaise was born, an’ was there fer the tree plantin’ ceremony fer ‘im, I was in th’ Eastern Mojave. Th’ man says I don’na know what I’m talkin’ abou’, yet I ‘ave family members who live on a reservation in Nevada, near th’ California border, an’ one who’s family lives on th’ Reservation near Alamagordo, New Mexico. I think I bloody well know wha’ th’ fok I’m talkin’ abou’. Th’ man is a nincompoop if ‘e thinks singing’ some Kiss song off of Psycho Circus can scare me. This is’na a singin’ contest. This is na’ a me ancestors could beat yer ancestores. This is abou’ who is true t’ th’ sport an’ their callin’. This is now abou’ me. Shaman ‘as tried t’ call me out. Fer my honor, I’ll go toe t’ toe, an’ nose t’ nose with th’ man. Vendetti ‘as called me ou’. I’ll fokin’ show ‘im wha’ th’ ‘ead master ‘o a real wrestlin’ school, unlike tha’ XUW one, can teach ‘im. Fer ‘im, class is in session. School o’ ‘Ard Knocks’ 101 will begin. Ice? Who th’ fok cares. ‘E’s wastin’ me oxygen as far as I’m concerned. DDT? ‘E fokin’ lost t’ Moraless, who then turned aroun’ an’ foked it all up. ‘E dropped th’ belt. ‘E dropped th’ belt after beatin’ DDT an’ figurin’ ou’ tha’ *I* was hell bent on bein’ in this match fer th’ former champions. Tha’ *I* was comin’ back t’ beat ‘is scrawny arse. Look a’ wha’ ‘e’s done in th’ UWF since I ga’ there. Gone int’ ‘iding. Th’ bastard took a victory over me, an’ squandered it as soon as ‘e knew I’d be back. Annie: Sean. Sean: An’ Kyo. Kyo Kushinaga is th’ worst one o’ th’ bunch. An’ the sad thing is, ‘e’s na’ from Amercia like th’ others. ‘E’s one o’ these people. ‘E’s Japanese. Bu’ th’ man ‘as not one ounce o’ honor. Na’ one fokin’ gram. Tha’ man will learn wha’ it is like t’ face someone such as me when I personally be wantin’ t’ rip yer intestines ou’ an’ feed them t’ me damn dogs. I will na’ stand fer this invasion o’ me privacy, ‘er Patti’s. Th’ other four are fer me. Bu’ ‘im, ‘im I’ll be breakin’ tha’ foker in ‘alf jus’ fer ‘er. Annie: An’ ye’ ‘ave full riogh’ t’ do so. Bu’ please Sean. Na’ now. Let’s get ou’ o’ the rain, an’ get ye dried off. Patti will be back in a few hours. Sean: Yes. It’s th’ riogh’ time t’ do somethin’ tha’ I need t’. Sean stepped towards her his hand reached up and brushed the wet hair from her forehead. He leaned forward and kissed it. Sean: Annie I must ask you a favor. I need yer ‘elp, an’ right now yer th’ only one I can trust to give me a honest answer. Annie looked around them, and then into the window of the store. Annie: I’ll ‘elp ye in any way I can, me Prince. Sean: Good. Let’s step inside riogh’ ‘ere. With that, the two rain soaked individuals step inside of the store. We fade to green here.
