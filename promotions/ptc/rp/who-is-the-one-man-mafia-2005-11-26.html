Who is the One Man Mafia?

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Who is this man known as Maggot? Fade into a dark, dank, damp corner of the MBE Arena(obviously a few years ago, since MBE has closed down long since then), where a hardened, dirty, grizzled man sits on a broken down cot, with a makeshift mantle on the wall next to him with the MBE North American Championship sitting on it. There are empty liquor bottles everywhere. The figure sits up, his face becoming visible to the camera. Announcer V/O: This is Maggot… a man who seeks his home in the bowels of the arena, a man who prides himself on being the most hardcore and the most resilient man anywhere he goes. But really, who is he? Cut to Maggot trashing a locker room in the MBE Arena. Announcer V/O: He's ruthless in his vengeance… Cut to a topped steel cage structure, Maggot battling it out with WhiteNoise. Cut to the outside with Freakfish holding the MBE North American Championship, guarding the door. AV/O: He leaves it all in the ring when there's something on the line. Cut to the FZM Arena, Maggot kicking the living crap out of Troy Windham. AV/O: He can handle anyone in this business, whether they're on the bottom or whether they've climbed the mountain. Cut to Maggot hoisting the MBE World Heavyweight Championship over his head. AV/O: He's a Champion. Cut to a montage of Maggot facing various foes. First to him grappling with The Spoiler, a mountain of muscle and sinew, wearing a red adn black mask. AV/O: He's faced legends. Cut to Maggot tussling with El Tremendo, a smallish lucha masked individual. AV/O: He's faced clown princes. Cut to Maggot battling Beau Michaels-Cruise. AV/O: With bisexuals. Cut to Maggot beating down on Housefly. AV/O: And with self-proclaimed Kings of Insects. Cut to Maggot kicking the crap out of Troy Windham, the CSWA Heavyweight Champion. AV/O: And he's even gotten it on with Epitomés. …but he's never faced a vampire. I'm sure this is a challenge he's up to though. He's faced nearly everyone else. So in light of all this, who is the man known as the One Man Mafia? Can words even descr… Muffled noise, like a shove. A new voice takes over. New V/O: Ah, this is startin' to get annoyin'. Cut to a live shot of Maggot, in a dark, dank, damp corner of the FZM Arena, home of NFW East. MAGGOT: If I had to hear any more o' that pretentious shit, I mighta had to hurl. All this talk 'bout who I is, and they wasn't even gonna ax me in my own words. So I guess I had to be a little pushy. So, who am I? Well, I think the simplest way to put it is, I am Maggot. Duh, right? Well, some of you might not even care to know what my name is. You guys would just rather throw a quarter at me and tell me not to spend it on booze. And if I told you I was a pro wrestler, you prolly wouldn't believe me and insist I was a part of that Bumfights thing they got goin' on in the underground. But I is a wrestler, and I'm a damn good one as well. I won every title there was to win in MBE before they closed. I's currently in the NFW in Japan beatin' the crap out of everyone they's been puttin' in front of me. But I don't get the thrill outta winnin' the matches. Naw, I get more of a thrill of beatin' the crap out of folks, breakin' their bones, spillin' their blood… takin' whatever best shots they done gots to give me and then givin' it back to them sevenfold. Usually, that results in me winnin' the match, but that's just the gravy on top of the meat. So, that's who I is. I'm a punisher. And it don't even matter if you'se a regular john or a vampire, like Virgil Ambrosi is, you gots it comin' to you in this tournament. And that's that! Maggot stares hard into the camera as it fades into the GTT5 logo. One hype piece down, four to go.
