The Calm Before the Storm (Pt. 1: The Calm)

3 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-11-03 There is static on the screen of a television, then abruptly it turns to the image of The Phantom Republican without his mask. A voice begins speaking in a tone reserved for politics. "Gordon O. Powell didn’t have what it took to make it past the first round of the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Is this the man you want to win?" The image changes to some footage of The Phantom Republican in a more candid sense. "Gordon O. Powell is a Republican. His allegiances lie with a political party who sends our sons and daughters to their deaths every day. Gordon O. Powell like most in the Republican party is dead set in his ways, strongly against the death penalty for men convicted of heinous crimes. A man who cares more about the life of a murderer then those who were killed by them." The image fades away leaving only black on the screen as the voice over continues. "Is this the kind of man you want to see as your Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Champion?" The blackness is replaced with the smiling image of The Ironman Joshua Kosidlo. "Joshua M. is a working man’s man. He believes in hard work and dedication. Joshua’s beliefs are not corrupt by an oil hungry political party. Joshua has no allegiances to any political party, for his allegiances lie with the American people." The image of Joshua fades away to be replaced with a video of Joshua. "Hi, I’m Joshua M. You may know me better as former NTICW U.S. Champion, The Josh, or Global’s former PrimeStarr Champion The Ironman Joshua Kosidlo and current PRIME Killah. The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament is the most prestigious tournament for professional wrestlers in the business today. I represent the working man, the independent men and women of the United States of America. Like most blue collar men and women not much is expected of me and I am often overlooked. I ask you citizens of the United States of America, you citizens of the world for your support. Let me be your tournament champion, because I am just like the rest of you. " Joshua smiles bringing his right hand to his forehead in the manner of a salute. "For I am proud to serve you all in that ring, and hope to be your Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Champion. Thank you." The screen returns to black as the voice over returns. "This has been a paid advertisement by the friends of Joshua M. a real American." And finally the screen returns to static. ******************** The night air contains a slight chill, the sky possesses few clouds yet still there is a perception of malice. Joshua finds himself prone to his perceptions crouching atop the roof of a Los Angeles building. As he peers out across the "City of Angels" he notes an eerie calm, like the calm that comes before the storm. Something is about to go down. What it is and where it is going to happen are the crucial points that elude Joshua. A mild breeze blows the long white strands of hair back from Joshua’s face while rippling the cloth of his long black trench coat. Joshua hardly notices the soft breeze against his gaunt face or bare chest. His leather pants protest as he stands from his crouching position, with curiosity causing a need for action. Joshua’s anxious feet echo softly upon the surface of the concrete rooftop. This anxiety has become present in Joshua’s demeanor as of late. Consequentially the loss of a friend and a child could make even the calmest of men, anxious. Doing what he did now was physically the only way he could deal with the sorrow. Following in the footsteps of Bruce Wayne, Peter Parker and Frank Castle, he would seek his vengeance and in doing so aid the helpless. Whether Joshua had wanted to accept it or not this was a war, and he was merely a lone soldier battling the forces of evil. A vigilante was what he had become, like those from the pages of a comic book. Unlike them however Joshua was real, and he possessed something they didn’t have. Lycanthropy. On the fateful evening that he lost his son Andy he had been here in Los Angeles and his long time friend Scott had been watching over his family. Both Scott and Andy were attacked by the beast that had made him, while Angel and Bonita had escaped. Joshua could do nothing about it more than 1,400 miles away. Now however he knew his wife and daughter would be safe, with the vampire Adam guarding them. Joshua’s mind drifts to his upcoming tournament match with The Phantom Republican. Though he knew his heart and soul would not be in the match, perhaps he could use it as a form of release. Hessian had served that purpose for him on Halloween night and maybe he could do the same. After all Joshua was the PRIME Killah, The Ironman, Global’s premiere superstar, and who was this Phantom Republican? Some masked politician too entrenched in false promises to allow the public to bare witness to his face? This was wrestling not politics. Even Jesse Ventura got out of the wrestling business before becoming a governor. These things however were not important and dwelling on them would get Joshua nowhere. When the time comes to step in the ring is when he would need to think about it, not before. When Joshua reaches the far side of the roof he steps over the edge finding the fire escape one story below him. Joshua drops onto the rusted fire escape causing it to rattle. Joshua pays no mind to the unsteady metal fixture and hastily makes his way down it. Joshua reaches the last landing and leaps to the ground causing his coat to pan out like a cape. His feet slam on the concrete sending an echo down the narrow one-way alley. Joshua exits the alley into a thoroughfare leading into a short avenue. Joshua finds himself contemplating the inevitable. He had been told his suffering would not end until he confronted his maker. This battle could not end until one of them killed the other. Though Joshua longs for his chance to exact his revenge for the loss of his son he knows he is not yet ready. Harold is much older than Joshua, and has had the disease in his blood longer. If he hopes to live however, Joshua will need to find a way to kill Harold. But that is much easier said then done. Perhaps O’Malley can guide him, maybe point him in the right direction. After all O’Malley can guide him but he cannot intervene. Much as the good Lord told, each of us has free will. O’Malley is not allowed to interfere with that. Joshua was the master of his own destiny, and no one could change it but himself. If anyone could help him now, it would be O’Malley, O’Malley had to know how he could defeat Harold and finally ‘bare arms against a sea of troubles’. Either way when Joshua did finally fight Harold it would be over whether Joshua lived or died. Joshua sighs, taking in his surroundings. At the end of the avenue he has come out to a small park with a metal bench and a swing set. Other than those two key items, the park is empty. "Way too quiet" Joshua comments to himself as he takes a seat upon the bench. Joshua reaches into the pocket of his trench coat removing a pack of Marlboro 100s and a golden zippo lighter with a cross upon it. Joshua puts a cigarette in his mouth and lights it, taking a long drag. Nicotine has been his best friend as of late. Then again there was a time when cocain was what eased his pain. Joshua has always been prone to addiction. At least tobacco was a better dependent then cocain. Joshua takes a drag from his cigarette as he waits. Exhaling he watches the smoke rise into the air. The dancing smoke intoxicates him combining its simple circles with intricate designs. This observation is cut short as a firm hand falls upon his shoulder. Joshua does not look up nor behind him as he knows who it is, he merely gazes at his cigarette noticing the stub of a filter where a cigarette had been. "I’ve heard of distraction due to shiny objects, but cigarette smoke is a new one." The man behind Joshua says as he walks around the bench taking a seat next to The Ironman. He wears a simple scarlet knit sweater with olive green cargo khakis. His mouth opens forcing the creases of his mouth to emphasize the thickness of his light brown goatee. "I am sorry to have kept you waiting. I had some matters in needing of settling first." Joshua nods his head never turning to face or look at his friend. In his mind he can see the long blond hair with its tint of red, the deep piercing olive green eyes and the smooth round face of David O’Malley. "It’s no big deal. I was enjoying a smoke anyway." O’Malley smiles as he places his hands upon his thighs. "Well things on the home front have been calm," O’Malley pauses, "too calm." Joshua shifts his weight trying to make himself comfortable though it does him now good, the discomfort isn’t a physical manner. "I was thinking that too." Joshua thinks to himself, but utters nothing aloud. "Angel is still shaken up about things and I am afraid it will be a long time before she can focus on much of anything." O’Malley comments keeping things on track. "That is no however saying that she has neglected Bonita, as if anything she has been more attentive but that is to be expected." "Has Damian found out anything?" Joshua asks trying to divert the subject from painful thoughts. "About where I can find Harold?" "Nothing yet." O’Malley replies taking the hint. "However he and I did come up with something to help you." Taking the bait, Joshua turns to face his advisor for the first time of this meeting, a spark of interest in his eyes. "Oh? What may that be?" "Before I tell you more about that, I want you to understand something first." O’Malley begins with a warning in his tone. "In most cases when the blood of a Werewolf is mixed with the blood of a Vampire, the cells begin to deteriorate almost instantly. There are however rare cases where the combined blood’s cells begin reproducing at speeds thousandths of times faster then either would on their own." Joshua nods awaiting the point. One might say that Joshua has not been as virtuous as of late as far as patience goes. But this time he tries his best because if what O’Malley has to say can benefit him on his quest for peace and vengeance then he is all ears. "What I am trying to say is that, if you could find a way to safely combine your blood with that of a powerful Vampire’s, such as Damian’s perhaps then you could be unstoppable." "Unstoppable? What do you mean?" Joshua questions, already intrigued by the concept. "Meaning you could quite possibly be a true immortal. Your powers could potentially be limitless, and I don’t mean just your survival but your speed and strength as well." Joshua nods as the thought fascinates him. He could be the most powerful immortal alive. Harold would be no match for him then, but who would? He decides to consider it thoroughly. "How could I find a way to safely combine our blood?" Joshua asks, trying to work out the details. David smiles as he reaches into a side pocket of his pants retrieving a small device. The device is silver with small handles made as finger grips. There is a short needle at one end and a simple piece of metal at the other used for injecting. "I took a sample of Damian’s blood and combined it with my own. My blood should help the two bloods combine better, but that does not mean it is guaranteed to make it work." Joshua’s eyes fixate upon the one time injection considering the risks. "How will that affect my everyday life though?" Questions Joshua. "In theory, a hybrid of the two species will offer the strengths of both with few if not any of the weaknesses." O’Malley responds. "That is however only in theory." "Well what have those tests proved in the past?" Joshua asks pressing the matter. "That’s just it… no one has ever attempted a live test before. We are in the dark in those regards." And so there was a catch. If by chance this would work, there was no telling what it would do for Joshua. He could be immune to sunlight, silver and all of the things that would affect either a Werewolf or a Vampire. Or, he could in fact be affected by all of the things either species could be harmed by. It may just make him easier to kill, and as a result alienate him from his family even more than he had been before. "Either way you do not have to decide tonight." Reassures O’Malley. "I will however leave this serum with you in case you find yourself in need of it." "Thank you." Joshua responds almost distantly. "I will consider this a great deal before I make any decisions." "That would be your best bet." Confirms O’Malley as he places the serum upon the park bench. "Just know there are many risks in taking this path, but there are also many risks in fighting Harold without increasing your power. Both could lead to death." "Yeah, doesn’t that just make decision making fun?" Joshua asks more animated then before. "Basically I’m fucked if I take the serum and I’m fucked if I don’t." David nods as he understands Joshua’s predicament. He also understands however that the frustration Joshua is showing is not rooted in attempting to make a decision, rather because he feels powerless to help himself. "Unfortunately some things are out of our hands. We can only rely on hope and faith." These words hit home to Joshua. God has shown him the way all of these years, and God will surely lead him along the right path. "Then I shall do just that. If I do not dwell on it then maybe the right decision will present itself to me." David understood there was no malice in Joshua’s words, and he was confident that Joshua would do just as he said. In truth, it was his best option. Content with how the meeting had progressed David rises from the bench, preparing to leave. "I have a few things to attend to yet, but I hope I have provided you with some assistance." "Yes, you have." Joshua replies, "Quite adequately to be precise. Thank you" David merely nods in acknowledgment as he turns to leave Joshua to his thoughts knowing full well he will need enough time to mull things over. Before going too far however David stops remembering something ironic. "Oh and Joshua." O’Malley shouts grabbing Joshua’s attention. "I saw that ad you had on the television." "Oh really? What did you think?" Asks Joshua with a grin. "Well since I saw one from your opponent before hand I had a tough time deciding which of you had been more creative. Aside from that it did provide an ample amount of humor." "Oh? Why is that?" "Because when you smile like that, you kind of creep me out." Joshua chuckles, "I’ll keep that in mind next time around." "Good idea, you don’t want to make any babies cry." "I have a bad habit of making that happen." Joshua comments with a cocky grin upon his face. David turns and finally walks away leaving Joshua to his thoughts. Despite what logic would say, for the first time in months Joshua finds his mind at ease. No memories, worries or concerns fill his head. It’s actually a comforting feeling, compared to the norm. Though he has a tough choice to make soon, he is confident that God will point down the right path. At ease with himself and his situation Joshua pockets the serum and rises to his feet. Once again he takes notice of the quiet. "I just wish it weren’t so quiet." With all logic aside there are some superstitions that just stick with a person. The only one to stick with Joshua is that after every lull there is a storm. This is the lull and when it comes to an end there will be a storm to rock the foundations. When that happens, Joshua will be there to battle it. When that happens, Joshua may have to make a very tough choice. "Well God, you are my Shepard, so lead the way."
