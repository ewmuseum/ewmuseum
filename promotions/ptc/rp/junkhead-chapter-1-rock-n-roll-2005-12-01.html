Junkhead - Chapter 1: "Rock N' Roll"

1 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-01 "It’s a long way to the top if you wanna rock n’ roll." – AC/DC There’s this thing that’s been around for a long time. It’s not a positive thing. It’s actually quite negative. A lot of people hit it. It’s the place that so many stories begin at. It’s a place that’s filled with hope and pure misery. There’s absolutely nothing like it and you can’t actually comprehend it unless you’ve actually been there. You can’t visit it. You stumble upon it, but once you’re there you figure out that you’ve been leading yourself towards it from the beginning. You might have heard of this thing; this place. It’s called Rock Bottom. And Rock Bottom is exactly where our main character is. I’m going to tell you right now, before we really begin and get elbow-deep in the thick of this story, it’s not going to be a happy one. To be honest, it might be terrible. To be perfectly truthful, it might be depressing. To be bitingly serious, it might smash our main character deeper than Rock Bottom and straight to Hell. I can’t even tell you that there’s going to be a happy ending or that there’s going to be a sad ending, because the ending hasn’t even been written yet. The ending depends on our main character. So let’s meet him. He’s a big guy, standing at six foot four inches and weighing in at 245 pounds in his prime, but he’s lost a little muscle since those days so he’s now around 230. His hair used to be black and buzzed, but now it’s a very dark gray and it’s grown out to four and a half inches. There’s stubble on his face and it doesn’t look cool like Dane Cook’s or Scott Hall’s. It looks gross like a homeless man’s. He’s getting on in years. His birthday is on July 31st, but everyone thinks he’s forgotten the exact year. It’s between 1968 and 1973 as he’s said all five of those years at some point, but he usually says either ’69 or ’70. Let’s just say he’s in his mid-thirties, but right now he looks like he’s about thirty years dead. He’s a professional wrestler. Or he was at one point in his life. Now he just seems to go back to it for a quick paycheck so he can score more of his drug of choice. His name is Davis Leonard Branxton. His ring moniker is Twisted or Twizzy for short. He’s been called The Junkie Man, The Addict, The Druggy, The Co-Dependent, and the GCW Paper Champion. He is a drug addict. He’s addicted to one drug and one drug only: Twistodium. An illegal steroid made from some of the adrenaline found in goat blood. Yes, goats. G-O-A-T. The side effects are extreme and quick physical improvement, spasms, depression, excitement, dementia, an obsession with goats, and even more that haven’t quite yet been discovered. The drug Twistodium is hard to come by as it isn’t very popular, but it is highly addictive so those who do want it find ways to get it. Davis Branxton is one of those addicts and he finds himself in poor situations quite often. He’s on the street right now in an alley on the upper east side of Manhattan. He has found the man in a baseball cap that he was told about. It’s cold outside. Davis doesn’t have a jacket and he is shivering, from the chill or from withdrawal no one knows. "Goat… is… Good…" Davis says. It was his infamous catchphrase during his highest points in wrestling. He got to hold the GCW World Championship with that catchphrase. This man in the baseball cap doesn’t seem to care at all however. "Look, I don’t care who you are. This never happened." He pulls out an orange prescription pill bottle from the pockets of his thick winter coat. He shakes it three times and Davis knows simply from the sound how many white tablets are inside: forty-five. "This is all yours. Now I just need 110 bucks. Now that’s a great price for this bottle. It’s straight from the laboratories of Drugs N’ Stuff." DNS being the company that originally produced the drug Twistodium, got Davis addicted, and even at one point had Twizzy as their official spokesman. He’d beaten out Mark McGuire and Robert Downey Jr. for the position. Twizzy held a good career for a few years. Now he searches the pockets of his jeans, dirty with booze, mud, and piss. There isn’t anything inside. He looks up at the man in the baseball cap with horror splashed over his face. "I don’t have it," he says fearfully. "Then we don’t have a deal," he says as the bottle went back into his jacket pocket, "and you’re just wasting my time. Come back and see me with $110." He starts to walk away, but Twisted grabs him by the arm. "No," he says sternly. He is serious. He isn’t shivering now. His heart is twitching like a vibrator, but his outer shell is all business; all addiction. One of those old Twisted power punches or palm strikes could knock the baseball cap clear off of this man’s skull. A good old fashioned neckbreaker would get him that bottle. Maybe Twizzy could drive the cap deep into his cranium with a Pill Popper, the Forward Falling DDT finishing move he used to use back in the day. "Don’t go," he says. Will he put him in the submission hold With a Twist? Will him give him the Screw Loose? Maybe the Trash Compactor? "I’ll do anything. Anything. I need that bottle. Please. Please!" Twizzy, two time GCW Tag Team Champion, GCW World Champion, wrestling great, wrestling legend, going out like a pathetic little bitch. "Anything?" the man in the baseball cap asks. Davis nods. The man in the baseball cap, who holds the one thing that Davis "Twizzy" Branxton wants, unzips his fly and… And I’d like to tell you that Davis Branxton has more dignity and pride than that. And I’d like to tell you that Davis Branxton beats the goat-loving snot out of this freak. And I’d like to tell you that the man in the baseball cap didn’t make him swallow. And I’d like to tell you that Davis Branxton doesn’t cry when he pops the first pill of the bottle back in the abandoned store he’s squatting in. I’d like to tell you all of that, but I can’t, because it wouldn’t be true. No, Davis Leonard Branxton sucked the man in the baseball cap’s dick for Twistodium like Bob Saget for coke in Half Baked. And Twizzy hit rock bottom. Mouth first.
