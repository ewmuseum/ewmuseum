A Psychotic Hangover, Part I: Did it Really Happen

18 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-18 Who is 'Sinister' Steven Lee? What is so special about him? When did he show up? Where did he come from? Why is he here? How did he get here? The classic questions. Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How? Truth be told, these questions could be asked about any one wrestler in the business today, because no matter how "known" you may be, there is always someone who's yet to experience what you bring to the table. But even with these questions answered, there's more to leave the imagination to wonder about, and it will be this way for the end of time. Only the wrestlers themselves known all there is to tell about themselves, and most of the time they won't be willing cough up that information. The only way you can truly come to know a wrestler is to, through a course of time, become a part of them yourself. Think like them, act like them, be like them... and of course pay very close attention to detail. Becasue when you want to know about a wrestler's past, present, and future, it's not all about what they say, but how they act and say it when they do. So, once again, who/what/when/where/why/how is 'Sinister' Steven Lee? Who exactly am I? As it's been said already, I'm not willing to share that information. But then again, you pay close enough attention to what you are about to witness, you just might learn something! -------------------------------------- Pitch black... Quiet... Lonely... Just imagine it. There I was, for all I knew, in the middle of nowhere. The only thing that could be seen was a lonely light in the distance. A light at the end of a tunnel? I was unsure. I was lonely, almost afraid... Maybe?! I wasn't taking the time to think about it. I was just running. The sound of pavement under my feet that lead to seemingly nowhere was the only comfort I had at this particular moment. No recollection to any events prior to me being here were known, and I was unsure as to what I should do. The light in the distance seemed promising, so once I got there, surely I could think of something. Or would I allow myself to? The run, which I was sure only took a good five to ten minutes, seemed like it had taken forever, but I had finally made it to the one visible thing out here in the middle of nowhere. The light, which had turned out to be a street light, was shining down, right on top of a phone booth. A scene right out of a horror flick is what this was turning out to be. But without stopping to think too much about it, I entered the phone booth, picked up the receiver, and started dialing phone numbers. I was unsure of who I just dialed, why I just dialed those numbers, and what it was going to mean for me, but I did know this... Nothing! I was sure someone on the other end had just picked up, because I started talking, but I couldn't hear what I was saying, nor did I have any clue as to what was being said either. I was just simply there, almost out of body, mind, and soul, talking on the phone in a phone booth out in the middle of nowhere. The only thing running through my head for the entire fifteen minute conversation was the images of a woman, one I wasn't sure I had seen before, telling me to stop what I was doing. Telling me I was going to hurt myself. Almost pleading for my life, like I was about to damage it in some way, shape, or form. What did all this mean? Though, as I exited the phone booth that night, wherever I was, and whatever I had been doing prior, I blacked out. As I woke up, I was safely home in my own bed. Was it a dream, or had it been real? Those were the first thoughts to enter my mind as I threw the covers off. I unsteadily got up out of bed, a sharp pain running through my back for just a split second, but after it subsided, I steadied myself on my own two feet. I walked tot he window in my room and pulled back the curtains. The bright light from the sun smashed into my face, and my eyes closed almost immediately. The almost cloudless day was unlike the night prior where it seemed as if clouds had painted the sky, keeping the stars from twinkling in the night. I slowly backed away from the window, though; keeping the curtains pulled back, and began rummaging through my closet and dresser. But upon second inspection of myself, I had realized I was already dressed. Dressed in the same clothes that I wore in my... Dream?! I paid no more mind to the thought, however, as I slipped into my tennis shoes, and began my day. As I walked out of my bedroom, and into the hall, however, I heard something. That was my first sign that something was wrong. I shouldn't be hearing anything. So I paused. Frozen dead in my tracks, I waited for another sound, just in case it was only my mind playing tricks with me. Sure enough, it wasn't, as I heard it again. The sound of glass being moved about. Coffee cups? I slowly made my way through the hall and peaked around the corner through the living room, and into the kitchen. Sure enough, two fairly large men dressed in all white sat there, cups in hand, at my bar, drinking their coffee. The two almost looked like twins, with the only differences between the two being one had a completely shaven head, and the other had a short haircut of dirty blonde hair. A thought ran through my mind instantly, however, but the thought would not remain only in my mind. 'What the Hell are you doing in my house?!' My eyes widened as I said it, but no fear actually ran through my body. The two men perked up, and looked my way. No expression on either of their faces. One of them did roll his eyes fairly quickly, but neither of them said anything. They just sat their cups down, and began to walk towards me. I wasn't going to think twice about my next decision though, and I headed for the door. As I hurriedly unbolted, unlocked, and opened up my door to make a break for it, I could hear one of the men say something. 'Don't make us chase you again!' I ran for a few minutes in a town that would take 25 seconds to run from one end to the other. Basically ran circles around the town in hopes to confuse the two large men who were after me for some reason. I had stopped just outside of "Old Man" Spencer's Drug Store, though; huffing and puffing from the run I had just taken. Maybe it would be safe for me to hide in here for a while, so I went inside. When I looked at the counter, I was surprised to see a very attractive young woman standing back there, and it was almost as if she was given me the look over. She looked VERY familiar though, for some reason I wasn't sure of. I wandered around the store for a few minutes before noticing the young woman approaching, though, and I tried to ignore her, thinking maybe she knew something about why I was being chased and would turn me in. 'Can I help you with something?' She asked as she tapped me on the back as I had turned it in her direction. I paused for a good second or two before finally turning around and acknowledging her being there. I looked around, as if I might be interested in something. 'No, no, you know, I think, yeah, I think I am just looking around.' I said, stuttering the whole time. Despite being the person I am, the ladies always seem to find that soft spot in me, without really doing anything at all. She smiled at me as she picked up a box of condoms off of the rack I had managed to stop right next to. 'You're, what, probably twenty-five, twenty-six years old, and can't even admit you've got some lucky girl at home that you've come here to buy these for?' The question was asked in a very sarcastic tone, maybe almost as if she was hoping I really didn't have a "lucky girl" at home waiting for me to bring these here. My face had turned a bright red as I looked at the floor. Any other person in the world would have gotten a piece of my mind, or more, for saying less, but as I've said, the ladies... I can't resist. 'No, no lucky lady at home. I'm, well, I'm actually just in here because when I woke up this morning, these two large guys dressed in funny looking white outfits were in my house, and had obviously captured me last night or something. For some reason I've got no recollection of it ever happening.' I finished my story, and her face showed she didn't believe me. She rolled her eyes, shoved the condoms into my grasps, and under her breath said, 'Yeah right,' as she was walking off. I sat the condoms back down into their rightful place and went after her. She kept her back turned the whole time until she was back at her post behind the counter, the same spot she had been in when I entered the store. She gave me this "what the hell do you want?" look and I was just about to start explaining myself when her face filled with curiosity and she began to stare out the front of the store. Just about that time I heard the screeching of tires as they brought a large white van, that I had noticed as I turned around, to an abrupt halt. I watched the two large men dressed in white hop out of the van and start approaching the store. I turned back around and looked at the young woman behind the counter in the eyes. 'See Lia, told you there were two crazy fucks after me!' The irony of the comment I had just made, as it pertains to me, was overshadowed by the fact that I had just spit a random name out like I had known this girl before. Her face filled with surprise though, and her smile was a mile wide, showing off her pearly white teeth. Right before I had turned back around, though, I had noticed her cheeks begin to get red, a shade that matched her hair perfectly. The little bell that rings when the door to the drug store opens began to ring. The two muscle heads stepped inside and let the door close behind them before the bald one found it funny to crack a joke at this time. 'Hey Franklin, you hear that bell? You know what that means don't you?' The other remained silent, but had a shit eating grin on his face. He just shook his head. 'Well, every time a bell rings, a psycho gets his straight jacket!' The two instantly burst into laughter that could only be explained as almost robotic. I shook my head at the poor attempt to make this situation into a joke for them, and I glanced back at the women, whose name I had obviously guessed right, or I have known from sometime in the past. I then turned my attention back to the two who I would now assume to be working for some insane asylum. But why they had come to get me, unknown, and who could have told on me? 'Pssssssst.' I heard from behind me. I didn't turn around though, just leant an ear really quick. 'Steven, I know this isn't time for me to get all excited about you remembering me and everything, so we'll save that for later. For now, you head out the back while these two distract themselves. I'll keep them busy for a while too. I don't think my father has gotten back from breakfast yet, so you should be safe.' I nodded my head once, and just once, really quickly as she finished up her idea for my mistake. I began to back up slowly through the little door in the counter to make my escape. Not only did I have these two asylum workers to contend with now, but I also had to figure out where I had known Lia from as well. I looked at her and gave her a quick smile, though, as I headed to the door that lead tot he back where I would make my escape into the alleyway. The plan was short-lived. The door had opened for me, and sure enough, "Old Man" Spencer has shown his ugly face. 'Where do you think you are going you crazy piece of trash?!' He said with force. Not only did it grab mine and Lia's attentions, but the two meat heads had also stopped their robotic laugh to notice me trying to get away. I just hung my head as Franklin and his unnamed, as far as I knew; twin brother came and grabbed me. 'Hell!' That was the choice word for me as I was being drug out the front of the store. The door never did close behind us though, as Lia had charged after us, pleading for them to let me go. It was no use; they threw me into the back of the van, equipped with seating on either side, and closed the doors behind me. I could hear them arguing, but it was just muffled through the sheets of metal from the van. I sat down and tried to release all thoughts from my mind. Hopefully this would all turn out into a big mistake or something, and I wouldn't have to worry about missing the big event. Though, with my luck, shit had yet to begin hitting the fan. The scene fades to black.
