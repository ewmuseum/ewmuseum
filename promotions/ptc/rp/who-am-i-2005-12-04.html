Who Am I…?

4 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-04 "Who am I, you wonder?" That was the answer to everytime Sledge got asked who he was. Never did somebody get a straight up answer. Nobody knows really much about him. He likes to keep his stuff to himself. Because well…it’s his stuff. That included personal information. Information that eluded many people who asked him questions about him. Sledge likes it this way. Being mysterious and all. At this way, people are getting curious. And when people get curious, they start to try to dig up every piece of information they can get, and that means people are interested in you. And that means getting attention. Sledge loves getting attention. But really, even you really want to get to know me, then watch me. Observe me when I make an action. Observe me whenever I talk. Observe me for whatever reason, just do it. Maybe, just maybe, you can dig up some information about me. Maybe, just maybe, you can get to know me. If you’re lucky, maybe just then you can get the answer to the question that everybody asks… "Who is Sledge?" ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Beer, beer, beer, beer…I need beer… That was the only thing that floated through Sledge his mind. He thought of nothing else then just getting beer. He walked over the street, being focussed on his goal in retrieving beer, and drinking it. Drinking it all. All the beer would belong to him. Beer that would go through his mouth, then being gulped down through his throat inside his stomach, where it was safe. Where it should belong in the first place. He walked down the street. It was cold. He’s wearing long pants. Jeans to be precise. And he was wearing gloves to. Other then that, the usual clothing. A black shirt without sleeves and holes in it and his black boots. People stared at him as he walked by people, while the people wore winter clothing. Warm clothing. Not Sledge, he didn’t like warm clothing. They all felt so…warm. That isn’t what Sledge wants. Besides, to him, it wasn’t that cold at all, even though a small white cloud would fly out of his mouth every time he would breathe out. Sledge didn’t care about the cold. Sledge didn’t care about the people that were staring at him like he was stupid. Fuck ‘m, he thought. They don’t know him, so why should he be worried about them. Even better, why should THEY be worried about him? Sure, he likes the surrounding attention, but not this attention. Not negative attention. If you don’t like what you’re seeing, then turn around and mind your own business. You run your life and I’ll run mine, he thought to himself again. He continued his travel towards the nearby shop where they would sell beer. Good beer, ofcourse. Sledge doesn’t want anything of that amateur crap they also sell for a small price. No, good beer, no matter the cost. If it tastes good, then there is no price good enough in Sledge’s mind. Beer always hits the spot with Sledge. Some worry that he drinks too much of it and that it will give him problems with his body, especially his liver. Ah well, it’s my liver, isn’t it? So what do you care that I fuck it up? Like it hurts you or anything? The only one it does hurt is me, he thought to himself once again. You shouldn’t worry too much about Sledge, he’s doing fine. He’s smart, if he does have problems, he will do something about them. He continues to walk, only to see a small incident occuring. A lady is getting robbed. Robbed by a kid. He pulled out his knife and then grabbed her purse. The lady started screaming for help. "My purse! He just stole my purse! Police!", the lady yelled. Nobody really put any effort of helping. Even better, they were just standing there, doing nothing to help out, either looking stunned or suprised by the incident. The thief starts running and running, but the street were slippery. He didn’t have a tight grip to the ground, so he just ran, and if he needed to stop, he would slide a metre or two before he would actually stand still. And that was his mistake. The kid was just plain stupid and too desperate to get away. Concentrated about running away and standing on his feet, he didn’t pay attention to who or whatever would stand before his path. In the first place, he was staring to the purse, revealing a grin of succesion. But before he knew it, he was lying on the ground. He was knocked of his feet by something. Something big. Something hard. Was it a wall? No, not in the closest way. Was it a truck? What, a truck on the street? Not a chance. In fact, it wasn’t something made of stone or steel. In fact, it was made of muscles and bone. It was an arm that knocked him down. An arm where he ran against. Stupid of him. He tried to look up, but his vision blurred. He was dazed. He tried to focus, and he saw Sledge standing over him, even though he didn’t knew him. To him it just was a big guerilla standing above him, only looking a bit more human then a guerilla. Sledge looks down, shaking his head as he stares to the kid. The kid had dropped the purse and the knife on the ground, coughing in the cold. He couldn’t get up, his head just hurt so much. And it had all the reason to feel the pain. First a big arm that knocked him off his feet, and then the hard concrete ground that cushioned his fall. He dropped hard on the back of his head. "Look at you. You just got knocked the fuck out. By me. You could have easily run pass me, but no. You weren’t paying attention. You’re weak. You’re stupid. You’re young. You could’ve had a great future. If you studied hard, you could’ve been a doctor or a lawyer, making money the good way. The correct way to make money. But no, instead you threw your future out of the window and took this life. The life of a criminal. The life of a thief. A failed thief. If you decide to do something, do it right. Always have your head clear. You’ve won until you have won, and that isn’t halfway the game." "It was a stupid mistake to do this, on the middle of the street, in the middle of the day. I’m not even a thief, and even I know that you don’t have to do that in the middle of the day." Sledge shakes his head some more while telling this all. He steps over the kid and walks into an alley. He walks further without looking back. Over his shoulder, a police officer grabs the kid and puts him into handcuffs. The lady grabs her purse back, and she looks into the alley. The police officer is suprised himself by the man that just helped the lady retrieve her purse and let the police have another criminal in their possesion. "Who was that guy?", the police officer asked to the lady. "I don’t know…", the lady answered back. "But I do know how he looks!" "Good, maybe with that we can get some information about the guy. I’m curious myself now.", the police officer answered, lifting the kid of the ground, back on his feet. Both stare into the depth of the alley, as they see Sledge walk away. Sledge just continues his quest to what he originally wanted to have. Beer. Lots and lots of beer. He does deserve a reward for helping that lady back there. What better reward then beer?, he thought to himself. He continued to walk through the alley. It was a big alley. It feels like he has walked for ages through the damn thing. He tries to stop thinking about it and just continues his walk, his cold and long walk. It’s so damn cold. Don’t be such a pussy. You’ve felt worse then this., Sledge thought in himself. He finally saw the end of the alley. He sighed in relief. Finally he could get out of this damn place. It’s so small. Not in height, but in width. As he is almost out of the alley, something is blocking his path. He looks down, and sees a small thing of life. An organism. An animal. A dog. A puppy to be more precise. The puppy is staring to Sledge. He’s hungry and it’s cold for him. Sledge stares back, just blinking his eyes. With this, the puppy reaches through his eyes, into his soul. Sledge knows the puppy wants something. He wants warmth and food. Alot of food. Food that Sledge can’t provide at the moment, unless he kills himself and let the dog chew on his body. Like that was going to happen. The puppy barks softly. Sledge continues his stare. The puppy barks once again, softly. "Sorry, my small friend. I don’t have anything for you…" Sledge answered back to the barks of the puppy. The puppy didn’t care about that. He just wanted to be with Sledge now at the moment. Like he is the first thing he has seen in a long time. The puppy barks again, and this time his tongue falls out of it’s mouth. He breathes in and out in rapid speed. Everytime he breathes out, a small cloud flies out of his mouth and disappears into the cold, fresh air. The puppy continues to stare at Sledge, and with this, Sledge sighs. He picks up the small puppy who doesn’t mind it. Sledge holds the puppy in front of himself and looks him straight in the eyes. The puppy continues to breathe in and out with rapid speed. "Your breath smells.", was the only thing Sledge said. The puppy continues to just stare at him in the eyes. "I’m sorry, little puppy. I don’t have any food or drink for you. I can’t even provide you a home, well, maybe I can, but not my home. I have a smelly house. Wait, you’re smelly as well. Eerrr…Anyway, it’s better for you to just stay here. It’s cold, yes. But if you hang in tough, you’ll survive. It isn’t all that bad.", Sledge said to the puppy, trying to act wise. The puppy responds bag by barking once again, and he licks Sledge’s nose. Sledge just stands still, as the puppy is looking quite happy. He licks Sledge’s nose again, and again, Sledge doesn’t do a thing. He sighs and puts the puppy down to the ground. "I’m sorry, small puppy. Take care…" Sledge walks off, and wants to exit the alley, but the puppy continues to follow him. Sledge stops and looks down. He blinks his eyes. The puppy blinks back. Sledge shrugs his shoulders and continues to walk again. He exits the alley, with the puppy following him in hot pursuit. He finally arrives where he needed to be. He’s finally at the store. He immediatly goes inside without thinking, and the door closes slowly, and the puppy sees his chance and quickly jumps inside before the door closes. Inside the shop, a Korean guy notices Sledge. He smiles. "Howdy, Sledge!" the Korean guy said immediatly. "Hi Jimmy, how are you doing today?" Sledge said back. "Good, I guess so. It’s been a cold day today, but hey, I’m inside here, safe in my shop. I have a heater, so I’m not complaining!" Jimmy answered back. He looks down and sees the puppy standing next to Sledge, rapidly breathing in and out and wagging his tail. "Hey, no dogs allowed in my shop! Or else I’m gonna choppy choppy him into pieces!" "Dog, what dog?" Sledge answered back confused. He looks down, and notices the puppy. He sighs once again, and picks up the puppy. He holds the puppy under his arm and looks down on him. "He’s okay. Don’t worry, he won’t shit in your store." "He’s your responsibility, Sledge! If I see him start to eat something, I’m gonna eat him! I’ve got enough hot sauce in the store so I can create one fine meal!" Jimmy fired back, licking his lips in the progress. "You’re sick, Jimmy. It’s just a dog. A small puppy, dude. I oughtta slap you right now, just because you are thinking about eating him. Crazy ass Korean…" Sledge said in return. Sledge walks over to the cooler and grabs a six-pack of beer. He smiles happily as he looks down on his beer. He wants to walk back, but just then, the puppy barks again. Sledge looks down and sees the puppy trying to wiggle out of Sledge’s grip under his arm, as he stares at the doggy biscuits. Sledge smiles and grabs the biscuits as well. The puppy stops wiggling and barks happily and wiggles his tail again. Sledge walks back to Jimmy and Jimmy looks down to the stuff Sledge got. Jimmy types something down on his cash-register. "And that’s going to be eight bucks, Sledge." Jimmy answered. Sledge digs down his pocket and gives Jimmy a ten dollar bill. Jimmy grabs Sledge’s change. "Can I have a bag for this, please? It’s kinda hard walking around with this in just one hand." Sledge said. "Certainly." Jimmy answered back as he puts the biscuits and the beer in one white plastic bag. He gives it back to Sledge, who grabs the bag and leaves the store. "See you later, Sledge!" Jimmy said quickly, but Sledge didn’t hear it anymore. He didn’t say anything back without turning around. Jimmy mumbles something about Sledge in the progress. Another customer who was in the shop, hiding a bit, being very sneaky, comes up to Jimmy. "Who was that guy?", the customer asked. "That was Sledge." Jimmy answered back. "That’s all I know about him. That, and that he comes in here alot, just to buy beer, sometimes an extra snack, but that’s it…" Back outside, Sledge looks down to the puppy again. He sighs and doesn’t know what to do with him. He looks around him, as the puppy wiggles his tail. Sledge then notices a young girl playing across the street. Sledge grins and with the puppy under his arm, he walks to the little girl, who firstly doesn’t notices anything, but then looks up and blinks her eyes. The puppy barks softly and the young girl smiles. "Wow, what a cute puppy, mister! Where did you get that? I want to have a puppy as well, but I don’t know where to find one, can you please help me?" The child asked Sledge, who grins. "Look no further, small girl. Why? Because this puppy isn’t mine. No, not at all. It is in fact your puppy. Congratulations with your new puppy!" Sledge said back to the small girl with a smile so big now where you can stick a hamburger in. She jumps up and down because of happiness and gladly accepts the puppy, who barks softly and wiggles his tail. The young girl embraces her new pet, and smiles some more. "I’m gonna call you Micky! Nice to meet you Micky!" The small girl yelled. She continues to embrace the puppy, as Sledge grabs the doggy biscuits out of his bag. He gives it to the girl. "Here, feed that to him now. I guess he’s hungry. I’m glad you’re happy, now the puppy has a home as well, and that makes me happy inside as well. I have to go again, I’ll see you around, kid…" The child doesn’t pay any attention anymore, as Sledge walks off. Just like that, a man runs towards his child. He was worried as he just saw a big man standing with his baby girl. Just in a flash, he notices that his young girl is holding a small puppy. "Who the hell was that?", the father asked the young girl. "I don’t know, but it was a nice man! He just gave me a puppy and doggy biscuits! I named him Micky! Look at him, daddy, isn’t he cute?" the girl said to the father, who actually looks down in disgust. "Eeerr, yeah, great." The father said, not looking to happy. "Can we keep him, can we keep him?" The young girl asked with much delight. "Like I have a choice now…" The man answered back. "Thank you very much, sir." Said the father towards Sledge with much sarcasm. But Sledge has walked some further, but he did hear the conversation and grins. No problem, old bastard. Sledge thought to himself. He continues to walk. But now, a few people on just one day have again asked the question, who is Sledge? "Who am I?" Sledge said. "You really want to know? I’m Sledge. And I’m representing the UWF, and I’m all about making an impact, getting known by people because of my actions, and not my words. I’m participating in the GTT5 where my first opponent will be Greg Jackson. Because he is my first opponent, he will be the first who will get defeated and get kicked out of the tournament by my hands, so I can move on to Round Two where a new opponent will be waiting for me and will get defeated. Sledge vs Greg Jackson, place your money on me and you have extra money guaranteed. Sorry Greg Jackson, but that is the truth. Here you have your fucking answer of who the fuck I really am…" And with those last words, Sledge continues to walk with the with bag with beer in his hand…
