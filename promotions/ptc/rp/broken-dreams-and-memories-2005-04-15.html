Broken Dreams and Memories

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 An unfamiliar face reflected through the shards of broken glass, which scattered across the tile of the floor. Prisms of light dulled by drops of crimson stained the crystalline glass, which scattered in a million pieces around his feet. The mirror shone reflecting the future he could have had, but he dropped it shattering his soul like glass. He continued to look at his broken image but he didn’t know the person whom he saw. It looked like his twin but it could not have been. This person was a liar, a cheater, and didn’t care about anything at all; this couldn’t have been him, or was it? Ebony hands stained scarlet clutched a discombobulated head as Chris Collins fell to a bed in the darkness of his home. In his head voices ragged demanding to be heard causing him to clutch in even more confusion. “What’s going on?” He asked of himself, shaking and squirming in a pain-liked trance. “Get out, get out!” He demanded. But it was no use. The voices continued to howl through his skull bouncing off the walls vibrating like the strings of a wooden guitar. He reached out grabbing a piercing shard, clenching his hands around it tightly before placing it to his wrist. On impact from his shakiness vicious red liquid seeped through like a sponge. He could’ve ended it right there and then but something came over him. It was like his life flashed right before his eyes showing the good and bad. Giving him a broken glimpse of his childhood, making him wish that he could only transport himself back to those days. He felt angry and hatred for what he had done; he could never forgive himself. “All this should end now.” A tear rolled down salting the darkness of his skin. “What is the use of this life?” He pressed the shard of glass against his skin and gently slid it across. Was his pain and suffering over? Did he put himself out of his misery? He blacked out. A tender hand caressed Chris’ face blindfolding him with soft words. “Wake up sleepy head. You’ve been sleep all day!” The voice revealed of a woman cried out. She pounced on top of Chris like a Lion on its prey and straddled his waist with her butterfly hips. “I’m up, I’m up.” He replied angry as he tried to push her up off him. His eyes opened slightly before closing to readjust to his new surrounding. Where was he, Heaven? The hands are gentle as they caress, the soft words still holding his eyes close. He shook his head and the blindfold fell. “I made you breakfast.” “Huh?” Chris asked confused. “Breakfast? Where am I or better yet what time is it?” From shock of the words his eyes jolted open as quick as a cheetah. His hazel eyes were now engaged on his fiancé, model chick, Ambyr Dalton. She had a smile from ear to ear and a tray with a bowl of cereal in her hands like a servant. “Ten O’clock in the morning.” She told him placing the tray on the cocktail table in front of the couch where Chris lay before taking a seat of her own. “I thought you’d never wake up.” “Yeah.” He replied. “Me too.” “I made your favorite,” she assured him. “Captain Crunch cereal.” Chris looked at her dazed. The two thick pieces of hair, which sat in the middle of his head like a dead raccoon, rose in confusion. “Captain Crunch? Made?” He asked confused. “Yeah!” Came an excited reply. She reached to the table grabbing the remote control turning on the television with a press of the button. “I thought I’d keep your meal light this morning.” “Why is that?” He asked. “Well,” she huffed. “You know you’re in that Primetime Central Cruiserweight Tournament thing. I just don’t want your weight to suddenly exceed beyond limits forcing you to drop from the tournament.” “I doubt that it could and hmm let’s see,” looking at his watch. “Less than fourteen hours.” “I’m just making sure.” She said angrily falling violently back into the couch like she was no help at all. “That’s what I get for trying to take care of you I guess.” Chris shook his head, biting his tongue from saying anything at all. He reached forward for the spoon, which rested coldly in the milk bowl of cereal in front of him. Taking a few kibbles and bits of Crunch Berries he placed the spoon in his mouth like a Cho Cho train forcing the sweet goodness down the dry pits of his throat. “I guess I too am a Cereal Killer!” He joked. The joke brought a smile to Ambyr’s face, Chris noticed and with a devious smile he leaned over tickling her. “Come here you!” That is when he first noticed the white band wrapped around his right wrist. As soon as he did he pulled back like he had just touched fire and leapt to his feet holding it like a disease. Ambyr’s eyes widened a state of confusion suddenly coming across her. “What’s wrong with you?” Her nose twitched. Her finger mysteriously found its way to the base of her lips. Chris continued to clutch his wrist. “What… What’s this?” He asked. Ambyr smiled. “You mean you don’t remember?” Chris shook his head in disapproval. “No.” Ambyr moved in and grabbed his arm planting a kiss on the bruise. “You got a boo boo after you burned yourself on the stove and I wrapped it up for you while you were sleeping.” “Oh. What a relief.” He wiped the sweat from the glands of his forehead and sank back onto the couch like a misguided cartoon character. “Why? What did you think it was?” “Oh nothing,” he replied innocently. “Nothing at all. Just a dream I guess.” He sat there with his arm around Ambyr’s neck. Life was good, real good in fact. Suddenly thoughts and memories rushed jumbling up his thoughts and memories. Ten Years Ago He found himself back in his childhood years. Back when he was poor and living in Brooklyn, New York with his mother and father. Back when times were rough, and when you had to fight to stay alive; survival of the fittest. “I’m going to be the greatest wrestler alive.” A young confident Chris Collins yells to the top of his lungs. “The King of the World!” “Keep dreaming, kid.” His father replied monotonously. “You’re going to be scarping shit from the bottom of the barrel just like the rest of us.” Chris always hated his father. He never gave him any support and he didn’t believe in anything he had said. “You’re going to be nothing.” He told him over and over again. “You’ll never make it in the real world unless you start thinking.” “You’ll NEVER make it.” The words continuously echoed throughout his head for years. Unfortunately, Chris had proven his father wrong and because of his doubt of his son their relationship crumbled year after year. End Trance. “I hate my father.” He muttered under his breath. It was a proven fact and he found himself saying it over and over again. “What was that?” Ambyr asked removing herself from the ball underneath his broad shoulders. “Oh… uh.” He was short for words. “I said I need some water.” “Get up and get it then, silly.” She told him. “I’m not your maid you lazy bastard.” She punched him gently in the gut forcing him to double over just a little bit. “Oh. I am.” He told her. “But right now I just want to continue to sit here and cuddle up with you.” That sly velvet teddy bear he was. Always found his way back to her heart. Ambyr smiled. She cuddled herself back into his arms as the two looked out at the television monitor entertaining them. Sitting there watching, Chris gained a sense of urgency. He now realized that his father didn't want him to fail at all. He just used reverse psychology to motivate him. He realized that his father had always been there for him. He felt guilty. “Can I ask you a question?” Ambyr contradicted. “Yeah sure, I guess.” Chris replied. “Why have you been so down?” “Me down?” He stupidly asked in the form of a question. “Down where?” “So down in the dumps like you lost your best friend?” Chris shrugged. “I don’t know what you’re talking about.” “If you say so.” She told him. “I think you need to pay a visit to him.” Chris looked on confused. “A visit to who?” “Your father.” How did she know? Was he that obvious? “I-I’m not ready.” Like the great Langston Hughes once said: Hold fast to dreams For if dreams die Life is a broken-winged bird That cannot fly. Hold fast to dreams For when dreams go Life is a barren field Frozen with snow. -Langston Hughes (1929) So hold fast to dreams. Dreams give life to everything in the world; they let you have a goal. They allow you to reach out for something while living in a world created by you. Dreams are the landscapes of your mind, the paradise you work in. Dreams are the fighters of your mind, your friends at night. Dreams are the hopes of your soul. So hold fast, and don’t allow them to be broken.
