"Reasons" IG Round 18 RP Designs for honour and respect had never really been a big requirement with American based workers. I knew heading into a life of hard work and oppression that my ideals would be hated. Yet I had gone ahead all the same. Fool hearted my father had said. Over the three years I had been a pro wrestler there had been times I thought his words had held true. Though over time I had gained moments that had sort to quash those words and provide me with enough memories to continue working. My career had started me off in a place many fail to even enter. Prime the champions of champions home. It was a place brave men roamed. Where the bold played and the weak fade. Admittedly I found myself alittle taken with the demands that where heaped upon a man in my position. It wasn’t that I couldn’t handle the competition; I just hadn’t expected it to be as tough or as face as it had been. Despite that I however found my feet well. I had always been able to adapt to new surroundings. I began to win matches and while the fans seemed to rain abuse upon me I was progressing. Within a couple of months I found myself in the line for my very first title match. I’d been no more expecting a chance at gold then I had been expecting the hatred but there it was. I had quickly become the bad guy from Japan, but it worked in my favour and Prime found itself having to deal with me as a title contender. The match came and I took the belt. Months into my career and I had won a title in a company widely regarded as the best in the world. I would have never admitted it then, but later that night I found a quite spot to allow my tears of pride and joy to flow. I had been booed out of the arena, but I had become the Prime Alias champion and that had meant a lot to me. My life at that time was wonderful. Each day I woke with a sense of meaning and pride. Even my father had been unable to say anything negative. My life was a pleasure. Prime was fun to be in and I even had a newfound friendship with Poet and Black Angel. Yeah looking back life for me then was amazing. It got even better seven months into my career having lost the alias title fate handed me a second shot at the Title men saw as Primes highlight. The 5Star title was a prize I had earmarked as desiring. It was the corner stone the company seemed to be based around. I wanted it; I really sort to stand as the 5star champion. My first attempt had ended in defeat and the pain of that night had lived on within me. Even now that night I had to watch the title getting handed to another man hurts. My next chance came and I was determined not again. Never again. The night is one that lives on in the memories of those that where there live. The blood I lost, the sweat I poured and the tears I fought back are as close to me now as they where at that moment. Zyfloi was my opponent and a worthy one at that. I never gave him the credit but I’ve never given any man the credit he may have earned. The 5 Star Title however had come my way. I had wanted it from the first moment I had been accepted the contact offer. The night was and still is the greatest night in my wrestling career. The pride I saw in my father’s eyes. The few I allowed around me showed me more respect in those days then ever before. I had earned myself the title revered and my confidence was reaching new highs. More gold would come my way in Prime. Black Angel would partner me as we triumphed in a tag team title match and I became the first man to win three different titles. The days were insane and even today I couldn’t tell anyone just what we were thinking or trying to do. I only know I had made history and no one in Primes future could ever take that away. Men would go on to equal my feet but only I could state to be the first. While my career was reaching new heights my personal life was such a mess the movie about my life would be seven hours long. I had gambling losses, feuds with neighbours. I was being linked with mobsters. I was making more money in those days then ever before and yet couldn’t buy a new TV. I had no choices but my father came to my rescue and demand I leave prime. He got my debts cleared help clear my name and eased me out of a crowd of friends the police would regularly question. I had been on the fast track to the top but hadn’t been able to cope with the pressures. I had felt ready just a few months later and signed a contact from WWA. Things seemed to come easy. The pace wasn’t as face as prime and the number of great wrestlers was fewer. Yet the standard at the top seemed once again out of my reach. I walked through the lower ranks earning the TV Title in only my second match. The career was again the best thing in my life as my personal life quickly fell to pieces. I found myself arrested on murder charges and was linked to several unprovoked attacks on people. My nights out after events where littered with blank spots that police would question me about and I would find myself unable to find the answers. Once more I was left with no really choice I was been hounded by press at events and the eyes of those close to me seemed to questioning my innocence. How could I continue when not even my friends believed in me? So I left WWA; I needed to sort my life out. I had a friend who quickly moved away giving up his dreams to remove me from his life. I had a girlfriend who left me once the limelights faded. I had nothing left from my career but memories and I'm saddened to admit this, but I turned the wrong way. I turned to the people that would lead me to a live of robbery. Living a life as the enforcer to men who would do anything if the right price were offered. I was in a low place and would have remained there had it not been for an ache within me. My life had been about working on my art. I have dreamt of the day when I would be considered as one of the greats. As a world champion who upon entering a company. He would transform the look and feel for the viewing audience. Few men managed that feet and the pain of quitting before I had even tried to reach that point hurt me more then anything anyone else had ever done to me. The big turning point happened when I woke one day in a place I wouldn’t have every wanted to be. I was with a person who looked as if she would do anything for the change in a schoolboy’s pocket. I took myself home to my father. I was twenty-two years old and that day I walked into that home felling like a six-year-old brat. I was so angry at myself and so upset at the place I had allowed myself to be dragged. My father took me back to the gym. Took me back to training daily keeping my mind focused on the art of wrestling. I made a blood promise to myself that first week. I promised I would do things right this time. I would not allow people into my life. I would not allow anyone to stand in my way this time. I would allow the expectation and perceptions of a public who hated me to anchor me down. The respect from my peers would have to be earned I knew that. The coming days would be filled with my name being feared for my talents and respected for my triumphs. I will take my skills through the entire roster at Four Corners Wrestling and gain the world title that would step me into that elite group of wrestlers. I will export my talents into tournaments that were open to wrestlers from different companies and free agents. I am going to start with the Infinite Gauntlet round 18. I will showcase myself at such a high level that my name, Joshua Taro Freedom will be instantly known. I promise myself here and now I will be remembered from this Gauntlet and the honour and respect my name earns from winning will be the first step on my road.
