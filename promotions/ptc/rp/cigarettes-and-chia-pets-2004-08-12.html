Cigarettes and Chia-Pets

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-12 [Rich Rollins did very few things for pleasure. While most people spent the summer months soaking in the sun, enjoying the company of family and friends and generally having a good time, The Renegade was alone. Most people took a vacation to the lake; Rollins took a walk down the street to Jerry’s Bar. Most people saved money to get themselves the car of their dreams; Rollins scrounged enough change for a shot of tequilla on the weekends, and on Tuesday nights it was two-fers. What most people considered relaxing, Rich Rollins considered slavery to the American Dream and an undying addiction to consumerism. He didn’t take long walks in the park. He didn’t socialize with co-workers.] [And he damn sure didn’t spend a perfectly good Thursday afternoon in his bosses’ office.] [Emitting an exasperated sigh, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins catches the eye of an attractive secretary down a long hall in the confines of the UWF Arena, his attention suddenly pulled away from the door of President Richard Hayes. The pretty blonde sizes Rollins up, taking in his shoulder-length brown hair falling in waves down the nape of his neck and his pair of tattered blue jeans, a white tank top clinging to his body from the thick Chicago heat. Her eyebrows raise in disgust as she turns on her high heels, leaving Rollins to once again sit - alone - in front of Richard Hayes’ door.] [Women. God’s first and yet final plague to man.] [Instantly forgetting about the secretary’s brush-off, Rollins absently pulls a pack of Marlboros from his jacket pocket and puts a butt to his lips, digging for his lighter. Just as he fishes it out and flicks the small flame into existence, the office door finally swings open.] Richard: (off camera) Come in. [More than slightly irked, The Renegade tucks the cigarette behind one ear and rises, giving a last glance toward the dismissive secretary down the hall. Gathering his thoughts, he pushes past the heavy oak door with the goldtone nameplate, the true meaning of the phrase “behind enemy lines” dawning on him like the first ray of sunlight after a tropical storm.] [Needless to say, this was the last place Rich Rollins wanted to be today.] Richard: Well, well… if it isn’t the rebel with a cause, here in my very own domain. I’d offer you a seat, but I wouldn’t want to give you the impression that I like you. You’re fine right where you are. [Standing in the middle of UWF memorabilia heaven, The Renegade takes a few seconds to scan his surroundings. Plaques and promotional posters cover every inch of every wall, scattered between polished replicals of old championship belts hung on customized little ornate backboards. A massive oak desk sprinked with random business papers and a half empty mug of black coffee takes up the farthest end of the small chamber that has become Richard Hayes’ personal playground.] [The man himself seems almost tailor-made for his environment. And considering his spotless black Armani business suit, the term “tailor-made” seems more than appropriate.] [Leaning back in a large plush chair behind his even larger desk, Richard Hayes studies Rollins from across the room, locking his fingers behind a head full of dark slicked-back hair. Every bit the epitome of a media mogul, Hayes grins widely at his visitor.] Richard: Do you know why you’re standing in my doorway, Rollins? Renegade: How many guesses do I get? [A joke. Hayes doesn’t laugh.] [Swinging his legs out from under the desk, Hayes stands and absently brushes out the wrinkles in his suit pants. A wry grin tickling a corner of his mouth, he slowly starts to walk across the ornate carpet of his sitting area, gesturing broadly around the entire office. The massive window array overlooking the Chicago cityscape illuminates his form from behind, making him look almost serene.] [Almost.] Richard: You are standing in the House that Hayes Built. This is my empire, my passion and my play. (he continues to walk across the room toward Rollins) I brought you here because you are under contract to me, end of story. You do what I ask, when I ask, and most importantly… you don’t ask questions. Capiche? [Rollins’ searing blue eyes meet with Richard’s, his face a stoney mask of indifference. Licking his lips for moisture, The Renegade responds.] Renegade: Last I checked, I didn’t have to punch in a time card when I walked into this arena. What I do on my time is my own business. End of story. Richard: Your time is my money, Rollins. You’d do well to remember that. Renegade: Look, Hayes… (he frowns)… when I signed on to work for the UWF, did I come with fancy action accessories? Do I have a pull-cord on my back? Do you see me wearing a dress? I’m not your personal puppet, and if we don’t see eye-to-eye on that then you can take your contract and stick it up your… [Hayes raises his hands in a calming gesture.] Richard: Okay Rollins, okay. I didn’t bring you here to argue. Renegade: Well you sure have a funny way of showing it. [Hayes sighs, folding his arms across his chest. A handful of heartbeats pass between the two of them, Rollins refusing to speak and Hayes waiting for an opening.] [That opening doesn’t present itself, so Hayes drives on anyway.] Richard: Have you ever heard of Primetime Central, Richard? Renegade: Don’t call me Richard. And yes, I’ve competed in PTC in the past. Richard: (surprised) Oh really? Renegade: It was on my application. [Hayes laughs, brushing the comment aside.] Richard: I don’t go through the applications, personally. I have talent scouts that sort through all that junk. But hell, this is getting better and better by the minute! When did you compete in PTC? Renegade: Two years ago, in the two hundred fifty-six man Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Richard: How did you do? Renegade: Look, Hayes… if you wanted to bring me up here and play Twenty Questions, this is a massive waste of my time. Unless you’re offering me something of interest, I’m not buying. Richard: I’m about to make a massive investment in you, Rollins. All I ask in return is to know how qualified you may or may not be for what I may or may not offer you. [The Renegade pauses, sizing up Richard Hayes with a glance that speaks volumes. Less than a week ago, this man signed a stipulation that will force Rollins to defend his Television title in a sixty-man battle royal against his will. This entire encounter was more than a little confusing, and Hayes’ apperant sudden interest in him instantly put The Renegade on his guard. Taking a deep breath, he hesitates before answering.] Renegade: I made it to the sweet sixteen. Now what’s in this for me? [Hayes grins widely, his posture practically radiating a natural sleezy charm.] Richard: PTC is hosting a Survival event, and I want to put you on our four-man team. [Silence.] Renegade: Not interested. Richard: E… excuse me? [All trace of patience has expired from The Renegade’s already sour mood. Rollins responds with his voice down, but his tone is laced with solid steel.] Renegade: You want me to smile and nod and bust my ass to make you look like a big player in front of your peers? You’ve got a lot of nerve, Hayes. You think you can screw me over, practically strip me of my Television title and then stand here and toss me to the wolves to feed your own ego? (he laughs, a bitter sound) Forget it, Hayes. I said I’m not interested. [Turning on his heels, Rich Rollins ignores the obvious shock that has creeped into his bosses’ expression. Pulling the unlit cigarette from behind his ear, The Renegade fishes for his lighter once more as he reaches the massive oak door, preparing to step through.] [It’s just then that Richard Hayes loses his good humor.] Richard: I own your godamned title, Rollins. I own everything with a UWF logo, and more importantly… I own you. [Rollins stops.] [His cigarette now dangling from between his lips, The Renegade’s eyebrows furrow with rage. Both men stand there, staring at one another, the busy city of Chicago bustling about outside without regaurd to the sudden tension between them. People go about below, hailing cabs and taking trains, blasting car horns and sterios and screaming into cell phones. A world of productivity, caught in a moment of unblinking friction between a man and his boss.] [Slowly raising his lighter to his lips, Rollins lights his cigarette.] Renegade: Are you blackmailing me? [With a single breath, Rollins inhales the sweet nicotine and lets go, blowing a thick cloud of smoke directly into Richard Hayes’ face. Blinking away the stinging cloud in anger, Hayes doesn’t crack.] Richard: We can do this my way, or you can pack up your sorry ass and head back into that cavity of obscurity you crawled out of. You think you got a raw deal with your Regal Rumble Television title defense? You ain’t seen nothin’ yet, compadre. I’ll break you in half, so help me Christ. [The Renegade somehow manages to frown even deeper than he already is, taking another drag of his Marlboro. As Hayes stands there, waiting for some kind of response, Rollins notices something about the plush office that he hadn’t noticed before.] [There wasn’t a single plant in this entire room.] [Most offices had the obligatory potted tree, maybe a few hanging vines here and there to add some color. But Richard Hayes’ office was completely devoid of any natural fauna, anything even remotely resembling a fake pile of leaves in a plastic pot. All except for one small item, which is what caught Rollins’ attention in the first place.] [Placed on a bookshelf to his right, its short growth crawling over the form of what appears to be a hedgehog, a fuzzy green Chia-Pet sits basking in the afternoon warmth filtering in through the massive windows.] [Without drawing attention to his findings, The Renegade slowly pushes past Hayes, staring out the window into the Windy City beyond. Without looking at his boss, Rollins responds.] Renegade: I don’t want to be at odds with you, Hayes. Richard: Well, you sure have a funny way of showing it. [Rollins pauses.] Renegade: Touche. [He continues his walk, still focused out the window. The Chia-Pet gets closer, almost within arm’s reach.] Renegade: You want me to dance? I’ll dance. You want to toss me in the ring with sixty other men and make me defend my title? I’m game. And now… now, you want me to throw down with men I’ve never met, men I’ve never heard of, and start knocking heads around like billiard balls? Richard: That’s the idea. [Now directly next to the Chia-Pet, Rollins takes another deep drag of his cigarette, blowing it against the window glass. A wide smile spreads across his features, reaching all the way to his eyes. Turning around, he nods at Richard Hayes.] Renegade: Count me in. Richard: That’s fantastic, Rollins. You made the right ch… Renegade: But one last question… do you feed your little pet here MiracleGrow? [Hayes stops, his expression immediately falling from relieved to confused. Rollins nods his head ever so slightly at the small green hedgehog, ashing the tip of his cigarette on the obviously expensive carpet.] Richard: Excuse me? Renegade: MiracleGrow. It’s quite a bundle of green fur for such a small plant-animal thing, don’t you think? Richard: (perplexed) Well… yeah, I suppose… Renegade: I mean hell, it’s an impressive little plant. It’s quite a shame. Richard: Shame? How so? [Rollins smiles, blowing out one last cloud of thick smoke.] Renegade: Because most plant fertilizers are flammable. [With that, Rollins flicks the remains of his cigarette into the Chia-Pet’s pot. The smoldering stick immediately responds to the chemical coating all over the little green hedgehog’s backside, instantly igniting a flame that jumps up almost an inch, licking the bottom of the next shelf above it. A wordless cry emanates from Hayes as he lanches toward the flaming Chia-Pet, swatting at the fire with his expensive suit jacket.] [Grinning from ear to ear, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins saunters back towards the door.] Renegade: I’m always a team player, Richard. [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
