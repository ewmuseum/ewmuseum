International what?

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 Not really…now sit down and buckle up as I tell you about my stint in the Navy. Man this story rocks. Pat: Man can you believe they let us loose for 2 weeks! In JAPAN! What the hell are we going to do in Japan? Ricky: This is so gonna rock! The two friends grab the bags and haul ass into the bustling city. Ricky points out a small hotel, where the two decide they will make their stand. They head inside and take notice to the small Buddha statue on the counter. Pat: I thought that guy was Chinese or something… Ricky: You’re an idiot. Ricky laughs as he rings the little bell for service. A small woman in a kimono appears, her skin fairly light complex, but her eyes just the opposite, dark as midnight without a moon. Ricky is intrigued by the woman’s look. He stares before he is nudged to reality by Pat, who is just standing there grinning. Ricky: I’m sorry. Japanese Woman: 私がいかにかもしれないか助けなさい Ricky: uhh. Pat: Do you speak Japanese? Ricky: Not even a little… Pat: Mind if I try? Ricky: Be my guest! Pat steps up to the counter, and smiles at the woman who simply smiles back…Pat clears his throat and slowly starts to speak. Pat: We…are…Americans… Pat looks back and Ricky and gives him a thumbs up. Pat: We..need..a..room The Japanese woman stands there almost in shock, and then starts to speak again. Japanese Woman: O..K..One..Bed..Or..Two? Ricky shoves Pat out of the way and steps back up to the counter. Ricky: Excuse my friend he is a little slow. And we would like TWO rooms. Not every rumor about the navy is true ya know. The woman chuckles a bit as she gathers their ID’s and punches some numbers into the computer, and activates to key cards and hands the over. Woman: You are in room 305. She then points at Pat, and hands him his key… Woman: And….you…..are…..in…..306. Pat takes his key and storms off in a huff as the woman in left standing there smiling, which also brings a smile to Ricky’s face. Ricky looks around then back at the cute woman in front of him. Ricky: So…uhh….would you want to … Woman: 私は英語を話さない Ricky stares blankly as the woman smiles and walks away… Ricky: Well I guess that’s a no. Ricky gathers up his room key and heads upstairs. He gets to the elevator where Pat is still standing. He glances at the button that is not lit up. Ricky: Did you push the button? Pat: Damnit! I was wondering why it was taking so long! Ricky: Moron. Ricky pushes the button and the doors slide open. The two friends step into the elevator, and stand quietly as the doors close and Pat hits floor 3. There is a quiet music flowing from the ceiling as Pat speaks out again. Pat: Sounds like a Chinese restaurant. Ricky: … The doors slide open and the two men start down the hallway, where Ricky spots his room and slides the key card in and pushes the door open. Ricky enters and closes the door behind him as he tosses his bag on the floor in the closet. Ricky collapses on the bed and flips on the TV. Ricky: Finally some good ole Americ….. Ricky is interrupted by the strange voices coming from the TV. He sits upright and flips through the channels pretty quickly. He sighs dejectedly as he turns off the TV and tosses the remote on the bed. Ricky lies back again and no sooner then he does the phone ring. Ricky: uhh…hello? Pat: Ricky! Let’s go grab some beer dude! Ricky: Where? Pat: This is a big ass city; they have to have some beer here somewhere! Ricky: Ok, gimme a few to get dressed. Pat: No problem, 10 minutes? Ricky: Sounds good. Ricky hangs up the phone and stands up stretching when he hears a knock at his door. Ricky stares confused and opens it to find pat standing there. Pat: Ready? Ricky: Can’t tell time you dick? Pat: Sorry, I’m thirsty! Let’s roll! Ricky just grabs his jacket and heads out the door with his friend. They get to the elevator and head downstairs through the semi-elaborate hotel, and out onto the bustling streets of the city. Pat points across the street at a neon Budweiser sign. The two have found their homestead. Ricky: Good enough for me. The two run across the traffic filled streets ducking cars as they clear the traffic. They enter the bar and the classic movie scene breaks. The bar falls silent and the record scratches. Which was weird, because there was no DJ…? Ricky: Hmmm, maybe we should….Pat? Ricky looks to the bar where pat is standing, and is suddenly clubbed in the back of the head with a beer bottle. Ricky jumps into the brawl and the bar turns to hell. The door swings open as several Navy men enter from the street, and see Ricky & Pat fighting. One of them makes a quick call from a bag phone, and they join the ruckus. Within a short time the bar is flooded with Navy men rushing to the aid of their comrades. The brawl spills out into the streets where the police show up to intervene. 2 Hours Later Voice: Thunder, you’re outta here! The cell door slides open as Ricky Thunder is released. He makes his way up the stairs through booking and finds his superior standing there. Boss Man: Thunder, you get your sorry ass back to the boat and report to my office ASAP. Ricky: Yessir! So I was accused of starting an international incident even though all I did was help Pat out. Not sure if he popped off at the lip or if he just had that one coming. But yah, I got tagged with this dishonorable discharge for it, which I wasn’t too happy about, but hey…what can ya do? You are probably asking why I told you that story, and here is why. I have a knack for ending up in tight spots, and no way out. I couldn’t talk my way out of that. I couldn’t pretend it never happened. Hell I can’t even pretend I came out on top. I got my ass kicked. But one thing for sure…I made headlines. I may not be the smartest, strongest, biggest dog in the yard….but goddamnit you’ll hear about me when it’s all said and done!
