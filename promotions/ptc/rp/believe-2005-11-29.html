Believe

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 Believe is defined as to accept as being true or real. Belief in something is something that you must choose to do on your own, such as believing in yourself when no one else does. You must believe that the best is yet to come, even if you are so completely out of control, you are considered to be the new Jim Hellwig of the wrestling business. Or, to put it more precise, the new Warrior of the wrestling business, considering how said person changed their name legally so they could be their character. But belief is more than just a simple word. It's a way of being. Belief is believing that you are better than everyone else in, oh say, a tournament of doom called GTT5. But everybody already knows this… They also already know that Pete the PG Tips Chimp is not related to the Legend of Pete. Pete is potty trained. No, the first Pete. Which Pete am I talking about? Nobody knows. Because when you are so completely out of control… Anything you say becomes rational. I can come on here and say "Jorge Samuelsson is a Mexican Viking who clubs baby seals" and you'll all believe that I think it to be true. Why? Because you don't BELIEVE in me. I'm too out of control. But do you actually, truly, in your heart of hearts, believe in James von Doom? Do you actually believe what I'm saying? No, you probably don't. You're all too busy giving your boyfriends oral or sitting there watching Spongebob Squarepants and not giving anything I say a second thought. Oh sure, I can come on here and say "Spartan eats children" and you'll believe me. Why? Because James von Doom said it, it must be true. Well, here's some more for ya wrestling nerds… "Seymour Almasy plays with dolls while eating peanut butter." "Angelo Deville had sex with a man but was sickened when he realized it was simply Karina Wolfenden." "Axel Action has no penis." But, who will believe me when I say the following statement… "James von Doom will win GTT5." … Nobody. Because nobody believes in me other than myself. So, to all of you doubters out there and to all of my potential opponents in the tournament, I leave you with these words of wisdom… "Go look at boobie magazines and play with your penis."
