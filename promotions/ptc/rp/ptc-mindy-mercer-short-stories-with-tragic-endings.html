Short Stories With Tragic Endings

30 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-May 18- It was disbelief that kept Jimmy Stone awake that night. The doctors gave him approximately one day to live- or have a steady stream of consciousness, anyway- as his body began to lose its strength; fading to the will of the cancer in his lungs. Jimmy's parents always warned him about what would happen if he didn't kick that smoking habit, but if the group of rebellious teenagers that he befriended had chosen not to listen to their others, why should he? It was that karma that left those others outside partying nonstop, while he sat in reflection in the emptiness of a cold room on nineteen-sixty North Date street: the Ben Archer Health Facility in Truth or Consequence, New Mexico. It just wasn't fair. Sure, he disobeyed the wishes of others, but did that mean he had to pay his life in full in return? Whether Jimmy agreed to such a stipulation, however, did not matter. It would not, and it could not change the fact that he was going to die. Still, the refusal in Jimmy's mind to take the truth and run with it kept him from crying himself to sleep on that dreadful late Tuesday night. Julia and Eric weren't there at his bedside, and if they were not there, it must have been good news. They couldn't possibly miss their own son's last hours of life, could they? Furthermore, he still felt as though he could jump up and burst out into song. Perhaps his soul wouldn't have enjoyed it so much, but that wasn't to say his body wouldn't allow it. Other than the weight of bad news being carried on his shoulders, there were no ailments that Jimmy Stone could sense in his body. As far as he was concerned, he felt just the way he always did; and in that sense, it somewhat scared him. Even if Jimmy didn't want to believe it, he had to admit that doctors wouldn't have teased him about such a horrid fate if it wasn't true... "This is just so.. fucking.. unreal." Jimmy took the golden cross that hung from his neck on its matching chain, and ripped it off. Without a second thought, he sat up, and with every bit of strength in him, threw his thirteenth birthday present from his own mother out of an open third story window. He threw his head back and into the uncomforting flatness of the pillow underneath him, all the while mumbling obscenities. They were no worse than curses that would be heard during an R rated movie, but the hurt and sorrow behind each word made them mean so much more. "Fucking God... there is no God. If there is, then that son of a bitch should go to Hell for making me suffer like this. That fucking asshole." It was next to impossible for him to growl that sentence without so much as shedding a single tear, but somehow he managed. His green eyes blinked repeatedly, forcing the water away, but the temptation to break down and bawl right then and there was overwhelming. "I'm seventeen years old... what the shit did I ever do to anybody? I got good grades in high school, I started a band that people loved, I did everything for my parents... what did I ever do to deserve this, huh? I had a few cigarettes.. is that such a sin that I have to die by it?" Sure, it might have been the last thing that Jimmy wanted, but it was what he was going to get. The more he thought about that lack of fairness, the harder he bit his bottom lip in disgust. The bitterness in the teenager's mind ate away at him, until he had no more control. Disregarding the chances of anyone hearing him, Jimmy cried like a baby. The last time he had ever cried so much was at his grandfather's funeral, but even then, it wasn't as though he was crying for his own death. Hell, he had no reason to think about death back then. But here, it was him crying soon before people would be gathering at his funeral. And then that door to his room opened, and Jimmy was brought to silence. The tears continued to stream down his face, without a care in the world for what whoever on the other side of that door had thought of him. "You'll be just fine," whispered the nurse, as a young woman walked slowly into the room, holding her head. "Just rest, and I'll get doctor Henderson to come up in just a moment." In a voice that was strangely familiar to one that Jimmy knew, the black and pink haired patient replied. "Okie dokie, thank ya." 'For fuck's sake,' thought Jimmy coldly, 'just what I need. A chick in the same room with me while I'm crying my eyes out.' The girl plopped down into the bed next to Jimmy's, and turned to face him. Floppy pigtails, vibrant green eyes, cherry red lips curled into a pained, yet hopeful smile... and suddenly, it was more than the girl's voice that was familiar. 'Shit...' "Do I..." Jimmy started, but soon trailed off when the girl's eyes concentrated on him. "Er... sorry, nevermind." Mindy propped herself up on her right elbow, and tilted her head in confusion. "Hm? No hello or nothin'? Some roommate you are." She grinned innocently, her bubbly eyes eminating the most beautiful gaze Jimmy had ever seen. She was the person he was thinking she was, although he was way too chicken shit to question it to her. He remembered the one other time he met a wrestler, and it was a nightmare. 'Ultimo Dragon... fucker looks away when my friend tries taking a picture with me and him. That bastard...' "Sorry," Jimmy answered meekly, "I'm not really in the mood to greet anyone." Slowly, Mindy's smile turned upside down. It was really the first time he had seen her with a look of sadness, other than the times Mike Biddle had bothered her. Jimmy knew better, though; everyone looks sad when they encounter Mike Biddle. Mindy said something then, that surprised Jimmy a little bit. "Yeah, that nice nurse lady told me you weren't doing so well..." Mindy looked like she wanted to say 'I'm sorry', but just couldn't find the courage to do it. Instead, she finished the sentence another way. "She didn't mean to have you bothered.. she just thought that you'd like it if I came in and talked with ya." 'So that is her...' Jimmy forced a smile out of his 'once-thought-to-be-permanent' frown, and shrugged. "Well... I'm glad I'll spend these times with somebody." Mindy took note of the small smile Jimmy had on his face, and she tried to mimic the same expression. It gave Jimmy the heart to push on into conversation. "I'm surprised that nurse would do something nice for me.. whenever I see you on the TV, she can never get me to shut up. Thought I annoyed the crap outta her." Mindy giggled. It was tough for her not to blush from that compliment, but she couldn't help herself. Jimmy noticed the shade of red on the nineteen year old's otherwise pale cheeks, and his smile got slightly wider. It felt good being able to flatter someone famous- especially one who was only two years older than him. "Aww, I'm sure you're not the type who could annoy anyone. If anyone can annoy people, that would be me!" Jimmy didn't think he would be able to chuckle during any of these past few days, but somehow he managed it at that moment. He had to; it was the first time he would meet her, and unless a miracle happened, it would be the only time. "Eh, I wouldn't jump the gun there. I can be quite annoying if the time calls for it." Mindy eyed the younger boy suspiciously, and pouted. "Oh yeah? Lemme hear you be annoying." Jimmy blinked. He looked at Mindy as if to ask 'are you sure about that?', but upon getting no answer- physical or verbal- in return, he decided to put his most annoying antics to the test. "....I'm Henry the Eighth, I am, Henry the Eighth I am, I am.. I got married to the widow next door..." Mindy's hands clutched her ears in an instant. Oh, how she despised that song. "Okay okay, you're right, you're right! Cut it out!" Both teens shared a laugh together as if they knew eachother for years. It was a moment Jimmy wished he could keep himself in forever.. anything to prevent time from moving forward. Upon knowing it was safe for her to remove her hands from her ears, Mindy gently rubbed her forehead. It was still ringing from that match she had that night, and for good reason. 'Stupid Jake Hix. Stupid three hundred pound idiot.... stupid me for losing to him.' The mere thought of replaying that loss in her mind generated physical pain. She had a man two hundred pounds heavier than her on the ropes, and she knew it... she just let herself get carried away, and she lost herself the match. There were no other excuses; she and she alone lost that match. "Something wrong?" Mindy was abruptly shaken from her thoughts. "Mew?" She glanced at the black haired boy across from her, and smiled. "Nah, hun, nothing's wrong." After years of experience in talking to women, Jimmy obviously knew by that response that there was something indeed wrong. He tried thinking of what it could be, and it was then that he remembered... "Oh that's right.. Wrestlecade was tonight, wasn't it?" Ouch. Slowly, Mindy nodded her head. "Yup." Jimmy noticed the hesitation in Mindy's answer, and frowned. It could really only mean one thing, and it was reinforced by Jimmy's sudden realization that Mindy was without her Television Title. "...You lost, huh?" Again, Mindy slowly nodded her head. Sure, she was only a wrestler for a good five months, but it was still tough to admit that she had her first real loss at the biggest Global Wrestling Pay Per View of the year- so much that it felt damn near humiliating. Not being one to want to have a woman depressed with him, he quickly tried to cheer the usually hyper teenage vixen. "Hey, it's no big deal. I mean damn.. you're the smallest wrestler there, and you never really got pinned until tonight!" "Well, I didn't exactly get pinned tonight, either," Mindy corrected him. Jimmy gulped. "Man.. you tapped out?" Mindy shook her head. "Well.. then what?" "Knocked out," Mindy sighed. Jimmy recoiled slightly from that answer, before a smile crossed his face. "Good to know a chick that has to be knocked out to stay down." There went that warm blush of Mindy's again. "Eh heh.. thanks." Jimmy's smile remained, until he remembered where it was that they were both talking. "You're not like... really hurt, are you?" Mindy sensed the concern in the boy's eyes and voice, and grinned. "Me? Nah. I'm just here to make sure I didn't get dropped on mah head too hard or nuffin'." Jimmy nodded in appreciation of knowing that Mindy was alright. At least one of them would be out of that hospital the next morning, why not let it be the nicest girl that he ever knew- without really knowing her? "Can I ask you one question?" Mindy grinned, twirling a finger in one of her pigtails. "Sure, hun, ask away!" "Okay... I dunno how to really ask this, so I'll just go out and ask. The Mindy Mercer I see on television.. is that the real Mindy? Like.. is it the one I'm talking to now?" Mindy tilted her head once more, a bit puzzled by what Jimmy was asking her. It honestly did take her a moment to understand the question, before she managed to field a response. "Well the only person I can be is me! What ever would I gain by being a total bitch all the time?" Jimmy laughed. Mindy's answer was a motto that he wished that he had applied to his life; it was too bad, however, that he hadn't heard it sooner. "Well, I'm glad that some people can still have that outlook on life nowadays." The former champion paused for a moment, looking at Jimmy with a bit of sadness. She knew what that remark had meant, and she wanted desperately to say something comforting. Of course, being taken, she couldn't exactly proclaim love for the boy, but she needed to give him some kind of hope; something that would at least keep his spirits brightened for... whenever those last few moments came. Struggling to get out of her bed, Mindy pulled herself to the edge and crawled out, ambling over to Jimmy's bedside. Stunned, all Jimmy could do was blush heavily as Mindy hugged him and gave him a kiss on the forehead. "I have no idea what you must be going through right now, and I'm not gonna pretend ta. But I will say that I feel terrible that you have ta go through it at all." Jimmy was naturally touched by that. To have someone so famous be so caring to him- even moreso than his own mother and father, no less- was the biggest relief that he could ask for. It almost made him feel happy, even though it was still a bitch to swallow the fact that it took him coming up with cancer for someone to care at all for him. "Eh.. if my parents were here at least, it would be easier. If anyone I knew came here, actually.." "Well, you know me!" Jimmy nodded slowly. "Yeah, yeah I do.. I just.. I don't know you like I know all my friends that I've been with since high school and what not." Mindy understood all too well what Jimmy meant. It felt horrible knowing that no matter what she could think of, nothing could improve Jimmy's condition. Here he was, wishing for the presence of people who hadn't gave a damn enough to so much as see their friend or family member for the last time- it sickened her to think that she cared more than those people after a simple five minute conversation. "Eh.. parents aren't all that important. My stepmother and stepfather are perfect examples of that, really." "Yeah?" "Yeah..." Jimmy grew a bit bold. "Would you mind telling me about them?" The young girl thought Jimmy would never ask. Smiling, she sat back down over the edge of her bed and opened her mouth to speak, when that door behind her opened once more. "Sorry about that," the blonde nurse stated apologetically, "the doctor will be up in just a minute, he was finishing up with another patient." "S'okay," Mindy answered cutely as she turned to face the young woman, before the door closed once more. Mindy took a couple of deep breaths, and then her eyes fell back upon Jimmy Stone. "So.. about my stepparents..." They talked about their families the rest of that night, neither one of them going to sleep. The next morning when Mindy left, Jimmy was still very much alive. They said their goodbyes, and Mindy Mercer was soon a memory. Mindy knew how much that time she spent with Jimmy had meant, and she wasn't going to let it be in vain. Primetime Central, the wrestling ring Global was a part of, was in talks of opening the Infinite Gauntlet. Through this, Mindy was going to prove that she would not let her loss the night before keep her from fighting on. She had to continue. It was the right thing to do for both her, and those like Jimmy Stone; those who would never even have the option to continue with the most important thing of all: their lives.
