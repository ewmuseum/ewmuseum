Training Hard?

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 Another year and another GTT, which can only mean one thing. George W. Bush is going to invade PTC looking for Weapons on Mass Destruction? Unfortunately, no, it means its time for me to crawl out from under that rock I call NGW and make another attempt at PTC fame. To try and climb that preverbal mountain, hoping not to loose my footing and fall into that valley of disappointment that so many superstars will visit. Its well known that I am no stranger to failure in this tournament, three straight GTTs and I’m 0-3, unless you count that spectacular victory over "bye" in GTT4. This year the string of bad luck will come to an end and that means hitting the gym and training my ass off. Late That Night ::The shot opens and we see a slightly dented can of Budweiser sitting on a table, suddenly it’s swatted out of the frame by a hand. The camera pans out and we see Scottywood sitting back in a chair, remote in one hand, flipping through channels.:: Scottywood: Frankie! Another beer! I need to keep up my training. Frankie: Don’t you think you’ve had enough? You’ve been watching static on the TV for the past 5 minutes, and hitting the volume button trying to change it. Scottywood: I was wondering why this show sucked…..its almost as bad as watching Frankie wrestle. But don’t tell him I said that though. Frankie: I’m right here. Scottywood: Good you can get me another beer. Frankie: Really I think you’ve had enough. Scottywood: I’m only on my like 6th or 7th. Frankie: No, your somewhere in the 20’s, I lost count after you threw that telephone at me for disagreeing with you that Peter Griffin isn’t a real person. Scottywood: I don’t care what you say, I saw him in person on TV. Now get me another beer. ::Frankie gets up from his seat and opens up the refrigerator as he pulls out another can of Budweiser and walks it over to Scotty’s waiting hand.:: Scottywood: Damn, I’ve been beerless for like a whole minute….Frankie, get me a beer. Frankie: I just gave you one, your holding it right now. Scottywood: Shit…..your quick, like magic, your like David Balinifield. Now get me another beer before this one is empty. Frankie: You haven’t even opened that one yet! Errrrr…Your damn lucky I’m having a problem falling asleep tonight, or else you’d be screwed and honestly what kind of training do you call this? You’re about to enter the biggest tournament of the year and your getting drunk off your ass. Scottywood: Its called the Budweiser program……or something like that. I read it in some fitness magazine….or was it in a dream I had….oh well. And if your having problems sleeping then take some of those pills on the counter, they will help. Frankie: Really? I’ll give them a try, cause I really need some sleep ::Frankie gets up again and walks to the counter where he opens up the bottle and takes two of the red pills out. He grabs a glass of water from the sink and takes the two pills.:: Frankie: Well I guess I’m gonna go to bed, hopefully these pills will work. Scottywood: Ya, I think I’ll hit the sack too, this TV show is so damn boring. Frankie: That’s still snow Scotty. Scottywood: No wonder I was getting cold, well see ya tomorrow….afternoon, hehe. ::Scotty slowly gets up from his chair, barely being able to stand as he stumbles the 20 feet over to his bedroom door which takes him a couple of attempts to open. He finally succeeds and stumbles into his bedroom where he collapses onto his bed.:: The next morning ::Scotty’s eyes slowly open, the light entering the window sends shockwaves of pain through his head. Scotty rolls over in his bed and squints at his clock to see the time, as he sees the red LCD screen read 11:47. Scotty moans as he rolls back over and pulls the sheets closer to his body. As he tries to go back to sleep he starts to hear a loud noise coming from the living room.:: Scottywood: Fuckin eh ::He tries to block the noise by pulling the sheets up and over his head, but the almost sound of what seems to be cartoons penetrates the sheets as Scotty’s grows more and more aggravated. Finally he tosses the sheets to the side and sits up in his bed, placing his hand on the side of his head in response to the headache.:: Scottywood: I’m gonna fuckin kill someone. ::Scotty stands up from his bed, realizing he just has a pair of shorts on, when he was wearing a t-shirt and jeans when he passed out on his bed. He shakes his head not even caring to know as he walks across his room wincing from the pain of his throbbing head. He makes it over to the door, which he opens up and looks out into the living room to see Frankie sitting on the couch watching TV and scribbling into a notebook. At the sound of the door opening Frankie’s head snaps around to see Scotty standing in the doorway as Frankie starts waving.:: Frankie: Morning Scotty, what’s up? ::Scotty just stares at Frankie for a moment with a blank face, trying to keep somewhat calm, until he just can’t hold it in.:: Scottywood: What the fuck are you doing? It’s 11 in the morning and your blasting cartoons. I’m trying to sleep off my damn hangover and it isn’t easy with this noise. Frankie: Sorry, but last night I still wasn’t able to sleep after I took those pills, so I went and took two more, then I looked at the bottle and saw they were pills that keep ya awake. So I started bouncing off the walls, literally and I thought maybe I’ll do some research on your GTT competition. So I went to Wal-Mart and bought me, with your credit card, Winnie The Pooh box set. I’ve been watching it for the past 4 hours and I have a whole notebook full of notes on them…. Scottywood: What the he…… Frankie: Everyone makes such a big deal about Pooh, but he really looks like a push over bitch. Tigger now, he’s gonna be tough to beat, he has that powerful tail that could probably knock you out. Then ya got Piglet, at first glance he….she…..it…. ::Scottywood just starts shaking his head:: Scottywood: Maybe Piglet is a cross dresser. Frankie: Yes! That would make perfect sense. It’s Disney’s fault for all those cross dressing freaks. But back to Piglet, it may look like an innocent little pig, but I’m sure it’s just a front. I bet he is really a sadistic little fuck, so watch out for him….her…whatever….. Scottywood: Right…..you do know that those are only the names of the brackets. Plus they are just cartoons, they are not real. Frankie: So who are you fighting in round one? Scottywood: Token Weed from Asylum Wrestling. Frankie: So instead of drinking all that beer maybe we should have been getting high. Scottywood: Maybe you shouldn’t be watching Winnie the Pooh, and pot is the last thing you need right now. Though it may help put some weight on ya. But turn that crap off and get some sleep, when those pills wear off your gonna crash. ::Frankie shuts off the TV and gets up from the couch as he walks over to Scottywood and hands him the notebook he was writing in as he turns and walks into the guest bedroom closing the door behind him.:: Scottywood: I really need to meet his parents one day, how could two parents fuck up so badly on one kid. ::Scotty looks down at the notepad that Frankie handed him and he can’t even make out what the writing is, seems to be just gibberish and random words. He flips through the pages and it is just more of the same. Shaking his head Scotty tosses the notebook into the trashcan.:: Scottywood: Maybe I should check into getting him some Ritalin. Now where is the Aspirin, this headache ain’t feelin any better. ::From inside the room we can hear the sounds of what probably is Frankie jumping on his bed. Because God knows he isn’t getting laid in there. Suddenly there is a loud crashing noise and everything is quite as Scott chuckles to himself.:: Scottywood: That will keep him asleep for a while. Later That Day ::Frankie opens the door to the guest room as he stumbles into the living room, his hand holding his forehead as he starts to moan in pain which causes Scotty to turn his head from his computer screen.:: Scottywood: Sleep well? I didn’t even hear you talking in your sleep. Frankie: I guess, but my head is killing me, everything is sorta blurry and it really hurts up here. ::Frankie removes his hand from his forehead revealing a half dollar sized bruise as Scotty starts to chuckle to himself.:: Frankie: What’s so funny, I can’t even remember how I could have gotten it. Did I try and wrestle you again? Scottywood: No, you were jumping on the bed and probably smacked your head on the backboard. Frankie: And you just left me there? Scottywood: And risk you waking back up? Hell no. Think I wanted to watch Winnie the Pooh all night with a hyperactive cameraman? But since you’re up now I guess we better take you to the ER and get you checked out. You probably have a concussion. Frankie: Good, just as long as we don’t go to the hospital. Those guys in the white robes are creepy. Scottywood: Frankie……the ER is….part of……ya know what, just grab your shit and lets go. ::Frankie stumbles back into the guest room to grab his wallet and shoes as Scotty shakes his head as he walks over to the counter to get his keys.:: Scottywood: Just what he needs, more brain damage. 2 Hours Later at the NYU Medical Center ER ::Scottywood and Frankie sit alone in one of the small rooms inside the NYU ER. Frankie has on one of the ugly blue gowns as paces around the room playing with every little object he can get his hands on.:: Scottywood: If you break anything I’m deducting it from your paycheck. I’m not gonna pay again for you breaking shit, like last time in Toys R Us. Frankie: I really didn’t think that was gonna knock the whole shelf over. Scottywood: That why you don’t race Power Wheels in a store. Frankie: I had that race won too, it wasn’t for that stray Barbie on the floor. I still think someone put it there on purpose. They knew my weakness. ::Finally the door opens and in walks a man in his upper 50’s, with a white coat and stethoscope around his neck. He walks over to Scottywood and shakes his hand.:: Doctor: Hello Mr. Woodson, I’m Doctor Nelson. Scottywood: Hey, so what did the CAT scan say? Frankie: I thought it was gonna have something to do with a kitty, I was a bit disappointed. Doctor: Well it did confirm he has a concussion, but it’s not a very serious one. I wrote a prescription here for him, just get it filled tonight and he should be back to…..normal in a weeks time. Scottywood: Ha, ya the words normal and Frankie really don’t go all that well together. Doctor: Yes I see, I had one other question, has he taken one too many chair shots to head or has he always been on the…..slow side? Scottywood: He’s been like this as long as I’ve known him; I think he was just raised weird by his parents. Though I’m sure all the shots to the head he has taken over the past few years hasn’t helped. Doctor: Well that is the down side to professional wrestling, huge chance of injuries. I mean we have seen more then our share of you NGW guys here over the years. Honestly I’m surprised a few of you, especially yourself aren’t dead or in a coma. Scottywood: Well I guess I can say I’ve been extremely lucky in my career, worst I’ve ever had was a knife wound…..But anyhow, thank you Doctor for chec….. ::Suddenly Scotty and the Doctor hear a choking noise and they turn to Frankie who has the blood pressure monitor around his neck and pumped full of air so that it is strangling him. The Doctor runs over and releases the air pressure as Frankie coughs and gasps for air.:: Scottywood: Do I even wanna ask what the hell you were doing? I turn my back from you for a moment and you almost kill yourself. Frankie: I wanted to see if it was stronger then a sleeper hold…. ::Scotty just shakes his head as he turns back to the Doctor.:: Scottywood: Like I was saying, thanks for checking him out, and sorry bout the blood pressure monitor. Doctor: No problem Mr. Woodson. And Mr. Clarrisson, you get some rest. I don’t wanna see you in the ring for at least a week, and then I want you to get a check up to clear you. If you take another shot to the head this soon you could do some real damage. Frankie: Ok Doctor, will do. It will give me a chance to watch my Fraggle Rock DVDs. Doctor: Right…..Well you take care Mr. Woodson and Mr. Clarrisson. Scottywood: Thanks Doctor Nelson. ::The Doctor walks out of the room as Scotty shakes his head again and turns to Frankie smacking him in the back of the head.:: Scottywood: Now get your clothes on and lets go. Frankie: I think I’m gonna wear the blue robe out, I think it looks good on me. Scottywood: Whatever, lets just go then. Frankie: Ok, oh, do you think they will mind if I take this container of cotton balls? Scottywood: Why do you want a container of cotton balls?…….Second thought, I don’t even wanna know. ::Frankie grabs his clothes and the container of cotton balls as the two of the exit the ER room.:: Well so much for training my ass off, but I did make a killing off bottle returns. Anyhow, all that leaves me to do is to step into the ring with Token Weed and to turn him into the next masterpiece of The Hardcore Artist. One may fear that since we’re not fighting under hardcore rule that I may be at a disadvantage, but NGW fans don’t worry. Just because you won’t see me swinging a hockey stick or digging barbwire into someone arm, doesn’t mean the blood of Token Weed won’t be spilled onto my canvas.
