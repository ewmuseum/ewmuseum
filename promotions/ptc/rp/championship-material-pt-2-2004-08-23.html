Championship Material pt.2

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 ~~~{ When we last left off with the Shadow Riders, we found out that LOW had appointed a babysitter to keep them in line and out of trouble until their PTC Tag Team title match against the New Royal Legion at PTC Survival. From there the Shadow Riders decided to duck out on their babysitter in favor of heading out to the famed Score’s strip club in New York much against the wishes of the LOW. Little did they know however that at the same time, the LOW appointed babysitter was in the process of tracking them down. Then in a strange turn of events, we found out that the LOW appointed babysitter was none other then the Shadow Riders’ mentor the Snake. Taking charge like only he can, the Snake dragged the Shadow Riders out of the strip club to begin their preparation for their match. This however did not happen without a mishap of it’s own. What exactly was this mishap? Let’s find out now in Championship Material pt.2 }~~~ ::The scene opens inside of the emergency room of a hospital. A buzz caries through the air as a few nurses and doctors in bleached white uniforms race back and forth, scurrying from room to room. Suddenly a man’s painful scream pierces the air, temporarily catching the attention of a few workers. Within seconds however, they resume their various duties. The shot now begins to move toward the door to one of the many examining rooms. As it does so, we hear a man’s voice escape the room once again.:: Voice: “Ooooow! Take it easy you bastard! I’m gonna kick you’re a$$ when this is done.” ::As the shot approaches the door where the voice had just come from, we hear the voice of another man as he responds in a calm and even tone.:: Voice Two: “Mr. Kage, you’re going to have to calm down. I know it’s painful but the bone has to be set before we can put a cast on it so that it heals properly.” ::The shot now turns into the doorway of the room as we a re finally given a view of its occupants. Inside the small examination room, one half of the Shadow Riders, Jason Kage, sits atop an examination table. In front of him a young doctor sits on a small stool holding onto the wrestler’s arm. Off in the corner of the room Kage’s tag team partner, Eric Russo stands reading a Pro Wrestling Insider magazine. Back in the center of the room, the doctor in front of Kage now runs his hand along the Shadow Rider’s arm resulting in yet another scream in pain. With a sharp look, the doctor raises his head and glances at his patient as he begins to speak.:: Doctor: “You’re lucky I can fix this with a closed reduction of the fracture. If the break wasn’t clean, I’d have to go in and insert a few pins or a plate. In fact if you keep screaming and squirming, there’s still a chance that you may end up chipping the bone and making me do that anyway.” ::The prospect of needing his arm cut open to repair the damage is enough to quickly quiet Kage back down. Knowing that his plan to shut his patient up has succeeded, the doctor goes back to the task of setting the bone with a sly smile. After another moment of fiddling with the wrestler’s arm, the doctor stands up from his stool as he speaks.:: Doctor: “There it’s set. Now all we have to do is put the cast on. Just wait here and keep your arm still. I’m going to go and get the materials, put the cast on, then you can get out of here. I’ll be right back.” ::As the doctor turns and exits the room, Russo lowers his magazine and approaches his tag team partner as he begins to speak.:: Russo: “So, how does it feel?” ::Kage shoots an evil glance at his partner as he responds.:: Kage: “It feels like my arm is getting a great massage. How the hell do you think it feels!? My arm is throbbing, I think it would hurt less if it was chopped off!” ::Russo backs off a few steps as he continues.:: Russo: “Jeez, calm down. I just wanted to know if you’re gonna be able to wrestle like that. We do have a pretty big match coming up.” ::Hearing this, Kage lets out a little chuckle.:: Kage: “If it were any other team, any other match, I’d be concerned. We’re talking about the New Royal Legion though.” ::With a smile, Russo agrees with his partner.:: Russo: “That is true, we could walk through the New Royal Legion with now problem even if both of us had broken arms. I just wanted to make sure that you were up to it though.” ::Nodding his head, Kage responds.:: Kage: “Yeah, I’m up to it. Don’t worry about that. Only thing we have to worry about is which one of us gets which PTC tag title belt.” Russo: “More important then that though is which tag titles we wear to the ring in LOW. I mean we’ve held the LOW ones so many times that it would be strange not to have them on. Then again, the PTC tag team titles are the PTC tag team titles. Decisions, decisions.” ::Russo’s comments seem to lift Kage’s spirits as he laughs at the prospect of deciding which title belts to wear down to the ring. He then responds with a smile.:: Kage: “It’s simple, we’ll wear one over each shoulder. Then we don’t have to decide.” ::Just as Kage finishes his sentence, the young doctor re-enters the room with a smile on his face, carrying a tray of various rolls of materials and a bowl of water. As he approaches Kage, he begins to speak.:: Doctor: “ OK, you ready to get this over with?” ::His mood now significantly improved due to his conversation with Russo, Kage responds to the doctor’s question with a smile.:: Kage: “Cast away doc.” ::Obeying Kage’s wishes, the doctor reaches out and once again grabs hold of the wrestler’s injured arm. With yet another loud scream from Kage, the scene begins to fade out.:: ********************************LATER********************************** ~~~{ After Kage’s arm was wrapped inside of it’s cast, the Shadow Riders made their way out of the hospital and hopped into a limo and headed straight for the airport. At the airport, they boarded their private jet in hopes a some serious relaxation time before their match for the PTC Tag Team titles. It seems however that their mentor, the Snake, had something else in mind. Waiting for Russo and Kage on their jet along with the other half of the Shadow Rider stable in the form of Luke Justice and Brody Dahmer. The Snake felt his anger toward the present situation building to a breaking point. How could the Shadow Riders expect to win their match acting the way they were? Maybe it was about time that he put them in their place and let them know exactly what needed to happen. }~~~ ::The scene reopens in the plush interior of the Shadow Rider’s jet. We can see that it is some time in the early morning hours of the day by the streams of light shining through the cabin windows. As the camera continues to pan around the room, we see the door to the backroom swing open. Eric Russo storms into the main cabin of the jet followed closely by Jason Kage. Right behind them the Snake stomps after them, slamming the door shut behind him as he enters the cabin in a rage.:: Snake: “I can’t believe you two! The chance of a lifetime and you go out and get Kage injured! Do you two even care about winning this match!?” ::Furious that the Snake would even ask a question like that, Russo spins around to confront him.:: Russo: “Of course we care if we win or lose this match! We wouldn’t have come this far if we didn’t care about it! We’re just not concerned with it, why should we be!? We’re the Shadow Riders, we can go toe to toe with any other team out there any time we damn well please. Worrying about the New Royal Legion would be a waste of our time!” ::Hearing Russo’s explanation of the situation, the snake shakes his head as he calms himself. He then begins to speak in a serious tone.:: Snake: “If that’s what you really think then you’re beyond my help.” ::The Snake quietly turns and begins to walk toward the cabin door. Seeing this drastic change in his demeanor catches the attention of both Shadow Riders. Russo and Kage quickly look to each other and exchange a look of concern. Kage then calls out to his mentor.:: Kage: “Snake . . . wait a minute.” ::Knowing that his ploy worked, a slick smirk of slides across the grizzled veteran’s face for a moment before he turns around, once again resuming his dejected demeanor.:: Snake: “What?” Kage: “What do you mean by what you just said?” ::Baiting his students in, the Snake only hints at what he is about to say.:: Snake: “What does it matter? You already have your minds set that concerning yourself with your match is a waste of time.” ::Russo and Kage shake their heads in disagreement with their mentor’s words as Kage responds.:: Kage: “No, you got it wrong. We just said we don’t think we should be concerned. We didn’t definitely say that we weren’t. Is there something we should know about? Something we should prepare ourselves for?” ::Now sure that he has his students exactly where he wants them, the Snake goes in for the kill.:: Snake: “Actually, yeah there is.” ::Eager to hear what they are lacking, Russo and Kage listen intently as the Snake continues.:: Snake: “I’ve been watching you two since you’ve started in this business. I know all of the ins and outs of your game. You two are confident in your abilities which is a great thing. Sometimes though even too much of a great thing can be a negative attribute. It’s all well and good to be sure that you are better then your opponents, but to not even concern yourselves with them is a mistake. I’ve watched you two evolve into one of the greatest tag teams ever to step a foot inside of a wrestling ring. If you could actually focus on your opponents though, you could be the greatest. Too many times have I watched you two fall behind and lose simply because you worried more about hanging out and partying then actually focusing on wrestling.” ::Russo and Kage continue to listen as the Snake further pushes his points.:: Snake: “Take your opponents for example. Do you think their just casting you off as a joke? No, their preparing to face you head on. The New Royal Legion wants to be the PTC Tag Team champions just as much as you two. They’re going to do whatever they need to do to be ready to take you to the limit when the time comes. That is why you have to push yourselves now. You have to be ready to go to your limits and then surpass them. This is the chance of a lifetime and if you don’t look at it for what it is . . . if you don’t prepare to make this moment yours, then you might as well not even show up to compete.” ::Russo and Kage sit silently as the Snake continues.:: Snake: “Thanks to Kage’s broken arm, you are already going to be at a disadvantage. You two act like that injury will have no effect on the match but it will. It’s your choice however what kind of effect that will be. I came back to you two to finally push you to that next level where you belong. I can’t get you there without some effort on your parts though. The choice is yours, are you going to let another opportunity, possibly your last, slip away or are you going to grab on and ride this to the finish like a legendary team should?” ::Russo and Kage do not speak. They sit in silence as the prophetic words of their mentor finally sink in. After a short pause, Russo begins to speak.:: Russo: “I’m ready. If this is what you say we need to be the best team out there, then I for one am willing to do it.” ::Kage nods his head in agreement as he responds.:: Kage: “We’re putting ourselves in your hands Snake.” ::The Snake nods his head in understanding. Knowing that his two students, who rarely ever place faith in anyone or anything other then themselves, have finally given in and placed their trust back in him causes the Snake to grin as he responds.:: Snake: “I will make sure that you are ready. The renaissance era of the Shadow Riders is about to begin and it will be an era that none will soon forget.” ::Russo and Kage look at each other and give a nod of silent agreement, knowing that they are about to be put through the living hell that the Snake calls training. The scene now fades out briefly before filling once again with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over which states "Life may end but legends never die.". The screen now fades to black.::
