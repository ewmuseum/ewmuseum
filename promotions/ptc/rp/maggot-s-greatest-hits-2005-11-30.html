Maggot's Greatest Hits

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Cue up Pardon The Interruption's "Happy Happy Time" music and cut to a knockoff PTI set with Tony Kornheiser and Michael Wilbon lookalikes manning each seat. TKL: Alright boys and girls, what did we learn today? Cut to a shot of Maggot. MWL V/O: That Maggot is one onery dude. Cut to a shot of Virgil Ambrosi. TKL V/O: That Virgil Ambrosi is… a vampire! Cut to a shot of the Castlevania knockoff game, Maggot-sprite trouncing Ambrosi-sprite. MWL V/O: That [name withheld] isn't the only one who makes wrestling-related sprites. Cut to a shot of Maggot yelling into the camera. TKL V/O: That Ambrosi doesn't need to bite Maggs' neck in order to get a good blood feast. Cut to a shot of Maggot downing Vagrant's Choice Mash Liquor like it's water. MWL V/O: And finally, that Maggot is really an alcoholic! Cut back to the studio. TKL: Time to find out what we screwed up. Numbers Lad, let us have it! Cut to a Tony Reali Lookalike. TRL: Well guys, you're both bald, and you both got the capital of Turkey wrong, it's Ankara, not Istanbul or the giblets. Cut back to the studio. TKL: Alright, our time is over, but before we go, here's a video montage showing career highlights of Maggot. Enjoy! Cut to a screen of Maggot standing in the ring, cue up Nirvana, "Hairspray Queen." Cut to Maggot busting open Justin Evitable during their MBE Extreme Championship Match as the thumping bassline of the song. Cut to Maggot pummeling Joe Retro in a tag match as the guitar parts come in. Cut to Maggot in the Boiler Room Overtime portion of the Homefield Advantage Match for the MBE Unified Championship, delivering the Trash Compactor to LuvGun on the concrete floor as the lyrics start to cut in. "I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your" Cut to Maggot trashing BobbyR's locker room upon dissolution of the MidwestMafia. "You're in a you rang yours rang" Cut to Maggot and the Twins destroying IrishRed's Pub as the ascending guitar riff cuts in. Cut to Maggot and WhiteNoise both climbing the ladder at MBE's High Stakes '01, both staking a claim to the North American Championship and crashing to the floor. "I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your You're in a" Cut to security footage from MBE owner Mr. Amazing!'s office, where Maggot is threatening Mr. A! via the security cameras after sneaking into his office. "At night, wishful gardens At night, where the heart is At night, disco gardens At night, we'll be gaaah" Cut to Maggot grabbing the North American Championship and escaping from the topped steel cage from WhiteNoise's grasp, claiming the NA Championship for himself as the guitar part comes in. Cut to Maggot shaving off Hida Yakamo's hair after defeating him in a title vs. hair match. "I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your You're in a" Cut to Maggot emptying the ring of everyone from Team Amazing! in the War Games match at Blaze of Glory, tangling with Freakfish. "Wishful gardens, at night Where the heart is, at night Wishful gardens, at night Wishful gardens" Cut to Maggot delivering the Driver Fantastique to Freakfish in the Hardcore Hell match at Northern Explosion '01 as the guitar part cuts in. Cut to Maggot delivering the Mutilator to Spoiler on the ring apron, kncoking him off and eliminating him to capture the MBE World Heavyweight Championship. "I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your I was your mine, you were mine I didn't mean you were, mine I was your You're in a" Cut to Maggot thrashing Housefly at High Stakes '02 "Your in a Your rang Your's rang" Cut to Maggot delivering the Trash Compactor ot Lindsay Troy at NFW WrestleBowl '05. "At night wishful gardens at night, where the heart is at night you were so modest at night, crisco lockers at night, the mount for homely it's like, fistful garbage at night, the whiz go gaaah…" Cut to Maggot pummeling Jason Payne as the guitar goes into hyperdrive. Cut to Maggot laying out Cameron Cruise in his locker room over the breakdown. Cut to Maggot thrashing Troy Windham all across the FZM Arena ring over Cobain's grunting to end the song. Cut to Maggot standing tall and with an angry look on his face as the screen fades to black. Credits!
