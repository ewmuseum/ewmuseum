Date: 2005-11-26 Annual Benefit Supports Children In Need. By Phillip Eidson GREENWICH--It’s the holiday’s once again, a time for joy and laughter between family and friends. However sometimes we forget those less fortunate than ourselves. This isn’t the case of the local Stevens clan. Family patriarch William Stevens, CEO of Stevens Industries, has for the last ten years held a benefit masquerade ball and silent auction, all the proceeds of which go to support the America’s Children Hunger Network. An organization that provides life saving emergency food and shelter to the children of USA, The Philippines and Mexico. "We’re really excited about this years event, last year we raised three quarters of a million dollars this year we hope to shatter that record," said Stevens. "Thanksgiving is more than anything about eating a big meal, but it’s also a time to remember that some children in the world are starving." The guest list of the event reads like a who’s who of political figures and captains of industry. Each guest is expected to donate a minimum of $5,000 as an attendance fee. Noticeably absent from the guest list is Stevens’s son professional wrestler Michael Stevens. "Unfortunately, Michael had professional commitments that bar him from attending. However he’s generously donated twenty thousand dollars, as well as an autographed replica of his GCW US Title Belt for the silent auction," Stevens commented. "We’ll obviously miss him at the gala but in his business it’s difficult to find time off." The event takes place on Thanksgiving night at 7:00PM at The Delamar On Greenwich Harbor Hotel. To help support America’s Children’s Hunger Network visit their website, www.americaschildrenhunger.org.
