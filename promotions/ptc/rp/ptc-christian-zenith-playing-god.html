Playing God (Losing His Touch)

14 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Beep... Beep... Beep... He could hear his own heart beat like a heart monitor, tracing the last moments of his own life. Trapped… like a rat in a maze he ran aimlessly through the halls. Beads of sweat dripped down his forehead, and it felt like all the cold air was being sucked right out of his body. Exhaustion was starting to set in, and his glasses began to fog, blurring his perception of his surroundings. Then again, the flickering lights didn’t help either… it was truly like being trapped inside an honest-to-God Haunted House. The EXIT sign ahead shone like a beacon through his distorted reality. His last hope was on the other side of that door. Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Picking up the pace he pushed his hands across the walls to make his way to freedom. The sudden horror which had swooped down over the last few minutes of his life would be over once-and-for-all. Out of nowhere his feet felt as if they were taken out from under him, pain shot up his right leg. A flashing light shown a small table now turned over beside him. Trying to pull himself up to his feet, his ankle couldn’t take the weight. Squinting in agony he fell back onto the ground. The fog thickened on the lenses of his glasses while he dug his fingers into the carpet, clawing his way to safety. Hopefully he’d gained enough ground to make it in time. Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep He could feel his pursuer closing in behind him when his hand fell just a few feet away from the exit door. Looking up at the door he could almost reach it. In a panic he quickly looked behind him, while getting up into a seated position. He backed into the door, his heavy breathing making his glasses useless. At the last second he took off his glasses and threw the across the room, before suddenly the door opened behind him. A terrorized scream escaped his lips as he tried to scurry away. Once again running into the small table he fell onto his stomach. He turned over trying to back away in fear as he watched the blurred nemesis approach through the flickering light. Backing into the corner his near-sighted eyes widened. "Please don't kill me!" he pleaded for his life as his opponent drew near, cold and silent. "Who in the WORLD ever gave you the right to PLAY GOD?! They'll hunt you down and lock you in a cage like the animal you are! You don't want that..." He went on the offensive while he tried desperately to see the face of his adversary. The heartbeat in his ears almost prevented him from hearing what's next. "Don't make me laugh..." The man said. His voice was cold and fearless, much like his movements, "God? Everything you know and love about 'God' is a lie. It's a children's story used to manipulate the weak minded into acting a certain way, doing whatever is asked of them so the powers that be can sleep easy at night. Pressing uniformity through mere threat tactics... I'm too smart for that, I'm ABOVE that. Where is your precious God now when you need him the most?! There is no plagues of locusts, there is no smiting, there is no Jesus fuckin Christ, and there is no God! You should FEAR ME. I TOOK the right... because I'm far better than any God you could possibly fathom. You know what I want, and I'm getting tired of your petty games of cat and mouse." "I CAN'T DO THAT!" He screeched through his tears, "They'll figure me out! I'll lose my job... I'LL GO TO JAIL! I'll be embarrassed and stripped of everything! What will my family do then?!" "I'll tell you what they'll do if you don't..." The man said, followed by a click. He figured it was the barrel locking into his silver gun. He could almost see the reflection of it when the light flickered exposing the man's cadet blue eyes. "First it'll come out in the newspaper, and the police will say it's under investigation. Your family cries... now poor from the lack of income, only collecting just enough money to cover your funeral costs. They claim they won't rest until your killer is found... but trust me, they'll never find a thing. Not only am I good... I have the police resting in my pocket, we have a 'mutual understanding.' They don't question my motifs and I pay for their new squad cars. Years will go by, your murder turns into a cold case and eventually your family grows tired of looking. Your wife finds a new husband who your children despise at first, but he grows on them and they become a happy family. They have new children, and your children love their new brothers and sisters very much. Last but not least... she and her new husband are buried together, leaving you cold and lonely beneath the dirt... until this earth ceases to exist." A chuckle escapes his lips, "The truth hurts don't it?" "I'll do it... PLEASE, I'll do whatever you want! Just don't hurt me..." He pleaded. The man with the cadet blue eyes puts his gun away inside of his black custom-made suit jacket. "Good. Now you do exactly as I say… and I’ll promise you no one will ever find out." The moon hangs high over the blazing neon. A city that never sleeps, a city of sin, and above all else a diamond in the rough. Las Vegas is a gambler's oasis in the middle of the desert. A city that can bring so much pleasure can also bring so much pain... raising some to levels they could never dream of, and crushing the dreams of others. A fitting place for a fight organization to hold their shows. 1 Hour till air for Evolution, and the crowds out behind the arena await to see their favorite Global superstars. A murmur of rumors begin to circulate as a black limo pulls up, followed by a Global Security guys running out the back door to escort the incoming star of their destination. The security guard swings the door open. Flashbulbs begin to flash everywhere from the gathered crowd as Vivica J. Valentine made her way out of the limo. Followed by Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, Tessa Windsor, Demitri Miasnikov, Jason LeMaster, and finally the crowd erupts into boos as the sight of Christian Zenith. Vivica waited over to the side for him to walk by, letting Shadow lead the way into the arena. The cool, dry desert breeze blew through his jet black hair. As his cadet blue eyes surveyed the surrounding crowd, he adjusted his black custom-made suit jacket making his way towards the door. The gold chain connecting the two sides of his jacket glared as the flashbulbs flashed around him. Although the hatred from the crowd was hard to ignore, it had become a habit for Christian Zenith as various pieces of trash flew his way from the hateful crowd. After all... who could blame them in all honesty? He'd made a habit out of crushing their heroes. 'Superstar' Ken Partridge Jr. was Superman to most... to Zenith he was nothing more than a flimsy road block on his way to the Global World Championship. No one cried foul when Zenith became the first man ever to lose the Global World Title by Disqualification... and Zenith proceeded to force Jean-Claude Dafoe out of retirement to get his hands on him for that decision. Only to screw him out of winning the match with a little pre-match key swap. The jeers... they brought him pleasure and satisfaction. To Zenith, they were a sign of his dominance. A warm confidence grew over Zenith as the door shut behind him. "What's on the ticket tonight?" Vivica asked as they made their way to the locker room. "Shadow and Dawn both get a shot at bringing another title to the Hunting Grounds in the TV Title match tonight," Zenith says, walking unwavered through the backstage area, "Tessa has the night off, seems Bubba wants her to rest after her CW2 run. As for me... I have the privilege of Jean-Claude Dafoe booking my match tonight." "Right..." Vivica answered, remembering Bubba's announcement during the Key To The Bank Ceremony, "Any clue on what that jackass has in mind?" "I've heard Hell In A Cell thrown around a few times..." Zenith replied, "That's right down my alley though. I'm considering tonight as a demonstration of what is to come next for Global. An opportunity to show them what they're champion is like... what a REAL champion is like." "ZENITH." A man said aloud, wearing a Global Staff shirt. A mail boy. Surely he was here to give Zenith a letter stating his match for the night. Christian waited patiently as the mail boy shuffled through his letters. He stopped on an enveloped marked by the PrimeTime Central Tournament committee. Zenith reached down and pulled the envelope out of his hand. "That isn't for you." "Elia Smith..." Christian read aloud, "What a joke. New girl around here can't even win a title. She's under-par... nothing more than a glorified joke with a nice ass. Of course PrimeTime Central would notice her, he has boobs... and boobs sells tickets. All the great female talent in this place... Dawn McFarlane, Tessa Windsor, Shadow, Vivica J. Valentine... the invite the one girl who isn't even good enough to wash my car." Zenith opened the envelope pulling out what appeared to be a sealed letter: Dear Elia Smith, PrimeTime Central congratulates you on your selection to take part in the third round of our Infinite Gauntlet Tournament. You will be representing Global Wrestling in a match against our current champion, The Uber Judge. As a complimentary prize, an automatic bid into this year’s GTT5 tournament will be up for grabs as well. With changes to GTT5 pending, this may be your last shot at a chance. Attached is a list of times you will need to know in order to take part. The best of luck to you and Global Wrestling for this event. Sincerely, PrimeTime Central Zenith slides the letter back into the envelope and throws it at the mail boy before walking away with Hunting Grounds by his side. "You want me to show Elia what a real woman is made of?" Vivica J. Valentine asks as they make their way towards the locker room. Tessa, Dawn, and Shadow all look back at Zenith... almost begging for an answer. "No, I'm sure all of you could do that without much trouble..." Zenith answered, "But I have a better idea..." "What's that?" Shadow asked with an intrigued smile across her face. "I'm going to take care of this myself, and become the next Infinite Gauntlet Champion. Maybe PTC will get it through their thick skulls... that I'm the best Global has to offer, and NO ONE represents me." The glowing was only offset by the scattered stars in the clear night's sky. Below a burning red shone like a beacon in the darkness. The limo pulled up at the door, the color matching that of the black sky above. The back door opened and out stepped a man, dressed in a black custom-made suit... matching the vehicle in which he arrived. He stuck his arm in the air, waving off his ride. His eyes met with the hostess behind the desk through the glass door. Pushing the door open, she greeted him with a smile. His foot steps echoed off the tile floor as he approached. "Welcome to the Days Inn sir, would you like a room for the evening?" The Lady said. On her name badge it read "Melissa". Christian just assumed that was her real name and replied. "No... Melissa." Christian was disgusted on the inside about the idea of staying at a Days Inn, but continued on in his usual expressionless manner. "I'm here to visit a close friend of mine." "And who would that be?" Melissa responded, with her usual fake smile. "Jake Ramada... I already know his room number, do you mind if I got pay him a visit?" "Of course, go right ahead!" Melissa said, overly spunky. As Christian once heard on a television show, if she was any cheerier he'd get diabetes. Zenith turned around and headed up the stairs, flipping open a NEXTEL on the way up. The usual beep greeted him before he put it up to his mouth... "I'm on my way to Room 211." Zenith said, "How is the programming coming along?" "Great." Jason LeMaster replied on the other end, "Getting in was so simple... we can have some real fun with this place. The stairwells have electronic locks, I have access to all the names and room numbers. I'm going to turn off the A/C... and suggestions with the lights?" "Not particularly." "Good... I have something in mind." Jason responded, "Do you need Vivica? She's dying to get out of his limo." "No thanks... I think I can handle this myself." Christian thought he could almost hear Vivica J. Valentine going on a rant on the other end of the NEXTEL as he flipped it shut and placed it back into his slacks pocket. The soles of his dress shoes clicked on the stone steps, playing games with the ears of whoever else may be in the stairwell. When arriving at the second floor, Christian twisted the oblong handle to the door and quietly pushed it open. Silently he made his way across the hallway, his cadet blue eyes scanning for Room 211. Upon his arrival he flipped open the NEXTEL once again "Open the lock on Room 211." Christian commanded of Jason LeMaster. "Done." Jason said right as the light on the lock went from Red to Green. Zenith pushed the door open to find Jake Ramada talking on his cell phone. Jake's eyes grew wide as Christian closed the door behind him. Jake took his phone away from his ear and slowly closed it watching as The Conscious Crisis headed his way. "Who are you? What do you want?" Jake demanded, "What in the hell do you think you're doing in my room." "Don't play dumb Jake, you're a PTC guy... you know who I am." Christian said, standing firmly between Jake and the door. "You forgot to send me my invitation for Infinite Gauntlet, Round 3. You are the Invitation Guy are you not? You type them up, put the little stamps on them and everything... correct?" "Yeah, that's me. I didn't forget shit..." He responded, "You'll get your chance if you're lucky." "Jake... I don't believe you understand me." Zenith said as he reached inside of his black coat jacket, "I was expecting to receive an invitation you see... I heard you wanted THE BEST of THE BEST. And I am what I am... I was in Seven of the Best for a reason. I can do whatever in the hell I want in Global because no one is good enough or has the balls to stop me. I say jump they jump... and in a matter of time, I expect PTC to be the same way. This round of Infinite Gauntlet has some importance to me... you see, I NEED that GTT5 invite." "Humor me Christian..." Jake said sarcastically, standing up from across the room. "Well you see... Seven of the Best wasn't exactly my style. You see, I look upon multiple person matches as the poor man's way to win. You sit back and you let the two best people beat the living hell out of each other. At the last minute you just cover one of the two guys... it's pathetic. Now... One-on-one, that's a challenge. It's man against man, one man can't win without dominance over the other. GTT5 is something I think I'll enjoy a lot Jake." Christian advanced forward with his hand still inside of his jacket, "Destroying opponent after opponent until there aren't anymore. It's only a matter of time until PTC acknowledges my greatness, and I always get what I want Jake. So we can do this the easy way... where you get me an invitation and hand it to me and I'll leave you alone. Or we can do this the hard way." "The hard way huh? I'm not scared of a character on TV. What in the hell are you gonna do Zenith? Beat me up? Shoot me?" Jake lit a cigarette as Christian heard the Air Conditioning shut off. "Now we're talking." Christian Zenith says as he pulls his silver gun Ares out of his jacket pocket and pointed it at Jake who dropped his cigarette on the ground in shock, "So tell me something... Do you believe in God?" "Yeah..." Jake responds quietly, fighting through a lump in his throat which seemed to be the size of a softball. "Ah... then you do believe in me." Christian said, cocking the gun. "Are you crazy or something? What in the hell are you doing with that thing?" Jake screams as he backs up against the wall. "Let's call it... punishing the wicked." A shot fired from the gun and Jake fell to the ground, soiling him self. He then clawed his way up to his feet and ran by Christian Zenith, then out the door. Christian puts the gun back into his suit jacket when suddenly the lights go out. Outside Jake sprints to the stairwell and grabs a hold of the handle. He shook the handle violently, but the door was locked. "DAMN!" Jake exclaimed as he turned around and ran down the pitch black hallway, trying to feel his way to freedom.
