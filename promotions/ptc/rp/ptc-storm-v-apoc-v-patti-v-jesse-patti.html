A knock on the door disrupts Patti’s thoughts for the moment. “Come in!.” Patti barely got the words out when Adrian came pouncing through the door. His hands holding several pages in his hand. His blue eyes constantly going over them time and time again. “Patti, you are just not going to believe this. Ian and Fey just signed you up for the Global Title Division, and you are scheduled to compete in April. Oh my goodness, it seems that you are needed for appearances clear through Maelstrom Three. All of a sudden, Patti’s grasp loosened, causing the almost limp Adrian to fall to the ground. Patti stares at the crumpled from of Adrian. Her concern starting to show for her friend, bending down Patti brushes the bangs out of the way of his eyes. Her instincts telling her something seriously was wrong with him. Patti started to really get nervous when she noticed the bruises beginning to appear around Adrian’s throat. Patti stared at her reflection as if trying to figure out where the blood came from. The dried blood was still refusing to come off of her face no matter how hard she scrubbed. Three used bars of bar soap were proving that point very clearly. The frustrations were mounting clearly as Patti grabbed another hotel wash cloth and went back to work scrubbing her face, still hell bound to remove the stain. Patti lifted her head up and stared into the mirror. Her hazel eyes reflecting the fear that had been there since the first encounter with visions. The first set of visions of attacking and blooding Todd Dream were enough to send her into her World Title match hell bent on winning it, but it also bothered her that this was going on. Somewhere, someone didn’t like her, and this demon was proving this theory very strongly. The reflection just scared Patti. Her lightly tanned skin seemed to glow, something that was not normal.
