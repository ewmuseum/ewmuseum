[PLAIN]What Happens in Vegas… (Feat. Nitz Donnelly [PRIME])[/PLAIN] There are certain words that wrestlers really don’t want to hear this day in age from their superiors or in the pages and pictures of the media. The first would have to be drugs. With the recent explosion of hearsay and accusations, there’s no surprise that defensive hands would shoot up to swat back any offensive arrows laced with fire. It would be a fire that could bake the insides of any federation and cripple the on screen product that is required. It would also damage the main product and superstars involved in the eyes of the people who technically pay their salaries... the fans. The second word that wrestlers really don’t want to hear is ‘fired’. Mind you, the number of opportunities out there in the wrestling world are starting to grow and there are openings for anyone with a shred of talent with the competition. The fights are sometimes tooth and nail and sought after talent could reel in some large contracts. Although, no one likes to be uprooted from something that they are used to so being relieved of your duties is not exactly good news. The third word is a word used many times in professional sports. Normally reserved for misconducts on or off the playing surface, it’s a basic punishment especially when it makes the team as a whole look bad. Monetary damages are usually sought followed by warming the bench for a game or two. But in professional wrestling, it could mean the end of the much earned push in the right direction. The pinnacle of what one has worked so hard for could be pushed farther away in one swift motion by the Board of Directors. |~o~| Sunday, September 2, 2007 “Suspension?!” The digitized voice rang out through the ear piece. The flaps of his unbuttoned dress shirt were swaying as he stalked his way through the group of people. Many bright colored lights were reflecting against the shiny case of his brand new cell phone as a familiar chime sounded out through the large room. The carpet was red, a particularly bloody tint that stood out amongst even the most decorative clothing that could be found worn by the casino bravers in the Sin City. The only thing that compared was an equally red, revealing dress worn by a young woman that The Ego didn’t take his eyes off of even though his steps were increasing in length and overall speed. “Yeah suspension. But you sound worried... no way are they letting me go cause I’ll show up on GCW’s or FUSE’s doorstep in five minutes to sign with them. They know that. But don’t worry about me. I have some extra plans in the meantime.” “Plans? What plans? Where the hell are you Nick?” Hailey asked from all the way back in Boston. His sister was always so nosey... no, not nosey. The way she always put it was being concerned for her little brother. If Nitz had his choice, no one would be concerned about him at all. He saw it as more of a burden, almost like having to worry what someone else would think about his actions. “I’m taking a little bit of me time. Is that so fuckin’ bad?!” A woman at a nearby table turned and gave an odd look at The Ego’s choice of colorful language. “You were supposed to come back and take mom to her chemo this week. Now I gotta take off work to do it. I... argh!” She was flustered on the other end of the line and Donnelly could hear the phone change hands. Just what he needed. Who was coming on the line now? Mom? Dad? God forbid it was his jackass brother Chad. “Dude, where the hell are you?” “Mikey? You at the restaurant?” “Hailey came in for dinner. Now she is confused about you. What’s going on man?” |~o~| Friday, August 31, 2007 Boston Radio – The Davidson Sports Show Transcript Part 1 Chuck Davidson: Welcome back to the show. We’re only a week away from the start of the NFL season and you can bet the Davidson show will be here all next week talking about it. We’re gonna be hot on the heels of the Colts home opener with the Saints on Thursday and the Pats with the big debut of Randy Moss on Sunday, but right now we switch to a rarely talked about topic here on the show and that’s professional wrestling. We usually cover more UFC on the show but we just couldn’t turn down the “Bring the Pain” mouth of our next guest. He was recently suspended from PRIME Wrestling. “The Ego of New England” in the hooooooooouuuuse! Nitz Donnelly: That’s right man, the High Class Ass on High Class muthaf***in’ radio. How you been man? Chuck Davidson: I’ve been good but this is not satellite Nitz. So we need to watch that language. Every swear will be beeped out but we still need to be careful. Nitz Donnelly: Gag me then cause this is me. You wanted The Ego on the show and you got the full blown man himself. Chuck Davidson: Well let’s first of all talk about your suspension from PRIME. What’s rolling through your mind about that? Nitz Donnelly: Plenty of things run through my mind about it. Like what kinda dyke is running the shows all the time. How about the useless hacks in the Board of Directors? Not much can be said about this except for the fact that I had a message to deliver and I think the point got across. Chuck Davidson: Well from what I understand, Lindsay Troy... who also happens to live just outside of Boston now, belted you with a truck door first and this was just retaliation. Plus you have the highest selling product on their website in your t-shirt. How could a company suspend someone for something so trivial while she also attacked you a few weeks earlier? Nitz Donnelly: What else is new Chuckie? PRIME once again just doesn’t make any sense. Same for when that fat ass Chet Worth was running the show. Yeah, the Amazon she-beast did belt me with a truck door while I was busy fending off the inbreeding of Wade Elliott. So I decide to take matters into my own hands and belt her in the leg with a pipe. I owned that b*tch and got my message across. I don’t care about your titles, or accomplishments or your popularity. Just because there is a slab of gold around your waist doesn’t make you untouchable to me. Cross me and I double cross you. Chuck Davidson: Sure but now it’s cost you money with this suspension. We all need to live. Nitz Donnelly: I’ll enjoy the time off for a little while. I am heading to Vegas this week. Got some “business” to take care of. |~o~| Sunday, September 2, 2007 “Mikey, don’t you think it would be a little odd that I tell my sister I’m out in Vegas trollin’ for booty? Like fuck man, I hear enough about what I do. Why can’t I enjoy myself? Didn’t you guys listen to the Davidson Show on the radio?” “Perhaps we would have if we knew you were on it. You haven’t told your family a thing for over a week now. Where you staying in Vegas?” Nitz could hear his sister in the background, complaining about Nitz wanting time to himself in Las Vegas while she needs to sacrifice herself to fulfill his promises. “I’m staying at the Wynn. Tell Hailey that life is a bitch and to...” “Tell her yourself man. This is on you.” He can hear the phone being passed back to Hailey. He could make out the faint mumbles from Mikey. Something about letting him unwind and things will be ok. Yeah, shows what he knew. “Vegas? What the fuck Nick?” Her voice shrieked. He pulled the phone away from his ear for an instant, coming to the bar in the hotel and placing an order for a stiff drink. “Yeah, Vegas. I have some things to take care of. Life is a bitch sis, so deal with it.” “Why do you have to be such an ass about it? You could have told me... errrr... something!” He pulled the phone away from his ear again and cursed it as he swiped the glass from the bar, leaving a five spot in the small puddle the condensation on the cold glass left behind. He took a long sip, leaving his sister to wonder if he was going to answer the allegation of being an ass. Not like he’d deny it. “I need to relax and take a load off Hail. You of all people should fuckin’ understand THAT! Plus like I said, I have things I need to tend to.” “Things? What th...” She paused, reality setting in. She wondered if Nicholas had completely stopped doing it. “Please tell me it’s not what I think Nick.” |~o~| Friday, August 31, 2007 Boston Radio – The Davidson Sports Show Transcript Part 2 Nitz Donnelly: Losing the money is the least of my concerns right now. Regardless of the incentive based contract, there are pluses to it as well. Most contracts have a no compete clause in them. Me? I have no such thing. Nor do I have a time span to live up to. I can leave anytime I want. Soon as they abuse this suspension, I take off for the competition. They won’t let that happen. They may be brainless airheads in PRIME but they can’t possibly be that stupid. Chuck Davidson: So then why don’t you take the easy way out and sign with someone else? There are so many other options out there for a draw like you. Nitz Donnelly: GCW couldn’t handle me. Steven Caldera would lose his mind in a couple weeks and fire me just to feed his own ego. That jack*** has a serious problem, more so than the obvious absence of talent on that shallow roster. But seriously? I don’t, in so many words, mind PRIME. I hang out with a couple guys here and there and they put on a good show. Don’t mistake that for them carrying a major source of talent. I have more talent in my prostate. They just have high production values, not to mention a decent fan base. Chuck Davidson: You’re talking about the recent influx of Donnelly followers heard cheering in the crowd. Nitz Donnelly: Smartest fans on the planet Chuck. Chuck Davidson: So what can we expect from Nitz Donnelly then in the near future? Nitz Donnelly: Well after I tend to my business in Vegas and take a short breather. I’m gonna walk into PTC for the first time ever and turn the damn place on its head. Think I’ll start with the Infinite Gauntlet Open Invite. A nice payday coming from that one too. Chuck Davidson: Yes, where the winner faces the seemingly unbeatable Tchu. Nitz Donnelly: Unbeatable my nutsack Chuck. He lost his last few matches before running from PRIME with his tail tucked and his Hall of Fame trophy tucked further. No one knows who will show up for this round of the Gauntlet, but I can say that every one will be crawling home to their mommas after defeat when I move on. Chuck Davidson: Rumor has it that PRIME is also investigating something else behind the scenes. It wouldn’t be related to this recent drug craze that has been all over the media would it? Nitz Donnelly: More like obsessed the media. You have no idea how many times some jerk with a mic has approached me since my suspension asking me if I’m high. Never been on anything and never will be. My name isn’t Kennedy and I’m not going to lie knowing full well I’d get caught sometime down the line. Who could possibly be that absent minded? Chuck Davidson: So it’s not drugs then, but how is PRIME dealing with the drug explosion? Nitz Donnelly: Why don’t you ask them? I’m sure Lisa Tyler would give you fellatio for some exposure. I could care less as long as I know I’m clean. Chuck Davidson: Anything to shed some light on this supposed investigation they are taking out on you? Nitz Donnelly: You know I could get in trouble for mentioning it... Chuck Davidson: Well then let’s move on... Nitz Donnelly: BUT I DON’T CARE! It’s quite simple really... |~o~| Back Las Vegas Way, September 2 “That’s what that noise is, you’re in a casino!” Tears were falling from the face of Hailey in Boston, once again thinking that Nicholas was sinking into a gambling depression once again. It was not well known that over a year prior to his PRIME tenure, Nitz has been bitten by the gambling bug. He had started to sink all his independent wrestling earnings in the devil’s game. He was almost evicted for not paying rent. He was constantly stuck at the blackjack and poker tables. Even as he made his way through the very raucous casino crowd, seeing the blackjack tables brought that familiar feeling out. The poker pros brought butterflies into his stomach, remembering all the big paper bills he lost to the men in the cowboy hats and the expensive Armani suits. But he had everything under control. To be honest, seeing and hearing the people at these tables made him want to avoid them altogether. He didn’t have a craving at all. “Yeah, what the hell else is in Vegas Hail? Of course it’s a casino!” “Don’t sound all defensive. You’re gambling!” Her voice was cracking as she tried to hold back sobs. “Thanks for having faith. I haven’t thrown a single cocksuckin’ dime down.” “Yet... so that’s why you’ve been weird lately. PRIME is all into this gambling arc they have going on and you were getting frisky about gambling again.” Nitz listened to his sister accusing him as he took a deep gulp from his drink, almost finishing it. “Nah, but you’re gonna drive me to alcoholism if you don’t stop this PMS spree and quit the blame laying. I’m not gambling.” “Then why are you there Nick? Tell me the truth!” She whined as he could hear Mikey mumbling something in the background. “Tell Mikey he’d be welcome to try to hand me my ass but I’m not a slut to the one armed bandit or anything. I’m here to relax... and...” He was thinking twice about offending his sister but he had to clear the air, no matter how funky and perverted he sounded. “... and the pussy. There I said it. Sue me.” He heard silence on the line for a good few seconds, making him look at the phone in his hand to see if the connection was still active. He finished the drink as he walked to the far end of the casino and put the glass on a waitress’s tray. After blowing into the mouthpiece to see if the connection was really still on, he finally heard Hailey. “So you’re there to get laid? No gambling?” “That’s what I said. Dad gave me some money to split on NFL game odds and Super Bowl odds, but nothing else. That’s the “business” I have to do Hailey.” “But... that’s gambling.” She weakly argued. Her protests held no water with The Ego though, causing his stride through the casino to halt. He could picture himself jumping up and down like a child, yelling into an electronic device and looking like a moron defending himself until he was blue in the face. Yeah, that would make the ladies come running. “Look, you’ll just have to trust me. It’s some money on some football games. I’m not going near the tables or the slots. I’m long over that shit Hail.” “I want to believe you Nick.” “Then fuckin’ do it! I’ll call you later on.” Following the generic send offs, he closed the phone and pocketed it. After fixing the unbuttoned dress shirt and the t-shirt underneath, he made his way over to the window, pulling a large roll of cash, 7 G’s to be exact, from his pocket and presenting it. A couple passing members of the female persuasion gave the money a quick look and gave him a large smile. He smiled back as they passed and walked away. Boy did he enjoy watching them leave as he pursed his lips and shook his head faster than their asses. He knew that betting on games was a form of gambling, but it was nothing close to what he did before. That part of his life was over. There was nothing wrong with making the Super Bowl more interesting. There was also nothing wrong with hooking up in Vegas. After placing his bets, he took off after the two ladies who passed him by just a moment ago. He could place cash on the tables or spend it on a woman. The payoff was always much better with the latter option.
