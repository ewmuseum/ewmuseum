Scent of A Woman: Realization

12 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-12 Moraless and Raijin have both been disposed of, their shots at the PTC Cruiserweight Division’s top spot is now gone and Coricidon’s shot continues to increase as she is on top of the world. Her next match is against… Well, her next match is against an easy opponent. One who doesn’t even belong in a tournament with wrestlers and the fact that this person won the first match in the tournament. This person goes by the name the Cockjockey. All of a sudden she is the underdog in this that is a shock to her because this guy is really small, something that a wrestler shouldn’t be and when she says small, she means REALLY small as in only a few feet tall, if that. Her Cadillac slowly begins to roll into the entrance of the motel as flashes of red and blue lights splash across her face. This becomes a complete shock to her; like the dagger of uncertainty begins piercing her heart as the mere thought of something happening to her life partner begins to race through her head. She knows that nothing could happen to her beloved but what if their small quarrel sent her over the deep end? She pulls up behind one of the cop car’s and gets out but a fleeting police force combined with a fire engine blocks her view from seeing what room they are clearing out although her heart begins pounding quicker as the realization of them being near her room hits her like a ton of bricks. Could it be her room that they’re in? Could Sky have done something to herself that would be the cause of this? Coricidon get out of her car but suddenly grows very weak, so weak that it is hard for her to hold herself up to her complete 5 foot 9 stature. Her knees begin to grow wobbly as she holds onto the driver’s side door for support. They couldn’t be in there; there isn’t a thing wrong with Sky that would designate such problems because she has always been the one that was messed up in the head. She releases her death grip on the door and walks around it, still leaning on it for the support she needs but having enough strength to forge ahead to what may very well end her life. Everything is still blocking her view as she notices police officers still running back and forth between squad cars and one of the rooms while she continues to advance towards an officer who is filling out paperwork on top of his car. “Excuse me”, she asks, very weakly while her stomach does those kinds of backflips that make you feel like you were dropped on your head after each one. “What room are you investigating?” Her words are soft, with more emotion behind them then ever before. This may not even be a concern, they could be a few rooms down investigating a meth lab or arresting a burglar, there is no need to get so worked up. But she is wrong and she knows that she is wrong as the officer stops his paperwork and stares at her for a brief moment before setting his pen on the top of the car and walking over towards her. “Are you a Mrs… Coricidon?” he asks with a regretful look on his face as Coricidon’s whole life falls apart in an instant. She can be heard from miles as she lets out a monstrous scream and breaks down next to her wheel wells, tears rapidly flowing from her eyes as the inside of her body turns to absolute jelly. “I’m sorry ma’am”, the police officer says as he walks over and tries to console her. “No!” she yells out, pushing his hand away as she huddles herself up against the tire trying to speak more but only shooting out saliva. She begins to peer around at the scene, her tears blurring her vision and causing every light to reflect right into her corneas until her eyes stop on the coroner’s van, people continuing to unload various things. The police officer still stands next to her as he tries to console but every word out of his mouth fumbles and turns into gibberish. “I- I”, she says, trying to exclaim the words but every thought back to her lover sticks thousands of daggers straight into her heart. “I want to- See… HER!” Her head suddenly falls forward to disguise her red face with all of her black hair that splashes against the ground, soaking in all of the tears and sweat, making it hang extra heavy. But she continues to have the same things running through her head. Why her? Why did she have to be taken? What happened? What did she do? So many questions yet none of them will ever be answered. She grabs onto the hood of her car with her right hand as she slowly begins to rise back to her feet although her legs aren’t the sturdiest things to be on at this moment. Yet she continues to struggle in hopes to solve these mysteries that plague her as she finally rises to her feet, looking like a handicapped person who dropped their crutches. Her body contorts forward and sickness begins to overtake her body as she has many aborted attempts to vomit. The cop continues to take steps to putting his arm around her in comfort but every time he does, she continues to lift her arm up and push his away. Finally, with a short amount of strength, she begins taking steps towards the scene of the crime. “I want to see it…”, she says. “I want to see her.” The cop begins walking as if to stop her but it is like trying to stop a building from collapsing, it isn’t going to happen. Her strength gets stronger as she continues forward to the doorway but her vision is moved to see something else, something that knocks her back to the ground. All of the coroners gather around the gurney making its way out of the room, a white sheet placed over the body in order for nobody to see her. Blood stains the sheet and continues dripping as a line of drops lead them out. All of a sudden all the emotion bottles up into one as Coricidon falls to the ground and puts her arms up so that her sullen face does not touch the hard concrete. Sobs exhale from her as she has given up all hope in trying to get back to her feet. Instead she raises her head up and looks at the horrific scene one more time. She sees it but suddenly all of the tears that she is weeping clog her eyes and everything goes into a blur once again. Her body temperature continues to rise as she can feel that her passing out will be imminent before the night is over from this. “I love you Sky!”, she blurts out, slobber spilling from her mouth. That is when everything overwhelms her and she can feel her entire body growing limp. Her eyes roll back into her head as she falls forward and the entire world stops as her head slams against the ground, opening up a small gash on her head from her last match as she lays comatose on the ground.
