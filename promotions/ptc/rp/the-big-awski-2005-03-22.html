The Big AWSki

22 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-22 Quote of the Day (From Aqua Teen Hunger Force) Shake: What's the opposite of scary? Meatwad: Tacos? Shake: Taco?! Where?! Get outta my way, those are mine I made them! [We open with a view floating above the city of Cleveland. As we meander along above the mid-day traffic, a voice with a thick Western accent speaks.] Voice-over : Out Midwest there was a fella, name of Bill … at least, that seems to be the handle his parents gave ‘im, but he always went by AWS Man himself. Or usually both; and sometimes neither. That whole durned name thing confused the mess out of me, myself. Anyway, this AWS Man, he called himself the Insane Pres. Cause he was the President of some wrasslin’ organ-i-zation named Wrestling Midwest, and well, he was loonier than one-a them cattle with Mad Cow disease, to boot. [The view is in downtown Cleveland by now, high rises stretching up far past our viewpoint.] Voice-over : Now this story I’m about to unfold took place … well, it’s taking place pretty durn close to right this instant, to tell the God-honest truth. I’m not even sure how I know it already. But there’s a lot about this story that won’t make sense. The only reason I’m telling it is because sometimes … sometimes, there’s a man … not a hero, because whatever this here AWS Man is, it’s not that… But sometimes there’s a man – even if it’s a stupid man, and he could very well be a top contender for stupidest man in all of the pro wrestling in-dus-try, which would put him in a good running for stupidest non-mentally disabled man alive … But sometimes there’s a man… Aw, hell, I done ripped off this movie enough. [We now float into a rather unremarkable corporate office. The room is therefore filled with more or less unremarkable furniture: a large mahogany desk occupying the majority of one side of the room, a leather couch along one wall of the room, an entertainment center directly across from the desk. About the only thing out of the ordinary is the sizeable collection of pornography in the room, with stacks of videos with titles flanking the TV with such as “Tea Bagger Vance,” “Moulin Splooge,” and “Romancing the Bone” (hey, nobody said these would be Shakespeare … although “Shagspeare in Louise” is in there), and pornographic magazines mingled with the disorganized paperwork strewn across the desk. In fact, there would be much more mature/highly immature content than there is, were the office’s occupant here more often. The owner of the office is in the midst of one of his infrequent visits there at the moment, however, as even such irresponsible company presidents as AWS Man (also known as Bill) must make the occasional appearance at their company headquarters, even if just to keep up form. Therefore the Insane Pres is currently reclining in his office chair, feet up on his desk, perusing the latest copy of Hustler. He chuckles as he flips through a story pictorial.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Heh heh freakin’ heh, this is pretty good stuff. Hey, I should rip this off for a WMW storyline! Hmmm, but where am I gonna find a fake spaceship and a freakin’ robot costume? … What do you think, Pen? [Pen – AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s spatula, constant companion, and high-ranking WMW official – is lying nearby on top of the Insane Pres’ desk. He responds in his usual fashion.] Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Too expensive? That’s what you freakin’ said about that tsunami insurance I bought. And we needed that! Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : What? Cleveland’s not near the ocean? Then what’s that big freakin’ body of water right outside our headquarters? Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Ocean, man-made pond for a Putt-Putt, same freakin’ difference. If it was up to you, we’d taunt the tsunami gods with our insolence by not buying the insurance, and then wind up like … uh … Canada, a few freakin’ months ago. Do you want that? [AWS Man (also known as Bill) is cut off mid-“argument” when his phone rings. He shoves various adult literature and WMW documents out of the way until he discovers the buried phone, and answers it.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : What’s the freakin’ dilly-o? [There is a sigh on the other end of the phone, as the view switches to a split-screen shot, in which we can see the other end of the line is being held by one Brian Blade, long-time acquaintance of the Insane Pres, who serves as one part friend, three parts daily supervisor, and half a part jazz enthusiast. Not that the latter thing was at all relevant, but hey, he has a life outside of providing a voice of reason to AWS Man (also known as Bill) … though not much of one.] Blade : You still answer the phone like that? What if I’d been somebody important? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : What … you mean like the freakin’ Pope? Cause JP knows he’s only supposed to call me at home. Blade : Nooo, I mean like someone who might need to reach you as pertains to your job as WMW President… On second thought, any important calls like that certainly wouldn’t go to you. [The Insane Pres rolls his eyes in irritation. Talking to Blade is never at the top of his list of favorite activities, especially not when it begins with more pointless nagging.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, and this one shouldn’t freakin’ have either. You know I’m not supposed to be disturbed while I’m working. How’d you get transferred through from the secretary, anyway? Blade : Because, um, you don’t have one. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Susan quit?! Oh, freakin’ great; she didn’t even give me a two weeks notice. Blade : There never was a Susan, moron. You tried to have Pen as your secretary at first, but the board put a stop to that when nobody was answering their calls. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh, that was just because he didn’t freakin’ like any of them. So what was so important that you had to drag me out of my meeting, anyway? Blade : Well, I just got a phone call from the board; they would’ve called you, but they didn’t expect you to be at the office, plus were afraid of Pen screening their calls again. It’s not necessarily bad news, but it might be an inconvenience to you. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Oh man, did freakin’ Blade die? Blade : Huh? … No, I’m talking to you right now! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : So you’re just hospitalized, then? Blade : No, nothing bad’s happened to me. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well then why the freak are you trying to get my hopes- I mean, freakin’ scare me like that? You almost gave Pen … whatever the inanimate freakin’ version of a heart attack is. Blade : I never even mentioned me! Just listen. You know how Shadow was going to be the WMW representative for that upcoming PTC event, Seven of the Best? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Sure thing. Blade : …You have no clue what I’m talking about, do you? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Sure thing. Blade : (Sigh) OK, it’s a mini-tournament thing where fourteen people enter- You know what, that’s not important now, you’ll never understand the simple rules anyway. There was a match a few Burnouts ago which Shadow won against Fury to represent WMW in this thing; but, as you know, she’s leaving now, and we had to put in a last minute replacement. Fury would’ve been the next logical choice, as he was the other person who wanted the spot, but he’s got previous obligations. So, knowing that you wouldn’t really have anything better to do, and also since you have the most experience in PTC matches of the rest of the people in WMW, you were given the nomination instead. Unfortunately, they forgot to tell us until now, and the match is next week. [There’s a pause in the conversation for several moments, as AWS Man (also known as Bill) fiddles with Pen and absorbs this information.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Wait a minute here … you’re telling me I never had anybody named Susan freakin’ here at all? Well, who was that sleeping out in the lobby for like two weeks?! Blade : …Get with the program, man! You’re entered in Seven of the Best and you’ve got a match next week against LeStatt Knight from GCW! Win that, and you’re on to a seven-man battle royal to determine the next Global title contender. This could be, and probably is, your final chance to even have another shot at that belt … so you need to make the most of it. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Lester Rook, got it. What do you freakin’ know about him? Blade : Well, not much, besides that he comes from a galaxy far, far away in a time long, long ago… AWS Man (also known as Bill) : What’s that? [Blade smirks, having made a rare attempt at a joke.] Blade : Nothing. Just thinking how people that do movie parody promos are morons. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yes, they freakin’ are. (Looks pointedly into camera) All of them. Blade : Well, besides that, I don’t really have much info on the guy. You’ll have to do a littler research on him on your own … which in your case, I know, means none at all. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Yeah, like I freakin’ need it. I’ve had two Global shots already; I know what it takes to get there. Blade : (Under his breath) Yeah, if only you knew what it takes to actually win it… AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hey, I freakin’ heard that! You leave that to me this time; I have the feeling the third time will have the freakin’ Lucky Charms. And those are delicious. Blade : What do you mean, leave it to you? You’re in the match, who else would I leave it to?! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Um … Pen? [Blade looks exasperated, and glances around for an excuse to end the conversation, and spies one.] Blade : Look, I gotta go. The Nude … um … tripped outside and got it lodged in a fire ant nest again. I was gonna wait until after Oprah to take him to the hospital, but he’s starting to convulse or something. Just … do some actual training for the match this time, hmm? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Why? I didn’t train any for my match against freakin’ Slasher I just had at Ides of March, and I won that. Blade : Yes, and you also made yourself the referee. Plus you had interference. [It’s true. He did.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hey, I was a completely impartial ref, and nobody can freakin’ deny it. Blade : Yeah, except people actually watching the match. Bye. [Blade hangs up the phone, and his side of the screen momentarily takes up the entire view, and pans out to reveal AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s best (animate) friend, the Nude, lying on the couch with an ice pack over his private parts, the area surrounding his crotch covered in red welts.] Nude : (Hopefully, while in a great deal of pain) So you’re taking me to the hospital now? Blade : (Picking up the remote) Hell no. I just said that to get off the line. (The Nude sinks back and begins moaning.) Blade : Oh, relax and try to enjoy the show. Look, Oprah’s got [name withheld] on today. [We cut back to AWS Man (also known as Bill) in his office, who’s returned to his reading/office work/method of training for matches. Suddenly, his office door bursts open, and two muscular men rush in: one Caucasian, the other Oriental. The white guy vaults across the room and grabs the startled Insane Pres in a headlock, slamming his head against the desk. While formidable as a wrestler, given the proper motivation and time to prepare a strategy, AWS Man (also known as Bill) is quite cowardly outside the ring, and generally worthless in a fight unless against someone much smaller or weaker than him. As such, he does not make much attempt to fight back. As the man begins to question him, each question is punctuated by slamming his head once more into the desk; luckily for AWS Man (also known as Bill), his metallic paintball mask absorbs most of the blows, something which apparently never occurs to his assaulters.] White Guy : Where’s the money, Rutherford?! (SLAM!) Where’s the money?! (SLAM!) AWS Man (also known as Bill) : I don’t know, but I’ll pay you to freakin’ stop! … Do you take porn? [The man slams the Insane Pres’ head onto the desk once more, then shoves him back in his chair, before getting in his face.] White Guy : WHERE’S THE FUCKING MONEY?! Your company owes money to Rupert Murdoch, that means you owe money to Rupert Murdoch. And Mr. Murdoch always gets his debts paid in full. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Uh … look, I don’t know any freakin’ Rumplestiltskin, but if I ever meet him I’ll give him a cushy job here. How about freakin’ Assistant Head of Janitors? White Guy : You think this is funny, shithead?! [Meanwhile, the Oriental man is looking over AWS Man (also known as Bill)’s porn collection. He knocks over the stacks of tapes, then unzips his pants and begins to urinate on them.] AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hey, man, not on the freakin’ porn! We have a rug for that! [The white thug grabs him more forcibly by the collar.] White Guy : See that?! That’s what happens to guys who stiff Murdoch on his money, Rutherford! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Wait, I’m freakin’ Rootytooty? That’s not my name; I’m AWS Man (also known as Bill), sometimes called AWS Man, Bill, or Hey, Guy Who Says Freak A Lot. White Guy : Shut up, Rutherford! We’ve done our homework on you; you go by the Insane Pres, right?! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Well, yeah, but I’ve never even freakin’ heard of this Roo guy … except on Winnie the Pooh. That was a good show; needed more freakin’ full-frontal nudity, though. I would’ve really liked to see how far down his mom could get a- White Guy : I said shut up! (Picks up Pen and looks at it incredulously) What the fuck is this? AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Obviously you’re not a freakin’ chef… Oh wait, I think they do use spatulas. [The Oriental thug zips up his pants and looks around the room quizzically.] Oriental Guy : Hey, wasn’t this guy supposed to be rich? (Pointing to trophy case full of wrestling titles) And, like, head of a big corporation, not a small time wrestling promotion? [Both men look around some more.] White Guy : …FUCK! I knew we should’ve just read the name on the side of the building before sneaking in. Oriental Guy : This guy’s just a loser wrestler. AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Hey! At least I freakin’ know how to treat fine porn! [Both men stomp out, the white guy glaring back as he walks out.] White Guy : Thanks a lot, asshole! AWS Man (also known as Bill) : That’s President Asshole to freakin’ you! … Pen, tell Susan to have security intercept them on the way out. Leave no freakin’ bodies. Pen : … AWS Man (also known as Bill) : Aw, not again?! She really needs to start giving notices! [The scene fades out as the Insane Pres sits in dejection in his office, looking at his ruined porn collection.]
