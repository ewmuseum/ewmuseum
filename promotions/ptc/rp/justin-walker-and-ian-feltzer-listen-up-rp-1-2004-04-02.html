Justin Walker and Ian Feltzer listen up! RP#1

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 ::Scene:: The scene opens up with Robb Hardy walking on the streets of Los Angeles, California. Robb has his hair down in dreadlocks, he has a black pullover on, Phat Farm jean, and Converse shoes. His hands are in his pullover pockets as he walks down the street. It's about 2:24 A.M. in the morning, and all of a sudden two gun shots are heard. Robb looks around, and theirs two guys shooting at each other. Their is also a mother and daughter caught in the crossfire. Robb Hardy looks down and walks away, then thinks about what hes doing. ::Robb Hardy thinking:: Should I save them? I have my own wife and child to think of, DAMN! What am I going to do. Think..... Think... I can't just walk away, I have to save this woman and child. Here I go. ::Scene:: Robb Hardy begins to run towards the woman and child, and a bullet skids off his arm, cutting it, but not hitting him all the way. Robb continues to run as if it doesn't bother him, and he grabs the mother and child in his arms, and runs quick as hell, using so much strength its amazing. After getting about 100 yards away from the gun fire, Robb sets down the mother and child. Robb Hardy: Are you okay? Mother: Yes... Thank you.. Robb Hardy: ...... Mother: What? ::Scene:: Robb Hardy gazes into her eyes, she looks and speaks so much like his first wife who was killed. Robb Hardy: Ca- Cassandra? Is that you? Mother: Ha-how do you know my name? Robb Hardy: Cassandra, it really is you! It's you! I thought you were dead, and the clhild of mine you were carrying, but your not! Your alive! Cassandra: Yes Robb, it's true, I am alive. Damn, my cover is blown! Robb Hardy: Are you saying, you- you...... Cassandra: Yes, I faked my death. Robb Hardy: But why? You made me think you and my child were dead. WHY CASSANDRA! Cassandra: Robb, it wasn't supose to happen this way, the government needed me for something, and they said I had to fake my death. You weren't supose to get married again. The whole thing with the gun fire, I don't know how I got caught up in it, but I did. Robb Hardy: Why were you out this late? Cassandra: I had to go take care of my mother. Robb Hardy: Well what did the government need you for? Cassandra: Sorry, top secret, I can't tell you. Robb Hardy: Why you though? You didn't go to college or anything. Cassandra: Well, this is all I will tell you. My father had something top secret, and only my DNA and stuff could unlock this top secret thing. I am the only person left with the same blood as my father. My father was a top CIA agent also. Thats all I can tell you Robb. Robb Hardy: No wonder your dad hated me. If he was a CIA agent, why didn't he ever bust me? Cassandra: Because he loved you like a son. Robb, I also want you to meet your daughter..... Rachael. Rachael: Dada? Robb Hardy: ::Eyes Water:: Yes, I am your daddy. I can't believe you guys are alive, it's a shocker. But now what am I supose to do.... We never got divorced and you were suposedly dead, so now I got two wives. Cassandra: Robb, I just want you to know..... That I love you. ::Scene:: Robb Hardy and Cassandra begin to kiss, and keep going for 15 minutes, and finally Robb begins to speak. Robb Hardy: Listen, Robb Jr. is at his grammie's house, and Veronica is there too, Veronica is healing still from a gun shot wound she got 2 weeks ago, and since I am on the road a lot she isn't home. So, you wanna come to my place? Cassandra: Yes! ::Scene:: Robb Hardy and Cassandra and there daughter Rachael, walk to Robb's car a block away. Robb starts it and takes off quickly, as if he wants to get home really fast. Within minutes Robb makes it to the house, and gets out of the car. Robb gets into the house and puts Rachael in little Robb's bed. Robb and Cassandra go to Robb bed room as the door closes. They are in there for hours, Cassandra moaning and screaming, and the baby luckily still stays asleep. After five hours of love making, Robb falls asleep with Cassandra in his arms. Robb and Cassandra both wake up at 12:34 P.M. and start going at it again. After about three hours, the baby begins to cry, and they have to stop. Robb Hardy: I love you Cassandra....... I've missed you for so long. Cassandra: Robb, I never thought we'd be together again but we are, it is awesome. I have missed you for so very long. Robb Hardy: Well I thought you were dead these two years, and your actually not. I can't believe it, I just don't know what to do. Cassandra:Listen Robb, theres no problem wit having two wives, Veronica won't know about me, alright? Robb Hardy: Good idea. I don't know how this will work, but it's worth a shot. Cassandra: Robb, it's been so long, the only piece of you I got was spying on you, and watching NGW. When you left EPW I was sad because I could not see you, but now that your in NGW I could see you all the time. I you win in this cruiserweight PTC tournament. Robb Hardy: Don't worry I will, they are bitches, and they don't know what there in now.... They will lose the tournament, no doubt about it. They don't even know who I am.... But soon enough.... They will.. ::Doorbell:: ::Scene:: Robb Hardy walks up to the door and opens it. Standing there is "The Wolverine" TJ CODAIR! Codair has a cigarette in his mouth, and his hands are in his pockets. Robb Hardy: TJ! Haven't seen you seen you since EPW, which has been awhile.... But whats up man? TJ Codair: Nothing man, I just came by to see you. Robb Hardy: Alright, well, come in. ::Scene:: TJ Codair walks into the house and he looks at Cassandra and it looks like he has seen a ghost. TJ Codair: Holy shit man, is that..... That..... Cassandra? Cassandra: Yes it's me. Robb's first wife. TJ Codair: I thought you died back in the EPW days? I thought Red Wolf killed you? We almost killed Red Wolf because of that. Cassandra: No, the FBI payed him to make up that story, why don't you think he didn't get arrested? The FBI or CIA whatever they are needed me for something, and I had to help them or else I would be arrested. They can do anything these days. Robb Hardy: Damn police, they don't know what kind of shit they have put me through. Nights of tears and bad dreams. Some nights I didn't even get sleep, and when I did I would be woken up by nightmares. DAMN FEDERAL AGENTS! Cassandra: I think they were CIA.... Yeah they were. Robb Hardy: So what....... I don't care what they were.... FUCK THE GOVERNMENT IS ALL I GOT TO SAY! TJ Codair: ::Takes a drag out of his cigarette:: Robb, I came here to wish you good luck in this PTC Tournament, I have a good feeling that you may win. Robb Hardy: Yeah, I will just do my best against Justin Walker and Ian Feltzer. TJ Codair: Listen Robb, you need get to going to raise hell Robb rather then little bunny rabbits, with flowers of love Robb! Robb Hardy: HELL YEAH! TJ Codair: And who's gonna raise some damn hell with "THE GANGSTA" ROBB HARDY? Robb Hardy: TJ CODAIR DAMN IT! TJ Codair: Damn right Robb, you and me are gonna go out tonight and raise some hell, we are gonna party like animals! It will be great! We will have a pre celebration! Robb Hardy: Yeah, that'd be cool, but I don't think I can go. I want to spend some time with Cassandra. It's been so long. I just can't do it TJ. I can't. I need to be with her. Cassandra: Robb, whats going to happen between Veronica and I though? Robb Hardy: Well, you'll just be my little..... Secret.... Cassandra: Really? But Robb, I want you all. I never had to share you, but the CIA made me leave you. I hate this, they ruined my life! Robb Hardy: Don't worry Cassandra, I'll figure something out, I love you and Veronica both, I just don't know what in the world to do. But I promise something will be figured out. But right now I need to focus on this match. I need to put my heart and soul into it. No matter whats happening in my life, I must forget about it during this match, or else it will hold me down. Cassandra: Just win it for me Robb, please. TJ Codair: Robb, I know you can do it. Back in EPW, you beat me, and you were even EPW Champion. You defeated all the odds and proved everyone wrong. Now you can do it all over again. Just believe in yourself. I mean look at all these stats, EPW Champion, EPW American Champion, EPW High-Velocity Champion, EPW Tag Team Champion, and EPW Hall of Famer.... You were also ranked 8th and 10th place in PTC rankings. Can you get any better then that? Maybe, but you not only have the criteria for this, but you have the skill. Robb Hardy: You know what Teej? Your right. I am gonna go out there, and win this, it doesn't matter how long it takes, I will win it no matter what. I will put my life on the line for this! I will! Cassandra: Just do you best honey, just do you best. Robb Hardy: Well, all I got to say is, Ian Feltzer, Justin Walker, for being in this tournament and facing me.... "You've Just Dun Fucked Up!" ::Scene:: The scene fades with TJ, Robb, and Cassandra all standing there catching up on things. ::Scene Fades::
