Date: 2004-04-01 Normaly I would be getting ready for a match in my home fed, Wrestling Midwest but right now I am preparing myself for a tournament 30 or more people entered in a tournament to prove who is the best Cruiserweight in the ptc, with a weight limit of 225 I barely making I am 6'2 225 I am Fury the WMW Greatlakes champion, and I am going back to my roots my roots of highflying no stop speed and action, representing the WMW along with me is Primetime and Dawn Mcfarlane and the three of us plan on bringing the respect that the WMW deserves, well shit I know I do. (The scene opens to the WMW arena, Fury is in the ring walking around with the Greatlakes title around his waist, he stops and looks out at the empty arena, then looks and stares at the camera) Fury:Its a well known fact that in every single federation you turn your t.v. to, or every single federation that you go and watch there are always those wrestlings that stand out, there are always those select few that do thing no one else could think about or do things only few can dream of, and there is a special name for these wrestler, in the wrestling world we call them cruiserweights. (Fury smiles and begins to pace alittle he looks down at the WMW title around his waist and pats it) Fury: And I myself am glad to say that I am a crusierweight I am one of those guys that you will see, will risk my own body to get the job done, I am one of those guys that will take to the skys any given minute, hell its what I do I live and love the thrill, and I am very excited to face others like me. (Fury unbuckles the Greatlakes title) Fury:You know I could sit here and brag about how I am the best in the WMW and how I am on the top of the world in my federation, but to be all honest non of you give a damn, none of that matters the only thing that matters is the tournament, the only thing that matters is representing my federation and showing the PTC that we shouldn't be looked past that we are indeed contenders to be the top federation all throughout the PTC, we had Aws man go against your champion, sure he came up short but you wouldl think cause of the fight he put up against them that he and the federation as a whole would get alot more respect but did that happen? (Fury shakes his head) Fury:Sadly it didn't, things stayed the same, when asked about the WMW there was alot of " I don't knows" alot of question marks and confused looks on a bunch of faces, and as a wrestler hell as the figure head the top in the WMW it bugs me it pisses me off, and I thought and thought of a way to make it stop I thought of a way to get us noticed, and then the Cruiserweight tournament was mentioned, and it hit me, I thrive on the high risk moves I love putting my body on the line to get the job done, then why not join this tournament, why not bring the skill and the Fury that all the WMW fans have come to love to the ptc, why not give all the others in the ptc a taste of Fury and what the WMW is truely made of, why the hell not go put my name on the sign up sheet, show up and win this thing. (Fury shrugs his shoulders, the draps the Greatlakes title over his shoulder) Fury:When it comes outta my mouth it sounds so damn easy, join the tournament and win the thing, go in there against the best from other feds and see how I fair, I mean its like I am going in there blind,I don't know much about the PTC but what I do know is that there is alot of good talent in this thing , I know that each round is going to be a nail bitter, that with every round and every star that has entered this thing they all have one goal and thats to represent for there federation, to prove that they are the best in the whole damn cruiserweight in the whole ptc, I mean that would open doors and put a spot light on that person and his/her fed, thats why I have to win, thats why I have to be the one that has his hand raised in victory. (Fury smiles, then a sudden frown comes on his face) Fury:But fate so made it that I have to be one of the lucky people that has drawn a triple threat, and from what I hear its going to be pretty damn tough, its going to be a challenge and I am ready to meet it head on, Fury the best Crusierweight in the PTC by god that has a good ring to it, when this starts you can bet your ass I will be ready to bring the trophy home to the WMW. (Fury walks out the ring and the scene) FADES TO BLACK ooc:This bits ass I know, sorry about that but some family things came up and I couldn't really focus .
