I didn't start the fire, I just tossed gas on it (JA's IG21 Thread)

26 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
26 June 2006 Fade into an Empire Pro Wrestling backdrop, white backdrop with the letters "EPW" in black, only the EPW is crossed out in red spraypaint and underneath, the letters "PTC" are sloppily written in the same red. In front stands six-foot-nothing, two-hundred-fifteen-and-three-eighths pounds... and nothing... does that even make any sense? Anyway, he's there, dressed in a pair of khakis, a t-shirt that says "Huked on fonixs werkz 4 Archibald MackGreggggerr" and of course, the trademark lucha mask, holding a microphone. This man is Jericoholic Anonymous, and you're all in a lot of trouble now. Behind him is a pigtailed, buxom young blond woman wearing black horn-rimmed Weezer glasses and a spaghetti strap dress. She's Lollipop. Anyway, JA holds the microphone to his face. He's gonna say something. Let's listen. JA: My name is Aldo Montoya. You killed my career. Prepare to die. Lolli: Babe, wrong speech. This isn't, like, for your WWE tryout parody video. JA: Yes... I knew that. JA grumbles. JA: Anyhoo, for those of you who don't know, my name is Jericoholic Anonymous. Some people call me Jay-Aye. Others call me Jerko, Jackass or something else a little less flattering. I'm used to it you know. I've lived in Philadelphia for most of my life, and that city is about as polite as My Cousin Vinny. But they'll tell you what they think, honest, truthfully. They won't sugarcoat it, and that's why I love Philly and being a Philadelphian. They tell it like it is. And now, I'm about to tell it like it is. You monkeys don't stand a chance. 'Oh-em-Gee' you're probably all saying to yourselves right now. 'Who is this cocky cretin? Why does he think he can come in and beat us, the greatest wrestlers in the world? What drug was my mom on that made her name me Archibald? Was it peyote? And furthermore, what was I smoking when I thought being a twenty-five-hundred year old vampire was a good idea...' Lolli: (interrupting again) Babe, he like doesn't wrestle in that circle anymore and stuff. JA: Oh really? I guess the next thing you're going to tell me is that in order for him to believably play a real vampire, he had to bend the rules and cheat, creating a firestorm around him so great that it could flash fry a buffalo in thirty seconds? Lolli: Like, basically. JA: That makes sense. All it was missing then was Homer Simpson saying "But I'm hungry now." Lolli: I think he, like, might have. JA: I am intrigued and wish to subscribe to your newsletter. JA rubs his chin and turns back to the camera. JA: Anyway, back on point here, there's a reason why I'm so confident. It's because I'm among the best at what I do. I'm a damn good wrestler, but that goes without saying. Everyone in this match is. But what I am that none of you are is a first-class trash talker. That's right, I know how things run over here. You guys flash back to traumatic times in your lives or peer into your every day dealings, like driving to your church group or making pot brownies or even hanging out at a bar. Man you don't even have cameras there... wait a second, then how do I even know what's going on? Does not... compute... how do I know... where's the remote... gaaaahahahahaah... domo... arigato for... Mr. Roboto... My head is going to explode for Christ's sake. Now, that's all well and good, and I'm not knocking what this little corner of the wrestling world has become, but let's face it, you super-evolved primates have lost the art of getting inside someone's head with the words you say. You all subconsciously think it's beneath you, and when the cameras roll and the chance to come back and trash talk comes up, you look at it like it's nostalgia. Well, for you it might be, but for me, it's my bread and butter. It's my meal ticket. It's my... well, I should stop talking about food or else the woman is going to get hungry and pester me. Lollipop rubs her belly and shoots a look at JA. JA: See? But anyway, it's painfully obvious from our first offering, one Darth Vardaman, a self-proclaimed Sith Lord. Christ, and I thought the vampire was bad. Well bucko, Jedi tricks only work on the weak-minded, and the Force, it ain't strong with you. If it was, maybe you could have made some sense. Even if Yoda was intelligible if you changed the order of the words. I mean, people shriek in the streets and tell me my whole game is cheesy, but if I'm cheesy, than you're the State of Wisconsin, Darthy Poo. The point is though, I don't need a lightsaber to rip you apart. Just my own bare hands. Hopefully, none of you other dopes are as lame as this guy is. I mean, it's hard to top him, especially seeing that he'll probably be forking over his entire purse from this match to George Lucas for royalties. I don't have very high hopes though. I mean, Cher Silver? This is the best you got? Some guy who's the most famous for riding the Queen's coattails to glory in the eff-Dubya-oh and not-so-subtly infringing on my gimmick of Gimmick Infringement? ® Seriously, Sonny D-Light, you washed up, bearded dirty old man, did your desk job as commissioner of Aye-See-Dubbya not thrill you anymore? Or was training Manpower giving you a stiffy to get back in the ring? Well, it's nice and all that you're having these comeback thoughts, but the truth is, you've always been inadequate in your career, you've always been in someone else's shadow. Whether it be Manhands Troy or Pawn Black or Lowell Dot Co Dot Uk, you've never shone through. Hell, you even chose to emulate a supporting cast member rather than a big star. Commodore Charisma? Way to pick the lesser light there. And before you start giving the inspirational speech, it ain't happening here either. As for anyone else in this match, blah blah blah, I'm sure you've all got something you think is important to say. And I'm sure I'll respond to it and make you all look more foolish than Craig Miles trying to sober up in the middle of Las Vegas. I'm going to do exactly what I say I'm going to do. Burn you like Paris Hilton burns pot roast in front of the cameras, run circles around you in the ring and then collect the bounty on Archibutt's head. And you know what the best thing is? Not only will I come out of this smelling like roses with yet another title around my waist, it won't make this situation any better. Think about it. I'm not a member of a Pee-Tee-See member fed either. And what's worse, I'm this cocky little ant of a man who is from a whole other world. I bet that scares the living piss out of every single one of you, that you'll end up worse off than before. That's what gets me off at night. Well, that and Lollipop's vagina. Lolli sticks her tongue out at JA. JA: Heh... but yeah, it would be fan-fucking-tastic to come here and wipe the smirks off each and every one of your faces, Archinutsac included. Because you see, I outclass the interloper in every way possible. I'm much better looking than he is... take my word for it, because the mask doesn't come off. You won't find any awkward grade school pictures of me. My ridiculous name doesn't sound pretentious. And the best reason... ...well, EYE'll at least entertain you in the process, and that'll make you even more pissed off. So c'mon, hit me with your best shot, Pee-Tee-See. I'm waiting. Because the way I see it, I'm not leaving here without the Extreme Title and without fanning the flames that the interloper already started. Fade. ---- 1 July 2006 Another soundstage, this time, there are two banners on the wall behind. A smaller banner is draped across the larger banner. On the larger one, you can make out the tops and bottoms of the letter A, the number 1 and the letter E, while the smaller banner reads "Nope, this is still PTC." Once again, Jericoholic Anonymous, wearing his ring gear and a t-shirt with Archibald MacGregor's grade school picture crossed out with a big red X on it, is standing by with a microphone. His ebullient girlfriend Lollipop once again stands in the background. JA: So Darthy-poo, you want any help packing your bags for the Extreme Championship match? Seems like you already think you have your ticket punched and all, so hey, maybe I'll be a good sport and humor you. Maybe I'll just stick a few front row tickets to the big event in your overnight bag, seeing that's about as close to the Extreme match you're going to get, and beacuse of that, I'm not even sure why you need to concern yourself with Red Spock or Kyo Sushimongo or Kay-Wolf, the Amazing Wrestling Transvestite Emo Kid. You're not facing them, Force Lightning. You're facing me and some other dude with his head up his ass about automatically winning this thing. And they say that Arr-Two Dee-Two has delusions of grandeur. That bucket of bolts ain't got nothin' on you, Sith-head. You see, you bemoan the fact that this is the best Pee-Tee-See can throw at you, like it's a waste of your time. Well, I'm sorry to hear that, old chum, but after the match is over, it will have been a waste of your time. I mean, much like it was a waste of your parents' time the thirty seconds they spent in the sac conceiving your sorry excuse for a Force Master's ass, or much like it was a waste of whomever it was who trained you... in wrestling, not in the ways of the Jedi, it will be a waste of time for you to head into this ring just to get neck snapped via the Karelin Driver and your shoulders pinned to the mat. You're not onto the Title match yet. You've got to deal with me first. I've won Championships wherever I've gone. I've raised head-dropping to an art just below neo-Classicism but somewhere above painting with feces. And of course, you won't be able to get your hands on me, because I'm not just quick... I'm sudden. JA winks at the blatant use of his catchphrase. JA: And when your title hopes are gone in a sudden, well, at least you can say you got in some pretty good jabs at the Extreme Title competitors. Well, you could if you weren't such a retard who sucked at trash talking, much like you suck at Force choking, flying star cruisers and at life in general. As for the other clown... I'm not even going to address him, because he did the same damn thing that Sith-face did. It's a damn shame that no one seems to want to be in this match. I mean, of the two other people who showed barely even mentioned it. Seriously, is it a bad thing that I'm the only one who wants to be in this match? It's not that I'm just happy to be here. It's not. I just like going to different places and making as many different people possible my bitches. I have two now, and there will be a whole lot more once I win this match. And I will, because I want to be here. I'm the only one. Why is that? Why is it that the two other douche-monkeys who showed up don't want any part of this match, or why the other folks who said they'd be here haven't arrived yet. What could it be? I could be a pompous ass and say it's because they're scared of me, but really, my ego isn't that big... yet. No, there's only one reason why they're not here. It's because Artichoke MacGreggings isn't the prize catch that he and everyone else make him out to be. Everyone wants you to believe that this turd-burglar is the biggest threat to Pee-Tee-See because he doesn't belong to a member fed and has the Extreme Championship. For a minute there, I believed it. I thought everyone would be chomping at the bit for a chance to take this turkey down and bring pride back to Pee-Tee-See. But something funny happened along the way. No one bought all the hype surrounding our friend Artichoke. He's not viewed seriously, no matter what anyone would have you believe. He's harmless, because in the end, he's still the same as any one of you. All in all, he's as impotent as Bob Dole before the invention of that little blue penis pill. He's got as much bite as a newborn child. I dare anyone to prove me wrong on this, because if he wasn't, there'd be more than just three people gunning for a shot to get into a match to take him down. No, Archie, you're toothless. You're a joke. No one respects you and no one takes you seriously. If they did, they'd pay more attention than this. Hell, I'm not even here because of you. I'm here because of the gold you have and because I want to show this whole world what a real interloper looks like. You think you're causing hysteria now? Well, if you do, then you're even more delusional than Sithy McSithlord, practioner of autoerotic asphyxiation. The real hysteria starts now, when I plow through this match and the rest of the field afterwards and claim the Extreme Championship for myself. Fade.
