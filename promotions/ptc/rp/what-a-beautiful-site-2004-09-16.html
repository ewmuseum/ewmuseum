What A Beautiful Site

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 -[*The Thoughts*]- Your eyes are closed as you find yourself deep in meditation. Your heart rate is steady as you lose yourself in thought. Then it happens. You fill the cold course texture of the object pressed against your head. Your mind begins to race as you try to think your way out of it. Do you open your eyes? No. Any quick movement may leave you laying on the floor in a pool of your own blood. Do you try to strike quickly hoping to catch your assailant by surprise before he has an opportunity to strike? Possibly… …but what if you miss? What is the right reaction? What do you do, when a man, holds a loaded gun to your temple? -[*The Truth*]- There is always silence before the strike. A momentary pause where the victim knows and understands what is about to happen. But he can’t stop it…and neither can you. Everyone has those dreams of gander, born with stars in their eyes. Whether it’s the urge to become a doctor, lawyer, banker, or even the all star athlete, you believe that nothing can stand in your way of reaching the mountain top. Until one day that dream seems to have turned into a nightmare… A nightmare because you are no longer looking through the eyes of babies but through the mirror of reality. There is an even larger question that may have to be answered; what do you do when your dreams have been swept away by an unforeseen accident, a bump in the road, a splinter in the lion’s foot, or an event that not only could end your career but the whole of your life as well? Though unclear…you smile. You find yourself at a spot in life that you never wanted to dream of being, on the edge of death. Running thoughts through your mind of not being able to hug your spouse, or kiss your children to sleep one more night, but this is all you have, these few seconds to ponder on how you want to live them and suddenly… ‘BANG’ Your dead. You must now realize that this has been the greatest thing ever to happen to you within your life. You have been cleansed of all sins that have clung to you for most of your life. You can live the way you want to live now…alone. No lies, no cheating…not more grudges…this is you… …this is your delight…your joy. Let’s take the time to think about this… All the signs are pointing to no. The critics say that you can’t get the job done… …a mountainous plateau that seems unreachable and soon you begin to lose your focus; self doubt creeps its way in and then something happens, something that shakes you to your foundation. It’s the sign that you’ve been waiting for…and finally... you stare me in the eye. ------------- b]-[*The Story*]- “They fight in the openness of his space with bright jagged waves of electricity. The two tango, back and forth, no one giving an inch and each stroke is seen for miles. What magnificent seas of color; jumping to and fro just knowing that each hit creates a monstrous wave of sound like herds of elephants stampeding down a moon lit alley.” And there I stand--staring into the night from a place where I used to come to alone as a kid during the tough times-the times where you feel completely broke down and you think to yourself why you keep going. The sky is lit with the full moon glistening across the horizon of the water from a dock of which I stand; as close to the edge as possible because it feels like I’m flying. The water glistens ever so beautifully as the win blows a few locks of my hair out of my eyes. “The silence of the night makes it even more powerful now. As the tears of our ancestors pond the black tar marked pavement and earth shattering stillness consumes every creature, they know that his work is at hand. Sins being washed away of those whom have passed us by, not knowing if ours will be next. Oh how beautiful the grayness is that swallows us whole. Asking no questions, giving no answers, but only pondering the mysteries of life’s ever changing hands.” I rub the haze from my eyes with my course hands. “I stand gazing out at you as you go about your work, and all I can say… …is what a beautiful sight.” As I stand here, I tried to sink my mind into the thoughts in life that usually are found forgotten. I try not to think of the downside of things of life, but to acknowledge the reason why I find myself where I am today. “I am respected, I am strong, and I am smart. I have the attributes to excel in life but I too, like every living thing on this earth, have my downfalls. I am not perfect. I do not always know the answers to life‘s questions, I find myself insecure at times, and there are many times where I find myself defeated.” I pull out the pack cigarettes from my pocket and take out the last one. I crumble up the empty pack and put it back in my pocket--this world is too beautiful to destroy. I reach in my other pocket and pull out my Zippo. I snap it open and on the third flick, I am successful on lighting it. Pulling the flame up to my cigarette, my face glows and you can mark out the scars from my career. I suddenly snap the Zippo shut and put it in my pocket. The cigarette glow is faint yet distinct as it glows brighter as I breathe in the toxins that do nothing good for me, but relax me. I exhale a cloud of blue smoke and stare back out into the distance. “There are many things in life that make me take the time to think. In a few days I find myself stepping into the ring once again to accomplish a goal of 180 other men and women--to advance to the next round of the GTT4 inside of Primetime Central. I remember the last GTT I was in and I did make it to the second round, but that was as far as I got. More than two years later, I find myself here once again, with the same goals in my head. This time though, I won’t focus on winning so much, but what I will start focusing on is who I am competing against. I will soon find myself face to face with new UWF superstar Alex Grey…” I take another drag of my cigarette and release a small chuckle as I exhale. “…Also known as Payne. Payne huh? That’s an intimidating one. But anyways, I realize I must start focusing on the things that matter such as who I am going up against without pulling the ego out of the back pocket and assuming that I know I will win because that has been my problem in the past and I only found myself earning the losses myself, and also focusing on the reason why I do this--the constant competition inside the ring that revolves around my life as I refer to it as my career. Why do we fight?” My eyes clench as I try to distinguish that answer. “Is it just to prove that I’m right and my opponent is wrong? Is it to prove that I’m a better athlete than my opponent, more seasoned in the arts of war and better trained in technique? What about to prove that I am stronger, faster, or more agile?” I extinguish my cigarette on the edge of the rail and hold it in my hand. “No, that’s not it at all. Mankind fights for the right to believe in a cause, a movement, an event for freedom. I fight, with the very same purpose to defend my right, my freedom, and my cause to lead a movement for the ones that I love. Entire worlds have been conquered under this very same premise. When men go to war they believe that their fighting to protect the innocent--their babies, their wives, their mothers, their fathers, son’s, daughters, friends, and numerous other loved one’s. Sometimes when I think of my opponents, I think about how they feel about this topic. I wonder if Alex Grey believes in fighting for what he believes in? I wonder if he believes in the phrase ‘by any means necessary’.” I look up at the star blanketed night as something pops in my mind. “Oh yes, that is correct. He fights for the sole purpose of revenge and only revenge for some reason regardless or not if he knows who he finds himself toe to toe with. Revenge…a dish best served as the old saying goes. What happens when he walks the path of vengeance only to be foiled by determination? What happens when his wrath wasn’t enough to get the job done? What would happen if his target proved him wrong?” I smirk as I realize that making sense with my words and focusing on the playing field prior to running on it clears a lot of unanswered answers. I turn around to a lit path back into what seems to be a quiet city about two miles down. I start making my way off of the dock and soon reach a dirt path. I pull the balled up cigarette pack in the garbage can nearby including the cigarette butt. “There are no rules of engagement when it comes to war; no right way or wrong way to take the fight to those who may oppress you. All you have is your morals and values for thy fellow man--in the art of war do those words even exist. When your enemy doesn’t focus on those things that matter in order to achieve anything in life, or when their actions go far beyond the ideas of winning and losing, far beyond purpose, far beyond a movement and when their actions border on insanity is when you know that you are one step above them. He pleads for me to stop him, and that plea I can respond to because it is something that I enjoy to do. “ I begin to walk through the lit path as I stop and turn my head once more and gain the relief that the view of the night over the water creates on me. I turn my head forward and begin to walk again until I fade into the darkness a bit further down the path. ------------- -[*The End*]-
