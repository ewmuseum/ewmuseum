[IG71] Civilization #7: The Perps

3 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Hell's waking up every goddamn day and not even knowing why you're here.” - Marv, Sin City 04/01/2010 Afternoon. Training for my matches has been tough here recently especially considering that the financial backer of the group I’m affiliated with, which is named Civilization, has decided to have me investigate a series of murders within the organization. This is clearly interfering with my ability to train for my wrestling matches. Wrestling is my main career but this has completely taken over all aspects of my life. My side job as a hired hitman also has become a major part of the this too with me doing all of the dirty work that Natasha doesn’t want anyone to know about. But here I am, finally able to train at the gym inside the facility when my radio starts going off. However I decide to ignore it and continue working out before some God awful thing happens that throws my day completely off track. Soon the radio goes silent which brings a smile to my face. However, only moments later, my cell phone starts buzzing. I let it buzz four times before it goes silent. There’s no way I’m letting anything or anyone stop me from being the best in the world at what I do. I’m on a mission to show the PTC and everybody else in the wrestling world to prove that I am the superstar that I always knew I could be. As my mind starts to drift off again with thoughts of wrestling glory and gold, my phone begins buzzing yet again which only causes me to let out a sigh. Looks like the cards aren’t in my favor today which sucks. I think I can win this Infinite Gauntlet for once but nothing seems to be going my way. Hopefully there’s not another dead body for me to investigate… * * * 04/02/2010 In the eastern section of the main Civilization facility that is located in an undisclosed location somewhere in the world, the von Doom Boys are walking in one of the lesser used hallways in the facility. It’s mainly used in the day time but since it’s five in the morning it’s basically barren of anyone. The fact that energy saving lighting sucks this time of the night doesn't help matters either. The two men in black, leather trench coats continue walking down the hallway until they hear something crash in the darkness behind them. Both Bebop and Rocksteady stop when they hear the crash. Simultaneously they look behind them to see a bucket rolling across the hallway that somehow was knocked off of a shelf. They give each other a look before turning to look in the direction of the bucket once more. Both of them know first hand about what is going on within the organization. They found a body of a murder victim that was killed. The body was part of series of serial murders that has been taking place within the protected base that houses the Civilization organization. The fact that they are in the main building where every single one of the murders has taken place only puts them more on edge. After a moment of silence, the calm before the storm finally dissipates as Bebop von Doom gets annoyed to the point where he calls out to whomever it was that knocked over the bucket. “Who’s there?” he calls out. Rocksteady von Doom quickly joins in on the act as they get no response. “Yeah, who is it?” Rocksteady yells at the mysterious presence in the hallway with them. Still no answer from whomever it is so the von Doom Boys decide to try and get nasty with them. “If you don’t answer, we’re going to pulverize you!” Rocksteady says in a loud voice. “Are you ready for von Doom Sensation?!” Bebop yells at whomever it is at the top of his lungs. After he yells this, someone comes out from behind the shadows. The von Doom Boys stop looking angry before smiling at whomever it is. “Why hello there,” Bebop says to the man. “Didn’t know you were on this side of the building. What’s up, man? What are you doing up so early?” The man then pulls out a knife and quickly stabs Bebop on the stomach three times! Before Rocksteady can react, he pushes over Bebop and stabs Rocksteady in the stomach twice. The von Doom Boys fall to the ground with a thud. Bebop is conscious enough to crawl over and pull an alarm on the wall as the attacker puts his knife away. As soon as the alarms go off, security comes rushing over to see what the alarm is all about. However someone beat them to the victims. When the Civilization Security members get to the site of the incident they are greeted by Slash, one of the four members of the Roadkill Warriors. “What happened here?” one of the security members asks him. “There’s been another murder,” he tells them. “I think the guy ran off in the other direction.” The security team members quickly call in what happened through their radios before checking on the bodies as Slash looks on. “They’re still alive,” one of the security members says to his partner. “Get a medical team here immediately.” * * * Twenty minutes later… After a fire alarm went off in the building, I was called from my quarters to go to Natasha Smirnoff’s office about it. My partner Juan Martinez also was sent there but told to wait outside while I went in to talk with Natasha. Once the door closed behind me upon entering, while sitting at her desk, Natasha looks very concerned which is the complete opposite of how she was the previous times I talked with her. She was angry and frustrated those times but now she actually seems human which scares me more than I would ever let on to anyone. Natasha motions for me to sit down in the chair at the front of her desk which I do before she starts to speak. “von Doom, that alarm was a part of another attempted murder,” she tells me. She looks a little choked up and before she can continue with what she was going to say, I step in to tell her what she already knows I’m going to tell her. “I know whose behind the murders here inside the facility,” I tell her as she gives me a quixotic look. “Who are you talking about?” “The people behind the killings are the von Doom Boys,” I tell her as she seems a bit taken aback by this. “They’re mentally unstable and have a history of violence plus they found one of the bodies you investigative.” Natasha still seems to be a bit upset which troubles me since she normally is the most cold hearted, ruthless woman on the planet Earth. “James, I’m sorry to inform you of this but the von Doom Boys were both stabbed multiple times earlier today,” she tells me as now I’m the one with the look of shock on their face. “They’re in critical condition and being treated by Dr. Feelgood right now as we speak.” “What?” “And the only reason I’m not kicking you out of the organization is because I know that you’re friends with them.” This makes me raise an eyebrow. I thought for sure they were the ones behind all of this but apparently I was wrong. “What happened?” “One of our patrols found them on the east end of the complex,” she replies. “He was found by Slash of the Roadkill Warriors.” “Slash?” “Yes, I said Slash. Now you get the hell out of here before you’re busted down to being on the patrols too,” she says as now she’s back to her usual scowling self and pointing at the door for me to leave. That’s an encouraging sign. “You want me to stop doing my investigation?” She hesitates for a few seconds before finally answering my question. “Get out of here before I have you killed,” she responds, again motioning for me to leave out the door. Now I’m really pissed over this garbage. She’s still in this delusional fantasy land where Blue Jay and Cave Hulk are the only people who could possibly be behind the killings. “You do that, bitch, and I’ll kill anybody you throw in my direction,” I growl at her as I stand up out of the chair before pushing it back in. “You know what I’m capable of. Anybody you send to track me down will be killed and you know it! So don’t fuck with me, bitch!” She’s just as pissed as I am right now. “YOU ASSHOLE!” she yells at me at the top of her lungs. This must be what marriage feels like. “And don’t even get me started on how you botched up the investigation on Blue Jay and Cave Hulk with your little stunt. You let them get away and now they committed another murder.” Everything she just said has blown me out of the water like a torpedo hitting a submarine. Is she really this dense? I really hope not considering that she is the money behind this entire organization. “At a place they don’t even know about? How in the hell can they commit a series of murders in a place they don’t know the location of? That’s the fucking stupidest rationale I’ve ever heard in my life. It‘s an inside job.” “Are you saying that the von Doom Boys stabbed themselves to get you off their case?” “I was wrong about them but I do know…” She cuts me off before I can finish which only pisses me off more. “Then you’re probably wrong about whomever you are thinking about now. Get out of here and cool off. Get your head together too because you’re WAY off in your thinking on this situation. Civilization members do not kill other Civilization members.” “Whatever,” I mutter to her as I shake my head and storm out, slamming the door behind me. This is becoming an all too familiar thing as Juan gets up out of his chair in the waiting room to follow me out. I’d better tell him what the hell is going on before one or both of us gets killed. * * * An hour later… After the argument in Natasha Smirnoff’s office, me and my investigative partner Juan Martinez are walking outside the main facility in the forested area nearby. I honestly don’t want any prying ears to hear what I’m going to tell Juan about what’s going on in regards to the perps who are killing everyone. Juan’s anxiously been waiting since I slammed Natasha’s office door before storming down the hallway to find out the truth. Now he’s about to get all the answers from me and honestly I need to get all of this off my chest because if I get killed, they win. If I tell Juan and we both get killed, they win. If one us makes it, then their plan is fucked and that’s exactly what I want to happen. “Why is Natasha so mad at you?” Juan asks. “Basically she’s pissed because I told her that Blue Jay and Cave Hulk weren’t behind the murders,” I tell Juan who seems to be a bit shocked. Honestly I don’t blame him for this reaction. He knew I had a theory but probably thought that it involved those two men. Clearly it does not and I know for a fact now that the people behind it are the complete opposite of those two. “What I’m going to tell you is so far fetched I can barely believe it myself to be honest about it.” Juan gives me a look as we continue walking through the forested area. “James, with you, anything you say isn't going to come as a surprise,” he responds. This brings a little smirk to my face. I’ve heard that so many times in my wrestling career over in SCCW, the PTC, and various other places that it’s become almost commonplace to hear but still amusing nonetheless. “Well what if I told you that a bunch of rookie Civilization members were the ones behind the killings,” I tell him. Juan gives me a look over this comment like I’m completely out of my mind or something. Normally he’d be correct but this time he’s not. “But the von Doom Boys got stabbed today and are in critical condition,” he says to me. "Dr. Feelgood is trying to save their lies even as we speak." “They aren’t the ones I’m talking about,” I reply to him as he raises an eyebrow over this statement. “I’m talking about the Roadkill Warriors.” This leaves Juan shaking his head in disbelief. The Warriors are the main protection unit within the organization for the highest level members. “But that’s insane,” he replies, still in a state of shock of what I've just told him. “I can see why this is happening, doesn’t surprise me at all. With so many deviants, sociopaths, miscreants, psychopaths, and, well, generally uncivilized people in it that it was bound to happen sooner or later. I think that there may be an actual sub organization within in the group that is trying to kill off the weak until only the strong remain. It’s kind of like what Apocalypse used to do in Marvel Comics all the time in the X-Men comics.” “It only makes sense with Slash being there before the patrol got there when the von Doom Boys were stabbed.” “We need to watch out backs because right now, the only person we can trust is Dr. Feelgood. Even Natasha may be involved in this. Don’t trust anybody until we find everyone involved.” “But how will we do that?” I smile as I think about what I have in mind. This may be the perfect plan to get the job done. “Live bait.” Juan and I smile at each other, nodding our heads in agreement before shaking hands. Nice to know that we were on the same page the whole time without even realizing it. We have a plan and we’re going to burn the whole place down until no one survives.
