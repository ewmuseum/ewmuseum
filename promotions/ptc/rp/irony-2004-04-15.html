Irony.

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-15 It came down to this she was Carys, a female demon hunter and continual fighter for innocent people all over the globe. Inklings of fear were running rampant in her head, like a bullet train taking everything in its path, only without the control that the tracks offer. Since she had departed from her position that demanded about 40% of all the hatred the fans in RPW had and lost three friends immediately with that she had not been able to sleep. In fact she had not been able to do many things. In her mind there was no room for little bits of friendships or cliques: happiness, when you’re trying to please fans twenty four hours a day. Getting her life back on track after the band wasn’t going to be easy. The only thing which came easily to her now was worry; sleep being the thing that rode high, but there was also her old place in ‘Il Mafioso’ that could well be threatened, on top of her suitability as a protector of the good. These days she was left to let those creatures come to her. If she’d had chance to think at all then a few things could well have dawned on her – it’s not that she’s unintelligent, just patiently challenged – those things that would occur would have done if she’d have rested for more than a two hour cycle were: Chryste was always there for her, no matter what her professional choices were…best friends, and that if she devoted a little more time to hunting those blasted creatures of evil, she’d be sleeping like a baby coming down from a five hour sugar fit. Instead she was on bent legs looking up into the seven eyes – really little more than bullet type holes encircled in bloody mounds that made it look as though his eyes had been ripped out by Edward Scissorhands coming down from a Heroine induced nirvana – its bloody spittle dripped from its crooked mouth that held three rows of jagged and broken teeth. Some of its sparse oily tangled hair hung in its eye sockets as though trying to rip out its own brain; if it did indeed have one. All Carys could feel was fear in her body while looking at this thing that should have been shot in the face before it even exited its mother’s womb; if it were indeed born naturally. It’s shrivelled, wart-loaded and snake like tongue, was drawn past those paralyzing rows of teeth to lick at the fear that presented itself as sweat on her silky smooth skin. A fly swooned around the room – probably the first of many, attracted to the stench more foul than anything possibly imaginable – and landed on his disfigured and badly burnt nose; distracting his tongue for the time being as he licked it off. There was a different fear clogging the muffled cries in her throat now; one of complete revulsion. Most of it came from this disfigured and twisted beast that stood hovering over her like a moth to a flame. However there was the smallest part of her that was repulsed at her own actions; she failed to look away, and worse than that….she feared for her life. His both pleasured and pained smile cracked with the sound of her whimpers, sending a small dribble of his murky spit dribbling down his chin, and burning a mark into her marble floor. His malformed ears twisted like they were filled with muscles that were thick in side, deriving a better way to get more revulsion from the girl. “Leave,” her words came out as a plea, unlike the order she had planned on putting choked and restrained with her fear. “How dare … you speak to Niap this way?” each word came out after a wheezy breath as it rattled up what we would call the throat, if he had one. His final breath became much more pained as he felt the thing – it would be unjust to refer to it as a heart – that pumped whatever kept hi going around his rancid, decrepit body. Carys’ left hand hung inside his bear trap-like rib cage and yanked art the muscle. His chest was cold as ice and Carys had to wrench as hard as she could just to get it to budge. Niap looked down on his pray with slight amusement, the pain in his chest was what he lived for as a follower of Etah and he didn’t even flinch. “Foolish human, you can’t kill me.” Carys used her right hand to hold on to the bracer that clad her hand, the extra pressure supplied was appreciated by her other hand as it ripped the muscle from its resting place. “Yeah, well maybe you should’a told me that earlier, fucker?” Carys tossed the muscle to the ground and sighed heavily as she got back to her feet. Just another run of the mill demonic killing, for some this was an extraordinary event, yet Carys has done it four times. Four times too many, she said in her mind. Soon this would have to stop, especially when they were searching her out now. She was supposed to be the hunter, she was- She sighed as her tired and weary blue eyes scraped along the messy dresser where most of her kit stood. Make up, to hide battle scars - like the barely visible one that lay across her chest, and the bruises that still lined her face from weeks past – money, what very little she held on her person, and that which any seventeen year old wrestler would make her prized possession; the RPW Eclipse Champion. -Allowing this whole thing to distract her from what was coming up this week, never mind defending the Eclipse Title every week as she made a promise to do to herself, what has come second nowadays to that is making her way through the rounds of the Prime Time Central Cruiserweight tournament. It’s not so much that she doubted herself going into the ring with Jay Phoenix, it’s not that she doubted her ability to perform, because that would be imbecilic. There are no other seventeen year olds anywhere who’ve matched the success of Carys since she started just over a year ago. What it boiled down to as she sat on the edge of her velvet covered bed spread was that she knew Phoenix could well be the battle of her life, in the ring at least. He had many accomplishments that Carys lacked, he had an in ring finesse that was not to be mocked, and he was considered a veteran in many circles. “I am going to win,” she sounded so resolved, even in the eternally defensive foetal position she had adopted as she sat on her bed, knees drawn into her chin. “I have to, it’s that simple.” She continued mumbling, like the sound of her own voice soothed her, even with that beast in the living room. She swiped her feet down from the bed with a quick withdrawal as her feet banged down on the floorboards, a hollow sound reverberated back at her. Her eyes looked at her reflection in the mirror that adorned the wall in a landscaped fashion. She gave herself an ‘I shouldn’t’ look before putting her feet back down on the carpeted floor. It seemed so warm, so inviting. “No,” again she talked under her breath; the knowledge that she was the online person alive in the apartment was again comforting. Still she felt the terrible pain of something drawing her to that thing she placed under there, her unconscious self. Her conscious self however halted that by going over to the wardrobe; nothing fights a girl’s fashion instinct. Absent mindedly, she pulled a leather piece suit and had it wrapped around her frame in little to no time. It was an old piece that Jade – her mentor – had bought when getting her out of that crappy preppy stage she went through. When she felt the way the leather empowered her as a second skin she still felt somehow odd. She looked down again at the floorboards that were not exposed. “Screw it, I AM GOING TO FUCKING WIN! No matter the cost.” She yelped before diving at the carpet on the floor of the bedroom and ripping it up from where it stood. The floor board as attached to the carpet, the intention behind it to keep everything closed tightly. Once inside the floorboards a blue box was revealed, dust and rust coating its shell in copious amounts around the sides and edges like it had been broken into on more than one occasion. Carys clearly knew the box well as her fingers retraced the locks with their extended nails, a small click and the box opened. The box may as well have held a sign reading ‘cheating stuff’, it held her fingerless mesh gloves, a couple of pound coins – English Sterling working much better than your quarters for a make shift knuckle duster and steel toed boots. An evil laugh accompanied the sudden full on stench of the beast, it had not floated into the room so much as been drug in by the walking corpse and shell of pain that was Niap. Sure you thought he was dead kiddies, but apparently it’s not coming that easily to her tonight. “How?” she complained, it was a whine at best. “I have more than one heart, stupid hussy.” All Carys could think about while he tossed her through her bedroom window and down to the ground was the irony of having more than one heart and not knowing how to use them at the same time. She could still feel small shards digging into her skin through the leather that adorned her body. Her crawling back, away from Niap – against the dirty gravel of the floor, eventually sliding into grassy, and littered, mud - didn’t help that any. Niap’s broken boils on his hands pussed everywhere, visible even on his black skin and the torrential rain. His hands - that looked scarily boneless; breakable even - drew closer, closer to her throat, wrapping around like coil tightened tentacles. Her own hands went to push away his advances, yet her fingers burnt when they touched his’ juices. Quickly she withdrew and found with desperate dismay that her retreat was halted, by solid stone. Get out of my sight!” a young, spirited and boyish shadow demanded, a flicker of light from his right palm sets aside the few shadows it took to cover his body. His face was covered like the back of his boy in purple cloth, making a sleeve of warmth and dryness around him that would be labelled a cloak. Niap turned his disfigured head, as his body followed suit, revealing the smoky hole Carys had created when she ripped his heart from it. Then everything change, the bright beams of light and fire that seemed to emanate from this child’s hands flew across the open car lot and into the beasts body. What Carys – the demon hunter - couldn’t destroy had been done by a child, a child who approached the bloody remnants of her. “Aunty Carys?” “Huh?” -End.
