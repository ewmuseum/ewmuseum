Moraless #3 (UWF)vs DDT #5 (XUW)

10 March 2004

10 March 2004 At the end of the day, DDT was right, wounds never truly heal. Even after the physical pain has gone, the scars begin to fade and the bones have set, the wounds are always there. However you can take them to mean one of a few things. The next time you face a challenge the wounds may trigger a memory, which in turn could lead to a stray emotion such as fear. That fear could stop you in your tracks causing you to turn away, to back away from the challenge like a scolded dog. That however is not how I intend to work. For wounds can also be a guide, conditioning if you will. Dogs can be conditioned to drool at the slightest sound of a tin opening, a trigger is activated and they know that soon they will be able to eat, we are the same. If we get punched in the face we know it hurts, so if the same person goes to attack you again you will move, it’s as simple as that. The wounds of the past push you forward, not back. What happens though when the wounds are so severe, that you think the pain is never going to end, every muscle and sinew aches and throbs with a burning intensity that makes you want to tear off your own limbs just to quench the pain. You just stop caring… The cool air brushed against my face as I stood atop the hill, gazing down at the town that I called my home, it looked so quiet and peaceful at night time, just a number of glowing lights, from up here you could, if only for a few moments pretend that there weren’t any muggings happening in alleys, women being raped and murdered or homeless people on the street, a sense of tranquillity and relaxation. I allowed myself to smile, something I hadn’t done too often recently, fluctuating in and out of form back in UWF losing almost as many as I had won, yet at the same time being able to take down Seymour Almasy, a milestone in my career. Yet I had blown it when it counted most. “I had it all there in front of me, the chance to become a champion, a champion that meant something not the piddling little now defunct television championship but a title of some worth yet through my own greed I go and blow it.” I spoke to the world in general, I often did this no-one was around to hear my voice no-one to argue against me or reply a chance to let out all the raw emotion I had brewing inside me, without anyone else’s input other than my own. “I decide to try and prove myself elsewhere, try and stake claim to a championship far more brutal than any hardcore title, the PTC extreme title. It was a dangerous division to be involved in, no rules the competitors deciding exactly how to hurt and maim the other man, weather It was a simple last man standing match to something involving explosives.” A wry smile seeped from my face, explosives were always fun the feeling of being flung through the air by something with incomprehensible power was always a buzz. Deep down that was why I had signed up to the division, I didn’t really care about points or rankings, the only reason I had come third in the rumble was the thrill of the fight, I had entered the rumble with men that I had not faced before at all, I didn’t know their styles I knew jack. It was all on impulse and most of all it was fun the pain subsides but the memories of being dropped head first onto a trashcan would last for a lifetime…well after the concussion stopped anyway. It takes a special kind of man to wrestle, to put their body on the line every night but it takes something more to willingly take blows to the head, to have your skin torn and tattered by barbed wire and have your limbs mangled inside the joins of ladders. “You have to be crazy pushed so far to the edge that you just lose all respect for the human body, both yours and your opponents, the division was a prime example of this. Shadow, not exactly a happy name is it. Everyone of us in the division, has issues in the past which we try to turn away from, try to walk away from but no-matter what we do the pain of the past propels us forwards towards our inevitable destiny.” The sky above me rumbled as a number of thick clouds began to be blown across the night sky. I raised my head to view the stars for one last time as they were encompassed by the clouds, their dance in the night sky over for now at least. A small trickle of rain began to fall splashing gently on my forehead before cascading down my face, the scar down my cheek yet another symbol of the past. “Every time you step into the ring in an extreme bout, you always know that it maybe your last; an accidental slip could result in paralysis or even death. It’s a fact of life that accidents happen and that eventually we are all going to die. It used to scare me once, the notion that one day there would be nothing but why should we worry about it? When we are born to the world all we have is this one promise that we will die.” I spoke the words not containing any harshness or bitterness just simple fact, I honestly didn’t care anymore when you had been fucked over the amount of times I had, sometimes you just wanted it to end, when the fighting became too much. “All I know is that when I go down, I’m going to go down fighting” I tried to say this with confidence but my voice wavered, the rain was becoming harder now and beginning to seep through my black jeans onto my legs, I’d forgotten to bring a jacket and my t-shirt was already sodden. I dropped my head and rested it in my hands staring at the floor below, the rain bouncing off the asphalt. “Battle lines have been drawn and I am now to face a man whose initials even spell out the name of a wrestling move, it’s as though destiny has decided that he would be a wrestler, there is just one thing though. I don’t believe in destiny. The decisions we make not the choices we take determine our destiny? It’s all bollocks. I’ve said it before we are born and we die that is all our destiny entails, there is a heap of crap in the middle that we have to sift through in order to get there, a cruel world trying to destroy us all.” I can feel myself getting angry, agitated and I wanted to leave, something inside was telling me to run and hide, but I was sick and tired of hiding. I didn’t want to hide in the shadows anymore, even if it was all I knew. I wanted to touch the light, if only for a little while. “DDT, bring all you have got to the ring, we fight because I saw talent in you, I saw the raw grit deep in your soul that enabled you to progress as far as you did, even with injuries you still came out and called out someone to fight, not caring of your own condition, in that essence we are the same, fighting is what we live for, we have nothing else. I know that you won’t let me down…we are going to paint the town red.” I raised my head and smiled to myself before rising to my feet and walking back down towards town, my view of tranquillity had been tainted by my own mind, tonight would be a sleepless night again. It wouldn’t be for the first time… ---- 16 March 2004 The darkness consumes some people, it has always been that way, the sun will always rise and fall but there will always be those who stray from the path of the righteous and eventually let the darkness take over them. It’s those people that are weak, the lower end of the gene pool unable to face the truth of life and trying to find solace in something that isn’t there, talking to themselves in mirrors, something in their minds telling them that if they speak someone will listen. Why would they listen though? Everyone has their own problems. It seems like a quiet world out there huh; the silence at time can seem deafening but if you could see inside people, hear their thoughts and their worries. It’s deafening. Deal with your problems yourself because at the end of the day, nobody cares, because they are going through their very own problems. You don’t care about them do you? ???: My precious… The room is dark, but the light emanating from the small box in the corner of the room causes sharp outlines of objects to be seen, a small table, a sofa and probably most importantly a person sat on the sofa, staring forwards his eyes wide open. ???:MR FRODO. The sound echoing around the room from its original position of the box reveals the fact that this is not the scene of a man with no options left, talking to himself with some sort of crazy split personality disorder, skulking around in the darkness hiding himself away from the pain and the suffering because he thinks it will help. Oh no this is something much worse, for the man in question is Moraless and he is watching ‘The Lord Of The Rings The Two Towers’ why is this a bad thing I hear you ask, surely it’s just a film albeit a great one. True. Moraless however is on enigma wrapped up in confusion, a lot of the time he will sit there, unaware of the world around him, unhappy confused and angry. Yet occasionally he will change into what can only be described as happy, the sort of happy drug addicts get when they get their first hit in the morning. It’s a release, and he becomes totally relaxed. A little too much though. His mind wonders and his grasp on reality isn’t quite what it should be. Moraless: Ah Arwen, you should have done so much more, with your weapon by your side you should have marched into battle instead of crying for the best part of two hours. There is something about swordplay the way that movement can be so fluid and graceful, yet at the same time so deadly. The time of chivalry is long gone; battles are no longer about honour. It’s just about the overall win. Governments don’t care about who gets harmed along the way, as long as the final result goes their way. I guess that is why the world is slowly crumbling. Moraless slowly rises to his feet and stumbles in the dark towards the television set, flicking it off plunging the room into darkness. Footsteps can be heard, slow calculating for fear of falling over a table most likely, a sharp click and the room was drowned in light, the room was bizarrely tidy, going against the typical conventions that are conjured with a single man living on his own. He strides slowly across his room squinting as he does so, the power of the bulb a bit more than he had expected after a few hours sat in the dark and stares at a large plaque situated proudly on the wall ahead of him a replica of Frodo’s sword from lord of the rings ‘Sting’. He takes it gently off the wall and runs the flat of the blade through his hand, a puzzled look on his face as the cold metal causes him to shiver slightly. Moraless: It’s strange, I see this blade and imagine the pain that could be caused with it, having the blade rammed through skin cutting through like a hot knife through butter, yet something would not be right. There could be the greatest of battles two men fighting for honour and victory yet the victory would mean nothing. I can talk about the lack of nobility and chivalry in these times but it is the way that these things have to be times have changed. Moraless clutches the sword tightly and begins to pace around his living room taking wild swings with ‘Sting’ at seemingly random intervals, the blade slicing through the air with great ease. Moraless: It is and has always been survival of the fittest but with time comes progression. Mankind has evolved from apes for one reason, being able to make the most out of our surroundings, what use is honour, if you will not live to see the world. Do you think that the world will remember any of us when we are gone? No. Once we are gone that is it, the world will continue turning as always and we shall be forgotten. Moraless launches the sword in a large spiralling arc and follows the blade as it lands solidly on the floor the blade stabbing into the carpet and the wooden flooring underneath, the blade wobbles slightly before coming to a complete stop. Moraless’ eyes watch the blade intensely for a second almost anticipating something to happen, but nothing does he turns his back on the blade and stands staring forwards. Moraless: DDT, I understand that things aren’t exactly right for you at the moment, but that is not my fault. You asked for someone to step up to the challenge and I did that. I know I told you that I wanted to fight you out of respect. That was true; however there shall be no honour in the battle. For honour is just a loser’s excuse. How many times has it been said, please let me die with honour. Honour is something that we strive for when we have lost all other hope, all other possibilities have dried up and we just wait for the inevitable. Moraless grins to himself slowly walking towards the sword, his eyes wide as he watches the glistening steel the light from the bulb reflecting off the blade as he takes it in his hand once more raising the blade so that it is eye level. Moraless: So Damien, although this maybe my weapon of choice, don’t expect any honour, as if I get the chance I will more than happily take your weapon and proceed to pummel you with it depending on whatever you choose. I will use anything I can find around the ring, hell I will take someone’s drink can from the crowd if only to tear it in two and slice your skin with it. We are no longer in an age where we can take liberties thinking about whether or not we have done the right or wrong thing. All we can do is fight. And that my friend is what I intend to do. Moraless grins once more before turning away and casually flicking on his stereo situated in the same unit as the television as Fatboy Slim’s ‘Weapon Of Choice’ becomes to play around the room. Moraless: Wow, kind of ironic huh.
