What A Day...! Pt. 2

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 (Very cold looking walls enclose Emilio Dervante who, one week ago was arrested... quite possibly for no apparent reason. Emilio, wearing some baggy blue jeans with his hair up in a pony tail and a Paul Pierce #34 Celtics' home jersey on, is sitting in a steel chair with a silver steel table sitting in front of him. He his slouching in the seat and has his arms crossed staring at the door waiting for someone to walk in. Almost on cue the door swings open and a balding man in his middle ages wearing a navy blue blazer and matching suit walks in and slams his hands down in front of the table as he kicks the door closed with his legs) Investigator: "So you think you're real slick, huh?" Emilio Dervante: "What?" Investigator: "Don't play dumb with me asshole, I know all about your underground scheme." Emilio Dervante: "You know about that?" (Emilio thinks to himself for amount almost fighting with himself to say something or not.) Emilio Dervante: "Ok ok... I only took ONE extra crouton in the lunch line. I know that those British folk like their croutons and stuff but, holmes, I shouldn't get raided for it." Investigator: "So that's what you kid's are calling it these days? Croutons. Back in my day we called it the white pony..." (As the investigator sits back in his chair Emilio puts a confused face on.) Emilio Dervante: "Wait... white pony?" Investigator: "Yeah you know... Coke... Cocaine... Lines... DON'T PLAY DUMB!" Emilio Dervante:"You think I have something to do with cocaine?" (Emilio laughs a bit) Investigator:"You think it's funny? You think it's funny that you can along on your wrestling career, fight a dude and then just get so doped up after that match that it doesn't matter. Do you think that you are above the law? Well you're not, and guess what, we caught up to you. Emilio Dervante:"I've never done coke in my life.." Investigator:"Explain this...!" (The Investigator pulls out a little plastic baggy filled with a white substance.) Emilio Dervante:"Where'd you get that?" Investigator:"Your hotel kitchen!" (Emilio looks away at the "mirror" and then turns back to the investigator.) Emilio Dervante:"Did you test it?" Investigator:"Did we test it...of cou..." (A knock from the other side of the mirror comes and the investigator gets out of the room... after a few minutes the investigator comes back in and sits down with the plastic baggy. He has a disappointed look on his face mixed with defeat.) Investigator:"No... we didn't check it..." Emilio Dervante:"Did you even do a taste test on it? I mean jeez that would be the obvious thing to do." (The Investigator looks up at Emilio and then at the bag in his hands. He opens the sealable top and puts his finger in the white substance. He pulls his finger out and then looks at Emilio.) Investigator:"How do I know this isn't poison?" Emilio Dervante:"I don't know, smell it?" (Emilio shrugs and the Investigator nods and opens the bag wider and goes to sniff it but Emilio jumps up and smacks the bag into the face of the Investigator. The investigator's eyes begin to bleed and Emilio runs around the table shouting...) Emilio Dervante: "IT WAS WRESTLING SALT BITCH! AHAHAHHAHAHAH!" (Emilio rips off the jersey and tosses it at the investigator. Emilio then runs out the door and dropkicks the man who must've been supervising the interrogation. Emilio then rips off his jeans to reveal some tiger stripped underwear. Emilio runs around and jumps up on top of one of the desks in the front of the police station.) Emilio Dervante:"AEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!" (Emilio jumps down and runs out of the police station while the scene stays in the station and goes to a prostitute with an eye patch who is being booked.) Prostitute: I want what he had... (The scene shoots to Emilio Dervante running down the streets shouting and bumping into people. Emilio's hair has now fallen out of its elastic and is flowing wildly in the wind. Emilio bumps into an old man and knocks the man down. Emilio picks up the old man's cane and whacks it against the ground snapping off the handle.) Emilio Dervante: "AEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!" (Emilio continues running after pounding on his chest a few times. Emilio is now hunched and with the broken cane in his hand he his usually his knuckles to run as a gorilla would. Emilio begins to slow down at a phone booth. After circling the both Emilio begins to climb it and one he reaches the top he pounds his chest once again.) Emilio Dervante: ANIMALS COME TO ME! (A pigeon flies by but nothing else comes Emilios way... except for a large swarm of pig... you guessed it, not regular pig, Cops, with tazors and nets and tranqs to put Emilio out. Emilio looks to his left towards the inner side walk and makes a huge leap onto a ledge of a building. Emilio climbs up the fire escape but before he can make it to the top he is hit with a tranq in the back and begins to swing his arms wildly. Emilio makes one last effort to get to the top and then falls... FALLS! In almost slow motion Emilio falls and then...) BOOOM! (A slip and fall awakes Dervante. Emilio Dervante then rubs his head, yawns and rolls over the bed in the direction of the bathroom. Emilio stands up and places his arm up against the wall to use for some support. After a few steps he turns into the bathroom to find the woman from the club, Jenn, sitting on the toilet with her panties down at her ankles and a bra on. She looked up at Emilio and spoke with a very regretful voice.) "I fell..." The hangover almost oozed out of her body as she spoke and tried to sit. (Emilio looks straight ahead, almost staring in your soul.) "Cocaine's a helluva drug..." THE END
