The Year of the Wraith

17 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-17 \ | / The promo opens with footage of a raven haired figure driving through the darkness on a 2004 Honda Interceptor ABS. It is jet black with a wicked spider design done in silver on the body. The figure riding it is hard to see but as soon as the voice over begins it is easy to tell who it is. \ | / "Being given time off is never an easy thing to deal with. I was as close to being fired as anyone could be without having it actually happen. I had been hyped up by many people to get a contract here in WMW and on my very first match I blew it big time. I let down my friends, I let down my family, I let down myself. This really is not about throwing a pity party though. It is about getting back up onto the bull after you have been thrown off a half a dozen times and jumping right back on it another half dozen. I may have the failure tag placed upon me but now that I have a chance to wrestle in front of many people that have never seen me before as well as being in a match sponsored by the PTC, which from what I have been told is a big deal." \ | / The bike stops at a gas station where Wraith pulls up to one of the pumps to fill up his gas tank as it is running pretty low and he’s on a tight schedule to try and make it to start getting ready and to meet with some of the other participants that are representing Wrestling Midwest in this Survival Match. Riding on a motorcycle from Minneapolis to Cleveland is something Wraith enjoys but it is not something that he wants to do repeatedly. After taking time to get things in his mind straightened out he was ready to get back into things and make sure people knew who he was and what he was all about. Wraith walks back out of the gas station and is just about to get back on his bike when his cell phone goes off. He unzips one of the pockets in his cargo pants to get his phone out to look at the caller id. It is a friend of his so he answers it instead of making them leave a message on his voicemail. \ | / "Hey, what’s going on? Oh man not much, I’m on my way back to Cleveland. I needed to spend some time with my girlfriend. She’s just not ready to come down there yet and believe me I don’t blame her. It is not really the fact that Cleveland is bad it is just that there is so much more to enjoy for me in the twin cities. I watched Hardkore Hell before I left, in fact just as it ended was when I grabbed my things and walked out the door. Congratulations on winning your match man. Nobody knew more than my brother and I that you were going to do it. I think you might have done better had he not dropped you on your hip. *laughs* Yeah well he’s good at bringing out the best in people. He’s probably got a 2x4 waiting for my back when I get home. I saw that he broke the Kindred up. I think he should have consulted the two of us before he decided to go psycho on Epitome and Shadow. I’m still surprised at that I mean they just got things straightened up between them. I guess that is why I am never going to understand my brother. Listen, I need to get back on the road. I just filled the tank up and I’m running a little behind schedule. When I get some sleep after I get back before I start seriously getting into my routine we’ll get a drink or something to celebrate your victory. Sound like a plan? Ok....good, I’ll see you when I get back to Cleveland, later man.” \ | / Wraith gets back onto the bike and as he continues to zip down the highway on his bike he gives the viewers a brief little description of himself so that the other teams in this match he is in will get a little idea of what and who Wraith is. \ | / "Many of you fans do not really know me...I prefer some aspects of that and others I do not. I’m going to give you a little bit of information to wet your thirst for knowledge when it comes to my world. They call me Wraith, I have been a wrestler for several years now but most people know my older brother more than they do me. I’m going to be changing all that very soon. That’s right. I am the younger brother of the current Great Lakes Champion of Wrestling Midwest. He goes by the moniker of Corvus. He’s one of the most feared grapplers in this sport. Since this is not about him, I am not going to talk about him at length as he will only watch this and allow his ego to inflate. I’ve had to deal with everyone trying to compare me with him and how I will never live up to meet the accomplishments that he’s made within the wrestling community. You want to know what I think of that? I think it is complete bull because I’ve never been given a chance. I’m 6’2” and 220 pounds. I’m not the biggest guy on the block but I’ve been able to take a lot of beatings and get up from them then most people just lie down and die from. When I get into the ring I’m going to be as competitive as the next guy. I don’t take shortcuts and I don’t half-ass things. I’m a true sportsman. There are not a lot of guys these days that can say that they are. I don’t complain about what I’m getting paid and I just go out there and work as hard as I can. A lot of the competition in this match I have never heard of. I mean no disrespect to them but as a person you can’t keep track of every wrestler or promotion that exists. I wish these guys the best of luck and I hope they will do the same for me as I have a lot to prove just as they do. Whether or not my fellow teammates for WMW are going to try and put out the effort that I will remains to be seen but with names like Noah Hanson and Matrix on board there is an impressive chance we will go far into this event. The only concern that I have about that right now is how I’m going to be treated by someone like Noah Hanson who seems to have a hatred for my brother. I’m sure he is going to realize that Corvus and I are worlds apart in many different ways. Aside from my teammates in this match I have a few goals other than proving to the wrestling world that I belong here. My goal is to make sure that the WMW team advances to face the team of GW wrestlers. I have a few scores to settle with Sage Sabbot and Leinad Soma. I came really close to beating them in the handicap match at the Crossfire PPV between Global Wrestling and Wrestling Midwest. It didn’t seem like they had a chance of winning as our team was stacked with talent. Unfortunately I’m the one who dropped the ball and I know I can beat them and I’m going to do my best to try and show them that I can." \ | / Time seems to fast forward through Wraith’s talking and the next thing we see is his motorbike pulling up to a fancy looking house. The bike’s engine stops and Wraith steps off of it; reaches into his pocket for some keys; then walks towards the front door. He looks up towards the sky and realizes he made pretty good time as it is nearly starting to get light out. Wraith pushes the door open before closing it behind him quickly. There is a note waiting for him on the kitchen table as he sets his bags down to get something to drink from the refrigerator. It is from his brother and it states that the upstairs has been cleaned out and is now his if he wants it. He shrugs and figures he might as well since nobody is using it any longer. Wraith rinses out his glass as is customary in the house for people to do that before putting the glasses into the dish washer. \ | / "I would say this is home sweet home but the place I came from is what I call home and what I have called home for a good many years. This is more or less a pseudo-home to me as I am always welcome here but I just don’t feel comfortable most of the time. I think I’m going to call it a day though since I have been driving for hours on the road and I’ll be up dark and early tomorrow night to begin building my body to perfection so that I can make a statement to all these wrestlers from different promotions that Wrestling Midwest is here to stay and we’re not going to be denied. Starting now it will be known as the “Year of the Wraith” because I will strike quickly and I will strike when it is least expected. Good luck to all of you in this little event we call Survivor because once you’ve been given your Ethereal Release...it’s going to be all over like *holds up three fingers slowly into the form of a w* one, two, three." \ | / Wraith picks up his bag and walks out of the kitchen towards the staircase which is near the front door. The house is entirely desolate as most people are in bed still or are just about to get up when he’s finally getting himself to sleep. The scene slowly fades to black as Wraith ascends the staircase towards what is now appearing to be his portion of the house as it is no longer being occupied by its former inhabitant. This may be discussed another time as it is not exactly pertinent to the Survival match. \ | /
