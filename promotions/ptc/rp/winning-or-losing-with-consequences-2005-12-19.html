Winning Or Losing With Consequences

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-19 "Seriously now, you’ve got to pay attention!" Kelly looks over to Sledge with frustration. Sledge on the other hand isn’t frustrated. His eyes are almost shut and he looks very bored. Kelly stares at him with a raised eyebrow. Sledge rests his head on his hands, who get supported by his elbows on the table. Kelly, the always lovely brunette and Sledge’s girlfriend, is sitting on the opposite side of the table, trying to tell Sledge, who wears his usual outfit of ripped jeans and a black shirt with no sleeves and some holes in it, something. They are sitting in Sledge’s house. It’s easy to recognize, since the bottles and cans of beer are scattered around the house. If Sledge doesn’t clean up his house soon, he’s going to get buried by a sea of cans and bottles. It’s such a huge mess. But this isn’t the time to worry about a messy house. However, it is time to worry about something called the GTT5. Where Sledge participates in and is facing a tough opponent in Dawn McFarlane. Sledge reached Round Two, but very many people doubt that Sledge can continue winning and they predict that Sledge will get defeated in this round. Dawn McFarlane is seen as the better and most experienced of the two, not to forget that the people are mostly behind Dawn, so Sledge is in a disadvantage. Not only that, Sledge has many more problems then just the fact that people are rooting for Dawn. It’s that he doesn’t know a thing about Dawn McFarlane, except that she is his opponent and it turns out that she is a female. Sledge didn’t even try to take his time and dig up some information about her. He just sat around in his house, drinking beer, while he knew the people on the streets were talking more about Dawn McFarlane and how she is going to beat Sledge than vice-versa. Sledge has lost most of his hope. He just shrugged his shoulders and sat around his house, but that didn’t change the fact that the match was still on. Sledge against Dawn McFarlane was going to happen one way or another, and even if Sledge wanted to do something about it, he couldn’t. It sucks when you can’t take control of a situation. But now there could be hope. Now Sledge could be on top after all. There is still a chance. That chance goes by the name of Kelly. Kelly, even though she is barely known in wrestling, knows enough about how the female thinks. She knows what females can endure, and she knows that the life of girls can be much more complicated then those of guys. Kelly has plenty of examples from the past to prove it, but so does Sledge. But this discussion isn’t about who had the worst past, this discussion is about how to understand the female mind. Problem is, the discussion is quite boring… "Sledge, you really need to stay with me!" Kelly yelled at him, trying to get his attention. Sledge is slipping, slipping away to the Dreamworld. If it was just a bit more boring, Sledge would fall asleep like a big stone. Kelly doesn’t give up, as she continues to scream at Sledge. "Look, I know that the female race is difficult, but that doesn’t mean you can fall asleep! We’ve got a match to win! And if you fall asleep, you can’t really win a match, now can you!?" Sledge just stares at Kelly, as she silently awaits his response. Sledge's eyes are barely open now. "…What?" Was all Sledge could mutter. Kelly slaps her forehead and is speechless. She shakes her head as Sledge just stares at her. Kelly looks at him and is unable to speak. "Okay, I give up. I tried to tell you something, but you won’t pay me any attention. I’m sorry that you have to do it on your own now, but maybe my talk was a waste of time. You haven’t even made any notes about what I said! Why the hell am I still sitting here!? All I wanted was to help you and you repay me with a bit of muttering and barely any attention! Sledge, honey, I’m sorry…" It’s silent for a moment. Sledge still stares at Kelly without doing much but resting his head on his hands. Kelly looks back, without saying or doing anything. Just then, Sledge's hands slip away from under his head and Sledge’s head falls onto the table. Kelly is startled as she looks at Sledge that is laying on the table with his head and arms. After a couple of seconds, Sledge starts to snore. Kelly gets up and is infuriated. "That’s it! I’ve had it with you! I’m going home and leaving you to it. You’re on your own with Dawn McFarlane! I will talk to you whenever you have time for me again and when you apologize for your behaviour! I'm trying to help you and you're falling asleep, you insulting bastard. Wasting my time like this while I was just trying to help, you're such a jerk sometimes!" As Kelly continues to complain, she grabs her coat and heads out for the door as Sledge stays asleep. He snores really loud like a bear, and with this, we leave Sledge to his sleep… -------------------------------- What you get by achieving your goals is not as important as what you become by achieving your goals. ~a quote by Zig Ziglar~ My goal is set: Winning, it all depends on my own ability, my passion and if I really want to have it. I have and I want it all. I want the victory and I will not stop before I know I gave it my all. I know it’s hard. I know it’s difficult, but I have to do it, no matter the circumstances. Even though the match is coming, there is enough time to prepare… Mentally… It is in my mind that I’m at a disadvantage. People think that I don’t stand a chance. My mind picks that up as negative energy and uses it against me. People agree that Dawn McFarlane is in fact the much better wrestler and more experienced, even though she is a female. My mind picks that up and uses it against me. Once again in the form of negative energy. Negative energy is bad. Positive energy is good. How do I create the positive energy? Easy, but saying my own things and let my mind pick it up to use it for positive energy. But there is a problem here. I already picked up so much negative energy that I probably think that I’m not good. Whatever I think, whatever I try to say to myself, positive or not, the opinion of somebody else is always harder than your very own. What is the use? I talk to myself and try to make it positive while I have all that negative energy dancing in the back of my mind. What can I do to beat all that negative energy in my mind? Smash my head into a wall hoping to forget all those negative thoughts? Not really a good idea. Slice my head open and shake all the negative thoughts out of my brain? How the hell is that possible? Where is the logic around here? These are all stupid and useless ideas. I need to find positive energy, but where? Can I create it myself? How? Maybe it’s time not to rely on my mind and thoughts. It’s time to believe in myself and achieve my goal, and that is winning. Maybe that is the positive energy I was looking for. The fire of passion starts to heat up whenever I think about the match and think of winning, especially since I know what can happen if I win the match. It would mean no more underestimations. No more being the laughing stock and an easy opponent. Then it will be time to look serious at me. People will be rooting for me then. If I win. Wait, if is bad. If is more negative energy. When I win, yes there is some positive energy for you, then I will be known and recognized by people. The people start to cheer for me. They will follow me and watch me as I continue to climb the ladder of greatness higher and higher. Much will be gained when I get this victory! After I defeat Dawn McFarlane, it will be Sledge that is proud and it is Sledge that will be the better one! Then it is Sledge that will be the man who is something to build on. Somebody who can take the ball and run with it. Maybe he is the new era of this industry? …But what if I lose…? -------------------------------- He who fears being conquered is sure of defeat. ~a quote by Napoleon~ Now what would happen if I lost the match? What would happen to me? Would I be laughed at because I lost to a girl, a talented one, but still, a girl. Will the people not recognize me, just as a chump who was in the GTT5 tournament that got to Round Two, and nothing more? Yes, getting to Round Two is great, but it’s not much compared to getting to Round Three, or Round Four. Half of the competitors were defeated and eliminated from the tournament, thus not even getting to Round Two. So compared to them, I have done something really good. But now I’m in Round Two, and now what? Now I have to start all over again. I’m above the ones that lost in Round One, but I’m at the same height as my opponent. If I will lose, that would mean that I will still stand above the Round One losers, but that would mean I’m under the Round Two winners. I’m stuck in between. Me, the guy in the middle. Higher than Round One, lower than Round Two. Would that make me the guy that is standing in Round One and a Half? I don’t want to be known as the guy that is standing in Round One and a Half in the GTT5 tournament! I really want to get this win and move on to Round Three, thus standing above Round Two and Round One and a Half. But I still can’t be sure. What if. It’s always if. If there are aliens in space, would that mean the downfall of the earthlings? If God was walking on the earth, would that mean that the earth would be covered with clouds? You can always say if. But it’s not always the possibility to say when. I do believe that I stand a chance. Do I stand a big chance? I don’t know. But if I did stand a big chance, would that mean that people were rooting for me instead of Dawn McFarlane? Dawn McFarlane has been chosen by the crowd as the favourite one to win the match. That leaves me in a world of uncertainty. People don’t see that. It is fucked up to stand in a world of uncertainty. What can I do about it? Step out of it? That is easier said then done. The crowd has chosen, and they didn’t choose for me. Does that mean I don’t have a fair chance at winning anymore? Probably not, but maybe it would be better for me if the crowd would cheer for me. Maybe it would be better if the crowd had my back, and not that of Dawn McFarlane. Hell, even guys from my very own federation of the UWF are thinking that Dawn McFarlane is going to take the victory. Don’t they have faith in me? If they don’t, who does? If nobody has faith in me, then only one person has to have faith in me. And that is none other than myself. But do I have enough faith to win? Maybe I don’t have enough faith in me to win. If I don’t have faith in me anymore, who will have faith in me? If nobody has faith in me, then most of the hope is lost. Not only is the hope lost, but it will be a matter of time before I have lost. Lost to Dawn McFarlane… -------------------------------- You don't have to be great to start, but you have to start to be great. ~a quote by Zig Ziglar~ It’s time not to think about losing. It’s time to show the world that Sledge is going to be one of the greatest ever alive. Maybe the road to the top is pacing slowly, but atleast I’m moving. It’s one long road. It’s difficult to achieve that goal. The goal to finally get to the top. For just this match, the purpose isn’t to get to the top. This time it is to stay on top. Dawn McFarlane maybe is much bigger then Sledge in fame, but it isn’t about fame. It is about the ability to defeat your opponents. Fame is just an extra that you receive whenever you win. It’s about winning and defeating your opponents. There is no turning back whenever you get the defeated. When you get defeated, that defeat will haunt you forever and even if you win a thousand times of that same opponent, there is still that one single defeat. It’s better not to get that defeat. It’s better to get the victory and win. It’s not about the past now. It’s not about the future now. It’s about the presence, and the presence is about Sledge against Dawn McFarlane, who is going to win tha battle? Over and over again, I hear people say that Dawn McFarlane will take home the victory. Not only that, they add an insult. They say it’s going to be an easy victory for Dawn McFarlane. Now that is an insult to my very own ability. Either they underestimate me or they think they I don’t have what it takes to defeat Dawn McFarlane. Either way, it stays an insult. I can see that they still don’t know who I am and what I can do when given the chance. But on the other hand, I haven’t shown my true ability just yet. I have to save some of it for the bigger fights. I survived the first round, but remember, that was just the first round. How many rounds are still going to come? Ten? Twenty? I can’t save all of my talent for my tenth match or my twentieth. I have to throw it out now or I get the risk of elimination. I don’t want to get eliminated. I want to survive and move on. But there is a problem. Whenever I show my ability, by next opponent can study me. That leaves me in a new disadvantage. I don’t need anymore disadvantages. I’m in enough of them already, and I had enough of all of them. It’s time to start being great. It’s time to show my true ability to the people. It’s time that I won’t be weak anymore. No more holding back. Dawn McFarlane or not. Female or not. It’s time that I show that I have what it takes to succeed. Maybe I’m not the greatest wrestler ever that entered a wrestling ring. Maybe I am the greatest wrestler that has entered a wrestling ring. It doesn’t matter. It’s time to start to be great, even if I was it already or not. My name is Sledge, and I’m on my way to get what belongs to me. That is sweet victory…
