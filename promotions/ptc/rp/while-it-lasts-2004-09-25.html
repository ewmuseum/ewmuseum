While it lasts

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 1. Alex Grey “Alex?” “Alex are you ok?” Slowly... “Alex what’s wrong?” Reality returns. “Alex you’re scaring me!” For a single moment the pain lingers from the waking hallucination and I think that it’s real, my chest heaving and teeth clenched I ride out the phantom wave of anguish with Martin’s words echoing in my ears. “Should have gotten me first Alex...” “A-Alex?” “S’ok babe...I’m ok...” “Alex what happened?” she asks staring into my eyes “You put your hand on the doorknob then you looked like you saw a ghost!” (I see them quite frequently) I want to tell her (The people I’ve killed...the people I should kill...they come to see me with disturbing regularity) “It’s nothing...just an old injury acting up babe” I recover lamely but she seems to buy the excuse, which is fine by me because frankly the truth wouldn’t get me laid tonight, and after that particular hallucination I desperately need to get my mind in a happier place. My apartment door is shut, no cigarette smoke drifts out from underneath, there’s no one waiting in there for me but for a second I freeze anyway like a child laying awake at night knowing...just knowing that there’s a monster under the bed waiting for him...sure it isn’t true but the imagination just won’t be denied sometimes. Besides... Sometimes even though we don’t see them, the monsters are staring us right in the face. “After you babe” I smile opening the door, slapping her on the ass as she walks by Hallucinations be damned...it’s not going to spoil my night. -- 2. Martin Smalls “He’s not well Martin” Alexander Price is my assistant, the man I’ve handpicked to run my business when I retire...but his biggest downfall is stating the obvious. “Am I supposed to be surprised Mister Price?” I ask him with a raised eyebrow “Mister Grey has always been a few sandwiches short of a picnic basket...but that’s his charm” “Martin his mental health has been failing for years, I’m concerned he could hurt you...” “Won’t happen Mister Price...” “I’m still concerned Martin...” Alexander seems to be quite persistent tonight, It’s more annoying then helpful, I hate having my judgement questioned. “Alexander what’s got you so uptight about Mister Grey...” “Well you asked me to follow him...and I did” “And what did you find?” I’m taping my foot on the floor and only dimly aware of it, My secretary waits in her office for me for some after business activities and Alexander is unknowingly holding up my evening. “He’s been having conversations...” “Mister Price most people tend to have conversations...it comes with being human” I cut him off “With himself” Alexander finishes driving his point home “Vivid Hallucinations Martin...I don’t even have to be close to him to hear him...I can stand outside his door and listen to him yell” “Alexander...” I stand up and put my arm on his shoulder leading him out of my office “That man is dangerous but he’s not going to harm me...I’m his meal ticket and he knows it, he harms me and his cash flow stops, and he needs his cash Mister Price...to feed all his dirty little vices that he thinks no one knows about, I’m his dealer you could say...and he’s my dirty little junkie whore” “Martin I...” “Shut up Alexander...take the rest of the evening off and leave me to mine” “But Martin I really think...” I slam the door in his face leaving his last words unfinished “That’s your problem Mister Price” I mutter “You think too damn much” Exiting my office from another door I find Kim waiting for me, she spins around in her chair decked out in a particularly revealing bit of fabric I picked out for her last week, the site of her makes me smile. “Now my dear...where were we before we were so rudely interrupted?” Her smile broadens as she holds up a pair of handcuffs “Ahh yes...thank you for reminding me” I smile sliding off my coat. 3. Alex Grey Hours later I lay awake staring at the ceiling Try as I might I can’t get Martins words out of my head... You should have gotten me first... I trusted Martin about as far as I could throw a piano...the man was a snake...but he was a greedy little shit who cared only for profit, all the years I’d known him had taught me that much about him. Martin had learned his own lessons from me, we’d learned to work together yet keep a respectable distance, and that was as things should have been... I’d never thought about killing Martin...but floating through my head that night was one question. Did Martin ever think about Killing me? Was he that paranoid...did he have some sort of plan B with my career... Question upon question add themselves to the equation until I heave a sigh and turn over. To find myself staring into her open eyes. “Alex? Are you awake?” “Yeah...” “What are you thinking about?” Here she goes again “Nothing babe...” “Is that really true Alex?” Digging...stop digging you bitch...you can’t handle the truth “No...No it’s not” “Do you want to talk baby?” God damn these pet names... “No...” “Oh...ok...” And like that she gets that look on her face again, when she gets like that there’s really only one thing to do... Wrapping my arms around her I press my lips against hers, in seconds I’m on top of her and the look on her face has been replaced with one of lust. I know just what buttons to push... In bed and in the ring, Mister Banner...he’s an unknown at this point...but he made it past the first round just like I did so I have to consider him a threat, and I deal with my threats directly and quite violently. Mister Banner like the woman beneath me...can consider himself fucked. I hope wherever he is right now...he’s enjoying it...living it up while it lasts. Because I’m going to crucify him... And leave him to rot with the critics.
