Piece by Piece: Part Six

20 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-20 Reluctance. A feeling that, as a wrestler, Seymour must often combat. Never has that statement been truer for him than now. In order to be best prepared for Craig Goat, his next opponent, he had to converse with his wife. One very terse phone call later, and a meeting had been scheduled to occur in a park near the Almasy apartment. To say that Seymour was reluctant would have been a grave understatement. ----- Outside, the sun was shining. There were a few clouds in the sky, and it was more than a little cold out, but all in all, most would consider it a very nice day. To Seymour Almasy, however, it served as a perfect contrast to the storminess within his soul. Seymour Almasy: I can’t believe I’m doing this… The Final Fantasy grimaced, even as his feet continued to carry him to the appointed meeting place. On one hand, he would have been perfectly happy never to see his wife again. On the other, however, he didn’t want Laura to completely disappear from his life. He sincerely doubted that she ever would. Whether he liked it or not, Laura had virtually saved his life. Seymour Almasy: If only someone would have had the guts to tell me sooner…maybe things would be different. Blaming others for his problems was vintage Seymour Almasy. In fairness, though, this was a case in which he had an axe to grind. Seymour Almasy: Parents who didn’t take care of me, loved ones who fed my dysfunctions…it’s incredible I’m as sane as I am today. Sane, of course, was a word that could only be used loosely when dealing with the Final Fantasy. Seymour Almasy: Ah well. No use dwelling on the past for any longer than I’m going to have to when I see Laura. He continued walking, eyes focused squarely ahead of him, thousands of thoughts going through his mind. What exactly was he supposed to say to her, anyway? There were almost an infinite amount of things that could come out of his mouth. Just as long as he didn’t say the one thing that had been haunting him ever since the couple had broke up. Seymour Almasy: …I love you. The Final Fantasy shuddered visibly, as if he had been dipped in ice water. It was perhaps the most painful realization that he’d ever come to in his life, made even more agonizing by the fact that HE had been the one to fuck up. If he wasn’t sleeping around on his wife, there wouldn’t even be an issue. Seymour Almasy: I should have just talked to her about not having independence. She would have probably let me have it. But I was so intent on being independent that I didn’t think. He couldn’t and wouldn’t tell her, however. It was partially out of pride, and at the same time, partially out of the gnawing fear that she would forgive him for something Almasy himself found personally unforgivable. Despite what he showed in public…despite the displays he would make, inwardly he felt like the world’s biggest jerk. Seymour Almasy: Come on Seymour, get yourself together. This is about business, not pleasure. As much of a professional as he was, he found himself locked in battle with his own mind to convince himself of that fact. It was a battle that wouldn’t last much longer, as Seymour found himself at the gates of the park. Seymour Almasy: This is it, Seymour. Now or never. Despite the bravado, he peeked around the corner of the gate. If he was hoping that his wife wouldn’t be readily visible, he was quite disappointed by what he saw. Seymour Almasy: There she is… Laura Winters had changed. Seymour couldn’t clearly state how, but he knew that she had. For one thing, the usual pink that she wore was replaced by black and tan. Rather than smiling, like she always used to, she seemed somewhat sad. Well…that could probably be explained by the fact that she was waiting to meet her soon to be ex, Seymour realized. Seymour Almasy: Fuck it. I can’t be afraid of her. Besides, I need this information… The Global Champion’s professional life once again trumped his personal life. Setting his jaw, and trying desperately not to give anything away emotionally, he stepped into the park, walking towards the one person in the world who knew him the best. To his great surprise, Laura spoke first. Laura Winters: Hello, Seymour. It was a better start than he thought would happen. Sure, Laura wasn’t waving flowers at him, but there was none of the venom in her voice that he thought there would be. As such, Seymour responded with a similar tone. Seymour Almasy: Hello, Laura. Laura Winters: You wanted to talk to me? Like Seymour, she seemed ready to get down to business too. He had no idea what she thought he was here to tell her. For that reason, he immediately made his intentions clear. Seymour Almasy: Laura, I need your help with a match I have coming up. If Laura was expecting an apology, she didn’t show it. Laura Winters: Who are you facing? Seymour Almasy: Craig Goat. Nurse Wretched’s assistant. The mere mention of Nurse Wretched’s name sends sparks of anger shooting through Laura’s body. Her XUDF career had been going perfectly, with her defeating all in her path, until… Laura Winters: That bitch rigged it so that she wouldn’t have to defend her title against me. I could care less if you were a mass murderer right now, I’ll help. No one could say that Laura Winters wasn’t a vindictive person. It was a side of her that Seymour saw more and more as their time together increased. At first, he thought it was unusual, but now, he just chalked it up to a part of her personality she had tried to hide. Seymour Almasy: Not the most flattering comment, but beggars can’t be choosers, can they? You know anything about Craig? Laura Almasy: He’s probably going to snap someday. Nurse Wretched kept him on a really tight leash. It’s a shame, too, because he seemed like a nice guy. The conclusion was similar to the one Seymour had already reached. In other words, Laura wasn’t of very much help at all. This was bad, in that for Seymour to leave now would look quite ridiculous. Conversation had to be made, Seymour Almasy: So…how’ve you been? Laura Winters: Fine, yourself? Seymour Almasy: Eh, so-so. The usual for me, really Generic answers, and each knew it. Neither wanted to be the one to truly begin the conversation. Seymour closed the distance between himself and his soon to be ex-wife, sitting on the other end of the bench that she sat upon. Laura Winters: Still losing your mind worrying over matches, I see. It’s a bit sad that it took something like this for you to come talk to me. The expression on her face was a sad one, which sent Seymour’s mind spinning. She seemed far less angry than she had when they had first agreed to untie the knot. It looked almost as if she had accepted it, as if she was entirely ready to move on. Seymour Almasy: I’ve been afraid to. Laura Winters: Afraid of what? Seymour Almasy: Afraid that I’d come see you and we’d start yelling again. Or, on the other end of the spectrum, afraid I’d see you, and want to start things back up again. I didn’t do what I did because I didn’t love you, Laura, I--fuck. Well, so much for not saying THAT. The Final Fantasy’s eyes were full of panic, but to his shock, Laura didn’t seem surprised. Her eyes and facial expression remained perfectly calm, before she said the last two words that he expected to hear. Laura Winters: …I know. Seymour looked at her quizzically, before she continued. Laura Winters: There’s never been malice towards me in anything you’ve ever done, really. You didn’t sleep with another woman to get back at me for anything, and I sincerely doubt you did it because of a lack of prowess on my part in the bedroom, so there has to be some other reason. Given our history together, and how long we’ve known each other, I think you owe me an explanation. He sighed. This was the type of conversation he didn’t want to get into. He had hoped to get the dirt on Craig Goat, maybe exchange some hellos, and then leave. But now, he’d been trapped. He had no choice. Seymour Almasy: Alright, Laura…I’ll tell you. The pained expression on his face was difficult for Laura to read. She wondered what he could possibly say. In her heart, she knew that there was a reason for his betrayal, but what it was had been haunting her for almost two months now. Seymour Almasy: You were a wonderful wife, and a wonderful friend. Too wonderful, almost. You did everything for me. Made dinners, did my laundry…while I was lost in my fantasy world, that was okay. But now, I realize what life is. And as nice as you were…I needed some space, space that you didn’t seem to want to let me have. The Battle Arena became the only place where I had control anymore. I needed SOME semblance of control over some aspect of my life…and there you have it. She blinked once, and then again. The former Aerith Heartilly was more than a little bit surprised. Laura Almasy: Seymour, all you had to do was tell me, you know. Seymour Almasy: It’s not just that though…it’s that no one told me that RPGs weren’t real. For twenty-two years of my life, I was living a lie. I blew up at Grandpa Cid about it earlier…I just can’t accept the fact that of the two people who loved me, neither was brave enough to tell me something that important. The words hit like a sledgehammer. Laura’s one regret in her dealings with her childhood friend had turned out to be the straw that broke the camel’s back. Laura Almasy: We wanted to tell you, Seymour…every night when I fell asleep next to you, I almost told you. But I couldn’t do it. I couldn’t bear to watch your world collapse around you. I’m almost shocked that you’ve made it out of that revelation this well. I was terrified that you’d be committed to a mental institution. Please believe me, Seymour…I didn’t withhold that information from you out of spite, but instead out of love… It was one thing for Seymour to deny that statement’s validity when it came from his grandfather’s mouth. It was another thing entirely for him to deny it when it came from the pouty lips of the woman that he still loved. Some things had changed about her, certainly, but nowhere near enough to prevent Seymour from noticing the beauty and grace that had attracted him to her. Seymour Almasy: I believe you. I…I’m… Laura Almasy: Don’t say it. No apologies, Seymour. Neither of us is blameless here, and both of us, in some way, are justified for what we did. But in spite of that…I don’t think divorce is the answer. Nor do I think it’s healthy for us to continue living together. We have differences, Seymour. For now…I think its best that we simply stay…friends. The outcome was better than Seymour expected. In his heart, he knew that Laura was right. They DID have differences. He was enjoying his independence. “For now” gave him hope for the future. But as for right now…he would enjoy his role as friend. Seymour Almasy: You want to come see me beat Craig Goat? Laura Almasy: Of course! If Nurse Wretched’s there, I’ll hop the barricade and-- Seymour Almasy: No, Laura, I really don’t think that’s such a good idea. I don’t want to have to bail you out of prison after the police arrest you. She laughed slightly, as Seymour sighed. There was another question on his mind. Seymour Almasy: Have you, err, dated anyone since we separated? Laura froze. It was the last question she expected to be asked. She hadn’t DATED anyone, per se…but… Laura Almasy: I’ve gone out partying a few times…nothing too serious, you? Seymour Almasy: Same, pretty much… Each turned away from the other, faces bright red. Neither would admit it, but there was more than a little bit of anger that each felt. Anger mixed with sadness was a potent combination. Almost simultaneously, they turned, to bid one another leave. Laura Almasy: Well, Seymour, I have to go. See you at the match! Seymour Almasy: You too, Laura! Both stood, and began walking in opposite directions. Laura was somewhat hurt, but she was sure that it would go away. All she had to do was go get hold of some alcohol, and a night in the clubs would take the pain away. As for Seymour? He’d hit the gym, work out a little bit, and get ready for the challenge of Craig Goat. Thinking about work always helped Seymour calm down. And then… He would go find the girl he’d met at the Gaia.
