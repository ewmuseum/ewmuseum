Date: 2005-11-27 I started in this business a little under a year ago. And already I’ve managed to get quite far compared to other competitors. I’ve travelled the world with my wife, finding challenges in Japan, Puerto Rico and Canada among other places. Puerto Rico is the interesting one. I wrestled there for two months, and I always looked up to a couple of people. And, as it so happens, one of those role-models has been selected as my first opponent. Digital Mortality. I will face Digital Mortality in the first round of the fifth annual Golden Turnbuckle Tournament… In a small room at the back of a large mansion, pro wrestler "SuperNova" Stephen Baker sits. A bright orange fire dimly lights the room as Baker, looking worn out from his hard day’s work, gazes hard into it. This is my chance to rise… He seems content with himself, smiling briefly before a frown takes over his face. Something seems to be missing. My chance at success… He glances at a large grandfather clock, positioned above the fire surround, and sighs. Turning to the oak door behind him, his frown seems to grow worse. With the help of my soul mate Charlotte, I’m sure to overcome the challenge that Digital Mortality has promised me… As he turns back to the fire, the door slowly creeks open. Standing in the doorway, a beautiful blonde woman smiles nervously at Stephen. As she takes her first steps into the room, she slides something back on her hand. I feel I can conquer anything with her by my side… "Where were you?" Stephen asks. "I had…something to take care of." Charlotte smiles. She kneels down to Stephen, placing her hands on his lap and smiling. The two share a passionate kiss as the fire slowly dims. Anything…
