Midnight ponderings... who is the Angel?

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-30 In the far distance a bell tolls twelve times, ominously. Night had fallen over the world like a blanket over a sleeping child many hours before. It was the time many souls laid down their weary heads to rest, but aha… there was no such as rest for the wicked. For the children of the night… which is what those who live in the darkness are, it was just the beginning of what commonly was called the “dark dawn.” A layer of thick, dense fog rolls like a wave in the ocean over the ground, curling as it reaches outwards to cover anything in its path. Distorted shapes and shadows are made more eerie and the mournful call of a wolf in the far distance sends a chill up any spine. A double set of iron gates appear in the foggy distance and the black cloaked figure that stands holding the gates open gives this scene just the right amount of eerie that is needed to fit the theme. Fog swirls around the shrouded legs, licking upwards into the night sky. Why this is the place that has been chosen for the evil purpose about to be unleashed upon the unsuspecting world could be asked and that would be a good question. If there was an answer that would sooth the fear of course. After all.. Fear is an ally to those that use it well. A set of footsteps crunch the gravel noisily, disturbing the quiet that had once again fallen. Fabled whispers, heard only by those that have fallen deep into the abyss of power the darkness has, touch the ears of the youth that disturbs the silence so rudely. The seventeen-year old boy pauses, his face flushing a bright, embarrassed red as he hears the reprimands carried upon the single, solitary wind. He gazes through baby blue eyes at the ominous steel that surrounds the place in front of him, a graveyard he knew, an old one that carried many secrets that he had no desire to ever find out. Blonde spiky hair, tipped in blue which could only be seen in light goes in every direction as if the youth did nothing more then spray hairspray on the mass when he awoke. Baggy blue jeans are belted at below his waist in the current fashion and paired with a white wife beater and flannel shirt left open. Scuffed and dirty combat boots that are left untied adorn his feet and only one silver chain hangs from his neck of which has the most oddest thing ever.. A cross hanging upon it. A hand passed nervously through his hair as he looks at the cloaked figure in front of him. He knew that this was a messenger from the Mistress waiting for him inside of the graveyard. It meant that she was growing impatient with his tardiness in returning with news of the Cruiserweight Tournament she had entered herself into. Jacob Andrew Martin, or just Jake as he preferred to be called, stepped closer to the black shrouded figure, his hands shaking ever so slightly as he removed the headphones from his ears. “Disease” by Matchbox 20 blared out into the night, causing shrieks and groans from the one’s that rest inside of the gates. Jacob paid no mind for he was about to phrase a question for the one that stood in front of him. Instead though he found his tongue glued to the roof of his suddenly dry mouth and debated whether that perhaps was a good thing. The stench of fresh turned earth mingled with the cloak of death that permeated the whole graveyard and wrinkled Jacob’s nose in distaste. To someone that was not used to being so close to death this was in itself a traumatic experience. Yet it was an experience he continued to push on with as he saw that a black finger was pointing in the direction of the mausoleum that he knew stood far back removed from the tombstones. Jacob swallowed convulsively, from fear, and from the depth of emotion that welled in him at the mere thought of where he must go. He was tempted to turn and to run, make the Angel come for him but he wisely choose to take his chances inside of the graveyard instead. Slowly he stepped past the shrouded figure, a warning bell ringing in his ears or was that from listening to his CD player at top volume so much? He skirted the tombstones and memorials that lined each side of the graveyards and jumped any time the shadows danced to close to him. A wolf howled once more and sent a streak of fear up and down his spine. He hated coming here. It was something that he knew she understood but persisted in only because she knew he was afraid. It was a lesson to control fear, use it for one’s own advantage. Always she was teaching him a lesson in some dark, often demented way. That was the way that the Fallen Angel was. She led by examples, taught by lesson and did things without ever explaining her reasons unless she determined that it was necessary. Which was never often for as the Angel herself once had said, “’Mortals could never fathom the plans of the Angel and she does not fathom the foolishness of mortals.”’ A half-smile tugged at the youth’s lips as he thought of the Mistress that he sought out in this dark and gloomy place. Many questioned who the Fallen Angel was and he did not deny that he was of them. His first own memory of the woman he followed now was shrouded in pain, addiction and darkness. He had been a lost soul in the dark underworld of New York City. He’d left home at thirteen because he couldn’t stand the family that had adopted him. Couldn’t tolerate the abuse, both physical and mental any longer. The scathing words of hatred that always fell from their lips had taken their toll and he had decided death was way more preferable then this. So he took to the streets. He had been a clean kid then, hated alcohol, drugs, and hated most especially the violence he found himself wrapped up in. It hadn’t even taken a month before he had got himself hooked on heroine, cocaine, and anything else he could get his hands on. He’d become everything he hated and prayed for the Grim Reaper to grab him with one cold hand to end the pain he was in. He found that no one would hire a kid that had only a basic education and wasn’t yet sixteen. Least of all when the kid was as strung out as he most always was. So he got beat up for the money that feed his obsession, his need. Night after night after night he had the crap kicked out of him by nameless faces that were just looking for a punching bag to take their aggression out upon. A year of taking that makes a boy think he’s a tough man but then he had run into the wrong person to start a fight with. That had been two years ago Jacob thought with a faint twinge to his lips. He had been broke for a week and his body was racked with the thirst of addition as well as pain from being beaten up all those weeks prior. He had decided to hustle some people and get himself some cash. That was also the night where he had met a six-foot-three, two hundred sixty-three pound behemoth that had said his name was Rage. Jacob had never met a person bigger then Rage and knew it was going to be a beating that was going to hurt for a real long time to come. He had punched Rage in the stomach as hard as he could. Or so he thought when he had noticed that Rage hadn’t taken a step back or seem phased at all by the punch. He had been terrified of the anger that was in Rage‘s eyes and feared that the Reaper had indeed just found him. He damn near had pissed his pants when the guy had taken his black t-shirt off to reveal the well conditioned body beneath. His heart had stopped beating when Rage had cracked his knuckles and stepped forward, threatening to disconnect his head from his spine. It was Sheri that had stepped in with a laugh and told Rage, or “Brian” as she had called him affectionately to knock it off. That was before the nightmare that would become Sheri’s life had led her to this dark spiral and to the Fallen Angel’s emergence. He had thought her an angel then, a good one because she had such a beautiful smile and warm eyes. That was what had gotten his attention. Her eyes had held a warmth and vitality that he had never seen before in another human being. Knowing of the beating that he was still in danger of getting, he had turned to run but Sheri had easily caught him and detained him. Afraid as he was, as hooked on drugs as he was, as jaded as he had become, that sweet smile and the honesty that had been in the depths of those black eyes had convinced him that this wasn’t someone who wanted to use him. To hurt him. That she meant it when she said that she wanted to help him. He didn‘t get a choice in the matter in the end he thought with a rueful shake of his head. Rage had connected with a punch to his jaw that had knocked him on his ass. Before he had sunk into the wonderful black oblivion of unconsciousness he had heard the heated reprimand that had come from Sheri. He felt it had almost been worth the punch then, just to hear someone defend him. God knows it had been so long since someone had. Sheri had put him in a drug rehab center to clean him up, watched him to make sure he didn‘t try to break out. Which he had tried to do a few times. He had always been drug back by either Sheri and Rage or Sheri’s twin sister Rayne and Kaos, their best friend. Sheri had cared enough about him to bring him home once he was clean of the drugs too. She had been firm in saying she wouldn‘t put up with him returning to his former ways and that she was bringing him home to show him a real family. “’Give him a real home”’ she had said and give him the same chance at life that her adopted parents, Elizabeth and Jerome Ashanti had given her. Family. Something that he hadn‘t had since he was a kid. He sometimes wondered if before he had gone to live with the Ashanti’s if he had ever had a family. The Ashanti’s had welcomed him with open arms. They didn’t ask for much of him, just that he go to school every day and not return to the drugs he had used before he had met them. He had agreed without hesitation. They had never treated him as if he was a horrible kid for the things he had done and they never kicked him around after that one time Brian had socked him unconscious. He felt that it was the least he could do to repay them. He found a home, he had a family that loved him and he found something he loved at the same time…. Wrestling. “’You’ll never find a greater thrill then being in front of twenty thousand plus people who are screaming your name Jake,”’ Sheri had told him once after she had taken to training him to be a wrestler herself. She had been right. He’d attended the shows she wrestled in for whatever federation she got work in, watched as what she had said came true. Just like the Angel now, Sheri had carried a sadistic streak, a favor to use weaponry if it was needed. In XUW that weapon had turned into something that became associated with Sheri’s name, the sledgehammer. Whenever she could get away with it, Sheri would use it. Quite effectively he thought with a chuckle. Sheri had also introduced him to one of her own very best friends and to him the greatest technical wrestler the wrestling industry had ever seen. Chris Benoit. He remembered being struck dumb, much like a “mark” when Sheri had lead him into the gym and he had seen Benoit standing in that ring. It had to have been the single greatest moment in his life. Meeting a man that he had gotten to see a lot of great wrestling matches from and watching Sheri and Chris spar off against each other at the same time. Chris had knew everything there was about him and had not treated him any differently then he treated Sheri or Brian. He in fact had called him into the ring and given him a good lesson he had never forgot about wrestling. It was also Chris who had told him about how Sheri had grown up on the streets in San Francisco with nothing but Rage and Rayne. It had told him why she would give a punk kid a chance and have so much faith in him. Because Sheri had herself lived that underworld scene, and had no parents until she had met Jerome Ashanti. But life doesn’t always stay on such a happy scale Jacob thought silently, his mouth tightening in a thin, hard line. The succession of hard break ups with two other wrestlers and the loss of a group that Sheri had considered as close as family had taken a toll on Sheri mentally. She had jumped at the chance to join former Horsemen Sidhe Nimh, who was known now as the Fallen Fae and his wife Kat. Jacob had never remembered seeing Sheri do some of the things she had done then. Pudding matches, nudity, and a ton of sexuality that had made Rage keep him from watching XUW. Which he admitted, had ticked him off to no end. Somewhere in that middle ground though is when the origin of the Angel had come into existence. How Jacob wasn’t sure still but according to what the Angel had told him, it had been during that brief time. Yet also in that brief time had come the greatest moments of happiness as well. Anthony or Numark as was his professional wrestling name in XUW had brought balance, order, and peace into Sheri‘s life. He had liked Anthony and liked how happy he and Sheri had been together. That was when problems always started. It was a system that he himself was used to. Addiction. Pain. Darkness. Anthony had left his whole life behind, Sheri included after seemingly being kidnapped from a big XUW show and was never going to be found. The result was a vortex which left Sheri torn into two people. Two personalities. She had dealt with her own addition then. The alcoholism, the guilt, the pain, and continuing her own life until it all just became to much. Jacob knew when it all became to much. When Anthony had suddenly come back and made it known that Sheri was one of the reasons he had left XUW. The Angel came to life fully then. A shot given by a sledgehammer and taken for another mortal soul had opened up the chasm that needed to be created for existence to become permanent. No. It wasn’t that way. It was the pain and the shatter that had happened in Sheri’s mind that allowed the Angel to become the “dominant” one as she called it. The Angel had revealed herself when Anthony had shown up to the hospital afterwards to “explain“ why he had done what he did. The Angel had done her best to make Anthony angry for she was afraid of him. Knew that the only person that could destroy her was in fact the one man that had been so loved by her lighter half. Love could have set Sheri free but she had been afraid of getting hurt once more. So she had slipped away. Allowed the Angel to play the games she wanted. Yet the Angel was herself naught but a puppet in the end. A tool to fulfill the dark mission that Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy had set her upon. Never had it been explained totally what the mission was beyond returning a former protégé of the Ripper’s to the path he had before walked. Jacob often wondered if the mission the Angel had been given was to just wreak havoc upon the XUW. It had never made sense to him, this seeming mission. This seeming change. This new person that had severed all ties to those people that Sheri had loved. Save for one. Him. In the middle of all this dark chaos and with a reason he did not understand, the Angel had come for him. Late one night in October in fact. He had been asleep in his bed, tired from a long day of training with Rayne and Rage at the gym when he had heard the soft whispers of the dead for the first time. Opening his eyes he had beheld a face that he knew by sight but the warmth and vitality that had once filled those dark eyes was gone. Vanquished like a flame. The Angel had sat on the edge of his bed and ran a gloved hand over his cheek while whispering in a harsh, cold voice that she knew he desired more then to be what the Ashanti clan had planned for him. She promised to show him a world of power, a world of darkness that would feed everything that was inside of his soul and teach him things he would have never known before. He had been intrigued. By the change in Sheri‘s looks, her voice, her demeanor. To intrigued for his own good. So he had gone with her. Secreted away from the family he had adopted to be his own underneath the heavy cloak that had adorned the lithe figure that now was to cold. He had been drug to a place of such darkness that it both shocked and terrified him. It was like nothing he had ever seen before, not even when he had been a street rat for those years. Everything that had been promised had been fulfilled. Angel showed him a new way of wrestling, extreme as he knew it to be but more sadistic and more careless of the one’s that were picked to be the victim’s. She also had included him in all the secrets that were inside of her mind. Made plans with him for the things that she had been told to do by the Master and listened to him when she asked his opinion. Angel seemed much like how Sheri but now was darker, more evil. Angel had tried her best in the XUW to bring Pete back from his brink of near collapse. She had gave him space, peace, an out one could call it to make a decision. Live or die. Pete chose to live. He demanded answers and Angel in fact broke her Master’s vow to never speak by telling Pete the truth. Pete had scoffed, doubting what was being said to him for he knew the Angel as Sheri as well. Just he saw the change in her, sensed the darker, more hateful demeanor that permeated everything that was the Angel. Slowly he began to listen. Inside, the Angel knew she had yet to prove she was different. That she was not Sheri playing a new mind game. She was kept from in ring action and had no way to finalize the change inside without a match…. Until the announcement of the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. Angel had been pleased for here was opportunity that was needed. So as she had learned that her Master would also return from his place in Hell to compete in the Chris Williams Memorial tournament, so did the Angel. Determined to prove herself as the Fallen Angel, not Sheri, she tossed her name into the proverbial hat of contestants as a free agent for she represented no federation. Everything Angel had done prior to this time was for a man that was every inch as cunning, as ruthless, as vicious and as cold-hearted as the Angel was herself. Every bid of the Ripper, from taking a man that Jacob felt was beyond the hope of redemption under a wing to desiring to destroy those that were former enemies would have been done, had been done in fact. Without hesitation. Without question. Yet desire blossomed inside of the dark heart. A desire to be her own Mistress, to have none to answer to save for herself. Desire made Angel make a decision she looked back upon now and considered a poor attempt at rebellion. She had failed, miserably. Been paid back in kind with the kind of treatment she had herself planned for the Ripper. It stung the pride of the Angel, her foolishness in believing that the Ripper did not know she had placed her dark sight upon the middle of his broad back. He had been there to watch it all with a front row ticket. From the finale of the Chris Williams Memorial Cup of 2003: Both Angel and Fey McClancy get into the ring at the same time, and as the referee indicates that one of them should leave, they both pull Shadow to his feet, propelling him to the ropes and then catching him with a double dropkick as he returns … but missing a very important action as they do so. In the background we hear voices raised in exclamation say, MB: Fey and Angel are both going after Shadow, but they haven’t realized … TJ: Realized what? MB: … that Shadow made the blind tag to the Ripper as he came off the ropes … he is not the legal man … RIPPER IS!!! Barely pausing, Ripper’s arm, chest and neck muscles strain as he lifts the women up .. one in each hand … before throwing them to the canvas again with a double choke slam. Both women hit the canvas hard and don’t move and with a disgusting grin, Ripper grabs Shadow by the hair and hoists him over his head with a military press … dropping the large man onto the prone bodies of both fallen women. Throwing Shadow across the ring by his hair, Ripper leans down, laying an arm across both Angel and Fey McClancy at the same time, and after a second’s pause the referee makes the count… 1.….. 2.…….. 3. Thinking back to the CWMC, Jake decided that the Angel was exactly what Sheri had always been. A survivor. Sheri had always been able to just re-deal out the cards of her life to fit into the way that she wanted things to go. Just because she had suffered a stinging defeat at the hands of her own Master did not mean that the Angel was far from over. Never. There was none who were going to be able to stop her once she found the way to achieve the greatness she sought. Angel knew the path she needed to walk upon if she wished to be everything that she had set her sights upon becoming. The seed of desire for freedom had been planted and the Angel had determined she would be free, one way or another. Bidding adieu to M V Pete in a simple farewell and walking away from the federation that had given Sheri her name, Angel had looked to find a new home after the CWMC had drew to a close. She would need a new place to wreak her havoc and cause her dark, demented destruction. Yet, family would catch up to the Angel at the CWMC for they had seen her name appear as one of those competing in the tournament and knew exactly where she would be. Jacob had still been in his seat watching the finals of the show when Rage had caught the Angel backstage. From the little the Angel had told him, she and Rage had clashed physically until he had landed a punch that had stunned her. Stunning her was all it had taken because Rage, with his friend Ashe had drug Angel off to a mental institution in upstate New York. He had been alone after that. Didn’t know what to do without the Angel to watch over him. Afraid to go home because of the hurt, worry and concern his leaving with Sheri had probably caused. So he had sought out the Ripper after the show and got rewarded with what the Angel had always threatened but never fulfilled. A brutal beating. He had awoke in a Canadian hospital a few days later, every bone in his body aching and him not able to really remember why. He was surprised to see Rayne sitting there in a chair by the bed. She had looked up from the magazine she had been reading and smiled, asking him how he was doing. He’d apologized for running away instead, for not doing what he had promised. Rayne had gently shook her head and said softly that she was glad to know that someone had been with Sheri through the whole ordeal. She said that his being with Sheri meant that there was still hope inside, that Sheri could be made well and their family could again be whole. It would not be so. That would have been to easy he thought near bitterly. The cloaked figure he had seen earlier at the cemetery gates would find him alone one night in the Ashanti house and bade him to follow him to where the Angel was. Jacob had refused at first, not wanting any more of the life that the Angel had given him. Yet, he couldn’t say no to her either. Many would say that their relationship was a sick and twisted one, bordering upon unhealthy but to Jacob it was like a little brother that worshipped his older sibling. Sheri was the Fallen Angel to him. He believed that the Angel still had some of the traits that had made Sheri the woman that she was. She was simply on a meltdown emotionally that just as she had done before, she would bounce back from. That desire to be seen as more then one single, solitary, feminine persona still was there inside of the Angel. Pride was evident in everything that she did. A stubborn will to do as she wanted no matter the consequences. There was also one more emotion. One that the Angel denied with every fiber of her being. Love. That was what made the Fallen Angel so deadly an adversary. She hated the emotion of love and at the same time let it be her strength and fuel of hatred. She would not have kept him close to her if she did not love him in her own, now dark fashion, Jacob knew that. So he had gone to the asylum that the Angel had been locked inside of and had done the last thing he had ever thought he would do. He had broke the Angel out. Sheri had always been known for her use of C4 explosives and in fact had nearly been arrested for a few of her explosive stunts while in XUW. Jacob found that it was quite a good way to cause a distraction. People seemed to believe someone was serious when something went kaboom. The Angel had been quite pleased with him as well and took him beneath her dark wing once more, strengthening their bond even further. Thus they had hidden away in the church Angel had purchased and converted to suit her needs. They searched for a new home, offering themselves as a package now to the various promotions they sent inquiries to. He became the Angel’s protégé and she taught him the things she had promised to teach to Pete. When he reached eighteen he would be ready to compete inside of the ring professionally and his training would be complete. The thought brought pride, excitement and extreme fear. He was ready to become a wrestler, it was all that he knew how to do but he was not sure he could be as ruthless as the Angel was. As cold hearted, as angry. As sadistic. Angel had found their home, PWW on an excursion. It had been quite by accident in fact. After searching many federations that she had proclaimed to find “loathsome,” she had decided she would pay close attention to one she had seen advertised on television. She watched the federation for a while, doing research upon the federation’s past. The people who were on the roster intrigued the Angel. The thrill of being a part of something where no one knew her name had drew her closer. Finally she had sent him to talk to the heads of the PWW, a month after careful deliberation and hiding to ensure that they would not be found this time by their family. They had been accepted quite easily, a deal not hard to agree on for the Angel had demanded little but the basics. The Angel again had a home… and a link to wreak hellish damnation upon many souls in this industry at the same time. This Cruiserweight Tournament by the PTC was a far cry removed from the Chris Williams Memorial Cup he felt. This focused upon Cruiserweights this time and not all comers of all sizes. Ah but the Angel was intelligent in that regard. She was herself a Cruiserweight and could better reestablish her manner of wrestling by facing those that were nearest to her own weight division. She had after all trained with Chris Benoit and Christopher Daniels both when she had been known as Sheri. The Angel would show a larger populace just how deadly a person she could be. Angel’s first two victims in the first round brackets of the Cruiserweight tournament were Syko Arsonist and Alex Caine. Jacob felt sorry for the two. The rage harbored in the Angel had been carefully kept banked beneath the surface, fed during her time spent in an institution that treated her like a psycho. That rage was harbored deep inside a soul that was already ravaged by hate. By anger. By pain. All Angel was doing was waiting for the right time to let this emotion loose. To let it become the focus of one person, or persons so that she could expel what she had held inside of herself outwards as physical pain upon others. The anticipation that hung in the air told him that the Angel was more then ready for the challenge that lay in front of her. In fact? It seemed like the Angel was down right eager for this challenge, which was why she had signed her name to a piece of paper that placed her in bracket two, match seven. These two individuals did not know what they were stepping into the ring with, much as the Angel did not know what she was stepping into the ring with. Jacob frowned as that particular thought entered his mind. He knew that Angel wanted to prove that she was no longer a puppet to a dead man, or a person that should be overlooked but what length would she really go to prove her point? Would she hurt people needlessly? Or would she get hurt herself to prove her point? He pondered that thought for it held many answers to things he had not thought to question. He did not know that the shadows grew darker around him. They closed in slowly, like a hand that crept from the darkness of a corner to startle an unsuspecting victim. Jacob was so preoccupied with his thoughts that he was not aware that the shadows drew so close until a cold breeze snaked around behind him, caressing the back of his neck and causing him to shiver. Whispers of those he knew were not alive touched his ears and quickly he turned his head to look around him. He noticed the shadows were closing in on him and once again that temptation to run entered his mind. She was here, he knew it. She was closing in on him, knew that he was here and hesitating because he was unsure of her mind frame. He stopped walking forward once he was sure the Angel was drawing close to the spot where he stood. He shoved his hands into his pants pocket and kicked the dirt around with the toe of his shoe as he waited for his mistress to appear. He felt very much like a child that was about to be punished and knew he had done something to displease his parents. From the dark cluster of shadow and night began a slight shape no bigger then a tree limb. Slowly the figure grew in shape, taking on the appearance of the woman that he now knew so well. Jacob didn’t need to look at the figure to know who the figure was. He knew it was the Fallen Angel. His mistress. How could it not of course be? There was no other that he was awaiting here in a God forsaken graveyard. The five-foot-five foot shape came beneath a beam of the moon that glittered down upon the world from above. A long cloak of black that blended into the shadows that surrounded her fell to the ground and enveloped her entire form. Waist length blue-black hair was hidden beneath the cowl of the cloak pulled about her pale face. Jacob was surprised. The black knee length boots she wore were hidden. The tight fitting stretch pants she always wore could not be seen beneath the cloak. The white ruffled blouse not seen at all. Everything pointed to one thing, the Angel had left the Church. He had known she was frequenting the graveyard more since her escape from the asylum that Brian and Rayne had placed her in, but the attire told him she had been doing more then simple planning. He wondered what she was about. Closer the Angel came, her cloaks hem dragging the ground. Frequenting a graveyard at midnight did not bother the Angel, of that Jacob was quite certain. She was quite comfortable in places like this after all. The Angel had of course been created in a place called the “Domicile” of Darkness. Jacob shuddered at just the thought that such a place could even exist. He watched as the Angel swept like a haunt towards him, her unseen booted feet making the illusion that she floated, not walked. Soon enough she paused as she stood in front of him, concealed as she was. “The Angel sees that you have appeared as called Jacob.. She is pleased by this.” The grating voice, harsh and hoarse from disuse breaks the ominous silence that had fallen a few seconds before. Yet, it was still a voice that Jacob knew well. It belonged to the Angel. He took a deep breath, preparing an apology that he knew was expected to be given. “Yes Angel, I apologize for my tardiness but…” A single, solitary black gloved hand was raised to silence the rest of the words that would have fallen from his lips. “It is of no matter for your tardiness this time to the Angel for she was away on business of her own. Business of which proved very profitable in discovery.” Jacob frowned in concern at her admission that she had gone off on business of her own. This Cruiserweight tournament would prove to be most dangerous for them both as was, she should not be going off on her own alone. “Business of your own? Angel, you know that it is dangerous still for you to wander about alone. We do not know where Rage may lurk, looking for the sister that he wants back,” He reprimanded softly, knowing he could dare for the Angel would find amusement in the action. “Let Rage try and find the Angel this time. He will be disappointed when he is refrained from accomplishing that which he believes he has a right to.” Silence fell after those coldly uttered words. Jacob sensed the deep abiding hatred that was contained for the man that had locked her into a place that had caged the Angel like an animal. Rage was a bane to the Angel for that reason, for he represented something the Angel hated. A block to the dominance that she craved to have over herself. Jacob stood there looking at the Angel, wondering what thoughts were going on in her mind and not having to really think hard about them. Anger was coursing through those veins filled with ice. An anger that could quickly snap out of control if it was not suppressed. The shadows knew this and thus they whispered softly to their mistress, attempting to calm the disquiet that they had sensed had appeared at the mention of Rage. “Where did you go Angel?” Jacob finally asked softly, giving things a few minutes to calm before he said anything. “The Angel sought out a place she could find information upon those that she was set to face very soon,” Angel gave as her explanation. A simple explanation with nothing there as an answer. “She found nothing of course that suited her intellectual needs.” The same Angel as always he noticed with a faint smirk. The Angel was typically egotistical, condescending, and doubting towards the intelligence of those she called “mortals.” It was a mistake that in the end, could be costly. Especially with a tournament like the Cruiserweight one looming so close over her head. The Angel needed a second good showing in a big tournament situation and possible win to be able to establish herself not only as a legit contender in the PTC, but also for the PWW who had yet to open it’s doors officially. “Angel,” He began, choosing his words with care. “Believing that these two that you face in the first round brackets to be beneath you could become a costly mistake. There is much at stake in this tournament, and foolishness in the first round could cause us a steep setback. I‘m hoping that you do realize that.” Silence came from the figure that stood in front of him. Quite a few moments of silence in fact which formed a lump of fear deep in his throat. The Angel was compassionate, generous with her leniency most often but even she could be pushed to moments of great anger. He did not desire to be on the receiving end of her anger at this moment in time. He was forming the apology that was stuck in his throat when a soft chuckle, harsh and chilly as an arctic breeze came from the woman in front of him. “The protégé becomes the Master for a moment. You are right Jacob. The Angel sees yours words as truth and honors the wisdom of those words. She thanks you dearly for pointing out the ill wise choice she had made in doubting her opponents. Come now, there is much for us to discuss this evening for there is much planning that must be done if the Angel is to perform as she demands of herself to do.” Jacob was surprised. He had never expected that the Angel would be so lenient with what he had to say, so accepting of the words. It brought a beam of hope into his heart. A beam that said perhaps more of Sheri returned every day. Perhaps the Angel’s hold over Sheri wasn’t as tenuous as she had thought. It was a good hope to have. Smiling genuinely now and feeling less afraid of the surroundings he found himself standing in, Jacob nodded his head. “Shall we adjourn to the mausoleum then?” “No,” Came the firm answer from Angel as she turned, the cloak sweeping the dirt and rocks upon the ground into an arc that splattered his shoes and pants. He tilted an eyebrow at the sudden change in her mind of their meeting place but then again, it was not that much of a surprise. The Angel was after all, very capable of changing her mind about situations when they suited her to do so. “Then where shall we be going?” He questioned softly, noting how the figure slowly merged back into the shadows that she had come from minutes before. “We are going to a place that Sheri used to frequent quite often. It is a distance from here, but it will serve to rid the Angel of the dust of rust that lingers on her form from not wrestling for a few months. Come Jacob, the journey is long and you will be tired before we reach there.” Jacob nodded his head, slowly beginning to follow after her and wondering exactly where it is that the Angel was taking him. Hope blossomed more though as he realized that she was heading to a place that once was Sheri’s domain. That meant only one thing. It meant only one place. Excitement coursed through him as the answer became clear to him. They were heading to the private gym that Sheri owned. The smile that had been on his lips before was widened at the implication. Angel inside was slowly losing her tight grip and allowing more of Sheri’s characteristics to peek through that cold veneer. Perhaps one day he thought… perhaps one day we’ll see Sheri come back. That was something that he really wished would happen. As much as he admired and adored the Angel… as much as he had Sheri really, he missed the warmth that had once been inside of Sheri’s eyes. He missed the gentle humor. The way she would gently reprimand him for something that he hadn’t intended to do. In all, he missed Sheri and he knew that Rage, their parents, Rayne and a lot of other people did as well. Noticing that the figure of the Angel had paused and was waiting for him with her back turned, Jacob sped up his steps. After all, keeping someone like the Angel waiting was pushing things a little to far he decided. One day would be soon enough to test the patience of the woman waiting for him. For now, he would follow. Soon he was enveloped in the darkness that surrounded his mistress. All fell back into the silence of a little while before.. With the exception of the wolf’s howl that shattered the sudden silence. Then everything fell in silence and shadow once more.
