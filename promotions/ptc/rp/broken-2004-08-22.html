Broken

Date: 2004-08-22 I know there is a reason why I take the pain, week-in and week-out. I bleed for my dreams, ones that been a live for what seems to be forever. I shred tears, both for the high points and the low points of my bumpy career. I shallow the pain, I ignore the blood, and wipe away the tears. Yet as of late, I have be heading down, even though I have been trying the hardest to win, it just that the competition must be getting tougher or am I growing weaker. The injuries been mounting up, first my ribs were crush, then my neck sprained, and finally all over my body are minor injuries adding up into one completely sore wreck. My allies say, take a break, recover from those battle wounds, but I simply refused after each injury. However, the recent result of my title defense has enlightened me, that I am not indestructible but instead very fragile. Yet I have more matches before I take my rest to heal old and new wounds, it is a fight for survival… a fight for my survival. I have to battle pass my limits, I have to ignore the pain and feed off the adrenaline pumping through my veins. I lost because of those spots of momentary selfish thoughts, yet why should I care about myself while I still have a debt to the man that made me become what I am, the man that fuel my desire to become on of these wrestlers, and the man who showed me there is always going to be a light at the end the tunnel. I must pay tribute to my father, for this is what he would have done if it wasn’t for his premature death… I am sitting down on the black leather couch, resting for a bit to ease the stress on my wounds, as I just sits there wondering why? Why couldn’t I make it to the ladder in time to bring down the thief? I will not blame the crimson mask that I had on my face, with the blood sting my eyes and the plain taste of it in my mouth. I will not blame my crushed ribs, the damage that was caused earlier in the match. I blame myself, for that moment I gave up my dreams, a crime in my opinion, one that instead of giving me some reward, taken has taken what mine as I just watched as my belt was taken down from the ladder. I tried to pull him down, off the top of the ladder, but it was those few moments of rest. I gave myself that cost me the most. Granted, I made a few mistakes on that night, but I wrestled better than both and I was automatically the only loser, as my other opponent, Garvin walked in with nothing and walked out with nothing. I had to pay the price for making that choice, the choice that might have well ended my career in Wrestling Midwest. I notice the pieces of scattered glass in the corer, the remains of the broken beer bottle, the only one that didn’t get cleaned up after his act of gluttony. Not just my wrestling career is going sour, but it seems like my life is going down faster, as it has a rocket strapped to my back. As of late, there has been nothing but deaths, deaths, and even more deaths invading my life, also knocking me off balance. Maybe I have been broken and scattered, causing the recent string of losses that been replaying in my head. Everything bad happens to me remains inside my head, they dominate my dreams or should I say my nightmares. I used alcohol as a way to escape the suffering, but no more will I rely on gluttony, I shall take out the stress by wrestling, the only really passion left in my life. None of my loved ones should have to live though something like that again, they don’t deserve that. Still, I hope these loses don’t turn into a losing streak.. Suddenly the door opens up, in the door way appears her again, why is this woman is howling after me? I scan up her smooth legs, noticing how tight her black shorts are to her hips and ass, before standing back in awe before standing up due to being discomfort. She moves towards me, wrapping her arms around my neck, before looking into my eyes, making me for some reason just stare back into her pretty emerald eyes. She steps up onto her toes, pressing her soft pink lips up against mine, then we just both at the same open our mouths to accept a kiss. Our lips remain together for what seems to be a long tongue for me, guilt slipping into my throat as it’s a gag, Athena could be hurt because of this. But why don’t I stop? I fought off the temptation for her back in Rochester, did one more lost made me this desperate? Am I this bad of a man? I have to stop, but for some reason I can’t Maybe my wound is still not unhealed; maybe I’m still in love with her. I couldn’t take anymore of this, as I softly push her down away from my now sweating body, as she looks up smiling with a cute smile, one that I just want love to stand and adore. However, I stare down at her with a frown, the internal battle in my heart has flip-flop as my logic has finally taken back the control of my body. She shows a newly formed expression of hurt as her words just float gracefully from her mouth, “What’s the problem? Adam? I know you and I still love each other. I knew what the link felt like in the past. I felt the connection back in Rochester… I could feel it now. Just tell, Adam… what is stopping you from unleashing your passion onto me?” “A love for another, you know that. I told you back in Rochester that I have someone else in my life,” and in fact I did tell her if I remember correctly. When I broke off the kiss back then on that cool night, in front of the restaurant, I thought she understand that. Athena is here for me; she endured the drinking problem, she was there for him when all the sudden deaths of other close ones occurred, and she is still there. “You telling me, this woman is that much of saint that you…” a single tear flows down her right cheek, a sincere tear that symbolizes the feeling I felt down inside too, but she just wipes it off before continuing on, “you are willing to pass up this chance to continuity on what we left off.” For a second I could feel my heart twist in pain but then reinforcements comes with recent memories of Athena, I reply, “Look, I admit I still have feelings for you. But Athena has been here for me, okay? You weren’t there when I wanted to go abroad to enhance my skills at wrestling.” “That is sooo unfair!” her face is now red but then she stops for a moment, before looking down at the hardwood floor, “You don’t understand, I too had dreams. You didn’t realize mine, why should I realize yours? But what am I saying, I’m asking you to break up your girlfriend… who is probably really nice, and sweet.” “Don’t worry, we can still be best of friends right?” I ask her, everything is coming straight from my heart, but suddenly she glances into my eyes once again, but this time filled with anger. She yells, I know she probably didn’t mean to yell, but she does, “You’re rejecting me with this, how about we’re just be friends line! I used that on many people, now you… of all people, are using that on me! You’re total asshole!” Before I could get any words out, she quickly once again run away from me, but inside of out into the night streets of Rochester, this time out of my apartment in Cleveland. For a second, just like a week ago, I try to chase after her before quitting due to me not knowing what I would say to fix this situation. All I know, I am getting completely knocked out with this shit, I have to train for my match. Suddenly another door swings open, just like one of those 50s television shows, well they all just walk into the family room by swinging open the door. This time is, ironically I’m happy that it’s not Athena right now, but instead his sister, Maria. She has that completely arrogant face showing, but she stares at me like if she got something on me now. I damn know there is something up her sleeve, probably the blackmail card, because I pretty sure she was listening behind that door. Well, she can go ahead and try to fuck up with my life, because you can’t screw up what already screwed up. Yet, I’m surprise that she doesn’t give me enough credit, I feed her, I put a roof over her head now, I buy her clothes, and everything. She always treats me like shit it seems, I haven’t done a thing to her in years. God, I hate younger siblings… sooo annoying. However, she did snap him out of his binge of drinking non-stop and going around acting like complete evil asshole, but what are siblings for. You help out when you only have to help out. Yet she is still looking at me with much obvious disrespect so I just had it, “What the hell are you looking at, sis… If I were you, I would just turn around and go back into your room.” “Just calm down, Adam… what have I done as of late to piss you off? Nothing! You’re acting like a total prick for some reason, what? Did Garvin and Syberus shove the ladder so far up your ass, your being an asshole because you lost?” Maria says, standing right up to her face, even if she had a good point of her not really doing anything as of late to him that deserve a good bitching, I still don’t like her standing up to me like that. I try to think of something smart to say back but alas my mind has other ordeals, but then she laughs at him, replying to him, “Are you that empty inside that you no longer know what to say to me?” Suddenly her works strikes me, am I really empty? I might be broken, I might be misled, I might be undecided, hell… but am I empty. For all my life, all I have known is to fight to keep my dreams alive, which in return will make my father rest in peace. However, will I have going to succeed in this mission, take away that, but I got nothing but hardship. Family honor is the only thing motivating me… Shaking off those disturbing thoughts from my head, I turn around before heading off though the same doorway that Sandra just ran through moments before me. It’s time to go to the gym, I clear have to train for this event to make sure I don’t get in any trouble losing. There are big people in this event, mainly the people I look up to in LOW, Core has a good team too, but it time to make sure Wrestling Midwest get some fame… earn some respect. I’m going out there not only gunning to win, but I’m going out there mainly to work my ass off to impress the fans, to impress the management in Primetime Central, proving WMW is the real deal. If Wraith doesn’t try, I shall make up for his weakness, a thing that is common in the Kindred. If Matrix gets his ass pin, I’ll just be waiting out on Burnout to tell him off, tell him it’s his fault if he no shows. Noah and I will be in there taking out everyone left and right, Soul Purpose shall dominate. I got a feeling when I got out there the pain in my body will fade as the adrenaline just take over, so I’ll probably be good as new by then. Wrestling Midwest is going to be the one that is going to be look up at, I’m going to break myself if need it, because I was told by a wise man that if you try your hardest, you respect yourself as well as others, you don’t… you end up with nothing. WMW will rule all come PTC: Survival, just wait, and be enlightened by us…
