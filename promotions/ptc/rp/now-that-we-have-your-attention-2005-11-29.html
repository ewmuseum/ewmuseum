Now That We Have Your Attention…

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 By and large, conspiracy theorists are whack jobs, living in their parents basement, wearing tin foil hats, and watching reruns of "Gilligan's Island" to look for clues about what our government has in store for us. But, every so often, one man stumbles along a conspiracy running so deep, about a company so crooked, corrupt and vile, that it will literally turn your stomach inside out. We attempted to contact Michael Moore, in hopes he would take our case, but, he was too busy pandering to Hollywood to notice us. We had to look for another fat man with absolutely no moral integrity, and no idea what he was talking about, yet still had a modicum of charisma. We found him, and secured backing for what would be our documentary. Thankfully, he's gotten your attention by being a non-douchebag, so that when we force him to be a Moore-esque douchebag, you'll hate him that much more. The company: Prime Time Central Enterprises, based in Melbourne, Australia, with offices in Cleveland, Orlando, Seattle, and London. It's leader, a nearly cult like icon with his followers, a man named [name withheld], who insists that his followers call him "Argyle". And yet, the man doesn't even own a pair of argyle socks. Curious, no? Even more curious is the posting made by Argyle when this "tournament" opened. Using an algorhythm passed down from the bible, we were able find more information to look for more of our conspiracy theory. - Before Roleplaying starts there will be a five day "hype period". The roleplay period will be five days after that (10 days total). - During this five days, you must post from a minimum of one hype on the GTT5 site to a full maximum of five, but only one per day. - Hypes will have a word limit - 1000 words (a couple over is okay, but not many). - The purpose of posting these hypes is to get your character over, BAMN. You can use written words, you can post images (you need to provide hosting for them, and you need to make sure they don't break my site layout!). We looked deeper and found even more messages, hidden in the second part. - For content, you can trash talk, conduct media interviews, post weblogs, hype the match, hype your character, have a mock debate (co-operative with your opponent)… basically anything you can think of that will make your character get noticed and can be posted on the site (usual no-porn rules apply ). - The hype post does not have to directly relate to the RP (but smart hype will add value to the RP). - Judges will be encouraged to read the hypes… your hypes will be linked at the top of your RP's once they are posted. Officially, the hype is to be considered "Support Material" - i.e. non-essential reading. The trick is, the better you do, the more people will talk about your work on the boards, the more people are aware of your presence in GTT5. - You may consider five to be too much, you may consider one to be not enough… how many hypes you do up to your limit of five is a gamble you take. Judges may not read all five if you do five uninteresting ones. Even more curious, huh? Next, we'll address the rumors of gifts given to judges of the GTT.
