The Chronicles of Muppet V - The Search

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 It was King Muppet's turn to be exhausted, and damn, was he feeling it. Sprinting down the steps leading up to Muppet Manor, forgetting his keys in the rush, he felt it course through his entire body, slowing him down like a lead weight. Still, he gritted his teeth and pushed through, doing his best to ignore the pain. His brain was screaming at him to stop, to give up, to go back and lay down, but he persevered. He knew he had to find the Muppet Man, before he did something he would regret. What that something was, King didn't know, nor particularly care. He just knew that he had to prevent it. Heading as fast as his legs would allow him, he rummaged around inside his pocket, pulling out his cellphone, nearly dropping it in the rush. After a clumsy looking juggle, he regained control, and began keying in numbers a little too fast, and put it to his ear. He skidded to a halt, almost falling over onto the road in the path of a speeding car, but managed to stop himself. He waited impatiently as it rang. It was answered. King punched the air in triumph. He was one step closer. "Hello, Pannuci's Pizza, how can I help yo..." "Fuck," he muttered under his breath, so that the woman on the other end couldn't hear him, hanging up without a word. It was cold, and he could feel his fingers slowly numbing, but he continued to dial his partner's number, putting the phone to his ear again. There was not even a ring. The bastard had turned his phone off. Shoving the phone roughly back into his pocket, he jogged up to his car, pulling in vain at the door before realising he actually needed to unlock it. Again, his hand shot into his pocket, looking for his keys. He cursed rather loudly again, sprinting back to the house. Not the ideal start, but all the same... The chase was on. ---- And so, the road goes on. On and on it winds, with no clear end in sight. Even if I lose in the GTT, even if I stumble and fall, even if I fail to deliver what I have promised over and over again, to myself and to the people... it goes on. It never ends. Do I want it to? No... no, of course not. This is my life. This is what I have done for as long as I can clearly remember. I don't know anything else, I couldn't possibly consider a life without it. At least that's what I keep telling myself. Do I really want this? Do I really want to carry on living my life like this? I have to find out... I have to find the answer for myself. Otherwise I can never go on. ---- King Muppet felt himself drifting off as he sat behind the wheel of his car, and shook his head violently. It took most of the willpower he had left to just keep his eyes open as he drove, but he did it. He was deep in thought, barely concentrating on the road ahead. It was lucky it wasn't busy. As he went rather speedily down the country road, not another car in sight, he glanced up at the sky above. Steadily greying clouds were gathering, the last few rays of sunlight being ever so slowly blocked out. King sighed. Nothing was going right. To be blunt and honest, he really had no idea where he was going. This was more of an instinct thing, instead of sitting down and forcing himself to think of the most likely place a fleeing Muppet Man was likely to go. Looking back on it, it was probably... scratch that, definitely the wrong decision to make. But, when King looked back over the majority of his life, that was what had always happened. He was no good under pressure, but he'd turned out fine, hadn't he? Probably best not to think about that.... The time flew by. Before he knew it, King Muppet had arrived at the shopping centre slash train station, moments after he had decided that was where he was going. He thought it was probably the most logical place for Muppet to head. After all, he hadn't had the option of taking the car, so he had to catch a bus or a train. They all went here. From here, you could get basically anywhere you wanted. Thinking back on it, King wondered why he'd even considered going anywhere else. Pulling into the parking lot, he had to drive around for a good 4 minutes before found a space. He quickly pulled up, getting out and almost walking away before locking up, before realising his error. Parking prices were as ridiculously high as ever, but that didn't matter. Not now. ---- Horazon. He is next. He is the one that challenges me, he is the one that stands in my way, preventing me from ever finding that answer that I so desire. He is the greatest test of my life. It's funny. A man I've never heard of, from no federation in particular, is suddenly a massive brick wall, impeding my slow but steady progress. I know nothing of him. He came from nowhere. I have never even spoken to him in my life, nor he to me. But none of that matters. All that matters is that he is here, now, and he is the one that I must defeat. He is not the only one, though. There is a part of me, full of doubt, that still remains, after all I have done to subdue it. I have fought it so many times, it's almost... natural, now. This time, though, it comes back with it's greatest weapon yet. I don't know how to fight it, and if I lose... well, then there's no coming back. And that's why I have to find the answer. ---- Bursting through the doors from the staircase leading up from the parking lot, King Muppet strolled into the shopping centre. This really was the fastest route to wherever he wanted to go from here. At least, it usually was. Tonight, it was packed. There was absolutely no other word for it. As soon as he made it 5 metres or so in, King found himself unable to move. "Come on, come on, get out of the way..." he spoke quietly to himself, trying to find a way through the overwhelming mass of people. It was no use. He began to look around for another route down into the train station, where he didn't have to make it through a swamp of people to get there. Luckily, this was quite a complicated and convoluted shopping centre. You could enter a shop, and come out the other end somewhere completely different. Almost every shop had two entrances, too. Unluckily, though, he had no idea where he was going. Doing his best to break out of the crowd, King made his way into a nearby music store. Ignoring everything and everyone as best he could, he moved through it swiftly. He was getting more than a few strange looks from passers by, but he ignored them. He didn't care. Darting out of the store, he glanced around. He knew vaguely where he was, and where he needed to go from here. Spotting a clear route to a psychiatrist's reasonably close, he made a beeline for it, doing his best not to give in to temptation and run. He didn't want to look like a complete twat on top of all of this. He wondered why there was a psychiatrist in a shopping centre, before realising he didn't care, and entered. This, however, didn't go quite to plan. "Ah, welcome. I've been expecting you." The rough voice of the psychiatrist stunned King momentarily, giving the man enough time to take him firmly by the arm and guide him into a comfortable leather armchair, sitting him down. He smiled warmly, almost patronisingly. "Well, where shall we start?" "Um... I'm not meant to be here," King said, wanting nothing more than to just burst from the room, but he didn't want to cause a scene. He'd probably end up getting arrested, or something. He'd had some pretty bad experiences like that in the past... "Ah, I see. You clearly have a case of amnesia. You have come here, you have been drawn here, but you can't quite remember why. Don't worry, sir, this is perfectly natural. Please, sir, relax..." the psychiatrist said, edging King back into the seat. He wasn't going to get away that easily. For anyone listening in, what the man was saying sounded perfectly reasonable, but it was really a load of crap. "No, I'm serious... I'm really not meant to be here," King said again, hesitantly glancing around. He was nervous, and not quite sure why. "Amnesia and denial? This could be more serious than I thought. Relax, Mr, um..." he lifted a piece of paper, checking the name of the client he was actually expecting, "...Jackington." "What the..." "Now, now, sir, please keep calm. We'll have you as good as new in no time." "I'M FINE!" King roared, getting straight up to his feet. "Denial helps nothing!" the psychiatrist roared back, also getting. "You're not leaving here until you're cured! Now will you sit down, sir!" With a sigh of exasperation, King fell back down into the seat. I'm coming, Andy! ---- Once again, Will... I'm sorry. I could have stayed that little bit longer. I could have told you, I could have given you some sort of warning. Hell, I could have even brought you along for the ride. It wouldn't have worked. I know you're looking for me right now. I know you're searching high and low, and you're not far behind. You won't find me. It's better that way. We're a good team, but this is something I have to do on my own. This is an enemy only I can fight. This is a trial only I can overcome. Where do I go now? How can I work this all out? I don't have the answers, Horazon, not yet... but neither do you. ---- Having escaped from the mad psychiatrist when the real Mr. "Jackington" turned up, King Muppet sprinted down the staircase into the train station, counting his luck. His spirits were immediately dampened, though, when he saw the train schedule. He was so close, but he realised that he had no idea where the Muppet Man was going. Even if he did, it wouldn't have been much help. There were a good 5 trains heading to each destination. Cursing out loud, no longer caring what those around him thought, King sprinted down any random corridor, with all of the reserves of energy he had left. Arriving on the platform, he skidded to a halt, just in time to see the train that had been there set off, slowly at first, building speed. Then he noticed the figure staring back at him through the glass, smiling apologetically, as if to say 'nice try'. There was no mask, but it was unmistakeable. The Muppet Man had gone. For the umpteenth time this night, King Muppet cursed under his breath, exhaustion finally mastering him. He fell to his knees, unable to steady himself anymore, breathing heavily. He had lost. He only hoped the Muppet Man had more luck against Horazon. To be continued... ...Maybe. *Pannuci's Pizza is a fictional pizza company from Futurama. Not sure if I should mention that or not...
