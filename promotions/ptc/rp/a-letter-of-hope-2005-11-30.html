A Letter of Hope

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Present Day Havoc Entertainment, Inc. Justin Michaels (Address Withheld) Christopher (Kaden) Calix (Address Withheld) Dear Mr. Calix: I would first like to say how much I enjoyed watching your video. Your ring work is spectacular, and had a lot of those guys you worked with inside the ring in Athens Underground there in Greece been as good as you, I would have liked to come watch some of your guy's "shows" myself. On to business. I know that it is almost the end of 2005, and the date on this video that I got my hand on was dated clear back to 2002, but you have to understand I didn't have the resources in 2002 that I have now. In fact, I was still wrestling back in 2002. Anyway, I held onto this tape, and since then, my wrestling business has grown into a multi-billion dollar corporation. Training facilities and promotional federations strategically placed all over the world, excellent training staff at every facility, not to mention all the hard working people we have to hire as managers as well as others to help benefit you in your every need. What I am getting at here Kaden, Mr. Calix, is I would like to fly you to America. Enclosed with this letter is a first class plane ticket to Atlanta, Georgia, where I would like you to meet me at Havoc Entertainment, Inc. headquarters. All the services to get you here have all been set up. Not only am I flying you here for a meeting, but I have a training schedule worked up for you already, I have a personal trainer picked out, all the sponsorship paper work is filled out ready for you to sign when you get here, and I, myself, would be honored if you allowed me to manage your up and coming career. By the way, I have already made my first big decision for you by signing you up for one of the world's largest tournaments, GTT5. This thing is hyped like no other, and includes the top talent on Earth. I think you would enjoy it, and it would be a great way to start your career… With a bang! All you need to do is use the ticket on the exact date, and everything will fall into place after that. Looking forward to seeing you in what looks to be a few weeks. Sincerely, Justin 'Stormm' Michaels Founder/President/CEO
