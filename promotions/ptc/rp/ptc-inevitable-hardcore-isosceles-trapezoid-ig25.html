Inevitable (The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, IG25)

7 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Justin Williamson was happy. No, really, he was. He had a gift for teaching wrestling. PCW had recognized it back in the day when they had tapped him to be an instructor at the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Some of his students included Skylar Green, former multi-time World Champion, and Joshua Kosildo, conqueror of Hoyt Williams in GTT6. Years later, PRIME had given him a call. He'd worked for PRIME for a year or so, off and on. He was probably best known there for his wars with Karina Wolfenden, and his partnership with Angelo Deville. Now, though, he was training the next generation. He had a hand in the development of Sun Tzu and Alexander Kannon. Even so, though, Justin Williamson was...changing. It had been subtle at first. Like his decision to not re-dye his hair brown. Williamson was a natural redhead, but he had been dying his hair for a few years now. "Eh," he'd figured, when he threw out the bottle of hair dye. "Time for a change." That, of course, had only been the start. His hair had grown out. Justin hadn't seen a barber in forever. He shrugged it all off, but the students noticed. He knew they did. He heard them whispering about it. You see, the white elephant in the room was the fact that Justin Williamson was far better known by another name. It was by his nom de guerre that he had carved out a reputation as the most violent man in professional wrestling. As a man with more named moves than anyone in the business. As an innovative, insane lunatic with absolutely no regard for his own well being. As the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. But that phase of his life was over. Never mind the fact that every time he looked at himself in the mirror, he saw the tattoos he had gotten done back then. Saw the words "Hardcore Calculus" written on his midsection. Saw the barbed wire tattooed over his arms. More importantly, he saw the scars criss-crossing his body. He still remembered which he got from his Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch with Sirian in the UWF. He still remembered which was from a PCW Mall Brawl. Justin Williamson loved wrestling more than anything else. He had loved teaching, too, but those loves were entirely different. One was borne from the goodness of his heart, the other borne from three decades of resentment that he had harbored towards the world. The HIT was his coping mechanism, a zany way to lash back at the world. But if that part of his life was over...why was he letting his hair grow out? Why was he letting it remain the fiery red color that was his trademark back then? Why, in a few more weeks, he would have the full-fledged afro that had made The HIT the sort of wrestler that you knew when you saw... Justin chuckled. He had tried to fight it for so long that he had almost convinced himself that he didn't want to come back. But his students knew otherwise. No longer had he been simply training them. The moves he had used to half-ass against them were now snapped off and crisply done. Practice matches against Justin had once been a technical contest. Now, he was throwing out moves he hadn't used in years. Taking chances that he had told his students were far too dangerous. As students of the sport, they knew that they hadn't been in the ring with Justin Williamson. No, they'd been in the ring with the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. A day or so later, it had been confirmed. In the middle of a practice bout, Justin had rolled to the outside, grabbed a chair, and clocked one of his trainees over the head. Of course, he had apologized profusely, but that wasn’t the point. The instinct was back. Justin Williamson had enjoyed displaying his technical superiority over opponents. As much fun as that could be, though, he had always known that it could never compare to the HIT’s balls-to-the-wall, use-what-you’ve-got style. In a strange, twisted way, he missed the scars. He missed the agony of being shredded. It let him know that he was alive. And so, no one was really surprised when Justin Williamson gathered his students together after a long practice session and told them that he was going to enter IG25. This, he knew, was probably it. His best chance to make a name for himself. If he won IG25, he’d win a third round spot in GTT6, against either AWC’s reigning Transatlantic Champion, a former PTC Global Champion, or Jason Snow. The time came to sign up a few days later. When it did, Justin Williamson smiled, letting his pen sketch out something he had not written in years. When he finished, there was a look of smug satisfaction on his face. For, written there beside the word “Name” were three simple letters. HIT. ------------------------------------- I’ve almost forgotten what it’s like to wrap my hands in barbed wire. A few years off from unimaginable pain has rejuvenated me. Sure, I’m not 100%, but what wrestler is? Besides, it doesn’t matter. The Infinite Gauntlet is worth any amount of pain that I will have to withstand. I was in the original GTT, many years ago. I defeated Dan Hollywood in round one, only to lose to some upstart in the second round. It has haunted me to this day. One of the few regrets I have, along with never being a World Champion. Now, I have the chance to avenge that first tournament. I have the chance to avenge everything. I can laugh now, as the barbed wire digs into the sensitive flesh of my palms. There is pain, yes, but it’s the sort of pain that makes me smile. A familiar pain. It’s been ages, seemingly, since I’ve turned my hands into true weapons. It reminds me of the last time I truly felt alive, against Karina Wolfenden at Culture Shock two years ago. With our hands and legs wrapped in barbed wire, exchanging blows, until I could not answer the bell. I may have lost that night, but I have never forgotten that feeling. ….I almost feel sorry for them. I almost feel sorry for everyone who’s never had to fight for their life. It is an exhilaration beyond all others. But no matter. I shall bring them that fight. The Infinite Gauntlet is everything to me. PRIME believes that my role is in the Academy, training the next generation. My contract forbids me from competing for another organization. But that contract says nothing about the Gauntlet. Being a trainer at the Academy is the cruelest of teases. It lets me be in the ring, but not in front of the crowds. No longer can I hear the chants of “TRAP-EZ-OID!”. No longer can I hear the people gasp as I defy gravity or logic. The Infinite Gauntlet gives me a chance to hear that, one more time. And with a little luck, a little blood, and a little bit of barbed wire, the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid will reign victorious over a pile of broken bodies. Jason Snow, GBJ, Saij, do you know what I am? I am a man with nothing to lose. A man with everything to gain. If I’m willing to wrap my hands in barbed wire just to get a crack at one of you, just IMAGINE what will happen if I win… Sleep well, gentlemen. For now, all you can do is hope against the inevitable.
