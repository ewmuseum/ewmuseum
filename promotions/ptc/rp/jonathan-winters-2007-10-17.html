Jonathan Winters

Jonathan Winters So I guess this the part where I, Jonathan Winters - the future of PRIME, am supposed to explain why I deserve the supposed ‘opportunity’ to face an Edward Norton wannabe so dubiously named ‘The Illusionist’ in the next round of the Infinite Gauntlet? I’m supposed to make some kind of verbal list, to pour over all the things that I’ve thus far accomplished in my illustrious and prestigious career, so that the officials can decide whether I’m worthy of receiving such a…gift. Well fuck you, fuck all of you. I’m not lowering myself to level of some kind performing monkey for the likes of you fuckwits, I’m not going to audition for something that I should‘ve been granted automatically and without a second thought. Why? You want to know the reason behind my undoubtedly perceived arrogance? Well fuck you, fuck all of you all over again. Despite all of your undeniable ignorance, I am the PTC. I am the Infinite Gauntlet. There is no choice in the matter, not anymore, now there is only fact and that fact is there should be no question who is to challenge this pretender, but there is. The PTC officials and backstage politicians don’t think I’m a suitable role model, they don’t won’t be anywhere near the Infinite Gauntlet or any such PTC event. They are cowards hiding behind lies and disinformation. Well fuck you all. I say to you all, come out of hiding and step with me into the light. Book me in round thirty-eight of the never-ending battle against this Illusionist patsy and see justice be done, not because I deserve the chance more than anyone of the others, do it because it is mine by right. I am Jonathan Winters - the future of Prime. I am the Infinite Gauntlet. I am the PTC. Fuck the rest.
