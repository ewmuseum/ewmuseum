The Reunion, Part I: Long Time No See

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 There comes a time in any man's life when he must run from something. It doesn't matter how unafraid he is of anything, or how much he hides how scared he really is. You can never be sure what life will throw you either, and for most they go down swinging. Me, I chose to not even show up and try my luck at the plate, I ran. There was a time a few years ago when I had to, and I'll pay for it now. -------------------------------------- Have you ever wondered if there was something in the dark that isn't there in the light? You can assume not, but when the mind starts to play tricks on you, it makes you wonder. I've never been a strong believer with the theory myself, until now. There I was, sitting in the dark, and letting my mind play tricks on me. Why was I there? Simple! I was still running. There I thought that a part of my life was gone forever, and now it's come back to bite me in the ass. So I forced myself into that tight little space, pitch black, wondering how long it was going to take before I could have left. In the process of worrying, the slightest noise would frighten, even me. I am someone who leads on to be afraid of nothing, someone he isn't scared of anyone, and there I was, sitting in the dark, curled up into the fetal position, worrying something was going to get me. I could easily hear people who walked by from time to time, and in my position right then, that should have been what scared me the most, but it wasn't. It was the small little ticks, scratches, and creeks that got to me. I had turned myself into a paranoid little boy, someone I had used to be, and for good reasons too. So why was I still running? What had I done? To put those answers out there would be too hard to explain. The easiest way to put it would be to go back a few years, back when I was still a person I'm glad I'm not anymore. I fled to Japan, for more than one reason at that. The main reason I went was to start my wrestling career, the other was to get away from some people. I didn't necessarily know what I was doing most of the time back then either. I couldn't control myself. And when a certain event happened in my life, it sent me ever further off the deep end. I took a few things from a group of people I shouldn't have. It was only fair though, they had taken something from me. Two years in Japan, a couple of great years for me in the ring and out, and I figured I was ready to come back to the states, and so I did. I managed to land a couple of fairly decent deals, moving me around, keeping my mind off things, and letting me do what I have come to love... wrestle. Then I took my self even farther, I admitted myself into a mental institute, hoping to better myself. In a way I did, I no longer saw things that have plagued me for years. I did and still do, however, think bad thoughts, even worse thoughts, than I had before. But it is my style, and I was happy with the outcome still, until those guys had found me as a result of me being in Southwind (the mental institute) in the confines of the Midwest. So why was I huddled in a corner, inside of that little dark room? Because someone managed to find my crazy ass a couple of months ago in the state I figured would have been the safest for me, Kansas. Since then, I've found myself within about one-third of the states of the continental US. Where was I right now? If my memory served me correctly, I was somewhere in Louisiana, possibly in New Orleans. I wasn't sure. I was just worried about staying away from the suits. 'Hey, Louie! Maybe in here?' The voiced called from outside, and the hair on my body stood straight up. Not that I could have seen it, but I felt it anyway. I tensed up, knowing that I couldn't run now, I was just going to have to let them find me. I sat there, with my eyes closed tight, waiting for the door to fly open. A couple minutes flew by, nothing. I heard the voice call out once more. 'Never mind, I found it.' I sigh of relief came from my body, and I relaxed my muscles. Was that the wrong thing to do in a time like this? Yes it was. The door then flew open, a dark suited man looked at me for a second, realized who I was, and a smile then came across his face. My gut reaction and the adrenaline flowing through my body made me do the one and only thing I could do. 'Oh hell!' I blurted as I got to my feet as quickly as I could, and pushed the man out of my way. It wasn't going to do any good, though, as two more suits drug me to the ground as soon as I turned to start running. 'Come on now Stevie, it's been a while, and you already want to leave? I thought we were friends.' The man that I had pushed down said this to me as he got up off the ground. 'I didn't want to hurt you, really I didn't. I was actually hoping that little insane asylum trip would have done you some good. Evidentially you aren't going to make this easy on yourself, so you're going to have to go through this the hard way. Boys, tie him up. Hands behind his back, and his feet too.' He finished as he tossed them the rope to do it with. I struggled a little bit, but I was smart enough to know it wasn't going to do me any good. After they got my hands tied behind my back, I just gave up and let them tie my feet up. They stood me up, and I seen Mr. Sarcasm snooping around in the coat closet I had been hiding in. 'Hey, what's this?' He asked as he pulled the replica PTC Global Title belt out of the closet I had been dragging around lately. I mean, I wondered where that had come from. I just shook my head at him, and I seen a big smile come across his face. 'None of your business, so just put it down, I'll be coming back for it later.' I said, trying to act unafraid. I didn't want to show any weakness to these guys, because had I remembered right, they would tear you apart even more if you showed a weakness. I then glared at the guy though, waiting for him to drop it. He walked over to me and put the belt over my left shoulder. 'Seems Stevie here has made something of himself. I'm sure boss will be happy to hear it.' I held back my laughter as he actually thought the thing was real. I was sure it was made bad enough to not look real, but then again, maybe these guys weren't as smart as I thought they used to be. Age must be getting to them or something. 'Alright, let's get this sucker outside.' He said as he walked by the two, now holding me by my arms. They turned me around, and drug my by my arms, letting my feet drag the ground, through the lobby of the hotel I had found to hid in. Instead of going out the front though, they walked through the lobby, and made a sharp turn leading down a narrow hallway that smelled like it was leading towards a kitchen. 'I told him to meet us back here in a bit, so we'll go out this way.' The two grunts nodded to the guy as they continued to drag me, through the kitchen now, which eventually lead to exiting out through a metal door into an alleyway. The man who I had pushed earlier then pointed, and the guys continued to drag me. We came to a dead end in the alley, however, and they all stopped. 'This will work for now. Set him down boys.' Set me down they did, as they threw me to the ground that is. My hands being tied behind my back, though, didn't give me anything to brace with, and my head was of the first parts of my body to hit the ground, besides my knees. The replica title belt had flew off my shoulder as I hit the ground, and one of the guys who had yet to speak went and picked it back up. I winced in pain as I sat up, though, and I could feel the blood start to trickle down my forehead. 'What do you want with me? I've done nothing to you.' I was only stalling, trying to weasel my way out when I knew it wasn't going to work. The one who seemed to be in charge for now just laughed and I wondered if the other two faces were frozen as they remained the same. 'Stevie old boy, you are as stubborn as your parents were, you know that right. It didn't help them any either. So why don't you just answer me a few questions, and we won't have to worry about this going any further than it has to.' His smile then turned back to the bland look of his natural frown. We had a stare down for a few seconds as I refused to say yes to this henchman. I slowly just shook my head. He then nodded to the two other guys who had moved behind me. I seen the replica title belt fly through the air to more important of the three, and it caught my attention instead of bracing myself for a knee to the back of the head from one of the two. As I fell forward, back onto my head, I propped myself up with my forehead and knees. I caught a little bit, and became really dizzy. 'Now that we understand each other, where did you hide it? Don't get smart with me either; I'm tired of wasting my time with you. I could have been doing bigger and better things for the past month or so instead of chasing a peon such as yourself around. Now where the hell is it?' He asked, and I just remained propped up on my forehead and knees. I said nothing. He, along with the other two, pulled out their guns. I could tell as I heard the clicking of them putting the clips in, and the snapping in of the silencers. I remained unafraid on the outside, but on the inside my heart was beating harder than it had in a long time. 'Don't mess with us Lee, we want to get out of here as much as you do. Now let me ask you one more time...' He paused there as he tossed the replica belt to my side. '...for the sake of your career and your life, where the hell did you stash that briefcase?!' I coughed a little more, this time some blood came up, and I brought myself to just my knees, still holding the blood in my mouth. I looked the guy in the eyes, and then spit the blood at his feet. 'Wouldn't you all like to know? Thing is, I'll be dead before anyone finds out where I put that, and when I'm dead, then I guess everyone is shit out of luck for it anyway. Just give up, because I never will.' I then let out a short sigh as I braced for impact. 'Should we just ice him?' One of the other two finally said something, and the leader of the pact just shook his head before letting out a high pitched whistle. I squinted a little as the high pitched whistled echoed in my ears wrong, hurting a little. I let out a sigh of relief as I was waiting to get shot. A little further back up the alley, though, some headlights came on, and a black car strolled down to us. The guy driving then got out, and walked up to the main suit, for now anyway. 'We whacking this guy or what?' He asked as he looked over all three of the other suits. He shook his head as he snapped his fingers. I questioned to myself why he did it, but then the handle of a gun came down on top of my head, almost knocking me clean out, instead if just dazed me quite a bit, and took me flat to the ground. My vision was blurred, but I could see my replica title belt being picked up as I felt myself being picked up as well. The rest was all fuzzy too, but I do know I was tossed into the backseat of the car, the belt being tossed into my face shortly after, and the four suits got into the car as well. I just closed my eyes as to hopefully not be bothered for the length of the car ride. I did hear a little bit more before I passed out though. 'Where to then?' I was pretty sure it was the driver asking. There was a short pause as the engine in the car was started, and I felt it start to move. 'Home, I suppose. Boss isn’t going to be too happy though.' I heard a couple of grumbles right after, and as I waited to pass out, I thought about where "home" might be for them these days. I was sure it wasn't where it used to be though. The scene fades to black. To be continued...
