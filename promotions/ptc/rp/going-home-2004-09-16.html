Going home

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 The figures filled the room, moving around from small gathering to small gathering like leaves blown on the Autumn wind, no meaning, no reason to their chosen path apart from the assurance that they would be seen to be being seen, that they would mingle with the right people, and that all the jaw-achingly false smiles … the empty platitudes … and the sweat soaked handshakes … would somehow be worthwhile. Almost as if a swarm of locusts had entered the brightly lit lounge … decorated in a minimalist style as only the noveau rich can, with a plethora of plain white surfaces broken up occasionally by a single chrome ornament with a shape that would give Rosarch nightmares or a mirror hung at a deliberately oblique angle; the trite but oh so expected black leather chairs and sofa taking up pride of place in the centre of the room whose only saving grace was the two walls made of glass that brought the star filled night into the void that was this soulless space … a chatter and hum filled the air as small talk babbled forth from the macabre social dancers, all of whom were busy in ensuring that they were the centre of attention … that they were in the spotlight … that they were noticed. All except one. All except for the calm in the centre of the storm … all except for the oasis of peace in the battle for dominance … all except for the silence in the bedlam. All except for the one man who, in a room filled with faces, appeared to be alone in a crowd. All except for Jay Phoenix who almost blended into the couch on which he sat as the tailored form of the Black Armani suit he wore became his camouflage … his shield from the masses of falsely smiling people who circled around the room … circled around his safe haven … like sharks smelling blood. With his long auburn hair tightly tied back into a long plait that fell over his left shoulder to cascade down across his chest almost to his belt line, Phoenix’s face was exposed to view as he stared down at the white envelope that he held in both hands. His fore-fingers and thumbs balanced the envelope between them as he stared at it, the intensity of his gaze … the eyes piercingly bright jade … seeming almost enough to cause the paper to spontaneously combust as frown lines marred the surface of his otherwise perfect and bronzed skin. With a small movement of his hands Phoenix caused the envelope to spin around on the pivot point that his hands formed; the rectangular white envelope briefly becoming a blurred sphere. As it came again to a stop Phoenix sighed, his gaze not moving from the paper held by him. “So, enjoying yourself Jay?” came a happy sounding voice as a figure flopped down beside Phoenix, a large smile on his face. “No” came the reply, with no emotion evident in the tone, as Phoenix slowly looked up and stared … or almost glared … into the blue eyes of his best friend. Rick James lolled in the chair, his gaze a little unsteady thanks to the effects of the socialising that had taken up the two hours of the party so far, as his smiled faltered a little at the unexpected response. Pulling himself straight, resting his elbows on his knees and his head in his hands, he grinned again, winking at Phoenix. “Ah ... you nearly had me there I thought for a second that you were serious” laughed Rick, tapping the side of his nose as he shared the ‘joke’ with his friend. “I am serious Rick, I mean why are we even here tonight?” replied Phoenix, his tone this time leaving no room for error that his words were exactly what he meant … no humour evident at all. Rubbing his hand through his shaggy blond hair, his tanned face looking like a youthful surfer instead of the twenty-seven year old journalist that he was, Rick realised that there was no joke being played on him and leant forward slightly as he took a deep breath. Looking around the room at the people who, obliviously, still kept up the never-ending chase of popularity, Rick gave a small shake of his head. “You know why we are here Jay, these are the movers and shakers in the industry who have come here to Arizona just for you … the people who have the power, influence and ability to make or break a career. Since you retired from wrestling the offers have been coming in for you to endorse products or move into acting”, Rick started slowly, then pointed over at a obese and sweating man in an ill-fighting blue suit, his thinning hair swept over his shining cranium, “I mean like Carl Johnson over there, he has more money in movies than probably anyone and he LIKES you Jay … he could be your ticket into the big time!” A small smile played over Phoenix’s face at the obvious enthusiasm in Rick’s voice, but it slipped away as his gaze followed his friends pointing finger and fell upon the countenance of the movie mogul. “He is also someone that started off in exploitation and porn movies though Rick” came the soft reply, “ … so do I really want to build my ‘career’ on the backs of the people he has used?” Rick stared into the profile of his friend, noticing the intensity in his expression and reached out to lay a reassuring hand on his shoulder as he started to reply, only to be cut off as Phoenix continued to speak without looking around, his gaze simply moving from the figure of Johnson to the night sky outside. “I know that you meant well by bringing me here Rick, and yes I realise that the people in this room can do wonders for me … for my career … but I still don’t know WHY we are here; or more to the point why I am here I suppose” began Phoenix, his voice soft, his tone uncertain. “These people don’t want to help me, they are not really interested in who I am or what I want, they only want to get what they can” he continued, “… Hollywood may be in the City of Angels, but there sure as Hell ain’t no actual angels here … it is everyone for themselves.” “… but I thought that this was what you wanted Jay, that you wanted me to help you get a career in the movies now …” interjected Rick, sounding uncertain. Standing up suddenly Phoenix sighed as he placed the envelope into the inside pocket of his jacket, shaking his head as if to clear it as he forced a smile onto his face. Reaching down he patted his friend on the shoulder. “You know the only thing that I am sure I want right now Rick?” Phoenix asked with a small laugh. “A drink, the air-conditioning in this place is drying me out.” Rick shook his hand to signify that he didn’t want anything as he watched his friend walk over to the bar where a young man … probably in his early twenties at most … was standing waiting, dressed in black trousers and a double breasted white jacket. A false smile appeared on his face, one that didn’t reach his eyes, as he placed an empty glass on the bar in front of him expectantly. “Just a lemonade please” Phoenix said to the bartender, turning around to mouth the words, ‘are you sure’ to Rick as he mimed taking a drink with one hand. Rick shook his head negatively in reply. Upon hearing Phoenix’s request for a soft drink, the young bartender raised a single eyebrow and then grinned to himself as he lifted a bottle of vodka in one hand, a bottle of lemonade in the other, and as Phoenix’s back was turned to him he filled the glass up from both bottles … heavy on the alcohol. Placing the vodka back under the bar, the tender smiled at Phoenix as he turned back around to him. “Ice with that chief?” he asked as he gestured to the drink. A small look of distaste passed across Phoenix’s face as he heard the word ‘chief’, but he quickly covered it up with a shake of his head as he reached out and lifted the glass. “No thanks, just as it comes is fine” he replied, taking a large gulp as he started to turn to walk away; the liquid was forcibly expelled from his mouth, spat out in a spray across the room as Phoenix visibly choked, bending over and coughing as he reached out to lean on the bar. The sound of laughter rang out like bells pealing for a new bride as the coughing subsided, and as Phoenix slowly stood erect again he looked up into the grinning face of the barman his own eyes cold as his knuckles whitened, his fingers tightening around the glass. “What’s the matter chief, that bad medicine a little too much for you?” the bartender mocked, laughing at his own joke. Wiping his free hand across his mouth slowly, Phoenix’s eyes didn’t leave those of the bartended as he brought the glass up to his face sniffing at the liquid. The corners of his eyes tightened as his face took on an expression of distaste at the aroma. “I thought that the lemonade was a joke you know, a bit too girly for a big man like you … but maybe fire water is too much for you too handle, ey chief?” laughed the bartender as he reached out to pass a cloth to Phoenix. Reaching out his free hand … slowly and surely, his gaze still firmly locked on the eyes of the bartender … to slowly enfold the cloth, and the hand of the bartender, Phoenix started to smile himself though not a hint of humour lit up his eyes; cold fury, intense anger, and something more … something guarded … shone out from the so called mirrors of the soul. The bartender suddenly seemed to intuit that while Phoenix may be smiling things were no longer funny and started to pull back, only to find that his hand was tightly locked in the grasp of the slightly bigger man. With obvious effort the bartender tried to wrench his hand away, but this only caused Phoenix’s smile to increase as without any effort on his part he kept his hold on the young man. “Listen to me your stereotypical moron, in the first place I am NOT a chief … that was my father’s role … I am a shaman, and my name is NOT chief either, it is Jay Phoenix. Feel free to call me that if you have to call me anything at all!” snarled Phoenix, his voice harsh with barely constrained vitriol burning his mouth. The bartender opened his mouth, obviously about to reply, but with a barely noticeable twist of his wrist Phoenix elicited a gasp of pain from the young man. “I am not finished so don’t even THINK about interrupting me or your sex life is going to suffer when I break every finger on your ‘girlfriend’ here … so listen carefully as it seems that you have some trouble hearing the words that I say” Phoenix continued with a small grin, “ … I asked you for a lemonade, not a vodka, not bad medicine, and not fire water. This is not because I am a girl, or a wuss, or anything else that your little immature brain can come up with … it is simply because it is my choice to make … and NOT your decision to make for me!” Twisting his hand further around the cloth … and the hand wrapped within it … Phoenix pulled the bartender closer to him until they were both leant across the bar from opposite sides, faces almost touching as Phoenix barely whispered to the now pale and shaking young man. “I used to face men twice your size each and every night … men who would gladly break me in two and cause me a World of pain and misery … and now I find myself here with you trying to think of a single reason why I shouldn’t teach YOU how your little racist and stereotypical life could be filled with that very same pain that was promised to me …” came the softly spoken threat. With a thunderous crack the tumbler in Phoenix’s hand suddenly exploded into shards of flying glass as his whitened knuckles finally put too much pressure onto it’s fragile surface and streams of liquid engulfed Phoenix, the bar, and the bartender himself, in mini-tidal waves. A stunned expression came over the bartenders face as he watched the rivulets of clear liquid suddenly become darker until they ran red down Phoenix’s arm and onto the marble floor; with a look of confusion replacing the anger, the adrenaline rush making comprehension slow to arrive, Phoenix stared at his blood soaked hand. “God … Jay, are you ok man?” came the panicked query from Rick who was suddenly at his friend’s side. The bartender forgotten now Phoenix let go of his grip and the young man scuttled away with relief obvious on his face. Rick reached out and took the cloth that hung limply from his friend’s hand and slowly took Phoenix’s other hand … now as red as the stereotypical skin that formed the basis of the bartender’s jibes … gently in his own, opening it up to see the damage that had been done. “… oh Jesus … this is bad Jay, this is bad … we need to get you to a hospital right away” Rick murmured more to himself than to Phoenix as he eyes played across the gaping tear in the palm of his friend’s hand, and the small shards of glass that were still embedded therein. Gently wrapping the cloth around the palm, and then closing the hand around it to hold it in place, Rick took Phoenix by the arm and guided him to the doorway, the stares and open mouths of shock of the party goers completely ignored. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The antiseptic smell of hospitals always made his nose itch, as well as ‘tickling’ a primeval part of his mind that he didn’t even think had a name. It brought feelings of claustrophobia to a man who loved to explore forgotten caves, it brought feelings of anxiety to a man that was used to competing in front of thousands of people, and it made a man in his twenty-eighth year of life feel small and insecure; a child again. Dealing with the only part of the problem he could, Jay Phoenix scratched his nose. With a small sigh he surreptitiously glanced at his boyhood friend, Rick James, who sat in the seat beside him, his eyes half closed … hooded like an owl … as he leant back with his legs outstretched. “I am really sorry about this Rick, I now that it is not how you planned to spend your Friday night” Phoenix apologised, his voice soft. Without moving, without opening his eyes, Rick smiled. “I know Jay, and like I have told you the other sixteen times that you have apologised tonight there is no need … honestly” Rick replied “Well, as long as you know … it was a stupid thing to do and now we are wasting our lives away in this white-washed Hello hole … what time is it anyway?” continued Phoenix. “It is only ten minutes since the last time that you asked Jay, and YES it was a pretty stupid thing to do, which is why I would love to know what you were thinking, I mean c’mon that kid may have been an idiot but I have seen you walk away from worse insults than he managed” Rick queried as he opened his eyes and stared at his friends shoulders, hunched over slightly as he cradled one hand in the other … the cloth wrapped around it streaked with crimson. Sighing loudly, Phoenix got to his feet and started pacing up and down in a ten foot pattern … walking from the seats where Rick remained to a nearby drinks dispenser, the familiar Coca Cola logo flashing red on and off, and then back again. “I don’t know Rick, I really don’t know. The last few weeks … months I suppose … I have been getting more and more irritated about the smallest of things, I have been feeling almost trapped even though we both know that right now I can do whatever I want … go wherever I want to … I don’t have any deadlines, any places to be at all. That bartender caught me at a bad time I suppose, and hit a nerve that I wasn’t ready to deal with … all that stuff about my heritage, sometimes it still hits me that people see me as different … don’t expect you to understand though …” Phoenix spoke quickly between paces, not looking directly at Rick, who suddenly got to his feet and put a hand out to Phoenix’s chest, stopping him dead in his tracks. “Hang on a minute … you say that I wouldn’t understand what it is like to be different Jay … have you forgotten just exactly who it is that you are speaking to?” Rick said with anger evident in his voice. With a look of shock playing across his face, Phoenix stared into the eyes of his friend. “Hey look, it is the ghost boy!” came the sarcastic shout from one of the group of five young boys as they made their way along in the hot afternoon sunshine. All of them appeared to be between ten and twelve years old and were dressed pretty similarly in blue jeans and t-shirts, and all have swarthy, tanned skin and dark hair ranging from just below the ears to shoulders in length. The object of their ire stands facing them, slightly smaller though probably about the same age and with features that could almost be a negative of the larger grouping. Light blond … almost white … hair is cropped quite short except for the bangs that hang down to his eyebrows, which are also so faint as to be almost invisible. Blue eyes, set in skin so pale as to be milky, are only just visible as he glances up towards the group, his face pensive and drawn before his gaze returns to his feet as he scuffs the ground below him nervously, his hands wrapped around three large books. “Hi there guys, what’s up” came the soft reply from the pale youth, as he quickly glanced at their faces and then back down to the ground. The five larger kids moved towards the paler boy, surrounding him as the jostled each other and laughed, waiting expectantly as the largest of the bunch … easily two to three inches taller than the rest … took the lead. Standing right in front of the paler boy, he poked him in the chest with one finger, grinning viciously. “Hey look at this, my finger isn’t going through him … he looks like a ghost, he talks like a ghost, but it doesn’t seem that he can do all the cool stuff that ghosts can. So tell me this ghost boy, why are you floating around here pretending to be a ghost?” the larger boy jibed, still poking at the smaller boy’s chest, actually pushing him back slightly. “Erm … I am … I mean … not … I …” stammered the smaller boy, his gaze staying firmly on the ground as he fought to keep his balance. With both hands the larger boy suddenly pushed the pale boy to the ground, laughing as the books went flying. Stepping forward he placed one foot on the boy’s chest, holding him on the ground as he spat down on his face. “You know what pale face, I don’t care if your father is the doctor for the reservation, all I see when I look at you is a stupid little ghost boy who one day soon will wake up to find himself scalped just as his ancestors were by my ancestors … what do you think about that?” growled the larger boy. Squirming to try to get to his feet, the smaller boy whimpered slightly as the pressure from the attacker’s foot pressed into his chest making it harder to breathe. Realising that he couldn’t get out he suddenly went limp and stared up at the sky, tears welling up in his eyes. “Hey look at this, ghost boy is crying … what are you going to do next ghost boy, wet your pants?” cackled the larger boy, his friends joining in with the laughter as the small pale boy tried his best to vanish into the ground below him, and failed. “Let him go” came the soft voice from behind the larger attacker, the statement causing everyone to go silent as they stared behind them to glimpse the speaker. Standing staring at them stood a figure that was silhouetted by the afternoon Sun behind him, but as he stepped forward he came into view. “I said let him go” the speaker repeated as he folded his arms across his chest, allowing his biceps to bunch up. While looking no older than the rest of the youths there was a calm serenity in his composure … that coupled with the fact that he was already tightly defined and muscled … made him stand out. The auburn hair, flashing gold in places as the sunlight caught it combined with the piercing green eyes, also made him stand out as something different from his peers. “Stay out of this Jay, it doesn’t concern you” snarled the larger youth, not taking his foot of the pale boy below him “ … why don’t you just go back to learning all that mumbo jumbo that your grandfather teaches you and leave this ‘cowboy’ to us?” With a subtle sigh the youth now named as Jay stepped forward and with little effort grabbed the shoulder and arm of the larger boy, the obvious ring leader, and with a twist of his hips sent him flying up and over his shoulders to land heavily on his back, dust flying up in a halo around him as the air was expelled from his body. Continuing his momentum, and ignoring the now fallen attacker, Jay stepped into the now grouping of youths and spun a slow circuit staring intently into each of the boys who were stilled circled around the fallen boy. “Leave now” Jay stated, softly and with no overt threat in his voice, but the ire flashing in his eyes belayed that omission. Reacting as one, the heard mentality kicking in, the remaining boys backed off to stand behind their ring leader who had no got back to his feet and was moving towards the back of Jay. Without looking around, without seeming to even know that the attack was coming in, Jay reached down and held his hand out to the fallen boy. “Don’t do it Jesse, just go away and don’t embarrass yourself anymore” Jay said over his shoulder to the oncoming attacker, who suddenly looked less sure of himself and stepped backwards so that he was once again surrounded by his peers. “You know what, you are as bad as each other … one a ghost in our World and one only red on the outside but white inside … you are not worth it!” spat Jesse as he motioned for his friends to follow him as he stormed away. Looking up at the silhouetted face above him, the pale youth didn’t move seemingly still in a state of shock. Jay reached down and took his hand in his own, pulling him to his feet and smiling at him his face open and honest, concern evident in his eyes. “Hi there, my name is Jay … Jay Phoenix” he said reassuringly, as if trying to calm a skittish animal. “… Rick … my name is Rick” came the halting reply, followed by a shy smile. “Are you Jay Phoenix?” the voice interrupted his reverie and brought Jay back to his senses, where he realised that only a second had passed. Looking around he saw a young doctor walking towards him, a file in his hand. “Yes doctor that is me” Phoenix replied reaching out to shake his hand and then sheepishly pulling the blood soaked appendage back again. Grinning at Phoenix, the doctor looked down at the hand. “Well I don’t think I need to ask you what you are here for do I? Want to follow me and we will get you cleaned up and out of here as fast as I can” the doctor stated, indicating that Phoenix should follow him. “Sure thing doc … listen Rick, do you want to wait in the jeep and I will come out there and meet you when I am finished in here?” Phoenix asked his friend as he reached into his trouser pockets and threw a set of keys into the air. “No problems Jay, I could do with some air anyway to wake me up” replied his friend with a smile as he snapped the keys out of the air. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Phoenix flexed his hand, wincing a little as underneath the bandages that stitches pulled at the enflamed skin. Returning it to the steering wheel he stared out of the front window, watching as the white lines on the road flashed and then vanished as the headlights of his Renegade jeep played over them as he drove along the empty lane. The sky outside the vehicle was dark and tranquil, thousands upon thousands of stars filling the cloudless night with their illumination. Distant and alien they twinkled away in their endless canopy far above, the cat’s eyes on the road below a pale mimicry of their celestial brightness. Glancing at the clock on his dashboard that proclaimed that it was just after fifteen minutes past four o’clock in the morning, Phoenix grinned to himself as a small snore came from the seat beside him. Looking over he smile again as he looked at the face of his friend, Rick James, as his head lolled from side to side with the rhythm of the moving vehicle. When he had left the hospital, promising to follow the instructions of the young doctor who had removed the remnants of glass from his wound before cleaning, stitching and dressing it, Phoenix had discovered Rick fast asleep in the jeep; he hadn’t eve moved when the jeep had come to life, it’s engine roaring. Now for just over an hour Phoenix had been driving along a deserted highway, the sleeping form of his best friend the only company on the journey. … and just like every journey, this one had a destination, one that was seemingly reached as Phoenix pulled the jeep off the road and started to drive along what appeared to be little more than a dusty dirt track through the desert like environment. Going over a particularly rough piece of road, the jeep bounced and then lurched to one side eliciting a loud snort from Rick as his head bounced off the head rest, but even this was not enough to rouse him from his sleep. Giggling to himself, Phoenix pulled the jeep over and turned the engine off, plunging everything into darkness for a few seconds until his eyes acclimatized themselves to the night’s natural illumination. Exiting out of he jeep Phoenix gently closed the door as he walked around to the front of the vehicle and stepping onto the chrome bumper he climbed onto the mat black hood, smiling as he relished the warmth that still came from the engine. Sitting on the hood itself, Phoenix brought his keens up and wrapped his arms around his legs as he rested his chin on them. A sigh, relaxed and content, escaped Phoenix’s mouth … a small trail of air billowing up and out to dance it’s way to the sky as if climbing some crazy spiral staircase … as he looked around him and the dark and deserted landscape, the illumination only enough to allow him to see a few hundred feet either side. The natural sounds of the night were not enough to intrude, and here … in the middle of nowhere … Phoenix finally looked comfortable, finally looked content. He finally looked himself. Reaching into his jacket pocket, Phoenix removed the white envelope that had attracted so much of his attention earlier that night while at the party. Holding it in front of his face he stared intently at the two words on the front of it; so familiar, so insignificant. Two words. One name. His own … Jay Phoenix. The letter had arrived three days previously and in a haze of sleep he had picked it up from the floor by the door way and opened the seal gently without looking to see who had sent it. Pulling out the single page from inside Phoenix scanned the contents, his face suddenly becoming very awake as he took in the words contained therein. Leaning his back against the wall behind him, he had stared at the piece of paper fro what seemed ages, as the envelope hung loosely in his other hand, until without any expression he folded the paper once more and placed it back into the envelope, not thinking about it again… not looking at it … until this night. A faint nimbus of light suddenly started to enfold the envelope, almost as if it had ascended and earned a halo. Phoenix slowly looked up, over his knees and the envelope in his hands, and stared off into the distance, off into the horizon, as slowly … ever so slowly … the Sun began it’s expected rise into morning. Far off into the distance large buttes, finger like in their shape, rose up from the ground to reach up to the sky as if imploring some higher power for help. Jet black they were silhouetted as the Sun rose inch by inch behind them, it’s majestic corona sending red and yellow colours dancing across the desert floor, mingling with the shadows of the finger like appendages as they seem to crawl their way inexorably towards Phoenix who sat still … breathless … statue like … on the hood of the jeep, staring directly at the buttes and the Sun rising behind it. Unaware of the time passing, unaware of the envelope still in his hands … seemingly unaware of everything and anything around him …everything but this moment, but this time, but this single, solitary Sun rise, Phoenix sat and stared as night turned to day and the beams of the Sun and the shadows of the buttes reached out for him … closer and closer they came. Nothing else seemed to exist, Rick in the jeep was forgotten, the pain in his hand forgotten, the jibes of the bartender forgotten … the past itself … the failures … the isolation … the loneliness … all forgotten. …. And then like the mythical Phoenix itself rising from the ashes the Sun breached the buttes that blocked it from full view, and in all it’s glory it suddenly rose up, golden and unforgiving in it’s intensity to reach out it’s fire … to reach out it’s light … to reach out it’s touch … to reach out it’s heat … right into the face of Jay Phoenix himself, who for a split second was engulfed in the illumination as the light of the risen Sun swallowed everything. Temporarily blinded, Phoenix reached up and wiped the tears from his eyes not stopping the ones the fell down his cheeks … tears that could have been from the intensity of the light that he had stared into, or from something more … some deeper emotion … some cleansing power that the new day and the fire of the Sun had brought to him. With a sight to himself he slid down from the hood of the car, smiling to himself as he watched the life in the so-called lifeless desert move all around him … the birds that had taken flight, the insects that chirped away to themselves, the cacti that brought life saving moisture to the small lizard that basked in it’s shade. “Good morning Mother Earth … Father Sky … thank you for this day” he murmured to the air around him before sliding the envelope into his jacket again and hopping back into the jeep, turning the key and revving the engine back to life. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ “Hey Rick, do you want to wake up sometime today my friend?” laughed Phoenix softly as he shook his friend’s shoulder, trying to wake him up. One eye on Rick James’ face opened up blearily, wincing as the bright sunlight coming through the windscreen hit him full in the face. Wiping away a small trail of drool from one corner of his mouth, Rick looked around him, disorientated. “Where are we Jay … this isn’t the hospital?” he stammered to himself sleepily, rubbing at his neck as he winced, realising that he had slept with his head at an angle. “That was nearly six hours ago Rick, you have slept for over one hundred and fifty miles my friend” Phoenix replied with a grin as he pointed outside the passenger window. Rick’s gaze followed his friends hand and his face took on a look of shock as he realised that for miles around there was nothing but the red sand of the desert … apart from the one white-washed building that stood solitary and alone alongside the highway that appeared to go on forever. Opening his own door Phoenix stepped out of the vehicle before poking his head back inside and grinning across at the still dazed Rick. “C’mon Rick, you have to try this place it is like Heaven on Earth … my treat!” Phoenix laughed as he slammed the door and started walking over to the building as Rick slowly opened the door and stumbled out, following along behind him. Holding the door open for his friend Phoenix looked inside the building which appeared to be a deserted diner, the only sound coming from a TV set high up in the corner of one wall that was playing some country and western station to an empty audience. As Rick reached him Phoenix walked inside the diner, a large smile on his face. “Hey pretty lady, you have customers!” he shouted out to the empty room as Rick looked on in confusion. Clicking noises could suddenly be heard, like large metallic nails being tapped rhythmically against a chalk-board, getting louder as they got closer. “Is that you Jay … God it’s been an age since I have seen you baby!” came the shouted reply from behind the counter. A platinum blond lady of indistinguishable years … though it would be fair to say that middle-aged was probably a distant memory for her … came into view, a large and bright red smile upon her heavily made up face. While the makeup was heavy and the colours bright there was nothing fake about the emotion on her face, as she was obviously happy to see Jay Phoenix. Coming around the counter another of her attributes … two of them in actuality … came into prominence as she reached out and enveloped Phoenix in a massive hug, nearly smothering him with her body as she gave him a resounding kiss. Pushing him back and holding him at arms length, the woman took a long look at Phoenix before reaching out and squeezing his stomach. “You are not looking after yourself Jay, you are getting scrawny … sit you and your friend down and I will bring you both over some of my famous breakfast!” the woman almost shouted, her natural timbre being obviously one of many decibels. With a grin Phoenix nodded and pulled the stunned Rick over to a booth and sat down as the woman went back behind the counter and the sounds of pots and pans clanging suddenly competed with the TV to see which could be the de-facto jukebox for the place. “Who on Earth is that woman Jay?” Rick asked with a stunned voice. “That is Davina Rick, and you will not find a better or more caring person on this planet, I can promise you that, she has a heart of sold gold!” Phoenix replied with a smile, the as if realising something grinned to himself and shouted out over the counter “Hey Davina, can you bring my friend Rick here one of your special breakfast coffees please?” “Sure thing honey, coming right up came the muffled reply. “Heart of gold … how ca you tell Jay, I mean it is pretty well hidden under that body of hers … I mean if Bush want to find weapons of mass destruction he should ignore Iraq and just come in her!” Rick quipped. “Hey don’t diss Davina Rick, like I told you she is one special lady … almost like a mother to all the waifs and strays that she comes across, and she has treated me well for all the years that I have been travelling up and down” Phoenix pointed out, then started to grin himself “ … but yeah she sure is proud of those things, she says that the ‘Double D’s’ as she calls them are the best three thousand dollars she ever spent.” Both guys started to laugh but then stopped with just smiles on their faces as Davina came out with a glass of milk in one hand and a steaming mug of black coffee in the other. Placing the milk in front of Phoenix and the coffee in front of Rick she beamed down at them, patting Rick on the cheek, like a mother to a small child. “Nice to meet you Rick, Jay here has told me about you but it is nice to finally meet one of his friends … actually his only friend from the way he tells it” Davina roared in her delicate way. Blushing slightly, Phoenix took a sip of his milk as Rick smiled up at her. “Well we go way back Davina, in fact Jay’s grandfather even joked once if it wasn’t for the fact that I am white and he is Native that we could be twins … of course I am the better looking one” he quipped with a wink, that elicited an earth shattering laugh from Davina. “Well honey, it is good to know that Jay has someone like you around, I worry about this kid you know, he is always so introspective and alone all the time” she replied with obvious concern. “ … hey, is that bacon I can smell burning Davina?” Phoenix interjected with some embarrassment. Turning around and muttering to herself about breakfast being ruined, Davina walked back behind the counter and the sounds of cooking … or carnage … resumed. A pregnant silence grew between Phoenix and Rick, neither one speaking. Taking a sip of his coffee Rick suddenly went bug-eyed and started to slap himself on the chest as he reached out for a napkin as Phoenix’s eyes lit up in mirth, a large grin coming over his face. “What on Earth IS this Jay?” spluttered Rick as he stared in horror at the cup in front of him. “Hey that is Davina’s special wake up morning coffee … three parts coffee, one part lemon, and two parts bourbon” laughed Phoenix as he watched Rick’s eyes open in a mix of shock and wonder as he listened to the ingredients. “… well next time warn my Jay” Rick replied taking a small sip and then smiling as the heat of the drink hit his chest “… but I will give her this, it is a DAMN good cup of coffee!” Another silence resumed but this time it was more relaxed, and as both men sat back and enjoyed their drink they listened to the sounds of singing coming from the kitchen area, and another smile played across Phoenix’s face. “I don’t know what has changed Jay, but you seem different somehow from when we left the party” Rick stated gently, as if afraid to intrude or ruin the relaxed atmosphere. An answering smile however was his answer as Phoenix stared intently at his friend before looking out through the window and across the desert landscape. “What can I tell you Rick, it seems that since my birth I have not really fitted in anywhere. My grandfather said that I was born with a destiny, that I was the Eternal Flame reborn and had a fate to fulfil … my parents both died and left me feeling betrayed by everyone that had promised to look after me and love me … on the reservation I wasn’t ‘native’ enough for my friends being interested in archaeology and studying like my parents, but was ‘too’ native for them when I followed my grandfathers shamanistic roots.” started Phoenix as Rick sat back, his hands wrapped around the steaming cup of coffee, and listened intently not saying anything in case he stopped the rush of feelings and memories coming from his friend. “Then when we got to high-school I was too interested in studying to be a jock, and too good at sports to be a nerd … I turned down the chance to got to the Olympics so that I could wrestle … and that meant that my grandfather thought that I had betrayed him. All my life I have been on the outside looking in, all my life I have been on the outskirts of everything but never quite part of it.” Phoenix continued, his voice getting softer as he almost spoke to himself. “You know I remember my grandfather telling me the history of the People, how the white man came and took for themselves all that we had shared with nature … and then gave us back small parcels of land that we could live on. The GAVE us what was not theirs to take in the first place and thought that we would be grateful for it … … well I sometimes feel like that land Rick, I sometimes feel that I am something that has been taken and given away, not belonging to anyone in particular but claimed by all.” Phoenix drifted off, his words running dry as he reached into his pocket and pulled out the envelope that had so haunted his waking moments recently and slid it across the table towards Rick who picked it up and looked at Phoenix with a raised eyebrow as he slowly opened it. “My grandfather owns the land that our old reservation is on … I have three houses myself … and yet I don’t feel like I have a real home. Except for one place Rick … except for one thing. In the ring … wrestling” Phoenix said with a small smile. Unfolding the piece of paper, Rick read through the letter as his eyes opened wide. “This is from Argyle, the man that owns the PTC … he is personally asking you to take part on this years GTT!” Rick exploded, his voice raised “ … isn’t that the tournament that you nearly won a few years ago?” “Thanks for bringing THAT up Rick” Phoenix replied with a laugh “but yes it is the same tournament, this is the fourth instalment of it, and yes that is an invitation from Argyle to take part this year. Still holding onto the paper Rick stared at his friend. “… but you retired Jay, remember, you left that all behind you a few months ago” he pointed out gently. “ … and since that day I have been lost Rick, since that day I haven’t been myself … I haven’t slept properly, I haven’t known what to do, and then to top it all of tonight I nearly took a guys head of for stupid insults. Wrestling wasn’t just my career Rick, it was my home … my life … “ Phoenix interrupted before trailing off. Silence came between the pair again, the noise of the TV and of Davina’s culinary escapades in the kitchen fading away to nothing as the only thing of importance was the moment itself. … and then Rick laughed. “Well then my friend it is simple” he said with a smile at Phoenix’s shocked expression. Reaching into his jeans Rick pulled out his cell and looking down at the piece of paper he tapped in a number on it before passing the phone over to Jay. “Here you go Jay … time to go home … time to live again … time to be you” he said gently as he rested a hand on his friends shoulder before walking over to the counter and peering over to see what Davina was doing. Holding the phone up to his ear Phoenix listened to the ringing tone until a gruff voice on the other end answered. “Erm … hi there Argyle, it is Jay here … Jay Phoenix” he said into the phone, pulling it slightly away from his ear as the reply came back. “Sorry about that, no I didn’t realise that it was so late … ok … so early there or I wouldn’t have phoned. Your note DID say to phone you at anytime though as soon as I made up my mind” Phoenix stated into the receiver wincing slightly as he listened to the voice of the Australian owner of the World famous PTC. “Four hours ey? No I suppose that isn’t much sleep when you think about it so again I apologise Argyle, but you DID tell me to phone you … so I am phoning you!” Phoenix pointed out, smiling to himself as the irate tone on the other end of the voice changed. A confused look played across Phoenix’s face, his eyes clouding over as he listened to whatever was being said on the other end of the phone. “There is no need to threaten me Argyle, promising me desolation if I turn up at the GTT … I mean it was YOU that asked me to take part after all …” he started to say in a slightly irate tone then drifted off as Argyle obviously interrupted him; Phoenix’s cheeks flushing with embarrassment as the conversation continued. “Oh … Desolation is a wrestler is he … from Warr’s WWA? OK then that makes more sense I suppose, sorry for blowing up like that I have been feeling a little out of sorts recently, but I have a feeling that that is all about to change you know” Phoenix relied, a little chagrined to start with but finishing off with a positive smile. After a few more seconds of listening to Argyle speak, Phoenix nodded to himself. “Yeah that is exactly what I am saying Argyle … I am in … I am part of the GTT4 tournament” came the emphatic statement as Phoenix’s eyes flashed with their customary intensity … and intensity that had been missing for the last few months, and then suddenly his face creased up as laughter exploded from his mouth. “Yeah … OK Argyle … I love you too mate!” he laughed as he replied in a false Australian accent before closing the flip phone and leaning back into the chair, a long sigh escaping from him as his body visibly and obviously relaxed as Rick walked over towards him. “So … what was the decision Jay?” he questioned with a carefully neutral expression. Standing up Phoenix handed the phone back to Rick as he draped an arm comfortably around his friend’s shoulder, his own face carefree and smiling as he looked out of the window towards the horizon. “I am going home Rick … I am going home!”
