(IG 70) Wrestling Rehab

30 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Narcotics Anonymous “Hello, I am Jack Marley.” “Hi, hello, hey,” are all the numerous sayings that a group of people all sitting on hard metal chairs in a circle say. A woman stands up and claps her hands. Jack Marley dressed in ripped khakis and a 'Iron Zion' Bob Marley shirt nods. He looks like he has not slept in days and his eyes dart side to side looking at the other people in the room. He finally locks eyes with the woman and nods slowly. Jack sits patiently as he listens to the other patrons of the Narcotic Anonymous meeting speak about their plights with drugs. “I stole my little sisters jewelry for crystal-meth money.” “I cannot have sex unless I am on ecstasy.” “I was arrested for prostitution because I needed money for crack.” On and on it went. Jack Marley just sat idly by listening to all of the crazy stories the other addicts had. His eyes would widen after listening to everyone explain why they got themselves into rehab. It was all the same though, people hit rock bottom and realized it was time to change. Jack could only think to himself though, knowing that he had to make a change as well. It had finally come around to Jack though and he nervously tapped his fingers on his knee not knowing yet how to explain his situation. It was nothing like these other people and he did not feel comfortable telling them his drug of choice, of course that was for reasons beyond embarrassment. It was time--- “Jack, could you explain why you are here with us today?” The woman asks softly. “I—Uh...well you see, I have a problem,” he says quietly. She nods her head, “do not worry Jack. This is a safe place where you can share anything you want. Everyone here has admitted their addictions and are attempting to recover.” Jack looks around in an attempt to feel comfortable in his new surroundings and sighs. “I feel like I cannot live without getting high. It has been ten years since I remember being sober for more then two days.” “Are you high now, Jack?” Jack shrugs, “no, I have not gotten high in days and with that has brought insomnia. I sit at home and just stare at that TV unable to understand where the last ten years have gone. I eat ice cream and reminisce about the good days when getting high was more then just fun but also part of who I was.” The woman looks at Jack with her left eyebrow raising, “what do you mean?” “I use to get high every once in a while but then I met someone.” “Did this person help your addiction get worse?” “No. This man was powerful. This man was on the TV every week wrestling against numerous people. I happened to become an employee of his. I did not care about having to carry his bags or pull a prank against his rivals because it was amazing. I became more then a guy who liked to get high, I was on TV.” Jack said with a satisfied tone on his voice, obviously still wishing those days were still around “Having trouble getting over your past is understandable. You need to move on though, those days are gone and getting high to remember will only lead you to more heartbreak.” Jack breathes slowly and nods in agreement. “I wrestle daily with what could have been. One day he up and retired and left me and Rico, he was a fellow employee without any job. Sure he has kept us around doing yard work but I cannot rely on his generosity forever. I have no real identity other then a guy who liked to get high and act like a white Rastafarian.” “We are here to help you understand there is more then getting high.” Jack shakes his head, “that might be true—but that is who I have been for ten years. I use to use the slogan 'Legalize It,' I had a tag team with Rico where we saved Rockapella from Carmen Sandiego. Can you honestly say not being high can replace things that fun?” “Uh...well, Jack if you feel that way, why are you here?” Jack looks up at everyone and chuckles to himself, “Those memories are old. I want to make new ones that I can be proud of. I only feel like I have these great memories because I was high. I remember this one time Rico and I joined a tag team tourney and made it to finals. We were high the whole time and almost won the damn thing. I think we would have too if we were not told by our boss we were not cleared to wrestle. The night before we had a pipe accident where well...broken shards got stuck up Rico's butt, haha. ” The room began laughing. “That is not funny Jack and group. That is the dangers of being an addict. You think being high helped you do a lot of things but when you were at the cusp of something great that same drug was your downfall. You need to wrestle this addiction now, Jack.” “I once wrestled with a paraplegic.” “Do not make this a joke.” Jack shakes his head, “No for real. I was real high and he got the last cup of pudding in a cafeteria but I NEEDED it.” “Again, do you see what it has done to you?” Jack puts a serious look on his face but cannot keep it long, “sorry, ma'am it was funny. I can see how mean it was but back then I was the lackey for this crazy stable that did crazy evil things. Like steal candy from babies and glue staplers on desks. Man we were always high. We would all get in the locker room and do it up...BIG.” “You need to take this serious, Jack or you will find yourself the brunt of someone else's joke. Their joke though might be robbing you or stabbing you. Do you think that is funny?” “Of course not. Listen, every day I try to think of how I can make this addiction tap out. I just do not have the mental strength to put it in an arm bar or a figure four and make it give up,” Jack says as he pretends to put an arm bar on an invisible body. The woman rolls her eyes, “we are here to be your support in beating your addiction.” “I would really love to get it in an STF and watch its pipe base snap in half. I would then tell it to eat its vitamins and say its prays. I might even give it an elbow drop but not before I put some elbow pads on. I learned about glass shards,” Jack says his voice getting more exuberant. “Uh, okay...” Jack cuts her off, “I would take my addiction and DDT it inside the ring and pin it for the three count. I would be NA Champion of the WORLD.” Jack smiles and looks around the room at all the other people who are dumbfounded by his outburst of trying to out-wrestle (literally) his addiction. “Okay we need to move on, Jack,” the woman says beginning to get annoyed by his outlandish antics “Sounds good, mon.” “What?” “I said sounds good ma'am,” Jack smiles. *-*-* Jack Marley is leaving the NA meeting when one of the other people in the meeting comes out and walks up to him. The man is well dressed but that cannot hide his bloodshot eyes looking like he is fiending worse then Whitney Houston. “Hey.” Jack turns and looks at him, “hey mon, what's crackin?” The guys looks at him surprised by how different Jack is talking now. “I just wanted to tell you I understand completely what you are talking about. I use to get high to remember the past as well. I felt like it was who I was. I just wanted to let you know not to let the crack or heroin control you.” Jack puts his hand in his pocket and pulls out a pack of cigarettes. He puts one in his mouth and lights it quickly. He puffs on it quickly watching the cherry blossom in front of his new friend. He blows a smoke ring and laughs, “man I am not dumb enough to do those drugs, mon.” “What did you do?” Jack takes another drag and blows it towards the person, the person sniffs the air and then looks back at Jack, “WEED? Are you telling me you are addicted to weed? I am positive you have seen Half Baked, I am about to go Bob Saget on your lame ass.” Jack laughs, “dude...heh, I mean DUDE, shouldn't you be stopping me?” he coughs. The guys grits his teeth angrily and cocks back his fist and swings it at Jack. Jack dodges and then gives the addict an arm drag, “booya!” Jack yells. Jack begins jumping up and down his cigarette slash joint hanging from his mouth. “That is what I miss. I miss getting high and wrestling. I did it for fun, that is what my life is without.” *-*-* I am Jack Marley. I was famous at being a weed head lackey of a once prominent wrestler. Rico and I would smoke weed and then copyright infringe on High Times and host our own wrestling talk show, interestingly enough called...High Times. Heh. I was not always a lackey though, I once wrestled for a short lived federation called DWI. I watched as future greats like Eli Flair, Lawrence Stanley, Jack Assterson, Ellis Jackson, La Raza, Jack Leer, Mussolini, and Cameron Cruise wrestled their asses off. I need to be high to even look back at those times because each one of them became something in the wrestling business while I won less then ten matches in my career. I did nothing in DWI but get choke slammed by a guy almost two feet taller then me. You would think I would have learned something from guys of this caliber but no—I got stopped at the Airport trying to bring weed on the plane. I was put on suspension and when it was almost done the federation closed leaving me just one thing to do. Hah, no not smoke go back to the lackey business. I sit back in my beanbag chair with Rico and watch TV while smoking weed. I am pathetic I think to myself. Going to NA was not a joke, I honestly believed the weed was holding me back. The weed is what has made me sane after all this time. I really did not know what to do but now I understand. The Weedevilics are not making a comeback but 'Legalize It' Jack Marley is all about flying high around the ring once again. Sign your life insurance, fellas because I am as spotty as I am high. I wrestle because it is where my memories lie, it is what my life has been missing the past four years. I am Jack Marley, I am addicted to wrestling and I have been without it for far too long.
