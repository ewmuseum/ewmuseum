Final PTC Extreme Title Match RP Thread - post'em here

12 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Any day filled with Business and dreams! By Shaman He was dressed to the nines in a spectacular black and white tuxedo. He didn’t know how she managed to find shoes to match the tux but his new assistant was like a dream. He just hoped she had dressed to meet that dream tonight. Shaman’s normally, unruly, black hair managed to be groomed completely straight and rested against his back in a tight ponytail. His blue eyes sparkled with an intensity that would make the stars jealous. His wing-tipped black and white shoes sparkled from the fresh polishing that they received. His anxiety began to kick in as he adjusted the red-tipped black rose that had been delivered to him this morning by the floral company that she had recommended. “What if she doesn’t show? What if she isn’t dressed properly?” His mind kept playing over and over again. He sighed deeply and forced a smiled at the other thoughts that Kawanaha was eager to please. She never asked for anything for herself. She appeared unselfish that way. Her ideas seem to compliment everything he had suggested or in some cases made his ideas better. With her head for business, he noticed his profits soaring to higher levels then he and his clientèle seemed hadn’t seen before. Plus his cliental seemed more apt to spend money then before. He chuckled to himself as he thought about the positive comments that constantly flowed from those who frequented the Casino. Shaman peered in the mirror one more time, straightening his tie for what seemed to be the hundredth time. Even though this governor’s ball wasn’t any different from the several hundred he’s been to in the past, but this one…this one was going to be different. He seemed confident that Kawanaha would turn more then just heads. “Your just being silly. I’m taking the most beautiful assistant this state and probably the world has ever seen, and yet I’m sounding like a school-boy with his first crush.” He chided himself. His thoughts and he were startled by a soft knock on the door. He cleared his throat and walked the few steps to the door. He steadied his nerves as he quickly opened the door. He had to swallow hard as he was graced with an eye full of what only could be described as a fallen angel. Kawanaha had surely outdone herself this time. Before him, she stood, in a floor-length, white dress with a beaded and lace pattern that was meant to draw attention to her cleavage. A heart-shaped diamond necklace hung an in above the intricate bead work. Her hair was pulled-up and back in an elegant chiffon twist. Small white flowers stuck out of the twist giving her an almost virginal look. Her stylish hands and arms were sheltered with white cloves that came up to her elbows. Kawanaha’s feet scarcely poked out from the bottom of her dress, giving off the illusion of walking on air. Staring and admiring her Beauty Shaman swallows hard. He smiles at her and extends a hand towards her. "You are looking radiant." His eyes come to hers as he steps beside her offering her his arm. She takes it as he smiles and they begin to walk through the halls of his Casino. He reaches the elevator with her and looks at her once again. His eyes admiring her as they walked. The elevator door opens as he ushers her in first and then follows himself. "Thank you for accepting the job i gave you. I have an offer for you to take over the Atlantic city expansion and you receive 15 percent of the profits of that venture." Smiling he looked at her and smiled. "Think it over will you. Since all of this Casinos aspect is locked up." The elevator door opened as he gently ushered her forward and offered her his arm again. Walking with her to the limousine he stops and opens the door for her. He smiles to her as she climbs in and then he follows shutting the door behind himself. "Kawanaha why did you come for this job with me? I am nothing special and i am sure i am not the reason you came to work for me. I understand that you are a very successful woman in your own right as well. You could have had any job that you have wanted out there." He leans back smiling as he watches her. Thinking for a moment. "You don't have to answer that. I am sure you have had your reasons. I have very much enjoyed your implementations of business into my Casino. I had things running stronger, but now they are running stronger with no foreseeable downside." Running his hand across the window slightly he reaches down and grabs two wine glasses and pours two glasses of merlot. He slowly hands her one as he leans back and relaxes. "The Governor's ball this year is to help enable some of the Casino taxes to be waved this year in Nevada. The Casino gambling commission wants to rake in a little extra in cash. We are currently looking into expanding our Casino a bit with some different styled aspects, but i do not wish for it to be overwhelmed by the glitz of Vegas." Shaman looks to his left and motions towards the bright lights of Vegas as the drive down the strip. Kawanaha looks at him very intrigued in what he has to say. Shaman smiles as he knows she is listening to him fully. "My buying a Casino was actually by accident. I was coming into Vegas for an autograph signing session and noticed a building they were tearing down. It had alot of appeal and life to it. Especially for a building that was dead and dying or even gone. I wanted to do something more. Wrestling i had thought was coming to an end for me in the WWA. I wanted to make sure the rest of my life was comfortable. The World Wrestling Alliance didn't see me as a valuable asset even though i had years of dedicated service to them. So when my contract was up i came here and planned on living my life out." Taking a sip of the wine he swirls it around in the glass slowly. Turning his eyes back to the beautiful woman. "The the mail started coming. Lots and lots of fan mail. They wanted to see Shaman back in the ring. It seemed i made a bigger impact than i had thought. The next day i got a call from Steven Caldera owner of GCW. I returned to wrestling and made a huge impact. Now i am GCW's current Television Champion. It is another gold title to place around my waist. I was told by some people i would not survive anywhere else other than the WWA. I did just that. I survived and i own a Casino with just a little extra in my coffer." Shaman smiles as he watches the city slowly recede into the night as he watches the lights. His eyes study the city in the growing distance between them. He admires the work of man in a desert with nothing. Shaman drinks the merlot down and places the glass in the ice upside down. He looks over at Kawanaha. "The days have grown long, but my fire still burns. I have had many things in my life. This time for the first time i feel like i am in control of my destiny. Look at things this way. The PTC Extreme title is on the line and i have a shot at it. I do not think i am going to lay down and accept defeat. No way will i ever quit. I have a dream and have had the same dream since i was a child." Shaman gets quiet and runs his hand slowly over his chin and then looks at his tuxedo. "Albeit with alot less money and not as much fanfare, but that dream was to make a mark in the world. I have made that mark and now it is time to do something with it." Shaman quietly observes Kawahana as they ride towards the Governors mansion. “We don’t get much of a chance to talk when we’re at work, please feel free to tell me about yourself.” Kawanaha raised an eyebrow as she slammed back the rest of her merlot.* She hated drinking about as much as she hated talking about her past.* But since he wanted to know, she might as well tell the whole sordid story. “When I was sixteen my parents kicked myself and my sister out when they discovered us in an unwelcome position. So what’s a sixteen year old supposed to do? I set my goal to be the world’s greatest model. My sister and I set up a home studio and took a few head and body shots of me and sent them off for me to be discovered. About six months into my attempts to be a model a place called the House of King saw my sister’s work and hired me right away. I was a huge hit within just a few months. I had surpassed the ranks of their top models and was rapidly approaching supermodel status.” Kawanaha took a deep breath, her eyes watching his looking for a sign of disapproval. She secretly knew he wouldn’t disapprove because he valued her help to much. “As I climbed the ladder of success, I found that the relationship with my sister was satisfying but I needed more then just her. So I secretly started sleeping around with clients, photographers, anyone that I found attractive. Unfortunately, my sexual desires landed me in bed with the head of the rival company. That episode alone gave me a fat financial payoff that has kept me out of the working business for a while. My sister is living in a nice place with her share of the money,, she manages my money as well as the money of two of my models.” “So that’s my life and how I came to be where I am now.” Nodding as he considered all factors from this conversation. He smiled at her and nodded respectfully. “Your life seems to have been fraught with complications. Our evening should be pleasant to a point. I mean after all it is a big political brujah. Not everyone is comfortable with it and truthfully it is something i am not looking forward too. I guess sometimes things just need to be done.” Shaman looks out the window as headlights flash by and the rest of the night seems to flash by faster than even he realizes. *** Hours later at his own home. Sleeping*** Dreaming in his world of clouds and mist and mysteries. Shaman sits up on a white bed of cloud. He looks over as a naked Kawanaha is seen laying next to him. Gently he rubs his temple trying to figure out what is going on. “Did someone slip me some good drugs at the governors ball?” looking around three animals are seen walking through the mist towards him. Each animal seems to represent his natural totems for his beliefs and heritage. A large bear, a snow white wolf, and a large eagle all take up positions near one another and look at Shaman. Then again they seem to be looking through Shaman. Through him straight into his soul. The bear moves to one side of him and looks at him as it rises high onto its back legs towering over the large Shaman. The bear looks down at Shaman. It's eyes seemingly coming to life with an eternal burning fire. “You have been wandering for too long without our visit. Your destiny is not come to pass. You have many trials and revelations before you. You must find your inner strengths once again. You have become too complacent in the world around you. You are a warrior and a spiritual being. Your people have already given you their blessing. Now it is time to find your own blessing” The bear knocks Shaman down and drops down placing both paws upon his chest. Shaman grabs the paws and struggles mightily to lift the great bear off his chest. The bear looks at him with fire burning in its eyes even greater. “You would have fought me with every ounce you once were. Look at you know reduced to something less than what you were.” Shaman's eyes begin to glow with a fire of rage. He slowly begins to lift the bear as the bear roars at being lifted off of its targets chest. Shaman rolls to one side and gets to his feet and looks at the great bear. His eyes burning with anger and rage. “How dare Think i am less than what i see myself as.” Suddenly the crafty wolf dashes forward knocking Shaman's feet out from under him. Shaman looks around and slowly gets back to his feet as the wolf does it once again and Shaman this the ground hard. Shaman rolls to his knees and looks straight into the wolves silvery blue eyes. The wolf howls high into the air and then looks back at Shaman. “You keep falling, but will you get back up once again? You begin to feel weaker than you ever were before. Have you ever thought of quitting. It seems the common path of mortal men.” The crafty wolf dashes once again as Shaman was getting up. Shaman dodges this time grabbing the wolf by the scruff of the neck and slings him into the bear as they both stumble over each other as they both stop and sit side by side. Shaman looks at the wolf as his rage burns even greater. “I do not quit ever. Do you understand that? This is my fight and i will fight it on my terms. Where do you have any right to question my dedication at my own destiny and wants. I know what i want and will do. The fire still burns.” Shaman turns and looks at the bear and wolf as if daring them to come again. He has not even a moment to react as the giant eagle swoops in and grabs his shoulders and flies high into the air. The eagle soars higher and higher into the mist and clouds. The eagle turns its head slightly looking at him. “You are ready to see these images then. Your fire still burns. Now the question is will you be able to answer the questions you have now.” The eagle soars high and then releases Shaman as he begins to fall. Before him appears a younger version of himself. One from around the age of 19. His fall stops as he lands hard on a ground that he cannot see. He stands as the younger version of himself stands there looking at him. “Why did you leave behind everything that i am? Is this the path you chose for me? Leaving your dreams and aspects behind?” looking at his younger self he stares hard. He raises his arm and places it upon his younger selfs shoulder. “I am who i am and always will be. The paths i had chose when i was your age has become more and more true to what i am now. I fight the good fight and i fight for what i am. My dream is still here.” Shaman places a hand over his younger selfs heart. The younger self nods as he becomes older. The version of Shaman as he had first joined the WWA decked out in his native garb and war paint. Shaman backs away from this version of himself as he stares at him. The version of himself steps forward in a very aggressive manner. “Why do you hide what you are? You are rage and fire burning out o9f control. You are weak and nothing now. Why do you give into yourself more than ever before. No path or road had ever held you down.” Shaman grabs himself by the throat. He pulls him closer and looks deep in the eyes. As the darkness that was once his eyes burns the desire once lost in his earlier years. His former self smiles and begins to morph once again into the Shaman that had lost to Shadow now known as Lia Ambrosi in a previous GTT tournament. His last tournament in PTC if he recalled correctly. Shaman stares at himself as this version stands tall and proud even though he had lost. This version of himself looks back with the hard edge that shows a experienced warrior. “You know the path. You shall learn from your mistakes. Never ever forget the lessons you have once learned. Spread the pain and blood to others once again. Take pride in what you are and make them suffer. You are a proud warrior.” This last incantation of himself disappears revealing only a mirror image of himself as they both stare eye to eye unmoving. Shaman grabs his present self and pulls him into himself. He stands momentarily as the animal totems slowly walk forward and begin to run at him and disappear into a mist flowing into Shaman's being. Shaman stands and looks at both of his hands as everything becomes blurry. His eyes open in the darkness of the room. He stares at blank nothingness smelling the air and its familiarity. Realizing he is back in his own room he reaches over and turns on the lamp. “What an enlightening dream. Do i forget my past to simply realize i exist. No i don't think that is right. I think i will fight for everything i want. Crush the doubts in peoples minds. I have had enough of the nay sayers doubting me.” Shaman stands up and walks to the closet and reaches in pulling out a pair of pants and slips them on as he walks to the door opening it. He looks around to observe the room one more time. Turning he opens the door and exits walking down the dark hallway only lit by the moon. He flexes his muscles in an angry aspect as he approaches another door. He opens this door and enters slowly as he reaches to his side with his large shadowed arm. He slowly flips on a light revealing a gym full of free weights and workout machines. He walks to the wall and stares at the pictures of his PTC Extreme title opponents. He wants the Extreme title as it works into his very soul like a worm. He stares at each opponent and then stops as he stares straight at Nova. “You are the man who should suffer the most. You will never retain this title ever. I will stop you Nova and i will fight to get that little piece of tin. Nobody took my words seriously before. Now it is time to do this the right way. The right way is the painful way.” Shaman walks to the weight bench and sits down laying back and begins to push the bar up. His eyes focus on the bar as it rises up and then back down with aggressive motions. Shaman stares beyond the bar as he continues to work out aggressively. “This time everyone will begin to open their eyes. Regardless of the outcome i am going to make an impact and do something about this little world of inbreeding disbelievers.” Shaman growls slightly and places the bar back down upon the cross members. He stands slowly and walks to the bar and reaches over the top and grabs a bottle of tequila. He opens the bottle and begins to drink it down fast and heavy as he stops and looks at the bottle. “No pain is no gain for anyone. Pain is going to be sweeter than watching a candy making show.” Shaman reaches over the bar and grabs the phone. He dials it and grins as he waits and hears the rings. “Kawanaha book some plain tickets to Reno and Atlantic city. We are going to make some money.” Shaman grins as she is obviously speaking on the other end. “Yes i am serious. This is going to be an enjoyable venture for once. Time to use some of that capital i have been saving.” Shaman smiles and hangs up the phone and walks back to the wall. His mood going from smiling back to sadistically pleased. “First the WWA, Then GCW, and now finally PTC. It is time to shut up the critics and beat them down. Nobody will ever see the world through my eyes so i will show them why i am a dangerous individual.” Shaman turns and walks down the hall and shuts a door on the other end as he grins and turns and reaches up and smashes the light above his head as he disappears into the darkness. **Note the use of Kawahana was allowed by her handler and was described at how she would act.**
