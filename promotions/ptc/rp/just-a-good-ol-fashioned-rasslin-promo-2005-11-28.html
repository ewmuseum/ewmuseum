Just a good ol' fashioned rasslin' promo

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 Fade into a dark, dank, damp corner of the FZM Arena in Tokyo, Japan, home of New Frontier Wrestling's Eastern Conference. The camera slowly zooms into a figure, dressed in tattered jeans, shit-kicking boots, a sleeveless flannel vest, wrists taped up from halfway up his hand to halfway up his forearm. The figure lifts his head up slowly to greet the camera with his eyes. He looks at the camera intently, as the sliver of light that is streaming into bowels of the arena gives illumination to his face. It's Maggot, the One Man Mafia. MAGGOT: Well, here we is, on the eve of the fifth GTT, and I'm still tryin' to grapple wit' the fact that I's goin' up against a vampire in the first round a' this thing. I used to think that vampires wasn't real or none of that bullshit. I thought it was just somethin' they wrote books about and made movies. For a street hardened guy like myself, the thought of a guy who turns into a bat and sucks blood, that's a load of baloney. But lately, I've been seein' a lot of strange, strange shit in this business, stuff that defies even my own eyes. I seen talkin' apes wrestle against grown men. I seen a bisexual man become pregnant and then miscarry in the ring. Hell, I even seen a monkey wit' a stick come out and cause general destruction. So I'm willin' to believe that you'se a vampire, Virgil. And really, that don't bother me none. I mean, you can't be not like anyone else is when it comes to bein' in the ring and all that jazz. So what, you might sit up like the Undertaker from time to time. That just means I can kick you right back down again. I'm even down wit' this whole dining on blood thing. Hell, I'll happily oblige. You won't even have to sink yer fangs into my neck either. I'll happily bleed it out for you, that is, if you'se can land a good shot to my forehead. Just don't go turnin' into no bats now. I ain't like Ace Ventura and crap myself at the sight a' one, but I sure as hell ain't tryin' to catch no bat an' look like an idiot in front a' this new crowd. I just wanna look like the man I always been lookin' like. And that's the One Man Mafia, MBE's Last Hardcore Icon… Maggot. Maggot lowers his head back down as the camera zooms out and fades to the GTT5 logo.
