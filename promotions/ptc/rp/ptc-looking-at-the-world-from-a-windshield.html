Looking At The World From A Windshield It’s not easy controlling the world and everything in it. When you’re the son of man things are a bit clearer in the efforts of creating a New World Order. Most people think you need to control the man who is at the top creating a ripple effect down. But the ripple effect is wrong, and Hoyt Williams and the RISP understand you need to start at the bottom and work your way up. We join the great one in his headquarters in the plush Lincoln Park neighborhood at the RISP base in Chicago, Illinois. The front office looks like a common election headquarters with phone banks and cubicles. The back office is full of computers, charts, dry erase boards and other sources of gathering and storing information. A young man of about thirty-three is sitting at the main desk going over some information. He is Max Martin head of the RISP election influencing foundation. The EIF’s main concern is to influence and control the outcome of elections to favor RISP candidates, or to remove anti-RISP incumbents. He, who controls the offices, controls the world but that never worked for the mafia. He who controls the trash controls the world. Again stop thinking big it’s about starting at the bottom. Hoyt Williams dressed in a black Reebok jump suit and standing out with the PTC Extreme title around his waist walks into the office. Max Martin is busy, but everybody always has time for the boss when he walks into the room. Hoyt looks at a dry erase board like he understands what’s going on before extending his hand and giving the secret handshake to Max Martin. Watching all this is a reporter from an underground news rag who is shadowing Hoyt Williams for the day. He is going to print a special report on the man, myth, and legend that is Hoyt Williams. Hoyt introduces Ted Riley to Max Martin. Hoyt Williams: Mr. Martin, are we ready for the election this coming week? Max Martin: Of course! Hoyt Williams: Max this is Ted he is covering the RISP, and myself for a newspaper column of sorts. Ted feel free to ask us anything you want we’ll deny it all later. You dig? Ted Riley: I gotcha. Tell me a bit about this building and what you do here? Hoyt Williams: We have a bath room over there where people use it to excrement and urinate. As a matter of fact I think I’m going to take a Archibald right now. Max forces a polite laugh as he has heard that joke told over and over with different opponents placed in the punch line seemingly forever. Hoyt’s jokes are as stale as South Park’s yet nobody has the balls or as some would say the hairy Alcaeus to tell him. Max Martin: Over here we house 40 phones and an automated dialing system which scrabbles both the incoming and out going numbers. So the phone will call people at random and the display on their caller id bogus numbers. Ted Riley: Keeps things confidential? Max Martin: Right, some people refuse to pick up from blocked numbers, so this is how we get around it. Plus it gives us an advantage on phone votes where they scan the numbers and only allow certain amount of calls from one location. Ted Riley: What are these computers for? Ted Riley points over to some scary looking computers spitting out all kinds of date. They look like something straight out of the movie War Games and make noises like Star Trek’s control board. Max Martin: We monitor the internet and everything on it. Ted Riley: For pro, and anti RISP websites? Max Martin: Nope, we monitor everything. We control the internet and everything on it. We have the ability to know what everybody at anytime is looking at, or searching for. We can read your e-mails, follow your site activity, and control where and what you see. We know your credit card numbers and all your pertinent information. WE can seize your on-line payments making you miss paying your bills, we can turn you in to the authority’s for your sexual endeavors, we can even find out about your nerd activities like e-wrestling, governments sims, and letters to the editor. Ted Riley: Isn’t this an invasion of privacy? Max Martin: No, we are just looking out for you! God knows all, and sees all, and now so does his earthly counterparts. Look, if you don’t want us to know what you are doing, don’t use the internet. If you’re not using it, we can’t look at what you are doing. Ted Riley: Do you want people watching you? Max Martin: Nobody has a reason to watch me, and if they did they would find nothing because I’m a follower of Hoyt Williams and the RISP and you can’t get any purer then that. Ted Riley: What do you use this information for? Max Martin: To blackmail sinners, find out what people are thinking, and to maintain Hoyt’s Extreme Title. Really we’re no different then the Bush Administration or MTV. Ted Riley: Well I guess that’s a point. The two men walk around and discuss the equipment as busy workers do their jobs in the name of Hoyt. To be in control isn’t something somebody can do by their self. They need a team of responsible individuals who can maintain and do the grunt work that allows the person in charge the luxury to move forward and set up new opportunities. If George didn’t have Weeze he never would have moved on up to the East side. Hoyt Williams is a man of principle and being the son of God that almost goes with out saying. He helps homeless people by giving them work then paying them in prayer. He helps the blacks by banning their music, and fighting against drug dealers like KFC and Churches chicken who lace their chicken with crack to enslave the blacks. Some would say Hoyt Williams is the greatest human being a live today. They would be wrong and silly for saying that. Hoyt Williams is the greatest human being to ever live, dead or alive, including Jesus Christ, Johnny Carson, Caron Daley, and George Wendt. The great one exits the bathroom and rejoins the two men who are hovering over an electoral map of the south. Ted Riley: So what are your guy’s plans for the Illinois Gubernatorial race? Are you supporting Topinka or Blagojevich in the big race? The RISP insider and leader look at each other with a bit of confusion. Hoyt can’t hold a straight face and begins to laugh out loud. The reporter is now confused on how and why his question could bring a smile to anybody’s face. Hoyt Williams: We don’t waste our time on political elections, they mean nothing to anybody. Nobody likes politicians and what they do really means nothing. We go after what America cares about! We make the pulse of America beat. Hoyt glares at the reporter with a cold stare that tells him what Hoyt is saying if fact. The reporter is now drawn in wondering what all is going on at these headquarters, and what kinds of elections the RISP are rigging. Ted Riley: If we’re not talking about political elections, then what? Max Martin: Don’t you get it man?? The world revolves around the water cooler. Why do you think Hoyt Williams wrestles instead of running for congress? Kids watch wrestling not C-SPAN and that’s who we market to. We control what America and the rest of the world thinks and talks about. You see that Chinese guy over there? Hey Wu! A chainman with buck teeth and thick glasses wave yet never looks up from the computer screen he is enslaved to. Max Martin: He is the writer for the hit TV show The Soprano’s. He is under the direction of Hoyt Williams and the RISP and they single handily have made the mafia cool. Why? Because we control the real Italian mafia and this is a public relations campaign to give us better PR so we can be more visible in our actions. Ted Riley: A Chinaman writes the Soprano’s? Max Martin: Oh yea Wu even wrote the number one song in America “Cus you had a bad day”. I mean really that song sucks but we know how to work the charts. Wait till you see what we do with Lost when we revel its all an RISP plot. Ted Riley: What else have you done? Max Martin: Ever hear of Vin Diesel? Ted Riley: Yup. Max Martin: Another Wu creation. I mean if it wasn’t for the RISP, you think a muscle bound dolt who could barely talk could be a household name? I mean come on the fact the world is so blind to the obvious makes me laugh. Hoyt Williams: Nobody understands PR anymore and how to work the world. You work the bottom feeders because they get the work done, not the congressmen. We can stop the trucks, planes, taxi’s, grocers, and everything that makes us work as a nation. We are the pulse of America and what dreams are made of. Most of your top Hollywood stars, news makers, and newscasters are RISP members. Ted Riley: Wow. So what is up with all this stuff? What elections are you guys controlling? The two RISP leaders look at each other then smile as they point to the big board to the left which has a picture of a slightly overweight guy with gray hair. Ted Riley: Is that who I think it is? Hoyt Williams: The one and only. Ted Riley: Tyler Hicks is an RISP plant? You guys are rigging American Idol?? The two men have a proud smile on their face. Hoyt Williams: 17 billion viewers every week tune in and we make magic. You ever see Tyler dance? Ted Riley: Sure, he’s a lame white guy with no moves. Hoyt Williams: Oh no my friend, what he is doing is dancenotizing you. Ted Riley: Dancenotize? What the hell is that??? Hoyt Williams: Well the RISP is so advanced we have ways of brainwashing and controlling the American people. You ever study bees? Ted Riley: Like bumble bees? Not really. Hoyt Williams: You see kid, Bee’s dance to communicate. They do a little jig that tells the other bees where the honey is at. Our scientists have found ways to do the same thing through dance. We trigger the Bee in the brain that makes you do things. Ever eat at White Castle sober? Ted Riley: No. Hoyt Williams: Right, only after you have poisoned the brain with booze or dope. You see the devil engages in this practice as well. He even puts sparkle dust in the KFC chicken to control the blacks. Oh its true he’s playing these games as well we’re just countering. Alcohol works like a bee’s dance it makes you do things you never would do otherwise. You see Tyler’s dance like a Bee’s does the same thing. Ted Riley: So Tyler Hicks is an RISP insider? Max Martin: Are we going to split hairs over this? I mean come on look at the guy he’s 40 years old and looks like Newt Gingrich, as a matter of fact he is Newt’s kid. I mean Kelly Pickler was America’s sweetheart but our phone banks and techniques kicked her little ass right off the stage. Ted Riley: Why did you get rid of her? Max Martin: She was dumb, her father is a jail bird, and really she’s pure white trash. I mean this isn’t what the RISP is about. SOUL PATROL BABY SOUL PATROL!! Hoyt Williams: We gonna rock with The SOUL PATROL! The two men laugh as they do their impersonation of Tyler Hicks which comes off as a cross between Belushi’s Joe Cocker and Ray Charles’s Ray Charles. Hoyt Williams: Come on Ted we got to pick up my new baby’s mother and drop her off at the doctor. The two men exit the building into the warm sunshine, another product of the RISP. Its more God’s doing then any machines but since he is really the top guy in the organization you have to credit them with your warmth. How are two middle of the road wrestlers supposed to beat the guy who has a direct line to the guy who controls the sun? Hoyt and Ted find themselves in the back of a long limousine complete with a carrot juice bar. Hoyt Williams has a perfect body reflecting what the bible tells us about our body’s being the temple to the lord. It’s not something that was handed to Hoyt he spends lots of hours in the gym and drinks his daily glass of carrot juice. Carrot juice is the lord’s secret to the chosen people of Israel, also known as the Jews. Its not common knowledge as the Jews keep this gift from the lord very private as they don’t want the rest of the world to catch up to them in dollars. Its not that they are better at business then everybody else that makes them so rich, it’s just that the carrot juice allows them to work longer hours and concentrate more. Now that you know this clandestine information of the old world feel free to join in on the Hebrew revolution. It’s true the Jews are the chosen people. Even God makes mistakes, Moziltov! Hoyt sips on his carrot juice like its coffee as he types away a rp for the fantasy wrestling league he’s in. He doesn’t mention it to the reporter because Hoyt doesn’t want to ruin his reputation as ‘cool’. While typing and sipping he chats on the phone with one of his other projects. Hoyt Williams: Listen if the keyboardist is going to give us trouble we’ll have to have him replaced……right…GONE! Make it happen! Hoyt slams the limo phone down hard like John Holms on Jenna Jameson in the ‘what if’ scenario of porn. He pounds away on his keyboard before sending off a PM to Argyle about the poor treatment he has been receiving by the liberal, communist, homosexual, lame crowd at the CTP who refuse to bow down to the greatness of Hoyt Williams the e-wrestle. He argues he should be given super mod status, and a permanent title that he never has to defend yet is the top title in the federation. Ted Riley: Another band giving you trouble? Hoyt Williams: Yea, the gosh damned purple monkey who plays the keyboard in the Chucky Cheese’s restaurant band wants more money. Ted Riley: You mean those aren’t automatronic robots? Hoyt looks both ponderous and confused as he lets the words sink into his head. Hoyt Williams: Don’t be a moron everybody knows they are robots. I’m talking about the dude who voices the monkey robot. He’s asking for more money, like anybody really gives a shit who voices the gorilla in the Chucky Cheese’s band. Most of those kids only care about the crap pizza and how many coins they have left to play our machines. Ted Riley: It thinks its odd you guys also run the Chucky Cheese’s is that to brain wash more kids? Hoyt Williams: No it’s more so to weed out the crap kids. You see all the machines there are wager games where kids put coins in to try to win tickets. Understand we can see which kids are addicts by the ones who keep going back for the free sugar pop and drop their coins trying to get the tickets like little gamblers. Ted Riley: So Chuck Cheese’s is a kid’s casino? Hoyt Williams: Bingo. Did you know John McCain is a RISP robot and is automatronic? The reporter is in shock and is writing on his pad like a dying old maid writing her will. Ted Riley: You’re joking? Hoyt Williams: Yes I am. Both men laugh hard as Ted Riley falls for the line of bullshit. Hoyt finishes up his carrot juice and puts the cup in the cup holder. The two men sit in silence for a moment as Hoyt finishes up on his laptop and closes it gently. He puts it to the side as he watches the world fly past to the left. Watching the world from a car window is a great way to look at it. What you see is snippets of people’s dreams, and nothing of their pains. You see dream houses, family business, and people walking in the sun shine. You never see the roof rotting and the two mortgages due or the failing business that a family invested their entire life in. From a car window even the homeless seem happy, really life is always great from a distance. Wasn’t there a Bette Midler song called ‘From a Distance’ with the same concept?? I swear I’m not gay. Being Hoyt Williams you always see the world from a distance as you never really can grasp what it means being mortal when you are the son of God. It’s hard for him to understand why normal people continue in their pathetic lives with out PTC extreme titles, hot wives, or the ability to control American Idol. Ted Riley: What about presidential elections? You ever mess with those? Ted asks breaking the silence and the turning off the brain from getting lost in the passing world. Hoyt Williams: We make it so pathetic to vote it keeps the kids away, and we keep great Presidents like Bush in office. You see kids buy into the devils PR like network news, the Daily Show, or any Michael Moore production. Those people fire up susceptible kids who don’t see the big picture and understand politics into buying into crap. They want to crucify George Bush for doing good just like they did to Jesus. As a matter of fact the same people who hate George Bush hate Jesus and use the same PR tools to sell that jive. Ted Riley: So how do you combat that? Hoyt Williams: Fight lame fire with lame fire. Ever hear of that shine Puff Daddy? Ted Riley: You mean P-diddy? Hoyt Williams: Right. Wu created him some time ago to help brainwash the colored kids. But Mr. Combs become so lame even they turned on him. So what we did is create this campaign called ‘vote or die’ where P-diddy told kids it was cool to vote. Naturally they revolted because that’s what sheep kids do, and like planned they refused to vote. All that fire they had under their asses was zapped by a stupid slogan like ‘vote or die’. It’s as lame as an Alcaeus promo and has as much depth as MacGregor after a bottle of Jack Daniels giving a lecture to high school kids at the Dairy Queen. Ted Riley: He sure made me not want to vote! Hoyt Williams: Exactly it drains you of all want and need. It’s exhausting just thinking about that nincompoop, and his lame speaking style. When he talks about anything you just want to die. Forget voting you just want to put a gun to your head and blow your head away. Ted Riley: Much like listening to Alcaeus. Hoyt Williams: Exactly! He’s another mental zombie who zaps your soul with his boredom. Listen I have a lot of pull and power thanks to running the RISP. I’ll admit when I won the PTC title I had a map taking me straight to it, and some divine help along the way. Its one of the benefits of being the son of God, and one hell of a guy. Ted Riley: But Alcaeus? Hoyt Williams: How he got invited into this match is beyond me. The only thing I can figure is he’s blowing one of the nerds who run the event. I mean come on, I’m a world renowned athlete and political icon. This guy is named Alcaeus, that’s the best his parents could have done? Plus it’s wrestling if you don’t like your name you can change it!! Elwood Williams…..DUKE WILLIAMS! His name blew so he changed it. You see that means Alcaeus consciously accepted this stupid flucking name and runs with it. Ted Riley: Isn’t making fun of a guys name weak? Hoyt Williams: Listen all your lines can’t be golden. Ted Riley: What about Archibald MacGregor? Hoyt Williams: Who? Ted Riley: Archibald MacGregor! Hoyt Williams: Oh yea that guy. Listen just replay what I said about the other dude’s name then add a little more about being a nobody. Keith Richard’s falling out of a coconut tree is interesting and funny. Those two are not. Unless they have promos featuring the two of them climbing and falling out of coconut tree’s I think their screwed as far as fan recognition. As a matter of fact pull over so we can get some press if we can find a coconut tree. Ted Riley: Not going to find many here in Chicago. Hoyt Williams: I was being sarcastic! Come on kid get with me I’m going a mile a minute here my man. I’m fast like an alcoholic at the last call or horse at a race track. Ted Riley: What’s it like being as extreme as you are? Hoyt Williams: I tell you what; its not easy being the best the sport has to offer. You know they told me I’d never beat Killean and I did, they told me Youngblood was going to take my title and he didn’t. They told me Trashcan Man was the toughest but I beat him in a hard core match. Then Seymour Almasy shows up and I hand him his ass too. Rich Rollins is afraid of me or I’d have the global title. The one time he showed up in PRIME I rolled over him like a Cadillac on a kitty kat. That is why I represent PTC as the top champion. Forget the tournaments because they are meaningless. What matters is who holds the Extreme title and that is me. Rollins is fraud I’m a SAVIOR. Ted Riley: Where are we going again? Hoyt Williams: To pick up my baby’s mother. Ted Riley: Mother Mary? Hoyt Williams: Oh no, this is a side project to create a non-savior baby to handle all of Hoyt Juniors public relations and personal affairs. I’m giving him a hot little sister to be his wife. The man’s jaw drops at hearing this stunning revelation. To the common man this is disgusting yet in the biblical days the sister and brother hook up was as common as girl on girl is now. Think back fifty years when squares shuttered at the thought of two babes eating at the ‘Y’. Now it’s not cool to be a girl who doesn’t visit the man in the boat. Naw, are world isn’t in a moral decline. Ted Riley: What? Hoyt Williams: Oh yea you see you’ve been brainwashed to believe that sister on brother sex is wrong. You think they are going to make retarded babies and other crap like that. Come on does that make any sense? Animals have nepotism relationships all the time and when’s the last time you saw a retarded animal? Ted Riley: Well how do you tell? Hoyt Williams: Oh come on you know a retard when you see a retard. Ever meet Brandon Youngblood? You have never seen a retarded animal. Ted Riley: Well I guess I never have. Hoyt Williams: NO!! You have never seen a retard animal despite them bunny’s flucking like bunny’s to their sisters. The retarded thing doesn’t happen because it’s not true. It’s just bullshit and lies they the people, the MAN, the devil want you to believe. You see when siblings make love they create smarter more evolved baby’s. That’s why we are digressing as a species. When’s the last time you heard of siblings having sex? Ted Riley: Well I heard a lot of Alabama jokes. Hoyt Williams: Yup a tactic of Satan to make everything seem stupid. Honestly when’s the last time you came into contact with a sibling love couple? Ted Riley: Never. Hoyt Williams: When was the last time you saw the human species evolve? Ted Riley: Never. Hoyt Williams: I rest my case. Ted thinks for a minute about arguing the point but wisely decides against it as the limo begins to pull over. Ted looks over the landscape to spot a huge mansion where a pregnant blonde begins heading toward the limo. The door opens and there stands a pregnant Britney Spears. Ted Riley: Britney Spears? Britney Spears: Hi! How are you? Hoyt Williams: Oh?!? You know my baby’s mother. Ted Riley: That’s Britney Spears. Hoyt Williams: I know who she is I flucked her. How do you know her? Ted Riley: She’s a singer. Hoyt Williams: I know!! My wife and I picked her out of a magazine. How do you know her? Ted Riley: I don’t know her, I know of her. Britney Spears: Well it’s a pleasure to meet you Teddy. Ted Riley: Sure thing. Can I ask you some questions? Britney Spears: Sure. Ted Riley: Aren’t you married to some other guy? Britney Spears: Yup, but this is for the RISP. This is for God! It’s bigger then you or me…like Star Jones before the surgery. Ted Riley: Your husband doesn’t mind? Britney Spears: Did Joseph mind when Mary had Jesus? Ted Riley: Isn’t this baby going to be the lover of the new Jesus? Hoyt Williams: Just to clarify I’m the new Jesus. I’m the second coming. My son is the third and final coming. The baby Britney is carrying would be the keeper of the Savior. Somebody who has enough bible in them to understand the greatness that is Hoyt Junior. Ted Riley: The second coming of Jesus? Wouldn’t that still be Jesus? Hoyt Williams: No, I’m the second coming of Christ. You see the Christ surname is the title. I’m Hoyt Christ but I really don’t like flaunting so I kept my earth fathers last name Williams. I mean listen it’s a lot of pressure to go into a parent teacher conference as Hoyt Christ. It makes people uncomfortable. I’m all about comfort. Britney Spears: You are the king of comfort baby! Ted Riley: Why did you pick Britney? Hoyt Williams: She’s a loyal RISP solider created by the great Wu and the RISP machine. She is gorgeous and that’s what it’s all about. I mean I’m a Greek God, literally. Britney Spears: The hottest man I have ever been with. Hoyt Williams: And trust me, that is no small number. So with my perfect savior seamen I need perfect eggs. This young, horney, fertile vixen has great genes. I have super savior sperm that needs to be housed in greatness. Plus you know I have to do the deed to get the holy job done by violating her holiest of holes and I want a good looking dame to get me there. Britney Spears: AMEN!! I’m getting biblical wet just thinking about it. Ted Riley: So the two of you are going to create the perfect and most attractive girl created? Hoyt Williams: Now you’re getting it. Britney Spears: What’s going on with the temple? Hoyt Williams: A few problems with the lazy foreman Rick James. Britney Spears: I thought Rick James was dead? Hoyt Williams: He is. This is a different Rick James. We call him RJ he’s an inept foreman and I’m going to have to go down to the temple and get crap straightened out. It’s been months since the projects hasbeen stalled and he’s simply not getting the job done. Britney Spears: I hate incompetence. Hoyt Williams: So does God. Announcer: Join HOYT WILLIAMS next time as Ted Riley and Hoyt Williams head over to the construction site to get things straightened out. Plus the Government moves in on Hoyt Williams. Does he talk his way out of it or does our savior get shut down before even gets to the match. Also we learn more on the private life of the world most powerful man and your personal savior. =w=
