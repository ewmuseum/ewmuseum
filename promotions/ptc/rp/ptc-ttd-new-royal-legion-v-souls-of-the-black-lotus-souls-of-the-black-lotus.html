13 April 2004 GW’s – Souls of the Black Lotus Tag Title Division Week 2 RP Title: The Key To Winning –Trust- .::Trust, it is merely a word, but the word itself conveys a concept that is much more in depth. Many people can talk for days about what trust truly is, but to every man trust can be something different. It is a paradigm in it’s being, the word trust. The world would not function without trust. Every human being must be able to accept the fact that one day they will need another person to help them out. If not, then they were fools and are destined to fail. However, those with a willingness to embrace it, and to work together to achieve their goals, those people are the souls who are destined for great things. Take Bill Gates for example, would one man have been able to build an entire Fortune 500 company from the ground up? No, he trusted those he employed to do their jobs, and now because of this trust he has become one of the wealthiest men in the world::. The room is cold and dark, one step inside is sure to rob a weak and corrupt man of his soul. The room is completely dark and gloomy. Water drips down from its leaky plumbing and echoes throughout the entire room. In the middle are a small radio and a raggedy, wooden rocking chair. The radio is deadly silent, and the only sound louder then the leaking plumbing is coming from the creak of the old chair. The chair rocks back and forth, carried by the soft draft in the room. The radio begins to hum slightly out of nowhere, soon the sounds of humming are replaced by loud cries of static. The static is then replaced by the words as an unidentified broadcast begins to play… trust 1. Firm reliance on the integrity, ability, or character of a person or thing. 2. Something committed into the care of another; charge. The glory of friendship is not the outstretched hand, nor the kindly smile nor the joy of companionship; it is the spiritual inspiration that comes to one when they discover that someone else believes in them and is willing to trust them. - Ralph Waldo Emerson Ichiro looked slowly around the lonely apartment, it was early in the morning and most normal people of the city were long asleep. However, it seemed to him he could never sleep before a big match, like this one for PTC coming up. He and his brother’s first match had gone well and now a new test stood before them, a team unlike any they had faced before, XUW’s New Royal Legion. As he sat on his long leather couch, the fatigue grew in his body, but still he thought was not enough to put his eyes to rest. He continued watching the television set, Scarface starring Al Pacino was on. A classic movie, and one of great influence in the world, however Ichiro’s mind did not stay focused on the screen for long. His mind began to race with thoughts of his upcoming match in the PTC Tag Title Division. Unity had been their strength in the first match, he thought. He had told Jushin that it would be extremely important and it had helped them greatly, but now it seemed as if something else had fallen out of place. Something was wrong in the world of the Souls of the Black Lotus. Ichiro had a strong feeling that he knew what it was. Although, he and Jushin were able to combine for a great match, and were able to think alike and be on the same page, there was a slight pause, a miscommunication between the two. Jushin delayed greatly in all of their combined efforts, most of the double-team moves they used, Jushin hesitated on. Almost as if he did not trust Ichiro, that could be a huge problem and as the fatigue began to grow, this thought also weighed heavily on Ichiro’s mind. He had to empty his thoughts, he had to relax. He closed his eyes, and he allowed all thoughts to leave his head. He laid his head back on the arm rest and sprawled himself out, he kept his eyes shut for a few minutes, allowing his body to relax completely. He thought the sleep would come soon, he hoped at least, he would need at least a couple of hours of sleep before the match. He opened his eyes what seemed like five minutes later, he felt refreshed greatly. He looked at the television, as he had expected Scarface was still on. However, it seemed like the movie had fast-forwarded on its own, it was later in the film then when he had left it, much later. He shrugged his shoulders, what the hell was going on. He looked at his apartment, everything seemed to be alright, except for much of the posters on his walls, advertising old movies were different. They looked much younger, much more authentic. “What the hell?!” thought Ichiro. “Am I dreaming or something?” His thoughts were soon confirmed. Although he had managed to shake the cobwebs from his head and refocused on watching Scarface he soon realized that indeed he was dreaming. As he sat and watched the screen, the screen began to fill with static. “Not again,” he mumbled to himself. He got up and tried to adjust the television set. He fumbled with the cables at the back of the television set, he fiddled with a couple of the dials and finally the picture began to come in clearly again. However, now as he looked into the TV he did not see Scarface, or any movie for that matter. What he was looking at now was a scene from his past. He looked into the screen, and his mind began to race. His eyes filled with shock and bewilderment. He was in shock. He couldn’t move, couldn’t talk, he couldn’t do anything but sit there and stare into the screen as the scene from his past played on the screen. He watched the screen and remembered the scenery from his memory… The winds whispered through the cool, mountain night. The twilight was beginning to fade into darkness, but that did not at all hush or shade the bright lights of the Shao-Lin Temple. The light from the temple illuminates in a radiant glow through the open windows and the small cracks in between the wooden frame. Soft voices can be heard as the entire temple is in a very quiet and relaxed time. The majority of the population sit engulfed in deep thought and meditation. Throughout every room children can be seen in deep concentration, an elder monk sits at the front on a small pillow chair, as he seems to be leading the class in their meditation. The same scenario occurs in many other of the rooms, although as the age of the children learning seems to grow, so does the length of the monk's beards. The monk's beard is a sign of his wisdom and age. The longer the beard, the longer since he has achieved his one-ness and has become one of the elder monks. Those who instruct others in the ways of the Lotus. Then we hear a slight sound of grunting, panting and then a loud thud. The noises echo seemingly throughout the whole temple. However, this does not seem to affect the many in deep meditation, as their faces stay still as paintings. There is a sound of a struggle down the hallway, but again it does not seem to affect the many concentrating in meditation. Down the hallway there lies a large room. One of the only rooms in the temple with full lighting, besides simply candles and in scents. The room viewed from the outside seems large and very ancient looking. The walls are made of mere bamboo and paper. Through the paper we can see shadows moving, we can see what seems to be a fight going on inside. We see two men engaged in combat with some type of ancient swords. We continue the tour of the ancient temple, scanning room from room. In some rooms there are children practicing their martial arts, in other rooms children practicing scripture writing, in some of the rooms there is a group of men chatting and discussing events at the temple, in others men were sleeping and many other events taking place throughout the temple. Finally we come to a large staircase made of wood and bamboo. The stairway rises steeply and is dark at the top, no light comes from outside in the night air. The only thing that seems to be coming from outside at the top of the stairs is a cool mountain breeze blowing into the temple. Suddenly two boys came walking towards the staircase, one of the boys looked much smaller and much younger than the other boy. They were both dressed in their traditional Japanese Shao-Lin garments. Each of them looked nearly identical, both with dark brown eyes, both with black hair and both with a similar body structure. They stop at the stairway and make their way up the stairs. At the top they walk out and are standing atop a rooftop balcony. Ichiro sat bewildered still, not even on the couch anymore. He simply sat cross-legged on the floor in front of the television, leaned forward. He couldn’t believe it, this must be a dream, but everything seemed so real, it was impossible to explain. This must be important he thought, as he turned his attentions back to watching the happenings from his past on the television screen. Of course he knew what was going on, he and his brother were going to a form of confession. More like a test, a place where he and his brother would talk about their trials and tribulations. He did not however, know why they looked so young in this dream. They did not go to these confessions until they had returned from their father’s service, at ages 22 and 19 respectively. That’s when it clicked in, it was a dream. It wasn’t supposed to make sense. He smiled and just returned to watching the television, play a misinterpreted version of his past. The darkness of the night time sky, impaired their vision but not their sense of direction. They rounded the corner towards the mountain-overlook and could see a smile fire had been started in the small pit. They walked over and sat in the chairs prepared for them. They looked around but could see no one, anywhere. The shadows seemed to consume all of the roof but the fire. However, the two brothers had a feeling that all was not as it seems. They sensed another presence, lurking somewhere in the shadows. From the shadows behind their chairs, walked Master Tanaka. He smiled as he rested his hands on both of their shoulders. He laughed slightly at the jump and startle of the two brothers. ”Relax, it is merely me.” came the soft and comforting voice of their master. ‘'Are you ready to see the message that I have to teach you now, with the use of your past trials and tribulations?” “Yes master Tanaka,” replied Ichiro. “Yes, master,” answered Jushin slowly. They watched in silence as the monk sat on a large array of pillows on the other side of the fire, staring back at them. They tried to concentrate on their master, but the flicker of the flames kept drawing them in. Tanaka, smiled noticing their fixation on the flames. He grabbed the bag sitting next to the fire, and opened it up. Reaching his hand inside, he pulled out many black and purple flower petals. He smiled as he dropped them into the fire. The fire died for a few seconds, and the three sat in silence, as the dead of night consumed them. The two brothers sat focused on where the bright, crackling flames had lived not seconds before. Then in an eruption of smoke, and flame a purple and black fire sprouted from the ashes. The two brothers' eyes remained focused on the flames, their faces stern and fixated, their glare rock hard and impenetrable. “Honor is our vessel, strength is our fuel, knowledge is our bearing, and let our souls be our guides,” replied the two brothers in a monotone unison. Their eyes had not yet left the intense black flames. Ichiro nodded, and for the first time his eyes left the flames. He looked to his brother, who in turn returned the glance. They both wanted this knowledge more then anything. Pride and honor was what made them unique from all of the others, they both knew it. That is why this knowledge would be so important to them. “Now my students, you must learn about the strength of trusting in one another. To do this I will show you a journey of your future, a journey which you will encounter later on in your lives, a mission for your father.” Ichiro’s jaw dropped in shock, this was from his past, but nothing like this ever happened. That’s when it clicked in, they were young to show their innocence when learning the ways of the Lotus, the open mind to learn when they were young which had taught them so much. That is how he and his brother would have to learn how to trust each other again, by using their childhood teachings. The sirens were screaming overhead. All around them was yelling and shouting, everywhere there was loud noise. They looked behind them as they ran, over their shoulders they saw a group of about ten armed guards forming. The two brothers held their custom built pistols and kept running as fast as they could. Ichiro shook his head as he ran, why the fuck did Jushin have to be so grabby. If he would have just waited to check and see if the safe was armed then they could have avoided this whole situation, Ichiro did not let his mind slow him down, he continued making his way towards the stairwell as fast as he could. They were on the top floor of an executive office building in downtown Tokyo. The building was 18 stories tall and a slight mishap could lead to their dead bodies falling all 18 of them down to the ground. As Ichiro thought, he felt Jushin grab tightly on his arm. They burst through a door into an unlocked office, Jushin was panting heavily, but he ran to the window and looked outside. He ran back to Ichiro and grabbed him by the arm. “Let’s go,” Jushin said. “That is a fucking window, which will lead to a large 18 story fall, I don’t think I’m ready for that.” “Just trust me!” said Jushin as he began to tug at Ichiro’s jacket as he ran at the window. “That’s what you said last time, and now look where that got us..” thought Ichiro to himself. But he followed his little brothers lead, he had to admit, he didn’t have any bright ideas himself. Jushin shot the glass and it began to shatter as the two brothers jumped out the window feet first. They fell for several seconds and Ichiro began to get nervous, however it must have been three or four floors down when they landed on some window scaffolding. The two brothers landed shakily and there was a scary wobble to the scaffolding as they landed but they were safe. Better yet it had gone unnoticed by the security guards, and the guards were quickly running down the stairs, thinking they were following the two brothers down to the lobby. The brothers quickly shot out another pane of glass and jumped back inside the building, inside the office was empty, but papers lying everywhere showed that the office was being used. However, the building had been evacuated when the sirens went off. ---- 13 April 2004 The two brothers quickly walked to the door leading to the stairwell. They sat quietly and patiently on the other side of the thick, steel door. They waited for their chance to jump out and take care of the guards. With them out of the way, it was a safe sprint back to their car in the underground garage and out of this mess. Then they heard it, the stampede of footsteps as the guards quickly ran down the stairs. Each one yelling orders at one another, then the noise passed and the brothers made their move. They quickly ran into the stairwell and began shooting. Within ten seconds they had killed all of the guards, most of which before they knew that there was no one following them anymore. The brothers smiled at a job well done, and Ichiro patted his younger brother on the back. “You did well, I was wrong not to trust you brother,” said Ichiro. “It’s alright, let’s just get the fuck out of here now,” Jushin smirked as he finished the sentence. With that the two brothers ran down the stairs and made their way out of the building, and out of the huge mess. Ichiro saw it, he had trusted his brother then. Now he realized it, no matter what goes wrong he and Jushin could not give up their faith and trust in one another. Then seemingly the dream had done its purpose and everything began to return to normal. Ichiro’s apartment returned to normal and he began to wake up… Ichiro’s eyes opened up, he shook the cobwebs from the dream away from his mind. He looked at the television screen with a keen interest, it was about half hour from when he had fallen asleep. He smiled as he thought about trust, and the lesson he had just learned from his dream. He and Jushin would need a stronger trust in one another if they were going to pull off a victory in their upcoming match for the PTC Tag Title Division, and Ichiro had a feeling that after a slight talk and reminiscing a win would not be out of reach at all. His ears perked up to the screen as he heard the word trust coming from the screen. He looked down and saw a popular scene from Scarface, titled “Bathroom Philosophy.” .::The scene opens up to show Tony Montana sitting naked in his bathtub enjoying a Cuban cigar, a warm bubble bath and watching television. Behind him is his beautiful wife, calmly snorting lines of cocaine. And beside him sits his partner and best friend Manny. Manny sits on a chair watching television alongside Tony, he is dressed in a pair of khakis and a red golf shirt. Tony’s wife storms out of the room furious with Tony, which appears to be contagious as Manny and Tony start yelling at one another after Tony tells Manny to stay away from a drug deal that Manny himself set up::. Tony: “Come on Manny, you’re not a negotiator, you know that. You like the ladies more than you like money that’s your problem!” Manny: “What the fuck are you talking about, okay? I’m your partner, okay? If you’re not going to trust me with that kind of thing, who the fuck are you going to trust?” Tony: “Chu and your partner.” Manny: “Bullshit maing!” Tony: “And hey, don’t talk to me about trust. I don’t like it!” Manny: “You know what maing, you should listen to your wife she’s right, you are an asshole, maing!” .::With that Manny turns and starts to walk out the door::. Tony: “Come here, give me a kiss. Come here, come here. Hey! Hey! Fuck you maing! Who put this thing together?! Me, that’s who! Who do I trust? ME!!” .::Tony talks to himself, mumbling under his breath for a few more seconds then returns to watching the television::. The words ran through Ichiro’s head endlessly. Over and over they played, haunting him. It was a constant reminder to him of how important trust really was in a relationship like that. In a relationship like he and Jushin had, trust was of endless importance. This scene was a reminder of what could go wrong in a relationship when there is no trust. Without trusting one another in this PTC match, they would never succeed…
