Pain……..I Cannot Die

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 [Scene fades up as the two old men are sitting at the bar. They are both drinking some Sam Adams.] Man 1: Jeffrey, I tell you, that kid is like a phoenix. He can rise up from everything. Jeffrey: Marshall, he can't. There's no way. He fell off a scaffold and nearly got himself killed. Then the f***ing thing fell on him. Marshall: They said that he wasn't going to come back after being put on the shelf after a car accident. Jeffrey: Many wrestlers do that? Marshall: And nearly being killed at the hands of BloodAngel. Jeffrey: You make it sound like Lambert threw him from the top of the arena through that table. Marshall: Sam Wolack is going to come back from this. There is no doubt about it. Just as Vin Diesel says, "Pain never hurts, it only helps." Jeffrey: It was Ving Rhames to drunk f***, and it was "Pride only hurts, it never helps." Marshall: Yeah, whatever. Wolack don't have time to bleed in this anyway. Jeffrey: You fool, now you're quoting James Janos. Marshall: I thought that it was Jesse Ventura in that Commando movie. Jeffrey: You drunken idiot, James Janos is Jesse Ventura, and he wasn't in Commando. He was in Predator. See, you're alzheimers is starting to get to you. This is why you are wrong on whether or not Sam Wolack is going to do anything but lose at GTT5. Marshall: You don't know what you're talking about. Wolack can win. You saw that he's capable of doing anything to win. He let his own partner fall in a match to make sure that he could win it. Jeffrey: Yeah, whatever you say. Hey, barkeep, cut this motherf***er off. Marshall: Just remember that when Wolack is able to shock the world. After all, he's the man that I chose to win the Hurricane Cup. Jeffrey: Good call on that one, he lost. Marshall: You sure? Jeffrey: Yes, I'm sure. Marshall: Well, this time will be different. He's going to win this one. Jeffrey: Yeah, and next you're going to say that he's standing behind me. [Camera zooms out to see that Sam Wolack is indeed behind them, laughing.] Wolack: Grampa Marshall, I think that it's time for you to go home. [Jeffrey turns around to see that Wolack is there.] Wolack: Jeffrey, you should know better….after all, aren't you a Texas fan. Then you should know that you should Believe the Hype. [Scene fades to black.]
