IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

2 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 July 2007 Ian Nackedy of tag team Sound and the Fury The Start Of Something Stupid [Ian Nackedy walks into a random spot in space and time and looks at the camera that just so happens to be there.] Ian Nackedy: Hello, Jason Snow. I've never been in a PTC fed before, and there's a good chance that my tag team's application to PRIME won't be accepted because they have a policy against Magicians and Vampires and supernatural shit. [Ian puts his hand to his chin.] Ian: Maybe Global's hiring. Anyway. [Ian waves this idea off with his hand.] Ian: My point is, I've never been in a PTC fed before, but I hate you with every fiber of my being. I hate you more than you can possibly imagine. My hate for you, if judged in electric current, would keep the port city of Shreveport, Louisiana running halogen bulbs in every home for over forty-seven years. Yes, Mr. Snow, my hate is that strong. Why do I hate you, I'm sure you're asking? [I'm sure he's not. I'm sure he's somewhere doing something overly dramatic and suspenseful.] Ian: I hate you because you've got every young gun pointing their cameras at piles of steaming crap and sending it your -- and my -- way. I hate you because the smell of an open challenge for your coveted award has thousands of ugly, stupid nimcompoops thinking that this is On The Lot, shoving their terrible home movies into your face and hoping that they can have what you have. I hate you because, due to this stupid contest, I had to see James von Doom. Again. Do you understand what Hell is, Snow? Hell is seeing some guy who spends his time basing his terrible ideas and gimmicks off of wrestlers in some alternate universe that only he knows about. Hell is watching an entire federation actual give him notice, booing him and treating him like a roster member instead of a toothless disgusting harpy with cerebral palsy. Hell is seeing him losing and him pretending he enjoys it. Hell is James von Doom. Hell is this parallelism. And it's all your fault. [Ian Nackedy shakes his head, sighing.] Ian: I have been happily retired for three years, and now when the wrestling bug began biting again, I expected a different world. And for the most part I was right. There are none of the old shitheads who I knew, just new ones. [Ian whispers.] Ian: You're the second one, Jason. [He continues.] Ian: I expected a different world, and instead I see a different world WITH JAMES VON DOOM IN IT. It's like stepping in a pile of dog shit, going to sleep for eight hours, and waking up to see the dog shit hasn't dried from your shoe. Not that I've ever done that or anything. [Ian looks down at his shoes and nervously chuckles.] Ian: The point is, my eyes had just gotten over the horror that is that piece of shit and now I have to wash them out with sulfuric acid all over again. And I'm holding you personally responsible. Why? [Ian appears to think about this for a minute.] Ian: Not really sure about that one. I guess because you're king of the playground, and you're supposed to regulate who can and cannot use the monkey bars. You've got, what, two titles saying you're a badass? And you sit on top of the federation I may be calling my home as well? Yeah, you've got the power. Use it to get rid of this prick. If you don't, I will. [Here Ian's eyes begin to glaze over as he thinks of happy thoughts. Like boobies. And puppies. And a world without James von Doom.] Ian: See, if I beat you, and I can make the rules, and I can choose who I face...that kid is going to be the one held inside for recess, watching the blackboard as the other kids play. That's pretty much my entire motivation. Sad, isn't it? [Ian mock frowns.] Ian: Because I know that picking on James von Doom isn't new. In fact, making fun of von Doom is a lot like masturbation, when you think about it. Because when you're doing it, you're into it, and you have a lot of fun. But when you're finished, you feel embarrassed and maybe a little ashamed. But you can always find solace in the fact that everyone else is doing it. [Ian smiles as he makes a fake masturbation gesture, the one that usually is taken to mean "no big deal."] Ian: So I guess I should be telling you how you suck instead of telling you how von Doom sucks. Maybe later. But for now I'm just going to give everyone a look at what the Infinite Gauntlet world would look like with Ian Nackedy as the Champion. [Suddenly bright images flash across the screen. There are rainbows, and a bunny snuggling up to his mother, and a dog urinating on fire hydrants (hey, it's a happy image to me). Then, a newspaper article flies by saying "Ian Nackedy is Champion; crime down 48%". Louis Armstrong is singing "What a Wonderful World" in the background with much bravado and conviction. We fade on these words:] PAID FOR BY THE IAN NACKEDY FOR INFINITE GAUNTLET CHAMPION FOUNDATION, AND CO-FUNDED BY THE NATIONAL INSTITUTION OF KEEPING JAMES VON DOOM AWAY FROM ANYTHING THAT RESEMBLES HUMANITY ---- 3 July 2007 Ian Nackedy of the tag team Sound and the Fury Where's my Walker? [We begin this scene with a piece of paper being shoved directly in the lens of the camera. After a few seconds of adjusting, the name Ian Nackedy can be seen. It becomes obvious after a second that this paper is a certificate of birth.] Ian Nackedy: Dear James von Doom. [The camera scrolls down to different areas of the birth certificate. Ian was born in Greenland. Strange.] Ian: The man who described me as an old fart. Not just once, either. Several times. Even called me older than shit. [Scrolling over…the gender box is marked boy, surprise surprise. And then, focusing on the date of birth…] 9/16/1982 Ian: Yeah, Doomy, 24 is fucking ancient, you dumbass piece of losing-all-the-time-and-yet-never-realizing-that-the-reason-you-always-lose-is-because-you-don’t-belong-in-this-world-so-why-don’t-you-take-a-gun-and-blow-your-brains-out-SHIT. [Ian drops the certificate and pauses.] Ian: And yes, that was all one word. Moving on. I am not an old fart, just because when you remember me last I was immensely successful and well-known. That might be just because I’m that fucking good. I know it’s never a good thing to dwell on history, but when you knew me, von Doom, I was setting world records and losing once in 14 months. And I was 21 at the time. So pack your old shit in a suitcase and send it to Saskatchewan, because that’s not going to help you here. [Ian smiles.] Ian: But seriously, James, I wanted to apologize. I want to apologize for coming out and saying really unoriginal stuff. I want to apologize for not being inventive or original at all. I’ll never steal your gimmick again. [Ian chuckles.] Ian: What do I mean? Oh, yeah, the unoriginal stuff is your field, right? You come out here and say trite, basic, mundane and typical shit, and when someone tells you something you’ve heard already you blow the whistle. Well, before I close the books once and for all on this particular piece of dogshit, I want to state something for the record. Saying that James von Doom sucks might not be the most original thing in the world. Hell, I myself mentioned in last time, if you were paying any fucking attention. But no, saying that you suck won’t win me an award for innovativeness. But it doesn’t mean it’s not fucking true, either. [Ian shrugs.] Ian: You are worthless. Nothing. Everyone says you suck because you do! And it obviously must work, because every time someone says you suck, you lose a match horribly. I’m sensing a connection there, dipshit. You dissect every word I said and try to turn it into something stupid and goofy so it doesn’t look so bad. Hey, now you’re stealing MY gimmick. This from someone who calls himself Hollywood and tries to act like he’s too big for the wrestling business, I’m not so surprised. Well, James von Doom, I have said my piece, and have sufficiently buried you. I believe that anything further in your direction would be much like the whole horse-head scene in Godfather. At this point, when talking trash against someone like you, all it takes is one comment to acknowledge that you are an idiot and all of your points are invalid and wrong. [Ian clears his throat.] Ian: James von Doom, you’re an idiot, and all of your points are invalid and wrong. There, that should do it. Now on to people who aren’t as stupid and pathetic. Ian dusts his hands off, very final in a metaphoric sense.] Ian: Sonny Silver sends me a backhand volley, “Why is a tag wrestler going for a singles belt?” My answer, essentially, is the following: “For shits and giggles. what’s it to you, you narcissistic fuzzy-faced pigfucker?” I’m going for a singles title because I feel like it. Because I goddamn want to. I’m pretty sure motive isn’t something we should be attacking here, or else everyone is going to kind of look silly. “Wait, aren’t you a member of PRIME? Why would you want a belt that isn’t in your federation when there are plenty you could shoot for?” See my point? Motive isn’t too important. According to Hunchblack, nothing seems important. Well, at least that’s the message I got. He certainly didn’t seem to talk much. Oh well. [Ian sighs.] Ian: Jason Snow isn’t going to show, guys. Or if he is, he’s going to make sure he shows at the last possible moment to make us look silly and won’t give us a chance to sling back. That’s the way this works. What this is really all about is showing which of us can insult each other the best. And so far, none of us are really doing that good of a job. We all look like four year olds. Eh, I got no problem with that. Sonny Silver, you’re a poopiehead! So there! [Ian sticks out his tongue, making a raspberry noise. You know, the one that’s like “phhhbt”?] Ian: We all want to do this because we think it’ll get our name out there. We all want to prove we can fight. I want to prove that after sitting on my ass eating Edy’s slow-churned ice cream for the past three years I can actually run across a ring twice without having to stop to catch my breath. So really it comes down to who looks the coolest making other people look bad. [Ian whispers, pointing at himself.] Ian: That would be me. Go away, suckos, this is my title to win. [Ian walks off from a setting that I didn’t describe, but I promise wasn’t too important anyway.] ---- 5 July 2007 [Apparently we need more description. So here goes.] [Ian Nackedy has brown hair, is about six foot tall, and is currently BURNING EVERYONE WITH HIS AWESOME INSULTS.] Ian Nackedy: Tchu doesn't know what I look like, so I figured I'll stand here for a little bit longer and give you a better picture. I might not be short, or tall, and maybe mostly indistinguishable, but know this much: I don't have an ugly tat on my neck, because if I did I'D PROBABLY KILL MYSELF. I think the point you were trying to make was that I'm not known around these parts. And that's true. I'm a new person to this world, and even though they keep telling me my PRIME acceptance is in the mail... [Ian Nackedy winks.] Ian: I know that without any proof of how utterly awesome I am, no one's gonna believe what I am capable of doing. I get that. But this isn't about what I'm capable of. It's what you're not capable of. And that's looking like anything other than stupid poopheads. Or something like that. [Ian shrugs.] Ian: This is not a game like we used to play back in the NWC where we would trick people into falling over their words in hopes that it would somehow affect the outcome of our match. This is everyone just having a little fun, spitting insults and shit. The only real thing, though, IS YOU ALL SUCK AT HAVING FUN. Tchu has so much trouble even getting insults out with all the personality he's been showcasing. His few attempts at jokes are the only thing inhuman about him, and yes that was a crappy joke but I don't care because at least that way it's on par with everything everyone else has said. That was a long sentence, I feel like Will Faulkner. [Ian pauses for a second, thinking.] Ian: Hunchblack is so afraid of saying anything that would potentially hurt someone's feelings that he uses tactics like the alphabet and open letters to express his opinion. I mean, come on, man: Sonny Silver isn't just rude, he isn't just defiling the English language, he's a cum-guzzling megalomaniacal untalented guy-who-is-placed-in-any-particular-situation-for-the-sole-purpose-of-making-everyone-in-that-situation-look-like-fucking-GOLD. And that's the worst treatment you've given everyone else. Jackass? Come on, man, you're a pimp. You've got a baser vocabulary than that, I'm sure. Hall, Munsoon, and a few others all want to take their personal beef with Snow so much that they forget that the fucker hasn't even graced us with his presence yet and they're doing the equivalent of talking to a brick wall. Observe. [Ian Nackedy walks over to a nearby wall.] Ian: Hey, Wall. What's going on? [The Wall doesn't answer, most likely because he's either mad at Ian for not addressing him by his real name OR BECAUSE WALLS CAN'T FUCKING TALK.] Ian: I'm really pissed at you, Wall, always getting in my way when I want to use the door and I run into you instead. I'm going to make you feel my pain and kick your ass! What do you say in reply? [Again, there is silence.] Ian: What say you, Wall? [...] Ian: Exactly. Man, I sure showed that wall. That wall had nothing to say to me. I guess that means if, were the wall and I ever in a wrestling match, I would totally win. [An amusing side story is that in his first wrestling match ever in a small place called KNF, Ian Nackedy lost to a wall. He's blatantly ignoring it here.] Ian: So you should probably mention the guys who actually pose a bigger threat to you than our unseen champion. I know you don't think us important enough, in fact Mr. Easton Hall dug all the way in the bottom of the insult bag to call us "schmucks," but the way things are going, I'm not sure if the champ is going to retain. The odds are too much stacked against him. Other competitors: Phil Atken. Dear Phil, you should make a career out of snapp dialogue. Because it gets you laughs, it gets you chuckles, but unless I'm mistaken it hasn't done too well in getting you wrestling victories. So you and your agent can sign a deal and go Hollywood like Danny Ferguson, but you should probably cut the "guy who makes jokes before a wrestling match and is a joke during a wrestling match" part out of your resume, because you might not get hired. Who else? [Ian puts a hand to his chin in a thoughtful -- and mocking -- manner.] Ian: And I said I'm not getting into a word battle with von Doom -- it's honestly more painful than dry bones -- but I will say one thing. Before you say any sort of blanket statements like "you got carried in NWC," please remember that you are always wrong and everyone wants you to die. Good night, everyone. [Ian walks by the wall, punching it as he leaves the room.] Ian: Take that, fucking Wall.
