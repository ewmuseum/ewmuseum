A Dirty Tory!

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 The bells of London strike midnight. The gothic like atmosphere can mean only one thing, a late night meeting of the conservative party. For it is only that late at night, when the streets are silent, the press silenced that this conversation could take place. “Where going to lose Micky, how could you?” Domineeringly bickers a voice sounding very manly. “No we won’t my darling.” Michel Howard the daft party leader replies. “We have no chairman, we have no minister for South Downs, and you’re going to end up like Willy (William Hague) just another laughing stock.” “No I won’t my beautiful.” The gayness of the situation is enough to throw anyone off voting conservative but there where bigger shocks. Like an episode of 24 this scene was about to take a dramatic twist. “For I have a man like no other, I have a man who will silence all critics; I have a man who won’t mess a thing up! I have Mildred McMann.” Everything goes silent. Neither Michael nor his gay lover speaks until the gay lover steps forward out of the dark… “Oh Michel why do I always doubt you?” It’s no man… Michel Howard was meeting Anne Widdecombe. Today Mildred woke up remarkable happy with himself for a seat less Tory. It was because of a phone call the previous evening from the leader of the party Mr Howard. The call was inviting Mildred to become the new Conservative candidate for South Downs, a Tory safe seat and more importantly offering Mildred the role of party chairman. This job opportunity opened up after a bust up between Mr. Howard and previous chairman Mr. Flight had a bust up, over a gaffe that dug the conservative’s a deep hole. So being the party bitch that he was, Mildred jumped to the rescue of his leader and got ready to lunge himself down the throat of the voters of South Downs. Mildred couldn't wait to get out of bed for he was a silly man who believes his party has a genuine chance in the upcoming elections. He actually believes that his cause was just, that his leaders manifesto was the golden plan for his countries revival from disappear. Which infact it hadn't been in since his party where last hauled out of government. Mildred infact didn't really know anything about Politics. He was an accountant and a bad one at that, when he was 20 Mr. McMann senior introduced him into the party and being the hereditary joke it is, without an interest in politics, Mildred soon found himself's in amongst the thick of the party. Standing at 5 foot 11, weighing just 180 pounds, looking like a high school geek despite being in his mid thirties Mildred is hardly a man anyone who look to aspire to. Really when you look at Mildred you just want to thump the Muppet, all said and done he actually fits right into the party. Michael Howard would be proud of such a member, already catching up with him in the balding race. His pale complication representing the opaque policies his party presents the people of the United Kingdom. Going to the wardrobe Mildred searches long and hard for the appropriate suit to address the people of South Downs. All the suits are gray, all the suits are the same standard cut but still Mildred vigorously debates over which suit would throw the voters his way. Mildred being completely oblivious from politics doesn’t even no that he could run no campaign in South Downs and still walk away with a victory. Finally picking the suit to wear Mildred moves out of his room and out onto the landing. The landing is painting a thick blue with party pride. There are oak doors lining the well lit corridor. At the end of the corridor is a double oak door which Mildred is heading towards. Stopping halfway Mildred looks slightly over his right shoulder, he smiles gently and waves, the pompous git begins to recite from memory. “Vote Conservative!!!” He shouts. Mildred stares harder into the mirror. Like a bulldog he begins to build up with rage, he then starts to flex his muscles. “What ya… gonna do when…” In his gray suit Mildred flexes his muscles, replicating WWE legend Holk Hogan. “What are you doing chap?” Interrupts Mildred’s dad; speaking with a stereotypically Yankee idea of an English accent. 10.32 Ye Old Bingo Club, South Downs The air is misty, there a few pensioners ‘the gray voters’ scattered around the room puffing away on there cigarettes not giving two British shits about killing themselves. The gray voters start a slow very unenergetic clap as Mildred appears on the small bingo hall stage. Mildred holds his right hand up waving like the President of the United States of America, it takes seconds for the half hearted clap to die down to an awkward silence. In a world of his own he continues to wave. Stepping in front of the microphone Mildred pulls out what looks like a small scrap piece of paper from his suit jacket. The paper is infact a small section on toilet paper he had scribbled on at home. Mildred places the toilet paper on the bingo machine which is acting as an impromptu speech stand. “Why vote Conservative?” Mildred pauses for a moment almost falling into his own question. His brain looks like it’s working hard, he looks flustered, breaking his deep concentration a member of the audience shouts out. “Get on with it!!!” “What?’ Replies Mildred McMann in Stone Cold Steve Austin fashion. No one understands Mildred who continues to rant down the microphone “What” over and over again. “See all the people in this bingo hall, all the people in the constituency of South Downs are going to be voting Mildred McMann my bet is you’re voting for Sharon, eh?” The man in the crowd doesn’t even reply. “You red, you make me sick, voting for the new communist party didn’t Ramsey McDonald teach you anything?” The man stands up and turns his back on Mildred who seems to be ranting on stage in the style of a wrestling promo. “See what I mean ladies and gentlemen the reds are back, don’t vote Labour, vote blue, vote old school, vote for the future, vote for Thatcher, vote for Willy and most of all vote for Michael!!!” Mildred jumps off of the stage. He runs through the crowd tapping the gray voters hand as if he had just won a wrestling match and was heading to the back. He held his hand his in the air as he left the room before running off into his taxi. 14:14 The chip shop, South Downs Mildred exits his personal blue taxi and enters the only chip shop in the small area of South Downs. The chip shop smells of fat, the workers are either plastered in spots or in need of the highest quality of wrinkle cream. On the right hand side of the room hangs a dominating poster with big black writing covering an election box; the writing reads “Vote here… May 5th 2005” Mildred is followed into the chip shop by a series of local camera crews and newspaper reporters. Moving up to the counter Mildred nearly collides with a poor little girl trying to get her chips but the girl begrudgingly moves out of Mildred’s path. Leaning against the counter Mildred faces the camera crew. Pushing back his hair he gives one of the female reporters a cheeky wink. “Mr McMann why have you chosen this fish and chip shop as the place to address the press?” asks the reporter. “Well Miss Bell it is at the chip shop that we can truly get to the bottom of public beliefs, it is at the chip shop we can clearly access the needs of the customer, the customer being the residents of the local community.” The answer seems flawless but the camera can easily catch Mildred reading these off of his palm. The answer written in the smallest handwriting. “Oi!!! Are you buying?” screams an old voice from behind the counter Mildred is propped up against. Blanking the voice out, assuming it is aimed at somebody else Mildred asks the media for another question. “Mildred how do you plan to help small businesses, such as this local fish and chip shop benefit if you win the vote of the people of South Downs?” Pausing Mildred ponders on the question and to stall the reporters nods. Then he gradually lowers his left hand while highering his right hand. He does this on the sly hoping that nobody notices. “Milly, Milly McMann… Buy something or move!!!” blasts the battle axe voice from behind the counter. “Well Mr. Wallace the answer to that question is simple yet effective…” The counter that Mildred is propped up against is opened and before he can finish his sentence Mildred falls backwards. The fall is completely unexpected as Mildred hits his head hard. The old lady stands above Mildred and starts to beat him back to life with her tea towel. “And you can all get out to if your not buying.” She informs the camera screws and reporters. “Nan.” Whines Mildred getting up. “What do you think your doing, you hooligan, bringing all your friends down to my shop. You think because your Nanny runs this place she’s going to let you and your posse hangs her?” “Nan, they where the press.” “I don’t care if they where members of the royal family, get them out of here, and sling your hook to.” Meanwhile somewhere in the depths of the Conservative party headquarters… “Mr. Howard… I think I have something you should watch?” Michael Howard turns around in his big black leather chair and switches on the television via a remote control shaped like Margaret Thatcher. Mildred is on the news. The idiot Michael had put in control of South Downs a seat it was said couldn’t be lost is doing his best unintentionally to loss the seat. “We are going to have to sort this out.” “Today Mildred McMann…” the television reporter continues. “John, get me the problem solver.” 20.17 Back at the bingo hall, with local wrestling action!!! “Ladies and gentlemen, children of all ages, before we continue this evenings wrestling a man by the name of Mildred McMann has requested the opportunity to speak to you.” The ring announcer steps back as Mildred comes out through the curtain mimicking the style of a wrestler. The crowd this time is packed to the rafters unlike this morning; there are at least 200 people in attendance. Mildred dives under the bottom rope. Mildred slowly gets up trying not to harm his back, the crowd boo as he reaches out for the microphone off of the ring announcer. “Good evening.” A small child in the crowd boo’s at the first word. The child’s actions seem to give his parents the giggles. His dad has a Labour rosette on his coat. Pissed off, Mildred walks over to there side of the ring. Mildred gets out of the ring and walks adjacent to the little boy. “So, your dad think’s you should boo me?” The little boy quivers at the lip and turns to his dad. His dad whispers something into his ear then the child turns back to Mildred, Mildred puts the microphone towards the child. “Yes. Daddy say’s your going to lose the election because you’re a milk snatcher.” Looking back at the child Mildred is furious. Moving his eyes off the child, Mildred concentrates on the dad almost burning a hole through the Labour supporter. Mildred thinks back to the morning in the same club, remembering the man, they where both same person. “It’s you again.” Remarks Mildred with a snooty middle class tone clearly enraged that this man had returned for round 2. “Who do you think you are the people of South Downs, look at this man. This type of man stands in the way of true justice, true peace and the British people reaping the benefits of there land.” The man stands. Stopping Mildred the man grabs the microphone. Stunned Mildred being a cowardly man steps back. “Do you know who I am?” Mildred shakes his head negatively. “How long you lived in South Downs Mr McMann?” The man puts the microphone under Mildred’s mouth. “I don’t.” “Hmmm… Well that will explain why you can’t address the mayor properly then and why you insult him at any given opportunity.” Shocked Mildred almost faints, his eyes almost pop out of his head, the crowd all instantly starts up a massive boo as Mildred falls back onto the ring canvas propping himself up against the ropes. ---------------------------------------------------------- What will Mildred do?
