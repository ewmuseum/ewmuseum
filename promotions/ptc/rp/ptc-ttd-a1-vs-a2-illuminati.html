I L L U M N A T I Jayson Kenny - Current mVw United States Champion Rhys CarteL - Number 2 Contender for mVw World Championship Some teams are just unfair I L L U M N A T I It's time for RESPECT ________________________________________________________ Revealed – Reveal \Re*veal"\, v. t. [imp. & p. p. Revealed; p. pr. & vb. n. Revealing.] [F. r['e]v['e]ler, L. revelare, revelatum, to unveil, reveal; pref. re- re- + velare to veil; fr. velum a veil. See Veil.] 1. To make known (that which has been concealed or kept secret); to unveil; to disclose; to show. Light was the wound, the prince's care unknown, She might not, would not, yet reveal her own. --Waller. 2. Specifically, to communicate (that which could not be known or discovered without divine or supernatural instruction or agency). Syn: To communicate; disclose; divulge; unveil; uncover; open; discover; impart; show. Usage: See Communicate. -- Reveal, Divulge. To reveal is literally to lift the veil, and thus make known what was previously concealed; to divulge is to scatter abroad among the people, or make publicly known. A mystery or hidden doctrine may be revealed; something long confined to the knowledge of a few is at length divulged. ``Time, which reveals all things, is itself not to be discovered.'' --Locke. ``A tragic history of facts divulged.'' --Wordsworth. ________________________________________________________ Running his hand over the back of his skull reminded him of the attack he had endured the week before. Two lumps, no…that’s three…how could he forget the attack? Three on one is bad odds, especially when you are the one. His memories raced, being beaten by Johnson, Thomas and a guy in a hood… he would later learn that the masked man’s name is Suicidal, not that it really matter…it was three on one no matter who was involved… Beaten like an unloved stepchild… He shook his head a little, pain shooting down the back of his neck. His arm swings back to allow his hand to caress the ache. This was getting out of hand. “I’m tired of this shit.” he mumbles to himself. Now he has a match with his newly formed tag tem. Shake it off, be a man…the words rolled through his mind like a scoreboard in a stadium. Now is the time to prove to the rest of the world that you are a Divinity. A godlike character, someone people would beg to be… He stood from the overstuffed chair in the corner of the messy room. Standing in a pair of gym shorts and a wife beater. The room feeling of a nice seventy degrees, not too hot…not too cold…just right. Damn Maid hasn’t been there in like a week…looking around his surroundings he realizes how much this hotel suite was bothering him. Glancing around the room that had become his home over the last month. His only solace is when his eyes met the United States title leaning against the back of the opposite oversized recliner. That image always has a way of putting a smile on his face. Taking a deep breath hie moves toward the door, twisting the knob he opens the door to the world. The remnant smell of eggs and bacon filled his nostrils. That is a smell that always lightens his day, it reminds him of waking up in his mothers house. Scanning the seating area… It is there that he sees… The man who before last Monday stayed in the shadows, only interacting with him over the phone… the man, the legend, the last remaining undefeated mVw Superstar…Rhys CarteL…his partner in what is going to take the world by storm… I L L U M I N A T I… No one was ready for what is about to hit them. “Welcome to the afternoon, Payne.” CarteL says to his new partner. “My name isn’t Payne anymore, man. You know this.” Kenny shot back. “You’ll always be Payne to me, man.” CarteL spews over his afternoon paper. “Where’s Jefferies?” Kenny asks, looking around for the eccentric lawyer. “He’s out doing his job right now.” CarteL says, not bothering to raise his eyes from the news. Kenny chuckles to himself, when did that lawyer ever work. Most of his time is spent with a different skank every night. That’s what he considered work…his train of though is broken by CarteL. “They are talking about us.” CarteL says to him in a sly tone. “Any good?” Kenny responds, moving toward the couch sitting in front of the fifty-inch plasma screen television…snapping the remote control up and pressing the power button. “Not really, most folks don’t even know who we are.” His fellow combatant says to him before taking a sip of his coffee. Swallowing hard he continues, “They say these two pud whackers, Invincibleness will handle us easily.” Pausing a moment to take in what he just heard, Kenny looks back over his shoulder saying, “Invincibleness…what the hell kind of name is that?” his disbelief in the comments were evident. He turned back to the television and began flipping around the channels. Landing on a replay of Unleashed I, the place of his finest moment…also where he was jumped for second time by two or more pussies. This forces him to run his hand over his shaven scalp, feeling the lumps…the constant reminder of the night. KNOCK, KNOCK The fist smacking the door breaks both of their concentration. CarteL goes back to his paper, Kenny slowly moves to the door. “Who is it?” Kenny says in a raised voice through the thick door. “It’s Jefferies, open the door.” The slimy lawyer says loud enough to be audible on Kenny’s side of the door. “Where’s your key card, fool?” Kenny says with a chuckle. “You guys took it away from me, remember?” The lawyer says, his voice increasing in volume. “Maybe that’s a sign, jerky.” Kenny says in an almost guffaw. “Quit being a dick and let me in.” Jefferies says, his voice taking almost a pleading turn. Kenny cranks the handle and the door slowly creaks open. Jefferies slides through holding his lucky briefcase, wearing a huge smile. “What the hell are you grinning about, chuckles?” Kenny asks him. “Oh nothing, just had a good day at the office.” Jefferies says, his smile getting wider as he speaks, “Especially good news for you.” The comment perks Kenny’s ears as he sits back into the couch. Jefferies moves around the room, setting his briefcase on the mini kitchen bar. He quickly grabs a couple of mini bottles of Jim Beam. Tossing one to Kenny, he cracks it and takes a shot. Kenny stares down at his bottle, moving his gaze back to the overtly happy lawyer. “What’s the deal, man?” Kenny asks his lawyer. Wiping his mouth, Jefferies takes a quick breath before sitting on the couch. Pausing a moment before beginning, Kenny’s blood begins to boil waiting on this information. “It goes like this…your ex-wife has decided to drop the assault charges on you.” His lawyer lets him in on, he continues “Given that you allow her to have what she wants from your estate.” The first part almost made him forgive her for initially accusing him of the assault, the second part brings him to the realization that the first part was just complete and utter bullshit. He stands, the bottle of whiskey harmlessly bounces off the carpeted floor. “Is this suppose to make me happy?” Kenny asks his lawyer. He slowly shakes his head, looking down at the floor. He locks his gaze back to his lawyer, the displeasure can be read across his face… “You said the charge was bullshit to begin with, and that I would be able to keep the things I wanted. Now you tell me that she’ll let the charge go…if I let her have anything she wants.” He says without emotion on his face, the anger burning inside. “What kind of slick lawyer are you, Cochrane? “You’re suppose to lie to them, not me…or do you always get this backward?” Kenny’s anger level begins to rise. He looks over at CarteL, who seems to be getting a lot of humor from something. “What’s so funny, man?” CarteL turns his attention away from his paper before responding, “What? Family circle” he says pointing down at the paper. Kenny just sits there for a moment, a little stunned. A little unsure, he looks down at his feet for a moment, before turning his gaze back toward CarteL, “You think the family circle is funny?” CarteL looks toward Kenny for a moment before responding, “Yeah…is there a problem with the family circle?” “Other then that being the worst cartoon ever, no.” Kenny says, the anger leaving him with a laugh. CarteL looks up at his friend as if he has just been slapped across the face. “Are you dissing my family circle?” He questions Kenny, who now has a dumbfounded look across his face. CarteL, feigning hurt, continues, “How can you diss such a family oriented cartoon, doesn’t it just take you back to your childhood?” Kenny, still not catching on to the rouse, replies, “Not my childhood, unless they are showing them being born in a hut with a dirt floor, growing up in a government built house, near a casino.” “Uh…you do know I was joking, right?” CarteL finally lets go on the truth, staring at the man who had just unveiled his true childhood. CarteL cracks a small smile as he continues, “Damn, chief, you seriously need to relax.” CarteL turns his attention to the lawyer who is trying his best to stay straight faced. “Damn, Jefferies you were suppose to get him laid, ease some of his tension.” ”I tried man, he always seems to find a problem with chicks.” The lawyer spouts off, pausing a moment, ”I would think he was gay, if he hadn’t slept with that one chick.” Kenny’s face goes blank, he just stares at the diminutive lawyer. The lawyer cracks an uncertain smile, a face you make if you’re afraid you might have stepped over the line. Kenny just shakes his head, chuckles and sits back against the couch. Leaning down to grab the little bottle of whiskey, he cracks the seal and downs it in one gulp. He starts laughing, a heavy belly laugh. As the laugh grows, the two other men in the room stare at the laughing man with concern across their faces. The laughter quickly switches off as Kenny grabs the remote control and turns the television on. “Think whatever you want, Jefferies.” Kenny says to his lawyer. He looks back to CarteL who is glancing over…he says, “As for you man, don’t worry about my tension…my tension earned me that United States belt and is gonna help us earn those PTC Tag Team Championships… some folks just deal with their shit differently.” He finishes turning his attention back to the television. He flips through the channels, landing on a sports show…the topic of discussion… Jayson Kenny and Rhys CarteL…the unlikely team. This forced another smile to cross his face…he flips to another wrestling oriented channel… The topic changed to the federation rankings… The mVw barely cracking the top ten on most lists… The smile disappears as quickly as it appeared. Replaced by a look of anger… How could people think so little of his fed, the fed he had given his heart to… the fed that is his home…these dipshits continue to overlook the mVw. The topic changes to the Primetime Central Tag Team Division…most of the PTC fans interviewed couldn’t even name the members of Illuminati. Even after the reporter supplied them with the names, the faces remained blank. DISRESPECT… This tournament has become for something bigger then just two shiny belts. The wrestling world thought very little of the mVw. This was time to earn the respect of fans everywhere. He could feel his stomach turn, the words coming from the television were causing the bile to build in the back of his throat. The quality of show the mVw has been putting on was falling on mostly deaf ears in the rest of the world. The great talent in the mVw is getting the blind eye... Is this because the mVw is relatively new to the PTC, or was this just flat out disrespect from them? The times are changing… The stage has been set… It is time to reveal to this judgmental world, the wrestling world’s best kept secret… I L L U M I N A T I You’ve been warned…
