Jesse Jamester sits up realizing it’s daytime. Almost 11 A.M to be exact. Jesse sits up brushing the hair out of his face to show his blue eyes. His goatee brown with black whiskers he rubs next as though a morning ritual. Blinking and rubbing his face he gets up and takes a seat in a chair. He ties his shoes and wipes off the lint from his shirt. Jesse goes to grab his journal but nothing is there. He looks up and sees that the journal is missing. Just at the same moment Jesse is startled as the door opens and Sam comes in. The big man tosses Jesse a wallet and inside is an ID card for Jesse to get back to the United States. “It’s time we got you back to the U.S. Jesse,” says Sam looking at Jesse. “Awesome…” replies Jesse. Jesse follows Sam out into the garage where they get into a black Jaguar. Jesse looks at the dashboard to see a nice CD player with amplitude, bass, and other options. The back has two huge stereos and they pound the car p in down with the hydraulics. Jesse is all pimping in the car as they pull out. He puts on a pair of silver glasses that have see through orange tinted lens. He smiles as the car peels out of the garage and drives down the dirt road. [Airport Arrival] Bladez and Steve rush through the gates grabbing their bags at the turmoil. The two are in a huge hurry almost forgetting what they brought. “What the heck are we doing?” asks Steve. “Were finding Jesse remember?” replies Bladez. “Oh yeah!” says Steve. “Yeah well were do we start Steve?” asks Bladez. “I’d say we start in the closest city,” replies Steve. “Are you a total idiot or what?” says Bladez. “What!?” replies Steve. “You are the one who sent him down here and now your clueless of whether he is here or not!” yells Bladez. “Hey you were the one who planned it all,” says Steve. “So, that doesn’t mean it’s my fault that you messed everything up!” yells Bladez again. “Okay let’s just go look for him,” says Steve. [Parking Lot] Jesse and Sam pull into the parking lot of the airport as Steve and Bladez leave it searching the west coast of Cuba. Jesse jumps out grabbing his bags and giving the sunglasses back. Him and Sam shake hands as Jesse heads up to the front doors for a ticket. He buys one for the flight back to America. Jesse realizes he forgot his journal and goes back looking but as he does he steps on something. Glancing down, he sees his brown journal beneath his foot. He picks it up wondering how it got there but more importantly how it’s in perfect shape. He opens up the book and reads on the page after his last entry: “Be all you can be… Don’t hold anything back, time will dwell upon the arrival of you and your glory,” your friend Sam.
