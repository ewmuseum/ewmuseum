Date: 2005-11-30 When the world began, all was a formless darkness, all blind clashing in the chaos, no reason, no rhyme, no breath of redemption. The Creator, in his wisdom, made order out of chaos. A formless mist descended from the new-molded mountains, and from the mist came man in all his glory—primordial and mute, but the Creator taught him how to sing of the miracle of his birth. Man, of course, was fallible. And it was this weakness, this inability to accept contentment without greedily striving for something more that brought the Curse of Cain. From His Curse came something even the Creator had perhaps not expected… monsters that fed upon man. Upon poor, defenseless…. pathetic, ignorant, man. ‘There are more things on heaven and earth, Horatio, than are dreamt of in your philosophy." ASPIRAT PRIMO FORTUNA LABORI ME DUCE TUTUS ERIS VOX POPULI VOX DEI AD MAIOREM DEI GLORIAM… AD INFINITUM For years we listened, listened to the plaint and murmur of their masses. What has one said that the others didn’t repeat, in ceaseless murmurs of conformity? Coexistence with such creatures is an impossibility—but when have they ever shown that they are worthy to meet their betters? You know just who I am Don't be so distant Cause when you're lost I am solely there to share your grief And so we exist in their shadows, creeping along and stealing a stray sheep now and then. Wolves prowling a dark forest, whose edges they barely probe—staring into that incomprehensible blackness, terrified that they might find some of it within themselves. There are times when it’s quite degrading to be what I am. Wailing for your sorrow Is only my way to comfort you Reminders of innocent youth Waiting for morrow you're lonely I name your solitude I speaketh the truth That begs the question: why? Why must we continue to put ourselves in this situation, to deal with creatures we disdain? Now tell me all about your pain Down to the detail Don't say it's love Your fragile heart feeds my contempt Allow me to answer your question with a smile. Look inward, deep inward. Look into your own heart, its deepest dark recesses. What you find there may surprise you. Once, perhaps, you enjoyed a lover’s pinch. You sucked the blood off a cut finger, without being repulsed by the taste. You laughed at someone else’s misfortune, and you felt no guilt. Whatever your good points, it is inside you. No matter what cathartic you use, you will never purge it. You will think you’ve hidden it deep down where no one can find it, but in truth, it is as glaring as the day that it first came wailing into the world in an afterbirth of lust and violence. That makes you sad, doesn’t it? What will you do, weep for yourself? Hah. Wailing for your sorrow Is only my way to comfort you Reminders of innocent youth Waiting for morrow you're lonely I name your solitude I speaketh the truth But it’s not your darkness that’s the point… rather, it’s my own, which I’ve long since come to accept as bare fact. It’s your pain I want. It was accident that lured me into this world, but it is the pain that keeps me here, the pain that sustains me, the fresh feel of agonized flesh beneath my fingertips. Perhaps that disturbs you? But I make no elaborate stories to justify my motives. Not as you do. Your good points make up for your bad, do they not? You’re just doing the best you can, aren’t you? The fables sustain your existence. As long as you continue to put on masks and play parts in elaborate fictions, you can look yourself in the eye. And you remain as predictable as the rising of the sun. Chase the heathen call We belong…you and I Unison in all you deny This can’t possibly go on, can it? But you’d be selling your own kind short if you thought that change was the only constant. It’s been happening for eons. Time inescapable has provided no difference in this behavior—if anything, it’s even more pathetic than it was before. You can’t accept that you’re already part of the fallen. Good is your illusion, a little spotlight that you dance under… and when you leave it, there is nothing but darkness and shadow all around. Are you expecting me to enlighten you? Why should I? Why should I, when it’s so fun to watch you dance on the ends of your puppet strings, pretending your lives away? It’s all the more beauty for me when you realize the truth on your own. You feed vices that you are scared to admit that you yourself have… for that, I suppose I should thank you. I am the thorn in your side That seeks accomplishment Reminding the mortal of death I am the spore of your pride An angel heavensent The master of all I am the urge of the flesh That is the summation of my soul—you writhe, I smile. You suffer, my heart is made joyous. You bleed, I am sustained, and you are left to discover for yourself that the lies you have told yourself about the world are no longer sustainable. I can only smile. I, devil. I, sadist. I, monster. Dance for me—for I hunger. Song&Lyrics- "March of Mephisto" by Kamelot Quote- Shakespeare’s "Hamlet"
