You kiddin' me?

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 Christmas? The doorbell of The Real Deal’s house rings during a brisk cold session of the day and he makes his way to see who it is. Once opening the door he finds five people standing before him. Two adults, one male and one female. The other three are children, two girls and a boy. They all huddle together like some sports teams do before games. I would say American football teams but I don’t actually know that much about it. Anyway… The female steps forward and moves her glove covered hands out towards Deal, it is a cold night after all. He looks down confused and then looks backup towards the female. She smiles at him before embracing him with a warm gesture… "Hello fine sir. We have all come to sing you some Christmas carols." If not confused before this took things to a new level for Deal. He scans around the group with a taste of disgust in his mouth. Thoughts consume his mind… "It’s not even fucking Christmas!" "Who does this chick think she is?" "That guy’s head is shaped funny heh" Now the man steps forward with a twinkle in his eye. Actually stepping a bit too close for comfort. Toe with toe with Deal. This situation doesn’t sit well with him as he is used to being attacked when in these situations. Without hesitation he jumps back which in turn causes the man to move even closer… Unfortunately so close that the slammed door smacks him on the face and send him to the ground. Deal smirks as he peaks through the small window on his door before talking to himself. "Christmas carols? You must be joking! It’s on November!" Outside the group gather round the man and hold him close before they help him up and run away… Inside the house Deal is now met by his cell phone ringing. He answers politely and he hears a soft female voice… "Dude! All these wrestlers have been hyping themselves for this tournament! What the fuck have you mean doing?" Deal laughs and places a cigarette into his lips. He lights up before replying… "Look I don’t need to do no hype! I’ll get noticed by just standing in the ring against Vangelus." "I hope you’re right my friend! How’s Erin gonna notice you if you don’t? Have you stopped drinking yet?" "Hell no! I’m going for a drink now!" Before the female gets a chance to reply Deal hangs up his cell and makes his way to the door, swinging on a coat as he does. Opening the door he is shocked to find two police officers standing in his path. One of them shows him their badge before speaking in a clear voice. "Mr Stevenson. We are placing you under arrest for the murder of Lisa Stevenson." Deal stands in awe, not knowing what to say. Had Erin got to him already? For he sure as hell hadn’t murdered his daughter! Only time will tell…
