Lost Sister (PT.3)

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-28 Lost Sister (PT.3) CH.3-Town of the Dead. The town was very old and run down…with the clear signs of age around the shingles of the roofs of the buildings and the fading paint on the stucco. The fog was heavy but only ankle high…the fire was still blazing in the background as the two, Samantha and an injured John Adams move forward towards the town. They are missing the full moon in the night sky due to the black smoke filling the air. John Adams was still moving quite slowly due to the injury that he received in the truck when it crashed. Samantha was doing her best that any fifteen year old could do to hold up a three hundred pound man. She could feel a sharp piercing pain in her back that was indicating that she needed to rest somewhere and quick. Up ahead about ten feet was an old, brown, wooden shack with an equally old well to the right side of it. "That’s a good place to rest." She thought as her and Adams slowly began to trudge through to the old abandon shack. Luckily Adams was mustering enough strength to make the walk or the weight would be well overbearing to Samantha. Birds flew over head to get away from the smoke infested sky, even the lowly insects of the earth were scattering to some type of safety due to the fire behind them. When they got to the shack, Samantha saw that the door that led inside was off the hinges…just like other things that have been going on as of late since she has found her brother. First she had to escape a town filled with undead monsters, second she had to endure a chase from these undead monsters and only the Almighty knew what was next in store for them. She nudged the door part way to allow it to fall inward so she and John could go inside with it. When the door fell it made little noise as the old rotten oak wood broke partway down the middle of the top of it…and another indication of the age of the place was the old dust cloud that was caused after it hit the floor on the inside. John and Samantha slowly crept inside to the left inside the shack and sat down leaning against the wall. The dust inside was still heavy and thick, the mice scavengers were trembling in fear in the far right corner of the building, due to the harsh conditions on the outside environment. Spider webs on each of the ceiling corner were finely spun in random designs to catch unsuspecting prey. There was even an old, black and finely aged stove in the middle of the room. Definitely some history with the old abandoned shack…but probably the most disturbing was the words she found painted on the wall behind her. The words were hard to make out because of the age of the paint and how much it had already faded…and with a closer look she realizes that the paint looks like a red blood substance was used to make the words. After four minutes of studying the painting she realizes it says: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen. The Lords Prayer, one of the most inspirational prayers in mankind, but why was it written so long ago and in blood? Rat and mice droppings and remains were scattered around the old stove. "Ugh" Her Brother was badly hurt, each passing moment his strength seems to be diminishing faster and faster. What was she supposed to do? How could she help her brother heal his wounds? With that, call it fate or even call it dumb luck but what seems to be on command makes her whole body glow a soothing light blue glow. The light blue glow travels from Samantha all the way to John Adams. The bruised head of Adams started to fade and become a mere memory…John’s complexion started to change also as it became a real healthy color. After the phenomenon the blue glow faded away and Samantha was left with only a question…. "What happened?" she thought to herself as she was witness to her brother being healed by a unknown source…but it seems that whatever it was it was coming from her. John Adams started to stir a lot, left and right till he finally creaked open his right eye slightly. The experience was almost of that of a newborn looking upon the world for the very first time, after coming out of the mothers’ womb. "W…what happened?" John Adams says still strangely feeling reenergized. "I…I have no idea, but I’m glad you’re ok." Samantha said as she lets a smile be known to her brother. Now John Adams knows that there are defiantly unexplained things that happen with the world that some people just have no idea or explanation…but this point of his life really does take the cake. John stands to a fully erected posture then stretches as if he had slept all night. His eyes quickly adjust to the scene around him. "Man what a dump!" John says as his nostril twitches to the dust inside the shack. The two began to walk around the shack hand in hand as a child would do with a parent. The wind outside started to pick up drastically, but the good thing was for the two is that it caused the fire to shift away from them. The two watched through a single pane half broken window as the fire started to shift away from them. "Well we have to wait here till morning, then we can take off and find away out of Alabama." John said to his sister as she began to yawn from the sleep that started to creep up on her. As the two continued to look out the window, they notice that the wind had strangely ceased…in fact everything has ceased. The birds, wind and everything that was making a noise around the environment that they were in. John has a special gift, a gift that he has always had a problem determining between reality and fantasy with. Ever since birth he has had the ability to see and communicate with the dead. Well today is no different as his gift will be used to the extreme as suddenly he witnesses a silhouette outline of a young woman passing the window that the two were watching the outside with. "Did you see that?" John asked his sister. "Yes what was it?" Now he has learned in the past that his gift was passed to different generations of his blood line. Some different variations but generally the same concept, could it be that his sister had the same gift? "No wonder Devilin wants to destroy you." John thought to himself as he led his sister with him to the outside through the entrance where the broken door once stood. Outside he could see that there were more silhouette outlines of people that once were. There were children, women, and men of all different shapes and sizes. John could sense that there was an urgency in these shadows of the dead…what was it that interrupted their peaceful slumber? John and his sister stood there watching…John could feel his sisters fear causing her to tremble. John squeezed her hand firmly to reassure her that all would be alright. John decided to move a little closer with his sister, so he could hear what was going on when he saw the spirits conversing with one another. "What do we do?" "He is back!" "But we had cast him out…it cost us our very existence!" What was it that these spirits were talking about? Was it how they died in the first place? As he continues to move forward, his feet accidentally steps on a stick from a branch on one of the near by trees. The spirits see him…as they look into the eyes of Adams their eyes begin to glow a bright blood red. "How dare you Devilin Rubis, how dare you step back to the one place you had destroyed so long ago!" One of the spirits says as they begin to point there right transparent finger at Adams. "Listen I’m not Devilin…my name is Jonathan Adams! I and my sister are running from Rubis, please you must tell me…what he did to you?" John said real quickly in an attempt to defend himself. "How dare you think you can trick us Devilin, you will be sent away again condemned to never step foot upon our town again!" The spirit said in response not trusting that John was telling them the truth. The spirits begin to half circle surround Adams and his sister chanting a weird chant…it seems to be that the incantation is in Latin. Samantha begins to let her fears get the best of her as she begins to cry. Adams picks her up and holds her close to him as a humongous, transparent wall of force hits the two with so much force that it begins to push them out of the town. "You are making a big mistake!!!" John tried to yell at them but it was to late…a familiar blackness circumference the two for latterly a half an hour. When the light finally started to seep in and the two opened their eyes they found themselves at a gas station that was thirty miles away from there previous location. "What happened?" Samantha said as her fear was slowly leaving her. "It seems that those spirits wanted us out, seems like our dear brother Devilin and Black Alabama has a lot more connection to it then we were able to find out!" John said as he leads his sister to the brightly lit entrance to the gas station. "Don’t worry were going to find out exactly what is going on here…I assure you!" John said to reassure his sister. … To be Continued …
