Click For Spoiler Posted for Jaime of the 4WW Broken I had felt like I was on top of the world; like there wasn't a thing in the world that could hold me down. Then two things happened, and my life was shattered... Forever. Scene One Fade in. The scene is set in an average living room. A few vinyl couches, a television set, nothing fancy. The cheap, shabby sofas are bleeding yellow stuffing. A short, skinny man is plunked down on one of the couches. He has a glazed look on his face, with a bit of drool escaping out of his slightly open mouth. This man is Jaime. His eyes are fixed on the television screen. "Mommy, wow, I'm a big kid now!" Jaime is not visibly disgusted by the inane diaper commercial, in fact, he shows no outward reactions towards any stimulation whatsoever. For example, his cell phone is vibrating against his hip, and he is making no motion to pick it up to see who is calling. Jaime is in a stupor, and it doesn't look like anything can get him out of it. I am sad to say that Jaime had been in this torpid stupor ever since he had been told by the doctors that he had suffered an injury to his neck. Jaime had been told to get on a rehabilitation program for his neck, but he had refused, opting to sit on his ass for a few months to wait for his neck to heal by itself. Jaime had seemingly lost all will to live, an incredible transformation from the once exuberant and fun-loving person that everybody knew. He had needed time, time to think about things. When he had just disappeared from 4WW, the fans were quick to notice. They emailed him, some even went as far as looking up his phone number in a phone book and calling him. But he never bothered to respond to any of them. His phone had been ringing off the hook for 7 days straight, and his answering machine had answered every single one of the calls. And Jaime couldn't care less about any of this. The phone lying on the coffee table that is placed in the middle of the room starts to ring. No surprises here, Jaime makes no move to pick it up. He doesn't even acknowledge it; his eyes are glued completely to the television set. Though it is safe to say that none of the content was actually being absorbed by Jaime. After the tenth ring, the answering machine picked up for Jaime. "Hi, I'm not home right now. Please leave a message after the beep." *BEEP* "Jaime, where've you been?! I really need to talk to you, man! It's about your Uncle!" This was Eric, Jaime's best friend since kindergarten. Unlike most childhood friends, they have kept in constant contact, even after secondary school. They were as thick as thieves, pardon the cliche. But Jaime fails to recognize that this was a life and death situation... How could he know that his Uncle is about to be asphyxiated with a pillow? Jaime has no idea what is about to happen. It will change his life so completely, friends will later say that he was transformed into another person after this ordeal. He had gotten into an argument with his Uncle. He had left in a huff, and hadn't been back to the house since. The argument was about something stupid that Jaime didn't care to remember. The television has been left on for about a week. Jaime hasn't gotten off the couch he's lying on, except to fix something to eat or to go to the bathroom. Jaime is on auto-pilot; he had hit his mid-life crisis twenty years early. His hair was oily, his breath was smelly, he hasn't changed out of the clothes he is wearing for about a month now, and the beginnings of a beard has formed on his face. Worst of all, he stank. Stank of days spent crying in his sleep, stank of crushed dreams, stank of things he should have done... And the worst was yet to come. There was a sudden tinkling of glass breaking, and a few moments later, Eric appears in the doorway, with a crowbar in his hand. Jaime's glazed eyes look up slowly at his best friend. He doesn't bother to acknowledge his friend, not even a nod of the head. He has sank so far into the abyss, it would be damn hard for him to climb out again. "What the heck have you been doing?? I've called you about a hundred times in the last few months, and your answering machine has picked up every single one of my calls." Jaime's eyes return to the television. "Uncle Benny's in the hospital! He got into a car crash a few days ago! He said he wants to see you, but no one's been able to contact you!" Jaime's eyes show some life. He is clearly disturbed by what Eric has just said. He clears his throat. He hadn't used his voice in over two months, and his larynx was sort of rusty. Jaime moves his right hand to his throat, massaging it to get his voice back. He can't find the right words at first, as if he has forgotten how to speak English. "Is-is he okay?" "He lost a lot of blood, but the doctors say he'll be okay. He's depressed, he wants to see you." "I'll go." Jaime gets up, yawning. He hadn't slept in a few days now, all hours spent staring at the television, thinking morbid thoughts. His joints creak as Jaime does a few warmup stretches to get himself going. Eric goes over to the television to turn it off, and his hand accidentally touches the base of the television. He mutters a swear word as he brings his hand to his mouth, hoping to sooth the burn. "Just how long have you had this damn thing on?" Jaime shrugs, a gesture of nonchalance. He hadn't cared to remember the days and the months that had passed by. Night and day had just melted together to form a single blur of time spent thinking. The calendar that is tacked to the living room wall has been collecting dust for about two months, the cheap cardboard proclaiming that the month was April. Eric casts his gaze at the calendar and shakes his head in disgust. "Three months, Jaime. Three months, and not a single soul has seen hide or hair of you. Uncle Benny refused to talk about you. I assumed you two got into a huge argument. It was really stupid, you know. It left Benny all burnt out, and you know he isn't exactly young. He used to be so... Waspish, making snippy comments all the time. But he lost the spark, Jaime, and so have you. And I bet the argument you had was stupid. It was about something silly, wasn't it? Did you forget to mow the lawn?" "Well, shit, it wasn't like that. I got injured while I was wrestling; Doc said I must've landed awkwardly on my neck sometime, I don't exactly remember when. I hadn't been feeling right for months, and that was the cause. Doc said we found about it late, and it would take a longer time to heal. I had to take a hiatus from 4WW, to heal more than just my neck. I had lost energy, I was drained... Childress knew it, don't ask me how. He is strangely perceptive. Anyway, I think we should get to the hospital now, I'll explain more later." "Erm, I don't think we should go just yet. You should shower and change, first... I don't think the world is ready for this kind of stench." "Oh, shutup." Scene Two After breaking every speeding law in California, the battered, little Ford Anglia drives into the parking lot of the hospital. Jaime double-parks his Ford, locks the anti-theft device into the steering wheel, and gets out of the car in record time. Jaime emerges from the Ford, freshly shaven, clean, for the first time in a few months. He felt good, but there is still a hint of worry lined on his face. It is clear from the look on his face that Jaime is very anxious to see his Uncle again. It would be the first time in two months that Jaime has seen his Uncle. They had to talk; there had been some things that had puzzled him those two months that he had lay on that couch, thinking. He is desperate to get answers to these questions, questions that had lay in the back of his mind for a long time. Jaime jogs his way to the entrance of the giant hospital, shadowed closely by Eric. He forces the door open, breezing through it like an autumn wind. Eric is struggling to keep up; all those potato chips and hot dogs are getting to him. Jaime pads softly over to the front desk, and clears his throat to get the attention of an attractive red-headed nurse wearing a crisp, white uniform. The uniform matches its surroundings to the letter. The walls and floor were also white, giving the hospital a neat, surgical atmosphere. The nurse looks up and smiles at Jaime. "Hello, how may I help you?" "Can you tell me which room Benny Chan is staying in?" The nurse doesn't even bother searching the computer for the necessary information. She responds without looking up at Jaime. "You can find him in Room 302. Take the elevator. I'd advise that you get there quickly. Run." Jaime heeds the nurse's words, and breaks out into a sprint. Eric heaves a giant sigh of resignation, and follows. Jaime got to the elevator, pressing the "UP" arrow rapidly, and decides it is too slow. He pushes the door to the fire escape open and takes the stairs up two at a time. Eric decides to wait for the elevator. Jaime glances at the sign that indicates the floor he is on, and sees that he is at the third floor already. He pulls the door open and flies down the corridor, nearly knocking down a nurse in the process. He slows down to a fast walk, checking the numbers of every room. He finally reaches 302, and is about to pull open the door, when a blood curdling scream emits from inside. Jaime's blood runs cold, his brain suspecting what has happened, yet his heart would not let him believe it. He goes inside, and looks at the nurse hunching over a bed, breathing hard. The other patients are fast asleep, apparently undisturbed by the nurse's loud scream. Jaime walks to the nurse, and looks down at the person lying on the bed. It was his Uncle. His Uncle has stopped breathing, from what Jaime could see, but it has not sunk in to him yet. It was so surreal. His Uncle, his guardian, his protector... Dead. It all seemed impossible to Jaime. His head was swirling, and he felt dizzy. He staggered onto the frame of the bed, clutching it. Just then , Eric entered the room, breathing hard. He took one look at Benny, and a look of dread formed on his face. Eric whispered, breaking the silence... "He isn't..." "He is." The nurse was the first to snap out of it. She looked at Jaime and Eric, and assumed an apologetic expression. She told them in a gruff voice. "I'm sorry about this. I assume he's a relative. I'll go get someone.. A doctor." The nurse breezed out of the room, apparently fully recovered from the shock. Eric was still dazed, and Jaime was hit so hard, it looked like he'd never recover. Jaime kneels down and cradles Benny's head in his arms, and begins openly sobbing on Benny's shoulder. All that he ever wanted was to apologize to his Uncle... Now, he would never get the chance. The utter sadness of this event had hit Jaime like a sledgehammer to the skull. This was bar none, the worst day of his life. Uncle had been his guardian ever since his dad had died when he was 5. Jaime had never asked the cause of his dad's death, never thought to. This had been one of the questions he had meant to ask his Uncle, one of the questions that had been itching to be asked. Eric's voice broke in on the sombre moment. "Say, why is everybody in here sleeping? And where is that nurse?" Then, something struck Jaime... It all came whirling back to him, the discrepancies. Why had the nurse screamed when she had discovered Benny dead? It would be reasonable to assume that she had seen other deaths before, if she really was a nurse. And her voice was so gruff, too gruff to be a woman's voice.. Then the third and final clue came back to Jaime. Her legs had been hairy. Not just the kind of peach fuzz that some women get. It was really, and truly hairy, the stuff that only men are able to get. No, it couldn't be possible, but it was. The reality struck him like a fierce lightning bolt. Benny had been murdered, Jaime was sure of it. "Eric, go find a nurse, and make sure she doesn't have hairy legs." Eric obliges the cryptic orders, though he was thoroughly mystified as to what Jaime is up to. Jaime stands up, and wipes tears away from his face with his sleeve. His eyes are still red and glistening, and there is no doubt that Jaime is in a lot of pain. He had a hunch about something, and wanted to see if he was right about it. He walks out of the room and goes to the waste bin that is located outside the women's rest room. Sure enough, there is a nurse uniform in there, and a blonde wig. Whoever the murderer was, Jaime would not catch him today. He notices a piece of paper sticking out of the breast pocket of the blouse, and slips it into his own pocket. It might come in handy. Then he takes the fire exit downstairs and runs towards the service counter. He looks around, but there is no redhead there. He asks the nurse working at the desk. "Excuse me, but do you know of a redhead nurse that works at the counter here?" "Umm, there are no redheads working here currently, I don't think." "Okay, thanks." As Jaime turns his back, the nurse smiles secretively to herself. She reaches under her nurse's cap and pulls off her black wig, revealing a full head of glossy red hair. She should really stop wasting time and get away from this place, before the Asian guy starts suspecting. He was way smarter than he looked, something that she hadn't given him credit for. She slips out of the front desk, leaving the other nurses there bound and gagged... Jaime went back up to the third floor and went into room 302. This was all a big mystery, and he felt like he was just a pawn being played in a giant game of chess. This was all way over his head; sure he had lived in Los Angeles all his life, but murder had never come to him. A doctor is standing over Benny, surveying him, and muttering to himself. "Hmm, the death looks peaceful to me.. I'll check for any signs of him being murdered, but I doubt he was murdered.." "Umm, excuse me doctor, but can I please check one thing?" "Sure, go ahead. I don't think there's been any foul play going on here. Although I do find it a bit odd that every patient in this room is asleep. No harm done, though, I suppose." Jaime thought to himself, if he only he knew... His face still streaked with tears, he gets a grip of Uncle Benny's left arm and lifted it up, looking at the palm of his hand. Sure enough, there was blue ink there, miniscule writing, written in Chinese. Jaime memorizes them, storing them in his brain. He then licks his index finger with his tongue and begins erasing the ink from Benny's palm. He had thought to look at Benny's left palm because whenever he needed to remember something of importance, he would write it down on his hand. Normally, he would not look here, but this was anything but normal. There was something enormously suspicious going on here. Eric is standing by patiently, watching Jaime do a number of odd things. Eric thought Jaime had lost his mind, and did not suspect that there was anything going on. Jaime turned to the doctor. "Okay, we'll be leaving now. I'll have to arrange the funeral and everything, I suppose.." As they walk out of the room, Jaime turns to Eric and whispers, as if someone was listening in to their every word with recording devices, and he felt it was not safe to speak normally. "I think there's something weird going on around here." "Why?" "First of all, where the hell did that nurse come from? And where did he go?" "He?" "I looked at his legs. They were hairy like nothing else I've ever seen. It's like he's part Italian and part Greek, that's the only way he could have legs that hairy. And second of all, Uncle wrote something on his palm. I've memorized it, burned it into my memory. I have a feeling that it's an important clue." "To what?" Jaime looks at Eric, dumbfounded. "Isn't is obvious by now? Uncle was murdered. Just when he was about to tell me something important too. Somebody wanted to keep him silent. Here, I'll show you something." He reaches into his jeans pocket and pulls out the note he had found in the nurse's uniform. It was a dirty piece of paper, with words scrawled illegiblely on it with a red pen. It said: "Hey, kid. If you're reading this, you're a lot smarter than I thought you were. Your Uncle was a good guy; I knew him personally. Just so you don't get any ideas about me being an evil asshole, it wasn't my decision to kill your Uncle. I was all for keeping him alive, but your Uncle sealed his fate when he decided to violate the Compact. I'm sorry it had to end this way, kid. Yours truly, Jack" Jaime began to shake with anger, his face turning beet red, tears starting to cascade down his cheeks again, like a waterfall. He crunches the note up into a little tiny ball, but doesn't throw it into the trash. He keeps it. He had a feeling that he will need it later on. His hands are balled up into fists, his teeth are clenched. He didn't know why this was happening to him. It all seemed so simple back then. Wrestle once a week, make a couple of grand, enough to live comfortably, but not extravagantly. Now he was brought into this little web of horseshit. What the fuck was the Compact, and why did they need to kill his Uncle over it?! It sounded so stupid, and so petty, to Jaime. It was utterly unfair, that's what it was. How could anyone end someone's life over something that so trivial, so silly?? It was like back in the schoolyard, where kids got into arguments over the stupidest things; except the arguments were magnified into adult versions. He was so frustrated, he felt like he could explode. But no, he did the next best thing, he punches the wall. As most people know, punching a wall out of frustration is not a smart thing to do, because it results in pain for only one of the parties involved. Thus was the case with Jaime. He clutches his fist in agony, leaning up against the wall. Just then, his cell phone starts to ring. Jaime slid the case up with his left hand, and answered it. "Hello?" "'Ello, is this Jaime?" "Yeah.." "This is Fey Childress. I jus' wan'ted t' let ye know tha' your Uncle 'as signed ye up for th' Extreme Title Divison Royale for PrimeTime Central occurin' this upcomin' Maelstrom." Jaime looks shellshocked, and his face clearly showed it. Eric was looking on in curiosity. Jaime hadn't wrestled in three months, scratch that, he hadn't done anything that could even be considered athletic in three months.. His muscles were creaky with unuse, and it would be even harder to cope with the mental side of wrestling once he got into the ring. Especially against a large number of opponents. "Umm.. What the..?" "Ye did na' know abou' this?" "Well, I kinda hadn't talked to my Uncle for 2 months or so, and now he's dead." Jaime says this in a matter-of-fact voice, but his eyes are welling up with tears. He puts a hand to his mouth, "Oh... Sorry t' hear tha'... Will ye still be competin'? It's in Vancouver, Canada. Know where tha' is?" "Yeah, it's not far from here. One to two hour flight. I can make it.. Where will the competition be held?" "General Motors Place. I'm sure ye can ask th' locals for directions once ye arrive there. Is tha' okay wi' ye?" Sure, his whole life had been fucked up within a matter of hours, but this was exactly the type of thing that he needed. His mind needed to be sharp, his body needed to be fit, if he wanted to find out anything about his Uncle's murderers and his Uncle's secret life. It was imperative that he was fit in mind and body. What better way to sharpen his wits than to pit himself against the best that PTC can offer? After he did this one little show, he would be fresh and his mind would be sharp, sharp enough to tackle whatever lay ahead of him in his quest to find his Uncle's killer. "Okay, I'll do it. I'll find my own transportation to get there." "Alriogh." "Oh, one more question, Mrs. Childress. What day will Maelstrom be held on?" "It will be 'eld on Sunday. Ye've ga' a few days t' prepare for th' match." "Thanks for telling me. Bye" "Take care." Jaime closes his cell-phone and puts it in his pocket. He puts his right arm against the wall, for support, closes his eyes, and starts rubbing the bridge of his nose with his left hand. He had to remove some of this stress. He feared if he didn't, he would suffer some kind of nervous breakdown. Jaime needed to see some sort of psychiatrist, to share some of his problems. It could possibly lift that titanic weight off of his shoulders. Eric puts hand on Jaime's shoulder. "Who was that?" "That was Fey Childress, she told me about some stupid tournament that Uncle had signed me up for. Something about an Extreme Title Division match." "Oh, your Uncle had told me about that on the day of the crash. He was really wanting to talk to you about something, but that horrible car crash happened. I could tell he was really anxious to tell you. He kept muttering under his breath, though I couldn't quite understand what he was saying." Eric's words seem to bring back the sorrow of his Uncles' death, magnifying it a hundred fold. Jaime slams an open palm against the wall, and gives a cry of raw anger. Screw the Fates, he thought, screw every single living thing on this planet. He knew from now on that he could not rely on anybody. He would never allow himself to be hurt like this again. He wouldn't cower from those who wanted to kill him. He would fight back... And unfortunately for them, Jaime would fight dirty.
