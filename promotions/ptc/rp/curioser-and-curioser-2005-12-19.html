Date: 2005-12-19 The quiet sound of birdsong drifted through the haze surrounding Dawn’s mind, the soothing sounds chasing in a ray of sunlight that seemed to pierce right through the clouds and strike Dawn directly in the face. She shifted uncomfortably in an unfamiliar bed, whining a little in the back of her throat as she tried to roll over and block out both the light and the sound. There was another sound as well, something that was nagging at the back of her mind, trying to pierce through the foggy veil surrounding it and demand her attention. It was hard to focus on it though, hard to bring that whining sound to the front of her mind…hard because her head hurt so badly, like someone had spiked a nail through her forehead. She whimpered this time, bringing her arms up protectively around her aching skull, trying to ease the blinding pain through contact alone. A hand touched her arm, gently pulling it away from her head. Dawn tried to fight against it, but found that she had no more strength then an infant, and was powerless to stop herself from being rolled onto her back once more. A large hand looped behind her head and pulled her into a half sit, pain exploding out from her forehead as she was forced into movement. Something cold pressed against her mouth, and a smoky tasting liquid slipped past her lips and down the back of her throat. Her head was eased back to the pillow again and her eyelids became very heavy. As she drifted off to sleep, one voice finally penetrated the haze of her mind. "She’ll sleep…and when she awakens, hopefully she’ll be able to explain some of this." ***************************************************************************************** "Dawn…" Oh gods not again. Please don’t let this happen again, I can’t handle this…not now. "Dawn…Dawn sweety…" Sweety? You bastard, it’s a little late in the game for cute pet names. Why don’t you drag your sorry ass out of hiding and get this over with?! "Dawn you’ve got it all wrong, I’m not your enemy…" Oh really? That’s a fucking laugh. You beat me like a bad animal; you ripped away my pride and my dignity. You…you raped me in my own home…you’re the ONLY fucking enemy. "Dawn…I’m not Marcus…I’m not who you think I am…" A…woman? Who are you? Am I dreaming again? "In a manner of speaking, you’re actually just waking up. You’ve been dreaming for almost your whole life my dear…and now it’s time for you to start waking up." My whole life? What are you talking about? "You’ll see, I’ll be able to show you soon enough. And don’t trouble yourself over that pesky bloodsucker…I’ll see to him." Who ARE you? "All in time…for now, you need to wake up…open your eyes Dawn…" ******************************************************************************************* "Ah!" Dawn came awake with a start, sitting bolt upright in the small futon she’d been occupying. Night had fallen, and silvery moonlight streamed gently in through the glass window above her bed. Breathing was difficult, and it took her several moments to slow her breathing down from short gasps to a more regular pace. "Dawn? Are you okay?" Alex had risen from her chair when Dawn sat up, her face a mask of worry. Worry quickly turned to embarrassment, and she turned away from Dawn as a faint blush crept into her cheeks. "Uh Dawn…sheet…" Dawn looked down in surprise at her own nakedness, fumbling the heavy comforter back up around her previously exposed breasts as she turned a bright shade of crimson herself. "Oh thanks…I’m covered up now…" Alex turned slowly back around, peeking at first to ensure Dawn was right. "No problem. Are you alright? You passed out in the gym, after you said you’d heard singing." "I passed out?" Dawn raised a hand to her forehead, gingerly rubbing it against her still aching skull. "I don’t remember much at all. We were talking about Marcus, and then I heard the most beautiful singing…very faintly, like it was a long way away. Then…there was some kind of drink?" Alex nodded firmly in response. "Master Mifune brewed something up for you after you passed out. You seemed like you were in some kind of fever, tossing around a little and making some noise, but like you couldn’t even hear anyone." "I remember that I think, there was noise all around me but I just couldn’t focus on anything…it was just noise. And then…I had another dream." Alex knelt down next to the futon, reaching out to touch the back of one of Dawn’s hands. "Marcus again?" "No it was different this time. There was a female voice, and she was telling me that I’d been asleep for most of my life…and now I had to wake up. I don’t know what it means really." "Well maybe it was…just…a dream…" Alex’s voice trailed off in astonishment as she stared plainly at Dawn for a moment, her eyes wide. Blinking in surprise, and tugging the comforter a little tighter around her chest, Dawn tried her best to return Alex’s gaze levelly. "You okay Alex? You look kind of like you’ve seen a ghost." Tearing her eyes away from Dawn’s face, Alex found a particularly interesting piece of the floor to stare at. "Uh sorry, I’ve just never seen…’those’ on you before…" "Those?" Dawn put one hand to her face, patting around it in alarm and confusion. "Have I got something on my face?" "Your uh…your teeth Dawn." Alex half mumbled. Sliding one hand to her mouth, Dawn slipped her thumb inside and ran it along the op row of teeth. She didn’t get very fair before encountering a much larger tooth in place of her right incisor, and a quick inspection showed her left was in the same state. "Oh," Dawn sighed, "Those." "Those? Have you always had…fangs?!" Alex tried to keep her voice level, but couldn’t stop it from gaining decibel or two near the end of her question. Dawn sighed again, letting her hand drop back into her lap, and staring down at the floral pattern of her comforter. "No…they didn’t show up until I met Marcus. I always kind of figured he’d done something to me when I was sleeping." Alex shook her head slowly. "I’ve never heard of anything like that happening with vampires. Even a vampires closest servants don’t just suddenly manifest fangs." "I don’t really have any other explanation for them." Dawn shot back, starting to feel a little aggravated. "They weren’t there before Marcus, and they haven’t gone away since he left." "How come I’ve never seen them before?" "They’re not always out. It’s like there’s this…sheath, I guess, in my gums. When I’m really super mad, or feeling really threatened or…nevermind the last one. The bottom line is they only pop out at certain times, and then they’re just kind of there." Alex shrugged slightly, rubbing the back of her neck in embarrassment. "Well…I don’t really have any explanation for them, sorry. Do you…drink blood with them?" "NO!" Dawn yelled her eyes wide. "Holy fuck no. I do my best to ignore them until they go away. Jesus Christ Alex, I’m not like a vampire wannabe or anything okay?" Alex held up her hands in surrender, nodding quickly. "Okay okay, I didn’t mean anything by it…I was just trying to learn a little more about them okay?" "Sorry…they kind of freak me out to be honest. It’s just easier to pretend they’re not there you know?" Alex looked back up at Dawn. "Have you thought about seeing a dentist or doctor about them? Maybe it’s some kind of condition?" "Er not really…I try to avoid doctors when I can, and my dentist hasn’t ever said anything about them, so I don’t feel the need to bring it up. It’s just something…I try to ignore." "Does Lia know about them?" Dawn turned a bright shade of pink, her cheeks and neck very quickly becoming a shade normally left to roses and undergarments. "Uhm…yeah she knows." Alex decided it was best not to push that particular line of questioning. "Uh…you said it they only come out when you’re in the grip of something extreme right? Like rage?" Dawn nodded, the flush of embarrassment finally starting to fade from her face. "Yeah basically…near as I can tell anyway." "What about fear?" "I…suppose that could be it. I dunno, I just feel like there’s more going on here then I’m totally aware of you know? What’s with my weird dreams?" Alex shrugged. "I wish I could tell you Dawn, but I just don’t know. It’s a little more then I’m used to dealing with." The other woman just nodded, hugging the comforter tighter around her naked body and staring out the window. Alex watched her for a moment in silence, before perking up slightly. "Oh! I have good news." Chuckling, Dawn looked back at her. "That would be a lovely change from how things have been recently. What have you got?" "Michi and I found the weapons cache I was talking about. They moved out most of the really heavy hardware, but we managed to get enough that I’m sure we can put a stop to Marcus and his dicking around in your mind." A wan smile slipped onto Dawn’s lips. "That IS good news. Any idea how we need to go about this?" "I have a few ideas rattling around in my brain, but I think those can wait until you’re feeling a little better. No sense in rushing off into combat if you’re not even sure you can stay upright." "Yeah…" Dawn sighed. "I wish I knew what was going on with me Alex, I’m just so unsure about everything right now." Reaching out to squeeze Dawn by the shoulder, Alex smiled in what she hoped was a reassuring manner. "Don’t worry Dawn, we’ll figure it all out. In the meantime you should go back to sleep. I’ll stay with you." "Alright… thanks Alex…for everything." Dawn laid back down, staring up at the ceiling in a mixture of confusion and frustration. ‘What’s wrong with me?’ She thought. ‘What’s going on anymore?’
