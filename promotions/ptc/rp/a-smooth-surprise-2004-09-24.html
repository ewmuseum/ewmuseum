Date: 2004-09-24 PART 2—Continued from GTT round 1, pieces taken from Tarrant’s interactive journal. If you haven't read my GGT Round 1 RP, please read that before judgeing my Round 2 RP's....Thatnkyou ********** So what do you think so far, am I the monster that people make me out to be or am I something else all together? I assure you, sacrificing someone’s child is not something I would do ordinarily, I am not usually THAT cold hearted, but sometimes sacrifices must be made for the greater good, for MY good, and in the end that is truly what matters. As you read this interactive journal you will learn more and more about me, everything from my cold hearted nature to the times where I have been made the fool of, to yes, even the rare times of compassion that I tend to show, as I said before, this is all for you, so that after I have ascended from this world completely, you may form your own opinion about me and my deeds. I do truly wish I could be there in person with you right now to tell you all of this face to face, but as before, real life issues take precedence over everything else. I have now learned that after defeating my opponent Khaled in the GTT, I am now to go on to round two and face Erica Lopez in the second round of what promises to be entertaining to saw the least. Now its not that I am taking Ms. Lopez lightly by not being there with you in person to talk about her, but the simple fact is that there is just simply more important things to do then sit around and talk meaningless trash about someone I have never heard of until today, simply put, I will allow my own actions in two days to speak for itself when I meet this women for the first time, and I have the confidence in my own ability that the result will be much the same as it was last round. Me advancing to the next round. Now, enough of that dribble, it’s on to the continence of my last journal entry, in this edition, I make it through the gateway and into The VOID, however, its different then I had though originally, and due to the events that happened there, it is a place in which I will never go into again, at least not until I am powerful enough to face the beings there on even ground. It still pains me to admit to their vast power especially when compared to my own but I give credit where credit is due. I will let the journal take over and show you the rest as I am now off onto other things. I do hope you enjoy the story told and remember, let no outside opinions of myself influence your own, that’s what this journal is for, to allow you to form your own individual opinion of me and my exploits, it is my hopes that you will see that I was just a victim of choice, the choices of others that drove me to be who I am today. Heartless monster I am not, victim of choice? Let that be a decision that you yourself come to. --Tarrant ********** The VOID, it was just as the word made it sound, black, empty and devoid of anything what so ever, or so at least that’s what it appears to be on the surface. A space between realities it is pocket of space where beings of such immense power dwell, that even the Gods themselves dare not venture to it. Beings that were neither good or evil, but only cared for what was known as the “The Balance”. The Balance was what making sure that neither good nor evil were never truly more powerful then the other, refereed to. Since the dawn of creation, these beings knew that should one ever truly triumph over the other that it would mean the end of creation itself. Thus FATE was created, she made to be the brother of the gods and was to remain a neutral party during the eternal conflict of good vs. evil, but now with her playing favorites, Tarrant hoped to sway these powerful beings to grant him the power to remove her, and take her spot to ensure the balance, or so that was the plan. “He comes.” The hollow sounding statement echoed through out the vast darkness. “He must be desperate for our aid, no one has chosen to perform the rights to come here in centuries.” Another voice rang out in response, this one just as hollow soundings as the one before it. “Indeed.” A third voice made itself known, this one sounded to be female. "Tarrant is driven, of that there is no mistake, but if he seeks out aid, he must be humbled, or else he will continue to grow more and more cocky and sure of himself, only causing more chaos to the balance." “And how do you propose we do so?” The second voice interjected. “I have been watching him, and though the method may seem a bit eccentric, trust me in saying it will get the point across well enough.” ********** Stepping through the gateway between worlds, Tarrant did so with a slight smile of anticipation on his face. FINALLY he would be taking his first steps of ridding himself his the nuisance known as Fate, finally the bitch would be taken care of and he would rise to her spot of power. Finally. Stepping though, The VOID was not as he though it would be, he was greeted by a bright light, so bright it was blinding in fact, shielding his eyes Tarrant tried to look through it, but to no avail. Finally as it subsided he was in complete and total shock to his surroundings. Walking through a door, Tarrant looked around to see himself to be in a night club set back in the American 1930’s it appeared, everyone around him was wearing the old style gangster clothing, the women have the short loose dresses with feather bolas around their necks. Tarrant was the only one wearing a white suit. Everyone was staring at him for no good reason it appeared and Tarrant himself had a look of total and complete confusion upon his face. “What the hell am I doing here and just where the hell IS here?” Tarrant asked more to himself then anyone else. Then in a very jerky motion, as if he had no control over his body, Tarrant reached into his pocket, fished out a quarter and flipped it across the room. Everyone in the room, Tarrant included (those in a very confused fashion) watched the coin as it spun across the open expanse until finally landing in a juke box across the room. Looking on in disbelief, Tarrant’s body convulsed then kicking out a leg he screamed. “OW!” As he began to walk forward others began to gather around him while at the same time, a repetitive beat began blaring gin the background. ”What the hell is happening?!” Tarrant tried to scream but found no words came out of his mouth but instead began moving his head back and forth in fluid motions as he also began to dance. Once again he tried to scream but the words that came out of mouth were not what he intended them to be. "As he came into the window It was the sound of a crescendo He came into her apartment He left the bloodstains on the carpet She ran underneath the table He could see she was unable So she ran into the bedroom She was struck down, it was her doom" Kicking out his leg and spinning around Tarrant thought to himself "This has got to be some kind of mistake, or a joke, this isn’t right, I’m supposed to be in The Void, not this knock off of a music video, I swear, I will kill whoever is doing this to me, I MUST regain control of my motor functions." Try as he may though, it wasn’t happening as he yet again spun around Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok Are you ok, annie Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok Are you ok, annie Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok Are you ok, annie Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok, are you ok, annie Annie are you ok will you tell us that you’re ok there’s a sign in the window that he struck you-a crescendo annie he came into your apartment he left the bloodstains on the carpet then you ran into the bedroom you were struck down it was your doom Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok Are you ok annie Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok Are you ok annie Annie are you ok So, annie are you ok Are you ok annie You’ve been hit by You’ve been hit by- A smooth criminal” By this time, Tarrant was up on a stage, with a mic in hand. "Oh dear lord, I have seen this before, please don’t let me...” Just as he thought the words He spun around three times, kicked out his leg and grabbed his crotch ”I swear I am going to gut who is responsible for this...” he thought to himself with a scowl on his face. It continued on, for another two minutes, the entire club was dancing along with him, matching him move for move, some were singing back up, some were just watching on with smiles on their faces, a few could even be seen hiding in the shadows looking on at the spectacle taking place before them. Finally everything began to wind down, at this point, Tarrant and four others began leaning forward at ninety degree angles, something that appeared to be impossible looking at it from afar, finally the music died down and everyone went back to what they were doing. Tarrant finally had control of his motor functions. Breathing heavily and with a look that would turn death himself away, Tarrant stood there in the middle of the dance floor looking around. His eyes glowing an icy blue he screamed and was engulfed in a blue pillar of flame. "MAKE A FOOL OUT OF ME WILL YOU?! I DEMAND YOU SHOW YOURSELF, SHOW YOUR FACE NOW!!” The people looking on now backed away in horror, but no one stepped forward. "VERY WELL, IF YOU WONT SHOW YOURSELF, THEN I WILL FIND YOU MYSELF!!!" Tarrant screamed in an inhuman voice, a voice that was filled with anger, malice and was dripped with touches of insanity. In response to his words the blue pillar of flame that surrounded him began to spin at a ferocious rate of speed and all at once seemed to explode outward engulfing everything it touched completely, in its wake it left nothing but ashes that instantly blew away from the winds caused by the spinning maelstrom of fire. In an instant, there was nothing left of the night club and only Tarrant, still screaming remained left, nothing but darkness surrounded him now. “ENOUGH!!” A female voice screamed out, and at once Tarrant’s flames disappeared, he was once again in his old attire of a black overcoat, pants and shirt, It was as if nothing happened. Tarrant looked around in anger and confusion. "Who are you, show yourself!” Tarrant demanded "Calm yourself, we answer to NO one, we are the beings of The VOID that you have come to seeking aid, we know why you come, so silence yourself before I grow annoyed with you, we have things to discuss.” Tarrant was having none of it. "I demand to know why you made me the brunt of some sick joke, I came here to make a proposal, and you INSULE ME!?” At first there was nothing but silence until moments later the female voice answered back. “We had our reasons, now calm yourself, we have much to talk about if you are going to take the spot of the one known as Fate.” At that Tarrant closed his mouth and with a small smile on his face he crossed his arms in front of him. “Then let us begin.” TO BE CONTINUED IN PART TWO
