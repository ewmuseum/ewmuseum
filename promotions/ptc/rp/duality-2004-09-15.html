Duality

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 (The scene is of the northern California landscape. Images flash about of the surrounding area, the massive hills that surround little towns like Lafayette and Concord, and slightly larger ones such as Berkeley. Finally, it makes it toward the tunnels and the bridge leading toward San Francisco. "Sand Dunes" by Groove Armada slowly starts up, as a jet black 1967 Ford Mustang can be seen barrelling through the tunnel. The orange lights that fill the massive concrete structure flow over the car, passing a orange flash as the car passes each one. The car makes it onto the bridge leading into the city, past a sign for the Treasure Island exit. The camera moves toward the side of the car, as it shows the beautiful night, the moon watching over all below with its magnificent light, a glowing orb for all to see. The camera fades to black, and fades back in, as now it sits in the park-like area off of the Golden Gate Bridge, the camera showing the magnificence of the bridge illuminated at the dead of night. A young man sits on the hood of the black Mustang in the parking lot, smoking a cigarette and looking up toward the stars and moon. "All the People" by Money Mark pumps out rather loudly from the stereo system, as the young man rests there, decked out in somewhat-baggy jeans, a navy blue t-shirt reading "I Swear, I Didn't Know She Was 3", black DC sneakers, and a DC black knitted skullcap. His blonde-streaked hair is spiked up, and his face is that of complete indifference, the cigarette hanging off his lower lip. He looks as if he could try out and win the part of the main character of "Varsity Blues 2", yet one might never know that he is in fact half-Japanese, half-American. His good All-American looks however don't stop him from offending others, as a couple in their mid-40s give a look to him, as if to say turn down the music. In return, he gives them a scornful look and flicks the ashes in their general vicinity, as they grumble and walk away. He chuckles to himself, as he feels proud of his accomplishment. For he is Triumph Frost, and he probably doesn't give a good goddamn what you think about him. He sits there for a beat, as a young hot blonde dressed in a white spaghetti-strap top, pink shorts, a pink Versace bag, and black raised sandals jumps up onto the car with him to sit next to him. She grips at his arm, resting her head on his shoulder, as she sighs, but he remains indifferent. The young woman, a stunning model-like beauty that happens to be his wife, seems fully sated, yet he looks to be the opposite, anxious and unneeding of her attention. The two of them look out toward the bridge, as she seems to revel in the moment while he just puffs away at his cigarette.) Triumph Frost: What the hell we doin' here, Alexis? Alexis Frost: Ahh.....just enjoying the scenery.... (Alexis continues in her state of ecstasy, as Triumph groans and continues to look out, puffing away at the cancer stick.) Triumph Frost: I'm gettin' increasingly pissed with these decisions you're makin' for me, you know.... Alexis Frost: Well, you want "great" exposure, right? You talk all this stuff...so I'm doin' it for you. Triumph Frost: ....fine. What's this dick's name, anyways? Alexis Frost: His name is Steven Caldera. Triumph Frost: ...Caldera? He sounds like an 80's hair band frontman. Christ....of all the suck-ass luck in some random-ass draw. Alexis Frost: I think it's worse for you! This guy is pretty good. He's had a lot of experience, he's very popular and respected and--- Triumph Frost: Yeah, yeah, yeah. I don't really care. I don't plan on watching anything he has to say or what he does. I'm not preparing for the Steelers defense here. It's wrestling, for chrissake! Alexis Frost: Umm....Triumph, I don't think you shou-- Triumph Frost: Just 'cause some monkey men actually fight and make it somewhat far in some obscure tournament filled with jokes and hacks and third-grade dropouts with pretentious names and cliched stories and idealistic visions don't mean I'm gonna try and "prepare" for this. It's not that hard to beat someone's head in. These assholes show they can have the intelligence level of a gnat and still become popular. Granted, accountants and all their friends will rob them blind of all their riches, but they deserve it anyways. It's their own fault for being stupid. Well, that and their families for breeding and sending their defenseless and naive young out into the wild to be eaten alive by predators like me and George English. Besides, these people and their tournaments. That shit irks me to no end, you know? Alexis Frost: People are gonna get the wrong idea and you're gonna piss the wrong peo-- Triumph Frost: A tournament. Of nobodies. A tournament, comprised up of people that shouldn't be allowed to operate heavy machinery or walk down the street without being accompanied by a parent or guardian or someone who makes sure they don't walk into oncoming traffic like fuckin' lemmings. Alexis Frost: I think you should at least focus on Caldera. You don't have any experience in something like this, and he's expected to go very far in this tournament and--- Triumph Frost: Yeah, yeah...but he hasn't made it far in his own life. Besides, something like this is like workin' at McDonalds. No experience necessary. I don't need experience to pound this fuckface's nose all the way through the back of his skull. I don't need experience to walk all over him, just like everyone else in his life likely has. I don't need experience to crush every single hope, dream, wish, aspiration, or desire this dickhead has mustered over an "illustrious" career. He interests me just as much as a network programming new fall lineup. (There's a slight pause, as Alexis looks toward him, serious enough to talk game.) Alexis Frost: Well, if you happen to be curious on some strategy, he supposedly has some injury problems. Bad knees. Triumph Frost: He's a gimp? (Triumph lets out a groan, as he looks up toward the sky, as if pondering something deeper.) Triumph Frost: Oh man...this just doesn't get any better. Alexis Frost: Triumph...I understand what you wanna do...but are you willin' to live with permanently injuring someone? Triumph Frost: .....Yeah. Yeah, I'd be fine with it. Alexis Frost: Jesus, Tri. That's too much. What would people think? Triumph Frost: I dunno. Let's find out. (Triumph jumps down off of the car and finds some random couple walking along. He grabs the guy and stops him. At 6'5", Triumph intimidates the guy at first, as he stands there gripping the guy's arm to stop him. Alexis meanwhile rubs her eyes, shaking her head in disbelief.) Triumph Frost: Hey. Is it right to do somethign like that? Random Person: ...huh? Triumph Frost: Put someone in the hospital? Random Person: Wha.....Well, uhh....it depends, I guess. Why are you asking? Triumph Frost: Say I was fighting someone that had bad knees, some kind of surgery jargon or blah blah shit. I fought him, and I went right for his knees. I made sure he couldn't walk again. Would that be okay? Alexis Frost: Let's go now, Triumph. Triumph Frost: Wait, wait. I wanna hear what this guy has to say. Random Person: (after long pause) ...I guess it would-- Triumph Frost: There. He said yes. Happy? (Alexis sighs, as she walks off. Triumph shakes the guy's hand and follows her as she walks off. He stops her, getting in front of her as she looks visibly upset.) Triumph Frost: C'mon...what's wrong? Alexis Frost: I....I don't want to see you injure someone... Triumph Frost: Awww....Well....okay. (She smiles, as he wipes the tears out of her eyes that are beginning to stream down. Triumph Frost: Then you'll just have to stay home, woncha? (Triumph laughs, as she gets pissed and storms off. He continues laughing, as he stops her, seemingly convincing her to get into the car with him. He revvs it up, annoying just about everyone nearby, as he speeds off out of the parking area, nearly side-swiping another car. He goes roaring off like a bat out of hell through the San Francisco highway, as the camera watches him leave. With that, the scene fades.) --------------------------------- (The scene is that of southern California. The 405, one of the more travelled highways, especially in the greater Los Angeles area, is where Triumph Frost is, driving down in his Mustang. He seems content a bit at the moment, smoking a cigarette and bobbing his head to M.F. Doom's "Arrow Root", with Alexis at his side gabbing away on a cell phone to God-knows-who. Suddenly, out of nowhere, someone in a beige Lexus cuts him off, as the Lexus drives rather hastily off ahead of him. Triumph begins to boil, as his face gains looks, that of malice and discontent. He begins to speed up, gripping tightly at the steering wheel, as he begins to catch up to the Lexus. Alexis Frost: Triumph, don't. Please! (Triumph just growls, as he starts to swerve in and out of lanes to catch up. Finally he makes it to the guy, who is also on his cellphone. Triumph looks over at the guy, and rolls down his window, throwing him cigarette Alexis Frost: Tri-umph! (The guy is surprised, as he rolls down his window and gives Triumph a "what's up" kinda look. Triumph just begins to scream without hesitation.) Triumph Frost: Hey! You fuckin' asshole! ...Yeah, you! Learn to drive you piece of shit! You almost killed us you fuckin' dick! ....Yeah! Pull over and say that, you prick! C'mon, motherfucker! Do somethin'! Fuckin' do something! (The guy seems a bit rattled, as he just rolls up his window and acts like he's ignoring the angered Triumph. But nonetheless he continues yelling.) Triumph Frost: Yeah, that's what I thought! Roll up your leased Lexus window and shut your fuckin' mouth, stupid! (Triumph pumps on the horn as he speeds off, giving the guy the finger as he barrels off down the highway, swerving in and out of traffic again. He turns up the music louder and lets a huge smile wash over his face, quietly mumbling to himself about how great he was, while Alexis looks rather embarassed. Almost prematurely, it seems, the scene fades... The scene reappears at a small gym overlooking the beach of Newport, California. With a glass window showing out onto the beach, it is a rather posh facility, with state-of-the-art equipment that would make the Governator jealous. The camera moves through the facility, and seemingly finds the person it was looking for. Triumph Frost is wearing a white tank top/wifebeater-style shirt and Nike sports pants, as his hands are wrapped up in tape. Working with a punching bag, people around him are working on various equipment. "So Long" by Mr. Scruff is pumping out in the background, as Triumph stops and notices the camera, taking a second to stop and shoot it a smile. He looks as if he was expecting it, but goes back to the bag, letting out some much needed...tension.) Triumph Frost: Training. You always need to train. Me? I'm training for this "big" important match. Normally, I don't need to train, and especially not for this match either, but I just get a feeling to do something to pass the time between sending this guy into the intensive care ward of his local neighborhood hospital. (Triumph lays in a barrage of fists, as suddenly he grabs the bag as it flies toward him. He stops, looking down to the ground, as he looks over to the camera with a serious look upon his face.) Triumph Frost: You know what? We're all adults here, so let me be discreet and extremely honest to this guy...who's name happens to escape me at the moment. But luckily for me, I seem to think he has a controlling interest in what I have to say, so he's probably watching this right now. (Triumph smashes into the bag with a right cross, pushing the punching bag backwards. He turns to the camera, a demeanor of true anger.) Triumph Frost: Asshole, I'm gonna make your first match in this joke of an event an absolute living hell. And yes, I figure you wanna try and beat my ass just as bad as the next loser I'll be goin' up against, but the point is I'm not afraid of you. I'm not afraid of what you think you have to offer, I'm not afraid of who you're friends with. If you tried and pulled strings with your "pull" to get ahead, it wouldn't surprise me one bit. Jokes like you use what marginal talents you have and abuse them to no end. It gets so bad that people forget that you do, in fact, suck dick more than a high-priced whore at an electronics convention. And yet...by the time figure out you have no talent, you have them dispersed of so the secret never gets out that you're just another faceless hack. Sure, you abused every resource you had at your past federations...maybe you even started one to just state your overtly obvious pretentious behaviour to the world, even though it didn't need to be done. You blow your own horn around like you're king of the hill or...cock of the walk or somethin'. Well, you are in fact a cock, that's for sure, but not a big one. A tiny shriveled up 80-year-old cock that tries to preserve it's life by injecting itself full of drugs and tryin' for one last run. And even though I do in fact realize the folly by sounding very...err...odd...by what I just described...it is in fact a true analogy to your fake excuse for a fuckin' life. Hell, I don't even know you or anything even remotely relating to you, and already I've got you pegged...so what does that say about your tired ol' washed-up has-been never-will-be-again muthahumpin' ass? Not a whole helluva lot, huh? Nah...you ain't complex...you're not complicated. You're not even special enough to disperse yourself from the pack. I know for a fact that you aren't better than me, especially. You have no discerning qualities. Your demeanor is weak, your skills are overplayed. I'm not even sure how old you are, but it's not old enough to be fighting me in the first round. You're gonna need all the help you can get, like an oxygen tank and a wheelchair. Luckily, people who run this might even take pity, and provide those necessary items. Hell, they may not, seeing how they run this joke of a tournament. I mean, hell, I didn't find out I was in this. I had to cancel an autograph session, turn down watching "Saved By The Bell" completely drunk off my ass, driving around town and finding a good dive bar to start a fight in...I had shit I had to do. Very unorganized...but I simply digress. The fact is I know that I'm better than you. Everyone else pretty much has a feel that I'm better than you. Your family knows that this is a mistake, and that you're in for a heap of trouble, old man... And yet, I get this feeling in my stomach of everyone's uncertainty. And it pisses me off....PISSES me off. To absolute no end... (Triumph stops and takes a swig from a bottle of water sitting nearby. He wipes off his brow, as he squints out as if to catch hi breath. He takes another drink from the bottle, and whips it off, almost hitting two people in the boxing ring trying to get into a sparring match. They look over to him, as he just glares, and looks back toward the camera.) Triumph Frost: Understand this, fool. It continually becomes painfully obvious that people don't respect me. Frankly, I don't need respect, admiration, and other petty traits that loser fanboys continually exhibit so that egomaniac sociopaths like yourself can feel better about their pathetic accomplishments. I don't need respect because I know I'm good. I know what talent I possess and that I can go farther than anyone at any time if I damn well feel like it. It's a natural gift, and I've come to accept it, as should everyone else. But they don't, and I don't care. To be typecasted like this....man. I know what I can...no wait, fuck that. I know what I will be doing, and it's makin' it all the goddamned way. But does anyone acknowledge that fact? Does anyone think I can make it? Does anyone believe I have an inkling of a chance? Huh? Do they? (Triumph smashes the bag with a front kick that sends a reverberating echo throughout the gym. He turns to the camera afterwards, the bag swaying back and forth near him.) Triumph Frost: No! Of course not! Instead I have to listen to some bullshit! Constantly! Jesus, nothing but underestimation. You and your fuckin' stupid friends jabber on I bet, about how great you think you are or how far you plan on goin'...How you're gonna defeat everyone like sanctemonious pricks, whining about how good....grrr.... (Triumph lays into the bag again, a furious flurry of punches and kicks, as he stops, letting the bag fly behind him.) Triumph Frost: It's shits like you, that--- (Suddenly, the bag smashes into Triumph, startling him for a moment. People have begun to notice his misfortunate accident, a few even snickering. The look upon his face, however, becomes that of true malevolence in it's purest form.) Triumph Frost: God-DAMNIT!!! (Triumph swings around with a mighty blast, so much that it sends the bag crashing toward the ground, snapping the chain that hung the bag, as it hits the ground with a mighty thud. People who weren't already watching his barrage now have turned their full attention to Triumph, and those who thought his accident was funny no longer have smiles on their faces, as he walks right up to the camera, grabbing it by the lens and getting his face into full view.) Triumph Frost: I'm gonna make this short. I'm gonna hurt you. I'm gonna make it a very bad comeback experience. I will win. It's not a threat or even a promise.... It's a fuckin' guarantee. (Triumph pushes the camera backwards, as he grabs his towel off the bench and swings it over his shoulders. He walks off, as he sees some people who were watching. He stops and turns toward them, as they quickly act like they have something else to do, going back to their various machines. Triumph grunts, as he walks off, and they turn their attention back to him, mumbling stuff as he walks off. With that, the scene fades.)
