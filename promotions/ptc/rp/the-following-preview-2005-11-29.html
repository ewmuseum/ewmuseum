The Following Preview…

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 The following preview has been rated R by the Motion Picture Association of America for violence, adult language, and adult content. In the town of Aurora, all has been peaceful for the last decade. It is a town known mostly for its tabloid stories and folklore of Big Foot and giant sea serpents. But over the years those tales have been falsified by experts. Scientist: -holding up a photograph – What we have here is a digitally enhanced photo. You can see how the colors sort of overlap each other, a nice work, but certainly not scientific evidence. Man: That line you see swimming in the water is nothing more than a fallen log. At a distance it looks like a remarkable creature, but there is nothing remarkable about it. Aurora has remained peaceful and outside the microscope, that is until one man arrived. He is the world’s greatest known scientist, Dr. Colin Gilchrist. Dr. Gilchrist: -press conference – I can not tell you how glad I am to be in such a beautiful town as Aurora. I promise you, today marks a new day in the town of Aurora – a day of advancement, success, and science! Dr. Colin Gilchrist is warm, intelligent, charismatic, and a family man. Dr. Gilchrist is shown posing for photos alongside his family. Dr. Colin Gilchrist is insane, deranged, evil, and calculating. Dr. Gilchrist is shown smirking over his research center where dozens of men are strapped to their cots. And now an entire town must prepare for the grotesque outcomes of one man’s perverted obsessions. For this time, the horror isn’t made up. It’s real. *cue eerie action music* Veronica: You promised me! Dr. Gilchrist: -slapping her- Shut up! Shut up! Shut up! A frightened man is seen standing at the edge of a large pool. In the dark distance, a shadowy creature rises above the waters in majestic fashion. The man quivers in his boots. Man: T-That’s… Dr. Gilchrist: That’s right. Man: You found… Dr. Gilchrist: I found it. And now it obeys me. A slow zoom in into a dark cell reveals a pale man with elongated features. He growls like an animal and the only thing human about him is his ability to walk upright. Man: What is that? Dr. Gilchrist: Someone I like you to meet. Man: Why are you doing this? Dr. Gilchrist: I don’t know. I just know I can. Man screams. But one man will arise above all odds to confront the doctor, detective Clark Madison. But for all his experiences and hard work, Clark Madison will learn that the real task won’t be getting to the doctor, but getting the town on his side. Clark Madison is overlooking a mauled body at a death scene. Mr. Madison: A suicide? Who ruled this suicide? Cop: I did. There’s no sign of a struggle, no other DNA prints, or fingerprints. Mr. Madison: You’re telling me he was able to slice his own throat with a broken arm? Clark is visiting Dr. Gilchrist at his home. Clark: You’re coming down Dr. Gilchrist. One way or another, all this is going to end. Dr. Gilchrist: You are trespassing on private property detective. But I can let that go. But do not come here and threaten me again. Clark: I’ll do more than that. A masked man is seen with his hands raised in the GTT5 ring. Announcer: And the winner is…the Savior. Commentator: The masked man wins, yet again. Folks, he is seriously becoming a major competitor in this year’s GTT. Demetri Vieri, Dr. Gilchrist’s assistant, is seen holding a mask in his hands and rubbing his head from a severe headache. Demetri: W-What happened? I-I can’t remember a damn thing. Police cars are surrounding Dr. Gilchrist’s home. On the roof is Demetri Vieri. Demetri: All you…just leave me alone! Detective Clark Madison arrives home to see his wife, Molly Madison laughing uncontrollably with a knife in her hand. Clark: Honey! Put that down, please! Molly: It’s okay sweetie. The doctor told me it was for my own good. Clark: NOOOOOOOOOOO! Dr. Colin Gilchrist is seen on a chair looking down at his bulging arms. His veins appear like they are going to pop and his skin goes through a series of discolorations. Dr. Gilchrist: It was all worth it. All of them. The projects, the research, the deaths…they were all worth it. Dr. Gilchrist stands in the middle of the ring holding up the GTT championship… Dr. Gilchrist: I’ve already won. Project Eos: The Gilchrist Story. …coming soon to GTT5.
