TTD A3 -v- A4

5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a Camry...a freaking Toyota Camry. A piddly little piece of metal, compared to his Chevy pickup truck. There’s no reason it should have happened like this, none at all. A small car, a large truck...but it came out of nowhere, ran a red light, and blindsided him. T-boned, they call it. And it had landed him in this hospital room. The accident not only threatened his head with a concussion and his left arm with a hairline fracture, but it also threatened two of the most important matches in the history of the Lunchroom Patrol. They were slated to defend their XUW tag team championship at XUW’s biggest pay-per-view event of the year, Reborn. And, the following week, they were scheduled for their first round match in PTC’s tag team tournament. But now he finds himself in a hospital, bruised, shaken, and fighting against a doctor who refuses to release him or clear him to wrestle. This wasn’t supposed to happen to him. Not to the Latch Key Kid, someone who had been fighting his whole life to get to the top. It was nothing to new him, however, as he had been facing adversity ever since he joined the ranks of XUW. During his time in XUDF, the developmental federation, he faced a constant struggle to maintain a place at the top, a top he never reached, never having won either of the titles in XUDF. But that changed, though, at the first ever call-up match to get to XUW. LKK found himself squaring off against the XUDF United States champion, DarkSeid, and the XUDF North American champion, Doughboy. Doughboy, with whom he formed a friendship and an alliance, and later, a tag team. On that night, LKK was the underdog. But true to his underdog spirit, he rallied and refused to succumb to the pressure, and he defeated the two champions to become the first XUDF-bred wrestler to be called up to XUW. Doughboy: Hello?..........An accident? Is he-........Which hospital?.........I'll be there in a bit. A Camry...it just came out of nowhere, blindsided me. There was no reason it should have happened like that, but that’s life, I guess. Just goes to show, you’re always susceptible to small challenges, even when you’re bigger and mightier than them. A pick-up truck and a compact car. I can’t believe I didn’t win that one. Maybe it really can happen at any time. Somebody comes out of nowhere and surprises you, and takes what matters most to you. My chances of wrestling in that match, gone...if you listen to the doctor. Good thing I don’t. Perhaps it was fitting, then, that one-half of XUW’s tag team champions was laid up in a hospital due to an accident caused by a vehicle much smaller than his own. Finally, after reaching the top and cementing his place in XUW lore, he was struck down by the underdog. A Camry against a Chevy pick-up? What are the odds? Pretty freaking good in this instance, unfortunately. He and Doughboy were the underdogs in their very first match as a tag team. Doughboy had just been called up to XUW, and he and LKK were scheduled to take on Blake Wolfson and the Anarchist, the original Ultra Violents, at XUW's Cause and Effect pay-per-view. Blake was able to turn the match into an Ultra Violent rules match, and as such, all rules went out the window. They went toe-to-toe with Wolfson and the Anarchist, and, while they did not emerge without wounds, scars and brutal memories, neither did the Ultra Violents. They had been matched by a couple of rookies, a couple of upstart young punks, and they had nearly lost to them. And they knew it. When Wolfson and the Anarchist offered their respect to the Lunchroom Patrol after the match, LKK and Doughboy knew they belonged. Not just in XUW, but at the top of XUW. Doughboy: I hope it's nothing serious. We've got a lot of big matches coming up and I can't win them on my own. Sheesh, why the hell did he have to go and have an accident? I swear, I've known doctors who spent less time in the hospital than Brad. If he's gone and screwed stuff up for us I'll have to kick his ass. We've worked to damn hard to lose our belts to the likes of Cutter and Eliminator or any of those other teams. And this PTC tourney. Man, this is the chance of a lifetime. We could be the first guys from XUW to hold a PTC title, and that's a f*cking big deal. When I get to that hospital I'll do whatever it takes to make sure he'll be out of there on time. Good morning, Brad. How are you feeling today? I...ready for Reborn. Brad, you should slow down. I don’t think you can count on wrestling at Reborn. Not next Sunday. Tag tournament. No, there won't be a tournament for you, not anytime soon. I’m sorry, Brad. Doc, there’s no way around it. I’m going to be there. You’ll see. Brad, you’ll be in the hospital next Sunday. Maybe so, but it’ll take one of those other teams to put me back here, because I will make it to that match. Brad, I need to go check on other patients, but I’ll be back to see you later. Just get some rest. You need it. Bye, doc. Some time later, the Lunchroom Patrol met up once again with the Ultra Violents. This time, however, the Ultra Violents were XUW tag team champions, so there was more importance placed on the match. The battle was close, the rookies had their chance, but in the end, they fell just short thanks to the lead pipe wielded by the Shadow, an old nemesis of theirs and the newest Ultra Violent. After that, the Lunchroom Patrol regrouped. XUW’s prestigious Stable Wars tournament was approaching, and they were placed on a team with two other XUDF Alumni. It was a reluctant team and perhaps their haphazard nature doomed them from the start. After winning their first round match at Stable Wars, Team XUDF Alumni fell to the Ultra Violents. Doughboy: And here we go, Memorial Hermann Hospital. I've spent more than a few hours wandering around these halls. Of course, it hasn't always been the same halls. It sure wasn't fun up in that psych ward last summer but we thought it would be for the best. Of course we thought the same thing about letting him out at the end of that week too. Heh, I guess in some ways it was for the best. After all, if Latch had stayed locked up we would've had to forfeit our title shot and who knows when we would've gotten another one. Overall though, him getting out and us winning caused a lot more trouble than we needed. Hell, that bad decision almost broke up the team. Lucky for him I was obsessed with playing this perfect character that would never do anything wrong and who would never turn his back on a friend. I was so naive back then, thinking that all I had to do was continually play up to the fans and I'd be instantly accepted by everyone. Oh, it worked for a while. The people loved to cheer for me because I gave them exactly what they wanted and I even had plenty of friends around me. In fact, everyone was happy except for a single person. Me. The real me. Matt Dixon, the man behind the created facade of Doughboy. And now, nearly a year later, LKK finally knew what DarkSeid felt like during that week before Stable Wars. All DarkSeid wanted to do was get out of that retreat camp and get away from the Lunchroom Patrol. Now, all LKK wanted to was get out of this hospital and get away from Doctor Brown. He wanted to wrestle at Reborn and in the PTC tournament. He needed to wrestle in those contests. He would wrestle in those contests. Even if he had to escape. This wasn’t Latch’s first stint in a hospital. Prior to the match in which they would win their first XUW tag team championship, Latch spent some time in a different hospital. Well, the same hospital actually, but a very different ward of the hospital. The psych ward. The week was filled with halucinations, but in the end he was able to convince the doctor that he was well enough to be released, a decision the doctor would come to regret. When push came to shove in that three-way match , Latch had no problem resorting to shove. With the referee unconscious, Latch grabbed a steel chair and began swinging at everybody in sight. They all went down, even his own partner, but he did it. He covered Danyon, and the referee counted to three, and suddenly, the Lunchroom Patrol was on top of XUW’s tag team ranks. He slipped into a fit of dementia that lasted for months. The Lunchroom Patrol continued to wrestle and defend their tag team titles, and Latch continued to struggle with his inner demons. Doughboy never turned his back on his partner and friend, and constantly struggled to bring Latch back around. On the other side, there was good old DarkSeid, who struggled to the opposite of Doughboy. DarkSeid was the imp on Latch’s shoulder, the voice of hatred and despair. DarkSeid struggled to pull Latch over to his side, furthering the rift in the Lunchroom Patrol. This led to a three-way match, LKK vs. Doughboy vs. DarkSeid. The winner would be awarded both tag team title belts, and would have to pick a partner of his choosing. In another example of the underdog triumphing, LKK once again defeated both Doughboy and DarkSeid, to become the single tag team champion. After much hesitation, and a period of great controversy, Latch realized that he couldn’t turn his back on the partner who never turned his back on him. He picked Doughboy, sending even greater hatred through DarkSeid. Doughboy: I was able to keep things under control for some time. Sure, Matt was getting harder and harder to keep suppressed, but I had to keep up as Doughboy. The people wouldn't love me if they found out that I was a normal person with flaws just like all of them. So I kept it up. All of the balls that I was juggling stayed in the air, sometimes just barely, but they stayed there until a new ball, one named Timmy, was tossed into the mix. Timmy, the son I never knew I had, or at least so I was led to believe. Yeah, yeah, it sounds pretty cliched, but I'm telling you that nobody was more shocked than I was about this. His mother Jen had been going out with a good friend of mine a while back and when they had a huge fight I ended up comforting her for a night. It was one of the moments in my life that I was least proud of and I thought it was buried forever. Boy was I wrong. Imagine how this left me feeling. I'd spent almost a year and a half building up this reputation in the XUW family as the ultimate good guy who did no wrong. Suddenly both I and all of my fans were confronted with damning evidence that I was far from perfect. The walls of my facade were cracking. He would escape, of course, by playing a prank on Nurse Julie, the attractive yet dimwitted nurse tasked with caring for LKK during his hospital stay. He fashioned a rope out of bed sheets, and formed a body of sheets and towels, which he concealed with a blanket in bed. Even the paper plate with a drawn-on smiley face fooled Nurse Julie. He did make it back to the XUW arena in time to get to Reborn. He came through for his partner. The match would take place. DarkSeid vowed revenge on the Lunchroom Patrol, and after an elaborate mind game trick, DarkSeid revealed his new partner to be none other than the returning Shadow. Thus was formed the Dark Alliance, one of the most vile and hated tag teams in XUW history. Their first match would be against the Lunchroom Patrol, for the XUW tag team titles and once again the dark men used their trusty weapons to steal away a victory. They held the belts until the Lunchroom Patrol earned a rematch, this time with the stipulation that the losing team would not be given another tag title shot for the rest of 2004. With everything on the line, the Lunchroom Patrol reached out, regained the spirit that once drove them, and defeated the Dark Alliance, members of the Ultra Violents, in an Ultra Violents rules match. Finally, they had defeated the Ultra Violents at their own game. Doughboy: And then came the final straw. Just when I've started to accept the changes in my life and have begun to enjoy them Jen tells me that Tim might not be mine after all and then disappears. Well, that did it. Pain like that cuts you to the core and totally changes everything. At that moment the real emotions inside of me swelled up and I was unable to maintain my perfect image any longer. Everything came pouring out, years of repressed frustration, anger, rage, and all sorts of things. I'll tell you know, it wasn't a pretty sight and things still aren't much better. Perhaps I'd be ok if I had some friends to talk with, but despite what they might think I don't have any. Everyone around me who acts so nice to me doesn't really know me. They know the image of Doughboy, not the reality of Matt Dixon, so I'm left alone in a crowd of people. And now, they had a four-way match at Reborn, to defend their tag team championship. Their opponents would be X-Factor and the Fool, Ric Austin and the Amazing Playboy Steven St. John, and Robert Cutter and "Eliminator" Danny Williams. The Lunchroom Patrol was confident in themselves heading into Reborn and that confidence was well-founded as they left Boston with their tag titles intact. The next stop is the PTC Tag Title Tournament, and despite their recent struggles, they would be ready for that, as well. Doughboy: So now I must rebuild. I must find out who I really am and begin to make everyone else realize that things are different now. No longer can I afford to suppress my true self in order to try and win over the fans. From this day forward you will be seeing the reality of Matt Dixon, the good, the bad, and the ugly. You say you don't like how I'm acting now? Well I say that I don't give a damn. I only have one thing left at this moment and that's the XUW Tag Team Titles, and if you want them you're going to have to pry them away from my cold, dead hands. Those belts aren't enough though, so I've set my sights on a new goal, the PTC Tag Team Titles. I don't care what teams stand in our path, they're going to get taken to school by the Lunchroom Patrol. The Latch Key Kid isn't in the hospital anymore and we've put Reborn behind us. Top Shelf, you're the next target in our sights. Bring your best shots, 'cause I guarantee that we will rise to the challenge.
