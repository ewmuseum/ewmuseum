The amazing spider, man

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-21 Ah, there you are again. I suppose though that I shouldn’t be too surprised. Part of me, the egotistical part of course, would like to take credit for your return by assuming that it is simply a desire to once more bask in the glory that is my voice. The sumptuous delight that is to be had in the sound of my voice, and the play of my mind. Of course to assume is to make an ass out of you and me, so I won’t do that, instead I will let the more rationale part of me take the fore and admit that you are here because you have to be. You are a captive audience my dear friend, and while I suppose I should find it in me to feel sympathy for you I have to admit that I don’t. This way you have to listen to me. So to give a brief recap you now know who I am in some small way; you also know that I am here to represent the WWA in this little tete a tete that is amusingly called fourteen of the best. I could point out that really there can’t be fourteen of the best, there can only be THE best and thirteen runners ups, but I won’t … I am not THAT crass you know. I think that I have finally gotten my head around the fact that in just a few short days I am going to be involved in not one but two PTC matches. Go me! … hmm I really must work on that cheer-leading as even I didn’t really believe that one. Anyway, as I was saying in the very near future I am going to find my inter-fed virginity is snatched from me, and I am going to be so wanton as to not be satisfied with just one experience, oh no, I am going to go for two! The pedants among you will probably point out that it is actually one tournament, one event, so as such I should only claim to be taking part in one thing … but hey I wasn’t crass enough to point out the misnomer in the title of the tournament, so you guys can just shut up and let me have my moment here ok? Good, glad that we got that sorted. So, yes ... I will be having a one on one match against the legend in his own mind that is Scott Monroe first of all and then after that I will be entering into the ring once more to face six other people in an over the top extravaganza. Who these people are, who my opponents will be, is still unknown, though of course one could hazard a guess as to … … sorry, what was that? Hmmm … ok I suppose it IS a little presumptuous of me to state that I will be in the final and that Scoot won’t because that implies that I will beat him and progress. There is a saying that goes that the only definite things in life are death and taxes … well I would like to add a third thing to that list if I may? Well even if I don’t have your permission I am going to do it anyway. From now on the only definite things in life are death, taxes and the fact that I will beat Scott Monroe. How can I be so sure about that fact? Well there is of course the simple fact that I KNOW how good I am, I know just what I am capable of and just where my natural skills, talents and expertise inside the ring can take me. I also know what the past has taught me and how to use that experience to my own ends to pull out the best of my technique in order to triumph. … on top of that I cheat. Seriously though, I have looked into my opponent, my little Scooter, and I have to admit that I am less than impressed. I had thought that it was going to be hard to find anyone outside the WWA who could inspire me less, but Monroe has managed to do just that. I am not sure if that is a good thing or a bad thing of course, it is like getting the worst dressed award at the Oscars. On one hand it is an embarrassing epitaph to have on one’s gravestone, but on the other hand it is … at least … an accolade! Go you Scooter, you have won something already, so you can go home happy now! Of course I realise that I am opening myself up for some abuse with my words, for accusations of arrogance and conceit … well the truth is that I AM pretty arrogant, I AM pretty conceited. The difference between me and countless faceless wannabes who proclaim their greatness with no actual way of backing it up is two fold. In the first instance I KNOW that I sound just like those others, I know that my words are like the prancing of the peacock, strutting it’s colors in the hope of attracting attention. The second, and more important factor is simple: I am good enough to back up my words. Yes, I KNOW that everyone says that as well, but I fully intend to prove myself true in the next few days, and Scott Monroe lying beaten at my feet will be the exclamation mark of my statement. Sorry Scooter, but I have looked into your eyes, I have looked into your soul, and I have found you wanting … I have found you lacking … I have found you a disappointment. I would love to say that I have found you even a little interesting or worrying, but that would be a lie, and I have found that the truth is MUCH better than lies. Like I said previously, the truth shall set us free, and the truth is that I have already beaten you Scooter. The match hasn’t even begun yet but already the war is over. Do you know why Scooter … can you fathom it? … it is because I am not afraid of you! You se one of the best and worst aspects of this tournament is that there is an element of fear involved in it ... or at least concern. We didn’t know who we were going to face, and we still have no idea of who we WILL face in the final … but I do know who I am GOING to face to start with, and that knowledge is enough to give me a warm and fluffy feeling deep inside me. … not quite the same warm and fluffy feeling as watching an Angelina Jolie movie late at night you understand, but it will do for now. The fear though, the anxiety, the anticipation of meeting you in battle … it just isn’t there. Now I know that there are people in this tournament who are supposedly worthy of fear, but even there I am not quite sure why. The name Karina Wolfenden seems to evoke an air of defeat before the first blow is struck. It seems that most people assume that the little slip of a girl will walk all over everyone else in this thing and come out the victor … or is that victoress? … without breaking a sweat. Why is that though? What makes her special? Yes, I know that she has one half of the tag team belts around her waist, but could that not be a fluke, could that not be the work of her partner? Sure I know that she has had some success in other places around the World but the way that her name is mentioned with complete terror in case someone has to face her baffles me to be honest. … it is almost like she has a history that my eyes cannot see, like she is the second coming of the Messiah. Maybe someone has checked out her past life and unlike most people who claim to be the Queen of Sheeba, Karina is actually a former World class wrestler in a previous incarnation. … stranger things, after all, have happened. Each and every entrant in this tournament seems to have something in their past, something in their repertoires, that evokes fear in their opponents, fear in the people who may have to face them. …. But fear is an interesting thing isn’t it? I mean you can be afraid of so many things after all, and that fear can affect the way that you live your life. An agoraphobic f or example would not be able to set foot outside their own house, no matter the circumstances. Let the walls burn down around them in a blazing inferno and even THAT would not be as terrifying as the open spaces outside their front door. Trypanophobia means that no matter the reason … be it a life saving blood transfusion, an antidote to a life-threatening poison or even to save the life of their own child … a person’s fear of needles would send them screaming into the hills. So many others, some understandable … guns, snakes, fire … some not so … clouds, clowns, the number thirteen. Whatever the fear, whatever the reason, it can rule our lives if we but let it … as it seems to be doing to some, if not all, of the people who will be taking part in this tournament. Each and everyone in this tournament has something to fear … and perhaps gives something to be feared. Karina has an aura that evokes fear, AWS Man has a name that does the same. Oni brings us the oft misunderstood fear of clowns while woof woof actually makes me concerned in case he is not house trained. Through it all though they all have two fears in common. The first is called triskadekaphobia … the fear of the number thirteen. Pretty understandable really when you think about it, as they don’t really know which of the other thirteen wrestlers will make it through to the final, so no-one really knows what is coming next … and so fear of the unknown infects each and every one of them. Deadening their reactions, filling their stomachs with bile and their minds with nightmares. Which suits me just fine to be honest, because the more that they fear the more chance they have of making a mistake. ... and the more chance I have of winning this whole damn thing. As for me though I AM the unknown, I am the one person in this tournament that no-one knows about, that no-one CAN know about. I am the x-factor, I am the ghost in the machine, I am the thing that goes bump in the night. I AM the thing to be feared. Why is that? Simple, just as the truth will set us free I know the truth of the matter in this as all things. There is nothing to be afraid of but fear itself. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ … before he realised what it was that he was doing his finger squeezed. [ONE HOUR AGO] The bell above the door chimed out it’s ringing welcome, announcing to all that heard it that the shop had a customer. The noise from the busy street outside did it’s best to submerge the bell under it’s cacophony of sounds … the March wind blustering, sounding like a thousand asthmatics at a convention, between the narrow buildings that tried to contain it; the rumble of diesel engines as they passed in a seemingly never-ending convoy of gears and metal; the protesting squeal of brakes and sound of galvanised rubber scrabbling for purchase on the asphalt as two juggernauts jockeyed for position; and the constant babble of voices raised in their desire to be heard, hoping against hope that their message would be the one heard. All in all just a regular day in downtown Manhattan. The bell rang out a second time as the door hit it a glancing blow as it closed, once more ringing out it’s warning to it’s regular audience of one. “You’re late” Mathew Walters didn’t even glance up as he spoke the two words, the nasal tone of his voice as familiar to him as a favourite item of clothing. With the tip of his tongue poking out from the left corner of his mouth, his eyes tight with concentration, he focussed his entirety on the daunting task at hand. The slipping of a comic into a protective polythene bag. As his stubby fingers managed, with dexterity that was astonishing for a man of his mammoth size, to slip the comic inside the bag with the minimum of flesh to paper contact he quickly sealed it with a small piece of clear tape before holding it up with a flourish. “JLA issue 1, with a rare variant cover AND signed by Grant Morrison himself!” he stated proudly, gesticulating to the cause of his pride as his face flushed with child-like excitement. H placed the comic on the glass counter in front of him slowly, with almost religious reverence as he breathed, “… first edition Andy, FIRST EDITION!” Sharing in his friends obvious excitement, joining in with something akin to shared hysteria, Andrew Jackson reached out a trembling hand and then paused, his fingers hovering scant millimetres above the object that evoked such a reaction, the object of their shared emotion. “…does it have Morrison’s blood in the ink too?” Andrew asked tremulously as his gaze flickered up to catch the eyes of his best friend and employer. “Oh don’t be stupid Andy they didn’t need that sort of gimmicky crap for the JLA, that was reserved for the likes of the Kiss ‘so-called’ comic!” came the sarcastic snort of reply as Mat rolled his eyes. After nearly two decades of growing up together Andy still had the ability to amaze Mat with the inanity that came out of his mouth. “Morrison is meant to be a bit creepy though Mat, into all that occult stuff you know …” Andy continued, sticking to his guns in the face of Andy’s building ire. Hardly a day ever went by without a discussion … to outsiders it could have very well been termed an argument, but those outsiders didn’t know Mat and Andy the way that they knew each other … centred around an aspect of the comic or movie industry. Well the movie industry only if it was about comics of course. For the owner … and his faithful sidekick employee of course … of the ‘Four Color Emporium’, Manhattan’s premiere comic and collectibles shop (at least as they saw it), there was no better way to spend the day than in deep philosophical debate about their only love in life. “Andy, this is one of the seven best selling comic books of all time, written by one of the seven top creators since time began …” Mat started to intone. “… saying all time is a bit spurious though Andy, I mean they didn’t have comics two thousand years ago now did they?” Andy interrupted with a grin. “There is a school of thought that thinks that ancient Egyptian hieroglyphics could actually be the earliest adventures of the X-Men Andy” Mat said with a straight face, eliciting a raspberry from his friend as they both leant companionably on the counter facing each other. “… but I digress, as I was saying Morrison’s Justice League of America didn’t need any crazy hype like using blood in the ink Andy, it stood on it’s own merits. It brought together seven of the best heroes in the DC World and teamed them together for truth, for justice and …” “… to pay a weird Scottish magician a tonne of money?” Andy quipped, once again interrupting his friend, laughing as he saw the look of frustration that crossed his face. “No it wasn’t about Grant Morrison getting his pocket lined, it was about the big guns, the top guys in the World of comics, banding together … Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, Flash, Green Lantern, Aquaman and the Martian Manhuter. Seven of the best heroes … THE best heroes … ever assembled into one comic!” Mat finished, breathing slightly heavily, his tirade with a slap of his palm on the glass beside the comic as if for emphasis. The two young men stared at each other across the counter, each one waiting for the next volley to being … knowing from past experience that it would. “ … what about a few issues later when they opened their doors to everyone and they ended up with a league of fourteen heroes, fourteen more mediocre ones at that? Dilution of talent!” Andy crowed, victory … he thought … in his grasp. “Fourteen were quickly brought back to seven though Andy, the chaff were separated from the wheat so to speak, and soon enough it was back to the crème de la cream, the seven it should have been” Mat pointed out patiently, as if speaking to a child, his eyes alight with humor. “ … yeah but then they got carried away and seven became one. The way that they wrote Batman as the best of the best, the unstoppable one, they may as well have called it the Justice League of Batman. I mean why bother having seven of the best, when you can have THE best after all!” Andy pointed out, grinning at the obvious struggle that Mat was having coming up with a counter to that. “Hmmm … well that is as may be Andy, but we don’t have time to talk about this now anyway. Give me a hand and empty that crate that arrived this morning please” Mat asked sheepishly, knowing that he had lost the argument and trying to save face at the same time as he indicated a small packing crate a few feet from the counter. “Sure thing boss … what is it anyway?” Andy asked, smiling to himself in victory, as he looked closely at the crate. “A shipment I won of E-bay Andy, from some guy in Mexico. Not sure exactly what it is but there should be about one hundred mixed comics from the last twenty years in there. Could be the best or the worst fifty dollars I have spent … you never know we could get lucky and find something amazing in there!” Mat replied with a resigned tone of resignation. After five years of using E-bay, and traversing the country to attend comic fairs all over the place, he was yet to find his Holy Grail, his golden egg, of the comic world that would set him up for life. He kept on trying though. Taking the object of their earlier discussion, the bagged and sealed comic, Mat opened the glass counter and put it pride of place inside as he heard Andy opening the crate. “Well it looks like you got your fifty dollar’s worth in straw Mat, as he definitely packed this thing up nice and safe, you know I … hey look at that amazing spider, man!” Andy cried out in surprise. Closing the glass cabinet door sharply, Mat spun around, his face flushed in excitement. ‘Amazing Spider-Man’ he thought to himself. What year, what issue? Could this be his lucky day, had he struck oil? It wasn’t like Andy to get worked up over regular comics so for him to actually exclaim over an issue of Spider-Man then it had to be something special. It HAD to be. Hurrying around the counter Mat moved as quickly as possible, for one of his prodigious girth, over to where he friend crouched beside the now open crate. Placing a hand on Andy’s shoulder Mat leaned over him, staring into the shadowed crate. [TWENTY MINUTES AGO] “Arrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrggggggggggggggggggggghhhhhhhhhhhhhhh!” The scream that pierced the relative silence of the interior of the shop seemed to hang in the air with a life of it’s own even after Mat had run out of the oxygen necessary to keep it going. As Andy literally fell over, as if pole-axed, and stared up at his friend with a look of all consuming shock on his face, Mat seemed oblivious to it all. His eyes bulging, his mouth wide open as if uncomprehending that he was no longer screaming, his skin waxen, he stared into the crate. … and started to visibly shake as hairy legs, as black as night, began to creep over the edge of the crate, their movements bizarre as multi-jointed limbs … eight of them … worked in cooperation to move the tarantula out of it’s confinement. Hyperventilating, his eyes widening even more … if that was humanly possible … Mat staggered backwards as the spider dragged itself up and out of the crate, landing on the floor with an audible impact. Slightly smaller … though not by much … than a compact disc the spider appeared, to Mat’s terror fuelled brain, to fill the whole room. With purposeful and deliberate strides, the joints on the legs again making it seem like something unnatural, the spider began to take a step forward. Mat’s journey continued backwards and then suddenly the world turned upside down as his legs became entangled with something else, something that brought him tumbling to the ground in a heap of quivering flesh. Whimpering to himself Mat lay on his back, staring wide-eyed up into the lights above him and then screamed again as he felt something touch his arm. Kicking out he had the satisfaction of feeling his leg connect with something solid … but a scream that seemed to echo his own brought him back to his senses. Sitting up with some effort he stared into the accusatory eyes of Andy as he held a hand up to his bloody nose. “What the Hell did you do that for Mat, I just wanted to make sure that you were ok. That was quite a landing you did after tripping over my legs … but no need to kick me man!” Andy said nasally, his voice tight with pain. Concern played out over Mat’s face as he watched a small trickle of blood make it’s way down Andy’s face. As he reached out a hand consolingly towards his friend, a flicker of movement … barely seen out of the corner of his eye … caught his attention and brought him back to the here and now with a jolt. Scrabbling to his hands and knees, concern for Andy forgotten … Andy himself forgotten … Mat lumbered to his feet and stared wild-eyed at the area around the crate. Empty. His eyes moved from side to side, taking in the whole room as quickly as possible without actually moving. Moving would mean possibly getting closer to the … don’t think about it! Glancing at the shelves that filled the room, all of them filled with his life’s joy, his life’s work, and the shadows that fell around them, between them, under them, a cold sweat broke out on Mat’s forehead. He barely registered the fact that Andy was talking to him, couldn’t make out the words at all, as he scanned the floor for any signs of movement at all. Nothing. Where is it, Where has it gone, Is it there under the fifty cents rack … is it behind the full size cut-out of Lara Croft … is it looking at me … is it waiting to jump out and … Mat jumped again, almost seeming to levitate as his body left the ground. Twirling in mid-air like a super-sized parody of a ballet dancer he came down hard and slapped away at the ‘thing’ that had touched his arm. Yanking his hand away in shock Andy stared at his friend … or at least at the crazed figure who moments before had been his friend but was now someone that he didn’t even recognise. “… I said are you ok man?” Andy repeated for the third time, concern evident in his voice. “ … where … where is it?” Mat panted, his gaze not staying anywhere for more than a second as he tried to see everywhere at once. “What … the spider? Is that what this is Mat, are you afraid of spiders?” Andy asked as realisation hit him. Sure seeing a spider that size was a bit disconcerting but Andy had always loved spiders, he had found them to be amazing. The way that they could survive anywhere, the webs that they spun, the way that their legs moved with a gait that showed nature’s perfection at work. It was, after all, his love of spiders that had got him into comics … but no matter how hard he had tried he couldn’t get a spider to bite him and give him the same powers as his hero. “No … not afraid Andy … no afraid … terrified, I am phobic of them ... totally phobic!” Mat panted, tears forming at the corners of his eyes as the adrenaline that had flooded his body began to recede. It wasn’t that he felt any safer; it was just that his body … so unused to any form of exercise at all … had run out of the ability to keep him going. Panic and despair began to set in as he realised that the one thing in the whole world that he was afraid of … that he loathed with his whole being … was in his shop. In his home. In his sanctuary. At any other time Mat would have felt foolish for admitting his weakness to his friend, but then it is easy to be filled with bravado … easy to never voice your fears … when they are not made real in front of you. If they had talked about fears in the past, Mat had laughed and joked that the only thing that truly scared him was people who folded the corners of books and comics instead of buying a place marker; that was in the cold light of day though, that was when the REAL cause of his fear was not evident. He had never told anyone of his fear, knowing that it was irrational and feeling embarrassed that a grown man would be afraid of spiders. ‘Incy wincy spider’ … the nursery rhyme played through his head, taunting him with the memory of the taste of bile in his throat as his mother read him the mantra from a storybook and showed him the pictures that went with it. The memory of the fear was as real as that which he felt right now. He had never been able to figure out why he was afraid, what had caused it, all he knew was that he was. … and that was enough. Realising that the taste of bile was stronger than the memory Mat only had time to lurch away from his friend before his stomach emptied itself violently. Vomit erupted from his mouth in a seemingly never-ending wave and with a choking sob Mat realised that even his nostrils were filled with the stinking half solid, half liquid, mess. Feeling a hand pat his back, Mat looked sideways through bleary eyes and smile a weak thank you towards Andy. “God I am sorry Andy, but it is that … that thing …” Mat almost whimpered, unable to even say the word, “… you don’t know what it is like man, I mean I can’t even see them on the TV without freaking out let alone have one near me …” the words choked out as a racking sob filled Mat’s chest, the well of tears broken as they flooded unbidden down his cheeks. Andy stared at his friend, a mixture of real compassion for the man that was as close as a brother to him mixed with embarrassment at seeing him cry for the first time since they were both ten years old. Mat had fallen off his bike and badly skinned his knee that time, and Andy had had to get his mother to come help his sobbing friend. All that blood had freaked them both out at the time, but even then … even with a child’s limited understanding of the world ... they had both known that there was nothing too serious about the situation. Know though Andy looked into Mat’s eyes and so only total hopelessness there. Reaching into the pockets of his jeans he fumbled around for a tissue to hand to Mat, but realised that they were empty. “Listen, just stay right there while I get you some tissues from the store next door … you can’t go outside in a state like that, you look a mess, worse even than the Joker with his make-up running” Andy said gently trying to lighten the mood. “No … don’t go …” Mat whimpered, gripping Andy’s arm in desperation. “It will be ok Mat, I will get the tissues and come straight back. You just stay right here and you will be fine. Trust me. The spider is more afraid of you than you are of it you know ... and besides you can see the whole room from here” Andy pointed out. “I wouldn’t bet on it” Mat muttered to himself in reply, bringing a small smile to Andy’s face. “OK, you are probably right and you ARE more afraid but end of the day that spider cannot hurt you, you know, it can only make you feel scared … and a little fear never hurt anyone. Make like Daredevil and be the man without fear for two minutes and then when I get back I can play the hero and rescue you from the evil eight legged freak” Andy attempted the joke, but winced as he saw the look of fear in Mat’s eyes. Patting him on the back again he moved towards the door, not saying anything else for fear of saying the wrong thing. [FIVE MINUTES AGO] Mat’s eyes didn’t leave Andy’s back until the door had closed behind him, the bell trilling out. To Mat’s ears it sounded like mocking laughter and he straightened his shoulders in defiance and glared at the offending darkness of the shadowed areas of the room. Subconsciously he knew that his bravado was false, brought on by the fact that he could no longer see the spider, but for a few seconds he felt as much angry as afraid. ‘Come into my shop, my home, and scare the shit out of my like that you freak’ he thought to himself, ‘ well we will see about that!’ Taking one step after the other, moving gingerly with exaggerated care, Mat made his way back behind the counter, his eyes constantly moving, constantly scanning the floor for any sign of movement. ‘The good thing about glass’, he thought to himself with satisfaction, ‘is that you can see everything through it. Reaching down slowly, he felt his hand close around the hilt of the pistol that was on one of the shelves in the cabinet. Three months ago some jerk had held him up and cleaned out his whole days takings. After that he had bought a small gun and kept it close at hand. A small chuckle, more out of hysteria than humor, escaped his mouth in a trembling bubble as he saw the label that was beside the gun. ‘Replica of gun used by James Bond’ Hidden in plain sight. Lots of people had seen the gun, day after day and week after week, and never once realised that if they messed with him, if they came into his HOME and violated him the way that he had been violated before, that they would end up facing the business end of the pistol. “Mat Walters … licences to kill” Mat laughed to himself under his breath, suddenly feeling a little brave now that he had a loaded weapon in his hand. Cocking the gun as his instructor had shown him … giggling inanely as it took him two attempts to move the hammer with his sweaty thumb … Mat gingerly moved back to his prior location on the outside of the counter, keeping the gun moving from shadow to shadow. ‘That is right, you had BETTER not let me see you’ he though to himself with mock bravado, his fingers clenched around the butt of the gun so tightly that his knuckles were shockingly white. [NOW] The bell chimed and a draught breezed through the door as it opened, tickling the small, sweat soaked, spot at the base of Mat’s spine, like the gentle caress of a lover’s fingers ... or to Mat’s mind like the obscene hair covered legs of a large spider. Before he realised what it was that he was doing he had spun full circle to face the doorway, and the figure that stood there, open mouthed with shock and fear. Before he realised what it was that he was doing he had lifted the gun and pointed it directly at the door way and the figure that stood there, eyes widening in panic at the vision of oncoming doom. Before he realised what it was that he was doing he had aimed the gun at directly at the figure in front of him … not registering that it WAS a figure … and the image that was emblazoned on the red and blue t-shirt that he wore. … the image of a large, black spider on the Spider-Man t-shirt. … before he realised what it was that he was doing his finger began to squeeze. “Nooooooooo!” the scream that came out of Andy’s mouth was the only thing that saved him. A look of horror at what he had nearly done flashed across Mat’s sweat soaked face as he relaxed his finger on the trigger and allowed the tension to leave his body. He had nearly pulled the trigger … he had nearly shot his friend … he had nearly KILLED Andy! Another wave of nausea flooded through his stomach as Mat stared into the shocked gaze of his best friend, each one realising how close to disaster they had just come. So caught up in what had nearly happened was Mat that he didn’t notice the scurrying movement from beneath the discarded lid of the crate near his feet. So thankful that he hadn’t hurt his friend … that he hadn’t killed his friend … was Mat that he didn’t see the many-legged black form totter out on stilt like legs and scuttle towards him. So caught up was Mat that he nearly didn’t realise what was happening at all until the spider and crept onto his foot and under the hem of his trousers. Once it’s momentum had started it seemed that the spider was not going to be stopped. It could feel the warmth coming from within, it knew that there was shade and moisture within, and so it made it’s way to what … in it’s rudimentary brain … was perhaps going to be it’s new home. With mounting horror Mat looked down, everything seemingly happening in slow motion. He saw the bulging movement of ‘something’ underneath the material of his trousers, just passed his knee, as he felt the touch … light and fleeting … of a multitude of small hairs and heavier legs against his skin. His eyes widened with totally irrational fear as he realised that the thing … the SPIDER … was nearly at his groin. His mind went blank, a haze of black and unrelenting instinct wiped the last shred of consciousness from him. With autonomic motion his arm brought the gun up in one swift movement, pointing it towards his own body … his own groin … and before he realised what it was that he was doing his finger squeezed. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ As fragile as a baby’s first breath the spark clung to life with the tenacity of one that would not … could not … be denied. Small and insubstantial it barely registered on the senses at all, just a hint of smoke at the corner of the eye. Clinging to itself, wrapping itself around it’s own form, the spark multiplied, it duplicated it became more than the sum of it’s parts. The spark became an ember … and the ember caught hold of it’s once chance at life, it’s last chance at existence and it willed itself to be more. Catching an errant breeze in it’s embrace it enclosed it within itself, holding onto it with a desperate touch. The oxygen fuelled it, it fed it, and with burgeoning colors, like the blossoming of a rose made of fire, the ember grew. Slowly, ever so slowly, it changed,, it increased, it lived. The ember became a flame. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ To be continued in part three: "Eyes wide open"
