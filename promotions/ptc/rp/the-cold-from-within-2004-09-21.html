The Cold from within

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 The cold night air crept in from the sea as Joshua walked along the docks lost in his own mind. His walk and aura out of place with the world around him but no one sort to mention it as he passed them by. The late night factor light clicked off plunging the docks into an almost complete darkness, the faint yellow glow was the only thing holding the darkness at bay. Joshua: “You have some sort of name value I hear, not with me but name value you have all the same. I’ll admit it right now, to be placed in the area people place you takes hard work, but past triumphs and respect of people I wouldn’t listen to if my life depending on it means you are to me just a name, an opponent, and most importantly in my way. Will you stand firm? Or will you fold? Digital Morality, I can wait to make that choice for you.” ----------------------------------<>---------------------------------- An empty warehouse Joshua had spent his hard earned money on now lay home to a prime standard ring, a Japanese standard ring, a few gym machines and a few overprized furnishings. Joshua was happy with this place, since his fallout and bitter feud with his father Joshua had been to many gyms to prepare for his matches. None had come even close to being half as good as he demanded. The Japanese styled ring alone was something no gym but his fathers had, it was bigger, harder and the ropes stronger. Joshua was use to that ring; it made the Prime ring feel like a bouncy castle. The place looked like it had just landed here via one of the many hurricanes that bullied the south states of America but Joshua found comfort with its walls. Being comfortable to Joshua was a major part of his build up, he never liked not really knowing. He had not known anything about Façade, nothing at all yet he had worked it to a point he found comfort in. Now an even bigger advantage had opened up, Joshua had his own little playground. This place was a private and personal tool for Joshua to tweak and test his skills. Joshua looked around, the walls a mass of pealing paint, rusty supports and time worn wholes; it felt like the perfect place for him. Most liked polished floors, perfect rings, brand new machines with sparkling buildings for there training. Joshua however, Joshua liked the run down building; he liked seeing a few rats running across his floor looking for food. This place was were he sort, planned and prepared to torture a human being. Such thoughts, such planning should not be done in a place of beauty; beauty was a place for love, victory celebrations. Joshua knew these places, knew them well and torture was not a welcome guest within them. “No this place is perfect; lots of screaming will go unheard in here.” ----------------------------------<>---------------------------------- The dim lit docks were the perfect setting for Joshua mind to rumble on through the many thoughts going on in his head. Digital Morality a name many in PTC know and respect, many however did not include one Joshua Taro Freedom. Joshua: “The ‘great’ Digital Morality, UWF hall of famer and holder of the longest tag team title rein in the history of UWF. Impressive if really true, I can’t say for sure because when you consider the source the info came from, they may have just been talking you up. I’ll assume that the info is correct, I’m likely giving you way too much respect and credit but seeing as I’ll soon be beating you and kicking you out of this tournament, a second time for you I believe, you’ll need all the help I care to give.” The wind picked up alittle causing Joshua to shake off the sudden chill; the air was cold to begin with but was now getting to a point Joshua would soon need to cover up. Joshua: “I’ll be honest shall I, I often am and I often find myself pissing people off. It seems that in America the truth doesn’t set you free like advertised, it makes you the bad guy. Then what else is new in a country where the drunk and unskilled are the cool kids, and the retarded are allowed to lead. However back to you Digital, you and me, not really all that fair of a match up is it. There’s you this PTC legend, full of joy because you got a second round place given to you for nothing. Here’s me, working hard and showing the world how much better I am then anyone else. I beat an opponent to get my spot in the second round; you did something I care not to repeat because it disgusts me so much. You know some people have the nerve to suggest that I am alittle arrogant. I mean me arrogant? Is it my fault am better then everyone else? Is it my fault that if I feel like it, I can simply take you down, twist you up in knots and use you as my personal scream machine? People have been too quick, and to green with envy to brand me arrogant, if they worked as hard as me then maybe they would stand a chance. Maybe you would stand a chance, but a history of glory doesn’t count in the ring with me, you should know that. Digital, does this match seem important to you? It does to me, I mean how often do I get to take a PTC legend like you, and rip them apart? How often?” ----------------------------------<>---------------------------------- Inside the old warehouse as two overgrown rats began fighting over a scrap piece of dried up bread Joshua’s phone rang. He knew who was calling, knew it would be a call he wouldn’t take. Monday morning 4:52am, same time, same day, same result. Joshua’s father was calling him, this call was the only and pathetic attempt he ever made. Joshua wouldn’t feel so bad about this at all if his father lived over in Japan, even the other side of the country, but no. Joshua’s anger at his father grew each and every week his phone rang, his father lived less then seven feet from his home, he lived across and the hall in a matching apartment yet all he ever tried was a 5am phone call on a Monday morning. “That mans a joke; I can’t believe we have the same blood running through us.” Joshua now hadn’t said a single word to his own father for four months, considering they lived next to each other and he made a point of flying home the same day as Prime events, it was hard to keep missing each other. Joshua’s outlook was it was on his father to make the move, he was the one in the wrong, he was the one trying to take over. Joshua had and still just wanted his father to say one thing and one thing only, he wanted his father to be proud of him. Just to hear the words would be more than enough to end the silence but no, early calls that he knew would stay unanswered were all he tried. More then likely he would merely tell him his work was slipping up, or that he needed to leave his girlfriend to focus solely on wrestling. Joshua still burned inside at his fathers demand, he had learned of his relationship with Jessica and saw there love and demanded it be ended. There were no congratulations just a simple demand for it to be over with no other option even entered into his mind. “Why can’t he just for once be happy for me, but no time after time he makes it about him. My own father envious of me, he’s just like all the rest. Poorly blessed with something so he gets envious of those that have what he seeks, just another pathetic loser.” ----------------------------------<>---------------------------------- The winded had kicked up another wave of cold air, looking around at the few people Joshua could see, all where covering up and heading for warmer surroundings. Joshua however had taken the option to remain in the cold night air, though it tested his resolve it fitted his mood as of late. He had been so obsessed and anger that he had become cold to everyone but Jessica. Joshua had seen his actions change from feeling the moment, to cold, calculated and methodical in there lust of pain. He wasn’t sure which felt better, knowing he could hurt people if he wanted to, or the actual hurting of people. The screams they cried at the end where a joy to hear, but that was always the end of a chase and torture. The chasing brought its own screams its own fear out of people, Joshua loved to see the eyes of a man across from him in the ring knowing his life was about to become hell. The look of complete fear for his body, it was almost as good as sex with Jessica, almost but not quite. He took pleasure in just putting the thought of torture in peoples mind and that was something Digital Morality need to understand and learn quickly. Joshua: “I could if I wanted to you know, I could rip you apart almost effortlessly. The right pressure at the right point, with the right moment and out pops a joint. I’ve had this ability for a long time, you tear out a man's arm in front of a few people, and pretty soon everyone that knows you, or has seen you hear about it.” Joshua tone carried its own coldness with it, the careless tone of a man who would do the actions he spoke of, then sleep on them with a warm glow in his heart and a sense of enjoyment unknown by those that care. Joshua “You ever seen a room full of people look at you in fear? I have, it felt so good that someone like you can’t even begin to understand. They bow in respect, no not respect, in fear as you walk by. They hope and pray they don’t do or say the wrong thing to you, they remember every second of pain and torture the person still lives through while you’re near. They remember because you ripped his arm from is socket and proceeded to twist and turn the arm causing massive amounts of damage. They remember that, they feel sorry for that person yet a huge amount of relief because it wasn’t them.” Joshua begins to remember the events of his actions long ago, it was at a time when he was hurt from the loss of a friend. Yet the pain he caused others eased his own and brought alittle joy into his life. Joshua: “I had that once, then I came to America and people didn’t know who I was, people didn’t care. I’ve started to make people care though, I’ve started to make people look at me again with that fear in there eyes. I fell good again, powerful because I see people bowing as I stroll on past them. Those that face me know all to well, one slip, one mistake, and the end is coming. I never, never let up, I won’t allow myself the stupidity of giving someone mercy. You are there for me to beat Digital, while I am here for you to fear. A truth that will remain when round two is over and done with.” ----------------------------------<>---------------------------------- Joshua walked by his two wrestling rings, the rats had left as the darkness was being driven away by the rising sun, 5:34am and his phone rang once more, his father was calling him again. “Maybe I should…” Joshua turned off his phone, for the first time in four months his father had made two attempts to call him in the space of an hour. Two times Joshua had ignored his call. “If he really wanted to talk, he would open his damn door when I past.” The hatred still burned, and the hurt of his rejections had scared deeper then even he was willing to admit. It was clear an early morning phone call was nowhere near want Joshua wanted from his father, nowhere near at all.
