Lacuna Debris/Sam Alotti Network Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 105 Location: Derby/Notts, England Posted: Thu Apr 10, 2003 7:50 pm Post subject: Introducting Jamie: A Man of Power (PoE TT #1) The unforgiving, merciless spirit on evil attempted to drum his fingers on his mahogany desk, somewhat fruitlessly, as his shadowy fingers dispersed upon impact, sending particles of shadow flicking around under the high intensity of the strip lighting floating above his head. It was a hard eternity for the living embodiment of evil, as things in his demonic world never tended to move quickly enough. This can be partially explained through the fact that time does not exist there, but Evil didn’t want to think like that. Evil, as he was known, was created by the humans, or more specifically by their intense imaginations. He was the sum of all their fears, their superstitious beliefs and their overly inventive minds. He was created to strike them down in fits of fear and anguish, and he had done very successfully for millennia, causing no end of natural disaster, drought and famine. The problem was, over the last few thousand years; he had actually become quite fascinated by their simple, powerless lives. They were quite magnificent things, the humans. They were so resourceful and logically minded, which had surprised Evil, who had witnessed their evolution over the millennia from superstitious ape-creatures into even more superstitious idiots into the pinnacle of their race: the less superstitious yet strangely more logical creatures who have destroyed nearly everything around them in order for their success. They were ruthless, just like he once was. Evil sighed a loud, long sigh that probably caused some sort of thunderstorm over the little plant below him, and as the booming died down he looked up and examined the mahogany desk. It was of human creation and its little quirks and features delighted him. He had power beyond imagination, yet he was not capable of creating a decent desk to save his immortal life. “Janet!” he boomed, and he literally boomed. His ultra-deep voice cutting through the airless void like a loudspeaker pressed to an ear. The desk shook as the shock waves pelted at it. The shadow of Evil picked himself up and hovered across the room. He was a shadow by human terms, except for three gashes in his face that glowed a luminous red. This was how the human imagined him, and so this was how he was created. It was a funny world that caused the creation of anything that was held in high enough belief amongst the many little humans. Evil was one of the first of the creations, he also knew the Seven Deadly Sins personally, and occasionally met up with Fate for drinks. “Janet!” his voice echoed around the infinite eternity of space “I am feeling slightly irate as of now!” He was yet to master the art of adding emotion to his terrifying voice, and as a result his language had slightly tedious after effects. At times, he sounded like Lloyd Grossman with a more powerful voice. It was hard to see quite where you are going when your standing on the brink of infinity, but when you have spent your entire life revolving around it, you slowly begin to differentiate between black and the absence of light. The ‘room’ was the absence of light, as they had purposely blocked out the sun so as not to distract the spirits from their work. This was one of the things that added to Evil’s obsession with humans: they are so damn distracting! “Rah!” shouted Evil in an attempt to roar. “That was pathetic dear” came the voice of Janet, who had drifted into the room. Janet was also a shadow, except she was different. She was created by Evil instead of the humans, as he felt that he might at one point need a secretary to help him in his evil ways. She was dressed in a neat three-piece suit, so as to make it clear of her position in society. “So, you decided to show up then?” his voice dripped in accidental sarcasm, as he had originally intended for the statement to be a question, except his deadpan voice did little to help. He drifted back to his desk and stood behind it, a knowingly smug glance crossing the three gashes representing his face. “Dear, we have had a memo from The Others” she continued, ignoring the tone, and indeed statement, of her boss in order to press on with matters. “The Others? What are they after now, one must wonder” his voice drifted off into a deep contemplation as he closed two of his facial gashes “They wish to see you immediately, Evil” So he waved his shadowed arm, and they disappeared. _______ The absence of light had grown stronger as they reappeared, and in this premature dark, three trios of red gashes flashed menacingly. The Others were the council of human created spirits, hence rulers of the land in which Evil inhabits. He had had run-ins with them before, mostly due to extreme violence relating to humans. They hardly care dif he killed a few thousand or so, but if he threatened enough of them, it would result in the deaths of all of the shadowed creatures. If nobody was around to believe in them, then they would have absolutely no purpose in their eternities and would cease to exist. “You appear to have summoned me” started Evil, but he was not allowed to finish as a loud gibbering rose from The Others. “Thou” gibbered one. “Shalt” came the second. “Not” chimed the third” “Speak!” they screamed in unison before breaking down into a wailing chorus. In the centuries that The Others had existed, they had been locked inside their absence of light, thus rendering them one hundred percent, purely insane. Yeah, they were good at their jobs; it was just the way they did them that was out of the ordinary. Sharing sentences was just one little habit they had developed; alongside the charter of innumerable rules they had forgotten to inform the shadows of since creation. “Welcome… to… over there!” wailed one, his pitched voice shattering a little glass on the nearest continent. Evil and Janet watched on as their Elders giggled loudly, staying silent in the hope that they would get this over with much faster than if they interrupted and had to listen to the ‘speech’ rule again. “Thou hast been summoned… by… us! Nyahahahahaha” “And we shall deliver your reckoning… now!” They broke down into another chorus of screaming laughs as Janet and Evil glanced at each other, delivering as much of a confused shrug as two shadows could manage despite one being fully clothed. “You have been spending… too much time… looking at…” “…HUMANS!!!” they sounded like some sort of tape being played backwards, grating hugely on the nerves of not only Evil and Janet, but also themselves. “The punishment is… spending a human life as… a human life! Nyahahaha!” Janet looked at Evil, and Evil smiled happily. He had been angling for this for roughly one hundred and sixty eight years now, and it had finally occurred! He must celebrate! No! If he shows too much enthusiasm, then they would surely turn him into a bad human, or, an American! “What is…” “…Your…” “…Plea?” Evil glanced at Janet, and then around the absence of light. He smiled again, this time a thing grin of someone with a somewhat evil plan. “Can I take Janet with me?” _______ He woke with a start and glanced around nervously. The bright, clinical light burnt into his eyes as shock protruded from every ounce of him. “Urgh, my brain hurts.” He said as he rolled over, his voice still as boomingly loud as ever. He examined the room. It had walls, windows with curtains, hey this wasn’t too shabby! He lied in bed and scrunched up his black duvet around him tightly as the cold began to bite sharply into his thin skin. Then it dawned on him. “Skin… Brain… Pain… I’m Human!” The shockwave his voice caused echoed loudly around the small box like room as he threw himself violently out of bed, wincing as his feet hit the floor. “Hey! That’s not nice!” He looked down and realised what the pain was. He had never touched the floor before. It was all so new and strange… He crossed the room and examined himself in the plastic framed mirror hanging on the rear of the thin wooden door. “Oh… My… Lord…” Mop of hair, spotty and greasy skin, under developed arms, he had seen this all before somewhere, in some human magazine. It was a period humans suffered from during the early era of their lives. Some went mad here, some ruined their self-confidence for eternities to come.
