Finally...An Answer

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 Who is the Angel indeed… Smothered in cold blackness, the Angel drowns in mystery. Her agenda, her life…all of it seems engulfed in endless riddles. I do not know what it is that stirs her. I do not know what motivates her. I do not know what on this earth brings her pleasure, be it sadistic pain or misery. She seems like a person who shall forever be engulfed in black. The Angel…lives for the obscurity. Only those she tolerates can stand her presence. Will she tolerate me? If I accept the chance to fight, I will see her for myself. There’s still the matter of Alex Caine though. He doesn’t seem to carry with him the kind of awe-inspiring aura that the Angel does. There’s no enigma about him. He’s been advertised to me as an open book. A very skilled Martial Artist who has experiences his own temper scenes. He’s crippled a man during a tournament. That black mark deformed his career for the rest of his life. Instead of swimming in fame and fortune and being dubbed as the “Face of Martial Arts” as he was destined to be, he lived in obscurity. He lived going from underground cell to underground cell fighting for the highest bidder. Such a tragedy to see such a waste. Yet now, he’s entered into a tournament filled with the best Cruiserweights, or Light-Heavyweights, or however you want to call them. No doubt he and the one that I can only refer to as The Angel have victory in their mind. Alas, victory doesn’t always come easy, nor does it always come alone. Jacob wants me to fight for him. I’m nothing more than a pawn in his get-rich-fast scheme. He’s a standup guy, but seldom does he think things through. So now here I stand in the fork of the road. One road leads to Jacob and accepting his offer. It leads to him getting paid and his family being supported. However, up ahead the road it gets blurry. I can’t tell where I’m going, or how I will end up there. The other road seems smooth and easy to walk on. If I go down that road, I know where I’m going. Yet I abandon my friend Jacob in his time of need. Either way I go, nothing will be all right. Damn you… Thursday Morning 7:40 AM I walked out of the bathroom in my shower shoes and white cotton robe. My hair dangled down in a glimmering web of crimson strings. I walked down the long hallway towards my bedroom. It’s Thursday morning. It’s still two days before the start of the tournament Jacob told me about. No…the tournament Jacobs signed me up for without my consent. I know Jacob will be wanting an answer from me. The truth is I don’t have an answer. I shook my head in disgust even thinking about it. I turned and passed by the only room in my house that will always be locked. The Forbidden Room It was my late fiancée’s room. I gazed a bit at the door, and then continued on my way. One day I’ll open the door. One day… Thursday Afternoon 1:11 PM I’ve avoided Jacob at all costs thus far. I can’t bear to answer his questions anymore. I can’t even bear to fight anymore. I stand here in the middle of my gym, in the middle of my own irony. I can’t fight. I won’t fight anymore. Yet I train others to do that. Yet I have everyone wanting me to fight. In front of me, training in their own way, were a handful of students of mine. I stared at them. I stared at their amateur attempts to perform moves they either saw on TV or somehow ‘invented.’ I looked over all of them, each trying to outdo the other, and perhaps try to outdo me. That was their reason for being in this Gym in the first place. They’ve heard good things about me…mostly over exaggerated things. So like any other time, they’ve asked questions. I’ve been too…preoccupied to answer them. They don’t know me as the wrestling world does. They don’t know me as Prime Time Central does. All they know is that I was supposedly some promising superstar who lost his break. They know me as Mr. Del Fuego, the owner of Del Fuego Gym. That’s all. As little as they know, I still don’t want them to know anymore. I don’t want them to know about the fighter I am. I don’t want them to know about the enemies I’ve made. I don’t want them to know of who I am. I do not want them to know that I am Syko Arsonist. “I heard about a cruiserweight tournament this weekend.” I heard one of them say. It came from one of the few females I had in the gym. She was a blonde who was very energetic. It seemed like she put 200 percent in everything she did, not because she wanted to, but because she felt she had to. Shame…I wished everyone had her work ethic. The cruiserweight tournament? This weekend? Jacob’s tournament is this weekend… Early Friday Morning 2:45AM Creak… The floors gave out an eerie creak, almost like a wail, as I walked upon them. Something about this seemed familiar. This floor. This ceiling. These white walls. All of them seemed familiar. I remember this place. I looked up ahead in the hallway and saw two white jackets walking towards me carrying a clipboard with them. As they approached closer and closer, they neither heard nor saw me. I stood in front of them in an attempt to get their attention. I knew where I was. This was the Asylum Grounds Psychiatric Ward. I remember who was here also. Mandy Veron. Poor thing. She saw her parents die in a fire. She saw her younger sister die in front of her. The former left her angry, bitter, and cold for life. It left her mind in a wreck, with little repair possible. The latter finished the job. She went insane, blamed everything on the devil and I. Then she ended up here. She slowly faded away from this world. Slowly faded away until she could no longer remember who she was. Eventually she escaped. Her goal? Try to kill me. I’ll never forget the way she looked, with her tangled hair and torn clothes as she ran at me with the knife. All of that during the last time I fought with anyone…the Prime Time Invitational Tournament. So I stood in front of the medics, but they walked right through me…literally. I turned around a bit frightened, a bit upset. I sighed and walked down the hall to where I knew she was going to be. There were a couple of medics walking down the hallway, all of them oblivious to me. It had gotten useless trying to get their attention, thus I just kept on walking. I eventually found the room. I looked into the port window and saw her still crouched up in the corner. Her robe was tattered and torn; her hair a tangled web. She no longer screamed or became frantic. It seemed like her body had given up. She had given up. I sighed as I looked through the window. Wondering if it really was my fault. No…it wasn’t. it wasn’t my fault. Emilia however…was. I turned away from the window and proceeded to walk out of the building. Who is the Angel? The voice…it sounded full of pain and anguish. I took a look around me seeing if anyone was nearby. There were a few medics walking up and down the hallway, but none that would have said that. The Dark explanation will be told… The voice seemed to originate from the room right next to Mandy’s. I took a quick look inside Mandy’s room and saw nothing outside of the ordinary…as much as that is. I stepped over to the next window and gazed inside. The lights were dim inside and it was hard to make out exactly what was inside. But eyes turned to scan the room until I saw a lady sitting in the corner. The lady sat down with her legs crossed in a very sultry and rich manner. Black tresses came down to cover her face. She wore everything black. Black boots. Black skirt. Black blouse. She wore crimson lipstick and she sat in the corner without any worry. I looked at her face. Her eyes seemed cold. They also looked away. Suddenly, her eyes slowly started to gaze up and follow a trial towards me. They found me. She stared at me. She smiled an eerie smile. It was as if she knew I was here. Impossible! I gasped and stepped back, only to hear a light chuckle. Realize now that what you see is no longer a scorpion. The scorpion is in hiding, broken, empty, vengeful. <<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>> I woke up. Another Dream The same dream, this time with something new. Who was that girl? Was it someone from my past? Was it someone of my future? I laid back in bed. The cruiserweight tournament was but a day away, and I still had not told Jacob anything. I felt like a prick not giving him an answer. I laid in bed and closed my eyes, trying to go back to sleep. I was going to see Jacob in a few hours. I was going to tell him my answer. Friday Morning 10:10 AM This is it. No more delays. I knocked on the door to Jacob’s office and then let myself in. He was already in his suit and on the phone. He quickly dismissed himself on the phone and hung up when he saw me enter the room. “Good morning, Mr. Del Fuego.” He said rather nervously. “Good morning.” I replied to him trying to add some gusto into it. “Nice office you’re keeping….” Jacob nodded. “I’m anxious to know something Jacob.” I said. “Have you learned anything about Alex Caine and The Angel?” Jacob seemed shock by the question. After all, I had already made it clear I did not approve of fighting, so why ask of my opponents? Would be opponents, mind you. “Um…not much” Jacob said. “Alex Caine is the more dangerous of the two…” “Really?” I asked crossing my arms. “Why?” “He’s more in control…it seems.” Jacob said stopping to cough hoarsely. “Sorry…um, The Angel doesn’t seem to be much of a fighter. I guess it’s all a disguise though. She isn’t entirely mentally sane, having spent some time in a psych ward herself.” My eyes lit up. “A psych ward?” I asked. “Do you know how she looks?” Jacob shook his head. “I only heard that she likes to wear black.” Interesting I nodded. “Why is Alex more dangerous?” “Because he’s angry. He’s crippled a man before. The Angel just seems to scare people. Alex hurts them.” Jacob answered me. Now it was his turn to ask me the questions. “Why are you interested in knowing about them. Did you change your mind?” I chuckled. “No…I’m just interested in knowing who they were. I already have my answer for you.” “So…what is your answer?” Jacob asked me rather solemnly. I sighed. I thought I knew the answer to that question, but I was wrong. I spent all yesterday wondering what I needed to do. Sometimes what you want to do is not what you need to do. Jacob never let his gaze stray from me. His face was full of desperation and hopelessness. It almost seemed as if he knew I was going to say no. But I couldn’t say no. I couldn’t say a word. This is a man who’s depending on me to fight for him. More than that, he’s depending on me to win for him. Me. Who am I? I’m just a renowned fighter who’s slowly worked his way up the ladder. I’ve climbed each rung one step at a time losing blood, skin, and loved ones on the way. I’ve won several awards in trophies in the federations I’ve been employed in. I’ve developed a reputation for doing well in events, especially those sponsored by this Cruiserweight Tournament, Prime Time Central. I’ve gone my whole life fighting only because I could. I’ve gone through each person just because they were put in front of me. Some lost. Some won. Yet none of the victories gave me a sense of contentment. None of the titles and shillings made me any better of a man. People always remembered me as a fighter. They always associated my name with wrestling success. They always filled in the blanks with Syko is a great writer. Never did they speak of what I lost. Never did they speak of what I’ve sacrificed. Never did they speak of anything other than fighting me. Jacob scratched his temple, still waiting for my answer. “Yes?” he asked once more. I started to open my mouth and give him my answer. Yet my eyes saw something that perhaps I was meant to see. On his table was the picture that he treasures so much. It was a picture of him with his wife and their two daughters. They all seemed happy together. If you looked at the picture, you would never guess the hardships they endured. I looked at how happy they are. I should have been that way. I should have had a family. On the day I lost my would-be wife, I also lost a son. Though I never knew it until the autopsy was released to me. What a cruel joke that was. Seeing that picture made me remember what I’ve forgotten Happiness Family Purpose How could I ruin Jacob’s family? I couldn’t There was only one thing for me to say “…yes.”
