Elite Uber Judge Victory Propulsion Squad Meeting Commences

25 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-09-25 It’s a beautiful day in the neighborhood, a beautiful day for a top secret meeting of the Elite Uber Judge Victory Propulsion Squad. Just weeks before, the Uber Judge and the Under Miner had engaged in secret preparatory construction. Home Depot was visited. Purchases were made. Traveling stealthily in the early afternoon, the Uber Judge and the Under Miner had arrived at their top secret construction site. There, they did some hammering, some sawing, some nailing, and of course some sanding. A ladder may or may not have been used. There was also singing. “If I haaaad a million dollars,” “We’d build a tree fort in our yard,” “If I haaaad a million dollars,” “You could help, it wouldn’t be that hard.” The Uber Judge and the Under Miner had constructed a Tree Fort in Jhonen and Ricky’s back yard. Jhonen and Ricky had no complaints about this. Their mom made lemonaid too. This also was met with no complaints. The Tree Fort was completed ahead of schedule, and below budget. “I call this secret metting of the Elite Uber Judge Victory Propulsion Squad to order,” said the Uber Judge, who did not slam his Uber-Sized gavel too hard, as he was a little protective of the tree fort he’d worked hard on. “Uber Secret Agent Jhonen?” “Here,” replied Jhonen. “Uber Secret Agent Ricky?” “Here,” replied Ricky. “Uber Secret Agent Steve?” “Here,” replied Steve. “The Under Miner,” “Present,” said the Under Miner. “All are present and accounted for,” said the Uber Judge, “Well gentlemen, we all know why we’re here. Super Series awaits.” “Yep,” said Ricky. “At Super Series, I, the Uber Judge, will be carrying the PTC Extreme Title into the Scavanger Hunt Match. Though I ought to be, I'm not yet the Extreme Champion until I finish the match with it. It is my goal to keep the Title until the end, so that I will become the new PTC Extreme Champion.” “Huzzah!” said the Under Miner. “Woot!” said Steve, and was promptly elbowed by Ricky for his outburst. “But that’s not all, gentlemen,” said the Uber Judge, “Somewhere, hidden in the playing field of that match, will be the PTC Global Title, and it is my goal to claim that Title and also finish the match with it in my possession, becoming the new PTC Global Champion.” “Huzzah!” said the Under Miner. “A worthy goal!” said Steve, who was trying hard to get into the spirit of this meeting. Ricky elbowed him again. Steve, the sixteen year old who lived across the street, whispered to Ricky, the nine year old, “You’re not still mad about the Uno game are you?” Ricky replied, “One day, I will destroy you utterly.” This prompted Jhonen to go, “Shhhh!” “These goals,” said the Uber Judge, “are indispensable. And both will be sought with equal determination. However, there are several other nuanced opportunities due to the nature of this kind of match. Some have suggested it’s a silly match.” Ricky and Jhonen glared at Steve. “Hey, I was just stating my observations,” retorted Steve defensively. “But even so,” said the Uber Judge, “Just because it’s silly, doesn’t mean that we shouldn’t take it seriously.” The Under Miner, who had been taking the minutes of the meeting said, “Let the record show that the Uber Judge has clarified the issue regarding the sillyness of the match, stating that it is, in fact, silly.” Steve smirked at Ricky, who responded by making a not-so-subtle cut throat motion at him. “So,” said the Uber Judge, “at this point we open the floor to the Uber Secret Agents for their comments, observations, and suggestions regarding the match.” Before the other Uber Secret Agents could speak, Ricky blurted out, “Rich Rollins must be destroyed.” Steve and Jhonen groaned, as this topic was apparently close to Ricky’s heart. “Please elaborate, Uber Secret Agent Ricky,” prompted the Under Miner. “Rich Rollins beat the Uber Judge for the Infinite Gauntlet Title. We must have revenge.” “Yes, and?” said the Uber Judge. “Also, his chance to pick his first round opponent for the Gee Tee Tee Tee Four Tournament-” “Let the record show that inspite of adding an extra T, Uber Secret Agent Ricky meant the GTT4 Tournament, not the GTTT4 Tournament, which doesn’t actually exist,” added the Under Miner Ricky glared at him. The Under Miner prompted Ricky to continue, “Rich’s chance to pick his first round opponent is on the line if you pin or knock him out. So, in addition to revenge, there’s a neat thing you get to have for getting him too.” “Interesting,” said the Uber Judge, “Do the other Uber Secret Agents have comments? Uber Secret Agent Jhonen?” Jhonen went, “Hmmmm,” as he liked to take his time when he had the floor, “The way I look at it, we already have our entry into GTT4. And this perk that Rollins has, isn’t that big of a prize. Also, the Uber Judge and Rollins have a good amount of mutual respect. I don’t think we should throw that away. In a match of this type, alliances could prove to be very helpful. You have the Extreme Title to protect, Rollins has his perk of choosing his opponent to protect, I say the both of you should watch each other’s backs since various people will be targeting the both of you during the match to claim your prize.” “Very good thinking,” said the Uber Judge, prompting Jhonen to grin smugly, which in turn prompted Ricky to glare at him, “What do you think Uber Secret Agent Steve?” “I agree with Jhonen,” said Steve, which produced a growl from Ricky, “But we all know that such alliances are short lived. If the opportunity presents itself, you’d best take your chance and knock Rollins out. While his perk isn’t that important to us, the bigger deal is that Rollins is a very capable wrestler, and will likely take an interest in looking for a chance to get the Extreme Title.” “So we have a suggestion of an alliance with Rich Rollins, but with a clause for opportunistic betrayal when the time is right. Shall we put it to a vote?” asked the Uber Judge. “I second the call for a vote,” said the Under Miner. Jhonen and Steve raised their hands. Ricky stubbornly crossed his arms. “I’d like to remind Uber Secret Agent Ricky,” said Steve, “that the betrayal attack on Rollins will mean that we do eventually get our revenge.” With some reluctance, Ricky raised his hand. “The vote is recorded,” said the Under Miner, “with the Uber Secret Agents voting in favor of Alliance than Betrayal of Rich Rollins unanimously.” “Very good,” said the Uber Judge, “Moving on then-” “I have another idea,” said Ricky. “You have the floor, Uber Secret Agent Ricky,” said the Under Miner. “Well,” began Ricky, “We need to get the keys for the tether to Karina and Seymour Almasy.” “We do?” asked Jhonen. “I have the floor, Uber Stupid Agent Jhonen!” shouted Ricky. “Let the record show that in spite of calling Uber Secret Agent Jhonen Uber Stupid Agent Jhonen, Uber Secret Agent Ricky was referring to Uber Secret Agent Jhonen, and not Uber Stupid Agent Jhonen, who does not exist,” said the Under Miner. “Anyway,” said Ricky, while Jhonen sulked, “With the keys, we can attack both Karina and Seymour, knock them out, tie them up properly with the tether, and flush the keys down the toilet, effectively removing them from the match.” The Uber Judge blinked at Ricky, “That’s a little … complicated. I don’t know if I’ll be able to do all that while keeping the Extreme Title, and searching for the Global Title.” “That would be hard to set up,” said Steve, “But it’s not that bad of an idea.” Jhonen, who was not pleased with this line of thinking said, “That would mean finding who had the keys, having to deal with at least two more opponents at least, and that’s if we get lucky enough to find the keys on the first try, and then finding Seymour and Karina, and defeating them both. I don’t think we should do it.” “It’d be near impossible,” said Steve, “I’ll admit that much. But only if we didn’t have help.” “Are you suggesting that we get Rollins in on this plan?” asked Jhonen. “No!” shouted Ricky, unable to contain his hatred for Rich Rollins. “At least Rollins,” said Steve, “If not a bunch of the others.” “No no no no NO!” shouted Ricky, as he stood up in the tree fort, “We should destroy everyone, take all their stuff, and win the match, becoming the Extreme Champ, the Global Champ, get revenge on Rollins and take his chance to pick, kidnap Karina Wolfenden, keep her as our slave, choose HER for the first round opponent in the GTTThingy, keep her locked in my basement so she no shows, and we can advance into the second round! That’s what we should do! Let’s vote!” The rest of the Elite Uber Judge Victory Propulsion Squad stared at Ricky with open mouths. Finally, the Under Miner said, “Does anyone second the call for a vote?” No one said anything. “Dammit!” shouted Ricky. Steve coughed and said, “I’d like to ask Uber Secret Agent Ricky what he intends to do with Karina Wolfenden once the first round of the GTT4 has passed?” “Kill her,” said Ricky. “Uhm,” said the Uber Judge. “Or keep her as a slave forever,” said Ricky, “I don’t care.” “Gulp,” said the Under Miner. “There’s no way our mom is going to let us keep Karina as a slave, Ricky,” said Jhonen. The Uber Judge finally catching his bearings, said, “There is no way the Uber Judge will abide by slavery. Or kidnapping for that matter. Or murder too. Not a chance.” “The Uber Judge,” said Ricky menacingly, “Will abide by the Elite Squad’s Vote.” “I suppose we could keep Karina at my house,” said Steve. “Steve!” shouted the Uber Judge in horror. “Will you second my call for a vote Steve?” asked Ricky with interest. “Steven,” said the Under Miner warily. “Sure, I’ll second the vote,” said Steve. “Oh crap,” said the Uber Judge, “What do we do?” “Filibuster,” said the Under Miner. “No!” shouted Ricky. “Uhm,” said the Uber Judge, “I’d like to take this time before the vote to discuss Occam’s Razor, Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle, the Emancipation Proclamation, The US Constitution, the Patriot Act’s shortcomings …” “ARGH!” shouted Ricky. == a few hours later == “And I also believe,” said the Uber Judge, “that our individual rights and freedoms, while having a danger of being abused or used poorly, in specific cases can be argued for or against by circumstance, but imprisonment, the imposition against so many rights and privileges by confinement has to have appropriate justification, justification which indicates that the alternative to confinement is not only much worse for other peoples, but additionally outweighs the revoking of those rights. By this I mean to say that imprisonment of one can’t just improve the lives of others, but also has to carry additional weight against the removal of the rights of the individual imprisoned.” “Jhonen! Ricky! Time to get ready for bed!” “DAMMIT!” shouted Ricky. “Thank you, Uber Judge,” said the Under Miner, “Very illuminating. As it’s bedtime, this meeting of the Elite Uber Judge Victory Propulsion Squad as at close, and all pending topics are tabled until the next meeting.” “I will kill all of you,” said Ricky as he climbed down the ladder of the tree fort. “See ya tomorrow,” said Jhonen. “Ah well,” said Steve, “Maybe we’ll get the vote through next time, Ricky. Later guys.” Alone in the tree fort, the Under Miner said to the Uber Judge, “Thank goodness for your ability to talk for great lengths of time.” The Uber Judge nodded, his voice having gone hoarse, “Indeed. In any case, we have some real planning to do.” “We do?” asked the Under Miner. “There’s some things I wanted to save for GTT4, but I think I’ll pull the trigger on them ahead of time. Let’s go; I'm thirsty.”
