A deal is a deal is a deal

8 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was not just one day like any other. Doctor Curiosity slumped back on his locker room bench and breathed in deeply. His white towel was draped over his head, one hand clamping it there and occasionally reaching up to wipe the sweat that would stop forming across his brow. So, the dream had ended. There would be no second GTT win. His best efforts had come up just a little bit short in an enthralling contest with Vangelus Olsig, but for some reason it didn't feel as bad as he would have expected it to. He went out and entertained the crowd, and perhaps more importantly, he stayed alive. A grudging smile reached his face as he raised himself up unsteadily to his feet, various muscles registering their protest through the medium of pain. He staggered to the shower and the smile fully erupted once the hot water cascaded over him. There's nothing like a hot shower to remind you that you're alive. It wasn't supposed to happen like that. Doctor Curiosity had won GTT4 a couple of years back, but to do so he had genuinely sold his soul to a demon. Prosperity, good fortune and fame followed, but in recent weeks Merrick, the demon, had returned and wanted his proverbial pound of flesh... or rather, the doctor's immortal soul. Thankfully for Curiosity, it would take his death before the demon could claim his soul, but not-so-thankfully, the demon prophesied that Doctor Curiosity would perish during the tournament, at the arena. To this end, the doctor had been trying to eke the information from Merrick, which turned out to be an apparent attack by tigers during his match. Yet here he was, relatively unscathed and getting out of the shower, throwing on clothes and preparing to leave the arena. He'd not win the tournament, but he'd survive. “Leaving so soon?” said a smooth voice, and Curiosity turned to see Merrick sat on the bench, where he himself had been scant minutes earlier. The demon was dressed in his usual attire of a smart suit and slicked back hair. He looked like the kind of used car salesman that would happily take the life savings of an old couple and give them a death trap in return... and in a manner of speaking he was. “No tigers, eh?” muttered Curiosity, packing some of his wrestling gear and failed curious contraptions into a duffel bag. “I don't know what happened...” “Yes you do,” said the doc, his voice assertive. “You tried to trick me into killing myself. You told me that tigers would eat me and so I went and found some tigers and tried to prepare... but you were hoping all along that I would die. You were just trying to trick me into giving you my soul.” “Your soul is already mine. It's just a matter of time, doctor.” “Is it?” “A deal is a deal is a deal.” “That's not what I heard,” whispered Doctor Curiosity, slipping his hand into one pocket and withdrawing his sister's silver cross. “What are you doing with that?” asked Merrick, growing agitated. “Repentance, my infernal friend. You see, I made a mistake in our first negotiations. Maybe I should have had more faith in myself instead of investing it all in hocus pocus and fantastic schemes.” Curiosity had dropped all pretense of the false German accent. “You are what you are because of me!” spat the demon, with menace in his tone. “All of your achievements were down to me, and to the power you drew upon! You cannot just turn your back on that now. That is not how it works, I'm afraid.” “I've read Doctor Faustus, you know. If I truly repent and give myself to God, then your claim on me is invalid.” Merrick visibly seethed. His suit shimmered as if it was made of snakes, and his hands seemed longer and more sinuous. Fingernails began to lengthen, to become sharp claws. “Put down the cross, fool. This is a great deal more real than you seem to realize. You know not of what you speak.” Curiosity dangled the pendant down from its thin chain and smiled. “Then why are you so worried?” In an instant the Merrick moved at a speed that was far from human and snatched up the silver cross. “You will not take it back! The soul is mine! MINE!” The demon cast the pendant from his hand, recoiling as he did so. The doctor was almost surprised not to see steam rising from the taloned hand, but he wasn't going to let this stop him. “It doesn't matter, Merrick. The cross is just a symbol, something to hang on to. It's about what's in my heart, and I can repent. There's more to life than this.” “No,” said Merrick, his speech impaired by the rivulets of slobber and drool flowing over his lips as he turned back to something approaching a truer state. Flames almost seemed to be burning at his feet, but his demented grin was fixed in place, and his eyes were rigidly staring at Curiosity, imploring him. “You want the fame, you wanted to prove them all wrong and you still can. With a free pass back in to the tournament you could...” “I could get killed like you wanted me to.” Curiosity stood his ground against the demon. “A petty misunderstanding,” buzzed Merrick. “I can get you back in to the tournament. There is one spot to be taken by anybody, and I have the paperwork in this envelope. It can be yours if you want! I can give you that; nobody else can!” The demon held up a silver envelope, stamped with an official looking wax seal. “I can give you more strength. I can make you invincible. The likes of Jason Snow, Garbage Bag Johnny and Saij will be like insects for you to crush. You can rise to true greatness now. People will not just recognize you... they will fear you!” Doctor Curiosity looked into the eyes of his tempter, and genuinely considered the offer. It was all he'd ever wanted, but the price... the price... “No.” “NO?” bellowed the creature, resembling a human being less and less. “I've had my time in the sun. I lost, and that's the end of it. I don't want your play-in and I don't want your favors. I'm going to take back my life; I'm going to find a church somewhere secluded and get it off my chest.” “NO!!!” was the response, the primeval scream coming not just from the demon's mouth but from its entire being. The doctor's eyes widened as he stumbled backwards. The enormity of what he was doing was only just beginning to hit home. Tongues of orange and red fire filled the room, searching out the corners and licking dangerously close to his feet. “You cannot just sign a contract with Hell and renege on it, human!” With a smirk and a confidence that he did not feel, Curiosity simply replied, “Watch me.” He turned his back and walked towards the door, trying to will the flames from the room. He didn't believe that they could hurt him, for surely they were as illusory as anything the demon had worn as a costume. He reached down towards the handle of the door and hesitated for the briefest of moments as his arm swung down, his mind's doubt clouding his body's desire to leave. “If you touch that, you end it all!” declared Merrick. “That's what I intend to do,” replied the doctor, his words finally filling his body with the confidence necessary to open the door. Apart from it failed to open. It seemed to be sealed tightly, the hinges and lock melted and fused together. As Curiosity turned to face Merrick, a hand with three long, red, pointed talons grasped around his throat. “You do not get away.” The GTT4 Champion struggled for breath as the clawed hand tightened its grip around his throat. He tried to speak, to explain himself, but it grew ever tighter, sending sparks of color and then of gray across his vision. Trying to think straight, Curiosity thought of what he had done in the past; of his successes from his inaugural tournament win onwards, but the details were fading. At the very last though, he looked back upon it not with regret and a feeling of penitence, but with the greatest of all the sins. Pride. When the doctor stopped breathing, Merrick snapped his neck like a toothpick and threw his body into the corner of the room. Doctor Curiosity was broken, still, and very, very dead. Sometimes even false prophecies can come true. *Note to judges: Whilst the doctor is indeed dead, the story will continue into future rounds if he is granted the play-in spot. This is not the end...
