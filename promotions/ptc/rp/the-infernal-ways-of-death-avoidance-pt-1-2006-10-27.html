The Infernal Ways of Death Avoidance (pt 1)

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-27 Poor old Doctor Curiosity; he's had a lot to contend with recently. If it wasn't enough that he had to defend his XUW Intercontinental Title against thirteen men in a hardcore elimination musical chairs match in the same week that his bisexual sister turned up and started hitting on his new girlfriend, he was also visited by another even more unwelcome face from his murky past. This one was the demon he knew as Merrick, who gave him quite a shocking revelation; that Doctor Curiosity would die at some point during the GTT6 tournament. This upset the doctor for a number of reasons, not least that he would be unable to become the first man to win the tournament twice. Oh, and the whole being dead thing was not at the top of his 'to do' list in general. In fact, that much vaunted list only had two entries on it. The first was to continue his quest to recruit himself a new Eegor, and the second was to coax the information of his upcoming death from Merrick, and use it to prevent such an occurrence. Which might have counted as three legitimate entries, but Curiosity was not one to quibble over such matters. He was too busy searching through his sister's panties. "I know you keep it in here somevhere, Leona…" muttered the doctor. "Come on, vhere are you… aha!" He slipped something into his hand, just as the door creaked open and his sister sashayed into the room. "What the hell are you doing in my room?" yelled Leona. "I tzink you vill find it is my room, given zat I pay ze rent on ze apartment." "I don't give a fuck," snapped the doctor's sister. "Get your hands off my things!" Curiosity quickly slipped that certain something into his pocket, whilst flourishing his other arm in a gesture of innocence. Inwardly, he was quite thankful for having picked up some aptitude at the art of misdirection over recent years. It had served him well countless times in the ring to cover up his constant cheating, and now it absolved him of guilt… or at least of discovery. "Now, what are you doing in here? I thought you'd have grown out of this stupid crap by now. You've got a girlfriend, can't you see hers?" "I vasn't looking… oh forget it." "And you can lose the stupid accent when you're around me, Chris." Doctor Curiosity looked down at his shoes for a minute. He was so used to the character that he had taken on, so accustomed to using it to cope with what life threw at him that often it was hard to see where the doctor ended and where Chris Goodman began. Really, there were only two people who would ever call him by his real name. One was his brother, still serving a life sentence for murder, and the other was his older sister. "Okay, I'll drop it if you quit yelling, and if you tell me when you're leaving," said Curiosity, or rather Chris. "Leaving?" "Yes, leaving. You dropped in here for no apparent reason other than to leech off my success. Now, despite being a professional wrestler I am not loaded vith money," said the doctor, still occasionally dropping in an accented word. "I have high outgoings vhich just kill the money I get from XUW, and even from these independent bookings. I have to pay for travel, for my hotels, for everything like that." "And to have an entourage follow you around everywhere." "They aren't an entourage… they're my Eegors." "Your what? I thought Paul was your 'Eegor'?" "Jah, but he is in prison, and suggested I find a replacement. So I am advertising to see who would make a good replacement for him. It's often useful to have a helping hand around, both inside and outside of ze ring." "So, you actually get applicants to become your slave?" asked Leona, with a combination of interest and repulsion. "Jah! Ze fools come in zeir hundreds! I guess zey vant a quick shortcut to fame. After all, if zey are a success zen zey vill be viewed by a television audience of millions. It's not every day zat a chance like zis comes along." "Accent, Chris," chided Leona. "Sorry… but they love it. I almost can't believe it myself, but I'm not going to fail to take advantage of what could be a decisive factor, even if it does mean paying a few air fares." "And hotel rooms and transport…" "What? You can stick eight of them in a hostel for twenty dollars a night! They don't get to go to the hotels!" Leona rolled her eyes and was about to respond when Curiosity's cell phone rang. He held up a hand to his sister, and left the room to take the call. "Jah?" … "Fine. I vill be zere shortly." Curiosity grabbed a kit bag from near the doorway and checked himself in the mirror as a prelude to going down to the local gym where he would handpick the Eegors who would be spending the week with him. The best of the bunch would then proceed to the final, in the time honored traditions of quiz shows and reality shows. With his mind fizzing from a hundred different trains of thought, he turned to leave, but as he id so, the doctor was almost certain that he had briefly glimpsed Merrick in the mirror, casually looking at his watch and smiling. *** Doctor Curiosity arrived at the gym and entered the building through a side door to avoid the line of fools outside the front entrance. "How many here today?" he asked a female receptionist. "We've got about eighty." "Eighty?" "Well, you won another match, and now everyone thinks you're going to repeat your feat from GTT4. Also, some of them saw your… interesting match at the last XUW PPV and thought it might be fun to see you up close and personal." The doctor paused for a second. "You're awfully well informed for a receptionist." "It's my job." Curiosity shrugged and moved on past the desk and on to where a wrestling ring was set up in the middle of a gym hall. It was slightly tattered and he was certain that it would not be sprung correctly, but it had come at the right price, and getting a genuine tournament ring was not within the amount he was willing to spend for the exercise. With a slightly resigned sigh, the doctor turned around and prepared to signal to the doormen that it was time to let the proles in. Before he could do so, though, he heard a familiar little voice whispering in his ear. "Do you think they'll save your life?" Curiosity turned slowly, knowing that he would see his nemesis of sorts, Merrick. The demon stood around six feet tall, and looked for all the world like a slimy salesman of some description; probably the type to sell you a second hand car that would turn out to be something of a lemon, but upon attempting to return it you would find all sorts of clauses and minutiae of small print that basically said that you were not just entitled to nothing but that you ought to be happy and donate a gift to an organization that could rip you off so completely whilst never straining beyond the barriers of the law. And he had a killer little mustache. "My life? One can but hope," replied the doctor. "So sad for one to be taken from us so young," said the demon, theatrically. Curiosity tried to keep his breathing steady as he spoke to the creature that he believed held the key to saving both his body and soul. He also didn't want to appear too alarmed, as nobody else appeared to be able to see Merrick, and he didn't want to seem as if he was too weird. "Are you still gloating?" he asked of the demon. "I wouldn't call it that. Do you know, I watched your performance against John Hitchin, and you'd have a chance in this tournament if it weren't for the unfortunate accident." "So you keep on saying, and yet I never get any more details about where or when," hissed the doctor, his accent vanished under the blanket of self-preservation. "Just a little hint!" "So you can change the future? I think not. No, even if it would protect you during your match with Olsig, I'll never tell." "Hah!" exclaimed the doctor, startling the doormen. He turned towards them and gestured to his face. "I've had a Bluetooth phone headset embedded into my face, jah!" The burly men seemed to believe that it was fairly likely, having seen the escapades of Curiosity on various television channels. "I've said too much," said Merrick. "Your future shouldn't be altered… though you do spend the whole match looking over your shoulder. The commentators find it incredibly strange, right up until the point where it eats you… just after Vangelus Olsig pins your shoulders to the canvas." "Vhat! Zat bastard's never going to pin me… wait, I mean… something eats me?" exclaimed the doctor, his accent veering around Europe like a schematic of World War II in fast forward. "Oh, where's the harm?" asked the demon, of itself. "I have seen it. You spend the entire match trying to hide from it, but in the end it takes you completely unaware. Of course security and your Eegors try to do something, but who wants to interrupt a fully grown tiget when it is having something of a feast?" "A tiger?" "Yes." "I get eaten by a tiger." Curiosity's voice had become a monotone, but the demon merely nodded. "Oh fuck off." "You can doubt it, but look at the newspapers for Lisbon." Merrick was suddenly holding up three different newspapers, with headlines in big letters next to pictures of tigers. "I can't read French… what does it say?" "French?" "Whatever… what do the headlines say?" "That there have been a number of escaped tigers in Lisbon over the past few days, and that they have yet to catch them all." "But they've gotta catch 'em all!" Both man and demon stopped for a second, before shaking their heads at the potential joke and carrying on. "So, I get eaten by a tiger?" "Not eaten so much, really. More savaged. It takes most of your face off, and tears an artery. You live beyond the arena, but are dead before you hit the hospital." "You're lying." "I'm really not. I get your soul no matter when you die, because you sold it to me to win the last one of these tournaments that you entered. So it shouldn't matter to me when you die… but really, I've grown fond of you, doctor." "Jah?" "Yes, of course. You have one of my favorite souls. Did you know that you can gain prestige as a demon by seducing more and more important souls to Hell?" Curiosity shook his head. "If you can take the soul of a famous man, or one of great power then he will bring not glory as such to Hell, but a certain amount of satisfaction to our leader. The more leaders and famous men that get taken to our side, the easier it can get to seduce others, and ultimately, the more strength we will have when we make our assault on the Gates of Heaven." "What does this mean?" "It means that I've changed my mind, Herr Doctor." "You mean that I don't die?" "I mean that you will die unless you heed my advice. You see, if I can keep you alive for long enough then you could very well become the most famous wrestler on the planet by winning GTT for the second time. If you think getting eighty applicants to be your lackey is good then wait until the days of the future! You could go on to get roles in movies, to become a crossover star and live like a prince, or even a king. Do you like the sound of that?" "Jah! But how can I avoid dying?" pleaded the doctor, with hope crystallizing in his eyes. "Well, do you know anything about tigers?" "No." "Have you ever seen one in real life, or handled one?" "No." "Then I think it might be a good time for you to start. Your flight isn't until tomorrow morning; you've got until then to work out the best way to tackle a tiger. If you know how to stop it, you'll be confident enough to defeat Olsig and then deal with the tiger when it arrives. Do you understand?" "Jah," said Curiosity, almost in awe of the fiend in front of him. "Good. Then do it." And without even a puff of smoke, he was gone. *** A couple of hours later, and Doctor Curiosity was sitting in a locker room in the back of the gymnasium. He was exhausted, but he knew that he could only rest for a short amount of time. He had picked out the Eegors in a different way today than from his usual methods. The prospective assistants to the doctor had been slightly bemused about the way he was weeding them out; coming at their legs constantly, growling and even trying to bite them, but Curiosity had not made his name on being predictable. In the end, the doctor had decided on the best five potential Eegors from the group, and had named them Sporty, Ginger, Scary, Posh and Baby. They didn't really possess any characteristics that would lead him to choose those names for them, but it was easier than remembering their real names, and he could never place all of the guys from N*Sync. The Eegors had been given instructions to meet with their new master that very evening, near the Las Vegas Zooquarium, where Curiosity had been reliably informed they had tigers. He wasn't too clear on what kind of tigers they might be, but surely one was much like another? He would be taking precautions though after seeing what had happened to Siegfried. Or to Roy, whichever one it was. Curiosity believed that his plan was simple. First they would arrive at the zoo in the dead of night. From there they would proceed to break in through whatever security measures were in place. Once inside they would covertly find their way to the tiger enclosure, and he would throw one of the Eegors to the tiger, to see in what kind of way they usually attacked (Curiosity made a mental note not to tell any of the Eegors this before the time was right… preferably during the tiger attack). From there he would be next in to the enclosure, or cage or whatever method there was of constraining the tigers. He would use his newfound wisdom of tigers and an array of sensational anti-tiger devices to subdue any amount of tigers that could potentially attack him. What could possibly go wrong? The doctor smiled to himself as he got to his feet and started the journey home, to pack his bags and then face a new challenge with the joy and relish he faced all his challenges with. *** As many people have said over the years, demons are not to be trusted. They are not to be trusted especially when engaged in negotiations to win your eternal soul. And it was such a foolish trap that Doctor Curiosity had fallen in to. Merrick smiled to himself and smoothed down his lovely mustache as he dialed a number on his cellphone. The display on his handset was not quite the same as you would find in the shops, but even the massed legions of Hell were getting to grips with technology now, and the mobile phone was a lot easier than always having to conjure up incense, candles and a pentagram. "Hello?" … "Yes, he's still falling for it. I've got him visiting the tigers tonight." … "Of course you can come and watch. This is going to be one of our best captures yet!" … The demon snapped the phone shut and smiled with all the satisfaction of someone miserable eagerly anticipating company. He had been lying all along to Doctor Curiosity. There were no portents, and he could not see into the future any more than he could genuinely escape from Hell merely by having a physical presence on the Earth. There was no glamor or strength to be had in capturing celebrity souls. The dead could not talk, and there were no apocalyptic wars planned… not for the next couple of millenia anyhow. No, the sole reason Merrick had returned was for an early closure. He was a salesman, after all, and he wanted to complete the transaction. The only thing that his words had done was to convince Doctor Curiosity that he would die at the end of the week, giving him reason to believe that he was immortal, unable to be killed until that time. All lies, all quite literally damned lies. The real plan, the real plot was for the doctor to attempt to learn how to tame tigers in a setting not conducive to it at all. Breaking and entering a zoo and jumping into the tiger enclosure? It was hardly well thought out or liable to success. No, the mental games had been won by the demon. He had outsmarted the self-styled smartest man in the world, and had defeated him. Success was almost sweet enough to keep the fires of the Hell in which he dwelt from filling his mouth and nostrils. Misery does indeed love company, and when your sentence is an eternity of misery, you must delight in the suffering of others. Merrick was particularly good at his job, and it was going to cost Doctor Curiosity his life. And his soul.
