A star emerges

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 (Scene opens at “The Shamrock,” a redneck bar in Richmond. Mr. Shito, a large Polynesian gentleman, pushes John Crigger through the doors of the bar. Crigger takes in the air, scours the room, and works his way out of his wheelbarrow.) Crigger: Some of you may not know me. I am John Crigger, the NGW’s #1 wrestler. Truth be told, I have my buddy Mr. Shito here push me around in a wheelbarrow because walking is for suckers. I pay him well. Don’t worry. (Shito grumbles.) Crigger: Hey! You pipe down. Where were you before I found you? (Shito doesn’t answer.) Crigger: EXACTLY. So I’m in this GTT4 Tournament. My boss, Scottywood, recruited me for it, because he knows I am the very best at what I do. I am... (The bartender interrupts.) Bartender: What’ll it be, Mack? Crigger: Michelob Ultra for me, and a jar of pickled eggs for the big guy there. Bartender: You got it. (Crigger pats his stomach.) Crigger: I’ve got to watch my carb intake. (Shito raises his eyebrow.) Crigger: What?!? I’ve been dragging ass in the ring lately. Let the truth be known. I know very little about the competitors in this tournament, with the exception of the NGW guys. Basically, if I haven’t heard of you, you don’t impress me. Sadly, this rule, too, applies to you, Drayton MacPhereson. I’ve never heard of your fed, I’ve never heard of your owner, and I certainly have never heard of you. I am the NGW’s brightest star. I am the only man in that company that wrestles every week. I am the only man in that company that WINS every week. I’ve been there three months, and not a soul has pinned my shoulders to the ground. I made my debut on this biggest stage of them all, Xtrememania. Better than that, I won. I went on to become ½ of the NGW Tag Team Champions, defeating a man that is in this tournament now. He’s one of the very best, but on that day, I was better. When I face you, I’ll be better. You’ll fall to the “Stupor Kick” like so many have before you. I rock their chin, their eyes glaze over as they look up at those lights, and they don’t even hear the three count, and neither will you. I’m actually about to take on my tag team partner in the finals of our tournament over there? Why? Because I easily disposed of three people to do it. You, and everybody in this tournament won’t be the slightest bit different. (The bartender returns with the beer and jar of pickled eggs.) Crigger: Took you long enough. (The bartender glares at Crigger. Crigger looks back at the camera.) Crigger: Redneck. There’s very little left to say, Drayton. I know what you’re thinking. The fact that I don’t know you means I will underestimate you. Not so fast. I expect the unexpected. My ring presence is unparalleled. I am “the” ring general in this tournament. Everyone else here is small time in comparison to the NGW. Dallas Reeves, John Hitchin, Scottywood, Emperor Riley, and myself tear the house down night in night out, week in week out, month in month out. Sorry, Drayton. You’re going to be making that trip to the tournament all for nothing. The world will take me seriously, and after Round 1, I hope you do, too. (Just as Crigger finishes talking, two police officers show up. Shito, with a worried look on his face, looks at Crigger. Crigger chugs his beer.) Crigger: Just stay cool. (The officers walk up to Crigger, one of them beating his nightstick in his hand.) Officer #1: If it isn’t John Crigger. Crigger: The one and only. Officer #2: We have reason to believe you had something to do with that panty raid down at the Chi Omega house about two hours ago. Crigger: What makes you think that? Officer #2: Those pink panties on your head gave us a pretty good idea. Crigger: Touche’, policeman. Officer #1: We’re going to need you to come with us. Crigger: Sorry, Johnny Law. I’ve got a tournament I have to wrestle in. I’ll see if I can fit you guys in after I win it all. Officer #2: I’m afraid you don’t understand. Crigger: I understand fine. Officer #1: Come with us. Crigger: I know what this is all about. When you were my age a hundred years ago, you didn’t have the grapes to walk right into a sorority house, and steal all those yummy panties. So envy me, and resent the fact that I do have the grapes to go and do such a thing. Officer #2: So you admit it? Crigger: I didn’t say that. I just said I had the grapes to do it. Are you done wasting my time? Officer #2: You’re resisting arrest, Mr. Crigger. (Crigger shoves one of the officers into a waiter, and then throws the panties in the other one’s face. He hops in the wheelbarrow, and he and Shito take off out the back.) Bartender: You didn’t pay your tab! Crigger: I tasted a hint of urine in that beer, dick! And, hey pigs! Now I’m resisting arrest! (Scene closes as the officers chase Crigger out the back and into the night.)
