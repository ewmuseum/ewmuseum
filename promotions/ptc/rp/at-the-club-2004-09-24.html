GTT4 — Roleplay archive

At the club

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 ***PROMO ON*** The scene opens at a nightclub. There, a group of friends have gathered for a night out: Johnny “Blaze” Medina, his girlfriend Dacia Talamandez, and his buddies James and Jamal. James, like Blaze, is a former Marine. Jamal, on the other hand, was in the Navy but was kicked out due to using Marijuana. Dacia is a ring announcer, who is currently working in Louisiana, but has worked in promotions in California and the IWZ, where she met Blaze. It is a fun, energetic atmosphere in the club tonight. The music in the place is bumpin’ pretty loud, The booze is flowin’, and everyone seems to be having a good time. Jamal: “HaHAH! Yo mane, check out dat big booty bitch o’er thurr! I say, I like da way you do dat right thurr! Right Thurr! Big ol’ booty ass shakin’ and be… OOW!” For those of you not in the know, whenever Jamal says something stupid or just gets annoying… which is almost all the time… he soon gets smacked upside his damned head by James. This has just happened. Jamal: “Damn man, you ain’t gotsta be smackin’ me like dat, y’heard?” James: “Well then shut your damn mouth, and quit getting’ on my nerves with your constant blabbering.” Jamal: “Yeah, I know… I do be talkin’ a lot, cuz I gets excited, ya feel me?” James: “I feel the back of your head each time I smack you for being stupid.” Jamal: “Damn man, it’s like dat?” Blaze: “Shh! Listen up!” Blaze looks up at the TV screen, as there is some news about the GTT4. Then, a promo comes up, showing none other than Blaze’s next opponent, Arch Angel. Not too much is said about the match, only that Mike was a crooked cop, but then, aren’t they all? Besides, 90% of the time, they’re doing their job, which makes them more heroic than any judge or lawyer could ever hope to be. Still, one thing is said that causes Blaze to raise an eyebrow… Angel (from his own promo): “Just one... Hey Johnny boy, THIS PROMO BE OVER BEEOTCH!” The rest of the group looks over to see how Blaze is going to react. Still, he doesn’t get upset. In fact, he smirks. Blaze: “Heh heh… so… ol’ crooked cop ass Mikey’s got jokes huh?” Dacia: “So, he went from being a corrupt S.W.A.T. team member, to an ‘Arch Angel’? I mean… why is he called Arch Angel?” Blaze: “I dunno… but then, why am I called ‘Blaze’?” Jamal: “Yeah mane, dat should be mah name up in here, but den, y’all can call me Smoke Doggy Dogg, it’s all good, up in da hood, knaam’sayin’? OW!” Yes, that was James smacking Jamal upside his damn head again. Jamal: “Damn man, you be needin’ to stop wif dat shizzy brah.” James: “OK, I’ll think about it. Umm… no. So Johnny, it looks like ol’ piggy-boy is trying to taunt you. You gonna take that?” Blaze: “I ain’t sweating that. If he thinks he’s going to play psychological warfare with me, he’s got another thing coming. I’m gonna go up into that GTT4 ring, meet him in the center, and beat him just like I did to Jeremy Page. Look man, in this business, you’re going to have all kinds of people trying to pull your punk card, stamp it, and drop it on ya. Everyone is looking to taunt you. It’s a psyche-out thing, that’s all. If you let it get to you, then you’ve fallen into a trap. Remember how it was in the Corps?” Jamal: “…no. OW!” James smacks Jamal yet again. James: “Not you, ya pothead swab-jockey, he was talking to me.” Jamal: “A’ight den, wif yo’ Jarhead ass… ” Both James and Blaze give Jamal a look of disbelief. James: “Man, you don’t want to get your ass whipped tonight… you know you’re over here with TWO former Marines, right?” Jamal: “Um, yeah… an’ yo’ point be? OW! Dammit!” This time, Jamal is smacked upside his damn head by Blaze. Blaze: “Man, shut up. In fact, both of ya, shut the fuck up. I’d rather sit through a series of Arch Angel promos than listen to you two morons squabble all the damn time. I got enough stress knowing that I could either lose this match and be out of this damn tournament, or win and possibly leave Arch Angel paralyzed. Plus, I gotta defend my xUw Submission title at Road Rage 4 against Sheri Dedmon, who at one time was my employer over in the IWZ, AND I got an Inter-Promotional Triple-Threat match with Jake Salos and Mike Montgomery. I got enough things going through my mind without you two fuckerheads driving me insane.” Jamal: “Damn man, a’ight. I didn’t know it was like dat. Sheesh.” James: “Hey man, if you want us to leave you alone, you know, just say so.” Blaze: “Nah man, my bad. It’s just… damn, I got a lot of stuff coming up. If I lose to Arch Angel, then my whole purpose for entering this tournament is ruined. If I lose to Salos or Montgomery, that’s gonna be a damn embarrassment to xUw. If I lose to Sheri, that’s going to be a loss to a part-time wrestler who ain’t even on my level, and it’s going to be the end of my submission title reign… and now, I gotta worry that I could beat Arch Angel, but leave him hurt and unable to support his family. It’s a lot, y’know? Damn… I’ll be glad when this next week and a half is behind me.” James: “Yeah, I know man. But, you’re usually at your very best when working under pressure. Like when that Black Hawk went down in Somalia… you were under more pressure in that situation, but you came out of it. And hell, anyone who’s worked as many missions as you have as a sniper, and on reconnaissance missions. All this stuff you got going on right now… it’s nothing. You’ve overcome some really bad situations, so I know you can overcome in this one.” Jamal: “Yeah mane, and den, when you get yo’ ass back here, we gon’ par-tay… haHAH!” James: “Hell yeah.” James: “You, me, an’ dis foolio James… we gon get drunk, get lit, and I’m gon’ git wif’ all da big booty bitches up in here. James, he gon’ do… whatever he do, heh… and you and Dacia, aw man… it’s gon’ be on like Donkey Kong brah!” James: “Yeah, or in English… when you beat Arch Angel, shame Montgomery and Salos, and retain your Submission title against Dedmon… we’re all gonna be back here, getting toasted and partying our asses off.” Blaze: “Yeah, maybe. But there’s also the possibility of me going 0 for 3. I’m not going to feel like partying if that happens. Or, if I hurt Arch Angel, I’m going to have to go to the hospital with him. Or hell, there’s that chance that I could be the one in the hospital bed, and I damn sure won’t be partying.” Dacia: “No, no… don’t think like that. Look, I don’t want anyone getting hurt in your matches either. I’ve sat there, in my chair by the timekeeper, and have watched people be put on gurneys and taken to the hospital. I know, it comes with the territory in this business. I’ve even been the one taken to get medical attention because Max Payne decided to show off how much of an asshole he is, or when Gunslinger bashed me in the head with his guitar because I called him the North American Heavyweight Champion, when he wanted to be called the New Mexico Champion. But I don’t think about it when I go in to announce the next match. Don’t think about what might happen, just think about what you need to do. You want to win, right?” Blaze: “Hell yeah.” Dacia: “Well, do what you need to do in order to win. If Arch Angel thought about how he might get severely injured, he wouldn’t get into the ring. He didn’t think about how he might get shot when he was in S.W.A.T., I don’t think. You didn’t think about getting killed when you was on mission in the Corps, right?” Blaze: “Only every time I was deployed. I thought about how I might get killed, or how I would probably have to kill someone. Even after having done it. They say that it gets easier… well, it never got easier for me to pull the trigger.” Dacia: “But you did it, right?” Blaze: “ …yeah, I did.” Dacia: “Well, I don’t know how much this helps, but… that’s what you’re going to have to do here.” Blaze: “ …yeah, you’re right. He knows the risk he’s taking. I shouldn’t worry myself with it. Really, the only thing I’m worried about is that, they’re about to have a child, and… well… Dacia, do you ever want to have children?” Dacia: “ …Uh, well… I… I dunno, I… I guess.” Blaze: “Well, the reason I ask is… say if you were pregnant with our child, and… I got hurt. Hurt so bad that not only could I never wrestle again, but, I wouldn’t be able to walk again. I mean, what would we do?” Dacia: “ …yeah, but like you said, Arch Angel knows that risk going in. They’ve already dealt with what might happen and… ” Blaze: “How could they have dealt with it, if it hasn’t happened?” Dacia: “Ugh… look, it’s not your problem.” Blaze: “Right, it’s not my problem… not right now. But, if I hit the Funky Cold Medina on him, and it does leave him paralyzed… then, well… “ Dacia: “Then you would be trying to win a match. You’re not going in there, trying to hurt him. You know that, he knows that. Damn, I mean… you talk about how he can’t play Psychological Warfare with you… it seems to be working, and he’s not even trying. You already have it plotted out in your mind. Boom, you hit the Emerald Fusion on him, and he now can’t feel anything from the neck down, and that it’s your fault. I’d say, he’s doing a damn good job at playing Psychological Warfare… just imagine if he was, you know… trying.” Blaze grins, a bit embarrassed, because he knows that Dacia is right. Blaze: “You know… I guess I knew that, but I needed someone else to actually tell me that.” Blaze then kisses Dacia. Blaze: “I’m so glad it was you who told me.” Dacia: “Johnny, that’s what I’m here for. I’ll always support you.” Blaze: “And that’s why I’ll always do my best for you.” Jamal: “Oh mah gawd! Enough wif dis mushy shizz y’all… y’all mahfuggas is makin’ me sober, and I don’ like bein’ sober, knaa’mean?” James: “Damn, what do you know? Jamal said something that makes sense. Good work guys!” Dacia: “You know, we do what we can, heh.” Blaze: “And I guess when it comes to this match against Arch Angel, I’m gonna have to do what I can to make sure he doesn’t risk his neck in this tournament anymore.” Jamal: “Lit-ra-lee, y’heard me.” Blaze: “Well, you guys… me and Dacia has gotta go, but before we do, I wanna say something.” James: “You don’t have any more beer money?” Jamal: “You’z gon’ be gettin’ up in dat booty?” Dacia: “Hey! That’s… none of your damn business.” Blaze: “Well, I was thinkin’ more like… hey Mikey! THIS PROMO BE OVER BEEOTCH!” Jamal: “…dis a promo?” The scene ends as Blaze and James laugh a bit, Dacia smiles her always sweet smile, and Jamal is left his usual confused self. They exchange some quick handshakes, then Dacia and Blaze leave as this scene… fades… …to… …black. ***PROMO OVER***
