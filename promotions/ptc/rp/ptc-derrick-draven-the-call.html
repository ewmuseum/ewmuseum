The Call...

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
||~The Call...~|| Name: ‘The Hitman’ Derrick Draven Fed: Free Agent ‘Where could she be?’ I asked myself while beating my thumbs upon the dashboard of the car. I had told her I’d be out in the car fifteen minutes ago but apparently I misjudged her. I guess I was only praying she had all her make-up on and her hair had been propped up perfectly. My hands run down the sides of the wheel in front of me. I can feel the sense of driving down the winding driveway ahead and off to Lai Lai. My fingers stroke the horn but I resist. That wouldn’t be me. At least, that wouldn’t be me anymore. I glance up to our home. I could see why the passerby’s stared enviously up into our windows and at our landscape. The steps that they must see leading to the Red Cherry wooded door, the large marble pillars that stood on each side of that door, and the white paint coating the walls could do no more then put you in a trance. A trance that would make you feel as if you are living in the times of King Arthur. You feel as if you are a peasant looking onto a colossal castle. I shake the daze from my eyes and see her. Cass makes her way down the Marble steps in her favorite dress. The red one with the diamond coated edges. I can see the pearls around her neck, the same ones that I had bought her for our two year anniversary. She walks slowly managing to stay atop her matching high heels. That smiles she shows me sends a chill down my spine just like the last time I looked upon her pearly white teeth. I make my way out of the car and over toward the passengers side door. She looks at me. “Dear, are you driving?” I nod slowly to her. She isn’t used to me driving. Hell, why drive when you have others willing to do it for you. “Yes. I have been dying to get behind the wheel of the Escalade. Do you mind?” I ask her hoping she doesn’t disagree with my reasoning. “If you insist. I just didn’t think you enjoyed driving.” Cass says to me as I open the door for my fair lady. “Lately, I see why you would say that. It’s been so long though. I figure…why not?” I reply closing the door gently making sure her dress isn’t caught in the path. On my way back to the driver’s seat I notice a ruffle in the Magnolias. Before I can walk over to investigate though, my own horn gets honked at me. I look up to Cass and notice that she’s now the one waiting for me. For all the men out there, that’s not a good thing. I make my way to my door. Before I get in though, I look at the trampled flower and wonder what happened to it. “Sorry dear, I thought I saw…a lizard.” I lie. I figure that telling her I saw one of her prized flowers trodden was a bad idea. “A lizard. When we get back you must talk to Jeffery about that.” She says with a look of disgust on her face. “As for now…let’s get some sushi.” I agree entirely. Sushi is just what I need to hit the spot at this time. I turn the key in the slot and on roars the engine. The sound of something so beautiful has been absent from my ears for far too long. I drop my right foot over the pedal and off we go. The sensation of steering down our driveway is just inconceivable. After the two minutes it takes to find the front gates, I give a wave to Frederick in the booth. He opens the iron cast doors and off we go once more. Off to Lai Lai. ||~Later That Night~|| ~~~~~~~~~~ “Mr. Draven and Mrs. Draven, nice of you to join us tonight.” My short slightly pudgy friend said to us. “Come with me, we have your room waiting.” Wing continued as he began walking off. We followed him past the sushi bar and by the normal seats. We went on down a corridor. The corridor went on for days in my mind but it was the best seating they could offer. Finally, we reached the entranceway to our candlelit dinner table. Two pillows sit on each side of the lowered table. This was real Japanese style. “Thank you so much Wing.” I bowed to him since we had an inside joke having to do with that. “Of course Mr. Draven. If you need anything, don’t hesitate to ask for me.” He answered back to me. Wing bowed back before leaving the room. At this point, we found ourselves alone. That is if the water man wasn’t there. He took our napkins from our water glasses and placed them on each of our laps. He went on and filled our glasses to a reasonable amount before exiting to just outside the door. “It’s marvelous every time we come…don’t you think?” Cass asked me. I smile at her. My smile must be contagious since she smiles right back. “Of course darling. I just don’t know what to get.” Cass laughed. “Ha, same hear. Whatever we get though, it always seems to come out just right.” I nodded again and reached for my menu. As I flipped open the book I took a glance at Cass again. “Gosh, you look radiant tonight.” Cass continued to giggle at my remark. It seems like every time I used that one she found that state of happiness. “And you Derrick, you look great as well. Is that the Armani I had bought you for Christmas?” I looked over kidding around. “Yes, it sure is. I love this suit.” I really did love the suit. It was comfortable and even looked pretty nice. I took a peek back toward my menu and read some of the names in my head. ‘Sushi Love Boat’, ‘Volcano Roll’, ‘Spicy Yellow Tail Roll’. Each selection sounded amazing. I figured the Love Boat sounded romantic though. “Cass, how would you like to get the Love Boat?” Before answering she grasped the water glass between her fingers and took a sip. The icy cold water ran down her throat making her show that look. A look of Shear enjoyment and pleasure. She placed the glass above her plate as it had been before and looked at me. “Oh, we’ve never had that. Let’s get it.” I smiled. Good thing she agreed, I was set on getting it anyways. “So what have you been up t…” I began before getting cut off. The man who had done the crime was an Asian man with his order card in his left hand. His smile stretched far across his face. I couldn’t tell if it was genuine or if he was told to look happy. “I’m sorry, did I interrupt you?” He asks. “Well…” Cass began before I cut her off now. “It’s quite alright. I am thirsty anyways.” I said. Cass was sure to disrespect the man. She darted a look at me and I pretended to not see it. I continued looking up at our waiter. “Bring us your finest Champagne.” I thought quick of what they had brought last time. “Cristal; that is if my memory serves me right.” “Yes, I’ll bring it right out” Our waiter said. He walked off fast knowing how we usually were. “Why did you stop me?” Cass asks still gazing upon me. I turned my eyes over toward her. Before I explained, I took my glass to my lips and drank as much as I could. It saved some time. “Well…we weren’t really speaking of anything. All I wanted to ask was what you had done all day. So uh…what did you do?” I ask shifting the subject around. Cass straightens her dress and thinks to herself of her sure-to-be eventful day. “Let’s see. First, I woke up and went to the indoor pool. I got in a few laps before I had to jump into the shower. After getting out, I had to rush off to my hair appointment. For the rest of the day I read and sat in the sun. And you?” I couldn’t match with her day. She always has the luxury times. “Jack and Brad invited me over to their pool. We had a little bar-b-que. Then…” Cass sticks her pointer finger into the air. “You went to a bar-b-que without me?” She says. I think she’s pretty angry. I gave her the nod. “Well, you remember the last time you went over there. You called them good for nothing and petty. I wasn’t ready for you to do that again.” “OK, maybe I was out of hand. Go on then.” Cass agreed with me. For once, she actually agreed with me. “From there I went to a High school basketball game…Brad’s boy plays Junior Varsity ball. That was about it.” I finished. It was perfect timing too, the waiter had returned with our champagne. Not only that though, he brought along our order. “One bottle of Cristal champagne and one love boat for two.” He said placing each down on our table. I looked down at the boat he brought. It looked much like a small scale pirate ship. Instead of villains and plunderers though, sushi rest on it. I couldn’t help but wonder how he knew. “Um, how did you find out we wanted this?” He smiled at me still. “The man outside the door is there for more reasons than filling your water.” As if called he entered and began to fill our diminishing glasses to the top again. I thanked him and the waiter and each disappeared. “So what is on this thing anyways?” Cass wondered aloud. I took a look over it and began naming them out while pointing. “Maguro…Tuna, Sake…Salmon, Tai…Red snapper, Hokki-Gai…Surf Clam, Ika…Squid, and Masako…fish egg. Most of your favorites?” I asked hoping I was right in my thinking. Cass Looked over each in disgust. She then looked at me with that same look. “Well….great job!” Her cringe shifted to a smile. I bent over our table and kissed her over her lush red lips. When I returned to my seat I remembered the champagne had yet to be opened. I began fiddling with the top of the bottle and peeled it away. The man who fills our water was now back, he had a cork opener in hand. After popping it off, he poured us each a glassful. He placed the remains on its ice bed once more. I grabbed my glass and rose it high into the air for my fair lady. “I would like to make a toast…” I started. Cass caught on and grabbed her glass as well. She rose it high next to mine and looked into my eyes. “What for may I ask?” “A toast for you my dear. A toast for such a wonderful wife and all the wonderful times we have ha…” My phone began to ring. The jingle from Star Wars filled the air making me wonder who was calling at such an inconvenient time. It continued ringing and Cass Just nodded. We each dropped our glasses to the table top once more and I reached for the phone. When I retrieved it from my pocket I looked into the screen. I read it to myself. ‘Gino: [detail withheld]’. Gino? What was Gino calling for? It had been nearly five years since I had stopped with Gino. What did he want now? “Who was that dear?” Cass asked breaking my thought process. “No one important. Just…an old friend.” I replied. The name Gino was stuck in my mind forever. The day we met was there as well. Nothing could take it away. ||~Ten years Earlier~|| ~~~~~~~~~~ ‘Gah, look at this place’ I bellowed to myself. There I sat inside a small room waiting around for an answer. From who? For what? To answer who it was for, I couldn’t even tell you. I had never spoken to the man before directly. I had only found him through his ‘friends’. For what though, I can tell the story. When I look around my run-down apartment walls every morning when I woke up, I fell like a disgrace. At eighteen years old, I am living on my own with hardly enough money for food. God, life just sucks. Every time I feel I can make it, god takes a dump on me. That’s life though. Right? No! I can’t take that. Here I am now looking around this room hoping the man is the answer to my problems. I’m crossing my fingers, crossing my toes, and even crossing my legs. This has to be it. I sat there for nearly twenty minutes before that wooden door with the tinted glass windows slid open. The man I had met earlier looked at me. “You still here kid?” he asked. I was surprised. ‘Was I supposed to leave?’ I wondered to myself. “Yeah Rocco, is that OK?” Rocco nodded and motioned for me to follow him into the room. I did just that. I sprung up from the crappy chair and made my way to the door. Before walking in I looked to see exactly what I was getting myself into. “Get in here…Derrick?” A large man behind a desk asks to Rocco. I didn’t waste anytime. I walked right in and shut the door behind me. “Yes, Derrick Draven. Who are you?” I think I asked a dumb question because the room began laughing. I heard random callings from the six or so men surrounding the man at the desk. “Who is he?” They asked aloud. I became a mockery of sorts. The man behind the desk quits laughing and reaches his hand to me. In it he has a Cigar. I’m not that big on Cigars but this man looks important. I grab the cigar and he points to the chair in front of me. “Sit…Mr. Draven.” I conform to his request and sit across from him. “So who are you?” The man looks offended. “You’re telling me you’ve never heard of Big Gino? Gino? Or even the BG?” I shake my head. “No sir, never have.” Gino looks at his goons in an angry stare. The scar across his face even cringes. He takes a look back at me and smiles. “You’re up for a job though?” I nod at that instant. The sound of ‘job’ is music to my ears. “Yes. Yes very much would I like a job!” Gino nods. He takes a drag from the Cigar and I do the same. “Anything…am I right?” Gino says while blowing the smoke my direction. “Anything that pays money that is…” I respond to his question. Gino looks me over. He sees my wife beater top and ripped jeans. He can see my ruffled hair and bad boy look. “Of course.” He takes another drag. “You seem to fit the job just right as well.” I look over myself. All I can think about now is what could he have in mind? What is this man thinking? I find the courage to spit it out. “May I ask what exactly you wish for me to do?” Gino nods. He looks to a man at his right. This is a man I am unfamiliar with. His clean look and tight athletic build tells me he must work-out. The man reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out an envelope. I can’t take my eyes off the white rectangle that Gino now holds between his fingers. “This here will explain your first task. When you’re done…come back.” Gino reaches the hand with the letter in it across the table and I grab it from him. “When will I get paid?” I couldn’t help but ask. It’s all I’m doing this for anyways. “Up front kind of guy ‘eh?” Gino asks. “I like that. You’ll get your money when you have finished the task at hand.” I nod. Gino nods back and as he does, his henchmen come toward me. “Come on, let’s move on now.” One of the men says. I take this time to stand from the chair and leave the premises. As I leave though, I hear Gino whispering to Rocco. “Follow him. Make sure nothing goes wrong.” I leave the room and no one comes out. I know Rocco’s supposed to follow, but when will he do so? I make sure now to leave the room before looking inside of the envelope. A hallway with worn white paint leads the way to the outside world. Once I push through the screen door, I can feel a soothing soft breeze. I look at the envelope now. On the front it reads ‘To The Hitman’. I figure I’m the Hitman? Who knows? I peel it open and look at the papers inside. The first one I see is a picture. It is a picture of a man slightly balding. He has a stubble beard around his chin. Below the picture I see the words ‘Here is the target…eliminate him.’ I nearly drop the papers to the pavement after that. I must be a Hitman… “Derrick… Derrick… Derrick… Derrick” ||~Present~|| ~~~~~~~~~~ “Derrick…” My wife continues to reiterate. I come back from that stupor and look her in the eyes. I can tell mine are wide. “Sorry hun…I sorta’ drifted away.” I replied. Of course it was pretty obvious though. Cass nods. She looks me over. “I’d say. Are you OK?” I nod. With a shake of my head I push the daze away. At this time my phone comes on again. The Star Wars jingle returns and I look at the screen once more. ‘Gino: [detail withheld]’ it says. I hold it for a second and look at Cass. “I’m gonna’….take this.” Cass nods and I walk off into the hallway. I shoe the water man away and flip the phone open. I place the ear piece to my right ear and begin to speak. “Hello…” I ask hoping Gino’s voice doesn’t come from the other side. My hopes are dashed in that instant. That familiar voice came into my ear once again. “Derrick…nice to see you picked up this time.” I feel a bit wary about this but what can I do? “Yeah, I was having dinner with my wife. What can I do for you?” An awkward silence goes on and I wait for an answer. I don’t want to hear it but I still wait. “I got a job for you…there’s this gauntlet going on…” The word ‘job’ comes back to my mind. The memories pour into my brain reminding me of my past. “I’m through with that man, you know that.” I could just see Gino shaking his head at me. “You may be through with me but I’m not thorugh with you…” I figured he would say something like that. “Listen…” Gino cuts me off just like old times. He means business once more. “No you listen! You do this Infinite Gauntlet. And if you don’t, your lady friend may have to meet Rocco.” The image of the trampled Magnolias comes to mind. Could Rocco be at my home now? Is he waiting for us to return? I can’t take the chance. “Ok…I’ll do it. At the office?” “I’ll see you there…” Gino hangs up leaving me to think about my old life. Am I the Hitman once more? ||~END~|| ~~~~~~~~~~
