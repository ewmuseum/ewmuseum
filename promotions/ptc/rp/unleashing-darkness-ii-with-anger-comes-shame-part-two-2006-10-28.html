Unleashing Darkness II : With Anger Comes Shame. Part Two.

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-28 ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Anger. The Wildman Estate. New York. Ten Years Ago… "Come on, Jacob," Jean’s father said, clicking the top of his gold plated ball point pen and scribbling on his cheque book. "Every man has a price." McKail shook his head, desperately trying to wrap his head around what was being proposed here. He’s serious, McKail considered, my girlfriend‘s father is trying to buy me off, just to keep me away from her… "It’s just some prices are more substantial than others," her father added, but McKail wasn’t listening anymore. What the fuck? He didn’t know what to do, he didn’t know what to think. Should I take the money and leave? Go back to the apartment, pack up my things and go? Fuck no… He wanted to chastise himself for even considering the notion for a millisecond, but he wouldn’t be human if the question didn’t at least pass through his head. Or at least that’s what he told himself. "What’s you’re price, Jacob?" Her father questioned, the nib of his pen looming over the cheque ominously. "One hundred? Or Two?" He remained silent for a moment and then added, "thousand, of course." Fuck, he silently cursed, still overwhelmed by all of this. That’s a lot of money… "What do you do for a living, Jacob?" Her father continued. "Do you go to the same college as Jean? Or do you actually get your hands dirty and work?" Two hundred grand, McKail silently repeated. "Oh that’s right," her father began, "I remember now. You’re a professional wrestler, correct? I bet those wrestling boots and tights are expensive, even more so when they’re personalised." He paused, as if judging how well his pitch was working. Whatever conclusion he came to, he continued regardless, "but they do say image maketh the man, don’t they?" This could be it for me, McKail considered. This could be my making. With that amount of money, I… He glared at her father sternly and replied, "No." He wasn’t prepared to think about it anymore. "Fuck you!" McKail turned to walk away, to get Jean and get the hell out of the place. However, he didn’t get very before the stern hand clamped down upon his left should. McKail looked at the hand and then at her father. "Don’t you walk away from me, boy," her father warned. "You’re going to take my money and you’re going to leave my daughter alone." The words washed over McKail like a tsunami, but he wasn’t listening anymore. Anger raged within him and he concentrated full upon maintaining it. He had no idea what he’d do, or what it’d do to him, if he allowed to escape. At this point, her father’s bribe was irrelevant. "Are you listening to me, boy?" Her father continued. Don’t do it old man! "You’re not good enough for Jean and you never will be!" Then, something at back of McKail’s mind just snapped and he lost control of everything in an instant. He could no longer be held responsible for his actions, as he wrench his shoulder free from her father’s grip and hurled a wild fist in his direction. Jean’s father was taken by surprise and didn’t even see the first coming, before it connected with his jaw and sent him sprawling backwards, crashing through his elegant chinese design coffee table in a heap of birch and glass. McKail regained control of himself in time to see the carnage laid out before him and found himself panting heavily like a wild animal after a kill. He couldn’t really remember what had happened, but seeing Jean’s father in myriad of wood and glass, his mind pieced together the events the best it could. "Fuck…" He muttered, rubbing his hand painfully. McKail was so distracted, he even see her enter the room. "Daddy?" Jean said, horrified. Her hand over her mouth, tears beginning to well in the corner of her eyes. "It looks much worse then it is," McKail offered, instantly regretting his words. "What have you done?" She asked him, crouching down by her father’s side. Good question, McKail silently replied. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Torture. Police Precinct. Orchard Beach. One day before GTT6 round one… "Do you remember that day, Jacob?" Roy, the plain-looking police officer questioned. The file lay open on the desk. His black and white arrest pictures taken all of those years ago stared back at him with lifeless eyes that McKail hardly recognised anymore. He hadn’t been that man for a long time and now, remembering back to what it was like back then, he didn’t want to become that man again. "Tough shit, kid," McKail’s hallucination told him. Mickey fully garbed in the same training gear in those damned Rocky movies sat opposite him. "Don’t kind yerself. This is tha path ya chose." McKail glared at his figment of his imagination for a second, wanting to tell it to fuck off again, but he couldn’t bring himself to do it. After all, it was right. "Do you remember what you did?" His tormentor continued. Roy realised he was getting to him and moved in for the kill. He leant over McKail’s shoulder and turned the pages of the file, past the neatly typed up police report, past the thumb prints and stopped at the pictures. McKail closed his eyes. He couldn’t look at their faces. I’m not ready for this… "Look at her, Jacob," he yelled. "Look at what you did to her!" His mouth couldn’t form words and his bowels felt as though they were about ready to let loose their contents. No, he silently replied, I can’t… "Look, kid," his hallucination began. His tone was soothing and held undertones of concern. "Ya gotta do it. Get it over with. Do it an’ repent." No… "Look at your victim, Jacob," the police officer screamed. "Look at what you did to Jean!" God, no… "God won’t save ya now, kid," his figment of imagination told him. "Get this over with. Redemption is waitin’." McKail opened his eyes and the horrors enveloped him. "Good," the police officer said. "Take a good look at what you’ve done." McKail took the repulsion in and tears welled in his eyes. I’m not ready for this, his mind babbled, stop it. Stop it now! Roy fingered his nightstick for a couple of seconds, as if contemplating whether it was worth wasting his anger on McKail. Apparently, McKail was worthy. His torturer extracted the nightstick and quickly struck out at McKail across the jaw. McKail tumbled out of his chair, hitting the stiff carpeted floor with a sickening thud. "Fuck, kid," his hallucination cursed. "Yer sorry about what happened. That’s enough. Yer don’t have ta take this." As soon as McKail hit the floor, Roy was on him, cramming the nightstick under his chin, effectively pinning him to the floor by the throat. McKail gagged for air, as Roy’s face lit up with sadistic delight. "How does it feel to be helplessly beaten?" Roy questioned, his eyes aflame with fury. "Did Jean feel like this when you beat the shit out of her?" ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Rage. The Wildman Estate. New York. Ten Years Ago… "I didn’t mean…I…" McKail trailed off. Her father was being a prick, but that didn’t give him the right to lash out like a damned thug. Jean, in tears now, gently cradled her father’s head, picking away at the glass here and there and examined the mostly shallow cuts intently. "Why, Jacob?" She asked him. "Why did you do this?" Answers formulated in his head, but each seemed more ridiculously than the rest. He wanted to just tell her outright. Your father tried to pay me to get me out of your life! But somehow, standing over his unconscious body, it didn’t seem like it’d be enough. Instead, he remained silent and could only look at his feet in shame. "You’re not like this, Jacob," she said. "You don’t attack people and especially for no reason…" McKail couldn’t even bring himself to look at her, but looked at her father instead, who’d begun to murmur back into consciousness. Her father tried to sit up and Jean helped him. "What happened?" She asked him. By now, she’d realised that she wasn’t going to get anything from McKail, no matter how much she pried. Her father glared at him. "He…he…ugh…" Her father paused to compose himself, obviously constructing some kind of yarn to fool his daughter. "He attacked me…" "I can see that," she responded, her eyes flickering between the two of them, "but why?" McKail opened his mouth to explain, but her father beat him to it. "It was just a little misunderstanding." "A little misunderstanding?" McKail repeated. "Fuck you! I understood well enough." "Jacob!" She yelled. "It’s okay, hon…" Her father said. Great, McKail thought, now he’s the good guy and I’m the bad guy. Fuck it. I need to tell her. "You’re father offered me money," McKail blurted. He originally wanted it to come out smoothly, but with his mind racing away with itself, his choice of words was limited. "He offered me money to get out of your life." She glared at her father. "But…but…why?" "Because he thinks I’m not good enough for you," McKail responded, before he could open his mouth again. "Perhaps he was right." "Yeah, I was right," her father admitted. It took McKail as quite a shock. After all it, was only his word against her father’s. He still could’ve gotten away with it. "Daddy?" She whimpered. "How could you?" "It was a test," her father explained. "I’m sorry, hon, but I just wanted to know whether he was just dating you for the money…" Fuck no, McKail thought. It wasn’t like that, was it? "Daddy!" She screamed. "How could you?" "You ought to be thanking me," he told her. "It’s better we found out he’s a thug now, rather than further down the line." "Fuck you," McKail responded. The tears were rolling off her cheeks now, as she stood and wandered over to him. She placed both hands upon his shoulders and looked him directly in the eye. "I think you should probably go, Jacob." I’ve played right into his hand, McKail considered, fuck… "What?" He responded, a little shocked and definitely hurt. It didn’t help matters, that over her should he could see her father grinning and waving him goodbye. His anger reached new levels. "Look at him!" He pointed toward her father. She followed his direction, by which time of course, the waving had stopped and the smile had evaporated. "He’s playing you…" "Jacob, he’s my father," she told him. "He was just trying to protect me. He’s a very rich man, he has a right to be a little paranoid." What the fuck? "But Jean," McKail began, "he was being serious. He wanted to pay me to get out of your life." Jean shook her head. "He wouldn’t do that to me, Jacob," she told him. "He’s my father." Is she serious? "I fucking know who and what he is!" McKail screamed in response. "And you’re too blind to see it!" Her father pulled himself up from the floor, using the side of a white leather couch as leverage. He stumbled over toward them and propped himself up with hand upon Jean’s shoulder. "Be careful, Jean," her father warned. "He isn’t stable. He could hurt you." You’re not taking her away from me, you bastard! I won’t let you… The rage ascended from his core again and his mind blacked-out as lunged forward at her father’s throat, wrapping both hands around his neck and forcing him back into his elegant sofa. In doing so, he knocked Jean out of the way, but he was past caring now. His anger had taken over and there was no stopping it now. "Stop it, Jacob!" Jean screamed, climbing back to her feet. "You’re killing him!" McKail ignored her and her father glared up, into his rage encrusted eyes and knew he’d made a mistake. He knew he’d tried this on the wrong man. He knew it’d gotten too far. He was in real danger now and there was nothing he could do about it. Jean rushed over and grabbed McKail’s arm, trying desperately to tug it away. "Stop it!" "Get off me," McKail found himself saying. He just wanted to get the job done. She tugged harder. "Stop it!" McKail heard sirens in the background. The police were here and he was out of options. He got more desperate. He had to exact his anger. Nothing else mattered now. Nothing… That’s when it happened. Afterwards, he told himself over and over again that it was unintentional, but he knew he was lying to himself. He lashed out, trying to get Jean off him and his out of control fist slammed into her cheekbone, sending her sprawling across the room. No! His mind screamed, fighting off the mindless rage. Jean… He realised his grip and stood, staring at Jean as she cradled her cheek, crying. I’m sorry! He couldn’t put it in words. Sorry seemed so shallow. The police burst into the mansion and immediately clamped the cuffs around his wrists. McKail put up no resistance. As he was dragged out of the room, Jean glared at him, as she crawled over to her father’s side and looked at him as though he was the scum of the earth. I’m sorry, Jean… Jostled out of the mansion, he hit the stone chip and the police officer talked into their two-way radio’s and were talking at him. But it useless, McKail wasn’t listening. I’m sorry, Jean… ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Redemption. Police Precinct. Orchard Beach. One day before GTT6 round one… The nightstick still pressed tightly against McKail’s throat, his tortured produced a toothy grin -- he was enjoying the revenge he was dishing out. But, from experience, McKail knew that wouldn’t last too long afterwards, when he time to actually think about what he was doing. "I guess wondering what I have to do with any of this," Roy said, through gritted teeth. McKail couldn’t respond even he wanted to. He wasn’t think about much more than being chocked to death by a maniac wearing a police uniform at the moment. But then, Roy released his grip and stood, leaving McKail spluttering and coughing on the floor. "She’s my wife," Roy admitted. "She’s my wife and you beat her." What the fuck? "Ya don’t owe him anythin’, kid," his hallucination chimed him, now kneeling over him. "Ya owe her an’ her alone…" "It still bothers her, you know," Roy said. "She still thinks about you and what you did that day…" "…her father…" McKail managed. Roy chuckled to himself and glanced upward at the ceiling. "Yeah," he began, "that old bastard. He tried to bribe you too, I take it." What? He knows? it happened to him too…? "Explained it was a test when I caught him out," Roy continued. "But I could tell it was bullshit." He knelt down at McKail’s side. "Didn’t quite react the same as you did though." "What tha fuck?" McKail’s hallucination questioned, glaring at police officer intently. "What’s his problem, then?" "Afterwards, Jean was reminded of what happened to you and she told me all about it," He said. "She felt sorry for what happened to you and blamed herself." Roy raised his eyebrows. "Can you believe that? She blamed herself because you couldn’t control your rage. You could’ve just controlled yourself and explained it to her. You never know, she could’ve believed you." He paused. "But instead you acted like an animal. And you hit her. I couldn’t let that slide." McKail thought the police officer was just circling for the kill. He thought that this was it. Game over. But instead, he just walked over to the other side of the room, just as the interrogation room door clunked open and the pretty boy police officer entered, eying up McKail lying prone on the floor. The pretty boy cop, glared at Roy. "What the hell happened here?" Roy was about to answer, but instead McKail pushed himself up from the floor and responded, "I slipped." Roy remained silent and the pretty boy glared at them both in turn. "I don’t know what the fuck is going on here, but the counsellor’s ready to see him." The pretty boy headed out of the room. "So get your shit together." Roy remained silent, head resting upon the wall breathing heavily. McKail managed to get to his feet and propped himself up on the chrome table. "Are ya alright, kid?" His hallucination asked. McKail didn’t respond, he just glared at him for second and headed after the pretty boy officer. "I don’t want to see you around here again," Roy told him. "Or I’ll finish what I started." "I go wherever the fuck I want to go," McKail managed. "Because the revenge was never yours to reap." He left.
