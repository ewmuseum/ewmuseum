rkvanilla Seasoned Network Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 223 Location: Norwalk, CT Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 5:30 am Post subject: A Mythological David: Life and Times A Mythological David: Life and Times The Battle for the Extreme Title - Part One -- Remembrance: The Neighborhood, Johnny Rico -- Robert Alexander Pendragon was the only child of Jake and Martha Pendragon. In the news reports after their unexpected car crash death, Robert learned that his father Jake was a failing Wall Street broker and that Martha was just a stay at home mom. He also learned that they always struggled to make ends meet, and at one point almost had to sell their house just to pay off some bills. They lived in the wrong part of the neighborhood, and eventually their payments got the worst of them. They had to give up the best thing that had happened to the two of them, their only son. The family lived in the shadows of New York City, in a small town known as Norwalk Connecticut. The population was not big back in that day in the year nineteen eighty-four. In fact, there were probably more animals crossing the street or being raised on the nearby farms then there were people. It was not a prosperous town. Norwalk never struck rich with oil or gold during the famous ‘rush periods ‘. Simply put, Norwalk did its best in catching up with the newest technologies, but also had its troubles with keeping up when tax times came around. Robert was too young to understand the plights of his city, and he did not even remember a thing about his parents until he recognized the last name in the obituaries. All he knew about them is that they left him in the darkest, coldest alleyway in Norwalk. It was always polluted with dust and grime, and always had the occasional drunkards as well as the unexpected rabid dogs. The street looked like it was disowned by the state, and there was always gang fights. Police always patrolled the area to check up oh the drug lords and prostitutes that ran amuck among the city. It seemed like every other day, there was another body sketch being drawn on the pavement. It was a graveyard for the week and the weary. Young Robert thought that he would never survive the chill of this new place, it was like a prison without any form of escape. All his hope was lost at this moment. He crawled his way into a cardboard box, making it his home no matter what size it was. He used newspapers for covers while avoiding rats and hypodermic needles. He did not need a disease on top of his troubles, and he was very afraid of his newest surroundings. The only thing that saved him was perhaps finding a place to fit in, a place outside of his cardboard box and with other neighborhood children. Perhaps they were kicked out of their houses, or perhaps they were runaways. It did not matter to Robert, he need to learn how to survive on the dark and desolate treats. The children taught the young and adventurous Pendragon child all about surviving. They even showed him where to get freshly thrown away garbage for eating and where to gather some extra cash by rummaging through pockets of careless people. Within a matter of months, Robert was doing things on his own. Of course, the children always helped him in whatever way they could. Robert would soon return the favor on his birthday. It was his twelfth birthday, and one that he would not forget for the rest of his days as the man that he is today. A call of “fight” woke Robert from his slumber in the early morning. What in the world could it have been? He woke himself up, pulling himself out of the box and recognizing a circular shape near the end of the alleyway. But the circle had children all around it. Robert thought that maybe they were all playing a game, but why would they be shouting fight? Being curious, Robert made his way near the circle and saw one of his neighborhood friends Jose being beaten up by a bigger and broader kid. He might have even been a teenager! After shoving Jose to the ground, this bully scared away all of the other children except for Robert. He stood up to this grown kid, looking him right back as he came closer. The other children looked on from underneath any hiding place that they could find while the bully came even closer to Robert. The taunting continued as the bully just kept rising on the young Pendragon, and his breathes came deep as he breathed on Rob’s neck. It ruffled his t-shirt, but once again it didn’t prove any purpose. But the bullies mood was about to take a swing for the worse, and Rob would have to choose to either let him continue his degrading actions or take a stand in what he believed in. It turns out that he shouted at Robert, asking him if he knew who he was. Robert just responded with a shrug and a shake of his head, and then the boy explained that he was the toughest teen that Norwalk has ever seen. His name was Johnny Rico. More shouting poured from Rico’s mouth, and he demanded Rob’s respect. Rob did not budge from fear, and that is when Johnny started to shove. He yelled even louder as Rob stood his ground, and even more shoves were thrown his way. You could see that a fire was starting to build in Rob’s eyes. Rob wanted to take the big man down, you could see the desire building inside of him with each moment that passed on. One more shove and Rob set off to destroy the bully with all of his force. Rob quickly kneed Rico right into the stomach, forcing him to hunch over in an incredible amount of pain. But what came next was something that neither man would ever think would happen. Rob put Rico’s head in-between his thighs, slowly but surely lifting him up slightly above his shoulders before drilling him right down into the concrete face first. Rico’s breathing was slow and barely noticeable as the children came out of hiding. This was the first experience where Robert took down a bigger man, and he has gotten used to it ever since the incident. The children cheered his victory and hoisted Rob above their heads, and soon enough paramedics rushed them all away. An old woman saw the carnage to Johnny on the cement and called the ambulance, and when they came Rico had to be put on an iron lung before being transported to the local area hospital. From that day on, the neighborhood children all walked with Rob to different children on a couple of blocks, putting him in the main “roughage” of the day between the biggest bullies. Robert soon won the battle of the blocks and was crowned “Neighborhood Fight Champion” by his friends for all of his great efforts. Robert had to leave the neighborhood a while after that beheaded bowling trophy was given to him, he had to make a name for himself elsewhere. He would run into his friends later down the road, whether if he wanted to or not. -- Remembrance: The Dojo w/ Jade – After years of isolation and traveling, Robert finally decided to venture across the United States to the busy town of Los Angeles California. His inspiration was none other than a newspaper article that stated new comers were invited to an open doors challenge at the Lion’s Den Wrestling Dojo. It would showcase the best wrestlers from around the world, and the winner would become the newly crowned Dojo Champion. Robert had to hitchhike to get there and pay the man that generously gave him the ride with the shirt off his very back, but it was well worth the sacrifice. Robert entered as his wrestler, Iron Lung. It was the only honorable thing to do since it was a tribute to his “breakthrough” performance. Over the course of the tournament, Jade taught Iron Lung everything she knew and Lung learned quickly. Soon, he was at the final match and actually won the tournament at the end, becoming the youngest to complete the task. All of his competitors stared at Rob when he first stepped through the door, he was yet again made fun of for being too small and too young. But he stepped up against every man and defeated them, including a grueling half-hour final. He had done the unthinkable thanks to following his heart and the determination that ran throughout his whole body, his whole blood, and of course his whole soul. Jade released him from the Dojo a week or so later, telling Rob to engage in professional wrestling. She also reminded Robert to respect other competitors, but never let anybody make you feel like you don’t deserve what you want. -- Remembrance: The First Years of Failure -- Indeed, Robert made the promise to Jade and kept it for her. He did not say quit when he was first fired from, and he did not say die either after waiting for another opportunity for a year. He simply just trained harder and made himself even better than he was before, mastering some arts of defense along the way. New opportunities arose for Robert, and he made each organization a promise to do the best that he can do. He also promised to be the best that he could be, and he has held that until this very day. -- Remembrance: Hardships Turned Successes -- Once more, he was assumed to be a ‘shrimp in the spotlight’. But Robert grew tall against the opposition in his experiences, including being the first ever Triple Champion in one federation, holding three belts at the same time and winning them all in the same night. Everyone now began to look at this mythological David differently as he climbed the stars, destined for stardom. The organization’s foundations crumbled, but he would just simply pick himself right back up and dust off the rubble. He then went into search again for new knowledge, and in search of a new owner desperate for a main talent. Iron Lung was becoming quite popular now, and getting better with each organization he entered. He wanted to make the biggest step of them all soon, and it would all soon happen when a man known as Trigger approached his stature. -- The IL We Know Now -- Trigger was a great man, and indeed a smart man. Robert had recently commented on a search for superstars by him, giving it his best shot in trying to reconcile a ‘shoot interview’. It was a success, and Iron Lung was born into Lights Out Wrestling. Of course, he did not expect the kind of competition that he was about to endure. He strolled in with a mindset on winning, but soon had those chances blown away in the ring by awesome wrestlers that he had not even heard of. His courage had to stay strong, and he had to stay determined and focused like he was before. The devotion would pay off. Once again, the fighters expected a no-show out of this oddly named individual. But being the complete shocker that he was, he stayed in Lights Out Wrestling for better or for worse and didn’t give up until he succeeded. He was gratefully rewarded with numerous title shots. When he didn’t take them seriously, he was kicked right back down the ladder, only to regain his composure again and re-climb towards the summit. After a few times of being knocked down, Robert set his eyes on the gold and won it. The Aftermath Championship was now in his hands, and he was dedicated to keeping it around his waist. Nobody expected a small town boy to now develop into a grown man with the heart of a warrior and a champion, and he was just making the crowd go even wilder for him for believing in him and his promises of keeping great competition over nowadays disqualifications and all out brawl for all’s. -- Summing it All Up – It was now the time to step forward again, an enterprise was at his door step. A shot at the Primetme Central Xtreme Championship in a clustered rumble, Iron Lung immediately entering. He knew that he would half to go through his trials and tribulations, but somewhere inside of him his heart still pumped with the revolutionary thoughts of old. He would overcome all of the opposition, he would not say die and he would not quit. He would keep his eyes on the prize and his eyes on the finish line until it was all said and done. He would scream in pain until his voice ran old, but he would not submit. His passion was too high to be eliminated that way, he was putting it all on the line this time. No more tricks, it was just time to shut up and fight. And indeed, the fight would be on in just a matter of days. Bringing back the title to Lights Out Wrestling would make him proud to be a part of his new community, and it would make him the proudest man on earth. He has what it takes to be a champion, he has what it takes to defy all of the judgments passed on him and come out on the top of the mountain once more. There was only one more question left for everyone to answer. Were they ready to see what their eyes could not describe? Were they ready to feel what their feelings could not show? And were they ready to hear the multiple praises set upon him when the task was completed. Ready or not, it was coming fast…. Iron Lung was coming fast, and there wasn’t a stoppage in him until the end of the road! _________________ *~ TWILIGHT SLAMS FED HEAD: ROB K. *~ Iron Lung's LOW record: 9 - 9 - 1 Accomplishments: Aftermath Champion PTC Ranking: 25 / 97
