Metallo Zords TTC RP #1 --- The Saga Begins

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Metallo Zords Affiliated Pimp Joined: 05 Apr 2003 Posts: 6 Location: My Zord Posted: Sat Apr 12, 2003 7:25 pm Post subject: Metallo Zords TTC RP #1 --- The Saga Begins ::The scene opens on a large bustling fairgrounds, it then travels through the fairgrounds to the site of a tall colorful tent in an extremely fluid motion. Once there, someone passes in front of our view with a sign that reads “Martial Arts Exhibition Today!”. As the sign moves out of view, the form of two men approach our position. The one on the right is wearing is wearing a metallic looking silver vest along with an intricately designed silver helmet. Too his left, the other man wears a similar attire but his is colored gold. Both of the men are also wearing a pair of baggy black karate pants. The two men grip hands briefly showing signs of an unsaid comradery before continuing into the tent. Inside the tent, the two men approach what appears to be the head of the fair who is standing just off to the side of a set of bleachers as he waches them fill with paying patrons. The two men walk up behind the other as the gold one begins to speak with an almost roboticly manufactured sound to his voice.:: Gold Ranger- We’re here. ::The head of the fair turns slightly startled by the vice from behind him to see the two men. Upon seeing them, a smile crosses his face.:: Head of Fair- Oh, glad you guys could make it. I was starting to worry. ::The three men look on as a group of other performers rehearse their parts of the show that is about to take place. From the looks of things, the show is going to have a great variety as there are magicians, a unicyclist, a couple of Shakespearean actors, musicians with different instruments, and even a gymnast who dances who is wearing a pair of large green monster feet that have long straggly yellow toenails protruding from the tips of the toes. The head of the fair speaks up addressing all of his talent.:: Head of Fair- Ok people, we’re looking forward to a great show of talents today! ::The gold ranger perks up.:: Gold Ranger- This show is going to so totally rock the house. ::The head of the fair nods at the statement with a look of contentment on his face. Just then the silver ranger speaks for the first time.:: Silver Ranger- This was a really good idea to put us on this show. It gives us a good platform to spread our message of peace and happiness to all of these people here today. Thank you for the opportunity. ::The head of the fair smiles at this.:: Head of Fair- Don’t mention it guys. Look at the crowd you’re drawing. That’s thanks enough for me. ::The gold ranger now taps the silver on the arm and the two head over to the other preformers leaving the head of the fair watching the stands with dollar signs in his eyes as they fill up. Sitting on a wooden crate is a young boy wearing a cowboy hat and busy at work tuning up a guitar. Next to him sitting Indian style on the ground is a young girl who is holding a big cardboard box. Yet another girl passes into view briefly as she hurries around as if looking for something. As the two rangers approach, the gold one speaks up.:: Gold Ranger- So how is everything going guys? ::At this point in time, a man holding a clipboard walks into view. We can see the name Burt embroidered onto his shirt. He is followed closely by another young boy who passes behind him. Burt looks down at his clipboard and begins to address the query.:: Burt- Everything is going fine guys. The only problem we’re having right now is that we’re having a hard time keeping track of everyone’s props for their performances! ::Burt then bends down as he searches through the box of props. As he does this, the young girl sitting by it begins to speak.:: Young Girl- Yeah, we’re still looking for a few of the props for the show. I hope we can find them soon. ::The silver ranger perks up and quickly turns to the gold.:: Silver Ranger- Oh no. Thinking of props, I think we forgot our swords and daggers. Gold Ranger- Do not worry about it. We can get by without them for now. We can pick them up later. Silver Ranger- No. A good upholder of peace and happiness is always prepared for whatever happens. We must be no different. I am going to get them for us. I will be right back. ::The silver ranger slaps the gold on the back and turns to walk off.:: Gold Ranger- All right. I will await your return right here. ::Just then, a girl passes through the line of sight and throws some sort of object into the prop box. This catches the attention of the gold ranger who bends over and begins looking at the contents of the box.:: **MEANWHILE** ::The scene opens on a picture of a large castle that is set deep in a dark evil looking surrounding. The shot moves up the castle wall and in through a small window at the top of one of the towers. Once inside, we are treated to a view of a small troll like creature. As we move in closer to him, we can hear him cackling away in a high pitched voice that can be likened to the sound of nails against a chalkboard.:: Troll- This is wonderful! It’s marvelous! It’s stupendous! ::As we move into what appears to be the workshop of the troll, we see that he is closely reading from a large thick book bound in what looks like rotted human skin.:: Troll- Ah-haa . . . ah-ha! ::The troll drops his book on the table in front of him and stands up.:: Troll- Ahhh! ::The troll lets out a sinister chuckle as he looks to a clay model high above his head on a shelf. He reaches up and grabs hold of the figure. As he does this, we can see that there are many other grisly creations filling the shelves. Some of the creations move almost as if they are alive as they battle with each other. The troll places the clay figure down on his work station as he examines it and begins adding where he thinks his creation is lacking.:: Troll- Now let’s see what I can do to improve you. How about a few more fangs here and there. Maybe a tail here and there. You’re perfect! ::The creature holds up his creation proudly admiring his work.:: Troll- Oh she’ll be so happy with all of Raziel’s hard work. I can’t wait to see her face when she sees my surprise for her! ::The troll who we now know as Raziel puts a few more finishing touches on his latest creation and moves it over to a devious looking machine. He places the creation on top of a platform that is connected to the machine and steps aside as he puts on a pair of safety goggles. The platform on which the creation sits begins to rotate. Raziel smiles reveling a mouth full of jagged yellow teeth.:: Raziel- This is the perfect birthday present for my queen! She’s gonna love it and then I will be her favorite! ::Raziel laughs loudly as he flips a switch starting his infernal machine.:: Raziel- Beware Metallo Zords! My creation is coming to claim your broken bodies as a present for my queen! ::The infernal machine goes to work and Raziel’s creation is soon completed and ready for battle. Before the smoke around the creature even has a chance to clear, the monster is beamed to the general area of the two Metallo Zords inside of a greenish-blue energy beam. He emerges from a cloud of smoke and stired up dust on the edge of a steep cliff. Through the smoke, we can see that the monster make out that the monster has a slightly reptilian appearance to him. He stands on two legs and has a large tail that drapes down behind him dragging along the ground. The other finer details are obscured from sight by the glare of the sun behind the creature. A series of shots are now shown which showcase different parts of the monster’s body. Finally we focus on a shot of the monster’s gaping mouth in which sharp teeth are held in place by slimy yellowish-orange gums. The shot now switches to the creature’s eyes on his blue reptilian like face. His eyelids are shut tightly reveling a coarse red skin. Suddenly, the eyes blink open revealing a pair of bulging white eyes with a red pupil. The monster now spreads his black claw tipped fingers on one of his hands to reveal what looks like some sort of weapon with three sharp blades attached to it that can be used when needed. The creature now turns his arm concealing the blades from sight. The monster’s mouth is now shown once again before he takes off running into the woods. As he distances himself, we can see that down the front of his body, he has a yellow stretch of skin almost like the underbelly of a snake. On the shoulders and head of the creature are a set of painful looking spines. As the monster continues into the forest, he calls out loudly.:: Monster- It’s good to be free once again! Metallo Zords I will have my revenge on you! ::The picture then cuts off leaving us wondering what will happen to our heros. Will they survive or will they end up on the menu of this reptilian menace? Tune in next time to find out.:: _________________ Who farted?
