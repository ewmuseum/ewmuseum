UWF Episode 1. A new hope? No chance!

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Flicker Static "How the hell does this thing work?" Static "Smack the thing on the side that usually does it." "THWACK" Flicker Flicker "Jesus Christ! What’s that doing there." "Look at the bloody script. It’s the first prop we have to use." "We’re being paid for this right?" "YES" "Right lets go." "UWF…a place of Monkeys! The image changes to… "What the hells that?" "Sludge…the next name says Sludge." "That’s Sledge Moron!" "Sledge?" "It’s one of the UWF wrestlers! This is to show all the guys in GTT from UWF. Are you really that stupid." "Oh." The picture of mud slides out of shot and is replaced by … "What the hell is that? Isn’t that the dude from Beverley Hills Cop?" "No man. It’s Axel Action." "The person on the screen is Axel Foley! Eddie Murphy’s character." "Are you sure?" "Yes I’m, bloody sure. I’ve watched that film hundreds of times. Flickering slightly, Axel Foley is soon replaced…who could it be this time? "…" "…" "Why?" "Well I could find Digital Mortality…I thought this guy looked the same." "They look nothing alike!" "Well where else would I have drawn the connection?" "…sigh" The sham carries on by now the censors are on the case but even the sensors can’t stop… "Jesus?" "No John ‘The Prophet’ Adams…" "That’s Jesus…" "Adam’s is Jesus?" "Just change the damn picture." It’s like a magical mystery box by now… "Chris why the hell have you got her up there?" "She’s hot." "I know she’s hot, but this is supposed to be promotion for UWF! Not for your little fantasies!" "Well she kinda looks like that chick?" "Which one?" "That Eva lass from GCW!" "SHE’S NOT IN UWF!" "Oh yeah…." "Quick skip on maybe no-one will notice." What could be next what could be next! "Well Chris according to my script this is supposed to be James von Doom, a champion in UWF. Instead you have put Jake Lloyd as Anakin Skywalker." "I’m sorry." "Oh you will be when your living in a shoe box." "Look there’s just one more lets, get this done with." The final wrestler image flickers into life… "I’m leaving." "What? It’s Frosty The Snowman." "It’s Ethan Frost. That’s it I’m done I’m outta here before we get fired, maybe I’ll still have some credibility in Cuba." "I can talk to Digital Mortality for you?" "THWACK" "OW MY FACE." TEAM UWF…THEY REALLY DON’T STAND A CHANCE Paid for by Ethan Frost….what a waste of cash huh…
