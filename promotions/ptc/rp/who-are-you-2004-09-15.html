Who Are You?

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 I was born in Chicago, Illinois on the most patriotic of days July 4th, 1979. More explosive then a smuggled Chinese eyed firecracker I was ready to change the world. My father was a professional wrestler, which makes me a second-generation athlete and that’s all I have taken from that man. On my desk is a picture of my father leaning on a ring rope with a stoned out look on his face. Story is he was working a midwestern territory and was flying high on pills and booze. He hadn’t slept or showered in about five days and brother, you can smell the filth through the photo. While in his drugged out state he “scored” with my mother and I was the result. He told me that story when I was five years old after asking him why he was never around. Duke Williams is an idol in the ring but a real loser out of it. I was raised by mother in a strict Catholic up bringing I studied the bible like most kids study the back of a baseball cards. My “friends” had photos of Walter Payton, G.I. Joe, The Thundercats, and other childish brainwashing idols forced onto kids by lazy and uncreative parents. My walls were adorned with the images of Jesus, John, Paul, Mary, Job, Luke, Peter; you know true heroes and crafters of our world. I had denounced Catholicism at the age of eight due to its leniency towards sin. By the time I was ten years old I had a congregation of 25 Baptists at a small south side church. The words of the lord flew from my mouth like an eagle in the breeze. I was as smooth as Satin sheets and as polished as a millionaire’s shoe. I was and still am today the greatest speaker this world has ever seen. So how does one go from the bastard child of a drugged out professional wrestler to the second coming of Christ? The truth is I was always the son of man but it wasn’t till later in life that secret was told to me. Let me take you back to that life-altering event. It was my fourteenth birthday and I was waiting with sad eyes for my father to come and take me to a Cubs game. I remember being ecstatic, as I truly wanted to experience what the other boys had with their fathers. I wanted to feel that bond that only father and son could create. I watched through my teary eyes as this stream of purple fire faded into the night sky from my neighbors firework display just like my hopes of experiencing that bond. I heard Harry Carey end the game on WGN through our rabbit ears television set. All hope was lost and my poor mother tried to console me. The only remorse I take from my childhood was not understanding that my mother was trying to help me. I stormed out of the house to clear my head and figure things out. My neighborhood was full of blacks, which we all know are the foot soldier of Satan. They perpetuate filth and crime as it’s the only way they know. Diseased humans of a Godless mind which is ironic because there was a church on every corner. I walked past the Jewish delicatessen where even in its closed state you could smell the lies of the kosher food. Where as the blacks are the foot soldiers of Satan the Jews are the ones who control them. They are the money and brains behind everything. The Jews rejected Jesus as being the Savior because they are waiting for Lucifer. It’s amazing how out in the open this stuff really is. The world is blind but if you’d just open your eyes you would be able to put it all together. The Jews own everything and their products are what brainwash the blacks with the music to the shoes they are the promoters of “ghetto” life style. It’s all so obvious it’s sickening. I regress, after passing the delicatessen I turned the corner into a one light ally off of Monroe Street. It was dark and to be honest my mind was on my problems and not my surroundings. Really my brain was a whirlwind of revenge thinking about how I was going to get even with my delinquent father. Well before things got better they sure got worse. I remember looking up into the ugly face of my mugger as he smiled at me with solid gold teeth. He told me to give him all of my money or I would die on a sultry humid Chicago night. Not really the birthday present I was hoping for. I kicked him as hard as I could in the nuts and started to run. I didn’t get far as two of his counter parts were standing behind me. The obese one tripped me as I ran past him, I fell hard skinning my hands and knees. God does have weight restrictions in heaven so I know that fat sinner will never see his own cloud of white. He kicked me hard in the gut knocking the wind out of me as his skinny pimply-faced friend also started kicking me. At fourteen the thoughts of morality aren’t really in the picture yet. Still a baby to the world around you thoughts of death is reserved to grandparents and gold fish. You don’t comprehend the fact that you could go just as quickly as they do. However in my state of pain and fright I did understand that I was going to die. The gold-toothed thug who I did the world a favor by sterilizing was now getting up and very upset with me. Why I would never know after all he was the sinner. He spoke to me in some absurd language riddled with filth and pornographic imagery. As hard as he could he stomped on my skull quickly ending all thoughts as my mind faded black. If I were not the Son of God I would have died in that alley. So all you doubters should now fully accept me as your “Personal Jesus”. I awoke after being unconscious for God knows how long. I think he told me two hours. My mind was in a haze and my body felt broken. I tried to get up but I couldn’t. I rested there in a puddle of my own blood fading in and out of consciousness. A saw a light that words cannot explain. It was a bright white light but yet it was stunning and didn’t hurt my eyes. The voice of God was on the other end as he spoke with a tongue out of a Shakespearian play. He told me to rise and given the nature of the scenario I felt inclined to listen. Blue light come over my body as all my pain disappeared and my wounds healed up. He told me tales of Jesus and how his other son became a peace-loving hippie. He did not want the same to happen to me, as I was to be the vengeful savior. I was the second coming of Christ. My mission is really simple and that’s to build up an army of God and take over the world. That has to be done in order to fight the army of sin. I was shocked when he told me my craft would be that of a wrestler but I would do as he said. The light disappeared and I looked down at the puddle of blood that had spilled from my head. I remember touching my skull to reaffirm that it was healed and that what I had just witnessed was real. I went home that night with a felling of ecstasy. With in the next three years I would learn how to make miracles and to punish sinners. I ravaged the Jewish store with plague running the devils show out of business. I turned the blacks against each other as they fought them selves in gang wars. I helped the honest sick, give the white poor fortune, and even walked across lake Michigan. When I was seventeen I talked my earth father in letting me become a professional wrestler. You see God thought that wrestling world would be the best place to talk to youth on a missive stage. It also housed some of the most vicious sinners in the world. It would give me a chance to have epic battles on a large scale. Duke Williams knowing I was a spiritual person brought me to his friend the fine reverend Ted DiBiase. He played a heathen on TV know as “The Million Dollar Man” but after he retired he also found Jesus and became a minister of the lord. He is a good man and taught me everything I know in the ring. I worked on his wrestling circuit for about two years before singing with the newly formed PWC. I won its TV title with little to no resistance, as I am not only very well skilled but also very well built. Your body is your temple to God and even the slightest hint of fat is an insult to all his greatness. You should do jumping jacks for Jesus every chance you get in your day. Satan did not want me to continue in PWC so he possessed its owner causing the fed to close. OSE was my next stop and it met the same fate. You see I have a hard time in these federations because the big names were and still are afraid of me. They do not want to step in the ring with perfection and God’s champion. That’s a great story, when I was twenty, God UPSed me a solid gold belt lined with diamonds and jewels. It is more meaningful then any federation’s title as the bearer of the belt is GOD’s hand picked champion. That champion is me. I am the best the wrestling world has to offer. For the last few months I have been fixing sin in Prime and teaching the heathens the good word. However about two weeks ago God told me to take my game to the next level and prove myself as wrestling’s greatest AND the second coming of Christ. He told me to enter a mighty tournament and win it. So here I am today updating the RISP newsletter to all of Hoyt’s Witnesses. To those who are new to the RISP I welcome you, we are the political organization that I lead in efforts to influence and one day concur the world in the name of God. Recruitment is important so please urge your religious, white, fit, friends to join. We will enact the hagiocracy and run the world and defeat the devil. Step one of winning the GTT4 and proving myself to the wrestling world should be interesting one. I like Moses have to climb a mighty mountain and meet many obstacles on my way to the top. My first opponent has not given the common courteous of supplying any information about himself. What a sin. My staff of disciples right now are investigating every aspect of his life. Danny Furher will not be a mystery when he steps into the ring with his savior. I will know all of his sins and he will pay for everyone of them. His name will be quickly forgotten as I crucify and save him. Really the only thing that would save him is if he no showed. What Would Hoyt Do brother, WWHD? You I have lead a life of a deity and being so I have little remorse for my past. Those who are the sinners are the ones how live a life of remorse and sickness. It is my job to heel them and here I am, “Your Personal Jesus” Hoyt Williams. =w=
