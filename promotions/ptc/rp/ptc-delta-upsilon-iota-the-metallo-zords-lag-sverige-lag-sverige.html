Delta Upsilon Iota/The Metallo Zords/Lag Sverige

26 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wars may be fought with weapons, but they are won by men. A very dark and cloudy dirt field is the first shot. Explosions and quick reloads enter as all senses begin to get touched. Then a loud cry and across the screen flies the head of a pink bunny, with stuffing coming out of its head. The screen backs up and Adoph Nilsson is playing with a group of toys in what looks to be the garden section of a Wal-Mart. GI Joe, Action Man, Barbie, and her friends, pink stuffed bunnies and generic no name toys are leaned up against various flowerpots. Adoph Nilsson stands up and paces back and forth wearing some camouflage that is two sizes too small. Adoph Nilsson: “Men, this is not a game. This is a war and you have all been from across the toy aisle to come here and fight, fight for you freedom! Some of you may not agree with this war, but this is a war that must be won, for if it is not we are all screwed. Do not mess up, because if you do you will all be killed, by just one man’s mistake. Okay so here is the plan. We are going to rush the back door and when we approach the manure, we will jump into the daisies and seek camp there. MEN! ARE YOU PAYING ATTENTION!” The pink bunny in the back tips over and topples on top of Barbie who then slaps down Action Man and Action man goes to tip over Ken but Ken steps out of the way and hits Action Man with a spinning kick to the face. Ken: “That is for taking my girl biatch!” Adoph stares at the talking Ken doll and then jumps on top of it and starts stomping and punching the small plastic figure. Adoph Nilsson: “DEVIL KEN! AHHH! YOU CAN’T TAKE ME ALIVE! AHHH!” Soon an old man wearing a blue Wal-Mart vest walks past and looks at the Nilsson flipping out at the doll. Wal-Mart Greeter: Well son, what you gone doing? Adoph looks up at the man but continues pounding on the ground. Adoph Nilsson: Saving the world from the Anti-Christ! Wal-Mart Greeter: If that’s the case, ask him if he remembers where I put my diapers. Adoph stops and looks at the man, then at the doll and back at the elderly greeter. Adoph Nilsson: AHHH! Adoph plows through the greeter and runs through the store, straight through the hair care, girl’s clothing, and grocery aisles into Bjorn who has a handful of Spaghetti-Os. The cans go flying and both men are on the ground trying to recuperate. As they do, the intercom sounds. Intercom: Clean up in Aisle 71, and someone find Eugene’s diapers. Thank you. Okay so you’re asking yourself, what the hell does this have to do with anything. I don’t know I just thought I would show you what its like to be a wrestler and be recognized everywhere you go, but I didn’t. Instead I showed you this awesome clip of Adoph flipping out. Here’s a clip of Adoph dancing. Adoph is doing the robot and then stops and a disco ball drops down and then he magically changes into a white leather outfit and dances like Johnny Travolta in Saturday Night Fever. That really had no purpose, I just found it funny. Well I really don’t know what to tell you, maybe they’ll win maybe they won’t. But they did lose their tag titles, and they are probably pretty upset about it, so this wouldn’t be the greatest week to mess with them. Here’s what Bjorn had to say about his opponents, IN HIS SLEEP! Bjorn is laying in bed with a small blue duck in his tight grasp and he is snoring but speaking in full sentences. Bjorn Nilsson: (Asleep) Yeah those stupid teenage college punks think they have what it takes to wrestle with the likes of me. I’ll show them, I mean I can’t beat their asses; they would definitely like that too much. Just think about it, they are in a frat, all frat brothers are gay or just plain weird. They are giving off this stupid stereotypical jock frat and they are definitely in one of those queer frats. NO NO NO NO NO! LET ME PANTS UP! I could kick their asses in a second. Nevertheless, I’m going to have to beat those stupid Zords too. I don’t even know what a Zord is, I just hope it isn’t one of those things that likes to get their asses beat because then I will definitely lose. STAY AWAY! AHHH! AHHH! DON’T SHAVE MY MUSTACHE! They save the world from… oh yeah, grab it like that, yeah you’re a naughty little Norwegian aren’t you. You like it when I do that right there… yeah yeah you do, WHO’S YOUR DADDY!? Yeah sorry about that, ever since my girlfriend broke up with me I have been having some sexual issues and got a little too into the moment. Anyway, that’s what Bjorn said, in his sleep, the true secrets come out in the sleep, but that’s about it. Adoph was just talking about how he was going to take over the world by making a new magical potion that would make everyone eat their own clothing. A white business executive is seen sitting in the corner of his office eating his own clothing. Straight from the Dodo bird’s mouth. Now back to that wet dream... err... is this thing still on?
