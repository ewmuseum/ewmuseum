No Sympathies, Just Undastand My Situation

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 Dat up there? A painkilla. Dey killed Brain Pillman. Killed Crash Holly(Mike Lockwood). Helped kill "Latino Heat" Eddy Guerrero way befo' his time. I could go fa' days about what painkilla's do to da greats in dis sport. So why am I takin em? Look at her. GOT DAMMMIT LOOK AT HER. So I'm s'pposed to just bitch out right? Say da pain is too much for deez 40-year-old bones and call it quits after all these years, work some dock job until I can't go no mo right? Fuck that and fuck you. That's my daughta up there. She may be olda then that picture shows, but dat's da picture in my wallet EVERYDAY. Da lil girl I'll ALWAYS see no matter how old she gets. My sun rises and sets on dat picture of Carmen. You realize losing fa me means a missed child support check? You tryin' ta take me down is food comin out her mouth! CLOTHES OFF HER BACK. Yous might as well smack her plate off da dinna' table. I'm not askin' for ya fuckin tears or sympathies. Sons of the Bronx wipe dey asses with that weak shit. I don't care how broke down I am or how many miles I've racked up in dis bidness. Backing down from a tournament of glorified underwear models, gymnasts, and comedians just ain't mah style. I feel like I've gotta go thru a field of Star Search rejects to win this damn thing. What you bucks are missin' deez days is dat sense of urgency. Da ability to sacrifice. That lil angel ya saw earlier is a maja(major) issue to me and I'll do whateva it takes to keep her provided for. If I gotta bleed a muhfucka, I will. If I gotta come back 2 months befo' mah actual clearance, so be it. Da knee brace'll hold up, it's got to. If it means I risk joining the laundry list of guys who died from da "magic pills" dat make all da pain go away…so be it. Nobody'll miss a never-was like me anyway…shit, I'm no fucking Latino Heat. I'm Joe Balboa. Fuck dat, DA BACK ALLEY BRAWLER. I'm the meanest sumbitch dat eva claimed da Bronx. This ain't bout no braggin rights or sum title shot…this is about dat big ass check at da end. Dat check that's gonna feed my family back home for years to come. For dat…I'd have a full buffet uv dem Perc's up there. Anything that'll keep me straight enough to put the hurtin' on you monkeys. You see what I left behind for dis bidness? Most uv ya'll are so caught up in da glitz of it all dat you don't even think about shit like love…family. I gave up love and family to get what I got now…jack and his tag patna, shit. 20 years and still lookin' for the one moment that makes all da struggle worth it….this event is it. I hope Carmen and Karen are watchin'. I hope dey know I love dem' and that everythign I do is fo them. Every last one uv ya'll that gets laid out…dat's fo those ladies. THEY DESERVE NUTHIN LESS THAN A WIN FROM ME. The missed birthdays. The lack of a fatha around physically. The lonely nights in a bed fah two. They deserve sumthin fo all dat…GTT is it. So don't take it personal and don't give me no sympathy got dammit. When da bell rings, I'm crushin' ya jaw and makin' a necklace out da teeth. Just undastand my situation, undastand I'm just a terrible fatha and husband tryin' to give my family, what's left of it anyway, what they deserve. If there was a painkiller fo' all that frustration and pain I've had ta deal with the past 20 years, I'da OD'ed on it by now. Fa now…all I got is deez Percs and those two photos. Bottom line, I have no problem adding my name to a list of wrestlas(wrestlers) found dead in a hotel wit that dumfounded look on there face; wonderin' why it had to be right then. If they do there job and get me to the finish, da ends justify da means. I'm just not sure my place in Heaven is as assured as ol' Eddie. Now leave me be, I got some magic pills ta take. Undastand my situation.
