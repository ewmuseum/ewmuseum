Payback…

31 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-31 December 16, 2005 (Four days prior to the second round of GTT5) 9:00 a.m. "Hello?" "Brian Arson." The voice on the other end of the phone was very deep, and Brian recognized a sort of Baltic-like accent. "Yes? And who the fuck is this?" Brian replied forcefully, knowing by the tone of the man’s voice that the conversation wouldn’t be leading to anything legal. Brian’s crass response was something he’d developed over the years in order to intimidate any opposition. Unfortunately, the mystery caller seemed to be unmoved by the harshness of Brian’s voice. "Please, spare me any bullshit, I don’t have the time. My name is not important at the moment, but you will learn it soon enough. You are in Connecticut; I know this. Tonight, you will be in Columbus, Ohio, and in room 657 of the Hyatt Regency downtown, no later than 11 p.m." "Is that so?" Brian replied smugly, yet the man seemed to instill a fear inside him. "Well, I suppose this is where I say that I’m too busy to be pestered by a crazy foreigner over the telephone. But of course, I know how these things work, so what is it you have to keep me from denying your request?" "You will find out soon enough, but I suggest you come as this could be your last week of freedom should you choose to remain where you are. The flights are available; see you soon." 10:30 p.m. "Ah, Mr. Arson," he said, and Brian immediately recognized the voice as the one from earlier in the day. "I was worried your arrogance would get the best of you, and we’d miss you!" "Yes, well, I guess you’re not the best judge of character then," Brian grinned sarcastically. The man laughed and motioned for his partner to leave the room. The beast of a man left quietly, shutting the door behind him. "I’m assuming there are many questions that you have for me. The foremost being, who am I?" He paused for a moment, grabbing a glass sitting beside him and took a drink. "Well, my name is Mikael Solokov, and I am going to offer you a little proposition." The man paused, making sure he had Brian’s complete attention. "You see, I have only lived in the states for about five years now, but I am very familiar with you and your… business… on the West Coast, and it interests me. So much so that I’ve decided to take it over for you." "Ha!" Brian laughed. "Impossible. You could never buy it off me, I wouldn’t have it." "Oh, Mr. Arson, how naive you are. This is non-negotiable, in fact, whether or not you’ll allow it has nothing to do with this meeting. You see, you are the first person in your ‘organization’ to know any of this. Everything’s been planned out and negotiated. All of you on the West Coast are now completely out of the equation as I plan on spreading outward across the country with your network." "That’s impossible! We’ll stop you; your men would be taken care of before you had the chance to make a dime, assuming you’re not bluffing in the first place!" Mikael released a deep laugh that frightened Brian. "And that is where you come in handy, my friend! You will make sure your men stay out of this affair, or you may just find yourself rotting away behind bars." He pulled a manila folder out from beneath the load of papers he held in his hand and placed them on the coffee table in front of him so that it faced Brian. Brian looked down at the folder quizzically, unsure what to make of it. "This folder here can make or break you, Brian." Mikael smirked delightedly. "Well, maybe just break you." He opened the folder and Brian gazed down at its contents. His eyes widened, jaw dropping instantly. The manila folder contained a plethora of photographs, photographs of him. Incriminating images involving him and well-known Central American drug lords as well as men that were already being held in the American prison system. Brian reached down and flipped through the photographs, each one slowly spelling out his untimely demise. October 25, 2006 Brian Arson stood patiently out front of what appeared to be an abandoned apartment building, his arms folded as he waited for his men to get out of the black Lincoln parked behind him. Four more men emerged from the car, all as large as Brian but with looks twice as menacing. Adjusting their suits, the men glanced upward at the four-story facility, all five taking deep breaths. Brian’s eyes darted back and forth down the street and then he smiled. "I'm Brian Arson… and this here's the Russian-Mafia-Massacre trick!" He reached into his jacket and cocked a gun hidden underneath. Cue Jackass theme music. The men enter the old apartment building via kicking in the front door… All hell breaks loose. From outside, one could see the building light up like a firecracker as rounds upon rounds of bullets are fired. Inside, bloody bodies littered the ground as Brian and his cronies made their way down the second floor hallway, kicking open the worn wooden doors along the way. The moaning and screaming of mutilated and unsuspecting victims ripped through the decrepit halls as Brian and his crew moved on, their suits stained in crimson. The sound of feet scrambling above triggered Brian and his men to keep moving up the next flight of stairs to the third floor, reloading their weapons as they climbed. At the next landing, Brian paused as foreign shouts resounded from beyond the door. The voices became clearer as they approached the stairwell, and the intruders recognized the distinct Russian accents. Without hesitation, Brian unloaded a round from his black Uzi Carbine through the door and wall, jolting himself back as the 9mm cartridges dropped to the ground. Two dull thuds sounded, and one of Brian's men rushed passed him and kicked open the door. Slumped along the wall was a man in a white t-shirt and jeans with an AK-47 resting on his hip and bullet holes implanted in his chest. To his side another man lay face down on the ground, a stream of blood slowly spewing from underneath him. "Jon, Rick, search the rest of the floor," Brian ordered as he pointed at two of the men. "Sam, Chris, come with me!" Brian turned back into the stairwell and crept up to the top floor, his men following a short distance behind him. Once he reached the next landing, Brian listened closely for any movement and then propped the door open with his foot. Sam came up behind him to try and get a peak through the crack of the door but the awkward angle made it impossible to see in one direction. Brian motioned for him to go in, and Sam slipped through with Chris following. As soon as they entered, a stream of gunfire came hailing down upon them. "AH FUCK!" Chris yelled as two bullets ripped through his right shoulder. Sam and Brian immediately angled their guns in the direction of the shots and began firing, taking the shooter down within seconds. Brian quickly knelt down beside his injured friend. "Chris… shit… are you all right?" Brian tore open Chris's shirt to reveal the wound. "Fuck… all right… it's not that bad, but we can't leave him alone." Brian paused a moment and looked down the hallway trying to think of what to do. He twisted to look the other way and then turned back to Sam. "Okay, here's what's gonna happen. You stay here with Chris; I'm going to find Mikael's office before he has a chance to escape." "I can't let you do that boss," Sam said as he rose to his feet. "Well, guess what, we don't have much of a fuckin' choice! So you listen to me and stay right here with Chris and as soon as Rick and Jon get up here, you come and back me up." Sam gritted his teeth but reluctantly agreed. Brian grabbed Chris's gun and stood up, reloading once again. Brian took off down the hallway, gun at the ready. Sweat streamed down the side of his head as he bit into his lower lip. Breathing heavily, he walked toward the last door on the left and put his ear against it. Tick. Tick. Tick. Brian planted himself closer to try to identify the monotonous clicking sound, but he just couldn’t put a finger on it. It wasn’t a clock; he knew that for sure, so whatever it was he assumed it couldn’t be good. He knew he couldn’t wait any longer so with a quick burst from his gun he took out the door handle and kicked it in. BANG! Brian hit the deck as a huge blast shot past him through the doorframe followed by a hail of bullets. Creeping toward the edge of the door he looked up to see a turret gun standing no farther than three feet from the door, a motion sensor implanted on top. Working a commando crawl the best he could, he slithered under the hail of bullets as they nearly grazed the top of his head. By the time he was done crawling over the burning bullet shells, the turret gun had run out of ammunition. He quickly got up and looked around; the front room appeared empty. "Shit!" Brian gritted his teeth as he glanced down at an old wooden desk cluttered with tattered papers and a laptop. Apart from a small lamp in the corner, the computer screen provided the only light in the room. Shuffling through the papers, his eyes widened as he noticed something strange. Immediately he drew his gun as he realized the documents sitting on the table would be too important for Solokov to leave behind. Tip-toeing slowly, he headed toward the back room where he could barely see a foot in front of his face. He tried flipping the light switch to no avail, but a small glow caught his eye; a light shining from underneath another door against the far wall, a bathroom perhaps. Brian took a deep breath as he moved stealthily toward the door, but the loud creak of an old floorboard disrupted the silence of the room. He cringed and paused for a second. Nothing. When Brian finally reached the door he could hear the steady gush of a shower and let out a smiling sigh. "Here goes nothing," he whispered to himself. With a swift kick he knocked open the door and aimed his gun toward the shower curtain and shouted, "Solokov, get the fuc—" … "Shit." As Brian began to turn around, he already knew he was in trouble. A large object came barreling down on his back, shattering across the floor and knocking him to the ground. Brian screamed out in pain as he tried to get back up, but a large boot caught him square in the ribs knocking one of the Uzis loose from his pants. As soon as the gun hit the floor, the room lit up like a spastic strobe light. A bullet cut through Brian’s leg while another hit the other man in the foot. The large man cried out and forced himself on top of Brian as blood squirted out of his shoe. Brian flipped around just in time to come face-to-face with Mikael Solokov. It had been a just over a year since Brian had seen him last; the circumstances a little different then. Brian lay there struggling as Mikael’s large frame rested on top of him trying to keep him pinned. Brian tightened up each time the Russian’s leg dug into his wound, but he couldn’t help but smile right at the man, and they both knew. Brian was a fighter. No weapons, no problem. Mikael pushed all his weight down onto Brian, but the former professional wrestler grabbed the man’s arms to hold him up. The fall had torn Brian’s suit apart and the tears widened as his arms bulged through the fabric so as to not be crushed. With all the strength he could muster, Brian finally flipped the Russian on to his back and rolled over on top of him. Brian tried to get to his feet but collapsed under the agony he felt in his leg. Mikael’s eyes widened in horror as Brian grabbed a piece of broken glass and tried to jam it into his chest. This time Mikael had both of Brian’s arms, clinging on for his life, but Brian brought the glass down inch-by-inch, overpowering the man. But instead of stabbing Mikael, Brian came down with a crushing head butt that quite possibly broke the man’s nose. He rolled off the Russian who remained squirming in pain. "So this is it, big man?" Brian taunted between breaths. "This is what you call an operation? This is what you’ve done with the shit you stole from me? This is it?" Brian laughed as he grabbed a chair to pull himself up. He picked up the fallen Uzi and dug out a casing from his suit to reload. The smile on Brian’s face faded almost as quickly as it appeared, and he spat on Mikael. "This is pathetic!" Brian shouted. "No, no… it’s beyond pathetic to a point that… that for once I’m at a loss for words… you try to steal my business! MY NETWORK! Who the FUCK do you think you are!" Brian seethed heavily, nearly foaming at the mouth. "Oh, sir… you thought you had nailed me… you thought you had me down to a tee, but I’m afraid you greatly underestimated what you were getting yourself into…" Brian cocked the gun and pointed it down toward the Russian who looked up in horror. "WAIT, PLEASE NO WAIT!" Mikael pleaded on the verge of tears, his voice nasally. "I can fix this! YOU NEED ME!" "I need you?" Brian laughed. "And what… exactly… would I need you for? "I have information!" Mikael stuttered as Brian grinned like a man possessed. "Weapons! I have weapons too, anything you could possibly need! I HAVE IT!" "Well, Mr. Solokov, as inviting as that sounds, it looks to me as if I have all the information I need in these two rooms." Brian smiled and twisted around, holding his gun out in a lackadaisical manner. "And as far as weapons go… I’m sure we can manage." "WAIT, NO! DON’T—" "There’s no more waiting, sir; a year of waiting’s been long enough!" "BUT—" "You had me… and you let me go…" Brian lifted the gun toward Mikael’s head. "NO—" Brian unloaded a burst from the Uzi, turning the Russian’s chest into a bloody mess. He stood there for a second, a frown on his face, and shook his head. Sam stepped into the doorway a second later looking at Brian and then glancing down at the body. "How’s Chris?" Brian asked. "He’ll be fine… are you okay?" Sam asked, glancing down at Brian’s leg. "We need to do something about that." Brian shook his head. "We don’t have time; take Chris down to the car. Get Jon and Rick and tell them to get the hell up here quick and grab all the shit in here. We may find something we want but who knows. In any case, we’re done here, so let’s get moving." Without hesitation, Sam left the room to get his partners. Brian knelt down next to Mikael and reached into the man’s pocket, pulling out his wallet. He took it and stuck it in his own jacket for safe keeping. Grabbing a towel from the bathroom, he covered the dead man’s face. He looked around the room and spotted a safe half hidden by the silk curtains and locked with a keypad. Brian sat for several minutes trying to figure out how to get it open, his eyes darting back and forth in deep thought. Minutes later Jon and Rick came scurrying into the room, both covered in blood, and Rick with a long gash across his cheek that continued to bleed. "Battle scars," Brian smirked, "they build character. Go through both these rooms and grab what you can off the desks and whatever’s in the closet." Brian still stood staring at the safe in front of him. "And what about that?" Jon asked as he noticed the safe and realized Brian’s dilemma. His attention was then quickly diverted to the corpse lying on the ground. "Pretty sight, isn’t it?" Brian chuckled. "And yet," Brian turned to the body, "quite possibly the answer to my little problem." Brian reached down to Mikael’s bloody body and grabbed the right arm. Taking the sleeve of the man’s shirt, he ripped it apart. Jon stood looking at him quizzically. "Give me a light," Brian ordered. Reaching into his back pocket, Jon produced a small flashlight and pointed it in partner’s direction. Brian flipped Mikael’s wrist in the direction of the light, and upon closer look, a series of numbers could be seen just below the bloody palm. Brian smiled delightedly and looked up at Jon. "Not much use when you’re dead, right?" Brian dropped the arm to the ground and stood up. "Get that safe open; that’s the reason we’re here. Wipe as much as you can down; I’ll be waiting outside." Brian turned to the front room. "Rick, hurry up! Pack that shit in one of those boxes over there and bring it downstairs!" Right before exiting, he turned around. "And for God’s sake, make sure you get that man’s clothes, or else we’re all fucked. I’m leaking like a God damn runny faucet." The steps outside the apartment building were lined with a trail of blood that led to the black Lincoln. The street was as quiet and empty as it had been when the men first entered. The street lights created an eerie atmosphere as shadows seemed to appear within every alley, behind every dumpster. Brian appeared in the front entrance’s broken doorframe, sunglasses on, and his long, blonde locks dancing wildly in the cool night breeze. He limped down the stairs, his right pant leg stained in blood. Sam, who was sitting on the car smoking a cigarette, turned at the sound of footsteps, gun at the ready. Next to him lay a small wood pole with a damp rag wrapped around the end. As soon as he saw Brian, he rushed over to help him, supporting his friend’s bloodied side. Looking into the Lincoln, Brian grimaced as he caught a glimpse of Chris lying in the backseat, his chest covered in blood. He was comforted a little as he saw the slow rise and fall of his friend’s chest. The trunk of the car was open and empty save for a few half-bottles of ammonia and a small tank of gasoline that had been pushed to the back. "You made sure you covered everything, right?" Brian motioned toward the ammonia and gasoline. "Anything and everything, boss," Sam replied. "Once that baby goes up, we’ll need to be out of here like a bat out of hell." "You got that right," Brian shuttered. "Those boys better hurry the fuck up or else it won’t even matter." No sooner had the words left his mouth then Rick and Jon came clambering down the front steps with cardboard boxes overflowing with goods. "All right, men, show’s over!" Brian shouted. "Sam, light her up!" Sam grabbed the pole from the hood of the car and whipped a lighter out of his pocket. Jon and Rick threw the boxes into the trunk and slammed it shut. They jumped into the backseat of the car and tended to Chris. Brian hobbled his way over to the passenger side door while keeping an eye on Sam as he ran up the front steps and into the building. Moments later the bright yellow and orange blaze of the fire shone out the front door as Sam reemerged, running down to the car. Brian opened the passenger side door and took one more glance at the building as it went up in flames. His dark shades reflected the fiery glow, but his face remained free of emotion. Sam jumped into the driver’s seat and Brian hopped in next to him. "Let’s get movin’!" Sam shouted as he started the car and slammed down on the gas. Brian turned to the backseat to check on Chris and then noticed Jon and Rick looking awkwardly at each other. "So… how’d it go?" Brian asked with a hint of sarcasm. "Well, sir," Jon piped up, "the safe… you’re not gonna believe what we found…" To Be Continued…
