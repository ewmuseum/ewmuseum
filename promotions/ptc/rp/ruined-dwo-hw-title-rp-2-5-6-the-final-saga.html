Ruined....[DWO HW Title rp#2,5/6 The Final Saga]

Kaiowas Cable Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 52 Posted: Fri Apr 11, 2003 9:07 pm Post subject: Ruined....[DWO HW Title rp#2,5/6 The Final Saga] Loss? Do you know? Do you actually know what it means? "Loss"? To lose.... to misplace? Not in all meanings of the word.... The only thing Kaiowas has misplaced right now is his confidence. All due to loss. Loss has toppled him to where he now stands. He stands with doubt in his actions. He stands to lose everything because of loss.... He stands to lose, all because of Lecood.... Lecood, an old ally of Kaiowas, meaning a hell of a lot to the young Warrior of the Ktulu. The man he WAS going to rely upon to help him in his forthcoming battles. His next tests, his greatest trials. Donny Diamond, and then Donny Diamond and thirteen other men... But before that, Kaiowas' greatest moment in his life, would have to come to pass..... He had to do it... He had come to far to avoid it.... He had no wish to back down... The hatred had not relented... Not even due to his loss... For Kaiowas not only blamed himself for Lecood's death, but he blamed HIM also.... Him. The one to start all this. The one whose existence has brought about all of what Kaiowas has achieved and... Unfortunately.... All of what Kaiowas has lost. It is all down to him.... The Lord of Vengeance, the one they call.... Pultra. The End? Would it bring it to that? Would it close this seemingly everlasting conflict of jealousy, hatred, greed and bloodshed? Would Kaiowas be able to survive even if he could end it? Would it just be too much for the Tribal Warrior come Lord of the Ktulu? Could he muster the last few threads of his strength and overthrow the beast that had spited him and used him as a slave? Kaiowas did not believe that he would fail in slaying Pultra.... But would his own life be forfeit too? In the process of destroying the now mortal fallen Angel, would he fall to the blade of the huge impostor god? Or would Kaiowas reign triumphantly, bringing the final vengeance upon the creator of the word? Would Kaiowas bring the justice needed to the Ktulu, the people that Pultra had relied upon for centuries? Was he able to fight the emotional war also? He was strong, not just physically, but could he handle ANYTHING that Pultra could and would throw at him...? Would he be able to reach the moment when he plunges the blade of Lecood into the heart of his old master as justice for the old man's death...? He had decreed that the end is nigh.... But for who? Comrades? Kaiowas has never lacked comrades and their support, even the now deceased Lecood had offered to accompany him on his journey to destroy Pultra. The battle weary Kaiowas knows that the old man would have died in that conflict too, but he had wished that he could have survived to see the end of the fake god who had put the Ktulu through so much. The Ktulu themselves were full of praise for Kaiowas, but Kaiowas saw through it all, he could see the fear borne inside every single one of his warriors, hunters and even his only elder, Cerra. They all feared what might happen to Kaiowas and then directly to the Ktulu themselves if Kaiowas failed to be victorious... Kaiowas had no comrades on this last journey.... The ones that had offered to come either lie in shallow graves or sit in silent prayer, beseeching any god that will listen to rid them of this terrible curse... To rid them of Pultra. Kaiowas had only ever had one full time companion, and now she rested on the side of Pultra, a servant to the end it seemed... His own pet jaguar, Rula, gifted to him by Pultra in the early stages of their tumultuous relationship, was on the other side of the fence... She would face him, and the black as night jaguar would have to fall.... Revenge? The word rings true for everyone... Everyone wants this, everyone desires revenge.... As if it were a commodity, something you could buy or trade for.... Revenge. And Kaiowas desired it. He knows that it would never be easy and he knows that he has all the pressure of the world on his shoulders but..... But he would feel the sweet taste of the end of a sour story. The taste of victory never dulls, it is always invigorating, energising, rejuvenating and wonderful. It is the biggest rush that any single person can experience.... And it is all because Kaiowas hates his opponents.... And how sweet would this revenge be... How beautiful would it be to take Pultra's life from him, in his own home and with the blades of Lecood. Kaiowas had gathered these blades and as a mark of respect to the great old lithe hunter, he had attached them to the ends of his huge Tribal Staff. Kaiowas was armed and deadly with his bladed staff, and he would not let revenge escape him, he would not allow Lecood's vengeance to slip between his fingers, nor would he let the beast die without full punishment..... [The place that Kaiowas had first warned the foolish god, Pultra, that he was not going to live with everything that Pultra ordered was a palace. Full of gold and rare expensive artworks and sculptures. It is a world of luxury and relaxation. It is for many people, a view of what heaven would be for them. For Pultra, it is what his power brought him, for him it is a little reward to himself for being such a good "god". His luxury, his laxness, his power all showed off in one big mansion that houses him from now until eternity. Kaiowas had been there, and he had hated it. He hated the show of wealth, the show of "power". It appealed to him in no way. He would despise living like that. He was born to live rough, as he does everyday. Kaiowas saw the roots of Pultra's failures as a "god". He saw the weaknesses and now he even remembers the whole layout of the palace itself. He remembers where he entered through a large mirror portal, he remembers every single corridor he had to trek down to find where he was going. For Kaiowas this seemed easier than it seemed... Of course it had to be... Because killing Pultra would not be...] Kaiowas: (chanting) Ohleth Hallaph Thillu Dan. [Kaiowas stands on the outskirts of his village, never having returned after the burial of Lecood. He could not face the people before he left them to determine their future. Everything lies on his shoulders and he knows it, their expectations ask nothing less than all that he can give, and possibly even more than he could normally give. The night fell a long time ago. The moon that shone so brightly in the darkness of last night is now hiding behind a thick cloud that has fallen over the whole of Iowa at this time... The small fire in front of Kaiowas is rimmed with rocks and stones, keeping the burning within a ring, not letting it spread to the flammable objects that surround it. Kaiowas stands in a long black robe, with the hood thrown back, the tails of the robe flow in the strong breeze that seems to come from the east. His gaze is fixed on the fires before him but his whole body is moving seamlessly as he chants.] Kaiowas: (chanting) Ohleth Hallaph Thillu Dan. [He holds his staff in his hands, gripping and twirling it in time with his chants. His body moves slowly from pose to pose as the little ritual goes on. The flammable objects that surround the fire are long strips of linen, prepared especially for this ceremony. On them are painted many images of the history of this conflict. On one a picture of two large hands are depicted shaking. Painted with the green dye of the long grass in the woods, it symbolised the time of peace between Kaiowas and Pultra... Slowly Kaiowas breaks his cycle and ducks to pick up the thin cloth. Looking at it, reminiscing about the time when it was...... peaceful. Quickly the hatred enters his eyes and he throws the rag into the flames, erupting it silent burn into a haze of flame and smoke. The fire gobbles the extremely flammable cloth up quickly and soon returns to its silent ways. Kaiowas himself returns to moving and chanting...] Kaiowas: (chanting) Thaller Khilju Sadra Haimec. [Kaiowas arms seem to be the focus of his movement from pose to pose. His chanting has changed and the area around him darkens, while strangely the fire brightens... It's blades of heat lancing forth into the midnight sky. The hate and anger that was housed in the piece of cloth seem to have given the fire a new fuel to burn on... Emotion. Slowly and with the sways and movements of the dance, Kaiowas draws forth a little box, whittled from oak. Opening it and still dancing slowly, he daubs his two fingers inside. He smears the white gooey paste that he finds inside on his left breast over his heart. Lancing forth he lifts another of the thin strips of linen. This one he holds up to his eyes and stares at the image, taking a short rest from the dancing. He sees the symbols of friendship and of comradeship. He also sees a hunter with two wrist spikes rough painted on in a warm orange colour, mixed from the juices of many fruits. Again he chants...] Kaiowas: (chanting) Thaller Khilju Sadra Haimec. [Slowly moving the cloth in one hand towards the daubed on substance he continues his chanting. He glues the linen onto his breast with the substance, ensuring Lecood is in his heart. Again reaching into the little box, he applies more of the substance to his biceps. Picking up more cloth he proceeds to attach these too. These strips are lined with many many symbols of life and strength, youth and vitality. Swirling twisting tattoo-like symbols painted on with red berry juice now line his arms signifying his strength. Kaiowas repeats the daubing but on his legs and a few small daubs on his forehead. He now places a smaller piece of cloth with the Ktulu symbol for knowledge on his forehead. Next he adds lightning bolt decorated linen strips to his legs, hopefully protecting him from any sort of magic Pultra may try, and at the same time, symbolising speed and agility. Finally, with the remaining cloth, he wraps his staff up in it. The staff is covered in clenched fists, victorious symbols. Everything that he will need to defeat Pultra is emblazoned on his body or his weapon. Starting the dance again another chant erupts from his mouth. The dance is faster and Kaiowas moves a lot more. The fire erupts from within the small circle of rocks and grows large. Strangely it has no fuel source but the emotions and feelings that emanate from Kaiowas as he prepares for war...] Kaiowas: (chanting) Morte ALA Diuh Pahvrai [The fire burns bright at the new chant. The words burn true in Kaiowas' head and heart, they evoke the passion within him, bringing the fighter and warrior in him to the surface. Death to the fake god.... The heat itself raises the emotional temperature. Kaiowas begins to scowl as he contemplates what he is going to do. He thinks off all the things that Pultra has done that have angered him, and they anger him once more. The heat spreads the energy through his skin and then into his flesh as he finishes his dance...] Kaiowas: (chanting) Morte ALA Diuh Pahvrai. [Kaiowas stops and everything is still and silent bar the crackling of the now huge fire that seems to have been conjured by the whole ritual. Kaiowas looks deep into the fire and knows he is ready. Throwing the little box of salve into the flames. The fire nearly jumps at the little box and soon burns through it, the heat insatiable in its hunger. Kaiowas breathes in deeply and calms his heart beat with one of his relaxation techniques. Slowly stepping forward he enters the burning inferno-like furnace of the huge flames in front of him. No pain, no scream. The flames do not burn him, the cloth on his arms glow deep red, the ink of the berries slowly binding with the magic poured into the cloth hours ago. The other symbols all melt and mould, propagating their colour throughout the white linen. Soon the linen itself begins to melt. The symbols of strength melts into his biceps, the symbols of courage and his old companion Lecood melts into his heart and all the other assorted colours and images all melt slowly, becoming one with the Warrior's skin, forming semi-permanent tattoos and more importantly... A semi-permanent boon from the earth of all these attributes and strengths...] Kaiowas: Here I come Pultra.... [Kaiowas' last words on the mortal plane before he attempts to kill Pultra fall on deaf ears as no-one else is here to witness his last stand. Swiftly the waves of flames engulf Kaiowas and his body disappears into the night. The fire slowly burns down and finally out, leaving a pure black area where it was so bright just moments ago...] *** FLASH *** [... And it is once more... Except it isn't... All light has hit the place where Kaiowas steps out of the flames... but it isn't in Iowa. No... The light that flashes gives the large Brazilian Warrior a glimpse of his immediate surroundings. Dark dank ruins surround him. Smells of dead flesh and wasted food reach his nostrils. The sky above is brooding and bizarre. The sky is a mix of black and red clouds. Thunder strikes and the rumbling noise reverberates around the small roofless room that Kaiowas has arrived in. Behind him, the portal that had opened momentarily shut as quickly. The fire retracted and disappeared in mid air. Kaiowas stands alone with his newly skin paintings and his old bladed staff. Holding the notched weapon tightly Kaiowas tries to use his skills of hunting to figure out any clues of where he is...] Kaiowas: (whispered) Where in the....? [The place where Kaiowas remembered Pultra residing was nothing like this lifeless ruin that lay before his eyes. Where were the paintings, the colourful wallpapering, the beautiful sculptures and the large suits of armour? Where were the luscious carpets, the huge drapes and all the glorious fruit that lay in bowls scattered around the mansion? Was this even Pultra's residence? Would he be here? Was it a trap? It really did not ease Kaiowas' doubts and worries that he could not place where he was... Kaiowas drops to a crouch and scours the floor for anything flammable for a source of light. With his hands he searches, his eyes not as efficient in this total lack of light... The dark itself seemed to represent a brooding atmosphere. Everything seemed to be watching him, following him, waiting to strike.] Kaiowas: Aha.... [Plucking a big bouquet of dead and decaying flowers from the ground Kaiowas is successful. Now picking up two small stones he sets to lighting the end of the flowers by striking the stones against one another. Soon a spark flies into the dried foliage and it burns slowly into the fuel source. Kaiowas stands and realises that there is an open hallway exit from this room to his left. Holding his staff in one hand and the flowers burning bright in the other, he sets off into the darkness, choking it with the light of his torch... Entering the hallway he sees the ruined building more clearly. The rubble tat used to be proper walls lies scattered and the surviving masonry looks very suspect and dangerous. Kaiowas slowly steps through the dead hallway, avoiding the walls at all costs. Treading silently he soon reaches a T-junction. Stopping for a second Kaiowas gets a sharp jolt of deja-vu.... Was this the palace? Was it just a ruin of the place Kaiowas had visited to argue with Pultra three and a half months ago? Looking quickly to the floor he finds the evidence he needs..... A torn fine carpet lies in disarray, soiled and dirtied by every type of stain possible, through the dirt the fine red colour still shines through faintly. Kaiowas stoops and tears a bit off, it comes away easily. He stays crouched and examines the rug.... It's tassles and it's colour are all the same as the rug he had trodden on before... Maybe this was the palace..] Kaiowas: (pensive) Maybe.... [Maybe it was... But why was it like this? Why was it in such a foul and soiled state? No fake god or angel would want to live like this. Pultra especially would not stand for this if there were any alternative... Maybe that was it... Maybe there wasn't an alternative to this... Maybe Kaiowas' struggle against the great "Lord of Vengeance" had actually paid off in the form of a weakness forming in Pultra. It seems to Kaiowas now that his fight against Pultra had forced him to cast aside the unimportant things like luxury unless he would suffer even more. Perhaps Pultra had been weakened so much by the defection of the Ktulu people that the destruction of this palace symbolised the destruction of his support, power and reputation... For Kaiowas one thing was certain... And that was that this palace was where he first challenged the Lord of Vengeance... He now returns months later to finish what he had started and destroy the falsity that is Pultra... Kaiowas feels extremely encouraged by this and all the thoughts of his previous boldness bring the same feeling to the fore right now. He feels like he cannot fail, failure to him is now an impossibility. The boldness turns his thoughts to the fight itself and Kaiowas feels his muscles tense subconsciously preparing for the workout of their lives...] Kaiowas: (whispered to himself) Let's go.... [Soon Kaiowas is fully up to speed and he is recognising every single corridor as if it were just like it had been only months ago. Every luxury lay wasted and destroyed but Kaiowas could still follow the mind map he had stored from that encounter. He knew every turning, every hall and he knew where he must go to be able to find Pultra.... He was so close and his heart was beating so fast he nearly missed the huge black object that appeared in front of him suddenly..] *** CRUNCH *** [Unluckily he didn't miss it physically and Kaiowas tumbles as he collides with the waist high black shadow. His flowers-cum-torch fly out of his hand and set light to the carpet ten yards away, starting a slow burning inferno which eats up the carpet as it travels back towards Kaiowas and the shadow. Kaiowas pulls himself to his knees and, with the light provided by the flames, he sees what has knocked him over...] Kaiowas: You..... [Quickly reaching for his staff Kaiowas assumes a battle pose, ready to defend himself from his sneak attacker. As the attacker closes in, walking on all fours, it's shape comes into the bright light of the fire. There in front of Kaiowas, The Betrayer, stands the one loyal servant of Pultra. Rula the jaguar...] Kaiowas: Rula.... You betrayed me, I will not let anything block my path to Pultra, not even such an old friend as you.... Heed my words for they will be the last words you hear! [The anger inside Kaiowas wells up because of his old emotional link to Rula. She used to be his fighting companion. On multiple occasions Rula had saved Kaiowas' life and had done him a great service... But that was only when he had served Pultra. Now as Kaiowas was no longer Pultra's servant, Rula was bound to destroy him, bound by her allegiance and bound by her spirit. She was no normal jaguar... She was a familiar... A beast who was used by the gods as a link to their mortal servants.. Created by the gods to keep an eye on their loyal or disloyal workers... And Rula was Kaiowas' familiar...] Kaiowas: Rula.... I am bound.... to destroy you... Just as you are bound... to defend your master.... [Rula approaches Kaiowas in her slow lethargic way, only stopping but a yard from Kaiowas. Kaiowas senses something extremely weird about this. He senses no hostility from Rula, he feels as he did when she was loyal to him. Releasing his right hand from his staff he reaches forward, perhaps acting too much on instincts but he seems to trust Rula once more. She ducks her head and lets him touch her fine black shining coat. Purrs soon come from her animal voice box and Kaiowas seems to feel a little weird... As soon as he touched Rula... he started getting mental... well mental words...] *** Kaiowas... I will not fight you... I cannot fight you... I doubt Pultra also... I doubt his power, I doubt his stature... I will allow you to wipe aside my doubts...... I will give you... The opportunity to destroy him without my interventions.... The victor shall be my master.... Now GO! *** [Kaiowas is shocked and taken aback by the mental words, they most certainly come from Rula. Having no time to ponder it all Kaiowas lets go of his old pet and turns only to find the fire blocking his route. Taking a running jump he launches himself through the flames that lick at his body as it passes overhead. Kaiowas hits the ground running and is off around the corners again until he sees where he should be... The one last room of the palace is covered in the luxury of before... Over the doorway a cloth is draped. Kaiowas pushes it aside angrily and he enters. All around him he can see the only room left living is decaying... He sees the fruit in the bowls going bad. He sees the wooden furniture being eaten away by dust mites, he sees the masonry crumble and he sees the plants rot. One thing he cannot see however...is Pultra....] Kaiowas: Where are you?
