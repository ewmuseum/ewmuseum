Come Fly With Me

22 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-22 [I hate packed planes. I hate not sitting in First Class. I’m the leader of Devil’s Own for Christ’s sake, I should be allowed to sit with the freakin’ pilot if I so wish.] Harley Note: Obtain a Pilot’s Licence. In fact, that’s too much like hard work. Just get a Pilot’s hat – that should command some respect! [The WWA are currently touring the UK and Ireland. I’m on my way to our next Meltdown taping in Belfast. Unfortunately, the WWA being the cheap bastards they are, we’re travelling on a low-budget airline called EasyJet. This is really humiliating. I mean, look at these worthless losers. They don’t have two pennies to rub together. The guy sitting next to me smells so bad I could cry.] Harley Note: Abolish BO. On second thought, encourage BO…then I’ll smell even more gorgeous in comparison to these peons! [I struggle to get my hand into my pocket – it’s like performing Shakespeare in a coffin. I manage to pull out a sheet of paper that was given to me by my Personal Secretary. It is to provide me with a little background info on my next PTC opponent. His name is Robb Hardy.] Harley Note: Rid the world of people who’s surname is Hardy…except for Oliver Hardy. Man, that dude was funny. [I read a few of his statistics. Six foot one, two hundred and twenty six pounds…man, he’ll have his work cut out trying to beat me. He calls himself ‘The Gangsta’.] Harley Note: Chastise everyone who misspells words to sound cool. Open the Harley School of Grammar. [Before I can read any further, I’m interrupted by the sound of giggling coming from the seat in front of me. A really childish, annoying giggle. After the momentary distraction, I return to my sheet of paper, but before I have chance to read even a word I hear another childish laugh. I roll up the in-flight magazine and peer through the seats in front – ready to wallop the kid over the head and tell her to shut the hell up. I am shocked to discover two adults sitting there! A couple to be precise.] Harley Note: Rid the world of young love. I want my paperboy to deliver on time and not be distracted by the girl across the street! [I sit back. Maybe the woman – who I make to be about 23 – just has a really unfortunate laugh. I smirk as I think of those less fortunate than me and turn back to my briefing on Robb Hardy.] Harley Note: Rob is spelt with one ‘b’. Either Jill made a typo when compiling this information or this guy is stupider than he looks – either way, Jill’s not getting her Christmas bonus this year. [And that’s when the worst thing imaginable happens. The absolute worst case scenario. They started talking…] Man: Is my wittle baby waby looking forward to Meltdown? [My God, not only where they stupid. They were going to Meltdown!] Woman: Yes pwease. Fank you for the tickets. Man: Happy Birthday my wittle sweetie. Harley Note: Couples should never use baby talk in public. It makes me look bad when I don’t laugh at them. [She giggles some more. I can’t stand it. My eyes dart around the plane. There must be an emergency exit somewhere. I find it. They’re sitting in the emergency aisle! Great! Now all I have to do is reach over and jettison them. With my stealthy, super-cool, cat-like skills, I lean over and slowly reach towards the handle…] Man: Hey! I know you! You’re Dave Harley! Harley: I…uh…um… [I’m stunned. The sudden change from baby to adult seems to have confused my brain patterns and I can’t speak. I just stand there dumbfounded and sign autographs for them. I then sit back down and try to reboot my brain.] Harley Note: Never let your guard down. When these weirdo’s strike you never know what’ll happen. [As the plane lands I make a sharp exit. After all, if those idiots try to talk to me it might look like I was with them! At least I didn’t lose all my senses. I signed the autographs as Robb Hardy. Now he’ll have to live with the embarrassment of meeting those lunatics – Dave Harley never saw them in his life!]
