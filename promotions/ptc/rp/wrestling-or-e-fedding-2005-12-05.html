Wrestling… or E-fedding???

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 "I don’t get it Greg…" A large, bald, black man paced back and forth with his head down as he spoke to a man sitting on a couch. The man pacing, wearing black dress pants and a white long-sleeved dress shirt, is Matthew Jackson. He walked back and forth in front of his younger brother Greg, a much smaller person than Matthew. "You should be EMBRACING this kind of opportunity!" Despite the fact that he was being lectured in a very serious manner, Greg seemed very disinterested. His mind was elsewhere, and arguing with his brother wasn’t high on his priority list. At two o’clock in the afternoon, Greg was wearing gray sweatpants and no shirt, revealing his well-toned upper body. Looking at Greg’s eyes, one could assume he’d just woken up… or rather, been woken up by someone else. "And why would you bring this up now? I got news for ya! It’s too late!" So here are the questions that need to be answered… What is Matthew talking about? What did Greg do? Who is Sledge? …Cancel that last question. Turns out that nobody wants to know. "You’re wrestling in GTT5, there’s no way out of it. And after that’s over and done with, hopefully you’ll get a contract offer from a company. You can’t continue to sit around here and do nothing!" Greg simply stared blankly ahead with his elbow on his right knee, his head resting in his hand. Matthew stopped mid-stride and looked at his brother while crossing his arms. "Are you even listening to me? You don’t wanna wrestle? What DO you wanna do?" Finally, Greg’s attention was grabbed. His eyes wandered up to the large man standing in front of him and he replied, "I just wanna e-fed. All day…" Now it was Matthew’s turn to give the blank stare. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Greg Jackson had this situation come up many times before. Matthew would find him an opportunity to wrestle and Greg would almost always try to back out of it. Sometimes it was because Greg was scared of his opponent. Other times it was because he was hurt physically, and he didn’t want his pain increased by some gorilla of a man brutalizing him. There was always something. Greg loves wrestling, believe it or not. However, despite being an extremely gifted athlete, sometimes Greg seems like he should’ve been a spectator rather than a wrestler. His intolerance of pain, his phobia of facing much larger opponents, and a wrestling name that strikes fear into the hearts of absolutely no one, just to name a few things that don't bode well for him. In case it wasn’t blatantly obvious, his older brother is the person responsible for motivation. Every time Greg worked out, it was because Matthew pushed him. Every job Greg ever got in the wrestling industry, Matthew provided for him through whatever connections he had. It was actually amazing that Matthew was able to find opportunities for his younger brother. Given how lazy he was and how poorly he ate, Greg was still in great shape. In this instance, Matthew had found the perfect opportunity for Greg. GTT5 was a tournament that could possibly catapult Greg’s career. So, why was there a lack of enthusiasm on Greg’s part? There are two answers to that question. The first answer is that Greg has never been dependent on money. Not because he doesn’t like it or need it, but because Greg’s been spoiled his entire life. His parents spoiled Greg, the baby of the family. All his relatives did as well. Greg got a full ride scholarship to play basketball at a Canadian university before getting kicked off the team and dropping out of school due to his low grades. Even now, Matthew was spoiling him by doing all this work for him. And the second answer, of course, is that Greg Jackson currently wants to e-wrestle instead of actually wrestling. In fact, Greg’s now fully dressed and seated in front of the computer right now doing that very thing, as well as other things associated with it. He’s reading the results of his current e-fed, NWWFL, short for National Wrestlers of Wrestling Federation League. He’s talking with a few people on AOL Instant Messenger. Most importantly, he’s thinking of role-playing ideas. "Writer’s block," Greg started, "I need to think of an entirely original role-play. Something that’s NEVER been done before… I KNOW! I’ll have Destroyinator witness a mugging, and then he’ll stop the thief!" Ah yes, "The Destroyinator." Combine two unoriginal names like Destroyer and Terminator, and you get a completely original name, even if it’s not even a real word. This character was Greg’s pride and joy. A monster over seven feet tall, who finished opponents off by swinging them like a baseball bat into the turnbuckle several times. He called this move, "The Destroyinator." And yes, Greg is fully aware that his character’s name is exactly the same as his character’s finishing maneuver. Above all else, though, was the respect he received. Greg wasn’t just a typical e-fedder… he was THE e-fedder, at least in NWWFL. Greg began observing an NWWFL chat room conversation on AOL. Skate4urLIFE: rps up did it pwn??? PlanIsSimple: i dont think it pwned Skate4urLIFE: it wuz the best ive done! it had 2 pwn! Juggalochick4: it was pretty good so it might have pwned :) Skate4urLIFE: Destroyinator wat do u think??? That’s respect, people. Greg Jackson didn’t get respect like this anywhere else. His opinion would literally make or break this person’s day, and everyone wanted to know his thoughts. Eventually, a smirk crossed Greg’s face as he typed in a reply. Destroyinator316: pwnage This was what Greg couldn’t leave. Of course, he didn’t have to leave it all behind. However, with a possible wrestling career looming ahead and his brother breathing down his neck every moment to convince him to train, Greg wouldn’t be able to put the time into it like he was at the moment. Every opponent he faced in this e-fed panicked when they saw his name opposite of theirs. All the members of the e-fed looked up to him because he could post a lot… sometimes his posts were actually pretty good! The e-fed owners relied on him for help. And if he wrestled again, the respect would diminish, the posts would be shorter and less frequent, and no one would care about what he did or thought simply because he wouldn’t be putting in the time anymore. At that very moment, Greg made a decision. The next time he saw Matthew, he was going to take a stand. Heck, he was going to stand up to his big brother! He was going to refuse to wrestle, and he was going to commit to e-fedding full force! There was no way Matthew would convince him otherwise. And when Matthew heard his words, he would know that Greg was serious because they’d be strong and well thought-out. Matthew was going to know that Greg meant business. The apartment door opened and Matthew entered, shutting the door behind him. "Eeek!" Greg gasped. Matthew was wearing a black leather jacket and blue jeans, and he looked very cold. While Greg had been on the computer, Matthew had been taking their bags to a taxi, which was waiting for them outside. Matthew motioned with his right hand that it was time to go, accompanied by him saying, "Time to go." Now was the time. If Greg was about to say something, now was it… but Matthew could still beat him up. Greg brushed his short dreadlocks out of his eyes and looked over towards his brother, trying to find the words to say while nothing came out. "Come on," Matthew continued, "you’ve been on that computer forever." "I’ve been on it forever?!" Greg mimicked, "L.O.L. I just got on here two hours ago and you’re giving me a hard time again! W.T.F.? I mean, come on!" A look of concern came over Matthew’s face before responding, "Greg… did you just talk to me using internet abbreviations?" Maybe he did need to leave the computer. Feeling stupid after realizing what he’d said, Greg shut down the computer and put his jacket and shoes on. As he exited the apartment and made his way out of the building, he accepted the fact that he was going to participate in this tournament. Although, he hadn’t come to terms with the thought of his role in UWWFL diminishing, he figured he could do both for the time being. Thank God he’d packed his laptop.
