Kash vs Shadow vs Aidan DelaCroix

19 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
19 June 2004 A Line In The Sand (Our eyes slowly become attuned to the image on screen as it fades in. Luckily due to the long time between fading in and something happening, we have the time to take in everything on screen. A ring in the middle of an empty arena, an empty battle ground surrounded by seats that will soon be taken up by a rabid crowd, that is what we see, but there is something else, something else that catches our eye.) “(in whispered tones) over here...” (We focus our eyes more and we see a figure sitting far in back of the arena. Other than being dressed in black, we cannot see who it is, they are that far away.) “(more whispered tones) over here...” (We start to zoom in, the camera seemingly ignoring the barricades and chairs as it moves in a smooth motion to the man in question. The first thing we notice is the bleached blonde hair sitting atop his head, a contrast to the blackness of his clothes, to be honest, he looks like a pint of Guinness. We get closer still and now we pick up a reflection from around his waist, the reflection of the gold on his title belt.) “Come closer.” (As we get as close as we safely can, we can take a good look at the man we seek. We have already spoken of the blonde hair, now we can see the glasses, goatee and piercings that adorn his face. Dressed in a black shirt and black trousers, he looks like what would happen if a librarian went mad, but that’s enough character assassination for one day.) “So, you found me eventually, took you long enough.” (The camera stops in its final position and the man in the chair just looks at us, his feet resting on the chair in front of him.) “Now, for those of you who don’t know, my name is Kash and I am a two time, and the current WWA Champion. I am also a Wiccan, but you will learn about that later. You come to see me today because of one thing, the PTC Global Title and my match with Aidan DeLaCroix and Shadow, yes?” (That is true indeed, the match that is due to take place very soon and will give the winner a shot at the ultimate PTC title.) “Now normally, you would find me in some strange location, maybe an occult bookstore, maybe an old abandoned church, but today, I found myself compelled to talk to you on a more serious level, because I saw something today that sickened me, and that doesn’t happen very often. Now, my opponents for this match are Shadow, a man against whom I have no grudge, and Aidan DeLaCroix, a man towards whom I have a feeling of intense disgust.” (This is an emotion Kash does not feel often, and whilst it is not the best emotion to carry into a match, it will do.) “You see, people have called me many things, a freak, a savage, a lunatic, but in reality, I am not any of those things, I may be unique and slightly eccentric, but I am no monster, unlike you DeLaCroix, I am no murderer.” (The lights in the arena flicker as though they are linked to the man speaking. Kash rubs his right fist with his left hand, seemingly imagining punishing the evil face of DeLaCroix.) “In my time, I have stepped into the ring with brutal men, with dangerous men and I fought them with honour, with a level of respect, but you Aidan, you have crossed the line. You killed a woman in cold blood and I am expected to let that lie? You don’t understand me that well, do you.” (More flickering and the mood of the arena seems to be getting colder, if that makes sense.) “No, you don’t understand me at all, and if you did then you would know what I think of you. Aidan, I despise you with all of my heart, do you understand me?” (Despite the raw nature of the words, the voice speaking them is still cold and focused.) “You took that poor woman and stole the life from her, you killed her without a second thought whilst claiming it was just part of your search for that which you desire, well, you will get what you desire.” (Lifting his legs up off the chair in front, Kash gets to his feet and places his title belt on the chair he has just vacated. He looks up to the roof of the arena and closes his eyes, and in a flash, all of the lights in the arena drop so we can see nothing but pitch blackness.) “Aidan, I quote you when I say “I need to annihilate this feeling of nothingness and fill it with whatever may come of its presence around me, emotionally”. Now, to me and I think I am experienced enough to comment on this piece of bile you spewed, you are just making excuses. Killers and monsters rarely have a reason for what they do, they are merely rabid animals that need to be put down.” (The lights in the arena start to hum and then they explode back into life, but now they are ultraviolet and we can see the zinc oxide that Kash favours adorning his face. A trickle of neon blood is running down his forehead and behind it we can see a pair of pure black eyes.) “Whilst you stand there and claim that you will annihilate both myself and Shadow on your way to the PTC title, you haven’t stopped to think of what you really mean. You see, I don’t think the PTC title is your goal, you just want to kill you heartless bastard, and that will be your downfall.” (The neon blood runs down his face and drips onto the floor, a twisted smile crossing the face of the man staring at us.) “You see, I am not a normal man Monsieur DeLaCroix, I am no sane individual, and I am not someone you can annihilate. In the past, I have had my flesh torn asunder, my skull caved in and my bines broken by “monsters” and what did I do? I turned around and slayed them with the venom of the gods. So you Aidan are no different, I will take you down like the rabid beat that you are and you will be annihilating NOTHING.” (The camera closes in on the face of Kash and for a few brief moments, we are truly afraid.) “When you walk into that ring and look across from Shadow and myself, you will realise that none of the people you have faced before can hurt you as I can, I can hurt you physically by tearing your limbs from your torso and beating your limbless cadaver into a pulp, or I can take your black soul and curse it for eternity. You have crossed a line Aidan, and I am that which waits on the other side.” (His eyes close, a moment of peace perhaps?) “Aidan DeLaCroix, you have been found guilty of murder by a jury of one, and in accordance with the power vested in me by the Gods I worship, I will pass judgment on you and send you to the great beyond, your life force robbed, your soul destroyed and your chances of taking the PTC Global title, gone.” (The eyes open again and they have been flooded black.) “Aidan, welcome to the worst nightmare you will ever have, and the last.” (The lights go off again and we are left with nothing but laughter chilling us to the bone.) ---- 19 June 2004 A Page Of Shadows What is a shadow? I know there are many ways of saying it, I mean, a search on the net brought forth more answers than you could comprehend, but what, in essence, is a shadow? In terms of the English language, a shadow has such wide ranging meanings as shade within clear boundaries, a premonition of something adverse or even an inseparable companion. However, I like to think that my favourite is something that exists in perception only. To a Wiccan, a shadow has more of a spiritual meaning, and its how these two combine that Kash, the WWA champion, has been thinking about. .......... (As we fade in, we notice that all we are looking at is a tree in the middle of a clearing. The tree is one of those dead ones that looks gnarled and worn and almost like a dead man frozen in time. We can tell by the light level that we are around sundown, and the tree is casting a particularly evil looking shadow.) Voice: Look at the tree, look what it does. (The voice is coming from somewhere behind us, but we don’t know exactly where.) Voice: Look at the way it defies the light, the way that it fights the light and creates its own shadow. (We look but perhaps the man behind the voice has a more unique view on life.) Voice: The shadow is cast with a form to it that only a mother could love, the twisted shape is a testament to nature. (As we look at the tree, a figure steps out from behind us and instantly, we recognise the man we expect, ie Kash. Dressed in his wrestling attire as well as a long leather coat, he is a vision of sartorial splendour, or am I just being sarcastic.) Kash: Shadows are interesting things. To most people, a shadow is that which is created by putting something in front of the light, like a child making a shadow bird to amuse his parents or something for use in a film for cheap laughs. I admit to seeing The Spy Who Shagged Me, so sue me. (Kash walks up to the tree and places a hand on its trunk, closing his eyes and saying a silent prayer.) Kash: As a Wiccan, I see shadows as something different. I mean, the main book in my life is the Book of Shadows, something I have been crafting for years and will maybe pass down to someone in the years to come, who knows. But, right now, I find myself with a slight worry, something that has been vexing me for a few weeks. (Kash opens his eyes and looks at us and we can see that usual far away look in his eyes.) Kash: You see, there is a man out there, a man from Prime who calls himself Shadow. Shadow is a talented wrestler no doubt, but when it comes to his name, I find serious fault with that. A shadow is a thing of beauty, something that nature creates and nurtures through its short and temperamental life, however the Shadow I will be facing in that ring is not so much natures creation that a creation of his own making. Each man is created by their own past, created by what they do, and that is not a creation of nature, so how can Shadow call himself such? I do not know. (Kash jumps up and grabs hold of the nearest branch, then lithely swings up and plants himself on the branch, sitting there like a child with too much time on his hands.) Kash: As a shadow, it would seem that by his very nature, he will be erased by the light of the match for the number one contendership, so once he steps into the ring and the lights come on, he shouldn’t be a problem. (A thought crosses the mind of Kash and he looks like the proverbial light bulb is hovering over his head.) Kash: But I bet you have heard that before, haven’t you, I bet you have heard all the “the light will destroy the Shadow” and all that crap. Well, I wont say that at all because I just realised something. (Falling backwards, Kash spins to the floor, landing on his feet like a cat. For an impromptu gymnastics class, Kash isn’t doing too bad. Kash drops to one knee and he fishes something out of his pocket. It appears to be a lighter, but don’t quote me.) Kash: You see, I just realised, a shadow isn’t destroyed by the light, allow me to demonstrate. (Kash grabs a hold of a few twigs and sets light to them and he uses them to light some leaves and in a few seconds, the fire is burning bright, providing enough of a contrast with the sundown to create a mix of light and shadow.) Kash: You may sit back and think that when you shine a light into a shadow, the shadow is destroyed, that it is wiped from existence by the particles of light hitting it, but what actually happens is that the shadow merely retreats, is reabsorbed by the shadows surrounding it. Thus, if you think about it, a shadow is swallowed by the darkness, not destroyed by the light. (Kash stands in the light of the fire and his shadow is encompassed by the darkness around him.) Kash: Thus, Shadow, you see, you really don’t have a chance in this match. I am the darkness, I am fed by the power of the night, I am given life by the night and her messengers, so how you think you can get through the war that it will be without being assimilated by me, I do not know, (The fire starts to burn brighter and brighter still as the tree catches fire.) Kash Shadow, pay attention to my words, I wont say them again. You walk into that match as a shadow; you walk out encompassed by the darkness, so enjoy your last days Shadow, and enjoy them while you can. (The fire burns too bright for us, we have to leave.)
