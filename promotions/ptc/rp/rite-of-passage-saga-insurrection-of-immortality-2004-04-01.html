Rite of Passage Saga: Insurrection of Immortality

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-01 My lone solace comes through my own suffering. I praise the Old Ones, and I thank Father Shanahan for his guidance. For too long I embraced the veil of illusion that was this accursed realm. I was lost, but now I am found. Ia Shub Niggurath! The only path to atonement is abandonment of all that this society preaches as moral and righteous. Virtues are an anomaly bestowed only upon the pestilence called humanity, a foreign entity to those beings that exist beyond our sphere. If these so-called 'virtues' impede progression, they are no longer necessary and nullified by the greater society. 'Virtues' are but means to a greater end, and unfortunately humanity has become so dependent on them that entire belief systems blindly center around various 'righteous' merits. I curse all the wretched 'blessings' this world and its false 'gods' have damned me with. Wealth, social status, power......what are these really but cosmetic things? They are physical inclinations, products of the wanting mechanism inherent in us all. So much lies beyond the widely-accepted boundaries of space-time to be discovered......enigmas just beyond the grasp of humanity, the answers to the eternal questions of consciousness and the all-encompassing cosmic scheme. But in order to attain these treasures of the spirit and the mind, one must do the bidding of their very crafters and conceivers. And to do this, we must release the affairs of the world from our psyche. We must forever deny our mortality and seek immortality. And only then is reality truly ours' to behold. The Sect must grow. At any cost. By life or death, love or hate, loyalty or treachery. These are all tools of our masters beyond the cosmos, and none are to be taken with any different perspective than the other, for by doing so it hinders our progress and belies their purpose. And in respect to the resurrection of our masters, progress is the greatest cause of all. It is this essential knowledge I now take into the war ahead. It is my ambition, it is my system, and it is my weapon. The annihilation of one Rocky Adams shall be the initial sacrament I offer, alone, to the Old Ones for their boon. Throughout this trial I shall be their vassal, avatar and holy messenger to Primetime Central. And to all who deny their whims, I shall be as Aphoom Zhah pouring his icy wrath onto impudent Hyperborea. Their vengeance, my objective. To be both the guiding light to the willing and the angel of death to the skeptics. Father Shanahan and the Crawling Chaos Himself, Nyarlathotep, have entrusted me with this most divine of tasks. And I shall not fail those who have opened my eyes. * * * A thin smile crossed the lips of Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan, his brawny figure poised at the threshold of the chamber wherein the altar was contained and Ethan Knight now knelt in reverence. The words which flowed from his subordinate's mouth instilled great satisfaction in the high priest called Father Shanahan. For months now he had been pleased in the progress of Ethan Knight in his dedication to the new faith. Actually, reflecting on it, 'pleased' was a dramatic understatement. Ecstatic would probably be a more accurate reflection of his view on Knight's conviction to the cause. He offered a nod as his lieutenant arose from the base of the blasphemous altar and glanced in his direction. Knight was taken aback, visibly surprised in his superior's interest in his prayer. "Father Shanahan, I didn't know you were there. How are you this evening?" Bruce gestured down the corridor with a jerk of his head. "Walk with me, Ethan," requested Shanahan, beckoning to his lieutenant before striding down the dim and dusty hallway. Bruce wasn't a tremendously personable man these days; hence when he chose to refer to his inferior as Ethan rather than Brother Knight, the younger man knew he was presented an opportunity to hear advice rarely proposed from his elder. Not surprising considering the impending Rites of Ascension. Ethan eagerly followed his spiritual advisor through the halls of the sprawling orifice and into the weed-entangled courtyard on the northside of the house. The night was chill, the remnants of a harsh winter still hanging over the highlands of upstate Vermont. Pines surrounded the isolated mansion that served as base for the dreaded Sect of Black Wisdom, and nearby the obscure hamlet of Brayston village acted as a collective sentinel for those of the manor. "You are among my most trusted advisors, Ethan," stated the Bringer of the Black Gospel, walking amongst the fractured cobblestones. "Your initiative is astounding, and frankfully if not for you and Horace, this organization would grow stagnant. Even sanctified by the Great Old Ones I alone can do only so much. It's simply the way of things, and its reflected in all of life. From a seed grows the trunk of a tree, and from that trunk branches are developed. From there come the leaves. Such it is with us. The Old Ones spawned this glorious brotherhood, with myself as the chartering founder. My duty is to appoint my lieutenants and those subordinates I favor to uphold this faction's dogma. In essence, you are like one of those branches, Brother Knight.” Ethan could sense something in Father Shanahan, and he was urged to never be wary about bringing up his observations. He had the ability to read and accurately analyze others, and he detected something slightly amiss in his leader. “M’lord, something’s wrong. You appear troubled. What is it?” Shanahan bit his lip, eyes momentarily transfixed on the crumbling statue of a dragon-like entity across the yard. With a hint of apparent hesitation, he shook his head from side to side. "This is how the Sect of Black Wisdom came into being, Ethan," Shanahan sighed, thoughts swallowed in reflection. "At least in some pretense. My arrival in Primetime Central was an event nary worth mentioning to anyone outside the promotion I was obligated to. I was a sideshow......angry, bitter, engorged in hatred. However, though I didn't know it at the time, it was the beginning of a long and prosperous road. The GTT was a competition to gain renown among my peers; a quest to attain respect some respect from the notables in the industry, or so I said. But in truth....." He turned to look on Knight, whose shoulder-length hair had begun to stir and sway about in the cool air of the New England evening. ".....it was never about respect really, Ethan. It was about impact and notoriety, looking at the big picture and realizing the acknowledged 'greats' weren't my betters or superiors. Tyler Nelson, Ivan Stanislav, Meanstreak, Jason Snow, Miyagawa, Sebastian Toomes.....I felt they had merely been granted the 'luck of the draw', if you will. I wanted it to be my time. Right there and right then." His eyes deviated from Ethan Knight, drifting to the surrounding forest of conifer and thick pine, and the latter could observe a sense of disappointment in the one known worldwide as Violence Jack. Where this was going was obvious. "I made an impact, Ethan. But I didn't fulfill my ultimate goal. I resolved to not only decimate all in my way en route to the prize, but to be taken seriously as one of the truly elite. That didn't happen...neither one did. But in you, I sense something different. The intangible factor to do what I didn't. I see in you the ability to do what I did not.” “How?” asked Ethan. “I don’t have the experience you did going in there. I don’t understand how I can possibly succeed where you failed! Not with what you had already accomplished!” Bruce's hands folded across his chest, wrapped about in the spangled robes draped over his stocky form. The silver and gold trim sparkled minutely under the glimmer of the waxing moon as his upper lip curled and he continued to stare into the blanket of trees of below. "You are not me, Brother Knight. You must forge your own path. During what you are about to experience I can offer my counsel and aid in facilitating your success, but you must abstain from relying too heavily on me or this organization's benefaction." "And you fully trust in me, m'lord?" Knight asked with a forlorn sigh. V-Jack cracked a grim smile, still facing away from his neophyte peer. Through Ethan's aura he could still sense an air of doubt and reluctance about the task at hand. "If I didn't believe in your abilities and ambition to make the right decisions, I wouldn't have confided in you to embark on such a mission. Besides..." He trailed off momentarily, a hearty laugh echoing from his lungs. "It's either that or you can go use the new RPW promo wall. Ward and Al called me to say it's available." Knight snickered as well, shifting his eyes to gaze in awe over the rolling hills and valleys gouged below. Even after so many months now in the company of the high priest residing in Wyatt Manor, he still had yet to behold such a sight garnering such inspiration. "The heretic dogs must be destroyed," mused the diabolical mastermind of the cult, snapping Ethan from his momentary lapse into thought. "Long have we stood by the way-side and observed their antics. No matter who stands in your way, they must be dealt with. If they will not accept our doctrines, and the faith that will someday sweep across this abysmal planet, they must perish. None will stand in our way. And you, my son, have been chosen as the next great instrument of their coming." Ethan narrowed his eyes, arching an eyebrow as he looked on the head vicar of the Sect of Black Wisdom. Shanahan leered with hatred across the valleys below, his profound glare brushing over the nearby village of Brayston and over the hills cradled in shadow. "Know right now that affiliation is nothing!" the elder one breathed. "I don't care if you're led to trounce some no-name from XUW or 4WW or somebody from RPW. Whether it's Joe Blow, Bob the Temp, Raijin, Jay Phoenix, Tessa Windsor or Karina fuckin' Wolfenden, if they deny our truth their fate has been decided. There are no exceptions. It is the decree of the Great Old Ones. And thus, it is your burden to bear. And do you truly know why that is, Ethan? Exactly why you must bear that burden?" A test. These had become almost frighteningly common in recent weeks, and Knight found them to be typically directed at him. Shanahan slowly revolved to face the youth, awaiting the response he hoped for. "This is not Christianity," Ethan retorted, almost as if reciting from a book. "We're not a race to be coddled and betrayed by false messiahs and prophets. We strive to find the answers ourselves, and by doing so we must look beyond the meager teachings of some ancient book of lies penned from madmen and the deceived. We are not the weak, for we have no desire to shift our problems to some entity representing impotence and empty promises for the sake of retreat." That familiar smile crept over the visage of Shanahan as he absorbed the learnings of his pupil and valued subordinate. "The Old Ones demand servitude," resumed Knight, "but offer rewards in not only this life, but the afterlife. Our service comes not through passiveness and submission; we reaffirm the cosmic plan of the daemon-sultan Azathoth before His creation went awry and befell corruption.....strength and the will to dominate. In the end it shall be our kind who help topple the citadels of mankind's impotent and hollow deities. We shall be the ones to cleanse this Earth in preparation for the return of the Great Old Ones, the true overlords of this universe." It was shades of Shanahan's long periods in the dimension of Nyarlathotep's instruction. Except now he was the teacher. "And of your individual role?" inquired the high priest. "Are you proud?" "Yes. My role may seem insignificant, much of it being contained in a pro wrestling ring, but it is all part of the plan of the Great Old Ones and the Outer Gods. If even one former doubter can accept our gospel and one dissenter can be crushed, it is all worth it. Pride isn't a sin, contrary to that deplorable 'mainstream' religion. If there truly is any trait worth deeming sin, it is the ignorance shared by this unhallowed species.” His superior nodded his commendation. "Rocky Adams is vermin," snarled Shanahan. "He has no place in the ring with you, or anyone affiliated with this organization for that matter. Surely you...." "Yes, I know that. It's not hard to read in his body language. He's the same breed of one-dimensional miscreant I'd run into on the mid-west wrestling circuit time-in and time-out before joining the Sect of Black Wisdom." "Well look ahead of that," demanded V-Jack. "Not everyone you're going to face in that tournament is as inept as Rocky Adams. I still shudder when I ask myself how he was allowed into such a competition. Carys, Wolfenden, Phoenix, Almasy, Arsonist, Diablo.....there's a plethora of viable contenders, Ethan. This Adams wretch mustn’t lower your defenses." His subordinate could only nod his sympathy as Shanahan exhaled deeply with a glimpse at the moon overhead. "Almost time for the evening sacrament. Go and prepare, for it will be your final one for several weeks.” “Yes, m’lord,” consented the youngest of the cult leadership. “I’ll see you soon.” Shanahan grinned as Knight arose and bid his leave, leaves crunching beneath his feet as he crossed the courtyard. "He was born with a veritable silver spoon in his mouth," Shanahan silently attested as Ethan left to ready the altar. "But not even the notions of the world can overcome Their gospel. Woe be unto those in his way. Go forth, my son. And destroy the nonbelievers." Somewhere in the encompassing forest, one could almost detect an infernal agreement emanate from the darkness. Here in the wood evil dwelled and the superstitious shunned...... * * * I am beyond a wrestler, I am beyond a man. I have no love beyond that of my faith and no goal beyond the worship of the Old Ones. Still I question why I was chosen to walk this path on Their behalf, but I'm assured the Old Ones will guide me as I seek the grail that is enlightenment. I will vanquish the enemies of the Sect of Black Wisdom, raise faith in what resides in my soul, and prove that I belong in the company of the elite of this tournament. They call it darkness, but what do they truly know of darkness? Nothing but the teachings of the ignorant and the cowardly. I will deliver unto them their salvation. If they refuse, they will be demolished. Utterly....demolished. Rocky Adams will be the first. Remorse does not begin to describe my feelings for what is to befall that cretin. I don't know what lies ahead. I am scared, but I will overcome. The road ahead appears daunting, but I will persevere. And the war ahead may be fierce, but I shall emerge victorious. But's. Always but's.......
