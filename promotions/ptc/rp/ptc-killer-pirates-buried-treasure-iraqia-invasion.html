14 February 2005 Searching for Buried Treasure (RP 1 of 2) "Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean" Show Intro [We begin with a typical TV show intro for this one. There's a black bearded Pirate Captain on screen with an eye patch on his left eye.] Captain: Are you ready kids? Kids: Aye aye Captain! Captain: I can't hear you. Kids: Aye aye Captain! ["Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean" them music starts playing.] Captain: OHHHHHHH...Who lives on ship floating on top of the sea? Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Captain: Big and fat and hungry is he! Kids: Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: If no-brained wrestling nonsense be something you wish! Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Captain: Then sleep on the deck and smell like a fish! Kids: Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: Ready? Captain and Kids: Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Cap-Tain Mor-Gan! Kil-Ler Klown from Ou-Ter Space! Captain: Cap-Tain Mor-GAAAAANNNNNN!!! Captain: HA HA HA HA !!!! [We end the song with a flute playing for a moment before the "Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean" logo appears on the screen.] [Cut.] * * * SEARCHING FOR BURIED TREASURE SOMETIME LAST WEEK... [The scene opens on an uncharted island somewhere in the Caribbean. The Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean and their manager, the always controversial James von Doom, have sailed across the high seas to reach this island with their treasure map in hand. According to the map, there is buried treasure on this island. The treasure is one the likes of which the world has never seen. This island is TREASURE ISLAND. Their rinky dink, crappily made pirate ship the Black Nemo is docked on the beach of this island. On the beach, Captain Jack Morgan, who happens to be some New Yorker who got conked in the head and thinks he's a pirate now, has unfolded the map and is reading it over. He's dressed like Captain Jack Sparrow from "Pirates of the Caribbean." His fake pirate paraphernalia is clear to the naked eye, most noticeably his fake plastic pirate sword and fake pistol. He has a shovel, as do his two other companions. They do need something to dig up the treasure, don't they?] Jack: "Mates, there be treasure here. We're close." [James, who is wearing a gray tinged suit and has on some very cool looking black sunglasses, turns and gives him a look.] JV: "Jack, be serious. You two morons have a Tag Team Challenge match against a team from the WMW named Soul Purpose and instead we're out here in the middle of the fucking ocean looking for some treasure that, for all we know, doesn't exist to begin with." [The "Killer Klown from Outer Space," a 380-pound monstrosity who thinks he's a killer clown from the campy '80's film of the same name, turns and shakes his head. The ruffles on his clown suit shake as he does so.] KKFOS: "Treasurie buria herede!" [James shrugs his shoulder.] JV: "Whatever guys. Just get me back to Vegas before Monday. I have some, how shall I put this, important people I have to meet for my other job." [Jack turns to him and smiles.] Jack: "No worries, mate. We'll have you home on time. And, I must ask, what is this other job of yours that you've been hiding from us?" [James gives him a cold look.] JV: "I'm not going to compromise it by telling you two what it is. Let's just say I get to meet a LOT of important people and show them just how HAPPY my employers are with their decision making." Jack: "So, you go and meet a lot of people? Sounds nice. You basically are their consiglieri, correct?" [James smiles.] JV: "Something like that." [The KKFOS turns and starts pointing toward the interior of the island.] KKFOS: "TREASURIE! TREASURIE!" [The clown runs off. James and Jack look at each other before turning and heading after him as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * FINDING THE TREASURE TROVE A MINUTE OR SO AFTER THAT... [The scene opens in the interior of the island, in the middle of a tropical forest. The clown is digging a hole as James and Jack reach where he is at. James shakes his head.] JV: "You stupid moron. Do you want to get eaten by a leopard or something?" [The clown turns and waves at them.] KKFOS: "TREASURIE!" [James rolls his eyes.] JV: "Yeah right..." [Suddenly, as the clown is digging, there is a loud clanking noise. James and Jack look at each other.] Jack/JV: "TREASURE!" [The two of them run over to the clown with their shovels in hand. The clown reaches into his hole and lifts the treasure chest out of it.] Jack: “See! Told you, mate.” JV: [flabbergasted] “I…I can’t believe it.” Jack: “Believe it, mate. It’s real.” JV: “Well, let’s open it up and see what’s inside.” Jack: “Ok.” [There’s a lock on the treasure chest, so the clown sits it down. James then proceeds to bust the treasure chest's lock open with his shovel. Once he succeeds, he rips it off and throws it away. Jack then opens up the treasure chest and they all stare in awe of what is inside. It glows off and reflects off of them.] JV: We're rich men." Jack: "Very rich indeed, mate." [The three of them high five as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * PIRATES BE STEALING ME GOLD! A FEW MINUTES AFTER THAT... [The scene opens with Jack and James dragging the treasure chest they found toward the Black Nemo, Jack's pirate ship which is docked on the beach. The "Killer Klown" is following along, carrying the three shovels they brought with them as they wander the sandy edge of the forest.] Jack: "We're going to be rich, mate." [James nods his head.] JV: "Damn right. We can retire on this crap, buddy." [The clown gets excited, too.] KKFOS: "Treasurie..." [They continue dragging the treasure along before James notices something on the beach. He stops and motions for Jack.] JV: [trying to be quiet] “Jack, Jack.” Jack: [stopping] “What is it, mate?” [James points toward something on the beach.] JV: "Jack, what the hell is that?" [Jack and James set the treasure chest down. James points toward something on the beach. The camera angles around behind Jack so that we can clearly see what he's pointing at now. It's another ship.] Jack: "Pirates..." [Jack recognizes it immediately. It's from the port of Arcadia and the pirates on that vessel are the notorious Al Quiero Pirates that have been all over the news ever since they blew a hole in the H.M.S. Cole back in the year 2000 just because they wouldn't hand over their shipment of tea leaves. Jack turns to James.] Jack: "We'd best be going, mate." [James doesn't seem scared at all.] JV: "Why?" Jack: "These are the most dangerous pirates in the world. Their captain, a man so notorious that he is on top of the FBI's Top Ten Most Wanted Men in the Caribbean list. His name is Osama bin Fishskin and he is the most dangerous man in these waters. We'd best be going mates." [James gives him a look.] JV: "Jack, we can take'em! We can kick their arses all over this island!" [Just then, James is clonked on the head from behind by a wooden club by a giant, fat pirate. Jack turns around and is punched out by a buff, shirtless steroid pirate. The clown is attacked by two ugly pirates right after this happens. After all three of them are unconscious, the Al Quiero pirates start laughing evilly.] Fat, Ugly Pirate: "Garrr...here be our treasure, gents. Let's take it back to the captain." [The pirates grab the treasure chest and haul it off toward their pirate ship as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * QUESTION: SACK ARCADIA OR SACK IRAQIA? A FEW MINUTES LATER... [After being ambushed by the pirates, James finally regains consciousness and stumbles to his feet. He looks over and sees that Jack is starting to move and the clown is still knocked out. He puts his hand on the bump on his forehead and groans.] JV: [holding his head] "Fucking pirates. Grr...how can any associate themselves with such mindless fiends?" [James turns and looks at his cohorts.] JV: "Oh bother..." [James stumbles over and falls on his ass. He then crawls over to Jack and starts slapping him in the face.] JV: "Wake up! Wake up! The pirates stole the gold!" [Jack moans.] Jack: "..." JV: "Wake up you morons! We have to get it back!" [Jack finally starts stirring.] Jack: "Because they're pirates." [James gives him a look.] JV: "What?" Jack: "That's why they took the gold. They took the gold because they're pirates." [James pulls him to his feet.] JV: "Derrr! Now, let's get the clown." [Jack and James stumble over to where the clown is laying on the ground. They bend over and try to wake him up.] Jack: "Clown, wake up!" [James nods his head.] JV: "YEAH CLOWN! WAKE..." [Suddenly, the clown sits up and tries to eat James with his big, large, sharpened teeth but then realizes that it's his old buddy James, so he decides not to eat him. Instead, he gives him a big "Killer Klown" hug and points toward the sea.] KKFOS: "Piratano shala mo lasha!" [Jack turns to James.] Jack: "The clown is right, mate. We have to go after them across the sea and get our treasure back." [James nods his head.] JV: "Yes, but where are they headed?" [They all three put their thinking caps on. James starts stroking his chin, trying to analyze exactly where pirates would be headed with their treasure. Jack has an idea. The clown is too busy thinking about eating the pirates to care.] Jack: "I know where. They are headed for Arcadia." JV: "And how can you know that?" Jack: "Because Tortuga is too far away and Arcadia is the only pirate port in these waters." JV: "And how in the hell do you know that?" Jack: "I know everything there is to know about pirating. I'm sure we'll find them there." [A devilish smile appears on James' face now. He's up to no good. That much is obvious.] JV: "Ok. So what do we do now?" Jack: "Well, the people who attacked us are from Arcadia, but they are friendly toward us." [James looks confused.] JV: "What?" Jack: "They send us lots of money and paid off my father very generously when he was President of the New York Yacht Club. So, we can't attack them even though their people caused this heinous act. The closest thing we can do is attack the island that he tried to take over when he was President. And that island is Iraqia." JV: "RIGHT! [pause] So what's the plan then?" [The scene then fades out.] * * * BAD PLANNING FOR EVEN STUPIDER ATTACKS A FEW MINUTES LATER... [The three of them now have their shovels in hand again.] JV: "What's the plan then?" Jack: "Right." [The scene cuts to a dream sequence.] Jack: "We take the Black Nemo, we sail over to Iraqia, we go in, take care of the pirates - "I'm so sorry pirates". - then we grab our treasure, we go over to my secret pirate cave, hole up, have a cup of tea and wait for this whole thing to blow over." JV: "Why have we got to go to a secret pirate cave?" Jack: "Because we do." JV: "But you got kicked out!" Jack: "I have to know if my favorite hide-out is alright!" JV: "Why?" Jack: "Because I love that place, mate!" JV: "Alright... lame. Totally lame. But I'm not staying there, though." Jack: "Why not?" JV: "If we hole up, I wanna be somewhere familiar, I wanna know where the exits are, and I wanna be allowed to smoke, drink, and have a good time." Jack: "Okay." [The scene cuts to a dream sequence again.] Jack: "We take the Black Nemo, go around to Iraqia, go in, deal with the pirates - "Sorry pirates!" - grab our treasure, go to my pirate cave, see if it's OK, bring the treasure back here, have a cup of tea and wait for this whole thing to blow over." JV: "Perfect!" Jack: "No, no, no, no, no, wait, we can't bring her back here." JV: "Why not?" Jack: "Well, it's not really safe, is it?" JV: "Yeah, look at the state of it." Jack: "Where's safe? Where's familiar?" JV: "Where can I smoke?" [Jack and James pause for a moment, then slowly make a realization.] Jack: [cuts to dream sequence a third time] "Take pirate ship. Go to Iraqia. Kill pirates - "Sorry pirates." - grab our treasure, go to your flat in PT's Pub in Vegas, have a nice cold pint, and wait for all of this to blow over. How's that for a slice of fried gold?" JV: "Yeah, boyyyeee!" [James and Jack clang weapons together. So now, instead of go to Arcadia, they have decided to sack Iraqia instead. James, Jack, and the KKFOS run toward Jack's pirate ship, the Black Nemo, and jump onboard.] Jack: "GARRR! It's off to sack Iraqia!" [James smiles.] JV: "Bagpipe, here we come!" [The scene ends with the ship setting off and sailing toward the vast, endless blue ocean that goes as far as the eye can see in search of the mysterious pirates who stole their treasure. Hopefully, they can make it back in time for their match.] [Cut.] ---- 22 February 2005 The Iraqia Invasion SACKING IRAQIA [The scene opens with the rickety-rackety Black Nemo pirate ship in the Bay of Bagpipe, right near the capital of Iraqia. The Killer Pirates (Captain Jack Morgan and the "Killer Klown from Outer Space") and their manager, James von Doom, are edging closer to landfall. The three of them have magically changed into camoflaudge colored versions of their usual outfits. The camera is on James, who turns to his to two apprentices with a dead serious look on his face.] JV: "Mortar side six. Starboard side six. Move fast and steer close to those mortar holes. Keep the sand out of your weapons. Keep your exits clear. I'll see you on the beach." [Jack turns to the clown in dramatic fashion.] Jack: "One man is a waste of opportunity. Two men are a good use of ammo." [Suddenly, really fake gunshots are heard in the background followed by screams and then...an airplane flying overhead? Wait a second...they all turn in the direction of the sound guy. The camera turns and looks at the nerdy looking sound guy, who flips off the camera.] Sound Guy: "FUCK YOU CARL! FUCK YOU!" [James pulls out his script.] JV: "This isn't in the script you tards. We have to follow the script in order to beat Soul Purpose. I have a strategic plan in order for you two to kick their asses in the PTC Tag Team Division and we must follow it to a T in order to win this battle of attrition." [Jack turns around and pulls out his plastic sword, pointing it in the direction of the island city of Bagpipe.] Jack: "LAND HO! AVAST YE LANDLUBBERS! WE BE ABOUT TO MAKE LANDFALL! ARRRR..." [The ship makes it's way toward the beach head. Now, the Sound Guy plays the right sound effects (Thank God) and the platform at the front end of the ship lowers. James turns and looks back at the director.] JV: [yelling] "HEY! SINCE WHEN THE HELL DID THIS SHIP BECOME A PONTOON BOAT?!" [The director smiles at him, pulling out his megaphone.] Director: [through his megaphone] "Since I put it in the script silly." [James scratches his head.] JV: "Uhhhh...ok. Whatever dumbass, as long as I get paid I got no problem with distorting reality for all the plebeians and serfs in the viewing audience." [The director smiles. Jack taps James on the shoulder and he turns around.] JV: "Yes?" Jack: [scratching his head] "Um, James. Why did the boat get changed into a pontoon boat by the director?” JV: “So we can retake Omaha Beach.” Jack: [dumbfounded] “Oh…alright then, mate.” [The front platform is down and fake bullets come whizzing by, just missing them! Actually, they are Bullet Bills from the old Super Mario Bros. Video games. They explode into pixellated nothingness upon impact with the water around the Black Nemo. James motions for them.] JV: "OVER THE SIDE!" [The three of them jump over the side and, when they reach the water, starts heading for the beachhead. James leads the way as they wade through the murky waters until reaching the mined beachhead. The three of them charge up through the water until they reach the beach, holding out their weapons in a combat stance.] Jack: "GARRRRR!!!" KKFOS: "DARRRRR!!!" JV: "DIE BASTARDS!!!" [They then look around and notice that there is no one in sight. They then put their weapons down and look at each other, scratching their heads.] Jack: "So, what does this mean?" [James smiles and then holds up his shovel like he is He-Man holding up his sword from the "Masters of the Universe" or something.] JV: [holding up his shovel] "VICTORY!!!" [The scene ends with the really fake He-Man thing before fading out.] [Cut.] * * * OUR HEROES GET THEIR TREASURE BACK THREE MINUTES LATER... [The scene opens with James, Jack, and the clown standing over something, looking down at it.] JV: "Is that what I think it is?" [Jack nods his head.] Jack: "Yes it is, mate." [James smiles.] JV: "Who knew that it would be so easy to find." [Jack smiles.] Jack: "You damn right, mate. It's bloody marvelous!" [The clown reaches down and picks up whatever it is.] KKFOS: "Look-da!" [The clown then holds up the chest of buried treasure as the camera pans back to show the pirates who stole it dead on the beach after being stung by a swarm of jellyfish when they decided to go jellyfish collecting with butterfly nets like Spongebob once they returned to Arcadia. They were then planted here by Al Quiero special agent pirates to cover up any misdeeds and to place the blame solely on Iraqia. The clown smiles and holds up the treasure again.] KKFOS: "Treasurie!" [The scene then fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * MAKING UP STUFF FIVE MINTUES AFTER THAT... [The scene opens with James and the Killer Pirates sitting cross-legged around the treasure on the beach. James turns to Jack.] JV: "So, how are we going to explain this?" [Jack looks dumbfounded.] Jack: "I dunno, mate. But what I do know is that it's our asses if we don't come up with something soon." [James starts stroking his chin.] JV: Well, we could lie." [Jack snaps his fingers merrily.] Jack: "That's the spirit!" [Jack starts thinking.] Jack: "Now, what do we say?" [James starts pondering for a moment before it hits him.] JV: [proclaiming] "EUREKA! I've got it! First, we come up with some bullshit evidence against them. Then, we get some phony pictures to show that they are dangerous. Then, we tell them about the treasure being stolen but lie about where the pirates who stole it came from." [Jack jumps to his feet with joy.] Jack: "Congratulations! [pause] Now, let's go get some rum." [Jack heads off-screen toward the moonshine district as James and the clown simply look at each other, shrug their shoulders, and get up. They then drag the treasure chest with them as they follow Jack toward the city as the scene slowly fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * PRESS CONFERENCE TWO DAYS LATER... [The scene opens in a conference room at the U.P. (United Pirates) Building in the port of Tortuga with the Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean and James von Doom, the "Manager of Champions," standing at three separate podiums, ready to take questions from reporters over their attack on Iraqia. The room is filled with reporters, cameras, security guards, and all kinds of other riffraff. It's a circus-type atmosphere.] JV: "We will now take your questions." Reporter#43: "Why did you and your party sack the island of Iraqi when it is quite clear from the video evidence that the island of Arcadia was the one that was behind the attack on your vessel?" [James smiles and can only shake his head.] JV: "Silly, silly you. Director, cue up the evidence against the Island Nation of Iraqia please. Thank you." [James stands up and now, MAGICALLY, has a pool stick in his hand to help him show everyone the "evidence" of Iraqia's misdeeds. He even has a projector too and an assistant to show everyone the "evidence" in living color. He walks over to where the images are being projected and smiles.] JV: "Now, as you can see here [pointing] the Iraqis have amassed a HUGE army that is ready to obliterate the entire western Caribbean territories." [The picture being projected is showing a Storm Trooper riding a Bantha.] JV: "They are VERY, VERY dangerous people. And, as you can see in the next picture, [picture changes] they have built a huge network of nuclear arms manufacturing and solicitation that threatens the entire world!" [The pictured now being projected has a picture of an In-and-Out Burger located somewhere in the San Fernando Valley judging by the looks of the picture.] JV: "My next and last projection [picture changes] will show you people that they are making weapons of mass destruction that might [being over-dramatic] KILL US ALL!" [The last picture being projected is that of a dog squatting to take a dump on his neighbors yard. The picture then disappears and the camera switches to James' smiling face.] JV: "And that is all the evidence that needs to be shown, so all of you can bid us farewell as we and our Coalition of the Willing embark..." [Just then, the scene is taken over by an image of documentary filmmaker Michael Moore's marionette from the movie "Team America: World Police." He bobbles around and smiles.] Moore: "HA puny pirates! I will show the world your pathetic Coalition of the Willing! Roll that footage!" [The scene switches to a black and white screen with a nation's name on it.] Loud, mocking voice: "The Federated States of Micronesia!" [The scene changes to show a picture of the surfers surfing the ocean waves in the movie "Surf Ninjas." That pizza delivery guy from "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles 2: The Secret of the Ooze" is leading the way. The scene then shifts back to the previous black and white one but with a different nation's name.] Loud, mocking voice: "The Republic of Palau!" [The scene shifts to show a picture of two monkeys having sex in a palm tree by the beach before switching back to the same black and white picture with a different nation's name on it this time.] Loud, mocking voice: "The Democratic State of Djibouti!" [The scene shifts, now showing a picture of Starvin Marvin from "South Park" waving before shifting back to the black and white picture with a different country's name.] Loud, mocking voice: "The Incorporated Territory of St. Kitts and Nevis!" [The scene shifts to a picture of Master Higgins jumping over a bat in a screen shot from the video game "Adventure Island." The scene then shifts back to the black and white picture but with a different name on it again.] Loud, mocking voice: "The Socialist Monarchy of Nepal!" [The scene shifts to a picture of the Abominable Snowman from "Rudolph the Red Nosed Reindeer" looking menacing before going back to the black and white screen for one last time.] Loud, mocking voice: "The Fascist Police State of Monaco!" [The scene then shifts to a picture of Herbie the Love Bug and Gisselle the blue car that is Herbie's love interest in "Herbie Goes to Monte Carlo," holding doors before shifting scenes back to a laughing Michael Moore marionette.] Moore: "BWAHAHAHAHAHA! You imperialist pigs have nothing on me! Nothing! Muahahahahaha!" [Just then, something comes crashing down on top of the Michael Moore marionette. He goes "KERPLAT!" before laying motionless on the ground. The camera pans up a little bit to show that it's the UWF's possessed, killer, inanimate WWF Hulk Hogan Wrestling buddy the Huckster making a guest appearance. And, upon the camera panning further up, we see the UWF Television Champion "Big" Dick N. Uranus, a retarded, in-bred ex-porn star smiling and waving at the camera.] Dick: "HI! ME DO WHAT YOU SAID JAIME. ME MAKE HUCKSTER CRUSH PUPPET LIKE IN SCRIPT. ME DO! ME DO!" [Dick is another one of von Doom's protégés. Von Doom paid him in condoms to do this guest spot. It only took five of the blue ones. They also get an endorsement from Trojan for this spot as well, which means more money for them. Cheap product placements in movies are essential, especially in this day and age. Dick waves at the camera as he pulls up and hugs his favorite pal the Huckster.] Dick: "BYE! [making Huckster wave] BYE GUYS! WHAT'CA GONE DO WHAT HUCKSTA MANIA WILDLY DOES YOUR MOM?" [Dick poses, flexing his badly defined muscles before jumping off-screen to go count his blue condoms. The scene switches back to James, whose jaw is agape over what just happened. His acting is superb. He doesn't even hint that he's trying not to laugh his ass off over Dick's "guest spot" in the Pirate's promo. Anyway, James then turns to the reporters.] JV: "Press conference over. We'll see you all at the end of the road of the Pirate's tag championship glory! [motioning] Come on guys! Let's go buy us some hookers!" [James exits the podium area amidst a rush of questions from the reporters which he fails to answer as he leaves. The clown and Jack start to leave, but stop and start doing stupid poses for the cameras before James clears his throat loudly and they follow him out of the conference room as the scene fades to black.] [Cut.] * * * THE IRAQIA PLAN THE NEXT DAY… [After all has been said and done, James and the Killer Pirates are sitting in the top secret meeting room on the 24th level of the Black Nemo. Yes, I know. It’s a crappy pirate ship made by someone who didn’t know what they were doing and this is directed, written, and produced by someone who doesn’t know what they are doing, so bare with it for the time being. The really fake monitor screens on the walls are really paper with scenes from movies being projected on them. There is a goldfish in the water cooler in the background. James and the Pirates are sitting at a round table. Jack is drinking rum, the KKFOS is eating his cotton candy cocoon that contains his latest victim, and James is reading his script, trying to find his lines. It’s hard to do, considering the clown smeared blood all over it so he throws it down in anger. James sits back in his chair and sighs.] JV: “So, I say we keep our ultra-top secret Pirate Protection Force in Iraqia for a year, let their suicide drunkards kill some of them, let them have elections, and then go home in 2015. What do you say?” [The clown nods his head in agreement. Jack holds up his bottle of rum.] Jack: “Cheers to that, mate.” [Jack then chugs the rest of the rum down and then falls over in his chair with a thud. His legs are sticking up high in the air now and he’s out of it. James turns to the clown.] JV: “Well, that was interesting. [unamused] So, are you guys ready for the tag team match?” [The clown nods his head and continues chewing on his cotton candy covered victim. Fake blood is all over his mouth. James is all smiles.] JV: “Good. But how will you win?” [The clown reaches down under the table and pulls out a giant spiked club. James cackles evilly.] JV: “Excellent.” [The clown starts swinging the club only to accidentally drop it on Jack, who howls out in pain.] Jack: [angrily] “You stupid mother…” [And the scene quickly fades to black before the FCC fines them for being indecent. Their stupid television logo comes on the screen to end this madness] [Cut.]
