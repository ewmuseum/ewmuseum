Jay Phoenix RP 1

1 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-01 Just found out that I am flying over to London in less than five hours on business, so this will be my one and only RP for this round. Major apologies for that, as I intended on getting a much better RP out tomorrow, but that is not to be ... Jay ------------- The sound of a phone ringing competes with the sound of running water for dominance. Sitting on a small unit beside a large king-size bed, it’s white sheets crumpled and dishevelled while pillows are scattered from top to bottom, the phone continues to ring. Across the sparsely furnished room … the bed being the only piece of furniture at all apart from the unit that houses the phone and a small clock, the bare walls painted white, only the wall above the bed broken in it’s plainness with a large black and brown dream-catcher that hangs at rest … opposite the bed itself, is a partly open door, through which steam gently rolls, making it’s way around and under the door like fingers groping their way in the dark. The sound of running water comes from within, but as the phone rings for the third time, the sounds of water suddenly cease as a muffled voce can be heard coming from within. “Damn it, why is it that people wait until you have the shampoo on before they decide to ring … unfrikkin’ believable!” Faint sounds can be heard coming from within the bathroom, and then the door is opened fully, allowing a cloud of steam to roll out into the bedroom almost heraldic the entrance of the figure that walks out after it, silhouetted in the bright halogen light from behind. The familiar form of Jay Phoenix stands … naked apart from a white cotton towel being tied around his waist … dripping as he glares at the phone that continues to ring away to itself. At just shy of six feet in height and about two hundred and twenty pounds in weight, Phoenix has the graceful build of a martial artist, with a lithe muscular that betrays no hint of fat. A tattoo of a dream catcher glistens on the skin of his chest, the golden circle just above his heart with three red and white feathers tracing down to the middle of his rib cage, a jagged scar just visible behind it; while on his left arm a red and golden Phoenix tattoo is just visible, surrounded by a band of flames. Water runs off his toned body, down across his musculature … rivulets racing through the defined six pack of his stomach … to pool at his feet on the floor, while his normally thick, flowing auburn hair is a bedraggled and tangled mess of dark brown around his shoulders, suds still very evident within them. With a long suffering sigh he stomps over to the bedside table and grabs the receiver of the phone, stopping the ringing in mid tone. “ … this had better be good!” Slumping down on the bed, Phoenix listens to the voice on the other end of the phone, his face calming down as his expression changes from frustration to surprise and then finally curiosity. “ … so the PTC have finally announced the brackets for the cruise weight tournament have they? Yeah I am glad that you phoned Rick … though you could have waited another twenty minutes, but hey … because I hadn’t heard anything about it until now.” Brushing his dripping hair away from his face, Phoenix listens to the unheard voice on the other end of the phone. “Why did I sign up? Why not? I mean what have I got to lose at the end of the day? I have spent most of my career facing men twice my size, monsters who could literally pick me up and throw me from one side of the ring to another without breaking a sweat … and I not only managed to hold my own against them, I managed to come out on top and make a pretty good career for myself. … truth be told though I have always enjoyed competing with my peers ... with people about the same size as me … people who take risks, who use their whole body in the ring, who value athletic ability and grace over strength and power I have always enjoyed that more than anything else. So this cruiser weight tournament is a way for me to do something that I haven’t done for a long time in this industry. … enjoy myself” Phoenix laughs as he listens to the reply. “Yeah I know that for you getting beaten up in the middle of the ring as you put it is not enjoyment, but then I don’t see the appeal in a weekend at the tables in Vegas the same way that you do. We just have to agree to disagree on this and get our buzz from different things. Anyway, you haven’t told me who I am facing yet doofus!” Phoenix’s eyes widen slightly as he hears the name on the other end, and then he gives a high pitched shriek of pain and drops the receiver. Bringing both hands up to his face, Phoenix rubs furiously at his eye where a stream of soapy suds can be seen around it, and then after a few seconds he slowly … and carefully … opens his eye again, peering around him as he blinks furiously to clear the now bloodshot orb of any further suds. Reaching down he picks up the phone again. “Rick … you still there?” After a second Phoenix gives a bark of laughter. “No you idiot that wasn’t my response to finding out that I am facing Max Roswell, that was because I just got attacked by some shampoo to the eye thanks to you phoning me in the middle of a shower!” Phoenix keeps laughing as he continues. “What the Hell do you mean calling me a wuss … I will have you know that there is only one thing more painful in this World than soap to the eye … no, it isn’t going through the Spanish announce table … … it is a paper cut to the tongue! Anyway, listen thanks for the information Rick, means that I have some time to look into this Roswell guy, because while I know a little bit about him, like the fact that he has just returned Global Wrestling after a hiatus, and is a former Primetime champion there, I don’t know enough to go into a match against him. I hate being unprepared, and the little that I do know about Roswell is enough for me to realise that he is not someone to take lightly, and it doesn’t matter what other people will think in our match-up I am not going to assume that I have the edge in this. There are no easy victories in a tournament like this, there are no easy ways to the final, which is something that I learned in the Global Tournament.” Phoenix stands up, looking back over his shoulder to see that where he has been sitting is now soaked through and covered in suds. “Damn it … listen Rick I really need to go now, thanks to your timely phone call I may have enough to get prepared for my first match in the cruiser tourney, but I also seem to have inherited a water bed. … don’t ask … Bye” Phoenix hangs up the phone and stands staring at the pool of water on the bed, and the trail of droplets that lead all the way back to the bathroom itself that is now totally obscured by a wall of steam. “So Max Roswell is the first step in this thing ... and considering the federation he works for and what little I know of him he may even be the last step for me too. ... people seem to think that I have the advantage here, simply because of who I am. Well not so long ago I was the unkown man in a similar tournament, the wrestler that everyone thought had no chance at all. Thing is that I defied the odds and got all the way to the finals of that tournament and thanks to that a large part of my fame was made. .. so Maxwell could easily do the exact same thing here and defy the expectations and take me out of this thing right at the start.” The trademark grin plays across Phoenix's face. "He could ... but to do that he is going to need to be at the top of his game, he is going to need to be faster than me, stronger than me ... he is going to need to be better than me ... and he is going to have to want this more than me." Wakling back into the bathroom, Phoenix is enshrouded in the steam, out of sight as his voice filters back. " ... and that just isn't possible"
