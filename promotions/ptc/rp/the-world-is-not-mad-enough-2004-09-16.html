The World Is Not Mad Enough

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 "Bong. Jimmy Bong." Imagine taking the point of view of the inside of a gun barrel. Darkness surrounds you, except for a small white circle of light just a few inches away. The light slowly trails from the right to the left... and within your sights is a slender, devilishly handsome young man wearing a tuxedo. After a brief pause, he quickly turns and sees you, reaching into his holster with LIGHTNING-QUICK SPEED! But as it turns out, his gun is stuck. He struggles to pull it out, mumbling to himself with frustration. "Gah... bloody... Walther! Why does this... always happen to me?" He spins around a few times, yanking desperately at his gun... the one in his holster, that is. For a few moments, it seems as if he might as well be trying to pull that sword out of the stone that you heard about as a kid. This guy is certainly no Arthur. It starts to look like this will continue all day, when... "Aha! Finally!" He succeeds! Now all that's left is for him to aim directly at you, and... "Bloody hell, this is a toy water pistol!!" Well, that's unfortunate. He tosses the water pistol aside in frustration. Then, he slowly looks up at you once more, staring down the gun barrel pointed directly at him. His eyes go wide with mortal terror! "Wait! No! Don't!" BANG! "AGH!!" He howls a pitiful scream, grabs his right arm, and slumps to the ground, whimpering like a baby. "That really hurts, you know!" Blood seems to trickle down from the top of your view to the bottom, turning the entire scene a bright shade of red. And then, it fades away into blackness... ---------------------------- OHMCS HEADQUARTERS - N'S OFFICE A FEW DAYS AGO SOMETIME O'CLOCK IN THE MORNING It was yet another morning in London, and yet another debriefing for yet another failed mission... "Well, Double-Oh-Nothing, I do hope you're satisfied. That's the 365th consecutive mission you've managed to botch with your idiocy." N, the head of On Her Majesty's Catering Service (the oddly-named British secret intelligence agency, heretoforth OHMCS), stood in front of her window as she spoke, her figure casting a silhouette against the light of the rising sun. Her back was turned to Jimmy Bong, who was also gazing out the window of N's top-floor office from his seat in front of her desk. "You're kidding!" he exclaimed with amazement. "Has it been 365 missions already? How time flies! Why, that means we can start marketing one of those page-a-day calendars that you always hear about! Oh goody!" "Shut up, Bong!" N growled as she spun around to face her subordinate. Her figure became more visible as she moved away from the window. To put it bluntly, she looked rather like the average grandmother. Her permed hair was a thick shade of grey, and she was wearing entirely too much make-up. Her excess red lipstick and blue eyeshadow made her look like a circus clown -- or perhaps a whore, but "circus clown" is more family-friendly. In any case, she also wore a pink sweatshirt with the words "World's Best Grandmum" printed across in black, as well as purple sweatpants and sneakers. This was a sharp contrast to Bong, a dashing young man with dark brown hair and sparkling blue eyes, who was wearing his trademark black tuxedo, as always. N clenched her fist around a clear plastic binder in her right hand as she continued. "Now listen, this is hardly anything to be proud of, Double-Oh-Nothing. As long as you've been with our organization, you've failed to successfully complete a single mission that has been given to you." "Hang on! I nearly pulled off that one operation in Bahrain a couple of years back!" Bong interjected. "Yes, nearly, Bong," N replied, "but now half of that country is a large radioactive hole thanks to you." "Yes, but half of it isn't," Bong retorted with a childish smirk. N angrily held up the binder in her hand, so Bong could see it very clearly. "And this report you filed is... is..." N seethed. "It's not even a report! It's nothing but a clear plastic binder with an old Calvin & Hobbes strip stuck inside, the one where Calvin puts a poorly-written report into a clear plastic binder in hopes of making it look better!" She tossed it aside, and it landed in a nearby wastebasket. "Are you saying it didn't work?" Bong asked. N sighed, and started massaging her temples. "Bong, just shut up. I could stand here and berate you all morning about your dismal service record, and I would love nothing more than to terminate your position right now, but unfortunately, I need you to take on another assignment." "Naturally, otherwise we don't have much of a story, do we?" "Quit breaking the fourth wall, Bong!" "Oh, terribly sorry." "Right," N mumbled as she picked up a remote control from the top of her desk. She pointed it at the wall to her left (or Bong's right, depending on how picky you may be), and a panel slid open to reveal a projection screen. With the push of another button on the remote, the window blinds closed shut, and the room darkened. A projector hanging from the ceiling clicked on, and cast the image of a tall, bald man wearing a grey overcoat and grey slacks, and looking rather evil. "Do you recognize this man, Double-Oh-Nothing?" N asked. Bong nearly leapt out of his chair. "YES! I'd recognize that man anywhere! How could I possibly forget! I still have nightmares about him, the bastard!" He paused for a moment, then turned to N. "Wait, no. Who the bloody hell is that?" "Ugh, Bong! You dolt!" N cried, frustration mounting. "That's your archenemy, Garfeld!" "Garfield? You mean my archenemy is that delightful cartoon feline from across the pond?" Bong asked, confused. "No, it's not Garfield, it's Garfeld!" spat N. "Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld! International terrorist and part-time hairbrush salesman! He's the world's most fiendish fiend!" "Oh." replied Bong. "Oh yeah, that guy. I remember now. It amazes me how he looks strikingly similar to such fellows as Dr. Evil and Ernst Stavro Blofeld, and yet he's just different enough to avoid a copyright lawsuit. How's he been? Did he have a good summer?" "I don't know about 'good,' but I can certainly say 'productive,'" answered N. "We've received numerous intelligence reports over the last few weeks that indicate Garfeld is about to enact a plot that will allow him to take over the world." "Take over the world," Bong repeated thoughtfully, rubbing his chin. "What an ingenious idea! Nobody has ever thought of doing that before! The man is a genius!" "Yes, very original," N mumbled, rolling her eyes. "At any rate, we need you to stop him, Bong." "Ah yes, I see," Bong grinned. "So you need me for this case because Garfeld is my archenemy and I know him better than anyone else does, despite the fact that my dismal record in this organization isn't even good enough to warrant holding a janitorial position, let alone having the world's most fiendish fiend as my archenemy?" "No," N said bluntly. "We need you for this case because nobody else is available." "Oh. Uh, are you sure?" "I'm quite sure, Bong. Double-Oh-Something is on assignment in Eastern Europe. Double-Oh-Everything is taking care of an important matter in the Middle East. Double-Oh-Anything is on duty in Asia, while Double-Oh-Better-Than-Nothing is doing reconnaissance in South America." "You know, out of all the Double-Oh-Things we have on staff, I always took offense to that last agent name," Bong pouted. "And I always took offense to your being alive, Double-Oh-Nothing," N replied irritatedly. "But you don't see me complaining about it." "You complain about it all the time!" Bong started. "In fact, you once gave me a mission in which my sole objective was to commit suicide!" "Yes, and you failed that one too," N sighed. "Anyway, Double-Oh-Nothing, your mission is to track down Garfeld and stop his evil plan at all costs." "Do we have any leads?" Bong asked. N hit a button on the remote once again. Everything in the office went back to as it was before -- the panels closed, the lights came back up, and the windows opened. N opened her top desk drawer and procured a manila folder, labelled "MISSION BRIEFING." Bong peered over N's desk at it curiously. "Is that a 'For Your Eyes Only' dossier?" he asked, reaching out to take it. N held it back, away from Bong's grasp, and scowling. "No!" she barked. "This is a 'For Everyone Else's Eyes Except Yours' dossier!" "Drat," mumbled Bong under his breath with disappointment. "I never get to read anything." N cracked open the folder and skimmed through its contents for a moment. "According to what we know, Garfeld has apparently been in contact with one or more persons who work for a company known as Asylum Industries. We're not completely certain about this, of course, but it's the only lead we have." Bong's eyes lit up. "Of course!" he exclaimed, jumping to his feet. "It all makes sense now! Garfeld is planning to take over Asylum Industries, and then shut it down! Why, you may ask? Because everybody knows that Asylum Industries is the world's sole manufacturer of... asylums! Asylums and other mental institutions! It's all so clear now! If Asylum Industries were to be shut down, the world's supply of asylums would run dry! Ergo, every mental patient, unbalanced criminal, and all other sorts of crazy cads and goofy gooks would run amuck all over the world! It would be chaos! Insanity! And dare I say... MADNESS!" "I deal with enough madness just having you in this office, Double-Oh-Nothing," N sighed. "Now sit down!" "Yes'm," Bong replied sheepishly, taking a seat once again. "Anyway," N continued. "We can't say for sure that Garfeld has met face-to-face with anybody in Asylum Industries. However, their CEO seems to be the type of person who would conspire with such a madman for the purpose of global domination." "CEO?" repeated Bong questioningly. "Indeed," replied N, leafing through the mission dossier again. She took out a photograph and laid it on the desk in front of Bong. Excited about finally getting to see something from one of those top-secret files, Bong leaned forward to examine it. "I say, this fellow looks rather like Captain Hook," Bong remarked. "His name is Michael Montgomery," N explained. "The last time we checked, he was the CEO of Asylum Industries. If Garfeld is involved with Asylum in any way, then Montgomery is sure to know about it. He appears to be the type of person who loves power, so he's the best lead we have at the moment." "Power-hungry, you say?" asked Bong. "In addition to being the CEO at Asylum Industries, Montgomery also works as a professional wrestler with an American-based league called the Universal Wrestling Federation," N went on. "There, he acts as their Executive Vice President of Talent Relations, and is also their current heavyweight champion." "Well, he sounds like a delightful fellow, except for the power-hungry evil part," Bong quipped with delight. "So, I take it you want me to question him?" "I want you to get as much information from him as you can," answered N. "You must be careful about it though, we can't let Garfeld catch wind that we're on his trail. I've already made arrangements for you to have a meeting with Montgomery." "Oh? Do tell, then!" asked Bong excitedly. "A hotel bar? A dank alley in the middle of the night? A subway station?" "No, Bong. A wrestling tournament," N said very seriously. Bong look quite taken aback by this. "I... beg your pardon?" "Montgomery is due to take part in a major world-wide wrestling tournament called GTT4," N continued. "He is a frequent traveler and difficult to get close to, so this is the best chance we have. I have entered your name into this tournament, Double-Oh-Nothing, and have arranged for you to be Montgomery's first-round opponent." "Wh-what!?" exclaimed Bong. "Are you mad!? I can't wrestle! I don't have any athletic ability whatsoever! I don't have any muscles! I'm just a skinny little idiot who can't do anything right!" "And sadly, the fate of the world is in your hands," sighed N. "I'm sorry Bong, but this is the only way to do this. Not to worry, however... when you arrive at the tournament site, our man from Weapons Research & Development will be there to assist you in every way possible." "Oh? Who is that?" asked Bong. "U," said N. "Who?" "U." "Me?" "No, not you, U!" "Ah... wait, me?" "No, Bong! It's U! U will be there to help you!" "I'll be there to help myself?" Bong asked, just plain bloody confused now. "I have no idea what the hell you're talking about!" "U is going to be at the tournament site to equip you with the latest technology his department has developed, in order to assist you in your match with Montgomery!" N shouted. "Oh come now, N!" Bong sighed. "You're the head of OHMCS! At least use proper grammar! Honestly, 'you is...'" "Bong," N seethed through her teeth. "Let me explain this one more time. You are going to the GTT4 tournament site, to wrestle against Montgomery, in order to extract as much information as you can from him about Garfeld's fiendish plan to take over the world. Our man from Weapons Research & Development will be there to assist you. Got it?" "Yes," Bong said, hesitantly, "but one thing... what is that guy's name?" "It's U," replied N. "Me?" "GET OUT OF MY OFFICE!!!" N screamed at the top of her lungs. "YOU'RE DRIVING ME TO MADNESS!!!" "Er, yes sir! Ma'am! Sir!" Bong saluted N, then scurried out of the office in a hurry. N threw the mission dossier after him as he closed the door shut. As the folder flew open and papers scattered all over the floor, N took a few deep breaths, then sat back down at her desk, regaining her composure. She reached down into the bottom drawer of her desk, and pulled out a bottle of brandy, along with an empty glass. She poured herself a rather large portion, and then downed the whole thing with one gulp. She sighed, as she glanced out the window at the rising sun once more, and then poured herself another drink. "Do me a favor and don't come back alive," she mumbled. "I heard that!" a voice from the other side of the wall yelled back. And thus, Jimmy Bong, Agent Double-Oh-Nothing of On Her Majesty's Catering Service, was entered into the GTT4 wrestling tournament, for the purpose of stopping the world's most fiendish fiend, Johannes Frederick Sebastian Billy-Bob Garfeld, from taking over the world. We're all doomed. DISCLAIMER: "Jimmy Bong" and all associated characters are spoofed from Ian Fleming's "James Bond" character and series. All written content here is original.
