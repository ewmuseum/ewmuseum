Time is waiting

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 A house… well, you wouldn’t call it that unless you lived in a building that rivalled Windsor Palace. A mansion is a more fitting word for something of such size and bravado. The mansion that Salaok once called home and yet hasn’t seen it, since he was taken to hospital with a gun shot wound. Salaok had stepped out of that hospital a new man, not buy choice, but by the simple fact that he didn’t know who he was. With no memory of his once luxurious life filled with the fruits of theft and murder, Salaok has been walking the streets doing what he can to get by. With no recollection of the house, it has been left bare for the past year but soon it will be full again. Soon Salaok will return home and remember all that it is that this world has done to him, but more importantly, he will dredge up everything he has done to the world. There is but two things that will happen that day and that is the one thing, which can not be controlled through manipulation. Either Salaok will accept the man he once was and embrace it with open arms, or he will be sickened by the tortured soul he created for himself and turn away from it forever. When the door to the mansion unlock once more, the world will revolutionize again that day and one man’s life as he knows it will be destroyed. Dust now settles on the floor where his wife was shot but the stain in the wood will eternally be entrenched into it’s grain, while the window where the deathly bullet flew through has since been repaired. The door to his son’s bed room remains shut and has done so since the six year old’s body was removed, leaving the all-consuming memories shut off in a room now overflowing with only the bitter stale air. Soon he will return and that day will be one for the history books. That day is coming. They say that the eyes are the windows to a man’s soul… The blinds are about to be pulled up for the first time and the view isn’t going to be a good one.
