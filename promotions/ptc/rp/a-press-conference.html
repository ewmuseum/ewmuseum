rkvanilla Seasoned Network Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 223 Location: Norwalk, CT Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 7:11 pm Post subject: A Press Conference A Press Conference The Battle for the Extreme Title: Part 2 ------------------------------------------------- -- Waking Up, Driving Off -- Now with a clear mind set I set out on my second adventure before the Extreme Cluster Match. It was a regular question an answer session, but first thing was first. I had to get the hell up out of my bed. I could not even move. I must have slept the wrong way or something. It was hard for me to pull myself up to sit on the edge of the bed, collecting my clothing for the day. I looked like a professional mafia member with my pinstripes all the way down my body, topping it all off with a James Bond-like bowtie. I should have bought a Panama hat or a brimmed rim hat, and then I might have been mistaken for the original Al Capone. I chuckled as I looked at myself in the mirror. I looked ridiculous. This was not my regular clothing at all. I was very used to being a junky dresser, who did I have to impress right? It is not as if anybody had the guts to say anything to me if they did not like my attire for the day, the only people who actually cared were my good friends. However, I was not seeing them today. I did not even have time to take a shower this morning. I had to get immediately out the door and to the production studio for my session with the many broadcasters there. I was told that many great stations would be there from radio and television, including a possible camera operator there from the famed ESPN shows “Sportscenter”. They were there doing a segment on professional wrestling, and wanted to catch a glimpse at anyone that walked through the revolving doors of the studio. I must have been turning into their version of a spokesperson. Everywhere I went now, the people stopped and stared. They muttered under their breathes, “there goes the LOW Aftermath Champion, he’s a determined son of a –“ I had to shut their mouths by signing autographs and taking Polaroid’s with their children, teenagers, wives, and even mistresses. Nevertheless, it did not bother me in the least bit, I was a people person from the get go. Oh well, might as well hit the road now. My appointment was in an hour, and they wanted me there fifteen minutes beforehand to do a brief ‘meet and greet’ with the representatives from the different types of media. I took a deep breath in and told myself that everything would go fine, that nobody would interrupt the conference and that nothing was going to get on my nerves. Sometimes the tiniest things ticked me off, but for today, for the cameras sake, I would behave myself in front of the others. I got to my coffee table in the middle of the main room, and I picked up my belongings to put them in their respected pockets. My wallet always went in my left and my keys always went into my right. I then took my apartment keys and kept them in my hand so I could lock up after pulling the door closed behind me. I walked to my door, locking it and closing it behind me after grabbing a jacket. The weather was a bit windy today, so I had to pretend as if the elements bothered me just enough for me to wear the slicker. I then put my apartment keys in my left pocket along with my car keys, before walking down the hallway. The elevator was down there, along with some snack and soda machines to get the cure for a midnight munch attack. Of course, right next door to that was a workout room to burn out the extra calories. I kept walking past the computer room and basketball court where I loved to stop sometime to play ‘two bounce’. I then stopped right before the elevator and looked out the window, looking at the swimming pool from a couple of floors up. The view was always amazing, and it was as if you were on a cloud. I never wanted to leave this place since I first moved in, the people were nice and the scenery was even greater. However, low and behold I was a traveling man. Moreover, today I had some work to get through before I could sleep. I pushed the elevator button, and had to wait a while for it to mend its way up the six floors. Finally, its metallic doors opened and I was greeted with the cheesy tunes of the ‘80s. It was not pleasant, but it would have to do. Besides, it was usually a non-stop trip to the parking lot. I always parked my car in the first parking garage, it was easier to get in and out of and a lot closer to the road in case an emergency came up. Getting inside of the elevator, I now pushed the button to go down to the main parking garage. The doors quickly closed on me, trapping me within the walls of the elevator as it slowly but surely made its way down the rope track to the bottom of the apartment complex. The elevator stopped on the main garage floor, it was a non-stop travel like usual. Another reason for enjoying my complex was that nobody really bothered each other. However, when they did it was always in a friendly manor. Perhaps it was just to borrow the infamous cup of sugar, or maybe bickering wives would share rumors of cheating husbands and tips on where to get the best deals on a pedicure or facial wax for their date tonight. Anyway, it was time to get in my car and go to the production studio, I indeed had a long day ahead of me and I would need an aspirin by the end of the night as well. Some of the questions just got on my nerves, but I was not going to complain about some people that were simple minded. I received normal and intelligent questions too. You know the ones that I do not use just ayes or no as an answer. Those were the ones I enjoyed the most, because I loved to discuss things in my own world. I loved recalling my own beliefs and truly enlightening the people around me as they cheered me on. I now walked to my car, unlocking the door and opening it to sit inside of it. I buckled up (safety first) and then turned the engine on, it still purred like a kitten. What did I expect? It was a new car, well if you think of 1999 as new, then it was. I liked the car, besides the fact that it was mine of course. It was an awesome blue color and matched the luminosity of the moon as it caught the ocean just right at nighttime. Everyone’s head turned when it went by, and no they were not just looking at me either you wise asses. I was not that popular, but I had a feeling that I was going to be very soon. Now you may ask yourself, just why in the hell am I going to a press conference in the first place? The answer is easy, and the answer is to prepare for the Extreme Cluster Match. I loved letting the people’s questions get to me sometimes, they gave me inspiration beyond my wildest dreams and sometimes some of the questions let my imagination be the one that went wild. I always had crazy ideas about what the future might hold, and I never knew that some day people would actually applaud those ideas. I guess that they were happy that they finally found a cheery person to represent him or herself, someone that was like them. To them, I was someone who worked hard to earn his keep and someone who stood for people if I thought that they were doing the right thing in life. They looked up to me, and I looked right back into their eyes with the smile radiantly coming from my red eyes. It did not scare them or intimidate them, but that was only because they never saw me in the ring before. I am sure that if they did, their mind set would be very different. I did not want to come off as a bad person though. I wanted to stake my claim as everybody’s baby throughout my career. However, sometimes, the baby has to grow into a man. The tournament was my second one so far in my Lights Out Wrestling career. The first time out, I was creamed like a can of Green Giant corn. I did not stand a chance, but that was only because I did not have the slightest notion as to what I was doing. Now, things were different. Now, I was inspired and had a mindset. I was ready and willing to succeed at this fight finally, and I was ready to claim another belt around my waist. As I kept riding along the road to the production studio after pulling out of the garage and giving the valet parker a ‘token’ of my esteem, I thought over some things in my cerebral vortex (the part of the brain that makes you think and react, in case you never paid attention in anatomy or most likely psychology class). I always seemed to ask myself tougher questions that the folks sitting down at the table in front of me ever could. Maybe it was because I knew myself better than they did, even though they did not like to admit those kinds of facts. They just thought that they were right day in and day out, and it brought a smile to my face when they tried to tell me how to perform my job when they haven’t even seen the inside of an arena. Nevertheless, it was fine, because I always told them how to perform their job better too (mainly to just stop asking idiotic questions). It all went full circle for us, and amazingly it was always the same group of interviewers all the times I went there that asked the same boneheaded questions. So what were some of my questions that were running through my head now?: . How would I prove myself worthy against so many other tough competitors? I would have to be tough myself of course. I had to stay up all last night studying my videos. I did a complete cram session of books and tutorials. I was a better man today, I had more knowledge now and I was ready. I just had to go in there and prove myself worthy to go up against these kinds of people that wanted to take me down. One was already obsessing over me, and that kind of stuff always freaked me out. However, I was prepared to handle this beast in my own fashion, by slaying it royally and burying it early. Sure, I would get my bumps and bruises too, but it would all be worth it in the end. . What was I trying to prove by stepping into the ring? Was I insane, or was I just looking for a death wish in the match? What was I trying to prove? Of course, I was trying to prove that I was the best and that I deserved to have my name engraved on a slap of gold. In addition, as for being insane, that was not the deal with me. In addition, no, I did not have a death with either. I had more of a vision if you will, and the vision was that I because that much better by winning. I would not become an egomaniac. I would stay level with the crowd and wouldn’t put myself higher than anyone else in the PTC corporate ladder. Sure, people would look up to me to do things like this repeatedly, but that came with the territory. I am sure as soon as they saw that I was just a normal person with an average record as of now, they would not think of me any differently than if I had never stepped into the match. . Why was I wearing this stupid suit? Snap out of it Rob, you are thinking about your suit again. However, how could I not? It was so dumb, I wish I could just go home and throw it all off. I couldn’t get in with my pajamas though, and I couldn’t go in with some freaky dress either like I would wear for going out in the ring. But maybe I should have done that, I didn’t want to be thought of as the “sophisticated champion”. It was too late to turn around now though, I was only blocks away from the studio and there was still the threat of having to take a headache killer in my gut reaction. -- Getting There, Sitting Down – Ah, there was the building that I was used to seeing. It was so tall and stretched to the skies even more every time I stopped by to do one of these public events. I guess the fans always wanted to know what was going on with me, and I would tell them too out of the act of being polite. Today was just a good day to let it all hang out, at least in my opinion anyway. Getting out of the car in the first level-parking garage, I shut the door and walked down the ramp, letting the cool breeze touch my face and my hair. Ah, springtime was here finally. After months of cold and snow, the weather was finally warming towards summer. There was always one problem with that though, the occasional sore throat twice a year. But hey, I was human right? It would just be silly to think otherwise. Anyway, I gathered my body to the door after about a minute of walking down the ramp way. The door was glass and had a cherry oak pull to it. Of course, there was security at the main desk inside waiting to interrogate you like a criminal. But, after all the years of doing this, I was used to it and new almost all of the answers by heart. Sometimes I think that the guards thought I was telepathic. I once tried to answer their questions once before they even got the chance to finish them. They just looked at me weird of course, and I gave the same spaced-out eyes back as they chuckled under their breath before leading me to the right floor and the right room. I walked into the doors, swinging them open as if I were in an old western movie. I was waiting for a piano to grow silent or something, waiting for everyone’s heads to turn and say that I was the ‘rootenest tootenest’ something or other along those lines. Maybe I watched too much Yosemite Sam and Bugs Bunny when I was a kid, who knows. I walked up to the security check in point and told big Marcus there my name. He went through the random airport questions. Was this trip for business or for pleasure? Of course, it was always business here so I had to go along with those lines. What was the company that I was going to see? I was just doing a press conference for the Primetime Central Extreme Cluster Match, no big deal. Finally, what time was my appointment? I looked at my watch. It was in about twenty minutes or so. Marcus just nodded and sent me to the longest hallway that I have ever seen, telling me to go ten doors down and turn to the right. I did what I was told. Within a couple of minutes, I was inside, looking at these ‘vultures’ straight into their beady eyes. I was waiting for one of them to snap out a quick question before I was ready to sit down. They always tried to catch me off guard but they soon found out that I was always prepared for anything, even their Polaroid’s that flashed all around me as I went to my seat in front of the lot of them. -- Let the Questioning Begin – I tapped my fingers on the oak table as if to signal the start of trading on Wall Street. Immediately somebody stood up with a wireless microphone in his hand, and he cleared his throat as I cleared mine as well, trying to imitate him with a slight bit of comedic performance before the day began. “Yes Iron Lung. I am Jerrod Clark here with the Chicago Tribune. “ Ah, not a very local paper, but publicity none of the less. What was his question? “My question to you is this: How did you prepare for this exciting match?” I calmly breathed in and out, letting the wind catch my insides as I politely answered the kind man back before he sat down. There was a limit to the amount of questions, and he didn’t want to ask them all at once. If he did, he would be basking in silence for the rest of the day while watching eyes wondered just what in the world he was thinking. “Ah, a great question to kick things off Jerrod. Let me answer you with the best of my knowledge. I did a cram fest last night, stayed up all last night studying everything that I have learned so far. It was very exciting, and I wish you were there to see me jotting down notes. It was like a study session, very fun stuff. Does that answer your question well enough?” Jerrod nodded his head as I took a sip of water, waiting for the next questionnaire to arise from the mists of the seats. This time, a woman stepped forward to embark on the journey. She was sharply dressed, and I had to adjust my bowtie from fear of it steaming up around the collar. She flipped her hair slightly before addressing me. “Yes, Mr. Pendragon. I’m Stacey Bloom from CBS. Would you mind telling me exactly what is going through your mind at this very moment?” I didn’t mind responding to this at all. I simply took another sip of water while looking at her carefully, trying to figure out exactly what she wanted from my answer. Then, I finally responded after she sat down to wait patiently for my comeback. “What’s going through my mind at the moment is the fact that a very beautiful woman asked a very intelligent question. What is also going through my mind is that I am far from ready for this matchup, so you must all ask why I am even bothering to enter the ring? The answer is just as simple as proving you all wrong about me. You see, you still think I am going to get my ass kicked in this match, just as if I did last year. Last year to me was eons ago, because I am more prepared than ever. Now you say, but he just said he was not. According to my standards, I am never one hundred percent ready. However, I will give it my best damn shot and give it all I got tonight in front of the capacity crowd.” She nodded at me. Another question was successfully answered in the proper way. Amazingly, I didn’t have a headache yet. But I was sure that one was to come ay any moment, some goof would have to stand up and ask me how I was feeling today or if I slept well, or maybe even what was up with my suit. It was only a matter of moments. Sure enough, another man stood up in the mist of my grogginess. He was dressed in a Wall Street manor as well, and he looked a bit nervous to be conversing with me. But, I reassured him that I was a normal man by acknowledging him with a nod, and he seemed to relax. His voice was a bit cracked as he asked his question. ‘Um yes, I’m Thomas Redway from the New York Times. My question to you is how in the hell does someone like you become a spokesperson for the Home Depot?” I had to think a while before I gave off any type of a wise-mouthed answer. I wanted to reach out and slap the taste buds out of his mouth, but luckily, I was more polite than that. I simply just chuckled and breathed heavily before answering. “Well, I guess it was just dumb luck Tom. I never realized that I had the potential to do something like that, but I guess I did. But anyway, this press conference isn’t about that. This is about my shot at the PTC Extreme Title, and Home Depot’s wide selection of tools won’t help me in the least bit. Neither will my Home Depot credit card with the two point five-six APR on it. Nothing but my own courage and beliefs will help me tonight. So how does someone become a news reporter for the New York Times?” Tom didn’t answer me, he knew better than to answer my question. He knew that I was being spiteful, but that’s what he deserved for asking a non-relevant question. Who did these hot shots think they were anyway? They certainly weren’t impressing me any. The whole day was basically like that, questioning and answering all day. After the meeting, I had to have another meet and greet where I signed autographs that went to charity. It was mainly for the Ronald McDonald houses across the nation, a great charity to donate to if you ask me. Whenever I get leftover change at McDonald’s, it always ends up in that bank for the kids. It was my way of volunteering under my busy schedule, and every time I dropped a coin in there I thought of the children that I must be pleasing my doing so. After the meet and greet, it was finally time to go home. It couldn’t have came any sooner, I could feel the migraines filling my head as I walked to the parking lot to find my car. I got in and found the bottle of Aspirin gel caps that I received as a free sample in the mail, I also had a small fanny pack of water with me. I swigged the cap down and proceeded to exit the building, breathing a sigh of relief. The day was over, but the struggle for the title certainly wasn’t. I had to keep pressing on and keep training, it wouldn’t be an easy task. But for now I wouldn’t worry about it, I had a long drive ahead of me before I could sleep… _________________ *~ TWILIGHT SLAMS FED HEAD: ROB K. *~ Iron Lung's LOW record: 9 - 9 - 1 Accomplishments: Aftermath Champion PTC Ranking: 25 / 97
