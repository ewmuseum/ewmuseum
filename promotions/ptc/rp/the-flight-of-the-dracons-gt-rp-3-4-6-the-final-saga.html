The Flight of the Dracons[GT rp#3, 4/6 The Final Saga]

8 April 2003

Kaiowas Cable Pimp Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 52 Posted: Tue Apr 08, 2003 7:58 pm Post subject: The Flight of the Dracons[GT rp#3, 4/6 The Final Saga] Death? Not now... Not death, it cannot come now! Not at this moment of time. Not at this important moment, not at this turning point, at this revolutionary point. Nothing can get in the way! It must not be let to stop him! He cannot be pulled to a halt now! If he is he may never see his hardships and efforts come to fruitition. He could not let that happen. It meant so much to him to follow through on all his threats and preparation! People had died for his cause... He could not let their deaths go to waste. Their sacrifices should not be ended by a fatality miles away from his goal. No... He cannot die here... Not now, not before the Bastard Pultra falls and is sent to the bowels of wherever those who have sinned go to pay for their transgressions. Kaiowas had a future and he was not going to spurn it here, not with so much at stake, not with his life about to drastically improve as soon as he finally plunged the blade through Pultra's heart and took away from the lying trickster what he had possessed for too long. Kaiowas would bring Vengeance on the one who had invented the term on Earth. Kaiowas was going to fulfil his position as Angel of Retribution for the last time... He was going after Pultra and he wasn't going to let any demon kill him to stop him.... Not here and not ever... Blood? Little cells full of haemoglobin that fly around the body at the pump of a very strong muscular structure? That's not all though! Blood is more important than the refreshing oxygen that it brings to every single living cell in the body, it is more than the immune system that it carts around our network of vessels veins and arteries. Not just a cell which makes the body live on, exist under pressure and deliver everything that the body needs in liquid format. No, blood is much more than a biological substance that people require at least eight pints of constantly to survive. Blood, for some people, is honour... is victory.... is the greatest achievement that one can receive... Blood? Blood when it spills from an enemies gut... Blood when it is drawn forth during a duel, a head to head, a brawl.... Always the first to draw blood is either the luckiest or the strongest fighter of the two. Blood means so much... to some it is a victory to smear the blood of the conquered over your chest in a symbol of your utter dominance. Some even bathe in it, or drink it to maintain their everlasting immortal lives. Demons swear by its taste, the salty taste of a sworn enemy who has fallen. The taste that brings so much to mind. Blood itself is in no way pure.... It is as corrupt as its owner and the more corrupt the saltier the taste of the blood..... [Salt? Why could Kaiowas taste salt? It was really strong too...] Kaiowas: Eugghh.. [Now he remembers....] *** Consciousness *** [Kaiowas' eyes snap open and his vision is filled with that of a huge demon with blood red skin. It loomed over him, it's chest cut to shreds and the green flesh and blood that should have stayed firmly on the inside was now hanging and flowing freely. The green blood didn't just drip onto Kaiowas, staining his body with that vile deep green mossy colour, it covered him in waves as the demon's heart futiley sent the blood round the body over and over again. Kaiowas had wounded the beast but it had not been enough. It seemed to have such a high pain threshold. Kaiowas had even broken its kneecap with an extremely powerful blow to its right knee. Kaiowas had thrown every combat technique he could muster to gain any sort of advantage over this huge opponent and he had finally ended up on the chest of the beast, tearing into it's sternum and the fleshy areas around it with his bladed staff. Flesh had been torn away, severed and shredded but it still had not finished off the monster. Now the Demon's foot rests on Kaiowas' thick chestnut brown sternum, pressing down putting huge pressure on him. The Demon leant over him, getting closer by the second, gloating in the thought of inevitable victory as his long clawed right hand reached out ready to tear his head clean off his shoulders. Kaiowas lay under his huge cleft foot feeling the full effects of the battle of the previous ten minutes.] Demon: Now comes your time....... little warrior.... [That phrase... It had been used so many many times to tease and taunt Kaiowas... In the few seconds before his inevitable death Kaiowas reflected quickly on all those who had dared use that phrase... How it had angered him so... How it had brought the feeling of revolution in him. His rebellious streak strikes out, against the odds. Pultra had used it, his pet name for his Angel of Retribution. And now Pultra would have suffered for his ignorance and his stupidity. Would have.... The Slayer also used it... Kaiowas hated the phrase, the taunt that it brought along with it. Kaiowas was in no way insignificant and hated that anyone would imply it with the phrase. He despised the condescending tone that the phrase was used in. Kaiowas just really really despised anyone who thought themselves good enough to use that phrase. And they usually lived to regret their stupid words. That Vampire, Felrig, had tried to convince him to follow him by using it... That Vampire Felrig? And his Werewolf friend? His memory quickly flowed back into the brain cells inside his thick Brazilian skull. Kaiowas' eyes opened in realisation... For the first time since they captured him he knew what was going on. The Dracons? The group that needed Kaiowas? The ones who needed his help, who needed his power and who had helped him to overthrow the attack on the Ktulu mere days ago... Kaiowas knew it all, and he knew that this was their doing... The voices had asked him to show them his skills. To SHOW them... And before now Kaiowas had in no way ever guessed that the two were linked... Of course he hadn't.... He had not remembered them... It had become so clear... Kaiowas was fighting for their acceptance... To SHOW them his skills.....] Kaiowas: My time... [Kaiowas starts to struggle and wriggle as the beast reaches down for the final blow.] Kaiowas: Will never come! [Bursting into a flurry of action Kaiowas pulls his legs up, hooking them around the demon's leg standing on his chest. A look of pure fury has descended over Kaiowas' face and the hazy red mist has fallen over his vision as he fills with the bloodlust that he lives on during combat... That empowers his underdog advantage. The Demon looks at the warrior with a momentary expression of bewilderment before Kaiowas executes his escaping manoeuvre. With his hooked legs he presses down on the thigh of the demon, exerting huge pain on the broken kneecap taking the brunt of the pressure. With a surge of huge strength, Kaiowas swiftly pushes down on the thigh enough so that the Demon topples onto its back. The demon's face fills with fear and surprised shock. His arms fly into the air as his huge back makes a loud contact with the dusty surface of the collosseum. The scream roars forth from between sharp malicious teeth. Following through on the manoeuvre Kaiowas rolls up to standing and heaves the demon onto his belly, then applying the only thing that wrestling had taught him that would be ever used in this battle. Kaiowas has locked on the Brazilian kneelock! Yanking back and pulling on the torn and twisted knee Kaiowas exerts excruciating pain, torturing the demon to its threshold and well past it as the painful screams do not relent and the resistance of the beast dies away. Soon Kaiowas lets go, letting the huge leg slump to the ground causing a cloud of dust from the arena floor.] Kaiowas: But you time, beast... [Quickly snatching up his discard bladed staff Kaiowas scales the demon's chest one last time, this time standing over the ragged combatant. It's breathing is ragged and hurried, the pain it has suffered contorts its face into a hateful scowl but the inevitability of it's death doesn't seem to shock it. Raising the staff to use it as a spear to skewer the heart of the huge demon Kaiowas speaks his final words to the demon...] Kaiowas: ...has already run out! [Down falls the staff, straight and sure, aimed for the weakened area to enter and tear the final thread of life left from the beast and be victorious. As the staff falls, the light glints off Lecood's blades and the first disappears quickly into the heart cavity of the huge red demon. The body of the giant trembles at the pain and the shakes begin. Kaiowas waits a second then twists the serrated blade in further, destroying as much internal structure as possible. Soon the body has stopped even spasming and all signs of life have left the demon.... Pulling the notched bladed staff from the heart cavity of the beast with an almighty tug Kaiowas cleans it on the sands and...] *** FLICKER.......FLASH *** [Everything melts away.... Absolutely everything melts away. The collosseum, the dust that floats in the air, the turgid dry taste on the air itself disappears. The sky fades, the wind drops... All the noises that had inhabited this are only seconds ago have disappeared. Every speck of colour or definition has gone.... Looking down, Kaiowas sees that the green blood of the demon has vanished from his chest and that the bloody stains on the floor leading to the recently dead beast have all gone. Even the demon who had seemed so so real only instants before, had left this place. It was as if an artists had cleaned his palate of everything but Kaiowas. Kaiowas sat and watched everything surreally vanish and his mind began to jump again.... What was going on? Why was he standing here in another blank place... Would he ever be able to escape this blank emptiness that seems to follow him all the time?] Kaiowas: What the? [Suddenly, most unexpectedly a hollow sound resounds off all the walls of the area as the lights go up... Kaiowas stands alone in a huge grey steel walled room. Everything shines like chrome and there is no way that there could have been a whole collosseum here only minutes ago. The sound however was even more haunting. It was the sound of echoed sarcastic clapping, slow and with that underlying sentiment of sarcasm. The worst thing was, it was bodiless, Kaiowas could see no-one, no-one was here and no-one here was clapping. Yet he could still hear the sound echo off the sheer shining steel walls that surrounded him. Kaiowas begins to look all around him, bringing his staff up in defence. He no longer feels any of the fatigue of battle and he notices that he feels great and that any of the scratches and wounds he had received during the fight had also disappeared.... Kaiowas quickly wondered to himself if there ever was a huge blood red demon which he had slain, he wondered if he had ever fought in the great collosseum and he wondered if was ever on the brink of death under the hoof of a ten foot demon... But the clapping continued now backed up with an evil chilling disconcerting laughter....] Kaiowas: (deep and annoyed tone) Who's there? [The laughter stops abruptly and the clapping trails off into an uncertain silence. Kaiowas is still checking all around him and he is suddenly startled by...] *** CLUNK *** [Kaiowas jumps and turns full circle raising his staff aggressively. As he turns, he sees what is happening. By the time he has landed facing the opposite way, he sees a whole panel of the wall rising out of the wall and disappearing through the roof. Behind the wall shines a very bright white light. The light blinds Kaiowas' eyes a little and he raises his left arm, letting go of the staff with one hand, and shield his retinas from the bright rays. A little disorientated Kaiowas rambles a little confusedly...] Kaiowas: Who? Wha? [Soon Kaiowas felt confident enough that his eyes could cope with the intense light and he removes his hand, replacing it on the handle of the long bladed staff.... Peering into the white void he begins to make out a figure in the extremeness of the lighting. The silhouette was not familiar, a tall man, about the same height as Kaiowas stood in the glass protected cell. He stood in a fully black suit, black glasses and thick well trimmed black hair.... It struck one image into Kaiowas' mind... FBI. The memories of his flight from the FBI, the dead officer on his hilltop that had disappeared seconds later. The rings with the dragons in full flight... The Vampire and the Werewolf that had captured him after asking him to join with them... Everything seemed to be falling into place at last... Thoughts and suspicions fly through the neural networking of Kaiowas' mind, everything clicking together, everything finally making sense... He could see it all clearly now... All he needed was this fool in the suit to confirm it to him in words...Only Kaiowas wanted to know all about the capture and the fight with the seemingly imaginary demon.] Kaiowas: Finally... *** CSSSSSSHHHHHHH *** [The sound of an airlock opening and the pressure being released was extremely foreign to Kaiowas but the young Brazilian did not flinch at all as the oval shaped door swung open inwards. The man in the black pressed suit strode slowly in, his hands clasped in front of him, his face pensive and business like. He walks silently, the trod of his sharp shoes squeaking on the floor being the only sound. Kaiowas follows him with his gaze suspiciously. The man walks up and down in front of Kaiowas a few times before he stops in front of him, obviously thinking up his first statement.] Kaiowas: What do you want with me.... What does the Dracons want with me? [Suddenly something happens that does make Kaiowas jump... The room of pure grey begins to re-mould itself. It melts itself down and soon the room has become a comfortable darkened pool hall. The plush green pool tables rest in long lines under lowered lamps. In the corner rest the pool cues and behind Kaiowas is the seated section...] Agent Duddy: You like that little effect....Just as we used for your two little tests there my friend. I, sir.... am Agent Duddy. An agent of the FBI and a member of the Dracons. And I sir, you have tried to kill on two occasions.... [Rolling up his sleeve, Duddy shows Kaiowas a long and deep scar that runs along his lower arm... Thoughts and flashbacks of the tussle with the werewolf where Kaiowas nearly skewered the beast through the heart fly into Kaiowas' mind but he cannot re-call any past meeting with this man...] Kaiowas: You? The werewolf? [Fixing his blackened glasses, Agent Duddy rolls his sleeve back down..] Agent Duddy: Yes... me the werewolf... But I'm sure you do not remember our first encounter mr. Junior... [Kaiowas replies snappily to the usage of his real name..] Kaiowas: Kaiowas....... That is my name... no other... But no. I do not recall when we first met... I killed the last FBI officer that I encountered. [Kaiowas remembers the image of him knocking the poor officer unconscious and hurling him off the side of his hilltop, throwing the FBI momentarily off his case....] Agent Duddy: You think? You think he died.... Let me tell you a little something Tribal Warrior... [Duddy takes a seat on one of the pool tables, relaxing in his tight regimental suit. As he seems to be setting up for a long tale, Kaiowas drops his staff onto a pool table, where it hits a white sphere sending it into another which ends up falling into one on the holes at the corner. Kaiowas turns and grabs a seat from the seating area behind him. Seeing that Kaiowas has sat down, the man in the FBI uniform begins to speak...] Agent Duddy: Kaiowas, this is something I can only tell to those who are entrusted with service from the Dracons... But I see it imperative that you are not lead blind into this.... shall I say... Relationship. Now.... There is something very basic about the Dracons that you have not come to realise yet. The inklings might be in you head because of the form your capture took. It is something which all our members have in common and it is the most important part of our little alliance... Kaiowas... The Dracons' members are all supernaturally gifted, innately or gained during life.... [The idea hit Kaiowas as so obvious that he really should have realised it so much sooner. The Dracons had tried to hide their supernatural identities but when they came to capture him they had to overpower him and had to unmask their true selves... The Vampire, Felrig.... Agent Duddy, the FBI Agent come Werewolf... It seemed to fit... So Kaiowas listened on in awe...] Agent Duddy: We recruit members everyday, we search out our brothers and sisters, that is where I usually come in... I, in my role as an investigator for the FBI of supernatural happenings have the greatest chance to find and search out our new members... It works as a circle of loyal members. Each one of us is in some position of power and is a worker of the arts or a member if its legions... There are many many influential people that play a part in our little group. We control over fifty percent of America's economy, we hold power over 35 of all the states of America.... We number so many, werewolves, vampire, banshees, witches, wizards... Anything that you read of as a child and feared, we are all that and more. We hold dominance over the world and a huge chunk of the next plane.... You think you killed that man you threw off a cliff. If you knew your horror lore you would know that no werewolf can die unless he is shot through the heart by a pure silver bullet.... That was me that you threw off that precipice..... I was one of the Dracons searching for you. Yet we only number those who can mould themselves into the human society. We are the secret the government hides, we are too influential to be discovered, no scandal shall ever touch us... [The whole speech is definitely eye opening to Kaiowas. Somehow he seems quite displeased with the whole organisation, a scowl dressing his face during most of the tale. He still holds his anti-capitalistic views from his early life. He does not like those to be more powerful just because they have more money and contacts, but he had to admit that this organisation seemed to be run with very high efficiency and that the whole idea deserved respect...] Kaiowas:(angrily) Duddy..... Stop. I understand all this shit about your little business adventures and your power trips.... Why do you capture ME? Why do you put me through these tests that I have excelled in? Why do the Dracons have need of me... Felrig said it and I am sure you are aiming to talk me around with your little speech... Tell me now and I can decide quickly... [Duddy does not seem shocked by Kaiowas' ignorant and nasty tone of voice, nor does it ruffle his feathers that Kaiowas is impatient. He continues, answering Kaiowas in the process.] Agent Duddy: Well, Kaiowas... We, as they say in the business, have had our eye on you. For a very very long time. Do not think that your covert actions against Pultra went unnoticed.. Do not think that your aspirations of power from the Slayer and the title of Lord of the Ktulu have only been known by those you have told. We seek out those with the power, with the links to the supernatural and those that are soon to inherit great powers. We know that you hate Pultra.... We even gave you a little taste of the difficulty of your upcoming battle to test you out. First mentally, then physically... And as you say... you excelled... We know already that you are one of the strongest mentally and physically in this world and that your resolute attitude is renowned in the world of wrestling. Your title winning streak has left you with country wide recognition. How did you think that no-one would care about your active black art life...? Kaiowas:(again angry) What are you trying to say Duddy? Agent Duddy: Kaiowas.... The Dracon's have a problem.... Right now we are being threatened big time. We know that you do not agree our sort of organisation and the evil world of influence.... But we know you admire power.... and you yourself aspire to be the most powerful. Kaiowas: Get to the point you fool.... What is your problem? [Duddy is finally taken aback by Kaiowas' aggression but the look of shock is finally wiped off his face...] Agent Duddy: Satan.... bluntly and basically it is Satan... It is not that we fear the afterlife or that we fear that we sin too much. No... Satan is our main worry. He learnt of our alliance recently. His spies had infiltrated our ranks and had brought him news that we were exploiting this world more than he could allow. He feels we are pushing in on his territory... He sent a wave of attacks to try and halt us, but up until now we have been strong enough to send them packing. Unfortunately he plans to send his own son soon. The bastard is pulling out all the stops so that he can alone reign over evil... Kaiowas: His son? Damien? Agent Duddy: No... This is where you come in... His son is Donny Diamond... [Kaiowas had heard the rumours but he did not fully understand that his enemy Donny Diamond was the son of Satan. Kaiowas' mind wandered to the weeks ahead in which he would face Donny twice on the same night... He saw where this man was going with his speech...] Kaiowas: Donny Diamond... the evil bastard of the DWO deserves respect.... Agent Duddy: He may deserve respect but he will not get any from a Dracon... Especially if he destroys us.... As I said, this is where you come in... You Kaiowas, are about to slay Pultra in the biggest battle you have ever fought... You will take the powers of the Slayer as reward for your act of justice.... These powers are immense, extremely flexible and powerful. Donny has taken so many powers since his ascension as son of Satan. He is the beast sent by the devil, 666 and all that stuff. He will bring an end to the Dracons... Something none of us wish... Kaiowas: You want me to take Donny out?! I would gladly take Donny out for personal reasons.... But why the hell should I do it for a bunch of "supernaturally gifted" bastards like yourself? Why should I save the Dracons...? [Duddy pauses then strikes with his trump card...] Duddy: Power... simply put... power. As i said, we have dominance on this plane.... and the next... Even Rolan... the lord of Heaven knows the name of the Dracons.... Your reward would be close to limitless..... The powers of the Slayer are measurable, enough to kill off gods, at least weaker ones, but the power that we can offer you.... Has so much more worth... We can offer you... the powers of a leech..... Kaiowas: The power to leech life from anything? The power to drain strength and even intelligence? The power to take anything of physical or mental worth from my fallen opponents? [Kaiowas' eyebrows furrow with the look ofa pleased man, an evil smile erupts on his face as he stands up...] Kaiowas: If you offer me the power of the leech.... and allow me to remove Donny Diamond from my troubles, there is NO WAY that I can resist your offer....But now... Pultra shall feel the inevitable end, he shall fall and then I shall turn my thoughts to Satan's own love child... the spawn of Evil... I shall wipe him from this earth as I will reap my retribution on the Lord of Vengeance! The two shall fall, one by one... And I... I shall have my ascension to greatness! [With that last promise, the room darkens and Kaiowas soon awakes in the outskirts of his village, his staff laid beside him. He stands and strides away from the village, where he knew he would find the first of his two targets.... Pultra.] _________________ DWO World Champion and Most Improved 2002, PTC no1 january 8th and 14th and no7 too! [detail withheld]: he says shes ugly!
