A Brother's Voice

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 “It’s hard to understand the true pain after the loss of a loved one.” – Anonymous. Nobody in the world knew how true those words were, more than Ichiro Nakazura. His business suit jacket protected him from the cold of the breezy night. He walked quickly, keeping his head down most of the time as he walked. He was currently fighting two battles. He was in the second round of PTC’s Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. He knew that he had struggled in the first round, after being away and in rehab for four months that had been expected. He had continued his strong training; he struggled to become stronger in the next rounds. He knew he had more potential than what he had shown, he would do better in this round. However, right now his mind was not on the GTT. It was not on the upcoming match, nor was it on his opponent, “Sinister” Steven Lee. He was more focused on another battle. One that was much more complicated. One that was not happening in the outside world, instead one that was occurring inside his mind. He lifted his gaze from the ground. The cold midnight, September wind whistled through his long black hair; perfectly straightened to its full length, sending shivers throughout his entire body. He looked at the moon above, at three quarters full its light shone through the cracks in the tree branches overhead brightly. He walked through the small brush and forested area quickly. As he began to near his destination he began to slow his pace. He looked relieved. It was hard to tell why he looked this way, there was a slight look of dismay on his face. He was upset, but he also seemed to be struggling with something. Soon after however, he reached his destination. He came to a clearing in the woods, and the bright light of the moon shone down in full force. Its light seemed to revolve around a small tomb on the ground. The tomb had a small inscription at its base. Ichiro leaned down, and reached his hand out. As his hand reached out, his jacket retracted slightly, exposing a small part of his wrist. He looked down and saw the scratches and cuts, clearly matters of self-harm. It was apart of the battle he fought within, the fight for control. The fight for his own self. The fight for what seemed to be, his sanity. He shook his head, and brushed the few hairs that the wind had blown into his face away. He smiled softly as he brushed his hand across the top of the inscription palette. He closed his eyes and looked up, as he brushed the leaves and dirt away from the engraved marble stone. Although the smile never completely left his face, the change in his mood was more than visible. It started quite small and insignificantly. A quiver of the lip, a small tremble of his eyelids. Soon, his eyes began to dampen, he did not rub his eyes, and he merely let his head stay up. His smile began to fade slightly, a mere smirk now. His breathing became slightly louder, all of these small insignificant details, eventually culminating in a large event. A tear formed in the socket of Ichiro’s eye and rolled down his cheek. He did not wipe it away, he merely allowed the drop to roll all the way down his cheek and fall to the ground. How long had it last been since he had cried? He wondered to himself? It had been many months since he had last visited this gravesite. It was not pleasant, and he did not enjoy reliving the painful memories of the past. He did not like being reminded of the pain he went through when his younger brother was robbed from him. But, now was a different occasion. With so many things going on inside his mind, Ichiro needed these tears. He needed to know that he was still alive, he needed to know that he could still feel. But most of all, he needed to know that he was still in control. He brushed his fingers over the tombstone continuously. No longer was he brushing the dirt aside, no, now he was feeling. He was feeling the engravings on the marble stone. He leaned his head back and remembered all that he could of his brother who had been stolen from him. Too soon had his brother Jushin left this earth, and in these troubled times, Ichiro needed his help. Zenzen Miwasuremasen, Eiei Touitsuteki Ichiro’s fingers rolled across the tombstones words over and over again. Seemingly attached to the words inscribed there, his fingers began to become apart of the sentimental words. Ichiro’s smile grew slightly as another tear rolled off of his cheek and fell to the ground below. He leaned his head back and began to speak out loud. He knew there was no one around to hear him, but he knew that somewhere, somewhere in the afterlife. His brother was listening to him, and watching over him. Protecting him in times of need, and caring for him during times when it seemed no one else would. “I know you can hear me,” his words were soft and compassionate. “I need your help now. I am losing grip. I now have a war raging inside me, a battle for the control of my soul. I have control now, but I do not know for how long. Nor do I know how long I will last in competition with this demon.” Ichiro kept his eyes shut, but he now leaned his head forward. He rested his head on the wall of the tomb. He began to sob now. He truly was losing his grip. This demon that had been haunting him was not giving up, Ichiro felt like every time he woke up he was weaker and weaker. And with that, the demon must be getting stronger and stronger. The blackouts came on much more suddenly now, it seemed like he could lose control at any time. The memories in between were further in between, and he felt more and more fatigued the longer he maintained control. Suddenly, it was if an angelic voice began to speak to him. It was a voice he knew all too familiarly. But, that’s impossible he thought to himself. My brother is dead, I must be hallucinating. There is no way that he can be speaking to me. “My brother, do not fear. Do not doubt. Listen with an open mind. For if you do not I cannot help you,” came the voices soft words. Ichiro got up from his resting position against the wall of the tomb. He looked around in the woods, but he did not see a soul. All around him was brush and trees, but through all the shadows he saw none of a human silhouette. “Brother, why are you frightened so? Do you not recognize the voice of your brother? Have I truly been gone for so long that you have forgotten me?” came the voice again, this time slightly louder. Ichiro upon further review of the scene realized that there was not a soul within ten miles of this event. This must be the truth. His beliefs were true, even from the after life his brother was still watching over him. And now, his brother was speaking with him. “Are you listening now my brother?” came the voice. “Yes Jushin I am listening.” “Good, Ichiro. You are a strong warrior my brother, why do you come in such an emotional mess to me now. When I was alive, you were a man who never let his emotions get the best of him. It was the one thing in the world, you more than anyone swore off.” “I know my brother, I am weak. I am not performing to my full potential. I fear that I cannot and will not succeed in the PTC GTT4 Tournament if I don’t pull myself together.” “I can see that Ichiro. I can see it in your eyes and I can see it in your heart.” Ichiro buried his face into his hands, he began to sob again softly. His eyes moistened and the tears began to well up in his eyes. His entire body seemed to shake with each and every tear that rolled down his face. His weeping seemed to echo throughout the small clearing in the cold silence of the night. “It is not only that my brother,” Ichiro began to speak, still with tears in his eyes. His speech was a bit stuttered due to his crying. “I am losing control of myself.” “What is it that you mean by that?” “I can no longer control the demon living inside of me. After your death, I faced The Consiliu on my own, and I was severely beaten and spent over a month in the hospital, before retreating for my own training and rehabilitation. However, during this time, a demon grew inside of me. A demon of hate, of anger, of frustration.” “My brother? What kind of demon is it that plagues you? Surely, you do not believe in demons and ghosts. Ichiro, you are strong, I am certain that no matter what it is that is haunting you, whatever the problem, you can defeat it. And no matter who you face in the GTT you can defeat them too. My brother, you are a great warrior. Strong, courageous, noble, quick-minded. You have the ability to defeat anyone who stands in your way.” Ichiro could no longer hold back the war that raged inside of him. His head reared back, as if he was about to unleash a horrific scream. His facial muscles tensed up and his eyes began to flutter and flicker in their sockets. His body began to shake and convulse, nonchalantly at first, but becoming more and more violent every second. “No!! I can’t hold him back any longer Jushin. He is taking over!” “Brother, what are you talking about? Have you gone mad?!” the voice rang out in Ichiro’s ears, but there wasn’t anything he could do anymore. He was losing control. His legs were becoming weak and wobbly. His body began to go numb, his vision became blurry and blackened. “Good bye my brother. Know only this if nothing else, I miss you.” “Good bye Ichiro, fare you well my brother,” came the voices reply. It was the last thing Ichiro could remember. His legs finally gave out, his eyes closed and his body became entirely limp and numb. He collapsed to the ground shaking slightly. He had lost control, he had lost the battle. But, would he lose his battle in GTT4? No one truly knows the answer to that question, but we will soon find out.
