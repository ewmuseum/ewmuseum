What it's all about...

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 Demons? Possssion? Poor Irish Publicans who turn around, throw away their faith and sign on the dotted line to sell their soul to some big muscle bound former priest? That is one hand. The other is some pretty boy from Clearwater, Florida. One who’s been very quiet, and well, who knows who the hell he is? Who really cares? Poltergeist and someone using an overused name since the days of Motley Crue. This wasn’t going to be easy. But it sure as hell wasn’t going to be hard. But let’s forget about all of that. We’ll get to Blaise’s thoughts on these obstacles in his way in just a little bit. And that’s all they are to him... Obstacles in his way. But now, now we take you to scene that over the last few years, anyone who has followed the GIWF, UWF, PWW or 4WW knows would be the last thing to ever happen. It’s a sign of the coming apocalypse. Something that could make Poltergeist that much happier? Right? Yeah, onto the story here. For we now find ourselves in a small area on the outskirts of the Dallas-Ft Worth area. A small place that is known as the ‘Compound’ to those who know of the Order of the Hammer. It’s also known to some as the 4 Winds Academy. Yes, the same school that 4 Winds Wrestilng takes it’s name from. It’s owned by the same people. The same production company bankrolls it and has it’s name on the title, and the same organization runs it. This wouldn’t be anything new, except that currently driving up into it’s circular driveway is a large black limosine. Not just any limosine, but one with a distinctive licence plate. An Arizona license plate that reads as ‘PWW2’. The personal limosine of Samantha Gordon, the top person other then Jaymes Phoenix in the rebirth of PWW. The limo pulls up and the driver comes out and does his job as chauffer. He opens the door to allow the woman in the power suit to step out. She adjusts the bottom of her vest and then looks back as another woman climbs out of the limo also. A redhaired woman, not much more then a girl really. Her red hair is tied back, and she’s wearing a pair of tight jeans and a babydoll type t-shirt. Her belly still looks like it needs a little bit of work fromn what I was before. Then again, not but a few short months ago, this woman, Rebecca Fortune, gave birth to the twin daughters of Blaise McClancy. The twins are no where to be seen, as this is obviously a business only trip. The two women stand there in front of the building. It’s large stone walls. They stand in the shadow of the statue that shows a man with large feathery wings in a very sensual embrace with a wingless woman. Gordon: Never thought I’d be seeing this place. Now, will you tell me why we’re here? Becca: It’s easy. Blaise is a graduate of the Childress’ South Wind Academy in Okinawa. He’s now going here, learning the rest of their teachings. This is the final step in the traning path of the 4 Winds. Gordon: Why would he join PWW then? Becca: Because his cousins own the 4WW. No matter how successful he’s been, he’s not one to have someone play favorites. Or to think they’re playing favorites. Gordon: And why am I here again? Becca: Jaymes said he wanted you to check up on Blaise, and make sure he’s ready for this PTC Cruiserweight Tournement. Gordon: I can’t belive I’m doing this, while Jay is off playing referee for a match booked on that other fed. Becca: Just let Jay deal with his business, and Blaise deal with his. Gordon: Guess you’re right. The two women start to walk into the building, climbing the steps that lead from the driveway, up to the entrance foyer and the great hall beyond that. But they’ll be taking a slight detour and heading down to the basement training areas. They’re greeted by a man dressed in a monk’s robes at the top. The Acolyte of the Order nods and then motions for them to follow him. Gordon: They don’t speak much do they? Becca: They never do. He takes them into the foyer and then to a small door on the side. He produces a key that he then uses to allow the electronic door to open and then the elevator to be revield. The three climb in and another use of the key allows it to drop down to the training area. The second set of doors open when the elevator completes its decent, and both Ms. Fortune and Ms. Gordon walk out. They’re now in a gym. Something that looks not unlike what a Gold’s Gym in a basement would look like. Young men all around, working on their required studies here, training to be wrestlers, fighers, boxers, bascially warriors for the Order. Or at least those who follow its ways, and want to become that. Then there are some who don’t follow their philosophy. There are some that are not here to be part of the army for the Order. Some that are just here to get the best training that there is to offer. The two ladies, well one lady and Sam Gordon, both walk up to the leg press machine. They stand there as a man works his legs. He’s wearing a pair of tight shorts. His red hair is tied back into a ponytail. His slightly brown skin shows not a hint of being made froma tanning salon. The only thing off from the looks of a normal man doing leg presses, is the large cage like knee brace he wears on his right leg. Becca: Don’t hurt yourself there. Gordon: Please don’t. The man stops the lift at full extension. He pulls the bar that locks the weights in place and then comes up to a seated position. The man has the facial features of a McClancy. The hair of a McClancy, but he’s smaller, much smaller and his mother’s features show through also. He looks up with his emerald green eyes, and the Amerindian features, a strange mix. This man is Blaise McClancy. The cousin of the one that currenlty is running havoc in PTC in the Extreme Title Division and the one that runs both 4WW and FAW right now with her money. Blaise McClancy. “Hazard” To gamble or play a game of chance. An obstacle or trap. A challenge. All of these terms describe this man. Where his cousin is a man who is the epitome of hardcore and extreme fighting, this is the ultimate technical and fighting machine. A 3rd Dan in Ish Shin Ryu Karate, and a healthy smattering of Tae Kwon Do also. A high ranking sash in Hung Gar Wu Shu. The student of one G. Ian Childress and his first wife, Xian. Blaise: I’ve got to make sure it’s ready for this. It didn’t do me well in that UTT tourney those many months ago. It’s been a year since Sean broke it, it’s not painful anymore, so I’ve got to keep it’s strength up. Blaise comes to his feet and extends his hand to his new boss. Blaise: Ms. Gordon. I take is Jay is busy in Los Angeles this week? Sam Gordon extends her hand and takes his. Gordon: Mr. McClancy. So how is one of the top prospects of PWW doing right now? Blaise: Doing just fine. Knees doing well. Getting ready to fight in this tournement also. Although I’ve seen that PWW has kinda had a flood of members jump in. I don’t know how I’m going to fare along with the likes of Angel and Jay in there. Becca: Jay is representing Ian’s fed. He’s still under contract. Blaise: What? I thought he was doing all this other stuff for that contract? Gordon: Yeah, but mostly to ensure that the Childresses move the 4WW to a new location. We’re going to be out of Arizona. 4WW is moving. But first, Jay has some things to do in that deal. Blaise: Damn. Between me, Angel and him, we could probably guarantee a good shot at the finals there. Gordon smiles at that. She’s been around ego for some time. But that didn’t roll of his tongue with ego, but with confidence… self worth. Gordon: Well, I’m here to check up on you. You did have a big injury that forced you out for a little while. You’ve been doing the indy’s for a few months. You up to this? I don’t need you getting hurt if you aren’t. Blaise smirks in her direction. Blaise: Well, I’m sorry. But after what I’ve seen in this first round booking, I’m going to have to get in there. I mean, look at this. You know I have a history with people named Syxx as it is. Adam Syxx is not Cade “Syxx Figures” Candler. He’s nothing as far as I’m concerned. He’s not doing anything to impress me so far. Then again, he’s done nothing that I’ve been able to find. Gordon: Well some are torn in the magazines, radio and TV about you and Mr. Peitro Geist. They don’t know who to go for. What’s your take on him? Blaise: You sound like an interviewer. Gordon: Consider this an interview. I need to know that you’re ready for this. What’s your take on him? Blaise: He’s a goth? Right? Gordon: Well from what I’ve heard, he’s into taking souls and stuff like that. Supposedly a former priest who’s gone a bit on the way side on his faith. Talks about selling your soul and such as that. Blaise: I know. I saw his hype stuff they published. Wonder if he’s more interested with winning this match or maybe buying a new soul? Gordon: Huh? Blaise: I’m a die hard Agnostic. I don’t believe in that crap. But if it would get me to that belt, he can have it. Not like I’m using it anyway. Not that I belive in that crap. Personally I’d like to know when he’s going to worry about wrestling me? I’m not out here to debate religion or the existance of demons and stuff to him. I mean, he went to an Irish Pub. Does he think just because of my last night, I’m a drunkard like my cousin is? Becca chuckles under her breath. Gordon: Well, sounds like you’re ready for this. You’d better be. Blaise: I’m ready. I’m all about what goes on in the ring. I’m not about flaunting any crap for the others to see. I don’t’ want to hear about soul stealing. I don’t want to hear about anything except for how you do in the ring. I mean, doen’st anyone wrestle anymore? That’s the name of our sport, right? I’m going to go out there and represent you, Jay and that building in Flagstaff, Arizona. I’m not going out there to play a part other then that of Blaise “Hazard” McClancy. They don’t like it, they can kiss my red ass. Gordon chuckles a bit. Blaise: What? Gordon: You do know that’s your cousin’s own catch phrase. Blaise gets a look on his face with that. Gordon: Sorry. He doesn’t say red though. There are similarities though. Blaise: I’m nothing like my cousin. Nothing like him at all. Other then one thing. Both our fathers were a tag team and wrestlers. They both had the same values. We’re not about wrestle crap. We’re about wrestling. That you can take to the bank. Other then that, you just forward the word to who ever the hell is running this thing that someone is going to take the Risk of their life in that first round so far. I know it, they know it, and well, I’m going to guarantee it. Gordon: Good. I’ll make sure they know. In the meantime, you and Ms. Fortune here are haivng dinner with me, on the PWW. We’ve got to make sure that our stars are taken care of. We also have the initial merchandising stuff to still discuss. So get ready. Dress for success. Blaise: Yeah. He takes his towell that had been set aside and quickly runs it through his shoulder length hair, down the ponytail, and then tosses it into one of the recepticals for them. Becca and Ms. Gordon start to head out of the facility while Blaise heads for the locker room. ~Fini~ for now…
