IG 33 - Trash Talk Here!

4 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We find Phil Atken and Dick Dirkwood, Superagent sitting on a coach on a sitcom set somewhere as neither of the men actually own a house, a couch nor the television they are currently watching. Phil: Hey Dick, the quality of Letterman's show has really been in decline these days. They seem to let anyone on it these days, think I have a shot? Dick: Not until you stop insisting to wave your dick around in public. Phil: I was talking about you. Dick: No, no you were not. That's why the police were called in the first place. Phil: It's not my fault it's so huge, it deserves to be on display. Dick: I believe it was the phrase "My swift dick of justice will destroy all the true non-believers" that made the audience terrified in the first place. Phil: Why would I care if the cretins were terrified. Dick: Well first, it was Blue Clues... Phil: Point taken. Now shut up, aren't we meant to be insulting people other that ourselves? Dick: Why do that when insulting you is much easier. Phil: Well for starters my swift dick of justice will smash you to pieces like all my competition. Dick: You know this is why you got fired from the last place right? Phil: I thought that was the roofies. Dick: It was a combination of things, storming in to the bosses office and demanding that "The Penis Spike" becomes your new finisher was kinda the final problem though. Phil: Look, I just realised that the new tights made it look HUGE so I thought I was running on good ideas. Dick: You also think discussing backstage drama on-air is a good idea. Heck you thought dedicating several weeks to contract negotiations and meeting people only you knew and cared about was a good idea. Phil: It was a good idea, it has emotion, depth, emotion, armbar, emotion... Dick: I wasn't you're agent at the time but I watched that shit and it made me open my arms and embrace the sweet taste of death. Although I never did kill myself, I just stabbed myself in the eye with a rusty spork. Phil: I thought sporks were plastic. Dick: That one wasn't. Phil: And here I thought you were just going for the whole pirate motif. Dick: Hey, how can this Tchu guy swear on the David Letterman show? Phil: We're watching the live feed. Dick: How the hell are we managing that? Phil: Magic! Dick: That reminds me, why are we on a sitcom set with the cameras rolling? Phil: Well since I got fired I thought I'd try and shoot a pilot, it's called Dick and Dick and it's about you and my penis having wacky misadventures. Dick: Do any networks plan to air it? Phil: God no. Dick: So really you just wanted to bring up your penis again. Phil: Yes. Dick: I hate you. I was doing a bit of research recently and I noticed that you haven't actually done anything of note at all in the wrestling industry since 2004. Phil: When did I hire you again? Dick: ...2005. Phil: Well that certainly explains it. You're fired. Dick: I already quit when you got fired. Phil: Then why are you here? Dick: You seemed lonely. Phil: I am, why else would we be sitting here watching a wrestler on David Letterman's live feed. If I was employed and not incredibly lonely I doubt I'd do this shit. I doubt anyone would. I doubt anyone will. Dick: Why are we now re-watching the Top Ten. Phil: There's a joke in this pile somewhere and I'm not going to stop until I find it. Dick: The joke is James von Doom! Phil: Who? Dick: Buggered if I know, I just assumed insulting James von Doom was the pinacle of comedy otherwise everyone in the known universe wouldn't use that joke over and over again. Phil: Oh, James VON DOOM, why didn't you say. Yeah, I know, that guy totally, ha ha, he, ha ha, totally is something awful, ho ho. Dick: Yeah, that's not working for me. Phil: Me neither. Dick: The producer said the audience was laughing at it. Phil: What audience? Dick: The ones who will probably go home and kill themselves after this taping. Phil: I'd advise them too, in fact I'd advise everyone to go home and kill themselves as they'll never achieve the level of greatness that I have. Dick: Wow, I had no idea greatness was no based on being unemployed and unemployable. Phil: You'd be shocked at the things you learn in the Hobo Circle of Trust. Dick: You're not homeless. Phil: No, but if I sit next to them I get free money and then I can beat them off because I'm in better physical condition. Dick: I'm just stunned you didn't mention your penis in that. Phil: Oh did I forget to mention I beat them of with my Cock of Justice. Dick: Heh heh, you beat off cock. Suddenly there is hooting and hollering in the background out of nowhere. Phil: Oh awesome, we finally found the level of discourse that our competition is taking. I thought that would have taken all night. Now what do we do Dick? Dick: Well at this level, all I've got written next to everyone's names is either "You suck", "You really suck", "You suck a box of cocks" and "You suck your mothers box of cocks". Phil: So we're running on empty? Dick: Well next to Mr. Silver is says "You are a kicking rad gentleman". Phil: Oh yeah, I added that one in, I'm looking for a job I need to suck up to fellows like the Chairman of PRIME. Plus people that crazy tend to just give me title belts without having to wrestle for them. Dick: But you're in competition against him. Phil: Exactly, it's the perfect time to suck up! Dick: But he doesn't actually have that job... Phil: Non-sense, or if you will allow, tish and pish. So he's super rad awesome and the rest suck cocks is that right? Dick: You know this whole conversation has an awful lot of Dick in it. Phil: MUCH LIKE THE COMPETITION. AHAHAHAHAHAHAHA. THAT IS SUCH A BURN.
