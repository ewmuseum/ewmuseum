Just Another Night: Stalking a New Prey.

Date: 2006-01-08 "I’m really sorry to have to come out here so soon," Pride removed his jacket and folded it neatly over the back of his chair. It had been raining all afternoon, placing an even somber feel to the closed casket rosary that he had attended for Miguel Gutierrez; the skinless John Doe that had washed up on shore a few weeks ago. "But seeing as how you knew Miguel the best, I figured you would be able to answer a few questions I had." Sapphire held a tissue to his eyes, wiping away a few tears that had nearly messed up his mascara. One would be hard pressed to recognize him to be a man, he hid it well and had almost no manly features whatsoever. He was more feminine than most women, which was one of the traits that gave away his masculinity. "It’s okay, detective," Sapphire began, "I want to find out who did this to Lacey as much as you do. Would you care for something to drink?" "No thank you," Pride responded. "I have a few other leads in Lacey’s case to pursue when I’m through here." Lacey was the name Miguel went by for the last few years of his life, the life that was shunned by most of his family. They had all but written him off, until his death that is. Pride found it peculiar that the majority of Miguel’s family acted as if none of it had ever happened, never mentioning the name Lacey or his chosen lifestyle. All of the comments made by family members were of times long past, their fondest memories of a young boy that loved to ride his bicycle and play sports. It wasn’t until Sapphire had walked up to the podium that the whispers began, rude and loathsome comments had spread out through the masses as if Sapphire had been responsible for Miguel’s death. It was an awkward feeling to be sure, but Sapphire had held his ground and spoke of a man that most of the people in attendance did not know; nor did they care to know. "I noticed Lacey’s father was upset that you were there." Sapphire smiled as he realized what Detective Pride was talking about; it was the one of the most awkward moments of the night. Adolfo Gutierrez, Miguel’s father, had become enraged by the fact that Sapphire had talked about his son as if he were a woman. It was disgraceful in his eyes, and he did nothing to hide the fact that he was angry. Sapphire had soiled the memory of his son. "Upset," Sapphire scoffed, "the bastard wanted to kick my ass in the middle of the funeral home. He blamed it all on me, and people like me. He always thought this was a phase Lacey was going to grow out of, even though the way he remembered her was the phase she had grown out of." "Do you think he could have had anything to do with what happened to Lacey?" The question seemed absurd, considering it was his only son, but it had to be asked. "I don’t think he could have ever done something like that," dabbing the tissue against his eyes as he spoke. "Not the things you described." "Sometimes, in cases like these, we find that something personal fueled the murder; especially one as serious as this one." Sapphire could hardly believe what Pride was saying. "While I doubt his father was the one who did the heinous acts, it would not be hard to consider him a suspect. He was angry with his son, disgraced by the way he had tainted his family legacy." "Her family wasn’t like that," Sapphire interrupted. "Sure they were upset, but they wouldn’t do anything this drastic. They always felt that Lacey would just give it all up." "I wish I could believe that," Pride said with a grimace, "but there is just so much hatred in the way that Lacey was tortured and murdered to just consider it a random act." "I understand." "I have one other question before I go." "And what’s that, detective?" "Have any of the regulars of your scene been peculiarly absent lately…since Lacey’s death?" The question took sapphire a bit off guard, he had not thought about anyone that lived their lifestyle capable of something like that. "Not that I’ve noticed." Sapphire answered finally. "Look, I’ll be in touch with you." Pride rose from his seat, grabbing his coat from the back of the chair. "But if you think of anything that could help me before then, you have my number." "I’ll call you if I think of anything." It was the same woman he had seen outside of the rosary of that spic queer, the one he had enjoyed watching bleed to death as he ran his blade through the queer’s golden bronze skin. The thought brought a smile to his face, and a warm feeling in his heart. Cleansing the wretched was a task he enjoyed as much as life; it was what he was born to do. The sensation of watching the life fade from someone’s eyes was something Braun loved, he found a sick pleasure in the sight of death escaping with each strained breath. He couldn’t help but wonder why Detective Pride had followed the woman home. Did she have information on the fag, was she a relative…it ate at him not to know. What if she knew something, something that could keep this case opened. He couldn’t afford that, and he knew that something like that would eventually hurt Jayce’s career. He couldn’t let that happen. It was his meal ticket. Free travel to different cities and different victims, it was a virtual oasis of pain and suffering. Something he needed to protect if they were going to fulfill the Lord’s bidding. The door of the apartment building opened, and Braun knew that he had a decision to make. Should he continue to follow Pride, finding an opportune moment to question him in his own special way, or should he stay behind and find out just who the woman is and what she knows. It was not an easy decision, considering that the woman may not know a damn thing about the case, but he knew it was not worth taking that chance. The woman stood at the window, staring down at the detective as he entered his vehicle. Braun watched as Pride drove off, the taillights reflecting off the rain drenched city street before disappearing from view. Then he turned his attention to the woman standing by her window, and that same sick and twisted smile creased his lips. Oh how he would enjoy listening to her squeal in pain, much like the pigs on the farm he was raised on. All he had to do was wait until she was asleep, then he would find out what she knew…or at least make sure she would not tell anyone anything. However, despite the impending nature of the actualization of his true potential, it must be remembered that while he may only be a hair's breath from such, he has not attained it thus far and must continue to strive to do so until he actually captures that which would bring him his ultimate satisfaction. He could not allow himself to slip the great slope toward complacency and, hence, further from his goals. The experienced warrior, wise beyond his years, knows that no matter how close to destiny one comes, it is not until it is within your grasp that you may consider yourself able to step back for a moment and bask within your own glory, for the perpetual battle for fulfillment shall never be entirely completed. Once that satisfaction has been achieved, it is only human nature to seek satisfaction elsewhere and begin the struggle once more, though each task must be focused upon, each path trodden to the end. Jayce Bradley’s flesh pulsated angrily as he sat upright, bruised and aching, a purple hue having dominated the natural pigmentation of his skin to reveal the extent of the wounds sustained at the hands of his last opponent, Joe Miller. Slowly and gently, he lightly traced his fingertips over the odd shaped bruises left behind by the unforgiving knees he had taken to his rib cage. Jayce winced, as the slightest touch provided irritation, he pressed a little harder, attempting to massage his ribcage to alleviate some portion of the discomfort, and surprisingly he found that by maintaining deep, long breaths and control of his psychological responses to the sensation, he is able to exert substantially more pressure upon them than he expected. Smirking slightly, he reached down toward the bag adjacent to his ankles upon the floor and grasped a small black hand towel, using it to wipe the beads of sweat which decorated his naked upper body, reddened slightly due to the exercising completed a matter of moments ago. Jayce craned his neck from side to side as he stood, throwing the towel back down at his bag, before he straightened his black shorts and stretched his legs. He then proceeded to extend his arms to his sides and yawned, a little tired by another day of working out, and although he sometimes found such activities troublesome and mundane, he is also very much aware of how much it has done for him in terms of furthering his career in the sport. Turning away to look at the others working out, his back facing us, we are afforded a view of the bruises and welts which mark his Arian white flesh, the results of a sustained and brutal beating at the hands of a lesser man; one who did not further his standing in the tournament. That, however, was the past. Evan ‘Damage’ Jackson was his future. The man that stood between him and the fourth round of the tournament, somewhere he did not even dream of reaching when he decided to enter the tournament. At first, he only wished to make it through the first round without falling flat on his face. But things change. Well, at least for some people. It seems as if Jackson is still feeling sorry for himself, having yet another pity party in which he talks about no one knowing him. Comparing himself to some little tease that has a bitch fit when she doesn’t get the respect she thinks she deserves. That’s what happens when you have no real ambition, other than to whore yourself off for a little bit of attention. Katie chose to hide her true self from others during the day, but it was those same people that saw her as a completely different person at night; so who was she kidding. Katie was nothing more than an attention whore, screaming at the top of her lungs for people to take notice. She did whatever it took to grab the attention of anyone, even if it meant acting like a slut in front of her peers. Katie despised Jenna, not because of her popularity, but because she did not have to try that hard to attain it. Things like that came natural, much like her beauty. Something that Katie could only dream to accomplish, because whoring yourself out only attracts the attention of one man at a time. Katie had surprised them all and made Jenna pay for slapping her like the bitch she was, but by the end of the day there would be another Jenna. Someone else would stand tall in Jenna’s shoes and demand the attention of her peers, but it would not be Katie. No, Katie would still end up as the little whore that sleeps her way into your heart, only to be tossed to the curb when someone better comes along. It’s Evan’s turn to be tossed to the curb, as someone better has come along; ready to fill Jenna’s shoes, so to speak.
