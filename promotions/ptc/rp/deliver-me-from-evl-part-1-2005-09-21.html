Deliver me from EVL (part 1)

21 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-09-21 My name is Eva VanLorne. I am a wrestler in a federation called Global Championship Wrestling. It is not easy for women in wrestling as it is mainly a man's domain. It has been especially difficult for me. You see, when I joined GCW there were no other female competitors. Sure, there were female valets hanging about but that simply isn't the same. I came onto the scene with my twin brother Erik as a tag team called EVL. We were good. We were very good. In fact, we won the tag team title within a month of being there at Warpath and held onto it for ninety two days. During those months I had a few successes and even a few downfalls. I met and began dating Lestatt Knight. It was an unusual relationship to say the least, but this isn't about that. It is about everything else. A few weeks before Annihilation I was attacked brutally and hospitalized due to some shady dealings of my brother and I from the past. It was the beginning of my descent into a spiraling downward. After the attack I began having problems with Lestatt. Erik left. I lost the tag team belts. I gained two other belts only to lose them both days later. I found myself constantly surrounded by men. Their voices ringing in my ears day and night until it became nothing but a shrill whistling bent on bringing me to my knees. I was losing my touch. I was losing my focus. Nothing was going right. The mental and emotional stress became a burden constantly tearing me apart inside. I was beginning to think I would never pull out of it. I felt I would never recover from my fall from the top of the mountain into the bottom of the barrel. I was wrong. It is said that God works in mysterious ways. I used to not believe that crap, but today? Today I am a firm believer in the Almighty though some may just tell you I had a psychotic episode. Either way, it doesn't matter. It was a miracle for me. A few weeks ago I received a call from Steven Caldera, president of GCW, about a huge inter federation event. Normally I do not keep up with these things preferring to stay in my own corner of the wrestling world, but Mr. Caldera convinced me otherwise and reluctantly I agreed to an exhibition match against none other than Trey Tremendous. This was not to be an ordinary match. No, it was an asphalt, chains, wrenches, and forklift match. It was a hardcore match to the extreme. I have never competed in a hardcore match before much less one of this caliber. As a result it was decided I needed more training and conditioning for it. This is when my miracle would happen. This is when it all began and my world changed forever. My story begins here. It was a day like any other day. The sun had risen in the east and was due to set in the west. I had risen earlier than usual due to the new high impact training course my publicist and friend, Mist Demeanor, had demanded. Two weeks into this new hardcore training and still my body had been aching in places I never knew existed and I was loving every minute of it. A wise man in a movie had once said," Pain makes you know you are still alive." * For the life of me I can't remember the movie it came from. It is too bad, too, I would like to see it again. Rolling out of bed was easier that day, letting me know that my body was beginning to condition itself to the abuse it had been put through at my behest. A fresh shower…..fresh work out clothes…..hair pulled back into a simple ponytail….and I was ready to roll. With gym bag slung over my shoulder I headed out the door swiping up my car keys on my way out. It was time for some more pain, for more reminder that I am very much alive and that my world did not collapse along with my relationship to…him. The ride there would take about thirty minutes. It was one of the few things that continued to annoy, but it had been 'her' demand. Scowling at the early morning sun I pulled out of the parking garage; my shades found their way onto my nose. Damn it was a bright morning and oh lucky me!! I got to drive straight into the sun for most of the trip. At least I would have fun driving there. I always did in that sweet little ride. My baby, my favorite car and a gift from my brother, the Ashton Martin Vanquishe…it could turn corners like it was on rails. Five minutes and already I was pulling onto the busy highway weaving in out of traffic like a crazed woman. I even smiled as one car I cut off flipped the good old New York hello…you guessed it..the middle finger. It was a gesture I could not resist returning in kind with a saucy smile. Grand Theft Auto eat your heart out. It was about time for music. Grabbing the nifty little remote I flipped through the cds in the five cd disk changer and settled on some Stabbing Westward. I don't even know half the lyrics but that whole " I can not save you I can't even save myself"(*) kind of stuck in my head. Traffic was getting heavy, making it a bit more difficult to weave through it safely, not that safety was one of my main priorities. Soon it was getting impossible to get through, oddly enough it was one of those heavy traffic days, heavier than usual. It was time to take the service road for a while. A single glance into my rear view mirror would let me know I could veer a hard right onto the off ramp…too bad someone else had the same idea….. The sound of metal crunching against metal as my head hit the steering wheel was the last thing I remembered before waking up with a horrible hang over. I had no idea what happened at first. I could only remember that song going through my head and being on my way to the gym. At first I didn't even notice the smell of ocean air or heady sweat. In fact, all I could think about was the unrelenting pain in my skull. I thought I was dead until I heard that first voice. Everything kind of spiraled out of control after that….. ------------------------------------------- " Unh….." " Oh thanks be to the gods. She is alive!" Who the hell was that? I didn't recognize that voice. " My lady…can you hear me?" Another voice? My lady? Something was wrong and I was beginning to think it was my head. " Good God lads. Give the Captain room so she can breathe!" That thundering voice was enough to make my already throbbing head feel like someone had just inserted a hot poker in the back of it and swirled it around. " Christ! Would you please keep it down? You are giving me a fuggin' headache." My response was muffled muttering. I still can't believe how easily it worked. There was a struggle to open my eyes, the pain forcing me to pry them open only a second at a time for what seemed like forever. I finally managed to keep them open and work on focusing my blurred double vision. The feel of hands on me had me growling and slapping them away as I rolled to my side and continued the fight to focus. " Take yer time Cap'n. You took quite a blast from that cannon." Cannon? What the hell was he going on about? Last thing I remembered was veering toward the on ramp and then… Oh shit, I had an accident. My car!!! " God damn it! How bad is my car?" The awkward silence caused me to freeze as one of the three voices cleared his throat. " Car Cap'n?" "Yeah my car. You know the Ashton Martin?" Again silence. " We do not know this Ashton Martin you speak of Cap'n. Was he one of the newer crew members recently joined?" Crew members? What the hell was going on? Has everyone lost their god forsaken minds around here? Finally I was beginning to focus. My head was laying against the cold hard wooden surface of a floor. I found myself staring at two pairs of boots…one of them covered in metal. It wasn't just any metal. It was the kind that my statues of knights had on….you know, armored boots. Blinking slowly my head lifted off the surface as hand pressed against it to help push myself up. My eyes followed the rather peculiar boots up the length of two male bodies. Black britches…..swords…muskets….leather vest and pirate shirts? It was then that I was thinking I was dead. I had to be! And this…this was hell. " Great. I know I was bad but Jesus fucking christ..was I THAT bad?" The next hour is still kind of a blur to me. So much had happened and even now I am still unsure as to whether it was real or not. But if the pain I felt from the adventures I had there was any clue, it had been very real….and surreal. People say that it is often hard to believe the outrageous, but even in the outrageous..there was a grain of truth. I had been there…in that world, in that god forsaken place torn apart by war. I had smelled the ocean air, the smoke from gun powder and even the blood that hung like a cloak all around as I fought beside men who thought I was someone else all to save a world that was not my own. My apologies, I am getting off track. Where was I? Oh yes, an hour later…. " For the last time I AM NOT this Eve woman and I AM NOT your bloody captain!" The argument was getting me no where. Every time I made the statement my 'quartermaster' would merely shake his head at me with a sad sigh and mutter something about my brains being joggled about. Good lord that man was stubborn, likely more stubborn than I am. His name was Cedric, nicknamed the Marauder for his stealthy ways and ability to disguise himself in manners no one knew existed. From what I can remember, he was quite good at it. " Cap'n, you just need to rest. Get some sleep while we set course for Superia. The men need ya in top condition. You are their beacon of hope against that tyrant Jordius." Jordius. Jordius was called the Argent Noble, so called for his affinity to wearing white and the fact he never got his own hands dirty if he could have someone else do his dirty work. This was the man that the people fought. He was the one they wanted so desperately to overthrow so they could once again live in a world of peace. The problem was his right hand man. I will tell you about him when the time comes. " I don't need to sleep you half wit! I need to get back home." " Eve you are home lass." " My NAME is Eva." " Whatever you say Cap'n." He left after saying that, apparently he was tired of arguing with an addle brained woman. I took the opportunity to take a better look at my surroundings. The cabin was rather small for my taste, but somehow I think it was considered a luxury to those people. Still, as small as it was, the room did have its perks. Mahogany wood stained a deep cherry was used to make much of the furniture in there. The desk was decorated with elaborate carvings that still make me wish there had been a way for me to take it with me when I finally got back home. At the time I wondered if I would ever get back to where I belonged. I sat at that desk and shuffled through the maps and documents reading each piece avidly. By the time I was done, I had wished I hadn't. Jordius was a cruel and twisted man from the information I gathered. He took great pride in taking what did not belong to him crushing those he took it from beneath his boot heel. My research had named Cedric as one of those. Cedric, as it turned out was not a simple man. He had been a noble in this place I was in. A wealthy and respected man whose own greed blinded him to the devious nature of Jordius who had once been his friend. Greed, power, status….it kind of reminded me of the wrestling world. It reminded me of…home. By the time Cedric returned I had dozed off at the desk. My head was resting upon my arms having not found the strength to leave my seat. A gentle hand on my shoulder roused me. Jerking awake I nearly clobbered the man only to relax my fist and sigh wearily as my eyes fell back to the last thing that had caught my attention. Eve kept a journal, documenting her life. It was a careful log of everything she had done, both good and bad. It was the bad that had shocked me to the core. Much like myself, she had been a paid accomplice in the fall of Cedric to Jordius. It was a mirror image of what I had done to Johnny Lexicon not so long ago for Jordan White. The feelings it brought about were eerie…no..are eerie to say the least. " Cap'n. We are nearly upon our destination. Ye best get ready for battle. Treyial will be there." Treyial, otherwise known as the Terror. This was the right hand man I had mentioned earlier. He was Jordius's most deadly soldier. The man was psychotic, a wild card that Jordius did not try to contain, but rather unleashed whenever he saw fit,kind of like Jordan White does with Trey Tremendous. The battle that was coming up was one of those times. When I looked up at Cedric I wanted to berate him again. I wanted to tell him I was not this Eve, something stopped me. I told you…I wished I had never read those papers. I was saying something I never thought in a million years I would say before I even thought about the ramifications. " Get the men gathered. I will be out in a few minutes." Cedric had smiled. I felt the blood drain from my face. What the hell had I just done? " Glad to have ye back Cap'n." He left. I turned to call out to him, but the room was already empty. I had just agreed to do something I had no clue as to what I was doing in. Lovely thought eh? Yeah, I thought so too. So there I was standing there staring at the door when the first blast hit the ship. I had no idea what a cannon ball sounded like flying through the air. All I can remember is a shrill whistling before the thing came bursting through the far wall of my quarters. Wooden shrapnel was raining everywhere as I hit the deck. The sound had me reeling from it all. My ears were ringing, to this day I swear I can still hear that ringing in my ears at times. I don't know what angered me more. The fact that someone just tried to kill me or the fact that that beautiful desk had been shattered into saw dust and debris. I do remember not thinking. I just acted. As soon as I felt it was safe I jumped to my feet and rushed to the door..or what remained of it. I have to tell you…those cannons were powerful things. I was not prepared for the sight that would greet my disbelieving eyes. On the mid deck men were scrambling to their stations as other lay on the deck writhing in agony. The smell of fire and smoke had been heavy in the air. I was choking on it. " Cap'n!" " Yeah! I'm okay!" " Orders Cap'n we need orders!" Knowing that those men were entrusting their lives to my word was not the best feeling in the world. Like I said, I had no clue what I was doing. All motion seemed to stop on that ship as all eye fell to me. Stunned I stared back at them, mouth agape and mind whirling. What the hell was I supposed to do? The whistling was coming again. My world turned into a slow motion movie strip as I turned to stare like a deer caught in the headlights at the oncoming death ball. It wasn't until Cedric's body collided with my own that I snapped out of the stunned silence. " GET OFF OF ME YOU OAF!" Cedric rolled to his feet and pulled me up. " Cap'n we still need your orders." " What the hell do you think they are!? Blow those fuckers out of the water and straight into hell! NO ONE MESSES WITH EVA VANLORNE!" I think, had it not been for the immediate situation at hand, Cedric would have looked at me like I had grown another head. Thank God for the distraction. The men got the cannons ready quickly and before I knew it I was nearly deaf from the sound of them firing back. By now I was wishing I had been a better person when I was alive. To me this was hell, it was something I had wanted to escape in my real life and there I was….suffering the same thing in the afterlife. Karma is a bitch.' " The battle only lasted for around twenty minutes, but it seemed like forever to me. When it was over I was covered in dust, debris and blood. I wanted a bath badly. It taken the better part of an hour to assess the damage to the ship and how many men we had lost. The count had come to twenty two. Twenty two men were dead and it was because of me. The image of each of their faces still burns brightly in mind to this day. Three hours later we had them all sewn up into canvas and were depositing them into the waters below after service had been given asking their god to see them into heaven. Mentally exhausted and bone weary I went back to 'my' quarters and cleared the bed before flopping onto it. I didn't know what to think. I had just been in a gun fight between two ships in a world that I did not belong in. I needed the peace. I needed time to collect my thoughts and figure out what I was going to do. It was time I would not get. " Cap'n?" I slowly raised from the bed wincing at a lancing pain in my side. Looking down I saw a piece of a wooden plank protruding from my lower rib area. I have to tell you, it hurt like a son of a bitch. " What do you need now?" Cedric was staring at the wooden splinter in my side. " Nothing Cap'n. I came to see if ye needed anything. By the looks of it ye need the doc." That was the understatement of the year. " No shit Sherlock?" " Cedric ma'am." " I know your name is Cedric, it was an expression." He merely shrugged before he turned to hollar for the ship's surgeon. I flopped back on the bed and stared at the ceiling. The thing had to come out, but I wasn't so sure I wanted it too. It hurt only because I knew it was there. I knew it was going to hurt even worse when it was pulled out. While I was thinking about it, I had not noticed that my quartermaster had come over..at least not until he yanked it from my side. He got punched for that….hard. " GOD BLAST IT MAN! WARN A WOMAN!" " If I had warned ye ya would have tensed and it would have hurt worse." I wanted to punch him again just for good measure. Lucky for him, the doctor strolled in with a small canvas sack in hand. It didn't take long for him to have me patched up. The man was adept at his duties. I rather admired his handy work. He did not dally, as there were still other wounded to attend. Evidently the captain came first. Cedric lingered for a few minutes before I finally sent him away. I was tired and sleep was looking like a plan at the time. The next few days went by kind of fast. We stopped off in a couple of ports replenishing supplies and repairing the ship. I was relieved we had not met with anymore ill will. By now I was beginning to realize I wasn't in hell, nor was I dead. The mind is a strange and fickle thing. It often plays tricks on us. Mine was playing major tricks on me. I was beginning to think I was in some strange parallel world and that these people were the same people I knew at home. I wanted to avoid further confrontation, but much like my wrestling career of late, it was not my choice. As soon as the ship was fully repaired we were back on the high seas heading for Superia. It seems I had a date with destiny. Too bad I had no clue what that destiny would entail for me when it unfolded. We reached the shores of Superia in a couple of days time. The crew was a good band of men and I admit I was rather honored to be among them in retrospect. Our destination was the capital city of Cambridge, where we would launch our third attack on Jordius and his minions in hopes of defeating him. I had discovered the first two times had not gone as planned. The first one had been part of Eve's own doing. During our march to the capital I was enlightened as to Eve's involvement with some of the people we would be facing. Particularly Treyial. She had once been lovers with the Terror. It had been a short lived relationship and ended abruptly after she succeeded in her subterfuge against Cedric. The love had long been lost between she and Treyial, each of them having a death wish for the other. This was the man I was to face in a battle to the death. This was my forced destiny. I would tell you the events that happened right now, but I am tired and wish to sleep. Perhaps if you come tomorrow I will bring light to the subject. Enjoy your night. I look forward to seeing you tomorrow. (to be continued) *Note: The lyrics are by Stabbing Westward. and the Movie quote is from GI Jane.*
