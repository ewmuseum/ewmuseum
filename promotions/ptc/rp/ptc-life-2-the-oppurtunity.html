Life #2 - The Oppurtunity

1 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tyler Houston had decided that he would give the bully five dollars every day because next year he counted on not seeing him again, ever. And, if he did see him next year, well, he would make sure he would not. This was causing him so much stress that it started to give him stomach problems and he did not feel like eating at times. He eventually got over it and accepted the fact that he had to deal with this and stick with his decision. “What’s up Mr. McKinney?” Tyler said to his English teacher. Tyler was wearing blue, baggy jeans with a long white shirt and an American Eagle T-Shirt on top of that. Mr. McKinney was his favorite teacher because he always messed around with the kids and told his students stories about what his school was like and what he was like. Mr. McKinney was wearing a long, white, collared shirt with a blue tie and khaki’s. “Not much Houston, we have class work today.” Mr. McKinney usually referred to Tyler as Houston just because a lot of other people called him Houston. Tyler walked around the first few rows of seats and took his seat in the second row, second to last seat. Other freshmen started to file in quickly but surely a lot of them friends of Tyler. Tyler’s friend Jordan sat to the right of him in the first row and Monica, also Tyler’s friend who was very pretty, sat behind Tyler. Tyler always had an eye for Monica and he thought that Monica was interested in him also. “What’s up Damon?” Tyler said to his African-American friend Damon Harper. Damon was on the JV basketball team and Tyler thought he was one of the nicest kids he had ever met. Tyler and Damon always joked around with each other and always made jokes about people. “What’s up white boy?” Damon returned the greeting and Tyler took no offense to this because they were friends and it was just part of the routine. Damon was wearing jeans and a regular long white T-shirt. “Not much. Do your homework?” Tyler asked. Houston was known in every class to talk too much and his behavior grades affirmed that. It was not like he gave the teachers a problem it was just that he talked way too much but he was getting control of his mouth, slowly but surely. Tyler looked at the black chalkboard for class work and he found it. He contemplated whether to do it or not because Mr. McKinney only went over the first few questions and then moved on to the lesson plan. “Nope, had a basketball game yesterday. We lost…” Damon answered. Tyler smiled. “That’s no surprise. You guys suck! You lost to Gratz by 50 points, how many did you lose by this time?” A big smile came across Tyler’s face. He thought about trying out for the basketball team but he could only get rebounds and shoot. He could not dribble that good and he thought that he needed to be perfect to make the team so he decided against trying out. “It doesn’t matter, you didn’t make the team SON! But I did score eight points in only a few minutes.” Damon started to look in his schoolbag for a notebook for class work. He sat two rows across from Tyler so they were talking considerably loud even though they were supposed to be doing the class work that was assigned. “That girl Sierra,” Damon said as he picked his head up and looked at the teacher to see if he was looking, then at Tyler, “is so hot.” Before answering Tyler got out his notebook and textbook and flipped to the page that he was supposed to be doing. “She’s okay. You know Sarah though, right?” “She’s the one with the huge watermelon’s?” Damon asked referring to her breasts. “Yea, she’s bangin’. I’d hit that in a hot minute.” Tyler said which meant that he’d have sex with her at any time. “I wish she would just break up with her boyfriend for a few hours.” Damon laughed and so did Tyler. “Are you having fun, Harper and Houston?” Mr. McKinney looked up from his grading. “Focus on the work at hand and stop fooling around.” “Sorry Mr. McKinney.” Damon said so everybody could here him. Damon put his face down to the paper that was in front of him with a smile. Tyler whispered, “Brown noser.” Tyler smiled and chuckled a little bit, Mr. McKinney looked up immediately. Tyler picked up his pen and began to scribble as fast as he could with a smile still on his face. “Hey Houston, how many brother’s do you have?” Mr. McKinney asked and it caught Tyler off guard. “Uh...three.” Tyler looked up and answered his question, wondering why he would ask such a thing. Tyler continued to look at Mr. McKinney and Mr. McKinney looked back. “What are you looking at?” Mr. McKinney said but without an attitude. “Well, I was wondering why you would ask how many brothers I had? It’s an odd question.” Tyler was still confused. “I’m the wrestling coach, as you know and I’m looking for wrestlers.” Tyler’s face lit up. He had three older brothers whom he wrestled with whenever they came home from college but just for fun. He could hold his own and he was getting stronger every time they wrestled each other. Tyler knew he was improving and could tell but he never thought of being on a wrestling team. He was not too fond of the idea of wearing the tight uniforms that he thought they had to wear. Tyler did not like the idea that they could grab his you-know-what because it would definitely hurt. Not only that but Tyler and his brother’s did not wrestle the conventional way. They just tried to hurt each other as fast as they could and did not have any real moves. “I think it would be a good idea for you to try out for the team, Houston.” Mr. McKinney said. “Well, I don’t know…” Tyler was hesitant because he was not that big. He was about five feet eight inches and one hundred thirty five pounds. “Aren’t I too small?” “Nope, they do it by weight class.” “I still don’t think I’m strong enough to compete with other people. I mean, I do wrestle with my brother’s but they always win.” Tyler said. He still was not sure whether he wanted to join or not. The pain of wrestling was not an issue because he endured a lot of that with his brother. Tyler loves to win and he’s afraid of failing. In basketball he knows he always has a chance to win because he has an awesome jumper. He wondered to himself; how would I fair against stronger guys who were more experienced? “Ya know what, just come out. We’ll teach you some things and you will be set to go.” Mr. McKinney said still trying to persuade him. “I don’t know Mr. McKinney, I’ll talk it over with my family.” Tyler wanted to do this to prove to himself that he could do it. He just hates to lose because he thinks it’s embarrassing. It’s not that he can’t do it it’s just that he needs someone to give him a push. Plus, can’t isn’t in his vocabulary. He thought that if you used can’t, you weren’t a man. It was something he would have to really consider and he would ask his family to help him with this decision.
