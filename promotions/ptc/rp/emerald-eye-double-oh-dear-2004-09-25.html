Emerald-Eye: Double-Oh Dear

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 “Daaave…” The breathless whisper sending chills down my spine. It was not my brightest of days. Out of the corner of my eye, I saw two things that shocked me. Firstly, the Thai lady named Beatrix that I thought could have been the lady that I was going to marry. She is on her knees, staring blankly up at me, with a sword protruding out of her chest. The sharp steel is still giving off an intense humming sound, the blade shining a blinding white light into my eyes and the crimson blood dripping slowly from the sword. Her eyes slowly roll back, the whole of her body sliding down the blade of the sword making a sick tearing sound, the skin being torn away as the body fell from the sharp edge. She fell to the floor with a soft, but equally as memorable thud onto the hard wooden floor. I think she may be dead. Tilting my head to the side, I get a closer look at the person responsible for the terrible deed: A tall man with a white trench coat, blonde hair sticking out in all directions, making him look like one of those poofy hairdressers, and a pair of shades with a golden frame. He looks down at the fallen body, and up to me, his facial expression never changing. No emotion shown. No remorse at what he had just done. I crouch down and grab the nearest sharp object I could find, one of the swords left by a samurai from earlier. This guy is going to pay… Just as I get up, my hands tightening on the grip of the weapon, ready to swivel and charge whoever it was that has taken the lady away from me, only three words exit my mouth, the only thing that seems fitting right now: “You… …evil … …fucker.” I bring the sword over my shoulder and charge as fast as I can towards the fiend, my feet pressing harder and harder into the solid wooden floor, picking up the pace faster and faster as I approached the enigmatic man standing ahead. I have never wielded any kind of sharp weapon like this in my life outside of a bread knife for cutting a few chunky slices out of a crusty loaf. This guy looks to be pretty proficient with them, but I just don’t care. I gather my speed, getting very close to the guy, my heart pounding, my eyes firmly fixed on the golden shades. As I bring my sword back I notice the guy not moving at all, not even getting into a position to fight back, the only thing changing is the mouth. As I ready myself to pierce the man’s flesh and tear as much of his body apart as is humanly possible all he can do is stand there… and smirk. The man’s expression alarmed me. I would have expected anyone with an insane sword wielding maniac running at them to be at least slightly concerned about the situation, but this guy doesn’t seem to have a care in the world. It’s too late now though, I’ve got to do something now. I turn my body, I bring my arms over. As much force as I can is put into this effort as I swing the sword at his throat… The clang… In an instant, he effortlessly brought his sword up and parried my attack, making me lose my grip, the sword leaving my hands. In the same way the sword slowly gets further from me, circling in the air and falling closer to the floor, all hopes of my retribution fade… …The clang of the sword hitting the hard wooden floor… the thick chime that awakened me to the harsh reality of what has happened. I’m alerted. Not to the fact that all was over for my chances at getting my vengeance, but more to a cold hard fact. He has a sword… and I don’t. Oh shit… I take a moment to try and swallow the mass of saliva that has built up in my mouth. I close my eyes, bow my head, and await the inevitable. Any second now… this evil bastard is going to take my head clean off of my shoulders. Any moment now… Go on then! I open my eyes to check he’s still there, and he is… dammit! In another version that the writer did he might have had him gone, only to realise that it would really balls up the storyline and change it… hey what am I saying? The tall blonde girly boy turns his sword to his side, maybe he’s giving up! Maybe the pansy is going to run away! THWACK~! Or maybe he’s just ramming the butt of the sword into my face… Vision blurring, the normalness of my view distorted with a black mist and all shapes being pulled in different directions. Sound muffled, almost like being underwater, with no feeling in any part of my body. I watch the floor coming away from me, only to be replaced with more and more ceiling. Oh that’s right I’m falling over. I try as hard as I can to do something to break the fall, but I just can’t move anything. My body is completely numb. The distorted thud as my head smacks against the floor, making the black mist even thicker and all energy drained from my body. I’m blacking out, I have a splitting headache (my head may be split, I just don’t know that right now), that bastard really hit me hard you know. And the floor didn’t help either. Coming into my view, over the golden chandeliers of the ceiling is the guy with the golden shades. He gives his smirk once more and chuckles, the distorted reverberating chuckle via my muffled hearing, sounding ever the more evil and intimidating. He brings his hands to his face and pulls away the glasses. Giving me a good look at his face, as if he wanted me to know what he looked like underneath the glasses. As my vision, hearing and my consciousness slowly fades away, the deep heartless chuckle continuing. Peering down at me is the face of the man who killed Beatrix and did this to me. What’s that in his eye…? It’s… sort of… green… -----=====|=====----- “Wake up…” Who said that…? “Dave… Wake up…” Thank god everything was a dream. Oh yeah, that was right, I got smacked in the head and fell unconscious. SMACK~! “OW!” That really flaming hurt! Who the hell just slapped me?! I sit up sharply and my eyes snap open, the initial glare of the immediate sunlight causing my eyes to squint shut. The white haze begins to subside and my eyes start to adjust to the light. In front of me, the first thing coming into view, a cute set of fluttering eyelashes complimenting a pair of glowing hazel eyes… a pair of lips curled up slightly into an amused grin. Am I still dreaming? Either she’s very impressed by my dashing good looks, or I’m staring at her like a moose in the headlights with my head to one side and my mouth agape in wonder. Oh shit it’s the second one, isn’t it? She walks slowly towards me, leans towards me, her head moving very close to mine. She takes her hand and places it under my chin and closes my mouth shut, awakening me from my daze. “Good morning, Mr. Hurst.” She says with a smile, “My name is Uma Crotchich, but my codename is double-o eight, double-o ten, double-okay I admit it’s a fourteen!” “Well you look good for it, to be fair.” I say complimenting her. “Thanks Casanova,” she says (I might be in there… but I have a feeling she was being sarcastic, oh well) “but I’m here to tell you that you may have a very small problem.” “Who told you about that?!” I yell… I mean, I didn’t think anybody knew about my small- “Plastic duck,” she says, interrupting me mid-thought, “you had a plastic duck with you, correct?” “Ducky!” I scream at the woman, “Where is he? Is he alright?” “No… the man that took you down took him.” “What…?! What the hell does he want with my duck?” Uma doesn’t reply, instead turns and opens a briefcase lying on the floor, bending over with her skin-tight skirt riding up her nicely formed round buttocks… … … Sorry, I was miles away! Oh here she comes with a folder. She throws the folder down at me and it lands on my lap. I open it up to the first page and it’s a photo of the geezer that attacked me earlier, his stupid sticky-outy blonde hair and golden shades as usual. “His name is Emerald-Eye,” Uma informs me as I put the photo to one side and get the next one. “Why do they call him tha- oh!” I knew I saw something green in his eye! No wonder they call him Emerald-Eye! He has an emerald for an eye! That accounts for the ponce wearing those flaming shades all the time then, I suppose. “Your duck is the key to his whole operation,” she says flicking her curly red hair over her shoulder before looking at me with a straight face. God knows how she keeps a straight face after saying a sentence like that, but there you go I suppose. “You may not know this, but the duck is not just a regular duck.” “I KNEW IT!” I yell at the top of my voice sending the poor woman stepping back in shock. “It has a chip inside it which is very important for national security. And we need it back. The other annoying thing which the writer was going to link with the storyline, but got lazy and couldn’t be bothered to come up with anything other than a bullshit reason, so you’ve also got this:” “Eh? You what…?” “The evil bastard wants to take over the world! He will do this using the chip implanted into Ducky to locate every pub or bar in the entire world and turn them all into Irish theme pubs!” “DEAR GOD NO!” I can see it now… pubs all around the world. Painted flaming green! Selling Guinness and other crappy Irish ales, with a bicycle in the window, harps, a stupid wishing well, leprechauns! This man needs to be stopped. “He will use it to deploy his evil army of robotic leprechauns, called Smurphys and take over the world! The secret service will need to take care of this, but we will need your help.” “You need my help?” I ask. “Why am I so important? Why can’t you just go and do it yourself? I mean, you are the secret agent, right?” The woman places her hands on her hips and turns her back to me, seemingly seething at the last thing I said to her. She seemed to mumble something along the lines of ‘why is it so difficult to get this idiot to come with me?’ and then turns back, with a smile on her face. “Well… urgh… you are a very talented wrestler, sweetie.” She says, trying to soften me up to go with her. I know her game… “And because of that your skills will be needed when we reach the evil super genius’s evil genius base.” “Erm… okay…” She wants me. I fully understand it, women must fall for me all the time, even though they never admit it… but I’m not going to turn down an invitation to be a secret agent for the day! “So why don’t you get off your fucking ass and come with me?” she says sternly the impatience showing as she half-turns towards the door notions to get a move on. I didn’t say anything. I seem to have pissed her off somehow. Josh cow, isn’t she? Either way I got up as quick as a flash and followed her out. I don’t know why, but I get the distinct feeling that there’s something fishy going on here. It might be the tuna sandwich I had left in my pocket all day, or it could be something more sinister. For instance, if Ducky had a chip in it and it was a threat to national security for it to be in the wrong hands, why didn’t they take the chip from me? I mean, me and Ducky have been together for months! I have a feeling there’s something that Uma isn’t telling me. I’m sure eventually we’ll find out exactly what that is. TO BE CONTINUED…
