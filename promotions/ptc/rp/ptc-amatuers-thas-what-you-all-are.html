PTC — Roleplay

Amatuers, tha's what you all are.

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme? The word of the week. But these people on the playing field just don’t know what it’s all about. They don’t know what Extreme means. And just what does it mean? Outermost? Farthest? Being in or attaining the greatest or highest degree, very intense? Extending far beyond the norm? Of the greatest severity, drastic? Very dangerous or difficult? Final, last? Each of those is what extreme means. But what does it mean for wrestling? To some they think that it’s being ‘insane’ in the ring. Using weapons and doing stupid pet tricks for the fans. Showing that you have absolutely no talent what so ever, and that you can swing a chair, crowbar or pipe. To some it’s being able to take pain and punishment. Being able to brush off what has become the hardcore and extreme matches. To some it’s the way that things are done. Doing the high flying circus acts, and going through tables and high jumps from the stands. But is this really extreme? Take a look at the board. The playing field is full of all types. You’ve got your psychopaths, your insane people, your blood lusting ones, and those who are in full contention for being a word that is almost damnable in this business. A cliché. How many psychopaths are there? How many insane guys? How many have killed? Or how many have maimed? How many are gothic beasts that serve some dark purpose or lord? Cliché. A trite or overused expression or idea. A person or character whose behavior is predictable or superficial. An expression or idea that has lost its originality or force through overuse. Very few in this competition have sparked my interest, very few. The best of the most extreme are supposed to be here in this match. But who is really showing that they’ve got what it takes to be ‘extreme’? What has Kraze done to prove that he’s extreme? Oh, he’s named Kraze right? Big deal. How about Ice? Oh, yeah, he can use special effects to freeze things like a picture of Mike Marvelous? I’m scared. Really friggin’ scared. How about “The Lion”? Oh, I’m quacking in my boots just thinking about someone named the lion. Looking at this kid, he’s more like the baby Simba then the full on Lion King. How about the big bad Shadow man? He’s the only one I can see as a threat if he was entering at a good time. But he’s coming in early. He’s also got me with a score to settle aver he helped to eliminate my sister in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup a few months ago. He’s got a target on him if he’s still in that match. But really? What is there to this that makes him extreme? Oh, yeah, I’ve seen him beat up a camera man before. Don’t worry. I don’t carry a camera. Jason Marah? A man I can respect for where he comes from, but nothing else. He’s a hyped up man from PTC, a big time player. One that people put their money on just due to his name. Because he’s been around the PTC block a few times. But really? Is that what makes him extreme? Cyrus Skar? The same thing, oh my, he’s been around PTC for a while. But is he really someone I need to look out for? No. He’s not going to live up to his hype. To me a man who’s got that much hype is nothing but an empty promise waiting to happen. Lacuna Debris and Jade from the fed I represent, 4 Winds Wrestling, no, while they’re friends of my own fiancé, and, in the back of their minds, they’d like to think that if they met me I’d go easy on them, I really can’t. This isn’t for the 4WW alone this time. It’s for me. Who else is there? DDT? Houston? Vertigo? Vigilante? Lee? Hanson? Moraless? Thomas? Do I really need to go on? Again, I ask myself, what do these guys and girls think they’ve done to be considered to be hardcore or extreme? What have they done that I’ve not? I know that I’m the one who put the former “Hardcore Icon” Ace Thunder on the shelf. Forced him into an early retirement and then shook his hand as he passed the torch to me a few years ago. He’s the one who opened up a fire that brought me over to the ‘dark side’ of wrestling. He took a technical specialist and made him a monster. He took a man who was a wrestler, and turned him into his own image. Those of the UWF have heard the stories of Ace Thunder, the man who was the true first THC Champion. Yeah, Lew tries to claim that, but we know the truth. While Lew Spectre was a wrestler, he was nothing in the line of being hardcore. But the second real THC Title holder was me, Sean McClancy. Since that day, that day that I had to beat a man into submission, into retirement, I’ve seen myself as one of the extreme. What’s so different? Do I need to walk about how I’m some dark individual that kills rats for a living? Or cameramen for that matter? Do I need to talk about how I’m going to going to beat them? Or how I’m going to show them how extreme I am? They should know that’s going to happen by now. They should realize that’s how I do things already. I was one of the men who made it to the hide and seek match at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. I should have left with that belt already. I was the man with the home field advantage there. A former member of the PWW, I had been in that type of match before. But last time it was for the 4WW. This time it’s for me. I’m going to bring the blood of the McClancy’s to that ring, carry the mantle given to me by Ace Thunder, himself, and look at who’s left in the ring. And then it’ll be time to commence to doing the one thing that Sean McClancy lives for… Winning. The * BUMP * sign will be in use. It will be used. That is all that has to be said. I’m the Hardcore Icon. I am the one who will leave looking to take on the most Non-Extreme Extreme Champion the PTC has ever seen in Mike Marvelous. I am the Hardcore Icon. I’m entering last. The point of advantage on my part. I know what I’m doing. And the one thing that all of these supposed wannabe’s can’t comprehend. I can swing a chair with the best of them. But I can also swing my fist. I can lock you and bend you in directions you’d never dream of. And I can also throw you where I want you to be. This isn’t child’s play boys. I’m playing for keeps. And just what is Sean McClancy doing? What is he doing right now? How is he preparing? We find him as he’s sitting down in an empty room, a locker room. A bench rests under him, as most benches do. Sitting next to him is his infamous * BUMP * sign. A thing that had been handed to him by Jamie “Ace Thunder” Stenson-Preest that night that Sean crossed over from wrestler to Hardcore Icon. The night that he made history and started along his path of proving to all that he is truly the icon of all hardcore wrestlers. Icon: An important and enduring symbol. One who is the object of great attention and devotion; an idol. He’s sitting there, wrapping some red tape around his right wrist. The southpaw man sits there, his hair now combed, but his beard that he had grown in his vacation is still there, albeit a bit trimmed now, and looking like it should be. He’s in his wrestling trunks, and boots. Looking to be almost ready to enter the ring. He’s got a glistening of sweat upon him, not oiled or fake like some of them would use. Sean: Lo’ an’ behold. Fer in yer presence is a man whom others though’ would na’ make ‘is way back t’ the ring anytime soon. I’m returning t’ the ring in a rumble rules match. A match where my past ‘istory with this division lends t’ me bein’ in the prime spot. The real favored t’ take this match all the way. But look a’ wha’ I’m greeted with? Noah Hanson? ‘E calls me a lucky charms eatin’ whatever. Hanson, yer enterin’ 2 be’ind me. Ye’d better fokin’ ‘ope yer na’ goin’ t’ still be in th’ ring when I get in there. I’m sick an’ fokin’ tired of gobshites like ye always goin’ fer the low blow, bu ‘missin’ like a long shot. Then again, Mr. Hanson ‘as na’ exactly proven t’ be the fullest bottle in the case, now is ‘e? He stops rolling the tape onto the right wrist and then moves to smooth it down a bit. Sean: Listen t’ ye all. Talkin’ all tha’ crap abou’ ‘ow big an’ bad ye are. ‘Er ‘ow extreme ye are. Yer all amateurs compared t’ the Irish Bastard. Yer nothin’. Wha’ ‘ave ye done befer t’ make such claims? Ye’ve fought in cages? I think we’ve all done tha’. Ye’ve fought with chairs, chains, an’ fokin’ brass knuckles? They’re commonplace. ‘Ow abou’ tables, ladders, cookie sheets, stop-signs, brooms, trash cans, an’ all tha’ other usual stuff? Big fokin’ deal. Ye ever ‘ad yer best friend win a man’s motorcycle in a match, an’ then when ye get a match with ‘im, ye use th’ pieces an’ parts o’ tha’ motorcycle as yer weapons? Ye ever done a tiger driver from the top rope through a table? With the gas tank o’ the bike upon it? Bu’ enough abou’ that. Tha’s only one part o’ bein’ extreme. Fer anyone can swing a pipe. Anyone can grab a chair. I do believe we’re taught that as one of the first things after ‘ow t’ fall in wrestlin’ school, riogh’? No, t’ be extreme ‘er ‘ardcore, ye ‘ave t’ be capable of doin’ all of it fer real. He stops messing with the tape and puts his hands in his lap. He looks at the camera. His pale face with his hair the color of fire itself. Sean: I’m na’ some psycho. I’m na’ some deranged man. I’m a man. Tha’s it. A wrestler. One tha’ ‘as been able t’ reach down t’ ‘is guts an’ do things tha’ others would na’ do. One tha’ ‘as been able t’ fight an’ earn th’ respect tha’ was comin’ t’ ‘im. I’m a nobody in PTC though. I know this. I’ve na’ been ‘ere. A’ one time I was one o’ the top in one o’ the other groups o’ feds ou’ there. I was the man a’ the top of this game. Jus’ na’ ‘ere. Now, I’m ‘ere. Now I’ve come t’ claim wha’ is mine. What I claim is mine. I don’na care if yer the top o’ the RPW, the NGW, er even the fed tha’ took over from me old stomping ground, th’ UWF. I’m ‘ere. I’m representin’ 4WW, bu’ most of all, I’m representin’ me. An’ I’m the last one t’ enter th’ ring. I’m doin’ more then figh’in t’ get a shot a’ Mike Marvelous. I’m also fightin’ t’ keep my number one contendership. Tha’s riogh’ people. I’m the last one t’ enter because I’m th’ one who should be goin’ next. Yeah, I’ve already ‘ad a shot. Bu’ ye can na’ deny th’ simple fact tha’ I’m the one who should of beaten Fathom fer tha’ belt. He still sits there. Not screaming or doing anything but talking to the camera, calmly. Sean: It’s time fer ye all t’ realize tha’ there is a new kid on the block. I saw it with me sister when she entered th’ Cup’s tourney. I now see it again with me. As soon as some of yer ‘golden boys an’ girls’ get int’ the mix o’ things, ye think tha’ they’ll get a special treatment. Tha’ th’ rest o’ us ‘ave t’ be worse jus’ based on the’ fact that they ‘ave somethin’ we don’na ‘ave. A reputation ‘ere in PTC. I’m ‘ere t’ make me reputation. This rumble match is th’ first. Then a match with Mike Marvelous in a match tha’ ‘e wil na’ be able t’ contend with. I’ll take Mr. Marvelous t’ the limits an’ teach ‘im as I will teach ye all when I climb int’ tha’ ring. It matters na’ wha’ yer are, ‘er wha’ yer reputation is. Th’ Irish Bastard is comin’ t’ make ye wish ye’d na’ signed on fer th’ ride. The camera zooms in to his face. His emerald green eyes are visible clearly in contrast to the rest of his facial features. Sean: Gentlemen an’ ladies. I’m Sean Michael McClancy. A third generation wrestler from Dublin, Ireland. I’m th’ one tha’s comin’ fer ye. I’m a teacher of those who wish t’ take the lessons o’ life I ‘ave t’ offer. And when it comes t’ this match, boys an’ girls, I’m givin’ th’ order. Class is in Session. Welcome t’ Sean McClancy’s School o’ ‘Ard Knocks. ‘Ardcore an’ Extreme Wrestlin’ 101 is in session. Once ye’ve all seen jus’ ‘ow it’s cdone, it’ll be time fer th’ graduate course with Mr. Marvelous. Then it’ll be mine. An’ all will ‘ave t’ suddenly sit up an’ go… “Fok, Sean McClancy is one extreme bastard is’n ‘e?” An’ I’ll jus’ sit there an’ laugh as everyone else realizes tha’ it was their own blindness tha’ cost them their reputation. It zooms out a bit, looking to make a slow retreat away from him. Sean: An’ tha’, is wha’ I ‘ave t’ say t’ ye all. An’ fer those who discount me, ‘er be wantin’ t’ crack the same old Irish jokes an’ other crap time an’ again, I give ye this. Three little words. Pog… Me… Hoin… Don’na know wha’ it means? Don’na worry, I’ll show ye first ‘and… soon… soon. And we fade here.
