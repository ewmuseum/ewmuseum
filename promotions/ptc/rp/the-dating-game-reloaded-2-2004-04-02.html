The Dating Game Reloaded#2

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 Not too long ago a certain band said love is only a feeling. That my friends is little more than a lie. Ask anyone who has been in love and I mean truly in love if it’s just a feeling and they will laugh at you. Love is a disease, it seeps into our bodies when we aren’t noticing and begins to take control of us, turning us into slaves. Love makes you do things against what you feel is right, it makes you act on simple primordial impulses. At the end of the day we are all loves bitch we try to fight it, we try to go totally against the flow but it’s all in vein as at the end of the day we all know that we will be there…in love with them and nothing can ever EVER change that. That is the situation that our man James Edwards is in at this very moment in time. His life has never been simple, nobodies ever is, nor is he anything special just another man amongst men with his own believes, his own life and the problems that life often brings. You see he loved a girl. A girl called Michelle. What’s the big deal about that? I hear you say everybody falls in love. The thing was though, this was his first love, there had been previous flings but this was the real deal, true love. But as we all know true love never runs smooth. She left him, uncontent with life as the girlfriend of a wrestler, the later hours, the hectic schedule and the constant moving around all began to drag her down until the day she left. James never saw it coming, he was far too focused on his life in PCW a brand new world of bright lights and airships which ultimately dictated his life from there on in and when she did leave his heart left with him, no longer caring what the world thought of him nor of the distractions of love until he met her. Sarah. He had watched her from afar and liked what he saw, her generous heart, a loving smile and sincere personality. She was someone who wanted to be with James for who he was, not for what he had but again love never runs smooth. She had asked him out, after weeks maybe months of wishing it to be so but fate decided at that point to toss a joker into the deck a certain ex who happened to visit that coffee shop at that exact time. Life is a bitch huh. James stood silent staring at her once, not able to take his eyes off of hers, the woman who had left to Australia eight months ago was now mysteriously back in his life. “Michelle” he repeated ago as though if saying the words again would maybe change the past, or at least make it clearer. Michelle stared back at James her skin had turned a nice shade of white, half through shock the other half through even more shock, it was a very shocking time for her. Taking James’ hand gently in hers she gazed towards James swallowing hard as she cleared her throat looking for the words any words to clear the agonising silence that was encompassing the both of them. “Can we talk, I have a lot of things to say and I’m not sure how to say them…I owe you a lot of explanations about where I’ve been, what I’ve done and what I did to you.” The words came from her mouth true, no hint of lies hidden within them, and her sultry voice melting James as she spoke. “Can we go and get a coffee and allow me to explain?” James nodded and instinctively turned to re-enter the coffee shop, until his eyes fell upon Sarah, looking at him with confused eyes. The door was shut but she could clearly see the man she had just made her feelings known for holding hands for another woman. Panic gushed through James’ body, he didn’t want it this way. He didn’t want to hurt Sarah, she didn’t deserve to have the past rubbed in her face like this, not yet anyway, and some secrets take time to tell. “Ok” Spoke James hesitantly, trying not to tear his eyes away from Sarah whose face had fallen, a look of despair and sullenness taken over before she turned away, returning to her work he whole posture indicating sadness, regret and deceit as a pain began to gush through James’ body that was when he realised…he loved her and he had hurt her. “Not this place though, the coffee is lousy I know a place just around the corner good stuff there” James lied through his teeth as Michelle nodded still notably holding James’ hand refusing to let go as though holding onto the past, clutching at lost straws. So they walked around the corner but with totally different perspectives, one looking to the future of a world that is yet to dawn and the other stuck in the past wanting to put right wrongs wanting to turn back the clock. If only life was that simple. Sat in a corner table at a hustling and bustling starbucks James and Michelle sat, the awkwardness more than evident due to the total silence around them. James was waiting, waiting to know why Michelle had walked out on him and ruined his life. Michelle was simply stalling knowing what she must say, but too afraid to say it. Scared to push him away again, to risk losing him a second time, despite not actually having him the first time. “I suppose you want to know what happened” the words stumbled from Michelle’s lips as James stared at her, he was past the caring now, he just wanted an explanation, the look of Sarah’s face still burning through him the pain becoming too much. “It just became too hard James, your life style I hardly saw you and when I did all I could think of was when you were going to leave me. When you touched me all I could imagine was you leaving me to go and touch other men in the ring I just needed time to think things through” Silence. James continued to look at Michelle with a level of intenseness that wouldn’t be lacking at a police interrogation not saying a word, simply sipping his hot chocolate from the mug in front of him listening to what Michelle had to say. “Then I heard about all the closures, I know that your life has been, well simpler lately Axiom closed and you are jobless so maybe I was thinking, we could try it. You and me a normal happy couple.” The small glimmer of hope in her voice made James smile as he simply shook his head and rose to his feet, wanting to turn his back on her yet something needed to be said it was not finished yet. “So you came back because you thought things were different, that life’s circumstances had left me up shit creek without a paddle, that I could become you little lap dog who you could train. You don’t want me Michelle you want a pet. You always hated what I have done, yet you made pretence of being interested now I see your true colours. Guess what hun I’m not unemployed I’m alive and kicking working regularly in RPW and a little cruiserweight tournament that I’m involved in at the moment too. I’m afraid that my career is on the up more than ever so if you can’t deal with that I suggest that you get out of my life because I am not ready for you to dictate who I am. I am James Edwards and I will be whoever the fuck I want to be.” Spoke James calmly and coolly which is somewhat surprising considering the nature of the words and with that he turned away from Michelle not looking back he didn’t need to anymore he had someone else. The short walk to Sarah’s coffee shop was a journey of enlightenment for James, before he had been clouded with memories and thoughts of what if… What if she hadn’t left? What if he had told her he loved her? What if he hadn’t met her? But that didn’t matter now, he had seen her with his own eyes, seen the truth and now there was something he had to do, there was one more step he had to take to close the door on the past, to open the future. He pushed open the door to Sarah’s coffee shop and walked to the counter resting his arms on it gazing forwards a steel gaze of determination on his face. Spotting James, Sarah walked forwards slowly, her beautiful face not baring the smile it previously had, but a grimace of pain. “It’s ok I understand I mean you are an attractive guy” said Sarah, he voice barely audible as though the words themselves didn’t want to be said. “I just wish you had told me you were seeing someone else, I wouldn’t have put myself on the line. I…I’ve liked you for a long time but didn’t want to say anything. Past hurt, you know the usual stuff. It’s just I…” Sarah was cut off for James had made a decision; he had always been told that actions speak louder than words. He leaned forwards and gazed deeply into her eyes before kissing her softly with a passion he hadn’t felt in a long time and in that moment the world melted away. He didn’t care about RPW. He didn’t care about PTC He didn’t care about Coricidon He didn’t care about Raijin Because as a different band said quite a while ago…love is all you need.
