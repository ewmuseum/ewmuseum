Reliving the Past - For Better or Worse #1

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 A grainy style video flickers on and begins to light the screen. It’s bright and fruity, light and tangy. It can only be one era…the dreaded 80’s. Pastel colours hit the plasma television like a bad hangover, reminiscing a time of Miami Vice and all things spice, Family Ties and blue skies…and, oh you get the idea. Currently in the background we can hear the familiar thumping of the staple rhythm of the 1980’s: the good old electronic drum kit. Its ‘thunks!’ and ‘tchs!’ bring a certain nostalgic serenity to the occasion and add further to that all too original 80’s vibe. A smattering of letters come flying into the picture, fizzing with the effervescence of the aliens from Space Invaders, creeping and careering into the centre frame. As they fly by the ‘WHOOSH!’ sound effect can be heard, like everybody had Dolby Surround Sound back then and we hear the announcer; Announcer: (booming) WELCOME TO THE NCWA. The giant golden letters fix in place and a sliding line fits in right underneath them. They hold there for a few seconds before the screen begins to fade to black, but not totally as we cut to what looks like an opening segment for the show… We hear the birds chirping in the cool summer air of whatever year it is. It looks like it’s somewhere in California, what with all the palm trees and hot sandy beach off in the distance. In fact, the beach is right up where we are as the camera begins to reveal the entire scene, seeing more than we first anticipated. All around the camera are numerous bodybuilders, muscle-bound freaks who relish in pounding their pythons (all innuendo intended) with one another and seeing whose guns can be called the most powerful. They’re greased like pigs and their tans are shockingly bad, red raw meat on display for all to see. Each one of them is wearing a sickening pastel coloured thong and it all looks a bit surreal. Then again, that WAS the 80’s. But one of them, right in the centre looks different. While all around him are pumping their irons with cropped black hair and are straining in the effort, this man is hulking up, casually pulling what is an ungodly amount of weight from the ground with no effort. He is the one man to have a bit of decency, choosing shorts, distinctly black and white and stark in contrast to the others. Not only that he’s still pale in his complexion in comparison to the bubblegum pink flesh heads that are standing beside him. A short ponytail is tied tightly to keep his hair in but he has no facial hair. He now turns to meet the camera almost psychically. It’s Jack Murphy, but definitely not as we know him now. Jack Murphy: (flexing muscles) Such a gorgeous day…and of course it’s going to be gorgeous with yours truly, ‘The 8th Wonder’ Jack Murphy! He begins to stroll through the men, casually flexing his muscles every now and then subtly, but at the same time purposefully for the camera, making sure they catch every single style of flex he can give them. He passes a man struggling with a barbell, its weight stuck in the ground. Murphy halts and wags his finger in the direction of the man. Jack Murphy: (shaking his head) What a pity, it seems my friend here has not learnt his eight step plan to greatness. He has forgotten rule no.2, how pathetic that he has fallen so soon. He picks up the man brushes off his shoulders before admonishing him directly and pointing his finger at him. Jack Murphy: Rule No.2; always remember that strength comes from not within yourself, but from the powerful physique you’ve built with my patented quick step pills! Without a thought Murphy picks up the barbell and hands it to the man, dropping it right into his hands. The man struggles under the weight and then collapses, much to the hilarity of Murphy. He laughs away to himself, putting that off as if it was nothing. He continues to walk through the masses, a giant among men. Jack Murphy: So why bring you here? Why here, a place of people whom you think are the converted? That is the point my friends, they are further from converts than you can imagine. He spreads his arms out, displaying everyone around him like they were cars in a showroom. Jack Murphy: None of these are the definite article, none of them have achieved their peak, their perfection, their prowess like I have. I look at them and I see walking failures. A man needing rule no.3 over there, here no.4, further a field no.8, I could go on…what you see is that I just don’t look for the muscular and impressive looking; that is merely a mould. What I truly strive for…is the complete package and that is something everyone can achieve. He finishes at the front of the crowd and one of the men is on the ground, breathing heavily after some workout. Murphy looks at him disdainfully. Jack Murphy: So come one come all and don’t be afraid to join my crusade for a better tomorrow…a better future for the human race…as we buff for success! With a flick of his heel Murphy kicks sand right in the man’s face on the ground. He coughs and hawks up spit as Murphy smiles at the camera before flicking up a small ball over his head and kicking it behind his head with his heel. The camera cuts away suddenly and we see the ball land right in a dustbin. We close, with a smile.
