The Cell: Zero

2 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-02 I He hid in the shadows knowing that his opportunity at freedom would present itself soon enough. He had waited years for this moment, the moment where he could get from behind these prison bars and take back a throne that was rightfully his. Everyday his hatred for the man who put him there grew stronger, some days it was almost as if his chest would rip open to release it all. Thoughts of how he would end the life of one Roberto A. danced around in that dark mind of his, warming up that cold heart momentarily. He heard their voices and their footsteps grow louder as they came closer. LeStatt Knight was like a kid on Christmas Eve, excited beyond belief. It had been a long time since he had been able to roam free and it had been even longer since he had another man’s life in the palm of his hand. It was THAT feeling that he may have missed more. LeStatt did not give a damn if people respected him, but he loved that they feared him. "Alright FREAK! You know what to do." The voice invaded Knight’s ears. It was pitch black inside of that cell so the guards could not see the grin that was plastered on his face. He knew he needed them to come in a hurry. He released a groan and a sickening choking sound escaped his throat. If one were to look closely they have seen the sudden sparkle in those black eyes when the guards rushed for their keys and unlocked the cell. They both draw their pistols from their hip, one aims towards the darkness and the other slowly walks forward into the shadows. LeStatt waits patiently for the man to move closer towards him. Each step he takes, LeStatt’s excitement grows even more. There is no sign or warning, Knight just knows. He reaches out with both hands, cupping the guard’s mouth with one and locking up his hand with the pistol in the other. He muscles the gun from his hand and it falls to the ground. LeStatt’s hands come up to the guard’s chin and head. Slowly he turns the head until it cracks numerous times and the body becomes lifeless in those hands of his. "NO!" BANG! BANG! BANG! The gun shots lit the room up only momentarily. The panic raced inside of the only guard left standing. LeStatt kneels down and lifted the other pistol up. Its not even a matter of IF LeStatt was going to kill this man but how. He pointed the gun towards the figure of the guard, knowing that his bullets would actually find their mark. He slowly squeezed the trigger. The bullet went through his knee, dropping him instantly. The gun in the guard’s hand fell to the hard floor with him. Slowly LeStatt walks from behind the shadows, that grin on his face and those eyes lifeless. He cocks his head to the side slightly as he watches the tears flowing down his next victim’s eyes. "I’ll send your regards to Roberto." BANG! II "They were innocent men LeStatt." Roberto’s voice was soft and worn. He had been locked up since the night of LeStatt’s escape from the cell. This was the first time these two had talked since the incident. Roberto had hoped that death would have found him first, for fuck sake he shot himself in the face. For some sick reason LeStatt had other plans. He nursed him back to health and now had him locked in the same cell that Roberto once put LeStatt in for years. Irony at it’s best. "No one is innocent my friend. Besides it was you who killed them the day you put them in charge of me. Did you really believe that I could be held back forever?" "One could only hope LeStatt." LeStatt chuckled heartily. He always enjoyed tormenting others and tormenting his most hated rival was something he took great pride in. These two were complete opposites. Roberto was kind and emotional, loving and caring. LeStatt was full of hate and anger, merciless and violent. Roberto wished for nothing more but to live a normal life, enjoy his family and live peacefully. LeStatt wanted fame and fortune, LeStatt wanted the world to feed from his palm. They were each other’s most feared enemy. They knew every bit of each other’s lives, their thoughts, their emotions, their movements … everything. Their battles for supremacy are as inevitable as the sun setting every night. Roberto had tried his best to end the conflict without others being hurt but he learned that evil doesn’t die; sometimes it merely rests for awhile. LeStatt is no fool either. This cell will not hold Roberto forever and as vile as he is, Roberto is the one man who can end his reign of destruction. "I do not understand why you hate me so much Roberto. Together we could be unbeatable. Together we could rule ever aspect of life and our profession." "If you are so powerful then why can you not do it alone Knight?" "I do not NEED…" "BUT YOU DO! Oh you do Mr. Knight. I heard what happened. You no longer have the power of the GCW at your finger tips." Roberto could not see his capture as his hands were tied behind his back against the bars of the cell, but it didn’t matter because he could feel the hatred burning in the back of his head. A smile came to Roberto’s face now. LeStatt did not respond. "Nothing to say LeStatt? Afraid to admit when you have been beaten? That has always been your problem you know? You always fought a losing battle." "NO! You listen to me! I do not lose. What happened with Stevens was simply a minor set back." "Set back? You are nothing without that title LeStatt." "You would not have made a difference." "Who are you trying to convince?" LeStatt grits his teeth, doing his best to keep it straight. He knows what Roberto is attempting to do; he even gives kudos for such a thing. LeStatt understands the importance of mind games, something he prides himself on. "Stevens will enjoy that title reign of his. One day he will have to look across that ring against me again. When that day comes he will know what real fear is like. I will dispose of him just as I could have disposed of you years ago. I should have let you die that night of my escape … perhaps you could have died with some respect, unlike the sorry pieces of shits you called workers. Each one of them BEGGED me for their lives. Their tears streamed down their faces and their words got caught up in their throats. Their blood splattered all over my face." III Blood splattered up on his face and those eyes flashed red as the body of his victim tumbled to its side, lifeless. LeStatt turned his head towards the three others that were on their knees in front of him. He pointed the two pistols in his hands towards them and you could almost hear their hearts begin to pound faster and harder. Knight smiles towards them. "Now tell me … will you beg as he refused to? If you do not beg for your lives I promise you that I will rip your insides out and let you look at them as you die." He cocks his head to the side. "You first!" LeStatt moves closer and puts the barrel right to the temple of a young blonde haired man, probably no older than 22 or 23. His face was stained with tears and the blood of his co-workers. He cannot say anything even though he tries but all that comes out are sobs. "P-P-Please … I-I…" "BEG YOU FUCKING PIG!" Sobs "Fucking pathetic." BANG! Blood splatters behind the body and it falls as well. The screams of terror grow louder from the remaining two. One is a rather well built African-American man and the other a young woman. The woman is draped in the man’s arms; he is doing his best to be strong in such a time. LeStatt now looks up at the two survivors and aims towards them. "This could be so easy ya know?" Knight moves closer "Just beg me for your life and I won’t kill you the way I killed these two, all you have to do is beg." The man stares into Knight’s eyes, hatred filling him up. The woman has her head buried into her protector’s chest. The Male guard swallows hard. "I will not beg but you must spare her." "Excuse me?" Knight takes the barrel of one weapon and scratches the side of his head with it. "Are you telling ME what the hell to do? Listen here Superman, I understand you wish to be her savior and all but um … Actually." LeStatt moves closer and grabs the woman by her shirt and pulls her up to her feet. She screams louder and reaches out towards her friend. The man goes to move but Knight points the gun to her forehead, forcing the man to sit back down. Knight’s hand wraps around the back of her neck and her long brown hair moves slightly. "This is her chance … if SHE begs I won’t kill her the way I have everyone else. You will die though." "No please!" she howls "SHUT IT!" Knight snaps and then turns his look back to the man on the floor. "Tell her to BEG!" They don’t share any words but she receives a slight smile and nod from the man. She closes her eyes and after a moment begins to speak. "Please don’t kill me. I-I will do whatever you want…" "Get down on your knees and beg." She looks over for just a moment "NOW!" She slowly drops to her knees and begs for her life. With each tear and each screech of her voice, LeStatt’s sick grin grows wider. It seems to last forever before her head falls and she stops speaking and the sobs take over. LeStatt places one pistol in his pants and takes that hand and runs it through her hair. "Good Girl." The sound of a neck breaking is heard now… IV "You are disgusting." Roberto hisses towards Knight "You told her you wouldn’t…" "What I told them was that she would not die the way the rest had. I did not shoot her. I kept my end of the bargain. Besides should she really have lived through that night? What use would I have had for her? Perhaps I could have used her as a sex toy for whenever Mary or Cindy gets boring?" Roberto’s muscles tighten but he says nothing. What could he possibly do anyways? He was locked up and wasn’t going anywhere at the moment. LeStatt was in charge and it was his own fault that it happened. Roberto clears his throat before saying anything. "Look LeStatt why are we reminiscing about your escape? You obviously came down here for a reason. What the hell do you really want?" LeStatt’s eyes narrow at the back of Roberto’s head. He had been bullshitting around but it was a lot of fun to him. Mostly though he just did not wish to say what really was his purpose for this visit was. "Alright Roberto fine I’ll tell you." LeStatt stands up from the chair he has been sitting in and moves right up to the cell bars. "GTT5." Roberto’s head turns slightly. "GTT5?" "You know damn well what I am talking about. You were in GTT4 correct?" "Yes." "Well I have now thrown my name into the hat this year. Tell me why and how you failed to advance." "Wait …" Roberto chuckles. "Are you asking me for help?" "I DON’T NEED HELP!" LeStatt snarls "BUT you will cooperate." "Fuck you LeStatt I…" LeStatt places a knife to the back of his neck. "You WILL or else that pretty daughter of yours will not see her next age. Cooperation may not save your life in the long run but it can save those that you love. I am sure Starr and Cindy would be very appreciative." "Fine" Roberto says after a short delay. "Anything LeStatt." "Good." LeStatt moves back and sits back down. "How did you fail?" "I did not concentrate enough. I messed all of the little things up and it cost me. I had more pure talent and more desire than whom I face but what killed me was a bit of arrogance." "Alright and what do you know about a certain Clyde Walkins?" … V "Stay the hell back!" LeStatt roars. LeStatt was cornered like an animal even though he did have some little bit of leverage. A hostage was wrapped in his arm with the barrel of his pistol aimed at his temple. Two others were standing a few feet away, their guns pointed towards Knight. He just keeps telling himself that his mission is almost complete. The door that leads to Roberto’s office was mere feet away, he could not falter now. The sweat begins to mount, in his palms, on his forehead, and down his chest. Everyone involved was edgy and just waiting for this thing to blow up. "It’s over LeStatt! Put your weapons down and no one else has to get hurt." "Fuck you." Knight snarls "I live to cause others pain." "Then we will have no choice but to take you down." "Go for it." No motion No talking No breathing Everything seemed to be in slow motion. LeStatt pulled the barrel away from his hostage’s temple and spun to the ground landing on his side. He aims the guns upwards, between the legs of the man in front of him. BANG! BANG! BANG! The bullets found their mark, dropping the men like nothing. One died instantly while the other was caught in the neck. LeStatt smiles as he gets to his feet once again. BANG! A bullet goes into the knee of his original hostage, causing him to drop to his knees in serious pain. His screams of pain are drowned out though from the choking/gargling sound coming from the throat of one of the surviving guards. LeStatt stands over the body. "It’ll take more than THAT to take me down." The sound of LeStatt’s boot coming down and crushing the man’s skull is sickening. Blood runs down the hard floor like a river, Knight’s eyes follow it down the hallway until they reach the soul survivor. Knight points. "Loose end." LeStatt then turns and walks down the hallway, making his way towards the office of one Roberto A. as well as to face perhaps his most devastating challenge yet. When it was all said and done on THIS night … there would be only ONE. ONE! VI "PRIME?" Roberto asks. "PRIME." LeStatt says matter of factly. "Well you may just be destined to lose LeStatt; I mean let’s face the facts here. You JUST blew your shot at immortality by losing the World Title to Michael Stevens and now you are paired up with someone that works for the largest company in pro wrestling." LeStatt grips the cell bars and looks down onto the top of Roberto’s head. "So what? I’ve overcome odds before." "No you haven’t." Roberto said flatly. "Every time you have taken over I have went through the rough patches FOR you. Every time you decide to show your fucking face I have already been waist high in the mud! You now face a little professional adversity and feel like you are in a world of hurt … kiss my ass LeStatt." CRACK! It’s the sound of a fist knocking against the back of Roberto’s skull. "You are a mouthy one aren’t you? Well good because it’ll make when I win this thing and then kill you all the sweeter. Prime and more specifically Clyde Walkins are nothing to me. I will prove that what happened at NC-17 was a fluke. My legacy will remain in tact." "Say what you want LeStatt. If you thought you were going to win without a shadow of a doubt you wouldn’t be here trying to get information that I may have. You NEED me and that kills you inside." "I WILL RIP WALKINS’ HEAD OFF!" LeStatt howls "YOU AND THE REST OF THE WORLD WILL BOW BEFORE ME!" "I figured a man of your stature would be above threats by now." Roberto smirks "It’s a shame you are going to be beaten by a mentally retarded asshole from Prime." LeStatt’s eyes light up. "YOU KNOW OF HIM!?" "I know everything LeStatt; remember I am the smart one of us." VII The door crashes open as LeStatt kicks it in. He steps into the office and sitting at his desk, as if he had no cares in the world was Roberto A. His enemy The man who locked him away The man he would kill now LeStatt lifts his pistols and aims them at Roberto’s head. The man behind the desk doesn’t even bother to look at LeStatt. His eyes remain fixed on a picture that he has in his hands. LeStatt takes a few steps closer before even attempting to speak. Roberto speaks for him. "You know LeStatt I should probably thank you." "Shut up Roberto … I don’t want to hear any of this head game horseshit." "I need to thank you," continuing on as if LeStatt never said a word "You have helped me realize that things never change. You are always who you have been. I have taken lives just the way you have yet I am a ‘good man’ and you are evil." "ON YOUR KNEES!" "I wonder if they will love me when I am gone?" Roberto puts the picture down. "No one will love you Roberto." His eyes move upwards and meet LeStatt’s "I wonder how I will be remembered?" "With a bullet in your fucking head." Roberto stands up and it is obvious that he has a pistol in his hand too. "LeStatt … I won’t give you the satisfaction." He lifts the pistol to his own temple. "NO!" LeStatt roars "Good bye." BANG! The gun shot rings like thunder throughout the night sky. VIII "I meant what I said LeStatt." "What are you blabbering about now?" "The night we met in my office, about needing to thank you." "Save it, I am more concerned with GTT5." "One day soon I will thank you properly. One day soon I will prove one theory of yours correctly." "Yeah? And which one is that?" A long pause. "That we are more alike that we both have realized." A long pause. "Good night Roberto." LeStatt stands and begins walking down the long corridor. "Sweet dreams LeStatt," Roberto gives out a short but genuine laugh "You’ll need them."
