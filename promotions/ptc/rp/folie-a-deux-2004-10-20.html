Folie a Deux

20 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-20 Craig Goat sat in the almost empty train carriage and stared out the window at the lush green country side speeding past. The motion of the train and muted sounds of the wheels on the track were familiar and comforting. For a while, Craig forgot to worry about his destination and relaxed. This was the first time since the beginning of GTT4 that he had been just by himself, all alone with his thoughts. As they had been doing a lot recently, those thoughts drifted back to the tournament. Craig had been coerced into entering GTT4, and much as he railed against it at the time, he was glad for once that he was so easily manipulated. His round one match against Jonathan Borealis was not just his first showing in PTC, but also his first time ever stepping into a ring competitively. Defeating Borealis had been a fantastic start, but would pale into insignificance compared to what now lay ahead of him; Seymour Almasy. Craig bit back the fear beginning to rise in his gut. He had come so far in such a short time and was loathed to let Almasy or his doubts stop him now. He would be a challenge, but Craig was starting to get used to difficult challenges lately. Jenna Ryder, his second round opponent, and a favourite with the red blooded males of America, hadn’t been an easy victory. She was a beautiful sexy woman with a shady history in porn and women like that always won, didn’t they? Nurse Wretched most surely did. Craig tried his best to remember his third round opponent, Flyin’ Brian Calvin, but his mind was a blank. Worried because Craig seemed more comfortable in the ring against attractive women, Nurse Wretched had arranged for him to be hypnotised by El Fantassmo, a third rate and cheap stage hypnotist. Craig had been told that his mind had been twisted so he would find Calvin… sexy. But in doing this, his strange love for Nurse had been erased causing him to seek revenge on her for all the hurts she had inflicted upon him. Had he really kidnapped and tortured her to near death with a dildo? Craig shook his head at the absurdity of it all. He knew he was fortunate to have his mind cleared of its shackles and back to normality, or as close to it as he could get. Especially since El Fantassmo was now facing a serious malpractice suit brought by a gentleman who believed he was a potato. A woman with a wailing snotty child moved up the aisle towards him. Sensing she was about to sit near him, he met her eyes and snarled menacingly. With a whimper, she urged the child onwards, into the next carriage. Craig experienced a brief feeling of guilt but quickly quashed it. He deserved this quiet time. A couple of hours without Nurse Esme Matilda Wretched screaming like a harridan at him was a precious thing not to be wasted. Nurse Wretched. His thoughts came back to her even more often than the damned tournament.. Perhaps because it was she more than anyone else who had shaped them. It was a chance meeting with her a year ago when he was a patient in a psychiatric unit that had led him onto his path to the GTT4. His had been committed by his parents and diagnosed as agoraphobic. But Craig knew the doctors were wrong. He was simply a geeky kid with few friends bar those he met online through his wrestling efed. He smiled at the memory of those days when the most important thing to him was writing a fake role play for an imaginary wrestler. Nurse Wretched had shown him a different world and promised she would help make him into a real wrestler. It had been a full year coming, but finally he was almost there. A year under the tutelage of the most evil woman in xUw history. She had used him and abused him, treated him like dirt and pushed him about like an indentured servant. That the mansion and all the money was his seemed inconsequential, Nurse was the one in charge. Occasionally, he felt disappointed in himself for letting her control him so completely. One of his biggest regrets was letting her coerce him into admitting his own parents into an asylum. At the time he believed it to be the right thing to do, but a niggling guilt still lingered in his gut. But even that wasn’t the worst of her crimes. It was because of her that Craig had lost Trixie, his first girlfriend, the woman he had chosen as a surrogate vessel for the love he had long held for Nurse Wretched. Unrequited love was exhausting and having Trixie in his life gave Craig the feelings of self worth that Nurse had long denied him. Craig missed Trixie and longed for the sense of connection he had first experienced with her. It had become his biggest weakness. Unable to fill the gulf any other way, he had let Nurse back into his house and his life once more. He had expected her to be raging with anger towards him given that he had –had he really?- tortured her so thoroughly with a dildo so recently. But she had been calm, pleasant almost. Craig guessed he should have known she’d do the opposite of what he expected. After all, she thrived on keeping things unbalanced. Perhaps that was why he had taken this trip? To escape the disconcertingly… nice, atmosphere in Goat Mansion? The train slowed and stopped at a station. He was there. As he stepped out into the sunlight, he hesitated. Was he doing the right thing? With his match against Almasy so close, why was he dredging up the past like this? But he had come so far, and as much as it might hurt, he would go on. As the taxi made it’s way up the long gravel drive, Craig asked himself again, why was he here? Why now? Was it guilt that did this to him? Was it a sign of weakness or a sign of strength that he felt compelled to face his parents once more? He had no satisfactory answer to any of these questions, yet as the immense building that was St. Mary of the Lambs Mental Health Asylum came into view, he suddenly got a sense that he was doing the right thing. Soon after he first met Nurse Wretched, she was fired from her nursing job and was living in a dreary locker room at the XUDF Arena. Desperate to get her hands on Goat Mansion, the house Craig shared with his parents, she spun an elaborate plan to get her greasy hands on it. With the aid of three ex mafia hard men, three men called Dave, she kidnapped Craig’s parents and took them here to the asylum. Somehow, and even now Craig wasn’t sure how, she convinced him to go along with her scheme and play his part in this heinous act. Finally, Craig was returning to see the consequences of his actions. He was met in reception by Dr. Randy, a typically egotistical man for his profession. Craig had called in advance to set up this meeting and create a pretext for his being there. Dr. Randy stuck his hand out to shake Craig’s, “Detective Prescott I presume?” “Um, that’s right, you can call me Craig though. I don’t have much time I’m afraid, can you show me to the people I spoke to you about earlier?” Dr. Randy slapped Craig jovially between his shoulders, “Of course my good man, I’m afraid I’ll have to ask you to step through a metal detector first. Can’t be too cautious with so many violent nutjobs and patients roaming about.” Craig began to notice subtle differences to when he was last here, “I don’t remember security being so tight when Nurse Wretched worked here.” The doctor suddenly dropped to the floor in a crouch, his hands held protectively over his head, “N-nurse Wr-wretched? Where?” Nurse Wretched had once ruled this dreary hospital and under her care, many patients suffered needlessly cruel treatments and torture. After many reassurances that she had not indeed returned to reek her revenge on the hospital staff who had forced her out and set the police on her tail, Dr. Rand got up from the floor, grime sticking to his white coat. “I must apologise for that… but you don’t know what it was like! I’m still on the Valium. Terrible nightmares.” With a drawn face, Craig comforted the shaken doctor, “I understand, probably better then you know.” After a quick tour through the myriad of twisting corridors, they arrived at their destination; the main patient lounge of Ward B. Craig peered through the reinforced glass window at his parents. His mother appeared to be teaching a group of drooling men how to knit. Judging by the long trails of knotted wool hanging from some of the knitting needles, she didn’t seem to be having much success. She looked well, her hair tied back severely and her plain cotton smock, a uniform for both the men and the women, was clean and pressed to within an inch of it’s life. There was more grey in her hair then he’s remembered though, and when she turned her face slightly, he noticed that she was free of her usual make-up and lines were showing clearly beneath her eyes. His father looked shrunken and sat in a plastic chair in front of the TV, the remote control gripped protectively against his bony chest. Another man, unkempt and insane looking, dived at him attempting to wrest the control from him. His father wailed imploringly until his mother rushed over to break up the fight with a quick wag of her finger. The wild man, suitable admonished, slouched off leaving Craig’s father in peace. “So, is that the couple you’re investigating?” Unable to speak, Craig nods. Ushering Craig to a chair in the corridor, the doctor begins to speak, “Interesting case that pair. Both are suffering the same persecutory delusions. It’s a rare condition where one member of a couple is genuinely psychotic and the weaker member mirrors and reinforces the illness, in the medical profession, we call it Folie a Deux. It’s been very difficult to treat them too, no matter how much anti-psychotic we inject them with, they still persist with their delusions.” Horrified at the thought of his parents being stuck with needles, just like Nurse did to her opponents, Craig asks, “Why? Why are you doing that to them?” Dr. Randy shrugged, “Well, as we don’t know who they really are, we can’t check medical insurance details and an injection once a week is a lot cheaper than pills you have to take twice a day. Budgets suck, but what can I do? They’re so unwell; both believe there is a conspiracy against them and have created an elaborate false history. Can you believe that they think they are millionaires kidnapped by three eight foot tall giants and brought here against their will?” “Couldn’t that be true? I mean, have you actually checked?” The doctor snorts derisively, “Ha! What would be the point? Every bit of information they’ve given us is so transparently false. I mean, they say their names are Guy and Gabi Goat. No one could have such a ridiculous name as Goat!” Irritated now by the self important doctor, Craig shoots back, “There’s nothing wrong with that. My name is Goat!” “Oh, I do apologise Sir. Anyways, as I was saying, their persistent delusions of grandeur and persecution is more than enough reason f or them to be here. Eight foot tall mafia men? Displaced millionaires? The stuff of fantasy indeed.” “Well, Big Dave is more like seven and half feet and Medium and Little Dave are a bit shorter, but when the three of them are together, yeah, they do like giants. As for the money, I guess if you figure in the house and paintings…” A suspicion finally crept into Doctor Randy’s forethoughts. “You’re not a detective are you? You know these people! … Oh Lordy, they’re not delusional are they? Shit, shit, shit!” Unable to deny the truth, Craig nodded as shame started to fill him from his boots up. “They’re my parents. Things have been a bit… weird for the past year. Seriously, they were probably better off in here than at home.” Dr. Rand looked at Craig with disgust, “You do know we’ve been listed as the worst psychiatric facility in the country four years running now? I don’t care what things are going to be like on the outside for them, you’ve got to take them home with you. Now. Today. Oh Christ! They’ll sue, won’t they?” “Tell you what, you make sure the real police don’t come sniffing around about this and I’ll make sure they don’t sue. Deal?” Relieved, the doctor enveloped Craig in a bone-crushing hug, “Guess you better get reacquainted with your parents then?” Without waiting for an answer, he opened the heavy reinforced door and guided him through. His father was the first one to spot him. With a half choked yelp, he rushed at Craig and wrapped his arms around him, sobbing, “I knew you’d find us! I knew you’d come and help! Gabi didn’t think so, she thought…” He’s cut off by his wife, “I think you are the one responsible for this.” His mother looked him over slowly, her gaze penetrating through to his very soul. “Do you know what I always liked about you, son?” Craig shook his head. “Fuck all.”
