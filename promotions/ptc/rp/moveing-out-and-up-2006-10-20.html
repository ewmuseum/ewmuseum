Moveing out and up

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 An old man stands staring out the window. The man is dressed in a nice white suit with a blue silk shirt. A cigar hangs from his jeweled adorned fingers. He slowly lets out a puff of smoke as he looks out to the beautiful scenery on his villa. White marble statues placed here and there. His slicked back black hair and thin black and gray moustache and chin stripe make him look almost French. An eye patch covers one eye, a deep scar floes from his wrinkled forehead, down the side of his face, to his lip causing it to curl in a sneer. The old man drops the cigar on the marble floor and crushes it under his foot. The room is very well decorated with a few swords hanging on the wall tapestries of ancient battles and bullfights. A large fish tank fills one wall. In the room on a red, black, and gold area rug sits a golden and marble desk and a large black leather chair. Behind the desk a few black book shelf’s filled with a few books and an odd ornament here and there holding books in place. Another small desk matching the books shelves sits right behind the highly ornate. On the desk sits a flat top monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. The screen displays some statistics on investments in the stock market. One man stands in the room, dressed in black slacks and a black mussel under shirt. His long hair tied back into a ponytail, and a pair of black shades tries but fails to cover the large burse on the side of his head. His face blank and shows no emotion giving him a look of the same statues on the out side. He stands motionless with his hands behind his back Not every day you inform a father that his sons have died. Not every day you inform a father that you failed in your job to protect them. Not every day you fear your head would be missing after you told that same father. But today was that day for Juan Balbina. The father; He was Aquila Diego Estrada; One of the notorious crime lords of Mexico; One of the men responsible for the movement to get drugs legal in Mexico. Aquila Diego Estrada had ties in Japan, being half Japanese his self. He also had ties in America, and Spain. But with all that power he was still a human. Even so he was one of the more violent of the species. Thus a reason Juan feared the loss of his head. The last man who brought bad news lost his head, and that was over the fact that some street thugs robed one of his drug houses. Aquila Diego Estrada looks down at the cigar as his fingers of his left hand tract his moustache as he losses his self in thought. After a while of looking down as the now smashed cigar, Aquila Diego Estrada tilts his head and his words break the cold silence of the room. "Chu’ know the face of the man responsible Juan. I want this man found. I want this man brought to me. I want him to look into my eyes as I cut his throat my self. This is the only reason I spare your life right now. Chu’ have failed me more then any man has ever failed. Chu’ have one chance to redeem your self. I want you to find this gringo vaquero, I want him hurt, but not dead." Aquila Diego Estrada says in an ice-cold tone that chilled Juan to the bones. "Sí mi jefe." Juan says relived that he would not meet the dark reaper of souls tonight. "Juan chu’ have one month to find this gringo vaquero. If chu’ do not find him in that time. It will be you who suffer a fate worse then death." Aquila Diego Estrada says making it perfectly clear to Juan the price for failure. "Comprenda?" "Sí mi jefe." Juan says hoping to get Aquila’s eye off him. "Then I suggest chu’ get started Juan." Aquila Diego Estrada says as he turns and heads back to the window over looking his large estate. Juan had no clue how to start or where to look. The only thing he could do was pack his bags and leave to America. "One month to find a American male that could be anywhere in the world." Juan thought to his self. ------------------------------------------------------------------ Ely slowly ate the eggs and bacon in front of him. Hoping he got into the big tournament. The money would be good. Money was getting pretty thin, already they had to head down to the local Department of Human Service office and apply for food stamps and then to the Local Housing authority to apply for housing assistance. Factory work was not here. No one was hiring save for the Honkey Tonk over in the next town and fast food restaurants. "What is it darlin’." Came the feminine voice behind Ely. Bobbie Sue walked over and started massaging the shoulders of Ely Braxton. Ely moved his neck around and leaned back into her embrace. For a brief moment he found some peace. "Sorry. I just have a lot on my mind. This is no life for you." Ely says waving his hand at the small trailer that Bobbie’s family had given them. It was not even the size of a garage. A small stove, a bed next a place for a table to fold down in one corner Ely had attached a wooden rod to hold what little clothes they had. The other corner held a small refrigerator and an old TV, and then there was the shower / toilet area. Ely just shook his head and stood up, hitting his head on the roof as he did. Ely winced as he did. "Every damn time." Ely thought. He wondered if he would never get used to the height issue of the small place they had. "You deserve better Bobbie. I want to give you a house, with a nice yard and a fence, not this roach motel reject." Ely said as he picked up his white T-shirt. "Baby. Don’t worry about it. I lived in places like this growing up. I am not offended." Bobbie said reassuringly. Ely signed and opened the small door and squeezed his self-out. He reached back in and grabbed a beer out of the small refrigerator. Ely took a deep breath of the cool air. The smell of fresh rain hung in it as the sun had yet to burn away to coolness. Ely sat in the chair and looked around at the junk piles surrounding the house. Bobbie’s brother had gone into the junk and salvage business. Ely got a job at his tire shop down the road. It was a small little place next to the E-Z Mart. They money made was split twenty five to seventy five, the majority going to Bobbie’s brother, Cosmo Tucker. But it was cash and non-taxable so it sufficed until something better came along. "Well Darlin’ I got to go make what I can today." Ely said disappointed. Bobbie kissed Ely on the lips. "I got to go to town with Ma to get some more groceries, Ill bring you lunch later." Bobbie said as she bounced out of the trailer. Ely watched her short, short cut off jeans from behind as Bobbie trotted over to her Ma’s house. The sound of tires on gravel pulled him out of his trance like state. Ely turned around to see the mailman pull up, open the mailbox, and pull off once again, leaving a cloud of dust as he does. "At least there are some things to enjoy still." Ely thinks to his self with a sigh. Ely walks over to the mailbox and pulls out several envelopes. He looks though them and pulls out one envelope. He walks over to the old white house and walks up on the porch and sits down on the swing. The swing creaks in protest to his weight. Ely slowly opens the envelope and pulls out a letter. *************************** Dear Mr. Braxton, We here at PTC would like to thank you for your application form. We also have verified your past entry into the GTT series. We have also got confirmation that you are still contracted to Global. With everything looking in order, we would like to welcome you to GTT6. Brackets have been drawn and you have been picked to participate in the Tigger Bracket, Your round one opponent will be Danny Vista of WWA. This year GTT is going worldwide, as the Tigger Bracket will compete at the Singapore Indoor Stadium, for round one competition. We have also included an information packet along with two tickets (one for you and one for your manager.) on Singapore Airlines from New York to Singapore. Your room has been reserved at The Oriental Singapore. All identification and information on restaurants and sights are also included. Once again thank you for applying, good luck in the tournament. Sincerely, Argyle *************************** Ely smiles as he finishes his letter, and looks up as Bobbie walks out of the house. "MA! Come on!!" Bobbie shouts over her shoulder. She turns to see Ely sitting there with a large smile on his face. She cocks an eyebrow and tilts her head. And puts her hands on her hips. "What are you doing? You got to head down to the Tire Shop." Bobbie says as she chews on her gum. "Nope." Ely says with a small laugh. "What cha’ mean nope?" Bobbie says a bit upset. "… and what you smiling about?" "Well you can tell your brother Cosmo I quit, and me and you. We are going to Singapore." Ely says smiling. "Huh?" Bobbie says confused until Ely holds up the letter. "YOU GOT IN!!!" Bobbie screams and jumps up and down. "WHAT IN THE NINE HELL’S IS GOIN’ ON OUT HERE?!" Bellows an older fat lady that now makes her way out of the house on a cane. "Have you lost your damn mind Bobbie Sue Tucker?" "No MA! We are leaving for Singapore! Ely got his place in that big fighting tournament." Bobbie Sue exclaims. "Well I reckin’ yall’ got to do what yall’ got to do." Ma Tucker says "When yall’ leaving?" "As soon as Bobbie can get some bags packed, just one each Bobbie. We got to arrive a bit early, I got to report in and get a bit of trainin’ in." Ely says as he stands up. Bobbie sequels in excitement, and leaps off the porch, and races to their small trailer. Ely takes a few steps off the porch until Ma Tucker puts her cane on his shoulder. "Listen here son. That’s my pride right there. She is my only daughter. You better take good care of her." Ma Tucker says sternly. "Hey, I am doing this for her. I love her." Ely says with a smile. Ely realized he truly did love Bobbie Sue. It had been a long time since he had felt what he felt for the little woman. Ma smiled and nodded and turned to head back into the house. "BUD!!! GET OUT HERE!!! I NEED YOU TO RUN ME TO TOWN!!!" Ma Tucker bellowed causing a crash inside the house. A man in jeans a cut off jean button up shirt and a blue hat, all three cover in black grease and other stains stomped out. "What ya’ want ma! You made me drop my lucky charms!" Bud says with a scowl. Ma slaps him across the face. "Don’t you ever take that tone with me! You may be thirty years old, but as long as you live here you will respect me! No get in the damn car and take me to town." "But ma! Bobbie was supposed to. My soaps are about to come on!" Bud says in protest. "Boy get your ass in the car." Ma says as she points to the car. Ely can’t help but laugh as Bud stomps to the car, Bud stops and looks at Ely and then rethinks things and turns and gets into the old rust and brown colored Ford Lincoln. "WELL COME ON!" Bud shouts as he starts up the car. Ma climbs in and slaps Bud once more and then Bud hits the gas and backs out of the driveway sending rocks flying against the house. And then the car speeds down the dirt road leaving a trail of dust. Ely shakes his head and heads over to his old blue Ford truck and climbs in. A moment later Bobbie climbs out of the trailer and tosses the bags into the back and locks the pad lock on the trailer. She skips around to the passenger door and hops in. "So where we of to now?" Bobbie says excitedly. "Dallas. Got to get to the airport and get a flight to New York." Ely says. "But first I got o stop off somewhere in Dallas to get some money." Bobbie nodded and turned on the radio as Ely pulled out and headed down the long dirt road. ------------------------------------------------------------------ The old Ford pick up truck rolled into the parking lot of the office section of the Global Arena, in Dallas Texas. By the looks of things Ely was lucky to get a parking spot at all. The door creaked open and then make a loud pop as Ely opened his door. Ely stretched his back and popped his neck. Lifting his legs to work out the tingles in them. Three and a half hours of sitting while driving made his body ache a little. Bobbie Sue hoped out of the other side and yawned and stretched. She then walked around the front of the truck and stood next to Ely looking up at the Global logo on the side of the large domed arena. "WOW!" Bobbie says with a surprised and awed tone "You used to fight here? It is a lot bigger in person." Ely lifted his brown cowboy hat and ran his hand though his hair trying to make his self look presentable. "Yeah. Won some, lost some. Come on, I got to see Bubba." Ely says walking forward. Bobbie ran up and hooked her arm in his. The two walked though the sliding front doors and into the main reception area. Ely walked up to a middle-aged woman identified as Alice Watson by her nameplate. The woman looked up over glasses. "Mmmm… Yes. May I help you?" She asked in a nasally voice. "Acutely Mam, you just might be able to. I need speak with Bubba. Names Braxton, Ely Braxton." Ely says with confidence. His face changed however as the woman started to laugh. "Where have you been for the last year and a half, under a rock? Bubba retired and has not been here in that amount of time sonny." Explained Alice. "Oh. Well who is in charge now days? I need to see who ever is in charge." Ely says with less confidence then he had just moments ago. "Well, That would be Train." Alice says "Ahh damn. How in the… No… No I don’t want to know." Ely says lowering his head. "Thanks for your time Mam." Ely turns to leave, but stops as a younger lady stands up from behind a filing cabinet. "Acutely that is wrong Alice. Brandi Sights is in charge; Train is merely Commissioner of Fusion." Says the lady walking into view. "What? Since when did this happen Donna?" Alice asks in surprised shock. " I though Brandi was still out on injury leave." "Nope she came back while you were on vacation." Donna explains to Alice as she walks up to Ely. Donna eyes Ely up and down until Bobbie steps in front of him and holds his arm. Donna gives her an evil look and then looks at Ely. "I can take you to Miss. Sights right now if you want." Donna says. "That would be … Good." Ely says with a start as Bobbie elbows him in the ribs. Ely smiles and Donna turns. "Follow me." Donna says. Donna walks down the hall. Ely looks at Bobbie and shrugs and she shrugs her shoulders and follows. Ely shakes his head and follows as well. "Women. The most confusing creatures in the world." Ely thinks to his self as he heads after the two ladies. After a short walk they come to the door that reads Brandi Sights: Global President. "Well there you go. Bye." Donna says with an evil look at Bobbie. Bobbie goes to get her and Ely grabs her by the waist. "WHAT!" Bobbie shouts at Ely. "Look, you can not just go and attack her." Ely says trying to explain. "Ohh you like her don’t cha! You think she’s hot don’t you stud!" Bobbie says sarcastically. "No. I don’t. But I got to talk business and get the money to get to New York, and I can not have you getting arrested for assault." Ely explains. "Fine. I will let it slide this time." Bobbie says Ely smiles and nods his head and then knocks on the door. "It is open." Says a female voice from behind the door. Ely opens and both he and Bobbie walk into the office. Brandi Sights stands there pacing back and forth on the phone. "Look Dark Rituals is going to be BIG!! HUGE! Yeah! I want it on every Internet sight in every magazine and on the Dallas morning news! Yeah! Thanks J.L. Yeah later!" Brandi says. She then clicks the phone and spins around and sits down. "Well… Well… Well. Look what the cat dragged in. Ely Braxton." "Yeah. Look who’s moved up in the world. Your doing good for a former announcer." Ely says and then gets another jab in the ribs. "Ohh Ouch.. Umm Brandi this is my girlfriend and new manager Bobbie Sue Tucker." "Nice to meet you. So Ely what brings you back form the dead, so to speak?" Brandi says with a smile as she leans back in her large black leather chair. "Well I got into GTT." Ely says "So I heard… It also seams your contract is still good. Seams Bubba gave you a life time contract." Brandi says with a sort of upset tone. "Wow. Well I did not know that. But In my acceptance letter is said that I was still under contract. That means I got some back pay coming." Ely says with a slight laugh. "Sadly, I do not like paying people who do nothing. You better earn this in GTT Ely. Or I may have to make some choices you wont like." Brandi says as she reaches in her drawer. She lays a large envelope on her desk with a thud. Ely nods and picks it up. He looks in and make quick count and smiles. "Well thanks. Now I got to go make flight arrangements." Ely says. "Do not worry, I will represent Global proud." "You better. Good Luck Ely. And I look forward to seeing you back in the ring." Brandi says with a smile. "Yeah, about that. I read Global’s no longer the full blown pro wrestling federation of old." Ely says searchingly. "Well seams they do get news in Under-Rockville. But you heard correctly." Brandi says. "Good then I may return after all. I do not like politics and I do not like all that pre booked stuff and a person having to loose cause a booker decides." Ely says. "Understood. Things are different now. But you had best be on your way. Don’t want to miss the flight." Brandi says with a wink. Ely nods and again, he turns and heads out of the office. Bobbie follows and pinches his arm. "Old girlfriend?" Bobbie asks accusingly. "Ouch stop hurting me, and no. Never was a girlfriend, just a friend." Ely says as he heads back down the hall. "Well I don’t like her." Bobbie says as she rushes to catch up with Ely. "That’s becoming more common, Look darlin’ Past is past. No one can replace you. Believe me on that one. No come on lets get that plane." Ely says as he wraps his arm around Bobbie. The two hug and then head out of the Global Headquarters together. ------------------------------------------------------------------ Juan Balbina stepped out of the off ramp and into the airport terminal. Black jeans, black button up shirt, black boots with silver on the toes, a black western hat and black shaded on his face. He looked around and then walked over to gather his bag. "Welcome to Dallas Texas Mr." Says a young girl behind the baggage claim check out. Juan hands her his claim ticket and she walks off to get his bag. "Flight 235 to New York, Now arriving at gate 12" Rings a robotic human sounding voice. Juan turns and leans against the counter as he does he takes off his glasses. Heading to the terminal with two bags, and woman following him, is the man. Juan begins to head towards them as a crowd of people rush by. "Sir … Sir… Your bag." Says the young girl. Juan ignore her and pushes his way though the crowd. As he heads into the onramp a man steps in front of him. "Ticket please? Sir you ticket please?" the guard says. "Look I just saw a old friend get on that plane. I wanted to say hello." Juan says. "Sorry no can do buckaroo. No ticket, no enter. Got that amigo." The guard says with a smile as he starts to pull out his tazer gun. Juan nods and holds up his hands and backs away, He turns and walks over and grabs his bag and walks to the ticket counter. "I need a ticket to New York. Flight 235." Juan tells the young man at the counter. "Ahh sorry sit that flight is booked. Next flight should be leaving in one hour. I can get you a spot on flight 432." The man says with a happy look on his face. "Fine." Juan says handing the young man his credit card. The young man types a few lines into the computer and then hands Juan a ticket in an envelope. "Thank you for choosing American Airlines." The young man says. Juan grumbles and steps to the window and watches the Airplane pull off and head to the runway. "Soon gringo. I will get you.
