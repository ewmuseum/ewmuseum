A time to be killed

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-23 Daunting? There are times when the word that has been created to describe a certain feeling just doesn’t seem to come close to the feeling itself. This is one of those moments. Soon a man who could remember only small tid-bits of his life would step back into the ring with four men, something that was yet to be experienced in GTT5 and something that Salaok wasn’t too sure about. Was it the fact that two of his opponents were from that place which is Number One be Definition? Or that one of those two just so happened to be Deville, a man who everyone seemed to fear stepping into the ring with? Why fear that which you don’t know? Salaok not only hadn’t seen any of this man’s matches during the GTT5 event, but couldn’t remember anything about him from before he lost his memory so this so called Deville was and will forever be nothing more than another man. Another who stands in the way of something that had been put in front of Salaok to conquer. No, the opponents meant nothing to Salaok but the fact that there was going to be more than two men in the match did play on his mind more than most things had been during this tournament. However, there was a simple way of getting through and it was with the same sentiments that some one had mentioned during there round four lead up. While he couldn’t remember who it was that had blurted out the words that seemed so simple and yet powerful at the same time, the words had stuck. That had stuck because there was a simple statement to the world, but for Salaok they were now a simple statement to the men in whom he was about to step foot into the ring with. Fuck You! He didn’t care about these three other men nor of there reputations or the so called Hype in which surrounded them because he knew nothing about them nor did he want to. They were just men who did the same thing that Salaok and everyone else in this tournament did. The only difference was the these three men and Salaok had done it better than the people in which they had come up against in the previous rounds. A level playing ground is what they are all about to walk out onto and it’s always the ones you least expect that will hurt you. Salaok had walked around the tournament hearing that there was no chance in hell of him making through to the next round unless Deville decided not to show up. What ever happened to respecting your opponent and those in the business, allowing yourself to believe that anything could happened and that it all comes down to who is the better man on the night? Fuck You! It was the only thing going through his mind the more he thought about it. The feeling of being overwhelmed were quickly fading and being replaced with the anger that he remembered so well. The pain that comes with being over-looked and the hatred for those who write you off before you’ve been given a true chance. Sure he hadn’t yet been tested fully in this tournament with each victory at first coming as a surprise but never really pushing him near the limit in which he knew he was able to go. However, no one gave Salaok a chance in hell and to all of those who don’t he would show them by doing the one thing that everyone wanted to do. Tell Deville the two words that were running through his mind. Fuck You! But he won’t just stop with Deville, the same sentiments go out to Tsonda and Samuelsson. Daunting? Ever noticed that sometimes a word that was meant to describe the feeling never quite cuts it? This was one of those moments. Standing either side of the car, both Salaok and Michael stood looking at the building, which Salaok had demanded to get inside. Since loosing his memory, there had been many, many questions that had been left unanswered and sometimes that was the best way to leave them but not for a stubborn Salaok. For there had been too many doors left locked and no information to unlock them with. Michael had done what he had been told to do by Salaok’s older brother by showing him piece by piece of the past but the way in which Salaok was to remember things was the problem. He wanted everything now and this game in which his brother Johnathon had been playing with him was not only wearing him thing but taking his attention from other aspects of his life. An aspect as big as GTT5 was a mere day trip here and there because what was controlling him was the past which he knew nothing about. Sure, when Johnathon had left the note for Salaok to enter the bed room of his late child, it was a rush of memories that not only had put Salaok on his ass, but had sent a yearning through his body for more. Not more pain, but more information for is he had had a child in the past, then the child must have had a mother but where was she. Not only that, but if Salaok’s son Darrell had died the horrid death in which Salaok remembered finding, who was it that had killed him and why? The questions kept building into a sky scraper and all questions lead Salaok to this one man. A man who he knew through blood. A man who Salaok had once idolised and looked up to. The man was Salaok’s older brother, Johnathon. This building was something of elegance and beauty, two words which had never been used to describe it’s owner who peered down on the two men from a window high above with his white hair blowing in the breeze. Turning around, his hair flies out behind him in a circle before disappearing back into the darkness of the old church. It did seem somewhat strange that a man so well known for his torment of souls would now hide himself in a church. But what this his house or just another game to play on Salaok? "I can not guarantee that everything you see and hear in here will be truth." Michael’s words of warning did nothing to dissuade Salaok’s advance as one foot after the other edged up the stairs. The feeling of being watched is something that everyone knows all too well, and while we wonder who it is that is watching us and why, never do you turn around to see who it is for more than a split second. And a split second is all that Salaok glanced up for, but not seeing anything as Johnathon had backed away from the window second before hand. "Michael, we’ve done this your way for the last few weeks and my knowledge of my past has grown too slowly." Salaok’s response had sounded out without much thought behind it as everything at this point in time was being driven by emotion. A state that is dangerous to say the least for when emotion controls what you do, rationality disappears out the window closely followed by vigilance. With the gap between the door and his body closing quickly, Salaok’s aging hand reaches out and presses against the old wooden doors, which fly open a lot fast than Salaok had been expecting. Smashing against the walls behind them, the doors create a loud echoing crash able to wake the dead, however in this case, it wasn’t the dead which Salaok was wanting to wake, it was the one who holds Salaok’s memories of those who were dead. Johnathon held all the keys to the answers regarding his where his wife was, what happened to his son and who killed him, why it was that Salaok had been pronounced dead and who had shot him. There was so much else that Salaok was wanting to know and yet knew that if his brother was anything like how he remembered him, there was no chance in hell that all of the answers were going to be coming freely. Turning around to see if Michael was going to follow him into the old church only to receive a shake of the head and watch him get back into the car. The engine roars to life and in the dead of the night the vibrations rip through the chill in the air, breathing life to an otherwise dead atmosphere. With a shrug of the shoulders as Michael drives off into the streets, Salaok turns on his heel and finds his personal space not only being invaded, but crowded to the point of touching. And there he was in all of his evil glory. Salaok’s face instantly turns into a snarl while Johnathon begins to laugh at his younger brother, just like always, the two were at different ends of the scale. Johnathon’s white hair contrasting to Salaok’s pitch black, and while the color white is normally associated with things which are pure and innocent, there was none of that here in this building. Tension is something that was guaranteed in this situation but Salaok wasn’t expecting it to be in his face that much as Johnathon looks up at Salaok and begins to laugh, mocking Salaok’s search for the truth. Turning and walking away from Salaok, Johnathon knows the questions in which he looks for and jokes, "So you want to know how you died? You want to know who else you’ve killed?" Each word filled with torment and hissed like the snake that Johnathon truly is. While Salaok knows full well of the games that Johnathon would play, he is quickly engulf in the first one, unknowing to himself that with every response he gave his old brother, it was leading him down the opposite road in which he was search. "Do I want to know who I’ll killed? What do you mean Johnathon?" The question was one that never should have been asked but the answer was one that would answer nothing. "Oh, nothing Salaok, never mind. What is it you came here to ask me in the middle of the night? I know you want to ask about your wife but don’t as it’s not worth your time, and as for who killed your son, well I wont tell you that one either." "Johnathon, we are brothers and we stood side by side for so many years, why now do you do this to me? What have I done to deserve this?" Salaok’s voice almost begging with Johnathon and yet Johnathon’s tone remains the same. "You became weak my brother and bringing you back to being the man in which you once were is going to be process that I need to take great care with. You’re a crystal ball Salaok, the answers are within you but you need to be looking at things from the right point of view to see what needs to be seen. And much like a crystal ball, you need to be dealt with in the upmost care for you run the risk of shattering in a million pieces if dropped. And you’d be no use to me if you were a shattered man like you were when Darrell died." Salaok glares at his brother who has the nerve to speak to him with his back turned before a clicking sound is heard and a red glow forms near Johnathon’s face. Turning back to Salaok, Johnathon holds the cigarette loosely between his lips while continuing to hold the conversation, which seems to be going no where, until now. Placing his hands in side his black robe, Johnathon walks towards his brother once again, his grin more sinister than before however as he begins to hiss again. "Now brother, in one of my hands there is the picture of your wife who you did love with all of your heart despite what you knew, in the other is the picture of the man who caused you to loose your memory and thus cause you all this pain and remembering everything bad that has ever happened in your life. Which hand would you like to pick?" It was of no surprise to Salaok that Johnathon had to turn this into a game just like everything else in life. Never was anything straightforward and easy with Johnathon and this situation was going to hurt Salaok either way. Both questions were what he wanted the answers and yet he could only choose one and while the thoughts through his head were running at 100 miles an hour, his hand rose up slowly and pointed to the left hand side of Johnathon’s body. With a grin, Johnathon walks right up to Salaok and pulls the picture out of his pocket, but doesn’t hold it up straight away from Salaok to see. "Now Salaok, you’ve picked the picture of the person who caused your memory loss, are you ready?" A nod of the head was all Johnathon was waiting for and received, as the picture is flung up in front of Salaok’s face which at first received no response but slowly a grin forms on Salaok’s lips, only to slowly be replaced with that of rage as his hand reaches up and feels the wound on his forehead. The footsteps could be heard, but from where? Thud, Thud. While it was of no huge situation that footsteps would be heard, these ones almost seemed to be mocking Salaok, stalking him. It was dark as most people had left the arena for the night and only the cleaners were around as far as Salaok knew. And yet the footsteps which had been in the back of Salaok’s mind for most of the night had now stopped while Salaok put the last of his belongings in his bag. With the bag quickly flying up and swinging over his shoulder, he begins to leave until something stops him. A shadow A knowing of a presence. As Salaok stepped forward towards the doorway, those footsteps returned through the door. There he was. Face to face with a gun but that isn’t enough to put fear into Salaok’s heart. No, the fear came from the image of the man behind the gun which had been the man Salaok had been tormenting for the past 5 years. Now it was time. Salaok had known that one day he would push to far and Jamal would finally snap and today was that day. A fellow wrestler now holds a gun to Salaok’s head and there is no amount of talking that will get Salaok out of this one. "For years Salaok, years you have tortured my family and ruined every day of our life. No longer will I stand you destroying my family." Jamal pulls the trigger on the gun but nothing happens. "See Jamal, you can’t even kill me correctly. You’re still a failure, nothing you do is right otherwise you would have known that you have to take the safety off." At that point in time Salaok’s eyes widen as he realises what he has just told the man who holds a gun to his head. Click The safety is taken off. "Thanks" The deafening sound of the gun going off was all that was there in the memory as everything from that point ceased Salaok’s body snaps backwards with the force of the bullet shattering into his skull and out the back, slightly to the side and in a downward fashion. With the motor shut down, a car can only move down a hill on it’s own. With his brain shut down, Salaok could do nothing but fall to the ground in a pool of his own blood, his eye wide open. The arrogance which he had lived his life by had been the downfall as he couldn’t help but make fun of Jamal, and in turn tell him what to do. Was it a mistake or was it intentional as there wasn’t anything left to live for. Could Salaok really have allowed Jamal to win? With the motor shut down in a car, it can only move along flat ground when pushed or pulled by another force. With his brain shut down, Salaok only moved when a hand grabbed his leg and dragged him out of the locker room. Dead. Or Not With his hand pressing against his forehead, Salaok looks at Johnathon and the knowledge is there again. The knowledge that revenge is not only a dish best served cold, but best served by the one who was wronged in the first place. With a sinister grin on his face, Johnathon looks into the eyes of Salaok and gives a nod of satisfaction. The monster that he had created many years ago was slowly remembering what he needed to remember in order to be the man who would stand next to Johnathon again. Salaok was slowly remembering and this is what Johnathon needed. Control. "There are still other questions Johnathon." "You want to see the other picture don’t you? Not now Salaok, you have a journey ahead of you back to the tournament. But use the thoughts of Jamal during your next match and that fire that you once had is something you’ll need now. Michael will take you back home now." There was no room for arguing as Johnathon turns and walks away, dropping the picture of Jamal as he does so. Salaok looks at the image of the man who tried to kill him float to the floor and rest as his feet before he picks it up with hatred running rampant through his body. "There is a letter waiting for you at home Salaok." Johnathon says over his shoulder as he makes his way up the curved staircase. "A little reminder and of what you used to be." And with that Johnathon is gone and Salaok has nothing else to do but turn around and walk back to where the car used to be. With perfect timing, as Salaok’s foot hits the footpath, the car pulls around the corner and stops in front of Salaok. Tonight’s journey was only just beginning.
