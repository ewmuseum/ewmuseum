TTD - Invincibleness~! -v- Lunchroom Patrol

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What happens when you spend basically a year and a half straight on the road, going from show to show to wrestle for that one paycheck to cash and send home? What happens when you sleep in a Jeep every night and eat a protein bar every morning? How does it all build up on you? Ask Scott "Rampage" Matthews. Both members of Invincibleness~! were sitting there in the Waffle House, their steaming plates in front of them and utensils snatched up in their hands. Next to Rampage sat the diminuitive Alyssa Sanders, daintily picking at her salad and casting worried glances at Scott. The big man was basically oblivious to her concerns, keeping his attention focused on the plate of meat in front of him. Across the table, Invincible Gabe, aka Gabriel Brown, watched both of them over his dark sunglasses. Scott glanced over at Alyssa's serving and took an extra plate. He shovelled some of what passes for steak at Waffle House onto it andslid it over. "Have some protein, Aly, it's good for you," he said softly. She looked up at him and murmured what sounded like an agreement as the big man turned back to his meal. But if you knew how to look, you could see deep concern. Deep down, she knew something that troubled her incredibly. Scott was hurt. It was a mix of so many things. His constant, brutal travel schedule and wrestling every night. It was the incredibly hard amount of work he put into his ring performance. Even the revenge match he had with Violence Jack to avenge what had happened to Gabe. The man had several small muscle pulls and probably even tears all over his body. If someone paid attention to his ring work, they'd see he would go through stretches of only using one arm for lariats and such to compensate for whatever ailment might bother him. Scott never showed it, though. He was simply the big monster everyone was afraid of, even people bigger or crueler than him. He was Rampage, right? Hence, the power of the big man never wanted to give visible signs of being hurt, but Alyssa knew him, shall we say, intimately well, and knew here his hurts were. She had massauged many of them out of his body in their time together. Gabe, on the other hand, could simply tell from how his old friend moved. He could see the slightest limp and the attempts to cover it up. He knew what Scott's in-ring norms were, how his feet would plant, how his body would twist. Beneath the exterior of the bumbling duo of an untrained beast and a loudmouthed twerp, there was a team of incredible coordination and friendship. Even their big team finish was a result of knowing the other man's abilities and reaction times. So what came together in all of this? "Hey, Scott," Gabe said, casting a harsh eye at Alyssa, "what's the plan, big man? You gonna get a place to crash or what?" The question was basically a way to ask Scott how he was feeling after his match with V-Jack. "I'm going to get him a place," Alyssa said sternly. Scott shot her an unreadable look. "Scott, you know..." "Both of you quit," he growled around a mouthful of scrambled eggs. "I'm fine, okay? Jack ain't as rough as some people think." "I know how tough he is," Gabe replied. "You need to do something, Scott, I saw how hard he hit you." "Scotty," Alyssa put in, "You're not in great shape right now." "I am fine!" Scott ended up shouting. "I'm gonna go hit the crapper," he said, dropping his fork on an empty plate and forcing his big frame out of the booth seat. As Gabe and Alyssa watched him go, someone could almost hear the tempers flare. "You know, if you'd just let me talk to him," Gabe started, rounding on the woman, "he might see sense." "Oh, so I don't have any bearing?" she replied. "You think I don't have a clue how he ticks?" "I think someone that knew him ten years ago would have a better handle," he shot back. "Then again, you are sleeping with him. You knew how to manipulate people, didn't you? Maybe I shouldn't sell you short." Alyssa's eyes shone, her face a thin mask over the emotion welling up. "That's not how it is and you know it. That's what you're so afraid of!" Since no one else was in the Waffle House at the time, only the cook and waitress were trying to avoid looking at the fighting pair. Even not staring at them when they only sat eating with the monster was hard. "You listen to me," Gabe hissed across the table. "I am not putting up with some... with you trying to control my partner." "Dammit, Gabe," she replied, "I'm not trying to control him. I care about him." She stopped to take a breath. "We both know how hardheaded he is, right?" It was at that very moment that Mr. Brown got up from his seat and looked at Alyssa. “Listen, schemer. You are NOT coming between him and me. I’ve known AND dated people just like you. They pretend to be nice and sweet, but just turn out to be money-grubbing, good-for-nothing pansies. I mean I PLAY a retard on TV, but you…you just take the cake.” Alyssa only gasped as these words flew at her faster than any baseball a Major League Pitcher could throw. “How could you accuse me of such a thing? I did nothing to you.” “You did,” he quickly fired back. “You sit here and try to overshadow me. I’m his best friend and I’ll be damned if you decide to come around and mess with our rapport, especially when we have both RPW and a PTC title tournament to worry about!” “You’re worrying about phantoms, Gabe,” she tried to rationalize his jealousy any way she could, hopefully before Scott got back. “Bitch, I don’t believe in ghosts!” he screamed. “I DO believe in skeezers and if you aren’t going to leave, then I’ll park my happy ass on out of here. Good day!” He slammed a fist into the counter and marched heatedly out the door, leaving Alyssa all by her lonesome. She buried her face in her hands and started sobbing while the door slammed shut in the background. The employees who were there just sat idly by and watched the whole event transpire before their very eyes. A few moments went by as Scott came back. “Hey, Gabe, wanna get some more…what the hell?” The sight before him wasn’t good at all. Gabe: Missing. Alyssa: Crying. His food: Probably cold. Okay, the last one wasn’t all that important, but this couldn’t have been good at all. Scott ran over to Alyssa and held her tight. She cried into his arms and looked out the door. Gabe was long gone. "Where did Gabe go?" he asked to her in an unnatural quiet voice. Hey, he was a big guy; he normally sounded menacing. That and yelling didn’t seem to go over well with Alyssa, as seen earlier. She just looked up at him sadly and simply stated, "I don't know, Scotty, I don't know." Great, Scott thought to himself. Now I gotta contend with all of this. Right now, I could go for another V-Jack match. And on that note, we faded out.
