This is Bob's first match, or is it?

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 Promo Begin We once again fade in on the locker room of the Ric Austin Show. When we last left our...um...villians I guess, they were going to Wendy's to get something to eat so that they wouldn't starve to death. Also when we last left them they had not gotten back yet. Yea well now they have. Oh yea and they have also seen the two promos done by Tessa Windsor and the one done by Dawn McFarlaine. Yes a lot has happened since we last faded in on the Ric Austin Show. Look I can't help it we are only budgeted to do two promos and we can't waste them with showing you three idiots sitting on a couch making fun of promos done by other wrestlers. You get enough of that by watching the Critic on Comedy Central every Saturday. Well now that we have made a cheap plug for Comedy Central and set the scene I guess it is best that I let you listen into the promo. Austin: So now that we are back from Wendy's and we have already seen all three promos done by... Bob: Um...Ric, the narrator guy already said all that. Austin: Darn him. He is always taking all the good lines. Now what are we going to discuss? Derrick gives them both a look that says, "Well I remember Tessa said in her first promo that this is Bob's first match." Austin: So, what does that have to do with us doing a super cool promo that helps let everyone know how super cool Bob is? Bob: I think what he is trying to say is that this isn't my first match and that we should point that out. Austin: I thought this was your first match. Derrick gives Ric a look that says, "No don't you remember that match before Big brawl where Bob and I teamed up to take on Kevil and Dev?" Austin: Actually, I...no. Bob: I remember it like it was yesterday...yesterday....yesterday... The screen begins to get really waivey and then the flashback starts to happen. Suddenly we are taken back to May 2003 Derrick gives Bob a look that says, "Dude Bob please like save me and stuff." Bob: Derrick stand back I shall defeat these two losers. Come on Kevil and Dev take your beating like the loser you are. Kevil and Dev come running at Bob, but Bob just hits them each with standing hurricanranas. When Kevil gets up Bob easily hits him with a full nelson face buster. Bob then pins Dev as the ref counts... 1 2 3 Austin: Bob you won. You are my hero. I could never wrestle like you. I hope that one day I could be as good of a wrestler as you are. Bob: Don't worry Ric one day you will, one day you will. The screen begins to get waivey again and they it begins to to flashback to the present day where Bob is still staring off into space. Austin: What the hell was that? I hope to be as good as you some day? That never happened. That has to be the biggest crock of BS I've ever seen. I've been in numerous dream sequences too and none have ever been lacking that much reason. Bob: What are you talking about, that is exactly the way things happened. Derrick gives Bob a look that says, "Excuse me Bob, but I think Ric is right. I happen to remember that match alittle different than you just dreamed it. As I recall it happened alittle like this...like this...like this..." suddenly the screen begins to get waivey again as we go into yet another dream sequence type flashback. Once again we find ourselves in a Vortex ring in the month of May 2003. Derrick: Bob I think that I should start this match. Little buddy I would hate to see anything bad happen to you. I know that you are weak and that you are just a sissy manager while I am head of Ric Austin Show security. Bob gives derrick a smile that says, "okay Derrick you are the man, were as I am just a sissy litttle dork." Derrick: Ok little buddy just stay back and you should be safe. Derrick steps into the ring and as Kevil and Dev run at him, Derrick just clotheslines each of them. With one massive clothesline he has knocked out these two wrestlers. Derrick pinnes both wrestlers and the referee counts... 1 2 3!!!!! Derrick: I won!!!! I did it, I can't believe I finally won my first match!!!!! Bob gives derrick a smile that says, "Yes, now when I enter a cruiserweight tournament 1 year from now I can take all the glory. MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!" Suddenly the screen begins to go waivey once again and we are now flashed back to the present. Derrick is the same way Bob once was and he looks up and gives Bob a look that says, "And that is what REALLY happened little buddy." Bob: Were you too busy looking to see what really happened? That is in no way what happened. Your flashback has to be the most stupid flashback ever. Austin: Wait now guys there is one easy way to settle this and to find out who is right and who is wrong. Bob: Watch the video and have proof? Austin: Heck no, we can just do my flashback. And since I don't remember the match and am really just going on what you to told me, I think it happened alittle like this...like this...like this... The screen gets waivey, yet again, and we are taken back to the Vortex arena in May of 2003, yet again. Just be thankful that this is the last flashback. Anyway we see Bob and Derrick on their knees in the middle of the ring and they appear to be begging to Dev and Kevil Bob: Please Dev and Kevil spare us, we cannot wrestle because we suck. Though we both work for Ric Austin none of his greatness has rubbed off on us. Derrick gives them a look that says, "yes please spare us, we want to live." Kevil: I 'm sorry Bob The Temp and Derrick Miles, but by defeating the stooges of Ric Austin we will proove that we are not bigger losers than everyone thinks. Dev: We must now finish you before... before Dev can finish his sentence Ric Austin runs down the isle and slides into the ring. A golden glow surronds him and he is atleast twice as muscular as he really is. Kevil: Oh no it is Ric Austin, what should we do? Dev: We must run and save ourselves. Even in this tow on one situation we have no chance against the fury that is Ric Austin. The two flee the ring and leave through the crowd. Ric turns to the still kneeling Bob and Derrick. Austin: You two may rise up, they will not harm you. Bob and Derrick get up. Bob: Master we have failed you. Austin: Bob I forgive you, but just remember, I am strong, but one day I will not be there to protect you. The screen begins to get waivey and we fade back to the present where Ric is just smiling Bob: That did not happen!!!!! Austin: Sure it did, didn't you just see the flashback? Bob: Fine, that's it we are going to settle this once and for all. I'm getting the tape of the match and we are going to watch it. Bob gets a tape of the match and pops it into the VCR and we zoom in on the VCR. Dev takes the mic from Kevil. Dev: I don't care about you two. I'm a fighter, and I can break your bones any way I please. What I care about is Rujasu, making him feel pain. Making you people bay for blood, and then get rewarded with his, and seeing you jeer, but yet you will be satiated. Rujasu was never a true champion. He will not be one at Big Brawl 2... Dev drops the microphone and runs to the ring with Kevil. Dev picks up Bob The Temp as Kevil tries to unload on Derrick Miles, with very little success. The bell rings Tag Team Match: Derrick Miles and Bob The Temp v Kevil and Dev Mikey: Wow, these guys are all going at it before the match has even properly started. Lauwass: Of course it started, the bell rang. Suddenly the crowd pops, which is unusual seeing as all four men in the ring are fairly well hated. Mikey: It's Rujasu! Lauwass: Rujawho? Mikey: Rujasu! Lauwass: Rujujubee? Mikey: Don't start that again... Rujasu runs down to the ring and grabs Dev, hitting him with the R-Driver and dragging him out of the ring. Thw crowd continues to pop for this, even though Ric Austin is now coming to the ring also. Rujasu locks Dev in the Philly Cloverleaf, and pulls back on it. Dev struggles to break free, as Ric Austin enters the ring. Derrick Miles lifts up Kevil and hits him with a Chokeslam. Austin picks him up and hits a few Necktie Slams on him, leaving him trashed down on the floor. Mikey: It's chaos out there! Lauwass: Not as much as it will be later on, with the four way dance between The Anarchist, Fallen Fae ... and those other two losers. Referees come down to the ring and pull Rujasu off Dev, as well as getting Kevil to the back. Result: No Contest The camera zooms back in on the Ric Austin show and they all appear to be pretty amazed at the results of the video tape. Bob: Man that is just weird. We were all wrong about the match. I can't believe that. Austin: Speak for yourselves I was right you two were the ones who were wrong. Bob: What are you talking about? You said we were down on our knees begging for help. Austin: Exactly. You may not have been down on your knees but you did need my help and I did have to come down to the ring and save your loser tails from getting your buts kicked. Except fro a few minor details... Bob: Like us begging... Austin: I was getting to that. Except for a few minor details I was right and you two were wrong. Bob: Well either way, on Sunday you won't need to be there, because I know you have your tag match in XUW. So don't expect me to be begging for your help. Austin: Fine and don't expect me to be there. Lokk I need to cath a plane for my match this weekend Derrick is going to stay here with you. Ok? Bob: Fine I'll see you later good luck man. Austin: Good luck to you to. Ric Austin gets up off the couch and the two men shake hands. Ric walks the door and then leaves Derrick Miles and Bob the Temp as the promo fades to black. We then go to commercial as we go to the next promo. Promo end
