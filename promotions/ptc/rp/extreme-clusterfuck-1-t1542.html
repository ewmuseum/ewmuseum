Over here are my cupboards, well, they're kind of empty, because I haven't gotten time to run to the store, I have to maintain a busy schedule, plus I make next to nothing at these shows. He walks into his bedroom.) P.R.: Here's where some people say, "the magic happens." Yes, it's the master bedroom, it's small, but at least it's a damn bedroom. Here's my bed, small, oh look, a Dorito. This must of been from last night, man I ate so many Doritos my breath smelled like a cheese market. Here's another small TV over here. Now let me tell you about something more important, I am a valiant wrestler, I am one of the top guys in the XWF ranks, and now, it's time I take it to the big time, please, help me...hang on. Anyways, I hope the PTC sees me for who I really am, no not a poor man who lives in a crappy apartment with a bum living on the street corner, but for a valiant superstar who has dreams of making it and succeeding in the big interfed, just like PTC! Waiter: Hi, my name is Brad, I'll be your server today, and can I get you something to drink? P.R.: Get me an orange juice. Waiter: Coming right up. We rejoin him now, the time, 12:48 PM, the location, the local Gold's Gym, the scene, P.R. walks inside the Gold's Gym where he looks to get a workout.) P.R.: Here I am, and this next to me is my trainer Josh. So what are we doing today Josh? Josh: We're going to have a normal workout today, bench presses, weight lifting, and the treadmill. Josh: Great, you’re doing a great job. P.R.: Too easy, put some more weight on friend. Josh: Sure thing. (Josh adds 50 more pounds to the weights. Josh acts fast and gets it off of his chest.) Josh: Damn it P.R., how many damn times did I tell you not to be distracted by the ladies! C'mon, let's go do some more. P.R.: Screw that, I want some of that fine ass. Josh: What? I can't believe you, focused more on a woman's ass than your workout? How unbelieveable! P.R.: So what's next on the agenda? Josh: How about some sit-ups. (They both walk over to the mat. P.R. gets down and begins doing sit-ups with the greatest of ease.) Josh: 4...5...6...7...you're doing fantastic! Keep going! (He keeps doing sit-ups, when, again, he sees a fine looking woman.) P.R.: Look at that fine chick over there. Josh: We're not focused on that; we're focused on doing sit-ups, now go! Cynthia: Remember, call me! P.R.: Umm, uhh, sure thing baby. (P.R. takes his camera and walks out the door.) P.R.: Wow, that was cool, I got a woman's number! I think she's hot for me! You think she would want to...of course she does, the ladies love P.R.! Well, must run, see you all later, speaking of later; I hope to get me some action later.
