Oh, Larry, What Did You Do Now?

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Back when the New Royal Legion was dominating the tag team ranks in XUW and were menaces to the XUW administration with their rampant conspiracy theories, there was one name that was always associated with Bailey and Danyon (The New Royal Legion) and that was name of their manager, Larry the Legionnaire. Many considered him the mastermind behind some of the conspiracy claims that the New Royal Legion used to initiate on the XUW Head Office. Nowadays, Larry isn’t so much looked upon with such high regard any longer. He’s nothing more than a comedy sidekick that people like to crack jokes about. Something, somewhere along the way happened to Larry that essentially caused him to have these illusions of grandeur and essentially lose his mind. What happened to him? No one really knows for sure but one day he woke up believing he was the best wrestler ever from Antarctica. Some people suggest that it happens just from hanging out with the likes of Bailey and Danyon and dealing with conspiracy theories every other day. This caused Larry to crack and lose his mind as buckled under the pressure of dealing with the demands of The New Royal Legion. Others believe he followed the path of one of his other friends, “The Bastard” Al Crud. Crud was once the greatest wrestler in all of the land and is a legend around XUW. But he is now a mere shadow of himself and is the very definition of a jobber as he hasn’t won a singles match in ages. These people believe that Larry wanted to follow the path of his friend Al Crud and become a jobber like him. Originally, a lawyer by trade, Larry became a fan of the New Royal Legion. In fact he was their first fan they ever had. He was the original Legionnaire and the figurehead of the Legionnaires, the cult following of the New Royal Legion, which is now a million and one strong. He was the brains behind the creation of the L.O.S.T. Titles (the Legionnaires Only Significant Tag Titles) when the New Royal Legion went on strike in XUW because of unfair working conditions. He was also the lawyer for the New Royal Legion when they sued XUW over conspiring to keep them down. Larry was also behind one of the abductions of Bailey and Danyon, although many do not know this fact. So, in other words, Larry was once a bright and highly suspected manager/lawyer of the New Royal Legion family but things have gone in an entirely different direction lately. Nowadays, Larry isn’t much of a lawyer or a manager any more. He spends most of his days hanging out with his best friend, Stripes the Penguin, which is an actual penguin from Antarctica. Larry felt that he needed something to remind him of home so he went out and purchased a penguin to hang out with. Of course Larry really isn’t from Antarctica as he was born and raised in Canada so being from home is nothing more than him still furthering the tale of being from Antarctica. Some days the two of them hang out pool side with other Legionnaires. Other days they hang out in a hotel room that has been renovated and cooled to a brisk negative 5 degrees Celsius so that they can feel more like at home in Antarctica. And then there are days when Larry goes to the gym and pretends that he is a wrestler and trains there like he is one. Ironically, despite the fact that Larry is not from Antarctica and that he is not a real wrestler he is still probably the greatest wrestler from Antarctica for the reason being there has never been a wrestler from Antarctica before. So, Larry really isn’t lying when he claims this. ************ The scene opens up into the landmark Howard Johnson Hotel, located in downtown XUWville. It’s a landmark in XUWville because it has been the home to the New Royal Legion and countless Legionnaires over the past two years. Outside of the hotel in the parking lot is usually where the Legionnaires camp out and over the years it has transformed into a makeshift home for them. Most days they sit around the campfire and sing songs in honour of Bailey and Danyon waiting to catch a glimpse of their heroes. Today the Legionnaires are outside enjoying a nice September day, maybe one of the last nice days of an unusually chilly summer in XUWville. They are sitting around the pool, relaxing and working on their tans. Well, not all million and one of the Legionnaires are sitting around the pool as not all million and one are staying at the hotel. The Legionnaires are a worldwide phenomenon and live in all parts of the world but many of them about 10,000 have congregated to the Howard Johnson Hotel to be closer to their heroes. Relaxing poolside amongst the Legionnaires is the original Legionnaire himself, Larry along with his sidekick and best friend, Stripes the Pengiun. Stripes is wading in the water to help keep cool in the warm weather, while Larry is sitting in a chair next to him so he can watch over his good friend. Although, Larry isn’t sun tanning today, instead he is wearing what he calls traditional Antarctican clothing, which constitutes him wearing a heavy parka and winter pants. For once, the legends Bailey and Danyon are out swimming in the water amongst their cult followers. Bailey swims over to where Larry and Stripes are positioned in the pool. “How’s it going there, Larry? Why don’t you come in the water and go for a swim seeing as it’s such a beautiful day out?” “No, it’s okay Bailey. That water is way too warm for me. I prefer mine a lot colder and with ice as that is what I have in my native land. Plus, I have to save my energy for I have a big match coming up soon.” “A big match, what do you mean a big match? Mister Webster hasn’t booked you in an XUW match for quite some time now. You aren’t on the active roster anymore.” “I know that but I’m not in an XUW match.” Bailey stops swaying in the water a bit and is taken back a bit from Larry’s comment. “So, how are you in a match then?” “You remember last March when I was in the PTC Cruiserweight Tournament……” “Yeah, I remember that. That was the last time you wrestled in a singles match and you lost because of Al Crud farting and distracting the ref while you pinned your opponent.” “Yes, that’s right.” “I’m still a bit confused here Larry. What are you getting at?” “I have been thinking that I was screwed out of rightfully winning that match and I think I need to avenge my loss.” Bailey nods his head but is still a bit confused as to what Larry is getting at or talking about. “So, did you arrange to meet the person who beat you in that tournament in an interfed match? Actually I thought he was an independent now so I guess there is nothing stopping you from wrestling him.” “I did one better, Bailey. I entered the GTT4.” “Oh, okay you entered the GTT4,” Bailey says not really clueing in to exactly what Larry meant by that statement. Slowly, Bailey realizes what Larry meant and his eyes widen and raises his voice, “You did what? You entered the GTT4?” Stripes the Penguin suddenly stops swimming in his little circle and looks over at Bailey as he hears him raising his voice and realizes that something is up. Bailey turns around in the water and yells at Danyon to get over here. Danyon stops flirting with a few lady Legionnaires and swims his way over to where Bailey, Larry and Stripes are located. “Danyon, did you know that Larry entered the GTT4?” “No, I didn’t know that. You seriously entered it Larry?” “Yes, Danyon I entered the GTT4. I really fail to see the big deal about it. So, I entered a tournament. I was in one in the past. You guys weren’t entering into it so I thought I would toss my name in the hat and give it another try. You two have never entered a tournament as singles wrestlers so I thought I would. So, remember that, I’m the one with tournament experience around here.” Larry leans back in his chair looking pretty confident on the side of the pool at given his reason as to why he entered the GTT4 Bailey and Danyon are not quite so confident as they both have baffled looks on their faces as they look at each other and shake their heads in disbelief. “Larry,” Danyon says as he realizes somehow had to point out this fact, “you have a dismal winning record. You’ve won 2 or 3 matches in your whole entire XUW career. And those victories weren’t against the most challenging opponents might I add. Definitely, at the very least not the caliber as someone who has entered this tournament. The best of the best enter the GTT4 Larry. And quite frankly you aren’t the best of the best. To be blunt, you are without a doubt the worst wrestler in tournament. I have no doubt about that without even looking at the brackets. Lest you forget Larry, you aren’t even a real wrestler……” “What do you mean I’m not a real wrestler? I’m the best wrestler ever from Antarctica. There are going to be wrestlers from all over the world in this thing and maybe some from other worlds as well. I heard there is someone from Atlantis that has entered this tournament. Atlantis, guys. If someone is representing Atlantis than someone has to be there to represent Antarctica and since I’m the best there is, why not me?” “But Larry, you’re not even………” Danyon doesn’t bother finishing that statement and instead decides to burry his head in his hands as neither Danyon nor Bailey ever really acknowledge Larry’s belief that he is a wrestler from Antarctica. “I don’t know why I even bother sometimes.” “To be honest guys, I think that being from Antarctica will help me out in this tournament and give me an advantage over my opponent. And, also since you are from New Zealand Danyon you can be a big help to me too.” “Why’s that Larry?” “You see the tournament is divided up into brackets………” “Most tournaments are Larry, you do realize that?” “I know that. I’m the one with singles tournament experience, remember. Anyways, with the GTT this time the brackets are divided up by mountain chains and my mountain chain is the Flinders chain. So that means I’m going to be wrestling on the Flinders Mountain chain, which I think is in New Zealand and that’s how you can be of assistance to me Danyon. You can tell me all about the mountains therefore I can scout out the best locations to have the wrestling match.” Bailey and Danyon stand in the water motionless thinking about what Larry just said until Bailey looks over at Stripes and then turns and faces Danyon. “Should I take this one Danyon?” “Go right ahead.” Bailey turns back to face Larry on the side of the pool. “Um, Larry, I’m not really sure how to break this to you but just because you are in the Flinders bracket does not mean you are fighting on that mountain. That’s just what they chose to name the brackets instead of the old A, B, C, etc. Think about it you don’t wrestle on a giant A if you are in the A bracket. They probably just wanted to something unique and decided to name them off of mountain ranges as a nice change.” “Oh crap. That’s not good. Here I was thinking that all of my experience growing up in Antarctica would have been an advantage since the conditions of wrestling on a snow covered mountain would have resembled living and growing up in Antarctica. I’d have been used to the cold, snow and possible white out conditions but other wrestlers would have had to adapt to it as they wouldn’t be accustomed to it.” “Sorry, to break it to you Larry but you will be fighting in a normal squared circle like all of your other matches that you did in the past.” “Another thing Larry, the Flinders Range isn’t even in New Zealand,” as Danyon corrects his geography. “It’s in Australia. So, I wouldn’t have been able to help you even if you were wrestling on that mountain.” “Oh.” Bailey then speaks up and asks another question to Larry. “I just don’t get why you really entered it without asking us. You rarely do anything without asking us first. When did you become such a rebel? You normally do everything by the book.” “I thought that since you weren’t entering it I would. I thought someone should go in there and recognize the Legionnaire family and Stripes is a penguin so I doubt they’d allow him in so I entered it. And besides there is nothing wrong with being a rebel. Rebels can be good people. Luke Skywalker was part of the Rebellion in those Star Wars movies and he’s a hero.” Danyon chuckles, “yeah but Larry, those were movies and were fake. This is wrestling there is nothing fake about it.” “I know that but……” Larry slowly sinks down into his chair and starts to slouch a bit. He begins to realize that maybe, just maybe this wasn’t the brightest of ideas that he has done. Maybe he was in over his head entering in this tournament. Larry looks over at Bailey and Danyon who are still there in front of him just watching the changing expressions on his face. He looks over at Stripes the Penguin who is wading the water and keeping himself entertained while the three of them discuss Larry’s entrance into the GTT. Bailey sighs as he realizes the visible change in emotion in Larry, “who are you facing Larry? Perhaps Danyon and I can be of assistance in some ways. I did hold a few single titles in XUW after all.” Larry’s face lights up with a smile. “He’s a King. I can’t wait to defeat some royalty.” “A King? From where?” Bailey asks. “I’m not sure exactly where he is from but I believe he wrestles for NGW and his name is Scott King.” Bailey shakes his head once more, “Larry, just because his last name is King doesn’t necessarily mean that he is a King. My last name is Bailey and I’m not a bottle of Bailey’s Irish Cream.” “But I’m a Legionnaire and that’s in my name. I just assumed that he was a King.” “Yes, but you are Larry the Legionnaire. He isn’t Scott the King, he’s just plain old Scott King. That’s a big difference you know.” “Oh. That makes sense. I was all excited thinking that I was going to fight a real King.” Danyon speaks lowly to himself for a second, saying Scott King’s name over a few times as if he is trying to think of where he heard it from. “You know Scott King sounds really familiar. I think I remember seeing once where he was World Champ in NGW. You’re facing a World Champ, Larry, do you realize that?” “A World Champ? So what? I’m the best wrestler from Antarctica. I’m the best wrestler on a whole continent not just one fed.” “You’re also the only wrestler from Antarctica, Larry. I think what Danyon is hinting Larry is that you might be in over your head in this one,” Bailey says as looks over in the general direction of Danyon. “When you entered the Cruiserweight Tournament it was more or less because I forced you to enter in it as a punishment. Larry, Danyon and I want to look out for your health here. You are the first Legionnaire, therefore you are special to us. We’ve done some things in the past that perhaps we shouldn’t have but we were able to watch over you and protect you. We were there for you when you wrestled those matches before, we knew what was going on and who you were facing. I don’t know much about this Scott King guy but if he was some World Champion a PTC fed then chances are he is pretty good.” Larry keeps his eyes fixed on Bailey even though he has since stopped speaking. Slowly he turns his head and looks over in the direction of Danyon who is nodding his head in agreement as to what Bailey was speaking about. “Listen Larry,” Danyon says, “it’s not that Bailey and I don’t want you to wrestle but you are not a real wrestler. It is fine when you do those joke matches in XUW, like the other week when you won the Hardcore Title in that Hardcore Invitational on the Anniversary Show but lost it about 1 minute later. We were there to make sure that nothing happened to you. We knew what was going to happen when you went down there. Mind you getting pinned by a Cardboard Cutout of Chase Wilson wasn’t in the plans but hey that’s XUW for you.” Bailey interjects, “I think you should resign from the tournament Larry. I think you might be over your head here. It’s all fun and games with you pretending to be this great wrestler from Antarctica and all. But we all know that’s just a gimmick. I think it’s time to go back to being the lawyer that you once were.” “But …..but…..but……I am from Antarctica and I am a wrestler. I…….” Bailey shakes his head diligently no. “Larry I think you are really in too deep here. I think you should pull out why you can. There is no harm in that. You aren’t going to damage the name of the New Royal Legion and the Legionnaires. It’s okay Larry. For your own health, I think you should resign from the GTT4. I don’t want you getting hurt. Danyon doesn’t want you to get hurt. The other million Legionnaires don’t want you to get hurt……and…….and…..” Bailey turns his head to look at Stripes. He smiles at Stripes. “And most importantly Stripes doesn’t want you getting hurt.” Stripes the Penguin looks up at Bailey as for whatever reason Stripes is able to recognize his name when someone speaks. He hears Bailey say his name and looks at him and then he looks over at Larry who is staring back at him with a sad face. “But….but…….” Larry then gives up. He realizes that it is no use arguing with Bailey and Danyon. They will never allow him to fight in the tournament. “Fine, I will send in my resignation tomorrow morning then guys. I won’t fight in the tournament.” Larry looks towards both of them and sees that they are nodding as they approve of the move. Bailey acknowledges Larry’s decision, “I think that it’s for the best Larry that you step down in the morning.” Changing the subject, “now why don’t you take off that heavy parka and hop in the water and go for a swim. Help you relax a bit.” “Okay……I’ll go for a swim but just this once. I find that water way too warm.” Bailey and Danyon turn around and dive back under water and swim back out to the deep end of the pool. Larry sits in his chair still watching them swim away to go and flirt with the female Legionnaires. Larry starts to unzip his parka and takes it off, followed by his winter pants and the rest of his winter clothing. Underneath all that layering he has a pair of swim trunks. Slowly Larry takes some steps into the pool. “This is like a hot tub,” he remarks as he makes his way into the water. Larry wades his way over to where Stripes is playing in the water by bobbing up and down. Larry puts his hand out and pats the penguin on his head. He looks at him and then looks back in the general direction of Bailey and Danyon and then back to Stripes. “Stripes buddy, I’m going to let you in on a little secret. I’m going to fight that match. I’m not going to resign. For once I’m going to defy Bailey and Danyon and prove to them that I am a great wrestler by entering the GTT4 and winning it. You just wait and see Stripes. Just wait and see.”
