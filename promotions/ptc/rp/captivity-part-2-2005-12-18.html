Captivity Part 2

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 So I'd left the pathetic bastard in his room. I wonder what he's thinking, alone in the dark, his only companion on a forty-five. Maybe he's thinking about ending it, after all, he was about to die when I pulled him out from in front of that car. In a way, I almost hope he's pulled the trigger. After all, if he wants to die so damn bad there really isn't much I could work with anyway. Of course that'd mean I'd have to find someone else. He'd better not be dead. If I'm going to make Stevens into the man he should be, I'm going to need him to be with me on it. I can't do all the work myself. God knows that'd be easier though. If he isn't with me a hundred percent all this is going to be for nothing, and I'm going to have to find someone else. Fuck wouldn't that be a bother, all my hard work down the toilet for the first asshole in the history of assholes who actually wants to die, like, for real. Fuck. I should probably check on him. I swear to god, if he's dead already, I'm going to kick his ass. **************************************************************************** "Don't pull the trigger, squeeze. That will insure a kill, a kill is what you want. A kill is why we breed." ^1 He muttered the words, over and over again. A little mantra too himself, the lyrics of some song. He thought it could have been Rancid, maybe Guttermouth. He wasn't exactly sure, but he knew it was some song he'd enjoyed listening to. Although that was his old life, the one he'd left behind. The one he may never see again. It's so easy, wrap your lips around the barrel, close your eyes, squeeze the trigger. A loud noise, your mouth fills with the taste of gun oil. Your lungs sear for a split second, and then it's all over. At least that's what he imagined it being like, he really couldn't tell for sure. It's not like you really ever hear about what it's like to blow your own head off, those who do rarely stick around long enough to give a detailed account of those last few seconds. Since his self proclaimed Guardian Angel had left, he'd begun considering his options, changing up the game plan. Before he had simply been waiting to die, without hope, filled with a very vague dread. He didn't imagine starving to death would be a pleasant way to go really, and beyond that he wasn't exactly sure what he expected out of the after life. Never really a religious man he hadn't put a great deal of stock in the whole Heaven and Hell thing, they were just words to him. Although recent events had made him reconsider somewhat. He still wasn't exactly sure what he believed, but he knew if there was a hell, he didn't want to go there, even though he was fairly certain it was where he belonged. After all he'd killed a man right? Murders don't go to the good place, and least that was his understanding. Of course exceptions could be made to every rule right? It's not like he'd really meant to kill the kid, it's just something that had happened. If God was so great and forgiving, surely he'd be able to see that, wouldn't he? Yeah right. The pure and simple fact was that Michael Stevens was a murderer. He was going to hell, if in fact he wasn't already there. He'd come to this realization only a few hours ago. It left him cold inside, kind of like a sick hollow feeling of emptiness. So this was really it, a complete and utter lack of hope. In fact when he thought back, the only thing that had really held him back from pulling the trigger before had been the hope he might escape, but even that was gone now. He couldn't reach the windows and after checking the gun out, there was only one bullet. Too much chance involving trying to shoot the lock off the door. If he missed, he'd just be left to starve, and that wouldn't be pleasant. So really what was there left to hope for, there was no miracle coming, god had forgotten him. Well, maybe not forgotten, he'd cared enough to send this "Angel" into his life. That was something after all, malice perhaps. The truth was, god hated him, and wanted to see him punished, in life. The worst thing was knowing that if the bad place really did exist this crap on earth wasn't going to be able to hold a candle to what he was going to be going through soon enough. All he had to do was pull the trigger. NO, squeeze the trigger. That insured the kill right? What he wanted even less than starving was lying on the ground a big gaping hole in his throat bleeding out. White hot agony, gasping for breath, choking on his own blood. The thought made him shiver, he set the gun back down. What if he missed? Was it better to put the gun in your mouth, or under the chin? The temple should work? Right? Put the gun to your chest over the heart? What was the best way to do it? He couldn't decide, maybe starving was better than the alternative after all. Muscle tissue eaten away to power your body. Stomach swelling. A walking talking skeleton, barely strong enough to lift up your own skull. What did that feel like? Would it be like someone setting a fire in your belly as you devoured yourself from the inside out? That couldn't be pleasant could it? Maybe it was better to risk missing, that'd probably be quicker… He couldn't decide, his mind running around in circles, chasing its own tail. Figured he'd go out like this, wracked with indecision. Fitting irony for a man that had always been so sure of himself. So confident in every decision. Taking great risks only because he knew things would work out all right. He was the man that was never wrong, always right. And now he couldn't even decide the way he wanted to die. Fucking figures. Curling up into a tight little ball, he counted the seconds. It was his other little game, the one he had played before he started thinking about the gun. It kept his mind occupied, until the numbers grew so large they all began to run together. He'd always lose track somewhere in the thousands, and he'd have to start back up again. From the beginning, ground floor at number one, two, three. These are the things you think about while you're waiting to die. The seconds passing, what it feels like to die. Is there really a god, does heaven exist? If it does, are you going to make it in? Every second an eternity that passes in a heart beat, a blink that stretches for eons. Your life is over, your body just hasn't figured it out yet. He thinks, maybe that should tell him something. If the body hasn't given up, why should he? There's always a way, always a chance that he could get out. It's possible right? Wrong. "It's over." The gun's like ice too the touch, he feels his own warmth leaching away just from the contact. Doesn't matter, it'll heat up soon enough. Then he'll never be cold again, hell's supposed to be pretty damn hot. At least that's always what he'd heard. Then again there might not be anything there, and he'd just be cold forever, lost in the hollow emptiness of death. That could prove to be cold. Oh well, he was tired of thinking. Tired of waiting, tired of being tired. Tired of being hungry, thirsty, tired of being cold. He couldn't take it anymore, if he missed he missed. At least there'd be that bright little flash, a small little blaze of glory for a star burning out. At least he could romanticize a gunshot. Starvation, not so easily. Amazingly he didn't want to gag as the barrel came to rest on the back of his tongue. He thought he might, that had worried him a bit. Gagging as he pulled the trigger, no squeezed the trigger, the convulsion making his shoot off his cheek or something. A nice little none lethal and very painful wound. That'd be just his luck. Easily the hammer came back. His eyes closed, a prayer came to his lips. Well it would have, but the gun barrel wouldn't really allow him to speak. He just thought it, something he hadn't done for a good long time. He asked to be forgiven, not just for Lance, but for everything else. His life had been a never ending series of sin and depravity, and for the first time in his is life he realized there was more he regretted in life than he was proud of, typical. Already knowing the answer, promised god that if something happened, if he didn't die here, if he got free he'd make himself a better man. He'd right his wrongs, he'd become the man he was supposed to be. All he needed was a second chance, no a millionth chance, he'd already used up the rest. This time was different though, he really meant it. It was in that second as he began to squeeze, the sound of metal scrapping against metal filled the room. He couldn't be it, here it was, deliverance. His one chance, maybe his last chance. "Thank you, thank you, thank you, thank you, thank you, thank you." He whispered to himself as lips closed, now free of the intrusive gun barrel. With a shaking hand he pointed it towards the door. This was it, in this very second he had what's known as a moment of clarity. He could either get busy living, or get busy dying. ******************************************************************************** I think I can hear him moving. Maybe it's just my imagination, but I think he's talking to himself. Good boy not dead yet, maybe there's some fight in you after all. Come through this, and you're going to be a better man. Maybe you're finally ready. My eyes are really good in the dark. I've never been able to explain it, but I can see like a cat at night. As the door opens, I see him laying there, the gun's in his mouth. Bastard if he pulls that trigger it's all over. Can't show weakness though. Can't make the bastard think I actually want him to live. He's got to figure all this out on his own. Wait, what's he doing now, the gun's coming out of his mouth, pointing towards….. ********************************************************************************* If he wasn't already on the ground he knows it would've knocked him over. The thing kicked like a mule, and he wondered for a second if it had torn his arm off. Everything for that split second was too bright, his eyes stung, he must be deaf from too much sound too quickly. He never heard a scream, but he couldn't hear anything but the ringing. Adrenaline drug him up to his feet, heart nearly ready to explode from his chest he ran. Tripping his elbows ripped open against the ground, it was hot, wet sticky. BLOOD, his mind screaming, and pulling himself back up he ran again. He'd done it, this was his escape. He was going to be free, and for the second time in his life he'd killed a man. And right now, he didn't care. ********************************************************************************** "THAT'S MY BOY!" ~Fin~ ^1: Lyrics from "The Decline" NOFX.
