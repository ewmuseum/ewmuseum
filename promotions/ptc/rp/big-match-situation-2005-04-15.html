Big Match Situation

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 A man with bright red hair is standing in a middle of a ring which makes his identity unmistakable. If that's not enough he's wearing his old WMW T-Shirt Fred Grest I am The Show T-Shirt. I watch the kid take a few repeated back bumps and he does them well. I look at the skinny ass kid who can be no taller then 5'7. "Pretty good kid how long have you been here?" "Not long only a few weeks." "Not bad John will take good care of you. He's the one who trained me and I'm sure he'll make something of you" "He trained you? Will you stop here often" "I'll stop here once in awhile" "Great" I can tell by the look on the kids face that he was a huge fan of mine. I don't know how long he's known me hopefully before my WMW stint. I'll be happy to stop back here every now and then but nothing more. "Alright I'll show you some of the basics." "Excuse me!" "This is a ..." "Excuse me!" I look around to see who is interrupting me and then I see him. Dante... a man who I've been in the ring with before but like most of my rivals I've never beaten. I know I can beat Dante but since our last encounter he won't step into the ring with me again. It wasn't 1 on 1 otherwise I would have beaten him. Well I think I would..... Can I? The tall spaniard steps into the ring never taking his eyes off me. He was a great ring general. He just stares me down and then finally cracks a smile. No, not a glad too see you smile a disgusted smile as if he has shamed himself by stepping into the same ring with me. I feel like everyone that I've gone toe to toe with as of late have felt this way about me. I guess that's why I entered CW2 hoping to change that. I doubt it in GTT4 I was simply lucky to get as far as I did. It was over as soon as I stepped into the ring with Odium he was simply more confident. I never lacked that before WMW and now that I'm in GW I lack it even more. I never show it ofcourse. I simply hide my doubt behind a mask of jokes and confidence. "You really think you're going to teach these kids anything?" I nod my head in agreement and he rolls his eyes. "Fred... you've been a joke since the day I met you. You are a disgrace to this business and anything your involved in." The anger in his voice sacred the 16 and 17 year old kids in the area. They knew by the one in his vouce that anything could happen. "Is this why you came here? To mock me? I don't have time for this Dante" I wasn't about to get in a fight now. It was too close to my match with Enrique Moraless. "Coward" He said this sharply trying to get under my skin and I won't admit it to him bit it was working. I look at him with intensity in my eyes. "You know you won't win Grest. You never win the big match one including a blown title shot against me." I'm doing my best to remain but jackasses like this make it so hard. It'd be so easy to Freddy Kick him and get over with but then again Dante always has his guard up. "You won't even get to the big one in this tournament. You'll be a 1st round exit but don't worry half the people who entered will be. There is only one small difference between them and you Grest." I really do hate this man and he has me on the verge of snapping. "What's that?" With a slight chuckle in his voice "They have talent" "Get out" I replied sharply. Dante smirks "Alright just remember when I leave this building I'll go and win another title and you'll just leave" "GO!" I yell. He chuckles and steps out of the ring and showing is back to me for the 1st time walking out of the building. I am watching him the entire way making sure he leaves. As he pushes open the door and exit I breath a sigh of relief. I can't help but think he's write and I'll blow this chance to make a name for myself just like every other time. It doesn't matter if its Justin Evers or PTC's Extreme Champion Shadow I will always blow the big match. If I haven't I'd be a huge name around these parts. I was meant to be so much more but yet I am so ordinary.
