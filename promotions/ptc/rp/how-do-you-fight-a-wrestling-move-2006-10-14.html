How do you fight a wrestling move?

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 It has been a long time since planet Earth was graced by the presence of a being powerful enough that not only does he have Buddha and Mohammed, but also God on his speed dial. After a long hiatus, where he was forced to go back to his home planet Zebular IV, the self proclaimed "savior of all things human-like" … "Self-proclaimed?!", comes a wheezy and nasally voice. "Wheezy and nasally?! How dare you insult the magnificent voice of the GREATEST Mindtaker to ever grace any and all universes he has been to!" As I was stating before I was interrupted by the, erm… greatest mind taker to ever grace countless universes, the Savior of all things human-like has returned. Indeed, after about a year hiatus, Mentok the Mindtaker has returned to planet Earth… "Narrator~!, tell them why I have returned!" Again, if I had not been interrupted by Mister Impatient here, maybe I could narrate the story and explain to all those who stare in awe, with their jaws down to the floor, "TELL THEM, I COMMAND THEE!", Mentok shouts, interrupting the all powerful Narrator in his speech, yet again. In a galaxy far away from the one that human beings inhabit, Mentok received word of a tournament called the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. But it was not this tournament that brought him back, oh no. In fact, just as I narrate this for you, Mentok shakes his head in disapproving manner, so that all can know that it is not only I, the great NARRATOR~!, whom is making this up. No, in fact, it was the number 6 that followed the three initials of G.T.T that brought him back. "Indeed it was!", Mentok shouts, "and I shall explain to you all why it was this number that brought me back." Here he is, in all of his green glory, Mentok the Mindtaker. Sitting down in Indian position, floating above a chair (just because he can, and you cannot), Mentok the Mindtaker plays with a human brain that has been planted at the end of a pencil. His eyes are heavy, and he looks bored as he rests his head in his free hand. There is a reason for Mentok’s boredom however… "Oh yes there is, you spotlight hogging invisible Narrator! You see, this is one of the reasons I left. I was hoping that upon coming back, you would have disappeared. It obviously wasn’t meant to be, was it?" Obviously not Mentok, obviously not. "May I enlighten my legions of fans now mister Narrator? … Silence?! Great! Now, as my narrator was explaining, it was the number 6 that brought me back to Primetime Central. Well, that and the demise of the UWF. I was watching MIND-TELEVISION~! On my planet, and I heard news of the sad fate UWF had encountered. Oh gosh, I wish I had never left" Mentok is positive that it is his fault that the UWF has to close its doors. He believes that they never could handle the loss of his magnificent presence. Moving on… Mentok steadied himself, and then continued on with his nasally discharges. "Number 6 is wonderful, I mean, it’s got a loop, and a little twist! And that is why I came back!" Mmmm, Mentok was never one to have profound reasons for living. After all, he will live after you all are gone. And even then, he will be kicking ass and… and… At this moment in time, Mentok is picking his nose, and he is unaware that I have given him his cue. His eyes widen as he retracts his long finger from his nose, with a startled look on his face. And so we will start again. Mentok will come back to kicking asses and… "OOOOOOOOOOOOH WEEEE UHHHH OOOOOOOOOOH, TAKING MIIIIIIIIIINDS!" Mentok shouts before breaking into a fit of laughter reminiscent of Doctor Evil after he has hatched a diabolical yet stupid plan. "Narrator, may you enlighten me on who it is that I will be ‘rasslin?" Mentok’s mind isn’t really on wrestling, nor is it on his return to planet Earth. Recently, Mentok made some very bad decisions when he thought that he had gotten rid of me; THE ALL POWERFUL NARRATOR~! Just as I am reporting this to your television sets and minds, Mentok is shaking his head from side to side. See, he refuses to believe that he has made a mistake. While he floats towards his super high-tech refrigerator to get galactic space milk, I, the Narrator!, will let you in on his mistake. "GOD DAMN IT! Lemme ask you some’thin’ you old piece of crap Hoover! WHY DON‘T YOU LOVE ME ANYMORE?" came a heavily accented Texan voice. Along with the voice is Mentok’s mistake. You see, when Mentok fled planet Earth and thought he had rid himself of me, he hired a new companion. Mentok is unable to be alone for more than three seconds and a half, and so, he hired a janitor for his galactic ship. His name is Roy, and he is a forty three year old ex-con with unmentionable tattoos everywhere on his arms. Rumor has it that he once wanted to tattoo a vagina on the tip of his nose, but his wife, Samantha May refused to model hers for his tattoo artist. So yeah, no vagina on his nose. "Which is a damn shame Narrator!" Mentok shouts "I mean, how amusing would it be to always see a vagina whenever we looked into his eyes…Wait a second…" Mentok pets his perfectly trimmed pinch and his eyes narrow, as if he has a very good idea. "Quite correct Narrator! I do have a great idea! What if I were to use my all-mighty powers to replace the location of women’s vaginas, and put them on their noses?! That way, desperate human beings could actually use the excuse that they tripped and fell in! Oh yes, nose to nose penetration, that sounds like a mighty plan!! MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH" Errr, ok? "I like vaginas," Roy says as he drags his mistress into clear view. His "mistress" is not one that you would typically imagine. In f act, she is not even a she! Remember when I was telling you Roy wanted a vagina tattooed on his nose? Did you understand that he has some troubles that he hasn’t dealt with in a while? Well one of those troubles includes his relentless attraction to his janitorial vacuum machine. Yes yes, Hoover as he calls "her" is his mistress. Of course, Samantha May has no idea that she is being cheated on. "Ey Mentok, lemme ask you some’thin’, did you ever meet up with that friend of yers?! Y’know, the one you told me about when ya hired me?" "And you mean, long times ago, before I fired you about a million times?!" "Yeh, hahaha, you funny green bastard! Ya never fired me" Mentok is confused and aggravated. The truth is, he tried to fire Roy one day in his new contract. While Mentok was sleeping… "I DON’T SLEEP, I REGENERATE!" Mentok shouts while looking up at the ceiling …while Mentok was regenerating, Roy came in to clean the floors of his private quarters, but did not come alone. Roy brought his three children, Samantha May, his two illegitimate sons that eh claims he fathered while in prison, and a dog with a crooked eye that had a weird tendency to give the stink eye to Mentok. Since then, Mentok has adopted the dog, and frequently has staring contests with him. "His name is Crooky! And you are jealous of him, aren’t you Narrator!" "Damn it Hoover! How the hell am I s’posed to have half-man half-machine children if you won’t lend me yer god damned ovaries?!" "ROY, YOU’RE FIRED!!!!" "HAHAHAHAHAHA, come on Hoover, let’s go try and make some good ole’ lovin’" and with that, Roy walks off, carting his love machine towards situations and places that as a Narrator, I don’t even want to be aware of. "So Narrator, can I know who I will be ‘rasslin?! I asked you earlier" The Mindtaker is not very focused on his match as I mentioned earlier. Mentok is very focused on trying to figure out a way to get rid of his janitor, Roy. Indeed, not only can Roy not hear the mighty voice of the Narrator that Mentok loves so much, but quite frankly, he is disruptive. He farts all the time, loses his hair in the most inappropriate of places and is trying to get freaky with an old vacuum machine. He will be facing DDT in the very first round of the GTT tournament. A wrestler that has made a name for himself in his home federation of XUW and is hungry to make one in Primetime Central. Unfortunately for DDT, he is not the smartest of individuals that Mentok has faced. DDT thinks that Mentok is wrestling out of UWF, and even made a crack at the fact that the UWF was closing. "MWAHAHAHAHA, YOU MEAN TO TELL ME…. THAT I AM IN A MATCH AGAINST A WRESTLING MOVE?!" Mentok shouts, obviously unaware that the name of the wrestler is DDT. "How am I going to fight him?! By reversing the DDT into a Northern Lights Suplex?!" And with that, Mentok stares into the distance, as if he were looking someone right in the eye, and smirks. Yes, he does know his wrestling moves. Just because he chooses to have his zombie guards fight for him does not mean he doesn’t know how to fight. For God’s sakes, Mentok is a Mindtaker! He could probably control the Renegade’s mind and make him wrestle with a bra on… Oh no… "Very good idea Narrator, very good idea!" And so the wait ends. In less than an hour, Mentok will grace you all with his presence. Sure, he will float above the ring and let the zombie guards do his bidding, but he will still be there in spirit. And who knows, you might even get lucky enough to have Mentok control YOUR mind?! "And that is that… because MENTOK, WILLS IT SO~~!"
