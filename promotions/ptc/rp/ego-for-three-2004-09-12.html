Ego For Three?

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 Pride. 1)A sense of one's own proper dignity or value; self-respect. 2)Pleasure or satisfaction taken in an achievement, possession, or association: parental pride 3)Arrogant or disdainful conduct or treatment; Haughtiness. Since the beginning of time, man has been known to be the owners of five senses. These senses are Vision, the ability to see things. Touch, the ability to feel things when he place a part of your body on them. Taste, the ability to enjoy or dislike the sweetness or the savoury of a food when placed in your mouth. Hearing, the ability to know what something sounds like when a noise is made. And finally Smell, the ability to perceive the scent of something through the nose. This is the common fact that both your average, everyday person and scientists believe. The idea that there is a missing sense is proposterous in every way, "No way, if there was a sixth sense we would have realised it by now." But what they haven’t thought about is that there may well be a sixth sense on the inside of the body. Not something that you do, something that you feel. The only type of sense that scientists have thought of is an action, not a feeling. Have you ever felt vision? No. Has anyone ever felt hearing, felt the smell of something, even feel the taste of something? No, they haven’t. For all we know, there could be over ten senses. Some people could call love a sense. Some people could call Hatred a sense, even call Jealousy a sense. But these are just two of what are known as the Seven Deadly Sins. Of course there not senses, right? Who can say? Who knows… The sense that is being talked about here is something that you discover inside of yourself. It is something that is precious in life. You could call it a type of confidence. However, unlike all of the five known senses, with this you can have too much or too little. Too little, and you will let people walk all over you, believe them when they say that you are useless. Too much, and you become ignorant. You won’t even take constructive criticism, as well as the fact that you sneer at people you think are below you. For this sixth sense, you need the perfect proportion, otherwise you are in trouble. Have you guessed it yet? Pride. This word means nothing to anyone, unless you can take it, use it wisely, because, as some of us do not seem to realise, Pride is not the same thing as being big- headed. For longer than I can remember, there have been a lot of egos in wrestling, whether it be people like Quietus in the World Wrestling Alliance, or people like Lex Le Magnifique, the man who I face in Round 1 of the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. And I’ve heard about egos like his. They strut around the ring as if they own the place, and then they either win and have their ego even bigger, or they lose and have their ego tell everybody that they felt sorry for their opponent, so they let them win. Yes, it’s all been figured out. Lex Le Magnifique is one of those people that was mentioned earlier, one of those people who seem to have too much pride in themselves. Too much ego, yet they call it self-esteem. Now that very excuse is what proves the point that they have a rather large ego. How ironic, by denying your ego, and then having the tenacity to call it something like confidence or self-esteem, is actually just digging yourself a deeper hole, falling deeper in it. The amount of ‘Pride’ that Lex seems to have is unbelievable. He’s like everyone else I seem to face. They rack up alot of big winning streaks, and suddenly they seem to think that if they were put up against the people who deserve to have egos, people like Joshua Dunn, then they would have no problem. Because, yes, there is a difference in egos. Some people have just got their own nose up their ass, and they are snotty because they want to make themselves believe that they are the untouchables or something. But there are some people who are wondering how they can possibly believe this in themselves. People who deserve to have the largest of egos, people who can say ‘I am/have been the Primetime Central Global Champion’ or ‘I am the World Heavyweight Champion of a Primetime Central federation’ or even just ‘I have beaten all but a couple of Primetime Central members’ Those are the people that have the right to give themselves the largest of all egos. But, hell, a few of these people choose not to. A few of these people are decent, modest people. But these people are, sadly, a select few. And there are some amazingly idiotic people who will say something like ‘I am not someone with a large ego, it’s just that I know of my abilities, and I know that I am one of the biggest legends to hit this business. Simple’ Yet they do not realise that this in itself is one of the biggest ego-struck sentences somebody can say. But it isn’t the only way to be big-headed… "Look, Dennis, I’m gonna put it to ya bluntly. You are being a cocky moron.” The scene opens up to see two tall, rather muscular gentlemen walking down the wide pathway which stands next to an all sand beach, no stones, no pebbles, nothing except sand and the sea. As the screen faces the two men walking along, chatting with each other, mostly about the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, it is visibly clear that the man on the left, just heard calling Dennis a cocky moron, is wearing a red shirt with the words ‘I’m with stupid à’ on them, blue jeans and white trainers on. The other, Dennis, is, from bottom to top, wearing black trainers, also wearing blue jeans, but wearing a black top with writing that says ‘Odium – Where Hatred Lives’ on the front in white. Odium turns to face the man on the left, before responding to his, how is it put, frankly insulting comment. “No. You look, Jack. Take a look around us. Is there anyone here that looks remotely frightening? Besides me, of course. Of course not. We’re on the same side of the road here, Jack. We’re both wrestlers, of course, you not being as successful as me, but a wrestler nonetheless. Now, I enter the Golden Turnbuckle Tourney, right? I’m drawn against Lex Le Loser. Six foot six, three hundred and forty four pounds, sounds like a monster right? Of course it does. Well he’s near enough the same measurements as me, so that won’t be a problem. In fact, if anything, I’m looking forward to creaming this guy.” Jack, as we now know that that is his name, looks at Odium with a curious eye, before replying, his temper being slightly shortened. Of course, Odium doesn’t notice this, not too much of an attention payer, is Odium. He’s too busy taking a look at the sea that is about thirty metres away from him… Click! Click! Cli- “Dennis! You were day dreaming. Now where was I? Ah yes. Listen to me, Dennis, you can’t ever say that I am not as successful as you, considering the experience I have. But that’s not the point in my constant talking.” “So, you actually going to deliver the point you’re trying to make to me then, or are you just going to bore me with your verbal diarrhoea?” “I’m going to bore you with my verbal diarrhoea.” “You stupid plebeian…” Odium spoke with such fierceness in his voice that even Jack had to pay attention to. Fortunately for him, he was only joking. “Dennis, I was joking…anyway, what I was really going to say is nothing to do with that. What I notice of you is that you always seem to be underestimating your opponents wrestling talents. You always assume that you are better than all other people. You’re, I dare say it, bigheaded, to be quite frank. What about all the Seymour Almasys? What about all of the Joshua Dunns? All of the Karina Wolfendens? What happens if you come up against one of these people?” The fierceness in Jack’s eyes, now apparently appearing to be just angry with Odium for his intolerance for others, and he turns to Odium, who has still yet to look directly in the eyes of Jack tonight, awaiting his reply. Odium sighs, loudly, before giving the response Jack wasn’t exactly wanting. “Poor overestimated fools. Sinking in their own confidence, too much of it. Too much is expected of them, they’re not as good as they think- “When are you going to learn Dennis?!?! Look. We have been friends for over fifteen years now. But never, not once, have you been as bigheaded as I have seen of, and heard of you today. What happened to you? All the fame gone to your head. Maybe that’s why you failed to win the WWA Extreme Title. Maybe that’s just why you have never been quite able to make your mark in Primetime Central…” As the last two comments are spoken, Odium’s head sharply turns, and the fire in his eyes is obvious. He listens to what Jack has said, and his cheeks start to glow red with anger. As Jack stops speaking, Odium pushes him into the wall, before keeping him there, tightening his grip of him by holding the scruff of his shirt collar. “Or maybe I didn’t get the WWA Extreme Title because I am too good for it. And maybe I haven’t made my damn mark in Primetime Central because I was waiting for the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament to do it! Think about that, Jack…” “No. YOU think about it, Dennis. Because when you do, you will realise that what you just said was extremely bigheaded. ‘I am too good for it’ is what you said. Now that is just proving my point. Dennis, I’m not trying to insult you with what I’m saying, just a few home truths I think you should know.” Odium slowly releases Jack from his grip, before walking ahead of Jack, before calling out behind him to Jack. “I’ll see you sometime. I’ve got some thinking to do…” As he walks away, Odium looks behind to see, and hear Jack mumble to himself ‘Fool, too much pride…’ Odium though, after saying he had some thinking to do, appeared to mean it very seriously. “Is it true?” He takes a few strides forward, and continues to talk, or rather, mumble to himself. “Am I that bigheaded?” He stops, before shaking his head, and looking to the side. He notices a child, from the looks of it about the age of thirteen. Him and what appears to be his little brother, are standing beside a rather large boulder, and trying to impress each other by attempting to lift it up. The little brother, looking about the age of six or seven, attempts, but fails to get it off the ground. The thirteen year old, looks at his little brother, jokingly flexes his muscles, or what little muscles he has, and starts to lift it. He lifts it to his shoulders, before dropping it, and raising his arms in triumph. Odium chuckles, before starting to walk down the steps, and walking over, ready to show the little kids how to do it. However, when he is half-way there, he stops himself in his tracks. “What am I doing? Jack was right, I am a bigheaded moron…” Odium turns around in shame, before hanging his head low. As he takes a few paces forward, once more, he stops himself in his tracks. “Hang on, I’ve got an idea!” He turns around, before jogging over to the children, and speaking to them rather rushing. “Hey kids. My name is Dennis, and you probably don’t know me, but I, well I guess I just wanted to help you. I noticed you trying to carry this particular boulder. Well, I also noticed your technique.” “Yes, I got it to my shoulders too!” “Hey kid, how would you like to be able to lift that boulder above your head?” “I’d love too!” “Well, instead of putting your arms underneath it and lifting it, put your arms underneath it, but as you get it to your chest, quickly move your arms to face your chest, and then you can push your arms up above your head.” “Hey, thanks mister.” “No problem, kid.” As the child lifts the boulder high above his head, Odium walks away, and back onto the walkway he was originally on with jack. He swiftly brushes his hand through his hair, before walking on into the car park. He stops, before turning around, and noticing Jack standing about two metres away from him. “Hey Jack.” “Hey…Hey, I didn’t mean what I said earlier. It’s your life, and I shouldn’t have assumed you wanted my advice. Forgive me?” “Well…no. There’s nothing to forgive. I guess you were right. I was being a bigheaded idiot.” “But if it’s any consolation whatsoever, I do reckon that you’ll beat Lex Le Loser…” Odium stops, before looking, for once, and this time with a friendly face, into the eyes of his friend Jack. He, once again, brushes his hand through his slightly long hair, before chuckling. “Heh. To be perfectly honest, so do I Jack, So do I…”
