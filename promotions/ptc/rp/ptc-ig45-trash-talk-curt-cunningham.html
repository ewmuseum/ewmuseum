1 June 2008 From the cunningly infernal desk of Curt Cunningham!: Peons! Imbeciles! Lend me your ears! For years and years you listened to Tears for Fears, holding beers near and dear, hoping for someone to cheer. And now he's here! It is I, “Cunning” Curt Cunningham! And you, you Storm you, you think that you've done something momentous! No, my drizzling friend, defeating some ugly cackspitter named Hyundai Subaru isn't momentous. I will regale you with a story of cunning momentousity that will force your eyeballs out of the palms of your hands! Eons ago when I was called Cunningus, Master of Cunning, I was the Chief of Cunning Affairs in the great and not-so-eternal Rome. It happened that something momentous was happening, but the Imperator Caligula appeared troubled. There was a crisis! But lo, no crisis has ever proved too great for my unfathomable cunning! And so I cunningly manipulate Caligula into telling me the story, the story of a bull being led into the city by the name of Christophus Lightbreezicus, the story of the bull who had a long ton of gold cunningly woven into its stomach, but could not be killed or else the gold would melt and disintegrate! 'What to do, Cunningus!' he beseeches me, clutching at my robes and looking ineffectual, as was his wont. 'Fear not, Caligula!' bellows my cunning voice to the distraught emperor, 'for I have a cunning plan! Let us go to the bull under the cover of cunning darkness and I will fetch the gold!' And so, our course of action set, we fly at night towards the stable where the bull is being kept, away from all harm and under guard at all hours. Throwing off my cloak, I spied the bull – a lame and withered beast with proud haunches but a profound weakness in its eyes. The terrifying gears of my incredible cunning were already whirling in full motion! Leaping at the drizzle-soaked abomination, I wrapped my arms about its throat in a cunning hold that I learned in my time amongst the hypershephards of the wretched, demonaic steppes of greater southeastern Hungolia. With a Iroquoian twist, I wrenched the bull's head clear around and off its neck, only for a sheer and complete long ton of golden ingots to spill from that opened orifice! Am I implying that I would cunningly twist affairs into a situation where I could break your neck? That should be a prime fear of yours, my fairweather friend, for I have indeed visited those ancient and secret shrines dedicated to the Elder Altgods, deep in the forbidden and untrespassable jungle-tundras of sub-Asia. I have indeed studied under the secret chiefs of those forsaken lands and only my insurmountable cunning saw me through the ordeal, but I escaped with terrible powers and an awesome maneuver known as the Thainese Neck Crank that can easily snap the necks of those not gifted with supreme cunning (e.g., you). I cannot help but highly appreciate and commend your probable bravery in the face of my earth-shattering cunning, but it is for naught. I expect to see you soon, when I will undoubtedly fell you and ascend, with the greatest amount of cunning, to the throne of the Infinte Gauntlet! Cower if you must, for your demise is at hand! With Love, “Cunning” Curt Cunningham ---- 1 June 2008 Please! Ladies! Hah, did you see that, I have cunningly implied that you are all women! What supreme wit! Ladies, do not trouble yourself further. I would like to make clear that “Open Invitational” does not mean this is a free cheesecake buffet or a 30% off sale at Macy's. You can return to your Ally McBeal and your tub of Edy's ice cream – I know, it was on sale so you went with it over the Ben and Jerry's and anyway you're so over him, Mister Rust, but it's okay. No one is judging you. Except for I, who in cunning secret, laughs at your every misfortune! Stuff your face with ice cream and tremble! You bandy words against each other, which is good! Good for you! Waste your breath on each other and, once you have been sufficiently winded, feel the cunning shadow grow in your midst and choke the life out of you! You are arrayed against Chris Storm and against each other, not heeding the swelling darkness that will swiftly penetrate your ranks and explode with cunning force onto the world stage! Sexual innuendos, hah! Laughable, hence my laughing! Let me tell you, I may have had chlamydia or syphillis or the crab plague, but those meek virii could not withstand my cunning for even a second and withered away to nothing! I have had sex with thousands of women (hundreds hyperdaily!) and one abominable creature. One of them was your mother! I will leave you to guess which one was your mother, but will cunningly drop you a hint: it was that abominable creature I mentioned. Am I implying you all had one mother between you? Yes! You, all of you, sprung forth from one set of warty, grease-dripping loins, the loins of that cyst-ridden Whore, the Matron of Insufferable Banality. I indeed had cunning relations with that Pimply Princess of Prostitutes in order to bring it up later as a supreme and masterful insult, but only after cunningly forging an iron cun-dam with which to armor my mighty Rod of Cunning Manipulation! Andy Murray, you appear to be a Scot, which is something I hold against you! The Scots are scum! If you remember, the Irish were driven from their lands by a cunning plot (forged by my own incredibly cunning self, when I was Curtis, Lord of Cunningwick) to steal every potato in Ireland, robbing them of their food source. We would have done the same to the Scots if we could pilfer dirt and manure! Von Doom, you are a buffoon of the highest order, which is an insult to other buffoons! If I could have stopped your mother from squirting you out I would have, but alas, the affairs of the stupid are never realized by the cunning until too late! You could not even defeat Andy Murray, who is a mere Scot and of not much more worth than a sack of poorly-made bricks! The Scots! I laugh again! That Endless Glove of Steel will be upon my altogether too-cunning wrist in due time, my friends! It is fitting that you chose for yourself such a ridiculous and bland name, Chris Storm, because your reign as champion will be as equally ridiculous and bland: ridiculous because you actually managed to defeat Heineken Samichlaus and bland because it will last only a short time and therefore be uneventful! Enjoy the Infinite (soon to be Infernal!) Gauntlet while you can! Your Enemy, “Cunning” Curt Cunningham P.S.: Oh, and that other one... ummm... Garrison Kane? If your mother asks about me, tell that bitch to stop calling the CunningPhone, I do not want to talk to her again.
