Nobody Does It Worser - Episode 2

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-14 INSIDE A DARK HOTEL ROOM – LOCATION UNKNOWN DATE UNKNOWN EXACTLY 11:45 PM…YES, SOMEHOW WE KNEW THE TIME. WE’RE GOOD LIKE THAT. Rrrrrring. Rrrrrring. Click. "Yes?" "It’s me." "You’re on the secure line, I trust?" "Yes." "Deliver your report." "It seems there was an error. Bong is still alive." "Alive? Don’t tell me that you actually FAILED to eliminate JIMMY BONG? The man is a complete fool! I’m amazed he hasn’t accidentally killed himself already!" "With all due respect, I don’t believe I’m at fault. I simply went to his home address. There was a man sitting outside wearing…" "I’m not interested in hearing excuses, valid or not. The bottom line is that Bong was supposed to have been killed, and he wasn’t." "Of course… my sincere apologies. I’ll fix the situation immediately." "You’d best see to that, otherwise I’ll have someone else do it for you." "That won’t be necessary." "I hope not, for your sake. That will be all." "Of course." Click. --- MAYBE ENTERPRISES, 70TH FLOOR – CHICAGO THE DAY AFTER BONG’S UNEXPLAINABLE VICTORY OVER ELIA SMITH A REASONABLE TIME IN THE MORNING (NOT TOO EARLY, NOT TOO LATE) Jimmy Bong had been waiting outside the office of Professor Stuart Maybe, CEO of Maybe Enterprises, for quite a while. With a frown, he glanced over at the pit bull of a receptionist that diligently guarded the oaken gate to Professor Maybe’s inner sanctum. His frustration increased with every step as he paced the length of the room. Bong had very few pet peeves, but being made to wait for an extended period of time when he had an appointment was certainly one of them. As it was however, he was mild mannered to a fault—slow to anger and quick to calm. Thus, his only recourse was to periodically ask whether or not he was any closer to accessing his GTT5 sponsor. "Excuse me…" he started, stealing a quick look at the name placard on the desk’s front edge. "…Carol. May I see Professor Maybe yet?" "For the SEVENTH TIME, NO! How many times do I have to TELL you? He’s IN a MEETING!" Incidentally, Carol had a pet peeve of her own: being asked the same stupid question over and over again. Plus she was quite ill tempered to begin with anyway. Of course, Bong supposed to himself, if he were a middle-aged divorcee mother of three slovenly teenage boys, a chain smoker with the complexion of a desert snake, and more saggy than a fried egg on a doornail, he’d be pretty grumpy too. Bong sighed and turned around… then turned back again just a moment later. "How about now?" "NO! You just asked that like a SECOND ago! Are you DEAF or something!?" "No, just stupid. At least that’s what my mother always told me," Bong replied earnestly. "But look, I have an appointment and I’ve been kept waiting out here for nearly an hour! An hour!" "You arrived TWO hours before your scheduled time!" Carol snapped back. "I fail to see how that makes a difference! It’s very rude all the same!" "Just SIT DOWN and SHUT UP before I JAM this PEN up your FREAKING URETHRA, you STUPID MORON!!" With obedient haste, Bong took the farthest seat away from the desk he possibly could. "…As I was saying, I’ll just wait here quietly until he’s ready to see me." He lingered his gaze on the enraged receptionist a few moments more, almost fearful that she might leap over her desk and chomp into his jugular with her vile fangs the second he looked away. He let out a sigh of relief when the phone rang. "Maybe Enterprises, what in GOD’S NAME do you want!?" Bong felt sorry for the poor chap on the other end of the line, but at least Carol’s ire had temporarily been directed elsewhere. He stared out the window at the Chicago skyline, letting out a small "hmph" as he reminded himself that he was doomed to wait for another hour. The December weather had turned the Windy City into a winter wonderland. Thick clouds and heavy snowfall obscured the tops of the Sears Tower and the Hancock Building from view, and indeed it was nearly impossible to see all the way to the ground from Bong’s seventieth-story perch. He supposed the streets were bustling with thousands of ant-like figures right now, all going about their daily routines. Thick panes of glass shut out the wind, the cold, the snow, and the noise. It was like looking into a giant snow globe. Or perhaps it was more like the snow globe was looking at him—Bong wasn’t quite sure. What he did know for sure was that it was peaceful. Quite peaceful. The peace was rudely shattered by sudden noises of shouting coming from within Professor Maybe’s office—shouts that not even the mighty oak door could muffle. Moments later, the door swung open. Out stormed a portly man in a reddish brown suit and tie, looking quite frustrated. His face had so reddened that his thinning orange hair looked paler than it normally would. Following behind him and closing the office door was a tall, wiry man of no more than thirty-eight years, also looking rather perturbed. His shaggy, dark brown tresses bobbed slightly with each step he took. A thick goatee decked his facade, and he wore a navy blue suit with a handsome wine-colored tie. This man was Professor Stuart Maybe. "Cyprus, wait!" he nearly shouted after the other man. "This isn’t a problem! I’ll personally see to it that the situation is resolved!" The other man immediately stopped and whipped around to face Maybe. "You had better resolve the situation, Stuart," he growled slowly. "I didn’t invest my millions into this project just to have everything ruined by that sniveling Pendable." "Don’t you worry about Xavier," Maybe said reassuringly. "He won’t be a problem after I’ve dealt with him." "I hope so, for both our sakes," the other man replied. Jimmy Bong, who had remained quietly in his seat for those few moments, finally stood up. "Professor Maybe!" he exclaimed, walking over to greet the CEO. Maybe and the other man both stopped and looked at the tuxedo-clad Bong, who was grinning like a delighted fool. "Ah, Jimmy!" Maybe said with a smile, shaking Bong’s hand. "Good to see you! You’re here awful early, if I’m not mistaken…" "Yes, your receptionist was kind enough to remind me of that," Bong said through gritted teeth. He quickly glanced over in her direction. She was still on the phone, but was looking quite annoyed that there were people in her area… talking while she was on the phone, no less. "Ah yes, she does have a bit of a temper, doesn’t she?" Maybe chuckled. "Oh, by the way Jimmy… this is one of my business associates, Cyprus Coppertoe. Cyprus, this is…" Bong finished his sentence. "Bong. Jimmy Bong." "Pleased to meet you," grunted Coppertoe, in a way that didn’t sound at all like he was pleased. "You as well!" Bong smiled, offering his hand. Coppertoe simply looked down at it and sneered. Maybe noticed the impending snub. "Now Cyprus," he said with slight admonishment, "Jimmy is selflessly performing an important service for this company, and it would be rude not to show him a bit of courtesy." Coppertoe looked begrudgingly at Maybe for a moment, then snarled as he quickly shook Bong’s hand once and let go. "You are most kind, sir!" Bong said, completely oblivious to the fact that Coppertoe was about as kind as a kick to the scrotum. "Anyway, Stuart," Coppertoe continued. "Do take care of the… erm…" he glanced sideways at Bong, "…the situation, will you? We can’t have the deal falling apart with the investors’ meeting just a few days from now." "Yes, yes, of course," Maybe said hastily. "Now you’d best be getting along, Cyprus… I have important matters to discuss with our friend here. I’ll have a chat with Pendable before we meet again, don’t you worry." "Right. Good day, Stuart… Mr. Bong." Coppertoe nodded curtly, then turned on his heel and exited the room. "So, Jimmy!" said Maybe, turning back to the man he was sponsoring in the GTT5 tournament. "Let’s step into my office, shall we? Oh yes, Carol, what are…" "How many TIMES to I have to TELL you!? He’s NOT FREAKING INTERESTED! If you EVER call here again, I SWEAR I will HUNT YOU DOWN and give you a GODDAMN SICILIAN NECKTIE!!" Carol slammed the receiver with such force that Bong was genuinely surprised it didn’t shatter into a million pieces. By contrast, Maybe didn’t seem phased at all. "Who was that?" he asked calmly. "Those stupid alumni jackasses again," Carol snarled. "Asking for donations. They’ve called asking you for money more times than I can count. You KNOW how much I FREAKING HATE being asked the SAME STUPID QUESTIONS OVER and OVER and OVER AGAIN!" "You’re not kidding," Bong muttered under his breath. "Haven’t they gotten the hint yet?" Maybe asked, shaking his head. "Ironic how they were so keen to disassociate themselves with me all those years ago, and now… well, if they call again just hang up on them. I don’t think taking revenge via mafia-style killings is the image this company is shooting for. Now Carol, Mr. Bong will be in my office for a while so we’ll have to move a couple of my appointments. Who else am I seeing this morning?" Carol flipped open a planner sitting on her desk and ran her finger over the current day’s page. "Let’s see," she said. "Aha, here we are… Koman Itzlov… Lee Weather…" "…FOR A SLEIGH RIDE TOGETHER WITH YOOOOOU!" Bong suddenly burst out in song. Carol and Maybe stared oddly at him. "What?" he asked. "Uh, nothing," Maybe said finally with a roll of his eyes. "Anyway, just move those appointments up on my schedule, Carol. Oh, and please call Lang up to my office as well, would you? Thanks." Carol nodded, and picked up the phone once more. As he followed Maybe into his office, Bong couldn’t help but think that if phones were capable of knowing fear, this would certainly be one of those instances where it was called for. "Have a seat, Jimmy," Maybe invited warmly. Bong felt very much obliged, sitting down in a rather comfortable armchair at the foot of Maybe’s paperwork-laden oak desk. There was a cheery fireplace to his right, inside which was a roaring orange fire. Hanging on the mantle directly above it were a few framed diplomas from DePaul University—degrees in various fields of chemistry and biological science. "The last remaining fragments of more troubling times," Maybe sighed as he noticed Bong staring at the diplomas. Bong shook his head briskly as if coming out of a trance. "I beg your pardon?" "I was a faculty member at DePaul several years ago," Maybe continued. "Unfortunately I had a bit of a falling out with them, and needless to say I ended up going into business for myself. But enough about me." He walked around to the other side of his desk and sat down across from Bong. "Let’s discuss what’s been going on with you, my friend! That was quite a win last night, quite a win indeed! I can’t imagine Elia Smith was easily vanquished." "No kidding, what with her being possessed by a demon from centuries past," Bong said with genuine fascination. "It’s amazing how often you run across soul-possessing demons in professional wrestling. You almost have to be an exorcist or something to even have a chance at winning these tournaments." "How… strange," came Maybe’s reply after a moment. "Anyway Jimmy, this is remarkable! I can’t even begin to describe to you how important our victory over Elia Smith is to Maybe Enterprises!" Bong blinked. "Our victory?" he asked, confused. "Why of course! It was a perfect demonstration of how well our new, innovative athletic training program works! Each victory in the ring is a marketing victory for this company! I’m sure without our help, you’d have been defeated in no more than three seconds." "I don’t know about that. After all, I did reach the bracket finals last year without any such help." Maybe laughed. "Ho-ho, come now, Jimmy! Everyone knows that you only advanced that far purely because of an incredible string of dumb luck!" "HEY!" Bong shouted. "How dare you insult me like—well, actually, you’re right. But you didn’t have to remind me!" "It’s quite alright, Jimmy," said Maybe. "Luck, after all, is a small but essential part of what it takes to win a tournament of this magnitude. However, there are other things you need besides mere luck, especially if you wish to advance beyond someone of Kenny Mercury’s stature. You need strength and skill, naturally—you also need the right image." Bong arched an eyebrow curiously. "The right… image?" he asked. The front door swung open before Maybe had a chance to elaborate. Another man Bong had not yet seen bustled into the office with a flourish. The man, Asian in descent and small in stature, looked as if he’d just emerged from the center of a tornado—he had wild hair that had been dyed electric blond, wore ragged blue jeans, and was clad in a wrinkly, untucked white dress shirt. A horrid pink-and-purple polka-dotted tie adorned his neck in a Windsor knot that he quit tying halfway through. He clutched a clipboard in his arm and had a pen tucked behind each ear. Bong wasn’t sure from the man’s movements if he had consumed entirely too much coffee, suffered from Parkinson’s, or was just constantly on edge for no reason. "Jimmy Bong, meet Erik Lang, one of the top marketing minds here at Maybe Enterprises!" came Maybe’s enthusiastic introduction. Bong stood up and turned to Lang with a smile, and went to shake his hand. Lang however whisked right past Bong, pacing around the room in a circle and sizing up the GTT5 competitor. Bong’s smile immediately faded, and his eyes remained locked on his eccentric ogler. "Hmm…" Lang pondered aloud. He stopped in front of Bong, now looking him up and down very slowly. "Hmm." After a few moments he continued circling, now more deliberately than before. He came to a halt again behind Bong, staring fixedly at his backside. "HMMMMM…" Lang scribbled a quick note on his clipboard then resumed walking. "Is… is he hitting on me!?" Bong whispered unpleasantly in Maybe’s direction. Maybe just gave him a small smile and shrugged, as if this sort of behavior was normal. "Right!" Lang finally said after a few more moments. "I have reached the conclusion that this man… HAS A PERFECTLY-FORMED ASS~!" Bong stared oddly at Lang. "Wh-what the bloody hell does that have to do with anything!?" "Absolutely nothing!" Lang said gleefully. "I’m just fond of the human body, God’s most wondrous and glorious of creations! I adore and admire the many shapes and sizes it can take! Every time I meet a new face, I simply have to drink in their image, like hot cocoa and marshmallows on a chilly day! Living, breathing art! Oh, it sends shivers down my spine, and thunder rumbling through my soul!" "You lie!" Bong exclaimed. "Alright, fine. I just like asses," Lang sighed in confession. "Anyway, the exercise has given me time to center my thoughts." "Tell us what you have planned, Lang," requested Maybe. "Of course, Professor," Lang replied, bowing deeply to his employer in melodramatic fashion. "As you know, Mr. Bong is representing our great company in GTT5 while demonstrating the effectiveness of our revolutionary training regimen. What we need to do is really endear him to the public. We need to give his image a major face-lift, and define just who ‘Jimmy Bong’ really is!" "But," Bong protested, "but I don’t see why that would be necessary. I mean, I am who I really am!" "My dear Mr. Bong," dripped Lang in a patronizing manner, "Does anybody in this world truly know who they really are?" "Well…" "The answer is ‘yes,’ because I tell them who they are!" he finished before Bong could answer. "And I’ll tell you who you are, Mr. Bong… YOU…" Lang waved his hands in one exquisite motion, "…are a HERO~!" Bong blinked. If anyone who ever knew him was dead, they were surely rolling over in their graves right at that moment—like his mother, for example. "A… a hero?" he asked quietly. "Yes!" proclaimed Lang. "A hero who shall rise up and vanquish Kenny Mercury! He is a dastardly man who for years has been a plague upon our fair city of Chicago… NAY! A plague upon THE ENTIRE WORLD~!" "Are you sure about that?" Bong asked. "I mean, he doesn’t seem like too bad of a fellow to me…" "STOP~!" Lang wailed, shielding his face as if he was about to be hit by an oncoming train. "STOP I SAY~!" He clapped both hands on Bong’s shoulders and looked him squarely in the eye. It was quite unsettling. "Mr. Bong, you give your opponents too much credit!" Lang spouted, as if he was in dire pain. "Don’t you see, you have a nasty habit of complimenting everybody you ever face! You thought Elia Smith seemed ‘delightful!’ I ask you, how can someone with the potential to usher in the destruction of mankind be ‘delightful!?’ You, Mr. Bong, are COMPLETELY INSANE~!" "I don’t really think you’re a reliable judge of a person’s sanity," Bong muttered. Lang pulled himself away and resumed walking back and forth in agitated fashion. "And Kenny Mercury is even WORSE!" he clamored on. "For you see, he is… PURE EVIL~!" "Pure evil?" "YES~! Just look at what he’s done throughout his life! He’s stolen money from the elderly! He’s kidnapped elderly people from banks! He’s burned down orphanages with fires started by rubbing two orphans together! He’s killed men simply for looking at him the wrong way! He’s killed blind men for not looking at him at all! He’s killed deaf men for not being blind! He’s plundered entire villages and had his way with monkeys! MONKEYS I TELL YOU~! OH ALMIGHTY GOD, WON’T SOMEONE THINK OF THE MONKEYS!?" "Oh come on, you expect me to believe Kenny Mercury did all that?" Bong asked incredulously. Lang frowned, and drew himself to within an inch of Bong’s face. Their noses barely touched. His piercing gaze looked deep into the fearful eyes of Bong. He slowly asked in a low, fearful whisper: "Have you ever met him?" "Well, no…" Lang jumped back and pointed wildly at Bong. "THEN FOR ALL YOU KNOW HE DID, THAT EVILEST OF BASTARDS~!" Bong’s mouth was agape. Words utterly failed him. He turned to Professor Maybe with a look that screamed, "This man is a raving lunatic!" This was immediately followed by the words, "This man is a raving lunatic!" coming from Bong’s mouth. What happened next was certainly not the response he was hoping for. "Brilliant!" Maybe declared with genuine delight. "Simply brilliant, Lang! Smear the name of Mercury, and make Jimmy Bong look like a golden boy in the process! Pure genius, you’ve done it again!" Bong was shocked. "You’re joking!" he cried. "This… this man isn’t even making sense! He’s out of his bloody mind! No one is ever going to believe Mercury did all those outlandish things! Not in a hundred years!" "He’s from UWF," Lang said simply. "I stand corrected," Bong replied without missing a beat. "And," Lang went on, "as the finishing touch, we need a tagline to define the image of ‘Jimmy Bong: Hero.’ Something that sets the tone for your upcoming battle against the evil forces of Kenny Mercury! Something like—" Lang paused. His eyes went wide as if he’d just had an epiphany—or possibly an orgasm. Perhaps both. It was certainly at least one of the two. "THAT’S IT~!" he cried. "The Forces of Evil shall be vanquished by…" "…by the Forces of Good!" Bong finished. "NO! BY THE FORCES OF STUPID~!" corrected Lang. "That’s… accurate, I suppose," Bong said meekly. "And that doesn’t go for just Mercury!" Lang continued. "It goes for everyone! From this moment on, anybody who goes up against Jimmy Bong in GTT5 must surely be PURE EVIL~! And as such, they will meet their doom, because… The Forces of Evil shall be vanquished by THE FORCES OF STUPID~!" "Excellent Lang, excellent!" roared Maybe with delight. "We’ll endear Mr. Bong to the masses with our new ‘Forces of Stupid’ campaign! It’s brilliant! People can’t help but love a total idiot!" "Except for all the ones who hate me, and wish death upon me, which is pretty much everyone," Bong chimed. "And what is hate really, but a poor man’s form of love!" declared Lang. "…That doesn’t make sense!" Bong said after a moment. Lang narrowed his eyes and folded his arms. "You’re Jimmy Bong. What do you care about making sense?" "Touché," Bong replied. "Well, it all sounds good to me then." "Splendid!" said Maybe with a smile. "I believe that about wraps up this meeting. Jimmy, you have a training session slated for this afternoon, correct?" "Part of your ‘innovative regimen,’ yes," answered Bong. "Good! I want Lang to go with you, so you can get to work molding your new image straightaway!" Lang practically jumped up and down with glee. "Hooray! The latest masterpiece of Erik Lang SHALL COME TO BE~!" Then he bounded out of the office like an excited child. "Do you run a corporation or an insane asylum?" asked Bong a moment later. "Ha! I'll be damned if I ever figure that one out," Maybe chuckled. Bong groaned. "Wonderful." "Anyway, that will be all, Jimmy." Maybe turned his attention to the mess of paperwork on his desk. "And good luck against Mercury. Do Maybe Enterprises proud!" "Right, thanks!" smiled Bong, who turned around and left the office. The pleasant expression on Maybe’s face faded instantly. "You stupid pissant," he grumbled under his breath. He pressed a button on his desk intercom. "Carol, please kick Mr. Bong in the shin on his way out would you?" he asked. "With pleasure!" Professor Maybe leaned back in his chair, a contemptible sneer creeping over his face. A moment later, a scream came from outside his door. "OW! MY SHIN!"
