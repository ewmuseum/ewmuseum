Act of faith.

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Flash of light. Smoke and stain coil through the air as the devils dance methodically and playfully until the burning of their tails settles to a dull roasting of the spirit. Then their stench rules the air around, choking and suffocating until they permeate everything nearby. Stain them with their disharmony. Show everything the true nature of the world. And Reverend Springwell, my protector, my caretaker for the time that I am on this material plane, sucks them in, sucks them down to a place of cleansing. Within his self. Within his soul. He is truly a saint for doing so. Reverend Springwell inhales through the cigarette, staining his insides so that others can be clean. Giving himself in to the darkness so that he may bring light. The world's personal savior. The world's greatest prophet since John the Baptist. The world's only hope. Thin lips pierce the air with a crackle as he speaks God's word through his mouth. "This is it, my boy. This is what we've been waiting a long, long time for." His body is weak. Tired. Old. But it is his soul that rages with a fire that none will ever know. And it rages that way because the Lord wills it so. For He has spoken to Reverend Springwell, told His servant the truth of the flame that will soon be set to burn the world to ashes before freezing them in the fires. And only the righteous will survive. Reverend Springwell uses what physical strength he has in him to walk toward our lodging for the night. A motel a good distance from the arena that holds the future. His voice is like an iceberg's crackling in the crisp, cool air as he speaks with the fullest of knowledge. "Ever since the Lord has blessed me with finding you upon my doorstep, I have been awaiting the day that you would finally be prepared to be unleashed on the world. Just as Christ needed time to understand his duty and train his body, mind, and spirit to accept the Lord's great plan, so too have you needed time. So too have you needed preparation. And now…we have reached that point. It is time for this world of sinners to be purged. It is time for the Lord's plan to be enacted." Sins against God. They are everywhere. They are in the whites and in the blacks, in the hatred and in the false love. Lies and misbegotten faith and improper worship of One who asks for simple dedication. So concerned with their own selves, and not giving God what is due God. That is seemingly the essence of what it is to be a modern man. Every person I have met thus far in my life cares only for idol gods of money, power, fortune, fame, prestige, or self service. Even those who call themselves humble are only humble so that people think of them as such. Even those that call themselves martyrs are only martyrs so that that is what they are known as, not because they love the Lord. …all who lose on purpose do so not to lose gloriously, but rather to try to shine brighter when they win. All except for Reverend Springwell. He is the only man I have ever seen, known, or heard of that does not fall into the sick circle of selfishness. His devotion to God is one that does not sleep and never loses its vigilance. And it is that very same vigilance that Reverend Springwell has tried to teach me. The very same strength that he feels I am ready to observe. End the world? No. I am simply an instrument of God. I am simply the tool through which his intentions shall flow. "What is the Lord's plan?" Reverend Springwell looks at me with cold eyes. Cold from how many sleepless nights in his lifetime? How many times has the voice of God Himself shattered this brittle old man's life with its booming presence? He must be tired. He must be ready to collapse. And yet, he stands straight and announces the Lord's plan that was revealed to him so long ago. He says it for the first time as though he is saying it for the millionth. And I listen as though it were the same. "The Lord's will is to use the Devil's arms against the Devil's work. The connection to the masses, the media that they feed in to, that is what we shall use to show God's vengeance. The very television and radio and entertainment that the sinning masses hold themselves to will be used to annihilate their sins and their presence so that the Lord may cast down the souls of all sinners to the eternal flame. As the angel of death came upon Egypt, so must you come upon this land. Except he only took the firstborn of the Egyptians. That was a warning in the form of the tenth plague. Since then, the hearts of men have grown blacker than ever. Children killed before birth. Sex sold on the shelves of every store. Men mating with men and women with women, money ruling absolutely everyone, and material posessions literally at the click of a button! The world has become Babylon, the whore. With the technologies of the internet and cellular phones as its Tower of Babel. Every sinner has his place and every place has its sinners. And it is time for God's wrath to be known once again. And you shall be his harbinger." "But professional wrestling?" We have reached the hotel room. Reverend Springwell keys open the door and I lay down his suitcases inside. Suitcases filled with black suits bibles, and crosses for him along with the plain clothes of a servant of God for me. Reverend Springwell remains at the door for a moment. Snuffing the flame of the devils with the tip of his shoe, he finally takes off his wide brimmed hat and views the world with cold, steel blue eyes. A curt nod shows that this room will suffice for lodging. Careful, intentional steps bring him inside while he begins methodically blessing the room with holy water, a bible, and a crucifix. "Do not question God's plan, young one. You never have before. I almost fear that perhaps the civilization you have seen so far hath corrupted your spirit. That perhaps you have tasted the forbidden fruit of doubting God's command." "No, Reverend. Never!" "…good. For you are my last hope, boy. All others before you have failed, like the prophets before Christ's coming. Just as they were taunted and cast out in the streets, stoned by the very people that they were sent to give celebration to, so too have your predecessors been scourged before their time. They, however, had been tempted by the Devil to disobey the first among all commandments; to love the Lord your Father above all else. And that is why they, once servants of God, burn eternally." Father, forgive me. For I had doubted you. I pray to give me the strength to never doubt you again. Ignorance, doubt, and blind faith should have been added to the list of deadly sins. For they lead to more tragedies than any I have ever seen. They are what leads to the lust, avarice, wrath, envy, sloth, gluttony, and pride that will lead to one's damnation. …time passes before I ask Reverend Springwell a question that has been burning in my mind for a long time. He has finished his blessing and sits, reading from the Good Book, reading words that he has long since committed to memory. "Reverend…do I have a soul?" Piercing steel blue cuts through my very soul. It is the only thing I see. I cannot tell if he is angered or inquisitive as a brow furrows and a dead, cold tongue wets his lips before he tries to answer me. "What do you mean, boy?" "You say that those that do wrong will burn eternally in the fires of damnation. Yet…you say that the Lord's plan is for me to bring the ruin of so many, to bring justice to the unjust, and perhaps even to bring death to lives unworthy of life. But does that last mission not break one of the commandments? Thou shalt not kill." "It does." "So to do God's will and not be punished by it…that must mean that I do not have a soul." "…perhaps it does." "But…if I do not have a soul, how can I bask in the Lord's eternal bliss after my death?" For the first time, I can tell that Reverend Springwell must search for an answer. Is it not clearly stated? If I am to scourge the world of the sins against God, and I am myself a sin against God, should I not thus cleanse the world of myself after all others? And to say that I am a sin against God, how can I not burn in eternal fire for bringing hatred to my brothers and sisters? And if I do not have a soul, what purpose do I have for serving God? I didn't just say that. Serving God is the only purpose I know! Please Lord forgive me for such thoughts! Please God forget that I have said such a horrid thing. Regardless of reward, my duty is to serve you and only you! To have no wants, to have no desires, to have no sense of regret or pain and to never question you or The Prophet Reverend Springwell. Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou amongst women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. … … Please forgive me. "You have a soul, boy. But you are not like those that you shall cleanse the world of. The reason is because God works through you directly. You are a hollow shell, and His hand passes you by, like a house with lamb's blood above the door. For you have the blood of the Lamb of God upon your soul, and thus you are washed clean by Him. Those that you befall, they are not protected because of their faith. Those like Fatbadger, who have no faith in the Lord. Rather, he has faith in the futility of the situation. He knows that there is no way out of what shall eventually happen. He knows that the Lord will enact His plan to rend Fatbadger's soul for eternity, but he does not have faith that the Lord shall also pass him by if he only believes. Fatbadger knows that it is inevitable that he shall lose, and yet he will fight the unbeatable fight. Forever shall men like this run from God. Just as they will try to fight you. Just as they will refuse to allow God to cleanse their lives with the punishment on Earth. Rather, instead of accepting the cleansing light through material bruises, they shall opt to hide in the darkness and try to fight the light, only to meet their demise at your hands and burn in their own place set for them in eternal fire." Never wage an unjust war. That is the act of wrath. "Has this man named Fatbadger done anything to earn the awesome wrath of God? The only just wrath." "He has put other things before the Lord. That is worthy of God's wrath if anything ever was. This failure, this sin against God, puts paraphernalia above what the Lord commands. The Lord commands all of His creatures to worship him, love him, and love their fellow man. And yet, this waste of life finds it more useful to abuse the very body, the very temple, that God gave him. He finds it better to waste his time, your time, and everybody else's time with meaningless rants and gestures that do little more than make the sinners out there appreciate their meaningless, petty, empty lives more than his. His work ethic is a joke. His attempt to overcome a monster like you, boy, is equal as such. In all, he is a poor excuse for a human being. Wasting time where he has little to waste. Remember, boy. Killing time is not murder, it's suicide. And suicide leads to the fifth level of Hell." His wisdom knows only the bounds that the Lord has put on him. Reverend Springwell lays back on his bed and closes his old, tired eyes. He is not young. He is only strong in the spirit. The body is weak, and tired, and needs to rest. Do not worry, my Lord. Just as I would have watched over Christ in the Garden, so too shall I watch over Reverend Springwell whilst he closes his eyes and tries to forget about the sinners of the world for a few moments. I am not tired, for I do not tire. I am not hungry, for I do not hunger. It is your quail and manna that I pray for, Lord, and those that ask shall recieve. I am simply to wait until you answer. I am simply to wait until I defeat my first of many opponents who shall fruitlessly stand up to me. Whether they claim to win or claim only to exist so that they may lose gloriously, fail like few others ever have, they shall meet their fate at the hand of God's weapon, God's shell on Earth. Direct me, Lord. For you are my engine and my guidance. I shall simply break through the barriers set before me.
