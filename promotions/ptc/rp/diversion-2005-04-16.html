Date: 2005-04-16 April 15, 2005 He shot him in the face. The brunt of lead and tears was a beautiful sight when mixed in with the blood. For a split-second, you could have sworn he smiled; a distinct smirk he revealed moments before the bullet shattered his skull. He wanted this…possibly more than I did. I proceeded to the man and handed him a duffel bag that enclosed the twenty-thousand dollars that we agreed on. I was no cheap skate, I pay for the services. As he grabs the bag out of my hand, I give a nod and proceed to walk away until the man grabs my shoulder. Never attempt to catch me off-guard. Now let’s look at my choices. I’m sure he has a few bullets left in his gun which he could use if he’s looking for more money. I could spin around and knock the fucker out… …or just let it slide and maybe realize he meant no harm. I’m a lover, not a fighter. I turned around moments after the man grabbed my shoulder and looked at him. His eyes stood glazed and his façade was nothing but bland, but you knew that he just got his fix… …his kill… …his winning lottery ticket. As I stood concerned, yet prepared on what he wanted, he spoke no words, but with slight tilt of his head towards the kid with a smoking forehead, I understood. I didn’t have anymore money, and I really didn’t feel like doing the bitch work…the cleanup. I shook my head and he acknowledged the fact that I wanted nothing to do with cleaning up the body nor paying him more to do it himself. We both faced away at the same time and left the young kid lying there in what seemed to be a pool of dirt and blood, but to him I knew it was a symbol of freedom. --- An Hour Earlier The impact of a solid fist against a now broken face is flashed before your eyes and screams of pain and torment are heard with complete distinction as tears burst out of clinched eyelids as the body of a young kid with long ,dirty blonde hair falls to the floor. “Fuck you Dad! Atop hitting me!” The plea for mercy is doused with the crying and anger within the boys words. As he stutters backwards in fear as his father approaches him with a look of intoxication, the boy quickly stands up and runs towards the door and makes his way out of the screen door and as it shuts behind him, a large 40-ounce bottle is fired at the closed door denting it and shattering the bottle. The father then passes out on the torn couch as the boy continues to run down the street. Panting. Tears. Jailbreak. A few minutes after running as far as his breath capacity could carry him, he turns between two large buildings which he felt safe from anything…even his father. Until he could walk no further, he leans up against a wall and just collapses onto the ground with his hands in his face and the tears soak every crease in the young boys hands and begins to seep down his forearm and soon dripping to the ground. For a young man…that is a lot of tears. This young man with long, dirty blonde hair and incredible green eyes has been emotionally, physically, and now mentally defeated it seems. His dress attire consisted of black shorts, and a torn, bright red hooded sweatshirt that was extremely dirty as if he wore it every minute of the day. The tears soon begin to slow down and the young boy lifts his head out of his hands. With a red blemished face, tired, wet eyes, and an overall façade of depression is when he started to calm down. “Hey guys look at this.” Faintly in the distance, you can hear a voice and scuffling begins to appear until you see three teenagers. All look somewhat similar. With the long, black hair and black trench coats that were wore open with dirty, wife beaters on, you knew that these guys hung out together almost all the time. “Is he crying?” One of the teens chuckle as the others laugh inside their breath. They walk up to the crying boy and stare at him, helpless on the ground. “Where’s your mommy?” With a taunt from one of the teens, the crying boy jumps up and shoves his face into his. Don’t talk about my mother. The teens are quickly to respond. “Or what? What can you possibly do before we do this.” A knee to the groin and a pound on the kid’s back was all that took to bring him down to the ground. He was already weak. A complete assault on this boy was taking place. Thick black boots scuffed his face and fists became imprinted on his body. Here were the tears again. He had nothing left in him…he was destroyed. It seemed that the beating would never stop until you heard a yell from down the alley. The boys quickly stopped and ran the opposite direction until the man at the other end made his way down here. “Charlie, are you okay?” “Tyler, the boy replies. Remember when I saved your life?” Tyler O’Neill…last seen at the Face-Off tournament is now seen…quite intrigued to help this boy as he questions Tyler as he lays in pain. “Yes Char, I do…let me help you up.” “No Tyler, I can do it myself. The boy slowly stands to his feet and looks at Tyler and places his hand on his shoulder. He looks into Tyler’s eyes and speaks. “You’ve always been a big brother to me and I’m going to ask you to help me. Save my life Tyler, that’s all I ask. Please do it soon.” “How?” Charlie shakes his head and walks away with Tyler standing there. As the boy walks away, the boy mutters… “I don’t feel like dying this young…please help me.” 15 Minutes before the first scene Tyler stands with Charlie behind his house. Tyler needed to help him now. Tyler speaks to Charlie. “I’m going to help you.” Without allowing the boy to speak any words, he points to the corner of his house. “Go there, quickly…please. I owe you one.” The boy turns around and smiles because he loved when Tyler brought him gifts. As Tyler turns his head away from Charlie, who suddenly turns the corner, a gun shot is heard…
