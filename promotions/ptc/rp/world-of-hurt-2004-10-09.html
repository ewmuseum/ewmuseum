World of Hurt

9 October 2004

Date: 2004-10-09 People have always feared the Mafia, it’s a known fact. But why it is that crime group that has always been fear and the others never noticed? Is it that the Mafia is more ruthless and control more things? Or is it simply that via television and printed media that they have gained their reputation as owning the underworld? And why is it that they have received so much of the media attention and the other groups who are just as powerful, if not more so have gone unnoticed? The simple answer is that the Mafia go out oft heir way to make sure that their stories, and yet not their faces or names are on the news. This was something that The Lair has never needed. Never have they needed to put their stories out there in order to gain the amount of attention as those who have heard the stories through word of mouth know that fear is something that you must associated with the group of men who have controlled so much for so long. Salaok stands in his office over looking his casino below as his security guards manhandle the loose cannon who is causing a scene. Knowing full well that none of this will be reported on the news and that was exactly how Salaok preferred it. Those people who are in the casino at the moment are the only ones who need to see this act of violence and pain, as they are the ones who are frequenting the casino and therefore are Salaok’s biggest risk. It is those people who sit at the tables in his casino who need to see what happens should you try to step out of line. They see it often enough and soon they will learn that there is no point to even try and steal money from The Nevermore Casino. Still the TV in the background of Salaok’s office blurts out the news of the recent happenings of the GTT4 tournament. A tournament in whom Salaok has found himself entered. Not for the love of wrestling, but for the love of pain and the love of causing it. He isn’t a wrestler, never claimed to be. Someone even said to him as he was leaving the venue last week the exact that. “You’re not a wrestler Salaok, you’re a hit-man.” It was true and Salaok did nothing more than stroll along as he had been and replied in a simple manner. “And then some.” And tomorrow, Salaok would do the same thing he’s been doing since the start of this tournament. Fly across on the day, get off the plane, and go straight to the venue. There was never a need to hire a hotel room for the night as the last thing he wanted to do was to spend extra time around the area knowing full well exactly the control that was behind the competition. The GTT4 tournament was an event that was bet on highly and the amount of money that would change hands during this tournament is more than enough to fund a small country. And where there is money changing hands, there are those who want to fix the event. Those people who have stayed around the arena have found themselves subjected to threats and attacks ever since the thing started. So many people have dropped out of the tournament for reasons that have been never spoken about in public. However everyone knows why. With the right amount of money, it doesn’t take much to break into someone’s hotel room and smash a baseball bat into someone’s knee putting them out for the round. Who was to blame for this? Never spoken about. Why? The fear of life. Salaok knew all of this and had made the choice to stay away from the happenings, despite the fact that some of his group where the ones bringing down the wrestlers. He wasn’t the one making the orders, but he knew about it none-the-less. And once again, it would happen this tournament. Already three men had handed in there papers stating that they couldn’t compete in this round of the tournament and thus having to give up completely. Salaok had stayed at the casino since the start of the tournament and luckily enough too as his hotel room across the road from the GTT4 arena which was booked for him had been broken into twice now. One time was just yesterday which meant that people with power wanted this Harley guy to win and yet they still weren’t sure about whether or not he would be able to. And in Salaok’s mind, those fools had wasted there money yet again. Someone out there doesn’t want Salaok to get any further than what he has, and seemingly, he wasn’t meant to get this far to begin with. Normally his mind would have gone straight to the owner of Freedom, Salaok’s last federation. The owner was Chris D., his father and no man had wanted Salaok to fail more than him. Except that problem had been taken care of a few weeks ago with Chris’ death. A death that Salaok had taken great pride in. But that was the past now. Now there was a situation that would be about to arrive in the back alleyway. A situation that Salaok just wanted to be apart of. Turning his attention to the security screens, he notices that that he had already begun to miss the fun as they had reached the backdoor already. Quickly turning to the flat screen television where the highlights of last week are currently being played from the tournament, Salaok grabs the remote off his red wood desk and turns it off. “Sorry, PTC. But you aren’t the important thing on my mind as I simply don’t care.” Salaok leaves his office and it’s clear that the windows in the office were sound proof as the noise from the mass of people is almost unbearable. People shouting out bets and arguing about who’s chips below to who is the sea of humanity that Salaok steps into and begins to walks amongst them. A man with such power being able to walk through the crowds of people with no one knowing who he is, is something he is happy with. The last thing he wants are people knowing that he owns the place as they would approach him and ask things that would simply end with a reply of ‘no’. What was it about these people that make them think that because he is in the public eye that he is simply going to lend them money. The fact that they don’t know him at all doesn’t seem to stop them. “I guess that Vincent and Gabriel were right all those years ago. This truly is a Tragic Kingdom in which we live.”He mumbles to himself as he walks through these lower classes of people. And he truly believed that was the case. He knew he was better than these people and it showed in the way in which he carried himself. His walk was full of confidence and his head was slightly tilted back as he looks down on the small people around him. Standing at 6’6”, there were very few people in the casino who was his size, even the security guards found it hard to match Salaok’s size, so he is still able to keep an eye on a lot of area simply by looking over the heads of those around him. But he wasn’t there to watch the floor, no, he was moving through the area for the sole reason to see what will happen with this man his guards have taken out. Through a number of doors and corridors Salaok picks up his pace as he seems almost eager to get to the action. The thought of being able to watch his guards in action is almost enough to make him want to light up another cigarette. The kind of cigarette that you have after hot passionate sex. For Salaok, pain was just as good as sex, if not better. Sick? That is certainly is, and doesn’t deny it. But finally his gets to the door which will bring him out to the alleyway and stops for a brief second. Closing his eyes and taking a deep breath in hope that they haven’t finished with the man yet, he then pushes on the door and it swings open. Everyone stops and turns to look at who has sprung them and all is silent. “Yes boss?” One of the bigger men dressed in a fine suit like the rest of the guards speaks out. “Don’t let me stop you Diasporo. I’m simply here to watch, for now.” With that said, the four men begin to beat down the already bloodied and beaten man. His jaw clearly has been dislocated, or broken already as with every screen of pain that escapes his lungs he cuts it short. The area in which this unholy act takes place is nothing like the casino itself. A back alleyway that actually has no exists. A brick walled square out the back of the casino which looks to have been purpose built for just this situation. People need to be taken care of and this kind of situation isn’t the kind that the general public needs to know about. Repeatedly the hands and feet of the four men, all massive in size. With each blow, another crack can be heard as yet another bone breaks. All this man was trying to do was take a couple of chips. He never would have thought it would have cost him all of this. Although the smile on his face was unmistakable, Salaok still didn’t feel total happy with the situation and steps forward which instantly causing the four men to stop again. The only sound is that of the man on the ground coughing out large blobs of blood as it then drops to the ground and mixes with the dirt and gravel. Salaok motions to the guards to lift the man up to his feet, which they do instantly without a word being spoken. As Salaok walks forward, he reaches inside his jacket and pulls out a small butterfly knife and scratches his cheek with it. The smile that forms on his face is due to the fear that forms on the man in whom he approaches now. Sadistic? Very much so. The idea of causing pain brings happiness to his life. Happiness which had been void from his life since the murder of his wife and son. But with every step, the glazed over look in his eyes haunts his already destroyed victim. Finally face to face, only an inch away Salaok looks straight into the man’s eyes and begins to laugh. Salaok’s laughing continues as the man’s eyes close in pain, but his mouth stays shut as he jaw simply will no longer move. The knife has dug itself into the man’s stomach and has been twisted around a few times. Removing the knife, while still laughing, Salaok wipes the blade clean on his cheek and then turns away. Moving back to the door, he looks over his shoulder as the four guards toss the man over the wall and follow Salaok inside. Within a minute, an ambulance will arrive and take the man to a hospital. His hospital fees will already be covered. There is a need to cause pain and send a message. There is no reason for this man to die however. The door shuts behind Salaok and the guards and each goes there separate way, not wanting to make it seem as though something has been happening. With blood still on his face, Salaok enters the main area of the casino and walks through the sea of humanity again. This time, just like before, he gains no attention from those around him. Even with blood smeared on his face, no one pays attention. Until he reaches a woman who looks like she’s lost everything. Her eyes scale up to meet Salaok’s and her words are so pure at first. “Sir, you seem to be bleeding, can I help you in anyway?” Her words soon begin to become less innocent. “Perhaps I can help you relax and calm down from what ever has caused this?” And then her words become absent of all innocence. “I’ll blow you for five bucks!” Looking down at the woman, he smiles and indicates to follow him as he begins to walk off. She gleefully follows the large man through the crowds and surprise comes over her face as he leads her up the stairs and into the Authorized Only areas. He walks up to the door marked, “Owner” and opens it which causes the woman to stop dead in her tracks. He motions for her to enter the room which she hesitantly does. Closing the door behind her, Salaok looks at the woman, and for some unknown reason takes pity on her as she gets down onto her knees in front of him. “Get up!” Salaok’s voice gruff and strict as he helps the woman up to her feet. “You’re not going to blow me, but I’ll give you the five bucks. What you are going to do, is go down to Roulette table 6 and place the money on number three after the ball lands on number ten. Then you must leave and never come back again.” Her eyes open wide in the acknowledgement that the tables are rigged. She nods and quickly snatches the money from out of Salaok’s hand as soon as he presents it. Within seconds the woman is out of the office and making her way down to table six. Salaok walks over to the windows and watches as the woman sits down at the table and does nothing for a few spins as the ball hasn’t yet landed on ten. But finally it does and she looks up to the windows, but can’t see Salaok standing behind them as they are tinted extremely heavily. She places the five dollars worth of chips on ‘3’. She sits there eager and watches as the ball is spun. Salaok watches as her head follows the ball around in the circles and begins to stand up in excitement. The ball bounces a few times as it falls from the ridge. The ball slows down and finally lands on the number next to it, 35. The woman visually sinks back down into her chair and her head hits the table, but it is only a few second before two guards appear and pull her from her seat. Walking back to his table, Salaok grabs the remote for the television and turns it back on. The voice on the screen is clear as he announces the next match which will be reviewed for round three. “Salaok Vs Davis Harley.” Salaok grins to himself knowing that no matter what they say about him, they truly have no idea what is about to happen. This tournament was nothing more than a bit of fun for Salaok. A place where he can get his intake of pain. A Pain which had been taught to him throughout his life and had been apart of him for years. The pain of death wasn’t the one in which he lived for. No, it is the pain of the mind. The torment that he has learnt to cause. He was The Harvester of Minds and the games in which he plays were ones that those he was associated with loved to be able to see in progress. Whether it be his wars with Brick in GW with the kidnapping of Brick’s close friend and torturing him for almost a month, or it be the family war against the two Welch brothers which saw the death of Talena, Brian Welch’s girlfriend. These wars have been watched by many, and yet Salaok has never been satisfied. Not happy to move on until the bigger prizes have been claimed. It doesn’t mean a title belt or some tournament win…. No, it’s all about fucking with as many people’s minds as possible. That’s the pain in which Salaok now lives for. Which would be the greatest mind game for this tournament though? Seeming as though he wasn’t going to show up and then coming out of nowhere in the last few minutes? Or would it be simply walking away from it all despite the fact that many people believe that he could stroll through the next few rounds. Mind games are hard to plan for such a greater mass of people and he would have no other choice but to do whatever felt right at the time. The worst possible situation that the people around Salaok want to find themselves as it is when your back is to the wall that you pull something out of no where. And it’s always the most unexpected act that you can imagine.
