A Plea For Help

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 Biminy Alpha Company Outpost Location: Top Secret 10 November 2005 0225 Hours If you find this letter, then Heaven has shed some hope for humanity, for I fear that humanity’s worst nightmare is soon to come. I implore you to read this letter thoroughly and take credit all the information that lies within. If you truly enjoy being alive, if you are truly human, if you truly relish the survival of our way of life you will not disregard this letter. I beg of you! My name is Matthew Cartwright and I am a Gunnery Sergeant in the United States Army. I was located to Biminy, exact location unknown, in the vicinity of the Bahamas Islands, along with a dozen of the best train Special Ops forces. We were assigned mostly as bodyguards to the renowned Dr. Colin Gilchrist and his research within the jungles of Biminy. We were not told the exact extends of our mission here, but we were implored to keep them classified. As a soldier, and a protector of my great nation, I obliged. But I cannot do so any longer. To do so will go against my creed as a soldier, as an American, as a human being. I can no longer stand by and let the atrocities I have seen with my own eyes go unanswered and unfounded! The mission was supposed to be simple: protect the doctor. But we soon learned that it wasn’t a simple mission. In fact, our mission was grossly mistaken. It wasn’t the doctor we should be protecting. We were assigned to his center because of increasing attacks on the research center by natives. He had sustained a dozen injured workers and two had died from such guerilla tactics. Unfortunately for the natives, we saw them as the threat, unaware of the real enemy. The natives of Biminy were protecting something very dear to them. They were not only protecting their lives, their families, their way of life, but they were protecting everyone in the world. It was rumored that deep within the jungles of Biminy, there exists a secret so powerful, so rejuvenating, so unreal, that is has remained hidden from the World for eons. I always found that to be nothing more than a fairy tale. That is until we heard reports that the Dr. Colin Gilchrist had found it! I cannot tell you the exact extend of the secrecy Dr. Colin Gilchrist has uncovered in the jungles of Biminy. But I can assure you, that whether or not he has that, is irrelevant. I watched him destroy the lives of countless men, and smiled in doing so! He is a mad man! When we realized the gross mistaken our supervisors had bestowed upon us, it was too late. We were deep in the jungles of Biminy with no one to provide us communication with the outside world but the Doctor himself. We could not go to him. We knew that he already suspected us. So, we tried to flee. Twenty years of training, thousands and thousands of combined in-field work experience, and some of the most advanced gadgetry in the world could not save us! As well-prepared as we may have thought we were, we were no match for Dr. Gilchrist and his savage science. One by one, he managed to pick us off in the most disturbing ways. I watched one of my men simply explode from the inside out, another one took his gun and shot himself through his rectum. I watched my men succumb their minds to this mad man’s perverse imaginations. I watched them all die! I beg of you! I watched as he slaughtered dozens of natives without so much as lifting a finger. His science is far beyond that of any man I have ever known. I watched his neurotoxins reduce men to slithering pieces of jellyfish. I watched his science destroy all but one of my men. Yes! I watched him reduce dozens of the best train soldiers in the United States to quivering masses without even lifting a finger. He killed all but one of my men. The only one "spared" was a new recruit assigned to my team, Alexander Jackson. He was the youngest member of my team. He was energetic, good-looking, well-fit, and was everything everyone strives to be. I weep at what he has now become. I weep knowing that Dr. Gilchrist transformed that modern-day Achilles into a savage beast with the mind of a caveman. I said that he only spared one of my men. I lied. What he has now become – a slave to Gilchrist – is far worse than death. Those who died were truly spared! I swear I tried to save him when I made my mistake. I swear I tried! But I couldn’t, and now I must beg forgiveness. I beg the world to forgive me for failing to beat evil here. I beg Alexander’s parents for forgiveness for not protecting their son. Oh, the humanity! By the time you find this, I may have already been captured by Gilchrist, or, if Heaven is kind, I may be dead, for I cannot find the courage to face this evil. Not after what I’ve seen. You must know that Gilchrist killed a hundred natives here and has found a weapon more powerful than any man-made device of mass destruction. He has taken that weapon, his science, and dozens of slaves back to the United States. The only other piece of information that I can give you, is that he has gone to a town called Aurora. You must go to Aurora and stop him at all costs! I said I did not know what secret weapon he found, but I do now. How could I have ignored it so much? Why was I so stupid as to not realize it before!
