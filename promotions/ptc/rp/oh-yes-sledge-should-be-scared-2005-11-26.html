Oh Yes… Sledge SHOULD Be Scared

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 The sound of an electric guitar racing through a solo of a heavy metal song is the first thing heard before anything is seen. More specifically, the song is "Welcome Home (Sanitarium)" by Metallica. After a few moments without any visuals, we finally see a man with his head down, head banging to the music as he sits in front of a computer screen. Looking further, we see that the man is African American, wearing a white tank top over his coffee colored skin. Aside from the black and white checkered boxers that he’s wearing, he’s not wearing anything else. He continues thrashing around in his chair with his short dreadlocks in his eyes, until he hears a noise that stops him. He sits up straight in his chair with his eyes opened wide and mouth slightly opened as he listens intently. Realizing the sound was exactly what he’d thought and feared, he took hold of the mouse and began clicking frantically. The sound was that of a key unlocking a door. Just as the door opened, Metallica was cut off and quickly replaced by "Just A Lil Bit" by 50 Cent. "Hey Greg," the man entering the door said. "BOY! 50 CENT SURE IS GOOD! I LOOOOOOOOOOVE HIP HOP!" the person we can assume is Greg Jackson exclaimed. No one could EVER know his horrific secret… No one would ever find out that he actually hated hip hop with a passion… A black man who hates hip hop? Unheard of! The man entering the two-bedroom apartment was Greg’s older brother Matthew. He was a few inches taller than Greg, who at five feet and eleven inches tall, was about average height. Matthew took off his black leather jacket and set it over top of a chair in their small kitchen area before walking over to Greg, who was still on the computer. "You don’t need to yell, I know what kind of music you like," Matthew started, "I got a surprise for ya." Despite just being informed that there was a surprise for him, Greg seemed unenthused about the whole idea of being surprised at all. He made no acknowledgement to his brother, who was now right behind him, as he kept clicking away on the computer. Matthew continued, "I found you some work. And not just ANY kind of work! This could be your big break!" "Uh huh. What is it?" Greg replied nonchalantly. "A tournament," his older brother answered. Greg spun around in his chair and glared at his brother before questioning him further. "It’s not… Mortal Kombat, is it?" Not sure if his brother was actually being serious or not, Matthew decided to clarify the details for his brother. "No… wrestling, of course. Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Five, one hundred and twenty eight men, single elimination style. I’ve already entered you, and I just found out you’ve been accepted. In a little over a week, you’ll have your first of hopefully many matches." Matthew smiled at his younger sibling; pleased with the good news he’d given him. Greg, however, furrowed his eyebrows and bit his lower lip, obviously not overjoyed to hear the news. After a few moments of silence, Greg thought of the perfect response. "WHAT?!" Matthew was caught off guard by Greg’s reaction, but that wasn’t all he had in store for him. He walked over to the door and opened it, and in walked a cameraman and reporter from a local television station in Swift Current, Saskatchewan. "This is relatively big news here in Swift Current, so the good people at C.T.V. wanted to get your initial reactions," Matthew explained to his brother, who appeared to be VERY nervous. The reporter, who was a young red headed woman wearing a long black leather jacket and blue jeans, already looked ready to go with a microphone in her right hand. With her left hand, she motioned for Greg to get out of his seat and step in front of the camera. He did so, albeit very slowly. Greg gave Matthew a worried look before stepping in front of the camera, which was already rolling. "Hello, I’m Natalie Armstrong, and I’ve got Swift Current’s own, Greg Jackson with me! If you’re not aware, Greg’s been confirmed as an entrant in one of wrestling’s biggest tournaments, Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Five. Greg, how does it feel?" she asked. For a few seconds, which felt like hours to Greg, he stood there in silence. He simply stared at the camera before muttering, "Uhhhh, feels good… I’m happy…?" The reported chuckled quietly, obviously expecting more of a response. She continued, "This tournament has catapulted the careers of many superstars in the past. Do you feel it can do the same for you?" "It’s possible, ummm, I hope so," Greg quickly responded. Realizing the interview had nowhere else to go, she asked the only question that there was left to ask. "Do you have any comments for your opponent?" Greg thought for a few seconds about what he wanted to say before realizing that he had no clue about who his opponent was. In a panic, he glanced over towards his brother, who was already prepared for an instance like this. On a piece of paper, he had the words, "Greg Jackson vs. Sledge" written in large black lettering. Greg focused back on the camera and began his message to Sledge. "Yeah I got something to say to him… Sledge…Kinda sounds like "sludge". Uhhhhh, bet that means you’re slow, and I’m fast…" He stalled again, giving a look that implied that he MAY have dropped a load in his pants. Finally he composed himself, and said the word that would SURELY strike fear into the hearts of Sledge and whoever saw this footage! He flared his teeth and with his right hand, he made a clawing motion towards the camera before saying… "GGGGGRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!" And that’s a wrap.
