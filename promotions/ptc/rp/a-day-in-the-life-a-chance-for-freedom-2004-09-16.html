A Day In the Life...A Chance for Freedom

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 Moonlight, soft and white as it refracted off the small slit of a window in the wall, casted its illumination across the floor of this darkened room. Through the haze of the light, a figure emerges, drawn into a corner with knees pulled to her chest, arms wrapped tightly around them. Nothing of her is visible save her hands, eyes, and the mess of hair atop her head as he stares distantly off into nothing, as if waiting, or expecting someone. Just outside the room, noises are heard, the moving of rusty wheeled carts and the quiet murmur of conversation. All these she ignored though, as if life didn’t matter outside this room and the beating of her own heart. But she was not meant to be alone this night, as the door on the far wall was slowly opened, and a darkened figure stepped into the silence that was her own. She drew within herself more, as if in fear, as the figure closed the door and walked to stand in the center of the room, unseen eyes boring a hole into her she could feel more than see. It was ‘him’…’he’ had come. ”I thought I told you to prepare for the little test I have set up for you, Luna. You don’t look like you’re preparing to me.” She jerked at the deep, sultry sound of the voice, closing her eyes tightly, her nails digging into the skin of the black, skin-tight suit wrought about her form. Slowly she re-opened them, letting them slowly lift to the shadow that stood in the center of the room. Her mouth felt very dry as she struggled to find her voice, a shiver running up her spine as she spoke. ”I…I practiced since dawn…all hours…I…it was night…I needed…needed to rest…” ”YOU REST WHEN I TELL YOU TO REST!!!” She jerked violently, immediately trying to draw herself further into the corner as if the voice itself was enough to destroy her. Such fear, such fear from a woman who had been cast into this place for horrid violence. She had been the one that had almost killed those men in the bar, but it was not of her own doing. She had no control…’he’ did. ‘He’ had what she wanted, what she needed, what she had prayed for, for so long. She closed her eyes again, feeling the heat of his hatred strike her like something solid as she waited for more words. (His voice dipping down again into calm control) “Don’t you understand? You’re an animal. You belong to me. You don’t eat with everyone else. You don’t sleep like everyone else. You do what I tell you when I tell you, and nothing more or less. Is that understood, Luna? Or shall destroy what I hold, forever taking it away from you.” ”Please!! No, I’m…I’m sorry! I’ll try harder! I will! Don’t…please don’t…” The tears started again. So much pain locked up inside, and ‘he’ knew it. It was her strength, her will, her violence, if you will. She needed what ‘he’ had like the body needs water. And ‘he’ would hold onto it until the time came to give it back…if ‘he’ decided to give it back at all. But she had to try. She had to be what ‘he’ wanted her to be. She had lost everything…her memory, her name, her past…all she knew was this strong and passionate love in her heart for what ‘he’ held, and she prayed that it would restore to her all that had been taken away. ”Good girl. I brought you food, I want you to eat, then get back to work. This…Rollins fool has the idea he can beat you. And maybe he can. But if you –work- like I tell you to work, then that won’t happen, will it?” ’He’ slid the plate across the floor to her, and she took it with trembling hands, pulling the mask down off her nose but keeping her features hidden within the shadows of the room. She wasn’t allowed forks, spoons…nothing. She lived and ate like an animal, as she was doing now, burying her face into the slop that passed for edibility and quickly swallowing it down her throat. Inside she could feel the violence swirling again, the need to get up, to release it. ‘He’ knew it too…she could tell ‘he’ was smiling at her without even looking. (Sliding the empty plate back across the floor) “I…will work now…I’m sorry…I rested, I won’t…I won’t do it again…” She pulled the mask back up across her face and pushed to her feet, her legs trembling with fear and exhaustion for a moment before she steeled herself, walking out toward ‘him’ to stand before ‘him’, he bowed low to keep her eyes on the floor. Glowing eyes…the fractured light of the moon finally gave a better glimpse of this woman; short, midnight black hair that sat mussed and uncombed on her head; eyes that glowed a fierce white as if she weren’t human at all; and nails that were painted black, the tips done in deep crimson. It was the look ‘he’ had chosen for her. It didn’t matter whether she liked it or not, this was it. ”Rollins is bigger and stronger than you, Luna. He can hurt you really, really bad if he wants. Which is why you will be faster, and most certainly smarter. Clear?” She nodded her head a little, hands clasped pensively in front of her. ”I understand…” ‘He’ smiled, and snapped his fingers, and the door was opened again. She turned her head even so slightly, catching a glance of a man being pushed into the room…a man twice her size and height. He was mumbling incoherently, and covered in tats from head to toe. She could see the lost, yet violent look in his eyes, and she knew what was coming. Sparring? No, sparring was practice…this would be for real. She would have to hurt this man she didn’t know and didn’t care about in order to please ‘him’. (Gesturing toward the tattooed man) “This is Brock. Killed his entire family with a pickaxe…how charming. And now he’s here, and loose…and he wants to hurt someone again. And since he cannot see me, looks like that’s gonna be you Luna. I suggest you try and survive…else your prize gets destroyed along with you, hmmm?” She nodded faintly, and let the heat of anger and pain boil inside her and through her veins. If it was a show ‘he’ wanted, then that’s what ‘he’ was going to get. She lifted her head and her eyes to Brock, who was now staring at her, a sudden look of lust mixed in with the gristly need for pain in his eyes. So pain and rape is what he had on his mind…while she thought of simply pain…her pain delivered unto him. Her agony to become his agony. ”What tha fuck are ya lookin’ at, bitch?” Crude. Very crude. Did she smile behind that mask? It was within every inch of her now, that feeling of all of it needing to be released. She turned fully to face him, crossing her arms over her chest, her countenance going from fear to sudden arrogance. ”I’m looking at a fat, tattooed ugly fuck who has decided to share a room with me. Care to come give me a hug, big boy? Or are you just gonna stand there and stare at my tits all night?” He grinned a sadistic grin and moved toward her, spreading his arms open wide. Stupid. As soon as he was close enough, she suddenly lurched forward, her knee slamming into his groin harshly. To see the look of pain on his face was sheer delight to her, but he wasn’t out for the count. He growled in pain, and shot out with a backhand that caught her across the jaw and sent her stumbling across the room to hit the wall. She could feel the blood filling her mouth as her eyes caught him again, charging her. Too late to move, his entire body weight smashed her against the wall, feeling the air driven from her as he slid down it to the floor. ”Ya slut! Now I’m gonna fuck ya up real good!” He reached down and grabbed her suit, trying to rip it from her skin. She could feel his fingers bruisingly, and she open her eyes, reaching up quickly to sink her nails into the skin of his face and yank hard. Scarlet rivulets smattered her mask as she reached up again around his violent growl, grabbing the back of his head as she shot upward, her forehead turning his nose into crimson mush. He staggered back away from her, grabbing at his face with a sound of mixed anger and pain. (Through gritted teeth) “Gonna fuck me up real good, are you? You couldn’t fuck a dead whore if they rolled her in for you.” ’He’ smiled from his corner of the room. She was a scared, frightened girl most of the time…but let her anger and pain rise and she became a force to be reckoned with, as Brock was steadily finding out. But she moved too late, and through his blurred vision Brock launched out with a straight left punch that rocked her and sent her crashing back to the wall. She could see her vision trying to fade to black, her head spinning from the impact. She clenched her jaw, desperately trying to focus her eyes on him. When she finally did, she could just glimpse him charging her again. At the last moment she stepped aside, and he went head first into the wall, falling to the floor. She stepped back, and settled herself, waiting. ”I have to piss in a different room…I have to eat alone…I have to sleep at different times…I’m an animal…” She hissed these words as she watched him struggle to his feet, centering her weight above her horse stance. He reached a knee, and started to turn toward her, which is exactly what she was waiting on. Spinning on her front foot, she brought the back around with speed and cruel intent…*THWACK!*…her foot connected hard with the side of his head, and his eyes rolled back, and he crumpled to the floor. ’He’ clapped as she stood over Brock, her chest heaving and her breathing short, as if she expected him to rise. ‘He’ grabbed her shoulder and pushed her hard against the wall, then picked up Brock as if he weighed nothing, tossing him over ‘his’ shoulder. ‘He’ walked the limp form to the door and pulled it open, non-chalantly tossing it into the hallway. She let her eyes glimpse it for just a moment, that hallway…white clad orderlies moving up and down the rows of steel doors. It was her escape route…when the time was right. (Closing the door once more) “Not bad, Luna…but you allowed him to hit you! What the fuck is wrong with you?!” ’He’ turned on her with sudden hatred, and she slid along the wall, fists clenching tight again as ‘he’ approached. But there was nothing she could do…if she fought back, she would lose. ‘He’ grabbed her throat and slammed her back against the wall again, settling eyes she could not see on her own. ”I said FASTER and SMARTER…not SLOWER and MORE ARROGANT! You will DO what I SAY, or I will HURT YOU!” She nodded quickly, gagging as ‘his’ fingers tightened around her throat. So it didn’t matter at all that she had won, that she had stood over the fallen form of Brock. She hadn’t done it right, done it to ‘his’ standards. She was still a failure. The heat sank within her, and she curled into fear once more beneath his brutal fingers. ”Y…yes…yes…faster…smarter…I’m sorry…please…it hurts…stop…” ’He’ chuckled amusedly and released her, and she immediately dropped to the floor and slid into the corner, drawing her knees upward and hiding once again within her shell. ‘He’ watched her do this, then stepped away toward the door, calling over his shoulder. ”You may rest now. I will be back tomorrow…and we will do this again. And do it MY way, clear? And for your failure, you will relieve yourself –in here-…no more bathroom for you. Consider it a lesson.” Without another word, ‘he’ yanked the door open and stepped through, slamming it behind ‘him’ and leaving her alone once again with the silent darkness. She sobbed quietly, tears falling beneath the rim of the mask to mingle with the blood that still slowly ebbed from her mouth. She closed her eyes tightly, repeating the name Rollins in her head over and over, memorizing it. He was the target, he was the real and true test of her desire to get back what was hers. She would not forget, she would not fail. She would do it right, as ‘he’ wanted her to, and Rollins would feel the violent anguish that boiled within her. Slowly sleep took her…and the dreams started again… ~A Dark Alleyway, Rome, Italy~ She stood with her back against the wall, long flowing locks of black hair buffeted by the wind and soaked by the torrential rain that poured from the angry sky above. Lightening flashed across the sky, illuminating a face partly shrouded by the dark hood of a cloak wrapped tightly around her form. Night black lips sat in a smirk, as if she awaited someone, or something to arrive in this little place, stuck between the walls of the buildings around her. Footsteps echoed off the cobblestones in the distance, and she turned her eyes that way, settling them on the lone man, soaked from head to toe, approaching her. His head was downcast, messy waves of blonde covering his eyes as he stopped a good distance from her. She eyed him up and down slowly, letting the silence linger, only the distance clap of thunder and the pounding of the rain breaking the silence. She nodded, waving an idle hand to beckon his approach, which he did, still keeping his eyes downward. ”What was so important you brought me out in this rain for?” His voice was timid when it came, struck with a deep fear for her. ”You…you wanted news on her…she has left, aware of you being here. She is gone.” She snarled harshly, enough to make him step back and suck in a deep breath. She seethed for a moment, then all at once calmed down, letting her head bob in a slow nod. A fucking waste of time. She had chased her here from New York, and like always, she knew she had come, and fled again. This chase was becoming annoying. Very annoying. (Her voice remaining a soft growl) “Any ideas as to where she went this time?” He nodded, reaching into his rain soaked shirt to produce a ticket stub, and lightly put it in her hand. She glanced down at it and nodded, taking note of the destination before stuffing it between her breasts. ”Go…your payment of food and water awaits you in the southern hall of the hotel.” He nodded quickly, and turned away, leaving her alone once again with the lightening and rain. She contemplated slowly, how to go about ensnaring her prey without giving herself away. She shook her head in frustration, and pushed off the wall, heading down the alleyway opposite of the departing man. She needed dry clothes and hot food, and the hotel wouldn’t do. Another journey awaited her… ~Present Day~ She awoke with a start, blazing sunlight peering in through the slit of window to illuminate the room around her. She hugged her knees close to her body, shivering up and down her spine as her eyes settled on the food before her, slopped together on a plate without care. These dreams had been getting persistent as of late, little links to her past, but never enough to put together or hold on to. She stared at the food, which, as usual was there for her to eat like an animal, as she tried desperately to hold on to it, but like all the others, it slowly faded. She was left with only one thought: Rich Rollins, and her need to win. If she was to ever pull herself out of this hell, out of this life of living in a cage, eating like a pig, pissing in the floor…she was gonna have to please ‘him’. And to do that…she had to win. Slowly she rose to her feet, remaining her safe corner as she lifted her eyes from the plate to the sunlight, her muscles tingling with the need to for violence. Sometime soon…sometime soon she would be free, at least for a while, free to unleash this bitter anguish on her opponent. Dawn was here. Dawn on a new day of life and breathing and being. Light breaks the darkness, and brings the world into view. For now, her view is this room, a little place of hell, but a place that has allowed her to focus her anger and her pain on the man chosen to be her release. Failure would have severe punishments, worse than anything she had faced here. She could live without being human…but could she live through the beating ‘he’ would give her? Like the light, soon enough, all things would be revealed… ~
