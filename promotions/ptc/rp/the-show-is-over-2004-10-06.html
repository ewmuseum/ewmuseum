The Show Is Over

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-06 *I am in my home watching TV is it just me or do most of my promos take place here lately? I so need to get a social life but not like I can have one with how demanding WMW and GTT4 have been. GTT4 I have done well in so far beating Zach Johns and Moraless. Now I face a 6 foot 6 man named Odium from WWA. A man who we say tied up and the only way a 6 foot 6 man gets tied up is if he likes it. Ask AWS Man about that kind of thing he rents all kinds of porn videos and I do mean all kinds! The phone rings and thankfully as my mind is wondering since I am not paying attetntion to crappy day time TV. Ellen? Sharon Osbourne? Errr why are all these annoying women getting on TV?! Well atleast its not Ricki Lake or Opera. My guess is they give good head. I pick up the phone and its the TV Station I air MY SHOW on. My show on latenight which is why I am forced to watch all these crappy shows.* Fred Grest- Hello [Pause] Fred Grest- Yes sir. [Pause] Fred Grest- WHAT?! [Pause] Fred Grest- I know I haven't been putting out new shows I've been busy as hell with wrestling. I told you when I decided to do this earlier this year wrestling came 1st! [Pause] Fred Grest- YOUR DAMN RATINGS WILL GO UP! I am gaining more and more of a fan base each day. I may be at the bottom of the barrell right now but the fans love me! [Pause] Fred Grest- WHAT?! You want to give Rosie my show. [Pause] Fred Grest- FIne.... talk to her about your show I am sure that will fly..... again. Oh wait it got canceled! You stupid fuck! [I hang up without my TV Show where he interviews fake celebrities is now over. I always did care more about wrestling then I did that TV show. Too bad they wont have anything good to watch now though. No more beating up a fake Martha Stewert for calling you Fredrick, no more making fun of a fake Micheal Jackson, no more Twisted Cowboy. Well in the Twisted Cowboy's case thats a good thing. I don't need that fucking jobber trying to attack me anymore! I should have never of hired him to make fun of Jestin Mounts. The aftermath wasn't worth it since it was only a dark match for WMW. So the show is over? Like my WMW T-Shirt reads "Fred Grest...... I am the show". Folks.... and the show is about to be kicked into high gear.* Fred Grest- Off the air.... *The always sexy Valkyrie walks into the house coming home from lord knows where.* Valkyrie- Hey honey... whats up? Fred Grest- My show was canceled.... Valkyrie- What?! Fred Grest- Its because I haven't been putting out shows lately the last one was against Luis and that one sucked. The ratings have been failing from what they told me over the phone and now they want to give Rosie my slot. Valkyrie- Oh yeah that'll work.... did so well the last time. Fred Grest- Yeah why don't they have a sing a long with Ozzy Osbourne. All the classic baseball tunes all the time! At least him singin "Take me out to the ballgame" has some entertainment value to it. Valkyrie- Actually that would just be sad.... he should just stick to songs like Crazy Train. Fred Grest- Well your probably right on that one but its better then that one. It was still better when MTV had Marilyn Manson join Eminem to sing some crappy rap song of his and way better then some fat lesbian telling me to adopt kids. Rosie.... the reason you are adopting is because you are a lesbian. Not that I can blame you after all it is by force since not even an ugly man would lower himself to your annoying ass. I doubt there is a man alive that would go that low to get a piece of ass. Valkyrie- You should so have done a show with ummm "her" on it. Fred Grest- Yeah I should of but I can't now can I? Valkyrie- Well no but its still a great idea. Fred Grest- It is isnt it? Valkyrie- Yeah.... so any news from Odium? Fred Grest- No but I am sure he's just tied up at the moment. Valkyrie- No pun intended right?' Fred Grest- Actually it was intended Valk. You know its always fun to be tied up or get tied up. Valkyrie- Ofcourse its always fun to get kinky. Fred Grest- Yeah and since he's 6 foot 6 thats the only way he's getting tied up. Valkyrie- Odium seems like he enjoy it he seems like one of those people that live an alternative lifestyle. Fred Grest- What makes you say that? Valkyrie- Nothing just felt like a good way to insult Odium. Fred Grest- You really need to try harder when you do this. Valkyrie- Why? Fred Grest- Because you suck at it and it gives them more ammo on me. Valkyrie- Shut up Fred! Fred Grest- Is that the best you can do? Valkyrie- Keep this up and I'll have a headache tonight! Fred Grest- Blackmail! Valkyrie- No I am a white women..... if I was a black male we would both be gay. Fred Grest- Moron.... Valkyrie- Was that better? Fred Grest- Yeah actually good job. Too bad it wasn't directed towards my opponent everyone knows you can insult me to no end. Valkyrie- This is true..... So are you going to cut a promo or anything or are we just going to keep rambling. Fred Grest- I enjoy rambling its always the best part of my promos. Valkyrie- So the best part lasts from start to finish? Fred Grest- See you can insult me you just suck at it when you do it to anyone else. Am I that easy of a target? Valkyrie- Since I know you better then most people yes you are. Anyway cut your damn promo about Odium. Fred Grest- What if I don't want to? What if I just want to end it now and just say time to get ready? Valkyrie- Then your promo would suck like the last one did. Fred Grest- Fine have it your way. Valkyrie- What are you Burger King?' *I just shake my head at her lameness and figure I'll cut a promo to end it. Not to talk trash to my opponent just to end this lame conversation.* Fred Grest- Odium.... Valkyrie- Alright! A Fred Grest promo! *I just stare at her for a second and laugh. I turn back to looking into the camera to cut another classic promo.* Fred Grest- I doubt you know much about me so let give you a quick run down. I entered this tournament based on the simple fact that it could be my big chance to prove I am the best like I say I am. I win this I am the best that PTC has to offer. Sure I may not have the PTC Global Championship over my shoulder but is he not in this? He sure as hell is and he has the biggest target on his back out of everyone in this tournament. I really don't understand why though. You don't have to beat him to prove that your best here. You just have to win the fucking thing and that says you are better then everyone who enterned this. Better then Justin Evers, better then Jay Phoenix, and yes even better then Seymour Alsmay. My one concern is that I am not giving you everything I have Odium. No, I am not over looking beyond you and into future rounds that I may or may not be in. I have so much going on right now I don't know what to do. My best friend is in the hospital with a compound fracture that I blame myself for. I am in an Ace Suprior Title match against Shadow right after I face you. This is hell on the body! I don't know how long I can keep this up and I am one of the best condition wrestlers today. *I pause for a moment and think about...... well everything.* Fred Grest- You know Odium when its just you and I in that ring I wish you the best of luck. I have nothing against you so lets just have a good clean match. Two guys just going at it to prove they are the best. If I have it my way when its all said and done it will be me coming out on top. Odium.... you've messed with the rest now mess with the best because there isn't anybody like Fred Grest. *The scene fades to black.*
