Wrestler Profile with Richard Bacon

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 We find ourselves Red Rock's flat and for those who do not know Red Rock he lives in a building that was once early 1900's offices but were converted in to flats in the mid 1970's. The are probably the cheapest most run down flats in the area, but it's all Red Rock can afford. Being the kind of person he is the AWC pr agents find it increasingly difficult to place him with any endorsements because he's just not marketable. Red Rock is sprawled across his dirty cream leather sofa. He is watching Holly Oaks and laughing at the poor acting and feeble story lines. Sat to his left is his good friend and loyal sidekick Boolie. Boolie is a 7'7 man and comes from a large family but despite being tall he is comically stupid and really doesn't do much for the stereotype of large idiot sidekicks. He is pulling his feet toward his mouth and gnawing at his toe nails, much to Red Rock's disgust. Red Rock: Boolie. Don't do that, that is sick! Boolie replies and his words are disturbed by his mouthful of foot. Boolie: It's got to be done though Red! Red Rock: You really are a knob jockey! Boolie: Wasn't that program supposed to come round to interview you today? Suddenly a cheesy Midi theme tune just finishes playing and we find ourselves outside the block of flats where Red Rock currently resides. The camera shot is now pointing on Richard Bacon's face as the opening sequence to a new show being shown on the Wrestling Channel in the UK Richard Bacon: Hello! My name is Richard Bacon, and this IS Wrestler profile. We're outside this old…rather dirty looking building to meet Red Rock! Lets go in! A another cheesy low budget midi jingle hits as Richard Bacon enters the building. The post production has added a crappy sped up effect to Richard Bacon is running up the stairs in double time. Red Rock: What TV program? Boolie: I guess I MIGHT of forgot to tell you… Red Rock: WHAT THE FUCK?! When are they coming? DING DONG! Red Rock: Son of a bitch! Red Rock who isn't really dressed for an interview in his tatty old cure t-shirt, still with milk stains from this morning's bowl of Asda's own brand Co-Co pops and his baked bean stained week old jeans gets up and makes his way to answer the door. Red Rock peers through the peep hole and notices a camera crew with their cheap cameras and their fluffing boom mic and of course Richard Bacon standing there with a grin on his face. Red Rock: Oh not that damn coke head Richard Bacon. Red Rock opens the door and applies a grin. Red Rock: Ah Richard I've been waiting for you, come in! Richard: Nice to meet you too…err…whatever. We'll just go set up over there! about 30 minutes later Everything is now set up so Red Rock and Richard Bacon face each other and prepare to begin the interview. Richard Bacon: Right lets get things started. OK it says here that "you are a loner that with the death of his mentor has found a new path to walk…" Red Rock stares blankly at Richard Bacon. Red Rock: My mentor isn't dead…he's just really fat now. I'm sure he'll be dead soon and who you calling a loner?! I'm not a loner… Richard Bacon: OH my mistake that's you're opponent in the forth coming GTT tournament that was the next question! Red Rock: Jason Donovan? Richard Bacon: It says here Donovan Midas. Red Rock: Oh yeah that's the one! Jason Donovan sucked though! Especially for you? What was that all about? …what a rasclart! Richard Bacon delivers a strange look in Red Rock's direction. Richard Bacon: Right… you're colleagues say that you describe your self as a bed wetter, a bit of a pussy and a bit of a moody little bitch. Why is that? Red Rock's grits his teeth and his eye bulges out of his head. Red Rock: Who the bastarding hell told that? Boolie can be heard behind the camera covering his mouth and giggling! Red Rock: BOOLIE YOU BASTARD! Red Rock jumps up from his seat towards the camera and everything suddenly turns to static. Another 20 minutes later Richard Bacon: RIGHT now lets move on! You're currently wrestling in AWC how is that going there? Red Rock: Well it's not going tooooo bad i must say, I've been the Live Wire champion but I've seen stricken with a lot of injury recently so it's been moving a bit slow. But I'm fit and healthy now so expect me to probably win a match soon! Richard Bacon: You are described as "The Anti Hero" why is that? Red Rock: I think it's mainly because I'm not your typical wrestler, I don't train a million hours a week and I'm not really the typical image of a wrestler. I think people just like an underdog and that's me in a nutshell. Richard Bacon: So basically your a bed wetting pussy? Red Rock: Exactl- HEY! Shut your mouth Bacon! Go snort a line! Richard Bacon: Hey don't turn this on me, that was a long time ago and I've put it behind me. Red Rock: Fuck you Bacon, you were my favourite on Blue Peter and you let me down! GET OUT! GET OUT OF MY HOUSE YOU…YOU PENISLY DISADVANTAGED BASTARD I FUCKING HATE YOU! THIS INTERVIEW IS OVER! ARRRGGHGHHH! NGHHHHHAAAAGH!!!! Red Rock breaks out in to a little hissy fit before throwing things at Richard Bacon who by now is looking very intimidated by this grown mans actions. Richard Bacon and his crew abruptly leave as the credits roll over Red Rock huffing and puffing as Boolie rolls on the floor laughing at Red Rock.
