(His)tory of Blood - Part One

19 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-19 Good evening. You may or may not know me but that’s not important right now because by the time I’m done you’ll know all you need to know and then some. I’m here to enlighten you about some things you may have never known about the world you live in that has been happening for thousands of years. The story I’m about to tell you isn’t like any other story and it may or may not have a happy ending. For all I know you could be viewing this on your local news or on your computer and I may be six feet below ground…heck most of the people around you might be too. This story…my story is one way for me to explain to you some of the darker elements of the world that you’ve never seen or ever cared to know about but it is something that you cannot go without knowing. Creatures…no…I have to be more specific: vampires have walked this earth for millennia without your knowledge because of their ability to cloud your mind and manipulate it in such a way that you wouldn’t know that it was happening. They have their hand in everything from law enforcement, multi-media, branches of government…the porn industry you name it they have had regular average everyday people under their thumbs for nearly as long as time has carried on. A secret war has been raging since nearly the dawn of time and we are in the midst of it in which many casualties will be taken not only human ones but those of the undead as well. Nothing can stop it and nothing can change it…it is in essence the apocalypse and we’re sitting smack in the middle. You may be thinking that I am delusional or crazy but I assure you that is not the case; I have proof beyond a shadow of a doubt that this has gone on and I am sure you would be interested to know why. My name is Eryc Morgan and I was once a vampire. I know enough to say that we’re all in danger because something big is about to happen and it’s coming on fast. The oldest still living among the vampire world are going to awaken from a deep sleep and when they do all hell will break loose…literally. They will be hungry and they will need blood but they only seek that of their kind but if you think you are safe? You’ve got another thing coming. Every undead creature in existence will be fighting in the war to save themselves and won’t be afraid to use any of us as pawns to get the job done. Consider this as your biblical Armageddon just a lot more fucked up. I’m not saying this to try and scare you…actually maybe I am because I want you to be better prepared for the nightmare that is going to be unleashed upon you The vampires you see in books and on television are only stereotypes for the kind of monsters these beings really are and coming from someone with experience in this area, I find it hard to deal with the evil that ebbs and flows from their veins. A real vampire is like two separate people wrapped up in one demonic package. You have the side that most people see: the façade. Then there is the other half that people never get to see and that is the purest form of depravity known to mankind; the type that would dismember their sweet old grandma if she were to cheat in a card game. While I can’t promise you that the world won’t end…I can assure you that I am going to be one of those on the frontline fighting for this world’s survival and that is why I am coming to you now to tell my story. And just in case someone comes across this fifty or even more then a hundred years from now they can know about someone who has been on both sides of this struggle and how brave he was to try and make things right. **Eryc reaches over to the finely stained wood table next to him and reaches for a glass of water. He only drinks a little just to keep his throat wet because his story is not one that is very easily told nor would it take a short time to do so. He’s dressed in a basic designer suit with black slacks and coat with a crimson red shirt and black tie to match the outfit along with of course the traditional leather shoes with a silver buckle on them. His long light brown hair with lighter highlights is pulled back behind him as he didn’t want to have to fuss with it while he was taking care of this business.** I am going to give an account of some of my pursuits, acquaintances, and some of the more historical facts of my lifespan and as it may not be a complete account, I shall not omit anything I do not deem necessary or worthy of knowing and if I am going to do this right then I am going to start at the beginning of where all this started: with my birth. I was born Charles Alexander Martel in the year 780 A.D. in what was then called Western Francia to Emmanuelle and the man who was known as the "King of Franks", Charlemagne. He liked his women and this is something that was left out of historical texts of course but my mother bore two children by him, the other my brother whom many know today as Ash. My mother was one of his many mistresses (from what I’ve found out through research) and there was no assurance that either of her boys would ever have anything to do with their father’s legacy as a King. My brother and I were kept in secret and were considered bastard children and because I was rather large in stature by the time I became and adult it made things even worse. The people of the city more or less did not have much to do with us or our mother because of it. We could only hope that my father would make good on his pledge to us to see that we were given what was rightfully ours. He saw more promise in us then he did with his other sons (the actual heirs to his throne). My father was not completely wrong with his assumptions that his sons from his wife were rather useless. After my father’s death, my half brothers did eventually piss his legacy away but by that time it was much too late for either myself or my brother to do anything about it and we or should I say I had more concerns to take care of. My young adult life was very troubled and addled with tragedy but it was not always that way. All of this is very hazy but this is how my mind’s eye remembers it. I’ve discovered documents that have proved the falsity of this event but until there is concrete proof I shall When I was fifteen years old my mother came into contact with the Vlazsy family in which wanted to pair their daughter, Lia, with me. There family had been very prominent and figured that by marrying off their daughter to the son of a King that they may earn more moral standing and status. My mother knowing they were already of noble blood wanted to make sure at least one of her sons had a secured future and me being the eldest of the two was the better choice to betroth her son to. It was also a ploy for her to keep me from going to be a part of my father’s army as well because they would have taken me due to my size and strength even if I was thought of by them to be abnormal. After two years Lia and I were married and there was pressure coming from her family more then my own to produce children (they hoped we had a boy first so that they only had one grandchild) so that their would be another heir to my family fortune even if I did not have a chance to claim it, my offspring would. Their wishes came half true when my wife and I had a daughter, Angelia, on the thirteenth of October in the year 800 A.D. While my family was thrilled I could sense that my in-laws were not so acceptable of the fact that we did not have a son first. It was always my impression that they did not approve of me so much as they wanted what riches I might have garnered from my father if it ever came about (which it never did and will be explained later). Our daughter was very beautiful but the fates decided that it was not meant to be and this would bring about a course in which history might prove to have been different if the events I am to describe were not to have happened. The year 805 A.D. proved to be one of both the greatest joys and painful sorrows to be burned into my memories. Lia and I were still very deep in love and had a wonderful family that we hoped would continue to grow and in fact did as she gave birth to our second child who would be born in September of 804. However, the happiness was not meant to last as a plague started to sweep through the countryside and Angelia contracted it and while every doctor we could find looked her over at the time medicine was at a very weak stage; nothing could be done for her. Angelia died in March of 805 and left a huge rift within our family. **Eryc sighs heavily after drudging up the painful memory of his daughter’s death and this can be seen visibly on his face as the hint of water welled up in the corners of his eyes yet he remains calm and poised; set to continue on.** Lia’s mother insisted that she return back home and without any notice to me she was packed up and swept away to Zdice, her origin of birth. I had done all I could to get Lia to return back to me and our son back in Lyon but it was a feat too difficult to handle and I was left with a son to care for on my own and the grief over my deceased daughter. Drinking had become my way to cope and I made nightly visits to the nearby tavern to drown myself in whatever alcohol I could get my hands on which was not a good example to set for my young son but I still managed to keep him fed, clothed and safe with the help of my brother and his new wife, Gisele. Even with the love of my son to try and keep me sane it just was not enough and one night I shouldn’t have gone to the tavern would be the last night that I might ever see on this earth. I went in for my nightly occurrence of ale and more ale and a few spirits when I could see out of the corner of my eye a buxom redhead who I most certainly knew was a prostitute but what did I care? I was dying from the inside out as it was. After a while she approached me and I wasn’t going to refuse her services and just like clockwork we went to the upstairs of the tavern where they had rooms for people to rent for the night. I was drunk out of my mind so what would a little fun hurt right? Wrong. After I had finished with her and was even more spent she proceeded to drink the very life out of me and there was my first encounter with what would probably be known back then as a succubus. The feeling was the most sensual and erotic that I had ever felt in my life. It was like my mind and body were in complete bliss and nothing but the moment was what I could focus on. My body became sickly pale as the blood was being drained from me and I could hear and feel my heartbeat slipping all the while as this woman was drinking the fluid of my mortal coil and just before my body was about to give out I was given a choice. She told me that I could either live or die; it was up to me. At the time I could only think about my son and the thought of leaving him was too much to bare, however, little did I know is that I wouldn’t be able to return to him even if I had wanted to. My life as I knew it was over. I chose to live yet I was still choosing to die as she cut open her wrist with her elongated finger nail and held it to my lips and told me to drink from it and so I did: the essence she had taken from me was being given back but only a thousand times more potent. It was then that I faded out of consciousness only to be reborn a night later…a different man. My body was aflame with sensations…burning like I had been set on fire. The atoms of my genetic makeup were pushing and pulling in every direction they could and my stomach was paining with the sensation of being run through a hundred times. Immediately the contents of my stomach and bowels expelled themselves as my body no longer functioned like it once did and the smell of it was enough to gag a maggot on a gut wagon and with my senses becoming magnified it wasn’t the most pleasant aroma to contend with. There was fresh clothing for me set out after I took a bath which I definitely needed. I cleaned up and was now at the service of my maker, whom I had now found out that her name was Helena Reinard and she had only merely been posing as a prostitute as means to try to ensnare me. Why this was the case was beyond me and I never really thought that because I had royal blood in my veins that I’d be handpicked by a mistress of the night to be a companion of sorts and little did I know that I was going to have to fend for myself soon. That night she taught me the finer points to finding a meal and already I had begun to miss the taste of wines and meats which now was exchanged with the metallic taste of vitae (the vampire term for blood). It was explained to me that not only did blood sustain my re-animated form but also would give me abilities as I grew stronger and older. My concerns were just to quench my nagging thirst; it was the only thing on my mind and all that I desired at that moment. After dining on a few socialites who were making their way home from a dinner party they had been to, I explained to Helena that there was one thing that I needed to take care of before I was completely in her servitude. I needed to see my son for one last time. My family had been given a large estate as a gift of sorts from my father who when he could saw that we had everything we needed and did not struggle in poverty as my mother had done before she had been intimate with the King. After my wife’s departure, my son Brennan slept downstairs to be closer to my master bedroom in case he could not sleep or if he had bad dreams then I could handle the situation better. I stood outside his window and watched as he slept and knew that it was foolish to disturb him. He wouldn’t understand any of what was happening and I couldn’t see to hurt him when I was never going to be able to come home. My brother came in to check on him as well and saw me looking in and if I was unlucky enough to be an intruder I would have been run through with a sword but he came to the window and asked me where I had been the last night and a half. I could not tell him and he knew something was unnatural about my appearance: my skin was pale and deathly and my hair and eyes had become a different color as a result of my change (which I have been told does not happen to everyone). He began to recite verses from the bible and I grew uneasy. My brother told me to leave and never to come back and I did as he asked because it would have been tough for me not to have harmed him or my son if I were hungry. Hunger is something you cannot allow to get the best of you or it will take you places you do not wish to go and if unlucky one may never recover from it and be relegated to that of an animal without any control. I returned to where I agreed to meet Helena and she was not there. At first I panicked but then calmed myself with the ration that she was just entertaining someplace else maybe to corral future meals for us both. It was a mistake I made being a naïve newly created bloodsucker. The next few nights I spent looking for Helena without success which gave me the impression that without a teacher I would not be around for very much longer no matter what I did. Some vampire I was turning out to be. I didn’t expect to find others that were of the garden variety blood drinking type but I was lucky on a night I decided to go and hunt for food. This was when I meant Frederick DuBois, he was like myself but was much older and had served in the Roman army under Julius Caesar before being brought across into the darkness. Luckily for me he was willing to assist me and at first thought I was what our kind called a Caitiff, a vampire who did not belong to specific clans of vampires. I told him that I had overheard my creator talk about someone or something called a Toreador. Frederick explained to me that the word I had heard was associated with the type of vampire that I was: a toreador. He also told me that most of these vampires were artisans of various types and basically enjoyed as much attention as they could get. I now knew who I was affiliated with but it would be tough trying to find more like me to try and find out just exactly what I was capable of doing with the powers I was told that I would have and Frederick also explained to me that I could be in danger if I had been made without the consent of the governing vampire of Francia. With that discovery it was clear to me that if you went against this Prince as Frederick referred to him as, the penalty was very harsh and could lead to death if found guilty of it. Was I made without consent? I do not know but I was not likely to stick around to find out and run the risk of being destroyed because my creator was stupid enough to break the rules. The last good piece of advice that Frederick gave to me before we parted was that it was always a good idea to find out who the Prince was in whatever part of the world I might end up in and ask for his permission to grace the lands out of common courtesy; it is advice that has saved me on quite a few occasions. I packed up my things that night and would set out the next for a new beginning and to look for those who I shared a common lineage…the Toreador clan. **Eryc took another sip of water. He doesn’t seem to be very sure that what he is doing is the right thing but at the same time knows that if it were him in the situation of all the people who did not know what was going on in the world around him that having this story out in public would be a huge help.** The first place I decided to look for my fellow blood brothers’ (and sisters’) was in a place that was known for artistic expression and that was what is known today as Paris and while I wasn’t expecting to be welcomed with open arms I at least hoped that I would get a few answers to keep myself alive a little longer. Paris (I shall call it that as that is what it is now) was a much different place at that time then it is today with all the museums and romanticism that it brings with tourists and such but in the early 800’s it was not as fascinating as you might think. Bits and pieces of information floated my way as I managed to encounter a few of my fellow clan members but they were too busy with themselves to really be considerate. At least I found out that I was going to turn into a snob at some point when I grew in age. What I could pry out of them was that my kind could read minds and be able to influence people as well but there was a downside to being a toreador: they have this flaw when it comes to beauty. If something strikes them as being beautiful they become entranced by it and if they are not cautious it could cost them their undead lives. My thoughts at the time were that I knew I had to find a mentor but at what cost to me would it be? It should be said that anything that drinks blood and doesn’t die shouldn’t be trusted and at that time I fell under that category but I was without proper guidance. However, things would begin change for me momentarily. At the beginning of the New Year I would come across a man who would not only give me all of the knowledge that I would need to possess but also would become like a father to me (unlike my real father, whom I never truly knew all that well). One night I was having trouble securing food or what modern vampires like to refer to as a juice bag (human) when I came across a horse drawn cart with a man who I had a gut instinct was a vampire as well but could not tell. He must have sensed it in me and that is why he stopped to talk to me. Maybe it was just to find out if he had any competition or because I looked like a pathetic whelp…I don’t know really. He was a winery owner named Audric Du Chateau or well at least that is what he was as a cover because in reality he too was a vampire who had lived maybe a few hundred years before I was born. I did not know how he could sense my trouble but he offered me a spot in his carriage to ride to town with him as he was going there to drop off some of his labors to a local tavern. On the way there we talked some about how things were going in the land and what places were best to get a good meal and then he asked me about my story which I figured he seemed sincere enough so I told him my plight thus far and lucky for me he was a toreador as well and the most forthcoming one that I had encountered as well. With the knowledge that my maker (or sire as they are referred to) had abandoned me, Audric took it upon himself to take me under his wing and teach me more about my new heritage and in time would teach me more about my abilities and in return I would help him with his business in whatever way he asked me to. I would also be taught how to dress more appropriately as I was not as sophisticatedly dressed as I was informed I should be. Needless to say Audric was a man of his word and in the 100 some years I spent with him he taught me all that I was destined to know about the powers of the blood in which flowed through my veins, giving me new advantages in hunting down victims. We traveled throughout Francia as it was in parts a battlefield with the Vikings trying to take over the Franks; I guess that my sire had brought me over to the dark side because I was the son of the King thinking maybe it could be used as leverage but with her bailing on me it didn’t do much for her cause I would imagine. I’m sure you are wondering when I am going to get to the good part now that am to the part of the story where I know about my dark gifts. I will do well in telling you with some limitation what I was capable of. Our kind of vampires (and there are different types which I have encountered) have powers which are called disciplines and each clan has their own set of them but others can be learned if you can find someone willing enough to teach you. Otherwise you are mostly told to piss off which you can’t blame someone for not wanting to give you an advantage over them if you are a quick study and can master it better then they can. I’d love to sit here and explain all of the different clans and their abilities but I just lack the inclination to and only so much can fit onto a DVD you know? One of the first things I was taught was how to read people (mortals and other kindred) and scan their aura’s which gave me a huge advantage when it came time to find out if someone was cheating or lying. That particular discipline came in handy many times…I almost wish I still had it. I already knew that my hearing and vision were adept but Audric helped me with augmenting them as Toreador’s were able to do. Tracking and hunting became easier as well with the help of this new found ability. Audric also explained to me that in time more of these kinds of abilities would be possible such as communicating with people telepathically, be able to sever my spirit from my body and walk through the world, and be able to tell where someone I know is wherever they may be in the world. Of course there were others that fit the category in which you were able to use your mind but the ones I have described to you by far are the most important. Two of my favorites were the ability to look someone in the eye and back them down like a scalded dog if they even had remote thoughts of trying to lay a finger on me and also the ability to gain attention to myself which led to being able to persuade them to do things they might not normally do. I could virtually control any human I desired to and even some vampires who were weaker then me. As I would get older I would also learn how to render someone unable to act upon violence or express emotion if I wished to and the most interesting thing Audric told me was that in time I’d develop the ability to think about someone anywhere in the world and request their presence and they would come whenever I called them which would be very helpful when I needed help. I also learned to manipulate my undead body into moving at fast speeds unable to be traced by the human eye and if lucky a vampire as well. You would be able to use Superman’s running at the speed of a bullet as an example for how fast I would be able to run. NASCAR couldn’t even touch the velocity of a vampire on a good day. Having special abilities could be likened to a kid receiving money for a good report card: they want to run out and spend it on whatever they could get their hands around and in that respect I was not any different. **A smile forms on Eryc’s lips as he recalls some of his first memories of using his supernatural powers. Whether it was to gain fast money in a game (which he was able to manipulate and not get caught) or having droves of women at his side in which he would use for his own personal stockpile of food…you could say he definitely had it made in the shade.** The turn of the century was fast approaching, Audric and I agreed that it was time for us to part ways for the time being; both of us knew that staying in that particular area of Europe might not be such a great idea with the Vikings being such a pain in the ass. A few times I encountered those heathens and let me tell you and after I was finished it wasn’t exactly a fun evening (for them). **Eryc’s voice is starting to fade a little bit so he reaches for the bottled water he has been drinking throughout his tale and is very close to emptying it; he clears his throat of something that was bothering him before he continues on.** To move things along a little further and because I do not recall much that happened for the first couple hundred years of the new millennium, I will start with the first thing that comes to mind. I tired of Europe and chose to take my chances and get on a sea vessel and where it took me was where I would find shelter for a while until I considered it to be safe to return as the longer you stay in one place and do not age it tends to make people nervous and why shouldn’t it? Not knowing where I was headed I chose to not feed for a day or two and that was a mistake because in my hunger I had managed to eat a third of the crew on the ship and toss their bodies overboard. Can you say oops? I managed to ration the members of the crew because they started to become nervous when their numbers began to dwindle but eventually it was down to the first mate and Captain before they realized what was happening on their ship. Figuring that I could manage to use the Captain to get wherever I desired to go I decided that I could expend the first mate and if anything read the mind of the Captain in order to help him control the ship at night when I could be free to man it. To my utter surprise the Captain plummeted himself into the sea while I was down in the cabin drinking the first mate dry; it only dawned on me when I went up onto the deck that I was all alone on a ship without any crew and just myself who knew nothing about boats. My thoughts at the time was that God was definitely getting back at me for becoming a monster…and also that I was pretty much fucked. (Such language was not present at that time but it was pretty close to what I was actually thinking.) Instead of panicking I did the best I could to control the ship and maneuvered it in any direction I could which would coincidentally be east. Audric mentioned that there were some ferocious beings in the east and those of us who visited rarely came back only if we were extremely lucky. I was someone who did not let fear be a crutch and if I were to fight then I would do so whether I lived or was sent to my final demise. The ship reached shore in what I believe to be April or May of 1358 but in a day and age where wall calendars were not invented yet, all one could do was guess by the how long the days were and the type of weather it was. I waited until the cover of night to exit the boat and found myself on nothing but endless beach which lead to forests and what was in those forests even gave me the creeps and all I had to defend myself with was the dagger that I was given by Audric. While the dagger should have been enough, I would have preferred a sword just in case I was faced with the challenge of multiple attackers. Moving through the leaves and trees was not my idea of a good time but I did find a large log hut which I would know later on to be a dojo. This was a place where a secret guild of assassins (ninjas to be exact) trained in their arts. Being as brash and bold as I could be I just walked right in there expecting to be sent to hell or wherever I was going because the fact of the matter was there was only me and about twenty of these warriors looking for real practice. The situation called for one of those times where I used my powers of negotiation to weasel my way out of them using my head for a strengthening ball and was able to make a bargain with them. Our understanding of each other was not exactly the best but I think they truly feared that I would in fact kill them in their sleep as I was a demon sent to destroy them. It worked out in the way that they would be able to live if they taught me their ways. I managed to get them help me hide the boat so that their arch rivals would not find it and that way I had a place to rest. (Their huts allowed too much sunlight and I could not possibly risk myself being caught in the harmful rays.) The warriors I encountered were a family and went by the name of Arashikage. Their lineage dates back to a very long time and possibly even before my own birth but I am uncertain for sure. The ninja clans head mentor, Makoto Arashikage, was a very hard man but was also honest and sincere; I think he could sense that even though there was a distinct evil in me that there was also good as well. In time I really began to respect the family and would help them keep their mortal enemies, the Bafuku clan, from destroying their homes and way of life and besides that it was a way to for me to eat since I did not want to do it to the people who had given me so many new gifts. Those of which included the training necessary to be even more stealth like, the discipline of martial arts, and also a very well crafted katana (which I still have to this very day). I stayed in Japan for a good many years with this family and watched as young grew old and died and were replaced by the young after them and the cycle repeated itself. Before I left I helped them find a way to keep themselves out of the Bafuku’s way and situated them in land that would not be bothered and they became humble farmers and remained low key and under everybody’s radar. I am pretty sure there are still members of the family alive today in the world that continue on with the clans legacy but I have not come across any of them nor would they probably know who I was other than by a description passed on in legend which has probably varied over the years. My desires to go back home were growing stronger and by this time there would be none of my family left to recognize me or people for that matter that I encountered my last time there. The boat was still where I left it and made sure that it would survive more travel after it had been through many seasons before I took it to the shore. I was not going to go all the way around like I had done the first time which meant that I would have to dock someplace in the middle of where modern day Russia stands now. I stopped through Romania in 1456 where I had seen the morbidity of a man named Vlad Tepes, who earned the nickname of "The Impaler". The beast in me was starting to finally come out and my human side took a back seat as I started to play games with the dark ruler; going as far as leaving corpses in worse shape in comparison to his. Vlad had become acquainted with other kindred and knew just how to gain my attention and he did by sending word through others of my race that he was looking for the man who dared to compete against him. Of course I was not going to disappoint him and visited his castle a night later. It was in his chambers in which my presence nearly stopped his heart right then and there. Maybe to him the thought of a creature so cold hearted and vile excited him too much but who am I to say? After we spoke he chose not to send his army to destroy me and left it open for me to possibly do some scouting (and possible killing) for him against his enemies with the ability to remain undetected to them. My aiding him never came to fruition but if it had there could be no telling what kind of destruction the two of us could have caused. I was not necessarily reluctant to help him out but I had heard whispers of other vampires looking for me because it seemed they thought I was giving away secrets about our kind which was false; everything that Vlad knew was already divulged to him by another source. Romania became too much of a problem so I picked up and traveled west to nearby Hungary where I kept a quiet existence and kept well hidden from any vampire that might be residing in the area. Most of the early 1500’s were a bore with nothing too exciting going on while I was still keeping a low profile until I began to hear utterances about a woman who was being called "The Blood Countess". I needed to hear and see more for myself. Elizabeth Bathory, a beautiful woman with a sick and twisted soul that could rival many a man or woman I have known in my past. She was as gorgeous as she was demented and in my beginning state of deviance I was enamored by her. It was her methods of torturing young peasant girls that had my senses tingling and also helped make my decision to introduce myself to her. A social gathering was being held one night that I had not been invited to yet I used my charming ways to gain entrance. When the right moment came I quickly moved to her and told her that I knew what she was doing and I could help her only if I was given something in return. Elizabeth never discovered my secret even though I fed on her several times during the course of our partnership…she had a sweet taste if I can recall. Such things no longer mean anything to me as the lust and hunger for human blood are a part of me now but the memories that have been stored within my mind still linger every so often as a reminder of where I came from. Eventually I no longer wanted to be involved and she found other helpers to procure her the necessary victims by pretending to have a school for girls to teach them to be graceful and also proper etiquette. It didn’t take long after that for her to be suspected of the disappearances of girls after screams had been heard outside of her castle; they became very sloppy in the way they went about their torturing. Elizabeth and her conspirators were charged with sadistic torture and mass murder. All but one of her helpers was put to death and she herself had been able to use her power to not be executed but instead they blocked her in by using bricks and only fed her through a small slot in the brick. She died not long after that but remained a very strong legend in the area which probably have resulted in many folk tales the people tell now to their children. I should note that during my time with Lady Bathory, I earned the name that I carried with me for so long to different places across time and the earth. She had said to me that my motives were like a scavenger and I was much like a raven. Bestowed upon me was the name Corvus: which is shorthand for Corvus-Corax, the generic name for the raven and crow family of birds. Later on I would use other combinations of names that would have to do with the same family such as: Alexander Rook, which was my identity in the later part of the 1600’s before I had to go in a coma like state that vampires refer to as torpor. I’ll be getting to that momentarily.
