Funeralis

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-28 So… this was a real funeral. Definitely nothing like what X to the P had seen in the movies or on television. This one was well…actually sad. From what Summer Love told him, not only was his entire family there, but friends of the family, and then other representatives from Cherokee tribes ranging from all over. You would have thought the Native American Pope had passed, the way people were crying and hollering. It was only one man… but then again… one man was enough to break the hearts and souls of an entire congregation of men, women, and children. The service was held in a large Catholic church over on 12th Street NW, and parking was an issue that you might as well thrown out the window. By the time XP and the Bontecou women arrived, the lot was already full, and people had already began parking blocks away. They were gathering from all directions, and they were already crying before they could get within a block of the church. Hanson Love was… loved. "You two go on and find seats," Summer said, bringing the VW bus to park. "I’ll go on and find a parking spot around here… somewhere." Bizzy and XP climbed out and stood there as her mom drove off. They both stared up at the large dark gray cathedral. It’s seen better days, the stone and mortar weathered away into a dingy, dreary looking structure perfect for funerals. XP looked down at Bizzy, watching her stand there staring hard at the top of the building. He looked back up at the cross… and couldn’t help but stare at it for a moment herself. It was times like this that made you ponder everything about your life… like just how long DID you have left? It made you think about things, like what have you done with your life? Was it meaningful thus far? Or have you squandered a precious gift that was taken from people on a daily basis? What was there in life that you wanted to do, and why weren’t you doing them? Stuff like that was running through both their minds… and it left them breathless. Finally XP nudged her in the shoulder with his knuckle. She looked up at him, her gaze still as chilled at the breeze sweeping past them. "You set there, Biz?" She nodded, turning back to give one last look at the spire before walking on ahead. She actually looked less cyber-punk and more formal for the occasion, a V neck black dress with the skirt sloped longer than one side and black heels. XP was starting to feel bad about how casual he was dressed; no doubt he would be the only one in the church with sneakers on. He followed after her, climbing the stairs into the chapel. The usher had escorted them to a pew somewhere about the middle, but closer to the back as the front was reserved for the family. They sat on the end of the pew with a few spaces to spare, enough for Summer to squeeze in and fill the slot. Organ music played softly over the murmurs and soft crying of the people around them, making it hard to keep from being depressed yourself. Not that XP expected balloons and techno music playing… but no one wanted to be depressed. As they sat there waiting for the service to start, XP took some time to think about what he had seen of Peter Vetra. Thoughts of holds, strikes, and strategies to counter his moves were formulated fast; there was something about combat strategy that always came natural to him. Coach had made sure that he was prepared from all angles, often muttering about how the Boy Scouts were a thieving bunch of little pukes. Even so, XP learned his lessons well, and had managed to stay undefeated all this time. He was as virgin to defeat as Vetra was to staying in a lasting relationship… but that was something that he had read about online in his personal history. Vetra wouldn’t pop XP’s cherry though… Xian wouldn’t allow an average bloke like him to have the honors. He was waiting for Mr. Right for that… Okay, that just sounds gay. Summer arrived a few minutes later, sitting on the other side of Bizzy. Now giving the unspoken dress code for funerals, Summer Love would be the only person to come to a funeral wearing earth colors, a green blouse with a long brown skirt and matching boots. She really didn’t get any looks for her choice of attire; her eccentric ways were already known throughout the community here it seemed. No sooner than she sat down did the priest walk up to the front and begin the service. The casket sat at the front, a dark pine box surrounded by flowers, and from what XP could see, a few tomahawks, spears, and a Chief’s headdress. "I would like to thank you all for coming," the gray haired man said, straightening his glasses before clasping his hands in front of him. "We’re here to honor the life of Hanson Love, brother, uncle, cousin, friend, and son to many; not just in the Cherokee community… but in life as well." That was enough to pop a few crying corks, sobs echoing off the walls around them. He paused to let them ease back down, and continued. "We will begin now with a ceremonial dance performed by his brothers, uncle, and father." 5 men walked in from the side, dressed in full Cherokee warrior attire, and began their dance, offering both praise and remorse for their fallen family member. XP couldn’t help but stare with a blank look. He didn’t know what to make of it; he had never seen anything of the sort. It was bold… but it was tame at the same time, he felt. He couldn’t respond at all, actually… he found himself sucked into the ritual, finding peace himself in the meaning of it all. Not the dance… he had no idea what the dance itself was talking about. But the idea of dying… if he knew he would die, he would be at peace knowing that there were people left behind that both loved and respected him in life. He didn’t have family to do that for him… his funeral would be empty if not for the friends that he could make his family. He felt something warm in his hand. He glanced over and found Bizzy’s hand in his. He looked at her, but she kept her focus locked on the dance ahead. Guess she had a little heart after all, that cold blooded bitch… It was a beautiful service, that much could definitely be said. Afterwards Summer, Bizzy, and Xian went to the reception, where it became a huge family reunion of sorts. People Summer haven’t seen in years she was catching up with. XP just sat there with his slice of pie and bottle of water and watched everyone go around speaking to people. After a while, he noticed that Bizzy was in the bathroom longer than expected. Going to check on her, he found out she wasn’t in there at all after a tall Cherokee woman checked for him. Then a taller man with a ponytail came over, recognized Bizzy’s description, and said she went outside. "Why is she out here?" Muttering under his breath, he walked down the stairs of the hotel where the reception was held, and walked down the sidewalk some towards where they had parked their car. He could see her standing under the lamp they had parked the V-dub next to, leaning against the door with her arms folded against her chest tightly. She had her eyes down, looking at her shoes or the ground. XP walked over and leaned against the car next to her. "Nice service," first thing that came out without thinking involved. It got a nod out of her though. He nodded back, slipping his hands into the pockets of his black khakis. "Family’s pretty cool. No one gave me slack about coming in wearing Chucks and khakis." "Yeah…" The Biz was more quiet than usual. Maybe the thought of death had put a chill over her. XP glanced in her direction, squinting like he was searching for something to say to break the silence. "Look, I know death’s not cool, but I wouldn’t worry about it right now. I know we’ve still got plenty of---" He lost his train of thought for a moment. Cat got his tongue? No… more like Bizzy got it… with her own tongue. She had pounced on him, grabbing his head and pulling him down so she could have an easier level for jamming her tongue down his throat. XP allowed it, but then broke off for a minute and stepped back. "Whoa, whoa, Bijou!" He held his hands up to her to halt. "This is wrong." "Yeah…" that didn’t stop her from lunging in and locking lips again. He kissed her back but stopped again, holding her back. Boy, did XP know how to ruin things. "No, wait. This isn’t right, okay?" He waited to see if she was listening. When he knew that he had her undivided attention, he continued. "I don’t have any condoms." She rolled her eyes. "Neither do I." They stared at each other for a moment of silence, for both Love and their own Lust. Finally XP grabbed the handle of the VW bus and yanked it open. That was enough sign for Bizzy to return to business, grabbing XP and pushing him back onto the seat. She mounted him and closed the door behind her. And as the saying goes… if the bus is rocking… don’t come a knocking.
