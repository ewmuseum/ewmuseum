Attack of the Primordial Squid of DOOM***

19 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-19 After their latest debacle, the Killer Pirates TV show producers have been scrambling to find something to do to repair the image of the show after the incident with the teenagers at the McDonald's. Because of said incident, the Killer Pirates were fired from their job. Now, they have nothing to do except stand around their ship and do nothing like couch potatoes. The producer is getting frantic. He has to come up with some sort of lame brain scheme to get the viewers to tune back in and see his two stars before he loses his job and his career goes down the toilet. But, the funny thing is, the viewership has gone up ever since the incident. It's almost like people want to see what these two idiots are going to do next. But the slimy producer wants to shake some things up on the show. His brainstorming has led him to a brilliant idea he's throwing at his stars right now. "Pirating," he says to the Pirates as they look at each other, amused by this suggestion. "The network wants pirating. We here at the television network want to see what the life of the Killer Pirates is really like. We want to see how life is like living as a pirate and a clown." Jack quickly corrects him. "That's killer clown, mate," Jack says with a smile. "Uhhhh…right," the producer responds. "Anyway, let's see what kind of adventures you guys get yourselves into on a daily basis." Jack smiles deviously and looks at the clown. "Well, I'm not so sure the network can handle what we do on a daily basis," Jack replies with a smirk on his face. "The daily life of a pirate involves many violent actions, drinking, sex, more drinking, plundering, drinking, and then some sailing to boot, mate. And the life of a killer clown involves eating people, making balloon animals, eating all of the food in Darth von Doom's refrigerator, watching Jerry Springer, eating cotton candy, luring victims into his circus tent of doom, and doing various gags on people. Now, the question is mate, can your network handle what we do?" The producer smiles at him. "Jack," he replies. "Our network shows violent images on its television shows all the time. Cursing happens on all of our nightly prime time dramas. We show scenes of sex or using sex related humor on almost every show on the schedule that's not kid related! There are sleazy characters and scantily clad women all over our network's programming. I think we can handle anything you guys dish out." Jack and the clown look pleased. "Good," Jack says to the producer. "Then we head for Tortuga at once. What you say, clown?" The clown jumps up for joy, clapping his hands together like a giddy little kid awaiting the big prize at the Toys R Us his mom is taking him to. "Aye!" the clown exclaims. "We're going to plunder, rape, pillage, and eat lots of food!" Jack turns to the producer, who still doesn't seem to have any idea about what's he's just gotten himself into. "Well, I guess it's settled, mate," Jack says to him. "It's off to Tortuga for us!" * * * Later that day… Jack is steering the ship on the sea as the producer, the film crew, and the Killer Klown are all on deck as the crew films the Pirates doing their work on their pirate ship the Black Nemo. Jack turns and sees the camera right in his face. He funnily jukes and jives to miss it hitting him in the face. "Hey clown," Jack says to his best friend. "What do you think we should do at the pay-per-view?" The Killer Klown doesn't seem to know what to say. "Ummm…about what?" he asks Jack. The producer seems confused as to what the hell they are talking about and also doesn't find this to be very entertaining. "What pay-per-view?" he asks Jack. "The network didn't say anything about a pay-per-view." Jack turns to him and looks a bit sarcastic at the moment. "Oh, didn't you hear?" Jack asks with a smirk on his face. "We, I mean us, I mean we, me and the clown, per se, are scheduled to face off against some of the best teams in the world for the PTC World Tag Team Championships that we never lost against three teams we've never faced on a pay-per-view we've never been on before. Well, that's because it's never been done before. But it was done before, but not with the same guy, it was a different guy with the same concept, only it wasn't a pay-per-view, it was a tournament, I think. Anyway, we're on it." The producer blinks his eyes and looks blankly at him. "You're telling me that you guys are going to be on a wrestling pay-per-view?" he asks in utter disbelief. Jack nods his head. "Yeah," Jack replies. "We're facing some retards, some stoners, and some Mortal Kombat movie extras in a battle to the death over some belt that only like five teams want but everybody else forgot about, so we're going to win it and then bury it in the desert with the body of the Spartan!" "Who's the Spartan?" the producer asks, even more confused than before. "Some guy," Jack replies. The producer grows impatient. "I know that, but who is he?" the producer asks again. "Oh, just some bitch boy who'se related to Archibald MacGregor." "Who's Archibald MacGregor?" the producer asks. "Exactly," Jack replies with a devious grin. The Killer Pirates start laughing hysterically, but the producer has no idea what's so funny. "What is it guys?" he asks frantically. "What's so funny about that joke?" Jack smiles and replies, "Well, the pay-per-view is called 'PTC vs. Archibald MacGregor.' You see, Archibald MacGregor is an outsider who won a PTC Title and now, the PTC is sending like everybody on their rosters after him and are trying to win the title back!" "Wow!" the producer exclaims. "That's fascinating and would make some great television. So guys, what do you think will happen?" Jack answers without hesitation. "I think MacGregor's going to kick everyone's asses and keep the belt," he replies. The Killer Klown nods his head. "Yep," the clown replies. "He's going to kick everyone's ass." "Why do you guys say that?" the producer asks. The Killer Pirates look at each. "Because the PTC has like fifty billion people wrestling or fighting or jerking off there and like he kicked everyone's ass before," the clown replies. "It's only logical that he does it again." "He's right," Jack replies. "We're going to get our asses handed to us. Well, everyone who's not in GLOBAL, that is." "Why not GLOBAL?" the producer asks them again, still even more confused than ever. "Because we're in GLOBAL and we're WAY better than everyone else," the clown replies. Jack nods his head. "Exactly!" Jack responds. "Except for that time when our website went down because the Cave Hulk ate it." The Killer Klown nods his head. "The same thing happened in the UWF," the clown replies. Jack nods his head. "Aye," he says. "Fucking Cave Hulk. He'll eat anything." "Now let me get this right," the producer says. "There's an actual creature, wrestler, whatever, named the Cave Hulk wrestling in GLOBAL?" Jack nods his head. "Aye," he replies. "But he's a fighter, not a wrestler. He eats cows, fucks women, and bashes thing with his club!" Just as the producer is about to interrogate them some more, the ship starts shaking. The crew and the producer start looking to see what is the matter. "What's that?" he asks the Pirates. Jack looks around and sniffs. He then catches some sort of smell in his nose and immediatly knows what it is, but is too frightened to say anything to anyone about it. "WHAT IS IT?" The producer yells at the Pirates. Jack gulps and knows the true terror of what he is about to tell them. "It's a giant squid," he mutters out, still shaking in fear. The producer looks at him like he's nuts. "Seen Pirates of the Caribbean one too many times Captain Morgan?" he asks Jack. A noise is then hear on the side of the ship. Jack shakily points in the direction of the noise and yells, "GIANT SQUID!" Jack and the clown then run over to the side of the ship to look down at the spot where they heard the noise just to see how close the squid is to eating their ship. They both run over and look down to see how close it is. They look down over the edge and all they see are some dolphins swimming alongside the ship. The producer runs over with a cameraman to shoot the squid attacking. "GET A GOOD SHOT OF THE TENTACLES!" he says as the cameraman leans over and takes a shot of the dolphins down below. The producer looks and then stares at the dolphins, stunned not to see a squid attacking. He then looks back up, glares at Jack, and then heads down below decks. Jack then shrugs his shoulders. "What did I say?" he asks the production crew, but no one answers. * * * The ship has now arrived at Tortuga and the Killer Pirates have brought the producer and the production crew to the Squid's Hole Tavern and Pub in Tortuga's party district. Inside, there are pirates everywhere who are drinking and playing cards. At the table near the back, the producer and the Killer Pirates are drinking rum, all three casually checking out the scene. "WOW!" the producer exclaims. "I never knew that rum tasted so good!" Jack nods his head. "Yes," he replies. "It tastes better than some fat poontang in the morning, mate." The producer glares at him. "We can't air that on TV, you know." "I know," Jack replies. "It's all a moral dilemma in life, isn't it? What do, what not to do. What to say, what not to say. It's all a matter of good parenting. While I, in fact, was not a product of parenting, most of the viewers watching at home were. Well, except for the cave men in the Geico commercials." "True that," the clown replies after chugging down a cup full of rum. "Although there is nothing wrong with them," Jack continues. "But those commercials remind me of that movie 'Encino Man.'" The clown slams his fists on the table, scaring the producer. "YEAH!" the clown yells. "That movie was so cool! I mean, he was like climbing on trees and drinking from the Slurpee machine at the 7-11 and eating junk food." Jack smiles. "Yeah," he responds. "And then he was getting hit on by hot chicks with big boobs!" "Yeah," the clown replies with a smile. "Boobies." The producer looks like he's getting all sexed up over this conversation. "I SO have to see that movie!" he exclaims as he chugs down the rest of the rum from his mug. Jack nods his head. "Classic cinema," he says. "It won the Academy Award for Greatest Movie in the History of the Known Universe and All Dimensions in it." The clown nods his head. "Oh yeah," he replies. "It was good." The producer gets excited and calls for some more rum. "Three more mugs over here bartender!" he yells across the tavern. Some nasty looking pirates glare in his direction. One of them shows his ugly teeth as he growls at Jack, who is taken aback and moves away from the mouth of death. "HA HA HA!" the producer exclaims. "ENCINO MAN! What a great fucking idea for a movie! It's like the Cave Hulk thing, or something. It's like, think about it. It's like…it's like, this man…from a different time and place, thaws out, and looks at boobies for sixty minutes! It's the greatest show ever conceived in the world! I'm going to produce it all over the world and get lots and lots of boobies!" The bartender comes over with the mugs and starts setting them down, but the producer gets over zealous and flings the tray up in the air! The mugs crash into the table next to them where some rather tough looking pirates are playing a game of poker. The pirates get angry, blaming each other for the beer bath and start fighting! "Oh bugger!" Jack exclaims as he and the clown go to hide as now the entire tavern is one big pirate brawl! Pirates are punching each other all over the place and the producer even gets involved. He smashes an empty bottle of rum over the head of a tough, nasty pirate! "Wow," Jack says while hiding underneath a table. "He's got some balls." The clown nods his head. "True that," he replies with a smirk. But soon, the producer's luck runs out. He gets grabbed by a giant pirate, who grabs him by the collar. The pirate growls at him and the producer grabs a bottle of rum off the table next to him. "Wanna drink?" he asks the giant pirate, holding the bottle up from him to see. The pirate takes the bottle and then punches the producer, causing him to fly half-way across the room and right into the camera! The feed from the show cuts out and the viewers are left with nothing but static. (/The end./)
