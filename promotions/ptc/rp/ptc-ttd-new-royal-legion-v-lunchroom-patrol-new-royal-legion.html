TTD - New Royal Legion -v- Lunchroom Patrol

10 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The New Royal Go Back To School The New Royal Legion were one of two undefeated teams in the Round Robin of the Tag Team Division Tournament to crown a new champion in the PTC. However, being one of two undefeated teams did not guarantee them a spot in the final. Instead, they are now fighting in the #1 Contenders Match against another team from XUW - The Lunchroom Patrol. If one was to ask any Legionnaire why their saviours were in the #1 Contenders Match when they clearly should have been in the Final it would be due to the ongoing conspiracy that has plagued the New Royal Legion since they started out. However, like always, the New Royal Legion fight on in hopes of defeating this conspiracy. This is just another chapter in the ongoing saga of their battle against the mysterious conspirators. And like the other times, the New Royal Legion hope to beat the Lunchroom Patrol in the #1 Contenders Match and move onto a title shot against whoever the champion will be at the time. And then, maybe just then, the conspiracy will finally have been defeated once and for all as they proved they really are the greatest tag team ever. Of course if someone had a nickel for everytime that was said by the New Royal Legion they'd be a millionaire now. *** The scene fades into parking lot where there is a Blue PT Cruiser pulling into the parking lot. Slowly the PT Cruiser comes to a stop as it pulls into a parking spot. The driver’s door opens and out walks Larry the Legionnaire. Larry quickly walks to the back door and opens it. He quickly walks around to the other side of the car and opens the other door as well. On one side of the car, the legend from New Zealand, Danyon walks out and peers around the parking lot. On the other side of the car, the greatest tag team wrestler ever, Bailey steps out from the car. Bailey nods at Larry and then he turns around to see another car pulling into the parking lot as well. “Born to be Wild” can be heard really loudly from the brown, rusted 1984 Grand Am pulling into the parking. That can only mean one thing; the legendary Crudmobile is pulling into the parking lot. The Crudmobile is of course the legendary car that is driven by the Whiskey Warrior, the Pungent Pugilist, the Flatulent Fighter, the Smelly Superstar, the one the only “The Bastard” Al Crud. The Crudmobile pulls up next to the Legionmobile as it could be known as too. And Al Crud, the one time legendary GIWF world champion steps out of the car. Al Crud was once the greatest wrestler in all of the land. He was selling out crowds from Alaska to Australia, from Barbados to Burma, from Chile to Chad, from well…..the picture is clear. However, Al has gone on a losing streak of late and is half the man he used to be, figuratively speaking that is, since he has actually gained a lot of weight during this losing streak. Still, the crowds love Al and every now and then you see glimpses of his greatness. As Al steps out of the car, he lets out a big fart that surely would have made someone shake their head in disgust if they weren’t used to Al and his infamous gas problems. That’s the other thing about Al, he has a notorious gas problem that makes him fart and belch constantly. Because of the association that Al has had lately with Bailey, Danyon and Larry they have become accustomed to his smell and his somewhat loveable faults. Collectively these 4 have become known as the New Beer Legion. Bailey and Danyon are the New Royal Legion, the most decorated team in XUW History and the self proclaimed greatest tag team ever. Larry the Legionnaire is the New Royal Legion’s lawyer as well as their manager. Larry is also significant because he was the first Legionnaire ever, the Legionnaires are of course being the New Royal Legion’s fanatical fans and are very cult like in their following of Bailey and Danyon. Somewhat surprisingly the parking lot that these two cars just pulled up in is not the normal spot in which these four men are normally seen. Instead of the Howard Johnson Hotel in XUWville, these four men have just parked in a school yard parking lot and are now approaching the entrance to the school. Larry: Why exactly are we here again? Why are we visiting this school? Bailey: I think we need an edge on the Lunchroom Patrol. We have faced them twice now when in one on one matches or two on two I suppose. We won the first one but they beat us last time. This is the rubber match. Plus, if you add in the three way tag match we had with the Lunchroom Patrol and the Hardcore Icons they beat us twice now in the past year. We can’t let them beat us once more. We have to get a leg up on them somehow. I figure if we went to a school and patrolled a lunchroom over lunchtime we can understand where these bully haters are coming from. Larry: Don’t you think that someone else would have done this before in the past? This seems a bit lame to me and like you are running out of ideas. Bailey: Me? Run out of ideas? Never. Danyon: I think that it has more to do with that we don’t want to stay in the hotel room any longer. Ever since the New Royal Legion Suite went under renovations, I’ve wanted to leave the Howard Johnson Hotel more now. I feel really claustrophobic in that room. Al: Don’t forget that we have to leave the room sometimes to get more beer, since the fridge in the new suite doesn’t hold as much as in the other one. Al farts. Al: Uh….do you think they’ll have a beer fridge in this school? I could use a nice refreshing Iron City Beer. Bailey: Al, I have a feeling that this school won’t have any beer to offer you. And if they did I somehow suspect it wouldn’t be Iron City Beer. Al: Oh yeah. I guess these kids would be too young to drink. I guess everyone can’t start at the age of 10 like me. Larry: You started drinking at age 10? Al: Uh…yeah, my dad sat me on his lap and gave me a small sip of the Iron City Beer. It was great. Larry: Wow. Bailey: I had my first beer when I was young…………… Larry: But you are from Canada where you have to drink to pass the long cold winters. Bailey: You are from Canada as well. Larry: Oh yeah……….umm…….so why exactly are we here at the school again? I’m sure that some team in the past has gone to a school to pretend to be like the Lunchroom Patrol. Shouldn’t you have come up with something original? Bailey: That’s simple, just because some other team did this and it didn’t work for them doesn’t mean that it won’t work for us. You are forgetting that we are the greatest tag team that ever lived. Us doing this is totally different from some other team doing it. We are smarter and better than any other team. You are forgetting that I’m the greatest tag team wrestler in the world and I know that these little things will work. Larry: So, going to a school and watching over the lunchroom will do this? Bailey: Yes. Larry: If you say so. Did you think of calling before hand to make sure it was okay with the principal? Bailey: Ah, no, no I didn’t call before hand? Why wouldn’t they let us in? Larry: I don’t know most people would have a problem with four grown men, complete strangers walking into a school and wanting to watch over the lunchroom. Does the word pedophile have any meaning to you? Bailey: Are you implying that………..we are The New Royal Legion. We’d never do such things. Larry: I know that and all the Legionnaires know that but this school principal doesn’t know that. All he sees is four grown men coming into school asking to watch over the lunchroom. Wouldn’t that strike you as a bit odd? Danyon: He does have a point there. I mean back in New Zealand I’d never allow a stranger to look over my flock of sheep so I doubt that a principal would allow us to do that here. Bailey: But you two are forgetting one thing. The principal of this very school is a Legionnaire, therefore he will surely let us in to watch over the kids. Larry: He’s a Legionnaire? Why didn’t you say so in the first place? Bailey: Give me some credit here guys. I’m the greatest tag team wrestler on the planet for a reason. I thought this whole thing out and found which schools in XUWville had Legionnaires teaching there. And this school here was the school of choice because the principal was a Legionnaire. Larry: Sometimes I question why I ever doubt you. Bailey: You’d think that after two years you’d know better……… Larry: I guess there those few months last year after you returned from being abducted that you were an arrogant prick……… Bailey: Shhh….we shall never speak about that time. I’d soon as like to forget that. In actual fact you were right it was during that period when we last fought the Lunchroom Patrol and lost that match. So, Danyon and I were having our differences then and now that we are back on the same page length we will win this match. Danyon: That is true. The last time we fought and lost to them we were having some differences between the two of us. He was more worried about his Submission Title than about possibly getting anothe shot at the tag titles. But now we don’t have to worry about anything even though you are the XUW Intercontinental Champion. Bailey: Well, I don’t know about that. There is always something we have to worry about. Danyon: Right, that damned conspiracy. It’s amazing that we were only one of two teams to be undefeated in the tag title division during that little round robin tournament yet we are not in the final match going for the title. Instead we are relegated to the #1 Contenders Match for the title. Clearly the conspirators had something to do with that. We should have been in the final because we never lost a match. We’ll just have to prove to them by winning this match. Finally, after what seemed like an eternity, the four of them reach the front doors to the school and walk in. That must have been the biggest parking lot ever in a school but that’s beside the point. Once in the school, they go straight to the office to try and find the Principal. They walk up to where the secretary is sitting. Bailey: I’d like to see Principal Jason Burris. The secretary looks at the four men standing there. She politely smiles at them. Secretary: Do you have an appointment? Bailey: No we don’t. Secretary: Oh, well, then you are going to have to book an appointment and come back another day. Bailey: I don’t think that’s possible. I think we should be able to see Principal Burris now. Secretary: I’m sorry sir. That’s not possible. You have to book an appointment. Danyon: May interject here for a second. I think it is possible that we can meet that principal. I think you should go back there and tell him that we are here to see him. I’m sure he’d be more than willing to meet with us once he knows that we are here. Secretary: And exactly who are you guys? And why is that slob over there digging in our staff refrigerator? The secretary points to Al Crud who has opened the door to the fridge and is searching through it. Danyon: Don’t mind him he’s looking for some beer. We told him that chances are you wouldn’t have any in there but he was determined to look. Secretary: You were right and you can tell your smelly friend that we don’t allow alcohol in this building. That goes against all regulations. And please will you get that man away from the food in the fridge. His smell is probably killing any good food in that fridge and I dare think of any diseases that might living on him right now and infect the food. Danyon: Hey Al. Come over here for a second. This nice secretary would like to talk to you. Al takes his head out of the fridge and looks back around at the secretary. He stands up and dusts off his shirt. He licks his fingers and parts his hair and eye brows. He proudly and confidently makes his way over to the secretary. Al: Hey baby, you wanted to see him. What can Al “The Love Machine” Crud do for you? Did you want me to give you a detention because I've been a very naughty boy? The secretary shudders. Secretary: I just wanted you to get away from the food that’s all. And I also want you to leave this place or I will have to get security out here. Bailey: Listen, I don’t think you realize who we are. I’m Bailey, this is Danyon, that’s Larry the Legionnaire and that’s The Bastard Al Crud. Secretary: You don’t say? I’ve never heard of any of you. Bailey: Yeah, well, I think Principal Burris has heard of us and I think that you should go and tell him that we are here. Secretary: You don’t have an appointment, I can’t let you in. Larry: Listen ma’am. I’ll tell you what. If you go in and tell him that we are here and he says that he doesn’t know us or won’t speak to us then we’ll leave. How about you do that? The Secretary weighs her options. She looks at the four men standing in front of her. Secretary: So that’s Bailey, Danyon, Larry and Al Crud. Is that right? Larry: That’s right. The secretary picks up the phone and dials a number. Secretary: Yes, Principal Burris there are four gentlemen and I use that in the loosest sense of the term wanting to see you. They claim to know you and that you’ll speak to them right away. There names are Bailey, Danyon, Larry and Al Crud………..Sir? Hello are you there sir? The secretary hangs up the phone and looks over at the New Beer Legion. Secretary: He hung up so I guess that means he doesn’t want to speak with you. No sooner did she say that the door to the principles office opened up and Principal Burris came out. He turns to the secretary. Burris: Hold all my calls for the rest of the time that these guys are here. Secretary: You mean you actually know them? Burris: But of course. That’s Bailey and Danyon, they form the greatest tag team in history of the world, the New Royal Legion. That’s Larry the Legionnaire, he was the very first Legionnaire, the Legionnaires being the greatest fans in all of wrestling of which I am proudly a member of. And that other guy is the one million and first Legionnaire, the legendary Al Crud. You know how many championships this guy has won? No, of course you wouldn’t but he has more than you could have ever imagined. In fact get him a drink of whiskey, that man deserves one. Secretary: But we don’t have any alcohol in this school. That goes against all the regulations. Burris: I know that you have a bottle of whiskey in your bottom right drawer. So, don’t act like it’s not there. And pull it out and pour the man of a whiskey with some soda on the side. The secretary reluctantly pulls out the bottle of whiskey and pulls out a can of soda water as well. She hands the bottle and can to Al Crud who now has a very large smile on his face. Burris: So, what brings you guys to my school today? Danyon: You know how we have this match against the Lunchroom Patrol for the #1 Contenders Spot of the PTC Tag Titles so we figured it would be a good way to understand the Lunchroom Patrol if we came to a school and watched over a lunchroom. Bailey: Yeah, we thought what better school to come to than one run by a Legionnaire. So, Principal Burris……… Burris: Call me Jason please. Bailey: Alright, Jason it is. So, Jason is it possible we can look over the lunchroom for awhile. Burris: I have no problem with that. Let me inform Michelle Stancel our lunchlady that she will be having four guests with her today. Secretary: You have to be kidding me, sir. Parents are going to be all up in arms that you are allowing four complete strangers into the lunchroom. Burris: I will deal with that. But I have a feeling that they won’t be upset after all these are four complete strangers. These are four of the biggest superstars in XUW today and in the world of wrestling. I will simply explain that some international superstars wished to come to our school and speak. It’s not everyday that the New Royal Legion show up at your school. Bailey, Danyon, Larry and Al all have smiles come across their faces. Well, Al’s already had a big smile from being handed a bottle of whiskey but for Bailey, Danyon and Larry a smile came across their faces. Burris: We have some time until lunch and we have to meet Ms. Stancel, so let’s go into my office and talk for a bit. It’s every Legionnaire’s dream to sit and have a talk with you guys. Bailey, Danyon, Larry and Al Crud follow Principal Burris into his office as they wait until they have to meet Lunchlady Stancel. The scene slowly fades out as the secretary shakes her head in disbelief of what just happened in front on her. She looks like she could actually use a drink from that bottle of whiskey that was in the bottom right drawer if she hadn't given it to Al Crud. There is definitely no chance she will ever see that bottle again.
