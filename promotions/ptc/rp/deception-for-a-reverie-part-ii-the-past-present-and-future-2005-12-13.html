Deception for a Reverie, Part II: The Past, Present, and Future of Violence

13 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-13 The river of crimson that was gushing from her head was more than any person should be able to bear witnessing. But one man in particular was able to stand there and witness the agony and defeat of one female. He was even able to muster up a smirk. His victory was not met with regret for what he had done, but with a pride no man should feel after abusing a female such as he had. 'Please?!' The only word she could find the courage to say, ask even. She was begging, possibly for her life. The man that stood before her would not listen. Nor would he care. All he would end up doing is take his wedding ring from his left ring finger, wipe the blood from its shiny yellow gold surface, and carefully place it back onto his finger. The women he cares for, longs for, vowed to spend the rest of his life with, lying helplessly on the floor beneath him. At this point in time he did not love or cherish her. He was not upholding his part of the bond between them, nor would it trouble him in the least that he had beaten the "love oh his life" within an inch of her life. The woman, with outstretched arms, covered in blood from covering her face from the beating, spoke once more. 'Please!?' This time said in a more stern voice. It was more of a plead than the last. The man, who had previously been without words, finally found it within his heartless soul to converse back. 'If you've got nothing important to say, just keep your mouth shut. Need I repeat myself again?' He asked back, his smile turning into a glare that can only mean bad things to come. She began to tear up more than she already had. The droplets coming from here eyes instantaneously turned red as they blended with the dried blood that rested upon her fragile face. She had managed to make it to her knees, her outstretched arms still in the air. Her strength was damn near gone, but one thing had kept her strong. 'Please!' She shrieked. 'If not for me… Do it for him!' She continued to exclaim. The man's face had quickly been drained of any emotion. It was almost as if he'd been caught in the act by a police officer, and he would immediately be taken to jail. But it wasn't a police officer, or even another adult. No, the man had turned around to find me, his two year old son, standing in the doorway to the dimmed lighted garage. I was too in shock to cry. The event was to be a traumatic one in my life as well. It was the first time I had seen my father abuse my mother. It would also be the last time he ever laid a hand on her, for a while, despite the fact that she was too big of a person to split the family up after the event. The love she thought he had for her, accompanied by the love she knew she had for him, and even more so, the love she would have for me, her baby boy, would keep her hopes high that this would never happen again. I, however, despite being the age I was, would always remember the event. I would never forgive him for what he had done, despite it being the last time. Not to mention the fact that an internal fear of my father would haunt me for the rest of my life. -------------------------------------- 'Do you remember that Kaden?' My mother asked me? I did nothing but nod my head. I had actually put the memory aside, wishing for it to never come back. It was only on this day when Mom came back to ask how I was going to tell my Father I was leaving. We were sitting in the living room, safe and sound, as the father figure was out doing things "more important" to him than anything in the world. He was off getting a personal meeting for me and the Greek Olympic head. I couldn't have "been more excited" in my life. Yes, I was still stuck at home with the parents. My plain ticket wasn't scheduled to get me onto a plain to the US for a couple of more days. Mom and I were discussing ways to tell my father I was leaving. All of which brought back bad memories of the past. Since the day I had received "the letter" my life had become worth much more than it ever had. The letter that would get me out on my own, and out of reach from my father, that is if I could get away from him. 'So?' Mom questioned, trying to break an awkward kind of silence from the story time we had just shared. 'So?' I said back, as if it to be the obvious response to her saying it as well. I was rocking back and fourth in the chair on the opposite side of the coffee table to mom, who was positioned in another rocking chair. The sunlight coming through the window from behind me lit up the room. Despite mom's attempt to break the silence, though, the only sound to be heard was the creaking in the chair as I rocked back and fourth. The silence lasted a few more minutes before Mom had thought of something to get our minds off of the past. 'Well, you never did explain how you got to round 2 of this GTO5 tournament thing. Why don't you go ahead and do that since we've got time while your father is out.' She said, finally getting a chance to hear a little about my career, and a little about what I want to do with my life. I smiled to her, kind of looked down at the floor, and then back to her. I gathered myself a bit, and then put the past back behind me as I reflected on something more recent, and of happier memories. 'First off, Mom, its GTT5, not GTO5.' I kind of chuckled. 'Well, anyway, it was brought to my attention a day or so right before the Round 1 match that I didn't even have to leave Greece to participate. My opponent came here to face off for the rights to move on to round two, and it turns out that my independent federation was happy to host the match. So the other night when I went out on my date with whoever it was I said I went with, I was actually at the Athens Underground making it to round 2.' I commented while Mom smiled ear to ear. I knew she didn't really understand any of it at all, but she was happy to pretend she did. 'So basically these girls you've been going out with have been to cover for going to wrestle every week?' She asked, as if she didn't know the answer already. 'Exactly!' I said back to her. 'It was the one thing I figured father would be proud of me for. "Gunna go get you some ass, eh son?" He would ask me. I'd be happy to lie to him so I could go out and have fun. Anything I could do to get away from him and HIS stupid dream. Now with the opportunity of a lifetime, I've got the chance to fulfill my own dreams. It's just too bad I'm leaving you behind with the guy. Who knows what may happen to him when he realizes his dreams of me becoming the Olympic gold medalist he's been working so hard for me to become. I just hope nothing happens to you again.' I said, with a stern, yet worried, tone to my voice. She smiled at me, with her eyes closed, and nodded her head. 'I'll be fine Kaden. Don't you worry about me. You just get out of here. Enjoy the World. Enjoy America. It's a wonderful place. Enjoy the rest of your life.' She said, her eyes starting to water up. I nodded my head. 'I plan on enjoying it all. I'm just not too sure how to break all the news to him yet. I still can't forget the first and only time I tried telling him I wanted to wrestle and not to actually wrestle.' I said as I began to reflect back on the past. I could tell Mom started to think about the incident too. 'Yeah, that was a horrible day. We are so lucky he didn't snap on us then.' She said as we both started to think about it. I remembered it all too well. -------------------------------------- The slam of the door had startled me and my mother. We were sitting on the couch in the living room when my father had returned. He had left extremely upset, but wasn't about to tell us why, well, until now. 'Alright you two, what the fuck have you been hiding from me?! I know something is up. That singlet I found in Kaden's room is NOT the one issued to him through the Athens Recreational Sporting Association. Now tell me what you two are hiding from me, and I won't be so hard on you!' He bellowed. His face a shade of red that made him look like he had been under a summer’s hot sun for days. Mom and I just looked at each other, trying to think of a way out of this one. I was actually seventeen at the time, and was starting to get it into my mind that I was gone in a year or so anyway. I had stopped caring about hiding my stuff as much as I had in the past. Little did I know I was going to be stuck hiding in my own lies for five more years. Mom was the first to think of something. 'What are you doing snooping in his room to begin with?' She asked, keeping her cool. 'I'm his father!' He exclaimed. To him, that's all the excuse he needed to do anything. My big mouth ended up getting the best of me in this situation, however. 'Yeah, father. Not much of one.' I said, slightly under my breath. I had realized it was said out loud, though, shortly after I shut my mouth. 'What was that boy?!' My father had asked. I felt his stare burning through the back of my skull. 'Now Alec…' My mother said, trying to interrupt this altercation. He glared her down; no words were even needed to stop her from speaking. The fear of that glare was still inside of her. It was the same one she got shortly before the beat down of her life. She sat straight back into her chair, and remained silent. He then turned his attention back to me. 'Alright you little shit, I could care less what you think of me, I know what kind of father I am. The one you look up to. I'm the kind of father you should be proud of having. I've turned you into a machine that will one day be in the Olympics. I know that's what you want, you are just like me. So give it to me, what the hell are you doing with that other singlet?!' He asked, still with an angry and loud tone. Despite being scared to death of my own father, something inside of me sparked, and I bolted up out of my chair, and stood to him, face to face. 'It doesn't matter what it is. It's just something for me to wrestle in. Why the fuck does it matter to you? Huh? I'm nothing more than a lab rat to you. I have been my whole life. Feeding me with shit, trying to get me to do what you want. Haven't you done enough to me in my life that I need to be questioned about more now? Just be fucking happen I haven't moved out already. If it weren't for Mom, I would be gone by now!' I screeched. I could feel my face turn red as I waited to be popped in the face. I could just sense the violence coming on, and to say I wasn't scared would have been a lie. 'Stop it right now!' My mom yelled as she stood up. That's all that was left to be said on this day too. It triggered something in my father, and her being the one who triggered it, she took the first hit. This time, though, I was old enough to do something about it. I quickly kicked him in the knee cap, trying to possibly break it if possible. He stumbled, but gained his balance as quick as he lost it. I had no time to gain any sort of advantage before he popped me in the face five or six times before I even knew it. I was out cold, and who knows what he continued to do to my mother after I was knocked unconscious. Neither of us knows, but it was the second time my father lashed out at us. It was a second time too many, and just one of the many reasons why I was hoping to get the Hell out of there. -------------------------------------- 'What in the World are you two talking about? Huh?' My father asked. He had crept in undetected to us in our daze of the past. 'Nothing!' I said in response. 'I've got good news for you Kade.' He said back. Mom reacted before me. 'What is it honey?' She asked with fake happiness in her voice. My father was all smiles at this point. 'The Greek Olympic Council has decided to give Kaden his shot at the 2008 Olympics. The Head of Council will be over tomorrow to have a talk with him, and after that, his training and qualifications begin. Isn't that great?' He asked, proud as can be of himself. I stood up, as if to bring back bad memories, only this time staying out of his space. 'Dad?' I said, asking for his attention. 'That's the first time you've called me Dad in a while Kade. What's up?' He asked back. I couldn't believe it myself, but I was about to drop the bomb on him, maybe. 'I don't…' I paused for a second as I watched my father's head tilt to the side a little. His face would have started to fill with anger after this point had I continued. 'I can't be in the Olympics. At least to support the Greek cause.' I said to him, waiting for him to lash out at me. 'What the Hell do you mean?!' He asked back, a slight temper to his voice. I gave a slight sigh. 'I got this letter the other day from a major sponsor company based in the US. They want to…' I pause, trying to think of a way to put this so he wouldn't just knock my lights out, again. 'They want to fly me to the US, and put me on their sponsored wrestling team. Traveling the World, wrestling. Big money for doing what I "love", and all that good stuff.' I finished. I was expecting a decent response from him, or at least hoped for, because of how I presented the idea. I had actually told the truth, just in a way to where he would think of the wrong kind of wrestling as to what I was actually talking about. His response, though, was still a bad one. 'Are you kidding me? All your hard work into the toilet because some company wants to put you on their team in a foreign country! You have got to be kidding me! You are kidding me! You are staying here, and that's final!' I shook my head. My mom could see the anger coming out of me through the expression on my face. I looked to her and nodded slightly, as if I was thinking the same thing as her. She backed off, and exited the room. I looked back at my father, straight in the eyes. 'No, I am not staying here. I am finally going to do what I want to do. I'm not going to live the life you never could have had because of YOUR dumb mistakes. Now if you want to beat me half to death, that's fine, but it's not keeping me here either way. I've got a tournament to finish. Yes! I said tournament. One I've already advanced to round 2 in. And I'm finally going to get criticized for how good I do, and not how bad I did, or what kind of "pud" opponent I had. So what if the guy I faced in the first round sliced his hand up before the match, and couldn't really compete to his full potential, I didn't care, it was still a great match. It feels good to not have you riding me over everything. So whether you like it or not, I'm fucking out that door later this week. Just try and stop me.' I concluded. I was expecting a lightning quick reaction out of my father, but I got none. So I proceeded to walk back to my room where all that I needed was already packed and ready to go. I could hear one last thing before I confined myself to my room for the remainder of my stay in Greece; my father mumbling. 'Stupid little good for nothing prick son of mine. Who fucking cares anymore? I don't! Ungrateful fuck! He'll never even make it to the States! I hope his fucking plane crashes or something. Kills him, and only him. That'll show him. Karma is going to bite him in the ass, and when it does, he's going to be sorry.' He ceased his mumbling. I shook my head and yelled out my door. 'Fuck off!' I didn't care how he reacted to that. I shut my door, and locked it. If he was going to come after me, so be it. I was leaving in a couple of days anyway, and he wasn't going to stop me from leaving. Hell or high water, I was going to finish this tournament, and I was going to make a career for myself. With or without the support of others.' The scene fades to black.
