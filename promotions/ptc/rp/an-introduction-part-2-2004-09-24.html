An Introduction part 2

24 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-24 The rope snaps tight as a parachute flies open as the body snatches hard instantly slowing its decent towards the street below. The chords strain at the sudden rush of air as the canvas whips in the wind as the hands control by pulling the handles steering its sudden slowed decent, the man looking about as he wonders why he had not done this sooner. Looking about he feels the wind as he closes his eyes looking about. “They still look like ants marching back and forth, leaving the small little worlds behind in apartments or meaningless jobs. Makes me wonder why you and I must Dance Shadow. You seem to have many issues about you. I have many myself and sometimes they can be very confusing if not disturbing. This would officially be my third PTC tournament type of thing I was involved in. Well maybe the second or even perhaps the first. Three times in the PTC events. I see you cling to your darker shaded self in how you want people to see you as. I have a whole folder on information about you. It is amazing what a little money and the internet can tell you about an opponent who hides in the shadows. I once hid there and I also ruled there. Yet perceiving me as a threat would be the correct course of action for you. Pain comes in he who was once named Famine of the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse.” As the ground rushes up to meet his feet he pulls on the controls as they suddenly pull up and his feet touch down as a soft and deliberate landing is made in the center of the street. He unclips the clasps and hooks as he drops the shoot to the ground and he begins wrapping and rolling it up. He walks over to a black suburban smiling as he climbs into the suburban as a large shadowy type of man is sitting in the passenger seat, grinning Shaman looks over and smiles. “Thought you weren’t going to make it this time?” The shadowed man leans over and looks at Shaman with no emotion as flicks lights brush across his face as cars scream by and he reaches and pulls a cigarette out as the lights illuminate a scorpion tattoo. “Would I leave a longtime friend in distress or abandon him when I think he has finally comeback to his path?” Looking serious Shaman looks at the man and grins sadistically. “You have done it before why stop now?” The man inhales deeply upon the cigarette and flicks it out the window and motions for Shaman to drive. “Drive and we’ll talk.” Chuckling as he pulls the suburban into gear. “Sure whatever you Say BS.” Driving down the street watching the lights pass over casting the city like orange glare though the interior of the suburban the shadowy man sits back looking at the road ahead. Shaman concentrates on driving as he occasionally looks over at the man sitting near him. The man begins to speak, but Shaman seems to be listening half heartedly. “Paying more attention to what I say may help you avoid certain issues.” Shaman frowns as he suddenly jerks the suburban around a car as he speeds up. “What issues the fact that you retired and left the WWA? I didn’t ask you to comeback. You are having what they call withdrawal. I am much younger than you and trust me I could have retired, but hell I am only 30 years old and have made a great career out of wrestling.” The man leans back shaking his head. “You’re facing an opponent named Shadow. She took the name for some reason. Perhaps she hides more than she is revealing.” Pulling the suburban to the side of the road slowly he looks over at the man. “Look BS this Shadow hides from her past. I have the research and have done more digging than any normal man should. She faces me in a match that I want to win. I have personal goals and stakes in this matter. I look to set the bar at its highest level. Show the rest of the world that I am not to be taken lightly any longer. Perhaps I haven’t had the best choices these last few months, but do know this I intend on making it to the end. You can either sit there and shut up or actually for once tell me I have the right intentions.” The man looks over at Shaman and shakes his head and speaks. “Do you truly have the right intentions or are they more of greed?” Slamming his hands against the steering wheel Shaman wheels about and squeals out into traffic speeding away from the city out into the darkness of the road beyond. “My intentions are simple. I look to bring back some pride to the mess that is my career. My intentions are to represent the organization I have worked for. I intend on doing this because I can plain and simple. I haven’t felt this way since I was a rookie. I am a proven veteran and recently I have reawakened the drive that I had thought lost. Will I lose? Perhaps, but it won’t be without a big fight. Didn’t you know fear is never boring?” Coming to a stop sign the man climbs out of the suburban as it comes to a stop. Turning Shaman watches him closely. “You will see me again Shaman. I tend to show up when you least expect it.” Laughing Shaman responds. “Yeah like a bad rash that goes untreated.” Walking away from the suburban the man disappears down the road into the darkness as Shaman drives on down the road thinking of his opponent Shadow. “So where does this little chapter go now Shadow. Do you take the road less traveled or do you continue in the eye of the beholder?” Reaching up and wiping a few strands of his jet black hair out of his face he looks at the next road sign. “Louisville 80 miles could be the place for me to relax and rest until we meet. I do hope you are not underestimating me Shadow. It would be a grave under sight on your part. I think the darkness may prove to make you blind to the man that is hunting you. We are moving in the same direction on a single road. Your direction right at me and mine straight at you, as the Appalachian Mountains loom closer I begin to ponder the brackets and look at all the other competitors in this tournament. Regardless of who is in it we still have to concentrate on the here and now. I was caught with the idea you were looking past me. Wouldn’t be to wise in my honest unbiased opinion.” Changing lanes he passes a big semi truck as he continues down the highway at a decent clip. “Lemme give you an example of some people looking past me. Chance, Kash, Grunge to name a few in a four way match where I beat them all to win the WWA Heavyweight Championship belt back in the spring. They continue to look past me even though I have been known to shut the critics up. What are you going to do Shadow? Are you going to ignore me and look past me much like everyone else? Are you going to deem yourself the victor before the match even takes place? Shadow I am waiting for you in the ring of destiny. I will come for you and I will hurt you. If you are lucky you will even bleed. There are things in life you should look past. I am not one of them.” Continuing the drive he pulls off on an off ramp and pulls into a truck stop. He gets out of the suburban and makes his way over into the restaurant. Curious enough it is a Flying J that is situated after Louisville Kentucky. He walks into the restaurant portion and sits down at a booth. A waitress walks up to him and she takes his order. “Sir Can I help you this evening?” Looking at the menu he speaks calmly. “Yes I’d like the steak from the kitchen and I will also have the buffet here as well. Also can you give me a large glass of sweet tea.” Nodding the waitress writes the order down and then stands straight adjusting her dress. “Will that be all for this evening sir?” Nodding as he responds. “Yes thank you.” Rubbing his hands together he stares at the shakers of salt and pepper. “Dragging this on could make things ugly and boring couldn’t it Shadow? I mean what can I say you and I are like salt and pepper two complete opposites regardless of the fact that I am male and you are female. We come from two different worlds. Mine is a road fraught with pains and hard decisions. Yours is of confusion and missed opportunities. I have learned a bit about your history and I follow it with interest. You see I do enjoy knowing a few things about my opponents especially the reasons they hide their real names from everyone. I once did this, but have chosen to reveal who I truly am. Am I afraid of what is to come of it? Not in the slightest bit. You see I tend to embrace my past. I learned from doing time as a teenager. Yes I went to jail for doing a crime. What type of crime would you ask? The crime is simple it as a crime of anger where I beat a man so severely that he had to be hospitalized for months. What they did to me was put me away in jail for exactly the amount of time that man was in the hospital. In the end I served in jail as an innocent man because it was a provoked attack. I use this as a rallying point in my life and something to feed off of with energy.” Looking at the waitress as she set the iced tea down and walked away Shaman frowns. “Wish Suzy could have come and watched me this time, but she has family business to attend to. She is like an anchor for me away from the ring. In the ring my abilities and my drive are my anchor. At home Suzy is my rock. She was there for me in the WWA when everything seemed to be going south. Unlike you Shadow my friends and companions haven’t abandoned me. I do hope my words will eventually sink in to your head. Things aren’t as easy to overcome as you would think. I am not some mere obstacle in your path this time Shadow. I am a competitor that wants to hurt you to move on in a tournament.” Grabbing his fork as the waitress returns he turns his plate after she sets it down and walks away he stabs his fork into some of the steak as he lifts the knife and cuts it with one smooth slow stroke. Cutting a small piece into a bite sized shape he chews it and swallows it down enjoying the moist seasoned piece. “There will be no quarter asked for nor will there be any quarter given. This is the fight of your life and I hope to hell that you are ready. There is no mercy in what I am going to do. I hope you are prepared for whatever eventuality will come of this. Only one thing I will guarantee is this. There will be a world of pain to follow in the footsteps of the man they call Shaman.” Sipping at his tea he smiles and looks about. “Sleep well Shadow, but not too soundly for there is a hunter that is going to haunt your every nightmare.” Slowly he begins to eat his food as several people walk in front of him as he seems to disappear into the restaurant crowd.
