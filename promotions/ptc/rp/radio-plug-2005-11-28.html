Radio Plug

28 November 2005

Date: 2005-11-28 "I could pick Almasy or Deville, an underdog or two like Paddy and Ember. Maybe go a safe bet with Shadow, or Tessa Windsor, Rollins, Michael Stevens, The Uber Judge. The fact is this tournament is so full of recognized talent that it's impossible to pick a winner based on their name. Just because somebody did well last year doesn't mean they'll do well this year. But I was told I have to pick, so I'm looking at the names and I see somebody I didn't expect in the tournament. Johnny Lexicon. He's got a few years experience in big money situations and he knows the inside of a wrestling ring better than veterans with ten ten years on him. He's got motivation to win, he's got the ability to win, he's got the momentum to carry him into the finals. He's coming out of a huge angle with GCW's Toxic and he is in the best shape of his life. A dark horse for Lexicon fever has been contracted and is starting to spread. My pick to surprise alot of people and take this thing all the way. Drake Coleman is an experienced wrestler but I'm not marking, Lexicon is a much more exciting personality. He's got an energy to him, when he's in the ring and doing his thing the fans can't help but stand up. It doesn't hurt his image either to be rumored as Eva's lover. Lexicon doesn't have the credentials as half the people in this tournament with a career his length, but he's got wins over people like Jeff Wylde and Draven Masters, the only wrestler to ever make Lestatt Knight tap out. These are hardcore pros and they've all admitted to respecting his talent, and that, right there, speaks volumes for a man's ability. Fans are easy, but impressing your egomaniacle peers, that's something." - Midas T. Gold, guest host on 'All That Glitters is Not Gold'.
