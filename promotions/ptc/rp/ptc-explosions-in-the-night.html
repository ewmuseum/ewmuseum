IG 63 Promo - Explosions in the Night

2 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Competitor: James Monroe Title: Explosions in the Night ---- Santa Maria, California The night air smells like the ocean. A slight breeze comes over the hills toward the city below. It blows through my hair as I look down toward the city that I call home. It's almost midnight and I have not been home for hours. It's been a long night. Can't remember half of it. Been drinking since the break of dawn. I can vaguely remember losing my job sometime in the morning. Got a pink slip out the door of my locker as did everyone else at the factory that I used to work at. After that I know I went to a bar. Then I kept drinking...and drinking...and drinking some more. I don't remember too much aftet the third beer. I know that I was headbanging to some Snoop Dogg song and had some weird chick that looked like Lady Gaga trying to pick me up. Got her number. Look down at my right forearm and see it written on there in big, blue letters written in pen by her in misshapen letters. [detail withheld] Feel my pocket for my cell phone but don’t find it. The only thing I find is a condom. Put it back in my pocket and look around for my cell phone to no avail. I probably dropped it somewhere in my drunken stupor. I feel like an idiot for that to be honest. Makes me feel inferior or like an alcoholic, to put it more precisely. More images of her scroll through my head like a picture slide show that you would see on a computer. Remember watching highlights of the MTV Movie Awards at the bar with her. She thought that the Bruno thing was hilarious. I, on the other hand, went to the bathroom and threw up before washing up. I truly hope Eminem busts a cap in his ass over that stupidity. Came back from the bathroom and sat back next to her. Only remember bits and pieces between now and then. Know I made out with her for a good period. Probably had sex with her too. After that, all I remember is wandering the hills outside of the city like a homeless traveling vagabond. The wild hills by the ocean are the only wild part left of this area. Everything else has been built up. The city has grown at a rapid rate since about 1995. All of the losers from other states who thought they could make it big here failed epicly and now we have a bad housing market. Those idiots ruined my city. Now it is nothing more than a morally, socially, and structurally decaying cesspool in the middle of the California coast. This city is the murder capital of the world much like Santa Carla, a city down the coast, once was back in the late eighties. As I continue to walk, something catches my eye and rings through my ears as well. An explosion on the east side of the city. A huge orange fireball appears from the origin point of the explosion that I just heard. Smoke starts billowing out from the where the explosion took place. I hear a loud siren in the distance that echoes through the night. See lights of emergency vehicles speeding to the scene. Decide to stop walking and just to watch the event unfold. I mean, it could be a chemical or gas leak and I don't want to die because I decided to be like a bug going toward a light only to be shocked when it gets too close. Soon there is another explosion in the same area of town as the first one. Many thoughts go through my head. Could it be terrorists? Is it a plane crash or a meteorite hitting the ground? Who knows. I hope it's none of the above. Hear screams now. What the hell is happening? Listen to the noises of the city below. Hear screams, sirens, and a third explosion. Look and see more chaos. Hear more screams. It's a terrifying scene. Now I feel compelled to go down there and see if I can help out. Drunkenly stumble down the hills back into town. Upon arriving at the outskirts of the city, a fourth explosion occurs. The city is now in a state of complete chaos. I can hear glass breaking and more screams. Looking around, I notice two more things. The city is eerily quiet now and devoid of any people whatsoever. It seems almost like the Roanoke Colony back in the 1800's. Maybe the same thing happened that is going on here. Continue to wander toward the east side of town. Still no people anywhere to be seen. This is scaring me and I don't scare easy. Don't hear any crickets chirping anymore. There is nothing but silence save for my footsteps on the pavement. After walking awhile I finally reach the origin of the explosions. Find a factory of some kind burning out of control. Nothing really gives any indication as to what has happened here this day. Suddenly, I hear a noise. Look around. No one there. Decide to see if I get a reply from someone if I say something out loud. Maybe that might coax someone out to tell me just what the hell is going on. Not getting my hopes up, though. So I wipe the sweat from my brow, look around at the desolate surroundings, and ask one question. "Hello?" I ask hesitantly before waiting patiently for a few moments to see if anyone answers me. No reply. Hear some more noises behind me. The slight breeze from earlier picks up. A smell fills the air. Smells like someone died. Hopefully that's not the case and it's just a sewage leak or something like that. Whatever it is, it smells nasty and I try to cover my nose to keep from smelling any more of that horrid smell. More noises. Look around but still see nothing. Then something creeps up behind me. I turn around and see something that horrifies me. Quickly run before whatever it is gets me. Run inside the nearest apartment building and hide inside. Just then, I feel dizzy and soon everything fades to black.
