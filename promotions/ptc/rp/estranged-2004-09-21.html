Estranged

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 A light rain has begun to fall as a small pleasure craft cuts through the impeding waves of the Atlantic Ocean. The boat is only about sixteen feet in length, with the rough seas; a larger boat would have been needed. Though cutting through wakes at nearly fifty miles an hour, the blue and white boat seems to be out there for a purpose. A suicidal purpose, but a purpose none the less. Standing over the steering wheel with a mix of salt and rain water dripping off him is Tony Stecchino. He disappeared over a week ago into Hurricane Ivan, searching for his one true love that he managed to estrange nearly three years ago. Something that made him snap mentally, develop a whole new personality. Sever him from the rest of the world. It’s the moment that his former friends figured he went cold. He no longer feels human emotion. Except for one. He never stopped loving Melissa. Ever since Melissa left him, left him for Maverick Reno, regret, hatred and love have clouded his mind. He hurt others to make himself feel better, though it didn’t make him feel better. So he began to kill, though every person he killed, he saw his face instead of theirs. He could never kill himself, though his tough exterior was a little boy. His mother beat him severely as a child with everything imaginable. Sometimes the words hurt more. He hated his mother. He wanted her dead. Nothing would make him happier. So on that fateful, hot summer night in a parking garage of Miami, Florida, he pulled the trigger, he put two in his mothers chest and one in her head. His little brother, Chenzo ran and hid in the car. A smile of relief spread across his face, he was happy that he had done it, the torture of his body and mind would end at that moment. It had just begun. As Anthony Sr. ran over to hold his dying wife in his arms, Anthony Jr. still had his weapon pointed at his mother. He looked at his father, the man he respected more than anyone in the whole entire world, tears running down his face, the blood of his dead wife soaking into his shirt, staining his hands, not saying a word. He looked up at his son, shaking his head in disbelief. This happened in the movies, not in real life, this is just a dream. One more shot was fired into the humid air. Anthony Sr.’s head jerked back and slowly faded to the ground. His arms still around his dead wife. Their physical bodies together on earth, their souls on a path to heaven together. Both devout Roman Catholic. Tony had shot his father because he never stood up to his mother, he allowed the beatings to continue, and he kept that woman in his life. The beatings could have stopped if he was ever home or he did something about it. But no he was always in America, wrestling, becoming a dominant World Champion. It was his fault, ‘I got beat.’ ‘I got beat, is my mind actually working in the first person, for my memories have always been in third person, like watching them from a movie.’ A smile grows across his face; the rain comes down and pelts him. His ripped and soiled T shirt flaps behind his back, with drops of water flying of it in the wind, back into the ocean. “Stevie, I think I just had a break through! I see myself as me again!” There is no response; he is talking to the wind. “Stevie? Did you hear me? I think I am getting better!” Again his statement goes unheard; the dark clouds get a bit darker. When you're talkin to yourself And nobody's home You can fool yourself You came in this world alone (Alone) Tony looks around the boat only to find that he is alone in the boat. He looks down at the steering wheel; he could have sworn that Stevie was on the boat with him. He looks up again, he grips the steering wheel tighter in his hands, and he closes his eyes. Stevie Nickels is standing on the dock, shivering, he is soaked, his designer suit ruined, a look of a concern in his eye and infectious. It is spread across his face. He looks down into a boat; Tony is hooking up the fuel lines to the engine. “Tony I am begging you not to go out to that island, Melissa won’t be there.” Nickels pleads. Tony stops and stands up, he looks at his friend right in the eye. “I have to my friend, I must know for myself, I need to find her, she is the key to my sanity and my eternal happiness, some say God and Jesus bring inner peace, she is my holy figure, and she is the one I worship…” Tony pauses for a bit, tears boil up in his eyes, he puts his head down, he doesn’t want his best friend to see him cry, though he has before on the matter, not right now though. He grabs the mast line and unties it from the dock, he pushes off the dock, the boat pushes through the dark marina water. It stops. Tony looks up and sees the massive arms of his friend holding on to the boat. It nearly had pulled him into the water. “Stevie, please let go.” “Tony I cant let you do this, this is suicide.” A single tear falls from the eye Stevie Nickels and runs down his cheek; it washes away with the rain water. “Are you high?” “Stevie, if I die, no one will care, I will not be missed at all. That’s OK I accept it. But if I am suppose to die today it’s gonna happen no matter where I am. At home, in that limo or out here in this boat. I need to go.” Stevie watches as hope comes over the face of Tony, but it soon is replaced by the familiar look of hopelessness. Tony reaches his leg up and pushes hard off the dock. Splash Stevie had never let go of the boat, he was pulled into the oily water of the marina, he disappeared into the abyss below the shiny surface. Tony starts up the boat and turns it toward the break between the rocks to the ocean, he pushes the throttle forward and begins his ascent toward the hope that he believes is on his private island in the mid Atlantic. Stevie pushes his head through the surface of the water and he takes in a huge breath, oily water drips down his head, his face and into his mouth, he spits it out. He searches the area, trying to gain his bearings. Though his search ends once he sees the water being split by a speeding boat heading out of the marina into the ocean. He closes his eyes and leans back, letting the rest of his body come to the surface in a float. He opens his eyes looking into the heavens. “Please God, protect my friend.” So nobody ever told you baby How it was gonna be So what'll happen to you baby Guess we'll have to wait and see ‘Where have I been at these last few hours? Mentally. I could have sworn Stevie was on the boat with me, but he was under the water when… I left.’ “Oh my god, my best friend has drowned and it’s my fault! My own selfish reasons, my best friend is dead.” Tears begin to flow from the eyes of Tony as he pushes through the ever increasing wave height in front of him. His bilge pump is not pumping out water fast enough; it has now reached the knees of his black sweats. He doesn’t seem to notice. “Where the fuck am I at?” Tony says aloud. He looks down at his compass, it reads north, he turns the boat hard to the left, the compass doesn’t move, he redirects the boat into the path it had been on before. ‘I think I am still heading south south east. GOD DAMNIT!!’ Tony slams his right hand down hard on top of the steering wheel, the boat turns left slightly, but not much, he recorrects his path. ‘OK, so I have no idea, where I’m at, my best friend is dead and no one loves me.’ Old at heart but I'm only 28 And I'm much too young To let love break my heart Young at heart but it's getting much too late To find ourselves so far apart Tony keeps pushing through, the wind has really picked up, it’s slowly tearing off his T shirt, he doesn’t notice. The rain pelts him in his face, seeming to slap and taunt him, dangling his life inches in front of the boat, but never letting him reach it. The sea holds his life and he will never get it back. “I know I am going to die, so let me die peacefully.” He says to the sea. A gust of wind drops down on top of the boat from his left side, nearly pushing him out of the boat and into the water, a message from the sea telling him that, ‘You don’t die on your terms, you die on mine.’ Control for the first time in years out of his hands. Stecchino closes his eyes as the rain lets up a bit, he parts his lips to allow some water to hydrate his mouth. The rain almost stops, the bilge pump is happy, it can catch up on the work it has to do to keep the boat afloat. “He sweetie, would you like more potato salad?” Tony lifts his head up, it’s sunny, and it’s back yard in Seattle. He looks down at a bar-b-qued hamburger that is half eaten and stained spot where there was some potato salad, a few chips litter the plate. He looks up into Melissa’s beautiful green eyes, she’s smiling. He hasn’t seen this face in years, only in dreams, but never with real air between them. “Sweetie?” She asks again, cradling that bowl of potato salad between her body and arm, her other hand holding the spoon in the salad. Tony just looks at her smiling, could this be too good to be true? He nods. She scoops some up out of the bowl and plops it right down where some had been earlier. She puts the spoon back in the bowl and sets it on the mahogany picnic table. She sits back down and takes a bite out her burger and smiles. “Honey, you really outdone yourself with these burgers again, you get better and better. I am going to have to have another one after this, your cooking is going to get me fat.” She proclaims as she smiles. Tony unable to speak, just stares and smiles at her, its been forever and he cant get over it, this moment right here and now in time, is the greatest moment since she said ‘I do’ and he said ‘I do’ She takes another bite of her burger and smiles. “You need to wake up dear.” She says. Tony looks confused now as she has grown a stern look across her face. “Tony you need to wake up now if you ever want to seem again.” “Huh?” Tony questions back. “WAKE UP!!!” she screams. Tony sits up in the back of the boat, his shirt now gone, and his head killing him. ‘How did I get on the floor?’ he wonders. He looks around the boat, he leans against the motor cover. He feels the back of his head, it’s very tender. “Ow!” he says out loud. He pulls his hand from the back of his head he looks at it, watered down blood, he must have been knocked conscious. He goes to stand up, but is knocked right back down as the boat crashes over a wave. He notices there is about six inches of standing water in the bottom of the boat and the once screaming bilge pump has died. The boat is slowly going to sink. He looks to the sky and feels and torrential rain on his face. It’s raining harder than it had before. He uses the side of the boat to pull himself up, he steadies himself before he trudges over to the steering wheel. He grabs it and tries to focus on the spinning ocean in front of him. He has no idea where he is or where he is going. Panic begins to set in. He looks behind him, the water level slowly rising behind him from the downpour above. He closes his eyes and looks forward. “Please God.” Tony says silently, almost in a breath, but not heard over the pulsing motor and the ripping wind. I don't know how you're s'posed To find me lately An what more could you ask from me How could you say that I never needed you When you took everything Said you took everything from me Tony slumps down on the wheel, flashing in and out of consciousness, in the darkness ahead he sees the exact moment he met Melissa Phillips, his future wife. Standing there in the hallway of National Championship Wrestling offices in New York. She just left a meeting with Victor Van Hausen, the owner. Tony was waiting outside the door to sign his contract, she walked out and smiled at him and continued on her way. Tony just stood there and smiled, he knew that he wanted to marry that woman at that exact moment. It was true love at first sight. Little did he know she felt the same way. A wave crashes over the boat, nearly knocking Tony out of it, he has a death hold on the steering wheel. He doesn’t want it to end here and now. He needs to finish his mission. She has to hear ‘I’m sorry’ from his lips. He needs to hold her one more time; he needs to kiss her one more time. He needs to love her for the rest of his life with her on his arm. It can’t end like this. The sea has other plans and he knows it. He closes his eyes. Tony walks into his house on his island in the Caribbean, he just returned home from America after pulling a huge swerve job in GW. The alleged death of his wife. The one that Silent Wind had done, or had he? Tony had hired Silent Wind to kill his wife, to gain sympathy from the wrestling world and get at Silent Wind. Though it was Tony who had hired Silent Wind to do it. Melissa was never killed, it was just an act. Most didn’t know it though. Most thought she was actually dead. Young at heart and it gets so hard to wait When no one I know can seem to help me now Old at heart but I mustn't hesitate If I'm to find my own way out “Melissa? I’m home, everything went so well, you have no idea!” He walks into his kitchen and drops his bag, there on the counter is a note from his wife, he cocks his head to the side, looking at it. ‘She must have gone to the mainland to get some food, deodorant, whatever.’ He walks over and picks it up with a smile on his face, the smile fades as he begins to read the note out loud to himself. “Tony, I wish I could have told you face to face, but after watching Evolution, I can’t remain with you. I find what you did so sick and disgusting, that I no longer love. Maybe deep inside I do. But I don’t know right now. I wish I could end this note saying that I do love you, but I can’t. Melissa” Tony crumbles the note in his hand as his eyes well up with tears, the throws the note down in the sink, he drops his head down on the marble counter top, running his right hand over his then bald head. He punches the counter top as he uprights himself. He couldn’t be in this house any longer. He was going to burn it down. Tony opens his eyes again and looks into the darkness again; he could be half way to Africa or Texas for all he knew. “MELISSA I LOVE YOU!!!” Tony screams over the sounds of death that surround him. Still talkin' to myself And nobody's home (Alone) He looks behind him, the back of the boat is just above the water, and it’s going down. That one wave must have nearly filled it up. His death getting closer and closer to reality. He was almost in a watery grave nearly a year ago at the hands of Maverick Reno. The man she ran to. Maverick Reno always claimed to be bigger than God. If he could escape Reno’s hand of death, then maybe he could escape God’s. Who was he kidding, this was it. Tony starts searching the boat frantically for something he could start bailing the water out of the boat with. But there was nothing, the bilge pump was as dead as he was going to be. ‘I’m so stupid for doing this. I wonder what she is doing right now? I should have listened to Stevie.’ He thinks to himself, the thought of her is the only thing that is keeping him going at this point. But he wouldn’t be out here if it wasn’t for her. He should be in her arms right now in front of a huge fireplace, having a glass of wine, reading a book. So nobody ever told us baby How it was gonna be So what'll happen to us baby Guess we'll have to wait and see He drifts off into his dreamland again. He is holding her hand on a bluff over looking Los Angeles, NCW just had a show there, this was their first date. He looks down and smiles at her, she returns the look and smiles. He leans forward and kisses her and moves his left hand behind her head, into her soft brunette hair. She places her hand on his hip and steps in closer to him. A very intimate first kiss, the one kiss that would go into his mind as the best kiss ever. For her it was the same thing. She had adored that kiss. In note she never sent to him, she wanted to meet him on that bluff in Los Angeles to relive that kiss and reconcile. She never sent it. When I find out all the reasons Maybe I'll find another way Find another day With all the changing seasons of my life Maybe I'll get it right next time An now that you've been broken down Got your head out of the clouds You're back down on the ground And you don't talk so loud An you don't walk so proud Any more, and what for Tony is down on his knees, pushing water out of the boat with his hands, furiously trying to get the water out to save his life, perchance to see his one true love again. He making no difference in the water level, if anything, it’s gotten higher. He stands up and backs up against the steering wheel, the water cascading over the back and into the boat. The front of the boat begins to rise as the back begins to lower. It’s sinking. He is going to die. Tony climbs up on to the front of the boat, near the nose and feels himself gaining in altitude, his eyes fixated on the back of the sinking boat. To his right in the darkness, a wave of about twenty-five feet in height is coming on top of him. Tony brings his knees into his chest and rests his chin on them. Well I jumped into the river Too many times to make it home I'm out here on my own, and drifting all alone If it doesn't show give it time To read between the lines The wave crashes into the boat, going right through it. Tony is caught in the wave and is ripped away from the now sunken vessel. He looks up through the lightning, ten feet below the surface of the water; the current is pulling him hard and fast to wherever its destination is. He’ll be dead before he reaches it. 'Cause I see the storm getting closer And the waves they get so high Seems everything we’ve ever known's here Why must it drift away and die The salt water makes his eyes burn, the current pulling him blindly, his lungs aching with stale air, he exhales as he surfaces, he gasps for air and then is pulled right back under the water. Lighting breaks the dark again. He closes his eyes again. Stevie Nickels is standing there in a black suit next to a door. He has black sunglasses on. Tony walks up to him, he is also dressed in an all black suit. His friend seems, calm and relaxed. Tony sensing this relaxes a bit himself. He knew he was going to die, but at least he saw his best friend as he passed the gate of the living to the dead. “Where am I Stevie?” Tony says as he walks up to shake his friends’ hand. Stevie though does not return the hand shake. Tony removes his hand from the air and now feels a bit uncomfortable. Stevie reaches down and turns the door knob and pushes the door open, its an all black room, Tony steps through the threshold tenitively, Stevie gives him a good push and follows him into the room, closing the door behind him. Tony is feeling very uncomfortable, is this hell? Will he spend the rest of his life getting his ass kicked by his friend who he accidentally killed hours earlier? What seems to be a stage light illuminates from above, to a woman sitting on a couch directly in front of him. He feels like this is high school all over again and being in the school play. It’s Melissa on the couch! “Melissa!” Tony runs over and sits down on the couch next to Melissa who is crying. “Tony it was never supposed to be this way. Find me!” “I’m dead, you’re the one who’s gonna have to find my body.” He jokes, though his smile fades as she looks him in the eye, her makeup running down her cheek with the tears. She leans over and kisses him. “You’re not dead and I don’t want to be responsible for your death. SWIM!” “Swim?” He questions. He could have sworn he was dead and this was going to be his hell. “Just swim for me baby.” She kisses him again He closes his eyes and melts into the kiss, he opens his eyes, and the salt is burning them once again. He’s back in the ocean, not sure where to go, he begins to kick, hopefully he goes to the surface. I'll never find anyone to replace you Guess I'll have to make it thru, this time- Oh this time Without you Tony breaks the surface of the water and he looks around, still rain from above, and large waves surrounding him. He just puts his face in the water and starts stroking and kicking through the water, hopefully this is the way to go. What seems like hours pass, Tony is just about of energy when on a down stroke his hand hits something hard. ‘Oh shit a shark, just what I need.’ He thinks to himself, he lowers his legs into the water and they both hit something hard. ROCKS!!! I knew the storm was getting closer And all my friends said I was high But everything we've ever known's here I never wanted it to die Tony trudges along the rocks as the waves break across, most trying to knock down his weary body and pull him back out to his watery grave. The water level is at his waist, his knees, and his ankles. He is fully on shore. No chance of getting back out in the water. He continues to walk on the rocks, which have now seemed to form a path. His foot hits something and trips forward, landing on some jagged edged wood. It steps. Is he at his former home? He looks up into the darkness and closes his eyes; he lies his head down on his right arm and falls asleep. To Be Continued.
