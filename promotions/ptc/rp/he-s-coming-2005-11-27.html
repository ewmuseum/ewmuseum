He's Coming…

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 The sound of rain pouring upon a rooftop can be heard. Thunder can be heard far in the distance as a door opens and footsteps can be heard inside this house. The door gets slammed shut and a man, completely drenched in rain, enters the room, probably the living room of the house. The house is dark, and you can’t see much. Only the light of the streetlanterns enlighten the house, and that isn’t much at all. The man walks through the room, as beer cans and bottles can be heard being kicked as they cover the floor. The man walks over to a light switch and flips it. The room gets enlightened and we can see that it is indeed the living room of the house, with much dirt and dust covering the walls and floor, not to forget the beer cans and bottles on the floor. The man, wearing an old hoody, and also jeans with a few holes in it, looks down on the table and sees a letter. He takes down his hood and reveals himself as the man known as Sledge, but is probably unknown to many people. He grabs the letter that is lying on the table and starts reading it. The rain continues pouring, as raindrops is dripping from his clothing. Dear Sledge, I visited your home this afternoon, only to found out you weren’t home. I waited a while, but you weren’t coming in, and I left because I saw the clouds filling up the sky. As I got bored, I sorted out your mail, only to find a unique letter. It was an acceptance letter from the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Five Committee. The letter said that you were accepted to participate in this tournament. It also had a copy of your very own application. I’m sorry that I checked your mail without your own permission, but my curiosity lead me to check it out. I hope you didn’t mind and that you aren’t angry or something. I hope to see you soon again. Much Love and Kisses, Kelly P.S. If I’m not mistaken, your opponent will be somebody by the name of Greg Jackson. Good luck in the tournament! I’ll be rooting for you! Sledge stares at the letter for a moment, and then he puts down the letter. "So, I have been accepted, eh? That’s good. That’s very good indeed." Sledge said as he grins confidently. Sledge walks over to his kitchen and opens the door of his fridge. He grabs a bottle of beer out of it and opens the can of beer. He takes a big drink out of the can, as the raindrops are still dripping from his drenched clothing. As Sledge stops drinking, he walks back into the living room. "The Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Five. Sledge has been accepted and not only that, my first opponent will be somebody carrying the name Greg Jackson. Never heard of the guy, and I don’t care about the guy. What I do care about is me fighting him. What I also carry about, is winning. Greg Jackson vs Sledge, for the first time ever! Meh, doesn’t sound that special, probably because we are both relatively unknown." Sledge walks over to his couch and sits down. He looks out of his window and sees the rain continue to pour down on the streets. Thunder can be heard far away in the distance, and Sledge doesn’t care about it. He takes another drink from his beer. He finishes the beer and looks forward. "Ah yes. My first big tournament and I’m not planning to lose. But the question remains to many people, who am I? That’s good to know that people actually don’t know me. In fact, this is my chance to be an underdog and actually climb up high as I crush people as they don’t seem to know me. Nobody fucking knows me, but they will soon." Sledge grins again. "Greg Jackson, you are seemingly my first shitty opponent of this tournament, and nobody knows you as well, atleast, I don’t. That doesn’t change anything, what does change is that you will be defeated and kicked out of the tournament, as I continue in the tournament and move on to round fucking two." Sledge grins once more, and starts lowering his voice, so low that you he starts to whisper. "GTT5, prepare for new and fresh talent. Prepare to know me and watch me go through people. GTT5…Sledge is coming…" With those words, Sledge crushes his beer can with his hands, wrinkling it so hard that it is almost a small ball. This time, the thunder strikes close to the house and because of the thunder, the light falls out, probably a short-circuit. Darkness hits the house again. Nothing can be seen, as Sledge chuckles in confidence…
