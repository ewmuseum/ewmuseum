The Past…….The Present…….The Future

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 Voice 1: From the time he first stepped foot in my wrestling school, I knew that he had the potential for great things. [An image of Sam Wolack flying through the air comes onto the screen.] Voice 2: This kid has all the markings of a great superstar. He's got five years in this business, and he can only get better. He's got flash, he's got flair. Wrestling is in his blood. [An old image of Wolack giving an opponent a huracanrana.] Voice 1: This kid he has a gift for this. Hell, within 10 years, I highly doubt that he won't have a trophy room full of trophies and titles. Voice 2: He's come to Global Championship Wrestling as a man that is looking to become the best there is. Voice 1: Personally, I think that Sam Wolack is starting to get there. He's been a National Wrestling Council mainstay. [Scene fades up to two old men sitting at a bar. They are drinking their beer and looking over at the lineup for GTT5.] Man 2: Kid has talent, there's no denying it. Man 1: I think that the kid has what he needs to go all the way. They say about this competition that we shouldn't Believe the Hype about the wrestlers. Funny thing is, this kid doesn't have any. Man 2: Yeah, he's an underdog alright. But I think he can do it. After all, not everyone can get into this tournament. Only a select few get chosen, and he was one of them. Man 1: Let's see if the kid can exceed the expectations that the experts have placed on him. I think that this kid, he can win the whole enchilada. Man 2: Maybe then, this kid will be viewed as a future legend, being able to walk the same line as Uber-Judge, Angelo Deville and Dr. Curiosity. Man 1: You never know. Personally, I'm going to put my money on one of the other guys. Man 2: I'll take that bet. This kid is special. This kid, he's going places. Sooner than you think. [Scene fades to black.]
