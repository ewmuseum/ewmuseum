GTT5 and Monkey Business!

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 {Well here I am again at the grandest stage of them all, and my first match is with a monkey of all people! I have spent most of my life fighting top contenders of this business and I get to the biggest tournament of the year and I’m facing a monkey, a worthless bag of fleas that likes to bite the nuts off of its victims! Well it really doesn’t matter if I’m facing a monkey, giraffe, or even a elephant….the same thing will happen I will come out victorious in my quest to become the best! So PTC you can bring on your animals, clowns, and acrobats cause The Prophet will still defeat the three ring circus to fulfill his destiny of dominance!} ~ schiz•o•phre•ni•a(skts-frn-, -frn-)n. Any of a group of psychotic disorders usually characterized by withdrawal from reality, illogical patterns of thinking, delusions, and hallucinations, and accompanied in varying degrees by other emotional, behavioral, or intellectual disturbances. Schizophrenia is associated with dopamine imbalances in the brain and defects of the frontal lobe and is caused by genetic, other biological, and psychosocial factors. A situation or condition that results from the coexistence of disparate or antagonistic qualities, identities, or activities: the national schizophrenia that results from carrying out an unpopular war. – Dictionary. ~ A Monkey of a Time in the Shrinks office! Date: Sunday December 4, 2005 Time: Appears to be 1:44 PM Location: An office building of a Dr. Quagmire The office of the illustrious Doctors was small but roomy because it was brightly illuminating the whole room making the atmosphere very inviting and relaxing. The back wall was filled with hanging achievements and diplomas of the good doctor. There is a long plush couch in the middle of the room with a red cushion where a seven foot giant of a man is lying down to reveal his most inner demons. Right next to the couch is a short old gentleman sitting down on a chair writing on a clipboard…the old man was balding on top and had a coke bottle glasses on his head. His whole attire would be best described as one of an English professor…either that or something that you would find from your 75 year old grandpa. Doctor: Well Mr. Adams, I feel for today’s session we can talk about your inability to determine reality from your own delusions. Adams stirs uneasy in the couch as the doctor says those words. Doctor: Mr. Adams we have covered almost everything in your past but the thing that troubles me the most is your apparent concern with your first match in the GTT5 tournament being held in Prime Time Central! John Adams (turns his head to the doctor): A monkey Doctor, I’m facing a monkey! Doctor: I think the correct term is Chimpanzee John! But what I would like to know is what is this built up frustration about having to face an animal? Adams is a little hesitant about revealing a dark secret of his, so hesitant that he just about rips into the blue pillow he is holding in his hands. Doctor: John… John Adams: Fine I’ll tell you what happened…. (Flash Back) Time: 2:58 PM December 4, 1986 Location: LA Zoo. It all started when I was 10 years old and my father Frank Adams finally let me and my Mother go to the Zoo. It was the first time that I had ever been to the Zoo let alone anywhere for that matter. All that day I was wondering why my father was ever nice to me that day, little did I know that his niceness would later be explained with a close…too close encounter with the animals at the Zoo. When we got there my father got me a T-shirt that said LA on the front of it making sure that my Mother saw it and was pleased. Now since then I had to live with the man for 10 long years of hell, but in my mind being 10 I decided to let my guard down a little to try to enjoy a new experience in my life. I remember the first animals I saw there were the pink flamingos in the front…father always said that they were an animal that only a faggot would love. The sun was so bright that day that even the animals were sweating. "Tour bus leaves in 10 minutes!" The conductor’s voice was one that reminded me of a jolly old man…like Santa Clause almost. I still couldn’t believe that my father got the whole tour bus to ourselves. While we were on the bus I could remember all of the new smells that I experienced for the very first time…like the smells of the animals to the clean air, hell I even remember the animal feces…"Hey John, that shit smells better then you huh boy?" my Father would say…I said he was acting nice never said he should get a Oscar for his performance. Then the bus stopped at the chimpanzee section…. "Common chimpanzees and bonobos are considered our closest living relatives by a majority of the scientific community. From analyzing extensive genetic evidence, we shared a common ancestor about 6 million years ago. From that point forward however, a divergence took place and led to modern gorillas. Bonobos then diverged from ancestral chimpanzees about 1.5 million years ago, when a group of them moved south of the Congo River and became an isolated population. This isolation and change to a lowland tropical forest habitat, resulted in their evolving into a separate species." The tour director gave us a brief explanation on what a Chimpanzee was, it was quite interesting. So interesting that I never did notice my father was standing next to me…till it was too late. The short free fall into the chimpanzee cages felt like a eternity, when I landed the bumps and bruises was nothing compared to what my father would give to me and of course I had to startle the other chimpanzees as they looked upon me in a bloody rage. "Oh my God John!!!" My mother screamed in terror to the unending doom that waited before me. What could I do, where could I go…I tried to run, hell I even tried to defend myself but that could not stop the biting or scratching that I got. The bottom line was that I was there prey….. (Flash Back Ends) John Adams looks with a sigh of relief to the good Doctor as if a big weight has been lifted off of his shoulders. Doctor: What brutality, but then again from what you described of your father it comes to no surprise. How did you ever make it out? John Adams: Well the Zoo officials finally got me out in the nick of time and I ended up waking up in Children’s Hospital LA. I think my father was more upset at the Hospital Bill then that I lived…but no matter what he threw at me or no matter how many times he tried to throw me away like yesterdays news I still keep on coming back stronger! Doctor: Well I think I heard enough, and I feel that your first match in GTT5 will be great therapy for you! John Adams (with an evil look): Me too Doc, after all I’m not ten anymore…and this time the prey will become the hunted! As John Adams leaves he looks back to the doctor to thank him but then realizes that the good Doctor wasn’t there, in fact that brightly lit room was darken as the soul of The Prophet himself. The couch was in fact tattered and old, not like the picture of excellence that it was. The wall where the hanging achievements once stood were now but dust in the wind. For the wall was broken and cracked mostly by age and the fact that the building is condemned. "Wow, the delusions are getting worse each day. No matter for the time of make believe is over and the path is set for my true destiny…GTT5!" He thinks to himself as he departs the unsafe condemned building. GTT5 is here and for John "The Prophet" opponents…may God have mercy on there souls because The Prophet of Wrestling shall not. (Fades to Black.)
