Are You Fighting?

29 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-29 “You signed me up for what?!” Thud! A middle-aged man’s back is slammed against the wall; his head nearly ricocheting off the concrete wall. Sweat drips down his temple, and his glasses hangs in an off balance manner from his nose. He’s breathing hard as he looks ahead to his assailant. However this is no assailant. “What did you sign me up for?!” growls a rather rugged man, He has medium length crimson colored hair, with hazel eyes. He sports a scar down the left side of his face. A scar that the middle-aged man, known as Jacob, now sees larger than before. “I…I” Jacobs stammers trying to find his words. “I signed you up for a wrestling tour…” His assailant won’t let him finish as he pulls him off the wall and slams him against the opposite wall. The man lands with a loud thud and falls to the ground, his clothes in wrinkles. His assailant steps back trying to restrain himself. On the floor, Jacob begins to get back to his feet ever so slowly. “It’s just a small Cruiserweight Tournament. Probably shouldn’t take more than a few weeks.” The assailant snarls and shakes his head. Jacob doesn’t deserve an answer, he thinks. He turns to leave the room, before he does things he will really regret. “Syko…don’t leave here…” Jacob says, using his desk to pull himself back up. The man whom was called Syko, now turns to face Jacob. “I told you never to call me by that name.” Syko says walking back towards Jacob. “Now tell me why I am still here. Tell me why my name is entered in this Cruiserweight Tournament you speak of.” Jacob fixes his glasses, and his shirt. Despite the rude involuntarily signing into the tournament, Jacob was one of the nicer guys. He had been working here, in the Del Fuego Gym, for Syko for nearly six months now. In that time, he had risen to be one of Syko’s hardest workers, and best friends. This moment, couldn’t have been without incident. “I know you are going to think I am selfish, Syko.” Jacobs starts and then quickly realizes what he just said. “Yes, I said Syko. To me, that is your real name. Del Fuego is not your real name. I know what happened long ago with…her. I can’t…” “You can’t understand…” Syko answers back avoiding eye contact. “You can’t understand someone dying in front of you. Sure you have your wife and kids, and you love them. I know for sure if they left before you did, you would grief. But until that happens, you will never understand why I do the things I do.” Jacob cleared his throat and nodded. “You’re right. But you can’t beat yourself over that, forever. This tournament could be the thing for you. You don’t even have to win it. All you have to do is just do this one match. Just win the first round, and you can drop the second.” Syko smiled a bit. “First you sign me up for this wrestling tournament without my consent. Now you want me to just throw in the towel as if nothing else mattered. You STILL haven’t told me why I am in it.” “Like I said, you would think I’m selfish.” Jacob answered, a tint of uneasiness in his voice. No, it was more than uneasiness, it was regret. “I want to win the money I put on you. I know, I should know better. But this isn’t so I could say I won a lot of money. This isn’t a little kid trying to strike it rich fast. This is for my family. You know me Syko.” Syko sighed. He knew Jacob well enough to know that he had been in financial trouble his whole life. He felt sorry for him when Del Fuego Gym first opened, and offered him a small job cleaning. That job led to other jobs, small time jobs. Now he was here, in charge of registrations for the gym. Syko also knew that Jacob has a large family that he was trying to support. Jacob’s lack of job skills and his age made him a liability for any company. Syko knew this, and Syko knew why he would do such a thing, “Yes, I know.” Syko sighed, fearing that he had given in to fighting once again. Jacob half smiled for a bit. He felt that he would get what he wanted. He felt that Syko had already said yes. “So you will fight for me?” Jacob asked. “One match is all I ask.” Syko gazed into his eyes and remained silent for a moment. “Doesn’t this tournament have a weight restriction?” Jacob smiled like a Cheshire cat. “Yes, it does. The limit is two hundred twenty five pounds and lower. You’re around the two hundred thirty mark. The tournament won’t be until a few days from now. I thought you might be able to lose 5 pounds in a week.” “What do you think I’m eating here? Subway?” Syko shouted, surprised at Jacob’s words. Jacob laughed a bit and then quickly gathered his senses. “Sorry, but please tell me you will fight.” Syko nodded. Syko looked away. Syko walked towards the door. “Wait!” Jacob yelled after him, trying to catch him before he left the office. “Don’t do this to me. Don’t do this to my wife and kids. You know I wouldn’t do this out of greed, unless I needed it. Look into my eyes and tell me you wouldn’t do anything in this world to make sure you’re family still had a home!” Jacob forcefully grabbed Syko by the arm, preventing him from leaving the room. Syko didn’t want to look into the old man’s eyes. Jacob felt that he was being selfish asking Syko to win him money. Syko felt he was being selfish in refusing, and he felt ashamed. “Look into my eyes, and tell me you wouldn’t fight for your family. Tell me you wouldn’t let everything go if it meant fixing the past, the wrongs, everything. Look into my eyes and tell me you won’t do it!” Jacob shouted at him, his voice filled with desperation and plead. Syko pulled his arm away and stared dead straight into Jacob’s eyes. You could see Syko’s temple furrow up and his fists clench. For a moment, everything was silent as Jacob waited to hear Syko speak. “You forget…that I have no family.” Syko answers him in his raspy voice. Jacob says nothing after the fact, and lets Syko walk out of the office. <<<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>> Later that day The Del Fuego Gym was actually a wrestling gym. It was Syko’s wrestling gym, named after the woman he lost. This was his place now. How ironic for a man who wants to give up fighting, to own a gym designed to train people to walk into the path he was trying to leave. A path that now seems to be laid in front of him once again if he accepts to fight in the tournament. A path he doesn’t want to take anymore. Syko sat on the lawn chair against the wall in the ring floor. He stared ahead at a handful of students there who were free-styling their exercises. The students here all ranged from 16 years old to as old as 30 years old. The Gym was becoming very popular and some people began to see it as more than just a wrestling gym. Syko knew that he should be supervising the students, especially those practicing in the ring. However, he couldn’t bring his mind and body together. They kept wondering off…thinking about what Jacob said. All you have to do is one match “I can do one match…” Syko told himself, as he leaned forward on the chair. But he didn’t want to do one match. One match would lead to two. Two would lead to three, and so on. Syko had sworn off fighting, and for the greater part of the past year had kept his word. He was almost dragged into other tournaments, but found a way out. Why should this time be any different? Tell me you wouldn’t make sure your family still had a home! “Ugh…” Syko muttered. Why did Jacob had to bring his family up? Syko knew Jacob wasn’t in the greatest of situations. But still, he felt that he could have left the F word out of the conversation. Syko had a family once. He was going to have a child too. A son to be exact. Yet as the winds change, and sinners are saints, and saints are sinners, people died. They died. Tell me you wouldn’t fight for your family! Syko shook his head. He didn’t want reminiscence in the fighting anymore. He got up from the chair and walked towards the exit. He needed to get away from the Gym, if even for a moment. Though, as he walked towards the exit, he could not help feel a sense of abandonment towards Jacob. Syko sighed, and turned back towards Jacob’s office. <<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>>> Jacob locked his desk, and fixed his cheap white shirt that had been wrinkled from the scrimmage earlier. He picked up his small backpack and slung it over his shoulder. One step towards the door, and the door opened with Syko entering the room. “What happened?” Jacob asked him. Syko looked around the room, and then at Jacob who was ready to leave work. “You’re going home, already?” Syko asked, trying to incite a conversation. “It’s five o’clock” Jacob answered pointing towards the wall clock. “You need me here?” Syko chuckled a bit and shook his head. “No…tell me about my opponents….” Jacob’s eyes lit up. “You mean, you are going to fight for me after all?!” Jacob was overjoyed at the notion of it, and set his backpack down. Syko sighed. “Tell me about my opponents.” Jacob cleared his throat. “Um…there isn’t much information on them. All I know is their names. One is a man named Alex Caine who just barely made the weight limit. He’s supposedly an extremely skilled Martial Artist having already been successful in a few minor organizations. There is one other.” Syko nodded crossing his arms. “A martial artist? A guy like that is very lethal at long range, short range if he has the right moves. He seems very talented. However this is a wrestling tournament. I’ve faced people like him before. People I’ve beaten.” Jacob smiled and nodded. He could just feel it in him that Syko could win this match. “He also had a violent temper you might want to be aware of.” “Who is my other opponent?” “Oh yes…” Jacob stammered back remembering the task at hand. “Your other opponent is an enigma. No one really know who she….” “She?” Syko interrupted. “Um…yes…” Jacob answered “The other opponent is a female who calls herself The Angel. The Fallen Angel, even. No one knows her real name, or where she lives, or where she is right now. Everything about her is a mystery. But judging from what I heard from the people in the tournament sign ups, she could be a legit contender to winning the entire tournament.” Syko nodded and then turned back towards the door. “Hey, wait!” Jacob called after him. “Are you fighting?” Syko turned back and looked at Jacob. Syko didn’t speak. Syko turned away. Syko left. <<<<<<<<<<0>>>>>>>>>>> Syko left the gym pondering about the tournament and his would-be opponents. Alex Caine was like any other, he thought. He was a great Martial Artist, but like most Martial Artist, he would most certainly leave himself open to a rushed attack. Syko had fared and beaten many types of fighters. There were those who were considered “Submissionist” by the sport, and those who were simply brawlers. Alex Caine had a temper, but Syko knew would be able to deal with him. The Angel The Angel on the other hand, was different. Syko had always experiences a sense of mystery with his opponents. Yet somehow, this one seemed different. Could it be because it was a female? No, Syko hated discrimination. Could it be that he had not fought a woman his entire career? Perhaps… The Angel was definitely an enigma. “The Angel?” Syko thought to himself. Who is the Angel?
