When Night Falls

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 It was night and the city hadn't slept on yet. The business district had closed its doors for the night and trade would cease until dawn. All that was left for now were the structures and the framework, imposing and casting shadows over the ground level of the district, a constant reminder that industry, entrepreneurship, work and economy still stood their ground and were very much still a significant part of the city, especially the downtown. Still, silence reigned supreme save for the occasional sound of vehicle engine and footsteps. Yet however, on the other side of the downtown, there was clear evidence that the city did not intend to remain completely static. The bright kaleidescope of colours from neon lights, the sound, movement and life were very much apart of the night at the downtown as well. Same old city, another district of extreme difference. Establishments which were usually relatively dormant under the sun would be pulsing with life as the night takes it turn. The transience in the nocturnal district would rival that of the business district during the day, if not surpassing it in a sense that there was more of an elemant of unpredictablity, surprise and a constant spectrum of shape, colour, movement and sound in the great kaleidescope of life. The myriad of characters that breathed, moved, talked, danced and lived also varied greatly, coming in all shapes and sizes, from almost all walks of life from everywhere and everywhere, perhaps nowhere as well. The Dark Room was apart of it. For the wild tapestry of characters, preferences and tastes, there was an equally varied amount of establishments that attempted to cater for all walks of life who lived, breathed and were very much apart of the downtown night life. Just like the Dark Room. Tonight, it seemed particularly packed with life. The Dark Room had always been one of the more popular spots of the district, a fine example of catering to a target market which were significant in numbers and increasingly so. Taking a good glance around, it would deliver the impression that whoever came up with the idea took the words "Dance Macabre" deadly serious. Emphasized by the seemingly nuevo-gothic decor and punctuated by the chains hanging from the roof, the character of the place would not be difficult to determine. The reason for The Dark Room being packed was written on a banner hanging above the bar, "Fright Night, free Graveyard and discount on beer". No surprise as to the full occupancy there. Everybody seemed dressed for the occasion, a man leans onto the bar with a suit made of leather and small chains while another picks up a drink in a Jason mask. A woman and man flirt, she was wearing a black gown and pale, white makeup while the man wore the outfit of a reverend. At the corner of the bar, a man sits nursing his drink. He seemed dressed for the occasion as well, his shining silver hair hanging loose with the lighting playing off the colour. From the neon light of the bar, his face was smooth and without a doubt, handsome in a sense. He wore a long coat which would have trailed to the floor had he not tucked it under him as he had seated himself earlier. He finishes his drink in a shot and calls the bartender over, who comes along polishing a glass. "Another one?" He nodded and the bartender makes his way to the back to serve up another shooter. The bartender serves it up and receives a couple of notes in return. "Thanks. Busy night huh?" The bartender nods in acknowledgement. He takes a glance at the long-haired customer in front of him and gestures with a another nod. "You seem to be getting in the mood too" He had indicated the customer's hand with a tip of his head. The long-haired man shifted his attention momentarily from the drink to his right hand, which was smeared in dried-up red fluid, resembling blood. He smiled and opened his coat, revealing a shirt which was smeared with something resembling blood. The bartender nodded and responded likewise with a smile of his own. "Well, can't really enjoy this sort of thing without getting involved as well" The customer downs the shot and thuds the glass back onto the table with some semblence of authority. He calls for another shooter. As the bartender walks off momentarily, he sighs deeply and looks around him, the myriad of characters on the dance floor, sitting on the tables, in conversation, flirting, moving and some just like himself, drinking. They were all in the mood as well. At least he thought he was when he looked down at his left hand. Which seemed to be dripping blood. Or something resembling it. ===================================== They hadn't had it in months. It seemed to be off-season but somehow tonight, fortune took a liking to them. At least so it seemed. They came from wherever it was they came from. It didn't really matter, at least to themselves it didn't. All they cared for was the thrill, the rush, the creative aspect of it... and above that.. the pleas the screams the struggle and oh the sweet..sweet... Blood. That was their passion, their taboo of taboos, their indulgence. Their hunt. He was tied up onto a chair, a youth perhaps no older than 14. One of them had lured him. He was the angler, their sweettalker, their breadwinner. He was good at what he did and he had lived up to his role, baiting and catching for them a sweet, tender one. They had blindfolded him for the moment. Soon, however, they would remove the blindfold and sate their desire. He squirmed. There were five of them, each holding their tools, each one specialising in their own way. Some were surprisingly crude and simple while others were painstakingly complex and methodotical. However varied their outlets and methods might be, their passion was the same. They removed the blindfold and he saw them, those who seeked to use him solely for their amusement, their enjoyment, their one unspeakable pleasure which they would induldge in. They all seemed to converge on him, seemingly wanting to satisfy their lust at once. However, one among them spoke up and they listened. "It know it has been long yet we must remember..." He looked to the tender youth, young and firm and suitable for what they hand in mind. His smile was more of a leer, sadistic and morbid. "To take our turns" He squirmed and struggled as one of them stepped up first. His smile as leering and sadistic as the one who spoke first, if not more. He had an apparatus which resembeled a pincer, a knife of sorts in his hand as he approached him ever so closely. With a blood-curdling scream. All became dark. And he came upon them. Like the fury of a thousand knives and voices. ======================================== He took another shooter. Raguel had needed a drink badly enough to pay another visit to The Dark Room. The first time was a long time ago. Although things have indeed changed, the essence of the room remained the same, tha macabre and somewhat sordid nature of it made commercial to the masses of the night. He hadn't borthered to tie up his long, shining silver hair and didn't really care to clean himself up before going into the place. After all, he fitted right in. Raguel retrieved a packet of cigarettes from within his coat. He took out a cigarette and lit it with a match, inhaling deeply the nicotine-rich smoke and exhaling a faint trail of it. Coupled with the effect of the previous shooter, it served to make him more complacent and relaxed, even with all the life in The Dark Room. For a moment, he raised his left hand, which seemed drenched in rich, bright red fluid. At least it had stopped dripping. He clenched it into a fist and then let it drop alongside of him. For a moment, he felt remorse, leaving him to whatever may come. After all, he was only a boy. For a moment, a trail of what-ifs and what-nots ran through his mind like a bullet train, or more like a train filled with bullets. Then as the moment passed him by, he shook his head and downed the shooter. It was not in his purpose. There will be no remorse. And the night went on.
