Date: 2004-08-22 All of us reach a turning point in our lives. We are taught from an early age the difference between right and wrong, between good and evil. Nonetheless, we are all tempted by the wrong and the evil sides of life at various times. Perhaps it is other evil that drives us to consider the dark side. When dealing with bullies or criminals, any means necessary seems perfectly acceptable. But we must all remember that more often than not, in our quest to avenge a wrong by committing a wrong, the wrong that is usually committed is against the innocent. Seymour Almasy is the latest to fall to such a mentality. In order to destroy his greatest nemesis, he has sacrificed every principle that he built his wife upon. Sacrificed his fans. Sacrificed his love. And sacrificed his soul. All to destroy Tsunami Oni… Well, that’s not entirely true. There is another motivating force behind the drastic shift in attitude of the man once known as the Final Fantasy. The Primetime Central Global Title. ---------- It had barely been three hours since Seymour Almasy had turned on his fans and his wife, siding with the Knightmare Brigade and the hated Samantha Gordon at the UWF’s Terror in Tokyo show. For those that had known him, it was a shock of biblical proportions. Seymour Almasy was good. Period. End of story. But hatred had blinded him, and now…everything that once identified Seymour Almasy was obliterated. Cut to the present. “Oh, God yes…” Seymour Almasy: You like that, don’t you, BITCH?! Three hours had apparently been enough for Seymour to locate a Japanese prostitute, who specialized in dressing up as various anime characters, and take her to the Japanese institution known as a ’love hotel’. The woman’s Rikku outfit lay on the floor next to the vibrating bed, as a virtually naked Seymour lay above her, revelling in his dominance of the situation. Seymour Almasy: You want me to go faster, don’t you? The woman grunted her approval, as Seymour smiled, pushing himself deeper into the prostitute, prompting more cries. Almasy almost couldn’t believe the situation. It was utterly foreign, and yet, utterly exciting at the same time. Before he quite knew what was happening, he poured into the woman’s body, gritting his teeth. Seymour Almasy: Ahh… Wordlessly, Almasy pulled out of her, reaching for the pocket of the pair of pants he had worn to give the woman a tip. Despite being evil…he wasn’t quite evil enough to deny the prostitute her extra pay. After all…she had been excellent. Before he could get hold of the yen notes, however, she surprised him by speaking. Prostitute: So, why’d you do this? Seymour Almasy: Huh? Prostitute: You don’t seem like the type. Mind you, you’re my first customer…but most of the guys I see who pay for services like this are either horribly ugly, incredibly disappointed in their love lives, or completely lack certain societal morals. You don’t appear to be any of those. The words were spoken in fluent English, which caught Almasy off guard. Their transaction had been carried out in Japanese, which Seymour also spoke. The price and time had been agreed upon. Her time was now certainly up, but she made no move for the door. Her hands went to her hips. Even though she was naked, the expression on her face told Seymour that she wanted an answer to her question. Seymour Almasy: Ever come to a point in life where you had to commit a wrong to right a wrong? Prostitute: Of course. Why the fuck do you think prostitutes become prostitutes? Not many people want this as a line of work. We’ve all been fucked over somehow, and we need money. Plain and simple, so we sell what we’ve got. But you didn’t ask for my life story, and besides, you still have to answer my question. Well, so much for the vague answer. Seymour Almasy: Well, it’s going to sound bizarre, but here goes nothing. I’m a professional wrestler. I just returned to the place of my greatest success, only to find this meglomaniac who tried to take over the world is now being cheered by the fans. I don’t buy him turning over a leaf, not for a second. So, I got myself a new contract…but to do it, I had to turn on my wife, join an evil group, and agree to run errands for this psycho bitch. Prostitute: You know, I probably shouldn’t believe you, but that’s way too much detail to be bullshit. So, what are you going to do? Seymour Almasy: I have no idea. For now though, I can’t worry about any of that. I’ve got a match for a really important title in about 72 hours. Prostitute: Ah, so you’re not staying. Seymour Almasy: Nope. Prostitute: Well, if nothing else, you’ve had an eventful stay. Not a lot of people can say they’ve had a romp with a cosplaying prostitute in a love hotel. Almasy took the opportunity to look at the woman’s body. He had already ravaged it, but even over the course of the hour-long romp, Seymour had never really looked at the woman. She was beautiful, strikingly so. Her breasts were full and rounded, and her body had an almost innocent smoothness to it. Based on Seymour’s preconceived caricatures of ‘women of the night’, this woman was a goddess. Seymour Almasy: Certainly not, and the night’s not over either. Prostitute: What else do you have planned? Inwardly, Almasy had no idea why the fuck the prostitute was being so inquisitive. What the hell should she care? Rather than blow her off, however, something made him call her bluff. Seymour Almasy: Put your clothes on and come with me if you want to find out. Almasy gracelessly reached for his pants, and yanked them on, along with a thin black shirt and a pair of shoes. Behind him, he noted with an odd degree of satisfaction that the woman was getting dressed to accompany him. Prostitute: I’ll come. Seymour Almasy: I thought you already did. Neither laughed at the bad joke. Instead, the prostitute continued on as if it hadn’t even been said. Prostitute: As I already told you, you were my first customer. Even so, I know shit like this doesn’t come up every day of the week. Seymour scooped up his duffel bag, the only luggage that he had brought, and headed out the door. The prostitute followed, as they walked through the halls of the love hotel. Seymour Almasy: Alright then. You know any place where I can get some art done that might be open at this time of night? Prostitute: No shops’ll be open, but I’ve got a friend who’s pretty damn good. Not licensed, but he’s got clean needles. He’s connected to the Yakuza, but that doesn’t seem like it’ll be a problem for you. Seymour Almasy: Not at all. Where is he? Prostitute: Couple blocks. Within walking distance. Seymour Almasy: Good. With that, they threw open the front door, and stepped out into a brisk Tokyo night. Almasy shivered slightly, as he took up the rear, allowing the prostitute to lead. Prostitute: Name’s Aya, by the way. Seymour Almasy: Seymour. Aya: Ah. So, explain this ‘really important match’ you have coming up. Seymour Almasy: It’s a rematch for a title I held a while ago. It’s considered one of the more prestigious championships in my line of work, because it’s fought for by members of lots of different federations. The Primetime Central Global Championship… The former Final Fantasy’s voice trailed off, prompting his new acquaintance to poke him in the ribs. Aya: What’s wrong? Seymour Almasy: It’s all so ludicrous. Earlier in the day, I was loved by the fans. I was a champion of the light, or whatever the hell they billed me as. But it wasn’t their fault. That’s what I was. I…I just feel like my hand’s been forced in all of this. I’m just tired of having to be the role model. My opponents have no such handicaps. Jesse Jamester can do whatever the hell he wants in his quest to regain the title. Saij is just a psychopath, so no one really cares. But me….I would have had to be the virtuous hero, using no illegal techniques or methods, while these two guys could hit me with a battleship if they really wanted to. Aya: It’s not exactly fair when people put expectations on you, is it? Seymour Almasy: Not at all. I’m known as one of the better men in my sport, but still. I’m not known for that. When people think of Seymour Almasy, they don’t think of a former world champion. They don’t think of one of only two men to successfully defend the Primetime Central Global Championship. They think of the joke obsessed with the Final Fantasy series. Aya: I was going to say something abut that when you told me your full name. Big Final Fantasy fan? Seymour Almasy: You could say that, yeah… He had never really thought about it before, but while he was angry at the fans, he was able to see injustice that he had never seen. To them, he WAS the Final Fantasy. A guy who could do cool moves, and make them laugh with his antics. Seymour Almasy: But they cheered me for so long, and now they boo me because I decide to take a stand. It’s bullshit. Aya: People are fickle. It sucks, but it’s the truth. Seymour Almasy: You sound like you know. There are a lot of questions that I need to ask you-- Aya: But don’t do it now please. Later. Maybe. He had to put his curiosity in check. Perhaps the first question on his mind was why such a striking girl had to resort to selling herself. He wanted to know how she knew so much English, how she was able to understand virtually all of his frustrations. For now, though, he switched subjects nimbly, heading back to his contest. Seymour Almasy: Saij is a lunatic. Jesse Jamester, my other opponent, seems to be obsessed with the title. They’re both capable competitors, but realistically, neither of them can hold a candle to me on in ring talent. Saij is too dumb to realize that, and Jamester too stubborn, but both likely think they can beat me on pure desire. Aya: What do you desire, then? The answer came quickly, unflinchingly. Seymour Almasy: To show everyone who turned their back on me that I’m not a joke. To regain the Global Title, and hold it against every single person who denies my resolve. It’s ironic. Everyone thinks that I turned my back on the fans…that I turned my back on my wife, but in reality, it is everyone else who turned on me. Aya: Sounds like you really need to stop worrying about other people, and what they want for you. Seymour Almasy: I know, I know. It’s difficult sometimes though. I’ve been dependant on people my whole life. My wife, well…more than likely ex-wife now…I’ve known her ever since I was a child. She helped me, protected me, taught me…but the more I think about it-- Aya: The more you realize that she did you a disservice in case anything ever happened and you wanted to be on your own again. Seymour Almasy: More or less that, yeah. Aya: Love is a wonderful thing, Seymour, but it is a luxury many of us can’t afford. Seymour Almasy: I can’t anymore…at least, not until I settle my scores. Teeth gritted hard as Seymour thought of Tsunami Oni and the fans. Tsunami was one thing, Almasy had always hated him. But the fans…he wanted to make them pay more than Oni would. It was their stupidity that had driven him to this. Their naivete that led them to believe that Oni wasn’t trying to take over the world as he had his entire life. Their foolishness to trust a con man over someone who had built his life as a champion of justice. Seymour’s frustration came out in one word, screamed out at the top of his lungs. Seymour Almasy: IDIOTS! Almasy’s companion looked at him, more than a touch of sympathy in her eyes. Still, they had reached their destination. Aya: This is a lovely conversation and all, but we’re here. Almasy found himself being led into a dark apartment building, and up several flights of stairs. Aya opened a door, revealing a heavily tattooed and burly Japanese man. Tattoo Artist: Aya. What can I do for you? Aya: Hi. Listen, I’ve got a friend here…had the impulse to get some work done at 3 in the morning, and I told him you’re the best at any hour. Tattoo Artist: Friend? I thought I told you not to get involved with any of your ‘friends’. Aya: Eh, he’s got an interesting story. Not the type of thing I’ll see for a while, so I figured I’d see it through to its conclusion. Tattoo Artist: I’ll assume if he’s got money to pay you that he’s got more than enough for me. Aya: Of course. Tattoo Artist: Well then, stranger, what do you want done? The question snapped Seymour out of his awkward trance. He had been a party to the conversation without even saying anything for several minutes. Shaking his head to clear the mental cobwebs, he responded. Seymour Almasy: I’m looking to get a bunch of roman numerals tattooed across my chest…in red, if you can. One through eleven, with X-2 in between ten and eleven. Tattoo Artist: The numbers of the Final Fantasy series. I’ve gotta admit, I’ve never had such a request before, and it’s probably going to take a good deal of time. Seymour Almasy: That’s alright with me. As long as you’re done so I can make a twelve o’clock flight out of Japan tomorrow, take all the time you need. The thin black shirt that Almasy wore was taken off, once again revealing the well-toned body that had been hidden for Seymour’s entire career behind thick armor. Tattoo Artist: Damn, you’d think a guy like you wouldn’t have to resort to a prostitute to get laid. Seymour Almasy: It’s a long story. I’ve told it once tonight, and I really don’t want to tell it again. Tattoo Artist: Suit yourself then. I’ll go get my equipment. The burly man stood, walking off to some corner of the apartment in search of his gear. Seymour turned to Aya, nodding his head. Seymour Almasy: He’s right. I didn’t need to find a prostitute. But I wanted to. Shit, my wife and I were having sex twice a day, and I’ve never wanted a fuck as badly as I did after the event tonight. Aya: You needed release. Nothing to be ashamed of. Hell, that’s probably why you’re here. To do something different, something that the old you would never have done. Seymour Almasy: You know, you’re probably right… Aya: Worry about your match tomorrow. You’ve had a busy day…and this is going to take more out of you then you can probably imagine. Seymour Almasy: More pain…the perfect way to end a day like this. Aya: With pleasure and pain? Seymour Almasy: For years, people always told me that there was a thin line between pleasure and pain. I always thought they were crazy, but now…I think I know what they mean. Aya: This probably isn’t going to be so much pleasure though… The tattoo artist had returned, his tools of the trade in hand. Seymour Almasy: Wonderful. He grimaced noticeably, but did his best to put on a brave face. Seconds later, all that could be heard were Almasy’s screams, echoing through the dark apartment. Aya took his hand, which Seymour squeezed frantically as he felt the needle pierce his flesh again and again. Whether or not he would admit it, in a small corner of Seymour’s mind, he was glad for the pain. It helped ease his guilty conscience. (To Be Continued)
