Terminating the Project

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 -TERMINATING THE PROJECT- “I had a really good time tonight Alex...” I watch her out of the corner of my eye as she speaks, while her words are genuine they’re laced with curiosity. “Glad to hear it babe” I catch flicker of disappointment on her face before she quickly replaces it with her happy go lucky smile, the one she uses to cover up hurt feelings, though she’ll never voice her feelings out loud to me she still hopes against hope that one day she and I can be a little more then we are. She’s never spoken to me about these dreams but I can read them on her face nonetheless. She wants something safe... Something secure... Something solid... She wants something to hold onto in a world where people just drift on the current called life, some of them drift with the current, some of them swim against it, some get tired and give up...the current pulls them under and they drown. And some... A Very special few...they find someone to cling to. The sooner she realizes I’m not a man to cling to the better... Because I’m swimming against this current, fighting life for all it’s worth, it’s a fight you can’t win but I’ll struggle on anyway...and in the end I’ll drown, and anyone who’s foolish enough to cling to me...will go down with me. Her included. “So what made you call...tonight?” she asks I hate questions, and I hate it when she asks them in that tone of voice, trying to sound casual when she’s really digging for answers she knows she can’t have. The truth is I wanted to celebrate, my first win in GTT4, you have to take these things as they come, you win? You embrace it, because wrestling fans and all those supposed “Friends” who attach themselves to you? They’ll put you on a pedestal when you win, they all love you... They’ll treat you like you’re everyone’s personal Jesus Christ and savior... If you lose they’ll crucify you just like the thieves they put on the crosses next to him. So tonight? Tonight was about having fun...living it up while it lasts. Because if I lose there wont be any celebration, only critics lined up with nails... “I wanted to have a little fun babe, and you’re the one who came to mind” The hurt in her eyes is obvious, and for a few seconds the path of the evening could fork in two different directions, she could slap me and leave forever, or hang onto what she can get...cling to the moment. And cling she does...just like I knew she would. Pressing herself up against me she shoves me back against my apartment door and kisses me, a kiss full of passion and heat. “Well you know me” she says with a fire in her eyes “I like to have fun” But even through the fire the hurt lingers in her eyes. The scent of her body mingles with mine, the expensive perfume she wore just for me mixed with a light sheen of sweat on her arms is an arousing aroma... But as I indulge in the pleasures of her clothed body a new odor catches my attention... The smell of cigarette smoke wafting out from under my doorway... “Get out of here...” I push her away ignoring the look of surprise on her face. The smell is strong, and since I don’t smoke it means someone is waiting for me. “A-Alex...what’s...what’s wrong?” Her voice is full of confusion and hurt, emotions I don’t have time for right now. “Get out of here bitch! NOW!” Her eyes widen at the harsh words and then narrow to slits. “Well fine!” she turns away and storms down the hall “FUCK YOU!” she screams over her shoulder. But her words fall on deaf ears, I’ve already put her out of my mind. My front door is ajar... Against my better judgment I throw open the door and march in, whoever has invaded my home had better have a damn good reason. “Did I spoil your little date Alex?” The voice is familiar. “Martin? What the hell are you doing here?” “I came to talk business Alex...” Through the darkness of my apartment I can make him out sitting in my recliner...I remember coming home one night to find Lestatt Knight sitting in that very same spot, it hadn’t been good then and I had no idea what the outcome of this would be now. “Well we can talk business tomorrow Smalls...” I don’t bother disguising the annoyance in my voice, Martin has crossed a line here, a line very few others have dared to tread upon. “No Alex...I think we’ll discuss it right now” The tone of his voice is the only thing that keeps me from removing him physically from my apartment. I recognize that tone, it’s the one he uses when he feels he has an advantage over someone. “Alright then...make it quick” I say deciding to give him a temporary reprieve before I throw him out on his ass. There’s silence for a moment while he takes a drag off his cigarette and then he speaks. “You’re a dangerous man Alex...and based on that I’ve come to a conclusion.” Silence again “See Alex...I’ve invested a lot in you...I’ve been more of a father to you then your old man ever was...and hell I’ve helped you out of more jams then your family ever did, I’ve been your family Alex...and your employer for years now” Thus far I don’t see what he’s getting at. “Do you expect a thank you Martin? Are you here expecting me to kiss your feet? Or maybe break down, cry, and have a Kodak moment?” “Shut up Alex...See it all started in xCw when I bailed you out of jail...how did you repay me? You killed two actresses hired by Jeff Wylde...you killed them because they helped him fuck with your head, I put myself at Risk trying to cover your ass and give you a reliable alibi” His words send an involuntary shiver up my spine, my dream of two nights ago comes back to haunt me...if only he knew. “Then there was DWA...I hired you again...with a carefully laid out plan for revenge against Lestatt Knight...It was Meticulous Alex! Perfect! But YOU jumped the gun Alex! You attacked him...forced your way into something you didn’t belong...and when you felt it suited you...you turned your back on me Alex...not only did you turn your back on me you broke into my house and threatened my life...WITH LESTATT BY YOUR SIDE!” His fist slams down on the recliner emphasizing his point, a bit of ash falls from the cigarette and onto my carpet...a fire waiting to happen, but the fire smoldering in Martin’s eyes is obvious as a bit of moonlight illuminates his face, eyes wide he crushes the cigarette in his hand. There is silence as he calms himself down and when he speaks again he’s calm and in control. “I’ve spent a great many nights wondering if you were going to come back Alex...some nights I still wake up thinking you’re standing over me ready to end it...you’re crazy...unpredictable...you won the first round of the tourney when no one said you would...when everyone said you wouldn’t...you surprise people like that Alex...you settle in and start working and before long you’ve got a body for me to bury, an alibi for me to create at my own risk...you expect me to bail you out when you wake up with blood on your hands...you have no damn idea how many whore’s I’ve had to pay off to keep quiet about your abuse Alex...all these things I’ve invested in you Alex...” His next words hit me like a hammer blow “And I’ve come to the conclusion...that you’re not worth it” My patience snaps at this point “Get out of my apartment Smalls...get out and don’t come back...don’t ever come back with a contract for me to sign again...you agree to eliminate yourself from my life and I wont kill you for what you just said you miserable little sh...” His hand shifting ever so slightly in his lap catches my attention...something glistens in the moonlight...a small piece of metal. (He’s got a gun!) My mind screams but my body doesn’t register...I’m frozen just like in my dream “You’re not worth it Alex...” he continues on brushing off my words because he knows I know... “You’ve been my Pet project Alex...my very personal and expensive pet project...but in light of all that’s happened over the years Alex...in light of all that I’ve lost I’ve decided...to terminate the project” He raises the gun and fires once... The bullet slams into my shoulder, I hit the wall and slide down my hand grasping at the wound as if I could pry the bullet out, the pain is excruciating...I hadn’t fathomed this kind of pain...and how it could freeze me. “I should have done this a long time ago Alex...” He fires again...the bullet strikes my knee setting off a whole new chain reaction of pain in my body. “A good business man knows when to cut his losses...remember the gambler Alex? Gotta know when to hold em...gotta know when to fold em” Martin rises from the chair and stands over me, lighting another cigarette he stares down at me. And all I can do is look up at him unable to even whimper in pain. Martin takes a drag and the embers reflect in his glasses...he looks like he could be the devil...I guess I’m going to that room in hell a little earlier then planned. Martin kneels in front of me and runs his hand through my hair the good natured way a father would do to his son. “You should have gotten me first Alex...You should have gotten me before I got you...you know who taught me that Alex?” Placing the barrel to my chest he pulls the trigger, I feel the hot flash and pain explodes like a bomb in my chest. “You did...” Something warm is running down my chin but I’m beyond those sensations...though I’m in my apartment my mind is in a different landscape altogether...laid out on a rocky beach as waves of pain pound over me unrelenting, a small part of wants him to end it...put the gun to my head and just finish it! “Well Alex...I think I’ve made my point...and now I’m going to leave you to consider my words...You don’t have much time left to consider them I know...but I want my words to be the last you ever hear Alex...for all those times you never listened to me...now you have no choice (NO!) I scream at him in my mind (FINISH THIS!) “Goodbye Alex...” (DON’T LEAVE ME LIKE THIS!!) With all the strength I can muster I reach out and grab his pant leg, he looks down and chuckles then shakes me off as if I was a child, walking out into the hallway he shuts the door behind him, I hear a key rattle in the lock and then a click...the lock snapping into place has a tone of finality to it. I’m alone... Alone with my pain and his words... Damn him... I writhe on the carpet beginning to hear voices... “Alex?” No not voices... “Alex?” Just one voice... “Alex are you ok?” And it sounds so familiar... “Alex? Answer me” “Answer me!” “Alex?” “Alex!!”
