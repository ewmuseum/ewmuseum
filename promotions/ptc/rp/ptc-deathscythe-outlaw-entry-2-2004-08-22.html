Date: 2004-08-22 Entry Title: The Case ==| Prologue |== The time is almost near… 6 extremists’ lives will soon meet their fate in the brutal and grueling Extreme Chamber in a couple of days. They will fight and fight and fight some more until there’s one extremist left standing. The one left standing will earn the prize of The PTC Extreme Title and perhaps the title as the King of Extreme. These extremists have trained and even bored us with their pitiful attempts to intimidate one another with their feeble trash talking. People like DDT… He’s not even making much effort as it would seem that he has either lost his drive to capture the gold or finally realizes that he doesn’t stand a ghost of chance of winning. I would say that this one is the smartest of the extremists since he already knows his fate. People like Joey Vendetti… Hmm…Next please! People like ICE… I can’t really say anything bad about this fellow I mean he did give me one a hell of match not too long at a recent 4 Winds’ event…but at the end there was nothing left except for “Crushed ICE” and the same thing will happen again. No… This time around the only thing that will be left of you is a watery puddle of your once strong career. People like Shaman… The big enigma of WWA... He preaches that he is WWA’s true hardcore legend and thinking that he will school the other extremist in the Extreme Chamber with his so-called hardcore knowledge. Such a delusional fool… By the time this match is said and done I will open your eyes and show you the true harsh reality that you’re nothing more than a hype machine built by your own league to give your insecure self something to believe in. Doubt me if you want but you will believe me after the Extreme Chamber. Count on it… People like Sean “The Irish Bastard” McClancy… The extremist who knows all about being hardcore if you know what I mean…? Before I stated that Sean’s relatives planted cameras in Sean’s hotel room which spawned the famous sex tape but in truth it was his obsessive fiancé who planted those cameras. Quite the fiancé I must say… I hear that you don’t get to see her as much as you want to. Since you want to be with your fiancé so badly I will do you favor I will personally defeat you and severely hurt you so you can spend more time with her…in the emergency room. Humph, it will be quite the thrill to defeat a former Extreme Champion and to make your Aussie fiancé more pissed at me. And people like me… Possible who cares less about the PTC Extreme Title I mean for all I care the belt can get tarnish. I only decided to use up my title as #1 Contender so I can see what Extreme Division has to offer and so far I’m quite disappointed. I expected to at least break a sweat but atlas that won’t be happening. My fellow extremists… Can you all feel it…? Can you all feel that your end is coming soon…? I do… Go ahead and try to prove me wrong… But at the end you all will fail as I will be the one left standing fanning the eternal flame of victory… ..:: † ::.. ==| Kairi’s New Look |== :: At the busy Okinawa Station, a familiar figure approaches and stands out in open among all the commotion. The figure is no other than a serious Kyo Kushinaga. :: “Following the lead that my new ninja aide and supposedly old friend, Kairi Silverwolf gave me led us to here…” :: Opens his hand and reveals the mysterious locker key in his palm. He stares down at the key for a moment before he closes his hand and turns his attention back at the station. :: “Can this mere train station have the answers I seek?” :: Takes a single deep breath. :: “Only one way to find out…” :: He heads to the entrance. :: “Kyo, wait up,” a familiar voice called out to him. :: He stops and turns his attention over to the voice. Curiously watches an unfamiliar girl running up to him. Her light violet hair wildly swings in her face after abruptly stopping before him. She guides her hand up and gentle moves the strands from her sapphire eyes, fully looking up at him with a gentle smile across her face. :: “Uh,” he curiously began. “Do I know you?” :: Quick blinks from her eyes before he stares up at him surprisingly. :: “Master, it’s me,” she explained. “Kairi…” :: Surprise fills his face. :: “Kairi,” he questioned. “You look very different.” “This is my human form,” she replied while she stares up at him with a nervous blush across her cheeks. “Don’t them these people to look strangely account of my ears.” :: He once more heads to the entrance. :: “We don’t want that,” he sarcastically said. :: She follows. :: ..:: † ::.. ==| Finding A Pandora’s Box And…A Special Surprise |== :: In the station, at the lockers, a few people are at the lockers storing their belongings in them or taking them out from them. :: :: At the end of a row of lockers, Kyo and Kairi stand before a certain locker. :: :: Kyo holds up the locker key near the locker and sees the numbers on the key matches the numbers on the locker. :: “This is it,” he seriously said. :: Kairi nods. :: “Uh, master,” she said with concern in her tone. :: He curiously looks at her. :: “Maybe I should be the one to the open the locker,” she suggested as she looks at her master with concern. “Oh,” he curiously said. “The locker could have some kind of booby trap on it,” she began. “Since it’s my job to protect my master then I think I shall be the one to open it.” :: He dully looks at her with a sweat drop tickling down from the side of his face. Clears his throat and changes his expression to a modest one. :: “I’ll take my chances,” he modestly said as he turns his attention over to the locker. “And besides what’s the worst that could happen? My face melts off…” :: Slips the key in the locker, unlocks the locker and opens the locker door wide. A soft sound escapes his lips and curiously looks in the locker. Kairi looks at her master for a moment before she approaches the open locker and she too looks curious when seeing a mysterious object resting inside. :: :: Kyo pulls out the object and reveals that it was a package. :: “It’s a package,” she curiously said. :: He rips the paper wrapping off the package revealing a black metal case. Shakes the case once and hears things moving inside. :: “Well, it’s not a bomb,” he sarcastically said and he curiously examines the case. :: He tries to open the box but it was no luck. :: “Damn,” he frustratingly said. “This thing doesn’t want to open.” :: She blinks once and looks more curiously when noticing something about the case. :: “Master,” she called. :: He stops and looks at Kairi for a moment before he follows her gaze. His face expression turns to great confusion when seeing a strange lock on the top of the case. :: “What the hell,” he confusingly said. “Hmm… It would seem to be a 16th century Japan puzzle lock.,” she intriguingly said. :: A grin appears across his lips. :: “Good eye,” he amusingly told her. “Remember we are from that time era,” she modestly told him. :: He examines the ancient lock, trying to figure out how to open the lock though he realizes this is not the proper place to do this. Places the case underneath his arm and looks at her seriously. :: “I can’t concentrate here,” he frustratingly said. “Need a quiet place to think.” :: She nods and begins to the close the locker for her master but stops when noticing a key hanging in the locker, curiously looking at it with her azure eyes. :: “Hey master,” she curiously said. “There’s another key in here.” “Another key,” he questioned. :: Approaches the open locker, looking inside of it as his face fills up with surprise though there is a hint of curiosity. He reaches in and pulls the single key from there, examining the key in his open palm. :: “This looks like a car key,” he confusingly said. “I wonder…” :: He closes the locker door, pulls out the locker key and puts it back in his pants pocket. :: “C’mon Kairi,” he said while leaving the lockers. :: She nods and follows him. :: :: In the indoor parking area, Kyo and Kairi walk through the dark shadows. Kyo searches for something among the rows of the parked cars. Kairi looks with confusion wanting to know why her master brought her here and what he was searching for. :: “What are we looking for,” she confusingly asked. “Something that has style,” he sarcastically said. :: She still looks at him with confusion wanting to know what he meant. :: :: He comes to a sudden stop when a certain something catches his eye. A satisfy grin appears across his face to go with the content look that fills his face. :: “And I think I found what I was looking for,” he thrillingly said. :: Kairi follows his gaze. Quick blinks from her eyes and looks shockingly from what she was seeing before her. :: “You’re kidding right,” she shockingly said though there is a slight hint of fear in her tone. :: Kyo simply looks over to her with a dark grin. :: :: Fear fills her face with a faint blue blush across her face when realizing that her master was not kidding. :: “Oh my God, he flies so fast and dangerously…I cannot imagine how he will do when riding on one of these.” :: Kyo still grinning. :: “Thanks for the gift, bro…” :: A roaring of a new life awakening escapes the parking area… :: :: An all black motorcycle that appears to be highly modified for special capabilities soars out before touching the concrete ground and rides off in the night. :: :: Kairi screams out with fear while having a tight grip around her master who rides the motorcycle that you only imagine seeing in comic books or anime. Kyo laughs out in great amusement enjoying the rush. :: “Isn’t this great, Kairi,” he yelled out to her. “Can you please slow down, master,” she frighteningly asked. “No way,” he yelled out. “I have to see how fast this baby goes!” :: Widens her eyes in much fear from hearing Kyo’s comment. :: :: Kyo pops a wheelie before increasing the speeds to greater limits. Kairi’s screams become more loud and terrifying. :: :: On top of a tall building, a figure clad in a dark cloak stares down, watching Kyo and Kairi speeding through the city of Okinawa like a couple of speed demons. :: :: The figure blurs away… :: ..:: † ::.. ==| An Explosive Entrance |== :: In the quiet and empty Okinawa Park, Kyo’s now silent motorcycle rests beside a metal bench. Kyo sits on the bench fiddling with the case wanting to open it so it can reveal the secrets of his past. Kairi lies on the soft grass, panting heavily as she still feels the aftershocks of her terrifying ride with Kyo. :: “Damn it to hell,” he frustratingly said. “Why the hell did I have to get a case with a tough puzzle!” :: He slides the puzzle pieces in random spots, hoping he was sliding the pieces in the right spots. Tries to the open the case but it still did not budge causing to growl out in frustration. :: “Damn it,” he said as he angrily glares down at the case. “What the hell is the combination to this thing.” :: Kairi takes a seat beside him and looks at him kindly seeming that she is better now. :: “Can I give it try, master,” she politely asked. :: Closes his eyes and frustratingly sighs as he hands the case over to her. :: “Knock yourself out,” he dully told her. :: She contently smiles and gives the puzzle a try. :: :: Kyo leans back against the bench having a relax expression. He opens his eyes halfway, tranquilly looking up at the beautiful sea of darkness that makes up the endless sky. :: “Kairi,” he tranquilly said. :: She shifts an eye over to him. :: “Yes,” she kindly replied. “How did we meet,” he queried. :: Surprise sound escapes her as she looks at him in the same manner not expecting him to ask her that. :: “I want to know how we met,” he tranquilly asked her while keeping his gaze up at the clear sky. :: She turns her attention back at the case and once more fiddles with the puzzles. Closes her eyes halfway and has a look of serenity on her face. :: “It was raining that day in the woods,” she started. “Bandits had just stormed my village the night before, killing everyone including my parents. I was the only sole survivor of the village.” :: Her words cause him to turn his attention to her. :: “I was filled with much rage and hatred,” she sadly said. “Then you came along…a mere wanderer passing through.” :: He lowers his head down slightly causing his hair to conceal his eyes. :: “You drew a sword at me,” he told her in a tranquil tone. :: She surprisingly gasps and glances over to him in the same manner. :: “Your thirst for blood was so strong that you would’ve killed the first person you saw,” he said. “So much hell you have seen the night before. I simply extended my hand to you asking if you want to come with me.” “Your memories,” she surprisingly told him. :: Lifts his head up and looks over to her with a tranquil gaze for a moment before he holds his arm out in front of him, staring down at the demonic marks that cover his entire arm. :: “After I fused with the Deathscythe it feels like a fog has lifted not a lot but some,” he explained as he tranquilly looks at her. “My memories are returning but slowly…” :: A content smile appears across her lips. :: “That’s wonderful,” she delightfully said. :: He leans back on the bench once more and stares up in dullness. :: “I guess so,” he dully replied. :: Kairi looks over to her master curiously not understanding why he was not pleased about getting his memories back. :: “My memories maybe slowly returning but it is just causing me to ask more questions to the point of frustration,” he said. “At least it’s a start,” she positively said as she continues to fiddle with the puzzle lock. :: Kyo has a cigarette in his mouth, lifts his hand up and uses his power to light it up. He takes a couple of puffs from the cig before exhaling the toxic fumes from his mouth. Closes his eyes and savors the nicotine taste though he soon opens his eyes halfway looking over to the mysterious case with burning curiosity. :: “And that could be the finish…” :: Kyo suddenly sharpens his eyes in seriousness sensing something powerful approaching them quickly. :: :: Instantly, a large explosion happens from where Kyo and Kairi are… :: :: The familiar figure clad in a dark cloak appears, having the dark hood off revealing that the figure is a serious looking Japanese man in his late-teens with creepy blue eyes and short black hair. Strange inscriptions written in crimson are on one side of his emotionless face. :: :: He stands before the tall dancing fire with a sadistic grin across his face, enjoying the sight of the fire burning the two heroes from the past. :: …To be continued
