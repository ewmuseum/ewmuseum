The End.

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 “John calm down honey, you’re getting it everywhere,” the female voice exclaimed with more than a hint of shock in her voice. In the bathroom John was rushing around like a headless chicken, a grin on his face that couldn’t have even been hidden with a brown paper bag. Turning around to his face smothered in shaving foam, a small amount of which flew across the room and landed softly on Louise’s neck, the source of the voice. She slowly walked over to John and wiped the foam off her neck, before rubbing it, lightly onto the tip of John’s nose playfully. “Honey, just take your time. We have got hours left to get there; you aren’t going to miss the launch. I’m going to get ready ok.” Louise speaks before turning around and almost sliding out of the room with an air or grace and elegance. John turned back to the mirror and clutched his razor in his hand. He had chosen to remove the stubble that usually riddled his face, as today was a special day. He had received the call about a week ago, his brother James had been recalled into the space program. His brief stint as a wrestler had hardly set the world on fire, yet they had always been there for each other. They were all they had. Their parents had died when they were both relatively young. Taking the blade and bringing it up across his neck, the razor began to cut through the hairs on his neck and chin, the noise of the bristles being sliced cut through John, but he persisted. James was finally going to believe his dream, he had been given the go-ahead to be part of the crew for the shuttle Nostradamus, it was just a routine mission, but to James it meant the world. For as long as he could remember James had wanted to touch the stars and John was not about to miss his brothers crowning moment, not by a long shot. “John, I’m making a cup of tea do you want one” Louise’s voice shouted up from downstairs causing John to jump, the razor blade slipped and nicked his skin, causing him to wince in pain. A few drops of blood dripped into the ivory basin below before becoming diluted in the mix of water and foam. John ripped off a piece of toilet tissue and applied it to the small wound. “No I’m ok thanks,” replied John as he tentatively dabbed the piece of tissue, the blood seeping through it slowly. A grimace on John’s face. Launch… T minus 5 hours and counting. “So we need to discuss what you are going to do about this tournament” Louise spoke glancing at John momentarily before returning her eyes to the road. John’s head dropped slightly, although he quickly disguised it as flicking through the collection of CD’s in the glove box. He always hated it when Louise tried to get involved in the wrestling side of the business. “I mean you’ve already lost so much time when you should be training, you’re facing too many distractions. Myself, UWF and the launch all times when you could be training.” Louise continued, a stern look on her face as some flash bastard in a BMW cut her up. “Ok, sorry but I have to completely dissect everything you just said there honey, The UWF is my job, its what brings the money in. How is it distracting? If anything it helps, I train for there normally as I always do, I do all right. Today is a one off, ok I admit I could be training, but it’s his dream hun, I can’t miss it.” John said before closing up the glove box and folding his arms. “What about me?” Questioned Louise, uncertainty and disappointment riddling her voice as she did her best to keep her eyes on the road, she really wanted to look and John. She had always been uncertain and unsure with things, especially John. “You, well you keep my active enough as it is. With you in my life I needn’t do any training ever again.” John smiled at Louise and rubbed her knee gently. “I love you, you aren’t a distraction at all, and you are the thing that keeps me grounded.” There was silence for a moment, a calm and comfortable silence as John kept his hand on Louise’s knee. “I love you too John.” Launch… T minus 3 hours and counting. “Wow it’s big huh, remind you of anything.” John grinned and elbowed Louise playfully in the ribs as she slapped him on the arm. Before the two of them stood the Shuttle Nostradamus, the sun glistening off its hull, standing proudly. The area around the launch pad was full of people dashing around, clutching clipboards of paper and making their final checks. The sun was beating down on the desert like plain with a sheer ferocity that John had not felt in a long time. Louise and John stood hand in hand, staring in awe at the shuttle in front of them. John couldn’t help but smile, if the truth was told it was a gorgeous day and a welcome distraction from the tournament. He knew that he had a hard enough draw as it was. He had grown lack lustre with regard to the tournament, the organisers seemingly unsure what they wanted, throwing off his regime. He had originally been informed that due to his status as a former fighting extreme champion, that he had been granted a bye. However that changed with the introduction of Bruce Lomand from Neon. Then of course Neon went bankrupt leaving exactly whom he was facing up in the air. If the truth was told, John had no idea if he would even have someone show up for his match in the tournament, every step further though more than likely meant the inevitable match with Angello Deville. The one who dominates everything he participates in. “Excuse me you two are in a restricted area I’m going to have to ask you to leave. John turned around with a start, as did Louise only to come face to face with the smile that was brighter than a thousand flashbulbs, James Edwards. John’s face turned into a fantastic gleam as he wrapped his arms around his brother and clasping him tightly. Louise took a step backwards nodding at James as she did so; she knew that this was their time. “So, you finally did it huh?” John asked his face grinning as he pulled away from James who was standing proudly in his NASA gear. “Yeah well, you know I had to give up that wrestling thing sooner or later, get a real job.” James winked at John who was still grinning, the friendly sibling rivalry had been present for years, and nothing was going to change it regardless of the day. “You know I’m just joking, fact of the matter is I was just never any good at it all. The whole grappling other men a bit too camp for my liking, anyway it looks like you fell on your feet there.” James indicated over at Louise who blushed slightly and turned away, returning her gaze to the shuttle, now perfectly aware that the brothers were talking about her. “Louise, yeah. Things started to go badly again for me, but she pulled me through it all. I’m happy now.” John spoke, his voice full of sincerity. “So, is she the one?” asked James. “Yeah, I think she is.” Replied John as he glanced over at the figure of Louise. “I have to go John, final checks and everything. You are staying around for the launch right, no big match to train for or anything?” John questioned, genuine concern in his voice, the bond that the two shared, he clearly wanted to share his special day with him. “I wouldn’t miss it for the world James, we’ll be here ok.” The two brothers stood facing each other silently for a moment before hugging each other once more, they clasped each others hands and shook them firmly, before James turned and walked away slowly, a rabble of people suddenly descending on him each vying for his attention sorting out the final preparations. Louise sauntered over and wrapped her arms around gently around his waist. “Are you ok honey?” John glanced over his shoulder and kissed Louise lightly on her cheek. “Yeah I’m fine, everything is fine.” Launch… T Minus 2 minutes… The rumble from the engines of the shuttle could be felt through the ground, even with the considerable distance between the launch centre and the shuttle itself, the people that had previously been running around like morons were now sat, watching the shuttle intently, none more so than John though. Louise squeezed John’s hand reassuringly and he stroked the back of her hand with his thumb in reply, not once taking his eyes off the rocket. “T minus 60 seconds.” The voice boomed through the visitors centre. John rose to his feet along with the rest of the launch centre. People say that everything in life is predetermined, that life is simply a road that we walk along “T minus 50 seconds.” If that is true, then surely we have no control, there would be no point in fighting against fate “T minus 40 seconds” The distractions that life throws at us would turn out to not be distractions at all, but part of a higher plan. “T minus 30 seconds” The ground shook more intensely, juddered a bit before continuing to build. The problem with fate is that it doesn’t always go the way that you want it to. “Something’s wrong with the fuel line” A voice spoke at one of the terminals, John’s face dropped something wrong was not what he wanted to hear. “Engaging secondary line” You can wish as much as you like, it won’t change what has happened, or in the case of fate. What is about to happen. “The secondary fuel line is failing” “Abort the launch, ABORT THE LAUNCH.” “The system isn’t responding sir.” “I said ABOR….” For a split second the world paused, John glanced at the screen in front of him and saw the shuttle raise a few meters from the launch pad, before exploding into a ball of flame, his brother trapped inside. “NOOOOOOOOOO!” 10 hours later… The counsellor had assured John that his brother had felt no pain. That the rupture in the fuel tank would have shot through the shuttle almost instantaneously. It was little consideration to John. The drive back had been totally silent In a zombie like fashion, John lurched into his house. Smashing into the front door at first, face on, not turning the handle. What did it matter? What did anything matter? Louise remained a few paces behind him, her face was pale and her eyes bloodshot, distress was plastered all over her face as she followed John into the living room. On the mantle piece the clock read quarter past nine, it had been exactly ten hours since the incident and the two had not said a word to each other. Louise scared that she may open some floodgates that she could not shut, John just had nothing to say his mind was a mass of thoughts, anger, hate, fear. He was truly lost within his own mind. Louise walked over to John and put her arms around him, he was shaking but his skin was warm to the touch, his eyes was focused squarely on the television, as though this would prove to be a way out. A distraction from the events of the day. Taking a few steps forward, out of the clutches of Louise, John slowly leant down to turn on the television before pausing. The television would just remind him of the events, it would be plastered all over the news, it wasn’t everyday that something like this occurred and the media were like vultures, clawing over the remains of tragedy, because they feel the people have a right to know. John alters the path of his hand and reaches for a video, cassette by the side of the television and slotted it into the VCR. The television whirred into life and static was immediately replaced by an image on the screen. “John, I’m going to make a cup of tea” Louise said, her voice barely louder than a whisper. John simply shook his head. Louise nodded to herself and wondered off into the kitchen, she couldn’t blame herself, and she also knew that John wasn’t blaming her. No one could be blamed, it was just one of those things that happened. Yet that thinking doesn’t help when you have just lost someone that you love. Let alone your brother. “I’ve never seen him like this, I’ve never seen anyone like this” Louise whispered to herself in the kitchen, she had been trying to save face in front of John, but the tears were now streaming freely down her face. “I’m going to call in sick for him, maybe they can arrange something for him. At worst he will just have to wait till next year. He can’t wrestle in this frame of mind, he’s far too distracted, he could hurt someone or he could hurt hims…” Louise came to a deathly pause, her face turned white as realisation set in; she remembered the videotape that John had put in. In the living room John simply stood in the middle of the room, his eyes staring at the screen as the plot of the season two finale of Buffy The Vampire Slayer unfolded before him. “Bottom line is, even if you see them coming you’re never ready for the big moments.” Slowly John reaches into his back pocket, clutching something deep within his fist, his entire arm is shaking as tears slowly begin to roll down his cheek. “No one asks for their lives to change, not really, but it does. So what are we helpless? Puppets? Opening his fist, John stares at the contraption in his hand, he breathes deeply and pushes a button on the side. A medium sized silver blade flicks out rapidly, catching the light and glinting, the sharpness almost radiating from it. “No. The big moments are going to come, you can’t help that.” Clasping the knife in his left hand he extends his right arm, the blade shaking as he holds it a few centimetres above his skin, he could feel the coldness of the blade beckoning him, calling him back to where he had been before. “It’s what you do afterwards that really counts” In one swift motion John plunges the blade deeply into his arm, the skin offering little resistance, as the sharp blade tore through flesh and sinew. A small piece of flesh landed on the floor just in front of his feet. He held the blade up to his eyes, the crimson liquid running down the blade and cascading off like a waterfall. “That’s when you find out who you really are.” “JOHN!” *Final part of the roleplay in Italics is a direct quote from Buffy the Vampire Slayer: Season 2: Episode 23.
