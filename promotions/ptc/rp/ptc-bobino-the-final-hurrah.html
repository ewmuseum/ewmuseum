"The Final Hurrah" - PTC Title Entry

23 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“The Final Hurrah” By: Bob Theriault Starring: “The New Shit” Bobino Entry For: PTC Unification Title Match “Why?” The question rang inside of Bobino's head for hours, as he lay awake in bed, trying to figure out what went wrong. Bobino rubbed the bandage above his left eye, covering a deep gash he suffered at the hands of Damien Cruz at an non-televised show. He was used to blood, bruises, and battery and the usual pain of the job seemed to fade away. Yet as he thought there, he hurt. He could feel the pain circulating throughout his body, and this was a new pain, one he wasn't able to simply block out. The picture played back in his head like a bad Tarantino movie. Ok, I lied, there's really no such thing as a “bad” Tarantino film, but it played like one of his pieces none-the-less. His thoughts were so frantic, he couldn't think straight, and he kept thinking of the last thing he spoke with to the FUSE higher-ups before leaving for XUW Mayhem. The words stung, as he tried to remember the story that led to the phrase trapped in his head. “Well, we hope XUW treats you the way you've always hoped. We're not entirely sure you're needed in FUSE anymore.” The last words spoken to him by the FUSE Board of Directors before making his suspension from FUSE final. No time frame. No estimated return date. No reason to keep fighting. ~*~ Bobino watched the numbers continually tick upwards on the taxi's counter. He felt more rushed than he has in years, as he knew he was late for a FUSE house show. He had instructed the cab driver to “step on it” but he knew there was only so fast you could go in downtown Boston traffic. He had been late multiple times before, but for the first time in a long time, he was excited about his match, about his entire storyline. He worked well with Damien Cruz, and he was scheduled to take a shady victory over him tonight, to promote one of the biggest matches of Bobino's career in just a few days. He knew that being late this time, could be detrimental to this happening. Finally the cab pulled up to the arena, as Bobino tossed an uncounted amount of money to the driver, and sprinted inside the building. When he got inside, he found that FUSE management wasn't as forgiving as they usual were. “We're going to have to let Cruz take the win tonight. You can't be late anymore.” the represenative from the Board of Directors told him. “What?! I've been late plenty of times before, and you've done nothing. Why all of a sudden do I have to be punished for something that's never been a problem?” Bobino honestly didn't agree with having to do the job for Damien Cruz, and wasn't very happy about it. “Calm down. It's just a house show, it doesn't really matter as much as a televised match. You missed the morning meeting, and we've decided to become more hard-lined on tardiness. We can't let you get away with it, just because you've been here so long. Everybody needs to be treated equally, hence, your match change.” The man walked away before he could be argued with anymore, knowing that nothing Bobino said would resolve the conflict. Bobino has a skewed sense of justice. He decided that the only fair solution to the situation was to put as little effort as possible into his show with Cruz. Multiple times, he refused to synchronize with Cruz, making suplexes look flawed, and punches look fake. Towards the end of the match, Cruz had enough, and just before setting up their finishing sequence, landed a quick, stiff kick to Bobino's face, cutting his eye open. Bobino felt the kick hard, and after going through the motions to end the match with Cruz, went directly to the back to speak with Management. “You see what he did to me!? That's what happens when you let inexperienced workers in go over. They hurt people!” Bobino wiped away some of the blood that was still on his face as he yelled at the board of directors. “Maybe... just maybe... if you had been doing anything in that match, other than throwing a bitch-fit, you wouldn't have gotten kicked in the face.” Clint Stanton, Member of the Board snapped back at the furious Darwinist. “I'm sorry, my drive to put on a show was sucked out of me, after I was punished for something that has never been a problem before.” Bobino was not about to back down from this. “Well, about that. The reason the match was changed, was because we've been forced to change the line up of KillZone 35. You're not going to be booked. We need to get some momentum behind Cruz before he goes into his next match.” Cheryl Madison dropped the bomb. Bobino looked on, not quite believing what he heard. They were pulling the one match he had been excited for in quite sometime. “You're pulling the match? How can you do that? Why are you doing this.?” Bobino's questions didn't remain unanswered for long at all as the third board member chimed in. “Well, it's hard to showcase you in a match, when we've decided to suspend you.” Erik Parker's words hit even harder than Cheyrl's, and Bobino had thought that was the worst that could happen. “You've got to be friggin' kidding me! Suspended, for being late? What's wrong with you people?” Bobino never did understand the lesson that insulting the people that sign your paychecks was a very bad idea. “Well, you're suspended because your tardiness has been chronic, and we need to set an example. Basically, your the sacrificial lamb to keep everyone else in line.” Clint responded. Bobino was fuming. The amount of anger he felt at that moment was nearly indescribable, as he lashed out. “You cannot do this. I've been here longer than all of you. I was only late because my flight back from XUW Mayhem got delayed, and traffic in this city is horrible. It's not like I wasn't trying to get here. This is ridiculous! This isn't how I want to be treated!” Cheryl looked at him, done with his outbursts. She ends the conversation with a phrase that feels like a hot blade in the spine of Bobino. “Well, we hope XUW treats you the way you've always hoped. We're not entirely sure you're needed in FUSE anymore.” ~*~ The flight across country to arrive for his appearance on XUW Mayhem wasn't as relaxed as usual. Bobino spent more money than he should have, using the plane's in-flight phone, trying to get advice. He called people he hadn't heard from in months, knowing they'd be the only ones that would understand. “It's not the end of the world. You've still got XUW, you're still the same talent in the ring and on the mic as you've always been. Either they'll see the error of their ways, or you'll find work another place.” Majin had always been the voice of reason during their time as a team. He always had to keep Bobino from exploding on FUSE management, and was one of the main reasons Bobino had been around for so long. Now, Yet again, Majin's cooler head prevails and for the first time, the idea of working exclusively for a company -NOT- named FUSE Wrestling came into Bobino's mind. “Eight years is a long time to just throw away, Maj. Think about it. I stepped into FUSE as a nobody. I came to them as “The Great Ninja” and they took me and molded me into what I am today. It would be like turning my back on my best friend, it would be like spitting directly in their face.” Bobino tried to communicate his side of things to Majin, even though he was pretty sure Majin already knew how he felt. Bobino felt glad he was on the red eye flight. Hardly any other passengers were awake, and he nearly had complete privacy as he spoke to his friend. “Bob, listen. Obviously it will be weird at first, but think for a moment. Can you really turn your back on something or someone that's already turned their back on you?” Sometimes, when you're deep in thought, you're not completely aware of your surroundings. Bobino at this point began nodding, agreeing with Majin's words. He obviously forgot at the moment that Majin wasn't able to see his body language. Bobino said a few other words that he can't entirely remember, and then hung up the phone with his former partner. The rest of the flight was quiet, at least for the other passengers. For Bobino, the flight was anything but silent. His mind raced, and everything kept playing in his mind. Still no sleep, he hadn't been able to rest since the altercation. ~*~ Mayhem 211. “The New Shit” Bobino one-on-one with the Fallen Fae. What was supposed to be a red hot opener, is yet again side-tracked by the mindset of “The Master of Darwinism”. Bobino sat down with XUW's Talent Relations manager, and explained the FUSE situation. He hadn't slept in over thirty-six hours, and was having trouble standing straight. He was scheduled to go over Fae, but it seemed that his body couldn't take the mental abuse. The XUW staff listened, and while they seemed concerned, they didn't have the same history with Bobino that he's used to. They cared about their talent, and didn't feel it best for business, if a man that could barely stand won a match against one of their inner roster. Bobino went out to the ring on Mayhem 211, and put on a show. They had been allotted a six minute time frame, and Bobino used every second. He and Fae decided to thrill the crowd, and he pulled the energy and ability to do so from reserves he didn't he realize he had. The Fallen Fae took the victory, but Bobino looked like the young kid that ruled over the FUSE upper card years ago. Bobino walked up the ramp, and after the camera stopped filming, did something he had never done in his entire career. Bobino walked around ringside after the match, and shook hands with the fans. He thanked them for coming to the show, and for cheering against him. While most of the fans seemed stand-offish towards the career heel, a few of the more educated fans could tell it wasn't the character of “Bobino” trying to interact with them, it was the man, Bob. As he stepped through the entrance, to the backstage, Bobino found the Fallen Fae, who was siting in a chair, trying to recover from their match. He showed his appreciation to the very person he just lost to, congratulating Fae on their match. After their conversation, Bobino returned to the office of the XUW management. He walked in with a genuine smile, and thanked them for letting him work tonight. He chuckled a bit to himself, as he turned to leave, really only needed to say thank you. “Hold on. I think we've got something that might take your mind off your troubles.” the voice in the room called to him. Bobino stopped in his tracks, and turned back into the room. He looked on with a curious look in his eyes. “You've been a part of Primetime Central before, right? You fought for FUSE in a couple contests, if our records are correct.” The question didn't seem of any importance, but Bobino nodded, remembering fighting for the Extreme Title against Archibald MacGreggor in a huge cluster match. “That's what we thought. How would you like to do it one more time, except this time, fly the banner of Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling?” he offered, seemingly placing his trust in a man who until today, claimed his gimmick was to embarrass the company he was now exclusively working for. “You're talking about their Title Unification match, right? Why wouldn't you send The Shadow, or Doctor Curiosity? Why trust me?” Bobino's voice reeked of uncertainty. “Bobino, listen. We don't hire anybody here without doing some research on them. We know what you're capable of. We've seen you put on some of the best shows of your career, and we've seen the passion you can put into a match. We know what you've been through with FUSE, and we saw the passion you just put into a match that would've been meaningless to nearly every other worker in this business. If you can put that hard work into a match that has no meaning, we know you won't disappoint over in Primetime Central, at least not if you can find that same passion again.” The trust in his voice was something that Bobino couldn't have prepared for. “Well... thank you. I'm not going to disappoint, not this time.” Bobino shook his hand and left the room, before he could notice the tears welling up in the corners of his eyes. Bobino went back to the hotel room that XUW had reserved for him, and thought long and hard. He thought about the trust this company was placing in him, and about the words Majin spoke to him earlier. He thought about his career, and his loyalty to a place he helped create. He realized that he was now a participant in the biggest match in the history of Primetime Central, and had a chance to prove to FUSE that he isn't a disappointment. He had a chance to prove to XUW that their trust was well-placed. He had a chance, sitting in front of him right now, to prove to everyone that he has accomplished everything he could think of. More importantly, he had a chance to prove to himself that he was still in this. He had a chance to rest his mind, and regain the confidence that made him so dangerous. He had a chance, to leave a legacy that he had always dreamed of. Had had the chance, if FUSE decided not to bring him back, to go out with a bang. Bobino placed his head on the pillow, and with the new found respect and pride he had in XUW, he closed his eyes and he did something he hadn't been able to accomplish in over forty-eight hours. He slept.
