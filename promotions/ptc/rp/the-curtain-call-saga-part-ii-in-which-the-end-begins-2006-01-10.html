The Curtain Call Saga: Part II, In Which the End Begins

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-10 It wouldn’t be an exaggeration to say that DarkStar was more surprised at that moment than he had been at any given time during the last three years. He had witnessed a lot during his tenure as a God, but nothing prepared him for seeing her again. "Harle?" DarkStar spoke the name as if he was trying to convince himself that she was real. "This must be one of Loki’s tricks, or even one of yours Lucifer." Lucifer smirked that knowing smirk that could melt the surface of the sun. "If you could combat death itself, than surely it should not bewilder you to know that others have the same ability." "But she is not a God, she is a harlot." Harle sucked air in between her teeth, "Tsk tsk mon amour. Now zat iz not ‘ow you should be greeting me. Maybe you have forgotten moi, no?" "As much as I wish I could have, no, I have not forgotten you Harle." In truth, it would have been impossible to forget her based solely on her appearance. She had changed slightly since the last time DarkStar had been in her presence, she no longer wore the surreal comic book style harlequin head gear. Her dirty blonde hair was on the short side and pushed rigidly behind her ears, DarkStar briefly thought that maybe it had been scared into that position by the sadist that it grew out of, her make up was the same as it had been before. She no longer wore the white face but the rest mimicked her court jester persona. Her outfit also remained the same- an orgy of colors and insanity. The perfect raiment for someone such as herself. A silence fell between the two, if the Guinness Book of World Records kept track of such things, this would fall as third on the all-time most uncomfortable silences list (shortly behind the moments after George Bush the First vomited on a Japanese offical and the audience at a Jerry Seinfeld show briefly after his failed "And whats the big deal with gas chambers" bit.) "I guess I should leave you two alone. You must have many things to discuss," said Lucifer, "of course, my morbid curiosity to see whether or not you two rip each others limbs off and beat the other to death with them far outweighs my manners." He paused for a second as Harle and DarkStar glared at him with a collective anger that would be able to stop Armageddon if they so wished to. Lucifer took this as a cue to vanish from the room with a small audible popping noise. Harle was the first to break the silence. "Oi feel like oi should offer you an explanasiown…" With a wave of his hand, DarkStar dismissed her. "I do not want to hear an explanation. I have had what could easily be considered one of the shittiest spans of time in the history of this sad and pathetic universe. You crawling out from whatever rock, or whatever supernatural being, you have been laying under for the last few years is really just icing on this gigantic cake of terribleness." She sighed, "Now zat eez entirely unfair to moi. You did not mind eet when oi was insatiable with you. But zee minute oi moved on moi promiscuity ‘as become a problem between us." "Moved on?" DarkStar yelled, "You didn’t move on. You left me, got yourself killed, and jumped into bed with any God you could get your little hands on. Which, in reality, was quite ironic." He made sure to stress the word ironic. Somewhere inside Harle’s mind something began to tick. She did not take to insults kindly, even if they were coming from somebody she once loved. "Our story to thiz point ‘as been complicated mon amour, oi would ‘ope you wouldn’t want to complicate things any further by forcing me to rip your heart out." DarkStar sneered. This was the last thing he wanted. Fighting Harle would be an annoyance, he was nothing more than a lost soul right now. He had no place in the God realms of the InBetween and he was being offered a slight reprieve from whatever sordid fate faced him on Earth. A battle with her could be the last straw of his existence at this point. But she was right. Their past was beyond complicated… From the Desk of Lucifer Morningstar, Father of Lies I write these words knowing truly that my life as a God, and more importantly my memories, are coming slowly to their end. In my time I have seen many things that men wouldn’t even dare believe existed. I have experienced times that were both good and bad, but always remarkable. Nothing I have done do I regret. So it is with that I write my last memoirs, to be left in the care of Lucifer Morningstar and returned to me after I have left this realm and began my stay on Earth as a mute. I will put my pen to paper and write what I wish to remember down on these pages, it is with this knowledge that I will attempt to salvage what is left of Talena and I’s lives and, in the end, exact my revenge upon God himself. I have decided that the first entry of my pre-mortem journal will be about the every tumultuous relationship I have had with Harle. Her history is ridiculous, of course. She was young, barely a teenager, when she was kidnapped from her town of Agincourt in France by a traveling circus. They trained as an acrobat and brainwashed by her new family. Her memories of a young child growing up in a wealthy family were destroyed and replaced with the desire to turn towards a path of crime. Her circus would make money on the side by holding up banks and committing many minor misdeeds. Eventually through a rash of mysterious disappearances, Harle became the leader of this band of circus freaks and turned them away from minor crimes and towards more nefarious means. She became quite a renowned felon in her country, and as she progressed her offenses became more violent. She reveled in the slaughter, blood being her ultimate turn on. Her insanity grew to such levels that even the circus family that created her shunned her from their group. That was their last mistake. With nowhere else to turn and the blood of many on her hands, she came to America. She attempted to curb her blood-lust and join the noble ranks of the countries exotic dancers in order to make enough money to live. Her harlequin gimmick was a hit in the stranger clubs. Our paths crossed by nothing more than fate. I had been in a bar in the Philadelphia suburbs drinking my sorrows about Talena away. At that time my love had left me due to past transgressions. She had learned that once I had shared the bed with Cerridwen, the great Celtic Goddess. This led to her spurning my first proposal for our marriage, and her leaving for her native land of Canada. She was powerful, and try as I may I could not find her dreams… I could not talk to her and convince her of my sorrow for my mistake. So it was with that sadness, the idea that I had lost the one true love of my life for something of my past I could not take back, that I tossed back various liquors in a mortal setting. Harle had been there, performing. We had each caught the scent of the other. She would tell me that in her entire life she had not met a man that could fulfill her (this explained her taste for the female gender quite well), ‘they were all to tame’ she would say to me time and again, so ours was a match made in heaven. In her I got the pure psychopathic drive that I needed to propel myself, and in me she got a God- one who could open her eyes to worlds that she needed to make her life whole. We coexisted for a while. Thriving in eachothers decadence and leaving a path of destruction in our wake. It had to come to an end. What an end it was. I had been at odds with the only man other than my true nemesis Clyde to ever give me a challenge, the misguided ‘demi-god’ Big Alan Steele. After a string of battles, he had kidnapped my Harle and created a nefarious plan to drop her into what we both believed to be a vat of acidic substance. When I could not reach her in time, he cut her bonds and sent her falling into the liquid. Harle screamed and thrashed and I eventually dragged her out of the vat, only to find out that the liquid itself was merely water. Steele laughed and made his way out before I could retaliate. I looked down and saw that Harle’s make up had washed off. Her face revealed to me in its true form for the very first time. She thrashed some more and eventually became still. It was only water, but it harmed her like the acid we believed it to be. When she woke up, she lost all memories of me. Our time spent together and even her traumatic time as a teenager were all forgotten. The shock of the drop and the removal of her makeup reverted her memories, she left me and returned home to find her family in France. I was bereft of a love once again. Believing this story to be over I sought to take my own life, without love what was I but a shell. A worthless God. But this story was far from over. "Look, Harle, this is just a shock. I have to collect my thoughts." He came close to apologizing for lashing out at her, but knew he wouldn’t give her that satisfaction. "You do not ‘ave much time. ‘as Lucifer not told you his plan?" DarkStar cocked his eyebrow, "What plan?" Soundlessly, Lucifer reappeared. Harle smiled that same batshit insane smile that sent shivers down DarkStar’s spine. "I plan on making up for my transgressions in the past, Drazail. I plan on doing what you could not." For a second DarkStar thought he could see the morningstars old wings, black and ragged and full of spite. "I plan on reclaiming the keys to my realm and destroying the gates of heaven. I do hope you will help me. Of course you have little choice if you want to regain yourself." DarkStar smiled. Of course he would. As long as he got to put the knife in Gods back himself.
