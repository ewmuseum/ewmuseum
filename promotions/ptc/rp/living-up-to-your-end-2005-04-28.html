Living up to your end

28 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-28 <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "You haven't got a shot in hell of winning this match up Morales, so you might as well pack it up and forget about it." "You might as well face the facts and realise that your going to be defeated by one of XUW's finest." "Forget this Cruiser tournament, cause you haven't got any business trying to hang with the best that Primetime Central has to offer." These are just some examples of the kind of things I've had to endure over the last ten days. The kind of cruel statements that are being thrown my way at ever single turn, by any and every wrestling fan that can possibly open their mouths to shout at me. Their message is so clear that even a blind, mute, deaf, American, racist, redneck could understand it. Racism is alive and well in the United States of America and I, along with my fellow Mexican brothers Los Diablos, are the poster children for it's wrath. It's clear to me that my lack of "showboating" luchadore offense is pissing off more and more simple minded, and stereo type loving, American wrestling fans by the minute. I suppose that I should just give up. Pack my bags and forget this whole wrestling career thing I've been trying to live out, just cause I am not going about doing it the way that everyone believes I should. I guess, gringos, that I could just walk into Hin See's office and hand in my resignation papers for PRIME and then forfiet my spot in the tournament. Sure…I could do those things, but I won't. I'm not going to allow the racism of a few to put out my flame of desire to succeed. It doesn't take a real smart man to realise when you've got to take a stand, and my time to take a stand is right now. With that stand in mind I have to pose a question to all those "doubters" out there who believe they have the entire wrestling world figured out just cause they managed to watch a match or two in their lives. Why is it that every time you turn around in this business that people, specially the fans, want to believe that people win on their name value alone? In my case how does the name Cereal Killer hold anymore weight on its own than that of Enrique Morales? How come just because the Cereal Killer comes from the XUW promotion does everyone believe that he is just going to steam roll his way through the second round and onward through the tournament? Why is it that people believe I am nothing more than the man with the funny "Jumping Bean" nickname? Is it confidence? Is it arrogance? Could it just be plain, good old fashion American stupidity? Possibly it's all of those things rolled up into one tightly knit ball of junk that clouds the judgment and bias of non Spanish wrestlers. I'm Enrique Morales damn it! I am the manager of the greatest tag team in the history of one of the greatest federations to ever grace the Primetime Central. I've lead my team to major victories, including one over the current PTC Global Champion. I've walked through hell and faced off with Hessian, only to walk away and live to tell about it. Yet every time I turn around I continue to hear people tell me how worthless I am. How I have done nothing more than waste my time and delay the inevitable. Well to you all I say this gringos… Ustedes chupan dick grande de asno de tiempo! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> Monday, April 25th, 2005. San Antonio, Texas "This is complete bullshit! I have never been so disrespected in my life!" The booming voice of Baltasar seemed to rattle the walls in the little court house that Los Diablos and their manager Enrique Morales stood in. Morales sits on the black bench with his head in his hands just trying to forget the entire ordeal from the previous weeks ReVolution. The ReVolution that should have seen Los Diablos finally climb that mountain they had been scaling up since their arrival in PRIME in early January. The ReVolution that should of seen them walking away as the PRIME Tag Team Champions of the World. "Deville is such a low life. If he didn't want to face us he could have just been a man and admited it to my face!" Instead they found themselves in shocked by tasers, handcuffed, and hauled off into the night on illegal deportation charges. False charges as you might have guessed. "Coward! This is just complete crap," Baltasar continued to rant and rave as he paced back and forth in front of his friends Enrique and Angelus. "Could you stop that! Your going to drive me insane with that God forsaken pacing!" Enrique shouted, catching both of his buddies off guard. "Whats eating you hermano," questioned Angelus with a look of concern. "Yeah, essa, it isn't like you to snap at one of us. Whats eating you?" Baltasar asked as he placed a hand on the shoulder of his manager. "I'm sorry boys. It's just my head is swimming with all this information I need to try and process. I've got that tournament I'm trying to win, along with getting you guys your rightful rematch for the tag team titles in PRIME, and now this. It's just more than one little Mexican should have to handle," explained Morales as he simply sighed and shook his head back and forth slowly. "Understood essa. I know how you feel. The disrespect is wearing on all of us, especially me. In fact I am seriously considering going back to Mexico and not returning to PRIME at all, due to this latest set of racism." Baltasar explained to them both as he sat down on the bench lining the wall next to them. Both of them looked at him like he might have hit his head to hard or something recently. "You'd let Deville run you off like that," questioned Morales with a look of confusion etched across his face. "That doesn't sound like the tag team partner I've come to know," Angelus chimed in with a tone that conveyed great concern for the knowledge that Baltasar had just bestowed upon them. "Maybe not like the old me, but the new me is fed up with this crap and the fact that Hin See and Pham are going to continue to allow. Whats the point if every time we get near the promise land, some smuck can simply bribe a ref, or a federal agent, and get out of beating us cleanly?" Baltasar questioned his two friends, more rhetorically than anything else. "We just need to get this matter with the deportation cleared up here today and forget about that. I've got a plan to get back at Deville for that little stunt last week. Now why the hell haven't we been able to see this guy yet," Enrique began to question as he stood up and began looking around in a frustrated manner. "I'm starting to get a bit ansy waiting for this to happen and be over with!" "Don't worry about it, I've taken care of it." All three men spun around on their heels and caught eyes with the master of the voice they had just heard. It was none other than Jacob Cross, the new manager, or agent if you will, for Morales and his tag team. Cross simply smiled as he walked up to the trio, flashing his golden Rolex watch and the high class suit as he did. "What are you talking about Cross? What did you take care of," Morales questioned while placing his hands into his pockets. Still not sure how much he could trust this Cross fellow Enrique was a bit apprehensive about what Cross might have done in a situation like this. "As I said I have taken care of it. The bogus stuff is dropped and it won't be an issue again. I have friends in high places too, you know, and I can play hard ball with the best of them. Having you deported to avoid a match with you was a stroke of genius, I must admit, and I can hardly see how you guys don't see the brilliance of that move. I must meet this Angelo Deville sometime. He seems like just my kind of guy." Cross explained while dusting off his perfect gray suit, a gleam of excitement dancing in his eyes. "That is just the kind of thing we're not happy about Senor Cross," Baltasar began while clenching his fists. "You seem too much like Angelo Deville for my taste. It's no wonder you find his actions amusing. Enrique I say we drop him now like a bad habit, before the guy can stab us in the back the way he is planning. He is nothing more than another worthless gringo out to screw us." "You might be right Baltasar, but I have given Cross my word, and a Spanish mans word is his bond. You know that as well as anyone," Morales gave his retort with a slight sigh. "True. But we don't have to like it," Angelus remarked as he stood up straight and stared down at Cross, a man who gave up a couple of inches to him. "Look fella's, boys, essa's," Cross began. "You have nothing to fear with me. As with Mr. Morales my word is my bond. I told him I'd become his, and your, agent so you that you could focus on other, more important matters, rather than events such as these. Today I've lived up to promise already and I will do it many more times if needed. Besides doesn't it benefit you to have someone like Deville working on yourside? Now you have a person who knows how to think like him and act like him. Its a win/win situation for everyone. Baltasar just shakes his head and walks off away from the group, with Angelus close behind. Enrique simply takes a deep breath as he rubs his temples, trying to get rid of the agony of the migrane that seems to be attempting to creep into his skull. "Forgive them Jacob, they only look out for my best intrests. Their trust is not easily gained, under most circumstances, and when it comes to people who remind them of bitter rivals it's even harder to gain that trust. You understand that I am sure." "Oh absolutely Enrique. They will come around," Cross began with a smile creeping across his face. "In time." The two new associates shake hands and begin to walk out of the court house together discussing plans for…well for whatever they feel like I suppose. What is this unholy alliance going to lead to in terms of how it affects not only the tournament, but the lives and careers of anyone involved with PRIME as well? Stay tuned.
