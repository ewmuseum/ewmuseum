A little less conversation, a little more action please

25 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-25 Trixie sits in the plush reception area idly swinging her sandal from her toe and flipping through the pages of a glossy magazine. She barely notices what’s on each page (which is a shame really, the article entitled ‘7 Signs That Your Boyfriend is Still Secretly in Lust With His Female Boss’ would have been quite enlightening), she has something more important to think about. Trixie hates making decisions, especially ones as big as this. Growing up with nothing; nothing on the table, nothing in her wallet, nothing to do and nothing between her pretty little ears, she finds it hard to cope with not just having something, but the concept of choosing between two somethings. Once, when she found five bucks in the bathroom of a Dairy Queen, it took her a full week to decide which six lottery numbers to play with it. Growing up so disadvantaged, she had always dreamed of being whisked away from the dismal trailer park by a knight in a white pick-up. Craig Goat may not be the most knightly of men and has never driven a pick-up, but when she first set eyes on him in the truck stop where she bused tables, she knew he was the one for her. Pretty and petite, she had always attracted the attention of men, but never men as well spoken, kind, caring and seemingly cultured as Craig. So donning her newest pair of cut-off denims, a boob tube and sparkly lip gloss, she snared her man. She had moved in with Craig to Goat Mansion a little over a month ago and everything was perfect… well, except for Nurse Wretched. Trixie just couldn’t understand why Nurse hated her so much. At first she had tried her best to get along with the formidable and twisted woman, but now it was all hair-pulling and handbags at dawn for some reason she can’t possibly fathom. Trixie is saved from the exhausting effort of thought by the receptionist approaching her with a smile. “Good morning, I’m Davina, and I’ll be your manicurist today, have you picked which color polish you’ll be having yet?” “I don’t know! Red is such a sexy color, but pink is hot this season. Then there’s my new Monolo Blahniks, red would probably look better with them, but most of the clothes in my wardrobe are pink! Oh! I like green too… and purple. I can’t decide!” “Less talking, more walking” Trixie struggles to keep up with the huge, hairy, army-boot wearing Davina as she follows her down the twisting corridors of the salon past myriads of treatment rooms where women are plucked, waxed, wrapped, steamed and occasionally beaten with cacti fronds, all in the name of beauty. She leads her to a door marked ‘exit’ and ushers her through into a litter-strewn alleyway. Seeing Trixie’s face scrunch up in confusion, Davina explains. “We’ve had to expand the salon recently; I’m just, umm, taking you to the new place.” Easily satisfied with this explanation, Trixie follows her up to the roof of a parking structure and into the back of a shiny Hummer. “Wow! This is what I call service! You’re going to do my nails in the back of this bling car?” Davina turns to her with a wry grin, “I guess you could say that… now hold out your hands and let me have a look at those talons.” Pigeons launch themselves from the roof in a startled flurry at the terrified screams coming from the Hummer as Davina, or as she’s better known (when not in drag), Medium Dave, gets to work with a hunting knife. ………………………………………………… Craig is still depressed and slouches around the house in a fairly good imitation of Sylvia Plath. Even the threat of Nurse’s ‘Plan B’ doesn’t worry him. After all, what more is there that she can do to hurt him? Beat him again? Mess with his psyche? She promised him once that she would make him immune to pain, and she has succeeded admirably. So, when she grins evilly and hands him a package wrapped in a pretty pink bow over breakfast, he just sighs and opens it expecting it to blow up in his face or something. However, this time Nurse has outdone herself. As he stares into the box, the blood drains from his face and anger wells up inside him like poisonous bile. “Her f-f-fing…” “Yes Craig, her finger! And if you don’t start putting some effort into preparing for Jenna Ryder, Trixie’ll be peeling your potatoes with a pirate hook!” Confused, he says to her, “There’s no finger in here just… her finger nails! I’d recognize these chewed pink parings anywhere. How could you, you sadistic bitch! What have you done with Trixie!” “Ack! I should have known Dave would wimp out. Next time you need some motivation, I’ll be the one harvesting body parts, not him. So get your shiny white ass in gear and get in that goddamn ring!” This defilation of his precious Trixie is more than Craig can bear. He was prepared to take what ever pain and torture Nurse could inflict on him, but didn’t even consider that she would sink as low as to hurt his girlfriend. With a roar, he launches himself at Nurse, knocking her to the cold hard floor and presses his hands about her throat as he screams, “How could you do this to her! She’s innocent, she’s never done anything to harm you. Where is she? I want her back… now!” Nurse easily wriggles her way out of Craig’s hold and reverses it so she is now the one straddling his chest in her famous ‘Thunder Thighs’ submission move. As she slowly squeezes the air out of his lungs, she whispers, “That slut is a distraction to you, just like your X-Box was, so I’ve confiscated her too. If you’re a good boy and beat Jenna Ryder, maybe I’ll let you have her back all safe and sound. But if I think you’re working even one percent under your true potential, you’ll get her back a little scratched, a bit broken and bearing a lot less digits. Understand?” As Craig loses consciousness, Nurse untangles herself from him and picking up her a suitcase by the door, leaves Goat Mansion. …………………………………………… When Big Dave arrives at the Mansion for training a few hours later, he finds Craig pacing about the house randomly destroying everything he can find belonging to Nurse. “You know, if the two of you keep ripping this place apart every time you’re in a bad mood, that fortune of yours isn’t going to last very long. Put down Esme’s underwear and come work out some of that aggression in the ring with me.” “Where is she Dave? Where’s Nurse? If she’s hurting Trixie, I’ll… I’ll” “Calm down! Even I know where Trixie is being held. My brother is taking care of Trixie, and I swear Nurse’ll never figure out where. I’m not sure where Esme has gone though. She knew you’d be more than a little ticked off and reckoned she better stay out of the way so you could concentrate on training. Speaking of which, we better get to the gym.” ………………………………………… Meanwhile, in an old abandoned warehouse by the docks… (Did you know that organized crime types have no imagination?) Trixie wiggles her toes with glee. “Davina, how clever of you, a different color on each nail! I wish I’d thought of that.” “You can call me Dave you know.” “But that’s such a masculine name! You wouldn’t want anyone thinking you were a man dressed up, now would you!” Medium Dave/Davina isn’t sure what to make of Trixie. Is she really so stupid as to believe he is a woman, or is she having fun at his expense? “So, can I go home now? Craig will be expecting me.” “Um, I told you already, Nurse Esme wants you to stay here with me for a while.” Thoughtful, Trixie chews this over. “I guess I underestimated her. She knows I’ve had trouble fitting in since I moved here, and now she’s gone out of her way to find a friend for me! How nice of her. I just know that you and I are going to be the best of the girl friends. Oh! Let’s talk about shoes! I have a red pair, and a blue pair and a shiny black pair of heels and some flip flops in like, loads of colors. I saw the cutest little pair of lemon kitten heels yesterday… do you think my ankles are too fat to wear that style?” “Unbelievable” Dave thinks to himself, “She really is that thick!” ………………………………………… When Nurse Wretched moved into Goat Mansion with Craig, the first thing she did was convert the elegant ballroom into a top class gym with a boxing ring in its centre. However, once Craig pointed out her mistake, she replaced it with a full regulation sized wrestling ring. This ring has seen much blood and sweat, most of it Craig’s, so you can’t blame him for being reluctant to step back in. “I don’t want to do this Dave. Wouldn’t my time be better spent looking for my Trixie?” “No. Esme really wants you to win this match against Jenna Ryder. If you don’t put the work in, she’ll do something worse to either you or your girlfriend, and even though I’d try, I can’t promise I’d be able to protect you from her wrath. Best thing you can do is train, and train hard.” “Why is this so important to Nurse? Does she have some history with this Jenna Ryder or something?” “She’s never met your opponent, but she does have a huge beef against any other woman who thinks she can wrestle. Nurse may use her looks to her advantage on occasion in the ring, but she despises women like Ryder who use it as their only weapon.” Shocked, Craig asks, “So Nurse is some kind of feminist then?” “God no! She just hates women more than men. If you ask me, she’s jealous! Have you seen the rack on Ryder? Maybe I’ll show you one of her tapes if you do well today.” Conversation over, the men get down to some serious work. Dave’s legendary Zen-like patience means he is normally a great teacher, but today, it is pushed to its very limit. Craig may not have any experience in proper wrestling matches, but Dave has seen him in the ring with Nurse on innumerable occasions, so why today is he wrestling like a three day old fetus? Craig tries and fails at every wrestling trick he’s ever learnt. No matter how much help Dave gives him, Craig fumbles each move, each hold, each throw. Exhausted and deflated, he slumps to the floor in defeat. Dave sits beside him. “Why can’t I do this? Christ, I’m never going to see Trixie again if I can’t get this right.” Dave, reluctantly asks, “Could it be that you’re unconsciously jeopardizing it because secretly you don’t want to see her again? Are you sure you’re totally over Esme?” “Don’t be stupid! Of course I’m not in love with Nurse anymore! I’d have to be insane.” “You stopped taking your medication, didn’t you?! “Yeah, but that has nothing to … you think I’m insane again? I don’t believe you Dave! I thought you were my friend. So what if I’m not taking my pills, all they ever did was make me lose concentration and slow me down so much I had drink gallons of coffee just to stay conscious. Trixie is the only medication I need now.” “Well, if it’s not that, then why all of a sudden have you forgotten how to wrestle?” “I dunno, it’s weird. When I’m stood there facing you, I can’t figure out where exactly I should stand and where to put my hands for throws. I always knew instinctively what to do with Nurse, but it just seems so wrong with you.” Dave gets up, grabs his kit bag and heads for the door, saying “I’ve got an idea, you wait here for me and I’ll be back as soon as I can. Actually, would you mind cooking something up? I’m starving. Oh, and we may have guests.” ……………………………………… Nurse sits on the bed in the sparse motel room and contemplates phoning Big Dave to ask how the training is going. She quickly dismisses this idea as she knows all too well that he’s not too pleased with her right now after the stunt with kidnapping Trixie. But what choice did she have? She needs Craig on top form without any distractions if he is going to beat Jenna Ryder. She feels such a strong compulsion to destroy her, and how better to destroy a porn star/wrestlers career than to have her lose spectacularly to someone like Craig? Nurse knows he could be a brilliant wrestler, but looking at him you’d never think it. He’s never grown out of that awkward geeky teen look, no matter how strong he’s gotten. Jenna Ryder has no place in GTT4. How can a porn star make it in wrestling? She hates to see her sport defiled by women like this by sluts who sleep there way to the top and women who use their god given superiority over men to gain the upper hand in sport. Nurse cheats too, but that’s different, she never, well rarely, uses her femininity to tip a match. For Nurse, Jenna Ryder represents everything that’s wrong with wrestling, and even if it kills her, or more likely, Craig, she’ll do her goddamn best to stop Ryder advancing. ……………………………………… Trixie and Davina/Dave return to the bleak warehouse laden with shopping bags. Excited by their purchases, they model all the new outfits for each other. “I told you floral print would look good on you! Why don’t you try on that pretty jacket with it?” “Gee, I never knew clothes could be so much fun! Do you really think this suits my shape?” Trixie casts an appraising eye over Davina/Dave’s new figure hugging mini-dress. “Umm, it’ll probably look a little better when I do your legs. I can’t believe you’ve never thought of waxing them!” ……………………………………… When Big Dave arrives at the door of Goat Mansion with three beautiful women in tow, Craig thinks all his birthdays have come at once. Nervous, he invites them in. The conversation at the dinner table is stunted. Craig has never had the best social skills and after a few mumbled questions, gives up altogether and Big Dave is too busy eating to take up the slack. It’s obvious that while the women know each other, they could hardly be called friends. Among the women, there seems to be some kind of competition going on over who can eat the least amount of calories. Watching the women glare menacingly at each other, Craig decides not to bother with dessert. Still unsure as to why they are here in the first place, Craig catches Dave’s eye in between mouthfuls and raises his eyebrow enquiringly. “I can see you’re dying to know what’s going on, so let’s get to the gym shall we?” Craig groans, “I’m tired, don’t you think we’ve done enough for today?” “No” Knowing he’ll never win an argument with Big Dave, he acquiesces. Once more in the gym, Dave finally introduces the three ladies, Selma, Harriet and Wilma, the local university’s all female wrestling team. “Craig, I’ve come to realize that the reason you had such difficulty today is because you’ve never really wrestled a man before. Except for Borealis, who lets face it isn’t exactly the most masculine of men, the only wrestling partner you’ve had is Nurse Wretched. I figured this out earlier when you said you just didn’t know where to put your hands. Women’s bodies are a lot different then ours, and so your wrestling moves are altered slightly for them.” “Aw come on Dave! That can’t be true!” Dave nods at Selma who then charges at Craig. She grabs him by the wrist and twists it behind him, but without even thinking Craig moves underneath her and reverses the move, before tripping her to the canvas. “See Craig? You instinctively knew how to counter her. I’m starting to believe that you can win this match. If you can go into a ring day after day with Nurse and not come out dead, then yeah, you’ve got a better than good chance.” Dave sends Wilma into the ring with Craig, and after a few minutes, she taps out. “Good work! Harriet, your turn next.” This time Craig is the one to tap out. Just like with Dave earlier today, Craig acts like a total newbie and stands there shocked as he is hit with Clothesline after Irish Whip after Suplex. Mystified, Dave sends Selma and Wilma back in the ring. Craig does well. Again he sends in Harriet, and he flumps it. An hour and a lot of bruises later, the girls have gone home $100 each richer and Dave gets a chance to discuss his revised revelation with Craig. “Ok, it looks like not only can you only wrestle women, but you can only wrestle women you find attractive. Guess it’s about time I showed you what Jenna Ryder looks like.” He sticks a tape in the VCR and sits back to watch Craig’s reaction. “She’s good, she’s got that guy pinned already. “Woah! Why is she taking off her top? Oh my God! What I’d do to get my hands on a pair like that, you were right about her rack Dave, almost as good as Nurse’s. “Hang on. No way is that a legal move! Why the hell is her opponent impaling her with his… hang on, I get it. She’s a porn star! Why didn’t you tell me a hell of a lot sooner? I can’t wait to get that body underneath me.” Dave smiles wryly, “I guess you’re ready then.” “Umm, I just gotta go… to the bathroom for a few minutes. Got any more of those tapes?” ……………………………………………… Trixie rips the wax strip from Davina/Daves top lip. With a scream he collapses back onto the floor, his new lace underwear showing. …………………………………………….. Nurse sits in the booth of a restaurant. As she scans the menu, she finds herself missing Craig’s cooking… or is it just Craig she misses? Nah, couldn’t be. ……………………………………………. That night, Craig dreams of women, or more correctly, a woman made up of many others. She has Jenna Ryder’s body, Nurse Wretched’s face and Trixie’s long blonde hair. She’s also pinned beneath him while pyros flare and the crowd cheers wildly. Definitely his best wet dream in some time. So does this mean Craig Goat is ready? Is he truly going to win the GTT4 Tournament?
