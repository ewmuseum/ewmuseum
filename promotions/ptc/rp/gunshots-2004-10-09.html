Gunshots!

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 *You see Fred Grest and Scott Vyper are sitting down drinking beer. Well at least Scott Vyper is drinking beer Fred Grest is only 20 why in the hell should he have a beer in his hand right now.* Scott Vyper- Its so nice to have a nice glass of beer. Fred Grest- Damn you! Scott Vyper- I love a good beer! Fred Grest- Give me one you bastard! Scott Vyper- No you're a minor Fred that would be wrong. Fred Grest- Two things wrong with that Scott. One being what the hell do you care about whats wrong or not. Two just give me the GOD DAMN! Its my house after all! Scott Vyper- Both good points but once again I have the thrump card. Fred Grest- Oh yeah whats that? Scott Vyper- There is a camera right there. Fred Grest- Son of a bitch how do those things get in here! They show up everywhere! Scott Vyper- I explained this to you before Fred these camera show up everywhere. Fred Grest- Like in your locker room huh Scott? Scott Vyper- You had to bring that up didn't you? Fred Grest- Oh yeah it gave you a much wider fan base too. Sure you got some female fans but that wasn't all was it Scott. Scott Vyper- Sadly I know, which you were kind enough to point out in one of your WMW promos. Fred Grest- Anytime Scott..... Anytime.... *Scott Vyper takes a chug of his beer just to spite Fred Grest.* Fred Grest- You mother fucker. Scott Vyper- You have a mouth on you for someone so young. Fred Grest- I hate you..... Scott Vyper- Everyone does Im heel remember? Anyway shouldn't Owen and Adidax be back by now? Fred Grest- Yes they should... bastard Owen turning 21 before me! Scott Vyper- Wow someone is bitter and it isn't the beer. Fred Grest- I am not bitter! Scott Vyper- Alright, buddy if you say so. Fred Grest- If anyone here is bitter its you. Scott Vyper- Me? Why is that? Fred Grest- A 20 year old made it farther then you in GTT4. Dallas Reeves the man who kicked your ass is facing a retart named Unstoppable Eugene. Scott Vyper - Eh, I'd rather be watching Dallas Reeves fight the retard then myself... Atleast I have some dignity left when I was eliminated from the tournament. Fred Grest- Dignity? didn't you once wrestle a guy named Bunny Mac? Scott Vyper - No, but Chill was tag team champions with him Fred Grest- Yeah I think Chill wrestled that Jared guy from Subway or was it Clay Henry? Scott Vyper - I dunno, but I know his biggest oponnent is his own wife Fred Grest- Sorta like you and that Fetish girl. You know the one that bitch slapped your drunken ass in a few weeks ago? Speaking of which that reminds me its good to know when to quit. How much did you have to drink that night? Scott Vyper - ..... IT WAS HARD ALCOHOL OKAY?!.... I have my limits.... I just don't like to be controlled Fred Grest- Didn't Fetish call you a lightweight? Scott Vyper - When it comes to alcohol yes... she knows better otherwise if you know what I mean. Fred Grest- I would be a smartass and ask you to explain anyway but I rather this promo make it to air. Scott Vyper- Smart man *You hear a knock on the door..... drone sounding.* Fred Grest- Wouldn't want you to get off your drunk ass and stumble your way to the door. Scott Vyper- Hey its your house where you've cut every promo for GTT4. Fred Grest- Hey on every part on one I was on my way to my house. Scott Vyper- If you wan't to call that a promo. *Grest turns away ingoring Scott and opens the door. He sees Adidax and Owen with there hands up as The Twisted Cowboy stands behind them.* The Twisted Cowboy- In the house you two....... NOW!. *Owen and Adidax walk into the house and you can see Twisted Cowboy has two guns there not ordinary guns oh no not for The Twisted Cowboy. He has a little kids cowboy hat on for one. You know the things with the strap underneath anyway back to the guns. The guns are get this CAP GUNS!* The Twisted Cowboy- On the floor all of you! *Adidax and Owen defenseless get on the ground in a hurry but Fred Grest and Scott Vyper pull out there CAP GUNS! We have a wild west show down here folks.* The Twisted Cowboy- Oh so you two want to play huh? Fred Grest- Drop your weapons Twisted! I know your mad about me kicking your ass about five times over the the past few months but its no reason to take it this far! Scott Vyper- Listen too the man don't get stupid....... oh wait too late for that. The Twisted Cowboy- This coming from a man with blue hair! Scott Vyper- What you have on is much worse then my blue hair. *BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG! In a blink of an eye three shots were fired. The Twisted Cowboy is dead..... you can tell my the ketchup on his shirt. Wait! OH NO!* Fred Grest- Owen! Scott Vyper- Adidax! *They both have ketchup stains on there shirts. Which ofcourse means there dead.* Fred Grest- He was my best friend! Scott Vyper- Adidax! .............. wait I never really liked him anyway. Adidax- Jackass! *All 4 of them laugh at what they're doing.* Fred Grest- Quit ruining it! Owen- Can I get up now or do you have more drama to do? Fred Grest- Bastards! Owen- Ofcourse! Fred Grest- Err! I have to get ready for Odium anwayway..... *The scene starts to fade to black.* Fred Grest- Wait! My catch phrase! *The scene goes back to the 4 morons you put up listening too for the past few minutes.* Fred Grest- You've messed with the rest now mess with the best because there isnt anybody like Fred Grest! *Now the scene fades to black.*
