Date: 2004-12-12 Road To Triumph – Part III: Compassion In utter silence, the doctor stands beside his incredible chemical warhead as he prepares for an attack on the world. The doctor’s face contorts into a wicked, devious smile. He looks down the rocket at all its splendour and impressiveness. The moment has arrived, now the evil doctor will get to test out his latest and greatest work. He reaches down to the panel at the base of the rocket. Pulling it open, he reveals the fuelling chamber. Sitting on the counter, looking out at the doctor preparing the rocket for it’s next duck-induced fuelling, is Dave’s trusted bath toy, Ducky. The doctor looks over to Ducky and haunts him with his devious smile. The duck quivers with fear as he knows exactly what is about to happen to him, he heard the conversation earlier as he sat in Dave’s pocket, he knows his death is approaching. The doctor slowly reaches down into his hospital coat’s pocket, past all the pens and pulls out a box of matches. If the duck could break out in a cold sweat, right now it would be. The doctor slowly pulls open the box ready to light the rocket. “Vhat…?!” the doctor suddenly cries out, “Zere ist no matches?! Vhat dumb luck!” If it weren’t an inanimate object, Ducky would breath a heavy sigh of relief. He was safe. The doctor cannot light the chamber, so the duck cannot burn. The doctor wanders back across the room to the duck to wag his finger at him violently. “You vill get your roasting, duck! I just need to go get some more matches!” He walks across the room to where Dave and Wendy are being held captive, looks up to the nurse who looks about ready to tear the two of them apart and his evil smile slowly returns. “I am off to get some matches… Nurse Putrid… keep our guests… company…” -----=====|=====----- And you know I will… I’ll keep these two fuck’s company, and so will my trusty bullwhip… Walking slowly over to the two of them, I shoot a look at Little Miss Beautiful. She trembles in fear of what I’m about to do to her. This is going to be so sweet, hearing the sound of that little runt screaming his little lungs out with tears down his face as I whip the skin from his slut of a girlfriend. I turn to widdle Wendy, taking my whip and loosen the grip letting it extend to the floor. “Don’t you fucking dare!” screamed Dave beside me. This will be so great, I’m getting entertained already and I haven’t even started yet. “Get away from her, bitch! You touch her I swear I’ll fucking kill you!” Does this guy ever shut up? I turn to the guy, pull the cigarette out of my mouth blowing a smoke cloud into his face and hissing a chuckle I retort, “I’d like to see you try, honey…” I grip the leather tightly, rotate my shoulder and arm slowly, getting the whip moving ready for the big lashing. “Yeah, you’re so fucking tough aren’t you? Whipping a poor, little woman tied up and completely defenceless.” “You need to learn to shut up, you know that?” “Yeah, well you need to learn to lose weight, just look at you!” I’m not I’m just big boned! That’s it. I’m going to teach this little fuck a lesson. Nobody calls me fat! Plus, this guy is irritating me now. I bring back my whip ready to send it forward and send this woman to an inch of her pretty little life. “You fat fuck!” CRACK! The whip sent forth, but not where I intended it. That last-second scream of abuse sent me over the edge, I no longer want to whip this woman. I’ve changed my mind. The whip sent with as much force as I could send collided with the chest of ‘Exceptional’ Dave Hurst. He made no sound, gritting his teeth and bearing the front of my abuse. Hmm… that’s strange. Usually this little wuss screams like a schoolgirl. He’s not actually acting like a man now and bearing my abuse? I tighten my grip once more and swing my arm forward in a quick motion. CRACK! He still makes no noise, no screams, not even a whimper, instead he looks at me with the most intimidating look I’ve ever seen. I never realised this before, but damn he looks good. I don’t know if it’s the unbelievable anger on his face. I don’t know if it’s the consistent threats that he’s going to do irreparable damage to me, but this man is gorgeous. CRACK! He gets yet another lashing, this time across his upper arm. And all the time I do this he gets more and more angry, and more and more… irresistible. His muscles convulse with each and every swing of my whip, the tight, hard, rippled body. How did I not know until recently that he looked so good? He’s on the thin side, but he’s as hard as a rock. CRACK! Right across the face, a massive scar-like slash appears diagonally across his face, from the side of his forehead across his nose and down past his chin. But all the time he stayed still, his eyes never leaving mine, the pure hatred across his face. He can’t take much more punishment. I’m going to break this man. CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! Frustrated, I send four more completely random lashings in his direction, each and every one taken by him and he still looks right at me as if possessed, as if in another world. He gazes at me with burning rage. This man is going to scream, I’m going to make sure of it. CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! He still bears the pain! I don’t believe this! “WHY DON’T YOU SCREAM…? WHY…?” I scream at the top of my lungs, my frustration taking me over. I need to do something. I need to do something now. This man needs to pay. Rushing over across the room, I grab the nearest and heaviest object I could find, the doctor’s chair. I fold up his steel chair into a nice rectangle, easy to carry, easy to wield and easy to swing with as much force as I can. I hold the chair above my head, and then rush back towards Dave running at full pace as soon as I get to him I swing forward with everything I have and the steel seat section of the chair impacts straight over his crown. SMACK! The adrenaline flowing through my veins, my heart beating as much as it has done for as long as I can remember, I break down into tears. I collapse down onto his legs, my arms wrap around him and my head on his shoulder as streams of tears flow down my cheeks. For the very first moment in my life, I feel benevolence. I feel compassion for this man. I want this man. I want to comfort him, to protect him, to heal him. But all I’ve managed to do, all this time, is send whip after whip of attacks, tearing the skin from his body, and then this. “What the hell did you do that for?” cries an extremely concerned Wendy from the other side of the room. She should be grateful, at least she has this man for herself. At least she has the one thing I can’t have. “I’m sorry,” I yell back her way, a loud shriek escaping my lungs, “it was an accident! I lost it!” “It didn’t look like an accident to me!” she screams even louder, tears now streaming down her face, her head bowed and shaking from side to side. I can’t take it any more, I can’t help the way I feel for this man. Pushing his head up and resting it against my chest, smothering him with wet kisses and salty tears. Why can’t you just love me!? -----=====|=====----- Ow…. An intense throbbing pain radiates through my head. I slowly flicker my eyes open, adjusting slowly to the light. As I start to make out the shapes around me, I see in front of me scraggly blonde hair, a chubby pasty face with boils protruding from all over the place, and a look of sheer worry across her face. Then she starts to notice me coming back to consciousness and her look suddenly changes to that of relief and joy… I thought this was the bitch that wanted to whip me to death just now? “I’m so sorry,” she says, tears forming around the already sore rings of her eyes. “I never meant to hurt you this way. I just want you to be okay…” “Dave’s okay…?” asks Wendy from the other side of the room. The nurse gives a harrowing look her way. “You stay out of this, whore!” she snarls. What the hell went on while I was unconscious? This woman has had a nervous break down or something. Why is she suddenly feeling so guilty? Why does she want Wendy to stay out of this? I don’t get it! “The thing is… I… I…” she gets off of the chair and shakes her head in denial. I don’t know what she wanted to say, but it seems to be eating her up inside. She wanders around the room in a circle, breathing really heavily and working herself into frenzy. Then she walks over to me, with her hands around my neck and brings her face right up to mine. “Okay, fine! If you must know… I’m… in love with you.” Her face contorts into a really ugly cringe. She looks like a bulldog chewing a wasp; it’s hideous. So she’s in love with me? I heard that when girls are really mean to you it means they have feelings for you, but this takes the piss! And now she’s burst into tears again. Aw mate, this sucks. How am I supposed to break this gently to her that I am not interested and have a girlfriend already anyway without her smacking the shit out of me again, or attacking Wendy? That’s it. I’ll have her set me free first, then I’ll free Wendy, grab Ducky and we can find a way of escaping. “Just tell me… Dave… please… I beg of you!” The nurse pleads with me, “Just tell me how I can make you love me?” Make me love her? You don’t make someone love you! “Well… you could start by getting me out of these ropes and off of this chair.” I inform her, as much as I’m deceiving her into setting me free, and I do feel pretty guilty for doing it, but she did a few moments ago whip me what seemed like an endless amount of times before running at me and smacking me over the head with a steel chair. “Okay, my love,” she says utterly infatuated, I don’t know how I managed to get so irresistible all of a sudden, but she’s going to let me free so I shouldn’t complain. She walks behind me and tugs at the knot, unfolding it and loosening the rope so that I can wriggle it to the floor around the chair and step out of it. I walk across the room to behind Wendy and instantly start untying her, as she starts to shuffle the rope from her body, the nurse stops completely bemused by what’s happening. “What are you doing, Dave?” she says exasperated. “I’m letting Wendy go.” I tell her, giving an obvious answer to an obvious question. “I’m afraid I can’t let you do that Dave,” she says as I look up and see the worst possible object I could see in this situation. She stands there, shaking tremendously in a nervous wreck, wanting desperately to do something to stop me from running off with Wendy. And to make matters much worse… …She has a gun in her hands… TO BE CONTINUED…
