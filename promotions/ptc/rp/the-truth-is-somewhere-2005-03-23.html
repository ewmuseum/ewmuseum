The Truth is... Somewhere...

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-23 When we last saw dorky goth kid/wrestling reject Benny Bloodsuck, he had been blinded by the EVIL BLUE MIST~ and kicked in the nads by Tsunami Oni, the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. While this would be uncomfortable at any time, it’s even worse on the edge of a roof. So Benny Bloodsuck was sent plummeting to his untimely, yet not entirely unexpected, death. When Benny’s body hit the Gremlin parked on the street, he wasn’t surprised that everything went black. It’s what happened next that he wasn’t expecting. Benny Bloodsuck opens his eyes and sees… rock. Lots and lots of rock. Slowly Benny Bloodsuck sits up. Pretty much every cell in his body is in pain. Benny groans and looks around. He seems to be in a cavern, dark and gloomy. Benny takes a deep breath and immediately starts hacking. The air is hot and smoky, like he was French kissing a tailpipe. “Where the hell am I?” asks Benny Bloodsuck to himself. “Got it on this first try!” says a deep and booming voice from behind him. Benny Bloodsuck spins around to look at the speaker. His draw drops and his eyes nearly pop out of his skull. There, sitting on a throne of skull is a massive red skinned man with huge horns jutting from his forehead, eyes burning with the pain of countless lost souls. And he has a pointy tail. The fiend grins, well, fiendishly at the pale, quivering figure on the cavern floor. Benny Bloodsuck immediately begins bowing before the throne of bones. “My lord Satan!” says Benny. “I am your most humble servant, O dark master!” “Fuck that shit,” Satan replies. “What would I want with you? Why is it that only scrawny yutzes like you kneel before me? What happened to the chicks? Those gals in Salem, they were the bomb! Those Puritan dresses hid some bangin’ bods! These days... nothin.’ All the lonely women now worship at the alter of the one called Oprah. How can I compete with her? I don’t have cars to give away. Halle Barry never shows up in Hell to chat. And I haven’t read a book since the Necronomicron. It’s hopeless.” Satan lets out a big sigh and stares off wistfully. “Uhhh…” says Benny timidly. “So what happens to me?” “You?” says Satan, suddenly turning to look at Benny Bloodsuck. “You burn in the Lake of Fire for all eternity. Two imps fly up behinds Benny and each grabs an arm. “No!” screams Benny. “Why, my dark lord?” “You took hostages and threatened to kill them, dumb ass,” Satan answers coldly. “And you masturbated. A lot. How you avoided friction burn, I have no idea. Take him away, boys.” The imps drag Benny Bloodsuck away kicking and screaming. “This isn’t fair!” Benny howls. “First I get laughed at by every wresting fed around! Then I get kicked off of a roof by that asshole Oni! And now this!” Satan perks up when Benny Bloodsuck says the name “Oni.” “Wait!” Satan exclaims. The imps stop dragging Benny away. “Did you say Oni? As in Tsunami Oni?” “Y-y-yes, sir,” says a fearful Benny Bloodsuck. “He’s the one that kicked me off that roof. I thought you knew that. Don’t you see everything?” “No, that’s the other guy,” Satan replied. “I only see the sins in people. Somewhat limited, but it does makes trips to Vegas fun. But enough about me, I want to talk about Tsunami Oni. How do you feel about this so-called “Devil from the Deep Blue Sea,” Benny?” “I, uh, hate him,” Benny answers timidly. “He made fun of me. And then he killed me.” “Excellent!” exclaims Satan. “I hate him too!” “Why is that, O ye dark master?” asks a relived Benny. He tries bowing down but the imps still have his arms. “Is it because he stole your gimmick?” “No, not that,” says Satan. “This goes back to when he was dead.” “Tsunami Oni was dead?” “It didn’t last long,” Satan explains. “But while he was dead, Tsunami Oni was sent to my realm. At the trial for his very soul, he cleerly argued his away out of charge after charge. Then I finally nailed his ass with deviant sex acts out of wedlock!” “Y-you raped him?” stammers Benny Bloodsuck, his butt cheeks tightening on reflex. “No!” yells Satan. “I meant that kinky little number he has topside! You have no idea the sick things those two have done to each other.” “You could tell me,” says a hopeful Benny. The imps nod rapidly in agreement. “Forget it, kid,” says Satan. “Let’s just the last time Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu went to Disneyland, they gave “Mister Toad’s Wild Ride” a whole new meaning. Anyhoo, I had Tsunami Oni dead to rights. But then he had a secret weapon.” “That EVIL BLUE MIST~ stuff?” “No, Buddhism,” says Satan. “Tsunami Oni isn’t Christian so he’s out of my jurisdiction. He made me look like a big red schmuck! I hate him!” “I hate him too!” yells Benny Bloodsuck. “I hate him more than anyone on earth!” “Me too!” shouts Satan, flames shooting from his eyes. “More than anyone on… earth…” He suddenly turns thought and begins stroking his beard. Suddenly there’s a “PING~!” and a fiendish light bulb appears over the head of the Prince of Lies. “Benny!” a grinning Satan exclaims. “You gave me a great idea! I will finally have my revenge on Tsunami Oni!” Satan throws his head back and cackles. “NYAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” Still held by the imps, Benny Bloodsuck joins him. “NYAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” Benny stops when he sees Satan glaring at him. “Shut up, kid. Only I get to do that.” “Sorry, O dark lord.” Meanwhile, back in the land of the living, Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu are finally getting to order dinner at Planet Bollywood. “I’ll have the tandoori chicken,” Oni tells the waiter. “And I’ll have the mushroom saag.” orders Shiatsu. The waiter takes their menus and walks away. Shiatsu gives Tsunami Oni a small smile. “Well, now that your back, I got something to show you,” says Shiatsu. “Oh, really?” Tsunami Oni raises an eyebrow. Shiatsu reaches under the table and pulls out a large manila envelope. “Darling,” says Oni. “If these are those pictures of you and Paris Hilton, they’re already all over the internet.” “Duh,” says Shiatsu. “Who do you think put ‘em there, sweetie? Nah, this is about you. It’s 411 on your competition for Seven of the Best.” Shiatsu opens the envelopes and takes out breakdowns and images of thirteen other competitors. “I was thinking we could discuss the other wrestlers and then make fun of them,” she says. “Sounds good to me. It’ll kill time before our food gets here.” “Well, let’s start with the one you’ll be facing one-on-one.” Shiatsu hands Tsunami Oni an 8x10 glossy of Karina Wolfenden. “Why is there lipstick all over this?” asks Oni. “Oh, I dunno,” says Shiatsu coyly. Tsunami Oni is giving her a look. “Hey! She’s hot! And she has a move called the Teardrop Explodes! How freakin’ cool is that?” “If you could be serious for a second, you’d realize Karina is considered a favorite to win this event,” Oni points out “She was devious enough to lead Pájaro de Presa on, then kick him in the face.” “The Trix Rabbit could outsmart Pájaro, sweetie. But I don’t think Karina’ll be in as good a shape as she usually is,” says Shiatsu. “The grapevine says K-Wolf and Tessa Windsor broke. And whenever a woman is involved in a break-up, she heads straight to the Ben and Jerry’s.” “I don’t think it’s that kind of break-up,” says Oni. “They were a tag team, not lovers.” “Yeah, whatever,” says Shiatsu. She looks at the PRIME logo on Karina Wolfeneden’s print-out. “PRIME… Number One by Definition.” “You know,” observes Oni. “Prime also means a number that can only be divided by itself and one. So PRIME could be number seventeen, or twenty-three, or ninety-seven, or…” “I get it, sweetie,” Shiatsu cuts in. “Let’s move on. This is the entry from core Wrestling. Although they should change their name to snore Wrestling. Whenever I can’t sleep, I just pop in a tape of one of their shows. Well, this is the guy they’re sending.” She slides the picture of Tim Shipley across the table to Tsunami Oni. “Didn’t he deliver a pizza to our place once?” asks Oni as he glances at the photo. “Maybe,” replies Shiatsu. “He’s working is way through college as a wrestler. And he’s really fond of one of his professors. Like, really fond. I mean, sure, I schtupped a few teachers to get out of tests, but this is just plain creepy.” Shiatsu takes out the next breakdown. “Oh, here’s something you might find interesting, sweetie. That Shadow chick isn’t representing WMW anymore.” “Really?” says Oni. “I was looking forward to a rematch with her. So who is the competitor from Wrestling Mid-West?” “You know,” says Shiatsu. “Whenever I hear that name I imagine a fourteen year old girl in Kentucky trying to keep her cousin from dry-humping her. But back to Seven of the Best. Shadow’s place is being taken by AWS Man, who also is known as Bill.” “Bill?” Oni says. “What kind of nickname is that? It’s not nearly as cool as “Devil from the Deep Blue Sea” or “Vivaldi of Violence.”” “Well, don’t underestimate him,” Shiatsu warns. “He’s had shots at the PTC Global title before.” “And has he ever won it?” asks Oni. “Actually, no,” Shiatsu admits. “So go ahead and underestimate him, sweetie. Hmmm… let’s see who we got from XUW…” “Where?” “Xtreme Ultimo Wrestling. They must be cool because they have an X in their name!” Shiatsu rolls her eyes makes the universal male masturbation gesture, which looks so cool when a hot chick does it. “I spent some time in Mexico and I picked up a lot of Spanish,” observes Oni. “And “ultimo” means “last.” What kind of wrestler would want to belong to an organization that declares itself extremely last?” “His name is woof woof.” “Well, that does make sense,” Oni admits. “What about 4WW? I’m hoping they brought back Cap’n Kenny Cutthroat.” Shiatsu checks her information. “Dammit!” she says. “It’s some guy named Syckle. I was hoping for some honey like Caitlin Fairchild or Luscious Lady Luck. Mmmm… Triple L…” “You know,” says Oni. “This is a tournament to crown the next Global contender, not to provide you with live softcore porn.” “Hey, do I ridicule the fact you spend hours on end online checking out bondage sites?” Shiatsu retorts. “Uh, that’s you, darling,” Oni points out. “Oh yeah,” admits Shiatsu. “Hey, if this yutz from 4WW liked both chicks and dudes would he be bi-Syckle?” She gives Tsunami Oni a shit-eating grin. He tries not to smile, but can’t help it. “That was so bad,” he says, shaking his head. “Just read the next one.” “OK, let’s see,” says Shiatsu as she checks her envelope. She takes out another print out and looks at Tsunami Oni to hand it to him. Her jaw drops and she looks shocked. “What’s wrong?” asks Tsunami Oni. “Someone wearing your dress? I’m sure they don’t look as good in it. Or out of it, for that matter.” “Sweetie! You’re glowing!” Tsunami Oni looks down at his body. Sure enough, there is an aura of light around him. He feels a strange sensation spreading through his body, like a million pinpricks all over his skin. Then the glow intensifies into a flash of light! When it fades, Tsunami Oni is gone! “Oh damn,” says Shiatsu. “He has the keys to the penthouse.” Meanwhile, Tsunami Oni is slowly getting his vision back. The flash of light hand temporarily blinded him, but eventually the spots faded away. But before he see anything, his ears noticed the sounds of… “John Tesh?” asks Oni. “Am I in Hell again?” “Do the hymns of Tesh displease you, Chosen One?” says a high-pitched voice with an accent that even a world traveler like Tsunami Oni can’t place. The Vivaldi of Violence turns to the voice and only makes out a blurred from. Slowly it comes into focus. The being before him is dressed in a very stylish navy jacket, khaki pants, white shirt, and striped tie. He also has an enormous hairless gray head with huge black eyes and no nose. “Wow!” gasps Oni. “Either I’ve been abducted by aliens or I’m at the Michael Jackson trial!” The alien points an accusing finger at Tsunami Oni. “You are not the Chosen One!” squeaks the alien. “Never said I was,” replies Oni. He looks around and sees other aliens, also dressed very well, some formally, others more casual. And all are looking at the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. The room they are in has hardwood floors and is lit with what appear to be antique lamps. Tsunami Oni notices he is not on some metal examination table like you would expect with aliens, but on a lovely divan with floral print cushions. An alien in a very tasteful blouse and peasant skirt is holding a wicker tray with a piece of quiche, a glass of sparkling water, and a tulip in a thin vase. Tsunami Oni turns back to the alien that spoke to him. “Who are you people?” he asks. “Where am I?” “I am Commander Sheec of the starship “Potpourri,” which you are on. These are my people, the Savwa’faar. And you…” the alien again points a finger at Tsunami Oni. “You are not the Chosen One!” “I never said I was,” Oni retorts. “Who is this Chosen Oni you keep talking about?” Some of the aliens move aside so a section of the wall can be seen by Tsunami Oni. Hanging there, in a lovely white gold frame, is a portrait of none other than… “Martha Stewart?” “So you know of the Chosen One!” says Commander Sheec. “She is the one that has brought enlightenment to our people!” “How’d she do that?” asks Oni. “Once we were a technologically advance race, but one devoid of taste whatsoever,” Sheec explains. “We were content to go around naked, fly the clunkiest looking ships, and kidnap humans from trailer parks for the sole purpose of inserting random objects in their rectal cavities. But inside, we knew our souls needed to be filled, not the orifices of these poor sops. Then one day we intercepted a transmission from your world. There she was, the Chosen One. She knew what was missing in the lives of my people and she remedied that. The Chosen One taught us so much… how to make our own scarves and mittens, recipes for delicious no-bake cookies, and the proper way to write a thank you card. All that the Savwa’faar are, we one to the one you call Martha Stewart!” “So you came to Earth to get autographs?” asks Oni. “We are here on a crusade!” declares Commander Sheec. “We learned of your people imprisoning the Chosen One! We came to free her from that torment!” “Uh, she’s been out of prison for a few weeks now,” Oni says. “We know that!” snaps Commander Sheec. “Our world is very far away. By the time the “Potpourri” reached your world, her incarceration was over.” “So what are you still doing here?” “We observed your planet and we were shocked. Shocked! How could such a tasteless world create the Chosen One?” “Hey!” says Oni defensively. “Earth has plenty of style! I live in San Francisco!” “Do you deny your world created the monstrosity known as the mullet?” snaps Commander Sheec. “Or that your people leave their cell phones on in theaters and libraries? Or that polo shirts are now considered business attire? Or the construction of those wooden objects humans stick in their yards that look like someone bending over? Or, worst of all, that hideous spectacle called professional wrestling?” “OK, you had me at the mullet,” admits Oni. “So let me guess, you planned on beaming good ol’ Martha up to your ship and taking her away from her backwater planet but somehow got me instead, right?” “Close, human,” says Sheec. “We are planning on taking the Chosen One back to the Savw,faar homeworld. But not before we give the Earth an… Extreme Makeover!!!” For some reason those last two words echo through the ship. “So that would mean what?” asks Oni. “Sending down an alien Fab Five? Extraterrestrial Eye for the Straight Guy?” “No, human,” answers Commander Sheec. “Your planet is so devoid of good taste that we have no choice but to demolish it and construct a new planet!” “Demolish the Earth?” exclaims Oni. “But what of all the lifeforms on it?” “The winds of style are harsh, but they blow true,” states Commander Sheec. “Now hold still while I activate the transport divan and send you back to your world. “You can’t do that!” yells Oni as he jumps out of the divan. “Why not?” “Because… because…” says Oni, trying to think f something. “Because there’s a new “Desperate Houswives” on Sunday! Do you know how long it’s been since we’ve had one with all the awards shows and TV movies? These last few weeks have been repeats! But we finally have a new one! C’mon, guys… I know you must be Bree fans!” The Savwa’faar turn to each other, chanting “Bree, Bree” in their odd alien voices. They are silenced when Commander Sheec raises his hand. “I am sorry, but some sacrifices have to be made,” he says. “Now return to the divan or I shall be forced to be unpleasant.” “What? You won’t let me have a tomato from your garden?” cracks a smirking Tsunami Oni. “No, I’ll simply disintegrate you,” states Commander Sheec while drawing an ominous looking pistol from a handmade leather holster. “And I have rose bushes, not tomato plants.” “Even though Commander Sheec is an alien life form, Tsunami Oni can tell he means business. There’s no way he can get to Sheec before he can fire that raygun. Although surrender isn’t normally part of his vocabulary, Oni realizes he would much rather die with Shiatsu than alone miles above the Earth. He slowly backs to the divan, eyes on Commander Sheec, looking for any possible opening. And then it hits him. “Hey!” says Oni. “You have no right to the judge the Earth on matters of taste and style!” “How can you say that, human?” Sheec replies. “The Savwa’faar have mastered the teaching of the Chosen One!” “Just look at you, Sheec,” says Oni. “Your tie and handkerchief match.” “So? They are supposed to match.” “No, they are supposed to compliment each other, not match,” Oni retorts. All the Savwa’faar murmur amongst themselves. Commander Sheec doesn’t look as sure of himself as he was before. “Are… are you positive about this?” he asks. “Yes, I am,” Oni answers. Commander Sheec slowly holsters his raygun. “I am not sure if I believe you, human, states Sheec. “But to be sure we will return to our homeworld on consults the sacred scriptures of Martha Stewart Living. Until we can be sure of our good taste and style, we can not dare to face the Chosen One Your world is spared for now, human. Return knowing your tacky race will live for at least a little while longer.” “Thank you, Commander,” says Oni. “Do not thank me, human,” Sheec replies. “Just tell your people belly button piercings stopped being cool two seconds after Alicia Silverstone got one in that Aerosmith video.” “I will, Commander.” That said, Tsunami Oni has no trouble sitting back in the transport divan. He feels the same sensation as before, signaling his imminent return to Earth and Shiatsu. All of Hell trembles with his rage “NO~!” says a heavenly voice from above. Benny Bloodsuck looks confused. “What happened, O dark master?” he asks. “It all went wrong!” snaps Satan, smoke coming out of his horns like steam from a tea kettle. “Tsunami Oni was supposed to be abducted by aliens and taken away! That way I wouldn’t have to deal with him on Earth anymore! But those idiotic Savwa’faar sent him back! And he ended up saving the planet!” “Dude, Tsunami Oni saved the planet?” says Benny. “Man, if it wasn’t for him, think of all the future souls you would’ve lost!” Satan glares at Benny Bloodsuck with a malice only he can muster. “Take him away, boys.’ The imps snicker as they drag Benny Bloodsuck kicking and screaming to the Lake of Fire, leaving Satan alone with his own dark thoughts. “Maybe I should start a book club after all…” Back at Planet Bollywood, Shiatsu is quite pleased when Tsunami Oni suddenly appears back in his eat across from her. “Hey! Food’s here!” he says. “So where were you, sweetie?” asks Shiatsu. “Saving the world from alien redecorators,” Oni replies. “Oh, OK,” says Shiatsu. “So you wanna go back to looking over your competition for Seven of the Best or just eat?” “You know, he says. “With all that’s gone on this evening, I was hoping things would end with a bang.” Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu look into each other’s eyes. Then Oni knocks all the plates and silverware off of the table in one sweep. Shiatsu quickly hops onto the table and lays back, Tsunami Oni leaping on top of her. And the rest was shear pornography…
