A Trip to the Doctors

10 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-11-10 Traffic wasn’t too bad today as my wife and I drove down the main street of town making our way to the doctors. I hated this time of year, my annual physical wasn’t something that I looked forward too, I had nothing against being checked out, I just hated going to the doctors. I’m not sure if it was all the waiting, or the fact that another man had his hands all over me, just something about it never set well with me. Don’t get me wrong, I understood the importance of it all, but that didn’t change the fact in a matter of minutes I would have an old man fondling my tentacles and telling me to cough. “Ugh.” I didn’t really mean to make my distaste audible, for fear that it would…. “NOW what is wrong?” …..my wife would hear me. “Nothing dear, just um, had something in my eye and it was annoying me.” I began to rub my left eye to try to verify my story to her, but it wasn’t working. “You liar, your still upset we are going in for your physical aren’t you.” It wasn’t so much a question as it was a statement “If you MUST know yes.” “What is your big problem with going to the doctor, it is important that go in to make sure everything is in working order, what on EARTH could you have a problem with?!” Again the vision of a man in his sixties cupping my balls ran through my head. “I would rather not talk about it.” “Humph.” Was her only response and the rest of the drive was pretty much quiet, TOO quiet if you ask me, pardon the only movie quote. So I reached for the radio and pushed the power button only to hear an advertisement about GTT, with ME in it. I listened closely as I switched lanes and turned onto the street where my doctor was located. “In two days the next round of the most famous wrestling tournament in the world will take place and only on PPV. So far in GTT we have seen wrestlers shed blood, sweat and tears to achieve one of the most precious titles in the wrestling world, the GTT Champion. Among some of the of the matches expected, we will see The Latch Key Kid vs. Karina Wolfenden, Dave Hurst vs. Angelo Deville, Dr. Curiosity vs. Sebastian Moraco and watch first hand as Dave Harley takes on our own Chris Tarrant in what will promise to be a heated battle. That’s right, our own legendary Chris Tarrant has made it to the bracket finals, will he advance even further? Show your support and tune in to find out first hand. Contact your cable company now so you don’t miss a moment of this sports spectacle!! “ “Card subject to change without notice.” I reached up to turn off the radio as we had unfortunately arrived at the doctor’s office. I parked our car and got out, walked around and opened up my wife’s door for her. She thanked me, and we then walked into the small, family doctor office. There were only a few people there, 2 parents with two children who couldn’t have been more then ten years old. I paid them no attention as I walked over to the receptionist and checked in. “The Doctor will be with you in a few moments Mr. Tarrant.” The children who were playing behind be got quiet suddenly as I knew what had just happened. Turning around I saw in an instant they had made their ways from across the waiting room and were only a foot away from me, looking up in awe. “Are you REALLY Tarrant!?” the child asked in awe. Smiling, I reached down and roughed up the boys hair. “Indeed I am, are you a wrestling fan?” “YES!!! I watch you every week, or I did before you entered the tournament thingy, since its been on PPV mom and dad wont let me watch, I told them I would pay for it with my allowance, but they still said no.” I laughed at that and could picture the entire thing, as my own children had done the same thing with me with past events on the TV. The other child finally spoke up. “Mr. Tarrant, can I have your autograph?” Just as the words came out of the child’s mouth a door on the opposite side of the waiting room opened and a nurse walked though. “Chris Tarrant? The doctor will see you now.” I looked back at the child who know I was about to tell him no, but then I looked at my wife, and at the child’s parents, who from there looks didn’t have a lot of money and I felt bad. “I tell you what, you talk to my wife over there, and have your parents do the same, and we will arrange it so you can have backstage passes and ring side seats at the GTT even and you can see me wrestle first hand, then afterwards, get mine and a bunch of others autographs, if your parents say its ok that is, how’s that sound?” All at once they seemed to have forgotten about me as they turned and ran for their parents screaming “could they could they?” I laughed and nodded to my wife, who smiled back as I made my way to the door lead back to yet another small waiting room. I sat down and the nurse took my blood pressure, noted it was a bit high. Took my temperature and asked me a few questions and then told me the doctor would be right in. As she got to the door, she stopped and turned. “Oh, how could I forget, there is a gown behind that screen over there, please stripe down and the doctor will be in in just a few moments.” She closed the door behind her and I turned to look at the changing screen in disgust, but knew that there was no way out of it at this point and changed out of my clothes and into the thin gown that did nothing to help the fact I felt like an idiot wearing a thin piece of cloth. Moments later surprisingly, the doctor walked through the door, clip board in hand. He was about sixty five of so and had been my doctor for many years now. “Ah, hello Chris, here for your physical I see?” “So my wife tells me.” The fact that the only thing that separated my “boys” from him was a piece of cloth that was no thicker then a piece of paper, I found my eyes kept darting nervously to the box of gloves on the wall. “Well your wife was correct, have a seat and we will get started.” The Physical itself went pretty much as expected, tested my reflexes, looked in my ears, mouth and nose, listened to my chest, finally the end came and the words I dreaded above all others was muttered. “Pleas turn your head and cough.” I did so, but to my surprise, he didn’t let go……dear god why wasn’t he letting go!? “Again please?” I did so as fast as I could, anything to get his hands away from there. “Hummm, again please.” OH DEAR GOD WHY IS HE STILL DOING THIS?!” For good measure I coughed about 5 times and finally he let go and took his glove off. Breathing a sigh of relief I looked at the doctor who had a strange look on his face. Not something I want to see after he just got done feeling up my junk, know what I mean? “What’s wrong?” “Well, it feels like you have a bit of swelling down there, I may want to run more tests later. Tell me, have you had any nagging pain in the back, hips, or pelvis?” More tests?! NO!! “Well, a bit I suppose, but at my age, and with what I do, that’s not uncommon.” “Trouble urinating?” Oh dear god, as if I didn’t feel uncomfortable as it was. “Um….well, kind of.” Please god let me be allowed to get dressed and leave now!! “Hummm, I see, have you notice and blood in your urine or semen?” “WHAT?! Gee Doc, I don’t know, let me get my wife in here and I’ll ask her for you……COME ON!!!” “OK OK, fine, I know you hate this, I will ask her later. Go ahead and get dressed, I am going to schedule you for some tests tomorrow up at the hospital so you know, its nothing major, I just want to rule out some things for peace of mind.” “Yeah, yeah fine.” I said as I was already behind the changing screen putting my clothes on. I heard the door shut and finished changing my clothes and made my way back out to the main waiting room, saw my wife getting my appointment information from the nurse and saw that the children and their parents were gone, in seeing the doctor no doubt. My wife turned to me and smiled. “Ready to go?” “God yes.” I responded and began to walk towards the door. “What happened with those children?” “Well, their parents said they could go, I will make the arrangements with [name withheld], I am sure he wont mind if we bring a few extra guests to the event, he never does.” Nodding my head in agreement, I opened the passenger door for Amber then walked over to the drivers side once she was in and began to pull away from the office. “You know, I think I can see why you are so uneasy about going there?” “Oh? Whys that?” “Well...while you were changing, the doctor came out to the waiting room and asked me the weirdest question....” I felt my face instantly get hot and could only imagine the color of red it had turned. In response I heard my wife laugh as she leaned over to kiss me on the cheek. “We can look when we get home.” She said playfully. I noted that my foot was pressing harder on the accelerator and we made it home in record time. Maybe a trip to the doctors wasn’t so bad after all I thought as we pulled into our driveway, let my wife out of the car and carried her into the house, up the stairs in into our bedroom, shutting the door softly behind us. -End-
