Caine… True talent

30 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-30 "Like all things there must have been a beginning…" The video package begins to roll. The fans watch on as referee Brian Daniels continues to ask Caine if he gives up. Caine refuses, despite being red in the face. Gryffin, seeing that Caine won't give up, lifts Caine up all the way into a gorilla press position… The fans buzz as Gryffin holds Caine up, which is not the easiest of tasks. But Caine manages to get away from Gryffin by dropping down behind him and landing on his feet. Before Gryffin can recover, Caine looks to apply an abdominal stretch, but the second he gets a hold of Gryffin, Gryffin turns around with a snap jab to the side of the head. Caine stumbles a great deal and Gryffin now hits the ropes, running full speed at Caine. The attempted clothesline. Caine ducks. Gryffin keeps his momentum going, and bounces off the ropes on the right side of the ring. Gryffin comes charging back looking to hit the clothesline still, but this time Caine side steps with a drop toe hold! Mark Perletta: Gryffin face first onto the mat… and Caine's got him now… An ankle lock! "They tend too have a middle…" Mark Perletta: I'd say Gryffin deserved it! He's underestimating Caine based on one match, a match Logan Caine went toe to toe with Gryffin in. Teretoni checks on Gryffin, asking if he gives up now, but Gryffin says no and immediately starts towards the ropes, looking to break out of the hold. Gryffin gets there within a matter of seconds and Caine breaks the hold and is up to his feet. Gryffin pulls himself up to his feet via the ropes, turning to face Caine with narrowed eyes. He charges at Caine and the two lock into grapple, but violently Gryffin takes Caine out of the grapple and just catches him with a forearm under the chin! Caine's head snaps back and Gryffin kicks him in the stomach. Caine keels over and Gryffin again with a rising forearm shot that sends Caine's head snapping back. Gryffin whips Caine into the ropes and as Caine comes back Gryffin picks him up and sends him down via a back body drop! Caine arches his back up in pain and Gryffin charges with a knee SQUARE in the back of Caine's head. Caine slumps forward and Gryffin looks to Devastation as he pushes Caine all the way down onto the mat and makes the cover yet again. "…and come the end there can only ever be one result" Page kicks out with his legs and is up to his feet… Caine up though… swings with a lariat, but Page ducks it… Page gets Caine around the back… Caine counters… turns to get Page in a waist lock… GERMAN RELEASE SUPLEX!!! Mark Perletta: And that's going to add more damage to the back of Page! Page is down just about center of the ring… Caine moves right up next to him… and for the second time in the match he goes through the motions… locks Page into a regular STF then turns onto his back to elevate Page into the air!! Mark Perletta: And the Inverted STF once again and this time no distraction, no interference… PAGE HAS NOWHERE TO GO! The fans cheer as Page screams bloody murder… and soon after Daniels asks if Page gives up… Page nods his head up and down repeatedly! The cheers increase in volume as Daniels calls for the bell and Caine drops Page off to the side. Jen X.: Here is your winner of the match… Logan Caine!!! "In the ring or outside of it I work hard. In this minute I’ll attempt too shine" Mark Perletta: Again though tap out rules DO NOT apply and really whoever comes out of this, may have their pick of the elimination, and that looks to be Stein. Caine breaks his ankle lock on Marion now as he struggles against Dan Stein's sleeper hold. Stein starts walking backwards now, pulling Caine closer and closer to the ropes. Caine continues to fight and Marion slowly rising to his full vertical base… staggering though and limping on the freshly hurt ankle. Stein is up against the ropes now and all at once he turns his body, now having Caine face the ropes. Stein shoves forward on the sleeper hold, forcing Caine to hang over the top rope… still struggling against the sleeper, but face turning bright red. Marion gets into the mix as the fans are on the edge of their seat, and Marion pushes on Stein, trying to send both guys over… looking to be THE only man in the ring. Stein now feeling himself going over a bit now… lets go of Caine and throws a blind back elbow to Marion. Marion stumbles back and Stein now tries to push Caine the rest of the way over, but Caine flips over and rolls back in under the bottom rope. Stein seems upset and starts to pull Caine up off the mat… but just as he does so, Marion hits the ropes and charges at Stein from behind… HE NAILS Stein with a full on body check and as Stein jolts forward, Caine gets up to his full vertical base… "And whatever else. I’ll have given it my all" (matches quoted from and copyright of True Talent Wrestling)
