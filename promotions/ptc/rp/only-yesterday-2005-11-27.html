Only Yesterday

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 It seems like only yesterday when Rollins thought he could conquer the world. One by one they fell away as step by step his plan unfurled and not a soul in PWA could have expected his star to shine so bold. "Who is this kid?" the critics cried, unsure of how to take the news. All the while Rich Rollins smiled as his arrival met with rave reviews. Yet despite his sudden glowing success, there was but one Rollins sought to impress. It was then his savior came anon; it was He, the Devil’s Don. When was it Rollins fell astray? It seems like only yesterday. Some people thought he felt teh f34r~! In reality he disappeared into a sphere of dismal doubt and loathsome sorrow as his anguish built like Kilimanjaro, for he knew he’d skipped out on his own crusade. Some stars are born, yet others are made. Two years went by without a trace of the Renegade’s stoic and stony face, but his heart yearned for closure to his storied past. To the UWF he’d come at last. In a land where chimps are crowned as kings and a plethora of lewd and silly things occur as if it were the norm, Rich Rollins returned in splendid form. The stage was set for Live and Loud, and to the delight of an elated crowd the Renegade released an atomic bomb: Angelo Deville had come. Then he was gone. Again the Renegade fell astray. It seems like only yesterday. A teacher born, a student bred, a future filled with paths untread. It’s the story you’ve heard a million times about the eldest lion and the challenging youth. Deville was a thing of wrestling lore; Rollins merely needed to find his truth. Can the Devil be killed? Did he possess the skill? Oh the Devil does bleed, but his name’s Deville. Will the teacher get taught, or the student get schooled? No judge will be bought nor call overruled. Do you remember the day that time stood still? It was the day the Renegade met the Deville.
