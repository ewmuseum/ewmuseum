What It Comes Down To (Part One)

28 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-05-28 Some called it a luck of the draw, others called it talent, either way it was undeniably surprising to most to see Tessa Windsor rack up another win and make it to the final round of Primetime Centrals second annual Cruiserweight Tournament. The popular pink princess had never had never been this successful in her entire three year career. Now she had the chance to top it all by capturing the win, defying all those who dared to say her days of glory were long since over. “Here she is, Miss Americaaaaa.” Dee’s pitchy performance, as it would be deemed by America’s favorite European, Simon Cowell , managed to get quite a chuckle out of Tessa, as she leapt into Dee’s arms, hiding none of the overwhelming excitement that rushed through her petite five foot four figure. “I can’t believe it!” Sounding more like a teenage girl who had just met her favorite male celebrity than a mature twenty three year old with two children of her own , Tessa clung on to Dee’s neck to keep from falling backwards should Dee’s arm strength give out. “I’m going to the finals!” Inching her way towards the couch planted smack in the middle of their friend Champ’s living room, Dee collapsed before the couch, so that she could easily shed the added one hundred ten pounds that had latched onto her in the form of Tessa Windsor. “That great dear, but you seem to be forgetting one thing.” “What would that be?” Questioned the petite pink princess as she crashed back first onto the leather cushioning of her temporary throne. “The tapes.” Responded Dee who had to pivot her body a full ninety degrees in order to face Tessa. ”Don’t tell me you’ve given up on finding out who did this to you, after you put Chris and the kids through all this. Not that it matters to me, but I’m sure he wouldn’t be too thrilled….” “I didn’t forget.” Lying wasn’t exactly Tessa’s forte, so it was no surprise to Dee to hear the excuse that followed. “I just figured I’d put it aside for awhile.” With all the recent luck that had fallen her way, Tessa actually had been able to forger about the accusations floating around the media, regarding several scandalous tapes that had surfaced, supposedly starring the lone female finalist of CW2. With little support and a lot to focus on in the upcoming days, now seemed like the perfect time for Tessa to fall apart both mentally and inside the ring, should she chose to focus on tracking down the guilty party, but she had the support of one of her biggest fans and just as great of a friend, Dee, keeping her afloat as the tides started to roughen. “I’m sure.“ Dee responded with an abundant amount of sarcasm. “Well, seeing as through we are on the subject, why don’t you just figure some kind of statement to put out about the tapes, until you can figure out what else you could possibly do.” She clearly wasn’t planning on letting Tessa forget about it, at least not until Tessa finally took some initiative and became a leader just for once, rather than depending on others to bring the success and respect she desired, to her. “It can wait.” Tessa replied as her feet followed her eyes off the couch and towards the door. “I’ve got a match to focus on, it’s much more important. ” Of course when you stick your friend on a plane, have them fly out to Vegas , and then stick them in a house with a man who has no more than a business relationship with either party all for the sake of clearing the air of false rumors, you aren’t going to be able to escape the company of your guest. So, it was no surprise to Tessa then when she turned to look back at Dee, she wasn’t there. Instead, Dee stood at Tessa’s side, vociferously voicing her opinion through the best way to get any point across, rhetorical questioning. “You mean we came all the way out here for nothing? And now we are sticking around here for nothing?” “How was I supposed to know Gaz wasn’t the one responsible?” Tessa shot back with her own defensive questioning, “Besides, we only stayed the extra week because it was pointless to fly all the way home just to have to come all the way back here just for one appearance on Evolution.” “That’s beyond the point.” Dee responded, sans any dramatic movement to add to the tension that had already seemed to grow between the two at this point in time, when it seemed everything was on the line not only in the ring, but out of it as well. “We came here to set things straight, not to wrestle, and certainly not to relax, since I’ve done anything but that!” “And we set one thing straight, it wasn’t Gaz.” Remarked Tessa in true premenstrual fashion, as she led her pouting up the red carpeting covering the stairs, with Dee still following a few feet behind. “I’ve got no clue who else could have possibly gotten the equipment needed to put together such a tape. What I am supposed to do, point fingers at everyone I meet?” “You know that’s not what I’m asking you to do!” Dee hissed back, clearly annoyed at Tessa’s resistance to the problem at hand. “Then what am I supposed to do?” Raising her voice in belief that Dee would get he message to back off, Tessa was quite surprised to see the opposite come true when Dee too started to pull out the claws. “ Obviously not sit back and let everyone else do the work!” Dee shouted, as the gap between the two started to grow mentally, but fade physically. Thankfully their voices had nabbed the attentions of others in the house, particularly the pair belonging to their mutual friend who went by the nickname, Champ. Of course like any guy he would have loved to sit back and watch the two go at it and pretended that it was love and not blood being spilled all over his lavish red carpeting that extended from wall to wall, but considering they both were some kind of asset to him in the end, he could hardly afford it. “Girls girls girls! Would you two settle it?” “She started it!” Was the duet he got in response as both girls pointed not only their index fingers, but a narrowed pair of eyes, at the other. “I don’t care who started it!” Champ remarked as he squeezed his toned six foot five body between the two beauties that stood before him.” Just tell me what the problem is, I’m sure we can work this out.” “She’s going back on her word.” Dee answered for Tessa, who started to float in the opposite direction in hopes to avoid any sort of added pressure Champ might shoot her way. Instead, Champ took the opposite path than either expected, and lent Tessa his ears. “I’m not. I’d rather focus on my match before I focus on those tapes.” “Ah, those tapes.” It helped Tessa none to hear Champ say it in such a way that made it seem that even he had seen the tapes in full, and enjoyed it, especially considering he was just a friend. It only drew more nerves from Tessa., when his interest seemed to be growing on this subject. “We never got to talk about those did we?” “We don’t need to.” Intruded Tessa, as her nerves caused her body temperature to rise and allow perspiration to break through the skin. “I’ll take care of whoever put that crap out later.” "Not like you did in those tapes, I'd hope," Champ replied coolly. Tessa's glare, in response, seemed to throw daggers Champ's way. Avoiding eye-contact, he continued, "The angles were hard to catch, and I'd feel personally violated if I were---in your words---'taken care of' especially after all the effort I put into it." Both Tessa and Dee seemed to be a bit confused by what exactly was going on, each raising an individual brow, but only Tessa doing the questioning. “What exactly do you mean?” “Those tapes that you’re making such a fuss about, those were MY tapes.” Champ responded, as he calmly brushed a strand of his jet black hair behind his ears, expecting Tessa to instantly change her mind about the tapes, and jump into his arms the same way his girlfriend Vivica had anytime he‘d done anything beyond the word great. Instead of an added one hundred ten pounds, Champ found himself facing the deep blue eyes of guilt, as Tessa stood their speechless and let Dee assume the role of big sister. “How could you do such a thing to her?” “Dee, or do you prefer that I call you Dawn just as everyone else does?” Champ piped up, hoping to smooth things over in a business type manner, the way he handled every issue tossed his way. “Just get to the point.” Dee cut off Champs attempt to dance around the topic at hand, though he himself seemed none to intimidated by either girl, considering he towered over both. “Why’d you do this to her?” “I was simply trying to help her out.” It seemed like a pretty pathetic response, but granted help is something Tessa always needed, it made some (though not much) sense. “Help her out, by doing what? Making her out to be wrestling own Paris Hilton?” Dee stabbed back as she attended to her friend in need by offering a supportive embrace. “If you really want to know, then yes.” As much as his answer made have only added salt to the wounds, it was honest, which is all Tessa asked for at this point. “But can you really complain? It seems to have done it‘s job quite well, just look at where she is now.” “It’s not because of you.” Tessa finally spoke up as she tore her head away from Dee’s shoulders to defend herself . “I did it on my own.” “I’m not taking away from your performance in the ring.” He cut in to keep her from jumping to conclusion, one of her poor traits. “I simply cushioned your way to the top by adding a bit more interest to it.” Tessa hardly cared to listen though, and instead left Dee to the final words as she left for the safety of her temporary bedroom inside Champs estate. “I can’t believe you Christian. She didn’t deserve this.” Even Christian, who was all business, seemed a bit moved by the pain caused by his actions, even if Tessa’s tapes and performance in the ring meant more money on his behalf. “I’m sorry, but business is business.” “You had better pull those tapes, or I guarantee you she won’t stick around. ” Dee, otherwise known to the world as Dawn McFarlane warned as she too headed back in the direction of the room she shared with Tessa. “I’ll do it.” Christian promised, but not without a loophole. “Just make sure she goes out there and wins.” “You bet.” Dawn obliged, but not before adding in a loophole of her own. “But so help me god Christian Zenith if you pull another stunt like this, I’ll be gone as well. I promise it.” “That’s fair enough, just make sure she gets the job done.” Christian replied, hoping to cut the sweetly short, as he had plenty of other stuff to deal with aside from making sure his associates looked just as well in the ring as he did. “Deal.”
