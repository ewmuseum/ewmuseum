What is extreme?

27 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What exactly is extreme? Is it simply a word? The word is used in everyday situations all the time; such as if you don’t collect this package the consequences will be extreme. Yet they never really are the repercussions are never usually more than a slap on the wrist, being told don’t let it happen again or your job maybe put on the line. So what is extreme then? Is it the actions and the decisions that we make and take in this life, if a man was walking down the street and for no apparent reason hit another man in the face, would that be considered an extreme action? On the surface maybe but you have to know the true story, things don’t just happen for no reason. The man who was hit could have threatened the other, bullied him at school, hell he may even be having an affair with his wife. To the casual observer though that would remain unknown. So I still haven’t answered the question. What is extreme? If it isn’t a word and it isn’t an action what is the actual embodiment of extreme. Extreme is simply a matter of mind, no more and no less. The mind is what drives us through life controls every single one of our actions. If something smells bad then our brain tells us not to eat it. If something feels too hot then our brain tells us not to touch it and if it looks like something is going to hurt, then our mind tells us not to do it. So what if it were possible to train our mind to overlook these things, to not care whether or not it tasted bad, burnt or hurt then there would be no boundaries, the human body would be free to reach it’s limitless potentials the body could be pushed to its absolute limits before breaking and only then when the body was beaten, shattered and broken would it truly be considered extreme. A cool dark desolate location, a large gust of air breezes through from a shallow opening on the far side of the area, small rays of light penetrate the dark exterior and allows you to see two figures within this cave like formation, one of them is standing pacing back and forth a long quite thin object in his hand, the other is kneeling in front of him his head bowed remaining completely still. The man behind the knelt man stands a few feet to the side of him looking down pensively, then in one fell swoop he draws back the object in his hands and swings it towards the back of the knelt man, the man makes a small grunting noise before slumping forwards holding his back as he writhes around the floor in agony. ???: Did that hurt? Did it make you want to cry out in pain, as my stick met your back what was your first instinct? Was it to scream like a baby looking for its bottle? Was it to keel over and sob, hoping that the pain would pass? Or did you want to turn around and retaliate? Did the blow fill you with so much hate, anger and aggression that the only thing you wanted to do was turn around and make me suffer, take the stick from my hands and pummel me into submission? The figure on the floor writhes around and slowly pulls himself to his knees, still clutching desperately at his back looking up at the figure before him, the standing man extends his hands and helps the fallen figure to his feet before leading him out of the cave into the glistening sunlight, its rays causing both men to raise their arms and cover their faces from the sun. One of the faces we recognise, Moraless standing with a large kendo stick in his right hand, the other is not from around PTC parts, a wrestler all the same but usually stationed in the American Atlantic. James Edwards the lunartic rubs his back gently as Moraless moves into a position before him, the normally ever-happy Edwards grimacing the pain still seeping through his back and muscles. James: Yes I wanted to hurt you, my first instinct was to turn around and beat you like a red headed stepchild, but I couldn’t do it. As soon as the Kendo stick collided with my back I just fell. I had no control over it, you saw me I just fell like a sack of shit. No wonder I’m not doing too well in NWC. Moraless paces up and down in front of Edwards, noticing that his hands have now formed a fist, readying himself for some sort of attack clutching his own Kendo stick just that little bit tighter. Moraless steps forwards once more and begins to walk with James Edwards following a short distance behind him. Moraless: Come now James, I’m sure that your just settling in, I know what it’s like to be in a new place, new situation. In fact that’s part of the reason I called you, I was just shocked you came. James increases his stride to catch up and walk side by side with Moraless, he can’t help but gawk at the numerous scars all of Moraless’ body but decides against asking anything about them after all he was the one that was holding the big stick, if Moraless had taught James one thing it was to stop being so blunt as it lead to a lot of pain. James: Look I’ve been meaning to ask you something; we haven’t talked for a while so why call me now? I know you’ve got things coming up but come on you’ve always been able to take care of yourself. Moraless: You will see in a moment, all will be revealed then. Continuing their trek, the terrain changes leaving the cave behind them, walking along a quite small stony beach, each tiny stone ground away through years of abuse from the sea. Walking up a small path artificially built into the cliff, until they reached the very top. Moraless steps forwards and looks out towards the sea, James scratches his head in confusion and steps forward to be side by side with Moraless once more. James: Err nice view, why are we here exactly? Moraless: This is where it happened, nearly a year ago this is where I tried to end it all, I picked up the knife and rammed it into my stomach on this very spot. I don’t know why it happened not really, I know I was in a bad place mentally, hating the world, hating everyone in it to my very soul, hating you. James steps back and nods slightly, he knows what he is referring to, he’d known for a while, things just always seem to get in the way. Moraless runs his fingers gently across his stomach, the wound feeling as fresh and tender as it did a year ago memories coursing through his mind. Moraless: It was a day just like today, the sights and the sounds were all the same. Listen carefully, the waves crashing on the rocks below us, hypnotic inviting us to join them, like a deadly sirens song. James: I know things were bad then John, but things have changed we’re both doing well now; you are healing better than any of us could have imagined. Moraless is now completely in a world of his own, staring out into the void of sea in front of him. James however is becoming far more frantic, his actions becoming quicker, edgier preparing for something bad to happen. Moraless: Do you know what gets to me the most when I return here? The memories of pain, I didn’t think it would be like that. I thought it would be simple, I would ram the blade into me and fall, thus bringing the end, but it wasn’t like that at all. I didn’t fall straight away as the cold steel ran through me I felt the pain, unbearable pain, the like of which I’ve never felt before. It will be different this time. Moraless reaches into the pocket of his jeans and pulls out a large kitchen knife, which glistens as it catches the sunlight; James’ face turns white with fear as he stands watching Moraless wave the blade slowly and methodically in front of his face. Moraless: This time it will all be different, I’ve risen to a new level, pain is no longer a barrier for me. Unlike you little one this blade will not bring me any pain any grief any sorrow. Moraless clasps the blade in both hands and raises it high above his head, James bolts forwards throwing Moraless to the ground and wrenching the blade from his hand before throwing it over the cliff edge into the blue ocean below. James turns around panting hard his chest heaving up and down before pinning down Moraless and punching him solidly in the face. James: NO, but what about my sorrow? My pain? My grief? Do you know the pain I felt last time I heard about you trying to kill yourself. How do you think I felt when I had to find out through the news on television? HUH? DO YOU EVEN CARE? John, you and me are all that are left, what would our parents say huh? Would they really want us to be doing this, how would they feel if they saw you doing this? James stares straight down at Moraless who is staring straight back up at him, then out of no-where for no apparent reason Moraless smiles, this causes James to back off slightly climbing to a standing position over him as Moraless grins on the ground straight back up at James. Moraless: So what was your reaction then? Did it hurt? Did it want to make you cry out? Sure maybe it did but the thing is you didn’t did you? You reacted, you did what you had to do, that is something I had to make sure of. James stares bewildered at Moraless as he clambers to his feet and turns around once more staring out at the ocean, causing James to jolt slightly just for Moraless to raise his hand stopping James in his tracks. Moraless: James, I was never going to do anything here today, I heard something the other day. That being extreme is simply a state of mind but I think I proved that wrong, to be extreme is not to get rid of all conscious thought, that is just stupidity. When I attempted to end it all last year it was stupid, I didn’t think of the pain that I was causing everyone around me even those who weren’t always around. Moraless turns once more and looks at James who is hanging is head in shame, he walks over to him puts an arm round his shoulder and walks him towards the cliff edge, the two looking out as the sun slowly begins to set on the horizon. Moraless: Being extreme is about taking risks, but not so much so that it causes you… Moraless takes a long pause and stares at James who is looking back at him. Moraless: Or your family grief, I want to thank you for making me realise this James. I know that you are my brother, I know that you love me, I love you too but we both have our own lives and existences, besides we tried the whole tag wrestling thing and that never really worked out did it? James grins giving Moraless a playful dig in the chest as the two turn around and begin walking away from the cliff edge, for the first time in a long time Moraless is smiling, the usually sullen man who enjoys stalking alleyways late at night moaning about how awful the world is. James is smiling too no real change there but his eyes are sparkling, somewhat happier than before you man say. James: I guess not, you always got jealous when I got the pins. Moraless: I did not get jealous I was the one that always did most of the work, you just…ah forget it, water under the bridge and all that. Before I was focusing on the battle royal the wrong way, thinking of how crazy and insane I would have to go in order to compete in it. I know that it’s extreme rules, I know that my chances are slim but I have to do this my way. Extreme is everything you want it to be and I am going to bring it to a whole new level. James nods at Moraless, or John Edwards as he may now be known and firmly grasps his hand clasping it tightly, Moraless nods at James and clasps his hand around his and for a moment the two brothers were reunited before destiny sends them their separate ways, sure they had always known each other was around, they had always known of the others existence, but they had never truly been brothers. So what is extreme? You tell me.
