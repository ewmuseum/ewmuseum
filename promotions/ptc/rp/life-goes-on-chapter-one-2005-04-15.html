Life Goes On: Chapter One

15 April 2005

Date: 2005-04-15 Huff, huff, huff… shit… damn it… fuck! The path pushed on further, intertwining through the urban labyrinth of nocturnal Tokyo. Miles of street rushed from underneath his treaded soles, and his lungs were ready to collapse from overuse, overuse and nicotine. Despite his cardiovascular handicap he actually had no choice in the matter. Becoming one of many residing in a society of cowardice and compliance, he fled from his fears. Terror and testosterone mixed a strong, bitter dry taste in his mouth, film clinging to the roof of his mouth pushed to the back of his throat as he gulped between breaths. His wide brown eyes fluttered from the forward to backward, searching for any sight of the hunter. He was alone on that street, surrounded by the faceless masses of nightlife Shinjuku. They parted around the youth much like a running river flowing around a jutting stone. Darting in and out of the crowd, he tried to pinpoint the location of that monster before it got too close. Fuck! Where is it?? That thing’s gotta be around here, watching me… waiting for me to slip up! Then my ass is toast!! Where… are… you?? He wasn’t satisfied for his safety after surveying the streets, and bolted through the crowd again. In his panic shopping bags were knocked from hands, held hands broken, and people knocked to the ground like unbalanced pins. Even though the phrase declared there were safety in numbers, he felt that the… thing… that hunted for him could strike anytime and anywhere, no matter if he were alone or surrounded by the entire population of Tokyo. It would find a way… it always did. A young child was knocked from her mother’s side, falling to the pavement with a yelp. Stopping, he turned around anxiously looking down at the girl. The mother turned towards him angrily, yanking her child up by her arm roughly and brushing off her yellow dress. “Watch where you’re going!” she snapped, shuffling her daughter off. He apologized with a short bow before turning back around. He barely had time to consciously realize his position before he was yanked off his feet by the collar of his red windbreaker. With the ground leaving him he flew through the air, landing hard on his back groaning in pain. He looked up through winced eyes at the culprit. In his flight from one menace he found another. “Yo, keep ya fuckin’ distance, asshole!” A trio of rough looking punks in black leather jackets, ripped or torn dingy blue jeans, and assorted T-shirts stood in front of him, the culprit for his unscheduled flight being an angry looking youngster with flaring eyes and wild brown hair rising high like a straight haired afro. “Ya nearly knocked my babe off’a tha sidewalk. You better keep better manners about you, transfer!” The Raging Tyrant Kurosawa RYO He could only stare up at the muscled captain of the weightlifting club jaw askew. Tonight was a plethora of problems and with him running into the LEADER of the Shubultz Cuts gang, the danger level would erupt. On RYO’s arm was a tall girl, an inch taller than RYO but with the same thuggish glare in her eye. In some lights she could be considered attractive, but her bad attitude kept her on the low end of the meter yet made her top notch for a troublemaking ruffian like Kurosawa. If RYO ever had a twin sister, the deep voiced girl on his arm would be his female doppelganger. She looked down her nose at the teen, eyes narrowing as her annoyance grew. “You’ve only been here so long and you’re already looking to die, transfer. You must be CURSED, huh?” Violent Amazon Hashimoto Kyoko He glanced from Ryo to Kyoko, and then looked past them to the other two. They were all paired off boy-girl, a member of Shubultz Cuts with a member of Hashimoto’s clique. He had shifted attention in the wrong place, as he found Kyoko’s boot in his face kicking him flat on his back. They all laughed as they walked around him, one of the boys punting him in the ribs as he walked by. “Put that in ya notes, bitch!!” Instinct coiled him into a fetal position, protecting and cradling his inner bones aching and sore. But he couldn’t let himself be brought down by the likes of that street trash. He had a much greater problem on his heels. He had to get up, he had to keep moving, and he had to keep alive. Fuck those guys… I don’t have time for them! They only slowed me down! Shit! It’s probably caught ground because of them! Damn punks! Focus; just remember… you have to stay alive. You have to keep alive so you can warn— He left his thoughts as he found himself in the intersection, nearly run over by a deliveryman on bike. His insults jumped out his mouth and into his ears, but were lost in urgent thoughts floating around in the youth’s head. He finally came to the conclusion that he couldn’t keep civility and caution as bedfellows in a time of crisis. He’d have to become selfish and think about himself long enough to think further. His feet propelled him on a few more blocks until his eyes yanked his awareness to a shady side alley. He slid in and pressed himself against the wall, chest pumping air through a revolving door. Short dark bangs were matted to his brow slick with sweat, but style and appearance weren’t even on the back burner. He just didn’t want to appear dead. Hunching over, his hands pressed through his jeans to brace himself on his knees. He could see his white tee was sheer with perspiration, underneath his body dewed over. He checked the opening of the alley, finding the people walking past the narrow opening without even acknowledging it. Checking the other end, he found a dumpster placed against a chain link fence. For now, he had a place to catch his breath, regroup with his thoughts. What have I done to have this devil chase me? I thought everything was closed! And then THIS! No… I won’t let my work be in vain! Not like this… I won’t let it end like this! Title: Life Goes On Chapter One: The End of the Beginning of the End -=-=-=-=-=- •ΞΘΞ• -=-=-=-=-=- ---My name is Hayabusa Tajima, and my fate is sealed. I know that may seem rather morbid to you, but it’s only a proclamation of my destiny. You see, it seems like I’m the only person that can save the world from annihilation. Wait—I know it sounds like bullshit coming from a 17 year old high school kid that he’s going to prevent an oncoming apocalypse from ever occurring, but yes, I will. Okay, you can stop laughing now. Seriously, cut it out. STOP! Ahem… sorry, didn’t mean to yell at you. I just hate to be patronized. What I say is very serious, and if you take what I’m about to tell you as a joke, well then I’m going to be the last comic standing when the world’s ending. If you believe me and know that what I’m saying is truth, then I’m glad. As unreal and bogus as all of this will sound, I assure you that it’s all fact, not fiction, friend. Let me start from the beginning. These students walked the same path every day for months. They had learned to gather at certain spots so they could chat with each other on the way, discussing how hard the homework was or whom which student was madly in love with, or which teacher was the absolute worst. It was their little social time before class, and their method of unwinding after the final bell had rung for the day. So when something different is found on their path, it hardly goes without notice. A group of four students stood at a street corner watching a lone boy stand waiting at the train tracks. The train whizzed by him, causing his dark bangs to flutter up from the gust. The students were about half a mile back, but still kept the teen in their sights. They were keeping their distance for some reason; what that reason was, none of them had deciphered yet. The only male of the quartet peered over the girls’ heads to scrutiny the stranger. “I can’t see his crest from here, but that looks like our uniform from the back.” “You know how many schools have black uniforms around here?” said a shorthaired girl with a black and silver soccer ball under her arm. The boy cut his eyes at her before looking back up at the student ahead of them. Next to the soccer player was a shorter blonde with a slight tan and loud makeup. Her rainbow assorted bracelets jingled as she raised her hands and placed them behind her head. “How long are we gonna follow him anyway?” she whined in a high pitched voice. “This is just BOR-ING!” “I feel like we’re tailing a criminal back to his hideout,” said a slightly taller girl with black-rimmed spectacles on, “amateurishly, at best.” “Yeah, why ARE we back here waiting anyway?” the soccer player glared up at the boy, dropping her ball and stomping on it while she folded her arms. “I wonder whose bright idea that was!” The boy looked up at the sky, hand becoming a visor over his eyes. “Man, it looks like it might rain.” “I’m gonna talk to him! You guys wait here!” The blonde ran to catch up to the stranger, making the boy reach out to fail at stopping her. The teen waiting at the tracks was digging into his pockets looking for something to light the cigarette in his mouth when he looked over his shoulder at the approaching girl. The cigarette dangled in his mouth as he watched her wave her arm wildly, yelling a high ‘HI’ over the sound of the passing train and warning chimes. She bowed and looked up with a huge friendly smile on her face. “HI! MY NAME’S KAWAII MICHIKO!! WHAT’S YOUR NAME?” The Stylish Sweetheart Kawaii Michiko He stared at her with a blank expression, muttering something lost under the engines. She looked at the school crest on his chest and she clasped her hands together happily. “OH YOU GO TO SHINYO TOO? THAT’S WHERE ME AND MY FRIENDS GO! I WONDER IF WE’LL BE IN THE SAME CLASS!!” Finally the train passed, and the silence reminded him that he was looking for his lighter. He finally found it hidden inside his blazer’s inner pocket. He held the nozzle of the black lighter under his cigarette and was prepared to ignite when… “HEY YOU SMOKE? WOW, YOU’RE SO MATURE!” The boy stared at her, her loud enthusiastic nature drawing his eyebrow up. He thought she was just yelling to be heard over the train, but it seems that it was standard for her to happily yell her business into the streets. Girls like this were a dime a quarter a carton, dime a dozen, and a penny a pack in urban Japan, they were always out to be seen silently begging for AFFECTION and attention. He lit his cigarette regardless, putting his lighter up and stepping over the rail guards before they rose. Kawaii stood there, waving after him as the warning chimes continued to blare. “HEY WAIT UP! YOU’RE SUPPOSED TO WAIT UNTIL THE GUARD RISES FIRST!” Her friends caught up with her as the chimes stopped and the rail lifted. The boy slipped his hands into his pockets, looking down at her for results. “Well? How’d it go?” Kawaii turned around with a smile, tilting her head to the side. “I guess he’s real eager to start his first day. He just ran off!” ---I’d say that my problems as soon as I learned from my father that he’d enrolled me into Shinyo Academy. It was rather strange of him to do such a thing since I was so close to finishing school completely. He had insisted that it would be best for both of us if I had changed schools, so I told my friends that I’d be leaving. We had a goodbye party, got shitfaced, and woke up with one of the chicks that had a crush on me for months but never had the nerve to make a move until urged by the persuasion of liquor. With all my ties severed more or less, I was ready to start fresh. I learned that there was a bigger purpose for me attending this school, and realized that sometimes it wasn’t about what I wanted, but the needs of others. The class was already in their seats with scattered chats jumping about the class. The door slid open, and silence gripped the class as an astute woman wearing a long black skirt with a white blouse and black jacket walked in on black heels. She was the model for Brainy Babes Biweekly; jet black bun tied back tightly with black glasses perched on her nose with a chain hanging from each arm going around her neck telling everyone that she was a frequent reader. She sat her stack of books and folders on the desk at the front of the class and looked at them. “Class,” she began in an intellectual tone. “We have a new student. Introduce yourself, please.” He could see all the new empty faces with their eyes in his direction. He decided there was no way out of this awkward situation, so he nodded and gripped the handle of his bag. “Hayabusa Tajima,” he bowed, the sound of chalk scribing his name on the blackboard thanks to the teacher. “Welcome,” the class answered in a tinny automatic tone. The teacher placed the chalk down and just as fast as she had wrote Tajima’s name it was erased. “You may sit over there between Mr. Saotome and Mr. Hasegawa, please.” “Yes, thank you, ma’am.” Wandering eyes followed him on his path to his new seat. As he sat down and unpacked his bag to prepare for class, he glanced up and watched as Saotome looked across his desk at Hasegawa on Hayabusa’s left. Hasegawa was a suave looking guy, with gelled back hair and a cool look that made the girls swoon. On contrast Saotome was a shady looking individual that looked like he had something to hide. With the two of them giving each other knowing looks, Hayabusa’s brow wrinkled in query before focusing on the lesson. ---I am a watcher. I am inserted into a location to observe and take detailed notes in correlation to the assignment. I hate to sound so analytical and professional about what I do, but that’s how it was explained to me. Basically while I’m there I’m supposed to look for suspicious shit and write it down, then I’m supposed to compare notes to certain character traits to identify possible culprits. I know, I know; you’re sitting there thinking ‘What the fuck is this kid on? I want some!’ It’s a long story. I’ll try and explain things as quickly as I can. You see, right after my father transferred me into Shinyo Academy, he turned up dead. Reports say it was suicide, but I think otherwise. My father wasn’t the type; he had too much pride and spirit to end it himself; someone ended it for him, definitely against his will. While I was tending to his paperwork I came across something unusual in his recent work. My father was a historian, and he kept an avid journal of details of history, some fact and some myths. Before his death he had been taking a lot of interest into the whole story of Shimabara Christian revolt, more specifically in the life and times of the rebellion leader, a 16 year old boy named Amakusa Shiro. I personally can’t see how a kid this young could lead people to revolt against the Tokugawa shogunate, but they say this kid was hand picked by God and blessed with charisma to move normal men against trained soldiers and win. Anyway, the kid was only brought down when Tokugawa’s army swarmed the castle they held refuge in and overtook them. That’s how the history books told it of course. My dad had details saying that Amakusa and the rebellion were squashed by a single team of ninjas. Amakusa Shiro was beheaded and that was that. At least, that’s what the history books say. My father was investigating the story afterwards, the one that said Amakusa Shiro came back, and came back pissed off. He hated how God supposedly shafted him and the people that trusted in him, how He’d allowed them to die at heathen hands. Pretty much Amakusa denounced God, sold his soul to the devil in the bargain that he’d destroy the world as revenge for the Christians killed by the Tokugawa shogunate. It’s kind of outlandish to read that someone would damn himself in the honor of saints, but hey, Amakusa was just that nice a guy apparently. It’s said though that he recruited warriors resurrected from hell for his army and was prepared to hold the initial assault popping off the whole holocaust party, but had a little trouble from those same ninjas that stopped him before. Luck just wasn’t with him. He got beat by the same guy twice, and before he disappeared he vowed to return in a millennium to continue his prophecy of destruction. That was a while ago, centuries ago, but my father did some calculations and determined somehow that if all this weren’t grade A+ bullshit, then Amakusa Shiro and his boys would be due for a visit sometime soon. At first I wasn’t buying into my dad’s cockamamie fairy tale. I thought about it and I just couldn’t see this being truth. It had to be a story, another myth that wanted to continue haunting the world even after its death. When someone writes a story and stops it before it’s completion, the world that story exists in ends. I had thought the resurrection of the Amakusa Shiro tale was just someone trying to recreate that world all over again, but then after my father died I knew something was up that wasn’t natural. I’d continue his work, and I’d make sure that his death wasn’t in vain. -=-=-=-=-=- •ΞΘΞ• -=-=-=-=-=- The arcade was an AV bombardment, likely enough to strike you both blind and deaf from the blaring music and seizure inducing lights. People jumped from machine to machine pumping in yen for pleasure, the machines brought to life at their command. It seemed like everyone was having fun… everyone except one girl. The Easy Breezy Babe Miyazaki Tomoko Her black boots clicked across the tiled floor, stopping in the center of the arcade looking around. She brushed a tuff of her dyed reddish brown hair out of her face for a more accurate examination, and after turning up empty handed she walked off on her search, stopping to glance at her reflection in one of the mirrored walls. After her brief moment of VAINITY, she shoved her hands into her pink jacket pockets as she stopped behind two people playing a shooter. The players were deeply into the game, ducking and weaving like there were actual bullets flying at them. “Maru,” her sultry voice had to carry over both the music and the players’ voices. “Have you seen Jo?” The small girl on the right in a yellow tank top and jeans glanced back for a split second before going back to the game. “Nope! Ask Maezono—SHIT! HE ALMOST HIT ME!!” Rolling her eyes she looked to the left, where a tall leather jacket clad guy was waving around an orange pistol outlandishly. “AHAHAHAHA!! YEAH TAKE THAT BITCH!” Maezono,” the girl began, but she quickly realized that he wasn’t listening when he stepped back to reload the light gun. “Maezono,” she tried again, but he was still laughing, focused on the game. Finally she tugged lightly at the bottom of his jacket. “Maezono?” Suddenly he whipped around, pointing the plastic pistol right between her eyes with a wild glare. “You made me MISS!!” EXTREME INSANITY Maezono Kenji “Maezono!” Tomoko was surprised at his sudden gesture, but not so much his behavior. Hanging around him and the other Shubultz Cuts conditioned Tomoko for the various personalities of the gang. “Have you seen Jo?” He stared her down with that CRAZY glare of his, and then a smile spread across his face making him look more deranged. “Yeah, he’s outside takin’ a smoke.” “Hey! Little help here!!” At the girl’s request Maezono’s focus was given back to the game. Tomoko turned her head towards the doors, making a beeline for the front doors. As she neared, she saw Jo standing there leaning against the wall with a cigarette dangling from his lips. What made her slow her pace was that he was chatting with a girl a few feet away from him. She watched as he muttered something to the girl, nodding his head off in the distance. She bowed and walked off, prompting Tomoko to exit the arcade and approach him from the side. “Who was that?” jealousy saturated into her words. Within natural reincarnation the spirit is reborn unaware of its past life. When Amakusa is reborn, he’s reborn under the guise of a completely new soul. What that means is that whatever vessel was chosen to harbor his spirit, it was given a new soul along with the reincarnated soul. Two souls inhabiting the same vessel, only one is dormant while the other is dominant. In this case, the newborn soul is dominant, with the docile Amakusa Shiro awaiting the moment of rebirth from within. I don’t know how this was managed personally, but my father must’ve been talking to the Buddhist priests or something because he got the analytical details of it. Before in the time of Tokugawa, my father had documented that Amakusa was brought back to life through the sacrifice of another soul. With two souls being in one body, it’s as simple as flicking a switch for the dormant demon within to take over the soul as well as the body, and there’d be no outside change witnessed. Really sneaky of those bastards… but I’ve been forewarned of the signs. There are six roles to be taken by Amakusa Shiro and his host: A Charismatic Leader A Vain Egoist An Affectionate Hedonist An Aloof Leader A Crazy Anarchist A Cursed Creature Shinyo Academy was cursed with the abnormal, my father wrote. It’s been documented for years of its strange events and the like. Somewhere in Shinyo, demons walked unconsciously in school uniforms. I have my suspicions… the list of suspects is long. This is a contest, and I’ll stop him before he can start. He won’t pass by me… I won’t allow it. Because if he does… I’d have failed, and then that would mean the end of the world, as we know it. -=-=-=-=-=- •ΞΘΞ• -=-=-=-=-=- … what was that? Shit! Something’s there! I gotta hide! Where… WHERE?? It was nearly impossible to filter through all of the footsteps that passed by the alley, but somehow Tajima had managed to pinpoint one particular pair of feet. It was something deep in the back of his mind that told him someone had approached the entrance of the alley; that someone was standing there watching and waiting for him to make a peep or jerk… some sign of life that would herald his death. Tajima tried to pancake himself against the brick wall, hoping and praying with every bit of faith in his heart that he could miraculously take on chameleon traits and blend into his surroundings. Though the reptilian ability didn’t come upon him inexplicably, for the meantime he remained unseen. He couldn’t bear to look towards the entrance. He still knew it was there; he knew it was looking for him. There was no doubt it had tracked him exactly to this alley. It just couldn’t see him. It had felt him out from the start to this dead end, following his stench of fear like a basset hound. Tajima’s neck tingled, hairs pricking up on the nape of his neck and forearms. Everything was getting cold, like all of a sudden a icy chill had flushed through the alley. Something urged Tajima to take a glance, to make sure everything was safe again. He wanted visual guarantee that his life was secure for the meantime. Turning his head slowly not to rustle his clothing, he peered down the lane towards the street. Nothing… Exhaling with relief he stepped away from the wall, half smiling that his sealed fate held a few cracks. He nearly choked on that breath of fresh air when a shadowy figure slinked back into the alleyway, long hairs hanging down about its head. There you are… Tajima gasped, nearly shrieking femininely as he staggered backwards, falling on his back as he tried to escape. It seemed too cliché for him to fall at the EXACT moment the monster came for him, and he cursed himself for his stereotypical nature. Crawling backwards, he twisted around and came to his feet, running away from the hunter. Of course he realized that he was running deeper and deeper into a dead end. His sneakers slid against the street as he braked before the dumpster. Behind him he heard the monster’s paws smack into the walls, claws scraping against the brick as it crept towards him. There’s no escaping your destiny… everything has been created for destruction since you knew my name… there’s no hope for you, I’m afraid… One idea came to mind, though it wasn’t the best considering what was happening. Tajima jumped, grabbing the edge of the dumpster and pulled himself in. It was an act of desperation, sitting in that metal cell surrounded by garbage and vile unmentionables as a means of survival. He crawled up against the back of the bin and reached up, closing the lid and hiding in the darkness. You should have realized the second you saw me that this is the end. The road has been long and fruitful for you, but your life ends here… now. You try and protect yourself, try to fight back against something you cannot hope to survive. You and your foolish faith… Everything in his mind was silent as he listened to the footsteps approach one by one, the talons still whining scratchily. It was drawing closer, and the trash dumpster would only serve to amplify the sounds lurking towards him. It came upon him, and finally everything was quiet. Tajima sat there staring through the metal, imagining that demon standing opposite of him, doing the same exact thing he was. Everything that you knew as truth has now become false… including yourself. Suddenly the whole dumpster began to rock, an entire earthquake localized in that metal container. Tajima rolled into a corner and tried to brace himself as the corners of the dumpster began rumbling, lifting off the ground and crashing into the ground heavily. Trash bounced around, empty cans and diapers slapping him in the face. Imprints began dimpling the sides, Tajima narrowly escaping harm as he dove into the center of the dumpster. Grabbing nearby bags he threw them on top of himself for protection, grounding himself as the quakes abruptly… stopped. He waited to see if there would be some aftershocks from that beast, but once he found things calm, he pushed the bags aside and looked up. Slowly he crawled over to the left side of the dumpster, lifting the lid slightly to look through the crack. Suddenly the lid snapped up, and Tajima jumped out screaming with his fists balled up. “AHHH!! WHAT THE HELL?!” His look of pure terror died when he found a waiter jumping back with a bag of garbage in his hands. Tajima stared at him, watching as the jittery server glared at him. “Damn punk kid!” he threw the load of garbage at him, Tajima holding his arms up as it hit and fell into the dumpster. Not sitting around to wait, Tajima crawled out of the dumpster and raced out of the alley, the waiter scratching his shaved head confused. “Damn kids these days… think they’re the shit.”
