Re-Enter The Horror

21 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue: For those of you in Primetime Central who have not had the chance to live in a tormented life, it is now time for you to rip the smiles from your faces and realize what a truly bad life is like. This man has been in PTC for many months now but few times has he unleashed his emotion on the rest of the world. From 360 to DWO, LOW to EWA, all have felt his pain, his agony. And, for the first time since a terrible lose to Global Champion Seymour Almasy, he returns and asks that you only do one thing and one thing only... FATHOM his words. Content: The scene opens to the Primetime Central arena, the REAL Primetime Central arena. A match is going on in the ring and we see the final moments of it where Tristan Belmont, one of the greatest wrestlers of our time has hooked the leg of Fathom, another influential wrestler. The referee drops to the ground and slaps his hands to the mat in succession three times. A bell sounds and Tristan Belmont rises to his feet with his hands in the air. At that point, the scene changes to an up-close picture of an eyeball.It is a turquoise eye that shows much emotion in it although the rest of the face has been witheld from us. No other sounds can be heard although inside of the eye, back where we cannot see, what was just viewed by us continues to play over and over as if a replay of the tragedy continues to unfold. The camera slowly zooms out and more of the face is shown while the eye stays open and does not blink. We come to a whole headshot to see that it is Fathom himself. Stuck in a position of horror but not moving. For, he has not moved for many days and that statement should give a general sense of what has occured. Now, the camera is at a full body shot to see the look of fear on his face while dried blood sits on the outside of one of his veins. The only comfort to him is the carpet underneath him to keep the corpse comfortable. But more uncomfortable times are in front of him as various types of insects have found their way to his body. Maggots, worms, rats, all have found a place to burrow on the rotting corpse that lays adorned in nothing more then blue denim jean-shorts. But as we stop and stare for a mere second at this dead body, we see something. Not something that would be visual to the mere eye but something underneath. Something as if the soul of Fathom has stayed back with its body and is trying to wake up the life that is now nonexistant inside of him. And with that, we move closer. Closer towards the body and back towards the eyes, the entrance to the soul. And as we listen, we can hear it. We can hear the cheers, the boos, the legacy of Fathom. But one noise stands out of all of the other ones. It is the sound of an announcer, one that is very familiar to the fans of Primetime Central. "The winner of the match... TRISTAN BELMONT!" says the announcer, leading to the masses to cheer. All of a sudden the face of Fathom contorts. Thats right, the corpse has actually moved. It has grown angry yet nothing else moves. Are we crazy? Did it really move? But, again there is a sound that rings out from the eyes of the dead Fathom. "Fathom, you made the deal and now you must follow through." says the cocky man known as Tristan Belmont. There is a slight twitch of hate in Fathom's face but he is dead, how can that be? Silence has begun to fill the odd room once again before cheers flow once again. "I, Fathom, am now Tristan Belmont's... bitch..." says the voice of Fathom, not from his mouth but from his memory. And that's when the face really begins to move as all emotion once again fills and it is like he is there. His face begins shakingmore and more as if he is having a seizure but we are suddenly pulled into his eyes, into his memory, into his tortured life. We are pulled into a place that, as eery as it sounds, Fathom controls. Inside, we zoom past memories of Fathom's that are played continuously on big screens. The first one is of young Fathom leaving his grandmother to die, the next one is of Fathom being tossed from a ladder by Enigma in the four-way 360 Championship match, after that is Fathom winning the 360 Brutality Championship but something peculiar is noticed as we approach the next one. This next one is from where all the noise was resonating from. We can hear the cheers and as we approach closer, we come to see the face of Tristan Belmont. His casual, cocky smile appears on his face as he looks down at a sweaty Fathom, who is on his knees looking up at Belmont. Cheers blast our eardrums as we continue to watch this memory from so long ago. "That's right Fathom, thats what you are." says Belmont, laughing through his words. "You are nothing more then.. my bitch!" With that, all of the fans begin to laugh out loud as Fathom looks around at them all growing embarrased. Massive amounts of sweat continues to drip from his face as he looks around at all of them. He tries not to look up at Belmont but that concentration to stay away is suddenly broken as Belmont reaches down with his right hand and raises Fathom's head up to see anger getting ready to blow up inside of Fathom. Belmont starts to laugh once again as he takes his hand off Fathom's head and takes two steps back looking down at Fathom. "It's alright Fathom." he says. "But as my bitch, I COMMAND you to stay there for the amusement of me and all of these fans!" Fathom begins shaking as all that fills his ears is the laughing. It is continuous and does not stop. All that he sees is Belmont pointing forward at him laughing continuously. Fathom's constant shaking dos not stop and suddenly his eyes go shut with anger and frustration but suddenly they open and we are focused on a different Fathom. This one is lying down and it is unbelievable to see life inside of the once-dead Fathom. We were inside of his brain and saw what he is feeling and that anger has given him the motivation to push death away and return. He sits up and looks around to see all of the insects around him along with the used needles next to his arm. He looks down and raises his left arm up to see the dried blood with maggots slowly moving around on them. He wipes them off although not as if he cared they were there because he did it with such nonchalance. He lifts his legs up and wraps his arms around them to comfort himself while sticking his head in between his kneecaps as we can almost see tears from the frustration. All of a sudden the television flashes on and it is the head director of the Extreme Title Division who stands with a paper in his hand staring into the camera while a microphone is shoved into his face. "And the eight competitors who shall compete in our match to find a number one contender for Tristan Belmont's Extreme Championship…” says the man, adding intrigue to the people who cannot wait to find out who it is. “From Global Wrestling we have Ice and Max Roswell. From World Wrestling Midwest we have Ladysman Noah Hanson. From Four Winds Wrestling we have Sean McClancy, Chris West and Mike Marvelous. From Furiously Addictive Wrestling we have “The Leegend” Lee Smith and from the WWA we have Fathom. I wish luck to all competitors and cannot wait to see who will challenge Tristan Belmont at the Chris Williams Memorial Cup.” With that the television screen fizzles out to a Primetime Central logo but the mere mention of his name reverts Fathom from his legs and looking up at the screen. Even with nothing now on the screen he continues to stare as if he feels redemption shall soon come. He looks around and sees what has become of his life before slowly getting up to his feet. He looks down at the floor to see all of the shattered pieces of his life laying on the ground as he picks up a used needle and stares at it for a second. He then looks up at the screen as his brown dreadlocks begin to fall forward into his face so it is not very visible. "Eight men shall walk into the ring and bye for a shot at the PTC Extreme Championship. They shall all walk in and be the most extreme that they can be. Chairs, tables, ladders, hell even this needle could be used." says Fathom, still fixated on the needle that once killed him. "One thing that Primetime Central already knows is that I can be "extreme". They know that my sadistic mind could turn the other seven participants into grated cheese. For the other seven men come from great federations, yes, but their history is not as great as mine. Were any of them some of the top-rated rookies when first arrival to PTC? No. Did they jump leaps and bounds over much of PTC in their time there? No. I have done that and Tristan Belmont was the man to put a hault to it. Now it is my turn to make Tristan Belmont my bitch and if it means beating the hell out of seven other men in the process then so be it, I will not miss this opportunity. For the seven unlucky souls paired with me, you shall all FATHOM my words." Fathom quickly looks up with pure unadulterated hate in his eyes. He stabs the needle into a nearby wooden table before letting go and stepping forward almost as if staring straight into Tristan Belmont's eyes. "And you, Belmont, you shall FEAR your actions for I shall hand you your reprecussions." says Fathom, ready to kill as the scene fades to black.
