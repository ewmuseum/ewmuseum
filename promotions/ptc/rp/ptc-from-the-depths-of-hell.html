From The Depths Of Hell...

20 September 2005

Prelude: My Pitch Black Home Darkness. Even though I know it's not right I've come to accept it's embrace as my own. Lying here surrounded by the night really makes you think, that's probably why I cry myself to sleep every night. Hell, who am I kidding? I don't know what's night and day anymore, trapped in my little steel prison. I spend most of my waking moments pounding and kicking... hoping someone, somewhere will answer my pleas. Over time I've realized it's useless, but it's all I have. Wake up every morning just to be treated like a dog, being thrown food through a slit in the wall. Pulling at this collar around my neck just opens up the blisters again. What did I ever do to deserve this? No one knows the answers... no one has the time to answer me. Forsaken in my own personal steel hell. You try not to think about what'll happen when the food stops coming, perhaps that's why I cry myself to sleep. Now I'm just waiting to die, praying that my time will come sometime soon. I really don't know what comes after death, but it has to be better than here. Anything is better than here right? What I'd do to stare up into the sun once again and breathe clean air. The little things people take for granted, stripped away from me. Most of the time I wonder if this is an idea of somebody's sick joke. Laughing at me while I stare at my scars in the mirror, trying to come up with answers for questions that I don't even know. Maybe someday I'll be free and things will be different. Somebody outside these walls has to know the truth. I'm sorry... I never did introduce myself, did I? I would but... I don't even know who I am. My reflection doesn't know the answers, do you? Chapter One: Where No One Can Hear You Scream Just another cold day in hell. The same thoughts consumed her mind every morning as she rose into the darkness. Usually finding that a plate of mush had be not-so-delicately slid through a slot in the wall onto a table that lied at the end of her mattress. Dazed from her dreams, she rolled onto the dirt floor. Under her battered body she could feel the warmth of the earth pulsating. It was one of the only times that she truly felt alive anymore. Her optimistic thoughts were quickly dashed by a clear look at her surroundings. The cold steel walls were a constant reminder to her own misfortune. Reality was setting in. Numbing sleep wore off in due time, her aching joints from the unforgiving ground and her stinging blisters around neck arose as the blood began to come back through her body. Although that was one of the last things on her mind, as she fought through the discomfort while shoved mush into her mouth in globs out of her hands. Her stomach didn't feel any better as the last morsel touched her tongue. The nasty taste rested in the back of her mouth well after the mush was gone, but it was better than nothing at all. Beggars can't be choosers after all. Slowly, she made her way over to the opposite corner of the one room steel box, where a cheap toilet lies. Up against the wall propped a full-size mirror. She'd spend hours on end staring at her own reflection, hoping one of these days the sight of her face would snap something in her memory. Seemingly months had gone by with no success. Her health was starting to deteriorate from malnourishment. Through the dark she peered into the mirror. A pair of cadet blue eyes looked back at her... gazing into her soul. With her right hand she moved her straggly and matted long black hair out of her face. Patches of dirt and dust covered her body from head to toe. A black bra and a pair of ripped denim blue jeans is all the remained of her outfit, wherever she was before here. She held her left arm out closer to the mirror in front of her. Running her right hand up and down... she could feel the mountains and valleys on her skin. Seemingly wounds that didn't properly heal, or never got the chance to. She simply shook her head and ran her hands through her hair before sitting on the bare earth. Water consumed her vision, and steams of tears flowed down her dirt ridden face. You'd think it'd get easier over time, but it just didn't. The more she looked at herself the more she found out, and the more questions she had. Answers just didn't come to those questions. Slamming her right fist into the ground she pushed herself up to her feet. Cocking her fist back with tears streaming down her cheeks, she looked back in the mirror. She wanted so bad just to destroy that mirror... but it was all that she had. Closing her eyes for a moment, she turned away from the mirror and then looked over at the pile of dirty paper bowls that had piled up over time. "This isn't fair..." She said aloud before landing a heavy roundhouse on the steel wall, "Why do you do this to me?! Why do you keep me locked up in this God forsaken box?! Why?! What did I ever do to you?! I just want to know who I am! I just want to live." She slammed her right fist repeatedly on the steel wall. It was futile, and she knew that... but what else could she do? Sitting on her bed and eating mush all day certainly wouldn't accomplish anything. Everyday though... her punches and kicks grew weaker and weaker. Although she had already made a dent in the wall from seemingly an eternity of abuse, it just wasn't enough. She screamed at the top of her lungs for help as she pulled on the black collar around her neck. The collar looked newer than everything else on her body, and didn't seem to take the same abuse as her other clothes. Solid black all the way around, but a steel ring lie inside the innocent looking yet durable cloth-like material. Rubbing the blisters certainly didn't help, as drips of blood began to roll down the side of her neck. As much pain as it caused, when she looked down at her blood-soaked fingers it was a sick sense of satisfaction. She bled to make sure she was still alive. "Is this some kinda sick joke?" She screamed from inside the box towards the slot on the wall, "I hope this makes your REAL fucking happy! Please… just let me out, I’ll do anything. C’mon… PLEASE. CAN ANYONE HELP ME?! PLEASE!! LET ME OUT OF THIS PLACE! WHAT DO YOU WANT FROM ME?!" She collapsed onto the floor in tears, shouting muffled and incomprehensible screams into the darkness. Chapter Two: From The Outside Looking In "Is this some kinda sick joke?" A woman's voice pleaded through the speaker, "I hope this makes your REAL fucking happy! Please... just let me out, I'll do anything. C'mon... PLEASE. CAN ANYONE HELP ME?! PLEASE!! LET ME OUT OF THIS PLACE! WHAT DO YOU WANT FROM ME?!" A look of concern crossed the man's face as he watched the woman fall to her knees and begin to scream nonsensical pleas into the ground. Day in and day out it never did get any easier to watch over time like he thought it would. He closed his eyes and looked away from the screen. It just became to hard to watch after these past two months. Footsteps began to approach from behind him, making him quickly look back at the green night-vision video feed. Louder and louder they approached from behind him before the door opened up to his little security room. “How’s she coming along?” The deep, raspy sound of a man’s voice rang from behind him. The man had long gray hair pulled back into a pony tail and small circular shaped glasses. His face was expressionless as he looked over the security man’s shoulder to take a glance at the monitor. “She’s been doing the same now for about a week.” The security supervisor answered, glaring at the girl on her hands and knees sobbing into the dirt floor, “I think she’s back to health... although she appeared to be suffering from malnourishment. Other than that... no noticeable limps or anything physically. It’s quite the miracle you’ve worked. I’ve never seen anything like it before in my life. But... are you sure that’s her?” “It has to be her...” The man answered with a smile. “No one else could’ve possibly recovered like that, even with my help. This girl is special. You’ve seen the ways she’s tried to escape... incredible. Intelligent, resourceful, and highly talented. She’s the one we’ve been looking for Logan, no doubt in my mind.” “But what was she doing out th...” “That’s none of our concern, is it?” The man with a pony tail looked down at Logan, a look of scorn in his eyes. Logan bowed his head at the reply... bringing a smile back to the man’s face. “The only important thing is that she’s here... and she’s ours.” The man observed the girl’s movements with a sinister smile that crossed his aging face from ear to ear. His dark brown eyes followed her every movement with passion. The way she looked and the way she moved was like a work of art. He thought to himself that he could sit back and watch the girl helplessly struggle for hours on end and remain entertained. Such beauty couldn’t be seen in a museum or a gallery. “Mr. Hathaway...” Logan meekly said aloud, still a bit scared of what the much larger and powerful man may do if you speak to him the wrong way, “Don’t you think it’s about time we let her out? I mean she’s been recovered for quite sometime and she seems to be becoming weaker and weaker everyday.” “Not a bad idea Logan,” Hathaway paused before prying his eyes away from the monitor and looking into the eyes of Logan,”I think it’s just about time to meet our diamond in the rough. Open the bay door, I’m going to go meet our fallen angel.” “Yessir.” Logan replied before slamming his fist down on a button not too far away from the monitor that showed her picture. A smile crossed Logan’s face as Mr. Hathaway turned around and closed the door behind him. Maybe now he could sleep at night without hearing her screams. The only thing Logan didn’t know was who she was... and why she was there. But most important, what did Mr. Hathaway want her for? Chapter Three: Independence Day Dirty and Fallen, she laid on the mattress up at the metal ceiling above. Many nights, or as she knew them as the time she became exhausted or tired, she spend staring up at the same metal ceiling wondering what it looked like above. She tried her best to picture the stars and the moon above her as she feel asleep. The surface of the ceiling was more embedded into her mind than most of her faintest memories of what a normal life was. Without looking she could describe every dent and every rivet... it had become her night sky. Often the sporadicness of her memories crossed her mind. Wondering every now and again why she could remember the sun and the sky so readily, but couldn't remember the simplest things such as her name or her family. In her own mind, she'd even start making excuses and her own theories on why that was. Among her favorites were a traumatizing event or brainwash. Quickly her eyelids had become heavy and the ceiling began to blur. "Maybe tomorrow..." she said aloud. It was the same thing she said every night as she either passed out from exhaustion or cried herself to sleep. As her eyelids closed and her deep breaths turned into quiet but audible snores, a loud clank resonated through the steel shed. Her eyelids sprung up and she sat up quickly looking around the darkness. The seconds of sleep she received made her eyes have to readjust to the darkness. Was it a meal? No... it couldn't have been, it was a much different clank. The first sign of escape she'd heard in years brought her weakened body up to her feet. Pressing her ear against the metal, she could hear a rhythmic stomp sound growing louder and louder. Was it too good to be true? A smile crossed her face for the first time in seemingly a lifetime. Tightly balled fists began to bang anxiously against the metal wall. "HELP ME!!!" She screamed with her voice cracking slightly from her earlier attempts at freedom, "GET ME OUT OF HERE!" Anxious, she could feel her heart beating like never before. It felt abnormal, almost as if it were about to burst from her chest as she awaited a reply. No words were returned back to her as the thumps on the dirt began to come closer. She could almost feel the earth shift below her. It was a new experience, and anything new was a step in the right direction. On the verge of freedom, she felt almost like she could reach out and touch the sky. The emotion took awhile before registering in her mind. This had to have been what love felt like. "Are you there?!" She yelled out from inside. A raw, burning sensation rose through her throat before suddenly the sound of a machine was deafening around her. Falling to her knees and covering her ears from the painful noise, watching with a smile as the sky opened up to her from the wall. Slowly the wall began to come down, straight into the ground below. She watched to world in all its beauty unfold before her very eyes... and as the wall came down she saw a man standing before her. Tall and silent. She walked quickly over the slot in which the wall fell into, never wanting to cross that line ever again before stopping. Looking around her, it looked as if she was in a glass house. Walls and a ceiling made out of glass with metal supports. The moon hung high in the night sky above her, she smiled under it's radiance. Free at last, what a wonderful feeling. Inside the glass house around her was various trees and plant-life, mostly shrubs. Even the air tasted different, a brilliant gleaming smile was across her face when she looked back at the man. Was he responsible for freeing her? An unexpected sense of cautiousness approached her when she went to walk towards the man who appeared to observe her movements. What if he was the bastard who kept me locked up in this hell? What if it was a mistake and he's going to make me go back? Second thoughts consumed her while she examined his exterior. The man's circular shaped glasses reflected the moonlight above, shrouding his eyes. His face remained emotionless and his gray hair was pulled back into a long ponytail, that went down to about his shoulder blades. "Who are you?" She asked of him, eyeing him carefully and keeping his distance. Up close he could see the physical ramifications of keeping her locked up for the past several months. Perhaps he should've let her out sooner, but tonight seemed like the right time. He looked at the fresh scabs around her neck under the collar. What a feisty specimen she was, "Are you going to answer me?!" "My name is Johnathan Hathaway." He answered in a deep, raspy voice, "You can call me John. I am... a scientist, but I am also a business man." "What am I doing here? Who put me in this place? Who am I?" She asked. She had so many unanswered questions... she couldn't possibly think of them all at the time. "Those will all be answered in time, Tera." Hathaway answered, "Looks like we need to get you cleaned up and a little more comfortable first, don't you think?" "Yes but..." Tera tried to answer, but she was quickly interrupted. He had called her Tera... was that her real name? She'd thought it to herself while he spoke. "Wie fühlen Sie Fräulein?" Hathway said, looking curiously at her. Not seeming to notice nor care that she was trying to speak. "Ich fühle frei, und es ist wunderbar. Obwohl ich sehr müde bin." Tera responded reluctantly, not knowing fully why she understood him or how she responded so quickly without thinking about what she had said. She just knew, it was a very uncomfortable feeling at first, but being so close to answers... she had a feeling she'd figure it out soon enough. "Good... you remember German." Hathaway responded, "Are you ready to come inside Tera? I have much to teach you." "I am." Tera said before Hathaway turned his back on her. Suddenly something didn't feel right as she followed him towards the exit. His silence towards her pleading screams, his lack of questions towards why she was locked up, how he knew her name and so much about her. If he knew all these things, why didn't he save her before? He had to have been the bastard that did this to her... it was the only explanation. Tera quickly picked up the pace, catching up to Hathway on the way towards the exit of the odd glass house. Breathing down his neck, she did the first thing her instincts had instructed. She kicked him in the back of the knee knocking him down to one knee before wrapping her left arm around his neck. She pushed her right forearm into the back of his head. One motion could snap his neck and end his life. As much as she felt like she wanted to at the time, he could quite possibly be the only person in the world who had the answers she was looking for. "Let me go..." Hathaway choked out, "If you don't you'll..." "I'll be sorry?" Tera interrupted him, "Why are you doing this to me? Why have you kept me locked up in a fuc..." An electric current shot through Tera's body and she screamed out towards the sky, releasing her hold on Hathaway and falling to the ground. Her body began to tingle as she laid on the dirt. Smoke began to rise from the collar around her neck, while she grabbed it trying to ease the pain. Her heart beat irregularly while she squirmed around, trying to get back to her feet... but was too numb to brace her own body weight. "No, you'll hurt yourself." Hathaway said, brushing himself off as he got back to his feet, looking down at Tera while she tried to regain herself from the electric shock that felt as if it almost killed her, "That collar around your neck isn't for decoration Tera. It's an electric shock device, controlled by a security system I and my team have total control over. That last one was compliments of my head of security Logan. He's watching from one of the 10 cameras that are mounted around the room. I also have several remotes to the device that I can use as a I please. Trust me my dear Tera, the last thing I want to do it hurt you... but I have to protect myself. And protect you from doing something before you understand the consequences. Now, there is much more you must learn... and I'd recommend listening to me. I am a very caring man once you get to know me... I can do a lot for you, as long as you learn to control yourself. Are you going to control yourself Tera?" "I guess..." Tera replied, "I'll behave, I suppose I don't have much choice." "Trust me Tera," Mr. Hathaway replied with a smile, "You'll be much happier this way, I assure you." Tera picked herself up off the ground with a painful grunt, trying best not to show her emotions towards Mr. Hathaway. It wasn't the most pleasant scenario for freedom but it was freedom none the less. As much as she felt this was a painful sight of things to come, nothing could be any worse than being locked up in that box. Right? Chapter Four: Prison Break 3:00AM The clock across the room said all Tera wanted to see. The green glow of the clock lit up the beautiful face of Tera. It was amazing how much a shower and a week of eating right can do to a girl. Her cadet blue eyes looked at the camera that moved across the room in the darkness. It was the movement that let Tera know, that Logan was walking away... and it was time for his replacement to take over for a shift. Timing how long it took Logan to leave his post, Tera rolled out of bed and crawled over to the wall. Staying close she moved over towards the door, inching to her own freedom. Her right hand grasped the doorknob and she spoke quietly to herself. "It's time to find yourself Tera." Holding her breath, Tera listened as the footsteps of the night security patrol walked by on the marble floors. Slowly they began to echo into the darkness. The thump of shoes on marble turned into the thump of the human heart. Closing her eyes, Tera turns the doorknob and quickly but silently makes her way out into the hallway. After a light surveillance, Tera places her foot on the darkened moonlit wall and thrusted herself into the air. With a quick tug she pulled the wire out of the back of the camera above her head. Leaning against the wall with a sign of relief, Tera inched across the hallway. With a sudden halt she stopped at the corner. Slowly peering over the corner, Tera saw the security guard making his way down the hallway. The security guard took a look back down the hallway, sending Tera back around the corner as fast as she could. A suspicious feeling came over the young guard. Turning around and coming back, he searched both walls very carefully. Turning over his shoulder every once in a while just to be careful. Tera's heart rate picked up and her breath became heavier. Like a trapped animal she became frantic, not knowing what to do as the guard grew closer and closer. CLICK... CLICK... CLICK... She listened like a deer in headlights. Every click of his foot was a step closer to a harsh punishment. How would she face John Hathaway after this? Who knows what he'd do to her. She had to do something... if she stood there she was surely a goner. But it looked like it was too late... the shadow casted by the security guard could be seen in front of her on the marble floor. Tera closed her eyes and took a gulp, it was just about over. The security guard approached the corner and drew his gun. With a young explosiveness, the guard turned the corner with his gun drawn to see... Nothing. "That's odd..." He said aloud, "I could've swor..." The was the last audible thing that Security Guard Matt Schafer would say for well over a week as he suddenly watched his handgun skid across the floor. Instinctively he turned around just in time for Tera to grab his arm and shove her foot into his jaw, breaking a joint that would have Matt drinking threw a straw for a good couple weeks. Unsuccessfully screaming out for help Matt fell to his knees in pain before he was struck in the back of his head by a kick, that would leave him unconscious laying on the floor to be found by another guard in around 20 minutes. Tera kneeled down and unclipped a tazer and a switchblade knife from his belt. Tera looks around the corner with the switchblade knife in her hand, looking at the beauty of the blade before flinging it across the hallway edge first into the lens of a security camera by the stairwell. A spark ignited the hallway for a split second from the knife before smoke began to rise, signaling it's death. Tera looked down at her hands confused... "How in the world..." She said aloud. Finally she realized that there was much more to this whole situation than she thought. Perhaps there was never this normal life out there waiting for her, perhaps her life was everything but normal. What had just happened certainly wasn't normal... but she had to know the truth. It waited outside the doors of the facility, waiting for her to grab it. There wasn't much time. Tera sprinted down the hallway towards the stairwell, which lead down to the exit on the first floor from her second floor bedroom. She descended the stairwell with unmatched speed, with tunnel vision located towards the exit that was just over 30 yards away. Tera took a glance out of the window from the stairwell before sliding the door open and squeezing out into the open hallway. She sprinted towards a rotating security camera that stood between herself and freedom. As it turned the other direction, Tera used the wall to jump up to it and tag it with the tazer... making the wires short circuit and sparks shoot out the sides. CLICK... CLICK... CLICK... Faster and faster she ran as she heard the first floor security guard approaching the stairwell entrance from behind her. Out into the open she ran in front of the front exit of the building, lowering her shoulder and blasting through the locked glass automatic door. To her surprise, no security alarms went off as she sprinted down the driveway towards the gate. She could see the sun beginning to rise on the other side of the razor-wire fenced gate. Adrenaline pumped through her veins as a few feet separated her from her destined future. As she reached out, suddenly she fell to her knees in agony. Her body tingled as she lay face first on the asphalt, just 5 feet away from a new freedom. Pulling at the collar you think would ease the burns, but the pain the continued to shoot through her body didn't seem to go away as she laid kicking on the asphalt. Smoke rose from her collar and tears came down her eyes as she reached out towards the gate, only to see it open... and John Hathaway walk through. He scowled at her through the small circular glasses on the end of his bulbous nose. She looked up in agony looking for forgiveness and pity, only to find none. "This proves two things to me Tera Zenith..." Hathaway says, standing over the grounded escapee, "One... I can't trust you yet, and that saddens me beyond description. Two, you really are Die Erntemaschine." Die Erntemaschine. Those two words resonated through Tera's mind as she tried to claw her way up at John's feet. They sounded so familiar, but it's meaning eluded Tera. She just couldn't shake how familiar it was to her. It felt like it belonged so much more than Tera ever has. Interlude: Complacency Over the last couple weeks I've learned a lot about myself, but I still feel this open void inside. Something is missing, there is a part of me that wants to learn more... and it pains me to know that John has the answers. Why wouldn't he tell me the truth? It's been weeks since I tried to escape, I've done everything he's said ever since. It's a matter of getting them from him and it isn't going to be easy, but a woman has her ways. I can't let it come to that though, this damn thing around my neck and my constant surveillance makes every effort I can possibly come up with useless. Although I'm free I still feel like I'm a piece of meat. I've come to ignore the camera that watches me take a shower. I'm sure Logan is enjoying himself every night to that one, that sick little pervert moves it wherever I go. Maybe one day he'll regret that, but maybe again one day these feelings will go away. I know now that life could be much, much worse... perhaps I'm just too stubborn and need to accept this as my life. Whichever way my life may fall, it by no means is going to be easy. I train for hours everyday... running, punching a bag, practicing balance and gymnastics. I must admit I do enjoy it, it's the one thing I love the most about my life. I may not have the best friends or any family that I know of, but I get to do the things that I enjoy the most. Every once in a while John even brings in someone new to spar against. I see myself getting drastically better everyday. There is nothing like the thrill and excitement of a good fight. I wouldn't trade it for anything in the world. Chapter Five: Unknown Truth "We're sitting on a gold mine with this girl." Logan said as he walked down the hallway, along side John Hathaway who was sipping away at some morning tea. He tried not to pay much attention to Logan's ideas, especially after the last idea he had put Hathaway out about $5,000. "How's that?" John Hathaway asked between sips of mocha. "Have you seen this girl sparring?" Logan said, "She's a machine... it's incredible, I've never seen anyone like her before in my life. Could you imagine how much we could get off her kicking some guys ass? It'd be great." "We'd get like what? $200?" Hathaway responded, "I'm not interested." "Here me out here Mr. Hathaway." Logan tried to explain, "I know we're just keeping this girl up to see what she's capable of, she's one of the most fabled people in the history of Chicago but... think about the possibilities. Boxing matches, Mixed-Martial Arts, Wrestling! This girl is a phenom, she could do it all. I mean I was watching this Primetime Central thing the other night, with the exposure and the prices in wrestling today. It could be huge! I mean... these people are making MILLIONS of dollars. What would Tera do with a million dollars? She's happy just fighting. Who do you think would get the million dollars Mr. Hathaway? Professional Athletes... and this girl is unstoppable." "Keep going..." Hathaway hated to admit to himself that he was becoming interested in the whole prospect, but would be the first to admit that professional fighting wasn't exactly a hobby of his. "This is just contracts Mr. Hathaway. On top of that we're talking like incentives, merchandise sales. The female fan base of wrestling would be ALL OVER this stuff. She'd be an icon to girls wanting to fight all over the world!" Logan begins to get overly excited and knocks a cup of coffee out of John Hathaway's hands as they stop in front of a gymnasium door, "...sorry." "I'll do some research." Hathaway said as he pushed the doors open to see Tera Zenith punching away at a black bag hanging from the ceiling on the far side of the room. Logan watched from outside as the swinging door closed and John walked across the room towards Tera. He knew very well about Primetime Central, even if he didn't follow wrestling. He knew Christian Zenith was there, a big part of Global Wrestling which was a part of that place.... and if Christian Zenith was there, Tera wasn't safe. But with that kind of money... "It's time we talk Tera." With a stiff kick Tera Zenith backed up from the bag and took a look back at John Hathaway who sat down on the bench across from her. Tera wiped the sweat away from her forehead as she walked over towards John. She was definitely the one alright... Die Erntemaschine. German for The Reaper. What a fitting name on such a talented woman. She had definitely recovered nicely, there were only a handful of women Hathaway could honestly say was as attractive as she was at this exact moment in time. "Are you finally going to tell me the truth?" Tera glared back at John, her adrenaline still pumping from her very lengthy morning workout. "Actually... I am." John replied, much to Tera's surprise. You could almost see her eyes light up, it'd been the moment she'd been waiting for as long as she could remember. The truth about who she was, and her family and friends, why John kept her locked up like some kind of pet. Claiming it was for her own protection, "You ready?" "I've been ready for as long as I can remember." Tera replied, sitting down on the concrete floor in front of him. "Your name is Tera Elise Zenith." John paused before going on, "You grew up in Germany and moved to the United States when you were young. Your father, he owns a major insurance company... or did. Your mother was a great woman, she took care of you and your younger brother Christian when you were younger. He now owns the insurance company in Chicago, Illinois." "You see when you were a teenager... you ran away from home. Everyone took you for dead, even the police department, but of course you weren't. From what I've gathered you got together with a group of people who taught you the street life. Violence, guns, control. Eventually you became one of the greatest assassins ever in Chicago..." "Die Erntemaschine." Tera said quietly in response. "Exactly." John stopped before continuing on, "The police suspected Christian of the assassinations... that was until you assassinated him yourself, months after your parents were found dead in their own home with Christian as the only witness. I don't know what got into you, but everything ended up about 100 miles down the road from here where we found you. You were shot 4 times, and were laying out in the desert to die. I don't know who did it or how you got there, but I used my computer to match your fingerprints to the police records from when you ran away. They matched. I suspect your brother was behind it... payback for your assassination attempt." "How in the hell do you know I'm Die Erntemaschine." Tera retaliated, looking back at John. "I've seen what you can do, and it's things only Die Ertnemaschine could do." John looked back over at Tera, who knew the truth now but couldn't admit to herself that she's a murderer, even though she knows she's done things now that very well merit that claim, "Also, your brother Christian Zenith was tried as the killer in all of your murders. He said he didn't know who the murderer was in a lie detector test and lied... he knew who you were. He would just never admit it. Cause if he did they would look for you, and they would find you and find out what he did to you and he would be in serious trouble." "I'll take care of him..." "No, you can't." John interrupted her in mid-sentence, "You have to stay away from your brother Christian. You may be able to beat him in a fight but there are millions of people and millions of dollars that Christian could use to make sure that you actually died this time. We rescued you from the desert and tried to save you for 3 days before you finally stabled out.
