To Many Early Morning Visits Challenges are often rewarding, they can define the person that you become as core values can be molded in the very being that you need to be. I had done everything I could have done as far as training goes for the match against the Baller and Dean. Sometimes though things cannot be helped and I ended up being put through a table. My head was still sore from the fact of that going through the table. I still have to prepare for the Awards Show Battle Royal and now I also have a few other matches like an Interfed match against the Killer Pirates from the Outer Caribbean. Now I am not really that worried about that match, as I need to get a better standing in the PTC tag team division. So that is why I am in this match against the Pirates , nothing real big just want to get that shot at the PTC tag team titles. Then I also have a match in the other fed that I am in against Alexander Koresch, Fury, Luther Jackson and Tricky D. So yeah I am pretty booked solid at the moment. But hell that is just fine with whatever the idea of being so busy that each and every match seems to kind of flow into each other. So whatever I don’t know I guess I should be more concerned with the Pirates but I am not and maybe that is a character flaw but I am just not that concerned. I need to be more focused on certain things and this unfortunately is not one of those things. I have a wedding to plan, a battle royal to win and a history to carve out in WMW. This is my destiny, this is the path I choose and it my curse… Noah’s House Saturday, February 12, 2005 Kansas City, Kansas Time: Unknown “Why do I always seem to overbook myself?” I said to myself as I sighed deeply. I hate it when I seem to over book myself like I have, but I need to keep myself busy like I do. I need the release; the feeling of beating someone senseless is always something that has made me feel better. I heard the rustling of someone moving about, must be Kristina. She always liked to sleep in and this was not that big a deal. I walked out and went to the door to get the morning paper. I slowly opened the door, and when I opened the door there was someone standing there already holding my paper. “Can I help you?” I asked holding my hand out, waiting for my paper to be placed in it. The person looked up, she was an older lady. She was wearing some old horn rimmed glasses, kind of frumpy looking if you know what I mean. “Hello, young man…” she replied as she handed my paper, and then shook my hand. “And you are?” I asked as I placed my paper under my armpit. “Kelsey Partiot, I represent the Loyal Order of the Water Buffalo…”She said as she handed me a flyer. I looked at the flyer, smiled kindly as I listened to her little speech. It was really too early for something like this and I was not really in the mood for all of this. Nevertheless, I was being nice and decided to give her a chance to speak her stuff. She was really quite good at talking but all it seemed like she was saying was “blah, blah, blah”. “We are doing a door to door pledge drive to raise funds for a new hall.” She said as showed me the form. “Will you help us?” I looked over the form, smiled and then reached into my pocket, pulling out my checkbook. “How about I make a donation right now?” “That would be splendid sir.” She said as a broad smile grew across her face. I grabbed my pen, scribbled down $25,ooo and then handed her the check. I smiled as it made me feel good to be able to give back to the community. She looked at the check, and then a huge smile came across her face and then a yelp escaped from her mouth. “25,ooo dollars, Oh my god this is amazing thank you Mr. Hanson, thank you again!!” she beamed as she gave me a huge hug. I shook my head as I watched the lady walk down the aisle to the gate. I looked at the paper as I closed the door and then walked to the kitchen. Kristina was sitting there looking over the stuff that had come in the mail yesterday. “Who was that baby?” She asked. “Just someone that was looking for a donation…” I replied as I tossed the paper on the counter, and then went to the fridge. I settled on cooking some bacon, eggs and some French toast. “So how much did you donate?” she asked. “25 grand, just a drop in the bucket for us.” I replied as I cooked the food. I poked at the bacon with my spatula, making it sizzle a bit. The eggs were flipped over, and then the French toast was prepped. “You are such a giving man…” she replied with a smile. Sometimes I like to give back to the place I grew up. I believe that by doing all this, it can in someway repay what my former career had done when I was younger. I hated what I did before, now that I have seen that what I did before was wrong. I now know that I need to try and make the best of my life outside my career. I grabbed a couple of plates from the cupboard, placing some bacon, a couple eggs and two pieces of French toast on each plate. I slide the plate over to Kristina, who was all smiles for it. She grabbed the syrup, butter and then her utensils and started to eat. “Are you sure this is all smart, I mean all this globe-trotting and all these matches?” She said as she ate some bacon. “This cannot be good on your body; it has to be physically draining. Even though she had a point, I knew that this was for the best I needed to get WMW noticed as much as possible. Soul Purpose is a former tag team champion and yet when we applied for the tag team division people were like “who?”, but I have a plan to make everyone in the PTC make notice of us. Whether it is in the PTC tag team division and this team called the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean or in WWW. We will make everyone stand up and take notice, we are the premier team in the industry, sure I lost to Baller and Dean, but that happens I could not get there in time to break up the pin attempt. In addition, even though this is more like a world wide tour of wrestling feds, I plan getting our name out there in whatever way possible. The pirates will be plundered during this match, we will reign supreme over this team that is not anywhere near our class. I sat across from Kristina, eating my food and reading the morning paper, but then something happened… ***The Doorbell Rings*** “Who could be here now?” I asked as I sighed and then walked to the door. I slowly opened the door, and when I saw what was there I was truly disappointed…it was another solicitor…. “Hello, I represent the ACME vacuum company of Walla Walla, Washington. Are you the man of the house?” the salesman asked. “Yeah, that would be me.” I said as I rolled my eyes. “Great, great if I could just have a moment of your time…” He asked as he opened his case. I grabbed my cell phone out of my pocket, and then dialed the number of my security chief. “Hello?” the voice replied I could here the voice of a female in the background. “Yeah, boss…” TJ replied. “If you value your job, you will get over to my main gate and out of the guest house and get rid of this salesman…TODAY!!” I said as I turned off the phone. The man was still going strong as he was going through his sales pitch, even though I was not going to buy anything. I stood there in silence, almost in a trance from what the guy was saying. I was amazed at how he could just go on and on about a damn vacuum cleaner. I saw TJ finally making his way to the door, and a large smile slithered across my face. “So good sir, how many can I put you down for?” he said as he finally looked up. “Maybe you can ask my head of security?” I said as I pointed toward the large man walking towards him. “Want me to rough him up a bit boss?” TJ asked as he grabbed the man by his shirt collar. “No, just show him the way out and the sign that says “Private Property” on the way out. You can do that right TJ?” I asked as I watched a scantily clad young lady walking out of the guest house and into her car. “Yes sir, I can do that…” TJ replied as he walked away with the salesman. I walked back inside, closing the door behind me. I looked around and saw that Kristina was getting into the hot tub. It actually sounded like a great idea, good enough to join her. “Care for some company?” I said as I stepped in the tub with her. “I was hoping you would join me.” She replied. We sat there in each others arms, almost getting to the point where we were going to have a little afternoon delight if you know what I mean…and then… “Hello, I am selling Girl Scout Cookies and I was wondering if I could get you to buy some of my delicious cookies?” a small voice was heard from the side of the hot tub. I leaned over the side and saw a small girl, dressed just like the Girl Scouts that everyone has seen from one time to another. I sighed, as this was becoming a really bad day as far as people selling stuff go. “Does my security even work today, or is this just that kind of day?” I asked Kristina. “Actually I gave TJ a box of Tagalongs and he let me in…” the little girl replied. {The camera cuts out to TJ sitting in his security office eating a box of cookies.} “He is so close to being fired. God, like I need this…” I mumbled to myself as I looked over at Kristina. “She’s a cutie, help her and maybe we can get a little closer if you know what I mean?” Kristina replied. She smiled seductively and then slides her foot between my thighs which got my attention… “Ok, what about 5 of everything?” I said, again reaching for my coat and producing my now angry checkbook. “Really?” the girl asked. I nodded in agreement as I started writing the check. I handed her a check and then filled out all the needed info. She smiled and then kissed me on the cheek. “Thanx, Mr. Hanson…” she replied as she tucked the check into her money pouch. The little girl walked away with a huge smile on her face. I guess I did something nice, but it was still the middle of the day and already had visits from way to many people. Now my cell was buzzing… “Hello?” I asked. “This is Candy…we have a problem at the club….” She replied. “What kind of problem?” I asked as I grabbed my glasses. “The musical group for tonight canceled.” She replied. That was terrible news, could not have come at a worse time either. I have to really scramble to see what I can do to fix this before the club opens for business tonight. “Hmmm, ok Candy thanx I can handle it from here.” I replied. “I will have a new act in the house soon, do not worry.” “Ok, thanx boss…” she exclaimed as if a huge burden had been lifted off her back. Kristina looked at me, she looked concerned. “You ok?” she asked. “The band for tonight canceled, I need to find a replacement soon.” I replied, I was already trying to think of a replacement. “What about your friend Fred Durst he still does the band Limp Bizkit, right?” Kristina replied. “That is a great idea, he owes me a favor and this is the time to collect.” I replied as I grabbed my cell phone and dialed the number. “Yeallo?” the voice replied. “Hey Fred? This is Noah, what up man?” I asked. “What can I do for you old friend?” Freddy asked. “I need a favor, I need you to come over and play at my club in KC? Can you do it?” I asked. “Sure buddy, when do we need to be there?” he asked. “I will send the private jet to pick you up this afternoon. And you will have everything paid for by me.” I replied. “Cool…” he replied as I could hear the others talking in the background. “Just be ready and be ready for a packed house tonight!” I replied. “And I will meet you at the airport.” “You got it, Noah.” Fred replied. “And thanx Fred now I owe you!” I remarked. “Anytime man.” He replied and then I hung up the phone. Well I had fixed a few things, but I still needed to get things straightened away for my match on Fear and for that Inter-fed match with the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean. I love working hard, but sometimes things can get out of hand and I can get overloaded with work for myself. “Are you sure, you can handle all this?” Kristina asked a concerned look growing on her face was replacing a smile. “The only people that need to be concerned are the ones that are going to be in the ring with me, simple as that….”
