Interlude -The End of Ends

23 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-23 Present day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle is kneeling by a smaller grave. You read the name on it, “Lotus Blossom St. Croix-Roberts. He places a rose on the top of her grave and then kisses the stone. He begins to speak again,] Syckle: Anubis had transported me to the inside of a burial chamber underneath a pyramid. I pulled my little flashlight out of my pocket and looked around. I saw sand that had been marked with Hieroglyphs. I couldn’t understand what they were saying so I moved on. I found an unlit torch on the wall. I grabbed it so that I could light it but as my hand closed around the hilt the torch lit by itself. The one by one torches on either side of a long corridor lit. I walked down and when I found the end I glanced around and saw that I had entered a room with a large stone tomb. I studied the tomb. On the cover were more hieroglyphs. I couldn’t translate them. I decided then to open the tomb. That was when I heard Anubis. * * * Burial Chamber A few years ago [Anubis steps in from the darkness. He is waging a finger and smiles a bit,] Anubis: No, No, No, I wouldn’t do that, not yet anyway. William: And why not? Anubis: Because you could break the whole key to the puzzle. William: You mean this slab? Anubis: Yes, William. William: A lot of good that does me. Anubis: Oh that’s right you can’t read hieroglyphs can you? [William stands, and lets a deep breath go,] William: Anubis, all you have been doing for years is playing games with me. I won’t play twenty questions anymore. Either tell me what is going on or leave me alone and reverse what ever you have turned me into. [Anubis studies William with a straight face. William returns his gaze unblinking. It was Anubis, who speaks first,] Anubis: Very well William. You have been patient, very patient. But I ask that you, as you put it, play one more game. William: And that would be? Anubis: You must answer my riddle. If you answer the riddle correctly I shall make it so that you can read what is on that tablet. If you answer incorrectly then your questions shall remained unanswered for the rest of your life. William: What is your riddle? Anubis: In the village of the blind this man is king after the water flooded the village when the “d” fell off repeatedly and all of his flock couldn’t breed when the sheep and lamb were washed away. When you put these clues together minus one, the answer to all of your questions will be revealed. [William looked at Anubis with a cocked eyebrow,] William: Okay that made no sense but neither does any of this. The first one is easy. In the village of the blind the one eyed man is king. If a village flooded that means the dam broke. If his flock couldn’t breed because the sheep and the lamb were gone that would leave the ewes left. So the parts that were left were one eyed, dam, and ewe. Wait I forgot a part. The “d” fell off repeatedly. So if I were to take off the d’s that would leave me with one eye am ewe. That still doesn’t make sense. Oh damn it of course when you put all the clues together minus one, so if I take away one. That leaves eye am ewe. [Anubis smiles,] William: I am you! Anubis: Correction, I am you. William: You have to be joking. Anubis: You don’t believe me? Look at the tablet. [William looks at the tablet and it laid out his whole history. William looked up at Anubis,] Anubis: I see there is still some doubt left in your eyes then open the burial chamber and all shall be remembered. [William looks at Anubis and then back at the tomb. William then opens the tomb by pushing the tablet over. William looks up at Anubis and then reaches into the tomb and pulls out a white mask. William holds it up to the torch light. The white mask was ornate and ceremonial. Around the right eye was a spiral design colored in pure black. William turned to Anubis,] William: I am supposed to believe that both you and I are the same person and this mask is supposed to clear up any loose ends? Anubis: Yes, but if you don’t believe me put it on. You have nothing to loose and the world to gain. [William shakes his head as he moves the mask to his face,] William: Some days I should have stayed in bed. [William’s head snaps back and everything that was blocked was unlocked. Memories and sights came back to him. The infinite knowledge of the guardian of the graveyard flooded his mind. The last thing William hears before he blacks out is the voice of Anubis,] Anubis: William I am you and you are me. But the world will come to know us buy another name. A name that shall live up to the darkness inside you and a name that will cut through to souls with crimson precision. That name is Syckle and your symbol shall be that of the dark horse. With the powers of Anubis at your finger tips go forth and punish the guilty and protect the innocent, and issue your judgment with the decisiveness of the Scales of Truth. [William now stands in a uniform of a black leather pants, black sleeveless mesh shirt with a coat of arms of a silver shield, a black cross sits in the middle of the shield and has crossed sickles in the back of the shield. William also wears a long black leather hooded duster. Syckle’s face turned stark white and had black tribal lines around his eyes and mouth. Syckle wears black gloves and as he steps forward we catch a glimpse of a long barreled, onyx handled, six shooter. William raises his right hand and a black void opens up. Syckle steps forward into the void and leaves the chamber.] * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery Syckle: For the next few years I hunted down and killed the demons and sent the criminals to jail. Vertigo Heights was a safe city again. Some liked that I was there but there was more opposition. Many wanted to see me gone because they thought of me as a vigilante or a monster. If I showed up at a crime scene police would more then likely start to shoot at me. Detective Jennifer Lee and I learned to trust each other and when she succeeded her father as Detective Inspector she came to me when she felt that a certain case was out of her purview or hit a brick wall. One case in particular was about a string of museum robberies up and down the east coast. There was no sign of forced entry, there was nothing on the security cameras, and the thief seemed to have a taste for things to do with female mythological figures. Detective Lee called me and told me that the Vertigo Heights Metropolitan Museum was going to have an exhibit dedicated to the Egyptian goddess Bast. * * * The Abbey A few years ago [Syckle sits in The Abbey, a series of three hidden rooms on the Roberts’ Estate. The three rooms consist of library full of books on the occult and stocked with occult paraphernalia. The second room was where Syckle kept his weapons here, everything from guns and ammo to swords and shields. The third room is where Syckle sits now in a high back leather chair. In front of him is a bank of monitors. He stares at the large one that sits in the middle, occasionally glancing at one of the other ten monitors that surround it. To his right a phone rings. Syckle leans forward and presses the speaker button. Detective Lee speaks from the other end,] Lee: Syckle. [Syckle leans back and continues his vigil of the monitors,] Syckle: Good evening, Detective Lee. What can I do for you? Lee: I just got off the phone with an informant. He said that we might want to keeps our eyes and ears open. Syckle: About what? Lee: Up and down the eastern seaboard museums have been targeted and items have been stolen. Syckle: What type of items? Lee: That is one of the peculiar things. The artifacts stolen are from exhibits featuring female mythological beings. Syckle: What has been stolen? Lee: Well there have been five robberies so far. There was a bow and arrow from an Artemis exhibit, a set of sais from an Isis one, a pendant from an Aphrodite show, a box that was featured in a Hera one, and a cloak from the Nephthys exhibit. Syckle: And seeing how we are about to have an exhibit of Bast right here in our own backyard. Lee: Exactly. Syckle: Alright Lee you have caught my attention. I’ll look into it and keep you informed. Lee: Thank you. * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle throws a few more pieces of wood onto the fire and it surges,] Syckle: For the rest of that day I studied the other five robberies. The only detail that became concrete was that the thief was a woman. As to her motives and such I had no clue. But I would be there that night when the robbery would to take place. I went to the museum around 11 pm. I knew that the thief would strike around midnight. That is when the night guards are replaced with overnight guards. Once I looked over the items and narrowed then down to one. I staked out a position and just waited. As if on cue the clock struck midnight and I watched as a figure dropped from the ceiling and picked up the small golden statue of a cat that was encrusted with rubies. I watched her put it in a black bag. That was when I came out of the shadows. * * * Vertigo Heights Metropolitan Museum A few years ago Syckle: If you put that back now. I’ll let you go quietly and in peace. Try to take it and you leave in peace if not pieces. Your choice. [Syckle has his hand on figure’s arm. The woman looks at him through her mask. She twirls out of his grasp and back hands him. Syckle’s head snaps to the side but then he comes back with a smile on his face,] Syckle: I was hoping you would choose the latter. Let’s rock and roll. Woman: Fine by me. [The woman attacked with a straight kick. Syckle blocked and wiped her to the floor with a sweep. The woman hits Syckle with a forearm. Then she backflips out and Syckle stands. He smiles and then runs to attack with a fist. The woman side steps and Syckle puts on the breaks. He manages grab a hold of her mask and pull it off and that is when he stops,] Syckle: Deanna! [The woman stops at the sound of her name being said. She faces the man and stares at him with her violet eyes. They lit up as she recognized the man behind the green eyes,] Deanna: William! Syckle: I should have known this was your handy work. You always did have a fascination with mythology. Deanna: So what now? Syckle: That’s up to you. If you return the statue then there is no problem here but if you feel the need to climb the rope you came in on then I’ll have to hand you over to the authorities. [Deanna put the statue you back and then she and Syckle left the museum through the skylight.] * * * Present day Campfire in the Cemetery [Syckle sits with a small smile on his face,] Syckle: Deanna and I go back a few years. She and I had a very short lived wild relationship. After awhile we drifted apart only to find ourselves staring at each that night. After we left the museum we went back to the Roberts Estate and brought up old times while downing a few bottles of wine. Through a mix of old memories and too much wine we ended up in bed. When I woke up from that night of passion I saw a note on my pillow that said only “sorry”. I turned on the TV and on the news there was report that a small golden statue of the goddess Bast was stolen. I just leaned back into my pillow and laughed. I had been beaten at my own game. A few months later I was sitting in the Abbey taking up my nightly vigil when I heard a call over the police scanner. The scanner had said that a murder had just occurred. It was in the Riverview section of Vertigo Heights. I sped off and then got a call on my cell phone from Lee. * * * Syckle’s car A few years ago [Syckle sped along the night in his black Porsche. His cell phone rang,] Syckle: Go ahead. Lee: Syckle this is Lee. I have something that I want you to come take a look at. Syckle: Where? Lee: Riverview. It is a pretty grizzly murder and it’s him again. We caught a break though. Syckle: I’m heading that way now. I heard it over the scanner. I’m pulling into Riverview now. I’ll be there in a few minutes. [Syckle pulls up into the parking lot of a three story apartment building. He followed the trail of cops to the top right corner of the building. He found Detective Lee standing outside the apartment door. She led him in and then out to the balcony where a woman was hanging from the railing by her ankles,] Lee: Her name is Sarah Noir. She is thirty-seven, single mother, and a retired model. Syckle: Coroner’s on scene evaluation? Lee: She was killed by three stabs around her heart that severed all the main arteries. Syckle: On the phone you said that “he” had struck again. I assume that there was two quarters found under her tongue. Lee: Same as the other three. Syckle: The child lives with the mother? Lee: Yes and it is a daughter. Her name is Lillian. Syckle: Okay, where is Lillian? Lee: We couldn’t find her. Syckle: Any signs of kidnapping? Lee: It is a possibility but there was no ransom note left or calls with demands made. Syckle: I’m going to go out on a limb and say that she wasn’t kidnapped but that she is hiding somewhere nearby. Lee: My men have comb a ten block radius and we haven’t found her. Syckle: You just have to know where to look detective. Lee: Anything else that you can tell me? Syckle: The Charon Killer is definitely trying to make himself stand out but he is smart. He knew where to cut. He is probably out of the city by now. I would see if there have been any other murders like this. I’ll go search for the kid. When I find her I’ll send her to you. Then I would like for you to send her to a friend of mine. He’ll take good care of her. I’ll send his name and number along with her. Lee: Alright, thanks. [Syckle takes off to go look for Lillian,] * * * Present day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle pushed the fire around with a stick,] Syckle: Lillian’s mother had been killed by a man who the press dubbed the “Charon killer”. He struck the same every time. Three cuts around the heart that severed the main arteries and then he stuck two quarters underneath the deceased tongue. In Greek mythology Charon was the river man that took souls across the river Styx. When a person died the family put money on their eyelids so as they passed on they would have money to pay Charon. Once the soul was brought to the other side they would put the two bits under Charon’s tongue. He was secondary to the child Lillian at the time. I found the girl three hours later hiding up on her roof. I manage to talk her into going to see detective Lee. She told all she could to Lee and then Lee called the number I had given her. The number was to me. I told her that Syckle had informed me on the situation and that I would be happy to take Lillian in. Lillian herself was fifteen and had long white hair with red streaks in it. She had brown eyes and a bad temper if you got onto her bad side. As the next few months went by and Lillian came to call my house her new home. When Lillian turned sixteen both of our lives started to change. First of all I had stepped back into the wrestling world this time as member of the 4WW. Then I had come to find out that half the roster turned out to be family. The first member of the family was Apocolypse. It turns out the she was my half sister. We both had the same mother but different father. The other members of the family centered around Patti Anderson. Patti and her two sisters Melinda and Caitlin were my great grand nieces. Aphrodite, my sister sired a child by the name of Eros (cupid) who married a nymph named Psyche. Together they had a son named Minos who married three different women. From those three marriages came Caitlin, Melinda, and Patti herself. At the time when I found this out Patti and I weren’t seeing eye to eye. But I have since put aside my differences with her and she has earned a grudging respect from me. But through all of this my world was about to take another turn for the worse. It was after a show we did in North Carolina that I got a call from Hammer Bay Mercy Hospital. Hammer Bay was the next city over from Vertigo Heights. But the call was from a nurse saying that my wife had just been brought in. They said she had a miscarriage due to severe trauma to the stomach. I asked the nurse what my wife’s name was and she had said it was Deanna St. Croix. I hung up the phone and came to Hammer Bay. I was there when Deanna awoke and told me everything. A purse snatcher had tried to steal her purse as she was coming out of a movie. She chased him down and cornered him in the alley. That was when she said that a stone around his neck started to glow bright black and then kicked her to the ground. She felt him kick her again in the stomach and the she heard him call himself “The Dreammaker.” Once Deanna was healthy enough I brought her back to the Roberts Estate and helped her through the pain and she did the same. You see Deanna was carrying my child. A little daughter she was going to call Lotus. I used every resource I had with in my power but I could find nothing on “The Dreammaker”. That was until a few months ago when the Dreammaker came looking for me. [Syckle’s hand began to clench into fists as he repeated the name,] Syckle: The Dreammaker was a spirit that was imprisoned in an amulet. The only way that the Dreammaker could come out was to have a host wear the amulet and he would then transfer their soul into the amulet and his soul would enter the body. The Dreammaker had taken over my rival “Superstar” Eddie B. Eddie surrounded himself with a cult and sent one after one to try and stop me from getting my revenge on him. But Eddie soon fell under the crushing weight of my boot. The Dreammaker survived and I have no idea where he is now or who it inhabits. There is no telling whether he will be back or not. To celebrate our victory over the Dreammaker Deanna who had taken the name Black Orchid and I had exchanged wedding vows. But before I could do that with her I had to come here and do one thing. * * * Lourdes’ Grave Six months ago [Syckle pulls up to the gates of a graveyard and gets out of his car. He carries a single rose to a grave that sits high upon a hill surrounded by three smaller graves. The name on the grave is “Lourdes Annabelle Roberts”. This was William’s first wife. He stood silent for a moment and let the wind whip his white hair around his face and his large black leather duster around his body. His white mask seem to float in the air as he spoke to her grave,] Syckle: Hello Lordy…it has been awhile since I came here. But you said a long time ago…things change and people change. Things have changed and the people in my life have changed. But I had to come here and tell something. Lourdes I have always tried to change things because I lied to myself. That’s why I wore this mask. [He takes of the mask and looks at it,] Syckle: I used it to hide the scars that I never wanted to acknowledge were there. If I showed the scars it would be seen as a sign of weakness. I had to be the strongest man in the world if I was to dole out justice. In reality hiding my scars made me no better than the criminals and demons alike that I have run into. I think that is what you were trying to tell me so long ago. The strongest man in the world is the one that admits he is weak. I admit that I was weak but now I know that I am strong for the simple fact that I have something to fight for again…I have Orchid to fight for. [Syckle takes the mask and drops it on the ground. Then he smashed it under his boots. He stares at the pieces and then raises a hand to his face and it changes from human features to one of stark white with inlaid black lines,] Syckle: I now bare the scars that are meant to define me and my existence in this realm of Mortalis. I have no need to hide behind a mask. With the passing of the mask I can finally let the past lie where it belongs…in pieces of memories. [Syckle kisses the rose and drops it on the ground next to her grave,] Syckle: I will always love you Lourdes but our legacy ends here and now. The legacy of Orchid and I now begins. Good bye Lourdes. * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle looks back over to the grave of Lourdes and then looks directly at you,] Syckle: It was only three weeks ago that Orchid and I were able to take our honeymoon. It was a working one for me but we made time for one conversation as we stood atop Notre Dame in Paris. * * * Three weeks ago Paris, France [Syckle and Orchid walk into the Cathedral of Notre Dame and climb up to the highest point. The night air is crisp and the moon is on the rise. They stare at the gothic architecture. Syckle stands next to a large stone cross in a large black hooded leather duster. The jacket covers the black sheer sleeveless shirt and black pants. Around his neck is a black choker. On his hands are black gloves. Orchid lounges like a cat by his side stretching in her skintight black vinyl pants and black knee high boots. Her black hair is pulled back into a sleek pony tail and around her neck is a black choker with steel ring for a centerpiece. She also wears black gloves. A black vest with silver studded buttons finishes off this fashion plate. Syckle speaks to no one in particular,] Syckle: I have spent this journey walking and coming around full circle. I have had to travel through the worst part of me to get to where I first started. Some might look at what I have done and scolded me for wasting time and getting lost only to end back at where I started. Others my just simply laugh and point. Certain doctors would say that I am trapped in a vicious circle. I used to think that way but now all I can do is think of it as a second chance. A chance to get things right from a new beginning. A new beginning built on acceptance. Acceptance of both sides of the coin. Acceptance of both William Roberts and Syckle. I have made the journey as both men separately with bitter hatred toward each other and all it has led me to was death. Not mine but those around me. I have also learned to accept the there are fights, people, and things worth fighting for and that there are wars that will lead to nothing in the end. The three most important lessons of acceptance that I have learned are that no matter how well you build up personal, mental, or emotional walls someone will get in and knock them down. There are some things that we have no control over…period. The final lesson is that there is no time limit to acceptance. Acceptance is a lesson that goes on…forever. [Orchid laughs a little and Syckle looks down to her and cocks an eyebrow,] Syckle: What’s so funny? Orchid: What you just said reminds me of something I was told a long time ago by my father. Syckle: That was? Orchid: A host of grotesque stone figures peered down out of the darkness from the ledge that supported the vaulted ceilings and various niches set high in the walls. They took on many shapes and forms, some recognizable as men or beasts, some a combination of both or a strange distortion of natural forms, the invention of some mad artist's sacrilegious imagination. Figures with the wings of eagles or bats mounted the ledges as if preparing to spring. In the flickering candlelight the lifeless beasts seemed to stir in writhe. Their lidless eyes, chipped and cracked, lined with dust, followed him as he stepped onto the balcony. In a voice deep and hushed, sepulchral and cruel, now emanated from the man as he spoke among the gargoyles... Syckle: Abandon hope all ye who enter here. * * * Present Day Campfire in the cemetery [Syckle stands and looks at the full moon in the sky and closes his eyes. He looks back at you and then extends his hand to help you to a standing position,] Syckle: There is still one more place we must go before this story ends. [Syckle extends his hand again and in a brilliant green flash something appears in his hand. It was series of metallic orbs strung together with thick rope. Syckle throws the orbs to the right of him and then hover vertically in mid air. They start to spin and lightning crackles around them. With in the circle of metal orbs a void is opening and Syckle steps through it. Taking a deep breath you step through the void too.] * * * Present Day Seven of the Best Ring [You stand with Syckle in the Seven of the Best ring. He looks around and then stands in middle of the ring. He stares at the S.O.B. logo and then smiles,] Syckle: Everything leads to here. It leads to the Primetime Central Seven of the Best tournament. Where fourteen men compete for the number one contendership of the Global Title. Fourteen men enter the brackets and the winning seven compete in an over the top battle royal. The one person left standing in the middle of the ring will be the number one contender for the Global Title. No one in this tournament knows who I am and what I am capable of. But you see I go into this tournament for a very different reason then the other thirteen. I go into this tournament representing the 4WW. I am going into this tournament not to win some belt but to show just exactly what the men and women from the 4 Winds can and will do. I will stop at nothing to blaze a path to that number one contendership if not for any other reason to make the 4WW the most dominate federation in this mother company. You have seen the Darek DeAngleo’s and the Wesley Williams of the federation but you haven’t seen anything like me. I will knock you out like the proverbial Mack truck. There is no stopping this dark horse from showing and proving to the world that if you challenge the 4 Winds you are playing with fire. But I’m not only a man of words I am man of actions as well. [Syckle reaches under his duster and pulls out his six shooter. He pushes out the chamber and then spins it. He then locks it in place and then takes aim at the S.O.B. logo,] Syckle: John Adams from LOW will fall and then when it comes time for the over the top. Let the countdown begin. [Syckle squeezes the trigger,] [BANG!] Syckle: 6… [BANG!] Syckle: 5… [BANG!] Syckle: 4… [BANG!] Syckle: 3… [BANG!] Syckle: 2… [BANG!] Syckle: 1… [CLICK! CLICK! CLICK!] Syckle: Six people will be thrown over the top even if I have to do it single handedly. Because there will only be one left. That one will be me. On this mat it says S.O.B. which stands for “Seven of the Best” but it will stand for “Son of a Bitch.” I am going to be the only S.O.B. that is left standing in this ring and then I’ll be the Son of a bitch that takes the Global Title to the 4WW. If you don’t like it then stay out of my way. You want to challenge me on it then step up. Either way there is only one question left to ask! [The lights go out and all you see are a pair of green glowing eyes,] Syckle: ARE YOU READY? [Fade to Black]
