Silent Tears

16 September 2004

Date: 2004-09-16 [ “ Mind Set “ ] Silent Tears. I look into my own eyes, and what do I see? I see the culmination of time and effort…a strand of DNA unlike none other. Tried to blame the teachings of others, but can’t hide the truth…the reality. I always was this type of person. Change? Why would I…there are none other like me. I’m the last of the few, while these others are only dimes in a dozen. Hail the king; the crown worn so proudly that sits atop an emotionless soul of despair not yet known a king that will rise up to take what belongs to him. Black blood pumps through his iced veins. That king is I. I was chosen by God to lead the flock of sheep into a foreseen realm. My goal has been one of disruption, which has led me to believe that there are way too many sheep…and not enough shepherds. This world has become nothing more than a terribly played out horror film. Murder seems like an everyday thing, and they’re starting to hold less and less of a precedence in the news offices world wide. America, living off the marquee of “ Home of the Brave “. But what happens when the brave aren’t as brave as projected. What happens when evidence becomes facts…and the soldiers and leaders that this country host are revealed of their fault. Home of the brave? More like home of the corrupt…because that is all this country has become…a land full of hatred for one another…a land full of torment and destruction under the wing of its fellow man. Being born in the country of Bogota, Columbia…I didn’t have the great life that you people take for granted. I earned my meals by working my fingers to their bones…and sometimes even beyond that. I tried to play the good guy…I tried to duplicate the actions of my savior Jesus Christ and give hope for a better tomorrow, but American’s society has corrupted me with its garbage and all they seem to want to do is brush me under a mat. Well the charades are over…the pretending is over…the acting is done with. Posers will be exposed of their fault…and the truth will be revealed to all of those to blind to recognize it. Time for play is over…for too long have I been the good guy…trying everything to keep my slate clean…trying to remain passive…where has that gotten me, what has it provided for me…nothing but unsighted and Venetian glory. Now I must change…not for the good of anyone else…but for the good of myself. Lets see how America, or better yet the world treats their “ Apostle “ now… [ “ Prelude “ ] Passiveness. The perfect word to describe Dani Furher. Dani began his career in a little city in the country of Columbia. After being recognized by small industries in the U.S.A. , he moved on to bigger and better things. Wrestling for small promotions though gave him little to no glory or fame. He remained Passive throughout the hold process. He remained steadfast and focused on his goal to lead the fellowship of sheep into a new and better realm. Throughout the sturdy years though, his life presented nothing but trials and tribulations…yet he has come into PTC…coming into the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament looking not only to make a name for himself…but to lead by example. To carry the sheep into another era. His passion was one that he hoped would be fulfilled…but knew that as long as this world was under such a malevolence era…hope was really the only thing he could hope for. [ “ Training Mode “ ] Low, Dark, and Menacing. The Metallic surfaces of the gym bristled with protuberances. Dani Furher had never really believed in training after such a vague span of time…He knew only one real wrestling form, and that was freestyle…the style that he originated from. After over two years away from the ring though, he needed every bit of preparation that he could get before the big Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. ’ COME ON KID ! YOU’LL GET NOWHERE WITH THAT ! ‘ It was an instantly captivating, indefinable quality that combined the aggression of trainer Rhett Mcnamara and the will of the young Dani Furher that created the passion. “ Glory “ would be the word, he supposed. Rhett, by contrast, spiked black hair cropped short, crumpled Black Tee Shirt draped awkwardly over his broad figure. He also sported black contoured gym shorts that enhanced his muscular proportion of leg. Dani laid flat on his back in the middle of the mat, staring up at the laser heads, control consoles, and other equipments that made up the ceiling of the custom constructed “ Dynasty Center “. Mcnamara had just given Dani a sign of things to come with a huge monkey flip. ’ GET UP FURHER ! SHOW ME WHAT YOU REALLY GOT ! ‘ The screams petrified him to the point where he shot up off the mat and met Rhett face to face in the middle of the ring. He knew that ring rust would come into play soon…but he didn’t know it would be this soon. ’ Now, were going to work a little on your right hands…because you can’t get down the more advanced moves if you can’t get down the basics first. Now, show me what you got…hit me right here in the arm with your best shot. ‘ Dani took full pledge of the command, drew his arm back, and delivered a big shot to the arm of his trainer. Rhett stumble only a tad, but then shot a look at the face of Dani that could and would strike fear into the hearts of even the most bravest and passive of men. ‘ Please tell me that wasn’t your best shot. If that was your best shot we might as well stop now because you won’t even make it the first five minutes against Hoyt Williams. Maybe you haven’t got it through your thick skull yet…you’ve got one of the biggest challenges in the first round against Hoyt Williams…now quit acting and FIGHT LIKE A MAN ! ‘ Though distorted about the way that he was coming at me, He couldn’t help but agree with him. Hoyt Williams was one of the projected winners to win the entire tournament…he couldn’t screw this up or it could be lights out for his career. ’ Listen up because I’m only saying this once. Draw your fist back and push with a forward motion crunching your knuckles upon impact. Now try it and do it right this time. ‘ He took a deep breath and focused on the tips he had provided for him. He took a deep gaze at him, drew his arm back, pushed in a forward motion, but forgot to crunch his knuckles upon impact. He became suddenly nervous…not knowing what to expect from the already pissed veteran. ’ Close…but not close enough. Try it again…see if you can knock me down. ‘ This time he was focused. He drew his arm back as forcefully as his arm would possibly allow him…He then proceeded to push forward, this time crunching his knuckles upon impact and… BAM ! A shot so hard and so big that spit went shooting from his mouth and crashing down to the mat with a big splat. Mcnamara laid flat out cold on his back in the center of the mat, eyes closed. Dani stood waiting for Rhett to stand and give him applause for his big hit. He waited for a while, a little while longer…but Rhett only laid there without an inch of movement. Finally the young Dani Furher realized that something was wrong as he knelt down beside the fallen Veteran and began a cry for help. ’ HELP ! SOMEONE GET IN HERE ! Obviously no one was present in the gym in order to hear his cries. He quickly ran over to his gym bag on the outside of the ring and pulled out his cell phone. He dialed 911 and began to cry out for help. ’ Help…I need help, something is wrong with my trainer Rhett Mcnamara and he isn’t moving. [detail withheld]….please hurry. ‘ He threw the phone down and slid back into the ring and knelt down beside Rhett once again, trying to revive him…but to no avail. He placed his hand over the heart of Rhett and felt no heartbeat…that’s when the tarnished and degraded thoughts began to rush through his head…what if he had caused this man’s career to suddenly vanish. What if he had caused him sudden health problems…but the worse of them all. What if he had killed him. [ “ The Aftermath “ ] He paced back and forth through the waiting room of the First Baptist hospital. He didn’t know what to think…he had always tried to remain passive and not think the worst of things…but the thoughts couldn’t help but swarm throughout his head. Finally, his pacing was brought to a sudden halt as a doctor entered the waiting room. He stopped in front of Dani, clipboard in hand, holding his head down as if the announcement that he was preparing himself to make would not be one of celebration or happiness. This sent a wave of worry through the inner pit of Dani’s stomach. ’ Doctor…what’s wrong with him? Is he okay? ‘ ‘ I’m afraid I have some terrible news. We’ve lost him. Rhett Mcnamara Is dead. ‘ Isolation. That’s what he felt as those words reached his ear. He couldn’t believe this…the man that very well brought him into wrestling…the man that dedicated his life to teaching him and leading him to become a better wrestler…and now he was gone, gone all in a single punch. But then he thought to myself…was it really his punch that killed him? Did he kill Rhett Mcnamara? He turned away from the doctor and began to slowly make his way out of the door, head hung down. He reached the door and took a final look at the doctor as a single tear rolled down the cheek of his face. He proceeded to walk out of the door knowing that he had been torn mentally. He made his way out of the Hospital and into the parking lot where he had parked his car. He reached the car but as he did, he was wrestled unto the hood of the car. ’ Keep your hands down ! ‘ That was the only thing he heard as he looked over and saw two policemen handcuffing his wrist together. He was afraid for life…He didn’t know what was going on or why it was going on. Panic was the only factor that introduced itself at this moment of time. ’ You have the right to remain silent…anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law.’ Those were the last words he heard before being shoved into the back of the police car. His heart pounded like a hammer beating a nail. He didn’t know where to go, what to do, who to turn to…his Passive state of mind had been corrupt. He was afraid. [ “ Meeting Your Match “ ] ' I need to call you back, I have a very special client to attend to' He sat in the brown leather chair, feet softly tapping the wooden board floor in, nervous as ever. He still could not understand the reasoning for his being here...but there was one thing that he did understand, and that was the fact that he wasn't here for anything relating to good intentions. He sat in a small room with a security guard on his left side. In the room sat an oak wooden desk in the middle, and two leather chairs that sat in front and behind the desk. The lawyer held a cell phone to his ear as he slowly turned around, hung up the cell phone, and looked into Dani's burning eyes. He gave somewhat of a devious look expressed on his face. He flipped through the provided files on the desk, still with that same smile on his face, and then looked at Dani. ' Its obvious from the confused look on your face that you have forgotten who I am. ' He was right...the look on Dani's face was one of confusion...but became one of even more confusion as those words were spoken. Dani began to backtrack his thoughts, trying to picture the face of the lawyer in his head...but the pieces to the picture just wouldn't come together...he didn't know who this man was. ' Maybe the name Simon Kershaw rings bell. Yea thats right its me...former owner of Souled Out Wrestling and the man that put your father in prison. ' Everything suddenly became so clear for Dani. He now knew exactly who the man was....and that was enough to make him want to jump out of seat and beat the living hell out of him. He knew though, that would only present more problems. ' Why am I here Kershaw ' ' Its quite simple Furher...you were the one that punched Rhett Mcnamara..and after that punch, he never took a breath again. Dani...you killed Rhett Mcnamara...You're a murder. ' He felt the very depth of his soul drop down like a weightball into the pit of his stomach. Mixed emotions of Disturbed appealing Confusion are what he felt. If he would have thought about such things, there would have been no reason for him to be shocked nor surprised. He didn't want to believe the obvious fact that truth relied in this, instead he pushed it to the back of his mind in the illogical way that people do inconvenient truths, as if that might somehow change things. Still remaining motionless, he directed his gaze slowly over the near surroundings, looking for any possible escape route out of this hole that he had dug himself into. It seemed as if the walls of the already minimum sized room were among sinous growths, looming closer and closer in on him as if in attempt to trap him in this perdicament. ' You know Dani...I hate your father...that why I pulled out all the stops to make sure that he ended his life in prison...but I don't feel my work is done, YOU are going to become my sacrificial lamb for revenge against your father. Maybe you don't know this...but you have been subjected to life in prison...and don't think for a second that I am going to aid any cause to stop that from happening. ' That struck the hanging nerve in Dani as he began to leap out of his chair at the devious man, but he was quickly restained by the security guard. He squeezed the arm of the chair in absolute rage. He was livid and that was something that had become very evident. ' There is one thing you can do to keep you out of jail though Furher. ' ' And whats that ' ' Its very simple. You're in the big GTT4 tournament...and seeing that your father owed me bundles of money...I have come up with a way for him to pay me back through you. Your sentencing states that you will be able to fight in the tournament no matter what the outcome is of your trial, so I can't stop you from entering. But You've got Hoyt Williams in round one...I am going to be betting big dollars on Hoyt Williams meaning I want YOU to lay down for Hoyt and lose...therefore ensuring me my money and keeping you out of jail. I mean its an easy choice Dani...everyone knows you're not experienced enough, nor are you old enough to defeat such a well built athlete as furher...you're going to lose anyways...so why don't you just make the smart choice....go the right way....MY way. ' Dani had ramped thoughts running through his head...three things at once. One being the fact that he did indeed want to stay as far away from prison as I possibly could. Another being that he wanted to indeed win GTT4 and make his name known the world over. And the third being that he wanted to jump out of his chair and beat that prick to a bloody pulp. ' So whats it going to be Furher? ' ' Take you offer and shove it Kershaw...I wouldn't piss on you if you were on fire. ' Kershaw gave Dani a look of pure madness as if he wanted to shoot him on the spot for saying that simple word....no. Instead though, he forced a cocky smile unto his face. ' Very well then...you want to play rough...well you get what you ask for. Guards ! LOCK HIM UP ! ' Dani began to sweat nervously as the guard forced him out of his chair and forced his hands behind his back. Before the guard could capture Dani's other hand though, Dani hit the guard in the face releasing the guards hold. The guard grunted in anger, drew out his nightstick quickly and clocked Dani in the temple with it. He than began to beat Dani over and over again with the nightstick before taking his hands and locking them in the handcuffs. He forced Dani up to reveal blood dripping from his face. He began to drag the lifeless Dani Furher out of the room but before he got him out fully, Kershaw walked over and said his last final words to his enemy. ' Play with fire kid...and you'll get BURNED ' [ “ Mental Destruction “ ] There Dani sat on the bottom bunk of his cell. Guards stood everywhere surrounding the cell so there was no possible escape. He held his head down, wounds still present from the brutal attack...a single tear rolled down his cheek. He held out his fist and stared deep at them...fear. He had killed a man with these lethal weapons...he feared that fact that it could happen again. He feared the fact that he could end up being the murderer of many others. He feared the fact that he was a killer. ' Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. A night where superstars will be born, legends will be made, and the best will be crowned. ' ' I have the pleasure of going square to square with the son of Wrestling legend Duke Williams. A projected winner of this contest in Hoyt Williams. ' ' Hoyt...both of us follow the ministry of Christ...but I do it with faith...while you do it only for the laughs of these fans. ' ' You are nothing more than a hypocrite...a failure to society. You are pathetic. ' ' You have yet to be taught of your fault...but in that I will take pleasure in achieving. ' ' I look foward to teaching you the meaning of fear...and why you should never be on the opposite side of it. ' ' One problem I have witnessed in the wrestling world is that their are too many talkers...and not enough fighters. ' ' Too much talking...and not enough fist being thrown. ' ' Its time for a change in this society. ' ' A change that will bring forth hope for a btter tommorrow. ' ' That change should begin with me. ' ' So Hoyt...while you're out, making yourself look like a complete idiot. ' ' I'll be here...preparing myself for war. Bracing myself to show you that I am indeed a force like none other. ' ' No weapon formed against me shall prosper. ' ' Though tribulation presents itself, I still have faith ' ' Faith that I can overcome the battle. ' ' Faith that I can stop the madness. ' ' Faith that I will forever remain.... ' I M M O R T A L To Be Continued
