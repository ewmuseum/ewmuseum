The Flames Of The Heart

9 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-09 (wonm't get time to finish like I'd like so better then nothing." ”We on fire / Up in here / It’s burnin’ hot, we on fire / Just take it off, if it get too hot / Up in this spot, we on fire / Tears the roof off this muhfucka, light the roof on fire / (What you say?) / Tear the roof of this muhfucka…light the roof on FIRE, FIRE, FIRE!”- “Fire” Lloyd Banks There she was in his arms, an innocent angel in the midst of hellfire on earth. Merely moments ago Jamar Gordo found himself having breakfast with Sule, yet a couple of Molotov cocktails later, he found himself at the mercy of searing flames overtaking Vincent Gordo’s prized restaurant. It had been about three or four minutes, the flames now towering to the ceiling of the wooden building. The smoke grew unbearably thick, Jamar barely able to identify anything in front of him. The flames slowly unified to create a solid wall of heat, the Master of All Styles deadly certain that it would take no time at all for this building to collapse if the flames continued. It would have been quite easy to merely surrender to the power of the flames, hoping for mercy or a quick death at the very least. The thought certainly passed through his mind more then once as the walls of fire stood like Greek titans about him. But if he gave up, that would mean he would give up on Sule. In a rare moment in his life, this situation was not just about Jamar. If he quit, it meant that the life of another would suffer the consequences for his choice or lack of choice. A look into Sule’s eyes emerald green eyes, barely open as the thick smoke bombarded them with no remorse, assured Jamar of the path that had to be taken. His life may not have been worth sparing by the divine power above, but she certainly didn’t deserve to perish by default with him. For once in his life, his actions would be totally selfless for another….had Sule gained THAT kind of effect on him? He’d never know if they didn’t survive this blaze first. “Sule, you stay right where you are, don’t move an inch. You hear me,” asked Gordo protectively. She responded with a nod after a loud cough, covering her mouth with hands as she curled up into a fetal position in the midst of the spreading blaze. The Master of All Styles removed himself from atop Sule and got into a crouching stance as he surveyed the area as best as he could, the smoke being a major hindrance in this task. His despair turned into a slight ray of hope as he took sight of what could have been the saving grace….a water pipe in the corner of the dining area. Vincent was never a fan of hiding his water pipes, seeing as how sometimes he had to illegally cut them on, and Jamar was proud of his decision at that moment in time. The flames managed not to spread far enough to block the straight path to the pipe. If he was going to save the day, now was the time. With his adrenaline at its peak and focused, Jamar made a mad dash towards the pipe with the clock ticking against him. The scorching heat made him sweat harder then he had ever experienced, but issues like that mattered not when lives were at stake. The pipe drew closer, his legs pumping battery acid in an effort to get him to it fast enough. !~CRASH~! You didn’t expect it to be that easy for him did you? Of course not. Just mere feet away, a section of the roof above Gordo collapsed violently. The cocktail of debris rained down on the martial artist, trying his best to avoid the small shards, but falling victim to a huge section that smashed into him. The force took Jamar to the ground, pinning him by his back with overbearing weight. The wind has been snatched from his body with that blow, Gordo’s world not just black from the smoke about him, but also from his body failing to keep him conscious at this vital moment. “OH MY GOD JAMAR,” exclaimed Sule in overwhelming fright. Her eyes were locked to the debris besting Jamar the entire time, like the sight of a car crash. She knew she should have turned her head away, but was drawn at the same time by what was going to happen. She could recall telling him minutes ago that his obsession was victory and that he knew nothing of how to act selflessly. It certainly was amazing what life or death situations do to people. He took the ultimate risk and was paying the price right now as the debris continued to impose its power upon Jamar. Overtaken by the situation, Sulie made a move to help, but the erratic flames connected suddenly in front of her. The shock sent her falling to her posterior, now caught in a makeshift figure of flame which grew ever closer to her. A tear managed to escape her eye, despair setting in as she felt the hand of Death begin to encompass her….ready to take her to a castle above the clouds. But she didn’t count on breath still being in Jamar’s body. With breath left, Jamar refused to give up and he certainly did not as he used his unpinned arms to pull his way out of the debris. His muscles pulsated in ungodly strength and pain simultaneously, his fingers digging into the floor as he kept his eyes locked upon the water pipe. This was Jamar versus the flames. Just like most of his vital moments in life, this was merely another fight he would either win or lose. Losing meant the death of Sule, the loss of his mentor’s restaurant, and the loss of his precious time on earth. However, if he could somehow beat this violent opponent…he could live to keep fighting. His teeth grit angrily due to the struggle he was in. He was CONVINCED his next breath would be his last, but if he had to leave this earth, he would go out the way he came in. Fighting. And fight he did, pulling himself forward as he felt loose pieces of metal dragging slightly in his back. He could feel his crimson life blood escaping the back of his body, but refused to stop as he felt his whole upper body escape the debris’ grip. The pipe was ever closer, the sound of collapsing growing obnoxiously louder as Jamar’s struggle continued. His hand reached out for the pipe, his chance at sanctuary in this situation if he wanted to see another waking day. Giving it everything he had at that very moment, Jamar felt his right hand grip the bottom of the wide pipe as slight relief overcame his body. But it was by no means over and he knew it as his other hand caught ahold of the pipe as well. He closed his eyes and dreamed of being anywhere at that moment. A tropical paradise. A world in the clouds. Even back home in his private dojo. Just anywhere but having to survive a raging fire. He pulled as best as he could, his legs FINALLY coming free from the debris as he lay bloodied and battered on the floor trying to regain his strength. He then looked back, catching sight of the predicament that had befallen Sule as the flames looked to embrace her at any moment. There was no more time to waste unless he wanted to be responsible for a dead body. With that, he sluggishly pulled himself up to a vertical base, in a daze from all the inhaled smoke as he set his knee back then smashed it intensely into the pipe. A noticeable dent had been left, but more impact would be needed. It was going to take everything he had at that moment if he hoped to defeat the fire. The question? Did Jamar have enough to give? “If you take me God, take me fast.” Those were the worlds spoken to whatever force could hear him as Jamar backed away from the pipe, giving himself enough space for a huge strike. Taking the shot before he felt himself about to drop, Gordo rushed at the pipe and HURTLED his knee into the pipe, falling back to the floor after the blow was delivered. His eyes fought a battle of their own as they tried to stay open, but they fell to fatigue as Jamar’s world went black all around him. Was that knee enough to spare him more time to live? Or would he meet his maker? Would a fight be one or be lost? Forever to be continued...
