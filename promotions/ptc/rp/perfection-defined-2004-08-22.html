Perfection Defined

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-22 "He takes a three step drop. Givens rushes through the line! Givens! Givens! No, he couldn't get the quarter back. Wait, Summers... Sack! Ladies and gentleman his third sack of the evening." Here I am sitting down on the couch reliving the past shall we say. I decided to take a relaxing seat in my living room on the new leather couch and watch past football games of Boston College. It's heavily raining outside, and there is no way I am getting this body wet. I continue to watch on, I review it with a steady concentration. I see the determination in my eye's as I played linebacker that night. The night of the Championship. Boston College Eagle Vs the Florida Gators. I conquered five sacks that game, I wasn't leaving without the title. I took a quick sip of my water jug, still concentrating on the television set. I've been sitting here for a good hour, just watching this one game. Some would say I am boring, some say I'm nuts for watching this game over and over. Well, this game keeps me moving. I left the Boston College Eagles for a reason. I entered the draft, then dropped out. I was a top twenty prospect waiting to get picked up by any on of them thirty-two professional football teams. I left because I already felt a championship in football, now it's wrestling. "The Perfect Package" Ricky Summers, and they don't call me the Perfect Package because of my under the belt growth. No, I am perfect in everyway. My talent exceeds any others. I hold six High School football records, tow High School Baseball records, and three NCAA records damnit. I am just too damn good. This week, UPWA Vs Neon Wrestling, Vs the great E P Fucking Dubya. I led the Boston College Eagles to the championship, and I'll lead Evolution Pro Wrestling to the championship. "Ladies and gentleman, The Boston College Eagles call for a time out here in the second quarter. Ricky Summers is playing sensational tonight. It's fourteen seventeen Boston College, half time is on it's way." The commentators announced. My eye's are glued to the television set, I watch closely as I slowly continue to sip my water. Watching myself fight for something so important keeps me going. I know at Prime-time Centals Survival Pay-Per-View I will be not only determined, and Intensified, but I will have that ruthless aggression. On the television set it shows myself celebrating after a great three yard loss tackle on the tail back. My stats show up, and all I could do was grin. My era is not done, I will continue to fight for more accomplishments. I may not have the Boston College Eagles as a team. But I do have the EPW. The Phone Rings as I try to ignore it. My eye's won't leave the game. After a few more rings I let out a grudge, then stand up and answer the phone next to the refrigerator in the kitchen. "Yea what is it?" I answered. "Yo man it's John Adams." Adamns replies. "How's it going?" I asked. I was starting to question myself on why "The Prophet" John Adams would be calling me. I took a last sip of my water and continued to conversation. "Good, I'm doing very well. Anyway Summers I wanted to talk to you about the I-PPV." Adamns replied. "All right. Hey don't worry you picked the best man for the job, no doubt." "Yea that's what I was hoping for. You see Ace is also on the team." Adams said. I drop the phone to my chest with a confused look on my face. I beat the hell out of Ace in my debut match. I despise that drunk punk. I figured with him on the team he will probably show up to the event drunk. Therefore leaving my with the championship. "That's not going to be a problem correct?" Adams asked. "What? Well, I hope not. Don't you think Ace should be visiting AA instead of visiting the I-PPV." "No, he is not visiting, he's wrestling. And I want you to make sure nothing goes on at the Survival pay-per-view. If any Evolution Pro Wrestling fights break out I will hold you responsible. Take this team and win like you have done in the past with the Boston College Eagles." Adams said with a determined voice. "Oh no worries about anything John, I have everything under control. UPWA and Neon Wrestling will be two teams not moving on in the tournament. But if Ace shows up half smashed I am not going to let him wrestle." I said. "You see you are not hearing me Summers, Ace is wrestling drunk, wasted, sober it doesn't matter. We need every member of this team fighting. Take it like a war." He said. "John, if he shows up drunk he will ruin the match, he will ruin it for Evolution Pro Wrestling." I replied quickly. "Summers tell me something how good are you?" Adams asked. "Excuse me?" I asked. "You say you are the Perfect Package correct?" "Yes." The mail man walks up to the screen door and drops off the mail. I give a quick wave with a grin, then get back into the conversation with Adams. I couldn't believe what I was hearing. Ace is on the team, this man showed up to every even drunk off his ass. I wrestled the damn man drunk. It's not everyday you can say your first wrestling match was against a drunk little puke. "Well, if you are so damn good, why is Ace being drunk at the event such a problem? Summers if you can't hold this team together, that who can. I you really The Perfect Package, or was Tom Conway right, you are the Perfect Fraud?" Adams said with pity. "Now damnit I am "The Perfect Package" Ricky Summers. But I'm telling you this John, if Ace screws this up for me then I will personally make sure Ace doesn't walk out of the pay-per-view holding playing cards, he will be holding his fingers." I said angrely. I slammed the phone down hanging it up, then got back to my game. I sat on the couch and took a few sips of my water again. I combs my hand through my light brown hair and shake my head.
