Future Imperfect (Part One)

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-14 I hate taking the Hover Bus, you never know who you’re going to meet or who you’re going to have to talk to, I mean I would have taken the hover cab….but the flying monkey beat me to it. I hate flying monkeys. So it was in the cramped confines of the hover bus I found myself flying (Or hovering as the case may be) towards my destination, the driver skillfully maneuvered his weathered bucket of bolts through the oncoming hover traffic and I sat very still and quietly hoping this guy wouldn’t crash into the side of a building….see I would have prayed but ever since the concept of God had been proven false or more less an urban legend in the year 3000 in a freak accident involving Jello and a bible, prayer had become something of a novelty or something Hardcore God heads were still in denial over. Needless to say god was not with us brave passengers of hover bus 41. I’d paint you a picture of the world I live in (Earth the year 6000) but frankly I don’t have enough words or run on sentences to do it justice, I’ll just cover the bases as I go along and I suppose you can be happy with that. So there I sat in the back of the Hover bus trying to appear anti social or mean, or at the very least someone a little old lady would cross the street to avoid, but as the bus continued to pick up additional passengers and the seats began to fill I knew it was only a matter of time before the inevitable happened. "Hey! You’re…that guy!" Sigh I looked up to see a middle aged balding man in a suit, worse still I recognized him as someone who worked in my building, without waiting for an invitation he slid into my seat forcing me to back up against the wall of the bus, my personal space had been officially violated. "Joe…Joe Miller right?" "Guilty as charged" "The guy who’s going back in time right?" "Indeed…" "What year was it? 2042? 2030?" "2005" "That’s right you’re going back in time to compete in some sort of Roman gig where they threw Christians to the lions right?" "No…they didn’t start doing that again until 2007 I’m going back in time to Compete in GTT5" **"Ahhh 2005…that was five years before President Deville right?" he said getting more familiar with the time period" "That would be correct" "Didn’t that guy get in some sort of trouble later on? A big scandle?" "No that was his opponent…it was later discovered several of Deville’s speech cards had been stolen and used against him" "What?" "His speech cards, the guy could write a good speech, it was something about a dog" "What?" "A dog" "A what?" "A fucking dog!" "Oh…ok, I recall there was a big mess with that and Deville won by a landslide victory" "Yeah….DevilleGate…the press had a field day" "Wasn’t there a third party in the running too?" "Yeah…an independent party….Schwarzenegger I think his name was" "What happened to him?" "Deville Terminated him at the polls" "Excuse me sir…" Crap…Busted….I’d been so caught up in the conversation that I hadn’t noticed the officer a few seats back, now he stood in front of me and flashed his badge. "Censorship Police sir…are you aware you violated articles 457 and 329 of the Censorship protection law?" "Uh…no?" The officer didn’t seem fazed by my ignorance and rattled off the codes I’d violated "Article 457…quoting material without permission from a wrestling promotional video and Article 329 quoting Movie material and taking verbal shots at a popular political figure without permission, I should fine you for these offenses sir but I’ll let you off with a warning this time" "Uh thanks…" My seatmate and I watched as the officer made his way back to his seat and sat down. "Stupid Censorship police…." I muttered If there was one thing I hated more then flying Monkeys it was Censorship police. The conversation was more or less killed with the intervention of the Censorship officer so we sat in silence until my stop where my temporary companion uttered a quiet "Good Luck" before I found myself standing on the platform to the International House of Pancakes, Starbucks and Time Travel, your one stop shop for all your pancake, coffee and time traveling needs…if you asked me Advertising was just getting desperate, but Starbucks was legendary in the fact that it had stood the test of time and still maintained a powerful presence even with the passage of thousands of years. Then again so had roaches and still no one cared. I entered the building and joined my fellow time travelers, two spots ahead of me I noticed much to my irritation the flying monkey who’d taken my cab earlier, he hovered there a little fella on a jetpack looking quite out of place in a line full of humanoids, however alone he might have appeared he was no doubt forging new territory for his kind, only three years ago the "Discrimination against Flying Monkeys on Jetpacks" law had been passed allowing the furry little critters the right to go anywhere that humans could go, though they were still met with some minimal racism even now they were far from defenseless….the little bastard’s could still throw their fecal matter with incredible accuracy, needless to say I was happy there was another person in between me and him. The man in between us was absorbed in a book, at a closer glance I saw he was reading a title that had been very popular a few years back… "One Hundred TRUE facts about Brock E." "Good book?" I inquired "Oh man it’s sweet!" the man replied "Hey did you know Brock E. once actually Saved Jesus Christ?" "Really?" "Yeah with a pair of uzi’s and a…." he turned back a few pages looking for the quote "And Jesus had a sweet Ninja sword!" "Who publishes this stuff?" "Earnest Wayne Niles" "Ahhh…well don’t believe everything you read alright?" "Why not man it could have happened, the past is like a wide open book, nobody knows if it actually happened or not" "The past?" "Yeah! I’m going back in time so I can see this go down!’ he said clutching to the book with the reverence I’d seen Godheads treat the now outdated bible with "So where are you going?" "I’m going back to the year 2005 to wrestle in GTT5" The kid looked at me and blinked "Dude you should come back with me and meet Brock!" "Wouldn’t the chance to meet Jesus Christ be a little more exciting?" I asked "Who?" the kid looked at me confused "Never mind…enjoy your book kid" I turned back to my line which moved with the slow nerve grating slowness that lines had always moved in for thousands of years, some things never change I guess, I tried to fight off the time travel jitters which never having traveled before I decided to liken to "First time flying Jitters" or "Just before the big game Jitters" or finding out after all these years that your mother wasn’t really a woman…or you mother….or from this planet Jitters" ….Shut up…I have issues "Ahem….Excuse me" "Yes?" I was face to face with the flying monkey who’d been ahead of me in line, the little fella had cut back it seemed to talk to me. "Wait…you’re…" It was Ben the tech monkey! The little varment who’d chased me around the room and thrown his feces at me. "I uh…wanted to apologize…I don’t know what came over me…Look I wanted to ask you…You’re destination seems so much more exciting then mine…I wanted to ask if maybe I could go with you instead" "Where were you going?" "The UWF" "The what?" "I wanted to see Pete The PG Tips Chimp…but it sounds like he’d be in your time period as well, I hear he entered into GTT5" "Does this mean if you go back with me you could very well ditch me and wind up rooting for Pete instead?" "Possibly…" "Oh alright what the hell…just no feces throwing alright?" "It’s a deal!" So that was how I came to the counter of The International House of Pancakes, Starbucks and Time Travel with a flying monkey in my party, we explained things to the gentleman behind the counter and he quickly made the appropriate adjustments. Our trip would begin the next morning. Until then it was just one man and a little monkey in a big city… Hey that sounds kinda cool…like a movie or something. Maybe if this GTT thing doesn’t work out I could promote it in Hollywood… Whoops better end this before the Judges get me for rambling…. -The End- Yeah that’s good….it’s got a note of finality to it. -The End- -- **And now a note brought to you by the author and read by Ben The Tech Monkey** Ben: Greetings humans, The author of this witty and prize winning tale… *The monkey rolls his eyes* Ben: Would like me to brief you good readers on a few matters, First and foremost being The Angelo Deville issue, Unless you’ve been living under an E-Rock then you’ve probably noticed a cheap shot at one of Deville’s wonderful Rp’s…the author would like to assure you good readers that he took all the precautionary measures even contacted Jeff himself to make sure there would be no hard feelings involved, or angry mobs, or fires, or pitchforks…you know those kind of drastic measures. Ben: The author would also like to point out that while he has no legal ownership over Starbucks, The International House of Pancakes, or Arnold Schwarzenegger he feels no remorse or regret in using them. Ben: Finally Just in case you were wondering about my character "Ben The Tech Monkey" He is indeed based on Ben The President from Global Championship Wrestling, the author thought it would be amusing to make Ben a monkey in GTT5 of all places…There is a limit to the Likeness of our characters however…Ben is NOT a monkey….though the author is pretty sure Ben jumps up and down and throws his own feces when he’s angry with his roster. -The End…Until The second Post that is…-
