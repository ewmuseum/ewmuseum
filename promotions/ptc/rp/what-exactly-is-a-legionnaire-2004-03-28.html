What Exactly Is A Legionnaire?

28 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-28 What exactly is a Legionnaire? Legionnaires: An acute, sometimes fatal respiratory disease caused by a bacterium of the genus Legionella, especially L. pneumophila, and characterized by severe pneumonia, headache, and a dry cough. – source: American Heritage Dictionary. Legionnaire: A member of a legion. – source: American Heritage Dictionary. Legionnaire: The devout, zealous, cult following of the greatest tag team ever, The New Royal Legion. – source: XUW’s dictionary. The scene fades into what has become the most famous hotel in all of XUWville, the Howard Johnson Hotel. The sun in a cloudless sky is beaming down on the now legendary hotel. Down at the foot of the hotel mulling about in the parking lot like always are the most loyal fans in all of wrestling, the Legionnaires. Now some people might call the Legionnaires a cult but they disagree and just say they have come together and rallied behind a greater cause in helping Bailey and Danyon otherwise known as the New Royal Legion defeat the ongoing conspiracy that has plagued their otherwise amazing career. They were 4 time Tag Team Champions, Bailey had a 5th with the original Royal Legion as well as a run as Submission Title holder. Unlike some other fans in wrestling that have turned on wrestlers at some point in time, the Legionnaires have never and will never turn on the New Royal Legion. They have stuck through the thick and the thin of it, even creating a title for the New Royal Legion to defend as the Legionnaires were upset that XUW did not give them the tag title rematch they so rightly deserved – The Legionnaires Only Significant Tag Titles or L.O.S.T. Titles as they came to be known. The L.O.S.T. Titles became the most respected and most important tag titles in XUW history as they were only defending under specific rules in the L.O.S.T. Title Rule Book. The Legionnaires were there when they went on strike from XUW only to come back and defeat one of the 2nd greatest team ever in XUW history, the Ultra Violents, (2nd only to the New Royal Legion but of course) which was definitely one of the high points in the long career of the New Royal Legion. Furthermore, Legionnaires were also there during some of the low points like the first abduction by aliens and were there during the absolute lowest point back in December when tension boiled over between Bailey and Danyon and they fought in a match against each other, only, however for both of them to get abducted at the end of the match. Bailey returned a month later while Danyon, has not been seen since. But that’s all in the history, this is about the present, today to be exact, March 28th, 2004. Outside of the Howard Johnson Hotel the Legionnaires are happily going about their lives in the sun in the parking lot. Some are just sitting around reminiscing of matches and conspiracies that the New Royal Legion took place in. Others are standing by the rail, waiting, hoping that Bailey is going to leave the hotel any minute now so that they can catch a glimpse of their saviour. There are those who are standing around singing such classic Legionnaire sing-a-longs as “I’m a Legionnaire,” and “Blame it on the Conspiracy.” So, pretty much it is what is considered a normal day for wrestling’s most fanatical fans. Way up 15 floors above the Legionnaires in the New Royal Legion Suite are none other than Bailey and Larry The Legionnaire – the first Legionnaire there ever was. However, lately in the room there has been a 3rd person joining Bailey and Larry. And no it is not Danyon, as it was already mentioned that he is still abducted, however, his whereabouts is supposedly known by Larry but he is keeping that location under wraps for the time being for an unknown reason. Instead, the 3rd person in the room is the latest and the one million and first member of the Legionnaires. He is someone that is world famous in the wrestling circle, a multiple title holder including 3 times as world champ in the now defunct but forever remembered GIWF. He was also the owner of UWF for a short period last year. That’s right the 3rd person in the room is none other than the whiskey warrior, the pungent pugilist, the flatulent fighter, the smelly superstar, “The Bastard” Al Crud. It seems that Al became an honourary Legionnaire about two months ago after Bailey and Al were teamed up in the Deadly Draw in XUW. These two were underdogs and were expected to go out in the first round of the tournament due to Al’s seemingly forever losing streak. However, Bailey proved that he was the greatest tag team wrestler ever by carrying Al Crud to a few victories in the tournament, way past anyone’s pre-tournament predictions. The unlikely duo bonded over a few beers and became friends. Al was accepted into the Legionnaires and now regularly hangs out with Bailey and Larry in the confines of the New Royal Legion Suite. In the suite itself, Larry is sitting in his usual position at the desk with his silver briefcase on the desk open and with papers scattered about. Bailey is standing at the window over looking all of his faithful Legionnaires down below while Al is in a chair with a beer in hand. Next to Al is a stack of empty beer cases and about a half dozen empty bottles scattered on the ground. Larry turns around and shakes his head at all the mess that surrounds Al. The Bastard Al Crud is a messy individual which is a definite downside to having him around. He rarely cleans up after himself. Larry gets up and walks over and picks up the empties. Larry: I swear Al, do you ever clean up after yourself? Al farts. Al’s gas problem is known worldwide. Al: Uh…sorry about that. I’ll try better next time. Larry: I think you said that yesterday and the day before that and the day before that and well, you get the point. Al you have to seriously start cleaning up after yourself. Al: I’m getting better. Look there are only ……..uh…..six bottles down there now. Larry: That’s true. But what about all of these cases of empty bottles beside you. Why are they stacked up like this? Al: Oh, that’s simple. Everytime I finish …..uhm…..I think it was 8 cases I return the bottles and I get enough from bottle deposit back for a new case. It’s like buy 8, get 1 free. It’s easier to keep track of them when they are beside me. Plus, I want them here beside me in case someone else has caught onto my little scam and tries to steal some empties from me. Larry: I have to give you some credit there Al, that’s not a bad little scheme you have going. Al belches. Larry: Now if we could only somehow market your smell and gas problems in some positive way, we can be rich. Larry puts away some bottles and goes back towards his desk. Bailey turns around from the window and looks back at Larry. Bailey: Larry, I noticed the other day that there was an interfed tag team tournament being hosted by the PTC. Larry: Were you planning on entering it and showing to the world that you are the greatest tag team wrestler ever? Bailey: No. Larry: No? Why not? Bailey: Because my tag team partner is still abducted that’s why. And you won’t tell me where he is. Larry: It’s for you own safety that Danyon is not around. Last time you two nearly killed each other, I’m not letting that happen again. Larry looks over at Al. Larry: Besides, you could partner with Al. Al looks over at Larry and then at Bailey and then shrugs. Bailey: I’m sorry to say this but Al is no Danyon. Bailey turns to Al. Bailey: I’m sorry buddy. Al: No worries. I’m the worst tag team wrestler ever. Although, tag team matches are probably the only matches I’ve won in the past year. Bailey: I mean, I know we had success and all a few months ago tagging but with all due respect you aren’t Danyon. Al belches. Al: I know that. Bailey: It would be funny to see the Crudspiracy, otherwise known as the New Beer Legion, go through that and win. But that is just not the same as the legendary New Royal Legion and I couldn’t do it to the Legionnaires. Which is a shame too because I really wanted some form of the Legionnaire family to get some PTC representation. Larry: You are right. It must be about time for the Legionnaires to make an appearance over there. I know we are known worldwide but we should show our faces. There must be another tournament that is going on that you could enter. Bailey: Oh there is another tournament. There is a cruiserweight tournament that is starting up. A confused looks comes over Larry’s face. Larry: But you aren’t a cruiserweight and Al surely isn’t a cruiserweight either. Bailey: Oh I know that. We aren’t cruiserweights……….but you are…………. Larry: Oh………….. Larry’s eyes widen as it takes him a few seconds to clue in to what Bailey just said to him and a frantic look comes over his face. Larry: W…w….ww……ww…what? Wait a second……….whoa, hold up here……..hold on one second here……Are you saying that you entered me in this tournament? Bailey: That’s what I’m saying. Larry: But I’m not a wrestler. I’m your lawyer. I’m not a wrestler. I’m in charge of the Legionnaires. Did I mention that I’m not a wrestler? Bailey: That’s what makes this all that much more fun. Larry: This isn’t fair. You just thought it was right just to go up and enter me in the tournament without asking. You should have asked me. Bailey: I didn’t think it was necessary. Larry: This is so unfair, it isn’t funny. Bailey: I think its fair, considering that you are withholding Danyon from me, consider this your punishment of some sort. Larry: So if I find Danyon, then this can be called off is what you are saying? Bailey: No, it’s too late for that. You are entered and I even confirmed it the other day. Larry sits in his chair and shakes his head as he looks down at the ground. Al: Hey, Larry. It’s okay if you lose. Look at me, I always lose and I’m still doing fine. I’ve put on a few pounds, not as slim as I used to be mind you but otherwise I’m doing fine. Al farts. Larry: I’m not worried about losing Al. It’s just not fair that I’m not a wrestler and that I’m in a wrestling tournament. It’s not fair. In fact it’s a conspiracy really. This is somehow part of the conspiracy I bet. This just isn’t right. Bailey: Sorry, Larry, for once this has nothing to do with the conspiracy. I just thought that after all these years of supporting us in XUW the Legionnaires should be able to cheer on someone in another event. Since, I was unable to do the TTD I thought you should do the Cruiserweight Tournament. Think about Larry. What other wrestler in the tournament is going to have one million fans cheering for them? No one will. You are forgetting that you are going to have one million Legionnaires there at this tournament on your side chanting you name. Larry: Wouldn’t there be one million and one Legionnaires chanting my name since Al was the one million and first member? Bailey: No, there would be only one million Legionnaires chanting your name because you are a Legionnaire and you can’t really cheer yourself on in your own match and chant your own name. Although, I guess you could but you would lose as you would be distracting yourself. But then I wouldn’t put anything past you to do something so stupid in the ring. When I wrestle there are one million and one Legionnaires chanting my name. When you wrestle there is only a million. Larry: I suppose you are right about that. But I’m not a wrestler Bailey. I’m a lawyer and a damn good one at that. Bailey: But you’ve wrestled before………. Larry: I know but I could still die in the ring. Bailey: That chances of that are slim to none…….. Larry: But there still is a chance that I might die in there. It has happened before you know. Bailey: Larry, you are more likely to get seriously injured or maimed in the ring than die. You could go get paralyzed or break a leg in there or tear a quad. Those types of thing are more likely than death. Death is a little far reaching. Wouldn’t you agree Al? Al farts and then rubs his hand through is slimy, greasy hair and then picks his ear before noticing that they were waiting for an answer. Al: Uh…yeah, more likely what he said. Larry: Oh my god, I didn’t even think of that stuff. Bailey: So what, you break a leg or something in there but hey at least you gave it your all. Larry buries his head in his hands and shakes his head. Larry: What did you get me into? Bailey looks over at Al Crud and winks at him. Al smiles back even though he is confused as to why Bailey winked at him. Bailey: I got you into nothing you can’t handle Larry. You’ve wrestled before and won a match before, albeit Danyon and I helped you win that but you still won. Larry looks up from his hands with a bit of smile on his face. Larry: You are right. I did win a match didn’t I? I did beat the root of evil, Kevil. I can win another match. I even have a year’s more experience of being around wrestling so I will have more knowledge in the ring. Bailey: Yeah, maybe you can perfect more than a headlock. You really had that down last year by your last match. Larry suddenly gets really happy and excited at the thought of actually winning his match in the Cruiserweight Tournament. He starts speaking a lot quicker. Larry: Yeah, I did, didn’t I? I bet if I were to practice a little more I could incorporate some suplexes into as well and maybe a moonsault off the top rope. I’ve always wanted to do a top rope moonsault. Bailey: I wouldn’t go that far Larry. But yes, if you practice over the week, you should be able to incorporate some moves. Larry: You are right Bailey. I can win. I can do this. Bailey: That’s the spirit. Al: Larry, I have faith in you too. If……… Al pauses so he can belch. Al: That was a good one. Uh…..where was I? Oh right. Larry if I can win a match every now and then, you can win a match. Larry: You guys are right. I am going to beat my opponent. Who am I facing? Bailey: He’s in FAW, his name is Horazon. Larry: Bah, it doesn’t matter who I’m facing. I can win. I can beat anyone. You know what? I’m going to go down to the gym in the basement of the hotel and start working out. I should make sure that I’m in tip-top shape for this match. I have to be fit. When’s the match? Bailey: I think it is in a week or so. Larry: Great. That gives me a week’s worth of training. And with you having Reborn coming up, we can train together. Maybe you can show me some secret trades of the sport. I’m going to the gym right now. You want to come along Bailey? Bailey: No, I think I’m okay for now. Larry: Alright, that’s fine. I’m off now though. I can do this. I can win another match, my 2nd victory in just under a year. In fact my last match I wrestled I won. I have winning streak to keep going. Larry goes over and grabs a gym bag and with his new found confidence he opens the door and leaves to go to the gym to work out. Al and Bailey watch him leave. After the door shuts, Al looks over at Bailey. Al: Do you really think that he has a shot at winning? Bailey: No. He’s so going to lose, which will be even funnier. Al farts and then picks out his wedgie. Al: There can be a lot of humour in losing. If anyone knows that it’s me. Al belches. Bailey: Indeed you would Al. Bailey pauses for a second. Bailey: Want a beer Al? Al: I thought you would never ask. Bailey walks over to the fridge and opens up the fridge door and pulls out two Iron City Beers. He tosses one to Al and keeps the other one for himself. The scene slowly fades to black.
