A New Day, A New Suffering: Part 5

8 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-08 Most of his doubts had vanished, but some of them still lingered. His determination had a new found resolution. It was do or die. The time for him to show his worth one more time. It was his time now and he wouldn’t let it go to waste. If he did it would have been on of the biggest mistakes of his life. He was ready. There comes a time in everyone’s life, a stepping stone of sorts that helps decide who can really transcend as a person. We all get those moments. Some of those realize that the decision will affect the rest of our lives and some just have no idea what hit them. We all get second chances though, and if we so choose to right the first blunder, we can. It happened to him once when he tried to kill himself. He could not withstand the pain and misery of what had happened. The guilt that was all over him and inside his blood. He tried to pry the blood out with steel on bone. He was lucky that day. He had been given new life and now he didn’t plan on wasting it. He planned on catching this moment and never letting it go. As he sat there, thoughts of how he would do it crossed his mind. They all ended with laughter from the other patrons and a cake in his face. He knew it would not happen that way, but he was still nervous. He had asked her to meet him at her favorite restaurant for dinner. He had not mentioned how urgent it was. This was it, the moment of his life. Defeating his demons by downing Hoyt in the tournament was nothing compared to this. He had decided that he wanted to spend his life with Veronica and he would not rest until he got what he wanted. He nervously fixed his shirt even though he knew that it wouldn’t matter to her. Nervousness can be one’s biggest enemy. Sometimes it takes a hold and it doesn’t seem to ever want to let go. It could be one’s undoing. Digital placed his hands on the table and inhaled deeply with his eyes closed. When he opened them, the couple on the table right besides him was looking at him awkwardly. Digital couldn’t help but smile. "I’m proposing tonight. A little nervous here." "You’ll do just fine. Don’t be nervous about it," he was answered by the female of the pair. She then turned her look of tenderness that she had held towards Digital and turned it into a leer and turned towards his boyfriend. "Now you! We’ve been going out for four years already and he has not gotten the courage to ask me to marry him. Can you believe him?" The man dropped his head and kept eating silently from his plate. Digital could not help but chuckle and shake his head. He didn’t want to say anything stupid that would make the guy look worst than he did. He thought silence would be the best answer here. Digital took the case where the ring came and opened it. He gazed at the new ring he had bought and couldn’t help but compare it to the one he had buried the morning of that same day. They were similar, but as soon as he had seen this one, he could picture her face. He could see the smile on her face. He could see all of her troubles disappearing for at least that moment in time, and at the same time, his own problems would fade for those few moments of joy. He could see the tears on her cheeks. That was the ring he had to buy, no matter how expensive it was. At that moment he smelled the aroma of the restaurant, the food, the candles and in between all those fragrances he could feel the scent of that one perfume that he loved. He didn’t know its name or anything; he just knew that he loved it. She was finally here. He inhaled deeply one last time and this time he saw her coming his way while the air was still inside his lungs. He almost choked. She looked beautiful. She was beautiful. Suddenly, all the doubts he had were not there anymore. They vanished quickly. She looked regal on her short red dress. She used her right hand to take some of the hair away from her face. The black short hair had a little bit of curl; she looked like she had jumped out of a fashion magazine. She put her bag and jacket on the chair and went over to Digital who after staring dumbfounded had stood up. She hugged him tightly and kissed him on the lips. Her soft lips were everything he had needed. The feeling that someone really cared by kissing him. He inhaled the smell of the coconut conditioner and body wash that she uses. How he loved the smell. He could get lost in that smell, in a trance, and never come out of it. "Cómo estas, mi amor?" Something seemed weird though. He was used to seeing Vero wear dark glasses, but why would she need them inside of a restaurant. Maybe she had been wearing them in the car and just forgot. "I’m good." There was something there in her answer, but Digital’s mind was somewhere else. He had one of his hands in his pocket nervously touching the case with the ring inside. At that moment the waiter arrived with a bottle of champagne Digital had ordered before. Glasses were served. "To life." "To you." "To us." They both drank. Digital just couldn’t bear to keep waiting. He knew he would screw it up if he kept waiting. "I have something I need to tell you, Vero." "I have something I need to talk to you about as well." He could sense the urgency in her voice. He decided that proposing could wait. What she had to say seemed way too important for him to not care. He stopped using his hand to move the ring around. Instead he used it to reach over and remove her glasses. "Why do you still have glasses on?" After he took them off of her, he dropped them on the table. She saw his awestruck look and started to cry silently. After watching the tears streaming down, he stood up and went over to her. He kneeled down and tried to console her. He hated to see her like this. He preferred to die than to see her in this state. He kept gently telling her that everything would be alright. She stopped crying for a moment. The makeup smeared all over her cheeks. She tried to clean herself up for a moment. She tried hard to clean her eyes up, but she had to do it carefully because the black eye she had was really hurting her. Digital’s expression had gone from shock to compassion to sadness and now they had reached one of the places that Digital only reserved for his enemies, hate. "Who did this to you?" "Promise me, Antonio… promise me that you won’t hurt him." "It was your father wasn’t it?" She was pleading now, the tears streaming back down again. She had tried to be strong for him, but she had lost that battle, and who could blame her? Her own father had given her a black eye. Digital could only think of beating the hell out of him. He couldn’t believe that he dared to put his hands on Veronica like that. What Alberto did was unfathomable. To treat your own child that way was something irresponsible and wrong. All the sympathy that he held for him was gone. "I won’t hurt him, but you and I are going over there right now and we are going to talk to him. This can’t keep going on like this. Am I supposed to be nervous every time you go visit him because I fear that he might beat you up again? I can’t live with this, and you don’t have to live like this. You have to stand up to him." "I know… I know… but I just can’t face him right now… not now." Digital calmed down a little. After turning it around in his head he knew that she was right. They had to go another day. The wounds were fresh and they both had to be on their right state of minds so that they could tackle this problem head on. This wasn’t a small problem anymore. This had become a crisis that had to be worked out once and for all. "Ok, we will go tomorrow. Tonight, we will enjoy this evening and you will stay at my house. He still has the keys to your house. Plus, mine seems so empty without you." "Agreed." They continued on with their dinner awkwardly, trying not to talk about the subject. It was like the elephant in the living room that nobody mentions. Dinner went by rather quickly and silently. Towards the end, she remembered something. "Didn’t you have something to tell me?" Digital was taken by surprise. He had completely forgotten about the ring and the proposal and instead he had his mind on what he was going to say and do when he finally met with Alberto. The proposal had seemed like a dream until his hand felt the case inside his pocket. "Um… it was not really that important." Things were left unsaid, but the next day Digital would make sure that other things were said. ---- They both walked up to the door of the apartment. She had on her dark sunglasses to cover up the bruise surrounding her eye. She was walking a little bit behind Digital with her hand tightly interlocked with his. She was afraid of her father. She knew she shouldn’t be, because it was his father and that was the first time he had ever hit her, but this new person was not her father. At first, Digital had felt pity for the man. He knew that Alberto was not in his right state of mind, that alcohol and paranoia had taken a hold of him and wouldn’t let go. No longer did he feel pity for him. A one time threat and in avertedly injuring your daughter could be forgiven but not forgotten. Putting your hands on your daughter and crushing her emotionally should not be forgiven or forgotten. The confrontation was about to take place. Veronica had a nagging fear that Digital would just lose it and beat up her father but at the same time she wanted him to learn a lesson. If violence was the only way for him to understand what was going on, then so be it. Digital knocked on the door rather forcefully. There was no answer so he knocked again, louder. The third time, it went from knocking to banging. A grumbling could be heard from inside. "Damn it! Who the hell is it?" "It’s Digital." He used the name most people knew him as because Digital knew Alberto hated it. He hated the whole idea of using a created name. He had never called him Digital. Alberto also hated the business Digital was involved with. The wrestling business was like some sort of hell spawn to the ultra conservatives. It didn’t matter to Digital though. Alberto opened the door and squinted as the light of the midday sun hit him in the eyes. The inside of the house was very dark, all the windows closed and the curtains drawn. Digital could see the state of the room, there were bottles of liquor strewn everywhere, cans of beer on the sofa, on top of the TV. It was a mess. Alberto silently held the door open for them as they both went inside. The first thing Digital did was to start opening the windows. The apartment had a stench of old beer and the smell of decay was too much to bear. After the windows were all open and Alberto was done covering his eyes, Digital stood in front of him. "How the hell do you explain that?" Digital pointed at Vero’s eye. "I can’t believe you could have done that. A few days ago I would have stood by your side, against everyone else to tell them that you wouldn’t be able to hit your own daughter. You proved me so wrong. Not only that, but you abuse the person I care about most in this rotting world." He didn’t seem worried at all. "You are no one to tell me how I treat my own children. You are no one at all. So if that is why you came over here, I want you to leave my house immediately. You have no right to tell me what to do in my house or with my daughter." Digital’s eyes flared with anger. He couldn’t believe it would come down to this. "You need help, Alberto. If you don’t go someplace to help you, then I will have to take you there by force, and believe me, I can take you down right now if I wanted to." The alcohol inside Alberto’s body flashed into overdrive. It was a sight to see. His face had become a dark shade of red and his eyes were almost out of their sockets. He rushed towards Digital and got in his face. "Who the hell do you think you are? You are just one more of those minions of the devil doing his will against us! You have no power here though!" Veronica looked shaken and went over to try and stop her father. She didn’t want him to get hurt and she knew there was no way he would be able to take down Digital. She put her hands on his chest and out of instinct he swung hard on her direction. Digital was quick enough to grab Alberto’s hand before it had landed on its target. Veronica surprised, walked backwards without saying anything. Alberto swung hard towards Digital with his other arm, but his drunkenly swing was no match for Digital’s lightning quick reflexes. Digital grabbed his arm, twisted it around Alberto’s neck and with his right leg, tripped him to the ground. Veronica winced as if she was the one that had been hurt. He kept screaming at Digital, cursing him, maybe even trying to find someway to exorcize a demon inside of the man who was now holding him against his will. "Why the fuck do you care so much about what happens to me or her? Let me go you devil." "You still don’t understand, don’t you? I care because I want to spend the rest of my life with your daughter. I care because I don’t want her to suffer all the time because her family is breaking apart at the seams. I care because I want to marry your daughter… I want to marry you Vero…" This surprised Veronica. She hadn’t expected this. She looked into his eyes and saw that this was really it. He was proposing to her. This is not what she imagined a proposal would look like, but she felt it. The father had heard what he had to say and was calm. Digital let go of him and went over to Veronica. He knelt down before her and he took out the case that held the ring inside. He opened it. "Veronica, will you marry me?" Questions were asked… Answers were left unsaid…
