Star Power

Date: 2004-08-22 Sometimes the pains of our existence can drudge down our lives. With a little bit of creativity, perseverance and courage anything can happen. 2:07 p.m. August 22, 2004 Noahs’ Estate Kansas City, Kansas Things had certainly changed in a major way, in my absence. Wrestling Midwest has gone though a whirlwind of changes. The whole fed has been turned upside down, and shaken by its ankles. Like a bully trying to get money from a small child, Corvus is holding WMW hostage, claiming bodies and souls like the grim reaper. Yet, I have the unenviable task of teaming with his brother Wraith along with Matrix and my teammate Adam Allocco against 3 other 4 men teams from LOW, WWA and PRIME. This is a challenge in itself, but something I realize as a chance to shine. I leaned back looking up at the ceiling, watching the ceiling fan whirl. What do I need to do in this match? Do I need to carry this match like I have carried so many before? I doubt it, but the thought that someone from my team may not show had crossed my mind before. It would not really surprise me that much. I had been MIA for quite a while, but at the ppv I made my intentions known. I did something that a lot of people did not think I had the guts to do, challenge the Great Lakes Champion Corvus to a match. He is as dominate as they come, he will be a challenge, but I will prevail. As I leaned forward, looking down at the carpet slightly. I thought that this match at the ppv would be something special. Maybe I could use this match to get into the head of the champ just a bit. I am teaming with his brother Wraith, so it could have its advantages. But, I think that I really needed to concentrate on what I needed to do first and that was the match against the other three teams. I thought that maybe I should do something that I had never done before, something special for this occasion!! I reached over, sliding my chair across the carpet until I reached my cell phone. I dialed a number, a person on the end was yelling at someone in the background. “Hey, I said get me a mocha roca latte!!” the voice yelled “Hello, who is this?” “You need to watch your mouth, or I will watch it for you!” I said with an angry tone, but was not really angry at all. I knew Paul would get angry but I wanted to rattle his cage a bit! “Who the hell is this?” Paul angrily snapped backed “Maybe, the IRS we have a lead that you have been doing some illegal business in anal butt plugs!” I said as I smiled slyly. “What?” he snapped in response. “I never had any dealings in sexual aides!” “Oh, is that so?” I asked twirling in my seat. “What about this website Pauls’ Butt Plugs.com? It even has your picture on it!” “What? I swear that was just learning faze, I never meant for it do so well!!” Paul exclaimed almost stammering to get answer out. “I swear I will close the site down!” “Well, I have one more question to ask!?” I said as I smiled. “Do you ever use them on yourself?” “Ahhhhh…No that is absurd!” Paul scoffed. “Well I did once, but that was in college and the girl said I needed to feel the same thing she did!” I cringed at hearing the description, looking at the phone like it had a disease. “You are a sick bastard, Paul!” “Wha…?” Paul stammered as I laughed. “Who the hell is this?” “Paul, its Noah calm down man!!” I said as I was still laughing. “Noah, you asshole!! What do you want, I thought you were dead?” Paul said angrily. “Not at all, Paul. I just need a TV crew to come and set up at my house. I am doing Star Power live from home!” I said as I smiled and threw a dart towards the distant wall! “Really, you got it!” Paul said. “They will be there shortly!” “Great!” I said as I turned and looked in the direction of the dart, seeing what it had hit was a picture of Corvus I had taped to the wall. “Things are going to be great now that I am back!” I knew that I would have to do something that would draw the champs attention, and nothing would do that better than doing Star Power again. He thought that his little show was the only one and there for the better one. Well he is sadly mistaken as I have always had the better show!! And now that things are going back into full swing I am ready to make a return with Star Power. 5:o6 p.m. August 22, 2004 Noahs’ Estate in the Stage Area Kansas City, Kansas Well I was looking over things; I had a set up done. The TV crew was in place and also had made the show open to the public so I had a live audience for the show. I had a lot of surprise for this show, and wanted this to be something extra special since I had not done this for quite a while and was out of practice! The music blared for the show and my stage announcer stepped out from the curtain. “Ladies and Gentleman, WMW proudly brings to you the return of one of the most dynamic and entertaining men in all of sports entertainment. The man that is longest reigning Ace Superior champ, one half of the tag team champs and the man that every woman wants but can never have… He is the one and only Noahhhhh Hansssssonnnn!!” the announcer bellowed as he pointed towards me as I walked out. I grabbed a mic, walking toward the applauding crowd. I waved to the fans, giving high fives to a few! “Thank you all for coming and welcome to the return edition of Star Power!! I have a very special show planned for this evening, but first thing I wanna say is that I AM BACK!” I said as I raised my arms in the air!! The crowd roared loudly at the announcement. I was glad to finally be back in full swing, this being the first step. I walked to desk, sitting down and taking a drink of water. “Well, things have picked up a bit in my absence. Corvus destroyed his friends and coffin bodies Shadow and Epitome. I mean it was something that they had coming, sucking like they did was an embarrassment to everyone in WMW not just to Corvus! But when you’re the champ what does that say when you have to beat down your girlfriend and another follower. Hell, I knew he was a cold hearted bastard but I had no idea he was THAT cold. But to you Corvus, I tip my hat. It tells me one thing that you indeed are a true bastard, I know now that I can do everything and anything to piss you off!” I looked at the crowd as some chanted Corvus, Corvus!! “Some pro Corvus fans out there, huh? Well that is just as well cause this is a message that is directed at the man that holds that Great Lakes title that I want so badly, that I can almost taste the leather of the strap. Some people may have already written me off in a match versus Corvus, and to be honest that is just fine with me. Much like Wrestling Midwest in its match against the match against three other teams, we have been written off there as well. But you see I see this as one of the biggest challenges of my career. This sis again a chance to shine on a nation wide scale, to prove something in this match would be a huge accomplishment on anyone’s resume. I do plan on putting on a good performance; I plan on putting on THE performance. The kind of performance that has people talking at the water cooler the next day, and on internet chat rooms.” I walked to the front of the desk; smiling as jaded as I ever was. "You see that brings me to my first group of guests. They all come from the great fed called PRIME, I am not even going to try and figure out what that means but without further ado TEAM PRIME!!" I watched as four midgets dressed as the team representing Prime appeared. The crowd laughed at the one that depicted Thug Roc was incredibly laughable as he looked like a junior version of Ja Rule! “Yo, where all my dogs at?” Mini Thug Roc exclaimed holding up his fingers in a gang sign. “Would you shut the bloody, hell up?” Mini Ian English replied as he sat down wearing a Union Jack t-shirt, carrying a blackjack in one hand. “Blimey, he’s a fool!” I watched as the largest of the midgets sat next to Mini Hug Roc. He had a shirt on that read “Tony Rolo”. Obviously, so that he would not forget who he was. “Me, not like to be here, where is here? Is that spaghetti, I smell!” Soon mini Tony Rolo disappeared behind the curtain. Meanwhile, the last of Team PRIME appeared and quietly sat in his chair. It had to be Joey Troy or the mini version. He was dressed nicely, wearing a graduation hat on his head that had “Head of the Class” written in chalk across the front. “Well my first group has arrived for the most part, and although I am not totally impressed I have to say you guys are a hell of challenge.” I exclaimed with a broad smile, as I was having a hard time containing my laughter. “Yo, dog this is wack!!” Mini Thug Roc yelled. “Yo, have more bling, bling that 2Pac!” “Uhh…right!” I said as I figured he was looking at my tag team title, gold necklace and my trademark lime green sunglasses. You know it was really feeling good doing this again, as I had missed doing Star Power for a long time but had never really had a chance to do it again. The music started as I looked over my notes and saw the next groups of guests were scheduled to appear. The group from LOW was next, they had an intriguing group representing them, Annexation, Jeffrey Damper and Black Lung headlines a “talented” LOW team. I remember beating Annexation in a PTC tournament a few years ago; I was not even concerned about him. Then there was Jeffrey Dahmer, hell I thought he was dead or something but whatever if she/he whatever wants a piece of the most delicious wrestler to date he will just have to wait his turn. I had also heard of the Lung and although he drew much attention during the time when he was a free agent, he is no match for me and it really is that simple. I looked at my watch, wondering where the hell the team from LOW was. A stage director walked to my desk, handing me a note. I looked at the note, smiled at the contents and looked towards the crowd. They were now ripe with anticipation. “I am sorry to disappoint all of you great fans, but the powers over at Lights Out Wrestling have told me not to do any parodies of any of their talent. Well that is all well and fine cause it seems like the boys and girls over at LOW are afraid of a little competition, not that I blame them. I really believe that LOW has some of the most overrated talent. They believe that big names equal big results. Well I am here to tell each and every one of them that this star is going to show each and every star from LOW what I am all about.” I looked around and waved the crowd on to get pumped up!! “The funny thing is that I am more concerned about one person and one person only. He goes by the name of the Muffin Man!” I said. Just then Fury, my tag team partner appeared. “Yo, man his Muppet, Muppet Man!” “What?” I said as I most have my notes mixed up. “Is he like Fozzy Bear or Kermit or something?” Fury sighs deeply as I could see my comment was weighing heavily on him as he sat next to me. “Look, Noah he is not a Muppet like the Jim Henson variety, but he wears a mask it is his gimmick.” “Shhhhh….” I said covering his mouth with my hand. “Exbnay on the Etrade Wordsnase!” “What?” Fury asked as he removed my hand from his mouth. “We are not supposed to talk using words like that you know that too!” I said sounding like I was scolding him. “Well, what kinda name is that? I mean that is like Mr. Darkness he sounds like he is Corvus’ dad or something or maybe what Corvus uses at the end of his contract signing!” I said as I had a confused look on my face. “Be careful saying something like that because I hear that Wraith is somewhere in the area!” Fury said like he was reading from a script, and reading it badly. “What?” I said in mock terror, cupping my hands to my face. “Where is he??” As if on cue a smallish, gothic looking wrestler appears from the back of the room. PsuedoWraith calmly walked to a coffin in one corner, lying down inside. “What are you doing, Wraith??” I asked looking on in disappointment, shaking my head in disbelief. “You shall speak to me as the “Crown Prince of Darkness in training!” PsuedoWraith spoke as if he was on his last swirl down the toilet bowl of life. “Great, Corvus would be proud. You are carrying on the mantle of depressive maniac to the hilt!” I said with a deep, resentful sigh. “Well, what do plan on doing in our match with LOW, WWA and Prime?” I asked Pseudo Wraith as he lay in the coffin. “I dunno, don’t really matter the dark forces will help us in the end!” Pseudo Wraith said with a satanic laugh, closing the coffin which only muffled the laughter. A few seconds latter a large ripping noise was heard. “Whoa, that is nasty I should have not had enchiladas for dinner!” I rolled my eyes as one of my “partners” was passing unholy gas in his bedroom! I looked around and suddenly the room grew dark, a greenish fog emanated from the entrance as “Matrix” appeared. He was wearing a trench coat, sunglasses and had a toy WMW Midwest title over his shoulder. He slowly made his way to his seat next to Fury, barely smiling as he removed his title from his shoulder. “Hey, glad you could join us ‘Trix! But I have to say that the Midwest title has shrunken in size since you have gotten a hold of it. You accidentally get it wet or something??” I asked as I patted Fury on the shoulder, trying not to laugh. “Yo, Matrix my man what is up with my title??” Fury asked. “You know that I plan on taking that title from you very soon, so I hope you are gonna polish it up real nice for me!?” “This title will always be with me, nothing you do will ever matter.” NeoMatrix announced in a wooden, stilted voice. “With the “Powers That Be Kicking Your Ass” in control of WMW, all that oppose us will fall.” “Are you serious?” I asked with a blank look on my face. “Cause that just sounds gay. Well fans, thanks for coming here today. For my partner Fury and all of my very special guests, this is Noah Hanson good night!!” I watched as the fans gave me a standing ovation. I walked to the crowd as Fury and I signed a few autographs. As we left the room Fury grabbed my shoulder. “Look, Noah it is really good to have you back!” Fury patted me on the back, smiling as he walked ahead of me slightly. “Well, partner I am glad to be back. Win, lose or whatever happens at the ppv all that really matters is that I am back!!” I said as I high-fived Fury. “That is all that matters….” *end*
