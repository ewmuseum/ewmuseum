It's not UWF vs HATE. It's Frost vs Krazie.

4 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-04 "Well, that could have been worse." Oh yeah, it could have been worse, he could be dead, but fate wasn’t ready to let him get off that easily, not yet anyway. After all this story was just beginning, it was far too soon to shut the book. ~~~ It had all started to go wrong the moment that Ethan Frost had threatened a janitor in the aftermath of Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, UWF’s flagship show, where the stars of today and the future paraded in front of thousands, taking in the adulation, respect and jeers, all depending on how good or bad you were. Events at the show had led to, for the first time in his life, Ethan Frost losing control. Words were had and it ended with Frost mopping up urine, as most of the new superstars do sooner or later, or so he had been told at least. It was there that fate had decided to grab Ethan Frost by the balls and drag him along for a ride, a sudden gust of wind blowing a leaflet into his eye line. With Frost being an intuitive person he naturally picked it up and read it, it was there he first learned about PTC’s GTT. Next of course came the phone call where he found out the enormity of the tournament. This was the big one, apparently people had been talking about it for months, everyone knew it was coming it seemed as the excited woman on the other end of the phone fed Frost an endless stream of information. One hundred and twenty-seven of the best names in the industry had either been invited or applied for a spot in the event, countless others had been turned away, either not having the right credentials or they were simply not good enough. Then of course there was him. The mighty Ethan Frost, the six match veteran of UWF, the man who’s only claim to fame was losing a television title shot at Live and Loud 5, UWF’s biggest event of the year. A title, which it should be pointed out, has been passed around more than the village bike. As the details were being reeled off to Ethan, dates and deadlines, locations, press conferences, the alarm bells were beginning to sound in Ethan’s head. He had only called up to enquire but had now found himself on a roller coaster where he was soon to be included in the line up. He was only really looking for something to pre-occupy himself for a while. He had been getting far too attached to the business, the world of wrestling was sucking him in, and he needed the distance for fear of what may happen without it. However as has been proved countless times in love, war and general intelligent decisions, the heart often over rules the mind, so as the words. "Sure, count me in." left Ethan’s mouth with a degree of nauseating enthusiasm, his brain made the conscious decision to shut down, leaving Ethan to run purely on emotion, without any sense of rational thought. Sure he could get hurt, but at least he wouldn’t care about it. ~~~ "How the hell could it be worse?" The voice of a man beaten and broken in spirit questioned. Without even looking at him, you just knew that his head was buried in his hands. There were no tears just yet, but the wrong word could lead to a disaster." ~~~ So Ethan, was now officially registered for GTT5, it was all happening so fast, already the hype was beginning to filter through the wrestling community. Although the Internet fans had been rumbling about how big a deal the tournament was, nothing could even compared to the official standpoint the tournament committee were taking. That said though, there was no real need for the hype at all. Simply looking at the list of competitors was a wrestling fans wet dream. Even Craig Miles had been coaxed into the tournament, breaking away from the enclosed fWo circle, widely regarded as one of the most professional and best outfits in the wrestling world, despite it’s quietness over the past few weeks, fWo would sell out an arena in a matter of minutes. This tournament was the real deal. Yet none of this seemed to faze him. Ethan was young and exceptionally green in the industry, to Frost this was the equivalent of a child getting to play soccer with Pele. He knew he wouldn’t win, but he’d certainly have fun along the way. By entering the tournament, you wore the crest of your company. There was never anything official, it was more about honor, no wrestler wanted to lose in the first round, neither did their respective companies, neither did a federation want to be the first to be eliminated totally, especially with the growing trend for free agents to enter tournaments, in the hope of catching someone’s eye, making their own impact. Frost was pleasantly surprised to find out that he was not alone in carrying the UWF banner, that is the sort of pressure that could kill a man, or at the very least, keep him awake at night with worry. Samantha Gordon, UWF commissioner had called a meeting for the GTT participants and whilst it was intended to be a good luck, here are your itineraries, we’ll do all we can to help you along the way, sort of thing, Frost couldn’t help but notice the hostility in the room, most of it being directed from her to him. Despite being new to UWF, Frost had already had a run in with Gordon; a woman who it was once said could melt ice with her gaze. To put it bluntly she didn’t like Frost, seeing him as some rich stuck up kid, trying to live out his dream. She just wanted to get fans in seats, to keep the turnover going and at the moment, she felt Frost had potential, but was not using it. Sat around a large table glancing around, somewhat nervously were the members of team UWF, the seven men who would go into battle for the honor of their company. John Adams, James von Doom, Sledge, Axel Action, Digital Mortality, Alex Lancer and himself. There was of course the eighth member of the team. Who sat grinning at the rest of us, his top lip curling backwards over his teeth to reveal yellowed menacing teeth at anyone who dared to look at him too long. Well he was a monkey after all, don’t call him an ape though, or he’ll bite your balls off, literally, just ask Jamie Shock. ~~~ "Well at least your name was spelt right on the poster that’s something, the picture looked great as well." The voice in reply was trying to tread as carefully as he could around the broken man, he felt uncomfortable around crying people at the best of times, it’s a man thing, they just don’t know how to react and he sure as hell wasn’t about to hug another man, he wasn’t comfortable enough to do that. ~~~ It was at the meeting where Frost, as well as the other competitors were told how tournaments often made and broke people. Names that people had previously not heard of suddenly emerging as stars of the future, one’s to watch. The man to Frost’s right Digital Mortality was a testament to this, in GTT3 he proceeded to make the final sixteen from two hundred and fifty-six competitors, before being eliminated by the eventual winner, as well as one of the favorites for this years tournament. Angelo Deville. Frost couldn’t help but pause and look at Mortality with a look of admiration in what he had done. Not just Mortality though, every single man and chimp around the table had achieved something and there was Frost, who felt like he was sticking out like a sore thumb, a newbie, the underdog. To any casual fan, it would have been a strange setting, well stranger than seeing a chimp paying attention to a woman talking, occasionally throwing something unmentionable at James von Doom. Many people believe that the hatred in the ring transcends into real life, that the feuds and petty differences interfere with their working relationships, but they really don’t. To be involved in the business, you had to be able to trust those around you quite literally, with your life, but rarely were they all sat around in such a formal meeting as this, battle plans were being drawn, to continue UWF’s dominance in PTC events, "The first step is hype. Each one of you is going to have to make themselves known, by any means necessary. We’ve been told that this is mandatory, any choosing not to participate in this, WILL be removed from the competition." Sam Gordon spoke, her acid tongue flicking the words, her gaze was focused on Frost, almost as though she wanted him to fail, regardless of the effect on UWF. "Anything will be accepted, videos, posters, radio show interviews, if you can think of it, they will let you do it, so please. Do something and don’t let us down, otherwise it will be opening Wednesday Night Underground for you, I don’t care if you’re a hall of famer, or a new guy who is still wet behind the ears, let us down and you will know about it." Sam Gordon paused, allowing the thoughts to sink in, the room fell silent for a moment, the collective minds of the competitors were whirring away, even Petey had fallen silent and was scratching himself, clearly deep in thought. "Now, what you’ve all been waiting for, who you are going to be facing in this little competition." Miss Gordon began to read through the entrants and who they were facing, the room fell silent as it was revealed that Petey and John Adams were to face each other, a distinct tinge of anxiety filled the air, it didn’t help matters that Petey curled his lip backward and bared his teeth at Adams, staring at his crotch as he did so. That was the nature of tournaments though; you could literally be paired up against anybody. "Ethan Frost you will be facing a wrestler by the name of Kid Krazie." Frost glanced around the room, trying to gauge some sort of reaction from his peers, he didn’t even know everyone in UWF yet, let alone other companies. The name Krazie just did not jump out at him. The room though gave no indication as to who he maybe, it could have been worse, if they had all taken a deep intake of breath and sighed, he’d have known he would be in trouble. "It might also interest you to know that Kid Krazie is representing the HATE federation, so I don’t have to tell you how important it is that you win." The room suddenly turned icy around Frost. He wasn’t the best at picking up on things, to him subtly may as well be a brick to the temples, but even Frost noticed this, the anger and ill-feeling that had suddenly filled the room, the low mumblings that were being muttered underneath breaths. "What difference does that make?" Frost asked, suddenly all eyes were on him, in one simple question he had shown exactly how out of touch he was, how he not yet fully integrated the UWF community. It turned out that there had been ‘issues’ between the two companies. Copyright infringements had led to legal proceedings and in the process both companies had suffered considerable losses, both had been made to look petty and ridiculous and it was the employees that suffered. So the whole story was laid out in front of Frost who sat silently and took it all in. "So, now you realize why it’s so important that you beat someone representing HATE." Ethan felt the eyes of Sam Gordon and the other competitors burn into him with a fiery intensity. In his heart Frost knew that he should simply nod and agree, but it would be going against everything he believed, he was just one man and didn’t know enough to form a strong opinion on the matter. "No." Spoke Frost flatly. "You guys may have your problems with HATE, but I don’t, hell I don’t know the guys and I will not be dragged into your petty war. When I get out there it’s just going to be Ethan Frost vs. Kid Krazie, not UWF vs. HATE. I won’t let it become that." And it was with that Ethan Frost rose to his feet and left the meeting, he ignored the protests of his peers and the threats of Sam Gordon as she docked numerous weeks wages for decent. If he was going to advance past round one in this competition it was going to be all about him and not the company. ~~~ "I guess it doesn’t really matter now does it." Ethan Frost mumbled from between his fingers, which were covering his face. He was sat on a small wooden bench in a luminously lit dressing room. The walls were vibrating with noise coming from the arena, every cheer and clap magnified a thousand times and more. From what had simply started life as an idea, a little leaflet had suddenly grown and caught him in a whirlwind. GTT had finally arrived and he was up next, the hype had been done, albeit badly, the words had been said, all that was left now was to wrestle. Stood silently in front of him was Ethan Frost’s father, his arms folded looking down at his son smiling. He was a proud and happy man and regardless of the result, he would know that Ethan had done all he possibly could do. "So are they going to throw you out of the company for decent?" Asked his father as he sat down next to Frost who lifted his head from his hands and let out a deep sigh. "No, they just docked me some more wages, I think technically I owe them money now, Sam Gordon really doesn’t like me, but the fact of the matter is, she needs me in this competition. It’s just… I’m scared. I don’t want to be the guy the goes out first round. The guy that everyone forgets, I want to make a name for myself, I want to achieve something." Ethan said, turning to his father, like he had done many times in his childhood, looking for guidance, looking for the magic words to make everything right, however as his lips parted a loud knock came on the dressing room door. "Frost, you’re up!" The voice echoed through the door and into Ethan’s ears like the bells of doomsday, there was no more time for words. Slowly Frost drew himself to his feet and through his head back to stare at the lights. Then without another word he nodded towards his father and left the dressing room, leaving his father sat on the bench who ran his hands lightly through his thinning hair. "Go get em son, just fight your own fight. This is about you, no one else."
