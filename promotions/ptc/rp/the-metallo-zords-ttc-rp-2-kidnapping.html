The Metallo Zords TTC RP #2 --- Kidnapping

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Metallo Zords Affiliated Pimp Joined: 05 Apr 2003 Posts: 6 Location: My Zord Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 7:42 am Post subject: The Metallo Zords TTC RP #2 --- Kidnapping ::The camera fades in to a picture of the fairgrounds where we last left our heroes. The grounds seem to be less crowded than they were when we saw them earlier, but there is still a fair *no pun intended* amount of people there. Behind the stage curtain, someone can be seen looking through a gap. One of the young girls from earlier, who we now know is known as Thin n’ Trim Tammy, is now clearly in view. The Gold Ranger can be seen on the floor next to the stage, looking over the clipboard held by Burt. Burt taps the Gold Ranger and asks him to slide over so he can talk to the children.:: Burt - Children, children, help me sort through this stuff. Thin n’ Trim Tammy - No problem Burt. ::Thin n’ Trim Tammy walks quickly down the stage steps, where one of the young boys from earlier is standing. The boy, who is nicknamed Brainiac Billy because of his awesome intelligence, is fixing a basket of eggs that was left on the stage. He finishes up and walks away from them, as the voice of Vill, the neighborhood bully, is heard.:: Vill's voice - Hey, I smell boiled eggs! ::Vill advances on the egg basket while Eee, Vill’s best friend, lurks in the background. He quickly rushes forward at Vill's urging. Vill points at the basket and exclaims with excitement.:: Vill - Ohh! Eee, I found the eggs! I found the eggs! Eggs, eggs, eggs!! ::Vill fishes an egg out of the basket as Eee points at it.:: Eee - Egg! ::Vill gives Eee a disgusted look. A voice is heard in the distance:: Head of the Fair's voice - Looking for something, boys? ::Vill chokes and quickly replaces the egg. Vill & Eee do their best to look inconspicuous as the Head of the Fair approaches.:: Vill - Ah, no, no! ::Vill begins searching for words to make up an excuse with.:: Vill – I wa- I was just- I was just looking for- uh, looking for- I was just looking for my- ummm- tuba! Yeah, I was just looking for my tuba! ::The Head of the Fair chuckles to himself.:: Eee - Yeah. He likes to check his instruments before he performs! Head of the Fair - Oh, really? Well then, good luck with your performance. ::Vill nods in appreciation. Before the Head of the Fair leaves, he suspiciously asks another question:: Head of the Fair - And you, Eee? What are you doing here? ::Vill makes a subtle motion of encouragement to Eee. Eee thinks for a moment, then grins winningly and picks up a large gong stick. He is so excited by this sudden brainwave that he fails to notice Vill frantically gesturing "No!".:: Eee - Just checking on the gong, Mr Fair Chief Man!! ::Eee clashes the gong stick on a nearby cymbal, producing an irritated reaction from Vill and the Head of the Fair. The vibrations also manage to somehow flip the Head of the Fair's toupee off his head. The scene now cuts and fades back in to the woods, where we last left the reptilian monster.:: Monster - Hehe-hehehe! Er-heh! Destroy! DESTROY! Heh-heh-heh-heh! ::The shot pulls back as if to suggest someone is watching through a telescope. The focus draws away from the planet Earthy and shows the starry outerspace. The view settles on a eccentrically dressed woman looking though a telescope on the balcony of some type of castle. She moves away from her telescope, her surprise quickly turning to anger.:: Queen Mogambu – I demand to know who did this right now! How sent this monster down there without my authorization?! ::Queen Mogambu looks at her personal advisor and general of her army, Tim. In turn, Tim glances angrily and two of their goons, the raging French monster Lulu de Blu and the grizzly from Mount Hizzly, Big Bad Barry Bear.:: Tim - Yes, who authorized this? Lulu de Blu – Well – I uh – Big Bad Barry Bear - Not me! Queen Mogambu - I know! I told Raziel it's my birthday today. He created it on his own and sent it as a surprise! Lulu de Blu & Big Bad Barry Bear - Happy Birthday to you, Happy Birthday to you, Happy Birthday dear Queen Mogambu, Happy Birthday to you! ::Queen Mogambu acknowledges this with a nod and a laugh.:: Queen Mogambu – The beast looks like it may need some help! Big Bad Barry Bear – What kind of help? Queen Mogambu - So get down there, Tim, and show him the ropes!! ::Tim bows to the Queen as he responds.:: Tim - Yes, O Evil One! Big Bad Barry Bear - Right! Lulu de Blu – Yes, mon sheri, I will assure that he receives aid! ::Everyone in the room pauses for a moment in a silent chuckle at the thought of a Frenchmen giving aid to someone then continue with their plan.:: Tim - To the park! Big Bad Barry Bear - Okay; we're off! ::Tim, Big Bad Barry Bear, and Lulu de Blu blink out of sight. Queen Mogambu walks towards the edge of the Balcony and grins wildly. The scene zaps back to earth to show an above view of the woods along with the fair. The Silver Ranger is shown running along in a wooded section. He has a certain bounce in his step as he is excited about the upcoming talent show. He is so excited in fact, that he begins talking to himself:: The Silver Ranger - This is going to be so super cool! After I win I will be a Martial Arts champion! I rock! ::The Silver Ranger spin-kicks a branch, knocking a leaf free.:: The Silver Ranger - It's going to be cool! Yeah.. ::The scene shoots back up away from Earth, focusing in on Queen Mogambu's Palace.:: Queen Mogambu - Raziel! RAZIEL!!! ::Queen Mogambu starts to walk away from the Balcony. Raziel can be heard from his workshop:: Raziel - Yes, my Queen? Queen Mogambu - Tim and that monster you made, Egg Suckin’ Sammie, are about to attack, but the Silver Ranger's down there, and he could ruin everything! Raziel - Ah-hah. Queen Mogambu - What are you gonna do about it, Raziel? ::Queen Mogambu is now smiling, apparently confident that Raziel will have the answer.:: Raziel - We wouldn't want him to do that. I shall send the Ich Van Herbe Patrol! ::The Silver Ranger is now walking along at a more steady pace. All of a sudden, four Ich Van Herbes appear out of nowhere and move swiftly to encircle him. Each Ich Van Herbe flips over to swap places with its opposite number in the circle, and they are labeled according to their place in this new formation; starting with Ich Van Herbe #1 to The Silver Ranger's immediate left and continuing clockwise through to Ich Van Herbe #4. After a tense moment, The Silver Ranger begins the fight by superkicking Ich Van Herbe #1. Without missing a beat, he turns and takes out the knee Ich Van Herbe #2 with a chop to the back of the leg. He pops back up to his feet and grabs Ich Van Herbe #3 in a rock bottom position, then plants him to the ground. Without even getting up, he sweep kicks Ich Van Herbe #4. Ich Van Herbe #1 knees The Silver Ranger and stamps down at him while he is on the ground. The Silver Ranger dodges aside and kicks Ich Van Herbe #1's legs out from under it. Remaining in this crouched position, he delivers a low blow to Ich Van Herbe #2 and then stands back up. He ducks a punch from Ich Van Herbe #2, blocks a second, and then throws his opponent to the ground. Ich Van Herbes #1 and #2 are now down for the count. There is no further sign of Ich Van Herbe #3. Ich Van Herbe #4 runs over and drops The Silver Ranger with a flying kick to his chest. The Silver Ranger springs back up, grabs Ich Van Herbe’s arm, and locks him in a half nelson. With a quick slam, Ich Van Herbe #4 is sent down hard onto the leafy ground. The three remaining Ich Van Herbes lie in an arc around The Silver Ranger, starting with Ich Van Herbe #4 in front and continuing anti-clockwise through Ich Van Herbes #1 and #2. However, they don't stay still for long; all three quickly spring back up again. Ich Van Herbes #1 and #4 close in for a joint attack. The Silver Ranger grabs hold of their arms and clunks the Ich Van Herbes together, back-to-back. He delivers a knife edge chop to each in rapid succession. Both Ich Van Herbes collapse. Now charged with adrenaline, The Silver Ranger is almost caught off guard by Ich Van Herbe #2 as it rolls past. He waits for it to stand up and then closes the distance with three knee lifts. Ich Van Herbe #2 crouches down, legs wide apart, and blocks the last of these attacks with its hands. The Silver Ranger throws a quick left, not at all prepared for what is about to happen. Ich Van Herbe #2 seizes his arm and steals the utility band off his wrist, flinging him back with the same motion. It holds out the purloined utility band in a taunting manner. The Silver Ranger gets back up; Ich Van Herbe #2 prepares to make a run for it.:: The Silver Ranger - Oh; so you want to play, huh? I could use the practice before the competition anyway! ::The Silver Ranger chases after the cunning Ich Van Herbe and soon comes to a halt near a large tree, obviously fed up.:: The Silver Ranger - Give me the utility band back! ::Ich Van Herbe #2 dances irritatingly in front of The Silver Ranger, mockingly holding out his utility band.:: The Silver Ranger - Look! I'm done fooling around! ::And with that, the trap is sprung. A net gathers around The Silver Ranger and hauls him up into the tree, dragging up a number of dead leaves and branches with him.:: The Silver Ranger - Huh? Whoa! Ohh! Oh! Ah! Come on! Let me out of here! Ich Van Herbe #2 - Nope! The Silver Ranger - Ah! You Ich Van Herbe! Come on! ::The jeering Ich Van Herbe gibbers in delight as it holds The Silver Ranger's utility band above its head, remaining oblivious to the shower of dry leaves raining down from above.:: The Silver Ranger - Oh! Uh! You just wait! ::As The Silver Ranger struggles, his com link tumbles down out of the net. A hand belonging to an Ich Van Herbe reaches out and snatches it.:: The Silver Ranger - Uh! Come on!! Let me out of here! Oh! ::The scene goes to Queen Mogambu spying through the telescope; her magnified eye is shown on the glass. She cackles in delight, either from witnessing The Silver Ranger's capture, or from observing the following scene.:: ::Tim, Lulu de Blu, and Big Bad Barry Bear are moving through a mountainous area which resembles the place where the monster (now identified as Egg Suckin’ Sammie) first appeared.:: Big Bad Barry Bear - Hey, I'm getting hungry! Tim - Quiet; you're always hungry! Lulu de Blu - I'm a little peckish myself! Big Bad Barry Bear - Yeah! Tim – Bear’s are always hungry, and the French are always Peckish! Lulu de Blu - Uh, look! There's some eggs! ::Lulu de Blu has spotted a couple of blue speckled eggs under a bush. He and Big Bad Barry Bear go to investigate.:: Tim – What are you fools doing?? Get back here!!! ::Lulu de Blu and Big Bad Barry Bear ignore him. They each pick up an egg to examine.:: Lulu de Blu - Ooh, yum-yum! Big Bad Barry Bear - Yeah! Lulu de Blu - Woohoo! Ooh, I like this! ::Big Bad Barry Bear cracks open his egg(leaving one half on the ground), chuckles, and feasts on the yolk inside.:: Big Bad Barry Bear - Oohh! Mm! That was delicious! ::Big Bad Barry Bear throws the shell away and starts wiping the yolk off his mouth. Lulu de Blu finishes off the contents of the other egg in a less messy fashion.:: Lulu de Blu - Tasty! Scrumptious! ::Lulu de Blu flings the cracked egg over his shoulder. Its halves land near the half shell that Big Bad Barry Bear threw away.:: Tim - Put that down! Hey! There's Egg Suckin’ Sammie! ::A startled Egg Suckin’ Sammie has just turned up. He goes into battle stance. Queen Mogambu, who is watching through her telescope, stammers.:: Queen Mogambu - It looks like Egg Suckin’ Sammie is cracking up! ::Lulu de Blu and Big Bad Barry Bear hastily retreat behind Tim.:: Big Bad Barry Bear - Uh-oh, he sounds kinda huffy! What's the matter?? ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie advances angrily towards the startled henchmen.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - How could you? Tim - What? Big Bad Barry Bear - Whadaya meeean? Egg Suckin’ Sammie - You ate my supercalo protein eggs! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie drops to his knees and picks up the broken shells.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Do you know how hard it is to find these? You can't just walk into a store and buy these, you know! ::Queen Mogambu has been following all of this through her telescope.:: Queen Mogambu - Raziel! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie glares angrily at Big Bad Barry Bear, Lulu de Blu, and Tim.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Oh, I was going to have these eggs for lunch! Big Bad Barry Bear - Really? They were good! ::Probably not the wisest thing to say under the circumstances. Egg Suckin’ Sammie's eyes blink in rage.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Were good?!? ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie gently places the egg shells on the ground and then stands up.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Rrh! Ah! I'm gonna tear you apart! ::Big Bad Barry Bear and Lulu de Blu recoil in fright. Tim holds his sword in a defensive stance as Egg Suckin’ Sammie prepares to attack. The scene cuts. Back at the fair, a spotlight briefly illuminates Vill & Eee as they practice their musical work for the Talent Show. Eee has a cymbal on which he is pounding; Vill has an extremely large tuba. Eee now has a roll of yellow police tape wrapped like a sash around his left shoulder. Vill is wearing sunglasses. Brainiac Benji comes into view and walks over to The Gold Ranger. As they speak, it is revealed that Thin n’ Trim Tammy is standing nearby. The other two young children from earlier, Afro Otis and Pleasantly Plump Patty, walk over during the course of the conversation.:: Brainiac Benji - I wonder what's keeping The Silver Ranger! The Gold Ranger - I don't know. He's not answering his com link, and he should've been back half an hour ago. Thin n’ Trim Tammy - Yeah, did he go to Japan to pick up the weapons, or what? Afro Otis - Y'know, it's not funny, you guys. I mean...he could be in trouble. Brainiac Benji – I confirm and validate that notion. I'm sure something's wrong. ::The Gold Ranger's com link beeps. The Gold Ranger covers it with his hand and motions for the others to follow him out to the corridor. Once there, he activates the com link.:: The Gold Ranger - ::to com link:: We read you, Bobbalu. Bobbalu - Teleport to the Command Center; immediately! The Gold Ranger - ::to com link:: We are on our way. ::The five of them teleport away and reappear in the Command Center. They stand in front of Bobbalu, who talks to them about the situation:: The Gold Ranger - Bobbalu! The Silver Ranger does not answer his com link! Bobbalu - That is why I have called you here. Observe Sphere of Knowledge. ::The Gold Ranger and his pals turn to take a look at the Sphere of Knowledge. It shows a replay of The Silver Ranger's capture.:: Bobbalu - He is being held hostage by Queen Mogambu's Ich Van Herbe Patrol. Thin n’ Trim Tammy - Oh no, The Silver Ranger! Pleasantly Plump Patty - He's in a real pickle now. Afro Otis - We gotta help a brother out! Bobbalu - Not so fast, Afro Otis. We have another problem. ::The Sphere of Knowledge now shows images of Egg Suckin’ Sammie, including the sequence of him running through the woods. None of them relate to his current predicament with Queen Mogambu's henchmen.:: Bobbalu - Queen Mogambu has released a gnarling Egg Suckin’ Sammie monster, who intends to destroy the Earth. Afro Otis - Man. That is one ugly Mofo! Brainiac Benji - Precisely. An absolutely atrocious abomination! Thin n’ Trim Tammy - But, Bobbalu, shouldn't we help The Silver Ranger first? Bobbalu - He is capable of handling his situation. Your first priority must be to destroy Egg Suckin’ Sammie before he can unleash his awesome eggy power. The Gold Ranger – It is time to show that Egg Sucker who is boss! You guys wait here with Bobbalu, I can not endanger you by letting you come along. I will handle this. ::The scene fades out. Will the Gold Ranger be able to handle this on his own? Is the Silver Ranger done for? Are Vill & Eee really going to win the talent show? Tune in next time to find out.:: _________________ Who farted?
