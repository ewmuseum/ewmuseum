24 April 2004 THE CREATION CONTROVERSY ACT ONE: BEFORE THE BARING SCENE ONE A suburban town; Darkness prevailed on the soft and solemn streets, and with this pitch black, the end of another long and hard day was marked in stone. The midnight shift was grueling and unbearable, but he had to settle. For there weren’t any other jobs available, and even though the pay was poor, he would take what they gave. Night after night, he would walk through the streets to his home, as he was too poor to afford a mode of transportation. Masked within the darkness, he would scuff through the village, barely able to keep his footing balanced. Dragging his legs to the doorstep, he made such a racket to gather his keys. Finally, after all of his efforts, he would be able open the door and enter the main room, another dank and dark element to add to this blackness. A new day would begin in a matter of hours. Muttering to himself, he searched for a light switch. He did not dare disturb the peaceful slumber of his lifetime partner, a woman who he had fallen in love with so easily, and a woman who he had just engaged in matrimony with. Their life together seemed so impossible, yet he didn’t care. He would work everything out, for he said so to her every day. But with each day that went by in this month young marriage, the conditions only seemed to go from bad to worse. Trying to flick the light switch, there isn’t a response. Desperately praying for the fixture to work, he keeps flicking the switch to no avail. It had been like this for a week, yet with every new day also came a new hope that everything would be fine again. His hopes at this moment were grim. A new wife, he promised to treat her like royalty. But soon the royalty would turn to dirt, as they were turned away by their tenant in the city and forced to move to these outskirts. What a rotten place to live. It was worse then Hell’s Kitchen, this was Hell’s Playground. Only the poorest of people were pushed into here, as they all hoped that one day their luck would change. But for the lot of them, there wouldn’t be a brighter day, and they would die in their solitude. This area was infected with a plague of sin, and was always under the constant watching eyes of the police force. Gang action was constant, drug busts and drive bys seemed to be a nightly affair. Fear was always lurking in the minds of the residents, but for some, dying felt like a better option. Closing the door behind him, he would turn the knob on his deadbolt and clasp his steel latch as well. Breathing heavy with frustration, he would drag his tiresome body through the main room and into the kitchen. Putting a muddy and bloodied hand on the refrigerator door, he would pull it open and check for anything that had become sour. The light from the fridge allowed us to see the terrible state of this room, with plaster chipped away on the walls and broken wooden cabinets, hanging sideways from a broken hinge, being the obvious smallest of the problems. Now noticing the fridge, we see it is bare except for a few beers. He would grab at one, closing the door behind him and searching through the room once more to find his way out. Stuttering into the main room, he would feel around for his favorite place to sit. He would then sit down, putting his red-tinted palm on the cap, and twist the beverage open. A small mark was left on his hand as he dropped the cap to the floor, slowly bringing the beer to his lips. This was his only relaxation, as for the rest of the night he would depend on the warm beer to be his companion, and upon finishing the bottle, he would drift off into sleep until the sun rose. SCENE TWO The next morning; The sun peaked its way into the small dust-filled window of the main room. Slowly hitting his legs, the warmth of the new day moved its way onto his chest and then to his face. Muttering silently as the bright day gleamed on his cheekbone and his eye, he would open one eye and look around. Something felt different about this day, as we got our first look of him. Wavy black hair sat on the top of his skull, and even blacker eyes that were like two coals. He had a chiseled, expressionless face, a strong jaw and broadened shoulders. Yawning greatly, he would scratch at his grizzled beard, and then look over to his right arm. Noticing the beer gently tucked away there, he held his head a little with his other hand and muttered more, mostly in the form of obscenities. Pulling his hands away, he would look upon the blood and dirt from his deeds the previous night. Sitting up a little and pushing his way to the edge of his chair, he would rise up with a little of a wobble, and make his way to the sink. The bathroom was disgusting as well, with: grime, dirt, and rust forming anywhere you looked. The sink was rusty, but it would serve its purpose. Putting his hands under the sink and grabbing a sliver of soap, he would turn on the water, which was cold as the artic. With the heat being turned off, this was as warm as it would get. He rapidly moved his hands together between the soap, the dirt and blood slowly making their way into the already ruined sink. When he was done, he would throw the sliver of soap out in a small tin wastebasket, and wipe his hands clean with a rag he had found out on the street. Hanging the rag to dry on the trash basket, he exits the bathroom and wonders away through the main room, looking towards the door. He slowly turns his head, looking towards the stairwell, and upon noticing that nobody was there and all was silent, he unlatches the locks and heads out the door. Where he was heading, we already had a clue. Every morning around this same time, he would take a walk to a small store tucked away in a corner and grab a newspaper. The prices were cheap, and it helped to know the owner by name. You could get anything at the store, so maybe today he could get a good price on some soap as well. Soon enough, he would have enough money to rid of those lightless nights and heatless washes. He promised himself every day, but with every promise he made, the tone of his voice seemed to fade a bit more. He knew his life had changed, but he was too full of honor to think otherwise. Upon entering the store, he slowly nodded his head towards the counter. The manager of the store (and the only employee for that matter) waved to him, showing a smile with a couple of missing teeth. The manager was old, old enough to be retired already. But there wasn’t enough in this place to sit back and relax, so everyday he would run this store, and hope for the sale to strike him rich. Walking through the aisles of goods, the manager kept a close eye and a warm smile on him. Knowing the gentleman wouldn’t steal anything, the manager’s eyes slowly drifted back away to the register, as he clicked his fingernails onto the oak counter. Searching through the aisles more, the man finally finds the soaps. Picking a box up, he takes it into the back of the aisle with him. Quickly, he takes the soap in the wrapper from the box, placing the box on the floor with a gentle touch. He then places the soap inside of the wrapper up on the counter before grabbing a newspaper and putting it on there too. He uses the soap as a weight for the paper as the manager’s facial expression turns a little sour with a perked eyebrow. The gentleman just smiles as the register is opened, ready to take the money for the products. Manager: One newspaper and one bar of soap… The gentleman just nods towards the manager, as all he could do was sigh. His face turned a bit pale as the man looked upon him, putting his hand upon his shoulder. The clerk tried to show a smile and a chuckle, but it couldn’t quite come out right underneath the shroud of despair. Manager: How much do you have on you today… The fellow searches his pockets, and pulls out a small amount of change, change that was most likely gathered on his journey to the store. Putting the change on the counter, the manager takes it away and puts it inside of the register, closing the drawer quickly with a slight tone of a ring. Manager: One of these days, we’re going to get out of this place. The same motto he had been speaking every day for the past month. And with each day that went by, his voice seemed to grow weaker. He wanted nothing more to be out of this place too. But for now, this was the hand that was dealt to him and his wife. Manager: One of these days… Nodding once more towards the counter, the manager faintly smiles as the door opens once more. Stepping out of the store, he begins to walk again towards his home. Carrying the soap and the paper, he looks back upon the broken down pantry and shakes his head in disgust with a clicking of his teeth and tongue. He was now almost home, and he slowly took his steps through the street before getting to his door, fumbling around once more for his keys. Little did he expect what awaited him when he was to slowly turn the doorknob and push the door open, panting slowly from his walk. She stood between the main room and the kitchen, dressed in her garmented nightgown, holding the beer bottle in her hand and looking upon him with a stern face. He shook his head in disappointment as he entered the main room, shutting the door behind him and locking up once more. He slowly approached her without a word being said, as she kept still with the beer bottle in her hand. Suddenly, a rage entered her eyes, as she hauled the bottle back, throwing it directly towards him. Quickly ducking down, the bottle shatters against the wooden wall behind him, the few drops of beer left running down the wall. He looked up into her eyes and saw the rage that was building up from within. It was true that he had a drinking problem, but it was also true that he promised her that he would stop for good. This time he was caught out due to his own stupidity, and there wasn’t anyone else there to blame or to take the heat other than him. Woman: (In a furious tone) you’ve been drinking again… He couldn’t say a thing, all he could do was look into her eyes as he stood up from his previous ducking. She kept a stern look on her face, at least until a single tear rolled from her eye down to her cheek. Wiping it off quickly, she continues to look deep into his eyes with anger. Woman: You’ve been drinking again haven’t you?! Again, he didn’t answer her. This seemed to make her even more furious with him, as she came a closer to his view. Her hair was black and frazzled about, her face pale from lack of sleep and her body frame frail from lack of nourishment. Her ice blue eyes chilled him at the bone, sending tingles down his spine as she continued to try to search for answers. Woman: Why won’t you answer me?! I know damn well you’ve been drinking, I have the bottle in my hand Alex! What do you have to say for yourself?! Whatever it is, it had better be something good! Alexander: (Stuttering a little)… I have no excuse for you Val, except for it was a very long night at work… Valerie: You are full of such crap! You haven’t been to work in a week, I bet you they keep calling here but they can’t get through because the damn power is out! You’ve been going out drinking every night, haven’t you? Alexander: I -- Valerie: (Interrupting) You’ve been spending all of our money on your alcohol, while we’re left here without heat or power! Do you give a shit? No! You’d rather get drunk and get in fist fights. Look at your clothes! They are full of dirt and blood! Is that even your blood on there?! Alexander: I -- Valerie: (Interrupting again) I don’t want to hear your lies anybody Alex! You promised me you would stop drinking, we have a home to take care of. Since when did things become so bad that you have to drink? Is it because we were kicked out of the house in the city? Alexander: (In a slightly annoyed voice) I told you times were hard for me when we were kicked out of there. We had a great home, but we just couldn’t make ends meet over there. Valerie: We couldn’t make ends meet because you spent all of your damn money on drinking! You pissed it all away! Alexander: I didn’t piss it all away Valerie! Valerie: (Not listening to him) When are you going to learn to be responsible for your own actions?! We’re married now Alex, we’re married and for this whole month it’s been nothing but apologies and fights! Alexander: (In a softer tone) I’m sorry Val – Valerie: There you go again. Sometimes I don’t even think you know what you are sorry for, do you? Alexander: Sometimes I think I’m sorry for even being still here on this Earth in the first place… Valerie: Don’t you try to make up that sob story on me Alex, now get the hell out of the house and think about what you have done! Get the hell out and leave me alone for now! Alexander: But Val -- Valerie: I said get the hell out now! Alexander stood there, looking into Valerie’s eyes and seeing the chill in them once more. Valerie kept coming closer to Alexander, and as she did so, Alexander slowly crept up against the wall, not knowing what would happen next. Cringing with fear, Alexander began to mutter for mercy, trying to get Valerie to spare him from any pain. But it wasn’t any use, for Valerie would come even closer to him, taunting him and almost spitting in his face. It was always this sort of attitude towards him, she despised him for his nature, and more than likely only fell in love with him for the hope that one day he would die and she would become rich from his wealthy bloodlines. Poor Alexander was now putty in Valerie’s hands, as she stood strong and tall like an Amazon woman, blocking any path to escape. Trapped, trapped like the rats that crawled outside their home. Was violence the only solution? No, but in this timeframe, it was the option that suited this young bride the best. And so with all of her might, she laid her palm upon his cheek, with such force that a red mark was the result. Valerie: I told you to get out, don’t just sit there like a blubbering fool, obey me! Alexander: (Filling with a bit of anger) Why do you hit me Valerie, I have done nothing –- Not letting Alexander finish his response, Valerie quickly struck her palm across her companion’s other cheek. Now both cheeks were red as vibrant as tomatoes, and they seemed to pulsate. Alexander just looked upon his spouse, and seeing the pain in her eyes after seeing what she had done, he began to succumb to a red-tint of his own. Soon he was the one standing on his feet, breathing hard with sweat running from his brow. Soon he was the one getting ever so closer to Valerie, so much as she were to get a terrified look upon her face. Valerie: (Gasping) I didn’t mean to Alex. I didn’t mean to! Alexander: (With a lot of anger) You never mean to Val. But soon, you will feel my pain. Valerie: (Trying to plead) Please –- Alexander: (Interrupting) You’ve done this for me for too long Val. Time and time again, I obey you like a dog. And time after time, you find new ways to intimidate me. But those days are over now, because now, it’s your turn to get what’s coming to you. Valerie: (Gasping and filled with terror) Alex, PLEASE! Alexander: (With even more anger) I’ve had it with you and all of the things you do to torment me. If it weren’t for meeting you, I’d be living the life of a king. But you with your constant bickering, you drive me to drink every night as a guilty pleasure. It all stops here, and now... Valerie: Alex, please think about what you are doing –- Alexander: I WON’T BE A PART OF YOUR TYRANNY ANY LONGER! Valerie lets out a loud shrill as Alexander paces towards her, dragging his feet along and cracking his knuckles. Valerie suddenly loses her footing as she is trying to get away from this madman, and she stumbles onto the floor, clutching her ankle with one hand and raising her palm with the other, hoping to prolong the confrontation with her spouse. But it was no use, as Alexander’s eyes filled with even more rage as he got dangerously close, to the point that he looked right down at Valerie. Valerie: (While gasping) Alex, please don’t –- Alexander: (While looking up into the sky) forgive me Father for I have sinned... After saying this phrase, Valerie gets back up, trying to get away from her husband. But Alexander grabs her by the shoulder, clutching his nails into her. Valerie screams with pain as she spins around and tries to fire a slap to his face once more, but Alexander beats her to the punch, knocking her back down to the floor with a clenched fist. Valerie lies on the floor motionless, a black and blue spot clearly visible underneath her eye. Alexander huffs for his breath with his heart going a mile a minute as he looks at the deed he has done, slowly developing a sick and psychotic smile on his face. He shakes his fist a little and then looks again, noticing now what he had done. Holding his palm up to his mouth in an extreme fit of shock, his smile quickly turns to a frown, as his wife still hasn’t moved an inch underneath him. Slowly bending down, Alexander squats at her side, holding his palms together in what seems to be a silent prayer. Alexander: (Muttering silently) what have I done...? Slowly bringing his hand to her face, Alexander brushes his fingertips along his partner’s cheek. Carefully he slides his fingers towards her hair, flipping it back away from her eyes. He bends down after sighing and presses his lips on her forehead, motioning towards her more. Placing his hands underneath her back, he picks his wife up, cuddling her to his chest as if she were a toddler. Alexander: (In a sympathetic tone) what have I done to you...? Her body hung limp in his arms as he began to recreate their wedding night, carrying Valerie up the stairs towards their bedroom. He hadn’t been with her in that room in so long, for she had always commanded him to the chair downstairs, making it a “dog house” for his faults. As Alexander continued to carry her up the stairs, he kept muttering to himself and shaking his head in disappointment until he finally arrived at their bedroom. Like the rest of the house, it was dark and not so elegant. Scuffling his feet more, he finally gets to their bed, lying his better half down. Looking at her once more and still realizing that she hasn’t awoke, he bends over again and kisses her forehead. Slowly moving his face down, he takes a taste of her lips, lingering over them for a moment with his own. He smiles a little and makes his way onto the bed, starting to crawl over to his new wife. With this sudden burst of passion, Alexander hovers over Valerie, slowly sinking his body on top of hers. SCENE THREE Hours later; A slight groan can be heard as Alexander opens his eyes on the bed, looking over at Valerie, who has finally opened her eyes as well. She groans as well and rubs her forehead, but isn’t in bad enough of a mood that she couldn’t sport a smile. She kisses her husbands lips gently as their hands intertwine, their first sign of affection towards each other since their wedding night. Valerie: (With a slight groan) ... what happened? I can’t even open my eye. Alexander: (Lying through his teeth) you were resting and must have had a dream about a fight, because you were swinging. I tried to stop your hand, but you hit yourself right in the eye! Valerie: I didn’t hit you did I? Alexander: No, but you’d better get some ice on that before it swells up even more. Valerie: I can’t even sit up, I feel like I got beat up. Alexander: Maybe you had a hard fight in the dream, but you came out on top didn’t you, my little warrior? Valerie: I guess I did... Alexander: You just stay right there, I’ll go get you some ice. Valerie: All right –- Alexander sits up from the bed, scratching at his scalp lightly. Yawning a little, he scuffs his way down the stairs and to the kitchen, as he can’t help but perk a smile and chuckle. He mutters to himself about his gullible partner as he opens the icebox, getting out a couple pieces of ice and using another rag to wrap them up in. He takes the icy rag up the stairs, heading towards the bedroom once again. Handing the rag to Valerie, he smiles lightly towards her, and she can’t help but smile back, not knowing the mischief he had been up to. She presses the rag underneath her eye, relaxing underneath the covers as her husband continues a remedy. Valerie: Thanks, this helps a lot. Alexander: Let me know if you need anything else... Valerie: No, nothing else right now. But there is something that I wanted to know... Alexander: What’s that babe? Valerie: What happened to my evening gown? Alexander: (Trying to sound innocent) what do you mean? Valerie: (Looking down at it from under the covers) It’s wrinkled. What happened to it? Alexander: Maybe from all of your tossing and turning... Valerie: I don’t think so, because there’s a little tear at the bottom of it too... Alexander was stuck in another predicament. He had tore a little piece of Valerie’s gown in trying to make advances on her, and now she was giving him another stern look. What other excuse could he come up with? Or would he actually tell her his intensions? Valerie: Well, do you know what happened to them or not? Alexander: (In a subtle tone) I was trying to –- Valerie: (Interrupting) Wait, don’t even say it. I thought we talked about that before, and we both agreed that we would wait until the time was right for the both of us. Alexander: I know –- Valerie: (Gasping) Oh God, I think I’m gonna be sick... Valerie quickly sits up from the bed, holding one palm to her mouth and the other to her stomach as she runs to the bathroom, Alexander watching her as the door closes. All he can do is sigh, as it looked as if the troubles were starting all over again... END OF ACT ONE ---- 26 April 2004 THE CREATION CONTROVERSY ACT TWO: ABANDONMENT SCENE ONE In the bedroom; Alexander is worried, worried that he had done something extremely wrong to upset his wife of only one month. Now she had run off into the bathroom, most likely to wash away the pain and sorrow that was to be. Sitting up from the bed once more, he looks around in wonderment of whether or not to disturb her. She is most likely furious at him for trying to do yet another thing he had promised her he wouldn’t do, and just like the drinking, he had gotten caught red handed. It seemed that the only thing he could do was try to comfort her. But how could he comfort her, when he was always messing up everything in their lives? From the day the pair met, it had been a mistake. They even had met by Alexander’s own stupidity, as he bumped his carriage into hers at a local market when they both were back in the big city. That all seemed so far away now, as this new life was fogging up their minds like a poison. All the good times were faded, and now the havoc was on the rise. Scuffing his feet closer to the bathroom door, he hears sobbing from within, along with sniffling. He had to conjure up so much courage just to tap his bare knuckles against the charred and de-hinged doorway. At first, there wasn’t an answer, just more sobbing and sniffling. But finally, after more constant tapping, he was able to mutter inside to her. Alexander: (In a soft and comforting tone) Val? –- Valerie: (Through her sobbing) I don’t want to talk to you right now Alex. I can’t believe you would do something like this, even after we’ve talked about it and agreed. Alexander: I got caught up in the moment. It wasn’t my fault... Valerie: Nothing’s ever your fault is it?! Alexander: I didn’t say that. Look, just try to calm down ok? It might not be as bad as you think it is. Valerie: We’re poor Alex, how are we going to afford a child? Alexander: I didn’t think –- Valerie: (Interrupting) yah, you never think about anything fully, that’s your damn problem. You’re too stuck up in your own values to care about anyone elses way of life! Alexander: Val, please don’t –- Valerie: Don’t tell me what to do and what not to do! We need to get some help right now. I can’t live like this any longer. Alexander: Who is there to help us? We got in this mess all on our own, it’s our problem to fix. Valerie: We have to call someone Alex. We can’t keep avoiding the IRS and running away any longer. Look at where it’s brought us. We used to be so happy, we used to love each other... Alexander: I still love you Valerie, no matter what. Valerie: Sometimes I think otherwise... Alexander: What do you mean? Valerie: I never know about you Alex. I never know what you are doing out there. You say you are at work, but for all I know you are out with other women and out drinking. Alexander: Are you saying that you don’t trust me? What about you? Don’t you think that I worry about you when I’m at WORK? I always have you in my mind, trying to figure out if you are all right or if you need anything. It’s not just a one way street, it’s not just your feelings... Valerie: Don’t try to make me look like the bad person! You’ve cheated on me before. Alexander: That was when I wasn’t so sure of myself. But now, I know that you are the one I want to spend the rest of my life with... It is silent for a moment, and then the bathroom door slowly creaks open. We catch the corner of Valerie’s face as she sniffles and looks at her husband, trying to smile through the tears. Valerie: Do you really mean it? Alexander: Of course I do. Now why don’t you come out of there, and we’ll try to take care of this by talking about it, ok? Valerie: Okay... The door keeps creaking open until Valerie stands there, looking into Alexander’s eyes. She sees him sit on the edge of the bed, and slowly pat next to him with his palm. Sitting down next to him, she puts her head on his shoulder and intertwines her hand into his, still trying to get rid of her case of the sniffles. Valerie: So, what can we do? Alexander: I really don’t know. We have to get help somehow though, I can’t bear to stay like this any longer either. But who would have the heart to help us when they don’t even know us? Valerie: Who said they didn’t have to know us? Alexander: (In a confused tone) what do you mean? There was an uncomfortable silence between the pair. Alexander didn’t want to hear a particular person or people come out of his wife’s mouth, but those people were exactly what Valerie was thinking of. Alexander’s parents, the Pendragon’s... He hadn’t spoken to his parents since moving out of their house when he was twenty. Now one year later, they had disowned him upon hearing of his failures to continue the family’s strong ties with Wall Street, and upon hearing of his marriage to Valerie, who they demanded him not to see... Valerie lifted her head up and looked into her lover’s eyes, sighing a little in nervousness to ask of him to speak to his parents once more. Alexander continued a curious stare until his partner was to ready to put her idea upon him. Valerie: Why don’t you ask your parents...? Alexander: (With a harsh sigh) I knew you would say that... Valerie: Alex, they might be the only ones who can help us out. Like you said, everyone else is so cold and heartless, especially around here. Can’t you just TRY to talk to them? Alexander: It’s not that easy. I don’t even exist to them anymore, just because I didn’t have green running through my blood and money signs for eyeballs. Valerie: They can’t still be mad at you for that –- Alexander: (Interrupting) Of course they are still mad! I’m a failure to the family name for not running the business, and they’ll probably never forgive me. They hate me for marrying you too... Valerie: I didn’t get everything served to me on a silver platter, I had to work for everything on my own. And you know what? I’m damn happy I did it, so they can take their opinions of me and –- Alexander: (Interrupting) now you know why I won’t talk to them. I don’t want to make things worse than they already are between us and them. Valerie: We have to try to bury the hatchet Alex. I mean, we’ll pay them back whenever we get the money. Alexander: The way we are going, that won’t be for a long time. Valerie: (While picking up the phone) can you at least try? Alexander: Can’t... Valerie: Why not? Alexander: Power line is disconnected remember? Valerie: (With a growl) ugh, I forgot. Is there anything else that we can do? Alexander: I’m really not sure. Maybe I can go for a walk and pick up some loose change on the streets, there might be enough out there to go to a pay phone. Valerie: The nearest payphone is three blocks from here. Does the corner store have one? Alexander: Yah, but I was sort of... all ready in there today. Valerie: (With a sign) we have nobody to turn to, it really does look like we’re going to have to deal with this on our own... Alexander turned his head away from his bride, sternly looking at the chipped paint and the wall that lay beneath. He was focused on nothing, and just staring into space, as he hoped to find an answer for their problem. All that came to him were blanks. This situation was becoming too grim for words. Was there anybody out there who could help them? The answer would come shortly, in the form of a door knock. Upon hearing the disturbance, Alexander let go of his wife’s hand, advising her to stay in the room. Scuffing his feet and dragging his legs down the stairs, the pounding on the door gets louder and sterner. Finally, he is able to get down to the door and open it, perhaps ready to face his death if it were a break in or other gang related action. In the doorway stood a man in a long trench coat, his eyes and nose hidden from a brimmed hat. No emotion poured from his face as he stood there with his hands held together, with Alex protecting the door jam to the main room of the house. Man: Are you Alexander Pendragon...? All Alexander could do was nod, and look towards the end of the alleyway complex where a broken down car sputtered. The man in the trench coat kept silent, perhaps too silent, for moments before finally stepping a bit closer to Pendragon. Man: Are you related at all to a Leopold Pendragon? Once again, all Alexander could do was nod and look around him, hoping this altercation would end soon. The man in the trench coat took another long step, coming within inches of Alexander’s body. And then, the man slowly lifted the brim of his cap, and looked the young husband right into the eyes. Man: How are you two related...? Alexander: (Silently) he’s my father... Man: Mister Pendragon, I’m sorry to bring this news to you, especially since I know the predicament you are in at the moment... The man took in a deep breath, sighing out while looking deep into Alexander’s eyes. Slowly moving his hand towards his coat, the man pulls out a badge from his pocket, the badge of an officer of the law. Man: I’m Sheriff Lawrence Jones of the NYPD. We came a long way to find you... Alexander: (With much confusion) what’s going on? Lawrence: (Silently) your father, Mister Pendragon, died suddenly the other day... Alexander stood there with a shock in his eyes, as sweat started to develop under his hairline. Sheriff Jones put his badge away as we heard a jingling around. In the background of all this, we see that Valerie has snuck her way down the stairs, and is standing patiently in the main room. Valerie: Alex, what’s going on? Alexander: (Turning to Valerie) I thought I told you to stay upstairs... Valerie: Alex, tell me what’s going on! Through all the wounds and scars, Pendragon would flourish, and eventually overcome the biggest bully in another town, creating his wrestling style to be carved in stone forever. After a while, Iron Lung was born. The road was long and hard, but with each success on the street, came a new opportunity for the young star. Soon enough, he grew tired of the fighting life, and sought out for some professional training, finding a dojo advertisement in his local newspaper. Lung would travel cross-country to learn his new discipline, but when he would return, he would have left his name on the dojo. He became the youngest Dojo Championship winner at eleven years old, completing the course months before his twelfth birthday. Now back in his home state, Lung searched for work. The world was his oyster, and he had to take any opportunity that was open to make ends meet once more. Finding a dead-end curtain puller job at a local federation at first, he was fired after one event for badmouthing the competition. Now with his back to the wall, he was given the chance to shine again for one of his best bosses, Rob Hearon. Championships constantly garnished Lung’s waist inside of Hearon’s organization, and this is mostly where he learned the Hardcore way of life. He also accomplished a feat that still stands in the history books, winning three matches along with three Championships, all in the same night. But soon enough, the organization would leave the superstar behind, and he would have to keep his head high in this time of depression. Lung would search around for almost a year before finding a full-time job, it was always the same time of corporations that were hiring him, they shut down before the blink of an eye. There were many times where he would want to walk away all together, but he had his friends and his training to keep him strong in mind. Finally, the superstar would get his chance in Lights Out Wrestling, headed by one Trigger. This is where he would prove himself worthy to be a competitor, as he would stay in this home past their closing and back into their new generation. Lung would also take part in the UWF, where once again, he would prove himself worthy to fight for the greatest title of them all, and he would step up to another Hardcore challenge. All these thoughts went through the young Global Title contenders mind as he stroked his palm against his parents’ grave. He stood up from his prayer, and looked into the sky, searching for an answer as to why the Creator would take them away from his life so soon. Everything served a purpose, and he would have to find out that answer on his own. But for now, this child turned into a man by way of the streets, was on another fight for survival. Just like his family, he had no one to turn to in this situation. He was facing the biggest challenge of his life, and no one was around to help him this time. It was five other men, one belt, and one ladder to get there. This was almost impossible to win, but Lung had dealt and overcome the impossible before, would this be another situation? It wasn’t about talk anymore about being the greatest, it was about proving it in the ring. He had to pursue his dream of being in the spotlight, he had to make the crowd get on his side somehow. He was trained up to speed to defend against any attack, but this is where the other intangibles kicked in. He was too honored in his ways to fall too easily, he would surely be something to match up with. But would everyone else take him seriously? They had better...
