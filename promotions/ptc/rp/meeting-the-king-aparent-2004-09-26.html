Meeting the king aparent

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 Serpenti stood once more on his balcony. Breakfast sat on the table next to him, his small but silent staff accommodating their employer with barely being seen. If Serpenti did not know they were there staying out of view and mind. He would have thought things were done by magic. It had been a few days and he had not seen anything else suspicious. The last few days were blissful, just Kitty and himself. God he needed the relaxation, the release of stress. Serpenti looked over at his little muse. His inspiration. She slept curled up in the fluffy green comforter. He still did not know who sent a letter in his name, but the effects of it. He enjoyed. Kitty had a strange away of making everything vanish. Serpenti silently wished that he could stop time and have things stay like this forever. He shook his head knowing that it was all but impossible. They had their lives to live and careers to think about. Well both of Serpenti’s. Serpenti leaned against the wall and picked up his juice and drank it down. “Mango and passion fruit. Humm the Cook must be poking fun at me.” Serpenti thought. He took another sigh. No wonder his parents were so happy here. So peaceful and quiet, quite easy to get lost with each other. Serpenti chuckled to his self again looking over to Kitty. … Kitty! Gone? “Kitty?” Serpenti said nervous. “Yeah!” Kitty’s said causing Serpenti to spin around finding her leaning out the bathroom window. “Ahh There you are. For a moment I thought…” Serpenti said before getting cut off by Kitty. “I was gone? NO silly. I’ll be right there.” Kitty said flashing Serpenti a smile. Serpenti Sighed a happy sigh and leaned against the cool brick wall and closed his eyes. Shortly a warm had rested on his chest.. “Wake up!” Kitty said causing Serpenti’s eyes to snap open. “Ohh! I must have drifted back to sleep.” Serpenti said stretching. “Look. The sun is fixing to rise.” Kitty said flashing Serpenti a little smile. Serpenti smiled. Kitty did not think him odd for wanting to feel their first rays of the morning sun, or tease him about it. Instead she joined and shared his morning ritual with him. It was nice sharing one of the things that he enjoyed since being a young boy. If one could look inside one would swear the sun was already rising inside Serpenti. It had been a long time since he felt the way he did about Kitty. Not since… since Carmen. Serpenti’s mood changed slightly and he sighed and drank the rest of his juice. ”You know. I have been wondering something. What’s up with the juice man? Are you some sort of health nut or something?” Kitty asked teasingly. “Ahh. The juice. I like it, and it gives my body balance. And no I am not a heath nut. I just like fruit juice. Offsets the rum.” Serpenti said with a smile and a wink. “Ahh, Got ya.” Kitty said with a wink. The two stood in silence as the sun once again rose up and bathed them both in its morning rays of light. Absorbing the warming feel, burning off the cool mist from being out side in the early morning hours. For Serpenti it was like being rejuvenated. Serpenti looked at Kitty eyeing the food, and smiled to his self. He knew Kitty shared this ritual with him not cause she wanted to be up so early, but she did it just to be with him. “Man… I am starving. Let’s eat.!” Kitty said leaping into the chair and pulling her legs under her bottom. Slowly she removed the covering from the plates revealing the breakfast. On Serpenti’s plate; scrambled eggs, seasoned potatoes, tortillas and a grilled pork chop. On Kitty’s; scrambled eggs, Canadian bacon, and french toast with and a bowl of hot maple syrup. “You like chica?” Serpenti asks taking a bite of his potatoes. ”MMMM!” Kitty purred taking a bite of the french toast. “Delicious. This is so like my fa..” “Favorite breakfast? Si, I know. I paid the guy at the Global Arena to tell me. Don’t worry, your secret is safe with me.” Serpenti said smiling. Kitty dropped her fork with a clang into her plate, and looked at Serpenti in shock. Her face turning a paler shade as she looked around nervously. “What is it Chica? Did I do wrong?” Serpenti asked concerned. Kitty recovered fast and shook off the feeling of dread and nervousness. “No… It’s nothing. I am okay. Let’s finish eating. You said you had something important to do to day.” Kitty said trying to change the subject. “Something right there shocked her and scared her to death, seams like I am not the only one with demons in my past and secrets in the present.” Serpenti thought. “Yes. I have a meeting with Vennemen Pendragon.” Serpenti said instead, respecting Kitty’s privacy and boundaries. Kitty smiles a little making her self cheer up from that dreadful feeling. “The Television champion? Can I come along?” Kitty asked while finishing her breakfast. “Should I let her find out about that part of me? Do I show ultimate trust?” Serpenti thought to his self. “Very well chica. You can come with me. But before we go, there is something I have to tell you that you do not know about me.” Serpenti said once again causing Kitty to look nervous. Slowly Kitty slipped the knife from the table, hiding it in the sleeve of her robe. She adjusted her position as Serpenti stood up and walked to the edge of the balcony. Kitty for the first time eyed Serpenti with suspicion. Serpenti however oblivious as he looked out to the sea. “Kitty, I don’t know if you will like this or hate me because of it. I hope you can understand. Fighting in the ring, is not my only life. I love doing it. But Since a boy I have been what some would call a thief. But I say no… I am a pirate. I hunter of Lost treasures” Serpenti said waiting for Kitty’s response. Kitty sat there in complete shock. Slowly her body relaxed and she sat the knife back onto he table, stood up and walked over to Serpenti. Letting out a small laugh. “Is that all? That’s great! You had me going for a moment. Thought you were going to turn out to be some sort of psycho killer.” Kitty said with a laugh as she hugged Serpenti from behind. Both stood there embraced in each other’s arms and in silence for a while. The only sound heard were a few seagulls flying overhead, and the crashing of the waves in the distance. “Want to see my ship?” Serpenti asked breaking the silence. “Sure. I would love to see your ship.” Kitty said pulling away from Serpenti. “Then I shall see you down stairs in a moment mi amore.” Serpenti said. Serpenti turns to leave but Kitty caught his hand and pulled him to her. The two shared a deep kiss before turning to finish getting ready for the morning. La Máscara Carmesí’s ship set hidden behind a large rock outcropping in the cove. Castle Pendragon sowed its tops peaking over the tears in the distance. She sat hidden well in the rocks, looking out with her digital binoculars. La Máscara Carmesí looked down at her ship. In stark contrast with Serpenti’s ship. Hers was completely modern in the design, gone was the wood replaced with mettle. Sails replaced with a large engine. La Máscara Carmesí ship was a large black yacht; with a lot of custom modifications. “Serpenti is stupid to climbing to the old ways of piracy.” La Máscara Carmesí thought admiring her ship. “Soon his stupidity would cease. Soon the webs of her trap would be pulled tight” La Máscara Carmesí smiled at the thought. She turned her attention back to the entrance to the small cove. “It was only a matter of time.” She thought continuing her search. The Ship sailed thought the waters of the North Atlantic Ocean with ease. A strong breeze has the sails filled, pushing the ship along the water. Today… Today Serpenti was content in letting his first mate. Decker Bledsoe sail the ship. Bledsoe a rough looking man that could scare the life out of man, his rough exterior often hid the honorable nature of the Pirate. Over the years Bledsoe and Serpenti had become great friend and He was the only crewman that stayed at his captain’s side as the treacherous La Máscara Carmesí turned on Serpenti. Serpenti trusted Decker with his ship… and his life and knew the man would fight to the death for him. Serpenti stood at the front of the ship with Kitty, hugging her from behind. The water sprayed up over the bow and sprayed the two here and there. Serpenti inhaled the intoxicating mix of Kitty’s perfume and the cool sea air, and was in heaven. “This is awesome.” Kitty said laughing and leaning into Serpenti. “Si it is chica. Come let’s go below. We will arrive at our destination soon.” Serpenti said in Kitty’s ear. Kitty nodded and the two headed back and went down the steps to the lower hold of the ship. Serpenti unlocked a door leading into a separate room of the hold; his equipment room. Talon had made many modern adjustments to the old styled ship. This room was one of them. Talon was to only other person who had access to another similar room called the Amory of the ship. Weapons for the crew and the ammunition for large guns on the gun deck were stored in the armory. This room however was Serpenti’s private room. Filled with all the tools a persona in his profession might need. Kitty’s eyes closed as she slowly slid her fingers across a katana hanging on the wall. She swayed as if in a dream. Her eyes opened just as fast and she turned to Serpenti who was strapping on several belts with pouches. He decision made and mind set. “I am going with you.” Kitty said. Serpenti stopped pitting his gear on in mid motion. He turned to face Kitty who stood there with her hands on her hips. “Kitty… I love you and you’re a great fighter. But. This, this is not for you mi amore.” Serpenti said sadly. Kitty shook her head and walked up to Serpenti and poked him in the chest. “Are you sure of that? Because I am not. I… I don’t know why but all I do know is that I need to come with you. Please, please don’t ask why.” Kitty said looking down and leaning against him. Serpenti then realized that he knew nothing of Kitty’s past. He knew almost nothing of her life. He sighed deeply. “What had she been though? Why did she not want to tell him?” Serpenti thought in his mind. Serpenti looked down at Kitty for the first time with very different eyes. It was like looking at a puzzle that needed to be figured out. ”You may come mi amore.” Serpenti said pulling her closer and kissing her on the top of her head. “Thank you. I don’t know why. I just feel I need to be there.” Kitty said hugging Serpenti hard, before pulling back to kiss him deeply. The two shared the kiss before pulling back, grabbing gear. Slowly Kitty lifted up a leather trench coat. It was not it for a male, but for a female. Kitty looked over at Serpenti and cleared her throat getting his attention. “I guess Talon thought of every thing.” Serpenti said with a shrug. Kitty nodded and pulled it on. Looking at her self in the mirror she knew she was ready. How or why she did not know, but she just knew she was... It felt right. The two stood there ready for anything. “So. What the plan dude?” Kitty asked. “Meet with Pendragon.” Serpenti said. Kitty looked at there reflection and motioned to the mirror. “Really? Then why are we dressed like we are about to wade into a fight?” Kitty asked smirking. “Best to be prepared for anything.” Serpenti said with a smile. Kitty nodded and the two headed out of the door. Kitty stopped and grabbed the katana; on a last thought before heading out the door. La Máscara Carmesí watched as the cristal se levantó passed her and her crews hiding spot. Watched as the cristal se levantó dropped anchor. Watched as the small boat was dropped into the water and El Serpenti and his new girl Kitty Mac rowed to and into the river inlet. She waited until she was sure they was gone. La Máscara Carmesí stood up from her hiding position. Her dark leather outfit creaking with the sudden movement. She stood there and smiled. “Perfect.” She said in her seductive voice. La Máscara Carmesí was pleased. Part two of her trap was now in effect. La Máscara Carmesí climbed down and jumped onto the deck of her ship. “LOOK ALIVE MATES!!! We have a ship to take!” La Máscara Carmesí shouted rousing the hidden crew to life. Cheers went up as La Máscara Carmesí took the wheel and the ship was powered up to life. With a roar the black ship set off after its target. Decker Bledsoe head snapped to attention as the loud roar sounded from the port side of the ship. The crew nervously looked off into hidden areas of the cove. The crew cast suspicious notions as to the cause of the noise. “Some unknown sea monster? Maybe a dragon!” They cried out. “Pipe down ya dogs!” Decker shouted getting his crew under control. Then he noticed it. The Black ship Speeding at them. A flag with the red flame of La Máscara Carmesí fluttered in the air. “There would only be a small chance to win this.” Decker thought to his self. “Ready the guns!! Rouse Eagleclaw and get weapons!!!” Decker barked out sending the crew to complete his order. “GET Thos guns loaded NOW!!! “Guns ready sir!!!” Shouted a crewman. “THEN FIRE!!!” Decker bellowed. The guns blasted rocking the ship sending the cannonballs though the air. La Máscara Carmesí stood at her wheel smiling as she sped towards her target. Looking through her binoculars she spied him and hissed. “Decker!” La Máscara Carmesí screamed. She could not believe her fate. Bledsoe was a more then worthy advisory.. Then it happened the guns from the cristal se levantó blasted. “Cannonballs? How quaint.” La Máscara Carmesí thought to her self “Typical romantic stupidity of Serpenti” The first cannonballs came into contact with the water and exploded. “EXPLODED!!” La Máscara Carmesí mind shouted. “Evasive maneuvers. They have exploders!” La Máscara Carmesí shouted. “Return Fire!!! Prepare to board!!!” Decker smiled as the black ship spun around and retreated a bit. But his smile turned to a soul as he saw the ship maneuver past the cannonballs. “DAMN!” Decker shouted, The surprise spoiled. “Take cover! Prepare to be boarded!” Decker shouted to his crew as the sounds of machinegun fire ripped through the air the bullet belting the sides of the ship. Here and there an unlucky crewman screamed before tumbling over the side of the large ship. Soon the black ship pulled along side and the boarding ramps tossed hooking into the side of the larger ship. The black clothed pirates from La Máscara Carmesí’s ship quickly boarded. Pulling swords and other weapons. La Máscara Carmesí leaped onto the ship to be met by Decker Bledsoe. “I figured you had enough Decker, after the last time we met!” La Máscara Carmesí “Thinking was never your strong point Santiago.” Decker said with a scowl. “DIE!” La Máscara Carmesí shouted swinging her sword. Decker blocked effortlessly. The River was swift and it did not take long till they approached the large castle that loomed before them. The brick white walls of the castle seamed to sparkle as the golden flags fluttered high above, each flag adorned with the red dragon symbol letting all know what this castle was and whom it belonged to. “WOW!” Kitty said amazed at the size and beauty of the castle. “Welcome to Castle Pendragon, mi amor.” Serpenti said softly. As they landed the boat and climbed out a loud roar off in the distance sounded. Kitty spun around pulling out her guns on instinct. “What was that?” Kitty asked as she slowly placed her guns away. “Don’t know but word is, those flags are not the only dragons in this land.” Serpenti said as he slid the boat into the brush. “Come we must hurry. I have got to meet with this man and then get to London.” ” What is in London?” Kitty asked. “Fnord.” Serpenti said before heading down the dirt trail. “Fnord? What the hell is a Fnord?” Kitty shouted as she sped up to catch up with Serpenti. “There is a man claiming he has audience with you my lord.” A balding man said bowing before a large muscular blond haired man. The man known the world over as Lord Vennemen Pendragon sat on his throne in his castle. His golden shield with the red dragon crest rested against the throne to his left. A empty place for a sword to his right Pendragon snapped out of his musings and looked at the bald man. “Doth this man haveth a name?” Pendragon asked. “Aye sire! He calls his self El Serpenti. He is with a young lady as well.” The balding man said. “Hummm… Why would a fellow Global roster member come here to visit him?” Pendragon thought to his self. “Let them in then.” Pendragon said with a wave of his hand. “Fighters from that Wrastlin place you play in Pendragon?” The man known as Merlin asked. “Aye Merlin. Indeed. Fellow fighters they be.” Pendragon said. The doors creaked open as El Serpenti and Kitty Mac walked into the large room. The large doors slowly opened and Serpenti and Kitty looked to each other. “Does it seam to you like we are not expected Serpenti?” Kitty asked. Voicing the same though in Serpenti’s head. “Si. It does. How odd.” Serpenti said. The two walked into the room, Sitting before them was the large figure known as Vennemen Pendragon and an older man in a white beard and a mettle skullcap. “What be thine reason to visit Serpenti? “ Pendragon asked. “Well I had thought you got my letter.” Serpenti said confused. “I have not been presented with any letter.” Pendragon said leaning forward in his throne. “Well that is odd. Your man Ribald replied. Matters not. I need a part of a map that is in your possession.” Serpenti said. Kitty looked at Serpenti and the old man raised an eyebrow. “I have many a ma…” Vennemen Pendragon started when Merlin touched his shoulder and bent down to whisper in his ear. After a few moments Merlin stood back up. “The map you seek stranger, is not here.” Merlin said “I know this amigo. I know it is not in this world. But I do need it.” Serpenti said getting more stares from Kitty. “Tell me Serpenti. Why doth thou seek this map part?” Pendragon asked. Serpenti nodded. It was a deserving question. “I seek a treasure my father sought. I must complete this task my father sat out to complete.” Serpenti said hopefully. “Aye a noble reason. Ver…” Again Pendragon stopped interrupted by Merlin. After a few moments and Serpenti coming to the conclusion, he did not like this Merlin guy. Merlin stood back up. “Serpenti. I require you to go on a Quest for me in order to get the map.” Pendragon said. “Here we go again. Why could nothing ever be easy.” Serpenti thought. “Very well. What quest must I complete su majestad?” “Fear not Serpenti I shalt be with you in thine quest. I only need you to accompany me.. I need you to help me reclaim mine stolen sword; Excalibur.” Pendragon said. Serpenti’s eyes lit up. This he did not mind doing. Retrieving that which was stolen was a honorable task. “I’ll do it su majestad.” Serpenti said with a smile. “NO!” Kitty screamed.” We will do it!” “Si… We will do it su majestad.” Serpenti said. “Good then it is settled.” Pendragon said. “But I must be in London tomorrow night. I have a battle against Fnord.” Serpenti said. Pendragon nodded and walked down to Serpenti, and placed his large hand on his shoulder and smiled. “Don’t worry, the time flow different in the other world. And Merlin casts a spell on us and we age not a day.” Pendragon said smiling. “Good news. What are we waiting for?” Serpenti said. “We are not waiting for we are already here. This castle is in both places at the same time. Merlin has already cast the spell to transfer us to the castle in the other world. Come. You must be starving. Tonight we feast!” Pendragon said with a hearty slap to Serpenti’s back. Pendragon walked away leaving Serpenti and Kitty standing there. “You have some explaining to do Serpenti.” Kitty said as she turned to follow Pendragon. Serpenti stood there watching both walk away, he took a sigh and hurried to catch up. One by one Decker’s crew fell to the crew of La Máscara Carmesí. Decker though battled La Máscara Carmesí sending her on retreat. Decker ducked a wild swing from La Máscara Carmesí and tripped her and pressed the tip of his sword to her throat. “Tell your men to surrender, or so help me I will run your black heart though!” Decker shouted. La Máscara Carmesí laughed out loud causing Decker to think her totally insane… At least till the sharp pain filled his back, ripped though his body, and exploded though his chest in the form of a large sword. Decker looked down to see the sword sticking from his body. Grasping it with his hands to try and stop any more damage. Blood sprayed onto the laughing La Máscara Carmesí as she stood up. “Well look who has been ran though his self. Fare the well Decker Bledsoe. You were a worthy adversary. But in the end only one shall win any encounter.” La Máscara Carmesí said laughing as she did. The large man kicked Decker off his sword sending him over the ship’s railing down into the deep blue sea. “Once again you have pleased your captain Sanchez!” La Máscara Carmesí said. “Come we have wasted enough time. I must cast the spell at the right time to echo Merlin’s spell!” Sanchez said harshly causing La Máscara Carmesí to frown. But she knew he was right. Sanchez stood center ship. Eyes closed, one massive hand on the main mast, his other raised in the air as he chanted. Suddenly a green glow came from inside his raised hand and he tossed a glowing green ball into the air as an unseen air ripple spread from the castle. The ripple pulled to the source of the glowing ball encased the ship and vanished. Sanchez dropped to his knees gasping for air. “Is it done?” La Máscara Carmesí asked. “Si! It is done.” Sanchez said between breaths. The large man staggered to his feet and La Máscara Carmesí steadied him. She needed this man and she knew it. He was her ticket back home. The knights in the tower next to the small fishing village were taken aback as out of thin air appeared… A Pirate ship!
