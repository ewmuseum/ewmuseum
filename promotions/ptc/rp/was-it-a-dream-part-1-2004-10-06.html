Was it a dream...? (Part 1)

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-06 Emotions. Most of us if not all have felt one or two in our lives. Hate, sadness, joy, pain, remorse, and anger are just a few. But what are the true triggers in our lives that cause us to feel these emotions? For some they are things that we wish would come true every day. Some of them coming from a last minute hit of the ball at our son’s baseball game. Some may be the joy of seeing our little girl born in the hospital and getting to hold her for the first time. Yet some are so happy that in the vast amount of emotions in our everyday lives, we feel the one emotion that haunts us to the break of death, Pain. But that pain doesn’t just hold us down. It holds us tight with both arms and doesn’t let go. To the point that we can’t get out and we start to suffocate. We try running away but sooner or later it drags us in to it’s grave and makes us see what it wants us to see. Blinding us with it’s immortality. But running gets you know where. How do I know? Because I felt it first hand all my life. But the most pain I ever felt was the selfishness that I had that took away my father. Tearing away that very man that I loved. But as I sit here, looking out at the rainy day that has traveled into my big town, I sense that it’s touchier, it’s joy of my suffering, was far from over. It was early. The sun wasn’t even out and yet I wasn’t even asleep. It had been not even two days after we had buried my dad and I could still feel him gasping for air. Trying to call out my name one last time but death holding him back. But I somehow had to go out. Even though the energy for life was taken away from me, I had a strong step to climb. In five days, I had one name that was running through my mind. That name? “Jamie Fletcher…” I tried to find some way to get my mind off my father but I still felt as if it was my fault. The tragic death that he had to endure was to me, somehow connected with my desire’s and feeling for wrestling. But yet at the same time I couldn’t dear take my mind off what I had to do. I kept looking out at the grassy front yard that laid in the front view of the house. I just sat there, staring out the window as if it was something different. I kept my hands wrapped around the coffee cup that I had gotten just moment’s from sitting down. As I sat, I felt warm. Wearing the blue button up t-shirt that my dad had gotten me just a few months ago. I just sat there, thinking of what I had to do and what needed to be done. But my undisturbed thinking was soon to be interrupted. “What the?” As I looked out. Thinking about anything I could other then my dad, a faceless man came into the picture. Standing beside the oak tree that stood tall right next to the driveway of the house. His clothing was very familiar. He almost seemed to be staring right at me. I could only see his eye’s at the very first glance. They looked cold and weak. Showing sight’s of old age. And as the minutes passed, what I had been thinking about for the last two days would soon come true. “It can’t be…” I said, letting the coffee cup drop out of my hands and to the carpet. It couldn’t be true. I saw him lay there on that bed. As the man stood still, his pitch black face started to come to life. It was my father. But how could it be? I saw him die right in front of me. I could hear the last breaths he made all the way to them lowing his body into the depths of the earth. I grabbed my mouth with both hands, trying not to scream. But what joy might bring, another emotion might take away. As the man, my father, or so it seemed stood there, looking right into my window, another man appeared. Standing right behind my father. His face was very familiar as well. Though there was something about him that made me scared. As he stood there, not eyeing the likes of me, he stood tall right behind my father. I watched as the man grabbed my father around the neck, pulling him with all his might with a rope. “Father…” I screamed out loud. Slamming my hands on the window with all my mind. This couldn’t be happening. So many different things went through my head as my hands came against the glass one final time. The sound of breaking glass took over the room. I watched the other man’s face stop and look up at me. With one more glance of my angry and scared eyes, looking through the glass shattered window, I could see who the man was. “JAMIE NO!” The very opponent that I was to face next. But how could it be? I placed my hands on my face. Trying to rub my eyes as a felt a cold draft come through my body. I took my hands away, looking at them as the stains of my red cover blood covered them. But the draft wasn’t over. I turned around, face to face with the same man, Jamie Fletcher or so he was, as he stared right at me. My body wouldn’t move. As if I was in a cage tied to ever bar that restricted me from moving. I kept asking myself the same question. “Help me Thomas…” My lips screamed as I kept looking over at Thomas who was laying in the bed. Trying to break free from something that didn’t have a hold of me. The man then grabbed me by the neck. Pressing hard with both hands, his face changed. Turning to the face of my father. The same dark blue eyes. The same small and smooth hands that he had when I last held him. My lungs couldn’t move, falling to the floor, as the man with my father face kept choking. I screamed out one last time. Trying to get the attention of Thomas as I felt the icy cold chill that came with death. I wasn’t ready. I could feel my world coming down on me. Trapping my selfishness and placing it as the death charge on me. I tried to get free as my voice screamed. “THOMAS….” I looked up. Seeing Thomas standing over me with his hands on my shoulders. I quickly looked around. Not remembering anything that was just in my eye sight. “Where am I?” I said, not knowing who and where I was as I looking around the dark room. Was it all a dream? Was my mind trying to tell me something that I couldn’t understand? Was did it all mean. I looked up at Thomas, my eyes beginning to water up as a single tear rolled down my cheek. For the first time I was scared. Scared to be alone. Scared of wrestling. And most of all, scared of myself. My emotions were starting to catch up with me and I wasn’t ready to cry. I wasn’t ready to shed a tear for my father and maybe that is what it meant. Thomas pulled away from me, whipping away the tear that came out. “You were dreaming but you are safe with me now.” His words were true and honest. I was safe with him. He sat down beside me as I looked out the window, trying to find something that made sense. But all of this didn’t make sense. I could still feel my father near me. Not so much in my mind but in my soul. And that was something I needed for my fight with Jamie Fletcher. Two days ago was the end of my life. Tonight was the beginning of my new one. Maybe that was what my father was trying to say. To being my new life I was begin it against Jamie. I turned to Thomas, looking at his face as he held me tight and close. “Thank you.” I said. Giving him a small kiss on the cheek before laying my head on his shoulder. My future was in front of me. With the death of my father, it ended more then what I expected. It ended my father’s life. It ended my life as I knew it but one think I didn’t know was it helped build a new one. A new one with Thomas. I closed my eyes right as the sun started to rise and I couldn’t help but think of what my new life would bring.
