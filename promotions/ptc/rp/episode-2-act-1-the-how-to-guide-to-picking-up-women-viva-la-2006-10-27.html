Episode 2, ACT 1 : The How To Guide to Picking up Women/Viva La Revolucionez~!

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-27 EXT. AIRPORT -- HONG KONG -- DAY [EDDIE SCOTT POSER has advanced to a Nintendo DS, playing it like a small child on the edge of the sidewalk; wide open basking in the sunlight. Tony Davis stands next to him, legs firmly planted on the top of a garbage can, scanning the area. Jack Harmen, aka High Flyer, stands directly behind them. A fishing cap covered his eyes from view. Whether that was a result of a black eye given to him by the accountant or him attempting to poker face a lack of annoyance was anyone's guess. Poser continues to literally move with the game, jumping back a bit as he gets hit inside the virtual world. However, when a large van pulls up and parks not one foot away from him, Poser didn't even flinch.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Even though I don't understand it, Mother 3 is awesome. HIGH FLYER: Don't taunt me with games I can't understand. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Final Fantasy 3, Children of Mana. Dude, my schedule's packed. HIGH FLYER: Who gave you the five hundred dollar loan? EDDIE SCOTT POSER: [proud of self] Who asked for the five hundred dollar loan? HIGH FLYER: Oh yes. And what exactly did you tell me the loan was for? EDDIE SCOTT POSER: [playing the game intently] … Bacon. HIGH FLYER: I believe you said it was to, and I quote, "Fund a revolution." EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Viva la pokemon. HIGH FLYER: Do you have any of the five hundred dollars left Eddie? EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I knew you'd ask for it back, that's why I spent it all. HIGH FLYER: Welp, that's just great. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: It is. HIGH FLYER: There aren't any more flights out of Hong Kong. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: They're closing the airport? HIGH FLYER: Today. There's no more today. And our plan of staying in that van? Well, it's not showin' up. So we've gotta stay the night. [Tony's head perks up, and he twists over toward Flyer's direction in a confused state.] TONY DAVIS: Wait, we weren't gonna stay the night? HIGH FLYER: Oh that's right. You were sleeping on the way over. TONY DAVIS: Hey, if I want to recharge with a thirty minute power nap, that's my business. Don't believe it gives you powers. HIGH FLYER: Why should we stay here tonight. TONY DAVIS: Cuz I scored some awesome dates. HIGH FLYER: Really? TONY DAVIS: Oddly, they're Italian Filipino twins. HIGH FLYER: I'm only willing to believe you because you said twins. But what are you gonna do during this? TONY DAVIS: Dude, (Makes a radio sound while clutching his wrist close to his head) Chhhh, Wingman. It's been ages. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: What about me? TONY DAVIS: Go play your ds on the rooftop. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: But I don't have a large enough ladder, or a roof. Although I could use the airport roof. [Tony turns away from Poser in sheer disbelief, and begins to ignore him.] TONY DAVIS: And you'll get the twofer twinner. It's everyone's dream. HIGH FLYER: Everyone's? TONY DAVIS: Well, it sure is mine. HIGH FLYER: You don't actually have any of that, do you. TONY DAVIS: No, but I won't rest until I do. [Tony Davis yawns.] HIGH FLYER: Do you want another nap. TONY DAVIS: It's this time zone. I swear. [Tony walks over to the nearest bench and lies down, throwing his legs up over the legs of an elderly lady, sitting on the benches edge.] END OF TEASER -- THEME SONG MONTAGE INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH -- EVENING [HIGH FLYER sits down on a toilet inside of a hotel room. Behind him is a large mirror that reflects the image of the camera back into the camera, but Flyer doesn't realize this. Regardless.] HIGH FLYER: I remember love… it's a good feeling. What am I saying. That's like saying ice cream is cold. Which it almost invariantly is unless you're improperly describing milk… which kinda brings us full circle on the relationships of my life. [Flyer draws a circle with his hands.] HIGH FLYER: That's why circles are evil. Patterns, redundancy's, hell, deja vu's, I hate them all. Especially deja vu. I mean, is that like, me repeating myself from the past or me repeating a dream of myself in the future or is it like some weird ghost that's standing where I'm standing and it's like, what ghost sex feels like. INT. HOTEL ROOM -- EVENING [EDDIE SCOTT POSER is playing his ds on a small side table set up near the window of their hotel room, overlooking the city of Hong Kong below them. High Flyer tosses a gym bag over into the corner of the room, and props his feet up onto the nearest bed. He immediately grabs the remote and tosses on the television, showing the local Hong Kong channels.] HIGH FLYER: Yo, they got Law & Order here. Maybe it's less formulaic in Chinese. [TONY walks into the bathroom door and slams it shut.] INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH -- CONTINUOUS [TONY DAVIS enters the room, and walks into the tub on the other side of the room. He lies down, fully clothed, and shuts his eyes.] INT. HOTEL ROOM -- EVENING [Back in the hotel room, Poser is still sitting by the window playing on his DS while Flyer channel surfs. Eddie glances up from his DS over to the bathroom door, and then back to his game. He does this three more times, finally raising the attention of the Lunatic.] HIGH FLYER: I’ve been meaning to ask you something Eddie… EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I didn’t send the van to Pakistan. HIGH FLYER: I know… EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I sent it to Singapore. HIGH FLYER: I know that now. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Oh. HIGH FLYER: My question? Why. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Well… it is our ultimate destination. HIGH FLYER: Can’t you see where your logic failed you now? EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Logic fails unfathomably. [Flyer does a double take on Poser’s quip, but then proceeds to let the subject drop.] -- HOURS LATER -- INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH – EVENING [TONY DAVIS snores ever so slightly inside the bathroom tub, before rolling over onto his side. It seems he has also acquired a small piece of straw, which is hanging out the edge of his lips.] INT. HOTEL ROOM – EVENING [EDDIE SCOTT POSER sits in the corner of the room, alone. He plays his DS as the television goes off in the background. Suddenly, a commercial on the television gains Poser’s undetectable attention, which is saying something for a guy with ADD. It’s a commercial for a place called "Game Station," a ten thousand square foot building filled to the brim with arcade machines.] TV Announcer[Translated via subtitles on Screen] : Come to the Game Station! Welcome all to Game Palace. We have Beat-Mania, Guitar Mania, and Dance Dance Revolution. Show your "mad skills!" [Eddie Scott Poser stands up and throws his fist in the air triumphantly.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I SHALL show them ALL, my angry skills. RUAHH~! [Poser literally charges out the hotel room, pocketing his ds in the process.] INT. HOTEL HALLWAYS – EVENING [EDDIE POSER swings wildly out of the hotel room, literally sprinting to the nearest elevator. He passes by the Neighborhood Lunatic on his way, and the camera remains on him. Flyer is talking on his cell phone, pacing back and forth a bit as he does.] HIGH FLYER: Hey, it’s Jack. I just wanted to call you… I’m in Hong Kong now. I’ve joined this tournament. PTC it’s called. Based out of Australia of all places. Don’t worry, it’s not the WWA, Jeff Jarrett’s not gonna smack me with a guitar or anything. Tell Allocca I love him. I’m really only calling to tell you I sent you a package, you should be getting it in the next few days. Anyway, since I’m talkin’ to a machine I think I’ll hang up right about now. [Flyer flips the top end of the phone down and stuffs it into his pocket. He looks around slightly, no longer seeing Poser on this specific floor of the building, and shrugs his shoulders.] INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH – EVENING [TONY DAVIS continues to rest comfortably. But by this point, the documentary crew that has shared his room during all this time has grown increasingly impatient. They begin to poke Tony with a stick, and do it three more times before Davis finally wakes up, tossing his arms in the air in a mess of confusion.] TONY DAVIS: Stay away from my nuts you radioactive squirrels! [Tony scans the room for a second, and realizes he’s safe.] TONY DAVIS: It’s close to hibernating season… INT. HOTEL ROOM – CONTINUOUS [HIGH FLYER enters the room, and immediately walks to the bathroom door. He tosses his fist up to knock, but that’s when the door swings wide open, and Flyer basically punches Tony in the face from it.] HIGH FLYER: Can’t say I haven’t wanted to do that forever. TONY DAVIS: Haha. You ready to paint this town red? HIGH FLYER: I’ve got purple ready on stand by. TONY DAVIS: Sweet. I gotta use the bathroom then we’ll cover this city with our confetti. [TONY re-enters the bathroom door, slamming it behind him.] HIGH FLYER: … You were in the bathroom for six freaking hours! [Flyer sighs.] You’ve really gotta learn time management. INT. GAME STATION – NIGHT [EDDIE SCOTT POSER walks through the opening doors, swinging both open in a motion of power. Almost immediately, Poser’s jaw drops and he stands there awestruck at the number of people and machines before him. It’s almost as if it’s a stadium, developed simply for non-athletes of the world like himself. That’s when the doors he threw open return, smacking Eddie in the face and sending him back outside. Poser falls to a heap on the outside concrete, as a man in a business suit steps over him without breaking stride.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Gah. I was so close! [Eddie scrambles to his feet and once again thrusts both double doors open to enter the complex. He stands around, still taking in the air of everything. A few people over the in corner are watching two gamers shred guitar on guitar-mania, or guitar hero as it’s called over here in the state. Poser’s eyes lock in on the maddenly quick fingers of it’s players, long enough for the double doors to swing back once again, smacking Poser again in the face.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Fool me once, shame on me. Fool me twice, and I HATE YOU. [Poser angrily slams the doors open for a third time, and then quickly moves out of their way. Over in another corner, a few kids are playing Dance Dance Revolution, and Eddie immediately smiles.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Viva la revolution~! INT. BAR/CLUB – NIGHT [TONY DAVIS and HIGH FLYER take seats on the barstool of a club playing loud repetitive music that hasn’t changed since the mid-90’s. Davis and Flyer both order drinks, an Apple Teeny for Tony and a shot for Jack, who downs it almost as quickly as he gets it. Flyer turns his back to the bar and scans the crowd.] TONY DAVIS: Don’t get nervous dude. HIGH FLYER: I don’t get nervous. TONY DAVIS: Bull. I call bull on that. I call so much bull you’re Michael Jordan. HIGH FLYER: Or red Bull. TONY DAVIS: Or Running with the Bulls. HIGH FLYER: Or paying the bills. TONY DAVIS: What? HIGH FLYER: I got sick of mentioning famous bulls. TONY DAVIS: You just didn’t have another. HIGH FLYER: You see through me like a glass mirror at a strip club with a cum stain. TONY DAVIS: Mine’s streaky. [DAVIS begins to play with his index and thumb fingers, mimicking a sticky substance.] HIGH FLYER: Okay! Let’s do this. [Flyer downs another shot and stands up from the bar. He looks around aimlessly for a few seconds.] HIGH FLYER: Okay. How do we do this. TONY DAVIS: Easy. Step one, pick your target. I used to throw darts into a crowd and then give first aid to the person that was struck. It usually worked when you didn’t strike a guy. I’d say you’ve got a 60/40 chance here. HIGH FLYER: We don’t have darts. TONY DAVIS: But we’re in a bar. HIGH FLYER: We’re in a club. It’s different. TONY DAVIS: Drinks. Music. People… I don’t see the difference. HIGH FLYER: Well, there’s not a group of fifteen middle aged guys playing poker in the corner. TONY DAVIS: They probably keep that in the basement. HIGH FLYER: What else have you done to pick up girls? TONY DAVIS: I made flyers and passed them out to people on the streets. It would have the name of the bar we’re attending and it would say free drinks. Then when they would come to claim, I’d be there to buy. HIGH FLYER: How come that didn’t work tonight? TONY DAVIS: I fucking hate AltaVista’s Babel fish. Let’s just leave it at that. INT. HOTEL ROOM – THE DAY BEFORE [TONY DAVIS sits typing on a laptop inside of an empty room. He smiles.] TONY DAVIS: And so, people will come to the Wind Soak Bar at 8 to receive free drinks. Let’s see how it translates. [Tony does a bunch of typing and clicking, moving text and what have you. He seems to look more professional than his actual actions, as everything seems to take him three moves for what would take the normal person three. After a few moments, Tony throws his arms on the table in disgust.] TONY DAVIS: I certainly hope the army isn’t using this to translate… although it sure would explain a lot. INT. GAME STATION – NIGHT [EDDIE SCOTT POSER is next in line at the DDR machine, and he’s got a smile and a hop in his step. In fact, he’s basically warming up, almost jogging in place as he gains fluidity in his legs. His name is shortly called as one of the participants leaves with their head hung low.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: OH boy. Be prepared to be whooped on like a drunkards step child. I’ve got the backing of POLAND on my side… for now. [Poser throws over his cape in a dramatic flare, revealing a large polish flag. He tilts his burger king crown(of which the word Burger has been crossed out and replaced with "Poser"), and scans his opponent. And it was… a small girl? She couldn’t be more than 18, but Poser certainly hoped she was under. Her brunette hair swung in the breeze as she stomped and slammed her feet onto the various directional arrows. Poser stands there almost in awe. Whether it’s from her speed or her looks, who knows. All the while, their one on one contest has already begun. Poser’s eyes widen as he hears various negative sounding Chinese comments, and begins to slam foot in beat with the music.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Oh God! Poland has underestimated you! [He frantically tries to catch up, flailing around as if he was having a seizure. But even so, Eddie Scott Poser was playing DDR like a true King.] INT. BAR/CLUB – NIGHT [TONY finishes his apple teeny and another round of drinks are ordered. Jack immediately downs his, and winces while regaining his eye sight.] TONY DAVIS: C’mon Jack. Just go already. Find your target and annihilate her! Over and over! HIGH FLYER: How can I be so confident when I don’t know who speaks what language. TONY DAVIS: I’ve always found it better when you don’t have to understand them. HIGH FLYER: That’s because you usually put your foot in your mouth, and if you can’t understand them, they can’t understand you. TONY DAVIS: Exactly. Listen, you’re actin’ like a sears catalog tonight. You go out there, flaunt your wears, see if anyone wants expedited shipping. [Flyer turns to Tony with his eyes narrowed.] HIGH FLYER: No one orders from catalogs anymore. TONY DAVIS: Then fine, you’re Amazon dot jp or whatever extension China resides in. Start handin’ out your gift cards. HIGH FLYER: Are you using the word gift cards a euphonism for penis? TONY DAVIS: Aren’t I clever? [Tony literally shoves Flyer away from the bar and into a young woman. It spills her drink all over his dress, and Flyer immediately throws his arms up to apologize. He reaches behind to grab a bar rag, but Tony instead hands him a drink. The same drink she was drinking. Time passes in a montage sequence of sorts. Flyer and Tony speak to this girl and her friend and they dance and sing karaoke. They wander the town as each are progressively getting drunker and drunker from shot to shot. In fact, each different shot usually housed one of the four downing a shot of alcohol in the process. INT. GAME STATION -- EVENING [Eddie Scott Poser is frantically trying to catch up, when the game finally ends, and Poser falls to his knees in defeat. He points and screams at her feet.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: THOSE ARE WMD’S! I SWEAR IT! And you’re hot. I’LL LEANED YOU A DOLLAR! [Security walks up behind him and begins to drag him away.] EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I’ll be back! I SWEAR ON THE LIVES OF POLISH CHILDREN I SHALL BE BACK! [Security literally tosses him out of the double doors, sending him sprawling into a parked car, shoulder first. He winces and cries while lying on the concrete sidewalk.] [A quick time lapse occurs as the sun rises over the city once more.] INT. HOTEL ROOM -- MORNING [TONY DAVIS groggily awakens from his slumber, the sun coming in from the window awakening him. He looks around his surroundings, and it certainly isn’t HIS hotel room. He looks over to his side, and sees the very girl that Flyer had been hitting on last night. He raises his bed sheet ever so slightly, and stares dumbfounded.] TONY DAVIS: Huh… how’d that happen… END OF ACT 1
