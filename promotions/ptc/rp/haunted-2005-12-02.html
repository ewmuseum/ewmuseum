Haunted

2 December 2005

Date: 2005-12-02 "Mr. Wolack, I don't know how to tell you this, but your wife didn't make it." The words still haunt me in my sleep. "My son?" Why wasn't I there? I should have been there. "He didn't make it either." Nothing. It's been over a year and still, nothing. Is it wrong that I don't feel pain from the loss. Or is it that I've led such a tragic life that loss doesn't yet affect me. "Mr. Wolack, I don't know how to tell you this, but your wife didn't make it." I'm finding myself back in Tokyo. My son never even had a chance to come here. Yet I buried him here anyway. "He didn't make it, either." "He didn't make it, either." "He didn't make it, either." Maybe the fact that I never had a chance to meet him made it so that I didn't have a chance to grow that attached. Wrestling the night that she died. The night that my son was born, and too lost his life. The night that I lost a part of me, maybe forever. After I leave here, I'll go visit her parents. They've they've always welcomed me into their home, even after all this time. Days like today, being a wrestler is second to life. Tomorrow will be normal though, as I prepare for another match. Maybe she's why I still do it, maybe Keiko is who gives me the power to get back into the ring after everything that has happened. Maybe it's the guilt of not being there. Maybe the pain that I suffer in the ring is a reminder of the pain that I didn't feel when she passed. The sadness, the sorrow, I felt that. The pain never came. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- I find myself walking though downtown Tokyo. It's been years since I wrestled here. Still, fans come up to me and ask for autographs. At one time, I was as big as Bob Sapp and Ichiro. Now, it's great to have a layer of anonymity. "Can I have your autograph, sir?" This was a British kid. "Who should I make it out to?" Simple question. "Daniel." I signed the paper he gave me and quietly handed it back to him. The kid's name was Daniel. That was my son's name for the few hours that he was alive. Yet it doesn't phase me. "He didn't make it, either." Those words………..haunt me while I'm awake. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- Today, I'm back in Charlotte. Me and the man that I called Dad my entire life have decided that since he wasn't able to be in North Carolina often due to his work schedule, that I would bring his father, Marshall, out to Arizona. I found him a nice apartment, not far from where I live. "Now Samuel, you know that I don't want to leave my friends." The old man is hitting me with his cane. Not very hard, but he's hitting me none-the-less. "Charlie's coming too." I agreed to pay for the apartment. It's a nice two bedroom. Marshall and Charlie have been friends forever, it'll be nice to have them under the same roof. "What about Jeffrey?" Jeffrey, the guy that gets Marshall to go to the bar with him and talk about the days of old, back when Duke's basketball team was all white players. "Jeffrey is trouble, plus I heard he's going to be living in an assisted living center down there." OK, so I lied. This was the only way that I could get him to agree to this. "Where's that pretty little asian girlfriend of yours, anyway?" Sometimes, he doesn't remember that Keiko passed more than a year ago. Alzheimer's is creeping in on him. "She's back in Tokyo?" Where I buried her. "He didn't make it, either." Those words…..they haunt me when I'm moving family. "Whem will I see her again?" "Eventually, Grampa Marshall." I didn't lie, as long as there is Heaven. "Where's that no good father of yours." We never told him that my mother was really my sister. But to me, she was still mom. "Francis is in Germany right now." Marshall knows that he isn't my biological father. He still thinks that my biological father ran out on my sister when she was pregnant with me. We never told him that my father was her father, and that my mother was his secretary. -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- "Sam, can I get a word from you about GTT5?" It's GCW NC-17. I'm in a rather dangerous match, and all the New York press want to talk about is GTT5. They don't care that in about a three hours, I'm going to open the show in a match that is nothing, if not dangerous. Me and and tag team partner are going to battle another tag team on an unstable scaffold, and they want to talk about a tournament that is still over a week away. "What would you like to know?" "What do you think your chances are against Dr. Colin Gilchrist?" Good question. "About as good as his chances are against me." There's my soundbite. "He didn't make it, either." Those words…..they haunt me during interviews. "I think that I'm going to have to dedicate the tournament to my late wife, Keiko, and son, Daniel." There, I make myself a sentimental hero for the fans that will watch GTT5. "Any strategy on how you're going to defeat Gilchrist?" "Let me figure out how to win my match tonight, then talk to me about GTT5." -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- I'm hanging from a railing. I can feel the tether holding this scaffold up trying to give way. Keiko, I need you to give me an extra boost of strength right now. "JB, Wolack is starting to kick at Ehren Moore, trying to get him to release his grip." "Wolack just needs to hold on a little while longer, and he'll have the win for his team." If only Moore would let go of my leg. I just felt a pop. My shoulder is starting to burn. "He didn't make it, either." Those words…..they haunt me when I need them the least. That damn fool, he just wrapped his arms around both of my legs. If he's going to lose, he's going to go down fighting. Keiko, just give me the strength to finish this one off. "He let go! Sam Wolack let go of the scaffold and kicks Ehren Moore right in the face." "Moore hits the mat hard. Wolack crashes to the mat as the bell rings. Wolack and Li have done it!" -+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- Where am I? "Sam. There you are honey. I knew that you won't keep me waiting." I open my eyes. I expected to see a hospital room. Not this meadow. I remember this place. This is where I proposed to Keiko. "Keiko?" "Yes, I'm here." "Where are we?" "Back where we had our first date." This can't be. Am I dead? I look at my arms. My tattoos are back. I had them removed after Keiko and Daniel passed away. Where am I? "I have someone I want you to meet." I see a young child with her. He looks to be how old Daniel would have been, had they survived. "Is that?" "Yes, this is your son." Then all I see is white. "Doctor, we have a heartbeat." No, NO, NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! You took them away from me, again! "This is your son." Those words…..they haunt me upon being reborn.
