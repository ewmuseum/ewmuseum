Gone Campin Part 3

Date: 2004-08-24 A lot of people think they know what’s going to happen, some don’t. It’s those that don’t that would rather just sit back, relax and enjoy what’s sure to be a tremendous show. But nobody can truly know what will happen at PTC Survival, it’s all too unpredictable. The only certainty is that there is indeed no certainty at all… except for that one. It cannot be said that Core Wrestling is certain to beat Four Winds Wrestling, move on to the finals and then certain to win the Mt. Saint Hells match and be deemed the most talented team in all of PTC. No one can say that, nobody knows. But it is a likelihood, cW has to be the favorite going up against 4WW in many people’s minds. Then again, like it has been said, nothing is certain, no one can ever know what WILL happen. Perhaps 4WW could pull it out of the bag. It’s obvious Core doesn’t have the best four wrestlers on it’s roster in the match. No, a dream team for Core would likely be Fred Fusion, Jason Hughes, Eugene Fitzpatrick and Jack Diamond. This is no dream team, this is four men who are ready to make their own history and tread their own road, ready to make a name for themselves. Matt Epic, the Prodigal Son and current Legacy Champion. Munson the Montanian Madman and current Pride champion. Adolph Nilsson, the gay swede and last ever tag champion. And Smiley… the Smiler, and a former Classic Champion. RPW would have been the favorite to win, but they’re out of PTC and out of this pay per view event. NOW would have proven worthy competition, but they no longer run regular shows and it’s doubtful the organization will be able to stand on it’s own two feet much longer. This is what it’s come down to, just Core Wrestling, and 4WW, a repeat of the Cowboys and Redskins, and that isn’t talking about the NFL either, this is talking about real war. And once again it will happen. CW and 4WW will go to war in a no disqualification match. War, sure it’s the most cliché and overused word used about this Survival event, any idiot could think it up and say it. But there’s no word more accurate than war for the mayhem and human sacrifice that will occur as both team’s fight for the pride of their respective promotions. Pride, that’s what Munson is all about. He’s the Core Wrestling Pride champion, he wrestles with a pride and a determination like no one else exactly can. But he’s fought for Core in the past, and lost. In his first ever interpromotional match he wrestled a man by the name of “The Real Deal” from LOW, and lost the match on BDW’s Sunday Night Static show. That was only a few months ago, not at all long enough to be out of Munson’s recent memory. The match flows through his mind as he sits out in his tent on the campground of Lake Mudsling. In just a few short hours it will be over, he’ll have to pack his bags and head on down to the place he wants to be the most, wrestling for Core in PTC Survival. But the match is what’s on his mind at the moment, it isn’t a pleasant memory, or one he’s particularly proud of. He lost the match, why should he be proud of it of course? Suddenly his thoughts are interrupted as there’s a clawing noise on the outside of his tent. What could it be at this hour? Munson has just waken up, it’s just around 7 in the morning. Munson unzips his tent and looks outside to find a small raccoon clawing playfully at the side of the tent. The Montanian Madman owns a farm in Billings, so obviously animals is one thing he is far from afraid of. Munson- “Well boil me in grease pie look what we gots here! Hey there mister coony, whatcha doin to my tent boy?” The raccoon looks up confusedly. Munson- “Well go on now, git yer lil ole rear on outta here, don’t wanna be a nuisance do ya?” The raccoon walks away.
