Adventures in Babysitting Part Two

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 Mike Biddle’s day started with the makings of a fun and productive day but that was all changed by the ring of a doorbell and an unexpected visitor. After finding out that he has three sons and that he was stuck with them for the remainder of the day he still thought that he could get through the day without and trouble, boy was Mike wrong! Mere moments after Ken, Ben, and Glen entered the house they knocked over Mike’s newly acquired aquarium and caused quite a lot of water damage to the furniture in the living room. Mike couldn’t help but compare this horrible situation to the upcoming situation at the Survival pay-per-view; he just had a feeling that his sons were better behaved than his teammates though. However, the current situation was completely unknown to Hillary Watson as she parked her pink sports car in Mike’s driveway. She stepped out of the car with barely contained excitement for she expected an activity completely different from the one Mike actually had planned for her. She was a beautiful woman with intoxicating nymph like features and blonde hair that shimmered like finely spun gold in the California sun. Luckily for Mike, Hillary had decided to wear her new Doug Mientkiewicz Red Sox jersey and jeans instead of her little black dress that doesn’t leave anything up to the imagination. A good decision since unbeknownst to her Mike would of had a fit if she wore that on this particular afternoon. Hillary quietly entered the house through the front door by way of her personal key. The house was quiet so she naturally assumed that Mike was upstairs waiting for her. Not wanting to make a sound she quietly tiptoed out of the foyer and was immediately met with the sound of Mike mumbling to himself about something in a manner that suggested anger. Curious, she walked into the living room and what she found gave her a great surprise. She saw that the large aquarium that Mike bought while in Japan was shattered all over the floor and that a shallow lake had formed underneath his black leather couch. Even more surprising was the fact that Mike was cleaning up the mess himself. “Are you okay?” Hillary asked as she walked over to Mike, careful to step around the small lake. Mike looked up from his kneeling position with a scowl. “Of course I’m okay, but those damn kids aren’t going to be okay after I’m done cleaning their God damn mess,” he practically growled. “And that little bastard Ken is going to get the worse of it, I bet he’s the one that‘s responsible for this too!” Mike finished, his anger about the situation quite obvious. Hillary looked at Mike with a puzzled look. “What kids?” She asked. Mike sighed heavily and stood up in order to look his current lover in the eyes. “My kids,” Mike replied. “Carlotta showed up today with three little boys and they’re my sons.” Mike said in a particularly not-so-proud manner. “You never told me that you had kids.” Hillary said in a disappointed tone. The fact that Mike had children didn’t bother her in the least bit, but she would have liked to have known about it. “I never told you because I didn’t know that I had kids until today!” Mike said, trying his best not to yell. “Well,” Hillary began as she attempted to change the subject in order to avoid trying Mike’s already thin patience. “Where are they then?” Instead of replying to Hillary’s query Mike instead walked over to a nearby closet and opened the door. Behind the door were the three little boys, all bound and gagged to a chair. The boys began to squirm and let loose muffled pleas for help. Hillary rolled her eyes in disappointment and walked over to the closet and began to untie the triplets. “You should really treat your children better Mike.” She said in a stern voice. “Those little bastards got exactly what they deserved.” Mike said with a cold voice as he went back to cleaning up the mess. Once Hillary finished freeing the boys from their prison the little boy named Ben approached her with a curious look on his tear streaked face. “I’m hungry,” he declared as he tugged on her jersey. “Me too!” Quipped in his brother Glen. “Hey queer,” Ken said to Mike. “Are you going to feed us any time soon?” Mike didn’t say anything; he just glared at Ken. Mike had strong dislike for Ken, only because the little bastard reminded Mike of himself though. Hillary noticed Mike’s look and quickly herded the kids up before Mike did something stupid. “I’m sure there’s something in the kitchen guys.” She said as she quickly escorted the three boys out of the living room and into the kitchen. Mike sighed once more, a new habit he had picked up over the course of the day. He began to hope that he could pawn the babysitting job at Survival off on someone else. He just wanted to go out to the ring and work his frustrations from this day out on the wrestlers from CORE instead of stressing himself out over making sure Darek doesn’t hit Fnord. He then decided not to worry about Survival at this moment, besides the idea of Mike losing to four people so beneath him was rather ludicrous. Lost in thought he managed to pick up the small particles of glass in what felt like no time at all. He didn’t really know what to do about the small lake that had formed on his floor so he figured he’d go call his maid in to clean the mess instead. But before he’d do that he needed a nice cool beverage. He walked to the kitchen and once he entered the room what he saw nearly gave him a heart attack. The kitchen was completely covered in spaghetti sauce and small little raviolis. Right in the thick of it all were the triplets, they were throwing the raviolis at each other while they giggled with glee. Hillary stood in the corner pleading for the boys to stop. At this very moment Mike decided that he had finally had enough of this. He was sick of his home being destroyed by these supposed ‘little angels’. “THAT’S ENOUGH!” Mike yelled at the top of his lungs, catching the boy’s attention and even causing tears to stream down their dirty little faces. “Outside.” He ordered while pointing in the direction of the front door. Surprisingly the boys listen to their Father for the very first time. As the boys filed out Mike looked over to Hillary with a concerned look. “Are you okay?” He asked. “Yeah,” she replied with a bit of relief. “I just wish I had left them in that closet, they’re such little monsters!” Mike cracked a smile for the first time since the boys arrived. “I told you so,” he said with a bit of a chuckle. “Will you clean up the mess while I make sure they don’t cause anymore trouble while they wait for their Mother?” “Sure,” Hillary said. Mike made his way out of the kitchen and to the front door before she could say anything else. When Mike stepped outside he found the three boys sitting obediently on the front steps with their heads hung low. Also present was Carlotta Valdez, who tapped her foot impatiently as she waited for Mike. “Why are my children covered in sauce?” She asked Mike in her best motherly tone. “Because they’re little bastards that make a mess and break things no matter what they do.” Mike replied with a bitter voice as leaned against the doorway. “What are you talking about?” Carlotta asked. “They broke my new aquarium and instead of eating their food they made a big mess in my kitchen.” Mike answered while he glared at the three boys. “He tied us up Mommy.” Ken said, the young boy quickly ran to hide behind his Mother before Mike could smack him upside the head. An angry look came over Carlotta’s face as she learned this new piece of information. “This is why I’ve never let them meet you!” She said while waving her finger at Mike. “Come one kids, let’s get out of here.” She said as she motioned for her boys to follow her. Mike watched with a relieved smile on his face as they made their way down his driveway. After getting the children inside her van Carlotta looked over at Mike and yelled “You’re never going to see your sons again!” “Good!” Mike replied as he waved good-bye. As the van pulled out of his driveway Mike was glad he had been able to survive this disastrous situation for it made him much more confident about the outcome of the upcoming Ippv. -The End-
