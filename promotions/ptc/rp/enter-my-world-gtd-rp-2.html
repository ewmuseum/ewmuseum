Enter my world [GTD RP #2]

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Real Deal Public Access Pimp Joined: 10 Feb 2003 Posts: 30 Location: Over at SurReal's finishing off my Jack D! Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 11:21 pm Post subject: Enter my world [GTD RP #2] [When seen up close. The object looked much more delicate than it had before. It was a lot more detailed and also looked to made of the finest materials ever seen from gold to diamond along with misty grey glass. The object was in the shape of a perfect circle in the centre with multiple extra parts joined to the sides. The centre was made of glass and was hollow. It was filled with a small amount smoke. It flowed around the inside of the sphere of glass coming from a small hole at the bottom. It led to the bottom of the object, which was made of complete diamond. It was flat at the bottom but the edges around it were in an irregular jagged pattern. Above the sphere perched a phoenix made of shining gold and silver. Its wings swung out read to fly and its eyes fixed downwards towards the sphere. We could see two small swirling glowing objects floating around along with the smoke. They both started from the diamond enclosure and made its way up towards the phoenix. When it reached the top of the sphere the phoenix’s eyes began to glow. The left glowed red and the right glowed blue. The object was indeed The Real Deals orb. The view swung around and panned out away from the orb and we could see that it was in The Real Deal’s right hand. He looked at it with a pure gaze of amazement and understanding. He turned it around to face the phoenix. He then held it up with both hands just in front of his face. Two bolts of energy released into his hands and up into his arms. He then lowered the orb back down to where it was and put it in his pocket of his black trousers. The view then moved upwards to show his face. He was looking down at the ground before slowly looking upwards to look straight in front of him. His eyes had become, as the phoenixes were, his left eye red and his right one blue. A sly grin crept across his face. He looked to his left and then to his right observing the damage he had done on the guards who had stormed into the room. The view changed to show them. They were all bloodied from the glass that had shot into them from the orb. There were only scars left. None of the glass could be seen. The Real Deal walked over to one of the guards and gave the body a nudge with his foot] Real Deal: Dead…nice. [It was a tone of voice that no one had ever heard him talk in. It was rather joyful in a way along with triumphant. He was feeling good after taking out one of Axe’s teams. Kneeling down beside the body he ripped the guards mask off to reveal a boy who looked to be in his late teens] Real Deal: Obviously brainwashed little man. [The Real Deal then stood up and walked through the rest of the bodies lying on the floor, which was a mess of blood and other organs of the body. Nearing the exiting door to the lift he felt something holding onto his leg and pulling him back. As he turned around he saw one of the guards, who was somehow still alive. He was doing his best to hold onto The Real Deal until he looked up. The Real Deal’s head shot round and the two locked eyes. The guard could feel the fear flowing like a river through his body. The Real Deal’s eyes showed a sheer look of hate. In the redness of his left eye it could be seen that the fires of hell were burning bright, though his right eye was the complete opposite. It was ice cold, calm but also menacing. Real Deal moved towards him slowly and grabbed him by the neck with both hands. He then lifted him up and threw him against the wall] Real Deal: You should have stayed down. Your death would have been less painful. [The guard attempted to kick Real Deal in the mid-section but completely missed. Real Deal then gripped tighter round the neck of the guard before closing his right eye. The blue colour in his left eye intensified greatly and he looked straight at the guard who automatically went totally limp] Real Deal: Are you calm? Guard: Yea I am. Feel totally relaxed. Real Deal: Good. [The Real Deal then turned his head to the right and closed his left eye. He then opened his right eye and snapped his head to look at the guard. This time the red colour became deeper and greater. The guard’s reflection could be seen in it as he went from being pure calm to being afraid once more. The view then changed to a close up on The Real Deal’s eye. It was not the reflection of the guard that could be seen but a totally different place. The guard was still in view though. Standing in between two burning trees on a flat piece of destroyed land. Suddenly the fire engulfed him and a snap was heard. The view shot out to show both the guard and The Real Deal. The guard’s neck was twisted backwards. The skin looked as though it was going to tear. The Real Deal then took a knife from his pocket and started to etch into the guard’s skin. Once he finished he stood back and looked at his final product. It simply read “TRD”. He dropped the guard to the ground and started walking to the door once again] Guard Radio: Team one, what is your status. [Real Deal thinks to himself] **This could be an advantage** Guard Radio: Team one. Come in! [Real Deal turned around and began searching for one of the radios. After finding it he held it to his ear] **I wonder if it’s Axe** Guard Radio: This is Tyron. Team one…are you there? **Tyron? Hmm** [The Real Deal decided to talk back to Tyron. He put on a voice and talked back] Real Deal: Yes Tyron. We were under heavy attack from The Real Deal. Tyron: Have you disposed of him? Real Deal: Yes we have. The one time we hit him was a killer shot. Tyron: Good work. I will be down to observe the work. Real Deal: Not a problem. **What to do…** [The Real Deal went into his right pocket and pulled out the orb. He walked over to one of the guards and lifted him up. He then placed the orb between their hands] Real Deal: Change us! [Bolts of lightning came from all around the room. They all circulated around the point where The Real Deal and the guard were standing. The electricity careered along the walls and down around the floor. The Real Deal and the guard could not be seen at this point. All that could be seen was a large white space in the centre of the room. The light then started to die down and standing alive was the guard holding up The Real Deal] **That was one hell of a trip** [The Real Deal had used the orb to swap positions with the guard. Basically just like swapping shells. He had a feeling this would be his time to prevail. The door at the end of the room opened and in walked a tall man. His head was bald and he had a clean-shaven face. On the top half of his body he wore a black jacket with a white shirt. He wore plain black trousers on the lower half of his body. He was very confident looking until he saw all the dead bodies] Tyron: What the hell happened here? Real Deal/Guard: It was The Real Deal Sir. He took them all out. The only reason I survived was that I came in the back way. [Tyron walked up to The Real Deal’s body and checked it out] Tyron: You really have outdone yourself this time Will. I feel a promotion is at hand. Real Deal/Will: Thank you Sir. I won’t let you down. What do you want me to do with his body? Tyron: I’ll take it. Axe will be well pleased that he has been taken out. I will inform him of your efforts. I’m sure you will be well rewarded. [The Real Deal’s body was then handed over to Tyron who had no problem carrying it. His size was something that wasn’t seen often. He was ranging around the seven foot two mark. He put The Real Deal over his shoulder and turned to walk towards the door] Tyron: Get these bodies moved and you can have the week off. Real Deal/Will: Right on it! [Tyron continued towards the door. The Real Deal kept close to him disguised as Will the guard. As the door opened he brought out the orb quickly and a line of smoke could be seen moving into The Real Deal’s body. As the door closed, the guard’s eyes rolled into the back of his head and he fell backwards] [End Scene] [We return back with The Real Deal and Tyron. It is not evident at all, but The Real Deal had transported himself and the orb back to his own body. He was allowing Tyron to take him straight to Axe] **This is the best idea I’ve ever had** [The Real Deal felt very happy inside that he was able to use the orb in this way. He was finding it hard not to just snap Tyron’s back there and then. The two made their way down a small dark corridor only lit by small lights along the roof. Tyron seemed to be getting tired from the weight of Real Deal but he pressed on, making it to a door at the end of the corridor on the right. The door was carved out of fine mahogany with a gold plated axe attached to the door. The Real Deal couldn’t see it as he was looking at the ground. His body was starting to cramp up from staying still for a very long time. Tyron moved his hand towards the door and pull the handle of the axe out towards him. A large gong was heard being smashed from the other side. The door could be seen opening very slowly and a beam of light blasted out from the crack in the door into the eerie darkness of the corridor. Tyron then carried Real Deal into the room and the door closed much quicker than it had opened] [End Scene] [We now see a large office. The walls are covered with wood panelling. The floor is also made of wood. The whole room lights up due to the shine of the panels. To the left hand side of the room sat a large computer system with monitors attached to cameras around the building. Just to the side of this was a gun rack with a double barrel rifle sitting up. At the front centre of the room a large wooden desk could be seen with large leather chair behind it turned facing the wall. The lights lowered down a little and the glow of a fire could be seen around the leather chair. On the desk was a lot of paper and folders. They were all piled on top of each other. On the right hand side of the room was a metal table. With a closer look, bloodstains could be seen on the top and middle. Some blood had even encrusted itself while dripping down the sides] Tyron: Where do you want him boss? Axe: Put him on the table. I want him examined inside out. Tyron: No problem Sir. [Tyron carried The Real Deal over to the operating table and laid him on it face up. The leather chair swung round and Axe could be seen with an evil look on his face. He stood up slowly and began walking over to the table] Axe: Ahhh it’s good to finally see him dead. Tyron: He took out all of Team One apart from a young man Will. Axe: Will Frapt? Tyron: Yes Axe: He was reported to have died half an hour before you arrived here. Tyron: There’s no way. I was talking to him. **Hehe. It worked a treat. Now time for my party trick** Axe: You are obviously seeing things my friend. Get some rest. You have worked hard these past few days. Tyron: Thanks Boss. Enjoy your dissection. Axe: I will. **There isn’t going to be a dissection going on around here** [Axe and Tyron shook hands before they walked towards the door. The Real Deal took a quick look and waited for Tyron to leave the room. As soon as the door had been closed The Real Deal sat up. Axe did not see him until he was called] Real Deal: Hello Axe! Axe: What the… [Flash] [End] _________________ You will become one with the mat! - The Real Deal. #7 PTC - Week Beginning 20th January
