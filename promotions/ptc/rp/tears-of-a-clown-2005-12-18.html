Tears of a clown.

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 …all she'll be getting for Christmas is a black eye… One of the most difficult parts of our lives in this world is when the time comes for us to part physical company with those people who we hold dearest and have meant so much to us. At the time of their passing in to the other world many of us are left feeling as though we are unable to come to terms with the fact our loved ones have moved on. Not a lot of people know Red Rock's personal beliefs on this but he beliefs that they have not gone away from us, but they have simply progressed into another world, the world of the spirit. They have left their physical lives on earth but still remain with us, unseen to the physical eye. They continue to care for us and monitor our daily lives, but as they are not always with us in life, they won't always with us once they have passed on to this spirit world. But in times of stress and they will be there to protect and guide you, inspire you for a solution when a problem arises. The one thing they never do is abandon us. On the 18th of December 2005 we find ourselves within the four walls of Aldershot crematorium. The rows of seats are filled with loved one and their sounds of sorrow completely engulf the room, because of the high room the anguish reverberates around the room, rebounding off the concrete walls. Suddenly the sound of tears and despair is joined by the collective sound of everyone standing up as the song "plans" By Bloc Party begins to play. The undertakers enter the main entrances carrying a glorious looking oak coffin. People with their teary blood shot eyes watch the coffin by as they blow their noses. At this point around the back we see Red Rock and Boolie who are rarely smartly dressed. Red Rock simply stares at the coffin with a blank expression whilst Boolie is in a fit of uncontrollable tears. The Undertakers place the coffin down and they stand else where before the priest takes his place. Priest: Hello and welcome. We are here to remember the life of Heather Graham. As the priest continues we watch as Red Rock just sits down thinking to himself about Heather. She isn't a face who we have seen around but Red Rock was close to her in the sense of his secret spiritual beliefs. You see she her self believed the same as Red Rock and one day when they were both locked out of the block of flats they shared and got talking. Red Rock was delighted to have someone to share his thoughts with. He couldn't talk to many people about them because Red Rock is a fairly insecure person, he couldn't even talk to his good friend Boolie about them with the fear of being laughed at. But those weren't the only thoughts swimming around Red Rock's head. He was being tortured with feelings of guilt and the thoughts of "I could of done something, I could of helped." Priest: Her life was so unexpectedly snatched away from us and she will be missed by all. A kind soul, who loved her neighbors and always took their best interest before her own. You see Heather Graham was a young woman she had reached the age of 24 when she had died, and married at the age of 18 to a man named Edmond. Edmond depended on her so much; Heather would always look after him, feed him, and do his washing. How ever Edmond was by no means disabled or incapable, he was a savage of a man and Heather was succumbing to an abusive marriage. She always found her self with men who she ended up looking after. She was so insecure in her own ways that she never believed that she could do any better than this life, and this is why her problems went generally unnoticed. Priest: And now reading the Poem "death is nothing at all", Heather's sister Natalie. Natalie a nice 17 year old girl stands up at the front, fighting a losing battle against her tears. Her eyes are red and puffy and she wipes her nose. Red Rock stares at her as his guilt and regret worsens, like a boiling kettle. He can't help but think it is his fault that Natalie is now without her big sister, and her parents without their beautiful daughter. Natalie: This was a poem Heather read to me when our Grand Father died earlier this year. Natalie takes a breath and reads off a piece of paper. Death is nothing at all Death is nothing at all I have only slipped away in to the next room; I am I and you are you - What ever we were to each other, That we still are Call me by my old familiar name; Speak to me in the easy way Which you always used, Put no difference in your tone, Wear no forced air of solemnity or sorrow, Laugh as we always laughed At the little jokes we enjoyed together. Let my name be ever the household word. That it always was. Let it be spoken with effect Without the trace of a shadow on it It is the same as it ever was. There is unbroken continuity. Why should I be out of mind? Because I am out of sight I am waiting for you, for an interval. Somewhere very near, just around the corner, All is well! - Henry Scott Holland Natalie who by now has fully burst in to tears stands down back to her seat as everyone applauds loudly. Right now tears begin to roll down Red Rock's face, Boolie puts an arm around him in comfort. Red Rock: I could of said something Boolie! Boolie: You didn't know what was really going on Red, no one knew she had it that bad. Red Rock: I knew he was violent towards her though. Boolie: we all knew Red, but we didn't know that he'd do this. Red Rock: But I could of stopped him! Red Rock buries his face in his hands as he sobs painfully… Earlier this month… We find ourselves at the ground floor of Red Rock's block of flats as Red Rock along with some other tenants. They all stand waiting for the lift impatiently. It is a cold Monday afternoon and people are not in frame of mind to be kept waiting. Red Rock has a typically disgruntled expression on his face, which only worsens as more people cue for the lift. Other people waiting are a woman named Clare; she was a boisterous out going person who spoke her mind. Another was Heather, this would be one of the last times anyone would see her. Heather was looking quite timid as she was quite a timid natured person probably due to her years of repression from her husband. Red Rock: Bloody bastard lift… The doors of the lift struggle open and 7 people pile in to cramped pissed soaked area. Red Rock reluctantly squeezes in to the far left corner with Claire and Heather. The doors managed to force shut and the lift rumbles up the floors. Everyone stands in awkward silence whilst trying to ignore the repugnant smell of urine. Red Rock's face exclaims a feeling of discomfort because he is squashed up against so many people. It is not one of Red Rock's preferred situations as he doesn't like being in confined spaces. Red Rock pokes his head forward and notices Heather, Heather smiles at Red Rock who managed to raise his hand to wave at her. Red Rock: Hello Heather! Heather: Hello Red, how’s the wrestling business? Red Rock: It's alright…still not really paying much more. But I'm in the second round of GTT5! A small man with a thick bushy beard interrupts Red Rock for a second. He is dressed in a thick coat and a brown flat cap. This man's name is Ron. Ron: Whoa wait a minute? GTT5 is a wrestling tournament? I thought it was a golf tournament…no wonder I got a letter telling me my "gimmick sucked" and I'd only get over in UWF. Red Rock and Heather giggle at the old man's misconception. Suddenly Claire enters the convocation. She is one who loves to party, she goes out most nights and binges on alcohol. Most nights she can be heard wondering the hall ways at night trying to find her flat. Claire: 'ere Heather what you doing tonight? Heather: Erm…nothing? Claire: Want to come down the pub with me? Heather: Err…no thanks Claire…Edmond won't want me to. Claire puts a look of disgust on her face; she is an independent woman so for her to hear a fellow woman choosing the needs of a man over a time out with the ladies utterly sickened her. Claire: Are you really going to sit at home all your life wiping that bastards arse? Live a little bit Heather, you're 24 and you never had a good night out in your life! Heather: Yes I have… Heather who by now is slightly intimidated by Claire's outlandish behavior looks to the floor. Heather: Ok…I'll come out with you, but I will have to ask Edmond. I don't think he will be happy though. The lift stops at floor 3, this is Red Rock's floor so he negotiates his way through the crowded lift and out the door as he waves good bye to Heather. The lift doors close and continue to the 4th floor where Heather will alight. Heather waves good bye to Claire as grins back at her, Heather applies a false smile. As the doors slide closed the front she had put on drops. She sighs to her self as she takes out her keys and walks towards her door. She begins to look a little bit frightened almost as if she was entering a cage of lions. She inserts her key and turns the lock as she pushes the door open. The atmosphere now is dark and depressing; a feeling of fear and anger lingers in the air. The curtains are drawn shut at this tiny 1 bedroom flat. The light creeps through a gap in the curtains leaving a bright white stripe going down the middle of the room. Heather closes the door behind her and hangs her coat up on a hanger behind the door. Heather: Edmond. I'm home. Edmond is a middle aged man, much older than Heather, there is an age gap of 13 years between them. He is he is a stocky man and his hair is receding and thinning at the back. The lines of age are showing clearly on his face. He is an aggressive looking man and a very bitter soul. All over the place is aviation memorabilia, things like model aero planes hanging from the ceiling, small ornamental propellers hanging on the walls and a book shelf completely full of books about flight. Edmond served as a pilot in the Royal Air Force but he became injured and was reluctantly discharged from the Air Force. He was based in Aldershot at the time, as Aldershot is a town with military heritage. So rather than move anywhere else he bought this flat and has resided here for the last 6 years. He met Heather through a former friend, she was his daughter but obviously when the relationship Heather and Edmond came to light, the friendship ended. Edmond is sat at his work desk putting together his newest model plan. He swings his head round and glares down his long pointy nose with his beady piercing eyes. This man is not a nice man. Edmond: When you goin’ to start me fookin' dinnar? Edmond is also Scottish… Heather: I was thinking I would…never mind. What would you like? Edmond stands up so fast the chair he was sitting on falls over. He storms over to Heather and the fear of god runs right through her. Edmond: What were you thinkin' Heather: It doesn't matter Edmond, please! Heather recoils from Edmond and makes her way to the kitchen but Edmond grabs her arm tightly and drags her back in his personal space. Edmond: No Heather. What were you going to say? Heather: It was nothing, Claire just asked me if I wanted to come out with her for a drink. Edmond smirks and laughs lightly to himself as he shakes his head. Edmond: That stoopid bitch? She don' like you she thinks youz a pathetic idiot just like everyone else. Heather: That's not true Edmond, the guy down stairs likes me we talk all the time. Edmond: The bed wetting wrestler? You been talking to him? I should fookin' kill him! He knows youz my wife! Heather: No Edmond! Please he knows, he's not like that…I think he might be gay actually? Edmond: Youz not going out with that bitch shes no any good for you. Heather: I want to Edmond I can't be expected to stay in here all the time, please Edmond! Edmond: and have you go out and have other men look at you and lust after you, NO…WAY! Heather: I'm going Edmond…I don't care. Edmond is beginning to build up his rage. Heather tries to maintain a brave face as she rarely stands up to Edmond. He does have a short temper and she has tried to stand up to him before only to get beaten down for it. There is a tense pause between the two for a minute when Edmond utters a response. Edmond: Did you just disrespect me Heather? Heather: No I didn't I'm just saying - Edmond thrusts his hands around heathers throat her eyes spring open and she tries to pull Edmond's hands off her throat. At the same time down in Red Rock's flat Boolie: I think there’s something going on up there? Red Rock: Yeah you're right, sounds like they're having a fight! I bet that fucking Scottish rasclart is at it again. Someone ought to sort him out. Boolie: Maybe we should go up there and make sure everything is ok? Red Rock: Nah Better stay out of it. Red Rock and Boolie resume watching TV but can't help but notice the slight sound of thudding and things smashing, but because they don't hear screaming they are under the impression that things are just being thrown, but little do they know that Edmond has his large haggard hands wraps tightly round Heathers neck, squeezing the life out of her. It is in these moments of Red Rock's decision to stay down stairs Heathers lives out her final moments in her physical life. THUD! A sickening thud is suddenly heard in the flat above, it is so loud the lamp shade on Red Rock's ceiling swings. Suddenly all the crashing about stops dead, Red Rock and Boolie jump out of their seats and instantly realize what has happened. Red Rock and Boolie look at each other each with the same ill feeling in their stomach. Red Rock: Oh God… Boolie: He's …no Red Rock and Boolie run for their door and burst out in to the corridor, as the open the door it would seem the loud bang was heard through out the whole of his floor as fellow tenants Mr. Mclumpher the old man who lives opposite to Red Rock and D'avid the ginger Dutch pervert who lives down the hall are standing outside. D'avid: Heh, what the shit was that bang? Red Rock: I think it was Heather, the lady on floor 4 getting beaten up by her husband. Red Rock and Boolie burst through a set of double doors and rush up the stairs to the forth floor. Red Rock bangs on the door franticly. Red Rock: HEATHER…HEATHER ANSWER THE DOOR! There is no answer. Red Rock: For God sake answer the door! Edmond escaped through the window that night and the police are still hunting for him. And we return to the present time. Red Rock is now in the toilets of the crematorium to pull him self together. The service finished and others had already congregated outside before heading off to the wake, which Red Rock would not be attending due to wrestling commitments. Red Rock stares in the mirror and looks at his teary torn reflection. Red Rock: She was such a nice person, and I could of stopped it… Red Rock wipes away some of the tears with a hand towel when suddenly he feels a warm glowing feeling and is suddenly surrounded by love. All of a sudden his feeling of sorrow had disappeared he looks again in the mirror and behind him is Heather in spirit. This is very strange for Red Rock as he has believed in this for a long time but he has never experienced anything like this. He is shocked but not scared. She smiles at Red Rock but doesn't say a word and in an instant she disappears but he is left with the feeling of forgiveness. Sometimes people pass on to the spirit world and people feel responsible or sometimes they crossed swords but never got the chance to say sorry. They worry that the person who had passed on to the spirit realm doesn't know how much they regret what they did, or in Red Rock's case didn't do. But Heather was not one to hold a grudge she shared a lot of good hours with Red Rock talking about their shared interests and she wanted Red Rock to know she held no grudge.
