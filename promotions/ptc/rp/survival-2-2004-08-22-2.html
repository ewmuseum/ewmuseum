Survival #2

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-22 The scene opens directly inside of a UPWA Locker room. The room is sparsely decorated, just a table with an ash tray a top it and two chairs. On one of the chairs we see a bare foot man dressed in a poncho and a faded pair of blue jeans and on the other chair we see a man dressed in a Texas style suit. It is Rocky and Limp. Limp has a cigarette in hand and Rocky has a cigar. Limp looks up at the camera, smiles and begins to speak. Limp- Well howdy folks. You know I wasn't going to speak to you again but you know after my opponents had to run there mouths I thought it only fitting that I come out and responded to the miss guided fools. Well sirs that is just asking for a fight. Isn't that right Rocky? Rocky looks a little worried. Rocky- errrm yeh course I didn't, next you'll be saying I set a dog high on speed onto an opponent of a client. Rocky laughs in a worried sort of way. Limp looks concerned. Limp- Rocky, you did! How could you, and what the hell is this about a dog and speed? Rocky shoots back quick. Rocky- Oh come on it happened five years ago the fire incident and they say the guy will be out of hospital any day now. It's because I care and love about my clients so much, you know just like how you care and love everyone. And the Speed thing well how was I meant to know that speed would do that to a dog and it was just a coincidence I happened to feed it to him inside the poor blokes locker room and then accidentally locked the door once the bloke came back and then forgot to open it for an hour. It's all accidents, yes that's right. Limp looks confused but then smiles and speaks. Limp- Oh right yep that clears that all up. Anyway I'm sure that if anything should happen to my opponents at Survival it would just be an accident. Before all I felt was hate now I feel love, with this new outlook on life I have achieved so much more. Why should I have to beat you to prove that, from what I remember Chezina was the world champ before he faced me and then he snapped and got himself suspended and stripped of the title but when he came back I beat him at Destructicity. This is simple like a friendly football match, nothings on the line, there's nothing much to prove, I win all the better, you win who cares. You see that's your problem guys you like many are still under the spell that hate will win out over love and peace. You know in fact the only one who has something to lose in this matches are you. Limp pauses to extinguish his cigarette and promptly lights another and speaks. Limp- You know there's something that has been on my mind. David J that's what. Now that he is the new UPWA World Champion he is my main target. Personally I don't have a problem with the little man, he knows his place under my thumb as far as I can see, which is great for us all, especially little David. Now why David do you think I should be scared or intimidated by you? I have the world at my feet, you're the only one with something to prove, something I can't see you can do. I'm a giant compared to you David, I only have to move when provoked. And now you have provoked me I will step on you like the maggot you are. Yeah you want the old Limp back do you, you keep up the cheek sonny and maybe you'll see a one time return in this match where I truly shut that flapper for you. But hell that's not the Limp we have all come to love now is it? I'm sure after the match we can engage in a manly handshake, that's if you can stand to engage the handshake when I take your UPWA Title but first I will team with Boris Yeichstag and a couple of guys I don't like The Rodriguez Brothers, together we will get UPWA to win Survival. Dig peace my friends, dig peace. Limp finishes up there, puts out the cig in his hand and yet again lights up another. Rocky is still just sitting there puffing away at his large cigar with his eyes darting all over the room in a sinister sort of way. The scene fades into a Survival promo.
