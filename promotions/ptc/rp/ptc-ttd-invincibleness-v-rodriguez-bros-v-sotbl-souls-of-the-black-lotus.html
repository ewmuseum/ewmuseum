8 May 2004 A Brother’s Love….. Part 1 GW’s Souls of the Black Lotus – Tag Tournament Finals RP 1 How important is a brother’s love? How strong can the bond between blood be? How far is a brother willing to go to protect his family? Is there anything truly stronger than the strength of a brother on the lookout for another of his bloodline?? They say that when their child is in need, a mother can find the strength inside herself of ten women in order to save her child. The natural link between mother and child is unbreakable, however so too is the undying love of an elder brother to his little brother. The sense of needing to be together; a constant reminder of their blood link. The feelings of watching each other’s back; the constant reassurance that they will always be there for one another. The strength to succeed not for the betterment of self, but for the betterment of the two; a constant sign of the strength truly possessed by brothers. “Do you really think that we stand a chance at winning this match?” Jushin asked his older brother. “Of course we do, have you not seen what we are capable of?” started Ichiro’s reply. “We have overcome great obstacles in our lives, Jushin. This is merely the next step, the next step of many.” “Yes, we have done much to get here, but this is a serious match, this is the finals. We are facing two teams now something completely different than what we are used to,” said Jushin. “Have you learned nothing from this tournament yet Jushin?” Ichiro chuckled slightly as he spoke. “Why I thought you might have learned a few lessons along the way, throughout our training and matches. The virtues by which we have lived our lives have helped us to get this far, and I have no doubt that they will fail us now, brother.” “Yes, but they are merely lessons, lessons about life in general. They are not specific to wrestling, no matter what we learned they are not going to offer us any help inside that ring,” exclaimed Jushin. “I am afraid you could not be more wrong my brother. You see although what we have learned from this tournament truly is life lessons, there is nothing to say that those same lessons have not helped us inside the ring so far in this tournament. Lest we forget young brother, that we have already been victorious twice in this tournament,” came his older brother’s response. “Have you ever wondered what it would be like if you were born alone?” asked Jushin. “What are you talking about brother?” inquired Ichiro. “You know, if we weren’t born and raised together in the temple. What if only you went to that temple, and I was raised by father in his business, what if we never knew each other?” Jushin repeated himself. “Why are you talking like this brother? Who cares, the fact is we do know each other, and we have known each other all of our lives. And just like when we were young, we care about each other and we are here for one another, there is nothing that can break us apart. United we stand, united we fall, brother, that is the way it will always be with us!” answered Ichiro. Jushin looked at his brother, it was not often that they had these sort of heart to heart conversations, but when they did, they were definitely inspiring. Often his older brother’s words were soothing enough to take away a world of pressure and doubt. Jushin didn’t know what it was about Ichiro, but the sound of his voice was instantly soothing, the strong reassurance from even a soft whisper or a loud yell, the assurance that only an older brother’s protection can give. Jushin smiled at his brother, he extended his hand with a closed fist. “Mastudai Touitsuteki!” said Jushin firmly. Roughly translated the words meant united forever, and to the two Nakazura brothers it seemed to be something a little bit more. They were more than just words. It was seemingly the words that represented the two men’s lives together. They had faced many a crisis together and still they had overcome them. They had been in many fights together and still their love and resolve had pulled them through. It seemed that nothing could break them apart, training at an isolated temple in the mountains of Japan, fighting as street warriors in the Tokyo underworld, even the world of professional wrestling had taken a chance to break the brothers apart, but nothing had yet to work, as the Souls of the Black Lotus were still together and still strong. Ichiro looked back towards his brother, there was a light in his little brother’s eyes which he had not seen in a very long time. The light of innocence, the light of a youthful and foolish hope, obviously installed by his own words. Ichiro knew that Jushin looked up to him, but he did not consider the overwhelming effect his words had had on him tonight. When he looked into his brother’s eyes that night, he was not looking into the eyes of his little brother. He was looking into the eyes of a man possessed. A man ready to go the distance not for himself, but for his family and for his team. Together, they were a team, and together they could not be beat. No matter what the outcome of this match would be, they would always have each other, and somehow Ichiro managed to find common peace in his own words. He felt the tingle in his own eyes, almost as if the same light had now been ignited in his eyes. There was something that had happened tonight, that had not happened in a long time, something that he could not explain. Together the two brothers had done so much, and tonight just added to the list of the accomplishments. The Souls of the Black Lotus were now men on a mission. They needed to win the PTC Tag Team Titles, it was their destiny. It was merely the next step in the road, the right direction of the fork in their path and tonight they had shown each other that. As Ichiro looked into his brother’s outstretched arm, with his clenched fist. He looked calmly into his brothers eyes, and simultaneously the two of them shut their eyes. Their vision did not fade however, instead of blackness, they saw visions of the past. Ichiro saw the great triumphs they had as a team, and Jushin saw the many images of their past together. The brothers were ready, no matter what PTC had to offer, they were together, United Forever! Ichiro placed his open palm on top of his brother’s closed fist. The manner in which they always did, it was a form of respect to each other. Ichiro opened his eyes and looked into his little brother’s. “Mastudai Touitsuteki!” repeated Ichiro, and he left his hand on top of Jushin’s as Jushin smiled and nodded. Will we ever truly know what strength lies in the union of a brotherhood? Will we ever truly know how much power and love exists in a relationship between a man and his blood brother? How far is too far, for one brother to go for another? What borders is an older brother willing to cross in order to protect his little brother? To what lengths are they willing to travel as a team in order to achieve their goals, together? There was no way to find out for sure, but this was sure to be one hell of a week, for the Nakazura brothers. It truly would be an interesting finale to the tournament for the Souls of the Black Lotus. ---- 10 May 2004 A Brother’s Love…… Part 2 (Jushin’s Story) GW’s Souls of the Black Lotus – Tag Tournament Finals RP 2 Often have I wondered, what am I doing here? What is my purpose? What am I supposed to do before it is my time?? There are people dying everyday and to counter that, an increasingly high number of people being born every day. So why is it that I have only experienced the latter of the two and I have yet to experience death? Is there some greater purpose for my being? Is there a necessity for me to be here, is there a greater being at work that keeps pushing me on? Is it possible that I am merely a pawn in some bigger scheme? There is no way for me to find out. All that is important to me now are those things which come naturally to me, and those things which coincidentally walk hand in hand with one another. Wrestling and family, the only two things that keep me going. Together my brother and I have pushed our way through the wrestling industry, pushing forward with an iron fist and taking out all those who oppose us. And where has that gotten me as a person? It brings me to where I am now. I am stronger, smarter, faster and better than I have ever been in my life. My competitive level is at the highest of my life, I am eager, I am hungry. I am ready to take on any task, as each task is merely the next rung of the ladder. And now the next rung of that ladder of life, is the PTC Tag Team Titles and in my way stand two teams. But, as they prepare they have no idea that it is all in vain. I am ready and I am unbeatable. Jushin looked hard at the punching bag in front of him. The sweat was pouring out of him in buckets, but that was not enough to stop him. He was training hard and he had been for the past several hours. He stood there bouncing on his feet slightly, he had done all of his weight lifting already, he was moving into his cardiovascular exercises. Standing there bouncing slightly on his feet his eyes shifted to follow the swaying punching bag. He was dressed in a pair of black sweat pants, he did not have a shirt on. His stomach was covered in several scars from his past. Both his hands and feet were bare, except for the small amounts of tape and wrapping that covered them. His black hair hung in front of his face, almost down to his eyes, and at every single strand a bead of sweat was dripping off. He let loose a flurry of kicks that sent the bag swaying back and forth harder than ever, he took a step back and walked to his water bottle. What do I do it for? What is it that keeps me going? What is it that makes the world spin for me? That’s simple. It’s not the pleasure of doing hard work, no not at all. Nor is it the amount of success or the fame or the fortune or the glory. No, I do what I do, because I like it. There is nothing more fulfilling to me then beating my opponent. Nothing better then going one on one, man to man in a competition and clean out beating your opponent. Nothing is more fulfilling to me then seeing the pain and anguish on the face of my enemy right before he is defeated. It is a great satisfaction to me, to hear the screams of pain and the grunts of frustration from my opponent when we engage in combat. He walked back towards the punching bag that had finally stopped swaying. He wiped the beads of sweat from his forehead and began to refocus his mind on the task at hand. He needed to go back to training, he needed to be in the top physical shape of his life for this match. He reared back on his left leg and balanced himself. He extended his right leg fully to a point beside the bag, he focused his stance towards the hanging punching bag. With an explosion he jumped off of his one foot and delivered five kicks to the bag before landing firmly on his right leg. There are many things which I have gained throughout my life. Many lessons learned, people have come and gone, friends, family, live, dead all of it is a vicious cycle. My life has been a non-stop roller coaster, seemingly never slowing and never resting. Almost as if it is a car; a car in which if the speed drops, the car dies. Throughout my life I have overcome obstacles which many normal men would not have to face in their lifetimes. I have seen the untimely murder of my own father, I have grown up through hate and bullying only to flourish, I have had the warmth of another man’s blood on my own hands, I have felt the piercing pain of torture and throughout it all there is only one thing which has remained constant. My tag team partner, my business partner, my brother. Jushin’s body remained entirely still. He stood in a crouched stance, his right leg extended completely with both of his hands on his knees. He sprung forward standing on his hands before using his arm strength to launch himself upwards and deliver a huge kick to the punching bag which sent it swinging back and forth quickly. He landed back on his feet, and quickly delivered two jumping Thai kicks in rapid succession. The death of my father was a huge blow to me personally, but still I have overcome it. And as with my brother’s guidance I usually do, I have turned the negative into a positive. My father was killed, which is not something unusual for a Yakuza underworld boss. However, that did not make it any easier for me to cope with. However, vengeance was had. My father’s death avenged, my family’s name cleansed. Those who dared to disrespect the name of Nakazura were swiftly dealt with. What else could I do? There was something else that I could do, and this is something which I have only recently realized and something that I am currently completing. That is to teach the world what the Nakazuras are truly about, to prove to the world that the Souls of the Black Lotus are more then mere criminals, that we truly are a force to be reckoned with. To do this, I plan on winning the PTC Tag Team titles, along with the help of my brother and my partner. This is what we need to do, and that is what we will do. With the Lotus as my witness, I swear by my own life, that if it is truly needed then so it will be done! Jushin took a few steps away from the punching bag. His breathing was heavy and near wheezing. His body was exhausted and it was beginning to show. He saw it in his kicks, although his weakened kicks were still much stronger then that of an average man, his blows were beginning to weaken and beginning to come fewer and farther between. Jushin took his eye away from the bag for the first time in what seemed like an eternity. It took his eyes a moment to focus out of the light and towards the small clock on the wall, he read the time. 2:37 p.m, he had been training for nearly three hours. He turned back to the bag, he would be done soon, eight more minutes of hard training and it would be time for a well-deserved shower. Jushin refocused on the bag and began to attack it with a flurry of kicks and punches. The bag swung heavily back and forth, extremely quickly, putting great tension on the chain that kept it attached to the ceiling. This did not slow Jushin down however, as every time the bag made a movement, his body made an opposite movement to dodge the bag and deliver another flurry of strikes. As he kicked almost effortlessly it seemed, his mind began to race again. If winning this match is what it takes it shall be done. If beating the two teams in my way, is my final obstacle they will be surpassed and defeated. The bond between Ichiro and me, is at a new level. A level in which we cannot be defeated. The Rodriguez Brothers, a strong team and brothers like ourselves however, they are not ready to withstand the fury of the Lotus which we shall set upon them. Invincibleness, another great tag team. Made up of two great singles competitors, and the only team who has yet to be defeated in this tournament. Yet, still lacking the one thing to make them great, that strong connection, which will be their downfall. Ichiro and I, we cannot lose. We have gone too far, we have made too many sacrifices. Our teamwork, our loyalty, our continuity, our trust, our unity all stronger than ever. All of them, all of our virtues as a team, ready to be put to the ultimate test, the test to prove that we truly are the strongest tag team to ever compete in Primetime Central. The test that will prove truly what the Nakazura name means. A test that will finally prove, that The Souls of the Black Lotus are the single greatest unit to ever compete anywhere. For me… For my brother… We will not lose! Jushin takes a step back from the bag. He jumps high into the air and begins to spin, his upper body twisting first, followed by his legs which are moving at a high speed. There is a loud snap, almost as if thunder is erupting from the roof of the training facility as his foot collides with the bag. There is a slight rip, which quickly spreads. Jushin turns to walk away, satisfied with his workout. He bends over to pick up his towel and water bottle, before making his way towards the shower room. As he walks away the punching bag can be seen in the background, and a soft hissing sound can be heard. There is a large tear in the punching bag where Jushin had just made contact. The rip has opened up and sand poured all over the floor as the sand from the bag empties into a pile on the floor below. ---- 11 May 2004 A Brother’s Love…… Part 3 (Ichiro’s Story) GW’s Souls of the Black Lotus – Tag Tournament Finals RP 3 "United we stand, divided we fall." -- Aesop (620 -560 B.C.) Truer words have never been spoken, for as long as a brotherhood can last, then strong shall the brotherhood be. As the old saying goes, a team is only as strong as its weakest link, but in such cases of a brotherhood, two brothers equal in strength, intellect and desire will truly be a force to be reckoned with. Is there truly anyone who could fend off the strength of an army of a thousand men? Maybe so, but could that man do so, if each and every member of the army was a brother, fighting for one another with equal goals and strength? I do not think so, with the same goal in mind, the team of brothers is unbeatable. Together, strong, unified, trusting, loyal to one another, nothing can defeat such a team. Could any force other then the Hand of God defeat the strength of brotherhood? No, for it is not meant to be so, together brothers are the strongest of all foes, friends or acquaintances. When standing together against a common enemy, the strength of the brotherhood shall endure all hardships and come away unscathed. It has been many days… Many days since I have last felt the way I feel today. It has been many days since I have felt such a great thirst. Such a great thirst needing to be quenched. Such an inescapable hunger. Long has the time passed since I have wanted something so badly. Many moons have fallen and risen since the last time I felt such a burning desire, such a great passion for anything. Winning has always been my purpose, I have never gotten into a single thing in my life to lose. Ever since I set out on my journey in the world of professional wrestling, winning is what has kept me going. Long has my hunt been to make a name for myself, for my family. When I stepped foot into this business I had few reasons, those reasons have since dwindled down to only two. I fight only for my and for my brother. When I stepped into the wrestling business, my responsibilities were many. I had the responsibility of showcasing the Shao Lin Arts to the rest of the Western World. I had the responsibility of proving that the Nakazuras were more than just crooks and killers. I had the responsibility of doing my Shao Lin Master proud, the duty of making my father proud. But with one foul blow all those responsibilities were wiped away. When my father was murdered, the game changed. The reasons and the responsibilities changed….. The world changed… Everything changed. And I changed right along with it! Ichiro was seated atop the roof of his Dallas, Texas apartment building. He looked down at his watch slowly and silently. He listened as the soft whisper of the midnight wind spoke to him. He watched as the stars in the sky shimmered and wrote messages to him. Up here, atop this roof in the midnight sky, he was alone, but he felt that this is where he could truly find himself. This is where when things fell apart, where he could come to put the pieces back together. He reached behind him and opened the gym bag that was there. He pulled out his black mat and spread it across the top of the ledge of the roof. He climbed on top of the mat and seated himself cross-legged. He placed his hands in his lap, and began to relax. He closed his eyes and waited for the soothing, relaxing feeling of meditation to come over him. The world has changed and so have I. There is nothing that matters to me anymore, only two things matter now. My brother and the bond we share, and proving to the world that they can’t take the Souls of the Black Lotus lightly. My brother is now the most important thing in my life. Family has always been important, and he is the last bit that I have left. Through everything that has happened over the past few years I feel that the bond between my brother and I has flourished into what it is now. Now it is a bond that cannot be broken. A bond that can withstand the annals of time. A bond that when faced with the greatest of adversaries will stand strong. A bond that could plow its way through the fiery depths of hell and still make it through. Ichiro felt his body begin to relax as the soothing process of meditation began to come over him. His muscles went from tight and flexed to soft and relaxed. His mind began to sink. His eyes became heavy. His entire body felt like it was falling, down the wormhole it fell. Soon enough he would be engaged in his full meditative state. This match will be the single greatest achievement of our tag team career. The PTC Tag Titles shall be the crowning achievement in an already polished career. But, that is not the only reason I have for going into this match. My brother needs this just as much as I do. We need this. Together, the two of us, we need to win it together. To prove that the strength of the Brothers Nakazura cannot be brought down by any force. By the hand of men, or by the hand of God, nothing shall break the bond between my brother and I. He felt his body beginning to slip away. He felt his mind and soul begin to rise from his body. He was hovering over top of his body now. He glared down. It was a very strange feeling that he was experiencing now. One that he had never really gotten used to. Even after seeing it hundreds of time, you never quite get used to looking down onto your own motionless body. However, the glance only lasted a few seconds before the visions of his meditation began to sink in. The grass around him was soft and lush. He ran his bare feet across the soft, dew covered, morning grass. He was dressed only in a pair of white Gi styled pants. His black belt sat around his waste. His chest and stomach were bare, exposing the full amount of scars and tattoos that covered his upper torso and arms. He walked through the morning grass, smelling the soft senses that the Japanese mountain had to offer. He continued his walk, he began walking towards the large temple slightly out of view. He knew it was there, for he had been there many times. It was the place where he had grown up. He began to walk across the flatlands that he knew were there, however this time, something was in his way. Something that had never been there before was blocking his path. A forest. There had never been a forest there before, he thought to himself. Why the hell would it be here now? Of course, as with everything in his meditation visions, the forest must have some form of metaphorical effect. He glared in and slowly inched his way into the forest, through the thick brush and foliage. His feet were now the recipients of sharp pains. His bare feet felt sore as they made their way across the needles and sticks of the dirt floor of the forest. He pushed on nonetheless…. This part of the dream was not hard to interpret. As with most visions, there was a form of code. A method to the madness, a way to break down the code and understand the true meaning of your visions. Such things as a forest often represented a challenge, usually one that must be faced within a strict time frame. I knew what it meant, it was the tag match, no doubt about it. He pressed onwards, the forest seemingly never ending. He walked for what seemed like at least an hour, before he finally came upon the next part of his vision. Off to the side of his path, there were two wolves. The wolves’ fur shimmering in the soft sunlight. He smiled softly at the sight and knowing its importance stopped for a second to observe. The wolves began to encircle one another. They began to eye each other down, Ichiro knew where this was going but he stayed and watched. It was not long before the wolves turned on one another, they began to scratch, claw and bite at one another’s coats. The soft shimmer of their silver coats began to be replaced by the crimson trickles of blood. The ivory of their fangs, began to see red spots of blood, dripping off of them. They turned away from each other, finally going their separate ways. Ichiro watched closely as they left, they were very similar. However, they were not exact replicas, he tried putting the pieces together as the wolves walked out of the brush each on their separate ways. Another simple message, the wolves were clearly a representation of my opponents. Often enemies, were shown as fierce wild, savage animals. However, there was much more to this vision, then merely his enemies. I had to put together which opponents they were. I remembered that the wolves had many similar characteristics but also many different ones. The Rodriguez Brothers are related, meaning they would have a lot in common, but slight differences. This was clearly a representation of them. There was one way to corrupt that team and defeat them. Jushin and I had defeated them before, we would do it again. We need to turn them against one another I thought, however, the dream like state began once again… He continued to push on through the forest. His feet now bleeding from the walking, after a few hours more of torturous walking he saw the clearing up ahead. He walked towards it, and was just about to exit the forest when the final piece of the puzzle came together. The two figures dropped directly in front of him. A monkey and a bear. He was taken back slightly, not expecting to see such a strange combination of animals, however, he watched closely. Lurking somewhere in the distance were two large cats. They were silently waiting in the grass. Ichiro watched the action taking place between the bear and the monkey. He was shocked however, when the monkey merely climbed on top of the bear’s back and began to hitch a free ride. The bear did not seem to mind however, it allowed the monkey onto its back and began to make its way towards the exit. It was only a few hundred yards to the clearing in the woods, they would soon be there. However, the cats struck at the last possible second. They attacked the bear first, quickly knocking the monkey off and leaving it helpless on the ground. The two cats quickly changed their focus away from the bear, ready to fight and defend itself. They quickly grabbed the monkey, mauled it, and began to run away as the bear began to give chase. As soon as the animals were out of sight. Ichiro walked into the clearing. It was in this clearing that he would see the final piece of his vision. I know this. This is simple. The bear and the monkey are my other two opponents, Scott Matthews and Invincible Gabe. Everyone knows that Gabe is helpless inside the ring, the monkey must be him. Matthews is a fierce competitor he will be tough to defeat, he must beat the bear. But there in lies the key to our strategy. Divide and conquer, get Gabe and isolate him and we shall not lose. Ichiro stepped out of the forest. The sunlight blinded him momentarily, when he stepped out the forest. The entire world outside had stopped moving. There was no world, no temple, no grass, no mountains, nothing. Nothing but a still frame image in front of him, terribly significant but slightly blinding. It was tough for him to see exactly what it was, but he focused hard, sure that it was important. He caught the glimpse of his brother and himself hugging in their wrestling gear, the GW Tag Titles being awarded to them. This truly was an important factor. I know that image. And I know that it will be important. It represents the team work that Jushin and I must use if we want to win this match, if we are to be victorious we must work as a team. And so we shall, for losing is not an option, and we will come out victorious. The Souls of the Black Lotus have truly arrived in Primetime Central! MASTUDAI TOUITSUTEKI!! UNITED FOREVER!!
