Introductions, as if I Need One - Part I Chick. Chick. Chick. Matt Nash stands just outside Night Moves, lighting the second to last cigarette. His long, blonde hair floats behind his head, since the curls give it a mass equivalent to that of a large boulder. Nash is tall, not so dark, and not very handsome. The look of a construction worker, or a lumberjack; but not a model, no way in Hell. After getting into town, Matt stopped for some waffles and coffee, got the job, and found the strip club for some fun before getting back on the road; the road that never ends it seems. He takes a hard drag and watches the bright cherry light up. Exhaling, Yukon looks out over the dim lit highway and at the flat farmlands that go to the eye’s end. Funny, a strip club out in the middle of nowhere… perfect. Swiftly, Matt reaches inside his jacket pocket to pull out a picture. He takes another drag and looks at it shortly, and then puts it away, turning around to check for onlookers. “Bum a light, fella?” Matt turns back to find an older, wider man standing next to him. The older man smiles, mainly with the hopes that this blonde behemoth is one of those ‘don’t judge a book by its cover’ kind of guys. Nash stares for a few more seconds, pulling on the smoke again. He reaches into his pocket, pulling out a Zippo without saying anything, just staring. “I really appreciate it,” the older man says biting down on the filter and struggling to light his. Eventually, the flame connects and the man hands Matt his lighter back. After a good three drags, the older man holds up his cigarette, turning to Yukon, and chuckles a bit. “My wife keeps hiding the lighters and matches in my house from me, telling me I should quit. Those things will kill you, Henry! she always screams, heh heh. What does-“ “A lot of things can kill you,” Yukon pulls on his smoke again, exhaling it out of the corner of his mouth to make sure not to introduce Hank’s eyes to the smoke. “Just so happens you choose to smoke. Tell her to deal.” “Well, uhh… it’s not really that easy when she’s my wife and my only reason for living, you know?” Henry smokes again and then waves his hands around a bit, like most people do whenever they’re smoking and talking. “Besides, what she doesn’t know won’t hurt her. Right, big guy?” Mat Nash pulls the cigarette from his lips, exhaling and wearing a giant smile on his face. “Exactly, Hank.” “That’s funny, that you call me Hank, I mean.” Henry’s face gets a bit weary but it goes away with another smoke. “My wife is the only person to call me Hank. Here we are for one minute and we’re already gabbin’ like old pals!” “Yep, sure is funny.” Matt looks at his cancer stick and notices it’s almost out. He glances at his watch, realizing that its just about 2:09AM and there was a lot more driving to do. “Got any other funny stories?” “Thought you’d never ask!” Henry pulls on the smoke and drops it to the ground, still waving his hands around while he stomps it out. “I’ve got tons; I work for the Union here in town.” Matt’s eyes turn to the road, watching the traffic go by as well as the seconds he could be enjoying Natalie and Simone's beautiful money makers. Matt looks to Henry halfway through his funny story. “You got a car, Hank?” Henry stops talking, smiling timidly. “Ye- yeah, sure do. Nothin’ special, just this old Ninety-nine Mustang. Wh-why?” Henry says as he watches Matt’s eyes light up. “Nice car. That one, over there?” Pointing, Matt moves his head down and looks across the parking lot at a blazing orange and black Ford Mustang. Decent car Matt says to himself, inside his head, away from anybody else’s ears. “Business must be good.” “Doing alright I suppose. Look, I’ve got to run back inside so I’ll-“ Matt’s hand wraps around Henry’s collar quickly, grabbing his tie and reeling him in like the big catch. “Wait, wait! I didn’t-” Matt Nash smashes his head into the bridge of Henry’s nose. Blood pours out of the two nostrils and from the gash on Henry’s nose. The man falls down, only supported by Matt’s strong grip on the tie. Matt looks around, noticing somebody walking down the sidewalk under a nearby streetlight. Matt pulls up the bloody man and looks him in the limp face. The person is only steps away now. “Ya galdamn drunk! I told ya once, no touchin’ them girlies, now git!” Matt drops the man to the hard, cold concrete sidewalk as a slight chuckle comes from the passing woman dressed in a white dress. She smiles and walks on past the commotion as Matt watches her pass out of the corner of his eye. Looking around, Matt lifts up the old man and drags him into the parking lot shadows. Seconds later, the roar of a Mustang liven up the parking lot and the headlights on Henry’s car light up. “Who-who are you?” Henry says through the blood-stained lips, trying to lift his arm a bit. Matt looks over, reaches across the seat, and smashes Henry in the face to shut him up. “I’m the guy who you never wanted to see.” Matt says, staring straight ahead. Henry, meanwhile, searches his head for an answer. “Call me Yukon. Yukon Gold." Henry’s eyes open, finally, showing their whites and deep green color. His chest begins to heave up and down. Beginning to panic, Henry looks out the window with his lip beginning to quiver. His eyes look to the sky. “God help me.” Another fist connects with the back of Henry's head, sending him face first into the window and in more pain. "You wish."
