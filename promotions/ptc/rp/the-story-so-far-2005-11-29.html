The Story so Far…

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 His eyes are shut. His world is small. His mind closed to any hint of happiness. John Day is not your average wrestler. If it was up to John Day he wouldn’t wrestle. John only wrestles because he has to and it’s the worse possible profession he could find… after all he is emo! So having a job he loved would be a contradiction. Wouldn’t it? John was a good boy; he grew up in a prosperous middle class educated family with only one brother, each receiving a balanced portion of attention. Always one to look at a situation from the negative prospective John despised his brother with a passion. His brother represented all bad in the world. If his brother did it then it was stupid, if his brother wore it then it was lame, if his brother listened to it then it was meaningless and if his brother did it then there was no point in doing it. So Greg being the older brother attended University following family tradition. Studying Economics at Harvard Greg was respected by his father a Master in Chemistry. The following year at Christmas Dinner John announced he wasn’t going to attend University and instead planned on travelling Europe selling petty art. His family were distraught at the idea, John thrived off of the anger his family openly displayed. Packaging his bag’s John left home without remorse for his family’s expectations. He arrived in France and within days is enchanted by a fellow American export Lucy. Lucy and John hit it off. John feel in love with Lucy, the love was dark, it was deep and obsessive. His art wasn’t selling and Lucy was beginning to find John creepy. A month into the relationship Lucy left John and took the next plane from Paris to America. Depressed John found comfort in a bottle of whisky and the sharp end of a knife. He would drink the bottle of Whisky until it no longer hurt when he slashed the knife across his wrist. Crying in the fashion of a three month old baby until he passed out, waking on the hard concrete floor of his apartment, the blood and whisky staining his clothes. Finally the French police found John after he failed to pay his artist taxes. 30 Days in prison did John no harm. John under the administration of the French police cleaned up his act. Ditching the alcohol, the smokes, the self mutilation and the casual drug smoking he dedicated himself to a clean lifestyle. A lifestyle free of abuse, a lifestyle where he was in sole control of his body, John learnt the way of the Straight Edge. A friendly French Police guard introduced John into Hardcore and while in prison John would constantly do exercise in time to hardcore. After his time was up John asked to be extradited back to America. He was going to find Lucy, he was going to make amends with his parents and he was going to get his education. However events never panned out how John Day would have wanted. He got back to America and upon arrival was met by an armed police officer who informed him of the restrainment order placed on him by Miss Lucy Alice Gobbs. Taken by surprise John was shocked, he hadn’t been prepared for this and wondered what he should do. The only thing he could do was to go home…
