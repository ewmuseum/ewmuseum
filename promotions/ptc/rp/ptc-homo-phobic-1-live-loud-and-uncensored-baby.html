Homo/phobic #1 - Live, Loud And Uncensored Baby!

21 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***How did Vice Squad get the shot you ask, well... it probably happened like this.*** Four weeks ago… roughly… The scene opens up inside the much-publicised PTC offices. Campaign posters from various PTC staffing elections still have pride of place on the corridor walls, whilst new one ones are already being primed with paste by janitorial staff… who were probably also elected into their menial roles. The camera poises at one such poster emblazoned ‘Elect the Erect today’ – A bright pink advertisement belonging to an early electoral campaign staged for the sole purpose or elevating the Bum Boozler’s position within PTC, and thankfully one that is falling on deaf ears for now. The cameraman picks up the pace as it sets off down the long and illustrious corridor. The echoes of two sets of footsteps reverberate within the, otherwise, silent vacuum of the walled confines, but there is no other figure in sight. Doors line the corridor, each one emblazoned with a golden plaque… the regalia of an important member of PTC Office. The Global Title Division Commissioner, The Vice President, The Extreme Title Division Commissioner… The Cameraman stops as sounds emanate from the nearby door. Interfed Planning Committee… Voices are heard from within the room. “Look, we don’t have enough interfed tournaments… let’s make them weekly! Nah, hourly! Hourly I say! Mwahahahahaha! – You there, boy!” “Who? Me sir?” “Yes you! Check this week’s obituaries…” “Why sir?” “My dear, ignorant, child. Tell me, what’s the most important rule of interfed tournament success?” “I don’t know sir.” “Take a guess, boy.” “Umm… Spacing them out well enough so people can divide their time amongst all of their commitments?” “Poppycock, boy! Rule one of interfed tournament success is to dedicate it to a dead guy! Find me a dead guy! Better still, kill me a living one… Magnum Wolf will do!” “Wow, we’re really setting a low tone for today sir.” “Yes, yes we are.” The camera swivels from side to side with a visual display of disdain. Cameraman: “They say the walls are padded inside there… I’m inclined to believe them.” The cameraman proceeds once more. Several more steps bring the cameraman facing another similar door… this time the plaque upon it reads: ‘Tag Team Division Commissioner’. He gleefully announces their arrival to his unseen companion. Cameraman: “Here we are… the place where things happen.” The Cameraman’s hand comes into frame and boldly opens the door before stepping into the room. The sound of fingers tapping upon a keyboard immediately greets the intruders. A grey haired, aged, woman leans to her left to look beyond the large monitor that would otherwise obscure her tiny frame from view. She squints through her pink-rimmed glasses as she inspects her visitors. Secretary: “Yes? Can I help you?” The Cameraman jerks slightly as his silent companion now moves past him. The man is young, slightly out of shape, an assumption made purely upon what appears to be a rotund and portly belly, but despite this, he seems well turned out within a snug fitting grey suit. Man: “Hello there. I’m here to see… err… thingy.” The Secretary looks down her nose at the clumsily spoken individual. Secretary: “Thingy?” The man combs his hair away from his forehead. Man: “You know… that guy.” Secretary: “The Tag Team Commissioner?” Man: “Yes! Him!” She corrects him with a sombre tone. Secretary: “Her.” The Man puffs out his cheeks with exaggerated surprise. Man: “Her?” The Secretary nods once. Man: “I had no idea.” Secretary: “You missed the clues?” The Man shrugs. Man: “There were clues?” The Secretary rolls her eyes. Secretary: “Never mind… I’ll see if she’s free.” The Secretary presses the intercom button. Intercom: “Yes?” Secretary: “Miss Jones, there’s a Mr…” The Woman offers a prompting gaze his way… he stammers, caught off guard as he allowed his eyes to wander. Man: “Err… McQuiston, WWA Representative.” Secretary: “… McQuiston from the WWA here to see you.” … a brief pause ensues… The Secretary impatiently gazes off towards the window as she stabs at her cheek with her tongue. The Intercom sounds out again. Intercom: “Show him in please.” The secretary removes her finger from the intercom and looks back at Mr McQuiston. Secretary: “You can go in now.” McQuiston: “Thank you.” McQuiston and his dutiful Cameraman walk towards the large wooden door. He offers a polite knock and then steps in. Inside the immaculately kept room the young looking female rises from her desk, and already offers out her hand in greeting. McQuiston places his briefcase upon the desk and obliges her with his firm grip. Commissioner: “Please, won’t you take a seat?” She beckons him into the chair behind the desk, opposite her. McQuiston places his rump into the seat, and snuggles in. McQuiston: “I assume you know why I am here?” The Commissioner dons her reputed girlish charm with a delectable smile. Commissioner: “To thank me no doubt, for a job well done.” McQuiston looks at her soberly. McQuiston: “I’ll get right to the point. We at the WWA are appalled by the bout that went down at Fiesta last night. It was disgusting!” The Commissioner clasps the pencil upon her desk and nervously rolls it under her hand. Commish: “How do you mean… disgusting?” McQuiston angrily scoffs. McQuiston: “I don’t have time for games Miss Jones, so I’ll show you, ‘How’!” McQuiston opens up his briefcase and delves inside. He pulls out several bottles of Budweiser and sets them upon the desk. The Commissioner raises an inquisitive eyebrow. He looks at her, scornfully… defensively. McQuiston: “They’re not mine. The WWA Booker asked me to pick some up for him while I was out…” He continues with his explanation under the weight of her unconvinced expression… McQuiston: “… the elevator has broken in our office and the stairs are difficult for a man his size.” He shrugs her off with an exasperated huff and returns to delving in his case. The Commissioner grins. McQuiston this time emerges with a small digital camcorder with a large viewfinder screen. He points the viewfinder towards the Commissioner and hits play without explanation. She watches carefully as familiar WWA Footage commences… ---------------- Boozler begins to wind Somers’ leg around his own, locking in a figure four leg lock… Chase: A wrestling move? Oh, that’s not so bad… …but Boozler reaches out a large hand and grasps hard onto the prone groin through his wrestling trunks, and begins playing with Somers on live TV! Somers: PLEASE GOD, MAKE HIM STOP! The mixture of pain and having another man playing with his penis is too much and Somers begins slamming his hand down on the mat in submission! Chase: They retained! Home/Phobic retained and not through buggery! ---------------------- McQuiston stops the footage. McQuiston: “See that! Sick! Absolutely sick… The WWA were up in arms about this!” The Commissioner scowls. Commissioner: “Exactly what were you all expecting?” McQuiston: “Not, not… not this perversity of a wrestling bout! The Roster went crazy, and who could blame them… with the thought that this might happen to them next, this monster should never have been allowed on our show! Poor Mal Somers hasn’t been the same since…. He’s started talking to a belt for god sakes.” The Commissioner drops her head to the side as she tries to reason with him. Commissioner: “In fairness, Mal was talking to his belt waaaay before he met the Boozler or Shane.” He nods, admitting defeat on that account at least. McQuiston: “Look, look… I’m not here to question Mal’s sanity, or lack of for that matter, I just want to sort this mess out.” The Commissioner nods. Commissioner: “What did you have in mind? An apology from the PTC?” McQuiston shakes his head. McQuiston: “No, no. It’s gone beyond that now. The backlash from this has been immense… the WWA Roster has been turning on the staff, calling for blood, and all hell has broken loose, they blame us for letting this happen, for not protecting them from this… this… whatever it is.” The Commissioner eyes him suspiciously. Commissioner: “So, what do you want?” McQuiston: “First of all, we refuse to air this debacle on our networks.” The Commissioner sits up bolt straight. Commissioner: “But you can’t just pull it, you’re contracted! PTC has poured a lot of money into this promotion, and has even more riding on its success.” McQuiston: “No, you’re right we can’t. But we can edit it… In fact, we’ve already begun.” McQuiston points the camcorder towards the Commissioner again. She cranes her neck for a closer inspection. Commissioner: “That’s not the Boozler. His bulge is way too tiny.” McQuiston smiles. McQuiston: “No… it isn’t. I think the Bookers drafted in Magnum Wolf to play the Boozler’s part and re-enacted the whole thing… this time he wins by a pin.” Commissioner: “Wow! This Wolf guy is good isn’t he… he may not have the tackle but besides that you’d hardly be able to tell you subbed him for the Boozler, just look at the way he’s stroking himself.” McQuiston puts the camera away. McQuiston: “Yes. Luckily he’s had a lot of practice... and he pulled it off very well indeed.” Commissioner: “I bet he did…” The Commissioner nods. Commissioner: “Ok… I can live with that, the Boozler isn’t too popular… hopefully nobody will notice.” McQuiston looks unconcerned. McQuiston: “Secondly, I demand that you strip them of their titles!” Commissioner: “What?” The Commissioner shakes her head. McQuiston: “Or I’ll be forced to pursue legal action against them and PTC in this matter!” Commissioner: “I can’t strip them of the title just like that. We have a reputation to uphold.” McQuiston: “As do we, one that is being threatened by this!” McQuiston suddenly slams his hand upon the table. McQuiston: “We are a serious organisation, Miss Jones! This whole charade undermines everything we have done and stand for… we demand recompense, and this will do nicely!” He calms down slightly. McQuiston: “Fair and just I think, considering that had that not happened the WWA would have those titles right now, where they belong.” Commissioner: “I can’t simply take the belts off of them without reason.” McQuiston draws a deep breath… a compromising one. McQuiston: “Then I suggest you find someone capable of beating off these obscene idiots!” Commissioner: “How about Magnum Wolf?” McQuiston: “No. He’s too busy beating off Angelo Deville and that famous Russian, what’s his name?” Commission: “Zangief?” McQuiston: “Yes. He wants his job back, see.” Commissioner: “Oh.” McQuiston: “So that’s the deal… you take action, and we won’t have to.” The Commissioner sits back in her chair. They stare at one another for a few long moments… Suddenly the Commissioner smiles… immediately drawing McQuiston’s attention. McQuiston: “What?” Commissioner: “I’ve got an idea. There’s a team in FAW called Vice Squad…” His impatience gets the better of him. McQuiston: “And?” Commissioner: “And… not only are they a great team, but… they’re also Cops.” McQuiston: “Look… do you think working for the WWA was a career choice? What’s your damn point?!” The Commissioner grins. Commissioner: “Well. Even if Homo/Phobic win, which will no doubt be in their customary manner… chances are they might be arrested right there and then.” The Commissioner smugly smiles. McQuiston: “Excellent. Then we will have our revenge and Homo/Phobic will definitely be banged up by the Vice Squad boys.” The Commissioner grimaces, but nods. Commissioner: “Precisely. Then everyone is happy. Everyone!” The scene fades…
