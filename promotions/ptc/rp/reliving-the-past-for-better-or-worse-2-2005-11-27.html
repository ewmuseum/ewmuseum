Reliving the Past - For Better or Worse #2

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-27 A distinct, powerful electro-beat is to be heard as the screen flickers again after our original showing for a glance into Jack Murphy’s ‘illustrious’ past it seems. The electro-beat is very catchy and as it progresses a synthesised keyboard is added into the mix. The music is unmistakeable now, its pounding beat, the tinny keyboard, the sparse guitar strumming…it can only be one thing. Aha’s ‘Take on Me.’ As Scandinavian heartthrob Morten Harkett belts his lungs out the picture is now beginning to come into focus…and it’s not much better than the first segment that we had seen. We’ve moved indoors from the warm sandy beaches of California and are now in a sterile room. Surrounding the view of the camera are mirrors on every wall, reflecting everything that is within the perspective. What’s being reflected is a vision of flesh and pastel, sweat and taut asses. There’s nothing like a good old aerobics class to really open your eyes huh? Numerous tight butt cheeks framed by a thin elastic piece of the leotard suits that you all know and love litter the room, several male, several female. What begins to become creepy is which is which. All in the centre of the picture however is what we’re concerned with. Jack Murphy, ‘The 8th Wonder’ as he called himself back then. And what a sight. A bright pink leotard is stretched to the maximum over Murphy’s chiselled physique. It’s shocking pink to make it even worse and there’s a couple of dabs of black to complete the whole piece. He has a white sweatband on, keeping his stray hairs from covering his face and other than that he’s in pretty much the same sort of appearance as before. He’s currently side-stepping from side to side, leading the class in an intense workout. Jack Murphy: Can you feel the burn? Can you sense the pounds just falling right off? Here at my gym ‘The 8th Wonder,’ we specialise in my eight step programme to becoming more like…me! Murphy beams at the camera, not losing a step as he moves over towards a man right beside him. He’s getting along great with the workout and is sharing the same cheesy grin. Jack puts his arm over the man’s shoulder and winks. Jack Murphy: Kyle here has been with us now for only seven weeks and look at the amazing progress he’s made! Almost creepily in sync the screen halves and shows an extremely overweight, obese Kyle stuffing his face on some pies. Jack Murphy: As you can see by our graphic Kyle was a quitter, a person who was willing to just let things slide by and let himself become blubber, never leaving his mark on society except to ask ‘can I have more fries with that please?’ Now, Kyle is a lean, mean calorie pounding machine. Jack slaps Kyle’s chest area, smiling as he hears the solid thud against the strong, unyielding muscle. Jack Murphy: You see? With my help and the eight step programme, you people can be somebody! There are a lot of people right now who simply don’t understand where I’m coming from, people like Jake Robertson, people who are trying to take me down a peg or two. ‘The 8th Wonder’ continues to walk through the aerobics class, leaving Kyle to take over the class itself. Murphy has his hand under his chin and rubs it lightly, pondering something for a slight moment. He then continues as per usual. Jack Murphy: What people like Jake Robertson don’t realise is that I am no threat to them, I am in effect the opposite: someone who’s trying to bring to them a higher learning and achieve their peaks, reach the summit of the mountain and be thankful for myself…be thankful that I brought them there to become an icon. But they don’t see it that way, instead they feel that I, Jack Murphy, am a pest, a fad and a no good waste of talent! He scoffs loudly at this, idly picking up a piece of rubbish from the floor. He also grabs a towel and leaves it hanging round his shoulders. Jack Murphy: If I were such a waste of talent my friends, would I be the No.1 contender to the NCWA Championship? Would I? Diligence and discipline breed success - you better learn that Jake, otherwise you’ll end up like all the rest…in the trash! With a flick of the rubbish he lets it fly over his head and it’s casually back heeled over his head. The camera cuts to somewhere else, distinctly familiar to the other scene we have seen before as the piece of trash falls in the dustbin. We close again, with that beaming smile.
