Loudest. Silence. Ever.

20 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-20 “I sprinkle sand into the waters of night. The grains burn as they fall, reminding me of another in times long passed away. I watched him even then as he fell, his face undefeated, his eyes still proud. It is time for me to walk the abyss. Time to reclaim my own.” Sandman (preludes and nocturnes) Dawn, and the city was awash in those first golden rays that made everything seem so simple…so innocent. It was as if the gods had reached down and washed the evil taint of night away, allowing only what was good to shine through for those few precious seconds. Have you ever woken up to them…the seconds when the world seems suspended in that brilliant shimmer and everything seems to glow with the newness of a newborn child? Have you ever watched it fade? Watched the glow climb higher and higher, snatched back to a place we know we’ll never reach…leaving nothing but the grit and dirt to coat your windows? He has. He watches now, as the lights go out across New Orleans and the jaded city takes on a shine like new forged sword. Man how it bleeds, he thought, looking beyond the glow to the garbage in the alleys, hidden in the shadows now but reeking just the same. Listening to the sirens wail in the distance, another night over and with it another death, another child of the night who failed to survive the harsh reality of the city. Such gullible eyes, he thought, the new ones stepping off the buses and highways and flocking into the Quarter expecting to live a life that has always been denied them, searching for freedom in a place where freedom crushes the sprit and tramples all over the soul till there is nothing left. Such a simple illusion really…searching for freedom only to become a slave to sex, drugs, the limited warmth of brief moments spent in a hotel room…cheap whiskey and the heat of a southern city. He sighed, and turned away from the window. With dawn came the pressing need for sleep, so he closed the shades up tight, drew the black folds of cloth that served as curtains closed across the glass and lay down upon the bed, his white and green streaked hair falling across his face like a veil. Yet sleep seemed reluctant to find him, even as he focused on the sounds of his city, stretching, awaking and slowly coming alive. Funny, he mused, how night so closely resembled death, though it was the time when the city was most active and life teemed with activity on every level, and day, day when the world was slow and steady and pulsing along with the same monotonous beat seemed to him as death should be…devoid of everything wild, everything exciting, everything that brought heat to the blood in ones veins and let them know that they really lived. Another sigh…even his own musings weren’t enough to silence the memories that kept the blissful abyss of sleep at bay. He opened his eyes and stared towards the ceiling, the darkness in the room yielding little details in the plaster, but he knew what was there…he had painted the images himself, years ago when he had first claimed the third floor apartment as refuge. Yet even the thought of those fallen angels, and ancient gargoyles with their blood tipped wings did not bring him any solace, for the images in his mind burned even brighter. "You know, I’ve walked down more highways that I care to count in my lifetime and there were so many times that I thought…I hoped that one would be my last. So many nights, too many to count, I slept beneath the stars with little more than an old duster for a blanket and a rock or two for a pillow. Where I was going, where I’ve been, neither of those seemed to matter, I just had to get somewhere…anywhere but here." "You can waste a lifetime running, or you can watch it shrivel and die from the same piece of earth beside the sea where you were born. Life and death, so strong it worlds, are little more than a tiny bit in the cycle of time." "I remember reading somewhere that in some cultures the deaths of young ones weren’t mourned as much as the deaths of elders, for the youths had not had the time to accomplish anything. I guess they didn’t realize that just being young and surviving that time in your life was accomplishing everything." "For all I’ve seen and done between the coasts of this great country, the thing I’m proudest of the most is that I’m still out there doing exactly what I set out to do. I have my six string strapped to my back, and a bottle of Jack in my pocket. I’ve got my bike, and a contract with a place that will still allow me to get in the ring. It’s the closest thing I’ve ever come to finding peace and about as close as anyone will ever get to freedom." "You know, I no longer ask for forgiveness for my sins, what’s the point when I only plan to sin again. I’d rather just sit back and toast the good time, talk about the girl who got away or the bar fight that gets bloodier every time I tell the story. I’d rather relish in the past and all that I’ve done, then look ahead to what might be awaiting me when I’ve used up all my time and the good graces of the gods." "A lot of people believe I have an obsession with death, and dying, ghosts and the spirits of the afterlife. Bullshit, I say they have an obsession with me. I didn’t ask to be dancing on the edge so many times, I didn’t ask to hear their voices whispering to me and calling me home. But I’d be lying if I said I didn’t get a kick out of it. Some sick thrill that got the heart beating just a little bit harder and the goosebumps funning up and down my spine. Fear, naaaa, not so much the fear, but the thrill of walking the razors edge and sometimes plunging deep on the other side." "So give me something to work with Hurst, because these insults jokes are getting old, turn your attention towards me, give me some of the focus that you gave Jay Phoenix and I promise you that together you and I will wash an arena in blood." A slight chuckle is heard from Odium's room and he continues to speak what is basically a little speech for Dave Hurst. "Of course, it will be yours, but I promise the experience will go beyond any and all that you have ever felt before. You wanna be Primetime Central’s next rising star…I wish to be the flame that burn it to the ground. You wish to inflate your ego, I look to paint my face in its ashes…in your ashes and in the ashes of all those who stand in my way." "Do you know what its like, to sit back and listen to all the arrogance from men who will never see beyond the mirror, beyond their own eyes into what lies outside of their selfish ambitions...Its sickening, sickening to know the sport has been reduced to guys like you Hurst, guys whose only knowledge of sacrifice is pushing someone else in front of the train." "Do you honestly believe that I’m here for gold? If you do then you have it all wrong…I’m here for vengeance and soon the world will know my target and understand exactly who I am. I will take that razors edge that I walked for far too long and I will slice them with it, and I will taste their visera, and yours, Dave, and I will offer all that remains to the thirst and hunger that I am experiencing for your pain. So fear me, loath me, despise me, but don’t dare underestimate me Hurst, because looking past me to someone else can get you killed." Learn it… Remember it… Live it…
