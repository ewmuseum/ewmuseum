The Savage Garden

1 September 2005

Summer surprised us, coming over the Starbergersee With a shower of rain, we stopped in the collonade And went on in sunlight, into the Hofgarten, And drank coffee, and talked for an hour. TS Elliot - The Wasteland, 1922 The smell of her perfume was overpowering...completely overshadowing the other senses he had. Like a preternatural fragrance, floating idly over its unsuspecting worshipers, taking the souls of those who were fortunate enough to breath in this intoxicating fume. He mused that he was floating over it, like a raft floating over quiet waters, tranquil and undesturbed by anything that might cause concern on any other night...but not this night. Opening his eyes, her face greeted him with its pure beuaty. Surely Da Vinci had not invisioned her as he did now...pure of thought, pure of reason...pure of divinity. Her hair, black as night, waved majestically on some insular breeze, blowing around her, shielding her. Her skin...as soft as the petals of some forgotten rose...yet as pronounced and firm as a face of pure marble. So defined were her features. So imprinted. This was the vision of a genious. Her lips, caressed by time with the curls of maturity, and the fullness of majesty. They were smiling...capturing his gaze with their infinite benevolance. His eyes were drawn to hers just then, and it was as if this goddess...this patron...could pierce his very soul! He felt small, vulnerable and almost insignificant in front of this beauty. Once again, his eyes closed, sniffing in the aroma surrounding her. Never had such aromatherapy been attainable, and perhaps he thought...never would it be. It was perfect. Everything was perfect. Then her voice came floating over the vaccuum of space between them. As soft and gentle as a spring morning...carrying with it such beauty and fragrance, that any piece of art or music, would pale in insignificance. It's texture, like that of a symphony orchestra...yet not unlike a simple cello, speaking its song to the breeze. She held him a prisoner...unwillingly, yet so willingly, by her gentle trance. Voice: "You know who I am?" The elemental figure almost hovered in the air in front of him, as it posed its query. Her query. The trance over him tightening, as if some gentle hand were squeezing him, and he wanted to be squeezed! He pondered for a moment. His attentions drawn by everything about her...her words almost held no meaning for him...simply some combination of sounds, her sounds...and that was all that mattered. He was sure he was flying. Voice: "Of course you do." For the first time, he plucked the courage and state of mind to formulate his mouth into something other than a wide open gaping...in awe of this ceraphin. "My Angel. So beautiful...devine...you must be. But your appearance...it comes to me as..." Voice: "As you would have me." Of course, in his deepest mind, he knew this to be true...afterall, why would such an ethereal presence morph the truth...she didn't have to. Her words would be accepted like golden sovereigns showered upon a witless audience. But that begged the question...why did she look like this? Such hair, such eyes, such lips, such skin...yes she was a marvelous beauty. But why her, over all the other potential beauties. It was perhaps not her...but what she stood for. He had never known this beauty, but it was clear what she meant to him. When Da Vinci painted his Mona Lisa...the impact upon the world was astounding. But, the impact on his soul...from this portrait...was undeniable. Quite a cliche to have picked the most famous portrait the world had ever seen, to sacrifice his soul for...but it was just such a picture of innocence. That's what she stood for. Innocence. It may not have been what the great Leonardo had been looking for, when he created this marvel...but to him, she was his angel. His eyes closed for a moment, perhaps blinking, or perhaps resting. They opened, in what seemed like half a moment, and everything had changed. She was no longer an ethereal being, caressed in some unknown breeze. Her godly beauty still existed...but it seemed somehow, more human. She stood there, naked, wading in the oasis. She laughed as she let water sift through her fingers over her shoulder. The water was so clear, so crystal...it was almost as if it were part of something much greater...a glint of something...but it was still just an oasis. It gave her skin a glistening...which seemed to further the humanity of her. As she played and danced in the water, he felt drawn to her...as if pulled by an imaginable force. He felt his feet surrounded with water, as he waded in towards his goddess. The heat was searing, coming down, the light reflecting over the Oasis and into his eyes, brightening everything. Surrounding them, what seemed like the most beautiful forest he had ever seen. The trees seemed to have been crafted to perfection themselves. There was no symmetry to them, but there was an exactness that mathematics could never decipher. It was almost as if they intertwined in some dance, frozen in time forever by the graces of mother nature. Flowers of a thousand colours sprouted around the trees, dotting everywhere with rich vibrancy, and creating artificial rainbows, surrounding the oasis, keeping it as if under siege. The sounds of the various creatures dwelling within this ecosystem gave it a lustre that only served to heighten his infatuation for this angelic creature. This was paradise...more so because he shared it with her. Slowly, he moved forward, further into the clear water. He didn't feel cold. In fact, he didn't feel anything, except for the heat of the sun, mild, upon his naked face. That struck something in his mind. He could feel his face. There was no mask. There were no scars. He could feel the soft brush of sunlight across the face which he thought he had lost forever. It's gentle hand moved silently over his skin, like a mother caressing a weakened child. In the water, she stood, still playing with the clear water, letting it fall over her majestic body. She gleamed at him, her eyes opening to his, and her mouth upturned, beckoning him. He moved further forward. She outstretched her hand, as if asking for him to come further in. A brief laugh escaped her lips, as she draws her hand back to her side. He moves forward, his eyes never leaving hers. Like a Siren beckoning the lost sailors to their rocky prisons, she called to him...but there was nothing malicious about this angel. He took a further step into the water...which now caressed his thighs in its submerging blanket. She took a step back, laughing as she did...was this a game to her? He had to touch her. Had to feel that skin. He stepped forward again, and began to move his hand out. His fingers, outstretched, weaved their way towards this statuesque majesty. She stood still, the water covering her to her stomach, as his fingers moved further towards her. He stepped another giant step towards her...it seemed giant to him, but he never seemed to be getting closer to her. His fingers were but a foot away from her, and edging closer. He could feel her warm breath on the edge of his arm, he had to feel his angel...his Mona Lisa. He could almost feel her now. He closed his eyes, wanting to savour the moment. His fingers were warmer now...must be getting close to her skin. He felt the warmth caress his hand, and move slowly up his arm. He still had not felt her skin, but his body was warming under the sun, in this watery paradise. The warmth was intoxicating...he wanted to curl up in it. But no...he had to lay his fingers on her skin. The warmth began to rise, and still he edged on. It began to become uncomfortable. Hotter...he could feel a bead of sweat trickle down his forehead, and graze the tip of his eye. Then he felt his arm prickling, as if pinched by some discontented insect, looking for a way to stress its discomfort. The nipping became more rapid, more frequent, and made its way up his arm. His fingers were hotter now than before...they were almost burning. Must touch her. Then, the sensation of burning caught his finger tips. What was this? His hand withdrew immediately, out of instinct from the pain. His eyes opened suddenly, expecting to see his goddess standing before him. At first, his eyes did not seem to catch anything. It was as if all was black...but then, here he was...in the oasis. The idillic forest surrounding him. And before him...before him his mistress. But she was much different. Her skin was red...as if heated from the core by some element. Her hair no longer waved in the breeze, and the glistening skin was replaced by an almost marble texture. Her humanity seemed to have disappeared from her. Slowly, he noticed her smile dicipate. Her eyes narrowed, and sunk quietly. Her mouth began to take on the shape of a frown...his angel was no longer laughing. Her mouth opened, and for a moment, he thought he saw her shouting something, but no sound eminated from her lips...until it hit him in a torrent of sound. He was flung back by the sheer viciousness of the sound. A high pitched scream. The noise pierced his skin...his ears...his eyes...every vessel in his body. His hands raced up to his ears, he couldn't bear this. Why was his mistress screaming? What was happening? Her eyes still stared into his...with a pleading gesture, as if crying for him to do something. Her mouth shaped a perfect O, as she continued her monotone symphony of screaming. His eyes were glued to her, as if a pair of hands held his face towards hers. Then, the most horrible thing happened. Out of her left shoulder, a tongue of flame eminated into the air, that seemed like it had never been disturbed in its history. Her eyes widened, still gripped to his...and he found himself starting to scream. Another flame soared out of her chest, and a third from behind her neck. Within moments, her entire upper body was consumed in flame. It moved down her, even going under the Oasis surface. His Mona Lisa was burning! She was on fire! Frantically, he thought what he could do...but there was nothing he could do. Rooted to the spot, he could only watch as his angel burned and screamed, a cacophony of chaos, transpiring before his very eyes. All seemed to transcend the boundaries of sorrow and loss, as the water of the Oasis began to bubble with the effects of the flame. Her screaming became bolder, louder, and it seemed as if another voice had joined her...but it hadn't. Then a third voice, and a fourth. Soon, he could count at least a dozen voices, each screaming, in the same, or almost the same pitch, with different tones, and it was as if all eyes were on him. He managed to rip his eyes from hers, and turned to his left. There, on the bank, he saw the same...flames eminating from everywhere! The bark of the trees burnt with the scorching flames come from nowhere. The flowers, withering, charred. The grass and the very leaves falling into the Oasis, creating a floating convoy of lighted ships. His eyes returned to her, and her skin was peeling away. Her wondrous skin. And, to his utter horror...she began to melt. He stood, transfixed, locked on the spot, as his goddess began to sink. Her face seemed to distort itself, her left side sinking faster than her left, and she became a hideous testament to pain. Her screaming never dissipated...only began to become deeper. The voices conspiring to make one, all lowered, slowly, as if in time with her sinking form. All that remained, was her head, just above the water....though it was hardly distinguishable as a head any more. The flesh no longer existed...it was molten metal. Finally, the last of what had been his Mona Lisa, disappeared. All that was left was the screaming...and it was so low now...almost like a roar. He looked around him, and the bubbling water, now red as flame, was surrounded by charred remains of trees, dead and withered. The flames still existed...but there was nothing left to burn. It was as if the flames existed out of sheer will! The screaming now was so low...it was no longer screaming at all. It was a roar. The roar began to be more staccatto...broken up into spits of deep sound. This sound rose again, slightly, until it was obvious what it was. It was a laugh. But wholly different to how his Mona Lisa had laughed. This laugh was entirely malicious. Tainted by evil. This was no laugh for happiness...more a laugh for spite. He looked around him, and on the bank, the flowers had come back to life...but they no longer represented the beauty they had once eminated. The flowers were now all different shades of dark red, maroon, and blackened purple. They no longer sprouted petals, but flames. And their pollen had been replaced by rows of gnashing teeth...all hungry, starved...each one seeming to have its own will, no direction, sheer anarchy. The laughter took on the form of what he could hear as four different voices, all coming out of the bubbling molten pool. Slowly, the middle of the pool began to rise, as if something were trying to get out. It rose until it was as high as he was...and it continued to rise. Ten feet...twelve feet...the movement rose to fifteen feet, before the molten lava (for that's what it now was) began to slide down the figure that had surfaced. His eyes gasped, at what he saw. He found his hand over his mouth, to prevent himself from shouting something. Before him, fifteen feet tall, was a scorched being, features distorted...skin peeled off, replaced by charred material and patches of red magma, emenating flamte above the figure. A row of black teeth, sharp and filed to perfection, lined his jaw...and his eyes were filled with fire. But...something about this figure seemed strangely farmiliar. Despite all the charred skin, the deformity...he could tell who it was. This was a giant, destroyed form of Jason Spire! He was looking at himself...but deformed by the dance of the flame! Spire's eyes sank deep into his head, and his mouth let out a scream. He stumbled back in the water, that now burned his legs...causing more pain than imaginable. He stared at this daemon...and shouted up at it. Jason Spire: "What...What are you?" The figure in the molten lake let out a roar of laughter...again five voices seemed to laugh as one. Its eyes grew larger, and its hand raised, clenched. In its hand, it held what appeared to be...yes...it was his Mona Lisa! Clad in flame! She was still screaming...but it was drowned out by his laughter. Spire Daemon: "What am I? Do I not deserve respect enough to ask Who am I?" Spire just stared in horror, as the daemon crushed the poor suffering Mona Lisa in his powerful grip, grinding her into burning dust, letting it trickle back down to surface on the red molten water. Spire Daemon: "I think we both know who I am Jason. Search your soul...you know me..." Spire searched the daemon's eyes, as its mouth curved in a vicious smile. Yes. He knew this foul creature. He didn't know how...but he knew. A name popped into his head...the only name possibly fitting for this beast. The name had come to him as if planted there by someone, or something...maybe this thing...this daemon. Jason Spire: "...Radakast!" The daemon let out a high pitched roar...as if laughter, but nothing this horrible could be laughter. Spire Daemon: "Why does the name pain you so? For, I am you...you as you should be. Before this snivelling hollow skeleton of a man that stands before me took over. Suffice to say, I simply am!" Spire shook his head, but he couldn't deny this daemon's presense. His Mona Lisa...now nothing more than ashes, burning over the surface of the water. Spire Daemon: "Yes...what? Did you think you could save your soul? Did you believe that somehow, she could redeem you? Have you completely forgotten who you are? What you are?" Spire's mouth closed, as his eyes penetrated those of this daemon. His hands rested on the shore of the former Oasis...but they burned. Every inch of him burned. Spire Daemon: "Then, let me remind you peasant!" The Daemon let out a further roar of laughter, and raised its right arm high into the air, setting the very oxygen alight with its fiery grip. It sent its arm flying down, and struck Jason hard across the face. Spire felt himself flung what seemed like seven metres across the ground, his face burning with pain. He let out a scream, as all he could hear was the deafening laughter of this being...this painful aparition. His face was burried into the burning soil from the impact, and it was so painful to bring it out. He sat, turning around, to see the Daemon pointing at him with its eyes, accusing him. Spire's face was burning most of all now, and his hand moved up, out of instinct. Jason Spire: "No! No! You can't! You Couldn't! Not this...anything but..." His fingers fell across what he knew to be there. The scar across his face, surrounded by the burnt flesh. It was his face as he knew it must be. All this had been a fantasy...the hard truth was laid to bear with the restless screech of reality. His fingers moved inside the grove of the deep scar, and tears fell down his face, instantly burning up on his scorched flesh. He could see the smoke and steam rising from where his tears had been. And he screamed...a scream that he thought he could never scream. Above it, he heard the daemon's voice. Spire Daemon: "It was you, not I, Jason...that made this...all of this. It is your destiny Jason. I...am your destiny!" His screaming was unbearable...the pain! His eyes closed from the sheer pain! The heat lowered slightly, but his screaming continued. Wet. Pain still coarsing through his vains. The smell changing...liquid fire over his heels...burning. Wet. All over, wet. Uncomfortable. Distressed in his position. Wet still! ******************** Jason Spire rose suddenly in his makeshift bed. The sheets flung back...he was sweating prefusely. His hand instantly rose to his face...the scar, still there...but it seemed long ago that this scar had penetrated his skin. A dream. Yes...it had to have been. A sigh of relief escaped the lips of Spire, as he rose from his bed of two seperate mattresses...one halved, the other, torn in various places. He stood, eyes adjusting to the present situation. He was behind bars. Spire's eyes fell upon the bars that held him captive. Rusted by the decay of time, and out of them, nothingness...the hallway was so dark. A single lantern shone in his room...but it was only enough to uncover four feet radius of darkness. He looked to his left, to see the toilet, broken at its root, as if by some man's force. Above it, a sink that seemed like it gave out slime, not water. But why? Why was he here? Certainly, he couldn't remember. On the floor rested a newspaper...he picked it up. Last thing Spire remembered, he was going to sleep on the flight from Paris to Atlanta. And now, here? Was he even in the first world? Was he in the States? What had happened on that flight? It didn't matter. It was where he was, and he wasn't going anywhere. He got up, and went over to the bars, trying to pull them free. they rattled on their hinges, but would not budge. He kicked at them, making a scraping sound...but they still would not budge. Spire let out a scream, and slammed his fist into the bar, causing it to bleed just above the knuckles. His frustration was mounting...but this was not the way forward. Then, a voice... Man: "Hey man, shut the hell up!" Spire's eyes searched the darkness for the source, but realized it was impossible. He felt the wall of his cell, finding that a part of it gave way to another barred exit...possibly to another cell. Jason Spire: "Who...who said that?" Man: "Who the fuck do you think asshole?" Jason Spire: "Radakast?" Man: "No bitch! It's yo' Momma! Now, shut the hell up and give me some damn rest!" Jason Spire's eyes widened. He had never known his mother...but was pretty sure she didn't have a male voice...nor that she would use such profanities. He came to the conclusion that this was not his mother. Jason Spire: "very funny. But where...where am I?" Man: "Shit...are you for real?" Spire didn't respond to the comment. Man: "Damn...you in the Federal State Penetentiary, here in sunny old Florida! All I heard from you the past few days is your damn snoring...and you been talkin' some foreign shit. Wassup wit' that?" Florida? How had he gotten to Florida? Why was he there? And what was this "Foreign Shit"? These were questions that needed answers...but he could not find them. Jason Spire: "I...I don't know. I...I was on a plane...it doesn't matter. Do you know why I'm here? What did I do?" Man: "You trippin'? Come on man...it's late. I want my sleep. So, why don't you just shut yo'..." Disjecta Membra! Where did that voice come from? Those words...what did they mean? Was it another cell mate? It seemed to come from in his cell. Man: "There you go again...that foreign shit. Man, you gotto quiet down yo' twisted little mind and sleep bitch!" Jason Spire: "I...I didn't say that. Now please...tell me. Why am I here?" Man: "If it'll shut you up, you here like the rest of us...you here for life! Must've done some crazy shit on the outside to end up in here with us. All I knows, is that you been here for a long time boy...and ain't never said a word...not like this. But I ain't game for all this...first words in a year...I don't give a shit. Shut up, and go to sleep!" Spire stumbled back, falling against the opposite wall of his cell. A year? What was the man talking about? He had been on a plane coming from Paris to Atlanta just six days ago. He was sure of it. He had been at the Louvre...he had seen the Da Vinci Mona Lisa. She had called to him with her colour, vibrancy...with her brightness. He couldn't be here! What was this? Some kind of trick? In jail? For Life?! Jason Spire: "No. You're wrong. I'm not here for life. I must be...just here for a few days. Yes...that's it. I'm just here for a few days." There was no response...only the sound of snoring. Spire couldn't stand this. The darkness...the lack of anything. He stood at the bars, and rattled them. He struck them with all he could. Jason Spire: "Do you hear me? I'm only here for a few damn days! Only here on visit. Not life! No!" He was shouting now. Restless prisoners were heard waking up, and some were shouting down the hallway at the disturbance. Jason Spire: "Not here for life! No! Why do you lie to me!" Lights went on in the hallway, as Spire's shouts echoed across the stone walls, causing commotion everywhere. More lights were flicked on, and the main lights came on suddenly. Footsteps were heard, coming to his cell. The door was opened. He immediately rushed out...looking one way...dead end. The other way, three guards were walking towards him urgently, their batons outstretched. Spire turned and ran towards the dead end. The prisoners began to shout, and bang their bars with the edges of their water cups. Hs felt his face...scarred still. But this couldn't be real could it? He crashed into the wall of the dead end...half expecting to be able to fly through it. No...the pain coarsed through his body...this certainly felt real. Real stone. Cold, hard against him. He leant his hand out to touch it. He felt it. Scabby, rocky, hard surface.
