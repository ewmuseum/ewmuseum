Date: 2005-11-30 "Daddy! Tell me a story!" [Sitting next to his son’s bed, the old father quietly begins to reminisce…formulating the historical story he could serenade his young child with about his favorite subject: men in tight clothing beating on each other.] "Alright, bucko, but we’re going to have to be quick about it. And I have the perfect story…how would you like to hear the one about Pete, the little fella who triumphed over it all?" "Yes, daddy! Yes!" "Well…once upon a time, there lived a little chimpanzee. He was happy, and he had all of his family and friends around him all of the time. Kind of like you do, Timmy. But then, the evil white people came and stole him from his family!" "Oh! Darn those white people!" "Indeed. And they weren’t just white people…they were English. And that was like white squared. So the evil English people stole poor Pete away, and they took him to their evil laboratory…for, you see, the PG Tips Tea Company had bought Pete and many like him." "I hate the British." "But Pete wouldn’t live like a laboratory chimpanzee…Pete had big dreams. Dreams of becoming a professional wrestler. So Pete stole away…and he ran to America. And Pete, after living in gutters and traveling with a pack of midgets for many months, finally became a wrestler. Pete became very popular, and his Chimpy Chompy Crunch was devastating and feared across the land. And one day, he got to the very top…and he had a World title match!" "What happened then, daddy?" "Well…he bit off Jamie Shock’s left testicle. He was the biggest of them all, then, even though he was only very little…just like you." "How big was he, daddy?" "He was so big, he was the featured guest on The Tonight Show with Jay Leno…" ----------------------------- [Fading backwards in time, we return to an inconvenient moment on the Tonight Show. Jay Leno sits behind his desk, disturbingly quiet. Sitting near him in his chair, the UWF World Championship belt lying next to him, is Pete the PG Tips Chimp. Pete looks uncomfortable.] "So…eh…Petey the monkey …" "Aw oo oo aha oooo!!!" "Chimpanzee, sorry. Hey, uh…Petey…what do you call a monkey that works in a call center?" "Ooo?" "A who-rang-utang!" [Kevin Eubanks starts cackling madly in the background.] "Thank you, thank ---" [In a flash of furry madness, Pete is out of his chair and across the stage in seconds. Jay gasps, but it’s quickly drowned out by the maddening cries of Eubanks.] "OH GOD NO!!! HE’S BITING MY TESTICLES!! NO!! OH GOD!!!! GET HIM OFF!!! OHHHHHH NOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!" [With muffled chomping sounds, we cut back to our story.] --------------------------------- "But you see, it was after the big Tonight Show appearance…and, you know, the lifetime ban…that Pete’s life began to spiral out of control, Timmy." "But why, daddy? He had it all!" "He did, but unfortunately…little Pete had a terrible, terrible secret." ---------------------------------- [Now at a bar, Pete the PG Tips Chimp is slumped over, finishing off his last cup. With a grunt, he shoves his cup towards the bartender, who looks at him sadly.] "Now Pete, you’ve had twenty-seven already…I’m afraid I’m going to have to cut you off. This is just far too much tea for a chimp to—" [In a flash, Pete is standing on the bar, grabbing the bartender’s collar in his tiny little hands, and shaking the old man back and forth.] "OOOO OOOO OOO!!!!!" "Alright! Alright! You’re the champ! Whatever you want!" [Shaking his head, the bartender begins pouring another cup of PG Tips Tea, while his customer slouches back down into his seat. Passing the cup over, he mumbles to himself.] "Gosh darnit, Pete…can’t you see what you’re doing to yourself? What you’re doing to your friends? You’re killing us all!" [Pete downs the glass of tea with a mighty gulp, then shoves the cup back to the old man.] ------------------------------------- "Wait…tea?" "Yes, bucko. She’s a cruel mistress…and unfortunately, she had her dirty little claws clutched around our hero’s throat, and just wouldn’t let go." "But that doesn’t make any sense!" "Life doesn’t make sense sometimes, kid." "But tea’s not…but…I…" "It all came to a head after that, Timmy. Unfortunately, it was only the next week when Pete finally lost his hard earned World Championship… to some disturbed kid named Seymour, I think. And then…" "What, daddy?" "Then he appeared on … the Adam Corolla show." ---------------------------------------- [Pete the PG Tips Chimp is sitting in a chair. He looks dirty, his fur matted and frizzy at once. His eyelids open and close quickly as he shakes in place.] [Adam Corolla sits next to him…once again in an uncomfortable moment with his guest.] "So, Pete…I hear you lost your World Championship this week…" " *burp* " "So, uh…Pete. I know you’re a wrestler, but hey…what’s up with those tights? I don’t need to see that much!!! Eh? I don’t!!!!!! Come on!!!!!!! Put on some pants!!!!!" [You can hear a pin drop in the silent, dead audience. Seconds later, his furry, messy head is buried in Corolla’s crotch, trying to rip out his testicles.] "Pete! Oh…God no!!! Stop it…Pete…trying to do an interview…Pete…oh dear sweet lord no!!!!!" [Moments later, Pete the PG Tips Chimp is flipping over a chair on the set. He leaps onto the phone system and begins hopping up and down insanely, trying to drive it through the glass table.] "Aww dooh pleigh!!! Aww dooh pleigh!!!! Ooo ooo ooo!" --------------------------------------------------- "And that, kid … was the last time anyone heard from Pete… the PG Tips Chimp." "Wait… that’s it?" "Yes indeed. It’s a sad, sad story, but alas…that is life." "Wait …the chimp loses the title, drowns himself in tea, bites Adam Corolla in the nuts, and goes away? That’s your whole fucking story?!? No wonder mom left you!!" "No, your mom left me because she’s a crack whore. Now go to sleep, kido." "Poor Petey…" [Fin.]
