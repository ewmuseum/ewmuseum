Chapter Six: Dedication (GT RP #3)

Donny Diamond Emotionless Monster Joined: 03 Feb 2003 Posts: 59 Location: My own personal Hell. Posted: Sun Apr 13, 2003 10:28 pm Post subject: Chapter Six: Dedication (GT RP #3) Chapter Six: Dedication Dedication, a word that not many people knows the true meaning of anymore. Especially when it comes to this business. I remember back in the day when you were first signed to a company you either got fired, or stayed there until it closed. No anymore however, nowadays it’s all about who has the bigger payoff and who can give you a bigger stage to perform on. Only a handful of people can now make claim to achieving this form of dedication. Though dedication does not end by sticking with one federation it lives on in everything. Your style, and technique also has dedication in it. When all of these new generation kids got into this business they brought with them a complete and new style of wrestling, a lot of the older guys felt threaten by this and changed their style. Some, however, didn’t and till this day remains as some of the top stars this industry has. Decisions, dedication can also be found there. When you make a choice and stick with it for the rest of your life shows dedication to yourself, and it also shows everyone how dedicated you are to everything else, which in the end will earn you respect. Not many people have this amount of dedication left, but some however do. ~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~ †If death was the only option Then you would have no escape I look at death as a chance for the weak to run Never will I run from a foe† ~`~ Hospital in Las Angeles, California. ~`~ | ~`~ April 12, 2003. 5:33 PM. ~`~ ~¤~ Nothing can be heard except for the slow and steady beating of a heart coming from the cavity of Donny Diamond’s chest. Standing in front of the doorway he is there with his arms by his side and his turned slightly to the side as he stares towards the large shadow behind him through the corner of his eyes. Everything is painted in a very pale gray, except for Donny and the black shadow, and anything else that was animated with life is now suspended in time. He turns around and faces what once a large shadow. Floating in midair is a flowing black down and hood with cut up edges, within it is a skeleton that has red glowing eyes, and in his right hand is a long, and curvy wooden stick with a blade attached to complete the sickle. ~¤~ Donny Diamond: Death, how nice it is to finally meet you. You really live up to the dress code everyone talks about. As for your entrance, that was just marvelous. The whole freeze time thing is really cool; you’ll have to show me how to do it one day. Let’s cut to the chase, why are you here? [Death just sways around in the air as Donny stares deep into his red eyes. Getting tired of the games quickly, Donny steps forward getting closer to Death.] †Even if that foe is myself I will not hide Nor look for reasons to run Nothing is stronger than I† Donny Diamond: Come on and answer me. I know you can speak, so stop with the games because I am not in the mood. Death: Ahaha. I am here to carry the soul of one, to the land of the dead. The soul of your brother, Ron Diamond, it is time for him to pass on. Donny Diamond: I see. Normally I would have no problem with you doing your job, except this time your job is hitting a little close to home. Don’t take this personal or anything, because I’m not trying to tell you how to do your job. Although I am telling you, you’re not taking my brothers soul. Death: Do not try to interfere with my plans, or you will fall to the same fate as him. Donny Diamond: Is that so? Like I said though, you are not taking the soul of my brother. So I guess that means we have a little problem that needs to be dealt with. Death: Indeed we do. [After saying those three words, he immediately uses his sickle as if it was a boomerang as he sends flying at the head of Donny. He quickly drops to the ground to avoid the strike and does a small backwards roll to get back up on his feet. Using all of his speed he darts forward with a big clothesline type move to knock Death down, but he isn’t quick enough as Death use his sickle to cut Donny across the arm. Grabbing his arm in pain he backs against the wall as blood leaks through his fingers.] †Never can there be a challenge I cannot surpass I waited for a test that would push me to my limits Finally it has come Still I will not go and run† [Again the sickle is tossed, and Donny leaps forward to avoid it as hits the ground and rolls back to his feet while grabbing the small sword in the back of him inside of its sheath attached to the back of his pants behind his coat. He goes to slash death with it, but the returning sickle nails him in the back of the legs knocking him down, not before the sword goes right through Death’s body. Mocking Donny with a laugh he prepares to toss his weapon once again as Donny slowly climbs back to his feet with the sword clutched firmly in his hand as blood drips down his arm and onto the blade.] [Death quickly slides across the room in an attempt to end Donny with use of the sickle, barely Donny moves out of the way to avoid the attack. Donny lunges forward with the blade in front of him, Death quickly avoids it by darting to the left, but Donny has his other arm out stretched and slams it across the floating chest of Death. Death falls down and it starts screeching in pain as its chest can be seen burning. Looking down to his arm blood can be seen dripping from his fingertips and blade, and then he slowly looks back towards Death.] †I will show no remorse to any of my foes I will treat them as if they were my father I showed him no pity Because I spit on the weak† Donny Diamond: The one thing that separates the living from the dead is your only weakness. Flowing blood. This little fight between us may be far from over, but I suggest you leave now unless you want to feel more where this came from I suggest you leave while you can. Death: This is far from over between us. [Slowly he drifts towards the wall then passes right through it. The grayish scenario changes back to the normal colors as the cuts on Donny’s arm begin to close up. Ron’s heart rate picks up again as everything returns to normal, and Donny slides his small sword back into its sheath behind him as he walks out of the door to ICU. As he walks down the hall to get back towards the elevator several security guards can be seen running back towards ICU. A few minutes pass and Donny is once again in the parking lot, he gets on his bike and starts the engine back up. Turning around in the parking lot he then speeds off onto the highway.] [Time passes by fast as Donny soon arrives back at his home, we can tell this by the humming of the engine coming from his motorcycle. The door to the estate open, as Donny walks inside where his butler Alfred greets him. On the television in the corner we can see news coverage where it seems a small private detective agency has just explode with unknown causes.] †I pity no one Not even if you were lying on death’s bed Life is your punishment for the crimes you committed So live out your sentence like you should† Alfred: How is the condition of your brother sir? Donny Diamond: You could say he has had a brush with death, but he is doing just fine now. Take fifty dollars from my safe and send a fruit basket to the nurses in ICU, enclose a picture of me with it as well. Alfred: Will do sir. Is there anything else I can do that might help you prepare for your Global Title match? Donny Diamond: No, I got everything I need. Just make sure to keep all visitors away, no matter whom they are. I will need the next few days to prepare myself for this match, and no distractions can be allowed. Alfred: Very well sir. If I do say so myself you are becoming such a great athlete inside of a ring, and you are showing great dedication during your training. Donny Diamond: Thank you Alfred, however I am very dedicated for this match. This is my chance to reach the status my father could not even dream of. I only get this chance once, and I will not do myself wrong and let it slip from my grasp. [After sliding his coat off, and handing it to Alfred he then starts walking up the stairs to go into his room. Upon reaching his room he opens the door, then walks in closing it behind him. Reaching behind himself he grabs his lower back as mass amounts of pain run down his spine. Passing out he falls face-first onto the floor.] †I will prove to all what I am capable of So now is the time for you to stop depending on others Here they cannot be by your side No one will be able to rescue you† [Once more our scenery changes and from the miles of canyon and lava that can be seen one can easily assume we are once again back in Hell. A crowd of damned souls form a circle for what lies in the center. Two Minotaurs are holding chains in their hands; on the other end of the chains is Donny Diamond. Forced down onto his knees, his coat and shirt is ripped off as deep cuts are seen across his back. Standing before him is the master of all the is damned, and clutched firmly in his hand is a whip with barbs on every side.] Satan: For your actions you must be punished. Hold his head up so he can look at his father when I speak. [A shadowed demon comes forth, and pulls back Donny’s head by the hair forcing him to look a head.] Satan: You did something no one is allowed to do; you interfered in the plans of Death. Even though I gave you a special gift, it still does not give you the right to change the plans of Death. Now that you know you will be punished for the problems you caused, do you regret doing so? Donny Diamond: No. I stand by the choices I made, and I will not take them back. Satan: Very well, then this may make you a bit stronger after it’s over. It shows dedication to yourself my son, but you must learn that while dedication is good, the choices you make are not always the right ones. Donny Diamond: Maybe to you, but the choices I make are always right to me. Just you cannot see the reasons for my choices, and the reason I am put into these situations. So father, if you wish to punish me for doing what I believe to be right, then go ahead and do it. [He flicks his wrist and snaps the whip across the shoulders of Donny, cutting him deep. Donny screams out in pain as he leans forward only to feel the whip come, and slash into his flesh once again. He collapses onto the ground below due to the pain, and slowly everything around him fades to blackness as his eyes close shut. When they finally open again he is in a very dark room lying in a bed of silk blankets on his stomach. Right away he feels the stinging coming from the wounds placed across his shoulders and back from the lashing of a whip.] [Trying to get up is too much of an effort due to the pain from the whipping, soon the door to his room opens. Coming in is someone dressed in a complete black robe, and behind them is his father. Unable to turn on his back, he turns his head to the side so that he looks at the company before him. Donny watches as his father mutters something into the ear of the druid beside him, the druid nods and starts to speak in a weird tongue. A blue glowing light appears around his hands as he passes them over the wounds across Donny’s back. Red light appears over each of the wounds and soon they start closing up as maggots appear around the wounds to eat the dried blood away.] Satan: When he finishes his healing, get dressed and join us in the courtyard. [He leaves the room as the druid continues to cast his magic. Slowly the pain leaves as do the maggots, Donny then decides to turn himself over onto his back. Nodding to the druid as if saying his work is now complete the druid leaves the room. Getting up from his bed he looks around the room trying to find his clothes but they are nowhere to be found. Exiting the room he walks towards the back of the throne room, and goes out through the door, which leads into the courtyard. Sitting down on a bench is his father with a golden apple, and his pet three-headed dog by his side, giving the apple to the animal he then slowly looks back up towards Donny who slowly walks closer.] Satan: Glad you could make it. Ah, do not be afraid of Cerebus, he looks mean on the outside but he is very gentle to those that are in good graces with me. Donny Diamond: How can you say I am in good graces with you, after you just had me whipped in the center of town? You had me punished because I stood up to one of your minions, and still I believe in my decision to do that. Do you punish all of your children like this? That was my brother he tried to kill, and I was not going to stand there watching him take his life away. Satan: Now do you understand what Sorrow, and myself was trying to teach you? The hate for your brother is so intense that you will do anything to make him feel pain, including the death of someone close, but still you will not let anyone else take his life away. Out of all the souls I have seen pass through here, never have I seen one as demented as you Donny. None of my other children, or any of my minions has this form of evil within them. Donny Diamond: So now your going to tell me that I am somehow special, and different from everyone else you ever loved? I care not to hear you preach about I am different from everyone else, or how I am some type of evil prophet. You gave me this darkened soul because you believed me to be capable on dominating anything I set my sights on, because I am dedicated to everything I do. Just like I am dedicated to my upcoming matches where I will be given a chance to surpass my father’s dreams. Satan: Ah yes the tournament where you are to face others for your chance to accomplish all you ever dreamed of. Almost like a tournament between Gods, except it is a battle between mortals all fighting for the chance to become a legend. I hope you are preparing yourself for this battle my son, I will be watching to see how you fair against others that are set and determined to win this very match as well. Donny Diamond: Perhaps they are determined to win this, but I am dedicated to winning this Championship. All of my life I trained myself for this opportunity, so you can guarantee yourself that I am not going to allow this chance to be taken to from me at ease. Not only am I prepared to go forth to win this match, but I am also prepared to do it if murder is my only option. Soon my time to showcase my abilities will come forth, and then to the entire world I will be known as the Global Champion. †I show dedication in everything I do Time will soon come to stand still As we all battle for immortality I will show the world how dedicated I am to become Champion!† _________________ DWO Record: 9-5-0 PTC Record: 1-0 -I only mark for those deserving, and you know who you are.-
