Revelations ...

22 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-03-22 Life is tricky sometimes, this I have no doubt about. Things can be going so well at one moment, then in the next be horribly wrong. The sad part is at times it can switch at the blink of an eye, no warning signs and certainly no remorse for what it can do to you. It was barely over a month or so ago that I had found my way back to the world of professional wrestling. Not only that but Mary and myself had really built something of our relationship, it’s to the point where I have trouble thinking how it was without her around. When GCW reopened I was excited about the chance to prove what I could do to them, as I was badly held down in the beginning. I didn’t hate Steven Caldera or the GCW for this neglect though, actually I used it as motivation for my future endeavors. After GCW closed it’s doors I went on to thrive in Dragon Wrestling Association, eventually defeating my most hated enemy for the World Heavyweight Championship. It was a feeling I will never forget, and I doubt much else could top it. Steven Caldera helped light this fire under me that hadn’t been lit in quite sometime. I became what I knew I could … a champion, a warrior. So obviously it was almost as if I was going to get the chance to rub their noses in it a bit. Show Caldera and the rest of the GCW that I am what they always needed. Things began well, although I lost my first match I knew it wasn’t much to be upset about. I have yet to lose since as well, along with capturing the United States Title, I have beaten some of the best GCW has to offer, easily may I add. The men I beat have been considered legends of GCW, some even once were thought of as unbeatable by the likes of me. I thought this would fill this gaping hole that still sits inside of my very being. I thought that making them bow before me, and recognize my superiority over them would give me the satisfaction that I crave, but sadly it hasn’t done anything. It has almost seemed too easy defeating the likes of NBK, Jhub, and Khan. Its almost to the point where I know I am going to win everytime I step between those ropes … there are no butterflies, no fear, no wondering if I will get beat or not. There is no conflict with Payne, Loki, Zodiac, Jeff Wylde, or even Johnny Lexicon. Those competitors were the ones that would make you second guess yourself … you would look into their eyes and see nothing but confidence, just as if you looked into my own. Sure there is confidence, and even arrogance in the eyes of Octavian, Jason Dread, and Toxic but it’s not the same. None of these men in GCW have any kind of fear factor. Payne took me to levels, in the ring, physically, mentally, and personally that no other person has. Johnny Lexicon is the ONLY man to make me tap out, and I swear on my life that he will be the last. I have just been completed in disappointment with GCW thus far. Perhaps that’s why it didn’t take much convincing from Caldera when he asked me to jump into this Prime Time Central tournament. When he came to me with it, I knew I’d say yes but I had to see how badly he felt he needed me representing GCW, instead of the usual suspects. I signed the dotted line and put myself in … little did I know that I was setting myself up. These people that run this Inter-Fed know nothing about me, which puts me in an instant disadvantage. How do I overcome the obstacle of being written off before I even begin ? It’s a question that I have very little time to answer, and perhaps never will. There were plenty of speculation on the outcomes coming in, and very few gave me a chance, some even basically guaranteeing this AWS Man a victory over me. I wish those doubters would go speak to Alex or Johnny about guaranteed victories over me, and then they’d be singing a different fucking tune. No matter though, I am in this thing and as much as I feel everything is stacked against me I cannot just roll over and die. I must allow myself to be known, make sure that after that last punch is thrown or that last body is crushed, that people will say “Damn, that LeStatt guy sure looked good out there.” Absolute victory is not what I am looking for out of this … certainly not, because absolute victory in a fluke over the top battle royal is hard to guarantee. I will impress though, and I will earn the respect of my peers … I will give them a rush they have never felt before. Lifting up my hand I check the time on my watch, everything was a bit blurry at first, but soon got clearer. 1:03 AM was the time, far later than I wanted to leave this place. I was not usually big on going to bars and drinking, but something lead me here, now I am not sure if it were the smell of the liquor or the big neon light that hung outside, but I am sure it was SOMETHING. I had been sitting in this same spot for about two hours, only moving when the waitress would come and fill up my glass with, well … whatever it was I was drinking. I finally tried adjusting to the lighting and everything around me, but really it was to no avail. My sight wasn’t going to come back strong probably until the morning, but HEY … fuck it. I just needed to get home and crawl into bed, maybe even have sex with Mary, if she was still waiting for me. I finished the last drops of my drink and slammed the glass down. I stood up, on very wobbly legs, and draped my trench coat over my shoulders. I figured it’d be too much damn hassle to put my arms through, as I’d probably fall over attempting it … wonder if that’d hurt at all ? I walked towards the door, passing the bar as I left and throwing a stack of bills down … hell I don’t even think I had anymore money to lay down. I pushed open the sliding door to lead outside, and the cool air hit my face as if someone had just thrown a bucket of ice cold water at me. It moved me back a bit, the surprisingly crisp air, especially coming from the muggy bar. Now the next challenge was finding exactly where the hell I parked my car. I began pressing my alarm button, and I sure could hear that beep … but finding the direction of the beep was an obstacle all in itself. I held my eyes open, probably looked like some kind of deranged bug … which just the thought of made me chuckle. After a short search I found my car, the black Camaro never looked so good. I jingled my keys about before trying to fit the right key in. I misjudged the first attempt, getting nothing but air. I concentrated a little harder, and swiped at the key hole, this time connecting with the car and having the key slide, down … making a very noticeable mark. I had to hold everything in to not explode right there … I closed my eyes and took a few deep breaths, then went at it again, with strong concentration. I went forward, but it didn’t work again as I tumbled forward banging my forehead on the door, then falling to my knees. “SON OF A BITCH !” I reached up with both hands clenching at my forehead, which I knew would be red and have a new bump to go with this night. I sat there for a moment staring at my key chain, before realizing something. I moved my finger over a button on my key chain and pressed it down … unlocking the door automatically. “Figures…” I really didn’t feel like getting up yet, and was certainly glad no one saw my little mishap. A few moments passed of me just staring at my keys, before laughing a bit and pulling myself up using the door handle. I pulled the door open, then tumbled inside shutting the door behind me. Surprisingly getting the key into the ignition wasn’t a problem, and I let the engine hum awhile. I laid my head back in the chair and closed my eyes, trying to get myself together. I took in a deep breath and slowly let it out as I opened my eyes up. This was probably a horrible idea, and I should probably just sleep in my damn car … but Mary was waiting, and I couldn’t disappoint her. I shook my head, and took off from my parking spot heading home. Things seemed to just blow by me, almost in slow motion. That’s when my nightmare from the night before hit me hard. I loosened my grip on the steering wheel as the memories flushed my brain. A lump in my throat formed as I tried concentrating on the road in front of me. I looked down at my hands which had now begun to shake. My vision began to get worse … but not from intoxication, but from the tears, the tears from the memories … those memories of the night when … As I looked up I saw her face, as I was coming towards her full speed. “NO NOT AGAIN !” I swerved to avoid her … sliding off the road and in front of me I could see a tree. All that I could think of was how I just wanted to … Then everything went black.
