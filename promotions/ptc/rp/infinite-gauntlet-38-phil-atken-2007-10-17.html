Infinite Gauntlet 38 - Phil Atken

17 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phil: Why is everyone else sending in letters? Is this like a Jim'll Fix It where retarded, broken down wrestlers write to Jim in the hope he'll actually turn their career into something marginally successful by giving them the opportunity to get their face smashed in but on a larger stage? Dick: I don't think that show has been on the air since the 80s. Phil: Wow then these people are just plain needy. OH PLEASE I AM GREAT GIVE ME A SHOT AT KICKING ORPHANS IN THE FACE. Dick: Didn't we establish last time it is not an orphan face kicking contest? Phil: Then why did I kick all those orphans in their cute and adorable yet disgusting vomit stained faces last time? Dick: Frustration at losing, I believe. Phil: Do you blame me, I lose a match to a guy named Tchu which in itself sounds like some sort of high powered cleaning product. "Shoo away all the filth of your rape den with new Tchu Extra strength". Dick: I don't even think that's how you pronounce it. Phil: It is for the purposes of that joke. Dick: Which begs the question... Phil: OH AREN'T WE MR SASSYMOUTH SASSERSON MCGOO. Dick: McGoo? Phil: It's a new thing I'm trying Dick: May I point out that every new thing you try is an astounding failure. Phil: May I point out that you're a balding man in your forties who spends most of his life following about a young and pristine wrestler in his late twenties. Your life is either a failure or you have extreme homosexual tendencies and living in paradise. Dick: My wife wishes I was gay. Phil: The gay community doesn't, and they thank your wife for her drunken mistake. Of course every day for her is a drunken mistake. Dick: How do you know what the gay community wishes for. Phil: I don't, I just know no one wishes for you. Perhaps your vital organs but certainly not your personality. Dick: Should we move on, we ran out of tape last time. Phil: Oh yeah, well it's quite simple. I want the match because I'm talented. If I don't get the match, I remain talented and this rinky dink contest will no doubt be worse off, while I will remain abound in hookers. Now I think about it this contest really would just be Dick's payday. I mean I suppose he should have some money but I'll be buggered if I care. Dick: PLEASE GIVE HIM THE MATCH. OH GOD PLEASE. MY CHILDREN ARE CURRENTLY EATING MY WIFE'S HAM ENRICHED STOOLS TO SURVIVE. Phil: Mmm, that's good fiber. Dick: What is wrong with you? Phil: They're not my kids for a start. Henceforth verily thusly forsooth, I don't really need the money. Dick: How about I ask for the money you owe me instead? Phil: Oh, in that case... I'M GONNA KICK ASS, OH YEAH! Dick: That was pathetic. Phil: On par with the competition then.
