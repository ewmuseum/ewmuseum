Isolated

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-01-10 A stiff, cold breeze kicked up a whirl of snow around Dawn’s feet, the same breeze tugging sharply at the bottoms and sides of her duster until it flapped out around her legs like an oilskin skirt. Tugging her scarf closer around her nose and face, Dawn shoved her hands further into her pockets. "Maybe a walk in this weather wasn’t such a hot idea after all." She grumbled. In truth the whirling eddies of snow and sleet were only a pale reflection of the torment going on in Dawn’s mind, the indecision she felt pulling at her every footstep. Master Mifune’s words echoed in her ears, playing over and over again until she felt her head might split from the sound. "Am I ready?" She wondered aloud, her voice barely audible over the crunching sound of her boots in the snow. No voice came back with any kind of reply, at least not any voice directed in her direction. Instead she was jostled from all sides by the rapid conversations of the men and women around her, men and women that might as well have been trying to talk to her underwater. "I never thought," She murmured to herself, "That I could feel so totally isolated around so many people." Dawn’s Japanese was passable, and she could carry on a conversation with most of the inhabitants of Sapporo with minimal difficulty, but that often meant speaking with only one person at a time and sometimes slowly. The cacophony of foreign sound lent to a feeling of detachment, as though she were wandering alone through an empty cityscape. "No time to be waxing dramatic…" She grumbled to herself, giving her head a quick shake as if to dislodge the thoughts forcibly. "…or you might end up stumbling into traffic or something equally foolish." She wove through the crowd with ease, her large frame giving her even more leeway then it normally would. Among her western counterparts, a woman who was six feet tall was unusual, here it bordered between rare and unheard of. The strawberry blonde hair that poked out above her black ear band further marked her as a foreigner, although what that exactly meant in the current day and age differed greatly from person to person. "Run away…" A voice ghosted into ear, as though carried by the wind. Dawn stopped and turned sharply to her right, looking out over the straggling groups of people in an attempt to find the speaker. Only local pedestrian and automotive traffic caught her eye, no ghostly speaker hovered in the air or half hid behind snow covered bushes. "What the…I really AM losing my mind." Dawn shook herself again, "Next thing you know I’ll be see…" "He’s coming. You’re in danger." Dawn’s skin goose bumped and she felt the hairs on the back of her neck rise, a sudden dryness plaguing her mouth. She cast about frantically, scanning the sidewalks and cafés, the bushes and street lights as though an assailant were hiding behind each one. "Who’s coming?!" She demanded aloud, receiving some startled looks from passersby, "What are you talking about?" "Talking to yourself Dawn? They say that’s the sign of a deranged mind." A rich baritone interrupted her thoughts and wrapped itself around her heart like an icy hand. Dawn turned slowly towards the sound of the voice, finding herself almost face to face with an all too familiar man in a dapper black coat. "Speechless my love? That’s how I’ve always preferred you to be." The hand squeezed tightly, robbing her of any breath. The grin on Marcus’ face spread wide, the hint of sharpness lingering at the corners of his mouth. "You don’t look pleased to see me, I’m terribly hurt cherie." "Marcus…" The strangled sound that escaped Dawn’s lips sounded only vaguely like the one she used in everyday life. Her ex-lover grinned again, stepping close enough that his breath would have wafted across her face…had there been any. The sudden closeness kick started Dawn’s brain, reviving the all important flight instinct. She spun sharply on her heel to run, only to be arrested suddenly by a heavy grip on her arm. "Ah ah…I have something that’s important to you…I think you want to hear me out." In his other hand Marcus offered a steel watch, stained with…blood. The band was familiar, a gift that Dawn had given when she had studied at the school only a year before. "Michi…" She gasped, "You bastard." "I’ll have you know my parents were married." He threaded his arm through hers, turning towards one of the frozen paths leading into the central park. "Walk with me a moment my dear…if you want to see your young friend in one piece again of course." Her skin crawled at the feel of his touch, but Dawn fought down the rising gorge in her throat and fell into step next to Marcus, a bead of sweat slipping down her back despite the cold air. Marcus patted her on the shoulder, delighting in the obvious discomfort he was causing her. "Smile my dearest; you wouldn’t want anyone to think you didn’t want to come with me. It would terribly unfortunate for your friend if the police were to suddenly be involved…" Calling on her years in the ring, Dawn willed her expression to relax, forcing a cheerful smile onto her pretty lips…one that never reached her eyes. "That’s better Cherie." He cooed, patting her on the arm again. "So obedient, I always loved that in you." "What do you want Marcus?" She gritted out, practically spitting the words. "The only thing I’ve ever wanted of course love." He turned to look at her, his pale blue eyes shining unnaturally in the evening air. "You." "So you can destroy me? You’ll have to forgive me for not falling down at your feet and begging for the chance to let that happen." Dawn’s eyes scanned the approaching park, looking desperately for any way to escape her unwanted visitor. The further she got from the pressing crowds, the slimmer her chances of survival became. "Dearest I never wanted to destroy you; I wanted to make you better stronger. But you were so…flawed. I had to break it all down so we could start new, give you a fresh start…rebuild you the way you were meant to be." "You mean the way you want me to be. I may not always love myself Marcus, but I won’t go back to the way things were. Not ever." "You will if you want your young friend to survive." The reply was casual, almost offhanded, but it shivered down Dawn’s spine like a cold finger. Dawn licked her lips, watching the already slim margin of her escape narrow even further. "What…what do you want from me?" "You’ll come back with me of course. Come with me to one of my many estates, where I can shower you with all the…love you deserve." He smiled harshly, the light in his eyes speaking of all manner of things that had nothing to do with love. "Let Michi go first." Marcus laughed a genuinely amused sound that rang clearly through the almost empty park. "You know that’s not how it works my dear. You come with me, and then I give my people the word to let your friend go." "I’ve got no reason to believe you will Marcus…but you know I’m a girl of my word." Dawn’s mind reeled, almost unable to comprehend the situation she suddenly found herself in. Marcus nodded thoughtfully, pushing aside a copse of trees as they walked under their snow laden branches. "True true…but I’m afraid I must be honest with you love, there’s something else I haven’t told you." ‘Oh god.’ She thought, ‘He’s got Alex too.’ "You see Cherie, I don’t actually have your little friend at all. I just needed a chance…to get you alone." He turned sharply, pulling her towards him with their linked arms and driving the other heavily into her stomach. The wind whooshed out of Dawn’s lungs as she flew backwards, slamming into the nearest tree and sinking onto her behind. "Oh it’s been SUCH a long time since I had a chance to do that. How I miss the old days." He slipped out of his designer coat, folded it neatly and hung it up on a nearby branch. "You really should have been a little smarter my love, any fool could buy a watch like that from a street corner here…although I’ll admit the blood was my own idea." He rolled his neck from side to side, cracking his knuckles as he did so. Dawn scrambled up the tree, her lungs heaving for air as she coughed and gasped. Frantically she looked from side to side, seeking a way out of the small clearing he’d lured her into. A flicker of movement caught her eye and she suddenly found him atop her, fists hammering down on her face and shoulders. "Too slow love." She hit the ground again and kicked out blindly, sweeping one leg out from under Marcus and sending him crashing into the snow beside her. She rolled away quickly, forcing herself to get up as he calmly regained his feet and brushed the snow from his clothes. "You got snow all over my new clothes dear…I’m afraid I just can’t let a slight against my wardrobe stand." He charged again, but this time she anticipated him and flopped onto her back, raising both feet and flipping him over her head into the pine tree behind. He crashed against the unforgiving bark and she rolled away again, this time coming to her feet with a large fallen branch clutched in her hands. Marcus took his time rising again, the corner of one eye twitching in anger. "Good show love, nice to see you’re not totally out of shape." "Hold on." "Oh that’s a lot of fucking help!" Dawn almost screamed, her voice breaking in fear and desperation. Marcus raised one eyebrow, pausing in dusting the snow from his shirt. "Have you lost your mind Cherie? It would be a tragedy if our absence had broken you so." The frustration and helpless Dawn felt came boiling up from her stomach, flying from her lips in a torrent of hate. "You fucking bastard! DO you honestly think I would miss you?! Are you so fucking twisted that you can’t see I loathe everything about you? You raped me in my GODDAMN BEDROOM! I can’t wait for the day you shuffle off and fucking DIE!" The corner of Marcus’ mouth twitched in amusement, but his eyes showed only cold malice. "Are you quite done? Because that’s a lot to carve onto a tombstone." He charged again and she swung the stick two handed at his head. Marcus brought his right arm up in a chopping motion, smashing the rotting weapon into a pulpy mass as he struck her hard across the face with his left hand. Dawn hit the side of a tree hard, but he didn’t give her any time to fall. Blows thundered into her face and stomach, releasing a torrent of warm blood that ran from her nose and over her lips. As she pitched forward he kicked her savagely in the stomach, delivering a series of follow up kicks into her now exposed side. She rolled away frantically, her arms wrapped around her aching midsection as thick blood dripped from her lips into the snow. She rolled her head painfully to one side, hoping to see the last blow coming…to maybe escape it somehow… …and found herself staring at the back of an unfamiliar coat. Black boots and pants disappeared behind the billowing bottom of a white trench coat, just a shade off from the snow itself. She tried to raise her head, but her aching neck dropped her back into the snow with a whimper. A cold voice spoke. "Walk away." "You’ve got no concerns here." Came the hissed reply, Marcus’ normally warm tone replaced with a seething hate. "This has nothing to do with you or your mistress." "Let me put it another way. Walk away, while you still can." "You think to threaten me BOY?!" There was the sound of metal on leather, a hissing scrape that rasped against Dawn’s ears. "She is beyond you now. Persist…and I will kill you." There was a pause, then the sound of footsteps moving warily backward. "This isn’t over, I’ll not be cheated from my revenge by Roshael’s get. You’ve just signed your own death warrant." The footsteps became hurried, eventually fading into the background noise. Dawn coughed painfully, a splotch of blood appearing in the already stained snow by her face. Her rescuer turned and knelt by her side, a handsome face with warm brown eyes staring through the haze of pain. "Lie still. Help will be here any moment." "Who…" Dawn gasped, trying to force the words out through the pain. "Ookami (Wolf)." Came the reply. "But we’ll have time for proper introductions soon enough. A siren sounded in the background, and the sound of voices began to grow louder. Through it all Dawn thought she could hear forms crashing through the underbrush. Her rescuer bent low, pressing chapped lips to her forehead. "Stay well…I’ll explain everything soon enough." He stood then, turning sharply on his heel and disappearing into the bushes…for a long moment, Dawn thought she heard the beating of wings. "I really am…losing my mind…" She coughed, her vision darkening as paramedics burst into the clearing.
