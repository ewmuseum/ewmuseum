Why?

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 What is it that I’m doing here? Not literally here, like here here. But, of course I’m speaking metaphorically. What am I doing in GTT4. What am I doing back in America. After the losses that I have suffered, many human beings would have taken to hiding, they would have tucked their tail between their legs, and they would have hid. But not me, that’s not the way I am, that’s not the way I live. And that is not how I will ever be. A lot of people complain about time. About the time they are given, their time on earth. To side with those less fortunate it does seem true. There truly is so little time to get what you want accomplished. To complete the goals that everyone has in life. So much to pursue in life, in so little time. It’s about as cliché as it gets. The pursuit of love, happiness, money, power, respect… anything really. So what does it take to make a person realize this? Usually, a tragedy. There is nothing that will open a person’s eyes to how short life is like a bullet to the stomach and their life flashing before their eyes. There is nothing to smarted a diamond in the rough up than a grandfather falling deathly ill with cancer. The truth of the matter is, we are an ignorant species. We do not realize what is truly important until it is gone. We can see it every day, yet we are too ignorant to see it happening right in front of our faces. Every day the population grows, and every day we run out of land mass, but do we do anything about it? No. Every day we burn more holes in the O-Zone layer, do we do anything about it? No, we lie and say we are improving the safety of the smog, but it hasn’t changed. So all in all what the hell does this have to do with me? It’s all about time now to me. I’m losing control, fighting a demon. Inside of me a war wages, a war which I fear I cannot win. And what has all of this taught me? It has taught me that time is precious. But, it has been a long time coming. My life has been filled with tragedy, in the last six months I have seen the deaths of my father, my uncle and my brother, my only remaining kin. And now what do I have left? A hope, a dream and a chance to make them both come true. That’s why I’m here, in GTT4. To make the most of the time I have left. I have seen tragedy, I have seen what tragedy had to show me. Now I am here to make the best of it. Hope – the hope of vengeance for my family. The hope that I can make a difference in the wrestling world which I have grown to love. Dream – a dream that I can come out of this tournament; PTC’s GTT4 as the winner, and be called the greatest at what I do. Chance – the chance in front of me, the chance to step into that ring and do what I do best. Wrestle. “Like sand through the hourglass, so are the Days of Our Lives…” the voice came through the television clearly and loudly. Ichiro’s eyes opened groggily, and he sat up slowly. He looked around the room, unaware of what had happened to get him there. He looked strangely at the television set on the table in front of him. “Why the hell am I watching a soap opera?” he thought to himself. He did not remember anything in recent memory. He had lost control again, he knew it, but there was nothing he could do about it. The blackouts were more frequent now, and he never really knew when he would come out of it. All he could remember was a terrible dream, he remember hearing Jushin’s voice. Encouraging him, talking to him, helping him out, but it scared him. The dream had seemed so real, so deep. But, it couldn’t have been real, Jushin was dead. So how could he be truly talking to him? All these thoughts ran through his mind as he watched the television, only half paying attention. Suddenly however, the television caught his attention. The images began to speak to him. His visions and memories of his brother began to fade, everything seemed to fade. Ichiro tensed up, he felt as if maybe he was about to black out again. Instead however, he did the opposite. He began to pay more and more attention to the screen. On the screen… Taylor ran out from the church. He was screaming in frustration as he did so. His suit jacket was ruffled, he had ripped his tie off and the top few buttons of his shirt were undone. What can you do, sex sells. Tears were flowing from his eyes, he had nothing left and even a new viewer to the show could tell he was on the edge. Suddenly the door to the church burst open and here came another guy. This guy had longer hair, but seemed to be just as buff as Taylor who was now sobbing with his head in his hands and his back against the wall. The new guy, who’s name was still unknown came walking over compassionately to Taylor. “Come on man, you know you can’t be doing that stuff,” he spoke softly as he made his final strides towards Taylor. “You’re all your mom has left now, and you can’t just be running all over the place. She’s scared you’re going to do something stupid, and frankly so am I.” Taylor looked up, the tears were welled up inside of his eyes. He looked straight into the other man, he seemed to be glaring straight through him. The other guy, who’s name is still unknown reached his hand down, and placed it onto Taylor’s shoulder. He was being compassionate, not in the gay faggy way you might be thinking, more in the manly, “I’m here for you bro” way. “Thanks for looking out for me Brad,” Taylor began to speak, finally revealing mystery boy’s name. “You’ve been like a brother to me Brad, that’s why when I ask you this, I need you to do it.” “I know that look Taylor, you’re emotionally unstable. You’ve always been hot-headed, don’t do something you’re going to regret. We all know how tough this is for you…” Brad began to speak, still trying to comfort his visibly upset friend. “You don’t know what it’s like!! None of you know!!” yelled Taylor. (There probably would have been some swearing, but this is daytime television right?) “Taylor, I’m sorry man. I’m just trying…” Brad stammered, stunned and taken back his hands were now at his side in a defensive position. Taylor broke down, he could no longer hold back the sobs or the tears. He began to cry and cry he did, like a baby. Brad sat down beside his friend and wrapped his arm around him compassionately. Taylor began to cry less violently now, his tears began to stop flowing and his sobbing became softer. “I know. Brad, I know what you’re trying to do. And I know why you’re trying to do it. You think I’m going to do something stupid and something I’m going to regret. But, the truth is, I just need some time to myself. It’s a lot to deal with right now,” Taylor spoke softly to his friend. Brad merely nodded his head in his approval. It must have been a lot for his friend, his sister had been robbed from him. Only months after his father had been killed in a mysterious accident. It was a lot of loss for such a young man. At only 21, an age when most kids worry about drinking and partying, he was worrying about how he could support his mother. His only family left, he was just beginning to put the pieces of his life back together and now this. Brad began to step away, he knew the pain he had seen in his friends eyes when they first heard the news, and the pain had not seemed to diminish in the slightest. It must be hard to hear that your little sister has been raped and killed, it can’t be anything pleasant at all. But that’s life sometimes, and you deal. Taylor had his own way, and Brad knew it, he was going to have to let him deal with it his way. Brad put his hand on Taylor’s shoulder one more time. This time Taylor reached up and covered his friends hand with his own. It was a true sign of friendship and respect. Taylor soon after dropped his hand and fell backwards, slumped against the wall. Brad turned and walked back towards the church. Taylor heard the door of the church slam shut, a familiar noise to him now. He stood up and began to wipe the tears from his eyes. He reached deep into his pocket, he pulled out the newspaper article with his sister’s picture on the front. He read the article, his eyes scanning every detail. “Well, if you were last seen in the East Side, then that’s where I’m hunting you, you bastard. Take care mother, if I don’t see you,” Taylor’s words were cold and chilling to the bone. He stuffed the article back into his pocket, but his hands did not come out empty. He pulled out a small silver six-shooter along with it. The look on his face changed, from sadness to stone. He was going to do something he was going to regret, but he would have his vengeance. This was his chance he wasn’t going to miss it. Ichiro returned to normal. He looked around, he was confused. He looked at the clock, he saw that ten minutes had passed. But they had seemed like mere seconds. He was completely absorbed in the show. He knew it was a show for housewives, but it had spoken to him. It had shown him what he had to do. He was battling for control, but he was also battling in GTT4 and he wouldn’t stop battling until he had broken the final barrier, and overcome the final obstacle. He was not going to stop until he was GTT4 champion. So there it is. That’s why I’m here. That’s why I compete. There isn’t a material reason. It isn’t for the money, it isn’t for the fame. It’s for the power, it’s for the respect. It’s to prove what I can do. And when I win this, I’m going to win this for him. My fallen brother, my partner. Jushin. R.I.P
