I'll throw an entry in I suppose. Since I haven't written in awhile. Hope you all enjoy. (1900 words) Title: Bad Dreams _________________ Realm of Darkness Wrestling School 9:03 A.M. Calgary, AB, Canada Nov. 18/07 _________________ It's cold. A thick puff of fog pours out of Angelica's mouth; almost like a cartoon bubble without any words appearing over her head. The multi-colored, red, brown and yellow leafs have already fallen to the frost covered ground. The forecast for this day had called for sunny weather. Shows exactly what the weather man or woman for that matter, knows. Her heels click along the slippery paved sidewalk as she walks up towards the steps of an all too familiar building. Coming to an abrupt halt, Angelica raises her head and looks over the renovated old style building just outside the heart of Calgary's downtown area. Her eyes are immediately drawn to the large neon custom made sign hanging above the door. RoD School of Wrestling: Locally owned and operated. The old brick building seems to be in excellent condition and the location not near as busy as the downtown area just a few blocks away. Still, there are pedestrians continuing about their lives; each of them of them having something different on their agenda and some with the same thing as people pour into restaurants, stores and their vehicles. She seems to stand still for a seemingly endless moment, her vision adjusting to the vehicles passing by. Focused on the hustle and bustle in the streets, Angelica seems to let her consciousness slip away. Her mind has now become subject of its own limitless power. Consumed by this grief, this pain and that God awful stench. Angelica knew all too well where she was going and it was a place she wished to never dwell upon again. Yet her mind seemed to have its own objective, overcoming her conscious effort to awaken from the trance like state. She is whisked away into a world of memory, one where memory seems so much like reality. She is there again, the darkness so black no light can be seen. Then, a faint tinge of soothing yellow light peers beneath the door and wanders into the room. Angelica pulled the sheets up over her head and clenched her pillow tight; she didn't welcome the light nor the smell that soon followed. The glimmering silver-ish metal gives a reflection of deep fear in Angelica's eyes and small beads of sweat on her brow. The creaking sound of the door handle fills the soundless void. Frantic, quick gasps of breath fill Angelica's lungs as she closes her eyes and pretends she's not there. But no matter how hard she tries, the smell gets stronger as the light pours into the room when the door is opened. Wearing just a long white night gown, Angelica is just twelve years old. And yet her innocence has been taken from her by this monster, time and time again. It had come down to this last straw, this one instant. Her hand finds comfort on the wooden handle nestled under her pillow. Slowly the sheets are pulled off of her body. The cold air sends goose bumps up and down her body. The rugged, caulis fingertips of the monster begin to run down between her chest; slowing down as it gets closer and closer to the bottom of the gown. The fingers begin to pull the thin cotton gown up. With each inch upwards, Angelica just tries to close out the thought of it all happening again. She promised herself it wouldn't, that she would finally slay the demon that had caused her and her mother so much grief over the years. Her cotton covering has been pulled up to the top of her waste line. The rough touch of his fingers along her panty line is enough to make the small beads of sweat become streaming river. Instantly Angelica pulls the blade out from behind her pillow and makes one fluid, left to right swinging motion with the sharp edge just slicing cleanly through the flesh of her tormenter. His hand now inside her underwear, reverses out and begins to try to stop the crimson red substance oozing down his neck. By the time Angelica has realised what has happened, her eyes have begun to focus and she's standing outside the RoD Wrestling School. Shaking her head side to side, she tries to once again suppress the memories of that fated night. On the street beside her a red corvette pulls up and comes to a stop. The door swings open and in the cool morning breeze, Kirsten’s red hair flows freely. She is wearing a business suit with a matching skirt and the cutest red shoes. Closing the door to her car, she pushes the alarm button on her key chain. A high pitched beeping sound emits from the car and Kirsten heads towards Angelica with a smile pressed upon her gentle lips. Nearly slipping as she steps up the curb, Kirsten stretches out and Angelica reaches for her hand to help provide balance. "Careful," Angelica says as she helps Kirsten up the curb. "It's damn slippery out here today." Kirsten regains her composure and wipes her hands down the suit, straightening the wrinkles. "Thanks for the warning." Kirsten lets out a laugh as she walks past Angelica, heading for the steps to the RoD building. "I see you got the message?" "Yeah,” Angelica walks alongside Kirsten. “What’s so important that you had to wake me up at six am to have me meet you here?” Kirsten slides a small gold tinged key into the hole beneath the door knob. Turning the handle, she pulls the door and holds it open for Angelica. “Well, I got a call from a wrestling promotion just the other day and to top it off, I’m in negotiations with another promotion. Things are picking up for you Angelica, the ‘Fallen Angel’ may rise again.” Angelica brushes past Kirsten and into the embracing warmth of the building in which she had learned and blossomed as a competitor in the wrestling industry. “So what fool is interested in having me enter their company?” Angelica finally presses with a sarcastic tone. “Well Bubba McCoy of Global is very interested in your talent after the word that Amanda had left for you when she decided to finally retire.” Kirsten allows the door to close and follows closely behind Angelica. “Not to mention the negotiations with FUSE. I’ve sent them your information, I’m still waiting to hear back from them on the contract rate and compensation packages.” “Amanda always did have my best interest in mind, didn’t she?” Kirsten nods and proceeds to enter the office at the back corner of the wrestling school. “She believes what I believe…” “And that is?” Angelica asks. “That you could be the best. Better than her, better than Darek. You’ve already surpassed the accomplishments that Siefer and I have achieved. All we can manage to do with our ability in the ring is pass it on to a new generation to learn and love.” Kirsten pulls out the large leather chair from the desk and flops herself into it. Angelica mimics her mentor and takes a seat not far from Kirsten on a small wooden stool. “Angelica, you’re all I have left and I don’t mean to live through you, but I feel a deep sense of pride when you succeed.” “I understand.” Looking into Kirsten’s eyes, Angelica brushes her dark hair from her face and smiles intriguingly. “There’s something else you’re not telling me, isn’t there?” Kirsten spins the chair away from Angelica, “well, I sorta told the owners of both promotions that you’d compete in the Primetime Central Infinite Gauntlet match. You know, as an example of what you can do.” “Great.” Angelica gets up from the stool. “That’s the way to ease me back into a contract after how many months away from the ring? Send me to the lions in the PTC. Who needs enemies when they’ve got friends like you?” “Oh come on Angie, it’s not all that bad. You did well in the GTT. Why not prove to the PTC it wasn’t a one shot thing? After all, what have you got to lose?” Angelica shrugs and then smirks, “two potential contract offers?” “Ha-ha, I love your sarcasm at a serious moment like this.” Kirsten spins back and sees Angelica on her feet. “Look at it this way, you get to march back into the PTC and accomplish yet another thing the infamous Darek DeAngleo has yet to do. His photo’s are plastered across these walls; now imagine your photo’s scattered along beside them. You could be the best Angelica.” Turning away for a moment, Angelica finally responds. “Ok, I’ll do it. But not just for the money or the fame but for my parents.” “The Martell’s will always love you, no matter who you feel comfortable being or what you do for a living.” “They missed so much time with me, it’s hard to believe that I am who I think I am only because of a tumour. Who would I be if it wasn’t there, how would my life have turned out?” Kirsten reaches out for Angelica’s hand. “You’d be you. Your name may not have been Angelica as we all call you now, but you would have known your parents and that false reality you remember as your tormented past would never exist, in your mind or otherwise.” Kirsten moves Angelica back to the chair across from her. “If you’d prefer, I can call you by Kiara?” “No, Kiara was lost along with my parents, the Martell’s when I began to believe I was someone else. I don’t remember them, I don’t remember being Kiara and I know that the only real thing I remember is being tormented by that awful man.” Kirsten knew that due to Angelica’s brain tumour she had created an entire new reality for herself, one that forced her to become a warrior; a person bred to fight and eventually lead. Her disrupted past no matter how false was still real to Angelica and no matter how much she told Angelica it was just a bad dream, Angelica’s belief mad it real. “I know honey. Look, I’ve always been here for you. Since I found you that Christmas Eve. I even took legal custody of you after a lengthy hearing in the courts, I’ve treated you as if you were my own daughter and no matter how real you think your past is, it’s not. The only real thing here is my compassion for you and my faith that you will become more than I ever could. Whether you call yourself Angelica Dominique or Kiara Martell. It’s your life, your mind, you make what you believe and feel real. What do you feel Angelica?” After what seems like an eternity of silence, Angelica finally answers. “I feel like I am who I am. I am a girl with a family in you, that loves me; I feel like I may even get to know and remember my real family. But mostly I feel the need to prove to myself and the wrestling world that I was not a fluke. That I can compete with the best of the best and that one day the world will know the name, Angelica Dominique.”
