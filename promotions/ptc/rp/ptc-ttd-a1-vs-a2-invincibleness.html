Inviciblness~! RP Late night concerns Scott sat up slowly, pushing the white sheet off of his broad chest. What time was it, he wondered. Well, he knew why he had to get up, so he sat up and pulled on a pair of black mesh shorts. He stopped for a moment to try and blink the grit out of his eyes and took a deep breath. Scott did feel somewhat out of place, but then again, he had made it a habit to sleep in his Wrangler most nights. His hand drifted over to gently brush against Alyssa Sanders' bare shoulder. She stirred briefly before her breathing returned to a normal rise and fall pattern. She wouldn't let him tonight. But then, they couldn't have done some of the things they had been in a Wrangler. Not that the restrictions had stopped her before. For Scott, this whole thing had been a completely different experience. After years of fighting in the ring and being on the road, Scott had gotten into the routine of going to the ring, grappling, getting his check and getting back in his Jeep. That's the way his life had been ever since he'd gotten into the business. He never was endowed with money; he was Karina Wolfenden or Carys or even Alyssa. Scott would keep just enough to put gas in the Jeep, buy a few protein bars and send the rest of the money home to his mom and sister. Scott's bare feet shuffled on the carpet towards the bathroom. It sure beat the hell out of parking at a rest area and hitting the crapper there. Ever since his dad had died, Scott had been the caretaker for his mom and sister, simply because mom hadn't been able to finish college and his sister had just turned sixteen. Scott's big hands shoved the door opened and fumbled for the switch. The bright white light flooded the little room and left him absolutely blinded for a few seconds. Scott wasn't a terribly smart man, but he did know that hotel bathrooms never made sense. As he made his donation to the world's water supply, his head came around quickly and then he shook his head. Lights too bright for midnight bathroom trips were annoying enough, but why put mirrors like that in a hotel bathroom? Who in the world wanted to see themselves crap and piss? Perverts… Scott hit the chrome handle and smacked the switch again, trying to blink the bright spots out of his eyes when a few knocks came at the door. Scott took a deep breath. It better not be who he thought it was... Scott pulled the chain off the door and swung it open. Again, the lights from the hallway were entirely too bright, but he could tell who was standing there. "Fo shizzle, my student!" he said. Scott glanced behind him and saw Alyssa stir slightly. He ha to shut Gabe up. He didn't want to wake her up. Not again. It wasn't that she got grouchy, but... he hoped she stayed asleep. "Gabe," he said in a harsh whisper while turning back around, "what the hell do you want?" He tried his best to make his body language real clear about how not happy he was with Gabe. But then, Gabe being Gabe, of course didn't pick up on it. “Well,” the little bastard said bluntly. “I came to see if I could borrow some water. After performing the great, great sex acts with loose women that Invincible Gabe is known for, I’m afraid that The Almighty Master of Your Mom is fucking parched!” The Manbeast shushed Invincible Gabe and turned to Alyssa, who was still asleep in spite of Gabe’s usual ranting. “Why don’t you just go back to your room…where you reside alone, you deluded dick…and just go use your own water?” “Well, fuckface!” Gabe shouted again. “The tap water in my apartment looks like somebody mixed the sour tastes of Mountain Dew, Lucky Charms, and utter shit into it. Not to mention the proprietor of this establishment looks fatter than your girlfriend, so no way am I going near THAT shit.” Under any other normal circumstances, Invincible Gabe would’ve belted across his head, but now wasn’t the time for that. Scott did know that deep down inside, he was absolutely ready to beat the hell out of Gabe for insulting Alyssa again. Interestingly enough, Scott had whipped Gabe before, several times and always over different things. There never had been the problem of having a repeat beating on any issue at all. Gabe never really flinched, though, and Scott didn't know why not. Even in the recesses of Gabe's minuscule mind there had to be some form of recognition, of understanding. For the love of God, something other than that blank stare he was getting back. “Dammit, Gabe, are you listening or are you fantasizing about Delta Burke again?” was all Rampage asked. The Almighty Master Of Your Mom suddenly snapped out of the trance and looked up at him. “Hey, fuck you, fuckhole! I fucked your mom and I’ll do it aga…” it was then that Gabe laughed insanely, mid-sentence. Scott looked at him like his brain had been fried with electro-shock therapy. It was then that Gabe removed the sunglasses he was accustomed to wearing all the time and laughed. “Oh, man. I gotta tell you, Scott,” he smiled as he looked down at the glasses. He rubbed them off on his shirt. “It’s addicting, ya know? This character, Invincible Gabe, says all these things that I wouldn’t dare say in my right mind.” Scott chuckled lightly as well. It was one of their things, acting in character out of the show. Though it was sad at times, when it came to Scott’s current living situation. He and Gabriel Brown, when they met up in Pikeville, became best friends growing up. Gabriel “Invincible Gabe” Brown had the time of his life with his creation; Matthews was an entirely different story. Scott’s character, unfortunately, was hardly a character at all. The big man had dealt with much loss in his life, but was extremely fortunate to find his own clandestine catharsis through professional wrestling, not to mention this love in Alyssa Sanders. Gabriel was fortunate enough to pierce through the seemingly impregnable shell that Scott Matthews had been notorious for putting up when working with peers in the indy federations. But the past was the past. Gabriel didn’t just get up out of bed for no reason and bother him in the middle of the night and Scott knew that. So it was either one of two things bothering him: apprehension towards their upcoming match-up in the PTC Round Robin Tag Team Tournament, or something deeper. “Scott, you know we’ve been friends for a long time, dude,” Gabriel began. “I mean, we’ve had this great, unbreakable rapport with one another. Good stuff, ya know?” “Yeah,” Matthews knew when something had bothered the guy who played The Almighty Master Of Your Mom. He always had this weird glimmer of trepidation in his eyes. “What’s bothering you?” “God, I don’t even know how to say this…” With a deep, hesitant breath, Gabriel pointed at the source of his quandary… Alyssa. Curled up in the bed and deep within the proverbial Land of Nod, she had no idea of the goings-on around her. Scott turned back to Gabe, his face a mask of complete calm. "What? What about her?" he asked softly. Scott's voice was surprisingly soft and gentle. It wasn't the same low growls and gutteral roars that fans of his were used to, but instead, the soft whisper of a real human being. "C'mon, Gabe, what about her?" Gabriel took another deep breath and turned his face away slightly. Scott could tell the expression on his face. Gabriel was actually worried. "Look," he started, "I'm concerned, man. I know you drive to the shows by yourself, and that's fine. I know you usually sleep in the jeep and that's fine. Scott, those are your choices, but man..." he trailed off. "What are you afraid of, Gabe?" Scott asked. Gabriel's shuffling steps made it clear that he was incredibly nervous. For a man, in or out of character, who was never at a loss for words, he wasn't saying a thing and that bothered his old friend. "Gabe, listen to me, your girlfriends never did really get in our way, did they? I mean, Carmella? That controlling hag did her best, didn't she?" "This is different, Scott," Brown replied. "It's that, well, on the road, it's you and me. We rely on each other, we're buddies." He took a moment to rub at his blue sideburns. When his hand came away, the blue dye had flaked off. "Ain't that a hoot, man?" he said, looking at his aqua fingers. "I mean, it's great for the gimmick, but when it starts falling out..." he trailed off as Scott shook his head. "You're afraid of a phantom," Scott told him softly. "Gabe, you're afraid that she's the character she was. That manipulative little loose girl that ran around..." "No," Gabe answered. "No, I mean..." Scott had him dead to rights and he knew it. It was the hardest thing in the world to admit to his best friend what he thought of the man's first real girlfriend. "Yeah, Scott, that is it. I'm sorry, man, I really am." Scott leaned against the doorframe and took a deep breath. "Gabe, listen to me," he said holding up one hand, palm out. "Trust someone that knows, Gabe, that's not how it is…" “Listen, dude,” Gabe cut him off, but in more of a soft tone. “Okay, it’s just…it’s just that…I don’t know ANYTHING about her. You know how hard it is for me to trust anybody, especially after Carmella. I…mean…I hope you know what you’re getting into.” Scott was slightly stunned. He had no idea what to make of any of this. They’d been through it all and the new woman in his life was the thing that appeared to be driving a hard wedge into it. “I think you’re just overreacting, Gabe,” Scott bluntly stated. “I mean, aside from Carmella, I NEVER had one negative thing to say about any of your ladies. But the second I get one, it seems you’re all over her.” “YOU KNOW THAT ISN’T IT, SCOTT!” Gabriel yelled. Oh, he looked pissed and in his mind he was right about this whole thing. That was actually not a good thing. Alyssa sat up in bed, her eyes wide and the sheet clutched around her chest. Her free left hand padded at the bed next to her and a small wail escaped her lips. "Scott?" she asked, the fear coming through her voice clearly. "Scott, where are you?" "I'm at the door, Aly," he replied in a gentle tone. "Don't worry, okay? I'll be right back." He turned back to Gabe, letting out a deep sigh. "Look, Gabe, first off, this all real new to me. Having a girl like her that cares for me... it's just so new, man." Scott took a moment to put a big paw on Gabe's shoulder, while the little man smoldered at him. "Second, man, I know that's not it, but that's just what it seems like. Look, that was something real hasty to say, and I'm sorry about that." Scott relaxed a bit in his mind when he saw Gabe's angry face melt down. It was an expression you didn't see much from Gabriel Brown, that look of concern and sorrow. "Gabe, we're buddies. We're a team. We're champions together. Everything we are, we've spent years building. I'm not going to throw that away. Not now, not ever." Gabe's responding nod was enough for Scott, but what the beast couldn't know is that deep inside, Gabe was still afraid of that very thing. "Yeah, man, I know," he replied to his big buddy. "I just get freaked out sometimes, y'know?" With a smile and a nod, Scott patted Gabe on the shoulder again and slowly closed the door. His walk back to the bed was swift and when he slid in, Alyssa's arms wrapped around his neck. "Scotty," she whispered. She was the only person ever to call him anything like that, anything affectionate. "Scotty, I got scared. Someone was yelling and you weren't here and I was afraid..." she trailed off, "...I was afraid my big teddy bear had hurt someone again." Scott's big arms cradled her gently, holding her against his broad chest. Deep inside, he felt concern for this little girl, this little woman. But he also felt fear. He had meant what he said to Gabe. He prayed that they never had cause to worry.
