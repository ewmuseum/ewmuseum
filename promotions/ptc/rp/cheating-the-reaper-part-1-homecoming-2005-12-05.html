Cheating The Reaper (Part 1: Homecoming)

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 Assassination Shocks Chicago, Opens Old Wounds CHICAGO, Illinois (AP) - Early this morning in front of a low income housing complex in the south side of Chicago, two young men were found shot to death. The two men were believed to live inside the complex, and both were family men. Mark Mathis, 24 and Jerome Peterson, 26 both of Chicago were found lying in front of the complex early yesterday morning. When police arrived on the scene the most shocking feature found about the deaths was a single bullet to the head killed both me, seemingly from above. This of course was the trademark murder style of Die Erntemaschine, the sniper that plagued Chicago for years but disappeared over a year ago. "We don't know if this was the real thing or just an imitator," Officer Chad Jones said, "It's definitely very very close, if not identical to all those murders years ago." Chicago native and fortune 500 business owner Christian Zenith was tried as the killer in late 2004. While he was on trial, information surfaced that Zenith himself was a victim of the assassin. Christian was found innocent of all charges and was released, where he left control of his Chicago based business Zenith Corporation to a board of trustees and has since pursued a career in fighting. This is the first murder of this nature since the Zenith Trial in 2004. It is believe that he wasn't even in the state, on business matters when the shooting occurred. Still Police would like to contact Mr. Zenith and ask him a few questions about the shootings. If anything is known about his whereabouts, please contact the Chicago Police Department immediately. I. Homecoming Home. The saying goes that you can never go back home again, and Christian Zenith looked out his window hoping it was true. So many times he'd tried to put Chicago in the rear view mirror and never come back. There was always something calling though. He could never really escape his home, no matter how much he may want to. The skyline appeared as he traveled down the highway in the back of his black limo. There it is… sweet home Chicago. What a joke. Everyone in the town was a sheep, blindly following whatever it was they reached out for. Money, people, possessions… the entire city was filled with people fixated on their own petty desires. He even had a following before he left. A cult he never asked for or wanted, but he'd be lying if said they didn't provide their usefulness when he was trying to track down his sister a year ago. Tera, she crossed his mind once again… "She's dead." Christian said to himself under his breath as he looked out onto the Chicago skyline. "What did you say Chris?" Vivica asked alertly from the other side of the limo. Dressed in a black Hunting Grounds hooded sweatshirt and a pair of baggy black jeans, she looked comfortable but still very beautiful. She was one of those people that could make a potato sack look fashionable. Her blonde hair fell down over her teal colored eyes, which were very comforting to her fiancé who looked back at her slowly. Deep down inside they were both worried to death. As stubborn as they are, neither of them would ever admit it out loud. "Nothing." He replied, glancing back at her with his cadet blue eyes. Shielded under a pair of heavily tented black Oakley's, it was always hard to judge exactly what he was thinking or feeling. Christian wasn't exactly the type of person to wear his emotions on his sleeve. Many people back in Global Wrestling wondered if he had emotions at all. His confidence could easily be confused with a lack of caring, which most of the time was his intention. Intimidating others became much easier when they thought you didn't care what happened. They turned off the highway to make their way to Zenith Manor North. Located just outside of the city, it was the closest Christian had been to the city in nearly a year. Luckily he had the help of his housekeeper Francis to keep things in line while he was gone. The estate was huge, especially in comparison to the others built around when it was, priced at nearly 15 million dollars. The house was constructed under the designs of his father when they first came to American when Christian was a boy. His parents had big plans for their children, but surely they had no idea what the future would bring. His parents murdered in the very building in which his father built his empire, his sister missing for years and eventually found and killed out in the middle of a desert, and Christian… a billionaire but instead of making business deals he beats people up in Las Vegas. "Then what are you thinking about?" Vivica interrogated Christian, wondering what exactly had been on his mind the past couple days. He seemed more distant than usual, and she didn't even know that was possible until now. "Showdown in Tokyo… GTT5." Christian answered after a short pause, "I'd rather be in Japan right now… trust me. I just want to get to the bottom of this, talk with police… get if very clear in their little minds that it isn't me and get out of this hell hole. I'd much rather be giving Danielle Northsky a crash course in what happens when you open your mouth in defiance of me." "She won't be much trouble Christian…" Vivica said with a smile, "trust me, I didn't have any problems kicking her ass when she tried me." "Losing never crossed my mind Viv," Christian paused, "it's just a stress reliever for me. After she's laying on ground and unable to get back up again. Watching the paramedics carry her out of the arena is calming to me. The satisfaction of watching her fail takes all the stress of life away. No paperwork, no check book, no conference calls… just me in the ring hurting people because I can. It doesn't get any better than that. I can definitely see why these morons think I'm a serial killer. They just don't realize if I wanted to hurt someone, I'd make damn sure they knew who it was that crippled them. That's just the kind of man I am, I don't shoot people in the back." "I don't understand this at all," Vivica answered, "She's dead right?" "Yeah." Christian said before reassuring himself of the facts outloud, "She's dead, we both saw it. There is no way she survived that. She made a good meal for some coyotes somewhere." "Then who…" "I don't know, and honestly I do not care." Christian spoke cutting Vivica off in mid sentence, "It's not me and it's not her, end of story. As much as they may want to think it's me, I have an entire company to vouch for my innocence this time. They won't pin this on me again, I refuse to be their scapegoat this time." "I don't think they know that yet." Vivica said to Christian, pointing out her window from the back of the limo as they entered the gate to Zenith Manor. Up the drive in front of his estate waited 3 police cars with lights spinning. Christian silently shook his head in disappointment as they slowly approached their fate. Demitri Miasnikov and Jason LeMaster continued to stare silently out the back window. The whole situation was so baffling that there was not a whole lot to say either way. This was over and done with, a chapter that has since closed in their lives. While the limo came to a stop and Zenith looked outside at the 3 police officers waiting for his arrival, one phrase was clear in his mind. Those who fail to study history are doomed to repeat it. Francis the housekeeper opened the back door of the limo where Christian exited, followed by Vivica, Demitri, and Jason in that order. The police officers motioned for Francis to leave them alone with the group for a bit. Zenith watched from behind his sunglasses as Francis walked away from the scene. As for Vivica, if looks could kill there would be 3 dead men in front of the Zenith Manor right now. Imagine trying to explain that… "Why hello Mr. Zenith it certainly has been a while." The police officer reached out his hand, it was not shook by Christian as was originally intended, "Well… I am Officer Jake Townsend. I'm sure you heard the news about the murders that occurred last night. I am just here to ask you a few questions." "I'm sure you are." Christian said looking back at him as the other officers looked on with weapons in hand, "Let me assure you I wasn't even in Illinois until this morning, I was at an airport in Las Vegas heading to Tokyo for business when I heard the news. I was forced to come back so it didn't look like I was fleeing the country. I haven’t been in Chicago for nearly 6 months yet my name still comes up in all this. I'd appreciate it if you'd quit associating my name with that coward that's been shooting people around here." "But Mr. Zenith there are pretty obvious connections with yourself and…" The officer tried to respond before he was cut off. "I'm sure there is officer, but I'm an innocent man. A jury of my supposed 'peers' pointed that out nearly a year ago. As far as I'm concerned, this is your problem not mine. Perhaps if you spent a little more time looking for my sister that's been missing since I was a little kid and a little less time trying to make an innocent man out to be some kind of serial killer the public wouldn't hate you guys so much. Good luck Officer, but this is private property and I know damn well you don't have a warrant. So I suggest you get off my property before I call your superiors and have you removed. Demitri and Jason will see to it that you find the exit. Good day officer." Christian Zenith said as he walked around their cards and towards the steps of the estate. Vivica followed as Demitri and Jason kept an eye on the frustrated police officers. "Well that went well…" Vivica said sarcastically as they made their way inside of the estate. Christian Zenith looked out the window to see all three police officers reluctantly leave the property before he shut the curtains and looked back at Vivica. She looked pretty obviously worried. Who could blame her though… the demons of their past were coming back with full force. As much as they liked to think they could, there was not a thing they could do to stop it. They were just along for the ride. "They are gone, that's all I care about." Christian said as he made his way into the living room. Vivica followed him like a lost puppy dog. "Aren't you a little concerned about what happened?" Vivica questioned, "What if it's true? What if she is alive?" "She can't be alive…" Christian said aloud, "I won't let that happen." II. Outsider High above the towering skyscrapers and the noises of the city, the moon sat silently above… shining its icy glow all over the Windy City skyline. Howling winds weaved in and out of massive structures lining the streets. Sleet pounds the pavement, covering everything that can be seen in a sheet of solid ice. Icicles hang from the street lights, glistening in its light. With his face covered by a large black hood, he walked down the street wind beaten and defiant against the forces of nature. Eyeing his surroundings as he walked slowly along, remembering the many cold nights he spent on these streets. The glory and terrors of former days gone by. He'd often wondered would've happened to guys like Demitri or Jason, or even Vivica if he'd never put himself on the streets. Where would they be now, would they still be alive… would he still be alive? Right now he was more concerned about clearing his mind and his name, perhaps a visit with an old friend would do the trick. Anything to get his mind off of her. She'd left so early in his life… as days gone by he'd kept her memory in his heart. As the world around him became cold, dark, and political favoritism… she remained pure and innocent in his heart. He never thought honestly he'd ever see her face again. Everything he'd come to know and love was falling apart around him… everything was fake and underhanded, a facade to shield his childhood from the truth. She was the only thing he cared for anymore, perhaps that's why what happened felt so wrong to him. It had been almost a decade since the last time he'd seen her face but still he felt betrayed. That feeling was never going to go away it seemed. Everyday it was his job to be better and stronger than she ever was. He wanted her gone and never to be heard from ever again. New Mexico was supposed to solve that. Now this… it was more than history repeating itself. It was a reoccurring nightmare. Suddenly he stopped under a street light. He looked up at the building in front of him as the light glistened off his ice soaked nylon coat. The wind picked up and blew his hood off of his head, battering his face with ice as he looked down in front of him. It was the apartment Mika and Demitri used to live in. Also, it was the place where Vivica's entire world was shattered. He could still almost see the chalk lines on the sidewalk where James Carlisle had been killed. From out under the light he walked, grabbing the frozen steel light pole on the way making icicles crash and shatter onto the ground behind him. He looked up into the sky, seeing where he thought Die Erntemaschine would've did the damage from. Nothing stuck out in his mind as he looked around as being obvious, perhaps that's why she was so good at what she did. The Miasnikov Family still lived inside the apartment, but hadn't paid rent in nearly a year. Demitri paid for it with his salary he received from Christian as a Zenith Corporation employee. Perhaps a visit with the people he once thought would be his in-laws would be what the doctor ordered. They'd always been accepting of him, even after Mike took off with a little over a million dollars of his money. Only never to be heard from again when she was found dead and broke on some street corner. He ascended the small staircase to the entrance to the apartment. Under the balcony, he was shielded by the wind… it felt much warmer to his skin as he searched for the buzzer to apartment number C11. The numbers were nearly worn off due to the years of unpredictable and harsh Chicago weather, it was just another reminder of how much he hated this town. He pushed it in with a high pitched buzz, and waited patiently as the wind howled behind him. "Hey this is Christian Zenith, are you guys home?" Zenith spoke into the intercom, and once again waited for a response. He heard nothing and tried once more, seeing their car parked out front was a pretty good sign they were home. "You there? This is Christian Zenith." He said once again, before hearing something he certainly wasn't thrilled to hear. "Don't answer!" He heard a mumbling through the intercom, "Oh sorry…" That was the last thing he heard before the intercom shut. He didn't bother trying again. It was the damn police… telling everyone in the world he was a murderer. How could they believe such a thing, he hadn't even been in the city for months and no one even seemed to give a damn. With a scowl he put up his hood and walked off the porch, and back into the elements. Walking back towards the direction in which he came Christian looked around at his surroundings. After everything he'd done for this city… brought in jobs, donated to local charities… they just turned on him at the drop of a hate. People are ruthless… and they think he's the monster. "I wish this whole fucking town would burn to the ground." He said to himself as he continued on, watching his breath dance in the strong winds. Up ahead a group of men stood under a street light. Christian had remembered those men from his days on the streets, one of them he remember in particular named Michael Hagger. Michael was a local school kid back then, probably graduated or dropped out by now more than likely. Once in particular Vivica saved his ass when he picked a fight after school with a local gang and they pulled a box cutter on him. He remembered seeing his face in the crowd after he'd returned to the warehouse after leaving the hospital too. After Train's south side cowards had entered the warehouse and started shooting he remembered seeing Michael get smacked in the back of the head by one of their guns. Jason was closest to him, and actually left a card for him in the hospital while recovering from the concussion he'd received that night. As Christian approached the group spotted him and began to walk into the alley. Christian sped up his walk to catch up with them… surely they'd know he wasn't a murderer. One of them even attended his crusade against Die Erntemaschine. Michael knew the truth… he knew Zenith had been shot by the real killer. Turning the corner into the dark alley he could see Michael and his friends up ahead. Michael looked back and saw Zenith closing in and began to speed up it seemed. "Hey Michael!" Christian shouted as reached out for his shoulder. Michael Hagger stopped when he felt the tug on his shoulder, the rest of the group sprinted away as fast as they could. "Let me go!" Michael said, shrugging away from Christian before turning around and looking at him, "I don't associate with cowards." "Coming from the kid who had to have a girl save him from a public school gang." Christian's cadet blue eyes narrowed as he looked at Michael. He could sense the fear his voice when he told him to let him go. Something was very wrong. "You lied to us all." Michael said, "You told us all that you were shot and we all went on this huge hunt because you asked us to! We thought you were great, we worshipped you like a God. You were a legend in our eyes. Then you went up and left us, and didn't come back until now. You act like you're my fucking friend or something… you're nothing but a coward, you fucking murderer. I hope they give you the chair you lying bastard." Zenith stared cold and emotionless as Michael ran out of the alley and into the streets. Christian walked out slowly to get a glimpse of the little bastard, he didn't know the truth. What's happened to him? What's happened to this town? What in the world has happened to these people? They believed every word that came out of the media's mouth. Nothing but a bunch of fucking sheep. Just like he remembered. Even if it wasn't her, he'd prove everyone in this dump wrong. He may have killed someone before but he's not Die Erntemaschine. So much for plans to leave in the morning… the police wouldn't let him out of the country until this was over with. Suddenly a gun shot echoed through the mighty winds. Leaving Christian to watch helplessly as Michael fell limply to the ground around two blocks in front of him. TO BE CONTINUED (Hopefully)…
