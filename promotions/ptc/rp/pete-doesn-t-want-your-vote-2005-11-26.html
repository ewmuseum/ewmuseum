Pete Doesn't Want Your Vote.

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-26 I woke up at 8AM, Sunday morning to the telephone ringing. This is not a usual occurrence in my household, which means that someone’s either dead, or someone screwed up. Unfortunately, I was the one that screwed up. My agent told me a few weeks ago, that a place called Primetime Central was looking to aquire my services, and that they were holding a Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. He said something about threads on boards, and being a seamstress, my curiousity was piqued. I entered, hoping that they would select me, based on the fine work I did in PCW, making some of the greatest costumes known to man. What I didn’t understand, was that this is a wrestling tournament, not a competition to sew the best costume. I guess there’s this thing called the … interweb? Intrajet? Something. Whatever. Anyways, there’s this thing where like, people on computers talk to each other, and on that, there’s this thing called a message board. Message boards have threads, which is kind of neat, but, not a thread I’m really into working with. Lo and behold, I was signed up to wrestle again. I was crushed, and instantly tried to find a loophole to get out of it. There was no way in hell I was going to wrestle again, and I would be damned if anyone brought me out of retirement. Aside from a strange progression in some damned fantasy realm that I don’t even remember against some walking skeleton, I’ve never won a wrestling match, and I’m not about to start now. I’m 43 years old, I’m out of shape, I’m balding, and I’m not exactly the kind of guy you want to pin a championship on, and ride his coattails to success. I’m liable to have a heart attack, or break my hand sometime during the tournament. So, through my internet savy friend Jeremy, I’m writing this letter to the attention of the judges. I’d like to ask each and every one of you to vote for Spartan. Quite frankly, he wants it more than I do, and I probably won’t even show up. Seriously. I’m not coming. At all. Ever. Don’t vote for me. I don’t want to win. Seriously. Don’t. All of you are whores, and should get screwed with a rusty dildo, so that you get lock ass. I think you’re all on the take, and that you’re only going to vote for the people that pay you the most. Well, I’m broke. I’m not going to pay you a thing. I don’t want to pay you anything, because you’re all whores. Is that enough to make you jerks not vote for me? It isn’t? Well, then I screwed all your mothers. Twice. And yes, I am all your fathers. So, listen to your daddy, and don’t vote for Pete!
