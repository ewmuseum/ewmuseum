Things Change

15 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-15 I used to be a valued member of the WWA roster. That was until a few months ago, when things started going downhill. My name is Dave Harley, and until last week I was a member of the Extreme Corporation. Of course, you’ll remember X Corp as the fun-loving guys who challenged HomoPhobic for the PTC Tag Team Titles. But things have changed since that humiliating defeat. For two years, Mal and I were the best of friends. We played Scrabble, we drove around in the X Mobile, we won the WWA Tag Team Titles and we had a lot of fun. But things change. Things always change. I think back to my first ever tag team partner; his name was Jonny Carnage. I trained in his family’s gym in Detroit. I moved down there from my Chicago home and we hit it off straight away. My first ever professional match saw me and Jonny team up. For the next few years, we were almost unstoppable. We were on the road together for months at a time. We travelled the world and won title after title after title. But when we returned to America, things were different. The American fans had made a hero out of Jonny. And me? I was just the sidekick. People paid money to see Jony Carnage. They started buying Carnage T Shirts rather than Harley & Carnage T Shirts. I sat back and watched as Jonny moved higher and higher up that card. I watched as he took the spot that should have belonged to me. So, I decided to do something about it. At a house show event in his hometown of Detroit, I kicked him in the gut, locked his head between my legs, lifted him up and with all my mighty wrath I Harley Bombed that Son of a B*tch straight to Hell. That was five years ago. He’s not wrestled since. In fact, he hasn’t walked since either. I was hired by Phil Flaws’ Euromerican Wrestling Alliance (where I met Mal) and was content to step in to the spotlight that the inbred public thought belonged to Jonny Carnage. I promised myself that I’d never get stuck in another lousy tag team situation ever again. But Mal changed all that. For a while, at least. You see, a few months ago, I lost a match to Ian Tense. As per the stipulation, I was forcibly removed from X Corp and became a reluctant member of the Devil’s Own – the most ruthless and sadistic group in the WWA today. Whilst I struggled to free myself from their service, I watched X Corp move on as if they’d forgotten all about me. I watched as Mal and Chance scouted out a new member of X Corp, James Curzon – my replacement. The worst was yet to come. At Independence Day Rumble, the WWA World Title was held up and a League created that collected the six top contenders. As a former Tag Team Champion, GenX Champion, TV Champion and Intercontinental Champion, I should have been amongst them. But I was overlooked. The WWA chose to place Mal Somers amongst the World Title League Contenders. And at Labour Day Brawl two weeks ago, Mal became the new WWA World Champion. Once again, Dave Harley was screwed. And once again, I took matters in to my own hands. Last week, I took control of Devil’s Own. Seconds later, I left Mal Somers laying in a pool of his own blood. Let this be a lesson to PTC. Let this be a lesson to Black Cherry. I will no longer stand by and be second best. I will not allow anyone to get the better of me. I will strike down anyone who stands in my way with the same furious vengeance that befell Jonny Carnage and Mal Somers. I’m no longer the Co-Founder of a bunch of clowns, I’m the Owner of an army. I sat back and listened to Black Cherry’s ramblings. Pathetic. She has no idea hat I’m capable of. She has no idea how far I’m willing to go to get what I want. This time, I will walk in to PTC without any heat-stealing tag team partners. This time, I’ll walk out dominant. A few months ago, I would never have wrestled against a woman. But now, Black Cherry is just cannon fodder for the Extreme Machine. She’s the first in a long line of victims. And once I’ve chewed the rest of Primetime Central up and spat them out, I’ll turn my attention back to the worthless peons in the WWA locker room. I used to be a valued member of the WWA roster. That was until last week, when I ripped the spotlight from the World Champion. My name is Dave Harley. I was a member of the Extreme Corporation. Now, I seek my vengeance.
