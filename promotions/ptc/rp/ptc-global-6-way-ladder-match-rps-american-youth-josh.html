22 April 2004 The Analysis. My eyelids were squeezed together tightly, blocking out the light as I concentrated hard on the task at hand. My legs pumped viscously as nearly every vein bulged between the pair of black Nike gym shorts and the silver and black Nike Air Metal Max VI trainers I wore. Running had always been something I found great pleasure in even after turning down a college track scholarship at Louisiana Tech, one of the nations top programs nonetheless. But I must obey the inscrutable exhortations of my soul and they had led me to pursue my current career path, a path that seemed to be strengthened by my prior athletic exploits. Running improved my cardio by vast leaps and bounds and I had become world renowned for my work rate inside the Wrestling ring. My training as a martial artist had also prepared me for the world of Professional Wrestling. The ups and downs of winning and losing in Taekwondo tournaments had humbled me at times and uplifted me in other times. I had been prepared for everything, sometimes it almost seemed as if destiny had paved the path that I stumbled blindly along, oblivious to all guidance and depending upon my own twisted set of principles to survive amongst the sick world we live in. As these thoughts introspectively ran through my scheming mind, I was forced to ponder what strategy I would choose to employ in my upcoming Global Title match. I would wage war with five other worthy competitors. Competitors that had long been uplifted and smiled upon. Entering this contest I was seen as a dark horse, an underdog, a wild card. The role of underdog was no unfamiliar place to me. I had been the underdog countless times in my career. Entering a ring at a pound north of the two hundred mark and standing two inches shy of six feet in height I had never exactly been seen as an intimidating presence. But intimidation didn't factor into my game plan. I had never been one to scare my opponent and send him running away in cowardice with his tail tucked firmly between his legs. Instead I was the man looked down upon. But I had to admit that my reluctant acceptance of that role had done wonders for my career…after all it's hard to look down upon someone when their standing over you with their arm raised. My entire career I had been determined to prove that it wasn't about size, strength or intimidation…but rather about strategy and intelligence. Two attributes which I had strong reason to believe that I towered significantly over my competitors in. My brain was working almost as fast as my legs; my feet slamming down loudly on the treadmill as I opened my eyes to glance down at the distance I had ran. Staring back up at me the digital black numbers read "6.5" which, of course meant I had traveled six and a half miles. Snapping up a white Nike sports bottle with my right hand I squeezed it and shot a stream of water into my mouth. The cool liquid splashed across my teeth and dry tongue as it gushed down my throat briefly quenching my thirst. I squeezed the bottle again this time targeting the heavy drops of sweat that were running down my bare chest and soaking into the elastic waistband of my shorts. The icy water blasted my torso cooling me down, as I was soaked completely with sweat and boiling from what had already been a strenuous workout. Gazing back down to the digital numerals across the small display screen I now read "6.6". With a smile of satisfaction I placed the sports bottle back into its container on the treadmill and slowly began to turn the treadmill down. The speed dropped greatly. Just a bit at first…then a bit more…a bit more…before finally coming to a jog, a walk and then a complete stop. "Perfect." I said, to no one in particular as I assumed I was alone in the empty gym. I was wrong however as a voice interrupted me shattering my illusion of solitude. "What was perfect?" Said the voice belonging to Gory Pescado, Global Wrestling's photographer as he lifted a camera to his face and took a quick picture of Global Wrestling's number one contender to the World title stepping off of the treadmill. "My run was perfect…not that it's any of your business." I snapped at him a bit incensed by this blatantly disrespectful invasion of privacy. "It's good to see you're so full of enthusiasm." He simply responded as he let the camera rest on his plain white T-shirt where it hung from a neck strap. "Why wouldn't I be?" I asked with a raised eyebrow of both ire and curiosity. "Word around the office is that you're freaked out about your big matches that are coming up." He nonchalantly stated as he shoved his hands into the pockets of his jean shorts. I was a bit surprised at the revelation of this previously unheard rumor that I had been oblivious to. The rumor was legitimate…and very intriguing. I was a bit nervous about my upcoming Global title match and more importantly my Global Wrestling World title match. Two huge matches in the space of two weeks is something that would terrorize any human. But it wasn't something that I had spoken of to anyone. Then again…gossip will be gossip. The rumors that circulated could do nothing to harm me or throw off my game plan for I would simply ignore them and press on against the current as I always had. "Well the next time my name pops up around the water cooler you tell whomever is spreading this little rumor that I'm going to kick their ass." I sneered at the intruder to my private workout. Those words seem to catch him off guard as he quickly decided to make his next words count because he knew if I became further perturbed they could very well be his last. "Anywho dude…the reason I came down here is to let you know that Tachikara wants to see you." He told me trying to keep his cool as he stood there the victim of my withering glare. The "Tachikara" he spoke of was of course Global Wrestling's resident trainer, K.J. Tachikara, a legend of Japan and powerful force in the development of Global Wrestling's future stars. "He wants to see me now?" I demanded with a look of anger beginning to spread across my features. "Yeah man, he wants to see you like right now…a.s.a.p. He told me." Said Gory as the little pest began to back away from me. "Fine, tell him I'll be up in five minutes." I told him in an angry tone as sparks shot from my bluish gray eyes. Gory rubbed his hands over his buzzed off black hair as he responded. "Right dude, I'm on my way to tell him then." And with those words being spoken the local pest/photographer/errand boy took off like a bat out of hell seemingly a bit skittish as he saw my anger beginning to build. Snatching up the bottle of water I shot a stream of it into my shaggy black hair and scattered my locks about with my free hand. I took another drink from the thirst quenching liquid as I began to negotiate my way through the dead forest of training equipment that littered the floor of the Global Arena gym. Stepping around what seemed to be and endless line of weight machines I finally spotted my black gym bag lying carelessly on the concrete floor of this basement gymnasium. I quickly unzipped the bag and tugged out a white towel as I wiped the sweat from my face, torso and legs. I dropped the towel and sat down the bottle of water before reaching into the bag where I procured a sleeveless white shirt with the ingenious phrase "drunk chicks love me", written across it in small lower case black letters. Pulling the shirt over my head I shoved my arms through the sleeveless openings in the side. With my shirt on I zipped the gym bag shut and left it there, scooping up my water bottle as I made my way across the concrete floor of the gym. Turning toward the nearest elevator I spotted the big metal doors and walked the required twenty feet to reach my destination. With a simple push of the button from my unoccupied left hand the elevator doors opened quickly and I stepped inside standing upon the jade colored carpet. The doors of the powerful machine closed behind me as I scanned the set of buttons. Unlike most elevators the buttons weren't numbered but rather named. I chose the one that read "Staff offices" and hit that. The elevator hummed to life as I felt it ascend quickly. I took another drink of water and before I knew it I had arrived at the "business district" of the Global Arena. The heavy doors opened before me once again and I stepped out onto carpet matching that of the elevator. The heavy metal doors slammed behind me as my eyes began to soak everything in. The gray walls complimented the soft carpet perfectly as they opened into a wide room. The room was a referral office with a mish-mesh of desks, filing cabinets and computers. Walking across the carpet I stared straight ahead at a hallway behind the referral office. My legs stretched the distance towards that yawning hallway but a female voice quickly distracted me. "Josh, Tachikara needs to talk to you." Said a pretty brunette receptionist in her late twenties who I knew simply as Kelly. "Right Kel, that's where I'm headed." I replied, already annoyed by the persistent badgering of the Global Arena employees. Stepping into the hallway I walked past a variety of rooms, offices of Global Wrestling reporters and the editor of Global Wrestling's monthly magazine, the office of the spokesperson, president of talent relations, producer, and even the staff lounge. Finally at the end of the hall I came to a closed door marked "K.J. Tachikara. GW trainer". Turning the knob I opened the door without knocking and stepped inside. "K.J.?" I inquisitively asked as he sat at his desk holding a piece of paper in his hands. Sitting in his leather chair and dressed in a gray Armani suit complimented by a black silk Versace shirt with no tie and his Gucci shoes propped up on his desk K.J. looked to be a perfectly painted portrait of importance. He tugged thoughtfully at his black goatee, which matched what was left of his quickly departing hair. Looking up at me he removed his feet from the desk and returned them to the floor as I dropped comfortably onto the black leather couch opposite of his desk. Sitting straight he slapped the paper down on his desk before acknowledging me. "Josh, you know that I generally only help those that come to me and ask for assistance…but this is an exception." He told me as he slid the piece of paper across the desk. Snatching the paper from his desk my eyes danced across it as I examined it thoroughly. Upon this paper the PTC Global title was placed at the top and below it six names and faces. Representing Global Wrestling, Jesse Jamester. PTC Global Champion. My eyes soaked in the picture of Jesse as I thought hard about our prior encounter in which I had defeated him the night after he captured his Global title. Next to his picture I saw a sight that was far from being beautiful…the man known as Hessian. Representing RPW, Hessian. I knew next to nothing about Hessian. I had seen him Wrestle no more than ten times on tape, I had never actually seen him live and in person but his size was quite impressive and he would enter the Global Title match as the largest competitor in the squared circle. Below the picture of Hessian was the mug shot of the man I had defeated less than a month ago…Wolfson. Representing XUW, Blake Wolfson. Wolfson was an amazing talent; even I had to admit that. As much as I disliked his attitude I did admire his ability inside the ring, he was a technical wizard with the ability to pick apart and destroy a body part and I do mean any body part he so desired. Next to the picture of Wolfson was a man that seemed to elude me, I had seen him Wrestle live the night I battled Blake Wolfson on a 4WW Pay-Per-View. That night was when Patti Anderson had plucked his North wind title from his grasp. That man would be…Todd Dream. Representing 4WW, Todd Dream. I had to admire his skill inside the ring despite how little I knew about his tendencies. I vowed then and there to learn all that I could in an effort to overthrow him and become PTC Global Champion. My eyes continued to scan the page as I spotted another man of whom I had limited knowledge…Iron Lung. Representing UWF, Iron Lung. Perhaps my knowledge of him was limited but I had managed to catch him Wrestling on TV a few times. Of course…that was in LOW, I was unfamiliar with his more recent work. Yet to win I knew that I must see what he had done lately and judge him on those actions rather than actions of the past. Finally I saw the last picture, the sixth man…"American Youth" Josh. In the picture I bore a look of defiance, a look of anger, perhaps even the look of a madman. I scrutinized the picture heavily before K.J. snapped me away from my observance. "It's time to take action…its time to rise up against your enemies…it's time to break away from the shadows that have long hid your talent." K.J. told me in an inspirational voice. "Save the dramatic speeches K, I know I've got work to do and that's exactly what I was doing before you sent that moron photographer looking for me." I told him in an impatient tone. Standing to his feet K.J. slapped the palms of his hands down hard on his desk as he spoke loudly. "No!" He shouted at me. "The kind of training you speak of and that which I speak of is entirely different." He said, as emotion in his voice grew stronger. I arched my eyebrows in surprise at this fifty-two year old Japanese legend yelling at me about my training habits. "You train the body…yet you are clearly the most talented competitor going into the match. You are known as being fearless for your innovative aerial maneuvers and your technical abilities are famous amongst our Wrestling circuit. Your martial arts background and suicidal antics combined with your passion for this industry fuel a strong man. Yet as physically demanding as this match may be…it isn't the body you should train, but rather your strongest weapon." He said in a soft tone, his Japanese accent pronouncing every word in perfect English. Leaning across his desk his cold brown eyes stared into me. "You must hone your sharpest weapon to a razors edge." He said softly as he turned his eyes to a stack of videos resting on his desk. "One mans mind…can easily conquer the strength of five others. You must strategize, plot, plan and scheme. For this isn’t a battle of brawn…but rather a war of the minds." He ended his speech as he picked up a video bearing the initials "RPW" across the cover. Turning toward the Television that graced the edge of his room he quickly inserted the tape into the VCR. As Tachikara was set to pump me full of the knowledge that I would desperately need my mind began to contemplate all that he had spoken. This would indeed be an intellectual collision…and I planned to come out unscathed. The Global Title loomed on the horizon and all it took was one match…one moment…to fulfill the dreams of one lifetime. Yet the only one that could determine my fate was I. For win or lose…I would be the man to decide what happened to me. My fate was in my own hands and everything rested upon my strategy. I would strategize…I would devise a plan to achieve victory and then…the world would finally see me on the horizon. ---- 26 April 2004 The art of war. "You've brought more stuff for me to analyze?" I said with a raised eyebrow as Global Wrestling trainer K.J. Tachikara came bounding into my hotel room a spring in his black flip flops as he carried a briefcase in his right hand, which contrasted with his casual attire of Khaki shorts and a white linen button up shirt. He dropped into the couch beside me without so much as uttering a word in response and he slammed the black briefcase down on the table that sat before the hunter green sofa. "How much more of this crap do I have to look at?" I growled, my voice taking on a very unusual tone as I made sure my complaint had been heard. "Enough for you to win." K.J. grumbled at me with a steely resolve in his voice. I found it strikingly odd that he was this aggressive about my victory. In a strange twist of fate he seemed to be more obsessed with picking up the W than I was. I was about to mumble under my breath how irritating this had became yet I held my peace in gratitude as I tugged at my shaggy black mane in frustration. Dropping my hands palm down onto my tattered Ralph Lauren jeans I stretched out my feet clad in black Converse Chuck Taylor's and waited as K.J. procured a variety of papers from his black briefcase. Tugging out a clear binder that looked quite professional K.J. dropped it into my lap. "What's this?" I mumbled, too lazy to read it. "Your superior." He said with a sneer as I glanced down at the binder in which a photograph of Hessian and several papers resided. Opening the binder I glanced away from the ugly mug of Hessian before thumbing on to the next page. "Hessian." Real name: Angelus Von Kelsig. Height: Seven feet two inches. Weight: Three hundred and fifteen pounds. Representing: Rush Pro Wrestling. Career highlights: Two time OSW tag Team champion, Two time OSW Hardcore champion, Two time NTICW U.S. Champion, Global Title Tournament 2 semi-finalist. My bluish gray eyes danced down the paper to his history below the vital statistics. Staring at a long row of lines I twisted my lips in an annoyed state as I shut the folder and dropped it onto the green and gray carpeted floor. "What the hell did you mean by my superior, K?" I asked Global Wrestling's resident trainer as he continued to rifle through the contents of his briefcase. "Superior…it's simple, Josh. He's the favorite in this match, everyone has picked him to win, so until you prove otherwise…he's your superior." K.J. stated in a brutally honest way. "Dude, wait just a freakin' second, this guy is nothing but a bunch of hype. I mean look at his career highlights…sure he was big in OSW but where is OSW at these days? It's dead…just like his chances of winning that Global title." I shot at K.J. in a brash tone attempting to counteract his claim of Hessian being my superior. "Fine…" K.J. said with a dramatic pause before continuing on. "If he's such a pushover…explain to me how you're going to lift him? The guy is three hundred and fifteen pounds. He's massive, how are you going to take him off of his feet?" K.J. said as he fired back in retaliation. "I hadn't thought of that…I mean…it's a ladder match and aside from Hessian and I there are four other individuals that will partake in this match…let one of them lift him." I said selecting my words carefully as I began to slowly rethink my strategy. My mind was running in overdrive as K.J. Tachikara slapped another clear folder down on my lap this one bearing the picture of fellow Global Wrestling employee Jesse Jamester. "Oh please, don't tell me…you think I should do my homework on this idiot?" I said with an arrogant smile across my face. "I've already beat him once!" I proclaimed slapping my chest in pride and feeling my hand hit the soft cotton of my black Tank top. "That you did." K.J. admitted before continuing on. "Yet you heard what he said merely two weeks after that victory. He claimed that you got lucky…he claimed that he was tired from winning the PTC Global title. He gave you no respect whatsoever, so maybe you should do your homework…maybe you should prove him wrong." K.J. said sparking something within me as I flipped open the folder. "Jesse Jamester" Real name: Jesse Fristik. Height: Six feet and six inches. Weight: Two hundred and sixty six pounds. Representing: Global Wrestling. Career highlights: PTC Global Champion, GW International champion, GW PrimeStar champion, LOW Xtreme Champion, LOW Tag Team Champion, BDW Hardcore Champion. "Okay…his resume is a bit more padded than Hessian's." I blankly stated with a thoughtful expression dancing across my face. "And let's not forget he has something that Hessian does not possess…the PTC Global title." K.J. said trying to further spark my internal desire for victory. I took in this thought with the knowledge that I had defeated Jesse Jamester in his first match after becoming the PTC Global Champion. So perhaps in a way…I was the de facto number one contender to his precious championship and I knew that I could best him in a one on one situation, however, adding four other participants made that questionable. One time World Champion, One time IC Champion and Three time Tag Champion. He's the underdog…no one is expecting big things from him." K.J. thoughtfully said as he scrutinized the situation thoroughly. "Their fools to overlook him…I know the critics are overlooking him but do you think the other competitors are overlooking him as well?" I inquired, tapping into the great knowledge of the GW trainer. "I think everyone is overlooking him…everyone but you, and if you count him out as well that could in essence serve as the key to his victory." K.J. told me as he stroked the black goatee that decorated his weathered face. I didn't respond to KJ's comment as I simply nodded and dropped the Wolfson file onto the floor before extracting another file from the briefcase. I glanced at the file, which bore Iron Lung's name and photograph. My eyes traveled across it as I flipped the binder open and diligently searched the first page. "Iron Lung" Real name: Robert Alexander Pendragon. Height: Five feet eleven inches. Weight: One hundred and forty pounds. Representing: Lights Out Wrestling. Career highlights: Aftermath Champion, Xtreme Champion. "So…Iron Lung, is really that small?" I asked a bit surprised to see the weight he was listed at. "One hundred and forty pounds…only small physically because this guy has heart and lots of it." K.J pointed out to me. "Right, I just always thought he was bigger…like around one sixty or one seventy." K.J. warned me as I simply nodded in recognition of what the Global Wrestling trainer had said. Taking a final glance at Iron Lung's biography I dropped the binder to the floor. Reaching back into the black briefcase that set before me I removed the last folder within and stared down at the face of Todd Dream as his photograph returned my glare with eyes hidden behind a pair of shades. Flipping the folder open I quickly soaked in the contents. "Todd Dream." Height: Six feet, four inches. Weight: Two hundred and fifty five pounds. Representing: 4 Winds Wrestling. Career highlights: 4WW North Winds Champion. Todd Dream had been the franchise player of 4WW before falling to the famed Patti Anderson in a match refereed by a longtime idol of mine, Jay Phoenix. Unsure of what exactly to make of Todd Dream I turned to K.J. and slowly asked what he thought of Dream. "K, what's your take on Dream?" I said the words coming painfully as it wounded my pride to ask for help. "A great competitor…" He stated before pausing momentarily. "And a man that could render you defenseless for your lack of insight. Study him…observe him…for you must know the way he will react when you go to blows inside the ring." Once again the words of trainer K.J. Tachikara had struck an impression within me and I began to think. My mind raced back to something that Sun Tzu had written in his infamous book, the art of war. "One who knows the enemy and knows himself will not be in danger in a hundred battles." I knew myself very well…I was Global Wrestling's next World Champion, I was known throughout the Wrestling industry as a young man on the verge of breaking barriers and shooting into the atmosphere to unparalleled heights of success. But did I know Hessian? I am forced to reluctantly admit that I don't know the man they call Hessian…I simply know the myth. Did I know Jesse Jamester? Jamester was a different story entirely, after studying endless hours of the Global Champion inside the Wrestling ring I knew him perfectly…I knew him better than he knew himself. But those were merely two of the competitors…did I know Blake Wolfson? That I did…I had defeated him once already in a match shown on 4WW programming. But there were two other competitors of great ability that I knew only sparingly. "One who does not know the enemy but knows himself will sometimes win, sometimes lose." That's the category the last two seemed to fall into, for my knowledge of their ability was sparse. I had seen Todd Dream perform live…yet nothing more than that. Iron Lung however was someone I hadn't seen perform in the squared circle. He along with Hessian I had yet to see prove their abilities in person. But I must know them well to garner a much-desired victory. "One who does not know the enemy and does not know himself will be in danger in every battle." The final statement was one that seemed to help ease the burden from my shoulders, yet at the same time it resurrected a new fear… I knew my enemies, my range of knowledge on them ranged but all in all I knew each one of them in some way, shape or form. I yelled in defense. "Agreed." K.J. said with a nod before going on to further deflate my ego. "But in that match with five other competitors don't you think there will always be one that's available to grab your foot?" I met his words with stunned silence before finally and reluctantly realizing it would be best to listen to him. "Touché." I said with a nod. "Now that you've made your point what's this grand scheme you've concocted?" "Ok, how do you win a ladder match?" He asked in an excited voice. "By pulling the title off of the ring." I said dryly covering my mouth as I yawned in mock boredom. Rolling his eyes K.J. began to explain his idea. "Balance. It's all about balance. You may be a far more talented competitor than everyone in that match…you may be in better shape but it's going to be about balance." "Balance?" I asked with a raised eyebrow. "Balance." He responded with a determined look in his watery brown eyes. At that moment a man whom I presumed to be the aforementioned Pietro burst onto the scene as he stepped into my peripheral vision decked out in blue circus tights and a tank top to match. My suspicion of his identity was confirmed as Willard who had been standing silently spoke up. "Pietro!" He excitedly exclaimed before continuing on in a voice that was already beginning to hurt my ears. "I need your help…or more specifically these gentlemen need your help." Pietro glanced over at us and simply nodded in response to Willard's commands before turning to a large ladder and making a motion with his right hand for me to ascend the ladder. I raised a wary eyebrow as I looked up at the high wire that stretched high above before looking back at K.J. "Wait a minute…no. You've got to be kidding me K." I said with anger flashing in my eyes. "What…are you afraid?" K.J. said countering my glare with a mischievous smile. He knew he had struck a nerve…he knew if there was one thing that would force me to do something it was being told that I either couldn't do it or that I was afraid to do it. He was about to find out that there was a reason I was called the man without fear. I let out a barely audible growl as I jerked the black sleeveless AC/DC T-shirt off of my body and reached out for the ladder grasping it firmly. I pulled myself up the ladder quickly at the pace of an experienced climber. Once at the top I found myself on a small platform looking down at the ground fifty feet below. Great…what had I gotten myself into? Scowling uncomfortably I was happy to know that I at least had the safety net to break my fall…until I looked down and noticed Pietro lowering the safety net. "What the fu..?" I began before stopping and glaring down at K.J. Once again K only responded with a smile as I cautiously took my first step out onto the high wire leading with my right foot my left still behind me and planted firmly on the platform. In a slow motion I brought my left forward and placed it in front of my right. Taking another step with my right I began to relax. A step with my left and I broke out into a smile…this was actually kind of fun. "Way to go champ!" K.J. yelled with encouragement as he clapped his hands. A step with my right brought me further toward the middle before I took another step with my left. Looking down I was hesitant but slowly I lifted my leg and took a step with my right. My right foot touched the wire and suddenly something went wrong…I slipped! My body jerked trying to keep itself upright but it was a lost cause as I rocketed toward the dirt below! However what seemed like a fall to my death was barely six feet as on my plunge to certain death I managed to snare the wire with my right hand mere seconds from plunging to my untimely demise! My right hand clutched the wire tightly as I hung suspended over the hard packed ground below. It was at that very moment I heard K.J's voice dryly break the silence under the big top. "…If you do that in your match you may as well forget about becoming the next PTC Global Champion." I groaned loudly as I closed my eyes…today was going to be a long day, but I knew that if I became the Global Champion…it would all be worth it.
