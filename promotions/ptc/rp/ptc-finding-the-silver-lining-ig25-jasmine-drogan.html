Finding The Silver Lining - IG25 - Jasmine Drogan

8 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You're not going to believe my luck!” The exclamation was said before the door even fully opened. A thin, athletic woman frolicked through the door, a purple duffel bag swinging from her left hand. Her clothes were slightly less than formal; a pair of purple work out shorts and a loose fitting gray t-shirt hung from her body. She playfully spun, closing the door behind her. “Jasmine, can you please take your happy pills sometime when I don't have to study for an exam?” She looked up from a pile of paperwork, a rather large book laying open on the miniature coffee table in the center of the room. “Kill joy. Why can't you take a minute from your school work to hear about my big news?” Jasmine's slightly over exaggerated cheer toned down a bit, as she plopped down on the love seat across from her obviously overworked roommate. “I'm soooooo sorry. Let me drop everything and pay attention to the dropout.” She slams her book closed sarcastically, while leaning forward with her head being held up by her arms, which are both leaning on the table as she stares intently at Jasmine. “You're so funny, Becca. Now, seriously, you want to hear what's going on?” Rebecca finally gives in and opens her ears to Jasmine. “Fine, I'm all ears, tell me what's going on.” Jasmine grinned, finally able to share her glory with her roommate. "Ok, well, I have the chance of a lifetime." She paused letting that statement linger in the air for a few moments. "This week, I can enter a free-for-all contest to get into that big tournament I was telling you about. All I have to do is survive against a bunch of people that are on my same level!" Rebecca just stared at her for a moment, looking half uncaring, half shocked at the news. "So, your big news that you had to interrupt my work for is that while I sit here and study to be a massage therapist, you're jumping around in a ring with men?" Jasmine chuckled, brushing off the slight sarcasm in Rebecca's voice. "Look on the bright side, I can go out there, and make a name for myself, and then I can come back here and you can take care of all my nagging injuries." Jasmine smiled, trying to get her workhorse roommate to see the bright side, and cheer up a bit. "Fine, I hope you win, you really deserve it. Oh, if you're going to be in a big match, shouldn't you have some catchy name? I mean, as nice as it is, Jasmine Drogan doesn't exactly strike fear into big, sweaty men's hearts." Rebecca smiled for the first time since Jasmine came home. She was slowly starting to warm up to her new roommate, and decided to take this opportunity to try and strengthen their friendship. "True, give me some suggestions. Maybe something like 'the Silver Streak' you know, to play off my 'unique' look." Jasmine pulled the hair tie from her hair, letting it fall to her shoulders. Her black hair was streaked with purple and silver highlights, which caused the black to look even darker. "Wait, I got it. The perfect name, to play off the look, and your overly optimistic view on life. Ready, Jasmine 'The Silver Lining' Drogan." Both of them started to nod their heads in unison, as the name ran through their heads. Jasmine smiled as she accepted her friend's idea. "The Silver Lining. It's perfect. Thanks a lot Becca." Rebecca looked at Jasmine smile, and couldn't help but smile back as she started to pick her studies back up. "Notta problem, just trying to help. Knock 'em dead, Jasmi- I mean Silver Lining." Jasmine laughed as she head towards her room to unwind. She stopped at her door, and turned her head back towards Rebecca slightly as she spoke. "I knew you'd come around if I just got you out of your crabby mood. Oh, and thanks a lot not just for the name, but for all the help, Becca." As she closed her door, Rebecca just looked up and smiled. "You deserve it hun." She said as she went back to her studies.
