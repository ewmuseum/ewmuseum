Piece By Piece: Part One

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 On the surface, it was all perfect. Seymour Almasy was the PTC Global Champion again. He had a beautiful Asian companion who he had sex with on a nightly basis. But something wasn’t right. Seymour wasn’t happy. In the afterglow of absolutely incredible sex, Seymour Almasy wasn’t happy. If a man can’t be happy after getting his rocks off, then when CAN he be happy? And with GTT4 looming on the horizon, it was clear to the Final Fantasy that all was not right. Considering that everyone in the tournament wanted to be the man to knock him out, the Global Champion couldn’t afford to have even the slightest piece of the puzzle of his existence out of place. It was time for another quest. But this time, the quest would not be for a title. This time, it would be for himself. ----- “That was wonderful, Seymour.” Aya, Seymour’s Japanese companion, lay in bed, a thin sheet wrapped around her naked body. She was panting hard, her breathing slowly returning to normal. Almasy lay underneath a blanket next to her, but the clothes strewn all over the room told the tale of the evening’s celebration. Seymour Almasy: Yes, Aya. Wonderful. The words were said somewhat sadly, as Almasy surveyed the hotel room. His armor lay in pieces all over the room’s floor. The Primetime Central Global Title, the richest prize in Seymour’s line of work, had been haphazardly thrown so that it rested over the arm of a chair. Aya: You don’t sound like it was wonderful… Her pout made Seymour frown momentarily. It HAD been wonderful. She was absolute poetry in motion in bed. His long hair was practically strewn all over his body, and his tattooed chest was covered in scratches. The act itself had been wonderful, but… Seymour Almasy: It was incredible, but I just don’t think we should be doing it. Aya: Oh, come on. Don’t tell me you’re getting emotional hang-ups. We’re friends. With notable benefits. That’s not wrong! The Final Fantasy couldn’t help but stare. She was beautiful. Intoxicating, even. She was like a drug. That first night in Tokyo, when he had bought her services, he had gotten hooked. Now, he had the difficult task of quitting. Seymour Almasy: You’re a great friend, and what we’ve been doing is wonderful. Almost…too wonderful. She gave a disgusted snort, shaking her head at Seymour disbelievingly. Aya: Just because we’re not in love doesn’t mean that we can’t make each other feel wonderful! I mean, you were screaming my name fifteen minutes ago, and now this? He was just about as unable to explain it as she was. But something definitely wasn’t right. He couldn’t explain it, but he was absolutely positive that there was something entirely wrong. Not only with this, but with everything. Seymour Almasy: Look…it isn’t you, it’s me. I just need a little bit of time to clear my head. I’m going to go for a walk. Aya looked on in disbelief as Seymour cobbled together an outfit from the pile of dirty clothes in the corner of the room. Fully clothed, he began to head for the door, but not before Aya got the last word in. Aya: You’ll be back. Seymour practically slammed the door behind him, before stepping out into the hallway. His feet began to move as fast as they could, carrying him down the hall, and to the elevator before he quite knew what he was doing, almost knocking over several people in the process. After hasty apologies, Almasy pushed the button to the elevator, and was in it in a heartbeat. Seymour Almasy: I’ve got to get out of here…before I prove her right. As much fun as it was to romp with a beautiful woman, there was way too much on Almasy’s mind for fornication. GTT4 was going to begin tonight, and already, every person in the tournament, to a man, wanted to defeat the reigning Global Champion. Seymour Almasy: I don’t even know where the hell I’m going. Guess it doesn’t matter, anywhere’s better than here right now. The elevator door sounded, and Seymour practically sprang out of it, racing across the lobby, and through a revolving door. Finally, he was outside, in the crisp air of late afternoon. All around him, people hustled and bustled. Despite the huge number of people, Almasy felt…alone. Seymour Almasy: Ah, finally… As he walked, he couldn’t help but think of the tournament. Reminders of it were everywhere. The first telephone pole that he came to had an announcement on it. Seymour Almasy: “GTT4 First Round- Tonight! Come see Angelo Deville, Violence Jack, and PTC’s Global Champion Seymour Almasy start their path to glory!” He chuckled, and kept moving on. Seymour Almasy: Damn straight I start my path to glory tonight. I don’t even know who my opponent is, but it doesn’t matter. I’m the Global Champion… In all honesty, his opponent’s identity wasn’t even important to him at the moment. What was were the words reverberating in his head. Aya’s final words to him. “You’ll be back.” Seymour Almasy: So what if I do go back? She’s just a good friend…who happens to give unbelievable head. He shook his head. He couldn’t even convince himself of it. He acted like a horny teenager around her. It wasn’t love. Seymour knew full well that the former prostitute wasn’t capable of loving. That left one thing. Seymour Almasy: Lust. Lust explained it. She wanted him because he was a relatively attractive young man with a sizeable penis. He wanted her because she was pretty and had nice tits. Physical attraction, pure and simple. Infatuation, even. Seymour Almasy: Well, now I know what it is. Whoopie doo, that doesn’t help me. Knowledge wasn’t power. At least, not for Seymour. He continued to walk on, past a sports bar that happened to be showing coverage of the tournament. Almasy stopped outside the door, close enough to hear the broadcaster. Broadcaster: And tonight, PTC Global Champion Seymour Almasy, who’s been making the rounds of radio shows insulting his competiton, begins his quest to win a third Primetime Central tournament tonight against the free agent Ash…” Seymour Almasy: Ah yes…those radio broadcasts. The long hype train for GTT4 had been long and tiring. As Global Champion and PTC’s preeminent tournament specialist, Seymour had been trotted out in front of every audience he could possibly think of. Seymour Almasy: Good lord. Radio shows, TV appearances on local public access stations, newspaper interviews. I’ve been talking to people for the past weeks, giving them the soundbytes they want, the quotes for the papers…and then, that public bitchfest with Phoenix. It was almost embarrassing. Knowing what he did about Phoenix, he was pretty sure that Phoenix was mortified as well. They had taken up an autograph session with embarrassing back and forth prattling. Seymour Almasy: I don’t like getting into such things…but that bastard asked for it. He’s not better than I am. Just not. Fact of life. And then, there was the worst part of all. Seymour Almasy: …Bah, I sound like an idiot! I can’t talk like an arrogant asshole, it’s not me. This evil thing is ridiculous. A bunch of fans turn on me because I attack Tsunami Oni and all of a sudden I have to act like I’m Satan? Fuck that. He felt slightly better saying that. He was Seymour Almasy. He wasn’t going to change just because people thought of him differently. Seymour Almasy: I am who I am. I’ve always been the same person. It’s other people who change their perceptions of me, not me who changes his actions. As far as I’m concerned, I’m in the right. Always have been, and always will be. As he continued down the street, several people pointed. He had become somewhat what known since his debut at the age of 21. Seymour Almasy: Celebrity…there can’t possibly be anything else that is such a blessing and such a curse at the exact same time. I’m the Global Champion. The money’s great, and the fame is nice…but it also means everyone wants to beat me to get what I have. Every day’s a fight to keep what I have. It’s beyond stressful. He would have to fight the best to keep it, too. Karina Wolfenden had signed up, as had Violence Jack and Angelo Deville, proven tournament competitors. And as if that wasn’t enough, men from other circles had come in. Seymour Almasy: That Token Weed guy signed up. Thinks he’s tough shit because he was champion of the Asylum. Hate to tell him that pulling guns on people and smoking crack doesn’t fly in PTC. And Doctor Curiosity…another guy from one of the so-called big feds. Ze Doctor will not be winning zis tournament. I will. He would almost rather face one of them in the first round. Get it over with. But no. He had to face an unknown. A no name. Someone named Ash. Seymour Almasy: I don’t even know who Ash is. But then again, I’m sure Acid didn’t know who I was when I stepped into the ring with him in GTT3. Jack Panzer didn’t know either. Neither did Mordred. I made it to the bracket finals as a no-name, and I’m sure Ash thinks he can do the same. But Ash needs to be let in on a little secret. I don’t know if he’s heard of me, but if he does the research, he’ll find that I simply don’t lose in tournaments. Counting RPG4, GTT3, the PIT, PTC’s Cruiserweight Tournament, and that little UTT special bracket thing I did, I’ve lost a total of two matches. Two, Ash. And the men that beat me were two former PCW Champions, and one of them who went on to win the special tournament and become Universal Champion. Now, I could be wrong, but I don’t think Ash has that talent… He shrugged. He would have probably bet the Global Title and his career on victory in the first round if someone pressed him to. Seymour Almasy: Bah! I love what I do. I love wrestling…but sometimes it feels like I don’t. Fame, glory, money….all are good, all are wonderful…but they don’t really seem to mean anything. It was about then that he made a connection. Fame. Aya. Glory. Aya. Money. Aya. They were all the same thing. He knew what love was. It was the way he felt about wrestling, and the way he used to feel about his wife Laura. But he had grown almost addicted to fame, glory, money, and Aya. Now, he could define his lust for each. He could define his infatuation. Seymour Almasy: Okay, so infatuation’s when you feel sort of addicted to something on a completely superficial level. There we go. Who says I can’t figure things out on my own? He shook his head. People were so quick to forget that just six months ago, he had thought he was an RPG character, and that life was a video game! It was unbelievable that he was alive today, much less as well-adjusted as he had become after several months of daily counseling…but people forgot his past. People expected him to be a person now, not a video game character, and it was difficult….especially when they wanted him to be that character in the ring. Seymour Almasy: When I step in the ring…I am that warrior trying to defeat his foes that I always used to be. Only difference is, now I’m not that way outside the ring. I can channel that mindset, use it effectively, not let it control my life….like it’s done for my twenty-three years. It was a weary Seymour that plopped his body down on a bench, and began to watch people walk by. Happy teenagers walked by in crowds, men and women alike, on their way off to bars or coffeehouses. Still others went to the movie theater across the street, to take in a film. Still others were lined up to get ice cream at an ice cream parlor, no more than one hundred feet away from Seymour. He looked at the line of people going in, and the happy people coming out, cones and shakes in hand, and something gave in him. Seymour Almasy: Ah, what the hell… Reaching into the pocket of the jeans he had thrown on, Seymour pulled out a five dollar bill, and went to go wait in line. As he did, he saw the families in the parlor. Perfect families. A mother, a father, and two children, a boy and a girl. All contentedly licking their ice cream cones. It almost broke Seymour’s heart. Seymour Almasy: I’ve…never been normal. The more Seymour thought about it, the sadder he got. His father had died when he was young. His mother was embarrassed by him. Neither had cared even enough to get him a babysitter in his youth. They had left him alone, alone with the videogames that had made him what he was today. Seymour Almasy: An outcast…I’m always the outcast! Ice Cream Server: Yeah, yeah, that’s nice. Can I have your order? You’re holding up the line! Almasy looked around, and blushed. He really was next in line, and a long line of people were glaring at him angrily. Seymour Almasy: Sorry…I’ll have a large cookies and cream cone, please? The Global Champion slid the money across the counter, and soon after had his change, and his large ice cream cone. Slurping at it contently, Almasy exited the parlor, and continued his long march to nowhere. Seymour Almasy: Well, I guess this explains a lot. I’ve never been normal, so I’ve never had any real companions, except for my wife, and even she humored me in my delusions. Now, I’m in the real world, with no real friends. All I have is my career, and all of my infatuations…some of which I can also blame on my parents. Mom and Dad may not have been there, but if I wanted some stupid toy or video game, I got it. I lust for fame, glory, money, power… It felt as if a great weight had been lifted from his shoulders, and the final piece of a puzzle had been put into place. Finally, in some small way, he understood. Seymour Almasy: All this time I’ve wanted to be known as the best. And why? Not because I really wanted to be recognized as such, but because I loved what came with it, like the money and prestige. For the first time in a long time, Seymour smiled. Really, actually smiled. Seymour Almasy: GTT4. For many it is a chance to prove themselves. For me, it is not only a chance to prove myself, but to find myself. In the ring, Seymour Almasy has proven a lot to a lot of people. I gave Jay Phoenix the list of names in our little discussion. But as is the nature of what I do, people are not convinced. Still more athletes rise, convinced they can dethrone PTC’s virtual tournament deity. Better athletes. Stronger athletes. Those already defeated, and now improved in an effort to succeed where they once failed. Because of all of this, I must prove myself anew. But even more importantly, I must find myself… Solemnly, Almasy turned around, and began walking back the way he came. This time, it seemed as if he had a destination in mind. Seymour Almasy: It is time for me to live the life I should have been living. It is time to take myself back. No longer shall I be ruled by my silly infatuations! From this moment forward, I will live my life to the fullest, knowing what it is. Not a game, not an experiment…but the most precious thing in the world. I will experience new things, see new places…and at the same time, continue being what I am, and that is the best wrestler in Primetime Central. And now, in the tournament, I can prove even more. I can prove that, beyond a shadow of a doubt, I am the top wrestler in the entire world. I can prove what my Global Title already states. Seymour increased his speed, walking with intent towards an unknown goal. The spring in his step made him seem lively, almost cheery, in contrast to his gloomy state earlier. Seymour Almasy: Tonight, I face the first test in my journey to the top of the Applachians Bracket, my own personal Mount Gagazet. Ash will bring everything he has, this I know, but I have the tools and the skills to defeat him. I am sound of body, and sound of mind. When I am ready, truly prepared, I am indefatigable. Ash, you will be the first man to find that out. But first, I have a stop to make. The Final Fantasy made a hard right turn, poised to enter a store. Seymour Almasy: And then, after I beat Ash, I’m going to do the one thing that I’ve wanted to do my entire life. Almasy opened the door to the establishment, and stepped inside. The words above the door, “Al’s Spirits”, told the entire story, and explained Seymour’s final comments of the evening. Seymour Almasy: I’M GOING TO GET FUCKING WASTED! And with that, Seymour Almasy’s night was either ending…or just beginning.
