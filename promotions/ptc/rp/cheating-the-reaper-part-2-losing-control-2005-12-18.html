Cheating The Reaper (Part 2: Losing Control)

18 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-18 III. Imitation Is The Sincerest Form Of Flattery BANG! With a single blast the world seemed to come to a stop. Everything began to move in slow motion, little shards of ice slowly fell from the sky in a show of beauty. Slowly it bounced off the pavement all around Michael while his last image began to fade to black. His last breath left his body in a puff of smoke in the cold Chicago air. His life danced away through the falling daggers of ice and howling winds of the city. Disbelief had taken hold of Christian Zenith's consciousness. It appeared to be just like all the others. The hardships of time upon his memory couldn't make him forget those images… but something didn't seem right. "No…" he said aloud unknowingly, "She's dead…" Limply Michael fell to the ground and chaos ensued. His friends began to scream and look around in a panic. Anarchy had broken loose in their reality…. but not Zenith's. Not this time. Instinctively his cadet blue eyes began to navigate the skyline while a small crowd gathered around Michael's dead body. Women and children pleaded for help for anyone who would listen. Too bad Michael Hagger had already made it clear he wanted to fight his own battles. As much satisfaction he would see in watching that turncoat suffer, Christian had his own agenda for finding that assassin. The fire escape; high above the street, a nearby fire escape unnaturally rocked up and down. Higher he looked through the ice storm, squinting to see an image of black ascending the ladders. She wasn't that stupid… he could see the killer from more than six stories below. Die Erntemaschine would never be that sloppy, she was a perfectionist… turning the task of assassination into an admired art form. Leaving even those who lost a loved one in awe of her craftsmanship. As he watched the killer fumble up the escape, it reminded him of a seven year old butchering the works of Shakespeare… or an elementary school band plodding through the masterpieces of Beethoven. Whether or not it was the real thing, he had to get his answers… he had to clear his name. He had no choice but to follow. It was the only ticket out of this cesspool. As warm tears flowed down the icy red cheeks of the pedestrians of Chicago, he made his way towards the fire escape completely unnoticed. As fast as his quickly numbing limbs could take him, he ascended the ladder in tail of his target. A downpour of sleet continued to beat down from the heavens, turning his flesh a reddish hue. Fighting through the burning sensation across his face, he pursued the target. Closer and closer he came. Every rung of the ladder closed a bit of distance. At last, the murderer now only three stories above, had spotted The Conscious Crisis. How quickly the tides turn. The hunter had become the hunted. The predator was now the prey. Picking up speed but not gaining any distance due to the icy steel ladders, the masked assassin had reached the roof. The assassin tried to sprint across the roof to the door that lead down inside of the apartment building, but slipped twice on the way, bashing their knee on the icy concrete. Now with a noticeable limp the killer reached the door, trying to get a grip on the cold door knob through the unrelenting storm. Alas the door was locked. Trapped with no where to go but death, the assassin turned and faced their new adversary. Zenith pulled himself up onto the rooftop. Looking through the sheets of ice that poured down upon them, Christian's icy blue eyes struck fear into the one people had been referring to as The Reaper. He stood into the face of death, unafraid. Yet this game of cat and mouse had grown mundane for Zenith. As he approached the killer, he wondered how he was fooled by this novices charade. "You tell me what in the hell is going on and I might let you go…" Christian Zenith spoke over the thunderous sleet, "because let's face truth, that side jumping idiot probably deserved to die. You just did a justice to this world, making sure that thing didn't mate and reproduce." The assassin looked silently back at The Midnight Messiah. It was obviously a male, his face concealed by a black wool mask that only revealed his eyes and mouth. A heavy black nylon jacket covered his upper body along with a pair of black Mechanix gloves. The murder weapon was attached to his back by a strap that went across the mans body from his right shoulder to his left side. Black jeans and black combat boots, it was obvious he thought that he would never be seen through the storm. He had no idea what it took to be Die Erntemaschine… it began to cross Christian Zenith's mind about how much this fool really knew. "So you're not going to answer me are you?" Zenith shook his head, "Always must we do things the difficult way. I know you're not Die Erntemaschine… I know Die Erntemaschine. Do you take me for a fool? I will not be manipulated by you or anyone else. People go around and label me a murderer while you're going around killing innocent people on the streets from above like a coward. Yet when you are confronted for your wrong doings you simply stare back at me like a deer in headlights. I despise fucking cowards who try to pass the buck. What are you getting out of this? Money? Adrenaline? Maybe some crack? I will not be your scapegoat… it's time we settle this like men." The masked man's hand balled into a fist before he ran towards his nemesis. Zenith moved forwards slowly, making the trip across the rooftop a shorter one for the false assassin. Taking a swing at Christian, the man caught nothing but air as it was skillfully dodged. The next punch found it's way to Zenith's palm, where it was stopped before the man was forced to the ground. Somersaulting up, he tried to kick The Conscious Crisis but his leg was caught and spun around. A wild bunch was then ducked and Zenith slammed his foot down on the outside of the man's knee with a crack. Screaming out in pain the assassin tried to throw one last punch, but it was caught with his arm out straight as far as it could go. Zenith slammed his elbow down on the man's collarbone. Quickly the man found a knee into his abdomen to compliment the two broken bones already sustained from his lack of hand-to-hand preparation. "Global World Champion isn't a fucking joke… I'll assure you." Zenith buried his foot into the stomach of the man. All the man's air escaped his body in a single blow, before falling onto the iced concrete rooftop. Tears of pain trickled down the man's face and was quickly absorbed by the wool ski mask. Zenith got down on one knee and looked at his opponent nearly crippled. Reaching down, Christian grabbed the mask and violently ripped it off his head. Instantly he was surprised by the murderer's face. He was aging but looked very wise and gentle. SMACK! Zenith stumbled back holding the right side of his face as he slipped across the icy surface. Taking his hand off of his face he looked down and saw the sight of his own blood as the middle aged man pained his way up to his feet. Tears still streamed down his face from the obviously broken bones in his right leg and right shoulder. Christian's blood was smeared across the gun handle, but his intense stare didn't change as he looked back at the impostor. The sniper tried to level his gun with his only working had as Christian approached, but to no avail. With a kick the gun went skipping across the pavement. After that the man quickly fell to his knees… giving up his pointless struggle. "Who are you?" Zenith said as he looked down at the man, shaking from his wounds and the ice that continued to rain down from above. "M.. my name is… Logan." The man responded in slow grunts, hoping the cold winds would numb his body and take the pain away. "Who do you work for? What in the fuck do you want from me? I'm a very busy man… and I want answers." Zenith snapped back at Logan. "I'm not a murderer…." Logan responded, "I'm just a security supervisor." "You sure fooled me… you ignorant bastard." Zenith retaliated. "I suppose I deserve that." Logan looked back up at Christian, he didn't look like a terrible man at all. He looked quite normal and gentle. Something didn't seem right, this man didn't seem like the type of person to up and start murdering people to get something. "Really I'm not though… I'm a good man, or at least I suppose I was a good man. Until I got dragged into this. Are you going to kill me?" "That depends… Who do you work for?" Christian responded reaching his arm into his jacket, knowing full well he was not armed. It would never hurt to strike the fear of God into him. "Could you please?" Logan's answer shocked Zenith a bit, "I've lived a terrible life… I don't think I could go o…" BANG! With a single gun shot Logan fell limply to the ground. Who was that? Christian Zenith quickly ran to the side of the roof and looked all around him. Nothing; perhaps this one was the real deal. In a panic he looked down at Logan's dead body. Why did they shoot Logan? Nothing added up, nothing made any sense at all except… was it possibly Logan was nothing more than a decoy? It was too late now… blood was all over Christian's shoes, and his blood was all over the sniper rifle on the far side of the rooftop. It became painfully obvious almost immediately what had happened. He'd been set up, and now there was enough evidence to throw him back into hell he escaped from before. "It couldn't be…" Christian said aloud, but suddenly a glimmer caught his attention amongst the blood at his feet. A metallic object, possibly a collar laid at his feet. Slowly he picked it up out of the blood trying desperately not to leave anything else as he took off back down the fire escape. He studied the collar on his way down as he was escaping. It looked as if it was an electronic restraining device, like the ones used on dogs. This one was heavy duty though, it could put down a tiger if it was big enough. Logan had said he was a security advisor… but for what? Or for who? IV. Under Zenith (I Love How You Let Me Fall) A whistle rattled the windows of The Windy City. Outside was a heavenly sight… as slow fell by the dozens, covered the skyline and streets in a blanket of white. It looked almost like the winter wonderlands that you'd only see in movies. This wasn't the movies though… and the streets were nearly vacant, pardon a few bums walking around looking for a place to get warm. No children playing in the snow, no pedestrians walking the sidewalks taking in the beauty of mother nature. An eerie calm filled the northern portion of downtown Chicago, for it was quiet… a little too quiet. Christian Zenith pushed open the glass door labeled with the letter Z intertwined with a circle, entering the elements. The scene outside the Zenith Corporation building was one of serenity… like something you'd only see on a postcard. Turning around and forcing the door shut, he took a key out of his pocket, jamming it into the door. With a twist a popping sound resonated from the metal lock. Slowly he put the key back into his pocket and looked around for his black Chrysler Crossfire, parked somewhere nearby covered in snow. As he walked down the unshoveled sidewalk, a strange sensation came upon him. Snow was falling everywhere, and the wind was whistling through the city like a wind tunnel… yet he felt nothing. Not even the lightest bit of chill touched his body, covered elaborately in a custom made black suit. He reached out and touched a nearby light post, and felt nothing. Confused but apathetic towards this development he continued on his path looking around for his transportation. Up ahead he spotted his destination, under a beam of light and lightly layered with strategically placed bits of snow. His pace began to speed up, reaching inside for his jacket to hold his car keys. They were within his grasp and images of a nice night at home next to the fire seemed to look more and more like paradise. It would be paradise… but that wasn't where he was heading. BANG! The shot resonated through his ear drum for what seemed like an eternity. Limply he fell down onto the snowy sidewalk. He couldn't move… was he dead? Could anyone help him? He tried to scream but nothing came out. Perhaps this is what death feels like. Everything around him became a blur. The Crossfire in front of him seemed to grow farther and farther away as he lay helplessly. Was there anyone who could save him? The blur became worse and worse until his sight faded to black. Click… Click… Click… Awakened by the sound of footsteps, an image began to grow clear once again from the darkness. The red stained snow looked like a cherry sno-cone beneath him. In the distance he saw the silhouette of what appeared to be a woman. Perhaps she could help him. Once again his attempts at trying to move or speak were futile. She approached slowly shrouded in darkness, many would think of her as an angel… surrounded by white purity as she approached. Christian didn't believe in such things. As he tried to get a glimpse of her, his vision blurred once again before losing consciousness. Click… Click… Click… Slowly he readjusted to his surroundings, as a he saw a pair of boots just mere feet away from him walking before coming to a halt at him. He couldn't see her face, he just couldn't move nor speak. Wanting so badly to at least know her name, he became frustrated at his own weakness. Why couldn't he feel the pain? Had the cold air numbed his body? Perhaps she had done something to him. Droplets of blood began to run out of the side of his mouth as he saw her get down to one knee. She dropped a rifle on the ground next to her. Maybe he had her all wrong… this woman was not an angel, this woman was a minion. This woman was a murderer. Turning his head she looked into his eyes, he couldn't believe what he saw. It was her. Christian Zenith sat straight up with a rush of panic. Looking around the room anxiously his breaths were quick and short. Beads of sweat raced down his forehead with his heart pounding like he'd just ran a mile. He looked down at his bare chest and the blankets on top of him, relieved that it was only a dream. The clock read 3:06am, he only had five hours until he was to leave for Tokyo. Was this a sign that he should leave? What did this mean? There were so many unanswered questions in his life now that it made his head spin. They had to be connected, something or someone should be able to answer them all. It's all a matter of asking the right person the right questions. "She isn't real…" he said aloud to himself, alone lying in bed. Vivica J. Valentine, Demitri Miasnikov, and Jason LeMaster were all fast asleep in various corners of the Manor he was glad they could not hear him. They couldn't know about this, they couldn't know about what was going on. Die Erntemaschine hurt every single one of them in a different way. Die Erntemaschine had taken Vivica's ex-fiancé James Carlisle… not only was he her boyfriend, he also used to be one of her only reasons to live. Die Erntemaschine had killed Demitri's little sister Mika, who was Christian's own ex-fiancé. Jason's little brother was also murdered in cold blood. They never did find out the answers, they didn't want to give that sadistic bitch the chance. She was gone… she was dead, she can never come back. "I killed her. She's gone." Zenith repeated to himself a few times, looking over at the nightstand where the electronic collar he'd found still sat next to the digital alarm clock. Christian put his head inside of his hands and closed his eyes, trying to erase the images of the past that began to haunt him. He stayed up until sunrise, repeating "She's gone" until he couldn't any longer. V. Suspicion Abound "Christian!" A voice arose from behind The Midnight Messiah. A quick glance in the mirror had him making eye contact with Vivica J. Valentine, who stood in the doorway behind him. She was dressed warm, a pair of black cargo jeans and a black baby-tee under a black down feather jacket. Her beautiful teal colored eyes looked back at him from under her golden locks of hair, "Have you looked out the window?" "No…" Zenith replied, contemplating why Vivica would care about such an odd request, "Is there something going on?" "There is a black sedan and two police cars outside of the front entrance, they pulled up about 10 minutes ago." Vivica said to Christian, who took a deep breath before turning around. Dressed in his usual three piece custom made black suit, he was dressed and ready for the weather… but he didn't have time to deal with those crooked police officers. He knew why they were there, but they'd have to wait… Global Wrestling and their patrons awaited his arrival in Tokyo for the annual event. It was the event that put his name on the map a year ago, and now he returns as World Champion. "We have to be at the airport to leave for Tokyo in 30 minutes…" Christian spoke aloud to Vivica, who still stood in the doorway in confusion, "I don't have time for this." "Someone probably paid them to give you a hard time… possibly the local media looking for a story, you know how they operate." Vivica elaborated further, "You wave a dollar under their fucking greedy noses and they'll follow you to the ends of the earth. Money grubbing bastards would arrest their own grandmothers for the right price. Just slip them a couple green bills, you'll be amazed at how little they'll care about the whole Die Erntemaschine thing. It's a damn fucking shame too you know?" "I'll handle it Vivica." Christian said as he approached her, it was obvious that she wanted some attention but he walked on by. It was times like these that Vivica often wondered if she's wasting her time staying engaged to him. She knew the pact they made a long time ago after he was shot, but things had changed since then. Their entire lives have changed. She never spent every night wondering if she'll make it through another day alive, thinking about ways to scam a quick buck so that she could eat dinner for the first time that week. With a sigh she trailed Christian slowly, reluctantly making her way out to the spectacle outside. Vivica made her way down the staircase and approached the door slowly, before opening it up and walking out towards the scene. She saw Christian walking up to the police officers, which surrounded his waiting limo outside the door with the driver still inside. Two cops were outside in front of the back door, another officer stood outside the driver's door. Behind the limo the black sedan sat, with two men in suits sitting inside the heated vehicle. Pulling her hair out of her face and around her ear, she took Christian's side while buttoning up her jacket. "Well Mr. Zenith we heard you were leaving the country for Japan today… is this correct?" One of the officers asked Christian. "I am yes, the Showdown in Tokyo event for Global Wrestling is in two days. I am competing, now if you'll excuse me…" Christian responded making a motion towards the back door of the limo. "Not so fast Mr. Zenith," The officer stood in his path, "we are concerned and have reason to believe you may be fleeing the country." "Last time I checked officer, leaving the country to attend a wrestling event isn't something the police force is usually very interested in. Isn't there something important to do like… I don't know, bring down the crime rate by stopping thieves and rapists? Or even getting bagels and coffee? That seems to be pretty important." Vivica mouthed off to the officer, who simply gave her a blank look before answering. "There was a suspicious murder last night, two of them in fact. Michael Hagger… who I believe is an acquaintance of yours was found dead on the street. Also another man was found dead on the roof above. We have yet to confirm precisely who the man is, but a couple or Mr. Hagger's friends placed you at the scene of the crime that night. How convenient is that? Another assassination… another Christian Zenith spotting." The officer acted surprised like a jackass and continued, "We have yet to fully study the evidence but the murder weapon was found on the scene. We believe this activity is suspicious, and we cannot allow you to leave the country. Not without a full 24 hour government escort." "You have to be kidding…" Christian Zenith said before looking over at Vivica and then back at the officer, "We have to be at the airport in 30 minutes." "Isn't that a damn shame?" The officer told Zenith, "Unless you give full consent to an escort in writing, your cowardly ass isn't going anywhere." "I find it odd that you comment on his ass and not mine." Vivica said with a smile, "Now that's not a damn shame. That actually puts my mind at ease." "Where do I sign?" Christian asks obviously frustrated by the CPD, who are still baffled at exactly Vivica just said to them. The officer pulls out a clipboard and hands it over to The Midnight Messiah, who quickly scribbles down a signature and shoves it back at the officer almost making him fall over when he grabs it out of Zenith's hand. "You have a nice day Mr. Zenith, Miss Valentine…" The officer tipped his hat, "Oh… and one more thing. If you try to get away from the FBI, a warrant will immediately be put out for your arrest. I'm sure you understand Mr. Zenith, we simply cannot afford to let you operate unwatched. We've let you do that for years… and look what it's gotten us. A string of mysterious deaths that are always SOMEHOW related to Christian Zenith. So just a word of advice Christian… don't fuck up. Have a nice trip." A single glare from Zenith's cadet blue eyes sends the intimidated police officers back into their squad car as Christian opens the door for Vivica and they enter the limo. Inside Jason LeMaster and Demitri Miasnikov are waiting. Jason looks out of the tinted windows towards the FBI Agents now tailing their limo as they pull away from Zenith Manor. Demitri looks back at Christian and Vivica trying to get comfortable as they head towards the airport. "What is this all about?" Demitri asks Christian who is obviously annoyed. "These morons still think I'm the murderer." Christian responds, "Those two government goons behind us are our new escorts. Apparently there were two more murders last night. They think I'm the one responsible. I think this entire city is a joke." "Where you were you last night anyway Christian?" Jason asked questionably, "You too Demitri?" They both looked back at Jason aggravated before answering. "I was visiting my family last night…" Demitri answered, "I don't know where Christian was." "That's odd…" Christian said looking back at Demitri, "I went to visit your family last night too, but they weren't home. I was mainly just trying to clear my mind." "You must've just missed us." Demitri answered before looking out the window, "They would've been thrilled to see you again. It's been a long time since they've seen their ex-future son-in-law." "For some reason Demitri…" Christian paused for a minute, "I seriously doubt that." VI. Ransom Everyone stopped and looked as the Christian Zenith made his way down the backstage hallway. His head heavily bandaged from the chair shot he'd received from that bastard Seymour Almasy earlier in the night. Even the mighty PTC God Seymour Almasy couldn't match with Christian Zenith without trying to cheat like a coward. With the GW World Championship still firmly around his waist, he made his way towards the locker room favoring his right leg a little bit. Battered and bruised he was going to walk out of Tokyo, Japan a champion… regardless of how unhonorable if may have occurred. He approached the door where two FBI Agents stood guarding, waiting for Zenith's return. They acknowledged him with a slight nod as he made his way into the locker room. It was almost deserted, just his bag remained in a locker on the far side of the room. It was a shame that Tokyo couldn't give him his usual personal suite… he'd have to make sure that the Japanese get a little piece of his mind on the way out for their lack of hospitality towards a famous, wealthy, and powerful man such as himself. Taking the Global World Championship off from around his waist and placing it into the bad, Christian was about to prepare to jump into the showers when something stuck to the side of the bag with a pin caught his attention. A yellow sheet of college ruled notebook paper that appeared to be torn out of some tablet had a note on it written in black sharpie marker. He narrowed his eyes and began to read the note. DEAR CHRISTIAN ZENITH, I KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE DONE, YOU HAVE BEEN A VERY BAD MAN CHRISTIAN. YOUR FREEDOM TO DO WHAT YOU PLEASE IS IN JEOPARDY, I KNOW THE TRUTH ABOUT EVERYTHING. I SAW YOU WITH THE KID IN THE ALLEY, AND I ALSO SAW YOU ON THE ROOFTOP. I BELIEVE YOU'VE UNDERESTIMATED MY RESOURCES, AND MY SILENCE ABOUT YOUR DEALINGS WILL NOT COME WITHOUT A PRICE. I HAVE THE RIGHT CONNECTIONS TO MAKE SURE WHEN YOUR PLANE TOUCHES DOWN IN CHICAGO THAT YOU NEVER LEAVE THAT CITY ALIVE AGAIN. HOW MUCH DO I WANT YOU MAY ASK? A COUPLE THOUSAND? MAYBE A MILLION DOLLARS? NO, CHRISTIAN… I AM AFRAID I WANT MUCH MUCH MORE. I WILL ACCEPT NOTHING LESS THAN THE COMPLETE AND TOTAL CONTROL OF ZENITH CORPORATION, IN WRITING AND AUTHORIZED, NO QUESTIONS ASKED. IF YOU DO AS I PLEASE YOU WILL BE A FREE MAN… ABLE TO LIVE WITH YOUR LITTLE GIRLFRIEND IN PEACE OR HOWEVER YOU WISH. I WILL GIVE YOU A LITTLE BIT OF TIME TO MAKE YOUR DECISION, BUT REST ASSURED I WILL GET BACK WITH YOU. DON'T GET TOO COMFORTABLE CHRISTIAN, YOUR LIFE MAY VERY WELL DEPEND ON IT. Christian Zenith looks at the note expressionless before throwing it into his duffel bag and slinging it over his shoulder. Was it a member of Global Wrestling doing this to him? Maybe it was Danielle Northsky using her little group to spy on him and to spook him before their GTT5 match. Seymour Almasy was also a likely subject after his little "Fuck You" incident in the ring. Train could've done it also. They couldn't have Zenith Corporation… they had no idea what actually goes on there. With a deep breath Christian walked out of the locker room with his things and looked back at the FBI Agents. "Who has been in this room tonight?" Christian demanded an answer from the agents who stood their ground. "Every male active member of Global Wrestling has been in and out of this room Mr. Zenith" The Agent answered, wondering to himself why such an odd question would be asked of him. "There hasn't been a single person not signed to Global or anyone suspicious come in or out of this room?" Christian asked for clarification. "No Mr. Zenith" The same agent answered once again, "No one suspicious or not contracted by Global Wrestling has entered or left this room since we've arrived." Christian looked over at the Global Wrestling banner that hung on the wall across from his locker room. This was obviously much bigger and much more devious than he previously thought. How far back does this go? Who could be capable of this kind or power and greed? The more questions he had answered, the more questions he had. As he walked towards the exit with the FBI Agents following him, he wondered if this nightmare was ever going to end. If so… what he had to do to make it all stop. TO BE CONTINUED…
