Lost Sister (PT. 2)

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-27 Ch. 2 The Chase… The night sky was peaceful and serene; the desert that comforted the lonely stretch of highway was all but abandoned of life, except for the occasional desert nightlife that went on searching for their food. A closer look reveals, out from the shadows, a furry scavenger looking for food from the green lush cactus. A single life form; trying to make its way around its existence, trying to find means of sustenance…but scurrying away from anything in the environment harmful enough to threaten its life or compromise its food. The only thing that lights its path in the cold, dark environment of the desert surface is that of the soothing and comforting light of the full moon. All was peaceful and calm…until the cold white, exterior, a bony foot stepped with all its might, on top of the small scavenger letting the red liquid of life circumference the bony structure of the creatures’ toes. Moving up the fossil of what used to be human; we can see decaying flesh hanging off searching to go back to the ground from which the monster had risen from. The arms of the creature used to be filled with the biceps of a man who worked out…but now those biceps were decayed and exposed hanging off the arm to add to it’s grotesque form. The skull of the creature was somewhat large with the remains of the brains that once sat in the cranium spilling onto the earth in chunks. The hair of the creatures was thinned out do to the age and decay; they stood on the creatures head like a few weeds in the valley. The eyes of the creature had the stale but whole never the less, fleshy retina hanging from the socket. The teeth of the creature were rotten, with bugs and earth all through out the once whole gum line. A wide outlook on the dessert reveals a horrible sight that the monster is not alone. Waves of bony skeletal creatures marching like a army of the damned…one by one they marched on, letting nothing stand in the way of their ultimate evil desire. One of the creatures looked upon the heavens as if to ask God why they were the forsaken. But a response of a different nature was all they got as the moon started to fill its blue soft light with a more blood red, sinister light. The all too famous face in the moon started to twist and bend with the reality around it revealing a brand new face…one whose desire is to bring about the ultimate destruction to the world that had given him birth. The eyes of the face in the moon is glowing an eerie blood red to match the light illuminating the army of the dead. "My children get the girl and kill my brother! Let hell be received for all who dare stand in your way!" The voice came thundering down upon them so they all could understand their commands. Where was this hellish army of evil going? They started their epic march on…through rock, dirt and any vegetation or animal, straight to Black, Alabama. May God have mercy on the souls of whoever stands in their way. About forty five miles up the darkened highway; we see a lone vehicle driving to its destination. Aside from the people inside the vehicle, the tires apparent speed is the only thing telling the tale of what type of horror they have been through. The vehicle driving on the stretch of highway is a black ford pickup that the two survivors got in the last town that they were in. They are John Adams and his blonde haired fifteen year old sister Samantha Reynolds. They have been on the road for about three hours since the undead attack happened, and the only thing that is going through their minds, well maybe just John’s because his sister had finally got to sleep was what happened at the last town they were in. "What happened there? Why does my brother want the girl for?" John thought as he kept the truck running on the road. With that Adams is met with the red and blue lights flashing in the back of him, illuminating the whole cab of the truck. "Damn, wrong time to get pulled over." John thought as he against better judgment pulled to the side of the road. The Squad car pulled right behind the black truck leaving the flashing lights on. John could hear the CB on in the squad car with the radio traffic. Through the left hand side view mirror, John could see the police officer getting out of his car. The man looked like the classic Barney Fife of Mayberry. He had the classic goofy looking tan beige hat with the beige police uniform…in fact he was also skinny like Barney Fife. The only difference is that he had the black Aviator sunglasses on…at night even. As he walked towards the black pickup, it was evident that the sunglasses were affecting his vision because he was stumbling on some of the rocks and dirt…he just wore them to look cool. In fact it’s amazing he even was able to drive at night. "Well ok sir do you have any idea how fast you were going?" He said as he shined the flashlight on Adams, that he got from his left side pocket. "Well Officer, I assure you that I wasn’t going too fast. What is the speed limit on this highway anyways?" John says as he does a quick scan of the road ahead and back of him. The Officer pulls out a pad of paper from his right hand pocket and starts to write some things down on it. "Well buster I have you clocked going ninety five miles per hour in a forty five mile per hour zone. I’m going to have to see your license and registration please." Great one out of the two he didn’t have, what was he going to do now? As he pretended to look for his registration he didn’t notice that his sister was awake looking outside the back window of the truck cab. Her expression on her face told the tale of what she saw next…the army of dead had found them. John Adams attention wasn’t focused on that but to the officer writing him a ticket. Samantha tried to warn Adams of the impending doom but couldn’t for the fear had too much of a grip on her vocals. "Well let me see John Adams….and where is your registration John?" The officer says as he takes John’s ID to look at it. "Listen I’m sorry but I must have forgotten it at home…." John said but was suddenly interrupted when a bony hand suddenly went through the gut of the officer. The blood and fleshy stomach parts that used to belong to the officer splattered all over the truck and John. John was partly shocked with horror when he saw the skeletal humanoid sever the head of the dead officer and proceed to feed on its fleshy exterior and crunch the bony interior to get to the fleshy and bloody interior beyond that. John Adams finally snapped back to reality as he saw five of the creatures ahead of him on the road. There was an additional ten in back of the truck, that’s when John starts the truck again and slams it to drive to take off. He plows through the five in front to get through but not without them, with a supernatural strength grabbing both sides of the truck and smashing the windows to attempt to grab Adams and the little girl. One of the monsters bony hands grabbed hard, and got hold of Adams shirt. Adams tried to struggle, but every time he did the truck started to swerve from side to side greatly. "GET THE HELL OFF OF ME!!!" Adams yelled, but the grip just got tighter….until "BOOM!" The thunderous sound came from a nine millimeter gun, in the hands of the small fifteen year old sister of John’s. The small bullet from the gun traveled through the air, first hitting the Monsters hand then continuing to pierce through Adams shoulder, causing the truck to go spill over a small hill into a deserted town called Black which is located Alabama. Darkness hindered the whole area of their minds due to the nasty spill they both took when the truck crashed. Samantha was the first one to awake from the forced slumber. Her left eye slowly creaked open to take a peak on the outside world…to her surprise and shocked horror she was simply darkness. Her heart raced though because she could hear the creatures of the night rustle about due to the truck disturbing their resting place. She lifts her head slowly more to get a better look at what is going on…and that’s when she notices another small town…but this time it’s abandoned the buildings built around the town are the exact equivalent to tombstones telling about the people that once were flourished in here living day by day. She turns slightly to her Brother…who was still passed out by the crash. The dark blackish gray mark on his forehead is a good indication that John has a head injury to himself. Her fear is a big decider when she is contemplating whether she should go outside or not. She quickly starts to struggle though as she starts to smell a gas leak coming from the truck…she shakes John wildly to try and snap him back to conscience but realizes she is not getting anywhere by doing that. The injury must be too severe for him to wake. "WAKE UP PLEASE!!!" She starts to scream hysterically…she starts to panic even more when she hears a familiar monstrosity growl. She is greeted with the unfriendly sight of the skull humanoid monster looking straight at her and her brother. Apparently the monster was caught up in the wheels of the truck after Samantha shot him with the gun. The monster is only at part strength but still with its monstrosity of a hand rakes Adams chest…the pain shoots all over Adams enough to wake every nerve in his body enough to snap him back into reality. Groggy and holding his head in pain, he unlocks the passenger side door and slightly nudges his sister out of the truck. He shortly follows slowly behind her…the Monster is still pinned inside the truck so it can not follow. Slowly the two survivors begin to go through the grass on the ground closer to the town. As Adams slightly looked back behind him…he could see three more of those skeletal creatures that had made it to the crash sight of the truck. "Give me the gun…Sam…" Adams barely says to his sister who is still holding the nine millimeter with her. He was staggering a lot but even with the world spinning in his mind he was able to muster enough strength to aim the gun after taking it from his sister back at the truck. He took a long aim with the gun pointing towards the gas that was leaking from the truck. He also waited till all of the four monsters grabbed onto the truck… "BOOM!" The Bullet flew in the air towards the gas and the truck. The friction from the traveling bullet started to ignite the gas…a spark started to form from the impact of the bullet. The spark quickly traveled to the rest of the gas laying rest inside the gas tank of the black pickup. "KABOOM!" The truck exploded into a thousand pieces…along with the skeletal monsters with it. Embers flew every where but luckily not affecting John Adams or his sister. The fire spread all over the grass, the other creatures of the night were also on fire. The sister cried as she witnessed small animals flesh melting off from the extreme heat of the fire. "Shhh, it’s going to be fine Samantha…." Adams said with a weak tone. "I have to get you some help.." Samantha said wiping her tears as she notices her brother very weak from the gun shot wound to the shoulder and the blow to the head from the crash. "I’m…going…to…be…fine…" Adams tried to reassure her as the two looked upon the town that they were at. "Welcome to Black, Alabama!" The Welcome sign to the town was old and partly faded with the blue lettering…they had no idea where to go from here just that John needed to rest and so did she. But what awaits them inside this apparent ghost town may have them running for their lives… To…be…continued…
