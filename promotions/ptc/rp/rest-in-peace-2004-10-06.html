Rest In Peace

6 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-06 Dave Harley – cocky, arrogant, backstabbing leader of the Devil’s Own stands humbled. With his head bowed, he stands silently listening to the prayers being spoken around him. He glances up and watches as the coffin is slowly lowered in to the grave. There is a creak and a thud. And with that, Benjamin Carnage reaches his final resting place. Dave knew this day would finally come. Ben wasn’t getting any younger, after all. When he thinks back to his early days in the gym, Dave realises just how badly Ben had declined in health over the past few years. Benjamin Carnage had been the owner of the Carnage Family Gym in Detroit. It was here that Dave Harley was trained. It was under the loving attention of the Carnage family that Dave became the wrestler he is today. Of course, since those early days there had been a lot of…’unpleasantness’ between himself and the Carnage family. Most notably with Ben’s eldest and most gifted son, Jonny. Jonny and Dave had clicked very early on in training. They were inseparable. Realising their star potential, Ben had teamed his son up with Harley and sent them out on the road. They made a lot of money and won a lot of titles. They wrestled for promotions up and down the US, Mexico and Japan. But even at such an early age, Dave’s jealousy was untamed. Fearing his push would be lost to Jonny during a tag split angle, Harley power bombed his partner through the mat. Jonny Carnage never walked again. But Dave never lost his respect for Ben – the man who had trained Harley, the man who gave him his first break. It didn’t matter to Harley whether or not the Carnage Family would appreciate his presence or not – he was coming to pay his last respects to his fallen mentor. “Ashes to ashes…dust to dust.” Dave was pulled from his reverie. He looked up and met the gaze of Wendy. Man, he hadn’t seen her for years. She looked just as beautiful as ever. She wore a black shawl under which tears slowly dripped from her face in memory of her father. Perhaps I should try and speak to her later? Dave pondered, yet thought better of it. She had not spoken to him since he crippled her brother. Maybe it would be best to keep his distance. To Wendy’s left sat Jonny Carnage – his sunglasses hiding his grief. He sat in his wheelchair in a large black suit. Jonny had been built like Hercules, but his inactivity with being in the wheelchair had caused the muscle to slowly turn to flab. He looked like a retired Heavyweight Boxer – so much weight and no more use for it. With the funeral service at its conclusion, Dave headed towards his car. There was to be a reception at the Carnage Gym. He would attend and hopefully get a chance to speak to his former partner. The Gym was just how he remembered it. Very little had changed in the years since his last visit. The rings had been taken down to make room for guests and a large buffet table. Dave strolled around looking at the various pictures on the wall. There were several of Ben Carnage in his heyday – stood smiling with various trophies and belts adorning his magnificent frame. He stopped to examine a particular picture more closely. It was of a young Jonny Carnage and Dave Harley, taken moments after their first tag tournament victory in Japan. Jonny looked so chiselled, a born superstar. But Dave had very little remorse for what he had done to Jonny’s career. After all, he had only recently attempted to do the same thing to Mal Somers. “You’re looking well, Dave.” As before, a voice pulled Dave from his reverie. He turned into the once loving gaze of Wendy. “You sure this is a wise decision, talking to me? You don’t want to upset your brother do you?” Harley asked. “Who? Jonny? I’m a big girl Dave. I don’t need my brother to decide who I can and can’t talk to.” “Alright,” Dave sighed, glancing around to make sure Jonny didn’t see them. “I was…sorry to hear about your Dad. He was a good man. Taught me almost everything I know.” “I’m surprised to hear you talking so candidly,” Wendy teased, “what with your recent Devil’s Own takeover.” “That was business Wend, you know that.” “So how about what you did to my brother? Was that business too?” Wendy began to raise her voice and Dave knew were this was going. He tried to calm her down to avoid causing a scene. “Listen Wendy, what happened is between me and Jonny. You got that? You know we’ve spoken since and you know we’ve tried to work things out.” This much was true. Dave had tried to reconcile with Jonny back when he was with X Corp. He could only imagine what Jonny would think of Devil’s Own. Plus, Jonny liked Mal… “I missed you Dave.” Yeah right, he thought. She was one of the major factors in Harley crippling Jonny. She’d go on and on about how her brother would be such a big star and how he’d make more money than Harley would ever make. She’d called him a few hours after the ‘incident’ and refused to take any blame whatsoever. If you’d have asked Dave Harley a few months ago, he’d have understood why she said that. But the new Dave Harley, brimming with anger, jealousy and frustration… Luckily, Wendy was called away before Dave could really let rip on her about being such a self-centred bitch. He stood now in the corner of the gym and scanned the faces of those mingling around the buffet table. Dave was hungry. But approaching the buffet table might draw too much attention. He was a Carnage family traitor and an X Corp destroyer. Best at this occasion to keep a low profile – too many untested young rookies. He didn’t want to incur their wrath and end up looking a fool. He looked up to Ben’s old office on the second floor. It was from there that he would watch his young trainees. A figure sat in the window looking back at Harley. It was like looking at the ghost of Benjamin Carnage. The figure beckoned Harley and he realised it was Jonny sat in his wheelchair. He headed upstairs slowly to meet with his ex partner. Why did he feel he’d have to explain his recent actions to him? “I’ve been watching Meltdown…” Jonny informed him as he poured two glasses of whiskey. Dave stared at him as if waiting to be reprimanded by the headmaster. “All I’m gonna say Dave, is that I’m not surprised.” “You think I owe you some explan…” “I don’t want to hear about it!” Jonny cut him off. “You’re a son of a bitch and you’ll always be a son of a bitch. If you think you can get away with running that Devil’s Own army of yours then so be it. But expect to get burned, my friend. What comes around goes around.” Jonny took a swig of whiskey. Dave followed suit. Both men sat for a few moments before Dave spoke up. “I was surprised to be invited.” “You broke my father’s heart. You know that? But still…He’d have wanted you to be here.” “He was a good man. I owe him a lot.” Dave stated. Silently, he attributed his real fame – joining the EWA and later the WWA – to himself and the crippling of the man sat in front of him. But it certainly wasn’t the right place to say. Jonny sat at his father’s old desk nursing the empty whiskey glass. He seemed agitated. As if he had something to say, but didn’t know how to breach the subject. Eventually, he spoke, “…You know how he died?” Dave honestly didn’t know. Or care. He was dead. Dead is dead. “Heart attack?” A shot in the dark. “He was murdered…Shot dead right here at his desk.” Now that did surprise Harley. He sat upright in his seat. “What was it? A robbery? Some smack head or something?” “That’s what the Police think.” “And you?” “…I think my father could have handled a mugger or a drug addict easily.” That much was true, Dave thought, the man was in his late sixties but boy was he tough. Of course, this would mean there was something more foul afoot. “So who do you think it was?” Dave asked bluntly. Jonny stared at the Extreme Machine before letting out a short laugh. He poured two more glasses of whiskey. “Listen to us. We sound like freakin’ kids from Scooby Doo or something!” Harley had to admit it sounded pretty lame. But then again, so many rookies came and went from a facility like this. Perhaps there was a disagreement over money. Maybe Ben refused to train someone (as he often did). Perhaps a dispute over who could go on tour? The possibilities were endless. Dave sipped on his whiskey before adding; “well you must have some idea then. Who’d you think did it?” “Let’s just say I have a few rookies in mind. I’m keeping a close eye on them. I’m also going through Dad’s records. I want to know if there were any cause for dispute – fees, bookings, stuff like that.” “Well, let me know if there’s anything I can do, Jon. To be honest, it sounds like bullshit to me. But if you find any solid proof…” “I’ll let you know.” Dave left his whiskey glass on the desk as he headed for the door. As he touched the handle, Jonny called him back. “Dave. Stay clear of Wendy. I don’t want any more upset.” “I hear ya.” Dave headed back downstairs and tried to mingle with a few of the guests. He talked to a few family members, but the conversation seemed forced. The Carnage’s now held a lot of ill will towards Dave Harley. All except one, of course – Jonny’s younger sister, Wendy. She flirted with Dave at every available opportunity. Harley wasn’t interested. He’d had his fun with Wendy as a kid. Since then he’d been the Playboy Mansion and had since become rather picky about his choice of women. She still looked beautiful; enough to momentarily stun him earlier at the funeral. But she was still a psychopath. After making his excuses, Harley left the gym and hailed a cab back to his hotel. All that grieving had made him tired. He’d paid his last respects to a man he admired, but now he was tiring of making conversation with the worthless mourners at the gym. He made a call to his Personal Secretary in the cab, telling her to cancel any further funeral appointments he had between now and 2008. He’d forgotten how boring they were. He quickly zoomed up to his room when he arrived at the hotel. Minutes later, he was sat up in bed with a bag of Doritos watching The Sopranos on HBO. He made a note to himself to dress more like Tony Soprano at Devil’s Own meetings – might inspire confidence… Then he drifted off to sleep. And he dreamt. He dreamt of his younger days with Wendy. He dreamt of how she’d manipulate him in to doing things. He dreamt of her face during arguments, when she’d tell Harley his name would amount to nothing. She’d tell him the only reason he was a star was because he was hanging around the Carnage Family. She made him feel worthless. He dreamt of sitting in the locker room on the telephone minutes after crippling Jonny. He heard her screaming down the phone. All he could do was smile – Now who’s better than Carnage? And then he woke up with a revolver pressed against his forehead. “Wakey wakey Davey.” Christ, he thought, she is a complete and total nutjob. Should he hurl abuse at her? Should he quickly roll out of the way? He decided his best option would be to lay there. If he tried to speak, he’d reveal the terror in his voice. If he tried to escape, he’d expose the huge stain he’d just created on the bed. A no win scenario. “I’ve been waiting for you to come back for so long, Dave. I watch you every week on Meltdown and hope that they’ll announce a date in Detroit. I spoke to Jonny and begged him to tell me next time he saw you, but he wouldn’t. So I realised…there was only one way to bring my Extreme Machine back to me…by killing my father, his mentor.” Wow, this girl was so crazy she’d kill for me, Harley thought. This just proves my point that once you’ve rode the Harley, you settle for nothing less! She slowly climbed on top of his trembling body and pressed the gun against his face. “I saw the way you looked at me today, Dave. You looked at me like I’m some crazy fan. Well I’m not! I wanted to tell you…I understand why you crippled Jonny! You did it for me! It was a wedding present to me!” What?! Dave needed to do something fast! His eyes darted maniacally around the room. “You see? You’re doing it again! You look frightened of me! I’LL MAKE YOU FRIGHTENED!!!” Wendy cocked the barrel of the gun and started to squeeze on the trigger… …Dave put his pen down and sighed. “Man, what a crappy promo. You can’t have me die at the end! I’m the freakin’ Extreme Machine!” He scrunches the piece of paper up and looks over at Leviathan who sits reading a book. “You know what the main problem with a promo like that is? Sure, it’s unrealistic, but the main problem is that it has nothing to do with wrestling! It’d make a great movie of the week, but it tells me nothing about Salaok – my next opponent from PTC. It doesn’t even mention him for Christ’s sake! Nah, forget this story crap. I’m gonna stick to what Dave Harley does best – talk trash and wrestle. “After all, this is a wrestling show!”
