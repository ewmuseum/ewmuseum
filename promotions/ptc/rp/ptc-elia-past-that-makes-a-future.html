A past that makes a future

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 5, 1987 Friday night… It was a dark night in the streets of Texas. Blasting winds and a fierce powerful rain was coming down, knocking whatever was in it’s way down. Banging against the glass that was inside the houses and buildings near by. The dark clouds brought an even bigger evil. The golf ball sized hail came pouncing down from the sky, breaking a few near by car windows that were next to the tall brick faded building. “The weather man did say the storm was coming.” The strange voice said in the near by building. Inside was were all the people wear. Standing were the rain and mother nature couldn’t get to them. There seem to be a massive amount of people inside the place. Crowding what little space that they could get. “Has anyone seen my little girl?” The lonely young woman said in the corner. “No I haven’t. What is her name?” The bartender of the bar replied. “Her name is Elia…” the woman said. Both of them looked around the building, trying to find the girl somehow. She began to walk around, looking around every person that stood in her way. She passed by the table’s that were set up in the middle of the place for all the passed out and really drunk people that he sometimes got. As she walked through them the weather blasted open the door, letting the nasty rain bounce off the floor that the people stood on. “HELP ME!” The man screamed as he stood in the doorway. Long wet dark black haired man stood in the doorway as he held the small figure of a young girl in his two arms. A few of the strong farmer men grabbed the door and shut it behind him. He sat the female on a table that was just a few feet away. The girl looked familiar to the woman as she ran right for her, screaming at the top of her lungs. “NO! ELIA!” Screaming at the world it seemed as she grabbed the girl by her shoulder. “Help her please.” “We are miss. Just please calm down. Where did you find her Roy?” The man standing by the woman asked. “Down by the river. She was half way in the river almost drowning.” His lips said as he took off his coat that was soaking wet. Not even a pin could drop without hearing it as the other man began to breath into her lungs through her mouth. No life was coming out and the woman began to pry to the god as she got on her knee’s. He pressed on her chest, trying to power life right into her but it seemed like it wasn’t helping. He blow into her mouth once more, getting shocked by the girl raising up quickly off the table and looking upwards. “AHHHH!” Elia said as she fell back down. “Get me some water now…” The man said, screaming back at the people near the bar. “Oh god Elia, just lay back down.” The woman said as she grabbed her hand. “I’m here for you.” Elia froze on the table as she just couldn’t move it seemed. Frozen in her eyes it seemed as well. The man grabbed the water from someone, placing the cup on her lips as he began to pour a little down her mouth. “Come on baby, drink it.” He said. “Come on…” “Come on Elia…drink it…Please…” *************************************** May 28, 2005 Friday night… The water ran fast in the tub. Filling it up quickly and to the top. As it got to the top, a hand pressed in on the handle, stopping the water and the tub where it was. A young girl, naked to the eyes, her skin as fresh and silky looking as any young model, began to dip into the tub. Slowly and patently as she could even do as the steam ran from the hot water. Her hair hung from her head, laying across the broad slim shoulders that she had on her body. The girl leaned back against the wall of the tub, right before she slid down, submersed underneath the burning water that she had ran. But her silence and aloneness was coming to an end as she laid there. “You in there Elia?” A voice said on the other side of the door. Elia raised up from the water, hearing the voice that screamed at her. She brushed the water from her wet hair, trying to get her bearings in check before replying. “Yes…” She replied. “Hurry up. We are leaving in a few hours. We don’t want to be late.” The man said. She shook her head and leaned back against the water once again, this time not sliding down but rather looking away to the window that was up a few feet from the tub. The moon shined through the glass and right to her face as she pressed up from the water. Elia was shy sometimes and right now she was showing it. She grabbed the towel that was hanging next to the tub before she tossed it around her skinny body. She didn’t take to long to get out of there, walking towards her room as she did. A entire mess, almost something that a tornado would do. There were cloths on the floor, mixed with a few papers that seemed to fall from the dresser in the corner. She tossed the towel to the floor as well as she shut the door behind her, closing it from the screamed that were coming from down the hallway. She grabbed the cut off shorts that were on the bed. Pulling them on as she got dressed as quickly as she got out of the tub. Her hair was a mess. Though she didn’t seem to care as she looked at the mirror. “God I hate today.” She said as she turned from the mirror. She walked out of the room and towards the noise as her brother was standing there, an angry and concerned look on his face it seemed. “We need to talk.” He said, pressing her towards the hallway. “Justin took me what was going on. How come you didn’t tell me?” He eyes widened up like she was going to die. Her uncle was still missing and she was to blame. That was what he was asking and Elia seemed like she knew. She let out a deep breath before answering. “Because I didn’t want anyone else to get hurt. That is why.” She replied. “How could I of gotten hurt? I’m the one that looked out after you when you almost died. I’m the one that helped feed you, watched over you, and did everything with you when you were recovering. You should of told me.” He said, pointing at her as he did. “What do you want me to say Kevin. I didn’t want you to die like Uncle Johnson might. Besides, that was then and this is now.” She said, brushing the wet hair away from her eyes. “Remember, I’m not a young kid anymore. I’m a strong woman now and got it all under control.” “You got it under control huh?” Kevin said as he looked away from her with his angry eyes. “You got nothing under control till you get him back.” “Damn you!” She replied with a said voice. “Damn you. How can you judge me like this. I’m trying and failing but you don’t care.” Kevin’s face turned quickly towards her, his eyes looking right at her as she got off the wall and walked away from him, walking towards her room that she had came from. He followed her as she walked into the room. She went to slam the door shut but he caught it, pressing his way right into the room. “I don’t care…did you say that?” Kevin said as she sat on the bed. “I care more then you it seems.” “JUST SHUT UP!” Elia said, throwing a shirt at Kevin in the process. “I’ve done everything I possibly can to get him back. From tossing out everything Uncle Johnson taught me to dancing at a…” She said, pausing before she finished. Kevin’s eyes and mouth didn’t tell or say a thing. In the family you never did two things. You never danced for ANY REASON at a strip club and you always helped people. Elia dropped her head, grabbing it with both hands as she began to cry. Tears coming from the openings of her hands as Kevin sat down on the side of the bed. “Elia…” Kevin said as his hand rubbed the middle of her back. “I think it’s time we went to the police.” Elia didn’t respond right away. She sat there, rubbing her eyes from underneath the hands that she held her head with. “I hate my life…”
