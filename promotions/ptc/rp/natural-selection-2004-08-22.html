Natural Selection

Date: 2004-08-22 _______________ ::[Introduction]:: ______________ The lines had been drawn and the masses had made their choices. Respectively, men and women of great talent branched off into great nations of strength, wealth and prominance. Now, the chance to prove who amoung these people has learned to fight, for each of them have chosen to fight for their survival. People believe that survival is a necessity, a compulsary urge within people to adapt, outlast and out smart their nemesis. Yet, the word survival has already begun to change meaning for these people, it's no longer about standing up to fight for what you believe in, it's about being willing to do anything for their beliefs. Each of these poor souls would walk into war with the thought of victory dwelling within their minds and the need to survive to live their thoughts. This is when desperate measures do indeed call for desperate actions, these warriors march under the banner of their great nation. Each of them has become focused, determined, seemingly possessed to become the bitter-sweet victor of this brutallity that shall soon take place. To endure such a physical strain, to bring pain and suffering to others before they do so to you, this is what the survival has become about. Now, the pride of many great leagues rest firmly on the shoulders of Four Man teams; these team's trust and fate rest squarely on each other. Without the strength of trust, honor, there can be no advantage as a team. Individually these beings are weak against the opposing nations. Together, the warriors of the Winds would be unstopable. There would be many great individuals who would oppose the great nation of the Four Winds and of these individuals, none of them will be enough to tople the dynasty inwhich the Winds has begun to cement. A place in time and history will now be devoted to the legacy of a great nation, the one that can send it's men into Mount St. Hells and have them return victorious. Some may return beatten, but that was far better than not returning at all; showing up to the Four Winds with a loss under the belt would be a suicide mission. Failure would be unacceptable to these warriors, their honour, their pride, it was was worth far more than this mere victory and this is what each of them had set out to prove. For Darek, this mission was nothing more than another day and another chance to revert the lives of sinners. Under the watch of his Saviour and Lord in Heaven, he would move quietly into the PrimeTime like an assassain, only to purify it of it's evils. His mission was clear now, Darek knew that he would need to cleanse the souls of the damned, to bring them into the light of God and he would not stop until he suceeded. The PrimeTime Central was in for a surprise and none of them could expect what was coming. Darek wanted nothing more than to spread the love and light of God. But everywhere he travelled he saw the land was plagued with sin, destroyed by those who tried to be God. Darek coldly remembered his own days of repent and under the thought of his own actions, he mercilessly brought purification to those who sinned. This would be no different now, the PrimeTime was being played, and Darek was holding BlackJack. Though it would be unintentional on his behalf, his destiny holds a great success, a new beginning for the once damned soul and this Survival would mark the beginning of his journey to live his destiny. Forging a place in the very foundation of the Four Winds, Darek knew his legacy and that of the wretched being he once was would long linger over the Winds, as a symbol to those who would dare oppose the great land. As an Angel who would watch over the Four Winds, Darek wanted nothing more than to pass his trials and suceed in becoming and Elder in the highest Choir of Angels. He devoted his time to protecting the innocent and defending his new home, the same place that now dispatched him to fight, to cleanse the others of their sins. The Four Winds acedemy had produced many young stars, some of which had already begun to shine in the PrimeTime, but none so brightly as Darek would. It was his destiny, he would leave a legacy with this world that no one would soon forget. People had become famous, other made their mark, and now Darek was going to lead them all and become the living embodiment of Christ. He vowed to live his life according to the word and life of the lord, now was his opportunity to show the awesome power he possessed. ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ I have to take a good look at what I am soon to be up against. The battle has begun so to has the war of words and threats. What could I say that would motivate these impudent morons? Each of them hiding behind their false sense of security, when really their insecurity is so obvious to me. They fear the unknown, the were chosen but were volunteers and respectively had to be amoung the top of their league. Now, Each of them... Smiley. Munson. Epic. Nilsson. The men who stand to oppose the justice of God, I am his right hand and in one fell swoop each of them will fall judged by the almighty himself. One by one they will be purified and their soul will become morally clean, to stand before me and my mission, would be crime in itself. These four men, each of them destined for a greater purpose, to be the stepping stone for guides light to be shared and spread into the PrimeTime. No longer will I stand by and watch miscreants such as these proclaim themselves the leaders of the bright future. Their ultimate purpose at Survival would not be to survive, but to be cleansed from their wrongs. With warriors such as Biddle, Fnord and Jerms the team from Core is going to need alot more help than a swede, a crock, a freak and a steriod injector. It's been rumored that Smiley gets his uncanny ability to compete from a sreum that he uses to help boost his motabolism. This serum allows him to accomplish feats he never could as a mild mannered interviewer... Funny how things work out, Clark Kent was a mild mannered interviewer and he was also Superman. How about Peter Parker? A calm, quiet, brilliant prodigy that soon became Spiderman. Coincidence that you would find something such as this in the world of deadly combat? Probably not. Despite this being a way form beings to test their strength, others to play mind games, others to hide a terrible secret, this remains a business and in this business gimmicks sell. Obviously Core has taken this to factor when enlisted a fool named Smiley of all people into a war for Survival. For me, this will be nothing more than a chance to rectify the wrong doings, the alteration of his God given form... Smiley you have chosen to play God and for that, you have been judged. The time draws so close I can taste it, the time for your judgement will bring your hinsight into perspective. As for Munson? The man's a bit of a savage. A little on the brutish side he chooses to mow through his competition with sheer power. THis is something I personally have been looking forward to, even when the Dark Angel possessed my now cleansed soul. Munson has been a staple name around the PrimeTime, to bring this monster to his knees, crying for the mercy of God would be the satisfaction of my work. Soon he shall see the light, he will become subject to judgement from these two hands. A desperate attempt to bring fame into his life after a subjectful past, Munson began fighting at an older age. His dedication to the sport is seemingly lacking, what had he done to get this far. What mountain did Munson climb? Did Munson scare Tessa Windsor and Seymour Almasy into hiding, merely by opposing them? Come to think of it, Munson doesn't deserve half the credit I give him. When he and I are glaring into each others eyes; mine seeing hatred, rage, anger, and even fear, his seeing determination, the will of the lord; I will make him realise just how real the Modern Messiah is, soon the great Munson will genuflex in the light of Darek DeAngleo. And Adolph Nilsson? I can't even begin to fathom of where to begin with this embarassment of a man. His dedication to Bjorn is like that of Bert and Ernie, inseperable. Without the one another, each of them is lost and Adolph is walking into unchartered territory now. The winds hae risen and now the hurricane is about to come crashing in. Fast, angery, unforginving, furious, we will hit and when the dust settles and the smoke clears, it will be the warriors from the Four Winds that are left standing. There can be nothing to comfort your misguided soul, he seeks a new life in Football from his expiriences playing a video game.... that proves the man has the mental capacity of a three year old, standing in the same ring with him will drop the collective intellegence quotiant of the group by atleast fifty percent. The man does have some degree of talent and I can respect that, but without his life partner, I can forsee Adolph falling like the Third Reich. Matt Epic is all that was left to stand between myself and the glorification of climbing Mount St. Hell. He a champion in cW and has gladly chosen to march onto the field of battle to represent his federation. For that, I applaud the man in both his courage and stupidty, should any of them known that the redemption of God was going to come forth from Darek DeAngleo, none of them would have chosen to stand before my words, my goal. Now, I have average men who have become big names in small leagues trying to holster their reputation by defeating the light of God. Matt Epic could never stand next to me in a comparison of strength, psyche and intellegence, simply put he is the lesser of us. When the time comes to prove this, I will and hands down the Four Winds will prove to be the superior of the leagues in the PrimeTime. This is something that Matt Epic is going to learn the hard way, just as the entire PrimeTime will. _____________ ::[The Story]:: _____________ "Forgive me father for I have sinned." The sliding partition glides across the wooden frame, making a scratching sound. The shadowy outline of a being sitting in the next chamber is seen. Darek leans back against the wall and closes his eyes. "I have had impure thoughts, my mind dwells on a mission that I must embark upon, one that wants me to see the death and demise of several men. I know these thoughts deem me a wicked being, but I am truly a lamb of God, I must cleanse my mind and soul of the hatred." Stretching his feet to the corners of the booth, Darek allows his head to hang ominously. His arms find their way into a folded position across his cheast and a slight sigh escapes his mouth. The dark stained oak booth was located in a local church in a small city outside of Calgary Alberta. St. Basils chruch in the rural seventy thousand person city of Lethbridge had provided a memory of Darek's past that he could never forget. This would be the church that he had received his baptism, it was then Darek was cleansed of his sins and given a morally clean slate. Ocassionally he returned to give confession for his horrid thoughts. Darek's thoughts had become actions and he fell away from the church, away from God. His soul had been taken captive by two demons, the one of a great warrior Dominic Angelus and the Dark Angel of Death. Now, outstretched in the small cubed confessional booth. His eyes opened widely, dropping slowly, his chin and eyes gave Darek a shocked expression imprinted on his face. Turning to face the screen, Darek tilts his head, face still etched in shock. It had been a long time since he had confessed his sins, his poisonous thoughts and this time, it seemed different. As though someone wasn't listening. Darek leaned closer to the screen, trying to peer through the woven mesh. Heavy breathing eminateds from the other side but Darek cannot see anything other than the shadow of a person. "Father," he began, "are you all right?" Nothing but that constant heavy breathing. The shadow didn't seem to move, Darek moved away from the screen and sat upright in his small booth, listening. The breathing sounded so odd, not as though the man was gasping for air. But as though he was afraid, tilting his head back, Darek took a deep sniff in. It was clear, as the smell of fear travelled into Darek's body, he knew something was awry. Quickly his hand reaches out to the latch on the small door, but Darek stops dead in his tracks. "Don't go." Pulling his hand away from the door, Darek nods instinctively knowing full well the father would not be able to see him. "I will not," he replies immediately with concern upon his voice. "What is it father." More heavy breathing, "Forgive me, for I have sinned." Darek rises into a straight upright sitting position. Facing the front of the booth, his head turns back to the screen as those words have been said. Define the word irony, and Darek would give this to you as an example. Listening intentively to the breathing, and staggering words of the man next to him. Darek places his hand on the screen and tries to help how he can. "What if I were to tell you something," the man stops for a second, almost awaiting a quick response. "I have devoted my life to a cause that has no value to me anymore." "What do you mean?" The words spill from his mouth without even thinking first. Darek glares at the screen, the shadow begins to move slowly but then comes to a stop again. "God doesn't hear our prayers son." He let's the cat out of the bag with relative ease, he must have been convinced. "I'm not even certain he exits anymore." Taken back by these words, Darek remembered saying them himself at one low point in his life. Everything seemed to flash through his mind like a video: From signing on as a pro wrestler, to losing his soul to Dark Angel and Dominic, to losing his family. He had no pleasing memmories come to mind. He had recently devoted his time and attention to Patricia Anderson, she was dead to him now. After all that she had done to him, betraying him to her fiancee, destroying Shadows Ascension, manipulating the KnightMare Brigade, Darek had enough of Patricia's games and knew it was near time to bring an end to the demon that plagued her soul. First and foremost, he had to focus on now, the war that lingers. Darek was ready for anyone and anything, his ability to bring the light of God to the world was uncanny and he wanted nothing more than to clean the world. The Purification of those who sinned would be the justice of the lord through Darek. Jesus had died for man's sins and now man must face God's judgement to understand their sins, to be purified. Once cleansed by Darek DeAngleo, they would be able to start morally clean. Darek thought of Matt Epic, Smiley, Munson and Adolph Nilsson, he knew the battle would not be easy but his mission could not be failed. Once these men had owned up to Darek DeAngleo, he would seemingly move on to climb atop Mount St. Hell with his colleagues. K-CHACK... Snapping out of a trance like state, Darek opens his eyes at the familiar sound. The same heavy breathing, the smell of fear even thicker than before. "Father?" No Response. The silence filled the small booths and Darek sat impatiently, concerned for the well being of the desperate man on the other side of the wall. Bringing his face up against the screen, Darek tries to get a look at what exactly is going on. The shadow is rocking back and forth, holding something. There was poor illumantion in this area of the church, for it provided shelter and confidentiality to the confessions of many. Darek sat in the darkness, feeling trapped, trying to help. The walls of the small oak booth seemed to enclose Darek in a prison. Darek pushes himself close against the screen as he could. "Father are you still there?" Darek beckons at the shadow, knowing the man was still there. The breathing slows, "did you not understand what I told you? God doesn't exist my son, there is no reason to live. Not on this forsaken plane of existence." The shadow's arm moves an object up towards it's head. Darek squints, trying to make out the object but cannot. "What can I do to help you?" Darek had stood in God's presence, his whole life and death was a miracle. From being an innocent nine year old child that died in a storm, to becomeing the Angel of Death, and now Darek had hoped to write the wrongs in his life by passing the trials set before him. Taking another sniff, Darek had quickly realised that he knew this smell of fear... In all his years as the Angel of Death, there was something that death had brought out of people. The putrid stench of fear, the fear of dying and that smell now filled the confessional. Darek was not afraid to die, he lived in the light of God, he knew he had a hand to help him along the way. But what of the other man, the father had begun to believe God didn't exist this made him fear death. There would be nothing waiting for him after he pulled the trigger of the Dessert Eagle .50 he had in his mouth. The smell was so wretched now, in the moments while the discouraged lamb of God sweatily fumbled with the gun. "They say God works in mysterious ways," Darek begins, "as a one time follower of his light you should know this. Why all the mystery, why all the shrowd? What is the big secret? There are so many questions about faith, about religion, about God, is it possible that no religion has it right? Perhaps it was meant to be this way, if devine intervention was to occur what would mankind have to offer the lord?" Pausing for a moment, Darek notices the breathing of the man next to him has slowed slightly. "All that the best of us have to offer is the same as the next man, woman or child, our love and faith. Things in life go wrong for a reason, when the day of judgement comes my friend, it is those who endure these crisis' and maintain their love, faith and belief in the Lord that will be rewarded with everlasting life in utopia. You are a strong willed individual, a man of God, though wrongs have happened to you instead of turning away from your faith, turn to it for the answer. Look inside yourself and believe that you can survive this ordeal." For a long time there is nothing but an awkward silence. Pulling the gun out of his mouth, the father sets it beside him on the bench. "Your words come as though you have had lived such a tragic incident and managed to pull through." If only he knew half the truth, Darek had fought and endured more than most would pressume, but that was not what was on his mind. He was mostly worried about the well being of this poor soul next to him. No longer did he feel caged in the confessional, instead he felt the goodness of his words fill the area. "All that matters is that you pull through, that you hold your head high and believe... wait, KNOW that God will always be there to guide you. Just as people of this community know that you will be here to guide them. Your work, your life, it's special to more people than just you, it affects this whole area, the whole world. Whatever your situation is, you can beat it." Reaching out towards the confessional door, Darek turns the latch and pushes the door open. The bright colored lights from the stained glass windows now flows into the darkness. Darek rises to his feet and is about to leave the oak sanctuary. "Thank you." "No Father, thank you." Darek stops and looks back at the screen. "This talk with you has even opened my eyes as well. With people like you in this world, I know we can overcome the odds." With those words said, Darek left the booth. The door closed after him and the room was covered in complete darkness. The sliding partition between the two enclosed areas is still open. Darek looks back at the confessional and nods in approval before passing the last row of pues and heading into the lobby area of the church. Pushing the large light stained mahogony doors open, he steps out into the light of the world. Knowing that he can survive, that he HAS survived all this time. heeh heeh heeh... The heavy, quick breathes soon continue. Not everyone can endure the pain and hardships that life offers. Even the most faithful lose faith, even the strongest lose strength, and good men do die. The father reaches down next to him and places the cold steel barrell of the gun back into his mouth. His finger rubs along the smooth trigger as his sweat and tears mix together while rolling down his face. The words of the man that sat next to him were so true, but the father lost his reason to believe... BUCKAM! The sound echoed throughout the empty church, through the halls, out the winds and even into the streets. Darek stopped dead in his tracks and turned back to St. Basil's church. His face went white as a ghost as his feet slowly brought him closer and closer back the ominous sound. Standing before the doors of the church, Darek pulls them open and walks in once again. Everything is silent, nothing moves, and a cold air seems to fill the room as Darek feels the bumps on his arms run. Brushing past the last row of pues, Darek passes the cubed booth that he had sat in and stands before the other door. Reaching down to the latch, he turns and pulls... Blood is splattered all over the confessional and the body of the white colared priest falls onto the floor. blood flowing freely onto the carpet from the large hole in the backside of his head. Darek jumps back, not in fear but shock staring at the body of a man he thought was saved. It was now that Darek realised that not everyone was meant to live and not everyon could survive. _____________ ::[The Moral]:: _____________ Survival. Survival of the fittest. This war will prove to the world just exactly who was meant to survive, which nation was meant to lead. The world has always been a cruel cold place, but it had been made this way by the corrupt souls of mankind and their sins. Earth had become Hell, and surviving in this Hell was not an easy task. Nonetheless facing some of the strongest, fiercest warriors in the world. Yet eagerly Darek walked out of the Four Winds into battle. He had survived his childhood only to become stronger, he had survived his possession, and now he was determined to climb Mount St. Hell at all costs. He and the team of the Winds would not be taken lightly. This Survival war is about Natural Selection, it's time for the weak to crumble and the strong to thrive. Only Darek has embarked upon this war with a mission to cleanse the souls of these beings, time will only tell if he was meant to Survive.
