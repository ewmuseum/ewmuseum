Too Many Concerns

16 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-16 {open scene} The first round of GTT4 was a week away yet the concept of it made Arch Angel’s heart race like it was just a minute away. 194 men, one winner it was clanging at Angel’s mental structure like a nightmare. It would be a miracle if the anticipation alone didn’t drop Angel before his opponent had a chance. The gym was dimly lit as Arch Angel pounded away at his heavy bag trying to focus on the spots his trainer taught him. HARD Right, HARD Left, JAB! Angel executed a short combo on the bag envisioning his first round opponent in front of him. To no avail Dunn’s face appeared bold at the top notch of the bag as Angel gave a heavy right hook. SLAM! Angel grabbed the bag as he heard the door to his gym close. “Still avoiding that talk we planned, huh?” The woman took a couple steps forward and rested on the exercise machine. “Avoiding it only makes it worst, you know that.” Angel replied. “Just waiting for the right time.” Arch Angel glowered past his punching bag across the room at his fiancé, Rachel. He didn't like the willful tone that crept into her voice these days; he didn't like the way her blue eyes spread wide at him, the way her lips tensed, the way she drew herself up, tall and slim, an arch of determination. The damn girl had nothing but an attitude ever since GTT4 started, and that was the trouble. Rachel faced up to his scowl, shaky as she was. She knew what he was thinking, because he had told her enough times, she was a headstrong girl without a brain cell to her name; her college education had been a waste; worse than that, it had pumped her full of crazy ideas, had knocked her sense of values upside down. “What will you do when you go out there and hurt yourself again?” Rachel questioned. “How will you provide for our son whom is only a couple months from being born?” Arch Angel gave a heavy sigh and delivered another hard right to the punching bag. Knocking the bag around somehow alleviated some of the tension and anger that was building up in Angel’s body. Rachel shook her head in disappointment, “You may be able to beat your problems out of your head but what about my feelings, have you even stopped to take how I feel into consideration?” “RACHEL PLEASE! I’m already facing enough issues without having to worry about you lashing out at me. I’m going in there and squaring off against Joshua Dunn in the first round, he is a former UWF Heavyweight Champion for crying out loud!” Angel replied. Rachel’s eyes glazed over a bit as he crept towards her. “Just imagine if I win this tournament. We will be able to have everything we ever wanted... we could provide the life for our son that we never had the opportunity to have.” “IF!? That’s all you can give me Angel is hopes and dreams. IF isn’t good enough, IF is a chance at hurting yourself, IF is a chance at ruining us! I just can’t do an if Angel so if you go and do this just like you do everything else you want, you better think about me. And IF you win maybe you will have a home!” Rachel exclaimed. “It’s like that?” Angel said under his breath. SLAM! Rachel gave no reply as she exited the gym holding her stomach. “AAHHH!” Angel screamed as he gave one last blow to the bag in front of him. “There is no IF in my game!” Angel was exhausted bewteen yelling at Rachel and boxing with the heavy bag. Taking deeps breaths Angel grabbed his duffel bag and exited through the back door where his Honda CBR awaited him with a stealthy new black paint glimmering in the moonlight. Angel hopped on and left smoke and tread as he speeded off hoping to once again vent some steam. Speeding in and out of traffic thought of Rachel and GTT4 battle through his mind for first in line. Trying to block them out Angel squints his eyes hard and clears his mind. "Ahh silence at last." Angel sighed. BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR! Angels eyes lashed open as a car drove through the intersaection where Angel had the red light and almost ran. Angel smirked a little "Man so much for peace and quiet." Looking for a place to rest his head and settle down a bit he pulled into a little bar at the corner of the road. C-R-E-E-K! The door made a funny noise as Angel entered the bar and took a seat on the nearest stool. "What can I get ya hun?" The bartender questioned. "Whatever makes the noise go away." Angel swiftly answered. "Whatever makes the noise go away." "One of those days dear?" The bartender asked. "One of those weeks!" Angel replied. I hate all this damn stress and tension, my girlfriends nagging at me, my trainers nagging at me, and one of the biggest events of my life is only days away! I can't take all this, I wish they would just leave me alone and let me sort these things out myself." "Sounds as if you need someone to talk to?" The bartender implied. You know my nephew is Dr. Phil. Angel laughed as he took a big gulp of his beer. "Well let me tell you how it all started..." Angel lead off as the screen dissolved into black. {end scene}
