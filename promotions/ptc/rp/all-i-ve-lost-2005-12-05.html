All I've Lost…

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 [Clear blue skies with a hot sun beating down on a dry, burnt highway. A dragonfly flies around, flipping and twirling through the air, living its life knowing it has the ability to get off the ground and fly. It stops to rest on a small stone that sits in a dirt road parking lot. The dragonfly rubs each wing against each other and lowers itself to make a nice take off. Pounce! The dragonfly, now bent in an oddly, futile way, is attached to the inside beak of a crow. The crow tosses its head back and forces the dragonfly down its throat. It flies off leaving dirt and dust from the ground to fill the air. Once it clears we can see pub or diner of some sort, a wooden establishment, on the front it reads: "Betties". Looks like the place is falling apart, most of the windows don’t even have glass in them, saloon style doors (one side missing), and the step up onto the farmers porch in front has been replaced with cinder blocks because the wood must not have been strong enough. A door is slammed shut and a man with a pair of jeans and a white wife-beater tank, all greased up walks out of an outhouse, adjusting his private area very inappropriately and walks into the diner. In the lot there are only a couple of cars, a piece of junk pick-up truck with the hood up, rusted paint, and both the front and rear bumpers are missing. Towards the back of the lot sitting next to a field plow is a sports car, yellow with a white racing stripe, but there are no tires. Flats are lying all over the place along with other junk parts. A car then rolls into the lot, a Mustang convertible, it pulls up to the old-fashioned gas pumps, the driver hops over the door and yells back to the passenger who we really haven’t gotten a good look at yet.] Driver: Just fill her up; I’ll see if they can any tables open. [The passenger opens the door and gets out of the car and starts pumping gas. In the meantime the driver has gone to the inside of the diner. He walks in and hesitates before walking slowly to the counter.] Driver: Alright. [Inside the pub it’s also very old-fashioned and not updated. Old style diner, a decent amount of seats with a long counter with stools in front of the kitchen. There are a few folks sitting at the counter, some at some tables. It’s the cliché "hickville" town folk inside, eating eggs and drinking cider. There’s an older man sitting at the counter, wearing an older pair of overalls that look like they haven’t been washed in weeks. His Irish hat rests on his large glasses which rest on his rather large nose, which sits above his grey prickly mustache. Sipping a cup of coffee he glances at the driver of that mustang, easily noticing that he’s not from around these parts. The driver turns toward the man who has his eyes locked on him.] Driver: Hello. [The driver turns his head back towards the counter where there is a rather large woman in a classic waitress outfit, stains all over her apron. The driver pulls a folded map out of his pocket, unfolds it and lays it on the counter.] Driver: Yeah, uhm, I think we took a wrong turn about ten miles back east, we’re supposed to be heading towards- Waitress: What you be having there is a map of ole Tennessee. Driver: Thanks, I can see that…we’re trying to get to- Waitress: You’s a in Mississippi. Driver: Yes, I saw the sign, but what we’re trying to do is reach Nashville. Waitress: Yeah, but you’s a in Mississippi. [He just stares at the waitress in complete awe. Then the little bell on the saloon doors ring as the passenger from the car walks in, catching everybody’s attention from his eye catching features: the long hair, large size and clothing that one can easily tell that he’s not from the area. We can now see that its wrestling superstar, Damien Ryan. He walks in a takes the driver by the arm.] Damien Ryan: Don’t worry about it, come on, let’s get something to eat. [The two sit down and take a look at the paper menus in front of them.] Agent: You know, this tournament could blow your career wide open. I mean, you have been able to get your name out there with FAW, NGW, and HATE, but still this could be huge. You run through the first couple round, destroying the competition. You’ll upset everyone, and you’ll be known as the underdog and you’ll change everyone’s mind…. ************ [Inside Ryan’s head.] As he continues to talk his voice just fades away, I can’t seem to concentrate on his words because, honestly, his voice annoys me. Stupid asshole, thinks he can get off and get some exposure off of me. I hate agents, no matter how much of their own time they put into you, they still don’t mean nothing to me. This guy could choke on his food and I’d probably sit there and watch it happen. This will be my first PTC tournament, but I’m not nervous. I have nothing to be nervous about. If I was facing Ducky again, that might be something to fret about. I don’t know my opponent in the first round that well, whatever, I’m just going to do what I always do and that’s go out there and show the world that I am the best superstar ever to come out of the Canadian borders. Am I all talk? Maybe. I guess Logan Caine is just going to have to listen to me go on and on about great I am, take up all his time making him listen to me and maybe bore him to death. Once again, whatever, I’ll come that date and beat you so badly you’ll wish you had stayed home in your mother’s arms. ***************** [Ryan wakes up] Agent: …and then you leave the champion, you know what I mean? Damien? Are you listening? Ryan: Yeah. Agent: I'm going to run to their bathroom real quick. ****************** [Ryan once again drifts off into his own world.] What's the point of me doing this anyways? What did I get myself into? Do I really think I can defeat some of the best superstars in PTC? Hell, will I even get past the first round, I mean, I do have some decent competition here. Logan Caine, from what I hear is pretty good talent. Hell, he could stomp all over me for what I know. I've never seen him till recently, who am I to make judgements? I've never wrestled him before, been in the same organization with him before, never even had a conversation with him before. Ah, Christ! What am I doing here! I just traveled out of my way, away from HATE to catch up with the rest of PTC for this tournament! I think I'm taking an awful risk here…. [caught by his agent, back from the bathroom, calling his name] ******************* Agent: Hellllllllllloooooooooo. Dude, what world are you living in? Ryan: Sorry man. Agent: You're not getting seconds thoughts about this are you? Ryan: No. Agent: This is a big deal man, I mean if you want to, we can turn back. Ryan: No. Agent: I know how you get sometimes, bro. [Ryan slams the table cause the plates and utensils to shake. He gets up towering over his agent.] Ryan: I said no! Hell, I'm Damien Ryan! The best damn wrestler ever to come out of Canada! I'm going walk through this damn tournament like it was the yellow brick road! This is going to be a breeze! Logan Caine? Who the hell is Logan Caine?! Agent: That's what I'm talking about! ********************* [Ryan sits back down. Back into his head.] -Relax kid, don't you know what he's trying to do. Think about it Damo, he's an agent. All he's trying to do is get you fired up, he knows you got this pre-game phobia. Shut up, Damien, you know you have a phobia of losing. That's right, you're the best wrestler to ever come out of Canada? Whoop-di-do. Is that maple lead tattoo on your arm make you better than Logan Caine? A real big shot you are. -Ah, what the hell! Why am I doing this to myself. Look what this match has done to me…who am I talking to? I'm obviously not talking to this clown agent in front of me. What is he talking about anyways? Probably money. -No, I'm talking to Damien Ryan, me, myself. I can seem to grasp the fact why this happens to me… -Cause you're a freak! -No, I'm not! Cold feet, just a little tense. -You're a wimp, you're no better than your girlfriend trying to get on the kiddy coaster, you stupid pussy. Get it through that pathetic head of yours, DAMO, you run around everywhere, may it be HATE, PTC or whatever, saying you're the best then when it comes to game time, you can't hack it. You run and hide! This is a cat and mouse game with you Damien Ryan and you're not even the damn mouse, your the freaking cheese! You this blood sucking agent here that you can beat Logan Caine. Who are you to say that! -I can beat him. -Keep saying it wimp, look at you, you can't even pay attention Mr. I Don't Give A F*ck. They should call you Mr. ADHD. What's this guy saying to you right now, huh? -Something about…uh… -Yeah, that's what I thought…now how are you supposed to beat, Caine, you're worthless Ryan! ****************** [Suddenly Ryan screams throughout the restaurant.] Ryan: NO, I'M NOT! [Everyone turns around and looks at Ryan, and his agent looks at him funny along with the waitress who is taking his order.] Agent: Right….I'll try the corned beef hash. Waitress: [looks over to Ryan] I'll get you a cup of coffee. Decaf. [The waitress walks away looking at Ryan is a weird way.] Agent: What the hell is wrong with you. [Ryan completely embarassed, with his hands wrapped around his head.] Ryan: I'll see you in the car. [Ryan gets up and walks out of the restaurant, totally leaving his agent behind. He goes and sits in the car and lies his head back and he ponders about the voice in his head.] Damien Ryan versus Logan Caine, bracket two. Two superstars collide to continue to clinch a spot in the second round of the GTT5 tournament. Damien Ryan, attempting to work his way to the top of the game since day one of his wrestling career, not a bad one he's had so far. He's won his share of matches, lost a few….but he's lost more. Seems he's lost his sense of priority, where to be, at what time. Lost his sense of fun, entertain himself by doing the thing he loves, his job. Lost focus, something that bring him so far down, he could lose so badly he's never compete again. Finally, he lost the most important thing of all, his damn mind. This man who raves and rants, who people expect so much out of seems to be falling apart at the seams. Gain it all back and get his screws tightened would be the only way for him to win this match. Time is running out.
