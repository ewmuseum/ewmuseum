<IG 28> A Love Story.

19 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time: 10:00 AM Location: St Christopher’s Church SIZE=3 [detail withheld] [/SIZE] 25075 Cottonwood Ave Moreno Valley, CA 92553 Date: Sunday, February 18th, 2007 The scene of the church was somber and serene, as people were going inside for Sunday mass. The sun outside was bright and scorching outside. A light breeze was the only thing that was keeping the church goers cool as they rushed inside the chapel to beat the incoming heat. Inside the house of God people were inside praying in front of the altar. In the middle of the altar was a crucifix as altar boys were setting up for ten o’clock mass. Everybody in the cathedral was at peace and harmony…all except for one man. Off to the far right corner of the pews, in the shadows was a man praying and pouring his heart out to the lord almighty. The prayer is said over and over again in an attempt to cleanse his soul. A soul that is as dark and twisted then ever before, the man of Prophecy is plagued with the ability to see things that are not really there and they are about to get a lot worse. “Mister…Mister are you ok?” A little boy says as he sits down next to the man. The little boy was dressed in a light blue suit for church. There is a familiarity about the boy…something very familiar about him. “Y…Yeah I’m fine. Where are your parents?” Adams asked in wonder? “Daddy is on a business trip and mommy is across the way praying….my name is Johnny mister, what’s yours?” “M..My name is John also.” A sound from afar calls for the little boy…John Adams looks up to see what was a ghost from his past. Lorraine Adams, the woman who was John Adams mother was calling for the little boy. That thought echoed his head as he looked and gazed at her…majestic as ever with a faint angelic glow to her. “Well mister…I have to go, my Mommy is calling me…Oh and G..God Bless you!” As the little boy starts to leave with the lady revealed to be Adams mother, John Adams yells with the fire in his soul for them to come back. But as the more he yells, he starts to notice the ground around the angelic women and innocent small boy starts to crack as fire from below starts to surround them. Adams yells furiously for them to get away from the flames…but the more he yells the hotter the hellish flames get. Hands from the hell below start to reach out and grab for the two. It is so hot that John Adams starts to see the flesh of the little boy start to drop from the body like butter onto the hollow ground. The little boy looks back at John Adams and stares at him still holding his mothers hand. The face of the little boys was half gone on the right side; you can see part of the cheek bone exposed and the blood boiling over from the extreme heat…his right eye was blistering over his pupil until it finally burst. John Adams watched in horror as the child melted literally away to feed the lost souls below, but before the fire consumed the boy and his Mother…the little boy gave a final wave to the man he has became. Adams lets out a final scream with all the rage and anger inside his heart…until… “Ashes to Ashes, dust to dust. Wouldn’t you say so Brother?” Adams turns around to the familiar voice, only to find his worst fears to be true. There was the familiar sight of his brother Devilin Rubis, the brother that he is haunted by every day who shares the same face as Adams. Adams turns around to the familiar voice, only to find his worst fears to be true. There was the familiar corpse that has haunted him for almost his whole life. The corpse of his dead father was standing there with maggots and earthworms moving in and out of his flesh. The stench was almost too much to bear as the aroma of putrid rotten eggs and a hint of sulfur reached Adams nose. The corpse let out a diabolical laugh as the dust from his decimated body formed a cloud almost too thick to see. He looked at Adams almost peering inside of him to see his soul. “So surprised to see me? Why is that John, why are you not happy to see your old man again? You think anybody else would see you? Hell, your Mother would rather burn in hell just like your childhood then to see you! Not even your Ex-wife wants to see you!!” Ex-wife…Luna Storm, almost seems like an eternity ago as his mind wanders to that place in thoughts where you remember the times that was… Love is a funny thing, for it can make you sad…happy…mad…and all sorts of feelings. But what’s its pure use…it confuses the senses and makes your skull feel like it’s in a vice, slowly squeezing the very life’s blood out of you that makes reason a reality. Love should be a blessing sent from above…but it’s just an anchor that confuses your better judgment. There may be the warm feelings in the beginning, only to turn into the hellish reality in the end. It’s a disease, a virus that eats at your soul little by little. And to Adams it is the greatest epidemic known to man that there is no known cure for…the virus has claimed many through out the ages…even him so very long ago… The scene is one of calm and serenity, the apartments on Mission Grove near Alessandro blvd. is filled with kids getting ready for Halloween. Goblins, Ghost, and Ghouls all wanting one thing this all Hallows Eve…Candy. For Apt 66 there is only one thing in his mind…the celebration of the worst day in his life, his birth. Alone in his one bedroom apt with scattered wrestling magazines on the center coffee table lives a man who dealt with this day in horror and disgust. Disgust for the life it has brought him, disgust for the Father who supposedly fathered him, and disgust for the life he was destined with. He gets the occasional hate mail from around Riverside because ever since that horrid day everybody has thought it was him…John Adams who killed his mother, and for those who even knew the truth behind what happened still told the story in fear of what Frank Adams would do to them. John Adams had decided to get out of the apartment though this day of his birth, in hopes to do something more productive…there was a coffee shop by the name of Java Books that had a band tonight and John Adams decided to go over and listen to them. He has just started Wrestling School in downtown Riverside about a week ago and is currently working at a Fiber Glass company to make ends meet. The scene at the coffee shop was mostly filled with caffeine filled teenagers dressed up for the event. The band was some no named band that was doing a poor redemption of Metallica songs. And the security around the coffee shop is doubled to keep the teens in line. “Exit life, take my sand I’ll love you to Never, Never land!” “You Suck!” came from the crowd as there boredom became evident. “Daddy isn’t that the devil man who killed his mother?” The sound came from a child talking to his dad as they past by Adams. “Hush…it’s time to go!” Devil man…that’s what Adams was known as; well no matter cause it wasn’t the truth a truth no one in this town was willing to hear. John Adams decided to pack it up and leave…but as he was about to leave as fate might have it he accidentally bumped into the most beautiful women ever. She was 5’11’’ looked like she had silver hair, and had to be about 145 pounds of fit muscle. She truly had a very athletic build about her, and she was gorgeous. Her legs was a work of art as they nicely flowed with her milky white skin all over her body. “Oh! Sorry about that, please excuse me.” Adams said as he started to blush. “It’s alright…I actually was hoping to ask you a questions…are you very familiar with Riverside city?” “Yes…yes I am.” “This is silly…I mean I usually don’t do this but I was wondering if you would like to join me for a cup of coffee? I’m new here in California…and definitely could use a guide around town.” She said with a caring response in her crystal blue eyes. John Adams agreed with a new sense of warmth in his heart. The two talked for hours totally oblivious to the people and bad music around them. As they talked they also enjoyed there coffee and there company. John Adams after all these years was smiling and laughing. It was the first time in a long time that he didn’t feel any hurt or pain…for the first time since his mother’s life he felt at peace. The woman’s name was Luna Storm, it was quite an unusual name but it was also a very beautiful name also. The evening continued as they found themselves walking the neighborhood nearby passing by houses that were being visited by random “Trick or Treat!” from the happy kids collecting candy. The two talked about there childhood…well she talked about hers and Adams listened. He wasn’t sure if he should get into the talk about his childhood. But there was something about her that made him feel comfortable. The two talked so much oblivious to the time, they talked all the way to Adams apartment complex. When they got to the door they were holding each others hand…and Adams decided he was comfortable enough to tell her about his childhood. The shocked face on Luna Storm was a little disheartening but then after awhile it turned to a face of love and compassion. “John…thank you for telling me. I know it must have been hard to. I must say that you are so strong to be faced with that much travesty in your life and still be living your dreams. I’m a little surprised that you told me this soon though, after all we just met.” “I know…but I have never met anyone like you before…this might be weird saying this, but I feel like I have known you for all my life. Your beauty inside and out is truly captivating to me. Can I call you? May I have your number?” John responded to her. “John…you may have more then that…you may also have my heart!” She leaned in to give him a kiss, and of course John returned the kiss. The kiss lasted for what seemed to be like an eternity as they didn’t stop there. They went inside the apartment were the two lost souls in life had discovered each other in a more intimate fashion. From that day forward they were seeing each other on a day to day basis. After one week John ended up marrying her in holy matrimony…but the roller coaster of a relationship didn’t stop there… Later in there relationship they would have a falling out with each other as Adams found out that she was pregnant with his child…what could he do? He was always at risk of treating the child the same way his father treated him…that was what the liberalism of this new society was heading now. He did the only thing he could…he let her go….it was the most dreadful time of Adams life…even worse then the hell he went through with his father. He thought of the painful past some more then started to explode as he found himself back in the Chapel. “Not true! It’s not true…you took my childhood and my Mother, and because of you I have no family of my own!” “Well cry me a river you pansy! It’s done…over with! You are never going to get either of them back, so it’s about time for you to get over it and move on! Cause this crying game of yours is boring and pathetic!” A bright light started to peer out of the above stained glass right by the crucifix near the altar of the church. It was the holy light of all that is good…John’s Mother…the evil in the room seemed to disappear out of the room. When John Adams opened his eyes he noticed that he was sitting down in the pew of the church…the inside of the church was dark and empty. The front doors looked as if they were pried open. Was that all in his head? Was he dreaming, or was it a prophecy of things to come? John Adams gets up out of the pew holding his head. “My head hurts!” he thought to himself. He leaves the church before anyone sees him and heads over to his bike. He leaves on his bike with thoughts rushing through his head about the past that comes to haunt him. Will he ever be at peace with the world…let alone himself? As he goes down the road to his ultimate destiny with the wind passing through his hair…the voices inside start again. ASHES TO ASHES…DUST TO DUST! FIN.
