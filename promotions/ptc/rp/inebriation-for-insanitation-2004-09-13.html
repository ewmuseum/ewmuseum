Inebriation for Insanitation

13 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-13 -= Intro =- For as long as wrestling has existed, there has always been one name that has changed the face of this beloved sport. That man has dominated every opponent that standed before him, every obstacle that blocked him, and every fat chick that wanted him... That man, is not Scott Vyper sadly. However Scott has been considered a known name in the wrestling industry. Although he is unsigned, unrecognized and unpaid, Scott Vyper enters GTT4. To test his will? No. To see if he can still tango with the best? No. To get that prize money because bills don't retire? Damn straight. -= Scene =- You see Scott Vyper in a bar looking rather drunk. He looks at this bombshell for a woman and before he can even speak, gets slapped. Scott Vyper yells incoherent things at the woman as she walks away. Scott Vyper then orders another drink and damn near passes out on the table. A woman sits down next to him, rubs his back alil and begins to speak. -= Speech =- Woman - You look like you've had a long day, want to talk to someone about it? Scott Vyper - Actually? My day is just fine, it's ::recieves drink and downs it:: the crappy service that's got me down..... Where am I? Woman - Your at a bar... Scott Vyper - Oh yeah! Sorry... I forgot... Who are you again? Woman - ::smacks Scott upside the head:: Don't remember me why don't ya... It's Fetish! Remember? I use to manage you back in NLW and PWA? Your a bit inebriated aren't ya? ::Everyone looks at the camera with a thumbs up and the message "Word of the day" scrolls on screen. After seconds, they go back:: Scott Vyper - OH YEAH! I perfer the term wasted but hey! Whatever tickles... I'm so wasted I'd be on my way back to the hotel room, but I don't remember what hotel it is and where it's at... Fetish - Did you try looking at your card key? Scott Vyper - Wait... That's not a credit card? Oh man... I left my credit cards in the room along with my cash... eep... Fetish - ::laughing her ass off:: You were wasted before you even got here... Scott Vyper - HEY! I take offense to that... I'm a social drinker! I drink only in the accompany of perfect strangers in whom I which to piss off... See, that's where the strangers part comes in cause they don't know where I live... HA! Damn I'm good... ::passes out on the bar table:: Fetish - Ugh, you fool... Your lucky I loved your ass.... no literally, hated the rest of you, loved your ass. Hey bartender? Give me his bill... -= Scene =- Scott Vyper begins to snore as the bartender comes back with the bill. Fetish reads it and sees that Vyper only had 2 shots of Rum and Coke. She looks at Vyper and mumbles under her breath "What a lightweight..." and pays the bill. She helps Scott up and helps him to her car. She sits him on the passangers side and gets in the drivers side. She drives off down the highway to reach Vyper's hotel room. Along the way, highway patrol pulls over Fetish. The cop walks out of his car and begins to walk to Fetish as she has a look of discomfert on her face. The cop reaches the window and begins to speak. -= Speech =- Officer - Do you realize how fast you were going? Fetish - I could have sworn I stayed under the speed limit sir, I was just trying to get my friend here to his hotel room. He kinda got alil wasted if you know what I mean. Officer - ::looks into the window and shines flashlight in Vyper's face:: Have you been drinking sir? Scott Vyper - ::half asleep:: THERE'S NO BLOOD IN MY ALCOHOL! eh... Fetish - Sir, if I was going over the speed limit, can I please just get the ticket so I can leave? Officer - You sure you didn't drink anything ma'am? Why don't you step out of the car. Scott Vyper - Don't let him do a breathalizer on you! GET THE CAMERA! RODNEY KING! RODNEY KING! Fetish - IDIOT! ::knocks out Scott:: I'm so sorry, like I said, he's a bit wasted. I haven't had anything but I got no problem with the tests. Officer - Wait a sec... is that... blue hair, insane comments, it's gotta be... Fetish - Oh yeah, that's Scott Vyper... you might have heard of him somewhere. He's been all around the wrestling circuit for years. Officer - Actually I thought he was carrot top with a new gimmick, I hate wrestling. Fetish - Fabulous... ::sarcastic grin:: -= Scene =- Fetish takes the inebriation tests and proves to the cop that she's completely sober. The cop lets her go with only a 50 dollar ticket. Fetish gets back in the car and drives off. A slightly sobered up Vyper speaks to her while their in the car. -= Speech =- Scott Vyper - You know, if you hit me like that again I'm so molesting your ass... Fetish - ::laughs:: That's the Vyper I know and love... Scott Vyper - How the hell did I get in the car with you? I mean, not like I'm mad about it or anything... but normally when I go into a bar to get wasted I end up waking up in some alley next to a guy who hasn't bathed in weeks... Trust me, this is paradise compared to that. Fetish - I just happen to be in town and catch word about you being around. I heard you signed up for GTT4, is it true? Scott Vyper - What gave you the first clue? PTC is promoting it out the ass. They were messing with my head though, they waited til the last minute to tell me I'm in. ::laughs:: I like alil suspense in my life don't get me wrong but it was a bit of a shock. Fetish - Ah don't say that, your a talented wrestler, you have just as much of a shot at winning this then anyone else. Scott Vyper - You always did have high hopes for me... Fetish - And that is why I plan on sticking around for alil while. Get you motivated and back into the game. I saw your last run with WMW, and I wasn't impressed. Scott Vyper - Hey! I think I worked well despite the fact that was working for a company I hated... Fetish - Then why did you join then?! Scott Vyper - A few NWC comrades told me they wanted me around there after NWC became defunct. I had nothing better to do... Fetish - I still doubt you hate them... Scott Vyper - Eh, your right... hate is a pretty strong word. I was unhappy, it's hard for me to give my top performance when I'm unhappy and unmotivated. Fetish - Well big boy, I'm here to help with both. So that when you get into this GTT4, your ready to kick some ass! So did they tell you who your first Victim is? Scott Vyper - Dallas Reeves. I've seen him in action, he's not bad. He's apart of that NGW wrestling organization. In a way I wish I opened with another Free Agent, that way I don't look like so much of an underdog, but what am I gonna do you know? People want to see me lose, too bad I plan on making them go home disappointed. Fetish - That's what I like to hear. So what kind of dirt do you got on the kid? Scott Vyper - I'm still buzzed Fetish, I'm in no mood to remember research. He's one of those pretty blonde bitches. You know, the kind that would get raped in jail if they ever served time? Fetish - Poor soul... So when you going to do a promo for this opponent of yours? Scott Vyper - When I sober up alil more. I'm in no condition to be talking in front of a camera... Fetish - ... ::looks at camera:: Oh... Yeah I guess your right. Thank god I'm not secretly filming any of this or anything... Scott Vyper - Fetish... Tell me your joking. Fetish - Maybe... Scott Vyper - DAMN YOU WOMAN! YOU MEAN TO TELL ME THIS ENTIRE THING HAS BEEN RECORDED?! HOLY HELL MAN I'M GOING TO BE THE LAUGHING STOCK OF PTC! Fetish - You kinda already are.... Scott Vyper - Oh yeah... your right. Nevermind then, keep it. The only one who can sue me is Carrot Top and his broke ass can't afford a lawyer. Fetish - ::laughs:: Too true Vypz too true. At any rate, it's going to be a little while before we reach the hotel. Go ahead and take a nap, when you wake up we can shoot a promo. Scott Vyper - Eh, alright... sounds like a good plan. -= Scene =- Scott Vyper then leans back and takes a nap while Fetish continues to drive. They reach the hotel and Fetish shakes Vyper to awake. Vyper is a bit groggy bit he awakes and heads into the hotel. They go to his room and Fetish makes him some coffee. Vyper drinks the coffee and throws some water on his face. Scott Vyper then leans against the wall as Fetish picks up her camera and begins to film Vyper once more. Scott Vyper then looks at the camera and begins to speak. -= Speech =- Scott Vyper - It's been a while since I've told the moral of one of my promos, but this promo is no different. The moral of this promo folks is not to get inebriated in a bar full of people you don't know... you don't know the name of the bum your going to wake up spooning with. However I didn't join GTT4 to talk about spooning, despite the fact that it can be fun at times. I joined GTT4 because I'm not quite done with the wrestling world just yet. That and... I kinda need the prize money. ::smirks:: Furthermore, GTT4 always seems to bring in the big guns. So what better way to end my career with a bang then to do so at one of the biggest tournaments in the wrestling world. ::Scott Vyper moves over to his coffee and drinks some more. He puts the glass down and looks at the camera once more, some has dripped down his face:: Scott Vyper - So now onto my opponent... Fetish - Scott you got alil something on your cheek. Scott Vyper - Where? ::rubs wrong cheek:: Here? Fetish - No other side. Scott Vyper - GRR! ::rubs the other cheek and makes it alil worse:: Damn it! Fetish - OH HOLD ON... ::grabs a napkin and rubs it off:: There, continue on... Scott Vyper - ::mumbles:: God damn wench... Fetish - I HEARD THAT ASSHOLE! Scott Vyper - Sorry! Eh... anywho continuing about what I was GOING to mention. My opponent at GTT4 for my first round bout is Dallas Reeves. I've seen some of Dallas' work in NGW, definitely not a soul to take lightly. However if he saw my work in WMW, he knows not to take me likely either. See Dallas, I'm used to pretty boy egomaniacal pumpkin f***ers like yourself. Here's the deal Reeve's, I know what your expecting from me. I know your expecting "I'm going to beat you down and win." I'm not going to do that, it's too orthadox. I can see here and babble for hours on how I've defeated some of the greatest fighters in the wrestling world but what does that prove? Nothing. Same goes for you Reeves... What does any of your mumbo-jumbo prove? Nothing. All it is, is mindless ramblings of an inebriated soul. So I'm going to be brutally honest with you Reeves. At GTT4, I'm not afraid. Win or lose, I'm not going to cry rather it be tears of joy or sadness. I'm not going to march around the ring rather it's victory or defeat. I'm going to enter the ring with both hands clenched, and leave the same way I entered, with all my pride. ::Scott Vyper finishes his coffee and moves over to the door, then leans back.:: Scott Vyper - See Reeves, rather I win this damn thing or not, the same outcome is going to happen. I'm going to go through the door of retirement and say my goodbyes. Rather your that lucky man that takes me out or not doesn't really matter to me. The only thing that matters at this point is that I go out doing my best. If my best wasn't enough, then that's the true sign I needed that it's time to let go and let someone else do this shindig. Reeves, I've been in this business for almost 10 years. Those 10 years have been long and gruesome. I've broken bones, I've shed my own blood, and I've hurt people that behind the curtains I consider friends. 10 years Reeves, that's alot of pain and suffering. I've stayed in this business because it's what put food on my table. That's all that you, these wrestling organizations and fans are to me... dollar signs that go into my funds. You are all nothing more then digits to me. So I hate to break it to ya Reeves, but as far as I'm concerned... Your just another opponent win or lose. ::Scott Vyper then opens the door and partially walks out of it. He then turns back at the camera and speaks one last time:: Scott Vyper - So Reeves, obviously you know that I mean business. If you don't, then you have a serious mental defect. I may not be claiming an early victory like so many others would, but that doesn't mean that I won't be making it to the second round. I do plan on giving you hell AND THAT'S.. A PROMISE! -= Scene =- Scott Vyper walks out the door and begins walking down the hallway. You see the camera move to the hallway and watch Vyper as he begins to fade in the distance. Suddenly you hear wall banging from the room next door... You hear a man screaming "SAY MY NAME! SAY MY NAME GOD DAMN IT!" then to Fetish's surprise, another man says "BILLY! OH BILLY!" which immediately makes Fetish turn off the camera.
