OZ

8 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2009-11-08 My eyes open, free as they ever were. I am standing here in a forest. Nobody is around, just the whistling of the trees, the crumpling of the leaves as they fall down in unison onto the cold and damp grass. I still stand. I cannot move a single inch, my eyes are the only thing I can somewhat move. I just barely look to my left as I see myself holding an axe. Why? What messed up place is this? I shift my eyes downward and see a rotted down stump just sitting there, probably as old as I am. I keep wondering how long I have been here, but that is not the time to discuss these things, my body is cold and yet I feel nothing, no emotion, no pain, just stiffness. It is as if I have lost something, it is like there was nothing there at all to begin with. "Oh look there! A man is just sitting there, rusted, jiminy crickets, we have to help him!" A voice off in the distance calls out. Most definitely female, once heard a dame whistle to me from a mile away. "Mr.? Mr.? Are you alright?" "MRMGPH? TGP JKS MPHOMPH?!" My mouth feels like it is sewn shut, cut off from all contact from the world. I was trying to say where am I. "I think he said oil can! Hand me that oil can, Mr. Scarecrow!" She yells to the obviously funny name of the man she was with. All I could see is body parts moving in and out of my frame, only grasping tiny visuals one at a time. A splash of oil douses my face. I can finally move my jaw around again. "Jesus Christ, I can talk again." "Oh my oh my oh my, are you okay Mr.?" The girl asks as she continues to spray the oil on me. She sprays some oil in my axe hand, it finally drops to the ground and I give a sigh of relief. "Oh thank god, I have been holding that thing up for a god damn long time." They spray the oilcan on both my legs and I fall backwards. "Thanks there, little girl, guess you can't fix those two left feet, innit that right? Thanks to you too, stretch." I give thumbs up to the weird looking guy. I see a puddle nearby and walk towards it; I bend down and take a look at my reflection."What the hell is this? Is this some sort of joke? Am I a robot?" "Well what is it, pal?" The straw guy looks at me funny. "I'm made of fucking tin; do you expect me to react any differently? And what is this? Are you made from straw? And you are dressed in Argyle attire, isn't your name [name withheld]? And are you going to tell me that the little girl over there doesn't look like Pete? Because to be frankly honest, he looks exactly like this Pete guy, I know, except he is wearing a dress and pigtails. Can someone please explain to me why I have metal where my junk should be? I don't have a pulse either!? I apparently have no heart, that's just perfect, I am dead, and I am a dead zombie robot." I begin to sob a little, I have no heart, no feelings whatsoever, and this day couldn't get any worse. "No heart?" "No heart..." "All bloody hollow I am..." I take a stand and walk over to the funny looking people who obviously look like these guys Pete and [name withheld]. Suddenly there is music playing in the background, it is coming at me from all directions, I have no idea what to make of this, but I needed to get out of here, but I have an urge....to sing: "When I'm sitting on the kettle, When I know I'm made of metal. I know I want to depart. Because I'm presuming That I'm a monster not a human, Then this music had to start." This rush of adrenaline flows through my body, I couldn't explain what it was, I have no actual circulatory system nor do I have a brain to release any of these chemicals into my body. "W-was I just singing? Was I bloody well singing? And in key too? I must be in hell." I collapse on the ground and the two familiar faces run over to me and pick me up. "Mr. Tin Man, are you alright, jeepers!" "I sure hope he is Dorothy, we got to make that trip to see the Wizard of OZ!" "What? Wait, where are you going? Are we going to prison?" I ask. Any place has to be better than this hellhole. "No no no, oh heaven's no! We are going to see the Wizard so he can get me back home to Kansas!" "And we are going to see him so he can give me a brain! Hey, wait a minute, Dorothy, maybe the Wizard can go and give him a heart!" "That is a terrific idea, Scarecrow!" I interrupt them. "Wait a second, is his name seriously Scarecrow? Are your parent’s daft mate? You might as well call me Tin-Man!" I walk over to the axe and pick it up. I twirl it around in both my hands to get a feel for it. An apple comes flying towards my head, I duck and it hits the Scarecrow right in the guy, with precise comedic timing he flies backwards into the brushes. The man lady gasps as s/he runs over to check up on him. I glance over to my right and the most god-awful thing, a tree laughing and talking to the other trees. I carefully make my ways over to the trees. "And what do you think you are doing around here, woodsman?" The tree shoves me backwards a little bit. "You threw that apple at that rapidly creepy Scarecrow; I want you to apologize to him." I glare at the rest of the trees in the immediate area. "Oh yeah, and what the hell are you going to do about it tiny?" He tries to shove his branches towards my way again, but I take my axe and cut off what I presume to be fingers. "JESUS! You cut off my branches, what the hell is wrong with you?" I take another swing. THWACK THWACK THWACK That is one tree down. The tree can be seen wincing in what it looks like to be excruciating pain, his whole body shuts down and he slowly withers away into the darkness. "Now all you daft trees listen here, I am the bloody woodsman around here and when you assault the scarecrow over there it makes me and the little he-girl embarrassed, now what I have done is done taught your little tree friend here a lesson, you mess with me and you get bloody chopped down, cause I'm chopping my ways through. Now I am off to see a Wizard and when I come back, I have little trimmings to do, if you catch my drift, now get the hell out of here" I pause for a second. "OH that's RIGHT, you can't move!" I give a chuckle as I move toward my newly made friends, one a man girl and the other is an inanimate object. "Th-thanks I guess." The scarecrow says as he begins to whimper. "Was it really necessary to chop him down though?" "No problem there, legs, just looking out for my new friends, and yes, I do believe it was right to kill that tree, he was pretty much asking for it." "Jeepers jeepers jeepers jeepers jeepers jeepers..." The little girl becomes frantic. "But, but he may be dead, oh can't we help him Tin-Man? "No no no no, you need to listen here, not everything is good and pure, things must die so things must live, that is how the circle of life gets to keep on continuing, so you just rest your pretty little beard down and let me think of a way to dispose of the body. What we need here is a fire or something." Over in the distance a loud laughter can be heard. "HAHAHA HEHEHEHEHE HHUUHUHUHUUH. Look at this my pretty; it looks like you are in the escort of two gentlemen’s. It would be wise if you two left her alone!" Growls the witch. "Oi, and who the blooming hell are you? Are you a friend of that wanker tree? Come to claim him, eh? Well I spose we could use the help in moving 'im off to the side." I ask politely. I walk a few steps forward to get a closer look at the magician that seemed to appear out of nowhere. "So go on then, who are you?" "Listen, Tin can, I am your worst nightmare if you don't gets out of my way! They call me [name withheld]....The Wicked Witch of the West, and lest you forget that! I come to claim only one thing and that is on her/his tiny/big feet, those precious ruby red slippers. HEHEHEHHEHE." "Oh yeah, and what if we don't? What are you going to do? I've seen street magic too, I can levitate in the air and pull cards out my arse, why don't you get lost [name withheld].....Wicked Witch of the West, and I’ve had enough of your annoying voice." "How about this, Scarecrow, want a little fire? It's coming in hot!" The Witch summons a fireball from its hand and blasts the Scarecrow in the arm, as he is on fire, I can't but help to laugh at his situation, but then I swing the axe and cut off the scarecrows arm and place it on the tree that I chopped down. It starts to burn; the yellow brick road is not blocked anymore. Mission Accomplished. Not Quite. "My Arm?! What the hell did you do to my arm Tin-Man! I need that arm to scare off the crows! Now it's just burning with that tree you murdered! You are good at all! You are just as bad as that [name withheld]....Wicked Witch of the West!" "Just be grateful you are living, you would still be hanging from that wooden cross if it wasn't for me saving your sorry ass, and I’ve been in plenty of wars man, plenty of wars." "What do you mean?! You didn't get me down from there, Dorothy did! And we came and rescued you! Now I am missing an arm." The music plays up again, still no wonder where it is coming from. "I really wish I was pleasin' To find out you committed treason. For things, I can't explain. Maybe I will be one of the few I would take the cue and kill you If I only had my arm." Quickly I had to come up with something to counter him. "To find you sitting there cryin' I ain't got time for lyin' You are a brainless coward I have more brains than you It would not be fair to use my kung-fu Killing you would be its own reward." "BOYS BOYS BOYS! Stop it, you are fighting each other, when we should be fighting that nasty old wicked And....witch!" She interrupts us as we would have continued in some sort of infinite loop. "HEHEHEHE!" The witch screams as she looks into our inter-clan fighting, she seems eager to get rid of us, picking us off one by one as she has her way with Dorothy. "Not so fast, An.....Wicked Witch! Taste AXE!" I take out the body spray, throw it at the witch, hoping she would get bored, and just leave, but then I realized I had a real axe next to me. I pick up the better Axe and throw it at the witch. "TASTE TOMAHAWK!" Sure enough, time slowed down there, all eyes are looking upon the firely steel of that axe that is tumbling through the air nonstop. CRACK! It completely misses A....the witch and hits a rope tied to the tree in the background, thereby saving the munchkin who tried to commit suicide. It may also have been a crow. Who knows? It is an urban legend. "IS THAT ALL YOU CAN DO, TIN MAN? AHAHAHAHHAHA!" The witch continues on her laughter. I suddenly just remembered in this plot point of the story where I find a hose with a conveniently place hydrant next to it. I turn the hose on and begun to spray the witch down, hoping to drown her in a million different ways possible. "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! I'm melting! I'm melting! What a world, what a world, what a world, if only I could have told my friend Lucas he was better than me and would win against me at any time, noooooooo!" With the last gasp of breathe, the wicked witch dies. "Well, my little friends, it's been real, I am out of here." "Just like that?" "Just like that." "MY ARM!" "I sincerely hope you give the Wizard of Oz my best regards, maybe you shouldn't go into those woods alone, I hear there are lions prowling the area." "Good luck to you, Tin-Man! C'mon Scarecrow, we need to see the Wizard!" She waves to me and the Scarecrow flips me off as they descend into the forest hoping to find Criss Angel like they once were before. "God damn Technicolor nightmare..." I slowly walk the other way, towards to the woods in hoping to find what was once lost. EPILOGUE: The Tin-Man found an organ transplant cooler in the forest and it contained a human heart. Dorothy and the Scarecrow were mauled by a lion prowling in the woods. The Lion followed the yellow brick road as instructed by Dorothy by the Munchkins right back into Munchkin land. There were no survivors. THE END? * * * "Lucas...." BOOP "Lucas...." BEEP My eyes open once more. I seem to have been doing that lately. Once again, clear as day, instead of standing up stiff as a rock, I am laying down on a firm mattress, surrounded by what seems to be a hospital. As I blink, I see a woman come into the frame of my eyes. "Lucas, can you hear me?" She says, I could only reply with a moan. "I see, do you feel any pain on any part of your body?" I moan again. "Great, let me go and get you something to eat, is that alright Mr. Quinton?" I stood or more or less laid down silent as she smiled and exited the room. I prop my head up and look around the room; it was like a child's room. There were toys scattered around the floor and on the wall right across from in, big letter reads WELCOME TO OZ with a rainbow overlapping it. "No, no this cannot be happening!" Am I still dreaming? I sit up from the bed and unleash the covers that were bogging me down. "No, this has to be a dream, that's the only thing that is possible." "Mr. Quinton, please get back in the bed." The nurse comes back into the room with a tray of soup in hand. "And you are most certainly not dreaming, you are in the pediatric wing of the hospital." "Oh? Why am I here anyway? The children's section I mean." I wonder, any answer has to be as equally bad. I tuck my feet back into my sheets as she comes over to my bed and sets down the tray on the table. "All the other rooms are occupied by residents who unfortunately have the flu, it's a total lockdown here and you are lucky you weren't diverted to another hospital, you probably wouldn't have made it." "Is that so? So what the hell is wrong with me?" My curious mind enquired. Relaxing in this bed is alright and all, but I need answers. I always need answers. She takes the spoon and puts it in the bowl and mixes the soup around. The reflection of the spoon bounces off the walls as if it was let loose upon the world. She takes a small bite of the soup just to be sure; it wasn't too hot or too cold. I came to the realization that she was being motherly to me, was she sorry about what happened? Does she know something that I don't? "I am going to let the doctor explain that to you, I just get the soup." She chuckles, I smirk. We share some kind of moment there, he eyes locking into my eyes. Her face shows a caring and concern that I wish someone would have for me. "That's very funny, why exactly is this place called OZ? I realize it's from the book and the movie, but there has to be something else going on here." Not that I cared or anything about why they called this place OZ, I needed someone to talk to, I needed someone to care for me, in my weakest state, nobody else showed the kind of concern that this woman shared for me. "Haven't you ever wanted to visit another land so distant that you couldn't get their by a plane, train, or boat? Here at this hospital, we try and make their dreams come true, even if they can't go to OZ, we can bring OZ to them." She smiles, I reach over to her and brush her hair back, and she blushes. She has a genuine concern for the patients that come in and out of this hospital. Her compassion drives her to become something great. "This may be nothing serious, but I had the oddest dream while passed out on the floor. I dreamt I was actually in the land of OZ, isn't that rather peculiar? I opened my eyes and I was suddenly the Tin-Man, holding my axe up in the air and not being able to move one single inch, then Dorothy and the Scarecrow came and saved me from my what I thought to be my doom. Do you think any of it means anything? It sure is a coincidence since I dreamt of OZ and suddenly I wake up here in of all places....OZ." My smile turns into a puzzled look. I never thought I would be weak, not in a lifetime. I reach over towards the soup and take a sip, I sigh, and this was more than I could have ever asked for. She smiles once more after sitting there, blindly following the story I just told. In the 20 minutes I have met her, I want to know all about her, she is my savior and I needed to thank her. "I guess we should have a proper introduction. My name is Lucas." I push away the soup and extend my hand to her. "You can call me Gail." She reaches out her hand and grabs mine. The soft skin made me feel like a whole new person, the invigoration; the chills as it make my hairs stand up from my arms all the way to the back of my neck. I smile. "So when is my doctor going to be coming in? No rush at all, I actually like having the company." "His name is Dr. Nick Chopper and should be in here any moment." She smiles back at me. Together we wait and wait for the doctor to arrive. We talk about everything, I question why she doesn't have any other patients to look after, but she smiles and ignores me. The glow of her had me in shambles, thoughts racing round and round, I liked her, I wanted to get to know her outside of this prison, but I know this could never happen, not this time around. I couldn't ruin another person's life like I have done so before, like all those times before. I couldn't commit to the heartache that I would cause to another person, not again. I've been battered and broken for so long, I have no idea what belongingness is. Everything is in shades of black and white, for years I have yet to see the true spectrum of things, and it kills me inside and out. Karma is what brought me here. I believe everything has a price. I believe everything comes back to you, tenfold, and there is nothing you can do about it, no retribution, no praying, not one single thing. Any deed does not go unpunished, and that is breaking point. I am at my breaking point, and I feel as though I am paying for my sins, past and present and nothing can make that go away. KNOCK KNOCK "Hello, I am Dr. Chopper pleasure to meet you, Lucas is it? Gail, you can leave now." As she exits the room, she gets one last look at me, she smiles and mouths the words 'good-bye' as she whisks away towards another corridor. "Nice to meet you I guess, so what is exactly my problem here, doc, and your lovely nurse couldn't tell me anything and I would really love to know what I am up against here." "You have what is called Cardiomyopathy, it is treatable in only a clinical trial, but there are very few doctors in the world who will actually be willing to perform this kind of operation, It is a very risky operation and you could come out on the other end fine a whistle or worse....dead." dead? Dead? DEAD? He was no doctor. What right does he think he have by condemning me. No, not a doctor at all, he was a coroner, a mad man. He gave me the death sentence and he knew it, he didn't try and shield my pain, not at all. He was out of his mind. * * * "Lucas are you alright man?" My manager, Chris, was standing there at the door, admiring the childish things around the room. "Boy, they could not have picked a worse place to put you." He walks inside the room and sits down on the chair next to me and pulls out a flask from a pocket, quickly as he took it out, he took a sip and put it back into the pocket it came from. "Oh yes, Chris, I am just feeling fine, I am having the greatest time here, oh yes, please, you have no idea about the series of events that I have endured. You have no idea whatsoever, mate." I scowl. I wring my hands and look at him; it was the only thing I could do at the time, seeing as how I am confined to this god-forsaken bed. He smiles. "Listen, Lucas, about your condition, you may just want to jump out of that bed and hug the hell out of me, but I have something that may be of use to you." He reaches into another pocket and pulls out a piece of paper with scribbles on it. "Oh really? And what might that be? Are you going to grant me three wishes from your genie bottles? Are you going to make me click my heels three times and wish there was no place like home?" "Well there is a specialist that can fix you all prim and proper like, I can get you to see a man who will fix that heart condition of yours and make it look like you never had that whatsoever, how does that sound?" "I feel like there is a catch somewhere in your words, Chris, there is something you are not telling me." I knew him all too well. "Yes, yes I suppose so, well we have to go a little far to actually get this done, you might not like where we are going." "That doesn’t seem like a big deal, where is it precisely?" "Australia..." He trails off, my face flushes; he knew I wasn't going to like it. "What?" "I am telling you, this guy is a wizard at what he does." "So what you are telling me is that we have to go to Australia which nickname is OZ and meet with this doctor guy who will fix me up prim and proper...and he is a wizard." "Pretty much so yeah, we leave tomorrow..." "I guess we are off to see the Wizard...in OZ." Sonofabitch. * * * Next Day * * * I am standing there, hovering over the bed that I once occupied. I can just imagine myself lying in the bed, helpless as ever and I can just think of the words that would come out of my mouth. "You piece of shit." That's all I ever think of myself. Piece of shit. I shake my head and continue packing my clothes; it wasn't going to satisfy me though. I wanted to let go and be free, from all the obligations that I have ever had in my life, to go off and live on a beach, drinking booze with my feet up, letting the breeze from the ocean trickle down my skin. This is the life I chose. This is the life I am doomed to. I may have had good times, I may have helped a few people along my path, but they don't count for anything anymore. I am still outmatched, outwitted, outplayed. The whole game was rigged from the start. Death was sentenced even before I was born. Nothing could change that. "Oh, they discharged you early?" I quickly turn around; Gail was standing there, not in her typical scrubs uniform, but more pleasing civilian clothes. "I thought we were going to see more of each other, but I can see that won't happen." She walks closer to me and helps me folds the clothes. "What are you doing here? Did you come here to see me off?" I enquire; she had a strange look to her. "Are you even working today?" She shakes her head. "No, no, I just came to get something I left last night out of my locker, and I just happened to run into you." She lied. "You look like you are prepared to go on a long journey." "You don't know the half of it. Funny thing is, I never thought I would actually go to this length to protect myself, the last few weeks have been a bothersome journey and I just thought I was not going to make, not at all." I laugh, I put my hand on hers and we look into each other’s eyes. It was a perfect moment. I didn't want it to stop. She quickly slides her hand away and blushes. "Listen, I am leaving for Australia, I don't know how long this is going to take and I don't know how long before I actually come back, but I wanted to let you know, over these past couple of days that I am grateful for everything, and I mean everything that you have done for me." I try to close into her and seal the deal with a kiss, but she backs away and holds up her hand. A Wedding band. I sigh. "I should have guessed, a great lady like you is already hitched." I rub my eyebrows together and piece all the information in. All she can do is stand there and continue to fold my clothes and put them into the suitcase. "It's...it's not that. I haven't taken this wedding ring off since he died." A tear rolls down Gail's cheek as she struggles to fight the urge to cry. "About 4 years ago, my husband died in Afghanistan, it was a terrible nightmare, I thought I would go insane and never be with anyone else again. I wanted to kill myself the day I got the news, and I knew that I could never be with another person ever again. I don't want to lose them too." I quickly hand her one of my folded towels and she blows her nose in it. "The people...." I paused. I didn't know what to say, I wasn't good at making passionate speeches like this, but the fire inside me wanted to continue forward. "All the people I know are either dead or far away from me, I push and I push and I can't seem to let go of the impacts, whether good or bad, that I made on people. I like to think I make a difference, but in the end it doesn't matter, I have to atone for things, and it may not be now, it may not be tomorrow, it may not come for a long time, but I know some day I will have to pay for the terrible deeds I have done. Then...then there are people like you, the good people who are here to cherish and nurture the innocents, to be their protectors from all harm, this is what your husband was, he was a good man with the strength to fight and move on, just like you. You have the will and the way." She looks at me. She tries to process the information that I have given her. I see a twinkle in her eye and she cracks a small smile and moves closer to me. "You aren't a terrible person, the things you did in the past are in the past and nothing more. I didn't know you before then, but I can tell something inside you must have changed, because it is not in your nature to hurt, only to heal." "Gail..." "Shut up." She quickly reaches in, kisses me, and pulls away. "I want you to go to Australia and come back, and maybe we can continue this conversation." She pushes me back as she quickly makes her exit. During our exchange, she slipped something in my pocket. I reach inside and pull out a note; one by one, I unfold the pieces of paper and get to the content at heart. I read it. I gasp. I put the thing back into my pocket and pretend nothing ever happened. I finish folding my clothes and zip my suitcase. I grab the handle and make my exit from the hospital as I whistle 'Somewhere over the Rainbow'. Everything was going to be okay. * Wizard of Oz written by L.
