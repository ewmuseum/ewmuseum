Just when you know, it all changes (Part 2)

21 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-10-21 Four and a half hours away. Yes, that long away from the night that Thomas claimed would be my night. But after what happen at the store, I needed to get out of the streets and get somewhere I needed to be. “Hey Elia.” A voice spoke out, shocking me as I walked through the doors of the near by gym that my long time child hood friend worked at. Me and her grew up in the same school area. That is how I met Thomas and I thanked her for that very reason every day. My legs were tired but with my match in one day against a man that I knew nothing about, I needed some working out. But my mind wasn’t feeling tired. It was feeling confused and confused at everything that had been happening. I looked over at Trisha who was standing at the counter as I walked over towards her. “Hey,” Placing my hands on the counter for some added support that I really didn’t need. But I was always trying to over play my feelings when I was tired. “Is the gym free from the crowd?” “Yeah,” Trisha said, grabbing the book that was laying on the counter for sign ins. My dress, or should I say the dress, really wasn’t for me. I happen to get one a little ways down from the gym. But I could still feel the words and expression that the woman gave me. Her eyes lit up my mind like a light switch in a dark room. They looked dark and cold and they still hurt me. But I had to toss it all out. I had to learn to not believe what was forced on me. As I walked down the long red carpet hallway to the back of the gym, I could smell the sweat filled room in my nose. The smell, like a wake up call for me in more ways then one. It was a wake up call that told me that I wasn’t alone in this world. Even though my father was dead, my life was still going. That was something that I still, even to this very minute, hadn’t realized. And even more, it was a wake up call that told me that the fight of my life wasn’t over with Jamie Fletcher. No, the fight was still going with a man named Sebastian Moraco. Walking passed the iron metal machine’s, I could hear a familiar voice from a room on my right side. It was deep. Something that I wasn’t expecting. Stopping dead in my tracks, I looked over and noticed the door to the ladies locker room was opened. Getting there, the voices go a little louder. Almost to the point to were I could hear them clearly. “Oh come on, don’t talk about that now.” “But we have to.” “No we don’t. Remember, I still have to make Elia understand that she isn’t anything like her damn uncle.” Who was this. And how in the world did they know my uncle? I didn’t even fully know my uncle for that matter. I went through the doorway and stood next to the first set of lockers in the room. To my surprise’s, standing next to a woman that just shined from head to toe, was Thomas. What was he doing here? I kept quiet as I watched her long dark hair hang from her head like the sun. Neither one was smiling. Why didn’t Trisha tell me he was here? And who was this woman for that matter. “Listen. You go back to the hotel and tomorrow I’ll come by and prove to you that it will all be ok.” His voice was different. It was as if he felt something for this woman. I couldn’t put it on it but I knew he did. I leaned in closer, looking right into the heart of the problem. Thomas then placed his hand on her shoulder, rubbing it for a moment before he leaned down and pressed his lips on hers. “…What?” How could he? I thought he loved me as his only one? Falling off the lockers, a loud bang was lifted in the air, rattling the air around Thomas and this female. I jumped back against the closest thing I could feel which was the same lockers that I had been standing by. Trying not to breath at all, I felt as if someone was coming. Deep down and standing right on the tip of my heart, the ladies locker room door open, showing Trisha standing right in front of me. I reached out, taking her by the arms like a knight would he queen and slammed her against the door. Her eyes went side, completely unaware as to why as my smooth red lips took away the space between her and me, as I began to kiss her. “Oh I sorry ladies…” A voice behind me said. It sounded like Thomas but I was to captured and to confused to even turn. How did I get myself into this predicament? Was I to trusting? Did I rely on my feeling to much now that I was alone? It just seems that bad things are drawn to my like a female in heat. I pulled away, looking deep into the eyes of Trisha before the strangest feeling came in my stomach. “WHAT WAS THAT FOR?” Trisha said, still in shock that she was kissed. Not by a woman as I knew that she was into females, but to be kissed by me, one of her friends, and one friend that had stood with her throughout her life after school. I didn’t know what to say. How was I to explain what I saw? I couldn’t tell her that Thomas was kissing another woman. That was her brother. She would react in a way that I didn’t want her to do. But to me, there had to be a perfectly good explanation to this, but what? The only think I knew was, deep down in my stomach, I was starting to feel funny. Not sick. Not any type of funny from a joke. No, it was deeper. I looked deep into the green eyes of Trisha. It was as if I was starting to forget the image of Thomas and I was looking into paradise. “I…I don’t know…” The only thing I could say. Yet with the sound of my voice, Trisha knew something was up. I turned away. Walking towards the locker room door with the thought of really being alone. Looking through the glass window that was in the middle of the door, I saw the face of the woman that kissed my man. Her soft looking skin, her soft and caressing eyes, and her fancy looking dark black hair danced with every step she took. I knew deep down inside why Thomas would go for another woman. I wasn’t pretty, I knew that and I knew her felt it even if he stood beside me. “Are you ok Elia?” Trisha asked, placing her hand on my shoulder. “Yeah,” Why was the day that was promised for me turning into the worst possible day that I could have? I shook my head before turning around to face Trisha, knowing that I just kissed her and for that, it seemed I like it. “I’m just having a really bad day and with my match in two days against Moraco, I’m just not sure about anything.” Really, I had been doubting myself as of late. Not my ability to wrestle. No. I was doubting my ability as a human being on this earth. My father always had a saying. If you doubt you are alive, well, you probably aren’t. Well truth be told, my life was really being doubted by one person and that person was me. I couldn’t rake it. Slammed my hands against the locker room. Imprinting them deep against eh cold metal that was held up. My blood was pumping just like an engine would it’s oil. My eyes, watery from the inside and now the out, ran down the makeup covered face of mine. Trisha took me in her arms, holding me like a little child for a second. I felt warm and safe before pulling away from her. “What…?” Her sweet and kiddy like voice said. “I just got to go,” Walking out the locker room door. I wasn’t angry or upset. I just didn’t trust that feeling in my gut. I needed to be alone for a little bit before I meet with Thomas…or if I did. As I kept walking towards the front door of the gym, I saw that face of that female in my mind. Who was she? What was she trying to take my man? But most of all, the only question that I was asking myself was, where was I now with Thomas?
