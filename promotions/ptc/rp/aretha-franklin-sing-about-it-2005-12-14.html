Date: 2005-12-14 Sin number two is Gluttony. Gluttony is the inordinate desire to consume more than what one needs. Look at yourself, Kimbusa… you five-hundred pound bag of shit. You embody the physical aspect of this sin more than anyone in GTT5. From the looks of you, it seems to me that you never found a bowl of rice that you didn’t like. Stop. Cut tape. Quit recording. This shit isn’t happening. I am not going to subject you to the whole sin thing again. It worked with that whore bag of whore bags, Eva VanLorne. However, this is a whole new ball game and that just isn’t enough for my current opponent, so I am going to take the time to vent on a topic that matters. That subject is respect. It seems that I am not being taken seriously by others in this tournament. I may still be an unknown to many around the Primetime Central family of wrestling promotions and that causes me to be the underdog every single time I show my masked face. Yet, I showed E.V.L. that her boots were meant for walking. Meant for walking her skanky ass back to whatever whorehouse she stepped out of. I said I would do it and I did. It was no fluke, ladies and gentlemen. Though, you all don’t seem to see that. I am here for the long haul. Just like everyone else, I didn’t join this fucking thing to lose and I am out to prove it. Now, I am up against big bad Kimbusa, a.k.a. the human version of Gojira. His name strikes fear in a lot of people and all you can eat buffets. He’s a former Old School Fighting champion and has made it deep into these competitions before. Not many people want to see him standing across from them in the squared circle. I am pretty damn sure that no one truly believes my scrawny ass has a prayer in toppling this kaiju. You know what I have to say to you all? Fuck you. It doesn’t matter if you believe in me or not. I am going to light this tub of lard up light a Christmas tree. Let him try his sumo bullshit. I will kick him so fucking hard, he’ll starve to death rolling. It was fun and games with Eva. I got to talk about boobs, making whoopee, and pop out some blond jokes. Playtime is over, fuckers. Kimbusa is a warrior and so, he shall die like one. Returning to the topic of respect, my opponent must have no respect for himself. It is obvious from his appearance that he doesn’t respect his body, but only a man without pride or honor would ask those scumbags in the Yakuza for the tiniest bit of fucking help. I know what he did and who he spoke to. Word travels fast through the Japanese underworld, you know. I may have been out of the game for a few months, but my contacts are forever hard at work. Kimbusa, or as I like to call him "Stubby", was caught talking to the wrong man. That man was Shindo. It is not good making deals with such devils, let alone aligning with them. Shindo is a high-ranking official in the Yakuza for those out of the loop. I have dealt with their crime and corruption for too damn long. Now, I know all about those pieces of crap, right down to the most insignificant fact. From the debate over its origin, kabuki-mono or machi-yakko, to their current ties with Japan’s right-wing nationalists, I have put a lot of time and effort into studying it all. They cannot take a shit without me knowing how many logs and whether or not they used two-ply or one to wipe. One must know their enemies and be damn sure that I know mine better than they know themselves. I asked myself a few times, "How could a man like Kimbusa get so low that he has to deal with the 8-9-3?" As stated earlier, he is a former champion and can go with almost anyone either in a wrestling ring or doyo. I just can’t find a reasonable answer. He was on top of the world at one point and now, he has sunken this low? It is truly hard to believe. I have faced hard times myself. From my grandfather passing way to Nick Brandish making me blind in one eye, things have not always been spectacular with me. They have been downright shitty. I can promise you that. However, I have never once dishonored myself or my bloodline by lowering myself to the levels Kimbusa has. He has lost the respect of me, the people all around the world, and most importantly, himself. That is the lowest of lows. Respect isn’t just given out like Karina Wolfenden’s pussy at The X-Games. It must be earned and earning your own respect is the toughest. The various black widows that I have tattooed on my body are all the proof I need to back up my respect. Each one is a lasting reminder of a battle in which I defeated a master of a ninja clan and gained their respect. Some may fear me due to the pain I inflicted, but every single one of them respects me. That is what is important. Besides, a little fear never hurt anything. While it is difficult to obtain, respect is easy to teach and I have had to do it quite a few times… I remember back to a time when I was about seventeen or eighteen years old. I had been training with my grandfather a while by then. One evening over dinner, my grandmother handed my grandfather an envelope, after he was done with his rice and tea. I looked on with curiosity, wondering what was inside, as my grandfather opened it and read a piece of paper quietly. Outside of the monthly bills and letters from my parents, the mailman rarely dropped anything off. It was especially rare for my grandfather to get something, so I was really interested to find out what it was. He finished his reading of the letter and signed a quick message to my grandmother. I was never really any good with sign language and just caught bits and pieces of what he relayed to her. It wasn’t enough for me to figure it out, but I didn’t have to, as my grandmother replied aloud. "Oh! Good! I’ll make sure to set up the spare room for him," she smiled while clearing off the table. "When is he due to arrive?" My grandfather quickly signed a reply and luckily for me, it was a word that I knew. Whoever was coming to our home was coming Tuesday, which was only two days away. "Who is coming on Tuesday?" I asked with a smile while handing my empty dish to my grandmother. "Your cousin, Fujita," she replied happily and started to the kitchen. My grandfather rose from his seat and headed for his study, as I scampered into the kitchen after my all-knowing grandmother. "I have a cousin?" I quizzed her and took the leaning tower of dishes from her hands. "I guess your mother doesn’t bring up your uncle Koji very often," she replied, taking one dish at a time from my hands and washing it. "Not really," I answered. "All I know is that they don’t get along." "Well, your uncle chose to study karate instead of ninjitsu," she explained. "He wanted to be a samurai and by learning karate, your mother felt it dishonored your grandfather and his ancestors because we come from a storied clan of ninjas. Your grandfather is an open-minded man and welcomed Koji’s studying of other styles, but your mother just saw it as a great disrespect and disowned him. Koji is a stubborn man and wouldn’t back down, causing him to leave. From what I gather, the two haven’t spoken since. We keep contact with both. We don’t interfere in their conflict though, as they will one day learn from their mistake." "Uncle Koji is a samurai?" I asked in a little disbelief. "As close as a modern day man can get to one and he was training Fujita to be the same, from what I remember," she replied. The idea was a particularly interesting one. Someone in my family is a samurai? Who would have ever thought such a thing could happen? I guess there is always a black sheep in any family and my uncle Koji is it. It must be a trait in the males, as I am obviously not your run-of-the-mill ninja and my grandfather dwelled deep into kuji-in while his father didn’t believe in any form of magic. Perhaps Fujita was going to be the one to combine the two into a perfect form. That would be quite the feat, as samurais and ninjas didn’t always get along so well. Police and assassins never do. As the two days passed, I got as much information out of my grandmother as I could about Uncle Koji and Fujita. It seems Fujita was the first child out of the grandchildren that my grandfather tried to train. Seeing that Fujita preferred the samurai teachings and ways instead of the way of the ninja, he sent Fujita back to his father in Chicago. Well, as he got older, Fujita started to develop a rebellious streak and a shitty attitude. Basically, he became a prick. He got involved with the wrong crowds and my uncle repeatedly had to bail him out of jail for fighting and shit. It didn’t help that Uncle Koji was loaded and able to spoil Fujita beyond belief. Must be nice… Anyhow, Fujita had been sent to us in hopes that my grandfather could straighten him out. We would soon find out. Tuesday came and as I was finishing my morning workout, a taxicab pulled up the dirt road connecting my grandfather’s property to the main road. I draped my manriki-gusari around my neck and started back to the house. The taxi’s horn signaled its arrival and my grandparents stepped out onto the front porch. The driver raced out of the car and opened the rear driver’s side door, as soon as he parked. I stood on the front step, as Fujita stepped out of the car, and he didn’t look like any relative of mine. "What the fuck…" I quietly mumbled to myself and perked a brow. In front of me, stood the stupidest-looking fucktard that I have ever seen in my life. If there was a term for an Asian version of a wigger, this guy would have had his picture next to it in the dictionary. I just wanted to walk right up to him, slap the upside-down visor off his fucking head. Why do people need to dress like that? It makes them look like a petty criminal or drug dealer. No one would take him seriously. Lord knows that I sure as fuck don’t and I am betting that my grandfather doesn’t. Maybe he could use his supposed black belt in karate to stop his pants from sagging so low. "Yo, pops," he calls out to my grandfather with a small smile and then, turns to the driver. "Bring my shit into da house, dawg." He started walking to my grandparents. My grandfather didn’t even try to move from his post on the front porch and just examined the oldest grandchild closely. "Gramz," Fujita exclaimed and hugged my grandmother, as she stepped off the porch to greet him. "You have gotten so big," she grinned and returned the hug. "Wuzzup, pops?" He asked my grandfather and extended his fist towards my grandfather. Let’s just say, that flew like a brick shithouse. My grandfather didn’t see himself as one of "da boyz". He just looked down at Fujita’s hand and then, into his eyes. He was not amused in the least and just kept his arms crossed. "That’s cold, man," he announced, as he pulled his hand away. He traded quick evil glances with my grandfather before my grandmother stepped in and directed him over to me. "Fujita, this is your Aunt Miko’s son, Xin," she merrily informed him. "Nice to meet you," I said with a smile and I extended my hand. "Fo’ shizzle mah nizzle," he replied and did some over the top and exceedingly stupid handshake to which I just help my hand in place. "Nice to meet ya too, dawg." He quickly turned his attention to the front door and draped his arm around our grandmother. She led him inside and I followed them up the steps, stopping next to my grandfather. He still wasn’t very pleased with what he had seen in Fujita and the wheels were turning in his head. You could see it in his eyes. He was going to put the fear of god back into this boy. "Gramz, dis crib is sweet as all hell, yo," Fujita told our grandmother, as they stepped inside. "I could see me pimping mad hos up in here." I was going to step in and teach the primitive screwhead some manners, but my grandfather placed his hand on my chest, stopping me in place. His eyes were still focused on my hoodlum of a cousin and he just shook his head no. He wanted to deal with this personally. "He’s a fucking punk," I growled. "I should break his fucking jaw for talking like that in front of grandma." Things didn’t get any better from there. My cousin seemed to have no respect for anyone or anything. He constantly bitched and moaned about not having the modern conveniences that he was accustomed to like a stereo, video games, and various other shit. He just took shit without asking and constantly spoke down on ninjitsu while bragging about how great karate is. I did my best to keep myself in check, as every time I went to say something to the fucker, my grandmother defended him and my grandfather told me no. He was obviously my grandmother’s favorite grandchild, but my grandfather wasn’t a fan of what was going on and he wanted to solve the problem himself. The stubborn old coot should have just let me whoop his ass. It would have saved us a lot of trouble. My grandfather tried to break him by making him train in the harshest ways he could come up with, but Fujita just brushed him off or spent the entire day sleeping. Fujita thought this was going to be some sort of vacation and he was going to just take it easy until his dad called him home. Not in my grandfather’s house. The rage started to build in my grandfather and each day it got worse and worse. He kept himself in check for as long as he could, but even a man as strong as my grandfather has his limits. I was practicing sword-fighting against my grandfather with a bokken and my loser cousin decided to come watch us. A bokken is a mock katana made of wood. It may not cut you, but it hurts like a bitch when you get hit with it. Trust me on that one. It is also a handy tool when out on a mission, as it can be hidden better than a real katana and is lighter while still being able to cause major internal injuries. Anyhow, my grandfather and I squared off and he made short work of me. I wasn’t the top swordsman around at that point and I’m still not that great with the weapon. Give me my manriki-gusari any day of the week and I will get the job done. Give me a sword and I will have it holstered most of the time. Moving along, my grandfather was a surgeon with that bokken. I could come at him with any series of attacks and he would take me out, making me look easier to beat than the Global Wrestling roster. At that point of my life, I never wanted to give up until I beat the old coot. As I got older, I learned it would never happen, so I tried to avoid the pain as much as possible. With that said, I tried once more. I darted at my grandfather and unleashed a series of strikes to the upper portion of his body. He deflected them all and spun around behind me. I quickly whipped around and turned up the heat with a baseball like swing. He ducked the strike and I used the momentum of my attack to spin into a sweep with the bokken. He easily leapt over it and as my back was turned, he struck me in the back of the knee, the small of my back, and the back of my head, in that order. I dropped to one knee and he pressed the tip of the weapon against the back of my neck, signaling that he had once again whooped my ass. I growled in frustration. My young spirit hating that I just couldn’t seem to overcome my grandfather’s skill. I slowly rose to my feet and was greeted by a derogatory laughter. My eyes shifted to the origin of the sound and saw Fujita laughing mightily at my defeat. I nearly turned the bokken’s handle to sawdust, as I gripped it in a fist of rage. "Dog, yo ass got punk’d by da old man," he chuckled and covered his face, in a false attempt to hide his smile. "If you think you can do any better," I snarled at him and launched the wooden sword at him, "go right ahead." "I’ll fuck grandpa up like it ain’t no thang," he arrogantly replied and caught the bokken with his right hand, as my grandfather looked on. "Let me show you two how a samurai does dis shit." Fujita brought the weapon up into a traditional bushido stance, which looks a lot like a batting stance in baseball. My grandfather matched him with the same stance and the two began to circle. In a flash, Fujita charged at my grandfather. He let loose a powerful downward slice and just made a breeze upon missing his target. My grandfather instantly spun away from the strike and clipped Fujita’s leg, sending him crashing to the ground. Fujita sat up and chuckled. "Looks like grandpa knows what he is doing wit da blade," he smiled and climbed back to his feet. The two returned to their stances and Fujita was once again the aggressor. He darted towards my grandfather and tried the same strike again. Like before my grandfather spun around the attack. Fujita thought he had it planned out, as he leapt into the air thinking a sweep was coming. My grandfather was no fool when it came to combat and kicked his oldest grandson out of mid air with a roundhouse to the back. Fujita was sent rolling forward and he was not pleased. He rolled right back up to his feet and raced towards my grandfather once more. I was shocked by how fast and precise Fujita was with the bokken, as he came within moments of catching my grandfather with strike after strike. He just wasn’t experienced enough to take on my grandfather, but his pride was pushing him to keep trying. My grandfather bobbed, weaved, ducked, and leaned to avoid every strike Fujita sent his way, not even trying to deflect or block them. It was as if he was testing to see how well my uncle had trained my cousin in the art of sword play. When he finally got tired of toying with Fujita, my grandfather struck hard and fast. He deflected Fujita’s bokken to the ground with the sword in his right hand, struck him in the jaw with his left, spun to his right with the momentum of the punch, and placed the sharp side of the bokken against his oldest grandson’s neck. Fujita was in shock and glared at my grandfather with pure anger, knowing he was just shown up by an old man. My grandfather pulled his weapon away from Fujita’s neck turned to walk away. Once again, the old coot made beating us look easy. I’m talking Dawn McFarlane at a free kegger easy. Showing his lack of respect for my grandfather and great lack of honor, Fujita tried to catch my grandfather from behind with the bokken. He swung with all his might and… You better damn believe that he never hit his target. I snagged that bitch in midair with my right hand before it could even get close to my grandfather. My grandfather never turned around to see what was going to happen next. He just kept on walking away. I think he knew that I wasn’t going to let my cousin strike him, especially in such a manner. He trained me to be respectful and honor my ancestors, not be a self-centered, pride-driven bastard. He finally hit the point where he agreed that Fujita needed to be taught a lesson. Guess who was the guy to do it? My hand may have hurt more than you can ever imagine from the bokken crashing into it, but it was the last of my worries. My grandparents allowed this fucktard into their home out of the goodness of their hearts and love of their grandchildren. What did they get for it? I’ll be happy to tell you. They got nothing, but disrespect and headaches. I had hit my limit with Fujita. He may be blood, but you do not ever dishonor or try to hurt the ones I care about. His pride may have been a sin, but in my world, he committed a greater one. A sin that is only punishable by me beating the fucking shit out of him. His eyes locked on mine and he saw the blackest, most destructive soul ever looking back through the windows. I pulled the bokken from his grip and cast it aside. "Time for you to get a crash course in respect," I growled and started towards my disrespectful cousin. "Come get some, biatch," he fired back. "After I beat you, I’ll kick da shit out of da old man." My grandfather always said that something was not right about me. Something evil lurked deep within my soul and he would pray for those it would be unleashed upon. He could see it in my eyes when I am in battle. He called it "The kewashiikaotsuki" or "The grim look". When you look into my eyes, only the spirit of death exists. My dear cousin crossed the line and the reaper was now calling. He tried for a right hand and I slammed my forehead right into it. I could hear the crunching of his knuckles along the dense section of my cranium and it sent pain coursing through his entire arm. He tried to kick me with his right foot and I blocked it but kicking him with my own right in the inside of his calf. Wasting no motion, I fired an immediate sidekick with my right leg that struck him in the chest and sent him stumbling back. I proceeded to march towards him like a man possessed and in a sense I was. My anger had taken complete control. "I ain’t playing wit yo punk ass anymore, Xin," he enlightened me. "Time to fuck you up." Fujita turned up the heat and he shocked yours truly with a combination of punches. I was able to defend against them all and I caught his right arm by the wrist. With his arm in my possession, I struck him with a trio of kicks to his ribs before sweeping him down. I executed a cartwheel over him and used the momentum to pull him up off the ground just so I could slam my left shin into the dead center of his chest. He went flying backwards and he hurried to one knee. His hand clutched his chest tightly in agony and he looked up just in time to see me racing towards him with a sidekick. He could do nothing but brace for impact, as I tried to cave in his chest with my foot. I must admit that he was one tough fucker. He kept fighting back to his feet. He got back up and I let loose a barrage of punches. He did his best to cover up, but just as he got his hands up to block one to the head, another struck him in the gut, leaving him dazed and wide open for me to sock him in the jaw. He staggered back a few steps and tried to catch me with a sidekick of his own. The damage I was inflicting on him slowed him down immensely and I was able to drop down under the kick and thrust my foot upward, cramming his balls up between his ears. He let out a small squeak and fell to the ground in a heap. I kicked up to my feet and pounced upon him. Right hands rained down upon his face. Again and again, I punched him. My killer instinct in overdrive and after a while, his face was covered in a crimson mask. I just couldn’t stop myself. He had pressed the right buttons and I wasn’t in control of my actions anymore. My grandfather finally had to step in and pull me off of him. I glared down at the disrespectful punk. My body was trembling with rage and I was huffing and puffing. I wanted to kill him for what he did and I would have, if our grandfather hadn’t saved him. "You’re not a samurai," I thundered down at him. "You’re just Ralph Macchio." With that, I started back to the house. From that day on, he knew what respect was.
