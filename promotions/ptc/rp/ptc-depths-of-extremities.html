Depths Of Extremities

26 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When you want something bad enough you will go to the depth of extremities to get that “something”. If you don’t do what ya gotta do, then you obviously don’t want it that bad…nor do you deserve it. Me…I want the Extreme Title and to get a shot at it, I’ve gotta stand firm in a wrestling ring as my opponents are eliminated one by one. I am the first entry; I plan to be the last standing. I’ve called on my good friend Patrick Craig to assist me in my preparations. If anyone is going to get me ready, it’s him. He will take me to the depths I need to go…he will force me to do what is needed. He knows how I work and he knows how to exploit my abilities. He has figured out how to get the best out of me and that’s what I need – my very best. Anything less and I might as well stay in me bed. I trust Craig…I don’t always understand the way his mind works, I don’t think anyone ever will. All I know is that his ”training”, his “techniques” are what I need. All I need. Hanging from the crucifix, being able to see nothing but darkness with my eyelids firmly closed and my arms and legs tied to the cold wood with splinters prodding my back for eternity I listened to the aged, scruffy voice of Patrick Craig as the soothing fumes from the burning scented candles made their journey up my nostrils. The fumes slightly relaxed me, but not into any sense of peacefulness. It’s tough to be peaceful when little bastard splinters are poking your naked arse cheek. “Why am I hanging here as a mock Jesus with me balls freezing?” I asked myself numerous times while I shivered. I think that was Craig’s point. He wanted me to feel what Jesus felt like. It seemed a little blasphemous to me, but he was my religious guide and I had no right to question his ways. He performed a miracle with me. He saved my life. I was plummeting into the ocean in an aeroplane and then suddenly, I wake in his temple of sorts. And so I began my journey into Grandism. He saved my life, if he wanted me to go rape and kill a Mother Theresa if she was still alive and well (God bless her soul) I wouldn’t think twice about doing it. Well, I might think twice but in the end I’d probably comply and do whatever this bloke asked of me. I’m forever in debt to him. “Soon you shall enter a Royal Rumble.” my ears perked at the mention of my upcoming match. “This Royal Rumble…it’s a match to determine the standings of Prime Time Central’s Extreme Title Division. And you…you’re going to be the very first person to step into that ring.” Craig continued as the footsteps that echoed through my ears and the room we were in told me he was pacing back and forth. I remained motionless bar the uncontrollable shivering I felt, but I listened intently and soaking up every single word that came out of that man’s mouth. “You have the most undesirable placement in this match-up. You’ve got to fend off sixteen other fine competitors, most of which are probably more famous and higher in everyone’s estimation…everyone except for me.” Craig always, always had faith in my ability. He believes I can beat anyone and everyone if I put my mind to it. He thinks I have the attributes to be the champions of champions…and I gotta say, I agree with the lad. All due respect to everyone, but on my day I can rip anyone a new arsehole. “Next Generation Wrestling’s KrazE will also be in the ring from the very start. He’ll be the first opponent you’ll lock eyes on. He’ll be the first person you will come to blows with on the night,” Craig said as if giving a motivational speech…which he was giving, I suppose. “Marah…if you get eliminated by fucking KrazE, I will grab a katana and chop your penis straight the hell off!” Whoa their boss, I think my schlong shrunk just a little. I must agree with him though, I really shouldn’t lose to Wayne “KrazE” Minor. I’m above him…no, really I am. I couldn’t disappoint Patrick losing like that…I couldn’t disappoint myself. The patter of his shoes against the hard floor continued to bore a hole through my head. An annoying bang of a hair that tickled my nose divided my attention momentarily. I blew it back into place and rejoined the rant. “You could become the next Extreme Champion of PTC. Do you know what that means?” he asked me, I quickly graced him with an answer. The words had long gaps between them and slightly stuttered, but I got them out in my own time. “F…fame….sp-sponsor-sor-ship…money.” The noise of the lashing I received across my stomach was almost as painful as the callous stinging sensation that immediately followed. He wasn’t afraid to show me the error of my ways, a quick lashing now and then done me the world of could…well, that’s what he said. He never did anything hard enough to leave a permanent mark but it sure as hell hurt. “Do you really think I’ve got you up there so you’ll have a better chance at winning fame? Getting sponsorship? To earn money?” Craig scowled at me; I quickly got the point that I was wrong in my assumptions, in my goals. “Becoming the Extreme Championship means you’ll get a new sense of pride, winning the rumble means you’ll have new ambitions…it means responsibility. You are up there as naked as a skinned squirrel in preparation for this journey you’re about to embark on. I’m sure that being crucified in this manner will desensitise you to anything you could possibly encounter in the rumble…don’t you ever dare think again that I’m going to this trouble so a fuck-ass like you can become famous…” Okay, so let me get this straight. Being crucified is a training technique now? Julius Caesar must be so proud. For fuck sake, could he not just have me run through a snowy woodland with a big arse log on me shoulder or somethin’? “Do you really think you can survive this rumble? Survive sixteen wrestlers?” he inquired. Well, did I? Nah, I didn’t. I thought I’d do well and pocket a few quid and maybe get me name out there a bit, but I didn’t tell him that. Does it look like I want another bloody lashing? I nodded at him positively. I really don’t think he believed me. “Good,” he said, “You should think you can win this. You’ve got every chance. How many of these pillocks beat a seven foot tall Neo Void God with yakuza backing, eh?” “Not fuckin’ many.” I retorted. I actually don’t think any of them had beaten Akiyama. I feckin’ did. Aye, I sorted that chap out right. I’d beaten a good few big names in my time, bigger talents than the pricks and sluts I’ll be goin’ up against in this rumble. Craig was right, I have every bloody chance of winning this damn thing. I heard the crank of the wheel again…I felt it too. I don’t know what sorta set up the bloke had, but every few minutes he’d crank that wheel and the cross would stretch, in turn it stretched me. I don’t think I can stretch anymore. Me bloody muscles feel like burstin’ out of my skin. Trainin’ my bollocks, I have a sneaky feeling this lad is just trying to torture me for not giving him some of the dosh Binsei Kinaka, ahem, “donated” to me back in Japan (yeah, Aki isn’t the only one in RPW with yakuza connections, biatch). I tried to hold it in but the pain was too much and I let out a groan. Jesus, even groaning hurts at this point. Then my feet were abruptly granted freedom. Soon afterwards I felt cold steel on my left wrist and then that gained liberty. My right wrist was soon introduced to the same feeling…and then my face became introduced with cold floor. Aye, I fell right off that cross and smacked off that ground. I couldn’t help but laugh. Yes, it hurt like the morning after a night with a three dollar fifty Ukrainian whore but it musta looked bloody funny. My laughing grew maniacal; unfortunately Craig didn’t join in on the fun. I looked up at him with watered eyes (partly because of laughter, partly because I JUST FELL ON THE FECKIN’ FLOOR FACE FRONT, NAKED!) and suddenly my laughter ceased. That face could turn a room of toddlers on coke (no, not the mineral) into a bunch of old ladies at the pope’s funeral. He turned around towards a mural wall and walked to towards the wooden double doors that look like they were there since the Vikings invaded Ireland. “I’ll see you tomorrow” were the only words he articulated to me as he left the extraordinary large room (in all dimensions – height, breadth and length. I swear the roof musta been thirty feet high) into another part of the building while I clothed myself. Once I tied my sneaker laces I was off. I left through the side door that lead right onto a footpath on the side of a busy street. From the outside the building looked nothing more than another building with a massive amount of graffiti sprayed onto it’s faded red-bricked wall. I zipped up my black jacket to try protect me from the swift cold breeze that cut right through me. While I walked along the footpath, I felt like my shinbone was pushing its way through my calf muscle. That excruciating pain plus the wind made me wish more than once that I could be inside me own house in front of a nice fire while eating a nice stew. You couldn’t imagine my glee when a nice, long black limousine pulled up onto the curb. Yeah, it’s a kind of odd thing to happen but with the associates I have it doesn’t seem to put me in awe anymore when one pulls up beside me. I knew this car, ‘twas Binsei Kinaka’s (aye, the guy I mentioned earlier that gave me that “donation”). I opened the back door without a second thought and leapt in. A nice leather interior, a little TV, a mini-bar…yeah, ya get the point. It’s basically like every limo interior ya see in one of them cheesy films. It wasn’t a stew in front of a fire, but I wasn’t about to complain. Binsei looked his usual self, he must have three hundred and sixty six identical outfits for every day of a leap year. Who knew one guy could buy so many black, silk shirts and nicely pressed black trouser pants? He greeted me with the same smirk he greeted me when I first met him back in that restaurant in Japan. He seemed as cocky as ever and easily fitting into his new Western World surroundings. “A’right Binsei?” I spouted at him like I wasn’t intimidated by a feckin’ Yakuza boss. I figured that if I didn’t show me fear he wouldn’t harm me…like a dog. A dog with guns and a gagillion peons that would take enjoyment from blasting my brains all over a wall. “I am good, very good. This weather is kind of cold though.” I hated when he made small talk with me, it just didn’t sit right with me. “Ha, ya should see Ireland this time of year. It was like an iceberg the last time I was there, couldn’t take a slash outside without your piss freezin’ over half way through.” He gave me a big toothy smile, like he was imitating a Cheshire cat. He didn’t laugh though…I would have laughed; I thought it was well funny. I guess the Japs don’t get my sense of humour…the yanks usually don’t either. “I was wondering…” he said, when this guy wonders it’s usually about putting a bullet through somebody’s head. I hope this time it’s different, maybe he’s wonderin’ if I’d like to go to a football match…hopefully. “…About “BLING”. You know, that nightclub I gave you with the money to do it up?” God damn it, I haven’t been up there in months. I had left Rio Espines in charge, he’s probably turned it into a flamin’ whorehouse. I nodded at him confidently even though I was anything but. “I want to see it. I have never been there since I handed it over to you. But, I don’t know exactly where it is, so I need someone to show my driver here where it is.” Oh no, no, no, no, no, NO! Shite. Why the hell does he want to see it? What fuckin’ business is it of his? Son of a rotten potato. Bastard. Go back to Japan you bloody wankstain. “Sure, no problem. We can head up tonight if ya like. I’d just need to grab some gear from me apartment first.” Zuh? Jayo? What the hell did ya say that for? Ugh, at least I get a free trip to Sacramento. “Tremendous. I’ll have Hirotoshi here drop you off.” Binsei said with a smile. “Hirotoshi!! Drop us off at Mr. Marah’s home!” Jesus, when this guy shouts you fuckin’ listen, that’s for sure. “Another thing I wish to talk to you about is this Extreme Title Rumble you’re involved in.” Does he have to talk about that? I’ve had nothin’ else to think about for the last hour and a half hanging from that God forsaken cross and had that little pep talk from Paddy. And then he informed me of something I really didn’t want to know. “I have money on you to win.” Brilliant. Bloody brilliant. Like I didn’t have enough pressure on me from Craig, now this guy tells me he is going to lose money if I don’t win. This is the last guy I want to lose money. I value my knees thank ya very much. “If you do succeed in winning I will give you a large sum of money. A little incentive that you could use to do something new with “BLING” or spend it on whores or whatever. It’ll be yours to do whatever you wish with it.” Eh, okay. So, if I win I get a title shot and a lot of money (when this bloke says a large sum, he means it) and if I lose I could possibly get my knees beaten in…fair enough, I guess. I shoot him an appreciative smile, “Cheers. Very generous of ya there, lad.” “No need for that, Jason. Just win and we’ll both be happy.” Ah, that’s a nice thing to say. Fair play to him…but I bet that the money I get if I win will be nothing but pocket change to the money he’ll receive. Probably nothing but pocket change to whatever he bet. But still, better than nothing. “I want you to win, not just for the money but because I like to see you succeed. You’re a unique person Jason and have a lot of potential in the world of wrestling…and with a little incentive and a little push and shove to some promoters you could make us both a large amount of money.” I like his faith in me…I’m still undecided on where I stand on his intimidating promoters technique and I’m still just a little weary on the fact that I’m his “guy” in wrestling. It could get dangerous if things don’t go to plan…well, go to his plans. So, I’m going back to Saaaaaaaaaacramento, Californ-I-A. Going back to The X’s birthplace. It’ll bring back memories for sure, whether they’re good or bad is still to be seen. But what emotions are riled up by this visit is the least of my worries, the state of the place is just about at the top of that list. I wouldn’t be surprised if Rio messed things up big time. I’ve never really trusted him with these kinda things but I didn’t have the time to do it myself, so, perfect time for his second chance…this is probably his hundredth second chance. Still, that’s not at the very top of my list of concerns either. The Extreme Title Rumble is. Moraless. Jade. Debris. Shadow. KrazE. DDT. Thomas. McClancy. Lee. Houston. ICE. Vertigo. Tony V. Skar. Hamunya. Hanson. Those are the people on my mind right now. Nevermind Binsei or what he will do to me if I lose, nevermind the disappointment I will cause Craig, not alone to myself. Nevermind any of that garbage. All I’m worried about is tossing these bastards over that top rope and watch them plummet to the ground like the concorde. Simple as that.
