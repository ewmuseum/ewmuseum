Tyrone Grace Public Access Pimp Joined: 27 Feb 2003 Posts: 22 Location: Your mom's house Posted: Thu Apr 10, 2003 4:51 am Post subject: Prepared. [Extreme Cluster-Fuck RP 1] As we take a long, detailed look around PTC, many things are able to be seen with the naked eye. You have the legends in one corner, holding their records and accolades, and in the opposite corner, you have the pretenders. They are the legend want to be's, they are the one's who want fame, but don't want to put in any work or pay any dues. In another corner, their are those rookies, determined or not who just want to get known. There are a lot of people competing for spots, ranks and championships all around the organization, but this is just seen with the naked eye. Take a deeper look into PTC at the DWO. There are many determined individuals who want to topple each other every week, destroying opponent after opponent to get where they need to be. This is the final corner of the PTC look-around. Tyrone Grace rests in this corner, where he has been successfully defending titles, record and pride. This corner is different for every individual in PTC. Their corresponding organizations will be on the outside looking in at all others. What will make your federation stand out? Is there one person willing to step up and represent like Tyrone Grace will? Hopefully...because he won't. Ever since Tyrone came to the DWO, he has been unhappy. Not at the organization, but at himself. See, there are a lot of people who are happy where they are. Once they win one little piece of filthy gold, they think that they've already become legends. This isn't the case. You have to keep pushing for higher goals and set them frequently or you will become happy where you are, even if you are losing every week. Tyrone Grace wants to be known, not for the fans, but for his own credibility. He wants to be one of the many that come to mind when wrestling is thought about. How can this be achieved? This seems very impossible at this point, looking at the long line of competitors that stand before him, but once again...his unhappiness of being contempt will push him. The PTC has put together one tremendous event that will hopefully set the men apart from the boys and the winners from the losers. Real legends will prove why they have that title, and those trying to come up in the game will try their hardest just to get their names mentioned. Tyrone Grace will step into the ring with various individuals from various organizations, and try his best to hold his own. As the rain drops from the sky lightly on this gray day, Tyrone Grace sits at home in his living room. The large back window shows the wet backyard that sits behind his two story estate. Weather advisories flash across his screen, advising the people in his area of the thunderstorms that will roll through the area very soon. He pays no attention to these things, for he thinks of the possibilities of failure and victory in this match. Some names he'd heard of, some names were very new to him, but he didn't focus on this. The rain pelted his window while the wind picked up in velocity, and all Tyrone could think about were the millions of people that would see his performance as he represented the DWO in this fateful night. He could be injured, he could possibly be ended, but he would show everything extreme stood for, in his own definitions. He has a change of attitude as of late in the DWO, since his feelings for the fans had went down the drain. He realized that he no longer needed to fight for them, because they wouldn't be there on his darkest hour, he would. He needed to stop being a performer for them, but for himself. The only thing Tyrone Grace wanted at this point was when he viewed a show and saw the match that he participated in, that he surprised himself. This wasn't a very easy task. Thunder began to rumble the area while Tyrone Grace sat, still in a daze. "What do I have that sets me apart from the other competitors in this match?", Tyrone thought. Logically, this was a very good question. When he was younger, he'd come into a match, underestimating an opponent, not having a worry in the world. He'd gotten a lot wiser. Grace knew that if he was to have any chance in this match that he would need to be prepared mentally as well as physically. He knew that the other wrestlers could be bigger, small, quicker or more skilled that he was. There was one thing that set him apart from them; intelligence. Knowledge really was power, inside and outside of the ring. Tyrone Grace always prided himself on knowing what was going to happen and being aware, which was a vital part of his training and skills. Why was Tyrone Grace going to win this "extreme cluster-fuck" match? He had the intelligence and desire that some of these wrestlers and fighters lacked. What about those who didn't lack the intelligence and desire that he had? What about those that were just as focused as he was? That would all be determined in the ring. There was no predicting the future, all that was for certain....he would do his best. Call him cocky in the DWO, call him a performer, but make sure you call him determined. As Tyrone sat on the living room sofa watching the lightning leave stretch marks over the sky for split seconds, he wondered about what the other wrestlers were doing at this very moment. Were they working out or doing some type of meditation like him? Were they fucking off their time, or were they actually serious about being focused for this match? He didn't care. He had no sympathy for them and hoped that they slept on him. He knew that his name wasn't known by many, so he hoped that they underestimated him. As thunder sounded once again so loud that the cable flickered, he thought to himself, "I hope they sleep on me, because I will be prepared for them." _________________ "There's a thin line between confidence and cockiness, I have the guts to step over that line." 1/2 DWO Tag Team Champion
