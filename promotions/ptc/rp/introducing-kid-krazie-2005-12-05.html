Introducing Kid Krazie

5 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-12-05 The scene opens up in a large office where a desk made out of oak sits in one corner of the office littered with papers and books. Next to the desk is a wide bookshelf stocked full of medical books of various degrees from Philosophy to the maps of the human brain. In the center of the large office are a couple of high backed, thick cushiony leather couches with bronze studs positioned to hold the leather to the wood of the chair. Across from the chairs is a black leather couch that looks much like the chairs with the matching bronze studs and the thick cushions. There laying on the couch sprawled out with his feet crossed at the ankles and his hands entwined laying comfortably on his chest as his thumbs tap against each other is none other than patient number K3571-99. Doctor Lillian Walker sits comfortably behind the sprawled out patient with a clip boards on her lap and a pen in her hand as she scribbles notes on the paper. Dr. Walker: So when did you start talking to this Lou? Patient K smiles as his mind warps back to that fateful day. He remembers it like it was yesterday but he also sees it in his mind as if he were there right now! The office is transformed as the office is replaced with a parking lot, the furniture replaced with cars of various makes and models. There in the background is the local Kroger’s and leaving that store is a younger Patient K3571-99. He watches on as his younger self walks through the parking lot pushing a shopping cart full of groceries when out of no where Patient K3571-99 catches a slick spot on the ground and ends up slipping backwards with his feet in the air. He sees the clouds above him as he descends and whacks his head hard on the concrete. He looks around the parking lot with his vision blurry and his thoughts of pain jumbled with mysterious thoughts of pleasure. He smiles as he lies there on his back a small amount of blood oozing slowly out from the back of his head but his serene thoughts of pleasure are interrupted by the sweetest sounding voice a man can hear. It sounded like angels from heaven but Patient K3571-99 couldn’t figure out where the voice was coming from. Voice: You alright? …. That was a nasty spill! ….. Clean up on aisle 7! The patient can hear the laughter as this melody of a voice continues to speak. Patient K looks around the empty parking lot to discover that it was in fact an empty parking lot! No one was near him no one there unloading their groceries or making their way into the store. The only thing surrounding him was his blood oozing out onto the concrete his shopping cart sitting about a foot away and a few cars scattered throughout the parking lot, his own cherry red Mustang convertible sitting in one of the first spots nearby. Voice: So are you going to just lay there or am I supposed to load these groceries into your car by myself? Patient K sits up on his elbows as he again searches out that sweet sweet voice. A confused look comes across his face as he begins to climb to his feet dusting off the bits of gravel off his khaki shorts. As if he had just realized what happened he takes his right hand to the back of his head gently feeling a large gash on the back of his skull with his short brown buzzed hair offering little protection. Voice: I guess I will have to unload these by myself then… Patient K: Who is talking to me? Voice: Oh yeah sure, I watch you make a complete ass of your self and you pretend like I am not even here! That is a good way to save your self some embarrassment! Patient K: Seriously would you just come out from whatever hiding spot you are behind! As Patient K circles around looking for this mysterious voice he notices out the corner of his eye that his shopping cart is beginning to roll. The sliding doors from the Kroger entrance makes a buzzing noise as it swings open and a beautiful drop dead gorgeous woman walks out with daisy duke jeans, a sports bra that is two sizes too small for her double d ‘friends’ and her blonde hair bounces amongst her shoulders as she walks out from the grocery store. Patient K: So you are that joyful voice that I have been hearing my dearest Aphrodite… The blonde woman stops as she nears the patient and with a husky voice deeper than a lumber jack’s it says a simple greeting. But as it talks patient K can clearly see a large lump in the woman’s throat. Blonde: Like I don’t know who this Afro chick is cause my name is Nancy but like you’re pretty cute… Patient K stands there for a few moments shocked as he notices the large lump and the husky voice and a look of revelation crosses his face as the pieces fit the puzzle of the ‘Transexual Man/Woman.’ Patient K: Thanks you’re pretty hot yourself sweetness. Voice: Oh brother! You are actually interested in this she-male? If you look close enough you can see her… His… It’s… Nut poking out of those daisy dukes! The ‘it’ blushes and scoots closer to Patient K, oblivious to the female voice, but out of nowhere Patient K is rammed from behind pushing him into the he-she sending it stumbling backwards. Patient K turns and notices that the shopping cart is what attacked him! He looks incredulously from the shopping cart to the she-man. Voice: Yeah bitch get to steppin’! Patient K turns an astonished look upon the cart as once again the pieces of the puzzle begin to fall into place. Patient K stares long and hard at the shopping cart as the blonde thing shrugs it’s shoulders and walks off towards it’s car with her/his bags of groceries in her/his hands. Patient K: You… Voice: Bingo! Patient K: You can talk? Voice: You bet your sweet little ass I can and by the way that sweet little ass looks mighty fine from where I am standing! Patient K: Standing!? Voice: Rolling… Standing… Sitting… Positioned… All the same. Patient K: Who are you!!! Voice: Why I am Luna! Luna Luvadick! My friends call me Lu Lu though… Patient K: Luna-what!? Lu Lu: Yeah I know! My parents were whacko when they named me. But you gotta work with what ya got huh? The scene of the parking lot begins to fizzle and fade and is suddenly replaced with the large office with the doctor still sitting patiently behind him. He looks over at the clock that is ticking time away above the door to see that he was zoned out for a total of 2 minutes!!! Patient K: What was the question again? Dr. Walker: Lou? When did you start talking to this ‘person’? Patient K smiles again at the mere mention of this fabulous person… Patient K: It’s not Lou, but rather Lu Lu… Luna Luvadick. And it’s not some mere ‘person’ but rather the love of my life! She is my world and I must return to her at once! Dr. Walker: You are here because of this Lu Lu! You have lost your mind in laments terms and I am here to help you recover what is left so you may live a somewhat normal life! Do you even know where you are? Or what your name is? Patient K: Why of course! I am Kid Kraize and I am here getting a physical so I may return to the ring! Dr. Walker looks up from her note pad with a puzzled look on her face. Dr. Walker: Krazie!?!? Ring!?!? What in the world are you talking about? You are Ryan Kelly and you are in the Huntsville Mental Institution in Huntsville Texas! Krazie: Absurd! Krazie jumps up from the couch and gets right in the Doctors face with an intense fire burning behind his bright green eyes. He looks the Doctor right in the eyes and says… Krazie: GTT is calling and it is a call I must not refuse! For I am Kid Krazie and I must go home! Mommy please… You must understand! Doctor Walker screams out for help as Krazie grabs the poor woman by the shoulders and gives her a little shake. The door bursts open and two large orderlies come storming in followed by an old wrinkled frail woman in white carrying a large seven inch syringe. The two orderlies walk straight up to Krazie and grab him firmly by the shoulders and drag him over to the couch where the old woman waits flicking the syringe with his wrinkled old finger. Krazie: Ah you two must be my ride! I shall be ready for departure soon enough! Orderly 1: Yeah whatever psycho! Orderly 2: Prick this prick so we can throw him in his cage and get this day over with! The old woman pops the syringe in Krazie’s arm and after a few seconds Krazie’s body goes limp in the orderly’s arms. The two men begin to carry the man away off to his white walled and heavily padded room. Krazie looks up to Dr. Walker and with a smirk on his face he give her one last message. Krazie: It’s almost time… Dr. Walker: Time!? Krazie: Time for GTT5!!! Krazie begins to laugh as the medication from the syringe continues to course through his body. As the orderlies carry Krazie down the halls and through multiple doors Krazie passes out somewhere through the maze just before they reach his door. With a quick forceful kick to the door it pops open and the two large helpers throw Krazie down into his padded room. Krazie lays there squirming every now and then cuddled up holding an imaginary object which is too big to be a person… Krazie: Ah Lu we shall meet again my love! For cupid has struck me with his sharp and very painful syringe and as I speak my love for you courses through my very blood! The scene fades as Krazie continues to lay there in his small poorly lit padded room with thoughts of Lu on his mind and home in his future. This is not the last you have heard from the man they call Krazie…
