Faith in Destiny

Date: 2004-09-15 _______________ ::[Introduction]:: _______________ The GTT, a tournament to define a champion. To bring recognition to a man or woman of unbelievable ability. One that will stand the test of time and endure all that comes before their path. The GTT tournament was destined to be another successful draw of talent from far and wide. The world's greatest fighters and warriors from the PTC and from sources outside. This tournament proved crucial for any being to rise to success within the PrimeTime. First Darek was out to Survive, to fight to bring a name to the land he calls his home. The Four Winds nation has sought the respect from the PrimeTime since Darek has been there, but instead they have been a mockery for the others within the PrimeTime to ridicule. This was going to stop, Survival was the outbreak of the monsterous Darek DeAngleo, but the PTC just didn't know it yet. The Angel was willing to go that extra mile to complete his mission in cleansing the souls of the wicked. There are many sinners within the PrimeTime and Darek wanted to bring each of them to the revalation that their life could be better under Gods grace. This began seemingly with one man, Chaplain Graves. A free agent in the wrestling world and a Canadian to match. Something that made it a sure point that Darek was not going to underestimate the man. Some of the greatest technical stars and speedy wrestlers came from the Northern brother of America. Trained to wrestle, Chaplain was going to prove to be something more than an obsticle in Darek's path, he was going to be a stepping stone unto the next level. ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ For me, this is beyond the distinctive honor that comes with being crowned as the winner. It's about catching up with my destiny. Long ago, the words of a troubled soul, the ominous Dark Angel targetting two certain individuals. Seymour Almasy and Tessa Windsow, two of the most dominant names in the profession today and yet when it came time to exchange wrestling capabilities with the blood thirsty tyrant, both backed away like the fearsome cowards they truly are. Some Global Champion, the man that has the privallege of calling himself that should not even be worthy of such an honour. Seymour Almasy failed to bring Dark Angel to his knees, what made him worthy of being coronated the Global Champion? His status within the PrimeTime elite, a future hall of famer, the old-timer willingly ran from the Angel, but fights like a champion in this tournament. Everyday, I hear how people want a chance to stand before Seymour. I have awaited my opportunity since he wrote his name on the contract long ago. A signing that is bonded in blood and I intend full well on collecting in this tournament. Despite what the others believe, the time for the Modern Messiah has come, I have grown weary of Seymour's games. Should the two of us stand before each other, there will be no friends, no allies and no mercy. The influence of the Global Champion extends beyond the reach of myself, yet I feel compelled to levitate towards him, waiting for my opportunity. The masses have favoured the once talented champion to advance into the finals, something I am destined to prevent even if that means I fall too. As God as my wittness, Seymour Almasy, the trash talking, incapable of standing up for himself, lepton shall soon be genuflexing before a greater power. Which leads me to why? What is my reason for being here, is it soley to make an example of those who duck my challenges? No, it's beyond that, it's beyond the pride and dignity of one man, this about a continuation of the Survival incident. The Four Winds community has something to prove to the world, and I am fated to be the one who leads the great nation to the promise land. The outcome of Maelstrom shall soon reveal the greatest champion in the history of our great federation. As I stand atop our land, watching as the North Wind Champion, I'll be sure to sway enemy attack both foreign and domestic. There will be none who have the talent or power to stand before the Ominous warrior of God's word and light. Standing before the greatest names in wrestling history is what my next mission. Seymour, Tessa, Deville, Eugene, Saij, Patti, Blaise, it doesn't matter where the journey begins, all that matters is that the journey does indeed begin. The beginning of the end for one Chaplain Graves. The poor misguided, inferior leption has decided to share his thoughts and story with the world. It's just too bad he will be packing his bags and walking out the door when the time comes. For me this is beyond proving myself to others, it's beyond outshinning my fellow fedmates and it's even beyond the Global Title shot that follows. This tournament, this battle, it's about the will of divine light. God is our sheppard and we are his flock, a few black sheep wander astray and he is forced to bring them back to his flock. I am that force, that power that makes things so. Like the wind, that is why I shall master the Four Winds and stand before the greatest the world has to offer. I see others entering this for their own reasons, some for the desire, others to dominate, some just lust for the glory, there are many reasons people have partaken in this event. Their reasons shall now be put on display, each of them showing themself for what they are really worth. Let the strong survive and the weak crumble. _____________ ::[The Story]:: _____________ Everyone has a story to tell, some of them happy, full of joy and wonderful things. Others, not so pleasant, hatred, betrayal, lust, any of these things can encompass ones life and change who they want to become. The story of a beautiful young woman, unhappy with her present sitaution, she is someoe who longed to be free. Tormented by the shadows of darkness, her soul was slowly consumed by a plague. It was as if something or someone had taken control of her, almost as if she was driving, but no one was behind the wheel. It began with her accusations, believing the one man that was determined to love her forever, was out to destroy her. A deep feeling that brought a sense of self destruction into her mind. Amanda continues to stare at her reflection. Until she leaps forward driving her skull into the mirror. Shards of glass errupt from the wall and scatter all over the counter, sink and floor. The cracked pieces left hanging on the wall are speckled with red stains of blood. She reaches into the sink and pulls out a long, sharp edge of the mirror. Cautiously she places the sharpest tip onto the fleshy pink skin covering her wrists. She presses the glass deeper and deeper into the flesh of her wrist, until a small puncture is made and through the opacity of the glass a red tinge can be seen. Her eyes immediately close and squwinch together with balls of water forming at the outer edges of each. Her lips curl inwards as the cold glass in pulled upward. A rigid line filled with a gushing red river is slowly moved upwards. The tears have begun to fall as the pain sensation is unlike any other pain before. Slowly the salty tears fall down the curvature of her face. Each tear that falls, falls for a reason and purpose. As the overwhelming sensation of pain begins to sink in, she can only squint her eyes and be taken by pain. She had felt pain before, but not like this. There was alway heartache, the love for her parents or lack there of, even when she had given birth, there was no suffering that could amount to this feeling now. A large piece of mirror jutted out from her flesh and used to dig a line up the inside of the forarm. She had remembered now, her life. "Isn't this wonderful Darek? I mean this is what you wanted isn't it?" a slow calm, but deep and rugged voice calls out to Darek. "I can't seperate myself from all we've done. I've become lost inside these thoughts you have, but I am not dead." Her eyes wince open for a second only to see the blood fall from the open wound. Instantly she releases the glass in her hand; purtruding from her arm the mirror side glimmer's her reflection to her eyes. She falls back against the wall, both eyes open staring in shock at the mess. The tears have stopped falling as the shock sets in. Her world becomes reality again for a momment. Reaching over to the glass with a pink and red hand, she grasps the glass but her hand slides down the sharp edges again and shred. She winces in pain and leans further down the wall, her head and shoulders are propped up by the wall and the rest of her body is gentley curled on the floor. A sea of primordial red ooze flows freely over the white linolium floor. It is dammed off by her petite, frail and fragile, nearly lifeless body. Amanda slides her hand back up the glass and as it comes to the top edge, she gives it really nice tug. The mirror comes out of her arm and she drops it onto the floor. It shatters into tiny pieces, many stained red with her lifeforce. With her bloody hand she instinctively drops it onto her wound and begins to place some preasure on it. The river seems to have ceased flowing once her hand blocks off the source. Some small gushes manage to squeeze out of the cracks in her hand. Her eyes open up and she finds herself looking directly at the mess. The voice slowly coo's again, "the sheer splendor and magnificance of lie, look at this frail woman. You led her to this fate Darek, Xantos only tried to bring her down. How do you feel knowing that your lustful desire for Patricia have lead you to this?" She opens her mouth but nothing comes out. She tries to scream, but cannot manage to vocalize her thoughts. Still staring at the bloody massacre before her eyes, she turns her head away and closes her eyes once again. Before she would slip out of conciousness, Amanda had only remembered to do what came natural. Finally, she drifts away into a world of thought... Slipping in and out of conciousness, the faint sound of something familiar is heard in the distance. Her mind begins to swirl as it tries to grasp onto the conept that she may make it through this. The sound becomes louder, and louder yet. But she couldn't help but thinking that in this state she may really be dellusional and have slipped into a false reality. But still, she refused to let go of her hope that the sound would be what she thought and hoped it was. She could now fully recognize the sound as a voice. To her it sounded so angelic, so pefect, that she could only manage to smile. Through the sheer agony and pain, she smiled at the voice. Whether it was a figment of her misguided imagination or reality, she could have sworn that the voice had called her name. Trying depserately to come back to complete awarness she tries to call back. But only has the voice to hold onto. "Amanda?" A small pause, "are you in there?" The door cracks open and as the foot steps in a splashing sound is made. Darek looks down to the pool of blood at his feet. Immediately a look of deep concern covers his face as he turns and looks towards the counter. The mirror is shattered and splattered with speckles of blood. Instantly he turns ghost white and freezes standing in a pool of blood. Her mouth falls open slightly, "...darek." The soft, faintness in her voice has a hardtime carrying and she can only hope it was enough for someone, anyone to hear. "Don't worry Darek, I'm not finished with you by far. Everything you see, everything you know, your trials, the Four Winds and even the PrimeTime will slowly become intertwinded into one big ball of mass hysteria for you to absorb. Your end is near, and like God let the good reverand Dimiscus die, he let Dark Angel perrish." The voice pauses for a moment, "but don't think you've gotten rid of Dark Knight just yet Darek, you may be pure but deep inside of you I feel your contempt, that hatred, you were bred for dominance. Now dominate. Take your mind from the lord and bring yourself to the anger that once resided within you." Finally Darek snaps out of it and turns the corner to the side of the last stall. He instinctively followed the flow of the blood only to his dismay to find Amanda holding her own rigidly cut wound. The blood seems to be endless, but Darek can only hope there is still life in Amanda. He had to take Dimiscus, he could not bare taking the life of his love. He drops to one knee, the blood soaks into the fabric of his pants as he leans over her body while applying more preasure to the laceration that could be life ending. Darek brushes the fine strands of blonde hair from the eyes of his love and places two fingers on the side of her neck. A look of shock crosses his face as he reaches to his left sleeve and tears it off. He quickly wraps it around the wrist of Amanda and ties it tightly. He leans forward and kisses her on the cheek and whispers... "Everything will be okay." And faintly with her final breaths she replies, "I know." Snapping forward in a deep cold sweat, Darek brings his arm across his brow to wipe away the perspiration. His eyes open widely as he glances down at the woman next to him, It was like everything had changed. Darek could only dream about what had happened, almost as if it were an act of God, Amanda was laying next to him. Making the sign of the holy crucifix, Darek tilts his head to the heavens and begins to utter a small prayer. Pushing the velvet quilt away from his body, Darek brings his feet to the side of the bed and plants them on the cold hardwood floor. Taking one last glance of his shoulder, his eyes had not been deceiving him, Amanda was lying next to him. Shaking his head, he felt a throbbing pain clench onto the back of his mind and slowly pulsate, causing severe pain. Planting his feet firmly, Darek rises from his bed and makes his way over to the bathroom at the end of the room. Carefully turning the handle as to not make any noise, he steps into the bathroom, snugging the door tightly closed before turning on the lights. At an instant, Darek clenches his eyes shut in the daunting bright, white light, now staring at himself in the mirror of his medicine cabinet, he tries to smile. The whole night had been full of nightmares, the first in a long time. Confused to the meaning of these dreams, he believed each vision he saw was there for a reason. Interpretation and puzzling the clues together were what Darek had to do, yet it all seemed so much easier than it sounded. Bringing his left hand grasping around the back of his head, he pops the cabinet open. Passing the Trials of Lust, Darek had taken the second step of eight to becoming closer to having his own soul purified. He wanted nothing more than to take his rightful place in Heaven, where he would watch over the land, protecting it from wrong and harm. The trials were his attempt to get to that time, but they presented much more than the future events, they changed the past. Dark Angel, the demonic inception of Darek had pulled Amanda's life support from the hospital wall. He secretly did it to be with one Patricia Anderson, to follow her womanly touch. He lusted for her and there was going to be nothing that prevented him from obtaining, just one taste of her sweetness. Going back to right the wrongs that had been writen in time, Darek uncovered the truth. Amanda was meant to die, her very own husband and her own son, Xantos had both plotted her demise. Darek was taken under an unconcious state of possession by Dark Angel, but Xantos acted out of malice and spite. He hated her for how he was forced to grow up, from one orphanage to another, kept secret from the demonic Dark Angel. Shaking his head to the side, Darek wakes from the deep trance like state. His life seemed to be piecing together, everything now felt intertwind, like a jigsaw puzzle slowly coming together. Right now, his head hurt, reaching into the cabinet he pulls out a bottle of pain killers. Since giving the Angel of Death status, the pain he felt had become so much more sensational. He knew he was being drained of his immortality and his powers. The trails were going to solve that, Darek was confident in that, but what would he do until then. The pain was becoming unbearable, Survival, Blaise McClancy, then both Kyo Kushinaga and Patti Anderson at Maelstrom V, how much more could his body take. Closingh is eyes, he tilts his head back and swallows the pills. Settng the bottle back inside the cabinet, he reaches over and brings the mirror closed. In the reflection he watches the mirror pass the shower, the toilet, then show his side and as he pushed it closed more, the rest of his body. Jumping into the air as the cabinet closes, Darek turns around gasping for air. "You scared me. I didn't see or hear you." "Sorry love." Amanda begins, she is dressed in a white lace nightie, and underwear. The material has a fuzzy opacity, but her ample cheast can be formed from behind the material. "I saw the light on." Darek shakes his head and tries to regain his composure, "no, I am sorry. I shouldn't have turned the light on." He turns back towards the medicine cabinet, resting his hands on the frame of the sink and lifting his head slowly to stare into his cold reflection. "So much has changed Amanda, more than you could ever want to fathom. I am lost myself, now I have you here and things have become so much different." "I've never left you Darek, we were destined to be together. Through hell or highwater I vowed to love you for eternity." Her words sunk into Darek's mind. He closes his eyes and then rests his face in his now accepting hands. Amanda steps into the light a little more, her radiant red hair is tossed over her shoulder as she checks on her husband. Clueless to her own demise, it was like it never happened. But Darek still remembered, everyone remembered but Amanada. He was going to have to tell her, he was going to have to admit to everything. The demon that resided within his soul had finished with Amanda and tossed her away like yesterdays trash, what Dark Angel really wanted was a piece of what Patti Anderson was serving. The monster turned his back on his family, on his stablemates and then was lead to slaughter. The beautiful but deadly Anderson had manipulated Dark Angel to get what she wanted from him and then sent him to the gallows. Darek was what remained after the beatting the KnightMare Brigade of the UWF and 4ww had finished. Darek felt as though Dark Angel was dead, but those words and that voice. They echoed through his mind like a ringing gunshot. That dream, seeing Amanda lie in a poll of her own blood again, hearing the demon proclaim he was not finished with Darek. It all became too much of a burden on him, he wept, but within the own confines of his hands as they sheltered his face. The whole world was being turned upside down and he was stuck at the epicenter. He was about to own up to the mistakes he made, to admit to his lustful desires. This would kill Amanda inside, knowing that Darek and her son tried to end her life, and that Darek did it for the touch of another woman, it would destroy her. Lifting his head from his hands, his reddened face now exposed, showed the teary eyes of Darek DeAngleo. Amanda rushed to his side and wrapped her arm around his shoulder. This only made him feel worse, the woman truly did love him. The demon that once inhabited his body had made life hell for Darek, wondering how to begin, Darek stepped away from Amanda's touch. Walking towards the hottub, he sits on the outside rim and looks over at the beautiful woman. She is standing just inside the door frame, a look of loving concern has formed on her face as she meets Darek's eyes. She felt that Darek was somehoe different now, he was no longer consumed by he darkness that once plagued his soul. The demon that turned Darek's deepest desires to live, whatever Darek lusted for, he took. If he wanted a title, he destroyed the being who possessed it, if he wanted a woman, he charmed his way in. There was no stopping the demon, but Darek seemed free of this monster. "Alot has happened Amanda. Many things you may not understand and somethings that you won't want to understand. The situation itself confuses the hell out of me, but each day I wake and move on." Rubbing his hand across his face, Darek brings his eyes away from Amanda's. This was something even he didn't have the nerve to tell her face to face. "I am a changed man now, let me assure you that. Everything that transpired had a reason for happening, some I can justify and others I cannot. Rest assured, I have always loved you." Standing abruptly still, Amanda looks down at the wreak before her. The man was about to compete in the GTT, a PrimeTime Central tournament against Chaplain Graves and he could hardly hold himself together. For some reason she couldn't remember the last months of her life, she awoke in the hospital under the guidence of William and was reunited with Darek sometime after. Se never questioned being in the hospital, but seeing Darek struggling the way he was, the questions came rushing to her head. Looking down at her wrists, the scars were there and would always be there as a constant reminder of a shallow time in her life. "What do you have to tell me Darek?" She asks, her mind slowly began to imagine the worst case scenerarios. "Why was I in the hospital." "I had a nightmare tonight." Darek begins, never lifting his heavy eyes from the floor. "One that reminded me of who you really were, one that reminded me of who I was. A nightmare that has plagued me since you've come back into my life." "You're talking nonesense." She interupts fighting back tears of her own, "I have never left or abandoned you or our love. Together we are eternity." Shaking his head side to side, Darek just wavers her comments and continues his story. "You cut your wrists with pieces of glass from a broken mirror. The suicide attempt failed as I had found you lying in a sea of your own blood. A sight that haunts me in my dreams and memories. I wanted you to be better..." Trailing off, Darek lifts his eyes to see Amanda starring at the mirror and then looking down to her wrists. Her head twitches a little as she stares into the reflection. And like a great flash of lightning, a memory sears through her mind, burning itself into her memory. Amanda continues to stare at her reflection. Until she leaps forward driving her skull into the mirror. Shards of glass errupt from the wall and scatter all over the counter, sink and floor. The cracked pieces left hanging on the wall are speckled with red stains of blood. She reaches into the sink and pulls out a long, sharp edge of the mirror. Cautiously she places the sharpest tip onto the fleshy pink skin covering her wrists. "I remember." Darek lifts his head and brings his eyes to see Amanada's back as she glared into the mirror. "You do?" "Everything." And again, another memory shot into her mind. Everything was coming back to her bit by bit. She was destined to figure out what happened to her. But for now she was going to have to suffering seeing only bits and pieces at a time. Darek could see that she was in some trouble, but he knew that she would figure it out. Everything Dark Angel had done was a travesty, but the wall he put between Amanda and Darek was by far the worst thing of all. It was Dark Angel that pulled Amanda's respirators plug from the wall bringing the machine to a quick stop. Amanda didn't know this yet, but she was bound to find out as her memory came back. Placing his palm across her forhead he stops for a momment. A look of sadness seems to come across his face as he lowers his forhead onto hers... "I have always love you. You and no other." He raises his forhead and gently places his moist pink lips just above her eyebrow. Slowly he bring his lips away from her body... "We were meant to be eternal..." "I never meant for anything to happen the way it did. I wasn't myself, there was no control. I felt as though I was watching myself from the outside, starring at my actions and unable to stop myself." She turns away from the mirror and walks over to Darek. Sitting next to him, she runs her arm along his back. Bringing her fingers up to his hair, she moves them gentley through. Raising his head, Darek looks into Amanda's eyes, "let's not worry about this. You've been stressed out beyond belief. Survival, Maelstrom and now the GTT, you're over working yourself. Whatever it is that you need to tell me, it can wait until the afternoon." She looks to the clock resting on a shelving unit above the toilet, "it's two twenty three, let's go back to bed?" she begs. Sighing, Darek nods his head. All his sins were about to catch up with him and of them all, one of the seven deadlies sins of all, lust marked his soul guilty for what Dark Angel did to Amanada. Getting to his feet, he and his wife head back out of the bathroom. The pain in his head has seemeingly ceased, and he flips the light switch turning out the light. Making his way back to the bed, Darek crawls in, he looks over at Amanda and kisses her gentley on the lips. The two remain together for a moment before nestling snuggly under the covers to get some rest. To Be Continued...
