9 July 2009 [No fade, just a smash cut to the original king of all produce, the one and only King Blueberry.] [Hello my lovely square brackets. Oh, how I've missed you so.] [He's dressed simply in denim shorts (blue of course), a pale gray "This Foster (Nackedy) is Bananas, B-A-N-A-N-A-S" tee shirt, and his trademark blueberry mask. With his arms folded, he simply stares off to his left, as if the previous scene, that including the Baron Von Blackberry, is unfolding there before his eyes.] [He shakes his head.] [And again.] [He turns his attention to the camera before him, uncrosses his arms, flails them wildly in the air, and then lets them simply slap against his sides.] Blueberry: I... wow. I don't even know. [Once again, his attention is drawn off to the left. After a moment he buries his face in his hands, shaking his head.] Blueberry: What the... that doesn't even... oy. [The Yahweh of Yogurt refocuses his gaze on the camera before him, and silently raises his right hand across his chest, pointing off of the left. Shrugging his shoulder, he turns his right hand palm up, but only briefly.] [It's hand-gesture code for "what the fuck is up with that guy?!"] Blueberry: What the fuck's up with that guy? [See?] Blueberry: Seriously. [Again his attention is drawn off-screen and to the left. This time he's yelling.] Blueberry: Robot heads don't just grow on trees, you know! That's so coming out of your allowance! [Behind his mask his brow furrows.] Blueberry: I'm so going to slap that guy. [Having accomplished nothing at all, we end the scene.] ---- 16 July 2009 [Fade in.] [Two jobbers are totally sucking it up hardcore in the middle of the ring, and the fans are totally bored, but you’d never know it if you were listening to the announcers, because they’re selling every move like it’s a gunshot.] Jim Ross: WHAT A SLOBBERKNOCKER! Tony Schiavone: Fans, this is the greatest night in the history of our sport. [The jobbers, who we will refer to as Larry Borowitz and “Iron” Jake Sharpe, are just slapping each other like sissies when a very special song comes over the loudspeakers.] [It’s so awesome that every girl who hears it immediately gets pregnant.] He He is a man A blueberry man… [Every sane person in the building, plus those watching at home, are MARKING THE FUCK OUT.] Tiny Schiavino: Fans, this is the greatest night in the history of our sport. [Out through the curtain steps King Blueberry, and he’s looking mighty sexy tonight in his cut-off jeans and leather jacket. He’s so dark and mysterious in his fruitiness that you can’t not want to be just like him.] [Girls throw their panties at him while he calmly strides to the ring, ready to put a hurtin’ on a pair of unsuspecting schmucks.] Jim Ross: BAH GAWD WHATTAMATCH! Tony Toni Tone Schiavone: Fans, this is the greatest night in the history of our sport. [Tony Toni Tone has done it again.] [Larry and Jake don’t seem to give a shit that they’re about to get dealt with, berry-style.] [King Blueberry slides under the bottom rope, and then starts mercilessly kicking everyone in sight in the balls, following all these solid ball-shots up with DDTs, since it’s 2000 and everyone uses this as a finisher.] [Raven rulz!] Crowd: RRRRAAAAAAAAAAH!!! WE HATD THAT MATCH AND U SAVD US!!!! HAVE OUR BABEEZ!! [King Blueberraven sits in the corner for a minute, because he’s all goth and introspective and shit, and then he gets up and they give him a mic.] Tony Shineonyoucrazydiamond: Fans, this is the greatest night in the history of our sport. Blueberry: Pain, and things, and other things, and some dark stuff… this is the inner meat of my soul. It’s not all that tasty, unless you form it into patties and cook it on the grill of bitterness and sorrow, for that is where my inner essence of torment lives. [A million goth kids go off and cut themselves while they listen to The Cure because they are so moved by the King’s words.] [King Blueberraven points to the big screen, which now has this picture on it.] Blueberraven: I will take your Goblet of Infinity and when I get my hands on it will mark it with the darkest symbol known to mankind. Quote the berry, nevermore. [He drops the mic and leaves the ring, jumping over the guardrail to be with his people at ringside because they are the only ones who know his pain.] [Fade.]
