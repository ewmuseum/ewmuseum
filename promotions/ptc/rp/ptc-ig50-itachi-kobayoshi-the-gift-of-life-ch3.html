Itachi Kobayoshi versus Dave Hurst

“The Gift of Life” Featuring: Itachi Kobayoshi Written By: [name withheld] Word Count: 901 “This... this can't be. I just got two people back, now you're telling me... that my mother... Mom...” Gai's words faded into nonsense as he fell to his knees in front of the memorial, sobbing uncontrolably. Kisame began to step forward, before being held back by Itachi. “We need to let him deal with this for a few moments alone. We can't imagine what he's going through, and we shouldn't intrude.” Itachi explained to his partner, before motioning in another direction, as they walked off, keeping their distance, while trying to keep an eye on Gai. “This... this isn't fair... I started going to America with Itachi-san and Kisame-san for you... I was hoping you'd be watching, and recognize me. I was hoping you'd see me, and reach out to me... I was hoping I'd find you... but not like this...” Gai's head fell as he tried to hold back the tears, long enough to speak to his mother... long enough to make sense of all of this. “I don't even know your name... I don't even know our name...” He says as he leans forward, and moves some of the greenery from in front of the inscription. “Sarah Solah. Mom... I never hated you... I never got mad that I lost you, or that you had to leave me... I always loved you, and only thought of the day I'd finally get to meet you... You gave me the strength when I was small to brush off the people who would point and call me 'Gaijin', and refuse to let me eat, or spare me change. Working towards the day i got to find you made me who I am today... and for that... I love you Mom...” Gai can't continue as he begins crying again, not knowing what to do anymore. “She was a good woman, Gai. She's nothing short of a hero.” Itachi's words caused Gai to turn back, as Kisame and Itachi stood supportively behind him. “How did she... I mean... what caused her... why did she die?” Gai still struggling to form a sentence asks. “She got shot... by a stray bullet while passing by a store being robbed. She was eight months pregnant. The bullet had hit her and her baby, and the doctors told her that they had to operate right away if they were going to save the baby. She told them to do everything to save her baby, and they did. A healthy baby boy was born. She was able to hold him once... before complications from the surgery caused her to bleed internally... She died with you in her arms.” Itachi's recount had Gai in tears again, and even Kisame was looking like he was near tears. “She died... to save my life?” Gai was able to ask. “Yes. Her last wish was for you to be healthy, and so far, it seems that's come true.” Kisame responded, able to control his emotions. “All this time... I had thought she abandoned me... I thought she didn't want me... but really i was all she wanted...” Gai's statements were more spoken out loud for himself, than anyone else. The three stayed there for a few hours, letting Gai regain his composure, and telling him everything they had learned about his mother. The sun began to set in the horizon, and the three began to wlak back towards town. “What about the man that shot her?” Gai suddenly asked while walking. “What about him, Gai. He didn't mean to harm her, it was an accident, a robbery gone wrong.” Kisame tried to rationalize to him. “Did they ever catch him? Does he know what he did? What happened to him?” Gai bombarded the pair with questions, each as difficult for them to answer as any other. “Well... they caught him, yes. He knows what he did... and he was traumatized, as well. He didn't even mean to fire the gun, never mind shoot a person. He's serving a life term in the Tokyo Correctional Facility. So, justice was served.” Itachi explained to him, hoping that would quench his curiosity. “Alright... so tonight we find a hotel and tomorrow, we're off to the prison.” Gai said with an extra bit of certainty in his voice. “There's no need for that... like i said, he meant no harm towards her, and all of this will just be more traumatic to you. I can't allow it.” Kisame said, taken by surprise at Gai's request. “This is my journey. You don't get to decide what happens, I do. Therefore, tomorrow, we're going.” Gai turned and looked Kisame in the eyes as he spoke, showing no signs of pushing on the subject. “Either you can come with me... or I'll meet you back at home, I know my way home.” “Kisame, there's no point in arguing, he's right. This is his journey, and not even he can decide it's course. We have no choice.” Itachi said to try and calm the situation, as he stepped between the two. “Itachi... you know this is a bad idea.” Kisame snapped. “True, but Gai has grown since we last saw him... he's mature enough to handle his own mistakes now...” Itachi turned and looked towards Gai, who still stood strong, trying to prove his point. “We'll just have to be there when it all becomes too much for him.” Chapter 3 of 4 Chapter One: “The Longest Journey Begins With the Smallest Step” Chapter Two: “The Harshest Reality is the Truth” Chapter Three: “The Gift of Life” Chapter Four: “Where One Ends... Another Begins”
