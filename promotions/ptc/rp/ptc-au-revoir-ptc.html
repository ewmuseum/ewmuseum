IG-Last RP thread

20 December 2010

Au Revoir, PTC! 12/18/2010 Las Vegas, Nevada… The conference room at the Mandalay Bay Hotel is filled with wrestling media and both local and national sports reporters. Soon after the HOW.com camera starts up to record this for Best Studios, “The Franchise” James von Doom walks into the conference room wearing a cut-off sweat jacket and carrying his HOW ICON Championship belt. He walks over to the podium and sets down his newly won championship in front of him on the podium. His manager the New Jersey scumbag Eddie Pirelli stands over to the side holding what appears to be a severed head. James looks around to the room for a moment before he begins to address the reporters in attendance for another James von Doom Press Conference. Von Doom: I’ve called this James von Doom Press Conference to address a few things that have happened in the recent weeks and months. Firstly this belt... He motions to the title belt sitting in front of him on the podium. A smile creeps across his face like a slow moving slug before he continues. Von Doom: This past Tuesday night in London I won the ICON Title at HOW’s ICONIC pay-per-view. It was yet another match that nobody gave me a chance in hell of winning and I proved them wrong. It was one of the biggest upsets in HOW history which made it even more important. However that’s not what I want to discuss right now. What I want to discuss is some news I received in an e-mail from PTC management. It was a message in my e-mail that said Primetime Central, my home since 2004, was closing. Looking down toward the ground, he seems genuinely sad about the news of PTC’s closing. Wiping away a crocodile tear, he continues with a sadness in his voice not heard since his victory speech as manager of the Killer Pirates when they won the PTC Tag Team Championships back in the day. Von Doom: Now that has was a stab wound right to my heart. I couldn’t believe the news when I was reading it. I have been a major part of this organization since two thousand and four. Some even consider me to be the heart and soul of this place. For years I fought month in and month out for Infinite Gauntlet titles as well as competing in almost every PTC fed there was. I’ve been in PRIME, UWF, GCW, GLOBAL, LOW, NFW, SCCW, FUSE, TEAM, and just about any PTC fed that there’s ever been since 2004. I’ve been in more PTC matches than just about anybody and to see this place close is sad. Not just for me, but every other wrestler who busted their ass in PTC competitions over the last ten years. Then I saw that there was one last Infinite Gauntlet match and considering that I’ve been in more IG matches than anybody, I figured I might as well compete in the last one. I’m on a mission now that started with this belt. He points to the HOW ICON Championship belt that is directly in front of him on the podium which brings a smile to his face. Von Doom: I won this belt to stick it to everyone who said that I never had a chance to be a major star in the wrestling business. Throughout the years no one and I mean NO ONE has been as ridiculed as I have been. My career has been a joke to many people just because I’ve managed wrestlers such as Cave Hulk, Wolfen, and the Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean. Well let me tell you something. Just because the people I managed were jokes doesn’t mean that I’m a joke of a wrestler. Every single week almost people have doubted me my entire career. My opponents almost always think that I don’t have a chance against them and, in most cases, I burn them in big match situations. I’ve had to deal with criticisms, name-calling, and everything else but I’m still here. I’m still standing. Nobody’s gotten rid of me yet and no one will. That all changed a few months ago. It got too much. EVERYONE TURNED ON ME! EVERYONE SAID THAT I WAS A JOKE AND THAT MY CAREER WAS DEAD! DEAD! I WON AWARDS BUT EVEN THAT WASN’T ENOUGH TO SHOW EVERYONE THAT I’M NOT A JOKE! Taking a moment to calm himself down, he quickly gets his emotions in control. He then looks over at Pirelli and nods. Eddie nods back before scampering out of the room. James then turns back to the reporters. Von Doom: That’s when I made the decision to retire from wrestling and go compete in the WEC. First I left WWA, not because I wanted to but because I felt the need to retire. I thought my career was dead. Then I left SCCW, my home for years, after losing to Legion. At that point I walked off into the sunset with my head held low. My career was dead and I was headed to the WEC. Soon after the WEC is in talks to merge with the UFC. This is only after my first fight which was the weekend after the Legion match. After my second fight I get a call from a man named Lee Best. He said that he wanted me to join HOW. I said I would think about it but he persisted over the next few days. Finally I relented and signed up but only to do a Halloween gimmick character named Trick ‘r Treat. Honestly I didn’t want him getting any heat from the bloggers and the internet wrestling community as a whole so that was the reason for the gimmick. A smile then creeps across his face yet again just like earlier in the press conference. Von Doom: But then something happened. I got the Halloween gimmick over big time when nobody thought it would work. Then I won the HOW World Tag Team Championship three weeks after arriving in the organization. A few weeks later I lost at the Rumble at the Rock 3 pay-per-view and was forced to reveal who I really was underneath the mask. After going apeshit on the douche-tard fans that were in attendance that evening, it was all gangbusters from there. After that I joined the Best Alliance, defended the Tag Titles for the Alliance, and then pulled off one of the biggest upsets of all time in HOW. I won the ICON Championship in the Main Event Ladder Match in the biggest match of the decade when none of you fucks believed I had a chance to win! NOBODY GAVE ME A CHANCE AND I PROVED YOU ALL WRONG! ALL OF YOU! I WAS SUPPOSED TO BE A NON-FACTOR BUT I WENT IN THERE AND KICKED ASS! He starts pointing at everyone in attendance before brushing his hair back as he gets himself calmed down. Once he’s calm, he continues speaking to everyone in attendance. Von Doom: It should be very obvious right now that the reason that I’m not going anywhere is because I have the heart of a champion and there’s very few wrestlers out there that have the heart that I do. Sure, I’m not the best in the world. I’ve admitted that many times…but that doesn’t make me the worst in the world, either. That said, I am looking forward to one last shot at winning the Infinite Gauntlet Title that I‘ve never won before. I want the PTC go out with a bang in a good way. Instead of bringing back old shit from the past and getting into hate filled arguments with wrestlers I’ve had issues with in the past, I want to go out on a more positive note. The last four months have been the best of my career since 2006 and I plan on having an even better 2011. That past is past and in the last days of the PTC, I want to make sure no one forgets this place because I, for one, never will. Eddie Pirelli returns with a waiter carrying a tray that has a bottle of wine and two filled wine glasses on it. The waiter goes over and hands one to James before going back to Eddie and handing him the other wineglass. Von Doom holds up his wine glass as does Pirelli. Von Doom: One last toast to the PTC. He then drinks all of his wine in one chug before setting the glass down on the podium behind the belt. At this point he waves goodbye to the PTC. Von Doom: Au revoir and Hasta La Vista, PTC! After waving, he picks up the belt off of the podium before walking out of the conference room with Eddie Pirelli. This will be his last interaction with the PTC and he knows it. This is a sad day indeed, for everyone associated with the PTC.
