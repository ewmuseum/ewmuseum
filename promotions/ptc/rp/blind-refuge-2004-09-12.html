Blind Refuge

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 A week since his meeting with Cammy, and the bitter taste in his mouth was still as prominent as it was the moment he left her apartment. His mind was on an emotional roller coaster that he had never experienced, and it frightened the young fighter. He had become accustomed to physical pain, both before and during his wrestling career, but never had he felt such torment within. Trying to wrap his mind around this woman's reasoning lead to nowhere, and was easily masked by the anger he could not hold back. "How could I let this happen?" Tristan let his thoughts whisper through his chapped lips, as he scaled the steps to his destination. A few hundred feet in front of him lied the battleground that would make or break his career, yet the only reflection he could conjur up was the look on his beautiful ex's face when she broke the news. The regret in her eyes was menacing, and the sorrowful tremble in her voice was dreadful. --- "I have a daughter Tristan ... She's almost 2 and a half ... Her name is Kayla." In his inebriated state, this piece of information was not what he was expecting to hear from the woman he used to share his bed with. It is amazing however, just how quickly said information will sober a man up. With an awestruck look upon his face, Tristan tried to do the math in his head. He left for the Orient just over 3 years ago, and was with Cammy until the minute he left that terminal. 'This child has to be mine.' he thought to himself. 'How could she not tell me I have a daughter?' Anger was quickly exchanged with doubt, as he let the unthinkable slip into his mind. 'Maybe its not mine. Did she cheat on me?' Neither consideration pleased the 23 year old wrestler, so attempting to appear sober, he went into denial. "Wow, Ms. Cameron Withers has a daughter. Alert the media." Cammy tried cracking a smile, but was visibly holding back tears. Tristan, at a loss for words, stood straight up from his hunched stance, and opened his eyes wide. "Anyway, I'm sure she's sleeping, so I better get goin'. Don't want to make her up now do I?" Tristan chuckled pathetically as he awaited a response from his former love. His bloodshot eyes gazed into those of Cammy, as a single tear trickled down her fair cheek. She opened her mouth to answer, but was unable. Tristan nodded his head, and sniffled quietly. "Alright, well I hope she makes you very happy. I know you'll be a great mother." Tristan stumbled to the sofa, and retrieved his leather jacket. He rested it under his arm, and began his slow, awkward trek to the door. Finally, a gasp of air was able to escape Cammy's body, and her voice filled the room. "Tristan, please don't leave like this." Her words stopped the large man, as his cold stare penetrated the door. "And how is that Cam?" Disdain was now very evident in Tristan's voice. "Don't you want to know? I .. if she's yours?" Cammy now almost wished she had just let Tristan leave with no other words spoken, but it was too late now. "I don't know ... do I?" A silence fell over the room that could kill, as Cammy's face wrinkled up in self dissapointment. "Maybe not ... but you need to know." Tears now began to flow freely down her face as she struggled not to break down. "Cammy, it doesn't matter what the answer is. Either way, you didn't feel it was important enough to tell me then, so I don't want to hear it now. I'm going to be in Washington for a few weeks. I'd appreciate it if you didn't contact me ... I need focus more now than ever. If I'm lucky, I won't remember any of this in the morning. Good night." --- Unfortunately, it was the first thing Tristan woke up to along with a massive headache. 72 hours, and a plane ride later, his denial was doing him no justice. Without realizing it, he had already made his way through check-in, and was now in a large room filled with various reporters and characters alike. It was the first time he had the opportunity to size up the other individuals in the tournament, and he had to do it alone. He felt like a stranger in his own homeland. His mentor was unable to make the trip due to his medical condition, and a very passive Tristan Allen was left to fend off the media on his own. Due to the language barrier in Japan, Tristan always had at least an interpretor with him, but that was far from the case in our nation's capitol. He could handle interviews by himself just fine here, and it was inevitable that the horrible vultures would approach him very soon. Despite his new distaste for Cammy, he wanted nothing more than to have her by his side. She had a quality about her that always made him beam with confidence ... like nothing in the world could touch him. Confidence was far from what he was feeling as the first columnist approached. "Excuse me Falcon ... my name is Bill Connoly, I'm a reporter for Pro Wrestling Illustarted. I was curious as to if I could borrow a few minutes of your time ... just to ask a few questions about the tournament." Inside Tristan was screaming no, but recalled to when he first arrived in Japan, and Hayabusa told him to never skip an interview. Any press is good exposure, and the money is always better when people know you. "Uhh yeah, no problem."Tristan masks his nervous tick so not to look weak. "Alright, well little is known about you stateside, due in part to the majority of your career taking place in Japan under the guidance of Puroresu Legend, Hayabusa. Are you at all nervous making your re-debut in almost 3 years in such a prestigious tournament?" A slight smile lines Tristan's lips as a small deal of pressure is lifted from his shoulders. "I'd be a bold-faced liar if I said I didn't get nervous before a match. Any true athlete would tell you the same ... its what keeps us grounded. As for the prestige, I've competed in 2 of the past 3 Super J Cup's, which were both tremendous honors, and those for me were a bit unsettling. The respect for pro wrestling is so much greater in Japan, so I did the best I could to not dissapoint the fanbase, as well as some of the great athletes that I've looked up to for so long. As for being in the states, I'm not as affected by the attention. Everyone has off days, and as long as I don't leave anything in the back, I'll be happy with my performance." Connoly glances down at a small notepad as he nods in reaction to Tristan's answer. "Point taken, now let's cut to the chase ... your first round opponent. Luke Chadbourne, one of the newer members of the stacked 4WW roster. What are your thoughts on this man who ironically shares the exact same birthdate as yourself?" "Hehe, yeah I noticed that little fact when I was doing some research on the guy. Well for one, he's a student of the late, great Steve Chadbourne. That in itself is a credential to be admired. Unfortunately I never had the opportunity to meet Steve in person, but I was a fan of his work from watching some old tapes, and I was sad to hear of his passing. I'm not sure if this is going to work in favor of his grandson, or against, so I'm just heading into the first round like its the most important match of my life. I've gotten ahold of some tapes of Luke's work over the past few years, and can honestly say he is a great natural talent. We've had about the same experience in the ring, so it could easily come down to psychology." "So would you say you have a strategy going into this match?" "Oh most definently. I'd be a fool to go into it any other way. Luke still makes some mistakes, which he may or may not have fixed since the last I saw of him. Basically I'm gonna play it safe, and not go too high risk. I've heard a few promos from Chadbourne, and the guy seems to let his arrogance get the best of him sometimes. I'm not sure if its because he's gotten a lot in a short period of time or not ... but its like he's lost respect for the business. Something I really wouldn't expect from the grandson of Steve Chadbourne, but to each his own I suppose. We have mildly similar styles, so at the very least the people are gonna get a good show." "Alright, well I'm sure you have alot of business to attend to, so I'll let you be on your way. Thanks again, and good luck in the tournament." "I appreciate it." Connoly shuts off his recorder, and with a smile, makes his way back into the mass of people. The interview went fairly smooth, and served a great purpose by temporarily making Tristan forget about his personal woes. He had to stay in this mindset, and not let his solitude get to him. Soon enough, the tournament would be over and he could return to having a personal life. Until then, he was strictly business. Well, that is of course except the question of little Kayla. As hard as he tried, he couldn't stop invisioning what his possible daughter might look like. Maybe it was a character flaw, but Tristan wanted her to be his own. The odds against it were great, but as long as he didn't hear the answer from Cammy herself, there was always a chance. He was angry with himself for allowing a security blanket to engulf him, but he couldn't help it. Despite his lack of a companion, the void in his heart was filled with this conception alone. The debate of whether or not Tristan Allen was a father wasn't going to be answered anytime soon ... but the question of Falcon Mega's worth would be answered very shortly. His path had been paved, now it was his decision to follow it or not.
