Lemon Pie VS Ham Sandwiche II

12 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-12 Foolishly I take the line they offer me. The coke goes down like a pillow, and even before I can look up at them and meet the cold gaze of their eyes. I’m high. Johan: Chaplin, I mean this could be a good thing for you. I should tell them to fuck, and walk out the door… Katera: You owe me this Chaplin. But I’m going to stay, and listen to what they to have to say. Were in the penthouse suite of Ricman towers, the guy in the red and black, expensive looking business suit, with the longish hair tied into a short ponytail, that’s the Johan Ricman. Up until ten minutes ago, I used to trust him. The girl with the bright, sexy looking red hair that down to her shoulders. The one beautiful one with the plaid mini skirt, knee high socks and rock and roll T-shirt, is Katera. Once upon a time in a long, long ago I used to love her. There sitting on a leather couch, that matches everything else in this apartment. Minimalist and sleek. Chaplin: I told you last night, I’m retired for the time being. I don’t want to wrestle any more right now. I’m the gruff looking guy in the battered leather jacket. The one with the fading blue hair, once a brilliant midnight green, now a dirty sea foam green. I haven’t shaved in a couple of days, and my eyes are bloodshot from lack of sleep, and various mind alerting drugs. Johan: You know that not true, and besides your already in the tournament. No-Showing would really send your career into a tail spinning. Him and his know it all attitude, perfect teeth, the way he tries to explain in a business sense. I told him, I’m out, no more, I’m going back to Haven and amend all my sins. When I told him this four weeks ago, he smiled and shook his head. Told me, it’s all right. Chaplin: How can it hurt my career if I’m retiring? Katera: Oh get off it Chaplin, how long do you think you’ll actually stay retired? Then she had to come back into the picture. GTT4 she says, her guy, some big guy from up north, Jack Savage had been injured and need somebody to enter into this tournament. The bitch got Johan to enter me into this. Now I had my back up against the wall, stuck in a nasty imposition. I was here because Kat was right. How long could I actually stay gone? A few months maybe a year? No I couldn’t retire for good, it’s in my blood. Still, I felt like I had my dignity, and I wasn’t going to give up that easily, even if my back was up against the wall. Chaplin: Maybe, but still I don’t want to do this. I was hoping to leave the city and in Haven before the morning. Katera: Haven can wait. She took a silver cigarillo case, the one I gave her for birthday, the year she left me, for the first time, and removed a slender slim ciggerette from the case. She lit with a red BIC lighter, inhaled deeply, savoring the smoke, then exhaling a plume of white smoke. Katera: Besides your still under contract with Johan here, and he signed up for this. I’m staring right at the bastard, he can feel my eyes, and he shrinks a bit into the couch. Chaplin (Looking at Johan): Could I get one of those ciggerrette Kat? She shrugs whatever, and takes another slim ciggerette out of the cigarillo case. I take it, and lean into her, letting her use that blue lighter to light my smoke. The cocaine is telling me, I am so fucking cool. Johan: Listen Chaplin, I know your pissed, I know you just want to get away from it all. But this is big Chaplin, this is really big. I need this Chaplin, you need this. I admit defeat, Chaplin: Give me the 411 then. and she smirking a victory smile. -- Katera: Okay so here’s the facts, We’re sitting in Johan’s kitchen on high black chairs, sipping cold bottles of imported beer in a clear bottle, with a name I can’t pronounce. My enemies that have forced me to work for them sit across from me. I feel like a child again, getting scolded by my parents. Katera: You’ve been put into the Appalachians bracket. Your first round opponent, calls himself Darek DeAngleo. He’s a fellow Canadian, from Calgary actually. The bitch talks condensing to me, I sit there listen, and sip my strange foreign beer. It’s a bit to strong for me, and I’m craving another line of coke. Chaplin: Power wrestler from Canada, got that. Now give me some real information. Johan: You have this seriously Chaplin. They say this guy is good. I glare at him, and think about all the bad things I want to do to him. The bastard sold me out. Tell me to take it seriously, I am taking this serious. Chaplin: I am being serious here. I don’t care if he’s a power wrestler, or if likes to wear woman’s underwear. I couldn’t care less if he came from Calgary. Calgary just means he’ll bullshit about legacy, tradition, and the fucking Hart Family with their glorified basement they like to call the dungeon. You want me to take this serious Johan, tell me something I need to know. What are my chances? A predictable silence falls over this room. I just sip my beer, and marvel in my bits of small victory over these two. I had already known this all. Chaplin: What are the odds against me. I declare, and for the longest time they just stay silent until Johan starts to speaks. Johan: Um, well see Chaplin, you’re a relatively unknown in this tournament, and well Darek is… Um… Katera: Everybody’s putting their money on Darek. Chaplin: What about you? Katera: I’m putting my money on you. That’s the one thing, maybe the only thing I still love about this bitch. Her undying, unflinching faith in me. She told me I would be a hero no matter what happened, she said once that in the end, when the dust was settling, all imitators were down, I would be the one left standing. I went the low road, and played games, quoting of all people, Linkin Park. In The End it doesn’t even matter. No wait though I was wrong in the end it does matter. There are winners and there are losers. I knew now in my 22 years of life, that I could no longer stand in the middle ground. I had to prove myself once again. Darek DeAngleo, he was my proving ground. Chaplin: 463, Oh these stupid games we play. Katera: That’s life, death, Quoting lyrics, throwing sections of songs at each other, letting the other finish it off. Chaplin: And I can’t think of a better way to end the day. Buck 65 – 463. Johan just looks at us, not really knowing what to say. It must have been weird for outsiders like him to sit on this. It used to be cute between me and Kat, these games, now it’s ugly, vulgar, trying to outwit the other. I’m scowling at her, and I come with a plan. In the ring with this man who calls himself Darek DeAngleo, I’m going to envision he’s her, and slap him around like the bitch she is. Johan: Listen we don’t for these games you two. We have to be professional about this. Where all getting something out of this. And Chaplin your just going to have to work with us here. Once you get past Darek, and whoever else they throw in your way, I’ll sign over the rest of your contract to you and you can go back to Ariel and Haven, and retire for how ever long you want. Katera: All you got to do Chaplin is win. Chaplin: Cause it’s that simple you guys, all I got to do is win. You ain’t going in that ring, you ain’t wrestling, instead you two are holding me against my will forcing me wrestle in a tournament I didn’t even want to be in the first place. It’s getting late though, and I’ve had enough of you two already, I’ll wrestle in this tournament for you two, and I’ll win. But from now on I’m winning for myself, you two can fuck off and die for all I care. I grab my leather jacket and leave.
