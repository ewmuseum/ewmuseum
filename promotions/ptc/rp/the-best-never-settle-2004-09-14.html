The corporate Headquarters of the Universal Wrestling Federation: the premiere wrestling promotion in the Midwest, and one of the top federations in the nation. Through these hallowed halls have walked some of the greatest wrestlers to ever live. Some still do, too. Right up to the lobby and to the front desk, no doubt the greatest lobby ever in existence… why? Because Doctor Kenneth Mercury R.D. is in DA HOUSE!!] [A middle aged women with a beehive hairdo and thick glass, working as a secretary, looks up at the loud man before her. His long golden hair… (sigh)… like freakin' follicles of Midas … his hard and toned body… oooo…. like heaven… and that face… oh man… his face… it was like… like… totally sweet ass. He leaned his muscular forearm on the front desk and leaned forward.] “DOCTOR… Mercury?” “That is correct.” “R…D?” “RING DOCTOR, ignoramus! I've got a PhD in submissions! I'm surgical in the ring! I dissect my enemies, I am the ring doctor! The shooting star! The technical wizard! The technical spectacle! The VERY DEFINITION… of precision…” … “Yes well… um… Mr. Hayes will see you now…” “That's right he will. A shooting star like myself has bigger things too attend to than meeting with Mr. UWF Owner, Dick Hayes…” “Right, you can go in now.” “I can do anything… I'm a freakin' Demi-God!” [Kenny Mercury, who always wore his wrestling gear even outside of the ring, strolled on down a corridor and entered a door with a little bronze colored plate engraved “Richard Hayes” on it.] “OOOOOOOOOHHHHH DICKY!” [Behind a desk sat UWF owner, Richard Hayes. He was in his late thirties, a young entrepreneur, just out of school when he first created the UWF with his bare hands out of the ashes of the Global I. Wrestling Federation. His hair was auburn, combed and slicked back. He wore a brown suit, with a brown tie and a white undershirt. Kenny's chest was bare. He didn't care. He's the very definition of precision, damn it. When you're in the dictionary, you can do whatever the hell you want. And Kenny did just that.] “Kenny… Good to see you.” “The pleasure is all yours, I'm sure. Dicky, come on… look at me… I have more important things to attend to!” [Kenny flexed his pecs a few times in rapid succession.] “Kenny… look…” [Richard took out some papers from a manilla folder and eyeballed them quickly.] “This is important… this is very important.” “You're finally giving me a World Title push!?” “No…” “SHI-ITE! Shot down by backstage politics yet again… gah…” “Ken, I don't know what you're talking about… And I usually don't, and I usually don't care, ever since you mooned Mr. Powerful and Killer Dude in your first week in the federation years ago as Purple Haze… I learned to ignore you.” “And you listen to KILLER DUDE?!? DICK! The man's best friend is a golf club… He shaved his head and glued pubic hair to it…” “Yes, and you… you're past that… But something I dare not ignore… is your uncanny talent in the ring, your technical prowess.” “Naturally…” [Kenny flipped back his long blonde out of his face. I swear to God that divine glittering sparkles flew through the air off his hair. Honestly.] “And that is why, I have selected you, among others, to represent my promotion, my own creation, the UWF… my body mind and soul…. My child…. The UWF…. In the Primetime Central's Fourth Global Title Tournament.” [Kenny Mercury's eyes widened.] “Did… you… say… T-tournament?! PTC!?” “That's right Kenny…” [Kenny Mercury leapt forward, landing atop Richard Hayes' desk, sending papers and other office items flying to the ground. Mercury squatted down and grabbed Richard Hayes around the collar and yanked the UWF owner to his face, nose to nose. Mercury stared into his eyes…] “THE GLOBAL CHAMPIONSHIP?!?!” “FOR CHRISSAKE, KENNY! LEMME GO!” [Richard slapped Kenny's wrist and Mercury released his grip Hayes.] “GADZOOKS… The Global Title… I remember it all as if it were only yesterday…” [Kenny remembered the PTC Invitational Tournament and it flashed through his egomaniacal mind in a split second… Mercury, unknown at the time to the PTC world, entered the tournament under the UWF banner… first, he slaughtered Se7en… second, he annihilated Josh Starwind…. Then, he unraveled Argyle… afterwards, he nonchalantly made the Lights Out Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion and tournament favorite, Tristan Belmont… YAWN! But then… after shocking the wrestling world and making it to the final four, he dropped the ball and lost to his old nemesis, Seymour Almasy. It was a magically journey… but it was ended short. The rest went down in wrestling history, as Seymour defeated Roulette to become Global Champion. While Almasy would become known as one of, if not THE greatest wrestler in history… Kenny Mercury would be forgotten, retreating from Inter-federation competition and back to his home, The UWF. ] “Gad…. Zooks? Kenny, you've lost it, you really have.” “I KNOW I DID! YOU DON'T HAVE TO REMIND ME! IF I HADN'T I'D BE GLOBAL CHAMP, ASS-MUFFS~!” “Ok… look. Kenny… We've got three busses ready… one for Seymour, one for the rest of our competitors… and one for you.” “That's right, a whole bus to myself, you know you to treat your shooting star!” “Actually, Kenny, the rest of the boys signed a petition… and they didn't want you on their bus…” “WHAT?! POOP-COCK!?!? WHAT THE HELL, WHY!?” “Kenny… Everyone hates you.” “Nincompoops… imbeciles…” “And you have to get going right away… as in immediately… the bus is parked outside right now, the driver will take you to your apartment and from there you'll leave for the tournament.” “Ass-hat… you could have warned me!” “Just go… please… I've got about thirty-seven lawsuits to look over… PETA… NAACP… MAU…” “MAU?” “Mothers Against UWF… oh and there's one from MAKM…” “Who?” “Mothers Against Kenny Mercury.. now get off my desk you Rod damned monkey…” “Shazbot! BUT! THIS TIME THE GLOBAL TITLE IS MINE! I SWEAR IT! Or else I'm not the shooting star… the technical wizard… the ring doctor… THE VERY DEFINTION OF PRECISION!” “Which you are… good luck…” “Luck is a earthly concept… I'M NOT OF THIS PLANE~!” “JUST GO!” [Kenny hopped down from desk and briskly exited the office. There stood a face from the past… a tubby body of an old acquaintance of Kenny's… The old UWF fan site web master and former manager of one Purple Haze and indentured servant of one Kenny Mercury. The old, round ,and smelly know-it-all bastard rocked back and forth on his heels, wearing a bus driver's uniform. Kenny Mercury immediately made eye contact and Mercury's cocky smirk quickly changed to a sneer.] “… Dingus…” “Well Kenneth, it's soooooo nice to see you again.” “Fuck off. Let's just go.” [And they left the UWF towers and drove the UWF tour bus to Kenny's Chicago apartment. No, they didn't get along… ever. At one time the fat fuck known as Dingus (His birth name never revealed) pushed Kenny around, and kept him emotionally and psychologically down and the whim of Dingus' critical and cynical nature. Oh, and its okay to make fun of Dingus' weight problem… why? Because he's a freakin' ass hole. If he were nice, then it wouldn't be an issue and it'd be ignored, but when referring to an asshole the size of Dingus… it's okay, he freakin' deserves it. Kenny didn't own much… he didn't need much to survive… just his health and his body… and his eyes to look at said body… and his mind to constantly dwell on said body. Thus, he didn't really need to pack much… a towel… athletic tape… meh, you know, the usual stuff… But other than that… there's wasn't much. No extra clothes or anything… well, he did pack his Lyle 'In Style' Wile poster, his UWF hero, and his LOW foam World Heavyweight Championship belt, which he awarded himself during the PIT after felling Tristan Belmont.] “Are you one yet, we gotta tight schedule here… we gotta hit the road.” “Blow me… hold your horses… Or did you eat those too…” “Ooooo… clever. Bravo, Ken… bra-fucking-vo.” “I'm going to take a piss really quick… then we'll go… just hang on.” [Kenny Mercury headed to the bathroom. He shut the door behind him… A small FM radio sitting on the sink hummed out a rock 'n roll song. Kenny Mercury began to do his business… the sound of liquids consistently splashing about drowned out the radio. The skillful hands of Kenny Mercury, skillful in the right and with a joint or limb of his opponent, were very able in other tasks as well. FOGHAT, BAY-BAY!” [End song faded out…] “… Shit…” [The voice of the radio disk jockey crackled over the airwaves…] “That was “Slow Ride” and up next we've got some Zeppelin for ya! Here we go!” “Zeppelin!? Oh man!” [Kenny looked down and surveyed the scene… he was just about to finish up. Kenny looked at the radio, then back down at himself, then at the radio, then back down at himself, the radio, back down at himself, the door, the radio, back down at himself, the door, the radio, and then he nodded as Led Zeppelin's Immigrant Song started up, with the fast and hard hitting guitar of Jimmy Page bursting out of the radio.] “THIS SONG RULES! FUCK THAT! I'M TAKING A SHIT!” [Kenny quickly plopped down on the toilet and sang along with the powerful and piercing voice of Robert Plant… in the perfect marriage… two of the best things in this world… fused together… the LZ-BM…] [Outside, Dingus is getting impatient. He eyes the bathroom door, just as a voice avalanches into his ears.] “AWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWAAAAAAAAAAHAAAAAAAAA-AH!” “What the hell is he doing in there!?” “AWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWAAAAAAAAAAHAAAAAAAAA-AH!” “I don't have time for this!” [Dingus stomped out of the apartment and waited for Kenny in the bus… two minutes and twenty five seconds… plus some added time for cleansing... Kenny Mercury entered the bus.] “What the hell were you doing? We NEED to be going! We're behind schedule!” “Dingus, you obese jack-illac… I'm a changed man… I've had an epiphany.” “You've come to terms with your egotistical ways and are going to retire from pro wrestling to become a Franciscan monk? You MUST!” “Um… no.” “Do it! You'll have to do it sooner or later, but Dingus…” [Kenny lays his hands upon Dingus' on the steering wheel and looks him in the eye.] “When you do… do it right. Don't settle for less. I've been down that road… my whole life… and I don't settle… I'm the shooting star… the technical wizard… the ring doctor… the definition of precision…The best for the best. I'm worth it. Live it up… and let it out.” “Do… not… touch me…” “It's your choice… Be the best… You have the potential.” “Shut up! Sit down we're leaving…” “I'm never going back… I've tasted paradise and I want more… I don't settle, Dingus, I don't.” “Don't talk to me either…” [Kenny Mercury shoots his arm out towards the road, straight ahead, pointing forward.] “ONWARD! TO THE GLOBAL TITLE! THE SHOOTING STAR IS AT THE TOP OF HIS GAME AND AIMING RIGHT THROUGH… umm… What's his name?” “Bullseye…” “Yeah, him, he's going down, it's not his fault that I was born… born for this moment… born for this title…THIS IS MY BIRTHRIGHT! THE SHOOTING STAR IS BACK!!!!” “I hope you washed your hands…” “I…. AM… THE… BEST…” [And the bus left for the Global Title Tournament 4.]
