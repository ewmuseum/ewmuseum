Apprehension

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 The sudden rumble of a car engine interrupted Dawn’s morning reverie, her gloved hands pausing over the trench of black soil as driveway gravel crunched beneath the tires of her unannounced visitor. Gently she dug a small hole into the soil, placing the tulip bulb inside before packing it tightly back in. A little bit of water and then she moved on to the next spot in the line, parting the dirt with her hands to lay the next would be flower inside. Slam. Dawn’s head came up and around, looking at the back end of her house as though she could see through the large structure and into the driveway out front. “People only slam doors when they’re excited…” she mused to herself, “…or angry.” Adjusting her black ball cap back into place, she turned back to her task, not wanting to leave any of her new flowers lying out for too long in the murderous noon sun. The comforting weight of her ponytail against the back of her neck kept the skin there protected from that same sun, and the SPF50 slathered on her pale arms and shoulders kept the rest of her safe…at least for the most part. As she tucked the third bulb into its resting place the sound of heavy footfalls came from the side of the house, someone moving quickly around the flower beds that spread out form the McFarlane estate to interrupt her day of rest. Sammy wasn’t due back at the estate for several days yet, and the rest of the girls would be gone for months…if they ever returned at all. She and Corvus has parted ways, if you could look at it that way, nearly a year ago now and Marcus wouldn’t be out in the blazing sun. “That’s an interesting mystery…” She mumbled, indulging her habit of talking to herself, “…I wonder who’s come to bug me today. They have to have a key to get in, which limits the number of people for certain…unless of course I have a brazen burglar.” Scowling to herself, Dawn filled in the soil around the third bulb, not looking up as the footsteps rounded the corner of the house, not far from where she knelt in the dirt. “If you’ve crushed my petunias, I can assure you that you’re going to wish you hadn’t come here today.” The threat was mostly empty, but Dawn had locked the front gates to the estate after she had gotten up this morning, wanting nothing more then to spend a day alone with her flower beds. And she stubbornly refused to allow the intruder to interrupt her plans for the day, shifting over to the right and digging another hole in the soil, plopping down another bulb into the rich bed she had prepared. “Uhm…which ones were the petunias again?” The familiar voice brought Dawn out of stubborn trance, turning her brilliant emerald eyes on the man who stood uncomfortably in the shadow of the estate, trying to hide the dark soil clinging to his expensive dress shoes. Bounding to her feet with a cry of surprise, Dawn covered the distance between them quickly, throwing her arms around the startled intruder, nearly knocking him to the ground with the enthusiasm of her embrace. “DAN!” A rumbling laugh greeted Dawn’s enthusiastic charge as her former agent wrapped his arms around Dawn’s shoulders, returning the squeeze with equal enthusiasm, if a little less strength. “Hey Dawnie…I’m sorry about your flowers, but I had to find you.” Taking a step back Dawn flashed a winning smile, gesturing back over her shoulder to the row of bulbs laid out before the trench of soil she’d been kneeling in only moments earlier. “It’s alright; I’ll just make sure they’re alright before the sun hits them today. What are you doing here? I thought you’d moved to the US for ‘Greener Pastures’?” Chuckling uncomfortably Dan reached up to scratch the back of his head, raking his fingers through short cropped black hair. “Well ah…let’s say I haven’t really had so much luck down in the US right now. Seems there’s a glut of agents like me, snapping up all the new talent as fast as they hit the scene, so business has been rough.” Casting a critical eye over her guest, Dawn took in his summer suit and dress shoes, his expensive Oakley sunglasses and the Porsche keychain that hung from his right hand. The corner of her mouth tweaked up into a half smile and she folded her arms over her black tank top, just under her breasts. “Well if you’ve come to beg me for a loan, I think you should have come in your OTHER car, don’t you?” The mischievous twinkle in her eye took the sting out of her sarcasm, drawing another sheepish chuckle out of Dan. “Well I didn’t say that I was doing badly myself, I said BUSINESS way bad. I’ve always got a little extra to fall back on, and my stocks are doing well again. Besides, you know I’d never come beg you for a loan Dawnie, you’ve got enough on your plate money wise.” Dawn shrugged uncomfortably, turning back towards her previous task. “I do all right for myself Dan, you don’t need to be worrying about me.” She strobe back to where her tulip bulbs lay, kneeling down by the dirt again and preparing the bed for the fifth flower. “So is this just a social visit then? I’d love to spend a day catching up with you, but I’d need to finish these flowers first.” There was a long pause, Dawn grinning to herself as she imagined Dan squirming in his shoes, trying to push out the real reason behind his coming to visit her. “Weeeellll….” He hemmed and hawed, “Yes and no. It’s been too long since I’ve had the pleasure of your company, that much is true…but there’s more to it then that Dawnie.” “Alright, can you tell me about it out here? Or is it top secret?” Dawn grinned over one pale shoulder at him, her hands working on automatic in the dirt. “Can we go inside? I don’t want to ruin my tan; it’s hard to get it this even.” Dan fired back, grinning down at her in return. Dawn burst into laughter, looking from her own alabaster complexion to Dan’s Californian surfer’s tan. “Sure thing, we can get you delicate self inside…after I’ve finished here of course.” “Well the least I can do is give you a hand, it’ll make things go faster right?” Dan removed his jacket and shoes, placing them on the porch, his socks and car keys quickly following suit. Kneeling by the side of the trench opposite to Dawn he set about the same task with expert hands, ignoring the dirt and grass caking on the knees of his dress pants. Shaking her head in amazement Dawn returned to the task at hand, making sure each bulb had been placed in its proper spot. The sun had moved further west by the time they had finished their task, pushing themselves up off the ground to relieve aching knees. Dawn dusted her hands off on her faded Jeans, laughing as Dan followed suit on his now ruined dress pants. “Now we can go inside for you Dan, although I don’t think I can save your pants.” She said with a wink. Sighing dramatically Dan shrugged, “Well there’s nothing to be done about it, some things are just more important.” Chuckling Dawn led her guest into the kitchen, sliding the back door closed behind them and taking her dirt covered sneakers off on the welcome mat just inside the door. “I’m sure you’re thirsty after that. What would you like? There’s beer…although I’m not sure of my girls taste in alcohol.” Easing himself down into a chair by the kitchen table, Dan shook his head. “Nah I need to be driving again, just water would be fine. Unless you’ve got OJ of course, then that would be much better.” Dawn tossed open the fridge, pushing past her students cases of beer and fishing out the jug of orange juice from where it had been pushed against a bottle of thick red liquid…a “gift” from Corvus after they had parted ways. As she poured them each a glass, she looked back at her former agent, once again asking the important question. “So what’s this all about Dan? You’ve told me that coming up to visit was only half of your plan, what’s the other half?” Dan dug into the pocket of his jacket, having rescued it from the porch, and produced an envelope addressed to one “Dawn McFarlane”, with the return address clearly marked with PTC in large letters. The envelope was already opened, it’s contents spilling out slightly as he tossed it onto the table. “This came for you a little over a month ago. I guess they didn’t know where you lived, so they sent it to my old office. The landlady there still collects my mail and sends it to me once it’s built up some.” Setting a glass in front of Dan, Dawn plopped down into the chair opposite his, raising one eyebrow in amusement. “You read my mail?” Dan blushed a little at the rebuke, scratching his head in embarrassment. “Well…yeah, but it’s not what you think. I just got this…feeling about it, especially with the PTC logo on the envelope and the time of year.” “Time of year? What’s that got to do with anything?” Dan favored Dawn with pointed stare, rolling his eyes in exasperation. “You’re really out of the loop aren’t you? PTC’s cruiserweight tournament was last year at this time.” Sipping at her juice, Dawn shrugged non-chalantly. “Of course I’m out of the loop Dan, I’m retired. WMW was it for me, since I left there…” She shrugged again, leaving the statement open ended. “Well hopefully you’re not retired that you’ll be able to refuse an offer like this from a good friend like me.” It was Dan’s turned to wink mischievously, setting off a churning sensation in the pit of Dawn’s stomach as she set her glass down carefully. “What have you done?” “That was PTC’s invitation for you to return for this years Cruiserweight Tournament, CW2.” He gestured towards the envelope. “And since I wasn’t sure I’d be able to reach you in time, I submitted your application for you.” “You WHAT?!” Dawn’s stared at her ex-agent in disbelief, her green eyes wide with shock and anger. “Why would you do that Dan? You of all people should know that after last year…I…” Dawn trailed off, her hand moving to the black velvet choker around her neck, the wide strip of fabric covering from the base of her neck to halfway between that and her chin. Dan took a sip of his own juice, trying to look non-plussed by Dawn’s sudden outburst. “Look…I knew you were going to be upset with me Dawnie, but I think this will be good for you. Leave everything behind for a little while and get into the ring again, work with some of the best PTC has to offer, really work again.” Dawn settled back against her chair, running one finger around the rim of her glass. “I’m retired Dan,” she replied sullenly, “I don’t want to go back to PTC, and I certainly don’t want to go back to that tournament again.” Dan shifted uncomfortably in his chair, “Well then I guess you REALLY won’t like the rest of the news I’ve got for you.” Dawn froze, her green eyes shifting back over to where her friend sat, the churning sensation in her gut increasing with each passing moment. “Dan…what did you do?” Taking a deep breath Dan forced a smile to his face, pulling another envelope out of his jacket and sliding it across the table towards her. “This came early in the week. The accepted your application Dawn…you’re going to CW2.” There was a long pause as Dawn stared at Dan in shock and anger, each emotion warring for dominance on her pretty face. Anger won. Grabbing her glass of orange juice she splashed the sticky contents into Dan’s face, slamming the glass down and stalking out of the kitchen, her blonde hair snapping out behind her from the force of her passage. “Well that went better then it could have,” Dan said to thin air, licking at the OJ dripping from his nose. “I’m still breathing.” He counted to ten and then pushed himself away from the table, taking a moment to dry his face on a towel before following after his enraged host. He found her in the living room, staring out the front window, with her arms folded across her chest, her emerald eyes cloudy with thought. As his bare feet sank into the deep pile carpet she spoke, not turning her eyes from the window. “I’m really upset with you right now Dan, I’m retired…I don’t want to be doing this again.” He sighed, leaning against the door frame and shoving his hands into his pockets. “Give me a chance here Dawnie…at least give me one chance to convince you that this is a good thing, that this would be good for you again.” “There’s nothing you can say,” She replied sharply, turning around to stare angrily at him. “What could you possibly say that would make me want to go through all that again? The back stabbing, the betrayal, the insults, the depression…I left that life to get away from all that Dan, what could you possibly say that would make me want to go back?” “I have faith in you.” Dawn froze in place, her shoulders beginning to shake. “Dan that’s not fair…” She whispered, her voice thick with emotion. “That’s what he said to you though wasn’t it?” Seeing Dawn so upset was nearly enough to make Dan abandon his course, but he’d come too far now, believed too strongly that getting back into the ring again was the best thing for his friend. “James told you that he had faith in you before he passed on…and I’m telling you that I do as well.” Dawn put a hand to her face, trying in vain to control the shaking that plagued her entire body. “Daddy…” she whispered, the last words of James McFarlane ringing strongly in her ears. Walking carefully up behind her Dan put his hands on her shoulders, squeezing them gently to show he was nearby. “I’m sorry Dawn…I don’t want to hurt you, but I really think you’re hurting yourself by insisting you’re retired. You love being in the ring and you love showing off for that crowd.” “It’s not just the wrestling Dan...” She whispered, turning to face him. “It’s everything that goes along with it…I’m not sure I can ever go back to that.” Dan gripped her shoulders tighter, giving the Steel Rose a small shake as he did so. “So don’t go back to that. This is a chance to indulge in the part of the business that you love most Dawn, this is a chance for you to get out there and wrestle your heart out against some of the best men and women PTC has to offer. Forget the rest of it, forget the masks and the lies, forget the intrigue and the angles, just focus on one thing…the wrestling.” “Two things…” She murmured, pulling Dan against her with a fierce hug. “Focus on two things.” “What’s the second thing?” “The winning…” she said, pulling away and flashing a weak smile. Dan whooped for joy, grinning like a school boy at Dawn, “Does this mean?” Dawn held her hands up, forestalling the rest of his comments. “Maybe…I’m not entirely convinced I want to do this Dan…but I’m willing to let you try to convince me. And at the very least I should learn about the kind of people I’m going to be facing right?” “Right!” Still grinning like an idiot Dan took one of her hands, “You won’t be disappointed Dawnie I promise. Let’s go out for dinner alright? Let me buy you dinner and we can talk shop over an early dinner and good coffee. Sound good?” Dawn nodded slowly, forcing out another weak smile. “Alright, I don’t see the harm in being convinced on a full stomach. But let me change first okay? I need to clean up.” Waving her off Dan headed to the first floor washroom to do some cleaning up himself, letting Dawn head upstairs to the bathroom that adjoined onto her master bedroom. Slipping into the bathroom she closed the door firmly behind her, locking it and leaning against it for a long moment, letting out a breath she’d been holding. The moment passed and she walked to the sink, staring at her reflection in the mirror for a long moment, one hand reaching up behind her neck to unclasp the choker. The velvet cloth fluttered to the floor, leaving the angry red abrasions around her neck in plain sight. She ran one hand over the abused skin, her eyes closing. “I don’t know if I can do this again…” **************************************** Wide green eyes, a burning and searing sensation that built up from her chest, the sound of her own blood dripping to the floor, the creak of a door…the sudden burst of light behind her eyes…and…a silver crucifix, shining like a star… **************************************** A sudden pounding on the door brought Dawn around, she fumbled for the choker, snapping it back into place around her neck. “What?” “Are you alright? You’ve been in there for nearly an hour, and I didn’t hear the shower or anything.” “Yeah…yeah I’m fine Dan. Just let me grab a quick shower and I’ll be out okay?” Dawn waited for the sounds of his footsteps to retreat before letting out another long breath. “I guess there’s only one real way to find out…”
