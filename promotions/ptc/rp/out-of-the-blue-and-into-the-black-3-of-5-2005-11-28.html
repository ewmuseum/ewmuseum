Out of the blue, and into the black (3 of 5)

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-28 I don't get myself whored out to the media. I don't get a fanfare. I don't get web-hits. I don't get eager reporters trying to find out who I am, or where I came from. All I get is what barely amounts to a column inch, tucked away safely where nobody would see in the local paper. You've probably just forgotten what I said. I'm not some new, hot young thing. I'm not the potential future of this industry. I'm not worldly. I'm not cultured. I've never crossed an ocean. I've barely gotten a booking outside this state in the last 5 years. Odds are you've already found something to distract you. Nobody wants to face me. Nobody wants to avoid me. I don't even get that much. Man, woman, or animal… vegetable or mineral. I'm now just a distant buzz. A hum. Some nagging voice you can't even be bothered to turn off. I'm nobody's idol, and in return, I have none myself. Opponents are as much a stranger to me as I am to them. Sure, I know their names, hard not to when they’re shoved down your throat every day… but I’m so far outside that circle I couldn't tell half of them apart. If this was on tape, you'd be hammering on fast-forward as if it were the quick-fix to salvation itself. There's no protégé, and I’m no mentor. It begins with me, it ends with me… and end it will. Oh, did I just get the slightest bit of your attention? I can almost hear the mute buttons getting hammered. Obviously not. Sorry there's no backing music or video montage. But, let's face it, anything extra and I might as well just not be here at all. You've probably forgotten this is on, haven't you? Down in the bottom of the barrel, my soles scraping the bottom, I'm forced to look up at all above me. I wallow below grandeur, fester beneath fame. It's alright, I'd be just the same. They only sent me back forms to fill in for the first round, to claim one lot of bank and travel. I didn't even get a photocopied sheet with the dates and venues of later rounds. Then again, why buy Stevie Wonder a magic eye puzzle for Christmas. But, please, at least humour me. I didn't realise I was this far down the food chain, even at my pessimistic… realistic best. At least leave this playing until someone you care about replaces me. I really will be coming from nowhere. Truly out of the blue. You can pretend you heard what I said. Hopefully, it will all add to the mystique. I'll become folklore. Down the timeline, people will doubt I was ever real… which might just be an improvement on the anonymity I currently possess. It'll be a lonnnnnnnnnnng way up… Absolutely nothing. Into notoriety. Into the black.
