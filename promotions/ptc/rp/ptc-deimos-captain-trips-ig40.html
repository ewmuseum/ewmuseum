Deimos - Captain Trips Deimos - Captain Trips (2443 words) The night sky is as black as death and the stars above dot the infinite blackness like the torch lights of weary travelers trying to find their way through darkness. In the dreary old city below, the empty streets are filled with a melancholy silence that hides the treacherous dealings that going in the night all the time in this city. It is midnight in the old city. The city has been around for hundreds of years and will most likely be around for hundreds of years more despite the rapid rate of decay it is currently experiencing. There is a man wearing some sort of Satanic Native American painted facial decoration, a black Cannibal Corpse T-shirt, a black leather jacket, jeans, and Black Vans shoes. He has long, mangy black hair and has a small ring in his nose. This man looks very grungy and his face looks sinister. He walks past some stray dogs who are eating some garbage and has no fear that they will attack him as some of the dogs in this area are known to do. He does not care about dogs attacking him. His goal is to merely make it to his final destination. In the late night hours in this city there are various people are asleep whereas various people are not. Some of them are doing things, others are not. But some of these people who are awake and doing things are part of the city’s large criminal underbelly that has been giving the police headaches for months on end. One of these people is this man, a man named Deimos. This is a man who feels no human emotion and lacks compassion. His only mission in life is to create chaos and death. According to this man, all life lives only to die. This is his soul purpose in life. Deimos is now walking through an empty alleyway filled with vermin and infested even more so by homeless vagabonds and meth dealers. Deimos cannot stand these types so he does whatever he can to avoid even the slightest social contact with the infected infestation population. He has loathed the population of this area ever since he started meeting with contacts in this area of the city. The slums is the most dangerous area of the city. The crime rate in this area has jumped more than two hundred percent within the past year. Deimos is the reason for that. He has created a hidden underground network to transport supplies, drugs, and many other things all across the city and the surrounding areas. Right now he is on the way to meet with one of his many contacts. He wanders through the alleyway, pushing aside a homeless beggar who is about to ask him for some money. The befuddled homeless man looks a bit shocked at the blatant disregard for his situation that Deimos just showed him. Rats scurry about like they own the whole place and cockroaches are so abundant that they almost appear to be an invading army with their sheer mass of numbers. The smell of death permeates the air from all the unfound bodies hidden throughout the slums as Deimos crosses the empty street as he continues to head in the direction of his contact. For months now Deimos has been meeting with contacts and making secret deals with various people in hopes of getting together some information to get chemical weapons to, in his words, “purge” humanity of its life and send them home to his dark master known only as Death. His life’s goal has been to do this ever since he was fascinated as child watching the chaos that ensued while watching the movie “Stephen King’s The Stand” on the television. He was enthralled by what happened when the Super Flu virus was unleashed upon the masses. In fact, his nickname in high school was Captain Trips, which was the Super Flu’s nickname on the West Coast of North America. Then, years later, another movie called “28 Days Later” came out and it had a virus that turned people into mindless, savage, zombie-like freaks that attacked uninfected people. This concept is was proved to be spark the influenced his life’s work. Deimos is not the typical psychopath that you would expect to be plotting such schemes. He went to college to learn everything there is to know about chemistry, viruses, and anything else related to the subject matter so that he would know what he was doing. He didn’t want to screw everything up do the lack of knowledge like a certain former Texas Governor did when it came to an overly funded war. Everything had to be right for this to happen. Deimos has known this since day one. Over the past few months, he has been trying to get a hold of chemical and biological weaponry without any luck. He just missed a shipment about three weeks beforehand that would have given him everything that he ever wanted to complete this plan that he has orchestrated. Of course, things don’t always go according to plan. The goons he sent to complete the task screwed up and ended up dead. Luckily for him none of them had any information that would lead the investigators to him in regards to what happened. Deimos went and killed his last informant after that debacle. Now he is going to meet his new informant in an old factory near the waterfront. The waterfront area is the best area for these kinds of meetings. He has been doing this for a long time and knows what areas the cops patrol and what areas they don’t patrol. This is one of the areas that they don’t patrol so it’s an ideal spot for Deimos to do a lot of things and have no one notice. Deimos continues walking past some run down and boarded up old stores that haven’t been open in more than twenty years. There’s graffiti all over then and most of the barred up windows are broken. He then crosses another intersection and is now walking past the old cemetery that has been there since the city was first built. He smiles as he remembers that there are the bodies of two people who has killed in the past still rotting in unmarked graves in that very same cemetery. The memories that bring the smile quickly fade as he goes back to the task at hand with the same sullen look on his face that he always has. He continues walking until he reaches the slums’ industrial zone. The first building in this area that he comes across is the abandoned factory where he is meeting his new informant. ABC Industries is the name of the place. It closed down back in 1957 after an industrial accident killed fifteen workers and injured more than thirty others. The factory never reopened but the appeal of being in a place where so many people died was too much for Deimos to resist. He chose this place specifically due to this fact. He feels more alive around death. It invigorates him like caffeine and makes him excited. He walks up to the entrance of the decayed old factory and goes inside. Some rats scurry about and crickets can be heard chirping somewhere. Spider webs adorn all of the rusty and old equipment. The building smells almost as if there is a sewer leak nearby. It is a rancid smell that permeates the area and would make most people gag but not Deimos. He is at home in the filth and grime. He walks for a short period until he sees the figure of a man standing in the darkness. “You’re late,” the man growls as Deimos stops and looks him over. “You’re going to die,” Deimos replies to the man in a very callous manner. The man seems a bit taken aback by this comment. “You’re fucking crazy,” he replies, taking a step back. Deimos motions for him to stop. “I may or may not be crazy,” he says in a firm tone. “But we’re not here to debate my sanity or to talk about the infinite gauntlet that is life that eventually leads to the ultimate happiness that is death. What we are here to talk about is that information you have for me about the shipment that will be passing through this area in the coming days. That is what we are for, correct?” The man reluctantly nods his head. “Yes,” he says with a quiet sigh which Deimos picks up on and is annoyed by. “Good,” he growls back to the man. “Now tell me what you know before I kill you. And before you reply with some sort of a retort questioning my sanity, let me inform you of something about me. I kill without hesitation.” Deimos pulls out a knife from his pocket and starts staring at it. “I kill friends,” he continues, looking right into the man’s eyes as the man gulps and takes another step backward away from him. “I kill enemies, and I kill people who I don’t even know. Why? Because I could care less. We all are going die sometime. The better sooner than later considering that the afterlife is miserable and nonexistent. Oh, and my last informant turned out to be a cop. I slit his throat with this very same knife. In fact, I‘ve killed a lot of people in a lot of ways, so don‘t fuck with me. Otherwise I send your ass on a quick trip to Hell where you can tell Satan hello for me. Now think carefully before you speak.” The man gulps and thinks about what to say in response to this. “Okay,” he says hesitantly as he wipes some sweat off of his brow. “This is what I know. There is a transport ship called the Triton. It will be arriving here at 10 pm on Wednesday night. And it will be carrying the cargo that you have been searching for as well. Lots of this particular cargo.” This brings a sick smile to Deimos’ face. “Good,” he replies to the man. “Now I don’t have to kill you. Tell me some more about this ship before I change my mind about it. What I need is information. I need to know everything there is about that ship. When it is coming in, who the captain is, how many people will be onboard the ship, and so on and so forth.” The man smiles and pulls out a large manila envelope with papers inside of it. He then hands the envelope to Deimos. “Everything you need to know is in there,” he says as Deimos takes the envelope from his hand. “All the ship’s information is in there. I found everything.” Deimos opens the envelope and looks inside. After pulling out the papers and skimming through them, he seems impressed. “Nice work,” he says with a hint of happiness. “Now I don’t have to kill you…” Deimos pulls out his knife again and stabs the man right in the chest! “But I’m going to do it anyway,” he says with a chuckle. He pulls the knife back out and stares at it like the sick perverted killer he is. He watches the man’s face turn to a look of sheer fright as he sets the envelope down on an equipment table nearby and then watches with joy as the man’s life extinguishes right before his eyes. The man holds his gaping wound and falls backwards to the ground. Blood is spilling out of his chest like an oil company transport ship that had just sprung a leak. The blood flows down the side of the man like a crimson waterfall much to the sick pleasure of Deimos who just stares at the man’s dead body. “Nice doing business with you,” he says with a smile as he wipes off his knife with a rag who just pulled out of his pocket. Once he’s done cleaning the rag, he grabs the man’s arms and drags the body behind some mechanical equipment so no one can see it. He follows this by walking across the room to an old, rusty furnace and turns it on. He then goes back over to the body and drags it over. Deimos sets the body down in front of the furnace and wipes the sweat from his forehead to take a short break before finishing the job. Deimos looks down at the body and says, “Sorry old chap but I have no other option for you other than cremation.” He then picks up the body and throws it inside. After watching it burn for a few minutes, he turns the furnace off and walks back across the room. After walking back to where the murder took place, Deimos wipes his hands off on the rag and then puts it back in his pocket. He then goes over to the table and picks up the envelope he set down earlier. He opens up the envelope and pulls out the papers again. “The man was right,” Deimos says as he flips through the papers. “He did find everything I needed. Too bad he was an expendable asset.” He puts the papers back into the envelope and closes it up. With the envelope in hand, he heads out of the factory. As he exits through the front door, he closes it up so no one who walks by will get suspicious. He walks across the intersection and starts heading for the entrance to the dingy, grimy, decaying catacombs that are underneath the entire city. Once he makes it there, Deimos knows he is home free. No one is insane enough to go into the catacombs even during the day time hours. Deimos walks two more blocks surrounded by nothing more than silence. The only sounds he hears are his own footsteps tapping against the pavement. He sees the tunnel up ahead and picks up his pace. He walks up to the entrance to the catacombs and goes inside without any hesitation. Nocturnal creatures like him are not afraid of what lurks in the darkness. Fate has brought him to this point. Now he must bring about the darkness at the end of everything. The end time is coming and he is sure to be the harbinger of death that causes humanity’s bleak existence to expire. For he is the Lord of the Dead and he plans on sending more souls to the underworld before his life on this Earth comes to an end.
