The Road Less Travelled: Introduction to GCW

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-14 A soldier in a tank top and jungle green camouflage runs through what looks to be a munitions bunker. He fires off rounds from his M249 machine gun into other soldiers, trying to prevent him from exiting the bunker. A final soldier comes from no where and gets ready to throw a knife at the soldier before he’s cut down with five bullets to the chest, falling back. "CUT!" All of the fallen soldiers get back to their feet and brush themselves off. They all take off their helmets and goggles. The one with the knife re-sheaths it as he pulls his gear off. His hair was pulled back in a ponytail, which he releases, allowing it to fall to his shoulders. "Guys, that was good. I think that we have what we need. Dorian, you have a message." "What is it?" "Don’t know, I think it's something that Andrea got set up for you." Dorian takes his jacket off to show the squibs that were placed underneath that where meant to blow at the time. He pulls the board off and hands it to the pyrotechnic advisor on the set as he’s given a slip of paper from the director’s assistant. The next day, Dorian was finding himself going over costume designs for the X character that he would portray at the beginning of his time in Global Championship Wrestling. "You’re needed in the studio in two weeks. So we need to figure out what costume you’re going to go with. I like the full body that leaves the arms exposed. It gives less chance of people not realizing that the person in the promos isn’t the same person that’s going to be in the ring week in and week out." "I like the white and black two-tone." "You mean the one without the top?" "Yes, the one without the top. It’s not going to be that hard to find people that are the same height and the same build as me." "But…" "GCW isn’t hiring me to go into the ring yet, but they are giving me limited creative control of the X character. That includes the look of the character." "Fine, I’ll let the wardrobe people start to work on the costume." "By the way, can you get a covering over the eye?" The next day, Dorian is watching a group of wrestlers doing flips, dives and bumps in the ring. These are who GCW had gotten together to become the wrestlers that are going to wrestle as X. All are between 6’ and 6’2 and between 215 and 225. They also all have long hair. "What the hell is this, a cattle call for wrestlers?" One of the GCW road agents brings in the actors that are going to portray X’s handler and attorneys. They are accompanied by Andrea. "Dorian, I want you to meet Robert Larabee, Clint Darcy and Joe White. They are going to portray your attorneys and your mouthpiece." "What happened with my talking for the character?" "Well, the test interview we did with you under the mask, well it just didn’t have what we wanted in it. It lacked….something. Robert will be debuted in your next spot as the Storyteller, and will eventually be revealed to be your main handler. Andrea, is going to portray a junior partner at their firm, using her maiden name." "This was not a part of the deal." "Well, we’re going to push up the time table for your reveal as well. It’s not going to be the six months like we wanted. The fans are just not that receptive based on the numbers for your spot on the X:TV On-Demand service. We are going to create more drama for the character. Trust me, when it happens, it’s going to be big." "Fine, let’s talk about the new changes to the storyline then." "Well, it’s going to be based upon what we are doing for you. The X character is going to be a massive marketing campaign to prove that it doesn’t matter who’s behind the mask, but that the mask is what’s successful. Eventually, you’re going to get sick of it, attack one of these wrestlers after a match, and take your spot in the line. Either as X or as another name, we haven’t figured that part out yet." "Sounds like GCW have really thought this through." "Well, eventually, it’s going to get you pushed through to the top. If played right, you should be in the Main Event within a year." "Assuming that it all goes according to plan?" "Of course, you realize that you’re going to have to perform better than you ever have. The men that set this up are hoping that you aren’t much of a disappointment. They watched what you did in the National Wrestling Council. The story on you is that you have limitless potential, but never realizes it." A few months later, one of the wrestlers hired to be the X character was in the ring. Dorian Ryan is walking to the Gorilla position backstage with a mask in hand. Andrea is there with him. "Honey, are you ready?" "Yeah, I’m ready to do this. Tonight, the X storyline comes to an end." One of the road agents comes up to Dorian and pats him on the back. "Hey, Dorian, remember that when you go out there, you’re entering GCW as Sam Wolack." "Yeah, I know." "It’s time." "Wish me luck?" Andrea gives Dorian a kiss before he puts his mask on. He then runs down the entrance ramp and slides into the ring where he starts to attack another wrestler that’s portraying X. Later that night, Dorian and Andrea enter a bar that wasn’t far from the arena. The crowd of regulars in the bar notices him and tries to avoid him. He did his job at the show. No one wants to mess with him. He and Andrea sit at a booth and a waitress comes up to them. "Two of whatever you have on tap." "OK." "So, Dorian Ryan, you just made your in-ring debut with Global Championship Wrestling. What are you going to do next?" "Well, Andrea, first, I’m going to have a beer at a local bar. Then I’m going to go to bed with my lovely wife. Tomorrow, I’m going to go get a haircut, and follow that up with spending the day with the most important person in my life." "So, what do you have planned for that day?" "I’m going to go riding with Jay." "Hey!" "I’m kidding, I was thinking of doing whatever you want to do tomorrow." "You promise?" "I promise." The waitress brings over two pilsner glasses filled with whatever beer was on tap, Ryan places a twenty on the tray. "Keep the change." Andrea takes a sip from the glass. "Andrea, I think that it’s time for a toast." "What are we toasting?" "I’m going to make a toast to the start of a great thing in our lives."
