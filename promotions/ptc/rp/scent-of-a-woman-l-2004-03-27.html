Scent Of A Woman: L?

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-27 “Just stop wiggling…” Her words are very calm and suave as she looks at Coricidon. Her long, pink fingernail slowly scrapes along Coricidon’s bare midsection as both of the women are in nothing but their bra and panties, Coricidon’s matching black and the mysterious woman’s mismatched pink bra and red panties. Coricidon takes in a deep breath as the other woman’s finger continues down right between Coricidon’s ribs, slowly but steadily heading lower. “Wait”, Coricidon says, her arms stretching to their full extent and pushing herself higher up the pillow. “I’m not sure.” The fingernail stops above Coricidon’s belly button and begins circling around it. She lets out an audible sigh to show Coricidon her grief, as her eyes stay fixated on the belly button. It is obvious that she wants to go further yet Coricidon is having doubts, the way every woman is during their first. “I know you aren’t sure”, the woman says, still circling Coricidon’s belly button. “My first time I wasn’t sure but once I felt her, that was all I needed.” Coricidon replies with a sigh of uneasiness. A tingly feeling going through her body each lap the other woman’s fingernail takes around her belly button. It is obvious that this experience is going to be thrilling yet the repercussions of this could be anything from a one-night stand to love, something that Coricidon has never wanted to experience. Another sigh and, without saying anything, Coricidon nods her head as a smile finds it’s way to this mistresses’ face as the finger stops taking laps and heads further down, now right at the tip of Coricidon’s panty-line. Another deep sigh from Coricidon, as her head lies higher on the white pillow, her eyes going shut as the first experience always makes or breaks everything. “Just relax, everything is going to be fine”, she says as her finger digs into the panties, a weird feeling for Coricidon. “I love you, that’s all that matters.” And that is where the line is drawn. Coricidon’s hands raise and grab the other woman’s hand, pulling them out of Coricidon’s nether regions and is thrown back into the blonde bombshell. Confusion races through this other woman’s mind as Coricidon backs up and sits on the pillow, her legs arching up and her arms wrapped around them. “That is what you don’t say”, Coricidon says, not even trying to make eye contact with the woman as her eyes trail down to the bed. “You know my past, you know that I am incapable of being loved, I am a monster and that’s all I will ever be.” Coricidon sheds a tear that follows the contour of her sweet face as her friend puts a hand on Coricidon’s left foot, only for it to be kicked away by Coricidon as both her feet then recede backwards towards her. She is completely distraught and nothing can fix it, the L-word has become involved and that has become a big issue. “I’m sorry Cori, I didn’t mean to say that”, she says as her eyes follow the same trail towards the mattress. She is becoming distraught as well while she moves from her sitting position to now being on her knees, sitting on her feet. “But you know how I feel about you. Ever since I first laid my eyes on you, I have felt these feelings for you.” There is a bestilled silence in the room that has caused uneasiness between the two. Both of them have the same thoughts but very different perspectives of the situation. Coricidon knows that her friend is in love with her but ever since the night that Coricidon’s father molested her, love became perceived as something untrustworthy and wrong. Her friend, however, knows what love is actually about and wants to share the feeling with Coricidon, share all of this with her and let them live happy… Forever. Coricidon looks up, still distraught. Her friend stares at her, biting on her lip as she waits to see what the response from Coricidon will be. Both of their eyes stay locked on each other as they try to read their thoughts, something that people this close should be able to do however it is as if they have locked their vaults. “I have to go”, Coricidon says as she rolls off of the bed onto her feet and moves towards the dresser where her clothes sit. The other woman sits in awe as, although everything is happening at real time, it seems to be moving too fast for words to be spoken as her mouth sits open in awe. Grabbing her clothes, Coricidon drops the black stretch pants and puts them on, right leg first and left leg second. She holds her shirt in her left hand, turning back to look at her friend. She is still too surprised to say anything but her guard is down, Coricidon can see that her heart is broken as she shakes her head, feeling more disgust for herself. “I’ve got a match to prepare for… Primetime Central has booked me in a Cruiserweight Tournament and I am facing Raijin and James Edwards, I have to train.” She quickly turns and puts her shirt on as she continues towards the door. She gets fully adorned in the shirt before reaching and grabbing the doorknob with her left hand and pulling it open. She bends down and reaches outside to grab her white tennis shoes as she grabs them by their backs and stands back up to her full stature as she exits the door but pokes back in for one last word. “Sky… I’ll come back before I leave”, Coricidon says to her friend, now identified as Sky. Coricidon bites her top lip as her and Sky share one more look at each other before Sky’s head turns back towards the bed, sending her eyes away from Coricidon and now to the pillow in front of her as the emotion in the room is about to explode. Coricidon continues staring at her as she nods her head, not saying anything, and exits as she closes the door behind her. Standing outside of the door, she leans against the brick wall and stares into the blue sky, her eyes now shedding tear after tear as she continues to think of what she has just left inside. She shakes her head, as it is full of liquids surrounding her cheeks as she turns to her left and begins walking. Her black Cadillac Escalade sits idle while she stares at it from the front, unable to move as thoughts of Sky continues to move through her mind. She cannot believe what she has just done, the one girl that she cares for more than anything has just been hurt by selfishness. “I cannot believe I just did that”, she says as she turns back and looks at the door, hoping that the door will burst open and Sky will run after her although it doesn’t happen which saddens her. She turns and walks towards the Escalade, opening her door and crawling in as she now sits in the driver’s side seat. Coricidon finds her keys in the front pocket of her pants and puts them into the ignition as the car starts right up and sits idle, warming up. “James Edwards and Raijin, I feel sorry for you two”, she says as her anger begins to take over. “Messing with a woman scorned is a serious matter, you better hope you can handle it.” The car is put into reverse as it backs out of the motel. It stops in the middle of the parking lot and getting thrown into drive as she sits and stares at the door to see if Sky will come out but when nobody does, she turns and drives away. The door to the hotel slowly opens as Sky peeks out of it, tears streaming down her beautiful face. She notices that the car is gone and that Coricidon is not around. Her tears continue to streak down her face. “I still love you…”
