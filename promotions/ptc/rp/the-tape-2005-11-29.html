The Tape…

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 "Rudolph. There’s a problem with this tape." Christian Montiero is standing in front of his television set, clad in his Calvin Klein underwear and holding the telephone receiver to his ear. His eyes seem to be glued to the television set. "What’s the problem?" Polenski asks, seemingly confused as to how his ‘source’ of information is problematic. "Well…" Montiero smiles as he reaches for the remote and un-mutes the tape. "Diana, I’m coming in your temple!" A gruff male voice shouts. A hint of vigour appears to be present in his voice, which immediately alarms Polenski. Montiero’s smile grows broader as Polenski remains speechless. "I was told that the tape was to do with…" Polenski is interrupted by a series of loud pants and screams, coming from the television set. "Please mute that." Montiero appears to be engrossed in the television. "Christian?" "Yup?" Montiero responds like a zombie, his eyes now travelling with the movement of god knows what. "Christian, mute the television," Polenski says with a hint of urgency. "Oh, okay." Montiero ejects the tape almost immediately in an attempt to give Polenski more of his concentration. "What were you saying?" "Nevermind." Polenski sighs. "I really don’t know why they sent that tape over though." "Wait a second…" Montiero immediately places the tape back into the VCR. "Christian, please don’t…" Montiero skips a few frames and then pauses. Behind the naked couple, a somewhat familiar blonde figure can be seen wearing only a red Nazi armband. "I think Arson’s on this tape…" Montiero says, his eyes gazing forward. His shocked facial expression indicates something has upset him. "Really?" Polenski laughs on the other end as Montiero lowers the receiver slightly. "Is something wrong?" Polenski asks with concern. "You’re not laughing." "Something’s very wrong," Montiero replies at a slow pace. "What? What is it?" "His penis…" Montiero pauses. "It’s huge…" Montiero immediately fast-forwards the tape to the credits. Sure enough, past the ludicrous names of the main actors, the name "Bryan Arson" appears. As Montiero prepares to shed a tear however, he notices the caption above his name. VISUAL EDITING "Don’t worry." Montiero sighs with relief. "It says he did the editing. Judging by what I’ve already seen of him, he doesn’t seem like the type of person who would be able to sit at the computer. Even if it did involve editing pornography." "Yeah, I didn’t think you were right." Polenski says in consolement. "Anyways, we’ll be staying with my mother in Tampa Bay until we find a new place." Montiero un-pauses the tape as he turns his back to the television. As the pair continue their conversation, the caption "BIG DICKOFF THE NAZI" rolls on the screen. Below it is the name James von Doom…
