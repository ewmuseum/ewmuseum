Sin No More, Part I: The Doctor is In

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-10-20 We've all known for years what kind of person he really was, and most of us thought it was all an act. It seemed too fake to be real, bad acting, or call it what you will, but the truth of the matter is, something inside of that young man's mind just didn't seem to click with the rest of the world. Steven Markus Lee was born July 17, 1980; and although most of his past remains a secret, like anyone cares to know about it anyway, one thing we do know about this man is he never stood a chance. Sure his parents were far from being great, and the drugs they had ingested in their time didn't help them either. They also had a bad habit of running with the wrong people, and that's what got them killed. It was surprising enough for most that they left little Steven alone, but I think some of us know better. That fateful day in his life would have him leaving a trail behind him for the rest of his life. Not blood, not a will to live, but his mental capacity would always be diminishing, and it would never seem to stop… Even after it had all but left him. Present Day The tall and slender form of a middle aged man stood outside the confines of a cell. This was no prison cell, but one of few cells inside of this institute that hosted those who did not belong with the rest of the population. The man, fully dressed in scrubs and doctors coat, stood outside of the door looking through the small piece of Plexiglas that gave visual inside and out, depending on what point of view you were looking from. Smiling from ear to ear, it almost seemed as if this middle aged man was adoring what he saw on the inside of this cell, and as sick as it seems, it was almost as if a parent was looking through the glass at a newborn child. 'Patient Six Sixty-Six, you have no idea what kind of joy you have brought me since the day we brought you back.' The doctor spoke as he placed his right hand upon the door. If you were to inspect the man's coat a little closer, you would see a nice shiny silver name tag hung so carefully from the right side chest of the coat; and it read "Dr. Damian B. Sorrow, MD." Low pitched grunts and growls came from the inside of the cell, but were muffled through the thick walls and heavy steel door. 'Oh Patient Six Sixty-Six, I know it must be driving you insane, them keeping you cooped up in there like that. But don't you worry; old Doc here will take care of that.' Doctor Sorrow explained to the man on the other side of the door. 'Dr. Sorrow?' A female in the distance called out. 'The warden and the rest of the doctors are ready for you.' She said. Nodding his head and patting the door with his right hand that had still been resting there, the doctor look down the hall. 'Thank you.' He said to the female as the footsteps of the unknown female are heard training back off. Dr. Sorrow then looked back through the Plexiglas of the door. 'I'll get you out of there, not for good, but I'll get you out of this stupid cell. I promise Steven, I promise.' He explained to the now obvious identity of the man behind the thick walls and heavy steel door. However, with the mention of his real name, it seemed to have set the man off. Not only was his name being said followed by shouts and growls, but through the tiny Plexiglas window, you could see the man throwing himself up against the walls, and attacking himself. The doctor began to walk off as the commotion on the inside of the cell continued. 'You're a special one Patient Six Sixty-Six. You're a special one.' -------------------------------------- 'You mean to tell me, after this lunatic almost cost this institution the life of six of it's patients, you are wanting me to agree to continue giving him his exercise time with the rest of the population?!' A middle aged woman called out from the head of a conference table. At the other end of this table was Dr. Damian Sorrow, standing calm and with a smile on his face. Sitting on either side of the table were two other doctors, and what looked like two rather large assistants to the institution. 'Now I may not look like the kind of person who would have a problem with telling some off in a situation like this, and it makes it harder with you being our head doctor here, but this is ludicrous Damian. The answer is no!' She told him. While the doctors and assistants sat there, almost to themselves, and with no say, their faces remained straight. Dr. Sorrow's face, however, was still full of happiness, even after being told no. He shook his head, and began to make his way around the side of the table to his left. 'Lora.' He coughed. 'Excuse me, Dr. Siegle. What you aren't fully realizing here is this. Sure, Patient Six Sixty-Six looks and acts like a dangerous person, but maybe it's just you, us, who don't understand what kind of treatment this man needs.' He begins to explain. Shaking her head, the Doctor, obviously Southwind Garden's Warden continues to listen. 'Go on.' She says Still smiling, Damian is happy to continue. 'Now I assigned myself to SSL as soon as we brought him back, just for the fact that I was very interested in this young man. He is nothing like anyone else we have here, and for that, we should all be grateful. To my knowledge, this is the only patient we have that actually lived a life outside of these walls for a long period of time after the event that caused his corruption. Not only that, but he became talented enough in his trade to launch himself into a business that is greatly growing in the world today. Hell, you walk the streets of his home state, California, and ask anyone who 'Sinister' Steven Lee is, and I bet you at least fifty percent of those people know him.' He stated. 'I'm failing to see your point here, Dr. Sorrow.' Dr. Siegle mentioned. Nodding his head, he pressed on. 'Just hear me out here.' He said to her, and her tolerance level must have been kicked up a few notches as she calmed down slightly and nodded her head. 'If you look at it like this, the last thing Patient Six Sixty-Six did before losing complete control of himself was this business of sports entertainment wrestling. His mind now tells him that is what he likes to do. That is the only happiness he has now. You put him in exercise time with the patients here, sure, he'll get a little rowdy, but only in an attempt to get back to reality with himself, the only thing that is reality for him anymore.' He finished. The other two doctors nodded their heads, as well as the two muscle bound assistants. Even Dr. Siegle managed to nod her head, as if to know where Damian was going with this. 'OK, so now you are making this a little more interesting. But what can we do here? We don't have any place big enough, or the resources, to give this man what he needs to be a better patient for us.' She spoke towards Dr. Sorrow. 'That's the thing here, Doctor. We won't need to supply anything or find the resources to give Patient Six Sixty-Six what he needs. Instead, we take him to the resources.' He states. Coming out of her seat, Lora Sielge lashes out. 'You can't honestly think we are taking him outside of these walls do you?!' She asks loudly. Damian's smile grows. 'That's exactly what I'm saying. We have everything we need to keep this man in line. Not only does this patient trust me, but we also have Franklin and Robert here to keep him in line to and from.' He says as he places his hands on the shoulders of the two muscle bound, identical looking, men sitting side by side at the table. 'We can escort SSL to and from any venue with ease, and I don't foresee any event getting out of hand.' He says as he takes his hands away from the twins' shoulders, and walks back towards the Warden. Silence fills the meeting room for some time before Dr. Siegle shakes her head, and looks up at Damian. 'OK, your request is granted. But if anything goes wrong, it's your ass Dr. Sorrow, and I'm dead serious. If you kind find the means to get this done, or a place who is willing to take this on, then I guess I have no problem giving you what resources you need to make this patient happier. It would be against the policy I've given this place to keep him cooped up in that cell, after all.' She says as Damian instantly gets excited and grabs her hand and shakes it. 'You have no idea how happy this is going to make this patient. He will definitely be thankful for this.' Dr. Sorrow says to everyone sitting at the table as the other doctors applaud the situation. One of the assistants calls out in the middle of the celebration, however, interrupting Damian's happy moment. 'I want to know something, though, if I have to continue to deal with this guy. Why don't you just call him by his name? Is that Patient Six Sixty-Six, or SSL, bullshit needed?' He asks. Nodding his head, Damian smiles at the man. 'He hates his real name; he hates the name he wrestled by for so long. If you doubt me, I'll throw you in his cell and let you call him by his first name.' Damian said. The young, muscle bound assistant sits back down quickly. Everyone in the meeting room stands from their seats now, and begins to exit. Lora, however, calls for Dr. Sorrow to stay. 'Where exactly do you think you will find a place that will accept this man into their establishment. Let alone a place that will accept a man as vicious as this one has become?' She asks. The biggest smile comes to Damian's face, one bigger than we had ever seen, at least at this point. 'I've been looking into Lora, and trust me, I know of the perfect place.' -------------------------------------- Back outside the same door as earlier, the same smile we had seem plastered all over the face of Dr. Damian Sorrow was back, and sick as ever. This time, however, he was not standing up, but crouching down, and looking through an open food gate on the cell door. 'Oh SSL, we've done it! We've gotten the Warden right where we want her. Well, for you anyway, I still haven't gotten her in bed, but who cares?! This is a shining moment for you, for us, for me!' He explains in a loud, but seemingly quiet, tone of voice. A few grunts and heavy breaths come from inside the cell as if Steven Lee were responding to everything this man was saying. 'I know you've wanted this since we brought you back. Everyone now knows how important that business is to you, even thought you no longer truly understand what it's all about, except that it is legal for you to get into fights with anyone and everyone standing in your way. Not only that, but this is a chance for me to, well, never mind that, it's not important!' Damian continues to explain to a man who won't know what he's talking or will forget about in thirty seconds or less. No more grunts came from the inside of the cell, but a few more heavy breaths could be heard. The smile fades away from Dr. Sorrow's face now, however, and he tilts his head off to one side. 'Patient Six Sixty-Six? What are you doing?' He asks. A loud growl comes through the door as well as a whole arm, the hand of which easily wrapping around the slim neck of Dr. Sorrow. 'No! Patient Six Sixty-Six, stop!' The doctor exclaims to the best of his ability with his patient's hand clenched around his neck. He frantically reaches into the pockets of his coat and finds what he was looking for, he quickly pulls out a syringe from his left pocket, and removes the cap off of the needle. He quickly stabs the needle deep into the heavily scarred up arm of the patient, and pushes the liquid inside of Steven, or as he would prefer to be called from now on, SSL. The heavy growl quickly ceases, and the heavy breaths become less prominent and begin to slow down. The death grip around Damian's neck loosens, and he quickly back away from the door and leans up against the opposite wall. 'I sense your happiness Patient Six Sixty-Six, but there is no time for hugs and kisses now. You need to be good, the Warden is still watching us, but I'll be taking care of her.' He says and he gingerly stands himself up. Walking back to the door, he pushes SSL's mangled arm back through the food gate on the door. Beginning to take his steps away from the cell, he speaks once more, still close enough for the patient to hear, but knowing he won't understand, he says it aloud anyway. 'SSL, Patient Six Sixty-Six, you are about to make me a very rich, a very powerful, and very happy man. Thank you.' As the doctor walks off, and his footsteps are no longer heard, it seems as if SSL gets the last word in as a loud thud is heard coming from inside of the cell, almost as if an angered young psychopath has launched his fist into the heavy steel of the door that keeps him from exiting his cell.
