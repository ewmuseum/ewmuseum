Valentine vs. Danielle Northsky Danielle Northsky stares at Vivica J. Valentine as she makes her way towards the Global Octagon. Vivica peers back at Danielle from inside. Valentine flies off the top rope with a side kick, but Danielle sucks the move with an arrogant demeanor, just in time to get popped in the face by a hook kick from Vivica.
