Ely Braxton: I hate change [IG: Round 24]

24 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life has not been completely good for one Ely Braxton. At one time he was making a good living; how the hell is that? Pro Wrestling that is how. Three almost four years ago, Ely’s life turned around. He went from living in the streets to living on a ranch. He then started making Big bucks in Global Wrestling. Bubba he is a good man. But Wrestling? Ely found out that his hard-hitting style did not sit well with suits. He had to become softer. This was wrestling not some bar brawl. Softer being the word Ely hated. But for the money, He swallowed his pride and become just that soft. The only chance he got to not be soft was GTT3. A tournament where not only was there fame and fortune, but pride honor and dignity. But Ely had grown soft; too soft in fact and never made it far in that tournament. After that Ely walked away from Global. He told Bubba why… Ely just walked away. ------------------------------------ Ely sat at the bar drinking his beer. The Bar a little cantina south of the border called El salón rojo del Diablo, Roughly The Red Devil Saloon. The place was never that busy, but the owner a small Mexican man by the name of Hector liked having the big man there to keep any trouble down. It was good when the bikers showed up every now and then. Not only was it a bar but a restaurant, and the local general store all in one place. So not only did Ely work here as a bouncer, but his girlfriend for the past three years also worked here. Bobbie Sue Tucker. He met Bobbie on the road at a small town. His vehicle was down and out and she picked him up hitchhiking, and drove him to Dallas Texas A pretty brave thing for a girl to do theses day. Bobbie told him she was not worried. The sawed off shotgun next to her made sure to keep anything bad from happening. At the time Bobbie was a truck driver for her ma. Her and her three brothers. Until her ma and brothers got busted for theft drugs and a ton of other charges. Bobbie never went home; in fact she was leaving home when she picked Ely up. Life was good; Ely had been passing though Oklahoma at the time and was on his way for another Wrestling job when the two met. Seemed another promotion in Dallas had heard his name. Most likely as Bubba knew the owner that he got Ely the job. Ely got the job for WCWF a long running but smaller federation, but a federation that traveled extensively. Bobbie opted to keep her rig and travel with Ely. Not only did they make money on the side picking up cargo, but made the money from his wrestling matches. Ely had a few shows. But still they wanted him to be soft pull his punches… Not wanting to go down this road again, not willing to swallow his pride once more. Ely had Sue pack their bags up one night after getting his check from WCWF. That night Ely vanished off the planet. Not really. The two took their cash and headed down to Mexico. Why Mexico. It was cheep. With the money they had they could live a good life. That was three years ago. How do I know? Well hell son. I know because I am Ely Braxton. ------------------------------------ Ely sat there sharing his thoughts with a passing tourist. The man was happy that some one spoke English. But as fate had it the Man was also a wrestling nut. After a while he remembered Ely’s face. Of all the people, of all the places, this man recognized Ely. Hell no one in three years knew he was a professional wrestler. Not even when they went to a lucha show here and there. No fate had decided to slap Ely right in the face. But being a good hearted man like he is Ely bought the man a beer and sat down to answer this wrestling fans question. The fans name, [name withheld]. He was down in Mexico for Vacation with his family, Wife; Helen, The daughters; Kimberley and Ashley, and one son; Donovan. The father and Son sat down at the table while his wife and daughters took the chance to hit the showers in the back. “It is really nice of you to talk to Mr. Braxton.” James said “Ely’s the name bud. And it is nothing. Though why you want to talk to me, only you and god know. I ain’t famous, Hell I ain’t been in that biz in three years.” “Yes. I know. I was shocked when I realized just who it was that I was talking to. See I was a fan of yours when you were in Global. I heard you went to WCWF. But I never got to see a show. Then you vanished. Wrestling magazines did not know what happened.” James said between drinks of his beer. “I always wondered where you went. And why? I know the first part but why?” “Simple. I don’t want to shock you, or your son. But Wrestling is not completely real. The fights are rigged and you have to make sure not to really hurt your opponent. I always got in trouble for being to rough and hard. I had enough and so I left.” Ely said “Ahh I see. Well yeah I can understand that.” James said drinking the last of his beer as his wife and daughters approached. “James. Time to get going. I have all the stuff bought up, but if we are going to get to the next city before dark we need to go.” James’s wife Helen said. “Okay. Man I wish I had time to talk to you more. If you don’t mind can you sign this? I would like to add it to our collection of autographs.” James said. “Sure thing bud.” Ely said signing the post card James held out to him. “There you go, Nice meeting ya James. Hey kid. Behave and mind your pa now ya hear.” “Sure thing mister. Come on dad. We don’t want mom to get all upset. You know she don’t like driving at night.” Donovan said pulling on his dads arm. The two walked out and Ely could here them talk about how nice it was to meet him. Ely just shook his head. Ely stood up and noticed in the other seat a magazine. Ely picked it up and it was the latest issue of PWI. Ely looked over and headed to the door. To late as the big van pulled off leaving a trail of dust. Bobbie Sue walked up to him and put her hands on his shoulder. “What up? Who was that Ely?” She asked. “Just some wrestling fan. He left his magazine.” Ely said. “Ahh well Hector said he wants you in his office. He is not happy though.” Bobbie said Ely nodded and walked away letting Bobbie clear the table. He stuffed the magazine in his back pocket and headed over and up the stairs to Hector’s office. Ely opened the door seeing the old Mexican man. “Ya wanted me boss?” Ely asked puzzled. “Ahhh Vaquero. How are you today?” Hector asked “Look Cowboy. I like you. You kept things pretty peaceful. I know we are not busy but when the Bikers come you earn your money then.” “Thanks boss, But you did not call me up here just to praise me. So cut to the chase and let me have it.” Ely said taking a seat. “That’s what I like about you Vaquero, Blunt and to the point. Look I am out. I sold the place.” Hector said with a smile. “Excuse me?” Ely asked a bit puzzled. “What does that mean for us?” “Ahh well, See the guys I sold to is going to be here tonight. I told them about everyone. You will all be able to keep your jobs. But me I am going to the Caribbean. I just wanted to say goodbye my amigo.” Hector said with a smile. “Go ahead and close the place up, tell everyone to have a good day and to come back in tonight. I’ll introduce you to your new bosses and then I am gone.” Ely stood up and nodded to Hector and shook his hand and walked out of the office. After telling everyone the news Ely and Bobbie climbs into there truck and left and went to there home. The home was not much. It was single room contained the stove and fridge and sink. There bed sat on the other side with a TV with antennas on top of an old dresser and a radio next to that. Two doors at the back of the room one a closet the other the small bathroom. Ely poured his self a glass of tea and walked out on to the porch and sat down in his chair. Looking in the horizon Ely could see the storm clouds appearing. Every gut instinct told him that it was going to be a bad night. But he squished those quickly being a bit paranoid. Bobbie fixed them both a quick lunch. Left over Beans and Potatoes and cornbread. “ I wonder what these new owners are going to be like. I am going to miss hector. He was a nice old man.” Bobbie said sitting Ely’s bowl down on the table beside him. “Yeah same here darlin. You know how much I hate change.” Ely said as he took a bite of his food and washed it down with a drink of his tea. The two ate in silence once finished Bobbie gathered up the bowls and went in side. “I am going to take a nap. Wake me up in a few hours.” Bobbie said over her shoulders as she dropped the bowls into the sink. “Sure thing.” Ely said as he pulled the magazine out from his back pocket. A bit to his surprise a female holding a title covered the front. Shadow: PTC Champion, Women finally take there right full place in the sport. Was in red text next to her. “Well I’ll be damned.” Ely said to his self. This intrigued him a lot and he opened the magazine and read the article and interview with Shadow. Thumbing though he read about different places there champions their latest news. The he came across an article on the history of GTT; A look back to see the future. “Seams GTT had become a solid and respected tournament.” Ely thought to his self. Finally the article came to a close in thoughts about the up coming GTT. A picture of already announced participants filled the following page. Ely turned the Page behind that picture was a pick of previous champions and an application. Deep inside a desire to compete began. But Ely pushed it aside. Ely looked at his watch. WOW he must have zoned out because a lot of time had gone by while he read that magazine. “Bobbie! Time to go.” Ely said waking his girlfriend up. After a few moments Ely and Bobbie headed to the Red Devil Bar, as they drove something in Ely’s mind kept shouting at him to turn around. Ely shoved them away believe it just to be paranoia, being afraid of change. They pulled up and parked. A black stretch limo sat in front of the bar. A big Mexican man stood on the side. Arms folded. Ely quickly looked the man over. His years in the military came flooding back. The man whoever he was, he was not as relaxed he looked; Ely spotted the handle of the pistol under the man’s coat. Thunder sounded in the distance, as Ely looked into the horizon the Strom was getting closer; you could see the lightning coursing though the clouds. Once again the voice in his head told him to get the hell out of dodge. But he kicked and stomped it back down and nodded to the man upon entering. Bobbie must have felt the tension in Ely’s body, as she held tighter on to him. Inside it was quiet. “I guess we are early” Bobbie said a bit nervous. “Maybe. But something’s not right I can feel it in the air Bobbie. It’s probably just my mind playing tricks on me. But just be ready just in case.” Ely said Soon out of the office upstairs two men came down both dressed in suits one in silk the other in leather. The on in silk was the smaller of the two with his hair slicked back and a small moustache on his face, the other larger bald with a nasty scar along the side of his face. Hector though was nowhere to be found. “Ahh you must be the gringo. The Vaquero.” Said the smaller of the two. “Don’t looks like much.” Said the bigger man with a laugh. “Silencio Raul!” The smaller man said. “ Let me introduce my self. The name is Estaban Estrada This is my brother Raul Estrada.” ~Estrada~ Ely thought. ~The names sound familiar where have I heard the name before.~ “Names Ely.” Ely said putting out his hand. Estaban looked at it and laughed and Ely took his hand away. “Where is Hector?” The two brothers looked at each other and laughed a bit. “Hector… He had a change of heart.” Estaban said. “He won’t be here tonight.” Raul smiled on that as Estaban gave another laugh as if something about what he said was amusing. The two then turned there attention from Ely to Bobbie Sue. “hey Raul, check out this chica. Mmmmm. Nice one huh?” Estaban said with an eerie smile. Estaban’s smile was one that made Ely’s head seem to explode in warnings. Ely pushed them down again, but this time not all the way. His hand slowly slid behind his back and under his shirt. Raul advanced on Bobbie who stepped back behind Ely. Blocked, Raul looked Ely right in the eyes and backed up a little bit. “Okay enough chitter chatter. We are now your boss gringos. You will do as you are told. As well as the little chica behind you.” Estaban said with a serious look on his face. “We are going to turn this place into a money maker. This place will now be a strip club, and brothel. The little chica there will be our first working girl.” Estaban said once again with a smile. “And we are going to sample the goods first.” Raul said walking around Ely. “Hold up there hoss. No ones sampling anything. She is a waitress and that’s about it. Got it bud.” Ely said moving his self in front of Raul. “I do not think you get it.” Estaban said drawing out a pistol. “This is not America. This is not the land of the free. You are in Mexico. And in this part. AHORA SOMOS LA LEY DE FUCKIN! Meaning, We are now the fucking law hombre. So if you wish to keep livin you will sit your white American ass down and shut the fuck up, comprenda?” “Yeah. But there is one thing you should know.” Ely said with a tilt of his head. “What is that?” Estaban asked with a wave of his pistol. “I am not that good with authority.” Ely said with a flick of his wrist. Estaban looked down at his chest in shock. A knife sticking to the hilt in his chest. The pistol dropped followed by Estaban. Raul looked at his brother and the distraction was all Ely needed. A quick to the jaw sent Raul backwards Ely grabs Raul’s arm whipping him to the wall. Raul staggered back and Ely wrapped his arm around the mans neck and locked him into a dragon sleeper. Raul fought back flailing his arms and slamming his elbow into Ely’s side. Instead of putting pressure to cause the man to sleep. Ely lifted until he heard the bones snap. Raul went limp and Ely let him drop to the floor. “Time to go.” Ely said to a shocked and startled Bobbie Sue. “Damn I hate fucking change.” Bobbie snapped to her senses, she had never seen a man take another mans life. She heard her brothers talking about it, but had never seen it. But she knew If Ely had not, things may have gone a way she did not want to think about. Ely reached into there pockets and grabbed there wallets opening up to find a nice amount of cash, and in American not Mexican money. Ely put the wallets into his pocket and picked up the pistol. He took one last look around and headed to the door. Kicking it open gun ready Ely started the large man in the limo. “HEY! Calm down hombre!” The Mexican said putting his hands in the air. Ely nods and then slams the pistol into the side of his head dropping the man. Ely and Bobbie quickly make there way to there truck and peel out of the parking area and down the dirt road. Once to there small home the two quickly pack there things and toss them into the back of the truck. Once done, they leave once again. “Ely where are we going to go? What are we going to do?” Bobbie says though tears. “We are getting the hell out of Mexico. After that, I really don’t know.” Ely says as they speed down the dirt road. Five hours later the two approach the border. Ely stops the truck and tosses the gun into the desert followed by the wallets, and he steps on the gas once more. The two drive up and show Identification to the border patrol and head up into Texas. ------------------------------------ That was three days ago. The two with out stopping save to eat and re fuel continued heading north. The decision was now to go back to Bobbie’s home, Eageltown Oklahoma. Ely with no family to turn to agreed it was the best place to go for now. The two arrived early in the morning. Ma Tucker was happy to see her lil girl and her Brothers had to get to know just who has been with there lil sister. The two got a silver camper trailer and hooked it up in the trailer park Ma now ran. For days they watched and waited. But no one came. Nothing happened. However the money was starting to get thin. “What are we going to do Ely?” Bobbie said as the two sat in the small trailer. “I guess I’ll go looking for work tomorrow. Not going to be easy with no history for three years.” Ely said. “Well what about that McCoy fellow?” Bobbie asked propping her self on her elbows while lying on the small bed. “Checked into that. Bubba is not running Global. That thug train is. I don’t like him, he don’t like me. So there no way I am going back right now. I’ll just get a nice factory job around here.” Ely said. “Yeah. I guess that’s what we will have to do.” Bobbie said. Ely got up kissed her forehead and went to the restroom. Sitting there he rummaged though the magazines that Bobbie had put into the bathroom. The magazine he had left in the truck slid out and Ely picked it up. Looking at the application Ely thought. ------------------------------------ The Next morning Ely walked into the post office and dropped the application for GTT6 in the mail. The money for winning each round was nice. Plus who was he kidding, a factory job. Ely always had issues with someone breathing down his neck and bossing him around. This way he could do what he was best at and make some money. Maybe he could find a place after the tournament was over that would not be like the pro wrestling he was used to. A place where real fighters called home. Hopefully that Australian who ran the GTT tournament would send him an acceptance letter. If not. Ely did not want to think of the 'If not' right then. Instead he was going to take his girl out dancin tonight.
