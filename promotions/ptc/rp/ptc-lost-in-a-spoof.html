Lost in a Spoof

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[to Jin after Hurley gets stung on the foot by something in the water] Hurley: Pee on my feet! Hurley: Okay, that thing in the woods, maybe it's a monster, maybe it's a pissed-off giraffe. I don't know. -Quotes from Lost, Season 1 9/18/2005 "One of you will turn on the others..." That was the last thing James von Doom remembered before he got on the tour boat. He heard that from a psychic on the islands. He went there for a little away time from wrestling but then, something happened. "..." All he can see was a giant blur. He wakes up and looks around. He's on an island and he peers around for the ferry boat, which seems to have washed ashore. "What the..." James is on the edge of the jungle. He gets up and wanders to the beach where he finds their tour boat completely engulfed in flames on the beach. Various people are running around in a panic. James von Doom then realizes that he is... "Lost." * * * A few moments later... James is finally back in reality. James runs over to some of the other people and starts barking orders. JV: Get away from the boat! The fat guy looks at him funny. Guy 1: Why? James looks at the boat and then looks at the Guy. JV: Because it'll explode. The fat guy scratches his head. Guy 1: And how do you know that? JV: Because I know about these kinds of things. You should listen to me. The Guy still looks confused. Guy 1: But why? James growls and looks at him. JV: Because I'm James von Doom the wrestler. The Guy looks at him funny. Guy 1: Dude, like, I see you all the time on TV and... Just then, the tour boat explodes and everyone ducks for cover. After the explosion, James gets up and looks around. JV: Marvelous. * * * Five minutes later... James, being the leader that he is, has gathered the survivors together and they are now huddled together. They are a bunch of misfits, miscreants, and deviants. There's Count Fuckula, the first porno vampire. There's the Mariachis, a mariachi band that was on tour in the Caribbean. There's Captain Jack Morgan of Killer Pirates fame. There's Tom Sawyer, who seemingly had lost his way and made it to the ocean instead of the Mississippi River. There's Eva, the criminal. And then there's Jack Meehoff, the hunky doctor with an anxiety disrorder. But of all the people, they put one man in charge...James von Doom. JV: Ok. We're here now. What do we do? The fat guy, who James has learned is named Barf, is standing next to him. Barf: Dudes, like, we need to figure out what to do next. Cock, the bald headed survivalist lunatic in the group, steps forward. Cock: Like getting off this island. Everyone looks at Cock. JV: And what's your name? Cock: My name is Cock. The Middle Eastern guy starts giggling. James has a hard time keeping a straight face. Barf: Dude, like, that name rocks. Cock smiles. Cock: Don't know what's so funny. I might have to probe the island to see what we've got to deal with. James has a sly smile on his face. JV: Probe the island? Cock probing an island. Cock: Yes. JV: Well now we're getting somewhere. James turns to the Middle Eastern man. JV: What's your name? The man begins speaking in Arabic. Arab Man: (speaks in Arabic) James raises an eyebrow. JV: But what's your name, dude? Arab Man: Mohammed Ali. James tries to keep a straight face. JV: This is frickin' weird. Cock has no idea what he's talking about. Cock: What is it James? James looks around. JV: We need to start looking for supplies just in case the rescue team comes and gets us late. Cock: But how are we going to do that? Barf: Yeah dude. Like how are going to find supplies in the jungle? James pats him on the back. JV: We'll find them. James motions for Cock. JV: Follow me, Cock. We shall probe the island. Cock follows James into the jungle. Barf: (yelling) BUT HOW? * * * An hour later... James and Cock are in the jungle wandering around when they start making some small talk. JV: So Cock. How do you know so much about the jungle? Cock: I used to work for a box company. James raises an eyebrow. JV: Really? Cock nods his head. Cock: Yes. JV: So what does that have to do with hunting in the jungle with knives? Cock smiles. Cock: Nothing. James stops and points at something. JV: What is that? * * * Twenty minutes later... James and Cock are still staring at a hatch. It has no discernable way of opening it up because there is no way to open it. No wheel or knob to open the hatch. Just a hatch. JV: Dude, it's a hatch. Cock: I know. James looks at Cock. JV: But how do we open it? Cock gets on top of the hatch and starts biting the hatch. JV: DUDE~! Cock stops gnawing on the hatch and turns to James. Cock: Nope. Not that way. James starts looking for a button or something to open it up. The rest of the survivors come stumbling through the jungle and reach the hatch. James and Cock turn around to them. JV: What do you think you're doing? Barf shrugs his shoulders. Barf: Finding you guys. JV: Well why aren't you on the beach looking for the rescue ships? Barf: Uhhhhh... James looks at him with a scowl. JV: Well? Barf: Um...we got like, scared. Barf looks at the hatch and points to it. Barf: Dude, what's that? JV: Oh just some hatch we found. Cock was gnawing on it. Barf: A cock was gnawing on a hatch? James nods his head. JV: I guess you can say that. Barf: Dude, this is gonna make one fucked up children's novel. Mohammed Ali points to the hatch and starts speaking in Arabic. Mohammed Ali: (speaking in Arabic) Barf points to Mohammed. Barf: Dude, like, what the hell is he saying? JV: Like I would know. James turns back to Cock. JV: So what should we do? Cock: I say we wait here until morning. It's almost nightfall and we have no idea what's on this island. We can go explore some more tomorrow. JV: Alright Gilligan, we camp here tonight. Barf: Wow. We get to sleep next to the creepy hatch? James nods his head. JV: Yeah. Barf: Cool. * * * 9/19/2005 The next morning... James, Barf, Cock, and co. awake by the hatch. James looks over and sees a house. JV: There's a house...A HOUSE~! Everyone wakes up and looks at the house. Barf: We're saved! We're saved! Suddenly, some Coast Guard ships arrive on the beach and Johnny Depp comes out of the house. James is ecstatic. JV: It's Johnny! Johnny stays on the porch and yells at them. Johnny Depp: I TOLD YOU PAPARAZZI TO LEAVE ME ALONE! James looks at him funny as the Coast Guard runs over and arrests them all. JV: What the hell is he talking about? Coast Guard Man: You shipwrecked on Johnny Depp's private island and you're being arrested for trespassing. JV: But it's not like we're pirates! Captain Jack Morgan smiles at him. Jack: Think again, mate. JV: Oh bother. The scene fades out. Cut. Jack: Things could be worse. Hurley: How? -Quote: Lost, Season 1
