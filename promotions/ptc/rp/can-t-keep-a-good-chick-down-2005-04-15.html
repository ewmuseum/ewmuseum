Can't Keep A Good Chick Down

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-04-15 It all began with a dream. It all ended with a nightmare. What happened in between, no one cared about. At least that is what she wanted to pretend. In reality, however, it was what had made her what she was now, totally useless. It was no secret that the same gentle hands that signed her life away to a ring were the same hands that pulled the trigger on her career as well. Now the only thing left was silence. Even with one child, you would never expect to come home to absolute silence. With two children, it only doubles the shock. Multiply that by a month’s time, and it becomes even more understandable why even her enormous heart could break down to nothing more than a shard of broken safety glass. So, just as broken glass does, it chased her away from scene of the crime, into the same black hole of fear she vowed never to return to. She was hardly a demon, a devil, or anything even close to that sort. If anything, the pearly white light of her smile could illuminate the darkest of caves. But even the strongest of fires succumb to a lack of oxygen, just as her soul had. There was nothing left for her, but the woman whose mildly toned arms she had intentionally fallen into. She was the one last thing Tessa had left on this earth. Before the fallen diva, stood a woman who was much wiser than the twenty-three years on her face showed. “Something wrong?” “You’ve got to help me, Dee.” Burying her head into blonde locks that flowed over her acquaintances rounded shoulders, the woman brought up in the wrestling world by the likes Chris Storm continued to add the depths of the small lake that began to form on the bottom on her cheek. “With?” Dee’s emerald eyes perked up, doing all the questioning. “Everything.” “I thought you and Chris were fine again?” She questioned, as she ran her hands up and down Tessa’s back in attempts to soothe her. “We are.” She choked out in between weeps. “Then what is it?” Somehow, with no added words, only sobs, Dee knew exactly what she meant. “Can’t take sitting around the house anymore, can you?” As much as she wanted to deny it, she knew that denial was always a first sign of the problem. Considering she knew the answer would eventually force its way out of her mouth, there was no use in holding it back now. Therefore, a simple nod did the job. “I knew you’d eventually cave in.” Dee stated with a catty chuckle that she kept to herself. “But you‘re in luck.” “Right, just as I’ve been every other time anyone’s said that.” As selfish as it sounded, Tessa was hardly trying to draw pity towards herself; it was just that luck was not exactly something that followed the five foot four beauty around. “Believe me, have I got an idea for you. You‘re going to love it” With a smug smirk planted across her scarlet painted lips, one could only wonder if Dee was simply trying to get a small chuckle out of Tessa, or harass her into seeing things her way. Of course being Tessa, she bought into the much brighter outlook. Rising up from the couch, she followed Dee like a shadow into the kitchen, where everything needed was handed to her in a sealed envelope. “That’s it?” Drawing her eyes away from the envelope originally intended for Dee, Tessa looked back up at her friend with a questioning look. “This is going to change my life?” “Just take a look and think about it.” Like a puppet on a string, Tessa head fell back towards the envelope with a slight grumble, until her narrowed blue eyes rolled onto the words she wanted to see. Cruiserweights Only. “Count me in.” “That I’ll do.” Snatching back the letter as if it stolen, Dee grinned from ear to ear not deviously, but casually enough to let Tessa know there was a catch. “Just one thing….” “Anything.” Tessa replied without zero hesitation, though she hardly knew what she could be getting herself into. Right about now she would do just about anything to save herself from falling into the sea of fallen stars. “Anything?” Even Dee seemed a bit shocked to hear this, and though Dee’s intentions were not bad, she knew Tessa was just what she needed to help herself out as well. Which meant now, there was no turning back for either. “Great, then pack your bags for a week and be here bright and early. Believe me, I‘m going to turn things around for the both of us.” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Just as every other day since releasing herself from Prime, she came home to the same quiet that greeted her every morning, afternoon, and night. With it being nearly two in the morning though, this was much more expected. By now, the kids had worn themselves out and eventually crashed landed into their beds, and Chris was probably dead asleep on the couch dreaming (the only thing he could ever do) about his days of Global Champ. However, never before had Tessa been gone this long, which was enough to keep the former Global Champion his toes. The minute her Honda Civic peered it silver hood into the driveway of the small four-bedroom estate, he had slipped onto the porch and assumed the role of responsibility that was long overdue. “Where were you?” In the past they would have been the words that would have slipped off Tessa’s gloss covered lips. But with the way things were going lately, it almost seemed as if the roles had totally reversed. Instead in the doorway stood the man who boasted about ended the careers of many, but could not stand the chance of losing the one thing he prized most, her. “You had me worried.” “That’s a change.” She shouted back to him, with a slight bit of humor as she made her way in his direction. But, he failed to see any humor in her antics. To him it was almost as if she were mocking everything he driven himself away from, which is something he did not take lightly to. He had worked a long way to come to terms with whom he truly was inside to make her happy. And here she was pulling the same kind of stunts that she criticized him for. “You know if it were me coming in at this hour, you wouldn’t find it funny.” “You’re right, I’m sorry.” Stamping his face with the remaining traces of her pink lipstick, it seemed more like she were trying to play a game out cat and mouse than win over his sympathy . “But I needed a night out. You for one should understand that.” “And what about the kids?” Wrapping his arm around her petite frame, he glided her inside before she could find a reason to escape his questioning. “Just because I’m back around here, doesn’t mean I want to sit around playing father all day.” “Welcome to my life.” She muttered under her breath. If only he had been the one to stick around after their split. Here he was complaining about one night of staying home with the kids, try a years worth of it on your own. Then again, Tessa really had no room to speak on that front either. It is not exactly as if her job allowed her to tuck her kids in at night. And when it did, it was always at her other home, Holiday Inn‘s nationwide. She had a point, a point that kept him silent for a moment. He could not argue with that considering he was hardly a candidate for father of the year. Still, he had plenty of reason to question her behavior considering her quest to change his. “Listen; forget about the kids for a second. Just tell me what’s got you up so late.” “I just went to visit Dee, that’s it!” By the tone of anger in her voice, one would never be able to guess that she was the quieter and much more relaxed one in the relationship. “Is that something so wrong?” She had him there. So rather than give her more to chew apart, he backed away into the living room, and found his place on his makeshift bed, the leather couch that stretched across half the wall. Maybe then he’d get some peace, considering at the moment he felt as pinned against the wall as the couch was with the question directed towards him that brought his voice down to a mere whisper. “No, I’m just trying to watch out for you.” “I understand.” The assurance in her voice was all he needed to hear at the moment to chase away his fears, as his head collapsed against the arm of the couch. “But we’ll talk about it later.” So much for the assurance part. Maybe it was just time to give in and accept that for some reason she wanted to keep quiet about what she was up to, which could only mean trouble surely. He had things of his own to worry about though, so if that’s what she wanted, then all the power to her. “That’s fine. We’ll talk whenever I get back from New York.” His words caught her ears, and instantly drew her attention back where he wanted it, on him. “You’re going back? But what about…” Hardly in the mood for her bitchings at this late of an hour, he latched onto the nearest pillow and nestled his head underneath. “I’ll be back, calm yourself.” “But I’ve got…” Before she could finish, the phone had let its own voice be heard between the two, but only one welcomed it with open arms. “It‘s two in the fucking morning.” Grumbled Chris whose addiction to the sport was beginning to make him wondering if even six million dollars was a good enough price to keep him away from the ring, considering the alternative meant dealing with this until Tessa snapped out of her what about me stage. “WHO calls at two in the morning?” As lacking in life as Chris was, Tessa was the total opposite and sprung to life by the second ring, dashing to the receiving end in the kitchen. “Don’t worry I’ll get it.” Thus making it obvious that the call was expected. And now even Chris who was all bronze, okay scratch that, no bronze or brains, realized this, which is why he too leapt off the couch and sped up to catch her right before her hand wrapped around the portable device. “Chris, I said I’d get it!” Was Tessa’s whining response triggered as the answering machine beat her to it, allowing her children‘s voices to do all the inquiring. “It’s probably just a prank.” He suggested, before tuning even himself out, something he did very rarely. Instead, his ears turned their attention to the answering machine, which had something much more informative to say. “Hey Tessa, it‘s Dee. About that wrestling thing…” The first bit alone was enough to bring out the chill in Storms eyes when he locked them with Tessa’s. “Wrestling thing?” And even though he himself was hardly innocent about falling back into the business, he almost seemed ready to walk there. “When were you planning on telling me about this?” Being very aware of this, it would have been only natural for Tessa to shy away and let him dominate, but by this point, she had absolutely nothing left to lose. So, she snapped. “SHUT UP; LET ME HEAR WHAT SHE’S SAYING!” Surprisingly enough, it got him to listen. “I faxed in your application.” The machine continued on, as the two hovered over it as if it were a piece of hidden treasure. “And you wouldn’t believe it; they got you a spot right away, against Joshua Taro Freedom. Well, I’ve got to go. Take care dear, and give me a call as soon as you get this.” For a moment the two welcomed the silence, each inhaling thoughts of what to say to the other now. Tessa ultimately ended up being the one to break the silence. “It’s only a tournament.” “And then you’ll end up in yet another place.” Chris concluded, knowing quite well how that system worked. He had done the same after all, quite a few times. All she could do now is try to charm her way out, which is exactly what she did. “With you.” “Not with me.” He responded with a firm tone that assured that for once, this was out of love, not envy. “Tess, we’ve gone through this so many times now and every time you end up miserable. You don‘t need to prove anything.” “Yes I do!” Standing there with her hand on her hip as she whined, she looked much more like a teenager trying to bargain with their parents than a twenty three year old parent herself. “And say you do, then what?” He had caught her off guard there. She had never thought about it herself. Say by some huge kind of miracle she managed to pull off winning this entire thing, then what? She did not need money. The fame? It’s only temporary. So then what? She really should have acknowledged at least that fact, but her pride got the best of her, blocking those factors out. “We’ll see.” She responded with a quiet sigh, as she slowly started to slip away towards hall in hopes to avoid any further interrogation. If her guilt weren’t getting the best of her right now, the added weight of his pressure would surely cause her to cave. ”But I promise you this is the last time.” “Sure it is.” He whispered to himself, feeling a slight bit guilty inside his own heart for what she had become. Nevertheless, there was nothing that could be done now, she too had become dependent on the business just as he had, which is why he kept to himself the words once more. “Sure it is.”
