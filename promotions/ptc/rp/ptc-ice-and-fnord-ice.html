THIS is where ICE AND FNORD will RP!

11 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
11 March 2004 "Five days before the biggest match in my career, I walked into the arena I called home. I walked into the Global Arena, and watched on as the ring crew set up the ring, set up the lighting, and got things ready for the event that evening. I watched as they placed object after foreign object in and under the ring. I asked myself many times what I was doing, when in five days I was going to be in Detroit, facing Akodo Rokku for Global Wrestling's World Championship. But not only was I facing Akodo Rokku for the World Championship, I would be fighting for that title in a SIXTY MINUTE IRON MAN MATCH. So what was I doing, just five days before, in a match that could have injured me or ended my career before the biggest match I had ever had? What compelled me to be there? What was it that deep down inside, made this opportunity something I could not miss being apart of?" I looked over the camera crew, sitting there, watching, listening to my everyword as I stopped and gathered my thoughts. Some of them were on the edge of their seats, others we relaxed, enjoying the opportunity they were having. I, on the other hand, was very uncomfortable. The cheap "director's chair" that I was given was quite uncomfortable. It had very little give to it, and was small for a man my size. Not to mention those lights. Hot as hell, they were, and blinding. I've always hated these stupid sit down interviews. Why I agreed to it I still have no clue. My thinking, however, abruptly stopped as I was asked another question. I was a little angered that this young punk, had the nerve, to interrupt me. He had the nerve to not only interrupt my thinking, but interrupt my thinking with an ingnorant question. "So then, Ice, what made your mind up? What was that something deep down inside?" I confindently smirked at the twenty something little punk that was trying to act like a reporter, asking me a question like I was going to answer him. Like I cared what he said. I was in a decent mood, however, and instead of just tearing apart the poor boy's question like I normally would have done, I decided I would play along with him. Let him get his kicks by feeling "important", and comment on his pathetic little question. "As I stood there and overlooked that empty arena, I decided I had to be apart of the event, because these men were walking into MY HOME, and I wasn't going to allow them to just waltz in there withouth a nice warm welcome from the man who is the head of that house. No, I didn't win the match. I really didn't plan on winning. My mind was elsewhere. Focused on my match with Akodo Rokku, which was going to be in a mere five days, following that event." The boy opened his mouth to speak. He hesitated and shuffled through his notebook, before spitting the words out of his mouth. All the while, his eyes never looking up, never making contact with mine. They stayed glued to the notebook in his hands. As if he were intimidated, by me to even look at me while speaking to me. "So, is that your excuse for letting Global Wrestling down, and losing so early, as you did?" His question caught me off guard, and for a moment I was quite peturbed by it. I adjusted myself in my seat, and instantly saw the poor boy turn a ghostly shade of white, and his eyes widen in fear. I knew what he was thinking. He had done his homework, and knew my past. He was obviously afraid of me, and what I might do to him for a comment such as that. His fear slowly subsided as I stayed seated, after adjusting my positioning in the chair, however I could still see the uncertainty and uneasiness in the way his hands trembled and shook the small notepad he held between them. "Excuse? By the trembling of your hands, I can assume you have done your homework on me. So you should know that I make excuses for nothing. I went into that match looking for competition. I went into that match looking to make a decent showing for myself, and for GW. I went into that match, looking for a little warm up before the match that I really cared about. And from that match I got everything I wanted. Now however, I've been challenged. Challenged....to a match, by a man who sees this as a way to leap frog himself into this Extreme Title picture. Challenged by a man who does not seem to sense the seriousness of which this situation really is. Challenged by a man, who thinks I am just going to be some push over he can use as a stepping stone." Slowly I inhaled a long, deep breath, and gazed into the bright lights hanging from the ceiling above. The young man interviewing me opened his mouth to speak, but was quickly cut off as I exhaled. My gaze wandered back down from the bright lights hanging from the ceiling above, and locked into the eyes of the young interviewer sitting a few feet across from me. I watched as the sweat dripped down his forehead. I watched as he nerveously bit his bottom lip, awaiting something from me after the loud exhale that cut him off. I continued to stare at him before finally continuing on. "Allow me to make one thing perfectly clear. I am no push over, and I will not be used as a stepping stone. When those lights go down, the chill will hit your body, and you will know what is soon to come. There is no stopping it. There is no avoiding it. It is the inevitable. It is the fate destiny has set forth for you. And through me destiny will hand you that fate." The lights dimmed, and I saw the camera turn off. The young man, stood up and walk towards me, hand extened, but the gesture was not returned on my part. I simply stood, removed the microphone that was placed on me, and walked away. My job was done, and I wasn't going to stick around any longer than what I had to. I had other things to do. I had other things to prepare for. The night was young, and there were things that needed to be done. Things that needed to be organized, and prepared before destiny could deliver our fate. ---- 14 March 2004 The lights dimmed, and I saw the camera turn off. The young man, stood up and walk towards me, hand extened, but the gesture was not returned on my part. I simply stood, removed the microphone that was placed on me, and walked away. My job was done, and I wasn't going to stick around any longer than what I had to. I had other things to do. I had other things to prepare for. The night was young, and there were things that needed to be done. Things that needed to be organized, and prepared before destiny could deliver our fate. I walked from the small room, we had been placed in before the interview began. The walk seemed to take longer than it should have, but did not take that long in all reality. It was only a few minutes, from the room where the interview took place, to the parking lot where my black Ford Expedition was parked. I walked through the single glass door, which was the only entrance and the only exit to the building, and did not look back. My mind was focused, my thoughts centered, and my vision, of what needed to be done, clear as crystal. The drive down the highway, back to the run down, beat up, and broken motel I called home, was a blur. My mind was not on the road, the other cars on the road with me, or the path in which I was taking. It was not until I reached my destination, that I broke out of the trance-like state my mind was in. When I opened the door to my Expedition, and heard the intolerable dinging as the driver side door was open, I was instantly reminded of where I was at, and that I had reached my destination. I opened my eyes wide, trying to reawaken myself, as if I were just rolling out of bed and rising to meet the world. "Yo, Ice-man." A young boy, in a throw back Houston Astro's jersey with matching cap, turned slightly to the left, approached me as I stepped out of my vehicle. My feet hit the soft gravel, as the boy reached me. At first I did not even attempt to acknowledge him, I was far too busy with more pressing issues to deal with, and I certainly did not need this boy to keep me any longer from them. I reached back into the vehicle, and grabbed my cell phone. Slowly I slide myself back out of the Expedition. I turned around and was met face to face by the pale skinned, hip hop looking, wanna be. I sneered slightly at his sight, and shot the poor boy a glare of anger and annoyance. The boy however did not seem to take the hint. "Ice-man, wud-up? I be checkin' youz out an' be seeing you gotz some things goin' on this week. I figure youz be busy an' all, and waz thinkin' maybe youz could use youz a lil' help o' whatevah." Perturbed by the boy already, I was almost driven over the edge, when trying to figure out just what the hell he was saying with that jibberish that he called "english". The boy stared up at me, a nerveous expression on his face, and waiting for my reply. He was not to be so lucky. Instead I again attempted to give the boy a hint, by angrily slamming my door shut, and giving him the cold shoulder as I walked past him and around to the back of the Expedition. Once again, however, did not take the hint. He did however, proceed to follow me around to the back of my vehicle, like a lost child, or puppy looking for attention. By this time my patience was growing rather thin. I opened the back of the vehicle, and reached in to grab my bag. I pulled out my black gym back, and slung it over my right shoulder. Without even looking at the boy, I closed the back of the Expedition, and turn to head inside. "Man, don't be all ingnorin' me 'n whatevah. I knowz you gotz this fight comin' up with that Fnord dude, and was jus' tryin' to be a lil' helpful, takin' some shit off yo' mind." Suddenly I spun around and saw the boys caramel brown eyes widen in terror. Instantly following that I slung my black gym back back off of my shoulder, and thrusted it into the boy's chest. He stumbled back a few steps at the impact of the thrust, but quickly regathered himself. A wide smile of accomplishment engulfed his face as he wrapped the bag's strap across his body. "What's your name?" The boy didn't answer at first, I assume it was out of shock from finally hearing me speak to him. I tilted my head down and to the right slightly and asked the question once again. "Well? What is your name?" The boy's bottom lip began to quiver, as he fumbled out an answer. "J..Ja...James. James Gudeman." "How old are you?" Again the boy hesitated before answering. "I be seventeen. I live three rooms down from ya. I see ya comin' 'n goin' alot. I always be askin' myself, "Wonder if he needs help wit anytin." So, todayz when I seen ya I figure I ask." I nodded, and then looked away, gazing over the highway just a few feet from us and the cars that were frequently passing by. The boy readjusted the strap of my bag across his body, and turned to see what I was gazing at. "Well James, if you want to help, carry that bag for me. I'm going to be heading up to my room now." I turned and started towards the motel doors. James was quick to follow, but was struggling with the actual carrying of the rather heavy bag I had given him. An slightly entertained grin grew across my face, as I glanced back every so often and humored myself at the struggles of this poor sap. As I walked pass the elevator, and headed for the steps I could hear the sighing of James, and the grin which was on my face grew wider, almost to a smile. I proceeded to walk the entire three flights of stairs, listening to James's struggling efforts of lugging my bad up the stairs. He did not quit however, and that bag did not touch the floor. Finally we reached my room. It was the first time since being handed the bag, that James set it down. He hunched over, and began to gasp for air as I unlocked the door to my room. The door swung open and I walked in, leaving James and the bag outside the room. James was not out there long, as he hurrily picked the bag back up and darted into the room. "Mr.....Ice?" I could hear him ask unsuringly, as he stood in the living quarters of my small room, alone. I continued to walk towards the refrigerator, and pulled out two cold bottle of water before heading back to where I had left James. As I walked back into the room I saw a look of relief radiate from his eyes as he eyed the bottles of water in my hands. I offered one of the bottles to James, and he gladly snatched it from my hand. Slowly I twisted the cap off of the bottle I had, and watched as James tore his open, like a wild animal tearing at its prey. "Thank...*breath*......*gulp*....you...*breath*" I pulled the bottle away from my lips, and nodded in response to James, as he devouered the rest of the water in the bottle. As he finished off his water I walked over to the nightstand next to my bed and opened up the top drawer. I pulled out a crisp, clean, one hundred dollar bill, folded it in half, and walked back over to James. "Thank you for your help carrying my bag for me James." He smiled widely, as I offered my hand to him. He returned the offer, but gave me a quizzicle look as he felt the bill glide up against his palm. I pulled my hand away, and he turned his. His eyes lit up as he saw the bill that was given to him, and his wide smile grew even wider as he stared in astonishment at the one hundred dollar bill I had just given to him. "W...W...Why thank you VERY MUCH!" I walked over to James, and placed my hand firmly on his shoulder. He lovingly continued to gawk at the one hundred dollar bill in his hands, as I walked him out of my room. As I pushed him out into the hallway, James spun around. "Is there sumptin else ya need from me?" I arrogantly smirked at him. "Yes, there is. Learn to speak proper english." And with that I shut the door, and walked over to my bag. I dragged the bag over to the end of my bed, and took a seat. Slowly I pulled the zipper, opening up the black gym bag, to reveal it contents. A pair of black wind pants, a pair of black sneakers, my wrestling boots, a roll of athletic tape, a grey cut off t-shirt, and my GW World Championship belt, were all pulled out of the bag one by one. I gazed over the items I had just pulled out, before reaching back into the bag one last time. Slowly I pulled out a small black VHS video tape. I twirled the tape in my hands for a moment, before standing up and thrusting it into my VCR. After putting the tape in the VCR, I walked back over to my bed, and made myself comfortable in it. I then reached into the top drawer of the nightstand next to me, and pulled out a large blue notebook, a black ink pen, and the remote to the VCR. I hit the play button, and a packed arena filled the TV monitor. The announcers blared through my TV's speakers. "Next up, we have an exciting bout for you folks at home. As West Wind Champion Fnord defends his title." My concentration was annoyned by the sound, so I instantly hit the mute button, as Fnord made his way down the aisle, and into the ring. Just before the match began I repositioned myself, and audiably asked..... "Just what do you have to bring Mr. Fnord? And where is that flaw, that you undoubtably have that will seal your fate?"
