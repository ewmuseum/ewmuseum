Wrestler: Hunchblack Fed: Free Agent -------------------- I’ve spent my whole life wrestling for the amusement of others. Now, what do I do as soon as I manage to escape? That’s right; I enter a wrestling tournament. Upon signing up for IG31, I found myself pleasantly surprised that they would allow a hunchbacked nigga such as myself to compete with the ‘normals’. After finishing second place to a man called Jason Snow, I was invited back to wrestle in IG32. My hump trembled and pulsated in delight that I was to be given a second chance. In the meantime, I took the advice of the Infinite Gauntlet officials to keep a low profile. They told me that if the circus found me, that they couldn’t stop them dragging me back there by the hunch. Some sort of legal mumbo-jumbo that a humble Hunchblack wouldn’t understand, apparently. Of course the draw back of this agreement is that PTC wouldn’t pay for a hunched nigga to stay in a nice hotel room, or even a shitty one. I’m at the beginning of a professional wrestling career but still no better off materially, financially or indeed socially. I sleep my way through the day, so I can walk the streets at night. Just like a common whore, only less birth defects. Unlike a whore however, I can’t spend too much time on my back, or I’ll roll over. I’m Hunchblack you see. So I’m back to were I was upon leaving the circus; the streets. Streets that are unforgiving and restless, that whistled and whispered to me in the cold lonely nights. Streets that send a chill up my spine, that fizzles out upon reaching my hump. I found out from a discarded newspaper that I’m in New York City. ‘The Big Apple’ as I heard a homeless brother call it. I’ve yet to find this ‘Big Apple’ but alas, the night is young. Besides, I dined heavily on the contents of a posh restaurant’s garbage can. How could they discard perfectly good rancid fish heads? Of course I want to comprehend and embrace the culture of the normals. But part of me, I fear, will never truly understand them. Still their trash is my gourmet, and I had no complaints. The Mental Diary of Hunchblack Esq. Aged Twenty Three and Three Quarters. 11:00PM: I awoke in a small room inside an abandoned building. The night in this city was eerie; I hoped I wouldn’t be on my own for much longer. 11:09PM: After investigating a scuffling noise I discovered a rodent feasting on my left over fish heads (I was saving them for breakfast). The animal did not seem startled by my appearance, and I leapt on the opportunity to extend an offer of friendship. 11:10PM: Much to my distress, upon clutching the rodent to my chest, it soon suffocated. 11:15PM: I held a small funeral for ‘Squeaky’ in the grounds of the building, under the watchful eye of the moon. We were the only visitors present. I hoped there would be more people turn out for my funeral. Not that I plan to expire just yet. I still hadn’t experienced the delights of human love making or Bohemian Rhapsody. 11:31PM: Eager to explore more of the city and cover more ground, I decided to make use of the subway and travel to another district. I entered the carriage and found myself the only passenger, it was like the normals avoided me in advance. I sat down amidst some truly disgusting litter and lewd messages painted opposite me. I still couldn’t understand the cultural fascination with these words; it seemed the very taboo of the subject was the actual attraction. Perhaps someday they would learn to embrace me the same way. Out of nowhere a shaggy haired, humbly dressed old man entered the carriage; carrying a bunch of flowers (well, stalks) in one hand and a bag of ice cubes in t’other. Crazy Old Man: When’s the best time to read ‘Richard III’? Summer or winter? He looked at me straight in the eye. He was the first person to do that to me in a long time. A long time I waited, and when it came the ‘normal’ in question appeared to be insane. Nevertheless, I answered his question politely. Hunchblack: Winter. Now is the winter of our discontent, my friend. Crazy Old Man: Is the right answer! As only a crazy old man would do, he proceeded to drop the ice cubes down his vest and then his underpants. He offered me the bag, but I made my excuses and exited the train. Even the normals are too weird for ol’ Hunchblack. The Mental Diary of Hunchblack Esq. Aged Twenty Three and Three Quarters, Continued. 12:08AM: The streets were all but quiet as I hauled my black ass into every garbage can and dumpster I could find, searching for something to occupy my time. 12:33AM: In the dumpster outside the library I found a sizable collection of damaged and incomplete literature. Having not read a book since arriving in the city, I decided to have a browse. The only reading material on offer was decided by those who discarded their magazines in the trash. But I had no desire to read about Lindsay Lohan or Britney Spears, or how global warming will kill our grandchildren (why didn’t we listen?). In this particular stockade of literature, however, there was something that sparked my interest. It was entitled ‘The Elephant Man’ by Ashley Montagu. It told of a man named Joseph Merrick, ‘The Elephant Man’ they called him. He wasn’t as good looking as me, and not even black. Still, reading of his condition and his pain made tears run out of my eye (my good eye). Like me, Mr. Merrick was initially treated as nothing more than a sideshow and was taunted and ridiculed. I felt like I had a brother. Seeing there were more people like me gave me great hope. I decided that after the Infinite Gauntlet, I would use my participation fee to travel to England to meet Joseph Merrick; the Elephant Man. He always appeared to be a well dressed man, and knew I needed to be measured for a suit. I didn’t believe any of the major designers in this city would have the appropriate garments to house my mighty hump. But nevertheless I could see myself and Mr. Merrick forming a great and formidible friendship. The Mental Diary of Hunchblack Esq. Aged Twenty Three and Three Quarters, Continued. 1:07AM: I followed a small congregation of negroes into a dark alleyway, hoping to join their society. 1:10AM: Was left confused after their chieftain proceeded to sodomise each of them. I decided to find somewhere else to spend the night. 1:30AM: Disco danced my way into a gentlemen’s club. Was kicked out on account of not being a gentleman. I contested this by reciting a Rudyard Kipling poem. 2:01AM: I awoke in a daze after being knocked unconscious by a lofty white doorman. From then on, I had to block out the temptation to get funky. For now at least. I walked gingerly through the back alleys of apartment buildings, curious as to how the normals in this city lived their normal lives. On a clear day I could see everything, but at night all I could see was the lives of the so called normals that were glued to the television throughout the night. I regularly ask myself if they are truly better off than me, and always convince myself they are. At least they are accepted in their society (unless they are playing World of Warcraft). At least they have people who care about them (online). Still, I can cling on to the fact I’m not a cracker (or a dwarf). The Mental Diary of Hunchblank Esq. Aged Twenty Three and Three Quarters, Concluded. 2:15AM: I was bored. Every street corner was starting to look the same, the rain continued to beat mercilessly on my hump causing discomfort with my trench coat getting stuck to it. I prayed for the day someone would invent an inexpensive but fashionable clothing line for physically disfigured niggas, such as yours truly. I walked down the street as fast as I can without running. I didn’t want to direct too much attention to myself. Like the IG crackers told me, I wanted to keep a low profile. It’s all very well to be given orders and being expected to honour them, but they had no idea what it's like to be me. To be so grotesque nobody wants to look at you, speak to you or listen to you. It’s a lonely existence. Keep a low profile they said. All I ever wanted was a friend. Someone like Maria; who I know I can never see again- not if I want to be free. But what good is freedom when you’re alone? Stumbling up the curb, I came across a telephone box. I’d seen the normals activate this device before but I never had the will or the need to use one. Inside this box were pictures and phone numbers pasted on the side from top to bottom. One of which caught my eye (my good eye). It stated that upon phoning the said telephone number, that I would be granted the opportunity to partake in an ‘exciting’ and ‘stimulating’ conversation- albeit not written quite as eloquently as I made out. The female in the picture was partially nude and clutching a very suggestive looking object. To my understanding this ‘stimulating’ conversation they wrote of could only refer to one subject; literature. The female did not look like a scholar to me, the photograph must have been taken before receiving her diploma. This seemed too good to be true. A conversation with a female normal about literature, I could hardly wait! I put some currency into the machine and took a deep breath. The air filled my lungs with excitement (and air), and caused my hump to quiver with joyous anticipation. I dialled the number. The phone rang. It rang again. Then finally, an answer.
