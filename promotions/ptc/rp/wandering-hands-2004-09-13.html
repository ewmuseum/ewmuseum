Date: 2004-09-13 We open on the XUW Intercontinental Title. It has belonged to many legends of XUW, but not at the moment. It has been a stepping stone to greatness for a few select wrestlers, but it’s current occupier is not one of them. It is a gateway to the XUW World Title for the lucky few who are allowed to wrap it around their waists, but somehow, the current holder has already climbed that mountain, and likely, will never come close to doing it again … ever. It is many things to many people … but for now … it’s a pillow for Fatbadger. His hair falls across his name on the new shiny nameplate. Badger twitches a few times and whimpers and softly barks, probably dreaming of a either a new kind of snack, Funyuns: Code Red, or a new soda, Mountain BBQ: Code Funyuns. Either way, he is happy and not dreaming about any Prime Time Central tournaments. And he’s certainly not dreaming about the Ripper or the Shadow, who both have haunted his dreams. Don’t remind him that the Ripper and he were teammates at Survival. Or that the Ripper is now active again at XUW. Or that the Shadow is also in the Golden Turnbuckles Tournament with him. Or just go ahead and remind him, he’s not likely to remember five minutes later. Yikes. Whoever gets that belt next is gonna have to get it cleaned because Fatty is drooling pretty heavily on it. At least his teammates don’t mind, or at least they don’t notice, as they’re sitting around him. On the couch with him are Saij and Rujasu. Yeah, it’s a big couch. Dave Kipfer is in his recliner, as is good old Jim Ross, er-, not that they’re in the same recliner, just that they each have their own respective recliners. Cardboard Cutout Chase Wilson and the CockJockey’s giant inflatable penis are locked in a viscous staring contest, with the CockJockey himself wearing a tiny referee’s uniform and watching them both very closely. Careful folks, you don’t want to get involved in this, it’s been going on for days. Well, it seems the only Sooner not here is-- The locker room door busts open! In walks … Dave: Sheri! Saij: Shezzza! Ruj: Whatcha got there Sher? Sheri Dedmon enters the room with something cradled in her arms. Sheri: Look what I found sniffing around in the hallway. She holds out her arms to reveal a cute gray cat with a small reddish spot under it’s eye. Everyone (who‘s awake and vocal): Awwwwwww! Saij: Let me hold it. Saij gets off the couch and approaches Sheri, taking the cat in his hands. Sheri: It’s a ‘she’ stupid. Saij: Oh who cares, I’m just gonna throw ‘her’ on Fatty anyway! And with that Saij spins and tosses the cat down the couch and square onto Badger’s stomach. He doesn’t wake up though, just stirs a bit as the cat starts purring and rubbing against his chin. Sheri: Leave that poor innocent cat alone! Fatbadger: zzzzzzzzzzzzz … you need to shave grandpa … zzz … and no more kisses on the mouth dude … zzzzzz Everyone exchanged horrified glances. Even Cardboard Chase has a disturbed look without breaking his gaze. Badger finally stirs himself awake. He stretches and yawns and then flinches as he sees the cat on his chest. He wipes some drool of the cheek that has the imprint of ‘regdabtaf’ on it. Fatbadger: Oh thank god! I thought the CockJockey was trying to cuddle with me again. The CockJockey looks up from refereeing the staring contest with a frown and flips Badger off. This of course gives Cardboard Chase a chance to blink … somehow, and cheat, but there’s nothing the big bad dong-a-long of doom can do about it … for now. Badger scratches the cats head and looks under it‘s chin. Fatbadger: Hey, the little dude has a name tag. Sheri: It’s a girl damnit! Fatbadger: Says here that his name is … Woldfenden? Rujasu: Hey, isn’t that the same name as your opponent in the GTT? Fatbadger: Uh, I guess so. Kind of a dumb name for a cat though. Saij: Indeed. Ridiculous name. Rujasu: Without a doubt. The three exchange glances. Saij: Kind of an obvious joke though, wouldn’t you say? Badger looks confused. Fatbadger: Uh, not obvious enough, cuz like, I don’t get it. Saij: Well, you see -- Fatty’s face lights up Fatbadger: OOOOoohhh! I get it. Cuz they’re both pussies! Everyone gasps. Everyone: GASPS!!! Dave: Not cool Fatty. Sheri: I should kidney punch you for twenty seven minutes. Fatbadger: What? WHAT? Saij: Dude, Wolfenden is Katrina Wolfenden … a chick. Fatbadger: She’s a she?!! Sheri: Yes she is. And you never call a woman the ‘p’ word. Fatbadger: I swear dudes, I didn’t know. Sheri kicks Badger in the shin. Fatbadger: OOWWW!! Sheri: You don’t call us ‘dudes’ either. That’s strike two for you. Fatbadger: That’s just the way I talk and stuff du--, er, Sheri. Dave: He’s only at strike two? I’m at strike eight seven! Sheri: And that’s just since I’ve been back since Stable Wars. You know why you’re up there. Dave hangs his head slightly and kicks the ground. Dave: I guess so. Fatbadger: But … but … I’ve never wrestled a woman … that I remember. I think I might need some practice. Badger looks over at Sheri through the corner of his eyes, slowly turning his head toward her and sticking out his bottom lip, giving Sheri the puppy dog look. Dave and Saij raise their eyebrows and look over at Sheri too. Sheri: Oh no. Nooooo NO! Dave: C’mon babe, you heard him say he needs the practice. Saij: Yeah Sher, he needs help. Sheri: For what?! Isn’t he just gonna lose? Fatbadger: Oh yeah, I still plan on losing … SPECTACULARLY!!!… Badger poses for a few seconds. Fatbadger … I, like, just need to know how to um, reverse some holds and stuff into boobies grabs. Saij: BOOBIES!! Sheri: Why you son of a--! Dave: Hold on there babe. He might be onto something there. I mean, what could be more spectacular than seeing that. Sheri: Well then you throw on your old pair of panties and your bra and show him. Now that’s what I’d consider spectacular. Dave: Oh come on! That match was almost three years ago. The bra and panties are long gone. Sheri: Oh really … then what was that in your locker. She starts making her way toward the lockers on the wall. Dave turns white as a sheet and does the only thing he can do: tackle Sheri behind the couch and wrestle her to the ground. Saij sees that Dave has his hands full … literally, gets grin on his face and runs over to lend a hand (or two). Badger’s eyes go wide and slowly look back and forth before he runs over and jumps in the pile too. Ruj looks around, shrugs, and decides he better join in with the rest of the team and dives in too. The camera pans up and we hear a lot of girlish giggling, though it’s not all coming from Sheri. After a few seconds, we hear the giggling suddenly stop. Sheri: HEY GODDAMNIT! This is then followed by four loud slaps. The camera pans back to show each of the male Sooners rubbing their right cheeks, and Sheri rubbing her left hand. Sheri: Now, I didn’t give anyone permission to grab my ass. Who the hell was it?! Saij immediately points to Badger without hesitation. Dave sees this and quickly follows suit. Ruj sees this and points to Fatty as well. Fatty, on the other hand, has somehow lost track of what’s going on and has gotten a bag of Funyuns in his hands, loudly munching away. He looks around and sees everyone pointing at him and smiles. He drops the bag and does the RVD thumbs thing he loves to do. Fatbadger: FAT - BAD - GER!!! Badger, like everyone else, wonders why he did that, so he looks confused and puts his arms down. Fatbadger: What? Saij: Well, you just admitted to grabbing Sheri’s ass. Fatbadger: I did? Saij: Yeah, thanks. Fatbadger: But I didn’t -- Sheri: And that’s strike three! Sheri advances toward Badger, who falls to the ground and curls into the fetal postion as the sight of the imposing woman … probably a preview of his first round match of the GTT. Fatbadger: Wait, WAIT! I … AM … INNOCENT!!! Dave stops Sheri. Dave: Hold on there Sheri, Badger has claimed his innocence. Sheri: So? Saij: Well you see, now he’ll have to prove it before you can administer your beating. Sheri: How is he supposed to prove it? Rujasu: We’re going to Sooner’s Court!! Sheri: Oh good lord. Can’t anything be simple here. Dave: No … get used to it. The camera slowly turns toward the tv in front of the couch and pans all the way up to it. The title “Sooner’s Court” appears full screen as some cheesey judicial music plays. What’s judicial music? Well, if you don’t know, you should probably stop reading now, wait until the morning for one of those crappy ‘judge’ shows to start, and then read this part over quickly as it plays. Or, if you’re tight on time, just read this out loud to some random rhythm: “BUM! Bum - bum - bum --- bum. Bum bum bum --- bum - bum bum bum ------- bum.” JR appears on screen, hat and all, holding a microphone. JR: By god! And welcome to the first edition of “Sooner’s Court!” Good old JR here to get you up to speed on today’s case. The accuser today is one Sheri Dedmon! She has charged the defendant, one Fatbadger, with first degree ass-grabbery! If she wins today, she looks to stomp a mud hole in him like a government mule! We are taken inside the courtroom, which actually looks like a real courtroom. It must be the XUW Courthouse. I think they built one, seeing as they get sued every other storyline - er, I mean, week. Sheri is seated on the left, while Badger is being lead to his seat in full shackles on the right. JR: By god! The attorney for the prosecution is Mr. David Kipfer, and the attorney for the defense is Mr. R. U. Jasu. Dave and Ruj enter the courtroom at the same time, dressed in suits and carrying briefcases, empty no doubt. They both take their places next to their respective clients … once they realize they’re at the wrong tables and switch places. JR: By god! And now we await the arrival of the honorable judge! The camera pans around to the judge’s bench, showing Cardboard Cutout Chase Wilson sitting behind a shorthand typewriter … hey, we had to get him in here somehow. Saij comes out from a back room, wearing a white t-shirt with “bailiff” written across it. Saij: This is crap! Dave: C’mon Saij we already went through this. Saij: Yeah, well, why can’t I be a lawyer? Dave: Because me and Ruj are the serious ones. Saij: Ruj is serious? Dave: Compared to you? Saij: Fine. Then why couldn’t I be the judge. I mean, at this current moment in time, I am the PCT Global Champion. Saij gives a thumbs up and winks into the camera before posing. Dave: You know why you’re not the judge. Saij thinks for a moment before his face lights up. Saij: Oh yeah! This is gonna be hilarious! Saij clears his throat and stands at attention for some reason. Saij: HEAR YE! HEAR YE! Dave just shakes his head. Saij: All rise for the honorable … COCKJOCKEY!! The CockJockey rides out on his inflatable, flail-able, phonic phallace, dressed in a little judge’s robe. He does a lap around the courtroom and approaches the bench, disappearing behind it. We can only see the top of the inflatable penis. After a few seconds, the penis bends toward Saij and gives him a slight bludgeon to the side of the head region. Saij: Ow! What? Oh, yeah. Saij reaches down and grabs a booster seat and places it behind the bench. Finally we can see the CockJockey’s face once again as he steps up. He grimaces at the people in the courtroom and begins to bludgeon the bench frantically. Saij: ORDER! ORDER IN THE COURT!! Everyone exchanges glances. Dave: Uh, Saij. We weren’t saying anything. Judge CJ bludgeons some more. Saij: I SAID ORDER!! The CockJockey smiles and nods to Saij. Saij: You may begin with your opening statement. Dave stands and shuffles some papers. Dave: Your honor, the prosecution plans to prove, without a shadow of a doubt, that Fatbadger did indeed-- Some fierce bludgeoning from the judge. Saij: BOOOORING! GET TO THE INTERROGATION--er, I mean, witness. Dave just looks at Sheri and shrugs. Dave: We call Fatbadger. Badger rises and slowly makes his way to the witness stand, cuz like, he’s still covered in shackles. He takes his seat and Dave approaches him. Dave: Now Badger-- Fatbadger: I’M INNOCENT DUDE!! FATBADGER EQUALS INNOCENCE!! Bludgeoning. Saij: ORDER! Dave: Fatty, when you look at Sheri over there, what do you see? Fatbadger: BOOBIES!! Dave: The prosecution rests! Dave smiles to Sheri and retakes his seat. Ruj rustles through some papers of his own, takes a breath, and approaches the witness stand. He clears his throat. Rujasu: Over the years Fatbadger, you’ve racked up quite a list of accomplishments. Fatbadger: Uh, I have dude? Rujasu: Well sure. I mean, you’re the master of losing spectacularly for one. You were voted XUW’s most lovable jobber last year. Member of the Hurristoners. Member of the Boomer Sooners. Member of XUW’s PTC Survival Team. XUW’s first and only Grand Slam Champion. Current IC champion, and oh, you and me won Stable Wars this year with a couple other guys. Saij and Dave each cock and eyebrow and exchange annoyed looks. The CockJockey wails on the bench with his undomesticated dong of doom. Saij: Where is this going Ruj? Rujasu: You’ll see. Saij: Well, hurry the fark up then! Rujasu: Badger, of all those accomplishments, I wonder, what are you most proud of? Badger looks around and thinks, but it doesn’t take long. Fatbadger: That’s easy dude. It was my win over Ric Austin. Rujasu: Ahhh yes, so your World Title victory then? Fatbadger: I was World Champ? Wait, no. I mean, that I got revenge dude. Ric throwing Saij through that stage, it was like, one of the worst things ever. Like Pete breaking Chase’s ankle, or Sakie getting his leg broken. I never really got revenge for those things or those guys … or anything really, ever. But what Austin did, it pushed me over the limit and stuff. I’d been bullied my whole life dude, but like, my friends getting bullied, that was it. It was enough. For the first time in my life, I got revenge … and it was for my friend … and I’d do it again, for anyone in this room. Pinning Ric for Saij, that is like, my proudest moment. Everyone is shown slightly tearing up. Fatbadger: Ya know, at least, unless one of them, like, goes and joins some crappy group like the Ultra Violents … again. Dave: Goddamn it, will anyone ever drop that. Rujasu: Thank you Fatty. You see your honor, this is the point. Fatbadger is innocent of the crime he is accused of, because, well, he is simply too innocent. He is innocent by his innocence. He isn’t motivated by money or power or glory. He’s just motivated by friendships. Badger is unable to have committed this crime, because you see, he is … well, again, innocent. Everyone continues drying their eyes. Sheri and Dave whisper for a couple seconds. Dave stands. Dave: Your honor … due to this testimony, the prosecution is willing to … drop the charges. The CockJockey bangs the bench. Uh, and by that I mean in the not sexual way. Well, the least of the sexual ways. There’s still a giant penis involved of course. Saij: Fatbadger is not guilty! Everyone celebrates, except that is, for Fatbadger, who had is face buried in a bag of Funyuns that he pulled from somewhere. He quickly pulls his head from the bag and sees Dave and Sheri cheering and panics. Fatbadger: Oh no dudes! I’m too young to die! Badger gets down on his knees and begs to the CockJockey. Fatbadger: I throw myself at the mercy of like, the dude with the huge dong! Dave: Who, me? Sheri: He didn’t say … talent silly. Fatbadger: No, I’m talking about the huge rubber dong. Sheri: You’ve been going through my drawers again?! Everyone stares silently at Sheri. Sheri: What? Oh--damnit! Dave stands up and points accusingly at her. Dave: You told me that was a candle holder! Sheri: You never were that bright hon. Not that it was your mind I liked. Saij and Fatty and Ruj start laughing as the CockJockey bangs his gavel (large air-filled pleasure stick) to retain order in the Sooner Court ... or just to end it. Saij: Court is out of session! Everyone starts cleaning up. Sheri looks around. Sheri: Wait a minute. So who the hell grabbed my ass!?? Everyone exchanges scared shitless glances before fleeing, leaving Sheri by herself. You can sing the “Bum-bum-bum” stuff again if you wish, to signify the end of the show. Go ahead and scroll back up if you’ve forgotten the lyrics. The ending below can wait. Fade.
