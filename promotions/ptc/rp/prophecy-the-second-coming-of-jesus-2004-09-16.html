A wind-up toy stumbling along with an uneven gait. I am the good little girl that I should be. I am sweetness, patience, and soothing. Deep inside where the pretty pills don't go is sharp resentment. "...Lord Jesus, I open my heart to You and confess my many sins. Thank You for dying for my sins and resurrecting to be my life. I receive You to be my Lord and life. Lord Jesus, now You are my greatest treasure." Itagaki swallowed those spirit empowering words as a drop of crimson life giving liquid gold sucked from his dilated pupil back into the horrid wounds of Susej. Thunder streaks drained from pregnant dark clouds which absorbed rain drops reversing the cycle of water. Flashes of heaven's rage played backwards behind the unsanctified ceremony through the huge anti-Christ painted glass that decorated the background in the middle of an ancient wall. Like rewinding a never-ending story, Itagaki stood back as tears of melancholy flowed back into his pale white eyes fixated on God's only begotten son impaled to cross whose hideous whip wounds began rejuvenate. "Itagaki it's!" As if he were suffering from dyslexia, those words jumped back in the mouth of Sumoto Aisukurimu as his pointed finger came back to greet his side. Eccentric onlookers filling the pews watch Itagaki begin to walk in reverse up that long aisle as their muttering along with an organ setting the mood sounded like an AC/DC record spinning backwards. Feeling a subsiding transfiguration seed starting a melodious dance with time, Itagaki felt as if some warm hands took him to another land, and ambiance lost in space. The council composed of Itagaki's backstabbing brother Sumoto and his loyal followers stood with their shocked expressions receding while guards slowly stepped down. God pulled back his whip of lightening that unmercifully punished the skies and sent transcending vibrations that gave animation to the still. Searching for our shelter, we enter immortal tombs. Standing at the threshold of the church with the ground now raining on the skies behind him, the darkness that surrounded Itagaki almost in contentment slowly drew away. Tears of scarlet ran up his dead face as he let go of the silver cross hanging on his chest that he carried not for belief, but for memories of better times. Times the sun didn't hurt his eyes, times he still felt remorse. Innocent blood spilled every night. Swathed in the evening's ebony aura. Triumphing over the abominations that were his masters, Itagaki experienced freedom for the first time. It overwhelmed him. Everything was suddenly so detailed; he finally woke up from a life that was nothing more than an incubus. Then everyone exhaled a surprised gasp at the sight of Itagaki standing before them. Suddenly Akuma III yelled out something crazy like, "Now back turn! Run!" Not in concern for Itagaki as a person, but in an attempt to protect his property. Itagaki then pulling the grand door he gave his last ounce of strength to open in madness at which he thought he were pushing a boulder of gigantic proportions, shut the door before him as the crucified Susej became the point of concentration. Almost as if given a magical elixir, the spirit of Susej revived and took back his last breath of life straying away from the blinding array of virtuous luminescence he was returning home to. Woven leather ropes held by executioners cracked as they took away the slashes that brutally mutilated Susej's flesh. Then Sumoto switches his thumb up as the executioners pull the nails from Susej's hand healing the punctures he endured. "Aisukurimu Sumoto of name the in sacrificed be shall you, Counsel The threatening for." Said along with the reverse theme of things, Sumoto Aisukurimu read Susej his punishment. Sumoto condemned Susej to death after taking offense to Susej's words of God. "Me Threatening?" Come will day their, child my no." "Him not, me take! No!" Almost sounding like a pre-historic cave man, Akuma III actually felt an uncanny care for Susej as he pleaded for his release. Jumping back, the public were just about to vote for whom they wish to be sacrificed as Sumoto presented them with their choices. It was a classic bible story that Sumoto heinously set up as he stood on-top of the world as king. Un-educating the masses with how Susej and Akuma came to be in this current predicament, the malignant design of evil himself, Sumoto, stole back the thoughts of malice he tainted their minds with as he begun the ceremony. Once again, I've lost my identity, I am so tired here, in this world of gray. Stranded, I am just like the other lost souls, no death, all life, as we ponder a way out. In this world of so many shades of gray, I lay at the foot of a precipice, my mind fights the life that I despise, no hunger, no flight, just hatred in my eyes. No angels pity, all appalled by what I've done, no gods look for me, turned backs and violence, my world looks so beautiful now, the trifle remains of nature are my way out.
