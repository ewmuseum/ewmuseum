TTD - Illuminati -v- The Corporation

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up with the camera on Peter Thomas, Canadian Perfect’s agent. Peter is dressed in an expensive business suit and has a briefcase in one hand, his cell phone in the other. Peter then stops and steps off to the side, away from the human traffic walking through the building. He dials a number and begins to speak) (PETER) Hey Canadian Perfect, how’s the next PTC Tag Team Champion going... Hey I was meant to pick you up at the airport but I can’t now, I am stuck in a meeting… What do you mean you are not on that flight… You are where… Why are you there… La Envidia Mata is not there, he is in Philly, I saw him doing a promotional spot earlier… What do you mean you don’t care… Come on Canadian Perfect just leave it until you finish your match… You don’t need to do this, just keep it professional. There is no need to get that personal! Canadian Perfect! Are you there Canadian Perfect? Speak to me Canadian Perfect! Oh f**k! (Peter hangs up his phone and does not look entirely happy. The camera then cuts to Canadian Perfect. Canadian Perfect is inside a car but is not driving. He is just sitting there looking outside the car. The camera turns around and reveals Canadian Perfect’s car is stopped in a driveway, and Canadian Perfect is staring at a house. The house has one of the lights on, revealing someone is inside) (Canadian Perfect) I did not want it to get this far Illuminati… If I had it my way, you would have realized that you are no match for us and just given me the win and shake our hands… But you couldn’t do that could you Illuminati… And to make it worse you had to constantly run your mouth about this and that trying to make big men of yourselves. I am a kind and gentle man really Illuminati… But you have just annoyed me and annoyed me some more. Your propaganda makes me sick and everytime you open that hole you call a mouth, it just makes me more determined to shut it. (Canadian Perfect rubs his hand over his face) (Canadian Perfect) This was just about titles Illuminati. Our match was just meant to be about the PTC Tag Team Titles. It was meant to be a great spectacle of some of the greatest talents the PTC has to offer, and fought in the spirit of competition. But you changed that Illuminati. Your lies and propaganda has made it come to this Illuminati… Do you think I intended it to come to this? I didn’t Illuminati… I just wanted a nice and easy week, before I embark on my PTC Tag Title run. So why Illuminati, why? Why did you have to change it all? Do you like making things personal? Where you one of those kids at the zoo who threw rocks at the sleeping Crocodiles trying to wake them up? Because that has what you have been doing. Illuminati the savage, animalistic beast inside my soul has been sleeping. I have been cruising through the 4WW just on natural talent alone, and I would have won the PTC Tag Team Title in this tournament on natural talent alone… But I guess you just like punishment. But now your lies and self promotion have been like little pebbles thrown at me… It doesn’t cause me pain, it just pisses me off! You are annoying little kids Illuminati and it’s about time you got the ass kicking you deserved! But before that I think I might just make things go to another level! (Canadian Perfect turns to the camera with an evil smirk and then leaves the car. The camera quickly follows as Canadian Perfect walks up to the front door and knocks on the door. Movement can be heard, but it is a while before the door opens. Canadian Perfect then knocks on the door again and steps to the side, out of the view of the person inside when they open the door. The porch light comes on and the door opens. A woman is at the door, and as she opens the door the woman’s identity becomes known as The Yure’s girlfriend, Kelly. Kelly looks at the camera with confusion. Kelly sees the PTC logo on the shirt and becomes a bit more relaxed) (KELLY) Oh hi guys. The Yure said you may be around to drop off some tickets for the PTC Tag Team Title Tournament matches this week. (Canadian Perfect then steps out of the darkness. The color drains from Kelly’s face as she realises who it is) (Canadian Perfect) Where is he Kelly? (Kelly backs up a bit) (KELLY) He isn’t here… He left for Philadelphia this morning! (Canadian Perfect) Come on Kelly, I know your lying to protect him! Where is the maggot? (KELLY) He isn’t here! (Canadian Perfect grabs Kelly by the arm) (Canadian Perfect) Don’t make me do it Kelly! I am asking you one last time, where is The Yure? (KELLY) Honestly he left for Philadelphia this morning! (Canadian Perfect tightens his grip, causing Kelly to fall to her knees, as Canadian Perfect is hurting her) (Canadian Perfect) Well then Kelly… Aren’t you going to invite me in? (KELLY) Please go away… The Yure isn’t here! (Canadian Perfect) What was that Kelly? You would love to give me a tour… Why I would love one thank you! (Canadian Perfect barges in. He still has a tight grip on Kelly’s arm and drags her from the door. The camera follows Canadian Perfect as he makes his way to the main room. He pushes Kelly into the couch, as he looks around) (Canadian Perfect) Man what a dump! Don’t you ever clean around here woman? (Canadian Perfect looks around the room. There is a box on the table with stuff inside) (Canadian Perfect) What is this junk? (Canadian Perfect goes through it and then a very evil smile comes across his face) (Canadian Perfect) Awwww it’s all the stuff The Yure was looking at during one of his promos. Look it is his yearbook from grade one… The newspaper article when he won his first World Title in that crappy fed no one has ever heard about… Awww and look, a picture of his Daddy and his Uncle! (Canadian Perfect takes a look at the photo, and then shows it to Kelly) (Canadian Perfect) Who is this man next to The Yure’s Dad? (Kelly takes a look. She is still visibly shaking and her voice is very shaky) (KELLY) That isn’t a man, it is The Yure’s mother. And leave it alone! (Canadian Perfect laughs) (Canadian Perfect) God no wonder The Yure is one ugly mother. His Mom had a bigger mustache than his Dad did! (Canadian Perfect scrunches up the photos and throws them away. He then tosses the yearbook aside. He then looks at the newspaper article. He then gets right up close to Kelly. Kelly has tears in her eyes, as she is backed up against the wall) (Canadian Perfect) I bet The Yure really treasures this newspaper article. I mean it was his first major accomplishment and was the start for everything going good for him. It was also the beginning of the end for him! Why? Because this title set him on a path to his eventual death… Yes that’s right Kelly, your boy is coming to the end of his path and the path stops right when he steps into the ring with me. (Canadian Perfect is right up in Kelly’s face) (Canadian Perfect) I am so hoping you have ordered your ticket Kelly. I want you to sit here and watch your boy die at the hands of me. The most amusing thing is that The Yure does not know what he has truly gotten himself in to. I am a ruthless killer Kelly and The Yure does not realise that. You don’t go around taunting a ruthless killer that you know you will have to confront in a couple of days! So I am going to make him realize his fatal mistake. But I just wanted to pay you a visit to tell you that I am not sorry for what I will do to The Yure. The Yure brought this upon himself. The Yure laughed at death in the face and knows I will just destroy him for it! The Yure’s mouth has gotten him in trouble before but he has managed to squirm his way through… Well maybe his Momma or Daddy should have taught him that he should shut his stupid mouth, because it is going to get him killed one day… And that day is coming very soon! So what has that got to do with you Kelly? Well I know you will be grieving and distraught after that day as you would have lost your man! Well I am here to make you realize I can make The Yure live inside of you forever! How? By eating this! (Canadian Perfect shoves the newspaper article into Kelly’s face) (Canadian Perfect) EAT IT KELLY! EAT IT! (Kelly refuses. Canadian Perfect laughs a bit and then rears back and threatens to slap Kelly in the face. Kelly looks terrified, but cannot bring herself to eat the newspaper article which is so precious to her boyfriend) (Canadian Perfect) Come on Kelly… Don’t make me do it! (Kelly just can’t bring herself to do it and then Canadian Perfect slaps her in the face with the back of his hand, before shoving the article in her mouth, physically forcing Kelly to eat the article. Canadian Perfect then laughs as Kelly coughs and splutters after swallowing the article against her will) (Canadian Perfect) Look at you. You are pathetic! Why are you with such a loser like The Yure? Out of all the men around the world, you chose to be with a second rate man… But I suppose that was good for you… You are nothing special yourself! Do you know what I see when I look at you? I see a B-grade sleeper! You are disgusting and you make me sick! Pick yourself up woman and stop being so pathetic! (Kelly slowly rises only to have Canadian Perfect shove her back down) (Canadian Perfect) This personal enough for you Yure? Is this what you wanted? Remember Yure you brought this upon yourself with your lies and propaganda! You see Yure you are not in my league… Inside and outside of the ring I am the better man! I will prove that I am the better wrestler and the better superstar! You said you doubted yourself for the first time in your career! Why? Because you deep down inside you know that you cannot compete with me! It is eating away at you knowing you are powerless to stop me from ripping and humiliating you in that ring! The people are saying you are nothing but a transitional wrestler because it is the truth! They can see the gross mismatch that results from you going up against me! They look at you and see nothing, and look at me and see everything a champion should be! And if you think you will be able to get your buddies to bundle you out of this one, think again! The Corporation is going to be there for one reason and one reason only… To stop your friends from screwing me out! Everyone knows you cannot beat me one-on-one Yure… And it is no surprise that you are starting to think it yourself! By the time the match rolls around, you are going to be beaten before you even step into the ring… I am going to stand eye-to-eye, nose-to-nose with you in the middle of the ring and I know what I am going to see! FEAR! You won’t be able to hide it Yure, because I know you fear me, I can see it in your eyes! (Canadian Perfect begins to laugh but suddenly stops when he looks down and sees Kelly trying to get on the phone and call the police. Canadian Perfect yanks the phone from her and pulls the phone cord out. Canadian Perfect then grabs Kelly by the shirt and yanks her up) (Canadian Perfect) Kelly I want you to do one special favor for me… No Kelly not that kind of favor! I know that is what you like to do, but I wouldn’t let “my Perfection” go anywhere near your skank ass! No I want you to pass on a message to your dear Yure, and make sure he gets it before Sunday! (Canadian Perfect then grabs Kelly by the back of the head and kisses her on the lips before throwing her to the ground. Canadian Perfect then wipes his mouth and spits in Kelly’s direction) (Canadian Perfect) Kiss of Death Kelly… Make sure The Yure gets it! Illuminati, don't think I forgot about you two because this little exhibition could happen to the both of you if you decide to make this persona. So Illuminati, for your own sake just keep your mouths closed to prevent getting the ass kicking of a lifetime that The Corporation will give you in the Tag Team Title Tournament. You will see that I am the greatest technical wrestler in history, I am Wrestling and above all I am absolutely Perfect. (Canadian Perfect laughs before walking out of the house as the screen fades to black)
