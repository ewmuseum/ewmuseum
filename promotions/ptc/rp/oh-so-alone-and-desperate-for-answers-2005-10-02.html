Oh so alone and desperate for answers

2 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-10-02 While he was conversing in the mines with the hermit dwarf who had accompanied him on his failed quest to rescue Cereal Killer (before suffering a gruesome and ever-so-theatrical death at the hands of the vicious rottweiler guard dogs who prowl the backyard of the Domicile of Darkness), Scotty had mentioned to his ill-fated new friend his match at Super Series, and provided a brief description of his four opponents –not having known enough about any of them and being too dimwitted even if he had of to provide a thorough and in-depth one. The dwarf, believing himself an authority on all and sundry, hadn’t hesitated to lend his advice to the impressionable young XUW wrestler pertaining each opponent as well as the match in general, and now, as Scotty sat at the table in his apartment somewhere in Ohio, having ran all the way back there as Sirius Smythe had suggested he do, his thoughts are fixed firmly on Super Series and the dwarfs words of counsel echo through his mind… “Any kind of fight involving eight or more people will, inevitably, descend into complete chaos and madness. This can either be a help or a hindrance depending of course on your ability to think laterally, and take swift advantage of the opportunities presented which -in such a cluttered and confused environment- will no doubt come in abundance. When the fight is split with four allied men pitted against another group of four, it is vital that you know your friends strengths and weaknesses, but because you havnt really told me anything about your teammates, I can only be of limited assistance to you in that regard; all I can do is advise you in the strongest possible terms to study their behavior meticulously, and try to get to know them in a genuine and heartfelt sense. For I know as well as anyone, through my days as an emerging young basketball player, that no experience is as emancipating or thrilling as being a part of a team- nothing compares with the unspoken but ever-present feeling of masculine camaraderie and almost tangible sense of mutual support that it produces. This sense of solidarity and unity is paramount to success.” The apartment is a drear and empty shell of a place with white painted walls and a distinct lack of furniture or household appliances save for a small single bed in the corner and a gas cooker and teakettle sitting on an ironing board beside the door. It is difficult to fathom why Scotty was even chosen to be part of the four-man XUW team competing in the 8-man elimination match at Super Series against PRIME. He is not one of the companies top four wrestlers by a long shot and most likely never will be: utterly spineless and completely lacking in strength, skill, in-ring know-how and pretty much anything that defines a wrestler. Nearly every knowledgeable wrestling fan and respected commentator who has ever seen him in action before has at the same time expressed their complete befuddlement as to why he ever even became a wrestler in the first place, given his cowardice and complete lack of talent, while others have gone one further and insisted that he ISNT a wrestler, or any kind of athlete at all for that matter; that while he may be contracted to a professional wrestling league and paid to perform in a wrestling ring on a bi-weekly to monthly basis, what he does cannot rightly and truly be called wrestling as his entire in-ring strategy entails running away and hiding from opponents until they make a mistake, and he has yet to demonstrate any semblance of a clue as to how to really hurt anybody; his entire “moveset” being more or less completely made up of defensive, technically illegal moves such as eye-gouges and kicks to the crotch, which he only ever uses in moments where escape is absolutely impossible. On the other hand, with his heavily defensive routine of waiting for an opponent to make a fatal error and then capitalizing on it accordingly, Scotty has already attained enviable success in a career spanding just over two months, having captured the XUW Intercontinental title in a three-way match –after his opponents ran into each other and crashed heads together. He is a scavenger; a cowardly imbecile who, despite his yellow-belliedness and mind-boggling idiocy, is frustratingly able to almost instinctively spot opportunities before they even emerge and swarm in on them like a vulture, a skill which –as the dwarf suggested- may turn out to be more than useful in the chaotic all-in brawl that the 8-man elimination tag team match at Super Series could prove to be. However, in a match where team-work is imperative, Scotty’s lack of competitive spirit and chicken-hearted in-ring style could just as well prove to be a liability. There is no doubt in Scotty’s mind that a team comprised of four top wrestlers from an elite wrestling league would be ultra-super organized and have a definite gameplan in their heads, at the very least he assumes they would have each others backs and be willing to lend each other support when necessary, whereas the odds of him risking his own physical safety at any stage during the match in order to protect his teammates range from slim to nonexistant, which might make it almost impossible for the XUW team to build that same sense of solidarity and unity which –as the dwarf also said-is paramount to success… “You must be single-mindedly committed to the success of the team; your every conscious act must revolve around increasing the teams prospects of victory. Even though you are fighting a woman, do not relent! In fact try to take the aim of hitting her at least six times harder than you would a man, so as to eliminate any risk of subconsciously going easy on her! I have been in barroom fights with women, and stomped them in a brutally hideous fashion for so much as looking at me in a slightly displeasing manner. At my 21st birthday party my cousin made a somewhat embarrassing speech about me and I cracked her in the face with a bar-stool until she was crying, with a river of blood gushing down from out of her fat, stupid nose! Now, I’m fully aware of the fact that there are people out there who tend to take the dogmatic view that inflicting brutal, merciless beatings on women is always morally reprehensible, but just remember that those people are sexists by definition, because if you wouldn’t brutally beat a woman where you would normally brutally beat a man in her stead, then you obviously think she’s pathetic enough to be spared, and yeah, that makes you a sexist…” He ponders this advice while at the same time nibbling methodically on a savory tuna dumpling, unsure of how to put it to any real, practical use (not the savory tuna dumpling, the advice). For as a pants-pissing girlie-man, normally too afraid even to enter into fights with drunken old bridge club ladies and schoolchildren when provoked by the aforementioned, Scotty would need to overcome his fear and aversion towards physical confrontation in general first before even beginning to consider the moral dilemma of hitting a woman. And he is not about to suddenly recover from a lifelong fear of fighting within the course of the next few days so. The best strategy, he figures, would be to run away from her throughout the entire match and just hope she doesn’t catch him, and then, if she falls over or something, kick her. He quickly settles with this as the general plan of attack when dealing with Aimz, opting to disregard the dwarfs advice in this particular case… “As for this Kyle Lamen person, with a fast, high-risk wrestling style and a blue-haired, punk-rocker image, I would suggest that you keep him out of the match as much as possible during the early stages. If he is indeed a cruiserweight of the "Hyper-Kinetic" variety as his personal webpage would have us believe, then he will no doubt be a dangerous adversary in the earlier, more frantic and chaotic stages of the match, but as more and more people get eliminated the pace of the match will inevitably start to slow, and this will work against him in addition to having tired himself out early with a wrestling style that demands great energy expenditure. Besides, wrestlers like that tend to get frustrated when forced to work a slow kind of match for too long, and then they take greater risks without thinking as much about whether the potential payoff is great enough to be worth the possible consequences. So keep him out of the action as much as possible so as to minimize his impact early on, and then in the latter stages as the pace drops he will be almost a liability to them. Don’t be afraid of his flashy moveset, and make sure you don’t go all soft on him because you’ve confused his flamboyancy for actual in-ring presence or uniqueness; his spiked blue hair may seem like a defining mark of individuality to the untrained eye, but the reality of the situation is this; the punk movement has been reduced to an otiose, boring, bloated parody of itself. Besides which, I have always been a keen advocate of the position that people who willingly and knowingly have blue hair should be lynched from their communities gradually and purposefully until extinguished from the earth entirely, for the betterment of the species and the stimulation of the younger, more normal townsfolk, who -by engaging in physical activity in the form of tying nooses as well as chasing and dragging different-looking people through the streets- would thus be physically and mentally stimulated with an improved sense of community and togetherness and therefore –you would think- less vulnerable to the Great Moral Vices that so many of them are plagued with today such as drugs, teen-pregnancy and gang-related violence. I would therefore advise you to try to eliminate his teammates first, and then do everything in your power not only to beat him, but also to kill him, and encourage your teammates to do likewise…” Having finished his dumpling now, Scotty ways up the dwarfs suggestion carefully in his mind, slightly confused as to why a hideously disfigured, two-feet-six inches high gentlemen would be so enthusiastic about townsfolk “chasing and dragging different-looking people through the streets”. He would still run the dwarfs suggestion by his teammates, he decides, but feeling less than confident in his own ability to bring himself to directly and deliberately cause the death of another person, he quietly hopes that they will decline the suggestion and opt for the more traditional approach of trying to outwrestle and pin Lamen instead… “And as for Dave Gibson, well, it sounds as though he’s almost the complete opposite of Lamen; a methodical technician with a penchant for cheating. He might not be devastating right from the word “go”, but he will slowly and gradually wear you down if you give him enough time. So don’t. When dealing with him, employ the exact opposite strategy to what you would with Lamen. Allocate all your of resources to eliminating him right from the start. Keep him in the ring as much as possible in the very early stages of the match and take him out as quickly as you can.” This was all fair enough; the logic behind the strategy made sense. But to Scotty it had all sounded too simple, too clear-cut, as if there just had to be some kind of catch. He didn’t like it when the dwarf had initially suggested it to him and now, thinking about it in retrospect whilst cleaning dumpling crumbs off his apartment floor with a brush and shovel, he’s still not happy with it. Not at all, no, he wouldn’t be trying that, nor would he even run it by his teammates to see what they thought of the idea… “As for Alex Ross, who’s website hypes him as being “as American as apple pie” despite apple pie actually predating the discovery and settlement of America and in fact going all the way back to the times of Edward III and possibly even earlier, well, I don’t know, I suppose there really isn’t all that much to be said. Just try to beat him, I guess, and all will be well…” Scotty is happy enough with the last piece of advice. He closes his eyes and says a silent prayer for his devoured friend, then realizes he has yet to communicate with any of his Super Series teammates in any way whatsoever. Obviously he now knows that getting a meeting with Cereal Killer would not be possible, having of course gone to great lengths to do so and failed abysmally. He doesn’t really want to talk to woof woof, who reminds him too much of a whole slew of stronger, brainier kids who used to beat him up and steal his lunch money way back when he was in kindergarten. And so he pulls out his cellular phone and calls Saij, so as to feel better about himself as if he has made at least SOME kind of contribution to building some team-spirit and solidarity and so-forth… ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “Uh, hullo?” ”Saij! Scotty here! Just thought I’d call you, yanno to talk strategy and try and find out what everyone is doing in the days leading up to the match and all that jazz, you know, to make sure that we’re in sync.” “Oh, yeah” “Yeah” “Yeah. Well, woof woof is kind of gonna do his own thing, one would assume. And you, well, what have you been doing?” “Just brainstorming for strategies. What do you say we try to kill Kyle Lamen?” “Nar. We don’t wanna kill him, just hurt him a lot. But I'm glad you're thinking about the match, that’s good; I’m just here doing the same thing I’ve been doing for the past five months, more or less. So that’s the three of us covered. As for CK, well, I wouldn’t have the foggiest.” “Huh?! You mean to say you honest-for-real cross-your-heart-and-hope-to-die didn’t know he’d been kidnapped!?” “KIDNAPPED?!” “KIDNAPPED?!? WHERE? Oh, I mean, yes! Yes he has been! He was kidnapped by Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy after he won some tournament or other! So now Jack and Sirius are holding him prisoner at the Domicile of Darkness and nobody has heard from him in yonks! I even set out on a big epic journey, t…” “Whaddaya mean Jack’s keepin’ CK locked up in the Domicile!? Doesn’t that farkin’ bastard know we have a match coming up here!?” ““Hey, I tried to reason with Sirius, but he’s downright scary! I mean, he swung his freaking cane at me! Swung it like a madman pumped with steroids while having a serious case of road rage! Not only that, he was angry too! Angry and pissed and ticked off and what have you! He also seemed kinda mad.” “Dude, my five-month-old kid could whip his farkin’ arse. I’m surprised the slightest gust of wind doesn’t knock him over, or crush him, or something. What kinda wuss are you, anyway?” “The kind that runs away from scary old men, that’s what! But anyway, this is a serious problem! We need to get CK outta there, or else we’ll be a man short at Super Series and PRIME will surely kill us all in nasty ways!” “OH, FARKIN’ CRAP! DEAR MOTHER OF GOD, THIS IS THE WORST THING EVER!” “No kidding, we’re in a dire situation here. We finally have the opportunity to claim the title we should have won a year ago at Survival, and we’re short a man! What the hell are we gonna do!?” “Must… throw… away!” “Throw the match away, are you mad? I can’t believe YOU just said that! YOU! You’re Saij, damnit! You never back down from anything! You’ve built your career on defying the odds! Hell, I should know, I’ve seen it first-hand! And now, because we might be going into a four-on-three situation at Super Series, you’re going to just give up? You, sir, have just thrown away any respect I had for you!” “Phew… glad I managed to throw that away. What a stinking pile of crap that was!” “Wh-what!? How dare you, sir! I am insulted! Never in all my life have I been so offended! People have always spoken highly of you, but you’re nothing more than an unruly jackanapes!” ”Oh, please don’t cry. It’ll be over soon!” “Don’t talk to me as if I’m some sort of child! Just because you’re a former World Champion and in the XUW Hall of Fame doesn’t give you the right to talk down to me! And don’t act so casual about things, either! ‘It’ll be over soon,’ honestly! We have a lot of work to do and increasingly less time to do it in! The way you’re treating me, I’m not so sure that I want to continue teaming with you anyway!” “Damnit. I need those Huggies.” “WELL I’M NOT GIVING YOU ANY HUGS, YOU INSENSITIVE CLOD! I’ve had enough of your ornery cantankerousness! Forget it, I’ll figure out a solution to our problems myself, and you’ll be groveling at my feet and thanking me for doing so! Just you wait and see! You’ll rue the day you crossed Scotty Spalzcutoff, mark my words! I BID YOU GOOD DAY, SIR!” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ He presses the hang-up button, throws the phone down angrily onto the table, and then sits quietly for a full half hour, trying desperately to think of a winning strategy for Super Series with one teammate missing in action and another who seems to be completely convinced that the match is a lost cause and that they should give up all hope now. Upon realizing that he cant think of anything, he suddenly breaks down, pulls out a hanky and proceeds to sob loudly into it like a little girl whilst pounding the table frustratedly with his fists, utterly and desperately alone, afraid and confused, with no clue in the world what to do anymore…
