James von Doom (GLOBAL): The Plan August 20, 2007, 10:18 AM The hot Vegas air is already sizzling the sidewalk. It’s only ten in the morning but it’s already almost ninety five degrees outside. Just what James von Doom doesn’t need after the hellish week he’s had. He’s lost two matches, lost his GLOBAL Platinum Championship, lost another girlfriend, and just got back in town to find out that his car got stolen while he was gone so he had to go take a taxi to get a rental car and then go file a police report about the car. Now he’s outside of his house locking up the door. James turns around and notices the thermometer on the wall by the door. “Fuck,” he says as he sees that the temperature is now ninety-six degrees. James then strolls over to the red Ford Taurus he got yesterday from the rental car place and walks over the car. James then hit’s the button on the keys he got and the car doors unlock. “Nice,” James says as he climbs in the car and shuts the door behind him. James then starts the car up and starts to pull out. He then turns the car and starts driving. He drives across town before ending up at his best friend Art’s house. Art finally got out of the hospital the day before so they’re going out to mess around. James starts honking the horn, a bit agitated already. “GET THE HELL OUT HERE!” he yells out the car window after he rolls it down. James then looks over and sees Art coming out the door. He rolls the window back up. Art then comes over to the car and gets inside. Art then turns to James. “What the hell are you honking the car horn for man?” Art asks as he buckles up his seatbelt. “Are you pissed off or something?” James nods his head as he puts the car in drive and starts driving off. “You damn fucking right I am,” he snarls out in an angry tone. Art raises an eyebrow. “What the hell for?” he asks. James smiles and points to an entertainment magazine lying on the floor. “Because of that,” he growls out as they come to a red light. Art picks up the magazine and reads the cover. “Wrestler and Actor James von Doom calls another actor Fag,” he reads aloud before starting laughing. “Who the hell did you tell that to?” Art’s laughing hysterically now, much to the chagrin of James, who is still fuming mad over the whole thing. “Heath Ledger,” he replies. “You mean the Brokeback guy?” Art asks with a smile. James nods his head. “Yeah,” he replies. “Him and that fucker Colin Farrell got into it with me at a red carpet event in Los Angeles yesterday when I was promoting my new movie.” Art is trying to keep from laughing again. “You just can’t go anywhere without causing trouble, huh?” he asks. “You damn right,” he replies. “And those fucking foreigners had it coming.” Art looks amused by this as James continues driving. “How so?” he asks in an intrigued tone. “Well here’s how it happened,” he starts off. “I was walking down the carpet talking to some reporters when some jackass walks into me, right.” “Right,” Art replies. “So then I turn around and see those two fucktards glaring at me,” James says with his eyes burning a hole through the windshield. Art raises an eyebrow. “What’s a fucktard?” he asks in an inquisitive tone. “It’s a word I came up with to make fun of idiots,” he replies. “Oh, okay,” Art replies. “Continue.” James then smiles evilly as the light turns to green and he keeps driving toward where they’re going. “So then I turn around and tell the guy to watch it,” he continues. “Then the fucktard, meaning Ferrell, tells me he doesn’t have to watch a damn thing. Then I tell him to go fuck off and make a movie that people will actually want to watch.” Art replies, “OOOOO!!! BURN!” James smiles over that. “Damn right,” he replies. “Then that prissy boy Ledger tells me to shut up and I tell him ‘Keep out of it, Fag Boy.’” Art’s laughing again now. “How did you come up with that?” he giggles out. “Well, he’s playing Joker in the new Batman movie,” James replies. “There’s a new Batman movie?” Art asks. James nods his head. “Yeah,” he replies. “And Ledger is playing Joker in it. Anyway, I called them that because…” James pauses to think about how to explain it. “Well remember that time we were making fun of Batman Forever and you questioned whether Batman and Robin were gay and I called Robin Fag Boy?” he asks. Art nods his head. “Yeah,” Art replies. “Well that’s how I came up with that,” James says with a smile. “That makes more sense now,” Art says. “Then the fucktard Ledger pushes me and tells me that he’s going to kick my ass,” James continues. “But then Billy Crystal comes over to break it up.” “Billy Crystal was there?!” Art asks in an excited tone. James nods his head. “Yes he was,” James replies. “But let me finish. Then he gets pushed by Farrell and that’s when I lose it. I tell him ‘nobody pushes Billy and gets away with it.’” Art nods his head. “You DAMN RIGHT!” he replies. “Nobody fucks with Billy as long as we’re around!” James nods his head. “Exactly,” James says. “So then I punch Farrell in the face and then that pussy Ledger jumps on my back like I’m Jake Gyllenhal or something. Billy then takes his shoe off and starts hitting Ledger in the head with it. That’s when Tom Hanks and Sting come over with Jay-Z to break it up.” “Jay-Z broke up a fight?” Art asks, a by surprised by this. “He did,” James replies. “And he pushed that dumbass Farrell down like the asshole he is. It’s all over the news, dude. It has been for two days now. I turned Hollywood into GLOBAL.” “You mean your wrestling federation?” Art asks. “Yeah,” James replies. “GLOBAL’s about had it with my bullshit too. First the highway shoot out and now this mess. They’re calling me the Star Beater. Farrell is pressing charges against me, Billy Crystal, and Sting.” “Pussy,” Art replies. “And I counter sued both him and Ledger,” James continues. “Since Ledger sued me too. They also have restraining orders against me.” “Nice,” Art says with a laugh. James smiles, proud of himself. “I’m the biggest bad boy in Hollywood and there ain’t a damn thing that’s going to do to change me,” James replies with a grin. “I tell it like it is and I do what I want. If those rich snobs out there in Hollywood don’t like it, then fuck’em. I’ll fight the next bad actor that gets in my way. Most of their movies suck anyway.” “You’ve got that right,” Art replies with a grin. “And the best part is all the aspiring models, actresses, and…well, basically all the ditzy hoes in LA think I’m a God now,” James replies with a smile. Art looks at James in disbelief. “Are you serious?” he asks in shock. James nods his head. “Oh yeah,” he replies. “Natalie Portman already gave me her number.” Art blinks repeatedly. “Natalie Portman?” he asks. “Yes,” James replies. “And two other actresses who haven’t hit it big yet.” “You date actresses?” Art asks. “Oh yeah,” James replies with a devious grin. “Most all of them are a bunch of ditzy bimbos who have no clue about anything other than their hair.” Art starts laughing. “So you mean Hollywood chicks are just like Vegas chicks?” he asks with a grin. James nods his head. “Damn right they are,” James replies. “Hell, the men in this town are more womanly than some of these women. I swear, the guys are all half queer metrosexuals if you ask me.” “I KNOW!” Art exclaims in an exasperated tone. “They wear nail polish, perfume, have their hair done by stylists, and God knows what else,” James replies with a disgusted look on his face. “Bunch of pansies.” Art doesn’t want to talk about other men, so he thinks up a new line of conversation. “So what’s up with your wrestling?” he asks. James shakes his head, “Well I’m this thing called the Infinite Gauntlet AGAIN,” James says. “And this time I’m against the same pussies I fought the last time.” Art looks confused. “You didn’t win?” he asks. James shakes his head. “Nope,” he replies. “And I tell you why. That fucking NBA referee had something to do with it!” Art looks confused again, but that is the norm with him. “What the hell are you talking about?” he asks James. “I’m telling you, it’s a CONSPIRACY against me,” James replies in an exasperated tone. “All these other guys lost their balls and became women about halfway through the competition. Now, I have to prove once again that I’m the true Infinite Gauntlet Champion. I’m on a mission. They will all fall like leaves on a tree and I will finally win what is rightfully mine…the PTC IG Championship.” “And how do you expect to do that?” Art asks. “Easy,” James replies. “By creating chaos.” Art looks a bit perplexed. “Creating chaos?” he asks as James stops the car at the light. “Yeah,” James replies. “This is my plan. I want these guys so pissed off at me and distracted, there’s no way they can win. They’ll be too distracted and will be trembling in fear of me and what I’m capable of doing.” “What are you talking about?” Art says in a non-serious tone. “You’re a fucking coward. Why would they be distracted and lose to you?” James smiles as they finally pull up to the bar and park. “Watch this,” he says as they get out of the car and lock it up. “What?” Art asks as they go inside. Once inside the bar, which is called the Double Tap, they get their IDs checked and are allowed inside. James immediately goes to the bar and sits down on a stool with Art going and sitting next to him. “Two Coors Lights,” James says to the bartender and then the bartender brings over two bottles of Coors Light. “Seven dollars,” the bartender says to him. James looks at him, not believing the alcohol costs that much. “What the fuck is this?” he asks in an exasperated tone. “Why the hell does it costs so much?” The bartender shrugs his shoulders. “Don’t ask me,” he replies. “I’m not in management.” James, of course, ignores him. “This is such bullshit!” James exclaims. “They FUCK YOU with the alcohol prices! First they screw you and then they fuck you! They’re ripping us off on purpose! It’s all a ploy! And they’re ripping off him!” James tries to point to the guy next to him but knocks over Art’s drink and it spills on the big guy sitting next to him. James then turns and points to the guy beside him. “He did it,” James says hesitantly. The big guy then gets up and grabs the guy. The big guy then starts punching him as James and Art grab their beer bottles and escape as an all out brawl starts up right beside them. James and Art then look back at the bar fight James started and quietly sneak outside where they start drinking. “Nice,” Art replies. “Great plan, James.” James smiles deviously. “And we got free beers out of it,” he says with a big grin as they get back inside the car. James then starts the car up, hands Art his beer, and then they drive off again before everyone in the bar figures out what happened. “You see?” James says. “The plan worked. And that’s why I’m going to win.” Art’s getting bored with the wrestle talk so he tries to come up with something else for them to do. “So what are we going to do now?” Art asks. James shrugs his shoulders. “I dunno,” he replies. “What do you want to do?” They both start brainstorming but neither one comes up with anything. Then, James has an idea. “We could always go to my house and watch horror movies while getting drunk,” he says with a grin. Art nods his head. “Hell yeah,” Art replies. “Great plan.” “So what’re we going to watch?” James asks. “I dunno,” Art replies dumbfounded. “Did we watch the Ghoulies yet?” “Yeah,” James replies. “What about Critters?” “Nope,” Art says. “We haven’t watched that yet.” “So what do you have in mind to watch?” James asks. “Do you have the Howling?” Art asks in response. James nods his head. “Yeah,” he replies. “I have Howling one, three, five, and six.” Art about jumps for joy like a little kid at a candy store. “Can we watch Howling five?” he asks. “I think that’s the good one.” “Alright,” James replies with a grin. “Howling five it is.” Art then starts to thinking again. “Say, is that the one with the hot chick?” he asks James. James shrugs his shoulders. “I dunno,” he replies. “I haven’t watched it yet.” “Well I hope it is,” Art replies. “That chick is hot.” “Dude, they always have hot chicks in horror movies,” James says with a grin. “How else do you explain why I’m the only male in the Infinite Gauntlet this time around?” Art laughs and asks, “Are you saying that the rest of the people in this match are chicks?” “You damn right I am,” James replies with a grin. “A bunch of idiot fucktards is what they are. I’m telling you, it’s going to be a piece of cake. If they all didn’t have to come out on their on, then I would have to DRAG them to the ring and make them all surrender over to me!” Art starts laughing. “You’re fucking crazy,” he says with a laugh. “Damn right,” James replies. “I’m winning it this time and it’ll be an even worse slaughter than the one we’ll see on Howling five.” “That’s going to eb some sick shit,” Art says with a grin. James nods his head. “Yeah,” he replies. “But not as sick as people are going to get when they realize that they lost to me.” James then cackles maniacally as he puts the car back in drive and they head to James’ house to watch horror movies.
