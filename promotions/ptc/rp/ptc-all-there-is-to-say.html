All there is to say

28 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The black nothingness fades in to a close up shot of Ice sitting on the edge of a rooftop, overseeing a small city. Ice's legs are dangling off the edge of the building, as he looks out into the night sky, and over the city. Next to him on the ledge is a small red papered package. In his hand is a book of matches. Slowly Ice pulls out one match, lights it, and watches at is burns down to his finger tips. A calm expression fills his face, as the yellow glow, of a single security light shines directly above him. The night wind howls, and the stars twinkle in the sky as Ice again pulls out another match, and watches it burn down to his finger tips. Ice twirls the book of matches inbetween his thumb and index finger while slowly intaking a long, deep breath. He holds it, and looks up before exhaling his breath. "I hope you realize what you are doing by your decisions to enter this match. You want the recognition outside of GW, just so you have something to hold over everyone else. You are hoping that THE MONSTER comes out and gives you what you need to survive a match like this. But deep down, I know your real reason behind this plan, deep down, we both know the monster is dead. I know the truth behind your actions, I am the only one not fooled." Ice lowers his head, and again gazes out into the night. The security light begins to flicker. Ice's attention is not altered, as he doesn't even notice it, and continues to stare out over the city, and into the night. "You think so much of yourself. You think so much of what you have done, and the fact that no one has caught on. Well, I think that you think TOO MUCH of yourself. I know just what you are trying to do, and just what your "master plan" is. You may think you have a great idea, but the truth is you don't." Ice stands up from his seated position on the edge of the building. He adjusts his pants and his shirt, steps off the rooftop ledge, and back onto the roof of the building. He picks up the small red papered package and puts it into this pocket. Ice turns and looks directly into the camera. The camera zooms in on the intense stare, gleaming from his icy, blue eyes. "The facts are this. You are only putting yourself in harms way, and risking danger to yourself by entering a match like this. You are going to enter that match, badly you will be beaten,and I WILL NOT BE HELD ACCOUNTABLE FOR ANY OF IT!" Ice turns from the camera, and walks away. The camera quickly follows as Ice walks across the rooftop, and over to a small cage filled with pigeons. Ice stops and gazes at the pigeons. "Pigeons. Exactly what you think of the other superstars in this match. Pigeons, caged in by their contracts, elsewhere. Pigeons, waiting for the cage door to open and allow them to spread their wings and fly. However, there is one pigeon, who's being sent to do Christina McCoy's dirty work, despite your entry into this EXTREME match. But,does that pigeon really know what they are getting into, or is that pigeon just doing what he is told, so he has a chance to spread his wings?" Ice pulls the small red papered package out from his pocket. He pulls off the red paper and reveals a handful of firecrackers. Ice lights one of the matches from the book he holds in his other hand, and lights the firecracker. He watches the firecracker burn down, and tosses it at the cage just before it explodes. A loud pop echos through the night, and causes the bird to squawk and flutter in fear. Ice sadistically smirks. "Do you think they are now frightened? Did the minor explosion cause anyone to jump? You have wasted so much time playing the game, so many times before. These men don't care about explosions, or bodily harm. They are extreme for a reason. Do you not see that?" Ice pulls another firecracker out from his pocket. He lights it and just as he did the one before tosses it at the bird cage just before it explodes in his hand. "I am going to say this now, before I forget to say it later. What happens to you this week, is not my fault. I am not going to be held responsible, for the actions of a man trying to kill himself. You have been forewarned, I honestly hope you heed my warning." Ice walks over to the pigeon cage, and opens the door. Flock of pigeons burst through the opening, except one, which Ice manages to keep in the cage, by suddenly slamming the cage door shut on it. Again Ice smirks to himself. "The lone pigeon, thinking that being singled out by his capture is going to allow him to spread his wings and fly, when in all actuality it only cages and confines him more. That pigeon reminds me of you. I've seen what has happened to you. In fact, I found it quite amusing. It gave me chills to see you that vulnerable, that lost, that beaten. I just wished I had thought of that myself. And because of what has happened, I'm sure you have this match as some kind of challenge to prove you have not changed. To show the world that you are just as good as you have been. That the events that have occurred have not effected you in any way. Don't lie to yourself." Ice lights another match and sticks it through the tiny holes of the bird cage. The lone pigeon in the cage flutters around in fear of the flame. Ice's cold blue eyes glimmer with the reflection of the flame. "I know you are not up to this task that is before you. I know you are still in pain and agony from what happened to you just one month ago. But that does not change what you are going to do, does it? You see, through this match, a message will be sent to Christina McCoy, the members of the PTC, and the rest of the world that you will never EVER be the same man you were. And through this match, you are going to show the entire world just what it is that you are now, no longer capable of." Ice pulls off the last match from the book, and twirls it around, inbetween his right thumb and index finger. "You want your glory? You want this match? Then you will take the consequences." Ice lights the last match, and presses its flame gently against the tip of the last firecracker. He tosses the firecracker into the bird cage. "POP!" The firecracker explodes and a puff of smoke and feathers shoot out from inside the cage. Ice turns and evilly snarls at the camera. The camera zooms in on his cold, icy blue eyes, then turns to the random parts of the pigeon that remain from the explosing. The camera turns back to Ice. "You see, I've heard it all before. Hell, I just repeated it all, so that I didn't have to hear it again from anyone else. Seventeen men enter the ring, ONE man walks out with a shot for the Extreme Championship. To win this match, you need skill, you need tolerance for pain, and you need the luck of the draw. The last time I checked, I had all of the above. I don't need to sit here and tell you how "extreme" I am. My past track record, speaks for itself. There is no more need to hype yourself up, there is no more need to try and show just how "extreme" you are, and there sure as hell, is no more need for talk, because I've just said all there is to say." A smug, almost arrogant expression radiates from Ice's face as the camera fades to black.
