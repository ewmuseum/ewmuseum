This means Darek hasn't fought Chaplain yet... but time will pass, just read and I'll shut up! ;) _______________ ::[Introduction]:: _______________ The Trials of Redemption have guided Darek through thick and thin. Most recently his encounters within the PrimeTime Survival event, his loss to Blaise for the South Wind Title, Malestrom 5 and now the GTT. Darek had fought valliently to bring Chaplain Graves to a crumbled state. The light of our lord has shone down and like a watchful eye, has pushed Darek towards the looming danger. There was nothing more dignifying than becoming a champion, to defend the pride and honor of your federation. This is what Darek set out to accomplish, within the Four Winds and without. Seymour Almasy, the man who has been deemed worthy of standing the test of time and immortality. The almighty have risen like a great pheonix from the deepest, darkest pits of despair. Almasy has come along way since once again garnishing the coveted PrimeTime Global title. A meaningless victory in the eyes of the Angel. Darek had known it was his fate to stand before perhaps the greatest fighter to embrace a PrimeTime ring. Passing Chaplain Graves was just his test, one that he had passed with flying colors. Now, face to face, the Final Fantasy would meet the Modern Messiah. The cold unforgiving look of hatred and contempt glimmered in Darek's eyes, he had once before sought to stand before the warrior, but that was a fate not meant to be. This time there would be no Tessa Windsor to provide ample distraction, the focus, the drive, it would all be fixated on the one man the world saw as the perfect physical specimen in this industry. The destiny of the Modern Messiah has now crossed paths with a Final Fantasy, the world is going to be transformed before your eyes. From the battle ground inwhich Darek and Seymour are to engage in their fight, to a war grounds where there will be take no hostages and prisoners, escape with your life... Survive to win. There would be no blind faith of good guestures, Darek had enough of what Seymour was preaching and now the time for purification has come. The cowardice within the champion has humbled Darek into believing he may actually succeed in his war. Long ago, the PrimeTime was to hype a match that would have featured the Final Fantasy taking on Tessa Windsor and the unknown Dark Angel. A battle that was never meant to be through the eyes of the now champion. This time there will be no running, no hiding, the ability to win comes down to the instincts of war. Respect was something Seymour had lost every once of in regards to Darek, the so-called champion avoided the Dark Angel that once possessed Darek DeAngleo. Now all that remained in his heart for feelings towards the dwindling old man, were feelings of resentment and vengeance. ___________________ ::[Darek's Thoughts]:: ___________________ R-E-S-P-E-C-T Spelling that word in my mind over and over again, it has come to my attention that Seymour is someone who commands such a word. As it's obidient master, Seymour controls and demnads respect from each and every being in this industry. Holding close his shiny, illustrious Global Title, perched upon a pedistol dictating to the lemmings that follow. Each of these mindless fools are enslaved by his word, worshiping him as though he were the one true God. Thou shall have no other God or idol before the one true God. Yet, the world of the PrimeTime is consumed into his ill concieved will to dominate. Each lemming one by one follows the other to a sure damnation, I must cleanse these impudent fools of their wretched sins. Before the eyes of God, I shall bring purification upon the land of the PrimeTime. When this has happened, the name Darek DeAngleo will command respect. For the one who does not respect the Global Champion, will be the one to bring him down. The legend of the Final Fantasy begins to take it's final breath. Like Dark Angel pulled my beloved wife, Amanda's respirator plug from the hospital wall, Seymour will gasp his last, final breath. The only fantasy I will entertain, is the notion he believed he could defeat me. A tie, the unsettled war. We lost to Tessa Windsor and now, there would be no distraction. The Fantasy versus one bad harsh reality, in a match that will raise the standard of excellence inwhich people perceive in our industry. I forsee this war becoming one of the greatest battles of our present time. The mighty, world renowed Seymour Almasy, taking on the former South and East Wind Champions, a blossoming individual that has nothing to lose and everything to gain. Seymour has the respect of these people, of the mass, he represents the PrimeTime as their undisputed Global Champion. I have earned the faith of my colleagues, enemy or ally, they have come to respect and fear what comes with standing in the ring with Darek DeAngleo. At one time the misguided, yet demonic Dark Angel faced Seymour in a charity fight. It settled nothing, but now it's almost as if Almasy is facing an entirely different being. No longer am I bound by hatred, no longer do I feel the sensational need to feast on the innocent. Instead I have returned to the glory of God's light, but my soul is not cleansed yet. The sins of the Dark Messiah have damned me, now I long for salvation. As I walk through the Trials of Redemption, God leads me on a path of purification. Seymour Almasy will be purified by my hands, and it will be the upset of the century. _____________ ::[The Story]:: _____________ ...continued... "I never meant for anything to happen the way it did. She turns away from the mirror and walks over to Darek. Sitting next to him, she runs her arm along his back. Bringing her fingers up to his hair, she moves them gentley through. Whatever it is that you need to tell me, it can wait until the afternoon." She looks to the clock resting on a shelving unit above the toilet, "it's two twenty three, let's go back to bed?" she begs. Sighing, Darek nods his head. All his sins were about to catch up with him and of them all, one of the seven deadlies sins of all, lust marked his soul guilty for what Dark Angel did to Amanada. Getting to his feet, he and his wife head back out of the bathroom. The pain in his head has seemeingly ceased, and he flips the light switch turning out the light. Making his way back to the bed, Darek crawls in, he looks over at Amanda and kisses her gentley on the lips. The two remain together for a moment before nestling snuggly under the covers to get some rest. The night becomes day, and soon the beams of light shine through the black venetian blinds covering thie windows. Peeking through the cracks between the blinds, the light lands directly on Darek's face. Rolling onto his side, his face squinches up trying to avoid the light, but the attempt is futile. Opening his eyes, Darek brings two balled fists to his eyes as he sits up. As he wipes the 'sleep' from his eyes, he glances down at his digital alarm clock on the stained oak stand next to the bed. Flipping the black switch on the side back towards him, the red LED dot in the bottom corner disappears, signifying the alarm is no longer set. Turning his head, Darek looks over his shoulder at the fragile, delicate woman sleeping next to him. It had been so long since he had felt her touch. Reaching over to his side, Darek brushed her strawberry red hair away from her face with his index finger. This was his prize, his redemption for renewing his faith in the lord. What was once taken from him by a vengeful God, had now been given back. Perhaps Darek misunderstood the hiarchy from the beginning. God worked in mysterious ways, bringing death and misfortune to those who often did not deserve it. However, that was only half the story, their suffering, that aching pain all went away as they were embraced into the warmth of God's kingdom. It was a splendid place that Darek had come accustomed with in the past. Now he fought for the right to return to his kingdom, and with his battle came the reward of his wife, Amanda. She was pronounced dead, Darek remembered decisively how he felt when he pulled the respirator plug from the hospital wall. Under the possession of the demon known as the Dark Angel, Darek believed Amanda was a possessed woman. That and he wanted what the beautiful Patti Anderson was offering. Looking at her soft complexion, Darek begins to contemplate how he can begin to tell Amanda the truth. He set out to murder her for the love of another woman. A woman who inturn betrayed Dark Angel, bringing the demon to destruction. Atleast Darek thought, rubbing his forehead, the pain had been releived but had the damage been done? That taunting voice of darkness itself called back for Darek, haunting his dreams, memories and visions. The Dark Angel was not going to be so easily dispossed and Darek felt the plague spreading through his body. Slowly he was being consumed again, taken into the darkness and hellish rebellion. Fighting the urge to hate, holding back the contempt he felt for his situation, Darek placed his heart and faith within the love of the lord. The shepard was there to watch the flock, and while Darek felt he may wander astray, he knew the lord would guide him home. Lost in the radiance of his one true love, Darek managed to turn away and rise from his bed. Walking slowly to the dresser just across from his bed, Darek bends down supporting himself by resting his arms on the dresser. Starring hard into the mirror, his demons were beginning to catch up with him and he could see it in his eyes. Stepping away from the mirror, he backs towards the large shower about fifteen paces behind him. Turning around, he draws the water from the nozzle. Hot splashes of rain fall freely from the shower head. Steam begins to eminate from the enclosed shower area and fog up the mirrors and and any glass around. Sighing, Darek removes the silk boxers he had wore to bed and brushes the shower curtain back. Stepping inside the rejuvinating chamber, the water splashed over his body and he felt renewed. The aches and pains had gone away, and slowly he slipped into the subconcious of his mind as he poured Herbal Essences shampoo into his palm. ----------------- ::[flashback]:: ----------------- The rain pelted the window like nails falling from the dark sky. Rolling across the black mass of clouds, the thuder echoed throughout the quiet land. A great flash of electricity danced along the skyline, threatening anyone who dare harness it's awesome power. The bright white light from the great surges of lightning was enough to momentarily illuminate the darkened room. With the blinds drawn open, and the ticking sound of the rain falling into the glass. Sleep was hard to come by, yet when someone has worked as hard to train and be willing to sacrifice his existence for his cause, sleep came easily. But not undisturbed. A faint rapping came from his hotel door. Rolling to face the door, Darek struggled to open his eyes and wipe the sleep that had been building during the night. Squinting at the wooden device that seperated the outside world from Darek's glorious rest, the knocking sound was heard again. The faint murmmer of someone or something speaking to itself outside his door begins to frolic through his mind. Widening, his eyes shoot open and he rolls his body towards the edge of the bed. This was perhaps the best nights sleep he had in weeks. The thought of all that was going to happen to him was crashing through his thoughts endlessly all day, everday. There was this hellasious battle with the Outlaw and the woman who once held Dark Angel's heart, the North Wind Champion, Patti Anderson. Setting his feet on the cold, wood floor, Darek's mind slips back to his thoughts. Should he manage to survive this war, endure the onslaught and become The Four Winds true Saviour, he would then be set to climb an even greater mountain. The one that would establish the most dominant league within the walls of the mighty PrimeTime. Surviving the North Wind title match was one thing, but Survivng Mount St. hells was another. Snapping his head upward, Darek glances at the door as another knock comes from the outside. "Im coming," He slurred out as he pushed himself up to his feet. "Hold on." Sliding his feet into a pair of loafers next to the bed, Darek saunters across the room towards the door. And should he survive Mount St. Hells, what next? The GTT, in it's fourth installment. A tournament, who's bracket see names like Patti Anderson taking on Jade, and Darek DeAngleo taking on Chaplin, a free agent. Shaking his head side to side, Darek reaches down for the brass door handle and turns it gentley to the right. Pulling the door towards his body, the bright white lights in the corridor shine into his room. Once the door is fully open, Darek stands before a man in a brown uniform, the man is holding a Manilla envelope with Darek's name writen on it. Smiling at Darek, the man extends his hand, "I'm Darren. Sorry to bother you..." he begins to fumble through some papers, "Mr. DeAngleo." Darek just nods and shakes the man's hand, "Now that we are introduced, I don't suppose you have something for me?" "I see, have I kept you waiting?" Darek sighs, "no, infact I was sleeping." He reaches out for the envelope as it's passed to him. "I wasn't expecting anyone, to be honest." The man tips his hat to Darek as he lets go of the envelope and passes him the clipboard to sign. Grabbing the Bic Uniball black roler pen, Darek signs his name across the paper and turns away from the delivery man. Waving over his shoulder, the man in the brown outfit reaches for the door and brings it closed as he disappears down the hallway. Darek, now standing in complete darkness, walks over to the light switch next to the door an flips one switch. All of the lamps in the room illuminate, one by one. Slowly the darkness is sent out of the room, and light makes way for Darek's vision to read the letter he had received. The thunder booms outside Darek's window, lifting his head, he sees the wicked lightning flash across the night sky. Running his indez finger under the glued shut flab, he tears a smooth line along the top of the envelope. Slipping his first two fingers into the envelope, he seperates the two sides holding it open. He reaches inside and pulls out a small stack of papers that had been stapled together. Once out of the faded beige packaging, Darek finds himself glaring at a blank sheet of paper, turning it over, the words pop out in front of his eyes. His eyes widen, his jaw drops, and a look of sheer shoch and exaspiration overcomes his face. He drops the envelope onto the floor and paces back to the center of the room. At first he is overcome by tears, a saddened feeling that he had failed, but that soon passes and he is taken under a tidal wave of anger. Everything he had been working hard to obtain was slipping through his fingers, the trails had slowly begun to change everything. Darek found himself forgetting things that had happened to him, they had slipped his mind as if they never happened at all. WIlliam assured him that the trails were going to right the wrongs writen in Darek's life and thus far, Darek had wittnessed his once dead wife, alive... breathing, her heart beatting, and even a small smile as he chased after the man responsible for poisoning her that day Dark Angel tried to free her soul. It was as if everything was meant to be different and Darek was now getting a second opportunity for life, but his life could not be complete with this missing piece. "What the fuck is that woman thinking?" He asks himself, half expecting an answer. Again silence fills the room and Darek's eyes rest heavily on the papers he holds in his hands. "I cannot believe she would even dare. To think, I could have trusted her with time and understanding." The tears flow more freely down his face, the hurt within his icy blue eyes seemed to tell the whole story. Though Dark Angel was a merciless tyrant, Darek felt compelled to fight for the sins that were portrayed the day the KnightMare had encompased the demon. Now, just hours before the second step in Darek's quest for light and dominance, he found himself facing perhaps the worst sitaution imaginable. Tossing his papers onto the small wooden desk across from the bed, Darek walks into the bathroom and stares at his reflection. What had he become? A simple servant? A man devoted to living the will of one God? What happened to standing up for his rights, for his faith, for what he wanted? Darek felt the urge to let the hatred overcome his body, staring deep into his own cold, unforgiving eyes, he came to an understaning. He now knew that his destiny was not what God would make of it, but what path Darek travelled to get there. Should Patricia pay for this act of travesty that she had just commited, or was Darek going to let her walk free and get away with destroying his life again. Shaking his head 'no' Darek saw fit to walk into Maelstrom feeling that deep hatred within his soul. Of course he was willing to forgive and understand, but this situation had moved beyond discussion, it had come to actions. Patti made her attempt to solidify her position as the Four Winds, North Wind Champion, but Darek was going to dethrone her reign of treachery and lies. This was a battle Darek was willing to fight, not for himself, not to be proclaimed the best, but now to save the life of his grand daughter. "Raven Anderson-McClancy..." Darek murmmered to himself. Shaking his head back and forth, he pushes any soap away from his eyes and positions himself in the direct path of the water flow. As the water pours onto his face, he closes his eyes and becomes lost in thought. Should he defeat the man named Chaplain Graves, Darek could end up facing the powerful Seymour Almasy. The name echoed in Darek's mind like a gun shot to the ear, he felt as though his entire world had been collapsing in on him and his luck would be to draw the Global Champion. Through the agony of the web of lies created by Dark Angel, Darek now found himself struggling to understand the reality of his situation. Amanda was going to find out about the attempt at her life sooner or later, she was going to discover that Raven was in Patti's custody, for Darek, this was his death. He would rather be defeated by Graves or even Seymour than have to be the one to tell Amanda the truth. Lifting his hands to the shower head, he adjusts it to pour straight into his face. It was like everything, from his relationship with Patti, to the return of Amanda, to the excesive amount of fights Darek has become involved in; had become entangled into a massive web of deceit and lies, one that Darek had to break through to find his salvation. He looked to God for the answer to his problem, persistant as always, Darek was simply nudged along the same path. He felt pain, the things he had done while under possession of Dark Angel, made him feel a horrid gut wrenching pain. His life had become dirtied with the disdainful sins of a dead demon. It was almost as if he hoped the shower was going to wash away his sins, but he really knew the Trials of Redemption were his only means to do that. Set on a path of destruction with Chaplain Graves and perhaps many of the PrimeTime greats, Darek felt pain but he felt ready. Then I fight for the Survival of the Four Winds in an attempt to climb Mount St. Hells. Knowing my luck, I'll end up facing Almasy in round two of the tournament. I could use your support." I'm so sorry." to be concluded... _____________ ::[The Moral]:: _____________ The Lust of a man is never reason enough for crimes such as murder and treachery. Dark Angel, fell under the spell of Patricia Anderson his actions betrayed the trust of his family and his love. For the good of the world, a demon was destroyed... though what he did was inexecusable, his sins now rested on the tainted soul of Darek DeAngleo. A being who sought to claim the top spot within his federation's walls, a being walking a path of salvation now sought to cleanse the soul of his GTT opponents. Chaplain Graves was the beginning, whomever he drew next would be the example, and the fear would then embed itself within the souls of his opponents. There was going to be nothing that can stop the mighty hand of justice from proclaiming his fate as the one true champion. This battle was about respect, about dignity, it was to determine for this year who was the best of the best. People like Pheonix, Deville, Inphino, Almasy, Blaise, they all knew what it took to partake in such events, now it has come time for a new generation. A brighter future for the PrimeTime and the world.
