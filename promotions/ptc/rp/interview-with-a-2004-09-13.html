13 September 2004

Date: 2004-09-13 I laid back in the small couch and rested my hands behind my head. Slowly my eyes closed, and I took in a deep breath. I tried to wash away everything that had been fucking with my mind, but it simply wasn’t working. I cracked my neck to the right, then to the left, trying to get as comfortable as possible. It felt very relaxing, quiet, and almost peaceful. I say almost because as every turn in my life, peace has only been a dream. I have never known complete peace, and my happiness only comes in short spurts. This time was no different, because even as everything begins melting away, the voice of the man seated behind the desk in front of me speaks again. Yeah I understand it’s his job to speak, and hell I even am paying him to do it. I just didn’t care for his opinion at that very moment. I guess that’s what happens when you visit a psychiatrist though. “ So Mr. Abitua, it doesn’t say anywhere in your files why exactly you feel you need to have treatment. That is as good as any a place to begin wouldn’t you agree ?” My eyes open again, and I look towards the doctor. A slight grin spreads across my face, as I look into his eyes. He was a middle-aged man, of about 45 or so. He was more or less your typical, clichéd white-male psychiatrist that you see in all those movies. He wore a blue dress shirt, with black slacks and a blue and black striped tie. He had a mostly bald head, except a ring of black hair circling it. His glasses rested well on his nose, which stuck out a little far, and his dull brown eyes let it be known all too well, that his concern for me and perhaps any of his patients, was far from genuine. I knew I’d change that fact if nothing else. “Well Dr. Monroe, it’s quite simple. I believe that in my line of work, one’s mind can begin very fragile and ready to shatter at any given moment. You see, one can begin to doubt themselves … doubt their ability.” I could tell the doctor was even more puzzled than before, being vague was always one of my strong suits. He wrote a quick note on a piece of yellow notebook paper he had near him, then shifted in his seat. “ Well Roberto … what is it exactly that you do ?” “I’m a Vampire.” It was obvious that the good Doctor was taken back by my last comment, as his head jerked up and he tilted it to the side, almost as a dog would. One eyebrow eventually raises, but we both remain silent. I stare at him with my Icy blue eyes, and those dead brown eyes he has seem to come to life for a moment. He stutters over his words at first, trying to figure out how to respond to something like that. “We … um … well … Mr. Abitua, Do you mean this literally ?” “Of course Doctor, why else would I say such a thing ?” The look upon the doctor’s face was priceless, and if it weren’t for the ignorant looks he had plastered on his face, my toying with him would have worked even longer. It was his “ This mother fucker is too fucked up to be seeing me!” look that got to me though. I fought it as long as I could, but it was just no use. “HAHAHAHAHA … Sorry Doc, I was just playing with you. You’ll have to excuse me, some people don’t take kindly to my sense of humor.” “Well Mr. Abitua you certainly had me worried, I thought that I’d need to go and send in the people with the straight jackets.” The Doctor chuckled as he finished this sentence, and then scribbled down a few more notes before placing his pen on the desk. He clasps his hands together and leans in over the desk, looking right at me. “Roberto, please now let’s get serious. Why are you really here ?” You had to admire someone who was straight to the point, because most just fall into my little games with ease, kind of takes the fun out of the whole situation. Then again it’s his job to be blunt about things, so in return I feel I am obligated to do the same. “I am here, Doctor, because I shed innocent blood.” The doctor’s expression goes flat again. “I use violence and blood shed as weapons of mass destruction. I reach into people’s very souls, and I squeeze the life from their bodies … Problem being, I Like what I do.” “Well Roberto, what is it that you really do ?” “Does it matter Doc ? I don’t think it does. What I think matters, is the fact that I will not and cannot stop it. I believe the thing going through your mind, should be “WHAT MAKES HIM DO THESE THINGS!?”, am I right ?” The doctor swallows hard, and then takes in a larger than normal breath, and releases it. His eyes travel around some papers and such that lay on his desk, as if he were searching for his answer from ANYWHERE. Of course, he quickly regains his composure, and maybe the ordinary person wouldn’t recognize his brief moment of uncertainty. “You are absolutely correct Roberto, you are a very intelligent man. Let’s begin with your childhood, how are your parents?” “Dead … In every way imaginable. I have heard stories of their deaths. The kind of pain my mother went through, and the grief my father felt afterwards. I never knew either though, so the stories are just that to me … stories. I did not love either of them, because they never meant anything to me.” “Well Roberto … how did your mother die ?” My eyes slowly close again, almost as if I were drifting away. I begin telling my story of my mother’s death just the way I have been told it for years. It runs through my head almost like a movie, one with no happy ending though. ( Roberto A. aka LeStatt Knight’s vision of what happened to his mother floats through his head. It’s a hospital, A Doctor, some nurses, and a proud father to be, are rushing through the hallways, leading a pregnant woman towards her ddelivery room destination. ) “ By the way Mr. Abitua, I am Dr. Rodriguez, nice to meet you … and you too Mrs. Abitua. “ “Nice to meet you … just take good care of my wife alright?” Humberto says back They continue on with idle chit-chat amongst each other … and a few instructions are spit out towards the other doctors that are helping with this procedure. A few moments later they go into an operating room. They pull right up to another hospital bed, 1…2…3… they lift and place Maria onto it. Dr. Rodriguez begins barking out orders to his assistants and they obey quickly. Berto is very pleased with Dr. Rodriguez’s ability to run this operation … it appears as if he has done it thousands of times. Berto grabs ahold of Maria’s sweaty hand and holds it tight, and she returns the grasp. “ We’re almost there baby, we’re going to be parents!!” A smile is seen underneath Humberto’s mask as he spoke these words. Maria forced a smile, but again the pain over took her. Her breathing is getting very heavy and faster paced. She looks up at her loving husband, who is breathing with her, maybe not purposely but he is doing it. “ I Love you Berto …” Humberto doesn’t get the chance to say much else, just as he is going to Dr. Rodriguez cuts in. “ Okay this is it, Maria when I say so … start pushing !!” ( You see My Life began as any others would, proud parents supporting each other, not knowing exactly what was going to happen next, all they knew was that they were totally in love, and that this was the first big step in their lives. While this step isn’t an easy one, it would be that much more meaningful, knowing that they’d have their love for eternity. ) The action begins, Maria begins pushing and Humberto screams every step of the way with her. He tries his best to comfort his wife as she is giving the gift of life, but he is just so mesmerized by it all himself. “There’s the head …” The Doctor screamed out. “Now Maria just one more big pu… His voice trails off as he looks up at Maria, who doesn’t look well at all. Now giving birth won’t make you the next Beauty Queen, but He knew better. He yells out a few orders at his assistants, who scurry to find him some stuff frantically. Humberto’s look changes from joy to confusion. “What’s going on Doctor?” Humberto asked. ( Not everything is as it seems … There are things that you just never understand until they happen. Difficulties in pregnancy happen, as we all know, but you see not all of them can be over come. From what I understand the doctors could have done more, and should have done more. Yet they didn’t believe that the problems were very serious, they thought it could be fixed easily … Sure it seemed bad, but they had been through these kind of things before. I blame the results on them. ) “Some women have difficulties giving birth … like their bodies aren’t ready for it, and your wife seems to be having problems … SHIT!! There’s too much blood here! Angelica, get some towels!! And you Hector, get the RD-45 alright !? MARIA! MARIA! You need to push or this Baby isn’t coming out!! Just one more !!” Humberto looks down at his wife, who is really struggling now. She just seems completely exhausted … her brown eyes seem almost black as if they’re losing their shine. Humberto rubs the top of his Love’s head and whispers … “One more baby … and it’ll all be over…We’ll have our baby Boy … Roberto Juan A.” Maria let’s out a low moan but then puts all of her strength into one last push … and the next sound is like music to their ears. CRYING … The baby begins to cry as it comes out just as all normal children do. Immediately Dr. Rodriguez hands the baby over to get cleaned up and wrapped in a blanket. Tears of joy begin to stream down Humberto’s face, and he gives a smile to Maria who returns it back … ( There I was, in all of my glory Dr. Monroe. The birth of Roberto A. Now a days some people say it was the day the devil himself was born, but that is a subject for another time. Perhaps it was the final moment of pure happiness in my family’s life … because what happened next, was the first of many horrible things. ) But the joy only lasts a moment … “DAMN IT … Angelica, Hector why wasn’t I warned about this !?” The two nurses give a confused look at their doctor … “About what Doctor?” Dr. Rodriguez looks up at Maria and Humberto and gives a very worried expression. He grits his teeth and closes his eyes … “Did you know you were having twins?” asked the doctor. Maria barely could respond as she is in bad shape … and Humberto just freezes, unable to move … his voice comes out frantic. “ This can’t BE!! She can’t go through this again !! Look at her now, THIS CAN’T HAPPEN!!” “ But it is Mr. Abitua … she’s bleeding too much though, and this baby isn’t waiting he’s coming now …” Maria’s breathing is very shallow, the two nurses have pulled in a few more nurses, because this is going to take a lot more. They are all telling her to push … and Maria fights with every ounce of energy she has. Humberto Is holding onto her hand tighter than ever, waiting for his second child. They fight and fight for her … shuffling around, cleaning, putting needles in her, using different tools. Then more crying is heard … a long drug out cry … Humberto looks down at Maria, and she is very still. He looks towards his second baby, but his crying wasn’t very loud. The screech he is hearing … is from the machine that his Loving Wife was hooked onto. Dr. Rodriguez screams “ We got a flatline!! We need some juice in here !!” Humberto just backs away … and slumps down in a corner as the doctor’s fight for Maria’s life. Tears flow down his cheeks … as he watches the doctor’s put volts of electricity into his wife’s body, and all she does is lay there still and lifeless. Blood is soaked on the floor, on the doctors, and on his wife … He thought about raising his children, being a husband. The Love him and Maria shared … that would never be shared again … ( LeStatt’s eyes open up, as he finishes the story to the psychiatrist. Both of their faces are like stone. ) I said nothing to the doctor as my vision came back to normal, after re watching the tragic events in my head again, as I have done every day since I found out. The silence in the room was almost defeaning. The only sound filling the air were the sounds of our breathing, and then the sound of the Doctor’s pencil writing on his note pad. “I was born to death doctor, It was because of Me and my brother Damen that my mother’s life was taken.” “Roberto …” The doctor clears his throat “You can not blame yourself for your mother’s death. These things happen all of the time, you can’t explain them, you can’t judge them, and you certainly can’t predict them.” “Doctor I have destroyed more than a few lives in my day, and quite frankly it doesn’t seem to be about to change.” “What of your father ? What happened to him after your mother’s death ?” “He never recovered from it. Although it only took him a week to realize his life would never be the same. He couldn’t understand why “GOD” took his beloved Maria from him, and left him with Damen and I. He couldn’t get a grasp on the fact that whenever he came home from work, or whenever he laid down in bed, Maria wouldn’t be there. I doubt he ever even wished to give it a chance. There were news articles all over the place about his death. Sure he was just a common man, but it was the way his life was taken from him … or the way he took his life.” “So he killed himself ?” LeStatt nods towards the doctor. “How?” My eyes flutter shut again, as I get ready to finish my story. I did not know exactly why I was here, nor did I quite believe that I was really sharing these things. I did not know this man from a bar of soap, yet I was letting him know things that I never even told RaZiel, until three years into our friendship. I can’t believe that I have more faith in him, than I did for RaZ … I just don’t know how to explain it. I brushed these concerns aside, and simply continued with my story. ( Humberto Abitua stands in a darkened hospital hallway. He is wearing a black trench coat, white T-shirt, and dark blue jeans. His hands are extended and helping him balance himself on the glass that is in front of him. His eyes are fixed in front of him, as he stares into the window, as he stares at his two new born sons. Tears stream down his face, and a lump is in his throat. ) “Things couldn’t have went so wrong, they just could not have. The doctor’s are lying to me, I know it. Maria is fine, boys … your Mama, is just sleeping. I saw her already, she was only sleeping. Soon she’ll be holding you two in her arms, and we will all be one happy family.” Humberto’s hands tighten up, and he almost tries clawing at the glass window. “I Loved her … I LOVED HER !! She isn’t gone, she cant be gone. When I go home, I know she’ll be in the dining room, finishing up dinner, we will eat Enchiladas, frijoles, and tortillas. Your mom makes the best tortillas boys, just wait until you try them. Jus … Jus …” He lowers his head, and begins sobbing, close to hysterically. His upper body shakes, almost as if he were convulsing. He spits on the tile floor of the hospital, and slowly raises his head to look at his two sons. He has a different look in his eyes this time though. No longer does he look depressed, lost, confused … only angry. “You two did this !! If it weren’t for you I wouldn’t have to feel this way. If it weren’t for you, then I would not be missing the other part of my heart and my soul ! There is no way that I could have ever brought two bastards like you into this world !! You are murderer’s already, and I cannot allow you to plague my life any longer. I cannot …” Humberto reaches into his trench coat pocket and quickly pulls out a small, black pistol and he points it towards his two sons. His hand trembles greatly, and the tears of anger, sadness, and confusion simply pour down his cheeks. “If this is the only way to finish this then so be it … So FUCKING BE IT !!” Then Humberto turns the pistol to himself and pulls the trigger … ( LeStatt’s eyes swing open, and it is evident that his breathing has been altered. Heavier now than before. He regains himself, and looks right into the doctor’s eyes. ) “He couldn’t handle the loss … he refused to believe that his wife was gone, and that he was simply left with his two sons. He took the coward’s way out. Let me ask you Doctor, do you have any children?” “Yes … a boy and a girl.” “You love your children right ?” “With all my heart Roberto …” “That’s good Doctor, because you never know when you may never get the chance to see them again … only one person can really tell you that.” “God.” “HAHAHAHAHA … No Doctor, I don’t believe in religion. I simply believe it is a way to make people who are afraid of death, feel better about it.” “Then who Mr. Abitua.” “That, Dr. Monroe is for another session.” With that I raise myself off of the couch, and begin walking towards the door of the room. Dr. Monroe raises from his seat, quickly. “But wait … our session isn’t through.” I grin towards the doctor, and push my shoulder length hair back. “Doc, believe it or not … That is more than you deserve to know at this point.” “At least tell me what you do for a living.” “I am a Wrestler … LeStatt Knight.” I don’t allow him any more words, I just step out of his office. I didn’t quite believe what had just happened in that office, nor did I quite understand why I did what I did. I had so many distractions though, that some things just needed to be said. Be it 3WA’s apparent downhill skid, to this Big inter-fed tournament that I somehow got entered in, to the relationship with Mary and myself, to these fucked up messages I have been receiving … I do not know if my mind and body will hold up. Perhaps a psychiatrist isn’t the way to go, but frankly I needed to try something. Life would only get more stressful … but I guess I should take it one day at a time … One Match at a time … Could I really win this tournament ? Can I become the 3WA World Champion ? Is Mary the one I have awaited for since Cindy’s death ? I doubt it … life is never that easy for me … but I sure as hell intend to find out for sure. First things first. El Fresh Meat.
