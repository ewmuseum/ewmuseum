"Faith No More" or "New Blood" pick a title.

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-04-02 Competition is what we all need to survive. Without it, we would have no reason to strive for our own betterment. Be it with rivals or with strangers, any form will test both how strong we are and how weak we are. The tournament that lies before me is one such examination. Sure, I could just make Syxx and McClancy burst into flames with a wave of my hand, but that would prove nothing. I must lower myself to their mortal level. I can only learn how truly strong I am that way. I must prove that I am more powerful on their terms. By doing so, I shall gain respect and with respect, more shall listen to my words. Then it is in the bag. All I need is to be heard for me to overpower their pathetic wills and “convert” them to my congregation. This tournament will be my finest hour, as I shall obliterate all and gain the attention of everyone. Thus giving me the perfect opportunity to lead the lambs to their slaughter. How glorious that day will be… My daily jaunt through the town continues and I stand at the doorstep of the local Catholic Church. The sign out front says the exact name is the Church of New Hope. How comical. There can be no new hope as long as you keep preaching the old ways. That is the problem with Catholicism. They are never open to change and are beating the proverbial dead horse. No wonder their followers mourn their faith instead of celebrating it. I would be depressed about it as well, seeing how there seems to be no joy in it. Continuing on, I think I shall pay a little visit to the opposing squad and see what havoc I can wreak. It is always fun to bother the anal-retentive followers. It is always fun to sit in the front row and pretend to have Tourette’s syndrome. Nothing messes up a service better than an expletive right in the middle of a serious portion. Perhaps I shall make the hymnal’s pages turn into the pages of a Penthouse when I attend my next mass. That should get a nice reaction from the bible thumpers. “Shall we?” I ask Dinah and she smiles evilly with a nod, causing her blonde hair to bounce slightly. We casually stroll up several concrete steps to the main door of the church. Dinah makes like a good little servant and opens the door for me. My sunglasses are swiped from my face and vanish into thin air from the palm of my hand. Dinah and I make our way down the center isle of the church’s main room. Candles, which line the walls on each side of us, go out when we walk by them like we are an ominous wind whipping through the building. Truth be told, I have been in this place before. Let’s just say that some men here do not practice what they preach and we will leave it at that. Wooden benches cover most of the room and a young priest stands before them at an altar. He is flipping through the pages of the good book, as we take a seat in the front row. Like a pair of statues, we sit still and in total silence. After a few moments, the holy man looks up from his gospel and jumps in shock when catching sight of us. “I did not see you two come in,” he chuckles and brushes his dirty brown hair out of his eyes. “Are you here for the service? If so, you have to wait about two hours before it begins.” “We are not here for the service, Father,” I inform the black-clad man. “Come to do some soul searching then?” he asks with a smile, as he meanders over to us. “Something like that,” I reply with a small smirk finding its way to my face. “My name is Father Paul,” he let’s us in on the big secret. Yes, that is sarcasm. “Is their anything I can help you with?” “Well, we were wondering what made you become a priest, Father Paul?” I announce. “That would be a good place to start.” “Ah, I have been asked this question a few times,” he answers. “There was no exact moment when the decision was triggered. Throughout my entire life, God has played a major role. I have been attending church for as long as I can remember and my life has been one full of joy and good fortune. I decided that I should give back to the lord since he blessed me with so much good. I know that I cannot repay him fully, but I am doing my best and he loves me for it.” “Hmmm… That is a very self-centered point of view and it is a very valid one at the same time,” I respond while trying not to laugh at him and his worthless faith. “However, do you realize what you have done to your family by choosing this life?” “I do not understand,” he says with a puzzled look on his face. “My family has not said anything against my choice. They seem behind it fully.” “That is because they are your family,” I fire back. “Truth be told, you did ruin some of their hopes and dreams by choosing this life. You are an only child, are you not? Thus, with your life of celibacy, you shattered her dreams of being a grandmother like a brick crashing through a window. I bet she never told you, did she? She never told you that she wept the night you informed your mother and father of your plans. Heaven forbid, no pun intended, you think of someone else beside yourself.” “That is untrue. My mother is proud of me,” he softly growls and turns away from me. “She loves that I follow my faith. You are a liar.” “Am I? Perhaps you should ask her upon your next meeting,” I suggest and rise from my seat, as I feel words striking a nerve. “While you are at it, you should ask your father about the sorrow that lurks in his heart due to you.” “What are you talking about?” he growls a little more ferocious this time and he glares back at me. “Ask him why he does not sit and talk with you like he used to,” I command him. “He will tell you that he hates the fact that the family name will be laid to rest when you leave this plane of existence. Your father worked tirelessly to make that name worth something, so you could be proud of it, and you are just going to let it die because you have to repay a debt to something you cannot see, hear, or touch. Come to think of it, how do you even know that your lord even helped you? Why is it implausible that you did the right things and had a good life under your own power?” It is nearly impossible to contain my laughter from just exploding out. Nothing makes me happier than messing with a major follower’s faith, especially when their lives revolve around it. I know as well as any angel or demon that there is a lord in the heavens above, but to convince a man of god that there is not, that is a sport that one cannot help but play, my friends. Plus, much like Syxx to PTC, Father Paul is new blood to the church. If all goes as I expect, the bloodline will soon come to an end for the holy man and later for my opponents in the tournament. Now, for all those wondering, I started weaseling my way into this man’s mind the moment I caught sight of him. It is difficult at first, but as his anger grows, portion after portion of his mind open like a girl’s legs on prom night. It is now like an open book to me, so he must be at his wit’s end with me. Things should get interesting, now. “Lies! All lies! How dare you come in here and spew such rancid things?” He finally explodes and steps into my face. “I dare because I was once a man like you,” I say callously and without budging an inch. “I once placed my hopes and dreams into the hands of the supposed higher power. I even became a priest just like yourself and do you know what it got me? Heartache, that’s what I received. My mother and father died a long excruciating death, as they were trapped in a car engulfed by flames. I could not help them, but I could damn sure hear them, as they screamed for someone to save them. What god would let such a thing happen to a pair of people that lived the perfect Christian life? Just so you think I am expelling balderdash from my mouth, have a look at this.” A quick use of my powers and I push up the sleeve of my jacket to reveal a horribly scarred arm into sight. Everyone at Lucas Arts eat your heart out. The mutilation of my arm is so lifelike yet disturbing that Freddy Kreuger would flinch at the sight of it. The priest is no different. His eyes light up and he turns away in disgust. I tug my sleeve back down into place. “Besides, why serve a god that does not grant you your wishes unless he feels you deserve them?” I continue. “My lord grants the wishes because you should not have to work to gain rewards. Your belief is enough to him. I do know that you always wished to fall in love. However, you have failed in that venture several times. Perhaps, that is the real reason you joined the flock of believers. You could not stand another heartbreak or failure. Therefore, you took the cowardly way out and used your faith to hide behind.” “Shut up,” he snarls and places his hands over his ears. “Do not fret, dear man,” I command and motion for Dinah to join me. “I have not come to cause you any harm and if I have, I apologize. I have only come to enlighten you. I am sent from my lord to teach you the good of becoming a follower of his. Just give me a moment to show you a tiny sliver of the power he has given me.” My blue-eyed servant positions herself beside me and I place a hand on the troubled man’s shoulder. He turns around and looks up at me with reddened brown eyes. “Paul, I can give you the mate you desire.” I announce to him. “Just tell me what you wish.” I snap my fingers and Dinah turns into a ten-year old boy in a school uniform. The priest just looks to me in shock. “Please excuse me. That is for another priest that resides in this chapel,” I apologize with a nod. With another quick snap, the blonde vixen returns to her normal form, but she is in different attire. She is now garbed in a somewhat slutty schoolgirl costume with pigtails swinging from the sides of her head and a cherry lollipop in her right hand. She rolls the crimson lollipop between her lips and looks to the priest with big sparkling eyes. “Hiya,” she giggles with innocent smile. “Does not tickle your fancy?” I ask. In an instant, Dinah’s garb transforms into that of a latex-clad dominatrix. Her high spiked boots click against the ground, as she walks up to the holy man with whip in hand and grabs him by the collar. She pulls him towards herself, so their lips are just millimeters apart. “Get on your knees, slave,” the dominant blonde demands through gritted teeth. I let out a small chuckle escapes my lips, as I watch Father Paul stare at Dinah with fear in his eyes. I guess he is not into the kinky stuff. It is a shame too. The voluptuous vixen is amazing with handcuffs and the whip. Getting back on track, my fingers cause another snap and my servant returns to her original form and clothing. Father Paul backs away out of shock, in total disbelief of what his eyes just relayed to his brain. “Wha… what are you?” he asks with a trembling voice and he stumbles, causing him to drop to the floor. “I am but a humble servant here to teach you the joy of serving my lord. I thought we covered this,” I respond, as Dinah and I follow after the backpedaling priest. “You are Lucifer trying to get me to turn against the lord,” he exclaims and draws a silver crucifix form his pants’ pocket. Now, that put me into a good mood. He compared me to my lord. A demon does not get such a compliment often. He may get on good side, if he keeps it up. I have been very civil so far though. I could have just made his life a nightmare from the moment I entered this wretched building. He would have begged me to take his soul, so the horror would stop. However, I was kind and took the gentler approach. That must be the little glimmer of Pietro Geist’s soul left deep within this mortal shell. I shall give him one more chance and if he fails to come around, I will just take his soul by force. “Father Paul, what more can I do to show you the error in your ways?” I ask him, as I crouch down to his level. “I have shown you some of the power my lord possesses and I have told you the wrongs that your supposed god has brought down upon you and the ones you love. Do the smart thing and join us. Your reluctance shall be forgiven and you will be showered with a reward that only would appear in your wildest dreams. Now, give me your hand and take the first step towards the beginning of your new life.” I extend my hand to the holy man and his hand slowly travels towards it. He is still nervous, but I can sense that the fool believes my words ring true. Most often the people who devote their life to the church are those with the least amount of faith. It is much like the section of the bible that discusses why Jesus healed the centurion’s child. The centurion’s faith in his power was exponentially greater than the dozen apostles that traveled the land with Jesus. Many priests and nuns are just like those apostles. They have poor faith, so they give their life in hopes of making it seem that they do. One would not believe how many priests have sold their souls before a normal person, who does not attend church regularly, did. Most would find it rather appalling. “I am glad you have seen the light,” I let the dark-haired priest know and a glimmer of joy can be seen in his brown eyes. I assist him to his feet and turn to the massive entrance door, as Dinah threads her arm with his. We make our way to the door and I open it wide to display that it is now raining profusely with lightning streaking across the sky. I pull the hood of my black jacket up over my head and motion for Dinah and Paul to exit the building. “Come. A new world awaits you,” I instruct the “converted” priest and my blue-eyed servant walks with him out the door. “Now, let us discuss what you wish for.” Like taking candy from a baby…
