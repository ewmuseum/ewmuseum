Global Title Match

21 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
21 January 2004 The King of Games: Part One “Once more, the time has come.” Seymour Almasy is a man with little left. On Sunday Afternoon Supremacy in the UWF, he lost the UWF World and UWF THC Championships. His future in the UWF is greatly in question, even more so considering his image has appeared on Global Wrestling television. His wife is in a coma. In this time of great change, Seymour must turn back one of the most difficult challenges of his career. His second challenger for the Primetime Central Global Championship is Dave Harley, the only man to ever challenge for both the PTC Tag Team and Global Titles. His Tag challenge as one half of X-Corp ended in a loss, a fact that Seymour is all too aware of. Can a distracted Seymour defeat one of the WWA’s greatest superstars? Not if things keep going as they have. --- Seymour Almasy: BLAST! The Global Champion shakes his head. Ordinarily, the UWF World Title would be sitting proudly on his shoulder, but today it is only the Global Title that adorns Almasy’s body, wrapped around his waist. However, the source of his annoyance is not the loss on Supremacy to Beezlebozo. Seymour Almasy: THAT BASTARD KAIBA! THAT’S THE FOURTH TIME I’VE LOST TODAY! In the innermost chamber of Seymour’s apartment, namely, his room, his Nintendo Gamecube has its Game Boy Advance cables hooked up, allowing his GBA copy of Yu-Gi-Oh: Worldwide Edition to play on the huge TV. Kaiba’s Blue Eyes Ultimate Dragon, equipped with Megamorph, stands alone on the field, the damning words “YOU LOSE!” flashing on the screen. Seymour Almasy: Ah well, maybe I should take a break. Best not to get mad about it right now. The Final Fantasy smiles, as he heads over to his desk. Seymour Almasy: Besides, I should work on my real life deck. Almasy whips open his deck drawer, revealing a series of gigantic binders. It seems that everyone’s favorite delusion whackjob has found himself a hobby… Seymour Almasy: Ah, my pastime. When I am not in the ring, or playing Aerith’s favorite game…I believe she calls it “Hide the Ultimate Weapon”…I am working on my deck. Unlocking a metal box, Seymour pulls out a deck protector box, and then pulls out his deck. Made of exactly forty cards, each and every single piece of cardboard in its respective protective sleeve, the deck represents hundreds of hours of careful thought and play testing. With Aerith in the hospital recovering, the deck is the most important thing to Seymour currently in the house. Seymour Almasy: Ah...my brainchild. I don’t mind admitting that I’ve spent more Gil than I would have liked on this deck, but it has not failed me yet. He looks through the cards carefully, making sure they are well shuffled. Making sure they are to his liking, Almasy places them back into the box, seals it, and reaches for his coat. Seymour Almasy: It’s time for my weekly match with Grandpa Cid! I wonder if he’s managed to get any new cards this time…I’ve dominated our past few battles. Almasy smiles. While his exterior seems happy and lively, it is obvious that his card game is only hiding a more brooding interior. He manages to get his coat on, and zips it up tight, before heading for the apartment door. Once it is opened, the cold smacks into Seymour, penetrating his coat and causing him to shiver. Seymour Almasy: Gah…it’s freezing out here. The deck is placed ever so carefully into Seymour’s coat pocket, as he descends the stairs to the street. As he steps onto the sidewalk, the happy façade begins to crack, and a thoughtful Seymour emerges. Seymour Almasy: You know, this is about as bad of a week as there could be for me to have this battle. Or, so I’m sure most people, including Dave Harley think. When they look at me right now, they see a man at his most vulnerable. They see a man ripe for the picking. And yet, they could not possibly be more wrong. Almasy walks slowly down the street, hands in his pockets to repel the cold. Seymour Almasy: On the surface, I am a beaten and broken man. At Contest of Champions, I retained my UWF World Title…but at an unbearable cost. Jamie Shock accidentally shattered my wife with a crowbar. I went to the hospital to visit her that night. I was scared out of my mind. There is nothing in your life that can prepare you for that. Nothing. The Global Champion sighs, the air expelled from his lungs appearing as a plume of steam in the freezing cold. Tears begin to fall from his eyes, not freezing on impact, but certainly slowing as they flow down his face. Seymour Almasy: It was at that moment, in that room, that I realized I was now virtually on my own. The thought practically brought me to my knees. Aerith did everything for me. It’s a wonder I was able to survive before she was in my life. But now, until she is better, I must fend for myself. Almasy smiles, as if letting himself in on something everyone else knows. Seymour Almasy: I don’t think it’s a secret that most people think that, without Aerith, I’m nothing. I will admit, she has guided me for over a year. On a journey with an impossible amount of twists and turns, she has followed me all the way, her role always changing. From confidant to friend, from friend to enemy and back to friend again, to lover, and finally to wife…she was always there. But now, she isn’t. Yet, everyone seems to forget that in the face of adversity, I remain strong. The face of Seymour is impassioned, yet remains solid and strong. Seymour Almasy: During my path to the Global Title…Aerith left me. I was alone for several days, during which I questioned my abilities. I had to make a decision between Yevon and Aerith. And I chose Aerith. I still choose her, even to this day. Once again, I am faced with adversity. And once again, I shall not yield to it! Almasy prepares to launch into a diatribe on how sure he is he will win. Ready to tear into each and every one of Dave Harley’s flaws. However, as happens to Seymour so often, he is distracted. Seymour Almasy: OOH!! DUEL DISK! Seymour’s attention is diverted by an OFFICIAL Yu-Gi-Oh Duel Disc in a window. The temptation of the KaibaCorp invention is too much, as Seymour’s hand immediately goes into his pocket in search of Gil. Seymour Almasy: YES! I HAVE ENOUGH! The Final Fantasy charges into the toy store, and immediately wheels around to the display case of Duel Discs. There is but one left. Unfortunately, just as Seymour’s hands get on it, so too do the hands of one of the most feared fiends on Earth. ???: THAT’S FOR MY SON!! Seymour Almasy: NO! IT IS THE DREADED SOCCER MOM, HERE TO HALT MY NOBLE QUEST! Seymour stands eye to eye with the fearsome creature, hands locked on the Duel Disc box, as he begins to pull back. The Soccer Mom will not budge one inch, as she begins to pull back too. Seymour Almasy: Please, I need this to fulfill my quest! Soccer Mom: Like hell you do, you PSYCHO! Little Jimmy wants this, and what little Jimmy wants, LITTLE JIMMY GETS! The pair grapple continuously, no end in sight. Almasy suddenly realizes that drastic tactics shall be required. Seymour Almasy: Blast it! Drastic tactics shall be required! As the Soccer Mom prepares one final pull, Almasy releases the box with one hand. He readies his flash paper from his pocket, and lets loose with his signature weapon… Seymour Almasy: FIRAGA~! As he calls the name of the mighty fireball, the flash paper explodes in the Soccer Mom’s face. Instinctively, her hands go to claw at her eyes, to get the blinding flash away. This causes her to lose her grip on the Duel Disc. Seymour grabs hold, and quickly runs to the checkout area. Seymour Almasy: Good sir, ring this up, and quickly! The cashier nods, ringing up the purchase, as he takes in the scene of the blinded woman, and shakes his head. Cashier: Damn man, it’s not the holiday season for another 300 days! Ignoring the cashier, Seymour hands the man the money. The cashier places the Duel Disc in a bag, which Seymour happily takes. Seymour Almasy: YES! I have obtained the Duel Disc! Now I shall be able to play my grandfather on a new level of dueling! With this accomplished, I can now worry about Dave Harley and my--HEY! What in the?! YOU SON OF A SHOOPUF! The once-blinded woman has regained her sight, and has managed to snatch the package away from Seymour, laughing hysterically as she leaves the store. Soccer Mom: MINE! MINE! Seymour Almasy: HEY! GET BACK HERE! The PTC Global Champion takes off after the woman, running as fast as he can. The woman has deceptive speed, however, and is merely hundreds of feet away from her SUV. Seymour Almasy: BLAST IT! If she reaches her landship, I will have no chance of catching her! Reaching into his pocket, Seymour pulls out a large green marble, and raises it skyward. Seymour Almasy: TIME MATERIA! HASTE! Obviously, Seymour is not in control of magical powers. But, the belief that he is does help him run faster. Faster and faster the Final Fantasy goes, until he finally manages to catch up, and take back his package. The Soccer Mom is perturbed, but Almasy manages to make his escape, panting, and breathing heavily. Seymour Almasy: Sweet Shiva…if I can barely manage to catch a Soccer Mom without running out of breath, how am supposed to be able to defeat Dave Harley and keep my title? Still panting, Almasy continues down the street, headed for his grandfather’s apartment. He clutches his Duel Disc tightly, just in case any other Soccer Moms attempt to steal his treasure. Seymour Almasy: Determination is how. The simple fact of the matter is, I am more determined than he is. While Dave has challenged on this level before, he does not know what it takes to be a warrior of the highest caliber…to be the greatest warrior in the world. Dave, you may be the WWA Intercontinental Champion, but you do not understand the drive that this requires. To constantly be challenged by the top combatants every land has to offer. I have already repelled the challenge of the now deceased Lights Out land, by defeated Angelus Sorrow. Now, your land of the WWA has sent you as its greatest warrior to challenge me. You are strong, true…but your fate will be the same as Sorrow’s. Finally spotting his grandfather’s apartment, Seymour breathes a sigh of relief. Seymour Almasy: Many things have changed in my life. Instead of the Universal Wrestling Federation, I now represent Global Wrestling. My wife is in a coma, and I have new companions, new friends. But what has not changed is my ability. What has not changed is my technique, my intellect. In short, Dave, I am the same man I have always been, but with even more drive. Even more determination. From here on, all matches are dedicated to my beloved Aerith. And so long as I fight in her name…I cannot lose. I WILL NOT lose. Ascending the stairs to the apartment, Seymour goes to knock on the door, but sees it already open. Pulling it back completely, Seymour’s jaw drops in shock. Signs of a struggle are evident, and his grandfather’s coat and wallet lay strewn on the floor. Seymour Almasy: GRANDPA!! (To be continued.) ---- 22 January 2004 The King of Games: Part Two “Grandpa? Where are you? WHAT HAS HAPPENED HERE?!” A worried Seymour Almasy stands in the doorway of his grandfather’s apartment. But there is no sign of Cid Seymour Wilson anywhere. Seymour stoops, searching through his grandfather’s belongings. It is then that Seymour makes a horrifying discovery. Seymour Almasy: GRANDFATHER! You’ve been imprisoned in this…this CARD! It is a New York State driver’s license that Seymour holds in his hand. Obviously, it has his grandfather’s picture on it. Little details like the fact that Seymour ALSO has a driver’s license are lost on The Final Fantasy. Immediately, foul play is suspected. Seymour Almasy: I suspect foul play! But WHO could have imprisoned my grandfather in this card! It could not have been Pegasus…his Millennium Eye would be able to do the trick, but it was ripped from him by Bakura…no, it must be a new threat. A threat that I would not think of…but who? Who could have done such a horrible thing? It is then that Seymour’s eye catches the newspaper lying on his grandfather’s kitchen table. The picture of Dave Harley at the comedy club, a microphone in his hand. And the caption below it that reads, “Harley kidnapped your grandfather you idiot!” Well, no, it really doesn’t say THAT. What it does say is: Seymour Almasy: “WWA Superstar Dave Harley was spotted at a comedy club. The wrestler seemed to be blowing off some steam before his Global Title match with Seymour Almasy”…hey, that’s me…BUT WAIT A SECOND! The caption may as well have said the former. The Final Fantasy’s ire has been aroused. And not the same type of aroused as he is with Aerith. The bad type of aroused. The angry type. Seymour Almasy: That vile fiend. So DAVE HARLEY IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE IMPRISONMENT OF MY GRANDFATHER! He must have imprisoned Grandpa Cid using the might of his Millennium Microphone! I must find him…but wait! Yugi Moto had to obtain ten starchips before he could challenge Maximillion Pegasus, and thus, I must gain ten starchips before I can challenge Dave Harley! Almasy nods, the seriousness and solemnity of his mission weighing heavily on his mind. Seymour Almasy: But I shall not be able to do this alone. I will need to rely on the good powers of my OWN Millennium item! Going into his other coat pocket, Seymour pulls out the same green orb that was earlier used as a Time materia. Seymour Almasy: I must invoke the power of the MILLENNIUM MATERIA! Once I do so, I shall be known as…YAMI SEYMOUR! Seymour raises the Millennium Materia high, and screams. Seymour Almasy: YU-GI-OH!!! He immediately takes off, and runs for the bathroom. Minutes pass, before Seymour emerges. His long platinum white hair now defies gravity, and is all pointy. He reaches into his pocket, pulls out his deck, and holds it outwards. Yami Seymour: In order to be victorious, I must trust in the heart of the cards! The Spirit of the Millennium Materia nods, and continues. Yami Seymour: I must now find opponents in order to defeat them for their starchips! But, where may I find other duelists against which to test my skills…I KNOW! I shall get to my destination by riding Boco the Gold-- BLAST! Boco is back at my house! I shall need another way to get to my destination then! Seconds later, Seymour is trudging down the steps, Duel Disc now attached to his arm. Yami Seymour: Where’s a chocobo when you need one, ya? Having shamelessly quoted Wakka from Final Fantasy X, Almasy sets out on his journey. Yami Seymour begins his noble walk, somehow managing to draw even MORE stares than usual with his odd hairdo and Duel Disc. As Seymour walks on, he suddenly notices a package of half used stickers on the ground. Yami Seymour: Great Maju Garzett! THESE ARE STARCHIPS! How many are there…let’s see….SIX! What fortune, I’m over halfway there! Seymour stops in the middle of the sidewalk, and begins affixing the gold star stickers around his wrist. Once all six are in place, Seymour continues walking, and soon enough finds his destination. Yami Seymour: Ah, an elementary school! I am SURE to find a fellow duelist here who I can challenge for my last remaining starchips! Indeed, a potential opponent is currently making his way out of Miss Jones’ kindergarten class. A small child, deck in pocket, duel disc that ways as much as he does on his arm. The poor soul has the misfortune of having four gold star stickers on his chest from a good day of work in the classroom. His name is Simon. Yami Seymour: YOU! Simon has barely managed to make his way out of the school before he sees the larger man standing before him, looking for all the world like a character on his favorite TV show. In his mind, his dreams of being a real duelist like Yugi have come true. He can’t let his hero down. He must defeat his opponent. Simon: Who…who are you? Yami Seymour: I am Yami Seymour! I challenge you to a duel, for four starchips, under Battle City rules! Simon may be only in kindergarten, but he can certainly count. He spots six starchips on the strangers wrist, realizing that a victory will give the odd man ten. Simon: I accept! In the name of Yugi, I shall be victorious! Yami Seymour: But I have the power of the Millennium Materia! I must save my grandfather, and so, I shall give this duel all I have! Simon and Seymour each activate their duel discs, as a crowd of five and six year olds form around them to watch. Their life points are set to 4000 each, as they nod solemnly. Simon and Yami Seymour: LET’S DUEL! They each draw five cards, except for Simon, who draws six. Simon: I’ll go first! I set these two cards, and play Celtic Guardian, in attack mode! Simon slaps down the Celtic Guardian, a warrior card of moderate attack. Seymour nods, as Simon ends his turn. Yami Seymour: My turn! Almasy draws a card, looking at his options. He smirks, and looks at his opponent. Yami Seymour: Fine then, let’s start with THIS! I activate the magic card, POT OF GREED! Seymour slaps the Pot on the field, which allows him to draw two more cards. Seymour grins, as the Right Leg of the Forbidden One enters his hand, the first of the five pieces of Exodia that will allow him to win the duel. But for now, he has to do something about the Celtic Guardian. Selecting another card, he plays. Yami Seymour: Next, I set one card, and activate the magic card Fissure! Go! DESTROY THE CELTIC GUARDIAN! Simon is unfazed. Simon: HALT! I ACTIVATE MY TRAP CARD, MAGIC JAMMER! By discarding the Dark Magician from my hand, I stop your fissure! Seymour Almasy: NO!! Blast it, I’ll set this monster in defense mode and end my turn. Simon nods, and draws. Simon: Tell me, Seymour…why do you duel? Yami Seymour: I duel because my grandfather has been sealed inside a card by Dave Harley, keeper of the mysterious Millennium Microphone. Once I win this duel, I will have enough starchips to challenge him to a match to release my grandfather’s soul! Simon: A noble ambition indeed…and yet, you will not be able to defeat me to achieve it! For I NOW play the magic card POLYMERIZATION! Yami Seymour: OH NO! Simon: Oh yes! I use it to fuse Curse of Dragon and Gaia the Fierce Knight from my hand to form GAIA…THE DRAGON CHAMPION! Now, as per the Battle City rules, I cannot attack with Gaia this turn, but my Celtic Guardian can! GO, DESTROY HIS FACE DOWN MONSTER! Seymour cringes, as he flips face up his Sangan. Yami Seymour: You have destroyed Sangan, but now, I can search through my deck and find a new monster! Seymour shuffles through his deck, and selects the Left Leg of the Forbidden One. Simon declares his turn over, and Seymour draws another card, the Left Arm of the Forbidden One. Yami Seymour: Almost there…but BLAST! I have nothing to defend my life points! A look of triumph comes over Simon’s face, as he draws his next card. Simon: Excellent! GAIA, ATTACK HIS LIFE POINTS DIRECTLY! Yami Seymour: HALT! I ACTIVATE MY FACE-DOWN TRAP CARD…GRAVITY BIND! Simon: I COUNTER WITH SEVEN TOOLS OF THE BANDIT! I pay 1000 life points to destroy your Gravity Bind, and my creature’s attack goes through, dropping you to 1400 life points, which, coincidentally, is the same amount of LP that my Celtic Guardian has….ATTACK! Yami Seymour: WAIT! QUICK-PLAY MAGIC CARD…SCAPEGOAT! I can use Scapegoat to create four Goat Tokens! Celtic Guardian’s attack kills one, but three are left. Simon looks somewhat dejected, as Seymour draws. The fourth piece of Exodia, the Right Arm, appears. Yami Seymour: Hmmm…what shall I do now? Hmm, seems as if I’m almost out of options… I end my turn. Simon: Hmmm, in that case, I’ll now play Alpha the Magnet Warrior! All three of my monsters attack your three Goat Tokens! Unless you draw something you can use this turn, you’re finished! Yami Seymour: If I don’t get the last piece, I’M FINISHED! I’ll never be able to save my grandfather! HEART OF THE CARDS, GUIDE ME! Seymour draws. The card is a Skull Servant, with an attack of 300 and defense of 200. Yami Seymour: Well, fuck. Noticing Simon somewhat distracted by a car driving by, Seymour rifles through his deck, and draws the final piece of Exodia. Yami Seymour: Simon, this duel is over. For the Heart of the Cards has come through for me! The card I have drawn is the most powerful in my deck. With it, I am able to defeat you, and go save my grandfather! I will gain your four starchips, to give me a total of ten! It is a card that-- Simon: It’s Skull Servant, isn’t it? Yami Seymour: No, it is NOT Skull Servant! I HAVE ASSEMBLED ALL FIVE PIECES OF THE UNSTOPPABLE EXODIA! A look of stark terror comes over Simon’s face, as Seymour rises to his full height. Yami Seymour: THIS DUEL IS OVER! EXODIA, OBLITERATE! Seymour extends his arm forward, waiting for Exodia to emerge and destroy the kindergartener. Obviously, nothing happens. Yami Seymour: I SAID, EXODIA, OBLITERATE! Nothing. Yami Seymour: Um, well…could you just PRETEND to be obliterated? Simon: I’m five, I don’t even know what obliterate MEANS. Yami Seymour: Just fall to the ground and lie there in pain. Simon: OH! I can do that! Simon falls, writhing in mock agony. Simon: Owww! Ahhh!! Seymour walks over to the downed youth, and reaches down, plucking the stars from his chest, and adding them to those on his wrist. Yami Seymour: A noble battle, youth, but I am victorious! Now I have all ten star chips, and can go save my grandfather! Yami Seymour walks away, as he smoothes his hair back down. By anime logic, this means he has lost his Yami state, and is now regular Seymour again. Seymour Almasy: With that taken care of, it is time to find Dave Harley. I swear upon my honor as a champion…that vile miscreant shall pay for his crimes! I will challenge him to open combat in the Battle Arena, defeat him, AND FORCE HIM TO RELEASE MY GRANDFATHER FROM THAT CARD! Almasy begins to walk, conviction all over his face. Seymour Almasy: But until then, I shall return home. I must prepare for the war ahead. (To be continued) ---- 24 January 2004 Fantasy or Reality? (RP #3 and Final for GT Match) “CID! I SHALL AVENGE THEE!!” The time draws nearer and nearer. Soon enough, Seymour Almasy will do battle with Dave Harley in his second defense of the Primetime Central Global Title. To Seymour, everything is on the line: his title, his pride, and his grandfather. A battle of such dire importance requires Seymour to focus. And so, Seymour has detoured from his scheduled trip home. Now, he stands outside the hospital where his wife rests peacefully. Seymour Almasy: But before I can avenge anyone, or achieve any variety of success…I must find my source of inspiration. Every week, I come here to this hospital. For this is where my wife is. This is where Aerith is…and I have a present to give her. In Seymour’s hands, a large teddy bear is clutched tightly. His Duel Disc still hangs from his right arm, as Almasy shivers slightly from the cold. He looks up at the hospital tentatively, a sense of dread filling him. Seymour Almasy: No matter how many times I come here…it never gets any easier. Every time I step inside this building…I am instantly overwhelmed. It’s such a sterile environment. It almost lacks humanity. And then…to walk by and see all of these people in their rooms, some here for minor health problems, such as cuts or a broken bone…and still other here with worse. The halls are filled with tears. People crying for their loved ones…people with incurable illnesses that may never leave these halls alive. The Global Champion grows even more nervous, emotion pouring into his voice. Seymour Almasy: And the woman I love is here. Aerith lies in room 341, in a coma. I had no idea what a coma was. I thought she was just knocked out, and I could bring her back with a Phoenix Down, but no…it was something much worse. I remember the doctor pulled me aside after I arrived at the hospital that first night. He explained to me what a coma was. He told me that Aerith could be in it for a long time. Days, weeks, months…even years. And then…he told me she might never wake up from it. Ever. Hands shaking, Almasy nearly drops the stuffed bear in his hands. He holds it ever tighter, trying desperately to prevent it from slipping from his grasp. Seymour Almasy: I was devastated. And yet, it is for that very reason that I come here every week. Because, I know that Aerith could….could die any day. I pray with everything I have that it will not happen, but it could. If it were possible for me to live in room 341 with her, I would. But there is neither room enough nor permission for me to do so. Until she is better, I will continue to see her, hoping every time that she will wake up, and be able to accompany me once again. Seymour falls silent, feet slowly moving him towards the entrance of the hospital. His hand makes it to the door, yet cannot pull the handle just yet. Seymour Almasy: Please…please be awake, Aerith. This time, the quaking hand manages to pull the handle and open the door. Seymour steps inside quickly, going back to holding the teddy bear with both hands. The receptionist nods to him, knowing Seymour well. It is a scene that is repeated several times as Seymour heads for the elevator, doctors and nurses nodding to him, wishing him well. He, in truth, could care less about his own health. Seymour Almasy: Why did this even have to happen? Aerith is in here because of me. It’s my fault. I fought Jamie Shock in the Battle Arena, and he pulled out his metal weapon. He had me lined up, dead to rights. I knew it. Shock knew it. The crowd knew it. I closed my eyes and braced for impact. I heard the dull thud of the weapon, cracking against something…something that wasn’t me. It was then that I opened my eyes, and looked into Shock’s eyes. Shock is a callous man, a human being without feeling, without emotion…but even he was stock-still. Something had to be horribly wrong. And then…then I looked down. Almasy’s right hand shoots out, pointer finger jamming the ‘up’ button quickly and fiercely. Soon enough, the elevator comes, and Seymour steps in. The button for floor three is pressed, and the elevator doors close, sealing Seymour and his guilt inside. Seymour Almasy: I saw her on the ground…lying motionless. I looked from Shock’s weapon to her, and put two and two together. Aerith saved me from Shock’s blow, at the cost of her own health…possibly even at the cost of her own life. I should be the one lying in this hospital right now. Not her. I told her to stay behind, because I knew what Shock was capable of. Knew he would have struck her had she been at ringside. Even knowing what would likely happen, she came out anyway. She came to help me…to help the person she loves. The last few words send a plethora of emotions through Almasy’s mind. Pity, fear, anger, and love all race through Seymour, confusing and elating him at the same time. Seymour Almasy: What a feeling it is to love and be loved in return…something I could not possibly describe to anyone. It is something that must be experienced to be understood. Almost rudely, the elevator chimes, to indicate it has arrived at the third floor. Seymour takes a deep breath, then lets it out as the doors slide open. Seymour steps into the hallway, his steps growing faster and faster as he finds his way to the room he could locate blindfolded. Seymour Almasy: Here we are… Seymour smiles at the doctor in the doorway, going through the usual procedure of sterilization, so that he may see his wife. Once clad in a white hospital gown, Seymour is permitted to open the door. The small glimmer of hope that he has is extinguished when he sees Aerith lying in the bed, perfectly stable, and yet, still comatose. Seymour Almasy: Aww… Almasy places his Duel Disc down gently on a side table, and hangs up his coat. Pulling up a chair, Seymour sits at his wife’s side, holding the teddy bear behind his back. Seymour Almasy: Guess what Aerith? He waits for a response, before realizing rather quickly that none will come. Seymour Almasy: I got you a present! With a flourish, Seymour whips the teddy bear around from behind his back. He beams, and places the teddy bear next to Aerith on the left side. Seymour Almasy: …I guess you can’t respond to me, can you? Sadly, Seymour leans over his wife, gently resting his head next to hers. Immediately, he is swept up by her gentle warmth, a feeling he has missed greatly. Seymour smiles at her gently, all problems forgotten… Seymour Almasy: I’m so glad to see you again. I’ve got something I need to talk to you about. …But only temporarily. As much as he would love to lie next to Aerith forever, he has things he must discuss with her. Still, he figures he may as well do so while as physically close to her as possible. Seymour Almasy: Aerith, things haven’t been going so well. Where do I begin….Grandpa’s been kidnapped. All signs point to the deed being done by Dave Harley, my opponent. I’m defending the PTC Global Title against him. He used his Millennium Microphone to imprison Grandpa’s soul in a card! Had Aerith been conscious, she would have been able to talk Seymour out of his delusion, tell him that Cid was probably fine, which, in truth, he was. But without that voice of reason, Seymour was free to jump to every conclusion he wanted. Seymour Almasy: I now have the ten star chips I need to challenge him in the Battle Arena. He will come for my title, and I will show him no mercy for what he has done. He shall pay the price for attacking my family. For, with you in a coma…Grandfather is all I have… A tear falls from Seymour’s eye, landing on Aerith’s cheek. Almasy shivers, wanting desperately to wrap his arms around her, to feel hers wrap around him in response. To kiss her, taste her soft mouth once more. Pleasures that he hadn’t even known existed before Aerith came into his life. Seymour Almasy: It’s just so difficult, Aerith. You have no idea. Every day I wake up, and go to do whatever it is that I’m supposed to, be it fight in the Battle Arena, go play Grandpa in Yu-Gi-Oh, or just rest around the house. And every day, I roll to my side in our bed, and look for you. But you’re not there. I never get used to that. I never will. The Global Champion rises, going to the door, which he closes. This leaves himself and Aerith alone in the room. Seymour’s eyes squinch up tight as he turns to face Aerith. He looks down at her, a vision of loveliness as always. The sight of her motionless is too much for him to take. Almasy throws himself down at the foot of the bed, moving to take one of Aerith’s hands in his own. Seymour Almasy: I just can’t take this anymore! I can’t! Aerith, you mean EVERYTHING to me! Everything! Why do I even try going on? Without you…I’m nothing. You give everything I do meaning, you make it all worthwhile! Seymour looks skyward, eyes streaked with tears, cheeks red. Seymour Almasy: Please…I know Yevon no longer existed, but if there is anything or anyone up there, please hear my plea. Let Aerith wake up. She means more to me than anything. Strike me down if you have to…just let her be alright. Almasy’s head bows, unable to speak as the tears choke him, his words coming out as sobs. The world means nothing to him, as he desperately cries, trying to clutch at something to keep him going. All seems lost…until Seymour feels a slight pressure on his hand. Seymour Almasy: … The breath is caught in Seymour’s throat. He dares not breathe. He dares not move. Slowly, very slowly, his tear-blurred eyes look up, and see Aerith’s hand still in his, the pressure still there. Seymour Almasy: Oh my God… His eyes move up still further, and fixate on her face just in time for her eyes to open. Seymour is stunned into silence as his wife moves her legs, testing her capabilities. Everything seems to be fine. She smiles at Seymour, as Almasy is still frozen. Finally, after a seemingly infinite moment, he speaks. Seymour Almasy: H-h-how much of that did you hear? Aerith Almasy: Pretty much everything from the part where you said that I mean everything to you…how long was I out of it? Seymour Almasy: A-a--about a month. Aerith Almasy: Wow…that’s a long time. And you’ve been on your own for that long? Even after reviving from a coma, Aerith’s concern is for Seymour’s safety and sanity. Seymour Almasy: Well, yes. Aerith Almasy: …When was the last time I even let you go two DAYS without.. Seymour Almasy: Oh!! But wait, Aerith, we’re in the hospital! Aerith Almasy: So? Seymour Almasy: … Aerith Almasy: See, you’re out of excuses! Smiling, Aerith beckons Seymour forward. The Final Fantasy has no will to resist, and willingly climbs into the bed. Aerith throws the cover over them both, and gently places a hand on Seymour’s shoulder. Aerith Almasy: Well…I guess I SHOULD let you tell me a little bit about what’s happened… Seymour Almasy: Well…I have a PTC Global Title match coming up against Dave Harley soon. Aerith Almasy: Okay Seymour, that’s enough. Besides…look at you. Your poor face and eyes…you’ve been crying, and you look simply EXHAUSTED.. She reaches out, gathering her husband closer, and resting his head on her shoulder. Aerith Almasy: I’ll spare you tonight…and let you get some sleep. Seymour is already unconscious. Aerith smiles, despite herself. If she really has been out for a month, she knows that just waking up will not be enough to let her out of the hospital. But even though she cannot leave, she has her Seymour with her. She has all she could ever want.
