Survival RP#1

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-20 [We find ourselves in a small confined room, there is nothing but a door that is behind us and four chairs, a table sits in front of us. There is one light dangling from the top of the roof. Which makes the room seem like one of those interrogation rooms seen on really bad cop movies. Now the door opens and all we can hear is babbling from a bunch of people behind us. From the left of a screen these figures grab seats at all four sides of the table in the middle of the room. One man pulls out a pack of cards and throws them on the table. One of the other men takes the cards and starts to shuffle.] 1st Man: "So what do you guys say? Poker, or Hearts?" [The voice of this man seems very similar to us, but it is not yet clear who he is because of the flickering light. The only source of light in the room and it's half near burned out.] 2nd Man: "Poker Nicky, and would you please do something about this damn light?" 1st Man: "No problem, John." [The man we now know as Nicky stands up and hits the lamp with his hand, for some strange reason it seems to work and the room now goes light and all can be seen, dimly but still can be seen. The man known as Nicky is none other then Nicky Jam, the man about to step into the squared circle representing Team UPWA in the PTC Survival PPV. He looks around at each of his friends then sits down.] [The camera zooms in on each of the men, stays still for a second, then moves on the next.] [The first man is Nicky Jam, he sits back in his seat and starts to deal the cards. He's wearing the usual, a baby blue T-shirt, and a pair of Jean shorts. The second, the man identified to us as John is wearing similar clothing to Nicky Jam, but on the other side Nicky Jam's hair is unkept, and John's is done up very neatly. The third is dressed in a hoody and jeans, as is the fourth. Nicky Jam starts to deal the cards and everyone looks at their cards cautiously.] Nicky Jam: "Ok, antes up? John, Bill, Kory it's going to be 2 sets of 3.' [Finally we are able to identify all men in the room.] Nicky Jam: "Okay how many do you want?" John: "Two?" Bill: "One? no two" Kory: "One" Nicky Jam: "Dealer takes 2. Place your bets, starting with John." [An assortment of bills fly into the center of the table, then they each raise each other. Standard poker game.] [TIME PASSES] [The room is no longer filled with four friends rather 3 enemies and 1 loser. The amount of money in the middle of the table is an amazing amount compared to the amount that the first pile's size. From what we see the pot in the middle contains all but about 1 grand of the money, there is roughly 8 grand sitting dead smack in the center of the table. All the remaining competitors are looking at each other with pure hatred knowing that one of them is going to take home all the money. Nicky Jam looks at the others and then looks at his cards?] Nicky Jam: "How many do each of you want?" John: "One?" Bill: "Two?" Nicky Jam: "Dealer takes none." [A look of worry goes across Bill and John's face, but Nicky Jam's face remains the same it has the whole game? Leave your emotions at the door the first rule of poker.] Bill: "Screw this it's getting to hot for my blood I'm out, how am I going to explain $1,500.00 to my girl?" Nicky Jam: "Okay then, John you want to start the bets?" John: "Yeah I'll put in 100." [Nicky Jam looks at his cards and smirks, from what we see John has a flush in his hand, Nicky Jam's hand we aren't able to see however.] Nicky Jam: "See your three and raise you 300." John: "See your 300 and raise you 200." [Now there aren't any words, just money places in the middle. The bets are increasing 100 more, 200 more, 100 more, 200 more. Finally they're both out of money. Sweat is glistening on the top of John's brow. He looks at his cards one more time.] Nicky Jam: "I have no more money, but I'll place in here the keys to my car." [John grabs his keys from his pocket, he looks at the keys on the table the incredible amount of potentially lost money, is it worth it? He thinks to himself. Then he puts his keys back in his pocket. Puts his cards down face first to the table, looks at Nicky Jam?] John: "I fold, 9 grand is yours. F*ck! So tell me what did you have?" [Nicky Jam grabs the money, then stacks them into even piles and puts the piles into a bag, he smirks at his friends and they return the smile with looks of hate. Looks up at John and laughs.] Nicky Jam: "What I had, is irrelevant, I have the money, I walk away the winner, does it really matter how I won or how I beat you for your money?" Bill: "Whatever man...I'm leaving, Kory you want a ride?" [Without a word Kory and Bill leave the room, no doubt pissed off about their loss, and so it is, just John and Nicky Jam sitting alone at a dim lighted room. Nicky Jam looks at the money in the bag and smiles. He turns to John.] Nicky Jam: "It's only a matter of time John, until I'm back on my feet with money to my name, I'll be able to send the newspaper clips to my father to show him that I don't need his help, that I don't need anyone's help. This week is my big break man, I finally get a chance to prove myself in the ring, I can't wait. As for this PTC fellows, they're as good as dead, what the heck are they going to do against The Rodriguez Brothers, Limp and Boris Yeichstag?" [Nicky Jam reaches into a cooler beside him and grabs a frosty beverage.] Nicky Jam: "You want one?" John: "I promised my girlfriend I wouldn't, but I also promised her I wouldn't ever play poker again." [John laughs as Nicky Jam throws him a drink, he catches it and starts to drink. He grabs one for himself as well, opens it and takes a large swallow.] John: "Damn it Nicky Jam, what if this wrestling isn't for you man? What if you get fired? What'll you do for money? I got a job for you over at?" Nicky Jam: "Screw you man, I told you I don't want to be a stupid charity case, I didn't need my father's help and I sure as heck don't need your help. Look I've been in worse situations but I managed to come out on top right?" John: "Yeah but you've never been trying to make a name for yourself in PTC, this isn't the minors slick, this is the grand daddy of them all, I mean I want to have faith in you but heck, you're not exactly an experienced wrestler. (Looks at watch) Damn... I have to go now man, I needed to be home 5 minutes ago. So tell me, did I have you beat?" [Nicky Jam doesn't say anything, just smiles and takes a drink. John lets out a laugh and gets up, shakes hands with Nicky Jam.] John: "All right, you keep your damn secret, been a pleasure as always. Until that day." Nicky Jam: "Until that day." [With that John is gone, Nicky Jam grabs where he set his hand down and puts it face up, the camera zooms in to the cards. 2, 2, queen, 10, 9.] [Imagine that... he won 9 grand with 2 pair, Nicky Jam walks out of the room leaving us with the image of the cards on the lone table.] [Fade Out] Voice-over: "PTC, why is it that you're not taking UPWA seriously?" [Fade back In] [As the camera focus in we can see the rest of Nicky Jam's trailor, it's run down an old, but the items in the place however are very nice. The TV and DVD player are state of the art as are everything in the joint looks like it belongs in a wealthy man's home not in some rundown trailor park. Nicky Jam is seen leaning over the counter table in the kitchen admiring his very own piece of the world, even though to most of us ...it seems like crap; to Nicky Jam, it's his palace. Nicky Jam then looks at the camera and his face loses whatever happiness it once held.] Nicky Jam: "I've done all that I can do to try to get a little damn respect from you and every one else in PTC, but you don't realize this. Not only am I to be taken seriously but this week you're going to wish you had have listened to that little voice in your head saying, this guy's not to be taken lightly. I'm going to trance all over you this week. You just think I'm going to be another pushover that you can just breeze by until another victim is placed before you." [Nicky Jam takes a seat on a chair in the kitchen he puts his feet up on the counter and lets out a sigh of relief. Takes a second and then takes a drink. He puts his can beside him and looks at the camera.] Nicky Jam: "When you're looking up at me from the ground you will understand that I do mean business in PTC. But guys, how am I supposed to take you seriously? The majority of you hide in a blanket of darkness and utter mass words of confusion to anyone that will listen, I can't think of any other way to show a sure sign of fear." [Nicky Jam laughs to himself.] Nicky Jam: "PTC is still fairly new to me, I'm just now seeing UPWA's destiny, I on the other hand am destiny's abortion, the one it didn't want. I guess PTC and I are on different paths, it wants to achieve it's destiny and I want to run away from mine. This week it's just the small task of beating people up in the Survival PPV, next week...defending one of my titles and the small task of beating some other washed up wrestler who doesn't know the difference between a disgrace and a successful wrestler." [Nicky Jam gets a look of disgust on his face.] Nicky Jam: "When I come down to our match and make all of you lose, you will then become the reason people know my name, If it wasn't for you I wouldn't even be known. For that I will have to thank you. You will create a contagion and your creation is about to not only destroy you this week but humiliate you as well. The Mistake of one will cause pain and suffering to many as we bring the victory to UPWA." [With that Nicky Jam is done, he walks into another room and slams the door, we can hear the springs from his bed. The scene quickly dies and we fade to black.] [Fade Out.]
