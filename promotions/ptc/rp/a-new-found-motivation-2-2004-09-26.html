A New Found Motivation (2)

26 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-26 The tainted lives-- a poem by C. Shackleton Cruelty, dismay, and outright destruction, Termination of the lives of the innocent, A bomb here, a bomb there according to instruction, Millions of lives are thrown away with just one eruption. Not a war, nor conflict known can fathom this behavior, And without him it would be unknown, Shack the loyal savior. But lives have come and gone to pass, Without a word, and just a blast. But tonight we all will come to know. What Shackleton has in store. Satisfied with what my pen had created, I threw it down onto the desk, held up the parchment upon which I had written my most recent poem and then emerged myself into a state of deep thought. There had only been one point, one moment in the past where I had felt a sudden change in my emotional makeup. This was one of my first experiences with American business, American culture...I thought I had seen it all sitting on a deserted island, set apart from the rest of the world. How naiive could I have been? To look back at it all now seems immature, stupid, whatever you want to call it. But I am a constantly changing person, maybe it has something to do with my heritage...maybe it doesn't. But all I know is that the Christopher Shackleton that was on that Island 4 years ago, and the Christopher Shackleton that was on the mainland 3 years ago are different. Almost in the exact way that I, myself, right now am a different person than who you spoke to less than a week ago. Like I said before: I’m not about to lose this thing. Not at all. I know that confidence like this can’t be healthy, but maybe I should listen to Ricky. Maybe modesty isn’t the best policy. Maybe it’s time to get nasty. (RP ROUND 1) It seems like that intimidation got me somewhere, because look what happened...Salos? Nowhere to be found on the night of the match. I wonder what everyone thought of that? I wonder what Ricky Slickwater thought of that? Infact, let's find out for ourselves. With the push of a button, the most obnoxious face in wrestling appeared on my television screen. He looked at me from under his wide rimmed bucket cowboy hat and winked at the camera as the title music played. "Goooooooood afternoon America! This, as you all know, is Ricky's hour on national television. It's my time to rant, to rave and to save. Yes, you heard it right...this week is round two of the Global Title Tournament over at Primetime Central. Well whoop-de-doo, you all may be saying...but what I say is WOOOOOOOOWEEE! YEEHAW! I love me some tourn-e-ment action if I do say so myself" He once again stared into the camera with a slight pause. What. A. Scumbag. I thought to myself. Oh, and scumbag...such a great word. So...so much imagery involved in a simple phrase. One of the many perks of New York cab drivers. "I come to you live from New York City, but my heart is in my hometown of Memphis, Tennessee. A bit of a recap of last weeks tournament action...Tessa Windsor is knocked out surprisingly, The Shadow takes out Token Weed, and our good friend Christopher Shackleton advances due to a no-show from his opponent Jake Salos." A wide grin passed across Ricky's face. I knew what was coming. "Now, this leads me to my next question for all of you out there. Shackleton's victory: due to fear, or embarassment? Why should Salos step into the ring with someone as low down on the food chain as Shackleton? In my opinion, he was simply embarassed to even be spoken in the same sentence as him, as I apologize for doing right now Jake. However, Shackleton probably thinks it's because of his new 'persona'...A little advice Chris, the whole bad boy thing doesn't really work for a freakin' albino." There is a small chatter and laughter from the crowd. "I'll now be taking callers from all around the globe, tell me your opinion on this!" A phone ringing is heard in the background. "Hello? Sean from Georgia: your thoughts please." Ricky uttered. "Hey Ricky! Longtime fan here, love your show. Anyways, I think that punk Shackleton really needs to rethink his "new found confidence" I mean...give me a break...this kid has no idea what he's getting himself into. Who's he facing next round?" A short pause, and then Ricky answers "Token Weed, Sean". "Yeah," Sean continued "Token Weed...this guy is legendary. A member of the ideal four, a great champion in the Asylum, wrestled in Legacy of Champions, is doing great in tSC...I just don't see how Shackleton can build himself up for this match." "Thanks Sean, we have time for one more caller." Ricky cut him off. "Tina from San Francisco, your thoughts?" This was my chance, and in an attempt at pulling of a Californian womans voice...I was the one who was about to defend Christopher Shackleton. I was always good with other personas, but I had never tried Tina from San Francisco. "Hello? Tina? Have we lost you?" Ricky questioned into the phone. "AHEM" I answered. "No, no, I'm still here." My woman voice was terrible...but Ricky didn't notice. Apparently America sported some quite manly women. "So.." Ricky said. "Do you have something to contribute to the show?" Obviously I wasn't one of the more spirited guests he had ever had. So, pretending to swing back my long locks of hair, and pucker up my big red lips, I responded. "Why yes infact I do, Richard." Richard, oh how clever I was. "Christopher Shackleton is the man that you hate the most in the world, would you say this?" Ricky paused, I could see his pensive state on the television. "In the world? He could be. I highly dislike him to a huge extent." Ricky responded. "Well," I continued "Hate could be a strong word, Ricky. And as far as I know...you've never actually met him in the flesh, have you?" "W-well...no ma'am." I could tell he was beginning to become intimidated, I was possibly one of the first to ever go against him, live, on his own television show. "So then, Mr. Slickwater...I say you watch yourself. Look out this week in the GTT and you sit your fat self down on your sofa in wherever-the-hell Tennessee and you view first hand the true underdog story. Sure, Shackleton has little experience, but did you know he just signed with one of PTC's top feds, PRIME?" Ricky chuckled. "PRIME? PRIME is a good federation, Tina, but with all due respect, Token Weed has a far more impressive resume." I nearly screamed at him in my own voice, but caught myself just as I was able to contain my emotion. "Whether it be Ric Cronos, Ruben Ross, Alias, Rave Caprino, Erik Kelly, hell, Seymour Almasy for that matter...I don't think you can look down upon Shackleton. He's driven. He has a purpose." Ricky was confused by this, I had just rambled off some of the biggest names of all time. "And Tina, what might this purpose be...since you seem so close to him yourself?" "His motivation?" I answered. "He wants to be the best, he wants to prove everyone wrong who ever doubted him, he wants to show his dedication not only to the sport, but to victory, he wants to win and most importantly I" I had slipped, hmm...maybe he didn't notice. "...HE wants to show YOU that he's a lot better than how your talk show portrays him." "Ok..Tina was it?" He replied. "Yes." I answered. "Well, Tina, we've run out of time. Stay on the line to get a free t-shirt...and good luck in your match this weekend." With that, he winked at the camera. I had been found out. But maybe it was for the better...no, no it had to be for the worse. Now he knew, now he had seen that I actually cared about his perception of me. Shit. I mean, darn. What is an Antarctican to do? I know. I know what an Antarctican is to do. WIN. The idea of losing my cover, losing my matches... losing anything was something familiar to me. And to what I was elaborating on before, about change in personality...it all seems to come with one thing, a common archetype: paradise lost. Paradise was lost when Matthews put me out, many thought it was for my career. BOOM. Paradise was lost when everyone doubted me against someone like Jake Salos. BOOM. Paradise was lost when Ricky Slickwater belittled, insulted and down-graded me on national television. BOOM. Paradise was lost for the first time...when I realized what I had been conned into. When I realized that I wasn't a personality, or a face, or a great wrestler...but when I realized that all I was at the beginning of my career was a gimmick. Flashback: September 2001 Not the same ship that was described by my ancestors, but a larger and more powerful one, driven by a large humming sound came speeding into Shackleton Isle. There was a huge coloured flag on the top that read 'National Geographic'. A walkway sprouted from the ship and a man with a fairly large black device stepped down from the ship, looking around curiously. He seemed to be holding it up to his eye and a snapping noise came from it. I approached him, like my ancestors had with Shackelton before, and spoke to him. 'Hello.' I said, he looked at me with a wide-eyed face and called for his assistants. 'HELLO! I am James Scheifer with National Geographic, we were informed that there is a village here and wanted to take photos of it.' I was very confused about what he was trying to say until he began pointing at the black thing in his hand. Photos must have had something to do with that. 'What is a photo?' I said to him. He chuckled and showed me one form his pocket. It was amazing, a scene of a sunny beach with bright colors, it reminded me of the stories about Chile that I had heard form my relatives. I then led him into the main building where some traditional Antarctican Martial Arts fights were going on. Apparently the magazine had found out about the islets own type of wrestling 'Jamestyle'. I was the reigning champion of my weight class. The style consists of grappling and a bit of martial arts and capuera. It is very stylish. After they found out I was the champion the man expressed I was what they had been looking for. After interviewing and taking pictures of the other kids they took me, a 28-year-old man from my home. I wasn’t quick to go, but they told me about the wonders of the world outside of here. My family knew I would come back, and once I got there the man said I would make a fortune doing what I loved so I could give my family money and bring them to my new home. My new home would be in a country called the United States of America. I finally decided that I would go. They took me on a barge and I got to see about 30 minutes of Chile, my home country. We got into a loud and quite large structure they kept calling a car, it was a very useful but unique contraption. The beauty of it was breathtaking until we got to the city, lights everywhere and high buildings across the skyline. This was all coming at me too fast and I did not know how to do anything or what to expect. There were people with strange clothes while I was in my native moccasins and skins, the men stopped in front of a large building and came out with some of the strange clothing. They made me put on 'jeans' and a 't-shirt’ and we then came to a wider building and parked somewhat near it and walked in. There were people conversing in many different languages and it was a very diverse place, I walked with the men until they got to a place where there were many seats and somewhere I heard 'Final boarding call for flight 677 out of Chile to New York' after hearing that the men rushed through a small tunnel and into something that was a bit like the 'car' but much larger. From there, the man explained to me what would happen. Millions of dollars would be offered to me in American currency, just for me to participate in a wrestling league. How hard could that be? I asked myself. I took him up on the offer, signing my name on the dotted line towards the bottom of the contract that he had handed me. How would it feel to go from being one with nature, one with my kind…to one of the richest and most famous people on the face of this earth? All of the money, all of the fame…for what? A few minutes every week in a ring? I had to try it; how could I resist. Days Later There was a man sitting behind a desk in a high chair, I was standing outside the office. Shortly before they summoned me I had overheard him talking a little bit to someone who seemed to be his assistant. He said to her “The albino gimmick is here, bring him in”. I didn’t understand what he meant, or who he was talking about, but shortly afterwards the door was opened and I was called in to sit down in front of the massive desk. I stared the man in the eye and he looked me over with a smile of approval. “You’ll work perfectly into our forthcoming storyline, Mr. Shackleton” he said to me. Storyline? I thought. “Sir, what do you mean, storyline? I thought this was…erm…wrestling.” “Yes, yes it is.” He replied hastily. “However, part of American wrestling these days is the entertainment factor. We always have to keep the crowd, our fans on their toes. And you my friend, will make a great partner to Gunga Idabe, our African wrestler who is in need of a tag team partner. I call the name of the team: CONTRAST. Just imagine, one man, who is TOTALLY black…and you, who are pardon me, TOTALLY white. Perfect, perfect.” Now everything seemed to be falling into place. I, Christopher Shackleton was being used, for millions and millions of dollars mind you…but I was being used. “Your debut is this Saturday” Said he as I walked out the door, I barely heard him at all…there was too much going through my mind. “Knock ‘em dead!” he then hollered as the door slammed behind me. So here began my fall from grace. It took 20-something years for me to realize how great it was that I was surrounded by a loving family, but I always hoped for more. Now that I am here, at the top of it all…nothing seems better than my home; my freezing cold, sub-zero, miserable…but loving home. My paradise. Back to present day, New York City. Out the window, atop a fairly tall building adjacent to my lonely hotel, which was my headquarters during this tournament, stood a billboard with the face of Seymour Almasy on it. Underneath his face, with expressions of anger and hatred were El., Doctor Curiosity, Karina Wolfenden, Token Weed, and John Winters. This got me to thinking, what is it that I, Christopher Shackleton have to do in order to be placed amongst these men and women. Where do I stand in the grand scheme of things? How unclear things are after you advanced due to a fluke, do to a no-show. How I wished that Salos had showed up at the ring that night so I could have shown the world my new persona, so I could show the world my new anger. It's funny how things go...When Chris Matthews put together that move to send me to the hospital, he was doing it for a win. But with me right now, it's a lot bigger than that. It's something more meaningful...I now know that with every punch, with every toss, with every win I gain more respect. That is what I'm out for. VICTORY and RESPECT. Two things that I haven't seen much of over my short lived career. So, when I pick up Token Weed...and when I throw him into the hard surface of that wrestling mat, his face encrusted into the three letters which mean so much "G.T.T."...then I will be satisfied. Actually, scratch that...satisfaction will only come on my way out of the arena. On my way out when I can walk up to someone like Ricky Slickwater, or all of those puny analysts who put me down in the loss column, and not be violent at all. All I'll have to do is look someone like that in the eye, stare them down...and show them who I really am. No longer is Christopher Shackleton the smiling face that brings all the joy of seeing an albino on a weekly basis. No, those days are long gone. Now, he is a frowning face, a grimacing face, out to prove one thing: his own credebility. So, to all the doubters who still thinking he is a walking, living gimmick. Please, feel free to tune in this week, it would be to mine and your advantage.
