Here's to you Argyle… PTC…

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2005-11-29 (A lone spotlight is centered on the middle of a red curtain, atop a stage with a single microphone stand. Out from the back and into the light steps the familiar figure of Mr. Brian Arson. He smiles and grabs the microphone from the stand) Brian: So I got to thinking the other day, and I was thinking, how much Primetime Central has given to me. I was thinking how selfish can I be not to give back to the community that has given me so much, including ample opportunities to express my opinion to the Recycle Bin, multiple bannings, and an everlasting, special friendship with a wonderful girl. Yeah, you guessed it… I’m talking about you Kat. (Arson blows out a kiss accompanied by a wink) Brian: And for such a special occasion, I’d like to introduce you to the Arsonesttes… (The curtain is pulled up and four handsome looking men with tightly cut and greased up early-60s hairdos are seen standing right behind Mr. Arson) Brian: So Argyle… Primetime Central… this one’s for you! Who loves you pretty baby, Who's gonna help you through the night? Who loves you pretty mama, Who's always there to make it right? Who loves you pretty baby, Who's gonna help you through the night? Who loves you pretty mama, Who's always there to make it right? Who loves youuuuu, Who loves you pretty baby? Who's gonna love you mama? Who loves youuuuu, Who loves you pretty baby? When tears are in your eyes, And you can't find the way. It's hard to make believe, You're happy when you're gray. Baby when you're feelin' like, You'll never see the mornin' light. Come to me, Baby, you'll see. Who loves you pretty baby, Who's gonna help you through the night? Who loves you pretty mama, Who's always there to make it? Who loves youuuuu, Who loves you pretty baby? Who's gonna love you mama? Who loves youuuuu, Who loves you pretty baby? And when you think, The whole wide world has passed you by. You keep on tryin', But you really don't know why. Baby when you need a smile, To help the shadows drift away. Come to me, Baby, you'll see. Who loves you pretty baby, Who's gonna help you through the night? Who loves you pretty mama, Who's always there to make it? Who loves youuuuu, Who's gonna love you, love you? Who's gonna love you? Who loves youuuuu, Who's gonna love you, love you? Who's gonna love you? (Cue instrumental break) Brian: Argyle… I want to thank you… I honestly didn’t think you’d actually let me into the tournament, but oh, you did… you’re a good man. That’s possibly the dumbest or perhaps the best thing you could’ve ever done for me. Usually I'd go with the former, but let’s be optimists here, it's the holiday season for Christ's sakes! Baby, Baby, Doot-doo-doot. Come to me, Baby, you'll see. Who loves you pretty baby, Who's gonna help you through the night? Who loves you pretty mama, Who's always there to make it? Who loves youuuuu, Who's gonna love you, love you? Who's gonna love you? Who loves youuuuu, Who's gonna love you, love you? Who's gonna love you?
