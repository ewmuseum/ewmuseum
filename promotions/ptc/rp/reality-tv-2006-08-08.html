Reality TV

8 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2006-08-08 It's not easy being a pirate. And for the reasons you'd expect. It's not having to deal with other pirates or going spelunking on adventures in the Caribbean. The problems are much different. Ever since that stupid Pirates of the Caribbean movie came out, all people can do to the adorable Captain Jack Morgan is point and gawk at him. It's like these spoiled yuppie larvae have nothing better to do with their time than make fun of him for being different. It's not easy being a clown, either. But it's not easy for the same reasons. Wherever the Killer Klown goes, people point and gawk at him just like they do to Jack. Kids always come up to him and ask him to make them balloon animals or get scared and run away. That's why him and Jack get along so well. They go through the same exact problems. But all of that changed one day when a man representing a broadcasting network came to their pirate ship the Black Nemo one day and offered them a hefty sum to film their daily activities as part of a reality show called "Pirating with Clowns!" Jack and the clown thought it was a cool idea at the time, but not anymore. Now, they're sitting on their couch looking at porn on the TV with the aluminum foil covered rabbit ears on the top of it as the cameras shine on their faces. "GAHHH!" JAck exclaims as he motions for the camera to get the light out of his eyes. "Watch it! Watch it!" Jack rubs his eyes in pain as the cameraman simply says "Sorry" in response to his collosal blunder. Meanwhile, the producer is getting impatient with them. His grand vision of a show is becoming more like a crappy remake of "Married with Children" than a reality show about a clown and a pirate causing havoc at various undisclosed locations around the world. So, unbeknownst to his stars, he got them both jobs working at a very special place. "Hey guys," the producer says to them. "The network executives want more excitement from you guys and are growing a bit impatient because all you've done for three days is sit on teh couch with your hands down your pants watching pornography." Jack smiles at him. His little smirk gives off a sense that Jack knows exactly what he is doing and all of this is really a test of the network's patience. "So, we got you guys a job." Jack and the clown look at each other and then look back at the producer dumbfounded. "A job?" The producer nods his head and then looks down at his watch. "And according to my watch," he continues. "You two have only forty minutes to leave here and go to work." The producer laughs as he gets up and turns to leave the ship. Jack gets up and yells, "What kind of job did you get us?" * * * An hour later, Jack and the Killer Klown are staring blankly into space while wearing their really stupid looking work uniforms. You see, the producer got them a job working at a McDonald's. Once arriving there, they both got their uniforms and were told to get to work. Now, they are just staring off into space. Jack and the clown continue to doze off when a customer comes up and starts pounding his fist on the counter in front of them. Startled, they quickly get up and look around. "Huh?" Jack asks in a daze. "I'd like some service here!" the older looking man says to them. He is balding and is clearly pushing sixty. Jack smiles at him with his yellowy, unkept pirate teeth. "How may I help you sir?" Jack asks. "I've been waiting for service for more than half an hour!" the man angirly shouts out. "No you haven't you old geezer," Jack replies. The man looks angry. "What did you call me young feller?" the older man asks Jack. Jack smiles deviously at him. "I called you an old geezer, mate," Jack says with a grin. "Now why don't you run along back to the old folks home where you belong. Maybe an alien pod like 'Cocoon' will land in your hospice's home and you can live for a thousand earth years before you finally die." The old man raises his cane in the air. Jack and the clown eye the cane, looking unafraid and quite amused at the same time as the camera crew continues to film this utter nonsense behind them. "You see this sonny?" the man asks Jack. "This is going to be shoved up your young ass if you don't stop pissing me off!" Jack and the clown look at each other and start laughing their asses off. The old man has no idea why they are laughing at him. "What's so funny young fellers?" the man asks. "You afraid an old man might kick your asses, eh? Is that it?" Jack and the clown stop laughing and look at each other. "Hey, Old Man Winter," Jack replies with a chuckle. "Don't break your hip while your swinging that thing." The old man growls angrily. "YOU!" The old man lunges at Jack and puts him in a choke hold. Jack is choking and trying to break the man's grip to no avail. The clown grabs a hold of Jack's waist and starts pulling him backward but he is having no luck either. The old man's grip is too strong. The clown then gets an idea. He lets go of Jack and then grabs a canister of ketchup. He then turns around and squeezes the canister so that all of it's contents go right into the man's eyes! The man holds his eyes in pain. "ARGH!" the old man exclaims as he grabs his cane. The clown pulls Jack out of harm's way as the man starts swining wildly with his cane as the Killer Pirates cower in fear over by where the food comes out. "I'm going to get you!" the old amn exclaims. "YOU DAMN FOOLS! I'M GOING TO GET YOU BOTH!" The old man swings again and just barely misses them again as their boss Belinda comes out of the office in the back to see what the fuss is all about. "What the hell is going on here?" she asks Jack and the clown as they try to protect themselves from the cane. Jack turns to Belinda, who is a thirty year old Italian brunette who would be cute if she wasn't so premenstrual, and smiles. "Ma'am," he says calmly as he motions toward the customer. "The man attacked us, so we hid." Belinda then looks over and sees the red ketchup all over the man's face. She points to the ketchup and glares at them both. "What is that?" she asks in an angry tone. "Is that blood all over his face?" Jack and the clown look at each other before shaking their heads in unison. "No," the Killer Klown says with a thick alien accent. "It's only ketchup." Belinda puts her hands on her hips. "Only ketchup?" she asks in a harsh tone. "Why the hell did you idiots squirt ketchup in his face?" Jack motions to the clown. "He did it," Jack says calmly. The clown looks at him and growls. Jack hesistantly looks up at him and smiles innocently. "Is this true?" Belinda asks, crossing her arms and glaring at them some more. The clown finally concedes and nods his head. "Yes I did," the clown says hesitantly. "But he (motions toward Jack) was the one who instigated it." Jack looks up at the clown who smiles at him deviously. Jack gulps. Belinda shakes her head and goes to help the man. "You two are unbelievable." She goes over and tries to console the man and help him. "Are you okay?" she asks in a caring tone. The man then swings his cane and it hits her in the head! Jack and the Killer Klown both cringe at once. The old man then wipes the ketchup out of his eyes and says, "I'm going to sue all of your asses" as he heads to the door and exits the door. Jack and the clown look at each other. "Uh…" Jack mutters. "I need to take a piss," the clown says as he then turns and heads to the bathroom. "Uhhh…me too," Jack says as he runs after him. Both of them walk over their boss Belinda and run to the bathroom as fast as they can to hide from her before she wakes up. * * * Later on, as usual, the store is empty so now is as good of a time as any for the Pirates to talk about their upcoming PTC vs. AM Tag Team Championship match…or not. "Did you see that red head in that movie Ricky Bobby when we saw it?" Jack asks the clown. The Killer Klown licks his lips and smiles. "Oh yeah," the clown replies. "I'd shag her like the vixen she is." "As the Misogynist used to say, I'd fuck her into oblivion," Jack says much to the clown's amusement. "I'll pull out my pirate sword and show her what the Tortuga Tango is all about!" The clown looks perplexed. "What the hell is that? Some sort of dance move?" Jack shakes his head. "Nah," he replies. "It's a sexual position invented in Tortuga. You see, she has to get her leg above your head and then you bend your leg like this (Jack demonstrates for the clown what to do) and then you put her hands behind her head and you put your hands at equadistant positions on her body with your leg bent straight next to her body." "Wow, that's complicated," the clown replies. Jack nods his head. "Especially for you," he replies. "You're no rocket scientist, you know." The clown raises an eyebrow. "Yes I am!" he exclaims. "I built my own spaceship, didn't I?" Jack acknolwedges him. "Good point," he responds. "But have you ever done five women at the same time?" The clown and the pirate share an uncomfortable glance. "FuCK YOU!" the clown responds as he flips off Jack. Jack smiles, amused by this reaction. "I got you beat, mate," Jack says cockily. "That doesn't mean anything," the clown growls. Jack smiles deviously, relishing this moment of tormenting his teammate. "Yes it does," Jack replies. "It means I'm better than you because I got you beat." The clown growls at him and that's when they hear customers entering the store. Jack looks over and rolls his eyes. "Oh God," he says with sarcastic disdain. "Yuppies." The clown looks over and sees five teenage suburban yuppies walking into the resteraunt. The yuppie teenagers look over and see them. Then, to the disdain of the Killer Pirates, they start pointing and laughing at them. "Look at the clown!" one of them yells out. Another one points at Jack. "Look! One of them is a pirate too!" Now, all of the yuppies are laughing. Jack and the Killer Klown look at each other. They then point to the teenagers and start laughing. "Look clown! It's a bunch of teenage losers whose balls haven't dropped yet!" The Killer Pirates start laughing and the teenagers don't look pleased. "HEY!" one of them says angrily. "Who are you two to make fun of anybody?" The clown points to them. "Look Jack!" he exclaims. "It's Team Baked!" Jack laughs and nods his head. "Yeah stoners," he replies. "What are you going to do? Let a big clown and a swashbuckling pirate make fun of you for looking like idiots?" "No we're not!" one of the yuppies squeaks out. "We're…we're…" Jack glares at him. "We're what?" Jack asks with a growl. "We're going to kick your asses!" the teenager blurts out. Jack and the clown look at each other before they start laughing hysterically. "I'm more scared of the Furious Fists of God than you five dickwads!" Jack says with a laugh. One of the teenagers steps up to the counter. "You wanna go, boi?" he asks. "Nobody talks shit about the Furious Fists, understand, G? Huh boi? You understand me?" Jack starts giggling. "Hey MC Hammer," Jack says with a chuckle. "Me and my homie G will be all over you like a pitbull on a poodle, understand? We'll take you down faster than you can spell supercalifragilisticexpealadocious!" The teenager looks at his friends and starts trying to spell it out. "Super…cali…frag…a-licious…experience…uhhhh…," he says before pausing. He then gets frustrated and flips off Jack. "Fuck you pirate!" he angrily yells out. "Watch you gonna do, huh punk? Huh? We'll rip you and your gay clown friend apart!" The hip-hop wanna-be yuppie then spits on Jack's face. Jack laughs and then wipes the spit off his face. "You might not realize it yet," he says as he pulls out his pirate sword and the Killer Klown pulls out his clown hammer. "But the Grim Reaper's come to visit you tonight." The teenagers run and scream as Jack and the clown start chasing them around the McDonald's. The clown swings his hammer at one of them but destroys a table instead! Jack goes after the teenager who he called "MC Hammer" and tries to grab him but the teenager eludes his grasp and Jack runs into a pillar over by the counter. Jack then staggers around almost haphazardly and seems almost drunk as the cameras for their TV show keep filming all of this madness. Jack then shakes it off and turns around to see the clown chasing the five teenagers into the McDonald's PlayPlace. Jack looks on in disbelief. "Oh bugger," he says. Jack then runs after them and climbs into the PlayPlace. The clown is struggling to get through as the teenagers head for the ballroom, which then leads up to the slide. Jack jumps into the balls and starts trying to grab the teenagers. Two of them make it out and go down the slide. Jack grabs one of them and tackles them down into the colorful rainbow colored balls in the playroom! The other two teenagers who are still in there see this and jump on Jack! The clown continues to try and free himself as the teenagers try to escape from Jack. They get out of the ballroom and head up toward the slide as Jack finally swims out of the ballroom and goes after them. Jack then sees them sliding down the slide. Jack runs and jumps up to where the slide is. He then sits down and slides himself down to the bottom as the teenagers run out of the store. The clown finally frees himself and goes to help Jack chase them out. They raise their weapons high into the air. "GARR***" Jack exclaims. "And don't come back you fucking shitheads!" They then turn around and see their manager Belinda, who has bandages on her head from their previous little incident, standing behind them. Her arms are crossed and she glares at them both. Jack and the clown look shocked to see her. "Oh bugger," Jack says before cheesily smiling at his boss. And that's when the TV cameras run out of tape. (/To be continued…/)
