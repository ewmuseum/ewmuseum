Blinded by the Light

23 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-23 “If you don’t know, after 20 minutes of play, who the sucker at the poker table is, it’s you.” - Unknown Everything in Joey’s apartment was layered by an even coat of dust – just the way he liked it. He’d once heard a comic note that guys need to live in dust so that they remember where everything goes – he liked that saying a lot and if questioned about the questionable nature of his apartment, would steal it and pawn it off on his own that evening. Joey opened the fridge. 48 bottles of Miller High Life… that should be enough to cover him… check. A six-pack of panty wasters (i.e. Wine Coolers) for Tony Rolo… check. 4 Mickey’s Ice 40s and orange juice in case he wanted to make a poor-man’s brass monkey… T.Roc would have to appreciate the gesture… check. One bottle of everclear for Ian English… check. The brit was a notorious lightweight and Joey intended to produce some comedy with the liquor, as well as increase his personal finances. This night might not be so bad after all. The week had been a drain on Joey – forced integration with his Prime “teammates.” To him, it was equivalent of looking at naked pictures of Fran Drescher – a bit of a novelty at first, but once the thrill of the novelty had faded away, all that remained was a wildebeest with an unnecessarily hairy cooch. Since these were the breaks, and he was forced to hang out with his gangly nemesis, Ian English, he had decided that there was only one reasonable way to proceed – drink beer and take his money. Joey took a deep breath before opening the closet door. Somewhere Jerry Seinfeld was heard saying “Release the hoooounds!” as a flood of old pornography and randomly seldom used sports items fell on top of Joey and out of the closet. The sight was amusing to him until he realized he’d have to manage to get all this shit back in. Waste deep in what seemed to be an infinite sea of bad pornography, Joey waded through – bypassing the entire 69 tape series of “49er Girls go an Extra 20” to find one of Joey’s lost treasures. Pulling the felt matted table out of the closet, Joey hoisted the large unit on top of his sad looking folding card table to instantaneously transform it into a felt layered poker table with chip slots and drink holders to boot. **** The first knock at the door… 8:57 pm. Joey opened the door and both T.Roc and Tony Rolo were standing before him. Joey Troy: Are you guys dating now? Tony Rolo: Fuck you. We just showed up at the same time. T.Roc just brushes the comment off. A week of hanging out with Troy had taught him that sometimes it was better just to ignore Joey. Joey Troy: So Roc, what do you think of the place? Roc surveys Joey’s apartment. Clothes and dust everywhere, cracks in the ceiling, bars on the windows, and the sound of sirens outside. T.Roc: I think you live in the fucking hood. Joey Troy: Thanks man. That really means a lot coming from you. ***SCREECH*** ***BANG*** ***CRASH*** Voice: Facking muppets. Yer driving on the wrong side ‘of the road. Enter Ian English. The limey parked his beat up old Aston Martin on the curb outside Joey’s apartment complex – a car in such bad shape that the homeless guys outside who offer to wash your windshield for a dollar don’t even bother to get off their stoop. The car is old because well, its old. However, the various dents and dings in the appropriately licensed “14N 3NG715H” Aston Martin come from the fact that English has refused to accept the American custom of driving on the right (directional) side of the road, which coincidentally is the wrong side of the road. ***** Several hours later, the group are huddled around the poker table. English is leaning back in his chair, apparently trying to finagle a card that he stuffed up his sleeve out. Roc has embraced Joey’s ignorance and is sipping on one of the 40s out of the fridge. Rolo is rubbing his forehead trying to figure out how a dumbass like Joey was taking all of his money. The silence was broken… Joey Troy (singing): Blinded by the Li-ight! Heeeeld up like a loofa by the foreman of the night! Blinded by the Li-ight… held up… Tony Rolo: What in the holy name of fuck are you singing? Joey Troy: Its that song, “Blinded by the Light,” you dumbass. T. roC: (mumbling) Damned white people and their 80s music. Ian English: Oi? Tony Rolo: I know the song, dumbass, but what the fuck were you singing. Joey looks at Tony, in disbelief. Joey Troy (singing): BLINDED BY THE LIGHT! Tony Rolo: Yes, I know that part… what did you say was next. Joey Troy (singing): HELD UP LIKE A LOOFA BY THE FOREMAN OF THE NIGHT! T. roC raises both eyebrows, to avoid any accusations of trademark infringements. Tony Rolo: Are you high? Wait, don’t answer that. Held up like a loofa? Joey, you’re a fucking ‘tard. Joey Troy: Yeah, you know, like a loofa sponge. Ian English: Oi, I knew this muppet was a poof – e’s probably bin rubbin’ a sponge up his arse before we got here. Tony Rolo: Wait, just to play devil’s advocate here. Who exactly is holding up the loofa sponge? Joey Troy (trying to remember the lyrics he sang): The foreman of the night. Tony Rolo: Uh huh, and why is the “foreman of the night” (Tony makes the mock quotations in the air) holding up this loofa sponge, you fucking tard and a half? Joey Troy: Dude, I don’t know. I didn’t write the song. Maybe he’s trying to make a political statement or something. T. roC gets up, mumbling softly something inaudible followed by “damn white people”. Tony Rolo: Goddamn Joey, this might even be more retarded than the time you told me that if you play Beatles albums backwards, it tells you to worship the devil. Joey Troy: Hey! That shit is true. Not only that, but if you play it backwards in low-speed, it tells you to shave your cat and mail it to China. But I digress, if my version is wrong, then how the fuck does the song go? Tony Rolo: I know how it goes. I’m not gonna fucking singing it for you though. Joey Troy: Bullshit. You don’t know the words either. Tony Rolo: Yes, I do. Joey Troy: Prove it. Tony Rolo: Fine… the song goes (pause while Tony finds the tune… then singing) Blinded by the Light… Ian English: This is getting redundant. Tony Rolo (singing): Ripped up like a douche, you’re gonna throw away the night. Joey Troy: Ripped up like a douche??? I’m not the ‘tard here, douche-ripper. Tony Rolo: Yes, a douche. Haven’t you ever listened to the song? It clearly says “ripped up like a douche.” Ian English: ‘Ow do youse rip up a douche? For once, Joey Troy and Ian English are in complete agreement, with Joey nodding his head as if what a relevant question. Tony Rolo: I don’t think they’re talking about a douche like a hose that a chick sticks up her cooter to clean and excavate it. It’s a French reference to a “douche” meaning “small French bath towel.” Yeah, I think I saw it on vh1’s behind the music – its about this poor homeless woman lying in a ditch, with just a small French bath towel – a douche - to protect her from the night. Blinded by the Light, Ripped up like a douche, you’re gonna throw away the night. Joey Troy: Dude, I don’t know – I think my loofa-holding foreman of the night is a much more intelligent postulate than your douche-ripping whore in a ditch. Ian English: All those bullfrogs arses youse two ‘ave been lickin to get high have rotted yer brains. Tony Rolo: Like you know how the fucking song goes moss-mouth! You wouldn’t be bothered to listen to or know any American music. English, seeing the opportunity to fuck with Troy and Rolo, will always oblige. Ian English: Normally, I wouldn’t arse myself with this shite, but I happen to know the exact lyrics to this ‘ere song. Joey Troy: And they are? English clears his throat and then does a couple of practice singing notes which are so badly off any semblance of a tune, they’d make a deaf man be happy to be so afflicted. Ian English (singing, sort of): Blinded by the light… Rimmed up like a docient thru the humble of the might. English nods, proud that he made his way through the bs’ing. Tony Rolo: Rimmed up like a docient? Joey Troy: Wait, did he just say he rimmed a guy named doc? That’s fucking sick. Tony Rolo: Nice try slack jaw, that didn’t make any goddamned sense. Joey Troy: You’re one to talk douche-boy Ian English: Youse wankas want to make everything about butt sex. The song actually goes… The three men are now talking over each other, and all break into tune, singing their incorrect versions of the song at the top of their lungs… Troy/Rolo/English (simultaneously): BLINDED BY THE LI-IGHT! Troy: HELD UP LIKE A LOOFA BY THE FOREMAN OF THE NIGHT Rolo: RIPPED UP LIKE A DOUCHE, YOU’RE GONNA THROW AWAY THE NIGHT English: RIMMED UP LIKE A DOCIENT THROUGH THE HUMBLE OF THE MIGHT! On the second verse, they all continue singing, with Joey hopping up onto his feet and then onto his chair, screaming out the chorus of the song. The juvenile scene finally comes to an end with a loud… SMASH!!! T. roC has thrown an empty 40oz onto the ground of Joey’s apartment, smashing it into a million pieces. His nerves have snapped as he looks up at the three in front of him. T. roC: THE SONG IS… Blinded By The Light, R-a-c-k-e-d up like a DEUCE, another ROLLER in the night. Joey looks as if he’s about to say something and RoC turns right to him. T. roC: There are no Loofa sponges!!! (then towards Rolo) T. roC: There are no ripping of douches!!! And then to English… T. roC: And whatever the fuck you said, there’s none of that either! We got three of the whitest people I’ve ever met, arguing like a bunch of children over what the words to a ridiculous song are. And the irony of the situation is that it takes a black man to sort out this soft roCk bullshit for you. Goddamn man, I’m fed up with this shit. RoC boots the top of the poker table halfway across the room. T. roC: You clowns better bring you’re A-games for this pay-per-view. I’m not going to go out there and be made fun of by a bunch of imbeciles. RoC makes a b-line for the door. Just before shutting it, he looks over the group. T. roC: Damn white people. He slams the door. English, Troy and Rolo all look at the carnage, which truthfully, sort of fits the white-ghetto-trash motif of Joey’s apartment and then back at each other with inquisitive looks on their faces. Ian English: Only a poof would know the real words to that piece of shite of a song. Tony Rolo: No shit. Joey Troy: Yeah, someone seemed a little hot under the colander. English and Rolo both look at Joey as if he’s retarded. Tony Rolo: Hot under the what? Ian English: Facking Muppet. Joey Troy: What? What did I say? …and the cycle repeats itself.
