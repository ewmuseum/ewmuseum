The Crusade

20 October 2006

Date: 2006-10-20 October 1st, 2005 The man took the case out of his shirt pocket and opened it. He took out a cigar and smelled it before cutting it with the cigar cutter. He fixed the black beret he wore on his head as he waited in a small waiting room. The door opened and a very good looking young secretary motioned him to go inside. He fixed his guayabera and put on his game face. A meeting with the boss was not something he could take for granted. He stepped into the office and saw the boss sitting on a very expensive looking chair. The boss beckoned to him and signaled for him to sit on one of the chairs in front of his desk. Behind the boss, several monitors showed the news from around the world. The boss had become a very good businessman not just because he had the tongue for it, but because he actually immersed himself anytime he was dealing with foreign businesses. The boss showed no sign of emotion to someone like Alejandro Castro. He was still just starting to rise in the organization and he was surprisingly called to the boss’ office so quickly. Alejandro was afraid in a way. He was going back through all the things he had done for the organization and sifting through it all to see if he had made some kind of mistake that was punishable. The boss took the man in, similar to the way a wine connoisseur smells the wine, looks at its distinctive color and checks for consistency before actually tasting it. He was not a young guy by the standards of an organization like this, and he showed it. The scar on the side of his face was a great intimidation tool he used whenever possible. Right now, it made him seem older and wiser than he really thought he was. When he wanted, thought the boss, he could bring fear into his enemy’s souls. He analyzed Alejandro’s demeanor and even though he seemed a bit scared, he hid it well. This was the kind of man that the boss needed. "How long have you been working for the organization?" asked the boss in what Alejandro recalled was a tone of voice that not only made him feel comfortable but it was almost father like in a way. "I have been working for this organization for five years now, sir," Alejandro answered proudly. Thanks to him, a lot of the organization’s plans had come to fruition. Anything that was asked of him, he did and he did well. In his thirty five years of life, he had never felt as good at what he was doing as he did now. Sure he wasn’t a tuxedo wearing millionaire, but he was the best bet if you wanted the job to get done. Only a few people actually knew who the boss was. Those who knew were the top guys in the organization. Not a lot of people were trusted to keep secrets like these. Secrets are a mayor part in any association, group, whatever you want to call it. Without secrets, things don’t get done. There needs to be a level of mystery to any movement that was not entirely legal, and even the legal ones have shady dealings most of the times. "A very productive five years, Alejandro. I have to say that this organization and I are very proud of what you have accomplished so far for us, but what I am about to ask you is something far more difficult and far more dangerous than anything you have ever done for us," the boss said inching a little closer to the desk. "The time for our organization to act is near. We have lain dormant for far too long, and the attack on our way of thinking, on our traditions that the federal government committed on the holiday of our forefathers must be avenged," the boss said. "I know, sir. I believe in our cause and I think that they must pay for their actions. They think that just because we are not acknowledged globally as a threat that they can just come over here and ruin our local holiday by murdering one of us? They will pay," Alejandro said proudly. "That is why we want you for this mission. You have the passion for the organization. You believe in our cause and are not in it for money or whatever else kids these days want." The organization had been running for years looking for one thing, the independence of Puerto Rico from what they called American colonialism. They argued that the United States had made Puerto Rico into just another market for them. It made the island so dependent on American products that more than ninety percent of college students used federal loans in order to pay their tuition and they ruined the little agriculture that the island actually had. It was hard for the organization to run because the minds of the people had been poisoned, according to them, into believing that US occupation of the island was a good thing. Now that the US economy was in shambles and now that they had made a mess in Iraq, to the organization, this was the time to act. The US was spread too thin across the world. "We need you to take the position of leader of the organization," the boss said calmly, knowing what the reaction would be. "Me? I can’t possibly be the one in charge of the organization… I… I don’t know what to say," he said, looking flustered. "Do not worry; you will not really be the leader. You will be the face behind the face. Everyone thought that Filiberto was the leader but after the unfortunate events of last week, we need someone to be the face. They think that with his death, the organization is in turmoil, but they don’t know how deep it goes. We need you to be what Filiberto was." He sat back and fixed his grey hair while he waited for Alejandro’s answer. He knew what the answer would be. The man was full of fervor and he would never let an opportunity like this go by. He could not, by principle, decline the offer. Not only was this a chance to do something immensely vital for the organization that he loved, but this was also a way for him to further his vision of what Puerto Rico should be. "I think you know what my answer is already, sir. I only have one question though." "Ask away." "Is there anything planned? The fact that you are asking this of me leads me to the conclusion that there is something planned. What do I need to do to make this plan work?" The boss laughed. He had known this was the best choice. His advisors had told him that he needed to pick one of the old guard, but he knew that those men’s way of thought were too old fashioned. They needed a good shot of youth. This young man was the perfect choice. "Yes. We have something planned, but it will go down a year from now. There is one thing that we need to make it work though. Actually, he is more a person than a thing. I need you to gather as much information about him as you can and I need you to prepare the troops and when I give you the call, you need to get that person and make him comply with our wishes." "Who is this person?" "My son." ------- October 15th, 2006 Digital Mortality took a sip from the water bottle that lay right besides him. The grueling training was over for the day and it could be seen by the flushness of Digital’s face that his loss on last week’s Fusion had made him remember that training was the most important part of an athlete’s daily life. He made a fool out of himself when he lost in his first dark match. His training partner and long time friend was one of the fastest rising stars in the professional wrestling scene in Puerto Rico. Nick also worked as a bouncer in Digital’s bar whenever his schedule let him. He was much bigger than Digital but he was agile and that helped whenever Digital was facing bigger opponents; this time, however, it was probably going to be a high flying affair and Digital needed to prepare for that. "So, you think you can take this gal?" asked Nick as he sat down beside Digital. "She is very talented. She is probably one of the best ever." Digital knew that this would be a new test for him. New faces he had never seen before and old faces he had never fought before were always contenders. His past accomplishes meant nothing in tournaments like GTT if he could not get accustomed to their style. The ring seemed like it was a place Digital had never wrestled in before. In his mind though, it was no excuse. He used to be able to prepare for anything, and he did not want to let himself down. "She is good, no question about it. I know though, that when I am on the top of my game, very few people can actually beat me. Last time was just an anomaly, not the rule," he said as he took the remote control of a small TV they had in the gym and turned it on. The TV station was in a commercial break. There was some commercial for a fast food, telling people how big, juicy and fun their burgers were but leaving behind the part of the triple bypass surgery. When the commercials ended, there was some cheesy Mexican soap opera with big breasted women and the same plot of every other soap out there. There is a poor woman who falls in love with a rich guy who is about to get married to this venomous woman who only wants his money. During its course, the women battle for the love of the guy, we find out that the poor woman is actually the long lost daughter of some old rich guy and now she has a bunch of money. In the end, the two end up together. Sorry for ruining it for you. "We interrupt this program to bring you a special report," the busty anchorwoman announces all of the sudden. "After the Grito de Lares holiday that passed last month, the FBI and the governor have decided to put the island on a red alert. It has come to their attention that the Boricua Popular Army or better known as the Macheteros are planning different attacks on federal as well as local government buildings all around the island. The leader of the group, Filiberto Ojeda Ríos, was murdered last year by the FBI during the aforementioned celebration and since then, pro-independence groups have been mobilizing all across the island." "The FBI are said ‘to be in a manhunt of these terrorists’ and are using all of their resources to stop them from revolting like they did already in the 1950s. We will keep you posted as everything develops. We now return you to your regularly scheduled program." They went back to the soap opera. Nick and Digital were still shocked. They believed, like everyone else, that the Macheteros had already been caught or murdered. Now it appeared that they went deeper than everyone thought. It was very surprising. That didn’t really affect them directly though, so they continued discussing the wrestling news. "So, you got anything special planned for your match against Karina Wolfenden?" asked Nick, saying the name with a little bit of lust in his eyes. "No yet. I have been doing some planning though. You have to understand though. She is a legend. There are a few dream matches that I want to have before I retire and that was one of it. I just never thought that it was actually going to happen. I will go out there and give it my all, and if I lose, I know I lost to one of the best. On the other hand, I am the PRIME killer and she being from PRIME makes it a little bit more special," he said, standing up now that his body had balanced itself out in terms of temperature and he grabbed his gear. "Oh well, I have to go now," Nick said picking up his own gear. "You know how Sheila gets when I’m late," he said with a smile as he waved goodbye to Digital. "Good luck man. I’m sure you can beat them all," he said as the door to the gym closed behind him. Digital put all of his stuff in a black duffel bag and began to walk out. He looked at the ring one last time before leaving and smiled. No matter how many heartaches and headaches that ring had given him, he still loved the thing. Psychologists would call it ‘madness’. ------ "It is time." Alejandro Castro had been waiting for this moment for almost a year. He had successfully become the secret face of the organization. Nobody ever got together with the real boss anymore. Alejandro would give the boss status reports every two days. The boss’ plan had started to come together. The few that knew about the plan could be fully trusted and the others were kept in the dark. Secrets are always the key. The group got in their respective cars and they all went to the directions they had been given. They had formed a perimeter around the house so that no one was able to escape. They knew that their target was versatile so they didn’t want to take any chances. The plan had to go as smoothly as possible. Alejandro and his three bodyguards would take care of doing the job. The old adage was always imbedded in Alejandro’s mind, ‘if you want something done right, you must do it yourself’. The three bodyguards went to the front door dressed as electricians. They knocked on the door and waited for an answer. They heard the voice of a woman. "¡Por ahí voy! ¡Un momento!" They waited patiently while she answered the door. She looked through the window besides the door and saw the uniforms. She opened the door slowly and enough so that she could see clearly. They smiled at her reassuringly. "Good afternoon, ma’am. We work for the AEE and we got a call that there was some problem with the electric lines of your house," one of them said the speech they had been told to say. She opened the door now and looked at them strangely. "We don’t have any problems with our electricity. Are you sure you got the right house? Maybe you took a wrong turn or someone got confused with the numbers." "No. This is the right place." As he said this, the biggest of the bodyguards enveloped her in his huge arms. The three of them pushed in while the arms of the big bodyguard kept her immobilized. One of them took a roll of duct tape and silenced the muffled screams. She was pushed into a chair and tape was put on her arms and legs. Alejandro saw that the first part of the mission went by flawlessly and went inside the house himself. He took out his gun and aimed it at the thrashing woman. She stopped for a moment. "If you are calm and do as I say, I won’t have to kill you. I am sure you want to live right? If you promise to not make a sound and to let us do our job, I promise you that we won’t hurt him. If you do not do what I say, I am going to torture him in front of you then I’m going to torture you in front of him and finally I will find a way for you to kill him and then leave you alive so you have to live with it for the rest of your life." She calmed down. Alejandro knew that everything was going according to plan, and the last thing he wanted was to ruin everything when his target arrived. He motioned his henchmen to move her out of the living room and into the master bedroom where the target would not see her when he arrived. It was time, finally, after all these years to act. Alejandro knew that their organization was going to be persecuted, maligned by the media and by every citizen with an air of politician but he knew that their fight would eventually be the most beneficial to his island, to his home and he would do anything in his power to make his organization rise in the face of such turmoil. Now, they waited. ------- Digital got out of his car slowly. He was feeling the effects of his hard training regiment, but like they all said, ‘no pain, no gain’. He took his duffel bag from the passenger seat and walked towards his front door. He had called Vero a couple of times but he got no answer. He thought that she was probably in the shower. He took out the keys of the house from his pocket and opened the door. He set his duffel bag on the couch and walked on, not noticing the cigar sitting on the ashtray in front of him. Everything was normal. He walked towards his bathroom but he sensed something. He couldn’t hear anything. If she was taking a bath, he would hear it. He thought then that maybe she had gone out to the supermarket to buy something but her car was still in the garage. He felt like something was wrong. He turned around but saw no one. He turned back again and this time… Darkness.
