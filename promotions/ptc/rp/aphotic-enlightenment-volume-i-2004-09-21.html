Aphotic Enlightenment - Volume I

21 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-09-21 His body ached with bittersweet pleasure. 48 hours after his clash with Luke Chadbourne, and his lower back still rithed in pain. The effects of a well executed Sharpshooter shot through his spine, disguised only by half a dozen Motrin and a heated pad. With both legs resting atop the ovular wooden table in front of him, Falcon Mega could finally rest easy for the first time in weeks. His discomfort was merely an afterthought when it came to the gratification he felt for overcoming the odds he set before himself. Loneliness, self doubt, the inability to control his own human emotions. When the high pitched crash of that bell reverberated through his ears, only survival mattered. Tristan could have completely lost himself in the thought of accomplishment, had it not been for the horrific sound of a telephone echoing throughout the pastel walled room. With a grimace upon his face, he removed his feet from the oak surface, and did the only thing he could to stop the nuisance. "Hello?" Tristan cleared his throat as he awaited a voice on the other end. Expecting to hear the unfamiliar tone of a hotel employee, or perhaps a member of the GTT staff, Tristan reclined back in his original position. Replacing his look of displeasure with confusion, the fighter known as Falcon Mega quickly sat back up at a 90 degree angle. "Cammy!?! Hi ... How did you know where to find me?" Tristan now completely stood out of his chair, coming off a bit rude with his rather hostile initial comment. He shook his head in disaproval of himself, and awaited his former girlfriend's response. Annoyance now overtook him as the phone's cord jerked him back to its base. "My mother ... of course she did. So, what do I owe the pleasure of this phone call? Miss me?" His tone was rather sarcastic, but the tremble in his laugh suggested a hint of desperation. A solemn look then overcame his face as quick footsteps lead him in a small circle. "Yeah, about that. I had a little bit ... okay a lot of alcohol that night, and was just pissed off at the world. I'm sorry if I acted like a jackass." His silence now extended slightly longer as Tristan listened to the beautifully humble voice that he never truly appreciated until now. His pace stopped abruptly, as his gaze shot at the phone. "What do you mean you're here? As in here, here?" The events of the past few weeks seemingly vacated Tristan's mind, and he forget entirely about the fight he and his ex-lover partook in. He was now both nervous, yet excited. Still in the back of his mind, shrowded in denial, was the thought of Cameron's daughter, Kayla. The thought that gave him strength going into the First Round of the GTT, now petrified him greatly. Pretending to know who her Father now accomplished nothing. Within the next few minutes, he would meet with Cammy, and this time there was no escaping the truth. "Alright, well come on up. I'm in room 327. See ya in a minute." Tristan hung up the phone quietly, and again went into panic. He studied the room, and realized what a mess he had allowed to become of it. A greasy pizza box lay at the end of the bed, half covered by the floral patterned hotel comforter. A large gray suitcase lay wide open on the floor, with unfolded clothing spewing out its sides. Black slacks, still with last night's belt lay slung over a padded chair. Tristan than glanced up at a mirror above the dresser, and examined himself for the first time this morning. Two day gruff spanned his entire jawline, and continued down his neck. His short hair flowed in various directions, and his outfit consisted only of green basketball shorts. The generally confident athlete quickly became very self concious at the thought of seeing Cammy again, and Tristan grabbed the first shirt he could find that didn't look too wrinkled. The agony in his back was now completely irrelivent as he dashed around the room, cleaning up anything he could get his hands on. Just as he smashed the pizza box in half, and shoved it in the ridiculously small trash can, a light wrap on the door beckoned to him. So gentle, just as he had always remembered her. Tristan composed himself, and began the journey which seemed like forever towards the room's portal. He reached for the knob, and with a big grin lining his lips, opened the door. Expecting the angelic face of the woman he once intended to marry, he saw nothing. Agitation consumed him once again before he looked down, and saw the 2'9" frame of a blond haired, blue eyed, 2 year old girl. Tristan stopped breathing as his eyes met those of the innocent young beauty. The most adorable smile he had ever witnessed flashed his way, as a tiny voice escaped the little girl's mouth. "Hi Daddy." ---------- Bewilderment was the only adjective to describe how Tristan felt. For the first hour of Cammy and Kayla's arrival, he could manage no other emotion. He had to hand it to Cameron, she knew how to approach the situation. Unable to simply come out and tell Tristan he was the father, she chose the only way this man could fully enjoy the news. With this route, there was no awkward silence, just celebration. An informal greeting was all that was had between Cameron and Tristan, as the ladder quickly turned his full attention to his new daughter. Dishonesty didn't matter at this point, for he was a father. The three acted like the average family, as they spent the afternoon playing tourists in the nation's capitol. It was only when Kayla had worn herself out, and was taking a nap that her parents would have the inevitable talk that neither really wanted to have. "Why didn't you just tell me Cammy?" His approach was blunt, and caught Cammy off guard. "Not wasting any time are ya?" A desperate chuckle escaped the throat of Cameron as she squirmed in her chair to get comfortable. "I see no point in it. You came here ... you knew it would happen sooner or later. I'm just saving us some time." Cammy nodded yes in approval, and lowered her head in shame. Tristan braced himself for whatever Cameron had to say. "I didn't ... I didn't know if she was yours." Tristan's heart lept up into his throat, as his mouth sat agape. The news sent a deafening blow throughout his entire body, knocking the wind out of him. A whirlwind of emotions then ensued, as the sedated face of Cammy finally glanced in Tristan's direction. Anger and hatred came first, tearing apart every moral Tristan ever had about not hitting a woman. Luckily, soon there after came an even more powerful emotion ... dejection. The only woman Tristan ever loved as much as his mother had betrayed him in the worst way possible, and it broke him down. The two emotional states then clashed in a plethora of blues and reds, and reared its ugly head in the somber hotel room. "God Dammit!!" In a single motion, Tristan's giant paw hurled his laptop against the nearest wall of the room, smashing on impact. Fear overcame Cammy, as little Kayla abruptly sat up in the bed. "What happened Daddy?" The unoffending words of Kayla temporarily silenced the rage of Tristan. He stared at his only daughter with blood shot eyes, not knowing how to explain himself. Behind him came his only saving grace. "Nothing sweetie, Daddy just dropped his computer. Hey how about you go and get yourself a soda?" Kaylas' eyes lit up with joy as she nodded her head emphatically. She quickly jumped off the bed, and ran to the side of her mother. Cammy retrieved 75 cents from her purse, and placed it in the delicate hands of her child. "Thank you Mommy." Kayla turned, and began her sprint towards the door. Tristan admired her innocence as she fled past him. "Come right back sweetie." "I know, I know." Kayla reached the door, and with all her might, pulled the large object towards her. Just before the tiny individual exited, she grabbed a shoe, and placed it in the door way. Very intelligent, a trait passed on by her mother. As Kayla dissapeared down the hallways, Tristan turned back to the awaiting Cammy. "I'm sorry ... I forgot she was here." Regret now painted his face, as Cammy shook it off. Her nose wrinkled up as it always had before she was about to cry. "Its okay. Tristan its not what you think. Please let me explain." Tristan once again shifted gears, and his anger again came forward. "Explain what Cammy? How you were fucking someone behind my back?" "It wasn't like that Tris.." Tristan interrupted her as he stomped around the room. "Then tell me how it was! What, did ya think you should get a head start before I left the country? Just couldn't wait to let some other mother fucker up in ya huh? "Jesus Christ stop it, I was raped!" ... ... Tristan couldn't move. He deserved to be lynched for his actions. His eyes went unflinching at Cammy, as she buried her face in hands and began to cry hysterically. Words escaped Tristan as the silence of the room rang volumes. With the sobbing Cameron sitting in a heap at the room's corner, the telephone began to ring. Tristan ignored the first signal, but his animosity for the sound soon became relief as he realized this changed the subject at hand beautifully. Tristan stomped towards the phone, and with a stern voice, answered. "Hello ... This is he ... Yes, very comfortable thank you ... El? She advanced? ... Alright, thank you. I'll see you then." Tristan slowly hung up the phone, and turned back towards the room. The terrifying news Cameron just unleashed upon him momentarily left his mind, as his thoughts drifted to the Amazon, El. A 160 pound beast of a woman, making her pro wrestling debut in GTT. He had trouble wrapping his mind around the idea of a woman being victorious in such a prestigious event. Especially when her opponent was the well crafted Lestatt Knight. Tristan had the pleasure of seeing some of the best female talent in the world while working in Japan, but it was an extremely rare event for them to compete against men. "I'm ba-aaaack." Kayla kicked the shoe out of the way, and entered back into the room, soda in hand. Cameron quickly rid her face of any tears, and smiled at little Kayla. "Why are you crying Mommy?" "Oh sweetie, Daddy just made me laugh really hard. Hey, wanna watch some t.v.? "Sure!" Kayla then lept back onto the bed, and snatched the remote. As she began flipping through channel after channel, Tristan took a seat next to his former girlfriend, and placed both his forearms on the table. Cammy sniffled as her crimson eyes gazed into the royal blue orbs of Tristan. "I'm going to ask you this one time Cammy. Do not bullshit me. Who did this to you?" Tristan's hoarse whisper pierced through the soul of Cammy, and made her cringe. She took a deep breath, and with a tremble in her voice, answered Tristan honestly. "Your Father."
