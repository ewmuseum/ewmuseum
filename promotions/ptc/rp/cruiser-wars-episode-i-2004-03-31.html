Cruiser Wars! - Episode I

31 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-03-31 A short, short time ago, in a galaxy close, close by... The scene opens up in the cold recesses of space. There are hundreds, nay, thousands of stars dotting the infinite blackness laid out before us. Suddenly, the "Star Wars" theme begins to play, and we are greeted with two big, yellow words that quickly fade away into the background: "CRUISER WARS!" As these words vanish from our sight, a new set of words begins to scroll up from the bottom of the screen, and they too eventually vanish into the dark void: EPISODE I A Starr Is Born -- AND DESTROYED! It is a time of peril and uncertainty throughout the galaxy. A call has been sent forth to every corner of space, for only the most gallant and courageous of warriors to participate in the PTC's CRUISERWEIGHT TOURNAMENT, a contest which will surely determine the fate of mankind as we know it. Great evil has descended upon the tournament, and only THE COCKJOCKEY, a midget with extraordinary powers, can save the galaxy from CERTAIN DOOM. Armed with his legendary GIANT INFLATABLE PENIS, the pint-sized wunderkind steps up to his first challenge, facing off against the dastardly JASON STARR. Can our hero vanquish his foe? Or will all of us share in the same HORRIBLE FATE... whatever that may be? The words scroll off into infinity, and the music fades out. The camera pans downward, to reveal planet Earth. With great haste, the camera then zooms in on the planet. The view changes rapidly, passing through layer upon layer of atmosphere, breaking through the clouds, until we see that we are somewhere over the United States. The camera continues its speedy descent, zooming in closer and closer on a large metropolitan area. It isn't long before we can begin to discern city blocks, then buildings, and finally, a sports arena. We almost seem to pass through the ceiling, as the scene changes from outdoors to indoors, until finally, the camera stops and settles inside the locker room (which is really more of an apartment than a locker room) of one of XUW's longest-running and most decorated stables, the Boomer Sooners. The camera pans around to a black, leather reclining chair. Seated in the chair is the widely recognizable figure of RAW commentator, and the mentor/manager of the Boomer Sooners, Jim "JR" Ross. His face is quietly buried in the daily newspaper. In the background, the top of what appears to be a giant inflatable penis pokes up from behind a kitchen counter. As this happens, a motif from the "Jaws" theme plays... Duuuuuh-dun... JR looks up from his newspaper, as the top of the penis ducks back behind the counter. JR blinks, then looks around the room for a moment. He shrugs, and goes back to reading his paper. The penis pops up from behind the counter again... Duuuuuh-dun... JR pays no attention, as the penis ducks behind the counter once more. A few moments later, the penis shows up again in the background. This time, it appears to be in front of the counter, and entering from stage left. It wanders along the background until it walks off, stage right. Dun-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun... JR continues reading his newspaper... as the giant inflatable penis appears again, this time moving from stage right to stage left! Horrors! Dun-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun DUUUUUH-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun DUUUUUH-dun, dun-dun, dun-dun... The penis goes off the screen... and then it re-appears from stage left, this time, directly behind JR's chair! It draws ever closer to it's unsuspecting victim, as the music grows more intense! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! It moves off stage right, and then returns once more!! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! DUN-DUN! Then it stops, and sinks out of view behind JR's chair... JR looks up from his paper again, with a puzzled look on his face. He looks to his left, then to his right... and then leans around and looks behind the chair... KER-PLOW!!! JR screams, as he gets the hell bludgeoned out of him by the giant inflatable penis!!! JR: BY GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOD!!! The impact sends JR tumbling out of his chair and sprawling onto the floor in a tangle of limbs, lard, and Resistol! Who should emerge from behind the black leather chair, but... ...THE COCKJOCKEY! With a triumphant smile on his face, The Cockjockey leaps majestically up into the chair, and raises his giant inflatable penis in glorious victory! JR gets up to his feet, dusts himself off, and angrily addresses his midget attacker. JR: By God, what in the hell are you doing, sneaking around and ambushing poor ol' JR like that!? Through hellfire and brimstone, Cockjockey, you should be training for the Cruiserweight tournament! You have to carry on the Sooner tradition of winning every single PTC tournament that one of us has entered! By God, Saij took everybody to the woodshed in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup last year, and with God as my witness, you need to stomp some serious mudholes in everyone's asses, and walk every last sumbitch dry! You need to be serious about this, ol' JR says! And what're you doing? You're being a lazy bastard! By God, you're as pathetic as a one-legged man in an ass-kicking contest! An ass-kicking contest, by God! You give JR one good reason why you're slacking off like this! The Cockjockey, who is incapable of speech, looks oddly at JR and flails his arms wildly. JR: By God, ol' JR doesn't understand what you're trying to say! The Cockjockey frowns, and flails his arms again, exactly in the same fashion that he did the first time... only now, with a hint of frustration. JR: The Cockjockey's done lost his mind! Somebody stop the damn match!! The midget slaps his own forehead and shakes his head. Then, slowly, as if explaining something to a small child for the millionth time, he flails his arms yet again, exactly in the same manner as the previous two times. JR: Wait a minute, JR remembers now! You can't talk, so you use a strange form of sign language that involves flailing your arms! The Cockjockey nods briskly, thinking that JR finally understands what he's saying. JR: But JR has no idea what the hell your language means! The Cockjockey falls to the ground, a la Dragon Ball Z, or Super Mario RPG. Then he gets back up and shakes his head. JR: The only person in the world capable of understanding you is Saij, and that poor young stud is in a coma right now! How the hell are you going to communicate your thoughts to the PTC audience, Cockjockey!? By God, how are you going to talk trash to your opponents, and explain how you're going to crush everybody in the tournament!? Granted, you've already said more than your opponent, Jason Starr... which is a feat in and of itself, since you can't even speak a single word, but still! How, JR asks? By God, HOW!? As if on cue, who should burst through the locker room door, but... the XUW Co-World Champion, Fatbadger! ...AND... ...Cardboard Chase Wilson! (Who, if you are not already aware, is a life-size cardboard cut-out of the real former XUW wrestler Chase Wilson, and is actually credited with once eliminating a man with a genius IQ from a battle royale. There folks, that's your history lesson for the day.) Fatbadger walks into the room, with half of the XUW World Title belt (which he himself cut in half with a sword) slung over his shoulder, and Cardboard Chase tucked under his arm. He smiles broadly with an idiotic expression on his face, as he greets JR and Cockjockey. Fatbadger: JR! Cockford McJocksonberry! HUZZAH! JR looks oddly at Fatbadger. JR: By God, Badger, that's not your catchphrase! That's Saij's! Fatbadger: Oh, right... BUZZSAW! JR: That's better... JR supposes... Fatbadger: So what's goin' on here? You guys look as if you've been engrossed in conversation, which is hard to imagine, seeing how Cockjockey can't talk and all... JR: By God, that's exactly the problem, young Fatrick! The Cockjockey is incapable of speech, and nobody can understand his weird form of sign language! Fatbadger: Dude, Saij can totally understand his language! JR: HE'S IN A COMA!! Fatbadger: Oh... right, that... JR: By God, there must be something we can do to help Cockjockey communicate with the rest of the world... Fatbadger: Yeah, so let's try to think of a plan! JR and Fatbadger both sit down on the couch and assume their thinking positions. The screen goes all wavy, indicating a flashback. The scene switches over from the Boomer Sooners locker room, to... the Boomer Sooners locker room! Except, this locker room is in the not-too-distant, but distant-enough-so-Saij-isn't-in-a-coma-yet, past! Saij and Fatbadger are seen sitting on a couch, watching TV. Saij is slouched deep into the soft cushions, while Fatbadger is just looking aimlessly around the room. He turns to Saij with a puzzled look on his face. Fatbadger: Dude, Saij, how come you're the only person in the entire world who can understand The Cockjockey? Saij gives Fatbadger an odd look. Saij: Why the fark do you ask, Fatty? Fatbadger: Well, because for some reason, I have this inkling that I may need to know this information someday, most likely in flashback form. Saij: Dude, I could just tell you myself when that moment comes. Fatbadger: Yeah, but what if, like you're in a coma and nobody else can answer my question? Saij: A coma? Dude, that'll never happen. Fatbadger and Saij both look at the camera for a moment, then back to each other. Fatbadger: I dunno man, it could be important. Remember the time JR had amnesia, and we remembered in a flashback that JR once told us that the cure for amnesia is BBQ? Saij: No. But anyway, to answer your question... I'm actually not the only person in the world who understands Cockjockey. There is one other... Cardboard Chase! Fatbadger gasps! Fatbadger: GASP! Cardboard Chase, but how? Saij: It's simple, he and Cockjockey have a telepathic link and communicate with each other quite often. Saij and Fatbadger look over in the corner, to see Cardboard Chase and Cockjockey engrossed in an intense game of Battleship. Remarkably enough, Cockjockey's ships have all been sunk, while Cardboard Chase's remain untouched. The schlong-riding midget looks visibly frustrated, sitting and pouting with his arms folded. Cardboard Chase appears to have a smug, triumphant look on his face. The camera returns to Saij and Fatbadger. Fatbadger: Dude, that's incredible! Saij: Yeah, especially considering Cardboard Chase is an inanimate object with no brain... but then again I was never really big on logic. The scene goes wavy again, and we return to the locker room of present day. JR and Fatbadger look at each other. JR: By God, Cockjockey can communicate with Cardboard Chase!? That doesn't help us at all! Fatbadger: Huh? Why not? JR: Because, Cardboard Chase can't talk either! Fatbadger: Whaddaya mean he can't talk? ...YOU MEAN CHASE IS DEAD!? JR: By God, that's not what... Fatbadger: CHASE!!! NOOOOOOOOO!!! Fatbadger starts to sob like a little girl. JR: But... Fatbadger: WHY, CHASE, WHY!? JR: By God... Fatbadger: YOU WERE SO YOUNG!!! AND NUBILE!!! JR: BY GOD, LISTEN TO JR, BADGER!!! Chase was never capable of speech! He's a piece of damn cardboard!!! Fatbadger stops crying. Fatbadger: So... what you're saying is... I can have his stuff? JR: Ye--NO, damnit!! By God Badger, you're as goofy as a pet coon! JR and Fatbadger look over in the corner, and see Cockjockey and Cardboard Chase playing a rousing game of Chess. Oddly enough, Cardboard Chase seems to have the upper hand, having eliminated most of Cockjockey's pieces from the board. The schlong-riding midget is clearly upset, as he will apparently be checkmated in the next turn. The camera returns to JR and Fatbadger. Fatbadger: OW!!! Fatbadger rubs his head. JR: By God, what's wrong, Badger? Fatbadger: I just had an idea! My brain nearly split in half when it happened, but I managed to pull through. Anyway, I know how we can help Cockjockey, and use Chase's assistance as well! JR: By God! Fatbadger leans over and whispers into JR's ear. Unforunately, we don't get to hear what Fatbadger's brilliant plan is, because the scene fades to black. Rest assured, however, it's a brilliant plan. That's what we call a cliffhanger, folks. Yeah, that's it... cliffhanger.
