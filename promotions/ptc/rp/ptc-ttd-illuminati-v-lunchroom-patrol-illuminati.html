TTD - Illuminati -v- Lunchroom Patrol

19 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I L L U M I N A T I - Rp Title "Coming to a head" Finally a win in this tournament… Finally ILLUMINATI has risen up the ranks to where we belong And I guess second place isn’t that bad Although we are sharing the spotlight… We still have the loss to Invincible Gabe and Scott Rampage on our records…but at least we don’t have a loss to the Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling also. Now all we have to do is have a strong showing against the Lunchroom patrol. That shouldn’t be so hard…but we definitely can’t look past them. We still have a shot at the title match. If only we could have that first week back. We’ll get another shot…sometime. The darkness outside of the limo was broken into a dotted line by the streetlights. Each light casting a spotlight on it’s own section of road. The two men that folks have come to identify as I L L U M I N A T I, sit peacefully in the backseat…their backs lying against the black leather upolstry of the seats. Kenny’s eyes drift toward the window, he watches the lights appear and disappear as if an imaginary light switch is being flipped on and off. He turns his attention toward the seedy little lawyer, Kellan Jefferies, whom they can’t seem to get rid of now. Looks like they had acquired him as a permanent mascot. The ever-annoying lawyer decides it is a good idea to pick up some cocaine from one of his more unsavory clients. Both CarteL and Kenny had told him it wasn’t a good idea, but the persistent little shit had talked the two of them into allowing him this one venture. So he sits across from them with a lap sized mirror cutting himself lines. Kenny looks upon him with disgust. “And tell me again why this shit is a good idea?” Kenny ponders to anyone who will listen before letting out an audible sigh and leaning his head back against the leather seat. The words vanish into the air with the sound of Jefferies nostril pulling the yellowish white powder through a straw. After a few more sniffles from the lawyer as he cleared his left nostril. He looks up at the two mVw superstars before speaking, “It’s a celebration.” Jefferies spouts out as he dabs the powdery drug and rubs some across his gum-line. He attempts to pass the mirror to the opposite side of the car, but both men wave him off. “Man keep that shit to yourself. We don’t want none.” CarteL says to the geeked out lawyer. “I got something we can use for celebrating.” Out of his suit jacket pocket comes a nicely rolled blunt. “Good thing mVw doesn’t f*ckin’ drug test…or we’d both be gone by now.” Kenny says to the first man he has ever smoked weed with. Kenny chuckles to himself, amused with his humor. CarteL lights the end turning the blunt slowly making sure the cherry is even. Taking a long puff he hands the cigar to the left. Kenny takes a deep drag also. “Y-you guys gonna let me hit that too?” The jittery lawyer asks while Kenny pulls off the blunt. CarteL blows the smoke right into the lawyer’s face. “Do your shit and we’ll do ours.” CarteL says as Kenny hands the blunt back to him. Kenny holds the smoke as long as possible before releasing it in a choking, coughing breath. CarteL laughs at his tag team partner, who is still a little green to… well green. CarteL takes another long drag off the slowly burning blunt before handing it back to Kenny. Holds it for a moment before taking another pull. He slowly presses the unlit end to his lips and pulls another nice sized drag. Feeling the effects of the first drag already. The motion in the back of the limo begins to slow. Kenny’s mind begins to wander as he slowly releases the smoke into the cloudy backseat. Damn I need to stop doing this shit. I don’t want to tell this bastard I usually get sick as shit from this. But I can’t turn it down. I don’t want it to seem like a f*ckin’ insult to him either. Tag team partner or not I still don’t know him all that well. All I know is I’m about tired of that fucking conniving little prick of a lawyer. I guess I don’t need his services considering how court went today. I’m still trying to figure out why we are celebrating. The court battle didn’t go the way I had exactly planned. His ex-wife was awarded my house, my cars, full custody of our daughter and even my dog. Okay so court didn’t go anywhere near how I wanted it to turn out. I guess this celebration is because everything is basically done…cause I don’t have much to show from his efforts. But then again a lot of it can be my own fault. The assault charges two weeks after the initial arrest on spousal battery. The first charge was bull, though. I don’t remember putting my hands on Jana. In the twelve years I have known her, I have never put my hands on her. She was my life, well her and Jade. Now they had become nothing more then drains on my bank accounts. Nice way to think of people who use to be the most important things in my life, huh? I know I made some mistakes but this is awful. Damn I need to get my head straight. Nice thing to think about with another important match coming up, huh? Worrying about my personal problems. His attention is shattered by a burning sensation to his inner thigh. A girlish squeal escapes his lips as he bats the cherry from his lap. The other occupants of the backseat turn their attention to the six foot, four-inch man who just yelped like a young girl. Both men erupt in laughter. “Damn man, you let me burn myself.” Kenny says to his baked tag team partner, handing him the unlit blunt. “I just burned a hole in my new f*ckin’ suit.” The words roll out as he checks the leg of his suit pants. The suit he had paid two grand for earlier that day. He liked the suit… black with silver pinstripes and damn it looks good on him. After examining the burn to his left leg, Kenny turns his gaze toward the two men who were still locked in hysterical laughter. “Ha, ha…f*ckin’ funny.” Kenny says to his counterparts in the limo. “Damn, kid… t-that was s-some… of the f-funniest s-shit I have ever seen.” CarteL says fighting back the laughter. Kellan Jefferies on the other hand has already given up the laughter, reaching into his suit pocket to pull out the baggie containing the rest of his cocaine. He dumps the remnants of the baggie onto the mirror in his lap. Flipping the bag inside out and stuffing it into his mouth. Kenny and CarteL look on in wonderment at the scene happening across car from them. Both men shake their heads. CarteL looks out the window for a few moments before returning his attention toward Kenny. “Where are we flying tonight?” CarteL asks directing himself toward Kenny. “We are headed back to Miami, fool.” Kenny replies, “Maybe you need to lay off that ganja. It’s killing your memory. Especially when you can’t remember that we are going to party in your hometown.” “Man I don’t get paid to remember shit, that’s this fool’s job.” CarteL spouts back at Kenny, while pointing at Jefferies who has made all his cocaine disappear and has apparently passed out. “What’s wrong with that little bitch, who passes out on coke?” CarteL says with a laugh. “How you gonna ask me, like I have ever done that shit before.” Kenny says with a chuckle. Kenny looks out the window seeing the light flicker again…his thoughts take over again. Next opponents; the Lunchroom Patrol from XUW… Not exactly one of the favorites of this tournament, but then again XUW seems to get less respect then mVw. So we can’t look past them just because there is no hoopla surrounding them. Especially seeing as there wasn’t much hoopla around us and I know we are a threat to Invincibleness. I still can’t believe we lost to them. It was my fault we lost. No matter if Rhys tries to tell me he didn’t feel let down by me. I know he does, so I have to make it up to him this time. Make sure I don’t drop the ball. That is my job…and I accept the deal. His attention is pulled away as Kellan Jefferies begins to convulse. A foamy white substance dribbles from his nose and the sides of his mouth. “DAMN! That muthaf*cker is ODin'!” CarteL yells as he moves over to the lawyer helping him turn to his side. Kenny is snapped back quickly, he furiously knocks on the window between the driver and the backseat. After a few moments the window begins to lower, Kenny yells through the growing crack, “Get us to the hospital. We’re having a problem.” The driver nods and the limo begins to build speed. The two wrestlers do what they can to help Jefferies. “He’s gonna die, if we don’t get him to the f*ckin’ hospital.” CarteL reiterates to his teammate. “Damn this fool and his hard drugs, told his ass that shit wasn’t a good idea.” Kenny responds. “Man we sat here and watched him gobble all that shit up, too.” CarteL says, “We could have stopped him.” “But I’ve seen him do more then that in one sitting in the hotel in Minnesota.” Kenny responds, “I figured he could handle it.” The limo roars through the night sky while these two tried to help out someone they really don’t want around anymore. They didn’t want to see him dead, but he was slipping closer as they sped toward the hospital. The limo comes to a screeching halt at the entrance of the emergency room. Kenny flips the backdoor open and emerges from the backseat carrying Jefferies legs, CarteL has the top end and the two of them move through the door. “He overdosed!” Kenny yelled to no one in particular as they entered the hospital. They weren’t alone for long. The nurses rushed around them with a gurney. That’s when the questions came. “What did he do?” “Where you also doing what he did?” “How much did he take?” “What are your names?” This is turning into a great night…
