Turning the badger - part two

24 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2004-08-24 “The Ripper considers himself to be a fair man, tiny badger. He aims to help you steel yourself for the contest ahead; and to that end…” “…you’ve nailed my arms to the table.” The Ripper arched one dark eyebrow, his deep, rough voice coming close to conveying humour, but falling tragically just short of it. “No, that was merely to stop you fidgeting.” Fatbadger whimpered. He was not enjoying life inside the Domicile of Darkness. His long hair hung down in front of his face, and he looked even smaller than usual compared to the bulk of The Ripper sat opposite him. It wasn’t that Fatbadger was a particularly small person, being one inch greater than six feet tall, but the general demeanour he assumed when confronted with The Ripper made him look more like a midget than a professional wrestler… or even a professional wrestling midget. Equally, the Ripper was not some inhuman giant. He measured closer to seven feet tall than six, and his bulk was composed of pure muscle, but there had been taller, larger men in wrestling before him, and there would be bigger, stronger men again in the future. He had a certain something though; an intensity that transcended simple size, and made his authority something that could never be questioned. Why? He was The Ripper, and that in itself was enough. Thus it was that Fatbadger cowered before him, nails going through the sleeves of his jacket and in to a heavy wooden table inside the Domicile of Darkness. The nails were not actually piercing Fatbadger’s arms, for that would have been counter-productive. The Ripper was in this tournament to win, and to do so, changes would have to be made to Fatbadger, but not ones that involved him losing the use of his limbs. “You have to learn how to be strong, badger. When you were motivated by revenge, you managed to briefly get your hands on the World Title belt. Did that teach you anything?” Fatbadger tried to stroke his chin as he thought, but sadly for him the sleeves of his jacket were still nailed to the table. “It thought me…” “Yes?” “It taught me that I like Funyuns!” “No.” “It taught me that I like to drink Code Red?” “No. Do not try the patience of The Ripper.” “It taught me that The Shadow punches like a girl!” “True, but no.” “Then… I don’t know… but I don’t want to perish!” The Ripper came close to sighing, but he managed to draw on his spare reserves of patience that he had stockpiled for such an event as this. Truly, trying to reason with Fatbadger was a new challenge for the master of the darkness. “Foolish badger, you have all of the answers right in front of your face, but you refuse to acknowledge them. Truly, there are none so blind as those who will not see.” “Huh?” “It matters not, you intellect would not comprehend it.” “Oh, okay then.” Fatbadger sat back, a little happier in the knowledge that he wouldn’t have to think any more for a while. Of course, sitting back ripped the sleeves clean off his jacket, making him fall over backwards… but at least he wasn’t having to think! The Ripper shook his head, before picking up Fatbadger by the scruff of his neck. “There are ways to train people, Fatbadger, but this is The Ripper’s favourite.” With that, he turned with frightening sped for one of his size, and threw Fatbadger headlong across the table, causing him to slide off the edge and fall in a crumpled heap in the Fireplace of Darkness. By the time the lovable jobber had even managed to sit upright, he was getting covered in foul-smelling liquid. No, he hadn’t soiled himself… yet… for this was the sharp smell of gasoline being poured over him. At the table sat the smiling Ripper, holding a book of matches. “Do you wish to perish, tiny badger?” “Nooooooooooooooooooo!” “Then learn to fight back. It must be instinct, not a last resort. The Ripper cannot protect you in the ring at all times when we face all of PTC. You must be able to defend yourself.” Fatbadger lay back and whimpered, expecting a match to be struck at any minute. “Clean yourself up in the Showers of Darkness, and meet The Ripper in the Gym… of Darkness.” With that, the larger man left. ------------------------------------------------------------------ The Ripper pulled back on one of the ring ropes, and satisfied with the tension, he let go. When looking back over his career, many words had been used to describe Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy. They tended to involve domination, power, evil, seriousness, and above all of those… darkness. Yet The Ripper was never a man who surrounded himself either with sycophants or the ultra-serious. On the contrary, Jack’s stable had always been combined of joking misfits, the kind of people who The Ripper felt most comfortable around. Nobody was trying to impress him, act too sombre, or be anything other than themselves. It was that kind of mentality that went in to the naming scheme of the Domicile of Darkness. In an obtuse ‘screw you’ aimed clearly at those who take the following of dark secrets too seriously, everything was apportioned the suffix ‘… of Darkness’. An in-joke that let on perhaps more than the Army of Darkness wished to show of their own selves. In the end, the army crumbled, and at this time only two remained standing, The Ripper having distanced himself from their predicament since making his return to the land of the living. None of this is to say that The Ripper has ever slacked in his devotion to the darkness. On the contrary, his single-minded determination and astonishing force of will were what brought XUW to its knees on more than one occasion. These words just go to show that The Ripper was never an easy man to pin down. Something of an enigma, there was always one more side of his character to be seen. Multi-faceted like the rarest diamond, The Ripper always possessed the ability to surprise. The man inside the battered wrestling ring looked out to see Fatbadger in his ring attire. It wasn’t much to look at, seeing as how the Boomer Sooners member was cowering again from the glare of The Ripper. Was this the man who had avenged his friend and become XUW World Champion, if only for a brief time? The Ripper shook his head. The message was not getting through. “Enter the ring, pitiful fool.” Fatbadger slid under the bottom rope, and The Ripper moved towards him quickly. The smaller man immediately dropped down and tried to make himself look as small as possible. Disgust covered the face of The Ripper as he stopped short of his opponent. His boot lashed out, and Fatbadger made a sound not unlike that of a small child. “Get up, badger.” As Fatbadger was getting to his feet, The Ripper rushed in. He threw Fatbadger in to a corner and followed him in, crushing the air from the lungs of his fellow XUW team member. Fatbadger fell to the floor and lay there, wheezing. “Is this all the Boomer Sooners have? Is this all your friend, Saij, will be able to do when The Ripper chooses to finish him too? Is this all, tiny badger?” Fatbadger tried to get up from the floor, but The Ripper moved to stand directly over him. One black leather boot was pressed down on the chest of the smaller man, and The Ripper laughed to himself. “Pick yourself up, Fatbadger.” Fatbadger struggled, but he could not remove the boot from his chest. “You are pathetic. It would appear that all hope is lost. The Ripper should end your existence right now. You have been given more mercy than The Ripper normally enjoys.” The Prince of Darkness pressed his boot down even harder on the chest of Fatbadger, crushing his torso and squeezing the air forcibly from his lungs. The lovable jobber’s eyes bulged fit to burst from their sockets as he struggled to bring oxygen to his blood stream. With one last, desperate effort, he pushed hard to the side and rolled out from under the boot of The Ripper. “Better, tiny badger, but not yet good enough.” The Ripper pulled back his leg, and the last things Fatbadger saw before darkness took him once again, were stars. Stars… and the ominous black form of The Ripper’s right boot. --------------------------------------------------------- When Fatrick J. McBadgerson woke up again, he was in a kitchen. The gleaming chrome surfaces contrasted sharply with the solid oak table, a feature that reminded Fatbadger of exactly where he was. This was still the Domicile of Darkness, and despite the shiny appearances of the surfaces, which threatened to scorch his retina at any moment, this was the Kitchen of Darkness. In the corner stood the architect of it all, Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy. “Drink the coffee, it might help.” Fatbadger looked at the coffee and sniffed it suspiciously. “Is this poisoned?” “It is coffee.” “That wasn’t what I asked.” The Ripper came to stand in front of Fatbadger, his huge frame obscuring the single bare light bulb in the room, casting an immense shadow over Fatbadger. The bottom lip of Mister Badger started to quiver again. “Do you want some Code Red then, badger? Do you want some treats? Funyuns and pretzels? Do you?” asked The Ripper, his face moving steadily closer to the now openly shaking Fatbadger. “Dude… please don’t kiss me…” SMACK! The back of Jack’s hand flew through the air at great speed and connected with the face of the man he was tormenting. Fatbadger dropped to the floor, knocking the coffee all over the place. The member of the Boomer Sooners raised one hand to his lip, and checked to see if he was bleeding. The Ripper advanced briefly, and Fatbadger dodged backwards again. “You’re still weak, badger; still far too weak. Clean this mess up.” The Ripper turned on his heel and went to leave the room. “I’m sorry for spilling the coffee of darkness,” pleaded Fatbadger, but it was too late for apologies. ----------------------------------------------------------------- “This is your final chance, tiny badger. Should you fail in this task, then you are unfit to participate in The Ripper’s team.” Fatbadger took to more trembling. He’d been practicing the trembling all week, it would appear. He’d certainly got the technique down perfectly, but there was something more behind his eyes now, more than just the start of another flood of tears. Was it that Fatbadger was actually tiring of The Ripper’s unusual torture? “What’s this got to do with wrestling?” asked Fatbadger, before cowering as The Ripper strode towards him. “Does The Ripper have to explain everything to a fool like you? Is The Ripper suddenly answerable to a moron, whose understanding of the world is so polluted that Greenpeace regularly protest against it? Cease with your questions and you might actually learn something.” Silence, as Fatbadger’s eyes hardened and he thought of a biting retort. The Ripper edged closer, and the badger’s resolve broke. With a look like a puppy dog attached to a kicking machine, he turned back to the task that he had been assigned. No, this was a step too far. “Jack… ummm… Mister The Ripper, sir.” The dark behemoth turned round, his icy glare boring a hole through Fatbadger’s head. “I still don’t get why this is important.” “Silence, tiny badger… and finish the Ironing of Darkness.” “I don’t see how ironing your shirts is going to help me fight in the…” Fatbadger got no further in his sentence, as The Ripper reached across and almost nonchalantly gripped one huge hand around the throat of the smaller man, lifting him off the floor and slamming him in to the wall. “Do not question the darkness! It is not for those like you to talk about. You will never understand more than where your next snack is coming from, or how to lose at rock, paper, scissors by choosing ‘paper’ every single time!” “But paper is unbeatableaaaarrrggghhhh!” “No, badger, it is not. A knife can cut through your paper defences and expose everything underneath. You are just a worthless shell. The Ripper thought that there might be some strength left underneath, that there would be a chance of redeeming the choices that Webster had taken in bringing this team together. The Ripper thought that perhaps you were not what everyone had said about you. That perhaps Saij’s words behind your back weren’t true, and that you could become something.” Hearing those words, something went out of Fatbadger. His resolve, any last strength he had was gone. The possibility that his friends thought he was worthless was a blow right to the heart. The Ripper dropped him to the ground. “It’s true, badger. They all joke behind your back, laughing at how you fluked your way to the World Title. Saij isn’t even grateful that you tried to get revenge for his injuries. He just thinks you’re worthless. You see how Rujasu has been hanging around with you all recently?” “Y… yes. He’s… he’s the fourth member… *sniffle* …of the Boomer Sooners!” The Ripper shook his head. ”No, Fatbadger. He’s your replacement. Your friends have betrayed you.” “They… they wouldn’t!” “Of course they would! You’re a pathetic shell, Fatbadger. You’ll never amount to anything in this world. You’re going to rot as a useless jobber all your life, until someone gets angry and ruins your career by hitting you a little too hard and breaking your scrawny little back. The Sooners will continue on as ever before, but without you. They’ve been waiting to unload you for months now. Who would want a friend who tries to lose matches intentionally? Face it, Fatbadger. Your career is a joke, your life is a joke… but you knew that already, didn’t you? This one hurts though, doesn’t it? Finding it out after all this time in your delusions. Fatbadger, your friends are a joke.” The Ripper turned away, and paused. If all had gone to plan… …and it had. Fatbadger stood there, his blood boiling. His pulse raced, and his eyes drew daggers in to the cold, dark heart of Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy. He was lying, he had to be. It was one thing to insult him, but not his friends. He’d lived for those guys, and he’d die for them if needs be. He hesitated. This was The Ripper. This was a man who could indeed cause him to ‘perish’. He couldn’t just attack him, could he? Could he? SLAM! The hard, hot metal of the iron smashed in to the back of The Ripper’s head, and the huge man fell down hard to the floor. Fatbadger stood above him, triumphant. He never thought to ask himself why The Ripper had stood there, not walking away, seeming almost to invite the attack. All he knew was that he had to defend his friends, even their names, and if it meant standing up to someone like The Ripper, then he could do it. He’d never felt so confident about anything in his life. If he could face down Jack “The Ripper” Nobbledy, the constant source of his darkest terrors for the past few years, then the rest of PTC should be a cakewalk. In letting Fatbadger attack him, in putting Fatbadger through torment after torment, The Ripper had won again. He had given the weakest link of the team the strength that he needed to prosper. Fatbadger would now fight with the strength and purpose he had when he won the World Title. The Ripper had actually helped out Fatbadger. Even lying face down in the Laundry Room of Darkness, The Ripper had once more shown that he would never lose the ability to surprise everyone around him.
