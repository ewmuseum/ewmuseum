EPILOGUE

28 May 2005

Date: 2005-05-28 Wicked. Malevolent. Execrable. These were all words that had been said of Jack "The Ripper" Nobbledy. Sadistic. Tortuous. Vile. All words that had been said of the coldly calculating man who once fronted the most feared stable in XUW. Evil. This man, one who was never seen to be out of control, who put the 'logic' in 'pathological', was standing in the arena where the PTC Cruiserweight Two Final was about to begin. He was there to see how the man he had been trying to shape would do. There was a chance that he would fail, but also one that he would succeed. Of all the people in the arena, The Ripper was the most certain of his triumph. "He left, just as you said he would," said The Ripper's companion, Sirius Smythe. "He had to believe it was his own decisions. He had to believe that he had defeated The Ripper. Only then would he have strength enough to stand for himself and compete with them, and defeat them." The ancient lawyer nodded his agreement. "You never did tell me why you wanted him to win this thing. Was it some sort of set-up, to repay him at a future date?" "No," said The Ripper, with something approaching emotion in his voice. "The Ripper felt a certain sense of… call it duty. He felt that since this man had given so much of himself to be used up and further the career of The Ripper, that it would only be fair to return a similar favour." "Except that the ones used up would be MacGregor and Windsor?" "Not to mention Tsunami Oni, Chris Collins, Enrique Morales, and anyone else to fall in this tournament." Sirius Smythe ran a hand across his bald, wrinkled pate, and turned to face his companion. "That is unlike you. I have never seen you feel anything for those you have used up. I have never seen you bat so much as an eyelid before taking apart whole stables of people. Why now, why change now?" The Ripper looked as if he was about to speak, but then paused, and seemed to change his mind. "Does it trouble you?" asked Smythe. "Not at all," said The Ripper, as the music started to play, heralding Cereal Killer's arrival to the ring. "Nothing disturbs The Ripper. Some things just have to be done, regardless of reputation, or whoever believes you to be a certain thing. Sometimes people benefit from decisions that The Ripper makes, and sometimes they do not. Rest assured though, all decisions benefit The Ripper in the end. The darkness will be served." With that, he fell silent, and the match started in earnest. The Ripper saw Cereal Killer match himself up againbst first Tessa Windsor, and then against Archibald MacGregor. A smile creased his dark lips as he looked at the other man in the ring. The victory would bring Cereal Killer happiness, but it would not be so for the others arrayed there. It might drive Tessa Windsor to misery, but it could drive MacGregor all the way to Hell. As a fellow fifth generation sinner, The Ripper looked forwards to working alongside him in the future… the very near future. … The match was nearing an end, and The Ripper turned to Smythe, his manipulative mind running through possible scenarios, and future corruptions. Thinking back to his protege, Cereal Killer, he laughed as he looked Sirius in the eye, only evil on his mind. "He will be happy tonight, perhaps, but tomorrow… tomorrow is when he finds out just how addictive those drugs were…"
