A place with a capital called Midgar. The scene subtly goes from live-action to computer animated as the clown apologizes to Jack. "Sorriango," the clown says in his weird little unpronounceable alien language. The KKFOS is from a planet in some far off galaxy and was dropped here to feed on the blood of humans after turning them into cotton candy cocoons with his ray gun that never seems to work. Just then, the third party on the ship, their manager/agent/best buddy James von Doom appears from the other side of the ship. James immediately realizes where they are at when he notices that they have all become computer animated. James slaps Jack in the back of the head. "YOU IDIOTS!" he exclaims. "WE'RE INSIDE THE FUCKING FINAL FANTASY VIDEO GAME!" Jack scratches his head, looking confused. "We are?" James nods his head and motions toward the city. "YES WE ARE! THAT'S MIDGAR WHERE FINAL FANTASY VII BEGINS!" "Oh..." Jack replies. He looks around and then shrugs his shoulders. "Who knows. Maybe we'll find our pal Seymour, mate." James throws his arms up in disgust as they sail to the pixilated coast off of Midgar. James shakes his head. "Why do I always end up in video games when I'm with these two guys?" * * * MIDGAR An hour later... Our trio of heroes have arrived in Midgar and have docked their ship on the beach. They have walked to the city gates and snuck in. Once inside, they are in the Midgar Sector 7 Slums. James looks around. "I can't believe I'm inside a video game...AGAIN!" he says with disgust. What are you guys? Just a couple of guys in stupid costumes and that's how he's going to see you." Jack looks befuddled. "Really?" he asks James with a timid voice. James nods his head. "Now, you guys are going to have to go out there and show everybody that you're not just a comedy act," James says. He then looks over and sees the clown trying to balance a ball on the tip of his nose. "Oh great. I'm sure that'll win you guys some gold." Just as these words leave James' mouth, the infamous Final Fantasy VII battle music sounds and they are stuck in a battle against two Special Combatants, enemies with 60 HP, 28 XP, who sometimes drop Grenades and Antidotes. James looks around as HP/MP stat board comes across the screen and a yellow triangle appears above his head. "Oh fuck..." he mutters to himself. "What? What is it, mate?" Jack asks. "We have to fight them," James replies. "Ok. This'll be easy. I mean, the clown and I have fake pirate swords and you have your fake little pistol-thingy and we're good, right?" Jack says with a hint of a smile. James is carrying the gun he used on his last hit, which he has considering his second job is as a hitman. The clown has a fake platic ray gun of DOOM~! and a fake plastic pirate sword. Jack has a fake plastic pirate pistol with one shot in it and a fake plastic pirate sword. James nods his head. "We can take'em." James is up first. He pulls out his pistol and shoots one of the Special Combatants in the head, killing it. James cackles evilly as the KKFOS is up next. The KKFOS chooses "EAT" on the attack menu and walks over and eats the second Special Combatant. Victory music plays in the background as they do their various victory poses. They receive a grenade and 50 gil. Jack takes the gil and hides it in his pocket. After the victory music stops, the three of them look at each other. "Uh, what do we do now?" Jack asks. James and the clown shrug their shoulders and they continue on their way with video game music playing in the background. * * * UPPER JUNON The next morning... After getting lost in Midgar five times in the span of two hours, our heroes decided that it would be best if they got out of there. "That is where we need to go," he says. "We can stow aboard there and cross the sea." Jack gets a devious look on his face. "You're becoming more and more of a pirate everyday my friend. They trade head motions, trying to convey to one another what the plan is but before they know it, the KKFOS is already following the soldiers in hopes of getting close enough to eat one of them.
